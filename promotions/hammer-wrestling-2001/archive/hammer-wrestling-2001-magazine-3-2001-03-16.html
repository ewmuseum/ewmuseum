Sledge is on your hard drive! Inferno is in your mailbox! And Hammer is now on your mind! It's been some crazy times and Hammer is on a roll. So far, everyone has been giving us some great compliments -- whether it's a member of the fed or just a fan, people love Hammer! Again, I've got to thank everyone who's made Hammer what it is. This is you guys' fed; I'm just the fool who's been gluing it all together. If you'll notice, I've changed the schedule some. There's now a 10-day schedule. This schedule will give you guys four days to get stuff in and me six days to write. As it's shaping up, Sledge will be once every three Saturdays and every other three Wednesdays. There's also an extra Inferno thrown in there, too! Plus, as always, plenty of house shows and this magazine following each Sledge. Once more, thank you everyone who's helped this fed grow to the charging horse that we are. You're all great, and I love you... in a non-gay sorta way, ya know! Really. Stop looking at me like that! :( -> [name withheld] -> Founder of Hammer Wrestling -> "Flower Duet! I'll put ANYTHING up here as long as it has something to do with Hammer. Whether it's true or not is up to you guys. Send to [detail withheld] and make sure to label it as being a rumor for S!HT. And hey... this stuff counts towards participation, eh! 03.16.01 TED BRUCE ATTACKED! -> On the last edition of Sledge, Ted Bruce was brutally attacked by a pair of unknown assailants. Ted Bruce, who'd spent most of the evening worrying over the missing referees, had been locked outside of the Arthur Ashe Center complex by Vinnie Carlucci and Mike Simms; the two had been trying t buy off Bruce all evening. The suspected culprits may have something to do with the mysterious "d" person that had promised Ted sponsorship earlier in the week. 03.16.01 WILL TRUK PAWNED OFF TITLES! 03.16.01 -> It's only a rumour, but it seems that Will Truk has sold off Hammer's titles. Ted Bruce unknowingly mentioned this to the Hammer Fans when locked outside, stating that Truk had pawned them off to "some woman." 03.16.01 REFEREES DELAYED ON SLEDGE, UNABLE TO ATTEND! -> On the last Sledge, the referees never showed up. This also includes Marc Merciless, who didn't arrive until mid-way through the event. It is still unknown what happened to the referees on Sledge. Foul play is suspected, possibly from Tim Mercury, Merciless' nemesis. 03.16.01 CARLUCCI AND SIMMS: MOBSTERS! -> It looks to be official that Vinnie "The Enforcer" Carlucci and his partner, Mike "The Muzzle" Simms are New York mobsters. After having bought off Dave Risk and Will Truk with their seed money, the pair tried to work over Ted Bruce. When Ted declined their offers (rather angrily) they locked him outside the building. It is possible that Carlucci and Simms are the ones responsible for the attack on Bruce. 03.16.01 METRO AND GIOVANNI: SEMI-FINALISTS! -> Metro and Anthony "The Preacher" Giovanni have moved up in the Hammer Championship Title tournament to become semi-finalists. Metro defeated Jay "LiveWire" Reil to move ahead and Giovanni beat Jake Bishop. 03.16.01 HOOK WATKINS INJURED! -> Hammer's homeless wrestler, Hook Watkins, had been severly beaten and injured by The Underground's Damian Riot and Nathan Rancid. Watkins was originally supposed to deliver a box of chocolates to ring announcer Sherry Michelle in order to receive $1.50 payment from Rancid. Instead of giving it to Michelle, Watkins ate the candy himself, angering Rancid and Riot. ************** ** RANKINGS ** ************** Here are the current champs and top contenders for Hammer Wrestling: CURRENT HAMMER CHAMPION Vacant -> #1) Metro -> #2) Vinnie "The Enforcer" Carlucci -> #3) Anthony "The Preacher" Giovanni CURRENT HAMMER TAG TEAM CHAMPIONS Vacant and Vacant -> #1) to be determined -> #2) to be determined -> #3) to be determined TOP THREE FACE WRESTLERS -> #1) Jake Bishop -> #2) Anthony "The Preacher" Giovanni -> #3) Phreek TOP THREE HEEL WRESTLERS -> #1) Vinnie "The Enforcer" Carlucci -> #2) Metro -> #3) "Cooler Than You" Mark Marshall * Note: contenders will not always be the person with the highest points. JAKE BISHOP (02.26.01) Neutral Face Brawling Powerhouse (Singles) A former "World's Strongest Man" competitor and finalist, Jake Bishop is, without a doubt, the strongest man in Hammer Wrestling. He's been recently letting his problems with METRO, BOB, and TIM MERCURY get to him, increasingly becoming more and more violent. His sloppiness and anger have led to recent losses as well, including a loss to ANTHONY "THE PREACHER" GIOVANNI in the first round of the HAMMER CHAMPIONSHIP TITLE TOURNAMENT. -> Known allies: none -> Known enemies: Metro, Bob, and Tim Mercury -> Strengths: powerful and resilient -> Weaknesses: slow, sloppy, and tempermental BOB (02.27.01) Chaotic Heel Non-Wrestler (Jobber) METRO'S irritating sidekick, the notorius Bob, is one hated individual in Hammer Wrestling! Bob is the founder of his own self-indulgent following appropriately titled, "Bobamania." Recently, Bob's been stirring up trouble with his partner, METRO, by claiming JAKE BISHOP molested him in the showers. -> Known allies: Metro -> Known enemies: Jake Bishop -> Strengths: aggravating, his big mouth -> Weaknesses: being inside a wrestling ring VINNIE "THE ENFORCER" CARLUCCI (02.27.00) True Neutral Non-wrestling Technician (Singles) Vinnie Carlucci is the most controversial wrestler in Hammer Wrestling, as well as the highest ranked, and most hated. He and his manager, MIKE "THE MUZZLE" SIMMS came to Hammer Wrestling for unknown reasons. However, it has now been proved that he is an active member of the mafia. Recently, Carlucci and Simms have been trying to get TED BRUCE, Hammer's head booker, to take money from himself and Simms, but have been met with much resistance. WILL TRUK and DAVE RISK, the other 2/3s of Hammer's owners, have accepted Carlucci's money. -> Known allies: Mike Simms, Dave Risk, and Will Truk -> Known enemies: Ted Bruce and Anthony Giovanni -> Strengths: money, pull over Risk/Truk -> Weaknesses: not very strong ANTHONY "THE PREACHER" GIOVANNI (02.26.01) Chaotic Face Cheating Technician (Singles) This devout Catholic priest is one of the loudest wrestlers in Hammer. Being of good faith and having much respect for his religion, Giovanni immediately made waves in Hammer Wrestling by pointing out that DAVE RISK and WILL TRUK were conversing with a known criminal: VINNIE "THE ENFORCER" CARLUCCI. Giovanni also made big news when he won his opening match in the HAMMER CHAMPIONSHIP TITLE TOURNAMENT by submitting JAKE BISHOP. -> Known allies: the Catholic Church and God -> Known enemies: Dave Risk, Will Truk, Vinnie Carlucci, and Mike Simms -> Strengths: excellent mat technician and focused -> Weaknesses: religious devotion and brawling "BAD BOY" BOBBY KAMAKAZEE (03.04.01) Chaotic Face High-Flying All-Rounder (Singles) This focused newcomer has displayed quite a bit of talent since his debut in Hammer. Although he hasn't been too overly involved, he recently lost his match against "COOLER THAN YOU" MARK MARSHALL when TIM MERCURY ran in and attacked MARSHALL, giving Kamakazee the disqualification. -> Attitude/Gimmick: cocky, focused, and intimidating -> Known allies: none -> Known enemies: Tim Mercury -> Strengths: knowledgable fighter -> Weaknesses: not the best brawler or cheater "COOLER THAN YOU" MARK MARSHALL (03.01.01) Orderly Heel Cheating High-Flyer (Singles) Cocky as hell and more in love with himself than anything, Mark Marshall's been getting under the fans' skins by coming out, making a mockery of SHERRY MICHELLE, and announcing himself each time. -> Attitude/Gimmick: cocky, narcissitic, and cool -> Known allies: the man in the mirror -> Known enemies: none -> Strengths: very cunning -> Weaknesses: fairly weak, and has a narcissisism problem TIM MERCURY (03.04.01) True Neutral All-Rounder Powerhouse (Singles) What can be said about Tim Mercury? Later, Mercury attacked "COOLER THAN YOU" MARK MARSHALL, costing "BAD BOY" BOBBY KAMAKAZEE his match. Also, MARC MERCILESS, Hammer's head ref, has shown his blatant dislike for Mercury, chasing him out of the ring on many occassions. -> Attitude/Gimmick: mysterious, intimidiating, and focused -> Known allies: none -> Known enemies: Marc Merciless, Jake Bishop, Bobby Kamakazee, and Mark Marshall -> Strengths: powerful and resilient -> Weaknesses: rather slow witted and sporatic METRO (02.26.01) Neutral Heel Cheating Brawler (Singles) Another one of Hammer's hated individuals, Metro, allied with his lackey, BOB, has been a serious thorn in Hammer's side since day one. For the past couple of weeks, Metro has been feuding with JAKE BISHOP, steadily pushing BISHOP closer and closer to the edge. Just recently, Metro moved ahead in the HAMMER CHAMPIONSHIP TITLE TOURNAMENT, surpassing JAY "LIVEWIRE" REIL to proceed on to the semi-finals. -> Known allies: Bob -> Known enemies: Jake Bishop -> Strengths: very cunning and is a gifted brawler -> Weaknesses: having Bob as a partner and is a little weak "THE GOLDEN CHILD" JOHNNY MIDAS (02.22.01) Chaotic Heel Submission Technician (Singles) The FIRST official signing to Hammer Wrestler was by "The Golden Child" Johnny Midas, the son of a billionaire. Midas has not done much in Hammer and at one point was thought to have been released (however, he turned around and remained a member). Almost a week ago, Midas injured his leg at a house show when fighting ANTHONY "THE PREACHER" GIOVANNI. -> Known allies: none -> Known enemies: none -> Strengths: amazing technical wrestler and is extremely quick, and has his father's fortune -> Weaknesses: brawling, cunning, and power EWAN MILTON (03.15.01) Neutral Face Brawling Powerhouse (Singles) The most recent addition to Hammer's official roster, Ewan Milton is a big man from Glascow Scotland. He's been working house shows lately. -> Attitude/Gimmick: honest, intimidating, and focused -> Known allies: none -> Known enemies: none -> Strengths: power and brawling -> Weaknesses: rather slow and is very slow witted PHREEK (02.27.01) True Neutral Technical Shoot Fighter (Singles) Phreek is Hammer's resident circus sideshow runaway. He has green skin and black eyes and is known for catching a bad rap for his appearances. -> Known allies: none -> Known enemies: the cruel world that judges him -> Strengths: technical moves and suplexes -> Weaknesses: not overly bright and is often made fun of or feared for his appearance JAY "LIVEWIRE" REIL (02.26.01) Neutral Face Technical High-Flyer (Singles) Jay Reil, "LiveWire", has been a somewhat quiet member of Hammer until just recently. Teaming up with a Japanese Sumo Wrestler by the name of MAWASHI, Reil has just started to search for his foothold in Hammer. He recently lost his spot in the HAMMER CHAMPIONSHIP TITLE TOURNAMENT to METRO when BOB, METRO'S lackey, helped his partner cheat to get the win over Reil. -> Attitude/Gimmick: gloryhog, stubborn, and intelligent -> Known allies: Mawashi -> Known enemies: none -> Strengths: "Ship! Ha-ha!", speed, stamina, and technical ability -> Weaknesses: extremely weak ADAM W. RICHARDS (03.04.01) Chaotic Face Power Martial Artist (Singles) Veteran wrestler Adam W. Richards joined Hammer Wrestling and two days later, forgot he was even a member. Richards has a bit of a senility problem, but has been impressing fans with his martial arts skills. -> Attitude/Gimmick: insane, friendly, and stupid -> Known allies: none -> Known enemies: none -> Strengths: martial arts moves, power, and technique -> Weaknesses: cunning, stamina, and forgets a whole lot of things. DAMIAN RIOT (03.07.01) Chaotic Heel Power Brawler (Singles) An English punk rocker, Damian Riot, sided with his former tag team partner, NATHAN RANCID, has been working vigorously to establish himself as a villain in Hammer Wrestling. His first major heelish tactic involved trying to give candy to ring announcer, SHERRY MICHELLE by having HOOK WATKINS deliver it. RANCID and RIOT then beat WATKINS severely after WATKINS ate the gift. The two, together, are known as "THE UNDERGROUND". -> Attitude/Gimmick: bully, rebellious, and dark -> Known allies: Nathan Rancid -> Known enemies: Sherry Michelle and Hook Watkins -> Strengths: brawling and power -> Weaknesses: doesn't talk, very slow, and sloppy HOOK WATKINS (03.08.01) Chaotic Face Extreme Cheater (Singles) Hook was literally picked up off the streets by TED BRUCE to fill in for another wrestler. Stinking his way around Hammer Wrestling, Hook is a foul, stingy homeless man who believes he participated in Viet Nam. Just recently, NATHAN RANCID had given Watkins some candies to give to SHERRY MICHELLE. Instead of delivering the candies, Watkins ate them; in return, he was beaten severly by RANCID and DAMIAN RIOT. -> Attitude/Gimmick: gross, comical, and hardcore -> Known allies: Sherry Michelle and Ted Bruce -> Known enemies: Nathan Rancid and Damian Riot -> Strengths: overpowering stench, brawling, and cunning -> Weaknesses: power, any form of grappling FED OVERALL AVERAGES: -> [Brawling]: average (5.9).
