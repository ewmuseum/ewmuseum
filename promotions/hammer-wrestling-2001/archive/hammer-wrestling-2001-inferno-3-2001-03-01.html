Non-taped House Show* BEARTOOTH PARK GYMNASIUM Henrico County, Virginia * This show is considered a "non-taped house show." In other words, all matches appearing on this card are in a summarized format. House Shows are merely rankings builders -- rewards to players who've participated and worked. The regular cards will have fully written out matches and count a lot more towards a players' record.
Additional Note: the "jobbers" on Inferno are often "borrowed" from other independent federations. Their purpose is to either help the match along for the new talent or just get practice for themselves.
************* ** RESULTS ** ************* Definitely not one of Hammer's best shows. The limited crowd seemed very, very restless. There were technical errors throughout most of the night, and at one point, the ring even collapsed! In all, there were approxiamately 300 fans in attendance, a third of which left
mid-way. METRO p. "THE MEANING OF LIFE" TAYLOR BALTIMORE (3:05) -> Appearing again in a heelish fashion, the odd and hormone- driven Metro entered the gymnasium playing up the crowd again and catching plenty of jeers. However, the fans _did_ find another swift slap from Ring Announceress, Sherry
Michelle, highly amusing. -> Metro did a whole lot of heelish showboatting in this event. Starting off by smacking Baltimore around some, he then threw his black/red ski-cap in the man's face.
After that, it was nothing more than Metro mocking the older, slower Baltimore. -> Baltimore was able to get in a few nice spots, but overall, Metro dominated the match using his stiff style of brawling and illegal tactics. Metro captured a victory after scoring a DDT on the outside mats, and then rolling Baltimore back inside for a Nailbomb (brainbuster) and a pin.
PHREEK p. BOB (14:21) -> Definitely the oddest match in indy fed history: Phreek, best described as a green troll, went head to head with Bob, a man who looks almost like he just hopped out of the crowd to do the match. Though amazed with Phreek's green skin and ghastly features at first, the attending crowd quickly lost interest in the unusual and neverending match-up.
-> Almost the entire match was spent with Bob running away and Phreek chasing him! And whenever the green wrestler would finally get his hands on Bob, he'd waste most of his time setting up a suplex... and Bob'd just low-blow him!
-> Finally, after 13 long, arduous, TERRIBLE minutes of these two unusual Hammer wrestlers doing battle, Bob finally got cold-clocked with a chairshot to the head by Phreek. Phreek then pulled Bob up into the ring and nailed a Double-Arm DDT followed by a pin for the victory.
JAKE BISHOP & JAY "LIVE WIRE" REIL* p. "THE SICKNESS" DALE BURNS* & "THE CONFLICT" TONY SLATTS (7:06) -> Probably considered the best match of the evening from the crowd response it got, Jake Bishop, definitely the largest and most powerful Hammer wrestler teamed with high-flyin
Jay Reil to take on Dale Burns and Tony Slatts. -> Bishop/Reil were dynamic as a pairing. Reil would start things off by keeping the men down with his hasty, dare- devil manuevers. He'd then tag in Bishop, the powerhouse, who'd make sure that Slatts and Burns _stayed_ down.
-> The match ended with Bishop dropping Slatts via a powerbomb and Reil coming off the top-rope with his guillotine leg-drop, The LiveWire, across the neck.
Reil made the pin for the victory. Reil and Bishop exitted together, congratulating each another over the victory.
**************** ** MAIN EVENT ** **************** VINNIE "THE ENFORCER" CARLUCCI p. "THE GOLDEN CHILD" JOHNNY MIDAS (9:02) -> The main event caught some interest, but by then, most of the viewers were tired. Especially after having to watch Bob and Phreek run around the ring for fifteen minutes. Carlucci and Midas did great, though, Carlucci taking on the role of the 'good guy' for this match. -> It was definitely a brawl for the mats with these two. Both men weilded a large list of grapples, suplexes, and holds, and for a good point, Midas was slippery. However, Carlucci's stamina was the winning factor here. The Italian used it to wear down Midas, then turn and use his wide-variety of skills.
-> At the end of the bout, Midas fell victim to Carlucci's Sleeping With the Fishies (Dragon Sleeper) submission. Midas accepted the loss, but still looked to be fuming over it. As Carlucci was exitting the arena area, Hammer owners
