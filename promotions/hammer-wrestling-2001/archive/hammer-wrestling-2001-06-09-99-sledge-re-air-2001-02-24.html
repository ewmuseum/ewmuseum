06.09.99 Sledge Show to Re-Air Soon

24 February 2001

********************************** ** 06.09.99 SHOW TO RE-AIR SOON ** ********************************** 02.24.01, Daily Rivers In preperation for their debut back into the world of wrestling, the Hammer
Wrestling Triumvirate (Ted Bruce, Dave Risk, and Will Truk) are planning to rerelease a taping of the infamous and controversial 06.09.99 Sledge showing on Tuesday, February 27th, 2001. Perhaps best known for the abrupt departure
of "Hard Times" Brian Pritchard and nearly half of the Hammer Wrestling roster, the 06.09.99 show also includes two title match-ups and appearances from current BNWF superstars Brad Garett (one-half of Brad and Chip, who then
were Hammer Tag Team champions), Grendel Gracie, Stan McWade, and of course,
Brian Pritchard.
"Not only do we hope to regain the interest in former fans of the company," notes Dave Risk, "but we also hope to intrigue new watchers. And what a better way to start things off?" The 06.09.99 Sledge showing pulled remarkable ratings when it originally debutted on eastern cable broadcast stations. It later trippled its ratings with network replays.
Critics speculate that with the redistribution of this popular Sledge showing the Hammer Wrestling Triumvirate will successfully relaunch their company and even pick up a few sponsors in the process. Hammer Wrestling currently continues to struggle, finding difficulty in picking up new talent and financial backing.
*********** ** NOTES ** *********** Goodness... a re-air of a show? Of a show that never really aired in the first place? But how can that be done? Well, taking the advice from a very friendly (and well known!) fedhead, to try and capture the interest of e-wrestlers, I will be putting together a "one man card." In other words, I'm
piecing together a completely original card conceptualized, written, and roleplayed by only one person: myself. I plan to release this "one man card"
by Tuesday. It should be somewhere between 100-125k with five fully written out matches, a few in-rings, backstages -- the works.
Now this is nuts. I know this. But I'm _hoping_ it can get some people interested and turn a few heads towards my fed. To sort of prove to you, the
fans and players that if I can do all this work just for the heckuvit, then
Hammer Wrestling might be worth it. Also, it'll give you a taste of what I have to offer beyond just lengthy cards. You'll be able to review my roleplaying style -- how I write matches, and how I formulate angles.
So stay tuned!
