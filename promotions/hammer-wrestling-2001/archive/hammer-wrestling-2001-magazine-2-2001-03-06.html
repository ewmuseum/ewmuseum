I am! Hammer's been around for nearly two weeks and the first Sledge is creeping up on us. Hopefully, everyone in the fed, from me to you to everyone else that's handling, will do their parts to make it the most super-duper card put out! And if it's not... well, I'm sure it'll be pretty damn good anyways! Hammer started out with just one wrestler and is now riding on the shoulders of twelve super guys. Remember: this ain't my fed; it ain't my story I'm telling. Everything that Hammer Wrestling achieves is all due to the handlers. I'm just the guy who pieces the story together. I may be the cheese on this pizza, but these guys are the pepperonis, onions, mushrooms, and anchoives -- and we're all on top of the same saucey crust! Okay, so I didn't understand that analogy either. But Hammer's still doing it's best to be the best. And it wants to thank everyone who helped it get here! So pat yourself on the back you handlers, you fans, and you people who dissed us! Wait... actually, don't. You people who dissed us can go f[bleep]k yourselves! -> [name withheld] -> Founder of Hammer Wrestling -> "I have a dope beastie tee, nipple rings, and new tattoos!" PS Quit kicking me out of the darn chatroom, guys! And you _know_ you are! *sniffles* ****************** ** RUMOUR SHEET ** ****************** Submit all news and rumors to me. I'll put ANYTHING up here as long as it has something to do with Hammer. Whether it's true or not is up to you guys. Send to [detail withheld] and make sure to label it as being a rumor for S!HT. And hey... this stuff counts towards participation, eh! METRO AND JAKE BISHOP AT EACH OTHER'S THROATS? -> At the past Infernos, Metro and Jake Bishop have really been invoking themselves as thorns in one another's sides. It all began at the taped 03.03.01 house show when Jake Bishop fought Metro; Bishop dominated most of the match, as he was clearly more powerful than his opponent. However, a chairshot from Metro's lackey, Bob, got Metro a victory and a cheap win. Later, Jake Bishop teamed up with the mysterious Tim Mercury to pull off a win over Bob and Metro at the 03.05.01 house show, enacting a little vengeance on the sleazy duo. Both men have big tournament matches coming up at the first Wednesday Sledge. Everyone is wondering: will the two compete against each other in the finals, or blow each other's matches in the first round? Or is this feud over? WHERE THE HECK DID THIS GUY COME FROM? -> There's a little bit of mystery happening in Hammer Wrestling! At the past 03.05.01 house show, a large man going by the name of Tim Mercury paired up with Jake Bishop to take on the team of Bob and Metro. He assisted Bishop in manhandling the pair, then scoring a win by pinning Bob. But afterwards, he made an untimely departure without a single word. Why did he team with Bishop? Where did he come from? What's Tim Mercury's deal? CRIME FAMILY IN HAMMER WRESTLING? -> There's some big rumours going around backstage over the real reasons why Vinnie "The Enforcer" Carlucci and Mike "The Muzzle" Simms are in Virginia wrestling for Hammer. Recently, Anthony "The Preacher" Giovanni accused Dave Risk and Will Truk of fraternizing with members who are, as he says "obviously of a crime family." Coincidently, Simms, Carlucci, Risk, and Truk have all been discussing hushed business matters together. But about what? Could Carlucci be a member of the mafia? Or has The Preacher's toothpaste finally fallen off his brush? TED BRUCE FOUND A SPONSOR FOR HAMMER? -> Word around the campfire has it that Ted Bruce has nailed a sponsor for Hammer Wrestling. Apparently, this past Tuesday (03.06.01) he recieved a square card with no return address. On one side, the card read: "Dear Hammer Wrestling, As I understand it, you've been having some financial trouble. My resources are at your disposal. Just meet me on Wednesday in the parking lot of the Arthur Ashe Center, just before your show begins." On the backside of the card, there was a single letter: a lowercase "d" written formally in gold ink. Will Ted accept the mystery man's money? Who is "d"? And can Ted even trust someone who seemingly came out of nowhere? WHAT'S THE DEAL WITH THE TITLES? -> On Monday, Dave Risk went around searching for the Hammer Championship Titles. Will Truk, the man in charge of the championship belts, spent a great deal of time avoiding Risk's inquiries. So where are the titles? Are they still around? And why was Truk ignoring Risk when questioned? ************** ** RANKINGS ** ************** Here are the current champs and top contenders for Hammer Wrestling: CURRENT HAMMER CHAMPION Vacant -> #1) Vinnie "The Enforcer" Carlucci -> #2) Jake Bishop -> #3) Metro CURRENT HAMMER TAG TEAM CHAMPIONS Vacant and Vacant -> #1) to be determined -> #2) to be determined -> #3) to be determined * Note: contenders will not always be the person with the highest points. Please subject your stuff as follows: FLASHES: [Hammer] Flash for *Show* - Wrestler's Name MM.DD.YY -> On the Show part, put either "Sledge" or "Inferno". It is rare, though, that I will accept flashes for Sledge. The only type of stuff that'll be usable will be angle-type flash stuff which would work best on Sledge. But please speak to me first before going ahead with those types of flashes. STRATEGIES: [Hammer] Strategy for *Match/Match* - Wrestler's Name MM.DD.YY -> On Match/Match, put the last names of both wrestlers. For example, if it's Carlucci vs. Bob, put Carlucci/Bob. If it's a Royal Rumble or something, just put "Royal Rumble". This can also be used for run-ins. IN-RINGS: [Hammer] In-Ring for *Show* - Wrestler's Name MM.DD.YY -> Under Show, put either "Sledge" or "Inferno". I'll pick and choose which ones get placed on Sledge. More than likely, the vaguer an in-ring is, the more likely it'll go on Inferno. I suggest to everyone to write their roleplays on notepad, save the file, and then send it to me as an attachment. This makes things MUCH easier on me.
