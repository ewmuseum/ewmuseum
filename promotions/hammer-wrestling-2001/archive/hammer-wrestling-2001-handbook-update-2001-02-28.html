->[name withheld] ->Founder of Hammer Wrestling ************************************* ** HOW DID HAMMER WRESTLING START? ** ************************************* ->IN CHARACTER EXPLANATION ->Billionaire Wilson Brady Hammer, Jr., young owner of the multibillion Hammer Chemical corporation, had far too much money. In fact, he wasn't even sure what to do with most of it! So when former wrestlers Dave Risk, Ted Bruce, and Will Truk approached Hammer with the idea of beginning an independent wrestling federation based out of Richmond, Virginia, the corporate tycoon jumped on it immediately! Attaching Hammer's name to it, the Bruce/Risk/Truk operated wrestling federation, Hammer Wrestling, became an immediate success in the early 1990s, supplying east coast viewers with a staggering list of superstars, athletes, and on-air personalities.
->In 1996, Hammer Wrestling began to expand northward. By 1998, Hammer Wrestling had become a fierce competitor in the sports entertainment business, dominating a great deal of the eastern independent groups. The Bruce/Risk/Truk trio had found their niche, and were raking in the bucks.
->Unfortunately, after some contract disputes in 1999, many of the larger Hammer stars branched away from Hammer Wrestling in pursuit of more income and fame. When the new millenium began, over 2/3 the original Hammer Wrestling roster had deserted Bruce/Risk/Truk and the company. Without a sizable cast of characters to put on air, Hammer
Wrestling had no other option but to shut its doors. In fear of the company crumbling and falling into bankruptcy, Wilson Hammer Jr. left the project in 2000, selling it cheap back to Bruce, Risk, and Truk.
->Presently, the three are struggling to find support and sponsors while slowly rebuilding the organization. The trio hopes that with the coming of the new year, 2001, they'll be able to sign plenty of new talent, returning Hammer Wrestling to what it once was.
->OUT OF CHARACTER EXPLANATION ->Hammer Wrestling began out of [name withheld]'s boredom, actually. Wanting to do something more with his seemingly endless amount of spare time, he started up a five-match-a-week electronic mail federation. Realizing the difficulty in starting up an online writing organization, Dave felt a little wary at first. But finally, he decided that the experience would be good for him, allowing him to openly express his creativity and work with others.
->Storywise, Hammer Wrestling is currently in a state of transition. A major part of the storyline revolves around the angle of the three presidents rebuilding the organization and searching for not only wrestlers, but a sponsor to get them some capital. The included twist is the fact that in these struggling times, the three presidents have soured, growing an animosity towards one another.
->With the induction of a starting roster, there will more than likely (and yes, I know it is rather cliche) be a tournament for the vacant Hammer Title. However, the real goal here in Hammer Wrestling is not the imaginary titles and privelages one can achieve. Instead,
Hammer Wrestling will focus on having a good time, and enjoying one's self while they handle their character. Afterall, there is no one who continues doing this because they hate doing it. E-wrestling is a past time, nothing more and nothing less.
******************************************* ** HOW DO I SUCCEED IN HAMMER WRESTLING? ** ******************************************* Hammer Wrestling is an closed, booked flash fed. In other words, there is the possiblity that anyone can win, just as long as they do their part. In Hammer Wrestling, how well and successful an e- wrestler can become is dependant on five major factors:
->FLASHING ->40% ->ANGLES ->40% ->STRATEGIES ->10% ->PARTICIPATION -> 5% ->APPLICATION STATS -> 5% ->FLASHING ->The first major factor in an closed-booked flash fed is, obviously, flashes. Flashes are the on-air comments a wrestler, in character, expresses about what is currently taking place in the federation. Things such as current angles, champs, feuds, alliances, upcoming matches, or just gimmick flashes are all topics of discussion in a successful flash. Flashes are typically sent out in a 'flash packet' format each week before the main card. Forty percent of a wrestler's success is weighed by the quality (and NOT the quantity) of their flashes. If a wrestler flashes on a regular basis, writing gripping and interesting stories or speeches for their character, then he will no doubt get by with ease in Hammer Wrestling.
->ANGLES ->Angles are just as useful as flashes, counting for another forty percent of the handler's success. An angle is the current storyline of a wrestler. Angles include feuds, alliances, on-card events, and other 'soap opera' style happenings in wrestling. In theory, a
Hammer Wrestler can get by just on his angle alone. In fact, a wise, well thought-out angle will sometimes be all the player needs to succeed. These are HIGHLY encouraged by the Hammer Wrestling booking staff. Wrestlers which regularally partake in angles will rise much faster than those that do not.
->STRATEGIES ->A smaller part of a wrestler's success is determined by his match strategies. Match strategies are plans for moves, spots, combos, entrances, and endings. Run-ins on other match-ups are also included as a part of strategy. Wrestlers use these to shape the way their wrestler thinks during a match-up. Now, of course, the wrestler's won't ALWAYS do as they have planned. An inneffective way to write a strategy would be to blatantly put down, "I want to win." The vagueness of that comment or anything similar to it will definitely not work in Hammer Wrestling. Ten percent of a wrestler's success is measured by his strategies.
->PARTICIPATION ->It isn't required, but participation does hold some weight in Hammer Wrestling. This, of course, doesn't mean to start kissing ass. But when it comes time to determine matches, the people that are active in working with other people, helping out the fedhead, or just generally friendly will have an edge over the antisocial, unhelpful, fed-burdens.
->APPLICATION STATS ->The stats on the application are a sort of "if all else fails" option. These also give other wrestlers a slight idea on how to strat against their opponents. Naturally, a powerhouse-style wrestler with 10 Power Points is almost always going to strat to use his power; he'd easily dominate everyone else, and even do well against those with equal ability. If strategies, flashes, and angles can't be measured in a match-up (though, this will hopefully be very, very rare) it'll go down to the base stats to determine who wins.
*********************************************** ** WHAT SHOWS ARE THERE IN HAMMER WRESTLING? ** *********************************************** There are currently only two weekly shows in Hammer Wrestling. ->WEDNESDAY NIGHT SLEDGE ->This is the big show each week. Wednesday Sledge has nothing but the best matches, best bouts, best feuds, and best angles. Each showing is an hour and a half long, having five matches (or more, depending on available writers) and is chock full of in-rings, backstage interviews, and other events. It is also live, so anything can happen. The undercard ranges from 10-15k with a 20-25k main event. Wednesday Sledge's color commentation team includes Ted Bruce and Hanson Beckard.
->MONDAY NIGHT INFERNO ->Inferno, Hammer's prerecorded show, holds all the regular, non in-ring style flashes. But instead of just being a regular ol' flash packet, it also hosts 2-4 summarized, jobber/squash matches. There'll sometimes be a main event, hosting two stars (player ran), but generally, this card will be a rankings builder.
->UPDATE ->The update is, well... an update. It hosts news, rankings, current rosters, card line-ups, plus, whatever else the booking committee feels like throwing on there. These'll generally come out
2-3 times a week. It is suggested that these are saved and thoroughly reviewed. ******************************** ** WHO RUNS HAMMER WRESTLING? ** ******************************** Hammer Wrestling is currently ran and operated by one individual, [name withheld]. Being a part of the Hammer Wrestling triumvirate, Ted owns 1/3 of the company. Thus, he has a substantial pull in the way things works. In a basic system of checks and balances with the other two, Ted is allowed booking privelages.
However, he has no say in contract negotiations (Dave Risk's job), and can not book direct title matches (Will Truk's job). Ted Bruce is an egotistical moron, and tends to have a larger mouth than his ass can afford. Even though Ted is retired from wrestling, he is still in capable health and very able to wrestle when a situation calls for it. Bruce is also one half the Wednesday Day Sledge commentation team. His heelish commentation style is a bit crude, and definitely plays off the other commentator, Hanson Beckard.

Maynard may not know every move in the book, but his excitable profile and love for the sport gets the viewers rivveted.
He roots mostly for the heels of Hammer Wrestling, but has been known to mark for astounding moves and spots performed by faces.

Any that dare argue suffer his wrath.

Bruce's or Risk's. ->* Usable by any players (within reason). ->** Usable by match-writers only. ************************************************ ** WHAT TITLES CAN I WIN IN HAMMER WRESTLING? ** ************************************************ Right now, there are but two titles in Hammer Wrestling: ->HAMMER CHAMPIONSHIP TITLE ->The Hammer Title is THE penultimate title in Hammer Wrestling. Every man wants it, yet only one man can have it. As of now, this is the ONLY singles title in existance and the booking committee plans to keep it that way for a very, very long time. Many will argue that this will make succeeding in Hammer Wrestling difficult. However, this is far from the truth. One singles title DOES make it difficult to obtain, but also makes it a much more prestigious title to hold.
->TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP TITLES ->The second most important achievement in Hammer Wrestling is the tag team championship and the winning of the tag team titles. Tag teams are formed stricly through alliances and angle relationships;
there will be no tag teams directly applied for. Thus, handling the tag team championship titles can become a bit more difficult.
Players are forced to become symbiotic with each other in order to work in harmony. A tag team which doesn't work together, doesn't win together.
**************************************************** ** ARE THERE ANY OTHER RULES IN HAMMER WRESTLING? ** **************************************************** Hammer Wrestling has a simple, easy-to-read "WILL NOT TOLERATE" list. This list is as follows: -> Burdening egos, extremely cocky behaviour, excessive bragging, or just being a jerk to other handlers in any way, shape, or form. Hammer Wrestling doesn't care where a handler has been, what his status is on so-and-so rankings charts, or just how many titles he may have held in such-and-such e-fed. This is a game. If you want to brag about your successes, go to Vegas.
-> Handlers that do nothing more than take up roster space. In a perfect world, Hammer Wrestling would like to have at least one roleplay a week from handlers. However, only one every three weeks is required. After three weeks of idling, the handler will be ejected, no questions asked.
-> Handlers which argue match outcomes and decisions made by the booking committee. All decisions are final. Don't like it? Well, there's the door... -> Handlers that only want to win titles. This fed is for fun, and not for imaginary hunks of metal. -> Usage of any wrestler other than one's own, or any Hammer Wrestling character which has been previously instructed for non-player usage. Currently, only Ken Kritter is usable for interviews by players.
-> Breaking kayfabe. In Hammer Wrestling, all a wrestler should know is that he is in Hammer Wrestling... and nothing more. No WCW, WWF, ECW references. Plus, no mentioning past e-feds, past e-titles, or past e-wrestlers which have never been an active part of Hammer Wrestling.
-> Kissing the ass of the booking commitee. It's not a firing offense, and it can be done as much as one pleases; but, it's definitely not going to get them anywhere. But hey, the booking commitee doesn't discourage it!
