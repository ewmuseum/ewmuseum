IEA Roleplays, week of 27 February 2000

2000-02-27
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Thor: *Thor is at a small pub sitting with some old friends* Thor- Did you see what I did to that Belgian bastard Gnome, Larry? Larry- Yea..boy did you crush him. Hell, now he's injured ! Thor- Yea..I just wish I could have done the same thing to that cheap skank SledgeHammer. *The bartender passes another drink down to Thor and gets in on the conversation* Bartender- Nick Sloan is such a phony. He has never even used a whell barrow but he tells everyone that he's a construction worker. * Larry Slams his glass down in anger* Larry- I just wish I could have taken him on!! Thor- Hell, you have beaten him..IF..it was one-on-one. Not the triple threat bullsh!t that I was invovlved in. Larry- I'll tell ya one thing... I was talking to him on time and..WHOA BOY..did he ever smell. I mean serisouly, he can't be THAT poor. Bartender- Ya know why you never see that asshole in bars? Larry&Thor in unison- Why? Bartender- He can't afford a brew!! *the conversation goes on like this for hours* **************************************************************** Southwest Strangla: Jill and Frank get out of there van this time not at the abandoned warehouse but at a hotel. Jill: What the heck...one week he's slumming it out in a warehouse this week he's in a posh hotel. What gives? Frank: Well maybe buisennes is good. (laughs to himself) Jill: Thats not funny!!!! Frank: Sorry I thought it was. Jill: Well it's not. ***Jill walks in and checks the front desk for the room number*** desk clerk: Room 420. Jill: Thanx ***Jill and Frank make there way up the stairs*** Jill: Here it is room 420. ***Jill knocks on the door*** Strngla: Come on in it's open Jill. Jill: How'd you know it was me? Strangla: Well my room does face the front of teh hotel and your van does say IEW on it. Jill: Ow, ok. So what did you call us here for this time? Strangla: I'm pissed off. That biotch Skater hasn't answeared any of my challengs. Has he pussed out? Jill: I don't know what his state is but i'm sure he wouldn't like being called a biotch. Strangla: Well until he answears my challenges he is a biotch. Also, I think he just pussed out over the fact he doesn't have the balls to have a hardcore match. Jill: Well i know Skater and he's not a pussy. But i don't think he'll like the idea of a hardcore match. Strangla: Before i forget how's your leg I didn't mean to have that cup of coffee spill on you. Jill: It's ok. But, lets wrap it up whats your last words before we go? Strangla: Like i said before...Skater you'll either TAP OUT OR DIE WITH GRACE BIOTCH!!!!!!!!! Jill: Thank You and good day. Strangla: Bu-bye. ***Jill leaves the room*** Jill: What teh hell was with him? He wasn't his usally grummpy, psycotic self! Frank: Yeah, I know that was weird! Frank and Jill get in their van and leave. ********************************************************************* Inka Tupac: A camera crew enters Inka's house in Lima, Peru. Inka is sitting in front of his computer net surfing He opens the IEA website His face changes to angry Inka: What the f..., I cannot believe it I am not getting a title shot, neither for the International nor NAC, what's going on here I am the truly number one contender for both titles and the president doesn't even put me on afight what does he thinks that I am going to hold still while other undiserving wrestler gets the shot, this will not stay like this, I'll do something don't know yet but I sure do som... Inka turns and looks at the camera Inka: What do you think you're doing get out of here Inka stands up and charges against the cameraman , who get out running, Inka slams the door as soon as the guy is out of the house. ********************************************************** Cactus Funk: *The camera shows Funk's apartment building from the outside like usual, but instead of seeing his door being knocked on, you see Alec and Jorge walking down a dark staircase. With the little light all you can see is graffiti on the walls and an occasional rat run across the floor making both men jump. At the very end of this dark hallway you see a figure sitting on the dirty concrete floor wearing only a pair of ripped up jeans. Since Alec and Jorge were to meet Funk somewhere in that area they knew right away who it was. The camera turns on its own light to Funk's face, but there was something different about Cactus this time. He looks more evil, more insane, more dangerous. Alec and Jorge are odviously terrified, although neither one would ever admit it. The interveiw begins. Alec: Cactus, are you okay? You like, well, different. Cactus Funk: Of course I'm not okay. Have I ever truly been okay? A: Well, I assume so. CF: That question requires no assumption. It requires the truth, do you know the truth, Alec? A: I guess so. CF: YOU GUESS SO???!!! WHAT THE HELL IS YOUR PROBLEM DON'T YOU GET IT??? I'M NOT OKAY, I'VE NEVER BEEN OKAY, AND THERES A DAMN GOOD CHANCE THAT I'LL NEVER BE OKAY IN MY LIFE!!! A: Alright, I'm sorry I asked can on something different than how "okay" you are? *Funk gives Alec the most evil look straight into his eyes, Alec is so afraid he can barly move. A: Ummm, on with the interveiw I guess. Well, Cactus, how do feel about the International Title match you were in about a week ago? CF: I thought it was the worst match of career, and the outcome could'nt be worse. How could some little bitch in his debut match beat me?!?!?! Especially with a title on the line. And then I don't even get to wrestle at the Saturday Night Suplexe Showcase two weeks after that??? You know what that does, Alec? A: Ummmm... CF: WRONG. That sucks!!! There is no better word I can use to explain this strange phenomenon except for that it sucks!!! A: Can you tell me a little about your tag team? CF: Yes, I could. A: Will you? CF: Sure, Sarkkan and I are the Hardcore Icons. Our finisher is called Mercyful Fate. When there is a chair laying on the floor, either Sarkkan or I do a Spinebuster to our opponent on the chair, then on of us do an Arabian Facebuster to the opponent sandwiching the victim. We will conquer the tag team division and destroy anyone who gets in our path. Now get the hell out of here before I have to do one man Mercyful Fate on both of you. A: But, we have more questions. CF: Alright, get me a chair. A: Run, Jorge, Run!!!! *Jorge and Alec run like hell out of the building thinking that Cactus was right behind them. ******************************************************** Rizzo: *Rizzo has called a press conference. All of the IEA reporters, reporters from major newspapers as well as other federations are there. Rizzo makes his way to the podium* Greg Helens: Rizzo, why did you call us here? Rizzo: Well. I have some sad news. I am announcing my retirement from the IEA. *A collective gasp is heard from the crowd.* Alec Meyers: Why Bro? Rizzo; Well, I don't have the time anymore. You see, I'm under a contract with another federation, the CWF. I also know that my future is brighter there. I have thought long and hard about this. I don't want to leave my friends and even my brother but I must. I have too much to worry about. Jill Franklin; What about Wrecking Crew? You're just leaving them in the dark Rizzo: They already know about my decision. I feel bad, but it's just something that must be done. I just hope someone in the IEA has the balls to step up to the plate and take my place. I have an inside source and I know about the return of one of our greatest stars. I hope he takes my place. He'd fit nicely. I just hope that Wrecking Crew doesn't die out. I see a big future for them. Greg: One last question. Will you be back? Rizzo: No. I can't come back here. I love it here, but I just don't think the IEA is for me. I will miss Deamon, Saint, Daredevil, Pummy...er Sledgehammer, and JayRok. I wish them all the best of luck. I hope they make the IEA the best fed there is....other than the one I'm in. I'm sorry I have to cut this short, but I must catch a flight. Thank you all for coming, and thank you for letting me in *Rizzo leaves as the press corps discuss the events that just unfolded.*
