International E-wrestling Alliance: Wednesday Night House Show

18 July 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Everything can be found at http://www.ieawrestling.com Wednesday Night House Show* Liverpool, England July 18th, 2001 Match 1: Deth vs Apocoypes After Deth Squad's actions last week President Flanders felt it necessary for two of the men to settle their differences, however this match may have caused an even bigger rift between Chaos Inc & DS. The match started off with Deth getting the upperhand with some power moves but
Apocalypes turned the tables when Deth went for a high risk maneuver that backfired. From there Apocalypes took it to Deth with a series of suplexes capped of by a big legdrop. However Deth was only down for a 2 count. Apocaypes looked to put Deth away with a top rope powerbomb but Deth escaped the move.
Deth then put the hurt on Apoc's lower back with a big tilt-a-whirl backbreaker and a surfboard submission. However Chaos stuck his nose in the match which prompted Razor to grab him & start a brawl. Apoc then got the advantag on a distratced Deth. Apocalypes then sent Deth and the match to the outside where the ref double counted out both men.
Winner: None Match 2 - IEA World Title Tourney Match: Lucas " The Show" Steele vs. Viking
Viking came into this match a champion but one man stood in his way to gain another title, Lucas Steele. Both men let each other have it early.
However after some exchanged punches Steel got Viking right where he wanted him.. on his back. However Steel couldn't keep teh big Norwegian down for long. Vike finally came back and erupted on Steele. Vike took Steel outside, around & back inside the ring. Once they got back in the ring things took a turn for the worse for Viking. Steele wore Vike out with a couple of neck-concentrated submissions. After a small final burst of offense from the last ever TCT champ Steele ended the match with his top rope bulldog finisher called "The Jackpot" and after a three count Steele advances in the
Tournament.
Winner: Lucas "The Show" Steele Match 3 - IEA World Title Tourney Match: Mystic vs. Ken "Motley" Krew In the second of four tournament matches two stars from the old IEA Ken
Krew & Mystic went at it in a very heated battle. Mystic tarted off very strong showing why he was one of the best NAC holders from the old days.
However Krew reverted to some dirty tactics and he gained the upperhand with which he punished Mystic. Krew put the hurt on mystic with a big piledriver, then a DDT then another piledriver... but Mystic somehow kicked out. Krew's ignorance and arguing with the ref almost cost him the match as Mystic got him in a rollup but only for a 2 count.
After both men got back up Mystic used his speed to his advatage. Mystic used a variety of high risk maneuvers to score only a 2 count. After a final frog splash backfired Krew took the advantage and he never lost it. Krew finally ended it with the Motley Plunge for the 3 count.
Winner: Ken "Motley" Krew Match 4 - IEA World Title Tourney Match: Beyond vs. Warpath In one corner the man named Warpath sought to keep an undefeated streak going and in the other corner Beyodn was out to prove to everyone that he can win a title. In the match... it showed that both men had alot to prove.
Beyodn started off on top after nailing Warpath with flurry of standing dropkicks and clothesline. Beyond then wasted no time going airborne dropping Warpath with a huge top rope hurricanrana which only produced a 2 count.
Beyond brought Warpath back to his feet and it proved to be a big mistake as Warpath used his strength to beat the hell out of Beyond. Warpath slammed Beyond down with a spinebuster and then he caused even more damage with a short powerbomb, but it wasn't enough. Warpath then got furious and he made the mistake of arguing with the ref about a count. That's when
Beyond finally got back to his feet & regained the advantage. Before Beyond could put Warpath away Aurora got on the apron & started talkignto Beyond...
but then Aurora took Beyond & she nailed him with a throat-thingie on the top rope (you know what I mean). Warpath got the roll up from behind but only a 2 count again.
In the end though Beyond's experience paid off as he ended the match with a painful To the End and Back for the 3. Afterwards Beyond left the ring leaving Aurora to tend to her man. However, Ken Krew came out & he beat
Warpath up a little bit until Security came out & broke it up.
Winner: Beyond Match 5 - IEA World Title Tourney Match: Jason Black vs. Achilles In the last of the first round tournament matches Achilles & Jason Black faced off in a very close matchup. No one had the advatage early on as both men were hitting moves left and right, but Achille's strength proved to be too much in the early going for Black. Achilles wore Black down with a big powerslam followed up by a hanging vertical suplex. Black wouldn't quit & he kicked out at 2. Achilles then went for a big powerbomb which turned into a front face slam by Black. Black then took to the skies & he dropped Achilles like a bad habit with a beautiful tornado DDT. However it wouldn't be enough to keep Achilles down, and neither would Black's top rope elbow drop.
Frustrated Black went back to teh op but took too long letting Achilles roll out of the way of a senton bomb.
Achilles got back on top with a couple of knees to the face. Achilles then tried to go for a quick win with a 3/4 necbreaker but Black wouldn't stay down. Achilles then made Black suffer some more with a series of power moves including a sweet inverted brainbuster. It still wasn't enough to keep
Black down. Achilles then went for some ropes moves but Black came back strong. In the end Black's "Breakdown" proved to be too much for Achilles and he scored a surprise victory.
