Bastard tries to execute his own belly-to-belly Suplex. The two of them are locked up trying to Suplex each other. Saint give an almighty push and drives bastard into the ropes, and uses the momentum of Bastard bouncing off them to execute a overhead belly to belly Suplex. Saint waits for Bastard to get up and winds up for the KO, a vicious haymaker. Bastard gets up and sidesteps just as Saint swings. Bastard uses Saint's momentum to his advantage and knees him in the groin. Saint doubles over. Bastard bounces into the ropes, lets out the War Whoop and hits the Super Bastard Spear. Bastard then starts to climb the top rope. He executes the Psycho Crusher, a multiple summersault cannon ball. As bastard lands he quickly rolls over and does a front bridge with his forehead buried into the Saint's temple and pulling out Saint's arm. The referee starts the count. 1...2...3... BASTARD WINS! Bastard starts laughing and grabs the IEA World Heavyweight Title Belt off the referee and parades around the ring with it. He goes to the floor and starts posing for photos with his new belt. He starts making his way up to the dressing room with the belt round his waist and continually lets out the War Whoop! The paramedic teams run into the ring to check on Saint who hasn't moved since he got nailed with the Psycho Crusher.
