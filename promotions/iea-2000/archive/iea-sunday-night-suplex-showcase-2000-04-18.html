Sunday Night Suplex Showcase

posted 18 April 2000; billed 16 April 2000, Rome, Italy
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Here are the results for the April 16th card in Rome, Italy! Sunday Night Suplex Showcase Match 1 - Kid vs. Scheize *Scheize makes his way to the ring as the crowd yawns loudly. He gets in the ring and waits for The Kid* *The Kid makes his way down to the ring with The Warriors a few feet behind him. The Kid slaps hands with the fans as he passes them, and as he gets in the ring, a large sign comes down with pyros going off spelling "The Kid". The sign goes back up, and the match starts.**bell rings** Inka and Kalipa stand on the outside cheering The Kid on. Kid and Scheize lock up, and Scheize puts Kid in a side headlock. Kid pushes Scheize into the ropes, then knocks him down with a spin kick to the chest. Kid climbs the turnbuckle and lands an asai moonsault, then slaps on an armbar. Scheize slowly crawls to the bottom rope and grabs it, and Kid releases the hold. Kid climbs the turnbuckle again and waits for Scheize to get up, and knocks him back down with high cross body. They hit the mat, and Kid locks the leg, 1...2...weak kick out by Scheize. Scheize is slow to get up and is very groggy. Kid cinches him up, and plants him back down with a crucifix slam. Kid hooks the leg again, 1...2...again Scheize kicks out. Kid is extremely frustrated. He whips Scheize into the corner, and follows it up with a splash. Kid puts his foot up to Scheize's throat and starts choking, and the referee starts counting, 1...2...3...4...Kid puts his foot down, and lights up Scheize's chest with a few slaps. Kid sits Scheize up on the top turnbuckle, then climbs up as well, and plants Scheize on the mat with the top rope DDT, which The Kid calls Highlander. Kid hooks the leg again, 1...2...3**bell rings* Kid gets up and raises his arms in victory, and Gnome and Gado hit the ring! Gado kicks Kid in the gut, and plants him with a DDT. Here come the Warriors into the ring now, and they start brawling. Gnome tries to kick Kalipa, but Kalipa catches it and sweeps Gnome's other leg out from under him, then puts Gnome in a lion-tamer submission. Gado makes Kalipa release the hold with a closeline that nearly tears Kalipa's head off. Kid is up now, and he puts Gado in a cobra clutch. Inka starts kicking Gado in the gut. Meanwhile, Scheize and Gnome are brawling up the entrance ramp with Kalipa, and Kalipa is putting up a good fight. Kalipa hits Scheize with vertical suplex on the ramp, and Gnome drops an elbow into Kalipa's gut. Kid and Inca now get to the ramp and start kicking Gnome in the back of the head repeatedly. Scheize and Kalipa have now passed through the entrance, exchanging blows the whole way. Kid and Inka get to the top of the ramp, pick up Gnome, and plants him back down with a double vertical suplex. As they get up, Gado knocks them down with a double closeline, and Scheize crawls into the back. Kid gets up and chases him as Inka and Gado start trading punches. Inca blocks a punch, kicks Gado below the belt, and DDT's him. Inka runs into the back to help his teamates, and Gado gets up and runs after him through the entrance. Match 2 - Billy lovemuscle vs. Mexican PimpL *The Mexican Pimp Enters the ring with his Mexican Hoe's* *El Distorto De Melodica hits the speakers and we see Billy Lovemuscle make his way out from the back..ON A HORSE. The fans groan at his half-assed attempt to be attractive. He enters the ring* The Mexican Pimp is brought down by Billy with a kick to the groin. Billy gives a wish-bone to the Mexican Pimp's legs. The Mexican Pimp gets up and the two brutes lock up. The Mexican Pimp is whipped into the ropes and recives a spinning heel kick from Billy. Billy decides that he wants some Mexican Ho action and leaves the ring to greet the ladies. In anger the Mexican Pimp leaves the ring, too. A count starts 1..2. Billy is whipped into the barrier by the Mexican Pimp. 3... The Mexican Pimp tosses Billy LoveMuscle into the ring. The Mexican Pimp climbs into the ring and heads onto the turnbuckle. The Mexican Pimp attempts a Leg Drop but Billy avoids it. Billy gets up and picks Jefe up. Billy sets Jefe up on the top rope and takes him down with a superplex. Billy goes for the pin 1..2..3! Match 3 - Submission Match: Super Bastard vs. Bradley Culver *"Darkside" starts playing as the arena fades into darkness. There is an explosion of pyros, and blacklight bathes the arena. Super Bastard is wearing his reactive cape and singlet. He raises his arms, dropping the cape. As he heads to the ring, his headpiece swings from one of the straps. As he gets onto the ring apron, he puts the headpiece on. He jumps over the ring post, somersaulting as he lands. He stands up and starts bopping with the music, which starts to fade out.* *The lights stay off as the music, and sparks start shooting up around the entrance. A wall of flames shoot up in front of the entrance. They part in the middle and Brad Culver steps through to a huge crowdpop. He makes his way down to the ring eyeballing SB the whole time. He steps through the ropes, and the match gets under way.**bell rings** Bastard and Culver stalk each other, and tie up in the middle of the ring. Culver flips SB up, and plants him down with a Death Valley Driver. SB gets up quickly, and takes another DVD from Culver. As SB sits up, Culver again puts him back down with a spear. Bastard lies on the mat motionless, and Culver starts showboating. But Bastard was playing possum, and he quickly gets up and nails Culver with a DVD of his own, followed up with an inverted DVD. Culver is picked up by the hair, and Bastard hits a revers German suplex. SB climbs the ropes, and does a somersault cannonball. He quickly rolls over as he lands, and does a front bridge as his forehead pushes on Culvers face and Bastard pulls his arms. But the referee won't make the count, and reminds Bastard that it's a submission match. SB gets up quite pissed and gets in the referee's face. Bastard starts pushing the referee, and the referee shoves Bastard back. SB stumbles back right into Culver, who has gotten up off the mat, and Brad slaps on a cobra clutch. Bastard starts flailing his, grabs hold of Brad, and flips him over as he slams Culver face down onto the mat. Bastard picks Culver straight up for a vertical suplex, but instead hits a piledriver. SB picks him up again, and Culver hits him with a low blow. Culver tears Bastard's headpiece off and throws it into the crowd, and locks on a katihajime. They both fall to the mat, and Culver wraps up Bastard's legs. SB is struggling to get free, but Brad has the hold locked on tight. Bastard's face is starting to turn a deep purple, but he still doesn't tap out. He slowly stops moving and the referee moves over. He lits the arm, and it drops, 1. He lifts it again, and again it drops, 2. He lifts it one last time, and Bastard's arm falls limply to the mat, 3. **DING DING DING** Culver releases the hold, and the referee raises his arm in victory. As Brad hops down off the apron, the paramedics rush into the ring and put an oxygen mask over Bastard's nose and mouth. Bastard slowly comes to, and as the medics help him to the back, the crowd cheers and applauds. As Culver makes his way to the back he's jumped by Ray Wright and Pain of Destruction Inc. They execute a spike powerbomb on the concrete and leave him lying on the floor. The crowd boos Ray and Pain as they mae their way to the back. Match 4 - Harcore Rules Match: Cactus Funk vs. Sarkkhan *The arena goes black and "Superbeast" starts playing. Yellow lights flash randomly above the ring with the beat of the synthesizer at the start of the song. When the power chord strikes to start the guitar playing, there is an explosion of pyrotechnics on top of each post, with the flames burning until Sarkkhan enters the ring. Pain, Edgecrusher and Ray Wright are at ringside with Sarkkhan.* * "Been Caught Stealing" by Jane's Addiction is pumped through the speakers. Cactus Funk makes his way to a loud crowd cheer. He gets in the ring and starts trash talking.* Funk stands in the middle of the ring and looks around at Destruction Inc. Sarkkhan attack Funk while his back is turned toward the other 3 members. Sarkkhan picks Funk up and drops him with an inverted atomic drop. Sarkkhan levels Funk with a running clothesline. Sarkkhan stomps on Funk until he rolls out of the ring. The other three members of D.I. picks funk up and roll him back into the ring. Sarkkhan grabs Funk but Funk rakes his eyes. Funk drops Sarkkhan with a DDT. Funk drops a leg across the back of Sarkkhan's head. Funk rolls out of the ring and grabs a chair. Funk slides back into the ring with the chair. Funk nails Sarkkhan across the back with the chair. Funk picks Sarkkhan up and tries to piledrive him on the chair, but Sarkkhan is too strong. Sarkkhan reverses it into a back body drop. Pain grabs a cookie sheet from under the ring and throws it to Sarkkhan. Sarkkhan bends the sheet out of shape on Funk's head. Sarkkhan picks Funk up and throws him over the top rope onto the concrete. Sarkkhan follows closely. Sarkkhan lifts Funk up by his throat and throws him into the steel steps. Sarkkhan picks Funk back up but Funk kicks him in the groin. Funk drops Sarkkhan with a reverse neckbreaker. Funk pulls another chair from under the ring. The members of D.I. start advancing towards Funk. They surround him but Funk lays out each guy with a stiff chairshot. Funk uses the chair on Sarkkhan's head. Funk picks Sarkkhan back up and vertically suplexes him on the concrete. Funk covers Sarkkhan. 1....2... Sarkkhan gets an arm up. Funk lifts Sarkkhan back up and whips him into the guardrail. Sarkkhan hits it hard back first. Funk walks to Sarkkhan but EC trips him. Funk's face hits the concrete hard. Sarkkhan gets to his feet slowly and thanks EC. Sarkkhan grabs a table under the ring and sets it up on the outside. Sarkkhan rolls Funk into the ring. Sarkkhan slides under the bottom rope. Sarkkhan lifts Funk up and lands a couple of hard punches to the body. Sarkkhan takes Funk down with a facebuster. Funk writhes in pain while holding his face. Sarkkhan lifts Funk back up and sets him up on the top rope. Sarkkhan climbs the ropes and tries to lift Fun up. funk blocks it. Funk lifts Sarkkhan up and suplexes Sarkkhan onto the table on the outside. The table breaks with a loud crack and both men hit the concrete hard. Funk drapes his ram over Sarkkhan. The ref starts his count. 1.....2...thr Pain pulls Funk off of Sarkkhan. All three members of Destruction Inc start beating down Funk. Ray picks Funk up and whips him into EC who powerslams him on the concrete. The crowd suddenly bursts into cheers as JayRok and Banshee make their way to ringside. As they turn the corner Pain lays them out with a chair. Pain lands several more chairshots on them while they're down. Ray picks Funk back up and gives him the Wright Way on the concrete. The crowd turns back to cheering as Bradley Culver runs to ringside. however, a hard clothesline from EC stops him short. Pain and EC pick Brad up and double suplex him on the concrete. Ray lifts Funk back up and throws him into the ring. Ray helps Sarkkhan up and helps him back into the ring. In the ring Sarkkhan slowly gets to his feet. He grabs Funk and whips him hard into the corner. Sarkkhan kicks Funk several times in the stomach until he's sitting on the mat. Sarkkhan then uses the ropes as leverage as he chokes Funk with his foot. Sarkkhan lets go and picks Funk back up. Sarkkhan lifts Funk up and sets him up on the top rope. He tells Ray to set up a table on the outside, and Ray obliges. Sarkkhan lifts Funk up and executes the Dragon Fire through the table and onto the concrete. Sarkkhan covers. 1....2....3! Sarkkhan has defeated Cactus funk with help from destruction Inc. All 4 members of D.I. make their way to the back laughing while the members of Wrecking Crew, Culver, and Funk recover and get up slowly. Match 4 - Cage Match (with special guest ref) Daredevil vs. Gnome *The crowd begins to show their love (very loud boo's) as Gnome makes his way out, accompanied by the Giant and Thor. The French Napoleonic theme gets the crowd in even more of a stir, but the Gnome seems to enjoy it. He climbs into the ring as the Giant tries to climb over the railing to take a seat. Thor stays by ringside.* *Tear it Off" by Method man and Redman starts blasting through the speakers. Daredevil comes out to a large ovation from the crowd. He makes his way to the ring, and jumps up onto the apron. He gets in and waits for the ref.* *The lights go out. Suddenly, in a big bang, blue and green pyros shoot up from the ringpost and blue and green sparks shower down on the entranceway. "Mouth for War" by Pantera starts blasting. The crowd starts booing as Rizzo emerges from the sparks wearing the black and white striped ref’s shirt. Both men in the ring are shocked as Rizzo makes his way into the ring. He signals for the cage to be lowered, and when it hits the ground, he calls for the bell* DD and Gnome lunge at each other with fists a flyin’. DD gets the upperhand with an eyerake. DD swings at Gnome but Gnome blocks the punch. Gnome uses his overwhelming power to throw DD into a corner. Gnome grabs onto the second rope and nails a couple of shoulder blocks. Gnome lifts DD up and sits him up on the top rope. Gnome sets DD up for a superplex but DD blocks it. DD sends Gnome crashing to the ground with several rights to the face. DD stands up on the top rope and takes Gnome down with a missile dropkick. DD drops a knee on Gnome’s face while he’s down. DD picks Gnome up and whips him into the corner. DD follow with a running elbow smash. DD lands punch after punch in the corner. DD tries top whip Gnome into the opposite corner, but Gnome reverses it into a short-arm clothesline. Gnome picks DD up and executes a backbreaker. Gnome pins DD. 1....2... DD kicks out of the pin. Gnome picks DD up and gorilla presses him. Gnome rams DD face first into the steel cage. Gnome drops DD face first on the turnbuckle. Gnome picks DD up and whips him into the opposite corner. Gnome charges but DD steps out of the way. Gnome hits the corner hard face first. DD runs up the ropes an takes Gnome down with a top rope clothesline. DD climbs to the top rope and lands a flying splash on Gnome. DD covers Gnome. 1....2....Gnome kicks out with power. DD gets right back up and lays the boots on Gnome. DD picks Gnome up and scoop slams him. DD runs and springboards off the top rope for an asai moonsault. DD covers Gnome again. 1....2.... weak kick out by Gnome. DD tells Riz he thinks it was slow, but Riz stands by his decision. Gnome gets back to his feet while DD argues with Riz. When DD turns around Gnome levels him with a running clothesline. Gnome picks DD up and whips him into the ropes. Gnome catches DD and brings him down hard with a powerslam. Gnome covers, hooking DD’s leg.. 1....2.... DD kicks out in the nick of time. Gnome can’t believe it. Gnome picks DD up and executes a side suplex. Gnome starts to climb the cage. About half way up the cage DG gets to his feet. DD lunges at the cage and shakes it, throwing Gnome off of it. Gnome lands hard on his back in the ring. DD stars to climb the cage himself. Thor sees him and starts climbing the other side. Rizzo begins to climb up the cage too. All three men reach the top. Thor swings at DD but Riz blocks it,. Riz lands a right hook on Thor, knocking him off had first onto the concrete. Riz drops off the cage into the ring. DD stands up on the top of the cage and executes a senton bomb onto Gnome. DD covers Gnome. 1.....2...thr Gnome gets his shoulder off the ground. DD can’t believe it. The crowd is in a unanimous boo as DD starts screaming at Rizzo. DD motion to hit Riz but Riz warns him not to do it. Gnome slowly makes his way to his feet. DD turns around and rushes Gnome. Gnome catches DD and slams him down with a spinebuster. Gnome grabs DD’s legs and send him flying face first into the cage with a slingshot. Gnome picks DD back up and sits him up on the top rope. Gnome takes DD down with the "Au Revoir, Comrade" top rope crucifix slam. Gnome picks up a dazed DD and nails the Power of One. DD is out in the middle of the ring. Gnome starts to climb the cage very slowly. DD slowly gets to his feet. DD begins to climb the cage behind Gnome. Gnome finally reaches the top, but DD does as well. Both men start brawling on the top of the cage. DD swings at Gnome but Gnome ducks the punch. Gnome grabs DD by his throat and sends him flying onto the mat. Gnome climbs over the top and drops to the concrete. Rizzo calls for the bell and declares Gnome the winner. The cage is lifted as Gnome, Thor and giant get back into the ring. They start to beat up on DD. Rizzo has none of it and grabs Giant from behind. Rizzo takes him down with an inverted DDT. Rizzo grabs gnome from behind and sends him flying over the top rope. Rizzo spins Thor around a DDTs him. Rizzo gets out of the ring as DD makes it to his feet. DD sees Thor on the ground and goes bezerk. DD kicks Thor in the head without mercy. DD rolls Thor out of the ring. DD sets Thor up on a table on the outside. DD climbs the ropes. DD comes off the top rope onto Thor with the Daredevil drop, sending both men trough the table and onto pure concrete. DD gets to his feet, but Thor is not moving. The medics rush to ringside and put Thor on a stretcher. DD makes his way to the back as the crowd chants his name. Match 6 - International Title Match: Pain vs. Edgecrusher *The lights go dim as "Break Stuff" by Limp Bizkit starts to pound on the speakers. Pain makes his way to the ring as the crowd boos loudly. He ignores them all as he gets in the ring.* *"Edgecrusher" by Fear factory starts to rile the crowd up. Orange pyros go off and Blue sparks shower down on the entranceway. The crowd boos loudly as Edgecrusher makes his way out through the shower of sparks with the International Title slung over his shoulder. He gets in the ring, hands the belt to the referee, and shakes hands with Pain.**bell rings** To their surprise, several fans start yelling for them to start kicking each other's ass. Pain and EC have other plans, however, and they start slap boxing with each other while laughing. They stop for a while, and EC grabs a mic from the announcers table at ringside. EC: You stupid-ass f---ing people. Did you really expect us to go at each other? Damn you people are stupid. I thought you Italians were supposed to be smarter than that. Oh no. You are not going to see me and Pain kick the crap out of each other. As Edgecrusher tosses the mic back, Wrecking Crew hits the ring. JayRok starts brawling with Pain, and Banshee tosses Edgecrusher to the outside and follows him. Sarkkahn and Ray Wright now come down now to help out. Ray Wright slides into the ring and hits JayRok with a Samoan drop. Outside, EC and Sarkkahn are both putting the boots to Banshee, cursing at him the whole time. Sarkkahn starts choking Banshee with his boot, and EC is grinding his heel into Banshee's groin. Back in the ring, Pain and Wright nail JayRok with the Painful Way. The roll him out of the ring, and EC tosses Banshee in. Sarkkahn reaches under the table and pulls out a table, which he slides into the ring. Sarkkahn stands on the top rope as EC picks Banshee up and Pain and Ray set the table up in close to the corner. Ray and Pain slide out now, and Ray slides in another table, which EC sets near the post directly across from Sarkkahn. Pain rolls JayRok back in, and EC gets up on the other turnbuckle. Sarkkahn takes Banshee, and EC takes JayRok, and they each put both Wrecking Crew members through the tables with the Dragon's Fire. There is a deafening roar of boos as all four members of Destruction Inc stand in the ring with there arms raised. They all head to the locker room as paramedics rush to the ring with stretchers to help out JayRok and Banshee to the back. .
