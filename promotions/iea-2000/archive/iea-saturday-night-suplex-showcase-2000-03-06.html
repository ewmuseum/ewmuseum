Saturday Night Suplex Showcase

posted 6 March 2000 (billed “March 4th Card Results”)
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.

Part 1

Saturday Night Suplex Showcase       Match 1 - #1 Contender to International Title: Inka Tupac vs. Cactus Funk     *The arena goes completely dark as Andean music takes to the air. Inka screams a battle cry then a light focus on him, revealing his red and white painted face. He is carrying the Peruvian flag in one hand and his mace in the other. He runs to the ring and from the outside he climbs to the top turnbuckle and waves the flag, another battle cry and jumps in the ring*     *"Been Caught Stealing" by Jane's Addiction is pumped through the speakers. Cactus Funk makes his way to a small crowd cheer. He gets in the ring.**Bell Rings*       Inka extends his hand to Cactus Funk. Funk accepts it reluctantly. Both men lock up. Inka gets a side headlock on Fun. He whips cactus into the ropes and connects with a clothesline. funk gets up immediately only to be brought back down with a hard clothesline. Funk doesn't get up the second time. Inka picks Funk up and scoop slams him. He goes for a standing splash but Funk gets his knees up. Inka rolls off of Funk's knees holding his stomach. Funk gets to his feet and begins to stomp on Inka. Funk picks Inka up to his feet and takes him down with a side backbreaker. Inka yells in pain. Funk drops an elbow on Inka and covers him. 1... Inka kicks out easily. Cactus picks Inka up and sends him crashing down with a Michinoku driver. Funk covers Inka again.       1...2....Inka kicks out, but not as easily as before. Cactus picks Inka up and sends him face first into the corner. Inka bounces off into Funk's waiting arms. Funk tries a belly to back suplex but he can't lift the big man up. Inka spins around and nails a European uppercut that sends Funk staggering backwards. Inka pulls Funk back to himself and grabs on to Funk. Inka takes Funk down with an overhead belly to belly suplex. Inka gets up quickly and gets to Funk. Inka gets on top and lands a few solid punches. Inka lifts Funk to his feet and sends him down with a DDT. Inka covers.       1....2.... Funk kicks out right before the three count. Inka lifts Funk to his feet and whips him hard into the corner. Inka follows very closely with a corner splash. Funk looks to be out on his feet. Inka rams Funk's head 7 times into the top turnbuckle. Inka comes charging out of the corner and takes Funk down with a running knee lift. Inka lifts funk to his feet and jerks Funk's head back with several boxing punches. Inka whips Funk into the ropes and catches him with a belly to belly suplex. Inka slaps on the Inka dominator. Funk tries to reach for the ropes but can't reach. He refuses to give in. He keeps trying for the ropes but eventually he passes out from the pain. The ref lifts his arm. 1....2...3! Inka Tupac wins the match by submission and faces the International Champion next Saturday.   Match 2: Southwest Strangla vs. The Skater       *"Southwest Strangla" by ICP hits the speakers. Red lights flash as Southwest Strangla makes his way to ringside to a chorus of boos. He gets in the ring and taunts the crows with a choking motion. He waits for The Skater.*     *"Nookie" by limp Bizkit pumps through the sound system as blue lights flood the arena. The Skater comes out to no reaction whatsoever. He gets in the ring.**Bell Rings*       Strangla and Skater immediately clash in the middle of the ring. Strangla gets the upperhand with a hard knee to the stomach. Strangla nearly takes Skater's head off with a mean short-arm clothesline. Strangla holds onto Skater's arm and drops a knee on it. This causes Skater to scream out in agonizing pain. Strangla adds more pressure with his knee. Strangla decides to take another avenue and lifts Skater to his feet. Strangla lands solid jabs to Skater's face. Strangla then take him down with a cradle DDT. Skater is out on the ground twitching. Strangla continues the punishment with booth to the midsection. Strangla covers Skater.       1...2....thr Strangla lifts Skater up to break the count. Kyle Mackal warns that next time he'll be DQ'd. Strangla lifts Skater to his feet and lands a boot to the midsection. Strangla lifts Skater up and sends him face first with an inverted powerbomb. Strangla drops a leg across the back of Skater's neck. Strangla then applies the Strangla Deathgrip. Skater taps out immediately. Strangla doesn't let go. The ref threatens to reverse the decision, forcing Strangla to let go. He leaves the ring victorious as Skater is helped to the back by security guys.   Match 3 - IEA international Title Match: Edgecrusher vs. JayRok (c)       *"Edgecrusher" by Fear Factory fills the arena at a very loud volume. Edgecrusher makes his way out to loud boos from the crowd. He gets in the ring and waits for the champ.*     *Lights dim as "Unforgiven II" begins to play throughout the arena. Huge white streams of pyro shoot up into the air as out walks JayRok. The crowd turns to huge cheers for the champ. He's wearing dark blue wrestling pants with streaks of lightning running down each leg, black boots, with black knee pads. He is also wearing black arm length pads. He jumps up onto the ring apron, and climbs up to the top rope. JayRok gives a deep stare into the crowd followed by an evil like grin.**Bell Rings*       Both men waste no time as they land hard body punches. JayRok blocks a punch from Edgecrusher and headbutts him. The big man just looks at JR and grabs him with both hands by the throat. EC sends JR flying across the ring. JR quickly gets to his feet and rushes EC. EC catches JR and powerslams him hard. EC covers the champ. 1...2.... JR kicks out. EC thinks it was a slow count and lets John Carlile know it. EC turns around right in time to be dropkicked by JR. The big man staggers back. JR dropkicks EC's knee. EC falls to one knee. JR bounces off the ropes and takes EC down with a bulldog. JR drops a knee on the back of EC's head. EC rolls in pain holding his face. JR runs up the turnbuckles and comes off with an elbow drop of EC. JR covers.       1...2...EC kicks out. JR picks EC up and tries for a frankensteiner. EC grabs JR in mid air and slams him hard to the mat with a sit-down powerbomb. EC stays on for the cover. 1...2...thr JR barely gets his shoulder off the mat. EC can't believe JR got out of it. EC picks JR up and delivers a series of crushing knee thrusts. JR is on his knees holding his stomach. EC drops a double axe handle on JR's back, sending him face first into the mat. EC picks JR back up and whips him hard into the corner. JR just holds himself up by the ropes. EC charges JR but JR drops, causing EC to slam his face into the top turnbuckle. JR climbs the ropes slowly and lands a beautiful missile dropkick. Both men are not moving.       The ref begins his mandatory 10 count 1...2...3...4...5....6...EC starts to stir as does JR 7....8.....JR gets to his feet first using the ropes. EC gets up and charges but JR sends EC over the top rope and onto the concrete with a back body drop. EC is out cold on the concrete. JR gets up and uses the ropes to slingshot himself on top of EC with a legdrop. The crowd comes alive for JR after this move. The ref begins to count both men out. 1.... JR gets to his feet and drops another leg across EC's neck. 2.... JR lifts EC to his feet and boots him several times in the midsection 3... JR tries to lift the big man to pile drive him but EC reverses it into a back body drop. 4.... EC lifts JR up and gorilla press slams him on the concrete 5.... EC picks JR up and whips him hard into the steel steps. 6.... EC gets JR back up and rolls him into the ring. EC follows very closely.       EC continues the brutal beating with several kicks to the head. JR seems to be unconscious. EC lifts JR up and sits him up on the top turnbuckle. EC climbs them slowly. he sets JR up for a superplex, but JR blocks it. JR lands several punches to the face. JR reverses the superplex with a top rope front face slam. EC lands hard on his face. JR gets up very slowly and rolls EC onto his back. JR covers, hooking the leg. 1...2....thr. EC gets his ram up right before the three. The crowd and JR are stunned. JR gets up and just stares at EC. JR lifts EC up and kicks him in the stomach, bending the big man over. JR goes to EC's back and executes the Rok Buster. JR covers EC, hooking the leg once again. 1...2....3! JR has pulled of the upset and has retained the international Title! Suddenly the crowd bursts into boos as Pain and Ray Wright run to ringside. The get in the ring and double team JayRok, who is exhausted. Both men executes the Painful Way. The crowd erupts and Sledgehammer comes into the ring with a chair. He swings at a fleeing Destruction Inc, but he misses all three. They make their way to the back. Sledgehammer picks JR up and carries him to the back.   Match 4 - IEA North Atlantic Championship Match: Nick "Sledgehammer" Sloan vs. David "Daredevil" Dumont       *"Guerilla Radio" by Rage Against the Machine begins to pound the speakers. The crowd is on their feet as Nick "the Sledgehammer" Sloan makes his way to ring side to a loud collective cheer from the crowd. he gets in and waits for Daredevil.*     *"Tear it off" by Redman and Method Man starts playing in the speakers. Daredevil comes out to a good sized cheer from the crowd. He makes his way to the ring, staring SledgeHammer down the whole time. DD makes his way into the ring.**Bell Rings*       Both men stare each other down in the middle of the ring. Some not so friendly words are exchanged. DD pushes Sledgehammer. Sledgehammer returns with a right fist to the face. DD is sent backwards. Sloan grabs DD and takes him down with a gargoyle suplex. DD bounces off the mat hard. Sloan gets up quickly and stalks DD. Sloan grabs DD when he gets to his feet and applies a bearhug. DD gets out of it with an earringer. DD bounces off the ropes and takes Sledgehammer down with a flying leg clothesline. DD lands a standing splash on Sledgehammer but he doesn't cover. He climbs the ropes and comes off wit a crushing guillotine legdrop. He covers Sledgehammer.       1...2.... SledgeHammer kicks out with ease. DD lifts Sledgehammer to his feet and kicks him in the knee. Sledgehammer's knees buckle. DD comes off the ropes and hits a flying clothesline on Sledgehammer. Sledgehammer bends backward on his knees in a most painful way. Sloan drops to the mat. DD rolls the big man over and covers. 1...2.... kick out again by Sledgehammer. DD lifts Sledgehammer to his feet and climbs the top rope. He comes off with a flying cross body press but Sledgehammer just catches him. Sledgehammer brings DD down with a crushing backbreaker. Sledgehammer holds on and executes another backbreaker. DD just rolls off of Sloan's knee, holding his back with a grimace on his face. Sledgehammer grabs DD and picks up up. Sledgehammer puts DD up on his shoulder. Sledgehammer nails a running powerslam. Sloan covers DD       1.....2....thr DD kicks out right in the nick of time. The crowd is very into the match, cheering on both men. Sledgehammer picks DD up and whips him off the ropes. DD ducks a big boot from Sledgehammer. DD stops behind Sloan and takes him down with a jumping inverted DDT. Sloan is down and out. DD climbs the top rope and nails a frogsplash. DD stays on top of Sloan for the pin attempt. 1...2.... Sloan gets an arm up. DD is visibly upset but the crowd is loving it. DD picks Sloan up and executes a rocker dropper. Sloan's face hits the canvas hard. DD drives it into the mat further with a leg drop to the back of the head. DD rolls the fatman over and covers him. 1...2....thr arm up again by Sloan. He just will not stay down, and DD realizes this.       DD turns up the heat and picks Sledgehammer up. DD takes Sledgehammer down with a snap suplex. DD gets up and runs towards the ropes. He springboards off the second rope and lands a moonsault. DD covers. 1...2....thr Sledgehammer with an arm up again. DD doesn't understand it. DD slaps on a figure four on the big man. Sledgehammer yells from the pain that DD is inflicting. Sledgehammer starts to rock himself and DD side to side. Finally, Sloan manages to reverse the hold. DD is in pain. DD is close enough to the ropes to latch on. Both men get to their feet. Sloan charges DD but DD takes him down with a drop toe hold. Sledgehammer's face hits the mat hard again. DD rolls Sloan onto his back. DD climbs the ropes. He comes off with a shooting star press but Sloan rolls out of the way. DD hits the mat hard. Both men are down.       The ref starts another 10 count. 1....2....3....4....Sloan gets to his feet first. He slowly walks over to DD who's on the ground. Sloan drops an elbow on DD's back. Sloan picks DD up and executes the Sledge-O-Matic. Sloan covers DD. 1....2...thr DD gets his foot up on the bottom rope to break the count. The crowd is deafeningly loud at this point in the match. Sloan grabs DD by the leg and drags him to the middle of the ring. Sloan slaps on a Texas cloverleaf. DD screams in pain as Sloan leans back adding more pressure. DD tries for the ropes but can't get there. DD tells the ref he will not give up. DD keeps trying for the ropes, but his efforts lessen. DD falls into unconsciousness from the pain. The ref checks DD's arm.       1....2....thr DD keeps his arm from hitting the mat. He starts to get energy and begins to inch towards the ropes. he miraculously manages to get to the ropes and he holds on for dear life. Sledgehammer lets go immediately. Sledgehammer grabs DD and lifts him to his feet. He tries a powerbomb but DD reverses it into a huricanrana. DD hooks the legs for the cover. 1...2....3! DD manages to squeak a win out from Sledgehammer. DD is handed the North Atlantic Championship by the ref. The lights go out. A god awful battle cry is heard throughout the arena followed by a sound of blood curdling proportions. The lights come on to reveal Super Bastard standing over Daredevil. SB drapes the NAC over DD and walks to the back laughing at him. When SB gets to the back Destruction Inc makes their way through a very hostile crowd. Right when the get to the ring the crowd starts cheering as JayRok also comes out from the back. As all 5 men start brawling, the lights go out again. When they come back on, Ninja is standing in the middle of the ring. He looks at all of the men and viscously kicks Pain in the face with a standing side kick. Ninja sees EC charging him and uses his momentum to send him over the top rope. Ray Wright charges also but Sledgehammer catches him in mid stride with a clothesline. Sloan picks Wright up. JR grabs Wright by his legs as Sledgehammer grabs his head. Both men execute a double front face slam, now known as De-Construction. The two members of Wrecking Crew turn to Ninja and offer their hands. Ninja just shakes his head as smoke fills the arena. When it clears, only JR, Sledgehammer and a recovering DD are left in the ring. DD gets up and runs to the back looking for SB. Wrecking Crew make heir way to the back to a thunderous cheer from the crowd..   Credits   All matches written by [name withheld]
