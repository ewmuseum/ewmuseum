IEA Card, 11 March 2000

A loud BANG is heard followed by huge streams of Pyro shooting up all around the entrance way. A deep purple color fills the arena as out onto the entrance way walks Super Bastard. He walks down to the ring, let's out the War Whoop, jumps over the ring post, and awaits his opponent.* *"Helter Skelter" by The Beatles begins to play as "The Punisher" Ray Lopez makes his way out. He begins to walk down the ramp, as Super Bastard hops down onto the floor and charges towards Lopez.* *DING DING DING* Bell sounds as Bastard and Lopez begin to pummel each other on the outside. Lopez gets the upper hand early as he slams Super Bastard into the steel steps crushing his left shoulder. Lopez taunts Bastard a bit before slamming his forehead off of the ring apron. Super Bastard reverses an Irish whip attempt from Lopez, sending Lopez into the outside barrier. The ref is yelling for Bastard and Lopez to get into the ring. Super Bastard pulls Lopez up and pushes him into the ring. Bastard grabs a chair from ringside and slides it into the ring. Lopez makes it to his feet and sends Super Bastard crashing down onto the canvas with a huge spinebuster slam. Lopez goes for the cover. 1.....2...kickout. Super bastard gets his arm up at the last second. Lopez climbs to the top rope while Super Bastard is still laying flat on his back. Lopez goes for a moonsault but Bastard rolls out of the way causing Lopez to smash his nose right off the unforgiving canvas. Super Bastard gains momentum by landing a huge release belly to belly suplex sending Lopez crashing down. Super Bastard picks up Lopez and puts him in what looks to be a torture rack, but he's setting Lopez up for the Super Bastard Breaker. Lopez slides out and takes Bastard down with a Tornado DDT. Lopez climbs to the top rope, leaps off, and lands an overwhelming asai moonsault onto Super Bastard. Lopez goes for the cover. 1...2....kickout. Super Bastard just barely kicked out of that pin. Lopez slows picks Super Bastard up to his feet. Lopez sets Bastard up onto the top rope and signals for his finisher as he wraps his arms around Bastard's head. Super Bastard throws Lopez off of him before Lopez and land the Top Rope Bulldog. Super Bastard stumbles down from the top rope as Lopez makes his way to his feet. Super Bastard screams at the top of his lungs and lands the Super Bastard Spear onto Lopez. Bastard rolls Lopez over and goes for the cover....1....2.....3...DING DING DING...The bell sounds as Super Bastard has beaten "The Punisher" Ray Lopez. Lopez seems to be upset about something as he complains to the ref. The winner of this match......Super Bastard. Match 2: Thor vs. German Skull *"Eifersucht" by Rammstein plays, the only sound heard. A black screen is all that is shown. The song continues to play, and a yellow ball forms on the screen, getting bigger, until finally, with a glass-breaking sound, it explodes into the following words "The Baddest Man In Niedsachsen." The white words slowly dematerialise and reappear as a yellow horizontal stripe taking up a third of the screen. Another ball, this one red, speeds towards the screen and shatters into "The Greatest Physique In Germany." These words dematerialise and reappear as a red stripe, taking up another third of the screen. The final ball, this one black, spins towards the screen, shattering and forming the following "The Innovator Of The Most Feared Move In All Of Europe, The Flying Deadbutt." This forms a black stripe, forming a German flag. The colours fade out, leaving still more words "The German Skull." The German Skull then walks into the arena with his German flag and climbs into the ring.* *Du Hast is played and the crowd boos as thor makes his way to ringside.* Thor charges at German skull and attempts to hit him with a clothesline. German Skull ducks the clothesline and hits a throat strike at the incoming Thor. Thor grabs hit throat and drops to the mat gagging. Skull dropkicks him in the head and Thor crumples to the ground. German Skull runs towards the ring ropes and attempts a springboard moonsault. Thor rolls out of the way and German skull gets a face full of mat. Thor gets up and drags German skull to his feet and plants him with a 2 arm DDT. Thor stamps on his opponent mercilessly and takes the wind right out of him. Thor then drops his elbow on German Skull's chest. German Skulls coughs up a small amount of blood through his mask. Thor laughs and starts playing to the crowd. He picks up his French flag and waves it to the crowd who jeer him continuously. Meanwhile German Skull gets to his feet. He climbs the top rope just behind Thor. He waits for him to turn around and executes a Flying Spinning Wheel Kick. Thor falls to the ground grabbing at his face. He again climbs the top rope and executes a Top Rope Senton Bomb. He gets up and starts posing for the crowd screaming out "Look at the greatest physique in Germany!" The crowd starts booing loudly. German Skull turns to the crumbled body of Thor and repeatedly punches Thor in the face. German Skull grabs his hair and drags Thor to his feet. He attempts to German Suplex Thor, but German Skull is too weak to lift the 7'3" Frenchman. Thor laughs and mule kicks German Skull. Skull falls to the ground grabbing at his groin. Thor picks him up and belly-to-belly suplexes him. He picks him up again and kicks him in the chest. The German Skull doubles over and Thor piledrives him into the canvas. German skull grabs his head and rolls around. Thor laughs and goes for the cover. 1...2...weak kick out from German Skull. Thor is shocked but still manages to regain his composure and picks up German skull. He attempts to Powerbomb German Skull but somehow he manages to reverse it into a face plant. Thor rolls out of the ring down to the floor to regain his confidence. German Skull taunts him, comparing their sizes and builds. German skull starts posing and posturing to the crowd. Thor climbs to the apron and German skull hits his patented Sunset Powerbomb Suicida. The crowd goes wild when they see this awesome move and start chanting "Skull! Skull! Skull!" Both men hurt themselves in this move and both are slow to rise. The referee starts the count. 1...2...3...4...5...6...Skull starts to rise and pick up a chair...7...Skull throw chair into ring and starts to climb in, Thor rises...8...Skull is in ring, Thor starts to climb in...9...Thor is in the ring. As Thor climbs in German Skull grabs him and whips him into the ropes. Thor bounces off the ropes. German Skull attempts to superkick Thor, however Thor manages to avoid the dropkick, bounce off the other ropes and spears German Skull. Thor attempts to pin. 1...2...weak kick out by German skull. Thor drags his opponent up to his feet and chokeslams him into the corner. Thor runs at German skull and kicks him in the face. Blood gushes out of German Skull's mask. Thor grabs German Skull by the hair, yanks him to his feet and lifts him onto the turnbuckle. Thor looks at the crowd, makes a face and superplexes German Skull into the mat. Thor executes a legdrop on Skull. He gets up and stamps on Skull over and over. Thor screams to the crowd, "AGAIN!" picks up German Skull and places him on the turnbuckle. Skull takes the opportunity to hit another throat strike. Thor grabs at his throat and gives German Skull enough time to execute a Skullicanrana right onto the chair! German Skull screams "Deadbutt!" and climbs the turnbuckle and executes a Top Rope Diving Headbutt. Moments before impact Thor rolls away and German Skull eats a mouthful of steel. Thor looks at his opponent and laughs. Thor yanks him to his feet and executes a Hanging Vertical Suplex. Thor goes for the cover. 1...2...3...Thor wins! The referee attempts to raise his hand but Thor yanks his arm away and walks calmly out of the ring, picks up his flag and leaves. Match 3 - IEA Tag Tourney Match: Destruction Inc vs. Hardcore Icons *The arena goes black and "Superbeast" starts playing. Yellow lights flash randomly above the ring with the beat of the synthesizer at the start of the song. When the power chord strikes to start the guitar playing, there is an explosion of pyrotechnics on top of each post, with the flames burning until the Hardcore Icons enter the ring.* *The lights go dim as "Break Stuff" by Limp Bizkit starts to pound on the speakers. Pain and Ray Wright make their way to the ring. The crowd is heard booing both men loudly.**bell rings.* Cactus Funk and Ray Wright are in to start things off. Funk takes Ray over with a hip toss. Ray hops up and rushes Funk, and only gets his face smacked off the canvas from a drop toe hold. Funk takes off and bounces off the ropes, Ray tags in Pain, Funk comes runnin back with a clothes line to take down Pain. Pain rolls to his feet. Funk tries a DDT, but Pain likes the idea of giving a spinebuster better, so he snaps Funk down, hard. Pain stands over Funk for a few seconds admiring his work, and kicks him in the teeth. Sarkkhan comes haulin out from the corner, Pain tries a lariat, but really takes a Somoan drop. Ray gets in the ring, Sarkkhan G-presses him and drops him outside the ring. He helps Funk up and gets back out the ring, then Funk tags him in. Sarkkhan comes runnin at Pain and takes one to the nards. As he bends over, nearly in tears, Pain DDT's him. Sarkkhan rolls over, lookin groggy, Pain kicks him in the gut, then in the head with an inzuguri. Ray gets back into his corner, Pain tags him in and sets Sarkkhan up for a piledriver, Ray climbs the to the top rope and jumps off, his landing spikes Pain piledriver. Sarkkhan ain't movin. Ray drags him by the hair to the middle of the ring and slaps on a sleeper. Sarkkhan is out anyway, the ref raises his hand, and it falls like a broken tree branch. As h lifts his hand again, Funk kicks Ray in the neck. Ray turns around in surprise and kicked in the beer gut and power bombed. The ref yells at Funk to get, and he does, bringing in Pain on the other side. Funk tries to get back in the ring, but the ref restrains him. All the while, Pain is layin it to Sarkkhan. He lands a t-bone, a belly to belly, a power slam, and an elbow drop before the ref realize what's going on. The ref gets Pain out, then turns to see the other two both layed out. He starts his ten count. 1... 2... 3... 4... 5... 6... 7... 8... Ray throws an arm over Sarkkhan, the ref counts. 1... 2... Weak weak arm up by Sarkkhan. Ray pulls Sarkkhan up by the chin and whips him into the ropes, he tries a follow through boot to the face, but Sarkkhan ducks, tags Funk, and comes flyin back with a shoulder tackle. He rolls his way to his corner as Funk starts stomping on Ray. Pain comes out and punches Funk in the back of the head, gets nothing, and runs back out the ring. Funk goes after Pain, takes a low blow from Ray, as he bends over, Pain comes off the top rope with a leg drop to the back of Funks head (basically a top rope fame asser). Pain pulls Ray on top off Funk, and get clotheslined by Sarkkhan out the ring. The ref counts. 1... 2... surprisingly strong arm up by Funk. On the outside, Sarkkhan power bombs Pain on the concrete, picks him up in a G-press and drops him throat first on the guard rail. In the ring, Ray and Funk are trading punches. Evenly until Funk blocks a cross from Ray and starts walaying on Ray. Funk sets Ray on the top rope. Outside, as Sarkkhan tries picking up Pain, he pinged on the crown of his dome by a 2x4. Back in the ring, Funk drops Ray in a superplex. Edgecrusher gets Pain up, Funk German suplexes Ray, Pain tries to get in the ring, but the ref stops him. Pain distracts him while EC climbs in. Funk lets go of the pin attempt to see whats going on. EC grabs him from behind and whips him into the ropes, when Funk comes back, he quickly gets dropped in a chokeslam. EC slides out the ring after slapping Ray to wake him up. The ref turns his attention to Ray, Pain slides in. Ray and Pain land the Painful Way on Funk. Sarkkhan comes to outside. Ray covers Funk. 1... (Sarkkhan slides in the ring) 2... (Sarkkhan leaps at Ray) 3... (Pain sticks his foot in front of Sarkkhan and sends him a different direction) The ref calls for the bell. Ray and Pain get out the ring and walk backwards up the ramp with EC, laughing at the two in the ring. Match 4 - International Title Match: Inka Tupac vs. JayRok(c) *The arena goes completely dark as Andean music takes to the air. Inka screams a battle cry then a light focus on him, revealing his red and white painted face. He is carrying the Peruvian flag in one hand and his mace in the other. He runs to the ring and from the outside he climbs to the top turnbuckle and waves the flag, another battle cry and jumps in the ring* *Lights dim as "Unforgiven II" begins to play throughout the arena. Huge white streams of pyro shoot up into the air as out walks JayRok. The crowd turns to huge cheers for the champ. He's wearing dark blue wrestling pants with streaks of lightning running down each leg, black boots, with black knee pads. He is also wearing black arm length pads. He jumps up onto the ring apron, and climbs up to the top rope. JayRok gives a deep stare into the crowd followed by an evil like grin.**Bell Rings* Both men meet in the ring and stare each other down. Inka nails JR with a knife edge chop. JR returns the chop. Inka and JR then throw down and start brawling like savage animals in the middle of the ring. JR grabs Inka by the face and knees him in it. JR clothesline Inka to the mat, but Inka gets up immediately. JR charges Inka but Inka turns it into a Samoan drop. Inka elbows JR on the mat. Inka lifts JR up and whips him off the ropes. Inka takes JR down with a spinebuster. Inka holds onto JR's legs and turns him over into a Boston crab. JR fights the pain as best as he can. JR's close enough to the ropes where he grabs on with one hand. The ref yells at Inka to let go but he won't. The ref starts to count Inka. 1...2..3...4.. Inka lets go before he is DQ'd. Inka starts yelling at the ref to mind his own business. This gives JR time to recover and dropkick Inka over the top rope from behind. The ref begins to count Inka out. 1...2.... Inka gets to his feet but is sent back down by a baseball slide from JR. The ref has to restart his count. 1...2....JR climbs the top rope and waits for Inka to get up. 3....4....Inka gets to his feet. JR comes off the top rope with a corkscrew plancha, sending both men hard to the concrete. The ref starts yet another count, but out of frustration he gives up and lets the match continue. JR gets to his feet first and starts to lay the boots to Inka. JR lifts Inka up and executes a belly to back suplex on the concrete. JR gets back up and lands a falling headbutt on Inka. JR rolls Inka back into the ring and follows with a slingshot legdrop over the top rope. JR stays on top of Inka for the cover. 1...2.... Inka kicks out of it. JR lifts Inka to his feet and whips him into the corner. JR follows closely with a clothesline. JR starts to kick Inka in the midsection while he's in the corner. JR tries to whip Inka into the opposite corner but Inka reverses it, sending JR face first into the corner. Inka grabs JR from behind and drops him with an inverted DDT. Inka covers JR. 1...2..... JR gets his arm up before he three. Inka gets mad and straddles JR, pummeling him with fists. The ref tries to pull Inka off but Inka accidentally elbows him. The ref lands on the mat and is out like a light. The crowd starts to scream as a masked man comes down and slides into the ring. The masked man looks at Inka then at JR. The masked man then lifts JR up and executes a piledriver. Inka then whips JR into the ropes and catches him with the belly to belly suplex. The masked man takes he mask off to reveal Kalipa Rosas. Inka then slaps on the Inka dominator and JR taps out, but the ref is in no shape to check JR. The crowd turns to a loud boo as a figure makes his way through the crowd. Edgecrusher imageries with a chair. he levels Kalipa from behind, sending him to the outside. He then bends the chair out of shape on Inka's face. He wakes the ref up and drapes JR's arm over Inka. The ref counts. 1....2....3! JayRok has retained the International title with the help of Edgecrusher. Edgecrusher then grabs a mic and says something. "JayRok, you owe me a title shot at the Pay Per View. That title is MINE and I will take it away from you." EC drops the mic and makes his way to the back to loud boos. JR gets up and runs to the back looking for EC. Kalipa gets up and helps Inka to the back.. . Match 5 - North Atlantic Championship Match: Grim Reaper vs. David "Daredevil" Dumont(c) *Smoke fills the arena as "Live Forever" by Oasis starts to play through the sound system. A single white light flashes in the ring. A red spotlight shines on the entrance as Grim Reaper makes his way to a chorus of boos. He gets in the ring and waits for Daredevil with a scowl on his face.* *"Tear it off" by Redman and Method Man starts playing in the speakers. Daredevil comes out to a good sized cheer from the crowd. He makes his way to the ring, staring Grim Reaper down the whole time. DD makes his way into the ring.**Bell Rings* Both men start exchanging punches the second the bell rings. Reaper grabs DD by the head and lands a crushing headbutt. DD stumbles backward but comes right back with several punches to the face. DD bounces off the ropes and tries for a cross body press but the big man catches him in his waiting arms. Reaper sends DD down with a fall forward slam. Reaper drops a big leg across DD's throat. Reaper stays on for the lazy cover. 1.... DD gets out of the pin easily. The crowd also appreciates it. Reaper picks DD up and nails a vertical suplex. Reaper drops a leg again on DD's throat. Reaper covers again. 1...2...DD gets up again, but not as easily. Reaper lifts DD up to his feet. Reaper goes for a scoop slam but DD manages to turn it into a small package. 1..2...kick out by Reaper, but to the crowd's displeasure. Reaper gets to his feet the same time DD does, but DD takes him back down with a gorgeous standing dropkick. DD bounces off the ropes and lands a dropkick on Reaper's knee. Reaper's leg collapses from below him. DD bounces off the ropes and takes the big man down with a bulldog. DD drops a knee on the back of Reaper's head, smashing his face even further into the mat. Reaper's holding his face on the mat in pain. DD continues to deliver punishment with a legdrop of his own. DD gets up immediately and drops another leg. DD picks Reaper up and manages to body slam him. DD climbs the ropes with lightning speed and comes crashing down on reaper with a guillotine legdrop. DD covers Reaper. 1...2....thr...Reaper gets his shoulder off the mat right before the ref's hand hits three. DD nor the crowd can believe it. DD gets up and looks at the ref in disbelief. DD turns back to GR and kicks him in the gut. DD lifts Reaper up and tries to whip him in the corner, but GR doesn't budge. GR reverses it with a destructive short-arm clothesline. DD does a flip in mid air from the impact. Reaper gets back on the offensive with kicks to the head. Reaper picks DD up and sends him on his back with a powerbomb. Reaper covers. 1...2.... DD gets his arm up. The crowd starts to chant DD's name. Reaper lifts DD to his feet and whips him off the ropes. Reaper takes DD down with a big boot. Uncharacteristically of him, Reaper climbs the ropes. He goes for a big splash but DD rolls out of the way, sending Reaper on a one way trip to belly flop land. Both men are down and the ref begins his count. 1....2....3....4.....5....DD starts to stir, but isn't quite able to get up. 6...7....DD and Reaper use the ropes to get to their feet. DD charges but Reaper sends DD over the top rope with a back body drop. However, DD lands on the apron with his feet. DD springboards off the top rope to bulldog Reaper. DD gets up slowly, but Reaper looks to be out on the mat. DD runs to the corner but just springboards off the top turnbuckles for a moonsault. DD doesn't cover. Instead he drags the big man to the corner. DD climbs the ropes and nails the Daredevil Drop. DD covers, with the leg hooks. 1...2....3! Daredevil has defeated the Grim Reaper to retain the NAC.
