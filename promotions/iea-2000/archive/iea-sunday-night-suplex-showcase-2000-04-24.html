Sunday Night Suplex Showcase

posted 24 April 2000; billed 23 April 2000, Oslo, Norway
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Here are the results for the April 23rd card in Oslo, Norway! Sunday Night Suplex Showcase Match 1 - Billy Lovemuscle vs. Diabolus *El Distorto De Melodica hits the speakers and we see Billy Lovemuscle make his way out from the back..ON A HORSE. The fans groan at his half-assed attempt to be attractive. He enters the ring* *It get's dark as Giant Anaconda walks to the ring, there's only one green stroboscope effect light that follows Giant Anaconda. Giant Anaconda walks over the top-rope, raises his arms and pyros fly off the four corners, then the lights go back on* Diablous charges at Billy but Billy gives Diablous a boot to the chin. Diablous is suplexed by Billy but quickly gets to his feet. Billy is whipped into the ropes by Diablous and recives a elbow to the face. Diablous gives Billy a few quick kicks to Billy's face before he can get up. Billy gives a low-blow to Diablous and whips him into the turnbuckle. Billy bulldogs Diablous and Diablous is left on the mat while Billy climbs to the top rope. Billy attempts a Asai Moonsault but cleary misses. Diablous gets up as Billy does the same. They lock up. Diablous gets the upperhand and gives Billy a Back Breaker. Diablous rolls Billy outside of the ring. Billy falls to the cement but qucikly gets to his feet. A count starts(1..2...3). Diablous climbs the ropes and gives Billy an Ax-Handle(4...5). Diablous trys to put Billy into a Lion Tamer but Billy tosses him aside and fists start flying(6...7...8). Both men enter the ring and the fist fight continues. Billy gives a DDT to Diablous and Diablous is can not get to his feet. Billy climbs the top rope to go for an Elbow-Drop. Suddenly, out of now where Giant Anacadona knocks Billy of the turnbuckle with a chair shot to the back. Billy lands right on the limp Diablous as GA sets up a table in the ring. GA puts Billy on the table followed by Diablous. GA climbs the rope and leg drops both men through table.Security comes out before anymore damage can be done to Billy or Diablous. Match 2: Chris Petty vs. Scheize *Schezie is already in the ring,waiting for Chris Petty* *The lights go out and a single bolt of lightning shoots down to the ring. The lights flicker to red then back out, and back to red. Chris Petty appears from the entrance and fire engulfs his surroundings. Then the lights go out again and he is standing in the ring when they come back on* Chris runs at Schezie but Scheize tosses Chris over his back. Scheize sticks his foot straight into Chris' throat but then allowes him to get up. Chris Petty whips Scheize into the ropes and as Schezie is running back he gets a kick to the chest. Chris goes for the quick pin(1..). Schezie rolls out of the pin and both men get to their feet. Scheize trys an Amtoic Drop on Chrisbut he is able to reverse into a Russian Leg Sweep.Scheize falls out of the ring/apron staright onto the cement out side. A count starts(1.....2.). Chris climbs the ropes and gives Scheize a flying Drop Kick(.3...4.) Schieze gets up and gives Chirs a heel to the groin follwed by an Inverted Atomic Drop.(...5...6.) Scheize picks up the now bloody Chris and sends him face-first into the ring steps. (.7...8.) Schieize enters the ring leaving Chirs outside, alone. (..9...10)Chris Petty losess due to countout. Match 3: Jack Beamer vs. Ching Wang Zing *Already awaiting his opponent in the ring is the martial arts expert, Ching.* *The Lights slowly fade to black while My Girlfriend's Girlfriend starts playing. Suddenly out from the back comes Jack Beamer in his orange jumpsuit with shackles on his ankles and wrists. He climbs into the ring and as he is unbuckling the shackles 4 white pyros blast through the arena.* Ding Ding Ding.....The bells sounds as Ching charges towards Beamer. Beamer counters Ching and takes him down with a drop toe hold. Ching gets up and turns around as Beamer lands an overwhelming belly to belly suplex sending Ching crashing down onto the unforgiving mat. Beamer runs off of the ropes and leaps onto Ching landing a leg drop. He quickly goes for the pin... 1...2...kickout. Ching gets his shoulder up at the last second and gives Beamer an eye rake. Beamer stumbles to his feet as Ching begins slapping Beamer across the chest. Beamer fights back with a few hard punches as he whips Ching off the ropes. Beamer catches Ching in a Choke hold and picks him up into the air. Beamer sends Ching down hard with a huge Choke Slam. Beamer picks Ching up to his feet, kicks Ching in the stomach, and slams him down with a DDT. Beamer goes for the cover... 1...2.....kickout... A long two count. Beamer complains to the ref about a slow count as Ching struggles to stand up. Beamer keeps the attack going as he lands an atomic drop onto Ching and finishes it off with a reverse DDT. Ching lands a low blow onto Beamer and the ref threatens to disqualify Ching. Ching goes for a type of martial arts kick but Beamer blocks it and kicks Ching in the stomach area. Beamer lands the Prison Plex and goes for the cover ....1....2....3....Ding Ding Ding....The winner of this match by pinfall....Jack Beamer. Match 4: Gado vs. Hades *The lights go out completely, and for several seconds there is nothing but darkness. A lone violin starts to play it's mellow music, and a large spotlight resembling a full moon shines on the center of the ring. Gado rises to his feet in the middle of the light with his head back and arms crossed. He sniffs the air around him hard, then cracks his knuckles at his sides, kicks the dirt away from his feet and takes his ready position, awaiting Hades.* *The lights come back up, and "Headup" by The Deftones starts pumping into the arena. The crowd gives Hades a good pop as he makes his way to the ring. He is carrying a steel chair wrapped in barbed wire, which he leaves at ringside. Hades gets in, and the music fades out.**bell rings** Hades and Gado size each other up, and Hades puts a side headlock on Gado. Gado pushes Hades into the rope, and as he comes back, Gado tackles him, rolls through, and starts stomping Hades in the head, but Hades grabs his foot and twists it violently, causing Gado to fall to the mat in agony. Hades continues twisting the ankle as he drags Gado towards the corner, then kicks him across the temple a few times for good measure. Hades climbs the to the top rope, and lands a senton bomb. He climbs the turnbuckles and lands another senton bomb. Hades now picks up Gado, and plants him back down with a sidewalk slam. Hades backs up and waits for Gado to stand up. Gado starts to get up, but he is still in pain from the ankle-lock, and is very unsteady. He turns to face Hades, and Hades turns him inside out with a closeline. Hades now picks up Gado, hoists him up, and drops him with a Samoan drop near the corner. Hades climbs the turnbuckle again for another senton bomb, but Gado rolls out and takes out the referee, who ends up taking the bomb. Gado is still unsteady, and Hades grabs his barbed-wire chair. He swings it full force and hits Gado right in the small of the back. Gado screams in pain and clutches at his back, where small drops of blood are starting to form. Hades picks up Gado and whips him into the corner. Hades checks on the referee, who is starting to come to and is getting up. Hades starts kicking Gado in the gut, then climbs the turnbuckle, pulling Gado up with him. Hades lifts Gado up, and hits the top rope brainbuster, From Heaven To Hell! Hades hooks the leg, 1...2...3**bell rings** The winner of this match is Hades. Match 5 - Inka vs. Strangla *Inka Tupac is shown walking through the backstage area as his opponent, Southwest Strangla, sneaks up behind Inka and punches him to the hard concrete floor. Strangla picks up Inka and drags him out onto the entrance way. He begins punching Inka towards the ring side area as Inka counters Strangla's attack and fights back with a few quick punches. Inka rolls Strangla into the ring and the bell sounds officially starting the match.* Ding Ding Ding.....Inka begins taking quick control of the match as he lands a neck breaker onto Strangla. Strangla struggles to a standing position but Inka sends Strangla crashing back down as Inka dropkicks Strangla directly in the mouth. Inka attempts to strap on the Inka Dominator but Strangla throws him off. Strangla begins nailing Inka with Loud Chest Chops. Strangla whips Inka off the ropes and lands a spinning heel kick. Strangla goes for the cover.....1.....kickout Strangla turns to complain to the ref as Inka wraps his arms around Strangla's head and lands a running bulldog. Inka goes for the cover....1...2...kickout. Strangla complains about a fast count as Inka begins to pummel Strangla with punches to the ribs and face. Inka gives a blank stare around the crowd as Strangla picks Inka up from behind and lands a bridged german suplex for a pin attempt...1...2..Inka kicks out. Strangla body slams Inka down to the mat but Inka fights his way to his feet and sends Strangla into one of the turnbuckles with a few rounds of hard jabs. Strangla kicks Inka in the stomach and attempts a piledriver onto Inka but Inka counters with a back body drop and straps on the Inka Dominator.......Strangla tries to fight it off...Inka applies more and more pressure....Strangla taps out....DING DING DING....The Winner of this match by submission...Inka Tupac Match 6 - 6 Man Tag Match: Wrecking Crew/Bradley Culver vs. Destruction Inc. *The lights die down, and "Guerilla Radio" by Rage Against The Machine starts pumping in through the speakers. Huge streams of white pyros shoot off at the entrance, and Banshee, Bradley Culver and JayRok come out. The crowd goes wild as the three make their way to the ring, giving the fans high fives as they go along. Banshee climbs in the ring and JR vaults himself over the top rope as they await their opponents. Culver springboards offt he top rope into the ring.* *Matthew Good Band's "Hello Time Bomb" starts blaring through the speakers. Sarkkhan, Pain and Ray Wright come out to a very hostile crowd. They make their way to the ring in a hurry. they slidei nto teh ring and the match begins* All three members of Destruction Inc hit the ring and immediately start brawling with Wrecking Crew. Ray Wright and JayRok take it to the floor as does Banshee and Pain. Sarkkhan and Culver keep it in the ring. Sarkkhan and Culver lock up and the referee rings the bell to begin the match. Culver swings Sarkkhan into the ropes and spears him. Culver starts stamping on Sarkkhan repeatedly. Both Ray Wright and JayRok break it up and move to their corners, as does Banshee and Pain. Culver pulls Sarkkhan to his feet. Sarkkhan punches Culver in the face and executes a Russian legs sweep. Sarkkhan places Culver into a figure 4 leg lock. Culver is in real pain but starts to power to the ropes. Sarkkhan can’ t believe it when Culver grabs the ropes and the ref orders him to release the hold. Sarkkhan hurls Culver into the ropes and attempts to clothesline him. Culver ducks the clothesline and executes a Belly to Back Crossed Arms Through Leg Suplex. The crowd goes nuts for Culver as he executes the move on the 7-foot tall behemoth. Culver drags Sarkkhan to his corner and tags in JayRok. Culver drags Sarkkhan to his feet while JayRok climbs to the top turnbuckle. JayRok hits a Top-Rope Tornado DDT. Culver gets out of the ring. JayRok goes for the pin. 1…2… strong kick out from Sarkkhan. JayRok cannot believe it and drags Sarkkhan to his feet. JayRok attempts to sling Sarkkhan into the ropes, but Sarkkhan reverses it and slings JayRok into the ropes. Sarkkhan moves to the centre of the ring. JayRok bounces back off the ropes. Sarkkhan hits a Samoan drop. Sarkkhan picks up JayRok again and hurls him into his corner. Pain holds him in the corner while Sarkkhan tags in Ray Wright. Ray Wright climbs into the ring and elbow smashes JayRok in the face a few times. Ray then executes a two arm release overhead belly to belly Suplex. JayRok looks to be in some serious pain. Ray puts his foot on JayRoks chest and poses for the crowd, who boo him intensely. 1…2…kick out from JayRok. Ray shrugs and drags JayRok to his feet. Ray puts him in position for a sit down piledriver. JayRok drops his head a little bit and then headbutts Ray in the groin. Jay runs to the ropes and executes a springboard spinning heel kick. It catches Ray square on the nose and it starts bleeding. JayRok starts to get pumped and attempts to execute a springboard guillotine legdrop. As JayRok jumps for the top rope Pain grabs it and drops to the floor, lowering the height of the rope and sending JayRok crashing into the guard rail. The crowd boo Pain intensely. Pain tells the crowd to shut up and gives them the finger before ruthlessly stomping on JayRok. He then climbs the top rope and elbow drops JayRok on the floor below. Ray walks to the ropes and tags Pain while he is brawling with JayRok. Meanwhile Culver and Banshee have seen what’s been going on and try to hit the ring. The ref gets in Banshee and Culver’s way for a few seconds, and is subsequently pushed aside. The referee falls into the corner and hits his head, knocking the referee out. They grab Ray Wright and execute a two man belly to back Suplex, hurling him over the top ropes onto the floor below. Banshee gets on his hands and knees. Culver bounces off the far set of ropes and uses Banshee as a springboard to hit a frog splash on Ray. Sarkkhan drops to the floor and runs over to help Ray. Banshee runs to help JayRok. JayRok has now got to his feet and is brawling with Pain. The fight moves back into the ring. Pain puts JayRok into a sleeper hold. Banshee comes up behind Pain and puts him in position for an inverted vertical suplex. Pain releases the sleeper. Banshee then hits the inverted vertical Suplex. The crowd goes nuts at this awesome display of power. JayRok and Banshee signal to the crowd. They pick up Pain by the hair and execute the De-Construction, a nasty stunner/Front Face Slam combo. Banshee drags the fallen referee over to JayRok and Pain. JayRok attempts to pin Pain. The referee starts to make a slow count. 1…2…Sarkkhan grabs Pain's legs from the outside and breaks the pinfall! JayRok is unbelievably pissed as is Banshee. Sarkkhan climbs up on the apron. JayRok charges but Sarkkhan drops down and pulls the rope, sending JayRok to the floor. Pain gets up and starts taking Culver out. Srakkhan gets back into teh ring and grabs a prematurely celebratign Banshee from behind. Sarkkhan drops him with in inverted DDT. Sarkkahn covers. 1...2....3! Destruction Inc has beaten the team of Wrecking Crew and Bradley Culver Meanwhile, Culver has fought off Pain and Ray Wright. JayRok climbs into the ring and saves Banshee from a beating by Sarkkhan. They throws Sarkkhan overt he top rope and follow closely. Culver, Banshee and JayRok start to get the upper hand over the duo and start brawling intensely. Pain gets up springboards off the guardrail onto everyone. . All 6 men start fighting all the way back into the dressing rooms. Match 7 - NAC Match: Cactus Funk vs Gnome *"Been Caught Stealing" by Jane's Addiction starts pumping into the arena, and there is a reasonably good pop from the crowd as Cactus Funk makes his way to the ring. He high-fives several fans as he does, climbs through the ropes, and waits for Gnome.* *The French Napoleonic Theme starts playing, and the crowd starts booing. Gnome steps out of the entrance, wearing the NAC Title, and the crowd boos even louder. Gnome takes off the belt and stands on the turnbuckle as he lifts the belt above his head. Cactus gets impatient and drop kicks Gnome in the back.**bell rings** Gnome drops the title to the mat and turns to face Funk and they start eyeballing each other. Cactus shoves Gnome, who shaves back, and Cactus slaps the taste out of his mouth. Gnome laughs at him and tells Funk to do it again. Cactus slaps him, and Gnome just laughs again, which infuriates Cactus. Cactus tries to punch Gnome, but Gnome blocks it and head-butts Funk 3 times in a row. Funk staggers back a bit, and Gnome closelines him to the mat. Gnome tries to lock on an arm bar, but Funk wriggles out and locks on an armbar of his own. The referee threatens Funk with disqualification, so Funk releases the hold and starts kicking Gnome in the ribs. Funk picks Gnome up and whips him into the ropes, and puts him back down with a drop toe hold. Gnome hits the mat face first and hard. Funk picks him up again and attempts a piledriver, but Gnome reverses it and hits a piledriver of his own. Gnome picks him up again, and plants Funk back down with a crucifix slam. Gnome goes for the cover, 1...2...strong kick out by Funk. Funk gets up and kicks Gnome in the gut, and follows it up with a double-arm DDT. Funk grabs him again, and this time hits the piledriver. Funk climbs the turnbuckle and goes for the flying elbow. Gnome tries to get out of the way, but he's too slow and Funk drops the elbow across the back of his knee. Funk picks him up, and gives him a crucifix slam of his own. He goes for the cover, 1...2...kick out by Gnome. Funk looks frustrated and starts complaining about a slow count. Gnome sneaks up behind Cactus and slaps on a sleeper hold. Funk starts flailing his arms about, then starts elbowing Gnome in the side of the head. Gnome releases the hold, and Gnome ends up on the receiving end of a vertical suplex. Funk makes the cover again, 1...kick out. Funk is frustrated and both men are visibly fatigued. Both men get up slowly, and Gnome puts Funk back down with a brainbuster. Gnome goes for the cover, 1...2...3**bell rings** Gnome wins the match and remains the IEA North Atlantic Champion..
