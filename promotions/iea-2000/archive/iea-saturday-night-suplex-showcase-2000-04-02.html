Saturday Night Suplex Showcase

posted 2 April 2000 (billed “4/1/00”)

Saturday Night Suplex Showcase Match 1 - Grudge Match: Deamon vs. Ray Wright *"N' Together Now blares over the speakers. The crowd's show of distaste doesn't lessen up any as Ray Wright comes walking out in color changing spotlight with Pain and Edgecrusher by his side. They stay at ringside. He gets into the ring and waits for Demaon to arrive.* *The lights go out. "Pledge" by ICP starts up, and somehow the basement is flooded by red light. . The crowd remains silent. As the song gets to full speed, a fog fills the basement. When the fog lifts, Deamon is seen standing in the entrance to the basement. The crowd goes nuts cheering for Deamon. . The normal dim lighting is restore and Deamon just stares at Ray. The bell sounds....* Ray and Deamon meet in the middle of the ring. Ray pushes Deamon but Deamon holds his ground. Deamon looks at Ray and kicks him in the gut. Deamon drops an elbow on Ray's back, sending him face first into the mat. Deamon drops a leg across the back of Ray's head. Deamon bounces of the ropes but Pain grabs his leg. Deamon turns his attention to Pain. Ray gets up and low blows Deamon from behind. Ray spins Deamon around and drops him with a twisting neck breaker. Ray bounces off the ropes and drops a forearm on Deamon's head. Ray picks Deamon up and whips him into the ropes. Ray connects with a dropkick that sends Deamon to the outside. Ray tires to climb outside but the ref stops him. With the ref's back turned to them, Edgecrusher and Pain start to beat Deamon down. Edgecrusher picks Deamon up and Pain and EC executes a spike powerbomb on the concrete. They rolls Deamon into the ring and Ray covers him. 1...2... Deamon kicks out. Ray picks Deamon back up and gets kicked in the gut for his effort. Deamon drops Ray with a jumping DDT. Deamon picks Ray back up and vertically suplexes him. Deamon holds on and executes another vertical suplex. Deamon floats over for the pin attempt. 1...2... Ray kicks out. Deamon seems angry and kicks Ray in frustration. Deamon picks Ray up and whips him hard into the corner. Deamon connects with a clothesline. Deamon whips Ray into the opposite corner. Deamon charges but Ray sidesteps him. Deamon crashes shoulder first into the steel post. Ray grabs Deamon from behind and drops his with a side Russian legsweep. Ray lifts Deamon up and punches him several times in the face. Deamon gets dropped with a side slam. Ray covers Deamon. 1...2... Deamon kicks out again. Ray can't believe it. He lifts Deamon back up and drops him with a belly to back suplex. Pain climbs the ropes and distracts the ref. Edgecrusher grabs a chair and throws it into the ring. Ray picks it up and cracks Deamon in the head with it. Ray lays the chair in the middle of the ring. He picks Deamon up and shows him the Wright Way to use the chair. Ray throws the chair out of the ring as Pain drops off of the apron. Ray covers. 1....2....3! Ray Wright has defeated Deamon. EC and Pain slide into the ring and start stomping Deamon. The crowd erupts in cheers as The Warriors make their way to the ring. The get in and start fending off D.I. D.I. gets the advantage. Soon the crowd becomes deafening cheers as Banshee and JayRok make their way out and clear the ring of D.I. W.C. and the Warriors pick Deamon up and help him to the back. Match 2 - #1 Contender to Tag Title Match: Dragon Knights vs. Wrecking Crew *The lights die down, and "Guerilla Radio" by Rage Against The Machine starts pumping in through the speakers. Huge streams of white pyros shoot off at the entrance, and Nick "Sledgehammer" Sloan and JayRok come out. The crowd goes wild as the two make their way to the ring, giving the fans high fives as they go along. Sloan climbs in the ring and JR vaults himself over the top rope as they await their opponents.* *The arena goes totally dark with the exception of blue lasers flashing giving a lightning effect. The entrance to the ring is briefly illuminated by a shower of sparks (music is Braveheart Revenge Theme)* JayRok stay in, and he ties up with Smaug. Smaug chops JR right on the collar bone, and Rok lets go. Smaug wrings JR's arm and starts yanking down on it, causing JR a lot of pain. Smaug turns the wring intoan armbar, and drags JR over to Majere, who tags in and kicks Rok several times in the ribs. Smaug lets go of Rok's arm, and JR flops to the mat. Majere starts stomping on JR's back, then picks him and gorilla press slams him to the mat hard. JR grabs the rope and starts to pull himself up, but Majere presses his throat into the second rope and starts choking JayRok. The referee comes over and starts giving a count for Majere to let go of JR, which he does but only at the count of 8. Rok's face is now a deep purple, and he's choking and gasping for air. Majere picks JayRok up and whips him into the ropes. Majere goes for the closeline, but JR somehow spears Majere, who's head bounces off the mat hard. Both men are now lying on the mat. JayRok is crawling over to his corner, where Sloan is bouncing on the bottom rope with his hand outstrecthed. Rok and Majere both tag there partners in, and here come Sloan and Smaug. Sloan knocks Smaug down with a right. Now he knocks down Majere with a right. Sloan scoops Smaug up, and slams him back down. He grabs Majere, and connects with a DDT. Majere rolls to his corner, and Sloan picks Smaug up. Sloan attempts a Samoan drop, but Smaug wiggles out of it, and locks on the Cobra Clutch. JayRok is trying to get in but Majere cuts him off and throws him over the top rope, where JayRok goes crashing through the announcers table! Smaug and Majere look at each other and nod, and start to set-up for the Death Drop. Smaug climbs to the top turnbuckle and turns around, waiting like a stalking cougar. Majere gets under Sloan, and stands up so that Sloan is seated on his shoulders. Smaug leaps off and nearly takes Sloan's head off as he knocks him off Majere's shoulder. Majere goes for the cover, 1...2...3 **bell rings** The Dragon Knights get the victory, and become the #1 contenders for the IEA Tag Team Titles Match 3 - #1 contenders match to International Title: Pain vs. Southwest Strangla "Southwest Strangla" by the Insane Clown Posse begins to play throughout the arena as Southwest Strangla steps out onto the entrance way. He paces towards the ring paying full attention to whats in front of him as his opponent Pain comes running behind Strangla and lands a huge bulldog, sending Strangla crashing down onto the concrete floor. Pain picks up Strangla and pushes him under the ropes and into the ring.* DING DING DING.....the bell sounds and the match has officially begun. Pain quickly takes control of Strangla landing a few punches to the face. Pain continues to swing away at Strangla as his partner in Destruction Inc, Ray Wright, makes his way to the ringside area. Ray Wright begins to walk circles around the ring as the fight inside the ring begins to progress. Pain sends Strangla crashing down the mat with a huge belly to belly suplex. Pain covers for the pin......1.....Southwest Strangla puts his foot on the ropes. Pain pulls Strangla up to his feet, reaches back and swings at Strangla. Strangla ducks the punch and kicks Pain in the ribs. Strangla sets Pain up for a power bomb and connects. Strangla goes for the cover.......1.......2....Pain kicks out. Strangla picks Pain up and sends him right back down in the middle of the ring with a scoop slam. Strangla climbs to the top rope and leaps off landing an overwhelming frog splash. As Strangla goes for the cover, Ray Wright jumps up onto the apron distracting the ref. Strangla complains as Pain stumbles to his feet. Strangla turns around as Pain kicks Strangla in the stomach. Pain sends Strangla off the ropes and connects with a powerslam. Pain goes for the cover. ....1.......2.....Strangla kicks out just in time. Pain attempts to whip Southwest Strangla into one of the turnbuckles as Strangla reverses the whip sending Pain into the opposite direction knocking down the ref. Ray Wright enters the ring and Strangla knocks him over the top rope with a few hard punches. Pain runs towards Strangla, but Strangla counters and Spinning Heel kicks Pain right in the mouth. Pain is down as Ray Wright enters the ring once again. Ray and Strangla begin to exchange punches as Pain finally gets up. Ray pushes Strangla towards Pain and Pain lands an overpowering Inverted DDT. Pain applies the Dragon Sleeper to Strangla as the ref finally shows some movement. Southwest Strangla taps out as the ref calls for the bell......DING DING DING....The winner of this match by submission....Pain. Pain is now the #1 contender for the International Title. Match 4 - #1 Contender to NAC: Cactus Funk vs. Grim Reaper *"Been Caught Stealing" by Jane's Addiction is pumped through the speakers. Cactus Funk makes his way to a small crowd cheer. He gets in the ring and waits for Grim Reaper* *Smoke fills the arena as "Live Forever" by Oasis starts to play through the sound system. A single white light flashes in the ring. A red spotlight shines on the entrance as Grim Reaper makes his way to a chorus of boos. He gets in the ring and just looks at Funk menacingly.**Bell rings.* The bell rings and both men start punching wildly. Funk lands a solid right to Reaper's stomach that knocks the wind out of him. Funk grabs Reaper by the head and lands several jabs. Fun drops Reaper with a right uppercut. Funk grabs Reaper on his way down and lifts him back to his feet. Funk kicks Reaper in the stomach and nails a piledriver. Funk lands a knee drop on Reaper's face. Reaper rolls on the mat in pain. Funk picks him back up and whips him into the corner. Funk connects with a follow through knee thrust. Funk lifts Reaper up onto the turnbuckle. Funk takes Reaper down with a top rope DDT. Funk covers. 1....2...thr Reaper kicks out. Funk can't believe it. Funk picks Reaper back up and whips him into the ropes. Funk catches Reaper in a powerslam. Funk covers, hooking the leg. 1...2...th. Reaper gets his arm up before the ref's hand hits the mat. Funk gives the ref a look of death. Funk picks Reaper back up but Reaper rakes Funk's eyes. Reaper picks Funk up and executes a back breaker. Reaper lifts Funk up and whips him into the ropes. Funk ducks a clothesline. Funk comes off the ropes again and spears Reaper. Funk straddles Reaper and starts punching Reaper in the face with lefts and rights. Funk gets up and grabs Reaper's arm. Funk drops an elbow on Reaper's shoulder. Funk picks Reaper up and applies an arm wrench. Funk then drops Reaper with a short-arm clothesline. Funk picks Reaper right back up and throws him into the corner. Funk delivers a couple of shoulderblocks to the midsection. Reaper is barely standing up. Funk sits reaper up on the turnbuckle. Funk climbs up to the top rope and brings Reaper crashing down with a superplex. Funk covers Reaper again. 1....2...thr..Reaper gets his shoulder up right in time. Funk screams the ref about counting slow. Funk turns back to Reaper. Funk stomps on Reaper's ribs. Reaper holds his ribs as Funk keeps dropping boots on them. Funk lifts Reaper but and scoop slams him. Funk climbs the ropes. Funk comes off the top rope but Reaper rolls out of the way of an elbow drop. Both men are down. The ref starts to count his mandatory ten count. 1.....2....3.....4.....5....6....Reaper begins to move slowly, but Funk makes it to his feet first. Funk charges Reaper but Reaper drops him with a stiff clothesline. Reaper picks Funk up and executes a pump handle slam. Reaper coves Funk. 1...2.. Funk kicks out. Reaper seems to think he had it won and let's the ref know. Funk gets up while Reaper argues with the ref. Funk spins Reaper around and nails a hard boot to the stomach. Funk lifts reaper up and sends him crashing down with the Crucifix Slam. Funk covers Reaper. 1....2...3! Cactus Funk has won the match and is now the #1 contender for the IEA North Atlantic Championship. Match 5 - #1 Contender's Match to World Title: Ninja vs. Sarkkhan *The arena goes black and "Superbeast" starts playing. Yellow lights flash randomly above the ring with the beat of the synthesizer at the start of the song. When the power chord strikes to start the guitar playing, there is an explosion of pyrotechnics on top of each post, with the flames burning until Sarkkahn enters the ring.* *The old Japanese song starts playing while the lights dim and change to a deep blue color spot light in the middle of the ring (the arena lights are dim, but still on). As the song comes close to an end, he leaps off from somewhere coming down into the ring, the only sound heard is him hitting the ground (even that makes little noise) which takes him to one knee. He rises up to his feet and stares down his opponent, remaining where he is until the bell rings.* *DING DING DING* Bell sounds and Sarkkhan lunges for Ninja who ducks him. Sarkkhan turns around but is met with a spinning heel kick from Ninja. Sarkkhan isn't even phased and Ninja looks amazed. Ninja runs off the ropes and delivers a flying forearm that staggers Sarkkhan. Ninja runs off the ropes again for a flying cross body press but is caught in mid air. Sarkkhan smiles to the crowd then hits a fall away slam. Sarkkhan picks up Ninja by the neck and tosses him into the corner. Sarkkhan looks to the fans for approval then follows up with a running elbow. Ninja avoids the elbow and Sarkkhan is in pain. Sarkkhan walks away holding his elbow and Ninja goes to the top ropes. Sarkkhan turns back to face Ninja but is met with a missile dropkick. Sarkkhan finally goes down. Ninja locks in an ankle lock but Sarkkhan is too big and easily reaches the ropes. Ninja doesn't release the hold until the referee threatens him with disqualification. Ninja then slaps on a choke hold and risks disqualification again. Ninja argues with the referee as Sarkkhan slowly gets to his feet. Ninja turns to face Sarkkhan but is met with a vicious clothesline that sends Ninja over the top rope and down to the floor. Sarkkhan steps over the top rope and climbs down after Ninja. Sarkkhan pulls Ninja slowly to his feet but is quickly sent to his knees with a low blow. Ninja then leaps up to the apron and hits Sarkkhan with a moonsault. Sarkkhan looks hurt and Ninja gets a chair from the time keeper's table. Ninja goes to hit Sarkkhan with the chair but Sarkkhan blocks it, grabs the chair, and tosses it away. Sarkkhan then picks Ninja up by the throat and tosses him back into the ring. Sarkkhan follows Ninja into the ring. Sarkkhan whips Ninja off the ropes and takes him down with a big boot. Sarkkhan picks up Ninja then gorilla press slams him. Sarkkhan goes up to the top rope. Sarkkhan comes down with a big elbow but Ninja rolls out of the way. Both Sarkkhan and Ninja get to their feet. Ninja ducks a big clothesline from Sarkkhan then takes him down with a superkick. Ninja goes for the pin. 1... 2... kickout. Ninja sits Sarkkhan up on the top ropes then hits the Shuki. Ninja goes for the pin. 1... 2... kickout. Ninja picks up Sarkkhan and argues with the ref about a slow count. Ninja goes to whip Sarkkhan into the corner but Sarkkhan reverses it and sends Ninja into the corner instead. Sarkkhan then follows up with a drop kick. Sarkkhan then gets up on the top rope and pulls Ninja up with him. Sarkkhan leaps off the top ropes, powerbombing Ninja in the process. Sarkkhan goes for the pin. 1... 2... 3... DING DING DING... The bell sounds as Sarkkhan pulls out the win. The winner of this bout... Sarkkhan. Sarkkhan is now the #1 contender for the IEA World Title!
