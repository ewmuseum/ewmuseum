Saturday Night Suplex Showcase

posted 8 May 2000; billed “Sunday, May 7th 2000,” Beijing, China
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
IEA - Internationa E-wrestling Alliance   presents   Saturday Night Suplex Showcase Sunday, May 7th 2000 Beijing China   Match 1 - Raremagic.com vs. Mexican pimp     *Jefe Martinez is already in the ring.*     *Strobe lights flash as "Eye of the Tiger" starts playing. Raremagic.com comes out with two beautiful women at his side. He gets in the ring.**Bell Rings*     Kyle Mackal starts the match. Raremagic charges at Jefe and spears him. Raremagic gets up to his feet and begins to stomp him. Raremagic picks Jefe up and whips him off the ropes. Raremagic takes him down with a hard clothesline that spins him in midair. Raremagic follows up with an elbow drop. Raremagic picks Jefe up and drops him with a powerbomb. Raremagic covers Jefe.             1....2... Jefe kicks out. Raremagic can't believe it. He gets up and stomps on Jefe mercilessly. Raremagic grabs Jefe by the hair and pick him up to his feet. Raremagic whips Jefe into the corner and follows with a hard clothesline. Raremagic sits Jefe up on the top rope. Raremagic climbs the ropes and take Jefe down with a top rope superplex. Raremagic picks Jefe up. Raremagic drops him with a Full Nelson slam. Raremagic slaps on a cobra clutch. Jefe immediately taps out. The winner of the match by submission is Raremagic! Match 2 : Ryan Miller vs. Ching Wang Zing     *Ching is in the ring waiting for Miller.*     *The arena stays lit while pyro shoots off all over. An echoing voice over the PA says "It's Miller Time" several times as Ryan runs to the ring slapping hands with the fans*     James Harrison calls for the bell. Ryan Miller and Ching size each other up in the middle of the ring. Ching attempt a spinning heel kick but Miller ducks it. Miller boots Ching in the gut. Miller takes Ching down with a DDT. Ryan picks Ching up and German suplexes. He bridges for the pin attempt. 1..2... Ching kicks out. Miller and Ching get up simultaneously. Ching connects with a chop to the chest. Ching bounces off the ropes but Miller catches him and powerslams him. Miller points to the crowd then covers.     1...2....thr Ching barely gets his arm up. Miller picks a dazed Ching up and whips him off the ropes. Miller backdrops Ching. Miller legdrops Ching across the face. Miller grabs Ching and lifts him to his feet. Miller executes a beautiful t-bone suplex that sends Ching to the outside. Miller does a little strut around the ring. Miller climbs to the outside. Harrison starts his ten count. 1.....Miller picks Ching up and throws him face first into the steel steps. 2.... Miller lifts Ching up and executes It's Miller Time on the concrete. 3.... Miller rolls an unconscious Ching into the ring. Miller gets on the second rope and poses for the crowd. he steps down and covers Ching 1.....2.....3! Miller has won his debut in the IEA! In Ring Interview - The Alliance *Ryan Miller stands in the ring as the lights go down, and the giant start showing clips and images from Warriors, Kid and Miller's matches. It all finishes a sign in blue and white with The Alliance logo. The lights go up.* *The Warriors start coming down, and approach the ring, they climb up, Inka grabs the mic.* Inka: As we said before a revolution is set to erupt here at IEA. Kal: The day has come for every fan around the world, to become stronger than ever. Inka: That's right Kal, we owe ourselves to you people Kal: That's why we have reunited with some old friends, and formed the strongest group ever to hit IEA. Inka: We are proud to present an old friend, a guy who's not afraid of anyone, a man that every night he fights gives 200%, you all know him, and love him here it is The Kid!!! *The Kid comes running down the ramp, slapping hands with the near by fans and also making eyes at the girls. He enters the ring and starts to talk.* Kid: Hey Warriors. Thanks for all of that recognition. You sure are right about us being the strongest group here in the IEA. I think we should now introduce our fourth and final member of this group, that we call the Alliance. Kid: Ladies and Gentlemen without further a do here is the fourth and final member of the Alliance- Ryan Miller. *Ryan grabs the mic. * Ryan: Thanks Kid. It sure is great to be here in the IEA but you know why I'm here and if you don't, you should find out because I'm gonna be the next household name! I would like to take a moment to thank Inka and Kal for introducing me to the prez of the IEA and helping get hired. This truly is a great fed with THE best fans in the world!!! Now, for all the fans sitting in the first ten rows, all the way around ringside, I have something for you...... (Ryan motions to the back with his hand as if calling someone out. four people come out wheeling carts with boxes on them.) Ryan: Now I bet you're all wondering what this is, well, for every fan at ringside tonight, you get a free "IT'S MILLER TIME" shirt, just like I wear, and after the show, I will be here to autograph them for anyone who would like to wait around. And now, it's down to that one last thing that you all know and love to say with me so go ahead, IT'S MILLER TIME!!! (Ryan hands the mic off to Inka and he begins to speak.) Inka: Together we will give you people the best wrestling in sports entertainment, we guarantee that. Kal: And also this goes for every bad guy out there, when you insult the fans or do something wrong beware cause The Alliance will be all over you. Inka: And remember people you're the ones who put the names on our hit list. Kid: You know guys, I think we should put an open challenge to any 4 wrestlers in the back to a 8 man tag team match next Sunday right here in this very ring. What do you think guys? *The four of them look at each other, and nod.* Inka: We will accept the challenge if any of the wrestlers dare to fight us! *The Four stretch hands, and go up the ramp towards the lockers, the giant video screen shows The Alliance Logo* Match 3 - JayRok vs. Diabolus     *The lights dim as "Unforgiven II" by Metallica fills the arena as the crowd gets to its feet. Huge white pyros shoot up into the air and JayRok steps out from the back, sending the crowd into a frenzy. He climbs into the ring as his music fades.*     *The lights stay light, and red sparks shower down into the arena, but Diabolus doesn't step out. The camera pans the stadium and we see Diabolus making his way through the crowd. He slides into the ring, and dropkicks Rok right in the back.**bell rings**     Diabolus starts stomping a whole into JayRok. He kicks him in the ribs, rolling JayRok over, and Diabolus locks on a figure four. Referee Kyle Mackal just stands by and watches, and Diabolus releases the hold. He kicks JR several times in the ribs, then locks on another figure four. Mackal just looks at his fingernails and Diabolus releases again. He waits for Rok to stand up, bounces off the ropes, and knocks JayRok back down with a drop kick. Diabolus gets up, and he has an absolute look of insanity in his eyes, and is in total control of this match. Diabolus once again locks in a figure four, and some of the fans start booing at what seems to be a total lack of action. Diabolus releases the hold and starts kicking JayRok in the ribs again.     Diabolus looks to the crowd and screams "That's It!," and the crowd starts cursing and booing him. The fans start chanting to try and rally JayRok, but it's no use as Diabolus plants him back down with a DDT in the middle of the ring. He climbs the turnbuckle, and lands a guillotine leg drop right across the throat. JayRok is very groggy and slowly staggers to his feet, and Diabolus introduces his foot to JayRok's jaw courtesy of a standing sidekick. He drags JR over to a corner, climbs the turnbuckle, and nails Satan's Plunge, a shooting star press. Diabolus hooks the leg, and Mackal starts to make the count, 1...2...3**bell rings**     The winner of this match via pinfall, Diabolus     Diabolus raises his arms his victory, then leaves the ring as JayRok slowly comes to. JayRok gets up, and stumbles back to his own locker room. Match 4 - Hardcore Rules Match: Strangla vs. Jack Beamer     *The lights slowly fade to black while "My Girlfriend's Girlfriend" starts playing. Suddenly out from the back comes Jack Beamer in his orange jumpsuit with his ankles and wrists in shackles. He clibs into the ring and as referee Kyle Mackal is unbuckling the shackles 4 white pyros blast through the arena.*     Beamer and Strangla are on separate sides of the ring eyeing each other, planning their moves. The bell is rung and Beamer charges Strangla attempting a spear but Strangla manages to move out of the way and roll to the outside. Beamer is following closely, and both men are under the ring searching for weapons. Strangla slides two chairs and a table into the ring, but Beamer has found a Kendo Stick with barbed wire wrapped around the top. They each head back into the ring.     Strangla is setting up a table in the corner with his back turned to Beamer, Beamer takes full advantage as he swings the Kendo Stick right into the back of Strangla. Strangla is screaming in pain as he tries to rip pieces of the barbed wire out of his back. Beamer is going for another swing to Strangla's back, he missed. Strangla kicks Beamer in the gut and picks up one of his chairs, BANG, shot to the skull. Strangla goes for a pin, 1, 2, Beamer puts his leg on the bottom rope.     Strangla picks Beamer up by his hair and throws him over the top rope. Strangla finishes setting up the table in the corner then heads to the outside with Beamer's Kendo Stick. Beamer is trying to pull himself up to his feet with the guard rail, Strangla jumps off the apron and puts all of his weight into the Kendo Stick as he drives it into the back of Beamer's head. Strangla now goes under the ring and pulls out another table and sets it up right on the concrete floor, he then picks up Beamer and shoves him into the ring. Strangla whips Beamer in to ropes, picks him up and does a spine buster through the table that was set up in the corner. He goes for the pin, 1, 2, Beamer kicks out.     Beamer is still laying on the mat, and Strangla puts a chair on the ribcage off Beamer. He goes to the top turnbuckle, BAM, legdrop right onto the chair. Beamer rolls to the outside off the ring in pain. Strangla jumps over the top rope and does a corkscrew cross body to Beamer's already hurting ribs, the crowd is going crazy, and Strangla loves it. Strangla picks Beamer up to his feet and pushes him onto the table that was set up earlier in the match, and heads back into the ring. Strangla is showboating to the large crowd and says he's gonna finish Beamer off. Strangla heads back to the outside of the ring, grabs a ladder, and pushes it into the ring. Strangla sets the huge ladder up near the end of the ring and begins to climb. Once at the top off the ladder he yells and jumps off, he does a 450 splash directly into Beamer's ribs, the table breaks with a loud noise that rings throughout the arena.     Both men are hurting after that move, but Strangla knows he has to finish the match. Strangla grabs Beamer by his hair again and throws him into the ring. Strangla locks in his finisher, the Strangla Death Grip! Beamer instantly taps out. And The Southwest Strangla is the winner by submission! Match 5 - Back Alley Brawl : Ray Wright vs. Banshee         *Both men walk to the alley where a ref is waiting* Banshee is walking around a parking lot in the back of the arena yelling for Wright. Out of nowhere Wright attacks Banshee with a crowbar in hand. Wright picks Banshee up and body slams him on the cement, Banshee gets back up surprisingly fast. Wright swings the crowbar, Banshee ducks out of the way and runs to the top of a near car. Wright drops the crowbar, runs after him, and jumps on the same car. Both men are throwing punches at each other, having little effect. Banshee blocks a left from Wright and gives him a huge forearm, Wright falls off the side of the car. Banshee jumps off the same side and lands a perfect ax handle smash in the skull of Wright. Banshee pulls Wright up and begins slamming his head into the same car, Banshee then whips Wright into the side of a different car. Glass is every where, especially in Wright's back as he bleeds like crazy. Banshee goes into the back seat of the car and pulls out a laptop computer. He slams into face of Wright, HARD, so hard that Wright falls to the ground in the pile of glass he was just in. Banshee picks up Wright and pulls him toward the building, he makes a stop at a near by dumpster, and whips Wright into the front of it. Banshee is looking into the dumpster searching for anything he can use, he comes out with a bottle and a 2x4. Banshee throws the 2x4 into Wright's face, then breaks the bottle on the top of the dumpster and begins carving the forehead of Wright, now he's bleeding from his back and face.     Banshee drops the bottle, and picks up Wright. Banshee is dragging a bloody Wright towards the loading dock area. He finds another dumpster and pushes it near a Semitruck. Wright is starting to get back to his feet and he finds a lead pipe. Wright charges Banshee and begins clubbing the living hell out of him with the pipe. Wright begins stabbing Banshee into his forehead trying bust him open, but Banshee is blocking is face with his life, and gets stabbed a few times in his forearms doing so. Wright throws the pipe and picks up a now very bloody Banshee. Wright begins moving the dumpster near the Semitruck just like Banshee was. Wright then spots a gas container and forgets all about the dumpster for now. He pours the gas all over the concrete and pulls a lighter out of his pocket, he starts a huge fire.     Banshee gets to his feet fast right as he sees the flames, both men are exchanging punches, kicks, forearms, and knee shots. Wright attempts to throw Banshee in the fire but he stops just in time and he starts climbing up the front of the Semitruck. As Banshee climbs, Wright is pouring gas into the dumpster and pushes the dumpster next to the truck. Wright finds his lighter and starts the dumpster on fire!! The flames are HUGE, and Wright begins climbing the front of the Semi. As soon he reaches the top Banshee begins throwing sloppy punches making it easy for Wright to block them. Wright picks Banshee up into a powerbomb but he floats over and gives Wright a powerful low blow. Banshee begins kicking and stomping the kidneys of Wright, Banshee then attempts pushing Wright off the top off the 15 foot truck, but Wright gives Banshee a thumb to the eye. Wright and Banshee go into a standard wrestling grapple with Wright kneeing Banshee in the gut.     Wright clotheslines Banshee them locks in a camel clutch. Wright stops the hold then DDT's Banshee, Wright is very dizzy from losing so much blood, so both men are laying down at the top of the truck. After a few seconds Wright gets up and pulls the antenna off the truck. He tries swinging it into the face of Banshee but he blocks it with his bloody forearms, then rips it from the hands of Wright. Crack! Crack! Crack! Banshee gets three solid hits with antenna before it breaks. Banshee picks Wright up for a gorilla press slam and throws him into the flaming dumpster!!!     The fire goes out right as Wright falls into the dumpster. Banshee can't see Wright through the smoke but that doesn't stop him from doing a frogsplash into the dumpster!! He hit Wright perfectly, and lands in a pinning position, the ref counts, 1, 2, 3. Banshee wins this extremely brutal match, both men were taken to the hospital afterwards. Match 6 - International Title Match: Hades vs. Bradley Culver(c)     *"Headup" by The Deftones starts blasting from the speakers. Hades steps out from the back, carrying a steel chair wrapped in barbed-wire, and the crowd goes nuts. He leaves the chair at ringside as he climbs into the ring.*     *The lights go out and sparks start shooting up in front of the entrance. They part in the middle, and Brad Culver steps out to a huge crowd pop. He makes his way to the ring, eyeing Hades the whole time. He glances at the chair, then gets in the ring.**bell rings**     Hades and Culver stalk each other, and tie up in the middle of the ring. Culver flips Hades up, and plants him down with a Death Valley Driver. Hades gets up quickly, and takes another DVD from Culver. As Hades sits up, Culver again puts him back down with a spear. He lies on the mat motionless, and Culver starts showboating. But Hades was playing possum, and he quickly gets up and nails Brad with a DVD of his own, followed up with an inverted DVD. BC is picked up by the hair, and Hades hits a reverse German suplex.     BC starts to get up, but he is somewhat dizzy from the suplex, and is very unsteady. He turns to face Hades, and Hades turns him inside out with a clothesline. Hades now picks up Culver, hoists him up, and drops him with a Samoan drop near the corner. Hades climbs the turnbuckle again for a senton bomb, but Culver rolls out and takes out referee James Harrison, who ends up taking the bomb. Culver is still unsteady, and Hades grabs his barbed-wire chair. He swings it full force and hits Brad right in the small of the back. He screams in pain and clutches at his back, where small drops of blood are starting to form. Hades picks him up and whips him into the corner. Hades charges at Culver, but is met with a boot to the face. He grabs his face and stumbles a bit, and Culver seizes the opportunity. Brad whips Hades into the corner hard, and as he stumbles backwards, Brad slaps on a cobra clutch. Hades starts flailing his arms, grabs hold of Brad, and flips him over as he slams Culver face down onto the mat. Hades picks Culver straight up and nails a vertical suplex. Hades picks him up again, and Culver hits him with a low blow.     Culver grabs Hades chair and tries to hit him with it, but Hades punches BC right in the crotch, forcing him to drop the chair. Hades kicks the chair out of the ring. He checks on Harrison, who is starting to come to and is getting up. Hades starts kicking Culver in the gut, then climbs the turnbuckle, pulling Brad up with him. Hades lifts Culver up, and hits the top rope brainbuster, From Heaven To Hell! Hades hooks the leg, 1...2...3**bell rings** The winner of this match and new IEA International Champion, Hades. Match 7 - North Atlantic Championship Match: Pain vs. Gnome(c)     *The French Napoleanic theme plays, and the arena is filled with boos. Gnome steps out from the back and lifts the North Atlantic Title over his head. Gnome gets in the ring and stands on each the middle turnbuckle of each post, lifting the title. His music fades out as John Carlile checks him for hidden weapons.*     *The lights dim and "Break Stuff" by Limp Bizkit starts pumping into the arena. Pain steps out, and he gets a surprisingly good pop from the crowd, which even surprises him. He gets in the ring, and Carlile checks him for weapons as the music fades and the lights come up.**bell rings**     Pain and Gnome tie up, and Gnome ties up from behind and hits a belly to back suplex. Gnome is up and starts putting the boots to Pain. Gnome sits him up on the turnbuckle, climbs up, and nails "Aurevoir, Comrade", a top rope crucifix slam. He picks up Pain again, and plants him down again with a powerbomb. Gnome hooks the leg, and Carlile makes the count, 1...2...strong kick out by Pain. The crowd starts to get on Gnome's case, and starts a chant of "You're not Belgian". Gnome yells at them to shut up, and Pain rolls him up with a schoolboy, 1.2.quick kick out by Gnome. Gnome gets in Carlile's face about the quick count, and Carlile backs away from Gnome. As Gnome turns back to face Pain, he1 kicks Gnome in the gut and nails a neckbreaker.     Pain locks in a camel clutch, but Gnome shows some strength and puts his foot on the bottom rope. Carlile does nothing at first, but then starts to count, 1...2...3...4...Pain releases the hold, and Gnome flops to the mat in agony. Pain climbs the turnbuckle now, and crouches like a puma waiting to pounce. Gnome slowly gets to his feet, only to be put back down with a missile drop kick. Gnome starts to get to his feet, and Pain attempts a flying clothesline, but Gnome moves and Pain accidentally nails Carlile.  The crowd starts booing as the Unknown Man comes to ringside wearing his evil clown mask. He gets on the apron and stares at Gnome, who starts yelling and cursing at the unknown man. Pain comes up and nails Gnome with an inverted DDT. The Unknown Man goes over and wakes the referee as Pain is stomping on Gnome.     Pain picks Gnome up and makes Gnome "Feel The Pain." John Carlile wakes up and slowly crawls over to the men. Gnome finally gives up and taps out. **bell rings** The winner by submission and new IEA North Atlantic Champion, Pain.     Pain releases the hold as the Unknown Man starts to make his way to the back. Gnome gets up and gingerly goes after him as the rest of Destruction Inc. comes and celebrates Pain's victory. .
