I am [name withheld], owner and President of the CWF. The CWF is a new e-fed opening with the thought in mind of an e-fed that is fun for roleplayers of all skill levels, not just the very best of the best. A roster of 25 is all that will be accepted, also keeping the fed itself small to allow me to help the less-experienced members with angles, storylines, and roleplays.
As for you experienced grapplers, tons of options round out a fed with great hand-written cards and site design. A dedicated staff heads the operation, and a superb webmaster keeps things running smoothly. Here's a little more information:
=============================================================== 1) Fake wrestlers only. This allows for more interesting angles, growth for the rookies, and more dedication from the handlers. 2) Message board used for roleplaying. Sorry, e-mail champs. 3) One card a week, one every two weeks, and a pay-per-view every month. 4) Japanese-style tournaments every threee months. Some for titles, some for contendership, some just for fun. 5) A seprate roleplay board used specifically for tournaments only. This allows people who are in the tournaments, as well as those not in them, to only read what applies to them.
6) A newsletter sent out once a week to give out site news, fed events, and rumours for both members and fans. 7) Links to other feds and schools for those who get bored *gasp* or for those who need a little extra help. 8) A staff open to ideas and angles, so as to not alienate the wrestlers. ============================================================= For those interested, you can visit the CWF website at: http://www.geocities.com/credibl420/ Or, if you know you want to apply right away, paste this form into an email and send it to:
