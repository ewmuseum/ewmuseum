Roster card — C.M. Vyrus

2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Ten Basic Moves: 1. Knife-edge chest chop 2. European uppercut 3. Tiger Suplex 4. German Suplex 5. Dragon Suplex 6. Frog Splash 7. Sharpshooter 8. Guillotine Choke 9. Gutwrench Suplex 10. Tornado DDT Signature Moves: 1. Carcinógeno ( Fujiwara Armbar ) 2. Biodegradación ( Tombstone Shoulder Breaker ) 3. Cortina de Acero ( Dragon Sleeper ) 4. Pasaje a Infierno ( Spike DDT, sometimes from top turnbuckle ) 5. Doble Hélice ( Diving Corkscrew European Uppercut ) Finishing Moves: 1. Veneno Mortiféro ( Combination STF/Border City Stretch ) Desperation Move: 1. O.R.C.A. ( Emerald Fusion ) Real Name: Martin Stahlmann Ring Name: C.M. Vyrus Hometown: Chicago, IL Height & Weight: 6’0”, 216 lbs Alignment: Tweener Gimmick: Bitter sociopath; living embodiment of the sleaze and corruption of the wrestling industry. For the most part, Vyrus hates everybody, and everybody hates Vyrus—except for most of the fans, who tend to think he’s a total bad-ass. Entrance Music: "A Dagger Through the Heart of St. Angeles" by Alexisonfire Entrance Description: The arena lights start pulsing in time with the pounding opening riffs of Alexisonfire’s “A Dagger Through the Heart of St. Angeles,” signaling the impending arrival of C.M. Vyrus. The buzz of the crowd, a relatively even mix of cheers and boos, rises to a fever pitch as the song approaches the 30-second mark. When the all-out aural assault that is George Pettit’s singing voice hits the speakers, the outline of C.M. Vyrus becomes barely visible amongst the flashing lights up on the stage. A single spotlight illuminates his position, revealing him completely and eliciting a mixed reaction from the crowd. Ignoring this background noise, he storms down the ramp, all business, irritably swatting away the few fans enthusiastic enough to reach out for a high five. The lights return to normal just in time for him to roll into the ring and climb one of the turnbuckle posts, flashing his signature cut-throat taunt at the fans. After carelessly throwing his shirt and shades at a hapless ring crew member, he paces around in his corner, waiting impatiently for the opening bell. Ring Attire: -Black pants with a red biohazard logo on the outside right knee and “C.M. VYRUS” running down the left leg in big red letters -Black boots with red laces -Black fingerless gloves -Black elbow pads with red side stripes -Patterned button-up shirt, worn open (Entrance only) -Aviator shades (Entrance only) Normal Attire: Vyrus’ wardrobe is almost exclusively casual, and all of it makes him look like a sleazy, grungy motherfucker. His style tends to be aggressive, loud, obnoxious, and in-your-face, as if he’s daring those around him to say something about it, just to have an excuse to slap them around a bit. Wrestling Style: Submissions/Lucha Libre Ring Persona: Vyrus is a technical mastermind, using all sorts of chain- and mat-wrestling techniques to wear down his opponent and set them up for a submission finish. He also has a deep lucha libre background, which shows through in his high-octane, daredevil/aerial style, keeping his opponent off-guard and one step behind. Despite his antisocial manner, Vyrus is an excellent tag team wrestler, having accumulated plenty of tag experience over the years with an eclectic assortment of partners. He has a rather violent streak, as well, and is very partial to using a Philips-head screwdriver to tear open someone’s face, but he’s extremely reluctant to use other weapons or hardcore offense unless he can somehow incorporate it into his usual fighting style creatively. Signature Taunt: CM rakes his thumb across his throat and points at his opponent. Title History: - eWo Champion (1) - eWo Tag-Team Champion (1; w/ Ryley Wynters) - MHW Tag Team Champion (1; w/ Eddie Buchalini, Jr.) - BWF United States Champion (1) - [BWF] World Heavyweight Champion (1) - BWF World Heavyweight Champion (Unified) (1) - BWF Tag Team Champion (1; w/ David Blazenwing) - ESW Tag Team Champion (1; w/ Starla Gardevoir) - LWA (Original) International Champion (1) - BWF/LWA Grand Slam Champion - LWA (New) Undisputed World Champion (1) - LWA (New) Crossfire Division Champion (1) History: With nearly ten years of international experience (particularly in Mexico), C.M. Vyrus is a pro wrestling veteran who, thanks to his natural talent and political intelligence, has stayed at or near the top of the wrestling business for most of his career. Infamous for his violent tendencies and antisocial, self-serving attitude towards his fellow wrestlers, Vyrus has burned plenty of bridges, earned more than his fair share of enemies, and created a hell of a lot of mutual animosity between himself and those around him over the years. The hostile environment normally would have been enough to keep Vyrus far away from the Elite Wrestling Organization, but his ego refuses to let him simply walk away from the wrestling business… Hence his decision to jump back into the fray and set his sights on the main event scene once more. Accompanying him into the eWo is Vyrus’ former partner, Ryley Wynters… During the latter half of the Legacy Wrestling Alliance’s existence, Vyrus encouraged Ryley’s darker, more violent side to fully emerge and take control, causing him to set off on a vicious rampage and never look back. Though one can argue that the “experiment” started out as little more than a means to gain power and influence, the two evolved into a serious team soon after it started… Until Vyrus mysteriously disappeared from the pro wrestling scene. Now, it would seem that Nuclear Wynter is back together, and they’ve got more than a few scores to settle—primarily with each other. Will we see them usher in a new era of violence in the eWo side-by-side… Or face-to-face?
