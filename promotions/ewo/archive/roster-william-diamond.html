Roster card — William Diamond

2008

Real Name : William Diamond Ring Name : William Diamond Nickname : WD Hometown : Halifax, Nova Scotia, Canada Height & Weight : 6'2", 232 lbs. Alignment : Neutral, but due to his natural affinity for being an asshole, he leans towards being a Heel. Gimmick : WD's a cocky asshole, but he'll do anything to try and get the fans on his side (And if they don't cheer for him, he'll throw a fit and call them all cocksuckers). He talks tough when he feels relatively safe (he likes to act like a real badass, actually), but he's a coward at heart and his true colors show whenever he's threatened. Also, since he's been doing this for quite a while, he's managed to amass a fair amount of money (he's rich, y'know) and he's had a habit of paying people to do his thinking for him. Thus, he's become somewhat of a dimwit over the years (Read: He's pretty much a fucking retarded child). Entrance Music : "Crazy Bitch" by Buckcherry Entrance Description : "ALLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLL RIGGGGGGGGGGGGGHHHTTTT!" The lyrical opening to "Crazy Bitch" by Buckcherry hits the speakers, and a mere moment afterwards red and gold pyro explodes with a BANG! from the stage, enveloping it in thick smoke. A vast majority of the fans cheer for their hero and savior as a spotlight shines down into the smokey stage. The smoke disburses enough around this time to reveal the figure of William Diamond to the fans, complete with black suit jacket, white dress shirt, solid black tie, and black shorts with a white flame patteren that show off his tanned legs rather nicely. A cocky smirk spreads across WD's face as he surveys the crowd and takes in their cheers (and jeers, even) for a moment. He then explodes in a straight run, down to ringside. Once there, he makes two complete laps on the ring, high fiving fans and hot women alike, before rolling into the ring under the bottom ropes. As if his being in the ring wasn't enough of a photo op, William jumps up onto the turnbuckle and throws his right arm above his head in a gesture ala The Rock. The camera flashes are nearly blinding as he holds this position for a few seconds and then jumps down and starts pacing the ring, waiting for his opponent. Ring Attire : Black suit jacket, white shirt and solid black tie, black shorts featuring a white flame design, black and white Airwalk sneakers (He will not, EVER, wrestle in wrestling boots. He says they hurt his feet). His hands are also heavily taped with black tape. This is done to make up for whatever lack of strength, or even possibly lack of skill, he may have. Normal Attire : Various hoodies, button up shirts, band/video game branded clothing, leather pants (he likes the leather)... Or just boxers. He likes walking around in only boxers. Gets a nice breeze "down there". Wrestling Style : WD loves to use High Flying and Submission moves when he has to ACTUALLY wrestle, but mostly he'd prefer to just go in there and brawl, throw some chairs around, maybe hit a guy in the jewels with a sledgehammer. You know, the usual. Ring Persona : He acts like a real cocky badass. In his mind he's the best, the greatest there ever could be, and he's not afraid to get up in anyone's face and tell them that. It's when they threaten to shut his mouth that his inner coward comes out. He'll talk himself up, but he'll do ANYTHING to get out of an actual fight (a FAIR fight, anyways). He also will do anything to entertain and get the fans on his side, and that includes posing at inopportune times (i.e. when he should be pinning opponents and winning matches). Signature Taunt : His signature cocky smile (trademark pending) IS a taunt. Title History : - SCW World Heavyweight Champion (2) - SCW Tag Team Champion (2; w/ Amik Dearkin, Freedom Morelez) - SCW 24/7 Champion (1) - SCW Intercontinental Champion (1) - SCW United States Champion (1) - FWW World Heavyweight Championship (2) - XWL New Blood Champion (1) - CCW Hardcore Champion (1) - IWF Hardcore Champion (1) - nWo Brutality Champion (1) Superstar History: William Diamond, a man better known as Neo, was born on June 13th, 1980, as the second son to a partly wealthy Detroit family. Jack and Laura Diamond were overjoyed by the new arrival, and quickly seemed to shun their first born, Donny (although there is evidence that they had been shunning Donny from the day he was born). The family was blessed with a daughter three years after William, and they then adopted another, named Jennifer Harte, a little while later. William's life was a normal one through pretty much all of his pre-adolesence years. He idolized his big brother and spent most of his time with his sisters, simply because his brother never seemed to have time for him (William had always suspected that Donny was rather troubled, but never mentioned it). Unfortunately for William, his normal life came to an end soon after his thirteenth birthday. His brother Donny left home to persue a career in the wrestling industry, and his sister Jennifer ran away. This devastated the young William to the point that he always seemed to be quite depressed. But it was nothing compared to what happened next... About three months after Jennifer ran away, the Diamond house caught fire and burned to the ground. William and his sister, Alexia, were lucky and got out in time. Jack and Laura, unfortunately, weren't quite as lucky. They perished in the blaze and left thirteen year old William and his ten year old sister seemingly alone in the world. The two years that followed saw William turn from a happy go lucky goodie two shoes into a confrontational survivalist, because he had to always fight for everything. For food, for his sister, and for himself as well. At the age of fifteen he got the idea that his only shot at living was to find his brother. He then left Alexia with a good friend and set off to join the wrestling industry. He trained in a place called the House of Xtreme, which was a training camp for his first federation, Hardcore Championship Wrestling. A year after training with the likes of Norman Smiley and Jazz, he made his break into the wrestling world by starting as an Opener for the HCW. He stayed there for quite a while and accumulated quite a list of accomplishments before the federation closed down. By that time, however, William had lost sight of his original goal. He was young and eager, and only wanted one thing: Championship gold. After HCW, he started a stint in the SCW. After six months or so in the SCW, William, who had then become known worldwide as "The Franchise" Neo, was swiftly reminded of why he originally broke into the wrestling industry, when his brother, Donny Diamond, showed up. The two immediately engaged in a long and bloody feud that also engulfed Panther (Katrina Diamond, Donny's ex-wife), Supernova (Ron Diamond, the mysterious brother William was never aware of), Jennifer (Who came to SCW to join in on the unholy reunion), and two longtime friends of Donny's, Grem and Shawn Diemond. The toll of this feud was taken on everyone involved, but mostly on Donny and William, who bled gallons of blood and were pushed through hell and back, as well as on their sister, Jennifer, who found out that she was adopted and started an affair with Donny. The feud lasted for about a year and a half before Donny and William broke off from each other. By this time, William was set to marry Jennifer Harte (and he did) and continue his career, which he also did. He hung onto his main event status in SCW until it's closing, and then spent a short time in the TWF before going onto Free World Wrestling, with his wife in tow. He stayed in the FWW until it's closing, which marked the second of William's down periods. Shortly after FWW closed, Jennifer Harte-Diamond informed William that she was leaving him, and taking their son, James, with her. Three weeks later the divorce was finalized and William lost everything, partly to Jennifer and partly to the government. After that, he just gave up on life. William spent his time drowning his sorrows in bottles of hard booze. It became so bad that he wasn't even the same person anymore. His sister, Alexia, eventually found him, but refused to have anything to do with him after only two days because of how much he had changed. A few months passed after Alexia left him completely and utterly alone, and William started to see the light. He cut back on his drinking and "found his smile", so to speak. He then set out to rebuild his life, starting with the most pressing of unfinished business... ... His brother. After the death of his sister, Alexia, he met up with his brother on the top of Angel Falls, the world's largest waterfall, in Venezuela. It was here that Donny brutally beat his brother In the end he crushed his heart (quite literally) and threw his brother's mangled body over the falls. This was the last anyone heard of William Diamond for three days. He then awoke in the wilderness by Denver, Colorado, and found himself to be an emotionless zombie, completely devoid of any feeling (physically, mentally, emotionally). He enlisted his friend, Zak Reynolds (The Enforcer) to help him find the answers as to what he was. A confrontation shortly thereafter with his other brother, Ron, a demon straight from hell, led him to some of his answers, and then to his brother Donny. The two brothers met up seven times, once for every deadly sin they had ever committed, so that one brother may atone, and the other may face all eternity with the burden of their combined sins. During their seventh encounter, Greed, something happened to the two brothers. The end result was William's memory being wiped completely, and his alter ego, Baralai Synclaire, taking over possession of his soul. During this time, Baralai had a struggle of his own, trying to keep his own "other" under control. He failed miserably, and it ended up in the ritualistic slaughter of an entire order of monks. This was a shock to the system of the good Baralai, and it ended up in successfully overthrowing his hold on William's soul. The Franchise was back! After being trapped in his own prison-like mind for months, William wasted no time getting back on top. He got back into his groove, got his mansion in Halifax back, and rejoined the HCW for a brief stint, before the federation collapsed again. Now he's a free agent, on the prowl and looking for work to prove that he's still the best, he's still the legend he once was. Most of all, to prove that he's still "The Franchise"! After making his return in such federations as nWo, EWE, and HSW, The Franchise settled on a rehash of the old Free World Wrestling, FWW. It was here that he was reintroduced to his old nemesis, Freedom Morelez, then known as Stan Greer. Neo found himself in a Universal Title match with Jango Cross, Jake Mtthews, and Hate. Jango Cross ended up winng that match, and this infuriated The Franchise, and put a sort've unspoken hatred between the two competitors. But, The Franchise never got another shot at Jango, as Neo was quckily tangled up in a small feud with Stan Greer, during which he managed to brutalize FWW Underground Champion Faith, and ruin her undefeated streak. The following week, at FWW's second Pay-Per-View, Sex and Violence, Neo showed up and was back to his old self, even lamenting what he did to Faith a week earlier. That didn't help him, however, as he was beaten by Faith in the Underground Title match, and then fired by FWW President Stan Greer. Neo trhen retired, just days before his 24th birthday. After numerious phone calls from his peers, William Diamond reinvented himself and decided that, although Neo is retired, he doesn't have to be. William Diamond was set to bring his unique brand of comedy and mat skills back to the world of Sports Entertainment! ...Unfortunately, William's unique brand of skills didn't take him very far. After parading around HcW as it's General Manager and (self-proclaimed) savior (as well as moonlighting in No Rules Wrestling) for a few months, William's past caught up with him. A former wrestler he assaulted in Canada after a partiularly embarrassing defeat, named Alex Munroe, stepped forward to tell his tale of woe. It wasn't long before another wrestler, named Adam Banta, also stepped forward to claim that Mr. Diamond had assaulted him also, during his time in the Fight Zone. William tried to argue the claims, even going so far as to act as his own lawyer (after he fired his real lawyer because the case was going downhill). None of it helped, and William was convicted of two counts summary assault, and sentenced to a year in a minimum security prison, as well as a $10, 000 dollar fine (Canadian funds). William was outraged and attempted to appeal, but it was denied. So he remained locked up for his full sentence, seething with rage the entire time. He was almost convicted on another count of summary assault during a lunch room brawl, but, fortunately or unfortunately, he dodged that bullet. A year and $10, 000 dollars later, William was a free man once again. He came back to the Wrestling World (though in what capacity, no onewas aware of yet.), and he brought some muscle with him in the form of Memphis, his new bodyguard (whom he met in prison, and formed a bond with). His federation of choice for his return was HcW (Not his beloved HCW, but the one run by nemesis Davey Joeson). He returned on Evolution and immediately started blasting Joeson and HcW's high ranking talent (Co-World Champions Genesis and Anubis Starscream, Number One Contender Rob Johnson, and Pete Ebdon, to name a few). Davey heard enough of WD's tirade and came out and revealed that, not only was William the General Manager, but he was also a wrestler. William was not pleased. His cries fell on deaf ears (and annoyed fans), however, and he was forced to wrestle Hardcore Champion Hiddukel, who brutalized him and pinned him in under three minutes. This upset William even more, and he set out on a crusade to end Hiddukel, even going as far as to enlist several roster members and some outside talent in assisting him to attack Hiddukel and Saint Xover during a match. This all led to an Asylum match at HcW Fever, in which Hiddukel and William faced each other, and the monster XIII. William barely escaped with his life as the moster thrashed both competitors, but Hiddukel was not so fortunate, and now resides six feet under. William was then seen talking with long time arch-rival Criminal, but just who or what was the topic of conversation may never be known, as HcW folded soon after. With no federation to call home, William started taking a few independent bookings, but he also started to focus more on the reason he got started in the business: His brother Donny. Donny had disappeared from the radar after their last encounter, as had all the rest of William's family. His brother Ron is suspected to be working with Donny somewhere, but no one is absolutely sure where or why. His estranged wife Jennifer Harte-Diamond (to whom he was divorced from, and then remarried) and their child, James (who is by now around age two) are nowhere to be found. His old flame and Donny's ex-wife Katrina roams from place to place as a hooker, rumor has it. William wants to see the splintering of his family ended, and he wants to face his brother Donny one final time. It may take all of his strength and will to do so, but William has set out on this quest, and is prepared to follow through. While on his quest for his family, William was contacted by an old friend, Johnny Rose. Johnny propsed that himself, WD, and a man who beat William in HcW, Matt Falcon, try their had at opening a federation themselves. William decided this was a good way to keep himself in the loop in the Wrestling World AND search for his family as well, so he took Rose up on his offer, and was then a co-owner of the Lawless Wrestling Renaissance. William helped recruit a slew of talent for the federation, like "The Man With ALL The Talent" Tony Roars (an old SCW/FWW rival), but nothing he could do helped. Co-Owner Johnny Rose left the federation before it's first Pay-Per-View, Anarchy, and neither William Diamond nor Matt Falcon could keep it all together. It may have had something to do with their bitter rivalry, which when completely unchecked after Johnny left, and spilled over into a sub main event at Anarchy (where William was humiliated and defeated with his own finisher, the Slither DDT). The Lawless Wrestling Renaissance closed it's doors right after the Anarchy Pay-Per-View, and then sold all of it's assets to Steve "Criminal" Whymark's reopened HcW. HcW picked up the contracts of all three LWR owners, as well as a few of its minor stars, and paid to let the rest go. Johnny Rose and Matt Falcon returned to active wrestling duty, but William Diamond took up a managerial role, once again being reinstated as HcW General Manager. Though he originally took the position so that he wouldn't have to wrestle, he found himself being drawn into opening matches at the first two HcW pay-per-views in order to try out new talent. The first match featured WD teaming up with long time rival Criminal against two nameless rookies, who were soundly beaten and never heard from again, and the second featured a humiliating, and pantsless, defeat for WD at the hands of rookie sensation Mike Steele. He also participated in a hardcore bloodbath against Corey Gein for the vacant HcW Hardcore championship, and was beaten half to death for his efforts. Not long after WD's return from his brutal beating, Steve Whymark left the company suddenly. With no backup plan the company stagnated for a month before Corey Gein and Anubis Starscream stepped in to take the reins (WD tried to take over, but everyone felt he was too inept, a fact which still escapes him to this day). WD tried to help out the duo where he could, but despite the hard work the three were putting in, the ship was sinking. Gein and Starscream managed to see the company through two pay-per-views, then left the federation to go on to greener pastures. With only a handful of wrestlers left (and most of them missing shows), WD was forced to close the company down for a third time. Since then, William has taken bookings in several small time companies, including an HcW offshoot, Red Star Wrestling, run by a former HcW mainstay, Mickey Thunder. He also was set to return to the new HcW, with Steve Whymark at the helm once again, but the company folded before the first show. Since then William Diamond has entered and unofficial retirement. He continues to make public appearances but has refused to wrestle, citing that his "love for the business has faded away". He was even reunited with his former love interest and Donny's estranged wife, Katrina Diamond, for a time, but it was brief and she left him once she had liberated a good chunk of his fortune from him. Since then William has given up on wrestling and trying to find his family. He's given up on nearly everything. He spent most of his time shut up in his mansion, with only his butler, Jeeves, for company. His funk lasted for a good two years of nothing but watching cheap porno on pay-per-view and eating himself nearly to death. While channel flipping one day, he managed to catch a glimpse of his brother, Donny, on in a upstart wrestling outfit out of Detroit, along with several other familiar faces from his past. Through the course of the hour long program he was inspired in a way he hadn't been in years. These guys were so good at the business! He even enjoyed watching them (He hadn't enjoyed watching anyone else wrestle since he won the FWW World Title years ago). Watching them wow the fans set a new fire in his heart. He knew he really missed how the fans would scream and yell for him. He knew he wanted to entertain again. It took months of hitting the gym hard and learning to eat... Well, not right, WD never ate right, but to have only two pizzas a day, instead of six, but William managed to get back in shape. Now, to find a company willing to hire him...
