Blackout ( F5 ) Real Name: Paul Herring Ring Name: Paul Herring Nickname: New King Hometown: Hollywood, California Height & Weight: 6’3” 240lbs. Alignment: Heel Gimmick: Rich, cocky athlete. Runs his mouth, but has the ability to back it up. Knows he his better than anyone he will meet in or out of the ring. Entrance Music: "The Clincher" Chevelle Entrance Description: "The Clincher" blasts through the speakers in the arena. 'New King' Paul Herring comes through the entrance, to a mixed-reaction from the audience. Paul gives the audience a cocky grin, as he stands at the top of the entrance ramp. The music continues to build in the arena. Paul uses the ring steps to get up onto the apron. Paul Herring sits on the middle rope and once again looks out into the crowd, pointing to himself before getting into the ring. Paul walks over to the turnbuckle and climbs the second rope, this time pointing to his chest with his thumbs. Chevelle continues to blast in the arena as Paul jumps down and prepares for the match in his corner. Ring Attire: Black and red wrestling tights, elbow pads, and black boots. Normal Attire: Designer jeans and button shirts. Wrestling Style: Technical/Dirty/Opportunistic Ring Persona: Cocky, but does not stop to pose for the fans. He would rather deliver boots or elbows to a downed opponent. Never shows respect for opponents. Signature Taunt: Points to his chest with thumbs. Title History: - n/a History: Paul Herring is a third generation superstar, coming from a large family of wrestlers. Being the most charismatic member of his family, Paul brings a new style of wrestling to the Herring name. Paul’s cocky, brash style often puts him in difficult situations, but his great wrestling ability helps him through. Born in the Sunny Hills of California, Paul has always known wealth and success in his life. Paul received a large amount of money from his family when he moved out at the age of 22, that’s when he began training for a career as a wrestler. Paul was the only member of his family to move away from the family, instead of training in the family facilities. Paul wanted to escape the old-tradition of wrestling and create a new image for the Herring family. Paul’s ego and cocky style was not received well by his family, who stopped coming to his matches after he spit on his opponent after winning his first professional match. Paul has no regret for his actions and has not contacted his parents since that night. Paul continued to improve as a wrestler and gained notoriety in small promotions in the states. Wrestling under the nickname “California Kid” Herring was able to capture several titles in small, independent promotions. In 2004, Paul finally broke into the Main Stream wrestling scene, when he signed with WPW- a large promotion based out of Vegas. The federation was open for two years. During this time, Paul was able to capture every title available and quickly became one of the most hated, but respected superstars in the company. Paul saw a series of endorsements come from his time in WPW, which only added to his impressive bank account. When WPW closed down in the end of 2006, Paul took a break from professional wrestling to star in a movie and appear on various television shows. Now, in the spring of 2008, “New King” Paul Herring returns to professional wrestling- in an attempt to gain international attention. Paul Herring hopes to expand his name throughout the world and finally put himself on the top of the Professional Wrestling World- to show his family and the wrestling world how dangerous the Herring name can be .
