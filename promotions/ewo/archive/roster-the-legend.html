Roster card — The Legend

2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Twenty Basic Moves: - Running Clothesline - Power Slam - Shoulder Thrusts in the corner - Sleeper Hold - Arn Anderson like Spinebuster - Harley Race like running leaping knee to the face - DDT - Brain buster - Gorilla Press Slam - Pump Handle Slam - Full Nelson Slam - Yakuza Kick - Russian leg Sweep - German Suplexes - Chin Breaker - Several Backbreakers - Reverse DDT - Hanging Vertical Suplex - Northern Lights Suplex - Full Nelson Signature Moves: 1. Ball Breaker (Elevated Walls Of Jericho. As The Legend bends him for the Walls, and he places his knee in their back for added pressure.) 2. Tragedy Clutch (Camel Clutch. The Legend takes his forearm and slams it into the opponent’s throat while they are prone on the mat before finalizing the Camel Clutch.) 3. All Falls Down (Fallaway Slam over the top rope. Usually, there is either a table set up or The Legend will throw his opponent into the first or second row of the crowd.) 4. Legend Special ’77 (The Legend grabs his opponent from behind in an elevated double chickenwing. The Legend then arches his back in a quick motion, slamming the opponent’s neck into the mat, sometimes keeping the arms locked for the pinfall.) 5. The Final Fall (Elevated Moonsault. The Legend's elevation is glorious in this moonsault, and he uses this once in a while, only on special occasions.) Finishing Moves: 1. The Requiem (The Legend places the opponent on his shoulders. He then swings them out in front of him, like an F-5, but he then catches them at his side, their head facing down and towards the front of The Legend. The Legend drops them on their neck, and he then lies on their neck for a bit. This is all done very quickly and is one fluid motion.) 2. Red Lottery (The Legend has his opponent on the rope, much like the set up for Randy Orton’s DDT but the opponent’s head is up looking at the ceiling. Legend grabs their head and hits the Test Drive [Spinning Neckbreaker] on the mat. This is a tribute to The Executioner, Danny Allan.) Desperation Finisher: (One Maximum) 1. A Perfect Circle (Legend has his opponent in the corner with the opponent’s back lying on the turnbuckle. He rushes towards them, hitting them with a shoulder thrust in the corner. The opponent comes rebounding out of the corner and The Legend hits him with an Angle Slam into the turnbuckle. After the opponent falls to the mat, Legend immediately locks in the Tragedy Clutch, almost always for the submission win.) Real Name: Anthony Johns Ring Name: The Legend Nickname: The Entity Hometown: Cincinnati, Ohio Height & Weight: 6’2” / 235 lbs Alignment: Super Heel Gimmick: “Fuck You. Pay Me.” Entrance Music: Rock Star by N*E*R*D (Rock Version) Entrance Description: Fuckin' posers! The words blare over the speakers as a loud drum and guitar sequence blast as ‘Rock Star’ by N*E*R*D plays. The lights shine a blue and white color and smoke starts to appear on the stage. Blue waterfall pyro falls from the entrance ramp as a platform rises from the ground. On the platform is The Legend, dressed in his blue and white spat shorts and a blue and white chainmail robe on his body. His mouth is covered by a white bandana as he holds his hands under his nose like he’s praying. The Legend’s eyes dart forward as he moves his hands to make a diamond and the falling pyro turns white as he steps off the platform. The crowd gives him mixed reactions but he doesn’t seem fazed by it. He looks around slowly before making his way down the entrance ramp. The Legend reaches the bottom of the entrance ramp and looks around. Fans boo him and shout things at him but The Legend’s eyes glare at all of them. He makes his way to the steel steps and gets up them quickly and walks along the apron. He places his foot on the second rope and gets on the turnbuckle ala The Rock. The Legend gets on the turnbuckle and throws his chainmail robe off and it hits the ground. He looks around and crosses his arms as the crowd boos at a fever pitch. He moves the bandana from around his mouth to his neck and his devilish smirk can be seen clearly. Legend shifts his weight and sits atop the turnbuckle looks around. He throws the bandana at the referee and the ref puts it in his pocket. The Legend leans back a bit as he waits for the match to begin. Ring Attire: The Legend has on blue spat shorts, with a white tribal design on both sides of the shorts, and the word “Entity” on the side of the pants in white lettering. On his arms are white elbow pads and white taped gloves. His boots are white, with the words “Legend” going down them vertically in a blue. Normal Attire: Anything stylish. One thing about The Legend is that he is one of the most well dressed, stylish people on the face of the Earth. Wrestling Style: The Legend is mostly a hybrid wrestler. He can pull a few submissions here and there, but he doesn’t base his fight on those. He can work the mat and sometimes master the aerial attack. The Legend is very talented in the ring, and he shows it with his altering style. He usually can adjust to the opponent he is facing and get himself into their wrestling style. Ring Persona: The Legend loves to play mind games. He will always try and work a situation to his advantage, even if it is at his disadvantage. Signature Taunt : After the match (if he wins), he'll take the bandana he gave to the ref, wipe his forehead and throw it on his downed opponent. Title History: - eWo Champion (2) - BWF Champion (1) - BWF World Heavyweight Champion (1) - HAW World Heavyweight Champion (1) - MWA Millennium Champion (1) - px:w Television Champion (1) - BWF Hardcore Champion (3) - BWF Tag Team Champion (1; w/ Mike Adams) - DWA Tag Team Champion (1; w/ Mike Adams) History: Upon entering The Legend’s initial federation, Blazenwing Wrestling Federation, Legend lost his first match against another BWF rookie, Kimek. This infuriated The Legend to the point that he became obsessed with facing Kimek again. David Blazenwing then made Legend a challenge... if he could go an entire month without losing a match, he would get his rematch. But if he lost just one match... he would NEVER face Kimek again in a BWF ring! Legend accepted and made it all the way, defeating Tim Murphy via forfeit in the last match at Vertigo 2005 to capture the Hardcore Championship AND his future shot at Kimek! The Legend battled for his Hardcore title over the months leading up to his Triple-Tier Cage Match against Kimek at Over the Edge. He defeated Brandon Beatdown in an extremely one-sided contest at Retribution, then lost the Hardcore title for the last time to Brian Cade shortly before the PPV. Legend entered OTE and waged war in an epic confrontation against Kimek, but in the end, it was interference from Havok's Captain Marticus that would swing the tides in Legend's favor enough for him to win the match. With Kimek out of the way, The Legend was finally free to set his sights on the one thing he wanted more than revenge... the World Heavyweight Championship. Legend spent the month of October battling the likes of Rob Torborg, Mark McNasty and more, all to set himself in line for a shot at the gold. He did so and joined Adriana Garcia, Bam, McNasty and World Champion Torborg in the first-ever Brutal Breakthrough Match, which saw The Legend resort to a chair shot off the top of a ladder on Torborg, leading to the final elimination and The Legend's capture of the World Heavyweight Championship! The Legend got what he wanted, and it was not time to rub it in everyone’s faces the only way he knows how… The Legend way. He showed the likes of Mark McNasty and countless others how to be a Legend, until a former friend and now long time foe Captain Marticus wanted a shot at what Legend had. It was WrestleFest III when Captain Marticus took Legend’s World Heavyweight Championship, pinning Mark McNasty, and ending Legend’s 62 day reign, one day short of beating the record. But Legend was not done there. He wanted revenge on who he thought screwed him out of the World Heavyweight Championship; Chamelion, the special guest referee for the match. The two finally faced off at Broken Resolutions, Legend ready to end the epic undefeated streak of Chamelion’s. Unfortunately, he lost that match, but his momentum never left him. Happenstance proved to be a defining moment in Legend’s career, defeating David Blazenwing in an Ultimate Submission match, after taunting David with kidnapping his wife and torturing her in his own home. An old rival, Brandon Beatdown, attacked Legend after his match with David, thusly sparking the new team of The Legend and Mike Adams – Requiem Ablaze – to take on Brandon Beatdown and Kirlia Gardevoir. Legend wrote the ending to Brandon’s career as he hit a Requiem to him on a trashcan with Kirlia in it! This put Requiem Ablaze on notice, as the next week on Havok, they faced the tag team champions Randall Moran and Phoenix. Requiem Ablaze rose to the heights of champion material, etching them in history as dominant tag champions. However, their reign would end short as David Blazenwing and Captain Marticus would end their 20 day reign at Tainted Twilight in a TLC match. Even though Requiem Ablaze lost, they still dominated most of the match, only to lose with the interference of Ghost (who was never seen again). This, however, sparked a feud of the century between The Legend and The Executioner. The Legend challenged Exie to a match at WrestleFest IV, saying he would “end his beloved streak”. Exie accepted, and continued to tease Legend, even calling commentary on the aforementioned TLC match. Legend, however, had plans for Exie… and the world. Rolling around was Beta Test 2006. The Legend’s debut event. He vowed he would not lose, to Eliminator or anyone else. Eliminator, however, vacated the championship, leaving The Legend and Mark McNasty to face off in the main event for the – then – BWF Heavyweight Championship. Earlier in the night, however, The Legend would announce he was the new president of the BWF, and he thusly fired David Blazenwing, his rival. Legend was then set on beating Mark for the championship. However, a then fired Exie came out and announced he was the special referee in the match, much to the dismay to Legend. The Legend had the match won, but due to interference by David Blazenwing, Mark McNasty walked out of Beta Test the champion. When the BWF came back to fruition, it was time for WrestleFest IV where Legend faced his biggest challenge yet, The Executioner. These two had an intense match, but in the end it was Legend forcing Exie to pass out to his signature Tragedy Clutch to secure his first WrestleFest win and Exie’s first WrestleFest loss. But that wasn’t enough for The Legend. He wanted to prove that he was the greatest wrestler BWF has ever seen. He entered the Elimination Chamber match at Survival of the Sickest, in which The Executioner, Casey Campbell, Eden Turner, Chris Roman, and Cody Clark were apart of. In the end, Legend’s devastating Requiem ended Cody’s run at the BWF Champion, crowning Legend the BWF Champion for the first time in his career. Legend, along with Cody Clark, had grown tired of BWF’s politics and decided to leave. In his last BWF match ever, The Legend defeated AJ Donavon to retain the BWF Championship but was named the first eWo Champion in the process. Legend entered Epic Wrestling Organization as champion. He had the championship since January and had no intentions of losing it, especially to his first eWo opponent, the last LWA Champion Xaria Linette. After going through her, he was faced with a challenge at eWo’s first PPV: Hell & Back. He was to face Czecher, C.M. Vyrus, and James Dean for his eWo Championship. Even with some unexpected motivation from The Executioner, Legend’s 117 day streak as champion was brought to an end as Czecher pinned James Dean to become the new eWo Champion. Legend will continue his run to get back his eWo Championship… no matter what it takes. The stage was set: Legend would face Czecher at Body Count to try and regain his eWo Championship. The match was intense, even causing Legend to throw Czecher into the front row with a fallaway slam and in the end, Legend's new finisher the Red Lottery was enough to regain his beloved eWo Championship. His next victim? The 2007 Body Count Rumble Winner... Erin Jacobs. In the weeks leading up to Supremacy, Erin and Legend did everything imaginable to get the upper hand on each other. From tequila bottle shots to Legend resorting to throwing powder in Erin's face, they wanted to tear each other apart. Finally, the day came. Supremacy was upon them both and while Erin was calm about this match, Legend was like a savage beast. He wanted blood and preferably Erin's blood. The two met in the ring and the action went back and forth with Erin hitting Legend with moonsaults and springboard cutters. During the match, Erin caused Legend's nose to become broken but that didn't stop the current eWo champion. He came back with rough attacks and even hitting a vicious Red Lottery on the announcers table. Erin got Legend knocked down in the ring and was attempting to put him away with the Quickiller. Much to Erin's surprise, Legend got up and showed an amazing vertical leap and countered it to a top rope Requiem that put them both through the ring. The special guest referee, Cody Clark, had no choice but to count the three as Legend emerged from the rubble still the ruler of the eWo kingdom. After the dust settled with Erin Jacobs, The Legend was searching for someone to face. He claimed that he has eliminated everyone in eWo and now he was going on Manifest Entity. He vowed to eliminate everyone in eWo so the company was his to control. Lawrence Jarvis appeared and demanded that he face Legend, since he beat Reno Drake at Supremacy. Legend didn’t mind because he’d already beaten Lawrence before so he figured he could do it again. During this time, The Legend did the unthinkable and aligned himself with his worst enemy: eWo General Manager The Executioner. The two, now calling themselves Manifest Entity, vowed to get power in eWo and run things their way. Between all of this, The Legend happened to snap mentally when Xaria Linette attacked him one week with a chair, further injuring his already broken nose. The next week, in a tag match, The Legend took matters into his own hands. After the match was over, The Legend knocked Xaria’s then boyfriend AJ Donavon out and caused him to have a concussion. This left innocent Xaria by herself. What happened next was too graphic for some. Legend proceeded to force Xaria to bleed profusely, even resorting to requieming her through both announcers’ tables. Legend thought this was the end of Xaria Linette and he seemed to be at peace. The match was set for License to Thrill and once the two got in the ring, everything was crazy. The Executioner accompanied him to the ring, since Reno Drake accompanied Lawrence Jarvis to the ring. Exie and Reno brawled to the back and Alexis attacked The Legend’s wife, Lisa, but this didn’t stop The Legend from retaining his eWo championship yet again. After the match, the lights went out and a figure appeared at the top of the entrance. Throughout the night, there was speculation that this hooded figure was Xaria Linette. No one believed it except for one person: The Legend. Legend called this mysterious person to the ring and the lights went out again and surrounding him were six glitter steel chairs. This confirmed what he had known from earlier in the night and Xaria proceeded to attack him, causing him to bleed. Knowing this feud, the match was made at Guilty As Charged for a Triple Threat Match between Alexis Sykes, Xaria Linette, and The Legend. Going into Guilty As Charged, the three battled for the upper hand. One week Legend would have the upper hand, another Alexis, and another Xaria. The match finally came and through the strong efforts by Alexis and Xaria, The Legend once again emerged as the eWo champion, making history in the process. He is not only the only eWo champion to break a 100 day reign but he is also the only person to do it twice. The Violence after Guilty As Charged saw a match between Alexis Sykes and Xaria Linette and the winner got to face The Legend at the next Violence for the eWo championship. Alexis walked away the winner and after Legend got his victory over Czecher, she charged the ring and sent a strong message to Legend... through a table. She then announced that it would be an ultimate tables match for the eWo championship! Leading up to The New Black, Frank Murdoch won a number one contenders match to make him Legend's opponent at The New Black. Alexis Sykes didn't like this and tried her all to get the eWo title off Legend, even making a eWo Ultimate Tables match for Violence. Legend defeated her in that but Alexis wasn't done. She decided to make herself the special referee in the eWo title match. All in all, Legend's 126 day reign ended when Frank Murdoch became the first man to pin Legend in eWo and becoming the NEW eWo champion. eWo closed shortly after Legend's title reign ended and with that, Legend needed a place to go. He was the hottest free agent in professional wrestling until he found Millennium Wrestling Alliance. He came in and continued Manifest Entity, a federation wide domination, and as Gabriel Gambino, Olivia K., and Heartless McMeanypants found out... The Legend meant business. So much business that he defeated those three in a three week tournament for the vacant Millennium Championship. It didn't take too long for Legend to have gold around his waist once again. The Legend, in later weeks at MWA, was having a difficult time showcasing Manifest Entity. He participated in a Fatal Four Way match which featured a major target of his: MWA World Champion Kacy Knight. Unfortunately, The Legend was not the victor of that match which gave Kacy a one up on him that he was trying to avoid. Legend then participated in a Millennium X match which consisted of Jackie Sweets, CJ Osbourne, and Heartless for his Millennium Championship. In the end, Jackie Sweets ended up dethroning Legend which caused him to become quite irate, even threatening to quit MWA. And so it happened. In a fit of rage and 'no creative direction', The Legend left MWA on bad terms. Whether or not he will return is still in the air. Shortly after he left MWA, he received a call from a good friend of his. Cody Clark. He informed him that his old stomping grounds, eWo, was returning. It feels good to be home.
