Roster card — Freddy Castro

2008

Ten Basic Moves: 1. Dropkick 2. Shooting star press 3. Bronco Buster 4. Moonsault 5. Sharpshooter 6. DDT 7. Spear 8. Powerdriver 9. Lionsault 10. German Suplex Signature Moves: 1. STF 2. Dragon Sleeper 3. Springboard SSP 4. HHH High Knee 5. Spinning Heel Kick Finishing Moves: 1. Castro's Way ( Superkick ) 2. Havanna's Love ( Boston Crab ) Desperation Finisher: 1. Desperate Times ( Leap of Faith ) Real Name: Freddy Castro Ring Name: Freddy "Little Papi" Castro Nickname: Little Papi Hometown: Miami, FL Height & Weight: 6'5'' & 210 Alignment: Tweener Gimmick: Spanish Spice, Pretty much a latin cocky son of a bitch Entrance Music: 'Who's Your Daddy?' by Daddy Yankee Entrance Description: 'Who's Your Daddy?' by Daddy Yankee hits over the P.A. system and a bunch of latina's come dancing on top of the entrance way. They are all waving the Cuban flag. The ladies on top of the ramp hold there flags in a circle and when there is an explosion they move the flags and Freddy Castro is seen standing there with a Cuban cigar in his mouth and a smirk across his face. He starts to dance with a random latina before he spins her around and makes his way down the ramp. He slides into the ring and leaps on the nearest turnbuckle and waves the Cuban flag for everyone in the arena to see. Ring Attire: Tights that are designed to look as the Cuban Flag. (Think Dynamite Kid but with the cuban flag) He has the Iverson arm band thing and the color of it is white. On his hands he'll rock the Rey Mysterio style gloves, on his back is his fully CASTRO back tattoo that really is like the Cuban island with Cuban flavors in it. On his neck he has the names of cats folks wouldnt know. Normal Attire: He'll wear the mordern day hip hop look, but mix it up from time to time with a suit, with match Mafioso style top hat. Always has some sort of a hat on his head. And to top the look off he'l lalways rock a Cuban in his mouth Wrestling Style: A little Luchador mixed with Shawn Micheals. Ring Persona: The fans love Castro, no matter how shady he'll act from time to time, he'll always do something to get the crowed into it. Signature Taunt: Laying across the turnbuckles blowing smoke into the air . Title History: - HIW Welterweight Championship (1) - HIW Tag-Team Championship (1; w/ Nando Castro) - HIW Lightweight Championship (1) History: Freddy is a immagrant who came into this country by crossing over the seas and landing into the harbor of Miami FL. Since a young nino he has always wanted to become a Wrestler, but didnt have the time to try out, since he was the youngest of 8 kids, he had to help his borther's and sisters bring in money any way they could. One day while on trash duty at a local mexican restraunt in Miami. Freddy caught a glimps of a Gym class, and every day after his long work shift he would set outside and watch the class, even started mimicking there moves. It was there a Local promotor gave Freddy Castro his shot in wrestling! Freddy started in AAA. While there, he won the AAA Welterweight Title 5 times and the AAA Light Heavyweight Title twice. While with AAA, Freddy had some major feuds with such future stars of Johnny Nice, and King Tut! Freddy was also a big star in the HIW. He won the HIW Lightweight Title 2 times, the HIW Welterweight Title 3 times, and even won the HIW Tag-Team Titles once with his now dead brother Nando Castro. He has been bouncing around the indey leages for some time, and after a short stint in a indy promotion called BLWA he is glad to be back in eWo.
