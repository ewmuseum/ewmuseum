AJ Epic started eWo back in 2004 with his good friend and business associated Blake Price. Together, they ran the company with AJ as the chairman and Black as the Commissioner. Everything was going great for the company until AJ went missing and without a chairman, Price made the decision to close the company. Weeks later it was discovered that disgruntled, ex-wrestler Scott Royal blamed AJ Epic for his drug problem and his lack of success in eWo. Scott had kidnapped AJ and then lured Dorian Black (who he also blamed for his failure in BWF) to a warehouse. On that night in the warehouse, things got heated, literally . Royal attempted to shoot Black but instead hit a drum of gasoline and the entire warehouse exploded, leaving all three men dead. Roger Steck, like Jerry Tans was the Announcer in px:w but he wasn't Cody Clark's first choice to be the eWo color commentator. Clark was in secret negotiations with the BWF's Rayne, to come to eWo. Rayne didn't want to leave his post in BWF and Clark respected that. After Rayne turned down the offer, Clark offered the job to his mentor and former commentator, Scott Palmer but Palmer said that he was more focussed on training the stars of tomorrow. He offered two people the job and it wasn't until Steck called Clark, begging him for the job that the Epic Messiah decided to reunite the team of Steck and Tans in eWo. Jerry Tans used to be the Television Announcer in px:w, so when Cody Clark re-opened eWo, he felt that Tans was the only logical choice to be the voice of the company. Tans has a vast knowledge of all kinds of wrestling and understands the unique dynamic of each style. He does his research and loves the business, something that Clark admires very much. Clark gave Tans a lifetime contract, ensuring that Steck could never be fired. More recently, Tans has become the acting eWo Chairman, until Reno Drake recovers or a new person emerges from the shadows. Felicia Preston worked for TAW and eWo at one time, so she was often not at one of the federation's events, working bi-weekly. Normally, only wrestlers would get this kind of treatment and freedom but Cody Clark has a soft spot for Felicia, so he allowed it. Since the closure of TAW, Preston has been working for eWo full time and the male viewers aren't complaining one bit. Jasmine Kent was an old girlfriend of Bobby Dynamite's so when eWo opened, Cody Clark gave his brother's love interest a job. The couple has since broken up but Kent proved her worth to the company, so she continues to be an active part of the broadcast team. Dr.Elliot Andrews is a big part of eWo and he has mended all of your favorite injuries since the re-opening of the company. From Cody Clark's concussion at Hell and Back to Legend's broken nose at Supremacy, Andrews has handled it all and with the money he earns, we expect those kinds of results. Vin Detta is an ex-con who once assisted Cody Clark in a bar fight. Vin, despite his size and grim attitude is a nice, honorable man. This is why Clark gave him a job with the company. Vin is always in the thick of things when big brawls need to be broken up and if you try to fight his 'rules', you may end up in Dr. Andrew's office, or worse.
