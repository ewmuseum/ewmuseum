ADMINISTRATION Cody Clark - Chairman The Chairman of eWo is a former four time World Champion, who bought what was left of eWo in 2007. After the initial 2004 run, the company sat, unused. So Clark spent his life savings and bought the company for an undisclosed sum. In Spring 2007 he revived the company and breathed new life into it. Although Clark was deceived by his own parents into signing the company over to (ultimately) Reno Drake, Clark fought to get the company back in a Canadian court. After a six month legal battle, the company was returned to him and now Cody Clark is the sole owner of eWo. Danny Allan - Co-General Manager Danny Allan was appointed the position of General Manager by Cody Clark originally. After Clark's absence from eWo, Allan remained as General Manager under Reno Drake's rule. When Drake was shot and Jerry Tans became acting Chairman, Allan tried to strong arm himself into the acting Chairman role but Tans gave that honor to Bill Dynamite instead. Before eWo closed in late 2007, Allan was over ruled by Bill Dynamite at every turn, making the General Manager irate. Shortly after eWo re-opened, Allan came home to eWo and announced his return to the position of General Manager. But instead of holding that position alone, he decided to appoint his wife, Adriana Garcia-Allan as his Co-General Manager. Adriana Garcia-Allan - Co-General Manager Adriana was in eWo before as a competitor but in this incarnation of the Epic, she holds a power position as Co-General Manager along side her husband Danny Allan. A former World Champion in her own right, Adriana looks as though she'll rule eWo with an iron fist and won't hide behind her husband if someone tries to do something about it. BROADCASTERS Jerry Tans - Face Commentary Jerry Tans is an avid wrestling fan turned commentator. He started his career in Project X: Wrestling watching the likes of Lawrence Jarvis, Cody Clark and Reno Drake come into their own. When eWo re-opened in 2007, Tans was hired by Clark as one half of the commentary team. And although they don't always get along, Tans requested that his long time partner, Roger Steck be hired aswell. Together, these two men called some legendary battles in eWo including Hell and Back 2007's viscious Hell in a Cell match, Supremacy 2007's amazing main event between the Legend and Erin Jacobs and of course, the infamous New Black Tournament. Tans was the acting Chairman of the company for a short period of time, but after being threatened by Danny Allan, he handed that title over to Bill Dynamite. Now Tans is back, serving as the conscience voice of eWo, the way he likes it. Roger Steck - Heel Commentary Roger Steck models himself after early Jerry Lawler. He feels a deep connection to the supposed 'bad guys' of eWo and often cheers on their cheating, ultraviolent ways. He often insults and disagrees with his partner Jerry Tans, but there is an unspoken respect between the two of them. This was apparent when Tans' physical well being was threatened by Danny Allan. Steck was very vocal about his anger towards Allan, something that many didn't expect but they fully understood. Jasmine Lee - Interviewer Jasmine is a former eWo Interviewer he was rehired due to her skill and love of this sport. She often flirts with her male subjects, but nothing has come of any of these events, yet. Billy Weston - Interviewer Weston is a new interviewer on the eWo scene who is determined to break the stereotype of wimpy, weak male interviewers in wrestling. But we know that many of the eWo Superstars will put him in his place. The Voice - Ring Announcer He's the veteran voice of eWo. He makes both Buffer's look like amateurs. 'Nuff said.
