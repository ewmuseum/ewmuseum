Also, we would like to congratulate Elvis Jagger Abdul Jabbar and Skye Cambridge, who have been promoted recently to the active roster and will make their televised debuts on this week's episode of Violence. 08.27.07 - Reno Drake & Cody Clark License To Thrill Live! At the start of the week heading towards License To Thrill, Reno Drake announced from the eWo offices today of three new acquisitions to the eWo roster. We would all like to welcome Charles Walls, who was signed to a full contract to the active roster moments after his dark match on Violence this past week. Also, he's announced that Elvis Jagger Abdul Jabbar, Adam Price, and Skye Cambridge have all been signed developmental contracts to eWo, making their debuts in dark matches at License To Thrill.
