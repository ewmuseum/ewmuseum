Roster card — Ryley Wynters

2008

Ten Basic Moves: 1.Skytwister Press 2.Superkick 3.Top-Rope Neckbreaker 4.Swinging Neckbreaker 5.Reverse Stunner Neckbreaker 6.Springboard Dropkick (while opponent is sitting on ground) 7.Wheel Kick 8.Springboard DDT 9.Springboard Suplex (opponent is standing facing ring on the ring apron; Wynters then puts one foot down on the second rope, applying as much pressure as he can, before finally putting his other foot on the rope and causing himself and his opponent to slingshot into the middle of the ring, completing a suplex 10.Dragonrana Signature Moves: 1. Blyzzard ( Double Underhook Sit-Out Bomb ) 2. Chemical Dreams ( Border City Stretch ) 3. The Order of Things ( A wheel kick, followed by a springboard dropkick, finished by a standing double stomp to the chest. ) Finishing Moves: 1. Hysteric ( Sunset Driver ) Desperation Finisher: 1. Vindication Mark III ( 450 Splash onto an opponent with a chair placed on top of them. ) Real Name: Riley Winters Ring Name: Ryley Wynters Hometown: Melbourne, Australia Height & Weight: 5'11", 209 lbs. Alignment: Heel (Violent)/ Neutral (Apathetic) Gimmick: Split Personality Entrance Music: "Hysteria" by MUSE Entrance Description: "Hysteria" by MUSE hits the PA system as blue lights flash on and off from the stage. As the guitar fully kicks in, Ryley Wynters emerges and stalks his way into the ring, completely ignoring everyone around him. He slides in and stays laying down, waiting for his opponent to enter the ring while staring up at the ceiling with his hands behind his head. Ring Attire: Black tights with red blood splatters on them. Normal Attire: Jeans, t-shirt, leather jacket, all kinds of sharp and potentially dangerous accessories. Wrestling Style: Ultraviolent/Mind Games Ring Persona: Ryley will, first and foremost, go for the kill at any given time. As such, he generally focuses on whatever body part he happens to get a good shot in at first and continues to pinpoint it relentlessly, although he can easily get distracted and attack other parts too. Ryley will occasionally do things to annoy the opponent, like slide out of the ring and turn his back on them, and this is usually when he's at his least aggressive. Signature Taunt: If blood happens to be on Ryley's hands, he'll lick it off in front of the crowd. That's sort of a taunt. Title History: - eWo Commonwealth Champion (1) - eWo Tag-Team Champion (1; w/ CM Vyrus) - eWo Valor Champion (1) - eWo Television Champion (1) [First] - LWA Trans-World Champion (1) History: Ryley Wynters has been a significant presence everywhere he's gone. From LWA to eWo, he's left bodies piled up in the ring and has the blood of many a competitor on his hands. Those who knew him from the time he debuted can agree that he's changed for the worse, extreme in his conviction that everyone is forgotten eventually and even more dedicated to his goal of escaping said conclusion. While his violent side has become more aggressive, wildly seeking an answer that may well not exist, his other side has remained dormant, taking control only outside of the ring and even then beginning to slip into the passenger's seat of his mind. He's had ties to a few superstars before but only one of those remains, and it's the strongest - C.M. Vyrus. What started out as a simple attempt at gaining power in LWA on Vyrus' part instead became a pairing that instilled fear in all who saw them. Wynters had finally found an outlet and then...Vyrus left. Disappeared. Alone and full of rage, Ryley became a wreck and proceeded to turn every opponent he faced into one as well. After the folding of eWo, Wynters disappeared from the scene completely, only now returning - and even worse, it seems Vyrus has returned as well. No one knows why they're back or how it happened, but they can all agree on one thing - terrible events are going to unfold, and it's only a matter of time before blood is spilled again.
