Roster card — Xaria Linette

2008

Basic Moves: 1. Chops (Backhand, Kesagiri, Overhead, Forearm, Mongolian.) 2. Spinning headlock elbow drop 3. Three headbutts in a row (Also makes Xaria dizzy.) 4. Shining Wizard 5. Muta elbow 6. Somersault corkscrew legdrop 7. Missile dropkick 8. 450 splash 9. Shooting star press 10. Frog splash Signature Moves: 1. Rain-BOOM! (Busaiku knee kick) 2. Hyper Jolt (European uppercut) 3. Graceful Impact (Superkick to the back of the head) 4. Total Chaos (Tilt-a-whirl slam; counter only) 5. OBJECTION! (Spiked hurricarana) Finishing Moves: 1. Blinding Colors (Super Shiranui) 2. Angelic Clutch (Fujiwara armbar) Desperation Finisher: 1. Glitter-painted steel chair shot (If the opponent tries to use a weapon, or attempts to injure someone unjustly, she gets the chair out and that person is gonna get hurt.) Real Name: Xaria Veronica Murdoch Ring Name: Xaria Linette Nickname: The Rainbow Angel Hometown: Paris, France Resides: Charlotte, North Carolina Height & Weight: 5’4”, 120 lbs. Alignment: Ultimate Babyface Gimmick: The epitome of love, happiness and mental insanity. Xaria is an angel without wings who can fly without fear. Possessing schizophrenia since the day she was born, her slightly unstable mind divides between hallucination, delirium and psychosis. Regardless of these side effects, she always tries to smile and stay positive, protecting those in need and standing up for serenity and equality. Entrance Music: “If Everyone Cared” by Nickelback Entrance Description: “Fill your hearts with love and peace, for tyranny, absolutism and evil cannot rule us.” Soft spoken, yet powerful words are said by the Rainbow Angel, then the piano begins to play for the introduction of “If Everyone Cared”. When the vocals come to life, the lights dim to darkness and a spotlight that slowly changes color shines on the center of the stage. Xaria Linette steps into the light, clapping her hands to the beat of the song, and the fans cheer and follow in her claps. When the chorus kicks in, Xaria jumps and throws a fist in the air, rainbow-colored pyros shoot off from the stage in four directions and the fans’ cheers grow louder. She makes her way down the ramp, tagging fans hands and praising them with humbleness, stopping for photography and hugs while she circles around the ring once. Reaching out, she grabs the ropes of the ring and bounces onto the apron, flipping over the top rope and landing on her feet. She spins in the middle of the ring and enthusiastically throws her rainbow hat off into the crowd for a lucky fan. She then proceeds to remove her matching earrings and bead necklace, setting them at her corner of the ring, and then hopping with excitement. Ring Attire: Upper body -White low-cut blouse -Orange tanktop with straps (Over the blouse) Lower body -Three silver chain-belts -Yellow star keychain -Blue with yellow and purple star-patterned bell-bottoms -Bright yellow socks -Light pink and blue discolored sneakers Accessories -Rainbow charm bracelets, one around each wrist (Given to her by Kim and Brendan) -Signature golden anklet around right ankle (Reads Xaria + Frank) Entrance only -Rainbow hat -Rainbow earrings -Rainbow bead necklace Normal Attire: If Xaria’s not in the ring, she’s still bright and colorful as always. This usually means casual clothes, a little girly or slightly boyish. When there is a special occasion, she will wear evening gowns, fitting with any bright color. Don’t be surprised if she comes in sporting a rainbow dress! Wrestling Style: Xaria has enhanced her martial arts, thanks to her sensei, and she is twice as fearless when it comes to aerial moves. Her submission skills have improved by working on her physical strength, though it is still a flaw unless an adrenaline rush takes over. Using her supreme agility and high endurance, she looks to wear out her opponents while still having energy left over to take them down. She will never cheat or use dirty tactics to win a match; no exceptions. She is a clean wrestler, fair and square all the way through, even if the opponent proves to be otherwise. Ring Persona: She is very playful in the ring, consistently optimistic and bubbly. To the fans, she is an angel on a quest to right all wrongs and spread love and peace. Even when the odds are against her, she never falters and keeps fighting until the very end, which usually means knocking her out cold. However, there are rare occurrences when there is no time for fun and she is straight-up fierce. This is only triggered by her worst enemies, yet it is not a side of her any smart person wants to initiate in the first place. Signature Taunt: Xaria toe-kicks the mat twice and bounces twice. “Sparkle-Up.” Title History: - eWo Tag-Team Champion (1; w/ Alexis Sykes) - LWA Crossfire Champion (1) [First] - LWA Undisputed World Champion (1) [First] Superstar History: “Most people did not think she could grow wings. Then she learned to fly without them, and those with doubt were in awe.” July 16th, 1986. This was the day Xaria Veronica Linette was born. Accompanying her new life was the mental illness, schizophrenia, with hallucination, delirium and psychosis as the side effects. She heard voices, saw non-existent life forms, interacted with nothing and spoke with alter egos of herself. She possessed multiple personalities from delirium, behaving like an adult one second, and acting like a five year old child the next. Growing up, she was insulted and abused by her father, Manuel Linette because of her estranged mentality and behaviors. When she was five, he left her and her mother, Olivia Linette, alone in poverty. Her mother never gave up on her, however, teaching her how to read, write and play piano, helping her become an optimist. Even through the hardest times, they were inseparably strong, until Olivia died of a fever-like illness when Xaria was ten. The little girl was forced to live out on the streets, alone. No, not alone. She had her spirit friends to aid her, especially the black demon wearing a white mask whom she named “Rai”. Years of vigorous work and self-defense training, contests for money, teaching children martial arts, and most importantly, surviving, went by. Eventually, she had enough money to fly across the ocean to the United States in Charlotte, North Carolina. There she met Alan, a Dojo Master, and his adopted children, Kimberly and Brendan, moving in with them and furthering her training. Around this time, she discovered wrestling and decided to give it a try at age twenty. Her first action in wrestling was in Legacy Wrestling Alliance: Era One. She became the company’s first LWA Crossfire Division Champion, a division that represented barbaric violence and weaponry by Kyle Cross’ primary logic. At first, she refused to use weapons, and so battled her opponents without them. One day, she took up the banner to become Guardian Angel, and in a conversation with Kyle, who became her mentor and father-figure, she was convinced that sometimes, all the skill in the world wouldn’t be enough to fight off evil-doers. That was when she created the glitter-painted steel chair and used it to defend those in need of help, no matter who they were. Eventually, she became the leader of a faction called The Trifecta Angels with Hamza Abdul-Aziz and James Dean. They battled against the opposing faction, Better Than You, which consisted of Scott Free, Nya Roux, Alek McTanner, and Ravage. Xaria and Scott would collide personally in a four-way ladder match, alongside Donnie Hoyland and Swift Nick, for the LWA Undisputed World championship at Lethal Lottery. Although she did not come out on top, on the following night, she won that same championship in a battle royal. But the doors closed and she was shut out before being able to defend her world title for the first time. Soon enough, Xaria joined a newly revived company, Epic Wrestling Organization. Among the roster, she met AJ Donovan at BWF’s Danger Zone. They immediately clicked and could get along rather easily, although it seemed like AJ was being his usual “player” self. During this time, LWA re-opened its doors to Era Two and she was given the opportunity to once again represent the company as the world champion. But before the first show could go on the air, the doors closed once again. It seemed to be a “world title curse”, but Xaria didn’t believe it, continuing her career in eWo. Surprisingly, she joined the Iconoclast with AJ, Reno Drake and Alexis Skyes at BodyCount, but she remained a bright, fun-loving optimist. This proved to be dangerous, as her fellow stable mates went to war against Cody Clark and her long-time mentor, Kyle Cross. She was trapped in the middle, and it was bluntly confirmed by Frank Murdoch. Mentioning Frank, he was placed in a match against ‘Clast member and current boyfriend, AJ. A rivalry sparked between them when he was about to use a weapon on AJ, and Xaria stopped him using the glitter chair, driving it against his head in order to save the Rebel Star. The man formally known as Konkrete Kid was not happy, and as his feud with AJ continued, he poured gasoline into the fire by striking Xaria with her own glitter chair-- in front of AJ-- and surrounding her head in a pool of her own blood. Their war finally came to an end at Supremacy when AJ picked up the win over Frank. But the end of one feud was the beginning of another, as Xaria and the Legend went toe-to-toe after she overheard Legend’s plan to betray her friendship. Their battles were not for the weak of heart. The glitter chair, steel chairs, tables, Requiem, multiple glitter chairs, world championship... for months, it was considered to be a wrestling world war, even when Skyes intervened. In one of these phases, AJ betrayed Xaria’s heart and she snapped. For a month, she became the Explosive Angel, one of her alter egos gone mad, and attempted to dethrone Legend. She was unsuccessful. Internally struggling with herself, she finally regained her true self and was once again the Rainbow Angel. The first night back as her old self, however, turned for the worst when Czecher, old of her friends in the company who had completely lost his mind, knocked her out and took her away. For weeks, no one knew what happened between the two, until Xaria returned, battered and scarred. The night Czecher disappeared with Xaria, he raped her. Angry and depressed, Xaria tried to get revenge in a one-on-one match, but it was no use. The following week, she encountered Ryley Wynters backstage, only to sudden faint in his arms. He took her to Dr. Elliot Andrews’ office, and it was soon discovered that she was pregnant with Czecher’s baby. This little ray of hope was killed when he struck her with Captain Shovel against her stomach. Having lost her virginity and child to the Shovelcidal Maniac, Xaria was going to end her life with a scalpel. Fortunately, she was stopped by a man who seemed to be an unlikely savior, Frank. She emotionally broke down in front of him, and while he attempted to comfort her, he threw caution to the wind and kissed her. It took her by surprise for a moment, but then she smiled and kissed him in return. From that day forward, they spent days and nights together, helping orphans, shopping, dining at restaurants and staying in the same hotels, all while holding hands. It was true love. When eWo suddenly closed before No Return could commence, Xaria and Frank disappeared. On March 1st, 2008, their marriage took place in Toronto, Canada, and Xaria’s new name became Xaria Veronica Murdoch. With the re-opening of eWo, Xaria makes her return with a smile on her face. Let the legacy of the Rainbow Angel begin anew in Era Three.
