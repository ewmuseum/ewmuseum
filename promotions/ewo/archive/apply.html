Epic Wrestling Organization Application Post your application in a new thread in the Almost Area on the forums with the subject being your character's name (i.e. Reno Drake). Fill out completely and correctly (if you can't fill out the app correctly, what makes you think you'll get accepted, ya know?) and make sure to check the SPOKEN FOR thread for taken pic bases as well as the Theme Music Index for taken theme music. As soon as you roleplay for your first match, your bio will be added to the roster. -=PERSONAL=- Preferred Name: E-Mail: -=WRESTLER=- Real Name: Ring Name: Nickname: (i.e. Stone Cold) Height & Weight: Classification: (Heavyweight or Cruiserweight? Cruiserweight is anything under 230 pounds, Heavyweight is anything 231 and over.) Hometown: Attire: Normai Attire - (What they wear outside of the ring.) Ring Attire - (Self explanatory.) Alignment: (Face (Good guy), Heel (Bad guy) or Neutral (either/or)? It's up to you. Also, you can change your alignment anytime.) Associates: (Who in the company is associated with your character. ONLY list managers/valets or stablemates/tag team partners, not every single person that your character knows or is friends with. Only lists the ones that would be regularly interacting with your character on a daily basis. If your character is associated with no one, then just put "None".) Entrance Music: (Check SPOKEN FOR thread.) Entrance Description: (A BRIEF description of your character’s entrance into the ring, meaning don't post the entire lyrics to a whole song in your description.) Fighting Style: (Submission? Brawler? Daredevil? What style are you?) Persona: (How your wrestler acts towards the opponent/crowd/etc.) Basic Strategy: (What is the main strategy that your character uses when in a match? Do they cheat? Do they concentrate on their opponent when on the ground? How do they build up towards getting the advantage?) Ten Moves: (Common moves that your character will use. DO NOT include signature moves or finishing moves in this list and ONLY include ten moves max...no more than that & no less.) Signature Moves & Descriptions: (Five maximum with brief descriptions - No YouTube vids or hyperlinks to other sites as descriptions) Finishing Moves & Descriptions: (Three Max with brief descriptions – at least one Impact move) Desperation Finisher: (A very impacting move that your character will use as a last resort - CAN ONLY HAVE ONE DESPERATION FINISHER) Superstar History: (What did your wrestler do before coming to eWo? And be realistic as well... don't tell us you were a WWI veteran when your character is only 25 years old. NOTE: Superstar History and Title History are NOT the same thing. Tell us a story with your superstar history.) Title History: (Have you ever held a championship title? Tell us all about your titles here, and only REAL titles you have actually held in another e-fed with the character your joining as. Don't make any up to make you sound better than you really are. Believe me, we'll know when you roleplay.) How did you hear about eWo?: Poser: (Check the SPOKEN FOR thread for pic bases that are taken.) Sample Roleplay: (It is required if you've never been here before. MUST BE ONE OF YOUR PAST WORK.)
