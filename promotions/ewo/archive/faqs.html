FAQs

2007
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
1. How many shows do you guys have a month? - eWo holds a show every week with a PPV being held at the end of the month. All the TV shows are held in the Thunderdome in Toronto, Ontario, Canada, while the PPVs are held at different venues all over the world. 2. Does eWo hold house shows? - No, so there should never be any mentioning of a house show anywhere in roleplays or segments. 3. What are the roleplay limits? - There is a two roleplay limit for Violence shows and a three roleplay limit for PPVs. Tag teams have to post one roleplay per character in tag matches, equating two roleplays for that team. 4. What are dark matches? - Dark matches are matches that are not shown on television but are more so to entertain the live crowd before the main show starts. These matches are usually either tryout matches, matches for newcomers, or Do or Die matches where you must roleplay in order to stay on the roster. 5. How do I sign up? - Go onto the forums and register then send in your application to the Almost Area of the forums under the proper application format. 6. What is eWo's version of the TitanTron called? - The EpicTron. Go figure. 7. I just lost my title and now I'm being booked below its level, what's the deal? - The brass feels that you didn't 'run' with your reign and you need more time at a lower level before moving up. Don't take it the wrong way, it's just we feel you can do better and aren't fulfilling your potential. 8. What do I need to know about eWo history to understand new eWo? - Nothing really, except [name withheld] is a douchebag and OOC Drama can kill a fed. 9. What style of e-fed is eWo? - In a basic sense, eWo is a roleplay based fed with wins and losses that will be granted depending on how well you write in your roleplays. This is not, of course, always true as there will be times when the match competitors' roleplays will be really close and we would have to judge through other means like a point system or by means of petition of vote. In situations like this, it is applicable to ask staff why the decision may have gone the way it did as long as you do so in a mature and private manner. Conversely, placement in angles, your overall place on the card and your overall progression towards title shots are based upon your commitment to the fed. Winning alone, in this case, isn’t enough - you only get out what you put in. 10. What is the proper protocol to ask for help with a signature pictures or avatars (also known as "sig series")? - Just go onto the forums under the Graphics Workshop board and send in your request. 11. How do I know no one has taken the song I want to use as my entrance? - Before choosing your entrance music, check the eWo Theme Music Index to see if your theme has been taken by someone else. If it has, then unfortunately, you have to pick another song. It's first come first serve, so if someone else got it first, we can't force them to change it just because you want it. Pretty fair, right? 12. How do I know I can use this pic base of John Doe? What if someone else has it? - It's the same way with the pic bases except this time you check the SPOKEN FOR thread instead of the Theme Music Index. If your desired pic base has been taken, then unfortunately you have to pick another. It's just like the theme music - first come first serve. 13. Are we allowed to do promos in the ring? - Being that there are no shows beyond the Violence shows and the PPVs, there are no in-ring promos in roleplays. Meaning that you must think outside the box and be creative with your roleplays. This eliminates the constant "come-down-to-the-ring-and-trash-talk-in-front-of-a-live-crowd" roleplays that are overwhelming in lots of efeds. This actually gives you a chance to be creative with your character and actually be able to build and develop them into a character that everyone can relate to, love or love to hate. 14. Is it okay for me to just be a spectator and just check things out to see if I'm comfortable with the environment? - Of course, you're more than welcome to sign up and just check things out. However, the only thing we ask is to be respectful to the active members and not cause any OOC drama between them. They pretty much work hard on their writing and it wouldn't be right for someone who's not on the roster to come into the forums and constantly be negative towards them. We're all in this for the fun, so let's keep it fun. 15. Every week I see that the fedhead or the staff is always talking about oncards and strats? What exactly are those? - Oncards are summarized versions of segments that are posted in the results every week. They are usually accompanied by a full segment. The full segments are not mandatory, however, they do help to add more to a current storyline or development of a certain character and also helps really understand what's going on in the scene. - Strats, which is a short version of saying "strategy tips", are methods of strategy which help put your character over or get a certain angle or feud over with the others. They are also means of setting up what your character would do inside of the ring, such as interaction with the crowd, mindset going into the match, certain spots or special moves that you want them to make, etc. These are to help really get your character over as a whole and to help develop your character into a more established character in the fed. 16. What if I'm not booked for a match for the week? Do I just have the week off? - That's up to you. If you really wanna build yourself up into an established character and get your name out there or try to develop a good feud, it would help to at least turn in some oncards or segments for that week so that your character isn't completely off for that week. Like you see on real wrestling television, there may be wrestlers that don't have a match for that night, but still are involved in the show. To the staff and all the other members, it shows consistency and it shows commitment to the fed. 17. Okay, I've finished what i think is a really good roleplay, but I'm uncertain if it will be accepted by the other members. What do I do? - First off, don't fret about it. We're all in this for fun and in eWo, we consider ourselves a second family to one another. If you are uncertain about a roleplay that you've done and just want to hear some positive or negative criticism on it, then just leave a request in the Feedback Thread and you'll more than likely get some feedback. Don't fret if you don't get feedback however. It's hard to really get to read everyone's RPs and leave feedback within a week with everyone pretty much have IRL (In Real Life) things to do, but if you must receive feedback, just ask one of your fellow members and they would more than likely try to help you out or give you some feedback. 18. How often do the titles have to be defended? - eWo Championship = 1 Time Every Month - U.S. Championship = 2 Times Every Month - Valor Championship = 3 Times Every Month - T.V. Championship = Every Week - Tag-Team Championship = 1 Time Every Month 19. When is the weekly deadline for roleplays? Okay, there are two deadlines for the week, the main one and a minor one. The main deadline for every week is Saturdays at 12:00 Midnight CST (check and see what this time is for your time zone). However, the minor deadline is on Fridays at 12:00 Midnight CST for first roleplays of the week. The reason why there is a deadline to put in your first roleplay is because there have been numerous problems in numerous feds dealing with sandbagging (stacking RPs at the last minute all on the same day just to hit the limit and try to get one on your opponent). So to refrain from this, you have to turn in your first roleplay before Friday night at 12 Midnight otherwise you will automatically lose your match by default. [More to be added as time progresses]
