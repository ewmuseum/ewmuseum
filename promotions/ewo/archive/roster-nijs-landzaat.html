Roster card — Nijs Landzaat

2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Ten Basic Moves: 1. Bear hug 2. Headbutt 3. Forearm crossface’s 4. Body slam 5. Bite 6. Big Splash (in corner) 7. Body smash (Albert style) 8. Choke (in corner) 9. Back breaker 10. Suplex Signature Moves: 1. Torture Rack 2. Standing vertical suplex 3. Press slam 4. Doube-underhook suplex 5. Running powerslam Finishing Moves: 1. The Overheersing ( Belly to Belly Suplex into the Corner ) Desperation Finisher: 1. Big Splash ( Top Rope Splash ) Real Name: Denny Landzaat Ring Name: Nijs Landzaat Nickname: The Freak's Freak Hometown: Eindhoven, Holland. Height & Weight: 6’1” & 310lbs Alignment: Heel Gimmick: Daniel Singh (advisor) is an integral part in the success of Nijs Landzaat. He truly is the master tactician behind the development of this freak . However, Singh appears not to be a man of patience and sometimes (ok, a lot..) becomes increasingly frustrated with his investments lack of intelligence. Now Singh is the only man, woman or thing to have any control over Nijs Landzaat, yet sometimes he can’t even control it. Nijs Landzaat is quite frankly retarded. To put it politically correct he is mentally challenged and has bursts of rage that can basically stem from anything. Oblivious to why these things happen, to us and his advisor Daniel Singh, Landzaat persistently struggles with everyday life experiences. Entrance Music: "Sakurasou" by Gackt Entrance Description: The ire sound of "Sakurasou" by Gackt thunders into the arena. The fans are instantly drawn to the sound and the melody is met by a mixed reaction. Odd, considering how much this guy is hated in the ring. Who knows? Maybe it’s purely down to the tune being so enticing that the fans are merely appreciative towards it. Believe it or not, but the smart marks are even heard ever so slightly singing along to the foreign lyrics. Meanwhile, the viewing on the big screen is not your Nijs Landzaat montage, oh no, but rather an array of dolls. Why? God knows. Regardless, as soon as the chorus kicks in both Daniel Singh and Nijs Landzaat enter the fray. The revolt against normality continues, however, as Singh leads the eWo powerhouse out on, exactly that.. a lead. A dog lead to be precise. A orange illumines dog lead to be even more precise. Meanwhile, the smart marks carry on singing into the chorus. This gets increasingly louder as a simple la la la is easier for the people to sing. Combining the small section of noise and that bizarre yet catchy sound; this gives Landzaat’s opponent a distinct chill down their spine. Meanwhile, Daniel Singh releases the Dutchman into the squared circle. Singh screams a whole heap of obscenities among his instructions on how to enter the ring, and with effort, Landzaat simply rolls into the ring. Another instruction is barked from Singh on where Nijs should be positioned. That being said, with effort, Nijs Landzaat stands in his corner opposite his opponent. Normality resumes as "Sakurasou" by Gakt ends. Ring Attire: Landzaat wears cheap, flogged, worn, and ripped long-length jeans from ASDA. He doesn’t wear elbow or knee pads, nor does he wear boots. Normal Attire: Anything ranging from Spiderman pajamas to smart casual. Wrestling Style: Powerhouse/Brawler Ring Persona: Will not engage in a wrestling match without instructions from his advisor, Daniel Singh. Anything Singh says goes, but Landzaat will over complicate things and become confused with seeming simple instructions. We know this as Landzaat will regulary go over to his corner for reassurance and additional instructions. His strengths lye in the shape of power and aggression, whilst his weaknesses are very much his intelligence and speed. He’s not a gimmick, he’s real. Signature Taunt: Generally looking gormless and bemused. Title History: - eWo Freedom Champion (1) - eWo Television Champion (1) History: Daniel Singh secured the services of Nijs Lanzaat on one faithful day when the circus came to town (America, Georgia). A successful businessman by trade, Mr. Singh is always on the lookout for potential moneymaking investments, and when he observed the talent of Landzaat he moved heaven and earth to haggle a deal with the Carney people. Nijs Landzaat was far from a circus clown or a tightrope trapeze artist, however. Oh no, Landzaat was nicknamed “ Hercules ”and we all know about The Herk. That’s right; Landzaat was a sideshow to Jugglez The Clown where he would showcase a freakish amount of strength in weightlifting. Remarkably, on one occasion he lifted a concrete slab with his pee-pee. Nevertheless, at a total cost of $2,000 and a bag of Jojo beans Daniel Singh became the legal guardian to Nijs Landzaat. Being merely a businessman and not a wrestling agent, it was only natural that Landzaat was going to practise in a whole new field of career opportunities. The first opportunity was an audition in the low budget movie, “ Voodoo Moon ”. However, this did not materialize as it became obvious that Nijs couldn’t read. His opportunities didn’t stop there though, oh no, that was merely the beginning. Singh dipped The Dutchman’s toes in football, golf, baseball, sumo wrestling and outrageously.. singing. Yes, you heard me correctly, with a flop single “ Mijn Ballen Zijn Okay ” (“ My Balls Are Ok ”). Whilst failing miserably and Nijs having the brain capacity of a retarded ape, Daniel Singh envisioned a career for Nijs Landzaat in the shape of professional wrestling. Although, I must admit, he did not fail to generate laughter from the Japanese when he removed his sumo belt during an amateur bout. With little next to no wrestling experience Nijs Landzaat signed a rolling contract with the epic Wrestling organization (eWo). Like Singh, chairman Cody Clark was in awe of Landzaat’s presence and appearance. This opted Clark to open up his cheque book and sign him to a contract where Landzaat (or should I say Singh) would be paid per appearance. In the beginning life in eWo proved difficult for Singh as it seemingly felt like it was the reign of Kamala mark two. Regardless, Singh was determined to make a success of his investment and achieved this by aligning Landzaat amongst formidable stable name: EgoMania. In the long haul that proved a shrewd move for Singh as Landzaat went from strength-to-strength. Crowning wins over the likes of the Melissa Charvez’s of the eWo world and ending Erin Jacobs’ mammoth winning streak to match. The communicational barrier between manager and wrestler was suddenly lifted; paying dividends to in-ring performances and results. This sharp brutal form was perfectly capped off with a Television Championship victory. But to quote a certain Tobey Maguire, “ with great power comes great responsibility ” and Daniel Singh was heavily criticized to have none of the latter. Meanwhile pushing for a lucrative contract that would see them fanatically secure, Cody Clark was not prepared to commit. As in-ring performances dipped because of this both Daniel Singh and Nijs Landzaat soon parted with the eWo on a mutual agreement basis.
