The Faceplant ( High Impact RKO ) Desperation Finisher: n/a Real Name: AJ Donavon Nickname: The Rebel Star/ The Trendsetter Hometown: Toronto, Ontario, Canada (Now Residing in Miami, Florida) Height & W eight: 5'10, 225 lbs. Alignment: Heel Gimmick: Simply put, AJ is arrogant, cocky and brash. Although always prepared, he acts like he could care less about his opponents and may crack a couple jokes here and there while he sternly shoots down the competition and lets the world know exactly how it’s going to be. Entrance Music: "The Enemy" by Godsmack Entrance Description: Coming Soon Ring Attire: Coming Soon Normal Attire: Coming Soon Wrestling Style: Hybrid Ring Persona: When it comes down to a match AJ’s basic strategy is to out think, out wrestle and out shine his opponent, so if need be he will pull out some crazy ‘high risk’ moves, in addition to his technical wrestling ability to out wrestle his opponent.(think Alex Shelley’s unique chain wrestling style) Combining all those aspects nobody can tell AJ Donavon that he’s not the fastest rising star. Signature Taunt: coming soon. Title History: - UX Uncensored Champion (1; Current) - UWF Tag-Team Champion (1; w/ Reno Drake) - eWo Valor Champion (1) - eWo Tag-Team Champion (1; w/ Bobby Dynamite) - BWF Happenstance King 2007 - BWF Primetime Champion (1) - MEW Tag-Team Champion (1 x; w/ Bobby Dynamite) Superstar History: Honestly if you don’t know who AJ Donavon is by now your either ignorant or you’ve been living under a rock for the last year. Simply put in every federation AJ has stepped into, he has held singles gold and been a consistent main eventer. Despite being mainly a singles wrestler, AJ has also managed to hold tag team gold three times. In fact he signs back on with eWo while still being the current UX Uncensored Champion. His reason behind entering Da Epic once again, is to look for his first World Championship as he knows he’s got more then what it takes to be the man. In his eyes, he’s simply an uncrowned champion. Look out eWo because AJ Donavon has come to claim that crown.
