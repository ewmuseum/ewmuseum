Violence — card

18 May 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Sunday, May 18th, 2008 from the Thunderdome in Toronto, Ontario, Canada The clock is ticking closer and closer with Hell and Back just one week away and this week shall be no exception when it comes to surprises. Who will be the final members revealed for Team Entity and Team Cross as two of the team members, The Legend and Frank Murdoch, reopen a chapter from Epic's past with a rematch that we won't forget? Who will step up as the number one contender to the Commonwealth Championship to face new champion Erik Dafoe? Speaking of which, will the team of Nick Carroll and Xaria Linette be able to get a head start into War Games with a win over the Commonwealth and Freedom Champions? Will The Takeover send a clear message to the Iconoclast or will the Night Shift prove them wrong? With all of these questions in more, the certainly won't be a Violence that you want to miss. So tune in, Sundays at 9pm GMT. [Tag Team Match] Eden Turner & Nick Toretto vs. The Savage Brothers [Single Match] Andrew Matters vs. Chris Maddox [Tag Team Match] The Night Shift vs. The Takeover Commonwealth Contendership Match [No DQ Match] Juan Ramirez vs. Paul Herring [Tag Team Match] Eryk Adams & Erik Dafoe vs. Nick Carroll & Xaria Linette [Single Match] HIBAKUSHA vs. Jacob Seldon [Single Match] The Legend vs. Frank Murdoch Card Subject to Change
