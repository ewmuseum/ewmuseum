Pure Canadian Driver ( Wheel Barrow Sit-Out Driver ) Real Name : Kyle Cross Nickname : The Guardian Angel, The Scarred Sentinel, The Spirit Of '77 Hometown : Toronto, Ontario Height & Weight : 6'0 & 238lbs. Alignment : Mega-Face Gimmick : Everything Kyle Cross does, he does for his fans. He's often said that regardless of where on the card he's wrestling... be it the opening match or the main event... he wants people to be talking about his match when they leave the arena at the end of the night. Cross is willing to put body, mind and soul on the line everytime he steps between the ropes. He despises heel gang tactics, and will often run-in to the aid of wrestlers who are being ganged up on. This is how he came to be known as 'The Guardian Angel'. Entrance Music : 'Defy You' by The Offspring Entrance Description : Kyle Cross comes out at the beginning of every show dressed in his ring attire and takes his seat with the fans. From there he watches the other superstars from above. When it's time for his match, 'Defy You' starts to play, and Cross makes his way down to the ring. After his matches he usually makes his way back up to sit with his fans. Ring Attire : Black boots, black tights with a silver 'barbed-wire' trim, black wrist, palm & finger tape. Normal Attire : Cross is a real t-shirt and jeans guy. He often wears t-shirts bearing the logo of the various Japanese promotions he has worked for (BJPW, FMW, NOAH, NJPW & AJPW), or bearing the likeness of various Japanese wrestlers (Muto, Misawa, Akiyama, Chono, Lyger, Vader, etc...). Cross is also an avid hockey fan, and can often be found wearing either his own Calgary Hitmen jersey (#22) or a Toronto Maple Leafs jersey. Wrestling Style : Hybrid Canadian Submission & Puro Light-Heavyweight Ring Persona : It's all about the fans. Signature Taunt : Cross doesn't have a signature taunt, but does interact with the fans often during his matches. Title History: - eWo Champion (1) - RXW World Champion (1) [Final] History: Born August 22nd, 1977, Kyle Cross was an exceptional athlete throughout his childhood, and a junior hockey sensation in his teens, destined for the NHL. In 1993, be began playing for the Calgary Hitmen of the Western Hockey League, a team partially owned by wrestler Bret Hart. Cross played well in Calgary, and widely became known as one of the strongest and fastest wingers in the game. A year later, and Cross was already being talked about for the NHL Draft, and after declaring that he was ready to go up to the NHL, speculation ran wild that he would be the number one pick overall. However, just one month before the draft, Cross was injured in a motorcycle accident in Ottawa. Surgery was required, and his left knee was reconstructed. He missed the draft, a fact that devastated him emotionally. Facing months of rehab to his damaged left knee, he felt totally alone. It was during this time that Cross was contacted by Bret Hart, who offered a alternative to straight medical rehabilitation. It turns out that a student at the wrestling school run by two of Bret's brothers had just undergone similar surgery, and Bret suggested that Kyle come to Calgary and the two men could work out together. Cross agreed and flew back to Calgary. During the fall and winter of '95-'96, Kyle Cross and John J. Nightside (the injured wrestling student) went from rehabbing their injuries into full-out wrestling training, joining up with The Hart Brothers Wrestling School that spring. Everyone was surprised at how quickly Kyle picked up the sport, and quickly adapted to the Canadian submission style that the Hart Brothers taught. In September 1994, Bruce Hart worked out a deal that would send Kyle, John and other students from the school to New Japan Pro Wrestling for six months while several junior NJPW superstars would come to Calgary to train at the school. Upon arriving in Japan, the bookers in NJPW suggested that Cross don a mask. Cross agreed, creating 'The Dark Spyder' persona. He participated in many NJPW matches, and during the fall and winter of 1996 worked with some of the best wrestlers in the world, including Ric Flair, Sting, Masahiro Chono, Shinya Hashimoto, Hiroyoshi Tenzan, Mitsuharu Misawa, Toshiaki Kawada, and a man who would play a very important role in his career, Keiji Muto. In the end though, the six month exchange was coming to a close, and it looked like Cross would end up returning to Canada and signing with a small, indy promotion. Then he got a phone call from Atsushi Onita. Atsushi Onita, widely viewed as the father of modern extreme, liked The Dark Spyder, and wanted to sign Cross to an extended contract with his FMW promotion. Cross agreed and was introduced to the brutal world of deathmatch wrestling. During those years, Cross (still known as The Dark Spyder) worked with an eclectic cast of characters, including Onita, Mr. Pogo, Hayabusa, 'The Gladiator' Mike Awesome, Masato Tanaka, Terry Funk, and others... often losing to these successful superstars, but learning from them every step of the way. T.D.S. quickly developed a following in Japan, bouncing back and forth between 'garbage' promotions like FMW & Big Japan. He eventually lost the mask and became known as 'The Guardian Angel' Kyle Cross. Again, he enjoyed moderate success. Kyle has spent the last few years bouncing between NJPW, AJPW, NOAH & Dragon's Gate. Yet Kyle was always looking for the opportunity to cross over to a North American wrestling audience. It would take well over a decade of barbed wire and spilled blood before the opportunity presented itself. Since coming to North America in January 2006, Kyle has wrestled for a number of promotions, including the BWF, LWA, RXW, VICE, eWo and CWC. Now, he has returned to his hometown of Toronto and the eWo, more motivated than he has been in some time.
