eWo returns with the fifth annual Hell and Back pay per view. The pay per view that has been hosted by the FWL, X2W and px:w returns to eWo for it's second time. In the main event, we will see Team Legend take on Team Cross in a four on four war games match! It's one fall to a finish with two rings enclosed in a double sized cage. If The Legend is able to pin Kyle Cross, he will be granted an eWo Championship match. And as long as Jacob Seldon participates, he will get an eWo contract and be able to cash in his money in the back contract. Also in that match, you know that Xaria Linette is going to want to put her issue with Jack Darling finally to rest and Scott Free wants to show the world why he deserves another crack at the title. And Alexis Sykes? Well she makes her in ring return at Hell and Back, wanting to fork some faces and fill some graves. Also at stake are the eWo Tag-Team Championships when the champions, the Iconoclast take on the former champions, the Takeover. Can the Clast continue their reign or will the Takeover win their second Tag-Team titles here in eWo? Tune in to Hell and Back to find out.
