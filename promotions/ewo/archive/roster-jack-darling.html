Roster card — Jack Darling

European Uppercut Signature Moves: 1. Pure Elite Pain ( Single Leg Crab/Twisting Anklelock ) 2. Busaiku Knee Kick 3. Rank Hath Its Privileges [R.H.I.P.] ( Eye of the Hurricane ) 4. Philly Spike ( Cradle DDT ) 5. Shining Wizard Finishing Moves: 1. Death Via Darling ( Cuthroat Driver ) 2. Brain Damage ( Dead Drop Brainbuster ) Desperation Finisher: 1. Just Stay Down ( Ganso Bomb ) Real Name: Jack Darling Nickname: "The Professional Bastard" "The Elite of the Industry" Hometown: Philadelphia, Pennsylvania Height & Weight: 6'2" & 246 pounds Alignment: Heel to the bone Gimmick: Arrogant bastard, to put it bluntly. Jack Darling is rich, talented, and he knows it. He also brags about it, and goes out of his way to get people to boo him. Entrance Music: "Battle Without Honor or Humanity" by Tomoyasu Hotei Entrance Description: The lights go low for the first several seconds of "Battle Without Honor or Humanity" by Tomoyasu Hotei, with a few spotlights sweeping over the crowd. When the three main musical stings hit, the lights flash red in time with them, before a single spotlight illuminates Jack at the top of the ramp. He slowly makes his way down to the ring, pausing every so often to pose for the crowd, especially the ladies, to whom he winks and blows kisses to, before promptly giving the male fans the finger. Each time the three stings hit during the music, the lights flash red. When Jack steps into the ring, it moves to the final stings of the song, as Jack turns a few times as he steps to the middle of the ring before finishing on one knee with arms raised, posing arrogantly. Ring Attire: Jack wears black wrestling tights with a red "hollow heart" (it has a hole in the center) design on the crotch and on the back. The name 'DARLING' is written in red and white letters down the left leg, with a thorny rose twining on the right. His boots are white with black trim, and he wears black wrist tape wound around his hands, wrists, and forearms. Normal Attire: When not wrestling, Jack is commonly found wearing very stylish clothing, usually a suit. Wrestling Style: Jack Darling is a student of the game, and has trained in multiple styles. He was an amateur wrestler in high school, furthered that background with technical wrestling training and European chain wrestling, enhanced his agility studying lucha libre, and finally trained with MMA instructors and toured extensively in Japan to pick up the Strong Style. He primarily works the technical style, and attacks weak points with strong strikes. He's not afraid to take any shortcut to win, either, be it low-blows, weapons, run-ins, whatever. Ring Persona: Jack is always supremely confident in his abilities, and will take every opportunity to taunt his opponent, the fans, and anyone else. Posing and even pausing to jaw with someone in the crowd is common, and anything that will draw the ire of the fans is fair game. Signature Taunt: "Release the Doves." The Randy Orton arrogant pose. Title History: - eWo Freedom Champion (1) [First] History: Jack Darling was born to great things. A scion of the ridiculously wealthy Darling Family of Philadelphia, son of ruthless financier Daniel Darling, and grandson of Irish immigrant boxer "Darling" Mickey Darling, Jack was raised in luxury and comfort, never wanting for anything. While his older siblings went on to become a businessman, a lawyer, and a producer, Jack instead went the athletic route. While in high school, he was a national finalist in amateur wrestling. Everything Jack ever set his mind to came easily to him. Gifted with natural ability, he was bored with living the life of a professional libertine. So he transitioned into professional wrestling, seeking the best training he could find, and traveling the world in search of more styles to adapt into his ever-expanding arsenal. He studied the Hart-inspired technical style in Canada, went to Europe to learn the fluid chain wrestling style prevalent there, toured in Mexico to observe the high-flying lucha libre wrestling, and even did several tours in Japan to further hone his craft, finally adopting aspects of the strong style. In every indie that booked him, he dominated, proving his arrogance was justified. He's not in the business for the money. He's got a near-endless supply from his trust fund and his smart investing. He's in the business for success, fame, and above all, championship gold. Furthermore, he's also got no qualms about pissing off anybody and everybody he comes in contact with, be it staff, wrestlers or the fans. In fact, he goes out of his way to antagonize the fans, believing that the more they hate him, the more they'll pay to see if someone can shut him up.
