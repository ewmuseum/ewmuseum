Version 1.0 Last Updated: December Eleventh, 2000 X-X-X-X-X-X-X-X-X-X-X-X-X-X-X-X-X TABLE OF CONTENTS 1) What is FU57? 1A) Fantasy Reasoning 1B) Real Reasoning 2) Who runs FU57? 3) Where is FU57 based? 4) What is the grading scale? 5) How do I win? 5A) Flashes 5B) On card interviews 5C) Strats 5D) Angles 6) What are FU57's shows? 7) What are the titles of FU57? 8) What are the rules of FU57? 9) Anything Else That I'm Too Lazy To Create a Category For 10) Application X-X-X-X-X-X-X-X-X-X-X-X-X-X-X-X-X 1) WHAT IS FU57? 1A) The Fantasy Reasoning One cold day sitting at his Pittsburgh home in the middle of downtown, one of the most hated wrestlers in the game of wrestling got tired of seeing worthless talent being pushed and past their prime stars because recognized as the best.
He wanted a new way to do things where their were no cliques, no back stabbing and no screwing. This man they call Shawn Ryder.
He took out his millions of dollars and bought some land around the Pittsburgh area. He soon built a warehouse holding a small 500 people, with room to add more. He set up spotlights, a ramp way and two small 27 inch TV's for the videos. He installed a concession stand in the right corner and bathrooms in the left and middle right. He made the small warehouse into a small arena… perfect for his federation!
With the hard work done, Shawn set up to create FU57 and make it the real deal. He decided on the unusual and original name to sponsor his unusual and creative way of cutting deals. He is now posting adds in papers, sending around flyers and calling a few old friends to make this federation happen.
1B) The Real Reasoning Hi, my name is [name withheld] and I've done my fair share of e-wrestling in the two years I've been in it. I've handled numerous characters from real feds to net feds; I've basically done it all. My most known character would be Shawn
Ryder, and that is probably why I've never gotten a federation off the ground. I've done a lot of bad things and pissed a lot of people off, so I left. I tried to hide from the e-wrestling world and let the hate and feelings pass, so far the planned has worked.
So now, I've deiced to put together a kick ass federation and use the ideas that are in my mind. I've created an original name based on my hometown. The
"FU" basically mean nothing, except implying those naughty words 'Fuck you" and the 57 is based on Heinz, which is the man support of life here in Pittsburgh.
FU57 will be a small indy federation with, hopefully, a big following. We will start out with shows in the small FU57 warehouse and then branch out as the months follow. FU57 will never go national but might end up an upscale indy… Sorta like ECW. We will start out without TV coverage, just a video camera capturing the show. And maybe after a few weeks we'll get on public access, then local Pittsburgh networks and finally cable. But it will be a process, a long hard one, but one I think we can easily do.

[detail withheld] . I might also be in the hunt for a VP eventually, but definitely not now. I'm fine for now that is. X-X-X-X-X-X-X-X-X-X-X-X-X-X-X-X-X 3) WHERE IS THE FU57 PROMOTION BASED? FU57 is based in my hometown Pittsburgh, PA, branching off into New York,
Ohio, Virginia and Maryland after a few months of kick ass wrestling. We will hold shows in small clubs and bars for the first six months, but mostly the
FU57 Warehouse in Plum, a suburb of Pittsburgh.
X-X-X-X-X-X-X-X-X-X-X-X-X-X-X-X-X 4) WHAT IS THE GRADING SCALE? This could also be tied into the section labeled "How do I win?" but eh, this is more of a separate topic. I first read your maximum of two flashes
(role-plays) and give them a score of 10. If you do two, you can basically double your score. On card interviews can go from 1-3 and strats (strategies)
1-5. The person with the highest point total wins, basically. Their might be a few occurrences where a angle decides the winner but only in a very close matched decided by a few tenths of a point (Yes, I use decimals too!).
X-X-X-X-X-X-X-X-X-X-X-X-X-X-X-X-X 5) HOW I DO WIN? You win by getting the most amount of points, which is done by flashing, strating and typing up on card interviews. Read on for more detail.
5A) Flashes The first being flashes. This is a must for a handler. It's the only thing that the federation is going to see from you, and gets the idea's of your wrestler(s) across to the rest of the federation, and is a huge part in deciding winners, and instrumental in success. The person with a better flash usually wins, but sometimes, even though you have a better flash than your opponent, it doesn't always mean you're going to win. Like for instance, you do a 5 flash and he does a 4. If he does a strat and on card interview, that will beat your one flash. I'm looking to develop each character and make them unique. A flash is just one way of doing it, on cards and strats help also.
5B) On Card Interviews A on card interview is typically called a shoot. It counts for 1-3 point and can be really short. As long as you make a point, its fine. The point of a on card to gain extra points and too get a final word in. You can do these on card interviews; anywhere and sometimes you can do more than one, as long as they build your character and only one talks about your opponent. Like for instance, you do some huge interview in the ring and go back to the back. 15 minutes later you cannot speak again, no matter what.
5C) Strats (Strategies) The last factor is strategies, or strats. This is the big ideal for me. Since if you include spots that is less work for me. If you include a ring entrance,
I don't gotta go hunting for your application. If you include your character's mindset, I can write your match appropriately to fit the character.
Ring Entrance (How you want your character to come to the ring): Attitude/Mindset (What you want your character to do during their match): Moves (A basic list of moves your character normally uses): Spots (Some ideas for moves and such during the match): If I Win…. (What they'd do if they won): If I lose…. (What they'd do if they lost): Other (Other things you want your wrestler to do during the show, match, Etc.): 5D) Angles Yes, angle do come into play, not often of course. If it is a close match, I might look on who needs the win more for the character. If a angle needs set up for a super card, I might take the time to see what angles need to be built more and that might cost you. But I'd of course email you and explain it before going ahead and do it aight?
X-X-X-X-X-X-X-X-X-X-X-X-X-X-X-X-X 6) WHAT ARE FU57'S SHOWS? The shows are fairly simple to start off. FU57'S MIND BLOWING DEVASTATION - A card held every Friday night at 11 pm eastern. The card will normally hold anywhere from 2-5 matches, titles defended if possible. A lot of set up, a lot of angles going on to make up for the lack of matches. The announcers will be president Shawn Ryder and Dixie
Smalls, the lovely American blonde. Tickets will cost around $5.
FU57'S PIRATE CONNECTION - The flash packet sent out every Tuesday and Thursday, the three days after the card (Tuesday) and the day before the card
(Thursday). Strats will be due Wednesday and on cards as late as 5 PM eastern time Friday.
X-X-X-X-X-X-X-X-X-X-X-X-X-X-X-X-X 7) WHAT ARE THE TITLES OF FU57? FU57's Golden Championship - The big daddy title of FU57, the prize everyone wants at. Want this gold? You gotta prove to me you deserve a shot and deserve a chance.
FU57's Top Notch Championship - The second best title, the second best wrestler, usually. Title verse title feuds are normally acceptable but not one person can hold both titles.
