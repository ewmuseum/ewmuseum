Wednesday Wargames October 2 , 2002 Cajundome Lafayette, Louisiana The fans in Lafayette explode as the pyros shoot off and the RAWF logo appears on the RAWFtron. The cameras pan around showing all the signs. You Have Been Outlawed!!! Crush 'Em Crusher!! Welcome Back Dameon!! Crippler - Back From the Dead 2002 Halie - Beautiful & Dangerous Hardcore Awesome!! The fans are deafening as the cameras finally center on the announce position showing both Chad Bordeaux and Paul Marshall. The two men smile and Chad begins to speak. CHAD BORDEAUX: Welcome fans to the Real Action Wrestling Federation's Wednesday Wargames!!! We have one hell of a show for you tonight!! Three awesome matches set for action tonight!! PAUL MARSHALL: We've got two returning RAWF superstars from yesteryear and an Intercontinental match. What more could you ask for? Except maybe some brutality or some T&A. CHAD BORDEAUX: Get your mind out of the gutter! Fans, tonight we start things off with Dameon returning to the ring against Night Stalker. PAUL MARSHALL: That should be a blow out. When is Stalker gonna do some training and actually do some decent in ring work? Then again, with all the ring rust on Dameon, they should be on an equal playing field. CHAD BORDEAUX: Then we have Crippler returning to the ring against J.D. Awesome in a nontitle match. PAUL MARSHALL: Awesome must have some friends upstairs to protect his title from the Crippler. I'm a big fan of the Dead Man. Other wrestlers may claim it, but Crippler is just that. The only man to ever get killed in a match and come back to fight some more. You remember that match Chad? CHAD BORDEAUX: Like it was yesterday. AK and I were sitting ringside as Darksider hung him from the top of the cell with barbwire. It was one of the most horrible sights I have ever seen. PAUL MARSHALL: Well he's back for more. And it's a good thing Darksider turned tail and ran or Crippler might return the favor. CHAD BORDEAUX: Guess we'll never know. But onto other matters, how about our main event tonight? PAUL MARSHALL: It's gonna be a great one. That little hottie Halie's getting a shot at the rookie champ James Outlaw. Wonder if he'll need help to retain his title like he needed it to win it. CHAD BORDEAUX: He could have done it on his own. Crippler only came out after that damn masked man stuck his nose in things. PAUL MARSHALL: Wonder if we'll see him tonight. We gotta find out who he is. CHAD BORDEAUX: Hopefully we'll find out soon. Hold on a sec, I'm getting word something is going on in the back.. The World Champion Brandy Sangria walks in with the title draped over her shoulder. She turns the corner and gets run over with a massive clothesline from the monster Crush. CHAD BORDEAUX: Our Champion has been attacked!! Captain Crush has run her over with a clothesline and he has knocked her to the ground. Crush stomps away on her and then drags her back to her feet. PAUL MARSHALL: I guess we know who the Crusher meant was gonna be his victim of choice this week. He picks Brandy up and lifts her high up in a gorilla press and slams her down on the hard concrete!! CHAD BORDEAUX: Crush is putting the boots to her and finally he drags her back up and whips her, no reversal and the champ sends Crusher face first into the brick wall!! Crush's head bounced off the bricks and he stumbles back into the clutches of Brandy Sangria. She grabs him and nails a reverse neckbreaker taking him down to the floor. PAUL MARSHALL: Brandy dusts off her hands and picks up her belt and continues on her way but Crush is back up and he tackles her from behind taking her down once again. Crusher grabs her by the hair and slams her face repeatedly into the concrete floor. CHAD BORDEAUX: Crush has busted her open and he drags her back to her feet, DDT on the concrete!! Crush has really split her open now! The champ is a bloody mess! PAUL MARSHALL: Crush pulls her up once again and tosses her head first through a door sending her sprawling into an empty locker room. Crush follows her in and picks her up by the hair. CHAD BORDEAUX: Crush slaps the taste out of her mouth and is now bad mouthing her spitting insults in her face but Brandy with a kick to the groin doubles the big man over!! PAUL MARSHALL: Brandy grabs his head and leaps up on a bench and goes for the Divine Rewind but Crush is too strong and he tosses her off of him and into the lockers!! Brandy hit hard and she slides down to the floor. Crush reaches next to her and opens a locker and slams the door into the side of her face. CHAD BORDEAUX: Crush is a monster and he repeatedly slams the door into her face!! This isn't right!! Bulldog onto the floor!!! Outlaw is pounding on the Crusher now!!! CHAD BORDEAUX: Outlaw drags Crush up and tosses him headfirst into the lockers!!! Outlaw has pulled something out of his pocket! He's got brass knuckles!!! PAUL MARSHALL: And now he's laying into the former IC Champ!! Crush has been laid out!! But wait!!! The Masked Man!!! And he has a baseball bat!!! The Masked Man just leveled Outlaw!!! CHAD BORDEAUX: The Masked Man has laid out the Outlaw!!! He throws down his bat and walks out as EMTs rush into the room!!! What the hell is going on tonight!?!? No DQ Match vs. Dameon vs. Night Stalker RING ANNOUNCER: And making his way to the ring Making his RAWF Return Please welcome back Dameon. The Crowd is lukewarm reception for the returning big man. This would be a big win for him as well. PAUL MARSHALL: Come on this isn't a big match at all. CHAD BORDEAUX: You cynical bastard. PAUL MARSHALL: Call the action jackass. CHAD BORDEAUX: Night Stalker is dumbfounded... PAUL MARSHALL: What else is new? Take the shot jack ass. *WHACK* CHAD BORDEAUX: Night Stalker agrees and just waffles the huge Dameon with the kendo stick across the face. The Big man stumbles back but doesn't go down. Night Stalker still has that deer in the head light's stare. PAUL MARSHALL: Dameon shakes off the effects and has a rather crazy look on his face. He screams for another shot and Stalker obliges. *Whack* CHAD BORDEAUX: This is insane. Dameon has taken two incredibly solid kendo stick shots and is still on his feet. He asks for a third and Bam he goes down! Dameon goes down. And is busted open. PAUL MARSHALL: Is this jackass gonna be the first man to lose to Night Stalker ever? The Seat on the chair shot up and the entire device is just dangling from Night Stalker's neck. PAUL MARSHALL: Before Night Stalker can die, Dameon shoot behind and crams him by the head with both hands. CHAD BORDEAUX: Cranium Crusher AKA the Over Dose and HIT NAILS IT Night Stalker could very well be dead folks. Dameon places foot over his fallen foe. PAUL MARSHALL: 1... 2.... 3. Its over. CHAD BORDEAUX: Impressive win by Dameon good to have the big man back on the payroll. Night Stalker should go back to the drawing board. Nontitle Match vs. Crippler vs. J.D.
