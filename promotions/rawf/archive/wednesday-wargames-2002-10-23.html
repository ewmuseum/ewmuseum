Wednesday Wargames October 23 , 2002 Dean Dome Charlotte, North Carolina The fans in Charlotte explode as the RAWFtron lights up. The pyros shoot off and then the cameras pan around the crowd showing off signs. 4 Years of Greatness The Biggest Outlaw Fan Alive Cripple Me Crippler I Love Giants Divine B*&ch or Hot Slut? Darksider 2002 & Forever I was there when BC destroyed Shlate!! The camera continues to pan around and finally comes to rest on the announce position where Chad and Paul are seated. As the camera comes to rest on them, Chad begins to speak. CHAD BORDEAUX: Welcome fans to the Real Action Wrestling Federation's Wednesday Wargames!!!! What a show we had last week!! But this week is fixing to be even better!! Chaos has put together a great card tonight. PAUL MARSHALL: Even I gotta agree with that. I'm actually really looking forward to tonight. It's gonna be great. CHAD BORDEAUX: Fans we're even got start things off well tonight as we're gonna start off with a high profile triple threat. Dameon, Captain Crush and Brandy Sangria. PAUL MARSHALL: Brandy really has her work cut out for her. She's facing two men who are just absolutely gigantic. The odds are definitely stacked against the champion tonight. CHAD BORDEAUX: That they are but that's when she tends to shine. We've got a really explosive match after that as the hated rivals Trever Leiss and Bone Crusher will meet one on one to settle the score. This one has been building for years. PAUL MARSHALL: Two men who absolutely hate each other. Bone has promised revenge while Trever has merely laughed in Bone's direction. Tonight is gonna get ugly quickly and I'm gonna love it. CHAD BORDEAUX: There is definitely no love lost between those two and tonight will show just that. But then we've got one hell of a main event tonight. PAUL MARSHALL: JD asked for it last week and this week he's got it. Maybe he'll learn to be wary of what you ask for, you just might get it. And tonight, he's gonna get it. Crippler and Darksider together for one night only to get their hands on JD and his girlfriend Halie. CHAD BORDEAUX: This should be an interesting tag team match and Awesome is confident he knows what he is doing. He's expecting the Crippler and Darksider team to fall apart with dissension. PAUL MARSHALL: He better pray it does or he's dead meat. Crippler and Darksider are no joke.. CHAD BORDEAUX: I'm getting word that Crippler has arrived in the back. Let's check it out. Camera pans on Crippler who walks down a narrow hallway backstage. The crowd pops for him somewhat. He stops and looks up at the dark figure slightly taller than him. CRIPPLER: Who the hell are y... SMACK! A small club tied to the figures wrist bashes Crippler right above the right eye, busting him wide open as he falls back into the wall, stumbling. CHAD BORDEAUX: That's... That's... PAUL MARSHALL: God no... CHAD BORDEAUX: IT'S DAVID WILSON! With a quick palm thrust Wilson smacks the club into Crippler's abdomen, and then uppercuts him with the club taking him down to the ground. DAVID WILSON: (Bends down to Crippler) Quite a shame it is, to see such "Hard" and "Seasoned" Veterans such as yourself back on RAWF Television... You were all deemed so hardcore, and vicious, but... when I look at you... and see the look in your eyes... I find your nothing better than the others. Just like the other gold mongering whores... it means nothing to you, to have the reputation that you have... the viciousness, and the violence... they mean nothing to you anymore either. Your like Leiss... turned into a soft, warm pile of dogsh!t. Your lust for titles and recognition have gone to your head. You have survived the most gruesome thing anyone has ever had done to them in the RAWF, and what do you do? You parade with it, and use it for a float to take you to the World Championship. Crippler struggles to get up, and WILSON stomps his foot onto his chest holding him still. DAVID WILSON: Easy now... You still have your match tonight... This... is just your wake up call. David Wilson steps off of Crippler, as he struggles up to his feet, but David Wilson is gone. CHAD BORDEAUX: David Wilson is back, and he just assaulted Crippler! PAUL MARSHALL: ..... CHAD BORDEAUX: I don't believe it! Triple Threat vs. vs. Dameon vs. Captain Crush vs. 'The Divine' Brandy Sangria RING ANNOUNCER: The following Nontitle triple threat match is set for one fall! Introducing first, weighing at 420 pounds, Captain Crush!!!! Jesus or a Gun by Fuel begins to play as the monster known as Captain Crush comes onto the stage to take in the boos of the crowd. He waves them off and continues, jumping onto the apron and going over the top rope with ease into the ring. RING ANNOUNCER: His opponent, she is the Real Action Wrestling Federation World Heavyweight Champion, weighing 143 pounds, "The Divine" Brandy Sangria!!!! Highway to Hell by AC/DC blares but is quickly drowned out by the fans disapproval of the as of late arrogant and ungrateful champion of Real Action Wrestling. Brandy saunters to the stage holding her title high above her head, prompting more boos. She heads to the ring and climbs in standing in the opposite corner of Captain Crush. RING ANNOUNCER: Their opponent, weighing in at 385 pounds, Dameon!!!! For Whom the Bell Tolls begins playing and the crowd is on their feet and cheering as Dameon lumbers out onto the stage. Holding an arm up in salute, he quickens his pace to the ring, climbs onto the apron and steps over the top rope into the ring. PAUL MARSHALL: Now does anyone but me find this the least bit unfair that Brandy is in that ring with two freakin' giants? CHAD BORDEAUX: Hey, she somehow got out of putting that title on the line so she can be grateful for that. PAUL MARSHALL: That doesn't make up for the fact that she's outweighed by like 250 pounds at LEAST by both her opponents and 2 feet in height. CHAD BORDEAUX: Perhaps that is why she's rolled out of the ring as the bell sounds and Dameon and Crush come at each other instead. PAUL MARSHALL: This should be some real fast paced action here. CHAD BORDEAUX: Oh shut up! What it will be is VERY hard hitting! PAUL MARSHALL: Crush comes at Dameon swinging a huge clothesline but Dameon is able to duck under it and come up behind Crush. Crush turns and swings a giant right hand. CHAD BORDEAUX: But Dameon catches it with his own giant left hand and returns with a right of his own straight in Captain Crush's face. Dameon repeats and he is backing Crush into the corner! PAUL MARSHALL: Crush taking a beating in the corner now by the slightly shorter and lighter Dameon... then again once you're over the 7 foot mark what the Hell does it matter, you're a freak regardless! CHAD BORDEAUX: Perhaps you'd like to say that to either one of those men's faces? PAUL MARSHALL: That's quite all right really Bordeaux. Crush has his hands full right now anyway as he continues getting a beating at the hands of Dameon trapped against the turnbuckles. CHAD BORDEAUX: Dameon has unloaded on Crush and is now backing up out of the corner... Uh oh! Dameon hits the opposite corner and charges back towards Crush!! PAUL MARSHALL: How would you like to be laying up against the corner and see that coming straight for you? CHAD BORDEAUX: Dameon barreling towards Crush... who brings the boot up and catches Dameon in the face! Dameon was charging full speed ahead and just got beheaded by that boot! Dameon goes down and he may have a bit of trouble getting up! PAUL MARSHALL: Especially with that elbow drop by Crush right down into his chest! The ring shook! CHAD BORDEAUX: Both men down and having a bit of trouble pulling themselves up now and now our world champion sees it fit to slide back into the ring! PAUL MARSHALL: She's an opportunist what can I say? Brandy into the ring and brings a knee drop down into the head of Dameon as he is trying to get up. Crush pulling himself up as Brandy turns and brings a drop kick into his face while he's on his knees! CHAD BORDEAUX: Crush is reeling on his knees now as Brandy jumps back up and comes at his face with another dropkick!! But Crush regains himself and swats her away like a fly before she connects with it and pulls himself to his feet! PAUL MARSHALL: Brandy back up now and charges Crush! He catches her by the throat as she comes at him! No! CHAD BORDEAUX: Chokeslam time! PAUL MARSHALL: But Brandy brings a kick to the groin of Crush and Dameon is back up and he comes up beside Sangria and they clothesline the Huge Captain Crush over the tope ropes to the outside! Crush crashes to the mats outside the ring! CHAD BORDEAUX: Crush is having trouble pulling himself up on the outside. Brandy in the ring now turns to Dameon and gives the big guy a rake to the eyes! Dameon taken off guard and Brandy with a kick below the midsection! PAUL MARSHALL: DDT on Dameon from the World Champion! Brandy turns to the turnbuckle now and hurries to the top! Wait a second! She turns towards the outside, AND FLIES OFF!!! CHAD BORDEAUX: Brandy with a leap off the top and flies down into a recovering Captain Crush! WHAT A COLLISION! PAUL MARSHALL: You can say that again! She completely took Captain Crush out! But at the same time Brandy is a little worse for wear as she tries to drag herself back up and Dameon recovers from the DDT and exits the ring to where Brandy is struggling. Let her get up bastard! CHAD BORDEAUX: No such luck, Dameon grabs Brandy by the hair and yanks her to his feet! Dameon grabs her and gorilla presses the World Champion up over his head! He turns and tosses Sangria right over the top ropes back into the ring! What strength! PAUL MARSHALL: Yeah yeah... CHAD BORDEAUX: Dameon back into the ring and pulls Brandy back up! Brandy breaks free of the big man and begins hitting him in the chest with all she's got. PAUL MARSHALL: Fists of fire by the Champion! CHAD BORDEAUX: And it's not enough as Dameon catches her arm in mid swing and pulls her forward! HUGE short arm clothesline! PAUL MARSHALL: Dameon pulls the champion back up and scoops her for a slam! But Brandy shifts her weight and slips over Dameon's back. Somehow she gets him at the side and takes him back in a HUGE Russian leg sweep! CHAD BORDEAUX: Brandy drags herself to her feet as Dameon pulls himself to his hands and knees. PAUL MARSHALL: And Brandy is ready bring down a scissors kick to the back of Dameon's head! Dameon down and Brandy rolls him over and covers! 1.... 2... CHAD BORDEAUX: No chance. Dameon POWERS out and sends her flying across the ring! PAUL MARSHALL: But Brandy and her tenacity has her jumping right to her feet as Dameon gets up. Brandy charging in for the kill! CHAD BORDEAUX: But instead of finding the kill she finds a big boot and goes down like a ton of bricks! PAUL MARSHALL: But here comes Crush on the apron and he looks pissed! He steps over the top rope and barrels at Dameon from behind! CHAD BORDEAUX: Dameon turns and brings his foot up catching Crush in the gut! Crush doubles over and Dameon grabs him and sets him up... Oh God! PAUL MARSHALL: This will be the biggest powerbomb in history!! CHAD BORDEAUX: Dameon is going to have his work cut out for him trying to get Crush up! A mistake could be a severe injury to either of these wrestlers! Dameon straining like I've never seen anyone strain before and he's lifting Crush up!! PAUL MARSHALL: S--- WHAT A POWERBOMB!!! CHAD BORDEAUX: I'm surprised the ring didn't break! And Dameon has held on to it!! No! He wouldn't do it again! PAUL MARSHALL: He's going to! He's going to pull Crush right back into another Powerbomb! CHAD BORDEAUX: But the World Champion from behind hits a low blow on Dameon undetected by the referee! Damn her! PAUL MARSHALL: Hey you gotta do what you gotta do. CHAD BORDEAUX: Dameon lets go of Crush and Brandy gets him in the side headlock. PAUL MARSHALL: Here comes the Divine Rewind!! Brandy runs and goes up the turnbuckles! She flies off!!! CHAD BORDEAUX: But never hits the mat as Dameon stays in a vertical positions holding Brandy up in the air before tossing her off of him and sending her flying over the top rope to the outside! God that was brutal! PAUL MARSHALL: That son of a bitch! CHAD BORDEAUX: Dameon turns to Crush who is just now fighting to his feet! Dameon comes fro behind and grabs him by the head! Dameon starts to squeeze! PAUL MARSHALL: This will be the biggest Cranium Crush ever!! CHAD BORDEAUX: Dameon lifts him up and slams him down!!! MY GOD WHAT AN OVERDOSE! Dameon covers! 1..... 2..... 3..... PAUL MARSHALL: This thing's over! CHAD BORDEAUX: Dameon wins it! Dameon has defeated the World Champion and the biggest man in the RAWF in one match! PAUL MARSHALL: He never pinned Brandy! CHAD BORDEAUX: But he's still the winner! Ladies and gentlemen, Dameon has just raised the bar! You can call me John...
