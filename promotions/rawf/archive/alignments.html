ALIGNMENTS Updated 1-15-03 Alignments are handled a little differently in the Real Action Wrestling Federation and that is why they have a page devoted to them rather than just being listed on the roster.  Faces are the fan favorites and the ones the people love.  Neutrals are those who do whatever they want and still get crowd support.  Heels are the truly evil ones in the sport.  These are the ones the fans love to hate. You should therefore try and stay in character in order to more fully play your role.  This will help with storylines and angles and will be reflected here.  The faces, neutrals and heels are listed here in order of how they stand.  The top face, neutral and heel being listed at the top. This does not necessarily reflect current standings or wrestling level, but how the crowd is responding. Being the top face, neutral or heel will however help you to be successful in your matches and will quickly promote you to the top spot in the sport.
