RAWF Friday Fights, 19 July 2002

July 19, 2002 Norfolk Scope Norfolk, Virginia The crowd in Norfolk explode as the the Real Action Wrestling Federation's pyros begin to explode. After a good forty five seconds of minor booms there is one extremely loud boom and then Born of a Broken Man begins to play. The pyros shoot up off the front of the stage in gold and the 1-8-7 logo flashes upon the RAWFtron. Then the Real Action Wrestling Federation owner walks out to the loud boos of the crowd. Accompanying him is the beautiful and dangerous Victoria. The brother and sister combination make their way down to the ring and enter, Synn holding the ropes for his sister. Victor is quickly passed a microphone by a RAWF stage hand. VICTOR SYNN: Three simple words. Told Ya So. The fans continue their booing as a Victor sucks chant begins. VICTOR SYNN: The boss does not suck. Otherwise I wouldn't be the boss. I am the single most important and powerful man in RAWF. I run this place and will do as I please!!! I told ya'll I would single-handedly dismantle The Real Deal and Darksider and what did I do? I destroyed them both!!! In fact, neither one of them is ever coming back!!! A Bullsh-- chant begins. VICTOR SYNN: Oh it's true. Both of them have been sent packing. Neither one of them ever wants to cross me again, so they've both packed their bags and went home where they belong!! In fact, those two losers were both supposed to defend those belts of theirs tonight. The belts they used their power to keep from defending. So what do they do when forced to defend them? The cowards don't show up. Well, we will have none of that crap in this company!!! I am hereby stripping both of them of their respective titles!!! An audible gasp is heard from the crowd. VICTOR SYNN: We shall have champions that defend their titles, not jackasses who hide and refuse to defend them. And unfortunately for both Captain Crush and James Outlaw, we don't give away titles in this here federation. Now I know you're both upset and, well, and, well, I don't give a sh--. you're feelings mean about as much to me as these fans. The booing begins with renewed vigor. VICTOR SYNN: But I decided I was gonna be fair anyway. Outlaw, tonight, you were supposed to get and Intercontinental Title shot. You'll get it. Crush, you were supposed to get a US Title Shot. Well, that ain't happening. The fans begin a Crush chant. VICTOR SYNN: You will however, receive an Intercontinental Title shot. In other words, Outlaw, Crush, you two, tonight, winner gets the Intercontinental Title!! The fans surprisingly cheer for once at words coming out of Victor's mouth. VICTOR SYNN: Now onto the other title. The US Title. Well, I thought about this one too. And here's what we gonna do. Next week, during Fights, anyone interested meet in the parking lot. Last one standing will be the RAWF US Champion!! The fans seem to like this idea as well. VICTOR SYNN: But back to tonight. Seems we got a couple of 1-8-7 family members who ain't exactly seeing eye to eye. JYD and Show been at each other's throat over that tag title loss at Hardcore Independence. Can't say I'm overly happy bout it neither, but tonight, we letting the two guys settle their differences man to man in that ring. And since I'm here for ratings, and titles mean ratings, I think we'll put that Hardcore Title on the line just to make things interesting. The fans continue to cheer even while chanting Victor sucks. Amazing how they can hate him but love his rulings. VICTOR SYNN: And one other matter. Brandy. In front of everyone let me say this, you do 1-8-7 proud. I knew ya had it in ya and ya did it. And tonight ya get to do one better. Beat Tony. You can do it. I got faith in ya. With that said, the brother and sister leave the ring and head for the back as the Victor sucks chants continue.
