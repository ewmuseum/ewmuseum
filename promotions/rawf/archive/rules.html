Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
RULES If you don't follow them, you won't wrestle here.   No excuses. 1.  First off, let get things straight.  The RAWF is a role playing game.  Therefore, everything is fantasy that happens in it. This is all for fun.  Out of character wars are totally unacceptable and will quickly get you fired. 2.  Using someone else's character without permission will get you fired immediately.  Don't do it unless you have permission. 3.  Do not impersonate someone else.  You will be fired. Never post as someone else. 4.  Watch the language.  If you can't say it on television, you can't say it on here.  And censor it.  A s--- is just as effective as writing the whole word.  Also, no sexual or racial bashing. Watch the sexuall innuendos.  Not everyone here is ready for R-rated material. 5.  No pornography.  No exceptions. 6.  No html in subject titles on the RP and OOC boards. 7.  All out of character messages for on the OOC board,.  RP board is for just that, RPs. 8.  Only original wrestlers. 9.  The RAWF is based both on angles and RPing. The bookers score three RPs per wrestler for the week and this is one of the two determining factor in who wins a match. This is the thing that you have the most control over to help your character succeed. Make your three RPs the best they can be, whether it be a promo or a character development RP. 10.  The other half of what determines the winner is any angles that are going on. Sometimes you may lose to help with the storyline and to keep a feud going well between you and someone, but don't get frustrated. Angles are here to keep the stories interesting and not to hold back quality RPers. Quality will eventually win out. 11. RP Weeks run from Wednesday to Monday. Rping for the Wednesday card is over at 1201am EST on Tuesday morning / Monday night. Cards will be up Wednesday nights by 1000pm EST. 12. Entrances and strategies for the card should be sent in by the RPing deadline. Also any on card interviews, attacks, special ideas, etc. should be send in by the same deadline. Back to RAWF
