REWF, the company who started Virtual Pro Wrestling, will be presenting
WrestleFest 1999 this Sunday Night.
http://www.angelfire.com/on/rewf/ JOIN THE E-BROADCAST NETWORK MAILING LIST FOR FREE TO RECIEVE THIS EVENT
LIVE THIS SUNDAY NIGHT! BE ONE OF THE HUNDREDS READING AND INTERACTING TO
THE BEST WRESTLING ON THE INTERNET! http://ebroadcast.listbot.com Christian Idol vs. Jeremy Knight (World Title) This started off when Trisha framed Idol in the sexual harrassment charge thing one and a half months ago. Idol was screwed out of the World Title at
Corruption, but it could be different this time because there is no Texas or
Trisha to interfiere. The last time Idol was in a huge situation like this, he won the title. Could it be the same this Sunday?
Krax vs. Darth Diablo (Mask vs. Mask; Number one contender match) One of these guys is going to loose their mask. We've seen what Krax looks like before, but seeing Diablo's face would be quite a treat. This feud has been feuled with hatrid for almost two months now, so this should be an amazing fight.
Joey Arson vs. Reckless Rebel (North American Championship) Arson & Rebel are two of the top talents in the REWF, and this should be an amazing contest. Rebel will have to go through the Firestarter to move up to the 'next level' here in the REWF.
Los Divertidos vs. The Black Panthers (Tag Team Championships) The three mexican clowns have made the Panthers mad, and that usually isn't something to brag about. The clowns could be ripped apart this Sunday, because the Panthers want more then just the gold in their teeth.
The BlackBloods vs. Kenny Psycore & Sin This one was originally suppost to be a 6 man tag, but was nixed last minute. Both teams formed the former Army of Death, so this should be a blockbuster encounter.
Plus: 'Mr. Wednesday Night' Mikey Loden vs. Paul Basso Igor Grishkov vs. Ashton King (Boxing Match) Garland vs. Calypso Mitsutaka Kojima vs. Akira Mishima (Death Match) Plus much, much more!
