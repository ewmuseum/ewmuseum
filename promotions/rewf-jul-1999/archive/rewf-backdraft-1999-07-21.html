He says that Knight has done alot of talking as of late, and he has decided that Krax is the man to shut his mouth. SSC then announces that this Monday on the television special 'Road to
WrestleFest', Ashton King will be live at the arena and will have a statement towards Igor Grishkov. SSC then announces that some of the men competing in the battle royal. Christian Idol, Joey Arson, Krax, Kenny
Psycore, Darth Diablo, Reckless Rebel, and Johnny Ramos are a few of the talents who will be in the match. Coach Arden then came to ringside and called SSC a piece of garbage and started bad mouthing him for signing the
Knight/Krax match. SSC then had Arden removed from the ringside area by security.
·> Mikey Loden defeated Asesino with a top rope split leg moonsault in
00:06:01. After the match, Loden got on the mic and said that from now on, he will be known as 'Mr. Wednesday Night' within the wrestling world.
Backstage - Los Divertidos are shown talking to some spanish women. One of the Divertidos made a move on one of the women, and touched her behind.
The woman slapped the clown across the face, enraging the REWF Tag Team
Champions. One of the other Divertidos then grabbed the woman violently by the arm. The Black Panthers then ran in and destroyed all three Los
Divertidos. While they were fighting, Johnny Ramos came into the scene and left with the women on his arm.
·> Calypso defeated Johnny Ramos with the Thunder Jam in 00:10:12 after
A.C. Berrera came out and crushed Ramos with a steel chair. Ramos was busted wide open. Afterwards, the three women went into the ring and tended to Ramos.
·> Kenny Psycore defeated Akira Mishima with the Psycho Downfall in 00:07:42. After the match, 'Mr. Wednesday Night' Mikey Loden came out and hit Mishima with a barbed wire baseball bat. He was then chased backstage by Mitsutaka Kojima, who was at ringside.
·> Interview with Christian Idol & Joey Arson - The fans errupt as the duo enters the arena. Idol congradulates Joey Arson on winning the REWF North
American Championship last week on Backdraft. Arson then stole the mic off
Idol and started talking about the Knight brothers. Idol looked puzzled, but went and sat in the corner while Arson spoke. Arson said that the
North American title is nice, but he will be the one who wins the Rumble this Monday Night. Idol then jumps over and steals the mic from Arson.
Idol says that there is no chance in hell Arson will win, because there can't be two winners and Idol is going to be the next champion. Arson then said that if it comes down to him and Idol, he is going to 'intoxicate him with HIS serial insanity'.
<vignette> Soon.... you will all believe.... soon.... you will all see the light... look to your orb.. and it will guide you.... ·> Brad Knight defeated Justin Mulhollan with a death valley driver in
00:02:10. After the match, Jeremy Knight came into the ring and both
Knight brothers beat the hell out of Mulhollan. They then spraypainted the word 'Idol' on his face, and slammed him with a steel chair.
·> The Black Panthers defeated Mystical & Enigma with a backdrop/neckbreaker combo in 00:06:43. After the match, they were attacked by Los Divertidos. They gave each Panther a top rope legdrop through a table, and then left with the tag belts raised in the air.
·> Interview with Reckless Rebel - Rebel comes out and says that this
Monday at the Rumble, he will do his best to climb the ladder to the 'next level', and he says that he wants nothing more than to be champion. Darth
Diablo then comes out, and says Rebel is nothing. He calls Rebel a
'wanna-be'. Rebel then tells Diablo to back up his words, and fight him tonight on Backdraft. Diablo accepts.
·> A.C. Berrera defeated Hexigon with a top rope dropkick in 00:05:56. After the match, Berrera got on the mic and challenged Johnny Ramos to meet him at WrestleFest in a 'Back Alley Street Fight'.
·> Darth Diablo def. Reckless Rebel via. disqualification after Krax & The
BlackBloods jumped in the ring and attacked Diablo.
The lights then went down, and when they came back on Darth Diablo had disappeared from the ring, but the Knight brothers & Coach Arden were in the ring and hit Krax & The BlackBloods with three steel chairs. They cleared the ring of the BlackBloods, and the main event was underway.
·> Jeremy Knight def. Krax with a roll-up in 00:12:34 to retain the REWF
World Heavyweight Championship. During the match, Christian Idol & Arson came to ringside and attacked Brad Knight & Coach Arden. The BlackBloods then pushed Arson & Idol aside and attacked Brad & Arden. That started a fight between Idol & Arson. Krax came to the ropes and swung at Idol outside the ring, and was rolled up by Knight. Afterwards, Idol went in the ring and gave Knight an Idolizer. Trisha then came in the ring and grabbed Idol's hand, but he moved away and gave her a cold stare as the show went off the air.
