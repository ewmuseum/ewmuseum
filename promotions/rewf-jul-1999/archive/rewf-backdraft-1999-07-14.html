rewf virtual pro wrestling - backdraft 7.14.99 REWF Website http://www.angelfire.com/on/rewf/ REWF Fan ConXion - POST YOUR FAVORITE REWF MEMORY THIS WEEK! http://disc.server.com/Indices/60800.html Ryan's Tape Trading - 'ROCK: KNOW YOUR ROLE' & 'HELL YEAH' ARE IN! http://www.angelfire.com/on2/ryanstapes/ ·> Interview with Krax & The BlackBloods - Krax says that nothing will interject itself into the unholy triad. He says at WrestleFest, he wants to finish this whole situation with a 6 man tag match from hell. He challenges Diablo, Kenny Psycore & Sin, if he is able to ever walk again, to show their faces at the Pay Per View and take them on.
·> Green Beret def. Scott Andrews with a powerbomb in 00:04:12. Afterwards, Igor Grishkov entered the ring with a pair of boxing gloves and punched Beret's hands, another attempt to get Ashton King to accept his challenge for a boxing match.
Clips of Jeremy Knight retaining the REWF title at Corruption are shown. The fans boo as he makes his way out with Coach Arden, Brad, Texas &
Trisha. ·> Interview with Jeremy Knight - Knight says that for the last time, he has defeated Christian Idol fair and square and has proved beyond a shadow of a doubt that he is the greatest REWF World Champion of all time. He addresses the July 26th TV Special, and says that he hopes someone like
Asesino wins the battle royal so he can have a better challenge than the one he had at Corruption. Brad Knight then takes the mic, and challenges
Christian Idol to a match later on tonight.
·> Reckless Rebel def. Calypso via forfiet after Calypso was unable to continue due to a severe cut over his left eye. Calypso did not want to leave the ringside area, but several referees forced him to the back.
·> Interview with Christian Idol - Idol accepts Brad Knight's match for later on tonight. Idol says that for the second time in a row, he has been screwed out of the REWF World Title. He says that the third time will be a charm, and that on July 26th he will be in the battle royal to determine the number one contender. REWF Owner SSC then made his way out, and said that since Idol has had two opertunities and has lost both matches, he would enter the battle royal as Number One.
·> Los Divertidos #2 & #3 def. The Badstreet Mafia in 00:09:12 after #2 hit the top rope legdrop on A.C. Berrera. Los Divertidos retained the REWF
World Tag Team Titles. After the match, Johnny Ramos grabbed a steel chair and crushed Berrera with it in the ring. The fans booed him as he walked off.
·> Interview with SSC - SSC says that next week, he will have the official responce of Ashton King's negotiationing and such. He says that things look good right now, and he hopes Grishkov knows what he is getting himself into.
Akira Mishima & Mitsutaka Kojima came out and challenged any two men to a
Hardcore Tag Team Death Match. They gave up after about one minute of no responce, but as they were about to leave the ring 'Make em Say Uhh..'
started to play over the speakers and The Black Panthers ran out to the ring with garbage cans filled with weapons.
·> The Black Panthers defeated Mishima & Kojima after Shabaz made Kojima submit after using a cheese grater on his head. Both Kojima & Mishima were stretchered off after the match.
Before the North American title match started, Kenny Psycore made his way out and accepted The BlackBloods & Krax' challenge for WrestleFest. He said that Diablo & Sin may be out, but they are with him in spirit. He then promised that the following match would be Joey Arson's last match because he was going to rip him apart.
·> Joey Arson defeated Kenny Psycore in 00:13:02 with the Jailhouse Burner to capture the REWF North American Championship. Krax & The BlackBloods cost Psycore the match when they attacked him when he was thrown out of the ring by Arson. Arson distraced the referee as the attack occured, and then Psycore was put in the ring to be hit with the Jailhouse Burner.
Afterwards, Brad & Jeremy Knight ran out and attacked Arson. Christian
Idol then came out, and clotheslined Jeremy over the top rope. Arson brawled with Jeremy backstage, and Idol and Brad locked up in the ring.
