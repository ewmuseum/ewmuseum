Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The New Computer Wrestling Alliance introduced itself on 26 April 2001 with a FAQ Version 1.0 describing “a new e-fed based from Yahoo! Groups, formerly eGroups, and simmed using the NCW 5.1 wrestling simulator.” It was careful, in that same document, to separate the two halves of that sentence: the simulator produced the results, but the fed “is still booked. That means the best role-players win.” Its own FAQ made the case for the sim directly, against an imagined objection from “die-hard” fans — “most people do NOT read the entire card every time one comes out” — and framed the simulator as protection against exactly that boredom, run so that “someone who doesn't role-play will NOT win any matches.”

The mailing-list format was its own selling point as much as the simulator was. Members could role-play “via e-mails, message boards, or flash packets,” each type routed only to those who had chosen it, and the FAQ noted that Yahoo! Groups let it add “lurkers” to the list — readers who could follow the fed without ever entering it.

Its full card slate ran five deep: NCWA PrimeTime on Monday nights as the main program, Saturday Night Wrestling as the secondary show, Sunday Suicide as a low-card program, a monthly supercard billed as NCWA Main Event, and an annual pay-per-view called NCWA Annual Anarchy. Four championships were named to go with them — a Heavyweight Title, a Lightweight Title restricted to wrestlers under 215 pounds, an Internet Title, and a set of Tag Team Titles.

The FAQ is the promotion's only surviving document, posted to rec.sport.pro-wrestling.fantasy and cross-posted across a run of unrelated newsgroups — alt.tv.mash, rec.sport.pro-wrestling, alt.punk, alt.tv.simpsons, and alt.games.wrestling among them. Whether the New Computer Wrestling Alliance ever ran a card under any of its five programs, or crowned a single champion under any of its four titles, isn't in the surviving record.
