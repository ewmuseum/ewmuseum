New Computer Wrestling Alliance FAQ Version 1.0 The NCWA is a new e-fed based from Yahoo! Groups, formerly eGroups, and simmed using the NCW 5.1 wrestling simulator found at
www.clownworks.net . Although NCWA uses the simulator to create results the fed is still booked. That means the best role-players win. By using Yahoo! Groups we are able to cater to most fans of e-wrestling. Active wrestlers can choose to participate in the fed via e-mails, message boards, or flash packets. All three types of role-playing will be sent to everyone through thy're chosen type, making it a lot easier to cater to certain types of people. Also, through Yahoo! Groups we can easily add lurkers to our list. This way people can read about the fed without having to participate in it.
NCWA uses a wrestling simulator: Most "die-hard" e-wrestling fans despise this. Some have both enough experience to have viable commentary and a valid reason. However, most people are hypocrites. Most people do NOT read the entire card every time one comes out. Most people skim through the results, looking for winners to important matches. Also, since the NCWA books the matches and only uses the simulator to write the matches, the fed is totally fair. Someone who doesn't role-play will
NOT win any matches. The simulator is only used to prevent boredom and allow me to actually run the fed. I do have a life you know.
