ASWF (1995) — Lords of Wrestling (Debut Card)

"ASWF Lords of Wrestling"

15 November 1995
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
ASWF Lords of Wrestling [Jack Simpson] Ladies and gentlemen, welcome to the ASWF Lords of Wrestling. My name is Jack Simpson, and joining me for the show is Union Michaels, and Union, the
ASWF is starting off with some great talent, right?
[Union Michaels] You got that right, Jack. We have assembled some of the hottest talent around, including the aerial styles of The White Angel, The Manchester Red
Devil and the veteran Young Dragon. If you like technical wrestling, we have
The Bigg Dogg and "Hitman" Nick Jerman. And our tag team scene will be second to none, with the Sicilians, the Patriot and Ultimate Soldier and The Midnight
Rockers.
[JS] Let's not waste any time here and get to our first match here on Lords! MATCH 1 ======= [Generic Ring Announcer Dude] Ladies and Gentlemen, This is the first match here on Lords of Wrestling!!! Introducing first, coming down the aisle from Toronto, Canada...."Bad Boy" Mark Bagwell!!!!!!!!!!!!!! And his opponent, from All 50 United States, The Super American!!!!!!!! [JS] These two competitors are primed and ready for action, and neither man wants to lose their debut match.
[UM] And Mark is looking good right now holding onto an arm bar. Super American is trying to power out of it, but he just can't do it. I hear that the ASWF
Title Tournament is coming up in a few weeks.
[JS] That's right, with the finals of the tournament at the Steel City Showdown. Mark hits with a suplex and immediately begins to work on the neck. The Super American is trying to stand up, and he succeeds. Super American is now on the offense here. He's locking in a torture rack! The pain on Bagwell's back must be immense.
[UM] I know. If I was ever put into one of those, I would try my hardest to wiggle free before it was locked on. The pain is tremendous and Bagwell looks to be on the point of submission.
[JS] Not yet. The American got tired of waiting, and he released the move. He's now delivering a Backbreaker. He drops onto Bagwell for the count of One.
[UM] A sloppy cover like that is a rookie mistake. The American should learn better than that. [JS] Bagwell gets to his feet and OH! He stuck a thumb in The American's eye. [UM] Lucky that mask has traditional eyeholes, or that desperation move would have been for not.
[JS] Bagwell locks the American into a side headlock, which he turns into a running Bulldog! Bagwell is in complete control of this match now. Wait, what is he doing?
[UM] He's turning The American onto his stomach. Bagwell off the ropes, and a stomp to the small of the back. Now he's using a piledriver!
[JS] Bagwell is grabbing The American by the legs and has sinched in the Boston
Crab! The American can not hold on and he submits to the pain!
[GRAD] Ladies and Gentlemen, the winner of this match, by submission, is "Bad Boy" Mark Bagwell!!!!!!!!! [JS] An impressive debut for both men. Unfortunatly, The Super American had to take the loss. [UM] He should hold his head high. He took a great competitor in Mark Bagwell to the limit and just lost because of a rookie mistake.
MATCH 2 ======= [GRAD] This is a tag team contest scheduled for one fall. Introducing first...... [Music] Theme from "The Godfather" [GRAD] From Sicily, Guido and Vincent, the Scilians!!!!!!!!! And their opponents, from
Los Angeles California, Christian and Milano, The Midnight Rockers!!!!!!!!!!
[JS] And it's Guido and Milano starting out for their respective teams. Guido applie an armbar and immediately lets it go. He's laughing at the much smaller Milano.
[UM] I would not do that if I was him. Milano and Christian could sneak up on him an suprise him.
[JS] Without a doubt. Milano with a single leg takedown. Guido gets up and is taken right back down by a Russian Leg Sweep. Guido gets up and is now complaining of a hair pull.
[UM] His hair is so greased up that Milano's hand would have slipped out of it quickly Guido now tags in Vincent, who is met with a thrust kick. [JS] Now Milano tags in Christian, and they hit a double dropkick. Wait, now Christian points to the heavens and the Rockers climb the turnbuckles. DOUBLE DROPKICK!!! Christian with the cover, the referee counts one, two, thr- no. Guido saves
Vincent only to get a Backbreaker for his troubles. Christian hits the lights out!
Referee counts one, two, three!!!!!! [GRAD] The winners of this match, the midnight rockers!!!!!!!!! [JS] And Guido is berating Vincent and Vincent is doing likewise. Meanwhile, the
Midnight Rockers are celebrating the victory by playing to the crowd. OH MY!
Guido and Vincent just caught both of them from behind! And the beating is is just unbelievable and the Sicilians hit the Armbuster! Milano is writhing in pain in the corner, Christian is protecting him! And Finally, Security comes down to save the Rockers.
[Guido] This ain't over, yunz punks! You still owe us Intrest! [UM] What a dastardly act by a couple of big, stinky, nasty Sicilians. [JS] Luckily, all Scilians are not like these two. MATCH 3 ======= [GRAD] This match is scheduled for one fall. Intorducing first, from the former Yugslavia, accompanied by his manager Cpl. Doom, The Serbian Terrorist!!!! [Music] "Glory Days" by Bruce Springsteen [GRAD] And his opponent, from Medfield, Massachusetts, Jeremy Peyton!!!! [JS] Our first look at both these young Superstars. Peyton is well recieved by the crowd, especially the women.
[UM] But the love of the crowd and wrestling ability are not the same. Jeremy has much to prove against this hulking Serb.
[JS] True. And the Terrorist is off and running with several crushing blows to the back of Jeremy. Oh my, now he has him locked in a bearhug, and he is making
Jeremy pay.
[UM] I don't think anyone realizes the power of that move. Sure it looks simple to apply, but the pain is nightmarish.
[JS] The referee is checking in on Peyton, and Jeremy says he wants to keep going. This is showing real courage from the youngster. [UM] Courage, yes. But he needs a way to escape the hold. [JS] And he finds it in the greco roman thumb to the eye. Illegal, yes, but very effective. Peyton is on the attack now, and he hits with a clothesline. Peyton drops a knee on the chest of the Terrorist. Peyton is working on the leg now, as he locks in a leglock. Terrorist is looking for a way out, and he gets it as Cpl. Doom reaches the rope out to the Serb. That's cheating!
[UM] But the ref didn't see it, Simpson. He can only yell if he sees the Corpral han the rope to The Terrorist.
[JS] Peyton releases the Leglock and grabs the head of the Serb. DDT! Peyton hit the
DDT! Now The Terrorist is in deep trouble. Peyton hauls the Serb up and he hits the Piledriver! Going for the pin! one, two, thr..NO! Cpl. Doom places the
Serb's leg on the rope, and the ref halts the count. Peyton is jumping up and down, wondering just what has happened!
[UM] If I was Cpl. Doom, I'd back off for a minute or two before trying anything else. [JS] Wait a minute, here comes the Young Dragon! He's scheduled to wrestle Riptor in the next match, but the interference of Cpl. Doom has brought him down early
[UM]
Big mistake. If he concentrates on helping Peyton, and not on his own match, he's gonna end up on the wrong end of the ledger.
[JS] Meanwhile, The Terrorist has been working on Peyton's back again. He hits a piledriver of his own and now he whips Peyton off of the rope and hits a spinebuster! He covers, wait! He's got his legs on the second rope! One, Two,
NO! The Young Dragon knocks his legs off the rope and has incurred the wrath of the Serb.
[UM] He should be concentrating on Riptor, not the Terrorist. [JS] Wait! The Serb turns around and is met by a Superkick! Referee counts one, two, THREE! [GRAD] The Winner of this match, Jeremy Peyton!!!!!!!!! [JS] Well, before we go, next week, The Young Dragon takes on Riptor, Tex Deathstar debuts against Mr. Steel. The Warriors, David and Chris O'Neill take on the
Patriot and the Ultimate Soldier. And in what may prove to be a great match, the Manchester Red Devil will take on The White Angel. Any closing comments,
Union?
[UM] Just that The Young Dragon will not last that long in the ASWF if he keeps playing martyr. [JS] See you next week, when we will also have word on the ASWF Heavyweight Title tournament!
