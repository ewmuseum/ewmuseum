A thin but genuine November 1995 fantasy e-fed that staged one full three-match debut show before an abrupt hiatus, then self-narrated its own reopening in February 1996; the fed itself never states a full-name expansion for its own bare acronym. Written by AI, 2026

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

ASWF announced itself on 1 November 1995 as a booked federation “with emphasis on Angles,” planning two weekly shows, Lords of Wrestling and Power Zone, plus a pay-per-view every couple of months.

Its debut card, Lords of Wrestling, introduced a full roster before the first bell — the aerial stylings of the White Angel and the Manchester Red Devil, the veteran Young Dragon, technical wrestlers the Bigg Dogg and “Hitman” Nick Jerman, and tag teams the Sicilians, the Midnight Rockers, and the Patriot and Ultimate Soldier — and opened its first-ever match between “Bad Boy” Mark Bagwell and a masked wrestler billed as the Super American, with an ASWF Title Tournament already announced to crown a champion at a coming Steel City Showdown.

That single three-match card was, by the promotion's own later account, the whole of its first life: “a *very* small federation... had one card, consisting of three matches, then died a quick and most definitely painful death.” Its operator announced a reopening on 12 February 1996, inviting former handlers back before a new simulator was even chosen. No document confirms whether that announced reopening ever produced a card of its own, and no closure notice survives either way.
