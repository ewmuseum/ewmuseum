WWF Raw is War: Week 1, July 1997

Posted 31 May 2019

Week 1, July 1997

America West Arena (Phoenix, Arizona)

ATT: 18,000

TV: 30.94

The show opens tonight to the screeching guitar of the Hart Foundation’s (or more accurately, Bret Hart’s) iconic theme, as the two men make their way down the aisle to a chorus of disapproval from these great fans in attendance. The duo climb into the ring and tell the sound people to cut their music, because they would like to invite someone to the ring……a true blue Canadian hero…….a main event calibre star…….without a doubt he is the best light heavyweight there is…..the best light heavyweight there was…..and the best light heavyweight there ever will be…….

“……but more importantly that’s that, he is someone that myself and Owen are proud to call a friend……and the ONLY man in the World Wrestling Federation that can be trusted…….ladies and gentlemen……..Chris Jericho……”

…..that does indeed bring Mr. Jericho out…..who is treated to the same sounds of disapproval from the crowd as the Hart Foundation were….although by his mannerism I don’t think he hears the boo’s, because he’s walking down the aisle like a man adored by millions…..he climbs into the ring, where Bret offers a hearty handshake……Chris shakes his head however, instead deciding to embrace the Hitman in a huge bear hug that actually cracks a smile on Bret’s usually sourpuss face…..Owen receives the same treatment, before we finally get down to business……Bret says that they’ve invited Jericho out here for one reason…..

“…..and that reason is pretty simple…..we were so impressed with your performance in last weeks main event…….that we went ahead and arranged ANOTHER match for you…..ANOTHER main event…..and you’re really going to like this one……”

……Bret says that for once, Vince McMahon did the right thing, and sanctioned a number one contenders match for the World Wrestling Federation Championship…..The British Bulldog Davey Boy Smith…….vs. The King of Harts Owen Hart……..

“……BUT…..since you don’t get the recognition you deserve around here……and since it was YOU that got the win for us last week, Owen has graciously decided to give his spot in that match to you……”

…..Rick Rude commends the Harts for that selfless act, declaring that it is much deserved….while Jim Ross states that if you ask him, this is more a case of Owen not wanting to be in there with an angry Bulldog rather than an act of kindness on the part of the Hart Foundation……..however as Rude points out, nobody did ask him……..and Jericho is over the moon at the notion of a second main event spot in two weeks…..and that’s before the realisation dawns on him that…..

“…..so not only do I get to main event tonight…..but after I beat your ungrateful brother in-law…..because lets face it, I will…..that means I get to go on and main event In Your House…….against Big Ploppa Dump……for the WWF Title?……”

…..Bret smiles and says yes, that’s exactly what’s going to happen……he tells Chris that he’s already proved to the world that he’s the best light heavyweight there is, was, and ever will be…..in fact, as far as Bret is concerned they should just retire the whole division since the Ayatollah of RocknRolla is no longer a part of it…..but now it’s time for the world to see, that Chris Jericho is so much more than that………

……speaking of the light heavyweight division, that brings out two men who just last week were robbed at a shot of competing against Scorpio for that very Championship, Dean Malenko and Rey Mysterio……the pair get a good reception as they make their way down the aisle, where they climb into the ring to confront the Canadian Contingent…..Malenko tells Jericho that for someone who thinks he’s so above the light heavyweight division, he sure seems to be spending a lot of time worrying about what’s happening…..he says that last week, two people that actually CARE about the light heavyweight division, himself and Rey Mysterio, were robbed of an opportunity to compete for the title……

“…….and not just by you Jericho……but by the Hart Foundation as well…….so since you and your brother want to go around sticking your nose in our business, how about you face me and Rey Mysterio…….tonight…..”

……blistering microphone work from Mr. Malenko there……I’m being sarcastic by the way, it was pretty weak….he really struggled in there surrounded by people far more comfortable without a script than he is…..but he got his point across none the less……he and Rey want the Hart Foundation in a match……

……all three Canadians crack up laughing at this, and when they finally regain their composure they are more than happy to accept…..in fact Owen goes so far as to call a referee out from the back, saying that they may as well do it right now…..because it’s not going to take the former Tag Champs long to put the little guys away……

Rating: A*

VS.

Rey Mysterio & Dean Malenko vs. The Hart Foundation


….and that match is indeed next…..but if Bret and Owen thought that Malenko and Mysterio were going to be a pushover, they were seriously mistaken, and seriously underestimating the light heavyweight division as a whole…..sure some of these guys might be smaller than your average superstar, but that doesn’t make them any less capable in the ring…..and Mysterio and Malenko are two of the best in ring workers, regardless of their size……and it showed in this match…..which (as always when the Hitman is involved) was fantastic, in fact, it was easily the match of the night….which was all the more impressive when you consider Malenko and Mysterio are much further down the totem pole than the Harts……these two teams actually match up pretty well……you have two mat technicians in Bret and Dean, along with two high flyers in Owen and Rey…..sure Owen might not be quite the daredevil that Mysterio is, but he’s certainly no slouch either……

…..The Harts got far more than they bargained for here, because Mysterio and Malenko dominated the opening portion of this match….in fact it was only the interference of Jericho who had decided to stick around at ringside that turned the tables for the Hart Foundation, and then they considerably slowed the pace of the match down as they split the ring in half and isolated the quintessential underdog Rey Mysterio…..as a lot of people have learned over the past year however, putting Rey down is one thing, keeping him down is an entirely different matter…..because regardless of the size of his body, he has the heart of a lion and doesn’t know the meaning of the word quit….finally managing to tag out to his partner/rival Malenko, who clears house as he hits the ring…..

……Bret and Owen are in serious danger of finding themselves on the receiving end of an embarrassing upset loss here, because Malenko is on fire, even managing to lock Bret into the Texas Cloverleaf……and he might have even picked up the victory, had it not been for the fact that the official was otherwise occupied with Owen and Mysterio…..which is why he also missed Jericho entering the fray, kicking Malenko hard in the back of the head, which gave Bret the opportunity to reverse the Cloverleaf into the Sharpshooter, right in the middle of the ring……and despite putting on a valiant display, the man of a thousand holds finally had no choice but to submit to the Hitman’s signature hold….and thanks to Chris Jericho, the Hart Foundation are victorious here tonight.

Winner(s): The Hart Foundation
Rating: A

We come back from commercial and are straight back to ringside, where Raven is making his way down the aisle, along with the Flock….the dysfunctional family climb into the ring, where Raven positions himself into a seated position in the corner, before pulling a microphone out of his jacket pocket…..

“…….the truly selfless souls in this life are often overlooked by the masses…..just as the actions and sometimes, what is seemingly inaction…..can often be misconstrued by the feeble minded…..the martyr, at his very core must choose between being forgotten…..being mocked….or being used……….but never being understood…….I accept my role in this world……I accept my position as the saviour of the soulless…….I strive for nothing but greatness for my dysfunctional family……but what I will NOT accept……is doubt……what I will NOT accept……is having my methods questioned…….last week I gave the Flock an order……and I saw the hesitation in their movement….the doubt in their eyes…….I saw the questions racing through their clouded minds……and that simply will not do…….I give my life to the betterment of YOUR lives…..YOUR careers…..YOUR sanity……and this is the thanks I get? What about me?……WHAT ABOUT RAVEN?…….”

……he tells the Flock that none of them are here under duress…..and if they truly believe the ramblings of a madman…..if they truly believe Psycho Sid’s claims that Raven doesn’t have their best interest at heart, then they are free to spread their wings and fly away…………go…..leave like everyone else in his life has always left…….but before they do…..they should ask themselves one thing…..WHERE were they without Raven?…..WHAT were they without Raven?……what direction was their life headed?…….were they travelling the road to glory?…..or were they lost on a desolate road to nowhere?……Raven says that tonight, he has arranged a match for the Dogs of War…..and the ONLY thing holding them back from a Tag Team Title opportunity…..is themselves……

“……destroy the imbecile……destroy the partner…..hell, destroy the mascot too…..and I will give my very lifeblood to guide you to your prize…..because this saviour, has already been crucified for your sins……Quote the Raven……Nevermore……”

Rating: B+

VS.

Al Snow & Chris Candido vs. The Dogs of War


So it’s the Dogs of War, Wrath and Crush, up against the Imbecile and his Partner…..or as they’re more commonly known, Al Snow and Chris Candido…..who do indeed have their “mascot” as Raven called her with them, after they rescued Head from the evil clutches of Edge and Christian at the King of the Ring…..and Snow hoists her high in the air as he walks the aisle, clearly back to his usual eccentric self after the re-union……and shockingly, even Candido has a brief smile on his face, clearly glad to see his partner back to his annoying best…..

…..for two men basically thrown together to fill up the numbers in the tag team ranks, Snow and Candido have gone from strength to strength, and are now getting to the point where a shot at the Tag Team Titles wouldn’t be out of the realms of possibility……for Wrath and Crush however, the polar opposite has been true, as they’ve sat spinning their wheels (despite what the man himself says) as Raven has utilised their size and strength to his own needs……which until last week didn’t appear to be a problem for the Dogs of War…..at least until Psycho Sid questioned whether their master really had their best interests at heart after all…..

…..Raven clearly wants to prove that he does indeed hold the key to victory and success, keeping himself and the rest of the Flock at ringside for this one, as Wrath and Crush unleash a vicious assault on Candido and Snow….but then this is hardly the first time that Al Snow has felt the teeth of the Dogs, after spending months embroiled in a bitter feud with Raven….and he’s picked up enough tricks along the way to ensure that he and Candido are able to claw their way back into this……so much so that Raven decides to up the ante……and as Candido and Crush battle on the outside, he orders DeMott and Daniels to distract the attention of the official, as he slides into the ring behind Al Snow, waiting for the perfect opportunity to strike……..

……..which is when Psycho Sid comes out of the crowd, climbing into the ring behind Raven……and he too is waiting for the perfect opportunity to strike…..and as Wrath points Sid out to Raven, he sends the leader of the Flock flying through the ropes and to the floor with a hard big boot, before kicking Wrath in the gut and planting him with a Powerbomb……

……Sid bails from the ring and back into the crowd as Al Snow covers the downed Wrath for the one, the two, and the three……and another big, big win for Snow and Skippy……uhm, sorry Chris…..I mean Candido and Snow.

Winner(s): Al Snow & Chris Candido
Rating: B

The camera follows Sid as he makes his escape through the crowd, before cutting to Raven on the arena floor, waving frantically to the back……where those men little white coats come charging out in force……Raven indicates to Sid’s rough whereabouts, telling them to go and get him, as Sid’s voice booms out over the house speakers……

…..he asks Raven how it feels when his mind games don’t work……how it feels when he chosen target turns the tables and puts Raven in his sights…..

“…….SEE MR. SMARTY PANTS, I’M NOT AS STUPID AS I LOOK…..AND YOU’RE NOT AS CLEVER AS YOU THINK YOU ARE…..I KNOW ALL YOUR TRICKS…..I KNOW ALL YOUR GAMES…..I KNOW YOU…..I KNOW WHAT YOU’RE GOING TO DO NEXT……BECAUSE I’VE BEEN SPEAKING TO THE MAN WHO TAUGHT YOU EVERYTHING YOU KNOW…..”

……Sid tells Raven that he might think he’s the master of mind games…..but Sid is the master and the ruler of the world….and by the time he’s finished, he’ll be the master and the ruler of Raven’s world too.

Rating: A

As the men in white coats look to try and take Psycho Sid away, we go to the back, where Dok Hendrix is standing by with the two men who will challenge for the WWF Tag Team Titles at In Your House, Matt and Jeff Hardy…..this is your basic hype style promo, with the Hardy’s talking about their road from the bottom of the card, to this opportunity against the Outlaws, where they can finally cement their place as one of the top teams in the World Wrestling Federation…..Dok again shows his worth as an interviewer, as he walks the two less than stellar talkers to an entertaining promo……..although it was a promo that was rudely interrupted…..

……by the current WWF Tag Team Champions, Billy Gunn and Jesse James, the New Age Outlaws….who burst into shot, nailing their In Your House opponents from behind with the Title belts, before viciously assaulting them as they lay writhing on the concrete floor. The Bad Ass continues to stomp on the fallen brothers, while the Road Dogg steps back and wraps an arm around Dok Hendrix’s shoulders, asking him if this is really the best challenge that the WWF has to offer? Stating that the camera man there and the sound guy would pose a bigger threat than these two jabroni’s…….

“……because at In Your House, the Hardy Boys Casefiles will reveal that it ain’t no mystery……and Frank and Joe here just can’t hang with the big boys…..because when that bell rings, the whole world is gonna hear it announced…..that your winners…..and STILL the WWF Tag Team Champions of the World…….The Road Dog Jesse James…..the Bad Ass Billy Gunn…..the New…..Age……Outlaws……”

….with that Billy Gunn delivers one final kick to both Matt and Jeff, before telling both men to “Suck It” as the Outlaws wander out of shot, and we go back down to ringside.

Rating: A

VS.

Rikishi vs. Ahmed Johnson (w/Jim Cornette)


Last week Ahmed Johnson defeated the mammoth Son of Samoa member Kokina…..but it was AFTER the match that he unleashed the real carnage, violently assaulting the former World Wrestling Federation Champion with the same throat crushing tennis racquet shot that the Undertaker received at the King of the Ring……tonight Rikishi is looking for retribution for the injury inflicted on his partner, as he goes on on one with the man who will be competing for the WWF Title at Summerslam…….the 1997 King of the Ring, Ahmed Johnson…..

……Rikishi may be looking for revenge here….but he didn’t get it…..if anything he received an even worse beating than his partner received last week, because Ahmed Johnson absolutely dominated this short six minute match from start to finish, before finally putting Rikishi (mercifully) away with the sloppiest looking Pearl River Plunge known to man…..Rikishi is a big man to get up, but Ahmed managed it in a fashion….and three seconds more was all it took to end this match.

Winner: Ahmed Johnson
Rating: B

If Rikishi thought he was going to be saved by the bell, he was severely mistaken….because Ahmed was nowhere near done, and he continues to viciously assault the lone Son of Samoa. He punctuates each blow with a verbal tirade, screaming at Rikishi that he shouldn’t have come looking for a fight, because now he’s going to get his ass taken out, before delivering a second (slightly better looking) Pearl River Plunge…..

……Ahmed paces around the ring, but even after that he doesn’t look satisfied, as he tells Cornette to toss him that tennis racquet that has caused so much damage recently, before climbing onto the second turnbuckle and flying off, driving the weapon straight into the throats of Rikishi, who begins thrashing around the ring immediately gasping for air that won’t come…..Ahmed cracks a smile at the sight of his destroyed opponent, before mounting the second turnbuckle once more, looking to fly again……

…:::!!!GLASS SHATTERS!!!:::…

……but before he can do anymore damage, the glass shatters and the crowd erupts…..as Stone Cold Steve Austin comes marching down the aisle……Jim Ross says that he doesn’t think this is Austin wanting to save Rikishi, because The Rattlesnake couldn’t give a damn about anyone…..this is clearly a case of Steve Austin wanting to deliver a little bit of payback for Ahmed laying his hands on him last week…….

……Ahmed to his credit doesn’t look like he has a problem with that, tossing the tennis racquet to one side and diving through the ropes to meet Austin in the aisle, as these two collide like charging bulls in a flurry of fists….

…..they don’t show any sign of slowing down either, as they battle up and down the aisle, and all around ringside, before finally making their way inside the ring where they continue to take part in a pretty even brawl that sees neither man backing down and neither man gaining the advantage…..until finally security runs down to break these two up before they seriously hurt each other…..it takes some time (and a lot of bodies) but Austin and Ahmed are finally pried apart, as Jim Ross sends us to commercial with the two men screaming threats at each other in the ring.

Rating: A*

VS.

Mr. Perfect, Barry Windham & Harlem Heat vs. The Nation of Domination


Last week Faarooq successfully defended the WWF Intercontinental Championship against Barry Windham…..not content with simply picking up the victory however, he then proceeded to run the veteran down verbally….before ordering the Nation to beat him down physically……Mr. Perfect mad his way out in an attempt to put a stop to that….but he too found himself in a five on one situation……it wasn’t until the Harlem Heat made their way out with steel chair’s in hand that the Nation were finally fought off……which brings us to tonight…..and our next eight man tag team encounter…..

……and it was a surprisingly good eight man tag team encounter too…..very good in fact…..the Nation of Domination are total heat magnets here in the World Wrestling Federation, with their (not so) veiled accusations of the (alleged) prejudice received against them, not only from the office and the other superstars, but from the WWF fans too….accusations that clearly don’t adhere them to the crowd, which makes this a particularly fiery contest…..

…….the Harlem Heat are living proof as well, that it’s not the colour of their skin that gets Faarooq and Co. booed around here, because considering that this is their in ring WWF debut, the crowd are solidly behind them….and in this very even contest, they put on a particularly strong showing, finishing off Scorpio with the Heat Seeker Electric Chair/ Missile Dropkick combination.

Winner(s): Mr. Perfect, Barry Windham & Harlem Heat
Rating: B+

The Harlem Heat might have picked up a big debut victory…..but they don’t get to celebrate for long…..because the Rock comes running down the aisle with a steel chair and nails Booker from behind, before crowning Stevie Ray right between the eyes with the steel as he turns around…..that leaves Perfect and Windham all alone against the entire Nation….and it’s not long before the Nation of Domination are once again standing tall…..and in Faarooq’s case, standing tall with a microphone, as he once again berates the fans for their treatment of the Nation…..before bemoaning the fact that we are only two weeks out from In Your House, and once again, just like last month, none of the Nation of Domination are booked to compete, in spite of the fact that they hold three of the five Championships here in the WWF……yet ANOTHER example of the MAN holding back the brotherhood……

…….speaking of “The Man”…….the MAIN man around here is the owner of the World Wrestling Federation, Mr. Vincent Kennedy McMahon…..and here he is……along with his personal problem solver, Kane……

…….he says that he is well aware that he is not the most popular guy around here (an understatement judging by the reaction that entices from the fans)…..but he says that what some people in the back, and all these idiot people in the crowd forget, is that the WWF is a BUSINESS…..and in business, you sometimes have to make the unpopular decisions for the good of said business……

“……but then sometimes you make a much more popular decision, and I venture that this right here shall prove to be one of those……see, I’ve tolerated you Faarooq and your constant accusations of prejudice…..of……inequality……but make no mistake, the World Wrestling Federation is an equal opportunity organisation…..which is why Ahmed Johnson, a fine representative of the WWF, shall be competing for the biggest prize in the sport at Summerslam……and I for one would be ecstatic to see Mr. Johnson wrest my World Championship from the hands of Scott Steiner……with that being said…..ALL WWF Championships shall be on the line in two weeks time at In Your House…..and that INCLUDES the Intercontinental, European and Light Heavyweight titles currently held by the Nation of Domination…….”

……McMahon states that since their was no clear winner in last weeks Light Heavyweight #1 Contenders match, BOTH Rey Mysterio AND Dean Malenko will face current Champion Scorpio……Faarooq WILL be defending the WWF Intercontinental Championship at In Your House too…..against Mr. Perfect…..should he accept the match……Perfect looks somewhat confused as to why he WOULDN’T accept the match…..a question that McMahon soon clarifies, because he tells Faarooq that since he thinks “the man” is so against him…..the Mac MAN is going to give him something to complain about…..because while the winner of that Intercontinental Championship match WILL walk out with the Title…….the loser……will be FIRED……

……Faarooq says never mind what Perfect accepts, HE doesn’t accept those terms…..unfortunately McMahon informs him, HE doesn’t have a choice in the matter…..because regardless of WHO his opponent is, the stipulation remains the same……Perfect looks hesitant to accept, but soon changes his mind when Rick Rude pipes up from behind him that the Mr. Perfect he knew wouldn’t have been too much of a coward to accept, and just like that a big match is set with huge implications……it’ll be all or nothing at In Your House when Mr. Perfect faces Faarooq…..

…….McMahon says it won’t just be the Champions of the Nation in action at In Your House, the ENTIRE Nation will be in action…..because Kama and Nelson will be going up against Harlem Heat as well…..The Rock then asks who HE will be facing at In Your House, but Vince informs him that he hasn’t yet decided that……bust rest assured, that European Championship WILL be on the line……

”……wait a minute, wait a minute, wait one damn minute…..so you’re telling me that The Rock…..the hottest young superstar ever to grace the face of this sport……scrap that…..the greatest superstar ever to grace the face of gods green earth……The Rock……will be entertaining the MILLIONS of the Rocks fans at In Your House…..and you don’t even have an opponent yet? Who is there?…….Bob Holly?…….The Brooklyn Brawler?……..The Goon, TL Hopper, The goddamn Gobbledy Gooker?…….”

“…..Well as I said……”

“……IT DOESN’T MATTER WHO THE ROCK FACES……”

……The Rock says that regardless of what name Vince McMahon pulls out of his ass, whichever pretty white boy he sends to the slaughter, the Rock will do what he and his brothers do best……Dominate……

…….clearly Vince doesn’t take too kindly about being interrupted OR embarrassed, angrily telling the Rock that if he’s so damn confident of victory, then he may be walking into In Your House as Champion……

”……or Challenger……..or maybe not at all……because since you’re such a superstar, you can defend that WWF European Championship right NOW………against whoever can make it through that curtain first……..and WHATEVER colour their skin is, I personally hope they kick your entitled ass…….”

……there’s a pause as the Rock and the Nation argue how unfair an unscheduled Title defence is…….but they are soon cut off……..

……..as the Wolves howl……and Scott Hall and Sean Waltman of the Wolfpac part the curtain and saunter out onto the stage to a thunderous ovation…….

”………Hey Yo……….did we hear you right?……the first person to walk out here gets a shot at that European Title?……well here we are man(g)……..”

……to be honest, Vince doesn’t look any more pleased at the prospect of the Wolfpac holding onto the European Title than he is at the Nation holding it……but there’s not much he can do about it now. He does however state that only one of the two men can compete for the title…….and after a quick game of Rock, Paper, Scissors………it’s Sean Waltman who makes his way down the aisle ready to compete…….

Rating: A

VS.

WWF European Championship Match

The Rock (c) vs. Sean Waltman


With that McMahon orders everybody to the back, leaving only The Rock and Sean Waltman in the ring to duke it out for the newest prize in the WWF, the European Championship……to say the Rock was not prepared for this unscheduled Title defence would be an understatement, and Waltman takes full advantage of that as he fully dominates the opening portion of this match…..and he’s cast free on that arm injured at the hands of Brian Pillman finally, meaning his range of movement is back to its full capacity……

…….obviously the Rock didn’t become European Champion in the first place without being able to weather the storm of an early onslaught, and he does manage to regain control of the match, using his superior strength to slow down his quicker opponent…….to Waltman’s credit, he’s finally ditched that “underdog” label, and is seen as a genuine threat to pretty much anyone he steps on the ring with now……a solid Upper-Midcard talent, who is not quite at the main event level just yet (despite him constantly insisting that he is) but is only really one big win away from breaking through that glass ceiling……and the same can be said for the Rock, a man just a fraction below the main event, but all it would take would be one good feud to push him over the edge into superstardom…..

…..as for this match itself, it wasn’t great…..it wasn’t bad…..it was……solid…..it certainly could have been better, but then the lack of an associated storyline didn’t really help…….maybe a few weeks build for the rematch at In Your House will yield a better result…….because there will be a rematch……why? Because the Rock was so unprepared for his opponent that Waltman was able to convert a Rock Bottom attempt into a small package, pinning the Champions shoulders to the mat out of nowhere for the one, the two and the three.

Winner: …….and New WWF European Champion…….Sean Waltman
Rating: B

VS.

WWF Championship: #1 Contenders Match

The British Bulldog vs. Chris Jericho

From a European Championship match we go now to a number one Contenders match to decide who will face the WWF Champion Scott Steiner at this months In Your House event……and it’s a match featuring a former European Champion Davey Boy Smith, who’s held every Championship there is to hold APPART from the WWF Title, facing off against the former WWF Light Heavyweight Champion, Chris Jericho…..a man desperate to shed his Light Heavyweight past and break through to a Heavyweight, Main Event future……

…..to make things a little more interesting (for the censors if no one else) Big Poppa Pump decides to make his way down to ringside to join the commentary table, chipping in throughout the match with such gems as ”…..CHRIS JERICHO THINKS HES ONE OF THE BIG BOYS NOW JIM ROSS?…..I’VE GOT A BIGGER BOY IN MY PANTS IF YOU KNOW WHAT I MEAN………AND IF YOU DON’T KNOW WHAT I MEAN IM TALKING ABOUT MY HUGE PENIS……”

…….and other delights like ”……WHY DO THEY CALL HIM THE BRITISH BULLDOG ANYWAY?…..I’LL TELL YOU WHY…..IT’S BECAUSE HIS BREATH SMELLS SO BAD YOU’D THINK HE’S BEEN SAT AROUND LICKING HIS ASSHOLE ALL DAY, THATS WHY……”

…….I could go on, but you see what I mean…..in fairness it certainly adds something to what was in fact a rather lacklustre match, because to be honest, for a main event match, this wasn’t great…..and it’s a shame, because Smith and Jericho actually have some damn good chemistry in there together (which makes you wonder how bad this could have been if they didn’t)…..both men spend their fair portion of the match jaw-jacking with Steiner on commentary, with the Bulldog not happy that Steiner allowed himself to be distracted by Ahmed Johnson last week, leaving the Bulldog alone against three men…..while Jericho……well to be honest, Jericho is just an obnoxious pr!ck who would jaw-jack with a brick wall…..

……when all is said and done however……..

……it’s these two men that make all the difference, as The Hart Foundation make their way out from the back……and as Bret distracts the official, Owen hits Davey with what looks to be a loaded right hand in the back of the head, before slipping the offending weapon into his tights and hopping down off the ring apron……Jericho locks on the Walls of Jericho, but it’s all redundant, because The Bulldog is out cold, and the official has no choice but to stop the match, declaring Chris Jericho the winner and new number one contender to the WWF Championship.

Winner: Chris Jericho
Rating: B-

The match is done, but Jericho doesn’t appear to be ready to release the walls of Jericho, as he continues to wrench back on the hold at the risk of (as Jim Ross points out) great potential injury……Scott Steiner stands to his feet, tossing the headset to one side, but before he can do anything he is pounced on by Bret and Owen, who drag him over the announce table and launch him into the steel ring steps, before stomping the crap out of him and rolling him into the ring……

…….where Jericho is waiting, who finally releases his hold on The Bulldog, only to quickly apply it on the World Wrestling Federation Champion, who unlike Davey Boy, is fully conscious…..as his screams of pain attest to……Owen Hart rolls under the bottom rope, and locks the helpless Bulldog in the Sharpshooter, while the Hitman picks up a microphone, asking the extremely volatile crowd where their hero’s are now…….

…:::!!!GLASS SHATTERS!!!:::…

…….well here’s one apparently……because not content with taking his frustrations out of Ahmed Johnson earlier in the evening, the Rattlesnake is back, and he’s marching to the ring with a purpose……lest anyone forgets that Bret and Owen Hart cost this man a chance of becoming the World Wrestling Federation Champion……because Stone Cold Steve Austin certainly hasn’t forgotten……

…….the Harts and Jericho are happy with their accomplishments for one evening however, and quickly roll to the outside away from the p!ssed off Rattlesnake, as the show comes to a close on an angle that was far more exciting than the match it followed.

Rating: A*

Final Rating: B

WWF Raw is War: Week 2, July 1997

Posted 31 May 2019

Week 2, July 1997

Rosemont Horizon (Rosemont, Illinois)

ATT: 18,537

TV: 31.60

The show opens this evening in daylight, as the WWF Champion Scott Steiner is seen pulling up to the arena. The words “earlier today” in the bottom left hand side of our screen (coupled with the fact that it IS daylight) tells us that this is pre-recorded footage…..pre-recorded footage of a p!ssed off looking Big Poppa Pump rummaging in the back of his vehicle….

“………Mr. Steiner, Mr. Steiner……Steven Goldstein, I was wondering if I could have a few words for WWF.com?……”

No?……that’s because Ahmed Johnson took them out…….Ahmed Johnson who embarrassed the Undertaker at Wrestlemania, and then destroyed him at the King of the Ring…….so much so that the so called Phenom of the World Wrestling Federation hasn’t been seen since……..Stone Cold Steve Austin? It was just last night that Ahmed Johnson left him battered and laying in a puddle of his own blood……and all that……that was just for fun……tell me Mr. Steiner……what do you think Big Bad Ahmed Johnson is going to do to YOU, in order to finally prove he’s the man around here when he’s crowned the brand new WWF Champion?……”

……Steiner smirks at that for a moment, before looking both Ahmed and Cornette up and down, removing his sunglasses and headgear, tossing them on the table in front of him and picks up a microphone……

”……..BIG BAD AHMED JOHNSON?…..LET ME TELL YOU SOMETHING BOY’S…..THERE’S ONLY ONE BIG, BAD JOHNSON IN THE WORLD WRESTLING FEDERATION…..AND THATS THE BIG, BAD JOHNSON IN MY PANTS…….YOU CAN TALK ALL THE TALK ABOUT AHMED JOHNSON BEEING THE BADDEST SUMMBITCH ON THE PLANET, BUT UNTIL HE’S STEPPED IN THE RING WITH THE BIG BAD BOOTY DADDY HE’S JUST A SCOTT STEINER WANNABE WITH A BIG MOUTH…….IN FACT, HE DON’T EVEN HAVE THAT GOING FOR HIM, BECAUSE HE HAS TO HAVE A SCRAWNY LITTLE B@STARD LIKE YOU DOING ALL HIS TALKING FOR HIM……BUT THATS OK, BECAUSE I’M NOT MUCH FOR TALKING……I’D RATHER BE FIGHTING……AND AT SUMMERSLAM, I’M GONNA MAKE BIG BAD AHMED JOHNSON, MY BIG BAD B!TCH…….”

……Ahmed is straight to his feet after that comment, throwing his chair to one side as the security guards on the outside storm in to the ring to keep the two men separated…..Steiner doesn’t care, telling Ahmed to try his luck, because he’ll drop him so quick he won’t even have time to pull his tights out of his ass crack……Cornette again has to calm his man down…..telling him to sign the contract before he does anything else……make the match official and they can get out of here…….

…….reluctantly Ahmed does exactly that, signing his name on the dotted line before tossing the contract and the pen at Steiner……Cornette apologises to Scotty that they don’t have a crayon he can use, but if he can just scrawl his X on the dotted line then that would be great…….

…….Steiner tells Cornette that he’ll carve an X in his damn face if he doesn’t shut his mouth, before also signing the contract and handing it to Jerry Lawler, who looks keen to wrap this all up before it gets out of hand…..thanking the two men for their time, before telling them that they are free to leave……before Steiner can stand however, Ahmed snatched the WWF Title up off the table and drapes it over his shoulder…..telling Steiner that he may as well just hand this over now, because at Summerslam…….

”…..YOU’RE GOING DOWN!!…..”

……obviously that doesn’t sit to well with Steiner, who upends the table separating them and dives at Ahmed, unloading with right hand after right hand, while Ahmed reply’s with each shot from Steiner with one of his own…….security do their best to get the two behemoths away from each other, but they get decked one by one as Champion and Challenger attempt to tear each other apart…….finally Steiner and Ahmed are overwhelmed by a seemingly endless number of security guards…..who force each man back into opposing corners, which is how the show finally ends……Steiner on one side, Ahmed on the other……screaming threats and insults at each other as the hapless security does their best to keep them separated.
