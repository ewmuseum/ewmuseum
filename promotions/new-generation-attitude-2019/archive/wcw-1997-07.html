WCW Monday Nitro: Week 1, July 1997

Posted 31 May 2019

Week 1, July 1997

Louisville Freedom Hall (Louisville, Kentucky)

ATT: 17,192

TV: 18.11

Announce Team: Tony Schiavone, Eric Bischoff and Mike Tenay

This weeks episode of Monday Nitro opens up much the same way that last weeks Monday Nitro did, with the Titans of Wrestling filling out the majority of the ring. Hollywood has a huge smile on his face tonight, as he tells the crowd that last week went down perfectly according to plan……..he says that they have got rid of Luger……the big stinky Giant…….the mighty Stinger didn’t get the job done……that just leaves that B-List movie star Rowdy Roddy Piper…….

”…….and you know what dudes, we’re not going to have to wait until Bash at the Beach to bring that WCW Title back to the folds of the Titans of Wrestling man…….we can bring that belt back home TONIGHT brother……”

……even the rest of the Titans look confused by that one, but Hogan soon expands his explanation, saying that tonight, it’s the Hot Rod against the General……against Jean Paul Levesque…….

”…….and since Hollywood has got a bye all the way to the finals, all YOU have to do brother……is the exact same thing that the Macho Man here did last week……and if there’s no-one to face Hollywood in the finals, then that no good Vince Russo will have no choice but to hand the title back to its rightful owner dudes……”

……Tony Schiavone is outraged at this, stating that Hogan is going to try and steal back the WCW Championship after wrestling just ONE match…..the rest of the Titans crack up laughing though, clearly finding Hollywood’s plan to be genius….well…..all accept one……because Mr. Levesque does not look as though he was consulted about this plan before hand……and as the Titans begin a somewhat premature celebration, he brings the proceedings to a halt, taking a microphone to tell Hogan that……

”……actually……that doesn’t work for me…..”

…….talk about a way to kill the mood…..as the celebrations come to an abrupt stop…..Levesque says that the way he sees it, the belt is coming home to the Titans of Wrestling one way or another……because when he beats Piper tonight, at Bash of the Beach it will be Hogan vs. Levesque….Titan vs. Titan……either way, that big gold belt is coming home……

……obviously Hogan is not best pleased with that……telling Levesque to stop going into business for himself…..this whole thing is about ONE thing, what’s best for the Titans of Wrestling…..and right now, the best thing would be for him to get a double count-out victory tonight……and for that WCW Title to be back around the waist of Hollywood……

……Savage on the other hand kind of agrees with Levesque, telling Hogan that either way the Titans win……so what if it’s Hogan vs. Levesque at the Bash……unless of course…..

”…….you don’t think you can beat him…..”

……much to the delight of the commentators that brings some serious dissension to the ranks of the Titans, who begin arguing amongst themselves about what’s best…..until finally the DO agree that what’s best is for them to stop arguing…..and (very) reluctantly Jean Paul agrees to do what’s “right” in our main event later tonight.

Rating: A*

VS.

WCW United States Championship Semi-Final Match

Diamond Dallas Page (w/Kimberly) vs. Mike Awesome

The first match of the evening will dictate the first man entered into the finals of the WCW US Title match at Bash at the Beach……will it be the man that handed the title over to Vince Russo, Diamond Dallas Page……or the man who’s been on a mission to prove that he’s the best big man in the business, Mike Awesome, who in recent weeks has taken out Vader, Bam Bam Bigelow…..and just last week, arguably cost the Giant a spot in the WCW Title tournament……

……this was a decent enough match (by WCW’s standards anyway) not as well received as Awesome’s last encounter with Bam Bam, but then Page isn’t quite at the level yet that former WCW Champion Bam Bam Bigelow is……..in the end though, it was another big man that made the difference here……..

…….the biggest big man there is actually…..as The Giant ambles out from the back……he doesn’t physically get involved in the match, but his mere presence is enough of a distraction that when Awesome turns back to face his opponent, he walks straight into a Diamond Cutter that puts him out of this match and out of the chase for the United States Championship.

Winner: Diamond Dallas Page
Rating: C+

By the time The Giant has made it to the ring, DDP has already celebrated his victory and began to head to the back, leaving Awesome all alone in the ring…..at least he WAS alone until the Giant stepped one giant tree trunk over the top rope, and with two strides he’s standing right over his attacker from last week……

……he yanks Awesome to his feet by his early 90’s mullet, and lets out a big roar as he opens up his big mitt and wraps it around the throat of Mike……Awesome doesn’t plan on taking that ride today however……and he lays a kick right between the legs of the Giant……and that old adage of ‘the bigger you are, the harder you fall’ rings especially true when you kick a seven foot giant in the balls…….

……The Giant drops to his knees clutching at his groin as Awesome slips out of his grasp and heads back up the aisle, telling a nearby camera that this is exactly why he’s the best big man in the sport today……because he’s big……he’s fast……and he’s intelligent too…..modest claims there from Mr. Awesome.

Rating: B+

VS.

Bill Goldberg vs. Sgt. Craig Pittman


Next up it was the debut of former American Football League star Bill Goldberg…….no one really knew what to expect from the newcomer, especially since it was no easy opponent to begin his WCW career, he was in there with Sgt. Craig Pittman…..not a spectacular wrestler by any stretch of the imagination, but he’s been around the company long enough that no one was expecting the newcomer to manhandle him quite as he did, as he put the established star away in around three minutes with a move the announcers declared was called the Jackhammer……..in what Tony Schiavone declared was possibly the greatest debut in the history of our sport……alright Tone, calm down a bit

Winner: Bill Goldberg
Rating: D

VS.

WCW Television Title Semi-Final Match

Brad Armstrong vs. Alex Wright

Would it to surprise you to know that if you compiled a top 20 face superstars in WCW in 1997, Brad Armstrong would come in at number 14? Because it bloody surprised me……it surprised me even more when I booked number 14 face Brad Armstrong to go over number 26 heel Alex Wright even more…..but that’s what I did…….and that’s also the reason I felt the need to bolster the WCW roster with a few of my guys……make the most of that number 14 spot Brad, you won’t be there long………although, I always had a soft spot for Brad Armstrong……maybe he and the Renegade would make a good team……can you tell how uninterested in this match I was? Brad Armstrong wins ladies and gentlemen.

Winner: Brad Armstrong
Rating: C+

As Brad celebrates a very rare victory, he’s interrupted by the wonderful music of Lord Steven Regal (Brum pum pum, Brum pum pum pum, duh duh duh duh, der der do der der derderderd……that’s my rendition of Regals music by the way……I think booking WCW is sending me mad) as the man that Armstrong will be facing in the finals of the WCW Television Title tournament makes his way out from the back, along with Earl Robert Eaton and The Duke, Douglas Williams……

……Regal tells Armstrong that while they are set to do battle at the Bash of the Beach in the finals of the tournament, this is the TELEVISION Title that they shall be competing for, and as such Regal believes it would be more fitting to crown a new TELEVISION champion on the TELEVISION…..say, next week on Monday Nitro if Mr. Armstrong there has the……what do you Americans say…..the balls?……most crude……but then that’s why his Lordship is going to bring some class to a rather tarnished Championship……Armstrong quickly accepts….and just like that we have a title match booked for next week…….

”……good show Mr. Armstrong, good show indeed……and in the spirit of good sportsmanship, I wish you nothing but luck in your endeavour………….as you will Gentlemen…….”

…….good sportsmanship indeed……because with that, The Earl and The Duke rush the ring and proceed to deliver a Royal Beatdown to his Lordships opponent next week, as we go off to commercial, with Brad Armstrong’s great night ending on rather a sour note.

Rating: B+

VS.

WCW United States Championship Semi-Final Match

Chris Benoit vs. Eddie Guerrero

It’s time to see who will be facing Diamond Dallas Page at Bash of the Beach next, for the vacant WCW United States Championship……as Chris Benoit faces off against Eddie Guerrero…..in what was actually the best match of the night…..not that this is surprising in itself considering the men involved, but it was also the best received match of the night, which was more of a surprise……a pleasant one, but a surprise non the less…..and hopefully a sign of things to come in this new WCW…..

……the match itself could have really gone either way, as both men go tooth (or toothless in Benoit’s case) and nail for the victory…….in the end however it was Guerrero who came out on top……but not without the help of a gentleman by the name of VK Wallstreet……who clearly wasn’t happy with his defeat last week at the hands of Benoit, causing enough of a distraction that Benoit spent too long perched on the top rope…..and as he let loose with that Flying Headbutt, Eddie was able to move and Benoit hit nothing but canvas, leaving him easy pickings for a Frog Splash.

Winner: Eddie Guerrero
Rating: B

From there we go to the aisle, where Mean Gene is standing by to shill that hotline (1-900-909-9900) telling us that this is an exciting time to be a WCW fan…..because Vince Russo might not be the only one making the trip down from up north…..and for just $1.59 a minute, he’s got the inside scoop waiting, kids get your parents permission…….but right now Gene has other business to attend to, because his guest right now is none other than the Hot Rod…..Rowdy Roddy Piper……telling Piper that he could be in for a very long night tonight, when he steps into that ring with Jean Paul Levesque……

”…..welllllllll……it seems to me like things aren’t quite so rosy in the Titans Garden as Hollywood would have you think………which is shock in itself when you realise how much manure Hulk Hogan spews our of his mouth on a weekly basis…….you know Gene, that Jean Paul Levesque, he’s a tough kid…….but then he had to be, growing up with a name like that…..and I’m not surprised that he’s not just bowing down and kissing the feet of Hogan like the rest of those reject wannabe’s…….”

…..Piper says he came here to WCW to fight…..he came here to WCW to win Titles……and if he can do both of those things at the expense of Hulk Hogan, then even better…..he says that he knew Hulk Hogan was a snake since the first day he set his eyes on him, and now the whole world can see the same thing…..and tonight, one way or another “Frenchy” is going down……because nothing is going to stop Piper from facing Hogan at the Bash at the Beach.

Rating: A

VS.

WCW Tag Team Championship Quarter-Final Match

Juventud Guerrera & Super Crazy vs. The Samoan SWAT Team

What do you do when the entire face side of your Tag Team Division consists of the Road Warriors and The Pitbulls? Why you start throwing random people together, that’s what you do…..which is why we had the pairing of Ernest Miller and Ice Train a few weeks ago (who at least got a sweet new tag team name…..The Soul Train) and that’s exactly why we get Juventud Guerrera teaming with Super Crazy this week (who don’t get a sweet new team name……uhm, Crazy Juice? Super-tude?…..I got nothing)

…..their opponents are the Samoan Swat Team, Fatu and Samu……no, not the blonde one, this is his brother, Sam Anoa’i Fatu…..the Samoan Savage….The Tonga Kid….he’s had a few names to be fair over the years…..for now however he’s back in WCW, and back teaming with his family, and Samu will be looking to recapture the tag team gold that he held not so long back with Buff Bagwell……

…..they moved one step closer tonight too…..because in the battle of the no-team name vs. the team name, it was the team name that won…..as the Samoans book a place in the semi-finals against the Road Warriors.

Winner(s): The Samoan Swat Team
Rating: B-

From there we see the entrance of the Enforcer, Ray Traylor……as Tony Schiavone tells us that we are going to see some pre-recorded comments…..from the Warrior……

”…….Sting…..many moons ago two great warriors united to fight for the freedom and justice of an unjust world…..the gods above saw the power that these two supreme beings could possess, and saw fit to divide and conquer, leaving these two cosmic entities to circle the same sun but in opposite directions……

……today however, the heavens and the stars have aligned to witness the reunion that could tear down the very foundations of a corrupt power structure……as we two few could lay waste to the scourge that is eating the WCW alive from the inside…..you may be a man that stands alone……I can respect that……I too stand alone, a beacon in the dark……we should stand alone together…..as the Warriors and the Stingers unite….alone as one…..to tear down the few…….”

……Schiavone asks Bischoff if he has a translator….Warrior to English……Bischoff doesn’t……he does however offer the little fact that The Warrior and Sting broke into this business as partners…..and it’s entirely possible that the Warrior is looking to join forces with Sting, to battle the evil Titans of Wrestling……of course, this is a Warrior promo…..he could have been ordering take-out for all we know……either way the crowd liked it……a lot actually…….which is all that really matters.

Rating: A*

VS.

The Warrior vs. Ray Traylor


Traylor finishes up his entrance, and the WCWfied generic Rock Riff that is the Warriors music begins to play…..as the Warrior himself hits the ring at 1000mph……considering the fact that these two have spent a good part of their careers working in the same companies, this may well be the first time that this pair have met in the ring…..in a one on one encounter however……

……Traylor is really relishing in this new role as the Titan’s Enforcer……and when the Warrior stuck his nose in Titan’s business last week, rescuing a rather ungrateful (to say the least) Sting from the planned assault, he became a problem that required teaching a lesson…..unfortunately for him, it was Traylor that received the lesson……a lesson in how to receive…..sell……and lose to the series of clothesline’s, shoulder-blocks and running Splash of the Warrior, that finally put him away

Winner: The Warrior
Rating: B-

The Warrior doesn’t get to celebrate for long however…….because Hogan, Savage, Levesque and the Nasty Boys soon join Traylor in the ring, as the entire group of Titans pounce on the Warrior and proceed to lay a beating on the face painted superstar, who does his best to fend off the attack, sending both Knobbs and Saggs tumbling to the floor with big right hands……one man can only do so much however….especially against the combined forces of Hogan, Savage and Levesque……and soon this mighty warrior is down and at the mercy of the Titans…..

…….as the camera spots Sting lurking high up in the arena, watching expressionlessly as the Warrior is assaulted…..the crowd pops big time when they realise that the Stinger is there to help……except he’s not……he simply stands watching from afar as the Warrior is destroyed……

…….fortunately for the Warrior…..not everyone is willing to stand around watching from the sidelines……as Roddy Piper, Lex Luger, The Giant and The Road Warriors come running down the aisle to halt the proceedings…..they hit the ring and manage to fight off the Titans, who make a hasty retreat up the aisleway once the numbers aren’t quite so overwhelmingly in their favour……

…….before they can get too far however, Roddy Piper grabs a microphone…..telling Levesque and the Titans that since the gang is all here, let’s get this Semi-Final match going right now…….

……Hogan tries to convince Jean Paul to head to the back, stating that they need to formulate their plan and do this when the Titans are ready, not when that skirt wearing freak dictates……but Levesque isn’t listening, and shrugs Hogan off before marching to the ring…….it looks like it’s main event time people……

Rating: A

VS.

WCW Championship Semi-Final Match

Rowdy Roddy Piper vs. Jean Paul Levesque

So here we are, it’s time to find out who will be facing Hollywood Hogan at the Bash of the Beach, for the vacant WCW Championship……and it’s a packed house……with Luger, The Giant and The Warrior opting to stick around on one side of the ring, while the rest of the Titans hang around on the other……emphasising the fact that (as Tony Schiavone emphatically points out) this may be the most important match in WCW history……..

……a win for Piper puts him on a collision course with Hollywood…….a win for Levesque means an all Titan Final…….and a draw……well, as far as Hogan is concerned, a draw means that he gets handed the WCW Title right here tonight……

……this match, honestly wasn’t great…..it was all about the result…..and the ending……the ending being both men battling it out on the floor, surrounded by their respective allegiances cheering them on, when (after breaking up the ten count numerous times) at a count of four, Levesque is able to catch Piper with a Pedigree, driving his skull into the ringside floor…..Piper is down…..and out…..and is probably going to stay down and out…….so Levesque goes to re-enter the ring, happy to take a count-out victory……..

…….Hogan grabs him by the arm however, indicating to Jean Paul to remain outside…..take the double count-out…..before making the universal “belt” motion around his waist, indicating that Hollywood will get the title right now if he does……Levesque isn’t happy about that however, and turns to re-enter the ring…..but again Hogan pulls him back…..except this time Levesque shoves him away, and an argument quickly breaks out between the Titans, who try to diffuse the situation before it escalates……

……..unfortunately that means that they don’t see The Giant lift Piper up dead weight from the floor and roll him back inside the ring……..they also don’t hear the referee reach a count of ten…….in fact it’s not until the bell rings that they realise that Jean Paul Levesque has just lost this match……and Hollywood Hogan has to face Rowdy Roddy Piper at the Bash at the Beach.

Winner: Roddy Piper (via Count-Out)
Rating: B-

If Levesque was mad before, he’s outright furious now at losing this one……and a shoving match breaks out between himself and Hollywood Hogan…….with Hogan equally furious that not only did Levesque fail to do as Hogan decreed, but the fact that he now has to face Roddy Piper in just 13 days time at the Bash at the Beach……speaking of Piper, he’s being helped to his feet in the ring by the WCW boys, who are congratulating him on the opportunity, while at the same time enjoying the sight of the Titans arguing amongst themselves……

……Savage of all people tries to be the voice of reason and calm the situation down…..but Levesque isn’t listening to anyone, shoving Hogan abruptly to one side and storming to the back, leaving his Titan team mates standing…….as the camera zooms in on a line figure standing high up in the crowd…..watching……unmoving…….as the show comes to a close……

Rating: A*

Final Rating: B

Final Thoughts

It’s that time of year again……well, half way to that time of the year again to be precise….as the Wrestling Observer puts out their (insert category here) of the year……so far awards……and to be fair, Dave’s been kind to us this year……obviously we were never going to pick up the Wrestler of the year….that just HAS to go to one of the Japanese guys, as Mitsuharu Misawa picked up the award……and the young wrestler of the year award went to the Far East too, with Michinoku Pro’s Masato Yakushiji getting the honours……and the Tag Team of the year went to All Japan’s Tamon Honda and Steve Williams, who to be fair have held onto the AJPW Unified World Tag Team Championships for almost a year now, after beating Toshiaki Kawada & Akira Taueu back in August of last year……

……surprisingly the independent wrestler of the year (so far) was former WWF Superstar Hercules Hernandez…..although since he’s only had six matches in six months I’m not entirely sure what he’s done to deserve that honour……..

……..the veteran of the year so far, the WWF’s own Nature Boy, Ric Flair……

……show of the year?…..why that would be the WWF’s April In Your House offering, which saw three outstanding matches in Scott Steiner and Shawn Michaels, The Hart Foundation going to a no contest with Flair and Anderson, and a surprisingly good Wolfpac vs. Brian Pillman and Kane match……

……the match of the year came from that very same show……Scott Steiner defeating Shawn Michaels in their five star classic battle over the WWF Championship…..

……and then there’s the company of the year…….or the “one more for the good guys” award as you could call it, because everyone’s favourite International company, the World Wrestling Federation is on it’s way to picking up that award for the second year running……not too bad, even if I do say so myself……follow that Russo…..

WCW Monday Nitro: Week 2, July 1997

Posted 31 May 2019

Week 2, July 1997

Charlotte Coliseum (Charlotte, North Carolina)

ATT: 17,192

TV: 17.94

Announce Team: Tony Schiavone, Eric Bischoff and Mike Tenay

In somewhat of a reoccurring theme here in WCW, Monday Nitro once again opens up with the arrival of the Titans of Wrestling…..these opening monologues however have been a little more…….let’s say, tense……with each passing week, and tonight is quite possibly (alright not possibly, it outright is) the tensest of the bunch…..with neither Hollywood Hogan, nor Jean Paul Levesque looking to be in the greatest of moods following the events of last week…..where Hogan essentially wanted Levesque to take a dive, and hand him the WCW Championship on a technicality…….Levesque however had different ideas, claiming that even if it was Hogan vs. Himself at Bash at the Beach, the end result would be the same, with the World Title coming home to the Titans of Wrestling……

…..Hogan isn’t buying it however…..telling Levesque that he needs to remember who bought him to the dance……

”…….after all dude, without Hollywood man, there would BE no dance……Hollywood Hogan built Wrestling……and I carried it on my massive shoulders to heights that no one had ever seen……Hollywood Hogan made WCW what it is today…….and when you were shown the door up north brother, it was Hollywood Hogan that welcomed you into the fold with open arms……it was Hollywood Hogan that made YOU relevant dude……and while MOST people would be down on their hands and knees worshiping the ground that Hollywood walks on, you basically slapped me in the face brother……..”

……Levesque outright laughs at that, telling Hogan that the LAST thing he did was make HIM relevant……

”…….let me tell you something…..”brother”…….just how ‘relevant’ were you when I was kicking your ass in the ratings week in week out?……ratings that have been solidly going UP may I add, ever since I arrived here in WCW…….so maybe…..just maybe…..it should be YOU down on your hands and knees, worshiping the ground that I walk on……for making WCW relevant again……and maybe……just maybe……..what would be even more of a ratings draw…….would be Jean Paul Levesque as the WCW Champion…….”

…….things get a little bit heated from there, with Savage, Traylor and the Nasty Boys having to get between the arguing Titans, because this looks like it may come to blows……fortunately Savage is able to calm both men down (amazing really considering he’s not the most stable of men himself) telling Hogan that he was in the wrong for interfering in Levesques match……..BUTTTTT……..on the same hand, it wouldn’t have hurt Levesque to take one for the team either……..

”…….because believe me, Jean Paul Levesque WILL be the World Champion, Yeah……..but sometimes you’ve got to know when to hold ‘em, and know when to fold ‘em, dig it?…….”

…….Savage says that right now, the only thing they should be doing, is making sure that the WCW Championship comes back to the Titans…..and as far away from Roddy Piper as possible……whatever it takes……

”…….and tonight, we get to eliminate a few problems along the way, yeah……because the Macho Man and Hollywood ride one more time tonight……and we get to crush Lex Luger for sicking his nose in our business…….the Giant’s gonna fall and won’t get back up………….and you Mr. Jean Paul Levesque……brother, brother, brother……you get to take care of a little problem for the Macho Man tonight, yeah…….”

……Macho says that at Bash at the Beach, he’s going to be going on on one with the Sting man again, because the Stinger wants another shot at taking Savage out….which is fine by Macho……he has no problem going to Bash at the Beach to face Sting…….whether Sting is in any condition to perform however, is an entirely different matter altogether, yeah……

……who would have ever thought Randy Savage would be the voice of reason?…..but he was…..and the Titans walk out of here tonight far more unified than they were when they came in.

Rating: A*

VS.

WCW Tag Team Championship Semi-Final Match

The Road Warriors vs. The Samoan SWAT Team

You could be forgiven for thinking you’d stepped into a time warp in our first match, and woken up in the NWA circa 1989……because it’s The Road Warriors facing off against the Samoan SWAT Team in the first semi-final match in the NWA Tag Te…….erm……WCW Tag Team Championship tournament……

……the match had a very old school feel to it too to be fair……the Samoan’s no sold any blows to the head……Road Warrior Hawk no sold a Piledriver (because why would an age old finishing move cause any damage eh Hawk?)……Animal did a powerslam……and the Road Warriors won with the Doomsday Device to Fatu……..Hawk and Animal best pack their swimsuits……because they’re off to the Bash at the Beach to compete for the WCW Tag Team Championships.

Winner(s): The Road Warriors
Rating: B

It’s backstage we go now, and it’s Mean Gene standing by with the man who will be facing off against Hollywood Hogan at the Bash of the Beach for the vacant WCW Title, Rowdy Roddy Piper……Gene says that last week, Hollywood Hogan was willing to go to any lengths in an attempt to get his hands on the Big Gold Belt, including sacrifice his own man…….Piper survived that…..but he’s got to be concerned about what lengths the Titans will go to in order to recapture that Championship……

”…….welllllllll, to be honest with you Gene, I don’t really remember too much about what happened last week…….I kinda got my bell rung out on that concrete floor……you ever taken one of those Pedigree’s?…..”

“……no, I can’t say I have……”

“…….yeah, do yourself a favour…..don’t……it ain’t fun…….but survive it I did, and on to Bash at the Beach we go……and as for wondering what lengths Hogan will go to to get hold of that belt, maybe he should be worrying about what lengths I will go to…….because let’s face it Gene, he might think he’s a bad guy now……but I’ve been a bad guy all my life……I’m pretty good at being bad ya know?……”

“……….yeah you might be bad…….but we’re Nasty…………”

……still churning out the cheesy (very) early 90’s lines, it’s Knobbs and Saggs….the Nasty Boys…….they tell Piper (who’s currently wrapping the belt from his kilt around his fist as a makeshift weapon) that “the big man” doesn’t want him to make it to the Bash…….

”…….and what the big man wants…..unfortunately for you……the big man gets………”

“……WELLLLL……WE’RE PRETTY BIG GUYS…….WHAT ABOUT WHAT WE WANT?……”

……it seems as though Hawk and Animal have made it to the back following their previous victory……because they appear directly behind the Nasty’s, still looking ready to fight……and there’s every possibility that they WILL be fighting Knobbs and Saggs this coming Sunday Night, should the Nasty’s overcome the Pitbulls later in the evening……but for now, the Road Warriors will have to wait…..because with the odds no longer in their favour, the two Titan members tuck their tails between their legs and walk off……telling both Piper and the Warriors that…..

”……this ain’t over……”

Rating: B+

VS.

The Renegade vs. Mike Awesome


Next up we see Mike Awesome in action, as he faces off against The Renegade…….Awesome is in a bad mood following his early elimination from the WCW United States Championship tournament, thanks in part to the distraction caused by the Giant…….originally I was going to have this a far more open contest than it turned out……but the Renegade decided he was going to turn up late for the show…..and then got p!ssy when he received a warning…….so f*ck him………squashhhhhhhh………and Awesome won with the Awesome bomb after dismantling the moody Renegade.

Winner: Mike Awesome
Rating: C

As the Renegade skulked off to buy a new wristwatch to make sure he’s not late for any future events, Mean Gene makes his way out onto the ramp, shilling the WCW Hotline, before stopping Mike Awesome in his way to the back to get a few words…….

…….Awesome does have a few words……not that he’s a great talker……he could do with a mouthpiece in all honesty……but he’s big and bad enough to get his point across…….he’s dismantled Vader……he destroyed Bam Bam Bigelow…….and now, since he’s not going to be competing for the US Title at Bash at the Beach……he wants the Giant instead…….

”……..so Giant, get ready……..because once I chop you down to size, there will be no doubt who the REAL best big man in the business is………ME!……”

Rating: B

VS.

WCW Tag Team Championship Semi-Final Match

The Pitbulls (w/Madusa) vs. The Nasty Boys

It’s time to see who the Road Warriors will be facing off against at the Bash of the Beach now…….as the Pitbulls face off against the Nasty Boys in the final semi-final match of the Tag Team Tournament……and in all honestly, considering the two teams involved, this wasn’t a bad match by any stretch of the imagination…….don’t get me wrong, it wasn’t pretty……these four men went out there and did what it is that they do best…….brawl………and it was a wild fight……but in the end, it was the Titans that came out on top, after The Enforcer Ray Traylor made his way out to introduce that nightstick to the back of Gary Wolfe’s head……and just like that, the Nasty Boys advance to the WCW Tag Team Title match at the Bash at the Beach.

Winner(s): The Nasty Boys
Rating: B

VS.

WCW Television Championship Match

Rating: B+

……but the beat down isn’t…..as the Titans continue to attack the Warrior like a pack of wild dogs….Hollywood pulls off his weightlifting belt and proceeds to tear strips off the Warriors back with it, before stepping back to let his men continue the assault, as he tells a nearby camera that……

”……the Warrior dude, he should have listened to Mr. Russo brother…..because now, Hollywood Hogan and the Titans of Wrestling are going to finish him off…..”

…….Hogan orders the Nasty Boys to pin the Warrior to the mat by his arms and legs, before sending the Macho Man up to the top rope….Savage stands perched on the top turnbuckle, pointing down at the Warrior, before raising his arms high in the air, signalling that he’s about to drop the elbow…….

”…….IT’S STING!!……STING IS HERE!!…..HE’S FINALLY SEEN ENOUGH, AND HES GOT A BASEBALL BAT!!!…….”

……..Tony Schiavone practically wets himself in excitement, as The Stinger FINALLY answers the Warriors call to action…..and he does indeed have a baseball bat……

…….he dives into the ring and nails both Nasty Boys in the back, crashing the nightstick from The Enforcers hand, just as Savage leaps with a double ax-handle attempt……Sting sees him coming however, and the Macho Man is met by a blow to the exposed ribs with the bat…..and as Sting swings wildly, the Titans of Wrestling scatter to the outside, where they opt to retreat to the safety of the aisleway……

…..Warrior slowly sits up and Sting offers a hand, surprisingly helping his one time partner to his feet…..and as the Warrior asks Sting if he’s ready to join the fight, Sting simply nods as the Warrior snorts and beats his chest in celebration……while the Titans look on from the aisle.

Rating: A*

Final Rating: B+

Final Thoughts

A really strong go home show this week ahead of Sunday’s In Your House Pay Per View……and that’s no small feet when you consider that not only was this (possibly) the first episode of Raw to not feature a single KLiQ member in nearly two years, but the main event featured Scott Steiner and Lance Storm in a one on one contest……and that’s not a knock on Lance, Lance is a tremendous worker, but he’s a good three spots below Scotty on the pecking order, which is why I was hesitant to have this main event the show…..but in the end I trusted my Champion, and Scotty repaid me in leaps and bounds with his performance…..so much so that I’m really excited to see what he and Jericho can do on Sunday……anyway, speaking of Sunday……

WCW Monday Nitro: Week 4, July 1997

Posted 31 May 2019

Week 4, July 1997

Birmingham-Jefferson Arena (Birmingham, Alabama)

ATT: 17,000

TV: 18.63

Announce Team: Tony Schiavone, Eric Bischoff and Mike Tenay

The show opens this week with a quick recap of the events that closed LAST weeks show……with Sting finally coming to the aid of the Warrior, after weeks of persuasion, as the two as the two face painted superstars fought off the Titans of Wrestling…..much to Hollywood Hogans chagrin…….

……and as Tony Schiavone, Eric Bischoff and Mike Tenay welcome us to the show, those same two gentlemen make their way to the ring…….Sting is here……the Warrior is here…..and it looks as though they have something to say……or at least the Warrior does, Sting seems happy enough to brood silently behind him as he announces that……

”……last week the balance of power shifted….the dark clouds of tyranny parted and the black shadow of the Titans of Wrestling faded away to the returning power of professional wrestling…….the stars and moons aligned as the world bore witness to an alliance of the immortals……and that was only the beginning, as the Warriors and the Stingers unite one more time to fight the fight of the celestial beings…….Hollywood Hogan……Macho Man Randy Savage…..you think yourself the Mega Powers of Wrestling……but the true power lies here…..in my hand……and in Stings hand…….and at Road Wild…….you will both be in OUR hands…….”

……Sting works as translator here, saying that what Warrior is saying, is that at Road Wild, he and the Warrior want a match with Hogan and Savage…..but he’s sure to tell a (very hyperactive) Warrior, to not think for one second that he’s going to let his guard down……he’ll team with his former partner if it means getting one step closer to Hogan and the WCW Title, but if the Warrior even LOOKS like he’s going to stab the Stinger in the back, he won’t hesitate to put the man from parts unknown down……..

…….that brings out the entire group of Titans, with Russo and Hogan openly laughing at the challenge……Russo says that Sting and the Warrior aren’t even a cohesive unit NOW, do they really think they have a chance against the two biggest names in professional wrestling history, Hollywood Hogan and Randy Savage?…….

”……I’ll tell you what bro…..how about we give you a little tune up match tonight……you can beat The Enforcer and Jean Paul Levesque tonight……without killing each other……then maybe…..MAYBE, You’ll get a shot at the two main men at Road Wild……”

……neither Sting nor the Warrior seem to have a problem with that……Levesque doesn’t look to pleased to be put in a match without consultation mind, not that Hogan or Savage notice……with Hogan proclaiming that Sting should have left well alone…..

”……you should have kept well away from Titans business brother……because now, you’re gonna get the same treatment that your little buddy there, the Warrior, got last week dude……and not that you will…..but IF you manage to cheat your way past my main general Jean Paul Levesque and The Enforcer tonight, The Mega Powers of Professional Wrestling will make you wish you were never born man…….”

“…….Oh Yeahhhh, Stinger, Stinger, Stinger……all that face paint must have gone to your brain brother……must be cutting off the air supply, Yeah…….because even if you DON’T make it to Road Wild, the Macho Man has a butt kicking with your name on waiting after you hit me with that bat last week……and Warrior, we ain’t done by any means…..we’ve been partners…..we’ve been enemies yeah……but what I’ve always been, is better than you…..oh yeahhhh…….”

…….Russo tells the Warrior and Sting that the Titans COULD put an end to them right here, right now……but where would be the fun in that……but they WILL be seeing them……later tonight.

Rating: A*

VS.

Chris Benoit vs. Buff Bagwell


These two men both made a statement in regards to the Titans of Wrestling last week…..with Chris Benoit stating that he doesn’t need to kiss the ring of Hollywood to get to the top around here…..all he needs to do is do what he does best……wrestle…..

…..Buff Bagwell on the other hand said the complete polar opposite, stating that he can’t think of anything better than to nuzzle at the teat of Hollywood…..ok, he didn’t say that exactly…..but he certainly didn’t sound opposed to working under the wing of the Titans…….

…..considering the huge main event we have set for later in the evening, this match right here was actually the match of the night…..although I suppose that shouldn’t come as too much of a shock when you have a mat mechanic like Chris Benoit in there……Bagwell isn’t the best worker in the world, but he has charisma bursting out of his biceps, which isn’t a bad thing…..although it didn’t look like all the charisma in the world was going to save him here……especially when Benoit had him furiously submitting to the Crippler Crossface…….

……..not that the official saw it…..because the Enforcer of the Titans randomly made his way down to the ring, distracting the attention of the referee long enough for Jean Paul Levesque to slide into the ring…..drag Benoit off Bagwell…..nail him with the Pedigree…..and roll Buff on top of his opponent……before grabbing Ray Traylor and heading back up the aisle, telling a nearby camera that Buff is clearly a smart man……because it’s better to work WITH the Titans rather than work AGAINST them…..and as the official turns his attention back to the match, there’s little else for him to do but count the three and declare Bagwell the winner.

Winner: Buff Bagwell
Rating: B

I don’t think that Buff is entirely sure what just happened…..what he DOES know however, is that he just picked up a big victory…..and he’s going to brag about it, treating the crowd to his ridiculous arm waving strut, before flexing those biceps…..he goes to exit the ring, but then sees Benoit still down…..and being the asshat that he is, he decides that he’s going to kick the man while he’s down…..stomping the crap out of the Crippler, before heading up to the second rope, waiting for Benoit to get back to his feet…..sizing him up for the Blockbuster……..

……which is when Diamond Dallas Page comes running down the aisle….clearly still ticked off from last weeks post match attack on the part of Bagwell……Buff doesn’t see Page till it’s too late however, and DDP is able to slide into the ring and knock Buffs lights out with a Diamond Cutter right from the second rope……

……he goes over to help Benoit to his feet, but The Crippler doesn’t realise Page was there to help, and he nails DDP right between the eyes with a right hand, knocking him down to the mat, before stepping over him and storming to the back.

Rating: B

VS.

The Renegade vs. Sgt. Craig Pittman


It’s The Renegade vs Craig Pittman now……The Renegade won with a splash from the top…….

……what? You were expecting more information? The match sucked, trust me, that’s all the information you need……it did serve it’s purpose though……

Winner: The Renegade
Rating: D

……which was to provide a victim for this mysterious masked man…..who runs down to the ring to put a beating on the unsuspecting Renegade…..who is soon joined by another smaller gentleman, who seems to be guiding the actions of this unknown newcomer…….(pstttt, between me and you……it’s enhancement talent Kanyon under a mask…..but don’t let the marks know that)……

……with his apparent manager directing traffic, the masked man destroys the poor (could have had a push if he didn’t p!ss me off) Renegade, finally finishing him off with a Sitout Reverse STO (that’s a Flatliner to you and me) as the announce team speculate on who these two men could be.

Rating: D

VS.

WCW World Tag Team Championship Match

The Road Warriors (c) vs. The Faces of Fear (w/Jimmy Hart)


The Road Warriors are in action next, putting their tag team championships on the line against possibly the only team in WCW that can match them for pure brute strength, Meng and The Barbarian, the Faces of Fear…..

……you get exactly what you were expecting here, two big burly teams stuffing the hell out of each other…..unsurprisingly the match was hurt slightly by the lack of selling on display (go figure) but it was still the second best match on the whole show, with the Warriors winning after Hawk nailed Meng with a Clothesline from the top rope.

Winner(s): ……and Still WCW Tag Team Champions……The Road Warriors
Rating: B

From there we head to the back, where the Blue Bloods appears to be having high tea, as they sit around a table surrounded in little cucumber sandwiches, scones, miniature cakes and a fancy china tea set…….Mean Gene is there, looking highly confused by all of this, interrupting the gathering to as his Lordship whether or not he accepts the challenge of Rick Martel……to face him one on one for the Television Title…..and leave Earl Robert and The Duke in the back……Regal looks at Gene like he’s something he’s just stepped in, before quickly regaining his composure as a British Gentleman…….

”………first of all Mr. Oakerlund, where are my manner’s……Robert, a cup of tea for this fine gentleman if you will…….”

…….the Earl pours Gene a cup, adding a splash of milk and two sugar cubes, while the Duke thrusts a plate of cucumber sandwiches at him……Gene takes the tea and has a sip…..grimaces…..and discreetly spits it back into the cup and placing both the tea and the sandwich back down……he again repeats his question, will Regal put the title on the line one more time or not?…..

”…….but of course my good man……if there’s one thing we brits are known for it’s our fighting spirit……and if Mr. Martel is so intimidated by these two fine figures of men, then I would be happy for them to remain in the back……after all, they are merely there in an advisory role after all…….”

…….an advisory role? Gene isn’t having any of that…..stating that not only did Eaton and Williams help him WIN the title, they have directly helped him to defend it ever since…..

”…….yes, they have advised me on the best strategy to guarantee victory…..what don’t you understand about that you cretin……”

…….Gene scoffs at that, stating that the two men in question have physically interfered in all of Regals matches……The Duke does a spit take at that, spraying tea across the table, telling Gene that he is besmirching their good names as Gentlemen and professionals…..and he may wish to employ the services of an optician, because clearly his eyesight is beginning to fail him……

……Regal tells Mean Gene that he can tell Martel that his challenge is accepted…..and when his Lordship stretches him into submission, he’ll have no more excuses to give……

”…….now if you don’t mind, we WERE trying to have a civilised conversation before you so rudely interrupted…..you know where the door is, off you pop there’s a good chap…..”

Rating: B+

VS.

WCW United States Championship Match

Eddie Guerrero (c) vs. Juventud Guerrera


We’ve already seen the WCW Tag Team Titles successfully defended this evening…..now it’s time for the US Champion Eddie Guerrero to put his gold on the line, as he goes up against the masked Juventud Guerrera……it was a good match too, the only thing that let it down was Juvi’s popularity seeing how he’s still relatively new to the company……

……hopefully there will be a few new arrivals somewhere nearer to Eddies level arriving soon for him to feud with, because right now the only two faces at his level are really DDP and Chris Benoit, and both of those men are going to be otherwise occupied for the next month or two……anyway, to no surprise, Eddie wins with the Frog Splash.

Winner: ……and Still WCW United States Champion……Eddie Guerrero
Rating: B-

VS.

Bill Goldberg vs. Disco Inferno


“Goooooldberg…….Goooooldberg…….Goooooldber g……..Goooold”……actually, we’re not there yet…….the man is certainly rising in stature but he’s still a nobody, even if he is at the midcard already……..his victim tonight? The Disco Inferno……this was actually Goldbergs best and longest match……clocking in at an impressive 4:38…..although at least two minutes of that was Disco running for his life from the seemingly unstoppable rookie……Goldberg caught him in the end however……Spear…..Jackhammer…..Splat……G oodnight Disco.

Winner: Bill Goldberg
Rating: C-

Gene is back on the stick next, backstage with Mike Awesome and his apparent manager, The Walking Riot, Missy Hyatt……Gene says that Awesome may have been making quite the name for himself recently……but he’s making quite the laundry list of enemies along the way…..and tonight, the Giant will be facing off against Vader…..but in reality, Gene thinks they would both rather be in there with Awesome…..

……Missy does the talking here…..that’s what she’s there for since Mike can’t talk for sh!t…..(he sure looks menacing in the background though)…..she says that Gene couldn’t be more wrong…….The Giant, Vader, Bam Bam Bigelow……they might all THINK they want to be in there with Mike Awesome, but they’d be wrong……she says that Mike Awesome is the BEST big man in professional wrestling today……she pauses for a moment to run her hand over Awesome’s chest, before saying that her client isn’t slow and lumbering like the Giant……he’s not overweight and past it like Vader…….he’s ALL man…….and if there’s one thing Missy knows, it’s a real man when she sees it……and one by one all the so called giants of WCW will come tumbling down…..

……Gene says that all of that may be true…..but no matter how big, strong or agile Mike Awesome is……how can one man possibly compete with the army of Giants chasing him?……

”……..one man Gene?……..who ever said that Mike was going to be alone?…….believe me, Missy Hyatt is too much woman for ANY one man to handle……even a man like Mike Awesome………we’ll see you around Gene…..”

……with that Missy gives Gene a little wink, before she and Awesome walk off, as Gene sends us (slightly confused) back to ringside.

Rating: B+

VS.

The Giant vs. Big Van Vader


And speaking of The Giant and Vader, those two men are up next……and boy was it ugly…..

…..in fairness, it didn’t help that both guys were currently faces, but still……it was what it was…..two beasts pummelling each other, seemingly for the rather poor excuse that both men want a piece of Mike Awesome…..well, that and the fact that Vader and Bam Bam cost The Giant his match last week, all be it by an unintentional disqualification, but that doesn’t matter to the Giant……

……the finish didn’t help the match either…….because there wasn’t really one……both men want Mike Awesome…..

…….both men got Mike Awesome……at ringside that is…….as the self proclaimed best big man in the business made his way down the aisle with his manager……stopping just short of ringside to goad both Vader and The Giant……..

……..which is why neither man saw this monstrosity of a man sliding into the ring behind them with a steel chair……smashing both competitors in the back with the weapon as Awesome storms the ring to join in in the assault…….no contest folks (eye roll)

Winner: No Contest
Rating: B-

The beating continues, with the announcers completely taken back by this unknown man……Schiavone says he’s big…..he’s very big…….and clearly this is the back up Missy was hinting at In her previous promo……as for Missy, she kindly hands Awesome a chair as well, and the two men absolutely smash the sh!t out of Vader and the Giant, leaving them sprawled out on the mat……..

……until The Total Package and Bam Bam Bigelow come running down the aisle, making the save for their respective (occasional) partners…….Awesome and his Compadre decide that they have inflicted enough damage for one night, and get the hell out of dodge before the Calvary hits the ring, retreating up the aisle with Missy rubbing their backs with a huge smile on her face, while Luger and Bigelow attend to Vader and the Giant.

Rating: A*

VS.

The Warrior & Sting vs. Jean Paul Levesque & Ray Traylor


It’s main event time now, and it’s Sting and The Warrior facing off against the Titans of Wrestling combination of Jean Paul Levesque and The Enforcer Ray Traylor……and this watch was epic…..ly bad…..ok it wasn’t that bad, but for a main event it was pretty poor…….

…….this was the first time in probably ten years that Sting and the Warrior had teamed up, and they still worked pretty well…..not as well as Levesque and Traylor however, who surprisingly had some really good chemistry as a team……in the end however, they couldn’t translate that chemistry to a victory…..because after the Warrior sent Levesque tumbling to the floor with a Clothesline over the top, Sting put Traylor away with the Scorpion Death Drop, booking he and the Warrior a match with Hollywood Hogan and Randy Savage at Road Wild.

Winner(s): Sting & The Warrior
Rating: B-

Speaking of Hogan and Savage……it didn’t take long for them to come running out the second the bell rang……along with the Nasty Boys……Savage picks up a steel chair on his way, clearly looking for retribution on the Stinger following that baseball bat shot last week…….he charges at Sting, steel chair raised, but the Warrior shoves Sting our of harms way, taking the chair shot himself as Sting falls to the outside…….The Titans descend on the fallen Warrior like a pack of wild dogs, kicking scratching and clawing at the face painted fighter……

……Sting however, still on the outside, is reaching under the ring for something…….and as he pulls out two black bats, it seems he came prepared…….he slides back into the ring and starts nailing the Titans with shots…….Knobbs, Saggs, Traylor……Savage takes another shot to the ribs…….and as the Warrior pulls himself to his feet, Sting tosses a bat to him…..and the Titans bail immediately……mainly because the Warrior is running around the ring like a lunatic swinging a baseball bat wildly over his head…….

…….Schiavone is going crazy……telling us that Warrior saved Sting…..Sting saved Warrior……at at Road Wild we will see four of the biggest names in ALL of professional wrestling go at it, in what could very well prove to be the most important night in the history of our sport……

……and as the Titans retreat, The Warrior is jubilant…..and even Sting cracks a rarely seen smile…….because he knows that in just a few short weeks, Hollywood Hogan will be standing across the ring from him……goodnight fans.

Rating: A*

Final Rating: B+

Final Thoughts

Another great show for us…..and a surprisingly decent effort from WCW…..although our ring work is head and tails above theirs, they have to rely on angles to put on a good show, we’re the best of both worlds…..that’s what happens when you only have our rejects to work with I guess Vinny…..there’s surprisingly little news either…..that will all be coming in a few weeks at Summerslam, where virtually every match has major ramifications……

…..in a little bit of news from Japan, apparently AJPW are not planning on renewing Jumbo Tsuruta’s contract when it’s up in a few weeks…..I can understand why, he’s 46 now and only semi-active, but he’s still over like rover, you’d think they could find something for him to do…..
