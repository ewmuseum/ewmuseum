A New Generation…..a new attitude? Pay-per-view

WWF In Your House: October 1996

Posted 19 May 2019

Week 3, October 1996

El Generico Stadium (North West)

ATT: 38,000

Buy: 1.28

Ladies and gentlemen, Halloween is right around the corner……and it’s somewhat fitting that after In Your House: Long Live the KLiQ…..and In Your House: KLiQ Rulez…….that on the eve of All Hallows’ eve…..we may well be witnessing In Your House: Death of the KLiQ…….we have a promo package to open the show…..but instead of your usual highlights and angles leading up to tonight’s matches, we have a multitude of shots of the KLiQ at each other’s throats……or more accurately…..the KLiQ at Shawn Michaels throat……as we see arguments…..slaps…….and lots and lots of Sweet Chin Music……as Sean Waltman, Justin Credible……and finally Kevin Nash are shown eating the sole of Shawn Michaels boot…..before we finish on a shot of the KLiQ arguing to close out Monday Night Raw……with their voices slowly being drowned out by the sound of Psycho Sid’s maniacal laughter…..as we go down to ringside where Vince McMahon, Jerry The King Lawler and Jim Ross welcome us to the show.

They also spend time discussing the problems within the KLiQ, with Lawler particularly giddy at the prospect of trouble in paradise…..he says that tonight Shawn Michaels head will NOT be in the game…..and without the KLiQ there to back him up, there is no way that the Heartbreak Kid is walking out tonight with that WWF Championship around his waist…..

Rating: N/A

VS.

WWF Intercontinental Championship Match

The British Bulldog (c) vs. Rich Steiner (w/Ted DiBiase)

We open up the action for the evening with our first title match, as The British Bulldog puts the prestigious Intercontinental Championship on the line against Ted DiBiase’s latest Million Dollar Corporation recruit…..the man who sold out his brother for a flashy suit….Rich Steiner. For all DiBiase’s boasts that “everyone has a price” and that he is going to lead his Corporation to glory…..his group, in all its various incarnations, has won precisely diddly squat since its inception back in 1994….so you’ve got to believe that Steiner is feeling the pressure here tonight.

If he is though, he isn’t showing it……in fact Steiner has developed quite the cocky streak since his alliance with DiBiase, strutting around the ring and looking down his nose at the peons in the crowd and the commoners across the ring from him…..The two men slowly circle each other as the bell rings….before coming face to face in a stare down, where the pair trade some insults that finally breaks down into a shoving match. They lock up in the collar and elbow and the Bulldog impressively powers Steiner into the corner where, after a clean break, he hits a double biceps pose to Steiner. It’s not often that Steiner is over powered, and he’s clearly not happy about it….because he charges at the Champion, only to find himself on the receiving end of an arm drag…..and then a second arm drag when he tries it again. Steiner is furious and wants another tie up with the Bulldog….Davey Boy accepts and this time Steiner finds himself locked into a side headlock. Rich uses the ropes to propel Smith off….but the Bulldog nails him with a running Shoulder Block on his return which sends the Challenger bailing to the outside, where he seeks council from his manager.

Davey looks to follow Steiner out the floor, but thinks better of it….instead choosing to bide his time in the ring as his opponent stretches out the ten count for as long as possible. Jim Ross remarks that the Bulldog is wrestling smart, because he doesn’t have to beat Steiner here, Steiner has to beat him if he wants to take home the Gold. Both men back inside and Steiner feigns a collar and elbow, but actually slips behind the Bulldog looking for a belly to back suplex……The Bulldog is able to counter with an Irish Whip however and he catches Steiner on the return and picks him up with a big delayed military press slam….the impact of which again sends Rich halfway across the ring…..and when he does finally pull himself back to his feet, Davey Boy sends him flying over the top rope with a running clothesline over the top rope and out to the floor, where DiBiase is waiting. This time the Bulldog does follow him to the outside, but it backfires, as Steiner is pulled out of the way of another Clothesline attempt by DiBiase….leaving Smith nothing to connect with but the steel post.

Back inside the ring and Steiner whips Davey Boy into the ropes….Bulldog attempts to counter into a crucifix pinning move, but Steiner is able to use his opponents momentum to spin him all the way around to his front, where he sends him flying overhead with a big release belly to belly suplex. Steiner applies a reverse chin lock to the downed Bulldog, but Davey fights to his feet and attempts to break free with a series of elbows……Steiner cuts him off however, sending Davey for a ride with an Irish Whip and delivering a back elbow of his own to the face of the Champion on his return. Steiner has slipped into the role of the arrogant heel rather well since his new found allegiance with the Million Dollar Man….and DiBiase is a real asset at ringside, because Steiner is the only member of the Corporation so far (past and present) that he has truly clicked with. Steiner sends Bulldog hard into the turnbuckles and follows in with a stiff corner clothesline that leaves Davey Boy sat slumped on the mat. Steiner mounts the second turnbuckle over his fallen opponent and screams at the crowd that “…..that’s why I’m Rich……and you’re not……” but he takes too long about it, and the Bulldog is able to catch hold of him from behind and deliver a belly to back suplex from the middle rope.

The two men get back to their feet and the Bulldog tries to continue the assault, but he’s more beaten down than Steiner by this point, and despite managing to deliver a trifecta of right hands, his opponent is able to gut him off with a knee to the midsection….then grabs him by the back of the head as he’s doubled over and drives his face into the mat. A series of Steinerlines almost take Davey’s head off…..but the it’s the Piledriver that follows that causes the most damage, and we very nearly have a new Champion after a longgggg two count. Steiner isn’t happy with the kick out, asking the referee how much its gonna cost to hire him a maths tutor……because by all accounts this official doesn’t know how to count to three. The ref tells him that it was a two however….and to concentrate on his opponent……Steiner does exactly that, and picks the Bulldog up over his shoulder in what looks to be the position for Davey Boys own Running Powerslam……but before Rich can add insult to injury, the Intercontinental Champion wriggles free and drops behind Steiner, before sending him chest first hard into the corner and catching a quick surprise roll up out out of nowhere for as equally as close count as Steiner scored a moment ago.

The near fall leaves Steiner shaken, as he realises that he very nearly lost this one. He swings wildly at the Bulldog, but Davey blocks each shot, countering with a pinch of his own, before backing Steiner into the corner with two stiff headbutts. The Champion mounts the middle rope and gives the ten punches of doom as the crowd count along with each one, then whips Steiner across the ring to the opposite corner. High Back Body Drop and a running clothesline from Smith is followed up with that patented delayed Vertical Suplex. The Bulldog signals to the crowd that this one is over, and he hoists Steiner up over his shoulder…..then hits hard with the Running Powerbomb for the……

……one……

……..two………

…….but not the three……

……..because at the last possible second, Ted DiBiase yanks Steiner to the outside by his ankle, breaking up the count. Deebs pulls Rich back to his feet at ringside, and the official reprimands DiBiase for interfering, but Steiner is having none of it…..screaming that “…..HE DIDN’T DO NOTHIN’……I KICKED OUT STOOPID……”

………and while that argument ensues Southern Justice come running down the aisle. Dennis Knight clips the Bulldogs knee from behind before Mark Canterbury drives him to the mat with an inverted DDT. The two men slither out of the ring, while Steiner informs the referee to shut up because he has a match to win. Steiner climbs up onto the apron, then goes all the way up to the top rope, biding his time until the Champion gets slowly to his feet……then the Corporation man lets fly…..and the Bulldog eats a Bulldog from the top. Steiner makes the cover…..and three seconds later, Ted DiBiase’s Million Dollar Corporation finally has its first taste of gold.

Winner: ….and New WWF Intercontinental Champion…..Rich Steiner

Rating: B+

We go to some pre recorded footage now from earlier in the evening, and we are treated to a visual representation of the KLiQ arriving at the arena……but…..not exactly together…..Nash, Hall, Waltman and Credible are shown walking the halls as a tightly knit, black and white shirt wearing cohesive unit. On the flip side we get a split screen shot of the rather lonesome looking Shawn Michaels and Sunny walking through the Parking lot and into the arena alone….clearly the issues between the WWF Champion and the rest of the KLiQ are at breaking point, but everybody knows that where the Gold goes, Sunny is sure to follow.

Rating: B-

It’s time for some Tag team action now……and while we do know that the Hardy Boys will be in action, we don’t know who against…….the Legion of Doom know however, and they’re out now alongside Matt and Jeff. Animal takes a microphone and tells the Hardys how impressed the LoD have been with the youngsters progress……they have gone from TV Cannon fodder, to a team with real heart…..beating the best that the Tag Team division had to offer, both past and present…….but they started this journey because they wanted to prove that they could hang with the big boys…..well tonight, they’ve got two of the biggest boys that the Legion of Doom could find…..“….TELL ‘EM HAWK…..”

“……WELLLLLL……IT SEEM TO ME LIKE MATT AND JEFF HAVE GONE FROM HARDY BOYS TO HARDY MEN…..A LITTLE L.oD LITE…..BECAUSE YOU CAN BOTH SOAR LIKE A HAWK…..NOW WE JUST NEED TO BRING OUT YOUR INNER ANIMAL……AND YOU MIGHT BE ABLE TO FLY……BUT IN TIMES OF NATURAL DISASTERS……ALL FLIGHTS ARE CANCELLED……ITS TIME TO GET DOWN AND DIRTY…….UHHHHH, WHAT A RUSHHHHHH!!!!…..”

With that the sound of rumbling fills the arena…..

……and out from the back come two mammoth men…..Typhoon and Earthquake…..The Natural Disasters.

Rating: C+

VS.

The Hardy Boys vs. The Natural Disasters

Matt and Jeff have been in this ring with men like the Harris Brothers…..they’ve been in the ring with monsters like Mankind and Kane……they have been in the ring with mountains like The Nation’s Nelson……but never before have they stood across the ring from TWO immovable objects at the same time…..even when fighting Nelson they had the less ominous Bobby Knight to focus on……but Earthquake and Typhoon are a different kettle of fish……each man is as monstrous as the other and there is no smaller team mate to isolate.

Hawk warned the Hardys that all flights are cancelled during times of Natural Disaster……and they probably should have listened……because despite the girth of their opponents they still try to mount an aerial offence…..all they manage to do is stagger their opponents briefly however……and as Matt and Jeff come flying off the top with stereo cross body’s, they are both plucked out of mid air……walked around the ring……and crushed with a Powerslam a piece from Earthquake and Typhoon. Matt is roughly tossed to the outside as the Disasters focus their offence onto Jeff…..and poor Jeff feels every second of that offence….

….these aren’t a pair of Ham and Eggers in the ring with the Hardys….these are former WWF Tag Team Champions…..and a team that took the Legion of Doom themselves to the limit on more than one occasions…..in fact, Earthquake himself has crushed many a wrestling legend….even the immortal Hulk Hogan found himself on the end of the debilitating Earthquake Splash. Obviously there’s nothing technical about this one…..the Disasters simply use their massive frames to squash the younger Hardy like a bug. It’s all high impact…..from running avalanche splashes in the corner….huge clotheslines…..and rib breaking body slams……in fact Typhoon almost picks up the victory after crushing Jeff with a running splash…..then remaining in place as Earthquake delivers a running splash to the back of his own partner….leaving Jeff to take the full force of almost a ton coming down on top of him….the only thing that saved the match there was Matt hitting the ring to break up the pin.

Hawk and Animal remain at ringside throughout the match, as they have for the previous Hardy Boys matches….and while they haven’t (and don’t) physically interfere in any of the bouts, it doesn’t stop them offering words of encouragement….shouting at Jeff to “come on…..you wanted testing…..show us what you’ve got”. Thins look pretty bleak for young Jeff…..at least until he taps into his inner animal…..because as Typhoon holds onto Hardy from behind, Earthquake comes running in, looking to make a Hardy Sandwich, with Jeff as the filling……Jeff however, kicks his leg back, catching Typhoon right below the belt…….Hardy moves out of the way, leaving The Natural Disasters to have a Disaster of their own as they collide into each other. Earthquake goes down and Typhoon goes tumbling to the outside….as Jeff makes the hot Tag.

Matt hits the ring (and hits Earthquake) with a series of strikes. He doubles the big man over and begins clubbing blows into the back of the hefty Canadian. Earthquake manages to send Matt across the ring however with a big shove…..but Jeff instantly takes his place, and now he is clubbing down on Quake. The former Sumo star manages to deliver a big shove to Jeff, just long enough to catch his breath…..but before he has chance to recover, BOTH Hardys come charging in….and go full Demolition with duel clubbing blows to the big Canadian, until he’s down on the mat. Typhoon gets back into the ring and charges at Matt, who sidesteps him and uses his own momentum to send him crashing into the turnbuckles. Matt drops down to all fours in front of Typhoon and Jeff comes sprinting across the ring, leaping off his brothers back and crashing into Typhoon with the Poetry in Motion.

Earthquake is back to his feet and he tries to run through both Hardys, but the Brothers move and again the Natural Disasters collide…..this time in the corner, and as Earthquake slumps against his partner with the air knocked out of him, again Matt drops to his hands and knees….and again Jeff lets fly with Poetry in Motion…..this time however as he collides with Earthquake, Earthquake crushes Typhoon, who drops to the mat and rolls to the outside. Big Quake staggers forward into the middle of the ring, where Matt is waiting to deliver a Twist of Fate….Jeff climbs to the top….and it looks like we have permission to fly once again, because he comes leaping off with a Swanton Bomb. Both Matt and Jeff jump on top of Earthquake for the cover…..and against much larger opposition, The Hardy Boys are your winners.

Winner(s): The Hardy Boys

Rating: C+

While the Legion of Doom congratulate the Hardys on another well earned victory, we head to the back where another celebration is taking place. Dok Hendrix is in the lavish locker room of the Million Dollar Corporation……and he’s trying to get word from the CEO Ted DiBiase…..as well as from the new WWF Intercontinental Champion, Rich Steiner. Unfortunately Steiner is in the process of receiving a Champagne Shower from Southern Justice and is too busy trying to use his new title belt as a makeshift umbrella to speak……DiBiase has something to say though. He tells Hendrix that this night was the culmination of months worth of negotiations with the intelligent half of the Steiner Brothers…..and that’s why Rich Steiner is stood here tonight as a singles Champion…..and Scott Steiner isn’t…..but this is just the first step on the Corporations master plan…..today the Intercontinental Championship…..tomorrow the World……that’s the World Tag Team Championships……and more importantly, the WWF World Heavyweight Championship.

But that’s for then…..and tonight is about the here and now….and in the here and now the Million Dollar Corporation are going to sit back and watch, as the Heartbreak Kid Shawn Michaels, kicks Psycho Sid’s ungrateful teeth down his seven foot throat. Hendrix tells DiBiase that Shawn Michaels head might not be in the game tonight, given the problems within the KLiQ……in fact there’s every chance that what we will see, is a brand new WWF Champion in Psycho Sid…..

DiBiase just laughs however…..before telling Hendrix that he’s forgetting one thing……all successful Corporations know when the time is right to subcontract their services out…..for the right price obviously…..and the Million Dollar Man will ALWAYS honour a business agreement.

Rating: A

VS.

Marc Mero (w/Sable) vs. Billy Gunn (w/Mr. Perfect)

It’s back to ringside now, as Marc Mero looks to teach Billy Gunn a lesson in chivalry….as Gunn and his Executive Consultant Mr. Perfect don’t seem to realise that no means no. For weeks now they have been hounding Mero’s Valet, the beautiful Sable…..and after constantly disregarding the young lady’s plea’s to be left alone, it’s time for Mero to handle this the old fashioned way.

Mero is fired up for this from the moment the bell rings…..and Perfect can see it too…..which is why he instructs his client to stall at the start of this one, leaving Mero to get more and more frustrated as Gunn repeatedly avoids the lock up. At the instruction on the official the two men eventually tie up, but Mr. Perfect’s stalling tactic worked like a charm, because Mero is so frustrated that Gunn is able to get the upper hand, locking in and armbar before clawing at the eyes of Mero. Mero backs off and the cocky Billy Gunn invites him to lock up again…..Mero isn’t waiting for any more stalling tactics however and catches Gunn square in the chin with a standing dropkick….he then catches his charging opponent with a snapmare before uncharacteristically raking the back of the seated Gunn.

Now it’s Gunn that is frustrated, and he comes running at Mero, but the Wildman is able to leap up and hit a clumsy Hurricanrana that he rolls through so that he is sat on the chest of his opponent…..and rains down blows onto the skull of Billy Gunn…..a second Dropkick by Mero, who then goes through the middle rope to the ring apron, before propelling himself back into the ring (and into his opponent) with a Springboard Clothesline that sends Gunn bailing to the outside to seek some guidance from his Consultant. Mero comes charging, looking for that somersault attack over the top rope…..but Perfect and Gunn take a walk, congratulating each other on their initiative…..unfortunately Mero has slid out under the bottom rope and appears behind the two men, smashing their heads together and tossing Gunn back into the ring.

Mero goes to follow his opponent to the inside, but Mr. Perfect is up and quickly grabs Mero by the ankle giving Gunn the opportunity to drop a knee to back of the Wildman’s neck. He picks up Mero and takes him to the corner, connecting with a series of closed fists…..Mero is able to seize hold of Gunn however and spin HIM into the corner…..but Billy goes to the eyes to escape, before tossing Mero hard to the outside. With Gunn distracting the attention of the official, Perfect picks up a chair and advances on the unsuspecting Mero…..but before he can deliver the blow, Sable comes running up behind him and drops to her knees to deliver a low blow. Perfect drops to the floor….and drops the chair…..which Sable retrieves and runs around the opposite side of the ring with, as Mero drags himself back into the ring.

Back on the inside and Gunn puts the boots to Mero, then picks him up and sends him into the corner. He once again unloads with a series of closed fists….this time Mero has no answer to them and Gunn is able to whip him hard into the opposite corner, which Mero collides with at such impact that he crumples to the mat in pain. On the outside Perfect has finally recovered from the low blow at the hands of Sable, and is currently stalking the terrified young lady around the floor. Mero attempts to mount a comeback, but he’s too distracted by Perfects antics to fully focus his attention on his opponent….and Gunn capitalises with a high knee to the kidneys. He slams Mero hard to the mat and then comes off the middle rope with a driving elbow drop. He makes the cover, but it’s only good for two and so he begins to stomp the tar out of his man, just as Perfect catches up with Sable on the outside.

Perfect grips hold of the young blonde, and forces her to look at the ring, where Billy is indicating that this one is over. He kicks Mero in the midsection, doubling him over…..then runs the ropes and looks to connect with the Famouser…..but Mero moves at the last second, and all Gunn accomplishes is to crash and burn ass first on the canvas. The Wildman drops to the outside, which causes Perfect to release Sable, shoving her into Mero’s waiting arms as he retreats around to the opposite side of the ring. Meanwhile Gunn has recovered enough to try and mount some offence and he comes leaping off the apron with a double Ax-Handle….but Mero sees him coming and cuts him off with a right hand to the mid section on his way down.

The two men get back into the ring and the match descends into a slug fest….with Mero getting the upper hand….but again Perfect continues his harassment of Sable….and again it acts as a distraction that leads to Mero getting dropped on his head with a belly to back Suplex. Desperate to escape the advances of Perfect, Sable hops up onto the apron……right into Billy Gunn’s waiting arms…..who plants a long kiss right on her as Sable desperately tries to break free. Gunn finally releases the kiss and fans himself mockingly…..but Sable is not impressed…..and slaps the taste out of Billy with a stinging blow that turns him all the way around……straight into a quick left jab from Marc Mero. Perfect jumps onto the apron and receives a jab of his own that knows him back down to the floor, which just leaves Mero the task of covering his unconscious opponent…..and three seconds later Sables honour is finally defended.

Winner: Marc Mero

Rating: B

VS.

Chris Candido vs. Sean Waltman (w/Justin Credible)

If you had asked the average fan on the street three months ago to tell you who they thought one of the biggest thorns in the KLiQs side would be, you could pretty much bet the house that not one person would have said Body Donna Skip……and yet here we are three months later…..and the repackaged Chris Candido has cost the KLiQ the Intercontinental Championship….the Light Heavyweight Championship…..and even a member….as he defeated Hunter Hearst Helmsley at last months In Your House, in a loser leaves town match…….so all in all that’s made him a pretty big damn thorn.

And while we are on the subject of the Light Heavyweight Championship….it’s a Championship that appears to have caught the eye of the former fitness fanatic, because while he has been quick to lend a hand when Champion Rey Mysterio has needed it, he has certainly been caught admiring the little luchador’s gold……but then so has his opponent this evening, Sean Waltman…..the man who Mysterio defeated to begin with to start his reign….and considering that both men have their eye on the same prize, it’s a pretty good guess that the winner of this one would have a pretty good claim to be next in line for a shot at that very Championship.

Candido is going to have his work cut out for him here tonight however, because Waltman hasn’t come to this one alone, he’s got Justin Credible by his side….and while these two do have an excellent match, it’s not without its share of interference from the KLiQ man…..although not to begin with…..in fact Candido starts of strong with a technical flurry that ends with Waltman locked in a waistlock. Candido tries a takedown from the rear which Waltman blocks…..then tries to hit a German Suplex which Waltman also blocks….and the two men eventually find themselves tangled up in the ropes. Clean break from Candido, but Waltman hits him with a cheap right hand which infuriates Chris, who drives his opponent into the corner with right hands of his own, before delivering a flurry of forearm strikes and a snap Suplex. Candido tries for a running clothesline, but Waltman ducks and unleashes a series of martial arts kicks that culminate with a big jumping back kick that floors Candido.

Waltman chops at his own crotch, then tells his opponent to “Suck It”, before lifting him to his feet and attempting a spinning forearm smash….this time however it’s Candido who ducks, and he nails his opponent with a back Suplex. Waltman gets back to his feet and attempts to connect with a high kick, but his foot gets caught and Candido hits a spinning leg whip, then wrenches on with a leg lock that Waltman can only break by clutching on to the bottom rope. Candido looks to keep up the offence, but clearly in need of a breather Waltman reaches up and grabs his opponent by the tights and flings him through the middle ropes and out to the floor. Then clutches at his knee screaming to draw the officials attention away from the face that as Candido gets back to his feet, Justin Credible sends him flying into the ring steps.

Waltman’s knee makes a miraculous recovery as Candido struggles back into the ring, and he whips His opponent hard into the corner and follows in with a running clothesline. Again he tells Candido to “Suck it”, then sends him into the ropes, where he nails a spinning heel kick on Candido’s return. He walks around the ring taunting his opponent, delivering a little kick here and there….more for insult than injury. He attempts to whip Candido into the corner again but this time Chris reverses it, and it’s Waltman that hits the turnbuckles. Candido runs in, looking for a corner clothesline of his own, but Waltman hits a back body drop that leaves Candido teetering on the apron….then sends him crashing to the floor with a springboard dropkick.

Waltman comes crashing down on his opponent with a pescado over the top rope, then attempts to whip Candido into the crowd rail, but Candido reverses it and it’s Waltman that hits the steel. Chris charges at the former Light Heavyweight Champion, but Credible is there to yank Waltman out of harms way and Candido crashes and burns into the barrier. Jerry Lawler on commentary screams at the official to do something about the constant interference of Justin Credible, but it’s falling on deaf ears for now, because he’s allowing this one to continue, and Waltman tosses Candido back into the ring to continue the offence. He hits a series of strikes, before nailing a number of kicks that leaves his opponent slumped against the turnbuckles……and a big body splash to follow up results in Candido being sat on the mat with his back against the turnbuckles. Waltman backs up and again gives a crop chop…..then runs the length of the ring and crashes into the chest of his opponent, riding him like a bronco…..in fact so much so that Credible screams at the King, asking him “…..do you like that Burger King? That’s called the Bronco Buster…..”

Waltman leans down and slaps Candido about the head, telling him that “….this is what happens when you mess with the KLiQ…..you get treated like a little b###h…..” before backing up and going for the Bronco Buster again…..he went to the well once to often though….because this time when he leaps Candido gets a foot up to protect himself…..and Waltman does not land well…..especially if he’s planning on having kids in the future. Slowly but surely Candido claws his way back into the fight and Credible isn’t happy about it on the outside. Candido pounds on Waltman, before slamming him to the mat and heading up top. Diving Elbow Drop from up high very nearly gives Candido the three count, but Waltman gets a shoulder up at the last possible second. He climbs the turnbuckles again, but this time he takes too long and Waltman is up to meet him. The two men battle it out up on the top turnbuckle, trading blows back and forth with each man looking to gain the advantage…..it’s Candido that comes out on top however, and unbelievably he looks as though he is trying to position his opponent for a top rope Powrbomb……

…….Credible is up on the apron as quick as a flash however, and he has a firm grip on the ankle of Candido…..at least until Candido is able to yank his foot free and kick Credible down off the apron and back down to the floor. Unfortunately, the distraction proved costly for Candido…..because in the midst of the confusion he is able to slip free of Candido’s grasp, and then grab a hold of his opponent, sending him crashing to the mat with a top rope version of that sit out Facebuster that has won him so many matches here in the WWF…..and it’s more than enough to ensure victory here tonight…..

Winner: Sean Waltman

Rating: B

It’s time to punctuate this victory….as the KLiQ look to make an example of the young man that has caused so many problems to their group. Credible joins Waltman in the ring, and the two competitors begin to stomp the living daylights out of Chris Candido. Credible rolls back to the outside and picks up a steel chair from ringside, before sliding back in and laying the steel furnishing flat down on the mat. Waltman drags Credible back to his feet and positions him over the chair……and it looks as though he is about to drive Candido’s skull onto the steel with a second Facebuster……which is when the sound of a headset being slammed down is audibly heard over the broadcast….

……and Jerry Lawler has seen enough…..the King comes bolting into the ring and begins laying waste to both Waltman and Credible…..at least long enough for Candido to get into the fight….then the four men pair off, with Lawler brawling with Credible and Waltman and Candido continuing their battle. Eventually the two KLiQ men are fought off….and Waltman and Credible scurry up the aisleway and to the back, leaving Candido and Lawler to celebrate in the ring.

Rating: B+

VS.

Raven’s Rules: Freedom vs. Servitude Match

Al Snow vs. Raven

It’s time for things to get a little crazy now, as Al Snow goes up against Raven for the second month in a row…..things are a little different this time however, and the stakes are most definitely higher….as should Al Snow fail to win here, both he AND Bill DeMott must become members of Raven’s Flock….and a subservient members at that…..couple that with the fact that this isn’t just any ordinary match either, this is a Ravens Rules match…..which essentially means that there are no rules….and while Raven has given his word that no member of the Flock will interfere, should they decide to show their faces there is absolutely nothing that the official can do about it.

Raven makes his way out first, and so far true to his word, because he comes to the ring alone. Snow makes his entrance next…..but he isn’t alone….he has Head in his hands, and Bill DeMott by his side. Seeing that there is no Flock accompanying Raven, Snow tells DeMott to go to the back, but Bill shakes his head….he has too much invested in this one to be stuck in the back should the Flock decide to make an appearance. Snow agrees but tells him to keep out of harms way, before heading down to the ring, where Raven is stood arms outstretched in a crucifix pose. Snow slides under the bottom rope and is instantly on the attack before the official can even ring the bell. It’s a pier six brawl with the two men hammering blows at each other. Raven has the slight edge when it comes to power and gets the advantage in the back and forth….but Snow has an edge when it comes to speed and agility and is able to break free and nail a dropkick that sends Raven bundling to the outside.

Raven takes a moment to compose himself, but Al is straight on the attack, nailing his opponent with a baseball slide, then slamming him down on the arena floor. Raven gets back to his feet but takes a hard steel chair shot across the back by Snow that knocks him back down. Al picks Raven up by the hair, looking to keep up the attack, but Raven tackles him and drives him spine first hard into the safety barrier, before setting Snow up for a Russian Leg Sweep, again using the barricade to his advantage as he drives Al’s spine into the steel structure. Raven rolls Snow back into the ring and makes the cover, but he only gets a two count. Sid drops to a knee……but he doesn’t go down…….until about two seconds later……because that’s when Shawn Michaels kicks him right in the face with the Sweet Chin Music. Michaels makes the cover and that’s all she wrote.

Winner: …and Still WWF Champion…….Shawn Michaels

Rating: A

The Corporation disappear back up the aisle way, with DeBiase laughing as the official hands Shawn Michaels his Championship. Sunny climbs into the ring next to him and the pair stand celebrating the victory, with Michaels placing one foot on the chest of his defeated opponent. He can clearly be heard shouting that he did it again…….and he did it all by himself “……..you hear me Nash…..I don’t need you……never have, never will……because the Heartbreak Kid Shawn Michaels is too much of a man…..for anybody…….”

Shawn wraps his arm around Sunnys waist and pulls her in close……before tossing her to the mat, Sherri Martel style and hitting his signature pose as pyro erupts around him…..and the show comes to a close…….but not before we see the figure of Kevin Nash walking out on the entrance way staring a hole through the Champion and applauding in a slow condescending manner…..because by hook or by crook……Shawn Michaels did it again.

Rating: A*

Final Rating: A

Final Thoughts

In Your House is in the books…..and what an understatement it would be if I didn’t say how pleased I was with how it went down….and for two reasons……the first being that I really had my doubts going into this one that my three biggest matches would deliver…..that being the Kane/Mankind showdown……the Tag Title match between Austin/Pillman and the KLiQ……and most importantly the main event itself…..historically heel vs heel matches haven’t gone so well for us, so for the two undercard matches to have been so well received was a real relief……but not as much a relief as how well the WWF Championship match went. Sid is an absolute beast, and he’s certainly over like rover, always has been, always will be……but when it comes to his In ring work….well let’s just say that can leave something to be desired…..but Shawn Michaels has been badgering me to let the world see what he can do with Sid ever since Bret Hart gave him the match of his career back in July……and credit where credit is due, Shawn did exactly what he said he would do…..carried the big lug to a hell of a match, and I was sure to let him know after the show……even Bret was impressed…..although he was saying it through gritted teeth at the time. Speaking of Bret, both he and Owen were MiA on this show….not by design but by time restraints…..I really wanted them to factor into the event, even if it was only a promo because the Tag Title match didn’t merit an appearance due to the finish…..but thems the breaks…..

….oh hang on…..I forgot the second reason I was so pleased with the event didn’t I…..well it seems as though the near two point five million dollars spent to up our visibility and name value in Japan paid off…..because with our new deals in place, not only are we airing in Japan, but people are watching too…..I thought it would take another six to eight months for us to take that next step….but with how well received this show was by oversea’s you can truly say that we are now an international company…..WCW might still have the bigger stars, but there is no doubting the fact that WE are the bigger promotion…..now we just have to make sure we stay there.

Earthquake and Typhoon made a surprise one shot appearance at the Pay Per View….and Earthquake made sure to convince me that one shot was one shot too many, wrestling totally out of it….off his tits on drink and drugs……bye bye Shark…..and take the Shockmaster with you.

Slight injury news, as the Nation of Dominations Nelson picked up a bruised knee in his match against the Son’s of Samoa. No one was to blame and he’ll be able to work through it so it’s not a major problem. Anyway, onwards to Raw as we creep ever closer to November, and one of my personal favourite Pay Per Views, Survivor Series.
