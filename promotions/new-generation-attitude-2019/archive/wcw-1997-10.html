WCW Monday Nitro: Week 1, October 1997

Posted 3 June 2019

Week 1, October 1997

Gallagher-Iba Arena (Stillwater, Oklahoma)

ATT: 13,228

TV: 18.44

Announce Team: Tony Schiavone, Bobby Heenan and Mike Tenay

It’s Monday Night, it’s TNT, and that can mean only one thing…..WCW Nitro is on the air and we are live as the Titans of Wrestling’s Jean Paul Levesque makes his way down the aisle…..and while opening the show with the Titans of Wrestling shouldn’t come as a huge surprise, opening the show with just ONE Titan certainly is….because this rebel group rarely travels alone….but Levesque is a group of one right now, and the number one contender to the WCW Championship doesn’t look particularly pleased…..

….he climbs into the ring with a microphone, and clearly has a few things to get off his chest concerning last week….

“…..see, I went back and I watched the footage of what went down, since the last thing a remember is an almighty crack across the back of my head, and then it was lights out…..and when I DID come back around, what did I see? I saw my “friend”…..my Titan “brother”…..standing there with a baseball bat in hand….so I put two and two together, and yeah, I may have come up with five, because Hollywood Hogan, you did NOT nail me in the back of the skull with that bat, Sting did……BUT, once the throbbing had died down I got to thinking……and what I got to thinking about, is that after rewatching that footage, the moment Sting landed in the middle of this canvas, my Titan “brother” hit the bricks and left me high and dry…….

……I gotta ask Hulk, what happened to one for all? Or is it less of a case of one for all, and more a case of all for Hollywood, because not only did you bail, but not a single member of the Titans of Wrestling was ANYWHERE to be seen…..in fact, it seems to me, like Jean Paul Levesque was fed to the Wolves last week, and frankly I’m damn sick of not getting any respect around here…..

……I went for over thirty minutes in that War Games match, I was the first man in and I gave my very life blood to ensure that the Titans came out with the victory, hell, half of the skin from my face is probably still hanging off the side of that goddamn cage wall….and then, when Eric Bischoff of all people recognises the sacrifices that I made during that match, and grants ME the opportunity to challenge for the WCW Championship, YOU expect me to just lay down and kiss the feet of Hollywood, because ACTUALLY, it’s not what’s best for the Titans that’s important, it’s what’s best for Hollywood that matters…..

…..well I’ve got news for you….I didn’t sign up to be a follower….I didn’t sign up to be a stooge…..I signed up to be an equal, and you can be damn sure that at Halloween Havoc, I am going to show the world that Jean Paul Levesque is every bit the Titan of Wrestling that Hollywood Hogan is……..”

…..it seems as though Hollywood has heard quite enough of Mr. Levesque’s ranting, as he makes his way out, along with the rest of the Titans of Wrestling…..the remainder of the group walks out as one, and as they enter the ring, Hogan (who you would think would be p!ssed by those comments) actually has almost an apologetic look on his face….

….he grabs hold of a microphone and tells Levesque that actually, he’s right…..last week, when Sting appeared out of nowhere and dropped down from the ceiling, it threw Hollywood for a loop…..Hogan says that he was standing there going toe to toe with the Giant Dude, and then out of nowhere Sting drops down like a ghost with a baseball bat, and all Hogan though about was that if he, the WCW Champion got injured and couldn’t defend his title, it would mean the Titans of Wrestling ALL lose the WCW Title……but Levesque is right, Hogan should have been there for his Titan Brother…..

…..he then turns to the rest of the Titans and rips into them for not being there for Levesque either, before turning back and going eye to eye with his Halloween Havoc opponent, telling him that (“…..from one Titan of Wrestling to another, it will be my honour to face you man to man for the WCW Title brother…..”) and may the best Titan win…..because when the best Titan win’s, the entire Titans of Wrestling win Dude, and when that bell ring, no matter if it’s Hollywood or Levesque, this World Title will still be exactly where it belongs, around the waist of a Titan of Wrestling…..

…..Hogan extends his hand, and Levesque gladly accepts it, telling Hollywood that’s all he ever wanted…..Levesque goes to walk away but Hogan doesn’t release the handshake, pulling him back to tell him that to show JUST how much faith they have in him, Vince Russo has arranged a match tonight…..the main event, for Levesque to show the world that HE’S the man, when he faces the biggest WCW Dude of them all……..The Giant……

“……and to show you that Hollywood has your back, jack…..I’m gonna be out there at ringside with you, to make sure that the little Stinger doesn’t try and use your skull to practice his swing on again brother……”

……if Hogan was expecting some sort of a reaction from that announcement he doesn’t get it….Levesque tells him that’s fine by him…..

“……I’ll see you out there…….Champ…..”

…..before pulling his hand free from Hogan’s and heading to the back (alone) to prepare for his match.

Rating: A

VS.

Jim Duggan vs. The Warrior


Our first match actually involves the Titans of Wrestling…..well, one of them anyway….because as Jim Duggan makes his entrance, the Warrior dismisses everyone else to the back with a wave of his arm. The Enforcer Ray Traylor asks to stick around to, you know….”Enforce”…..but the Warrior just stares blankly at him until he takes the hint and goes away…..

…..this match…..well, it wasn’t great…..it wasn’t horrible, but yeah, it won’t be winning any awards anytime soon….not unless PWI introduce a “least selling in a single match” end of year award, because there was none to be seen here….granted, Warrior didn’t exactly have a great deal TO sell, he just beat the shit out of Duggan for six minutes and then finished him off with the Running Splash.

Winner: The Warrior
Rating: B-

The Warrior doesn’t even bother celebrating his victory, he just slowly climbs to his feet and heads over to the ropes, looking to head to the back….before he’s even out of the ring however, Diamond Dallas Page makes his way out with a microphone in hand….

….he walks down the aisle towards the oncoming Warrior, saying there must be something wrong with his phone, his beeper…..AND his fax machine…..because he’s been waiting a week for an answer to his challenge, and as yet he’s got nothing…..

“……what’s the matter, all that face paint clogging up your ears? In that case allow me to reiterate…..that means repeat myself, since the Ultimate Jackoff is clearly all muscle and no brain…..Halloween Havoc…..three weeks time…..Diamond Dallas Page vs. The Warrior…..it’s a pretty simple challenge, whatcha say monkey?…..”

…..the two men continue walking towards each other, and it’s about this point that their paths cross…..maybe we’ll get the answer to that challenge after all…..

…..except we don’t…..because as the Warrior reaches Page, he just keeps walking…..he doesn’t even break his stride as he walks straight past DDP without even acknowledging his presence and disappears to the back, leaving Dallas standing with a pretty frustrated look on his face…..

……fortunately there is one man who does want to talk to Page, and that man makes his way out right now, Mean Gene Oakerlund……

….Gene says that it would appear as though the Warrior is not taking the challenge of Diamond Dallas Page seriously, in fact, he’s not acknowledging DDP at all…..between Hollywood Hogan claiming that Page isn’t fit to be in the same ring with him, and now this, Dallas has got to be getting fed up of constantly being underestimated?….

…..Page says that far from it…..in fact, Hollywood “Scum” Hogan and his little band of misfits can just go on underestimating DDP all they want, (“because it will make it just that little bit sweeter when I drop them flat on their face”)…..he says that Hogan thinks so little of DDP, that he had to personally come out here a few weeks ago to make sure he was facing his Boy at Halloween Havoc, not Page (“and look how that’s turned out Gene-O”)….Page says that he showed EVERYONE that he belongs in this ring with the very best this business has to offer, by walking into War Games at number one, with his head already busted open from an attack earlier in the night……and the only reason that the Titan Scumbags won, was because they handcuffed the rest of his team to the cage and gave Dallas an eight on one beating (“tough odds for anyone Gene”)….

“……but you know what, people have been underestimating me my whole life, and I just keep defying the odds…..you’re looking at the man who was the oldest rookie in the business….a man who should be contemplating his retirement, not just getting started….but as far as the Titans of Wrestling go, just getting started is exactly what I’m doing…..Hogan, Savage….Warrior…..whether they like it or not, they’re gonna find out the hard way that Diamond Dallas Page is the real deal, and Warrior, you don’t want to acknowledge me, that’s fine……I’ll make you acknowledge me, because no matter how big, how strong….or how thick headed you are…..you will…feel….the…..BANG…….”

……Page’s microphone work could still do with some work (he’s around a B average in his entertainment skills) but we sent him out there without a script for this one, sink or swim, and he performed like a real star with an impassioned promo (helped by the massive overness of the Warrior no doubt) and by the time he held those hands up in the sign of the Diamond Cutter, these fans were firmly on his side.

Rating: A*

VS.

Fit Finlay & Lance Storm vs. The Blue Bloods


Next up we have the Blue Bloods (Earl Robert Eaton and The Duke, Douglas Williams) taking on the rather unusual combination of Fit Finlay and Lance Storm….unusual not only because they were attempting to beat each other up last week, but because it’s not often you see a face and a heel on the same side either, and had it not been for the fact that should they win, the man who picks up the victory gets a shot at Steven Regal and the Television Title, they perhaps wouldn’t be teaming at all…..

….the match itself was pretty decent all things considered, with Finlay and Storm struggling to work as a team when they were both working towards their own agenda’s……

……a fact that his Lordship was keen to capitalise on, as he made his way out from the back….

……and as Douglas and Easton ensured the officials back was to the Television Champion, he nailed Finlay with a punch to the back of the head, that appeared to knock the Irishman out cold…..

…..Bobby Heenan complimented his Lordship on having such a devastating punch, even though the camera clearly caught him slipping pair of brass knuckles back into his tights as Williams tackles Lance Storm to the outside, Earl Robert makes the pin, and the Blue Bloods record the victory.

Winner(s): The Blue Bloods
Rating: B-

Regal is clearly very pleased with himself, as he leads his victorious troops back up the aisle, job done…..the trio come to a halt however as the face of the Championship Committee Eric Bischoff walks out to meet them in the aisleway, microphone in hand….

….his Lordship looks the new arrival up and down for a moment with a look of disdain, before asking if he could kindly step to one side so that he and his comrades can make their way to the back…..the tea is going the be stewed and the cucumber sandwiches are likely to start curling if they are out here much longer (so be a good chap and just bugger off out of the way will you”)…..

….Bischoff tells Regal to hold on, the cucumber sandwiches are going to have to wait, because there’s still the small matter of deciding who will be competing next for the Television Title…..Regal tells Bischoff that’s a problem for another day…..

“…..after all, it shan’t be either of those two gentlemen in the ring, they were thoroughly thrashed here tonight by the superior team…..maybe it’s time for a fresh challenge, someone like young Joseph Maggs…..or that Mr. Guerrero chap…..”

“…..Eddie Guerrero? You want to put the TV Title on the line against Eddie Guerrero?…..”

“……no, don’t be so bloody ridiculous, I’m talking about Mando Guerrero…..he seems like he’s from good stock, I’m sure he’d be a very worthy challenger……”

……Bischoff says that he’s sure he would, but there appears to have been a small misunderstanding on Mr. Regal’s part…..tonight wasn’t to decide IF Lance Storm or Fit Finlay would get a title opportunity, it was to decide who would get the FIRST title opportunity….

“…..you see Mr. Regal……”

“……that’s Lord Regal if you don’t mind sunshine…..”

“……Lord Regal, I do apologise…..no, you see, both Lance Storm and Fit Finlay have legitimate arguments for why they should be granted another opportunity at your Championship…..however, since we have not determined who will get the first shot, then they will BOTH have the first shot……next week……when Lord Steven Regal defends the Television Title against Fit Finlay……AND…..Lance Storm……in a triangle match…….and now Lord Regal, you can get back to your cucumber sandwiches…..”

…..with that, Eric Bischoff turns and heads to the back, leaving a gobsmacked Steven Regal to chase after him, arguing furiously all the way…..but it’s no use…..next week he WILL be defending the TV Title in a triangle match, no matter how much he protests.

Rating: B

VS.

Rick Martel vs. Kevin Sullivan


Kevin Sullivan has been pretty vocal just recently, after not being seen for quite some time….he saw his mission in life snatched away from him when Hulk Hogan voluntarily joined the dark side, killing Hulkamania in the process, and as such, feels like some what of a forgotten man…..fortunately for him he’s found a new raison d’etre, and with it, some new running buddies who also feel as though they have been forgotten and shoved to one side….and they’re a definite upgrade on Kamala and the Zodiac…..former WCW Champion “The Beast from the East” Bam Bam Bigelow, and former three time WCW Champion, “The Beast from the Far East” one time stalwart of Japan, Big Van Vader…..both of whom accompany Sullivan to the ring tonight

…..there has been a fresh influx of talent here to World Championship Wrestling in recent months…..a fact that hasn’t gone unnoticed by Sullivan and the Forgotten, who have made it their mission to ensure that they are not looked over or left by the wayside once more…..Rick Martel may not have arrived here straight from the WWF, but outside of his early AWA run, he’s certainly more “Titan” than anything else….he’s been a real asset since his arrival too, having no problems with using his experience to elevate the younger talent….has hasn’t won much, but he certainly hasn’t disgraced himself……

…..there was no disgrace in him losing here either…..especially since at times he was fighting three men….and as Bigelow distracted the official, Vader climbed into the ring to deliver a massive Powerbomb, which left Martel down and wide open for the Double Foot Stomp from Sullivan, which gave the Forgotten man the victory…..

Winner: Kevin Sullivan
Rating: B

….and with that victory, Pebbles and Bam Bam….erm, I mean Vader and Bigelow join Sullivan in the ring, where Sully has a microphone…..he says that the path of destruction being unleashed by the Forgotten is only just getting started…..and at Halloween, two of the mighty shall fall….Rikishi Fatu and The Rock are walking a road they are unfamiliar with, against opponents they can’t begin to comprehend…..they may have come to WCW looking for fresh starts and opportunity, but what they are going to find is destruction and chaos, because Bam Bam Bigelow is going to shut up the smart mouth of the Rock when he breaks his jaw…..and as for Rikishi, the Big Man, Vader, he has a Powerbomb waiting with his name on……..

…..that brings out the big ‘Kish himself, as Rikishi walks out onto the ramp….

…..he stays there too, happy to talk from afar instead of climbing into the ring with three men that would have no problems at all in delivering a three on one assault. Rikishi tells Sullivan that his little Trio of Terror down there talk big, but when it comes down to an actual one on one fight, they’d prefer to hide behind each other…..(“and if Big Van Vader thinks for even a second that he can get my big Samoan Ass up for a Powerbomb, he must have that damn muzzle on too tight”)…..Rikishi says that at Halloween Havoc, the Forgotten are going to be exactly that, because he’s gonna whip Vader’s ass, and the Rock……well, he’s got a Rock Bottom with Bigelow’s name all over it……

…..Sullivan says that just like his loud mouth pal The Rock last week, Rikishi talks a big game from the other end of the arena…..if he REALLY wants to impress The Forgotten, maybe he should walk down to this ring and repeat himself……

“…..oh I don’t need to do that….I’m not out here to fight, I’m just the distraction…..”

…….the crowd erupts as the Rock slides into the ring behind the trio, and Sullivan and Vader turn around just in time to catch the tail end of Bam Bam Bigelow getting Rock Bottomed down to the mat….

……they’re clearly unprepared for the assault, giving the Rock plenty of time to roll to the safety of the outside before they can react, where he retreats slowly up the ramp, talking trash the whole way until reaching Rikishi, where he takes the microphone to announce that at Halloween Havoc…..

“…..someone better tell Fred and Barney that Halloween is coming early this year….but Pebbles and Bam Bam, they won’t need no Halloween costumes, no, no, no, no, no……because Pebbles there, Big Van Vader, he already looks and smells, like a big steaming pile of Brontosaurus crap…..and as for Bam Bam, by the time the Rock’s done sticking this boot up his ass, he’s gonna look like a big, fat Yabba Dabba Don’t…..”

…..Rock tells Sullivan that this little mission they’re on, its gonna fade away faster than Kevin Sullivan’s hairline….but the Rock will do the “Walnut’s” a favour, he’s going to make sure that they will never be forgotten again……because no matter where they go, or what they do…..they will ALWAYS be remembered, as those three jabroni’s that got their asses whipped by the Rock on his WCW debut.

Rating: A*

We come back from commercial to the surprising sight of Colonel Robert Parker standing in the ring along side the Samoan SWAT Team…..this combination look completely out of place standing next to each other, with Parker kitted out in a pristine white suit, dabbing at his brow with a handkerchief while Samu and Fatu look frankly confused as to why they are there…..

…..Parker says that he’s been sat at home this last few months, he’s been taking it easy on a much deserved vacation, but after what he saw last week, he knew it was time to cut that vacation short…..

“…..because I saw these two boys right here….they were out here beating the Pitbulls like the scolded dogs they are……they had that match won, but they just couldn’t put it away because they had no focus…..well I stood up and I looked at myself in the mirror, and I said ‘Colonel, it’s time to go to work, because these two boys could be great……with just the right guidance……”

…….Parker says that on behalf of his new clients, he’s officially challenging the Pitbulls right here, right now to a rematch, that’s unless Madusa done gone and neutered them that is…..

……he doesn’t have to wait long for a response, as Madusa makes her way out from the back, with Pitbull #1 and #2 walking menacingly behind here, each man tethered to a steel chain that she holds in her hands…..

…..Madusa says that she doesn’t know what match HE was watching, but it couldn’t have been the Pitbulls, because they were out here kicking ass and taking a bite out of some Samoan ass last week…..but if it’s a fight he wants, her boys have never backed down from a fight in their life, and they’re not about to start now……

……so challenge……accepted……

Rating: B

VS.

The Pitbulls (w/Madusa) vs. The Samoan SWAT Team (w/Col. Robert Parker)


…..so here we are…..it’s the Pitbulls vs. The Samoan SWAT team again for the second week running, after Samu and Fatu found themselves disqualified last week, which is the exact reason that the Colonel claims to be here in his new role as SST Manager…..

…..honestly, I’m not sure that the SST even realise that Parker has made this challenge on their behalf…..and if he IS their new manager, I’m not sure they realise THAT either as they stand there staring at this Southern Gentleman, unsure as to what he wants, until finally they interpret Parkers frantic arm waves as an indication to act, and if that wasn’t clear enough, the Pitbulls charging at them the moment the bell rang was a dead giveaway…..

……this match went down pretty much exactly the same way as last weeks match, with the only exception being that if anything, Parkers presence caused the SST even more confusion, and lead to them being even more distracted, to the point that a group huddle on the outside actually lead to the Samoan’s being counted out, while Parker shouted loudly at them to get back into the ring……The Samoan’s lose again, and really, the Colonel is to blame.

Winner(s): The Pitbulls (via Count Out)
Rating: B-

The Pitbulls get the victory, and while the Muscled Mutt’s look as though they were looking forward to a longer fight, Madusa leads them away, happy with another mark in the win column……

…..Parker on the other hand is furious, and he throws his hat down in frustration before proceeding to tear into (a still confused as to why he’s there) Fatu and Samu…..Parker continues to berate the duo, calling them nothing more than a pair of stupid savages, before making the huge mistake of slapping Samu right in the mouth…..

……he realised he’d made a mistake too, you could tell by the way he backed off apologising profusely….unfortunately he could only back up as far as the ropes, and the sweat starts pouring off his brow as the Samoan’s in unison, each grab one white lapel of Parkers jacket, before letting out a savage cry, and nailing The Colonel right between the eyes with a huge double headbutt…..

…..Parker goes down, and Samu drags him over to the middle of the ring, while Fatu heads up to the top rope. Samu holds the disoriented manager in position, as his partner comes flying off with a big splash, crushing the life out of the Foghorn Leghorn like Southerner…..and despite Robert Parkers earlier claims, it looks as though he’s not about to become the Samoan’s manager anytime soon, because they’re leaving him laying, while they head to the back.

Rating: C

VS.

Bill Goldberg vs. Ice Train (w/Teddy Long)
It’s time for Bill Goldberg’s weekly workout….as he comes out here again putting his perfect record on the line….a record that now stands at 26-0 (although that’s the official in game number….if we were to include the house shows that get run he must be double that)…Goldberg is a powerful man, but his opponent tonight is no slouch in the strength department either, the 315lb Ice Train…..

……it’s another babyface vs. babyface outing for Bill here, but it’s no secret who the fans are rooting for, because after spending the last few months completely dismantling his opponents week in and week out, Bill Goldberg is getting quite the following….

….the fans want to see Goldberg beat people up, so that’s exactly what they get, as he ploughs through the Ice Train with a big spear, followed up with an equally impressive (given his opponents size) Jackhammer, that gives him the victory at 3:43……and for anyone keeping count, that’s 27-0 to the undefeated rookie sensation.

Winner: Bill Goldberg
Rating: C-

Next up we have the arrival of James Vandenberg, who makes his way out with his masked client, Mortis…..the duo enter the ring, where Vandenberg smiles sinisterly with a microphone in hand as he tells the crowd that the “Cleansing” continues to unfold, as Mortis purifies World Championship Wrestling, one unworthy soul at a time…..

…..he says that it began with the “Ultimate” Imposter….the Renegade, who has been purged from the screens of WCW entirely…..the young pretender Jeff Hardy fell next, and as they work ever closer to their end game, more of their hero’s will fall by the wayside, as the unworthy prove themselves exactly that, and only Mortis will be standing….the true heir to the throne…..

…..Vandenberg’s ranting is suddenly cut off by some generic oriental sounding music…..

……as Yoshihiro Tajiri rather randomly makes his way out from the back…..

…..Vandenberg looks somewhat amused by the interruption, and as Tajiri climbs into the ring he actually orders Mortis to stand down, clearly curious as to what the young man has to say….

…..truthfully, he doesn’t say much….well not in English anyway, as he unleashes a barrage of words in his native tongue. Vandenberg holds his hand up to halt the flow, telling Tajiri to slow down, because he thinks he knows what his problem is…..obviously Tajiri is looking for some form of retribution for the beating he took at the hands of Mortis a few weeks back…..

“….well make no mistake little man, Mortis will be more than happy to cleanse the WCW…..of you…..”

….Mortis steps up and positions himself in front of Tajiri, towering over him by at least half a foot….to his credit, the smaller man doesn’t back down…instead he brings the microphone to his lips once more, saying in broken English that Mortis….

“…..not cleaner……Mortis…..Jackass……”

…..and he nails the masked man right in the temple with the microphone……

…..the blow staggers Mortis, and Tajiri is quick to capitalise on the surprise attack, following up with punches and kicks, before finally driving the bigger man back into the corner with a dropkick….

…..Tajiri charges at Mortis with a body splash, but the masked man steps forward and catches him in mid air, before dropping back and dropping Tajiri (almost Stun Gun style) onto the top turnbuckle…..

…..it was a plucky attempt from Tajiri but now he’s lost the element of surprise, and Mortis is in complete control as he beats the snot out of the young Japanese star, before finally dragging him to his feet and positioning him for the Flatliner……

……which is when another figure comes running out from the back…..and it’s another figure with a score to settle with Mortis, because Jeff Hardy flys into the ring and starts hammering the masked man with rights and lefts, before finally sending him crashing to the outside with a big clothesline over the top rope…..

…..Hardy turns to help Tajiri to his feet, before turning to shout at a retreating Vandenberg and Mortis that they ain’t purged anything, because Jeff Hardy is still here, and he’s here to stay.

Rating: C

VS.

Curt Hennig & Chris Benoit vs. Randy Savage & Buff Bagwell


It was just fifteen days ago that Team WCW clashed with the Titans of Wrestling in the War Games match at Fall Brawl…….it was during the course of that match that Chris Benoit busted the Macho Man Randy Savage wide open, a fact that Savage clearly didn’t take too well, because the very next night he came out during Chris Benoit’s match with United States Champion Eddie Guerrero, costing Benoit his first singles Championship here in WCW…..last week, after the Titans of Wrestling’s Buff Bagwell assisted Savage to victory against Curt Hennig, Chris Benoit made his way out to put a stop to a two on one assault, challenging Randy Savage to a match at Halloween Havoc in the process……

…..Savage was quick to accept the challenge, but Curt Hennig was apparently not happy about the assault he suffered at the hands of the Titans, which is why we have this tag team match right now…..it’s Curt Hennig and Chris Benoit, facing off against Randy Savage and Buff Bagwell, and it’s next……

……in all honesty, I expected slightly better from this match….granted Buff Bagwell isn’t the worker that the other three competitors in this match are, but he’s not terrible by any stretch of the imagination……still, the match was decent at least if nothing else…..

…..the WCW pairing took control of the match in the opening portion of the match, sending the Titan twosome bailing to the outside to regroup….and as you could expect, it was a stretching of the rules that gave the Titans the advantage, and they went right to work isolating Curt Hennig, who as usual, sold like an absolute superstar for his opponents……until finally making the hot tag out to the waiting Crippler…..

…..Benoit hits the ring like a man with something to prove….probably because he does….because much like Diamond Dallas Page, he too has been told that he is u worthy of sharing the ring with the likes of Hollywood Hogan, Randy Savage, and the Warrior…..although he looks more than a match for anyone tonight, as he chops, kicks and German Suplex’s his way through Savage and Bagwell……….

…….which is probably why The Enforcer, Ray Traylor has to make his way out….trusty nightstick firmly in hand, which he tosses to Randy Savage, who uses the weapon behind the officials back to nail Benoit right between the eyes, picking up a cheap pinfall victory in the process…..advantage Macho Man in this war.

Winner(s): Randy Savage & Buff Bagwell
Rating: B

We head to the back now ahead of our main event of the evening, where Mean Gene is standing by with two men who are also set to clash at Halloween Havoc, it’s Lex Luger and the Giant…..

….this isn’t a long segment, it’s more of a stop gap between our last match and the next, and while Mean Gene tries to get the Giant’s opinion on his upcoming match with Jean Paul Levesque, The Giant is more interested in talking about Sting…..and specifically, Lex Luger’s long time friendship with Sting, convinced that Luger had Sting do his dirty work for him last week, trying to weaken the Giant ahead of his match with Luger…..

…..Luger out and out denies that accusation, stating that Sting hasn’t said two words to him in months…..and even if he HAD, Luger doesn’t need ANY help to defeat the Giant….as far as Lex can see, the only reason Sting laid his hands (or bat to be precise) on him last week, was because the big seven footer was being a Giant Ass…..

…..Giant isn’t buying it however, telling Luger to tell his little buddy that if he so much as THINKS about sticking his nose in the Giant’s match next, then it’ll be the last thing he does, because the Giant will break that bat over his head and put him in the hospital…..and if Luger has a problem with that, he’ll be sharing a room with him in the next bed over…..

Rating: B+

VS.

The Giant vs. Jean Paul Levesque


Remarkably, Lex Luger still chooses to accompany his friend(?) to ringside for this one, despite The Giant’s recent behaviour…..although it’s probably more to offset the fact that (as promised) WCW Champion, Hollywood Hogan is out with Jean Paul Levesque……

…..so it’s the Giant vs. Levesque, in a match that quite frankly, far exceeded my expectations, because it was fantastic…..in fact, it was perhaps one of the best main events we’ve seen on Nitro in quite some time….of course, that might be because this is the first main event in quite some time that BOTH competitors have been under the age of thirty…..no time decline in sight here folks…..

……having Hogan and Luger at ringside certainly heightened the tension levels in the match, it’s not often that a man is accompanied to the ring by a person they’re facing on Pay Per View in a few weeks, let alone having BOTH competitors accompanied by future opponents….obviously Hollywood attempts to interject himself in the match more than Luger does, as he tries to show Levesque that they are one big happy family, but that’s exactly what the Total Package is out there for, to put a stop to it……although, this being a match involving the Titans of Wrestling, you knew it wasn’t going to be without incident…….and that incident arrived around the same time that the Giant accidentally knocked the official out cold, colliding with him after Levesque sidesteps his charge……..

…….which is when Sting rappel’s down from the rafters…..once more landing in the middle of the ring, baseball bat in hand……..

…..eventually, following Hawk making the hot tag to Animal, the match breaks down……Animal initially takes it to both Awesome and Cain, before Hawk gets back into the fight, clotheslining Awesome over the top, before following him to the outside to continue the fight…..Animal and Cain aren’t far behing, and soon it’s a free for all on the outside…..a free for all that sees the Road Warriors Irish Whip both of Missy’s men right into the seated Titans, sending popcorn and soda flying…..

…..Traylor helps his team mates to their feet (actually dusting them down) before picking up the chairs, folding them up, and passing one each to Cage and Coleman, telling them that playtime is over……

……the younger Titans obviously agree too…..because they each give an almighty swing of the steel…..crashing the illegal objects across the back of Animal and Cain…..and you know what that means…..it’s a disqualification……a double disqualification to be precise, leaving both Awesome and Hawk without partners….and the Titans of Wrestling ready to strike…..

Winner(s): No Contest
Rating: B+

……war makes for strange bedfellows….because with Animal and Awesome still down, Hawk and Cain have to work together as the Titans strike…..unfortunately, the Totally Awesome Trinity are a well oiled cohesive unit (not bitter enemies) and there’s three of them….which totally gives them the upper hand…..and there’s the little matter of two steel chairs and a nightstick….which gives them a totally awesome upper hand…..

…..Hawk is tough, we know that…..and Cain is certainly making a name for himself in recent months…..but they’re seriously out gunned here, and despite an initial flurry of offence, first Gideon Cain is taken out by a nightstick shot to the back of the head, before Hawk gets dropped with a shot to the back….

……Awesome is back up, but a shot to the ribs with the Enforcer’s nightstick doubles him over…..where Copeland and Cage swing their weapons…..it’s a huge Con-Chair-To that drops Awesome motionless to the floor…..

……Animal is up next…..but gets a chair to the gut……and a Con-Chair-To……

…..Cain charges the Totally Awesome Trinity…….gets a few blows in too…..before Traylor doubles him over with a low blow, before a…….yep, you guessed it…….Con-Chair-To…..

…..three down, one to go…..and apparently they want a little fun with Hawk first, tossing him into the ring…..the three men beat on the co-holder of the WCW Tag Team Titles, before Christian finally picks the Road Warrior up…..and plants him with a piledriver…..

…..probably shouldn’t have done that…..

….because a piledriver to Hawk is like spinach to Popeye…..and he pops right back up to his feet, before running through both Copeland and Cage with a double clothesline…..

…..Traylor charges at Hawk, but the Road Warrior is “Hulked Up” to the max, and he begins unloading on The Enforcer, before finally snatching the nightstick from Traylor, brutalising him with his own weapon…..

…..it doesn’t last though…..because while Hawk is distracted, Copeland is back in with a perfect knee strike to the kidneys……a low blow……and (when joined by Cage), ANOTHER Con-Chair-To, and this one does put Hawk down….and he stays down….and the Titans of Wrestling’s Totally Awesome Trinity stand tall, arms raised, just six days before Copeland and Cage challenge for the WCW Tag Team Championships…..but what condition (Tony Schiavone ponders) will Mike Awesome, Gideon Cain, and the Road Warriors be in come Halloween Havoc?

Rating: A*

VS.

Curt Hennig & Jeff Hardy vs. Eddie Guerrero & Mortis (w/James Vandenberg)


We have back to back tag team action tonight on Nitro, as our penultimate match features Curt Hennig teaming up with Jeff Hardy, to face the combination of WCW United States Champion Eddie Guerrero, and the masked man Mortis…..

….this is a preview of two matches that are set to take place in just six days time, when Jeff Hardy and Mortis clash once more, while Curt Hennig challenges Eddie Guerrero for the US Title…..

….the match tonight was decent considering none of these men are tag team competitors usually….Hennig and Hardy rather surprisingly gelled fantastically, having excellent chemistry for two men that had never teamed up before, and it was young Hardy that took the brunt of the punishment as the face in peril, with Mortis revelling in the chance to assault his rival, while Guerrero revels in the opportunity to simply be a cocky, arrogant pr!ck….he’s not so cocky when Hardy makes the hot tag to Hennig though, because Curt hits the ring with purpose, tackling both his opponents head on, before finally focusing his attention on Guerrero…..

…..Guerrero soon has enough of this, and bails to the outside where he collects his Championship, telling a nearby camera that they shouldn’t be risking THE most important title holder they have here in WCW just six days before he puts the gold on the line…..and with that he simply walks out of the match, heads to the back….and leaves Mortis all alone against two men….

…..Chris Kanyon has come a long way in a short time since adopting the Mortis character….going from enhancement talent to midcarder/upper midcarder (depending on his booking at any given week) and I don’t imagine it will be long before he’s cemented as a permanent upper Midcard player…..he’s up against two men right now though…..and two men that have excellent chemistry as partners no less…..and eventually he’s overwhelmed, and Hennig nails him with the Fisherman’s Suplex…..instead of holding on for the win however Hennig releases the hold and rolls out of the way, just as Hardy comes flying off the top with the Swanton Bomb for the one, two, three.

Winner(s): Curt Hennig & Jeff Hardy
Rating: B

Mean Gene is in the back, giving the final rundown for what we will be seeing on Sunday Night as well as letting everyone know that all the results from Halloween Havoc will be live on the Hotline, as well as words from all the winners and losers that evening….as Eddie Guerrero makes his way past on his way to the locker rooms

“…..that’s 1-900-909-9900 for all the wrestling news that matters……wait a minute…..Eddie Guerrero…..I believe you have some explaining to do young man…..what in the world was that about? What sort of champion walks away from a match?…..”

“…..what sort of a champion? A smart Champion esè that’s who…..I gotta put the most prestigious title in ALL of wrestling on the line on Sunday night, I can’t afford to be getting injured in some pointless tag team match holmes……”

…..Gene disagrees with that, questioning Guerrero’s integrity as United States Champion, but Eddie just tells him to watch his mouth……

“…..integrity holmes?…..you’re looking at the most hard working champion in wrestling today esè….just look at the list…..Diamond Dallas Page….Chris Benoit….Jeff Hardy….that fat Samoan Rikishi…..I beat them all, and you could have added The Rock to that list too, but he knew he was never going to beat the Latino Legend, so he ran off to fight with Vader and Bigelow instead, because he knew, just like everybody else knows, that no one is taking this title from around my waist…..not Dallas Page….not Chris Benoit…..not The Rock……and certainly not Curt Hennig…..he used to be practically perfect, but now I’M the Papi of Perfection esè….and at Halloween Havoc, I’m going to show him what a real champion looks like……”

…..with that being said, Eddie’s mouth is soon shut…..because the #1 contender to the United States Championship comes bursting into shot and dives onto Guerrero, peppering him with punches…..Gene goes flying as Guerrero fights back, and soon we have a full scale war going on that security eventually has to separate, as we go to a promo package hyping Halloween Havoc…..

Rating: B+

VS.

The Giant vs. Jean Paul Levesque


So it’s main event time…..it’s WCW vs. ToW…..it’s The Giant vs. Jean Paul Levesque….and not for the first time either…..these two men have met on a number of occasions now, in both tag matches AND singles encounters….and funnily enough, those singles encounters have all come off the back of Levesque doing or saying something to p!ss Hollywood Hogan off…..coincidence? Probably not when you consider that it’s Vince Russo that books the matches around here, and it’s Vince Russo that worships the ground that Hollywood Hogan walks on……

….that being said, Hollywood is out here at ringside as promised in this one….not that Levesque looks particularly happy about him being there, but the Titans need to keep up appearances, so here he is….

….he actually works in Levesque’s favour on a few occasions too, interfering at appropriate times to tip the scales the Titan man’s way….each time he does however, Levesque is quick to (very politely) as him to back off, claiming he doesn’t need any help to beat the big man….although let’s be honest, there aren’t going to be many in World Championship Wrestling that could best the seven foot Giant without a little bit of assistance…..

….the match wasn’t fantastic, but it was more of a dog and pony show (with the storytelling surrounding Hollywood on the outside) than a match…..and the finish didn’t help either…..because as the Giant crushed the life out of Levesque in the corner, then signalled that it was time for the Chokeslam, Hollywood jumped up onto the apron to attempt to distract the big man…..it worked too, because instead of Chokeslamming Levesque The Giant turned around and clocked Hogan right in the jaw with a right hand, sending him tumbling down to the floor……

….Giant screams down at Hogan (spraying spittle as he does) that it doesn’t matter if it’s Levesque who wins Sunday, or Hogan that wins Sunday…..whoever leaves Halloween Havoc as the WCW Champion, is going to have a Giant problem to deal with……then turns back to face his opponent……who kicks him in the gut…..and drops him with the Pedigree……for the one, the two……….but not the three…….

…..not because Giant kicked out, but because Hogan is back in the ring, drags Levesque off the downed Giant…..and drops down beside the WCW man, where he starts hammering the Giant with blows (right in front of the official) clearly not happy that the Giant placed a hand on him……unfortunately, he referee is even less happy at that action, and has no choice but to disqualify Levesque, when he was less than half a second away from picking up the pinfall victory……

Winner: The Giant (via Disqualification)
Rating: B

…..the bell rings, and the rest of the Titans of Wrestling come rushing out from the back…..someone had the audacity to lay their hands on Hollywood? You’d better believe that’s a punishable offence….

…..they don’t even give the Giant chance to get back to his feet before they pounce on him simultaneously, and they stomp the living crap out of him…..Giant might be seven feet tall when he’s standing, but when he’s flat on his back that doesn’t help him at all…..

…..suddenly the crowd erupts…..because here comes the Total Package to make the save……

…..or not…..

…..because he goes running down the aisle, but comes to a complete stop at the bottom of the ramp…..and much as the Giant did a few weeks back, he slaps his hand against his head to indicate what a big dummy he is being, telling the nearby camera that Giant’s got this…..he said so earlier…..and with that Luger takes a slow walk back up the aisle, as the Giant screams at him to help…..

…..Jean Paul Levesque has been standing idly by during this beatdown…..but he interjects now, dragging Hogan off the Giant and spinning him around, demanding to know what the hell he is doing…..Hogan says that he’s dishing out some punishment Titan’s style, but Levesque says no….what he’s doing, is costing Levesque a huge victory six days before Halloween Havoc……

…..which is when Diamond Dallas Page and Chris Benoit come rushing down the aisle, passing Luger on the way (asking him what the hell he is doing) and dashing into the ring where they go right after Savage and The Warrior……

……the Totally Awesome Trinity are busy trying to defuse the situation between Hogan and Levesque, who have now moved on from a war of words to an out and out shoving match, but they are forced to abandon their quest as DDP and Benoit slowly begin to gain the upper hand over Warrior and Macho, to assist their Titan brothers in their fight…..

……Luger looks conflicted in the aisle way……clearly he only came out here to make a point to the Giant, after the big man left him to take a mammoth beating a few weeks ago, but on the same hand, he clearly doesn’t want Page and Benoit suffering the same fate, and currently they are fighting off five men….it doesn’t look good…..so reluctantly he heads into the ring to help out……

……Hogan and Levesque are still bickering, but it all comes to a head when Hollywood calls his Halloween Havoc a “disrespectful punk”, before slapping him square in the face…..sh!t gets real then…..and all the forced niceties go out of the window, because Levesque shows Hollywood exactly how disrespectful he can be, when he flips the WCW Champion off…..and punches him right in the jaw, before tackling him through the ropes and to the floor……

……with Luger by their side, Page and Benoit are able to get back into the fight…..especially when Copeland, Cage and Traylor head to the outside to try and separate Hogan and Levesque…..leaving Luger to battle with Buff, while DDP and The Crippler continue to brawl with Savage and Warrior…..of course, in the midst of all this commotion the Giant is back to his feet…..and he shoves Bagwell to one side, obviously not happy about that little taste of his own medicine from Luger, because he grabs the Total Package by the throat….looks like it could be Chokeslam city……at least until Lex kicks him in the guts, and the two begin trading right hands……

…..and that’s exactly how the show comes to a close…..Hogan and Levesque attempting to kill each other on the floor (with the Totally Awesome Trinity attempting to separate them)……Page, Benoit, Savage and Warrior kicking, clawing, scratching, and generally brawling for all they’re worth……while Lex Luger and The Giant attempt to punch each other’s heads off their shoulders…..as Tony Schiavone implores us to buy, what is set to be a monumental Halloween Havoc event…..we’ll see you Sunday folks.

Rating: A*
