Week 1, February 1997

River Centre (St. Paul, Minnesota)

ATT: 15,473

TV: 25.37

It’s Monday night, which can only mean one thing, it’s time for the number one programme in all of professional wrestling…..it’s time for Monday Night Raw……and we open the show with the entrance of the owner of the World Wrestling Federation, Vincent Kennedy McMahon….

……he begins by telling the very vocal crowd to quiet down if they want to hear what he has to say…..predictably the boo’s rain down even louder until McMahon finally gives up and just begins to talk over the noise……he says he’s out here tonight to take care of a few issues……first things first, due to some inept officiating last week, The Corporate Cannon was wrongly disqualified in his Intercontinental Championship match last week……and because of this, at In Your House……Scott Steiner will put that prestigious title on the line once again……and this time, Brian Pillman WILL be taking home the gold…..

…..he also wants to address the WWF Tag Team Championships…..he says that last week, Bret Hart made some disparaging remarks about the current Champions, Sean Waltman and Scott Hall…..going as far as to insinuate that the KLiQ were not worthy Champions……

“…..but rest assured, Sean Waltman and Scott Hall are going to be two of the most fighting Champions around…..so much so that they have already signed the necessary contracts to sign an as yet unknown combination of Nation of Domination members at Wrestlemania………at In Your House, I have hand picked a selection of the best Tag Teams…..that DON’T include the Hart Foundation…..to compete in an over the top rope battle royal…..with the winning team earning yet ANOTHER Tag Title opportunity……

……and that’s not to mention that tonight, they will put the titles on the line, against two of the roughest, toughest men that the WWF has to offer…..men that would make lesser Champions tremble in fear…..men that could hold Championships in any organisation in the world…….tonight they will be putting those titles on the line…….against Bob Holly………and Barry Horowitz……”

Predictably the crowd are not exactly enamoured with that announcement…..but Vince tells them to calm down…..because he’s saved the best announcement for last……because ahead of his huge Wrestlemania showdown with Scott Steiner…..The Man…..The Myth….The Icon…..The Reason for the Ratings…..The Heartbreak Kid Shawn Michaels……WILL put the WWF Title in the line at In Your House…..and Vince McMahon has the perfect opponent in mind………….and that man is…….

….::::!!!!GLASS SHATTERS!!!!::::….

……the crowd absolutely erupts……but judging by the look in Vince McMahon’s face, I don’t think this was the announcement he had planned…..as Austin enters the ring, flips him off…..and snatches the microphone from him……

“……..if the next four words that come outta your measly little mouth aren’t “Stone Cold Steve Austin”…..then hell son, they’d better be “call the damn cops” because I’m about to whip your ass up and down this ring…….”

….he tells McMahon that the way he sees it, he eliminated Brian Pillman from the Royal Rumble….and because of that, gained the world title opportunity that comes with it……and since Vince screwed him at Survivor Series……and got his new little Corporate Kiss-Ass to screw him at the Rumble, then it seems only fitting that Steve Austin screws up Vince McMahons plans for Wrestlemania…..by taking Shawn Michaels WWF Title at In Your House….and taking the Boy Toy….AND the KLiQ….out of the biggest main event of the year.

Vince looks slightly uncomfortable to have to tell Austin this face to face (and without back up)….but tells Austin that unfortunately, he will NOT be facing Shawn Michaels at the Pay Per View, because in fact, he already has a match booked for In Your House…..a punishment match for sticking his nose into the Heartbreak Kids affairs last week. Austin asks against who? Waltman? Hall? Credible?……he says it’s not Nash…..not Helmsley……Austin tells McMahon that the KLiQ members are dropping faster than Vince’s underwear at a prostate exam, but Stone Cold will be happy to drop a few more…..AFTER he whips Shawn Michaels ass for the World Wrestling Federation Title…….

…….McMahon slowly backs away, telling Austin that if he wants Shawn Michaels that badly then he’s got him……Vince climbs out of the ring and gets halfway up the aisle before stopping, turning, and informing Stone Cold that…..

“…….you’ve got Shawn Michaels…..but it won’t be at In Your House…….and it won’t be for the World Wrestling Federation Title……it will be TONIGHT……when you team up with Scott Steiner, to take on The Heartbreak Kid and the Corporate Cannon…….and because you have the nerve to threaten ME…..Vincent Kennedy McMahon……the man who pays your wages…….then get ready……because at In Your House, you’re going to be going one on one……with the Big Red Monster himself……..Kane……..and I hope he tears you apart you son of a b*tch…..”

Rating: A*

VS.

Ahmed Johnson vs. Rich Steiner (w/Ted DiBiase)

Poor old Rich Steiner hasn’t had a great time of it as of late…..he sold out his own brother to join the Million Dollar Corporation, proving that everybody really does have a price…..and then followed that up with his first singles Championship victory, defeating the British Bulldog for the Intercontinental Title back in October…..only to lose the Belt to baby brother Scott on the final Raw of 1996…..and he didn’t do any better in his attempt to recapture the title, coming up short again in the rematch…..

Ahmed hasn’t fared much better as of late…..he came up just short of dethroning Shawn Michaels for the WWF Title back in December, and failing to secure a second World Title opportunity at the Royal Rumble, when he was eliminated by the Rock….

Ultimately both men could do with the victory here tonight…..but only one man can…….and after the usual Corporation shenanigans, which saw Jeff Jarrett interfere on his stable mates behalf, that one man was Rich Steiner.

Winner: Rich Steiner
Rating: B-

We head to the back now, where Dok Hendrix is standing by with Lionheart, Chris Jericho. Dok tells Jericho that while not necessarily a victory, it must have been nice to get that monkey off his back with a modicum of revenge, when he eliminated Lance Storm from the Royal Rumble match? Jericho says that yes, it felt good…..but that monkey is going nowhere until he finally defeats Lance Storm, no questions asked in the middle of the ring in a one on one match…….and while in an ideal world, that one on one victory would come at Wrestlemania……Wrestlemania is still two months away, and Jericho doesn’t know if he can wait that long….especially when In Your House is right around the corner……

“……..so I’m just two weeks time, I am officially challenging Mr. Monotony Lance Storm……”

“…..hey Chico……you don’ need to worry about Lance Storm…….you need…..to worry…..about ME…….”

…..and that interruption comes courtesy of your friendly neighbourhood KLiQ……specifically Scott Hall……who is flanked by Sean Waltman and Justin Credible…..

……Jericho eyes the trio warily….after all, finding yourself alone around the KLiQ doesn’t usually end well, but Hall arrogantly laughs, telling Jericho to relax…..they aren’t here to hurt his little pretty boy looks…..Scott Hall is here to let Jericho know that he doesn’t like the way the youngster dumped him out of the Royal Rumble match…….sneaking up on him from behind and tossing him over the top rope when he wasn’t looking…….that’s not cool man…….Scott Hall would never attack someone when they weren’t looking….(yeah right)….which is why he’s here to let Jericho know that he’s already cleared it with Vince…..and at In Your House it’s gonna be Chris Jericho vs Scott Hall

Rating: B

VS.

WWF World Tag Team Championship Match

The KLiQ (c) vs. Bob Holly & Barry Horowitz


So this was Vince McMahons tough challenge for the new WWF Tag Team Champions……Bob Holly and Barry Horowitz…..not exactly the barn burner people were expecting……but then when this was a promise from Vince McMahon, why were you expecting anything else? In Bob Holly’s defence, he IS a former WWF Tag Team Champion himself, capturing the gold back at the 1995 Royal Rumble, where he teamed up, ironically enough, with tonight’s opponent Sean Waltman.

This match was nothing more than a squash for the Champs to pick up an easy title defence….which is exactly what the did after Scott Hall dropped Horowitz with the Razors Edge.

Winner(s): ….and Still WWF Tag Team Champions……the KLiQ
Rating: B

After that glorified squash match we head to the back, where Dok Hendrix is standing by with the Nation of Domination. He asks the Nation how it felt to be on the receiving end of their own questionable tactics last week, after the Hart Foundation’s Owen Hart defeated Kama in controversial fashion. Faarooq says it should come as no surprise to anybody to see those no good Hart boys cheat their way to a win over the Nation….because they know full well that they could never beat a Nation member in a fair fight…..just like the Bulldog is going to find out next, when he goes one on one with Big Nelson….

……The Rock talks next, saying that the only reason the Harts are messing with the Nation, is because the NoD did what the Hart Foundation can’t…..and that’s gain the right to challenge for the WWF Tag Team Championships at Wrestlemania……ANY combination of the Nation…..

“…….so whether it’s The Rock and Faarooq…….The Rock and Kama……..Faarooq and Scorpio……Scorpio and Nelson…..it doesn’t matter……all that matters is this……the Nation of Domination is finally going to start getting the recognition that it deserves around here……and Fart Foundation, you should just go back to milking elks in the snowy mountains or whatever the hell it is Canadians do for fun….because the Nation of Domination are taking you out……by any means necessary……”

Rating: A*

VS.

The British Bulldog vs. Nelson


Last week Owen picked up a victory over Kama…..tonight however the British Bulldog will have his hands full trying to do the same with his gargantuan opponent, the massive Nelson. As with last week both men are accompanied to the rig by their respective groups, making for a very busy ringside area. Davey Boy is without a doubt one of the strongest competitors here in the WWF….but even he struggled to contain the mammoth mass of Nelson…..eventually however he was able to send his huge opponent crashing to the outside after hitting him with a series of clotheslines…..

…..at the same time the official is attempting to settle down a war of words that has broken out between Bret Hart and Faarooq…….which is why he doesn’t see Owen come running around behind Nelson on the floor, where he Chop Blocks the big man’s leg right out from under him. Seeing that the distraction was successful, the Hitman breaks up his argument…..pointing out Nelson out to the official. The referee starts his mandatory ten count as the Nation members attempt to lift the Giant back to his feet……it’s no good though, even the combined force of the Nation don’t have the strength to lift Nelson up deadweight from the floor……and ten seconds later, the Hart Foundation have another (controversial) victory.

Winner: The British Bulldog (via Count-Out)
Rating: B

From there we head to the back, where Dok Hendrix is standing by with the owner of the WWF, Vince McMahon. Dok tells Vince that earlier tonight, it looked as though he was about to announce the name of the man who will be challenging Shawn Michaels for the WWF Championship at In Your House, before being rudely interrupted by Steve Austin…..he asks Vince if he would like to take this opportunity to finish his earlier announcement?

McMahon still looks annoyed about that aforementioned interruption from Stone Cold, but he says he has a Pay Per View to sell, and it’s time for Shawn Michaels to face his toughest Challenge yet. He says he’s heard people complaining that tonight’s challengers in the WWF Tag Team Title match were hand picked deliberately to give the KLiQ an easy win……well no one will be able to say that about Shawn Michaels opponent at In Your House…..

“…….because challenging for that prestigious title, will be a former WWF Intercontinental Champion……a former WWF Tag Team Champion……and a man who will be a huge, huge test for the Heartbreak Kid Shawn Michaels, because he does hold a victory over him……and that man’s name……………..is Marty Jannetty…….”

To say the live crowd is unimpressed with that announcement would be an understatement. Marty is a popular face with the crowd, but he’s been M.I.A for the best part of the year…..lost to Lance Storm in his return match……and really hasn’t done anything of merit since to warrant a World Title opportunity……all of which Dok Hendrix questions, stating that to an impartial bystander, Marty Jannetty……just like Bob Holly and Barry Horowitz earlier…..seems like an easy title defence for the KLiQ, just to ensure that the 30 day title defence quota is met….

….Vince isn’t having any of that however, stating that he thought Holly and Horowitz would have posed more of a threat….Bob Holly IS a former WWF Tag Team Champion after all…..and Marty Jannetty HAS defeated Shawn Michaels before in one on one competition……something that even Stone Cold Steve Austin hasn’t done. Hendrix says no, but Scott Steiner has……before asking if this is simply a case of keeping Shawn Michaels relatively fresh ahead of his possible Wrestlemania showdown with Big Poppa Pump, while Steiner himself has to face competition the calibre of Brian Pillman? Vince pauses for a moment, before telling Dok that it never even occurred to him………but it’s not a bad idea……………is it?

Rating: B+

VS.

The Legion of Doom vs. Southern Justice (w/Ted DiBiase)


If there was ever a good time to be facing the Legion of Doom, then tonight may be that night….they are coming in fresh off the back of three consecutive losses…..against teams that arguably they should and could have beat…..are Southern Justice going to make it four for four tonight? Hawk and Animal certainly seem to have lost some of their confidence…..and DiBiase plays on that, casting aspersions on the outside on the LoD’s abilities…….unfortunately for his men, this does nothing more than to fire up the Road Warriors……and after a tough few months, The Legion of Doom FINALLY pick up a victory…..after a Doomsday Device to Phineas Godw……uhh…..Dennis Knight……and an important win for Hawk and Animal.

Winner(s): The Legion of Doom
Rating: B

The match is over and DiBiase looks as though he is considering sending the Million Dollar Corporation in to attack the Legion of Doom……one look at the face of both Hawk and Animal however and he soon changes his mind…..because the Road Warriors look fired up. Hawk grabs a microphone and informs everyone that they might have had a rough few months, but just like a dose herpes, they’re back and badder than ever……and they are done playing teacher…..and they are certainly done being jumped from behind by a couple of nobodies…..but if either Matt, Jeff, Edge or Christian don’t believe that, then they know where to find the L.o.D…….snacking on danger, dining on death…….but Hawk and Animal have big appetites, and there’s always room for desert should either team want to try and make a name for themselves off the back of the Legion of Doom again.

Rating: B-

To the back we go now, where Dok Hendrix is standing by with Intercontinental Champion Scott Steiner. He asks the 1997 Royal Rumble winner his thoughts on the fact that he will be defending his Title once more against The Corporate Cannon Brian Pillman at In Your House…..

”…..BRIAN PILLMAN?…..BRIAN PILLMAN??……YOU KNOW, I USED TO THINK HE WAS ALRIGHT…..SEE BIG POPPA PUMP HAS ALWAYS BEEN A BIT OF A LOOSE CANNON HIMSELF…….OBVIOUSLY THOUGH, SCOTT STEINER’S “CANNON” IS MUCH BIGGER THAN THAT LITTLE B*TCH’S, BECAUSE I NEVER NEEDED TO SHOVE MY TONGUE UP VINCE MCMAHONS ASS TO GET TO WHERE I AM TODAY……..I GOT A BIG CANNON DOK………….”

“………………………”

“……….I’M TALKING ABOUT MY HUGE PENIS…………..”

“……(sigh)…….I know…….we all know……..”

“………HOW DO YOU KNOW YOU PERVERT?……YOU BETTER NOT BE PEEPING AT POPPA’S BIG PUMP!!……..”

“…….(incoherent stuttering)……..”

“………….I GOT MY EYE ON YOU HENDRIX…….AS FOR PILLMAN, I’LL KICK HIS ASS AT IN YOUR HOUSE……THEN I’M GONNA TAKE MY INTERCONTINENTAL CHAMPIONSHIP ALL THE WAY TO WRESTLEMANIA……AND TONIGHT, SHAWN MICHAELS IS GONNA GET JUST A LITTLE TASTE OF WHATS IN STORE FOR HIM…..BECAUSE SCOTT STEINERS TIME HAS COME, AND AIN’T NOBODY STOPPING ME NOW……………AND KEEP YOUR PEEPING EYES TO YOURSELF HENDRIX, I GOT SOME ASS TO KICK……..”

……..and with that Steiner storms out of shot, leaving a flustered Dok Hendrix to send us back down to ringside.

Rating: A*

VS.

Psycho Sid vs. Raven


It’s time for some action now…..and this match is a direct response to Raven’s interference in Psycho Sid’s match with Dude Love last week…..Raven was somewhat responsible for Sid’s victory….and while the big man wasn’t aware of it at the time, he’s aware of it now, and insisted on this match to show Raven and the world, that Psycho Sid doesn’t need anybody’s help to win a match….

…..to Raven’s defence, he doesn’t back down an inch…..and this Master of the Macabre has never been one to shy away from pain…….which is fortunate, because Sid has a whole lot of pain to give. The entire Flock accompanied their Shepherd to the ring for this one…..including Dude Love…..who has got to be questioning whether the family he has found, is actually the family he wants by now….because Raven has systematically used and abused him for months now……

……these two have a pretty good match to be fair…..Sid might be pretty limited in the ring, but Raven showed with his outstanding match with the Undertaker that he works well against a bigger opponent…..and certainly has enough psychology to carry Big Sid to a good match…….and he sells like a star for the Psycho one……including going up for the Powerbomb……although Sid doesn’t get to deliver the final blow…..because with Raven still on his shoulders, the Flock storm the ring…..rescuing their master and beating the big man down, giving the official little choice but to call for the bell.

Winner: Psycho Sid (via Disqualification)
Rating: B

The bell rings to signal the end of the match…..not the end of the action however, as the Flock continue to lay a colossal beating on the colossal Psycho Sid…..so much so that the dancing Dude stops jiving long enough to attempt to convince Raven to call off his dogs…..literally…….as the Dogs of War set up Sid for the Double Chokeslam….all he gets for his efforts however is a back handed slap from old Edgar Allen Woe himself, before being told in no uncertain terms, that his recently found compassionate side would be better served being lost again…..before it gets him seriously hurt…….

……which is when young Rey Mysterio once again makes his presence felt, as he comes running down the aisle-way at top speed, leaping into the ring with a Springboard Dropkick to Crush, that sends him crashing into Wrath….leading the Dogs of War to tumble to the floor. DeMott attempts to catch hold of Mysterio, but is no match for the youngsters speed, and the former Light Heavyweight Champion easily sidesteps him and DeMott stumbles through the ropes to the floor also…….followed by Christopher Daniels, who gets backdropped out of the ring……Mysterio turns and comes face to face with the smirking Raven, who is stood, arms outstretched in the middle of the ring……

……Rey looks at Raven for a moment, before backing up to gain some momentum and charging at the stoic leader of the Flock……which is when Dude Love kicks him in the gut and drives the little luchador’s masked skull into the mat with the Double Underhook DDT…….Psycho Sid is down……Rey Mysterio is down……Raven is laughing maniacally…..and Dude Love does not look to be his happy go lucky self, kneeling over the unconscious Mysterio, with an almost protective hand placed on they youngsters chest…..as we go to commercial ahead of our main event of the evening.

Rating: A

VS.

Steve Austin & Scott Steiner vs. Brian Pillman & Shawn Michaels


It’s time for the main event of the evening……and it’s the battle of the Nicknames…..as “Stone Cold” Steve Austin and “Big Poppa Pump” Scott Steiner, face off against “The Corporate Cannon” Brian Pillman and “The Heartbreak Kid…..The Showstopper…..The Icon……The Main Event…..The Reason for the Ratings…..the……well, you get the point” Shawn Michaels…..

…..this might be the first time Michaels and Pillman have faced off, but Austin and Steiner have actually teamed up before….to compete for the WWF Tag Team Titles non the less against the KLiQ…..back then it was only the fact that they could not get along that prevented them from gaining Championship gold……and while they still don’t particularly “get on”, they are on much more of the same wavelength now than they were then…..and both men take the opportunity to lay a beating on Shawn Michaels…..while Austin is more than happy to stomp a mud hole in the man who betrayed him at the Royal Rumble….his former partner, Brian Pillman…..

…….this one is a match of two halves…..a wild, violent, drag out brawl while Austin and Steiner are in control…..and a slow, methodical, mat based technical masterpiece while Michaels and Pillman have the edge……..or maybe that should be a match of two thirds…….because it reverts back to a wild, violent, drag out brawl after Austin makes the hot Tag to Steiner…..a wild, violent, drag out brawl that sees Stone Cold and the Corporate Cannon brawl up and down the aisle, while Scott Steiner actually locks The WWF Champion in the Steiner Recliner, right in the middle of the ring…….

…….which is when the KLiQ do what the KLiQ do best…..save Shawn Michaels ass…..and before the WWF Champion is forced to submit in front of the entire world, the Referee calls the match to a close with the disqualification, as the KLiQ jump Steiner in force.

Winner(s): Steve Austin & Scott Steiner (via Disqualification)
Rating: A

…..and much like in our previous bout, the end of the match does NOT signify the end of the action……because with Austin and Pillman still brawling in the aisle, the KLiQ go to work on Scott Steiner, while Shawn Michaels sits back and barks instructions……Hall, Waltman and Credible lay a beating on Big Poppa Pump…….who looks to be in serious trouble…….

…….when Chris Jericho comes running down the aisle……

…….he hits the ring and fights off the KLiQ trifecta just long enough for Scott Steiner to regain his composure…..then Steiner and Jericho begin to bounce the KLiQ all over the ring….Jericho even manages to get a few licks in on his old nemesis Shawn Michaels……

……it IS four against two however……and the KLiQ manage to fend off the attack and begin to turn the tides…..which is when Austin tosses Pillman into the crowd and turns to head back towards the ring, presumably to even up the odds……….

…….which is when the corner posts explode in a pyro of fire…..and just like at the Royal Rumble, Vince McMahon’s hired monster stalks down to the ring to ensure the safety of the WWF Champion……

……Austin turns to meet the Big Red Machine head on…..but he’s already been through a war, and Kane is fresh as a daisy. Meanwhile Pillman has joined the KLiQ in the ring and the five men easily overwhelm the pairing of Scott Steiner and Chris Jericho…….leaving Kane to deliver the final blow of the evening on the outside…..as he Chokeslams Austin onto the steel entrance ramp…..

……and the show comes to a close on that image…..as Michaels holds the WWF Championship high in the air……Hall and Waltman do the same with their Tag Team Titles……and Justin Credible hands Pillman the Intercontinental Championship……which he drapes over his shoulder while placing a foot on the chest of the downed Scott Steiner…..it would seem that Vince McMahon’s chequebook is serving him well here tonight…….we’ll see you next week folks……

Rating: A*

Final Rating: A

Week 2, February 1997

Week 2, February 1997

Thomas & Mack Centre (Las Vegas, Nevada)

ATT: 18,700

TV: 25.14

The sirens wail through the arena, which can only signal the arrival of one man…..reigning WWF Intercontinental Champion, and current #1 Contender to the World Wrestling Federation Championship, Big Poppa Pump, Scott Steiner……and judging by the steel pipe in his hand, Scotty is in a bad, bad, mood. He hits the ring and while a live microphone in the hands of Scott Steiner is always a little risky, he clearly has something to say tonight…..

“……..BRIAN PILLMAN!!…….IF YOU’RE HALF AS CRAZY AS YOU TRY AND MAKE OUT, AND IF YOU CAN DRAG YOUR TONGUE OUT OF VINCE MCMAHON’S ASS LONG ENOUGH, THEN GET YOUR ASS DOWN TO THIS RING……UNLESS YOU DON’T HAVE THE BALLS TO FACE A REAL MAN…….YOU PLACED YOUR HANDS ON ME……YOU PLACED YOUR HANDS ON MY TITLE……..YOU MIGHT CALL YOURSELF THE LOOSE CANNON, BUT WHEN I’M DONE WITH YOU, THE ONLY THINGS THAT ARE GOING TO BE LOOSE…….ARE YOUR DENTAL CAVITIES……AND YOUR RECTAL WALL……..BECAUSE FIRST I’M GONNA KNOCK YOUR TEETH OUT…….AND THEN I’M GONNA SHOVE THIS STEEL PIPE RIGHT UP YOUR ASS………”

…….Steiner paces around the ring for a moment waiting for Pillman to show…..but when the Corporate Cannon doesn’t come out, Big Poppa Pump gets even angrier (if that’s even possible)…….

“………..YOU’VE ALREADY P*SSED ME OFF BOY……AND WHEN BIG POPPA PUMP GETS P*SSED, LITTLE B*TCHES LIKE YOU GET HURT…….I’LL TELL YOU WHAT, PUT ON YOUR BIG BOY PANTS……GRAB THE BOY TOY……GRAB THAT BIG RED FRANKENSTEIN LOOKING MONKEY……GET HALL AND WALTMAN AND CREDIBLE……THEN ALL SIX OF YOU CAN WALK DOWN TO THIS RING…..TAKE YOUR BALLS OUT OF VINCE MCMAHONS BRIEFCASE…..AND I’LL TAKE YOU ALL OUT RIGHT HERE RIGHT NOW………”

….::::!!!!GLASS SHATTERS!!!!::::….

Apparently Mr. Pump isn’t the only one with a bone to pick with the McMahon administration…..because Stone Cold Steve Austin makes his way down the aisle, looking just as p*ssed off as Scott Steiner……he grabs a microphone of his own before climbing into the ring and going face to face with the Intercontinental Champion……

“……..I’ll tell you what…..if there’s someone around here that’s gonna be whipping some Corporate KLiQ ass, then you can be damn sure that Someone is gonna be Stone Cold Steve Austin…….see Shawn Michaels, Scott Hall, Sean Waltman…….even that ugly little rat faced b*stard, Justin Credible…..I’ve been whipping their ass for the best part of a year……but Brian Pillman and Kane, they just moved to the top of the list….and that’s Stone Colds list……and that’s the bottom line……so Scott Steiner, it goes like this……I don’t like you…..I don’t trust you…..and I sure as hell don’t respect you………..but hell son, if you’re fixing to start a fight with those Corporate sell-out sons of b*tches, it ain’t gonna be without Stone Cold Steve Austin, because I came here tonight to whip some ass …………”

……well that’s not the KLiQ……it’s not Brian Pillman……and it’s certainly not Kane…….it is however the young man who will be facing Scott Hall at In Your House, Chris Jericho. Jericho makes his way down the aisle and jumps into the ring in between two of the baddest men here in the WWF. Both Austin and Steiner stop and stare at the youngster slightly dumbfounded…..before Austin finally asks Jericho what exactly he thinks he’s doing? Jericho doesn’t back down an inch, telling both men that they aren’t the only ones looking for a fight tonight…..he says that he got his ass kicked last week…..THIS week it’s payback time……….

……before either Austin or Steiner can reply however, we are treated to the arrival of the KLiQ…….The Corporation?…….The Administration?….,,The Corporate KLiQ?…….who knows…….what we do know is that Vince McMahon is out, along with the WWF Champion Shawn Michaels, The Corporate Cannon Brian Pillman, and the hired monster Kane…..along with Scott Hall, Sean Waltman and Justin Credible……

…….Vince says that Austin, Steiner and Jericho all have matches up coming at the In Your House Pay Per View……matches that they SHOULD be preparing for, instead of coming out here making challenges that they can’t deliver on……but if they want a fight that badly, then a fight they shall have….when they team together tonight in six man tag team action, to take on the KLiQ combination of Scott Hall, Sean Waltman and Justin Credible…..and with a bit of luck, they will all be too banged up to even make it to the Pay Per View. Austin and Steiner aren’t happy about that…..both men wanted a piece of Michaels and Pillman……and Austin has been desperate to get his hands on Kane after being Chokeslammed in the floor last week……but Vince says no……Shawn Michaels has a big WWF Championship defence against Marty Jannetty to prepare for……Brian Pillman is busy study tapes of Big Poppa Pump in preparation for HIS upcoming Intercontinental Title match……and Kane…..well Kane would love to fight……but Vince has other plans for him tonight…..but don’t worry……because Hall, Waltman and Credible will provide all the pain they clearly want to endure.

Rating: A*

VS.

Ahmed Johnson vs. Jeff Jarrett (w/Ted DiBiase)


Ahmed Johnson came up short last week in his efforts to secure a victory over Million Dollar Corporation member Rich Steiner……partly due to the interference of tonight’s opponent Jeff Jarrett…..because of that, Ahmed is particularly motivated tonight…..not only to gain his revenge on the former Country Singer, but also to get back to the winning ways that saw him Challenge for the WWF Title at the tail end of last year.

These two know each other pretty well, having met each other a number of times in the past, and they always manage to deliver an entertaining match….and tonight is no different. Much like last week, the Corporation attempt to utilise their members to garner a victory……last week they were successful……tonight however, not so much……because as Rich Steiner runs down to the ring, he grabs a hold of Ahmed, leaving him prone for a strike by Jarretts trademark guitar…….tonight however Ahmed is prepared, and manages to move out of harms way at the last second, meaning it’s Steiner that eats the Acoustic Equaliser……Rich collapses in a pile of shattered wood, while Ahmed hits Jarrett with the Pearl River Plunge for the one, two, three.

Winner: Ahmed Johnson
Rating: B-

Ahmed celebrates his victory over the fallen form of Jarrett, while Rich Steiner is still down and out in the floor. Obviously not happy with his charges being embarrassed, DiBiase signals to the back, bringing out Mark Canterbury and Dennis Knight. Southern Justice hit the ring and begin laying into Big Ahmed……but as Johnson’s tights begin riding higher and higher into the crack of his ass, the big man Hulks up and begins to fight back, bouncing Canterbury and Knight around the ring. We air a quick promo video hyping next months Wrestlemania extravaganza and then it’s time for the main event of the evening, as we have Scott Steiner against the freak…….but not the freak he wanted…..because instead of Kane, tonight he’s facing off against the Bizarre One, Goldust…..and while Goldust has Marlena in his corner, the sounds of breaking glass signals the arrival of Stone Cold Steve Austin, who takes up position on the announce table as this one gets under way…..

……unfortunately, while I seriously underestimated the quality of our previous match, I seriously OVER estimated the potential quality of this one…..because despite the fact that both Scott Steiner and Goldust have been two of my most consistent performers in the last 12 months, tonight they just couldn’t deliver against each other…….it was probably a variation of circumstances that caused this…..how well the previous contest was received, the fact that neither men had any previous history with each other…….truth be told it was simply a poor match choice on my half……the guys did as well as they could under the conditions they had……and Steiner picked up another impressive victory ahead of his Intercontinental showdown with Brian Pillman in six days, as well as his World Title opportunity at Wrestlemania……but all in all it was a pretty disappointing main event.

Winner: Scott Steiner
Rating: B

Thankfully, once the final bell rang things seriously picked up……because as Steiner was celebrating his victory, we were treated to the arrival of Brian Pillman and Kane…….with The Corporate Cannon running down towards Steiner, while Kanes methodical stalk down the aisle way was met by the fists of fury of Stone Cold Steve Austin, who left the announce table in a hurry…….and with Shawn Michaels tending to the KLiQ, and Lance Storm still feeling the effects of Jannetty’s Superkick, we finally have a fair fight on our hands……..and the crowd absolutely erupts as bedlam ensues and Pillman and Steiner go to war in the ring….while Kane and Austin attempt to kill each other on the outside, as Jim Ross frantically signs off, informing us that we can’t afford to miss In Your House this Sunday, so contact your local cable provider right now people……

Rating: A*

Final Rating: B+

Final Thoughts

So that was a rather interesting show to say the least…..I honestly thought I would never see the day that a Marty Jannetty match out performed a Scott Steiner match, but tonight I saw first hand what a hot storyline and the abilities of both Shawn Michaels and Chris Jericho can do…….it can even carry Marty Jannetty’s drink and drug damaged body to a near instant classic. Truth be told, the thought of Marty Jannetty main eventing a Pay Per View…..any Pay Per View actually terrified me to the point that I was willing to break my own golden rule and have the WWF Championship match open the show, leaving Steiner and Pillman to headline with the Intercontinental Championship, which would probably be the first time since Summerslam 92 that the secondary title has been higher on the card than the Big One…..but now I’m not so sure, I’m actually curious to see what Shawn can do with Marty in a straight up one on one affair……although whether that curiosity actually leads to me putting them on last will probably be a decision that I’m still deciding on right up until showtime Sunday……which is less than six days away……tick tock, tick tock, decisions, decisions…….
