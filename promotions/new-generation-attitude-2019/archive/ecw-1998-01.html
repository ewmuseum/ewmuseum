ECW Hardcore TV: Week 1, January 1998

Posted 21 June 2019

Week 1, January 1998

Highlights from ECW Cyberslam 1998

We open up the first Hardcore TV of 1998 with Joey Styles in the middle of the ring, welcoming a rabid Philadelphia crowd to Cyberslam 1998…..and he promises us an extreme night of action here in the ECW Arena, telling us that the Hardcore Legend himself, 53 year old Terry Funk, will be challenging The Sandman, for the ECW World Heavyweight Championship…..

…..but that won’t be the only Title on the line, because a staple part of Extreme Championship Wrestling’s history makes its return tonight….the three way dance….when The Eliminators defend the ECW Tag Team Titles against The Gangsta’s and two (as yet un-named) members of the Dudley Family…..

…..in fact, the only title that WON’T be on this line this evening, is Tommy Dreamer’s (very) newly won Television Title, a title that he won just one week ago from 911…..

…..although that announcement is interrupted, as Man in the Box begins to play over the sound system (we don’t care about little things like copyright infringement around here) and the Innovator of Violence himself makes his way out from the back, with the beautiful Beulah on his arm…..

…..Tommy gets a great reception from this crowd (as he always does) especially with Beulah seductively strutting around the ring behind him, and he unhooks his newly won Television Title to drape it over the shoulder of his manager, before asking Joey if he heard him right?…..

…..Tommy says that this isn’t WCW…..this isn’t the WWF…..this is ECW, and if you’re a champion in ECW then you’d better be ready to step up and put that title on the line every single night…..

“…..there ain’t no thirty day saving grace around here Joey Styles, you got some gold in ECW then you either nut up, or shut up…..and I don’t feel like shutting up anytime soon…..”

…..Tommy says that he may have only just won this Television Title, but he’s damn sure going to put it on the line tonight…..so anyone in the back wants a shot, they got it…..

“…..Tommy, Tommy, Tommy…..you never did know just when to shut the hell up did you?…..”

…..talk about the pot calling the kettle black…..because if there’s one guy that doesn’t know how to shut up here in ECW, it’s probably this guy…..The Franchise, Shane Douglas…..and just as Tommy Dreamer has Beulah by his side, Douglas has Francine on his arm as he makes his way out…..

…..not out to the ring mind you…..

…..Shane and Francine are standing a safe distance away up in one of the production area’s, and he has a huge smirk on his face as he quickly answers Dreamers open challenge…..

…..he says that since that old fart Terry Funk has (for some godforsaken reason) been handed a shot at the ECW Title tonight, the Franchise WAS going to hop into a Limo with Francine, and drive as far away from this armpit of a city as quick as they could…..but then Tommy went and opened his big mouth…..

“…..now truth be told, I don’t even WANT your title Tommy…..because unlike you, whether it’s titles or women, I’m not happy to settle for second best….I want the World Title…..but the way we see it, more gold equals more money…..and unlike that cheap tramp on YOUR arm, Francine has expensive tastes, ha ha…..”

…..obviously Tommy doesn’t take too kindly to that, telling Shane to bring his ass down to the ring, so that they can do this right now, but Shane is in no rush, telling Tommy that this is exactly WHY he has a cheap tramp like Beulah on his arm instead of a real lady like Francine…..because he’s always been a little bit quick off the mark…..a little premature……the Franchise WILL take that title from him……but he’ll do it at his own pace…..later tonight…..

…..with that, Douglas and Francine turn and walk to the back (I’m guessing to prepare for Douglas’ upcoming title opportunity) while we go from Joey Styles in the arena, to Joey Styles in front of an ECW backdrop, officially welcoming up to Hardcore TV…..

Rating: C-

…..where he tells us that yes, this match did in fact take place….Tommy Dreamer DID put the ECW Television Title on the line against Shane Douglas, and we will be seeing that match in its entirety…..next week on ECW Hardcore TV……but that’s next week……TONIGHT, The Sandman will be defending the ECW Title against Terry Funk, a match which we will see later this evening…..

…..but from a veteran of the sport like Terry Funk, we go now to two relative newcomers…..two men who very recently graduated from ECW’s House of Hardcore training school by the name of Danny Doring and Roadkill…..and they are being tested straight off the bat, against an established ECW combination…..Bad Crew…..

Rating: C

Danny Doring & Roadkill (w/Miss Congeniality) vs. Bad Crew


…..now let me just say…..”established” doesn’t mean “good”…..because my God do the Bad Crew live up to their name…..they’re awful…..just really, really bad and exactly the kind of team ECW need to move away from if they have any chance of expanding from the little regional company that they are right now…..but even so they are at least more recognisable than their opponents…..although their opponents do have something that the Bad Crew don’t…..and that is a young lady accompanying them to the ring……

…..Joey Styles says that these two newcomers couldn’t be more different…..the brash, cocky (and colourful) Danny Doring and well, the Amish Roadkill…..but for some reason they seem to work…..and when you throw in the rather scantily clad Miss Congeniality on the outside, there is already more entertainment to the rookie team than the (rather bland) established one…..

…..all the Bad Crew really know what to do is brawl, so that’s how this goes…..thankfully it wasn’t a particularly long match, Danny Doring was severely outweighed by both members of Bad Crew, but Roadkill more than held his own, eventually fighting off both men before hooking Bad Crew #1 in a Wheelbarrow hold, as Danny Doring came flying off the top rope with a Leg Drop that drives his opponents skull into the mat to give the newcomers the one, two, three.

Winner(s): Danny Doring & Roadkill
Rating: E+

Following their big debut victory we cut straight to some prerecorded footage of ECW’s newest trio somewhere in the back…..it’s Doring that does the talking here (while Miss Congeniality sways seductively and Roadkill pulls some very strange faces) and while Doring is the better talker of the three, he’s is still raw….but then at only 23 years of age he still has plenty of time to improve…..

…..he basically tells us that he and Roadkill are here in ECW to splash some entertainment onto these blood soaked mats and take those ECW Tag Team Titles for themselves…..

“…..because we’ve got it all…..we got the glitz and the glamour…..we got the charisma and the ability…..we got skills…..we got pizzazz…..we got…..we got………….what else we got Roadkill?…..”

“…..chickens…..”

“…..we got chickens……wait, what?…..nevermind…..we got the whole package……we’ve got the beauty and the beast…..AND we got Miss Congeniality…..and before too long, we’ll be telling you that we got those ECW Tag Team Titles too…..”

…..but wait, there’s more…..as we go straight into another prerecorded shot…..this time it’s coming from the Double Cross Ranch in Amarillo Texas, and it’s a gentleman that most definitely DOES know how to cut a promo…..

…..the Hardcore Legend himself…..Terry Funk…..

…..Terry says that tonight, he steps into the ring to challenge for a championship that he last won back in December of 1993…..he says that for the last four years he’s sat back and watched jumped up little pissant’s like Shane Douglas hold that title…..he’s seen Mikey Whipwreck hold that title…..he’s seen the Sandman hold that title…..and not one of those men has been fit to scrape the horsesh#t off his boots let alone lace them…..

…..Terry says that he’s wrestled all around the world and back again, worked in every promotion that was ever worth a damn and a few that weren’t, and he has NEVER felt more at home than he does right here in ECW…..

“…..this is EXTREME Championship Wrestling…..they don’t care how old you are….they don’t care what they look like…..all they care about, is can you fight…..I am 53 years old, but I can fight…..I can scratch…..I can claw…..I can bite……I can damn sure wrap a steel chair around some young punks head…..and when it comes to the ECW Title, there is no one more extreme than Terry Funk…..”

Rating: B

Axl Rotten vs. D-Von Dudley (w/The Dudley Boys)


We go back to the action from Cyberslam now, and it’s Axl Rotten facing off against a member of the Dudley Clan…..the meanest member of the Dudley Clan to be fair…..because it’s D-Von Dudley that’s stepping up to the challenge…..

…..much like our previous “match” this is a straight up brawl…..unlike Bad Crew however, both of these men know how to have an entertaining garbage match, and that’s exactly what they have here as they beat the blood out of each other…..

…..the Dudley Boys have been somewhat of a happy go lucky group of oddballs thus far in their tenure with ECW…..a pretty hardcore group of oddballs granted, but they have always seemed to lack that killer instinct that you need to survive here in Extreme Championship Wrestling, something which D-Von Dudley is keen to change…..

…..it’s not a change that’s coming easy though…..

…..the whole family is here tonight…..the stuttering Buh Buh, the snarling Big Dick, the mute Sign Guy, the chewing Chubby and the smiling Dances with…..and D-Von spends a good portion of the match attempting to get his dysfunctional family to involve themselves, something that they appear reluctant to do, so much so that after constant badgering Buh Buh Ray finally DOES swing a steel chair…..

…..straight at his brothers head…..

…..unfortunately D-Von sees it coming…..moves…..and it’s Axl that takes the shot right between the eyes…..gifting D-Von the victory…..

Winner: D-Von Dudley
Rating: C-

…..not that a win is good enough for D-Von, who snatches the steel chair out of the hands of Buh Buh and proceeds to hammer blows down onto Axl Rotten…..

…..Rotten has been running solo ever since he and his brother Ian dissolved their team (and embarked on a brief but bitter feud) and made quite the impact as a singles competitor here in ECW, holding onto the Television Title from June of 1996 until January of 1997…..since losing the gold twelve months ago however he hasn’t seen quite the same levels of success, and right about now is probably wishing he was still a tag team competitor, because at least then he would have someone to watch his back…..

…..D-Von turns and thrusts the steel chair towards Chubby Dudley, ordering him to strike a blow to Axl…..Chubby isn’t exactly forthcoming, so D-Von grabs him by the throat (terrifying Chubby to the extent that he actually drops the donut he was casually chewing on) and forces the chair into his hand…..

…..Chubby has little option but to deliver the blow…..but it’s the softest, most unenthusiastic chair shot in history, before Chubby hands the chair back with an apologetic look on his face…..

…..one by one D-Von repeats the action with the rest of the Dudleys, and one by one they disappoint him….Dances with Dudley delivers a blow about as pathetic as Chubby Dudley’s was…..Sign Guy hits him with his cardboard sign…..Big Dick (who usually enjoys a bit of casual violence) obviously doesn’t like being told WHEN to hit people, because he just growls at D-Von, while Buh Buh just shakes his head…..

…..D-Von finally forces the chair into his hands, telling him to stop acting like a little b!tch and start acting like a ####ing Dudley…..

“…..you ARE a Dudley right? What’s your ####ing name?…..”

“…..my name is Buh, Buh, Buh, Buh, Buh…..”

…..it takes a big slap to the back from Big Dick to stop Buh Buh from stuttering, to which he shouts out a loud “#### YOU”before again swinging the steel at his brothers head…..

…..and again D-Von moves…..

…..and again (the just back on his feet) Axl takes the full shot right between the eyes…..

…..D-Von just looks on and laughs at the sight of the bloody Axl Rotten on the ground…..I guess he inadvertently got his way after all, because twice he’s told Buh Buh to nail Rotten, and twice (albeit by accident) Buh Buh has done just that…..

…..let’s go back to Joey Styles in the studio…..and by studio I mean Paul Heyman’s parents basement…..good job we have that nifty ECW banner to hide the fact…..

Rating: D

…..where he first of all remarks on what a piece of sh…I mean, what a piece of work D-Von Dudley is, before hyping up our next match…..a match for the ECW Championship that sees 53 year old Terry Funk, challenging the Sandman for a title that he hasn’t held in nearly four years…..

“…..and you can take it from me, this is going to be a match that you’re not going to want to miss…..”

Rating: N/A

ECW Championship Match

The Sandman (c) (w/Woman) vs. Terry Funk


Well, since Hardcore TV isn’t exactly structured like your regular wrestling programmes out there, we don’t have main event’s per say…..but this match WAS the main event at our Cyberslam event, so let’s just go with it…..

…..it’s The Sandman (still with Woman by his side) putting his ECW Title on the line against a man that embodies everything that Extreme Championship Wrestling stands for, the Hardcore Legend himself, Terry Funk…..

…..I know it’s been mentioned a few times already this evening, but Terry Funk IS a former ECW Champion, holding the Title way back in 1993 (when this place was still called Eastern Championship Wrestling) before losing the belt in March of 1994 to Shane Douglas…..in the near four years that followed, only three men have gone on to hold on to the championship since then…..Shane Douglas (obviously) a four time champion, with three of those reigns coming between March 1994 and now…..Mikey Whipwreck, a one time champion who upset our final name…..The Sandman…..the current (and five time) ECW Champion…..which tells us two things, one, Terry Funk could easily win the title here tonight…..he’s done it before……and two, ECW really need to freshen up their main event scene…..

…..for the record, for what it was (a hardcore brawl) this match was good…..no one will ever accuse Sandman of being a technical maestro…..in fact (to steal a quote) he probably doesn’t know a wristlock from a wristwatch, but if there is one thing he’s done well it’s connect with the ECW faithful…..hell, he could be one of them…..he’s your average beer swilling, cigarette smoking, gym dodging blue collar guy, but when that opening riff of Enter Sandman begins to play (again, because #### copyright infringement) these fans embrace him like no other…..

…..they love Terry Funk too…..

…..and they love extreme action…..which is exactly what these two men give them as they brawl all over the arena, using every available item as a weapon…..although as Joey Styles warned us, this was going to be a match you weren’t going to miss…..

…..because it marked the debut of this man…..

…..the former Aldo Montoya, but more recently he’s been known as Justin Credible, a former member of the dominant WWF “KLiQ” faction alongside Shawn Michaels, Triple H, Kevin Nash, Scott Hall and Sean Waltman…..and funnily enough (since I was talking about ECW’s rather stale World Title scene a few moments ago) because of that exposure he received in the WWF, he’s walked straight into the company at the top of the card, as main event as main event can get…..

…..so why is he here?…..well, to make an impression it would seem…..and after Sandman and Funk have beaten each other bloody, it doesn’t take much for him to lay them both out with a series of steel chair shots, until finally he drags The Sandman on top of Funks (equally) lifeless body, ordering the official to make the count…..and whether he wanted to win like this or not, The Sandman retains his title…..

Winner: ….and Still ECW Champion….The Sandman
Rating: C-

…..not that any of that appears to matter to this newcomer right now, as he poses for this very disapproving crowd…..ECW don’t really enforce the Face/Heel dynamics of traditional professional wrestling (something that I may have to address in the not so distant future) but if they DID then I would think that the loud and repeated “#### YOU ALDO” chant that has broken out in the arena would mean that Mr. Credible is most definitely a heel…..

…..he grabs a microphone to tell the arena (and the battered and bloodied Sandman and Funk) that ECW just got that little bit more interesting…..that little bit more important….he says that ECW just got that little bit more…..Credible…..

…..he says that for years now people have been whispering about the underground cult phenomenon that is Extreme Championship Wrestling…..so much so that he just had to check it out for himself…..

“…..and frankly…..I’m not impressed…..look at this place…..the “world famous” ECW arena, and by world famous I only mean in the minds of you morons in the crowd, because let’s face it, for you, this is as good as it gets…..the one night a week that you crawl out of your parents basements, try and mask your body odour with some cheap deodorant….and not very well, because you people stink by the way…..and waddle your fat asses down here for the “matches”…..

…..you come down here and watch a bunch of fat, no talent idiots throw themselves through tables for a couple of hours (probably feeling real good about yourselves, because for as sh#tty as your lives are, you’re not the one with a piece of barbed wire shoved straight up your ass) and then you crawl back to your basements and touch yourselves over the memory of that time that the Sandman spat beer all over you…..or the time Tommy Dreamer bled like a stuck pig all over your 5XL EC ‘F’n’ W shirt because “he’s hardcore”…..”

…..Credible stops his tirade for a second to pick up the Sandman’s trademark cane, which he uses to punish both Champion and Challenger, before telling the crowd that finally, all that is going to change…..they’re going to have a new idol…..a genuine superstar who has main evented Monday Night Raw…..a world class athlete, and once this drunk idiot sobers up, an ECW Champion that you can be proud of…..

…..and that’s not just the coolest…..that’s not just the best…..that my friends…..is Justin Credible!…..”

…..with that, Credible whales on both Funk and Sandman a little more, before making his exit from the arena…..with the Sandman’s Singapore Cane raised high in the air in celebration…..as this weeks Hardcore TV comes to a close.

Rating: B-

Final Rating: C

ECW Hardcore TV: Week 2, January 1998

Posted 17 July 2019

Week 2, January 1998

Highlights from ECW Cyberslam 1998

We continue to run down the events that transpired at our recent Cyberslam event, and the show opens this week with a recap of the end of LAST weeks show, and the ECW debut of Justin Credible…..

…..the clip highlights Credible’s disruption of our ECW Championship match between The Sandman and Terry Funk, as well as the newcomer’s brutal attack on both competitors post match that saw Justin Credible leave with The Sandman’s Singapore Cane…..

…..from there we go to Joey Style who officially welcomes us to the show. He spends a few minutes running down the actions of Justin Credible (who he claims is going to be in a world of hurt should either The Sandman or Terry Funk get hold of him) before letting us know that tonight we have two further title matches that Justin Credible definitely did NOT play a part in the outcome of…..

…..but before that (and fresh of a number of debut’s) we have another new arrival to the land of Extreme, as Steve Corino faces off against JT. Smith

Rating: N/A

JT Smith vs. Steve Corino


Ah, JT Smith…..if ever there was a guy that fell into his gimmick…..quite literally…..it’s JT Smith…..one botched dive some two and a half years ago left him with a big goose egg on his head and some gimmicked concussion that lead to Smith believing himself to be Italian…..and so the FBI (Full Blooded Italians) was born…..

…..despite founding the group, Smith has basically been ostracised from his own stable recently following a bad run of results, with The Big Don, Tommy Rich joining Little Guido and Tracy Smothers as the current incarnation of the FBI…..maybe a win here will go some way towards redeeming himself in the eyes of his Italian Brothers…..

…..he didn’t get a win…..

…..Steve Corino hasn’t done much of note in the wrestling business thus far, but he’s picked up his first victory in Extreme Championship Wrestling, and he did it in his very first match too…..good for him……let’s have some words from the gentlemen that closed out last weeks show shall we?…..

Winner: Steve Corino
Rating: D

…..although “gentlemen” might be a bit of a stretch for this trio…..because it’s the Sandman, Terry Funk…..and the latest arrival to the Land of Extreme…..Justin Credible……

…..it’s the Sandman who’s shown first, in a shot that’s clearly been recorded (judging by the blood still covering his face) directly following his Cyberslam encounter with Terry Funk…..he’s got a beer in one hand and the ECW Title in the other, but he’s clearly missing something…..that being his usually ever present Singapore Cane that is now in the possession of Justin Credible…..

…..he’s not a great promo our Sandy, but he’s not terrible either…..this was nothing groundbreaking, he simply stared into the camera as the blood flows down his face, telling Terry Funk that any time he wants another shot at this (holding the title up) he only has to ask…..

…..the brunt of his promo is aimed at Justin Credible, telling the newcomer that if HE wants a shot at this title, he only had to ask too, but if it’s just a fight he wants, Sandman isn’t hard to find…..and if he’s not in the arena, just go and look in the nearest bar…..but one way or another, Sandman is going to take that Singapore Cane back from Credible, and smash it over his stupid bald head……

…..the Funker is up next, and he too appears to be getting stitched up following his (failed) bid at capturing the ECW Title, as well as the post match assault from Credible…..

…..Funk too only briefly touches on his original opponent The Sandman (telling the ECW Champion that he hasn’t seen the last of him) before he also turns his attention to Credible, telling that “rat faced, buck tooth b@stard” that he’s going to wish he’d stayed up at the WWF with a Jock Strap on his head by the time Funk’s finished with him…..Funk says that he’s been taking snot nosed punks like Justin Credible for years and making them “bleed like a virgin on prom night” just for fun…..well Justin Credible has p!ssed him off, so what does he think Funk is going to do to him?…..

…..speaking of Justin Credible, he’s the final speaker in our segment, and he’s standing in front of that ECW backdrop, the Sandman’s cane resting against his shoulder…..

…..he says that here he is, in the so-called ECW arena…..the land of extreme? More like the land of the has-been’s and the never-was’…..he says that Terry Funk should have retired a decade ago, but he’s still hobbling around trying to keep his place in the spotlight at the expense of someone actually deserving……well Justin Credible is going to take him out and put him down, like the piece of garbage that he is…..

…..and as for the never-was…..The Sandman…..the fat, out of shape alcoholic that has the nerve to call himself a World Champion…..well Justin Credible knows what a World Champion should look like…..he spent a year along side one of the greatest champions of all time, his close personal friend Shawn Michaels…..

…..he says that the KLiQ might not be together in body, but they’re together in spirit…..all they’re doing is dividing and conquering…..Shawn and Hunter went to WCW, Kev, Scott and Sean are taking control of the WWF……and Justin Credible, he’s come to clean up and legitimise the sh!thole that is ECW…..

“…..and soon, all those fans in the ECW arena, they’ll have a champion they can be proud of…..a TRUE champion…..a SPORTS ENTERTAINMENT champion…..and that won’t be just the coolest……that won’t be just the best…..that’ll be Justin Credible…..”

Rating: B

We go back to Joey Styles from there, who stood with a look on his face like he’s just smelt something really, really bad…..like, Blue Meanie’s ass crack bad…..he says that of all the things Justin Credible could do, all the things he could say…..and THAT’S what he wants to do? Bring “sports entertainment” to Extreme Championship Wrestling?……

……Joey almost spits the two words out…..“sports….entertainment”……before saying that if you’re looking for sports entertainment then you are in the wrong place…..especially with our next match……the three way dance for the ECW Tag Team Titles…..let’s go back to ringside……

Rating: N/A

ECW Tag Team Championship: Three Way Dance Match

The Eliminators (c) vs. The Gangsta’s vs. The Dudley Boys


…..he’s not wrong either…..because straight from Cyberslam, comes The Eliminators defending the ECW Tag Titles against The Gangsta’s and The Dudley Boys, in a three way dance…..

…..and this was just carnage from start to finish…..

…..the Eliminators have held onto these titles since June of last year (with Perry Saturn even holding the ECW Television Title simultaneously for around four months) but they’re going to have their work cut out for them here…..because not only to they have the Gangsta’s to contend with, they have the Dudley Boys……ALL of the Dudley Boys…..because as we saw last week, D-Von Dudley is on a mission to instil a little killer instinct into his brothers, and winning the tag titles would be a good start…..

…..unfortunately, happy-go-lucky Buh Buh and the ever smiling Dances with Dudley don’t appear to have a great deal of killer instinct, and both men are bleeding before long as an arena wide (weapon filled) brawl breaks out…..

…..was this a good match? Oh god no…..did the fans care? Did they f##k…..they ate this up as all six men (nine men if you count D-Von, Big Dick and Chubby, all of whom got in on the action) battered and bloodied their way around the building…..

…..Kronus and Mustafa are both somewhat mentally unstable, and spent the majority of the match beating on each other……Saturn is the best worker of the bunch and put in a shift to prove it, while New Jack…..well, he just don’t give a sh!t, emphasised by the fact that he came leaping out of the balcony of the arena, taking out pretty much the entire Dudley family…..

…..all except Dances, who ate the first fall of the match, pinned in the ring by Mustafa…..who was quickly taken out himself with some Total Elimination, as the champions retain once more…..

Winner(s): …..and Still ECW Tag Team Champions…..The Eliminators
Rating: C+

…..we already know that D-Von was particularly tightly sprung at this Cyberslam event, after seeing the beatdown he attempted to force his brothers to inflict on Axl Rotten last week (or earlier this evening depending how you look at it) and he’s in no better mood following that loss…..

…..poor Dances gets the brunt of his ire here, as he verbally tears his brother apart, calling him a waste of time, effort “and Daddy Dudley’s d!ck juice”…..Chubby and Buh Buh try to comfort poor Dances, but they both get a slap in the face for their efforts, before Dances with Dudley gets kicked right in the b@lls…..

…..Buh Buh actually looks pretty ticked off by that slap, and he squares right up to D-Von…..a fact that actually pleases the angriest Dudley, telling his brother to finally grow a set and do something about it…..

…..he doesn’t though…..

…..and Buh Buh finally backs down, leaving D-Von to walk off in disgust (audibly calling his entire family a “bunch of f#####g b!tches” as he goes) while the rest of the brothers comfort poor Dances.

Rating: C

…..we have a brief merch shilling segment with Lance Wright, before we go right back to Joey Styles, who recaps what we saw last week between ECW Television Champion Tommy Dreamer and Shane Douglas…..Dreamer not having a match on Cyberslam…..issuing an open challenge…..and the Franchise instantly accepting…..

…..that much we saw LAST week…..THIS week it’s the match itself……

…..and that’s happening next…..

Rating: N/A

ECW Television Championship Match

Tommy Dreamer (c) (w/Beulah) vs. Shane Douglas (w/Francine)


…..so it’s The Innovator of Violence vs. The Franchise, for the Television Title that Tommy Dreamer won just one week ago…..

…..if you could epitomise the very spirit of ECW, Tommy Dreamer would perhaps be the living, breathing (and occasionally bleeding) perfect example of what Extreme Championship Wrestling is all about…..Tommy would give his last breath for these ECW fans, so it’s surprising that this Television Title is his lone championship win in the company…..

…..surely he’s going to have a long, memorable reign right?…..

…..nope…..

…..and he can thank Francine for that…..

…..because as Dreamer sets up Douglas for the DDT (hey, Louis Spicolli is still alive….no Spicolli Driver needed here) Francine rolls into the ring and nails the Television Champion with a low blow, leaving Douglas with plenty of opportunity to hit the Belly to Belly Suplex for the one, the two, the three…….and the Television Title…..

Winner: …..and New ECW Television Champion…..Shane Douglas
Rating: B-

…..the official hands Shane Douglas the Television Title, just as Beulah dives into the ring to take Francine down to the mat by the hair…..and yes, as Joey Styles emphatically informs us, we do indeed have a “catfight…..catfight…..catfight”…..

…..obviously the crowd likes that (particularly because both ladies are wearing very short outfits) but the Franchise doesn’t like it…..probably because Beulah is getting the better of the confrontation……

…..Douglas grabs hold of Beulah and drags her off Francine, before tossing her down to the mat, just as Tommy Dreamer pulls himself back to his feet…..

…..so the Franchise drills him right between the eyes with the Television Title belt…..

…..then turns…..grabs hold of Beulah again…..

…..and slams her to the canvas with that patented Belly to Belly Suplex…..

…..which brings out (surprisingly) the man that Tommy Dreamer defeated for the TV Title…..it would seem that Shane Douglas assaulting Beulah was a step too far here tonight, and 911 isn’t going to sit back and watch and do nothing……

…..looks like someone could be due a Chokeslam…..

…..well maybe, but maybe not…..either way it won’t be tonight…..because by the time 911 hits the ring, Shane Douglas, Francine AND the Television Title, are long gone…..leaving Joey Styles to bid us farewell……

…..and call the Franchise a no good son of a b!tch…..

…..we’ll see you next week with more action from Cyberslam 1998…..

Rating: C+

Final Rating: C+

ECW Hardcore TV: Week 3, January 1998

Posted 13 August 2019

…..D-Von throws his microphone down on the still flat on his back Dances with Dudley, gives Big Dick the finger…..slaps Buh Buh in the mouth…..and shoves Sign Guy down to the mat…..before telling ALL the Dudleys that they’re nothing more than a bunch of p###y’s…..before finally heading to the back, leaving his shell shocked Brothers in the ring…..

Rating: C+

…..and as we leave the Dudleys in the ring, we head off to the command centre, where Joey Styles is standing by…..

…..he recaps the events of Cyberslam, which saw Shane Douglas not only take the ECW Television Championship from Tommy Dreamer (less than a week after he won the title himself from 911) but he also committed the heinous act of driving Beulah into the mat too with the Franchise’s patented Belly to Belly…..

…..remarkably, it was the man that Tommy Dreamer defeated to win the ECW TV Title that made the save…..when 911 made his way out from the back to put a stop from the attack, and potentially Chokeslam Douglas through the mat…..

…..except he did t get a chance…..because before he’d climbed into the ring. The Franchise had already bailed…..

…..Joey says that he’s seen Tommy Dreamer since then…..he’s spoken to Tommy Dreamer since then too…..and with the mood Tommy is in, may god have mercy on Shane Douglas’ black soul when Tommy Dreamer finally catches up with him…..

“…..and if you don’t believe me, watch this…..”

Rating: N/A

…..with “this” being footage from an ECW live event….Fan Cam footage to be precise, in which Shane Douglas put the Television Title on the line against House of Hardcore graduate, Chris Chetti…..

…..we don’t see the whole match (which was already in progress when we joined it) but we see enough to realise that this is no run of the mill squash match…..Chetti may be a relative newcomer, but Heyman obviously sees something in him, because he’s taking Douglas to the very limit here…..in fact, it’s perhaps only the interference of Francine that stopped us from having our second Television Title change in as many weeks, as she raked the youngsters eyes long enough for Douglas to connect with the Belly to Belly…..and retain his championship…..

…..with the win in the bag, Douglas grabs the house mic to tell Chetti to come back in a couple of years and maybe…..maybe…..he’ll have a chance at beating the Franchise…..

“…..but probably not…..”

…..because Chris Chetti just found out the same thing that Tommy Dreamer found out…..that 911 WILL find out if he sticks his nose in Shane Douglas’ business again…..and the same thing that Beulah found out first hand…..

“…..and that’s that you don’t get up from the Belly to Belly…..”

…..Douglas and Francine get a good laugh from that…..but they’re not laughing when Tommy Dreamer suddenly comes rushing down the aisle…..

…..he slides into the ring and dives for Douglas, but the Franchise manages to avoid his grip, scurrying under the bottom rope and down to the floor…..

…..not to be deterred, Dreamer rolls right out after him, and a brief cat and mouse chase ensues…..Shane is a slippery son of a bitch though, and try as he might Tommy simply can’t catch hold of the Television Champion, who finally retreats up the aisle, taunting Dreamer as he goes…..

…..at least until he backs right into the huge form of 911…..

…..the former Television Champion snatches at Douglas, looking to wrap his hand around the throats of the Franchise, but with his exit blocked and Tommy Dreamer back in the ring, Douglas does the only thing he can…..

…..he bails through the crowd…..

…..not that the crowd are happy about it, but Douglas charges his way through until he reaches the eagles nest position, where (safety resumed) he continues to taunt both Dreamer AND 911, until we finally go back to Joey Styles…..

…..Shane Douglas man…..what a prick…..

Rating: B

Terry Funk vs. Tommy Rich (w/The FBI)


…..Joey spends a few moments again basically calling Shane Douglas a piece of sh!t, before sending us to our final match of the evening…..and we’re rolling back the years here, as two former NWA World Champions collide in Tommy Rich and Terry Funk…..

…..that probably makes this sound like a more grandiose affair than it actually was…..because when you bear in mind that Rich held the ten pounds of gold for all of four days, then it takes the shine off the “former world champions clashing” tag just a little bit…..

…..these days Tommy Rich is better known for his alliance with the Full Bloodied Italians, where he serves as “The Big Don”…..the head honcho…..the main made man if you will…..and his foot soldiers are right there with him, because Little Guido and Tracy Smothers accompany him to ringside…..

…..as you would expect, aesthetically this was hardly the best looking of matches…..it WAS however a wild brawl and everyone knows how much these ECW fans like a wild brawl…..so obviously, this was pretty well received…..

…..the two elder statesmen brawled all over the arena (with Smothers and Guido getting involved on more than one occasion) but it wasn’t the FBI that made the difference here…..

…..rather, the FORMER member of the FBI, JT Smith…..

…..Smith had previously tried to involve himself in the Full Bloodied Italians affairs (presumably in an effort to return to their good graces) but in typical Smith form, he botched the help and cost the FBI the match…..

…..he won’t make that mistake twice though right?…..

…..wrong…..

…..because he runs down the aisle (steel chair in hand) and goes to swing the weapon at Funk…..

…..unfortunately he trips over his own feet as he enters the ring and winds up accidentally nailing The Big Don in the back of the head with the steel…..

…..Funk tosses Smith back out of the ring, drags a groggy Rich back to his feet…..

…..piledrives him to the mat…..

…..and makes the cover for the one, two, three…..

…..silly JT Smith…..Terry Funk wins…..

Winner: Terry Funk
Rating: C

…..the FBI head to the back (with Smith apologising all the way) while Funk grabs a microphone…..

…..he tells the crowd that he came here tonight to fight, and he ain’t done fighting yet…..so either that “fat, beer swilling sweat hog, The Sandman” gets his ass out here, or that “jumped up little pissant, Justin Credible” comes down to the ring, because one way or another, Terry Funk isn’t finished with EITHER man…..

…..he wants The Sandman’s ECW Title…..he wants to shove his boot past Justin Credible’s yellow buck teeth…..

“…..hell…..I just want to fight…..so come on boys…..unless you don’t got the balls to pick a fight with the Funker…..”

…..copyright be damned…..because this building erupts as Metallica’s “Enter Sandman” begins to play…..and it gets even louder when The ECW Champion himself emerges in the crowd, guzzling down a cold one…..

…..then Justin Credible cracks him in the back of the head with the Kendo Stick…..

…..Funk might be looking for a fight, but the fight at the minute is taking place in the crowd, as Credible tosses Sandman down a flight of stairs, before commencing to lash him with cane shots across the back…..

…..The Funker isn’t happy to wait for the fight to come to him, so he throws himself over the guardrail and makes his way through the crowd to where the action is…..

…..by the time he gets there though, Credible has finished his assault…..and as Funk gets within ten feet of the brash young upstart, Credible gives a good old fashioned (KLiQ approved) Crotch Chop, along with the obligatory “Suck It old man”, before delivering one final shot to The Sandman and disappearing back to wherever the hell he came from…..

…..Terry Funk wanted a fight…..he’s not getting one……

…..Sandman is down, Justin Credible has gone…..and as Terry stands there looking frustrated in the crowd, Joey Styles signs off…..but not before calling Justin Credible a punk…..

…..we’ll see you next week, with results from House Party 1998…..

Rating: B+

Final Rating: C-
