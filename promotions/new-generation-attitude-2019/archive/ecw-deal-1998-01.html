The Call

Posted 11 June 2019

January 1st, 1998: 09:00am

The Call

…..:::::Ring Ring:::::…..

…..:::::Ring Ring:::::…..

…..:::::Ring Ring:::::…..

…..:::::Click:::::…..

“……………………….”

“…..hello?…..”

“……………………….”

“…..hello?…….what the hell sort of Mickey Mouse company…..”

“…..hello?…..my name……is Paul Heyman……”

“…..I know who you are Paul, I called you…..”

…..this was going to be one of THOSE sorts of conversations wasn’t it…..

“…..you did indeed call me Sir, and if I could ask how you got this number? Who you are? And perhaps most importantly of all, why in the name of all that is holy are you calling me just nine hours into the New Year, and a mere four hours after I went to bed?…..”

…..yeah…..definitely one of those sorts of conversation…..

….to be fair, I suppose I could have planned a better time and day to call, but I’ve always been one to act when the idea is still hot…..and this idea could be very hot…..

…..as you probably gathered, the gentleman on the receiving end of this phone call is named Paul Heyman, although you probably know him better by his stage name…..Paul E. Dangerously…..

…..as Paul E. Dangerously, Paul Heyman had great success in the early 90’s as both a commentator and manager for World Championship Wrestling. In fact, his stable of clients (christened the Dangerous Alliance) is still, even today, heralded as one of the greatest groups assembled, and this is some five years after they disbanded…..not bad for a group that lasted barely a year…..

…..the group would perhaps have lasted longer, had Paul not fell afoul of then booker Bill Watts’ cost cutting measures…..some alleged “anti-semitic” remarks, a law suit and a “contract dispute” later, means Paul E. Dangerously sailed off into the WCW sunset, before setting down anchor in Philadelphia and in Eastern Championship Wrestling. Heyman took the head booker role from Eddie Gilbert in late 1993 and he wasted no time in trying to step away from the old-school NWA feel that the company was employing at the time, instead deciding to partake in a very public screw-job…..

…..because following the finals of a tournament to crown a new NWA World Heavyweight Champion, winner Shane Douglas threw down his newly won title, calling the NWA a dead promotion, before raising the ECW Title and proclaiming himself the new EXTREME Championship Wrestling World Champion…..and so a revolution was born…..

…..but I digress…..you don’t want a history lesson, you want to know why I’m making the call…..

…..well here’s the thing, see Paul wasn’t happy with just being the head booker of ECW, he wanted ECW full stop…..so much so that he actually bought the promotion outright from then owner Todd Gordon back in 1995, and since then he’s done a pretty good job carving a little niche out of the wrestling world with his own brand of hardcore revolution that’s actually garnered somewhat of a cult following…..

…..I wanted to utilise some of that cult following…..

…..I explained to Paul who I was, but most importantly, what I wanted…..we (the WWF) had already helped ECW out a little bit by giving the Eliminators some mainstream exposure at the Survivor Series…..we’d even sent a few of our lesser known stars down there as well…..we wanted that to continue…..but in a more, extreme way if you will…..

“……wait, so you want what exactly? You want ECW to basically be your little training facility? Your talent farm?……you want EC F###ing W to become the ECW(WF) lite? With all due respect…..actually, scrap that…..with NO f###ing respect, are you out of your tiny little mind mind?…..”

…..like I said…..one of THOSE conversations…..

“…..we Sir, are ECW…..EXTREME Championship Wrestling…..we are changing the face of professional wrestling…..the outlaw promotion…..the revolution…..the red headed step child that no one wants, and doesn’t care……we made our name by NOT being the World Wrestling Federation…..by NOT being World Championship Wrestling…..by being the very OPPOSITE of the mainstream……and you want us to just abandon and abolish our principles?…..our ideals?…..our pride?……exactly WHY would we do that?…..”

“…..well…..for money obviously…..”

“………………………..”

“………………………..”

“…..how much money?…..”

“…..lots of money…..”

“………………………..”

“………………………..”

“…..ok, I’m listening…..”

“…..I thought you might…..”

…..he’d be stupid not too as well…..ECW are quite literally haemorrhaging money on a monthly basis…..last month alone they lost over 100 grand…..they’ve got roughly 400 grand in their bank account, which by my reckoning puts them in debt by May and quite possibly out of business by July/August…..the only way they’re going to stay afloat is by upping their income, and since most tv stations wont touch them, and most Pay Per View carriers would rather pay the public NOT to watch possible ECW events, that’s going to prove rather tricky…..

…..luckily I have a solution to that…..

“…..so you’re going to pay all my bills?…..”

“…..no, I’m going to pay all your monthly debts…..if you lose 100K next month, I’ll pay you 100K…..you lose 70 thousand, I’ll pay you 70 thousand…..you will not lose a penny during the course of this deal…..”

“…..but I won’t MAKE anything during the course of this deal either…..”

“…..short term, no…..long term? Long term you’re not losing any money…..you’re getting some bigger drawing names AND some of the hottest prospects in the sport…..bigger drawing names means bigger crowds, more exposure and eventually, better Television and Pay Per View revenue…..and as an added bonus, I can guarantee that neither the WWF…….or WCW…..will raid your talent without giving you ample warning to prepare……”

“…..well firstly, who says you COULD raid my ‘talent’, my guys are incredibly loyal…..and secondly, just how exactly are you planning on doing that? You and WCW are at war, they won’t think twice about trying to sign a Shane Douglas or a Taz before you do…..”

“……I could raid your talent in a heartbeat Paul…..do you really think Shane Douglas wouldn’t jump at the chance to get back in the big leagues? With his ego? Money talks Paul……if it didn’t then we wouldn’t even be having this conversation in the first place…..the only guys loyal to you are the guys I don’t even want……no offence to a Tommy Dreamer, or a Sandman, they work great for you in this environment, but in the WWF? Not a chance……and as for your second point, there is a War in professional wrestling you’re right…..and WCW do need bodies…..I hear Vince Russo got a nice Christmas Bonus from Turner in the form of a brand new show…..WCW Thunder…..that’s two more hours of television he’s got to fill…..but he won’t fill it with your guys…..because as Keyser Söze said, “the greatest trick the devil ever pulled, was convincing the world that he didn’t exist”……

…..the professional wrestling audience believe that they are witnessing and participating in (by choosing with their remote controls) a war…..WWF vs. WCW…..WWF fans vs. WCW fans…..Vince McMahon and Ted Turner ARE at war…..but in reality…..Vince Russo and I, we have an understanding…..”

“…..so this ‘war’ is a work?…..”

“…..no…..it’s very much a war…..to the people who matter…..and competition creates an environment that means EVERYONE has to up their game…..competition means more eyes on the product……people thought Turner was mad going head to head with Raw when he first started Nitro…..why split the audience right? When in reality, BOTH shows now have more viewers than ever before…..Raw’s ratings alone have doubled since I took the book…..imagine what would happen if we introduced a third ‘rogue’ element into the equation…..

…..so tell me Paul…..do you want to go to war?…..”

“…..I’m not going to stop bashing WCW and the WWF on my shows…..”

“…..I wouldn’t want you too…..I wouldn’t want it any other way…..”

“…..I’m not going to tone down what we do…..”

“…..Paul, you’re one of the most creative minds in the business…..why would I want to hold that back…..but imagine how creative you can be when you don’t have to worry about money…..”

“…..and no one is going to know about this?…..”

“…..not a soul…..”

“……………………..”

“……………………..”

“…..let’s go to war…..”

The week goes on…..

Posted 16 June 2019

”…..so let me get this straight…..I’m out here, week after week putting hours and hours worth of fricking high quality first run tv on…..so fricking quality in fact bro, that Ol’ Teddy has given me a whole other fricking show to put on……for free…..did I mention I was doing Thunder for fricking free bro?…..”

“…..you might have mentioned it in passing Vince, yeah……”

“…..for fricking free…..while this Paul Heyman guy just takes his live shows, chops them up in itty bitty pieces, says “f##k” here and there, calls WCW and the WWF pieces of sh!t all over the place, and HE’S the creative genius?…..”

“…..so the dirt sheets say Vince…..”

“…..well f##k him, how’s about that…..he’s not the only one that knows a few swears ya know bro…..HE’S the creative genius?…..HIM?…..has he ever tried to book a Shawn Michaels loss? Or convince Hulk Hogan that maybe laying down tonight would be the best thing? Because I’m telling you now bro, you have to be a pretty creative motherf###er to create your way around THAT sh!tstorm of a conversation…..”

“…..did I ever tell you how much I missed our little chats Vince?…..”

“…..yeah? F##k you too…..how’dya like them oranges?…..”

“…..apples…..”

“…..whatever bro…..do you know what I did after Nitro went off the air Monday Night? Did I fly home? Put my feet up? Kiss the kids goodnight and then spend some quality time between the sheets with [name withheld]?…..No…..no what I did, was go back to my hotel, get a pen and paper, tried to write a show that would USUALLY take me a week in just three days, drank half a bottle of scotch, jerked off and then cried myself to sleep under a paper blanket of fricking notes for Thursday’s Thunder…..actually, you know what, I take it back, that Paul Heyman, he IS a creative genius, because all HE had to do Monday, was pick a couple of matches from his Cybersex show…..”

“…..Cyberslam…..”

“…..spend 10 minutes editing them, and then send them off to the Network…..you’re right, he is a fricking creative genius…..”

…..it was a bit of a roundabout way of getting there, but actually Russo had a point…..it WAS kind of genius the way Paul sets up his shows…..run an event, film it, cut the matches, edit them to look as good as they possibly can, and presto, you have a show, no booking, no headaches, no last minute problems…..problem was, while it WAS kind of genius, it wasn’t without it’s problems either…..

…..first of all, ECW have zero pay per view coverage, NONE…..sure they want some…..hell, they need some if they want to ever make money, but come on, who’s going to pay to watch a show that you’re going to get for free over the course of three/four weeks on the tv anyway? Problem two, they’re stuck for what else to actually do…..they barely have a tv deal as it is…..they’re seen in Philly and in a smattering of homes in New York but that’s about it…..it doesn’t benefit them to run a tv show along the same format of Raw or Nitro…..for one it would cost them too much money, and for two, they don’t really have the man power for that…..Paul has a couple of camera men, gets the tape traders in to film “fan cam” shows on the condition that they distribute their stuff, and does most of the editing himself…..hell, he even has the boys to go out there in their own time to promote the shows…..he really is creating TV on a dime with very little effort…..

…..maybe he is a creative genius…..

”…..what can I tell you Vince, the man has a system…..problem is, while it IS a pretty creative system, he’s loosing a hundred grand a month, while you’re getting PAID a hundred grand a month…..”

“…..I’m not getting 100 large a month bro…..I SHOULD be, writing two fricking main shows, but I’m not…..”

“…..my point is, you’re making money hand over fist, he’s bleeding money like a sinking ship takes in water…..somethings got to change somewhere if he wants to stay in business…..”

“…..yeah well, speaking of hand over fist, I got some business of my own to take care of…..”

“…..don’t you have a show to finish writing?…..”

“…..I’ve finished that piece of sh!t…..now I’m gonna finish this bottle of scotch, rub one out and go to sleep…..[name withheld] ain’t gonna know what’s hit her when I get home I’m telling ya bro…..”

“…..please don’t tell me…..”

“…..she’s gonna be walking all bandy legged like one a them cowboys after a long ride down the trail, you can bet your limey ass on that…..”

“…..Vince, man…..c’mon…..”

“…..it’s gonna be like John Wayne just got off his horse up in Casà del Russo when I get home, home on the range bro…..”

…..:::::CLICK:::::…..

”…..motherf###er…..he hung up on me…..”

OOC:Since ECW have no real Pay Per View deals in place as such (they do, but only in their home region…..literally a tiny broadcaster) I’m not going to be writing up their monthly shows…..instead I’m going to be doing basically what ECW we’re doing anyway, which is cutting one event (in this case Cyberslam 98) up into matches that will air over the course of the month on the hour long Hardcore TV’s…..this also has the added bonus that I don’t actually have to plan a months worth of tv, because I already know the matches before hand…..

…..so with that being said, on with the show…..
