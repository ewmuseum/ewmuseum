WCW Monday Nitro: Week 1, November 1997

Posted 4 June 2019

Week 1, November 1997

Kansas Coliseum (Wichita, Kansas)

ATT: 11,496

TV: 19.25

Announce Team: Tony Schiavone, Bobby Heenan and Mike Tenay

We are just 24 hours removed from what Tony Schiavone is calling the most important night in all of wrestling history……we are just 24 hours removed from Halloween Havoc 97…..and as the announcers (and the still images that open the show) tell us, we are just 24 hours removed from the World Championship Wrestling debut……of Shawn Michaels……

…..with that being said, we don’t have to wait long to see the group that was there front and centre for that debut, because the Titans of Wrestling are making their way out, and much to Schiavone’s confusion (who clearly thinks that the appearance of Shawn Michaels should have these men looking more concerned than they do) they seem to be in a pretty damn good mood….and Vince Russo tells us why……

……he says that last night, a lot of people thought that they were going to see the beginning of the end of the Titans of Wrestling….they thought that Hollywood Hogan and Jean Paul Levesque were going to tear each other apart, tearing the Titans up with them as they went……well they were half right if nothing else, or a third at least…..because yes, Hollywood Hogan tore Levesque’s head clean off this shoulders, but the Titans…the Titans are standing here stronger than ever…….now yes, the arrival of Shawn Michaels was an unexpected turn of events, and an unexpected turn of events that the Titans weren’t happy about….BUT….after having a rather heated conversation with the face of the Championship Committee (Eric Bischoff) about why exactly the man who runs this place (Vincent Russo Esq) wasn’t informed about this new signing, Mr. Bischoff kindly informed Russo that it was because nobody signed Shawn Michaels…..Eric Bischoff didn’t sign Shawn Michaels…..WCW didn’t sign Shawn Michaels…..Shawn Michaels is NOT an employee of World Championship Wrestling……

“…..YET……….because you can consider this Shawn Michaels official invite to join the greatest group of men ever assembled…..the Titans of Wrestling…..”

….Russo says they might have started out on the wrong foot last night, but there is no doubt that Shawn Michaels is a TRUE Titan of Wrestling……seen it all, done it all, and a man the Vince Russo knows very, very well, because when Vince Russo was working for that little company up north, it was Vinny Ru himself that handed Shawn Michaels the ball to begin with, and now he wants to hand it to him all over again……

…..Hogan takes the microphone to let Michaels know that as far as he is concerned, he can’t think of a better man to join the Titans than Shawn Michaels, before passing the microphone around as every other Titan (with the exception of the mute Warrior) expresses the same sentiments….they’re really laying it on thick in an effort to get Shawn’s signature…..in fact, Hogan says that he would go so far as be willing to allow Jean Paul Levesque back into the Titans too if that would make Michaels happy…..

“…..and all he would have to do, is kiss my Hollywood boots, and we could put all this behind us brother……”

…..with the pitch made, and the Titans seemingly confident that Shawn Michaels will make the right choice, Russo moves on to other business…..he says that after a VERY successful Halloween Havoc for the Titans, which saw Randy Savage and The Warrior both walk out victorious….Adam Copeland and Christian Cage doing what the Nasty Boys couldn’t (bringing home the Tag Team Titles) and Hollywood doing what everyone knew he would (retaining the WCW Title)….it’s time to look forward to the NEXT big WCW event…..World War 3…..60 men….3 rings…..and a future WCW title opportunity waiting for the winner……except there’s just one problem with that……there aren’t 60 wrestlers in the world that are worthy of Wrestling for the opportunity to face Hollywood Hogan for the number one championship in all of professional wrestling……

“…..so basically, since I’m the boss……that ain’t happening…..”

….but when Russo takes away with one hand, he gives with the other…..he says that the World War 3 concept was a mess anyway…..so many bodies, so little space, no one could see what was happening and ANYONE could get a fluke win in a situation like that…..but there will be no fluke win at our November Pay Per View, but there will be…..Mayhem……

….because as WCW Mayhem, you’re going to see the Mayhem Match……ONE ring (not three) THIRTY superstars (not sixty) two men start, one man enters every two minutes, and the only way to eliminate your opponents, is to throw them over the top rope and down to the floor…..(sooooo, the Royal Rumble then right Vince?)….Russo says that to make things interesting, the winner WILL get a shot at the WCW Championship…….and they’ll get it at THE Pay Per View of the year…..the granddaddy of them all…..Starrcade…..as a promo video airs for the latest event on the calendar…..WCW Mayhem….

Rating: A*

VS.

Bill Goldberg vs. Earl Robert Eaton


….following which we have our first match of the evening, and it’s rookie vs veteran when the undefeated Bill Goldberg goes one on one with Earl Robert Eaton…..

….Eaton may be better known as one half of numerous successful tag teams, but he’s no slouch when it comes to singles action, capturing the highly coveted Television Title back in early 1991, and even taking the Nature Boy Ric Flair to the limit while holding that very Title, narrowly missing out on capturing his first World Title when Flair defeated him two falls to one in a best of three falls match at the Clash of the Champions XV……

…..that being said, Bill Goldberg is no normal wrestler….and after fighting his way through both Meng AND the Barbarian last night, The Earl doesn’t fare much better here tonight, as Goldberg takes his undefeated streak to 39-0 after a jarring Spear and Jackhammer combination…..

Winner: Bill Goldberg
Rating: B-

….they air the (few) highlights of the match (it wasn’t exactly a long one) and when we go back to the live show Lance Storm is making his way out, presumably for his upcoming title defence…..unfortunately Mr. Goldberg has yet to make his way to the back, and the two men cross paths in the aisleway….

….Storm (rather arrogantly) tells Goldberg to “step aside rookie, show some respect and let a REAL wrestler past” before dismissing the older and larger (yet less experienced) Goldberg with a wave of his hand….

…..obviously Billy Boy doesn’t take to kindly to that….and after taking a good long look at the title belt around Lance Storms waist, he shoves the Champion to one side (almost knocking him on his ass) and marches to the back…..Lance Storm might want to be more careful about how he speaks to certain individuals around here…..unless he’s looking to become another statistic in Bill Goldberg’s winning streak.

Rating: B+

VS.

WCW Television Championship Match

Lance Storm (c) vs. Brad Armstrong


Brad Armstrong has some pretty sweet entrance music…..and up until about two months ago, was getting featured on WCW television more than he had since probably his brief run as Light Heavyweight Champion back in 92….of course, that was before World Championship Wrestling got a little bit of a boost in the Midcard thanks to the new arrivals from up north, since then Brad hasn’t featured quite so much…..he’s still a good hand to have around when you need a solid match from a dependable opponent though, and Mr. Dependable did his job and THE job here, as Lance Storm picks up another win with the Canadian Maple Leaf.

Winner: ….and Still WCW Television Champion….Lance Storm
Rating: B

From there we go to the back, where Mean Gene is standing by with the man who defeated United States Champion Eddie Guerrero last night, and yet is NOT standing here with the US Title, because his opponent got himself intentionally disqualified as Hennig was literally one second away from capturing the gold….

….Hennig say that this is becoming a trend for him here in WCW….he SHOULD be standing here with the WCW Championship after defeating Hollywood Hogan, just as he SHOULD be standing here as US Champion after defeating Eddie Guerrero…..but he’s not…..because Eddie Guerrero knows, just like Hollywood Hogan knows…..

“…..that nobody beats Curt Hennig……NOBODY……”

“……hey holmes, I didn’t NEED to beat you esè…..I just needed to walk out as STILL the greatest United States Champion of all time…..”

…..that brings in the “Papi of Perfection” Eddie Guerrero, walking into shot with a sh!t eating grin on his face and the US title over his shoulder…..he pats the gold before smirking at Henning, saying that since this title is still over his shoulder, then obviously his plan worked…..

…..Hennig asks how Guerrero can call himself the greatest US Champion of all time, when he couldn’t defeat Curt last night? Eddie says that’s the champions prerogative holmes, Eddie doesn’t have to beat Curt, he just has to make sure he’s not pinned and doesn’t submit…..

“…..and esè, that’s exactly what I did…..”

…..Curt tells Guerrero that if he’s got any guts he would give Hennig another shot at the gold, but Eddie shakes his head, telling Curt that he had his shot…..and he blew it…..before turning to walk away…….

….and walks right into Eric Bischoff…..Bischoff tells Eddie that ACTUALLY, as the public face of the Championship committee, he’s going to make sure that Curt Henning DOES get another shot at that US title…..and he’s going to get it next week……live, on Monday Nitro…..

Rating: A

…..Guerrero and Hennig both walk out of shot, one far happier than the other, but Bischoff sticks around…..Gene asks him if Russo’s claims are true? That Shawn Michaels is NOT a member of the WCW roster? Bischoff says that unfortunately, as of right now, Shawn Michaels is indeed a free agent, but he’s hopeful that a deal can be reached which sees Mr. Michaels signature on the bottom of a contract signed by Eric Bischoff….NOT, Vince Russo….

…..speaking of Vince Russo, he announced earlier in the evening that the World War 3 event would be being replaced by a brand new Pay Per View, WCW Mayhem, in which the winner of that match would be in receipt of a shot at the World title at Starrcade…..the Championship Committee have no problem with that, in fact it makes sense that the challenger for the WCW Title at the biggest (and most important) show of the year has to earn it, and if you can defeat 29 other wrestlers you will certainly have proved that you deserve it…..BUT….with that being said, Mr. Russo neglected to mention who would be challenging for the WCW Title AT Mayhem…..after all, Hollywood Hogan doesn’t just get the night off…..

….so since the man who challenges for the gold at Starrcade will have to earn it, then the man to challenge at Mayhem will have to earn it too……and that man, will be the winner of tonight’s main event, which sees Lex Luger and The Giant (two men who’s match last night had no decisive winner) along with Diamond Dallas Page and Chris Benoit (two men that Bischoff says were robbed of victory last night) face off in a four way match, for a shot at Hollywood Hogan and the WCW Championship.

Rating: B

VS.

WCW World Tag Team Championship Match

Adam Copeland & Christian Cage (c) (w/Ray Traylor) vs. The Soul Train (w/Teddy Long)


The Totally Awesome Trinity make their way out next, as the Titans of Wrestling’s Adam Copeland and Christian Cage (along with The Enforcer, Ray Traylor) put their newly won Tag Team Titles on the line against The Soul Train (who have fat Teddy Long in their corner)….Copeland and Cage defeated four absolute behemoths in Road Warriors Hawk and Animal, Mike Awesome and Gideon Cain last night to capture these tag titles…..they’re not about to lose them again in less than 24 hours (little dig at Al Snow and Chris Candido’s short reign from the commentary team there) especially against a tag team that are rarely featured on Monday Nitro in Ernest Miller and Ice Train…….the Titans win, after a Downward Spiral from Christian to Miller.

Winner(s): ….and Still WCW Tag Team Champions….Adam Copeland and Christian Cage
Rating: B

Mean Gene makes his way out for a word with our new champions…..and Copeland and Cage do his job for him, totally shilling the Hotline…..call now (1-900-909-9900) to hear all the Totally Awesome news on the Totally Awesome Trinity…..wanna know Adam Copeland’s hair grooming tips? What’s The Enforcers favourite rom-com? (FYI, it’s Sleepless in Seattle) Call now, and key in option 3……

…..Gene thanks them for the plug, before asking them what happens now…..they did what the Titans hired them for, they won the WCW Tag Team Titles…..but with those belts they also gained a huge target on their backs…..there are going to be a slew of teams looking to take them out……Copeland disagrees, asking who are they supposed to be worried about?…..The Road Warriors? Awesome and Cain? (“we totally beat them both last night at the same time”) these two shlubs The Soul Train? (“you want soul Gene, you should totally hear the Enforcer singing in the showers…..he makes Marvin Gaye sound like Marvin No-Way”) the Pitbulls? The Samoan SWAT Team? (“we’ll just throw them a chicken bone and watch them fight it out over the scraps”)……

…..Copeland says that no one is taking these titles, because the Totally Awesome Trinity won’t let them…..now if he will excuse them, they’ve gotta go to the back and get ready to welcome the NEWEST Titan to the group, Shawn Michaels….after all…..he only has till the end of the show to make the right decision.

Rating: A

VS.

Super Crazy vs. Yoshihiro Tajiri (w/James Vandenberg)


Yoshihiro Tajiri had quite the evening last night, turning on the man who has been there on numerous occasions for him as of late (Jeff Hardy) to align himself with the men that have been trying to put him down (Vandenberg and Mortis)…..even so, it’s an unusual sight to see this clean cut superstar walking out with the Sinister James Vandenberg by his side…..be it the turn or the fact that he now has a manager in his ear, Tajiri is able to use this new found momentum to pick up an impressive victory over Super Crazy, in what was a decent little match….

Winner: Yoshihiro Tajiri
Rating: C

….the fun doesn’t end there though, and as Vandenberg joins his newest client in the ring, Mortis makes his way down the aisle, collecting a microphone from ringside which he presents to his manager….

….Vandenberg says that while the cleansing and purification of WCW continues, the cleansing of Yoshihiro Tajiri is complete, as he now marches to the beat of James Vandenberg’s drum….unlike Jeff Hardy, Tajiri knows better than to fight a losing battle, and this right here (indicating to himself, Mortis and Tajiri) is a losing battle for anyone stupid enough to take the challenge………and anyone not quick enough to get out of our way…..

….at that, Vandenberg indicates towards Super Crazy, who is still down on the canvas…..and Mortis doesn’t need asking twice, as he swoops on the fallen Mexican star, delivering an almighty beating that he punctuates with the Flatliner……

…..which finally bought out the man that’s been fighting that losing battle for months now…..Jeff Hardy…..so far Jeff has been unsuccessful in two attempts to defeat the Masked Mortis in one on one competition….although neither win has been clean…..but this latest loss must have left the sourest of tastes in his mouth (and sting in his eyes) after Tajiri, a man he thought to be an ally sprayed a green mist like substance in his face…..

…..Hardy is certainly fired up….and he uses that adrenaline to take the fight to both Tajiri AND Mortis, finally whipping both men into the same turnbuckle, and crushing two birds with one stone as he connects with a one man Poetry in Motion to both of Vandenberg’s wards……

…..unfortunately, adrenaline was only going to get Jeff so far, and eventually he takes his eye off the game…..or off Tajiri at least…..who sneaks up behind Hardy as the youngster is working over Mortis….spins him around……

……and sprays that green mist right in his face again…….

…..Hardy drops to the mat clutching at his eyes once more, as Vandenberg cackles that if Hardy won’t see the light, then maybe he should be forced to live in the dark……and it looks as though the beating is about to continue……

…..at least it does until these three men come walking out from the back…..

…..apparently this “losing battle” doesn’t include The Forgotten…..because as Sullivan, Bigelow and Vader make their way out, Vandenberg, Mortis and Tajiri (wisely) beat a hasty retreat….Jeff Hardy has a match with Big Van Vader up next, and while he is in no condition to defend himself, it doesn’t look as though the Forgotten care at all…..

Rating: C+

VS.

Jeff Hardy vs. Big Van Vader


Fighting a man like Big Van Vader for any normal man is a task in itself…….fighting a man like Big Van Vader when you’re a twenty year old, 216lb, half blind man? That’s just suicide…..and Vader knows it, as he climbs into the ring, bully’s the official into starting the match, and proceeds to give Jeff Hardy the biggest beating of his young life……

…..Hardy’s ONLY way into this match would have perhaps been his speed, ability, and devil may care attitude, but for any of that to be effective he would have to be able to see Vader to begin with, and since the majority of his face is still green from the mist of Tajiri, that’s probably not happening any time soon…..

….Jeff does try…..he does……he even manages to land a few blind swings that gets the girls squealing…..it barely phases Vader however….and mercifully for Jeff Hardy, the monster finally puts him down with the huge Powerbomb……

Winner: Big Van Vader
Rating: B-

…..although knowing the track record of the Forgotten, it’s probably not going to end there…..Jeff Hardy might want to decide who he is going to focus his attention on….is he settling scores with Mortis, Vandenberg and Tajiri…..or gaining revenge on Sullivan, Bigelow and Vader…..because right now he’s spreading himself too thin, and after already suffering at the hands of James Vandenberg’s Pariah’s of the Purged, now it looks as though the Forgotten would like to finish the job that they started a few weeks back, because it looks as though Kevin Sullivan and Bam Bam Bigelow are about to join Vader in the ring…….

……at least until the Rock comes flying down the aisle, steel chair in hand…..and he absolutely nails Bigelow in the back with the weapon as he’s climbing onto the apron…….

……Sullivan turns……and turns right into a chair shot between the eyes…..and he goes down……

……that just leaves Vader……

……Rock slides into the ring and swings the steel once more, crashing the chair across the back of the big man…..and despite the huge red welt that instantly appears, Vader barely budges……Rock tries again, much to the same effect, before deciding this time to swing for the head…..except Vader punches the chair right out of the Rock’s hands…and then proceeds to hammer the new arrival all the way back to the turnbuckles with swinging blows……

…..Vader unloads in the corner with blow after blow, before whipping Rock across the ring to the opposite corner, before coming charging in with a running splash…..

…..which The Rock avoids……and as Vader staggers back into the middle of the ring dazed, Rock picks him up and drives him down with a spinebuster right onto that steel chair…..

…..Rock walks around the big man and kicks his arm out of the way…..and Schiavone knows what’s coming, telling us that it looks like the Rock is going for that People’s Elbow on Big Van Vader……and as Rock runs the ropes it looks as though Tony may be right…..but before he can land the most electrifying move in ALL of sports entertainment, Bigelow and Sullivan both reach under the bottom rope and drag Vader to safety…..

…..the trio back slowly up the aisle as the Rock picks the steel chair up off the mat…..well…..Sullivan and Bigelow back slowly up the ramp anyway, while dragging an irate Vader along for the ride…..

…..seeing that the imminent danger has departed the Rock tosses down the chair, helps Hardy back to his feet, and grabs a microphone……he tells Vader that his fat monkey ass can only avoid the People’s Elbow for so long, but if the Rock isn’t going to be able to give the people what they want THIS week, then maybe he’ll have to give the people what they want NEXT week……..The Rock says that he wrestled on Pay Per View, he WON on Pay Per View, but now it’s time to make THE most electrifying debut in Nitro history……when Jeff Hardy teams up with the Rock (in his first televised match of his WCW career) to face Pebbles and Bam Bam, Sullivan and Bam Bam, Pebbles and Sullivan, it doesn’t really matter which two of those Forgotten Jabroni’s it is……because what the Rock guaran-damn-tees, is that he’s gonna walk the people’s aisle, climb into the people’s ring…..and wipe a monkeys ass with the face of WHOEVER is across the ring from him………

“……….IF YA SMELLLLLLLLLLLALALAOW……..what The Rock….is…..cooking……….”

Rating: A*

Next up we have some pre-recorded comments from not only the four competitors in tonight’s main event, but a series of other members of the roster too….Giant, Luger, Page and Benoit each briefly discuss tonight’s main event, with each man claiming that they are going to be the one to walk into Mayhem to face Hollywood Hogan……Page and Benoit both discuss being screwed out of victory last night, and the fact that the Titans of Wrestling clearly know that they can’t defeat them fairly…..Luger says that he could have claimed victory last night, but didn’t want to defeat his friend with the help of his OTHER friend, but tonight he’s going to win all by himself…..while the Giant…..well, the Giant is p!ssed…..he says that he had Luger beat last night….he was going to Chokeslam Lex through the mat, and then he was going to move on to Hollywood Hogan and the WCW Title…..well that didn’t happen……so tonight, The Giant is going to Chokeslam THREE guys through the mat……and if Sting sticks his stupid white painted face in the Giant’s business again, Giant will pop his head like a grape……

…..with the main event sufficiently hyped, a number of other (slightly lesser) roster members, both face and heel alike offering virtually the same plea…..Rikishi, Rick Martel, Brad Armstrong, Disco Inferno, Alex Wright, etc, etc, etc……telling Shawn Michaels to fight the good fight for World Championship Wrestling…..NOT for the Titans of Wrestling……Luger says the same thing…..as does Page…..as does Benoit……….Giant doesn’t…….in fact Giant says that he doesn’t give a damn what Shawn Michaels, Jean Paul Levesque, Lex Luger, or anyone else does…….all he cares about, is at SOME point (if he can find his way out of the rafters) getting his hands on Sting, getting his hands on Hollywood Hogan……and getting the WCW Championship around his waist where it belongs…….it seems that while four pairs of eyes are on the main event, ALL eyes are on what will happen AFTER the main event……and whether Shawn Michaels will respond to the Titans of Wrestling…….and more importantly, what that response will be.

Rating: A

VS. VS. VS.

WCW Championship #1 Contenders: Fatal Four Way Match

The Giant vs. Lex Luger vs. Dallas Page vs. Chris Benoit

So here is that main event……it’s the Giant vs. Lex Luger vs. Diamond Dallas Page vs. Chris Benoit, and the winner will be challenging Hollywood Hogan for the WCW Championship at this months brand new event, Mayhem……

…..not one of these men won last night….hardly prime candidates for number one contederships then right?….well yes….and no…..because while none of these men were victorious, each one had a valid reason for their loss, and truthfully, any one of them COULD have won last night……well one WILL win tonight…..the question is who?…..

…..well we find out who it WON’T be winning about 3/4’s of the way through the match….when Lex Luger and The Giant’s friendship finally goes completely off the rails….because after Giant constantly rags on Lex for having Sting do his dirty work for him last night, Luger snaps and the two men go at it full bawl, to the point that they eventually take themselves out of the equation by fighting all the way to the back…..

……that just leaves Page and Benoit, and it’s time to find out who wants it more, the guy that never had a shot (in DDP) or the guy that had a shot and got screwed out of his opportunity (that’ll be Benoit)…….

……turns out the answer to that (tonight at least) is Diamond Dallas Page….because as Benoit tackles the arm in an attempt to take Page down to the mat for the Crossface, Dallas is able to carry the momentum around and drop Benoit with a Diamond Cutter right in the middle of the ring for the one, the two, and the three……AND the number one contendership for the WCW Championship…..

Winner: Diamond Dallas Page
Rating: B+

…..Dallas helps Benoit back to his feet, and despite the obvious disappointment on his face The Crippler is at least man enough to congratulate the victor, before offering his hand, raising Page’s arm, and telling him to get it done for WCW…….

……of course, that’s if DDP even makes it to Mayhem…..because just to cut that celebration short, here come the Titans of Wrestling walking down the aisle, and Hollywood has a microphone in his hand…..

…..as the group make their way towards the ring Hogan scoffs at Page’s victory….he says that if this is really the best that WCW has to offer, it’s no surprise that Shawn Michaels is about five minutes away from joining the most dominant group in all of wrestling, the Titans of Wrestling….but while they are waiting for Mr. Michaels to make his (no doubt) grand entrance, there’s nothing stopping the Titans from having a little fun at these two jobbers expense…..and as Hogan and his crew surround the ring, it looks like that’s exactly what is about to happen……

……or not…..

…..because halt the locker room (again, face and heels alike) come rushing out from the back and they charge into the ring, surrounding Benoit and Page in a rare showing of solidarity…..

……the Titans aren’t happy about this, not at all……but it seems as though there are a lot of people waiting to hear what Shawn Michaels decision is going to be……..

……and we might not have to wait long to hear it…..because here comes exiled Titan Jean Paul Levesque, and he looks a lot happier than he has in recent weeks…..

……he too enters the ring, and he first of all offers his congratulations to Diamond Dallas Page (who rather suspiciously and somewhat reluctantly accepts) before turning to assess the scene before him, saying there sure is a whole lot of people out here interested in what little old Shawn Michaels has to say……so…….without further ado…….

“…..I give you the man…..the myth……the icon……and the TRUE reason you’re all here right now……..my best friend……..Shawn Michaels…….”

…..so here he is…..the man we’ve all been waiting for…….and if you want to know how badly both WCW and the Titans of Wrestling want Shawn Michaels signature, then look no further than the fact that not only does a man employed by nobody get to walk down that aisle, but he gets pyro……he gets a name graphic…..and more importantly, EVERYONE, WCW and Titan alike stands back and watches as the former World Wrestling Federation Champion picks up a microphone and climbs into the ring…….

…..which is when a camera catches sight of a lone man high above the ring in the rafters of the building……

…..even Sting has come out of hibernation to see what Shawn Michaels has to say……

….so what DOES Shawn Michaels have to say?……

……he begins by saying that he’s been sat at home for the last two months….sat at home and watched this man right here, Jean Paul Levesque….“wait, seriously…..that’s the best name they could give you?”…….Shawn shakes his head, saying that’s GOT to go……

“…..I’ve been sat at home for the last few months, and in those two months I’ve watched this man right here…..JPL get treated like a joke…..an afterthought by the establishment……and just another name to hold down by the ‘good old boys’……”

….Shawn says that he sat, he watched…..and quite frankly he’s seen enough…..he says that in this business there’s ONE man who’s never turned his back on him…..ONE man who never tried to make a living of the Shawn Michaels name…..and ONE man that he would go to war with the devil himself for…..

…..he says that he wants to make one thing perfectly clear…..as far as Shawn Michaels is concerned, he doesn’t take kindly to ultimatums, and he CERTAINLY doesn’t like being told where to be, and when to be there……

“…..I’m out here tonight for one reason, and one reason only……and that’s to let everyone know that I’m not here to sign with WCW…….I’m not here to sign with the Titans of Wrestling…..I’m not here for you-

…..Michaels points to each man inside the ring, and each Titan on the outside, one at a time….

-or you, you, you, or you…….I’m not here for that Giant in the back, I’m not here for you Vinnie Ru….I’m not here for the face painted eye in the sky…..I’m not even here for you people in the crowd or the people watching at home…….”

…..Shawn says that he’s here for JPL, and he’s going to make sure that EVERYONE knows who the REAL Titans of Wrestling are around here…..

…..with that Michaels tosses down the microphone and he and Levesque climb out of the ring and disappear to the back, leaving everyone in the arena with just as many questions as they had before the pair’s arrival, as the wrestlers in the ring (and at ringside) stand there and watch them leave, with Tony Schiavone closing the show with a simple statement…..and that statement is that while Shawn Michaels may not have aligned himself with World Championship Wrestling, he hasn’t aligned himself with the Titans of Wrestling either………..yet……

…..see you next week fans……

Rating: A*

Final Rating: B+

HUGE MATCHES ANNOUNCED FOR WCW MONDAY NITRO!

Fresh off this weeks monumental WCW Monday Nitro, Vince Russo has wasted no time in setting the groundwork for the upcoming edition, set to air LIVE this Monday night on TNT. Following the apparent rebuttal by the hottest free agent in wrestling today, when Shawn Michaels declined the offers of both World Championship Wrestling AND the Titans of Wrestling to join their ranks, Mr. Russo was anything but impressed, and since Shawn Michaels is not under the employ of WCW, and as such untouchable by the Titan Head Honcho, he’s decided to take his frustrations out on those people who’s careers he CAN effect.

In an evening being dubbed the “Titan’s Revenge”, Vince Russo’s first order of business is to make a match for Shawn Michaels best friend, Jean Paul Levesque (or JPL if you ask Michaels) who will be facing off against one of the Forgotten’s monsters. Which monster exactly? Time will tell, because Kevin Sullivan, Bam Bam Bigelow and Big Van Vader will ALL be in action Monday night. One un-named member will be facing off against Mr. Levesque, will the other two gentlemen will be joining forces to face off against The Rock and Jeff Hardy (the recap and reasoning of how this tag team match came about, complete with still images can be found here)

Not to be outdone, The Warrior will be making a rare in ring appearance when he goes up against the big Samoan, Rikishi Fatu, while the “Totally Awesome Trinity” of Adam Copeland, Christian Cage and Ray Traylor join forces to face the unusual combination of Fit Finlay, Juventud Guerrera & the Disco Inferno.

…..Russo agree’s….telling Giant to squash Luger tonight…..destroy Luger at Mayhem……and the number thirty spot is his…..IF he gets the job done…….

…..which brings out Eric Bischoff…..who says that as the face of the WCW Championship Committee, he takes this Mayhem match very seriously…..after all, the winner will be challenging for the WCW Title at the biggest event of the year, Starrcade…..so no….Russo won’t be just handing out number thirty spots…..

…..Bischoff says that while the Championship Committee wouldn’t have a problem with an athlete the calibre of the Giant winning this match, it’s not going to be handed to him on a silver platter……so how about this…..The Giant vs. Lex Luger…..one more time at Mayhem…..and the winner will be GUARANTEED an advantageous number…..let’s say, any number between 25 and 30…….BUT….on the flip side……the LOSER of the match, well he’s going to have his work cut out for him……because he’s going to be getting a very disadvantageous number…..anywhere from number 1 to number 5……..BUT…….should there be NO winner (much like with their Halloween Havoc encounter) then Lex Luger and The Giant will enter at numbers 1 and 2 respectively…..so good luck.

Rating: A

We come back from a quick commercial break, and we are treated to the arrival of the WCW Television Champion Lance Storm……

…..Storm isn’t heading down to ringside however….instead he veers off to the right and takes up position at the commentary table ahead of our next match, because he has a vested interest in one of the competitors…..

….he spends a few minutes running down the man he will be facing at Mayhem for HIS Television Championship, before telling Bill Goldberg that Lance Storm is not “Next”…..Lance Storm is LAST……the last man that Bill Goldberg will be facing as an undefeated rookie that is….because at Mayhem, he’s going to wrap Goldberg up in the Canadian Maple Leaf….he’s going to make Goldberg scream like a Girl Scout……and he IS going to defeat the undefeatable, regardless of what people think……why?……because he’s from Calgary……Alberta, Canada……and that simply means that he’s better than unbeatable……

“…..and Bill Goldberg…..that means that it is YOU who is “Next”…..”

Rating: B

VS.

Bill Goldberg vs. Hacksaw Jim Duggan


What a peculiar match…..but that’s the problem when you develop a decent winning streak, heel, face, rookie, veteran, EVERYBODY wants to be the one to claim that scalp for themselves…..which might explain why Jim Duggan asked for this match……I hate Jim Duggan…..which might explain why he ate a Spear and Jackhammer in quick concession……your winner…..the man that picked up wins 46 and 47 in our b-shows through the week…..Bill Goldberg…..

Winner: Bill Goldberg
Rating: C+

…..that’s win number fourty eight in the bag, and with win number fourty nine potentially coming midweek ahead of our Mayhem event, Bill Goldberg may well be looking to break TWO new landmarks this Sunday night…..picking up win number 50…..and capturing his first taste of championship gold……

……of course, the current champion might have something to say on the matter there…..because as Goldberg celebrates yet another victory, Lance Storm quickly leaves the commentary table and runs down the aisle, sliding into the ring behind Goldberg and crashing that solid gold Television Title belt right into the back of the Challengers knee….

……Goldberg might appear unstoppable, but take his legs away and he can’t Spear you…..he can’t Jackhammer you from down on the mat…..and he certainly can’t beat you from down on the mat…..and as Storm drives the Championship two, three, four more times into the knee, the commentators question whether Bill Goldberg will be even fit to compete at Mayhem, let alone in any condition to win……because while it seems that nothing can keep this rookie down (he’s back on his feet and staring a hole through Storm as he retreats) the man is certainly hurt, because try as he might to disguise it, there’s no hiding the fact that Mr. Goldberg is carrying a slight limp

Rating: B+

VS.

The Rock vs. Konnan


Last week, The Rock cut a promo stating that he was going to be heading to WCW Mayhem to stake his place in the Starrcade 97 main event……he referenced names like The Warrior and Eddie Guerrero as potential “opponents he’s never faced”, and in that same vein Vince Russo has set this match up tonight….The Rock against an opponent he’s never faced…..Konnan….

…..that being said, Konnan was never going to win, especially after he p!ssed me off a few weeks back…..this was your pretty standard match with The Rock getting all his shtick in, before finishing K-Dog off with the Rock Bottom.

Winner: The Rock
Rating: B-

Mean Gene makes his way out from the back as The Rock celebrates another victory here in WCW….he goes into shill mode trying to plug the WCW Hotline but The Rock cuts him off, telling him that “The Rock is a busy man Gene, so unless you hurry up and wrap this up, option one on the Hotline is going to be all about how The Rock stuck his boot straight up your candy ass”…..

…..Gene says that with just six days until WCW Mayhem, picking up a big victory here tonight must have given the Rock quite the confidence boost ahead of the actual Mayhem Match? The Rock raises an eyebrow at this…..asking Gene if The Rock looks like someone that’s lacked confidence a day in his life?…..The Rock says he was born confident…..in fact, The Rock was born so confident, that after he was delivered, he cut his own cord, slapped the nurse on the ass, slipped her ten bucks, and sent her down the street to get him a steak, because little baby Rock was hungry……

“…..and The Rock is hungry now Gene…..he’s hungry to prove to the world that he’s exactly what he says he is…..he’s hungry for gold…..and the Rock is going to Mayhem….the Rock is in the Mayhem Match…..but the Rock isn’t going to Mayhem to slap no nurses ass, no, no, no, no….The Rock is going to Mayhem to lay the smack down ON twenty nine candy asses…..The Rock is going to Mayhem to win….to walk into Starrcade…..to prove that he IS the great one…..and the Rock WILL be walking out of Starrcade as the WCW Champion…….

………..IF YA SMELLLLLLLLLLLALALAOW……..what The Rock….is…..cooking……….”

Rating: A*

VS.

Jean Paul Levesque, Diamond Dallas Page & Chris Benoit vs. The Totally Awesome Trinity


So that about brings us to our main event…..and it’s a main event that sees Diamond Dallas Page and Chris Benoit team up with their one time nemesis Jean Paul Levesque, to face off against the Titans Totally Awesome Trinity of Adam Copeland, Christian Cage, and The Enforcer Ray Traylor……

….there’s plenty of action inside the ring for this one, but there’s a fair bit on the outside too….because while Buff Bagwell has accompanied the Titan Trio out, JPL’s new “manager” Shawn Michaels is there on the floor too, and he and Buff spend a good portion of the match jaw jacking about Bagwell’s upcoming clash with JPL at Mayhem, and they almost come to blows on a number of occasions…..

…..inside the ring the action is pretty good…..Copeland and Cage have come on leaps and bounds since arriving here in WCW, and their surprisingly entertaining partnership with The Enforcer sees Ray Traylor main eventing Nitro in late 1997 (something I never saw happening)…..

…..of course, this was never going to end cleanly….not with the Titans involved…..because with Levesque and Benoit busy on the outside with Copeland and Traylor, DDP drops Cage with a Diamond Cutter…..before the official can register the three count however, Bagwell dives into the ring and breaks up the pinfall, leaving the referee no choice but to call for the bell…….

Winner(s): Jean Paul Levesque, Dallas Page & Chris Benoit (via disqualification)
Rating: B+

……but the fun doesn’t stop there…..because Hollywood Hogan, the Warrior, and Randy Savage make their way down the aisle, and it looks as though Diamond Dallas Page is going to find out first hand exactly what’s waiting for him on Sunday night…..

…..Benoit rolls into the ring as the Titans proceed to beat the snot out of Page, and the two men do rally back briefly….the odds are most definitely not in their favour however, and the pair soon find themselves overwhelmed…..

……on the outside, Shawn Michaels grabs Levesque by the arm and tries to lead him to the back, telling him that this is WCW’s problem, not theirs…..Levesque shakes his arm free however, loudly telling Michaels that JPL IS part of WCW, before rushing towards the ring….

…..right along with Lex Luger a few seconds later…..as the Total Package comes running down the aisle……

…..that evens the odds slightly…..and when Levesque gets briefly double teamed by Buff Bagwell and Randy Savage, Shawn Michaels FINALLY decides to interject himself into the action (after all, he IS only here to help out Levesque) and he, JPL, Savage and Bagwell wind up brawling all the way to the back, just as the WCW contingent inside the ring begin to get the upper hand……

…..Levesque and Michaels might have taken two Titans out of the equation, and Benoit, Luger and Page might (briefly) have the upper hand, but it’s still five Titans against three….and two of those five are Hollywood Hogan and The Warrior……

…….and things only go from bad to worse….when the 7’4″ Giant makes his way out from the back……

…….the big man climbs over the top rope, and makes a beeline straight for Lex Luger, blindsiding the Total Package with a huge head butt to the back of the skull…..

……Luger goes down…..the Totally Awesome Trinity beat Chris Benoit into the mat…..and Diamond Dallas Page is left to the mercy of Hollywood Hogan and The Warrior….not that there is much mercy to be seen, because as The Warrior holds Page’s hands behind his back, Hogan wraps his weightlifting belt around his fist and proceeds to hammer DDP right between the eyes with the thick leather strap……

…..which is when the cameras finally catch glimpse of Sting…..watching helplessly from high up in the rafters…..

…..Giant spots Sting watching from above, and points up at the man as he wraps his hand around the throat of Lex Luger, lifting the Total Package up, before Choke-slamming him hard into the mat…..

……Benoit and Page have been completely dismantled, and the Titans shove them to one side, clearing some space for the Giant to deliver a second Chokeslam to Luger…..and then burst out in laughter as the Total Package receives a third……the Titans of Wrestling (and The Giant) are destroying the WCW contingent here……

…….Giant stands tall, throwing insults at the eye in the sky, as Hollywood decides to take advantage of the fallen Luger, delivering a number of lashes from that weight lifting belt….not content with a simple whipping however, Hogan calls the rest of the Titans over and all five men begin kicking and stomping at the fallen Luger in unison…….

……and Sting has seen enough…..

……rappels down from the rafters and lands in the aisle-way…..

……pulling a baseball bat from under his coat as he walks towards the ring……

……the announcers go crazy….stating that Sting has been specifically banned from interfering in Titan business, and even had his job threatened…..but it seems like a vicious gang style beat down to his long time friend is too much for even the Stinger to sit back and watch…..

……Hogan sends the Trinity out to meet him, but Sting (and his baseball bat) neutralises each man in turn…..and as the Stinger climbs into the ring (swinging wildly as he goes), Hogan and The Warrior have seen enough, bailing to the outside and retreating up the aisle……

……bat or no bat, The Giant doesn’t look as though he’s planning on backing down, but as Benoit and Page regain their senses, and Luger gingerly pulls himself back to his feet, the seven footer decides that even HE doesn’t like those odds…..and he climbs back over the top rope and drops to the outside, following the actions of the Titans of Wrestling by ALSO retreating up the aisle……

……Page is up…..Benoit is up…..Luger is (mostly) up…….and Sting is standing square in the middle of the ring…..arm raised…..baseball bat pointing towards Hollywood……

….as Vince Russo makes it out onto the main entrance stage…..and he has a microphone in hand……

…..Russo says that he knows that these Charleston fans are clearly not the most intelligent of people…..but even THEY heard Russo’s ground rules earlier this evening…..

“……Sting…..you stupid son of a b!tch…..you had three choices…..get back in this ring and wrestle…..stay up in the rafters and watch…….or come swooping down from above, stick your nose in Titan business……and be released for breach of contract……

…..and guess which one you took……

…..ladies and gentlemen, take a good long look at your hero…..take a good long look at your enigma……and take a good long look at the career of Sting, disappearing down the crapper…..I can categorically guarantee…..that this will be the very last time that you see Sting inside a WCW ring……

…….because Sting…….

……YOU’RE FIREDDDDD!……”

Rating: A*

Final Rating: B+
