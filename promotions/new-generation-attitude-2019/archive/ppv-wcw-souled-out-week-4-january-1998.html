A New Generation…..a new attitude? Pay-per-view

WCW Souled Out: Week 4, January 1998

Week 1, January 1998

Greensboro Coliseum (Greensboro, North Carolina)

ATT: 30,000

PPV BUY: 1.96

Announce Team: Tony Schiavone, Dusty Rhodes & Bobby “The Brain” Heenan

This may not be the first Pay Per View in the year of 1998, but it is the first WCW Pay Per View in the year of 1998, it’s the ONLY Pay Per View you need to watch in the month of January…..and you can take it from me folks……World Championship Wrestling…..is Souled Out…..

…..the show opens at the announce position, where Tony Schiavone welcomes us to a historic event…..

[i]”…..because fans, with Sting defeating Hollywood Hogan last month at Starrcade, bringing the WCW Championship back where it belongs in the process, this event tonight marks the first Pay Per View in over twelve months NOT to feature the Titans of Wrestling in the main event…..”

“…..that’s right daddy…..the Stinger man, he showed everyone just why he’s the franchise of WCW baby, when he sent Hollywood Hogan running back to those Hollywood Hills, if you will…..but Bobby Heenan, he may have slain the Corporate Giant last month, but he’s got an honest to goodness real life Giant tonight Brain…..like Godzilla daddy…..”

“…..Sting…..you beat Hogan…..you did, and no one can take that away from you…..but tonight, you’re not ready for the Giant…..you’ve been preoccupied with Shawn Michaels and Jean Paul Levesque…..off having tag team matches here, brawls around the arenas there…..you’ve been concentrating on anyone BUT the Giant…..well tonight, you’ve got an angry, seven foot plus man across the ring from you…..you’d BETTER be giving him your full attention…..”[/]

…..speaking of Shawn Michaels and Jean Paul Levesque…..they haven’t been winning many friends around here lately, nor do they want to…..what they WANT is to take out the Titans of Wrestling once and for all, and they’ll have the opportunity to do so tonight…..in an anything goes brawl with Randy Savage and The Warrior…..although when anything goes, you’ve got to favour the Titans…..because they rarely go about their business without a little backup…..

…..but that’s all later…..right now it’s time to go down to ringside, for our first match (and title match) of the evening…..

Rating: N/A

WCW World Tag Team Championship Match

Diamond Dallas Page & Chris Benoit (w/Kimberly) (c) vs.

Big Van Vader & Bam Bam Bigelow (w/Kevin Sullivan)


…..and it’s the tag team titles on the line, as Diamond Dallas Page and Chris Benoit put the gold on the line, against two men that (Kevin Sullivan claims) refuse to be forgotten anymore…..Bam Bam Bigelow and Big Van Vader…..

…..it’s difficult to believe that anyone could “forget” these two mammoth men…..both men huge in stature…..both men around (and over in Vaders case) the 400lb mark…..both men former WCW Champions…..but as Kevin Sullivan (perhaps rightly) pointed out to them, two men that have been pushed to one side, neglected…..Forgotten…..in favour of the latest influx of former Titan Sports employees…..for Vader that’s nothing new…..Big Van Vader dominated WCW though most of the early 90’s, crushing even such fan favourites as our CURRENT world champion, Sting…..but that all came to a screeching halt when a certain red and yellow express (along with his whole host of friends) came crashing into World Championship Wrestling…..

…..Hulk Hogan walked through the doors…..and Vader was Forgotten…..

…..fast forward two years, and Bam Bam Bigelow did the unthinkable…..a decade in the business and a decade of failing to live up to his potential, Bigelow finally won the big one, defeating The Nature Boy Ric Flair to become the WCW Champion…..but while he thought his Cinderella story was just beginning, the reality was that the clock was ready to strike midnight, and his coach was already getting ready to turn back into a pumpkin…..because less than three months after winning it, the red and yellow express was about to turn black and white…..

…..Hollywood Hogan walked through the doors…..and Bigelow was Forgotten…..

…..from there, between the formation of the Titans of Wrestling, the war between them and WCW (not to mention the influx of new faces after a mass WWF exodus) took centre stage, and Bam Bam Bigelow and Big Van Vader meekly shuffled off into obscurity…..two huge, mean monsters, cast in the role of fan favourites when they SHOULD have been almost unstoppable machines…..two former World Champions without a SINGLE title opportunity between them in over a year now…..miscast, misused, missing…..then it all changed…..

…..Kevin Sullivan walked through the doors…..and he refuses to let Vader or Bigelow be forgotten…..

…..contrary to that, their opponents tonight have spent the last six months trying to prove themselves…..trying to break through that fabled glass ceiling, out of middlecard mediocrity, and into the main event scene where they feel they belong…..and they did just that…..even in losing efforts to Hollywood Hogan, to Randy Savage, to The Warrior, they both looked every bit the main event player they were claiming to be…..and then at Starrcade, they struck the first blood in the night long battle of WCW vs. The ToW, capturing the WCW Tag Team Titles from the Totally Awesome Twosome, Adam Copeland and Christian Cage…..

…..opportunity walked through the doors…..and Chris Benoit and Diamond Dallas Page answered…..

…..which brings us to tonight…..

…..there’s been a bit of a reoccurring theme in WCW as of late…..the theme that involves people not focusing on the matches they have upcoming, but rather being more preoccupied with the actions (or words) of Shawn Michaels and Jean Paul Levesque…..and our tag team champions tonight are just as guilty of it…..which is perhaps why Chris Benoit is coming into this match at certainly less than 100%, after not only getting Powerbombed on the steel entrance way on Nitro, but also going on to compete against Shawn Michaels just a few short moments later, when he really shouldn’t have…..

…..because of that, it’s perhaps no surprise that it’s the (half crippled) Canadian Crippler that has to play the face in peril role tonight…..

…..to be fair, the champions start out very strong…..they may be at a size disadvantage, but they’re certainly quicker than their opponents and they use that to control the opening portion of the match…..both men duck and weave, dip and dive, and when Bigelow charges at Benoit, the Canadian grabs hold of the top rope and drops down, leaving Bam Bam to tumble over the top and down to the floor…..

…..Vader clubs Benoit from behind, but Page joins his partner and the two of them hammer on the huge mastodon, before finally sending HIM tumbling over the top too with a huge double clothesline…..

…..advantage champs right?…..

…..not for long…..

…..because Benoit decides to leap over that top rope himself, looking to crash down onto Vader…..problem with that is that he former World Champion was waiting…..catches him in mid-air, and then drives him (injured) back first into the ring post…..

…..that puts the challengers firmly in control, and they use all of their considerable girth to punish Chris Benoit…..

…..with Kevin Sullivan barking orders from the floor they systematically cut the ring in half, keeping Benoit firmly in their corner…..and IN that corner they crush/splash/cuff/club/beat and headbutt the tag team champion, until Benoit is a broken mess…..

…..but as the Titans of Wrestling know, putting Chris Benoit down and KEEPING Chris Benoit down are two entirely different things…..and as Bigelow comes off the top with that Diving Headbutt, Benoit rolls to one side, leaving Bammers to crash face first on the mat, as he rolls towards his corner to make the hot tag…..

…..Page storms the ring and cuts off Bigelow before he can tag in his partner, sending Vader flying off the apron with a running shoulder charge, before turning his attention back to the still dazed Bigelow…..

…..Bam Bam isn’t quite the size of Vader, but he’s a huge man none the less, and he’s able to reverse an Irish Whip attempt before knocking the wind out of Page with a standing body splash on the return…..his diving headbutt didn’t work last time, but he’s going for it again here, scaling the turnbuckles for the second time this match…..

…..unbeknownst to the Beast from the East though, DDP isn’t even the legal man anymore…..because Benoit made the blind tag during that Irish Whip attempt, and he rushes across the ring, throwing himself at the ropes, leaving Bammers to lose his footing and crotch himself on the top turnbuckle…..

…..and with Bam Bam perched up top, Page climbs up to the second rope and hooks the head….lit looks like he’s going for the Super Diamond Cutter to the Super Heavyweight…..

…..I mentioned that Kevin Sullivan is at ringside right? Because it’s relevant…..Kimberly Page is there as well…..that’s relevant too…..because as Page hooks Bammer’s head, Sullivan grabs Kimberly by the hair on the outside, and Dallas quickly relinquishes the hold to rushes to the aid of his wife on the outside…..

…..unfortunately that leaves his partner alone…..

…..Benoit looks to pick up where his partner left off, scaling the ropes to deliver what looks to be a superplex to Bigelow, but Vader cuts him off…..

…..drags him to the centre of the ring…..

…..and then Powerbombs him straight into the mat…..

…..Bigelow comes off the top a few seconds later (finally connecting with the headbutt) before making the cover…..and as an oblivious Page chases Sullivan around on the floor, the official counts one…..two…..three…..

…..and we have brand new WCW World Tag Team Champions……

Winner(s): …..and New WCW Tag Team Champions…..Bam Bam Bigelow & Big Van Vader
Rating: A

…..the official hands The Forgotten their (ill-gotten) gold, and as Kevin Sullivan enters the ring to congratulate his men (with microphone in hand) Dallas Page helps his partner to the back…..

…..Sullivan tells the crowd that his prophecy has been fulfilled, as the forgotten monsters have once again infiltrated themselves into the forefront of WCW…..

“…..forgotten by the office, forgotten by the fans, forgotten even by the boys in the back…..but not forgotten by me…..”

…..Sully says that Diamond Dallas Page and Chris Benoit are a shining example of what happens when you forget that Bam Bam Bigelow and Big Van Vader are walking, talking warheads…..although Tony Schiavone doesn’t agree…..

“…..because if you ask ME Brain, the only thing that DDP and Chris Benoit ‘forgot’ tonight, was that Kevin Sullivan will sink to any depths to get what he wants…..”

“…..well they didn’t used to call him the Gamesmaster because he likes to play Checkers on his days off you know Schiavone…..they called him the gamesmaster because he knows how to play the game…..and if Doofus Dallas Page and the Crippled Canadian Chris Benoit had been doing their homework and focusing on their opponents (instead of forgetting about them to worry about Shawn Michaels and JPL) then MAYBE they could have come in with a game plan of their own…..one that DIDN’T involve Kimberly being at ringside…..and MAYBE they would be walking out of here tonight with the tag titles still around their waists…..but then what do I know, I’m only The Brain Schiavone…..”

…..Sullivan says that everyone had better either sit up and take notice, or pack their bags and leave…..because any team that wants to have the privilege of calling themselves the WCW World Tag Team Champions are going to have to go through these two men…..and honestly, whether it’s DDP and Chris Benoit, Shawn Michaels and Jean Paul Levesque, the Titans of Wrestling, the Public Enemy, the Faces of Fear…..the list goes on and on…..

“…..because it doesn’t matter WHO comes after these belts…..come one, come all…..because The Forgotten WILL be remembered…..and The Forgotten REFUSE to fade into insignificance again…..you want these belts, go ahead and try to take them…..”

…..yeah, and by the look on the faces of Vader and Bammers, good luck with that…..let’s go to the back……

Rating: B+

…..where Mike Tenay is standing by with the current WCW Cruiserweight Champion, Jeff Hardy…..

…..Tenay says that Jeff only won this Championship four short weeks ago at Starrcade, but in that time he’s not been shy about defending IT, and the Cruiserweight Division as a whole…..as his challenge to Randy Savage a few weeks back will demonstrate…..

“…..but tonight you’re facing (once again) a member of James Vandenberg’s twisted congregation…..you turned back Tajiri…..you turned back Jinsei Shinzaki…..you even recently defeated Mortis…..but this Sinister Minister seems determined to keep knocking at your door…..tonight you face Hayabusa…..is tonight the night that Vandenberg’s men finally break you down?…..”

…..I think I’ve mentioned before that his mic work is not exactly Jeff Hardy’s strong point…..it’s improved, don’t get me wrong…..but he’s still hardly a wordsmith…..fortunately he keeps this short and sweet…..

…..he tells Tenay that Vandenberg can keep knocking all he likes, he ain’t getting in, and he ain’t getting his grubby little hands on this Cruiserweight Title…..

“…..I’ve been fighting to prove myself since the day I walked through the doors here in World Championship Wrestling…..and I ain’t a Shawn Michaels Mike Tenay, I’m PROUD to be here in WCW, and I’m PROUD to be the WCW Cruiserweight Champion…..

…..James Vandenberg, he keeps going on about ‘cleansing’ this and ‘purifying’ that…..this title, it ain’t here to be clean or pure…..it’s here to be fought for…..blood, sweat and tears…..this title is here for all the guys that are willing to put their bodies on the line for the HONOUR of wearing it…..now, Hayabusa, he might be willing to risk it all…..but just like I ain’t Shawn Michaels, Hayabusa…..

…..he ain’t Jeff Hardy…..”

…..looks like our Cruiserweight Title match is up next…..let’s go down to ringside…..

Rating: B+

WCW Cruiserweight Championship Match

Jeff Hardy (c) vs. Hayabusa (w/James Vandenberg)


…..so it’s our second match of the evening, and our second TITLE match of the evening…..although Jeff Hardy will be looking to have more success in defending his championship than our champions in that previous match did…..

…..this is certainly going to be a faster paced affair than our Tag Team Championship Match was, with both men keen to utilise their aerial abilities…..because of this, the match wasn’t set out like your usual ‘babyface shine, heel heat spot, babyface comeback’ formula…..instead it was about as back and forth a match as you are likely to see…..

…..Hardy said earlier that the Cruiserweight Title wasn’t there to be pure…..it was there for the Cruiserweight’s that were willing to put their bodies on the line to claim that top spot….l

…..both men MORE than put their bodies on the line in this one…..in fact, they spend more time in the air than they did on the mat…..

…..Hardy connected with a one man poetry in motion, as well as a whisper in the wind, while Hayabusa took out the Champion with a series of splashes from the top, as well as a spectacular standing springboard moonsault (from the apron down to the floor) taking out Hardy in the process…..Jeff even sent James Vandenberg flying off the apron, with a dropkick that sends him crashing down to the concrete…..

…..unfortunately, Vandenberg isn’t the only one that Hardy has to worry about…..

…..in all honesty, Jeff Hardy shouldn’t even BE the Cruiserweight Champion right now…..he was SUPPOSED to put Yoshihiro Tajiri over at Starrcade, but the (severe and serious) injury to Tajiri quickly put a stop to that…..

…..an audible was called, and Jeff Hardy became Champion…..but since he was never supposed to be the Champion in the first place, does it really come as much of a shock that he doesn’t leave here tonight with that gold belt around his waist?…..

…..Vandenberg may have gotten taken out, but there is still Shinzaki and Mortis to contend with…..and they BOTH come into play here……because as Hardy mounts the top rope, looking for that Senton Bomb (and with Jinsei distracting the official) Mortis comes up onto the apron to crack Jeff in the back of the head with that Jester Cane, leaving the Cruiserweight Champion to crash to the mat…..

…..Hayabusa quickly takes up the space on the top rope that Jeff previously occupied, before nailing the half unconscious Hardy with a spectacular looking Phoenix Splash that’s enough for the one, the two……and the (tainted) three……

…..and FINALLY the WCW Cruiserweight Championship is in the possession of James Vandenberg’s Flock of Freaks…..

…..it’s not been a good night thus far for the champions of WCW…..

Winner: …..and New WCW Cruiserweight Champion…..Hayabusa
Rating: B

…..we air the replay of the ending of the match (leaving no doubt in anyone’s mind that Jeff Hardy was robbed) and when we come back to the live show, Mean Gene has joined Vandenberg, Mortis, Shinzaki and the new Cruiserweight Champion Hayabusa in the ring…..

“…..and I don’t mind telling you fans, that from my vantage point, YOU James Vandenberg, you just stole that title belt from a very deserving champion…..”

…..oh, it’s testy Gene that we have tonight…..someone didn’t get their scotch on the rocks…..Vandenberg just laughs at this statement however, and as Shinzaki straps the Title belt around the new champions waist, Vandenberg points at Hayabusa, telling Gene that “that man right there, is the ONLY deserving champion in WCW today” because Hayabusa is the ONLY champion that lives his life by the good word of James Vandenberg…..

“…..I heard a lot of talk tonight…..talk about my followers knocking at the door of Jeff Hardy…..well Young Jeffery, James Vandenberg is nothing if not a patient man…..and yes, there was knocking…..OPPORTUNITY was knocking for Mr. Hardy…..and Mr. Hardy chose to ignore that knock…..now look at him…..and look at the man who relished the opportunity to learn at the feet of this Misunderstood Minister…..”

…..Vandenberg says that this was the first step on the road to the purification of the Cruiserweight Division…..and the cleansing of WCW…..close the book on the story of Jeff Hardy, because the next chapter in his Ministry’s tale begins….now….

…..to the back we go again…..

Rating: B+

…..where things are a little more civilised than they currently are inside the ring, because we go to the locker room of Lance Storm…..a locker room that Mr. Storm is currently sharing with a group of gentlemen that he has been spending a lot of time with…..fellow Canadians Jacques Rougeau and Carl Oulette, His Lordship Steven Regal……Earl Robert Eaton…..and The Duke, Douglas Williams…..

…..the six men appear to be having a good old chuckle at the jolly good thrashing they gave to the Road Warriors on Thunder, with his Lordship claiming that it’s no wonder that two men with class, elegance and style were able to overcome two brutish thugs such as Hawk and Animal…..

“…..in fact, the only real surprise was the fact that those two knuckle-dragging neanderthals want a rematch tomorrow night…..AND that they’re bringing that red, white and blue waving imbecile, James Duggan…..especially after the thrashings that you yourself have delivered to him Mr. Storm…..”

…..speaking of which, Mike Tenay joins in the party…..and HE wants to know if the challenge to face Hawk, Animal and Jim Duggan tomorrow night is going to be answered?…..

…..Storm cuts Tenay off, telling him that first of all, he should know better than to interrupt a nobleman of LORD Steven Regal’s stature (to which Regal agree’s, muttering under his breath about the rudeness of these Americans) before confirming that yes…..yes the challenge is accepted…..

“…..three of us, against the three of them…..and once more we will demonstrate the civilianised worlds superiority against the Arrogance of America…..”

…..ok, good to know…..but Tenay is now curious as to WHICH three of “them” it will be that Faces the Road Warriors and Hacksaw tomorrow night on Nitro, but Regal cuts him off at that…..

“…..because not only did you have the audacity to waltz into this room (uninvited none the less) but you waltzed straight into an Embassy of the British Empire…..because make no mistake sunshine, wherever Lord Steven Regal is, he’s doing Her Majesty’s work and is governed only by the rules of the Sovereignty…..you don’t get to call the shots OR ask the questions in here unless WE allow it…..

…..you want to find out which three of Her Majesty’s servants will be answering the challenge, well then I suggest you bloody well tune in tomorrow night, because you will be finding out who at exactly the same time as those three meandering morons…..now I suggest you sod off, before you feel the back of my hand…..”

…..I guess you don’t have to tell Mike Tenay twice, because he quickly exits the (so-called) “Embassy”, before signing off and sending us back down to ringside…..

Rating: B+

Mike Awesome & Gideon Cain (w/Missy Hyatt) vs. The Faces of Fear (w/Jimmy Hart)


…..where it’s time to batten down the hatches and bolt the stable door…..because we’re about to have a hoss fight…..because it’s Missy Hyatt’s Mike Awesome and Gideon Cain squaring off against two members of Jimmy Hart’s (newly reformed) First Family…..the Faces of Fear, Meng and The Barbarian…..

…..this mini feud came about following a Faces of Fear beatdown to Mike Awesome, that was only thwarted by the return from (kayfabe) injury of Awesome’s partner, Gideon Cain…..since then the two sides have been pretty even…..Meng has defeated Awesome, but Cain has defeated The Barbarian…..time to see who is going to take the advantage in this Tag Team encounter…..

…..”bowling shoe ugly” is probably one term that a certain black hat wearing announcer might use to describe this one…..and that’s not to say it was a bad match (in fact far from it, because for what it was it was very good) but it was far from pretty, that’s for damn sure…..

…..Awesome and Cain have been quietly going about getting themselves over (and in Cain’s case, quietly improving at a steady rate thanks to the tutelage of Bobby Eaton) and are now comfortably on the same level as (if not slightly above) the Faces of Fear…..granted that doesn’t sound that impressive, but when you consider the fact that Meng and The Barbarian have been consistent upper-midcarders for a decade now, while Awesome was a virtual unknown eight months ago and Gideon Cain WAS an unknown (no virtual about it) fresh out of the Powerplant…..

…..the four men club, thump, punch, kick, headbutt and slam their way through a decent enough match, that sees both sides getting plenty of chance to shine…..in the end though, Jimmy Hart’s First Family don’t get off to the best of starts, after Cain finishes off The Barbarian with the East of Eden for the one, two, three.

Winner(s): Mike Awesome & Gideon Cain
Rating: B+

From there we have some pre-recorded footage from earlier in the evening, before this event went live…..footage of WCW.com’s Mark Madden, stopping Curt Hennig as he enters the building, clearly looking for an exclusive for the (still in its infancy) website…..

…..he asks Hennig whether he’s going to remain impartial here tonight? After all, he and The Rock have hardly been on the same page in these last few weeks, and in fact this past Thunder saw them out and out go at it…..so I guess the question

“…..Curt Hennig, should The Rock be worried?…..”

…..Curt doesn’t even break stride as he makes his way though the arena, but he does stop when he hears that question, before looking at Madden like he’s a moron…..

“…..should The Rock be worried?…..what a stupid question little man…..of course The Rock should be worried…..he’s facing a man in Eddie Guerrero, that even I couldn’t out-cheat in three tries…..he’s facing a man in Eddie Guerrero that he’s already lost to once…..The Rock should unquestionably be worried…..

…..but not about me…..I may be scheduled to be there at ringside later this evening, but I’m there purely in an Official Capacity…..I’m there to enforce the proceedings…..I’m there SOLEY and ONLY to make sure that The Rock gets a fair shot at that United States title, without any outside shenanigans…..other than that, I’m JUST there as a spectator like everybody else…..so if you’ll excuse me, I need to go and get ready…..”

…..although quite WHAT Curt needs to get ready for is anyone’s guess…..after all, other than keeping Chavo Guerrero Jr at bay, all he has to do is sit back and enjoy what should be a great match…..at least we know that Mr. Hennig is going to be an impartial advisor…..

…..apparently…..

Rating: A

…..we come back live to the feed, and we head straight to the back, where Mean Gene is standing by with the Titan’s of Wrestling’s own Buff(y the Icon Slayer) Bagwell…..

“…..and Buff Bagwell, it would seem to me that the last time you were backstage at a WCW show, you caused quite the bit of trouble…..”

…..Gene is referring of course to the assault inflicted upon The Total Package Lex Luger, who was left laying this past Monday Night, with some rather convenient graffiti on the walls which suggested that perhaps Shawn Michaels was responsible…..

…..Gene says that Lex Luger certainly believed that Shawn Michaels and/or Jean Paul Levesque had something to do with the assault, leading him to challenge Shawn Michaels to a match this past Thursday Night on Thunder…..unfortunately for Luger (as he found out during said match, distracting him long enough for Michaels to kick his head off his shoulders) it turned out to have been Buff who did the deed, in an obvious frame up…..

…..which leads us to tonight…..

…..because Lex Luger has officially challenged Buff to a match tonight, a fact which Bagwell finds hilarious…..

…..he tells Gene that Flexy Lexy might want to try and work out that old grey matter between his ears instead of flexing his sub-par pecs for hours on end, because he is clearly stoopid (with a double o)…..stoopid for thinking that Michaels and Levesque even care enough about a bum like him to beat him up, and stoopid for not realising that it was an obvious set up…..

“…..and even stoopider still if he thinks he can beat The Stuff here tonight…..I’m Buff Bagwell Mean Gene…..I am a Titan of Wrestling…..and Buff Daddy has already proved to the world that there ain’t no-one I can’t beat….and if you don’t believe me, ask Rowdy Roddy Piper…..

…..oh wait…..you can’t…..because I drove his crippled old ass out of WCW once and for all…..”

…..Buff has a great laugh at that, telling Gene that when he’s done with Luger, he’s just going to be another has-been from the 80’s, put out to pasture by the biggest….the baddest…..the best…..Buff “The Stuff” Bagwell…..

Rating: B+

Lex Luger vs. Buff Bagwell


…..ok, so after facing (and losing to) the supposed “culprit” of his Monday Nitro assault on this past Thursdays Thunder, Lex Luger now faces the ACTUAL culprit of his Monday Nitro assault, Buff Bagwell…..

…..Buff has had a run of good form in recent weeks…..in fact, he’s had a run of good form for the last few months, ever since he hooked up with the Titans of Wrestling back around July time of 1997…..he’s gone from habitual midcarder, forming an unlikely team with Samu of the SST, to solid upper-mid (occasional main event) star, holding victories over the likes of DDP, Chris Benoit, and even genuine Icon’s like Rowdy Roddy Piper…..

…..granted, most of those wins were dubious, but still, a win is a win…..

…..Lex Luger on the other hand has NOT had the best of luck recently…..he’s lost to the Giant, he’s lost to Shawn Michaels, he put on a tremendous showing in the Mayhem Match (lasting almost an hour) but ultimately lost…..and now his best friend is the World Champion…..so where does he go from here?…..

…..I guess a win against Buff Bagwell would be a good place to start…..

…..of course, whether he gets one remains to be seen, but for what it’s worth, in the opening moments of this match, you would be excused for thinking you were watching a bodybuilding contest, as these two oiled up men flex at each other like their opponent will spontaneously submit if they pump their biceps hard enough…..

…..obviously that doesn’t happen…..what DOES happen, is we have a decent enough match between two men that are comfortable enough in their roles to perform well (even if it’s not a mat classic)…..

…..Luger is at his shouty best as he runs through Buff with clothesline after clothesline, while Buff is at his cocky (but ultimately chickensh!t) best as he tries to escape from the wrath of Luger…..

…..The Total Package is on fire as he dominates the opening portion of the match, but that fire gets the better of him as he recklessly charges into the ringpost, wrapping his shoulder around the steel when Bagwell moves out of the way of a clothesline attempt on the floor…..

…..Buff takes control from there, as he works over Luger (in between poses) taunting him all the way…..he’s pretty fundamental in the ring our Buff, but his limitations are masked somewhat by the fact that this crowd love to hate him, and they pop big when Luger is able to dive out of the way of a Blockbuster attempt, which sees Buff crash and burn onto the mat…..

…..from there Luger rally’s back, absorbing every blow that Buff can deliver, before finally letting out a big battle cry as he hits Bagwell with a double bicep pose, before nailing him in the face with a right hand…..

…..right hand, right hand…..back to the corner, Irish whip to the turnbuckles…..running charge, corner clothesline…..Luger is on fire, and he tops it off with an impressive Military Press, slamming Bagwell back down to the mat before signalling for the Torture Rack…..

…..he racks him too…..but as he does, Bagwells trailing leg takes out the official, meaning there is no one there to call for the bell as Buff furiously submits…..

…..Luger finally tosses his opponent to the mat in frustration, and goes over to revive the official…..so Bagwell drops him with a low blow…..

…..the official is still down, and now so is Lex…..so Bagwell does what any self respecting Titan of Wrestling would do, he heads outside and gets a steel chair, then stands over The Total Package, weapon raised…..

…..as the Bagpipes begin to blare…..

…..and Rowdy Roddy Piper comes walking out from the back…..

…..actually, ‘walking’ is a tad generous…..limping is a more realistic word, because it’s only been a month since we last saw the Hot Rod, and it the crutch under his arm is any indication, he’s still not at 100%…..

…..Buff laughs that up too, mocking Piper as he hobbles down the aisle, telling him to watch this, because he’s about to finish Luger, just like he finished Piper…..

…..except Piper ISN’T finished…..far from it actually…..because he slides into the ring behind Bagwell (does a little Muhammad Ali shuffle to show us all that actually, his leg is fine) then waffles Buff across the back with the crutch…..

…..Piper rolls back out of the ring, just as the official is coming to his senses to see Luger hoist Bagwell up in the Torture Rack…..and this time there’s no mistaking Buff’s cry of surrender…..

…..your winner (by submission) The Total Package…..

Winner: Lex Luger
Rating: B+

…..Piper climbs into the ring (and raises Luger’s hand) as Bagwell scurry’s away to the back with his tail between his legs, before snatching up a microphone to tell the crowd (and Bagwell) that the rumours of his demise have been greatly exaggerated…..

…..the rest of the Titans head to the back (scraping up Randy Savage as they go) and they’ve barely made it through the curtains before the Giants entrance music hits…..

…..the number one contender strides down the aisle confidently (not even registering the existence of a battered and bloody JPL who is on route to Shawn Michaels still downed form) before grabbing a microphone and climbing into the ring…..

…..he cuts right to the chase, he’s waited long enough, the circus is over, everyone’s favourite distraction (Shawn Michaels) is a bloody mess over there somewhere, let’s get this giant sized ass kicking over with, so he can claim what’s his…..the WCW Championship…..

“…..so Sting, no more hiding behind your bats, no more hiding in the rafters, no more worrying about Shawn Michaels and his little buddy there…..all you have to worry about now, is this seven foot giant standing in the middle of this ring…..so get your ass out here, and bring MY title with you…..”

…..ask and you shall receive, because it only takes a few seconds for the arena to dim, the lights to flicker the illusion of lightning…..and for that ominous music begin to play…..Sting is here, and he DOES have that WCW Title with him…..

…..it’s wrapped right around his waist…..

…..in the midst of all this, JPL has revived Shawn Michaels, and the two men are on their feet beside the entrance ramp…..Sting walks a few feet down the aisle until he’s level with them, then turns to look down at the bloodied and defeated pair, his usual blank expression giving way to a look of almost satisfaction as he gives the pair a little taste of their own medicine…..

…..a crotch chop, followed by a “WCW says, you can Suck It”, before continuing on his way…..it looks as though our main event is happening right now…..and even if Shawn Michaels or JPL wanted to do something about it, they’re really in no condition to…..

…..on with the introductions Buffer…..

Rating: A*

WCW World Championship Match

Sting (c) vs. The Giant


…..this is it then…..the final match of the evening and it’s for all the marbles, as Sting puts his WCW Championship on the line, against the biggest challenge of his career, the biggest man on the roster……the biggest man in wrestling…..The Giant…..

…..this seven foot tall, 450lb monster would be enough of a challenge on the best of days, but THIS giant, he’s been waiting for a chance to get both a shot at the WCW Championship, AND Sting inside the squared circle for months now…..tonight, he gets both wishes in one…..

…..Giant might believe that Sting hasn’t been preparing for him (instead getting distracted by the antics of Michaels and Levesque) but you wouldn’t know it by the opening moments of this one…..Sting certainly knows well enough to stay out of the grips of the much bigger man, and he ducks and weaves, striking when an opening presents itself, while generally staying out of arms reach…..

…..you can see the challenger getting more and more frustrated too, a fact which Tony Schiavone says can only be in the Champions favour, because frustrated people get impatient, and impatient people make mistakes…..

…..as if to prove Schiavone’s point, Giant chooses that exact moment to charge towards Sting (who’s just ducked and weaved his way into the corner) with a running splash, but the Champion sidesteps at the last second, leaving Giant to collide sternum first into the turnbuckles…..Sting follows that up with a huge Stinger Splash to the back of the challenger (again driving his sternum into the turnbuckles) and a second…..then a third, which finally sees the challenger topple backwards like a big redwood in the forrest, landing flat on his back in the middle of the ring…..

…..looking to finish the man off Sting mounts the turnbuckles, then comes flying off with a top rope splash…..he makes the cover, but the Giant kicks out and kicks out with force, propelling Sting all the way off him…..

…..the two men get back to their feet, with Sting going straight back to the duck and weave tactic that’s been working for him so far…..and again the Giant charges into the corner (this time back first) and AGAIN the WCW Champion goes for that Stinger Splash…..Giant has it scouted this time though, and as Sting takes flight he lifts one big leg up in front of him , and the Stinger’s head is almost taken completely off his body as he connects face first with that Big Boot…..

…..now the big man is in control…..and he slows the pace right back down to his level, as he slowly and methodically uses his considerable size to punish the Champion…..The Giant can (and quite often does) display some astounding feats of athleticism for a man of his size, but he doesn’t bother with any of that here, instead it’s all heavy hitting, high impact offence, and if Sting WASN’T fully focused on the Giant, the big man certainly gets his attention when he steps on his chest with all of his considerable weight…..

…..Sting has faced his fair share of big men over the years (including two men we have seen tonight, Big Van Vader and Sid Vicious) so he knows better than most what it takes to defeat man man much bigger in stature than himself. In a match like this, it’s a case of weathering the storm and picking your spot….and the spot in question here, comes off the back of a Chokeslam attempt…..Giant wraps his hand around the Champions throat, hoisting him up high in the air…..unfortunately he’s too close to the ropes and Sting is able to shift his momentum in mid air, shaking himself free and landing feet first on the apron, where he grabs the Giant by the head and drops to the floor, driving his opponents throat across that top rope…..

…..Giant snaps his head back and stands there teetering, giving Sting enough time to slide in back under the bottom rope and hit an (extremely) impressive bodyslam, before beating his chest with a big “Owwwwww” to this capacity crowd…..

…..from there Sting begins to fire back…..he went through a hell of a lot to get to the position he’s in today, and Giant or not, this big man is going to have to practically kill him to take this title away from him…..

…..although from the look of things, the Giant doesn’t have a problem with that…..he also doesn’t have a problem with cheating, because after Sting mounts his big comeback, he’s able to catch a breather when he drops the charging Stinger with a big boot…..from there Giant heads to the outside to collect Sting’s baseball bat, a pretty stupid move according to Tony Schiavone, who points out that if Giant uses that foreign object, he not only loses the match, but he loses the opportunity to become the World Champion too…..

…..which is probably why he gives the bat up without a fight when the official snatches it from him…..although as the referee turns away to remove the weapon from the ring, Giant quickly rips the corner padding off the top turnbuckle, exposing the steel beneath…..

…..that whole “bat” scenario may well have been a cunning ploy…..

…..it backfires though, because after a back and forth tease, which sees Sting almost hit that steel on a number of occasions, Giant hoists Sting up onto his shoulder “Snake Eyes” style and runs towards that exposed corner…..

…..again Sting is able to wriggle free however, landing behind Giant and uses the big man’s own momentum to drive that giant skull into the exposed steel…..

…..Giant slumps forward into the turnbuckles, clearly dazed, so Sting mounts the second turnbuckle, grabs that big head in his hands, and throws himself backwards, driving the back of The Giant’s skull into the mat with a modified Scorpion Death Drop…..and as he makes the cover, the official registers the one…..the two…..and the three…..and Sting retains his WCW Championship, against giant odds…..

Winner: …..and Still WCW Champion…..Sting
Rating: B+

…..the bell rings and the official raises the hand of Sting, before handing him his Title belt…..

…..Giant looks as though he’s contemplating carrying on the fight (if not the match) but when Sting collects that black bat too, he soon changes his mind, deciding instead to head to the back as pyro erupts in celebration…..

…..Sting stands tall as the show comes to a close (with this crowd certainly going home happy) but not before he gets up close and personal with the camera man, staring directly down the lens to let everyone at home (and everyone in the back) know that HE is the WCW Champion…..

“…..and this belt, it ain’t going nowhere…..because THIS, is all that matters…..not the Titans of Wrestling…..not the Giant…..nobody…..this is history…..this is tradition…..and Shawn Michaels, this goes out directly to you pal…..because you can talk a big talk but at the end of the day, tonight…..you lost…..I won…..and no matter how big you THINK you are…..without THIS, when it comes to World Championship Wrestling…..when it comes to these great fans…..you’re nothing more than just another name on the roster, so how about you suck that…..”

…..we’re out of time fans…..we’ll see you tomorrow night…..on WCW Monday Nitro…..

Rating: A*

Final Rating: A
