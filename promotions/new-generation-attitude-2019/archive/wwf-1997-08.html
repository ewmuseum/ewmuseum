WWF Raw is War: Week 1, August 1997

Posted 31 May 2019

Week 1, August 1997

Reunion Arena (Dallas, Texas)

ATT: 15,556

TV: 33.03

We open up the show tonight to a far less chaotic scene than last week……as Shawn Michaels makes his way down the aisle (something that was a regular opening segment at one point) along with the gorgeous Sunny on his arm……the power couple of the WWF aren’t coming alone however…..the Corporate Cannon Brian Pillman is stalking his way down the aisle along with them, cackling maniacally as he goes, while The Nature Boy Ric Flair and The Enforcer Arn Anderson round up the bunch…..

……the quintet enter the ring, with Flair taking Sunny by the hand and spinning her around 360 degrees so that the crowd can appreciate how truly stunning she is…..it’s an appreciative crowd too…..Shawn Michaels has spent the best part of eighteen months being booed out of every building he’s stepped foot in, but he’s home in Texas right here…..and it might not be San Antonio, but this Dallas crowd certainly give The Heartbreak Kid (and everyone else by proxy) a warm welcome…..not that it lasts long……Shawn takes a microphone and thanks the crowd for the reception, telling them that it must be great for them to be in the presence of a truly great Texan……instead of being surrounded in mediocrity like they are for the other 364 days of the year…….

“…..because lets face it, Texas Born…..Texas Bred……most of you morons look…..AND smell……like you’ve been Texas Dead for a while now…….”

……..ah cheap heel heat, you’ve gotta love it…….Michaels says that luckily for them, The Heartbreak Kid is there to put a little bit of San Antonio shine into their pathetic little lives……

“…..and believe me when I say, you ain’t getting rid of me anytime soon……unfortunately, the same can’t be said for Kevin Nash……because in less than three weeks time, at Summerslam, the Heartbreak Kid Shawn Michaels is going to put Big Gimpy out of the WWF once and for all……”

……Michaels tells Nash that it’s been The Showstopper that has been carrying his big seven foot ass on his back since the day Nash arrived here four years ago…..he plucked him from obscurity and made him a mega star……made him the number two guy in the business (behind the Heartbreak Kid obviously)……and more importantly, made him a hell of a lot of money……but instead of being grateful…..instead of knowing his place…..Nash, and ALL of the Wolfpac got greedy, and stabbed the man that bought them all to the dance in the back…….and at Summerslam, Nash….Hall…..Waltman….they’re all going to find out what happens when you cross THE MAN……..because Ric Flair and Double A, they’re going to take care of the two C-Listers……while Kevin Nash finds himself out of a job……

“……because I’ve said it before……and I’ll say it again……The Heartbreak Kid lays down for NOBODY……..”

……Michaels passes the microphone to Double A, who tells Nash that he’s as stupid as he is big if he thinks putting his career on the line against Shawn Michaels is a good idea……he says that Shawn can MAKE a career inside this ring, he can BREAK a career inside this ring……and at Summerslam, he’s going to END a career inside this ring……which just leaves Hall and Waltman…..two men with no identity of their own……they spent twelve months playing second fiddle to the Heartbreak Kid, and the last six months as background characters in the Kevin Nash show……they’re unimportant…..inconsequential……bit part players……and at Summerslam…..Flair and Anderson are going to show the world just how insignificant those two wannabe wolves really are…….something that Ric Flair echoes when he takes the microphone……

“……..Scott Hall……..Sean Waltman……..there’s an old saying about being careful of wolves in sheep’s clothing…..well you two pissants, you are the complete antithesis of that……..you’re nothing but two sheep…..in wolves clothes……two followers without an independent thought of your own, PLAYING at being the tough guys while you hide behind your seven foot buddy there……Kevin Nash……Scott Hall………the only reason EITHER one of you two is even relevant in this great sport, is because Shawn Michaels MADE you relevant……Shawn Michaels took a seven foot lumbering idiot, gave him a black glove and a cheap pair of sunglasses, stood him next to the Heartbreak Kid…..and made him a STAR…….Shawn Michaels took a ladder…….wrapped it around Razor Ramon’s head……and he made that greasy haired, fake gold wearing chump a somebody……

…..Flair takes off his jacket and tosses it on the mat, before dragging the camera monkey in close and showing him the diamond encrusted gold Rolex around his wrist…..

“……..you see this right here? THIS is gold…..this is the REAL deal, just like the Nature Boy……this is a ten thousand dollar watch, and I’ve got three of them…..I can spend more on one piece of jewellery, than you people can spend on food for the entire year………..(Flair stops for a minute to point out a fan in the front row)………..well……….not you fat boy…….all the Rolex’s in the world aren’t going to be enough to feed your fat ass…….Hall, Waltman…….Summerslam is right around the corner…….and it’s going to be a bad night for you……and a great night for us……..because The Nature Boy……..The Enforcer………we’re going to put the lid on the coffin of the Wolfpac…….before the Heartbreak Kid nails it shut……….

……..Flair hands the microphone over to Brian Pillman, before treating the (now less than appreciative) crowd to a trademark strut……The Corporate Cannon is unusually reserved here…..choosing to speak directly to Dude Love through the camera……he says this would usually be the part where the protagonist would explain his motives……

“……the only problem with that, is the fact that I’m Brian “F’N” Pillman……and I’m not one for complying and following the rules…….and unlike YOU, I don’t need to pander to the wretched refuse in the crowd……I do what I want to do, when I want to do it…….and as far as giving you an explanation goes……right now, I just don’t want to…….what I WILL give you however, is a date…….August 24th……..Summerslam……..if your new found delicate persona has a problem with the Crowned Prince of Chaos slapping the tie dye off his fat ass……..well, you can always meet me in the ring and see if you can do something about it……..but “Dude Love”……..I don’t think you can…….”

……with that, Pillman tosses the microphone to the mat……and Summerslam is really shaping up, as we appear to have added another match……Brian Pillman against (should he accept) Dude Love.

Rating: A*

VS.

Winner to be entered into Four Way WWF European Title Match at Summerslam

Barry Windham vs. Jeff Jarrett (w/Ted DiBiase)

Last week Mr. McMahon announced a number of matches for Summerslam…..matches with severe consequences, if no competitors……because if you want entry into these Title bouts, you’re going to have to earn it…..AND put it all on the line…..because to the winner goes the spoils, while to the loser……goes nothing but a trip to the unemployment line……because one man/team from each match, will be fired…..

…..with that, it’s time to see who is going to be the first man entered into the match for the Rock’s European Championship…..as Barry Windham goes one on one with the Million Dollar Corporation’s Jeff Jarrett…..in what was actually, a very good match…..obviously both of these two are solid if not spectacular when it comes to ring work, and they worked great together here tonight as they treated to fans to a taste of southern grappling….

…..both of these men could do with a programme soon….in fact Jarrett in particular is probably begging out for something to do……after never really finding his niche as of yet in the Million Dollar Corporation, he’s somewhat of the forgotten man around here, someone I keep meaning to give a push too but then never seem to get around to it (incidentally, he’s quietly gone about his business, getting himself over enough to recently Auto-Push to main event with no real attempt on my behalf……a fact which in itself should really be rewarded with SOME kind of push)…….

…….Jarrett is certainly higher than the level I perceived the European Championship to be at as our third tier singles title…..but then realistically, so is current Champion The Rock…..regardless it’s Jarrett that advances to the Summerslam bout here, picking up the win with a handful of tights.

Winner: Jeff Jarrett
Rating: B+

From there we go backstage, where Dok Hendrix is with the man who will be defending the European Title against Jeff Jarrett (amongst others) at Summerslam, The Rock……obviously the man is not alone however, because where one Nation member goes, the whole Nation of Domination goes (well, the healthy ones anyway)……

……Dok says it’s going to be a big night for the Nation at Summerslam……because not only do they risk losing two Championships, they also risk losing two members……as both The Rock and Scorpio could find themselves fired should they be the participant that has their shoulders pinned to the mat……Faarooq snatches the microphone away from Hendrix, clearly the man isn’t in a good mood tonight……

“……first things first Hendrix, who the hell does Vince McMahon think he is? Not content with forcing ME to put my title AND my career on the line two weeks ago at In Your House……a plan which failed by the way, because I’m still here….and I’m still the Intercontinental Champion……not content with THAT, now he’s gonna do everything he can to try and screw the Nation out of not ONLY the European and Light Heavyweight Titles, but out of two of its members too……can you really look me in the eye Dok Hendrix, and tell me that “the man” isn’t trying to hold the Nation down?…….”

…….Dok says that in all fairness, it’s not just The Rock and Scorpio who will be putting their careers on the line in that match…..every participant will be……

“……..Dok you know as well as I do that every other participant in those matches will be there by CHOICE……if their stupid asses want to put their careers on the line that’s down to them……The Rock……Scorpio……they’re being FORCED to do so against their will…..because the MAN says so……..”

…….Faarooq says that no matter what McMahon throws at them however, it won’t stop them……one way or another, The Rock and Scorpio will be walking out victorious at Summerslam……

“……..against one man, two or three…..the result will still be the same…..because no matter how high you stack the deck, the Nation of Domination will overcome, I guarantee you that……by ANY means necessary……..”

……Faarooq hands the microphone to The Rock, who looks down at the European Championship on his shoulder before raising an eyebrow at Dok Hendrix…….

“……..and what do we mean by ANY means necessary Dok Hendrix……what do you think that means?……..”

“……..well, I……….”

“……..IT DOESN’T MATTER WHAT YOU THINK…….see the fact of the matter is this, the Rock is going to go out there…..August 24th…..Summerslam…….three opponents…..three jabroni’s…..three hot steaming pieces of monkey crap for the Rock take his pick from……or maybe Dok……maybe……just two……because if the Rock is correct…..and he usually is……these qualifying matches are open to anyone right?…..and it just so happens that Kama is dressed and ready to compete……MAYBE, the odds will be a little bit more in the Nation’s favour than Vince McMahon was expecting……”

……with that the Rock slaps Kama on his big shoulder, as the four men walk off, presumably in the direction of the ring.

Rating: A

VS.

Winner to be entered into Four Way WWF European Title Match at Summerslam

Matt Hardy vs. Kama

The Nation of Domination clearly believed they had found a little loophole here, as Kama looks to insert himself into the Rock’s European Title match at Summerslam…..and if they weren’t confident before, they were when Kama’s opponent was revealed to be Matt Hardy….a young man predominantly known as a tag team wrestler……a very young tag team wrestler at that…..and after the New Age Outlaws destroyed his brother at In Your House, a very young tag team wrestler without a partner……

……clearly seeing the writing on the wall here, the official does at least attempt to even the odds by ordering the rest of the Nation to return to the back…….but even one on one, picking up a victory against the much bigger Kama is going to be no easy task……highlighted by the fact that Kama brutalises this young man…..overpowers him, and beats the snot out of him…..and in all honestly, had he not been toying with his opponent, could have probably won the match on a number of occasions……finally it seemed like Kama had enough of playing with Matt, grabbing him by the face and telling him that he’s going to sharing a hospital room with his brother after this, before positioning him for a brainbuster……

……..the mention of his brother appeared to be somewhat of a wake up call to Hardy however, and as Kama lifted him high in the air he wiggled free, landing behind the big man, where he spins him around and drops him with the Twist of Fate, quickly making the cover…..and while Kama DID manage to get his shoulder up, it was a split second after the officials hand struck the canvas for the third time…..and as Kama angrily gets back to his feet, Matt Hardy bolts from the ring and away to safety……victory in the bag along with a shot at the European Championship at Summerslam.

Winner: Matt Hardy
Rating: B

With Kama still seething in the ring we go to commercial, and when we come back it’s to the unexpected sight of Bradshaw and Kane going at it in the back……..not that seeing a brawl on Raw is War is unexpected……what’s unexpected if the sight of someone WILLINGLY getting into a brawl with the Big Red Machine Kane…..a seven foot monster that most people would go out of their way to avoid…..not Bradshaw however, who after a Chokeslam on the outside last week, followed by a Tombstone on the inside, is clearly looking for a little bit of retribution here…….

…….the two men are brutalising each other throughout the bowels of the building, brawling their way through the backstage area…..and while Bradshaw is certainly getting his shots in, it’s safe to say that Kane’s blows are having the most impact……at least until they reach a production area, where Bradshaw slams Kane into a steel roller door, before grabbing hold of the long metal chain that operates the roller, wrapping it around his fist, and punching the holy hell out of the Big Red Machine with what is essentially now one big brass knuckle……

…….the Monster finally goes down, and Bradshaw unwraps the chain, before using it as a steel whip to punish the big man, before finally relinquishing the weapon and backing up, winding up his arm and looking for that big Clothesline From Hell……not that he gets to unleash it…..because that’s exactly when a group of security guards rush the area and drag the angry Texan away……the remaining backstage personnel turn to check on Kane, but before they reach him the Red Demon sits bolt upright……

……he stands to his feet and grabs two of the backstage workers by the throat, delivering a huge double Chokeslam to the unfortunate workers……there are three men left and they are stood frozen to the spot like deer in the headlights, as Kane launches them one by one into the hard walls, before storming off in the direction of Bradshaw……this one is far from over.

Rating: A

Winner to be entered into Four Way WWF Tag Team Title Match at Summerslam

The Headbangers vs. Edge & Christian vs. High Voltage vs. The Harris Boys

While this match was set to to add our fourth and final team into the WWF Tag Team Title match at Summerslam, it also served as a prime view of sorts for how that match was going to go down…..because much like our Summerslam bout, this was one fall to a finish……and truth be told, I was kind of expecting this match to go down about as well as a fart in church…..no knock on anyone’s ability, but The Headbangers and High Voltage have literally JUST arrived here in the WWF and are as unknown as unknown can be…….The Harris Boys aren’t even jobbers to the stars, they’re just jobbers in general……which just left Edge and Christian as the one team anyone was going to care about……but in all honesty, this really wasn’t that bad……

…….it was everything you could expect……lots of tagging in and out…..lots of scrambling to become the legal team….but in the end it was Edge and Christian that came out victorious, after Edge got the pinfall on (unsurprisingly) Don Harris……which means our Summerslam final four is set……The New Age Outlaws will be defending the WWF Tag Team Titles against Al Snow and Chris Candido…..Harlem Heat……and Edge and Christian…….one of these teams will be walking out with gold…….and one with pink……their pink slip that is……because the team that eats the loss…….will be fired.

Winner(s): Edge & Christian
Rating: B-

After a brief hype video, highlighting Summerslam and all the matches announced so far, it’s back down to ringside, where the seven foot Psycho Sid is making his way to the ring……and he’s not alone…..he has the man who made a surprise appearance last week…..Sid’s current (and Ravens former) mentor…..Jake Roberts……although quite why the Snake has chosen to guide Sid through his sea of madness is anyone’s guess……however as the two men take a microphone, we just might find out why…….

…..Sid gets a solid reception as always, the man is never not over (no matter how shocking his in ring work is) and Jake gets quite the pop too considering he hasn’t been seen in a WWF ring for near on five years now…….Sid tells the crowd that he warned Raven…..he told him that he had someone in his ear…..someone that knew Raven better than Raven knows himself…..and that man, is this man……Jake “The Snake” Roberts…….

…….Jake takes the microphone…..and says he’s been gone a long time…..and not just from the WWF…..he taps a finger to the side of his head, saying he was gone a long time in here too……

“…….and truthfully, I didn’t know if I would ever come back……didn’t know if I deserved to come back…..I’ve done a lot of things in my life, a lot of things I’m not proud of…..and not too much that I am proud of…..I lived most of my life in pain…..not physical pain, emotional pain….I walked a long, dark, lonely road…..and the only time I felt alive, was by tarnishing anything good that I touched…….until the purest good of all touched me……I saw the light…..I confessed my sins, and there were a LOT of them…..I asked for forgiveness and I was saved……and I thought that was it…..thought I was done with this life……until Sid reached out for me and I realised the task that had been placed before me……a chance to undo some of the evil I did……see, make no mistake, I’m not here as a manager……I’m not here as a mentor……I’m not here to guide the career of Psycho Sid……I’m here for one reason, and one reason only……..I’m here to save Raven……”

………of course, judging by the look on the face of Raven, as he makes his way out with the Flock…..Raven may not want to be saved……that’s if he even believes he needs saving in the first place……he comes to a halt on the entrance way, and obviously has a microphone with him, and it would seem that his initial shock from last week at Jake costing him the match with Sid has been replaced by anger……..

“…….save me?……SAVE ME?…….you think yourself some guardian angel now……it was YOU that pushed me into the dark, now you look for me in the light and you wonder why I’m not there?……do you know what hypocrisy is Snake?…..hypocrisy is the audacity to preach integrity from a den of corruption…..YOU can no more save ME than the devil could save a demon……I don’t need saving…..I don’t need you……MY Flock need ME……and if you continue to meddle in my affairs, they WILL destroy you……the fact that you’ve not already been reduced to a bloody puddle of viscera on that mat is the ONLY allowance I will make for the part YOU played in shaping me……but it’s an allowance I will make only once…..walk away…..slither back under whatever rock you crawled out from…….or my Flock will destroy you……”

…….Jake tells Raven that he can’t do that……because saving the Flock is the first step on the road to saving Raven……he says that the Flock is a perfect example of how far Jakes toxic touch has spread……as Raven has gone from the Snakes student, to the Flocks teacher, based on the things that Jake Roberts taught……..

”…….you need to free the Flock in order to free yourself…….Christopher Daniels, Crush…..Wrath……they’ve begun to see the light……they’ve begun to see the career they COULD have out from under the shadow of the Flock……Bill DeMott…..he’ll see too……and Raven….when you cast away the shackles that bind you……when you free yourself from the weight of the Flock around your neck…..you WILL fly to heights greater than even I could imagine……Trust Me…….”

……Raven looks around at the men surrounding him, the look of doubt clearly etched on their faces as he turns angrily back to Jake…..

”…….no, trust me…….you might think you’re saving me……but all you’re doing is condemning Sid……because while you waste your time in this futile redemption story…..while you TRY and save me……I’m going to destroy HIM……Quote the Raven…..Nevermore……”

…….and with that Raven turns and orders the Flock to the back……and while they do leave in unison, you can see the reluctance on the part of Daniels and The Dogs of War…..as the Snake’s words begin to take effect.

Rating: A*

From there we go to a quick cut scene of Chris Jericho and Test standing outside of Mr. McMahon’s office with the boss himself……we’ve obviously missed the bulk of the conversation, but Vince appears to be asking Jericho if he’s sure about something, to which Jericho reply’s that he’s never been surer……Vince shrugs his shoulders, telling him alright, it’s his career……before telling him that he has a deal, as Jericho smiles and walks away, bodyguard in tow.

Rating: N/A

VS.

Winner to be entered into Four Way WWF Light Heavyweight Title Match at Summerslam

Rey Mysterio vs. Chavo Guerrero

Speaking of careers, along with the Light Heavyweight Title, the careers of the four men involved will also be on the line at Summerslam, when Scorpio puts the gold on the line……we already know that Dean Malenko is going to be one of the opponents, it’s time to find out who’s going to be next, when Rey Mysterio battles Chavo Guerrero……

…..this was a decent match considering Chavo’s rather limited appearances here in the WWF (and usually on the loosing end of his matches) they two men showcased the Light Heavyweight division perfectly with their high flying display that really had the crowd on the edge of their seats……..

………at least until the Ayatollah of RocknRolla made his way down the aisle with Test……and there was nothing clandestine about their methods……Jericho simply ordered Test to go……and the big man hit the ring, nailing Mysterio right between the eyes with a running Big Boot that turned the man inside out……although on a brighter note for Rey, the disqualification victory does give him entry into the Summerslam fatal four way…..so maybe he should thank Test…….not right now obviously, he’s unconscious…..maybe when he comes to.

Winner: Rey Mysterio (via Disqualification)
Rating: B

Jericho walks slowly into the ring to a chorus of boo’s with a microphone in hand, where he stands over the fallen Mysterio…..Chavo doesn’t look to pleased at the fact that he’s just been cost a shot at the Light Heavyweight Championship, but a glare from Test soon has him hightailing in to the back…….Jericho says that people are probably wondering WHY he would guarantee Mysterio entry into the Light Heavyweight Fatal Four Way…….well the answer is quite simple……that’s because the fourth member of that match……

”…….IS ME…….see, Mr. McMahon, being the bastion of knowledge that he is, knows full well that Chris Jericho is the BEST light heavyweight there is……the BEST light heavyweight there was……and the BEST light heavyweight there ever will be……..and because of that, I’m going to be the LAST light heavyweight there ever was……

…….Jericho pauses for a moment to let that sink in, before explaining himself……because so confident is he in his abilities……so confident is he that he IS the light heavyweight division……that he’s selflessly done Scorpio, Malenko and Mysterio a solid……because their careers, will NOT be on the line at Summerslam…….only one career will……and that’s Chris Jericho’s……

”…….and it’s all or nothing for me…….Malenko beats Junior here, I’m gone……Scorpio beats Malenko, I’m out……Junior beats Scorpio, I’m out the door…….gone……finito……done…….

…….now I know you morons are probably asking yourselves why…….why would you do that Chris…….well the answers simple, so simple that even you Dallas Dimwits can figure it out…….because if I win…….not only do I become the WWF Light Heavyweight Champion, for the second time I may add……not only do I become the Champ……but the Light Heavyweight Division…….is DONE……GONE……FINITO……RETIRED……and WHY is my career the only one on the line? So Malenko…….so Mysterio…….so every other light heavyweight competitor can sit in the WWF knowing that they can NEVER call themselves the face of the light heavyweight division……because Chris Jericho IS the light heavyweight division…..and none of you monkeys will ever……EEEEEEEVER…..wear that light heavyweight title…..again……”

Rating: A

VS.

Winner to be entered into Four Way WWF European Title Match at Summerslam

Ken Shamrock vs. Goldust (w/Marlena)

So far we know that The Rock will be defending the WWF European Championship at Summerslam against Jeff Jarrett and Matt Hardy…..it’s time to find out who will be the final competitor to place his career on the line in that match, as Ken Shamrock faces off against the Bizarre One, Goldust…….

…….this is a decent little match, with Shamrock showing his acting chops as he sells the disgust at some of Goldusts more unusual antics……Goldie on the other hand seems almost distracted……as if his mind is elsewhere, and with Marcus surprisingly absent from ringside, The Bizarre One keeps checking in on Marlena, asking her if he’s got “it” yet…….quite what “it” is the commentators are unsure…….but it becomes a little clearer when Marcus makes his appearance halfway through the match, running down the aisle glancing nervously over his shoulder……with what looks suspiciously like Scott Steiner’s patented lead pipe in his hand……

…….he tosses the pipe to Goldust, who lets out a little shiver of joy as he catches it…..creeping just about everyone in attendance out as he holds the object under his nose and gives it a long sniff, before turning to face Shamrock…….instead of using the pipe as a weapon however, he appears to be somewhat worshipping it, caressing it with his fingers before blowing a kiss at his opponent as he performs a rather lewd looking act on the phallus shaped object with his hand…….and that’s too much for Shamrock to take…….the man absolutely snaps and charges at Goldust, taking him down to the mat where he flips the man over and locks on the Ankle Lock…..causing Goldust to submit almost instantly…….and while Shammy doesn’t want to let go……and doesn’t appear as if he’s going to let go……he does finally relent just as the official threatens to reverse the decision, before storming to the back, screaming as he goes, as Goldust lays on the mat, still cradling Big Poppa Pumps Big Pipe…..much to the confusion of the commentators.

Winner: Ken Shamrock
Rating: B

VS.

Scott Steiner & Steve Austin vs. Ahmed Johnson & Bret Hart


So with that, it’s main event time here……as once again Scott Steiner and Steve Austin are forced to forge an uneasy alliance against a common enemy, this time in the shape of the unlikely partnership of Ahmed Johnson and Bret Hart…..although in fairness, Ahmed is one of a very select few individuals that the Hitman doesn’t seem to have a problem with……although with Ahmed’s recent history, that’s probably a wise move……

……Austin on the other hand has a problem with everyone in this match, including his own partner, with Steiner and Stone Cold never really settling their differences in a one on one setting…….he still owes Ahmed Johnson an ass whipping after Ahmed’s (admittedly blood loss aided) victory over him a few weeks back…..and as for the Hitman, he and Austin have been feuding in and off for over a year now…..

……we get a preview of sorts for Summerslam here, where Steiner and Ahmed will be battling over the WWF Championship, while Austin and Hart will be battling for pride in a no holds barred match……and while each man gets a taste of his upcoming opponent here, Austin and Steiner spend as much time bickering amongst themselves as they do working over their opponents……it’s been a very promo heavy show too…..so the match didn’t get as long as we would usually afford our main event matches, but it was long enough to get the point across……..

……..and it was long enough for this man to play his part……as Owen made his way down the ring, both distracting the official and discreetly tossing his brother what appears to be a set of Brass Knucks at the same time……and as Austin and Steiner bicker, Ahmed sends Steiner bundling to the outside, while the Hitman lives up to his name, taking out Stone Cold with a shot to the back of the head with the foreign object that he quickly slips inside his trunks…….he slaps on the Sharpshooter as the official turns around, but Austin is in no state to submit……in fact he’s out cold……and when he’s unresponsive to the officials prompts, the ref has no choice but to stop the match.

Winner(s): Ahmed Johnson & Bret Hart
Rating: B+

With Austin down, Ahmed and The Hart Foundation turn their respective attention towards Big Poppa Pump…..who is soon overwhelmed by the three on one assault…..so much so that Ahmed tells Bret and Owen to back off so that he can punish his Summerslam opponent alone……Ahmed is a scary individual as of late, so the Harts don’t need asking twice, as they turn their attention back to Stone Cold, who is slowly coming back around……

……unsurprisingly this Texas crowd is going crazy for Austin, loudly chanting his name in an effort to rally the man……and it works…..sort of…….Austin does indeed manage to fight his way back to his feet and begins rocking Bret and Owen with right hands…….at least until he gets cut off by a chop block from the Hitman……and he’s down again……

…….which is when help arrives in the form of the British Bulldog Davey Boy Smith……who dives into the ring and begins to fight off his former teammates…..he fights a good fight, but again it’s two on one……and when Ahmed sees the intrusion he stops his assault of Steiner just long enough to nail Davey in the back of the head with a stiff Clothesline…..and just like that the Bulldog is down too…….so you would assume things looked pretty bleak for the fan favourites……

……had it not been for the fact that the brief reprise that the Bulldogs arrival had granted Austin was enough for the Texas native to slide to the outside and pick up a steel chair…….which he crashes into the backs of first Owen, then Bret…….and finally Ahmed…..but while The Harts quickly bail to the outside after the first blow, Ahmed is going nowhere……and and after three more strikes with the steel, all Austin has done is back the big bull up against the ropes……..

……..which is when Scotty comes charging across the ring, sending Ahmed over the top and crashing to the floor with a huge Steinerline…….

…….Ahmed looks as though he still wants to fight, but the Harts have had enough and they quickly exit to the back, as the show goes off the air to the sight of a frantic Jim Cornette attempting to convince a p!ssed off Ahmed Johnson to do the same……Summerslam is certainly going to be a heated affair if tonight’s scenes are anything to go by.

Rating: A

Final Rating: B+

WWF Raw is War: Week 2, August 1997

Posted 1 June 2019

Week 2, August 1997

Baltimore Arena (Baltimore, Maryland)

……Scotty turns to resume his assault of Goldust, but the Bizarre One is long gone, disappearing up the ramp and to the back with the assistance of Marlena who had dragged him out of the ring and away to safety…..although just how long he’s going to remain safe is anyone’s guess, because Steiner hops straight out of the ring in hot pursuit…..and as Big Poppa Pump disappears to the back in search of his prey with a face like thunder, Jim Ross remarks that Goldust may have to go to the ends of the earth just to get away from the ire of Scott Steiner…..and judging by Scotty’s behaviour tonight, even that might not be far enough to save him.

Rating: A*

VS.

Kevin Nash vs. Bret Hart


It’s main event time last night, and it’s two men that both had very successful evenings last night, with Kevin Nash not only defeating Shawn Michaels, but sending him packing from the WWF as well….while Bret Hart scored a hard hitting (and assisted) victory over arch rival Stone Cold Steve Austin……and victory here tonight would be the icing on the cake for both men, because Ahmed Johnson has agreed to put his newly won WWF Championship on the line against the winner at Ground Zero: In Your House…..

…..even without the added incentive of a WWF Title opportunity on the line, Bret Hart has been wanting to get his hands Kevin Nash and the rest of the Wolfpac for a while now, with all three members high on the list of people who have “screwed” the Hitman over……and while Bret isn’t wrong, they DID screw him over, Hart’s biggest gripe as of late is that the WWF audience seems to have forgotten all of the Wolfpac’s KLiQ related sins, embracing the Red and Black while shunning the Pink and Black…..

……these two have fought many times over the years….1994, 1995, 1996…..and now 1997…..and while all the matches have been different, the general story has always been the same, with the Hitman attempting to chop the big seven footer down to size, before softening up the leg for the Sharpshooter…..and unsurprisingly this one is no different…..the only difference between this match and all their previous encounters, is the sheer number of men surrounding the ring……Scott Hall, Sean Waltman, Owen Hart, Chris Jericho….Test…..so many people in fact you could almost be forgiven for thinking this was a lumberjack match…..(a match incidentally that you’re unlikely to see during my tenure booking the World Wrestling Federation, because I personally can’t stand them)…..

…….obviously with so many folks on the outside, tempers were always going to be frayed….in fact, not only were they frayed, they outright erupted…..because as Kevin Nash positioned the Hitman for the Jacknife Powerbomb, all three Canadians on the outside stormed the ring, diving on Nash and giving the official little choice but to call for the bell and award Big Sexy the Disqualification victory (and the WWF Title match at the upcoming Ground Zero event.)

Winner: Kevin Nash (via disqualification)
Rating: A

Obviously Hall and Waltman aren’t going to stand by and watch as Kevin Nash is assaulted by four men….and they are quickly into the ring to even out the numbers….although even three against four is hardly an even fight, even if one of those three is Kevin Nash, but the Wolfpac aren’t going to let a little thing like being out numbered make them back down from this fight……Hall and Test pair off, while Waltman and Owen go at it……unfortunately for Nash that leaves him with both Bret Hart and Chris Jericho to contend with…..and while he does hold his own initially, he’s soon overwhelmed, and the Hitman is able to wrap his long legs up, turn him over…..and lock in the Sharpshooter while Jericho stomps away at the back of Nash’s head……

…..when the glass shatters…..this place erupts……and Stone Cold Steve Austin comes running down the aisle…..

…..Austin still has the battle scars adorning his head after being busted wide open last night at Summerslam, but it’s not slowing him down any, as he slides into the ring and instantly drops Jericho with a Stunner, before tackling Bret Hart off Kevin Nash……not to save Kevin Nash you understand, Austin really doesn’t give a damn about anyone, he just wants Bret Hart…..

……he lays into his Summerslam opponent and the man responsible for the blood loss that Austin endured last night, and let’s fly with a rapid barrage of right hands to the skull of the Hitman, before Owen finally drags his brother free, and Test drags the half lifeless Jericho from the ring, before the four Canadians beat a hasty retreat to the back….

……Austin grabs a case full of beer (as Hall and Waltman help Nash to his feet) and mounts the second turnbuckle to chug a few Steveweisers for these fans. Austin hops back down, beer soaked and still hurling insults about the departed Bret Hart, only to turn around and come face to face with all three members of the Wolfpac…..

……Stone Cold certainly had his fair share of problems with the KLiQ during their reign of terror, so it’s a tense few moments to say the least…..until Austin tosses each man a beer (non-alcoholic for Scott Hall obviously) and the four men share a toast before Austin resumes his one man beer bash on all four corners of the ring……..

……but it seems like we still have one more arrival……well, two actually…..because the Owner of the World Wrestling Federation, Vince McMahon makes his way out, with his personal problem solver Kane by his side…..he tells Austin that while he looks to be having quite the time of it out there, getting beer all over his ring, he might want to put his drink down for two minutes and listen……

“……you see, Ahmed Johnson may have told Bret Hart and Kevin Nash that the winner of this match would be granted a title opportunity, but with all due respect, Ahmed Johnson isn’t the boss around here…..I am…….and because I am the boss, I have the power to make matches…..and I have the power to take matches away, and as such that makes a rather unique opportunity for YOU Steve…….see, quite frankly I don’t like Bret Hart……I don’t like Kevin Nash……what I do like however…..is ratings movers…..

……now, thanks to Mr. Nash there, my number one draw, the reason for the ratings Shawn Michaels is no longer employed by the World Wrestling Federation, and as such, that leaves me with both a problem and an opening……an opening that could SOLVE my problem….a spot that needs filling if you will, and there’s no denying that YOU Stone Cold Steve Austin, for reasons known only to these air headed morons in the crowd, YOU are a ratings mover…..so what I’m offering you…..is a spot…..THE spot, as my new Corporate Face……

…..now granted we are going to have to make some changes…..the beer swilling on live tv, the cursing……the clothes……these all have to go…..it’s going to take some time, because frankly, as a diamond in the rough you’re more rough than diamond, but with my expert guidance we could really make you a star that Corporate could be proud of……

……now, I know you’re a bit hot headed…..a bit rash……something else we need to work on……so take a week to think about it, but consider this too……that shot……against Ahmed Johnson for the WWF Championship? That shot could be yours…….and with it the full power of my Corporate Might right behind you…..and should you find Ahmed Johnson to be a bit of a…..’problem’……to beat……..well, let’s just say that I think by now everybody knows what happens, when Mr. McMahon has a problem……think about it Steve……and I’ll see YOU next week…….”

…..and with that, McMahon and Kane turn and head to the back, leaving Stone Cold Steve Austin standing in the middle of the ring with a p!ssed off looking Kevin Nash….and a big decision to make…..by next week……goodnight folks.

Rating: A*
