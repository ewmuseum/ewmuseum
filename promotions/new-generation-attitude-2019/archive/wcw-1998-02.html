WCW Monday Nitro: Week 1, February 1998

Posted 26 October 2019

Week 1, February 1998

Coleman Coliseum (Tuscaloosa, Alabama)

ATT: 15,000

TV: 29.34

Announce Team: Tony Schiavone, Bobby Heenan and Mike Tenay

We are twenty four hours removed from a huge Souled Out event, but with January in the rear view window, it’s time to look ahead…..and ahead, is Superbrawl VIII…..

…..the Titans of Wrestling make their way out to open the show, and (while Savage is sporting a dressing on his head…..a result of his blade job last night) it would seem that they too are looking ahead to Superbrawl, because after spending a few minutes bragging about their victories over Shawn Michaels, Jean Paul Levesque and The Pitbulls (while failing to mention Buff Bagwell’s loss to Lex Luger)they get right down to business…..that business, being the WCW Championship…..

…..Vince Russo (the man with a plan) says that after entertaining Shawn Michaels and Jean Paul Levesque’s ridiculous obsession with the Titans of Wrestling for long enough now (“and after showing them some good old WCW hospitality last night, when Macho and The Warrior showed them who has the real stroke around here”) he says that it’s time to bring the gold home to the Titans of Wrestling…..Russo claims that Adam Copeland and Christian Cage proved that they are the team that should be facing The Forgotten at Superbrawl, while it’s high time that Buff Bagwell got a title shot around here…..so instead of having to worry about that skirt wearing cripple, Rowdy Roddy Piper, he’s going to be facing Eddie Guerrero for the US Title…..and then there’s the small matter of the big one…..

“…..so Sting, sorry bro, but it’s time to take your ass back up into the rafters…..because at Superbrawl…..The Macho Man is gonna take you out…..”

…..which brings out the (equally) battered and bruised Shawn Michaels and Jean Paul Levesque…..guess we should have known that they weren’t going to take last night’s loss well…..

…..Michaels tells Russo that if he thinks for one second that last night proved ANYTHING then he’s got news for him (jack)…..he says that yeah, Warrior and Savage may have picked up the win last night, but let’s be perfectly clear…..

“…..Randy Savage didn’t win squat…..Randy Savage was left in a puddle of his own blood, surrounded by the broken remains of the table that I drove him through…..

…..The Warrior…..HE didn’t win squat either…..because it took him AND four other nimrods to put JPL down…..so you tell me ‘Macho Man’…..do you really think we’re done with you?…..”

…..Shawn says that CLEARLY kicking the Titans ass isn’t going to be enough, so he’s going to hit them where it REALLY hurts them…..

“…..I’m going to take that worthless piece of tin that you people call a ‘World Title’…..that second rate piece of crap, and I’m going to take it back to San Antonio, and you can all sit back and ask yourselves how…..how are you going to justify your so called ‘status’, when the title that supposedly makes you ‘the man’ around here, is around the waist of an ICON that doesn’t even want it…..”

…..Russo laughs at that, telling Michaels that there’s no way that the man who LOST last night is going to be getting a World Title shot…..in fact, the Titans are done with him…..there are no title shots on the horizon…..the best thing that Shawn and JPL can do now, is focus on those two other losers that they’ve been having a problem with…..maybe they should face Diamond Dallas Page and Chris Benoit at SuperBrawl…..

“…..because bro, make no mistake, there’s nothing second rate around here but YOU…..these men right here in the ring, these Titans of Wrestling, wherever they are, that’s the place to be…..whatever they say, that’s the thing to do……and whatever they want, that’s the thing to have…..what they want, is the WCW Championship…..if you DON’T want the WCW Championship, then Shawn, bro, you shouldn’t even be here…..because all that means, is that you’re happy being second best…..”

…..Shawn just smiles at that comment, telling Russo that “I ain’t your bro…..bro…..

…..and as for being second best, even on my worst day there’s not a man in that ring OR in the back there, that even comes close to being able to do what Shawn Michaels can do…..I ain’t never been second best a day in my life and I ain’t about to start now……maybe you want that WCW Title…..maybe I don’t…..but whether I want it or not, there ain’t NOBODY that can stop me from taking it…..”

“…..well, actually…..

…..’actually’ indeed…..because ‘actually’, Eric Bischoff (the head of the WCW Championship Committee) ‘actually’ can…..

…..Bischoff tells Shawn that for what it’s worth, his comments and actions as of late haven’t been making him any friends in the Championship Committee, and as such, he will NOT be getting the World Title opportunity at SuperBrawl…..

…..Russo smirks at this, clearly happy to see that his plans are going down without a hitch…..

…..there is a BIT of a hitch though…..

…..Eric tells Vince that he can wipe that stupid grin off his face…..because not only is Shawn Michaels not getting the World Title opportunity at SuperBrawl, neither is Randy Savage…..

“…..oh, and after their little display last night, neither Adam Copeland nor Christian Cage, Ray Traylor, or ANY of the Freaking Awful Foursome…..in fact, if I had my way, then NONE of the Titans of Wrestling would be competing for WCW Gold again…..

…..BUT…..

…..I do come bearing gifts…..”

…..Bischoff says that the first gift is the gift of opportunity…..see, Shawn Michaels might be a jackass, but he’s also got a point…..HE didn’t lose the match last night, and Randy Savage didn’t win it…..but he’s going to give BOTH men the chance to EARN a WCW Title opportunity…..at SuperBrawl…..because they’re going to face each other one last time…..

“…..and THIS time, one of you will WIN, one of you will LOSE…..and the rest of the Titans of Wrestling will not be an issue…..because we’re going to take a fifteen foot high steel cage…..we’re going to put a roof on it…..and we’re going to have a little two man, one ring War Games match, where the only way to win is by pinfall…..submit…..or surrender……and the winner of that match will go on to Uncensored, to face the WCW Champion…..whomever that may be…..”

…..and ‘whomever that may be’ truly is the question…..because much as he’s loathed to do so, the CURRENT Champion has personally requested his next challenger, and well, the committee (after rejecting his initial request, which was to face Shawn Michaels) has accepted that second proposal…..so after slaying the Giant, Sting’s next World Title contender will be none other than the man that sent him up to the rafters in the first place…..

…..his final betrayer…..

…..the Warrior…..

…..Randy Savage is clearly a little put out about that, but he soon congratulates the Warrior (who barely looks like he’s even heard the announcement going by the neutral expression on his face) before promising Shawn Michaels that he’s going to take him out permanently at SuperBrawl…..

…..Russo says that it looks like we’re going to have an all Titan main event at Uncensored then, because Warrior will crush Sting at SuperBrawl, while Savage will take out Michaels inside that War Games cage…..

…..especially since he’s going to be pretty beat up already by the time she gets there…..because if Bischoff wants to go around booking TITLE matches, then Vinnie Ru is going to book a match tonight too…..the night’s main event…..Mr. Ego, Shawn Michaels…..against Sid Vicious…..

…..good luck with that Shawn…..

Rating: A*

Jim Duggan & The Road Warriors vs. Jacques Rougeau & The Blue Bloods


The first match of the evening is one that was made (in part) at last night’s Souled Out event…..The Road Warriors and Jim Duggan have challenged any combination of the loose alliance of Lance Storm’s Canadians and Steven Regals Blue Bloods to a match tonight…..the Sovereignty accepted the challenge, but declined the opportunity to announce their competitors before hand…..

…..I think Hawk, Animal and Duggan were perhaps expecting both Storm AND Regal to be in the match, but after running down the Americans (and America) briefly, it soon became clear that they would NOT…..

…..instead we had the combination of Jacques Rougeau, Earl Robert Eaton and Duke Douglas Williams…..

…..as you would expect, it didn’t go well for them…..

…..even with Storm, Regal and Oulette remaining on the outside, these American Originals were able to vanquish these Foreign Fanatics, with the youngest man in the match (The Duke) taking the Doomsday Device that gave Duggan and The Warriors the one, two, three…..

Winner(s): Hacksaw Jim Duggan & The Road Warriors
Rating: B

…..as is to be expected, Her Majesty’s loyal subjects don’t take that loss too kindly…..Storm and Regal gather their respective team mates on the floor to regroup, before slowly circling the ring…..

…..Hacksaw already has his 2×4 in hand, so he’s ready to go, and while the Road Warriors are always ready to fight, even they must know that six on three odds aren’t great…..

…..maybe that’s why they have a little backup on stand by…..

…..as Lex Luger (sans Lex Express) comes rushing down the aisle along with Diamond Dallas Page, as the two men slide under the bottom rope and stand side by side with Duggan and The Road Warriors…..

…..Storm and Regal don’t look particularly pleased by this latest development, that’s certainly evened up the sides a little bit…..but odds are odds, and by my count, the Sovereignty still have a man advantage…..

…..Storm tells the men as such too, stating that even if the sides were equal these Americans would have little hope in conquering the United Forces of Canada and Britain, so it looks like we are still going to see that brawl after all…..

…..at least until Hacksaw tells everyone to hold on…..

“…..hold on and wait a minute…..now me and the Road Warriors, we knew that you Boys wouldn’t like the fact that the good old US of A just kicked your butts all over this ring…..that’s why we got two more American Originals out here to watch our backs…..”

…..Duggan stops for a minute, looking a little confused (not much acting required there Jim) as he counts the men in the ring…..finally Hawk puts him out of his misery as he pats him on the back to whisper something in his ear…..

“…..that’s right, that’s right…..see Old Hacksaw, he’s never been accused of being the smartest man in the room…..did I say TWO American Originals?…..

…..because I meant THREE…..”

…..as Stars and Stripes Forever begins to play…..

…..and this man comes rushing out from the back…..

…..The Patriot hasn’t been seen in a WCW ring (or an American ring since he’s been working in Japan for the last few years) in quite some time, but Tony Schiavone sells it like nobody’s business…..and to be fair, the crowd pops too, but then they just want to see America kick these Foreign Cowards asses, so they were bound too…..

…..Patriot stands beside Duggan, Luger, Page and The Road Warriors (that’s a lot of beef) and grabs a microphone from Jim Duggan…..

…..he says that he’s been away for a few years now, but when he heard Lance Storm and Steven Regal continually bash the greatest country in the world, he couldn’t stay away any longer…..

“…..so if you boys want a REAL taste of America, why don’t you all climb in this ring right now, and you can taste our red, white and blue boots getting kicked down your throat…..”

…..Regal is ready…..tally ho…..I guess it’s time to attack…..don’t fire until you see the whites of their eyes old chap…..Storm has other ideas though, telling the rest of the group that now isn’t the time or the place…..and he leads the Sovereignty to the back, leaving the six men in the ring to go through the old “USA…..USA…..USA…..Hooooooooo” shtick, and we head to the back…..

Rating: B+

…..where the Totally Awesome Trinity and Buff Bagwell (the Freaking Awesome Foursome if you will) are with Vince Russo in his makeshift office…..and to be honest, they don’t look too happy…..

…..Savage and Warrior are (for once) nowhere in sight, but then they have THEIR title and/or number one contender matches lined up, so I guess they’re happy…..

…..Copeland asks Russo what’s going on with them…..after all, he and Christian totally embarrassed the Pitbulls last night, why aren’t they getting a shot at the tag team titles?…..

“…..I mean, c’mon…..I thought you were Vince Freaking Russo man, you’re going to let Eric Bischoff push you around? That’s totally lame…..”

“…..totally lame…..”

“…..see, Christian knows…..”

“…..I totally know…..”

“…..who’s even facing the Forgotten at SuperBrawl?…..I mean, Old Man Page looks like he’s got his hands full with those Canadians…..”

“…..Canadians Rock…..”

“…..apart from Chris Benoit…..”

“…..oh yeah….he totally sucks…..”

“…..so who else is there?…..you need to get us that tag title shot Vinnie Ru…..Big Ray-Ray tell him…..”

…..’Big Ray-Ray’ doesn’t tell him…..he does look quite menacingly in Russo’s direction though…..and then there’s Buff Bagwell, who tells Vince that he would have won no questions asked last night had it not been for Rowdy Roddy Piper…..

“…..I already beat that skirt wearing freak once Vince, I don’t need to do it again…..I need a title shot, get me a US Title shot…..”

…..everyone sort of starts talking over each other then….with Copeland even making the (rather blasphemous) claim that there are as many, if not more, Totally Awesome Trinity shirts in the crowd as there are Titans of Wrestling ones, until finally Russo tells them all to…..

…..“SHUT UP!”…..

…..he tells the Foursome that when it comes to making title matches, right now his hands are tied…..as long as Bischoff is the face of the Championship Committee, then they will always have an obstacle to overcome…..

…..Cage wonders out loud then, what would happen if Eric Bischoff WASN’T the face of the Championship Committee…..what if someone took him out of the equation…..what then?…..

“…..I mean, sometimes you’ve totally got to ask yourself…..WWHD?…..”

“…..WWHD?…..”

“…..yeah, What Would Hollywood Do?…..I mean, you totally know he’s going to come back…..you KNOW he’s going to want his belt back…..do you think Eric Bischoff saying no will stop him?…..if you do, then you CLEARLY never followed the fourth demandment of Hulkamania……Train….Say your Prayers…..Totally eat your Vitamins…..and demandment number four, always believe in yourself brother……Vinnie Ru, we believe in YOU…..now go and get us our title shots…..do it for you…..do it for us……but most importantly, do it for Hulkamania dude…..”

…..Russo looks like he’s had about enough now, and he just gets up and walks out of the room, telling the Foursome that he’ll see what he can do…..

…..but he doesn’t look particularly hopeful…..

Rating: B+

…..and we STAY backstage, with a brief clandestine shot through the open door of Shawn Michaels and Jean Paul Levesque’s locker room…..

…..pair are discussing Shawn’s upcoming match with Sid Vicious this evening (“don’t sweat it, I’ve beat the big mook before, I’ll beat him again”) as well as the fact that Michaels has what amounts to basically a one on one War Games match with Randy Savage lined up at the upcoming SuperBrawl event, with a shot at the WCW Title on the line for the winner…..

…..again, Michaels is quite dismissive of the Big Gold Belt, saying that he’s held THE World Title, this is just A title, but it’s a title he’ll take (“just because I can, and just because it’ll p!ss everybody off”) and they shouldn’t stop there…..Shawn tells Levesque that the way he sees it, the only thing that’s going to p!ss these “dubbya see dubbya” inbred rednecks off more than Shawn Michaels holding the WCW Title, is if Shawn Michaels holds the WCW Title……and JPL holds the WCW US Title…..

“…..so let’s go make that happen…..”

…..the pair burst out of the room, with Michaels shoving the sneaky cameraman to one side as they go…..

…..I suppose we should have a match really…..

Rating: B+

Juventud Guerrera vs. Jinsei Shinzaki


…..and it’s a Cruiserweight Match…..yay…..

…..last night all of James Vandenbergs warnings and babbling FINALLY came to fruition…..after trying (and failing) with Tajiri, then trying again (and failing again) with Shinzaki, third time was the charm for our Sinister Minister, as Hayabusa liberated the Cruiserweight Championship from around the waist of young Jeff Hardy last night…..

…..Hayabusa is here tonight, but he’s not in action…..he remains on the outside (with Vandenberg) as Jinsei Shinzaki competes against another young cruiserweight, the masked Youth Warrior, Juventud Guerrera…..

…..I’m sure Vandenberg was hoping to continue his good fortunes here this evening, topping of his man’s victory last night with one for Shinzaki…..

…..he was going to be disappointed…..

…..because whether it was overconfidence stemming from Hayabusa’s victory last night, a lack of preparation by his manager, or simply the fact that on THIS night, Juvi was just the better man, it was Guerrera that came out victorious, finishing off Shinzaki with a 450 splash…..

…..Hayabusa hits the ring to try and avenge his partner, but it’s too little too late, as Guerrera is already halfway up the aisle, making that universal “belt” gesture to his waist…..

…..the new Cruiserweight Champion might have just found his first challenger……

Winner: Juventud Guerrera
Rating: C+

…..we take a quick commercial break, and when we return we find Eric Bischoff and Vince Russo right in the middle of a rather heated discussion…..

…..Russo (off the back of his earlier conversation with his Awesome Foursome) is clearly trying to make the case for Copeland and Cage to get a tag title shot, and apparently, Buff Bagwell to get a US Title shot…..

…..he doesn’t seem to be getting very far with it though…..

…..then Shawn Michaels and JPL wander into shot like a pair of smirking degenerates…..

…..Russo asks them what’s so funny? The fact that they both lost last night? The fact that they bled all over the arena? Or the fact that Randy Savage is going to grate Shawn Michaels pretty boy face into mincemeat on the wall of that cage in a few weeks time?…..

…..Shawn laughs it up, telling Russo that he’s a funny guy…..before asking him just how funny he’s going to find it when Michaels Superkicks his meal-ticket’s teeth down his throat at SuperBrawl, then takes the WCW Title at Uncensored?…..

“…..and while we’re on the subject of title shots, I got your next United States Champion right here, so sorry, but you can go back and tell little Buffy that the mean boys took away his toy, because JPL got his shot…..”

…..Russo tells Bischoff that he can’t be serious…..he gave LEVESQUE the title shot? The guys an asshole who’s done nothing lately other than run his mouth about WCW…..say what you will about the Titans of Wrestling, but at least they WANT to be the champions of WCW…..

…..Bischoff says that no, he didn’t give Levesque the shot…..but he DID give him the opportunity AT the shot…..because he’s going to be facing Curt Hennig later this evening, in a number one contenders match…..

…..Russo is p!ssed, but the match has already been made, so Michaels and Levesque both tell Russo to “Suck It”, before giving him a crotch chop and going on their merry way…..

…..surely then, if Bischoff is willing to grant both Shawn Michaels AND Jean Paul Levesque opportunities to capture gold, then the least he can do is give the former champions (“who never even received a rematch may I add”) THEIR Championship opportunity? Bischoff refuses though…..he tells Russo that he told him last night, no shot for ANY of the Not-So Awesome Foursome after their actions last night…..

“…..so who’s getting the fricking tag title shot then bro? Name me one better team that the former champions, Copeland and Cage?…..”

“…..I don’t care…..I will literally give the title shot to the first team that I see right now…..”

…..both Bischoff and Russo stop and look up the hallway…..

…..where Alex Wright and the Disco Inferno appear to be having a dance off…..Disco is busting out the Saturday Night Fever, while Wright is just throwing out European Techno shapes for fun…..both oblivious to their audience…..

“…..I mean…..the second team that I see right now…..”

…..and the pair turn and look down the OTHER end of the hallway…..

…..where the Nasty Boys look to be smuggling fried chicken out of catering…..

…..Bischoff sigh’s and shakes his head (muttering something about ‘not thinking this through properly’) before telling Russo that “they’ll do”, and chasing after Knobs and Sags, telling them that he has some great news for them…..

…..looks like Russo has got to go back and break the bad news to the Freaking Awesome Foursome…..no title shots for them…..

Rating: B+

We go back out to the arena now, where Mean Gene is getting ready to shill that Hotline one more time…..call 1-900-909-9900right now and choose option 4, where you can hear all about the backstage tensions in the WWF, particularly with one certain “Bad Guy”, who’s been a little bit OF a Bad Guy as of late…..as well as (of course) the full results from last nights Souled Out event…..

…..cheap plug out of the way, Gene gets on with his ACTUAL job, as he calls out his guests right now, WCW United States Champion Eddie Guerrero, and his nephew Chavo Guerrero Jr…..

…..the pair make their way out from the back, and my word does Eddie Guerrero look to be oozing confidence…..and why wouldn’t he be…..because everyone that comes for his title, some how, some WAY, he always finds a way to walk out with the United States Championship around his waist…..

…..Mean Gene isn’t as impressed as Eddie would like him to be though, telling the current US Champion that while he once again walked out of a WCW event with his title in tact, even HE must know that he owes Curt Hennig a big debt of gratitude…..

“…..because in MY opinion, had it not been for the actions of that man, I fully believe we would have a new United States Champion out here tonight…..”

…..Eddie scoffs at that, telling Gene that if that’s what he really thinks, then he clearly wasn’t paying attention to the match last night…..he says that Eddie Guerrero went out there and showed EVERYONE exactly why he’s the Papi of Perfection…..

“…..because hey Rocky, at the end of the day, IT DOESN’T MATTER who you are holmes…..all that matters is who you’re NOT…..and just like everyone knew all along, you’re NOT the WCW United States Champion esè…..Eddie Guerrero is the WCW United States Champion…..all Curt Hennig did, was stop me kicking your ass even longer holmes…..”

…..Gene disputes that claim, but tells Eddie that while he probably wants to continue his celebrations, being the US Champion puts a big bullseye on his chest, and right NOW, both Curt Hennig and Jean Paul Levesque are potentially next in line for a shot at the gold, which means that sooner or later…..Eddie Guerrero’s luck is going to run out…..

…..Eddie says that luck hasn’t got anything to do with it, he says that he’s the Papi of Perfection, not the Grandpapi of Good Fortune…..he says that it’s his god given natural ability, coupled with the generations of Guerrero blood running through his veins that’s kept him as the US Champion for the last six month…..

…..as for Curt Hennig and Jean Paul Levesque holmes, they don’t want any of this…..because they know (just like Hennig already knows) when you step in the ring with Eddie Guerrero, the only thing you’re taking with you when you leave, is the knowledge that no matter what you DO, you’ll never BE Eddie Guerrero…..

“…..Jean Paul Levesque, JPL, whatever you call yourself holmes, you’re nothing more than a glorified lapdog esè…..you went from carrying Shawn Michaels bags up north, to carrying Hulk Hogan’s bags down south, right back to Shawn Michaels again…..and Curt Hennig, I already gave you three shots at this title holmes…..three shots and you couldn’t get the job done…..Eddie Guerrero has the greatest streak in all of professional wrestling, but you Curt, you have a streak all of your own…..a streak of being a LOSER…..”

…..Eddie and Chavo crack up at that comment, but Gene interrupts them, saying that on the subject of streaks, there is only one streak that everyone is talking about, and that’s the undefeated streak of Bill Goldberg…..

“…..and you may THINK that you took the man out (with a series of cheap attacks) but Bill Goldberg still has 16 days to get medical clearance to defend his Television Championship, and from the conversations I’ve had with his physicians, that’s a deadline he’s likely to meet…..

…..and if he DOES show up any time soon, Eddie Guerrero, I wouldn’t want to be in your shoes…..”

…..again, Eddie laughs, he says that Bill Goldberg isn’t fit to lace up his boots…..all these morons in the crowd can chant his name all they want (cue the Gooooooldberg chants) but the fact remains, that Eddie Guerrero has got more experience in his little finger than Goldberg would know what to do with…..

“…..Goldberg, he might be a big boy esè, but where is he now? Back at home with his arm in a sling, watching Eddie Guerrero walk down that aisle…..he’s a big man holmes, so was Rikishi…..he got muscles esè, so did Mr. Rocky…..he’s got a little streak? So has Eddie Guerrero vato…..the only streak that matters……Bill Goldberg, when you come back, you might wanna stay outta the Papi of Perfections way, because there’s a lot of steel chairs in the back holmes…..”

…..hmm…..brave words there from the United States Champion…..smart words? That remains to be seen…..

…..maybe we should have a commercial break…..

Rating: B+

Rick Martel vs. VK Wallstreet


…..and it’s a big one, as The Model takes on IRS…..

…..wait, that’s not right…..

…..as Rick Martel takes on VK Wallstreet…..

…..ok, maybe it’s not the blockbuster match I’m making it out to be, but it has its purpose…..

…..in recent weeks Jimmy Hart has gone about attempting to restart his First Family…..Meng and The Barbarian were the first members (since Jimmy was already managing them) while Konnan was supposed to be the third…..Konnan declined the offer, so Hugh Morrus filled his spot…..not only that, but Hugh Morrus then went on to Souled Out, where he DEFEATED Konnan, thanks in no small part to this man right here, First Family member number four……VK Wallstreet…..an odd choice granted (given VK’s limited success in recent months) but he and Jimmy DO have history, with The Mouth of the South managing the then IRS to the WWF Tag Team Titles with Ted DiBiase……

…..this match here was their first outing as manager and client in WCW…..and it went pretty well…..the match was a decent little midcard match, after all, neither men is spectacular but they’re both solid workers…..in the end though, the First Family paid dividends, as Hugh Morrus returned the favour from last night, interfering on his new stablemates behalf and giving Wallstreet a rare victory…..

Winner: VK Wallstreet
Rating: C+

…..good lord, a win for VK Wallstreet…..better milk that puppy for all it’s worth by bringing Hugh Morrus in to do a little post match beatdown while Jimmy Hart prances around the ring like the annoying little man that he is…..

…..he tells the crowd that Konnan found out first hand what happens when you disrespect the First Family…..you get taken down and you get taken OUT…..just like Rick Martel is about to get taken out right now……

…..it doesn’t look like Konnan is out…..in fact, he looks to be positively fired up as he comes rushing down the aisle to crash the party…..

…..he sends Wallstreet crashing over the top ropes, before nailing Morrus with a series of right hands that stun the big man, before finally levelling him with a rolling clothesline…..

…..then he grabs Jimmy Hart by the scruff of the neck…..

…..he tells Jimmy that he might be known for his mouth, but that big mouth is about to get him in more trouble than he can handle…..

…..luckily for Jimmy, Wallstreet reaches under the bottom rope and grabs his manager by the ankle, yanking him from harms way to the outside of the ring, where the First Trio make their way to the back as Konnan helps Martel to his feet…..

…..and the First Family don’t look happy about this at all…..

WCW World Tag Team Championship Match

The Forgotten (c) (w/Kevin Sullivan) vs. The Public Enemy


…..what the hell did the Public Enemy do to warrant a WCW Tag Team Title opportunity? Not p!ss Eric Bischoff off? Was that it? Answers on a postcard if you know……

…..Levesque corrects Michaels…..telling him that there are at least a couple of prospects in WCW…..a couple of prospects that are in action next…..against someone that (unlike Rocky) Levesque is going to wipe the floor with on Sunday…..

“…..because maybe YOU can’t beat Eddie Guerrero…..but I sure as hell can…..I sure as hell WILL…..and when I do, we’ll see then who the god damn joke around here is, funny man…..”

…..with that, a frustrated Levesque barges past an amused looking Rock, presumably to head off and watch Copeland and Cage (who are in action next) leaving Michaels and Rocky to stare each other down a minute, before Shawn finally smiles and slaps Rocky on the shoulder, telling him that HE really couldn’t care less about The Rock, but JPL, he’s got a real bug up his ass about the so called people’s champion…..he might want to watch his mouth…..

…..AND his back…..

…..not that The Rock looks overly concerned…..

…..let’s go down to the ring…..

Rating: A*

…..where it’s time for tag team action…..but probably NOT the tag team action everyone was expecting…..after all, Copeland and Cage are hardly white hat wearing fan favourites around here…..while the Guerrero’s, well…..they’re not exactly everyone’s heroes either, particularly after their treatment of The Rock and more recently Bill Goldberg…..

…..so who to cheer for? That’s the question…..

…..it would seem like the answer (initially anyway) was Copeland and Cage…..yes they are technically “heels” and yes they are members of the Titans of Wrestling, but in all honesty, they’re pretty entertaining too, so it’s them that the crowd are rooting for in this (actually very good all things considered) match…..

…..or they WERE rooting for them…..at least until Jean Paul Levesque makes his way out…..

…..despite their hatred of all things WCW (and of the Titans of Wrestling) JPL and Shawn Michaels appear to have taken a bit of a shine to Copeland and Cage…..now whether that’s simply a means to an end (like in this match here, where the Totally Awesome Two have agreed to face JPL’s SuperBrawl opponent) or whether it’s simply to mess with the heads of the rest of the Titans (Shawn Michaels SuperBrawl opponent Randy Savage in particular) remains to be seen, but as Big Ray-Ray distracts the official, Levesque rolls into the ring behind Chavo Guerrero and drops him with a Pedigree, before telling Eddie to “Suck It” as he quickly makes his exit…..

…..Cage makes the cover on the fallen Guerrero and unfortunately for Eddie, he’s going into his WCW United States Championship match with JPL off the back of a loss…..that’s not good for moral…..

Winner(s): Adam Copeland & Christian Cage
Rating: B

…..we leave that scene and head to the back, where a nervous looking Mike Tenay is standing by with The Forgotten…..Bam Bam is staring menacingly at the camera (with his WCW Tag Team Title over one shoulder) while Vader barks that he feels no pain (with his WCW Tag Team Title Belt draped over HIS shoulder) as Tenay addresses Kevin Sullivan…..

…..Sully says that Shawn Michaels and Jean Paul Levesque were happily living their pathetic little lives, with nothing to fear but the jeers of these fans…..Shawn Michaels and Jean Paul Levesque were safe…..because they were not on the Forgotten’s radar…..

“…..see Big Van Vader and Bam Bam Bigelow, they were focused on their next victims…..their next examples…..their next night of destruction…..the Forgotten WERE focused on Brian Knobbs and Jerry Sags…..Shawn Michaels and Jean Paul Levesque were safe…..the Forgotten WERE focused on crushing the Nasty Boys……Shawn Michaels and Jean Paul Levesque were safe…..the Forgotten WERE focused on defending their WCW Tag Team Titles at SuperBrawl…..Shawn Michaels and Jean Paul Levesque were safe……then Shawn Michaels and Jean Paul Levesque decided to get our attention…..

…..Shawn Michaels and Jean Paul Levesque are now…..NOT safe…..”

…..Sully says that the Nasty Boys fate is a mere formality, but the destruction of Shawn Michaels next is an absolute certainty…..Bam Bam Bigelow has demanded the Pretty Boy Prima-donna and tonight, he’s going to shut Shawn Michaels up once and for all…..

“…..and if his little friend wants to get involved too…..if Jean Paul Levesque sticks his nose into this match…..tell them what time it’s going to be Vader…..”

“…..:::::!!!!!IT’S VADER TIME!!!!!:::::…..”

…..maybe it will be…..but before that, it’s backstage segment time…..

…..let’s go over to the Totally Awesome Trinity…..

Rating: B+

…..who are celebrating their “totally awesome” win in the back……Big Ray-Ray didn’t have such a good night tonight but he’s happy for his boys…..he even nearly smiled…..nearly…..

…..the Trinity look to be about done for the night (despite The Rock promising that HE wasn’t done with THEM) and are packing up their bags…..

…..that’s when Buff Bagwell comes walking into the room…..

…..the Trinity come to a dead halt when they see Buff (with Copeland and Cage doing a good job of staring at him with their mouths agape) before Christian asks Copeland if he’s totally seeing things?…..

“…..Buff Bagwell? It’s not Buff Bagwell…..”

“…..it’s totally not Buff Bagwell…..Buff is Totally Awesome…..Buff is totally in the Awesome Foursome…..”

“…..not this guy…..we never see this guy…..”

“…..not this guy…..this guy is a total d!ck…..”

“…..a TOTAL d!ck…..”

…..Buff tells Copeland and Cage to cut it out…..he says that they know who he is, he’s the same Buff Bagwell that’s been fighting by their side for month…..but Macho Man told him to come and find them here tonight to tell them th…..

“…..oh talk about a total d!ck!…..Macho Man couldn’t come down here himself?…..”

“…..dude, Macho totally doesn’t work Thursdays…..”

“…..neither does this guy anymore…..”

“…..Buff? No, he’s a total kiss-ass…..”

“…..and a messenger boy…..”

“…..a total messenger boy…..”

“…..what’s the message boy?…..”

…..Buff is clearly annoyed at that, telling the Awesome Two to quit it…..he says that he can see they’re packing and ready to go, Macho sent him here to give them a ride, to make sure that they get to where they need to be…..and where they need to be is at SuperBrawl on Sunday to take care of Titan Business…..

…..Ray Traylor hasn’t been much of a talker recently (Copeland and Cage do MORE than enough talking to make up for that) but he pipes up here, telling Bagwell that they don’t HAVE any Titan Business on Sunday…..they don’t even have a match…..

“…..but we’re supposed to be there to make sure YOU win?…..that HE win’s?…..that WARRIOR wins?…..and he sends his little gopher? I tell you what boy, you go back and you tell Randy Savage that if wants me…..he wants Adam…..Christian…..he wants us to be somewhere, ANYWHERE…..he knows where we are…..tell him to come and tell us himself…..”

…..Buff isn’t happy about that (honestly, I just don’t think he wants to go back and tell Savage what Traylor just said) and it all gets a bit shouty, resulting in Traylor finally grabbing Copeland and Cages bags and pulling all of their gear back out, throwing it onto the locker room benches as he tells Bagwell to get the hell out…..

…..he says that the Trinity WERE leaving…..now they’re not…..Randy Savage wants to know why, tell him to keep watching……he’ll see why…..

“…..now get out…..before I PUT you out…..”

“…..oh, you made Big Ray-Ray mad…..”

“…..you totally shouldn’t make Big Ray-Ray mad…..”

“…..you should probably run…..”

“…..I’d totally run…..”

…..Buff doesn’t run…..but he does leave, telling the Trinity that Macho isn’t going to like this, slamming the door behind him as he goes…..that just leaves Copeland and Cage to look at all their gear thrown all over the room…..

“…..dude…..you totally trashed our stuff…..”

“…..is that my jock?…..”

“…..Big Ray-Ray…..you totally touched Christians jockstrap…..”

“…..we forgive you Big Ray-Ray…..”

“…..we could never be mad at you…..”

“…..what are we going to do now?…..”

“…..I’ve totally got an idea…..”

…..best make it quick then Mr. Copeland…..there’s not long left in the show…..our main event is next…..

Rating: A

…..and it’s like an episode of Monday Night Raw circa 1995 up in here, as Shawn Michaels faces off against Bam Bam Bigelow…..except Shawn Michaels circa 1995 was still a year away from realising that ‘Boyhood Dream’, while Bam Bam of 1995 had just lost to the 123 Kid and Sparky Plugg and was a few months away from getting kicked out of the Million Dollar Corporation…..

…..hardly sounds like a match of the year candidate huh…..

…..what a difference a few years can make…..because in 1998 Shawn Michaels can comfortably call himself ‘The Icon that can still go’, as well as claiming to be one of the longer reigning WWF Champions of recent years……Bammers has been no slouch either…..a former WCW Champion (yikes…..and after defeating Ric Flair too no less) before joining forces with Vader and Sully to form the Forgotten, picking up a tag title reign along the way…..

…..he’s also seriously p!ssed off at Shawn Michaels and Jean Paul Levesque, after the pair cost him and Vader their match this past Monday Night…..Shawn sold well for him as well, as the most despised man in World Championship Wrestling had his ass handed to him in what was actually a pretty good match…..

…..in fact, Shawn received such an ass kicking that JPL had to make his way out from the back to try and even up the odds…..

…..except (true to his word) Vader ALSO made his way out, as he and Sullivan made sure that Levesque kept out of this match…..JPL is a slippery so and so though, so he stays well out of the two Forgotten men’s grips (routinely telling Vader to “Suck It”) and thoroughly distracting the official…..

…..so much so, that as Copeland and Cage (showing us what that ‘idea’ was all about) come rushing out from the locker rooms and crack a chair across the back of Vaders head, he misses The Enforcer sliding into the ring to nail Bammers with that nightstick…..

…..Bigelow staggers (as Levesque throws Sullivan into the safety rails on the outside) and Michaels comes flying across the ring to hit him right in the mouth with some Sweet Chin Music…..

…..and three seconds later, the Totally Awesome Trinity just handed Randy Savage’s SuperBrawl opponent a big victory…..and Macho can’t be happy about that…..

Winner: Shawn Michaels
Rating: B

…..Levesque continues whaling on Sullivan, as Ray Traylor begins to put the boots to Bigelow…..Vader got knocked to a knee by that chair shot, but it doesn’t put him down…..the Con-Chair-To that follows does though, as Copeland and Cage unleash their now signature assault…..

…..Shawn Michaels just watches for a moment, before grabbing a microphone to ask if this is the best that WCW has to offer?…..

“…..the big bad Forgotten don’t look so big and bad to me…..in fact, they just look like another afterthought…..just another example of WCW mediocrity…..”

…..even after that double chair attack, Vader is still trying to get back to a somewhat vertical position, and even Bammers is trying to roll over to the ropes to pull himself back up, so Michaels breaks away from the microphone for a minute to direct traffic, instructing the Trinity to finish what they started…..and remarkably that’s exactly what happens, as Shawn Michaels and Jean Paul Levesque fight side by side with the Titans of Wrestling to make sure that the Forgotten stay down…..

…..finally, with Sully, Vader and Bam Bam down, Copeland, Cage and Traylor join Michaels and JPL in the ring, where Shawn continues his tirade…..he says that in all of WCW, there are very few things that stand out……He is one…..JPL is one……the Totally Awesome Trinity is one…..but they’ve been dogged down and tarnished by Randy Savage and the rest of the Titans of Wrestling…..so much so, that at SuperBrawl, they don’t even have a match…..

“…..well that’s ok boys…..because you can watch how some REAL Titans do it, when JPL becomes Mr. USA and ol’ Mr. Wrestling himself teaches your fearless leader a lesson that he’ll never forget inside that steel cage…..and then…..maybe you’ll have the good sense to tell Randy Savage the same thing we’ve been telling all of WCW for months now…..

…..Suck It…..”

…..with that, Michaels throws down the microphone and raises his arms, as Levesque holds up the arms of Copeland and Cage respectively…..

…..Shawn Michaels has been promising to destroy the Titans of Wrestling for months now…..is he about to do just that, by tearing it down from the inside?…..is he about to do that, without even lifting a finger, simply by feeding the dissension that already exists within the group?…..

…..maybe we’ll find out Sunday…..

…..we’ll see you at SuperBrawl fans…..
