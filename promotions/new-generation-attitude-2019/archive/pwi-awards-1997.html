PWI Year End Awards for 1997

We close out 1997 with a look at the year end awards, and fresh off signing a one year extension with the World Wrestling Federation it’s the 40 year old Bret Hart that takes the prestigious Wrestler of the Year award…..

…..a surprise? Yes and no…..I think I’ve said multiple times during the course of this diary that Bret Hart could have a good match with quite literally anyone, and he has done exactly that throughout the course of the year and wrestling on eleven of the twelve pay per views…..of those eleven matches there was really only one stinker (the Badd Blood triple threat match that saw Ahmed Johnson sustain an injury) seven ‘A’ rated matches and three ‘B+’ rated matches…..the surprising part is that five of those ‘A’ rated matches were Tag Team bouts, seeing he and Owen facing off against Kevin Nash & Scott Hall, Scott Hall & Sean Waltman, Ric Flair & Arn Anderson, The New Age Outlaws and even (surprisingly) The British Bulldog & Jim Neidhart……Bret’s other two ‘A’ rated PPV outings both came in the form of victories over Stone Cold Steve Austin…..

…..so yeah, not a bad portfolio of Pay Per View matches but when you consider that Shawn Michaels (2), Scott Steiner (3) and Brian Pillman (also 3) were all involved in multiple A* matches throughout the year (with Michaels even carrying Marty Jannetty to two ‘A’ rated matches) you’ve got to think that consistency was the deciding factor….after all, Shawn Michaels for example only wrestled one match between August and December…..I guess that contract extension was justified after all…..

…..Company and Tag Team of the Year both come from the land of the rising sun, where NJPW, Keiji Mutoh & Hiroshi Hase took the honours respectivly…..

…..Match of the Year was a bit of an odd one, because it’s a match I don’t actually remember…..an eight man tag match featuring Shawn Michaels, Brian Pillman, Arn Anderson & Ric Flair battling Scott Steiner, Steve Austin, Scott Hall and Sean Waltman on week two of June’s Raw is War…..but then when you look at some of the names involved I guess that should come as too much of a shock…..

…..Event of the Year has quite literally just happened…..Decembers In Your House, entitled Dead Man Walking had some great matches topped off with the reunion of Kane and The Undertaker to close out the Year…..

…..the Titans of Wrestling were represented in both ends of the spectrum, with Christian Cage winning Young Worker of the Year, while Randy Savage won Veteran of the Year…..and it was perhaps the help of the Titans of Wrestling that garnered WCW the award of Most Improved Company of the Year as well……

…..the voices of the year went to Jim Ross and Bobby Heenan, as announcer and colour commentator respectively, while veteran official Earl Hebner rounded up the proceedings, picking up the Referee of the Year award…..

…..interestingly enough (and perhaps in an indication of the business as a whole) despite being more popular in every single region than they were this time last year, and despite having the Wrestler of the Year, Match of the Year, Event of the Year, Announcer of the Year AND the Referee of the Year all under their umberella, the World Wrestling Federation dropped from international to national on January 1st 1998…..they are still the number one company in the world, but that’s certainly caused them to take a hit in both momentum and importance…..the Monday Night Wars just got that little bit more interesting as we head towards a new millennium…..
