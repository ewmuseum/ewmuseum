ECW Hardcore TV: Week 1, March 1998

Posted 3 November 2021

Week 1, March 1998
Highlights from ECW Living Dangerously 1998

Quote:

…..ladies and gentlemen, we’ve left February in the rear view mirror and as we head on into March things are going to get a little more extreme…..a little more perilous…..a little more…..dangerous…..because this is ECW…..and here tonight, in the ECW arena…..we are…..Living Dangerously…..

…..Joey Styles is live in front of this capacity crowd, as he welcomes the raucous ECW fans to the 1998 edition of Living Dangerously…..

…..he briefly runs down the show, including the announcement that tonight we will get to see the match that we DIDN’T get to see last month, as The Sandman puts the ECW Championship on the line against the Hardcore Legend himself, Terry Funk…..

…..a statement that doesn’t sit well with this gentleman…..as Justin Credible cuts Styles off, matching to the ring with the Sandman’s Singapore Cane in one hand…..and a sheet of paper in the other…..

…..obviously Joey isn’t particularly happy at this interruption, asking what the young upstart is doing here…..

”…..didn’t you do enough last week?…..”

…..”last week” being when Credible (and the Philadelphia State Athletic Commissioner Buford T. Pinkerton) interrupted Terry Funk’s challenge to The Sandman, stating that he couldn’t in good conscious allow a man of Terry Funk’s advanced age to compete twice in one evening…..

”…..well Justin, I hate to break it to you, but Terry Funk will only be competing ONCE this evening…..but it will be for the ECW Championship…..”

“…..actually Joey…..no he won’t…..”

…..Credible says that what he has in his hand supersedes any crappy little contract that Terry Funk has for this evening…..because this piece of paper in his hand is a signed mandate from Buford T. Pinkerton…..and on this piece of paper, it states that if Extreme Championship Wrestling wish to run this Living Dangerously event here tonight…..in Philly…..then they will abide by the Committees wishes…..

…..and (after being Piledriven by the Hardcore Legend) Buford has declared that it will be Justin Credible challenging The Sandman this evening (not Terry Funk) and ECW will finally have a champion to be proud of…..

”…..and that’s not just the greatest…..that’s not just the best…..that’s Justin Credible…..YOUR next ECW Champion…..”

“…..hold on…..hold on…..hold on one goddamn minute…..”

…..hold on indeed…..because after being interrupted once already by Justin Credible, it seems like we have another participant in this opening segment…..Paul E. Dangerously…..

…..Dangerously marches down to the ring and climbs through the ropes, snatching the paper out of Credible’s hand as he tells him that he thinks he might have forgotten where he is…..

”…..because this isn’t Titan Towers…..this isn’t the Turner Offices…..this isn’t the WWF or WC f#@king W…..THIS is Extreme Championship Wrestling…..this is ECW, and (as Mr. Pinkerton found out last month) we don’t bow down to ANYBODY…..”

…..Paul E. says that this piece of paper (tearing it in half) isn’t worth a god damn thing here, and if Buford T. Pinkerton wants to come down to the goddamn ECW arena and tell THESE fans that Living Dangerously has been cancelled, then he’d better bring the riot gear…..because he won’t be getting out alive…..

”…..so let me guarantee you something right here, right now…..Terry Funk WILL be competing tonight against the Sandman for the ECW Title…..and so will you…..you see, we have a little tradition around here…..we have a certain match type that we pioneered…..it’s called the Three Way Dance…..

…..so since Terry Funk already has a title opportunity, and since YOU want a shot at the title too, I’m going to give you one…..not because Buford T. Pinkerton said so, but because quite frankly, I want to watch you get your skinny little, buck toothed bald ass kicked…..and Sandman…..Terry Funk…..they’re going to do it…..so f#@k you…..and f#@k Buford T. Pinkerton too…..Joey Styles, here’s your main event…..Justin Credible vs. The Sandman……vs. Terry Funk…..for the ECW Championship…..in a three way dance…..”

…..and that match will be happening…..tonight…..

…..although you won’t actually see it…..

…..at least not for about another three weeks anyway…..

…..sorry about that…..

Rating: B+

Quote:

…..instead, you get to watch Tracy Smothers face off against Steve Corino…..

…..you’re welcome…..

…..Smothers (as you’d perhaps expect) was accompanied to the ring for this one by the rest of the FBI, and while he’s always got a somewhat cocky manner, he’s REALLY confident here…..

…..maybe because he’s a near sixteen year veteran, while Steve Corino is a mere three years into his fledgling career…..

…..unfortunately for Smothers, this didn’t go quite as easily as he was expecting…..Corino might be young and (mildly) inexperienced, but he’s not intimidated by either his opponent, nor his team mates on the outside, twisting Guido inside out with a running lariet on the outside, as well as driving the Big Don into the guard rail with a knee to the small of the back…..

…..damn, the FBI could use a hand here…..

…..cue this gentleman…..former made man (and current wannabe re-made man) JT. Smith…..

…..Smith has made it clear that he wants the Big Don Tommy Rich to re-initiate him back into the Family, but thus far the hapless Smith hasn’t made the best of impressions, often ending up COSTING the FBI matches as opposed to helping them win…..

…..he cost the FBI the match here too…..

…..he comes rushing into the ring with a steel chair which he swings wildly at Corino…..unfortunately Corino ducks and it’s Smothers that eats the steel…..

…..Corino quickly disposes of JT, tossing him over the top rope, then (as Smothers slowly gets back to his feet) he snatches his opponent and drives his skull to the mat with an Implanter DDT for the one, the two and the three…..

Winner: Steve Corino
Rating: D

Quote:

…..Corino quickly gets out dodge as Rich and Guido dive into the ring, but it’s not him they’re looking for, it’s Smith…..

…..Smith has also headed into the ring, looking to make amends with (or at least apologise to) Tracy Smothers…..Smothers doesn’t want to hear it though…..and neither does the rest of the FBI…..

…..the Big Don walks over to Smith and shoves him in the chest, telling him that he needs to stay out of the FBI’s matches, because there ain’t no way that a Mook like him is EVER going to be welcomed into the Family…..

”…..but I tell ya what…..I tell ya what, since you’re too much of a schmuck to take my advice…..and since you keep costing the Family money, tonight…..Little Guido, he’s gonna make sure you’re sleeping with the fishes capiche?…..”

…..with that, the Big Don walks over to Smith, planting a kiss on each of his cheeks….which Smith instantly freaks out about…..

…..Joey Styles on commentary tells us that JT. Smith has just been marked with the Kiss of Death, it’s no wonder he’s not happy about it…..

…..looks like we’re going to be seeing Little Guido carry out the hit when he faces JT. Smith next…..

…..or rather next WEEK if you’re watching this on Hardcore TV…..

…..what? You wanted to see it sooner? Then you should have bought a ticket to Living Dangerously you cheap b@stard…..

Rating: D+

Quote:

…..instead, what you DO get are some promo’s cut in post production…..promo’s for a match that’s actually already happened, as we head to the back where the FBI are discussing Guido’s upcoming match…..

…..it’s nothing groundbreaking, in fact it’s pretty much a rehash of the promo we’ve just seen, with the Big Don telling Smith that they’ve been looking out back and they’ve finally found the concrete shoes they were looking for…..and they’re going to be a perfect fit for the hapless Mr. Smith…..

…..Smothers (who’s since recovered from his defeat) stands behind a snarling Little Guido, alternating between rubbing the smaller mans shoulders and biting his thumb at the camera…..they’re a charming bunch the Full Bloodied Italians…..

…..on the flip side of that we go to another backstage shot, where Smith is in the washroom of the ECW arena, frantically trying to wash the Kiss of Death off his face in a basin…..

…..honestly JT, I don’t think that’s how it works…..

…..Smith tells the screen (and the FBI) that it doesn’t have to be like this…..they can drop the hit…..hell, they can GIVE him a hit to carry out…..he’ll prove to them that he’s Made Man material…..that he’s not just some klutz…..some bad luck charm…..sure, he’s had a few mishaps…..made a few mistakes…..

”…..but that’s all behind me now…..all that bad luck, it’s gone Big Don…..you gotta believe me…..you gotta…..it’s all good for JT. Smith from here on in…..no more mistakes, no more accidents…..”

…..with that Smith turns…..and slips on a puddle of water that’s spilled from the washbasin, losing his footing and banging his head off the basin itself…..

…..no more accidents indeed…..

…..from there we switch to ANOTHER shot…..and another pre (or rather post) taped segment, this time with Steve Corino…..

…..Corino, while clearly happy with his victory, isn’t happy about HOW he achieved his victory…..not because he’s trying to be a rah-rah babyface, but because he says that this is everything that’s wrong with this business today…..

”…..fans would rather cheer a moron like JT. Smith…..applaud failure…..these fans would cheer louder for a fat drunk hitting someone with a Kendo Stick (like THAT takes any talent) than they would watching someone perform the perfect Bridging German Suplex…..fans have lost that Old School mentality…..technique…..skill…..talent…..n ow they’d rather watch a bar fight…..

…..well you won’t be watching any bar fights with Steve Corino…..because I’m bringing Old-School back…..and if you don’t like it…..I’ll wrestle you, come change my mind…..”

…..speaking of wrestling…..

Rating: C-

Quote:

…..this was NOT a wrestling match…..

…..this was a very angry 911 looking to gain a measure of revenge on Shane Douglas’ insurance policy…..the absolutely mammoth Hellraiser…..

…..The Hellraiser made his ECW debut at last months House Party event and he made an instant impression, helping Douglas retain his Television Championship, before doing the unthinkable…..

…..Chokeslamming Beulah straight through a wooden table…..

…..911 has always been the problem solver here in ECW…..someone’s running their mouth, call 911…..match sucks, call 911…..just wanna see some bum chokeslammed, call 911…..well, The Hellraiser might be the biggest problem that 911 has ever had to solve…..and Shane Douglas is out there too just to make that problem even bigger…..

…..this match was horrible…..most 911 matches are horrible in fairness, but this was especially bad…..luckily it was short…..911 got his licks in to start the match but nothing seemed to phase the newcomer, who eventually began to dominate the match…..

…..911 did rally back eventually as these two huge men began to brawl their way to the finish…..a finish that saw Shane Douglas clock 911 in the back of the head with a steel chain loaded punch to the back of the head…..

…..the big man doesn’t go down, but he does stagger forward, walking straight into the open hand of the Hellraiser, who picks him up and Chokeslam’s him to the mat, handing 911 a rare pinfall loss…..

Winner: The Hellraiser
Rating: D-

Quote:

…..not satisfied with that, Douglas tells Hellraiser to do it again…..and do it properly this time…..

”…..do it like you did that little b!tch Beulah…..”

…..happy to oblige (or happy to take orders at least) Hellraiser climbs to the outside and picks up a wooden table (conveniently) located under the ring…..he slides the wood under the bottom rope and then gives 911 a few stomps in between setting the table up…..then he picks up the big man…..

…..and Chokeslams him straight through the wood…..

…..which is when Tommy Dreamer’s music hits (Man in a Box…..f#@k you copyright laws) and the man himself walks out from the back…..

…..very calmly actually, considering what happened to Beulah…..

…..he doesn’t rush to the ring to meet a two on one onslaught…..he doesn’t do his best to fight the overwhelming odds…..no, he’s going to wait for that…..

…..wait until Shane Douglas accepts a match that is…..

…..Douglas scoffs at that, telling Dreamer that for one, he’s already beat his ass…..and his woman’s too……and for two, Douglas already has a match tonight…..

”…..and I don’t even know who it’s against…..see the brass back there, they want to punish me for what my Hellraiser did to Beulah last week…..apparently Chokeslamming women through tables is frowned upon all of a sudden…..but one thing they HAVE told me, is that my opponent isn’t YOU…..”

…..Dreamer says that he’s not looking for a title match, he just wants a fight…..and since Tommy knows that Douglas is only going to hide behind Hellraiser anyway, why don’t they make it a tag team match…..

”…..because after what that big son of a b!tch did to Beulah, I’m gonna tear his head off too…..”

…..Douglas isn’t going for it though…..and why should he, he has a title defence to worry about…..but, out of curiosity (since they already took out 911) who’s his partner?

…..Tommy just smiles and points to the back…..

…..as the rookie Chris Chetti makes his way out…..

…..and Douglas is in hysterics…..

…..he asks Dreamer if this is the best that he can do? A rookie that Shane beat recently anyway? This is the best he has to face off against them?…..

”…..you know what Tommy, what the hell…..you’re on…..Hellraiser could beat both you bums on his own, I won’t even have to break a sweat…..you want a match, you got a match…..bring your dumb asses down here so that Hellraiser can Chokeslamming you like he did your old lady…..”

…..another impromptu match that’s happening next? You know what that means…..we’ll see this match happen NEXT week…..right here on Hardcore TV…..

Rating: C

Quote:

…..which brings us to our main event…..and in reality it’s a pretty big one…..

…..Rob Van Dam and Taz have been circling each other on and off for a few months now (although Taz has certainly been the more vocal of the two, challenging Van Dam a number of times) and tonight is the night they finally cross paths…..

…..Van Dam has only been here since January, but already he gives all of his bouts that “big match” feel with his arrogance alone and (with Bill Alfonso and his f#@king annoying whistle at ringside) these fans are ready to see Taz tear the cocky RVD apart…..

…..Alfonso will be hoping that his current client has more luck than his previous one, because Sabu has been on the shelf with a broken neck for the best part of a year now following his last meeting with the Human Suplex Machine, which is exactly why Fonzie is dead set on Van Dam picking up the win here…..

…..these two men had a hell of a match too…..

…..Rob Van Dam is perhaps the most flexible son of a b!tch in wrestling today, so when Taz tosses him with those numerous suplex variations, RVD sells them all like a superstar, regularly getting folded up like an accordion…..likewise, Taz actually sells for his opponent (which is a rarity in itself) taking Van Dam’s unique offence, including getting dropped on the safety rail for Van Dam to deliver that spinning leg drop from the apron…..

…..back in the ring the two men nail each other with chairs, strikes, kicks, suplexes and punches, with neither competitor gaining a prolonged period of offence…..even Fonzie gets in on the action, getting his lights punched out by Taz…..

…..then Taz locks on the Kata-Ha-Jime…..and with RVD refusing to tap out, it looks li,e we could get another broken neck here…..

…..until the lights go out…..

…..and then come back on…..

…..to reveal the Homicidal, Suicidal, Genocidal Maniac that is Sabu standing right there in the middle of the ring…..

…..the crowd pops for that…..and so they should…..it’s been over twelve months since Sabu was seen inside an ECW ring, but he’s here now…..and he cracks a steel chair over the head of the man who put him on the shelf…..

…..that releases the choke that Taz had on Van Dam and also leaves the Human Suplex Machine open to an out and out assault from Sabu…..

…..Alfonso blows his whistle furiously, encouraging Van Dam back to his feet as the two men go about putting the boots to Taz, before finally they put him down for good with a moonsault through a table from Sabu, followed by a Five Star Frog Splash from RVD…..who makes the cockiest cover known to man for the one, two, three…..

Winner: Rob Van Dam
Rating: B

Quote:

…..Sabu has a lot of pent up aggression to get out of his system though…..so it’s perhaps no surprise that he’s not happy with just costing Taz a victory…..

…..Rob Van Dam IS happy to just celebrate his victory though…..standing there posing as Sabu continues to put the boots to the Human Suplex Machine, until he finally decides to join in…..

…..Fonzie climbs into the ring with a chair and (as Sabu holds the man in place) he holds in in front of he face of Taz, which RVD in turn kicks straight into the skull…..

…..that’s still not enough for Sabu though…..and he opens up the chair just enough to slide it over the head of Taz, before climbing up to the top rope…..

…..Fonzie hands Sabu a second chair (as Van Dam ensures Taz remains on the mat) and Sabu leaps, landing straight on the head (and in turn, the chair) with the Arabian Facebuster, which should realistically here be called the Arabian Neckbreaker…..because Taz looks to be in serious trouble…..

…..Styles has an “Oh My God” moment as he ponders whether that chair that was placed over the head of Taz has indeed broken the neck of the Human Suplex Machine, before condemning the trio of Bill Alfonso, Sabu…..and Rob Van Dam…..as the trio pose over the fallen body of Taz…..

…..we’ll see you next week for more action from Living Dangerously 1998, right here on Hardcore TV…..

Rating: B

Final Rating: C+

ECW Hardcore TV: Week 2, March 1998

Posted 18 November 2021

Week 2, March 1998

Highlights from ECW Living Dangerously 1998

Quote:

…..the show opens tonight, not with introductions or promos or anything of that kin…..instead it starts right inside the ring, as Tommy Dreamer looks to gain a measure of revenge on the two men responsible for hospitalising Beulah, as he takes on Shane Douglas and the enormous Hellraiser…..

…..on paper Tommy’s partner is an odd choice…..a relatively unknown rookie with more losses under his belt than wins, including a loss to Shane Douglas himself…..however, Joey Styles remarks that this is perhaps the exact reason Dreamer chose him…..

…..he suggests that while Chris Chetti has shown remarkable upside (even gaining that rarest of things recently, the respect of Taz in defeat) Shane Douglas surely sees him as a weak link…..and since there was no chance Douglas was accepting a fair fight with a p!ssed off Tommy Dreamer, he was happy to accept an UNFAIR fight…..while Tommy, he just wants to get his hands on Douglas and Hellraiser, and doesn’t care how badly he gets hurt in the process…..

…..which is lucky…..

…..because he gets hurt pretty bad…..

…..this match is a match of two styles…..Dreamer and Chetti have a more “wrestling” based match, while Dreamer and Hellraiser just beat the holy sh!t out of each other, all over the arena and high up into the balcony areas…..both men are busted open by the time they arrive, and for one horrible second it looks as though Hellraiser is going to Chokeslam Tommy from high up in the building, straight down to the floor…..

…..he wraps his hand around the throat of his opponent and positions him over the edge…..Tommy though, he just kicks the man square in the balls, before letting out a loud ”E.C f#@king W” and tossing the man over the edge and down to the floor below, sending him through a stack of tables in the process…..

…..he makes his way back to the ring, where he grabs two steel chairs (handing one to Chetti) as the two men circle a terrified looking Douglas…..

…..who then smiles…..

…..and raises three fingers…..

…..as Chetti crushes the chair over the top of Tommy Dreamers head…..

…..Chetti tosses the chair to the mat as Douglas drags Tommy back to his feet, before delivering a belly to belly on the steel for the one, two, three……

Winner(s): Shane Douglas and Hellraiser
Rating: C+

Quote:

…..so there it is…..Shane Douglas had been teasing the reformation of the Triple Threat for weeks now and it looks like that tease has finally come to fruition as Chris Chetti joins Douglas in beating the crap out of Tommy…..a fact that Joey Styles is appalled by…..

”…..as Chris Chetti, a man with unlimited potential, just sold all that potential down the river to Shane Douglas for a cheap buck…..”

…..Hellraiser took a hell of a fall earlier, but this is ECW dammit…..so he’s back on his feet now and joins his two partners in the ring…..the three men briefly stomp the snot out of Tommy, before Chetti finally pulls him to his feet and holds him in place, as Douglas wraps a chain around his fist and nails Dreamer right between the eyes…..

…..Tommy is basically done, but the Triple Threat aren’t, as Shane sends his young protege to the outside, telling Chetti to go fetch a table ”so we can do him like we did his old lady”…..

…..and that’s exactly what they do…..Chetti sets up the table, Hellraiser Chokeslams Tommy straight through it…..and the Triple Threat stand tall (three fingers raised) as Shane Douglas cackles on, leaving Joey Styles to muse that…..

”…..Shane Douglas, I don’t know who you’re defending that TV Title against later…..but I for one hope they kick your ass…..”

…..let’s go to some promos…..

Rating: B+

Quote:

…..promos that open up with the Dudley Boys…..ALL of the Dudley Boys…..including D-Von…..which looks to scare the ever loving p!ss out of Chubby, Sign Guy and Dances with, all of whom somewhat cower in the background as D-Von begins a rant…..

”…..because after trying and trying to make this band of brothers something befitting Big Daddy Dudleys name, finally something is paying off…..because while for these three fat f#@ks here, the best part of Daddy Dudley’s seed dribbled down their momma’s thighs, Buh-Buh and Big Dick…..they’re finally starting to think like Dudleys…..”

…..D-Von says that Balls Mahoney and Axl Rotten, they’re about to find out one fundamental fact when they meet in that ring…..

”…..thou shall not f#@k with the Dudleys…..come on my brothers…..”

…..with that D-Von jovially slaps both Buh-Buh and Big Dick on the chest (before slapping Chubby Dudley less jovially around the back of the head) before walking off, with Buh-Buh and Big Dick following semi reluctantly behind him…..

…..Chubby, Sign Guy and Dances with watch their brothers leave (as Buh-Buh looks on apologetically) before the trio discuss what to do next…..with Sign Guy having the final “word”, holding up a sign with four words written on it…..

”…..call the little guy…..”

…..who’s the little guy? Who knows…..for now let’s go over to everybody’s favourite moron…..

…..JT. Smith…..who’s pacing the locker room like a man with a lot of his mind…..

…..he said that he’s been coming out here week in and week out, trying to do right by the family…..and all it’s gotten him is the kiss of death from the Don (and a match with Little Guido which is coming up next week) well enough is enough…..

”…..because whether the Don likes it or not, I am a made man…..I’m not a joke…..I’m not a jinx…..I’m the man the family needs…..so if I have to fight my good, close, personal friend Little Guido to prove that, then what’s what I’ll do…..

…..but when I do that…..when I beat my main man Guido, Don, I want the hit called off!…..I want the hit called off and I want my Made Man status back…..because I’m about to prove that I’m not a problem, I’m a problem solver…..and the family needs me…..”

…..now all fired up, JT takes a step back and slams door the door right in the camera mans face…..except he does it with such force that it swings right back open and smacks him right between the eyes…..

…..yeah…..you’re definitely not jinxed JT…..

…..let’s get back to more action from Living Dangerously…..

Rating: C

Quote:

…..where it’s time for Val Venis to take on Stevie Richards…..and as always, the crowd is VERY happy to see the arrival of Miss Jenna Jameson in the arena…..

…..Val matches down the ring with his favourite co-star on his arm, grabbing a microphone to let his feelings known…..because…..

”…..hello, ladies…..

…..you know, the Big Valboski is here at Living Dangerously…..and I can’t think of a better place to announce my latest movie…..because it’s closely inspired by a man who’s always living dangerously…..James Bond himself…..and just like in Tomorrow Never Dies…..the Big Valboski is locked….cocked….loaded….and ALWAYS packing heat…..and while Bond is a Double-O Seven, the Big Valboski is a Double XL (if you know what I mean)…..he’s always surrounded by Pussy Galore…..and he ALWAYS has a Licence…..to thrill…..

…..because while Tomorrow Never Dies for Bond…..when Val Venis and Jenna Jameson get in front of those cameras…..Tomorrow Always Comes…..and Comes…..and Comes again……”

…..Jenna riles up those red blooded men in the crowd, before finally saying to send out Stevie and the Super-Virgins…..so that her man can make this short…..and sweet…..

”…..because judging by those tight little pants of yours…..Stevie honey…..SHORT is something you know a lot about…..”

…..Jenna wiggles her little finger at the entrance way (implying that Stevie is less than blessed in the trouser department) taunting the trio as they make their way out…..

…..let’s go to the match…..

Rating: C+

Quote:

…..outside of his gimmick, Val Venis is probably more suited to a ‘sports entertainment’ style company than an ECW type company…..but we’ve all got to start somewhere right…..granted the fact that he has Jenna Jameson at ringside certainly helps, but still…..this isn’t your typical (arena wide brawl) ECW type affair…..

…..it’s actually a decent enough match to be fair…..Val is still pretty green, but capable enough…..while outside of the comedy character, Stevie is a pretty decent worker too…..it definitely benefited from the dog and pony show that having Jenna, Nova and Meanie at ringside provided…..

…..both outside men interfered at times, but Jenna dropped them both (at separate occasions) with her signature move (the nut shot) and she was there for the finish too…..

…..Stevie tuned up the band and let loose with that Stevie Kick…..Val catches the foot though, and with Dancing Stevie turning into Hopping Stevie, Jenna climbs into the ring and punches him square in the exposed balls…..

…..Stevie goes down…..Val goes up…..and one Money Shot later, this one is all over…..

Winner: Val Venis
Rating: C

Quote:

…..we go to a pre-recorded segment with Taz now, fresh off his loss to Rob Van Dam, the return of Sabu, and the subsequent beat down that went along with it…..

…..he’s sat in a dark room, tatty black towel over his head, and what small part of his face that we can see looks to be pretty ticked off…..

…..he says that RVD and Bill Alfonso were already dead men walking BEFORE they conspired to bring back Sabu (which we saw on last weeks show) but now…..now they ain’t dead men walking anymore…..

”…..because believe me when I say, there are much worse things than death…..and I’m going to make sure that both of you jackasses get to experience every single one of them…..and Sabu…..you made one very serious mistake brother…..you left me breathing…..Rob Van Dam, Bill Alfonso…..they’re gonna know pain…..but you…..you’re going to have to face me sometime soon…..and when you do, you’re going to be begging me to put you out of your misery…..you and me Sabu…..one more time…..beat me if you can…..survive…..if I let you…..”

Rating: B

Quote:

…..it’s back to more action from Living Dangerously now…..and it’s title action…..tag team title actually, as the Eliminators put the ECW Tag Team Titles on the line (titles that they have held for nearly nine months now) against a relatively new team to ECW…..The Vampire Warrior and The Fallen Angel (along with Luna)…..

…..Perry Saturn and John Kronus had a huge boost in name value after getting an opportunity to perform live on a WWF Pay Per View last year, and while The Vampire Warrior is still somewhat of an unknown, Christopher Daniels stint as a member of Raven’s Flock (not to mention his brief spell as WWF Light Heavyweight Champion) means that even as a midcarder by the big two’s standards, he’s a massive name here…..

…..he’s also a decent worker too…..as is Perry Saturn…..so the ECW crowd really got a twofer here…..with Daniels and Saturn wrestling in the ring, while Kronus and Vampy brawled (and bled) all over the ECW arena…..

…..the Eliminators certainly have the experience edge, being that they are the established team…..and that team work is evident numerous times, particularly when they nearly broke Vampy in half with a Total Elimination…..they would have got the win too…..had Luna not dived into the ring to level Saturn with a low blow from behind, tossing him out of the ring (yes, she’s a bad M’Fer) as Daniels leaps to the second and then the top turnbuckle, crashing down onto the back of Kronus as he makes the cover with a triple jump moonsault…..

…..that breaks up the pin fall…..

…..Luna slides a chair into the middle of the ring…..

…..Daniels drags Kronus back to his feet…..

…..Angels Wings right to the steel chair…..

…..and one, two, three seconds later…..

…..the Eliminators seven month title reign is over…..

Winner(s): …..and [New] ECW Tag Team Champions…..The Brood…..
Rating: B-

Quote:

…..there’s no fancy angle to close out the show this evening, we simply go over to Paul Heyman’s parents basement (or Studio ECW if you will) where (with an ECW banner hiding Mrs. Heyman folding laundry) Joey Styles talks briefly about the title change we just saw, stating that…..

”…..for seven long months, The Eliminators were THE team to beat around here in ECW…..but they were also the team that NOBODY could beat…..until one team did…..The Brood…..and god have mercy on the souls that get sold trying to beat Satan’s own pairing of The Vampire Warrior…..and The Fallen Angel…..”

…..Styles then quickly runs over the matches still to come from Living Dangerously…..including next weeks offering of JT. …..okay, so here we are…..it’s main event time, as Terry Funk finally gets the shot he wants at the ECW Championship, even if it’s not in quite the manner he envisioned…..

…..Justin Credible rode into the land of Extreme only a short while ago, but he’s already made a big impression and shot straight to the top of the card with his actions (coupled with the fact that he’s insanely over by ECW standards thanks to his previous association with the KLiQ over the course of last year) and in his mind, all that remains is to capture the title that will make him the man around here…..

…..as for the Sandman…..well, he just wants a fight, a beer, and a cigarette…..so this is a standard evening for him…..

…..on paper, it was a stroke of genius by Credible to get this match changed to a Ladder Match at the last minute…..he’s easily the most athletic of the three, the fastest of the three, definitely the least broken down of the three…..the biggest problem he’s going to have, is that his opponents are certifiably insane…..and he’s just handed them a huge metal weapon to beat on him with……

…..and beat on him they do…..

…..as soon as the bell rings the ladder aspect to this “match” goes right out of the window for Sandman and Funk, because they go straight for Credible, brawling around ringside before finally tossing him into the crowd as they beat the snot out of the 24 year old upstart…..

…..eventually Funk and Sandman get bored of abusing Credible and turn their attention to each other, and Credible’s game plan becomes clear as he bides his time and waits for his two opponents to be occupied with each other, before attempting to climb that ladder, getting close to snatching the title a number of times, but getting stopped in the nick of time on each occasion…..

…..they go through a couple of ladders here too…..Sandman lays one between the between the apron and the guardrail, laying Credible on top before leaping off the turnbuckles with a leg drop that breaks both the ladder AND his opponent in two…..

…..Funk goes to an old faithful of his as he places a ladder on his head and spins around like a deranged helicopter, smashing the steel into the faces of both Credible and Sandman…..

…..Sandman drops a trash can over the head and then begins beating the holy hell out of it with a Singapore Cane…..Credible again tries to use this opportunity to climb the ladder to retrieve the title, but Sandman realises at the last second and rolls into the ring, upending the ladder and sending Justin flying from high above the ring and crashing down through a table on the floor that puts him out of commission for some time……

…..Funk and Sandman continue to beat on each other while Credible is down, culminating in Funk piledriving the Champion through a table on the outside…..

…..Terry takes his time sliding a ladder into the ring, and by the time he’s done setting it up his two opponents are beginning to pull themselves back to their feet on the outside…..Funk sees this and repositions the ladder by the ropes, climbs up…..and like the crazy old man that he is, hits a moonsault from the ladder inside the ring, down to the floor, and on top of his two opponents…..

…..all three men are bleeding like crazy…..Funk is first back to his feet and heads back inside the ring…..he positions the ladder and climbs, and is finger tips away from the gold when the Sandman climbs up to meet him…..the two men trade blows at the top, before Funk finally gets the upper hand, hitting Sandman with a series of headbutts, before shoving the champion backwards off the ladder…..

…..Funk takes a moment to regain his senses (what’s left of them anyway) before again attempting to reach for the title…..Credible is back in the ring and climbing the ladder though…..

…..and he has a Singapore Cane wrapped in barbed wire in his hand…..

…..he reaches the top and is met straight away by the fists of the veteran, and again Funk goes for the heatbutt that worked with Sandman…..it does shake Credible, but it knocks Funk silly too after only performing the same series of blows a few moments ago…..

…..Credible lays the barbed wire cane across the top of the ladder and slams Funk face first into it a few times, knocking him even loopier, before snatching up the weapon, winding back, and nailing Funk full force between the eyes with the barbed wire weapon…..

…..Funk wobbles…..then falls backwards with a flat back bump to the mat…..

…..Credible reaches up…..

…..unhooks the title…..

…..and we have a brand new ECW Champion…..

…..and that’s not just the coolest, that’s not just the best……

…..that my friends…..

…..is Justin Credible…..

Winner: and New ECW Champion…..Justin Credible
Rating: B

Quote:

…..Credible is a broken and bloody mess, but Buford T. Pinkerton is out there quick as a flash to help his hand picked representative to his feet…..

…..the crowd are far from pleased and (this being an ECW crowd) are very vocal about their displeasure…..Buford and Credible could care less though, as they celebrate the victory…..

…..they are somewhat rudely interrupted by the arrival of one Paul E. Dangerously though, who comes walking out with microphone in hand…..

…..he tells Buford that since the event is now over, he can tell him exactly how he feels about him without danger of closure and denying this crowd the event that they paid to see…..

”…..and how I feel about you ‘sir’…..is that you’re a fat…corporate…bureaucratic piece of sh!t…and the only thing stopping me from coming down there and slapping the crap out of you myself, is that we still need to hold events in your state…..

…..but believe me when I say, you can’t protect your chosen little pet project there indefinitely…..

…..in FACT…..

…..next month…..

…..you won’t be able to protect him at all…..

…..because we will be holding a little event on pay-per-view, called Wrestlepalooza…..

…..and the Pennsylvania Athletic Committee won’t have to worry about it being too violent…..too hardcore…..too extreme…..

…..because it will be coming from the Elks Lodge…..

…..in New York City, New York…..

…..so you sir….

…..can respectfully….

…..kiss MY New York ass…..”

…..Credible and Buford aren’t happy about this development, particularly when Dangerously tells them that if they thought tonight was hardcore, just wait and see how extreme things are going to be next month…..

”…..because I have an entire locker room waiting to make you bleed like the little b!tch that you are…..

…..good luck ‘champ’…..

…..you’re going to need it…..”
