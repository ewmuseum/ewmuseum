A New Generation…..a new attitude? Pay-per-view

WWF Royal Rumble 1997

Posted 25 May 2019

The blog’s images are not served; the posts’ text is given whole, as the author wrote it.

Week 3 January, 1997

Jack Murphy Stadium; San Diego, California

ATT: 50,918

Buy: 1.57

Welcome everyone, it’s time for the most exciting fast paced event of every calendar year….it’s time for the first event of THIS calendar year…..it’s time to find out, who is going to the grandest stage of all……it’s time to Rumble……it’s time…..for the Royal Rumble…….

……and as the graphic finishes playing, highlighting all the entrants in tonight match…..as well as the three possible entrants in Bret Hart, Owen Hart and Scott Steiner……we head down to ringside, where Jim Ross and Ravishing Rick Rude welcome us to the show…..they run down the card briefly, before each man makes his pick for tonight’s Royal Rumble match…..Rude says that coming in at number 30, Kane is a dead cert to walk out of this one the winner……while Jim Ross says he believes that Stone Cold Steve Austin has what it takes, to do a Shawn Michaels….come in at number one, and win the whole thing.

Rating: N/A

VS.

WWF Intercontinental Championship Match

Scott Steiner (c) vs. Curt Hennig (w/Billy Gunn & Jesse James


It’s time for the first match of the evening…..and we are starting with the Intercontinental Championship on the line. Rarely is losing his Title the LEAST of a Champions worries here in the WWF…..but tonight, if Scott Steiner loses the gold, he loses everything that he’s been working towards since June of 1996…..because if Scott Steiner loses the Intercontinental Championship to Mr. Perfect…..he also loses his spot in tonight’s Royal Rumble match…..which means he ALSO loses the opportunity, to write a wrong that dates back seven months now. King of the Ring 1996, Scott Steiner defeated Shawn Michaels, right in the middle of the ring to capture what he THOUGHT was his first WWF Championship…..only to have his dream snatched away just 24 hours later, in a loop-hole conjured up by one Vincent Kennedy McMahon……and if you believe Big Poppa Pump, come Wrestlemania, he’s going to shove that loop-hole straight up Shawn Michaels ass……

…….but to do that, he needs to pick up a victory here…..against a man no stranger to wearing the prestigious Intercontinental Championship, wearing the gold twice between 1990 and 1991….and that man’s name is Mr. Perfect. You could be excused for believing that Perfect is stretching himself a little thin here right now, not content with simply managing the career of Billy Gunn…..and subsequently Jesse James…..the itch of competition has also compelled him to lace those white boots up for another run between the ropes……and to be fair, since his return back at the Survivor Series, his record is exactly what he claims to be….absolutely perfect…..of course, the fact that he is often accompanied (as he is tonight) by his two wards certainly helps matters……and both Billy Gunn and Jesse James make their presence felt tonight, giving Perfect ample opportunity to work over the Champion……

……they are also prevalent in the ending of the contest….because once Perfect has decided that Steiner is suffered enough, he sets Big Poppa Pump up for his patented Perfect-Plex…..except there was two problems with that…..Steiner wasn’t beat up enough…..and the pair were two close to the ropes….meaning that before the Challenger could apply the move, Steiner hooked him with a Northern Lights Suplex that sent Perfect sailing over the top rope and crashing down onto Gunn and James. Steiner follows them to the outside where a pair of Overhead Belly to Bellys on the floor sees the entourage taken care of…..before the Champion rolls Perfect back into the ring……applies the Steiner Recliner……and just like that, that Perfect record, has a big Scott Steiner shaped blemish on it.

Winner: and Still WWF Intercontinental Champion…..Scott Steiner (via Submission)
Rating: B

With the first half of his night completed, Steiner heads his way up the aisle….scouting for potential Freaks in the crowd as he goes…..when he is cut off by Dok Hendrix looking for some comments on his chances later on now that he has qualified for the Rumble match itself….

“……..MY CHANCES?…….THERES NO CHANCES DOK……..JUST SCOTT STEINER, DOING WHAT SCOTT STEINER DOES BEST…..THATS STARTING STRONG……..AND FINISHING HARD……..AND JUST LIKE ALL MY FREAKS WILL TELL YOU…..BIG POPPA PUMP…..ALWAYS LEAVES YOU SATISFIED AND BEGGING FOR MORE…….SO WHEN THE NIGHT IS OVER, AND SCOTT STEINER HAS FINISHED HIS MÉNAGE ET TROIS-NTY NINE……SHAWN MICHAELS IS GOING TO FIND OUT EXACTLY WHY YOU DONT P*SS OFF THE BIG BAD BOOTY DADDY, WHEN I GO TO WRESTLEMANIA, SHOVE MY FIST UP HIS ASS…..AND WORK HIS MOUTH LIKE A PUPPET……BECAUSE SCOTT STEINER WILL BE YOUR NEXT WORLD WRESTLING FEDERATION CHAMPION…..HOLLA, IF YOU HEAR ME…….”

Rating: A

From Scott Steiner’s reaction at ringside, we go backstage, where Jerry Lawler is standing by with Vince McMahon…..who doesn’t look happy that Scott Steiner is now a part of the Royal Rumble match……he points to Steiner’s post match promo as the exact reason that Vince McMahon did not want Big Poppa Pump representing his organisation as the WWF Champion……a lewd, crude…..uneducated musclebound moron……..

…..Lawler tells Vince that things may go from bad to worse later…..because with Kevin Nash’s frame of mind following Monday night, it might be the perfect opportunity for the Hart Foundation to pull a victory out of the bag……and then it won’t just be Austin, Pillman and Steiner that have been added to the Royal Rumble……because Bret and Owen will be there too……

…….McMahon doesn’t look too concerned however…..saying that as far as Kevin Nash is concerned, money talks…….and Vincent Kennedy McMahon, always has a back up plan……

Rating: B+

VS.

The Legion of Doom vs. Edge & Christian


Edge and Christian have only been an onscreen part of the WWF for a little over a month now…..but they have made quite the impression in a short amount of time…..from assaulting the Hardy Boys at December’s In Your House…….to attacking them again on Monday Night Raw……..to calling out and finally accepting the Challenge of Hawk and Animal to a match here tonight……there aren’t many teams that can say they walked into a promotion as unknowns one month…..only to find themselves on Pay Per View against the Legion of Doom the next…..but here we are….

……..and if last months match with the Hardy Boys was called the “don’t p*ss off the booker man”……this months match should be called “creative has nothing for you……because you p*ssed off the booker man”…….Hawk and Animal have pretty much burnt their bridges here with me…..drinking, drugs, bad attitudes…..and that’s just Hawk…..so while the Legion of Doom SHOULD be contending for the WWF Tag Team Titles…..instead they are here…..losing to Edge and Christian……when Edge hit Hawk with the Downward Spiral Reverse STO onto a steel chair, whilst the official was distracted by Animal and Christian brawling (well, mainly Animal beating the s*it out of Christian) in the aisleway……a big, big win for the new kids on the block.

Winner(s): Edge & Christian
Rating: B

The match is over, and Animal is in the ring to check on his partner…..Edge and Christian have just made the best start possible to their young WWF careers……but they don’t look like they have finished……because the back of Animals head is firmly introduced to a second steel chair courtesy of Christian. Edge collects a second chair and the two young men stand over the fallen Road Warriors……weapons raised……ready to dent some steel over their skulls…………

………when in a role reversal of recent months, it’s Matt and Jeff Hardy out to make the save for the Legion of Doom…..they dive into the ring behind Edge and Christian, snatching the chairs out of the hands of the attackers and swing them wildly……fortunately for the newcomers they are able to get out of dodge and retreat up the aisle way before the Hardys can connect, leaving Matt and Jeff to tend to the Road Warriors as the victors celebrate out of harms way.

Rating: B-

We head over to the commentary table now, where Ross and Rude (or “R&R Announcing” if you want to give them a jazzy name) quickly talk over the match, with Ross claiming that Edge and Christian may have won the match, but the moral victory goes to the Legion of Doom…..Rude scoffs at that, telling his partner that “……moral victories don’t pay the bills Ross, actual victories do……and Edge and Christian just got an actual victory over one of the most decorated tag teams in history……”

Jim Ross begins to argue, but cuts himself short, saying that he has just had word in his headset that there is somewhat of a clandestine meeting going on in the back…….

…….and we are shown a shot of Vince McMahon in the back, talking with Faarooq while the rest of the Nation stands on……there is no sound, so Ross speculates as to what the meeting is about…..Rude calls Jim a stooge however, and whatever the boss is planning is fine by him….

……the meeting appears to be reaching its conclusion, because McMahon nods his head in agreement to whatever Faarooq has said, before offering his hand to the leader of the Nation….some kind of deal has been done…..but for what, we don’t know……yet.

Rating: B+

From one secret meeting, we go to another not so secret meeting…….and at this one, you could cut the tension with a knife…..as we go down to the locker room of the KLiQ, where Shawn Michaels is pacing around the room….WWF Championship over his shoulder…..generally laying down the law to a group of men who look more disinterested with every passing week……

…..he tells Hall, Nash, Waltman and Credible, that the course of action for the evening couldn’t be simpler if he tried…….Hall and Nash go out there next……and do WHATEVER it takes to ensure that the Hart Brothers do NOT make it to the Royal Rumble match…….and then in the Royal Rumble itself, he wants Austin, Pillman and Steiner gone…..the second they enter……

…..it doesn’t get much simpler than that……the four of them toss out the trash…….forfeit the World Title shots that they get for eliminating Austin and Pillman……and then make damn sure, that a suitable winner comes out the other side…..something so simple……

……he stops both his pacing and his tirade, coming to a halt in front of Kevin Nash……he tells Big Sexy that there must have been some kind of miscommunication last week…….but to be absolutely crystal clear tonight……what that big money contract that Big Sexy signed means…….is that Michaels 3:16 said I just owned your ass……..and Kevin Nash does, whatever the hell the Champ tells him to do…….you got that?……..Pal?…..

Nash tells Shawn not to sweat it…….because Big Daddy Cool is going to personally guarantee……..that come Wrestlemania, the Heartbreak Kid will get the opponent he deserves…..ok……buddy?…..

Rating: A*

VS.

If Bret and Owen win then they are entered into the Royal Rumble match

The Hart Foundation vs. The KLiQ

After Scott Steiner’s big win earlier in the evening, It’s time for another high stakes match up right now…..but even though the Tag Team title holders are in action, it’s not the straps that are on the line…..but rather the opportunity to once again challenge for a Championship…..THE Championship…..the WWF Championship currently held by the Heartbreak Kid, Shawn Michaels, that neither Bret Hart nor Owen Hart can compete for so long as Michaels holds the belt…..

…..the fact that neither man is able to challenge for the WWF Tag Team Titles while they are in possession of the KLiQ, means this match is obviously a non-title affair….which SHOULD have taken some of the pressure off Hall and Nash…..Shawn Michaels has made it perfectly clear however that failure tonight is not an option, which means it could be a long, long night for the KLiQ……

….as for the match itself, Bret Hart continues his year long tradition of never having a bad match with anybody, because this one was excellent. Scott Hall is still hampered slightly by tendon damage in his ankle which he’s working through (and will be for the next ten months) coupled with the fact that age is slowly catching up with him, but even that wasn’t enough to drag this match down. Obviously the crowd were red hot for it……unlike the Steiner/Perfect match earlier, which the crowd never really saw Steiner losing, there was a real danger of the KLiQ taking this one…..and taking Bret and Owen OUT of the Royal Rumble….in the end though that wasn’t what happened………

……no what happened was the Nation of Domination took Bret Hart and Owen Hart out of the Royal Rumble, as what actually transpired during that little secret meeting with Vince McMahon becomes that little bit clearer…..because with Scott Hall trapped in the Sharpshooter of Owen Hart, and Kevin Nash on the outside with Bret Hart, the Nation make their way out from the back in force…..and with Kama and Scorpio distracting the attention of the official, Nelson and The Rock jump the Hitman on the floor……leaving Faarooq free to nail Owen from behind, breaking up the submission attempt….before nailing him with a Dominator. The Nation flee like thieves in the night…..and one Razors Edge later, Bret Hart and Owen Hart are officially OUT of the Royal Rumble match.

Winner(s): The KLiQ
Rating: A

We air a brief promo package highlighting the upcoming Wrestlemania XIII that will take place in just eight weeks time……before we return to the evenings entertainment, and go straight backstage to a makeshift office, where Vincent Kennedy McMahon is with the Nation of Domination. McMahon thanks the group for their services, stating that not only have they just earned themselves a shot at the WWF Tag Team Championships…..they have earned it on the grandest stage of them all……Wrestlemania…..he says be that Nelson and Kama, Faarooq and The Rock….or any other combination of the Nation, they will be getting the Title shot that they feel they so richly deserve……..and as for tonight, since we appear to have two spots opened up in the Royal Rumble…..the ENTIRE Nation will be competing in the Royal Rumble match…..

“…….and just remember, not only does the winner of the Royal Rumble go on to Wrestlemania to Challenge for the World Wrestling Federation Championship, the man, or men, that eliminate Stone Cold Steve Austin and The Loose Cannon Brian Pillman, will also earn themselves a shot at the WWF Title somewhere down the road……that’s three potential World Title opportunities…..add that to your Tag Team Title match and that could spell a VERY good night for the Nation of Domination……”

Faarooq thanks McMahon, stating that it is nice to finally receive some recognition around here…..and he promises that by the end of the night, the Nation will have taken every opportunity that has come their way…….

……….which is when the Hart Foundation come bursting into the room……and they’ve not come empty handed…….no, they are brandishing some metal folding furniture…..that’s steel chairs to you and me……which they absolutely waffle Kama, Nelson and Scorpio with……before Bret and Owen turn their attention to Faarooq and The Rock, while Vince McMahon looks on, helplessly screaming for security as we go back down to ringside.

Rating: A

VS.

WWF Light Heavyweight Championship Match

Rey Mysterio (c) vs. Christopher Daniels


It’s time for the second title match of the evening, and what a fantastic showcase it was for our Light Heavyweight Division. Young Rey Mysterio has been the flag bearer for the division since capturing the title back in August at Summerslam…..five months later and nobody has managed to dethrone him, making him the longest reigning Champion in the Light Heavyweight Divisions (admittedly, short lived) history…..tonight however he’s facing a different kind of challenge than he’s used too……sure Christopher Daniels can fly…….he can wrestle…..he can brawl…….but he is also competing under the influence of one of the darkest minds in the WWF, Raven…..

……the two light heavyweights have an exciting back and forth battle, with both competitors taking to the air. Daniels isn’t a bad talker, but Raven has certainly helped when it came to the hype surrounding this match up……he certainly had a hand in when it came to the finish too……

………because why else would Flock members Bill DeMott and the Dogs of War be making their way down to ringside, if not at the behest of their fearless leader? Mysterio sees the arrivals coming, and attempts to cut them off at the pass with a springboard splash to the outside…..Bill DeMott catches the little luchador in mid air however……before Gorilla Pressing him back inside the ring through the middle rope. The official is all over that, and quick to berate DeMott for placing his hands on Rey…..but DeMott proclaims his innocence, stating he was just helping him back into the ring……unfortunately for Rey, the official did more harm than good……because while he was distracted admonishing Bill DeMott, he missed the Dogs of War entering the ring and completely destroying Mysterio with a brutal Double Chokeslam……..Daniels could have picked up the win there…..but opts to inflict more punishment with the Angels Wings…..and three seconds later, Rey’s five month reign is over.

Winner: and New WWF Light Heavyweight Champion…..The Fallen Angel, Christopher Daniels
Rating: B

From there we go to the back, where Raven is watching Daniels celebrate with his new found Championship on a monitor…..Dude Love is beside him, but the Dude looks uncharacteristically unhappy……he asks Raven if that was really necessary….but Raven tells him it is…..Raven says that he will do whatever it takes to lead his followers to glory…..but he can only help those willing to help themselves. He says he could walk Cactus Jack through the bloody valleys and out of purgatory……towards the road to immortality…….but claims that even he can not bring glory to those undeserving…..he asks the Dude if he truly believes that “Dude Love” has what it takes to outlast 29 other wrestlers…..and win the Royal Rumble?

The Dude seems to think so, and shows Raven a piece of paper that presumably contains his number……telling him that he won’t HAVE to outlast 29 other people by the time he gets in. Raven looks at the number, before reaching into his own pocket and pulling out his own. Raven hands HIS number to the Dude, and pockets Dude Love’s number for himself……telling him to see how far that misaligned confidence gets him now…..before we go to the usual Pre-Rumble Promo reel, featuring brief comments from the major players in our next match.

Rating: B+

THE 1997 ROYAL RUMBLE MATCH

It is now time……for the Royal Rumble match…….before we have our usual announcement of the rules however, we are joined at the commentary table by the owner of the World Wrestling Federation, Vincent Kennedy McMahon…..and the WWF Champion himself, Shawn Michaels. Jim Ross questions whether HBK would be better served by staying in the back and preparing for his upcoming title match with The Undertaker, but Shawn shrugs that off, saying he has his game plan in place, he’s here now to find out who his opponent at Wrestlemania is going to be……

…..for once, there is very little anticipation to find out who drew number one…..because we already know……and Stone Cold Steve Austin makes his way to the ring to a thunderous ovation from the crowd……who also already know the identity of the man entering at number two……The Loose Cannon, Brian Pillman……who may not get quite the reaction Steve Austin does, but he’s not far behind.

The two men have absolutely no problem fighting each other…..in fact I think they quite like the idea….and they spend the next two minutes brawling about the ring….and while both men DO try to eliminate the other, it’s clear that they aren’t trying especially hard…..a fact that is highlighted when entrant number three arrives…….

Entrant #3: Justin Credible

…..whether it’s a ploy by Vince McMahon to get a member of the KLiQ in their early to counteract Austin and Pillman…..or simply bad luck on the part of Justin Credible, the result is the same. Stone Cold and The Loose Cannon have spent the best part of two months trying to find ways to punish the KLiQ…..and now they have Justin Credible alllllllllll alone……for what is the longest 1:45 of Credibles life…..because fortunately for him Austin puts him out of his misery and tosses him from the match, just as the clock signals the arrival of the next competitor…..

Eliminated: Justin Credible (1:40) (Steve Austin)

Entrant #4: Kokina

……and number four is a biggie…….a very big, biggie…..as the Sons of Samoa’s Kokina comes lumbering down the aisle…..I don’t think Austin and Pillman are going to be tossing him out quite as easily as they (Austin) did Credible…..not that it stops them going right on the attack the second the big man climbs between the ropes. They even manage to wobble the mammoth Samoan…..but they can neither knock Kokina from his feet, or get anywhere near close to eliminating him, before Kokina drops each man with a stiff headbutt a piece…..it may be two against one here, but you wouldn’t know it right now…..maybe entry number five will help…..

Entrant #5: Marty Jannetty

…….or maybe not……because while Marty Jannetty comes pelting down the aisle way, his momentum soon comes to a screeching halt when he collides with the unmovable frame of Kokina. Pillman and Austin take a minute to catch their breath…..which is all it takes for Kokina to crush Jannetty with a huge leg drop….before once again attempting to double team the big man……and this time they have moderately more success, driving Kokina back into the ropes, but even with a man working on each leg they are unable to lift the Samoan……

Entrant #6: Nelson

……..with first Kokina, and now Nelson…..that’s a lot of beef in a ring that Jim Ross assures us has been reinforced especially for this match….the Nation member hits the ring….but instead of heading for the biggest dog in the fight in Kokina, he plucks Pillman off the Samoan by the hair and tosses him right over the top rope……clearly Nelson is looking to claim that bounty…..the Loose Cannon is able to scramble to safety on the apron however….and re-enters the ring with a springboard cross body onto Nelson…..who catches him mid air and drives him to the mat with a monstrous Power Slam, just as Kokina drops Austin…..and now these two big men come face to face……

Entrant #7: Jesse James

…….it’s Jesse James in at number seven…..and he instantly avoids the mass of flesh stood in the middle of the ring, circling each other like King Kong and Godzilla…..instead opting to launch an attack on Marty Jannetty. Rick Rude claims that this is exactly why James needs the guidance of Mr. Perfect…..because both Steve Austin and Brian Pillman are down…..eliminating both Steve Austin and Brian Pillman is an automatic World Title opportunity…..and Jesse James is attacking Marty Jannetty. Jim Ross claims that he believes Jesse James is just doing whatever it takes to keep out of the way of Nelson and Kokina, who have now began to hammer each other with blows……

Entrant #8: The British Bulldog

…….The British Bulldog is out next…..historically Davey Boy has always fared pretty well in this sort of environment, lasting thirty six minutes in the 1991 Royal Rumble, before winning the 20 man battle at the Royal Albert hall later in that year…..coming in at number one in the 1992 Rumble and lasting almost twenty five minutes…..with his biggest achievement being in 1995, when he came in at number two and THOUGHT he had won the whole thing, before being tossed out from behind by actual winner Shawn Michaels……maybe finally this is the Bulldogs year…….although you wouldn’t think so with how he starts…..because he charges straight for Nelson, looking for some payback for the Nation costing his brothers in-law a spot in the Rumble…..unfortunately Nelson and Kokina are currently battling it out to see who the dominant big man is, and they both drop the Bulldog with a double headbutt before returning to pounding on each other…..meanwhile Marty Jannetty has decided that he’s going to be the one to claim the bounty on Steve Austin’s head, as he is currently trying to tip Stone Cold over the top rope…….which is right about the time business picks up……

Entrant #9: Kevin Nash

…….because Big Sexy Kevin Nash is in at number nine……and he makes an immediate impact, launching Jesse James over the top rope and crashing to the floor……

Eliminated: Jesse James (4:26) (Kevin Nash)

…….before turning his attention to the huge mass of flesh that is Kokina and Nelson, who are currently both leaning against the ropes, trying to tip each other over the top……..they have both managed to get one leg of their opponent off the mat…..meaning they are both unbalanced…….so when Kevin Nash charges across the ring and gives the two men the little extra momentum they need, they both go tumbling over the top rope and to the floor, in a very impressive display from Big Sexy……

Eliminated: Kokina (10:38) (Kevin Nash)
Eliminated: Nelson (6:38) (Kevin Nash)

……..after completing three eliminations in less than a minute, Nash finally focuses his attack on his targets for the evening…..Steve Austin and Brian Pillman…..although Jim Ross questions whether Nash is doing it because he has been ordered to……or because the prospect of a guaranteed World Title shot is too tempting a prospect to turn down……

Entrant #10: Dude Love

……….number ten brings out what SHOULD have been Raven……but in what Raven would call a lesson….(the rest of us would just call it taking advantage of the Dude’s good nature)……Raven has taken Dude Loves number for himself……in the meantime Steve Austin has taken a break from battling Kevin Nash, to toss Marty Jannetty over the top rope…..Michaels finds that particularly funny…..saying it looks like Marty won’t be Rocking his way to Wrestlemania any time soon…..

Eliminated: Marty Jannetty (11:08) (Steve Austin)

……….before getting blindsided by the British Bulldog, who’s looking to take a member of the Hart Foundation to Wrestlemania……

Entrant #11: The Rock

………..the next entrant puts an end to the Bulldogs attack however…..as The Rock hits the ring and instantly goes after the only remaining Hart Foundation member, Davey Boy Smith…..Dude Love is locked in a battle with Brian Pillman in one corner, while Kevin Nash and Steve Austin are peppering each other with blows in the opposite corner……

Entrant #12: Rich Steiner

………..we have the first member of the Million Dollar Corporation entering the fray now…..as the former Intercontinental Champion, Rich Steiner makes his way into the action…..Austin and Nash continue their battle in the corner…..with Shawn Michaels shouting at Nash to stop slacking and do what he’s paid to do…..Nash halts his offence long enough to share some tense words with Michaels…..leaving Rich Steiner the perfect opening to attempt and eliminate Austin himself. Meanwhile The Rock and Davey Boy Smith continue the battle between the Nation and The Hart Foundation. Pillman on the other hand has knocked Dude Love to the mat and has moved over towards Austin and Steiner, helping his partner out…..

Entrant #13: Lance Storm

………as Lance Storm joins the action. This young man has had a tremendous start to his WWF career……even Shawn Michaels says it would be an honour to face this youngster at Wrestlemania……he bought Lance into the World Wrestling Federation after all. Jim Ross says that the ring is starting to fill up now…..as we see the unlikely combination of Lance Storm and Rich Steiner teaming up to try and eliminate Steve Austin….while the Bulldog tosses The Rock over the top…..only as far as the apron however and the Nation member scrambles back into the ring…..

Entrant #14: Ahmed Johnson

……..time to introduce some more muscle into the match…..and it’s Ahmed Johnson, who gets a great reception. He dives into the ring and turns Dude Love inside out with a Clothesline, as the Dude is stood (kind of proving Raven’s point) doing a little dance for the crowd….before turning his attention to Kevin Nash, clearly still with some unfinished business with the KLiQ……

Entrant #15: Sean Waltman

…….speaking of the KLiQ, we are at the halfway point, and Sean Waltman is in at number fifteen…..he clips the knee of Ahmed from behind, giving Nash a brief reprise….before the two KLiQ men double team Ahmed……Michaels says that Nash should be grateful that Walkman got there when he did, because he was in danger of embarrassing himself. Jim Ross says that the ring is really filling up with bodies now….claiming that McMahon’s bounty is having the opposite of its desired effect…..because the wrestlers are too busy trying to eliminate Austin and Pillman rather than each other………..

Entrant #16: Scott Steiner

………it’s number 16, and it’s the man who successfully defended the Intercontinental Championship earlier in the evening…..it’s Big Poppa Pump Scott Steiner…..he plows into the sea of brawling wrestlers, dragging his brother off Brian Pillman….not to save Pillman, but so that he can beat the holy hell out of Rich Steiner. Dude Love has re-focused after the dancing bug bit him, and is currently attempting to earn a World Title shot by dumping Steve Austin…..he probably should have stuck to dancing because Austin backdrops him over the top rope and out of the match…..

Eliminated: Dude Love (13:32) (Steve Austin)

…….and he’s not the only one….because Scott Steiner nails his brother with a huge Steinerline that sends him out as well……I’m sure DiBiase was hoping for a better showing than that by his client…..

Eliminated: Rich Steiner (9:45) (Scott Steiner)

Entrant #17: Jeff Jarrett

…….as Jeff Jarrett enters the fray at number 17, Jim Ross informs us that both Steve Austin and Brian Pillman have now been in the ring for thirty minutes…..eighteen for the British Bulldog…..and sixteen for Kevin Nash…..which Shawn Michaels complains is sixteen minutes that he hasn’t dumped either Austin or Pillman in….Vince tells him not to worry however, there’s still a long way to go……

Entrant #18: Marc Mero

……..as Marc Mero enters the fray……Scott Steiner has moved his attention onto the KLiQ, and is currently beating on Sean Waltman in the corner…..before Michaels tells Nash to stop standing around like a big dummy and help Sean out……Ahmed Johnson seems to have rekindled his ongoing battle with the Nation of Domination, slugging it out with their newest member, The Rock……

Entrant #19: Raven

……..and in at number nineteen…..which SHOULD have been Dude Love’s number……is the master of manipulation Raven…..who wastes no time going straight for Brian Pillman…..Raven has kept his interactions here in the WWF pretty limited to a select few competitors since his arrival…..so who he chooses to focus on in this match could be interesting……

Entrant #20: Al Snow

……..however his old nemesis Al Snow is in at number twenty…..and these two continue a battle that has been raging on in some way or another for the best part of six months now……meanwhile Scott Steiner has shaken off Kevin Nash’s attack and presses Sean Waltman high over head, before tossing him straight to the floor and out of the match…..much to Shawn Michaels disgust…..

Eliminated: Sean Waltman (11:38) (Scott Steiner)

……..Kevin Nash is not holding up his end of the deal here…..and both Michaels and McMahon are not impressed that Austin, Pillman and Steiner are all still in this match……

Entrant #21: Kama

……..The Rock gets a hand in his battle with Ahmed Johnson, as Nation brother Kama is out next…..and the two men put a big enough beating on the big man from Pearl River that The Rock is able to dump him out and to the floor……

Eliminated: Ahmed Johnson (15:11) (The Rock)

……..while Jim Ross remarks that Austin and Pillman, number one and number two are still in this thing….and coming up to forty minutes in……with both Bulldog and Nash lasting almost thirty minutes, which is no mean feat when you consider that Kevin Nash has competed once already tonight…..Shawn is quick to say that Scott Hall did all the hard work in that match anyway……

Entrant #22: Chris Jericho

………young Chris Jericho had a hell of a showing in last years Royal Rumble…..and has come a long, long way since then…..even main eventing his first Pay Per View, taking Shawn Michaels to the very limit….a fact that Jim Ross is quick to point out on commentary……and if Jericho lasts just half as long tonight as he did last year, then he WILL be headlining his first Wrestlemania…..but first there is the little matter of the man who sidelined his World Title aspirations….Lance Storm…..who has had the best of Chris Jericho at every turn…..not tonight however…..tonight Lance Storm finds himself tossed over the top rope, courtesy of his former partner……

Final Thoughts

And that, Ladies and Gentlemen…..was the 1997 Royal Rumble…….and it was pretty good even if I do say so myself…….I think the choice to give Scott Steiner the win took a few people by surprise, but truth be told, Scotty has been earmarked to win the Rumble since the end of July. At the end of last years Wrestlemania I had three people in mind to win this match……Bret Hart, Steve Austin (assuming I could get him up to that level in time)…..or Kevin Nash……Scott Steiner was never even a consideration……in fact the Steiner Brothers as a whole were not splitting up anytime soon, they were going to be the babyface figureheads of the Tag Team Division……..only problem was, in the space of a month back in June, Scott Steiner got himself over……..really over………and stayed over……..and in the meantime, put on the performance of the night week in and week out……so I booked him strong, but really it was Scotty that did all the hard work, and this victory was very much deserved.

Likewise a lot of people were surprised at the decision NOT to include Bret and Owen Hart in the Royal Rumble match…….in fact a lot of fans have questioned whether we have lost faith in the Harts, who seem to be taking a back seat of late……but believe me, nothing could be further from the truth……The Harts are very much a part of future plans, they simply have a story that they need to tell first……

Speaking of the Harts, Owen’s contract is currently up…..and we are once again locked in a bidding war with WCW…….much like Billy Gunn however (who actually re-signed with us the day of the Rumble) Owen isn’t really taking their offer seriously……being seen as the Number one promotion in the world has really helped us at the negotiating table…….and for the record, Owen thinks Edge is going to be a star……..the Road Warriors probably don’t agree however……and are frankly furious with the way their match went down at The Rumble…..I don’t care……hopefully it won’t be long before they do a Triple H, ask for their release……and trot on down to WCW to give Bischoff a headache instead of me……
