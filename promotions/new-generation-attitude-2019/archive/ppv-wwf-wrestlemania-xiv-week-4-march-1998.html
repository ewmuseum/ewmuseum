Week 4, March 1998

Fleet Centre (Boston, Massachusetts)

ATT: 20,000

PPV BUY: 1.22

Announce Team: Jim Ross, Jerry “The King” Lawler & Ravishing Rick Rude

Quote:

…..it’s time for the marquee event of the WWF calendar year…..THE event…..the ultimate grand stage…..where careers are defined and legends are born…..it’s time, for Wrestlemania XIV…..

…..a lengthy hype video highlights the matches that we have ahead of us this evening, before the pyros erupt in the Fleet Centre, as we go down to our customary (pay-per-view) three men team of Jim Ross, Jerry “The King” Lawler & Ravishing Rick Rude, who welcome us all to what is sure to be a jam packed show, with JR looking forward to our main event of the evening…..

”…..because while the cards are stacked against him, come hell or high water, Stone Cold Steve Austin is here, and King, he wants that World Wrestling Federation title…..”

“…..how are the cards stacked against him Jim Ross?…..there are three men vying for the prize tonight…..if anything the cards are stacked against the champion, because he doesn’t even have to be pinned to lose his title…..that doesn’t seem fair if you ask me…..you’ve never defended a championship Ross, me and Ravishing Rick on the other hand have, and we know all about the champions advantage…..”

“…..that’s right Lawler, every advantage that comes with being the man holding the gold has been stripped away from Brian Pillman this evening, but for what it’s worth, my money is still on the Loose Cannon…..there’s always an air of unpredictability in these multi man matches, and there’s no one more unpredictable than Brian Pillman…..”

…..Jim Ross disagrees, stating that while Pillman and Hart are competing against each other his evening, they have worked together on a number of occasions (including helping Brian Pillman to win that WWF Title from the Undertaker to begin with) while Stone Cold has always been an island…..a man alone, with little regard for anyone or anything other than claiming that World Wrestling Federation Championship…..and while JR still thinks that the odds are against him tonight, he believes that you’re going to have to kill the Rattlesnake to stop him from walking out with the gold this evening…..

…..the trio continue to bicker about our main event, but as the sirens begin to blare throughout the arena, it looks like the discussion is going to have to wait…..because Big Poppa Pump is making his way out…..it’s time to get the action underway……

Rating: N/A

Quote:

…..and our opening match is set to be an emotionally charged one without a doubt…..because Kevin Nash and Scott Steiner are about ready to flat out kill each other…..

…..interestingly enough, while Brian Pillman and Steve Austin opened the show at last year’s Wrestlemania (and are set to close it this year) Kevin Nash and Scott Steiner were both present in the closing of last years show, and here they are opening the fourteenth annual edition…..by design or sheer coincidence? I’ll let you be the judge of that…..

…..the problems between these two men really came to a head at Survivor Series this past November, after Steiner walked out on Nash during their four on four Survivor Series match with the Hart Foundation…..in fairness to Scotty, he had been largely left out of the match (as the Wolfpac members tagged in and out between each other for the majority of the match) and after both Hall and Waltman were eliminated, Steiner took offence to Nash only then attempting to tag him into the action (when he had no other choice) and decided to leave him high and dry instead……

…..from there, things escalated to such a point that Vince McMahon himself decreed that neither of these two men could lay a finger on each other until the bell rang to signal the start of this match…..a fact that Steiner used to his advantage as Big Sexy makes his entrance, deliberately rolling to the floor and blocking the Wolfpac man from entering the ring, before out and out spitting in Nash’s face and flipping him the bird…..tactics that Rick Rude actually approves of…..

”…..because Kevin Nash is a man about ready to explode Jim Ross…..and all Scott Steiner is doing here is getting more and more inside his head…..Steiner took out Sean Waltman a few weeks ago and forced Kevin Nash to stand by and watch…..now he’s spitting right in his face…..Kevin Nash is an angry, angry man…..and angry men make mistakes…..”

…..Jim Ross points out that Scott Steiner also had to stand back and watch as Kevin Nash powerbombed his own brother Rich Steiner off the entrance way this past Monday night, but again, Rude says that wasn’t the same…..Kevin Nash actually likes his Wolfpac “brothers”, while Scott Steiner only gives a damn about two things, his freaks and his peaks…..

…..the bell finally rings, and this one gets underway…..

…..and Kevin Nash absolutely explodes on Big Poppa Pump…..

…..Rick Rude wasn’t kidding when he said that Nash was an angry man…..former WWF Champion or not, Scott Steiner simply cannot contend with his opponent in the opening minutes of this contest, as Nash beats the living sh!t out of him…..he nails him with blow after blow that drives him into the corner, where Nash continues to unload with strikes and knee blows, so much so that the official tries to pull him off Scott Steiner, only to get shoved halfway across the ring for his trouble…..something that JR remarks was very silly…..

”…..Kevin Nash is going to have to control his emotions here, because the official would be well within his rights to disqualify Big Sexy for laying his hands on him…..”

“…..and who would win the match then JR? Scott Steiner, that’s who…..I told you that Big Poppa Pump was in Nash’s head, it’s only a matter of time before he slips up….”

…..that may be the case, but it looks as though Scott Steiner is in for a hell of an ass kicking before it gets to that point…..

…..Nash goes back to beating on his opponent in the corner, until Steiner is finally able to break free, bailing to the outside for a reprieve that never comes, because Big Sexy is hot on his tail…..he nails Steiner in the back of the head with a running clothesline, before picking him up and dropping him (snake eyes style) on the guard rail, before driving his skull into the ring post and rolling him back into the ring…..

…..Nash climbs into the apron and steps one long leg over the top rope…..he gets caught by Sable on the outside though (who grabs onto the other foot of Nash) long enough that Steiner is able to nail Nash in the head with a hard right hand, before kicking the top rope hard enough for it to nail Big Sexy right in the balls……much to Rick Rude’s delight…..

”…..and that’s exactly what I was talking about Jim Ross…..Scott Steiner bode his time until an opportunity presented itself for him to take control…..that’s just good strategy…..”

“…..opportunity my Oklahoma ass…..he can thank Sable for that and nothing else…..”

…..I’m sure Scotty will be sure to thank her later…..for now he takes advantage of the prone Nash to drag him all the way into the ring, where he proceeds to return the favour from earlier as he pummels the crap out of Big Sexy…..

…..he whips Nash hard into the turnbuckles and follows up with a running clothesline to the corner…..series of right hands and then another Irish whip to the other corner with such force that Nash comes staggering back to the middle of the ring, where Steiner meets him with an impressive belly to belly (and bicep kiss) for a close two count…..

…..Scotty drapes Nash’s neck over the second rope and plants all his weight down on the back of his opponent, choking the life out of him as he screams obscenities at the top of his voice…..the referee finally forces a break, leading to Steiner screaming at HIM (as well as causing an opening for Sable to grab hold of Nash’s head, choking him even more, before slapping the taste right out of his mouth) and then dragging Big Sexy to the middle of the ring, dropping an elbow and then knocking out ten quick push-ups for good measure…..a strategy that Jim Ross questions…..

”…..Scott Steiner might want to concentrate less on working out his biceps, and more on his opponent…..Kevin Nash might be down right now, but if there’s one thing we know, putting Big Sexy down and KEEPING Big Sexy down, are two entirely different things…..”

…..he’s not wrong either…..because for all the posing, Steiner can’t keep Nash down…..Scotty sends Big Sexy into the corner with another Irish whip and goes charging in, looking for another corner clothesline…..this time however he’s met by the big boot of his opponent that plants him on his ass…..

…..and now Nash takes back control…..and he really fires back, cutting off everything Steiner can throw at him, before finally hoisting Scotty up onto his shoulder, dropping him onto the top turnbuckle with Snake Eyes, and then running straight through him with another Big Boot…..

…..then he drops the straps on his singlet…..

…..grabs hold of Steiner…..

…..and plants him right in the middle of the ring with the Jackknife Powerbomb, before making the cover…..

…..except the official doesn’t make the count…..

…..because he’s distracted by the presence of Sable up there on the apron…..

…..a frustrated Nash slaps his hand on the mat in anger, before getting to his feet and walking over to the number one freak…..reaching over to the apron…..and lifting her up and right into the ring…..

…..before putting her over his knee and giving her the spanking of her life…..

…..let’s not forget that misogyny was (regretfully) alive and well in 1998…..so it’s no surprise that the crowd eats this up…..as well as The King…..who remarks that he doubts that’s the first spanking Sable has received…..

”…..and I think she likes it JR…..”

…..whether she likes it or not remains to be seen…..but she served her purpose here…..because as the official assists her from the ring, he fails to see Big Poppa Pump nail Big Sexy in the back of the head with that patented lead pipe…..

…..Nash drops to the mat like a sack of crap, and Scotty tosses the pipe, before locking in the Steiner Recliner, just as the official returns his attention to the in ring action…..

…..the ref checks on Nash (who is out like a light) and raises (and drops) the arm three times, before calling for the bell and awarding the match to Scott Steiner…..

…..and when Kevin Nash wakes up…..you’ve got to think he’s not going to be happy about this outcome…..but at the end of the day, all that matters is that it’s Scott Steiner who gets a check in the win column tonight…..

Winner: Scott Steiner
Rating: A

Quote:

…..Scotty and Sable make their way to the back, where the King remarks that he’s sure they have a BIG celebration ahead of them tonight…..

…..Rude is rather flippant at the result, stating that he predicted this all along, reiterating that Scott Steiner got in the head of Kevin Nash tonight, and Kevin Nash let emotion get the better of him…..again Jim Ross calls BS on that, stating that the only thing that got he better of Kevin Nash tonight were the numbers…..Kevin Nash tried to fight one on one, while Scott Steiner resorted to help from Sable to pick up a victory…..

…..Lawler being Lawler, can’t help but state that he wouldn’t mind a little bit of one on one action with Sable himself (”if you know what I mean JR”) before letting out that obnoxious cackle of his…..

”…..YOU THINK THIS IS FUNNY LITTLE MAN? DO I LOOK LIKE A JOKE TO YOU?…..”

…..oh dear…..Kevin Nash has recovered…..and after getting nailed with that lead pipe he’s like the proverbial bear with a sore head…..

…..he bats Lawlers crown from the announce table, asking him how funny he’d think he’d find it to be powerbombed straight through this desk here…..

…..I don’t think the King would find that funny at all, but he wisely keeps his mouth shut as Jim Ross goes on damage control…..he tells Nash that they saw that he had that match won before Sable involved herself…..the whole world saw it…..he tells Nash that he had Steiner pinned for at least a five count…..no one is laughing at him…..

…..Nash continues to give Lawler the stink eye, but evidently he’s too groggy to carry on the discussion, as he places a hand to the back of his head and begins to walk off…..but not before warning the King to watch his mouth, before Big Sexy puts a fist through it…..

…..the announcers stay silent for a moment as Nash heads to the back, with Rick Rude waiting for Nash to get out of earshot before announcing that he found it funny if no one else did…..and JR quietly sends us to a video package ahead of our next match…..

Rating: B+

Quote:

WWF European Championship: Harlem Street-fight Match

…..a match which sees our first championship being defended, as Booker T puts the European Title on the line, against his own brother (and current Nation of Domination member) Stevie Ray…..

…..and not just any match…..a Harlem Street-fight none the less…..

…..the stipulation in this one comes off the back of Stevie Ray claiming that Booker T had gone “soft”…..this after going through the usual trope that all turning tag team partners claim, stating that the success of Harlem Heat was all down to Stevie Ray…..except Stevie took it even further…..saying that not only had he been carrying Booker to success in Harlem Heat, but he was also solely responsible for Booker capturing that European Championship…..a European Championship that Stevie was now going to take…..

…..Booker responded to these claims by challenging Stevie to this Harlem Street-fight…..and well, here we are…..although Rick Rude questions how smart it was of Booker to make this challenge…..

”…..pride Jim Ross…..stupid pride…..not only has Booker T completely thrown away the champions advantage by eliminating both count-outs and disqualifications, but he’s also opened himself up for a whole world of hurt…..not just by big brother (who’s been beating little Booker up since before he was out of diapers) but from the entire Nation of Domination should Stevie Ray so choose…..everything is legal here Ross…..”

…..the Ravishing one may have a point, but Booker certainly doesn’t look to have the disadvantage as the bell sounds for this one, because he’s completely in control to open the bout…..he doesn’t even have to resort to the “street-fight” elements of the stipulation either, he simply outwrestles his brother…..

…..eventually Stevie takes it to the “streets” (or….ringside, if we want to be entirely accurate) and things break down a little more as they brawl unfettered on the floor…..the Challenger does have more success on the outside, using everything at his disposal to assault his brother, but even there Booker shows that he’s anything but soft, backdropping Stevie over the crowd rail as the two men brawl their way through the fans…..

…..they eventually work their way back to the ringside area, where they whip each other into steps and swing at each other with chairs until they finally brawl their way around to the announce tables…..

…..Ross, Lawler and Rude scramble as Stevie Ray rolls Booker onto their desk and climbs right up there with him…..he drags Booker to his feet and gets him in the double under-hook position…..

”…..Stevie Ray busca terminar a su hermano aquí con un Slapjack en la mesa!….”

…..our English speaking contingent may have scattered, but we can still hear Hugo Savinovich and Carlos Cabrera over at the Spanish Announce table, and as Hugo says, it does look as though Stevie is about to Slapjack his own brother right through that table…..

…..Booker isn’t going down without a fight though…..struggling to free his arms before managing to backdrop Stevie up and over…..

…..and straight through the Spanish announce desk…..

…..Stevie is a broken mess under a pile of splintered wood, as Booker wearily drags him back to his feet and rolls him into the ring…..surely all he has to do here is cover his brother and this one is all over…..

…..so it’s perhaps no surprise to see the Nation of Domination rushing out from the back to put a stop to that…..they quickly storm the ring and start putting the boots to the Champion…..

…..fortunately help isn’t far behind…..as the Nations opponents later (in the form of Faarooq, Bradshaw, Scorpio and Nelson) come running down to the ring to break up the assault…..

…..all eight men go at it (in a preview of what we will be seeing later this evening) and finally brawl all the way to the back, leaving the two brothers alone in the ring…..

…..again Stevie goes for the Slapjack, but again Booker is able to shake his arms free, taking Stevie’s legs out from under him and slingshotting him into the turnbuckles and then catching him with the Book End as he staggers back to the centre of the ring, driving him into the mat for the one, two, three, and a very hard fought victory…..

Winner: and Still WWF European Champion…..Booker T
Rating: B+

Quote:

…..we leave Booker celebrating his victory and head backstage, where a nervous Kevin Kelly is trying to get word from Kevin Nash for WWF.com…..

…..it’s not really the best time to be trying…..

…..currently they are in an undisclosed part of the building, where Big Sexy is letting out his frustrations on, well pretty much anything that’s not bolted down, as he hurls chairs and monitors about the place…..

…..Kelly wisely realises that this perhaps isn’t the time, and tries to back out of the room, only to get stopped in his tracks by a loud…..

”…..WHAT THE HELL DO YOU WANT?…..”

…..now arguably, the correct answer here would be “nothing Mr. Nash, sorry Mr. Nash”, but apparently Kevin Kelly ain’t so bright…..as he proceeds to ask Big Sexy for his thoughts on his match tonight…..

”…..MY THOUGHTS? I’LL TELL YOU MY THOUGHTS…..THREE YEARS AGO, WRESTLEMANIA XI, I WAS IN THE MAIN EVENT DEFENDING MY WWF TITLE…..TWO YEARS AGO, WRESTLEMANIA XII, I’M HELPING MY ‘BUDDY’ WIN THAT SAME TITLE…..LAST YEAR, WRESTLEMANIA XIII, I’M OUT THERE HELPING SCOTT FREAKING STEINER WIN THE STRAP…..AND THIS YEAR, I’M OPENING THE SHOW WITH THAT JACK-OFF, GETTING SMACKED UPSIDE THE HEAD WITH A GOD DAMN LEAD PIPE…..

…..YOU KNOW THE DIFFERENCE BETWEEN KEVIN NASH AT WRESTLEMANIA XI AND KEVIN NASH AT ALL THE WRESTLEMANIA’S THAT PROCEEDED IT?…..

…..ME! I’M THE DIFFERENCE…..

…..AND THAT ALL CHANGES TONIGHT…..”

…..Nash says that in the last three years, clearly he lost his edge…..spent too much time in KLiQ’s and Pack’s, and generally looking out for other people instead of number one…..

…..Big Sexy says that this is show business…..it ain’t show friends, and he didn’t see no one out there when a lead pipe was getting bent over the back of his head…..

”…..I’M DONE PLAYING GAMES…..I’M DONE WINNING TITLES FOR OTHER PEOPLE…..I WANT THAT GOD DAMN STRAP BACK AROUND MY WAIST…..

…..AND I DON’T GIVE A CRAP WHAT I HAVE TO DO…..

…..OR WHO I HAVE TO HURT…..

…..TO GET IT…..”

…..with that Nash suddenly grabs Kelly by the hair, shoves his head down between his legs…..

…..and Jackknifes the crap out of him through a table to the side of him…..

…..Kevin Kelly is done…..

…..and apparently, when it comes to being Mr. Nice Guy…..

…..so is Kevin Nash…..

Rating: A*

Quote:

…..we leave the medical personnel to treat Kevin Kelly and head back down to ringside, where Rick Rude quips that Jerry Lawler got off easy earlier this evening where Kevin Nash is concerned, before Owen Hart makes his entrance ready for our next match……

…..it’s going to be the self proclaimed “original light heavyweight” Owen Hart (the man who claims to have invented not only the high flying action that we see today in professional wrestling, but was the blueprint for both the lucha libre action of Mexico and the Junior Heavyweight style of Japan) facing off against the littlest underdog with the biggest heart, Rey Mysterio…..

…..Jerry Lawler has always been a cheerleader for Owen Hart, and tonight is no different…..he reels off all of Owen’s (fictional) accomplishments, citing him to not only be the King of Harts, but the King of the Light-Heavyweights too…..a statement that JR rebuts, making rare mention of the long defunct WWF Junior Heavyweight Championship that dated back to the late 1960’s, as well as citing former champions such as Tiger Mask and the Dynamite Kid as true pioneers of the division…..

”…..Owen Hart is a tremendously talented athlete King, one of the very best to lace his boots up between these ropes, no questions asked…..but as for being the blueprint for an entire division, that’s stretching it even for him…..”

…..Owen might be touting himself as a high flying machine in this one, but he certainly doesn’t start out that way…..it’s not often that Hart is the bigger man in the match, but he’s certainly bigger here…..slower too…..which is why Mysterio bounces around like a pinball, hitting his opponent from every direction, so much so that Hart bails from the ring a number of times in an effort to escape his more agile opponent…..

…..Hart wasn’t happy about being shown up here, breaking out a fast paced sequence which ended with both men running the ropes, but it was Rey that ended up on top again, catching Owen with a crucifix pin than damn near won him the match…..Hart is quickly back to his feet and almost takes Mysterio’s head off with a spinning heel kick, then whipping Rey hard into the corner and dropping him with a backbreaker for a two count of his own…..

…..Owen looks to lock on the Sharpshooter early but Mysterio is able to reposition himself before the hold can be applied, catching Owen with a small package for a near fall, then wriggles out of a bodyslam attempt, almost winning again with a roll up, before a frustrated Hart is finally able to catch a breather with a perfectly timed belly to belly suplex on the subsequent rope running sequence…..

…..Owen continues to try and one up the youngster, but gets outshone at every opportunity…..Hart hits a crossbody from the top, Mysterio goes one better with a moonsault to his standing opponent…..pescado dive to the outside by Owen, while Rey leaps off the apron and hits his opponent with an awesome hurricanrana on the floor…..top rope dropkick from Owen, a springboard leg drop from the apron into the ring for Rey…..everything Hart hits, Mysterio seems to have a more impressive reply……

…..the crowd is really getting into it and is firmly behind Rey…..who unfortunately jars his knee after a particularly reckless dive to the outside, and like a shark to blood Owen quickly capitalises on it…..

…..now Hart slows it down as he goes to work on the knee…..stomping and stretching the limb like he was back in the dungeon…..he drags Mysterio to the corner and slams the leg into the ring post, before propping the foot on the bottom rope and brings all of his weight down on the extremity, much as we have seen his brother do on many occasions…..before finally locking in the Sharpshooter…..much to the delight of Jerry Lawler…..

”…..and just like that this one is over JR…..all the flipping and flopping in the world isn’t going to do Rey Mysterio any good while he’s down on the mat, and with that bum leg of his, it’s only a matter of time before he quits…..”

…..Rick Rude agrees, saying that this is good strategy from Hart, not only grounding the high flyer, but also putting himself into a match winning situation…..Jim Ross however says not to count the heart of Mysterio…..words which prove to be true as Rey somehow manages to scratch and claw to the ropes, forcing a break…..

…..Owen goes to apply the hold a second time, but Mysterio is able to kick him off into the corner…..Rey charges at Owen in the corner, but he’s slowed considerably by that knee, meaning that Hart has time to get the foot up, catching his opponent right in the jaw…..

…..Hart mounts the second turnbuckle and climbs onto the shoulders of his dazed opponent, looking for a victory roll to seal the victory……but in a complete throwback to Owen’s Wrestlemania X encounter with his brother Bret, Rey is able to drop to his knees hook the legs and hold the shoulders to the mat for the pinfall victory out of nowhere…..

Winner: Rey Mysterio
Rating: A

Quote:

…..the commentators congratulate both men on an amazing match (with Lawler insisting that Owen was robbed) but Mysterio wasn’t able to celebrate for long…..because as the official raises his arm in victory, Owen kicks that leg out from underneath his leg in frustration, before again stomping the crap out of the already weakened knee…..

…..Hart is not happy about being shown up here (he wasn’t happy about doing the job at all if I’m honest…..hence the flash finish) and drags Mysterio to the middle of the ring, where he again turns Rey over into the Sharpshooter…..and this time there’s no rope break to save him…..

…..the official tries to break it up (and signals for the bell furiously) but Owen isn’t letting go…..eventually some backstage personnel run down to the ring and do manage to drag Hart off his opponent…..but not until the damage is done…..

…..Rey Mysterio is your winner…..

…..but he looks anything but that right now…..

…..let’s go to the back…..

Rating: B+

Quote:

…..backstage we go now, and Dok Hendrix is standing by with the Wolfpac…..well, two thirds of the Wolfpac (because Kevin Nash is nowhere to be seen) ahead of their upcoming hair vs. hair match with the Million Dollar Corporation…..

…..Dok says that he’s not entirely comfortable standing here with Hall and Waltman given their partners current mood (and desire to powerbomb interviewers through tables) but Hall tells him not to worry…..

”…..Big Kev, he’s just blowing off some steam mang, the only one that needs to worry around here is Scott Steiner…..because after what he did out there tonight? Chico, I don’t even KNOW what Big Sexy is gonna do to him…..”

…..be that as it may, Dok is clearly eager to get this wrapped up, quickly moving on to the Wolfpack’s upcoming match with Jeff Jarrett and Rich Steiner…..and it’s more than pride on the line…..hair is too…..

…..Hall laughs at that (“ain’t no one cutting my hair Chico”) before Waltman runs down the Million Dollar Corporation…..and Jeff Jarrett in particular…..

”…..because Jarrett, you’ve been going around for weeks now trying to cut my hair, saying this, saying that…..well pal, I already lost you the European Title…..tonight, you can kiss that raggedy blonde mane of yours goodbye too…..”

…..from there we cut to Michael Cole, who’s over with Ted DiBiase and his Million Dollar Corporation…..who are in a particularly jovial mood…..something that Cole questions, since Jarrett and Steiner could be minutes away from losing their hair…..

…..Jarrett laughs at that, saying they aren’t minutes away from losing anything…..he says that once he and Rich are done wiping the floor with Hall and Waltman in the match, they’ll be wiping the mat with their greasy hair AFTER the mat…..because the Corporation is going to shave them bald…..

…..Rich agrees, saying that just like with Kevin Nash earlier, the Wolfpac is about to get taken out by a Steiner…..again…..while DiBiase says he just wants this over with…..

”…..for too long now we have been sidetracked by the Wolfpac, and that all ends tonight Michael Cole…..tonight, we finally put the wolves to sleep…..we have the skill, we have the talent…..and yes, we have the numbers too…..Hall, Waltman, make the most of your fifteen minutes of fame sharing the spotlight with the Corporation, because the curtain comes down tonight boys…..and when Rich and Jeff are done shaving your head, it’s on to bigger and better things for us…..because while I have more money than every moron in that crowd tonight combined, what we are really interested in……

…..is gold…..”

…..DiBiase cackles his trademark laugh, before leading his Corporation from the interview area…..as Michael Cole sends us down to ringside…..

Rating: A

Quote:

Hair vs. Hair Match

…..okay, it’s time to put up or shut up…..quite literally, because there’s hair on the line in this one…..and while that might not be a huge concern to Rich Steiner (who’s hair is shorn pretty short to begin with) it is definitely a big deal to the other three participants…..

…..the match gets underway and it’s all Hall and Waltman from the get go (with The Kid being particularly eager to get his hands on Jarrett) and they soon send the Corporation bailing from the ring, and Lawler isn’t happy about the initial flurry by the Wolfpac…..

”…..look at that majestic head of hair on Double J…..it’s almost regal…..not like those greasy rat tails on Hall and Waltmans heads JR…..he was doing the world a favour by by trying to give Waltman a make over…..”

“…..Jeff Jarrett brought all this on himself King, and if he’s half as good as he claims he is then he shouldn’t have to worry about losing his hair, the match, or anything else for that matter…..”

“…..what about Steiner though Jim Ross? You know he’s not 100% after getting powerbombed off the stage by Kevin Nash on Monday, if Waltman or Hall can beat him, then poor Jeff gets his head shaved!…..”

“…..’poor Jeff’ my Oklahoma ass…..”

…..the King has a point…..Steiner can’t be anywhere near peak condition after the assault at the hands of Big Sexy this past Monday night…..but then Sean Waltman can’t be feeling anywhere near 100% either after suffering a brutal beating at the hands of Scott Steiner in the week prior to that…..Rick Rude says that all four of these men have taken punishment as a result of the war between Big Sexy and Big Poppa Pump, but tonight it’s two on two, and there won’t be any excuses for whichever team walks out of here with a shaved head…..

…..the Wolfpac duo continue to dominate the opening portion of the match, until DiBiase on the outside finally tips the scale their way as he trips Waltman on a rope running sequence, leading to Rich almost taking the former European Champion’s head off with a Steinerline…..

…..the Corporation cut the ring in half from there, focusing their attention on the smallest man in the match (as well as the man that took a hell of a beating from Scott Steiner not so long ago) with quick tags, with Steiner and Jarrett working particularly well as a team, something that Rude attributes to the experience of Rich…..

”…..because if you’re going to be in a high stakes tag team match, I can’t think of many better partners to have than Rich Steiner…..a tag team specialist that knows all about high stake matches with it all on the line…..”

…..it’s a good point from the Ravishing One…..and Steiner certainly has the most experience when it comes to tag team matches of anyone in the bout, teaming with his brother for close to a decade prior to this…..he clearly loves being in the ring with the smaller Waltman too, as he tosses him around like a rag doll in there…..

…..eventually Waltman is able to make the tag to Hall, and the Bad Guy hits the ring and absolutely unloads on both of his opponents…..before clotheslining Steiner over the top rope and then launching Jarrett across the ring with the fall away slam…..

…..he signals that it’s over as he positions Jarrett for the Razors Edge, but Double J is able to back Hall towards the turnbuckles, where Steiner comes off with a top rope bulldog that would have finished the match off had Waltman not been there to make the save…..

…..big brawl ensues as the official begins to lose control of the match, as all four men begin trading blows…..Hall and Steiner tumble through the ropes and to the outside, and as the official has his attention on that, DiBiase slides Jarrett his guitar, before hopping up onto the apron to grab hold of Waltman, holding him prone in position…..

…..Jarrett winds up and swings, but Waltman manages to free himself at the last second and it’s Deebs that eats the guitar…..Jarrett looks down at his manager in shock, before quickly turning around to face Waltman, only to be met with a Sitout Facebuster for the one, two, three…..

Winner(s): The Wolfpac
Rating: B+

Quote:

…..the match is over…..and unfortunately for Jeff Jarrett, so are his days of having hair…..because Waltman quickly drops to the outside to retrieve the clippers…..

…..it’s shaving time…..
