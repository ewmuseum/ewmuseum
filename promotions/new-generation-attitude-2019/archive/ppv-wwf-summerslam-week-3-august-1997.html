A New Generation…..a new attitude? Pay-per-view

WWF Summerslam: Week 3, August 1997

Week 3, August 1997

RCA Dome (Indianapolis, Indiana)

ATT: 46,705

Buy: 1.30

Ladies and Gentlemen, the time is finally upon us, as the hottest event of the summer rolls into your living room, live on Pay Per View…..it’s time for Summerslam 97……and by the end of this blockbuster event, the entire landscape of the World Wrestling Federation could be changed for good…..and after the usual hype video, highlighting the trials and tribulations that have lead us to this evening, we go down to ringside, where Jim Ross and Ravishing Rick Rude welcome us to the show……and as Jim Ross tells us, tonight….people are wrestling for pride……people are wrestling for titles…..and yes, people are wrestling for their very careers……

…….they discuss the fact that no less than five of tonight’s matches have career threatening stipulations attached to them…..with perhaps none of them bigger than the fact that when that final bell rings, either Kevin Nash, or Shawn Michaels, will no longer find themselves employed by the WWF……they discuss the situation surrounding Bret Hart and Stone Cold Steve Austin, speculating on what lengths either man will go to in order to secure victory in their anything goes street fight later tonight…..before Rick Rude 100% guarantees us that we will see the crowning of a brand new WWF Champion too…..when the seemingly unstoppable challenger Ahmed Johnson, faces off against the (surely, after Monday night) injured Champion Scott Steiner……and with that, the bell rings……signalling that it’s time for our very first match of a jam packed night……

Rating: N/A

VS.

Dude Love vs. Brian Pillman


….and that first match, sees the Coolest Cat in Town, facing off against the Kookiest Kat in Town…..as Dude Love goes up against The Corporate Cannon, Brian Pillman…..

…..poor Dude…..all he wanted was to make his kids happy…..all he wanted to do was send away “the bad man” for the sake of his relationship with his son and baby girl…..but apparently that didn’t sit well with Brian Pillman…..who claims that without Cactus Jack to hide behind, Mick Foley is nothing more than a joke…..an embarrassment…..and frankly, a man that could NEVER beat a Loose Cannon like Brian Pillman……

……that remains to be seen…..but Pillman has certainly got the upper hand in this one, after assaulting the Dude (both physically and verbally) on numerous occasions over the last few weeks, and even holds a victory over the Dude (albeit in six man Tag action, and AFTER Shawn Michaels interfered on behalf of Pillman) all facts that the Corporate Cannon has thrown in the face of the Dude to emphasise the fact that having a tie-dyed freak like him as a father, is more of an embarrassment to his family than the monstrous Cactus Jack ever was…..

……of course these jibes about his children only served to fire the Dude up…….and Brian Pullman’s cockiness is soon knocked off his face…..literally…..as his opponent attacks from the moment the bell is rung…….the Dude may have been looking to start a Summer of Love, but he has anything but love to give here as he unloads with a series of right hands that back Pillman all the way into the corner and down to a seated position on the mat…..the official backs the Dude off, but he doesn’t back off for long…..as he comes charging back in, driving a knee into the jaw of the sitting Cannon…..

……Pillman takes a powder and bails to the outside, but he’s not escaping Love that easily, as a blur of tie-dye follows him to the floor and sends him flying into the ring post……Dude looks to Irish Whip Pillman into the steps, but the Loose Cannon reverses it, and it’s Love that collides knee first into the steel as only Mick Foley can…..

……considering this was a largely brawl based match, the match itself was very good (helped by the fact that these two had some pretty good chemistry in there together) and the fans were solidly behind The Dude as Pillman went on the attack….Pillman is a lot of things…..a madman, deranged, unstable and unpredictable…..but more importantly, he’s very smart……which is why he changes the focus of his attack and zones in on the (now) bad wheels of the Dude, capitalising on the fact that he’s just crashed knees first into solid steel…..

……Pillman works the knees with stomps and kicks, but the Foley has never been one to let a little bit of pain get him down….and while the Dude may be the most mild mannered of all of Mick Foley’s (numerous) personalities, his pain threshold is still considerably higher than most…..and despite Pillman’s best efforts, he simply can’t keep the Dude down…..

……Rick Rude takes that opportunity to mention what an idiot Love is….telling us that all he is doing is giving Brain Pillman more excuse to beat the crap out of him……Jim Ross disagrees, saying that while Dude Love is one of the more unorthodox competitors here in the WWF, you can’t question his heart……and despite The Corporate Cannon’s claims to the contrary, he imagines that Mick Foleys family watching at home would be incredibly proud by this display…..

……The Dude obviously hopes so…..because his never say die attitude finally cuts him a break as Pillman goes to apply the Time Bomb…..his version of Rick Rudes “Rude Awakening” Hangman’s neckbreaker……Pillman spends too long showboating however (including pointing down to Rude on commentary and rotating those hips……which the Ravishing One gets great delight from) and he’s far too close to the ropes……so as he drops to the mat, the Dude hooks onto the top rope and Pillman crashes tailbone first into the mat…..

…..from there the Dude goes back on the offence, (including an old school atomic drop to the tender tailbone of Pillman who sells it like a champ), before catching the Corporate Cannon and dropping him with that Double Underhook DDT…..much like earlier however HE is now too close to the ropes, as Pillman was able to drape a foot on the bottom rope to break up the pinfall at the last possible second……

……the crowd is red hot now as these two men go fist for fist towards the final hurdle, tumbling through the ropes and to the outside where they continue their brawl on the floor…..Pillman is clearly frustrated at the fact that he can’t put the Dude down for good, and whips him into the steel barricade before picking up a steel chair……unlike the Bret Hart/Steve Austin Match scheduled for later this evening however, this is NOT an anything goes match, and the official hops to the outside to snatch the chair away from the maniacal Pillman…..

……Pillman isn’t happy about that and begins arguing with the referee, before finally shoving him in the chest……only for the official to shove him back, reading him the riot act as he does…..Pillman holds his hands up in mock surrender, cackling in the referees face……The Dude sees this as an opportunity to attack, and charges Pillman from behind……the Corporate Cannon obviously saw him coming however, sidestepping at the last minute leaving Love to crash into the official, sending Dude, the Referee AND the steel chair flying…..and with nobody there to uphold the law, Pillman quickly snatched up the chair, crashing in over the back of Dude Love’s head…..

……Pillman rolls his opponent back into the ring and follows him in (steel chair in hand) and stands there poised, waiting for Love to get back to his feet……only to crack his skull a second time with a second chair shot……

……the official slowly regains consciousness and pulls himself back into the ring, just in time to see Pillman drop The Dude with the Time Bomb neckbreaker…….which after those two brutal chair shots, was more than enough to grant him the one, the two and the three.

Winner: Brian Pillman
Rating: A

Pillman gets his hand raised, but he’s not here to win…..he’s here to humiliate…..as he grabs a microphone from the outside, returning to the ring and hitting the half unconscious Love with a series of degrading open handed slaps, telling him that he’s a joke…..a nothing…….an insignificant worm……and if his “precious little girl” didn’t want the bad man reading her bedtime stories, she certainly won’t want a loser like the Dude reading then……before telling Love to do the poor girl a favour and put her up for adoption…….

”……or even better, just leave……and don’t go back…….because even no father at all is better than this sorry sack of garbage right here…………..you’re a sorry excuse for a man…..and an even sorrier excuse for a father……”

……Pillman starts laughing at that……but he’s not laughing long…..because his words clearly lit a fire under the Dude……who reaches up and shoves his fingers right into Pullman’s laughing mouth, locking on that debilitating Mandible Claw……

……Pillman is kicking and thrashing in an effort to escape, but The Dude has it locked on good…..fortunately for Pillman, all that thrashing about places his hand right next to the steel chair…..and he desperately grabs hold of it, swinging it again into the face of the Dude, who finally loosens his grip long enough for Pillman to escape and bolt from the ring, quickly heading to the back as a far from “cool” looking Dude Love drags himself to his feet……in fact, the Dude looks damn right p!ssed off as Pillman escapes……so much so that Jim Ross doubts we’ve seen the last of this rivalry.

Rating: B+

VS.

VS.

If Chris Jericho wins, the Light Heavyweight Division is retired….if Chris Jericho loses, he leaves the WWF

Scorpio (c) vs. Rey Mysterio vs. Chris Jericho vs. Dean Malenko

If you ever wondered just how petty Chris Jericho could be, then take a look at this match stipulation right here……Chris Jericho is a man who’s ego is so inflated, he would put his very own career on the line, just to win something that he didn’t really want anyway, in order to take that something away from someone else……petty right?…..and that something in this case just happens to be the WWF Light Heavyweight Championship……a Championship (and division) may I add, that Jericho himself claimed he wanted no part of……at least until Dean Malenko proclaimed Rey Mysterio as the Light Heavyweight to beat…..and well…..here we are…..

……speaking of Dean Malenko, he’s the only participant in this match that has yet to hold this Championship……Scorpio is the current (and only two time) Champion, Chris Jericho ended Scorpio’s FIRST reign……and Rey Mysterio is the man that holds the record for longest reign…..which is exactly why Malenko is so dead set on winning this one…..

……interestingly enough there is no member of the Nation out there to accompany Scorpio, and while Test DOES come down to ringside with Jericho, the official quickly sends him to the back, much to the chagrin of Jericho, who was clearly planning on using his big bodyguard as back-up…..and he could have done with some back up too, because it was clear from the start that all three of his opponents were gunning for him…..after all, he’s the one that wants to take their division away from them, and the moment the bell rings Scorpio, Mysterio and Malenko all charge at Jericho, bouncing him around the ring like a pinball until he finally bails from the ring and halfway up the aisle to get away from the assault…..

…..his three opponents are happy enough with that, after all, it only takes one of them to win to not only become the new champion, but save the division too……and being the saviour of the Light Heavyweight Division would be quite the bragging rights to have……the action continues inside the ring as Jericho looks on, with Malenko and Mysterio actually teaming up to work over Scorpio, but it’s not long before that competitive spirit puts a stop to that, as Malenko smashes Rey to the mat with a hard Powerbomb (that ALMOST scored him the pinfall until Jericho dashed to the ring to break it up, before quickly bailing to the outside again)…..

……that remained widely the story of the match, with Jericho keeping largely out of the way of the action, but never so far away that he’s not there to break up each pinfall attempt as needed….a fact that frustrates the other three competitors so much that Rey Mysterio finally throws caution to the wind, running across the ring and leaping onto the top rope, before flying off and crashing into Jericho with a springboard crossbody that wipes him out…..Mysterio is up first and rolls Jericho back into the ring and back into the action…..

…….now the men pair off, with Mysterio and Scorpio going at it on the outside, while Malenko does his best to defeat Jericho INSIDE the ring….and he’s doing a good job of it too, because the Man of 1000 holds looks to be utilising every last one of them as he unleashes an unrelenting attack on Jericho…..and considering that this whole Light Heavyweight debacle STARTED with these two, I suppose it’s only fitting that it should end with these two as well…..and it certainly looked like the end was in sight as Malenko wrapped Jericho up, before turning him over into the Canadian Maple Leaf…….bit of course, nothing is ever that simple in the WWF………

……because the bodyguard of Chris Jericho, Test comes running down the aisle, taking out both Scorpio and Mysterio in his way with a Double Clothesline as he comes, before sliding into the ring and almost taking Malenko’s head off with a Running Big Boot…..and he couldn’t have timed it any better, because Jericho was about five seconds away from submitting……

…..obviously that would usually be a disqualification, but as we know by now the rules in these multi man matches are rather more relaxed, and there is little the official can do as Test backs up into the corner, waiting for Malenko to stand…..before knocking him straight back down with another Big Boot……Rey Mysterio is back on his feet, and hops onto the apron, before propelling himself off the top rope with a Springboard Crossbody to Test, but the big man catches him and impressively presses the little luchador overhead, before dropping him over the top and down to the floor, where he crashes into the oncoming Scorpio……

……Jericho runs across the ring and comes off the middle rope with the Lionsault, crushing the air out of the downed Malenko before making the cover for the one, the two………

…….and the three……

…….and just like that, not only is Chris Jericho your NEW WWF Light Heavyweight Champion……but he’s your LAST WWF Light Heavyweight Champion…..because the light heavyweight division……is no more.

Winner: ……and New (and last) WWF Light Heavyweight Champion…..Chris Jericho
Rating: B

Jericho grabs hold of the Championship and hoists it high in the air in celebration, screaming that he told us so….”I told you I was the best light heavyweight there is…..the best light heavyweight there was…..and the LAST light heavyweight there ever will be……yeahhhhh babyyyy”…..before hopping out of the ring (giving Rey Mysterio a kick as he passes) before jumping onto Test’s back in celebration, still holding HIS Light Heavyweight Title high in the air as his bodyguard piggybacks him all the way to the back, leaving Malenko, Mysterio, Scorpio……and probably every other light heavyweight in the back…..with a look of utter disappointment in their faces.

Rating: N/A

VS.
VS.

WWF World Tag Team Championship: Fatal Four Way Match; The Losing Team will be Fired

The New Age Outlaws (c) vs. Al Snow & Chris Candido vs. Edge & Christian vs. Harlem Heat

From one fatal four way title match with the possibility of a competitor being fired, we go to a fatal four way title match, where a competitor will DEFINITELY be fired……two competitors actually…..because the team that loses this match, also loses their WWF career…..as the reigning WWF Tag Team Champions, the New Age Outlaws, put the titles on the line against the teams of Al Snow and Chris Candido, the young Canadian team of Edge and Christian…..and last but not least, Harlem Heat……

……there may be four teams in this match, but only two men can be legal at any one time…..and although you can’t actually win the match on the apron, you’re not going to be the man to LOSE the match either…..something that (after essentially leaving the Road Dogg without a partner last Monday on Raw) Edge and Christian seem keen to capitalise on, because they are very reluctant to get into this one, often out and out refusing to tag into the match…..

……the other three teams in this one are more than eager to make sure they stay in this one…..there are blind tags galore as the illegal team on the apron does whatever they can to get themselves into the match and into a position to win……the Outlaws are particularly keen to get Edge and Christian in the match following the events from Raw, and eventually Al Snow grants them their wish, slapping Christian on the chest to get him into the match (much to the annoyance of Candido, but then pretty much Al Snow does annoys Chris Candido so there’s no change there)…..

……once they are actually in the match, Edge and Christian begin to dominate the Outlaws, with Christian being only a Booker T save away from picking up the victory on Road Dogg after hitting him with the Downward Spiral……and clearly frustrated at being stuck on the apron, both members of Harlem Heat then hit the ring…….which brings in Candido too……and finally Al Snow, who took some convincing as he stood in his corner with the Tag rope in hand, telling Candido that he can’t come in until he was tagged……Candido slaps Al on the arm, telling him ”there, you’re tagged” which apparently was good enough for Al……

……from there a pier 8 brawl breaks out, as all four teams begin going at it……and eventually the official decides to just go with it, counting the pinfall attempts for anyone willing to try it, regardless of who’s legal…..with so many men in the ring however it’s nigh on impossible to secure a victory without someone being there to break it up…..eventually however Harlem Heat and the New Age Outlaws find themselves fighting on the floor, which just leaves Snow and Candido to battle it out against Edge and Christian……Candido is determined to pick up the win here too, and he goes all out in an attempt to secure the Tag Team Titles……

…….Snow and Edge both go over the top rope from an Al Snow Clothesline, just as Candido slams Christian to the mat, heading to the top rope where he’s looking to hit the Suicide Blonde Diving Headbutt…….Christian isn’t done however, and he’s up top to meet him, setting up a Superplex…….

……Candido is able to break free however, and the two men jostle for position up top, until Candido finally manoeuvres Christians head down between his legs……..and sends him crashing hard to the mat with the Blonde Bombshell Top Rope Powerbomb…….and Christian is done……..Candido makes the cover……….

……for the one…….

……two…….

……..and quick as lightning Billy Gunn dives into the ring, tosses Candido by the scruff of the neck to the outside…….and covers Christian himself…….and this time there’s no one to break up the pin…….and as the official strikes the mat for the third time, the New Age Outlaws steal a victory……..and Edge and Christian are out of a job.

Winner(s): ……and Still WWF Tag Team Champions…..The New Age Outlaws
Rating: B

VS.

Career vs. Flock

Psycho Sid vs. Raven

We air a quick recap video highlighting the factors that have lead up to our next match (while Edge and Christian receive an unsympathetic goodbye from the crowd) and then it’s straight back down to ringside for Psycho Sid vs. Raven……

……and it’s a packed house at ringside for this one, as Sid has Jake Roberts in his corner, while the entire Flock are out with Raven to see their fate decided…..because yes, if Raven wins this one, Psycho Sid (much like Edge and Christian a moment ago) will be our of a job……but should Sid be victorious, Raven’s Flock will be no more…..and if Christopher Daniels and The Dogs of War’s recent actions are anything to go by, they may well be rooting for Sid, because these three men appear to be tiring of Raven’s intent to hold them down…..

……the match itself wasn’t great…..Raven IS capable of great matches, he’s had them with the Undertaker and even young Rey Mysterio……but Psycho Sid, (for all his popularity) is no Rey Mysterio…..and he’s certainly no Undertaker, so expecting Raven to carry him to a great match was an ask too far…..the match was also hindered slightly by the finish…..but more on that later……

…..Raven spent a good portion of the match spitting venom at the Snake on the floor…..telling him that he moulded Raven in his image, and now he doesn’t like what he sees? Telling him that he’s nothing but a hypocrite……Jake on the other hand assured Raven that all of this is for his own good…..I don’t know about that….having Psycho Sid hammering blows into you doesn’t sound like any good at all to me…..but then Raven seems to like pain, so this should be like all his Christmases rolled into one…..or Hanukkah’s I guess since Raven is Jewish….because Sid certainly unleashed the pain…..to his credit, Raven doesn’t back down an inch, and despite the size difference between the two men Raven more than holds his own, coming out of this looking incredibly strong……

……oh and remember that finish I was talking about? Well here it is……because with Raven firmly in control, he grabs hold of Sid from behind and walks him over to the ropes, ordering Daniels and the Dogs of War to get up onto the apron……they reluctantly comply…..but they aren’t so forthcoming with Raven’s next order……he wants them to each slap Sid across the face to show their loyalty……all three men repeatedly refuse, until Raven angrily throws Sid to the ground, screaming at the three that they are selfish ingrates…..before he finally nails Wrath across the face with a degrading backhanded slap…..

……obviously Wrath doesn’t take too kindly to that…..shoving Raven back with such force that we collides with (a once again standing) Psycho Sid…..who spins Raven around…..kicks him in the gut……and Powerbombs him straight to the mat……and as Daniels, Wrath and Crush hold DeMott back, the official registers the one, the two and the three……Psycho Sid is still employed, while the Flock…….are no more……

…..incidentally it was less the finish of the match that dinged the rating…..but the winner of the match……because while Psycho Sid is over, he’s not as over as Raven, who’s popularity has soared despite limited in ring action……but then sometimes, even your most over wrestlers need to lose to someone lesser than them now and then if it advances the story.

Winner: Psycho Sid
Rating: B-

Jake enters the ring to raise Psycho Sid’s hand, before looking down at Raven with an almost sorry looking expression on his face, telling his former protege that this is hurting Jake more than it’s hurting Raven…..before patting Sid on the back as the two men head to the back…….DeMott dives into the ring to check on his now FORMER boss, helping the groggy Raven to his feet as Daniels and The Dogs of War climb slowly into the ring……

……Raven steadies himself on his feet before shoving DeMott to one side, then kicks the giggling idiot in the gut, before driving him into the mat with an Evenflow DDT……

……Raven gets back to a standing base and begins berating Daniels, Crush and Wrath…..how can they do this? After everything he’s done for them? What about him? What about Raven?……before (in his second mistake of the evening, his first being the backhanded slap to Wrath) he slaps Crush right in the mouth……and much like his partner, the big Hawaiian doesn’t take too kindly to that…..and neither does Wrath……and the two Dogs of War reach out and grab hold of their former master……gripping him by the throat and slamming him hard into the mat with a huge Double Chokeslam……..

……and they’re not finished there…..because they motion at Christopher Daniels to head up top……which he does…..springing off the top turnbuckle with that double jump moonsault and crashing down onto the prone Raven, before all three men officially wipe their hands of the Flock and head to the back…..and all in all, this was not a good night for Raven

Rating: B+

VS.

VS.

WCW European Championship: Fatal Four Way Match
The Loser of the Match will be Fired

The Rock (c) vs. Matt Hardy vs. Ken Shamrock vs. Jeff Jarrett

So far we have had two title matches……one Champion has lost his gold (and his entire division) while our Tag Team Champions retained……its time for our third title match of the evening now…..and much like our Tag Team encounter, this is a fatal four way…..and the loser WILL be fired……unlike our Tag Team encounter however, all four competitors are legal in this one fall to a finish match…..

….there are some big names in this match…..the Rock and Jeff Jarrett in particular who have both seen their status rise to main event level in recent weeks……then you have Matt Hardy and Ken Shamrock, two men who are both a few steps below……quite a few steps below in Matt Hardy’s case, who is currently at more of a Midcard level, and way out of his element here, going from a Tag Team wrestler after the departure of his brother is a big step in itself, let alone having his first high profile singles encounter being against three other men…..two of which are genuine main eventers…..and not only that, should he be the man to lose, he’ll be fired too…..no pressure then Matt……

…….obviously learning from their earlier Light Heavyweight Championship loss, The Rock does NOT come out alone like Scorpio did…..instead he is accompanied by the menacing figure that is Kama….unfortunately for the Rock however, Kama is caught by the official interfering one too many times and finally gets ejected from ringside and ordered to the back by the referee, leaving the Rock p!ssed off and without backup….although in all fairness, the Rock is so good that he doesn’t actually NEED backup, he’s more than good enough to win this on his own merit……and he focuses the attention of his frustration on young Matt Hardy, who takes a serious beating here……

……meanwhile, Ken Shamrock has resumed his battle with Ted DiBiase’s Million Dollar Corporation, this time going at it with Jeff Jarrett…Double J has a wealth of experience despite his relatively young age, and while he doesn’t have the out and out fighting ability of a Ken Shamrock, he does have plenty of WRESTLING ability….and this is a wrestling match after all….he also has a whole list of underhand tactics in his back pocket, not to mention that Acoustic Equaliser, which he attempts to utilise on the outside…..Shamrock avoids it at the last minute however, and Jarrett smashes the guitar to pieces on the steel post…….the near miss however seems to have p!ssed Shamrock off to the extreme…..in fact, you could say the man snaps, as he beats the life out of Jarrett all the way up the aisle and onto the entrance ramp, where he crashes Double J onto the steel ramp with a belly to belly, before locking on that Ankle Lock…….and make no mistake, Jarrett is tapping out like a madman…..unfortunately this match has to finish in the ring, not that Shamrock looks to have any intention of breaking the hold….a fact which according to Rick Rude, shows us that Shamrock is letting his temper cost him the match, because if he just released the hold and took Jarrett back to the ring, he could probably win this……

…….speaking of winning this, while all of that is going on, that’s exactly what The Rock and Matt Hardy are trying to do inside the ring, as Hardy seems to have found his stride and has the European Champion in real trouble……….

……when the huge monstrosity that is Nelson makes his way out from the back…..the big man of the Nation hasn’t been seen since rupturing his bladder a month or so back, but it looks like he’s fit, healthy…..and after Kama’s earlier ejection…..ready to take care of Nation business…..

……he climbs into the ring and instantly puts a stop to Matt Hardy’s offence….and the Rock stands back and watches as he easily picks up Hardy, positioning him for a powerslam that would certainly finish him off……it’s do or die here however, and Matt Hardy does whatever he has to do in order to escape Nelson’s clutches……he gouges at the eyes……

……Nelson drops Hardy, before flailing blindly, clearly unable to see for the moment….the Rock charges at Matt, but the youngster sidesteps it and The Rock runs directly into Nelson, who quickly wraps his hands around his own team mates throat, driving him into the mat with a near 600lb Chokebomb……Nelson clearly has no idea that he’s just destroyed his own partner, as he stands rubbing at his eyes……Hardy isn’t about to miss the opportunity however…..and quickly drags the crushed Rock to his feet……before dropping him immediately with a Twist of Fate……

……and with Shamrock still trying to twist Jeff Jarretts ankle clean off up the aisle, and Nelson unable to see anything……there is no one there to break up Matt Hardy’s pinball attempt, as the official counts the one…….

…….the two……..

………and the three……..

…….and just like that, the biggest underdog in the match, Matt Hardy, is your NEW European Champion……and The Rock…..the highest profile star in this match……..is gone…..and by the hands of his own team mate none the less……Faarooq will NOT be happy about that.

Winner: ……and New WWF European Champion…..Matt Hardy
Rating: B+

The bell rings and Nelson raises his hand in celebration….clearly believing that he just helped the Rock on the road to victory…..and it’s only when Matt Hardy’s music begins to play, that he realises something isn’t right…….and he soon KNOWS something isn’t right when the rest of the Nation arrive……Faarooq is p!ssed…..the Rock was clearly the crown jewel of the Nation of Domination, and now….thanks to Nelson…..that crown jewel is no more……

……the whole group begin screaming at Nelson, dressing him down for his (very) costly mistake…..Nelson profusely apologises for his mistake, explaining that his vision was impaired by Matt Hardy’s gouge to the eyes…..that’s simply not good enough for Faarooq…..who tells Nelson to “get your fat ass to the back” because the Nation will deal with him there, before telling the crowd that this is NOT the last they have seen of the Rock in the Nation of Domination…..there’s no way that “the man” will get away with this…..but right now, he has a title match to prepare for.

Rating: B+

VS.

The Wolfpac vs. Ric Flair & Arn Anderson


There aren’t many matches this evening without SOME kind of stipulation attached…..this is one of them however, as the Wolfpac’s Hall and Waltman face off against Ric Flair and Arn Anderson…..and when you consider the fact that out of these four men, only Sean Waltman could be considered “in his prime”…..(in fact, Flair, Anderson…..AND Hall are all beginning to see their match qualities slipping as their abilities decline)…..this was actually a very good match……

…..of course that’s to be expected I suppose…..Flair and Anderson have already proved they can still have great matches, you only have to look at their battles with the Hart Foundation to see that…..and if nothing else, Ric Flair is still Ric Freaking Flair, and if there’s one man that knows how to get a crowd fired up, it’s the Nature Boy….so naturally the atmosphere for this one was great…..

…..Hall and Waltman have been spinning their wheels really for the last few months, due to the ongoing Kevin Nash and Shawn Michaels feud…..but one way or another they are finally going to be done with that by the end of the evening, and whether Nash goes, or Michaels goes, you’ve got to believe that these two Wolfpac members will be looking to step up their game, because while they may have been Wrestling predominantly as a tag team as of late, Waltman proved only a few weeks ago that singles gold is just as attainable, and a victory here tonight would certainly be a big confidence booster…..

……it’s the smallest man in the match playing the face in peril role, with Flair and Anderson isolating Sean Waltman while focusing their offence on the knee of the former European Champion…..Rude claims that’s smart Wrestling by the two veterans, because not only does it weaken the legs in preparation for the Nature Boys patented Figure Four, but it also cuts Waltman’s offensive arsenal in half, because if he can’t walk, he can’t fly either…..Rude might have a point there….but while he can’t fly, he CAN still hop….which is why he’s able to nail Anderson right in the back of the head with an Enziguri kick…..before dragging himself to the corner to tag in The Bad Guy……

……Hall hits the ring and goes to work on both Flair and Anderson….he’s plenty fired up after being stuck on the apron for half the match, sending Anderson flying with the Fallaway Slam, before sending Flair across the ring with an Irish Whip to the corner, which sends the Nature Boy up and over, landing on the apron…..Flair runs the length of the apron and quickly climbs to the top of the opposite corner…..but we all know that never ends well, as Hall catches hold of the Nature Boy before he can leap, and tosses him halfway across the ring…..

…….Ahmed struggles to his feet…..lifting the dead weight that is Scott Steiner off the mat…..before picking the WWF Champion up in the air and driving him down to the canvas with the Pearl River Plunge, before making the cover……and as Jim Cornette jumps up and down in anticipation on the outside, the official counts the one…..the two……and the three…….this one is over……Scott Steiner has been robbed……and Ahmed Johnson is your new WWF Champion.

Winner: ……and New WWF Champion……Ahmed Johnson
