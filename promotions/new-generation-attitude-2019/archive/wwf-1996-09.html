Week 1, September 1996

Kiel Center

ATT: 15,051

TV: 16.38

……….!!!GONG!!!……….

Welcome to another edition of Monday Night Raw…..and we’re starting out with a bang tonight…..or a gong at least…..the gong that signals the arrival of The Undertaker, who makes his methodical walk down the aisle, with manager Paul Bearer by his side. The Deadman enters the ring and brings up the lights, before bringing a microphone to his lips…..obviously the Phenom has something on his mind. He addresses Kane and Jim Cornette, stating that the longer they insist on continuing with this charade that The Undertaker had anything to do with the death of their parents, the longer the Deadman will spend torturing his brother in the fires of hell. He claims that the lies and deceit do nothing more than tarnish the memory of two people, who paid the ultimate price, when the only crime they were guilty of is loving their son……

“……Kane…..The Undertaker was born on that fateful night twenty years ago…..as all sense of humanity and normalcy burned away with my family and my home…..ultimately it was YOU Kane, that woke the Demon…..it was YOU Kane, that cast out the light from my world, forcing me to embrace the darkness……and it will be YOU Kane….who’s going to wish that those fires had served him the same fate that it dealt our parents…..when I make your miserable life a living hell…..”

The Deadman says that he accepts the challenge laid out by Jim Cornette…..he WILL face his brother at the Survivor Series…..on not only the anniversary of his parents passing, but the anniversary of the Deadman’s debut here in the WWF, six years earlier, at the 1990 Survivor Series……because at the Thanksgiving Tradition, at a time of family holidays, the Undertaker can think of nothing he would be more thankful for, than to have the opportunity to decimate his brother once and for all…….

“……..and Mankind…….you’re right…..three months is a long, long time……walk away while you still can…..because the next time you find yourself locked inside one of my caskets…….it will be for an eternity……because I will make sure……that you, Rest……in………Peace…….”

There’s a blaze of fire that erupts from the corner posts, signalling the arrival of Kane, Jim Cornette…..and rather reluctantly bringing up the rear, Mankind. The trio stay far away from the ring, choosing to remain at the top of the entrance way…..but unsurprisingly Jim Cornette has something to say……

“……you know what Deadman, that’s the first sensible thing I’ve heard you say…..that’s an excellent idea, and we accept your challenge….”

The Undertaker looks slightly confused by that….unsure as to what challenge he is supposed to have levied…..and Cornette smiles at the confusion….

“……you know……don’t be coy…..like you said, three months is a long time away……so I’m going to give you something to keep you occupied….because in just three weeks, we have a little event coming up called In Your House…..and at that event, it’s going to be The Undertaker…….vs Mankind….(Mankind’s head snaps up at the sound of that, clearly he wasn’t consulted first)…..in a good old fashioned….Casket Match”

Mankind likes the sound of that even less, and looks as though he is about to protest the decision, until Kane snaps his head in his direction, quieting him with a glare…..the Undertaker is happy enough though, and it looks like the Deadman and the Madman will be locking horns one more time.

Rating: A*

VS.

The Steiner Brothers vs. The Dogs of War

The opening match of the evening is a tag team match, that Jim Ross promises is going to be a full contact, hard hitting slugfest…..and he wasn’t wrong. The Steiner Brothers have never been afraid to mix it up, just as adapt at punching a man in the face as they are at suplexing him out of his boots…..while the Dogs of War are two big, bad, brawling machines. The Dogs came out on the losing end of their debut bout here in the WWF, but they dominated the entire match, only coming up short due to a fluke count out.

Unfortunately, here they are up against arguably one of the greatest Tag Teams of all time….and not only that….the Steiner’s are p####d and out to make a statement, with Scott having one eye on a possible clash with Psycho Sid at In Your House. Neither of the Dog’s are seven feet tall…..but they ain’t exactly Rey Mysterio either…..but with a glimpse into Sid’s future, Scotty still manages to send them both up and over with consecutive belly to belly suplexes.

Wrath and Crush are able to control the match for a while, isolating Rick….but they can’t do enough to bring them the win, with the Steiner’s catching a pinfall victory on Wrath, following a Steinerline/German Suplex double team combination

Winner(s): The Steiner Brothers

Rating: B

The Steiner’s are celebrating a good victory, shouting into the camera that they’re coming for Sid…..apparently he heard them, because the Million Dollar Man’s theme starts playing, and manager and client walk out from the back. They pause at the entrance way however, where DiBiase produces a microphone…..

“….Steiner morons…..not only did you show, just how thick headed you are by turning down my management services……you then went and made another moronic mistake, by challenging this man….this mountain…..to a match at In Your House. Now as far as the match goes, Sid will be more than happy to cut you down to size Scott Steiner, but he WON’T be coming down to that ring tonight…..what I will give you however…..is a lesson to learn……and that lesson, is that everybody has a price….for the Million Dollar Man…..”

The Steiner’s look more baffled that usual by that comment, but as they stand there trying to decipher DiBiase’s words, they miss the fact that two men have climbed into the ring behind them……

……smartly dressed in suits and sunglasses, McMahon claims that they look suspiciously like the Godwinns……if the Godwinns had class counters Lawler. Rick and Scott sense the men behind them, but it’s too late to stop the assault from coming and get soundly beaten to the mat, before the sharp dressed men pick the brothers up, only to drop them again with synchronised inverted DDT’s, before heading up the aisle to join Sid and their apparent new Manager Ted DiBiase, who is cackling away with that signature laugh of his, as we go to commercial.

Rating: B+

After that brief word from our sponsors, it’s back to the show, and we find ourselves at the announce desk, where Vince McMahon, Jim Ross and Jerry Lawler are recounting the events of last week, which saw the KLiQ run amok, systematically beating down anyone they judged to have slighted them. Marc Mero, Kokina and Rikishi are all absent tonight as a result of the KLiQ’s actions last week, while Chris Jericho, Ahmed Johnson and Rocky Maivia are all here and looking for revenge. All three commentators condemn the actions of the KLiQ, but none more so than Jerry Lawler, who is adamant that we will see the group get what’s coming to them tonight…..

……speak of the devil, because the KLiQ are here in force. They strut their way down the aisle, but they don’t head for the ring, instead they make their way over to the commentary table, where they inform McMahon and his team that they are out here tonight to call the action in the next match, when that paper Champion, Rey Mysterio, defends Sean Waltman’s WWF Lightheavyweight Championship…..so hit the road jack, before the road hits you. McMahon and Ross quickly exit their seats….Lawler is less forthcoming, until Kevin Nash forcibly removes him from his seat. Lawler looks ready to throw down, but Vince and Jim Ross pull him away, telling him it would be suicide. Justin Credible is particularly obnoxious here, snatching the King’s crown from the desk and wearing it himself.

Vince and co head to the back for the duration of the next match, while Shawn, Nash and Hall take up residence on the call. All six men turn and glare at the Spanish announce team, who rapidly get to their feet and depart as well…..tonight, ladies and gentlemen, your Comentario español will be bought to you by Triple H, Sean Waltman and King Justin Credible.

Rating: B

VS.

WWF Light Heavyweight Championship Match

Rey Mysterio (c) vs. Jerry Lynn

Much like the British Bulldog last week, Rey Mysterio didn’t wait long to get his first title defence under his belt….and like Davey Boy, has vowed to be a fighting Champion, taking on any and all comers, which is why we have two fan favourites facing off here. I don’t think the Champion was anticipating the audience at the commentary tables that he had in the form of the KLiQ however….who spend most of the match running the little luchador down, particularly Waltman on the Spanish announce table, who calls Rey a thief, just like all you Mexicans listening in.

These two had a good little match, and it’s a shame that the ‘commentators’ didn’t pay much attention to it. Instead they spent the match cracking jokes, commenting on Rey’s height and generally vowing to regain all the Titles that they recently lost. In the end Mysterio was able to complete his first successful defence, catching Lynn with a Hurracanrana into a pinfall combination.

Winner: …..and Still WWF Light Heavyweight Champion, Rey Mysterio

Rating: C+

Mysterio picks up the win and collects his Championship, when Waltman stands up with a microphone, declaring that Rey stole that title from him at Summerslam, but don’t get too used to it, because the second the ink is dry on the contract for the rematch, he’s going to be taking it back……“or maybe we’ll just take it back now”. Nash, Hall, Waltman and Credible (still wearing the Kings Crown) move out from commentary and surround the ring, each standing by one of the four aprons on the floor. Mysterio knows what’s coming next though, and he’s not sticking around for it. He runs the ring, leaps and springboards off the top rope…..right over Kevin Nash’s head…..and landing on the floor behind him, where he rolls through to absorb the impact and immediately bails into the crowd and away to safety. Nash has the decency to mutter that it was a “pretty impressive” move into a nearby camera, while Shawn Michaels sends us (rather professionally all things considered) to the back for our next segment.

Rating: B-

We do go to the back, where we find Dok Hendrix standing by with Al Snow, Bill DeMott and Head. Al does not look overly pleased though….and is in the middle of a disagreement with Head. Dok tries to ask Snow a number of questions, but Al and Head have other things on their mind…..

“……..do you think I’m not well aware of that?……well obviously you don’t, or you wouldn’t be saying it………..you can be very hurtful sometimes do you know that, it’s almost like you’ve got no heart at all…………I have thought about it……..I have…….YES I HAVE!!!…….well I don’t care if you think it’s a bad idea, I came this far, I’m not going to stop now…………..yes I know about the Bingo Hall?…….”

Dok finally manages to attract Al’s attention away from Head long enough to finally ask him a question or two. The first being how he felt about finally revealing the face of the mystery man behind the mask…..and secondly…..what’s he going to do about it now he has?

“……mystery man? That’s no mystery man Betty, I know exactly who that is….that my friend, is Raven…..”

Dok: “Raven? Like the black feathery bird, Raven?”

“…….like the harbinger of death, Raven you mean Betty?…….this isn’t your normal everyday weirdo like Bill over there….”

“HEY!”

“….no offence Bill, you know you’re weird…..”

“Yeah, but still……”

“This is the David Koresh of the wrestling world, the man has got a serious problem…..never mind the New Generation, he’s the leader of the Jilted Generation…..the Why Me Generation…..and to top all that off, he’s a seriously sick puppy too…..puppy?…..birdy?…….whatever, the man’s nuts……….but I digress…..what am I going to do about it you asked?……..I’m going to……..Head, shut up, I’m doing this…..I’m going to challenge him to a match……”

“And I accept…..”

…..with that the man himself walks into shot, as Raven had obviously been lurking in the shadows somewhere nearby. He tells Al that…..

“….clearly my reputation proceeds me, but you can’t always believe what you hear…..I’m not a leader of any generation…..I’m not a leader of anything….I don’t lead, I inspire….I inspire greatness, I inspire potential…..I unlock hidden depths and transcend people to places they could never possibly imagine…..the right people……worthy people…….people who don’t shun me…..or reject me…….I offered you the world on a plate Al……I offered you a chance at immortality…..and you rejected me……YOU rejected ME……why Al? Do you think you’re better than me? You bend over backwards to ensure the safety and survival of a simpleton like Bill DeMott…..but what about me? WHAT ABOUT RAVEN?…..I could have moulded your mind into a thing of beauty….a thing of brilliance…..instead you’d rather spend your time talking to a plastic head, longing for fairy-tails in a world of nightmares. I’m a sick puppy? Heh…..how does that saying go? You ain’t seen nothing yet…….I’ll be your nightmare Al…..Quote the Raven…..Nevermore.”

With that Raven walks away, leaving a giggling DeMott and a for once lost for words Dok Hendrix both staring at Al…..

“……see Betty, I told you he was nuts……

Rating: B

VS.

Bart Gunn vs. Triple H (w/Sunny)

We go back down to ringside now, and we are back to our regularly scheduled announce team, as Vince McMahon, Jim Ross and Jerry Lawler are back on the call…..there’s no sign of The Kings crown however….obviously Justin Credible took a liking to that. The KLiQ might be gone from the commentary table, but we still have a KLiQster in the ring (and a busty blonde KLiQette at ringside) as Triple H is set to go one on one with Bart Gunn.

Neither of these men had a good night at Summerslam, with Helmsley dropping the Intercontinental Championship to the British Bulldog, and Bart coming out on the losing end to his brother Billy. Hunter fares better here though, because with Bart still adjusting to life as a singles wrestler, Helmsley is able to finish him off with a Pedigree in the middle of the ring for the one, two, three.

Winner: Triple H

Rating: B-

With Bart disposed of, Sunny enters the ring with two microphones, one of which she hands to Helmsley, who uses it to call out the man who cost him the Intercontinental Championship, Skip. He tells the Bodydonna to come down to the ring and fight him now, like a man…….or is that the problem? That Skips only half the man, that Triple H is? “…..you’ll come out here and attack me when my back is turned, just so long as you have a weapon in your hand…..but when I’m ready and waiting, you’re nowhere to be seen….and THAT my friend, is why Sunny here spends every night with MY weapon in HER hand….and not yours….” Triple H continues to run Skip down, ranging from threats of violence, to more crude humour and d##k jokes.

When it becomes clear that Skip isn’t there this evening, Sunny takes a turn on the mic. She tells Skip that she doesn’t know exactly what he thinks he’s doing…..but it’s because of him that her man isn’t wearing Intercontinental Gold anymore….“and you know that gold is my favourite colour, just like diamonds are my best friends.”….she goes on to say that her least favourite colour is green….and that’s exactly what Skip is, green with envy because she traded up to a better model. She finishes up by telling him to go back to whatever hole it was that he crawled out of…..because if he insists on sticking his nose in KLiQ business?…well everyone saw last week, how well that worked out for people.

Helmsley once again takes the mic a final time, telling Skip to listen to the advice of his former manager, and make Summerslam a one off appearance….“because I’ve already taken your woman…..don’t make me take your career too……”

Rating: C+

With Kokina and Rikishi at home, resting up from the double assault last week, that means it’s young Rocky Maivia’s chance to come to the forefront of the Son’s of Samoa, as he takes part in his first real solo interview with Dok Hendrix here tonight. He does a decent job too, the kid might be greener that goose s##t but he’s obviously got a mouth on him waiting to be developed. He cuts a double promo here, first on the KLiQ and his opponent tonight Justin Credible for their heinous beat down of Kokina…..and secondly on the Texas Long Riders and Jesse James, for taken advantage of the Sons when they were in no condition to fight back. He promises that by In Your House, Rikishi and Kokina will be back to full strength……and they’ll be looking for revenge against Windham, Bradshaw and James.

But first things first, and tonight the KLiQ are gonna get a taste of what this Island boy can do.

Rating: B

VS.

Rocky Maivia vs. Justin Credible

This is a bit test for the young Samoan. Justin Credible might not be Shawn Michaels or Kevin Nash…..or even Sean Waltman…..but he’s still a member of the KLiQ, and that carries some weight around here. Credible obviously doesn’t sweat the relative newcomer, because for a change we see a KLiQ member flying solo….he does however come to the ring with Jerry Lawler’s crown perched on top of his head….which the King isn’t happy about.

Maivia is out alone too, but that’s more to do with circumstances than choice, because neither Kokina or Rikishi are here tonight. Credible confidence may be justified, because he does dominate a good portion of the match……he doesn’t dominate the whole thing though, and in the end Maivia is able to catch him on a cross body attempt, hoist him up….and deliver a running Shoulder Breaker that gives the youngster his first big victory here in the WWF.

Winner: Rocky Maivia

Rating: C-

Maivia rolls out of the ring and heads to the back, celebrating his impressive victory. Credible remains in the ring, looking thoroughly embarrassed by his defeat….not as embarrassed as he is when The King stands up on the commentary table with a microphone, and begins mocking the KLiQ man, telling him that he’d better hand that crown back, because the only thing he is…..is the King of the Losers. Credible looks like he’s considering and act of physicality towards Lawler, but in the end he just snatched up the stolen crown and storms to the back.

Rating: B-

We head to the back once more, where a busy Dok Hendrix is standing by with another interview. This time with the participants of the next match, Chris Jericho and Ahmed Johnson. Jericho is still sporting the marks that caused his crimson mask from the week prior, while the only thing Ahmed has on his face is the look of angry determination. Jericho’s promo last week was somewhat lighthearted, this week he is anything but. He tells Dok that the one thing he has always tried to do since arriving here in the WWF, is entertain. Entertain the fans around the world when I step between those ropes, and entertain himself…..because if he’s having fun, then his fans are having fun…..and last week, that wasn’t fun. He says that he took the KLiQ lightly and paid the price….luckily he still has his devilishly handsome good looks, even if he is sporting fresh battle scars. He says that last week was a learning curve…..and he learned…..he learned to strike them before they strike you….and strike hard, because they come with back up…..well tonight HE has back up….in the form of Ahmed Johnson.

Ahmed cuts the usual Ahmed “mumble mouth” Johnson promo…..it’s loud, it’s intense……it’s somewhat coherent…..the general theme seems to be that Scott Hall is going to pay for dropping him on his head last week…..he’s gonna get a little taste of him tonight, but he wants the main course in three weeks time at In Your House……because one way or another, Scott Hall and all the KLiQ are going down.

Rating: B

VS.

Chris Jericho & Ahmed Johnson vs. The KLiQ

It’s time for the semi main event of the evening, as the miss matched pairing of Ahmed Johnson and Chris Jericho go up against the well oiled machine that is Kevin Nash and Scott Hall of the KLiQ. On paper the former WWF Tag Team Champions should walk this….on tag team experience alone….but this is the WWF, and on paper doesn’t matter, because as Vince McMahon has been telling us for years, anything can happen.

What happened tonight was Ahmed and Jericho played off each other’s strengths tremendously…..with Ahmed’s strength being….well, his strength…..and Jericho using his speed and agility to compliment it. The KLiQ on the other hand, utilised their team approach, cutting the ring in half with Scott Hall doing most of the leg work, while Nash moved in for the heavy hits. In this case it was Chris Jericho that was the main focus of their attack, presumably in an attempt to soften the youngster up for Shawn Michaels……surprisingly though it didn’t work….because come the end of the match, Jericho sent Nash tumbling to the outside, leaving Ahmed to hit Scott Hall with the Pearl River Plunge……which was then followed up by a Lionsault from Jericho, giving a surprisingly squeaky clean pinfall victory to the face contingent.

Winner(s): Ahmed Johnson and Chris Jericho

Rating: B+

Nash stands on the outside with a look of shock on his face, while Hall…..Hall is still flat on his back in the middle of the ring. In an instant the WWF Champion comes storming out from the back, demanding to know what the hell that display by Hall and Nash was? Nash claims it was simply a fluke, and that they can take care of this little “problem” right now. The pair turn and look as though they are about to enter the ring where the victorious Lionheart and Ahmed are waiting…..they never get the opportunity though, because in a flash, Chris Jericho comes flying over the top rope, taking out the Heartbreak Kid and pummelling him with blow after blow after blow. Nash turns to help his buddy, but Ahmed is out of the ring to intercept him and we soon have a pier six brawl on the floor, that is only stopped when an army of backstage workers flood the arena to separate the four men. Vince McMahon frantically sends us too commercial, while Nash can be heard screaming in the background that Ahmed and Jericho are ” DEAD MEN WALKING!!!”

Rating: B+

VS.

Owen Hart vs. Steve Austin

It’s main event time, and after seeing Bret Hart defeat Brian Pillman last week on Monday Night Raw, it’s time to repeat the process tonight with their opposites, as Stone Cold Steve Austin goes one on one with The King of Harts, Owen Hart. This match isn’t quite on the level of their partners match last week, but it’s still a very, very good TV bout. Owen has aspirations of wearing Tag Team Gold with his brother…..with Bret’s win last week, another Hart win here tonight would surely give the challengers a serious confidence boost ahead of their upcoming title match….but if there’s one thing we’ve learnt about Stone Cold Steve Austin, it’s that he won’t go down without a fight……and he doesn’t here either…..because come the end of the match, much like last week, we see one half of the WWF Tag Team Champions locked in the Sharpshooter….unlike Brian Pillman and his instantaneous submission however, Steve Austin doesn’t look like he’s going to quit anytime soon, and the commentators question exactly how long Hart would have to have the hold locked on, before Austin begins to risk serious injury?

……turns out we aren’t finding out tonight…..because Brian Pillman comes running down the aisle, slides into the ring and nails Owen with the tag title belt in the back of the head to break up the hold, causing an obvious and blatant disqualification loss for Austin, with back to back victories for the Harts.

Winner: Owen Hart (via Disqualification)

Rating: B+

Austin turns on his partner, demanding to know what he thinks he’s doing…..“you just cost me the damn match….Stone Cold Steve Austin don’t quit……”and you didn’t quit, Pillman retorts…..” I got you disqualified…..because a broken leg from being locked in that Sharpshooter, could have cost us the damn Tag Titles….” Austin reluctantly concedes the point….“but I ain’t happy about it son…..” Pillman tells him that he’d rather apologise to one half of the Tag Team Champs now, than see the Harts take the titles at In Your House because Austin was no better than a lame mule.

In the meantime Owen has groggily gotten back to his feet, only to be met with a kick in the gut by Pillman and a Stone Cold Stunner from Austin that drops him back to the mat. The Lose Cannon smirks down at Hart, telling his partner to cheer up. He might have lost the match but they can still have some fun. This at least seems to please Austin, and the two men proceed to stomp the hell out of a fallen Hart…..

………at least until Bret and Davey run down to make the save. The four men brawl, with no clear advantage being gained by either team….until Owen makes it back to his feet, giving the Hart Foundation the edge that forces Austin and Pillman to finally snatch hold of their tag team titles and bolt to the back, leaving the Hart’s to celebrate, much to the delight of a cheering crowd.

Rating: A*

Final Rating: B+

Final Thoughts

We had our biggest win yet in the Monday Night ratings war this week, pulling ahead of Nitro by more than a full point, as our 16.38 easily dispatched their 15.00…..not a small feat when you take into account the fact that six months ago, WCW was beating us by a full point….and more, every week….and it’s not like Nitro has lost many viewers, they have always been in the 15.00 range…..it’s more to do with the fact that we have gained so many viewers, because we were wayyyy down in the 14.00’s when I first got the book.

One of the biggest surprises of this weeks show, was seeing the KLiQ go down clean to the thrown together combo of Ahmed Johnson and Chris Jericho…..yeah, Kevin Nash has Scott Hall to thank for that one. Ahmed and Jericho were always coming away with the win, but it was certainly not going to be such a decisive one…..at least not until Scott Hall decided to pick a fight with Rowdy Roddy Piper before the show even started. Ironically however, it was Hall that was put on his ass in front of all the boys by the former amateur boxer…..which I figured was embarrassing enough that I didn’t hit Hall with any formal punishment…..informally however, he got the pleasure of doing the job clean for Jericho in the middle of the ring. Between you and me, I’m getting kinda sick of the KLiQ’s s##t……and fines don’t seem to have an effect on them since they are earning so much god damn money….it’s time to start hitting them where it really hurts…..right in the ego.

While we are on the subject of the KLiQ, one Mr. Sean Waltman’s contract is up for renewal….I have offered an extension, but WCW have also made a counter offer….I don’t really want to be caught up in another bidding war so soon after the battle for Goldust’s signature…..but on the other hand, I don’t really want to lose Waltman either, so needs must.

In a more positive bit of news, The Undertaker took me to one side after the show….a rarity trust me…..to let me know that he thought Raven was absolute gold on the microphone, and to get a microphone in his hand as often possible….that’s good to know, since due to the nature of their characters, these two are bound to have a programme together somewhere down the line.

It won’t be this week though…..we have other plans this week…..although quite how the show will turn out is anyone’s guess, because it’s Chris Jericho’s first main event……hopefully he delivers since he has a spot waiting for him at the top of the card at this months In Your House.

Week 2, September 1996

Week 2, September 1996

Madison Square Garden

ATT: 20,789

TV: 16.19

No time for pleasantries tonight folks, because there aren’t many pleasant people in our opening segment. The KLiQ are out in force the second the intro has finished playing, and they are coming in off the back of a rather unsuccessful showing last week…..with Justin Credible, Kevin Nash and Scott Hall all on the losing side of their matches…..and with the WWF Champion Shawn Michaels finding out that Chris Jericho is far from intimidated by the Heartbreak Kid, there was really only Triple H that had a good night….of course whether or not he can continue that trend, all depends on how well he handles Ahmed Johnson tonight.

They tease removing the commentators from their position on the announce table again, but eventually bypass them and enter the ring….and while the King isn’t happy to see Justin Credible still sporting his crown, he has bought a replacement with him tonight….after all a King without a crown is no King at all. Michaels takes hold of a microphone and stares at the crowd for a moment, before finally beginning to speak. He tells the crowd that he’s a little bit worried…..worried about the mental health of a large number of the so called superstars of the WWF, because for every Bret Hart or Scott Steiner or Steve Austin that he puts in their place, another moron who doesn’t learn from others mistakes steps up and takes their place……

“……let me throw this out here, and I’ll say it nice and slow, so that even you inbred hicks in the crowd can comprehend what I’m saying…….no one…..and I mean no one…..has got what it takes, to step in this ring with me…..and take this WWF Championship from around my waist…..I don’t care if you’re the best technical wrestler in the world…..the best brawler in the world……the best high flyer in the world…..a hybrid of all three……rookie, veteran, legend……man, woman or child……I don’t care who you are or what you’ve done…..because I guarantee you this……..no matter who you’ve beat……no matter how many titles you’ve won…….for however good you THINK you are……….the Heartbreak Kid Shawn Michaels is BETTER……….I don’t call myself the Showstopper because it’s a catchy nickname……I call myself the Showstopper, because NOBODY can touch what I can do in this ring…….I proved it against Bret and Owen Hart…..I proved it against Scott Steiner and Steve Austin at the same time…….I proved it against the so called King of Memphis Jerry Lawler……and now I’m going to have to prove it one more time, against a no mark, no talent nobody by the name of Chris Jericho……”

Shawn says that frankly, he finds it an insult that someone who’s been in the WWF for no longer than a cup of coffee, has the audacity to call him out…….

“…..to challenge me…..the man who proved he’s better than the best there is…….Chris Jericho, I don’t know who you think you are…….although saying that, not many people DO know who you are……you’re a kid, a flash in the pan…….but don’t let it be said, that Shawn Michaels doesn’t give back…….I’m going to make all your dreams come true…….I’m going to make YOU….right before I break you…..because in less than two weeks time, your name will be known worldwide……as the latest man stupid enough to think that he could hang with the KLiQ…….enjoy your fifteen minutes in the spotlight Kid…..because what the Bad Guy leaves tonight, and believe me that won’t be much…..Shawn Michaels is going to finish at In Your House.”

Rating: B+

VS.

Rating: B+

VS.

The Sons of Samoa vs. The Nation of Domination

We do indeed have the Sons of Samoa facing off against the Nation of Domination next, as Rikishi and Kokina square up against Kama and Nelson. Rocky comes down with the SoS, taking up residence on the floor in their corner….while Faarooq and Scorpio accompany their Nation Brothers to the ring. Faarooq is very vocal on the outside…..particularly whenever young Rocky Maivia is in earshot……telling him that he should know the injustice and prejudice that the Nation fight every day…..after all, his father Rocky Johnson was one half of the first black WWF Tag Team Championship team in history…..HE knows the struggle with the “Man” is real. To his credit Maivia brushes the comments off, preferring to focus on the action in the ring.

As for the action in the ring…..we have two mammoth men going at it…..Kokina and Nelson Knight…..who the King informs us have the combined weight of a Jumbo Jet….(they don’t….obviously…..but you get the point he was trying to make….they heavy)….and then there’s Kama and Rikishi….neither of which are known for their finesse. So obviously we have a rather heavy hitting affair as four big old men punch the senses out of each other. Rocky keeps an eye on the match for as long as he can, but he’s soon sucked into an exchange of words with Faarooq…..which is when Scorpio is able to trip Rikishi from the outside…..who walks straight into a big boot from Kama….followed up by a HUGE splash from Nelson…..scrape that Samoan up off the mat, because he’s done.

Winner(s): The Nation of Domination

Rating: B-

There’s a brief promo package, pimping the In Your House encore, then we are back down to ringside, where Lionheart is making his way down the aisle. He has a disheartened look on his face, but the crowd don’t care that he lost last night…..if anything he has a fan base following the loss than he had before it. He thanks the crowd for the warm reception, but says that he was hoping to have been stood here tonight with the WWF Championship around his sexier than average waist….unfortunately that wasn’t to be…..and despite his best effort, he lost to the better man last night…..because like him or not, Shawn Michaels won that match with no help from anybody….no cheating….no interference…..he won, I lost….no excuses…….it was after the match that the true Shawn Michaels came out…..kicking me right in the face….instead of admiring the fact, that Chris Jericho took WWF Champion Shawn Michaels, to the very limit…..

“……and as much as it kills me to say it, following that match, I was fully prepared to walk down to this ring…..congratulate you on a job well done, and openly admit, that the Heartbreak Kid Shawn Michaels IS the Showstopper…..IS the icon…..IS the main event…..but instead, now I feel like coming out here and saying that the Heartbreak Kid Shawn Michaels IS a jackass…..IS a disappointment…..IS the worst Champion since Stan the man Stasiak……you might think you’re the Captain of the KLiQ………the Coolest Cat in town…..but Captain Crunch is cooler than you…..Chris Jericho is cooler than Shawn Michaels………and The Heartbreak Kid ain’t got nothing……on the Ayatollah of Rock n Rolla……”

…..Jericho goes on to say that is Shawn Michaels wants to kick him in the face again……then at least have the cajonies to do it in a rematch……for the WWF Championship…….or did Triple H, Old Happy Harry Hard-On….take Michaels balls with him when he was kicked out of the building last night?

……surprisingly that brings out the Champ, who walks out onto the entrance way with a microphone in one hand, while the other is on his hip as he over enthusiastically mimes a big laugh, before telling Jericho that…..

“……that was funny…….you’re a funny guy Chris…..of course, I’d rather be the World Wrestling Federation Champion than a comedian, but that’s just me…….now as far as giving you a rematch for the WWF Championship goes? Chris, I did a little research on you……after you clearly know so much about me, I thought it only fair that I learnt a little bit more about you……and see the thing is…..you’re going to be a little bit too busy, to be worrying about me and the WWF Championship…..”

….no sooner had Michaels finished talking, then a mystery man comes flying into the ring behind Jericho, almost taking his head off with a clothesline to the back of the head….

“…..I believe you two know each other…..but for the nice ladies and gentlemen in the audience and at home, let me introduce you to Lance Storm…..this is Chris Jericho’s Marty Jannetty….the former partner that he stabbed in the back for a taste of fame and fortune…..hey Lance, just be grateful that there were no windows near by when he dumped you……I’ll leave you two to get re-aquatinted….bye bye funny man….”

…..and they do get re-acquainted….or at least Chris Jericho’s face gets re-aquatinted with Lance Storms fist…..as the newcomer mounts young Jericho, raining down blows to his face, before gripping hold of him and spinning him over into a Single Leg Boston Crab, that has Jericho screaming in agony, until the officials finally hit the ring and break up the assault…..but Chris Jericho is still in a lot of pain as we go to commercial.

Rating: A*

We’re back from commercial, and we go from one promo straight to another…..although this is less of a promo and more of a Challenge…..as Al Snow makes his way down to the ring. He’s minus Head…..and minus Bill DeMott…..but what he does have, is a microphone in his hand and a determined look on his face. He climbs into the ring and instantly calls out Raven…..he says no Head, no Bill…..none of your little followers…..just you and me….right here…..right now…….

……Snow does get someone……but it’s not Raven…..instead it’s Mankind that comes walking down the aisle, much to the confusion of Al. Mankind rolls into the ring, taking the microphone off him and telling him that Raven won’t be coming out tonight…..he says that last night, Raven did something for him, that NOBODY else ever has……

“……and what he did for me…..was pay the ultimate sacrifice……he stared death himself in the face….and he felt my pain because of it…….you’ve got him all wrong Al…..if he’s got a place in his family for even a monster like me…..then he truly is a god among men……you shouldn’t be fighting him….you should be embracing him….he offered to take you to paradise….but you’d rather dance in purgatory with Bill DeMott, than bask in the glow of immortality at the right hand of greatness…..”

Snow looks at Mankind dumbfounded, before telling Mankind that he might just be the only one around here who can make even Al look sane…..he says that Mankind has never been more wrong about anything in his life….he says Raven is no God…..and there is no right hand of greatness…..just lies and manipulations…..he says that Raven will use Mankind, just like everybody else has…..and if Mankind is really looking for a home….for a family…..then he should be looking at Al Snow and Bill DeMott…..friendship and equality…..looking out for each other, not looking out for the interests of one…….

……this infuriates Mankind, who says that the only person Raven was looking out for on Sunday……when he offered himself up as a sacrifice to the Undertaker…..was Mankind…..and woe betide anyone that says differently……he says that Snow came out here tonight looking for a fight…..then a fight is what he’s going to get…….but it won’t be Raven….it will be Mankind.

Rating: B

VS.

Al Snow vs. Mankind

Mankind hasn’t had much luck in recent weeks….back to back Pay Per View losses to the Undertaker, abandonment from the people he thought closest to him….he was close to turning into the red headed stepchild of the WWF….unwanted and I liked by all…..at least until Raven came along…..and did something for Mankind that no one else ever has. Clearly Mankind doesn’t take that lightly, and is fiercely protective of his new found friend…..the question is however, is Raven looking for friendship? Or is Raven looking for a means to an end?

Al Snow obviously thinks the latter, and spends a good portion of the match trying to convince Mankind of exactly that….Snow tells the Deranged One, that he doesn’t want to fight him….he wants to help him……unfortunately Mankind isn’t listening. This is a pretty good brawl from these two, especially for a match that came out of nowhere…..unsurprisingly however Raven’s rabble of wrong doers made their impact in the match…..just not quite in the traditional way…..

…..because as the match goes on, the video screen flickers to life, showing Bill DeMott on the floor of the locker room, curled up into the foetal position….while Doink, Daniels and the Dogs of War beat the tar out of him. Snow sees this and halts his attack…..just long enough for Mankind to nail him with a Double Arm DDT….which gives him the victory.

Winner: Mankind

Rating: B-

The match is over…..but instead of the soothing sounds of Mankind’s victory music, we are treated to an explosion of fire…..as Kane makes his way down the aisle, with Jim Cornette by his side. The two men climb into the ring, stepping over Al Snow, who is slowly regaining his senses. Cornette tells Mankind that he had one job to do last night…..

“……and you couldn’t even do that right…..you failed at that, just like you failed in every other aspect of your pathetic life….it’s no wonder that you’ve been alone all your life…..because who the heck would want to be around a failure like you……if you want to run off and play happy families with Raven, then feel free…..but Kane has a little parting gift for you before you go…….”

With that Kane’s arm snaps out as he clutches Mankind by the throat. Al Snow is back to his feet and sees the scene unfold before him……he’s torn though, because he can also still see Raven’s followers surrounding a fallen Bill DeMott on the video screen. He looks back and forth between the two scenarios for a moment, before mouthing an apology to Mankind and dashing out of the ring and to the back, seemingly to help out Bill.

Cornette laughs, telling Mankind “….see…..no one cares about pathetic little Mankind….poor little monster that no one loves…..” He nods at Kane…..and one Chokeslam and a Tombstone later…..and Mankind has been left lying again.

Rating: A*

While they clear the carnage from the ring, we head to a quick video package of The Hardy’s latest training session with the Legion of Doom. Again we see Matt and Jeff getting pummelled by the Multi-Time Tag Team Champions…..but the Brothers Hardy are definitely getting more offence in than the have in recent weeks. When the session has ended the video transitions to a shot of all four men sat together, where Hawk hands the brothers a sheet of paper. He tells them to get ready…..because the team written down on that sheet, will be their next opponents. The Hardy’s look at the sheet and both seem mildly surprised at what they see, before nodding their heads and telling the LoD that they got this.

Rating: B-

Next up we have the Million Dollar Corporation making their way out….complete with newest recruit….an almost unrecognisable Rick Steiner…..I say almost unrecognisable, because gone is the trademark amateur wrestling head gear and letterman jacket……instead he is sporting an expensive looking blue designer suit….however fashion was clearly never Steiners strongest suit (pardon the pun) as he is shirtless under the expensive jacket….with a gold dollar sign medallion type necklace proudly adorning his upper chest. DiBiase tells the crowd to take a look at the new and improved Million Dollar Corporation…..he says that at least ONE of the Steiner Brothers had the good sense to take him up on his offer…..ladies and gentlemen, this is Rick Steiner.

There’s an abundance of boos as Steiner takes the microphone….he tells the crowd to shut up, before thanking DiBiase and adding that….

“……Rick Steiner? Make that Rich Steiner……because my wallet is bursting with cash…..you know, a lot of people keep asking me the same stupid question…..’Rich,’ they ask me…’why did you go and turn your back on your muscle head brother Scott?’……and the answer is simple…..I didn’t!!!…..Scott turned his back on me months ago, it’s just that you people were too busy cheering him to notice……”

Steiner goes on to say that he and his Brother are supposed to be a team…..but ever since he got his first shot at the WWF Title back at the King of the Ring, all Scott Steiner has cared about, is Scott Steiner…..He says that while Scotty was focused on Shawn Michaels and the WWF Title, it was Big Brother Rich, that got his ass handed to him, week in and week out by the KLiQ…..and Scott was never there to watch his back…..Scott was never by his side……Scott was too busy looking out for Scott…..well now it’s time that Rich Steiner, started looking out for number one……and you can be damn sure that as part of the Million Dollar Corporation….there will always be someone there to watch his back.

DiBiase takes back the microphone, and tells the crowd that thanks to his superior negotiating tactics, this man right here, Rick Steiner…..

“……that’s Rich Steiner Mr. DiBiase…..”

…..this man right here, Rich Steiner….will be going one on one at In Your House…..with the British Bulldog, for the WWF Intercontinental Championship……and once Psycho Sid here puts an end to Ahmed Johnson next…..HE will be facing Shawn Michaels, for the WWF Championship…..because everything is coming up gold, for the Million Dollar Man.

Ted is about to break out that cackle of his, when Sid again stops him with a hand on his shoulder….he tells him that yes, he will beat Ahmed Johnson next…..and yes, he will beat Shawn Michaels at In Your House……but tonight he’s going to prove that he can do it alone…..and he wants EVERYBODY….including DiBiase to go to the back and let him do what he does best……

“…..because I didn’t need those sheep farmers help to beat Rick Steiner here………”

“…….actually, that’s Rich Steiner……”

“……and I certainly didn’t need RICK Steiner’s help, to beat his brother Scott…..so do us all a favour…..get out of the ring…..walk to the back…..and let the Master and the Ruler of the World do his thing……”

For once DiBiase agrees without persuasion, and the remainder of the Corporation head to the back, leaving Sid in the ring, awaiting his opponent.

Rating: A*

VS.

Ahmed Johnson vs. Psycho Sid (w/Ted DiBiase)

That opponent is none other than Ahmed Johnson, who wastes no time in coming down to the ring. Ahmed has been on somewhat of a roll recently….and has been running through the KLiQ, looking for exactly this opportunity…..a chance at a shot at Shawn Michaels and the World Wrestling Federation Championship…..he’s going to have to work hard to earn that shot however…..as he’s up against a big, big man…..and a big, big man with a lot to prove….as much to himself as everybody else, because Psycho Sid truly believes that he doesn’t need anybody’s help to pick up a victory here in the WWF……and maybe he’s right…….but we don’t find that answer out tonight…..

…..because despite Sid’s explicit instructions, we still get Southern Justice making their way down to the ring mid way through this hard hitting contest. Sid turns and screams at Dennis Knight to get out of here…..and the official echo’s his sentiments…..which is why neither man sees Mark Canterbury roll into the ring and nail Johnson from behind with an Inverted DDT…..before leaving as quickly as he arrived. Sid turns around, and unaware of the interference, snatches Ahmed up and nails him with a Powerbomb, giving him both the victory, and the Title opportunity against Shawn Michaels at In Your House.

Winner: Psycho Sid

Rating: B

Sid is celebrating his victory when Southern Justice dash back into the ring celebrating. Sid shoved them away, telling them that this is HIS victory….and they need to leave. Canterbury and Knight talk to him for a moment….obviously explaining their part in tonight’s victory…..and Sid’s face turns to a look of pure fury. He grabs each man by the throat, screaming that he didn’t want any interference…..before hoisting both man up in the air with a huge double Chokeslam. He walks over to Ahmed and helps him to his feet, apologising for Southern Justice’s actions…..before screaming into a nearby camera to Ted DiBiase, that he’s sick of not being listened too…..

“…..I DON’T WANT HELP….I DON’T NEED HELP……AND I DON’T NEED THE CORPORATION………TED DIBIASE…….I QUIT!!!!…..”

Rating: B+

VS.

The Hart Foundation, Steve Austin & Brian Pillman vs. The KLiQ

It’s time for the main event of the evening…..and to say that we have a tale of two teams would be an understatement. On one side we have the well oiled machine that is the KLiQ….and on the other…..well…..to say that the WWF Tag Team Champions Steve Austin and Brian Pillman don’t get along with their reluctant partners Bret and Owen Hart, would be an understatement……and in all honesty the Champions find themselves at a bit of a disadvantage….because not only do their opponents want to take away their Championship gold, so do their teammates.

At the beginning of the match however, the Harts and the Tag Champs are able to surprisingly work well together, putting aside their dislike of each other long enough to dominate the opening minutes…..but predictably this doesn’t last long, and after Owen Hart accidentally collides with Steve Austin, Austin clocks him in the mouth, leaving him to the mercy of the merciless KLiQ. Much like at In Your House, Hart plays a fantastic face in peril….even more so here because the four KLiQ men frequently use their numbers to split the ring in half, as well as using one man to distract the official, while the other three triple team Hart.

Owen is eventually able to make the tag to his brother….and things descend into chaos as they are known to do….its in that ensuing chaos that the end of this comes…..because as the Harts, Austin and Pillman are cleaning house, the Hitman accidentally elbows Stone Cold in the face…..leading to Austin dropping him with a Stunner…..the rest of the KLiQ clear the ring, leaving Nash to stick the already hurt Hitman with the Jacknife…..and just like that the KLiQ win.

Winner(s): The KLiQ

Rating: B

With the match won, the KLiQ are still looking to make their mark after a lacklustre showing at In Your House…..and boy do they…..because with Bret Hart down and out, they already have a man advantage…..and when Hall nails Owen with a steel chair, that man advantage doubles as all four KLiQ members go to town on Steve Austin and Brian Pillman. With Hall, Waltman and Credible holding Austin back, Nash drags Pillman over to the announce table by his hair…..and after shoving McMahon, Lawler and Ross out of the way, Jacknifes the Loose Cannon straight through the wood.

Austin is struggling like a madman, but it gets him nowhere as his three captors beat him down to the ground. Nash walks over to the steel ring steps, and in a move we have seen a few times now, drags them away from the corner…..picks up Austin……and Jacknifes him down straight onto the unforgiving steel. Austin is down, Pillman is down…..both Hart Brothers are down…..the KLiQ are anything but…..and head back into the ring, Credible picking up the WWF Tag Team Championships on his way…..and as Hall and Nash are handed the straps, they hold them high in the air, telling the crowd that these belts are coming home to the KLiQ……at In Your House.

Rating: A

Final Rating: B+

Final Thoughts

Thank god for some unusually well received angles this week…..because that was all that saved Monday Night Raw from tanking. Outside of the excellent British Bulldog/Goldust encounter, a lot of the other bouts failed to deliver…..particularly the rather disappointing Main Event, which I had high hopes for…..by all accounts the crowd was rather put off by the heel/face team combination of Austin, Pillman and the Harts. I honestly expected better from Mankind and Al Snow too….both of whom usually put on a pretty solid show. They just didn’t click at all though, and it made for some pretty awkward watching…..

….not that the ratings reflected that…..we still absolutely destroyed Nitro in the ratings war, 17.04-15.08….almost a full two point victory….and in my defence, that’s all Vince McMahon cares about at the minute, wiping the smug grin off Eric Bischoff’s face. Eric is clearly feeling the pressure too, because they put on a pretty big (by their recent standards anyway) main event this week…..dusting the cobwebs off a rarely seen Hulk Hogan to have him team with Sting against the regular combination of Vader and the WCW Champion (I know, I still can’t believe it myself) Bam Bam Bigelow….and considering they had the Hulkster do the job, I’m surprised he agreed to appear at all…..obviously it was a screwy DQ finish, but still. They also finally took the Television Championship off the One Man Gang….your new Champion? The Disco Inferno. That’s a good win for Disco, who I’m kind of sorry I missed out on signing a few months back.

In a little bit of contract news, Brian Pillman approached me after Raw to enquire about the possibility of a raise. He’s slowly but surely risen up to a solid main event spot in recent months……in fact he’s even sneaked into the upper tier of the upper tier and is arguably my number five guy in the company right now….behind Bret Hart, Steve Austin, The Undertaker….and obviously Shawn Michaels right at the top. He wanted almost a $15,000 a month raise…..yeah, maybe when you pip the Hitman to fourth place…..he got almost $10,000 but he was happy with that……I really need to position both him and Austin away from the Tag Team division and on to bigger and better things….they honestly deserve better, but the Tag run is mainly due to myself being a huge Hollywood Blondes fan when they were teaming a few years back in WCW…..we’ll see where they go from here.

Vince always gets a bit twitchy anytime I go around giving one of the boys a raise…..but the fact that we are still clearing well over two and a half million dollars a month profit….and the fact that this calendar year alone we are over twenty million dollars better off soothes the savage beast a little bit. I’m trying to amass as much of a bankroll as we can because I honestly think we can take the WWF to a more global level with a little bit more hard work….and that’s going to cost money. Anyway……on to Raw
