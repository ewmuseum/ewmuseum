WCW Monday Nitro: Week 1, December 1997

Posted 5 June 2019

Week 1, December 1997

Birmingham-Jefferson Arena (Birmingham, Alabama)

ATT: 15,619

TV: 21.18

Announce Team: Tony Schiavone, Bobby Heenan and Mike Tenay

We are just 24 hours removed from a monumental Mayhem event, and if the still images from last night that open the show weren’t enough to tell you, the announcers soon inform everyone that yes, Sting returned from being fired….yes, Sting signed a new contract….yes, Sting won the Mayhem match……and perhaps most importantly of all, yes, Sting will be competing against Hollywood Hogan at Starrcade, for the WCW Championship…..

…..speaking of Hollywood, he’s out immediately following our opening monologue (along with the rest of the Titans of Wrestling) and none of them looks to be in a particularly good mood…..

…..Hollywood Hogan takes the microphone first, ranting about Sting and the decision to allow him into the Mayhem match, before finally handing the mic to a ticked off Vince Russo….Russo says that he doesn’t know exactly who Eric Bischoff thinks he is, but he wants to make one thing clear….HE calls the shots around here, and the whole world saw him fire “that Brandon Lee wannabe” Sting last week…..Sting shouldn’t have been in that match to begin with, and he will NOT be getting a shot at the WCW Title at Starrcade…..in fact, the way Russo sees it, tonight they should take everyone that Sting illegally (since he was fired) eliminated from Mayhem, put them all in a match, with the winner getting the privilege of facing Hollywood at Starrcade…..

…..predictably that brings out the public face of the WCW Championship Committee, Eric Bischoff, who tells Russo and Hollywood that unfortunately for them, when they DID fire Sting last week they presented him with a unique opportunity…..an opportunity to frankly right a wrong…..

……Bischoff said that he already announced weeks ago that the Mayhem match would be open to ANYONE (WCW employee’s or not) so long as competitors signed a contract with World Championship Wrestling….Russo fired Sting, Bischoff handed Sting a contract….like it or not, Sting IS a WCW employee…..and Sting IS going to be facing Hollywood Hogan at Starrcade for the World Title……

……Hogan balks at that, saying that Sting can’t fight for the WCW Title as long as Hollywood is Champion, thanks to the match stipulation dating back to their Road Wild tag team bout (which saw The Warrior turn his back on Sting and cost him the match)….Bischoff shakes his head however, stating that all stipulations relating to Sting’s previous contract were terminated along with him the moment Russo fired him…..new contract, no stipulations, and like it or not, this TITLE match WILL take place…………and speaking of title matches, since Bischoff is here he may as well let the Totally Awesome Trinity know that due to their VERY busy evening last night (cheating their way to a title defence and interfering in the World Title Match) Bischoff has arranged a match for them tonight…..Copeland and Cage don’t like that, telling him that he totally can’t do that….he’s not the boss of them…..

“…..actually gentlemen, as long as you hold on to those WCW Tag Team Titles, I totally AM the boss of you…..because the Championship Committee has decided that the Pitbulls deserve a rematch…..and the Pitbulls are totally going to have a rematch…..right now…..so send a referee out here…..”

Rating: A*

VS.

WCW World Tag Team Championship Match
Adam Copeland & Christian Cage (c) (w/Ray Traylor) vs. The Pitbulls (w/Madusa)

….so here comes a referee…..and here come The Pitbulls…..

…..I think it’s safe to say that while Copeland and Cage didn’t take these two men particularly seriously in the weeks leading up to their title bout last night, they’re taking them VERY seriously tonight, because Missy’s Mutt’s took the Tag Champs to the very limit….so much so that this “30 second victory” that the Trinity were promising was almost a victory for the challengers as they beat and battered the two younger men around the ring….and had it not been for the interjection of the Enforcer, the Pitbulls could very well be out here DEFENDING the gold tonight instead of challenging for it……

……this match tonight went very much the same way…..Pitbulls 1&2 have both Copeland and Cage seriously outmuscled, and they use every bit of that strength to punish the Tag Champions with big power moves…..

……the titles look to be in serious jeopardy here, and The Enforcer obviously knows it…..interjecting himself again on the outside, nailing Pitbull #1 in the head with his night stick (before choking Pitbull #2 out on the floor with the same weapon that he used to help eliminate Sid Vicious last night at Mayhem) leaving Cage to finish off a half out of it Pitbull #1 with the Downward Spiral……the Champions retain again…..but not without a LOT of help…..

Winner(s): ….and still WCW Tag Team Champion’s…..Adam Copeland & Christian Cage
Rating: B

…..speaking of that help…..The Enforcer has obviously had just about enough of the Pitbulls, and he tosses a half choked out Pitbull #2 into the ring, followed by a pair of steel chairs…..

……Copeland and Cage collect a chair each, and much as they did last night to Chris Benoit, absolutely destroy Pitbull #2 with a hard Con-Chair-To…..

……with one half of the team down and out it’s time to move on to the second member, and it looks as though they’re going for the ultimate insult….because as Cage brings a table into the ring (setting it up along with his partner) The Enforcer mounts the second rope, before sitting down on the top turnbuckle and calling for Pitbull #1…..

……Schiavone questions what they’re planning here, but Mike Tenay (being the professor) knows exactly what they have planned, telling his colleagues that it looks as though they are about to replicate that Pitbull Superbomb….straight through the table…..

…..although not if these men have anything to say about it….as Chris Benoit, Diamond Dallas Page, and Sid Vicious come running out from the back…..

…..all three of these men have issues with one or all of the Trinity…..Chris Benoit was the victim of a Con-Chair-To last night, Sid Vicious was eliminated from the Mayhem Match courtesy of Ray Traylor’s nightstick, while Diamond Dallas Page arguably had the WCW Title won last night, had the Trinity not made their presence felt……

…..the three men hit the ring, and the Totally Awesome Trinity hit the bricks, bailing quickly from the ring and retreating up the aisle to the back as Benoit, Page and Vicious check on the Pitbulls, and Mean Gene makes his way out from the back…..

Rating: A

…..Mean Gene climbs into the ring, stopping the trio of Benoit, Page and Vicious before they can leave, because he has some questions for these three men….

…..first and foremost, “Sid Vicious, what in the world are you doing here in WCW?” Gene says that the last time he checked, Sid was still “up north”…..Sid says that he WAS, but he isn’t anymore…..he says that when Eric Bischoff opened the door for anyone to enter Mayhem (whether they worked here or not) he knew that there was only one place he wanted to be and that’s where the big boys play, because there’s none bigger than Sid Vicious…..

……Gene turns to Page next, who while clearly fired up, certainly looks a little despondent at his failure to capture the WCW Title last night….Gene wants to know what’s next for DDP following his failed bid at dethroning Hollywood Hogan, but Page says that he honestly doesn’t know….he says that for months now he’s been lobbying for a shot….a shot to prove that he deserves to be in there with the Hollywood Hogans, the Warrior’s, the Randy Savage’s….but now?…..now he just just doesn’t know…..

……Benoit interrupts Page there….he’s not much of a talker but he’s got something to say here, telling Dallas to stop….he says that Page had that title won last night, he had Hollywood flat on his back in the middle of the ring, and had it not been for the Copeland, Cage and Traylor then he would be standing here as the Champion of the world…..

“…..so what now? I’ll tell you what now Dallas…..this so called Trinity took from you….they took from me too…..took away my chance of winning Mayhem, and then smashed two steel chairs across my head…..they took from you….they took from me…..it’s time we took from them…..”

……Benoit suggests that he and Dallas team up and challenge the Totally Awful Twosome to a match at Starrcade for the WCW Tag Team Titles, and hit the Titans where it hurts them most, by taking the Championships away from them…..

……Page hesitates for a second (but it is only a second) before agreeing…..I guess the challenge is on the table…..Adam Copeland and Christian Cage vs. Diamond Dallas Page and Chris Benoit, for the WCW Tag Team Titles at Starrcade……

……not to be outdone, Sid says that he has some unfinished business of his own with this Trinity….with Ray Traylor to be precise, before saying that if Page and Benoit are gonna come out here making challenges, then he has one of his own…..he wants the Enforcer…..and he wants him at Starrcade too……

Rating: A

VS.

WCW Cruiserweight Championship: First Round Match

Kid Kash vs. Alex Wright

Last week, Yoshihiro Tajiri was the first man to qualify for the WCW Cruiserweight Title tournament semi-finals, tonight two more men will be joining him (with the fourth to be determined next week) beginning with the winner of this next match between Kid Kash and Alex Wright…..

…..this was really was a good match, the only thing that really let it down was the crowd reaction due to neither competitor exactly being high in the pecking order (although Wright is comfortably more over than Kash) but then there has never been a real reason to showcase these smaller athletes…..hopefully the new Cruiserweight division will give some of these guys more exposure, for the time being however it’s Alex Wright that advances to the Semi-Finals with a German Suplex.

Winner: Alex Wright
Rating: C

We go to a commercial for the upcoming Starrcade event and when we come back it’s straight back down to ringside…..no match, no interviewer…..just a p!ssed off looking Jean Paul Levesque marching towards the ring…..

…..he snatches a microphone from ringside and steps between the ropes, getting right down to business….

……Levesque says that last night, the Titans of Wrestling crossed a line that they shouldn’t have crossed…..Randy Savage crossed a line he shouldn’t have crossed…..and The Warrior crossed a line he shouldn’t have crossed…..he says that by subjecting Shawn Michaels to a beating so severe that hey left him a bleeding and unconscious mess in the back “you may as well have taken that blood and used it to sign their own death warrant”…..Savage for doing the majority of the damage, Warrior for holding him back and forcing him to watch……

…..Levesque says that ever since leaving the Titans he has been doing everything he can to make up for his past transgressions….he’s fought the fight for WCW…..he says that he knows that Michaels has hardly been showering the company with praise, but after a rocky start JPL can honestly say that there is nowhere he would rather be than right here, and he knows Shawn would feel the same way if he just gave it a chance…..he apologises for the wrongdoings he has done in the past, “but I make no apologies for what I’m going to do to Randy Savage, The Warrior…..and the rest of the Titans of Wrestling…..”

…..JPL doesn’t know when (or if) Shawn Michaels will return after he spent last night getting stitched up and treated for concussion, but he hopes that while he is recuperating that he’s sat at home back in San Antonio…..and he hopes he’s watching his television…..

“…..because Shawn…..you can look into my eyes right now and you can take it from me, Savage, Warrior, they’re going to regret the day that you walked through the door…..I know how you feel about this company, but watch Starrcade…..watch the BIGGEST event of the year…..and watch me, make Savage pay for every drop of blood that he cost you last night…..you came here to WCW to have my back, now I’m going to have yours…..I’m going to tear Savage apart tonight, and then at Starrcade, I don’t care if it’s two on one….I want Savage again……I want the Warrior……I want pain……I want blood……I want the Titans of Wrestling down on their knees praying to their god to take them back to a time before they knew who Shawn Michaels was…..because Shawn, I’m going to tear them apart……and I’m not going to do it for WCW…..I’m not even going to do it for me…….I’m going to do it, for YOU…..”

…..suddenly the bagpipes begin to wail, and this crowd erupts, as Rowdy Roddy Piper makes his way out from the back and walks slowly to the ring (taking in the reception from the crowd as he goes) before stepping between the ropes and standing in front of Levesque……

……the two men stare each other down for a moment, after all, they share quite a lot of history after Pipers war with the Titans six months or so back, but in the end Roddy offers his hand, which Levesque takes, before handing the microphone to The Hot Scot……

…..Piper says that first of all, he’s got something that he wants to get off his chest, and that’s the dismissive (and frankly disrespectful) way that Shawn Michaels has been talking about WCW…..Roddy says that he’s been where Shawn has been…..he’s worked the same towns, dealt with the same politics, and raised the same hell…..he says that he’s worked for the WWF and he’s worked for WCW, and there is NO company he would rather work for that this one…..

…..Piper says that he’s left a lifetimes worth of blood, sweat and tears on these mats, and if could, he’d do it all over again…..the money, the fame, the ego that comes with working up north, it’s NOTHING compared to the respect that these fans give you…..and if Shawn just embraced them…..AND WCW…..then he’d see that these fans take Uncle Vince’s “stars” and turn them into Icons…..

“…..second of all, I’ve got something to say to YOU Mr. Jean Paul…..and that’s the fact that if you think for one second that you’re going to be able to take on the Titans single handedly, then that tight shirt must be cutting off the blood to your brain as well as your biceps, because that just ain’t gonna happen pal…..”

…..Piper says that Levesque should know better than most that beating ONE Titan is hard enough, let alone two….if he’s really looking to take on Savage and Warrior at Starrcade then he’s going to need a partner…..someone crazy enough to step into the ring with Warrior and Savage for a fight…..someone with an ax to grind, and an ax to bury right in the skull of the Titans……

“…..someone…..like me……”

…..apparently Piper is offering up his services to Jean Paul Levesque…..and to be fair, JPL doesn’t exactly have many choices when it comes to backup…..Shawn Michaels is in no condition to fight (and even if he was, he doesn’t even work here) and while Chris Benoit has been somewhat of an ally as of late, it would seem that the Canadian Crippler has plans for the Totally Awesome Trinity come Starrcade night….so really, what choice does Levesque have?….

…..none, clearly…..and as he accepts Pipers offer, it looks like we have another potential match for Starrcade set…..Jean Paul Levesque and Rowdy Roddy Piper, against The Mega Maniacs….Randy Savage, and The Warrior.

Rating: A*

Since Mean Gene wasn’t needed for that last segment, we go to a segment where we DO need him, as we go backstage, where Lance Storm is standing ready to be questioned, along with Jacques Rougeau and Carl Oulette, The Amazing French Canadians…..

…..Storm is still wearing the WCW Television Title around his waist, despite the fact that he lost that very title last night at Mayhem, and Gene is feeling particularly testy tonight, asking Storm “just what in the world do you think you are doing?”telling him that Bill Goldberg won that title fair and square last night, and Lance Storm has stolen it……

…..there is quite a rabble here as The French Canadians rally to Storms defence, shouting down Gene’s accusations, but Storm holds a hand up to quieten them down….

…..he tells Gene that he hasn’t stolen anything…..Canadians don’t steal…..what Lance Storm HAS done, is make an executive decision…..he begins reeling off a long list of names, from Ricky Steamboat, Tully Blanchard, and Arn Anderson, all the way up to Lord Steven Regal and Lance Storm….he says that since it’s inception, the Television Title has been a Championship for some of the best technical wrestlers that the sport has to offer….he said he wore this title proudly and upheld those same standards and he will NOT see that record tarnished by a Neanderthal like Bill Goldberg…..so as far as Lance Storm is concerned, he is STILL the WCW Television Title…..and he has the belt to prove it…..

…..Gene disagrees, saying what he is carrying right there is nothing more than a target, but none the less he turns his attention to the Jacques and Oulette, asking them exactly what their interest in this situation is?….Jacques does the talking, telling Gene that they may be the Amazing French Canadians, but the key part of that title is CANADIANS…..and as proud Canadians, they couldn’t stand by and watch a Crime against Canada go down without intervening (after all, they’re former lawmen don’t you know) and Bill Goldberg taking that title from Lance Storm is most certainly a Crime against Canada…..just like the fact that he and his partner have yet to receive a shot at those WCW Tag Team Titles is a Crime against Canada…..but tonight, they look to right that wrong, by defeating the Nasty Boys and moving one step closer to a shot at the gold.

Rating: B

VS.

The Nasty Boys vs. The Amazing French Canadians


Sigh, the WCW Tag Team division really needs a shot in the arm…..actually, I’ll rephrase that (because the heel side isn’t all that bad) the Babyface side of our WCW Tag Team division really needs a shot in the arm, because as far as actual face teams go, we have the Road Warriors, The Pitbulls……and The Nasty Boys…..sheesh……

…..to be fair, this match wasn’t all that bad, but considering the fact that we are a month away from 1998, this had WCW Saturday Night written all over it….instead here we are, live on TNT….with The Nasty Boys facing off against The Amazing French Canadians…..

…..we didn’t even get a clean finish either……and you can thank Lance Storm for that…..

…..and Bill Goldberg I suppose…..but mainly Lance Storm……

…..because as the FORMER Television Champion walks around there at ringside (interfering at various points throughout the match) the CURRENT Television Champion comes running out from the back, looking to formally claim his title…..

…..Storm runs like hell, diving into the ring with Goldberg in hot pursuit, and bailing out he other side of the ring, up the aisle….and to the back…..

…..Goldberg slides into the ring right behind him, but that’s as far as he gets…..because as Storm makes like a tree (and leaves) Jacques and Oulette jump the undefeated rookie, attempting to beat him to the mat…..

…..it doesn’t work…..

…..and as they go for a double clothesline attempt Goldberg ducks under, runs the ropes, and mows both men down with a double clothesline of his own…..

…..and that folks, will be a disqualification…..

Winner(s): The Amazing French Canadians (via disqualification)
Rating: B-

…..the Amazing French Canadians have won…..go figure…..someone had better let them know though, because they have NO idea whatsoever…..

…..Goldberg knocked them silly with that double clothesline, and he follows that up with a Spear for Jacques…..a Spear for Carl Oulette…..and a roar to the approving crowd…..

…..do you know who’s not so approving though? The Nasty Boys…..who quite frankly looked to be on their way to winning this one before Mr. Goldberg entered the fray…..Knobbs spins the Television Champion around, and Saggs joins his partner in hurling abuse at the rookie, until Knobbs makes the mistake of shoving Goldberg back hard….so hard in fact that Goldberg uses the momentum to spring back off the ropes and Spear Knobbs right out of his boots…..

…..Saggs springs to his partners defence but he runs straight into a kick to the head, which Goldberg follows up with a Jackhammer that shakes the ring…..

…..the crowd continues to voice their support, as Goldberg screams at the camera that he’s coming for his belt….before heading to the back….whaddya know, a bald guy with a goatee and black trunks beating up faces and heels alike to the delight of the crowd….wonder where we’ve seen that before.

Rating: B+

We come back from commercial break and Mean Gene is in the ring once more….he gives a huge introduction for his next guest, stating that despite already wrestling once that night, AND going into the match hurt, Lex Luger was STILL able to last for an unbelievable 57 minutes and 33 seconds, even eliminating five competitors along the way…..

“…..ladies and gentlemen please welcome my guest at this time….The Total Package, Lex Luger…..”

…..and welcome him this crowd does…..Luger gets a fantastic ovation following his performance last night, as he gingerly makes his way out from the back (still selling the ribs) and he joins Gene in the ring, thanking the fans for the support…..

…..Gene says this must be a bittersweet moment for him…..coming so close to winning the Mayhem Match…..coming so close to winning the shot to face Hollywood Hogan at Starrcade…..only to fall at the last hurdle, and have to watch Sting take that which he so deeply desired…..

…..bittersweet…..that’s certainly one way to describe it says Luger…..but if him not getting that shot means that Sting is finally going to come down from the rafters and join the war on the Titans of Wrestling…..if him not getting that shot means that he finally gets his friend back…..then he’s ok with it…..

“…..but I gotta tell you Gene….going out there for almost sixty minutes last night, it sparked something in me…..something I haven’t felt in nearly a decade…..see when I was busting my ass to make a name for myself in this business, going sixty minutes wasn’t anything new…..going sixty minutes was a cornerstone for your development, and I learned more in one hour with…well, I won’t mention any names, but you know who Gene….I learnt more in that one hour about this business than I did years of my training…..”

…..Luger says that almost hitting that hour mark last night, it sparked that same hunger he had a decade ago…..a hunger to be noticed…..to be recognised…..a hunger to prove that he still belongs in there with the biggest, the baddest, and the best…..which brings him onto what HE wants at Starrcade (now that a World Title opportunity is out of the window)…..

“…..you see Gene, The Giant might not be the BEST between these ropes, but he’s certainly the biggest, and if my ribs are any indication, he may well be the baddest…..which is why I want him one more time…..”

…..Lex says that Sting (rightly or wrongly) was a factor in their first match…..the Titans of Wrestling were a factor in their second…..how about The Giant proves that he can live up to his potential, and face the Total Package one more time…..at Starrcade…..no Sting, no Titans, just Lex Luger and The Giant….

…..well, I guess the Giant’s got nothing better to do tonight, because here he comes too…..

…..Mean Gene gets instantly nervous the second that the Seven Footer steps over the top rope, but Giant tells him not to worry himself…..and Lex doesn’t need to worry either…..not tonight anyway…..

…..he mocks the Total Package a little bit, saying that he can barely stand and he’s out here issuing challenges? Well that’s fine with the Giant…..Giant says he could break Luger in half right here, right now, and there is NOTHING that anyone could do about it…..not Luger…..not Page…..not Levesque…..and certainly not STING…..but he’s not going to, not when Lex is anything but The Total Package, because he doesn’t want any excuses when the Giant finally takes him out for good……and he has a message for Lex’s little buddy too…..

“…..you can tell Sting from me, this ain’t over…..and when I’m done breaking you in half at Starrcade, I’m coming for him next…..”

…..Giant looks at Luger (who is struggling to stand upright) and just smirks, shaking his head as he walks to the back without so much as a second glance….challenge accepted I guess, hopefully Luger is in better condition by the time Starrcade rolls around.

Rating: A

VS.

WCW Cruiserweight Championship: First Round Match

Jeff Hardy vs. Jinsei Shinzaki

So far we have seen Yoshihiro Tajiri and Alex Wright advance to the next round of the WCW Cruiserweight Title Tournament, and we’re about to find out who’s going to join them, when Jeff Hardy faces off with Jinsei Shinzaki….

…..Shinzaki has done little of note here in WCW since his arrival, but he’s no stranger to competing for Cruiserweight Gold, being an active part of the WWF’s Light Heavyweight division back in its infancy (as Hakushi) and if he was looking for the perfect time to take out a big name opponent, then tonight was that night…..because much like Lex Luger, Jeff Hardy was put through the ringers last night, coming in at number one in the Mayhem match and lasting close to fifty minutes himself before being abruptly tossed by Big Van Vader…..

…..Hardy was certainly not in top condition here, and Jinsei looked to capitalise on it…..and things looked to be about to go from bad to worse for young Jeff, as James Vandenberg, Tajiri, and (the man who orchestrated his elimination also night) Mortis make their way out…..

…..it certainly throws Hardy off his game, but true to his word (after promising that his men wouldn’t touch Hardy as long as he was alive in this tournament) the trio simply stand back and watch as Jeff sidesteps a hamspring elbow attempt and counters it with a Twist of Fate for the one, two, three……no Swanton Bombs tonight thank you, not after last nights gruelling match.

Winner: Jeff Hardy
Rating: B-

Interestingly enough, last night Jeff Hardy and Mortis shared the ring for around ten minutes, and despite their rather colourful history, Mortis didn’t lay so much as one finger on Jeff Hardy (not u too he tossed him to Vader that is, who promptly eliminated him) which is good enough for Bobby Heenan, who says that Vandenberg is clearly a man of his word…..

…..Schiavone isn’t quite so brainwashed, telling Bobby that he could trust Vandenberg about as far as he could throw him (which isn’t far) before stating that this could get very ugly for one Jeff Hardy…..

…..Vandenberg just cackles though, and repeats his promise of last week….not one hair on Jeff Hardy’s head will these two men place their hands on, before turning and walking to the back (along with Mortis and Tajiri) as Jeff Hardy cautiously watches them leave…..is James Vandenberg trustworthy? Well that remains to be seen folks, but we may well find out sooner rather than later.

Rating: B

We go to the back from there, and Mean Gene is standing by with THE most electrifying man in all of sports entertainment……the self proclaimed people’s champ, The Rock…..

…..Gene says that The Rock had some mighty strong words going into Mayhem…..words that suggested that he truly believed that he was going to win the match last night AND the WCW Title at Starrcade…..well now that’s not happening….Plan A didn’t work, what’s Plan B?…..

“…..Plan B?…..Plan B?…..why don’t you tell me Gene, what do you think Plan B is?…..”

“…..well I wo…..”

“…..IT DOESN’T MATTER WHAT YOU THINK PLAN B IS OKERLUND!…..

…..all that matters is that in one night The Rock went out there and electrified…..The Rock was tossing people left and right, The Rock was facing people he had NEVER faced, The Rock was slapping the ugly off Big Van Vader’s face….and that’s no small feat since Vader looks like the result of a Warthog having sex with a Blob Fish….but The Rock was doing it….and Vader was rocking, and Vader was reeling, and Vader was moaning like he’d just heard that Chuck E Cheese was going out of business, and then poof…..The Rock’s dream of headlining Starrcade went “Up in Smoke”…..now The Rock doesn’t know who it was…..was it Cheech, or was it Chong, it really doesn’t matter….but SOMEBODY hit The Rock in the back with a steel chair, and faster than you can say Chimichanga, The Rock was out of the match…..”

…..The Rock says that things get a little hazy after that, but fortunately for him Eric Bischoff decided that he wants Starrcade to be the most Electrifying show of the year…..Eric Bischoff wants The Rock to continue facing people that he has NEVER faced….and Eric Bischoff, the smiling face of the Championship Committee, he wants The Rock to fight for titles that he’s NEVER held…..

…..which is why at Starrcade, The Rock will be facing Eddie Guerrero…..and he’ll be facing him for the WCW United States Championship…..

….. it that’s at Starrcade….TONIGHT it’s going to be Vader getting electrified….it’s going to be Vader that finds out exactly how it feels to have the People’s Boot shoved straight up your candy ass, and it’s going to be Vader that’s “Forgotten” once and for all….

“………..IF YA SMELLLLLLLLLLLALALAOW……..what The Rock….is…..cooking……….”

Rating: B+

VS.

The Rock vs. Big Van Vader


This match has been brewing for quite some time now, and it’s finally here…..after dispatching of Kevin Sullivan and Bam Bam Bigelow, it’s finally Big Van Vader’s turn to go “one on one with the Great One”……as he faces The Rock……

…..this was never going to be an easy match for Rocky…..sure he’s defeated Bam Bam Bigelow, but Vader is a different animal…..he’s a few years past his prime, a few steps slower and a few pounds heavier, but he’s still as mean as a grizzly bear that’s been poked with a stick, and he’s been waiting to get his hands on The Rock for months now…..

…..the match perhaps wasn’t as long as it could have been (but that’s more down to Vader’s stamina than anything else) but they crammed a whole lot of action in this 12 minute match, including a whole hell of a lot of offence for the Forgotten member, who attempted to simply out muscle and power Rocky to the mat…..

…..he did it as well, for a while anyway…..he knocked his opponent silly with clubbing blows and power moves, but eventually The Rock rally’s back…….

…..to the point that finally Bam Bam Bigelow and Kevin Sullivan (who has his arm in a sling) make their way out from the back, looking to turn the tide back into the favour of their man…..

……luckily the official doesn’t even entertain the notion, hopping out of the ring and stopping the pair in the aisle…..

…..which is perhaps why he missed this man (WCW US Champion Eddie Guerrero) climbing over the guard rails out of the crowd with a steel chair in hand….and in a repeat performance of last night, he crashes the steel against the back of The Rock during a rope running sequence, leaving Rocky wide open to a Powerbomb from Vader, and the one, two, three-once the referee has turned around…..

…..your winner…..Big Van Vader…..

Winner: Big Van Vader
Rating: B+

Guerrero goes rushing out of the ringside area (job done on his part) and up the ramp, where he passes Sullivan and Bigelow…..Eddie fuels the fires a little bit, pointing out to the Forgotten pair that The Rock has been taunting, insulting, and belittling them for months now, before walking to the back with a sly grin on his face…..

…..his plan worked too…..because Sully and Bammers head to the ring to join Vader…..and the trio begin putting the boots to a helpless Rock…..

…..Rocky says that we are just six days away from Starrcade, and instead of letting James Vandenberg get inside his head, he SHOULD be getting ready for his first taste of gold, when he defeats Tajiri to become the WCW Cruiserweight Champion…..

“…..oralè vato, hey…..don’ listen to him holmes, James Vandenberg was right, you just a two time loser esè…..”

…..if Jeff Hardy was looking for solitude, he’s obviously chosen the wrong location…..it’s like Grand Central Station around here, because now the Papi of Perfection (and current US Champion) Eddie Guerrero has turned up too…..

…..he tells Hardy that if he’s looking for some REAL advice, take it from the champ, not the chump esè…..take Vandenberg’s offer…..after all, he’s already lost in his effort to become the Television Champion…..and he DEFINITELY lost in his effort to become the United States Champion…..

“…..although don’t feel so bad about that one holmes, you WERE facing the Papi of Perfection after all……EVERYONE has lost to me esè…..”

…..he tells Hardy that if he was to go out and try and win the Cruiserweight Title at Starrcade, he’d probably end up a THREE time loser, so why bother…..take Vandenberg’s offer, go join his little twisted ministry, and maybe the United States of Eddie Guerrero will give him another shot at the gold somewhere down the line…..

“…..woah, woah, woah….slow down there Tex-Mex…..you won’t be giving Jeff Hardy a shot at anything…..The Rock says that if ANYONE is going to give Jeff Hardy a shot at the United States Championship around here, then it’s going to be The Rock……Jeff Hardy, when The Rock wins the US Title on Sunday, you can have a shot…..”

“…..no vato, I’ll give him the shot…..”

“…..wait a minute, wait a minute, wait one minute…..do you actually think that you’re going to beat The Rock at Starrcade?…..”

“…..I don’t th…..”

“…..IT DOESN’T MATTER WHAT YOU THINK…..all that matters is that in six days time, at the granddaddy of them all, the Rock is going to walk into Starrcade and entertain the millions and millions of The Rock’s fans as only The Rock can, he’s going to slap you up and down that ring until all you are is the Papi of P!ssing your Pants, and he’s going to walk out again the NEW United States Champion……Eddie Guerrero, The Rock says this, you’ve been the US Champion for five months now, and yeah, you beat a lot of people…..you’re good, The Rock is a big enough man to say it, you’re very good…..but you’re not great…..and at Starrcade, being good just won’t be good enough when you go one on one with the great one……

…..IF YOU SMELLLLLLL….WH…..”

“…..IT DOESN’T MATTER WHAT YOU SMELL ESÈ…..”

…..oh The Rock doesn’t like that, he’s not used to being the one interrupted…..but Eddie cares little…..he says that all he can smell whenever The Rock is around, whenever Jeff Hardy is around, is the smell of second best…..because for five months now, Eddie Guerrero has been the best thing in WCW….period…..he says that he’s had more successful Title defences here in WCW than The Rock has had matches…..

“…..you come out here holmes and you talk about all these new faces and names….names you’ve never faced before…..well guess what esè, Eddie Guerrero has faced them all before, and Eddie Guerrero has DEFEATED them all before…..Jeff Hardy, your fat uncle cousin twice removed Rikishi, Diamond Dallas Page, Chris Benoit, Curt Hennig, the list goes on and on holmes, and at Starrcade, The Rock…..the Great One…..he’s just another name on the list of people that couldn’t take this title from me…..he’s just another name on the list of people who know first hand, that Eddie Guerrero is exactly what he says he is…..the Papi of Perfection esè…..”

…..Eddie says that he will lie, he will cheat, he will steal, he will do WHATEVER it takes to keep this title around his waist…..and the only thing that The Rock will be smelling at Starrcade will be the sour smell of failure…..

…..before shoving The Rock right in the chest…..

…..and that doesn’t go down well…..The Rock shoves Eddie back and soon the two men begin trading blows as they brawl right there in the back, until Doug Dillinger’s crack team finally storm the room, and security separates these two men…..

…..leaving just Jeff Hardy…..

…..who is still sat in the same position…..unmoved…..head down…..deep in thought…..in fact, I don’t think he’s noticed a damn thing that’s just happened right here in front of him…..time to air a Starrcade commercial.

Rating: A*

VS.

The Warlord vs. Lance Storm


Ok…..let me preface this match by making two points…..actually, make that three points…..point number one, The Warlord is only still employed by WCW because he was already here when I took over and I hate taking over a company and firing a load of people, so he’s still here…..point number two, The Warlord is a TERRIBLE babyface, but I have zero intention of using him for anything so I have zero interest in turning him heel…..and finally, point number three…..Lance Storm is delusional…..

….because despite the fact that Lance Storm is most definitely NOT the Television Champion (that would be Bill Goldberg) he IS in possession of the TV title belt, and as such, demands that the ring announcer declare this match a Title Defence…..now had it just been Lance Storm, maybe he wouldn’t have made that announcement, but the Amazing French Canadians are right there by Storm’s side, so as Oulette roughly grabs him by the lapels of his jacket, we do indeed receive the announcement that “this match is for the WCW Television Title”……go figure…..

…..as for the match itself, it wasn’t great…..it had The Warlord in it after all…..but the commentators inform us that (remarkably) Lance Storm actually requested this opponent, stating that he wanted to show the world (just like he’s going to show Goldberg at Starrcade) that all the muscles and power in the world are no good against a great WRESTLER in a WRESTLING match…..and it’s a point that almost sees him defeated on a number of occasions as the power man tosses him around the ring…..

…..in the end though, The Amazing French Canadians prove their worth, because after Oulette successfully distracts the official, Jacques rolls into the ring and takes out Warlord with a nasty looking chop-block…..and as Storm locks on the Canadian Maple Leaf, Warlord has little option but to submit…..a big win over a big man…..

Winner: Lance Storm
Rating: C+

…..Rougeau and Oulette join Storm in the ring, handing him “his” Television Title which he raises in victory…..before the trio can leave however, Mean Gene makes his way out….he’s got a few questions for these three gentlemen, the first being which one of them was driving the car that nearly took out Bill Goldberg last week?…..

…..Lance Storm is appalled by this accusation, asking Gene just what exactly he is suggesting?….he says that he doesn’t know who was driving that car last week, but it certainly wasn’t one of them….for two reasons, the first being that Canadians are the safest drivers in the world, they would never do something so reckless…..and secondly, Canadians don’t “try” to do anything, Canadians succeed…..if they had wanted to take Goldberg out last week then he would have been taken out…..

…..Gene isn’t having that though, stating that those Canadian plates on the car are too much of a coincidence, but again Storm is aghast, telling Gene that NO Canadian would do such a thing, and for all he knows (and the more likely scenario) it was some American drunk driver out joyriding in a stolen car……but enough of that, now that Gene has tried to disparage Storm’s good name, it’s time for him to get down to business…..he says that he’s just proved right here and right now that muscles don’t make the man…..ability make the man, and there’s no one more able in that ring than Lance Storm…..

…..he tells Gene that if Bill Goldberg wants another shot at the Television Title, then he’s going to have to do it the old fashioned way, he’s going to have to WRESTLE for it…..

…..Gene is confused here, telling Storm that HE is the challenger on Sunday, not Goldberg…..he has quite literally stolen the man’s property…..Storm doesn’t even hear this however as he continues on his tirade, he tells Goldberg that his muscle and strength aren’t going to be a factor, because if he wants a shot at THIS Television Title, then he’s going to have to agree to a Submission match…..no fluke pinfalls, no fluke count-outs…..no getting himself disqualified…..if he wants this Television Title then he’s going to have to learn a wrestling move and make Lance Storm submit…..

“…..and William, I don’t think you can do that…..”

…..that brings out the man himself, who strides purposefully down the aisle…..obviously he wants his Championship belt back (a championship that he hasn’t even touched since he won it) but Storm and the Amazing French Canadians bail quicker than you can say “hockey fight”…..

…..Goldberg looks as though he’s considering giving chase, but in the end obviously decides that he can wait till Sunday when Storm will have nowhere to run…..for now though he’s run all the way back to the top of the entrance ramp, where he asks Goldberg if he accepts the conditions……after all, no submission match, no title shot…..

…..Gene puts Goldberg on the spot, saying that while clearly Lance Storm is delusional to say the least, a challenge is a challenge…..will Goldberg put the title on the line in a submission match? Does Goldberg even know any submission holds?……

…..the usually mute Goldberg scowls at Gene for a moment with an intense look on his face, before snatching the microphone……he says if Storm wants a submission match……

“……then he’s got it……Lance Storm……YOU’RE NEXT…..”

…..I guess that’s challenge accepted…..Bill Goldberg vs. Lance Storm…..a Submission Match for the WCW Television Title…..does Goldberg actually KNOW any Submission holds? I guess we’ll find out in six days time…..lets go to commercial…..

Rating: B+

VS.

Diamond Dallas Page & Chris Benoit vs. The Faces of Fear


And when we come back, it’s just in time for our semi-main event, which sees the two men challenging for the WCW Tag Team Titles at Starrcade (Chris Benoit & Diamond Dallas Page) facing off against arguably two of the toughest men in WCW (and possibly the wrestling business as a whole) The Faces of Fear, Meng and The Barbarian…..

…..these are VERY different opponents than Benoit and Page will be facing on Sunday night, and they are in the fight of their lives here as they attempt to find a counter to put these two huge men away……speaking of countering, that’s exactly how The Crippler and DDP did manage to win this one……TWO counters actually…….

…..because as The Barbarian headed up to the top rope, Meng attempted to hook on the Tonga Death Grip to Chris Benoit…..before he is able to fully get his grip however Benoit is able to trap the arm and take the man to the mat, locking on the Crippler Crossface……

…..in the meantime the Barbarian leaps and attempts to take out Page with a top rope rope shoulder block…..unfortunately for him he leaps straight into a Diamond Cutter that drives that (notoriously hard) Tongan Skull into the mat with enough force that DDP was able to get the 123, in what was an admittedly pretty poor match.

Winner(s): Chris Benoit & Dallas Page
Rating: B-

Mean Gene makes his way out again to join Page and Benoit inside the ring…..he’s looking for a little bit more though, inviting the man who will be facing the Totally Awesome Trinity’s Ray Traylor at Starrcade out……Sid Vicious…..

…..Sid makes his way out to his usual monster pop (Sid is never not over) and Gene wants to know if these three gentlemen are going to be able to do what no one else has managed to do…..shut up the Totally Awesome Trinity?….

…..the Baron of Boredom…..sorry, I mean Chris Benoit is the first to answer that one…..he says that when it comes to stepping between these ropes (and unlike Copeland and Cage) he prefers to let his actions to the talking for him……he says that the tag team Champions might think that Chris Benoit is boring, but the Crippler can’t think of anything more entertaining, than the screams of agony at are going to leave either Copeland or Cages mouths, when he takes them down with the Crippler Crossface…..

…..sterling work there Mr. Benoit…..a promo masterclass…..

…..thankfully DDP has some insight of his own, telling “Gene-o” that the last time he checked, the name on the marquee was World Championship WRESTLING….it don’t say Comedy Central up there…..

“…..now me, I got a mouth on me Gene-o, but at Starrcade, I ain’t going in there to talk…..see me and Chris, we’ve had about as much “comedy” for one year to last us a lifetime…..for the past six months we’ve been nothing but a punchline to the Jack-off’s of Wrestling…..second rate, not fit to share a ring, not good enough, we should be kissing the ground that Hogan walks on…..you name it, we’ve heard it…..but you know what Gene, for a couple of midcard jabronies, it’s taken just about every one of those “Titans” to beat us each and every time…..

…..Copeland…..Cage…..we’re done playing around…..we’re done talking…..and we’re done being the butt of everyone’s jokes……you can call this man boring, I call him a machine…..and at Starrcade, we’re coming for a fight and I can’t think of anyone I’d rather have in my corner in a fight than the Canadian Crippler Chris Benoit……and the joke will be on you two jackasses……when you feel…..the……BANG…..”

…..Sid hasn’t said much, he’s just done what he does best (stood around in the background looking menacing) but Gene wants to know how he thinks he will fare against the third member of the so called Totally Awesome Trinity, Ray Traylor…..Gene says that Copeland and Cage have had great success here in WCW, thanks in no small part to their Enforcer, but this match will be one on one, and Traylor isn’t a man to be taken lightly…..

…..Sid just laughs his muttley laugh at that, before screaming that Ray Traylor is just a little man stepping into the ring with a monster…..Sid says that he came to WCW to show the world that he’s the man, by winning the WCW Title…..at Mayhem he was well on his way to doing that, before Traylor and Warrior eliminated him by almost garrotting him with Traylor’s nightstick…..

“…..WELL AT STARRCADE LITTLE MAN, YOU’RE GOING TO FIND OUT THAT ENFORCER IS JUST ANOTHER NAME FOR VICTIM, WHEN YOU’RE IN THE RING WITH THE MASTER….AND RULER…..OF THE WORLD…..”

…..yeah, Sid’s not a great promo either…..but the message is clear, the Totally Awesome Trinity are going to have their hands full with these three men at Starrcade…..now lets go to the back…..

Rating: B+

…..where Lex Luger is warming up ahead of his first match back since his monumental display in the Mayhem match, where he lasted a few minutes short of one hour…..

…..his ribs are still taped following the abuse he has taken recently at the hands of the Giant, and he’s clearly not quite 100%, but he’s stretching through the pain and while his body might not be 100% his mind clearly is, because he looks deathly focused…..

…..that’s when Jean Paul Levesque enters the room, looking over at Luger and shaking his head…..he tells Lex that he’s got a match with the Giant in just six days time, he shouldn’t be taking this match tonight, especially when Luger is front and centre on the Titans hit list (and Levesque should know) he says that Hogan is desperate to take all the major players out of the equation, and Russo has the Giant riled up enough that he might just finish the job before Lex even makes it to Starrcade…..

…..Luger thanks JPL for the concern, but tells him that he has to do this…..he says that he needs to know if his body is ready to face the Giant on Sunday, and he won’t know that without stepping in the ring…..

…..Levesque tries to talk Luger out of it, but the Total Package isn’t listening, telling Levesque to worry about his own problems (mainly the contract signing that he still has to do)…..JPL says that he’s got that under control, and if he has to face The Warrior and Macho Man alone then that’s what he’ll do, but Luger won’t be alone tonight…..he says that he will make sure that the Titans don’t try anything after the bell…..and he’s not coming alone…..because he has someone with him that has quite the score to settle with one Buff Bagwell……

…..as Rowdy Roddy Piper comes walking into shot…..

…..well, limping into shot actually…..on a pair of crutches…..

…..Luger tells Piper that it’s good to see him up and moving, but no disrespect, in that condition he’s going to wind up getting seriously hurt if he tries to get into it with the Titans…..Roddy tells Luger that he’s been seriously hurt all his life, one more night ain’t gonna make a difference…..he might only have one working leg, but he’s still ready to use that one leg to kick Buff right in the Stuff…..

…..Levesque tells Luger that they have his back tonight…..so go out there and show Buff Bagwell just who the Total Package really is…..

Rating: B+

VS.

Lex Luger vs. Buff Bagwell


…..and that match is right now…..it’s been quite the promo/angle heavy show tonight…..and most of those angles have been received very well…..good job really, because the In ring action hasn’t been particularly great…..

…..take this match for example, this was the main event of the show and I could have probably got a better grade with two midcarders facing off in the WWF…..of course, this isn’t the WWF, this is WCW, and this match sucked…..

…..Buff Bagwell is another tipped to be somewhat of a next big thing…..if he’s ever going to realise that potential then he’s going to have to seriously step up his game, because Gideon Cain is improving quicker in the ring than Mr. Bagwell (thanks to the mentoring of Bobby Eaton) and even Hollywood Hogan himself has said on a number of occasions to me that Cain is a star in the making…..makes me wonder if maybe it should have been Gideon taking Bagwell’s spot in the Titans all those months ago…..

…..that being said, Bagwell has benefitted from his run as a member of the Titans of Wrestling…..for example, Lex Luger would have walked a victory here a few months ago and not even CONSIDERED putting the youngster over and since he (rather inexplicably) has creative control, a fact that I didn’t realise until I tried to have him cut a scripted promo on day and he point blank refused, he wouldn’t have HAD to put Bagwell over either…..

…..he put Bagwell over here…..

…..not clean, oh no, no, no, no…..in fact the ending of the match saw the Giant make his presence felt, distracting Luger long enough for Buff to roll him up, hook a handful of tights…..and after working over the ribs all match, Luger simply didn’t have the power in his core to kick out…..but it was a win non the less for Bagwell…..and the biggest of his career to date…..

Winner: Buff Bagwell
Rating: B

…..and true to their word, Jean Paul Levesque and Roddy Piper are quickly out from the back (with Levesque admittedly being the MUCH faster of the two) with JPL dragging the Giant off the apron before he can step over that top rope, and Piper crashing a crutch across the back of Buff Bagwell, before the two men roll into the ring and help Luger to his feet…..

…..before anything else can happen however, the Holy Trinity of Professional Wrestling (Hollywood Hogan, Randy Savage and The Warrior) make their way out…..along with Vince Russo, who has what looks to be a contract in his hand…..

…..Russo tells everyone to calm down…..because before anyone gets too carried away, there is a small piece of business that they need to take care of……mainly a contract that needs signing to make Randy Savage and The Warriors destruction of Jean Paul Levesque at Starrcade official…..

“…..now bro, I’ve done you a huge favour…..even though you don’t have a partner……I’m assuming that you still don’t have a partner anyway, because judging by Mr. Piper’s crutches there I’m guessing that you don’t have medical clearance to compete?….”

…..Piper says no, but no doctor has ever stopped him from fighting before, he’ll sign that contract….put him in the match…..

…..Russo just laughs and says no…..he says there is no way that the executives at Turner will sign off on putting an already injured wrestler in a sanctioned WCW match….the law suits alone would be astronomical…..

…..Buff Bagwell (still selling the effects of the crutch shot from Piper) walks over to Russo and whispers something in his ear…..something that Russo obviously likes the sound of, because he breaks out in a huge grin…..

“…..I like it….that’s why they call you The Stuff……I tell you what Piper, since you want a fight so badly, you can have a fight…..”

“…..oh now you’re giving me a match? Well I want in the tag match, so bring that contract down here so we can sign it, make it official…..and then maybe I’ll shove this other crutch right up the Macho Man’s ass…..”

“…..hold on there bro…..I said a fight, I didn’t say anything about a match…..you wanna fight? You wanna be a one legged man in an ass kicking contest? You got it…..an UNSANCTIONED fight, completely free of litigation, liabilities and lawsuits, against the man that gave you those crutches in the first place……Buff Bagwell…..”

…..Russo says that so long as he signs a waiver the day of Starrcade, a waiver that says that if Piper gets seriously injured during his “fight” that he will not hold either WCW or Buff Bagwell responsible, then he can have Buff in a totally unsanctioned fight…..

….obviously they don’t call Piper the Hot Rod for nothing, and despite Levesques attempt to convince him otherwise, he quickly accepts those conditions…..

…..Russo calls him a stupid son of a b!tch, but says that suits him fine, because Bagwell is going to retire him on Sunday night, before getting back to the original order of business…..he tells Levesque that as he WAS saying, despite the fact that JPL doesn’t have a partner, he’s left space at the bottom for four signatures…..now Warrior and Savage have already signed, so if he has got a partner hiding in the back, all they have to do is walk out, sign on the dotted line, and they’re in the match…..

…..Levesque says that Russo knows damn well that he doesn’t have a partner, so just hand him the contract and lets get this over with…..

…..grinning like a Cheshire Cat that’s exactly what Russo does…..Levesque signs the legally binding contract, and just like that the match is set…..Jean Paul Levesque vs. Randy Savage and The Warrior at Starrcade…..in a two on one handicap match…..

…..with that business concluded Russo thanks everyone for attending, telling them to be sure to contact their local cable operators, because Starrcade is going to be unmissable…..

“……goodnight folks……”

“……oh, one more thing…..I’m such a doofus……there’s still the small matter of finding a replacement for Sting……

…..well Giant, Lex Luger is still standing…..he looks ready to compete Sunday to me……maybe you should do something about that…..”

Rating: A*

…..and with that The Giant heads back towards the ring…..and hell, since the Titans are here anyway, they decide to have a little fun too…..looks like we are going to have a big five on three beat down before we even get to Starrcade…..

…..or make that a five on FOUR beatdown…..because as the crowd absolutely erupts, the number one contender Sting descends from the rafters and lands right in the middle of the ring…..

…..Tony Schiavone has a one man orgasm on commentary, stating that Sting IS here…..that Sting WILL be at Starrcade…..and Hollywood Hogan HAS got a World Title opponent…..

…..obviously Hollywood isn’t happy about this…..and neither is the Giant, who had one eye on a World Title opportunity…..but since the Titans (and Giant) do still have the man advantage, they decide to attack anyway, in an effort to soften up their respective Starrcade opponents…..

…..Bagwell charges at Piper (and receives a crutch to the guts for his troubles) before getting tackled to the mat by the Hot Scot, who proceeds to hammer away at him with right hands…..Bagwell manages to scurry to freedom however and cuts Piper’s offence short with a kick to the hip, before delivering a number of blows of his own…..

…..Giant advances towards Luger as the two men begin to exchange blows, but the Giant soon gets the advantage as he drives Lex back into the corner where he delivers a series of shoulder thrusts to the already weakened midsection of the Total Package…..

…..Savage and Warrior both run at Levesque, who (despite an initial period of offence) is soon overwhelmed, and Savage tosses him to the outside where the Warrior holds JPL in place for Savage to deliver a series of hard right hand blows…..

…..WCW is in real trouble here…..and the only two men not to have moved are Sting and Hogan, who stand (Sting in the ring, Hogan on the floor) staring each other down…..and when it’s clear that Hollywood isn’t going to come to Sting, Sting decides to go to Hollywood, climbing through the ropes and heading up the aisle towards the (rapidly backing off) WCW Champion…..

…..Russo tries to put himself between Hogan and Sting, but Sting cracks him right between the eyes with that black bat, knocking Vince Russo out cold…..Hogan holds his hands up now, telling Sting to “lose the bat brother” saying only a coward would need a weapon…..to which Sting tosses the bat into the hands of Hogan, who quickly throws it down in an effort to placate his Starrcade opponent…..

…..back down at ringside Lex Luger is still being overwhelmed by The Giant…..Piper is somewhat fighting back against Bagwell, but Buff clearly still has the advantage…..and Levesque……well, Levesque is getting absolutely battered on the outside…..Warrior has stopped holding JPL back now…..mainly because he doesn’t need to anymore, Levesque isn’t even fighting back as Savage and Warrior beat on him……

…..that is until a steel chair swings wildly at Randy Savages head from the crowd…..

…..as Shawn Michaels comes flying over the guard rail…..

…..Tony Schiavone is obviously a more virile man than me…..because just a few minutes after his last one man orgasm, he has another at the sight of Shawn Michaels (me, I need at least 20 minutes -_-) and practically explodes when Michaels crashes that steel chair across the back of the Warrior, before diving on top of Randy Savage, and absolutely letting loose with stiff right hand shots…..

…..Hogan has just about seen enough now, he’s having none of this and he quickly turns and bolts to the back, leaving Sting alone in the aisle…..so what is a man to do? Sting simply picks up the discarded black bat, heads back down to the ring……the sight of which sends Bagwell running for the hills…..

…..Sting helps Piper back to his feet (foot) and then cracks the Giant right across the back with the weapon…..

…..it doesn’t put the big man down though…..

…..and Giant turns to stare down Sting with a look of fury on his face…..but Luger (determined to prove himself) rushes at the huge man and hits him with a series of running forearms that back him all the way up to the ropes, before he and Sting join hands to topple the mammoth man over the top with a double running clothesline…..

…..Michaels is still hammering down blows on top of Savage…..and after Savage left Michaels a bloody mess at Mayhem, Shawn Michaels has absolutely retuned the favour here, because Randy Savage is busted WIDE open, and the assault only stops when Warrior literally drags the half unconscious Savage out of harms way, as the two Titans beat a hast retreat up the aisle, leaving WCW standing tall in the middle of the ring…..

…..Jean Paul Levesque looks to be giving chase as he follows the pair up the aisle, but it soon becomes apparent that he’s not, as he stops beside the still unconscious Vince Russo…..

…..and fishes that contract and a pen out of his pocket…..

…..Levesque heads back down to the ring where (a still p!ssed off) Shawn Michaels is standing, and hands him the pen and contract…..Michaels has been reluctant to put pen to ANYTHING since his appearance here in WCW, refusing offers to join the ranks of both WCW and the Titans of Wrestling, but he doesn’t take long to make a decision here…..he snatches the pen from Levesque….drops to the outside…..dips the end of the pen into the puddle of viscera that Randy Savage has left behind…..and signs his name on the dotted line, quite literally in the Macho Man’s blood…..

…..Sting is back and ready to face Hollywood Hogan…..

…..we have our final match for Starrcade…..

…..Jean Paul Levesque has a tag team partner…..

…..and we have one blood soaked contract…..

…..Merry frigging Christmas indeed…..

…..we’ll see you at Starrcade fans…..
