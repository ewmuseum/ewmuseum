A New Generation…..a new attitude? Pay-per-view

WWF Ground Zero: Week 3, September 1997

Week 3, September 1997

The Spectrum (Philadelphia, Pennsylvania)

ATT: 20,000

PPV BUY: 2.00

Announce Team: Jim Ross & Ravishing Rick Rude

The show opens with the usual Pay Per View hype package, highlighting the main stories of the evening…..the ongoing saga of Scott Steiner and Goldust, that will finally see the two meet tonight in one on one action….the never ending battle between Dude Love and Brian Pillman, a battle which the Dude has yet to win a single match in…..the trail of destruction that Stone Cold Steve Austin has wreaked through the WWF in recent weeks…..and finally, the battle for the World Wrestling Federation Championship, that not only sees Kevin Nash challenging Ahmed Johnson this evening, but has expanded into a full scale war between the Wolfpac and the Hart Foundation…..

……the video finishes and we have the usual pyrotechnic display, before we head straight on down to ringside where Jim Ross and Ravishing Rick Rude are waiting to welcome us to the show. They briefly run down the show for us, with Jim Ross telling us that we have three big title matches tonight, and you’ve got to believe that there’s a very big chance we will have three new champions by shows end….obviously Rick Rude disagrees, stating that there’s no chance of the Wolfpac winning anything, ESPECIALLY the Intercontinental or World Titles….because Bret Hart said so…..and as for Al Snow and Chris Candido capturing the Tag Team Titles, the only thing that Al Snow is likely to win any time soon is a trip to the funny farm…..

……Jim Ross says that remains to be seen, but speaking of people that need a trip to the funny farm, it’s time to head over to Howard Finkel, because the Bizarre One, Goldust is about to make his entrance, as the first match of the evening is about to get under way…..

Rating: N/A

VS.

Scott Steiner (w/Sable) vs. Goldust (w/Marlena)


…..and that first match sees Big Poppa Pump looking for revenge….AND his sh!t back….when he takes on the man that not only cost him the World Wrestling Federation Championship back at Summerslam, but cost him the World Wrestling Federation Championship after nailing him around the back of his head with his own lead pipe……and his good lead pipe at that, after stealing (and soiling) the weapon, along with Steiner’s trademark chain mail headdress around six weeks back…..

…..Goldust has both Marlena and Marcus at ringside with him for this one, but not to be outdone, Steiner went out and got a valet of his own….a valet that Marcus in particular knows very well…..because out with Scotty tonight is his new number one freak, the beautiful Sable….

….. this match was always going to be decent, Steiner and Goldust have been two of my most consistent performers since I took this gig, with Scotty in particular having two 5* match of the year candidates under his belt this year alone…..this wasn’t a match of the year candidate, but it was an excellent match…..as usual both men worked their asses off (Steiner more so with an A* performance, but Goldust was no slouch, pulling out a solid A performance of his own) in this evenly fought, back and forth battle….

…..this may be the first time these two have met in one on one competition since this feud began, but they have faced off in a tag team encounter and an eight man clash….and while Goldust didn’t score the pinfall in either match, he WAS on the winning team both times, giving him a perfect record thus far against the former WWF Champion….of course, to win THIS match he actually has to defeat Big Poppa Pump, something which (as Shawn Michaels can attest to) is easier said than done…..

…..Goldust does manage to gain control for a good portion of the match, after Marlena distracted the official, leaving Marcus to catch Scotty on the jaw with a cheap left handed jab….(and say what you will about Marcus’ peculiar lifestyle choices, he stick packs a hell of a punch)…..that puts the Bizarre One in the drivers seat, as he slowly works over Steiner while also making sure to severely creep the crowd out with his unorthodox tactics, openly running his gloved hands over the massive muscles of Big Poppa Pump….

……of course Scotty doesn’t take kindly to that, and he begins to rally back with a cry of rage, pounding Goldust (and not in the way Goldie was hoping for) in the corner with clubbing blows….Marlena hops up onto the apron again looking to give her man a breather, but this table Sable is over like a shot, dragging her back down to the floor and slapping the taste right out of her mouth…..

…….Marcus comes running around from the opposite side of the ring, but Scott Steiner has obviously lit a fire under Sable, who doesn’t even blink as she kicks him square in the balls, dropping her one time client down to his knees…..Sable walks around the back of Marcus, places her foot between his shoulder blades and shoves him face first onto the safety mats, before standing over the incapacitated stylist…..

……Steiner whips Goldust hard into the opposite corner with such force that the Bizarre One comes staggering back out to the middle of the ring, where he’s planted to the mat with a spinning belly to belly, before, in a mirror image of what is happening on the outside, he stands over his equally as incapacitated opponent, before giving the signal to Sable, as the pair lock on stereo Steiner Recliners…..and as Marcus cries out in pain on the outside, Goldust rapidly submits on the inside….and this one is over.

Winner: Scott Steiner
Rating: A (94)

With the match in the bag, Big Poppa Pump mounts the second rope to unleash his double bicep pose for his “Little Pumpers” in the crowd (he doesn’t call his fans that by the way…..although maybe he should…..I could Team him with Test again, the best group of fans in the world right there…..the Testicles and the Pumpers ) as Goldust rolls to the outside, where he and Marcus are helped up the aisle by Marlena…..Marlena stops for a moment however and runs back down to ringside, where she snatches up Steiner’s “good” pipe and chain mail, before turning to escape like a thief in the night……

…..except Sable is standing in her way…..and after already having the make-up slapped off her face once, she sheepishly hands Steiners “sh!t” over, and quickly retreats up the aisle to join the other two members of MGM Productions…..

…..Sable climbs into the ring, where she presents her man with his items…..and Scotty is clearly delighted to have his stuff back……but now there’s just the small matter of regaining one more thing that Goldust took from him…..the World Wrestling Federation Championship.

Rating: N/A

VS.

Ken Shamrock vs. Jeff Jarrett (w/Ted DiBiase)


Our next match sees Ken Shamrock in action once again against a member of the Million Dollar Corporation….a group he’s been feuding with ever since his first appearance in the crowd as a fan, when Rich Steiner took exception to the claim that Mr. Shamrock was the Worlds Most Dangerous Man……

…..Shamrock backed up that claim by defeating Rich Steiner in his very first Pay Per View outing, but if Ken thought he was finished with the Corporation then he was very much mistaken, because after Jeff Jarrett attempted to break that acoustic equaliser over his head at Summerslam, Shamrock snapped……and by locking Double J into the Ankle Lock submission at the top of the ramp during their four way European Championship match….and then refusing to relinquish the hold….Shamrock wound up costing both Jarrett AND himself the chance to be crowned champion……Ken does claim to have his temper in check now, but after Jarrett once again attempted to play one of his greatest hits with that guitar just six days ago on Raw, you’ve got to wonder how much of a hold on that temper Shamrock really has…..

…….the match is decent, and Ken does appear to be keeping his cool….all the while he’s in control of the match anyway….DiBiase clips the foot of Shamrock to cause the distraction however, and the frustration certainly starts to show on the former UFC stars face as Jarrett goes on the offence….and he looks damn right p!ssed off when DiBiase drapes Jarretts foot over the bottom rope, after a hurricanrana from a “running the ropes” sequence sees Shamrock make a flash cover out of nowhere…..

….Deebs saved the match for his man there, but he might have signed his own death warrant in doing so, because Shamrock dives to the outside to give chase to the Million Dollar Manager, who runs for his life…..

…..the two men run around the outside of the ring, with Shamrock gaining ground…..until he turns a final corner and there is Jarrett waiting, with that guitar in hand……

…..Jarrett swings like he’s trying to hit a home run, but Shamrock is able to throw himself to the floor to avoid the shot, leaving the guitar to shatter harmlessly against the ringpost…..

…..Shamrock stands slowly to his feet, dusting the wood splinters off himself as he does so…..but something’s changed in his expression….there’s a fire in the eyes…..he may well have snapped folks…..

…..he charges at Jarrett, who drops the remains of the guitar and bolts to the ring, sliding under the bottom rope….Shamrock is right behind him however, and before Jarrett can even get back to his feet, the Worlds Most Dangerous Man has a vice like grip on his leg, as he positions Jarrett into the Ankle Lock, and within the space of about 4 seconds, Jarrett is screaming out in submission, and this one is over……

Winner: Ken Shamrock
Rating: B

……or at least the match is over….it doesn’t look as though Shamrock is finished, because despite the fact that the official is furiously ringing for the bell, he ain’t letting go….in fact, he’s so far in the zone that I don’t think he can even hear the bell…..or hear the pleading of Ted DiBiase to break the hold….or even feel the slew of officials who are desperately trying to drag him off Jarrett…..he’s not letting go however, and Double J is in danger of suffering serious injury here….so much so, that DiBiase has to call in the troops…..

…..and Rich Steiner, Mark Canterbury, and Dennis Knight come running down the aisle…..

…..the three men join the officials in trying to drag Shamrock off Jarrett, but even the three of them can’t force Ken to break the hold, until in the end, Steiner simply begins kicking at Shamrocks back and head, until FINALLY Shammy breaks the hold…..

…..he bursts to his feet giving a primal scream, that’s almost as wild as the expression on his face…..an expression that’s so wild in fact, that while the Million Dollar Corporation looked to be considering jumping Shamrock, they all back away with their hands up, as Southern Justice collect a hobbled Jeff Jarrett, as the group makes its way to the back…..and is ALMOST at the entrance way, when Howard Finkel’s voice declares that……

“…..I have just been informed, that due to Ken Shamrock’s refusal to break the Ankle Lock submission hold, and repeated ignoring of the referee’s requests to relinquish the hold……the official has decided to REVERSE his decision……therefore…..the winner of the match as a result of a disqualification……Jeff Jarrett……”

…..Shamrock might not have heard much in the last five minutes, but he heard that……and so did Jeff Jarrett…..who despite the fact that he can’t even walk under his own weight, still has the audacity to arrogantly raise his arm in victory, just before the entire Corporation disappear to the back…..leaving Shamrock to continue to lose his sh!t as he has a meltdown in the ring, leaving us to head to the back……

Winner: Jeff Jarrett (via Reversed Decision)
Rating: A

….where Dok Hendrix is standing by with the Hart Foundation, Bret and Owen Hart….as well as Chris Jericho and his imposing bodyguard, Test….

…..Dok says that tonight, the Wolfpac has the opportunity to not only capture the WWF Intercontinental Championship in just a few moments time, but later tonight, Kevin Nash may well be walking out of this arena as the World Wrestling Federation Championship……but despite this, Bret Hart continues to insist that the Wolfpac will be leaving Philadelphia tonight empty handed…..why is that?

……Chris Jericho (who is still wearing the retired WWF Light Heavyweight Championship belt around his waist) fields that question, saying that much more than that….not only will the Wolfpac not win a single title tonight, they won’t even win a match….because Chris Jericho, the Ayatollah of RocknRolla, is going to finish with Scott Hall, exactly what he started on Raw is War….and that’s kicking his ass baby……

……Owen Hart says that as far as Sean Waltman winning the Intercontinental Championship goes, “The Kid” would have more chance of winning the Mr. Universe contest that he has of winning here tonight….and looking at those scrawny little chicken legs, he ain’t winning Mr. Universe any time soon…..

…..Bret rounds the interview up, saying that Kevin Nash and the rest of the Wolfpac are nothing more than a cancer on this great sport…..mercenaries who, just like every other American, only care about two things….themselves, and the almighty dollar…..they don’t want to win championships for the honour it brings, they don’t care about the heritage of the championships, or the pride that comes with knowing you’re the best…..all they look at when they see those gold belts is money…..

“…..well to be the World Wrestling Federation Champion, you need to be the best…..and I AM the best…..the best there is……the best there was…..and the best there ever will be….why won’t Kevin Nash be the next World Wrestling Federation Champion?….because you’re looking at the next WWF Champion right here…..and you can take that to the bank Kevin Nash…..”

Rating: B+

VS.

WWF Intercontinental Championship Match

Owen Hart (c) vs. Sean Waltman


So that Intercontinental Championship Match is up next, as Sean Waltman challenges Owen Hart, and he’ll be looking to give the entire Wolfpac a moral booster, by starting their evening out on the winning foot….and despite what Bret Hart thinks, Sean Waltman is more than worthy of not only winning that Intercontinental Championship, but carrying it for a long time as well….in fact, Sean Waltman is a man highly tipped to be one of the next big things in Wrestling, and if you go by how steadily his overall popularity has risen in the past 18 months then that may be true, because realistically, not only is he marginally more over than Current Champion, Owen, he’s also more over than even team mate Scott Hall, to the point where Waltman himself believes he should be getting that main event push (and main event money no doubt)…..unfortunately for him there’s two problems with that….one, I’ve already got more main eventers than you can shake a sh!tty stick at…..and two, the thing that bought him to the dance to begin with, his Cruiserweight style…..is actually holding him back…..so at upper Midcard you stay for now Mr. Waltman…..

…..considering each man’s ability I was expecting good things from this match, even with the limitations I put them under in regards to the actual length of the contest…..they had about 15 minutes to show this crowd what they can do, and they didn’t disappoint…..because while this was no Rey Mysterio-esq high flying masterpiece, the two men certainly showcased their individual agility, in a very evenly fought contest……even more evenly fought because while neither man came out alone (Waltman with Hall and Hart with Test) the official instantly ordered both men to the back, leaving this as a true one on one match up…..

….after Owen controlled a good portion of the match the action really kicked up a gear as Waltman fought his heart out to get back into the match, and we almost see these two great workers come to a stalemate as the hit a series of counter and reversal manoeuvres and pinning combinations that sees at least six near falls in the space of around two minutes, until Waltman sends Owen hard into the corner, following up with a running shoulder block…..Hart manages to swing his body up and out of the way however, leaving Waltman to collide with nothing but ringpost as he drives his shoulder through the turnbuckles, before Owen’s momentum allows him to convert that into a Schoolboy roll-up…..and with both feet on the ropes for good measure, Owen steals the one, the two, and the three.

Winner: ….and Still WWF Intercontinental Champion…..Owen Hart
Rating: B+

We see a quick recap video now, highlighting Stone Cold Steve Austin’s recent path of destruction…..the snubbing and subsequent Stunning of Vince McMahon….McMahon declaring open season on Steve Austin, while Steve Austin declares open season right back, on the entire WWF, followed by snippets of his recent hit list, featuring everyone from Savio Vega to Ken Shamrock, as well as shots of the consecutive matches that he cost the Nation of Domination…..which is exactly the reason that our next match is taking place…..nothing to shout home about here, this was simply aired to give our previous competitors time to exit the ring.

Rating: N/A

VS.

Steve Austin vs. Faarooq


There’s a marked man in the World Wrestling Federation….and his name is Stone Cold Steve Austin…..although if you were to ask Mr. Austin himself, he’d probably tell you that it’s not he that is marked, but rather the entire WWF roster….because after turning down Mr. McMahons offer to become the newest Corporate face of the company (and a guaranteed World Title opportunity to boot) Austin has declared that he needs nothing and no one to take the top prize in wrestling, and if he has to Stun every man on the roster to do so, then so be it…..

…..The Nation of Domination were riding high just a few short weeks back….they walked into Summerslam with the Intercontinental, European AND Light Heavyweight Championship…..while they walked OUT of Summerslam with no titles….and one member short, as The Rock got his marching orders….so it goes without saying that Faarooq needs this win, and preferably needs to take Austin out in the process, because the man that serves up Stone Colds head will be handsomely rewarded…..

…..the match itself was fantastic as well….certainly one of, if not THE best match of Faarooq’s WWF career, who while he has had some decent bouts, hasn’t exactly been known for his stellar in ring work…..this was easily the second best match on this card, which is testament to Steve Austin’s form right now, because in terms of positioning on the card, Faarooq is not on his level at all….

….that shows in the opening portion of the match too, because Austin absolutely (for lack of a better word) dominates…..he’s completely on fire at the moment, and this crowd has never been hotter for him, and the roof nearly blows off the place as Faarooq gets battered from corner to corner, to the outside, up and down the aisle and back into the ring again…..in fact, it’s only a thunderous Spinebuster from Faarooq that finally halts the assault, as the Nation leader goes on the offence…..

…..he slows the pace right down, and while Austin may be the better brawler of the pair, he can’t match Faarooq in terms of raw strength, so it’s big high impact power moves that the former WCW Champion focuses on, trying to wear his opponent down to the point that he can attempt to finish him off with the Dominator…..

…..unfortunately that opportunity doesn’t arrive….Austin is a man on a mission, and he’s not about to let that mission get derailed any time soon, which is why he soon finds a way to fight back into this one, and although Faarooq DOES manage to get Austin up in position for the Dominator, Stone Cold is able to wriggle free, land behind his opponent…..spin Faarooq around….and one kick to the gut/Stunner combination later, this one is over.

Winner: Steve Austin
Rating: A (97)

The match is over, and as Austin mounts the turnbuckles to celebrate for these ecstatic fans, Rick Rude remarks that Faarooq may have been better served by having the Nation of Domination out here at ringside, with Jim Ross actually agreeing….it’s all redundant now however, this one is in the books and it’s Steve Austin’s music playing……

…..or at least it was…..until it comes to a screeching halt…..and some unfamiliar music starts to play……

…….and Nelson makes his way out from the back, as he begins to walk his mammoth frame down the aisle……

……Nelson hasn’t been seen since since costing the Nation heavily at Summerslam, before being brutally excommunicated by the group…..but as Rick Rude points out, what better way to atone for his previous mistakes, than to take out Stone Cold Steve Austin right here…..collect that bounty, and hand Faarooq those Championship rewards……..

…..Stone Cold stops his celebration and stands staring at the 6’9, near 500lb behemoth that is heading his way…..he backs up a few paces as Nelson steps effortlessly over the top rope and walks right up to Austin going face to face (well, face to chin, Nelson has a good seven inches on Austin) the two men stare each other down for a moment, before Nelson finally moves Austin to one side…..backs up to the ropes for a running start……and drops his entire half ton body on top of the still down Faarooq……

……the crowd actually pops pretty big for that…..Nelson is a god awful wrestler, but for some reason he’s over like rover, and the crowd always enjoys seeing Faarooq taken down a peg or two………..

…..the rest of the Nation come running out from the back, but with Austin and Nelson still in the ring, while Scorpio, D’Lo and Henry still have a match later in the evening to worry about, Kama collects the flattened form of Faarooq, dragging him under the bottom rope to the outside and helping him to the back, promising both Austin and Nelson that this is far from over, as we go to a hype video detailing the road that Al Snow and Chris Candido have taken on the road to their WWF Tag Team Title match….a match that’s coming up next.

Rating: A*

VS.

WWF World Tag Team Championship Match

The New Age Outlaws (c) vs. Al Snow & Chris Candido (w/Sunny)


That brings us to our second Championship match of the evening, as the New Age Outlaws put the gold on the line against Al Snow and Chris Candido…..in a match that could well have been Snow and Candido defending against the Outlaws, because Chris Candido was half a second away from picking up the victory and winning the titles at Summerslam, until the Outlaws quite literally stole the win out from under them…..

…..because of this, and to thank his for basically putting up with his wacky sh!t, Al Snow went out and personally arranged this match for himself and Candido….and as an added bonus, he’s even bought Candido’s former valet Sunny along for the ride…..not that the former Bodydonna is particularly pleased to have the back stabbing Sunny by his side again, but she’s here tonight, and looking absolutely gorgeous as ever….

…..the Outlaws have promised that while Snow and Candido have been an entertaining little act, that’s all they are…..an act…..they claim that they are the real deal, and while they can certainly entertain, they can get real serious, real quick……just ask Jeff Hardy….who’s last WWF appearance saw him launched off the side of the entrance stage, locked inside a dumpster……

……this match was (again) a really good match, there haven’t been any real stinkers tonight and this one continues the trend…..Snow and Candido start off very strong, clearly well up for this one, even if Candido is somewhat distracted by Sunny’s unwanted (by him anyway) presence at ringside…..although (perhaps unsurprisingly) he and Sunny have some excellent chemistry that actually helped the match…..unfortunately, while that chemistry helped the match, it hindered Candido, because after Sunny decided to interject herself into the contest, attempting to distract Billy Gunn with her “womanly charms”, Candido was the one that found himself distracted, as he was so intent on telling her that he doesn’t want her help OR her interference, he fails to see Billy Gunn running across the ring, who drives a knee into the back of Candido, which in turn sends Candido crashing into Sunny….and Sunny crashing off the apron down to the floor below……

……Snow tells Head to go and check on Sunny, but with Head seemingly reluctant to move (go figure) he goes and checks on her himself, missing an opportunity to tag into the match when Candido manages to make it to the corner, only to get dragged back to the middle of the ring when there’s no partner to save him…..

……the Outlaws then show what it was that bought them the tag team titles to begin with, as they cut the ring in half to isolate Candido, before tagging quickly in and out of the match, wearing Candido down and making full use of the official’s five count to inflict frequent double team moves (as well as using each other to distract the official, so the non-legal man can inflict some infraction of the rule, such as the Road Dogg choking Candido over the top ring rope…..

……Sunny is back on her feet, and both she and Snow begin pounding the mat in an effort to rally Candido, who does actually make the tag at one point, but the official fails to spot it thanks to the Road Dogg illegally entering the ring to distract him…..

……Candido is desperate to capture the gold here however, and he refuses to stay down…..and in perhaps the most impressive move of the night, manages to catch hold of Bad Ass Billy Gunn during a Famouser attempt, managing to counter it with a spectacular Powerbomb, before finally clawing his way over to his corner, where he makes the tag to Al Snow…..

……Snow comes into the match like a man on a mission, while Sunny checks on Candido, and takes it to both the Road Dogg and the Bad Ass….Snow is fully rested and keen to capitalise on this opportunity at the gold, and after Candido shoves Sunny away from him, he too rejoins the action, with the match briefly breaking down into a Texas Tornado style Brawl…..and while Candido didn’t want Sunny’s help, she was still involved in the finish of this one…..because after Candido has tackled Gunn to the outside, she again climbs up onto the apron, where she ensures she has the attention of the referee, before kicking Head into the ring right into the waiting hands of Al Snow……Al looks at Head…..then looks at Sunny (who is indicating with her hands for him to “just do it” as Nike would say)……before turning and nailing Jesse James right between the eyes…..

…..Road Dogg doesn’t go down however…..but he does stagger backwards into the ring ropes, the force of which propels him back towards Snow, who scoops him right up and plants him into the mat with the Snow Plow…..and as Candido clings on to Billy Gunn’s leg on the outside, the official counts the one, the two………and the three……this one is over, and we have brand new WWF Tag Team Champions.

Winner(s): ….and New WWF Tag Team Champions…..Al Snow & Chris Candido
Rating: B+

Candido rushes back into the ring, and in a rare show of emotion embraces Snow, as the official hands the pair the Tag Team Titles….even Sunny isn’t shunned quite so badly by Candido as she joins the new Champions in celebrating their victory…..

…..Billy Gunn on the other hand has dragged the Road Dogg to the outside, where he rouses his groggy partner, and the now former Tag Team Champions slam their hands on the announce desk in anger, screaming at Jim Ross that “this ain’t going down like that”, as they collect a pair of steel chairs and a microphone from ringside…..

…..they slide back inside the ring, weapons in hand……but they don’t attack….not yet anyway…..instead The Road Dogg has a message for them….he says that the Outlaws guaranteed that there was no way in hell that these two jokers were going to be walking out of here as the WWF Tag Team Champions…..

“…..and I’m telling you right now, there’s more chance of Al Snow passing a psychiatric evaluation than there is of you two boys walking out of this ring with those Championship Belts…..”

…..Gunn and James raise the chairs high, as Snow and Candido prepare to fight their way out of this one…..before swinging however, the Outlaws theatrically open up the chairs and sit themselves down, where Road Dogg pulls a sheet of paper out of his tights, handing it to the confused looking official…..who reads it over, before asking if this is legit?….Billy tells him to check the signatures “and make the call”…..which is exactly what he does, before heading over to Howard Finkel, who lets the crowd know that…..

“…….ladies and gentlemen, the winners of this match, Al Snow and Chris Candido…..HOWEVER……the official has just informed me that the contract for tonight match does NOT state that the Championships were on the line…….therefore, STILL……..”

…….the Fink doesn’t get to finish that statement however, because the Road Dogg snatches the microphone away…..

“……therefore, because cuckoo for coco pops here was too stupid to make sure he was signing for a title match……STILL your World Wrestling Federation, Tag Team Champions of the Worldddddd…….the Road Dogg Jesse James…..the Bad Ass Billy Gunn……the New Age Outlaw’s……….sorry pinky and the brain…..we guaranteed that you wouldn’t be winning the titles tonight, and we hate to say we told you so…….but we told you so……”

…….Gunn and James cackle like school girls as the official recovers the title belts from a devastated Candido and confused looking Snow, handing them back to the rightful Champions, who head to the back with the belts held high like they just won the match, as Jim Ross condemns them for stealing the title belts once again…..as Rick Rude rightly points out however, it was Al Snow that set this match up, and while the Outlaws may not have let anyone know before hand that this was a non-title match, the blame for this one lies solely on the shoulders of Al Snow.

Rating: A

VS.

Scott Hall vs. Chris Jericho


If you’re keeping score at home, the current score for the evening in the ongoing battle between the Wolfpac and the Hart Foundation, it’s Harts 1, ‘Pac 0, after Owen Hart successfully defended the WWF Intercontinental Championship against Sean Waltman earlier in the evening….there are no Championships on the line in this one (even if Chris Jericho IS still wearing the Light Heavyweight Championship) although had it not been for the self proclaimed “Ayatollah of RocknRolla’s” interference on Raw, nailing Hall in the back of the head, The Bad Guy may well have been walking into this evening with the WWF Title around his waist……

…..much like earlier in the evening, the official isn’t in the mood for external tangibles tonight….because while Test accompanies Chris Jericho to the ring, he’s not there for long….in fact he lasts all of around four minutes before the referee catches him attempting to interfere, and the big Canadian bodyguard gets evicted from ringside and ordered to the back….

…..Jericho is furious at this….and treats us to a Chris Jericho patented “temper tantrum” that winds up backfiring, because as he hops around kicking the ring ropes he hops right into a Scott Hall right hand that knocks him on square on his ass….

…..this is yet another great match on a night of great matches, even with (yes, I sound like a broken record I know) Scott Hall’s long term injury problems these two put on a hell of a match….granted it wasn’t quite up to Hall’s Monday Night effort with Bret Hart just six days ago, but then, for as good as Chris Jericho is, he’s not Bret Hart…..yet…..but then Jericho has only been in the business a paltry seven years, which is barely a blink of the eye compared to the Hitman’s almost two decades in the business…..and when you consider the fact that seven years into Bret Hart’s career he was still Wrestling in Canada (while seven years into Jericho’s he has already main evented two Pay Per Views for the largest Wrestling company in the world) it says a lot for how good Jericho really is……

……unfortunately for him, tonight he just wasn’t quite good enough, and despite Jericho getting a good portion of the evenings offence in, Hall still managed to get all his signature moves in, including catching a Jericho cross body attempt from the top and converting it into a Fallaway Slam…..before finishing the brash Jericho off with the Razor’s Edge, giving him the win, as well as tying the nights battle with the Harts up at one a piece.

Winner: Scott Hall
Rating: B+

VS.

Bradshaw, Rey Mysterio & Dean Malenko vs. The Nation of Domination


Our next match sees the Nation of Domination back in action, as they face the trio of Bradshaw, Rey Mysterio, and Dean Malenko……and this time it would seem that the Nation have learned from their earlier mistake, because this time they have Kama accompanying them to ringside…..and that’s probably a smart idea when you bear in mind that the newest Nation recruits, D’Lo and Henry have a total combined 4 years experience between them, with Brown debuting just three years ago, while Henry has JUST finished his first year in the sport….compare that to their opponents combined thirty years of experience, and the Nation might need all the help they can get here…..

……while this was a decent match (and it was, all things considered) it was easily the worst match on the card, but then due to its positioning that suited me just fine, because I knew that our next match was going to be good….very good….so if this gave the crowd a chance to catch a breather then that was fine by me……

…..these men all complimented each other pretty well too……you had Scorpio and Mysterio, the two high flyers, Malenko and Brown, two men that while pretty agile, still preferred to keep the action grounded…..and then you had Bradshaw and Henry, the two powerhouses…..although it’s not often that Bradshaw finds himself the weaker man in ANY contest, but then when you’re in there with the World’s Strongest Man, second best it what you’re going to have to settle for…..

…..second best is what Bradshaw had to settle for with the result here as well with Kama’s presence certainly paying dividends, shoving Mysterio off the top rope as the six men brawled, leaving Mysterio wide open for a running splash by Henry, and that was all she wrote.

Winner(s): The Nation of Domination
Rating: B

We head to the back once more, where Dok Hendrix is standing by with Dude Love ahead of his upcoming battle with Brian Pillman…a man who according to Dok, has run down, ridiculed, assaulted, embarrassed, and perhaps most importantly, defeated the Dude in every meeting they have had, be it singles, tag team, or even eight man matches…..

…..Dok says that Pillman has questioned the Dude’s manhood, and claimed on numerous occasions the Dude Love’s children should be embarrassed to have a joke for a father…..does the Dude really believe that he can defeat the Corporate Cannon tonight?….

“…..first of all Dok, the Dude can categorically declare right now……that his manhood is just fine, and his disco balls are right exactly where they should be…..I checked a few minutes ago….secondly, I don’t just THINK I can beat Brian Pillman……I know I can beat Brian Pillman ……”

…..the Dude says that Pillman can make all the claims he wants, he can say that Dude Love could never beat a man like Brian Pillman, but the Dude can get down and dirty with the best of them…..and if you don’t believe the Dude, then just ask all these great fans, right here….in Philadelphia, Pennsylvania…..because the Dude spent quite some time just down the road, in the little bingo hall that could……and the Philly fans know EXACTLY how EXTREME the Dude can get…..

…..as for Pillman’s constant claims that his baby dude and dudette are embarrassed by their father, he thinks not…..because for the first time in his career, they’re sat right there at ringside tonight, and they’re going to be rocking that tie dye in support of their big daddy…..

“……..owwwww, have mercy…….”

…..and with that, the Dudes smooth disco music can be heard playing over the house speakers, as the Coolest Cat in the WWF jives out of shot and on his way to the ring.

Rating: B+

VS.

Dude Love vs. Brian Pillman


That match is indeed next, it’s Dude Love vs. Brian Pillman for the second time in consecutive months, and after having the joint match of the night at Summerslam (tied with Austin/Hart) I was fairly confident that they would take the honours solo tonight……I was right as well……Austin/Faarooq came close, but this was as close to a perfect five star match as you can get, we’re talking four and nine tenths close right here……

….the Dude gets a great response from these Philadelphia faithfuls’s, although as Love stops off at ringside to kiss his wife and children, there is an audible boo and a chant of “Cactus Jack” from a small smarky section of the crowd….but then, it was here in Philly that a member of the crowd made a sign encouraging ECW star The Sandman to “Cane Dewey”, the Dude’s then 3 year old son, who is sat right here at ringside tonight, so that small response shouldn’t come as much of a surprise…..

….Pillman actually garners cheers from that same section of fans, that clearly remember BOTH men’s short stint down in the land of Extreme….and while Pillman’s was only a fleeting visit, he certainly made an impression, honing the “Loose Cannon” gimmick that we all know today, debuting at the February Cyberslam event in 1996 and threatening to “yank out my johnson and p!ss in this hell-hole”…..little did the ECW faithful know, but the only reason Pillman was there (in this little alternative universe of mine) was because he had just finished a WWF funded stint in rehab for drug and alcohol addictions, and was set to make his debut for us a month later at Wrestlemania….his little stop of at the land of extreme was a short term loan from us to rattle away any rust before he walked out onto the grandest stage of all……

……the two men start with a stare down….although Pillman was cackling and wild eyed as he stood face to face with the Dude….he stops laughing in quick measure though, because the Dude shows that he means business straight from the get go, nailing Pillman with a series of punches that send The Corporate Cannon bailing to the safety of the outside…..except it ain’t that safe…..because after promising to up his game tonight, the Dude follows his opponent straight to the outside and follows up with a spinning neck breaker right on the floor…..

…..back on the inside and the Dude stays on the offence, whipping Pillman hard into the turnbuckles, before unloading a series of right hands that leaves his opponent slumped on the mat in the corner, before the Dude backs it up and charges at Pillman, driving a running knee straight into the jaw of the Cannon…..

…..Pillman is clearly taken aback by the sheer brutality that the Dude is coming after him with, and has to take desperate measures to halt the assault, as he claws at his opponents eyes like a rabid animal, finally giving himself enough of a breather that he’s able to hurl the Dude right over the top rope with such force that he crashes into the crowd barrier, right in front of his family, who Pillman takes great delight in taunting, asking them “what do you think of your daddy now, you little brats” as he slams Love’s head into the steel, score tossing him back into the ring, where he continues the assault….

…..Jim Ross remarks that while the Dude’s attempt to remain the better man, vanquishing “the bad man” for the sake of his children, he’s going to have to do something a little different if he ever hopes to pick up the victory over Pillman…..with Rick Rude remarking that maybe…..MAYBE….Cactus Jack would have a chance of defeating the Corporate Cannon, “but this Dude Love…..he doesn’t have a hope in hell Jim Ross”….he looks to be right too, because the Dude is fading fast under the constant abuse he’s taking at the hands of Pillman…..Pillman however quite literally has no filter, and every now and then he takes things too far…..which is exactly what happens here……

…..because after tossing the Dude to the outside again, he drags him over to where Mrs. Love and the two Baby Love’s are sitting, again slamming the Dude’s head into the safety rail…..taunting the two children, before grabbing hold of the Dude’s Main Squeeze, and kissing her right on the lips…..he gets shoved away by the young lady…..AND slapped ring across the face as Mrs. Dude Love spits the taste of Pillman out of her mouth, but the Corporate Cannon doesn’t care, continuing to cackle like the hyena that he is…..at least until a blur of tie dye crashes into him, and the Dude unloads with a savage attack not seen since…..well, since his Cactus Jack days……

…..the Dude pounds Pillman’s head repeatedly into the crowd rail, before whipping his opponent face first into the ring post….Pillman tries to create some space between him and the Dude, the Dude on the other hand charges and tackles him back first into the ring apron, before whipping him hard into the ring steps, before again following it up with that patented running knee straight into the steel…..The Dude slowly rises to his feet with a sick smile on his face, before shouting out a very Un-Dude Love like “Bang, Bang” and reaching underneath the ring…….

…..where he pulls out a two by four plank…..wrapped in barbed wire…..much to the delight of this Philly crowd…..

….Jim Ross shouts out that this is NOT an anything goes match….and if the Dude uses that weapon he WILL be disqualified……I don’t think the Dude cares however….as he holds the barbed wire board up with glee to the crowd, before turning to face his family…….

…..which is when he stops……and drops the weapon……because both his son and daughter are hiding away from the sight of their father wielding a lethal weapon like a maniac…..

……the Dude goes over to attempt to reassure his offspring that it’s ok, the Bad Man isn’t coming back……he should have kept his eyes on his opponent though….because Pillman has picked up that barbed wire board and tossed it into the ring….now he nails Love with a knee to the kidneys, before picking him up, rolling him back into the ring….picking up a steel chair….and following the Dude back inside the Squared Circle…..

…..he stands over his opponent with the chair raised, but like Jim Ross said, this is NOT an anything goes match….which is why the official snatches the chair off him…turns away….and drops the weapon back to the outside…..keeping his back turned just long enough for Pillman to pick up that barbed wire board he so innocently tossed into the ring a few moments ago…..and drive it right between the eyes of the Dude……

……he drops the weapon to the mat and kicks it to the outside, before dropping down to make the cover…..the official turns around, and three seconds later, Brian Pillman has defeated Dude Love…….again.

Winner: Brian Pillman
Rating: A (99)

Brian Pillman is once again victorious over Dude Love…..and while the Corporate Cannon stands victorious, the Dude is busted wide open, a bloody mess on the mat courtesy of that shot from the barbed wire board…..

…..clearly Pillman isn’t happy to leave it at that however, as he drops to his knees to drive his fists into that open wound of the Dude, and with spittle flying like a rabid dog, he screams at him like a maniac, screams that he told him….“I told you that a pathetic piece of primordial slime like you can’t defeat the Crowned Prince of Chaos”…..before dragging the bloodied Dude to his feet and tossing him through the ropes to the outside, right in front of his family…..

…..the Dude slowly pulls himself to his feet, blood dripping down his face as he desperately yells at his wife to take the kids and go…..and as she gathers up her children and the trio disappear into the crowd, Pillman comes rushing into shot again, barbed wire board in hand, as he drives it once more into the skull of the Dude…..

…..the Dude is done….but Pillman isn’t….as he takes that barbed wire and grinds it into the flesh of the Dude, while Jim Ross shouts for security over at the announce desk to get out here before that maniac Brian Pillman inflicts permanent damage to Dude Love…..

…..security does indeed finally come out…..but it’s too late…..Pillman is finished anyway….finished with this assault, and according to him “……I’m finished with you…..you’re nothing….a nobody….an afterthought…..you’re an embarrassment to yourself, and your entire stinking bloodline……consider this the period, at the end of your pathetic existence…..”

….with that, Pillman tears the tie dye shirt from the chest of the Dude, before blowing his nose on it and throwing it down like a piece of garbage on top of the bloody remains of Dude Love, before storming to the back, as the paramedics rush to ringside to tend to his victim.

Rating: A*

VS.

WWF Championship Match

Ahmed Johnson (c) (w/Jim Cornette) vs. Kevin Nash


How do you follow a match (and angle) like that? Why with a technical masterclass like Ahmed Johnson vs. Kevin Nash naturally…..because it’s main event time, and the World Wrestling Federation Championship is on the line…..

….we air a quick promo package while they tend to, and wheel out the remains of Dude Love, highlighting the ascension of Ahmed Johnson…..from costing the Undertaker his Wrestlemania match against his brother Kane and aligning himself with Jim Cornette, to winning the King of the Ring (taking out the Undertaker again in the process) and finally capturing the WWF Championship from Scott Steiner last month at Summerslam…..to say it’s been an eventful six months for Ahmed Johnson would be an understatement, and he’s here tonight to face a (very) credible contender, the Wolfpac’s own…..Kevin Nash…..

….a lot of people (myself included) were rather sceptical as to exactly what we were going to get out of these two….I wasn’t expecting brilliance, but I was hoping not to have a repeat of the Diesel/Mable Summerslam snorefest…..thankfully, these two delivered far more than I was expecting, in what was actually a solid match…..of course it helped that it didn’t go long (around 15 minutes) because neither man has a lot in his tank, but these two are over enough that the sheer crowd reaction was enough to instantly improve the match…..

….Ahmed Johnson has been making a list (and checking it twice) it’s not a list of who’s naughty or nice however….it’s his list of victims, and thus far there are some pretty impressive names on there…..Scott Steiner, The Undertaker, Steve Austin, Cactus Jack…..even Kevin Nash has fallen to the big man from Pearl River (although that was with an assist from Shawn Michaels) so it’s no wonder that the WWF Champion as confident as confident can be…..and he doesn’t back down from Big Sexy for so much as a second, and even sends Nash flying halfway across the ring with a huge push off a lock up attempt……Nash isn’t one to walk away from a fight either however, and he returns the favour a few moments later…..from there these two big bulls just beat on each other…..it’s not pretty, but it is pretty entertaining….

……there is no real prolonged periods of offence for either man either, as they pretty much just exchange moves for the majority of the match, in what essentially becomes one big game of “can you top this”…..Nash hits a Sidewalk Slam, Ahmed hits a Spinebuster……Nash connects with the Big Boot, Johnson runs through him with a Clothesline…..Nash drills Ahmed with a Powerslam, while Ahmed gets the seven footer up for a Bodyslam…..and so on and so forth, until it finally breaks down to an out and out slugfest……that Kevin Nash actually gets the better of, as he drives Ahmed all the way back into the corner, where he continues to hammer Ahmed with blows……..

……which is when the feed switches to a spit-screen….showing Kevin Nash hitting the Snake-Eyes on Ahmed on one side………and a shot of backstage on the other……where The Harts, Jericho, and Test are putting the finishing touches on what appears to be a locker room beatdown of Sean Waltman and Scott Hall, before Bret puts a halt to it, telling his compadres to “come on…..let’s go take a closer look at this title match”…..as the four men step over the two Wolfpac members and walk out of shot…..presumably on their way to the ring……

…..Nash is oblivious to this however, and as we switch back to a single screen shot, he is in the process of whipping Ahmed into the ropes and dropping him with a Big Boot (giving a mocking version of the old Hulk Hogan ‘cupping the hand to the ear routine’) before dropping those straps……and we all know what that means…..it’s Jacknife time……

…….which is exactly the time that the Canadians part the curtain and come running down the aisle…..

…….Owen, Jericho and Test run interference on one side of the ring, as the Hitman rolls into the ring with a steel chair on the other…..and since Nash has Ahmed in position for the Powerbomb it leaves his back wide open for Hart to crash the steel chair across…..once…..twice…..three times…..before Bret quickly exits the ring……

…..ain’t no way Kevin Nash is going to agree to take the (sketchy at the best of times) Pearl River Plunge of Ahmed Johnson……which is why Ahmed picks him up and slams him hard back down to the mat with a Standing Spinebuster…..and as the official turns around to see Ahmed making the cover, that’s all she wrote, and Ahmed Johnson retains WWF Championship.

Winner: ….and Still WWF Champion…..Ahmed Johnson
Rating: B+

The official hands Ahmed Johnson the WWF Title Belt, but the World Champion barely even has time to raise the Winged Eagle over his head before that same steel chair that just put down Kevin Nash is crashed across HIS back…….Bret Hart is back in the ring…..and Owen, Jericho, and Test have joined him……

…..that first blow doesn’t actually do a lot other than to p!ss Ahmed Johnson off…..and he turns around looking like he’s ready to straight up murder someone…..unfortunately for him he turns around straight into another chair shot…..this one right between the eyes…..and THIS one does put him down…..

……Bret Hart promised that the Wolfpac would not be leaving Ground Zero with any Championship Gold, and he’s made 100% sure that this prediction came true here, because as all four Canadians start putting the boots to Ahmed, it’s clear that Hart didn’t help the Pearl River Powerhouse out of the goodness of his heart……

…..Jericho and Test goes over to lay some shots in on Nash while Owen keeps up the assault on Ahmed…..Bret on the other hand has broken away from the pack, and has picked up the WWF Championship, which he holds up to a nearby camera, letting everyone at home know that “the next World Wrestling Federation Champion isn’t going to be a Scott Hall or a Kevin Nash……it’s going to be the best…..the best there is, the best there was, and the best there ever will be……ME!” as a rather hectic night draws to a close.

Rating: A*
