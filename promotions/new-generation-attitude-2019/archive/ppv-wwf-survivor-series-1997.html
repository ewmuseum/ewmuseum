A New Generation…..a new attitude? Pay-per-view

WWF Survivor Series 1997

Posted 5 June 2019

Week 3, November 1997

The Spectrum (Philadelphia, Pennsylvania)

ATT: 20,000

PPV BUY: 2.21

Announce Team: Jim Ross, Jerry “The King” Lawler & Ravishing Rick Rude

It’s that time of year again…..a time to give thanks…..a time to gather with friends and family alike…..a time to reflect on all you have…..but for the stars of the WWF, it’s only time for one thing…..it’s time to survive…..because it’s time…..for the 1997 Survivor Series…..

…..once again Jerry “The King” Lawler joins “Good Old” Jim Ross and “Ravishing” Rick Rude at the commentary table, and these three have plenty to say about tonight’s proceedings, there is plenty at stake tonight…..championships and championship opportunities are both up for grabs here, but that’s perhaps not the most interesting thing to happen tonight (after all, that happens on a weekly basis here in the World Wrestling Federation)…..what doesn’t happen on a weekly basis, and what in fact has NEVER happened before….is the Owner of the World Wrestling Federation, Vincent Kennedy McMahon lacing up a pair of boots and stepping into the ring….but he’s going to tonight, as his war against Stone Cold Steve Austin forces him to take steps he never thought he would have to do….compete inside that very ring…..

…..Rick Rude is horrified at the prospect of “Mr. McMahon” having to step between those ropes, especially against a lowlife like Stone Cold Steve Austin, but he says that if anyone can take the starch out of the Rattlesnakes sails, it’s the Owner himself…..

…..Lawler and Ross aren’t so sure…..stating that stepping into the ring with a man like Stone Cold Steve Austin with little to no training is tantamount to suicide….Lawler says that this isn’t the boardroom, this is the squared circle, and it’s no place for the CEO of a Billion Dollar Corporation…..and while that remains to be seen, we will have to wait…..because our opening match is happening right now…..

Rating: N/A

Survivor Series Match

Nelson, The British Bulldog, Bradshaw, Dean Malenko & Rey Mysterio

vs. The Nation of Domination

….and since this IS the Survivor Series, we’re opening up with a Survivor Series match, as Nelson and his team look to continue the rebellion against the Nation….of Domination….

….Big Nels was one of the founding fathers of this particular Nation, when himself and his then partner Bobby Knight (Mo) joined forces with Kama and Faarooq…shortly after that, Scorpio was recruited, and thus, the Nation of Domination was born….

….the Nation had its success, capturing the WWF Light Heavyweight, European, and Intercontinental Championships, each on two occasions…..unfortunately, they managed to lose all three of those championships (and a member) in one night back at Summerslam….and while purely unintentional, it was Nelson that was at fault for the majority of the Nation’s misery that night, and as such was violently excommunicated from the group with the arrival of D’Lo and Mark Henry……

…..he didn’t stay gone for long however, making his return at Septembers Ground Zero event, and making his intentions crystal clear when he assaulted his former leader Faarooq….since that day, Faarooq (for all his talk) has done everything in his power to avoid crossing paths with his former partner, going so far as to physically run away from Nelson, leaving the rest of the Nation to fend for themselves….there’s no escape tonight however, because the two men will be on opposite sides of the ring…..

….of course, for these two men to finally clash, then Faarooq is going to have to actually tag himself into the match….and he does…..just not anytime Nelson is in there…..and it’s this problem that leads to the first elimination…..because after a brief “mad moment” sees Kama, D’Lo and Henry on the floor courtesy of Bradshaw, Mysterio, and Malenko, Nelson tosses Scorpio into the corner, telling him to tag Faarooq in……Faarooq declines the offer, leaving Scorpio stranded in the corner…..a p!ssed off Nelson crushed the life out of the former Light Heavyweight Champion with an Avalanche, before annihilating him with a near 500lb Big Splash for the one, the two, and the three……

Eliminated: Scorpio

…..time to re-group….and I don’t think the rest of the Nation are particularly pleased with the way Scorpio was eliminated, nor the fact that Faarooq stood by, watched, and did nothing as it happened….but Faarooq is the boss for a reason, and he soon rally’s the troops back into line…..

…..seeing that they are now at a one man disadvantage the strategy seems to be to attempt to isolate one of the two smaller opponents, either Rey Mysterio or Dean Malenko (and as far as Faarooq is concerned, definitely NOT Nelson) problem with that is that both Mysterio and Malenko are very good at what they do….and what they do, is defy the odds against larger opponents…..which is why after D’Lo heads up to the top rope, Rey Mysterio is able to clip his ankles (crotching him on the top rope) and climbs up to join him, before sending Brown flying with a top rope hurricanrana…..and that’s all she wrote for D’Lo Brown….

Eliminated: D’Lo Brown

….I think it’s safe to say that the Nation were not expecting this…..and now they are TWO men down….the only saving grace for Faarooq is that it’s the three biggest members….himself, Kama, and the Worlds Strongest Man, Mark Henry…..and it’s Henry that shows his power, when he catches a Mysterio cross body attempt from the top, before driving him into the mat with what has been dubbed the World’s Strongest Slam….and Rey isn’t getting up from that……

Eliminated: Rey Mysterio

….Bradshaw hits the ring immediately, and almost takes Henry’s head off his shoulders with a clothesline that looked like it came straight from hell, and it was only Faarooq breaking up the pinfall that saved the Nation man…..

…..Faarooq tagged himself in, and he goes at it with Bradshaw, with both men showcasing their power…..you don’t become the leader of a Nation without knowing when brains are more beneficial than brawn however, and on a rope running sequence, Kama slaps his leader on the back (making the blind tag) meaning that Bradshaw was completely unprepared for the running Big Boot that came his way a few moments later….and just like that, this is all square again…..

Eliminated: Bradshaw

…..it’s all even once more…..three men on one side, three on the other…..it could almost be classed as a fair fight…..I say almost…..because after a brief huddle, Faarooq obviously dished out the new game plan….a game plan that saw Mark Henry head to the outside to collect a chair…..

…..Henry gets back into the ring and smashes the steel instrument right between the eyes of Dead Malenko….and yes, pinfall’s, submissions, count-out’s and most importantly, disqualifications all count….which is why Mark Henry is instantly ejected from this match….

…..Jim Ross says that was a pretty stupid move by the Nation, but Rude isn’t so sure…..in fact, after (an already disqualified) Mark Henry then turns and smashes the Bulldog between the eyes with the weapon (knocking him off the apron in the process) and Faarooq lifts Malenko up and slams him into the mat with the Dominator…..just like that we’re all square once more….it’s two a piece, but as Nelson steps over the top rope, Henry again swings that steel chair, smashing it three times into the exposed Knee area of the big man……and while the sides are all equal, and Nelson is down on the mat clutching his knee…….NOW Faarooq is more than happy to step in the ring with the big man…..

Eliminated: Mark Henry (via disqualification)
Eliminated: Dean Malenko

…..and after two months of tactical retreat (that’s running away to you and me) Faarooq has got plenty of pent up aggression to unleash on his former team mate….Nelson might be damn near seven feet tall and close to half a ton, but on one leg all that size is meaningless…in fact, it’s some what of a hinderance, because every time he attempts to mount a comeback his limb buckles under his mammoth weight…..

…..Bulldog is back on the apron after having his bell rung by that chair shot, but he doesn’t look to be anywhere near 100%…..he still shouts words of encouragement to Big Nels, urging the hobbled giant to get over to him and make the tag….which is exactly what he does after a clash of heads puts both Nelson and Faarooq down, leading to both men crawling to their respective corners…..

…..Bulldog is in, Kama is in…..and they are going all out with rights and lefts…..Davey Boy has been somewhat of a forgotten commodity since his split from The Hart Foundation however, and he definitely has something to prove….with so many big names right now in the WWF it can be easy to get lost in the shuffle, a good showing on one of the “Big Four” shows could go a long way to revitalising his career, and a good showing is what he gives…….because as well as dealing with Kama, he manages to fight off Faarooq too, tossing the Nation leader over the top rope to the floor, before impressively scooping up Kama and driving him into the mat with that Running Powerslam……and three seconds later, Faarooq finds himself all alone…..

Eliminated: Kama

……and he’s not happy about that revelation either….the Nation aren’t used to being outnumbered….Faarooq tries to even out the numbers quickly, sliding into the ring and rolling up The Bulldog, but Davey Boy gets his shoulder up at two, so that doesn’t work…..and things go from bad to worse, because as Faarooq looks on in horror, Davey reaches back and tags in Nelson….and while the big man’s legs still aren’t at 100%, he’s certainly recovered enough to pose a sizeable threat to Faarooq’s well being……is Nelson FINALLY going to get that payback he’s been waiting for?……

…..well…..no……

…..oh he gets the win, no doubt about that…..but certainly not the win he was looking for…..because after a quick assessment of the situation (two against one, Nelson back on his feet) Faarooq decides that it would be perhaps a better idea to live to fight another day…..and as Nelson advances slowly towards him he can audibly be heard saying “screw this”, before rolling out of the ring and jogging to the back to join the rest of his defeated team…..the official delivers the mandatory ten count, and this one is all over……Nelson (and Davey Boy) win, but you’ve got to think that the big man is far from pleased about the fact that again he has not managed to get his hands on the slippery Faarooq……maybe one day Nels…..maybe one day…..

Eliminated: Faarooq (via deliberate Count-Out)

Survivor(s): Nelson & The British Bulldog
Rating: B+

We leave Nelson and the Bulldog celebrating their survival (admittedly, Bulldog is the happier of the two) and we head backstage to Dok Hendrix…..Dok is standing outside the locker room that Dok informs us is currently being utilised by Vince McMahon and his Corporate Cannons….Dok says that he is going to attempt to get word from the WWF Owner on his thoughts ahead of the match, as well as the question that the tag team division would like an answer to….who’s got the fourth spot in tonight’s Elimination Fatal Four Way match?……

…..Dok knocks, and it takes a moment but the door is finally opened….by Pat Patterson of all people….we head inside the room with Hendrix, where we see Vince (wearing a huge oversized sweatshirt) getting a crash course in hammerlocks by Gerald Brisco, as Brian Pillman, Arn Anderson, Ric Flair and Kane watch on offering words of encouragement…..

…..Hendrix apologies for the interruption, but asks Vince if he is feeling confident ahead of his first EVER professional wrestling match?…..confident? Of course Vince is confident…..you don’t become a billionaire by lacking confidence in your own abilities…..assuring Hendrix that Steve Austin has no idea of what’s in store for him tonight, when he gets a taste of the Big Mac-Attack…..

…..Dok asks Vince if he’s come to a decision on who will be the fourth team to compete in the Tag Title Match later this evening, but Vince shrugs the question off, saying he doesn’t care right now, he has too much preparation to do to worry about that, whoever wants the spot can have it……

“…..ahhh, Muy Bueno!…..”

…..holy guacamole…..it’s everyone’s favourite Masked Mystery Men, The Conquistadors!…..apparently if McMahon has no preference for who gets the spot in the Elimination Tag Match then they’re throwing their sombrero’s into the mix……

…..McMahon takes one look at them however and shakes his head…..

“…..no, no, no, no……I said I don’t care, I didn’t say I was stupid…..you two morons had to beat Flair and Arn Monday Night if you were going to get into this match…..you didn’t….you lost…..no match for you……get out, and take your stupid head with you!……”

“………Que?……Cabeza?……Heed?…….”

……you have to get up pretty early in the morning to put one over on Mr. McMahon…..clearly he’s seen straight through Al Snow and Chris Candido’s ruse…..after all, this wouldn’t be the first time they’ve switched identities to get into a title bout…..although actually, they have stepped their game up here…..because Head is nowhere to be seen……

“……..GET OUT!…….I have a match to prepare for……”

……with that Kane ushers all three men (Conquistador #1 and #2, along with Dok Hendrix) out of the room, slamming the door behind them……

…..Dok looks at the two Masked men, telling them that they are going to have to come up with something better than that…..although we are still no closer to finding out who the fourth team is going to be…….

“……hey Betty…..where’s the boss man?…..and don’t say WCW……..”

…..oh……maybe those ARE the real Conquistadors then…..because here come Al Snow and Chris Candido now…..and yes, Al does have Head with him…..

…..Dok tells them that he’s just through that door, but now is perhaps not a good time…..Snow isn’t listening though…..he tells Dok that Vince has a problem (a missing team to be precise) and they have the solution…….them…….before walking up to the door and swinging it right open…….

“…….Vince!……how ya doing……have we got an idea for y……”

“……..I SAID GET OUTTTT!…….”

…..with that there’s an almighty crash, as the door is slammed shut once again, and Snow comes wandering back into shot……

“……rude……what crawled up his butt……”

……Dok says that he did warn them, Candido just shakes his head, saying he knew this wouldn’t work, before telling Snow to come on…..there’s more than one way to rustle an Outlaw’s feathers….and walking off……Al looks at the Conquistadors for a moment, before turning to follow his partner……

“……nice masks……”

…..who’s going to be joining Harlem Heat and the Dogs of War in challenging the New Age Outlaws this evening?…….apparently not Al Snow and Chris Candido…..or the Conquistadors…..now lets go to Michael Cole, who’s with the Worlds Most Dangerous Team…..

Rating: A

……that being Ken Shamrock, Matt Hardy, Billy Kidman, and newest recruit…..Psycho Sid…..

….this is really just your basic pre-match promo….Shamrock says that he’s been waiting for another shot at Jeff Jarrett and that European Title…..he says he’s been his own worst enemy thus far here in the WWF, and could have captured the Championship on a number of occasions by now, had he not let his temper get the better of him…..well Shamrock says that he’s finally tempered that temper…..he’s calm….he’s collected…..he’s focused…..and when he’s done with the Million Dollar Corporation tonight, he’s coming for Jeff Jarrett and the European Championship next……

…..Hardy echoes those title aspirations, saying that he’s agreed to step aside for now, to give Shamrock the next shot at the gold…..BUT…..if Ken should let his emotions get the better of him again…..should Ken NOT manage to take the title from Jarrett, then Matt Hardy will be right there waiting to take back his Championship…..

…..did someone say Championships? Billy Kidman wouldn’t mind a shot at that opportunity too…..he says he’s worn gold here before in the WWF, he’s a former Light Heavyweight Champion……but since that title is no longer in existence, then the European Title seems the next logical step to him……

…..logical? Yeah right……then there’s this guy…..Psycho Sid…..who’s never done anything logical a day in his life……does he want a shot at Jarrett and his European Championship? Sure, why not……but what he REALLY wants…..is to make Rich Steiner suffer……to make Rich Steiner and ALL the Corporation pay for what they did to him a few weeks ago……and tonight, he’s going to Powerbomb each and every one of them…….

…..Shamrock rounds the interview off….saying that tonight, the Million Dollar Corporation are going to find out that money doesn’t make the man…..its heart and integrity…..and when it comes to THAT…..the Corporations account is empty.

Rating: B+

Survivor Series Match

Ken Shamrock, Matt Hardy, Billy Kidman & Psycho Sid

vs. The Million Dollar Corporation

That match is up next, as the owner of one of the most in-demand titles in the WWF makes his way out….that’s Jeff Jarrett (along with the rest of the Million Dollar Corporation)….with the European Championship wrapped firmly around his waist…..

…..the European Title is not even a year old, but already it’s made an impact here in the World Wrestling Federation, being held by such names as the British Bulldog, The Rock and Sean Waltman, while young Matt Hardy used the Title to showcase his singles ability as he attempted to distance himself from the tag team wrestler that the fans were accustomed with…add to that the fact that Ken Shamrock has been highly vocal about his desire to hold the gold (and now Billy Kidman throwing his name in the mix) and what you have is a title that everybody wants……perhaps being the one to eliminate the champion here tonight will go a long way to securing a shot for one of these men……

…..interestingly enough, and while many (including myself) may find it amusing that any team featuring Matt Hardy and Billy Kidman might be called the “Worlds Most Dangerous” anything, of the four men in said group, it’s the two smallest men that have had the most Championship success…..Matt Hardy being a former European Champion, while Billy Kidman is a former holder of the now retired Light Heavyweight Title…..as for the two heavy hitters, Sid and Shamrock, not a WWF belt has been held between them……maybe it’s Kidman and Hardy after all that make this team so dangerous…..

…..ok, that’s a stretch…..clearly if you’re looking for danger you look to the celebrated shoot fighter or the seven foot lunatic…..that’s what the Corporation did too…..and against all odds it was that seven foot lunatic that was the first to go, and after costing him the WWF European Championship a few weeks ago on Raw is War, it was Rich Steiner that once again cost Psycho Sid here, reaching under the bottom rope as the big man attempted to Powerbomb Jeff Jarrett…..Sid lost his footing and fell backwards…..Double J landed ass first on his chest…..hooked the leg…..and with Steiner hanging onto the free leg from the outside, Sid had no chance of kicking out……

Eliminated: Psycho Sid

……Sid is NOT happy about the way he was eliminated here….not at all….and the moment he’s informed that he’s out of here he throws a Ken Shamrock sized tantrum, rolling to the outside where an oblivious Rich Steiner is making the universal “rub your fingers together” money sign…..he should have paid more attention…..because Sid spins him around, wraps his big hand around his throat…..and Chokeslams him straight through the Spanish Announce Table, leaving Steiner down and broken amongst the debris of the desk…..and then (and only then) does the Psycho one leave……

…..well, the Million Dollar Corporation TECHNICALLY have the one man advantage, but Rich Steiner is good for nothing about now, and the WMDT (Worlds Most Dangerous Team don’tcha know) tries to capitalise on the shocked Jarrett, with Billy Kidman rolling him up quickly from behind…..luckily Canterbury is there to break up the pin attempt, and Jarrett hastily tags out to the former Hog Farmer…..

…..Kidman most definitely has the speed advantage here, and he runs rings around Canterbury, catching him with a dropkick to the chin that rocks the man, followed by a second drop kick to the Articulatio Genus as Gorilla Monsoon may call it (that’s the knee to you and me)……it drops Canterbury to one knee too…..and Kidman makes a quick tag to Matt Hardy, who keeps up the rapid fire offence…..Canterbury is clearly dazed, and Hardy knows it, tagging back out to Kidman, who heads up top……Billy let’s fly with a crossbody from the top, but old Hank is deceptively big and very deceptively strong…..he catches Kidman in mid air, holding him in almost a Powerslam position for a moment, before releasing the youngsters legs (but keeping hold of his head) and drives him to the mat with what WAS called the Slop Drop at one point (I have no idea what he called it when with Southern Justice)…..and three seconds later the WMDT are down to two…..

Eliminated: Billy Kidman

…..Hardy and Shamrock both look to get into the match, but Hardy is the quicker of the two (to Shammy’s slight annoyance) and he continues his earlier assault of Canterbury….Hardy has really come on leaps and bounds when you consider that last year he was out here alongside his brother, barely out of jobber status, face painted like a little Legion of Doomer, firmly under the wing of Hawk and Animal…..now look at him, firmly established midcarder with a very impressive European Title reign under his belt….granted it wasn’t the longest reign in the world, but he fit as many title defences in that few month period as SOME champions do in a year (I’m looking at you Hollywood Hogan)…..he keeps up that good display here too, and manages to take on both members of Southern Justice single handedly…..he whips Knight into the corner, then sidesteps a charging Canterbury, leaving the former H.O.G to crash into the former P.I.G….and with Dennis Knight slumped in the corner, Canterbury staggers backward and collapses to the mat, eventually pulling himself up to all fours…..which makes him the perfect springboard for a Matt Hardy Poetry in Motion on Knight, before dropping Canterbury again with the Twist of Fate……which probably would have been enough for a pinfall……had Jeff Jarrett not stormed the ring behind him and driven his face into the mat with what looked to be a Forward Russian Legsweep Facebuster, before rolling Canterbury on top of him and knocking Shamrock flying off the apron as the referee counts the one, two, three……

Eliminated: Matt Hardy

…..oh dear, things don’t look good for Ken Shamrock right now….he might be the Worlds Most Dangerous Man, but even he has a limit to the number of men he can take on alone surely…..

…..not if you ask Ken….(and don’t call me Shirley)….he tears into the ring and charges straight towards Jarrett, who quickly dives off the apron to safety…..Shammy’s hotheadedness may end up costing him here, because with his focus solely set on Double J, Dennis Knight is able to blindside him from behind…..the attack is brief however, because Shamrock blocks a strike, takes Knight down to the mat…..and hooks on that Ankle Lock…..and in about five seconds Dennis Knight is furiously tapping in submission…..

Eliminated: Dennis Knight

……learning from his past mistakes Shamrock quickly releases the hold and stands to his feet…..just in time to see Mark Canterbury charging towards him…..Shammy sidesteps the charging opponent (who crashes into the turnbuckles chest first and staggers backwards)…..Shamrock is waiting with a release German Suplex and then instantly hooks on the Ankle Lock…..and in the space of about a minute, Ken Shamrock has eliminated BOTH members of Southern Justice…..

Eliminated: Mark Canterbury

…..Jeff Jarrett doesn’t look particularly pleased about the prospect of facing Ken Shamrock….but with Southern Justice gone and Rick Steiner still one big splinter on the floor, he doesn’t have much choice…..Shamrock on the other hand is practically chomping at the bit at the chance to finally get his hands on Double J….after all, he makes the Champ tap here and he’s practically guaranteed a shot at the European Title….

….the two men have a pretty decent back and forth period (although Shamrock does get the better of it) as they each try to pick up an elimination….neither man is going down without a fight however, and it finally takes a hurricanranna from the former Ultimate Fighter to put Jarrett down…..and once he is down, Shammy wastes no time in slapping on the Ankle Lock…..

…..Jarrett is tapping furiously, and this one should rightfully be over…..but the Million Dollar Corporation always has a little bit left in the reserve, and its Ted DiBiase up on the apron (distracting the attention of the official) that saves Jarrett here……well, Ted DiBiase and Rich Steiner…..because with his focus solely on the European Champion, Steiner wearily rolls into the ring and nails Shamrock in the back of the head with the object of his desire, the WWF European Title…..

……Ken goes down like a sack of you know what, while Jarrett drapes himself over the unconscious star, as the referee registers the one, the two, and the three……and just like that Jeff Jarrett and (despite barely featuring in the match) Rich Steiner survive……that’s got to put a dent in Shamrocks Championship aspirations.

Survivor(s): Jeff Jarrett & Rich Steiner
Rating: B

We go to the back once more, and once again Dok Hendrix is attempting to get clarification on who exactly is going to be the fourth team in the WWF Tag Team Championship match…..

…..Vince has other things on his hands however…..mainly Gerald Briscoe’s head….as Pat Patterson goes through the basics of a Standing Side-Headlock, while a high spirited Brian Pillman and Ric Flair cheer him on…..

…..Dok apologises for interrupting, but says that they really need a decision ahead of the Tag Title encounter on who the final team will be……Vince screams at Dok that he already told him that he doesn’t care who it is, and to get the hell out of here…..

……that brings Goldust into the room….along with Marlena and Marcus….but this is a more subdued Goldust that we are used to…..there is no flourish, no flash….no movie quotes or suggestive Double Entendres….in fact, save for the face-paint and golden robe, this could almost be Dustin Rhodes…..

…..Goldust(in) apologises for the intrusion, but says that he couldn’t help overhearing the conversation, and if McMahon really has no interest in who should fill that spot (and I’m sure a few weeks ago there would have been a crude innuendo to “filling the gap”…..filling it with penis….says how much Raven’s words affected the man) then perhaps he and Marcus would be an option……

…..Vince releases the headlock for a moment to look at the new arrivals, before breaking out that big Yuk-Yuk laugh of his……before stopping when he realises that Goldust and Marcus aren’t laughing with him…..

“…..oh…..you were serious?…..well goddamn that’s even funnier…..”

…..with that, Vince breaks out into that laughter once more….with gusto this time, and everyone else (with the exception of Goldust, Marcus, and Marlena) joins in….even Dok gives a subtle little chuckle….before Vince says no….god no…..he says that Goldust and his little band of miscreants serve their purpose sure….they’ve got the controversy, the hot girl….they serve to cover certain…demographics….but there’s a reason that Goldust hasn’t had so much of a sniff at a title opportunity in almost two years, and that reason is that quite frankly, Mr. McMahon doesn’t want his Championships tarnished by being around the waist of this travelling hedonism act…..ESPECIALLY when the Champions he has, already have everything that Goldie has, and more…….they’ve got charisma…..they’ve got star quality…..they’ve got a really…really……….REALLY hot manager…..why would Vince want to downgrade from that?….so no……Goldust and Marcus will NOT be participating in the Tag Title Match later this evening…..before ordering Dok Hendrix (and a very dejected looking MGM) to get the hell out of there…..before he has them thrown out of there…..

Rating: B+

From there we go a little bit old-school, and get a flashback of Survivor Series’ of years gone by, as we go to two sets of pre-match promo’s from the Hart Foundation team and The Wolfpac team, both standing in front of the Survivor Series backdrop……

….they’re not long, just a couple of minutes each, but they both say pretty much the same thing…..it’s time to put this feud to bed and move on to the WWF Championship……

….Nash says that they’ve been playing with the Harts for too damn long now….it was fun at first, but now it’s just getting boring….all the crying, all the b!tching, all the “I got screwed” talk, it ends tonight…..and it ends with Big Sexy Jackknifing each and every Hart Member straight through the mat…..Hall and Waltman echo that…..saying that the Bad Guy has Intercontinental Gold, it’s time for the big man and the kid to get some gold too…..that WWF Title will look pretty sweet around Big Sexy’s waist…..and the Kid…..well, he’s been European Champion before…..maybe it’s time he took that title again……

……Scott Steiner is surprisingly quiet throughout, standing in the background flexing his muscles….but he certainly doesn’t look like he cares one way or another about ANY of the Wolfpac picking up gold…..his only contribution to this whole promo?…..is to agree with Nash…..the next World Wrestling Federation Champion IS standing right here…..but it’s gonna be Big Poppa Pump Scott Steiner……

……The Hart Foundation are slightly more unified than their counterparts here…..oh, they’re just as ready to put this whole feud behind them as the Wolfpac are….but the difference is, while THE Wolfpac seem unified in Kevin Nash’s goal to once again become the WWF Champion, TEAM “Wolfpac” certainly are not….because Scott Steiner also has his eye on the prize…..the WHOLE Hart Foundation however says they are fully focused on Bret Hart becoming the World Wrestling Federation Champion for the fourth time……and tonight, that Canadian Unity is going to be more than enough to defeat this Divided States of America team….

Rating: A*

Survivor Series Match

The Wolfpac & Scott Steiner vs. The Hart Foundation

So here we are…..(hopefully) at the culmination of a feud that began way back in March of 1996, when Kevin Nash and Scott Hall cost Bret “The Hitman” Hart the WWF Championship at Wrestlemania XII…..almost 20 months have past since then, but the bad blood is no less heated now than it was then…..

…..in that time, the Wolfpac have cost Bret Hart the WWF Championship (along with costing him a number of Championship opportunities), cost Owen Hart his opportunity at becoming the WWF Champion, beat down Chris Jericho following HIS unsuccessful attempt to become the WWF Champion….taken part in the Screwjob finish to the match that prevented the Harts from challenging “The KLiQ” for gold at last years Survivor Series, and most recently, taken the Intercontinental Championship from Owen Hart…….

…..that’s not to say that the Harts haven’t had their success…..they…..uhm……well Bret once….erm……Owen was victorious when……ahem…….oh wait, Bret and Owen defeated Scott Hall and Sean Waltman to win the WWF Tag Team Titles for a little bit……so yeah…..to say that Hall, Nash and Waltman have gotten the better of this feud to date would be an understatement……

……all of that means that the Canadians are certainly looking to put the final nail in the Wolfpac/Hart War…..although from the way this match started out, it didn’t look as though they were going to get it, as Hall (on Steiner) Nash (Bret) Waltman (Jericho) and Steiner (with Test) go on an absolute tear, sending all four Hart Foundation members scattering to the outside…..

…..the Hart’s do manage to get back into the fray, and they do the smart thing in isolating the smallest member of Team Wolfpac, and when Test almost takes his head clean off his shoulders with a Running Big Boot, it’s Sean Waltman that tastes the first taste of defeat here tonight…….

Eliminated: Sean Waltman

As McMahon celebrates pinning Stone Cold Steve Austin, Brian Pillman celebrates becoming the number one contender, and Kane celebrates……well….he he’s not really the celebrating type, but I’m sure he enjoyed slamming Austin through that table…..we go to a highlight/recap package ahead of our main event, reminding us of of the events that bought us to our next match…….it’s Ahmed Johnson vs. The Undertaker…..it’s for the World Wrestling Federation Championship……and it’s next…..

Rating: A

WWF Championship Match

Ahmed Johnson (c) vs. The Undertaker


…..so what are the events that bought us to tonight’s match? They actually start around eight months ago….at Wrestlemania XIII to be precise….because that was the night that Ahmed Johnson turned his back on the WWF fans, but more importantly, that was the night that Ahmed Johnson joined forces with Jim Cornette, costing the Undertaker what was a pretty impressive Wrestlemania winning streak when he interfered in the Deadman’s match with Kane….

……the Undertakers answer to that, was to come back one month later at In Your House, costing Johnson his chance at becoming the European Champion against the (then) title holder The British Bulldog….

……fast forward from there another month down the line, and the two men finally met inside the ring…..in Tag Team action…..when the Undertaker teamed with former foe Cactus Jack to face the awkward combination of Ahmed Johnson and Goldust…..it was Ahmed Johnson and the Bizarre One that came out victorious that night, but that didn’t stop the Undertaker from brutally assaulting his nemesis after the match…..

…..that takes us to the King of the Ring….and finally these two men met in singles competition…..in the finals of the King of the Ring tournament no less……but there was more at stake than just the Crown that night, because the winner of the match would be headlining the Summerslam Pay Per View, Challenging for the WWF Title…..once again Ahmed Johnson came out victorious, but not without controversy, because Johnson used his managers ever present tennis racquet to crush the larynx of the Deadman, not only winning the match, but putting the seemingly unstoppable Undertaker on the shelf at the same time…..

…..we all know what happened from there…..Ahmed Johnson did indeed go on to Summerslam, where he defeated Big Poppa Pump Scott Steiner, making history as he became the first African American WWF Champion in history, and he’s been seemingly unstoppable ever since, with manager Jim Cornette christening him “The Walking Armageddon”……Steve Austin, Bret Hart, Kevin Nash….no one could take the title from around the waist of the big man, and just last month, Ahmed defied the odds to defeat both Hart and Nash in the same match, and that was after fighting to PUT Kevin Nash into the match….there aren’t many wrestlers who would be actively lobbying for extra challengers…..of course, that victory bought something else…..it bought the Gong……because Ahmed Johnson barely had time to celebrate, before the Undertaker made his grand return after four months away……and here we are…..

…..ok, all caught up?….then let’s get on with it shall we…..

…..honestly, I was really worried about this match main eventing here tonight, I mean, Ahmed Johnson has been in some great matches since he’s held the World Title, but his last defence didn’t go so well, granted he picked up an injury midway through the match, but that (ongoing) injury coupled with the fact that Ahmed isn’t the best of workers at the best of times made me slightly nervous……turns out I shouldn’t have worried, because this was easily the best match of the night, and quite honestly NEITHER man disappointed…..The Undertaker carried the workload don’t get me wrong (with an A* performance) but considering the fact that he IS carrying an injury, Ahmed didn’t do too shabby either (B+ performance from the champ)….

…..the two men clashed, in what the announcers played up as a “must win” match for the Undertaker (considering the fact that Ahmed has defeated him at every opportunity to date)…this was never going to be a mat based scientific scuffle now was it….what it was, was a knock down, drag out fight, with the two men trading blows from the onset….Taker gets the better of the initial brawl, but Ahmed catches a breather with a whip to the corner, followed almost immediately with a big clothesline…..Ahmed decides to repeat the sequence, sending his opponent across the ring to the opposite corner, but this time The Deadman gets his boot up on a charging Ahmed, then levels him with a clothesline of his own…..

…..the back and forth continues, with neither man really gaining the advantage, until Ahmed sends the Undertaker over the top rope with a clothesline…..Taker lands on his feet however before reaching under the bottom rope to take hold of Ahmed’s ankles and yanking him out to the floor….The Deadman drives Johnson kidney first into the ring apron, then delivers a series of jabs to the ribs of the Champion….Ahmed fires back with blows of his own, but Taker finally blocks that and bounces Johnson’s head off the ring steps, before rolling him back into the ring…..

…..back inside the ring and the Undertaker looks to finish this off as he goes for the tombstone, it’s too early though, and Ahmed is able to wiggle out, landing on the mat behind the Deadman, before hitting a chop block to the back of the leg, knocking the Challenger down to one knee, then hitting a big boot to the back of the head…..

…..Ahmed takes the opportunity to pose for the crowd (who break out in a “rest in peace” chant) before sauntering around the ring, taunting the Undertaker to get back up to his feet….the Deadman does indeed drag himself back to a standing position, but that boot to the back of the head really rattled his brains, and a stiff right hand from Ahmed puts him straight back down again….

….Ahmed lands a number of stomps on his opponent before whipping him into the corner again and delivering a series of clubbing forearm blows, the final one of which the Undertaker blocks, before applying the arm wringer and heading up top for what today would be called “Old School”…..this is 1997 though so let’s just call it “Middle School” for now…..before he can leap off however, Ahmed just yanks his arm (don’t ask me why more people didn’t do this simple counter, because I don’t know) and the Deadman crashes down to the mat once more…..

….Ahmed is really punishing the Deadman now, and Rick Rude proclaims that the Undertaker should have stopped back at the graveyard, because clearly he can’t defeat Ahmed Johnson….never has, never will….Ross and Lawler aren’t so sure however, proclaiming that the Undertaker has overcome every monster to step foot in the WWF, and he’s not finished just yet…..

…..Taker begins to fight back, and the two men go back to trading blows, but the Deadman has received the majority of the punishment here tonight, and as the two run the ropes, Ahmed connects with a running axhandle shot right between the eyes that staggers the Deadman, and Ahmed capitalises (and catches a breather) by locking on a bearhug….

…..it takes a while, but the Undertaker finally breaks free, only for Ahmed to grab hold of a side headlock, which the Undertaker manages to reverse into a back suplex….remarkably the Deadman is up first…..he drops an elbow to keep the man down, then heads up to the top rope, and lets loose with an impressive looking flying clothesline……

…..Taker is slow to capitalise, finally going for the cover, but he only gets two…..the Deadman staggers back to his feet and runs a thumb across his throat….it’s Tombstone time…..

……the Deadman drags the WWF Champion to his feet, and lifts him up, positioning him for the finishing blow…..he’s taken a hell of a beating though, and because of this Ahmed once again manages to wriggle free, landing on his feet behind the Deadman, before spinning the Undertaker around, kicking him in the gut, before driving him into the mat with the Pearl River Plunge……Ahmed is too spent to make the cover though, and instead of going for the pin he lays back, spent on the mat…..

…..finally the Champion pulls himself backwards and gets to his feet with the use of the ropes….he looks down at the challenger and smiles, because the Undertaker is done…..

…..and that’s when the Deadman sits up…..

…..Ahmed looks on in shock as the Undertaker gets back to a seated position, and he advances on the challenger, swinging right hands…..Taker finally blacks the blows and wraps his hand around the throats of Johnson…..Ahmed swings wildly and finally pummels his way free……until the Deadman locks his hand back around the Champions throat…..again Ahmed fires right hands, and again it’s broken……Undertaker goes for it again, determined to hit with this Chokeslam, but Ahmed swipes the arm away and hits a back suplex out of nowhere…..unfortunately for him, Taker sits straight up, barely phased….

…..Ahmed looks completely shocked, but it doesn’t stop him from charging in with a couple shots……Taker fires right back, and the two men go toe to toe with a series of punches……the Deadman goes for an Irish Whip, which Ahmed reverses…..Taker ducks a clothesline on the return, runs the ropes again and hits the high jumping clothesline……

…..once more the Deadman wraps his hand around the throat of Ahmed….and this time he DOES connect with the Chokeslam…..that just leaves one more thing for him to do…..he drags Ahmed back to his feet……

…..TOMBSTONE…..

…..The Deadman crosses the arms as the official drops down to count the cover……

…..One….Two…

…..Three….

Winner: …and New WWF Champion…..The Undertaker
Rating: A

…..the lights dim as the new WWF Champion is handed his title belt….and as Ahmed Johnson rolls from the ring and disappears to the back, the Undertaker drops down to one knee to salute this Philadelphia crowd…..

…..Jim Ross says that revenge was a dish best served cold, and tonight, the Undertaker finally settled a score with the man who put him on the injured list for four months…..he says that the age of the Walking Armageddon has come to a screeching halt…..and the age of the Deadman is just beginning…..see you tomorrow night folks…..
