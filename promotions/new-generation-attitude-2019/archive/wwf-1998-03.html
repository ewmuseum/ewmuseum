WWF Raw is War: Week 1, March 1998

Posted 31 October 2020

Week 1, March 1998
Worcester Centrum (Worcester, Massachusetts)
ATT: 13,334
TV: 38.16

…..it’s Monday night…..Raw is War…..and we truly are on the march to Wrestlemania…..because it IS March…..which means that the biggest show of the year is a mere four weeks away and we all know that the chairman of the board is NOT happy about the main event that he has scheduled, where is chosen champion…..his CORPORATE Champion…..the WWF Champion Brian Pillman, has to put the gold on the line against not ONE opponent, but two…..because at Wrestlemania, it’s Brian Pillman vs. Bret “The Hitman” Hart…..vs. Stone Cold Steve Austin…..in the first ever three way Wrestlemania main event…..

…..in fact, Vince McMahon is SO unhappy about this match, that he specifically ordered Ric Flair and Arn Anderson to take Steve Austin out of commission last week…..OR ELSE…..

…..that ”or else” might be about to be revealed, because as we open the show, the chairman is making his way out and he has both Ric Flair and Arn Anderson (the WWF Tag Team Champions) with him, as well as the maniacal Mankind…..

…..although someone who’s NOT with him, is the WWF Champion Brian Pillman…..in fact, The Corporate Cannon hasn’t actually been seen since he stormed off in a temper tantrum last week…..

…..Vince tells the crowd to quiet down a little bit (which they don’t) before informing them that he needs to address a number of issues…..a few “in house” problems…..

”…..so if you inbred hicks could keep it down a minute, we can get to it…..”

…..Vince says that clearly Brian Pillman isn’t here this evening, and he isn’t here for one important reason…..Ric Flair, Arn Anderson and yes, even Mankind, they let the Corporate Champion down…..

”…..you see, there was ONE thing that we wanted done…..just one…..and that was make sure that Stone Cold Steve Austin (crowd pops) did NOT make it to that Wrestlemania main event…..ONE THING…..”

…..Vince says that Ric Flair didn’t have to win last week…..frankly that was the least of McMahon’s concerns…..but between the three of them, Steve Austin should be sitting in a hospital bed right now, but because of their failure, he’s sitting right there backstage lacing up his black boots…..

…..Flair stops McMahon right there, telling him that first of all, Ric Flair never failed at anything a day in his life…..

”…..just by waking up in the morning, opening my eyes and realising that I’m the Nature Boy (Woooooooo) Ric Flair, I’m already winning…..the Rolex on my wrist, the women on my arms and the gold around my waist…..I’ve had more success in the time it takes me to put on my $6000 custom suit, than any of these idiots in the crowd have had in their entire miserable lives…..yeah, I’m talking about you fat boy, sit down before you have a heart attack…..”

…..Flair says that the ONLY reason that Steve Austin is still walking around breathing the same air as Ric Flair, is because of the Undertaker and Kane…..

”…..ah hell son, I’d say that the only reason Steve Austin is still walking around breathing the same air as Ric Flair, is because Steve Austin is allowing Ric Flair’s old ass to breathe…..”

…..that’ll be the Rattlesnake…..he’s not making his way out though, because the Titantron does its thing and flickers to life, showing a camera man (from the camera’s perspective obviously) being dragged across a room, as Steve Austin walks behind what looks suspiciously like Vince McMahons desk, sits back, puts his feet up and tells Mr. McMahon that he’s a stupid son of a bitch…..

”…..you go out there saying Ric Flair didn’t do this, Arn Anderson didn’t do that, Mankind didn’t do squat…..hell son, if you thought those three sorry sons of bitches were going to do a damn thing to Steve Austin then you’re even stupider than you look…..and you look pretty stupid…..”

…..McMahon isn’t happy about the interruption and he’s even less happy that Austin is in his office, telling him to get his damn dirty boots off his desk…..

”…..oh I’ll get my little boots off your desk, Vince…..why don’t you come back here and I’ll take em off your desk right now and use them to stomp a mud hole in your ass…..”

…..Austin shuffles around some papers on the desk, before picking up what looks to be a match listing for the evening, before saying that he’s going to make his real easy for Vince…..since he wants his stooges to take him out so badly…..

”…..and since Mankind done pissed me off last week, I got your main event right here…..”

…..Austin grabs a pen and writes “Stone Cold Steve Austin vs. Mankind” in big letters across the top of the sheet…..

”…..Vince, imma say it slow, so that even your sorry ass can understand it…..you’re looking at the next World Wrestling Federation Champion son…..ain’t Brian Pillman going to stop me, ain’t Bret Hart going to stop me, ain’t none of those three jackasses standing behind you going to stop me…..but what I’m fixing to do in the meantime, is take out the trash and ain’t no bigger piece of trash going that that sorry sack of crap in a mask right there…..”

…..Austin says that in about an hour and a half he’s going to be wiping Mankind’s face off the bottom of his boot, but while he’s here, he may as well find something for the senior citizens to do too…..

…..Stone Cold looks down the sheet in front of him, venomously crossing SOMETHING out, before writing over the top of it “Jackass Ric Flair & Old Man River Arn Anderson vs. The Undertaker & Kane”, holding up the sheet of paper and telling Vince McMahon that he just made his show better with a swipe of the pen…..

”…..just like I’m going to make the whole damn company better at Wrestlemania, when I Stun Bret Hart, Stun Brian Pillman, and walk out the brand new WWF Champion…..and that’s the bottom line…..because Stone Cold said so…..”

Rating: A*

…..apparently Stone Cold has booked the WWF Tag Team Champions in a match…..while I’m sure Vince McMahon will probably over ride that tag team match, he’s not over riding this one…..

…..”this one” being Bradshaw and Faarooq facing off against The Road Dogg and Billy Gunn (with Sunny in their corner) the New Age Outlaws…..

…..Faarooq and Bradshaw have been on a bit of a roll as of late, including picking up a victory over the Nation of Domination at No Way Out (a victory that actually cost Scorpio his place in the Nation) while Gunn and James on the other hand have been on a roll of their own…..a roll of constantly demanding a shot at the Tag Team Titles…..a challenge was made last week by the Outlaws, Al Snow & Chris Candido AND Dustin Rhodes and Marc Mero, for the current champions (Flair and Arn) to put the gold on the line at Wrestlemania, against all three teams in a fatal four way match…..that challenge hasn’t actually been answered yet, but a win here would surely set the Outlaws in good standing for a future opportunity…..

…..it wasn’t going to be an easy victory though…..Bradshaw and Faarooq are two big, powerful (and angry) men, who have a clash with the Nation of Domination to contend with at Wrestlemania…..

…..which is maybe why the Nation interfered here, with D’Lo Brown and Mark Henry causing enough of a distraction that Billy Gunn was able to drop Bradshaw with the Famouser to pick up a cheap victory…..

Winner(s): The New Age Outlaws
Rating: B

…..from there we go to the back (to a decisively ‘Stone Cold Free’ office) where Mr. McMahon is still in a foul mood…..Flair and Arn are scoffing at the fact that Steve Austin thinks he can book the matches around here, stating that they obviously aren’t going bow down to whatever the Rattlesnake says……they aren’t going to be facing Kane and The Undertaker tonight, just like Mankind isn’t going to be facing Austin…..right?…..because between those idiots trying to book themselves into a four way tag title match at Mania, and then Austin tonight, that sounds like a lot of people telling Vincent Kennedy McMahon how to do his job…..

…..oh look…..it’s four of those “idiots” trying to book themselves into a tag title match at Mania…..as Al Snow, Chris Candido, Dustin Rhodes and Marc Mero (with Terri) come bursting into the room, each team jostling for position to be the team in front…..

…..the two teams (along with Flair and Anderson) begin shouting over each other as they try to argue their cases for (and against) making that four way match happen…..

…..until Vince McMahon finally slams his hands down on the desk…..

”…..ENOUGH!!…..ENOUGH, THATS IT!!…..”

…..Vince tells Flair and Arn that they’re right…..he HAS had enough of people dictating their matches around here, just like he’s had enough of THEM telling him which matches he should book…..

”…..so this is what is going to happen…..at Wrestlemania (since you let me down last week) it WILL be Ric Flair and Arn Anderson, defending the WWF Tag Team Titles…..against the New Age Outlaws…..

…..and Dustin Rhodes and Marc Mero…..

…..AND (sigh)……Al Snow and Chris Candido…..in a Fatal Four Way Match…..”

…..Flair and Anderson aren’t happy about that, loudly protesting until Vince tells them to be quiet because he isn’t finished…..because yes, he’s going to book that match…..but it’s not coming without a cost….. cause while the team that wins will become the WWF Tag Team Champions, the team that takes the loss (in this one fall to a finish match) that team will have to disband…..

”…..and as for tonight (and quite frankly, just because clearly some people need reminding exactly who the boss is around here) that match with the Undertaker and Kane……WILL be taking place…..”

…..Vince says that they can consider that further punishment for their failure last week, just like Mankind WILL be facing Steve Austin…..and as further incentive, should Steve Austin win that match, then at Wrestlemania it will be Mankind, going one on one with the Big Red Monster…..Kane…..

…..Snow finds that hilarious, but McMahon tells him to shut up, saying that since the New Age Outlaws competed this evening, both Snow & Candido and Rhodes & Mero will be in action next week…..

”…..now all of you, get out…..while I try and work out where the hell my WWF Champion is…..”

Rating: A

…..we leave Vince dialling a number on the phone (clearly trying to get hold of the elusive Brian Pillman) and head over to Dok Hendrix, who’s standing by with the Nation of Domination…..

…..Dok says that the Nation have a busy night upcoming at Wrestlemania, with Stevie Ray Challenging his brother Booker T for the WWF European Championship, while Ahmed, Kama, D’Lo and Henry have an eight man match booked against Faarooq, Bradshaw, Scorpio and Nelson…..the night could go very well…..or very, very badly…..

…..Stevie Ray cuts Dok off, telling him that first things first…..

”…..Booker T ain’t my Brother sucka…..THESE are my brothers right here…..Booker, he’s just some Fruit Booty that’s got something I want…..and when I want something, I TAKE it…..by any means necessary, you dig that Dok Hendrix?…..”

…..Ahmed agrees, telling Hendrix that there IS no “bad night” for the Nation…..Stevie will be bringing the gold home, while the rest of the Nation crush their three former members (AND that fat Texan Bradshaw) under their boots like the bugs that they are…..

…..looks like those bugs are here…..and Faarooq and Bradshaw clearly aren’t happy about the Nation costing them their match earlier, as they join Scorpio and Nelson in a little backstage attack…..

…..the Nation SHOULD have the advantage given their superior numbers, but when you factor in that they were unprepared for the assault, Nelson is the size of two men (maybe three) and the fact that Nelson crushes D,Lo against the wall with his opening blow, it’s actually pretty even as the two sides brawl all over the backstage set…..

…..send in security, we have a match to get down to…..

Rating: B+

…..a match which will see Marty Jannetty face off against former WWF European Champion, Jeff Jarrett…..

…..I’d like to say that this match was filled with hard fought, back and forth competitive action, but in reality (while Marty did have SOME offence) it was Double J that took the majority of the match…..

…..Marty hasn’t done a great deal of anything (other than a few high profile matches with Shawn Michaels) since his return from rehab…..he DOES have a job though…..and that job is to DO the job…..counting the lights here tonight after Jarrett drove his skull into the mat with a Facebuster for the one, two, three…..

Winner: Jeff Jarrett
Rating: B-

…..but the fun doesn’t stop there, as Rich Steiner makes his way out from the back, with a pair of scissors in his hand…..

…..last week Jeff Jarrett vowed to give Sean Waltman a makeover for costing him the European Championship (seeing how Waltman had nothing Jarrett deemed worth taking) and managed to clip a few locks of hair from the head of the Wolfpac man…..tonight it looks as though they’re planning on giving Marty Jannetty the same treatment…..

…..but not until they’ve had a little fun with him…..

…..Jarrett and Steiner join forces to beat and stomp on the former Rocker (as DiBiase cackles on in the background) with Rich finally driving him hard into the canvas with a Death Valley Driver…..

…..Steiner sits Marty up into a seated position and grabs a handful of hair, as Deebs passes Double J the scissors…..after looking (and dressing) exactly the same for the last ten years, it would appear as though Mr. Jannetty is about to have a haircut…..

…..or not…..

…..looks like Marty’s eighties style will live to see another day, because before Jarrett can snip, the wolves howl as Scott Hall and Sean Waltman come rushing out from the back…..

…..the red and black duo quickly send the Million Dollar Duo bailing from the ring and to the back, before Waltman pulls off that neckbrace that he’s been wearing ever since the Corporation beat him down a few weeks back…..clearly he feels like he doesn’t need it anymore…..

…..let’s go to commercial…..

Rating: B+

…..and when we return, Scott Hall and Sean Waltman are STILL in the ring…..except now they have microphones in hand…..

…..Waltman briefly mocks Double J, telling him that while the Million Dollar Corporation thought they were taking out the runt in the litter, Jeff Jarrett is about to find out that this Wolf is going to come back to bite him in the ass…..

…..Hall takes the microphone next, telling the crowd that last week Kevin Nash was barred from the building…..

”…..but this week, the Big Bad Wolf is back…..and since Little Scotty Steiner hasn’t answered the challenge yet, it looks like the Big Mang is gonna have to make that call himself…..”

…..with that, Hall and Waltman do the double armed point towards the Titantron, which flickers to life, to reveal a sweet ass ride pulling into the parking lot…..a sweet ass ride with a licence plate that reads “BIG SXY”…..

…..with this fine figure of a man sat behind the wheel…..

…..Nash waves through the windshield with a goofy grin on his face, before opening up that door and swinging one long leg out…..

…..only for that door to get violently slammed again, crashing it straight into Big Sexy’s knee…..

…..and as Nash cries out in pain, Big Poppa Pump slams the door into that long leg two more times, before turning to the camera to tell Scott Hall and Sean Waltman that he don’t need a pair of little b!tches like them calling him out…..

”…..AND IF KEVIN NASH HAS ANYTHING TO SAY TO ME, HE CAN SAY IT TO ME HIMSELF…..ONCE HES DONE CRYING LIKE A LITTLE GIRL THAT IS…..”

…..with that, Scotty slams that door one more time into the knee of Nash, before walking out of shot briefly and returning with that lead pipe in hand…..

”…..NOW THE WAY I SEE IT, I COULD TAKE OUT KEVIN NASH RIGHT HERE RIGHT NOW WITH ONE SWING OF THIS PIPE…..BUT I’M NOT GONNA DO THAT…..BECAUSE IF I DO THAT, THEN I WONT BE ABLE TO KICK HIS ASS AT WRESTLEMANIA…..SEE KEVIN, YOU CAN CONSIDER THIS AN OFFICIAL CHALLENGE…..FACE TO FACE, MAN TO MAN…..BECAUSE I’M ALL MAN…..MORE MAN THAN YOU’LL EVER BE…..AND AT WRESTLEMANIA, YOU’LL FIND OUT WHY THE CALL ME THE GENETIC FREAK…..AND YOU, YOU’LL JUST BE A SEVEN FOOT STAIN ON THE MAT…..”

…..with that, Scotty finally walks out of shot…..

…..briefly…..

…..before walking back into shot for a third time, when he uses that lead pipe to smash each and every window on that sweet ass ride (along with putting a few dents in the body) before finally tossing that pipe on the floor beside Nash, telling him that he’ll see him it Wrestlemania…..

”…..THAT’S IF YOU CAN STILL WALK…..”

…..so much for Kevin Nash’s grand entrance…..let’s go back to ringside…..

Rating: A

…..where Hall and Waltman have clearly headed to the back to check on the condition of Kevin Nash…..because it’s time for our next match and it involves a man that made his debut just last week, Steve Blackman vs. one half of the Harris Twins, Ron Harris…..

…..I know, I know…..barn burner right? To be fair this match wasn’t put here to win any match of the year accolades, it was put here to give Steve Blackman a victory on his debut, without (since Steve-O is an actual no-one) killing the overness of anyone that actually mattered…..

…..and idea when we actually last saw either Harris Boy? No me neither…..needless to say that Steve Blackman won, taking Harris’ head off with a wicked looking Bicycle Kick…..

Winner: Steve Blackman
Rating: C-

…..last week Savio Vega attacked Steve Blackman, clearly jealous that the debuting gentleman was getting more attention than Savio had gotten in months…..maybe years…..

…..that attack didn’t go so well…..in fact, it ended up with Savio getting his ass kicked, followed by a martial arts display from Blackman…..clearly Vega learned a little something from that, because his time he waited for Blackman to start his little stick show, before cracking him in the back with a chair when he was too engaged in his display to notice the Puerto Rican entering the ring…..

…..Blackman goes down, but Savio hits him a few more times just to make sure he STAYS down, before standing over the newcomer with his arms raised, throwing out cheesy looking karate chops, as we head backstage…..

…..ok, maybe this isn’t the hottest angle in the world…..but we’ve all gotta start somewhere right…..

Rating: C

…..we head over to Michael Cole now, who’s in the locker room of the Hart Foundation ahead of Owen Hart’s upcoming match with Chris Jericho’s Wrestlemania opponent Dean Malenko…..

…..Jericho might be putting his WWF Intercontinental Championship on the line against Malenko, but Malenko, he’s putting it ALL on the line…..because he says that if he can’t beat Chris Jericho at Wrestlemania, then he’s done…..finished…..no more Dean Malenko, he’s going home…..

…..a fact which Jericho finds hilarious……

“…..because let’s be real Michelangelo, Bore-Us would be doing EVERYONE a favour if he just went back home to his boring wife…..kissed his boring kids…..petted his boring dog…..waved at the boring mailman…..sat on his boring couch…..put on his boring pants…..took out his bor…..”

“…..is this going somewhere?…..”

“…..yes this is going somewhere Cole…..and don’t ever, eeeeeeeeeeever interrupt me again…..”

…..Jericho says that once Wrestlemania is over, no one will ever have to be bored to death by Bore-Us again, and you’ll be able to thank Chris Jericho for that (Paragon of Virtue, Your Hero and Mine, etc, etc) but tonight, after claiming that he came to test himself against the best Light-Heavyweight competitors in the world, Dean Malenko gets to step in there with the ORIGINAL Light-Heavyweight competitor…..

…..Jericho says that Owen Hart originated Light-Heavyweight wrestling in Canada…..he perfected it in Stampede and then took it to Japan (“where they copied it”) took it to Mexico (“where they stole it”) bought it here to the WWF (“where they couldn’t do it”) and tonight, Dean Malenko has the privilege of competing in his penultimate match…..

…..against Owen Hart…..

…..Owen stands there beaming as Jericho embellishes the truth, before confirming all the lies that Jericho just spurned as though the tales were gospel, before apologising to Jericho…..

”…..because I AM the original Light-Heavyweight…..and while I’ve packed about 30lbs of pure muscle on since my Light-Heavyweight peak (erm, no…..no you haven’t Owen) I’m still a Light-Heavyweight at heart…..and Chris, I’m sorry……but Dean Malenko might not even make it to Wrestlemania…..”

…..Owen and Jericho exchange a laugh, before Michael Cole goes on to question the Hitman…..he says that in recent weeks it would seem like Vince McMahon is doing everything possible to ensure that Steve Austin doesn’t make it to Wrestlemania, but he doesn’t seem too concerned about Bret Hart being in the match…..in fact, it would seem as though McMahon has completely forgotten about The Hitman…..

”…..I shouldn’t be surprised by that Michael Cole……Vince McMahon has been trying to forget about Bret Hart for the past two years…..ever since he watched me get screwed out of the WWF Title at Wrestlemania XII…..

…..well that’s fine by me…..if Vince McMahon wants to spend the next four weeks worrying about Steve Austin and massaging Brian Pillman’s fragile ego, I got no problem in that…..just like I’ll have no problem walking into Wrestlemania XIV and proving to the world exactly why they call me the best there is, the best there was and the best there ever will be…..when I become the WWF Champion for the FOURTH time…..”

Rating: A

…..never let it be said that either Chris Jericho or Owen Hart let a little thing like the truth get in the way of a good story…..because no matter how much they want you to believe it, Owen Hart is most certainly NOT the original Light-Heavyweight wrestler…..

…..what he IS though, is a very, very good all round performer…..yes, he has elements of your stereotypical Light-Heavyweight (he’s quick, agile…..he’s certainly not afraid to utilise the top rope) but he also has elements of submission wrestling…..technical wrestling…..he can talk…..in fact, there’s not a lot that he CAN’T do…..although granted (with the exception of being decent on the mic) Owen’s opponent tonight pretty much ticks all those boxes too…..

…..obviously from an in-ring standpoint, this match was excellent…..Malenko has been desperate to prove himself as of late, but he hasn’t been doing a very good job of it, loosing more matches than he’s won, with even his last major victory (over Rey Mysterio) coming off the back of interference from our Intercontinental Champion…..he lost to Jarrett, has lost to Jericho, lost to Test, lost singles matches, tag team encounters, Title matches…..he’s lost a lot…..

…..he was doing well here, certainly holding his own against the former multi-time title holder Owen Hart…..but as the match progresses (and Chris Jericho makes his way out from the back) it looks like he could be set to pick up another defeat right here…..

…..at least until Rey Mysterio comes rushing out from the back to make sure that Jericho stays far away from the action in the ring…..

…..speaking of the action in the ring, Owen Hart is momentarily distracted by the fact that Chris Jericho is no longer able to give him a helping hand…..so much so that Malenko has time to plan his attack…..to as Owen goes for a kick, Dean catches the leg, before anticipating the enzuguri that usually follows, ducking that too…..

…..and as Hart lands flat on his back (with his foot still in Malenko’s hands) the Man of 1000 Holds wraps his legs up and turns him over into the Texas Cloverleaf…..and with Mysterio preventing Chris Jericho from doing anything about it, Owen Hart has little option but to tap out…..

…..and that’s a HUGE victory for Dean Malenko…..

Winner: Dean Malenko
Rating: B+

…..Malenko rolls out of the ring and joins Mysterio in the aisle, where the two men celebrate as Chris Jericho throws a monumental hissy hit in the ring…..Owen isn’t much happier either, snatching up a microphone to tell the crowd that he didn’t tap out…..Malenko cheated…..he was just banging the mat to try and get himself closer to the ropes…..he was about to power out of the hold…..etc, etc, etc…..

…..no ones buying that, but Owen isn’t done…..he tells Mysterio that this is all his fault…..

”…..I’ve never been so disrespected in all my life…..don’t you know who I am? I’m Owen Hart…..I’m the reason that ALL you so called Light-Heavyweights have a career…..you know what, I’m GLAD Chris Jericho took the division away from you all…..you don’t deserve it…..you don’t deserve to walk through the door that I opened for you…..”

…..man, Owen is really going all in on this “Originator of the Light-Heavyweight Division” nonsense huh…..it IS utter nonsense too, but Chris Jericho has obviously convinced Owen that it’s fact and Hart isn’t happy that these new breed of Light-Heavyweight isn’t giving him the respect he obviously believes he deserves…..

”…..well enough is enough…..Dean Malenko you little punk, my personal protégé Chris Jericho will end your miserable little career at Wrestlemania, but Rey Mysterio, you’re all mine you little Mexican Jumping Bean…..”

…..I guess that’s a challenge…..a Wrestlemania challenge none the less…..and since Rey is still in the aisle way, he accepts the match there and then…..

…..challenge levied…..challenge accepted…..

…..Wrestlemania Match made…..

…..let’s go to the back…..

Rating: B+

…..where the Undertaker, Kane and Paul Bearer are in the bowels of the building…..I would say that they are deep in conversation, but in reality Kane isn’t much of a talker and Taker has plenty to say…..

…..he says that tonight he was hoping to unleash the fires of hell upon the false deity Raven tonight…..Raven wants to see the true dark side of the deadman…..well the Undertaker would happily show him…..he would “come” to Raven…..if Raven stopped hiding for one second…..

”…..and you brother…..oh the horrors you will inflict upon Mankind…..Mankind who cost you the WWF Championship…..Mankind, who’s own actions will cost him his very soul…..I don’t have Raven tonight…..you don’t have Mankind…..WE have Ric Flair and Arn Anderson…..THEY will pay the price for the sins of the others…..”

…..Paul Bearer says that Raven can only hide for so long…..sooner or later he’s going to have to come down from his nest, until then leave it to Paul Bearer…..he says that should Steve Austin defeat Mankind tonight (which Bearer thinks he will) then Kane will get his chance at cold blooded revenge…..Paul Bearer will make sure that match happens at Wrestlemania…..as for Raven, Paul Bearer is going to speak to Vince McMahon…..to see if he can’t get ANOTHER match booked for Wrestlemania…..The Deadman vs. The Dead Man……The Undertaker…..vs. Raven…..

”…..for now, all you two need to worry about is Arn Anderson and Ric Flair…..leave Raven and Mankind to me…..”

…..Kane and the Undertaker nod, before walking away and leaving Paul Bearer deep down there in the dark bowels of the building…..clearly he’s planning on speaking to Mr. McMahon, but perhaps hanging around in dark rooms isn’t the best idea…..because unbeknown to Bearer, two figures walk out of the gloom behind him…..

…..standing ominously behind the oblivious manager…..

…..that can’t bode well for Mr. Bearer…..

…..let’s go down to ringside…..

Rating: A

…..where it’s time for tag team action…..and tag team action featuring out tag team champions none the less…..but rest assured, with a fatal four way title defence looming at Wrestlemania, Vince McMahon has assured that this particular encounter is a non-title affair…..not that either The Undertaker or Kane are particularly bothered about the tag team titles right now, they have other things on their minds…..

…..things like Raven…..things like Mankind…..

…..both of these brothers of destruction have their sights (and their wrath) set on people other than Flair and Anderson…..but that’s not to say they’re not going to take their frustrations out on whomever is put in front of them…..if this truly was some form of punishment for the fact that the tag team champions failed to take Steve Austin out last week then consider them punished…..

…..because The Deadman and the Big Red Monster beat the snot out of them…..

…..Anderson and Flair were able to isolate the Undertaker for a while…..they even worked over a limb (the leg to be precise) but it was never going to be enough, as the brothers soon overcame the onslaught to continue their assault…..

”…..Deadman…..HEY DEADMAN!…..COME TO ME…..COME TO ME…..FOR I CAN SET YOU FREE…..”

…..until the sounds of Raven’s voice echoes through the building…..and the camera’s find him (again) high up in in the arena…..arms outstretched…..with Jake Roberts standing behind Paul Bearer with his arm around his throat…..

…..that sight alone is enough to make the brothers abandon their match, climbing out of the ring and over the barricade, making their way through the crowd as Raven continues to speak…..

…..and the official counts them out…..

Winner(s): Ric Flair & Arn Anderson (via Count-Out)
Rating: B+

…..Anderson and Flair get a reprieve as the brothers of destruction abandon them to head off through the stands…..they’ve got a long walk ahead of them though…..fortunately Raven has plenty to say to keep them entertained on their journey…..

…..he tells the Undertaker that this is exactly what he’s trying to save him from…..this banal normality…..this humanity…..this…..family…..

”…..family that is destined to be the downfall of the so-called deadman…..family that has taken a phenom and diluted him down into just another man…..Undertaker, true forces of darkness are not bound by blood…..nor are they concerned by the suffering of lesser beings and make no mistake, this IS a lesser being…..

…..no longer the phenom…..no longer that monster under the bed…..no longer a threat…..no longer the WWF Champion…..but you could be…..I can make you all that you should be…..

…..come to me…..”

…..the Undertaker is coming (as is Kane) but they have a way to go yet…..they continue their hurried climb through the crowd as they rush to Paul Bearer’s aid…..and they SHOULD rush…..after all, we’ve all seen what Raven is capable of…..just as a certain one eyed priest…..

”…..Undertaker, you were once the dark shadow that cast itself over the entire WWF…..you walked through the door and put the entire company on notice…..Warrior’s, Savage’s and Maniac’s all felt your cold grip on their throats…..heh, you even vanquished The Snake…..then what happened? You embraced the masses…..that was mistake number one…..

…..and then, baby brother came home…..and you embraced that sibling bond like a child desperate for the warm embrace of family…..mistake number two…..

…..I know the truth is still inside you…..I know that the dark blanket still cloaks your soul…..I know (just like The Snake knew) that no one truly realises the depths of their depravity until they try and swim to the surface…..the darkness is still inside you…..there is no escape from it…..everyone speaks about the speed of light, but the light can never outrace the dark…..because no matter how fast that light travels, the darkness has always got there first…..

…..YOU were once that darkness…..YOU were once the abyss that all men stared upon with fear and wonder…..YOU once mattered…..

…..now you’re just weak…..
