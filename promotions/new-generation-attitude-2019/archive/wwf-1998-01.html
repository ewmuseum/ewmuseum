WWF Raw is War: Week 1, January 1998

Posted 11 June 2019

Week 1, January 1998

Pittsburg Civic Arena (Pittsburg, Pennsylvania)

ATT: 17,000

TV: 38.52

The show opens with a recap of last weeks events…..events which saw Vince McMahon deny Bret Hart his (rightful AND earned) WWF Championship opportunity at the Royal Rumble, to announce that The Undertaker would once again be defending the World Title against Brian Pillman…..in a Casket Match…..an announcement that would have many people scratching their heads. After all, the Corporate Cannon has already failed to capture the gold in a standard one on one encounter, what chance does McMahon really think he has in a Casket Match?…..except as we soon found out, the Undertaker won’t be facing the Corporate Cannon at the Royal Rumble…..he’s going to be facing the Loose Cannon…..

…..Brian ‘F’n’ Pillman…..

…..and Pillman was quick to remind everyone exactly how he got that monicker in the first place, with a horrifying attack on the one time manager, mentor and friend of the Undertaker, Paul Bearer, an attack which saw Bearer’s leg utterly destroyed by the (apparent) Number One contender to the WWF Championship…..

…..which brings us to tonight…..and an almighty…..

….::::!!!!GONG!!!!::::….

…..as the current WWF Champion The Undertaker makes his iconic walk to the ring, with his baby brother Kane by his side…..and he does NOT look particularly happy…..

…..he grabs a microphone and says that he’s going to make this really simple…..he wants Brian Pillman, and he wants him right now….so unless Vince McMahon has his little rabid puppy dog make his way out here in a few minute, The Undertaker and Kane will head right to the back, and they will tear this building (and anyone that gets in their way) to pieces looking for him…..

“…..Brian Pillman…..you may fancy yourself some sort of Loose Cannon…..some anarchist…..some agent of chaos……but last week, you crossed a line that Satan himself would fear to cross…..you crossed a line that is going to cost you more than the chance of becoming WWF Champion…..that’s going to cost you more than your career…..you crossed a line…..that’s going to cost you your very soul…..”

…..speaking of soul, how about a bit of heart to go with it?……or rather Hart…..as the OTHER man who has a legitimate stake to call himself the number one contender to the WWF Championship makes his way out…..the man who earned HIS future Championship opportunity at last months Dead Man Walking event, defeating Stone Cold Steve Austin…..Bret Hart…..

…..the Hitman isn’t alone either…..he’s accompanied by the rest of the Hart Foundation, as Owen Hart, Chris Jericho, and (the Bodyguard) Test follow closely behind…..

…..Hart grabs a microphone and he’s not shy in letting the crowd know that once again, he’s getting screwed over here in the World Wrestling Federation…..he tells the Undertaker that everybody knows that it should be HIM getting that World Title opportunity at the Royal Rumble…..

“…..I am the best there is…..the best there was…..the best there ever will be…..and I am the number one contender to that World Wrestling Federation Title, whether you like it, Vince McMahon likes it…..or these idiot fans like it……I earned a shot at that title, and I want what’s coming to me right here, right now…..”

…..it looks like ‘what’s coming to him’ is going to be an almighty beating from a Deadman who is in no mood to play games…..Taker tells Hart that right now, he has a Casket prepared with Brian Pillman’s name on it……

“…..don’t make me have to build another one boy, because I am not in the mood for your whining right now…..”

…..The Undertaker says that tonight, he and Kane are going to stop the revolution in its tracks, because they are going to take Brian Pillman out of the miserable existence that he calls life…..and at the Royal Rumble, there will BE no Cannon to compete for the WWF Title, Corporate, Loose or otherwise……so maybe…..MAYBE…..Bret Hart should do the smart thing…..get out of The Deadman’s way……and let the Undertaker do what he’s come here tonight to do……and THEN…..at the Royal Rumble…..it will be Bret Hart that Rests…..in……Peace…..

“…..actually, I do believe that I have a better idea…..”

…..and just like that a chorus of boo’s breaks out through the arena…..as Vincent Kennedy McMahon makes his way out onto the entrance ramp (where he remains) along with The Enforcer Arn Anderson, and the former two time WWF Champion, The Nature Boy Ric Flair…..

“…..you see Bret…..yes…..YOU won at last Month’s In Your House event…..and YOU won the opportunity to challenge for the World Wrestling Federation Title…..but nowhere on that contract does it state that Bret Hart get’s to specify WHEN he has that match, and Bret, much like I said last week, it’s the Corporate Cannon that will be granted the first opportunity at the WWF Title…..

…..and Undertaker, as much as you seem to think otherwise, yes that match WILL be taking place as advertised, and no, you will not be getting your hands on Brian Pillman tonight……because Brian Pillman is not HERE tonight……”

…..the crowd does not like that, but it does bring McMahon to the point of his being out there right now……and that it the fact that if The Undertaker wants an opponent this evening, well he can have two……and if Bret Hart truly believes that he can take out the WWF Champion, then he can prove that right here tonight, because in the main event of the evening it’s going to be The Undertaker and Kane, teaming up to face Bret and Owen Hart……

…..of course, that leaves Chris Jericho and Test with little to do this evening…..

…..which is perhaps why the Wolves begin to howl, as the WWF Intercontinental Champion Scott Hall and his Wolfpac partner Sean Waltman come strutting out onto the stage alongside McMahon, Flair and Anderson…..

…..Hall tells McMahon that since he’s out here making matches, the Wolfpac have a match for him, because they want Chris Jericho and Test…..and they want them right now…..

“…..actually Chico, what we REALLY want is that jacked up jack-off Scott Steiner, but everybody knows that Big Poppa Dump ain’t man enough to face the Bad Guy head on, he has to jump people in the back…..”

…..Hall says it’s all good though, because the Wolfpac can take it to the back when they need to, and the Big Man(g), Kevin Nash, he’s gonna be back REAL soon to take care of business…..

…..Jericho stops Hall there, telling him to shut the hell up…..he says that he has no interest in facing him OR his little pipsqueak partner Sean Waltman tonight…..Jericho says that he beat Hall to within an inch of his life at Dead Man Walking (until Hall cheated to win), he beat Hall to within HALF an inch of his life last week…..the only time he needs to step into the ring with Scott Hall again, is when the Bad Guy finally grows a set and puts the Intercontinental Title on the line…..

“…..hey yo, I tol’ you man(g), you wan’ a shot at the belt, all you gotta do is ask…..I tell you what, you and your little blonde girlfriend there, you get in the ring with us tonight, and at the Royal Rumble, you got your shot…..”

…..and just like that we have two matches booked for this evening……The Undertaker & Kane facing Bret & Owen Hart in our main event…….and Scott Hall & Sean Waltman going up against Chris Jericho & Test…..and that match……is next…..

Rating: A*

The Wolfpac vs. Chris Jericho & Test


So this is the first match of 1998, and all things considered it’s got some pretty big names in it…..the current (and five time) WWF Intercontinental Champion Scott Hall, the former WWF Light Heavyweight, European and two time WWF Tag Team Champion Sean Waltman, former two time (and final) WWF Light Heavyweight Champion Chris Jericho….and Test…..

…..ok Test might not have quite the resume that the three other (main event) men have, but just a few months ago he was an absolute nobody with just a few months in the business, while now (after running with the Hart Foundation for a while) he is a very solid midcard star…..

…..with that being said, you’d think perhaps Jericho and Test would be at a disadvantage considering the newcomers rookie status, coupled with the fact that Hall and Waltman are former Tag Team Champions themselves…..interestingly enough it was the Hart Foundation that took those titles from them back at Wrestlemania…..granted not Jericho and Test….it was Bret and Owen….but that still gives the Canadians the psychological edge here tonight…..

…..the Wolfpac start strong, but it’s finally the smaller man that they isolate, working over Sean Waltman and cutting the ring in half…..Test uses his superior size to overpower the Wolfpac man, while Jericho uses his agility (and off the charts levels of obnoxiousness) to keep Waltman off balance…..

…..with a shot at Scott Hall’s Intercontinental looming at the Royal Rumble, a win here would do wonders for Jericho’s (admittedly already quite high) confidence levels, but it looked as though it wasn’t meant to be…..because Sean Waltman finally made the hot tag, and the Bad Guy cleared house…..

….::::!!!!SIRENS!!!!::::….

…..at least until the sirens blared…..and Big Poppa Pump Scott Steiner made his way out from the back, with Sable on his arm……and a lead pipe in hand…..

…..Waltman rolls out of the ring to try and head off Steiner on the floor, but a pipe shot to the skull soon takes him out of commission, and as Jericho and Test are double teaming Hall inside the ring, Steiner climbs through the ropes……

…..and absolutely nails Hall in the back of the head with that lead pipe, putting the Bad Guy down and giving the official little option but to call for the bell…..

Winner(s): Scott Hall & Sean Waltman (via Disqualification)
Rating: B

…..but you know that a little thing like the end of a match doesn’t mean the end of the action around here…..and Scott Steiner is far from finished, delivering a series of blows to the already half unconscious Scott Hall with that lead pipe…..

…..Jericho attempts to play nice with Steiner, slapping him on the back and looking for a high five…..Scotty being Scotty though bats the hand away angrily, telling Jericho to “GET YOUR HANDS OFF ME YOU LITTLE PISSANT” and as Steiner waves that lead pipe menacingly in his direction, Jericho backs away so quickly that he stumbles and winds up on his ass…..

…..Test (being that he IS the bodyguard) quickly gets in Steiner’s face…..and Jericho (obviously the smarter of the two) quickly drags him away, and the two men head to the outside where they proceed to deliver another two on one beatdown to Sean Waltman…..

…..that just leaves Steiner and Hall in the ring, and with Hall still down (and most definitely out) Sable hands her man a microphone…..

“…..YOU KNOW, I HEARD YOU OUT HERE EARLIER, RUNNING YOUR MOUTH…..YOU THINK BIG POPPA PUMP AIN’T MAN ENOUGH TO FACE YOU? WELL WHOS THE MAN NOW? BECAUSE WHILE IM STANDING HERE LIKE THE GENETIC FREAK THAT I AM, YOU’RE DOWN ON THE MAT LIKE THE LITTLE B!TCH YOU’VE ALWAYS BEEN…..

…..YOU THINK KEVIN NASH COMING BACK WORRIES ME? LOOK AT ME…..THERES ONLY ONE ‘BIG’ SEXY IN THE WWF, AND YOU’RE LOOKING AT HIM……AND UNLIKE KEVIN NASH, IM BIG ALL OVER……FROM MY BULGING MUSCLES TO MY THICK….LONG….HARD…..PIPE…..AND WHEN I SAY PIPE, IM ALSO TALKING ABOUT MY PENIS…..AINT THAT RIGHT SABLE…..”

…..Sable just smiles and nod’s, she’s turned into quite the bad girl since Scotty’s attitude adjustment around here, but Steiner isn’t done…..he says that since Kevin Nash is coming back, Scott Hall can deliver a little message for him…..and with that, much as we saw back at In Your House, Big Poppa Pump rolls the Bad Guy over and locks on the Steiner Recliner, using that lead pipe to choke the life out of Hall…..

…..a number of officials hit the ring and they do TRY to pry Steiner off of Hall…..they can’t budge this mass of muscle however, and it’s only finally Sable that gets Scott to break the hold, telling him that “….I’m bored Scotty…..let’s get out of here…..and I’ll do that thing you like…..”

…..I don’t know what ‘that thing’ is, but Steiner clearly does, because he releases Hall in a flash as the two lovebirds make their way to the back, message delivered…..

…..the officials check on Hall, but not for long…..because Test is suddenly back in the ring, and he tosses all but ONE referee to the mat, as Chris Jericho rolls the Intercontinental Champion over, places one foot on his chest and flexes those muscles…..

…..Test throws the final official down and places a foot on the small of his back, adding pressure as he tells the protesting official to “make the count”…..the referee clearly doesn’t want to, but as Test applies more pressure the man in the stripes finally slaps the mat three times, and Chris Jericho (being the obnoxious pr!ck that he is) runs around the ring with his arms raised, doing a lap of honour like he just beat Andre at Wrestlemania III…..

…..the official decision here this evening is a disqualification victory to the Wolfpac…..but right now, neither Hall nor Waltman look particularly like winners……let’s go to a commercial.

Rating: A*

We come back from commercial and we are treated to a shot of Stone Cold Steve Austin swinging open the doors and marching his way through the building like a man on a mission…..

…..he doesn’t even pause his stride as Kevin Kelly rushes over to keep up pace with him, asking the Rattlesnake exactly what it is he’s doing?…..

“…..what am I doing? Well hell son, what does it look like I’m doing?…..last week Vince McMahon found out first hand that don’t no one keep Steve Austin out of a building but Steve Austin, and Ric Flair found out that when the clock hits 3:16 then it’s time to get your ass whipped…..”

…..Austin says that he came here last week to open up a family sized can of whoop ass on Vince McMahon and all his little cronies, and he’s here tonight to finish the job…..Ric Flair….Arn Anderson, they cost Steve Austin the number one contender shot at In Your House…..

“…..and when you cost Steve Austin anything it’s gonna cost you your ass…..”

…..so what’s gonna happen now, is Ric Flair and Arn Anderson, they’re gonna get whipped like the sorry s.o.b’s that they are, and then, Vince McMahon’s worst nightmare is going to come true…..

“…..because while that little Corporate B@stard Brian Pillman is going to go to the Royal Rumble to face the Undertaker, Steve Austin is going to the Royal Rumble for one reason and one reason only….and that’s to win the Royal Rumble match, go on to Wrestlemania and become the World Wrestling Federation Champion, and that’s the bottom line……now get that damn microphone out of my face before I shove it up your ass…..”

…..you don’t have to tell Kevin Kelly twice, and he quickly halts in his step as we watch the Rattlesnake continue his march through the building……presumably looking for Ric Flair or Arn Anderson…..we’re staying in the back too, as we go to Michael Cole…..

Rating: B+

…..who’s standing by with Jake “The Snake” Roberts…..

…..Cole says that after months of games, riddles and mystery, Jake Roberts journey through Raven’s Nine Circles of Hell…..this ‘Divine Comedy’ is finally coming to an end at the Royal Rumble, when Jake steps into the final circle of hell…..Treachery……but not only that, he finally steps into the ring with Raven……

“…..and I suppose the question on everyone’s lips, is Jake Roberts…..has it been worth it?…..”

…..Jake ponders for a moment before saying that it really is the 64 thousand dollar question…..and it’s a question that can’t be answered yet…..

“…..you see, until this is done no one really knows the price it’s had on my soul…..Raven, regardless of what you might say, regardless of the way you have bastardised my intentions, this all came about because I wanted to become a better man…..because I wanted to atone for my past misdeeds…..because I wanted to make YOU a better man than I made you all those years ago…..

…..I truly believed that with the grace of God ANYONE could be saved…..anyone could step out of the darkness and into the light but I was wrong…..and I was wrong because in order to be saved you have to WANT to be saved, and you Raven, you don’t want to be saved, you just want to watch the world dance to your sick tune…..

…..you attacked me…..you attacked my faith…..you mutilated and humiliated my priest…..you did not want to be saved…..but I did…..and now…..now all I want is to watch you bleeding at my feet…..

…..was it worth it?…..

…..I went from wanting to sit at the right hand of God, to begging to dance with the devil just for the opportunity to inflict an iota of the mental anguish and suffering that you have inflicted on me…..and you ask me was it worth it……

…..Raven, you chose Treachery as our final destination and there is perhaps none more fitting, because there is none more treacherous than a Snake, and believe me, it’s The Snake you’re going to get…..not Jake Roberts the Man of God…..not Jake Roberts the recovering addict…..not Jake Roberts the man determined to make a difference……you’re getting the Snake…..the man responsible for your creation…..the man who will also be responsible for your destruction…..

…..was it worth it?…..

…..we’ll find out at the Royal Rumble…..

…..Trust Me…..”

Rating: A

The British Bulldog vs. Savio Vega


It’s back to ringside now for our second match of the evening, and unlike our previous encounter there is no real issue at stake here, just two men looking to pick up a win and possibly make an impression…..

…..it’s Davey Boy Smith facing off against Savio Vega (who is sporting both a new look AND a new attitude) and a victory for either man here would be very welcome……The British Bulldog has been a little bit more visible as of late than he had been in recent months, but if he really wants to get another shot at the European Championship (as he has eluded to) then he’s going to need to continue to pick up victories…..

…..Savio Vega on the other hand hasn’t really been since since back at November’s Survivor Series event…..and even THAT was in a losing effort…..he’s perhaps thinking that the new attire might bring him a bit more luck than his old one did…..

…..it didn’t…..

…..the Bulldog was the one to take home the win, finishing Savio off with the Running Powerslam for the one, two, three.

Winner: The British Bulldog
Rating: B

We head to the back again now, where Dok Hendrix is standing at the door of Mr. McMahon’s office, where he tells us that he’s hoping to get word from McMahon on not only the whereabouts of Brian Pillman, but his response to the words of Steve Austin earlier this evening…..

…..he knocks and enters the room, asking McMahon those exact questions…..and since the Chairman appears to be in somewhat of a jovial mood tonight, he even gets an answer…..

…..he says that as far as the location of Brian Pillman goes, McMahon has no idea where the Corporate Cannon is and after his actions of last week, that suits Vince just fine…..Vince says that he’s given Brian Pillman free reign to go out there and just be Brian Pillman…..no Corporate handcuffs, no judgement…..and (most importantly) no comeback on Vince McMahon or the WWF, because Brian Pillman is acting entirely on his own…..

“…..and quite frankly, people scoffed when I made the WWF Championship bout at the Royal Rumble a Casket Match…..they didn’t think that the Corporate Cannon stood a chance…..well, who’s scoffing now Dok? Certainly not Paul Bearer, that’s for sure…..”

…..McMahon says that in just a few short weeks, Brian Pillman will be standing here as the World Wrestling Federation Champion, and frankly it’s been a long time coming…..

…..as for Stone Cold Steve Austin…..

“…..Mr. Austin seems to be under the misguided impression that he is somehow automatically entered into the Royal Rumble match…..unfortunately, as he often is, Mr. Austin is very much mistaken…..you see, the thirty competitors in the Royal Rumble match have yet to even be announced, so while Steve Austin may well be in that match, he may well NOT be in that match also…..

…..but I can tell you what match Steve Austin most certainly WILL be in…..and that’s a match tonight…..with this gentleman right here…..because if Steve Austin wants to get his hands on Ric Flair so badly, the Nature Boy will be happy to show him exactly who the ‘man’ around here really is…..”

…..Ric Flair lets out a loud ‘Wooooooooo’, before telling Dok Hendrix that if Steve Austin is looking for him, the Nature Boy isn’t hard to find…..

“…..he’s the one in the custom suit…..with Gucci on his feet, Rolex on his wrist, and a beautiful lady on each arm…..see Austin, what a low rent Texas redneck like you will never understand, is that age is just a number…..and when you’re as good as the Nature Boy, your very worst day still tops most people’s best…..”

…..Flair says that Austin is quick to go around mocking the age of a Ric Flair…..of an Arn Anderson…..but what Steve Austin seems to forget, is that the reason he’s not the number one contender right now is because of these two old men, and they’d do it again in a heartbeat…..

“…..Steve Austin, you were offered the opportunity of a lifetime by this man…..Vince McMahon personally offered to take you under his wing and mentor you to become a champion that EVERYONE could be proud of…..but what did you do? You hit him with a Stunner…..that was mistake number one pal…..

…..mistake number two, was thinking that even for a second you could hang with the Nature Boy……see I’ve been going up and down this road for twenty five years…..since you were a snot nosed little kid watching me with awe on your little broken down trailer park tv…..I was taking titles and breaking hearts while you were still watching Saturday morning cartoons and you think you’re going to hang with ME?…..well let me steal a line from you ‘son’, because Uh-Uh, that ain’t gonna happen…..

…..if you really want to find me, I’ll be the one standing in the centre of that ring later tonight with the platinum blonde hair and the$10,000 robe, ready to take you to school…..and that’s the bottom line…..because The Nature Boy said so…..Whooooooo……”

Rating: A*

Dean Malenko vs. Jeff Jarrett (w/Ted DiBiase)


It’s back down to ringside now for our next match of the evening, and it’s the Million Dollar Corporation’s own European Champion Jeff Jarrett, competing in non-title action against Dean Malenko…..

…..the Million Dollar Corporation have been particularly ruthless when it comes to retaining their assets as of late…..don’t believe me? Ask Psycho Sid…..ask Ken Shamrock…..ask Chavo Guerrero Jr…..ask any number of #1 contenders that Jeff Jarrett has put away in recent months…..which begs the question, why would anyone be stepping up to challenge Jarrett and the Corporation at this time?…..

…..probably because since its inception, the European Championship has been one of the most coveted and hotly contested Titles here in the WWF…..it’s probably the most frequently defended championship too…..which is why so many men have tried, and so many men have failed to capture the gold…..Dean Malenko won’t be capturing the gold tonight either, because this is a non-title affair, but you’ve got to think that a victory here would be more than enough for Malenko to stake his claim to the #1 contendership…..

…..this was a pretty good match actually…..Dean Malenko (or Bore-is Malenko if you’re Chris Jericho) while a little bland, is one of the better workers on the roster, while Jarrett is no slouch either…..

…..there is also the small matter of the CEO of the Million Dollar Corporation, because Ted DiBiase is lurking at ringside and he’s not afraid to interject himself into the match should the opportunity present itself…..unfortunately for Jarrett, the opportunity really DIDN’T present itself…..and to the horror of Mr. DiBiase, he had to watch as Malenko converted a Jeff Jarrett Figure Four attempt into a small package…..and three seconds later, Dean Malenko just pinned the WWF European Champion…..

Winner: Dean Malenko
Rating: B

…..with the match in the bag, Dean Malenko grabs a microphone from ringside and lets it be known in no uncertain turns, that after beating Old Double J, he wants Jeff Jarrett again at the Royal Rumble…..and he wants the European Championship on the line, because he’s beaten Jarrett once, he can damn sure do it again…..and when he does, he wants the gold to prove it?….. himself is ‘

…..the rest of the Corporation soon make their way out and join Jarrett and Dibase on the ramp way…..and it looks as though Dean Malenko might only be seconds away from disaster…..but before Jarrett, Steiner and Southern Justice can make a move, DiBiase stops them in their tracks, telling Malenko that if he wants a shot (much to the surprise of Jarrett) then he can have a shot…..

“…..but just remember what’s happened to anyone else around here who thought they were going to take this Title away from Jeff Jarrett…..after all, everybody has a price for the Million Dollar Man and every shot at the European Championship comes at a cost……a cost that you might not be willing to pay…..”

…..DiBiase lets out his trademark cackle after delivering that thinly veiled threat, as the Corporation head to the back….and we have another title match set for the Royal Rumble…..and speaking of the Royal Rumble, we go to a promo video now hyping that very event, as Malenko stands celebrating in the ring.

Rating: B+

We come back to the arena, and we come back to a very happy looking Jerry “The King” Lawler standing in the middle of the ring, and he’s ready to hold court…..

…..this isn’t your usual King’s Court though…..because after last weeks altercation between Sunny and Terri, a special challenge has been made…..not a wrestling challenge (because lets face it, even by 1998 standards a match between Sunny and Terri would be atrocious) no this is a different kind of challenge altogether……

“…..so in order to get this challenge underway, let’s get our first contestant out here…..accompanied by The Road Dogg and The Bad Ass, it’s Little Miss Sweet Ass herself…..Sunny…..”

“…..Oh You Didn’t Know?…..”

…..well if you didn’t know, you’re about to…..because here come the New Age Outlaws, with Sunny leading the way…..and rather bizarrely she’s wearing a long robe…..

…..the trio climb into the ring, where Lawler is still beaming (and not even hiding the fact that he’s leering at Sunny’s rather skimpily robed figure) as he welcomes them…..Jerry puts the Outlaws over hugely, telling everyone that he is a huge, HUGE fan of theirs, and he’s an even bigger fan now that they have added Sunny to their entourage, before telling Gunn and James that he has no doubt that they will soon be wearing the WWF Tag Team Titles again for the third time…..

…..Road Dogg agrees, telling Lawler that it’s only a matter of time, and while Harlem Heat are running around defending the gold against the jabroni of the week, the REAL #1 contenders are standing right here……not Chris Candido and his moronic partner Al Snow, and certainly not the son of a son of a plumber Dustin Rhodes and his life partner Marc Mero…..

“…..and speaking of everybody’s favourite confused couple, that’s exactly why we’re here…..the challenge was made, the gauntlet was thrown down, and our own little Sweet Ass, Miss Sunny is out here to see if Terri with an i is gonna have the ‘assets’ to accept…..”

…..Sunny says that right, because she’s out here to show the world who the real ‘lady’ around here is……and by ‘lady’ she means “who’s got the best body”, because she has challenged Terri to a Bikini Contest…..

…..Sunny might be a heel…..and so is Terri…..but when it comes to seeing beautiful ladies wearing very little, the males in this crowd are still cheering like mad…..and so is The King as he turns his attention to the entrance to see if Terri is going to accept that challenge or not…..

…..obviously she does….because here she comes, flanked by Dustin Rhodes and Marc Mero…..

…..these three have undergone quite the makeover as of late…..or should that be make under…..because there is definitely less make-up on display with Dustin Rhodes and Marc Mero than there was with Goldust and Marcus……Terri on the other hand is wearing PLENTY of make-up…..she’s also (interestingly enough) also wearing a robe…..so I guess it’s safe to say that this Bikini Contest is officially happening…..

…..Dustin Rhodes grabs a microphone to let The New Age Outlaws know that their interference last week cost he and Mero a shot at the tag titles…..once this little circus here is over and done with, The Outlaws are going to regret that decision…..

…..Lawler tells Rhodes to quieten down, no one is here to listen to him talk, they’re here to see Sunny and Terri in their swimsuits…..

“…..so Sunny…..since you issued the challenge, why don’t you go first…..”

“…..WHAT DOES EVERYBODY WANT?…..”

…..someone has new theme music…..and that someone is Al Snow, as he makes his way out from the back along with his partner Chris Candido…..

…..and Head…..

…..I think…..

…..he’s carrying a robe that DEFINITELY has something underneath it…..

…..it’s probably Head…..

…..Lawler is NOT happy about this interruption, telling Snow and (don’t call me) Skippy to get the hell out of here so that they can get this Bikini Contest underway…..Snow declines…..telling Lawler that the contest couldn’t have gotten underway, because the final participant wasn’t here…..

“…..but she’s here now…..she’s ready…..and has she got a bikini for you…..”

…..Sunny and Terri look confused…..Lawler looks furious…..Candido, well, he’s given up looking embarrassed by his partners actions after all this time, so he’s got more of a look of acceptance on his face…..Snow tells the two other competitors that since neither of them are being very forthcoming then maybe Head should go first…..

“…..so music monkeys in the back…..hit that music…..”

…..they do…..they play something akin to ‘Simply Ravishing’…..

…..and in the most bizarre sight seen on Monday Nights in quite some time…..ok, ever…..Al Snow begins to sway Head from side to side, slowly playing with the robe as he does the least appealing strip tease known to man…..before finally whipping the robe clean off…..

…..revealing Head…..

…..with a Bikini wrapped around her face…..

…..Lawler is standing there dumbfounded now, asking Candido just what is Snow doing?…..

“…..isn’t that obvious King?…..he’s causing a distraction…..”

…..and with that Al Snow swings Head quickly around to one side and absolutely nails Billy Gunn between the eyes with her, before delivering the same blow to the Road Dogg, as Candido charges at Rhodes and Mero…..

…..I guess that’s the end of the Bikini Contest…..because just like that a brawl between the two teams that were competing last week (before the Outlaws intervened) breaks out…..

…..and it’s soon a brawl between all three teams, because the Outlaws don’t stay down for long…..and as security makes its way out to try and separate the warring teams (and Jerry Lawler bemoans the fact that we had no Bikini contest) we go to commercial, still no closer to finding out who the true #1 contenders to the tag team titles are.

Rating: A*

Bradshaw vs. Kama (w/The Nation)

…..regardless of what Booker said earlier, Ahmed Johnson DOES lead his troops out…..and with Booker down on the mat (and completely oblivious to the fact) the Nation surround DiBiase on the outside (who is frantically signalling for his Million Dollar reinforcements) distracting the official away from the fact that Ahmed slid into the ring and drilled Rich Steiner with a Pearl River Plunge…..

…..the Nation hit the bricks, just as Booker pulls himself to his feet….he sees his opponent pulling himself up to all fours…..and finishes Steiner off with an Ax Kick across the back of the neck…..

Winner: Booker T
Rating: B+

…..DiBiase climbs into the ring to chastise (the still oblivious) Booker T, while still frantically signalling for the rest of the Million Dollar Corporation to make their way out from the back…..

…..and again they don’t arrive, much to the annoyance and confusion of the Million Dollar Man…..

…..the reason becomes all the more clear as the Titantron flickers into life however, revealing a scene that has just unfolded backstage……

…..because Jeff Jarrett and Southern Justice are down, and most definitely OUT…..courtesy of Kevin Nash, Scott Hall and Sean Waltman…..

…..the Wolfpac step over the remains of DiBiase’s Corporation as security rush into save the day…..it’s way too late for that…..and Kevin Nash is in no mood to play games…..

…..he takes out each security guard one by one, pounding their skulls with punches and blows against the walls, before grabbing the final (and terrified) guard by the face and demanding to know where Vince McMahon, Scott Steiner, Owen Hart, Brian Pillman, Lord Lucan and Red Rum are…..

…..ok, not the last two…..but he wants to know where a LOT of people are…..

…..the guard stammers that he’s not sure where McMahon is right now, but he knows that Scott Steiner has a match coming up in a few minutes……this seems to placate Nash, who tells Hall and Waltman that “…..he’ll do for now…..” before telling the guard to pass a message on to Vince McMahon for him…..

…..and then Jackknife’s him straight through a conveniently positioned table in the hallway…..

…..the trio step over the mess (with Hall and Waltman laughing behind the deadly serious Nash) and continue on their way, presumably looking for Scott Steiner, who has a match coming up…..

…..probably after this commercial break…..

Rating: A

…..I say probably because we have a quick detour to make before we get there…..and it’s backstage again, where a crestfallen Dean Malenko is deep in conversation with Rey Mysterio, with the Light Heavyweight Championship on the table between them…..

…..the conversation is soon interrupted by the arrival of the new WWF Intercontinental Champion (and the man that handed that Light Heavyweight Championship to Malenko last night like some unwanted hand me down) Chris Jericho and his Bodyguard, Test…..

…..whatever deal Bret Hart has been broaching with Vince McMahon obviously doesn’t concern these pair too much, as Jericho is still in full celebration mode, walking into shot with a plastic cup in hand…..

…..he spends a few moments mocking the dreary looking Bore-Us Malenko Jr, again mentioning his loss last night, while pointing out that he doesn’t even HAVE a match tonight…..

“…..unlike moi…..because yours truly (being the fightingest champion that I am) is going to be putting this beautiful gold belt on the line later tonight…..against…..uhm…..I forget who….. it it doesn’t matter…..because whoever I’m facing later tonight, they’re going to be just like you Bore-Us…..a great big looooserrrrrrr…..”

…..Jericho sets his cup down on top of the Light Heavyweight Title (congratulating Malenko on his very impressive coaster) before telling both men to keep chipping away at that European Title…..maybe ONE day they will be able to call themselves champions, but it won’t be today…..

…..Mysterio stands up, telling Jericho that he and Malenko have more integrity and talent in their little fingers than he has in his entire body…..a statement that Jericho finds hilarious, asking Rey (for all his talent) where’s HIS title belt?…..and integrity? Ha (“……integrity is for losers Junior…..”) ‘Integrity and talent’ but no titles…..and by the look of Bore-Us, no confidence either…..

…..he pats Mysterio on the head (as Test towers over him) before walking off, wishing the pair good luck in paying their bills with integrity and talent…..

…..what an ass…..

…..let’s go down to ringside…..

Rating: A

Barry Windham vs. Scott Steiner (w/Sable)


…..where it’s time for Barry Windham to face off against Big Poppa Pump, Scott Steiner…..a match which Barry can thank Kevin Nash for, because after getting eliminated by Big Sexy in last night’s Royal Rumble match, Scotty was NOT a happy boy, beating the crap out of Windham as he made his entrance into the match…..

…..Scotty is STILL not a happy boy…..and he beats the crap out of Barry Windham right here, making short work of the man that creative currently has nothing for, before submitting him with the Steiner Recliner…..

Winner: Scott Steiner
Rating: B

…..he’s hardly finished though…..and as Sable massages his shoulders, Scotty grabs a microphone to tell the crowd that his elimination last night was “BULLSH!T” because everyone knows that it should be Scott Steiner going to Wrestlemania…..

“…..JUST LIKE EVERYONE KNOWS, THAT KEVIN NASH AIN’T FIT TO CARRY MY JOCKSTRAP…..AND ITS A BIG JOCKSTRAP…..BECAUSE I’VE GOT A BIG PENIS…..KEVIN NASH, YOU AND YOUR LITTLE BUDDY’S FOUND OUT FIRST HAND WHAT HAPPENS WHEN YOU P!SS OFF SCOTT STEINER…..NOW YOU GOT NO INTERCONTINENTAL TITLE, NO WORLD TITLE SHOT……AND NO BALL’S…..BECAUSE I TORE ‘EM OFF LAST NIGHT, AND NOW SABLE’S GOT ‘EM IN HER PURSE…..”

…..Steiner says that anytime Nash and Co want to find him, he’s not hard to find…..because he’s a Genetic Freak…..and he’s a one man wolfpac that’s about to take a bite out of Barry Windham’s ass…..

…..I dunno about taking a bite of it, but he certainly kicks it here, putting the boots to poor Barry as Sable retrieves his trusty pipe from the outside, which Scott uses to choke the life out of Windham with in the Steiner Recliner position…..

…..at least until the Wolves howl, and Nash, Hall and Waltman make their way out, with a p!ssed off Big Sexy leading he way…..

…..the trio hit the ring, just as Scott Steiner hits the bricks…..because for all that big talk, Big Poppa Pump apparently doesn’t feel like sharing the ring with Big Sexy right now…..he doesn’t even head up the aisle, instead choosing to disappear into the crowd, leaving the Wolfpac somewhat frustrated in the ring…..

…..Nash grabs a mic of his own, but before he can speak Scott Hall snatches it from his hand with a…..

“…..Hey Yo…..

…..hey Scott Steiner…..I’m pretty sure that the Big Man(g) here, he was about to tell you to come on down and fight like a man Chico…..but you and me man(g), we both know you not gonna do that…..

…..see Scott Hall, he’s been calling you out for weeks now man(g) and the only time I see you, is when I DON’T see you…..is when you’re jumping me from behind or costing me matches…..well last night Chico, you didn’t just cost me a match, you cost me my WWF Intercontinental Championship…..so that means that at No Way Out…..no more hiding…..no more running…..no more running your mouth man(g)…..at Now Way Out, don’t sing it…..

…..bring it…..”

…..Hall hands the microphone back to Nash (along with an apologetic shrug of the shoulders for making the challenge before he did) who pauses for a moment before telling the crowd that The Bad Guy kinds just stole his thunder…..

…..Nash says that unfortunately that’s been a running trend as of late…..and it ALWAYS revolves around Scott Steiner…..

…..Scott Steiner cost the Wolfpac the win at the Survivor Series…..Scott Steiner cost Kevin Nash the tag titles at December’s Dead Man Walking event…..Scott Steiner put him on the shelf for four weeks with a busted windpipe…..and last night, Scott Steiner cost him the opportunity it’s to headline Wrestlemania again…..

“…..but Scotty…..there’s more than one way to headline Wrestlemania…..you can do it going in as the challenger……or you can do it going in as the champion…..and since Scott Hall already challenged your ass, that means I ain’t got much to do at No Way Out…..and the way I see it, neither does Brian Pillman since he ain’t got a challenger……so Vince McMahon, get that match making pencil out, because Big Sexy is coming to find you…..and we have a few things to discuss…..”

…..and with that, Nash tosses down the microphone, as the three men head to the back, presumably looking for Vince McMahon…..as we go to a promo package highlighting the history between Steve Austin and Brian Pillman, ahead of their upcoming WWF Championship Match at Wrestlemania…..

Rating: A*

WWF Intercontinental Championship Match

Chris Jericho (c) (w/Test) vs. Matt Hardy


…..and when we come back, it’s time for Chris Jericho’s first official outing as the WWF Intercontinental Champion, as he puts his newly won gold on the line against Matt Hardy…..

…..Jericho might have been Mr. Confident earlier in the evening, going so far as to claim to not even know the name of his opponent tonight…..that overconfidence might come back to bit him in his Canadian Cheeks though, because Matt Hardy is no laughing matter. This young man surprised the world when he defeated the Rock in a four way match at Summerslam, to not only capture the WWF European Title, but banish the Rock from the World Wrestling Federation in the process…..he had a good reign too…..not overly long, but in terms of defences he literally took on any and all comers, before losing the gold to Jeff Jarrett…..

…..so yeah, The Ayatollah of Rock’n’Rolla should perhaps take his opponent more seriously…..

…..of course, it helps when you have a bodyguard on the outside that isn’t adverse to interfering on your behalf…..the match was great, and Hardy certainly came close to winning on a number of occasions, but as Matt scaled the second rope, Test was there to distract his attention long enough that Jericho was able to return to his feet…..and when Hardy FINALLY leapt off with a second rope dropkick attempt, Jericho caught the legs and when Matt hit the canvas, he quickly turned him over into the Lion Tamer, leaving the challenger little choice but to tap out in submission…..

Winner: …..and Still WWF Intercontinental Champion…..Chris Jericho
Rating: B

…..why let a little thing like the end of a match spoil a good time though? And as Test climbs into the ring to deliver a series of stomps to the back of Hardy’s head, Jericho just decides to keep the Lion Tamer locked on…..this is his first title defence after all, better make a good impression…..

…..the official victoriously calls for the bell again (which rings frantically, like that ever works) while ordering Jericho to break up the hold, even going so far as trying to physically force him to break the hold…..

…..probably shouldn’t have done that, what with Jericho having a bodyguard and all…..and Test quickly knocks the referee down with a Big Boot to the side of the head…..

…..help is on its way though, in the shape of Dean Malenko and Rey Mysterio, who come rushing down the aisle to make the save…..

…..Malenko is carrying that coaster with him…..sorry, I mean the retired Light Heavyweight Title belt, and as Mysterio sends Test staggering backwards with a springboard dropkick, Malenko sends him tumbling to the outside with a running strike to the skull with that gold belt…..

…..the pair turn to face Jericho, but he’s already released Matt Hardy and is halfway back up the aisle, far from pleased to see his celebration party interrupted prematurely…..

…..Mysterio grabs a microphone and tells Jericho that he (just like everyone else) is sick of hearing this Canadian Blowhard running his mouth…..and since Jericho took the Light Heavyweight Division away from them, maybe they should take the Intercontinental Championship away from HIM, before officially challenging him to a title match at No Way Out…..

…..Jericho’s confidence is already sky high, so he quickly accepts the challenge, before telling the frankly furious Test (who is about to re-enter the ring) to leave it, they’ll deal with these two next week….and then at No Way Out, Rey Mysterio will become just another Bore-Us Malenko Jr…..a Light Heavyweight Loser…..

…..let’s go to the back…..

Rating: A

…..where we find ourselves once again inside the office of Mr. McMahon…..

…..Vince is with his apparent chosen few, and as our main event is coming up next, he tells Pillman, Flair and Anderson to head out with Mankind…..not out to ringside, this is a match Mankind has to have himself (after all, he’s been inactive for almost three months) but somewhere near…..

“…..because if I can’t strip Steve Austin of the right to face MY Champion Brian Pillman, YOU can at least make sure, that Mr. Austin doesn’t actually make it to Wrestlemania in the first place…..”

…..Vince says that Steve Austin will be the WWF Champion over his dead body, and over THEIR dead careers…..because make no mistake, if by some miracle Steve Austin becomes the World Wrestling Federation Champion, he will make them rue the day that they ever put pen to paper on a WWF Contract…..

…..Pillman lets out one of his trademark cackles, telling Vince that he forgets who he is addressing…..The Nature Boy Ric Flair…..The Enforcer Arn Anderson…..and the Ruler of the Revolution (and WWF Champion) Brian ‘F’n’ Pillman……

[i]”…..and remember…..it was YOU that came to us…..we weren’t the moths seeking a flame…..opportunity knocked [i](Pillman pauses to slap his Title belt, along with Flair and Anderson’s) and we answered the call…..don’t you worry about Steve Austin…..you just worry about making sure that I only have ONE opponent at Wrestlemania…..and WE’LL do the rest…..”

…..seems like a fair enough compromise, and the four men head off about their business, leaving Vince to go about HIS business, until he’s interrupted by the sound of his office door slamming shut…..

…..Vince is a busy man, so he doesn’t even look up from his desk as he tells Pillman and Co to get down to the ring and take care of Austin……

…..although when he DOES look up, he doesn’t like the view…..because it’s a rather upset Kevin Nash (along with Hall and Waltman) that’s standing at his desk…..

…..Vince lets out a very big (and over emphasised) gulp, as Nash tells him that they need to talk, before grabbing the camera and camera man, tossing them (and us) out of the office…..and slamming the door…..

…..let’s leave them to it and head on down to ringside…..

Rating: A*

Steve Austin vs. Mankind


…..where Mankind is back in action inside a WWF ring for the first time in three months…..

…..the last time we saw this incarnation of Mick Foley was back at our Badd Blood In Your House event, where despite the fact that he beat the living hell out of Brian Pillman inside (and outside) of the demonic Hell in a Cell structure, Mrs. Foley’s Baby Boy came up short once again to the Loose Cannon (a man he has NEVER defeated) and then disappeared without a trace, only to re-emerge at the Royal Rumble, to HELP Brian Pillman defeat the Undertaker AND become the World Wrestling Federation Champion…..

…..why?…..

…..that’s a question we have yet to have an answer to…..but judging by his (admittedly still quite scruffy) Corporate Make Over, it’s clear that he is firmly in the camp of Vince McMahon…..

…..his opponent tonight is Stone Cold Steve Austin…..a man who (despite the best attempts of Brian Pillman, Ric Flair and Arn Anderson) walked out of last night’s Royal Rumble match with a one way ticket to Wrestlemania…..although if you ask Vince McMahon, it’s a destination that he’s never going to reach…..oh Mr. McMahon isn’t going to strip Steve Austin of that opportunity (heaven forgive) but he IS going to make life difficult for him along the way…..

…..and after spending three mo the on the sidelines, Mankind is VERY difficult…..but then The Rattlesnake is hardly a walk in the park himself, so as you would expect these two have a knock down drag out fight, pushing the official’s patience to its limit as they brawl in and out of the ring (with Austin even using Mankind’s fancy new tie to swing him headfirst into the ring post) generally brutalising each other…..

…..it was a great match, ruined by a screwy finish…..a finish which saw Brian Pillman, Ric Flair and Arn Anderson rush down the aisle (clearly having had enough of standing by the sidelines) and pouncing on Austin…..and that’s when the official’s patience came to an end, because he threw this one out…..

Winner: Steve Austin (by disqualification)
Rating: A

…..and these four men beat the holy hell out of the Rattlesnake, with Flair and Arn holding him in place as Mankind locks in the Mandible Claw while Brian Pillman (who seems happy to let the others do his dirty work for him) grabs a microphone from the outside…..

…..he tells Austin that the biggest mistake he ever made was winning the Royal Rumble, because all that’s done is put him right into the Loose Cannon’s crosshairs…..

“…..because just like The Undertaker found out…..and just like Mankind found out before he saw the light…..the revolution IS being televised…and you Steve Austin, are nothing more than collateral damage in the war of attrition…..an example to the unwashed masses…..a warning to the would be usurpers in the back…..that Brian ‘F’n’ Pillman is ushering in a new attitude across this sea of mediocrity, as he erodes away the banal and the belligerent…..and NO ONE is getting out alive…..least of all YOU…..”

…..Pillman says that lest this serve as an example to the Undertaker’s…..the Kevin Nash’s…..and yes, the Bret Hart’s too…..that there isn’t a single man in the back that can take this title from him…..

…..speak of the devil, here comes The Hitman himself…..along with the rest of the Hart Foundation…..

…..Bret and his group walk down the aisle coming to a halt at ringside, where Hart tells Pillman that he could care less about how much punishment they inflict upon Steve Austin (in fact, he actively encourages it) but as for no one taking that WWF Title away from the Loose Cannon, well Pillman is very much mistaken about that…..because at Wrestlemania, that World Wrestling Federation Championship is coming back to The Hitman…..

…..things get a little bit heated from there and it looks like Steve Austin might get a brief reprise, because Bret Hart (and his three team mates) are about two minutes away from an all out war with Brian Pillman (and his three team mates) at least until a voice cuts them off…..

…..that voice being Vince McMahon, who makes his way out onto the stage looking rather shaken up…..clearly Kevin Nash had quite the talk with him…..

…..he tells the men to stop what they’re doing…..he says that Bret Hart made a proposition at the start of the show, a proposition that would see the main event of Wrestlemania consisting of a three way contest between Brian Pillman, Steve Austin…..which is actually an interesting concept, because it seriously diminishes the chances of the Rattlesnake capturing the WWF Championship…..

…..BUT…..

…..he would rather Steve Austin wasn’t involved in that match at all…..so he has a counter offer…..

…..Vince says that he vowed not to strip Steve Austin of the opportunity to main event Wrestlemania, but that doesn’t mean he can’t present chances for Steve Austin to LOSE that opportunity…..

…..so at No Way Out, we will see how tight brotherly bonds really are, because should Bret accept then McMahon is making TWO matches official…..match number one……Stone Cold Steve Austin……vs. Owen Hart……

“…..with Mr. Austin’s Wrestlemania WWF Title opportunity on the line…..which means, should Owen Hart win, it will be HIM that goes on to Wrestlemania, NOT Steve Austin…..

…..but that’s only IF you Bret Hart, put something on the line as well…..”

…..see Vince says that in the interest of fairness (because he’s nothing if not a fair man) in order for OWEN Hart to be presented with this opportunity, BRET Hart has to put something on the line too…..

“…..which would mean…..after a……discussion with the Wolfpac, that at No Way Out, we will also see Bret Hart compete against Kevin Nash…..and Mr. Hart, YOUR guaranteed WWF Championship opportunity shall also be on the line…..”

…..Vince tells Bret that should he win, not only does he retain his guaranteed title shot, but he WILL be added to that Wrestlemania main event, while if Kevin Nash wins, Big Sexy has stated that he will take HIS shot on the very next edition of Raw that succeeds No Way Out…..

…..potential big win for Owen, vs a potential big loss for Bret…..the choice is up to him…..

….Bret looks at Owen for a second, before turning back to face McMahon, telling him that he has a deal…..and just like that, we have two matches set for No Way Out, and they BOTH have huge ramifications leading into Wrestlemania…..

…..not that Brian Pillman is happy about it…..loudly telling Vince that he’s out here giving out Title shots like a kid in a candy shop, but Vince tells him to quieten down…..for one, McMahon might be handing out opportunities, but he is confident that his Corporate Champion(S) will ensure that the WWF Title stays right where it is……and secondly, Brian Pillman gets to take the night off at No Way Out, because frankly, there is nobody not already competing that is worthy of facing HIS Corporate Champion……

…..and that’s when the lights go out……

…..and flames erupt from the four corner ring posts…..

…..as the Big Red Monster Kane walks out onto the entrance way (causing McMahon to almost fall over as he does his best to get out of his way) and he doesn’t stop there…..because as Jim Ross screams at us that we are out of time, he begins his march down the aisle way…..

…..although what he’s planning on doing when he gets there we don’t know…..

…..I guess you’ll just have to tune in next week now won’t you…..

…..see you then fans…..

…..and goodnight…..
