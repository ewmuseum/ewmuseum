Week 1 May, 1996

Posted 4 May 2019

Week 1 May, 1996

Hawaii

ATT: 10,000

TV: 14.21

The opens with a montage of still images from last night’s In Your House event, with black and white shots of Jerry Lawler’s busted open head, Kane stood over the fallen Undertaker, with a cross legged Mankind in the background, before finally closing on the image of the KLiQ and the Hart Foundation staring each other down.

We go live to the arena and Vince McMahon and Jim Ross welcome us to the show. McMahon tells us that due to the head wound and blood loss suffered by the King last night, that Jerry Lawler will not be joining us on commentary this evening, but himself and Jim Ross would like to wish the King a very speedy recovery.

Before McMahon can say anymore however, he is interrupted by the now familiar music of the KLiQ, as all four members make their way down the aisle, with Aldo Montoya in tow. Michaels and The 123 Kid are wearing their respective Championship belts over their shoulders, while Hall and Nash take turns polishing the gold mockingly as the group enter the ring. Michaels pics up a mic, saying that there’s KLiQ business to attend to tonight….but first of all he would just like to say….“that it’s good, to be the King” as he raises the WWF Title high in the air to a chorus of boo’s. He goes on to tell Jerry Lawler that “the old man put on a hell of a fight….wait….no actually he didn’t….he just bled a hell of a lot hahaha.” But enough about him, it’s time for more important things, as tonight, the KLiQ officially welcomes its newest member…..

“and I say officially, because this guy has been KLiQ 4 Life…..since before any of you morons had even heard the term KLiQ…..Ladies and Gentlemen, I give to you, my good friend….your good friend………but maybe not Chris Jericho’s good friend……the WWF Light Heavyweight Champion…..The 123 K……….”

Before HBK can finish Nash puts his hand over the mic and whispers in his ear. Shawn looks at his big buddy for a moment before raising his eyebrows at the Kid, mouthing “really?” To which The Kid nods his head.

“Ladies and Gentlemen, I give to you…..the WWF Light Heavyweight Champion…..the Kid who last night stepped up and became the Man…..I give to you…….Sean Waltman.”

The other four men applaud as Waltman takes the mic from the WWF Champion. He says that it’s nice to finally get some recognition around here…..and more importantly some respect….he says he’s “spent years always being thought of as the little guy…..the underdog……..the Kid! I’m more of a man than any of you losers in the crowd, with your fat girlfriends and dead end jobs will ever be…..I’m a superstar for god sake….I’m the MAN your children will look up to…..and I’m a hell of a role model…..because when I want something (he pats the gold belt draped over his shoulder) I go and get it…..and more importantly, I make sure I keep it……hey Chris Jericho, how do you like that no rematch clause now? I’m Sean Waltman……I’m the WWF Light Heavyweight Champion……and if you don’t like it……you can suck it.”

Vince McMahon fumbles over his words to quickly apologise on commentary, saying that we at the WWF certainly don’t condone that kind of language…..but he’s interrupted by Nash before he can finish, he tells the crowd not to worry, there’s more, they’re the KLiQ that keeps on giving….because just like the ladies know, Big Sexy can go on all night. He calls Aldo Montoya over and tells the youngster that when he first came to them and asked to join the KLiQ, Nash thought he was crazy……“this little guy that I jackknifed through a table, must have rattled his brains like Al Snow…..but you proved me wrong kid…..you’re not just some scrawny little dweeb…..you’re not just someone’s whipping boy…..in fact, when you came flying off that top rope a few weeks back, smashing that freak Snow through that table……hell, that was just incredible.”

Scott Hall pulls out a black and white KLiQ shirt from behind his back before taking the mic from Nash, telling the youngster that “last night Chico, I told you to earn your colours…..and HBK there, the Champ…..he says that you earned them in spades” he passes over the shirt and it’s quickly put on by an eager Montoya…..

“but Aldo Montoya? That name doesn’t fly man….from now on, you’re gonna be exactly what you are…..you’re gonna be Justin Credible.”

Again there are four KLiQ men applauding the crowning….and christening of a new member, before Michaels again takes the microphone. He asks the crowd what’s next for the KLiQ? He says they already have the top prize in the game, the WWF Championship…..Sean Waltman, he’s got the top prize in the Light Heavyweight division……he turns to Hall and Nash and looks them up and down…..“but I can’t help but notice that you two seem to be rather bare around the waist area there…..aren’t you missing something?” Nash and Hall look down confused for a second before Nash shrugs, saying “I don’t know Champ…..I mean, I’ve got my sweet KLiQ shirt on….what else do I need?” Hall leans up and whispers in his ear and a look of mock realisation breaks out on Big Sexy’s face….

“Oh yeah, that’s right…..Steiner Brothers….we told you that we were coming for your belts…..we beat you last night…..in three weeks time there’s a little Pay Per View coming up……it’s called In Your House, KLiQ Rulez…..seems as fitting place as any to take those Tag Titles from you…..so we’ll see you there boys.”

Just then the party is ruined by the arrival of the Hart Foundation….Bret, Owen and Davey walk out onto the entrance way and The Hitman has a mic. He tells the KLiQ that they might have screwed over Jerry Lawler last night…..they might have screwed over Chris Jericho, The Steiner’s and Al Snow……but the fun times are coming to an end, because the Hart Foundation want that WWF Title…..and they want it at In Your House. HBK shakes his head, saying that what they want doesn’t matter….“we’re running the place now and your days of free Title shots are over…..Its like the marquee says Bretty Boy…..KLiQ Rulez…..you want a shot at this belt, you gotta earn it…..if you three no bodies can beat three of my kick ass KLiQ buddies tonight…..well then maybe……MAYBE…..I’ll give you your shot.” Bret agrees and we go to commercial with tonight’s main event booked and ready to go.

Rating: A*

VS.

The Hardy Boys vs. The Harris Boys (w/Paul Ellerig)

It’s time for the first match up of the evening, and the Harris boys are in a bad mood following their defeat at the hands of the Legion of Doom last night. Matt and Jeff are still trying to make a name for themselves here in the WWF….in fact they have yet to pick up a win on any of the main shows so far…..it’s not for lack of trying though and McMahon on commentary claims that its only going to take one win to bolster these two youngsters confidence and set them on a win streak…..unfortunately that one win isn’t coming tonight, all be it not for the lack of trying. They nearly picked up the victory after Jeff nailed Don Harris with a spectacular swanton bomb but Paul Ellering was quick to place his mans foot on the ropes, saving the match for his team. Eventually the Harris Boys were able to flatten Matt with a big H-Bomb to pick up the victory.

Winner(s): The Harris Boys

Rating: C+

The match is over….but the Harris Boys aren’t….apparently they haven’t inflicted enough damage for their manager, as he barks orders at ringside to make them suffer…..and they do…..as Jeff also finds himself the recipient of a H-Bomb, before Ron and Don unleash with a barrage of stomps and kicks….right up until….

……The Legion of Doom run down to make the save. Before they have chance to inflict any damage though, the Harris Boys bail from the ring and up the aisle way, leaving Hawk and Animal to help the two Hardys to their feet. Ron, Don and Ellering look rather pleased with themselves…..and don’t even mind when the threats of violence are directed towards them…..but that might be because surprisingly it’s not the L.o.D shouting words of warning, it’s Matt and Jeff…..apparently they aren’t happy with the Harris Boys attitudes and are vowing revenge.

Rating: C+

In the back we see KLiQ members Kevin Nash, Scott Hall and Sean Waltman walking through the hallways laughing and joking with each other when they come across the desolate looking figure of former Light Heavyweight Champion Chris Jericho. The youngster is sat alone on a sound equipment box, staring at the floor. Waltman comes to a halt, telling his buddies to “look at this sorry looking mess…..it’s my old pal Chris Jericho…..how’s that no rematch clause feeling now pal?because it’s feeling pretty good to me.” Jericho slowly raises his head and takes in the scene in front of him, before telling Waltman that somehow, someway, he’s going to get that Championship back sooner or later…..and it won’t matter how many KLiQ members he brings, next time he’ll be ready. Waltman just laughs, telling him that there isn’t going to be a next time genius….no rematch means exactly that…..which is why he’s off to the ring now, where he can watch the crowning of a new number one contender to his title…..and spoiler alert….it’s NOT Chris Jericho.

With that the trio laughs again, before making their way out into the arena, where presumably they will be watching the next match up between Jerry Lynn, Rey Mysterio and Hakushi. They each pull up a chair at ringside and await the first participant….but who comes out? Certainly not a Light Heavyweight….

……it’s WWF President Gorilla Monsoon. He makes his way down the aisle and into the ring. He first of all addresses the KLiQ members on the outside, stating that their actions as of late have been diabolical….unsurprisingly they don’t care, they just sit their wriggling their fingers in mock fear…..Monsoon is unimpressed, telling them that they may want to listen to this, as he calls Chris Jericho to the ring. It doesn’t take long for his request to be answered as Lionheart walks out with a confused look on his face. He joins Monsoon as the KLiQ look on suspiciously from the outside. Before telling the crowd that

“Last night at In Your House, this man was robbed of the WWF Light Heavyweight Championship…..and while I COULD simply strip Mr Waltman of his ill-gotten gold, this is the WWF and our referees decisions are final…..therefore, the Light Heavyweight Title shall remain where it is……but the no rematch clause……..”

Before Monsoon can continue Waltman is up like a shot, he tells Gorilla not to get any ideas….that was an official WWF legally binding contract, that clearly states that no man named on it shall be entitled to a rematch as long as one of the other men holds the title…..and if Monsoon tries to over ride it then Waltman will sue both him AND the World Wrestling Federation for everything they have.

Monsoon tells Waltman that he didn’t let him finish……“the no rematch clause stands as well………HOWEVER…….as you so rightly pointed out, the contract states that no man named on it will be entitled to a rematch as long as one of the other named party’s are champion…….the names stated on that contract…..were Chris Jericho…..Scorpio………….and the 123 Kid…….and YOU sir…..you are not, by your own declaration…..the 123 Kid……the current Light Heavyweight Champion is Sean Waltman…..and he wasn’t named ANYWHERE on that contract…….THEREFORE, the no rematch clause…….is invalid.”

Waltman is furious at this term of events, screaming that it’s bull###t, to which Monsoon replies that “no….it’s called a loophole….just like the KLiQ used in the WWF Title match last night……” Jericho is ecstatic at the news, shouting at Waltman that he’s gonna get his belt back, when Monsoon interrupts him…..he tells Jericho “not so fast, because it IS a loop hole, the rematch isn’t automatic and this next match between Jerry Lynn, Rey Mysterio and Hakushi WILL crown a new number one contender…..if you want a shot after that…..well you’re going to have to earn it.”

Jericho says that he is happy to earn his shot, because he knows, not only will he earn that rematch…..he’ll win back his title too. Waltman is less happy, warning Jericho that he’ll never be the Champion again…..and if he gets in the KLiQs way, it’ll be the last thing he does….before He, Nash and Hall storm to the back, apparently losing interest in the next match after that revelation.

Rating: B+

VS. VS.

WWF Light Heavyweight Championship #1 Contenders Match: Three Way Dance

Jerry Lynn vs. Rey Mysterio vs. Hakushi

After witnessing the first three way dance featuring elimination ruled last night at In Your House, we are treated to another one here tonight. Mysterio made quite the splash in his debut a few weeks ago, where as Jerry Lynn and Hakushi have been floundering as late….not that either man is any less capable than Mysterio…..quite the opposite with Lynn, who is pound for pound one of the best workers here in the Light Heavyweight division. The three men start off fast and hard, each throwing caution to the wind due to the high stakes involved in this one. Hakushi demonstrates a particularly aggressive approach, obviously looking to finally break out and make a name for himself here in the WWF, however he gets caught with a school boy roll up by Mysterio and is the first man eliminated.

The two remaining competitors continue battling as Hakushi rolls to the outside, slamming the ring apron in frustration….seeing the chance to finally win Gold here in the WWF slip through his fingers has obviously angered him. Back in the ring, the two competitors remaining aren’t the only ones feeling the pain that such a fast pace of the match causes, as the official gets knocked down after both Mysterio and Lynn collide with him simultaneously. Rey hits a hurracanranna/pin combination, which should have been enough to secure the victory, but the official was still out of position, missing the pinfall……one man who wasn’t out of position however was Hakushi, who seeing the referee down, rolled back into the ring and sprayed Mysterio in the eyes with a mouthful of green mist. Oblivious to the interference, Lynn rolls Rey up in a tight small package and with the referee finally back in action, picks up the victory….and the Number One contenders spot.

Winner: ….and new #1 Contender to the Light Heavyweight Championship…..Jerry Lynn

Rating: C-

Jerry Lynn spots the fact that Mysterio’s mask is a mess of green mist and helps him to his feet, checking that he is ok. Mysterio runs at his eyes with a towel that the official provides and assures Lynn that he’s fine. Satisfied with that the new number one contender heads to the back…..which is when Hakushi re-enters the ring……and again sprays mist in the face of the little luchador. He slams Rey to the mat and heads up top, delivering a big moonsault for good measure before screaming at him in Japanese and heading to the back.

Rating: D-

The cameras head to the back where we see Bart Gunn walking the halls, he’s clearly looking for Billy, opening doors and shouting out name. We turn a corner and at the far end of a corridor we catch a glimpse of what appears to be Billy Gunn deep in conversation with Mr. Perfect. Bart shouts out to his partner, before shouting that he’s been looking for him everywhere, but before he can catch up with him Perfect has placed a hand on Billy’s shoulder, ushering him away and out of view. We follow Bart down the corridor but when he arrives at the corner both Billy and Perfect are nowhere to be seen. Bart kicks the wall in frustration with a “goddamn it” before walking off in the opposite direction, leaving the camera man without a subject….at least until this pair come walking into view…..

…..clearly in the middle of a conversation, Sunny appears to be almost berating Helmsley, telling him that “I want the Intercontinental Championship Hunter…..and you know full well that what Sunny wants, Sunny gets…….you know what you have to do.” Helmsley does not look in a good mood, telling his manager that yes, he knows exactly what he has to do, before finally noticing the camera there and shoving it out of the way to continue down the hall.

Rating: B-

Back in the arena and the lights go out….followed by a loud GONG! The crowd goes mad at the arrival of the Undertaker…..at least until a blaze of fire erupts from the corner posts and the lights turn on, but now a deep shade of red….as Jim Cornette makes his way to the ring with Kane and surprisingly Mankind in tow. The trio climb into the ring, Cornette with his ever present microphone in hand, where he proceeds to address the crowd. He tells them that last night, his Doomsday machine Kane completed two promises…..one made to Jim Cornette and one made to himself…..for Jim Cornette, he took that smelly, sweaty, sushi eating slob Yokozuna and drove him to the mat, before almost breaking the mans giant leg……but for himself, he finally achieved the dream of a lifetime…..

“and I’m not talking about no story-tale Shawn Michaels boyhood dream trash….I’m talking about a LIFELONG dream….the kind of dream that shapes a mans soul and burns him from the inside out….you people come out here, week after week, night after night, and you cheer the Undertaker like he’s your hero….like he deserves your admiration…..well he doesn’t…..this man deserves your cheers, this man deserves all the plaudits…..this man….the man mountain Kane…..he’s the real hero…..but you’ll find that out for yourselves soon enough……that’s IF the Undertaker ever shows his face around here again.”

Cornette laughs before passing the microphone to Mankind.

“Oh, I hope he does Uncle Jimmy….see Deadman, you got just a little taste of what I have in store for you last night…..you got a little glimpse of the darkness inside me head on and it blinded you like the brightest light….that’s the problem you see…..how can you face down my darkness….when you can’t even accept your own…..that’s the difference between you and me Undertaker…..I wear my darkness like a badge of honour, while you…..you hide yours away and hope that it stays gone……but it didn’t stay gone…..it’s here to stay…..and it’s seven foot tall and clad in red and black……please come back Undertaker……please……we’ve only just begun the fun…..we’ll be waiting for you…..Have a nice day.”

With that the twisted trio leave the ring and head back up the aisle way. They’re just about to exit the stage when…..

…….two of the recipients of Kane’s wrath last night come bursting out of the back dressed in street clothes. Phatu makes a beeline for Mankind, while a clearly limping Yokozuna takes out his frustrations on Kane. The element of surprise gives the pair an early advantage, as they hammer the dark duo with stiff blows, but Kane and Mankind soon fight back and before long the four men are brawling in the aisle way. Security is out to break it up…..then more security…..then even more…..all in all it takes almost a small army to keep the two pairs of men away from each other….but not before Mankind, who’s no stranger to pulling his own hair out, rips a clump out of Phatu’s long mane while McMahon screams that we are going to commercial.

Rating: B-

VS.

Billy Gunn vs. Justin Bradshaw

We are back from commercial and it’s match time here on Raw, as Billy Gunn goes one on one with Justin Bradshaw, after defeating the Texas Long Rider’s partner Barry Windham last week….all be it by dubious circumstances. Speaking of Windham, he’s out to accompany his partner, unlike Gunn who again comes out solo. While Billy would usually have the crowd on his side in this one, it’s difficult to tell who to cheer for, because Gunn is not wrestling his usual match, he’s far more aggressive than usual and doesn’t seem averse to….let’s say “bending” the rules….but then neither does Bradshaw, and he has the advantage of having Windham at ringside. In the end though it’s not Bradshaw, Windham or Billy that ushers in the finish…..it’s Bart Gunn. Because after the second ref bump of the night….and after Billy has nailed Windham with a steel chair, putting him out of commission, he turns on Bradshaw to inflict the same damage. Which is when Bart comes running down the aisle….he snatches the chair from Billy’s hands and tosses it to the mat, telling him that he doesn’t need to do that to win….and if he hadn’t been avoiding him all night then he would have already told him that. Billy shoves his partner, telling him to mind his own damn business, which is when Windham (who’s finally come to his senses) drags Bart to the outside, just as Billy turns around, only to be greeted by a huge Lariat….one, two, three….this ones over.

Winner: Justin Bradshaw

Rating: C+

The Long Riders head to the back, leaving Bart to try and rouse Billy. He succeeds and the fallen Gunn slowly regains his senses…..only to realise that it’s not his music playing….and it’s kind of his partners fault. He’s quick to his feet and yelling at Bart, accusing him of sabotaging his career, shoving him hard in the chest. Bart has had enough of being pushed around and we have soon descended into a shoving match, which is only halted when Mr. Perfect of all people comes running down to the aisle and pulls Billy away, whispering in his ear. It’s unclear what he said but it obviously did the trick, because Billy turns and heads to the back, with Perfect muttering words of wisdom all the way.

Rating: C+

We come back from commercial to find the entire Nation of Domination stood in the ring. Faarooq has a microphone and proceeds to tell the crowd that last night, he showed Ahmed Johnson that it doesn’t pay to cross the brotherhood. He says that there is a fine line between bravery and stupidity and that an intelligent man will always beat a brave man, hands down. He tells Ahmed that he’s got all the potential in the world, he just needs the correct mind to help him harness that potential….he says Faarooq is the right mind….the Nation is the right environment….so come on down and claim your spot.

Ahmed does indeed come down….but he says that the only thing he’s going to claim….is that Intercontinental Championship around your waist…..he says that the Faarooq didn’t beat him last night….the Nation beat him…..to which Faarooq replies “I AM THE NATION.” He tells Ahmed that if he’s not going to do the right thing and join the winning team…..“then I’m done with your punk ass….get the hell outta here.” Ahmed doesn’t look like he has any intention of leaving though…..which is when….

…..Hunter Hearst Helmsley and Sunny make their way out. He looks Ahmed up and down in disgust, before turning his attention to Faarooq, claiming that if anyone around here deserves a shot at that title, then it’s Hunter Hearst Helmsley….“I’ve been asking for a shot at the Intercontinental Championship for months…..I’m fed up of asking……so now I’m telling…..Give. Me. A. Shot……”

…..oh look, another one……this time it’s Marc Mero that’s out. He says that his memory might be playing tricks on him here….but as far as he know, out of himself, Ahmed and Helmsley….he’s the only one to pick up a victory last night….so if anyone deserves a shot around here, it’s him. Ahmed tells Mero that he’s only been in the company for a heartbeat and to step back, before he’s put back….that results in a shouting match between all the men….it’s getting a little chaotic….

……this ring is getting rather full…..now Monsoon is out to calm things down. He says that Ahmed has a legitimate claim to a rematch after the outside interference last night…..Helmsley, despite his attitude and defeat last night, has an impressive enough win loss record to argue that he deserves a shot……and if Helmsley deserves a shot, then surely the man who pinned him last night deserves a shot as well…..so this is what we’re going to do…..Faarooq…..since you like to utilise the Nation and exploit the numbers game so much….I’m going to give you some numbers……one, two, three, four, he says, pointing to each man in turn…..In Your House, it’s going to be a Fatal Four Way…..for the WWF Intercontinental Championship…..oh…..and to keep those numbers fair…..the rest of the Nation are barred from ringside.

Rating: B-

VS.

Tatanka vs. Steve Austin

Match time…..and Tatanka is looking to pic up some momentum following his defeat at the hands of Sid last night…..while Austin…..Austin just wants to wrestle. It’s a decent match, nothing special but nothing horrible either…..and despite Brian Pillmans insistence that Austin is a much better fighter than wrestler….it’s the wrestling that gives him the victory, because as Tatanka comes off the top rope with a Tomahawk Chop, Austin sidesteps, spins behind…..locks in the Million Dollar Dream…..and sends Tonto to sleep.

Winner: Steve Austin

Rating: B

As Austin is celebrating his victory we are treated to a split screen of Austin making his way to the back…..and Brian Pillman sat with Bret Hart IN the back. We can’t hear what’s being said but they were obviously watching Austin’s match on the monitor. McMahon questions what Pillman is doing with the Hitman but Jim Ross is quick to point out that Brian Pillman and the Hart Family go way, way back….with Pillman actually being a trainee of Stu and the rest of the Harts back in the Dungeon, before finally being given his first break in the Harts Stampede promotion. Vince says that considering that the main event is up next, Bret Hart might be better concentrating on that instead of reminiscing with Pillman…and with that we take our final commercial break of the evening.

Rating: B

VS.

The Hart Foundation vs. The KLiQ

We’re back and just in time, because that screeching guitar signals the arrival of the Hart Foundation…..and more importantly it signals the first time that Bret and Owen have teamed up since their ill fated attempt to win the WWF Tag Team Title at the Royal Rumble 1994 almost two and a half years ago…..a match which kickstarted their 28 month feud….hopefully their first match back together doesn’t have the same result.

The KLiQ contingent are out next and it looks like they are sticking with a winning formula as its Kevin Nash, Scott Hall and the newly minted Justin Credible making their way to the ring….along with Sean Waltman at their side. The match gets underway and the crowd are on fire at the prospect of seeing the Harts together again, in fact when Owen tags out to Bret the roof nearly shakes the emotions are that high.

Obviously the Harts are up for this one and they unload on the KLiQ members one by one, until Kevin Nash is able to shut down Owens offence with brute force, giving the advantage to his team. Much like at In Your House, Nash, Hall and Credible work together as a unit to isolate the younger Hart brother. At one point Hart is able to crawl over and make the tag to the British Bulldog but the official waved it off as he didn’t see it due to a well timed distraction by Waltman on the outside. The KLiQ try to utilise the same tactic later in the match, as Waltman again hops onto the apron to draw the attention of the referee, this time however Owen is able to send Scott Hall crashing into him, before diving over and making the super hot tag to his big brother Bret.

From there pandemonium breaks out and the action is everywhere as all six competitors begin to brawl. The Bulldog and Nash break off from the group and find themselves on the outside, where they proceed to beat the holy hell out of each other. The official attempts to prevent Big Sexy and Big Bulldog from killing each other. Unfortunately it also leaves the competitors in the ring without anyone to uphold the rules. This means Waltman is able to hop into the ring, this time with his Light Heavyweight Championship in hand, ready to nail Owen in the back of the head…..fortunately for the Harts….

…..Al Snow is out, obviously still feeling salty from Waltmans interference in his match last night. He rolls into the ring and disposes of the former 123 Kid over the top rope. Credible charges the newly arrived wrestler, but he’s caught with a kick to the gut and a Snow Plow for his troubles. In the meantime Bret slams Hall hard to the match and the two Hart Brothers exchange a look….before locking on duel Sharpshooters on both the Bad Guy and Credible. With Nash preoccupied with the Bulldog and Waltman kept at bay by Snow, Hall and Credible have little choice but to simultaneously submit giving the reunited Hart Clan the victory.

Winner(s): The Hart Foundation

Rating: B

Owen and Bret release the holds and Hall and Credible roll to the outside, where along with Nash and Waltman the back up the aisle way, while the Hart Foundation and Al Snow celebrate in the ring. The KLiQ come to a halt at the entrance ramp and the WWF Champion Shawn Michaels joins them….but he doesn’t look happy with the result. He tells Bret that “Ok Hitman…..kind of a cheap way to win with your new little buddy there interfering, but if you’re happy with that sort of victory then who am I to judge……you want your rematch, that’s fine, because at In Your House, KLiQ Rulez…..it’ll be history repeating itself, because just like at Wrestlemania it’s gonna be the Heartbreak Kid Shawn Michaels, kicking Bret Harts Canadian ass.”

Bret picks up a microphone while Owen and Davey pat him on the back, telling him that he’s gonna make Shawn scream when he locks in that patented Sharpshooter. Bret tells Shawn that they’re right…..and nothing will give him greater pleasure than to hear Michaels crying out in agony, locked in the Sharpshooter…….but it won’t be me that’s making you scream……it’s going to be my brother, Owen Hart.

Owen doesn’t register what Bret has said at first, but when he does a look of surprise breaks out in his face, that’s soon replaced by a huge smile when he realises that his brother has sacrificed a shot at the World Title so that Owen can have a shot. Michaels also looks surprised for a moment, but soon composes himself and becomes his usual cocky self. He says that whether it’s Bret, Owen, Bruce, Stu, Helen…..it doesn’t matter….because there’s not a single one of the Harts that he can’t take out with one Sweet Chin Music.

Rating: A

Final Rating: B

Final Thoughts

It was a slightly disappointing show this week….not from my point of view, I thought it was great, but the feedback was definitely more split than usual, but then I guess that’s the price you pay for trying to build up newer stars…..the Hardys, Rey Mysterio, Jerry Lynn, none of them carry much stock with the fans just yet…..and if I’d told you a month ago that both Avatar and Aldo Montoya would be featured predominantly in the main event of Raw, you’d probably think I’d gone mad….I just have to remind myself that with time their face value will rise…sooner or later the ends will justify the means…..I hope.

Week 2 May, 1996

Posted 4 May 2019

Week 2 May, 1996

The Fleet Centre

ATT: 17,807

TV: 14.44

The show opens and we go straight to the commentary table, where Jerry “The King” Lawler is back following his sabbatical last week. Present as ever are Vince McMahon and Jim Ross, both of whom welcome the King back, before McMahon tells us that before we go any further, they have some footage recorded earlier in the day that needs to be shown.

Final Thoughts

It’s been a pretty quiet week to be fair, the show went off without a hitch (which is always a bonus) and even with the newer faces being heavily focused we didn’t take too much of a hit. The new gimmicks for Phatu and Yoko went down pretty well. Rikishi was floundering a little bit in the midcard and I hated that “making a difference” garbage that he was doing…..and as for Yoko, while he still sees himself as main event caliber, I don’t….he does still have some value though, so I’m hoping that this Sons of Samoa team takes off. One team that is definitely taking off didn’t even feature on the show, nor are they a team…..but Kane and Mankind teamed up in a dark match before the show, just too see how they meshed ahead of the PPV…..their chemistry as a team was through the roof. Everything that they did just flowed like they had been teaming for years, I’m not going to lie, I’m pretty happy with that……the same can be said for Steve Austin and The Bulldog, they were another pair who just clicked when they faced off against each other. To be honest it was a bit of a throwaway match used to build heat towards the Bret and Austin match, but Steve and Davey might end up working a programme if their future matches flow like this one……the same however can not be said for Bob Holly and Jesse James. Their match was a mess, missed spots, timing all over the place, miscommunication…..it’s safe to say that they don’t have a future as opponents. He says that maybe he should take next months King of the Ring event off, because the Challengers are getting a little thin on the ground……

……which surprisingly brings out the former WWF Tag Team Champions, the Steiner Brothers. Rick and Scott remain at the top of the entrance way (even the Steiner’s aren’t stupid enough to get in that ring right now) telling Michaels to shut his damn mouth, because they’ve got something to say to Hall and Nash. HBK is far from amused at being interrupted….and even less amused at being told to shut up, but The Steiner’s continue regardless. They tell Hall and Nash that the only way they beat them last night, was with the help of a baseball bat and if they’ve got any balls, they will give them a rematch for those Titles right now.

Nash takes the stick, telling the Steiner’s that they need to calm down and hold their horses….

“you can’t just go around demanding title shots….there’s a lot of other teams around here that deserve a shot too….I mean, you’ve got those two up and comers, Pat and Seth, The Hardly Boys…..there’s the Godwinns, Henry and his idiot cousin have been lighting it up around here….there’s those two fat Samoans….I mean, come on……you’re at the back of a very long line…..”

…….apparently the WWF President Gorilla Monsoon has something to say on the matter, as he walks out from the back. He tells the KLiQ that he has an answer to all the questions….saying that the Steiner Brothers will NOT be challenging for the Tag Team Titles at King of the Ring…..but Shawn Michaels WILL be defending the WWF Championship. He says that this year, the King of the Ring Tournament is one of the biggest and most important so far…..because not only will the winner have the privilege of being called the King of the WWF, he will also have the opportunity to Challenge the WWF Champion, whoever that may be, at July’s In Your House event. Because of the importance of this years tournament, 32 men have been drawn at random to take part…..and you Rick Steiner……your name was drawn, you’re one of the 32.

Scott pats his brother on the back, pleased that he has this opportunity….while Michaels points out that in that case, everyone’s taken up with the tournament…..so the Champ gets the night off. Monsoon tells him that no, the WWF Champion does not get “nights off”….he tells him that he will be defending that Title, against a man who was not lucky enough to be picked for the King of the Ring Tournament…..“and that man……is Scott Steiner.” This time it’s Ricks turn to congratulate his brother, while HBK looks on from the ring, clearly less than impressed at this news.

Rating: A*

King of the Ring: 1st Round Match

Marc Mero vs. Scorpio

The first match of the evening is a Ring of the Ring first round match. The luck of the draw has Marc Mero facing off against Nation member Scorpio. This is a decent little match, with both men unleashing their respectively unorthodox high flying manoeuvres. McMahon informs us that not only have all members of the Nation been entered into this years King of the Ring, but Waltman, Credible and Helmsley from the KLiQ have as well, giving us the potential of stablemate having to face stablemate……Scorpio doesn’t have to worry about that though…..because a Shooting Star Press from Mero ensures that he won’t be facing anyone else in this King of the Ring Tournament, because he’s outta there.

Winner: Marc Mero

Rating: B

Following that victory for Mero, we head to the back where Dok He drop is standing by with the Legion of Doom and the Hardy Boys. They talk briefly about the upcoming match and the fact that the Hardys are ready to prove themselves against the biggest teams that the WWF have to offer. Hendrix then asks about the Hardys chances in the King of the Ring tournament, as well as the L.o.D’s thoughts about not being in it. Matt and Jeff both say it’s a great opportunity and hopefully they will be able to translate that opportunity into a title shot. As for the Legion of Doom…..well……

BESIDE EVERYTHING?……DON’T BOTHER ANSWERING, BECAUSE I’M GOING TO TELL YOU…..IT SEEMS TO ME THAT WHEN KEVIN NASH AND SCOTT HALL WERE LISTING TAG TEAMS EARLIER, THEY LEFT SOMEONE OUT…….THATS BECAUSE THEY KNOW THAT THE MINUTE THEY STEP INTO THE RING WITH ME AND ANIMAL, THE ONLY KLiQ THEY’LL BE HEARING, IS THE CLICK OF THEIR ARMS BEING SEPARATED FROM THEIR SHOULDERS AND THEIR NECKS BEING SNAPPED LIKE DRY TWIGS…..SOMEDAY SOON, THE LEGION OF DOOM WILL BE COMING FOR HALL AND NASH…..AND WHEN WE DO……UHHHHHH, WHAT A RUSH!”

Rating: B-

VS.

The Legion of Doom & The Hardy Boys vs. The Harris Boys & The Nation of Domination

It’s time for the only tag team match of the evening…..an eight man tag non the less. This all stems from the Hardy’s attempt to break out of “enhancement” (they were jobbers, let’s not sugar coat it) level and finally be seen as credible contenders….unfortunately they have yet to pick up that elusive win, although they get closer with each passing week. Tonight is a big ask though…..a very big ask in the case of Nelson Knight…..but their one saving grace is the fact that their partners are the former WWF Tag Team Champions, the Legion of Doom.

This match was……well it was a match…..wasn’t good, wasn’t bad…..it just was. The L.o.D and the Hardys started strong, before the heels were able to isolate Matt and take control of the bout. They punished the youngster, but Matt just won’t die (hmmm) he takes a hell of a beating but is finally able to make the hot tag to Hawk. The end came quick and fast from there. Nelson got bundled to the outside where Matt and Jeff were brawling with the Harris Boys, while Animal lifted Bobby Knight up on his shoulders for the Doomsday Device……but that wasn’t it, because with Knight down, Jeff rolled back into the ring and climbed to the top rope, before flying off with a beautiful Swanton Bomb…..and three seconds later, the Hardys finally had a win under their belt.

Winner(s): The Legion of Doom and Hardy Boys

Rating: B-

As the LoD and the Hardys make their exit, we cut to the commentary table, where Vince McMahon talks about the situation that occurred between the Smoking Gunns last night, telling us that Bart Gunn is still in the medical facility, he has still got feeling in his extremities, but there is some serious neck issues that will require further examination….he says that we aren’t sure if or when we will see Bart Gunn again, but everyone here at the WWF certainly wish him a speedy recovery……

……which is exactly when these two gentlemen make their way down the aisle. Billy certainly looks different than the last time we saw him, gone are the cowboy apparel, the denim, the cowboy boots….instead he is wearing a more traditional wrestling attire, thigh length lime green trunks, knee pads and wrestling boots. Perfect has a microphone with him, and tells McMahon to shut up. The pair get into the ring, where Mr. Perfect says that last night Bart Gunn got exactly what was coming to him. He’s been holding this man back for three years now….well no more…..no more Smoking Gunns…..and no more talking about the lesser Gunn…..we don’t care about his health, his prognosis or his future……

“it’s time to stop talking about the Bitch…..and start talking about the Bad Ass.”

He says that the future starts next week, because Billy Gunn will be facing off against Rikishi, in his first step on the road to wining the King of the Ring Tournament…..and the first step in his path to glory. Billy takes the mic and runs down Bart, telling him that all that pain he’s feeling in his neck, is nothing to the pain that Billy’s back has been in all these years from carrying his no good ass to title after title. He says from now on, the only titles he’s going to be winning are going to be for himself, before telling Bart to “enjoy early retirement……Partner.”

“Hi Ho, Hi Ho, it’s to the back we go, with a Dok Hendrix and a Jericho, Hi Ho, Hi Ho, Hi Ho, Hi Ho” (Sorry) We head to the back where Dok is with Chris Jericho. Hendrix says that Lionheart is a man trying to do three things at once here in the WWF, he wants to regain the Light Heavyweight Championship…..win the King of the Ring…..and now he has the advances of Goldust to worry about…..is he not concerned that he’s spreading himself a little bit too thin? Jericho tells Dok not to worry, there’s plenty on his plate but he’s only worrying about one meal at a time, tonight it’s Justin Credible and the King of the Ring qualifier……the Light Heavyweight Championship will come in time…..and as for Goldust, he told him last night that he would be waiting for him, but no ones seen hind nor fake gold hair of him…..so tonight, I just have to go out there and do exactly what I did last week….and that’s beat Justin Credible.

Chris Jericho vs. Justin Credible

Credible is looking for revenge after his defeat last week at the hands of Jericho, the random draw has placed them against each other here…..with a possible date with Credible’s KLiQ cohort Sean Waltman on the cards for the winner, should Waltman make it past Al Snow later tonight.

As far as this match goes, well it goes pretty much the same way that it did last week. Credible has come on a long way since joining up with the KLiQ…..and is certainly leaps and bounds ahead of his Aldo Montoya days, but it’s Jericho that carries the match. Last week he picked up the submission victory with the Liontamer, this week he managed to pick up the pinfall victory following a springboard moonsault from the second rope, dubbed the Lionsault.

Winner: Chris Jericho

Rating: B-

Jericho is celebrating his win, when the sumptuous figure that is Marlena comes sauntering down the aisle. She climbs into the ring and congratulates Jericho on his victory, telling him that the King of the Ring crown would look good atop his long golden locks……unfortunately, Goldust is in the tournament too…..so what he wants me to ask you…..is if you’re the King……will you let him be your Queen? Jericho looks frankly horrified at this question…..but fortunately he doesn’t have to ponder it for long, as again a golden blur comes flying into the ring behind him……

……Goldust once again comes out of the crowd, blindsiding Jericho much as he did last week, beating him to the mat and again putting him down and out with a big Curtain Call. Both Henry and his partner have been floundering of late here, both victims of the cartoony type of gimmicks that I have been trying to slowly wean out. Bulldog, me and you are in the same brackets, so I hope I get to beat your ass just like I did a couple of weeks ago……as for you two jackasses, I don’t care which one of you goes through….because I’ll be waiting on the other side and that a guarantee.”

Al on the other hand should be looking for retribution, following Waltman’s assault of Dr. Coe last night, that arguably lead to Snow losing both control of the voices and control of the match.

Final Thoughts

It’s been a hell of a busy week here, starting out with the news that little regional company Championship Wrestling USA was having serious money problems….this opened up a world of possibilities for me…..why you ask? Well thanks for asking, I’ll tell you…..I’d been badging Vince for close to four months now about the possibility of opening a small development territory somewhere….something we can use to send some of the youngsters breaking into the business to give them regular experience in front of live crowds….while at the same time familiarising them with the WWF style of wrestling, instead of that rinky dink Indy style where no one sells a damn and a guy can take six chair shots without even falling down. Needless to say Vince wasn’t keen on the idea….well I’ll rephrase that….Vince WAS keen on the idea, just not on spending the money that it would cost to open. That’s why the CWUSA deal was such a good one…..for 200 grand we were able to gain not only a development territory, but a development territory with TV exposure…..granted, only exposure in the north west, but exposure is exposure and it gives the youngsters a taste of what it’s like to be performing for TV.

Needless to say, once the ink was dry I wasted no time in picking up one or two of the more promising prospects out there. Bret Hart suggested a couple of kids from Canada, they’ve been teaming as the Suicide Blondes and going by the names Sexton Hardcastle and Christian Cage respectively…..needless to say we’ll be changing that to something a bit snappier, because both men put paper to development deals and now work for us. Likewise for another pair of future Light Heavyweights, Shane Helms and Billy Kidman. I’m hoping both of these will be stand outs for the division, but they both still have some learning to do…..and hopefully some characters to develop, because both men are a little bit bland right now.

Speaking of signings, we made a couple to bolster the main roster, a young kid that Vince is really hot on….personally I don’t see the appeal myself, kid’s got a damn hair style like the b#####d child of a pineapple and a poodle….but (to steal a line from Sunny) what Vince wants, Vince gets so hopefully this Rocky Maivia kid does something. (I don’t have high hopes, stupid kid hasn’t even been in so much as a dark match yet and he’s already managed to p##s off a few of the boys by not shaking their hands when he got to the building….luckily Pat Patterson deals with that kind of thing, making the kid buy a round of drinks for everyone)

Speaking of kids, we signed Jerry Lawler’s to a pay per appearance deal. I’ve heard mixed reactions about him so I was reluctant to offer a written deal, he’ll slot nicely into the Light Heavyweight division though…..although we will have to think of a name for him, because Jerry isn’t keen on everyone knowing that he has a son Brian’s age. I like Jerry, but he has an unhealthy obsession with youth….both in his choice of life partners and in retaining his own.

Nothing of note to report from the competition, they continue to beat us in the ratings, despite rarely showcasing their biggest stars and despite the fact that we are constantly out performing them with our product…..the only real news was that Booker T, one half of the tag team Harlem Heat has a partial Achilles Tendon Rupture that’s going to put him on the shelf for about two months, no word on what they have planned for his partner in the mean time….not that I’m too concerned, I have a tournament to book.

Al Snow and Sean Waltman had a great first round match this past Monday, their chemistry was just through the roof. I’ve been impressed with Waltman since splitting him from the Corporation, he hasn’t had a bad performance. Speaking of impressive, Goldust has picked up right where he left off before his injury and has really carved a solid spot out for himself, with his stock rising so much since the beginning of the year I think I could easily put the WWF Title on him without the fans thinking I’d lost my mind.

I don’t usually bother talking about our B Show, Superstars on here….mainly because very little happens, but with so many King of the Ring first round matches to get through, the viewers got a rare treat this week as…..

Faarooq gained a measure of revenge on Jeff Hardy for the Nation (C+)

Nelson Knight was less successful against Ahmed Johnson, who continues his battles with the NoD (C+)

And Nelson’s partner Bobby Knight didn’t fare any better against Savio Vega (C-)
