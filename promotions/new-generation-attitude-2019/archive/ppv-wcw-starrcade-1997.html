Week 4, December 1997

Washington RFK Stadium (Washington DC)

ATT: 40,917

PPV BUY: 2.24

Announce Team: Tony Schiavone, Dusty Rhodes & Bobby “The Brain” Heenan

Ladies and gentlemen, welcome to the biggest and the most prestigious show of the calendar year…..welcome to the event that set the stage for ‘the big stage’…..welcome to the originator…..the spectacle…..the trailblazer…..welcome to the granddaddy of them all……

……ladies and gentlemen, welcome……to the fifteenth annual Starrcade……

……the show opens to an explosion of pyro, before we head to the announce desk, where Tony Schiavone welcomes us to the show, along with Dusty Rhodes and Bobby “The Brain” Heenan, and Schiavone tells us that tonight, almost twelve months to the day of their original formation, Sting can finally drive a steak into the heart of the Titans of Wrestling, when he faces Hollywood Hogan in our main event of the evening, with the WCW Title on the line…..

…..but Hollywood isn’t the only Titan in action tonight…..in fact, ALL the Titans will be in action tonight, because Ray Traylor will be facing off against the mammoth Sid Vicious, the other two thirds of the Totally Awesome Trinity (Adam Copeland and Christian Cage) will be putting the WCW Tag Team Titles up for grabs, when they defend against Chris Benoit and Diamond Dallas Page…..Buff Bagwell is facing a true Icon of the sport, when he squares off against Rowdy Roddy Piper in an “unsanctioned fight”, and then there’s the powder keg…..a match that wasn’t signed until this past Monday night, but was sealed in blood…..The Macho Man’s blood…..as Jean Paul Levesque has finally found a partner to join him in his fight with Randy Savage and The Warrior, none other than his best friend (and one time free agent)……Shawn Michaels…..

…..and if that wasn’t enough, we have three other huge title matches…..a huge six man Starrcade Street-Fight…..and then there’s the match built on a friendship lost, as former friends turned bitter enemies clash, when Lex Luger faces the Giant…..and that match ladies and gentlemen……is next…..

Rating: N/A

VS.

Lex Luger vs. The Giant


…..talk about a huge match to open the biggest event of the year, and they don’t get huger than the Giant, who parts the curtain first for this one……Luger makes his way out second (and gets a great reception as he makes his way down the aisle) but Bobby Heenan’s attention is instantly drawn to the thick white tape wrapped around the midsection of the Total Package…..Bobby says that while most bullseyes are red, he can’t think of a more obvious bullseye for the Giant than that big while patch right in the middle of Lex Luger…..

“…..and if I was managing the Giant Tony, that’s exactly what I’d be telling him to do…..”

“…..oh I know you would Bobby…..”

“…..I know you know, I just told you…..”

“…..because you’re The Brian right?…..”

“…..no, because it’s obvious…..a moron could figure that out…..”

…..obvious indeed…..the Giant figured it out too and it didn’t take him long…..Luger tried his best to out manoeuvre the larger man in the opening moments of this bout, after all, it’s not often that the Total Package has the speed advantage in a match (and it’s even LESS often that he has the power disadvantage as well, but even fully fit I’m not sure that Luger could outmuscle The Giant) and he does land a few opening blows on his opponent…..but all it takes is one big knee to the midsection to cut him off in his tracks, and from there the Giant goes to work…..

…..there was a time that these two men were united in their efforts to thwart the reign of terror of the Titans of Wrestling, and had they worked together then they MAY have got the job done…..unfortunately ego got in the way for both men, as each wanted to be the man to topple Hollywood Hogan…..Lex Luger was quick to realise that personal gain was perhaps not worth losing a friend over, unfortunately the Giant didn’t agree, and he’s been seemingly hellbent on two things ever since……1) ridding WCW of the Total Package Lex Luger, and 2) finally getting the shot at Hollywood Hogan (and the World Title) that he’s been waiting for…..

…..a victory here could perhaps put Giant one step closer to BOTH goals, and he’s certainly playing for the victory, Irish whipping Luger hard into the turnbuckles, before clutching onto the middle ropes for momentum as he drives his shoulder hard into the midsection of his opponent…..there’s no reprieve either, because no sooner has Luger dropped to the mat in pain that the Giant is straight over, where he places one foot on the centre of Lex’s torso, and steps over, forcing all of his considerable weight down on his opponent…..

…..Bobby Heenan says that Luger should just give up already and live to fight another day (before the Giant leaves a big Lex Luger shaped stain in the middle of the mat)…..Tony says that there’s no quit in the Total Package though, and as Lex gets Irish Whipped again into the corner he proves that to be true, sidestepping a running splash attempt and (in a remarkable display of strength) he picks up and body slams the staggered Giant…..

…..that takes a LOT out of Luger though, and since it was such a strain on the ribs of the Total Package, Giant is actually back on his feet before his opponent…..he drags Luger off the mat by his hair and screams in his face that he shouldn’t have done that, before throwing his hand in the air as a signal that it’s time to hit the Chokeslam…..

…..before he can wrap that massive hand around the throats of the Total Package though, Luger unloads with a series of right hands of his own, finally putting some space between he and his opponent…..again Giant cuts him off with a knee to the midsection though, and sends Lex into the ropes with an Irish Whip…..Luger ducks a clothesline attempt though, and nails Giant right between the eyes with that running forearm that staggers the big man…..Luger keeps his momentum going to come off the ropes with a second forearm…..and a third….and a fourth that finally knocks Giant back into the ropes, where he falls back and gets himself wrapped up Andre style…..

…..Luger doesn’t hesitate to advance on the now defenceless Giant, and he hammers the big man right in that exposed head with right hand after right hand…..the official finally backs Lex off, but Luger is determined to pick up the victory here, and with his opponent still wrapped up in the ropes, Luger charges across the ring, looking to connect with what would surely be the final forearm shot…..

…..or it would have been…..had Giant got his big boot up in time, and that size 18 hits Luger right in the jaw…..

…..finally the official manages to break Giant free of the ropes, and Dusty Rhodes remarks that he does NOT look happy…..

“…..fe, fi, fo, fum…..that’s one angry looking Giant Tony…..he look like he about to give Luger the clubberin’ of his life daddy…..”

…..it does indeed look like that, but Luger, he’s not going down without a fight, and a brawl breaks out between the two men……it’s turned into a pure slugfest now as these two men punch and kick at each other, and remarkably, it’s Luger that gets the upper hand, finally managing to knock the Giant down to one knee, before signalling for it……he’s going to try and get the big man up in the Torture Rack…..

…..Schiavone says that had Luger come into this match fully fit, then perhaps this one would already be over……and Tony believes that a healthy Total Package would have little problem getting Giant up in the Rack, but in this condition, Luger could be making a very big mistake…..

…..he is going for it though, and he gets Giant into position, before lifting with all he has…..you can see the pain etched on his face as he tries to get the big man up…..and he does get him up (much to the delight of the crowd) but he doesn’t have him up for long, because those ribs just won’t take it, and as he drops Giant back to the mat (clutching at his midsection) Giant doesn’t bother with the theatrics, he simply wraps a hand around the throats of his opponent, and Chokeslams him right to the mat for the one…..the two……and the three…..Lex Luger is done.

Winner: The Giant
Rating: B+

From there we go backstage, where Mean Gene is standing by with the (ahem) “Television Champion” Lance Storm and his new found allies, The Amazing French Canadians…..

…..Gene says that Lance Storm has done some pretty questionable things these last few weeks and months…..and made some “in my opinion, very unwise decisions”…..Gene says that choosing to antagonise the undefeated rookie Goldberg to begin with was an unwise decision….a decision that cost him the Television Championship…..but then to take that one step further, steal the belt…..attempt vehicular homicide…..and THEN challenge Goldberg to a submission match?…..a match that Goldberg can inflict as much punishment as he wants without fear of reprisal or disqualification?…..

“…..you’ll pardon me for saying so Lance Storm, but that’s just stupid…..”

…..there are loud “Woah’s” and “Hey’s” from the Amazing French Canadians at this remark, with Oulette even grabbing Gene by the scruff of the neck…..Storm calms them down though, telling them not to blame Gene, he says that it’s not Gene’s fault, he’s simply a product of his environment…..American…..

…..Storm pats Mean Gene down and straightens out his jacket, before responding to Gene’s statement…..he says that first of all, he doesn’t know what match HE watched, but Lance Storm never lost the Television Title….it’s right here…..second of all, that “vehicular homicide” accusation, he’s already addressed that, it wasn’t him…..Canadians are the safest drivers in the world…..and thirdly, choosing a submission match was the wisest thing that Lance Storm could have done…..he says that Goldberg doesn’t know a wrist lock from a wrist watch, and he DAMN sure doesn’t know any submission holds…..

“…..and as for Goldberg being able to do as he pleases within the confines of the match, without fear of reprisal or disqualification?…..”

…..Storm pauses for a moment there to look at his two countrymen, before breaking out in a smile…..

“…..so can we…..after you gentlemen…..”

…..with that Storm and the AFC walk off…..with Lance Storm’s Television Title….defence?…..challenge?…..what ever it is, it’s next…..

Rating: B

VS.

WCW Television Championship: Submission Match

Bill Goldberg (c) vs. Lance Storm

As the competitors make their way out for our first title match of the evening, Lance Storm’s game plan in demanding this match be a submission match becomes that much clearer….because as Mean Gene pointed out, there are NO disqualifications in this submission match…..and Lance Storm isn’t coming out alone, he has Jacques Rougeau and Carl Oulette with him…..not only that, but Dusty Rhodes believes that this stipulation only benefits the challenger…..

“…..tell me Tony, do Goldberg even KNOW a submission hold baby?…..all we seen him do is come out here and smash through people…..”

“…..well that’s a good point Dusty, and I don’t believe I’ve ever seen Bill Goldberg utilise a submission hold, have you Brain?…..”

“…..he’d only have to look at me and I’d submit…..that’s a scary man Tony…..”

…..so apparently the commentators don’t think this rookie is going to know a great number of submission holds, but as they discuss the match, they clearly don’t think Bill Goldberg would ever submit either, so it’s a catch 22…..

…..the match goes much as you would expect in the opening minutes, with Goldberg completely dominating Storm…..he quite literally throws him from pillar to post and back again…..Storm tries to grab hold of a submission hold…..ANY submission hold, but Goldberg simply powers him off every time…..

…..the action spills out to the floor as Storm looks to beat a hasty retreat from the Champions offence, but it’s here that the ‘no disqualification’ rule really comes into effect…..because as Goldberg stalks Storm, Rougeau comes running up from behind and clips the knee with a chop block, before he and Oulette both drive the Television Champion knee first into the steel ring steps, before rolling him back into the ring….they do this right in front of the official too….they don’t even try and hide the fact…..

…..back inside the ring, and with Goldberg down Lance Storm looks to keep him down, as he focuses his attack on the lower limbs of his opponent…..a sound strategy remarks the Brain, who says that Lance Storm’s Canadian Maple Leaf becomes that much more effective when you have a bum wheel…..

…..speaking of that Maple Leaf, Storm does go for it on a number of occasions, but Goldberg is able to power him away before he can fully lock it on at each occasion, and on the third attempt he switches it up to wrap the champion up in a figure four leg lock (which obviously receives a loud ‘Wooooooo’ from this WCW faithful in attendance) and as he reaches back, each of the French Canadians take one of his hands, pulling back to put extra leverage on the hold…..

…..does Goldberg know any submission holds? Perhaps not, but he does know one thing…..you flip that figure four over and (for some unknown reason) all that pain and pressure transfers to your opponent…..of course, that’s nigh on impossible with the Canadians holding onto Storm…..but then fortunately for Goldberg the official has had enough of their constant interference, and he kicks both men’s hands free, leaving Goldberg to flip Storm over and now it’s the challenger crying out in pain…..

…..Rougeau and Oulette are quickly in the ring to break that up before Storm submits, and the two men proceed to deliver a two on one beating to the champion…..but after Irish Whipping Goldberg hard into the turnbuckles, Goldberg fires straight back out of the corner, taking out both men with a double clothesline, before catching the oncoming Storm right in the chin with a (non-concussion causing) superkick variation…..

…..Oulette is back to his feet…..briefly…..before he gets Speared halfway across the ring…..and Jacques finds himself lifted high overhead with a Military Press that Goldberg transitions into a Powerslam, before rolling Rougeau to the outside…..turning…..and spearing Lance Storm right out of his boots…..

…..Goldberg roars for the approving crowd, before snatching Storm up from the mat and hoisting him up in that vertical suplex position, it must be time for the Jackhammer…..

…..it doesn’t come though…..because instead of driving his opponent into the canvas, Goldberg simply holds him up there in the air for so long that even the British Bulldog would be impressed…..Storm is wriggling and kicking his legs, but Goldberg isn’t releasing him, telling Storm that the only way he’s getting down is to submit…..

…..and as the blood continues to rush to the challengers head, he’s obviously had enough, because that’s exactly what he does, desperately telling the official that he submits, before telling Goldberg to put him down…..which (as the bell rings) is exactly what the Champion does…..finally finishing that Jackhammer off as he drives Lance Storm straight into the mat…..

…..that’s 56-0 for Mr. Goldberg…..but his first “submission” victory…..and finally, a month after winning it officially, the Television Title is handed to the rightful champion, who raises it high in the air as we go to the back…..

Winner: …and Still WCW Television Champion…..Bill Goldberg
Rating: B

…..where Mike Tenay is standing by with the man who will be challenging for the WCW United States Championship next…..The Rock…..

…..Tenay says that when the Rock first came to World Championship Wrestling several months back, he seemed to have his sights dead set on Eddie Guerrero and the United States Championship…..between then and now however he got sidetracked in a war with The Forgotten, but now with all that behind him, and some three months later, he finally gets to step into the ring with Eddie Guerrero…..and he gets to do it on the grandest stage of them all…..

“…..Mike Tenay, The Rock wants to make one thing perfectly clear…..and that is the fact that The Rock is FAR from done with The Forgotten……yeah The Rock defeated Father Time Kevin Sullivan……he beat that walking doodle pad Bam Bam Bigelow…..but there’s a 900lb walking sack of monkey crap by the name of Big Van Vader that The Rock has NOT defeated, and that is SOLELY because of Eddie Guerrero…..”

…..Rocky tells Vader that he will get to him again sometime soon, but tonight….tonight it’s all about Eddie Guerrero…..

…..Rock says that he came to WCW with one agenda…..not to be treated like some mega star….not to be handed opportunity on a plate…..but to EARN those opportunities, and to prove himself against the best in the world…..

“…..and Eddie Guerrero, as much as it pains The Rock to admit it, for the last five months you have been one of the very best in the world…..you’ve turned away any and all comers…..now granted, you’ve done that by being a snivelling little piss weasel, but against all the odds you are still the WCW United States Champion…..”

…..Rock says that tonight, if Eddie Guerrero really has a set of maracas between his legs instead of an empty taco shell, then he’ll fight the Rock man to man…..no lying, no cheating…..just two men stepping into the ring to prove who is the best…..

“…..and The Rock says that he’s going to walk out there tonight and entertain the millions (and millions) of The Rock’s fans like only he can…..he’s going to walk into Starrcade as the Champion of the people…..and he’s going to walk out of Starrcade as the champion of the good old US of A…..

…..IF YA SMELLLLLLLLLLLALALAOW……..what The Rock….is…..cooking……….”

Rating:

VS.

WCW United States Championship Match

Eddie Guerrero (c) vs. The Rock


So it’s finally time for the Great One to go one on one with the Papi of Perfection…..as after three months of waiting, baiting and anticipating, it’s time for Eddie Guerrero to put the United States Championship on the line against The Rock…..

…..say what you will about Eddie Guerrero’s methods, but he has been one of (if not THE) hardest working Champions here in WCW…..he has in one way or another defeated every challenger that has come his way…..from way on down the pecking order with the Bob and Brad Armstrongs, to wayyyyyy up the pecking order with the likes of Diamond Dallas Page, Chris Benoit and Curt Hennig…..one by one they have come, and one by one Eddie Guerrero has lied, cheated and stolen his way to a successful title defence…..but after Curt Hennig pushed him to the very limit, is his luck running out?…..Tony Schiavone certainly thinks so…..

“…..Eddie Guerrero could defeat 99% of opponents Brain, without having to resort to cheating….he can lie, he can cheat and he can steal, but sooner or later it’s going to come back to bite him in the behind…..”

“…..cheat? Who’s cheated?…..”

“…..Eddie Guerrero Bobby…..”

“…..against who?…..”

“…..well, practically everybody…..”

“…..no he didn’t…..”

“…..yes he did…..”

“…..who won the matches?…..”

“…..Eddie Guerrero won the matches Brain…..but he did it by ch…..”

“…..then he didn’t cheat…..if he’d cheated then he’d have been disqualified and lost the matches…..it’s only cheating if you get caught…..”

…..can’t argue with that logic I guess…..either way, if Eddie Guerrero does decide to try and cheat tonight, then he may wind up with The Rock’s boot shoved straight up his ass…..and judging by the opening minutes of this contest, Guerrero is going to need his wrestling boots on, because The Rock is fired up…..there is no doubt that fundamentally, Eddie Guerrero is the better pound for pound wrestler of these two men, but the Rock has the people in his corner, and with that comes an adrenaline burst that sees Rock’s send the Champion bailing to the floor (indicating that he wants a time-out) to slow down the pace of the match…..

…..Rocky isn’t here to go slow however, he’s here to win, and after Eddie Guerrero cost him the opportunity to be challenging for the WCW World Title tonight (by nailing him with a steel chair during the Mayhem Match that saw Vader eliminate him) before costing him a victory a few nights later (again against Vader) The Rock is taking out weeks and months of pent up aggression on his opponent….

…..he follows the Champion to the outside (probably a wise idea since Guerrero isn’t averse to taking a deliberate count-out loss to retain his title) and he introduces Eddie’s head to the steel post, before whipping him in to he crowd rail…..with Eddie slumped up against the steel, Rocky takes a drink out of the hands of a fan on the front row, and smashes it over his opponents head (a move which looks fantastic as the liquid explodes everywhere, but is more cosmetic than offensive, because lets face it, it looks great but it’s a plastic cup…..it’s hardly going to leave a mark now is it)…..

…..the two men head back inside the ring, and Eddie Guerrero shows that side of him that isn’t afraid to stretch the rules as he goes right to the eyes of The Rock, before snatching up the US Title from ringside…..he measures up the Rock and it looks like he’s about to drive the gold right into the skull of the challenger, but the official snatches the Championship from his grip and returns it to the timekeeper…..

…..the moment the referee turns his back however, a huge grin breaks out on the face of the champion, as he turns at swings a kick, doubling the Rock over with a huge low blow…..

…..Eddie quickly rolls the incapacitated Rock up, and might have got the three count, but the official is still out of position and The Rock is able to kick out in the nick of time…..from there however, Guerrero is in complete control and he goes on the offence, clearly eager to put his opponent away sooner rather than later…..he’s not shy to go for the cover either, drawing a large number of near falls in a very short space of time, all of which Bobby Heenan approves of, telling Schiavone and Dusty that every time the Rock is forced to kick out, it takes that little bit more gas out of the tank of the challenger…..

…..you can see the cockiness and the swagger growing in Guerrero the longer he’s in control of the match, and the chants of “Rocky” from the crowd only seem to fire him up more (as he tells the “people” that he’d rather be the United States Champion than THEIR Champion anyway esè) and he begins to chip away at the Rock with quick series of moves…..an Irish Whip into the corner followed by a Monkey Flip, followed by a Dropkick to the knee that drops Rocky to a kneeling position, immediately followed with a DDT and a cover for a near fall……a series of European Uppercuts that drive The Rock back into the ropes, followed by an Irish Whip with a Huracanrana on the return, that is immediately followed up with a Slingshot Somersault Senton from the apron for another (very) near fall…..Eddie is on fire, and looks to be dead set on his way to retaining his Championship…..and as he drives the Rock into the mat with a Brainbuster and heads up top, he looks to be about one Frog Splash away from doing just that…..

…..but his overconfidence gets the better of him…..

…..and after spending far too long taunting both the Rock AND the crowd from up their on the top turnbuckle, he leaps and flys…..but it’s not a good landing…..because The Rock gets his knees up, driving all the wind out of Eddies body (and his sails)…..

…..the echoing chant of “Rocky” gets even louder as the People’s Champion looks to get himself back into the match…..

…..the two men pull their way back to their feet, and Guerrero looks to unload with a series of right hands as he tries to regain the advantage, but each one is blocked as The Rock fires back with right’s of his own, and this time it’s Guerrero that finds himself backed up to the ropes……Irish Whip from the Rock but Guerrero ducks a clothesline attempt and runs the ropes a second time, only to run straight into a Samoan Drop from the Challenger for a near fall of his own…..a second Irish Whip attempt from the Rock is reversed and it’s the challenger who runs the ropes, but it doesn’t stop his offence, as he floats over a clothesline attempt to drop the Champion with a DDT, before kipping up to his feet and positioning himself…..

…..he’s waiting for Guerrero to stand…..

…..and he’s looking for that Rock Bottom…..

…..Guerrero groggily pulls himself to his feet, but he’s going to wish he stayed down, because Rocky hooks him straight away and drives him into the mat…..

…..Rock Bottom…..

…..before making the cover for the…..

…..one…..

…..two…..

…..three…..

…..no wait, it’s not a three…..why?…..

…..because this man hooks the official’s leg at the last second…..and drags the referee out to the floor…..

…..Tony Schiavone tells us that this is Chavo Guerrero Jr…..and as you can perhaps tell by the name, he’s the nephew of Eddie Guerrero…..

“…..but the last I heard this young gentleman was working for a certain organisation up North, what’s he doing here Dusty?…..”

“…..well Tony, this is WCW…..it’s where the big boys play daddy…..but it’s also where the Cruiserweights play, and since they don’t seem to appreciate the flippy flopping up there, maybe they want to come down here and see who wins our Cruiserweight title later…..”

…..that’s some surprisingly good insight from the Dream…..after all, the Light Heavyweight division in the WWF has been retired for some time now, leaving a lot of the Cruiserweight talent with not a lot to do, and since a lot of the Light Heavyweight division were working under Pay Per Appearance deals, they can switch their allegiances pretty easily, which is why we saw Chavo buried on Raw by the Million Dollar Corporation just a few days before his WCW debut here tonight…..

…..to say that the Rock is furious would be an understatement…..and it’s a good job that Chavo is an agile little fella, because Rocky is out of the ring like a shot to chase him…..

…..he’s not going to catch Chavo though, the younger Guerrero is too quick…..so quick in fact, that as he dives into the ring to escape the irate challenger, before diving out again on the opposite side, The Rock doesn’t notice him dropping something into the hand of his uncle….

…..the camera catches it though…..

…..it catches Eddie Guerrero placing those brass knuckles on his fist too…..

…..and as the official does his best to order Chavo Guerrero away from ringside, it catches Eddie Guerrero nailing the Rock right in the jaw with that foreign object…..

…..the official doesn’t catch that though…..all the official sees is the Papi of Perfection covering his challenger…..for the…..

…..one…..

…..two…..

…..and the three…..

…..and by hook or by crook, Eddie Guerrero has done it again…..

Winner: …and Still WCW United States Champion…..Eddie Guerrero
Rating: A

…..and he doesn’t waste any time in collecting his United States Championship and heading up the ramp (which is probably a good idea, because when The Rock wakes up he’s probably not going to be a happy Rock) where Mean Gene is waiting to cut off his exit…..

…..Gene isn’t about to let the pair get away with getting a word from them, he wants to know just what that was from Eddie Guerrero, and exactly who this young gentleman here is…..

“…..because Eddie Guerrero, I think it’s safe to say that without this young man’s help, you would NOT be leaving Starrcade tonight with that US Title around your waist…..”

…..Eddie looks dumbfounded by that remark, telling Gene that he doesn’t know what match HE was watching, but in THIS match, what happened was that the Papi of Perfection proved again that he is without a doubt the best wrestler in all of WCW……

“…..in fact, after that hard fought win, Eddie Guerrero might just be the best wrestler in the world esè…..and this, this is mi familia holmes…..this is my blood…..my nephew, Chavo Guerrero Jr…..and all he was doing was coming out to watch his idol….his hero…..his favourite Uncle Eddie, retain the United States Championship one more time…..”

…..Eddie says that family is very important to the Guerrero’s, because they are all a part of the greatest wrestling family EVER…..not those stupid Samoan’s, the Guerrero’s…..and after Chavo was inspired by Eddie, he’s going to watching VERY closely to see who walks out tonight the Cruiserweight Champion…..

…..Chavo agree’s, saying that watching the performance of his uncle tonight has inspired him to go for some gold of his own…..Cruiserweight gold…..so whoever the first champion is it doesn’t matter…..because they won’t be champion for long…..Chavo Guerrero Jr. will be…..

“…..and I’m going to be the greatest champion ever Gene…..”

“…..HEY!…..second greatest esè…..”

“…..and I’m going to be the SECOND greatest champion ever Gene…..”

…..Gene goes to ask another question, but The Rock is stirring, so the Guerrero’s decide to cut this short and head to the back…..but the message is clear, the Guerrero family want to add some more gold to their lineage…..

…..let’s ‘do a Guerrero’ as well shall we, and go to the back too…..

Rating: B+

…..where Mike Tenay is standing by with the Road Warriors…..

…..he looks a little bit distracted initially, staring at something just out of shot, but he soon regains his composure as he asks the Road Warriors how they feel about their upcoming match with The Forgotten?…..

…..Animal fields that question first, screaming at the top of his lungs that if Mike Tenay is expecting this to be a “match” then he’s going to be disappointed, because this is going to be nothing more than a fight…..a street-fight……and when it comes to fighting, no one fights like the Road Warriors…..

“…..TELL ‘EM HAWK…..”

“…..WELLLLLL!!!…..YOU KNOW WHAT MIKE TENAY?……THE ROAD WARRIOR’S, WE LOVE NOTHING MORE THAN A GOOD FIGHT…..WE’VE BEEN FIGHTING OUR WHOLE STINKING LIVES AND IT ALWAYS GOES THE SAME WAY….UP FOR US…DOWN FOR YOU…AND A HARD DAYS WORK FOR THE CLEAN UP CREW…..

…..THE FORGOTTEN MIGHT THINK THEY’RE TOUGH GUYS, BUT ME AND ANIMAL, WE CAME PUNCHING AND KICKING OUT OF THE BIRTH CANAL…..”

…..Hawk says that since the dawn of time, men have been settling their differences with violence, and tonight will be no different…..

“…..FROM THE TIME THAT FIRST CAVE MAN TOOK HIS CLUB, AND USED TO LOBOTOMISE THAT DIRTY NEANDERTHAL THAT WAS EYEING UP HIS OLD CAVE LADY, TO THE DAYS OF THE GLADIATORS AT THE COLISEUM…..

…..AND SPEAKING OF GLADIATORS…..LOOK WHAT WE HAVE…..”

…..I guess that thing that had Mike Tenay so distracted at the start of that promo was this man…..this man being Mike Awesome…..although he appears to have decided to join his team mates for the evening in donning the face paint…..because we have Hawk…..Animal…..and The Gladiator…..

…..it was as The Gladiator that Mike Awesome found his first taste of success way back in the early 90’s, competing in the hardcore Japanese promotion Frontier Martial-Arts Wrestling (FMW)…..and it looks like that for THIS street-fight tonight, he’s going back to his roots…..

…..Awesome isn’t a great promo (that’s what Missy is for) but tonight, after The Forgotten took his partner out, this one is personal, and he joins Hawk and Animal in unleashing a shouty promise of revenge…..

…..he tells the trio that he hasn’t “Forgotten” what they did to Gideon Cain…..he hasn’t “Forgotten” what they tried to do to him……and (as he picks up a trash can filled with all manner of weird and unusual weapons) he hasn’t “Forgotten” to bring his own supplies…..

“…..because Kevin Sullivan, Bam Bam Bigelow…..Vader…..by the time I’m done with you three tonight, I’ll have beat you so bad that you’ll have “Forgotten” your own names…..”

“…..UUHHHHHH!!!……WHAT A RUSHHHHHHH!!!!…..”

Rating: B+

VS.

Six-Man Starrcade Street-Fight

“…..I took every barb about being too small, every ‘that doesn’t work for me brother’, every comment about being just a tag team guy….just a midcarder…..just a flash in the pan…..I took every condescending comment that the so called Titans of Wrestling made about me over the years, and when they packed up their bags and followed the money, I picked up the ball and ran…..

…..Randy Savage…..you….Hogan…..Warrior…..you might think you are the Titans of Wrestling…..and you might have called me a lot of things over the years, but a LOT of other people have called me a hell of a lot more…..I’ve been called everything from the Heartbreak Kid to the Headliner, the Main Event to the Showstopper….I’ve been called the man that innovated the ladder match…..but whatever you call me, I’m Shawn Michaels and I am THE titan of wrestling…..I am THE man and I am THE icon….but unlike all of you I am the Icon that can still go…..”

…..Michaels says that they should have left well enough alone…..all he was here to do was watch JPL’s back, but now they poked the bear, and now they’re going to pay the price…..he tells Savage that the blood he spilled on Nitro was just a taster of what he’s got coming to him…..he says that the Titans bought this on themselves…..they MADE him come to WCW…..they’ve MADE him sign for WCW…..and now, they’re going to MAKE him end the Titans of Wrestling for good…..

…..with that Michaels slams the microphone back into the chest of Mike Tenay, before storming off into the direction of the ring, as Levesque lets out an exaggerated deep breath…..

“…..I’d say someone is ready to fight Mike, wouldn’t you?…..”

…..and cracking a big smile as he heads off towards the ring too…..our tag team match is next…..

Rating: A*

VS.

Jean Paul Levesque & Shawn Michaels vs. Randy Savage & The Warrior


…..this match has gone through quite the journey in a very short time…..in the space of a month it’s gone from Jean Paul Levesque vs. Randy Savage and The Warrior, to Jean Paul Levesque and Rowdy Roddy Piper vs. Randy Savage and The Warrior……back to just Jean Paul Levesque vs. Randy Savage and The Warrior, and finally to the match that we have right now (and the match that everyone knew was coming…..right?) Jean Paul Levesque and Shawn Michaels vs. The Macho Man Randy Savage and The Warrior…..

…..the biggest question going into this, was would this even be a match…..could these four men operate within the confines of the rules of a standard tag team encounter?…..

…..well, yes…..and no…..

…..let’s just say that since Savage and Warrior are both members of the Titans of Wrestling (as is ‘The Boss’ i.e. Vince Russo) the rules were slightly more, let’s say relaxed, than with your standard match…..

…..the Titans make their way out first, both decked out in black and white (with the Warrior making his now familiar slow walk to the ring) and make their way down the aisle towards the ring, where they actually come to a halt…..they don’t climb into the ring…..they just wait there at ringside…..

…..Levesque is out next, followed by Shawn Michaels, who (despite his less than complimentary remarks about World Championship Wrestling) gets a HUGE response…..and rightly so, because not only is this his first match for WCW, it’s his first match period since way back in August, a losing effort to Kevin Nash at the WWF’s Summerslam event…..

…..Michaels comes to a halt alongside Levesque, and the two stare down towards the ring, where the two Titans are still waiting on the floor…..and almost in unison, as Levesque and Michaels begin to walk down the ramp, Savage and Warrior begin to walk up…..a walk which increases to a jog, and then a full on sprint as the four men charge at each other, meeting halfway down the aisle where a wild brawl breaks out…..

…..the bell hasn’t sounded for this one (since no one has actually entered the ring yet) so there is no danger of disqualification…..good job too, because while Levesque and The Warrior brawl their way down the ramp to ringside, Savage and Michaels brawl their way up the aisle to the entrance way, and they are using every available surface on the way to punish each other with…..both of these men have spilled blood in recent weeks, and it looks as though they are desperate to open each other up again…..

…..Levesque is a pretty powerful guy, but it’s hard to match power with the Warrior, who reverses a whip to send JPL crashing into the steel steps…..

…..Savage and Michaels have made their way over to where the announce table is set up and (as Bobby Heenan scatters like the weasel that he is) Savage whips Michaels back first into the desk, before climbing on top of the structure and coming off with a double ax handle…..Savage goes for a piledriver right there, but Michaels reverses it into a backdrop, before dragging Savage back to his feet and bouncing his head off the table a few times, leaving Macho groggy…..so groggy in fact that Michaels decides that it’s time to tune up the band, and he lets fly with some Sweet Chin Music…..Savage wasn’t THAT groggy though…..and he grabs Dusty Rhodes by the shirt, pulling him in front of him and leaving the American Dream to take the kick to the face…..Michaels pauses briefly to check on Dusty and that brief pause is all Savage needs to pick up a steel chair, and as Michaels turns around he gets absolutely obliterated with a shot straight to the head…..

…..and Shawn Michaels is busted WIDE open…..

…..Savage drops down to unleash a flurry of quick jabs to the open cut on the head of Michaels, before dragging him by his hair back over to the entrance way…..we resume some semblance of normality back at the announce desk, because as Dusty Rhodes is taken away by the medics, Bobby Heenan returns to his seat, while Mike Tenay makes his way out from the back to replace the incapacitated American Dream…..

…..Levesque and Warrior haven’t inflicted quite as much damage on one another as their tag team partners have, but they have certainly been in a war, and as Savage drags a bloody Shawn Michaels down the aisle, Warrior crashes a steel chair across the back of Levesque, putting him down, leaving Savage to toss Michaels into the ring as this one officially gets underway…..

….this match was originally set to be a handicap match, and right now it is again…..because with Levesque down on the outside, Savage and Warrior go to work dismantling the already bloody Shawn Michaels…..obviously the Warrior isn’t the greatest worker in the world, but he doesn’t have to be here, as Michaels bumps and sells like his life depends on it…..

…..Levesque makes it back to his feet and heads over to his corner, where he implores his partner to make the tag, bit when you’re in there with Randy Savage and The Warrior that’s easier said than done…..Warrior punishes the former WWF Champion with power moves and punches, while Savage is exactly that…..savage…..he scratches and claws at that open cut on the head of Michaels, drives his face into the turnbuckles, stomps, punches, and even bites at one point in an attempt to keep the blood flowing…..and just when Michaels looks about done for, Savage heads to the top rope, looking for that patented double ax handle…..

…..but Michaels shifts his position as Savage is mid flight, and catches him on the landing with an inverted atomic drop, before sweeping his legs out from under him and catapulting him so far into the corner that Savages head connects with the steel ring post…..

…..and as the Macho Man sits up from the mat, wide eyed, he too is now busted wide open…..

…..with both men now wearing the proverbial crimson mask, they slowly make their way over to their respective corners, where they tag in Levesque and Warrior…..and after standing on the sidelines for some time now, JPL is more than ready to fight…..he rushes at the Warrior, who didn’t seem to be expecting the ferocity of Levesques attack, because the former Titan completely overwhelms his one time team mate, even getting him into position for the Pedigree…..fortunately for Warrior, Savage flys across the ring to break that up, followed almost immediately by Shawn Michaels, who clotheslines Macho straight over the top rope, before crashing down on top of him with a Plancha…..

…..Shawn gets to his feet (with the blood still flowing down his face) and takes a few steps back, where he begins tuning up the band, looking for that Sweet Chin Music on the floor…..and you can either blame the blood in his eyes, or his tunnel vision that is only focusing on Randy Savage, but either way what he DOESN’T see, is the Warrior gorilla pressing Jean Paul Levesque inside the ring…..walking him over to the ropes…..and tossing him down right on top of the former Heartbreak Kid…..

…..that might have thwarted the Sweet Chin Music attempt, but it doesn’t stop the war…..and as Warrior joins in the fray on the floor, the official has lost all semblance of order here as the four men proceed to do their best to murder each other live on Pay Per View…..

…..he lets as much go as he can, but as Warrior attempts to garrotte Levesque with a camera cable, and Savage picks up a steel chair (only to have it Superkicked right into his face by Michaels) he has finally seen enough, and he throws this one one…..security can deal with it now…..

Winner(s): No Contest
Rating: A

…..except security DON’T deal with it…..at all……because the moment Doug Dillinger and his crack team hit the ringside area, the four warring men turn their attention away from each other to lay out the security team, before going right back to trying to kill one another…..

….Dillinger clearly wasn’t expecting that, and he calls to the back for reinforcements…..reinforcements which arrive in the form of more security guards, backstage personnel and even one or two referees…..who all receive exactly the same treatment as the original security detail, getting tossed around by the four men, who quickly resume their battle…..

…..Savage and Michaels are still both a bloody mess, and Levesque is attempting to bust open The Warrior by repeatedly smashing his head into the ring post, which is when Dillinger calls in the big boys, as Washington’s finest boys in blue (black? brown? I don’t know what Washington cops wear) come rushing down the aisle (handcuff’s and all) to put a stop to this near riot…..

…..even they have a hard time stopping the violence, but eventually (and perhaps mercifully) they manage to cuff each of the four men, finally separating them long enough to drag them away from the ring area….to the back…..and presumably downtown, where logic would dictate that they’re going to be spending the night in the cells…..

…..and as we go to a promo package for our main event, Tony Schiavone remarks that much like with our Tag Team Title match earlier in the evening, you’ve got to believe that Hollywood Hogan just lost two major pieces of his game plan to retain the WCW Title next…..if he is going to defeat Sting, he’s going to have to do it without the help of the Macho Man and The Warrior…..

Rating: A*

“…..ladies and gentlemen it is now time for the Starrcade main event of the evening…..tonight, we are going to witness the most anticipated match….in the history of professional wrestling……for the heavyweight championship of the world…..

…..ladies and gentlemen……are you ready?…..

…..wrestling fans…..are…..you…..rrrrrrrrrready?…..

…..then, for the thousands in attendance…..for the millions watching around the world…..from the captial city of the United States of America, Washington DC……

…..let’s get to ready to rummmmmmmmbbbbbbleeee!…..”

“…..coming down the ring at this time, coming to you by way of Hollywood, California…..he weighs in tonight at 285lbs…..he is the leader of the Titans of Wrestling, an icon to millions and a face instantly recognisable the world over…..

…..he comes here tonight to face the biggest challenge of his championship reign to date…..he comes here tonight to defend his WCW Title in front of the world…..he comes here tonight….to win…..

…..ladies and gentlemen, the reigning and defending WCW Champion…….Hollywood…..Hulk…..Hogannnnnnn. ….”

…..and here he comes, playing air guitar down the aisle to the sounds of Jimi Hendrix’s Voodoo Child, the WCW Champion Hollywood Hogan…..and if he IS at all concerned about that fact that most of the Titans have either lost tonight (or in Savage and Warrior’s case, currently out of the equation) you wouldn’t know it by the cocky look on his face…..

…..Tony Schiavone remarks that usually the Champion would come out last for these title matches, but surprisingly Hogan has used his championship position to come out first…..a fact which Heenan says is pure mind games on the part of Hollywood, looking to get into the challengers head before the match even begins…..

…..speaking of the challenger, it may well be him that’s playing the mind games…..

…..because with Hogan in the ring, the lights go out as thunder crackles through the arena….the Turnertron flickers into life and as the house lights flash giving the illusion of lightning (to accompany the thunder) a child’s voice begins to speak over a highlight package of Sting…..

“…..when a man’s heart is full of deceit…..
…..It burns up, dies…..
…..and a dark shadow falls over his soul…..
…..from the ashes of a once great man, has risen a curse…..
…..a wrong that must be righted…..
…..we look to the skies for a vindicator, someone to strike fear into the black hearts of the same man who created him…..
…..the battle between good, and evil has begun…..
…..against an army of shadows, lies a dark warrior…..
…..the prevailer of good…..
…..with a voice of silence…..
…..and a mission of justice…..

…..this…..is Sting…..”

…..apparently this man doesn’t need Michael Buffer to announce his arrival…..and as the thunder and lighting gives way to the orchestral sound that is his new theme music, Sting descends one more time (as he has done so many times in the past) from the rafters, landing directly in the crowd…..

…..a lone spotlight picks him out as he stands surrounded by a sea of fans…..expression blank, but intent obvious, because he has that black bat in hand and he’s pointing it straight at Hollywood Hogan…..

…..Hogan looks decisively less cocky now…..and as Sting makes his way through the crowd and into the ring, it’s time to ring the bell, because our main event is happening right now…..

Rating: A*

VS.

WCW Championship Match

Hollywood Hogan (c) vs. Sting


…..so this is it…..the match that we have been building to for months now, and a match that can draw its origin back twelve months, to Starrcade 1996…..

…..see I booked Starrcade 1996…..I booked the months worth of storylines building up to it as well…..now this was before I took over WCW full time, I dipped in, tried to tidy the place up a little bit and then dipped back out again until June of 97, so I know the seeds of this match were planted there, because I planted them…..

…..Starrcade 1996, WCW was a very different place…..a young upstart by the name of Jean Paul Levesque had just arrived in the promotion, and (much as Shawn Michaels has done in recent months) instantly set about running the company down for being second rate…..and to be fair, he wasn’t wrong…..attendance was down, the Giant was Television Champion, the odd pairing of Headshrinker Samu and Buff Bagwell held the Tag Team Titles, Nitro’s rating was in the toilet…..and the reigning WCW World Champion amongst a sea of stars such as Hulk Hogan, Randy Savage, Sting, Lex Luger, Ric Flair and The Warrior, was Bam Bam Bigelow…..

…..that needed to change…..a lot needed to change…..a lot DID change…..in the space of one night…..

…..see the Starrcade 96 main event was a match that underwent some significant changes in the weeks leading up to the show…..what was originally scheduled to be Sting challenging Bigelow for the WCW Title was changed to Sting and Vader challenging Bigelow in a triangle match…..a match that was changed AGAIN when Hulk Hogan was added to the match (because Vader was a regular partner and friend to Bam Bam, and essentially promised that the two of them would ensure that Bammers kept the title) to make it a fatal four way, under the assurance of Hogan that he was just there to keep Vader from becoming an issue during the match…..

…..of course, this being Wrestling, things were never going to go that smoothly…..and as he had been so many times in the past, Sting was screwed over by a man he considered a friend, Hulk Hogan did the unthinkable and turned heel….and the Titans of Wrestling were born…..

…..Sting changed from that point…..he became withdrawn…..jaded…..his bleach blonde hair gave way to a darker look…..his once colourful face paint became a blank white canvas…..and he stopped trusting…..ANYONE…..until he trusted someone again…..The Warrior…..

….The Warrior spent weeks and months trying to convince the Stinger to join him in the fight against the Titans…..begged him to reunite the Blade Runners one last time against the Mega Powers…..until finally Sting accepted…..but there was a stipulation…..Sting pins Hogan, he gets a shot at the WCW Title…..Hogan pins Sting, and Sting never gets a shot at the gold as long as Hollywood holds it…..

…..it was at the Road Wild event back in August that the match took place…..and it was on that night that Sting was betrayed YET AGAIN…..as The Warrior turned heel to join up with the Titans, and that really sent Sting over the edge…..or rather, up into the rafters…..because that was the last time Sting competed in a WCW ring (save for last months Mayhem match) until tonight…..and here we are, four months since Sting’s last official match…..and it all rests on this…..

…..save for a brief time when the title was vacated back in June, Hollywood Hogan has essentially held this championship for one year straight, and try as they might, there hasn’t been a single man on the WCW roster that’s managed to take it away from him…..of course, in that twelve month period, he’s not had to defend the title against Sting…..

…..the bell finally sounds after all the theatrics have been dispensed with, and the two men come face to face in the middle of the ring…..and from the second the bell rang to signal the start of this match, to the second the bell rang to signal the end of this match, this was practically all Sting……he completely overwhelmed the champion, working four months worth of ring rust out on the face of Hollywood Hogan…..Hogan does have a few brief periods of offence, but they are very few and far between, and with no Randy Savage or Warrior there to back him up, Hogan finds himself slumped in the corner, and the recipient of a Stinger Splash…..

…..and that leaves the WCW Champion in prime position for the Scorpion Deathlock…..

…..there was ONE member of the Titans that was victorious tonight…..and that’s Buff Bagwell…..

…..he makes his way out now and comes rushing down the aisle, trying to distract the attention of the challenger before he can turn Hogan over…..

…..but he’s not alone out there for long…..because the man who was once considered Sting’s best friend (and the man who’s been lobbying to resume that friendship in recent months) Lex Luger comes rushing out behind Buff…..spins the man around…..and lays him out with a right hand…..

…..Sting points down at Luger in recognition for the assist, before turning Hogan over and sitting back, locking in the Scorpion Deathlock…..and with Luger cheering in encouragement, and after shaking his head no for around ten seconds, Hogan finally lets out a big cry of pain…..

…..before furiously submitting to the hold…..

…..and just like that, after months and months of waiting in the rafters, we have a brand new WCW champion…..and his name…..is Sting…..

Winner: ….and NEW WCW World Heavyweight Champion……Sting
Rating: B+

…..Sting is handed the Heavyweight Championship of the world, and he hoists it high overhead in celebration, as a jubilant Lex Luger enters the ring to congratulate him…..

…..and he’s not the only one…..because the locker room empties as WCW wrestlers, face and heel alike come rushing out to celebrate the end of Hollywood Hogan’s reign of terror…..even the usually emotionless Sting looks overjoyed at the victory, and doesn’t even offer any resistance as he gets lifted up onto the shoulders of The Road Warriors, as the congratulations keep on rolling in…..

…..Sid Vicious, The Rock, The Nasty Boys, Rick Martel, Chris Benoit, Diamond Dallas Page, Rikishi, Jeff Hardy, The Pitbull’s, Jim Duggan, Brad Armstrong…..the list goes on and on as the ring continues to fill up…..and everyone is ecstatic at the crowning of a new champion around here…..

…..well, ALMOST everyone…..

…..because while the Giant DOES come out, he doesn’t go any further than the entrance way…..and he CERTAINLY doesn’t look to happy about this latest development, as he stares a hole straight through the oblivious Sting…..

…..in fact everyone appears oblivious, because even the announcers are jubilant, and as we close out 1997 with WCW riding high over the Titans of Wrestling, we close out Starrcade with a shot of Sting holding the World Title high in the air, while the words of Tony Schiavone echo in our ears…..

“…..what a event…..and what a moment…..we have just witnessed possibly the most important night in all of professional wrestling….the night that the tide turned in the favour of WCW…..the night that the Titans of Wrestling and Hollywood Hogan found out that our big boys don’t just play, they fight…..and the night, where after months of betrayal, obstacles and adversity, Sting became the WCW Champion of the World…..we’ll see you in 1998 fans…..”

Rating: A*
