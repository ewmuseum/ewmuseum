Week 1, November 1996

Arrowhead Pond

ATT: 17,774

TV: 18.46

The show this week opens up with a recap of the events that closed LAST weeks show….where Gorilla Monsoon announced that should the team of Bret Hart, Owen Hart, Steve Austin and Brian Pillman defeat the KLiQ team of Shawn Michaels, Kevin Nash, Scott Hall and Sean Waltman at the Survivor Series, then the KLiQ as a unit will be no more……..along with Shawn Michaels blockbuster announcement that should the KLiQ be the victorious team, then their four opponents will no longer be eligible to Challenge for ANY title held by the KLiQ…..including any FUTURE Championships that they should win…..before we go down to ringside, where Vince McMahon, Jerry Lawler and Jim Ross welcome us to the show.

They discuss the ramifications of the Elimination clash, before McMahon mentions that while The KLiQ may be gone come Survivor Series, Jerry Lawler may find himself gone also…..at least from this Commentary table…..but Lawler tells McMahon not to worry…..the only thing that will be gone, is Justin Credible ugly face…..because when the King beats him, he’ll be back to wearing that gaudy yellow jock strap on his head…..

….::::!!!!GLASS SHATTERS!!!!::::….

…….they are interrupted however by the sound of breaking glass…..and the arrival of now former WWF Tag Team Champions, Stone Cold Steve Austin and The Loose Cannon Brian Pillman……and they don’t look happy at all. The two men stride down the aisle with purpose, before picking up a microphone each and getting into the ring. They get somewhat of a mixed reaction, which doesn’t go down well with Pillman, who tells the unwashed masses in attendance to save their cheers for someone that wants them “……because if I wanted a braying sea of foul smelling creatures clapping for me, I’d go down to the zoo and visit the sea lions…..where I’d probably get a more intelligent conversation than you morons could offer me…..” Now the reaction is less mixed and the boo’s ring out through the arena as Austin takes the stick……

…..he starts off by stating that he’s not happy about being forced to team with Bret and Owen Hart……and he’s even less happy to hear that should he lose the match at the Survivor Series, he potentially loses the opportunity to Challenge for the WWF Championship…..

“……not that Stone Cold Steve Austin couldn’t whip all the KLiQ’s ass with one hand tied behind his back…..especially that mealy-mouthed excuse for a Champion, the Boy Toy Shawn Michaels…..and Shawn, after that stunt you pulled at In Your House…..you know as well as I do, that somewhere down the road, your ass belongs to Stone Cold……but now, before I get the chance to take that WWF Title from around your waist, I’ve got to step in this ring with a man that dislikes me, ALMOST as much as I dislike him…..and trust that Bret Hart ain’t gonna try and screw Stone Cold over? Well uh-uh…..Steve Austin don’t trust anyone……especially a little pink tights wearing Jackass like Bret Hart……”

……speaking of that pink tights wearing jackass…..here he is…..(although he’s actually wearing black tights this evening)…..along with brother Owen, as the Hart Brothers make their way down the aisle. Bret tells Austin that they are a fine pair to talk about trust……Steve Austin is the biggest rattlesnake in the WWF, and Brian Pillman already turned his back on the family that bought him into the business once……so if anyone has a right to be untrusting…..it’s the Hart Foundation. Austin tells Bret Hart that if there’s one thing he can trust, that’s the fact that if either Bret or Owen costs him a shot at the World Wrestling Federation Championship…..then Stone Cold Steve Austin will personally tear him a new one.

Owen asks Stone Cold if he’s stupid…..or just drunk……he tells him that if they screw either him or Pillman over, then all they’re doing is screwing themselves over…..if they lose at the Survivor Series then they ALL lose at Survivor Series….and NO ONE will be getting a shot at the World Title……and that’s when things get a little heated…..clearly Austin doesn’t take kindly to being called stupid…..and soon all four men are pushing and shoving each other…..about as far away from a cohesive unit as you can get……

…….which is when the video screen flickers to life and we are treated to a shot of the KLiQ, all sat in a row on steel chairs. Sunny is stood behind Michaels massaging his shoulders while the boys sit eating popcorn and laughing. The action in the ring comes to a halt once they realise they have an audience…..but HBK tells them not to stop “…..this is the best thing I’ve watched in weeks….” He tosses a piece of pop corn into his mouth before telling the four men in the ring that at this rate, they won’t even make it to Survivor Series without killing each other…….that’s if they even make it through tonight…..because tonight, the KLiQ are going to show them what a real unit looks like…..because Shawn Michaels and his brother from another mother Kevin Nash are gonna ride again…..and make it one win for the good guys….when they face Bret Hart and Steve Austin “….keep up the arguing boys…..”

The KLiQ continue laughing and throwing popcorn at the camera lens as the screen goes black and Bret and Austin stand eyeing each other suspiciously.

Rating: A*

VS.

WWF Light Heavyweight Championship Match

Rey Mysterio (c) vs. Sean Waltman

Sean Waltman really should have taken Rey Mysterio more seriously to begin with…..then he wouldn’t find himself in this situation…..his second go around of trying to regain the Light Heavyweight Championship. Mysterio has come on in leaps and bounds since winning the Championship, and no one is taking him lightly any more. Waltman comes out with back up for this one, with Justin Credible by his side…..but he isn’t able to get up to any of the usual KLiQ shenanigans, because the second he tries to interfere in the match, Jerry Lawler takes off the headset and positions himself ringside in Mysterio’s corner.

After a very good match however, neither Lawler or Credible play a part in the ending of this……well they do…..just not directly…..because after spending most of the match circling each other on the outside and trading insults, The King and Credible finally come to blows on the outside…..which predictably takes the officials attention away from the match…..

……which is when Steve Austin comes flying down the aisle and into the ring. He spins Waltman around, kicks him in the gut…..then drops him with the Stone Cold Stunner. Waltman is down and out and Austin flips him a set of double birds before rolling back to the outside. Mysterio heads up top…..and Credible looks as though he’s about to try and knock his legs out from under him, before the King plants a big right hand on him…..leaving Rey free to hit a diving splash for the three count and the win.

Winner: and Still WWF Light Heavyweight Champion…..Rey Mysterio

Rating: B

From there we head to the back…..where in a scene reminiscent of last week, we find a member of the Flock sat in front of a screen, watching the same video that Mankind was watching last week. It’s not Mankind doing the watching however….its Al Snow…..Al Snow and Head actually, who is placed beside him facing the screen, the recipient of all Al’s thoughts…..as Al reaches forward and pauses the video, pointing at the figure about to jump from the roof of their house…..

“………there…..look…….no, look properly…..(Al picks Head up and holds her right in front of the screen)…..do you see it now?…….not even slightly?…….you don’t see any resemblance at all?…….what is wrong with you, it’s uncanny……..well personally I think we need to get your eyes checked, because that is obviously……….”

…..before Snow gets to finish that sentence however a hairy arm reaches over his shoulder and turns the monitor off with a leather clad finger…..

“…..that’s obviously nothing more than a stupid kid……and YOU shouldn’t be watching that……”

Mankind is stood behind Snow, but he’s not alone for long…..as Raven and Bill DeMott walk into shot seconds later. Raven tells Snow to stop playing with that stupid head and get down to the ring…..because he’s got a match. Snow looks confused, asking against who?

“……a young Team who have bought into the fallacies and fabrications of broken down men…..believing the lies that their fragile frames are built to withstand the trials and tribulations of battle, simply because they have found success against some washed up has-beens…..BECAUSE of two washed up has-beens…..”

Snow interrupts Raven, asking him if he means the Hardy Boys? Because from what Al has seen, they’ve been doing pretty well for themselves recently…..Raven laughs at this, saying that they have too easily believed the lies fed to them by the Legion of Doom that they can hang with monsters…..

“…..but I don’t blame them…..we all eat lies when our hearts are hungry…..tonight I want you to show them that their hopes and dreams are really nothing more than despair and nightmares……I want you and Bill DeMott to make them suffer…..I want them to feel my pain……I want you to show them what a monster can do…..go…..”

Snow looks at Raven, then at DeMott, before shaking his head and turning to walk out of the room, before stopping and telling Raven not everyone needs to wallow in the darkness like him……some people just want to stand in the light. Snow looks over at Mankind, saying that some people just want to have fun…..before asking him…..

“……who’s Dude Love?…..”

…..I don’t know how it’s possible to look sheepish while wearing a leather mask, but Mankind somehow manages it…..before snapping out of it and telling Snow to just go and make the Hardy’s suffer…..before I have to do it for you.

Rating: A*

VS.

The Hardy Boys vs. Al Snow & Bill DeMott

The Hardys have picked up some pretty impressive victories as of late…..and they have been in that ring with some of the biggest men that the wrestling world has to offer…..but they have never been in there with two men as….let’s say, challenged by sanity…..as their opponents tonight. Neither Al Snow or Bill DeMott are exactly known for their mental stability. Since revealing his allegiance to Raven back at In Your House, DeMott no longer feels the need to play the meek helpless simpleton that lulled Al Snow into a false pretence…..instead he can let his true persona out…..a big, strong bull with a penchant for hurting people and a fragile grip on reality. He takes great pleasure in brutalising the two Hardy’s.

Al Snow on the other hand, while clearly eager to pick up the victory, is far more respectful to his younger opponents, much to the chagrin of his partner. This was a pretty good match and either team could have picked up the victory on numerous occasions….in the end however….it was Raven’s instructions that bought this match to a close…..because with his Masters words echoing in his ears, DeMott drops to the outside to collect a steel chair which he brings back into the ring. He raises the weapon to swing it, but before he can connect Al Snow snatches it from his hands. An argument ensues with Snow telling DeMott that they don’t need to stoop so low to win the match……DeMott disagrees, but cant do much about it when Snow rolls to the outside, taking the chair with him. Bill shakes his head in disgust, before turning back to his opponents…..where Matt Hardy is waiting with a Twist of Fate….which is enough to give the Hardys the three count, just before Snow can re-enter the ring to break up the pinfall.

Winner(s): The Hardy Boys

Rating: B-

Al Snow looks disappointed, but he shakes the hand of both Matt and Jeff, who exit the ring and begin to walk to the back. Snow helps DeMott back to his feet…..who doesn’t look happy at all. He begins shoving Al, who reiterates his point that they didn’t need chairs to win…..which is when DeMott punches him in the face. Seeing this Matt and Jeff stop halfway up the aisle and turn around….looking as though they are contemplating giving Al a helping hand……

…….which is when Raven and Mankind come out from the back with steel chairs of their own, which they smash against their un-suspecting victims. Both Hardy’s drop to the floor…..only to be dragged by their hair down the aisle and tossed into the ring. Snow tries to talk the pair out of inflicting anymore punishment, but Raven simply unfolds both steel chairs…..sitting on one…..then ordering Snow to sit on the other to watch the carnage. Al is reluctant, but he has no choice, since losing his freedom to Raven at In Your House…..and he finally sits down beside the Shepherd of The Flock…..while Mankind and DeMott dismantle the two youngsters in front of him……

…….!!!UHHHHHH…..WHAT A RUSHHH!!!…….

……..it looks as though the Legion of Doom have seen enough of the punishment that their protégés are receiving…..and the Flock scatter as they burst into the ring….spiked shoulder pads and all……the Flock scatter…..but Raven does not…..as he remains sat unmoved on the steel chair. Hawk and Animal surround him and he simply stands with a smile on his face, before outstretching his arms and telling the Legion of Doom that “…..they wanted monsters……I gave them monsters……” then slowly turns and rolls out of the ring, while Animal and Hawk check on the Hardy’s.

Rating: A

While the Legion of Doom help the Hardy’s back to their feet, we head backstage where Dok Hendrix is surrounded by a absolute sea of bulging muscles, because his guests tonight are Psycho Sid, Ahmed Johnson and the British Bulldog, Davey Boy Smith. Hendrix says that each man’s individual problems with the Million Dollar Corporation, has lead to an unlikely show of unity from this trio…..where do they go from here?

Sid says that where they go from here….is onwards to next week….and onwards to the Survivor Series “…..because next week…..it’s gonna be me and you Steiner……and while the Intercontinental title isn’t on the line…..it’s not gonna stop me from kicking your ass little man…..and then in three weeks time…..at the Survivor Series……WE are going to take the Corporation out for good……”

Hendrix says that’s all and well, and he hates to the the bearer of bad news…..but the Survivor Series matches are four on four…..and you seem to be a man short…..

“……short?…..SHORT??…..do I look like I’ve ever been short a day in my life Dok?…..I’m seven foot tall…..in the Master……and the Ruler…..of the world……”

Dok shakes his head, telling Sid he misunderstood……Jeff Jarrett, Rich Steiner…..Southern Justice…..that’s four men……you three seem to be a man down. Ahmed interjects here, telling Dok not to worry…..

“……we got the prefect partner in mind…….and the only thing that’s going down…..is the Million Dollar Corporation….”

Rating: B+

VS.

Bart Gunn vs. Jesse James

This match up stems off the back of last weeks Marc Mero/Jesse James match……or more accurately, off the events that FOLLOWED last weeks Marc Mero/Jesse James match……as Billy Gunn attacked the man who defeated him at In Your House…..along with the help of James…..at least until Bart ran down to make the save.

Neither Bart Gunn nor Jesse James have had a great deal to do around here recently. Bart has been spinning his wheels ever since losing to his brother Billy back in August…..and James hasn’t exactly been setting the World on fire with his win/loss ratio….meaning that both of these two men could do with a victory…..unfortunately only one of them can……and that one was Jesse James…….although not without a helping hand……

……..because Mr. Perfect makes his way down the aisle….along with his client Billy Gunn……and with the Executive Consultant distracting the attention of the official, Billy reaches into the ring and trips a running Bart…..who stumbles straight into a Pump Handle Slam and defeat at the hands of Jesse James, who picks up possibly the biggest win of his current tenure here in the WWF.

Winner: Jesse James

Rating: C

We come back from commercial to find the Nation of Domination already in the ring. As usual Faarooq has a microphone in hand and has something to say…..he begins by calling all the Son’s of Samoa ignorant and naive…..ignorant because of their beliefs…..and naive for thinking that the “man” in his ivory tower will EVER view them as anything more than Savages…..

He tells the SoS that they are nothing more than your everyday run of the mill Samoan Stereotype…..and the only one of them with any real heritage…..any real history to be proud of…..would rather be remembered for his grandaddies accomplishments…..than for the real life struggle and hardship that his father went through as a proud black man in the good old boys club…..

……Faarooq says that he’s been saying all along that the second “W” in WWF should stand for Whitewashed…..and the Son’s of Samoa might think that he’s some militant delusional individual…..but here we are…..and it’s the Samoans against the Nation…..Faarooq says if he’s so delusional, then when did the SoS last have a title opportunity? He tells the Son’s that they can lie to themselves all they want…..but some day soon, even they will be forced to see the truth….but for now, they will just have to settle for an ass whooping at Survivor Series…..because it doesn’t matter which of the Islanders they choose for a partner……because an Island doesn’t stand a chance against a Nation.

Rating: B+

VS.

Chris Jericho vs. Billy Gunn (w/Mr. Perfect & Jesse James)

It’s time for the next match up of the evening now….and Billy Gunn is in action, against one of the hottest young stars in wrestling today, Chris Jericho. Facing Billy Gunn is a big enough task at the best of times….because wherever Gunn goes, his Executive Consultant, Mr. Perfect is never far behind…..and if that wasn’t enough, even Jesse James is out for this one….presumably to safeguard against any repercussions from their actions as of late.

Even with the constant interference of the two men at ringside, Chris Jericho takes Billy Gunn to the very limit…..and for good reason……Chris Jericho proved back in September that he can more than hang with the big boys, when he was very nearly successful in wrenching away the WWF Championship from the Heartbreak Kid Shawn Michaels at In Your House……and had it not been for the emergence of a new foe the following night, Jericho may well have been your new Champion if given another shot…..

……and while we are on the subject of that new foe…..he makes another appearance tonight, fresh off his vicious assault of Jericho last week….and with so many distracting factors on the outside, it was only natural that the official should miss Lance Storms blatant interference in the match, which gives Billy Gunn the perfect opportunity to connect with the Famouser…..and pick up a very big win here on Monday Night Raw.

Winner: Billy Gunn

Rating: B

Much like last weeks backstage attack, Lance Storm proceeds to lay a beating on his former tag team partner…..unlike last week however, he’s not going it alone…..because Billy Gunn and Jesse James have decided to help with the punishment. Jericho does manage a brief flurry of offence, fighting back against all three men……but it’s still three against one, and it’s not long before he’s overwhelmed again…..

……fortunately however help is at hand….as Marc Mero and Bart Gunn come running down the aisle to make the save….and before long all six men are brawling…..Jericho and Storm…..the former Smoking Gunns…..and Mero with Jesse James…..the three fighting pairs begin to brawl around ringside…..

…….which is when Gorilla Monsoon comes out from the back, ordering all six men to stop, before they face serious repercussions…..he says that since they obviously want to kill each other, then they can do it live on Pay Per View, at the Survivor Series…..and since the Survivor Series matches this year are four on four affairs, then they had both better choose a fourth partner…..and quickly…..because next week, those two fourth guys, will be facing off against each other….in a one on one match.

Rating: B+

Next up we go to a pre-recorded promo video from the Undertaker and his manager Paul Bearer. Bearer says that it was HE that was there to pick up the pieces that were left broken following the death of The Undertakers parents…..but for twenty years, his Undertaker has been missing one vital thing……revenge…..retribution…..closure…. .there has been nobody to make suffer for his parents death, because the one responsible was also presumed dead…..and because of that……EVERYBODY has been made to suffer for his parents death……every foe vanquished and opponent destroyed has worn the mark of Kane……well in a few short weeks….his Undertaker will no longer have to make false idols suffer……because the one responsible for all The Deadman’s pain, still lives……just as the Undertaker’s need for vengeance still lives.

Taker says that at Survivor Series…..Kane is going to have to do exactly that…..Survive……and frankly, the Undertaker does not believe he can…….Kane, at Survivor Series…..you will….Rest……in………Peace.

Rating: A*

VS.

Steve Austin & Bret Hart vs. The KLiQ

It’s time for the main event of the evening…..and while the main theme of the match is both Bret Hart and Steve Austin’s mutual dislike of the KLiQ…..arguably the bigger question is trust……because you have two men in Hart and Austin, who’s storied history makes it virtually impossible to trust each other……but on the other hand you have Shawn Michaels and Kevin Nash….and while the KLiQ appear to be on the same page (for now at least) these are two men who have spent the last few months at each other’s throats…..with Nash even “accidentally” eating the Sweet Chin Music a few short weeks back on Raw.

It’s crowded at ringside too…..because both Owen Hart and Brian Pillman are out there to support their respective partners…..while Hall, Waltman, Credible and Sunny are on the outside for the KLiQ….and while two weeks ago you would be questioning Michaels and Nash’s ability to operate as a team….they are certainly the more cohesive unit tonight…..especially compared to Steve Austin….who looks as though he would rather take all the punishment for the team, rather than have to admit that he needs to Tag out to the Hitman. Eventually however, Hart gives him little choice…..because after playing the face (or rather heel in Austin’s case) in peril for sometime….Bret finally makes the blind tag, slapping Stone Cold on the back to get into the action…..

…..and that’s where it goes wrong…..because that’s when the match breaks down….and an already p####d off Austin eventually gets back into the mix as all four men go at it……still seething from Austin’s interference costing him the Light Heavyweight Championship earlier in the evening however means that Sean Waltman eventually reaches into the ring to trip Stone Cold…..who loses his footing and collides with the Hitman. Bret believing it was intentional shoves Austin hard in the chest…..which he answers with a Stone Cold Stunner to his own partner. Bret goes down, just as Nash sends Austin out of the ring, while Michaels makes an easy cover……and three seconds later the KLiQ are victorious.

Winner(s): The KLiQ

Rating: B+

The match is over and done with, but the Harts are not happy with the way it went down….and are quick to let Austin know about it…..Pillman is there to back up his partner and the four men soon find themselves alone in the ring, as the KLiQ make their way up the aisle to watch the entertainment unfold…..and unfold it does…..first from a shouting match…..then to a shoving contest…..until finally Steve Austin flips both Bret and Owen off…..and this so called Survivor Series “team” break out into a wild brawl as the show goes off the air to a wide shot of the KLiQ standing back, laughing amongst each other…..because this week, it’s their opponents that are imploding.

Rating: A*

Final Rating: A

Final Thoughts

Im not entirely sure what WCW are trying to do……because fresh off his Title making match of the year contender at Halloween Havoc, WCW Champion Bam Bam Bigelow is still finding himself in the semi-main event of Nitro…..and is still inexplicably teaming with Vader regularly….I’m not sure if they are trying to get some of Vaders popularity to run off on Bam Bam…..but if that’s the case then make Vader your Champion to begin with……they teamed up again this week in a poor showing to defeat Bunkhouse Buck and Dick Slater…..while the Main Event saw Arn Anderson defeat Lex Luger, in a match that wasn’t much better……which is why we demolished them again in the ratings, 18.46-15.53.

I don’t know if they’ve given winning the ratings war up, but they have begun to put far better shows on their Saturday Night programme than anything they put out on a Monday….just this week they put a Pay Per View calibre main event on…..as Hulk Hogan teamed up with Ric Flair…..for what I think is the first time ever……to defeat Bigelow and Vader. I know I said the Champion is wasted in all this Tag Team action, but seeing Hogan and Flair team up is a pretty big deal.

Young Jesse James picked up an injury in his match against Bart Gunn….a depressed maxillary fracture in his neck…..ironic really considering Bart spent a few months out with the storyline neck injury from Billy Gunns steel chair Famouser. No one was to blame, just one of those freak accidents……but it does mean that James will miss Survivor Series, because he’s going to be out around six weeks. It’s a big blow to the youngster to be missing one of the big four shows…..it’s a bigger blow to me however…..because now I need to find another body for the Survivor Series match, on top of the mystery opponent that’s going to be revealed on Raw.

Jerry Lawler has obviously seen something he likes in Rocky Maivia, because he’s taken the kid under his wing as a protege of sorts……even spending this week giving Maivia tips on how to work as a heel…..that’s great Jerry, but Maivia is a face right now…..any face tips for him?

Anyway…..onto Raw, and my quest to find Billy Gunn and Lance Storm two partners.

Week 2, November 1996

Posted 21 May 2019

Week 2, November 1996

River Centre

ATT: 15,620

TV: 18.68

Its that time again Ladies and Gentlemen…..time for the number one show in all of professional wrestling…..from the number one company in all of professional wrestling…..it’s time….for Monday Night Raw…..are you ready? Because the KLiQ are ready…..and here they are…..walking down the aisle before the commentators even have chance to welcome us to the show. They strut around ringside, trading insults with Jerry “The King” Lawler, before finally entering the squared circle, microphones in hand.

This is a far more united KLiQ than we have seen in the weeks leading up to tonight…..Shawn Michaels reveal that he was implementing the no title shot rule to their opponents should the KLiQ win at Survivor Series, seem to have gone a long way towards mending bridges….along with Michaels and Nash’s successful outing as a team last week, means the group is looking pretty happy. The WWF Champion brings the microphone to his mouth and prepares to speak…..but stops himself, shaking his head, before dramatically passing the microphone to Kevin Nash…..who over-emphasises a look of shock, before laughing and taking the stick.

Nash says it’s funny…..three weeks ago the easy money was on the KLiQ coming out here….having a little falling out….seeing tempers flare….maybe a shove here…..a push there……but here we are……and when the going gets tough, the KLiQ gets tougher. Nash says instead of him and Shawn out here at each other’s throats…..it’s their opponents that just can’t get along…..

“…..and guess what boys…..you keep fighting amongst yourselves….because when you’ve done laying each other out, we’ll be there to pick up the pieces……just like we’ll be there to pick up OUR Championship Belts after the match……oh……and those are Championship Belts that you shmucks……won’t be having a shot at again…..”

The KLiQ find that hilarious…..apart from Sean Waltman….who says that HE would be standing here with a Championship belt too right now, if it wasn’t for Stone Cold Steve Austin sticking his big bald head where it didn’t belong…..he tells Austin that come Survivor Series, he’s going to make sure that it’s HIM that eliminates Stone Cold.

Justin Credible even gets in on the action, saying that while Waltman is busy eliminating Austin from the Survivor Series match….he’s going to be eliminating Jerry Lawler from the Raw announce Table. Lawler quips on commentary however that the only think that will be eliminated is the rodent type features of Credible, when he does the world a favour and puts that jockstrap back over his ugly mug.

Finally Michaels (over) graciously takes the microphone, before telling the crowd to take a good look at the sight before them…..the Heartbreak Kid, your WWF Champion…..Big Sexy Kevin Nash and the Bad Guy, Scott Hall….YOUR WWF Tag Team Champions……and that’s not gonna change now…..and it’s certainly not going to change after the Survivor Series…..

“…..because while the black and white express keeps chugging along down the tracks…..Austin, Pillman….the Fart Brothers…..they’re just gonna be spinning their wheels….with no chance of gold…..nothing to fight for…..and no reason to be here…..because the KLiQ is just too sweet…..and boys…..we ain’t going nowhere….”

Apparently El Presidente has something to say….because Gorilla Monsoon makes his way out onto the entrance way…..he tells Waltman that he doesn’t have to wait until Survivor Series to get his hands on Steve Austin…..because he’s going to be going one on one with Stone Cold tonight…..

“……and as for you Mr. Michaels…..you may well feel that you have tipped the scales in your favour…..and yes, should the KLiQ be victorious at Survivor Series, The Harts, Mr. Austin and Mr. Pillman will NOT be eligible to challenge for any Championship held by you “gentlemen”….and I use that term loosely….but Survivor Series is still two weeks away……and YOU Shawn Michaels might not even be the WWF Champion by then……”

Monsoon says that he may have had some fine print added to his contract…..but that doesn’t change the terms and conditions that come with holding gold here in the WWF……and those terms clearly state that ALL Championships must be defended within a thirty day window……and since the Title is NOT on the line at the Survivor Series….it’s going to be on the line tonight…..against the man who defeated you not so long ago on Monday Night Raw……and that man…..is the Loose Cannon, Brian Pillman…..good luck.

With that Monsoon turns and heads to the back, leaving Shawn Michaels to throw a trademark hissy fit as the rest of the KLiQ attempt to calm him down.

Rating: A*

VS.

Psycho Sid vs. Rich Steiner (w/Ted DiBiase)

This one truly had it all…..high flying death defying action….technical prowess….psychology up the yoo-ha….submission masterclasses……ok…..I’m lying…..it had none of that……it did have a hell of a lot of smash-mouth action though…..and the crowd loved it. Psycho Sid, for all of his shortcomings, is never not over….and since turning his allegiances towards the power of money and the Million Dollar Corporation, the fans have certainly not been shy about letting Rich Steiner know how they feel about him.

The Intercontinental Championship wasn’t on the line tonight….but as Brian Pillman just found out, beating a Champion in a non-title match gives you a pretty good chance of getting a shot at the gold somewhere down the line…couple that with Sid’s ongoing issues with the Corporation and it makes for a pretty fired up Psycho Sid. Steiner on the other hand clearly feels like he has nothing to gain here…..a fact that he demonstrates come the end of the march…..when DiBiase yanks his client free of a Sid Powerbomb attempt, and Steiner takes a walk up the aisle way, forcing the official to count him out and award the match to Sid.

Winner: Psycho Sid (via Count-Out)

Rating: B

Sid takes the victory, but it wasn’t the victory he had in mind clearly….because while Steiner is busy in the aisle strapping his Intercontinental Championship around his waist, Sid is screaming at him from the ring to “…..get his ass back down here and fight like a man…..”

“…..the men with a history of violence, live in fear of retribution……the men with the most to lose, live in fear of losing it all……and the men with the most to live for, live in fear of the day that Death places his cold hand on their shoulder……I don’t live in fear of anything…..I have nothing to live for…..I have nothing to lose……and as for retribution, I welcome it…….unleash your fury and your vengeance upon me Deadman…..come for me……make me feel YOUR pain…..but understand this…..you’re nothing more than a means to an end…..a tool……a reason for a madman to return…..I will welcome your painful embrace…..because sooner or later I will get what I want……He will come…….Quote the Raven…..Nevermore…..”

Rating: A*

VS.

Scott Steiner vs. Scott Hall

It’s time for the main event of the evening, and we have a packed house here tonight….as not only have the KLiQ joined Scott Hall at ringside, but Vince McMahon himself has decided to take a seat at the commentary table. Jim Ross questions the validity of Steiner gaining a title shot if he wins here tonight after McMahon’s recent actions……but Vince assures us that his word is his bond….and should Scott Steiner be able to defeat The Bad Guy tonight, then he will indeed get a title shot….BUT….that’s going to be a big ask, because Scott Hall is one of the very best.

He might be slowing down a little bit as he ages, but Scott Hall can still put on a main event calibre match up, even with his declining abilities…..and it certainly helped that he and Steiner has outstanding chemistry together…..it also helped that Scott Steiner picked right back up where he left off a few months back, putting in the performance of the evening….in the end the incentive was too great for Steiner to fail….and despite numerous attempts at outside interference, he was finally able to make Hall submit in the middle of the ring, with the Camel Clutch that he has christened the Steiner Recliner.

Winner: Scott Steiner

Rating: B+

The bell rings and Shawn Michaels does not look happy on the floor, screaming at McMahon that Steiner is a madman…..Vince does his best to calm the Champion down, as Steiner leans over the ropes screaming at Michaels, who looks close to a melt down when McMahon takes a microphone…..he tells Shawn to…..

“…..calm down damn it…..you’re the Heartbreak Kid Shawn Michaels….the World Wrestling Federation Champion…..the Showstopper….the Main Event…..you are the Reason for the Ratings, and you beat Scott Steiner AND Steve Austin, in the same night, in the same match…..you can beat that knucklehead one more time…………except……….you won’t need to at In Your House…….

….See Steiner…….I said if you could defeat Scott Hall then you would receive a title shot……I didn’t however…..say a World Title shot no, no, no……..see at In Your House, you get your title shot……your INTERCONTINENTAL title shot……against your brother, Rich Steiner………”

Unsurprisingly Steiner doesn’t take the news well……not that he doesn’t want another piece of his brother….more that he was looking forward to righting a wrong that dated back to June’s King of the Ring……McMahon is un-phased now that he is out of Big Poppa Pump’s reach, merrily informing him that…..

“…….and that is what you get for having the arrogance to demand ANYTHING of Vincent Kennedy McMahon……THIS however……is for having the nerve to place your hands on Vincent Kennedy McMahon……….”

…..and with that the KLiQ rush the ring and begin assaulting Steiner in a vicious four on one beating…..and Shawn Michaels is tuning up the band, ready to land the final blow…….

……until Ahmed Johnson comes charging down the aisle……and he’s got wood…..he’s got huge wood……a huge plank of wood that he uses to send the KLiQ running from the ring to the safety of the floor. He paces around hyping up the crowd for a minute, before helping Scott Steiner to his feet. Michaels is in an even worse mood now…..and it doesn’t get any better when Ahmed slowly points the plank of lumber in Shawn’s direction…..then motions to his waist that he’s gunning for the World Wrestling Federation Championship…..maybe we have found our new number one contender tonight after all.

Rating: A*

Final Rating: A

Final Thoughts

The biggest news coming out of Raw this week was Scott Hall picking up an Achilles Tendinitis injury during his match with Scott Steiner after attempting the Razors Edge……Hall really needs to stop trying to get the bigger guys up in the Edge, at least from the mat anyway….hooking them when they’re stood on the second rope is one thing, but Steiner is a big old mass of muscle to be trying to position over your shoulders……fortunately it’s not too serious, and should be cleared up in a few weeks….he can probably work on it too, but the last thing I need to be doing is to be giving Scott Hall a reason to be self medicating.

Jerry Lawler was backstage at Raw, passing on a few tips and tricks on psychology to The Rock…..this kids mic work has really improved recently….and if Lawler can continue to show him the ropes in the other aspects of the sport, we could have something special on our hands before long.

WCW held their World War 3 Pay Per View on Sunday…..it was underwhelming to say the least…..especially when you factor in their decision to have Kevin Sullivan of all people to win the World War 3 match……which they at least had the common sense to condense down to a 30 man battle royal this year, because that 60 man concept is a shambles.

WCW Champion Bam Bam Bigelow was relegated to the Semi-Main Event spot again…..fresh of his match of the year candidate against Randy Savage at Halloween Havoc, he successfully defended the gold in a rather lacklustre affair against Lex Luger……as for Savages reward for his showing at Halloween Havoc? He got to work Evad Sullivan….at least he went over. The rather unusual combination of Marcus Bagwell and former Headshrinker Samu captured the WCW Tag Team Titles from the Blue Bloods…..don’t ask……and One Man Gang began a second reign as Television Champion, taking the title back from the guy who beat him for it, Disco Inferno…..other results were….

Kevin Sullivan won the World War 3 Battle Royal B+
Bam Bam Bigelow defeated Lex Luger to retain the WCW Title B
DDP retained US Title over Earl Robert Eaton C
One Man Gang began his second Tv title reign defeating Disco Inferno D+
Randy Savage beat Evad Sullivan B
Marcus Bagwell and Samu captured the WCW Tag Team Titles from the Blue Bloods B
Lt. James Earl beat Ice Train D+
Buddy Parker beat Duke Droese D+

I’m not entirely sure how their World War 3 gimmick is working this year, but my God if Sullivan winning means they plan on running with Bam Bam Bigelow vs Kevin Sullivan for the World strap at Starrcade then heaven have mercy on the poor souls that buy the thing.
