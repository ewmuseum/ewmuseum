Week 2, August 1997

Sturgis Motorcycle Rally (Sturgis, South Dakota)

ATT: 6,700 unpaid bikers

PPV BUY: 1.66

Announce Team: Tony Schiavone, Mike Tenay and Bobby “The Brain” Heenan

Rev those engines folks, because we’re coming to you live from the Sturgis Motorcycle Rally in Sturgis, South Dakota, where 6700 drunk and surly bikers have flocked to witness this (free) spectacle from World Championship Wrestling…..we open at the announce desk, where Tony Schiavone, Mike Tenay and Bobby “The Brain” Heenan greet us (dressed like rejects from the Village People) and run down the nights proceedings, focusing on the “will he, or won’t he” mystery surrounding the condition of the Warrior, and whether the man will actually be here to compete this evening….because if not, Heenan says that Sting can kiss his chances of earning a title shot against Hogan, next month or any other month……

…..meanwhile the rest of the Titans will be in competition too….and looking to prove their worth to what is no doubt a p!ssed off Vince Russo following the blockbuster announcement from Eric Bischoff this past Monday night…..with Jean Paul Levesque looking to show his worth (following a few recent failures on behalf of the Titan’s youngest member) against Chris Benoit……Ray Traylor will have his hands full with WCW’s newest roster member Curt Hennig…..and last but not least, the most under pressure members of the Titans, Knobbs and Saggs, the Nasty Boys…..who have been told in no uncertain terms that they need to bring those Tag Team Titles home tonight, when they face Champions, the Road Warriors……

……the announce team wrap up their intro, before the thousands of engines rev to life, because our first match is about to start……

Rating: N/A

VS.

Curt Hennig vs. Ray Traylor


Curt Hennig made his WCW debut just six days ago on Monday Nitro, and in less than a week he has competed for the World Championship, defeated Hollywood Hogan, competed on Nitro and is now about to compete in his first Pay Per View outing……not a bad first week on the job…..in fact, you COULD say it’s the Perfect start to Mr. Hennig’s WCW career…….now all he has to do is actually WIN his first Pay Per View outing……

……of course, that’s going to be easier said than done…..after all, his opponent tonight is the Enforcer for the Titans of Wrestling, Ray Traylor….and you’ve got to believe that if Traylor is able to bring the scalp of Curt Hennig back to Vince Russo, his place within the Titans would be pretty much secured, especially when it was Hennig (along with Bischoff) that embarrassed Russo on Nitro to begin with……

……Heenan eludes to his past history with Hennig, stating that not only has he managed this man to Championships, he’s also managed this man AGAINST Traylor, and if anyone knows how to defeat the Enforcer, it’s Curt…..before stating however that this is a much different Ray Traylor…..meaner….colder…..more calculating…..and much, much more dangerous…..Traylor shows that here too, and Hennig sells like a superstar as always. He may be more well known for his heel work, but Hennig is really coming into his own as a fan favourite as of late, and plays the babyface in peril role to (pardon the pun) perfection….

…..if you’re wanting to build up a strong heel stable however, you need strong baby faces to face them….and Hennig proves that he’s more than just a selling machine as he fights his way back into this one, before finally finishing the man off with the Hennig Plex…….Mr. Russo will NOT be pleased with the Titans night so far, and we’re only one match in.

Winner: Curt Hennig
Rating: B

Hennig is clearly delighted with his victory, and he’s joined in the ring by Mean Gene (complete with leather bikers hat……there’s a disturbing image if ever you saw one) who congratulates him on win, before officially welcoming him to WCW……

…..Hennig says that it’s good to be here…..a company rich with tradition, it’s a company about WRESTLING…..not crotch chops, not middle fingers……not grown men telling other grown men to “suck it”……it’s a place for WRESTLERS, and he couldn’t think of a more…..perfect place for him to be…..which is EXACTLY why he signed the contract provided to him by Eric Bischoff…..he’s not here to be some pawn in the Titans if Wrestling, he’s here to win Championships…..and he WOULD have been standing here right now with the WCW Title around his waist, had it not been for the interference of Vince Russo……Hennig says that he’s man enough to admit that Hogan and his gang of cronies really gave him a going over in Nitro……but tonight, this victory over Ray Traylor…..was just the first step…..he’s going to run through the Titans of Wrestling one by one, until he FINALLY gets another shot at Hollywood Hogan and the WCW Championship……and this time, nothing is going to stop him from bringing home the gold.

Rating: A

VS.

WCW Television Championship Match

Lord Steven Regal (c) vs. Rick Martel


It’s time for our first title match of the evening, and time to find out “will he, or won’t he”…….that being whether or not our reigning Television Champion would keep to his word, and leave Earl Robert and The Duke in the back in this title defence against Rick Martel…….and to his credit, he did just that……this was a straight one on one contest…..no outside interference at all……

…….of course, that’s not to say that his Lordship didn’t bend the rules at times…..he may act the gentleman but he’s still a scoundrel at heart after all…..this was a really solid match too, with both men utilising a mat based offence as each tried to wear the other down for their respective submission holds…….in the end however, it was Martel that was able to lock on the Boston Crab……and as the bell rings, the engines roar in approval for our new Television Champion…….

……except we don’t have a new Television Champion……what we do have however, is a time limit draw……the ten minute time limit being the very backbone of which the Television Championship was built on to begin with……and as Martel pleads for five more minutes, Regal grabs his title and leaves, telling Martel that ”you had your shot sunshine……two shots actually….you shan’t get another” before heading to the back…..leaving Martel frustrated in the ring…..

Winner: Time Limit Draw
Rating: B-

…….and his frustrations are only going to grow, as he is suddenly attacked from behind as Lance Storm of all people comes running down the aisle out of nowhere, and nails Martel right in the back of the head with a stiff forearm shot that knocks the veteran down……

……Storm has had the Jeff Hardy treatment here, skyrocketing from the Lower to the Upper Midcard simply by jumping ship……and Tony Schiavone is in shock by what he’s seeing (naturally) stating that WCW is clearly the place to be, and can’t miss programming….because you never know WHO’S going to show up….and as Martel stumbles to his feet, he’s knocked straight back down again by a big Lance Storm Superkick…..

…..Storm stands over the fallen Martel with a look of utter destain on his face, before adding insult to injury, when he apply’s his OWN Boston Crab variation, the single legged version, the Canadian Maple Leaf on his (also) Canadian victim, and as the officials do their best to drag Lance Storm off a screaming Rick Martel, we air a commercial for our next up coming event, WCW Fall Brawl.

Rating: B-

We go backstage from there, where Mean Gene spends a few minutes shilling the Hotline (1-900-909-9900) saying that Jeff Hardy and Lance Storm were just the tip of the iceberg when it comes to new arrivals here…..he says that WCW is where the big boys play, and there are quite a few big names that want to make their way over to World Championship Wrestling…..call the Hotline right now, and choose option 1 for all the backstage gossip……before introducing his guest, Diamond Dallas Page, along with the beautiful Kimberly…..

……he says that up next, Page is going to be facing a man who’s been causing him quite a few problems as of late…..as well as clearly having one eye on the gorgeous Kimberly…..and that man is Buff Bagwell, who incidentally has made no secret of his desire to join forces with Hogan, Russo, and the Titans of Wrestling…..Page rolls his eyes at that, saying when you’ve got an ego the size of Buff Bagwell, then of course you’re gonna want to hang with Hollywood “Scum” Hogan…..

”…….of course he wants to slither his way to the top like the snake he is, but me, DDP……I’m gonna get to the top the old fashioned way…..I’m gonna earn it……I’m gonna take little Buffy out, and if he so much as LOOKS at Kimberly the wrong way then I’ll…..”

……Kim puts a hand on her husbands arm there, stopping him in mid sentence…..she tells him not to worry, because if he so much as breathes on her, she’ll slap the taste right out of his mouth……”because ‘Buff’ doesn’t have the ‘Stuff’ to deal with a real woman like me”……

……Page smiles at that, telling Gene that’s why he married her, he needs a feisty woman to keep him on his toes…….where as tonight, Buff Bagwell is going to be knocked off his feet, when Page drives him into the mat with the Diamond Cutter…..

”…….and then when I’m done with the Teenybopper Titan, it’s gonna be onto the real deal……Hogan, I already beat your “General”…..I’ll mow through every last one of your little stooges until I get to you……because like it or not, you will feel the BANG!…..”

“…….you’re gonna have to get in line Dallas…..because there’s a whole lot of people wanting to get their hands on Hollywood Hogan and the Titans of Wrestling…….”

…….apparently Page isn’t the only one with his sights set on Hollywood……as Lex Luger and the hulking sight that is The Giant come walking into frame…..Luger pats DDP on the back, wishing him well in his match next, before Gene turns his attention to the new arrivals, stating that with all due respect, the two men have somewhat bigger problems tonight……in fact, four very big problems in the form of Vader, Bam Bam Bigelow, Mike Awesome and Gideon Cain……four of the biggest men in this company, in a battle to prove who the biggest giant in WCW is……

……Luger shakes his head, saying that first of all, this isn’t his fight…..he’s no giant, never has been, never claimed to be…..he’s just here to back up his good friend……Vader, Bigelow…..Missy Hyatt’s two playthings, none of that matters in the long run……what matters to Luger, is getting his hands on the Titans of Wrestling, and that’s exactly what he’s going to do…..

”…….and second of all, there’s only one true ‘Giant’ in WCW…..and he’s stood right next to me…….”

……Gene turns the microphone to that one true Giant now, who says that tonight is finally the night that he gets his big hands on that wannabe big man Mike Awesome and his new little running buddy Gideon Cain…..he says it’s been a long time coming, but tonight, he’s gonna drive both men through the mat with a Chokeslam each……as for Vader and Bigelow, they’re just collateral damage that SHOULD have stayed out of the Giant’s business……

…..with that Luger and The Giant head off to prepare for their match, before Gene begins to sign off back to the commentary at ringside…..before stopping abruptly and calling an unseen passer by over…….

…..Lance Storm…….who Gene asks first of all what he’s doing in WCW……and secondly, why he attacked Rick Martel?…..

”……why WCW? I’d have thought that was pretty obvious Gene……in fact, I heard Curt Hennig give the exact same answer earlier……because WCW has always been the home of Wrestling…..and Lance Storm is the BEST wrestler on the planet….much better than Curt Hennig……and why wouldn’t I be? After all, I come from Calgary……Alberta, Canada……and I’m going to come to into World Championship Wrestling week in and week out, and wrestle circles around anyone dumb enough to step in the ring with me……”

……Gene again asks why the attack on Martel, a fellow Canadian who likes to utilise the Boston Crab, if anything Storm should be a supporter of Martel…..Storm disagrees, saying Martel may have been great…..ONCE…..but his day has gone, and every minute he try’s to stay relevant he’s embarrassing the whole country of Canada…..so Storm is going to take him out of the sport, once and for all.

Rating: A

VS.

Diamond Dallas Page (w/Kimberly) vs. Buff Bagwell


It’s back down to ringside now, and it’s time for Diamond Dallas Page to face off against Titan in Training, Buff Bagwell…..Page already has one victory in the bag against Bagwell, while the only win between the two men that Buff can claim, is the Tag Team victory picked up on Nitro, and even then it was his partner for the evening, Jean Pail Levesque that picked up the pinfall…..

…..Buff may have his Titan Training Wheels on, but Mr. Russo has obviously decided that young Bagwell needs to prove his worth solo tonight, because while Page has Kimberly at ringside, Buff is all alone….although the fact that Kim is out there is music too Bagwell’s ears…..and eyes, as he openly ogles the beautiful young woman (between flexing his pecs at her) although true to her word, the moment Bagwell struts and shimmy’s a little too close, he gets a stinging open handed slap for his troubles…..

…..these two couldn’t be more polar opposite when it comes to an in ring perspective…..Buff the cocky and arrogant upstart, willing to do anything (and align himself with anyone) to get to the top, while DDP is your hard working common man, looking to EARN his way to the top of the mountain…..although interestingly enough, despite their age difference (with Bagwell a sprightly 27 to Page’s rather advanced 41) they have virtually the same number of years worth of experience (in fact in terms of IN-ring work, Bagwell is actually the veteran of the two) and the two men even arrived in WCW around the same time as well, back in 1991…..

…..but anyway, I’m rambling there, back to the match……and it was a pretty decent match up too, and wide open to when it comes to offence, with Bagwell splitting his time between punishing Page and flexing for Kimberly. In the end though Bagwell could have maybe used a little bit of help from the Titans, because much like their last singles encounter, Dallas is able to finish the man off with a Diamond Cutter right in the middle of the ring…..and if his pre match interview is anything to go by, he’s got the Titans of Wrestling in his sights next.

Winner: Diamond Dallas Page
Rating: B-

VS. VS.

Triangle Match

Lex Luger & The Giant vs. Big Van Vader & Bam Bam Bigelow

vs. Mike Awesome & Gideon Cain (w/Missy Hyatt)

It’s time for our huge Triangle Tag Team Match now, and as Bobby Heenan remarks, hopefully they have reinforced the ring for this one, because there’s going to be a lot of beef in there…..Mike Awesome has been making quite the name for himself as of late here in WCW, and after declaring himself the “best big man in the business” he went out of his way to hone in on the biggest of the big…..Bam Bam Bigelow, Vader…..and finally the Giant……and in the process, ticked all three men off enough that they actually wound up fighting amongst themselves for the chance to fight against him……

……with three behemoths like that after him it was only natural that Awesome should seek out some back up……enter Gideon Cain….the 6’5” 285lb mountain of muscle from Hell’s Kitchen, New York…..who along with Missy Hyatt, quickly aligned himself with the man, the myth, the mullet, Mike Awesome, and the two had an impressive showing in their first match as partners, easily defeating the Soul Train and showing some excellent chemistry along the way…..

…….so with Awesome and Cain aligned, and Bammers and Vader regularly teaming up, that left the Giant as the man alone…..fortunately he had back up too….not from a man that had any issues with Awesome and Cain, but from his friend…..Lex Luger has the Titans of Wrestling in his sights, but he’s put all that to one side tonight to watch the Giants back…..because the sooner the big man is done with this whole battle of the giants, the sooner Luger can concentrate on capturing that WCW Championship from Hollywood Hogan…..

……it’s triangle rules here, one man from each team in the ring, one man from each team on the outside, anyone can tag anyone and it’s one fall to a finish…..and as Tenay points out (being the Professor that he is) this is a huge test for the pairing of Awesome and Cain, because all four of their opponents (yes even Bam Bam Bigelow, thanks for that Bischoff) is a former WCW Champion, not that Missy’s men let that intimidate them for a second, Awesome even makes sure to start this match (along with Vader and The Giant) and doesn’t back down an inch……the match itself is pretty good, no doubt helped by the fact that both Luger and the Giant are super over, and while Vader and Bam Bam aren’t quite at their level, they certainly aren’t far off……add in to that Awesome and Cains excellent chemistry as a team and Missy doing some good work on the outside, and you have a pretty good combination…..

……all four men get their chance to shine in this one, with Luger in particular impressing, getting not only Mike Awesome up in the Torture Rack, but Bam Bam Bigelow too, although both times the hold was broken up before Luger could pick up a submission victory…….and speaking of Luger…….

……the Titans of Wrestling obviously heard Lugers earlier promo with Mean Gene, and have decided to make a pre-emptive strike on the man who claims to be gunning for them AND the WCW Championship…..which is why the Enforcer comes running down the aisle, and with the rules in these multi-Man matches a little more relaxed, he drags Luger to the outside and nails him in the ribs with his nightstick, before dragging the man up the aisle…….the Giant abandons the match to give chase but the Nasty Boys cut him off, and soon the three men brawl their way to the back and out of the match…..

……and the ensuing distraction is enough for Cain to sent Vader flying off the apron with a running big boot, leaving Bam Bam all alone to eat the Assisted Powerbomb from the two men, giving Awesome and Cain a huge win here tonight.

Winner(s): Mike Awesome & Gideon Cain
Rating: B

From there the camera feed cuts to the back, where Luger and the Giant are still under siege from the three Titans…..the Giant briefly manages to fend off the Nasty’s, but gets nailed in the back of the leg by Traylor and his nightstick, and between them the Titan Trio begin to systematically beat down their two victims…….

……at least until the Road Warriors come rushing into shot, dragging Knobbs and Saggs off Luger and the Giant and proceeding to give them a good old fashioned backstage beating……Luger and the Giant quickly regain their composure, and run the Enforcer off, leaving Hawk and Animal to batter the Nasty’s all the way to the entrance way, through the curtain……and back down the aisle towards the ring, as the bikers Rev their engines in approval…..looks like our WCW Tag Team Title Match is going to be next……

Rating: A

VS.

WCW World Tag Team Championship Match

The Road Warriors (c) vs. The Nasty Boys


…….although it takes a while to officially start, because the four men spend near on ten minutes battling in the aisle and around ringside, with Hawk even taking a motorcycle helmet from a fan and nailing Knobbs between the eyes with it, before they finally enter the ring, and this one officially gets underway…..although it’s not long and it’s not pretty…..

……mainly because Knobbs is still selling that shot from the motorcycle helmet, leaving Saggs in the unenviable position of having to face both Hawk and Animal pretty much single handedly, which realistically was never going to end well……and it didn’t…..one Doomsday Device later and the Road Warriors retain the Titles…..and while the action may have gone for a little over 14 minutes with the pre-match brawl, the official match time clocked in at a mere 4:36……Vince Russo will NOT be happy with that.

Winner(s): ….and Still WCW Tag Team Champions……The Road Warriors
Rating: B

VS.

WCW United States Championship Match

Eddie Guerrero (c) vs. Jeff Hardy


We air a brief recap video on the arrival of Jeff Hardy to WCW, answering Eddie Guerrero’s open challenge as the masked Willow, gaining this Championship opportunity here tonight in the process….Tony Schiavone in particular is high on praise for the challenger, stating that with youngster like Jeff Hardy (and now Lance Storm) and up and comers like Mike Awesome and Gideon Cain climbing the ranks and rubbing shoulders with established stars like Lex Luger, you are witnessing the next generation of WCW superstar evolve before our very eyes……

……oh and spoiler alert, this is a fantastic match…..probably the second best match on the card, which I have say surprised me a lot…..not because these two aren’t super talented, because they are…..I just wasn’t expecting the reaction that it got…..Heenan says that Eddie Guerrero is a great wrestler, but he’s finding out first hand what Bobby figured out years ago…..and that is that when you’re good at something, never do it for free…..and when you have something everyone else wants, never give them a chance to take it……fortunately he believes that Eddie Guerrero is more than man enough to defeat “the Hardy kid” tonight……..

…….”the Hardy kid” as Heenan calls him is going to be no pushover however….after all, he’s already beaten Guerrero once before….and keen to earn his stripes here in WCW Hardy goes all out at the open of this match, throwing caution to the ring in his efforts to continue his winning ways and capture the US title…..unfortunately it’s that devil may cry attitude that costs him the advantage, as he crashes and burns on a Whisper in the Wind attempt, and from there Eddie goes on the attack……

….Guerrero often finds himself the smaller man in the match here where the “big boy’s” compete, but he’s able to utilise his strength advantage here punishing young Jeff to the point that he begins to get a little swagger in his step as his confidence rises, telling the crowd that last Monday was just a fluke…..he signals to the crowd that he has this all wrapped up, then slams Hardy to the mat and heads up to the top rope……Guerrero spends too long showboating however, and Hardy is far from done, rising to his knees and throwing himself at the ropes, causing Eddie to lose his footing and crotch himself up top…..Hardy then climbs up to join him….and delivers a massive Super-Plex that drives both men to the mat with a crash……

……the official reaches a count of nine before the two men struggle to their feet, Guerrero tries for a series of right hands, but Hardy blocks each one and counters with a shot of his own……he whips Guerrero into the corner and goes for a one man Poetry in Motion, but Eddie drags the official in front of himself and Hardy crushes the referee…..

……with no referee in sight Eddie quickly rolls to the outside and picks up a steel chair, before returning to the ring and charging at Hardy…..who meets him with a kick to the gut…..and a Twist if Fate……before heading up to the top looking for the Swanton Bomb…..he let’s fly, but Guerrero rolls out of the way at the last second and Jeff crashes into the steel chair that is laying on the mat……Eddie picks up the chair and nails the Challenger right in the skull, before tossing the weapon to the outside and heading up top to deliver the Frog Splash, just as the official comes around to count the one, the two…..and the three.

Winner: ….and Still WCW United States Champion……Eddie Guerrero
Rating: A(!)

Eddie takes his title and a microphone jubilantly, saying that for all the hype, Jeff Hardy just couldn’t get it done esè…..there’s not a wrestler in WCW that can do what Eddie Guerrero does in this ring, and there’s not a wrestler in WCW that can beat Eddie Guerrero when he’s prepared for them……

”…….because this business is in my blood holmes…..I’m a Guerrero…..I was bred for this……my family bled for this……and no one is going to take this title away from me…..”

…….that brings out the new face of the Championship Committee, and the commissioner of World Championship Wrestling, Eric Bischoff (complete with full leather biker gear)….he smiles that Million dollar smile at Eddie, congratulating him on his win……

”……you had to cheat just a little bit there didn’t you, but this is WCW, and the referees decisions are final after all……”

……Eddie just smirks at that, slapping a hand on his Championship, telling Bischoff that’s right, so why doesn’t he and the rest of the Championship Committee concentrate in lining opponent up for him, and he’ll keep doing what he does best, what his family has done best for generations……winning however it takes……

……Bischoff keeps that smile plastered on his face, telling Eddie that he DOES actually have his next Challenger already lined up…..

”……and it’s funny you should mention Wrestling families……because this man’s family has quite the heritage of his own…..and he just so happens to be the NEWEST addition to the WCW roster…….”

……Bischoff stands to one side and lets this newcomer make his entrance, before announcing his name…….

”…….Rikishi Fatu……”

…..Eddie is less than pleased at that, as Schiavone waxes lyrical about the heritage surrounding the Anoa’i family in this sport, he loudly protests the decision as Fatu smiles on from the entrance way.

Rating: B

VS.

The Renegade vs. Mortis (w/James Vandenberg)


It’s now time for probably the least anticipated match on the card, as the Renegade faces off against masked newcomer Mortis, with his manager James Vandenberg at ringside….Mortis seemingly attacked The Renegade with no provocation whatsoever, with the only real explanation offered by Vandenberg being that he was a man seeking purity and perfection…….and the Renegade is nothing more than a second rate attempt to recapture the essence of another (with that “other” presumably being the Ultimate Warrior) with Vandenberg going so far as to physically wipe away that yellow and red face paint that adorns the Renegades face on Nitro…..

……this match wasn’t great, nor was it expected to be, but it did what I wanted it to do…..which was put on a decent enough undercard performance, and give Mortis a decent win in his debut match…..Renegade got enough offence in as to not look like a complete jobber, but in the end it was the masked man’s Flatliner that sealed the deal, giving Mortis the one, the two and the three.

Winner: Mortis
Rating: C-

From there it’s back to Mean Gene, who’s standing by with a less than happy looking Sting….he says tonight, the Stinger is putting it all on the line….defeat Hollywood Hogan, and at Fall Brawl Sting will be competing for the WCW Championship……however, should Hogan defeat Sting, then Sting will not be granted ANY shots at that WCW Title, for as long as Hogan holds the belt…..but just to complicate matters, this is a tag team match……Sting has got to face the Mega Powers of Professional Wrestling……and his partner doesn’t appear to be anywhere in sight……

“……Sting, the question on everybody’s lips is…….where is The Warrior?….”

…….Sting says that he doesn’t know…..he says the last time he saw the Warrior was two weeks ago on Nitro, face first on the floor in the back and covered in spray paint…….

“……but it’s fine Gene, you know why? Because I knew I shouldn’t have counted on the Warrior to begin with…..I knew I should have fought this fight against the Titans alone…..but he just went on, and on, and on, and on……but you know what, I don’t need the Warrior…..I don’t need ANYONE……and if I have to defeat Hogan and Savage by myself, then that’s exactly what I’m going to do……”

…..with that Sting lifts a black baseball bat into view…..

“……even if I have to think outside the box to do so……I want Hollywood Hogan one on one for that WCW Title…….and I’m gonna get it……whatever it takes……”

Rating: A

VS.

Chris Benoit vs. Jean Paul Levesque


It’s time for our semi main event of the evening, and it’s Chris Benoit facing off against an under pressure Jean Paul Levesque……doubly under pressure because tonight has not been a good night thus far for the Titans of Wrestling……and in all honesty, this match was a bit of a let down…..Benoit and Levesque did not click at all, and not only did it make for an awkward match, but it effected both men’s performance too…..in fact, had I known how good our US Title match was going to be, I’d have probably switched these two bouts around…..it’s a shame as well, because realistically one day this could have been a match for the WCW Championship…..but based on this display we will have to keep these two men far apart from each other where one on one action is concerned…..

…….and it was, the match was supposed to showcase Chris Benoit as a serious threat….a man just one win away from a main event spot…..which is why the Crippler put on such a strong display here, and he really did look like he deserved to be sharing the ring with a true main event player in Levesque, even managing to lock on the Crippler Crossface……fortunately for Levesque, Ray Taylor came running down the aisle and out of the sight of the official, dragged Levesque to safety so that his foot was under the bottom rope, before promptly pointing that fact out to the referee, forcing the break…..Benoit found himself arguing with Traylor…..which was all the opening Levesque needed to hit the Pedigree and pick up the win.

Winner: Jean Paul Levesque
Rating: B

We air a promo package for Fall Brawl, and then it’s straight to the back, where Mean Gene is with the Titans of Wrestling…..he tells Russo that after the Mega Powers brutal attack of the Warrior two weeks ago on Nitro….and the Warriors subsequent no show tonight, surely Sting should be afforded the opportunity to name a replacement partner?

Russo just laughs at that, telling Gene that if Sting’s partner isn’t here tonight, then too bad for Sting…..maybe he shouldn’t go around handing out cheques that his ass can’t cash…..and if the Warrior isn’t here tonight……well then it looks like we’re gonna be having a handicap match Gene……“Isn’t that right Hollywood?…..

“…….Well you know something Mean Gene…..Hollywood Hogan has always known deep down in his heart of hearts, that the Warrior was nothing more than a yellow bellied coward dude…..see he knew that if he came out here tonight, the Mega Powers of Professional Wrestling, The Macho Man Randy Savage and Hollywood Hogan, we would embarrass and humiliate him and send him running back to parts unknown like the scalded dog he is…..he doesn’t want ANY part of the Titans of Wrestling brother……isn’t that right Mach…..”

“…..oh yeah, the Warrior, Warrior, Warrior…..not as stupid as he looks Gene, yeah……not as stupid as the Sting Man if he thinks he has any chance tonight…..he can kiss the match goodbye…..and he can kiss the WCW Title goodbye…..because he ain’t getting a shot again, uh-uh, not happening…..never again….dig it?…..”

“……so you tell me Sting…..what ya gonna do brother…..when the Mega Powers of Professional Wrestling run wild on you?…….”

…..with that Hogan and Savage hit that sweet Mega Powers handshake…..as a disgusted Gene sends us down to ringside……

Rating: A

VS.

If Sting pins Hogan, he gets a WCW Title shot next month…..if Hogan pins Sting, Sting won’t be granted a WCW Title shot as long as Hogan is Champion

Sting vs. The Mega Powers

So this has gone from a standard tag team encounter, to a tag team encounter with World Title ramifications, to a two on one handicap match in the space a few weeks….Schiavone talks about the Titans of Wrestling stacking the deck here against Sting, before expressing their disbelief at the Warrior no showing this event, after lobbying so hard for Sting to join him in this fight…..

…..to his credit, Sting is here, baseball bat in hand…..and he’s more than willing to go it alone against arguably two of the biggest names in all of professional wrestling, just to earn a shot at Hollywood Hogan and the WCW Championship……as for Hogan, he’s yet to truly deliver a great match since Russo’s arrival…..the same can’t be said for Savage and Sting however, with both being involved in at least one great match…..and thankfully, they drag Hogan UP to their level tonight, because this really was a great match by WCW’s recent standards…..

…..the Stinger starts out strong, taking care of both Hogan AND Savage like they were a pair of 1992 Saturday Night jobbers, not a pair of honest to goodness Icons….until the Mega Powers formulate the strategy of keeping Hollywood on the apron and Savage the legal man….after all, if Sting defeats Savage he doesn’t get the Championship opportunity next month….he has to beat Hogan to earn that…..and you can see the frustration on the painted face of Sting, who finally hits Savage with a crushing Stinger Splash that leaves Macho staggering out of the corner, where Sting launches him at Hogan…..and as the two men collide, the referee announces that as far as he is concerned, that constitutes a tag…..

….Hogan looks to tag out immediately, but Savage is down on the outside, and as he pulls himself up onto the apron Sting grabs Hollywood by the Skullet and drags him over to the neutral corner, and begins unloading on him with right after right….he whips Hogan across the ring, hard into the opposite neutral corner, delivering another Stinger Splash…..before sending him back to the original corner, going for the splash once more…..this time however Savage drags Hollywood out of harms way at the last second, and Sting hits nothing but turnbuckle….

……from there the Mega Powers go to work….after all, one man can only go against two for so long until the numbers game overwhelms him….and now with Sting down, Hollywood is more than happy to remain in the fight….he and Savage make the most of having a partner, with frequent tags ensuring that the fresh man is always in the ring….and Sting really looks to be all but done here……and as Savage goes to the top rope, Hogan holds Stings arms behind his back, leaving him wide open for the Double Ax-handle……Sting warned however that he was willing to think outside the box here, and he kicks his leg back with a Ric Flair inspired low blow, dropping out of Savages line of sight in the nick of time……and it’s Hollywood that eats the Ax-handle, before Sting clotheslines the Macho Man over the top rope, dropping to the mat in exhaustion……

…….when some familiar music begins to play……..

……and The Warrior comes bursting out of the back like a mad man and charging down the aisle and up onto the apron, where he shakes the ropes like a man possessed, crying out for Sting to make the tag……the weary Stinger drags his way over to his corner and pulls himself to his feet, before locking eyes with the Warrior and extending his hand for a much needed breather……..

…….which is when the Warrior smirks and drops off the apron……leaving Sting hanging like a fool with a look of….not shock…..more like disappointment on his face……at least until Hogan ploughs into him from behind with a hard clothesline……

…..then the Warrior rolls into the ring, where he proceeds to stomp the paint off his supposed partners face, finally dragging him to his feet…..raising him up over his head…..and dropping him with the Gorilla Press…….Savage instantly comes flying off the top with that patented elbow drop……leaving just Hollywood to drop the leg brother……and as he covers Sting, the result is a foregone conclusion……a one, a two and a three…..and Sting will NOT be granted an opportunity to face Hollywood Hogan for the WCW Title any time soon.

Winner(s): The Mega Powers of Professional Wrestling
Rating: A

Poor Sting……betrayed again, and after pledging to never trust a soul again…..and as the rest of the Titans come rushing out from the back to congratulate the Mega Powers, the Warrior proceeds to put the boots to the Stinger once more…..not that it’s needed, Sting is DONE…..Hogan rolls Sting onto his front as Traylor hands him the now faliliar can of spray paint……but as Hogan bends over to tag the Stinger, Warrior snatches the can from him…..telling him that HE wants to do it…..and as the show comes to a close, and the crowd voice their displeasure, the Warrior drops down to one knee…….leaving three black letters across the back of the Stinger……T.o.W……goodnight fans.

Rating: A*

Final Rating: B+
