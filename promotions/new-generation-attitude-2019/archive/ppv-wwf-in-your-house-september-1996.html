A New Generation…..a new attitude? Pay-per-view

WWF In Your House: September 1996

Week 3, September 1996

El Generico Stadium (South West)

ATT: 45,000

Buy: 1.25

It’s that time of the month again…..it’s time for you to welcome the World Wrestling Federation into your house. We have the usual opening promo package, running down the storylines and events that are due to culminate (or continue) this evening, before we head down to the commentary table, where Vince McMahon, Jim Ross and Jerry Lawler welcome us to the show. They quickly run down tonight’s matches, before the bell sounds and the Nation of Domination’s music starts to play……

Rating: N/A

VS.

WWF Intercontinental Championship Match

The British Bulldog (c) vs. Faarooq

…….indicating that our first match this evening, will be for the WWF Intercontinental Championship, as the British Bulldog puts the gold on the line against Faarooq, who makes his way to the ring right now, along with the entire Nation of Domination. Bulldog makes the walk down the aisle last, as is befitting to the Champions of the World Wrestling Federation….and is all smiles as he proudly wears the Intercontinental Title around his waist.

The match gets underway, and this was always going to be a hard hitting display. Both of these men are big brutish powerhouses that like to utilise high impact manoeuvres….and neither is particularly used to being on the receiving end. There’s a very old school vibe at the start of this one. The two men lock up and jockey for position but it ends in a stalemate. Second attempt and this time Faarooq is able to catch a side headlock. Bulldog backs him into the ropes however and uses the momentum to push himself free, sending his opponent across the ring. Faarooq rebounds off the opposite side of the ring and attempts to drop the Bulldog with a shoulder tackle, but neither man gives an inch. Davey Boy invites him to try again, and again the two men collide but neither goes down. Bulldog flexes his biceps which infuriates Faarooq, leading to the Nation Member challenging his opponent to try a shoulder barge of his own. Smith obliges, but instead of steadying himself for the blow, Faarooq swings for a clothesline instead. The Bulldog counters that into a crucifix pinning attempt however, which gives him a very near fall early into the match.

Faarooq gets back to his feet, flustered at how close he just came to losing the match, and offers the Bulldog a test of strength. Davey Boy didn’t back down for a second, but the second he raised his arms Faarooq hit a stiff kick to the midsection. He tries an Irish Whip but the Champion reverses it, leapfrogging his opponent on the rebound and then hits a spinning powerslam as Faarooq comes back off the ropes. Bulldog continues to show his power, pounding on the Challenger, before hoisting him up over his head, dropping him with a press slam, and Faarooq bails to the floor.

Bulldog follows the shook Challenger to the outside, and drives his opponent into the ring post post. Kama hops onto the apron to distract the official and Nelson Knight charges at Davey Boy…..who ducks…..leaving Knight to crash into Scorpio, sending him flying. The distraction was enough however, and Faarooq is able to blindside the Bulldog, sending him crashing kidney first into the steel ring steps….then rolls him back into the ring. Back between the ropes and Davey Boy swings with a wild right hand, but Faarooq ducks it and hits a big back suplex that leaves the Champion down and hurting. Bulldog rolls to his stomach in an attempt to push himself back to his feet, but Faarooq hits a series on knees to the back of his head and neck. The Challenger pulls Smith back to his feet and whips him into the turnbuckle, then drives his shoulder into the Bulldog’s midsection a series of times. Irish whip into the opposite corner, this time followed up with a high back body drop.

Bulldog uses the ropes to pull himself back to his feet, but Faarooq is waiting and runs through him with a big clothesline. He arrogantly places a foot on the Champions chest, raising his fist in the Nation’s salute, but the cocky cover only earns him a one count. Again Bulldog gets back to his feet, and again the arrogance of Faarooq is on display, as he utilises Davey Boys signature delayed vertical suplex against him, this time following it up with a near fall. Another Irish Whip from the challenger, who drops his head for the back body drop again, this time however Davey Boy leaps over with a sunset flip….but before he can pull his opponent down for the pinfall attempt, Faarooq punches him right between the eyes. He drops to his knees over the chest of the Bulldog, arms raised in victory as the official makes the count…..Bulldog is able to hook his legs up under his opponents arms however, pulling Faarooq down for a pinfall attempt of his own that gets a two.

This makes Faarooq mad, and he hurls the champion into the corner, where he stomps his opponent a few times, before mounting the middle rope to deliver a series of punches. Davey Boy is able to cut the flurry off midway through however, lifting Faarooq and walking him to the middle of the ring where he connects with an inverted atomic drop….backs up…..then flattens his opponent with a last ditch clothesline that leaves both men down on the mat. There is the customary ten count as neither man is on his feet, which the pair break by a count of nine, before laying into each other with punches. Bulldog gains the advantage here and goes on an offensive spree that sees him return the favour from earlier, hitting a delayed vertical suplex of his own. He goes for the cover but Kama jumps into the apron….only to be knocked off again by a running Dropkick from Smith. Scorpio is up next, but a right hand soon sends him back to the floor. The Champion goes to run the ropes, looking to connect with a clothesline, but Nelson reaches into the ring and trips the man, sending him crashing to the mat.

The official has seen enough by now, and contemplates disqualifying Faarooq for the Nation’s interference, but instead decides the best course of action is to send Kama, Nelson and Scorpio to the back, barring them from ringside. Faarooq is absolutely livid, screaming about conspiracies and that the “man” is just trying to screw the Nation again. He goes so far as to grab the referee by his shirt, but the official doesn’t back down, telling Faarooq to concentrate on his opponent, not the man in the stripes. Faarooq turns to do just that……right into Davey Boys waiting arms, who scoops him up and crushes him with the Running Powerslam……three seconds later and the Champion retains.

Winner: …..and Still WWF Intercontinental Champion, The British Bulldog.

Rating: B

We head to the back now for the first interview segment of the evening…..not that there is much of an interview to be had. Because while Dok Hendrix does his best to get Al Snow’s thoughts heading into the next match up….but Al isn’t quite with us at the minute……

“………it wasn’t a stupid thing to do…..what would you know?……..well I didn’t see you offering yourself up, ONE of us has got to have some guts……….I am not………it’s a figure of speech……….I’m not body shaming you………..stop changing the subject…….I can do it………how can you say that?……….I’ve already gone through the broken angel man, the creepy looking clown…….the pair of big apes…….Bill here too………it’s just one more match………I did not get dropped on my head as a baby………yes I got dropped on my head on Monday, but that’s irrelevant……..well if you’re not going to be supportive then I’m not talking to you, and you can stay in the back here with Bill………yes you can…….YOU CAN!!…….watch me……Bill, hold Head!!…..”

……with that Snow thrusts Head into DeMotts hands and storms off presumably towards the ring area. Hendrix asks Bill what that was all about…..Bill looks down at Head, then shakes his own…..

“……they’ve had a disagreement…..it’s complicated…..”

“….you know that’s just a mannequin head right?…”

“….not to Al….”

Hendrix asks DeMott if he will be accompanying Snow to ringside, but Bill shakes his head, saying that Al asked him not to come out….he wants to deal with this on his own.

“….and can he?….”

“……(sigh)…..not according to Head…..”

….and with that Bill DeMott and Head also walk off, leaving Hendrix alone and confused as he sends us back to ringside.

Rating: B-

VS.

Al Snow vs. Raven

This match up has been a long time coming for Al Snow, two months of trials, tribulations, assaults and sacrifice….all boiling down to this match….Al Snow vs Raven. Raven himself has made quite the impression since his arrival, charismatic and not afraid to talk…..(even if what he says is sometimes a riddle in itself)……he has been front and centre of many angles since his arrival….including a brief interaction with Mankind, that the Announcers have been speculating about ever since.

Al does indeed make his way out alone for this one….no Head, no Bill DeMott…..that may well prove to be his downfall however, because Raven is far from alone, flanked by the four man that Al defeated during Raven’s trials…..Doink, Christopher Daniels and the Dogs of War, Wrath and Crush. Jim Ross remarks that Al Snow has never been the most stable person in the WWF, but coming out here alone for this one may well be the craziest thing he’s done yet. The bell sounds and the two men come towards each other, where Raven stands, arms outstretched, telling Al that it’s not too late to come home….the door is always open…..Snow has come too far and suffered through too much to stop now, and his answer to Raven is to tackle the man to the ground, where he delivers a succession of punches.

Raven scurries away from the onslaught, and immediately rolls to the outside less than a minute into the match, but Snow is straight after him, continuing the attack, before finally slamming Raven’s head into the safety barricade and whipping him into the ring apron, before throwing him back into the ring. Snow hits an inverted atomic drop, followed by a series of blows that drive Raven back into the corner. Snow snapmares his opponent out of the corner to the mat, before connecting with a running kneedrop. Raven tries to fight back with a trio of punches, but Snow puts a stop to that with a hard headbutt and an overhead release belly to belly. Snow is displaying a different side to himself here…..with both his aggression and technical levels turned up a notch. McMahon remarks that he’s never seen Al Snow so focused….clearly Raven has awoken something in the usually distracted competitor. Raven tries to charge at Snow, but eats a swinging neckbreaker, then again bails to the floor.

Snow is quick to follow once more though, and smashes Raven into the steel barricade….but he gets distracted by the presence of Doink, who is stalking up from the rear. Snow turns and decks the

Clown…..but this gives Raven the breather that he needed, and as Al turns and charges him, Raven is able to deliver a drop toe hold that smashes Snow face first into the steel steps. Back into the ring and Raven connects with a snap suplex, then delivers a running low dropkick to Al as he sits back up. Raven sends Snow into the ropes….Al ducks a clothesline and comes back with a sunset flip that draws him a surprise two count, but Raven kicks out, then hits Al with a running knee lift. He whips Snow hard into the corner, following it up with a hard corner clothesline which he transitions into a running bulldog for a near fall.

Raven argues the count with the official, which causes enough of a distraction that The Fallen Angel is able to reach under the bottom rope and choke the life out of Snow. Clothesline attempt by Raven is ducked and countered with a sleeper attempt by Snow, but Raven soon counters that with a back suplex to break the hold and continue on the offence. He hits a series of right hand jabs, then decks his opponent with a discus lariat which scores him another near fall. Raven hooks Snow by the head and tries for the Evenflow DDT, but Al is too close to the ropes….so instead Raven whips Al across the ring and drops his head for a back drop attempt, but Snow stops short and manages to catch Raven with a double underhook suplex.

Both men are down, Raven should be the fresher of the two, but Snow isn’t going down without a fight. Back to their feet and it’s a straight out fist fight as the competitors each try to gain the upper hand. Surprisingly its Snow who backs Raven up against the ropes, then Irish Whipping him across the ring and catching him with a side slam on the rebound. Raven gets thrown hard into the turnbuckles and Al backs up ready to charge…..he can’t go anywhere though, because Doink has his foot gripped from on the floor. Snow finally shakes himself free (kicking his captor in the face for good measure) and runs the length of the ring looking to crush his opponent……unfortunately for him, Daniels has jumped onto the apron and pulled Raven out of harms way at the last moment, meaning Snow eats the turnbuckles……

……Bill DeMott has obviously seen enough, and comes running down the aisle, still with Head in his hands. Snow yells at him to get back to the locker rooms before he gets hurt, but DeMott isn’t listening…..instead he starts clearing house at ringside, knocking both Doink and Daniels silly with a shot off Head a piece…….there’s four men at ringside however…..and Wrath and Crush soon over power Bill, who despite his best efforts is on the receiving end of a Double Chokeslam onto the floor…..he probably should have followed Al’s advice and stayed in the back……especially since Snow was now paying closer attention to what’s happening on the outside of the ring, rather than what’s happening inside…..which is why Raven is able to spin him around and then drive his skull into the canvas with the Evenflow DDT…..three seconds later and Raven has his first Pay Per View victory of his WWF career.

Winner: Raven

Rating: B-

The bell rings and Raven gets passed a microphone from Wrath. He stands over the fallen Al Snow, then looks down at DeMott at ringside……

“……you should have heeded my advice Al……I told you….you can’t choose your family, but you CAN choose your friends…..and you?…..YOU need to make better choices….before your best friend, becomes your worst nightmare…..Quote the Raven, Nevermore!….”

Rating: B

While Raven and co exit the ring area, we head straight to the back, where Ahmed Johnson is standing by with Dok Hendrix, who asks, with his match against Scott Hall coming up next, what’s Ahmed’s plan to counteract the KLiQ? Ahmed says that he doesn’t need a plan, all he needs are these two fists…..”and my foot up Scott Hall’s ass”……he says that Scott and the KLiQ brought this all on themselves, after jumping Ahmed on Raw a few weeks back, where Scott Hall dropped him with a Razors Edge…..he says tonight it’s Hall, but tells the rest of the KLiQ not to worry, their time will come, and when it does……“you’re going down”…….

Rating: B

VS.

Ahmed Johnson vs. Scott Hall

Ahmed Johnson might be confident in his own abilities, but taking just one member of the KLiQ down will be hard enough…..let alone the whole group…..and unfortunately for Johnson, it’s not just ONE of the KLiQ that he has to contend with tonight, because Sean Waltman has opted to accompany the Bad Guy to the ring….and as everyone knows by now, the KLiQ are far from shy about using illegal tactics to ensure the upper hand…..so much so, that Hall is at it the moment the bell rings. The two men go for the collar and elbow, but Hall quickly jabs a thumb to the eye oh Ahmed before they can lock up…..hits him with a series of right hands…..then scoops the big man up and launches him across the ring with an impressive looking fall away slam. So impressed with his own move is Hall, that he is too busy bending through the ropes giving Waltman the “too sweet” sign to notice that Ahmed Johnson completely no sold the attack, and is instantly back on his feet, waiting for Hall to turn around.

Hall finally does just that, and is met by a rampaging Ahmed, who clotheslines the hell out of him, straight over the top rope and to the floor. Waltman is over quick as a flash to check on the condition of his buddy….he should have probably stayed where he was though, because Ahmed comes flailing over the top like a graceless boulder with a plancha that flattens both KLiQ men. Back inside the ring and Ahmed stays on the attack, hammering The Bad Guy around every side of the squared circle, before finally laying him out with some kind of one armed chokeslam/sit out spinebuster hybrid. This brings Waltman back into the action, as he hops up onto the apron, only to get sent flying from an Ahmed Johnson shoulder block that sends him sailing through the air and crashing into the steel barricade. It was an impressive sight, but it caused Ahmed to take his eye off the game, and Hall was quick to capitalise with a running knee to the kidneys and a back suplex to gain the advantage.

Finally in control, Hall slows the pace right down. The Bad Guy might not be best known for his technical abilities, but he can certainly out-wrestle Ahmed, who usually relies on brute strength to carry him through his matches. Hall catches a hammerlock on Johnson, then drives him down to one knee….transitioning into an armbar….and then arrogantly paintbrushing his opponent around the back of the head….if there’s one thing Scott Hall does well, it’s play a cocky p###k. He comes off the second turnbuckle with an ax-handle attempt, but Ahmed snatches him mid-air with a bear hug…..Waltman is again up on the apron like a shot, drawing the attention of the official, and Hall is able to break the hold by raking the eyes, then (taking advantage of Waltman’s distraction) hits Johnson right in the Johnson with a low blow.

Hall tosses Ahmed to the outside, and now it’s his turn to conveniently attract the attention of the referee, while Waltman gains a modicum of revenge for eating the steel barricade, by delivering a series of martial arts style kicks to the big man on the floor. Back inside the ring and the Bad Guy continues his attack. He picks up Ahmed, grabbing him by the scruff of the neck and the back of the tights (cue gratuitous buttock shot) and drives him shoulder first between the turnbuckles, hard into the steel post behind them. Johnson tries to fight back with a couple of right hands, but Hall cuts that off, dropping him throat first onto the top rope with a stun gun, then following right up with a bulldog that almost gets him the three count.

Hall indicates that he’s got this one in the bag, dusting his hands off and then motioning for the Razors Edge. He can’t quite get the big man up though, and Ahmed ends up backdropping out of it. This clearly annoys Hall, who is back to his feet, whipping Johnson hard into the corner. He follows him in and delivers a series of stinging slaps to the face…..which does nothing more that wake Ahmed up….and severely p##s him off…..so much so that when Hall tries to hip toss him out of the corner, a wide eyed Ahmed blocks it, spins him around so that Hall is in the corner, delivers a series of hard right hands of his own, then hip tosses the Bad Guy halfway across the ring.

Halls woken the sleeping giant now, and Ahmed goes on the attack with a series of hard hitting, high impact moves, that leaves The Bad Guy stunned. Ahmed connects with a spinebuster that very nearly gains him the three count, but Hall manage to get his shoulder up at the last second. Waltman is AGAIN back up onto the apron, this time Ahmed grabs him by the neck and tears his (gimmicked) KLiQ shirt right off his body, before beginning to shove it in his mouth. Scott Hall comes flying across the ring, looking to blindside Ahmed, but Johnson moves at the last minute and the two KLiQ men collide, with Waltman once more leaving his mark on the safety barrier. Hall staggers back to the middle of the ring and Ahmed spins him around, catches the double underhook, and drives him to the mat with the Pearl River Plunge……and with Waltman still down and out on the floor, there is no one there to break up the three count that follows…..Ahmed picks up a big, big victory here tonight against Scott Hall and the KLiQ.

Winner: Ahmed Johnson

Rating: B

Next up we go to some pre-recorded comments from the contestants in the next match…..first off it’s the Steiner Brothers, who say that Sid might have been able to cheat his way to victory on Monday Night Raw….but tonight, he won’t be that lucky…..(the camera pans back to show Rick Steiner bouncing a baseball bat up and down on the palm of his hand)….because Rick will be there at ringside…..and he’s coming prepared to dish out some “Justice” of his own, if those pig farmers try to stick their snouts into the match again.

From there we go straight to the locker room of the Million Dollar Corporation, where DiBiase is standing by with Big Psycho Sid and Southern Justice. Deebs tells us that Scott Steiner is going to discover exactly the same thing that his meat headed brother discovered on Raw…….and that is the fact that any little family run business on the corner, will be closing up shop and moving into the poor house, when the Corporations come into town…….there is a reason that Burger King is a world wide conglomerate……and Smiths Family Burgers isn’t…….and the reason for that is simple….it all comes down to who has the money and who has the power……and who doesn’t…..and the Steiner Peons don’t have the wealth….(Ted turns and looks at Southern Justice)….or the backing that The Million Dollar Corporation does.

DiBiase breaks out into his signature cackle, but Sid cuts that off, placing a big hand on his managers shoulder. He tells Ted that he though he had made himself clear on Raw…..he is the Master and the Ruler of the World….and he doesn’t need help beating up a Steiner…..or both Steiner’s for that matter…..he doesn’t need Southern Justice’s help…..he doesn’t want Souther Justice’s help…..and if they come down to that ring and interfere in Sid’s match…..then it might just be the last thing that they do. Sid tightens his grip on DiBiase’s shoulder a little bit, asking if he makes himself clear? To which Ted gulps that yes, he is crystal clear…..no Southern Justice…..

Rating: B-

VS.

Scott Steiner (w/Rick Steiner) vs. Psycho Sid (w/Ted DiBiase)

It’s time for two big hosses to go to work now…..and you know that this one is going to be a knock down drag out affair. Scott will be looking to fare better against Sid than his brother did when the two men faced off on Raw…..although you could argue that without the (unwanted) help of Souther Justice, it may well have been Sid that found himself on the losing end that night. Sid has made it pretty clear that he doesn’t want Canterbury and Knight sticking their noses into THIS match…..and DiBiase has agreed……but then the Million Dollar Man has never been known for his honesty, so time will tell.

If you were looking for a five star classic out of this one then you were definitely looking in the wrong place, as this was a far from refined affair….but then this is Sid we are talking about…..and while Scott Steiner has yet to put in a bad showing since his break out programme with Shawn Michaels….(and again tonight, while this isn’t a five star match, Scotty puts on a five star display)…..the only man to drag anything even resembling a five star match out of Sid is Bret Hart. Regardless of that the crowd is massively into this….Scott’s popularity has grown in leaps and bounds…..and Sid……well Sid is just never not over. He and Ahmed Johnson are two of the biggest lumbering behemoths in the WWF….and yet the fans love them regardless.

You may have noticed that I’ve spent longer talking about the combatants than the match itself……that’s because there are very few moves to actually call…..Sid and Steiner quite literally just beat the hell out of each other….as hard and as often as possible. There’s no lock up when the bell rings, just two big bulls hammering each other with right hands. Sid powers Scotty all the way back to into the corner, drives a knee into his midsection, then delivers a series of clubbing blows to the back of the neck….Steiner quickly shakes that off though and grips Sid around the waist…..spinning around and hitting a very impressive belly to belly on the near seven footer. Sid gets back to his feet and Scotty runs at him, connecting with a big Steinerline….which rocks Sid, but the big man doesn’t go down. Steiner runs the rope and tries for a second shot, this time however Sid gets the Big Boot up, almost taking his opponents head off.

And so the match goes…..no one ever really manages to sustain a serious amount of offence, before his opponent counters with a big move of his own. DiBiase looks to be contemplating interfering on a few occasions, but if a glare from Sid doesn’t put him off, then the sound of Rick Steiner slamming his baseball bat against the ring steps does. The match inevitably spills to the outside, where the action is just as (if not more) hard hitting. Both men get a taste of the ring post, Steiner because Sid slams him into it and Sid because Steiner moves when The big man charges at him. Sid taunts Rick Steiner a little bit on the floor, daring him to use the baseball bat….”if you’ve got the guts little man”….Rick obviously does have the guts, and goes to wind up the bat…..it’s only Scott’s interjection that prevents it, reminding his brother that he’s here for Southern Justice, “….I got this…..”

The two men get back into the ring and continue to hammer each other, with Sid finally managing to sustain a small portion of uninterrupted offence. He catches Steiner with a Chokeslam, and is only half a second away from victory before Scott weakly gets a shoulder up. Sid stands over and screams to the crowd that he’s the man, before signalling for the Powerbomb that would surely end the match. He does get the Steiner Brother up…..but in a last ditch effort, Scott manages to reverse it….not in a Frankensteiner per say, Sid is far too big for that……but he does hit a hurricanrana of sorts, which is enough to put the big man down. Scott crawls over to his opponent and mounts his back, locking in that Camel Clutch hold that he has utilised as of late……and that’s when things get interesting…..

…….because surely no one expected the Million Dollar Man to keep his word….and with Sid fading fast, Mark Canterbury and Dennis Knight come charging down to ringside. Knight and DiBiase draw the attention of the official their way, while Canterbury slides into the ring……before he has a chance to do anything, Rick is in there like a flash, bat in hand, putting himself between Canterbury and his Brother. Knowing that Rick will keep Southern Justice at bay, Scott keeps the Camel Clutch locked on…..unfortunately that leaves his head wide open…….

……..for Rick Steiner to turn around……

……and nail his brother right between the eyes with the baseball bat……

…….Scott goes down like a sack of s##t, unconscious and unaware of the betrayal he has just suffered. The crowd are not happy……and let Rick Steiner know it…..not that he cares, because he rolls the still groggy Sid on top of his brother and he and Canterbury both exit the ring. DiBiase turns the ref around…..and three seconds later, Psycho Sid is your winner.

Winner: Psycho Sid

Rating: B

If the crowd was shocked by the shocking betrayal they just witnessed, they were even more shocked by the events that followed…..because with both Sid and Steiner still down, DiBiase, Southern Justice and Rick Steiner re-enter the ring, where the Million Dollar Man retrieves a bulging envelope from his inside jacket pocket and passes it to Rick. Steiner opens it up and checks the contents, nods his head, then hands the package back to DiBiase for safe keeping…..because he needs both hands free for what comes next…..

……because what comes next, is Rick Steiner, Mark Canterbury and Dennis Knight, beating the living hell out of Scott Steiner….so much so, that when Sid finally regains his senses and pulls himself to his feet, even he can’t believe what he’s seeing. DiBiase tells Sid to join in the fun, but Sid is having none of it, screaming at DiBiase that he wanted to do this by himself, before shoving his manager to one side and storming out of the ring to the back…..

…..of course that doesn’t really help Scott Steiner any, who by now has been dragged up to a corner, where Rick is slapping him across the face, trying to bring him around…..he wants his brother to know exactly who it is that’s kicking his ass. Finally he tosses Scott to Mark Canterbury, who hoists the helpless Steiner up onto his shoulder……just as Rick makes it to the top rope, where he comes flying, driving his brothers skull into the mat with his patented Bulldog. Finally satisfied he retrieves his “package” from DiBiase and the four men stand with their hands raised over the broken and betrayed Scott Steiner.

Rating: B+

VS.

Loser Leaves the WWF Match

Chris Candido vs. Triple H (w/Sunny)

Well…..that escalated quickly……in the space of four weeks Chris Candido has, made his return to the WWF an eight month absence…..cost Triple H the Intercontinental Championship…..wrestled his first Raw match of 1996…..and now tonight, may well be wrestling his LAST match, as a World Wrestling Federation superstar……because make no mistake, this is no Terry Funk retirement angle, this is as real as it gets….following this match, either Hunter Hearst Helmsley or Chris Candido will be finding themselves unemployed.

You’ve got to admire Candido’s guts to be proposing a match like this in the first place…..especially against an opponent the calibre of Triple H…..not only did the man have an impressive undefeated record……not only is he a former WWF Intercontinental Champion…..but to top it all off, he’s a member of the KLiQ…..a group that most people would not go out of their way to antagonise…..and yet, after claiming he has nothing left to lose, here we are.

Once the introductions are made and the two men are in the ring, Sunny makes sure to let Candido know that she’s picked the winning side….and once her man wins tonight, Chris can run off back to the bingo halls where he belongs, and get out of HER spotlight. To his credit, Candido ignores Sunny’s barbs, and instead is utterly focused on his opponent. The two tie up to start the match, neither man gains the upper hand however and they end up jostling around the ring until the official finally has to separate them in the corner. Helmsley raises his arms indicating a clean break, but actually drives his knee into the midsection of his opponent. Hunter swings a right arm but Candido blocks it and turns it into an arm wringer, which he then transitions into a hammerlock and a drop toe hold….hopping back to his feet and performing some jumping jacks to mock Hunter.

Not to be outdone, Helmsley manages to apply a Hammerlock of his own, but Candido runs at the ropes, before dropping down and sending Helmsley tumbling through and to the outside. Hunter re-enters the ring, but doesn’t fare much better as Candido slowly dismantles him, until Helmsley is able to finally put a stop to that with a sucker punch and a hard clothesline. Candido is able to reverse an Irish Whip attempt to send Helmsley crashing into the corner, but posts himself with an attempted shoulder barge, when Sunny pulls her man out of harms way. Chris comes out of the corner, clearly favouring his shoulder, and Hunter is quick to capitalise with an arm breaker…..then Irish Whips Candido hard back into the corner, shoulder first. Again Candido comes out of the corner, but this time he walks right into a huge backbreaker from his opponent. Candido has a brief flurry of offensive moves in an attempt to make a comeback…..it doesn’t last long however, after Hunter connects with a swinging neckbreaker.

Back on the offence Helmsley whips Chris into the ropes, connecting with a high knee on the return. He pulls his opponent back to his feet, but Candido reverses it into a small package, which was a hairs width away from winning him the match…..but Hunter got the shoulder up, then almost takes Candido’s head off with a clothesline when the two men get back to their feet. Final Thoughts

The biggest shock to most of our fans coming out of In Your House, wasn’t Chris Jericho’s star making display……or Rick Steiner’s shocking turn on his brother Scott……or Raven’s sacrifice to save Mankind……no the biggest shock coming out of In Your House, was the fact that Mr. Paul Levesque…..Hunter Hearst Helmsley…..Triple H……is no longer employed by the World Wrestling Federation. I think most people believed that we had seen the last of Chris Candido…..either another Drug violation, or simply having nothing for him…..but nothing could be further from the truth…..the plain truth is that Helmsley requested his release…..and I was more than happy to grant it. Apparently (according to Mr. Helmsley) I didn’t know how to book a decent storyline, refused to put an obvious main event talent (his words not mine) in the main event…..didn’t know how to book an obvious star like him correctly…..etc, etc, etc…..

Obviously the rest of the KLiQ weren’t happy by this (the real life kliq that is…..not the on-screen one, because Justin Credible was f#####g ecstatic backstage) and cited these very reasons for their buddy leaving. However when I pointed out the fact that a) Helmsley had a pretty damn good undefeated run…… b) a WWF Intercontinental Title reign….. c) got paired with the number one female in wrestling today….. and d) had a pretty dominant run along with the rest of the KLiQ, they finally started to see things my way……and the fact was, that for every dickish move he made, I either fined his stupid ass, or punished him on screen…..he didn’t like the fact that I wouldn’t let him get away with being a douche…..and he took his ball and went home….they were still p####d don’t get me wrong…..but now at least some of their anger was directed at Mr. Levesque instead of just me. Nash said he could understand him leaving for a better deal somewhere else, but Hunter has just made himself unemployed with no official offer on the table anywhere else….and that’s just bad business sense……

Either way, goodbye Hunter Hearst Helmsley…..and good luck in your future endeavours….you for one will not be missed by me.

In other KLiQ news, a minor shock to some saw Ahmed Johnson pick a victory up over Scott Hall…..and in pretty convincing fashion. Scott Hall truly is the total package…..a big, big man, with the wrestling abilities, psychology, star presence and microphone skills that would put most others to shame…..Scott Hall could….and should…..be a World Champion in any promotion he’s in…..the reason Scott Hall HASN’T been a World Champion in every promotion that he’s in however….is Scott Hall….Scott has let his demons get the best of him on more than one occasion, and is simply too unreliable to push to the very top. The last thing you want come Wrestlemania, is your World Champion not turning up to the event, (or your Intercontinental Champion not showing up to the Royal Rumble as we saw this year) because he’s busy getting wasted down the ‘Friendly Tap’…..and now, at 37 years of age….probably due to the years of alcohol and pharmaceutical abuse, Scott is showing every single year of that age, and unfortunately it’s beginning to effect his ring work. Nothing overly noticeable yet, but he’s definitely on the decline…..I only hope he can stay straight and sober long enough to enjoy however many years he has left in the ring, because the man truly is a natural….as well as a tragic example of wasted talent.

Barry Windham did Rocky Maivia a huge favour giving him the pinfall at In Your House….I hope the kid realises what a privilege it should be to get the victory over a veteran of Barry’s calibre….especially when Windham went all out and broke his foot in the process. It’s nothing to serious, and he should be back in action within a month….luckily no one was to blame, it was just a freak accident, but at least the Samoan’s had the decency to seek Barry out after the show to check on him….good form. Anyway, that’s another Pay Per View down…..time to build towards the next.

OOC: On the subject of Triple H. Helmsley has been furious with me for the best part of four-five months now…..made even worse by numerous fines and warnings…..the fight with Scorpio, the fight with Credible…..another fight the day of IYH with Barry Horowitz of all people….he was a royal pain in my backside….and asked for his release the day after Summerslam. Now I could have refused and stroked his ego and probably got him back on side given enough time, money and willingness to look the other way to his crap…..then I thought f##k it…..he might be one of the biggest names in Wrestling today, but that’s with hindsight, and if this game was real, no way would I be pegging 1996 Hunter Hearst Helmsley as a 12 times (or whatever he is now) World Champion……and in fact, with the grief he causes backstage, I’d probably hold the door open for him to leave. So I quickly cobbled together a way to write him out…..just so happens that Candido was back from rehab at the same time. I was originally going to edit his contract down to one day remaining and let it lapse, but it turns out putting Candido over on this show was enough for him to request his release again, saving me the bother. Mr. Stephanie McMahon is now officially unemployed.
