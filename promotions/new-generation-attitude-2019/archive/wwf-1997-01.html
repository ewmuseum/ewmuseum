Week 1, January 1996

Arrowhead Pond

ATT: 17,354

TV: 21.05

It’s a new year here in the WWF….but the more things change, the more things stay the same…..because we are starting 1997 off with the group that arguably dominated 1996…..despite their obvious differences……The KLiQ. Shawn Michaels is out with the WWF Championship draped over the shoulder of the beautiful Sunny….who is in turn draped all over the WWF Champion. Hall and Nash have the Tag Team straps around their waists…..while Waltman and Credible bring up the rear…..and while the group should be on top of the world, they look far from happy. In fact both Hall and Nash are visibly limping from the vicious steel chair assault at the hands of Steve Austin and Brian Pillman last week.

Michaels takes a microphone…..and says he wants to address a few things……the first, being Steve Austin and Brian Pillman….he says that these two wormed their way into the Royal Rumble match on the back of cheap shots and cheap tactics…..and attacking people from behind….a trend that they continued last week, when they ruined Shawn Michaels Christmas gift to his boys…..but he says that’s alright….because tonight, his boys are going to ruin THEIR New Years…..it didn’t have to be this way….they had what they wanted…..they were in the Rumble match……

“……sure the fact that you’re going in at number one and number two means you were never gonna win it……after all, there’s only one man who’s gone in there at number one and lasted the distance, and believe me, neither one of you are Shawn Michaels……”

….but now, after last weeks actions, they won’t even make it to the Rumble….because they’ve gotta face Sean Waltman and Justin Credible in Tag team action….and you can be damn sure that Hall and Nash here will be looking for some payback too…..because the fact of the matter is that you shouldn’t even be in the Rumble….and neither should Bret and Owen Hart……but that’s ok…..because Shawn’s good buddy Big Sexy, he’s going put the Harts out of the Rumble before they even enter…..Hall and Nash are going to do what the KLiQ do best…..embarrass the Hart family once again, before rocking up into the Rumble match and tossing Austin and Pillman out of the match, and out of the title picture once and for all……along with that other wannabe pretender to the throne….Scott Steiner…..

“……Steiner, you’re a big boy I’ll give you that…..but just because you managed to beat your little inbred brother to win the number TWO title in the WWF, doesn’t mean squat…..and the fact that you’re now in the Rumble match, doesn’t mean squat…..because first of all, you’ve got to actually keep hold of that Title between now and the PPV….and second of all….even if you do….either Big Sexy or the Bad Guy….Waltman or Credible…..someone is tossing your inflated ass over the top rope and back down to your level….competing for the SECOND tier title…..the starter belt…..the “go to” belt for former tag team stars…..and I should know…..because I’ve been there, done that……..except the difference between you and me……and as much as I personally dislike the guy…..Bret Hart……is the Intercontinental Championship was just a stepping stone for us……a quick stop on our way to the top……but for you, it’s as good as it’s gonna get……”

……Which brings Shawn onto his final point…..and the latest in a long line of men who think they have what it takes to hang with the Heartbreak Kid…..except this man…..is a Deadman……

“…….and if you weren’t….you will be by the time I’m done with you…..you want a shot at the Title Deadman, I got no problem showing the Boogyman who the REAL Big Bad wolf of the WWF is…..because I’m not one of your usual sideshow freak opponents…..I’m not some burnt up lumbering mute, some hair pulling fruit loop ……and I’m certainly not some second rate Jim Jones wannabe…..I am the World Wrestling Federation Champion…..I’m the Icon, the Main Event….the Showstopper……I am the Reason for the Ratings and I’ve beaten EVERYONE that’s been put in front of me……and I’ll continue to beat everyone that’s put in front of me….Man and Deadman alike……”

……he tells the Undertaker to save his light shows and his parlour tricks, and concentrate on making a new head stone for his career……that reads “here lies the Undertaker…..he thought he could hang with the Heartbreak Kid….but everyone knows that there’s NOBODY in the WWF…..that’s hung like Shawn Michaels.

Rating: A*

VS.

WWF World Tag Team Championship Match

The KLiQ (c) vs. The Hardy Boys

It’s time for the first Title defence of 1997…..and it’s the KLiQ’s WWF Tag Team Championships that are on the line, against two men who picked up arguably the biggest win of their young careers at last months In Your House, when they defeated the legendary Legion of Doom….and if flying high on the momentum of that victory wasn’t enough to give The Hardy’s the edge over the dominant KLiQ duo, the fact that both Hall and Nash are pretty banged up, thanks to the chair wielding assault of Steve Austin and Brian Pillman. Jim Ross says that if ever there was a better night than tonight for the Hardy’s to take home the gold, then he doesn’t know when….because the ball is certainly in Matt and Jeffs favour tonight.

The Hardys spent a good portion of last year counting the lights as jobbers to the stars…..they certainly had more success in the second half of the year, as highlighted by this Championship opportunity tonight….and they go all out in their effort to win the gold…..and they put on a hell of a showing too, proving that their victory over Hawk and Animal was no fluke…..and with Kevin Nash out brawling on the floor with Matt Hardy, Jeff Hardy is perched up high on the top rope, preparing to fly with a Swanton Bomb onto the prone Scott Hall flat on his back on the mat………

…….when the same two mystery men who made their presence felt at In Your House appear at ringside, having made their way over the safety barrier from the crowd. They don’t physically interject themselves into the match, but their mere presence is enough to distract Jeff long enough for Scott Hall to regain his faculties, climb to his feet and hook hold of Jeff Hardy for the Razors Edge…….and three seconds later, this one is all over.

Winner(s): and Still WWF Tag Team Champions…..The KLiQ
Rating: B-

Hall and Nash grab their titles and happily return to the back to rest their battered bodies, leaving the two men to quickly slide into the ring, where they begin putting the boots to young Jeff. Matt is in there quickly to try and stop the assault, but it’s two against one and he stands no chance. Soon the two newcomers have both Hardy Boys down and out…..and they both pick up a steel chair and a microphone a piece. They don’t tell us anything groundbreaking……although we do find out their names…..Edge and Christian…..and they are here to show the Hardy’s….and the world…..that not all young teams need to be walked though their careers, hiding under the coat tails of has-beens……Matt and Jeff might have needed the Legion of Doom to make an impression for them here in the WWF, but Edge and Christian are perfectly capable of making an impression all by themselves.

These two are both certainly better talkers than the Brothers Hardy….and they get their point across perfectly….only to toss their microphones to one side and stand over the heads of a still down Matt and Jeff…..raising their steel chairs high, looking for the final blow…….

………when Hawk and Animal come charging down to ringside…..obviously not happy about being branded as has-beens…..and they hit the ring looking for a fight…..unfortunately they aren’t going to get one…..because Edge and Christian have bailed and are already disappearing into the sea of fans at ringside. Animal checks on Matt and Jeff, while Hawk screams loudly into a nearby camera that anytime those two goldilocks looking punks want to see just how much of a has-been the Legion of Doom are, they’re more than welcome to try their luck.

Rating: B-

VS.

Chris Candido vs. Lance Storm


It’s time for singles action now, and it’s Lance Storm facing off against Chris Candido. Both of these two men had a very good run towards the end of 1996. Candido may have been unsuccessful in his bid to become the WWF Light Heavyweight Champion, but he did rid the World Wrestling Federation of Hunter Hearst Helmsley (who is currently slumming it down in the minor leagues) as well as holding his own against the KLiQ and finally shedding the Body Donna stigma…..Skip may be no more, but Chris Candido is certainly here to stay. Lance Storm on the other hand has transformed himself from independent journeyman, to legitimate athlete….and aside from breaking his ass, he has been virtually unstoppable since his arrival.

Both men will be looking to continue their good run into 1997, unfortunately only one man can come out on top tonight…..and after proving that he is no Marty Jannetty, with his victory over (funnily enough) Marty Jannetty last week, Storm keeps up the winning ways here tonight…..forcing Candido to submit to the Canadian Maple Leaf.

Winner: Lance Storm
Rating: B-

Following that we head to the back, where Dude Love is doing a snazzy little disco two step jive, showing everyone why he truly is the coolest cat in the room……unfortunately the party of one comes to a screeching halt when Raven walks into the room, with a look on his face that suggests he was either sucking on a lemon before he entered….or he just really dislikes Dude Love…..either way he tells the Mayor of Mandible Town to quit acting like an idiot….and meet him in the ring, as we go to commercial.

Rating: N/A

And we’re back……..and we are back live in the arena, where Raven is waiting for the Dude Love chants to die down with a sour look on his face, before finally turning to the Dude and beginning to speak……

“………destiny is a funny thing……often we meet it head on, walking the very road we took to avoid it in the first place…..we can’t hide from destiny, we can only delay the inevitable…..you tried hiding behind a mask, but it didn’t work……you can try and hide behind this ridiculous tie-dyed facade…..but it won’t work……you can’t escape your destiny anymore than you can escape death itself……you have suppressed your true self for so long now that it must be eating you up from the inside out……to hold back the violence…..the carnage…..the destruction…..the pure unadulterated thirst for chaos…..it’s enough to make anyone snap…….”

Raven doesn’t look like he’s anywhere near done talking, but the Dude holds a hand up to finally cut the tirade off…….

“…….Raven baby, let the Dude cut you off right there daddio…….you need to stop happenin’ the chatterin’ and focus your audio….there’s no holding anything back for the Dude….other than the need to jive daddy……everything you THINK the Dude was repressing, are simply the things that kept me regressing…..now this cool cat is letting it all hang out and I’m seeing so clearly I’ve got X-Ray eyes…..because it’s not pain that makes the world rotate…..and it’s not violence that keeps the third rock from the sun spinning on it’s axis…..it’s love daddio…..love makes the world go round…..and all YOU need, to turn that frown upside down…..is a big hug from the Dude……”

With that the Dude drops his microphone, and wraps his big, hairy, scar filled arms around the Despot of the Downtrodden. Raven stands completely rigid for a heartbeat….before shoving the Dude away and slapping the taste out of his mouth. He tells the Dude that he is a patient man…..but the only one that sees things clearly around here….is Raven…..he says that he knows the true Mick Foley…..not his given name…..not his chosen names…..he knows his true name……because while he can wear the mask of Mankind…..and hide behind the naivety of Dude Love…..he can only suppress his true self for so long……and his true self…..is Cactus Jack……

……and with that the video screen flickers to life with a montage of some of Cactus Jack’s depravities dished out on the Japanese Deathmatch scene, starting off with Cactus carrying a barbed wire crucifix to the ring, and ending with a shot of the bloodied and battered Cactus Jack, proudly holding aloft the trophy that officially declares him the King of the Deathmatch. Raven has deliberately had the video edited so that the final image stays up on the screen….he points to it, telling the Dude to look…..look at the happiness on the face of that beautiful monster…..look at the destruction he left in his wake……look at yourself…..look at your true face…..look……and tell me how you can deny…..what is plain for the world to see……you’re not Mick Foley…..you’re not Mankind……you’re certainly not Dude Love……you are Cactus Jack…..the King of the Deathmatch…..the monster under the bed…..the whispered warning to miscreants everywhere……

“……you are Cactus Jack……and I WILL set you free……Quote the Raven…..Nevermore……”

With that Raven abruptly turns, leaves, and heads to the back……leaving the Dude to stand and stare at the carnage his alter ego caused……what happens in Vegas may stay in Vegas…..but can what happens in a Japanese Deathmatch, really stay locked away in Mick Foleys psyche? Apparently Raven doesn’t think so.

Rating: A*

VS.

Marc Mero & Bart Gunn (w/Sable) vs. Billy Gunn & Jesse James (w/Mr. Perfect)


It’s tag team action up next, as the combination of Marc Mero and Bart Gunn find themselves facing off against Billy Gunn and Mr. Perfects newest client, Jesse James. Mero and Bart Gunn have both been embroiled in one feud or another involving Billy Gunn for quite some time now…..but James is a new addition to the ongoing battles…..however having missed out on making his mark at Survivor Series due to injury, this talented talker is looking to make an impact one way or another.

As far as this match goes, it was pretty good. Nothing groundbreaking, but certainly entertaining enough. The combination of Mero and Bart certainly got their fair share of offence in….but Gunn and James look to have possible Tag Team Gold aspirations in the future, and manage to pick up a victory over the two singles stars after Billy put Bart away with the Famouser.

Winner(s): Billy Gunn and Jesse James
Rating: B-

Following that match up, the screeching guitar of Bret Hart plays throughout the arena, signalling the arrival of The Hart Foundation. All three members make their way down to the ring…..and while we do know that we will see the British Bulldog participating in the Royal Rumble match, Bret and Owens future is very much in the air…..because in order for them to earn their entry, they will first have to defeat the KLiQ, Kevin Nash and Scott Hall…..and as the Harts know all too well, that’s no easy feat in itself. Bret and Owen seem pretty up beat in their chances however, stating that they will fight until their dying breath…..they will compete twice in one night…..and one of the three of them, WILL be headlining Wrestlemania…..and there’s nothing that the KLiQ can do to stop that.

That brings out Vince McMahon…..who is rather unusually flanked by The Big Red Machine, Kane……and The Bizarre One, Goldust. Vince tells them that while they may think that there is nothing the KLiQ can do to stop them……there is something Vince McMahon can do to stop them and that is, make their lives extremely difficult……

“……you see, you may only have to defeat Kevin Nash and Scott Hall to earn your place in the Royal Rumble match…..what condition you are in when you GET there is anybody’s guess…..because you see, next week…..YOU Owen Hart, shall be going one on one…….with Kane……and not to leave your brother out…..because Bret Hart will be facing off against……well, you guessed it…….Goldust…..and as an added incentive, should either of these two fine gentlemen inflict enough punishment on you that they are able to gain a victory…….then they will be able to choose their OWN entry number, to the Royal Rumble match……”

McMahon informs us that he has already discussed the matter with Jim Cornette…..and should his Big Red Monster defeat Owen Hart……..then Kane will be entering the Royal Rumble…………at number thirty…..before turning to the Bizarre one, and asking what prize would it take to ensure victory for him next week?…..

“……’I’ve got a golden chance to make my way…..and with a golden ticket, it’s a golden day’…….Willy Wonka and the Chocolate Factory; 1971……and I want mmmmmm, the golden ticket…….”

Vince looks rather uneasy being stood quite so close to Goldust…..but the mention of the golden ticket appears to confuse him slightly…..

“……well I’m not entirely sure what golden ticket you………

“…….the golden number…….I’m ready for my close-up Mr. McMahon…..I’m ready for the whole world to see me…..shhhhhine……”

“……well, unfortunately the number 30 spot has already been claimed by Kane…..but should he fail to defeat Owen Ha……..”

Goldust places a finger on the lips of McMahon to stop him speaking, causing Vince to go about as wide eyed as possible……

“……..no, no, no, no……..just like everyone else in the World Wrestling Federation, you misunderstood me…..the golden number isn’t number 30…….the golden number…..is 27…….three men have won from that position…..and i will make a fabulous fourth…..the tail end of the match…..the rear of the rumble…..and believe me, I love nothing more……mmmm….than coming in from behind……”

McMahon slowly steps away from Goldust following that remark, edging closer and closer to Kane (probably the only time someone has willingly moved closer to the Big Red Machine)…..before agreeing to Goldust’s prize……and so the matches are set…..next week Bret Hart will be facing Goldust…..while Owen Hart has the unenviable task of going one on one…..with Kane.

Rating: A*

VS.

Rikishi vs. The Rock


It’s time to get back to the action now…..and the Sons of Samoa are looking to gain a little bit of payback on the Nation of Domination, for costing them the WWF Tag Team Championships at In Your House…..tonight it’s the Big Kishi going up against the black sheep of the family…..The Rock. The Rock has really been singled out as one to watch here in the World Wrestling Federation…..a young man with definite Championship pedigree in his future and tonight he shows why, running rings around the big Samoan…..at least until Rikishi catches him with a huge Samoan drop that slows the pace down and puts it back in his favour.

As always however, the Nation are quick to use their superior numbers to their advantage…..and in the end, there is little Rikishi can do other than count the lights……as he’s put down and out with the Rock Bottom from the Samoan Turncoat.

Winner: The Rock
Rating: B

The Nation enter the ring following their victory, with the Rock picking up a microphone to tell…..

“…….that big piece of monkey crap, to get out of the Rocks ring, before the Rock lays another beating on him like the jabroni that he is……now the way the Rock sees it, is that by the Rock’s reckoning, at the Royal Rumble, the Nation of Domination should be just about in line to finally get a shot at those WWF Tag Team Championships…….but it didn’t go down like that…..and the Rock has to wonder why……”

The Rock hands the microphone over to Faarooq…..who tells everyone that he knows exactly why…..because regardless of whether the Harts are eligible to Challenge for a title shot or not…..they still get the prime spot on the Pay Per View…..and they STILL get to face the Champions…..even if it is non-title……

“……and who do the Nation got at the Rumble? I’ll tell you who…..nobody…..that’s who…..hell, we don’t even know, if we’re in the Royal Rumble match……but that’s ok right, because we found a way to get the pretty white boys on the show……TWICE, if they beat the KLiQ…..well I for one have had just about as much of it as I’m going to take……”

Faarooq says that it’s time that the Nation started making a statement around here…..and if they aren’t going to be given the shots they deserve by choice…..then they’re just going to have to start taking them by force.

Rating: A

VS.

Steve Austin & Brian Pillman vs. The KLiQ


Austin and Pillman have been on a bit of a sabbatical as of late……and while they haven’t seen much in ring action, they have certainly kept theirselves busy…..but while Pillman has been happy enough just to wreak mayhem, Stone Cold has been itching for a fight for weeks now…..and unfortunately for Waltman and Credible, they get the full force of his pent up aggressions.

The KLiQ are looking for payback after the vicious chair shots at the hands of these two men…..they don’t get it tonight however…….what Credible does get is a Stone Cold Stunner right in the middle of the ring…..and covered for the three count.

Winner: Steve Austin and Brian Pillman
Rating: B+

Austin may have hit Credible with a Stunner…..but he’s not finished yet….because Waltman dives into the ring after Stone Cold…..only to receive a Stunner as well. That brings Hall and Nash out from the back…..and the two men are brandishing baseball bats……Austin could give a rats ass though…..and as Pillman hops to the floor to collect two steel chairs, Stone Cold sits on the middle rope, opening it up and inviting Hall and Nash into the ring……the Tag Champs are still worse for wear from the last time they were in the same room with Austin, Pillman and two steel chairs however…..and think better of it…..instead they drag Waltman and Credible down to the outside and head to the back, telling Steve Austin and Brian Pillman that they’re going to regret this……not today though……because Stone Cold has a six pack of beer, and he’s downing every one, much to the delight of the crowd as we go to commercial.

Rating: B+

The cackling laugh of Ted DiBiase signals the arrival of the Million Dollar Corporation, as DiBiase leads Rich Steiner down the aisle….they aren’t alone though…..because Jeff Jarrett and Southern Justice are there with them. The five men enter the ring, where DiBiase takes a microphone, telling Scott Steiner that he has something in his possession that belongs to the Million Dollar Corporation……

“……..now an intelligent man would walk down that aisle and save himself a lot of pain by simply handing that Intercontinental Championship back to its rightful owner……but since Rich Steiner is now a member of my Corporation…..and you’re not……then obviously you aren’t an intelligent man…..so you can consider this a hostile takeover…….”

Rich takes the microphone, telling his brother to get his ass down to this ring, and bring Rich’s belt with him…..so big brother can give him the ass kicking of a lifetime…….

Obviously Scott Steiner has never been one to back down from a fight…..but it’s four against one here….and Southern Justice are already making their way up the aisle to meet him head on…….

…….when Psycho Sid makes his way out from the back. Southern Justice slow their pace a little, but it’s too late for them, as Sid and Steiner dismantle them in the aisle way. Big Poppa Pump charges at the ring and begins brawling with his brother……while Sid drags Jarrett to the floor and bounces his head off the safety rail all the way up the aisle and to the back……looks like the playing field just levelled for tonight’s main event.

Rating: B+

VS.

WWF Intercontinental Championship Match

Scott Steiner (c) vs. Rich Steiner (w/Ted DiBiase)


And speaking of the main event of the evening, here it is…..and what should be the final in a trifecta of back to back matches between the Steiner Brothers…..the roles are reversed tonight however…..as it’s Scott that has the gold around his waist, while Rich is challenging…..and there are no short cuts tonight…..if Rich Steiner wants to become a two time Intercontinental Champion, then he’s going to have to do it the old fashioned way…..beat Scott Steiner by either pinfall or submission……

……that’s easier said than done though…..because Steiner knows that holding onto his title is a one way ticket to the Royal Rumble….and if he is to be believed, nothing is going to come between himself, Shawn Michaels…..and finally ‘officially’ winning the WWF Championship that eluded him back in June of last year…..especially not his backstabbing brother Rich, who is put away for the second straight week in a row…..because right in the middle of the ring, Rich Steiner submits for what may be the first time ever…..to the Steiner Recliner…..and Big Poppa Pumps Royal Rumble dream is still alive.

Winner: and Still WWF Intercontinental Champion…..Scott Steiner
Rating: A

Scott Steiner is up on the second rope, holding his title up high and flexing his massive peaks for all the freaks in the crowd…..when his music is cut short…..because Vincent Kennedy McMahon makes his way out from the back, flanked by Shawn Michaels and the KLiQ. Vince congratulates Steiner on a successful Title defence, but assures him it won’t be that simple…..he says that yes, for as long as he holds that Championship he is eligible to enter the Royal Rumble match…..but he certainly WON’T be winning said match……because AT the Royal Rumble…..Scott Steiner WILL be putting the Intercontinental Championship on the line……against either Chris Jericho…..The British Bulldog…..Kama……or Mr. Perfect….all of whom will be in action next week in the start of a two week tournament to crown a number one contender……

“…….which means, should you be able to successfully retain your Championship at the Royal Rumble…..you will be too beat up to have a hope in hells chance of winning the Royal Rumble match…..”

Michaels takes the microphone and tells Steiner that he warned him about having ideas above his status…..he tells Steiner that he’s Intercontinental Level…..nothing more…..although maybe less……

“……and Vin Man, you were right, he will be far too beat up to win the Rumble…..but speaking of beat up…..boys…..get him…..”

Michaels orders the troops to head down the aisle…..presumably to beat up Scott Steiner…..the KLiQ are still pretty beat up themselves however, both from their matches tonight, and from the assault at the hands of Austin and Pillman last week. Hall, Nash, Waltman and Credible, wearily make their way down the aisle and surround the ring………..

….::::!!!!GONG!!!!::::….

…….suddenly the lights go out………

….::::!!!!GONG!!!!::::….

…..and when they come back on, The Undertaker is stood on the entrance ramp behind Vince McMahon and Shawn Michaels. The KLiQ freeze and stare up the aisle at their mighty leader…..who is oblivious to the Deadmans presence…..at least until he takes a step back, bumping into the unseen seven foot frame of the Undertaker…..and in something reminiscent of a B-Movie Horror Film, Shawn gulps…..and then slowly turns around until he is face to face with the Phenom himself.

The wide-eyed WWF Champion quickly tries to back away, but the Deadman is surprisingly quick, and his hand snaps out and grasps the Heartbreak Kid by the throat. Michaels flails around like a madman trying to escape, while Vince screams at the Undertaker not to do it……

…….but he does it…….

……..he lifts Shawn Michaels high up in the air……and chokeslams him down onto the entrance way with a sickening thud, before unhooking the WWF Title from around the Heartbreak Kids waist, and holding it high in the air, signifying his intentions as the show comes to a close.

Rating: A*

Final Rating: A

Week 2, January 1997

Week 2, January 1997

River Centre

ATT: 15,957

TV: 23.67

….::::!!!!GONG!!!!::::….

We are just thirteen days away from the Royal Rumble….and tonight, we are starting the show with a bang……well…..a gong……as the man who will challenge Shawn Michaels for the World Wrestling Federation Title at that very event, The Undertaker, makes his way down the aisle with his manager Paul Bearer by his side. The Deadman climbs into the ring after his usual theatrics, and says that last week….both he and Shawn Michaels both got a little taste of what their futures hold at the Royal Rumble……for Shawn Michaels, that future is one of pain and suffering…..and despair……but for the Undertaker….he will once again be standing tall at the top of the World Wrestling Federation…..with the WWF Title firmly in his cold, dead hand……and if Shawn Michaels has a problem with what the Undertaker is saying, he’s more than welcome to come out here and try and shut his mouth…….

…..that does indeed bring the Champion out…..although he doesn’t look at anything close to 100% judging by the neck brace that he is sporting….presumably a result of the Chokeslam on the entrance ramp last week…..he also appears to be missing a few things….namely Kevin Nash, Scott Hall and Sean Waltman….because while Sunny and Justin Credible are by his side, the rest of the KLiQ are nowhere to be seen. The trio come to a halt at the top of the aisle….apparently that is close enough for now…..and Michaels tells the Deadman that while he would live to do nothing more than to walk down to that ring and kick the Undertakers teeth down his throat, he’s a little bit banged up right now…..

“……courtesy of YOUR cowardly sneak attack last week…..because believe me, if the Heartbreak Kid was going to take YOU out…..he’d do it right to your face, because Shawn Michaels is more of a man than your dead ass will ever be……”

Undertaker says that’s cute….considering the fact that it’s been sneak attacks, ambushes…..and having your little KLiQ do the dirty work for you, that has been the only reason you’ve been able to hold on to that title for the last the ten months….and when that looked like it wasn’t going to work anymore, you had to get the boss to tip the scales in your favour……well The Undertaker doesn’t need anyONE or anyTHING to tip the scales in his favour…..Taker says there’s a reason that Michaels has been ducking him his entire title reign…….because all it takes is one Tombstone……and the Title is gone…..it’s just that simple…..and at the Royal Rumble The Deadman is going to take Shawn Michaels pampered little ass…..and personally send it straight to hell……

…..Shawn goes to say something, but the Deadman cuts him off….stating that the Heartbreak Kid appears to be a few men down tonight…..and promises that if Michaels some kind of diversion…..and Hall, Nash or Waltman try to jump him….it’ll be the last thing either of them will ever do…..because the Undertaker will personally bury them. The Champion appears legitimately p*ssed by that comment….stating that first of all, The Heartbreak Kid doesn’t NEED anyone to fight his battles for him…..and second of all, he doesn’t need to worry about Nash, Hall or Waltman….because (and this appears to be what is ticking the Champ off) they aren’t even here at the arena yet……

“…….but like I said, The Heartbreak Kid Shawn Michaels became the World Wrestling Federation Champion off the back of his own god given talent…..and because I’m such a downright nice guy, I took Kevin Nash and Scott Hall along for the ride with me too……I should have probably taken them along for the ride with me when I left the hotel this morning too….they might have actually made it to the show on time…..but I digress……I did it on my own and I will keep doing it on my own…..in fact, why don’t I come down to that ring right now and show you…..”

The Undertaker is more than happy to start their Royal Rumble battle a few weeks early and so welcomes the sight of Shawn Michaels storming down towards the ring…..he gets about halfway down the aisle however before he stops and clutches at his neck, before raising his microphone to tell Justin Credible to……

“…….get down to that ring and show this idiot how I’m going to kick his ass on my own…..”

“……somewhere down the line Mick, something went very wrong in your life……your psyche splintered and scattered into pieces……you got lost in your own mind, to the point that you aren’t even comfortable in your own skin…….you’re like a faulty old TV, constantly flickering from one personality to the next, unable to see past the wall of static………that’s why I’m here…..that’s why I am the one to set you free…….because just like with an old TV, sometimes all you need to do to clear the static…….is hit it…….really hard……..”

……and with that, Raven delivers a hard openhanded slap, right to the side of the Dudes head. That staggers the Dude, but it doesn’t stop Raven from unleashing another…..and another……and another…….until finally on the fifth attempt, the Dude finally catches Ravens hand, and stares at him with a look that is anything but groovy…..in fact it looks damn right scary……and Raven loves it, openly smiling as he tries to coax “Jack” out……..but just as quickly as the anger arrived, it leaves again…..and the Dude releases Ravens hand……much to the frustration of the Monarch of Misery……who finally just tackles the Dude to the mat, where he begins pummelling him with blows that the Dude doesn’t even defend against………

…….until finally……and surprisingly……Rey Mysterio comes charging down the aisle……Raven sees his arrival and gets to his feet, but gets dropped with a springboard dropkick from Rey, who then shows his own mean streak as he begins to give Raven a taste of his own medicine, before finally driving the Shepherd of the Flock from the ring…..

…..clearly still seething from the constant abuse he has received at the hands of the Flock, Mysterio is here to make sure that the Dude doesn’t suffer the same fate…..he helps Dude Love to his feet and asks if he’s ok……..the Dude smiles, says he’s cool man……then pats little Rey on the back……..

……..and drops him with a Double Underhook DDT…..apparently the Dude didn’t want the assist.

Raven smiles at that……and smiles even more as the Dude rolls out of the ring to join him in the aisleway…..and we go to commercial.

Rating: A

We are back from commercial, and we are back to an explosion of fire…..as the Big Red Monster Kane makes his way down the aisle, along with his mouthpiece Jim Cornette. The duo take their place in the middle of the ring…..where Cornette addresses the crowd…..he says a lot of people want to know exactly why Kane cost his brother the Undertaker the WWF Championship last night……

“…….well the answer is so simple that even you Kansas City morons will be able to understand……first and foremost, Kane lives for the destruction of the Undertaker….and if he can come out here and put an end to the Deadman’s hopes and dreams, then that’s exactly what he’s going to do…..

……but more importantly, Kane has hopes and dreams of his own…..and when Scott Steiner fluked a win last night, guaranteeing him the main event spot at Wrestlemania against the WWF Champion…..well then lets just say, that Kane had to make sure that the WWF Champion wasn’t going to be the Undertaker…….”

Cornette says that the reason for that, is that Kane wants another shot at redemption…..another shot at destruction……Kane wants another shot at The Undertaker……and he wants it at Wrestlemania……and clearly that couldn’t happen if the Undertaker was busy fighting Scott Steiner……

…..but now, the Deadman is without a title and without an opponent…..and that’s where Kane comes in…..because he doesn’t just want ANY match…..he wants a very specific type of match…..and since the Undertaker barely….barely scraped out a pinfall victory the last time they met……Kane doesn’t want any pinfalls there to stop the match……he doesn’t want any count-outs or disqualifications stopping the match……the only way this match will end, will be just like the WWF Title match ended last night…..with the Undertaker unconscious for a count of ten…..broken….bloodied…..battered……becau se Kane…..wants a Last Man Standing match……

….::::!!!!GONG!!!!::::….

……..and with that, the lights go out…..and the video screen flickers into life……as the pale face of the Undertaker comes into focus…….he tells Kane and Cornette that they should have left well alone……they should have let sleeping dogs lie……because now the hounds of hell are coming to unleash the fury of the devil himself…….The Undertaker says that he accepts Kane’s challenge……and at Wrestlemania……just like at Survivor Series……The Big Red Monster will rest……in…….peace……

Rating: A*

VS.

WWF Intercontinental Championship Match

Scott Steiner (c) vs. Brian Pillman


It’s main event time…..and it’s time for The Corporate Cannon, Brian Pillman, to receive his just desserts for his services to the boss last night…..because after turning his back on his friend, eliminating Steve Austin from the Royal Rumble in the most cowardly way possible, his reward tonight is a shot at the Intercontinental Championship.

This is the first time these two men have faced off here in the WWF……and what a hell of a first showing it was…..because this match was spectacular…..in fact, with a little more build behind it, it could well have been a contender for match of the year, and we are only in January. Pillman had the crowd in the palm of his hand for the entirety of the contest, as they booed him out of the building for his new found alliance with Vince McMahon……while Big Poppa Pump is firmly entrenched as one of the most popular main eventers here in the WWF…..and they never screamed louder than when Steiner was finally able to lock Pillman in the Steiner Recliner, right in the middle of the ring………

…….which is when Shawn Michaels almost kicks Steiner’s teeth out with a Sweet Chin Music…..and an outstanding contest is forced to come to an end…..by a disqualification none the less.

Winner: ….and Still WWF Intercontinental Champion….Scott Steiner (via Disqualification)
Rating: A

It appears as though half of the reason for this match was to reward Brian Pillman….while the other half of the reason was to present Shawn Michaels with the opportunity to get the jump on his Wrestlemania opponent Scott Steiner…….which is exactly what happens how…..as Big Poppa Pump gets beaten mercilessly by both The Heartbreak Kid and the Corporate Cannon…..that is until……

….::::!!!!GLASS SHATTERS!!!!::::….

……….and Stone Cold Steve Austin comes tearing down the aisle-way at full pelt….he hits the ring and instantly goes on the attack……and not only against Pillman…..Michaels is on the receiving end too as Austin shows everybody exactly why he’s the toughest S.o.B in the WWF…..or at least ONE of the toughest S.o.B’s……because Scott Steiner is back to his feet and he’s taken over the assault of Shawn Michaels……..and that’s exactly how the show goes off the air…..with the Intercontinental Champion battling the WWF Champion……..while the former WWF Tag Team Champions attempt to kill each other in the aisle.

Rating: A*

Rating: B+

Final Thoughts

Another ratings record for us this week, as we raked in a whopping 24.20…….almost an enormous 8 point win over Nitro’s 17.38. Brian Pillman is obviously enjoying his time in the main event…..and he must be keen to stay there because he’s really been hitting the gym recently, shedding that traditional light heavyweight physique he’s always had for a more muscular frame…..it works for him too, it’s really boosted his star power, because now he ‘looks’ like a top guy……obviously he’s nowhere near the standards of say his opponent on Raw, Scott Steiner, but then if Pillman got to that size he would have to alter his entire moveset.

While we are on the subject of Pillman and Steiner……they put on a hell of a match on Raw…..and it’s not often that Scott Steiner is out performed around here, but Pillman clearly had his working boots on and was out to impress after his starring role in the Royal Rumble.

Owen Hart put pen to paper on a lucrative four year extension this week. WCW were desperate to secure his signature, but even after picking up a few losses as of late, Owen tells me that he’s happier now than he has been at any point in his career…..and working alongside Bret and Davey is a big part of that……I’m glad to still have him with us.

Of course it helps that the stories coming out of WCW at the minute are nothing but stories of backstage politics, as the Mega Powers continue to explode. After successfully managing to split the roster down the middle…..half of them love Hogan, while the other half are with Savage, the Macho Man has now gone on the record to proclaim that he is carrying the entire company on his back…..if he carries on like that, the half of the roster that actually likes him may well change their mind……..should have come home when you had the chance Randy…….anyway, on to Raw…..
