Previously, on WCW Monday Nitro

Posted 29 May 2019

The final Pay Per View of 1996, Starecade, saw the biggest betrayal in all of professional wrestling……as Hulk Hogan and Randy Savage, reunited….along with a new partner, a brash young upstart by the name of Jean Paul Levesque…..except this wasn’t the Mega Powers 2.0…..and they weren’t saying their prayers or taking their vitamins…..what they were doing…..was turning their back on the fans in what (for Hulk Hogan) was the biggest heel turn in the history of the sport…..

……in what was supposed to be Sting’s crowning glory, headlining the biggest event of WCW’s calendar year and capturing the World Title in the process, Hulk Hogan successfully bid to be added to that main event at the last moment….and it was a great match……but when Randy Savage and Jean Paul Levesque made their way down the aisle, no one was prepared for what happened next…..

…..because in a shocking turn of events, the two former enemies united, using the Macho Man to distract the official, while Levesque nailed Bam Bam Bigelow (then WCW Champion) and Vader (the fourth element in this fatal four way match) with a steel chair…..before kicking Sting in the gut and dropping him with the Pedigree….the fans roared, looking to the Hulkster to vanquish the arrogant youngster……except he didn’t…..what he did was drop the leg on longtime friend Sting…..make the cover……and embrace Levesque and Savage, holding his brand new WCW Championship overhead…..

…….the crowd was shocked…..and appalled….as the Hulkster took a microphone and declared that Hulkamania was dead…..Hulk Hogan was dead……and Hollywood Hogan had just been born…..he said he was sick of working his ass off for a second rate company….sick of the second rate fans……and sick of the second rate way that WCW was run, claiming that things were so much better when he worked “up north”, and that a Hollywood megastar like himself should have been the highlight of every show…..if he had have been, then maybe those boys up north wouldn’t be creeping so far ahead in the ratings brother…..but you kept Hollywood off TV……so now, Hollywood is STAYING off TV…..if you want to see Hollywood Hogan and the World Title, then you can damn well pay to do it, because Pay Per View is the ONLY place Hollywood will be from now on…..

…..and true to his word, Hogan’s appearances were few and far between, usually only to participate in a beatdown with his two new allies, or as the trio called themselves, the Titans of Wrestling (in an obvious play on words in regards to their previous employers Titan Sports) they also gained a few members along the way, as previous Titan employees the Nasty Boys joined up, capturing the WCW Tag Team Titles along the way…..and finally Big Bubba Rogers, who was revealed as the hired muscle of the group in a vicious assault of Ric Flair and Arn Anderson……both of whom have never been heard of since, leading to Bubba changing his name, and his persona…..to “The Enforcer” Ray Traylor (complete with black riot gear and trusty nightstick)….

……Sting took up the mantle for WCW, wanting to bring the Championship back home, and back to Nitro…..but the betrayal from Hogan (whom he considered a friend and ally) cut deep…..and he’s since developed a darker side…..a less trusting side…..seeing the world in only black or white, highlighted by his new appearance…..gone is the colourful blonde flat topped star, replaced with longer darker hair……and the bright face paint changed for a simple white design…..somewhat reminiscent of the shape his one time partner in the Blade Runners, The Warrior, wears……

…..speaking of the Warrior…..he too has chosen to fight in behalf of World Championship Wrestling……along with Lex Luger and the Giant……and a slew of stars that usually shunned the affections of the fans have come over to the side of good (Bam Bam Bigelow, Vader, Diamond Dallas Page) looking to wrench the reigns of control from the hands of the Titans of Wrestling…..but it’s not going to be an easy task……but as we creep ever closer to the Bash at the Beach, a new hand of power is arriving……a new creative force we are told, looking to drag World Championship Wrestling kicking and screaming into the promised land, and back to the top of the professional wrestling world……who is that man? Well……you’ll have to tune in to Monday Nitro to find out……..

WCW Monday Nitro: Week 3, June 1997

Posted 29 May 2019

Week 3, June 1997

Lawrence Joel Coliseum (Winston-Salem, North Carolina)

ATT: 14,600

TV: 18.12

Spotlight Segment

Tonight’s show opens up with a shot of the ring…..which currently has almost the entire WCW roster in and around it…..in fact the only people missing are the Titans of Wrestling, because WCW Champion Hollywood Hogan, Randy Savage, Jean Paul Levesque and WCW Tag Team Champions the Nasty Boys, are all conspicuous by their absence….as is the bodyguard of the group, The Enforcer Ray Traylor…..at the announce booth, Tony Schiavone, Bobby Heenan and Mike Tenay discuss the fact that last week, the blockbuster announcement was dropped that World Championship Wrestling was going to be going in a new direction in regards to creative….and that new direction would be starting…..tonight……

…..with this man who makes his way down the aisle now……Schiavone frankly looses his sh!t “…..oh my god, do you know who that is?…..” no Tony, we don’t actually…..he says that this man right here is the creative force behind all the WWF’s recent success…..that’s Vince Russo….and if he’s here to take the reigns of World Championship Wrestling, “….then this might just be THE most important night in all of Wrestling’s history….”

…..Russo climbs into the ring and stands amongst the sea of confused looking wrestlers, before introducing himself to everyone….he tells the crowd that his name is Vince Russo, and while it’s not a name that a lot of people will know, anyone in the business will tell you that he is directly responsible for single handedly turning the sinking ship that was the World Wrestling Federation around…..and now he’s here to do the same for WCW……he says that for too long now this company has been run into the ground by poor management, and frankly, the inmates have been running the asylum…..well that all stops here….and that all stops tonight…..he says this is a new start….a new WCW….and a clean slate…..EVERYONE is going to have the opportunity to shine, not just the select few with the lucrative contracts…..which is why he would like the so called Titans of Wrestling to join him and everyone else out here right now…..

…..it takes a few minutes, but eventually the “Titans” do indeed make their way out, with Hogan demanding to know what’s going on….Russo tells him that he’s just heard exactly what is going on…..a new day, a fresh slate….no preferential treatment….and no more stepping on everybody else to stay on top……Russo says he’s spent the last 18 months dealing with “kliq’s” in the back…and it’s not happening again here…..and when he says a fresh start, he means a fresh start….which is why, effective immediately….all Championships in the World Championship Wrestling……are vacant…..so if Hogan and the Nasty’s would be so kind as to hand over the title belts, then they can get on with the show…..obviously Hogan and the rest of the Titans find that statement hilarious, asking why in the world they would do that? Russo says that it’s quite simple really…..because due to Hogan’s limited schedule and previous influence in the back, neither he nor the Nasty Boys have defended those titles for 32 days…..two days past the thirty day regulations…..and as such, Russo is perfectly within his rights to vacate the Championships……he says that the Giant and Diamond Dallas Page have both willingly given up the Television and United States Championship….so all that’s left is for the Titans to hand over the World and the Tag Titles, and we can get underway…..

……Savage and Levesque both chime in, stating that Russo is making a huge mistake, but he tells them that they might reconsider their stance when they realise that BOTH of them, along with Hogan, will be entered into the tournament to crown the brand new WCW Champion….and while the pair try to still seem p!ssed, you can tell they are intrigued by this……after a lot of back and forth….insults and threats of suspension without pay, Hogan and the Nasty’s finally hand the titles over…..but the night goes from bad to worse for Hollywood, with Russo announcing that Hogan’s quarter final match will be tonight………against Lex Luger…..

“……woah, woah, woah……I don’t wrestle on free TV brother…..it’s right there in black and white in my contract…..if you want Hollywood to get in that ring, you’ve got to pay dude…..”

….Russo says that’s fine……but if Hogan DOESN’T wrestle Lex Luger tonight, then the Total Package shall be receiving a bye to the semi-finals, and Hollywood Hogan will be disqualified from the competition…..again Hogan is furious, but he finally relents, telling “Flexy Lexy” that he’ll see him tonight, before Russo reveals the other participants in the WCW Championship tournament……Hogan and Luger (obviously)…..Randy Savage, Jean Paul Levesque, Kevin Sullivan, Sting and The Giant…….

…..Russo pauses for a moment, counting on his fingers as he does so…..

“……hold on, that’s not right…….there’s someone missing…….”

…..and as the bagpipes start playing, Russo slaps his forehead in a eureka moment, saying that’s who he was forgetting……..Rowdy Roddy Piper…….and the crowd erupts as the Hot Scot makes his way down to the ring, much to the horror and disbelief of Hogan, who’s night has just gone from bad to worse all evening.

Rating: A

WCW Tag Team Titles Quarter-Final Match
The first match of the evening sees the now former WCW Tag Champs looking to regain their titles, as the Nasty Boys face off against The Soul Train……“Who?” I hear you ask….well the tag team division is awful…..just awful….so we’ve had to cobble together what we can…..The Soul Train are Ernest “The Cat” Miller and Ice Train, along with their new manager Teddy Long….they’re nothing special, but they are a face team, which is something seriously lacking…..not that it matters here, because the Nasty’s finished them off and advanced to the Semi-Finals.

Winner(s): The Nasty Boys
Rating: C+

Next up we go to the back, where Mean Gene is with the new and improved Blue Bloods…..that’s Lord Steven Regal, Earl Robert Eaton……and The Duke, Douglas Williams….a newcomer to the company, bought in simply because Regal was too good to waste in a tag team with Bobby Eaton and I like the Blue Bloods gimmick too much to just let it die, plus teaming with Eaton will do wonders for Williams……this is just a quick promo with Regal doing all the talking, he proclaims that The Blue Bloods will rule WCW, much like Britannia ruled the Waves…..when he himself captures the prestigious Television Champion, while his esteemed colleagues bring the Tag Team Titles home to her majesty Queen Elizabeth II.

Rating: C+

WCW Television Title Semi-Final Match
Lord Steven Regal gets to back up those claims now, as he goes one on one with The Renegade…..quite the contrast of styles to say the least…..and while I’m sure most people would be happy to see The Renegade jobbed out of existence, I will openly admit to enjoying the Warrior rip-off when he first emerged…..and in all honesty, he’s not THAT bad here….so don’t be surprised to see a mini push for the man……..oh, not tonight though……Regal beat him with a handful of tights.

Winner: Lord Steven Regal
Rating: C+

Eddie Guerrero is in the back with Mean Gene now, and he’s not happy…..he says that just a few weeks back he defeated Lex Luger live on Nitro….but tonight, Luger is fighting in the WCW Heavyweight Title tournament, while Eddie has to settle for the United States title? Eddie says that’s fine…..he’ll go all the way and win the belt, just so he can shove it in Vince Russo’s face. Gene says tonight he has a huge task though…..his opponent is former WCW Champion Vader….but Eddie isn’t phased, he reminds Gene that Lex Luger is a former WCW Champion too…..and Eddie beat him, just like he’ll beat Vader tonight…..whatever it takes.

Rating: C+

WCW United States Title Quarter-Final Match
This is certainly another clash of styles, much like our previous match…..Eddie must be the least over main eventer known to man, but he is genuinely auto pushing at main event, so here we are. Vader is working as a face (which isn’t his best attribute) but the fans are unlikely to accept a heel turn given that his last turn wasn’t too long ago. This really is a big ask from Eddie…..but he promised to win whatever it takes……apparently it takes Mike Awesome interfering in the match and nailing Vader with a huge clothesline….that knocks him down just long enough for Guerrero to finish the big man off with a Frog Splash from the top.

Winner: Eddie Guerrero
Rating: B-

Eddie mutters to a nearby camera that he’s taking the US Title home soon, before heading to the back, leaving Mike Awesome to continue his assault on Vader. He culminates his beating by placing a steel chair across the chest of the former WCW Champion and delivering a huge top rope splash of his own……he then grabs a microphone to inform the crowd that he’s out to prove that he’s the BEST big man in WCW….and if you don’t believe him, just ask Vader.

Rating: C+

WCW Tag Team Titles Quarter-Final Match
This was another WCW Tag Team tournament match……and Boy was it ugly, as a little bit of ECW worked its way onto Nitro…..it was the Pitbulls facing off against the Public Enemy….and these two teams beat the snot out of each other….the only saving grace was that at least these were two established teams…..and the babyface Pitbulls had Madusa out there in their corner to further please the red blooded guys in the crowd. The Pitbulls finally put the Public Enemy down with their Assisted Powerbomb.

Winner(s): The Pitbulls
Rating: D+

Mean Gene is called back into action (we really need a spare announcer here) as he stands in the middle of the ring to call out former United States Champion Diamond Dallas Page….and after just getting a look at Madusa, the guys will be even more pleased to see Kimberly at Page’s side. Gene asks DDP why he was so willing to hand over his United States Championship, just to wind up competing in the tournament for the vacant title anyway….Page smirks, saying he’s never been the sharpest tool in the box, but when he books his place in the finals, and becomes a two time, two time United States Champion, then everyone’s gonna know that DDP is the real deal…..and then it will only be a matter of time before Diamond Dallas Page gets a shot at that big gold belt.

Rating: B-

WCW World Heavyweight Title Quarter-Final Match
Rowdy Roddy Piper is wasting no time in getting his first match on Nitro under his belt, as he goes one on one with Kevin Sullivan here, looking for a spot in the quarter-finals….and considering BOTH men’s advancing years, this was a pretty decent match….Piper didn’t come to WCW to lose his debut match though, and he managed to put Sullivan to sleep, to get one step closer to that WCW Championship.

Winner: Rowdy Roddy Piper
Rating: B

With his first victory in the bag, Mean Gene (again) joins Piper in the ring, where Gene asks him what he’s doing here, last Gene heard, Roddy Piper was working for “the other guys”…..Piper says that he was…..but just when you think you have all the answers, Roddy Piper changes the questions….he says he’s here in WCW for two things…..one, to become the WCW Champion….and two, finally shut Hollywood Hulk Hogan’s mouth once and for all…..and with a bit of luck, he’ll be able to do both of them things at the same time.

Rating: A

WCW World Heavyweight Title Quarter-Final Match
It’s main event time, and a sight that we haven’t seen for quite some time here in WCW….and that’s Hollywood Hogan Wrestling on Nitro….it’s a huge match too, as he goes one on one with Lex Luger, in a match big enough to Main Event any Pay Per View……fortunately this WASN’T on Pay Per View…..because the match was good at best…..good enough for TV anyway….and the Titans of Wrestling showed why Hogan had held onto the WCW Title for so long before tonight, because Hogan was able to steal the victory here….with a little helping hand from “The Enforcer” Ray Traylor and his nightstick to advance to the Semi-Finals of this tournament.

Winner: Hollywood Hogan
Rating: B

The entire group of Titans closed the show out, with Savage, Levesque and the Nastys joining Hogan and Traylor in the ring…..Hogan whips Luger a few times with his weightlifting belt, before Levesque tosses him to the outside. Hogan says to make no mistake…..whether it’s Hollywood……the Macho Man…..or the new blood Jean Paul, with three members in this tournament, it’s only a matter of time before the WCW Title is back in the folds of the Titans of Wrestling……and no ones going to stop that……not Sting…..not the Giant…..not Roddy Piper…….certainly not Lex Luger clearly……he tells Russo and everyone in the back to get used to the fact that the Titans are here, because they’re here to stay.

Rating: A

Final Rating: B+

Final Thoughts

What the actual f*ck? What the f*ck is Vince Russo doing on WCW television……what the f*ck is Vince Russo doing calling himself “the man who singlehandedly saved the WWF”…….but more importantly…..what the f*ck is Vince Russo doing, actually putting on a half decent show? I can’t think about this right now……I’ve got the King of the Ring to book…….motherf***er……

WCW Monday Nitro: Week 4, June 1997

Posted 29 May 2019

Week 4, June 1997

Baltimore Arena (Baltimore, Maryland)

ATT: 14,000

TV: 17.92

Announce Team: Tony Schiavone, Eric Bischoff and Mike Tenay

The show opens up with the Titan’s of Wrestling already in the ring, where Hollywood Hogan has a microphone…….he tells the rest of his group that right now, every single one of them has a job to do…..and that job…..

”……..is to get that WCW Title back around my waist brothers……see, everybody knows, that the Titans of Wrestling are the true power in professional wrestling…..and no matter who Vince Russo and the WCW (yes, you heard him, “the” WCW…..”the” World Championship Wrestling apparently) throw at us, it doesn’t matter…….I already got rid of old Flexy Lexy, he didn’t cut the mustard dude……now tonight, it’s down to my two main men…..my Generals…..The Macho Man and JPL himself, Jean Paul(Jean Paul…..that just doesn’t work for me, that needs to change) to take out the little Stinger, and that big bad stinky Giant……then that just leaves Russo’s little surprise, the Hot Rod……and God knows I can kick his scrawny little ass, in a wrestling ring AND in Hollywood brothers……”

……Levesque takes the microphone, telling Hogan not to sweat it…..because with Hogan, Savage…..and him, still in the WCW Title tournament, there’s only one place that belt is going, and that’s back within the camp of the Titans of Wrestling…..Hogan is pleased to hear that, but is sure to reiterate that it’s coming back into the folds of the Titans of Wrestling……and back around his waist, to which Levesque reply’s with a non-committal ”sure, that’s what I meant” before handing the mic to Savage……

”…….brother, brother, brother, brother…..yeah…..have no fear, have no fear….the big bad Giant is gonna come tumbling down you can be sure of that……and as for the Stinger, yeah, the Stinger is gonna find out something that he should have learned a long time ago…….and that’s that you don’t mess with the Titans of Wrestling…..not if you wanna live, yeah…..

……he might have lost his colour, but at least he’s finally seeing things in black…..and white……but all the face paint in the World, ain’t gonna stop the Macho Man from stopping the Stinger Man…..you dig?…..”

……Hogan digs apparently, pleased that everyone is singing from the same hymn book, before telling the Nasty Boys and The Enforcer that they have their own roles to play…..with the Nasty’s capturing the WCW Tag Team Titles back……and Ray Traylor? Well The Enforcer is there to do exactly that, enforce the wishes of the Titans…..and bringing home the WCW World and Tag Titles are tight at the top of the Titans wishes today.

Rating: A*

VS.

WCW United States Championship Quarter-Final Match

Diamond Dallas Page (w/Kimberly) vs. Buff Bagwell

The first match of the evening sees Diamond Dallas Page look to regain the United Stares Championship that he never actually lost in the first place, when he goes one on one with brash young upstart Buff Bagwell. Bagwell spent the majority of last year teaming with former Headshrinker Samu of all people, even capturing the WCW Tag Team Titles along the way……(is there anyone he has teamed with that he HASN’T captured the Tag Titles with at this point? Bagwell is somewhat of a Tag Team specialist as Schiavone points out)…..

…..but now he’s flying solo, and looking to capture his first singles Championship…..unfortunately he’s a bit of a vain lad our Buff, and spends a bit too long flexing for Kimberly on the outside, giving Page ample time to catch him and drop him with the Diamond Cutter to move one step closer to regaining his gold.

Winner: Diamond Dallas Page
Rating: C

Page and Kimberly are met by Mean Gene in the aisle way, who first shills his hotline (1-900-909-9900 in case you were wondering) stating that things might not be as rosy as everyone thinks “up north” and he has some exclusive news on a few contracts that are due to expire very soon…..and the word is, there might be a few new faces around here before very long, so call the hotline now…..

….he then speaks to Dallas about his chances of recapturing the WCW United States Championship……Page says there no chance him NOT recapturing the United States Title…..he says he never lost it to begin with, so winning it back won’t be a problem…..that’s one down, two to go…..Page says he shocked the world when he decided to become a professional wrestler at the age of 35……he shocked them again when he captured his first Championship, the Television Title….and the third time was the charm when he shocked the world to win the WCW US Belt……..maybe now people will be a little less shocked at what Diamond Dallas Page can accomplish…..when he becomes a two time, two time United States Champion……

……from there Eddie Guerrero comes walking out to tell Page to forget about being a two time anything, other than a two time loser, because it’s time for the Guerrero name to mean something around here, starting by taking that United States Title home…..

…..nothing groundbreaking here, just your standard two guys claiming they’re going to win the belt promo…..however there are still some big names in this tournament, so there is a good chance that NEITHER man will walk out with the gold.

Rating: B-

The Blue Bloods are our next, Lord Steven Regal, Earl Robert Eaton and The Duke, Douglas Williams……Regal does the talking, stating that he is only one win away from taking the WCW Television Championship home to Her Majesty The Queen…..but it’s an absolute travesty that neither The Earl or The Duke have been informed WHO their opponent in tonight’s WCW Tag Team Quarter Final match are……but it doesn’t matter, because regardless of who it is, Britannia shall soon rule World Championship Wrestling……for Queen and Country……..

……and to the surprise of no-one (since the news of their departure from the WWF has already broke) its Hawk…..it’s Animal…..it’s the Legi…..uhmm…..it’s the Road Warriors!….and it’s amazing what a change of scenery can do…….because the Warriors get a huge pop ahead of this encounter with the Blue Bloods.

Rating: B+

VS.

WCW Tag Team Championship Quarter-Final Match

The Road Warriors vs. The Blue Bloods

The poor Blue Bloods weren’t expecting this…..and clearly the Warriors were pumped to be back in WCW, because they absolutely flew out of the starting blocks from the moment the bell sounded and stiffed the crap out of The Duke and Earl Robert…..you would imagine that this was nothing like Douglas Williams has ever experienced, a relative newcomer to the American scene after toiling his ware in Europe for the last few years…….welcome to America Doug…..and welcome to World Championship Wrestling…….and that loud thud you heard was your body crashing into the mat, after the Doomsday Device crashed the Blue Bloods out of the Tag Team Title tournament.

Winner(s): The Road Warriors
Rating: C+

Mean Gene is back out and back in the ring with a microphone to speak with WCW’s newest team, The Road Warriors…..he asks them first and foremost, what they are doing here? Animal sprays spittle across the ring as he screams that the Road Warriors are back where they belong, the Road Warriors are back in the number one wrestling company in the world……and the Road Warriors are here to take the WCW Tag Team Championships ”…….TELL EM HAWK…..”

“……….WELLLLLLL……MEAN GENE…….WE HEARD A LITTLE RUMOUR SEE……THAT THE WCW WAS WHERE THE BIG BOYS PLAY…….WELL THATS KIND OF THE PROBLEM…….BECAUSE THE ROAD WARRIORS HAVE BEEN UP NORTH PLAYING WITH ‘BOYS’ FOR FAR TOO LONG………BOY’S IN THE RING AND BOYS IN THE OFFICE……..THATS PROBLEM NUMBER ONE…….PROBLEM NUMBER TWO?……IS WE DON’T PLAY…….WE MAIM…..WE HURT…..WE DESTROY……..WE SNACK ON DANGER AND WE DINE ON DEATH……AND THATS BAD NEWS FOR ALL THOSE TEAMS IN THE BACK THERE…….BECAUSE THE ROAD WARRIORS ARE HUNGRY…….BUT DON’T WORRY……BECAUSE WHEN WE’RE DONE PICKING OUR TEETH WITH YOUR BONES…..WE’RE GOING TO USE YOUR WORTHLESS HIDES TO POLISH UP OUR BRAND NEW WCW TAG TEAM TITLES………UHHHHHHH……WHAT A RUSHHHH!!!……..”

Rating: B

VS.

WCW United States Championship Quarter-Final Match

Bam Bam Bigelow vs. Mike Awesome

Last week Mike Awesome assaulted the big man Vader…..claiming that he was going to show the world that he was the best big man here in WCW……this week he gets the chance to back up that claim…..as he faces Vader’s occasional tag team partner…..(and former WCW Champion!)……Bam Bam Bigelow…..”best big man in the business” was a title that many handed out to Bam Bam Bigelow over the years…..the Beast from the East that could take to the air with the best of them…….fast forward 10 years and just as many people are stating the same thing about Mr. Awesome……a super heavyweight that can fly like a cruiserweight…..

……this match was (excuse the pun) awesome……and easily the match of the night, thanks in part to the fact that these two huge men have phenomenal chemistry in that ring……along with an impressive display of strength from Awesome to finish the match out……because as Bam Bam climbed to the top rope, looking for that signature moonsault…..Awesome sprang to his feet and nailed him in the small of the back, before manoeuvring him into a Powerbomb position……running across the length of the ring and delivering a sit down Powerbomb for the one, the two and the three.

Winner: Mike Awesome
Rating: B+

If the crowd were (surprisingly) hot for that mach, they were even (more surprisingly) hotter for the aftermath……as not content with proving he was the better man on the night, Awesome looked to book Bigelow a place in the same hospital room as his partner Vader……turning the violence level up to a whole other level (by WCW standards anyway) as he reached under the ring until he found what he was looking for…..a wooden table…….which he drags back into the ring, setting it up as a groggy Bam Bam is pulling himself to his feet……he nails the Beast from the East with a boot to the midsection………and drives him through the table with another Powerbomb to fully make his mark on the evening

Rating: A

VS.

WCW United States Championship Quarter-Final Match

Chris Benoit vs. VK Wallstreet

Back to in ring action, as our final US Title quarter final match features one mat technician in Chris Benoit, and one solid and reliable hand in VK Wallstreet (if you’re looking to see an expertly applied rear chin lock then IRS is your man)…..it was a decent little match, but in the end Wallstreet did what Wallstreet does best……he put the better man over, submitting to the Crippler Crossface to see Benoit advance to a Semi-Final clash with Eddie Guerrero.

Winner: Chris Benoit
Rating: C

We head to the back now, where Mean Gene is back in the thick of it…..this time his guest is none other than Sting…..Sting is not the colourful character that he once was…..gone are the neon tights and bright face paint…..this is a darker Sting…..a different Sting……he tells Gene that there is only one man he is interested in getting his hands on here in WCW……and that man is the person that stabbed him in the back at Starrcade……the friend turned to foe……and the LAST…..in a very long line of people who have stepped over Sting to get what they want……well NO MORE……tonight, Sting is going to take out the Macho Man (another fake friend) ”…..and then Hollywood…..it’ll be you and me…..nowhere to run…..nowhere to hide……”

……with that the mammoth Giant walks into shot, along with the Total Package Lex Luger…..they tell Sting that they need to band together to fight off the evil forces of the Titans of Wrestling, saying that one man can’t defeat an army…….Sting’s answer to that is pretty simple ”…….no…….” Sting says that he doesn’t need friends…..he doesn’t need allies…..and he’s been stabbed in the back for the last time, telling both the Giant and Luger to ”……stay out of my way…..stay out of my business……and stay out of my life……”

Rating: A

WCW Championship Quarter-Final Match

The Giant vs. Jean Paul Levesque

While Sting might be happy to play the lone warrior, The Giant and Lex Luger have obviously decided that there are strength in numbers….because the Total Package is out to accompany the Giant for this one…..and that’s probably a good idea considering the fact that Levesque has The Enforcer, Ray Traylor In his corner…..

…..Hollywood might have decreed that Levesque must vanquish the Giant tonight…..but that’s a big ask…..because the Giant is a big, BIG man…..and he tosses the Titan around the ring like he’s nothing to open this match…..in fact the only thing that turns the tide for Levesque, is a strategically placed nightstick to the small of the back from Traylor, as the Giant is running the ropes……and from there the back is Levesque‘ main target, as he focuses all of his offence right there……

……it takes a lot to keep the big man down however……and it was only a matter of time before the Giant found his second wind…..which is the exact same time that Luger and Traylor begin battling on the outside……

……which is why he missed this man, Mike Awesome sliding into the ring with a steel chair……Levesque backs off at the sight of this Chair wielding madman, but it’s not the Titan that he’s here for…..it’s the Giant……because the Giant is the biggest big man going and number one on Awesome’s hit list……and he smashes the steel chair over the Giant’s head with such force that the steel dents, and the big man drops to one knee……Awesome tosses the chair to the mat telling Levesque to finish him off……which he does……with a Pedigree to the steel which is JUST enough for the one, the two…..and the three.

Winner: Jean Paul Levesque
Rating: B

VS.

WCW Championship Quarter-Final Match

Sting vs. Randy Savage

It’s Main Event time, and it’s the man who stands alone, Sting, facing off against the man with an army behind him in the Macho Man Randy Savage…..the commentators talk about Sting’s refusal to be a part of any groups, teams or partnerships anymore, stating that the betrayal by Hulk Hogan was the straw that broke the camels back, after spending a good portion of his career pulling knives out of his back, to the point that it was becoming more of a case of who HASN’T stabbed Sting in the back rather than who HAS…..

……truthfully, this match was somewhat of a let down…..but then with the exception of the Awesome/Bigelow match, pretty much ALL the matches tonight were a bit of a let down…..granted the finish probably didn’t help, but still, I was expecting better from two established stars like this……..as for how it finished, well the match ended much the way it began, with these two men fighting tooth and nail……Sting because a) Savage also stabbed him in the back, b) Savage is also a member of the Titans of Wrestling…..and c) if Sting really wants to get his hands on Hollywood Hogan, then he has to go through the Macho Man to do it……..as for Savage, he’s fighting tooth and nail because a) he’s Randy Savage……and b) see a

……in all seriousness The Macho Man is under strict instructions to eliminate the Stinger from the World Title equation, because Hogan clearly wants no part of the Stinger……which is why, when the action spills to the floor, and both men find themselves spent on the concrete, the Nasty Boys come crawling out from under the ring……and as the official reaches a count of six, Sting lays out Savage with a new move that he has dubbed the Scorpion Death Drop……as the count reaches 9 however and Sting tries to re-enter the ring, Knobbs and Saggs each grab an ankle each of the Stinger, halting him in his tracks……and just like that, Sting is out……Savage is out…..and Hollywood Hogan has a bye, all the way to the finals of the WCW Title tournament.

Winner: No Contest (Double Count-Out)
Rating: B

Sting may have shunned the help of Lex Luger and the Giant earlier this evening, but he’s got to be questioning that decision about now, as the entire Titan’s of Wrestling make their way out from the back to put a beating on the Stinger. Hogan seems content to watch from the sidelines, at least until it becomes clear that Sting poses no further threat…..at which point he finally enters the ring, ordering the Nasty’s to pin him to the mat face down……while Hogan unhooks his weightlifting belt from around his waist, looking to deliver a whipping to the Stinger…..

……which is when the Warrior comes absolutely flying out of the back and charging down the aisle, taking out both Nasty Boys (who run to try and intercept him) on his way with a double running clothesline……apparently he never got the memo that the Stinger isn’t looking for any help around here, as he flys into the ring at 1000mph……Hogan, Savage and Levesque has the good sense to bail quickly to the outside…..The Enforcer however decides to stand his ground, but he probably should have followed his compadres to the outside, because The Warrior hits him with a barrage of clothesline’s, followed up with a flying shoulderblock to knock the man down, then crushing him with a running splash…..

……Traylor rolls to the floor out of harms way as The Warrior helps The Stinger to his feet…..Sting shrugs the Warrior off however, telling him that he didn’t ask for, nor does he want his help…..before abruptly climbing out of the ring and walking angrily to the back, leaving the Warrior to shake the ropes for the crowd, in between throwing threats at Hogan and the Titans as the show comes to a close.

Rating: A

Final Rating: B+

Some thoughts on the show and WCW as a whole…….those match grades, wow they’re bad…..Sting/Savage and HHH/Giant only pulling out B’s is shocking, even with the screwy finishes, I’ve had A rated no-contests all day long with the WWF……Awesome/Bigelow being the match of the night was a huge surprise, even with the great chemistry note, it was dinged for booking decisions (obviously since Awesome is a midcarder and Bigelow is a main eventer) and dinged for short match time (around 10 minutes)…..makes you wonder what a 20 minutes match with Bigelow going over would have graded at…..

……every single match was hit with the low backstage moral negative…..because the backstage, double wow……backstage is currently at 33% compared to the WWF’s 100%…….Hogan and Savage aren’t helping, because they have split the roster in half, with one side having a negative relationship with Hogan and the other side having a negative relationship with Savage……it’s going to take a lot of work to manage this sh!t storm.
