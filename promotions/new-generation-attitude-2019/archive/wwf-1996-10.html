Week 1, October 1996

Rosemont Horizon

ATT: 18,496

TV: 17.93

Ladies and Gentlemen, welcome to the number one show in all of professional wrestling…..welcome to the World Wrestling Federation…….and welcome to Monday Night Raw….Vince McMahon, Jim Ross and Jerry Lawler are at ringside as always, and run down the events of last week, including the fact that we have a new number one contender for the WWF Championship, and that mans name……is Sid. Lawler says that this could quite possibly be Shawn Michaels biggest challenge since his reign began back in March……

……speak of the devil….or in this case the WWF Champion….and he shall appear….along with his KLiQ underlings…..although don’t let Hall and Nash hear you say that…..because as became apparent last week, the co-founders of the KLiQ are beginning to find Shawn Michaels growing arrogance just that little bit grating…..and what does he have to say for himself this week? He begins by gloating about the fact that not only did he single handedly….and decisively…..take out Chris Jericho at In Your House, but he also took him out of the title picture completely, by bringing and old friend of his to town to keep him busy. He gloats about the fact that he single handedly beat both Hart Brothers so badly…..that Bret and Owen ran off to the tag team division, to tangle with Steve Austin and Brian Pillman……that’s the same Steve Austin, who along with Scott Steiner by the way….was also beaten single handedly by The Heartbreak Kid Shawn Michaels……

“……..and you can add Jerry Lawler down there to the list too……a genuine who’s who of professional Wrestling Superstars, that THOUGHT they had what it takes to hang with the Heartbreak Kid….only to find out that the Champ lies down for NO ONE!…..there’s a reason that I am THE number one Wrestler in the world today……there’s a reason why I’m THE man to beat around here…..and there’s a reason why I am, the World Wrestling Federation Champion………..because…..”

“……because of us……”

Apparently one Kevin Nash has something to say on the matter…..

“…..single handedly? You’ve done all that single handedly? It’s funny, because I seem to remember spending the best part of seven months Jacknifing the hell out of anyone and everyone that was lining up for a shot at THAT title….and YOU seem to have forgotten who put that title around your waist in the first place……Waltman and Credible might have jumped on the bandwagon halfway through the ride…..but it was Kevin Nash and Scott Hall that hitched that waggon on our backs, and you rode that bad boy all the way to Wrestlemania…..to the WWF Championship……and beyond……”

“…..I won this title by…….”

“……SHUT UP!!!!……Shut up……for once in your life you’re going to shut your mouth and listen to me….for months now you’ve been coming out here week after week, criticising me…..criticising Scott…..Kid……because you’re still holding your Gold, while we lost ours? Did you ever stop to think that maybe WE lost our Gold, because we spent so long making sure that YOU didn’t lose yours?…….

……I’ve spent the last three years, watching your back in that ring….in the back……and in every dirty little dive and drinking hole down the road…..when you opened your mouth to the wrong crowd, I was there to make sure that YOUR words were backed up…..that YOU were backed up……and instead of gratitude, what do I get? Criticism? Are you kidding me? Even tonight……when Scott and me SHOULD be concentrating on Austin, Pillman, and the WWF Tag Team Title shot we have at In Your House……instead, I’ve got to come out here and step in the ring with YOUR In Your House opponent…..

……and that’s fine…..I got no problem kicking Sid’s ass…..in fact, I got no problem kicking ANYONE’S ass…..especially a jumped up spoilt brat, that doesn’t know when he’s on to a good thing….or when to stop running his mouth…….so tonight I’ll come out here and do what I always do…..I’ll soften up YOUR opponent……to make it easier for YOU to keep YOUR title….because that’s what’s in the best interest of The KLiQ…..maybe one of these weeks you’ll return the favour…..”

With that Nash shoves the microphone into Michaels chest, who’s stood with a shell shocked look on his face and walks to the back, with Hall by his side. Michaels expression soon turns to one of anger however, as he throws the microphone to the mat, shouting at Waltman that he’d better get the job done next, then hopping out of the ring and storming to the back, shouting insults to random fans as he goes into full Shawn Michaels temper tantrum mode.

Rating: A*

VS.

WWF Light Heavyweight Championship Match

Rey Mysterio (c) vs. Sean Waltman

No pressure then Sean…..as the WWF Light Heavyweight Champion, Rey Mysterio makes his way out to put the gold on the line against former Champion Sean Waltman. Waltman vastly underestimated the ability of the young Luchador back at Summerslam, and it cost him dearly…..he’ll be taking this rematch much more seriously….especially after the words from Shawn Michaels a few minutes ago.

While Sunny has toddled off to the back, presumably to try and soothe the bruised ego of the Heartbreak Kid, Justin Credible HAS remained at ringside to offer some moral (and no doubt some immoral) support to his teammate. This is a much more even affair than the last encounter, where Mysterio embarrassed the cocky, unprepared Champion….and the crowd are a lot more invested in the junior king of mystery now than they were back in August. Both men come close to picking up the victory….but none more so than Waltman, who is able to pick up a very near fall after Credible knocks Mysterio flying off the top rope behind the officials back.

Surprisingly its Jerry Lawler that comes to the rescue there, leaving the announce desk and clocking Justin Credible with some patented “King” right hands, that knocks Jerry’s stolen crown right off the head of the theiving Credible….and it’s while the official is focused on that…..

…..that this man, Chris Candido makes an appearance….because with Mysterio perched up on the top rope, The former Body Donna slides into the ring and nails Waltman with a Powerbomb….before rolling back out to the floor. Mysterio looks down at his challenger for a moment before shrugging his shoulders and coming flying off with a big top rope splash. The referee makes the count…..and Mysterio retains.

Winner: …..and Still WWF Light Heavyweight Champion, Rey Mysterio

Rating: B

Credible finally breaks free of the King and drags Waltman to the outside and halfway up the aisle. The two men consider rushing the ring but think better off it when Candido rolls back into the squared circle, with Mysterio’s Light Heavyweight Championship in hand. The KLiQ men back slowly up the aisle, vowing revenge against Mysterio, Candido AND the King.

Candido on the other hand walks over to Rey…..and stares down at the Gold Belt in his hand….holding onto it for just a fraction longer than Mysterio looks comfortable with….before finally handing it over to the Champion and raising his hand in victory. Chris Candido may have a problem with the KLiQ…..but it looks like the Light Heavyweight Championship has also caught his eye.

Rating: B

We head to the back now, where Dok Hendrix is standing by with the man who came out last week looking to the future, as he challenged the WWF Champion Shawn Michaels to a rematch…..instead what he got, was a glimpse into the past, as he was assaulted by (if you choose to believe the Heartbreak Kid) the man that Jericho stabbed in the back…and his former tag team partner….his own Marty Jannetty…..and that man’s name is Lance Storm.

Obviously Dok cuts right to the chase, asking Jericho who Lance Storm is…..and did he in fact, stab the man in the back? Jericho says that the only truth to come out of Shawn Michaels mouth last week, was the fact that Lance Storm and Chris Jericho USED to be tag team partners……and they were a pretty good team too……they won some titles….lost some titles…..then they went their separate ways because both men had aspirations to go on as singles competitors…..

“…..his problem seems to be, that I got called up to the big leagues…….and he didn’t…….I can’t help that Dok……there was certainly no betrayal, and Lance Storms back is decisively stab free…..in fact, the only one around here that’s getting screwed over by someone they THOUGHT was still a friend……is me…..but then that’s obviously the difference between Lance Storm and Chris Jericho…..Chris Jericho got called up to the World Wrestling Federation on the back of natural talent…..and Lance Storm got called up to the World Wrestling Federation on the back of MY name, and because Shawn Michaels wants to keep that WWF Title firmly where it is………instead of around my sexy waist……..but I promise you this Dok………

…….of course we don’t get to hear that promise, as for the second week in a row Jericho is ambushed from behind by his former friend Lance Storm. Last week he tasted a devastating Single Leg Boston Crab…..this week it’s a far more primitive display, as Storm simply beats the maple syrup out of the former Light Heavyweight Champion. When the damage is done, and Jericho is left laying on the ground, Storm drags Hendrix over to him to let him know that…..

[i]”…..everything that man tells you, is complete and utter fabrication…..believe me when I tell you that Dok, because I’m Lance Storm…..and I’m from Calgary….Alberta, Canada……a place so honest that even our Politicians tell the truth……not like those untrustworthy, lying Winnipeggians, who are always talk out of one side of their mouth…..I’m here for truth…..justice…..and the Canadian way…..”

With that Storm turns and walks away, but not without giving Jericho another stomp for good measure on his way out of shot, as Dok Hendrix sends us back down to ringside…..

Rating: A

…..and at ringside we find the Legion of Doom and the Hardy Boys, patiently awaiting the arrival of Matt and Jeff’s opponents for the evening. The Hardys haven’t been in action on Raw in quite some time, having spent the last few weeks locked into an intense training regiment with Hawk and Animal, who seem determined to get these two youngsters to the next level…..and you’ve got to assume that no matter who comes out tonight, it’s got to be an easier option than fighting the Road Warriors day in and day out…..although I guess we are about to find out, as some stereotypical “Oriental” type music starts to play…..

…..and the Orient Express make their way out from the back, along with the devious Mr. Fuji (who has been hanging around with nothing to do since Yokozuna shed his banzai gimmick) back in his more traditional black suit and hat ensemble. The trio make their way to the ring to a decent enough pop, where Matt and Jeff are waiting to get this one underway.

VS.

The Hardy Boys vs. The Orient Express

It’s time for a little bit of old school vs new school here, as the former AWA Tag Team Champions Tanaka and Kato re-emerge in the WWF for the first time in almost five years. Surprisingly the announcers actually acknowledge the AWA Title win (although they neglect to mention that it was under the name Badd Company) but then I suppose that since the AWA has been dead since late 1991, it’s not like anyone is going to be turning the channel from Raw to watch it. They also make sure to mention that Hawk and Animal are themselves also former AWA Tag Team Champions.

The Orient Express have been teaming on and off now for the best part of a decade and certainly have experience on their side….honestly though, they’re here tonight to make Matt and Jeff look good…..which is exactly what they do. These two teams have a fun little back and forth match, but in the end the Hardys pick up the victory…..with Jeff gaining the three count following a Swanton Bomb to Kato.

Winner(s): The Hardy Boys

Rating: C+

To the back-cave now, where Dok Hendrix is standing by with Al Snow, Head and Bill DeMott. Usually this would be somewhat of a lighthearted segment…..not today however…..even Head has nothing to say…..Al Snow however has plenty to say…..and it’s mainly directed towards Raven and his associates…..he begins by apologising to Mankind for leaving him to the mercy of Kane last week….but says that if Raven and his family were really so concerned about the welfare of Mankind, then they would have been there to help him, instead of being in the back, laying yet another beating on Bill DeMott…..he then tells Raven that enough is enough…..he says that the only reason he was able to pick up a victory at In Your House, was because his little minions kept interfering in the match……

He tells Raven that he wants another shot at this months In Your House….just the two of them, no interference, no excuses…..and WHEN….not if…..when Al wins, that’s it…..Raven lets DeMott go, no more four on one, five on one assaults….no more anything….Bill DeMott and Al Snow are officially off Raven’s radar

Rating: B+

VS.

Al Snow & Bill DeMott vs. Doink & Christopher Daniels

We have our second and final tag team match up now, as Al Snow and Bill DeMott look to make a mark on two of Raven’s followers ahead of a potential In Your House rematch between himself and snow…..,in all fairness Al has already defeated both Doink and the Fallen Angel before in singles competition, so tonight should be no different…..what is different however, is Bill DeMott himself…..who takes control of the majority of the match, clearly p####d off at being the recipient of so many recent assaults. He brutalises the two men, showing an aggressive streak that has arguably been missing since his arrival here in the WWF.

Not that Doink and Daniels don’t get their licks in, because they do…..DeMott is on a mission however, and after rolling out of the way of the Fallen Angels Moonsault attempt, he goes up top and connects with one of his own, which is enough to give himself and Al Snow the victory.

Winner(s): Al Snow and Bill DeMott.

Rating: B-

Snow picks up the microphone, wanting an answer from Raven to his challenge…..it’s not Raven that he gets however, it’s Crush and Wrath, the Dogs of War that come walking out from the back…..Snow tells them that he doesn’t want their masters pets….he wants the man himself. Crush and Wrath continue to head to the ring however, so Snow tosses the microphone to one side and he and DeMott prepare for a fight……

……which is when they get spun around, kicked in the stomach and dropped with a DDT a piece…..Double Underhook from Mankind to Bill DeMott and Evenflow from Raven to Al Snow. Mankind looks to continue the assault, but Raven places a hand across his chest to stop him, motioning for him to step back, as Doink, Daniels and The Dogs of War pounce on the two fallen combatants. Raven finally puts a halt to the beating, before picking up Al Snows discarded microphone…..

“……Al, be careful what you wish for……you should know by now that if you wish to play my games then you have to play by my rules, not yours…..you see I have nothing to gain in the stipulations your are trying to set……fight a man I’ve already defeated…..and lose the opportunity to make Bill DeMott see the error of his ways in the process? I don’t really see the incentive there Al……so I will provide you with a counter offer…..I will accept your challenge…..and I will accept your terms…..if you win, both yourself AND simple Bill there shall be set free from my suffering…..but if I win….WHEN I win…..neither of you gains your freedom…..in fact quite the opposite……because if I win, BOTH Al Snow and Bill DeMott belong to ME…..you will both join my dysfunctional little family…..and you will both join willingly and you both WILL do whatever I require of you…..those are my terms…..accept them or don’t….the choice is yours….but know this, if you do choose to accept…..that will be the last choice you will make freely……because all of your actions from there on in, will be at MY command….”

…..Raven then addresses Mankind, telling him that last week was a perfect example of why Raven is the only person in the WWF he can trust. He tells Mankind that he’s sorry he wasn’t here last week…..and he’s sorry that he had to pay because of that…….but it’s time for Mankind to seek his revenge……at In Your House, Raven has arranged a match………Mankind……..vs Kane……and you can finally make him pay the price, for betraying your trust…..for betraying your faith…….and for taking advantage of your loyalty…..for feeding you to his brother week in and week out…..for never being there for you, the way that I am there for you…..do it for yourself…..but more importantly, do it for Raven.

Rating: A

VS.

Marc Mero (w/Sable) vs. Rich Steiner (w/Ted DiBiase)

It’s time for the newest recruit to Ted DiBiase’s Million Dollar Corporation to show his worth now, as Rich Steiner goes one on one with Marc Mero. Steiner may have sold his brother down the river, but he’s bought himself an Intercontinental Title match against the British Bulldog this coming In Your House.

Mero on the other hand has been rather preoccupied of late with Billy Gunn and his advances towards the lovely Sable. The commentators inform us that Mero has requested a match against Gunn at In Your House….a request that Gorilla Monsoon has apparently granted…..

……and speaking of Billy Gunn, he makes his presence known again here tonight, as he and Perfect make their way out from the back to again harass the blonde bombshell at ringside. Unfortunately for Mero, it also serves as a distraction from the match…..and while his back is turned away from Steiner, Rich seizes the opportunity to nail him with a Bridging German Suplex for the win.

Winner: Rich Steiner

Rating: B-

Mero quickly shakes off the cobwebs and rolls to the outside, picking up a steel chair in the process, which he uses to chase Gunn and Perfect to the back. DiBiase and Steiner are joined by Southern Justice in the ring, as the Million Dollar Man informs the crowd that what they have just witnessed here, is an example of what’s waiting for the British Bulldog at In Your House….because it’s high time that the Million Dollar Corporation gained some Gold…..and Rich Steiner is just the man to do it….and as far as that ingrate Psycho Sid goes, he’s going to find out very soon…..that nobody crosses the Million Dollar Man……and nobody…..and I do mean nobody…..is irreplaceable.

Rating: A

We quickly head back to the locker room of the KLiQ…..where quite frankly you can cut the tension with a knife. There’s a frosty atmosphere between Shawn Michaels and Kevin Nash, with Scott Hall obviously leaning more towards Nash’s point of view than Michaels…..as for Michaels, he’s clearly still furious from Big Sexy’s outburst earlier in the evening….and currently taking it out on poor Sean Waltman, chastising him for not bringing back the WWF Light Heavyweight Title earlier tonight….after some pretty choice words he finally demands that Waltman face Credible at In Your House……and he’d better fight like his place in the KLiQ depended on it……because if he loses….then it just might.

As for now, he tells Nash that he’s coming down to the ring right now to sit on commentary for Nash’s match with Sid “……because never let it be said that the Heartbreak Kid doesn’t have your back, Jack….” before storming out of the room and presumably down to ringside, as we have our final commercial break of the evening.

Rating: A

VS.

Psycho Sid vs. Kevin Nash

Considering the contestants involved in this one, I think it’s safe to say that no one was expecting to have a surprise five star classic on our hands……good job really, because if you WERE expecting that then you were (delusional) going to be disappointed….not that we were treated to a bad match, it was perfectly passable, but there is only so much that these two big seven footers can do to each other.

True to his word, Shawn Michaels does indeed join us on commentary for this one, physically removing Jerry Lawler from his seat and taking up the Kings position. He may as well have not bothered however because he doesn’t call much of the action….instead he spends the majority of the match barking orders at Nash…..even with Big Sexy firmly in control of the match he doesn’t stop his tirade……and Nash is clearly becoming frustrated by this…..so much so that after an impressive Snake Eyes and Big Boot combination to Sid, Michaels jumps up from his seat and throws the headphones down, screaming at Nash to Jacknife him “……stick him…..break his damn back…..” At which point Nash finally snaps. He lets go of Sid and screams over to Michaels to “…..do it your god damn self…..” before stepping up over the top rope and heading up the aisle way to the back, coming to a halt at the top of the entrance way as the official finally counts him out…..Sid wins on the road to In Your House……and Shawn Michaels is NOT happy about it.

Winner: Psycho Sid (via intentional Count-Out)

Rating: B

……in fact so irate is Shawn Michaels, that he climbs into the ring with microphone in hand, demanding to know what the hell Kevin Nash thinks he’s playing at? Nash simply shouts back that he’s sick and tired of doing Shawn Michaels dirty work for him, and getting nothing in return…..

“……and right now, it’s not the seven foot giant in the aisle that you should be worried about…..it’s the seven foot giant behind you…..”

…….Michaels spins around, and was obviously so p####d off that he completely forgot about Psycho Sid…..who clutched him by the throat and lifts him high up in the air with a big Chokeslam. Sid paces around the ring for a moment, telling everyone that he’s the man….before picking the WWF Champion up by the hair and positioning him for the Powerbomb…..

……which brings the rest of the KLiQ bursting out from the back. They don’t get far though, because Nash tells them all to stop “…….he needs this…..he needs bringing down a peg or two…..” and the four men (and a distraught Sunny) stand back and watch, as the self proclaimed leader of the KLiQ is Powerbombed to hell right in the middle of the ring. Nash, Hall, Waltman, Credible and even Sunny slowly turn their backs on the fallen Champion and slowly walk out of sight, as Sid picks up the WWF Title that WAS around the waist of Shawn Michaels, before he went for the ride anyway. Sid places a foot on Michaels chest and holds the World Title high in the air as the show comes to an end.

Rating: A*

Final Rating: B+

Final Thoughts

Pretty quiet week this week all things considering. We destroyed Nitro in the ratings war again…..by our biggest margin yet, 17.93 to their 15.20…..over a two and a half point difference…..and yet WCW are still the number one company in the world….go figure. Again the desperation shows in Bischoff as he sends out the Huckster in the main event for the second week running….this time against Kevin Sullivan…..only to have him lose AGAIN by DQ…..I didn’t watch the show but the red and yellow machine doesn’t appear to be firing on all cylinders right now.

As for Raw, the newly christened Rich Steiner got his first official match with Ted DiBiase as his manager, and I couldn’t have asked for a better combination…..these two fit together so well and really click, especially when you consider that this was their first outing together. DiBiase really adds something to Rick….erm, Rich Steiner that was previously missing. The Orient Express made a surprise return, and for a one shot deal they really put on a good showing. It’s a shame that Father Time is catching up with both men, because I was seriously tempted to sign them to a longer deal after that display with the Hardy’s. Speaking of good displays, Raven’s recent good showings on the microphone garnered rave reviews from the Undertaker…..it also garnered him a small pay rise….slightly less than he was asking for at around three grand, but he’s stayed clean and sober since his visit to rehab, and he’s certainly cutting interesting promos….I’m hoping for big things for his unique character, so the three grand was paid out happily.

Week 2, October 1996

Week 2, October 1996

The Pit

ATT: 16,179

TV: 17.61

No time for formalities or hype today, because the show opens up with the WWF Champion Shawn Michaels already stood in the middle of the ring. He has the WWF Title strapped firmly around his waist and a microphone in hand, and while this is an obvious attempt of humility by the Heartbreak Kid, you can tell by his body language that there is a lot of anger bubbling underneath the surface. He starts off by apologising…..apologising to Sean Waltman, to Justin Credible…..but more importantly to Kevin Nash and Scott Hall. He apologises for forgetting just who it was that was with him for every step of this journey so far…..

“…….you know, I’ve never exactly been a humble guy…..and I’ve never been shy about leering people know that I know….and you know….just how good I am. When I step into this ring, there is nobody in wrestling today who can do what I do….obviously when you’re as confident as the Heartbreak Kid, you can rub a few people up the wrong way, so I don’t have many friends in this business…..but the friends I do have, have been with me through thick and thin from the day that Shawn Michaels stopped being a Rocker, and the Heartbreak Kid was born….but none more so that Kevin Nash and Scott Hall…..

…..Kevin and Scott have been with me for every wild ride, every time I doubted myself they were there to pick me up and dust me off, and somewhere along the way I forgot about that…..”

Michaels says that he’s not happy about the KLiQ’s actions last week…..but he understands why they did it. He says that everything he has done since Wrestlemania he COULD have done by himself…..and he DID beat Bret Hart at Summerslam….he DID beat Chris Jericho at In Your House…..and make no mistake about it, he WILL beat Psycho Sid in two weeks time….but maybe it’s time that he started to be more of a team player…..so starting right now, he’s officially challenged one of Hall and Nash’s In Your House opponents Brian Pillman, to a match tonight…..and next week, Stone Cold Steve Austin is going to get the same treatment…..and Michaels guarantees that come In Your House, Austin and Pillman will be in no condition to put up a fight, as Hall and Nash walk out with the gold…..it’s a new day…..and apparently, a new Shawn Michaels.

Rating: A*

VS.

Rey Mysterio & Chris Candido vs. The KLiQ

One of the under fire KLiQ members is in action next, as Sean Waltman and Justin Credible look to gain a modicum of revenge and a lot of momentum ahead of Waltman’s In Your House clash with one of his opponents tonight, Chris Candido. Candido is paired up with the current WWF Light Heavyweight Champion Rey Mysterio…..but the question is, does Candido have the Champions best interest at heart? Or is his true desire the Light Heavyweight Championship itself?

This is a pretty decent opener. Credible is clearly the weak link of the four men, but any flaws are easily hidden in tag team action…..and Candido, Waltman and Mysterio are more than talented enough to ensure that the match flows smoothly. Rey plays the face in peril role, and he plays it well. His size makes him the perfect sympathetic underdog, even in the Light Heavyweight division.

Candido makes the hot tag and the match finally breaks down (as it tends to do) resulting in the official taking an inadvertent Spinning Heel Kick from Waltman…..

…….which is when the WWF Champion Shawn Michaels makes an appearance. He runs down the aisle and slides into the ring. With Credible and Mysterio brawling on the outside, Waltman hooks Candido from behind and holds him there while Michaels tunes up the band. HBK let’s fly with some Sweet Chin Music…..but unfortunately Candido had wriggled free and all Michaels boot connects with…..is Sean Waltman’s chin……just as the official comes to his senses. Michaels drops to the outside and slams his hands against the ring apron in frustration, as Candido covers Waltman for the easiest three count of his life.

Winner(s): Rey Mysterio & Chris Candido

Rating: B-

Mysterio and Candido head to the back with their arms raised in victory, as Credible tries to revive his partner. Waltman finally comes to his senses and he and Credible roll to the outside, where Shawn Michaels is waiting with an apology, proclaiming his innocence. Waltman is unconvinced however, and can clearly be heard on camera asking Shawn “……what was that? Payback for last week? You get Powerbombed so I get kicked in the face?…..” Michaels repeatedly denies it, claiming Candido moved, it was an accident…..but Waltman and Credible don’t seem to be buying it…..and for the second week in a row The Heartbreak Kid finds himself the recipient of a KLiQ walkout, as both men turn their backs on him and head up the aisle to the locker room.

Rating: B

We head to the back now where Dok Hendrix is standing by with Marc Mero and Sable. Dok asks Sable how she is doing after the recent harassment she has been on the receiving end of at the hands of Billy Gunn and Mr. Perfect? Sable doesn’t say much (by design, she’s a terrible talker) basically telling Dok that Gunn and Perfect need to learn that no means no and to leave her alone. Mero is more vocal however. Telling Gunn that the two of them are going to be meeting at In Your House….and when Marc Mero and his left jab is done with him, no woman is going to be wanting to look at him twice, because his nose will be flattened and his jaw will be on the opposite side of his face…..but then again, that might be an improvement.

Rating: B

We go back down to ringside now, where Hawk and Animal are standing by with Matt and Jeff Hardy, waiting to see how their students fare with their latest hand picked opponents after their successful outing last week against the Orient Express…..

……and they don’t have to wait for long, as some classical sounding music signals the arrival of Beau and Blake, The Beverly Brothers….the privileged duo from Shaker Heights who haven’t been seen in the WWF in quite some time.

VS.

The Hardy Boys vs. The Beverly Brothers

Much like last week, this is an exercise in making the Hardy Boys look good…..and again the opponents are up to the task. The commentators again mention the fact that the opponents tonight are former AWA Tag Champs…..who knows, maybe one day soon the Hardys will be tangling with a team that’s held WWF Tag Team Gold. For tonight though the AWA will have to do, as Matt puts Blake away with a move dubbed the Twist of Fate.

Winner(s): The Hardy Boys

Rating: C-

To the back we go again, where Dok Hendrix continues to work the stick. He’s joined now by the Nation of Domination. Hendrix asks Faarooq about his upcoming match with the Son’s of Samoa member, Rocky Maivia, adding that the Son’s seem to have taken offence to the Nation’s claims that they never get the opportunities that others do around here……

“……’Men in rage, strike those that wish them the best’…..do you know what that’s from Hendrix? Don’t answer that, of course you don’t, because you’re an uneducated fool…..that line is from Othello…….one of the most important pieces of work that William Shakespeare ever wrote…..you know why Dok? It’s young Rocky that does most of the talking for the Samoan’s….because what he’s lacking in experience in the ring, he more than makes up for on the mic. He tells the Nation…..but mainly Faarooq…..that they have had enough of the unfair numbers advantage…..that they have had enough of the four on three’s……and Rocky Maivia has had enough, of hearing Faarooq bring up his fathers name and legacy week in week out…..

Maivia says that his father was a trailblazer in this business…..his father was one half of the first African American WWF Tag Team Champions…..and he didn’t do it by bitching and complaining every time something didn’t go his way….and he didn’t do it by blaming his losses on everybody else…..he came down to this ring and he handled his business like a man…..the same way that he raised his son to do……so that’s exactly what is going to happen at the Survivor Series…..because the Nation can come out…..they can bring all four members, it doesn’t matter…..because the Son’s of Samoa are going to handle THEIR business like men…..by whipping your ass.

Faarooq says that’s all fine and dandy, they can do this elimination style……but unless the Samoan’s fourth team mate is the invisible man, they still seem to be short a member…..Maivia tells him not to worry “…….because while you may CALL yourselves the Nation…..we COME from a nation……and there’s a whole nation back home, just waiting to shut your mouth…..so don’t worry about our fourth man…..worry about your four men…..because The Son’s of Samoa….we’re gonna kick your ass….

Rating: B+

We head to the back now, where Dok Hendrix is standing by with the Million Dollar Corporation…..who seem to have expanded as a group since the last time we saw them on Monday Night Raw, with the apparent addition of Jeff Jarrett into the fold. It’s this subject that Hendrix is most interested in, asking DIBiase about his newest recruit….as well as the fact that the Corporation directly cost Psycho Sid the WWF Title last night…..

…..DiBiase says of course they directly cost Sid the title….the Corporation doesn’t do anything indirectly….the Corporation wanted the WWF Intercontinental Championship, and thanks to Rich Steiner that’s exactly what we got….directly from the hands of the British Bulldog…..and next, thanks to this man right here (slapping Jeff Jarrett on the back) we will be bringing home the WWF World Championship next.

Hendrix asks Jarrett why he chose to join the Corporation, Jarrett says that’s simple….for months now old Double J, Jeff Jarrett has been overlooked around here. Someone in the back obviously had it in for Jeff, because he’s barely been seen on WWF TV for months, let alone on Pay Per View….but with Ted DiBiase guiding his career, the Million Dollar Man will make sure that no one overlooks Jarrett again……just like Jeff will make sure, that “….that big dummy Psycho Sid realises, what a mistake he made when he turned his back on Mr. DiBiase….but then again, ha-ha…..I’d say he’s realising it already…..because with the Corporation by his side last night, you would have had a guaranteed new WWF Champion…..instead of the seven foot guitar stand that we really got…..”

Jarrett tells Sid to watch the next match closely….because what Jeff does to Ahmed Johnson next, will be nothing compared to what’s in store for that big dummy if he gets in the way of the Million Dollar Corporation.

Rating: A

VS.

Ahmed Johnson vs. Jeff Jarrett (w/Ted DiBiase)

Ahmed Johnson has had one eye on the Corporation, ever since they cost Ahmed a shot at the WWF Championship a few weeks back. If he wants to get back into the Title mix, then a win here against the slightly repackaged Jeff Jarrett would go a long way towards doing that. Jarrett on the other hand is looking to show the world the potential that Ted DiBiase sees in him…..and a victory over Ahmed would certainly propel Double J a good few rungs up that World Heavyweight Contenders ladder.

These two had a decent match….nothing spectacular, but nothing terrible either…they know each other well enough, having tangled on a number of occasions in the early months of 1996….back then they were clashing over the chance to face the WWF Intercontinental Champion, now however they both have one eye on the bigger prize….the biggest prize….which is why it comes of no real surprise to see…..

…..the rest of The Million Dollar Corporation make their way out from the back and begin sneaking their way around the ringside area, looking for the perfect opportunity to interfere in this one for their new found friend…..luckily for Ahmed, he’s made a few friends along the way too….

……because before any interference can take place, two men who paid a heavy price at the hands DiBiase’s group last night come running out….former WWF Intercontinental Champion, the British Bulldog…..and the man that Jeff Jarrett El Kabong-ed out of the WWF Title last night, Psycho Sid. Davey Boy goes straight for Rich Steiner, while Sid starts clubbering Southern Justice…..unfortunately the action doesn’t remain on the outside….as the five men eventually spill into the ring and we soon have a mass brawl, that gives the official little option but to call for the bell.

Winner: No Contest

Rating: B-

If the crowd were slightly indifferent to the match they just witnessed, their reaction to the brawl that followed was anything but….in fact they absolutely loved it as these seven men went at it toe for toe and fist for fist. The Million Dollar Corporation had the numbers advantage, but then Psycho Sid picked up a steel chair…..and the sight of Psycho Sid with a steel chair is enough to make anyone reconsider their options…..and DiBiase quickly withdraws his troops, sending them to the back to fight another day, while Sid, Bulldog and Ahmed celebrate in the ring.

Rating: A

VS.

WWF World Tag Team Championship Match

The KLiQ vs. The Hart Foundation

We come back from commercial break just in time for our main event of the evening…..as the KLiQ duo of Kevin Nash and Scott Hall, put their (very) newly won WWF Tag Team Championships on the line, against the Hart Brothers who are desperate to reign together as Co-Champions….especially if it means taking the gold from the KLiQ. For once the Champions are actually at the disadvantage in this one, having been in a heavy hitting hard fought battle just last night against Steve Austin and Brian Pillman to win the titles……while Bret and Owen had the night off…..so the Challengers are fully rested going into this one.

And they definitely controlled a good portion of the match….but the KLiQ are able to drag themselves back into the contest and by the end of the match it’s a more even affair….at least until it all breaks down….because that’s when Bret Hart and Kevin Nash find themselves brawling on the floor……and Scott Hall finds himself locked in Owen Harts Sharpshooter, smack bang in the middle of the ring……and finally……finally, it looks as though the Hart Brothers are going to achieve their dream……because Scott Hall is about to quit……

……when Shawn Michaels hits the ring….and hits Owen Hart in the jaw…..with Some Sweet Chin Music…..right in front of the official, who has no other option but to call for the bell. The Harts are going to have their hands raised here…..but they won’t be taking home the gold.

Winner: The Hart Foundation (via Disqualification)

Rating: A

The Heartbreak Kid plucks the Tag Team Championships from the timekeepers table and thrusts them at an annoyed looking Kevin Nash and Scott Hall…..as an equally annoyed Bret and Owen Hart retreat up the aisle. Nash screams at Michaels that they didn’t want, ask for, or need his help…..but Shawn has a cocky looking smile on his face, telling them that they’ll be thanking him in a minute…..

……which is when Gorilla Monsoon comes walking out from the back and stands at the entrance beside Owen and Bret. He has a microphone, and tells Shawn Michaels that because of his actions, he’s just made the decision process a little bit easier for him……because he’s found the other half of the KLiQ’s Survivor Series opponents……and those two men…..are Bret Hart and Owen Hart. Hall and Nash glare at Michaels, who still has that cocky smile on his face…..he grabs a microphone of his own….telling Monsoon that “….you’re nothing if not predictable fat man…….” He asks Gorilla if that’s official? That at the Survivor Series it will be Bret Hart, Owen Hart, Steve Austin and Brian Pillman….against the KLiQ?

Monsoon says yes, it’s official…..and if the KLiQ looses, then the party is over….the KLiQ disbands and goes their separate ways…..their reign of terror is over. Michaels does a little mock tremble in fear…..before telling Gorilla that he has a little bit of bad news for him……he says that he has been speaking to his attorney Clarence Mason….and it turns out that when he became the WWF Champion, he signed a new contract that financially reflected his position in the company…..

“…..or in terms that even a moron like you can understand…..I got a big fat pay rise…..but in that contract…..buried at the bottom in very, very fine print…..was a little clause…..and that clause was put in place to protect myself against cruel and unusual stipulations….stipulations like say, I don’t know…..being forced to break up the band just because some power hungry monkey says so…….”

Monsoon tells Michaels to get to the point “…..what are you getting at?”

…..what I’m getting at, is the fact that while I can’t change YOUR stipulation…..I DO have the power, to add a little stipulation of my own….so this is what’s going to happen…..it’s going to be the Harts there….teaming up with two men they can’t stand in Steve Austin and Brian Pillman…..against the best looking WWF Champion in history, Shawn Michaels….Kevin Nash, Scott Hall and Sean Waltman……if your little hand picked patsies there win, then the KLiQ is done…..buttttttt…..and Kev, this is where you’re going to want to thank me…..if we win…..then your four guys there….can NEVER challenge…..for a Championship that’s held by the KLiQ again……not my World Title…..not those shiny Tag Titles there…..no future titles that we win…..they can’t even challenge Justin Credible for a shot at his Beer Pong Championship of the dorm that he won back in college…..no shots, no more….that’s the stip….deal with it…..and now Kevin Nash…..you can thank me…..”

And for the first time in a long time, that’s exactly what Nash does…..as he slaps his team mate on the back with a huge smile on his face…..and in a scene not witnessed in quite some weeks, the KLiQ stand united….for now at least…..but whether they will still be united come Survivor Series is anybody’s guess.

Rating: A*

Final Rating: A

Final Thoughts

And the record ratings continue to come in…..this week we destroyed the competition with a massive 18.41 to Nitro’s 15.10…..the only match of note was WCW’s most constant performer Ric Flair, beating Vader in what was to be fair, an excellent match…..the rest of the show was garbage….and that was fresh off the heels of one of the bigger name WCW Pay Per Views, Halloween Havoc….which you think would have popped a rating……but Havoc was even worse than Nitro so I doubt many people tuned in after that display….

…..to be fair, if they had ended the show about twenty minutes earlier than they actually did, it might not be remembered for being so bad…..because Bam Bam Bigelow did his best to show all the doubters and naysayers that he is a worthy World Champion, when he successfully defended the big gold belt in an absolute classic against the Macho Man Randy Savage…..although to be fair, even at his age, it’s pretty hard to have a bad match with Savage……and you’d think that was the main even right? The World Champion successfully defending his Title in an absolute barnburner? Yeah you’d think that…..but this is WCW….so you’d be thinking wrong……the actual main event of the show? Hulk Hogan teaming up with The Giant of all people, to defeat The Sullivan’s….Kevin and Dave……Kevin and Dave, they sound like the Aussie neighbours you’d invite over for a BBQ….and not only did they have no hope of following Bigelow and Savages match….they didn’t even try….for a main event that people paid to watch it was absolutely atrocious….and the rest of the card wasn’t much better….a quick run down would be…..

Giant & Hogan defeated the Sullivan’s – C+
Bam Bam Bigelow defeated Randy Savage to retain the WCW Championship – A
Diamond Dallas Page defeated Disco inferno to retain the US Championship – C
Booker T beat One Man Gang – C
The Gambler defeated Duke Droese – D
Rocco Rock defeated Johnny Grunge – C-
Buddy Lee Parker beat Ice attain – D
The Blue Bloods retained the Tag Team Titles against the American Males – B-

Following the poor showing, Sky Sports (who carry WCW events in the U.K.) decided to move future Pay Per Views to a later start time….more Late Night than anything….so even Sky can see what an embarrassment the main event was…..things going from bad to worse for Bischoff…..I should send them Russo down there…..because I doubt even he could do a worse job…..and he wanted Al Snow and Raven to wrestle in a Head on a Pole match at In Your House!

There’s not really much to report on our end…..other than the fact that Billy Gunn’s Executive Consultant, Mr. Perfect has been putting the hours in, training his ass off….apparently he’s desperate to get back in the ring if his back can keep up with him…..we’ll see…..I’m certainly not going to say no to having a worker of Curt’s ability back on the main roster…..but not at the expense of his health.
