WCW Monday Nitro: Week 1, March 1998

Posted 22 August 2020

Week 1, March 1998
Coleman Coliseum (Tuscaloosa, Alabama)
ATT: 15,000
TV: 29.34
Announce Team: Tony Schiavone, Bobby Heenan and Mike Tenay

…..we are just 24 hours removed from a monumental SuperBrawl event and we are opening up this evening (as we often do) with the Titans of Wrestling…..kinda…..I think…..

…..actually, we’re opening with three FORMER Titans of Wrestling, as fresh off the heels of their actions (and seemingly new found alliance) last night, the Totally Awesome Trinity are making their way out side by side…..with Shawn Michaels and JPL…..

…..the commentators can’t quite fathom the new union, but as Shawn Michaels (battle scars from last night still evident on his stitched up head) grabs a microphone, maybe he’ll shed some light on the matter…..

…..Michaels tells the crowd that he hates to be the bearer of bad news, but following his victory last night their little golden boy Sting is living on borrowed time…..

”…..and maybe…..MAYBE, you dubbya see dubbya redneck hillbillies will start to realise, that when Shawn Michaels tells you something is going to happen, you’d better believe it’s going to happen jack…..”

…..Shawn tells the crowd that the landscape is about to change dramatically around here…..because with his victory last night, he is the brand spanking, shiny and new number one contender to that piece of crap he’s christened the ‘Big Tin Belt’…..which means that in about three weeks time, he’ll be the WCW Champion and whether they like it, or they DON’T like it, it’s not going to change the fact…..

”…..I told you I was going to defeat the Macho Man (Oh Yeah!) and that’s exactly what I did…..I told you I was going to destroy the Titans of Wrestling (Brother) and that’s exactly what I did (when I took their very backbone away from them) so when I tell you that you’re looking at the next WCW Champion right here, you’re looking at the next WCW Champion right here…..you know it, I know it, JPL knows it, Sting knows it…..and the Totally Awesome Trinity ‘Totally’ know it too…..which is why they’re standing by MY side right now…..NOT Randy Savage’s…..”

…..Michaels passes the microphone to JPL, but he’s definitely in a less jovial mood than his friend, stating that while Shawn WILL be the next WCW Champion (there’s no doubt about that) Levesque SHOULD be standing here with the United States Championship around his waist…..but because of the Rock sticking his nose in JPL’s match last night, Eddie Guerrero still holds the gold…..

”…..well Rock, I’m done playing with you…..no more laughs, no more jokes, no more Jipple the Goddamn Nipple…..Uncensored…..three weeks time…..I’m going to beat the living crap outta you, live and in living colour in front of your ‘people’ and there isn’t a damn thing that you or anybody else can do about it…..”

…..Levesque continues ranting for a few moments (getting increasingly worked up with every new threat of violence) before Shawn finally takes the microphone off him, telling him to calm down before he gives himself an aneurism…..

…..Levesque continues to seethe in the background, as Shawn addresses the elephant in the room…..the question that’s gone unanswered…..why?…..

”…..why, after months of fighting, would we align ourselves with these three gentlemen right here…..why, after watching us try and dismantle the Titans of Wrestling week in and week out, would these three gentlemen align themselves with us…..actually, that ones pretty self explanatory…..after all, who WOULDN’T want to hitch their cart to the Shawn Michaels bandwagon…..why, when myself and JPL have made our feelings about WCW and ALL its wrestlers clear, why would we choose to take ANY dubbya see dubbya member under our wing, let alone three?…..I mean, I COULD tell you…..but maybe they should tell you why themselves…..after all, unlike Randy Savage, we TOTALLY want to give them their time to shine…..”

…..Shawn laughs at that as he hands the microphone to Adam Copeland, who graciously accepts it, saying that it’s Totally Cool to be actually allowed to talk…..

…..Copeland says that he, Christian and Big Ray-Ray have spent the last six months making the Titans of Wrestling relevant around here…..

”…..and what have we got to show for it? Totally nothing…..Six months surrounded by Randy “Macho D!ck” Savage and the Totally Lame Warrior and we cant even get a match at SuperBrawl? Christian, that totally sucks…..”

“…..totally sucks…..the Titans gave us nothing…..”

“…..except Big Ray-Ray…..”

“…..they totally gave us Big Ray-Ray…..”

“…..we love you Big Ray-Ray…..”

“…..we totally do…..”

“…..but other than Big Ray-Ray, we got nothing…..we don’t even have the Hulkster anymore ‘brother’ and Hulk Totally Ruled…..”

…..Shawn can’t help but interrupt there, telling the Trinity that Hogan is about a decade past his prime, but he’ll forgive their foolish ways seeing how they’re young, dumb and full of……potential…..

”…..potential that WE’RE going to polish…..potential that WE’RE going to shine…..and potential that WE’RE going to shove straight down your dubbya see dubbya throats…..because we ain’t your daddy’s wrestlers…..we ain’t the ‘good old boys’…..we’ve CERTAINLY got attitude, but we sure as sh!t ain’t the New Generation…..we’re the de-generation…..the degeneration of good taste…..the degeneration of standards and practices…..and come Uncensored, when I kick Crow Boy’s head off his shoulders, the degeneration of this whole goddamn company…..and if you don’t like it, well…..Christian can tell you what to do about it…..”

“…..you can Totally Suck It…..”

…..totally suck it indeed…..the landscape has most certainly changed…..but I don’t think people are going to like it……

…..let’s play a recap package from last night, before we go to our first match of the evening…..

Rating: A*

…..and our first match is between two gentlemen that had very different fortunes last night…..it’s Konnan (who was victorious in his match against the First Family) facing Curt Hennig (who was NOT victorious in his match against The Rock) and it was a surprisingly heated affair…..

…..maybe it was the fact that Konnan was looking to pick up a big singles victory after his part in the eight-man match last night, or maybe it was just the fact that Curt Hennig was looking to pick up a victory period…..either way, both of these men clearly wanted the win and they fought hard to try and accomplish it…..

…..it was always going to be a big ask for Konnan to pick up the duke here…..while he may be a household name in Mexico, here in America he has only recently started gaining traction and as such is (significantly) lower on the totem pole than Curt…..he put up one hell of a fight though, with Curt having to dig deep into his bag of tricks, catching Konnan with a sly low blow while the official was attempting to separate the two men…..one Hennig-Plex later and this one is all over, but while he may not have picked up the victory tonight, Konnan should be on a lot more people’s radar now with that display…..

Winner: Curt Hennig
Rating: B

…..Curt may have picked up the victory, it he’s far from happy (and in fact appears to be a little embarrassed) at quite how close Konnan came to picking up the victory in that one, so he looks to make even more of a statement AFTER the match has concluded…..

…..the official raises his arm but Hennig bats him away, before turning to his defeated opponent and proceeding to stomp the crap out of him…..Tony Schiavone cites poor showmanship from Hennig but The Brain isn’t buying it, telling his broadcast colleges that this is exactly what makes Curt Hennig so good, the fact that even victory isn’t good enough for him…..

”…..don’t forget Tony, I managed this man…..and when he lost (or wasn’t happy with the win) he would punish himself for days…..I mean, AFTER he’d punished his opponent obviously…..”

…..and punish is exactly what Curt does here, repeatedly putting the boots to Konnan as the official frantically calls for the bell…..

…..like that’s ever done anything…..

…..this man can do something though…..because fresh off joining forces with Konnan last night, Jeff Hardy again comes to the man’s aid, rushing down the ring and sliding into the ring…..

…..Hennig turns his attention to the new arrival, looking to continue unleashing those frustrations on Jeff Hardy…..unfortunately for him, Jeff Hardy is fresh as a daisy and after a brief scuffle is able to send Hennig bailing to the outside…..

…..Hennig quickly bails up the ramp (offering threats of violence towards Hardy) as young Jeff helps Konnan back to his feet and we go to commercial…..

…..but not until we see a brief shot of a black limousine pulling up outside the building…..

…..who could possibly be inside that then?…..

Rating: B+

…..I’ll tell you who’s inside…..The Titans of Wrestling…..guess how I know…..because as the crowd are being bored senseless by a match between eh, let’s say Joey Maggs and Johnny Attitude, they make their way out with Russo leading the way (microphone in hand, screaming at the pair of jobbers to get out of the ring) and make their way down the aisle…..

…..Maggs and Johnny come to a standstill, a little bit unsure what to do with themselves (“we’re shooting bro”) as the Titans enter the ring, but the four men barely pay them any attention at all…..instead, Vince Russo goes right into a tirade about Shawn Michaels…..

…..he says that while the Titans might not be the most popular guys around here, at least they respect the company bro…..they’re in WCW and WCW is better because of it, just like that WCW Title would be better around the waist of a member of the Titans of Wrestling…..

”…..instead, you’ve got that jacked up punk Shawn Michaels going for the gold, and bro…..he don’t even want the damn thing…..Randy Savage WANT’S the WCW Title…..Randy Savage WANT’S to represent WCW…..and bro, one way or another he’s going to get it…..”

…..Russo says that he’s not going to try and book Macho in a world title match tonight (because that jack-off Eric Bischoff would only veto it) but he IS going to book the Macho Man in a match…..and if it can’t be for the WCW Title, it CAN be against the WCW Champion…..

”…..and Sting…..sorry bro, but when the Macho Man pins your shoulders to the mat, one, two, three later tonight, Bischoff will HAVE to grant him a shot at the title…..”

…..Savage snatches the microphone from Russo, telling Sting that he’s going to slap the paint of his face later…..

”…..but Stinger, Stinger, Stinger, you only get a little bit…..you only get a little bit because a little bit of the Madness is all it will take…..Shawn Michaels, you’re gonna get it all, yeah, you dig…..and what’s left, what’s left is going alllllllll the way over to the snakes you’ve got at your side…..”

…..Macho says that he plucked those two punks Copeland and Cage out of the gutter and he made them stars…..made them SOMEONES…..made them Titans…..and how do they repay him? By walking around kissing Hollywood Hogan’s butt before going right ahead and biting the hand that feeds them…..

”…..Shawn Michaels, you think you’re clever yeah, think you’re the man with a plan…..well your plan just went right out from under you…..you wanna take the Trinity, you can have ‘em yeah…..you think that taking those three punks is gonna put a dent in the Titans of Wrestling? You’re really, really, really mistaken, yeah…..take those three, because I went one better brother, yeah…..keep the kids, we got the man…..”

…..hoo boy…..not just a man…..a giant…..THE Giant…..as all seven foot plus makes its way out from the back and down the aisle…..

…..and he’s wearing a Titans of Wrestling shirt to boot…..

…..the big man steps over the top rope, where he walks straight over to Joey Maggs and Johnny Attitude…..he wraps a hand around the throat of each man, drags them to the middle of the ring and hits a double choke slam, driving both men into the canvas simultaneously, before kicking the two enhancement stars out of the ring…..

…..Macho Man has a big smile on his face at the sight of that, offering the Giant that old Mega Powers handshake, before telling the crowd that as far as Shawn Michaels goes, he can talk about the degeneration of WCW all he wants, but this right here, these men right here, these are the Titans of WCW…..and the Titans of WCW are going to take out Shawn Michaels, take out JPL…..take the WCW Title…..and then finally, squash the Traitorous Trio too…..

”…..Ohhhhh Yeahhhhh!…..”

Rating: A*

WCW Cruiserweight: #1 Contenders Match

…..we air a brief promo package following that, highlighting James Vandenberg’s (eventually successful) quest to claim ownership of the Cruiserweight Title (including a brief shot of current Champion, Hayabusa’s victory over Juventud Guerrera last night) before we go down to ringside, where we are apparently about to decide Hayabusa’s NEXT challenger…..

…..it’s Psychosis taking on Chavo Guerrero and the number one contendership is on the line…..

…..the match (from an in ring perspective) was very good…..Psychosis is a near ten year decade, while Chavo Guerrero (while not as experienced) is part of the Guerrero family…..and the Guerrero family has DECADES of experience to share…..

…..speaking of the Guerrero family…..EDDIE Guerrero has been the United States Champion around here for close to eight months, so the pressure is on Chavo here to bring some more gold into the fold…..Cruiserweight Gold…..but first there’s the small matter of winning this match right here…..

…..he’s going the right way about it too, in fact (after a hard fought battle) it looks like it IS going to be Chavo Guerrero advancing to face Hayabusa at Uncensored…..

…..at least until Chavo freezes (and then panics) at the sound of the undefeated rookie, Bill Goldberg’s music starting to play, as the Turnertron flickers to life (and the crowd explodes) as the man himself comes bursting out of a locker room in the back…..

…..he doesn’t interfere per say, but as (a severely distracted )Chavo checks his exit options, Psychosis capitalises on the distraction, driving Chavo to the mat with a big belly to back suplex…..with Chavo down, Psychosis Climbs the top rope…..steadies himself…..and then finishes Chavo off once and for all with a top rope guillotine leg drop, giving him not only the win, but the #1 contender spot too…..

Winner: Psychosis
Rating: C+

…..and with the win in the bag Psychosis is outta there…..

…..probably a good idea too, because (as we see on the Turnertron) a p!ssed off Bill Goldberg is still making his way through the back…..

…..Goldberg made his return last night and has his sights set firmly on Eddie Guerrero…..last night a number of backstage personnel tried to halt his journey to the ring…..that didn’t go well for them…..tonight Goldberg turns a corner and walks straight into Alex Wright dancing obliviously in the halls…..

…..Goldberg doesn’t even break stride as he grabs Wright by the scruff of the neck and hurls him violently into the wall, before continuing his walk which finally culminates in Bill walking out onto the stage to a thunderous ovation…..

…..Chavo is just about coming around following his defeat a few moments ago and slowly starts getting back to his feet as Goldberg marches down the aisle and into the ring…..

…..and again (much like last night) he gets Speared right out of his boots…..

…..Goldberg snaps at the nearby camera, telling the audience at home in no uncertain terms that Eddie Guerrero is next…..unfortunately (as the announcers inform us) Eddie Guerrero is not here this evening to answer that challenge, a fact which Tony Schiavone is rather convenient given the return of Bill Goldberg last night…..

”…..and fans, one has got to assume that Eddie Guerrero is doing everything he can to stay as far away from Bill Goldberg as possible, even if that means that it’s his nephew Chavo that has to pay the price…..”

…..Tony may have a point there…..I mean, you’d have to be an absolute idiot to confront this raging rookie right about now…..

…..wait, did someone call for an idiot?…..because here one comes…..

….I don’t know if Disco got the memo, but he’s here…..and he’s marching right down the aisle, where he grabs a microphone and climbs into the ring…..

”…..see I know your game ‘Bill’ (if that IS your real name) you heard that Eric Bischoff was announcing the entrants into the WCW Television Title Tournament tonight, and you want to get your name down on the list…..that’s why you blindsided my good personal friend Alex Wright, to take his spot…..well I got news for you William…..if I want to GUARANTEE my place in that tournament, then defeating the myth that is Bill Goldberg is a good a place as any, so put on your dancing shoes and put up you’re dukes, because that little streak of yours is about to hit a hot streak of Disco Fever baby…..”

…..wait…..is…..is Disco actually challenging Bill Goldberg to a match?…..

…..I guess he is, because we have an official…..

…..ring the bell…..

Rating: B+

…..and you can go ahead and ring that bell again about 45 seconds later…..

…..Bill Goldberg hasn’t had a match here in WCW in well over a month…..he’s p!ssed off…..and the Disco Inferno has a big mouth…..

…..Spear…..Jackhammer…..

…..62-0…..

…..Eddie Guerrero might be in big, big trouble…..

Winner: Bill Goldberg
Rating: B

…..from there we go to a brief segment over at the WCWwrestling.com desk, where the new #1 Contender to the Cruiserweight Title (Psychosis) is with Mark Madden…..

…..the pair have a translator sitting with them as Madden looks to catch a few words with the man who will be facing Hayabusa some day soon and it seems to be going quite well…..at least until Brian Lawler rocks up into shot…..

…..Psychosis shoots to his feet but Lawler tells him to sit down before he takes someone’s eye out with those horns of his…..

”…..look at you Madden, is this what WCW has come to? Translators?…..we’ve got a Japanese Champion, a Mexican #1 contender…..the LAST #1 contender was Mexican too…..this is America Mark Madden, you wanna hold that belt then you’d better talk American, not Spanglish…..look at this idiot, he doesn’t even know what I’m saying…..”

…..Lawler girls some insults at Psychosis (in the politest way possible) but it really does look as though the Mexican Star has no idea what Lawler is saying…..

”…..see…..nothing…..and this guy might be our new champion? Not on my watch Madden, not on my watch…..let me just say goodbye to this idiot and then you can interview a REAL star…..ME!…..”

…..Lawler turns to Psychosis, but I did mention that there was a translator sat RIGHT there didn’t I?…..

…..turns out that the whole time Lawler was talking smack about Psycho, that translator was whispering right into his ear, because as Lawler goes to open his big mouth, Psychosis punches him right in the jaw…..

…..Lawler hits the deck as Psychosis stands up (shouts something in Spanish) and walks off, leaving Brian clutching at his jaw and staring blankly at the translator, who finally does his job…..he says that Psychosis wishes him good luck in running that big mouth…..

…..with a broken jaw…..

…..let’s go over to Mean Gene…..

Rating: C+

…..who’s standing by with the public face of the Championship Committee, Eric Bischoff…..

…..Bischoff says that (as promised last night) he’s here to announce the participants in the one night tournament to crown a new television champion, set to happen this coming Thursday on Thunder…..

…..Mean Gene says that we have seen a number of potential candidates here tonight, including the two gentlemen in our previous match, Disco Inferno and the FORMER TV Champion (who was never actually defeated for that title) Bill Goldberg…..

”…..so Eric, why don’t you go ahead and give us the names of the participants…..”

“…..Gene, first things first, we won’t be seeing Disco Inferno competing in this Television Title tournament, nor will we be seeing Bill Goldberg…..

…..obviously (given the fact that he just lost in less than a minute) Disco Inferno isn’t QUITE up to the standard that the Championship Committee are looking for, while as for Bill Goldberg, he’s going to have other things to concentrate on Gene…..”

…..other things? Well hold on a minute there Eric, Gene’s not going to let that one slip by…..what other things exactly are we talking about?…..

”…..what we’re talking about Gene, is the fact that Bill Goldberg is currently 62 matches undefeated…..never mind that, Bill Goldberg is undefeated period, he’s NEVER lost a match Gene…..ever…..

…..now the way myself and the championship committee see it, should Bill Goldberg wish to enter this Television Title tournament we would absolutely offer him a spot no questions asked…..

…..but…..

…..I think Mr. Goldberg has made his intentions crystal clear and with his win/loss record how it is, I can comfortably say right here and right now……Bill Goldberg is the current number one contender to the WCW United States Championship and he WILL face Eddie Guerrero for that championship at the upcoming Uncensored event…..”

…..Gene likes the sound of that and evidently so does Bischoff, who has a little smile on his face as he tells Eddie Guerrero that while (for seven months now) he has slipped and squirmed, lied cheated and stolen his way through title match after title match, he may find it a little difficult to weasel his way past Bill Goldberg with the US Title in tact…..

”…..but as I say Gene, that’s a match that’s scheduled for Uncensored…..you’re here for the participants in Thursdays tournament and after much deliberation Eric can finally reveal those names…..we will see Konnan (based off his performance tonight) Lord Steven Regal (based on previous TV Title pedigree) Jeff Hardy, Curt Hennig, Diamond Dallas Page, Buff Bagwell, Lance Storm…..and The Patriot…..all competing live on TBS this Thursday night to crown a new WCW Television Championship…..”

…..Bischoff has barely gotten those words out of his mouth, before he’s interrupted by the arrival of two of the gentlemen he’s just announced…..Lord Steven Regal and Lance Storm…..

…..interestingly enough BOTH of these two competitors are former Television Champions, with Lance Storm actually being the man to put an end to his (now) good friend Steven Regal’s last reign, before the formation of the Sovereignty…..

…..Storm says that while it’s a pleasant surprise that an American like Bischoff would actually see that two non-american’s like himself and his lordship should be included in this tournament, he actually has a suggestion of his own to make…..

…..Bischoff isn’t amused by the interruption, but he is willing to hear this “suggestion” out…..and as Storm leans over and whispers something in his ear, he actually looks surprised at what he’s hearing…..

…..Bischoff stares at Storm for a moment, before asking him if he’s sure about that…..Storm confirms that he is, so Bischoff tells him to have it his way, before telling Gene that he has one small amendment to make…..

”…..and while I don’t necessarily approve of Mr. Storms ethos OR the things that he has to say about America, this IS the USA and our forefathers fought with everything they had to ensure that he had the right to say them…..that being said, Lance Storm is unquestionably an exceptional competitor inside that ring, but an exceptional competitor that we WONT be seeing in action this Thursday, because he has offered his spot in the TV Title tournament up……to Chris Benoit…..”

…..Bischoff says that Mr. Storm has informed him that Chris Benoit is deserving of a shot at proving that singles Championship gold here in WCW is within his grasp and Eric for one wholeheartedly agrees with him, before saying that Chris Benoit is apparently high on everyone’s list right now, as Diamond Dallas Page has ALSO requested that Mr. Benoit receive a title shot…..

”…..a TAG TEAM title shot here tonight…..a rematch for the titles that those two gentlemen lost at Souled Out…..”

…..Storm goes wide eyed at that…..I think he was believing that he was doing something here to sway Chris Benoit’s favour in his and the Sovereignty’s direction…..maybe it did, maybe it didn’t…..but come Thursday night, should everything go their way, either DDP or Chris Benoit could go from holding zero titles, to potentially holding two…..

…..one half of the WCW Tag Team Titles…..and the WCW Television Title…..

Rating: A

…..he says that last night, the Hot Rod overcame the Hot Bod, when Rowdy Roddy Piper defeated the Titans of Wrestling’s Buff Bagwell…..

”…..but tonight, Hot Rod you’ve requested this time, so without further ado please join me in welcoming…..Rowdy Roddy Piper…..”

[i]”…..and that something, is Thank You…..”

”…..and I gotta tell you, if I had my way I’d be doing this crazy career for another 43 years…..I’ve been doing this for over twenty five years, more than half my life, and whether you booed me or whether you cheered me, I’ve loved every single second of it and I’d give my other good leg to do it all over again…..”

…..Roddy says to play those bagpipes one more time, play them as he walks all the way to the back…..because unlike at Satarrcade, he CAN walk out and he can walk out on his own terms…..which can mean only one thing…..

…..it’s time to hang up the kilt…..

”…..so while I started this with thank you, I’m ending this with goodbye…..”

…..the two men are really hamming it up as they walk toward the ring, with Buff even wiping away a few imaginary tears, before they climb through those ropes and flank Piper…..

…..Giant plucks the microphone from the hands of a terrified looking Mean Gene, before handing it to Buff, as Bagwell lets out an obnoxious laugh…..he tells Roddy that finally…..FINALLY he made a sensible decision around here…..

”…..because let’s face it, the whole world saw Buff Daddy humiliate, embarrass….and honestly, barely break a sweat on this beautiful body doing it, when I took you out at Starrcade…..last night, last night was nothing but a fluke…..everyone knows that Buff was only one Blockbuster away from doing it all again after I had you doing the Scottish Jig all over that ring…..”

…..unfortunately Bagwell isn’t here alone, so as Piper dives at the smirking Titan member, the NEWEST Titan member catches him with one big hand around his throat, hoists him high in the air and then Chokeslam’s him right to the canvas…..

…..Tony Schiavone is disgusted at the display, stating that all Roddy Piper wanted to do was address these great WCW fans one last time and then leave with his head held high…..instead, he’s currently getting the tar kicked out of him on the mat…..

…..The Total Package helps Piper back to his feet as Bagwell coaxes The Giant back up the ramp and away from the ring…..

”…..and now it would seem like the promise of championship glory hangs over your heads like the Sword of Damocles, because while you both have the opportunity to wear gold this evening by working together, this Thursday night (thanks to Lance Storm’s advocation) you potentially have the opportunity to capture SINGLES gold…..although you may well have to face each other to do so…..”
