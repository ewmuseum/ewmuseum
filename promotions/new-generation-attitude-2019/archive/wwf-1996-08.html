Week 1, August 1996

The Pit

ATT: 17,304

TV: 16.01

It’s that time of the week again folks, it’s Monday Night…..which means it’s time for Monday Night Raw. We barely have time for the opening credits to finish, before the Steiner Brothers come bursting out from the back and march down to the ring with a look of determination on their faces. They jump into the ring, but these aren’t the happy go lucky smiling Steiner’s of old….Scott looks ready to put his fist through someone’s face, while Rick looks as though barking for the crowd is the last thing on his mind. Scott has a microphone and speaks first, saying that he got into this business for three things….

“…..to team with my brother…..to win titles……and to kick as many people’s asses as possible….last week, I had the chance to do two of those things…..I could have been standing here with the WWF Tag Team Titles, but it wouldn’t have been with my brother…..so I kicked Steve Austin’s ass instead. Austin, I’m not finished with you, you little white trash redneck b###c….not by a long shot….but it seems to me that before I take you out for good, me and my brother have a little bit of unfinished business with the KLiQ, ain’t that right Rick?”

“Kevin Nash….Scott Hall…..and any of you other black and white wearing jackasses….as far as I’m concerned you should all be wearing yellow, because you’re nothing but a bunch of gutless cowards who can’t fight anybody man to man…..yeah, you’ve kicked my ass twice now, but each time it’s taken all six of you to do it….so at Summerslam, Hall and Nash, grow some balls, put the belts on the line…..and face me and my brother, so we can shove our fists down your throats and our boots up your asses….”

……….!!GLASS SHATTERS!!……….

……and out comes Steve Austin and Brian Pillman. Pillman has his ribs taped from the Powerbomb that he received on the steel steps courtesy of Kevin Nash last Sunday, but he seems to be moving ok with it…..in fact he’s bouncing around laughing manically as usual, taking every opportunity to antagonise the live crowd. The two men hit the ring and as usual have something to say. Austin goes face to face with Scott and the two stare each other down, before Steiner breaks the silence, telling Austin “let me guess, now you want another piece of the KLiQ too, for beating up your little buddy there?” but Austin shakes his head…..

“…..hell son, I ain’t out here to babysit Brian Pillman…..Brian Pillman is a grown man and if he goes and gets his ass whipped then that ain’t no skin of Stone Cold Steve Austin’s nose….just like Brian Pillman couldn’t give a damn if YOU whipped my ass last week……but you DIDN’T….and you COULDN’T if your life depended on it…so before you come out here running your mouth about Steve Austin, you might wanna take a step back, because I’ll beat you all over this building, up, down and back again until you’re down on your knees begging “Please Mr. Austin, no more”…….now as far as the KLiQ are concerned…..they might have beat the snot out of Brian at In Your House….and that’s understandable…..he’s an irritating little b#####d…..but last week they cracked a steel chair over the back of Steve Austin’s head….and UH-UH, that don’t stand with Stone Cold….so the way I see it, you….and your moronic brother their need to know your place in the pecking order around here….move aside…..and watch closely, as Stone Cold Steve Austin and the Loose Cannon Brian Pillman, whip some KLiQ ass and take those Tag Team Titles for ourselves.”

Obviously the Steiner’s don’t share that sentiment….and seeing how none of these four men are especially known for their calm and relaxed demeanours, it soon becomes clear that we are about to have a little unscheduled brawl right here……

……luckily the law is here…..and I don’t mean the Big Boss Man…..WWF President Gorilla Monsoon is out and he tells both Austin and Steiner that since they were unable to put their differences aside for one night against the KLiQ, then NEITHER man should get another shot at the belts…..fortunately for them however Monsoon said that he had a better idea…..“which is that at Summerslam, the KLiQ will be defending the WWF Tag Team Championships, against the Steiner Brothers………AND……….against Steve Austin and Brian Pillman, in a triple threat match.”

Monsoon then goes on to say that since Pillman and Rick Steiner were both given the week off last week, they will both be in competition tonight….Rick Steiner later when he takes on Kevin Nash…..and Brian Pillman right now…..against Scott Hall.

Rating: A

VS.

Brian Pillman vs. Scott Hall

It’s an unusual match this one….because while both men certainly have a vocal following, neither is what you would class as a fan favourite…..and while a face vs face match certainly can get over with the crowd, heel vs heel is done far more infrequently, simply because of the possibility of the crowd s######g all over it…..either that or just sitting on their hands with no one to cheer for.

Fortunately that wasn’t the case with this one…..the crowd was actually pretty split down the middle, with both men getting an equal amount of jeers to cheers. Pillman took the “babyface in peril” role, although he was quick to insult any crowd member who tried to cheer him on….(which just made them cheer him on louder)…..and Hall was his usual cocky self, messing up Pillman’s hair and arrogantly batting him about the head.

Pillman fought his way back into this one, using his speed and agility to outwit the bigger man, even hitting a move rarely seen now in his arsenal, a missile dropkick from the top. Hall was able to reverse an Irish Whip attempt and send Brian hurtling towards the corner….Pillman was able to leap at the last moment however, land on the second turnbuckle and come flying off with a cross body attempt………..which Hall caught……before delivering that patented fall away slam, which unfortunately sent Pillman colliding into the Official.

With the Referee down on the mat, Hall sees his opportunity and tries to connect with a low blow to his opponent……Pillman sees it coming however, and catches Halls foot before he can connect. With the Bad Guys foot still in his grasp, Brian gets that maniacal look in his eyes and breaks out in a huge grin…..Hall obviously senses what is coming, as he hops up and down on his one free leg, pleading with Pillman not to do it…..but it’s no good….and the Loose Cannon punts him right in the nuts. The official slowly comes around, just as Pillman connects with his Hangmans Neckbreaker, The Time Bomb….and three seconds later that’s all she wrote for Scott Hall

Winner: Brian Pillman

Rating: B-

We head to the back now, where Dok Hendrix is standing by with The Hart Foundation. He asks Owen about the challenge levied by Faarooq a week ago, as well as his chances against WWF Champion Shawn Michaels….considering the last time they met, Owen found himself on the injured list for a month.

Owen says that yes, Shawn Michaels injured him the last time they met, “but that was then, this is now….and tonight, I’m going to go out there and put a dent in the armour of the Shawn Michaels and the KLiQ…..before my brother Bret goes out there at Summerslam and twists The Heartbreak Kid up like a pretzel in the Sharpshooter, to bring the WWF Title back where it belongs…..and as for Faarooq…..he’s always got some excuse for why he is where he is, some excuse to why he lost or why he hasn’t had a title shot…..but at Summerslam, there won’t be any excuses…..because I accept your challenge Faarooq……and when I apply MY sharpshooter…..you’ll be screaming so loud that even my “senile old Daddy” up in Calgary will be able to hear you.”

The Bulldog is up next, he cuts a generic promo on Hunter Hearst Helmsley, telling him that he won the Intercontinental Championship at Summerslam four years ago, in Wembley, England……and this year, the venue might have changed, but the result is going to be the same….when the British Bulldog walks out of Summerslam, the WWF Intercontinental Champion.

And last but not least it’s the Hitman. He too laments the Nation of Domination, telling Faarooq to be careful what he wishes for….before moving on to Shawn Michaels…..he tells Dok that it’ll have been five long months come Summerslam, since Shawn Michaels and the KLiQ stole the WWF Championship away from him…..and in that time they have grown from strength to strength…..but for all the bad that the KLiQ has done, their biggest mistake, was causing the reunion of the Hart Foundation…..because they might have the numbers advantage, but now I’ve got blood watching my back….and they’ll be watching me, laying you flat out on yours.

Rating: B+

VS.

Rey Mysterio vs. Psicosis

It’s time for the Light Heavyweights to shine, as the number one contender to Sean Waltman’s Title is in action, against a man who is making his Monday Night Raw debut, Psicosis. While this Masked Mexican might be an unknown to the majority of the WWF crowd, he and Mysterio know each other very well and they dazzle the crowd with a series of high spots and lucha-esq moves, the particular highlight of which was Mysterio connecting with a diving hurricanrana from the apron to Psicosis who was on the floor.

The end came after Psicosis attempted to hit a guillotine leg drop from the top rope. Mysterio moved out of the way and Psicosis crashed and burned, landing tailbone first in the process. Rey quickly moved in for the kill and rolled his opponent up….and with Psicosis in too much pain to kick out, Mysterio picks up the victory.

Winner: Rey Mysterio

Rating: C+

Mysterio is up celebrating on the second rope when the Light Heavyweight Champion Sean Waltman hits the ring. He charges his Summerslam opponent but Mysterio obviously sees him coming and rather impressively jumps backwards to land on the shoulders of the Champion. Rey spins his body round until he’s in position to hit a hurricanrana, which sends Waltman flying through the ropes, out to the floor and scurrying up the aisle to the back, shouting promises of revenge while Mysterio resumes his celebrations.

Rating: C+

Hey, it’s Dok Hendrix….and this time he’s backstage with the Son’s of Samoa…Rikishi, Kokina and Rocky Maivia. This is just your generic promo to keep a feud alive. Rocky says that he’s still banged up following an injury that he picked up wrestling Bradshaw a few weeks ago, but he’s hoping to be fully fit in time for Summerslam, because the Son’s of Samoa have some unfinished business with The Texas Long Riders and Jesse James.

Rating: B-

While that last promo was pure filler, it also served as a break away from the arena, to get Mysterio out of the ring….and the three men that stand there now in it. Jim Cornette stands between his two bogey men, Kane and the opponent for the Undertaker at Summerslam, Mankind. Cornette takes the opportunity to once again call the Undertaker a liar and a murderer….before declaring that by the time Mankind has finished with the Deadman, it’ll be another three months before your dark hero dates to show his face again…..Kane stands there looking all big, red and monstery, before Mankind takes the stick…..

“All things truly wicked start from innocence….the innocence of youth….the innocence of trust….the innocence of love……….the innocence of a small boy, who idolised the ground that his big brother walked on…..only to have that idolisation burned to the ground, along with his parents, leaving nothing but the taste of death in the air and the pain of the seared flesh on his bones…….and instead of looking up at his brother as a deity, he saw him for the demon that he is…..a destroyer of families….a destroyer of dreams…..a destroyer of life’s…..Undertaker…..you may have destroyed that young boy…..but you bred a monster obsessed with your destruction…..at Wrestlemania I made you suffer……at Summerslam I’m going to make you feel pain………but when the time is right…..Kane is going to make you feel twenty years worth of torture and torment……and when that the time comes…..you won’t…..have a nice day!”

Kane raises his arms high in the air, looking to release that flame of pyro from the corner posts, but before he has chance……

…….the Hardy Boys inexplicably hit the ring….we haven’t seen Matt and Jeff since they were decimated by Kane and Mankind several weeks ago……presumably they are here for revenge……they don’t get it.

They manage to get a few licks in, but they have little to no effect on Kane…..and Mankind seems to enjoy the blows. Eventually Kane catches both brothers by the throat, lifting them high in the air before driving them to the mat with a vicious double Chokeslam……goodnight Matt…..goodnight Jeff…..thanks for coming…….and now Kane finally gets to unleash his pyro, as a blaze of fire erupts.

Rating: B+

It’s time for the first of Al Snows “trials”….although if you look at the man walking down the aisle, talking to a mannequin head, you could argue that Al’s battle with sanity is a trial in itself…..speaking of battles with sanity, the giggling Bill DeMott at his side there seems to be going through a struggle of his own. Snow enters the ring and places Head in the corner, before turning to the aisle way to await his opponent……

……which is when the video screen flickers to life in its now familiar sea of static, before clearing to reveal Him. He stands stoic for a moment, before finally beginning to talk, voice distortion and all…..

“There is perhaps no greater theft, than the theft of a man’s creation….of a man’s vision…..money can be earned, possessions bought and sold….but a man’s creation can only be crafted in the mind….you can put a price on a work of art, but not on the artist that envisioned it……and if the theft of creation is the worst kind of theft…..then to steal that ideal, only to bastardise it, water it down and try to remould it in your own image is even worse…..Al, your opponent tonight was much like you when he first came to me….lost, confused….directionless……he’d seen his greatest creation taken out from underneath him and given to another…..it left him empty inside….a barren shell, void of all sense of purpose…..so I gave him a purpose…..and I can do the same for you…..let the trials begin…..send in the clown.”

…….just then some familiar sounding circus music begins to play….and Doink the Clown makes his way out from the back….

…..but this ain’t your daddy’s Doink….it’s not the happy, playful clown that entertained children for years….there’s no Dink here…..this is the original macabre clown, without a hint of comic relief. His makeup is stripped back to the bare minimum….and while he still has his green wig, he doesn’t wear it for long, stripping it off hallway down the aisle to reveal a mass of dirty blonde hair as he climbs into the ring ready to face Snow.

Rating: C

VS.

Al Snow vs. Doink

The announcers are a little taken aback by the appearance of Doink the Clown to say the least. Jim Ross, always the historian is quick to point out that this is Matt Borne, the original incarnation of Doink, who’s personal demons lead to his firing in late 1993. Jim Ross said that the creepy clown character was given to another performer, who grew and blossomed into a wrestler beloved by kids all over the world. (Jeez, laying it on a lite thick there Jim don’t ya think) McMahon says that obviously Borne’s troubled and turbulent past wound up sending him down a dark road….and seemingly caused him to seek out guidance in the form of the mysterious “Him”.

As for the match itself, it’s pretty good….for all of Borne’s past indiscretions he’s a solid worker, can take a good bump and has enough experience to grind out a good bout….not that Snow is in need of any help to put on an entertaining match….his current persona is worlds apart (in terms of charisma) from the Avatar gimmick that he was saddled with….and Head is slowly picking up a bit of a cult following of her own. This is the first real time since he lost the mask, that Snow has an actual direction….that direction is being on a mission to get “Him” in the ring….and Doink is not going to stop him. After a decent amount of back and forth action Al was able to put the clown away with the Snow Plough for the three count.

Winner: Al Snow

Rating: C+

Snow and DeMott are celebrating the fact that Al has passed the first trial, when the video screen again flickers into life, showing “Him” stood there laughing…..

“Congratulations…..but don’t let one victory over the clown cloud your mind and your judgement with overconfidence….lest you suffer the same fate as Icarus, who despite warnings not to fly too close to the sun, found himself plummeting to Earth on broken wings, when hubristic overtures overrode common sense….the games are afoot and the trials continue…..trial number two will be next week…..and your opponent, much like Icarus, flew too close to the sun and paid the price for his pride…..next week you face the Angel with the Broken Wings”

Rating: B-

We head to the back….and to the Steiner Brothers locker room, where Scott is hyping Rick up ahead of the elder Steiner’s upcoming match with Kevin Nash….when the door swings open and in walk the Million Dollar Man and Psycho Sid (who has to duck to fit through the low doorway.) The Steiner’s turn ready to fight, but DiBiase tells them to calm down….he’s simply here to extend his offer of his services again, since the Steiner’s have yet to answer his generous proposal, he’s taking time out of his busy schedule to come here and finally receive a response.

Scott talks for both brothers, telling DiBiase that they appreciate his offer but the Steiner Brothers got to where they are today on the back of their own hard work….they’ve never needed any bodies help before and they don’t need any help now….so thanks…..but no thanks.

Ted is not happy…..clearly not a man used to not getting his own way…..he tells Scott that he’s just made the biggest mistake of his life…..“I could have lead you directly to the top…..Tag Titles, Intercontinental Titles…..the WWF Title….you could have had it all……now, you’re going to have nothing…..I guarantee it…..because nobody says no, to the Million Dollar Man.”

Scott tells Ted to keep walking….and don’t let the door hit you in the ass on your way out…..we don’t need you to do any of that…..at Summerslam we’re taking those Tag Team Titles…..and then the next time I get a shot at that World Title……you’re looking at the Champion right here.

Rating: B+

VS.

Rick Steiner (w/Scott Steiner) vs. Kevin Nash (w/Scott Hall)

It’s Kevin Nash and Rick Steiner up next….and both men are out with their respective partners in tow. Lawler talks on commentary about the Steiner’s decision to reject the offer of the Million Dollar Man, saying that the smart thing to do would have been to accept the offer….Lawler says that the KLiQ come out here week in and week out with the superior numbers….because they’re too gutless to do anything by themselves…..and they out muscle and out number everyone that gets in their way…..Jim Ross echo’s that, saying that there certainly seems to be strength in numbers around here……and Psycho Sid may have just been the difference maker that the Steiner’s needed to tip the scales in their favour.

Although as we have seen in recent months, and as we see in this contest tonight….when the numbers are equal….and it’s one on one, man to man, then neither Steiner Brother needs any help inside that squared circle. Kevin Nash is a big, big, bad man, but Rick Steiner has been on the receiving end of too man KLiQ sneak attacks and tonight is out for revenge. Nash tries to use his power, whipping Rick hard into the corner….but Rick rebounds straight out again and nailed the big man with a leaping Steinerline. In the end Nash does gain the advantage, but only as a result of Scott Hall tripping the leg of Rick Steiner from the floor.

Scott chases Hall around ringside, throwing choice words at him for his loss to Pillman earlier in the night while Nash goes to work on Rick. It’s not often that you see Rick Steiner manhandled but Big Sexy tosses him around the ring as effortlessly as he tosses his hair back for the ladies. The ending in this one comes not from the result of either of the two Scott’s at ringside, but from this man….

……Psycho Sid, who comes lumbering down the aisle, informing the Steiner’s that they are “stupid” for not accepting the Million Dollar Man’s offer. Scotty gets into a shouting match with the giant of a man and Rick (not known for his intelligence) also decides to send some choice words Sid’s way…..and when he turns back around gets nailed right in the jaw with a hellacious Big Boot from Big Daddy Cool. Hall sees his chance and nails the younger Steiner from behind with a steel chair while his attention is on Sid…..and back in the ring Nash drops Rick hard with the Jacknife for the one, two, three.

Winner: Kevin Nash

Rating: B-

We go backstage again to Dok Hendrix, who this time is standing by with the Nation of Domination. They (Faarooq) cut pretty much the same promo as last week, citing that the Hart Foundation get preferential treatment, that the Nation is always held back and held down by the man…..Faarooq says that at Summerslam he’s going to take care of Owen Hart…..but next week, himself, Kama and big Nelson Knight are officially challenging the Hart Foundation to a six man tag match…..a little preview of sorts for what Owen has waiting for him in a few weeks from now.

Rating: B+

VS.

Owen Hart vs. Shawn Michaels

So its main event time….and this one should be a good one with the men involved….it’s Owen Hart facing off against Shawn Michaels in a rematch of their In Your House encounter from back in May….hopefully this time Hart can make it through the match unscathed. The Title isn’t on the line this time around, but as always when you face the Champ, a victory would have to put you somewhere near, if not right at the top of the list when the next Title Contender is decided.

Unsurprisingly this was a very, very good TV match…..scrap that, this was a very, very good match full stop, TV or not….and one that could have easily headlined any Pay Per View. (So no pressure at Summerslam Bret) It had all the hallmarks of what made their last match great….without the handicap of Owen working through an injury halfway through the contest. Shawn has toned down his aerial arsenal since his heel turn back in March, he knows that if you keep giving the crowd moves to cheer you for, eventually they are going to cheer you more than boo you…..Shawn knows that he’s the number one heel in the company right now, so why ruin that……for one night though….and against an opponent who is more than capable of matching him in the air….the high flying Heartbreak Kid is back. He hits the elbow drop from the top, a big plancha to Owen on the floor, and perhaps most impressively a springboard moonsault from the apron to the second rope and finally to Hart on the outside.

Owen isn’t to be outdone however, with a missile dropkick and a suicide dive through the ropes that sends Michaels crashing into the crowd barrier. The two men go back and forth, with both coming close to finishing the other off….never as close as the closing sequence though…..because with Hart down, Michaels backs into the corner and begins to tune up the band, exploding towards his opponent with some Sweet Chin Music…..Owen is able to catch the foot though, sweeping Shawn’s other leg out from under him and is about two seconds away from locking in the Sharpshooter when out of nowhere the Nation of Dominations music starts to play.

Owen releases Michaels legs and turns in anticipation of an assault…..it’s an assault that never comes however, because the Nation don’t appear. Hart realises this and turns to continue his offence to Michaels…..but he turns straight into some Sweet Chin Music by the Champ. Michaels makes the cover…..and this one is over.

Winner: Shawn Michaels

Rating: A

Michaels rolls Hart out of the ring arrogantly, just as the video screen shows a shot of Scott Hall and Kevin Nash shoving some sound engineers around in the production truck. Obviously the KLiQ “persuaded” the intimidated team to play the Nations music as a distraction. It’s not long before the rest of the KLiQ join the Champion in the ring, as they all have a laugh at Owens expense.

Shawn takes a microphone and announces that this is exactly why no Hart will hold HIS WWF Championship again…..because Bret….just like the rest of his inbred clan….is stupid…..Michaels says that he embarrassed Bret at Wrestlemania….he took Owen out in May and embarrassed him again tonight…..Summerslam is going to be no different. Bret Hart flat on his back, humiliated in front of the world…..“because the Heartbreak Kid is the WWF Champion…..and the Heartbreak Kid is going to STAY the WWF Champion…..why? Because the Heartbreak Kid, doesn’t lay down for ANYONE.”

Rating: B+

Final Rating: A

Final Thoughts

WCW came up with a novel idea this month……and by novel I mean stupid…..they decided to premier a brand new Pay Per View event called Hog Wild….bit of a strange name for an event right…..not when you take into account its location….Sturgis, South Dakota…..smack bang in the middle of the Sturgis Motor Cycle Rally. That’s the novel part of the idea…..the stupid part? It was absolutely free to attend…..zero dollars made on the gate….not that there was a gate….more of a row of Harley Davidson’s…..so not only did the talent have to compete outdoors under a burning sun, they had the pleasure of 5000 motorcycle fumes to breathe in while they were sucking air……way to go Bischoff…..not only that but the event sucked…..Hogan actually wrestled surprisingly, beating the young up and comer Abdullah the Butcher…..while WCW Champion Ric Flair teamed up with Lex Luger in the Main Event….to face the formidable pairing of Dave and Kevin Sullivan (yawn)…..the only real noteworthy news was that Diamond Dallas Page knocked off Dean Malenko for the United States Title. Even worse for them, was that on the back of that lacklustre display we trounced them in the ratings by almost a full point….16.01 to 15.05…..all in all a pretty bad week for the competition.

The Olympic Games are ongoing at the minute and the good old US of A picked up a gold medal in freestyle wrestling….a young man by the name of Kurt Angle impressed everyone….not only because he won the gold medal….but it emerged after the event that he actually competed seriously hurt, severely injuring his neck during the trials where he fractured two of his cervical vertebrae, herniated two discs and pulled four muscles…..essentially, he won a gold medal in wrestling with a broken frigging neck. The Gold means that Angle managed to complete an armature wrestling Grand Slam, winning the junior nationals, NCAA, the world championships in 1995 and finally the Olympics accomplishments. Vince is adamant that he wants to try and get Angle to turn pro but I’m not so sure….there’s plenty of great amateur wrestlers about….and while this Kurt Angle may well be one of the best ever, good amateur skills don’t always carry over to pro wrestling…..but at the end of the day Vince is the boss….if he signs Angle and then tells me to push him, then that’s what I’ll do…..anyhoo, on to Raw.

Week 2, August 1996

Posted 10 May 2019

Week 2, August 1996

Kiel Center

ATT: 16,872

TV: 16.27

The show opens to a chaotic scene backstage, where the KLiQ are in the process of laying an almighty beatdown on the British Bulldog Davey Boy Smith. Six against one with a cackling Sunny looking on with glee in the background, pretty much spells disaster for the Englishman as he gets his head bounced repeatedly off lockers and walls, until he can barely stand. Credible picks the Brawny Brit to his feet, holding him there in place while Triple H takes pleasure in taunting the helpless competitor, telling him that he should have known better than to Challenge the KLiQ, thinking he had a chance in hell at taking the Intercontinental Championship…..“but if you want the belt that much….I’ll be happy to give it to you”…..before picking up the Title Belt, taking a step back…..and driving the prestigious Gold Plate straight into the head of the Bulldog.

Hunter stands over his Summerslam challenger, before telling Kevin Nash that “hey Kev, he looks tired…..why don’t you give him a seat?” Nash says that it would be his pleasure, picking up the Bulldog and Jacknifing him straight through a (gimmicked) wooden bench used for dressing…..and down to the concrete floor…..

……just as Bret and Owen come charging into the room….it’s too little too late though, the damage is done and Davey Boy is down and out. The KLiQ turn to continue their assault on the Hart Brothers, but The Heartbreak Kid puts a stop to that, telling his gang of thugs to not lay a finger on the Hitman…..he doesn’t want any excuses when he kicks his ass in just two weeks time at Summerslam. The KLiQ walk way laughing to themselves, while Bret and Owen tend to the Broken Bulldog.

Rating: B+

VS.

Ahmed Johnson vs. Barry Windham

After that shocking assault we head live to the arena, where the opening contest is being introduced. The commentators use this time to talk about the heinous actions of the KLiQ, with Lawler calling them a pack of cowardly dogs. McMahon questions how the Bulldog can possibly compete in the six man tag match later tonight.

It’s Ahmed and his amazing shrinking tights up against the Texas Long Rider, Barry Windham. Ahmed has been spinning his wheels as of late, with little to do, while Windham has been in the midst of a feud with the Son’s of Samoa, which came to a screeching halt when young Rocky Maivia picked up an injury a few weeks ago.

As for this match, it was pretty good. Johnson benefited from being in there with someone as experienced as Windham, because psychology wise Ahmed is often a bit lost….fortunately for him the crowd are still behind him, despite his inactivity recently. Tonight he was able to reverse Barry’s running Bulldog attempt, sending the man crashing into the corner, before finishing him off with the Pearl River Plunge for the three count.

Winner: Ahmed Johnson

Rating: B

This one is over, but the Big Texan isn’t happy and calls for reinforcements, as Justin Bradshaw and Jessie James come charging down the aisle. The three men mount an assault on Ahmed, who is able to hold his own for a while, even sending Jessie James crashing to the floor with a clothesline over the top rope. Eventually a big Lariat from Bradshaw puts him down though and then it’s time for the Texan’s to try and lynch the muscular superstar. James brings in the Bull Rope, which Bradshaw hooks around the neck of Johnson…..and is just about to hang the man over the top rope when….

…..the Son’s of Samoa come running to his rescue. Maivia is there more for verbal support than physical, due to his injury…..but Rikishi and Kokina are more than happy to get a little physical, as they proceed to beat the bejesus out of the Texan’s (and Jessie James) causing the trio to finally retreat to the back.

Ahmed is helped to his feet before he snatches a microphone from ringside. He says that he’s been sitting around with his thumb up his ass for too long recently….he wants to be the next WWF Champion, but he’s not going to get title shots without matches under his belt…..he says to mark this day on your calendar, because this is the date that Ahmed’s career was reborn…..and anyone that gets in his way….is going down.

Rating: B-

We head to the back, where Bret and Owen are with Davey Boy Smith in the trainers room, where medical personnel are tending to the Bulldog. For now however the KLiQ find themselves in a fair fight….although quite how any fight where one of the combatants is a near 600lb Island Boy could be classed as fair is anyone’s guess…..and Kokina does spend a good portion of the match throwing his ample weight around, much to the despair of the KLiQ….particularly Credible, who is on the receiving end of a brutal looking leg drop from the big men. With Kokina’s sheer weight, Rikishi’s experience and Maivia’s youthful enthusiasm and speed, the Son’s of Samoa almost pick up a big victory tonight…..but almost doesn’t pay the bills…..doesn’t win wrestling matches either….because after the KLiQ triple team Kokina to send him to the floor, then Waltman and Credible drag Rocky to the outside where they double team him, it’s left to old foes Rikishi and Triple H…..they’ve faced each other a number of times…..and this time ends just like those ones did, with Rikishi counting the lights after a Pedigree.

Winner(s): The KLiQ

Rating: B-

The KLiQ pick up a victory, but there are points to be proved tonight, and out from the back come the former WWF Tag Team Champions Scott Hall and Kevin Nash, to join their brethren. The five men make short work out of beating the snot out of Maivia and Rikishi, with a Powerbomb to the youngster and a second Pedigree to the veteran…..before the group turn their attention to Kokina, who has pulled himself up onto the apron, looking to help his family. ‘kina manages to hold his own for a moment against the onslaught, swinging meaty fists and rock hard headbutts….but finally he’s overwhelmed, and while still stood on the apron, Nash climbs to the outside, leans back and ducks through Kokina’s legs……surely he can’t be looking to Powebomb almost 600lbs?

He is looking to……he can’t do it though…..can’t even get the big man a millimetre off the apron. So instead he re-enters the ring, uses his hands to take aim at the Samoan mammoth…..then charges (for Kevin Nash) across the ring and nails Kokina square in the face with a hard Big Boot. Kokina lets go of the ropes and waves his arm in an effort to keep his balance…..it’s a losing battle however, and like a falling redwood the huge man topples backwards, where he explodes through the Spanish Announce table with a huge thud, that sends splintered wood flying everywhere……put a fork in the big man, because he is done.

Rating: B+

The Son’s of Samoa have been utterly decimated….but their night goes from bad to worse, because while medical personnel tend to the beached whale that is Kokina, the Texas Long Riders make their way down the aisle, along with Jessie James. The three men pass the shattered remains of the announce table and are practically rubbing their hands together in glee at the sight of a prone Rikishi and Maivia prone on the mat and ripe for the picking.

The commentary team point out that this is a night when the KLiQ should have been at their most vulnerable, and if the WWF locker room had banded together, their reign of terror could possibly have been bought to an end, but with Billy Gunn taking advantage of a fallen Mero earlier, and now these three looking to do the same to the Sons of Samoa, the KLiQ’s actions are inadvertently being justified.

Windham, Bradshaw and James could care less though, all they see is a chance to get one over on the Son’s…..and after Maivia takes a Pumphandle Slam from James, and Rikishi eats a huge Lariat that turns him inside out, that’s exactly what happens.

Rating: B-

We go to commercial while the medical staff continue to tend to the Son’s, and when we return the ring has been cleared out of Samoan’s to make room for some Canadians, as Bret and Owen Hart make their way down the aisle. Neither man has a smile on his face however, in fact if anything they look quite somber as they climb inside the squared circle. Bret pauses for a moment to acknowledge the ovation from the crowd, before holding his hand up to quieten them down and beginning to speak.

He starts off by saying that he would like to apologise…..apologise to the fans in attendance, everyone watching at home….and especially to the boys in the back….for not completing a clean sweet last night and snatching the WWF Championship away from Shawn Michaels and the KLiQ. He says that he has no excuses, no complaints….Michaels came down to this ring, fought him man to man, one on one…..and as much as it pains him to say so, last night the better man won…..and right now, he doesn’t know how to process that…..

“……you know, I’ve always said that I’m the best there is, the best there was and the best there ever will be…..and to me, that’s more than just a catchphrase….it’s something I’ve always believed. There aren’t many men that can say they stepped between these ropes with Bret Hart and pinned his shoulders to the mat cleanly with no questions asked…..Owen is one, Davey is another…..and now, as much as it pains me to say it….you can add Shawn Michaels to that list….”

Bret goes on to say that right now he’s at a crossroads, and he’s not entirely sure what the future holds for him. He says that wrestling has changed over the years….he says that people used to cheer the Heroes and boo the Villains, but lately it seems like the Villains are being cheered more than they’re being booed, along with the bad language and obscene gestures that they bring with it. Bret says that the World Wrestling Federation used to be a family friendly show that he was proud to show to his children……“now, I can’t even let them watch in case someone flips them the bird….or tells them to suck it……’suck it’….try explaining that to an eight year old….”

He says that tonight he has a match with Brian Pillman booked in the main event…..and he’ll honour that commitment….but after that…..(Bret hesitates to finish the sentence, he looks to be quite conflicted)…..after that, maybe it’s time for this old sentimental to hang up his boots for good. The crowd don’t like the sound of that, and let Bret know that they don’t want to see him go…..that’s when……

……….!!!GLASS SHATTERS!!!……….

……the new WWF Tag Team Champions, Steve Austin and Brian Pillman make their way to the ring. They climb into the squared circle with the Harts and each lay their title over their shoulders before Austin begins to speak…..He tells Hart to quit his bellyaching about losing to Shawn Michaels…..

“……what, you’re gonna lose one match, take your ball and go home? Is that it? Well maybe that’s for the best son, because you clearly ain’t got it anymore……you can make all the excuses in the world about obscene gestures and bad language, but the fact of the matter is that none of that matters….you’re running back home to Calgary and your momma’s apron strings, because you know, I know, and everyone else knows, that Bret Hart is a shadow of his former self…..and here’s some obscene gestures for ya…..”

Austin flips Hart the double bird while Pillman hops up and down, wide eyes and frothing at the mouth with mad glee.

“……so it goes like this son, you go back there, put on your pretty pink tights and your girly pink vest…….and at the back of those tights where it says the word ‘Hitman’…..go ahead and paint a big letter S in front of that, and you’ve got my exact opinion of you…..then when you’ve done all that, limp your broken old ass down that aisle, climb into this ring…..get your ass whipped by Brian Pillman, then you can go back to Canada and start a Moose S##t collection for all I care, because that’s all you’ll be good for……”

Bret goes to respond, but Owen stops him, taking the microphone for himself. He says that Bret might be doubting himself right now, but Owen doesn’t doubt him for a second…..he knows that whether Bret won, lost, or drew last night, that he’s still the best there is…..the best there was……and the best there ever will be…..I know that the Hitman still has plenty of gas left in the tank…..and because of that, when Bret comes out here later tonight…..and makes Brian Pillman submit to the Sharpshooter…….it would be Owens honour and dream, to team up with his brother……and take those tag team titles away from you two idiots. Apparently Bret likes the sound of that too, telling Owen that he would be privileged to hold those Tag Team Titles with him. Austin tells them to quit the sentimental crap, because there’s no way in hell that Bret Hart is beating Brian Pillman……but if he does, then they can have their shot, because Austin would love to whip Brets ass one last time.

Rating: B+

VS.

Ahmed Johnson vs. Goldust (w/Marlena)

This looks like a throwaway match on paper, but in reality it’s a throwback to a feud that was never really concluded. Ahmed Johnson and Goldust were slap bang in the middle of a war over the then Intercontinental Champion, Goldust’s Title, when the Bizarre One was struck down with injury that cut the rivalry short. It’s a shame that these two never managed to mix it up at Wrestlemania as planned, because they put on a hell of a match here (mainly thanks to Goldust’s excellent showing) and could have pulled four and a half to five stars out of the bag had they had five minutes or so longer.

As it was they had a fifteen minute battle that blew the roof off the place. Ahmed has been on a bit of a roll recently, and Goldust is fresh off a victory over Chris Jericho last night, so both men are hot. It was Ahmed who came out on top tonight however, finally getting a win over the Gold One after laying him out with the Pearl River Plunge.

Winner: Ahmed Johnson

Rating: B+

Ahmed is celebrating another victory, when once again tonight the wolves descend on the ring. The KLiQ pounce en mass on the muscle man, and despite his strength, Ahmed is soon overwhelmed. He’s finally laid out by Scott Hall, who drives Johnson hard into the mat with the Razors Edge. The Bad Guy then pulls out a can of spray paint and tags Ahmed with the ‘KLiQ’ mark…..with the tail end of the ‘K’ beginning on Ahmed’s exposed buttock….because as usual he’s chewing on his tights.

Rating: B+

While they scrape Ahmed Johnson up off the mat we head backstage, where Dok Hendrix is standing by with the Steiner Brothers. Dok talks briefly about the fact that Scott Steiner was on the verge of winning the WWF Tag Team Titles for the fourth time, when Psycho Sid decided to interfere in the match, costing them the victory. Dok says that it appears as though the Million Dollar Man does not take rejection well. Scott snatches the microphone and screams that he and his brother, don’t take people sticking their noses where they don’t belong well either…..and as far as Scott Steiner is concerned, Psycho Sid is a dead man walking. He says that the KLiQ were number one on the Steiner’s ass kicking list, but Sid has just granted them a reprieve…..because Scotty is officially challenging him to a match at In Your House…..

“…….AND YOU’RE GONNA NEED ALL OF TED DIBIASE’S MONEY, TO PAY FOR THE RECONSTRUCTIVE SURGERY……BECAUSE AT IN YOUR HOUSE, I’M GONNA BREAK YOUR FACE, WHILE MR MONEY BAGS WATCHES ON AS I PUT AN END TO HIS INVESTMENT FOR GOOD….”

Rating: B-

VS.

Bret Hart vs. Brian Pillman

Back to ringside, where it’s time for the main event of the evening…..and the stakes have been raised in this one, with a Bret Hart victory meaning that he and his brother will be granted a Tag Title Shot against the Hollywood Blo……uhm, I mean against Steve Austin and Brian Pillman. Bret might have been doubting himself earlier tonight, but he showed everyone right here that there’s plenty of life in the old dog yet, putting on an instant classic with Brian Pillman, who himself hasn’t had much chance to showcase what he can actually do before now.

These two actually have plenty of unresolved issues to work through here, will Pillman being the one to cost Bret Hart the King of the Ring Title this year, turning on the former World Champion to side with Stone Cold Steve Austin….and Bret hasn’t forgotten that…..and after 20 minutes of gruelling back and forth action, the Hitman was able to put away the Loose Cannon…..who submitted to the Sharpshooter a mere second after it was applied……not because he couldn’t take the pain, but because he was saving himself to fight another day.

Winner: Bret Hart

Rating: A

Vince McMahon cuts Bret Harts celebration short, telling us to send some camera men to the back, because he’s getting word that the KLiQ are in the process of dismantling another victim. We finally cut to a shot of the parking lot, where the KLiQ are stood around a battered Chris Jericho, who is slumped against the side of a car with blood covering most of his face. Michaels is screaming at him that he should have kept his head down and his mouth shut…..“you bought this on yourself……send him for a ride Kev….”With that drags the bloody youngster around to the front of the car….positions him for the Jacknife…..and Powerbombs the helpless Lionheart onto the hood of the car, leaving a Chris Jericho shaped dent in the metal. The KLiQ turn and walk away, with Michaels shouting over his shoulder that if Jericho wants a shot at the Gold…..he’s got a shot at the Gold.

Rating: B+

Final Rating: A

Final Thoughts

Another week, another ratings win for the good guys in the so called Monday Night Wars. This week we were up 16.55 to Bischoff’s 15.61….not that it’s surprising when the only thing of any worth on the whole show, was the main event….which saw Big Van Vader and Bam Bam Bigelow team up to beat the combination of Sting, and the WCW World Champion, Ric Flair…..One Man Gang successfully defended his Television Title against Duke Droese, who in fairness to himself, has had significantly more screen time in WCW than he ever had with us. Brad Armstrong was able to pick up a victory over Public Enemies, Johnny Grunge….although for some reason, Armstrong has been saddled again with the Arachniman/Spider-Man gimmick that he was saddled with in the early 90’s….don’t ask me why.

Shawn Michaels was loudly informing the locker room that Rocky Maivia has no idea of how to put a cohesive match together, with the psychology of a brick wall…..the kids green, I’ll agree with Shawn on that one, but he’s definitely improved since his arrival here in the WWF….I might have even been wrong in my first assumptions of the lad….time will tell but I think in a year or so, Shawn will be begging to be climbing into the ring with old ‘No Pschology Maivia’.

There were a few retirements this month, most notably Superfly Jimmy Snuka, and the former Masked Superstar Bill Eadie……ironically I had tentative plans to bring both men in for one shot deals this year…..Snuka as a possible Survivor Series mystery teammate for the Sons of Samoa…..and Eadie’s name, along with Demolition team mate Barry Darsow, were being considered for a one shot Surprise Challenge for the Hardy Boys, as part of the Legion of Dooms training plan, which would have seen Matt and Jeff facing off against former LoD opponents…..typical really……Leilani Kai also announced her retirement, and then opened a training dojo the very same day….someone was busy.

Anyway, onwards to September…..
