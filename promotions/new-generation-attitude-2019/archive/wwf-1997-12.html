WWF Raw is War: Week 1, December 1997

Posted 5 June 2019

Week 1, December 1997

Breslin Center (East Lansing, Michigan)

ATT: 15,000

TV: 36.97

We open up the first Raw of December with the screeching guitar that signals the arrival of the Hart Foundation…..Jim Ross says that Bret Hart is never short of something to say (or something to complain about) as of late, so it’s no surprise to see them making their way out now…..

….Rick Rude disagrees, saying it’s very much a surprise (considering that even though they were victorious at the Survivor Series, they weren’t even scheduled to be on the show last week) citing that while Ravishing Rick is as American as Apple Pie (a dish invented in the U.K. by the way, go figure) HE can see the Canadian bias around here, even if JR can’t…..

….The Hitman grabs a microphone and waits for the boo’s to die down a little before telling the crowd that just when they thought the disrespect couldn’t get any worse, the good old red, white and blue powers that be go and turn it up a notch….he says that if it wasn’t bad enough last week that The Hart Foundation wasn’t even featured on the show (just one night removed from defeating the Wolfpac may he add) but tonight….when they ARE featured on the show….it’s Chris Jericho having to lower himself to the level of a Dean Malenko…..it’s the Ayatollah of Rock’n’Rolla….a man who should at LEAST be getting an Intercontinental Shot considering he defeated Scott Hall at Survivor Series just over a week ago….facing off against a NOBODY, for NOTHING……

….that’s fine, Bret says that Dean Malenko is an excellent wrestler, no doubt about it, but Chris will do what Chris does best….show the world that even on their best day, no American can outwrestle a Canadian Hero…..and then, maybe Chris Jericho will get the respect he deserves….and the shot he deserves…because he’s been carrying around this dead light-heavyweight title for too long now (and yes, Jericho STILL has the belt around his waist) it’s time he replaced it with some REAL gold….

….speaking of which, the reason they’re out right now….Bret Hart said last week that he wanted a shot at the WWF Title….Bret Hart DESERVES a shot at the WWF Title (after being on the winning team at Survivor Series) but apparently, McMahon must have not heard his words last week, so he’s here tonight to repeat that sentiment in person….

“….I am, the best there is….the best there was….the best there ever will be….and all I want, is a shot….see McMahon, The Wolfpac, The WWF, everyone has been screwing me out of what’s rightfully mine for almost two years now….now I could come out here and complain, but being the true Canadian hero that I am, I’m not going to do that (yeah, ok Bret…whatever you say) all I’m going to do is politely request an opportunity, against WHOEVER is the World Wrestling Federation Champion at the time….at the Royal Rumble….”

….that brings out Kevin Nash (without the rest of the Wolfpac) who tells Bret that if he wants a shot at the strap, then he’s going to have to get in line….and that line starts behind Big Sexy….

….Bret tells Nash that he’s GOT to be kidding….he says that the Harts drew a line under all this Wolfpac crap when they beat them at Survivor Series….he says that if there’s one person that DOESN’T deserve an opportunity around here, it’s Big Lazy…..

….it would appear that one Big Poppa Pump Scott Steiner also has title aspirations….because he too makes his way out from the back, telling Hart to shut his damn mouth, and Nash that he’s delusional…..he says that the ONLY one around here that deserves to be next in line for a shot at the title, is the man that lost the title back in August, and NEVER got his rematch….and that man, is the Genetic Freak Scott Steiner…..

…:::!!!GLASS SHATTERS!!!:::…

….hoo boy, talk about a combustible element…..it’s Stone Cold Steve Austin, and he doesn’t look like he gives a damn WHO’S in the ring as he marches down the aisle….

….he completely blanks the group of wrestlers as he climbs between the ropes to march to each corner of the ring, saluting the crowd with that now famous middle finger, before grabbing a microphone of his own….he says that he doesn’t give a damn about who wants what and why they want it, Steve Austin is getting the next shot at the WWF Title, and that’s the bottom line…..

“…..see at Survivor Series, Brian Pillman might have won the match, but he didn’t prove squat….yeah I lost the match….yeah I took one hell of a beating….but it took five people to do it, and I still got my ass up…..at Survivor Series, Brian Pillman became the number one contender and Stone Cold didn’t, but unlike some people around here, I ain’t gonna cry and bitch and moan about it, it is what it is and that’s that…..”

….but Austin says that Pillman is getting his shot at In Your House (currently subtitled “Dead Man Walking”) and that’s exactly what Brian Pillman is….a dead man….because even if he scrapes his scrawny little ass past the Undertaker, and walks into the Royal Rumble as champion, Steve Austin is going to be there waiting…..

….and the people keep on coming….as the Chairman of the Board, Mr. Vincent Kennedy McMahon makes his way out, along with his Big Red Problem Solver Kane, and the Corporate Cannon Brian Pillman…..

…..he asks all the gentlemen currently in the ring just who in the hell they think they are to be coming out here demanding anything? Vince says that he SHOULD fine the lot of them for their disrespect, but since it is the Holiday Season he’s feeling rather more generous…..

…..he says that he’s going to give Bret Hart, Steve Austin, Kevin Nash, Scott Hall……and even Chris Jericho what they want…..Championship opportunities…..

…..he says that at In Your House: Dead Man Walking, Chris Jericho will challenge Scott Hall for the WWF Intercontinental Championship (The Hart Foundation are happy with that) Bret Hart will face Stone Cold Steve, with the winner meeting the WWF Champion at the Royal Rumble (The Hart Foundation are happy with that too….as is Steve Austin)…..

“…..which just leaves Kevin Nash and Scott Steiner….and since quite frankly, your little bickering as of late has amused me no end, you too will be competing for the World Title at In Your House…….the World TAG TEAM Titles…..that’s IF you can get along…..”

…..Nash and Steiner are happy with….oh wait, no…they’re not happy at all….in fact Scott Steiner can be heard proclaiming that this is all bullish!t….

…..Vince isn’t done though, he’s the gift that keeps on giving, telling Steve Austin that since he was so intent on sticking his nose in Corporate business last week and helping out The Undertaker, he can help him out tonight too…..by teaming up with the Deadman to face Brian Pillman…..and Kane…..and THIS time, Kane will make sure to do as he’s told…..if he knows what’s good for him.

Rating: A*

VS.

Ken Shamrock vs. Rich Steiner (w/Ted DiBiase)


The Hart Foundation weren’t the only faction to pick up a big victory over their rivals at the Survivor Series…..perhaps sick of not being taken seriously around here, The Million Dollar Corporation upped their game and were victorious over the team of Ken Shamrock, Matt Hardy, Billy Kidman and Psycho Sid…..and Rich Steiner followed that up just twenty four hours later with ANOTHER victory over Sid, before the Corporation utterly annihilated the seven footer, putting him through a table and out of the WWF…..

…..tonight Rich will be looking to continue those winning ways against Ken Shamrock…..a man who has been lobbying for a one on one shot at Jeff Jarrett and the European Title for quite some time now…..unfortunately for Steiner, he lost…..

…..but the Corporation won….

…..because before the match could conclude cleanly, DiBiase decided to sacrifice the victory, calling in Jeff Jarrett and Southern Justice to join Steiner in an all out assault, giving Shamrock the disqualification victory….but at what cost?….

Winner: Ken Shamrock (via disqualification)
Rating: B+

…..because even as the official calls for the bell to signal the end of this match, the violence continues, as all five Corporation members (with even DiBiase granting himself a few kicks) take great delight in punishing the former MMA fighter….

…..Deebs pauses his own assault to grab a microphone (leaving his men to keep up the offence) telling the crowd that they thought hey had made themselves clear last week….if you want to come and step up to Jeff Jarrett and the European Championship, you can expect to be taken out, with a VERY hostile takeover….

…..Southern Justice drag Shamrock to his feet, holding him in position (while Steiner slips to the outside to collect that Acoustic Equaliser) as Jarrett grabs Shammy by the hair, telling him that this is just a taste of what’s in store for him at In Your House…..because Double J is going to grant Ken that title shot he wants……IF……he can make it to Dead Man Walking in one piece…..

…..with that Jarrett collects the guitar from Steiner…..winds back…..and smashes it straight over Ken Shamrocks head……

…..I guess it’s official, Jeff Jarrett vs Ken Shamrock at In Your House…..that’s if Shamrock is in any condition to compete, as the Million Dollar Corporation continue to make an impression.

Rating: B+

…..and just like that, we go over to some pre-recorded comments from Raven, who is still walking through that old church….he says that the four circles Jake Roberts have passed through have bought him directly to the centre of his trials…..the faith circle of hell in Raven’s Divine Comedy…..Anger……

“…..a home for those who have let anger prevail….cast into the River Styx to drown eternally in the waters of anger and rage…..

…..Jake Roberts, as you descend deeper into the depths of my hell, you loose a little bit of your moral highground…..the religious man on a road of vengeance….the man of god filled with anger about the very thing he himself created…..’blessed are the peacemakers, for they shall be called children of god’…..Jake, are you a peacemaker? Or simply an angry man cloaked in the facade of hypocrisy….

……anger is destined to be your downfall Jake….are you angry at me for who I am?….for what I do?……or are you angry at yourself….because you can’t be the man you were born to be anymore?……are you angry at me for my actions?….or angry at yourself for your lack of action?…….will your anger define you, as it has defined your opponent tonight?……”

……Raven says that The Nation of Domination is a faction built on rage, with its founder trapped in an eternal loop of anger and fear in equal parts…..

……anger at the quote/unquote “Man”…..fear at the prospect of forever being an afterthought….anger at the lack of opportunity through no fault of his own…..fear at the prospect of receiving that opportunity, and just not being good enough….Raven says that Faarooq has spent the better part of two years lamenting the fact that he has not been presented with the opportunities that he was too afraid to demand…..and even now, anger and fear consumes him…..anger at the fact that he has fear…..fear of his In Your House opponent Nelson…..

“…..Faarooq has built a career on foundations of anger….now those foundations are crumbling…..what will he do when all that remains is a broken mess of fear and regret?…..

…..use your anger Jake…..use it to unleash the rage that your god forces you to keep inside…..unleash your fury…..unleash your anger…..on me…..for I can set you free…..Quote the Raven……Nevermore…..”

Rating: A

VS.

Nine Circles of Hell Match: Anger

Jake Roberts vs. Faarooq

So it’s back to Raven’s Divine Comedy once more….this fifth circle of his hell….anger….

….so far Jakes Journey has seen him meet with the misfits and the afterthoughts of the World Wrestling Federation….even Goldust, while still a credible threat to most anyone around here is hardly what you could call your “normal” athlete….tonight’s opponent is different….tonight’s opponent has been front and centre for the best part of two years….not a misfit, and with two Intercontinental Title reigns under his belt, certainly not an afterthought…..but make no mistake, Faarooq is a very angry man….and that anger HAS cost him here in the WWF…..but has that anger recently been replaced by fear?…..

…..well, I’ll let you be the judge of that….but while Faarooq is solidly in control of this one (and obviously confident, because he’s left the rest of the Nation in the back) the man he will be facing at In Your House comes walking out from the back…..and as Faarooq spots Nelson, he wastes no time in bailing from the ring and over the crowd rail before the big man makes it to the ring…..which means he gets to stand and watch as the official registers a ten count, and awards this match to Jake Roberts.

Winner: Jake Roberts (via deliberate count-out)
Rating: B

Faarooq doesn’t look as though that loss bothered him in the slightest, so long as it keeps him out of the reach of Big Nelson…..there won’t be anywhere to run at In Your House however, because at the request of Faarooq, these two men will finally meet after months of running scared on the part of Faarooq…..

…..now I know what you’re thinking….if Faarooq is so scared of Nelson, then why is he challenging him to a match?…..that’s a good question, I’m glad you asked…..and the answer lies with a group of men who we now cut to standing backstage, watching Faarooq beat a hasty retreat on a monitor…..

……Faarooq has always claimed to lead the Nation of Domination by example…..and if the faces and gestures of his Nation brothers recently have been anything to go by, he ain’t setting a good example…..even now the four men shake their heads and share a knowing look as their leader disappears from sight…..they’re not happy…..and why would you be, after all, a Nation is only as strong as its leader after all.

Rating: B+

Well, we’re in the back, so we may as well stay there….going to (presumably) the locker room of MGM Productions….and I say presumably because it’s an awfully odd shot…..

…..the camera is zoomed in so close that all we can really see is one half of Goldust’s face on one side of the screen, the top of Marcus’ head (from his *ahem* beautifully made-up eyes to the top of his thick hair) at the bottom of the screen, and Marlene’s slender arm on the other….like I say, an awfully odd shot….

…..Goldust speaks non the less, saying that sometimes, the worst thing that can happen to a thespian, is to become typecast….be it say Cary Grant as the smooth charmer, Christopher Lee as the evil villain, Christopher Lloyd as the madcap loon….more recently we’ve seen Jim Carrey as the zany goofball, Gary Oldman as the sadistic nutcase….or Samuel L. Jackson as the Badass Muthaf…..well, you know what I mean……

…..Goldust says that if you play a role for long enough, it is eventually going to define YOU, rather that you defining IT…..he says make no mistake, Goldust is just that…..a role…..a part….another example of Vince McMahon’s insistence of making a mockery of the Rhodes name……..well no more…..he has played the part for too long, and it’s gotten him nowhere…..

……as Goldust continues to speak there is suddenly a strange sound that forces him to raise his voice slightly…..he says that for two years now he has been the punchline to one big joke…..and starting tonight, the joke is over…..

……the camera slowly pans out to reveal the source of that random noise…..hairclippers……and that’s not all it reveals, because as Goldust rubs a hand through the thick hair of Marcus, the camera has zoomed out far enough to fully show his face…..and while one half of his face is painted in the usual gold and black, the other half is completely paint free…..

……Goldust snarls at the camera, exhaling deeply before telling the viewing audience that they can forget the name…….Goldusssstttttt…..before clutching hold of Marcus’ hair and lowering the clippers, as the screen abruptly cuts to black……

….I think we need a commercial break after that…..

Rating: B+

VS.

Dean Malenko vs. Chris Jericho (w/Test)


….and we’re back…..and it’s time to rekindle a somewhat old feud…..although it was a feud that never really got started…..

….Dean Malenko came into the WWF, looking to prove himself against the best light-heavyweight athletes in the world today…..and the name at the top of his list? Rey Mysterio…..a fact that Chris Jericho didn’t take too kindly too, deciding that HE was the greatest light-heavyweight of all time, and to prove it, he put his WWF Career on the line against the Light Heavyweight Title…..lose and he’s gone, win and he takes the WWF Light Heavyweight Title away from the entire division, retiring it permanently…..Jericho won, and the Title that Dean Malenko had is sights set on was suddenly no more…..what a petty little s.o.b…..

…..to be fair to Malenko, while he IS smaller in stature (there is no arguing that) he is so much more than just some faceless light heavyweight….the man can wrestle with the best of them, and while he may have only really been in the spotlight a short time, he’s a true veteran of the sport…..

…..obviously this match was good…..both guys know how to work a clinic in there, with Jericho adding the flare that Malenko perhaps misses…..this was an important match too what with Jericho being named the number one contender to the Intercontinental Championship earlier this evening….Jericho couldn’t afford a loss this close to his match with Scott Hall, while Malenko would have a pretty good argument as to why he should be next in line for a shot here, ESPECIALLY if Jericho wins the gold at In Your House…..

…..in the end though, that point was moot….because while Malenko came out of this looking strong (and almost with the victory on a number of occasions) it was Jericho that picked up the victory……but not without assistance from his bodyguard Test, who (while the official was concentrating on Chris Jericho and his potential for submission) climbed up onto the apron and socked Dean-o Machine-o right in the jaw while Jericho scrambled free of the Cloverleaf…….

…..Malenko goes down……Lionsault……Cover……one, two, three……it’s just that simple.

Winner: Chris Jericho
Rating: B-

Malenko rolls out of the ring as Test joins Jericho (clearly anticipating a two on one assault) and heads to the back…..but not before telling Jericho that he knows damn well that he couldn’t beat him without the help of Test…..

…..Jericho takes offence to that, taking a microphone to tell Malenko (and the crowd) that he doesn’t like these aspersions that Deano is throwing his way…….he says that Chris Jericho is the very definition of a true sports man…..a Canadian purist…..an honest man……and he would never (eeeeever) cheat…….

“…..clearly Mr. Personality, Dean Malenko has been overwhelmed by my Canadian Charisma…..because honestly Deano, I don’t think I’ve ever met a duller man than you…..they should have named you after your Daddy, Boris Malenko, they’d have just had to spell it a little bit differently……Bore-is……Bore-is Malenko Jr…….it has a ring to it don’t you think?…..”

……Jericho says that now he’s shown Bore-is exactly who the greatest light heavyweight in the world is, its onward and upward to bigger and better things….namely, Scott Hall and the Intercontinental Championship……

……In Your House: Dead Man Walking is only a few weeks away, and while Jericho wasn’t expecting to receive a title match, it shouldn’t come as any surprise that McMahon has finally seen sense…..after all, if you had to pick between “The Old Guy” Scott Hall and “The Ayatollah of Rock’n’Rolla” Chris Jericho as your Intercontinental Champion, well….that’s a no brainier….after all, Chris Jericho is younger, (true) stronger, (probably not) bigger, (definitely not) and a whole lot more sexier (erm….no comment) than Scott Hall will ever be……

……and BECAUSE he is an Honest Canadian, all these Chris Jericho fans….these….these…..Jerichoholics…..they can believe him when he says that at In Your House, Scott Hall is going to find out the same thing that Bore-is Jr. just found out…..and that’s the fact that whether you’re lightweight, heavyweight, overweight, or waiting for a bus….you are NOT…..Chris Jericho…..and at In Your House, you will NOT….be the Intercontinental Champion anymore……

“…..thank you!…..thank you all!…..”

Rating: A

Oh You Didn’t Know?….we’re back from commercial just in time for the New Age Outlaws to make their way down to the ring…..

……The Road Dogg and The Bad Ass look to be in a particularly sour mood this evening, and Sunny is pouting up a storm as the trio enter the ring, where Jesse James grabs a microphone…..

……he says that he’s not exactly sure what’s going on here, but him and Billy, they are about to change their last names to Hart, because he’s fairly sure they’re getting screwed here…..

“…..see we lost those WWF Tag Team Titles on a fluke because of a team that don’t even work here…..screwed!…..then last week (instead of getting our contracted rematch) we had to fight Kevin Nash and Scott Steiner…..screwed!…..now it ain’t our fault that the Genetic Jackass couldn’t be bothered to show up (and we beat Big Daddy Dumbass anyway) but tonight, it’s Pinky and the Lamebrain getting OUR title shot…..screwed!……but we figured that’s ok, because they’re just saving our title match for a bigger stage…..for Pay Per View…..for In Your House…..but no, instead that shot goes to two idiots we already beat, who aren’t even a team to begin with…..screwed again!…..”

……James says that Harlem Heat can duck them and duck them and duck them some more….but duck, duck, duck, because one day soon there are going to be a New Age Goose knocking at their door…..and tonight, the Outlaws are going to sit right down there on commentary, to find out exactly who they’re going to have to beat to become the THREE time Tag Team Champions of the Worlddddddd……and if anyone’s got a problem with that, then they can Suck It!……

Rating: A

VS.

WWF World Tag Team Championship Match

Harlem Heat (c) vs. Al Snow & Chris Candido


We have a bit of an odd one here….and not just because Al Snow is in the match….but because we have an awkward pairing of face vs. face teams…..when current Tag Champs Harlem Heat put the titles on the line against former (1 day) champions Al Snow and Chris Candido…..

…..say what you want about the odd pairing of Snow and “Don’t Call Me” Skippy, but these two have gotten incredibly over as a result of it…..Candido has recently joined his partner in the Upper Midcard, while Al Snow is unbelievably knocking on the door of the Main Event, and is really only one big win away from crashing through that glass ceiling….Snow could probably comfortably have a quick World Title programme right now (he wouldn’t win obviously) without embarrassing himself, and BOTH men could easily carry the Intercontinental Championship…..quite a step up from the Avatar and Body Donna gimmicks that were stuck with when I took the book here…..

…..tonight isn’t about singles titles though, it’s about tag titles…..tag team titles that Al Snow and Chris Candido want (and undoubtedly deserve) back….Harlem Heat aren’t about to give them back without a fight however, not after finally stepping into the Tag team “main event” after months of spinning their wheels…..of course, there’s a third element at play here…..a third team who believe that they deserve those titles back….a third team who REALLY don’t like Snow and Candido…..a third team that’s sitting right there at ringside…..and a third team that frankly ruined what was a very good, hard fought, and even contest……

……because as Sunny uses her womanly charms to distract the official, and while Booker T and Snow are brawling on the outside, they knock Booker to one side and nail Snow in the back of the head with the ring bell, before tossing him back into the ring and bailing to the back with Booker T and Chris Candido in hot persuit…..

……Stevie Ray on the other hand stays right here he is….after all, why waste a perfectly good opportunity…..and as Snow struggles back to his feet and nails him with a Double Leg Slam Spinebuster (or Alabama Slam to me and you) for the one, two, three.

Winner(s): ….and Still WWF Tag Tram Champions…..Harlem Heat
Rating: B+

We leave Harlem Heat to celebrate (or rather Stevie Ray to celebrate, Booker T doesn’t look particularly excited about the way they won the match) and head backstage, where Dok Hendrix is standing by with the Wolfpac, as well as Scott Steiner and Sable…..and Hendrix wants to know how Nash and Steiner feel to be challenging for the tag titles at In Your House, against presumably Harlem Heat given the result of the previous match?…..

…..truthfully, they don’t seem to be overly excited, but as Nash points out, gold is gold, and gold means bank…..that’s more money in Kevin Nash’s pocket, and as far as Big Sexy is concerned that’s never a bad thing…..Hendrix asks Steiner if he feels the same, and more importantly, can Kevin Nash expect his partner to show up….a fact that Kevin Nash obviously hadn’t considered, but now the issues been raised, he’s also curious as to the answer…..

……Sable asks Dok what he’s trying to do? Is he trying to cast aspersions on Scott Steiner’s good name?…..

“…..YEAH HENDRIX, STOP TRYING TO CAST ASTERSONS……APPERSI……STOP TRYING TO DO WHAT SHE SAID…..”

……Steiner says that while he wasn’t looking for a shot at the tag titles, there’s no one in the WWF that knows how to win tag titles like Scott Steiner, he’s been winning tag titles his whole career…..in fact, the only thing he’s collected more of than tag titles, is notches in bed post….so if McMahon wants to hand him the tag titles (because by giving Scott Steiner a shot at the gold that’s essentially what he’s doing) then that’s fine with him……because he’ll take that tag team title and shove it right up the ass of anyone…..ANYONE….that gets in his way of becoming the WWF Champion a second time…..

……Scotty and Nash share a look there….I think it’s safe to say that this is a very uneasy alliance……

……Hendrix tries to alleviate the tension in the room by switching his focus to two other upcoming title matches…..Scott Hall defending the Intercontinental Title against Chris Jericho at In Your House, and Sean Waltman getting a shot at a rejuvenated Million Dollar Corporation’s European Title, when he faces Jeff Jarrett next……

……Hall says that Chris Jericho is just a little man(g) with a big mouth….now Scott Hall, he’s a big man(g) with a big mouth Chico, and he’s always been able to back it up….if Chris Jericho wanted a shot at the Bad Guy, then (just like he told Goldust last week) all he had to do was ask…..“Jericho, you can sing it chico….but at In Your House, you gonna have to bring it”…..

……as for Waltman, he says that he’s been waiting for another shot at the European Title for a while now….yeah, the Million Dollar Corporation are on a roll….yeah they got the numbers…..but so does Sean Waltman…..and he’s not going out there alone tonight….so let’s see how well the Corporation does when the Wolves are at their door.

Rating: A

VS.

WWF European Championship Match

Jeff Jarrett (c) (w/Ted DiBiase) vs. Sean Waltman


Earlier tonight Jeff Jarrett informed Ken Shamrock that he was finally going to grant him his one on one match for the WWF European Championship at In Your House….I think he might have been overlooking the fact that he might not even BE the WWF European Champion by the time IYH: Dead Man Walking rolls around, because tonight, not only is he up against a member of the Wolfpac, he’s up against a member of the Wolfpac who has already held the European Title once……Sean Waltman…….

…..the entire Million Dollar Corporation have decided to come out with Jarrett for this one, and true to their word, the Wolfpac accompany Waltman to the ring….as does Big Poppa Pump and Sable, which makes for an interesting dynamic, because Scott Steiner and Rich Steiner haven’t shared a screen in quite some time (although while they do share a tense moment when they come face to face, they generally stay out of each other’s way here)…..

……the match itself is good, if not somewhat chaotic with the sheer number of bodies at ringside…..the ending was chaotic too, but that was more down to Scott Steiner and Kevin Nash….because after bickering on and off through the course of the match, a shoving match breaks out after Nash gets shoved into the back of Scotty by Rich Steiner, and while the Corporation looks on in amusement, Big Poppa Pump finally gives Nash a big “Up Your, before storming to the back along with Sable…..

……during that Waltman took his eye off the match, turning his attention to the bickering Nash and Steiner just long enough for Jarrett to catch him with a Schoolboy….and one handful of tights later, Double J retains the European Championship.

Winner: ….and Still WWF European Champion……Jeff Jarrett
Rating: B

From there we go backstage, where Vince McMahon is watching a replay of Steiner and Nash’s “altercation” and chuckling in that distinctive Vince laugh, before turning to Brian Pillman, telling him that this is exactly why he’s got these two teaming up at In Your House, it’s going to be a hoot to watch them explode live on Pay Per View…..

…..Pillman doesn’t really seem interested in that…..he’s more interested in tonight’s match….the match that’s coming up next in fact….and curious as to whether Kane is going to do his job tonight, or is Pillman going to be fighting three men out there? Vince tells Brian not to worry….he says that Kane will do exactly what he’s told to do out there…..because if he doesn’t, well, let’s just say that he won’t like the outcome that follows….

…..Anderson and Flair join McMahon and Pillman in staring a hole through the Big Red Machine, who stands motionless in the corner, before Flair finally lets out a big “Wooooooo”, telling Pillman not to worry about tonight, because in a few short weeks time he’s going to be standing there with the World Wrestling Federation Championship around his waist, finally “the man” just like Ric Flair always told him he would be…..

……Flair says that if there’s one thing he knows, it’s what greatness looks like (because he sees it every morning when he looks in the mirror) and he saw greatness in Brian Pillman from the day he first cast eyes on him….all he was missing was that mean streak…..and he can tell by that wild look in his eyes, that the Deadman has more than met his match with the Madman….the uncrowned prince of chaos will take the throne at In Your House….and tonight, The Undertaker is going to get a little taste of that…..

“…..isn’t that right Kane?…..”

……the room turns to look at Kane, who was obviously never going to answer…..but what the Big Red Monster does do, is crack his neck, adjust his black glove……and walk out of the room…..

…..our main event…..is next…..

Rating: B+

VS.

The Undertaker & Steve Austin vs. Brian Pillman & Kane

…..that’s Hall and Waltman both down, with Kevin Nash not even in the arena…..not a good night for the Wolfpac, but a great night for Chris Jericho, who tries to high five Scott Steiner…..

“…..GET YOUR HANDS OFF ME YOU MAPLE SYRUP SUCKING SON OF A B!TCH…..”

…..yeah, Scotty doesn’t play so well with others…..and Jericho decides to take his win and split, slapping Test on the back and telling him that it’s time they got out of here, while a frankly scary looking Scott Steiner stands over Hall and Waltman…..

…..he hands his lead pipe to Sable (who fondles it in a very un-PG way with her hands) before telling the Wolfpac that there’s only one Big Bad Booty Daddy Wolf in the WWF, and he just huffed and puffed and blew them both down…..the next time they call him out, he’ll finish the job…..permanently…..

“…..PLEASANT DREAMS BOYS…..C’MON SABLE, LETS GO…..ITS TIME FOR YOU TO MAKE LIKE MY BIG BAD WOLFETTE…..YOU CAN HUFF AND PUFF…….AND BLOW ME…..DOWN…..”

…..Sable just giggles like a schoolgirl as Scotty takes her by the hand and walks her out of shot…..the Wolfpac might be down on the ground groaning in pain, but it sounds like Sable is going to be groaning Scott Steiner’s name in about five minutes from now……

Rating: A

VS.

WWF European Championship Match

Jeff Jarrett (c) (w/Ted DiBiase) vs. Chavo Guerrero


We go back live to the arena, and we are back just in time for a Championship match, as (much like Harlem Heat earlier) Jeff Jarrett puts HIS title on the line for the second time in 24 hours, and while we know that he WON’T be defending against Bill DeMott anytime soon, tonight it’s (surprisingly) Chavo Guerrero that gets the nod…..

…..back when the (now retired) WWF Light Heavyweight title was a thing around here, a lot of smaller guys got snapped up on Pay Per Appearance deals (Chavo being one of them) mainly because it was cheaper when I knew they weren’t ALL going to be showcased every week, but it had its downsides too….the first of which being that they were free to work elsewhere, and pick up injuries for someone else (like Psychosis, who picked up an injury working for someone, I forget who, and was quietly released a few months back) while the second downside was that I had no real reason to push them since they were on PPA’s and once the Light Heavyweight division was finished, so we’re most of their appearances…..

…..which is exactly why Chavo Guerrero has barely been seen on TV since I don’t know when…..

…..it was also why he got squashed in very short order, as the Million Dollar European Champion continued his winning ways, submitting the lesser known Guerrero right in the middle of the ring with the Figure Four Leg Lock…..

Winner: ….and Still WWF European Champion…….Jeff Jarrett
Rating: B

…..oh dear…..Chavo Guerrero might be in a whole heap of trouble here…..because much as we have seen in recent weeks, the rest of the Million Dollar Corporation soon make their way out, and much as we have seen in recent weeks, they absolutely destroy young Guerrero…..

…..Rick Rude says that Bill DeMott should probably thank Jake Roberts…..not only for showing the world what a fraud he really is, but because he saved him from this Million Dollar beatdown that we’re watching right now…..

…..DiBiase is cackling over the microphone as his men put the finishing touches on their destruction of Chavo, before saying that the fun isn’t over yet, because this Corporation brings a lot more to the table than just business savvy…..

…..table?…..

…..ah yeah, that table…..the wooden table that Southern Justice are currently bringing into the ring and setting up…..

…..if Chavo wasn’t finished already, he would be before long…..because as Dennis Knight and Jeff Jarrett finish setting up the wood, Rich Steiner climbs to the second rope, where he sits on the top turnbuckle……Mark Canterbury shows his (deceptive) strength as he military presses the (much) smaller Guerrero and plonks him down on Rich’s shoulders…..and as the rest of the Corporation cheers him on, Rich drives Guerrero straight through that wood with the Steiner (Death Valley) Driver…..

…..and NOW young Chavo is done……

…..the Million Dollar Man shoves a hundred dollar bill into the mouth of their victim as Jeff Jarrett hoists the European Championship high, as we go to commercial…..

…..but not before the camera catches shot of Rich Steiner pilfering away that hundred dollar bill, wiping the saliva off it……and shoving it quickly into his singlet…..

…..we will see you after this brief word from our sponsors…..

Rating: B

We’re back, and it’s straight back down to ringside where the Nation of Domination are making their way out, fronted by not only their newest member, but their brand new leader, The Walking Armageddon, Ahmed Johnson…..

…..the group enter the ring and give us a big Nation salute, before Ahmed grabs a microphone and welcomes us to the Birth of a New Nation…..a stronger Nation…..a united Nation…..a Nation not built on one man’s agenda and paranoia, but a Nation that is set to do exactly what their names says…..Dominate…..

…..talking was never one of Ahmed Johnson’s strong suits, but he’s improved a little after spending almost nine months learning under the Mouth of the Mid-South (and a man conspicuous by his absence) Jim Cornette, and this was a perfectly acceptable promo…..

…..Ahmed says that Faarooq took the Nation for granted, using their strength in numbers to bolster HIS win/loss record, while giving nothing back to the group…..that all changes from here…..this Nation isn’t about race, it isn’t about prejudice, it isn’t about excuses…..it’s about Domination, and that’s exactly what they are going to do…..starting TONIGHT.

Rating: B+

VS.

Bradshaw vs. Ahmed Johnson


This is Ahmed Johnson’s first match back since dropping the WWF Championship to The Undertaker back at Survivor Series…..and yes, he’s still carrying an injury…..and no, he’s not likely to be carrying the WWF Championship again any time soon (because I don’t want an injured Champion, not because he did a bad job with the strap)…..but Ahmed is one of the higher paid stars around here, so he needs to something to earn his money, and as a part of the Nation, he doesn’t have to do QUITE as much as he would solo…..

…..his opponent this evening is a gentleman who has actually had his fair share of battles with the Nation, even finding himself on the winning side against them at the Survivor Series…..granted Bradshaw himself didn’t actually survive, but his team won so that’s got to count for something right?…..

…..well whether it does or not, it doesn’t really matter tonight, because THAT incarnation of the Nation didn’t include the former World Wrestling Federation Champion Ahmed Johnson….a man who holds victories over the likes of Scott Steiner, Kevin Nash, Bret Hart, and even Stone Cold Steve Austin……and while Bradshaw is good, he’s certainly nowhere near the level of those gentlemen, which is why Ahmed was able to put him away…..although he didn’t do it alone…..

…..because after a very hard hitting (hoss) match, it was Scorpio distracting the official so that Kama could interject himself into the match, leaving Bradshaw to fall victim to a (very unsteady looking….Bradshaw is a big lad) Pearl River Plunge for the one, two, three.

Winner: Ahmed Johnson
Rating: B

We leave the Nation celebrating the first victory of the new regime, as Jim Ross sends us to the back, where he tells us that there is some sort of situation developing…..

…..he’s not wrong either…..because that situation is Stone Cold Steve Austin being physically restrained from entering the building by a slew of security personnel, while Vince McMahon stands (safely out of arms reach) barking orders at them not to allow Austin into HIS arena…..

…..Austin is adamant that he’s coming in, telling McMahon that “hell son, I got a personal invite from you yourself last night, I’m just here to see Brian Pillman’s damn coronation”…..

…..Stone Cold said he missed the end of the show last night because he had a spot of bother with he law, but he’s guessing Brian Pillman won the WWF Title right? After all they were all so damn confident…..oh and while he’s here, he’s got something for Ric Flair too….

“…..an Austin 3:16 rated family sized can of whoop ass…..”

…..McMahon tells Austin to shut up, he knows damn well that Pillman didn’t bring home the WWF Title last night, but he will do at the Royal Rumble, which is more than Steve Austin can say after his loss last night…..but as for tonight, just like he told the Hart Foundation, Steve Austin’s services are not required…..so he can leave…..

…..it takes some doing, but the security team finally manages to remove the Rattlesnake, and they do indeed remove him from the building, before McMahon orders that extra personnel are put on every entrance, exit, skylight and god damn window in the place…..he does NOT want Steve Austin stepping foot in this building again tonight…..

…..let’s go back down to ringside…..

Rating: A

VS.

Al Snow & Chris Candido vs. Dustin Rhodes & Marc Mero (w/Terri)


Where it’s time for (I guess) a number one contenders match for the WWF Tag Team Titles…..I mean, we’ve had no OFFICIAL word that this is the case (and Vince McMahon has been quite vocal about not wanting Al Snow and Chris Candido representing the tag team division) but it seems like Harlem Heat are willing to face any and all comers, so it wouldn’t be a stretch to see the winners of this one challenging Booker T and Stevie Ray…..

…..this was a really good match too…..both Al Snow and Chris Candido are (shockingly) only a hairs width away from the main event thanks to this unlikely team they have formed (did you ever think you’d hear that about Avatar and Skip?) while Dustin Rhodes is the most solid and consistent performer on the WWF roster, and that was WITH the restraints that performing as the Bizarre One Goldust put upon him…..as plain old Dustin he’s free to wrestle without the distracting theatricals, and he does exactly that, as he, Mero, Snow and Candido work a hard fought back and forth battle that could have seen EITHER team pick up the victory on a number of occasions…..

“……Oh You Didn’t Know?…..”

…..well, as they say, your ass better call someone…..because the New Age Outlaws are in the house, and the Road Dogg is throwing out insults over the house mic his whole way down the aisle…..

…..once he’s finished with the insults he tells both teams that there is only ONE team worthy of becoming the WWF Tag Team Champions around here, and that’s the two time, two time former, and soon to be three time tag team champions of the worlddddd…..the New Age Outlaws…..

…..not that Snow, Candido, Rhodes or Mero are listening…..in fact, they completely ignore Gunn and James and simply continue with their match, a fact that the Outlaws obviously find highly insulting, because when their words have no effect, they decide to storm the ring instead, attacking both Mero and Candido (while Rhodes and Snow duke it out) at the same time, leaving the official little choice but to throw this one out…..

Winner: No Contest
Rating: B+

…..not that the end of the match means the end of the action…..because these three teams continue to battle long after the bell rings, and tensions are obviously at an all time high, because while Sunny and Terri initially stand on the outside cheering their respective men on, those cheers soon turn into insults directed at each other…..which turn into threats…..until finally both women climb into the ring to scream at each other…..

…..and finally fly at each other…..we’ve got a catfight ladies and gentlemen…..

…..these two blonds scratch and claw, pull each other’s hair, and generally roll about on the mat much to the delight of this crowd, with each of their (rather short) attires “accidentally” riding up and exposing the (rather skimpy) panties below…..again, much to the delight of this crowd…..

…..Snow and Candido stand back and enjoy the show as Dustin, Mero and the Outlaws attempt to separate these two screeching ladies…..they’re not having much luck though…..Jim Ross comments that this would be a good time to send out some security to attempt to diffuse the situation, unfortunately all of the security this evening has been positioned at the entrances of the arena, in an effort to keep Steve Austin OUT of the building, so that’s unlikely to be happening…..

…..finally Sunny and Terri are eventually separated, with Billy Gunn and Dustin Rhodes throwing their respective managers over their shoulders, as Jim Ross sends us to a commercial break…..it’s time to calm things down a little bit in here…..

Rating: A

VS.

Kane vs. Arn Anderson


We’re back, and it’s main event time…..I was a little bit concerned last week with how well a Undertaker/Anderson main event would do just a few weeks away from 1998…..turns out it went pretty well…..I was originally planning to do Kane/Flair here (again because I was worried about how well a Kane/Anderson main event would go) but in the end I figured Arn deserved the main event shot after his performance last week, and once again the match delivered…..in fact it was excellent…..

…..neither Kane nor Arn are coming out alone for this…..with The Enforcer accompanied by his best friend Ric Flair, while the Big Red Monster has the WWF Champion (and his big brother) the Undertaker in his corner…..

…..I’m sure Anderson thought that the Dirtiest Player in the Game would be there to help him pick up the victory in this one…..unfortunately for him, with The Deadman on the outside, Flair wasn’t even able to interject himself into the match…..plus…..he was kinda distracted…..

…..because we cut away about half way through this one to a shot of the parking lot, where a line of security personnel are standing their ground at the entrance to the parking facilities, but a loud honking of a horn followed by the revving of an engine sees the sea of security suddenly scatter, as a black pick-up truck with Austin 3:16 emblazoned along the side of it comes roaring at speed into the parking lot…..

…..Austin hops out of the truck and makes his way towards the entrance of the building, as a number of those same security guards rush over…..Austin is in no mood to play games however, and he opens up a can of whoop ass on each and every one of them (putting a few dents into the sides of parked vehicles as he sends body after body crashing into the stationary steel)…..

…..level one dealt with Austin continues to the entrance, where it’s time for level two, as a second wave of security rushes out to meet him…..new bodies to pound on, same result…..and as Stone Cold steps over the fallen body of the final security guard, he swings open the door and enters the arena…..

…..and despite Vince McMahons instructions, Stone Cold Steve Austin is in the building…..

…..back in the ring Anderson has resorted to that old family trait of isolating a limb and wearing it down, choosing to focus his attack this match on the leg of the big man (after all, Kane is only seven foot tall when he’s standing) and it was working well, he had the man in trouble, even dropping the Big Red Monster with that patented DDT……

….::::!!!!GLASS SHATTERS!!!!::::….

…..when all hell breaks loose, as the Rattlesnake makes his way onto the entrance ramp (tossing another security guard down the ramp by the scruff of his neck) before making his way down the aisle with his eyes locked on Ric Flair……

…..the Nature Boy doesn’t look thrilled at the sight of a p!ssed off looking Steve Austin marching down the ramp towards him either, and he backs off around to the opposite side of the ring begging off like only Ric Flair can…..Austin promised Flair a beating though, and it looks like he’s here to make good on that promise, because as Flair continues to back up, he backs right into the Undertaker…..talk about being stuck between a rock and a hard place…..

…..Flair lets out a gulp that even Vince McMahon would be proud of, just as Steve Austin arrives to begin laying into the Nature Boy with right hands…..and as The Deadman watches on with a look of almost amusement, Austin hammers Flair all the way back up the ramp and to the back, as Arn Anderson watches his best friend get battered out of sight…..

…..although to be fair, he doesn’t have to watch it for long…..

…..because as he turns back around he turns right into the waiting hands of Kane, who Chokeslam’s him straight to hell, for the one, two, three…..

Winner: Kane
Rating: A

…..to top of a very successful 24 hours for these brothers of destruction…..

…..Dead Man Walking closed out last night to the sight of Kane and The Undertaker celebrating The Deadman’s victory, tonight we close out Raw is War with the duo celebrating The Big Red Monsters victory…..and as the brothers each drop down to one knee, Jim Ross questions just how in the hell Vince McMahon believes that Brian Pillman can defeat the WWF Champion in a Casket Match of all things……

…..speaking of the Corporate Cannon, Vince McMahon told us that Brian Pillman wasn’t here this evening, stating that he was off somewhere doing his own thing…..and that much is obviously true, because the Titantron suddenly flickers to life, with an extreme close up of Pillman’s maniacal grinning face…..and while it’s unclear as to where he is, where he clearly ISN’T is in this arena tonight…..

…..Pillman cackles that while he’s always been somewhat of an anarchist, revelling in the chaos of an unbound world, even he knows that there must be a balance in all things…..a dark to the light…..the hot to the cold…..the yin to the yang…..the Joker to the Batman…..every action needs that equal and opposite reaction…..and boy, what a reaction this is going to get…..

…..he says that just as every protagonist needs an antagonist, every unstoppable machine needs that kink in his armour…..his weakness…..his…..kryptonite……

…..Pillman says that The Undertaker may think himself the Dark Knight…..and Kane his Boy Wonder…..

“…..but if that’s the case, then I got your Alfred AND your weakness right here…..

…..with that the camera pans out from Pillman’s grinning face, to reveal a battered and bloodied looking Paul Bearer almost cowering on the floor behind the Corporate Cannon…..

…..Bearer hasn’t been seen in the WWF in quite some time, but he’s here, and as Pillman cackles again, the shot changes to show The Undertaker looking both furious AND distraught at the same time, before flicking back to the Titantron shot, where Pillman unleashes a few stinging leather belt lashes to Bearer (who squeals like a pig) then cackles that maniacal laugh again…..

…..Pillman says that when Kane burned his parents alive…..when the Deadman’s own brother stole his childhood away from him…..The Undertaker took Paul Bearer as his surrogate father, much as Paul Bearer took the Undertaker as a surrogate son, taking all that pent up repressed anger and frustration, pointing it (and the Deadman) square at the World Wrestling Federation and releasing the Phenom that the world knows today…..

“…..except you’re NOT a Phenom…..you’re just a scared little boy playing dress up for the approval of his father figure…..I am the Phenom…..I am the Crowned Prince of Chaos and at the Royal Rumble, the Ascension of the Anarchist will begin with the Destruction of the Deadman…..as I take the final thing that you hold dear, the World Wrestling Federation Title, and prise it from your cold dead hands…..I’m going to take EVERYTHING that moulded Little Teenie Tiny Taker into the man he is today, and I’m going to tear it all down……and since your little story of desolation began with the destruction of dear old ma and pa, lets see what happens when we destroy daddy number two…..”

…..Pillman turns and dives onto the pleading Bearer, unleashing vicious blows in an animalistic attack, before finally pausing and turning to the camera, breathlessly telling the Deadman to watch this……

…..Pillman walks out of shot briefly, only to return with a steel chair in hand…..he tells the Deadman that while he can’t be in the arena tonight, he bought a little bit of the arena with him (waving the chair at the camera with a sick smile) before opening the folded steel up and sliding it onto Paul Bearers leg…..

…..the Corporate Cannon says that the Undertaker may have defeated him at Dead Man Walking…..but by the time Brian Pillman has finished here, Paul Bearer will never be walking again…..

…..and with that, Pillman turns and viciously stomps on the chair…..

…..and stomps…..

…..and stomps…..

…..and stomps again…..

…..in fact, he doesn’t stop stomping until Paul Bearers screams have stopped (because he’s passed out from the pain) and he’s too exhausted to continue…..

…..Pillman finally sits down beside (the unconscious) Paul Bearer with a sick satisfied smile on his face, before developing a very serious expression…..he looks straight into the camera, telling the Undertaker that by the time he’s finished with him, both Kane and the Undertaker are going to wish that they’d just handed him the WWF Championship at In Your House…..

“…..because now, you’re not getting the Corporate Cannon…..now you’re getting the Loose Cannon, Brian “F’n” Pillman…..and at the Royal Rumble, I’m going to lock you inside one of your own caskets, and bury the legacy of the Undertaker once and for all…..the revolution WILL be televised…..and the revolution begins…..with your end…..”

…..Pillman looks down at (the still unconscious) Paul Bearer with a smile, before telling him that he’ll see him at the Royal Rumble as the Titantron fades to black and the show comes to a close on a shot of the World Wrestling Federation Champion standing in the middle of the ring with a look on his face that says that somewhere down the line, there’s going to be hell to pay for Brian “F’n” Pillman…..

…..see you next week folks…..
