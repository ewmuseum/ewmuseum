Week 3, March 1998

Greensboro Coliseum (Greensboro, North Carolina)

ATT: 30,000

PPV BUY: 1.98

Announce Team: Tony Schiavone, Dusty Rhodes & Bobby “The Brain” Heenan

Quote:

…..it’s Sunday night, and things are about to get a little extreme up in here…..no, we aren’t in Philadelphia, but the rule book IS being ripped up, right here at WCW Uncensored 1998…..

…..we open up at the announce table, where Tony Schiavone, Dusty Rhodes & Bobby “The Brain” Heenan welcome us to what is sure to be a wild night…..

…..they run down not only the matches, but the stipulations surrounding them…..we have a street fight, a flag match, a tables match, and yes, the WCW Championship will be contested for in a strap match…..and if that wasn’t enough (just in case you didn’t get the memo) there are going to be no short cuts here tonight (I’m looking at you Eddie Guerrero) because even in the matches that have no stipulations, we are letting them go and relaxing those rules…..you want to get disqualified tonight, then you’re going to have to do something REALLY extreme to get the referees attention…..

…..and if you want to see extreme, it doesn’t come much more extreme than driving your opponent through a wooden table, but that’s exactly what Jeff Hardy or Curt Hennig will have to do to win this one…..

…..and that match is next…..

Rating: N/A

Quote:

Tables Match

…..the tables stipulation might be a recent addition to this match, but there’s still plenty of heat behind it, as Jeff Hardy and Curt Hennig have been brawling in the ring, outside of the ring, backstage, and even outside the building…..it’s safe to say there’s no love lost between these two…..

…..since this is the first tables match in a mainstream wrestling organisation, the announcers talk through the rules quickly before this starts…..

…..really quickly…..

…..there’s only one rule…..

…..put your opponent through a table…..

…..so that’s exactly what they tried to do…..

…..considering the fact that these two have been trying to kill each other for the past month, the match actually started out much as any other match would…..in the ring……

…..Jeff Hardy has come a long way in his tenure here in WCW…..from (barely) midcardtag team wrestler with his brother in the WWF, to stand out, solid upper card worker here in World Championship Wrestling…..and no, he hasn’t had the title success that his brother has had since the team was separated, but arguably, Matt Hardys brief (and lone) European Championship has ended with him getting a little bit lost in the shuffle up north, while Jeff Hardys stock has risen with every high profile rivalry that he’s been involved in…..and with opponents like Curt Hennig, he’s only going to get better…..

…..Hardy might be somewhat of a daredevil, but as I said earlier, this one actually starts out as a wrestling match, which is where Curt Hennig thrives…..Hennig hits Hardy with a series of chain wrestling holds that Jeff can’t keep up with, before finally batting the youngster round the back of the head with a cocky smirk on his face……

…..he’s not so cocky a few minutes later though when Hardy sends him sailing to the outside…..and he’s even less cocky when his opponent slingshots himself over the top right on top of him…..Hardy takes advantage of the lack of rules to whip Curt into the barricade, before running and leaping up and off the ring steps, sending his whole body crashing into Hennig (and Hennig crashing further against the barrier in the process)…..

…..Hardy slides a table into the ring (along with a steel chair) and rolls Hennig back inside…..he sets the table up against the turnbuckles in the corner and then crashes the chair across the back of his opponent (leading the announcers to talk about Curts history of back problems) before propping Hennig up against the table, setting the chair up in front of him, before running the length of the ring and springing off the chair for Poetry in Motion……

…..except Hennig moves…..

…..and Hardy smashes through the wood and crumples to the mat in a heap…..

…..the announcers question for a moment whether this match is over (its WCW after all, when have they ever been clear about match rules) but they eventually settle on the opinion that you have to drive your opponent through the table with an offensive move to win this match, not simply move to allow your opponent to table themselves…..

…..so with that cleared up (and the first big spot out of the way) all the wrestling goes out of the window…..Hennig may be a wrestler first and foremost but he knows how to fight when he needs to…..the two men get down and dirty as the attempt to beat each other down long enough to put the other through a table…..Hardys steel chair assisted Poetry in Motion gets attempted again after he whips Curt into the turnbuckles, and he’s successful the first time…..he’s not so successful the second time though, because as he comes running again Hennig whips up the steel chair and throws it full force at his oncoming foe…..

…..the difference in styles of these two men couldn’t be clearer than in this match…..while Hardy looks to utilise high flying offence to drive his opponent through the wood, you’re not going to be seeing Curt Hennig attempting a splash off the top through a table…..no, Curt is more focused with trying to use wrestling moves to do it…..body slams, suplex’s, even a Hennig Plex…..Hardy is able to wiggle free on each occasion though…..

…..eventually he catches a kick attempt on the outside, sweeps the other leg and then slingshots his opponent skull first into the steel ring post…..Hennig staggers backwards and Hardy positions a table behind him that he slumps backwards into…..

…..Jeff sets Curt into position…..

…..climbs onto the apron…..

…..and then to the top rope…..

…..where he pulls off his shirt…..

…..and gets crotched right there on the top turnbuckle…..

…..Hardy took way too long getting into position, and a veteran like Curt Hennig isn’t going to miss a wide open opportunity like that…..he’s rolled off the table and up on the apron, where he sweeps Jeffs legs out from under him…..Hennig reaches up and grabs hold of the youngster, and in a spot we have seen Ric Flair and Sting do a million times in their matches over the years, he launches Jeff off the top…..

…..the big difference between this spot and the Flair/Sting spot however, is that Curt throws Jeff to the floor…..

…..and through that same table that Hardy had set up…..

…..that’s an offensive move…..

…..and this one is over…..

Winner: Curt Hennig
Rating: A

Quote:

…..Hennig stumbles through the ropes and gets his hand raised by the official, before collapsing in exhaustion on the mat…..Heenan says that Curt Hennig won this match by sticking to the basics……

”…..this Jeff Hardy kid is good, but he’s stupid Schiavone…..all you have to do is put your opponent through a table, you don’t need to fly off the roof of the building to do that……Curt Hennig knew that, that’s why he has his hand raised while Jeff Hardy is broken in half on the outside…..”

…..Dusty actually agrees with Heenan, but both he and Schiavone applaud the effort of Hardy, saying that this young man has a bright future ahead of him here in WCW…..

…..Hennig slowly rolls to the outside and looks to make his way to the back, stepping around the broken table (and broken Hardy) but he stops for a minute to look down at his opponent, before reaching down and dragging the youngster back to his feet…..

…..he pulls Hardy over to the guardrail and props him up against it, as Schiavone and Rhodes yell on commentary that the kid has suffered enough……

…..Hennig actually agrees apparently…..because he backs away from his opponent, before softly slapping him on the chest in what looks to be a rare show of respect, before heading to the back…..

…..yes Curt Hennig picked up the victory here tonight, but Jeff Hardy showed a lot of people exactly what he’s about tonight…..and if he can win Hennig’s respect, he’s clearly on the right path……

…..let’s head to the back…..

Rating: B+

Quote:

…..where we have a couple of promos ahead of our next match……Mike Tenay is standing by with the Patriot, while Mean Gene is in a second location with Lance Storm…..

…..neither of these men are exactly the worlds greatest talkers so we’ll keep this short and sweet…..Patriot says that he’s had it up to his back teeth with Lance Storm and his Sovereignty buddies running down the good old US of A ”the greatest nation in the world Mike Tenay” and tonight, not only is he going to prove that when he raises Old Glory high in the air in victory, he’s gonna take that maple leaf of Lance storms, and shove it right down his Canadian throat…..

…..Storm on the other hand says that he and the Sovereignty (who save for Chris Benoit, flank him for this promo) have little left to prove…..he says that they’ve already proved that the Sovereign Nations produce superior athletes, superior wrestlers ”and frankly Mean Gene, superior men” and tonight will be no different…..and when Storm wins this match, and the Canadian National Anthem is ringing out in victory, the can close the door on this pointless debate once and for all……

…..let’s get to it shall we…..

Rating: B

Quote:

Flag Match

…..this whole USA vs The Sovereignty war has been waging on for some time now…..and what better way to end a war? Why by capturing a flag obviously!…..plus we all know Vinnie Ru loves a pole match on any description…..

…..so here we are…..it’s The Patriot facing off against Lance Storm, and the pride of a nation is on the line…..

…..I don’t think anyone was expecting a classic here…..not because the two men couldn’t have a good match, Patriot is a capable worker, and Lance Storm is good enough in the ring to take even a capable worker to a great match…..the biggest problem was always going to be the stipulation…..flag matches suck……it plays into the story though so let’s get to it……

…..Patriot takes charge in the early moments of the match, rallying the crowd behind him, but it’s not long before Storm takes control of the match…..we have the usual close calls where each man is only fingertips away from victory, but in the end it looks like Patriot is going to pick up the win, nailing Storm with the Uncle Slam and making the climb towards Old Glory…..

…..of course, that’s when the Sovereignty make an appearance……both junior members of the Blue Bloods, as well as The Amazing French Canadians…..

…..there’s no dq in this, so there’s not much that the official can do about it…..there’s plenty that the Patriot can do about it though, and he manages to fight off all four men so effectively that eventually His Lordship has to make his way out from the back to take charge…..

…..Regal shakes his head with a look of distaste on his face as he steps over the downed Sovereignty members at ringside, before sliding something into the ring and going to work distracting the Patriot…..the distraction works too…..as the Star Spangled Superstar misses Lance Storm sliding a set of brass knuckles on his fist…..

…..at least until Lance spins him round and nails him right in the jaw…..

…..Patriot is knocked out cold, leaving Storm with little to do but take a leisurely climb up the turnbuckles to recover that Canadian Flag…..

…..and this one is over…..

Winner: Lance Storm
Rating: B

Quote:

…..he says that finally they have proved once and for all that the Sovereign Nations are the superior ones, before saying that all there is left for the Sovereignty to do tonight, is head to the back, pour His Lordship a cup of tea (because he’s still seething that he even had to come out here, calling his men bloody incompetents) and watch their good friend Chris Benoit bring the Television Title home…..

”…..now if you’ll all kindly have some respect…..shut your mouths…..and all rise for the playing of the Canadian National anthem…..”

…..obviously there is little respect shown…..but then, since a laughing Jacques and Oulette drag the unconscious body of the Patriot to his feet and ‘Weekend at Bernies’ a salute out of him, there isn’t a lot of respect shown by these ‘gentlemen’ either…..

…..so let’s leave them to it…..

…..let’s play a promo package highlighting the history between the Forgotten and the team of Mike Awesome and Gideon Cain…..and then we’ll go to our tag title match…..

…..it’s gonna be ugly…..

Rating: B

Quote:

…..I wasn’t kidding when I said this was going to be ugly…..it was just four big bulls stiffing the snot out of each other…..so much so that it’s fortunate that we have relaxed the rules a little bit here tonight, otherwise this one would have been thrown out before it even really got started…..

…..the bell sounds and these four men pick right back up where they left it off in that bar room brawl that they were part of the other day…..because they just go right back to slugging the hell out of each other…..Kevin Sullivan is at ringside, but Awesome and Cain are alone…..usually they would have Missy Hyatt right there, but they obviously knew that a war was going to take place, because while Missy escorted her men down the aisle for their entrance, the duo ordered their reluctant manager to the back before the bell sounded…..

…..this is a big task for Mike Awesome and Gideon Cain…..particularly Cain, who is still a rookie on all fronts…..he’s come on a long, long way from his debut mind you…..he’s been somewhat of a pet project of mine, and his partnership with Awesome, pairing with Hyatt, and series of high profile opponents has seen his popularity and skill set rise quicker than Jerry Lawlers heart rate during a bikini contest…..

…..he puts on a good showing here too…..both of these teams have become guilty pleasures of mine and I’ve had plans for them to collide over the tag titles for some time…..and collide they do…..

…..it’s a free for all to start the match, with all four men going at it both in and out of the ring, before the official is finally able to gain a measure of control, as Vader and Bigelow take the advantage, isolating the rookie member of the opposition…..Vader goes to town with clubbing blows and splashes in the corner, while Bigelow does more of the ‘wrestling’ (if you can call a beating wrestling) to grind Gideon Cain down…..

…..eventually Cain is able to make the hot tag, bringing in Mike Awesome…..who really is something else for a man of his size…..he takes on both men while his opponent recovers, actually knocking Vader and Bigelow down with a double top rope clothesline, as well as (even more impressively) being inches from victory after managing to nail Bammers with a huge Awesome Bomb…..in fact it was only Sullivan dragging the official from the ring before his hand struck three that saved this match…..

…..Cain tags back in, and that four way donnybrook resumed, with the official again struggling to keep charge…..and ultimately that was what tipped the match in favour of the Forgotten…..because with the referee momentarily distracted as he tries to get the illegal men from the ring, Sullivan rolls under the bottom rope, just as Cain is attempting to hit the East of Eden on Vader…..

…..Sully spins Cain round, and in a throwback to his glory days, let’s off a fireball right in the face of the rookie…..

…..Cain drops to the mat clutching his face and Vader is quick to react, coming off the second turnbuckle with that Vader Splash to pick up the one, two and three…..

…..the Forgotten retain…..

…..but they really had to work for it…..

…..and you’ve got to think they haven’t heard the last of Mike Awesome and Gideon Cain…..

Winner(s): and Still WCW Tag Team Champions…..The Forgotten

Rating: B+

Quote:

…..who’s standing by with the longest reigning champion here in WCW today…..the Papi of Perfection…..the man who (by hook or by crook) has managed to keep hold of that United States Championship against some of the biggest names in the company…..Jeff Hardy, Curt Hennig, Rikishi, The Rock, JPL…..you name them, no one has been able to take this championship from him…..yes…..it’s Eddie Guerrero…..

…..pin Bill Goldberg…..”

”…..and that’s if he even makes it to the ring esè…..I mean, I don’t know where Chavito is holmes, it it can be pretty dangerous back here…..and we all know that Bill Goldberg is such an idiot, he can get injured before the bell even rings esè…..”

…..Tenay motions to a tv monitor (as we switch to a shot of of a dressing room door) which sees the making of a new entrance technique for Goldberg, as a group of security guards escort the challenger to the Gorilla Position…..we have it all here…..the knock, the guards, the pyro, the smoke breathing…..and as we cut back to a shot of Eddie Guerrero…..who does not look happy…..next to a smiling Mike Tenay…..

Quote:

…..so here it is, the biggest match of Bill Goldberg’s short career…..as he puts his undefeated streak on the line against Eddie Guerrero, for the United States Championship…..

…..and boy are the crowd hot for it…..

…..Eddie Guerrero wasn’t joking when he said he’d put this title on the line against all comers…..he might not have won every match (thank you ‘the title can only change hands on a pinfall or submission’ rule) but he’s been in there with some huge names…..and as such, his stock has skyrocketed…..in fact, he could headline this event quite comfortably against the Stinger, so over is he right now…..

…..likewise, Bill Goldberg isn’t far behind in terms of popularity…..granted he’s not quite at Eddie’s level, but 65 straight victories in a row have him wayyyyyy up there at the top of the card…..

…..he knows what it is to hold gold too…..a former champion in his own right after capturing the Television Title several months ago…..unfortunately (Eddie Guerrero and) injury forced him to vacate that championship…..

…..bigger and better things tonight?…..

…..the match itself is really very good…..it’s certainly the longest and most competitive of Goldberg’s career to date…..

…..Eddie shows exactly why he has been the US Champion for so long…..he’s he’s happy to take the easy way out sure, but he is one hell of an accomplished wrestler, there’s no doubt about that…..but he’s also one hell of a smart wrestler…..

…..see Bill Goldberg has been trying to get his hands on Eddie for MONTHS now…..months…..and Eddie has been poking that bear from a distance, making him gradually more and more mad…..and while most people would think you must be crazy to deliberately antagonise Bill Goldberg, angry people are rash…..reckless…..and they make mistakes…..

…..that’s not to say Eddie doesn’t get his comeuppance…..Goldberg DESTROYS him in the opening portion of the match…..just tosses him around like a rag doll…..but that anger does get the better of him, as he goes for a spear far too early, which Eddie dodges, leaving Goldberg to crash shoulder first into the turnbuckles…..

…..and it’s that shoulder that Eddie injured originally…..

…..from there the Champion takes control, focusing on that body part (which Bobby Heenan is particularly pleased about) “…..and if I was managing him Schiavone, that’s exactly what I would be telling him to do….that’s smart…..Goldberg might be the man, but he’s still A man…..and that man has a target on that shoulder since the injury, not to mention the fact that it’s hard to spear a guy with a separated shoulder…..”

…..and focus on that shoulder is what Eddie does…..even going so far as to wrap it around a steel ring post, before crushing it with a chair (telling the official to go ahead and disqualify him)…..the official doesn’t, obviously…..because as Dusty reminds us ”we’re uncensored baby”, JT the chair certainly does some damage…..

…..he climbs into the ring and raises the weapon…..

…..only to get speared out of his boots…..

…..Eddie climbs to the top rope (looking for that Frog Splash) but spends so long congratulating himself and taunting the fans, for the victory that will surely follow, that when he leaps he leaps right into a mid air Spear……

…..the crowd erupts as Goldberg suplex’s the man up, and holds him (one handed) above his head (bad shoulder remember) before Jack Hammering him straight to the mat for the one…..the two…..and after over eight long months……

…..the three…..

…..we have a new US Champion…..

…..and his name is Goldberg…..

…..66-0…..

Winner: and New WCW United States Champion…..Goldberg

Quote:

…..Michaels is a broken mess and Sting rolls him into the ring…..he begins to touch the corners but he’s cut off before he reaches the fourth, Adam Copeland and Christian Cage come rushing out from the back…..

…..they don’t get far though…..

…..because clearly still ticked off from their loss earlier in the evening, Randy Savage and The Warrior make their way out to cut them off…..

…..Sting is briefly distracted by the new arrivals, and when he turns back around he’s caught with some Sweet Chin Music…..

…..or rather he CATCHES some Sweet Chin Music…..

…..catches the foot of his opponent that is…..

…..then sweeps the leg…..

…..and hooks on the Scorpion Deathlock…..

…..Michaels is tapping like mad, but there’s no submissions here…..Sting is going to have to put his opponent out if he wants to win….so finally he releases the hold and drags his weary opponent to his feet, then whips him into the corner, where he hits a mighty Stinger Splash…..

…..Michaels is stunned but Sting isn’t giving up…..

…..he backs up and comes charging again, crushing the wind out of his opponent a second time…..

…..a third time he backs up, looking to finally shut the mouth of Michaels once and for all…..

…..he lets out a loud howl and then runs again…..

…..and leaping…..

…..face first into a last ditch Sweet Chin Music…..

…..Sting goes down hard…..the impact of the kick coupled with the force of Sting leaping into it means the champion is out cold…..

…..Michaels isn’t much better…..but he is conscious…..

…..and as the crowd rains trash at the ring as Michaels stumbles to one corner…..

…..staggers his way to a second…..

…..drags his way along the ropes to the third…..

…..and finally (after dropping to his knees in exhaustion) crawls his way to the fourth…..

…..and with one more slap of the hand on turnbuckle…..

…..we have a brand new WCW Champion…..

Winner: and New WCW Champion…..Shawn Michaels

…..the bell rings, and the crowd is NOT pleased…..

…..Copeland and Cage manage to scramble away from Warrior and Savage as Traylor and JPL drag them down to the ring, where the four of them help a battered and bruised Shawn Michaels to his feet…..

…..Copeland and Cage toss Sting to the outside (where surprisingly, Savage and the Warrior help him to his feet) as Levesque snatches the Big Gold Belt from the official, before Big Ray-Ray tosses HIM to the outside too…..

…..one by one the locker room empties onto the stage as Levesque straps the WCW Championship around the waist of Shawn Michaels, before Traylor hands the new champion a microphone…..

…..he first of all tells the crowd to shut up…..

”…..because Mr. Wrestling…..and YOUR brand new World Champion…..has something to say…..

…..so this goes out to all you idiots in the crowd…..all you jack-off’s watching at home in your momma’s basements…..you announcers up there, who spend all your time complaining about Shawn Michaels and his degenerates from behind your desk…..the referees who try to hold us down…..and more importantly, all you second rate losers up there on the ramp…..

…..I know it’s hard to comprehend…..because this is dubbya see dubbya after all, and you don’t see much of it…..but what you see in this ring right here, right now, is greatness…..forget your Horsemen, your Titans…..your Dangerous Alliances ain’t got nothing on this…..you’ve all been going around calling us this and calling us that…..

…..well from this day forward, you can just call me champ…..

…..and since you like to call us degenerates so much…..

…..you can go ahead and call us D-Generation X…..”

…..Shawn tells the wrestlers on the ramp to take a good hard look at the title around his waist…..because this is as close as any of them are going to come to it again…..

”…..because Savage can’t beat me…..Warrior can’t even lace up my boots…..Luger, Vader, The Giant…..even Mr. WCW there, the last great hope Sting…..we all just saw that he sure as hell can’t beat me…..in fact, there ain’t a man standing on that ramp that can do what I do in this ring…..

…..and if you ain’t down with that…..I got two words for ya…..

…..before some familiar music starts playing…..

…..and it’s some music we haven’t heard for some time…..

…..which causes the crowd to go bananas…..

…..and get even louder when the wrestlers on the entrance way part…..

…..as Hulk Hogan comes walking out from the back, coming to a halt and standing hands on hips at the top of the aisle…..

…..and this ain’t Hollywood…..this is the American Made, Red and Yellow wearing Hulkster that we haven’t seen in over two years now…..

…..Shawn looks pissed…..as does JPL…..it’s hard to say how Big Ray-Ray looks, with those aviators over his eyes…..Copeland and Cage however just looked shocked…..they never did manage to shake those little Hulkamanic feelings they had growing up after all (they’ve been wearing yellow for some time now, and it’s no coincidence)…..

…..Michaels unhooks the title from around his waist, laying it on the mat in front of him, as he mockingly hits some of Hogans signature poses, before acting like he just threw out his back and then throwing Crotch Chops Hogans way, telling him to “Suck It old man”…..Hogan on the other hand just smiles, staring around at the crowd with his hands on his yellow clad hips, before tearing off that shirt and throwing it on the ground in front of him…..

…..and with that scene, the show comes to a close…..Shawn Michaels and the rest of D-Generation X staring up at the aisle…..the crowd cheering a returning Hulk Hogan…..Randy Savage, Sting and The Warrior looking warily at the new arrival…..

…..where do we go from here?…..

…..I would say we’ll see you tomorrow night for Nitro and all the fall out from this evening…..

…..but our WCW journey has come to an end…..

…..goodnight fans…..
