Week 3, January 1998

Milwaukee County Stadium (Milwaukee, Wisconsin)

ATT: 44,000

PPV BUY: 1.51

Announce Team: Jim Ross, Jerry “The King” Lawler & Ravishing Rick Rude

It’s time for the first Pay Per View event of the year, and while this may be the first stop on the WWF’s Pay Per View Calendar, it’s also the first stop on the road to Wrestlemania for one man…..the one man that outlasts twenty nine others tonight…..because it’s time to rumble…..it’s time…..for the Royal Rumble…..

…..we open up at the announce table, where Jim Ross (along with Jerry Lawler and Rick Rude) welcomes us to an event not only of epic proportions, but an event which may have grave consequence for either The Undertaker or Brian Pillman, because they will be clashing tonight for the WWF Championship…..in a Casket Match…..

…..the three men all give their predictions, with Jim Ross predicting that The Undertaker will leave his signature match tonight with his championship in tact, while Rude and Lawler both believe that after Brian Pillman has already taken everything else that the Deadman holds dear, tonight will be the night that he takes away the WWF Title as well…..

…..from there they move onto the Royal Rumble match itself, a match which Jim Ross is convinced will be won by the one man Vince McMahon DOESN’T want to win…..Stone Cold Steve Austin…..Rude and Lawler may have agreed on who will win the WWF Title Match, but they don’t agree on this one……they DO however both pick former WWF Champions as their winners, with Lawler picking a man who has done practically everything in the WWF (INCLUDING win a Royal Rumble match) last years winner, Big Poppa Pump Scott Steiner, while Rude informs us the Ahmed Johnson is the man to watch tonight, predicting that the Walking Armageddon will be walking all the way to the Wrestlemania Main Event after tonight…..

…..and as if those two huge matches alone weren’t enough, we will also see all other titles defended this evening, with Dean Malenko challenging Jeff Jarrett for the European Championship, Ric Flair and Arn Anderson looking to bring the Tag Team Titles back to Vince McMahon from Harlem Heat…..but first and foremost we have a WWF Intercontinental Championship to decide…..and that match is happening…..next…..

WWF Intercontinental Championship Match

Scott Hall (c) vs. Chris Jericho (w/Test)


…..a match which sees Chris Jericho getting a second bite at the Intercontinental cherry, after an unsuccessful bid at dethroning Scott Hall at our December In Your House offering, Dead Man Walking…..Hall and Jericho opened up that event, with a match that was so good they were given the opening spot again here…..not because they didn’t deserve to be higher up the card, but because they were presented with the opportunity to once again set the standard for the evening…..

…..now, Chris Jericho will tell you that Scott Hall only won that match by cheating…..that if Scott Hall hadn’t stooped so low as to knock him out with a set of brass knuckles, that Chris Jericho would be DEFENDING the Intercontinental Championship tonight instead of challenging for it…..and that MIGHT be true…..because yes, Scott Hall DID use a set of brass knucks in the culmination of their last match, but it was a set of brass knuckles that Chris Jericho himself introduced into the match…..

…..Scott Hall just used them first…..

…..he IS the Bad Guy after all…..

…..but then Scott Hall at least has the decency to come out alone tonight, where as Chris Jericho has The Bodyguard Test right there at ringside…..and you KNOW that Jericho has no problem in bending the rules to get ahead…..

…..as I said earlier, the last match these two men competed in was excellent…..this one was just as good…..Chris Jericho is still young, but he’s already one of the best workers in the company (no real surprise considering the company he keeps in the Hart Foundation) while Scott Hall has always been able to put on a good match, and that includes the year and a half he was working through injury…..he’s fully fit now, and even that pesky ‘time decline’ isn’t really slowing him down too much…..

…..he starts off strong here as the two men feel each other out…..Jericho’s cocky side comes out, as he taunts the “Old Guy” (as he calls him) catching Hall with an arm drag before doing a little jig…..not to be outdone, Hall quickly catches an arm drag of his own, holding onto the limb and showing his own cocky side as he paintbrushes Jericho around the back of his head…..it clearly pays to embarrass Jericho too, because after having a monumental hissy fit that Hall was so dismissive of him, he charges straight into a barrage of offence from the Intercontinental Champion that finally sees him bail to the outside to regroup…..

…..Hall doesn’t give him that opportunity however, following his opponent to the floor where he runs him over with a clothesline, before rolling him back into the ring…..

…..Jericho has a lot of ring awareness for a young age though, and he desperately clutches at the official as Hall attempts to re-enter the ring, drawing the referee’s attention away from the sight of Test nailing Hall in the back of the head with a clubbing blow, before driving the FRONT of his head into the steel ring post and rolling him back into the ring…..

…..now it’s all Jericho…..I told you he wasn’t shy about bending the rules…..and he goes to work on the lower back of the Intercontinental Champion, clearly looking to utilise that Liontamer submission manoeuvre…..a strategy that Jerry Lawler approves of…..

“…..because if he locks that Liontamer on, Scott Hall AND his title reign is going to be in serious trouble JR…..”

“…..serious trouble or not, that remains to be seen, but you don’t become a five time Intercontinental Champion by giving up easily…..I think it’s going to take a lot more than that to put the Bad Guy down tonight…..”

…..Rick Rude doesn’t agree with that at all, telling Ross that being a five time Intercontinental Champion just means that Scott Hall lost the title four times already…..

“…..well be that as it may, I just don’t think Scott Hall is going to submit that easily…..in fact, I don’t think I’ve EVER seen Scott Hall quit…..”

“…..well I have Jim Ross…..and it was right here at the Royal Rumble, in 1993 when Bret Hart made him submit to the Sharpshooter…..you can call him the Bad Guy, but I say he has a history of submitting to Canadians at this event…..”

…..Rick Rude may well have a point, but so far Scott Hall doesn’t show any signs of quitting to THIS Canadian, no matter what Chris Jericho does…..in fact, after an extended period on defence, Hall actually manages to get back into this one, catching a Jericho crossbody attempt and launching him across the ring with a fall away slam…..

…..from there it’s all Scott Hall as he batters Chris Jericho around the ring…..Test hops up onto the apron, but he gets dropped with a running forearm that sends him back down to the floor…..Jericho tries to use the brief distraction to blindside his opponent, but Hall sees him coming and catches him by the neck, driving him to the mat with a chokeslam/rock bottom variation…..

…..and then he signals for the Razors Edge…..

…..he gets Jericho into position and hoists the man up onto his shoulders…..unfortunately, as he went up Jericho’s foot connects with the skull of the official, and we suddenly find ourselves without a referee…..

…..Test quickly climbs into the ring behind Hall and yanks on Jericho’s leg, pulling him down from the Razors Edge, and the two Hart Foundation members begin to double team the Intercontinental Champion……

…..at least that is until Sean Waltman comes rushing out from the back to even up the odds…..he takes out Test with a running spinning heel kick that knocks the bodyguard to the outside, where he proceeds to brawl with the big man all the way to the back…..

…..that leaves Hall and Jericho all alone…..and the Bad Guy is in a Bad Mood…..

…..he picks up right where he left off before Test decided to stick his nose in, battering Jericho from pillar to post, before sending him high in the air with a back body drop…..

…..then he signals for the Razors Edge…..

…..again…..

…..he drags Jericho off the mat and gets him in position, before outstretching his arms in that now signature pose…..looks like Jericho is going for the ride after all…..

…..or not…..

…..because before he can lift the man, Scott Steiner slides into the ring behind him, lead pipe in hand…..and he cracks Hall around the back of the head with the bar…..watches as the Intercontinental Champion crumples to the mat…..flips him off…..and leaves…..

…..never one to look a gift horse in the mouth, Chris Jericho grabs the legs and twists Hall over into the Liontamer…..

…..the official miraculously chooses now to come back to his senses…..

…..he checks on Hall (who has been knocked completely unconscious by that lead pipe) and then calls for the bell…..this one is over…..we have a brand new WWF Intercontinental Champion…..and his name is Chris Jericho…..

Winner: …..and New WWF Intercontinental Champion…..Chris Jericho
Rating: A

…..we leave Chris Jericho celebrating in typically over the top fashion in the ring (currently he’s raising both the Intercontinental Championship AND the retired Light Heavyweight Championship in the air) and go backstage, where Michael Cole is rushing after the man that just cost Scott Hall his title, Scott Steiner…..

“…..WHY THE HELL ARE YOU FOLLOWING ME DOWN THE HALL, SNIFFING AT MY ASS LIKE A DOG IN HEAT?…..WHAT DO YOU WANT?…..YOU WANNA HUMP MY LEG YOU SICK FREAK?…..”

“…..well no, I….I….I…..”

“…..YEAH, YOU’RE THINKING ABOUT IT AIN’T YOU…..THE GOD DAMN WWF AND ITS GOD DAMN PERVERT ANNOUNCERS…..WHAT DO YOU WANT?…..”

…..Cole finally spits his question out, asking Scott Steiner why (when he SHOULD be preparing for the Royal Rumble match later tonight) why is he coming out in matches that don’t concern him, costing Scott Hall the Intercontinental Championship…..has he not done enough already where the Wolfpac are concerned?…..

“…..ENOUGH?…..ENOUGH???…..ENOUGH AIN’T A WORD IN SCOTT STEINER’S VOCALB…..VOLCLAB…..”

“…..vocabulary?….”

“…..SHUT UP!!!…..ENOUGH AIN’T A WORD THAT SCOTT STEINER SAYS…..LOOK AT ME MICHAEL COLE…..I’M HUGE…..DO I LOOK LIKE A MAN THAT EVER HAS ENOUGH OF ANYTHING?…..WHETHER ITS WORKING UP A SWEAT IN THE GYM, OR WORKING UP A SWEAT BETWEEN SABLE’S THIGHS, THERE AIN’T NO SUCH THING AS ENOUGH WHERE BIG POPPA PUMP IS CONCERNED…..SCOTT HALL, SEAN WALTMAN, THEY BEEN RUNNING THEIR MOUTHS ABOUT THE GENETIC FREAK FOR WEEKS NOW…..WELL SCOTT HALL IS LUCKY THAT ALL I COST HIM WAS THE INTERCONTINENTAL TITLE, INSTEAD OF COSTING HIM HIS CAREER LIKE I DID KEVIN NASH…..”

…..Scotty says that he’s got all the time in the world when it comes to kicking Wolfpac ass, as far as the Royal Rumble goes, he won it last year, he’ll win it again this year…..who’s going to stop him? Ahmed Johnson? (Scotty says that he owes HIM an ass-kicking from Summerslam) Owen Hart? (Midget) Sean Waltman? (Makes Owen Hart look huge)…….

“…..STONE COLD STEVE AUSTIN? DON’T MAKE ME LAUGH…..THE TOUGHEST S.O.B IN THE WWF?…..SCOTT STEINER IS THE TOUGHEST S.O.B IN THE WORLD…..SCOTT STEINER IS THE TOUGHEST S.O.B IN ALL THE WORLDS…..YOU WANNA SEND A LITTLE GREEN MAN DOWN FROM PLANET 51, THIRD LEFT AFTER THE SUN?…..SCOTT STEINER WILL SEND HIM BACK TO HIS FLYING SAUCER WITH HIS LITTLE GREEN HEAD UP HIS LITTLE GREEN ASS…..

…..AIN’T A MAN ALIVE THAT IS TOUGH ENOUGH TO THROW SCOTT STEINER OVER THAT TOP ROPE MICHAEL COLE…..AND THE ROYAL RUMBLE 1998, IT’S GONNA BE LIKE A….A…..ONE OF THEM FRENCH THINGS COLE, WHEN YOU SEEN SOMETHING BEFORE…..”

“…..a déjà vu?…..”

“…..YEAH THAT’S IT…..A DAY JAR VIEW…..BECAUSE JUST LIKE LAST YEAR, YOU’RE LOOKING AT YOUR WINNER OF THE ROYAL RUMBLE MATCH RIGHT HERE…..AND CANT NO ONE STOP ME…..NOW GET OUTTA HERE COLE, BEFORE I KICK YOUR ASS TOO…..”

…..Michael Cole doesn’t need telling twice, and he quickly backs away as Scotty Storms off down the hall, before signing off to Dok Hendrix, who’s standing by with Jake “The Snake” Roberts…..

Rating: B+

…..who’s looking a little more disheveled than usual…..he’s not quite so clean shaven, he’s smiling but it’s not a smile that reaches his eyes, clearly this is a man with a lot on his mind…..

…..Dok says that tonight, after crucifixion’s, maiming’s, blasphemy, trials, tribulations and a journey through Raven’s Divine Comedy…..it all comes down to this…..FINALLY, Jake Roberts will get his hands on the self proclaimed Deity of the Damned…..but Dok has just one question…..what did it cost?…..

…..Jake smiles again at that…..briefly looking down at his hands, before looking up to the heavens above…..

“…..what did it cost…..now isn’t that the question…..but you see Dok Hendrix, the question shouldn’t be what was the cost…..rather, what is the reward?…..yes, my hands are a little bit dirtier than I would have liked, but they’ve been a whole heck of a lot dirtier in the past, for a LOT less noble causes…..”

…..Jake says that you can forget about Raven’s Divine Comedy…..THIS…..was a Divine Cause…..he says that EVERYTHING that happened to this point, was because Jake was trying to SAVE…..save the Flock…..save the soul of Raven (“and yes, I’ll admit it…. I hoped along the way that I could save myself as well”) to atone for just a fraction of the depravities that Jake had committed would be reward enough…..

“….see I know I was an evil man Dok…..and I accept my part in the atrocities Raven has committed…..my part, because as Raven says, he’s MY creation…..well tonight, the creator becomes the destructor…..because I can finally see clearly…..I can finally see that SOME men, just can’t be saved…..SOME men, don’t DESERVE to be saved…..and some men…..just need to be struck down by the righteous hand of God…..

…..for my God…..my Priest…..and my Sanity…..what I do tonight, I do in your name…..I created him then, as he has created me now…..mourn not for the man I was, because that man, is dead…..but Jake Roberts…..he will be reborn tonight…..trust me…..”

…..trust me indeed…..now let’s go down to ringside, because we are about to enter the final circle of Raven’s Personal Hell…..Treachery…..

Rating: B+

Nine Circles of Hell Match: Treachery

Jake Roberts vs. Raven

So for Jake “The Snake” Roberts, it all comes down to this…..after all of the words, all of the mind games…..after the assaults…..sneak attacks…..after sinking to depths lower than most anyone’s definition of good taste…..after priests were crucified…..after priests were half blinded…..after faith’s were tested and retribution’s promised…..after all that, finally we reach the lowest circle of hell…..the ninth circle…..treachery…..

…..Raven makes his way out first, and as he’s oft to do, he sits himself down in the corner of the ring, microphone in hand…..he tells the official (and the crowd) that since this is Raven’s Game, it’s Raven’s Rules…..and just as the puppet master has made Jake Roberts dance to the dark tune of the Deity of the Damned, again Raven is pulling the strings…..because the Rules to this match are simple…..there ARE no Rules…..so send out the Snake, because it’s time for the teacher to learn a lesson of his own…..

…..Jake does indeed come out…..and in a mirror image, the mentor musters enormous self control to walk past the student, making his way to the opposite corner and sitting down himself…..and as these two Men of the Macabre stare each other down, Jake tells the official (without ever taking his eyes off Raven) that he accepts the terms…..so ring the bell…..

…..the referee acquiesce’s to the request, and as the bell rings, both Raven and Roberts simultaneously reach up and take hold of the top rope, hoisting themselves to a standing position…..before charging straight towards each other…..

…..the announcers confirm that Jake Roberts has agreed to the terms and the referee has made it official…..here, in the ninth circle of hell…..there are NO rules…..and even if the commentary DIDN’T confirm it, the fact would be pretty clear within the opening minute, because the two men are punching, kicking, biting and clawing at each other, and the referee is just standing back and watching the carnage unfold…..

…..Jake has months and months of pent up frustration here, and he’s taking full advantage of the lack of rules to completely dominate his opponent from the get go…..these two men last men in an official capacity back at October’s In Your House event (aptly titled Badd Blood) where Raven was victorious via disqualification after (despite his priests plea’s to the contrary) Jake Roberts used a steel chair to punish his former student…..there’s no danger of that tonight…..Jake clotheslines Raven all the way over the top rope and down to the floor outside, where he follows him out, picks up a chair, nails his opponent……and the official doesn’t even blink an eye…..

…..Jake crashes the steel chair down across the back of the Deity of the Damned nine times (which Jim Ross decides is symbolic…..one strike for each circle of hell Jake has been forced to endure) before he drags Raven to his feet and Irish Whips him so hard into the guard rails that Raven lands almost upside down on his head…..

…..and Raven is smiling about it…..a sight that Jerry Lawler can’t believe…..

“…..he’s smiling JR, Jake Roberts just destroyed his back with solid steel and Raven is enjoying it…..”

“…..he’s one sick puppy King, there’s no question about that…..it takes a twisted soul to find pleasure in his own suffering…..”

“…..he’s not smiling about that Jim Ross…..and he’s not enjoying the pain Jerry Lawler…..Raven is smiling, because he knows that these are not the actions of a righteous man…..this is not ‘love thy neighbour’ and there’s no ‘turn the other cheek’ here…..Jake Roberts is proving that Raven was right all along…..that this so-called Man of God is nothing but a sick and twisted hypocrite……”

…..whether Jake is a hypocrite remains to be seen, but he’s certainly enjoying the beating that he is delivering here…..so much so that he decides to pull up the protective mats on the floor…..he is going to DDT Raven right out there on the concrete……or rather he WOULD have DDT’d Raven right out there on the concrete, had Raven not backdropped out of it, meaning it was Jake Roberts spine that connected with the cold, hard floor…..

…..Jim Ross says that since both of these men utilise the DDT variation, so it goes without saying that both men would know how to counter the hold…..and that counter onto the concrete certainly put a screeching halt to the offence of the Snake…..

…..Raven is clearly feeling the effects of the early (vicious) onslaught from Jake, but now that he is back on the offence he slows down the match and takes control with a more methodical approach…..not a less violent approach (oh no, no, no, no, no) just a slower paced one which guarantee’s maximum pain to Jake, with minimum suffering to himself…..highlighted by the fact that he whips Jake hard into the ring steps, before choking the life out of him with a camera chord…..

…..the two men climb back into the ring (well, Raven climbs back into the ring, Jake gets tossed back in) and it doesn’t get any easier for Roberts…..Raven sets up a chair in the middle of the mat and whips his opponent into the ropes, catching him with a drop toe hold on the return that sees Jakes skull crash into the steel surface…..

…..and when the veteran rolls onto his back, clutching at his face, we can see that he has been busted wide open…..

…..Raven looks delighted as he watches Jakes life blood pour out of him, and he stands over him shouting at Roberts to look at him…..“look into the eyes of your creation and know…..know that when you THOUGHT you were creating a monster, you actually created a Deity of your own…..the Deity of the Damned…..denounce your false idol…..come with me…..for I can set you free…..”

…..Jake struggles to his feet, selling expertly that the blood streaming down his face is impairing his vision…..reaching out for a cackling Raven, who stands there (arms outstretched, crucifix style) repeatedly telling Jake to come with him…..

…..finally Jakes blind hands find his tormentors head, and the ‘creator’ cups his ‘creations’ face for a moment, almost as a proud parent would to his child…..

…..then he snatches him by the head and snaps back with that patented DDT…..

…..not just any DDT either, because Ravens skull connects with that discarded steel chair, and as Jim Ross rightly points out, all Jake Roberts has to do here is make the cover and this one is over…..

…..unfortunately, blood loss and exhaustion means Jake CAN’T make the cover…..not for a good thirty seconds anyway, as he lays there spent beside his opponent…..only then does he finally muster the strength to rolls Raven over…..ring position is everything though, because Raven is already close to the ropes, and as Jake rolls him to make the cover Raven is able to roll right under the bottom rope and crash down to the floor outside, and that’s when the camera picks up the fact that not only is Raven in trouble, he TOO is now busted open, courtesy of that DDT to the steel chair…..

…..the two men take a moment to catch their breath, but as Jake finally wipes the blood from his eyes to clear his vision, the sight of a busted open Raven rejuvenate’s him, and he drops right out onto the outside to continue his assault…..and there is nothing Divine in these actions, this is as ungodly an assault as you’re likely to see as Jake punishes Raven to within an inch of his life, assaulting him with anything and everything that he can place his hands on, before finally rolling him back into the ring and following him in…..with an electrical cable in his hands…..

…..Jim Ross says that for all intent and purposes, this one is over…..all Jake has to do is make the cover…..Jerry Lawler however states that Jake isn’t looking just to win…..he’s looking for the complete destruction of Raven and it doesn’t look as though he is finished yet, a fact that Rick Rude tells us may well come back to bite the Snake…..

“…..if you’ve got a man beat you make the cover and you get the win…..this business is about winning and losing Jim Ross, and if you’re not trying to win then you’re eventually going to lose, Jake Roberts should know better…..”

…..Jake stands over the bloodied Raven, who pulls himself up to a kneeling position…..he sees Jake stood there, cable in hand and he just starts laughing…..blood is pouring down his face and this lunatic is delighted, telling Jake to do it…..do what he has to do…..set himself free…..

…..and God be Damned if that isn’t exactly what Jake does…..

…..he takes that electrical cable and wraps it right around the throat of Raven, before pulling back to completely restrict the airway…..and as Raven’s eyes bulge out of his head, even Jim Ross says that Jake is taking this too far…..

“…..rules or no rules there are certain lines that you just don’t cross…..this isn’t about ending the match, this is about ending Raven….permanently…..and by god if that isn’t exactly what’s going to happen…..”

“…..yeah, but who the hell is that Jim Ross?…..”

…..that?…..

…..oh…..’that’ would be the sight of Jake Roberts priest, rushing down the aisle…..

…..he climbs into the ring (eyepatch and all) and comes to a halt in front of Jake, pleading with him to stop…..telling him to end this…..it’s over…..don’t do something that he can’t redeem…..

…..Jake isn’t listening though…..the red mist has descended and Jake is intent on finishing what he started, telling his priest that he’s doing this for HIM…..for the humiliation he suffered…..for the crucifixion…..for the eye…..he’s doing God’s work and he’s not going to stop until he’s finished…..

…..the priest pleads with Jake, telling him not to do it…..release Raven and release HIMSELF…..don’t do this, don’t damn his soul…..“not in my name”…..

…..it takes a while, but finally Jake sees the light, and he lets go of the cable, releasing (a practically unconscious) Raven, who drops to the mat…..Jake’s face tells us that he realises what he did…..that he took it too far…..that he almost crossed a line that there would be NO redemption from, and he shamefully begs his priest for forgiveness…..for absolution of his sins…..he stands there a broken man, pleading for peace…..

…..so the Priest breaks him some more…..by kicking him right in the balls…..

…..Jake doubles over in agony from the shot…..but doubled over isn’t a good position to be in when you’re in the ring with a man that utilises the DDT…..and a staggering Raven hooks hold of Jakes head and drives him straight into the mat with the Evenflow, before rolling him over…..making the cover…..and as the official counts to three, this one is mercifully over…..

Winner: Raven
Rating: B+

…..Raven is utterly spent…..battered, bloodied…..but as this Priest helps him back to his feet, he is nowhere near as broken as Jake Roberts…..and not just physically, because as Roberts rolls over and looks up at the sight of his own priest dropping to his knees in worship of the Deity of the Damned, you see Jakes mind and spirit break right there and then…..

…..Raven takes a microphone and exhaustedly tells Jake to forget seeing the light…..

“…..embrace the dark…..embrace the darkness that’s ALWAYS been inside you…..forget the folly of the last six months…..forget the false idols…..and accept your one TRUE Deity into your life…..come to me…..and I will set you free…..”

…..Jake looks up at his Priest with disbelief, with a look of utter betrayal on his face…..a look which Raven relishes, telling Jake that even his ‘Man of God’ has seen the truth…..with eyes wide open this man was blinded by the lies of the light…..and ironically, only now that Raven has taken half of his sight can this Priest finally see clearly…..before repeating again…..

“…..come to me…..and I will set you free…..”

…..the Priest urges Jake to join him, telling him that he’s trodden the wrong path for too long now…..he tells Jake that he wanted a priest…..that he wanted absolution…..that he wanted forgiveness…..he can have ALL of that…..he can wage the divine war…..Raven’s divine war…..he can still be Jake’s Priest…..all he has to do, is let the darkness in…..because (as Raven reminds us all once again)…..

“…..come to me…..and I will set you free…..”

…..Raven outstretches his arms crucifix style……the Priest mirrors this from his kneeling position beside Raven…..and BOTH men stare down at the Snake…..

…..the crowd might be urging Jake not to do it, but there is a light that’s gone off in Jakes eyes…..broken…..mind, body and spirit, Jake Roberts slowly pulls himself up to a kneeling position from the mat, stares Raven right in the eye…..

…..and then bows his head…..

…..and outstretches his arms…..

…..and when he raises his head once more, he has a twisted smile on his face…..a smile that we haven’t seen since the early 90’s……the smile of a man that slapped Miss Elizabeth…..the smile of a man that took a King Cobra and forced it to bite Randy Savage…..the smile of a man that tortured the Ultimate Warrior in a room full of Snakes…..that made the Ultimate Warrior go to an undisclosed cemetery and dig up a human skull…..the smile of a man that went to the Undertakers own Funeral Parlour segment, slammed his hand stuck inside a casket and proceeded to assault both The Deadman AND his manager Paul Bearer…..

…..the smile of man…..who is free…..

…..Jim Ross says that he doesn’t know what this means for the future of Jake Roberts, but he knows one thing for sure…..and that’s the fact, that if Jake Roberts is actually going to follow the darkness…..if Jake Roberts is actually going to follow the gospel according to Raven…..then NOBODY is safe…..

…..trust me…..

Rating: A*

…..as we leave that sickening scene unfolding in the ring, we head to the back…..where Michael Cole is standing by with the WWF World Tag Team Champions, Harlem Heat…..

…..Cole says that Booker T and Stevie Ray are unquestionably one of the best Tag Teams in the history of this great sport, their opponents tonight however, are Arn Anderson, ALSO one of the greatest Tag Team wrestlers in the history of this sport, and his partner (and best friend) who is arguably THE greatest wrestler in the history of this sport, The Nature Boy Ric Flair…..

…..not to mention the fact that Stevie Ray only has one working leg…..

…..Booker tells Cole that Harlem Heat have been fighting against the odds their whole life, and they’ve been winning their whole lives…..the reason Stevie Ray only has one good leg at the minute is because Flair and Anderson know that they can’t beat Harlem Heat in a straight up match…..yeah Anderson has had some success as a Tag Team wrestler, and Ric Flair may well be the greatest SINGLES wrestler to ever lace up his boots, but Harlem Heat are THE best Tag Team in professional wrestling…..

“…..we been winning fights together since kindergarten…..we been winning titles since we decided to beat people up for a living…..and THATS why Flair and Anderson knew they had to take one of us out…..”

“…..is it?…..”

…..apparently Stevie Ray doesn’t agree…..

…..Stevie looks down at his knee which is strapped up to twice it’s normal size, before again asking Booker if that’s really why he’s standing here right now on a knee held together by crazy glue and bandages? Or is he standing here now half crippled because Booker wouldn’t entertain the Nation of Domination’s offer?…..

“…..you think those two fruit booty backwater boys woulda done this if there was someone there watching our backs? Yeah we grew up on the streets, and yeah we put a lotta chumps in the hospital, but everyone knows you don’t survive for long on 110 street without your crew there watching your back…..”

…..Stevie tells Booker that it was stupid not to at least hear Ahmed Johnson out…..he’d better hope that stupidity doesn’t cost them the Tag Team Titles…..

“…..you can wrap this crap up here, I’m gonna go down to the ring…..takes me a bit longer than it used to to get around if you know what I mean…..”

…..with that, a clearly frustrated Stevie Ray slowly limps out of shot (presumably to make his way to the ring) leaving Booker there to finish the interview off……not that there’s much left to say…..

…..he tells Cole that no matter what his brother thinks, Harlem Heat don’t need no cheap tricks or back up, or anything else…..they’re gonna go out there tonight, and do what they do best…..beat punks up……you dig that sucka?

…..with that, Booker heads off in the same direction as his brother, while Michael Cole sends us down to ringside…..

…..or at least he tries too…..

…..he does however get rather rudely interrupted first, by a very loud, very excited and very rambunctious Chris Jericho (followed closely by a very serious looking Test) who comes bounding into the shot with both the Intercontinental and Light Heavyweight Titles around his waist and midsection…..

…..Jericho lets out a big whoop, a “Yeah Baby!” and does a little jig, before spraying Michael Cole with some celebratory champagne and walking off again…..

…..Cole wipes the liquid from his face, and mutters that the new Intercontinental Champion is clearly very excited by his win, as he NOW sends us back down to ringside…..

Rating: B+

…..he springboards into the ring and drives the mammoth frame of Nelson right into Kevin Nash with a dropkick to the upper back of Big Nels’…..Nash and Nelson clash heads, but Kidman doesn’t give the biggest man in the match time to recover, as he hits Nelson with a trio of dropkick’s that leave the man rocking, followed by a fourth that actually drives the man backwards and leaves him propped up against the ropes…..so Kidman grabs one leg, and lifts…..

…..and doesn’t budge Nelson even an inch…..

…..Bulldog and Test see Nelson in a vulnerable position and decide to put their personal issues to one side to try an eliminate the largest man in the ring, and they each join Kidman in attempting to pivot Nelson over the top rope…..and when even THAT doesn’t work, Kevin Nash joins in…..and NOW we’re starting to see some movement…..

…..these Royal Rumbles clearly make for some strange bedfellows…..Big Poppa Pump usually loves some strange bedfellows (if ya know what I mean) but he ain’t down for this…..and instead of joining in on the action, he punches Kevin Nash square in the face, and as Big Sexy and Big Poppa resume their battle, the moment is lost as far as eliminating Nelson is concerned…..

…..and those two minutes are up…..

Entrant #21: Wrath
Joining: Scott Steiner (18:00) Kevin Nash (10:00) Test (8:00) The British Bulldog (6:00) Nelson (4:00) Billy Kidman (2:00)

…..we saw Crush out here earlier this evening (before being eliminated by Rey Mysterio of all people) and now it’s the turn of Wrath, who enters the fray at number twenty one…..

…..he decides to focus his attack on the British Bulldog and Kidman, joining forces with Test to fight the mismatched (in size at least) pairing…..Nelson is taking his time to recuperate in the corner of the ring (he’s been out there four minutes after all) while Steiner and Nash continue to try and kill each other over the other side of the ring…..

…..all in all it was a pretty uneventful two minutes…..

Entrant #22: Billy Gunn
Joining: Scott Steiner (20:00) Kevin Nash (12:00) Test (10:00) The British Bulldog (8:00) Nelson (6:00) Billy Kidman (4:00) Wrath (2:00)

…..twenty two…..it’s Billy Gunn…..

…..the Bad Ass comes running down the aisle at 100mph and slides under the bottom rope and into the ring…..Gunn is another one who thought he would be competing for the WWF Tag Team Titles tonight…..obviously Vince McMahon had other ideas, so here he is…..

…..he goes straight over to where Bulldog, Kidman, Wrath and Test are brawling (its best to avoid Nash and Steiner at the moment, they are currently attempting to murder one another…..seriously…..they each have their hands wrapped around the neck of their opponent) and he joins Wrath in double teaming the Bulldog, leaving Kidman to fend for himself against Test…..

…..he’s doing a pretty good job too, so much so that he’s able to take the Hart Foundation man down with a corner spinning bulldog, before making the cardinal error of scaling the ropes in a Royal Rumble match…..

…..he goes up top (possibly looking to deliver that Shooting Star Press) but with so many bodies in the ring he gets unbalanced from Bulldog fighting off an elimination attempt, and he crotches himself right there on the top turnbuckle…..

…..then Test gets back to his feet, and kicks him right in the face with a Big Boot, sending him tumbling down to the floor…..

…..bye bye billy…..

….:::::!!!GLASS SHATTERS!!!:::::…..

Entrant #23: Steve Austin
Joining: Scott Steiner (22:00) Kevin Nash (14:00) Test (12:00) The British Bulldog (10:00) Nelson (8:00) Wrath (4:00) Billy Gunn (2:00)
Eliminated: Billy Kidman (5:44) (by Test)

…..you might wanna hold onto your hats…..because as the glass shatters to signal the arrival of number twenty three, the roof blows off this sumbich, as Stone Cold Steve Austin parts the curtains…..

…..he comes marching down the aisle with purpose, spitting threats and venom at the people in the ring before he even arrives…..

…..he might wanna pay attention to what’s happening behind him though…..because as he marches down the aisle he suddenly gets jumped by Vince McMahon’s Corporate Champions, new Tag Team Champions Ric Flair and Arn Anderson, not to mention the brand new World Wrestling Federation Champion himself, Brian Pillman…..

…..they hammer Austin down to the ground, and as Pillman cackles on, Anderson takes that ever present tyre iron and cracks it across the knee of the Rattlesnake…..and Jim Ross is frankly appalled by these actions…..

“…..by god, Vince McMahon has made it crystal clear that he does NOT want Steve Austin as the World Wrestling Champion, but this just reeks of desperation, no matter which way you look at it Rick Rude…..to not even give the Rattlesnake a fair shot in this match, that’s a new low even for McMahon…..”

“…..that’s MR. McMahon to you Jim Ross……Mr. McMahon who writes your cheques by the way, something that you might want to remember…..and Mr. McMahon told Steve Austin that he had no chance in hell at winning tonight, and even LESS chance at headlining Wrestlemania……it looks to me like Mr. McMahon is a man of his word…..”

…..it certainly does…..because as Pillman drops to his haunches beside Austin (waving the WWF Title in front of his face) he wishes his former partner good luck in even making it to the ring, before the three men walk off laughing, leaving Stone Cold laying in the aisle…..

Entrant #24: Matt Hardy
Joining: Scott Steiner (24:00) Kevin Nash (16:00) Test (14:00) The British Bulldog (12:00) Nelson (10:00) Wrath (6:00) Billy Gunn (4:00) Steve Austin (2:00)

…..with the action that occurred on the outside a few moments ago, the action INSIDE the ring slowed down a little bit…..it picks back up now as the attention switches back to the Royal Rumble match itself…..

…..the British Bulldog is probably pleased to see number twenty four, Matt Hardy enter the mix, because at the moment, Wrath, Test and Billy Gunn are ALL beating on the burly brit…..unfortunately for Wrath, he’s SO focused on Davey Boy that he doesn’t even see Hardy come up behind him…..not until he gets tipped right over the top rope that is…..

…..as for Steiner and Nash, they still seem intent on taking one another out permanently…..and most other competitors have wisely given them a wide berth…..

…..since Nelson IS a wide berth, he obviously believes that he will have no problems getting between them…..

…..he was wrong…..

…..because as much as they want to kill each other, Steiner and Nash take exception to someone trying to STOP them from killing each other…..and they turn around and nail Nelson with a simultaneous shot to the mouth that sends Nelson back into the ropes…..

…..Steiner and Nash grab a leg each and they lift…..and they get Nelson off the mat…..

…..they only get him so far though, and it looks as though they are going to have to drop him back down…..at least it DID look like that, until Stone Cold Steve Austin limps his way onto the apron, yanking back on the head of Nelson…..

…..and finally (between the three of them) Nelson is eliminated…..

Entrant #25: Kama
Joining: Scott Steiner (26:00) Kevin Nash (18:00) Test (16:00) The British Bulldog (14:00) Billy Gunn (6:00) Steve Austin (4:00) Matt Hardy (2:00)
Eliminated: Wrath (6:22) (by Matt Hardy) Nelson (11:39) (by Scott Steiner, Kevin Nash & Steve Austin)

…..we almost get two entrants for the price of one here…..because as number twenty five (Kama) makes his way down the aisle, Stone Cold Steve Austin finally enters the ring, coming face to face with Big Poppa Pump Scott Steiner…..

…..these two men have had their differences in the past, that’s for sure, and there’s a definite buzz in the air as the two men stare each other down…..in fact the crowd positively erupts at the possibility of seeing these two men come to blows…..

…..Scotty isn’t a man that’s easily intimidated though, and he gives Austin a cocky smirk, before throwing up a double bicep pose…..

…..so Austin throws up a pair of middle fingers…..

…..as Kevin Nash grabs Steiner from behind and throws HIM up…..over the top rope and down to the floor…..and after lasting close to thirty minutes (and being involved in nine of the night’s eliminations) Big Poppa Pump is out of here…..

…..meanwhile, Test decides to refocus his attention onto Big Sexy, nailing him in the back with a clothesline…..Billy Gunn goes at it with Matt Hardy (while the Bulldog takes a breather in the corner) and Kama goes after the knee of Stone Cold…..

Entrant #26: Barry Windham
Joining: Kevin Nash (20:00) Test (18:00) The British Bulldog (16:00) Billy Gunn (8:00) Steve Austin (6:00) Matt Hardy (4:00) Kama (2:00)
Eliminated: Scott Steiner (26:55) (by Kevin Nash)

…..entrant number twenty six, and its Big, Bad Barry Windham…..someone who (in all honesty) I have absolutely nothing for right now…..I’m really just watching his contract slowly expire…..

…..so why even enter him into the Rumble? Oh that’s easy…..so that a clearly p!ssed off Scott Steiner can belt seven shades of sh!t out of him in the aisleway as the two men cross paths following Scotty’s elimination…..

…..he does as well…..

…..he beats the living daylights out of him, smashes him into the guard rails on a number of occasions, whips him into the steel steps….the ring post…..then tosses him back into the ring and storms off back to the locker room…..

…..Bulldog isn’t one to miss a trick either…..and as Barry gingerly gets back to his feet, Davey Boy sends him straight back out of the match, via a clothesline over the top rope…..

…..poor Barry…..he spent more time in the aisle than he did in the ring…..

Entrant #27: Ahmed Johnson
Joining: Kevin Nash (22:00) Test (20:00) The British Bulldog (18:00) Billy Gunn (10:00) Steve Austin (8:00) Matt Hardy (6:00) Kama (4:00)
Eliminated: Barry Windham (1:22) (by The British Bulldog)

…..business is just about to pick up, because coming in at number twenty seven is the Walking Armageddon (and former WWF Champion) Ahmed Johnson…..and it’s not a bad number at all, especially when you consider that the number twenty seven spot has produced more winners than any other slot (three to date…..Big John Studd in 1989, Yokozuna in 1993, and Bret Hart in 1994) and Ahmed is determined to get that World Wrestling Federation into the ranks of the Nation of Domination…..

…..speaking of the Nation, Ahmed quickly joins Kama in the attack of Steve Austin, who’s now severely selling the leg from the assault he received before he even entered this match…..

…..Nash and Test continue their own battle, and after eliminating the man that took HIM out a few weeks back, Big Sexy is now looking to eliminate the man that has been a thorn in the side of the Wolfpac in his absence…..Test has come a long way since his debut though, and the bodyguard is more than capable of holding his own with the former WWF Champion…..

….meanwhile, Davey Boy and Billy Gunn go at it, while Matt Hardy takes a breather in the corner…..

Entrant #28: Dustin Rhodes
Joining: Kevin Nash (24:00) Test (22:00) The British Bulldog (20:00) Billy Gunn (12:00) Steve Austin (10:00) Matt Hardy (8:00) Kama (6:00) Ahmed Johnson (2:00)

…..it’s number twenty eight, and the man that entered at twenty seven LAST year, albeit under a different guise…..last year he was out here as the Bizarre One, Goldust…..this year, he’s the P!ssed Off One, Dustin Rhodes…..

…..Dustin was somewhat of a casualty of the Raven/Jake Roberts feud, a stepping stone during Roberts journey through the circles of hell….and Raven’s less than complimentary words on the career success of ‘Goldust’ lead to somewhat of an awakening for Dustin, who (after being constantly overlooked while part of the rather strange Goldust/Marcus pairing) is now looking to capture Tag Team gold with partner Marc Mero…..

…..Dustin hits the ring and he goes right for Billy Gunn…..these two may both be heels, but there is plenty of bad blood between them…..and if you don’t believe me, you only have to watch Dustin setting Gunn up in the corner…..spreading his legs wide open…..and ‘shattering his dreams’ as he kicks The Bad Ass right in his Sore Front…..

…..Kevin Nash drops Test to the mat, or more specifically, he drops him face first on the top turnbuckle with Snake-eyes, before leaning back in the corner to take a breather…..

….Matt Hardy sees an opportunity and comes bounding across the ring, and (as Test begins to pull himself up to all fours) he leaps off the back of Test and flys at Nash with some Poetry in Motion…..

…..unfortunately Nash catches him…..

…..and dumps him over the top…..

…..meanwhile Austin is still on the receiving end of a Nation beat down, as Ahmed and Kama continue to double team the Rattlesnake…..fortunately help is at hand, because the Bulldog makes his way over to join in the fight and even up the numbers…..

Entrant #29: Mark Henry
Joining: Kevin Nash (26:00) Test (24:00) The British Bulldog (22:00) Billy Gunn (14:00) Steve Austin (12:00) Kama (8:00) Ahmed Johnson (4:00) Dustin Rhodes (2:00)
Eliminated: Matt Hardy (9:08) (by Kevin Nash)

…..although they don’t stay even for long…..because our penultimate entrant is Mark Henry, and he goes straight over to his Nation Brothers to once again put the numbers advantage in their corner…..

…..Austin is a wounded Rattlesnake, but that’s perhaps when he’s at his most dangerous…..because as Bulldog takes the full force of an Ahmed Johnson/Mark Henry assault, Austin drops Kama with a (one legged) Stunner, turns and sees Dustin Rhodes and Billy Gunn teetering dangerously as the attempt to eliminate each other…..

…..and tips both men over the top and down to the floor…..

…..not that it stops them from fighting, because Gunn and Rhodes continue to swing at each other right out there on the floor…..

…..they’re not alone either, because after a few moments their respective partners come rushing out from the back to join in the fight…..and if THAT wasn’t enough (and in a sight that won’t please Vince McMahon, since he already denied these folks a tag title opportunity because of things like this) Al Snow and Chris Candido make their way out too…..and we have a three team brawl right out there at ringside while the Royal Rumble rages on…..

Entrant #30: Owen Hart
Joining: Kevin Nash (28:00) Test (26:00) The British Bulldog (24:00) Steve Austin (14:00) Kama (10:00) Ahmed Johnson (6:00) Mark Henry (2:00)
Eliminated: Billy Gunn (15:11) (by Steve Austin) Dustin Rhodes (3:11) (by Steve Austin)

…..our final entrant should come as no surprise, because due to his involvement in Brian Pillman’s earlier WWF Title victory, coming in at number thirty, is Owen Hart…..and with all thirty men now in the ring, JR wants to know who his colleagues believe is going to heading to Wrestlemania XIV…..

“…..well JR, they say it takes a King to know a King, and THIS King is going for the 1997 King of the Ring, Ahmed Johnson…..what about you Rude?…..”

“…..that’s MR. Rude to you Lawler…..and it’s not often that we agree on something, but you might have made the right pick there…..Kevin Nash has already been in this match for thirty minutes, Stone Cold Steve Austin can barely stand…..if we’re talking Kings then this has got to come down to two men as far as I’m concerned…..Ahmed Johnson, or the 1994 King of the Ring, Owen Hart…..”

…..JR takes both of those comments on board, but as Owen joins Test in assaulting Big Sexy, while Ahmed and Mark Henry beat down The Rattlesnake (leaving Bulldog and Kama to duke it out) Jim Ross tells them not to count Nash or Austin out yet…..

…..and as if to emphasise JR’s point, Steve Austin chooses that exact moment to backdrop the huge Mark Henry over the top and to the outside…..although he clearly tweaked that knee doing it…..

…..and only a few seconds later (as Test holds Kevin Nash wide open) Big Sexy ducks an Owen Hart spinning heel kick, leaving the boot to connect with the jaw of Test, driving the young man back into the ropes…..Nash drops Owen with a big right hand, then turns around and clotheslines Test right out of this match…..

Eliminated: Mark Henry (3:45) (by Steve Austin)

Eliminated: Test (27:52) (by Kevin Nash)

…..just as the British Bulldog finally eliminates Kama…..only for Ahmed Johnson to instantly tip Davey Boy out from behind…..

Eliminated: Kama (13:22) (by The British Bulldog)

Eliminated: The British Bulldog (27:23) (by Ahmed Johnson)

…..and then there were four…..

…..Kevin Nash (32:00), Steve Austin (16:00), Ahmed Johnson (Ahmed Johnson 10:00) and the freshest of them all, Owen Hart (4:00)…..

…..of course, the action IN the ring is only half of the story…..because there is still action going on outside of the ring, as that three way Tag Team Brawl between Al Snow, Chris Candido, Dustin Rhodes and The New Age Outlaws continues, security finally rushes down to ringside to attempt to remove all non-legal competitors from the ring area…..

…..so with all the official’s attentions distracted by that, a still p!ssed off Scott Steiner comes running down the aisle (lead pipe in hand) jumps up onto the apron…..calls Big Sexy’s name…..and absolutely nails Kevin Nash right between the eyes with the lead pipe as he turns…..

…..Nash slumps forward, practically unconscious and finds himself draped over the top rope…..so Scotty gives him a helping hand…..just as Owen Hart grabs the feet…..and between them they flip the deadweight of Kevin Nash all the way over the top rope and down to the floor…..

Eliminated: Kevin Nash (32:48) (by Owen Hart)

…..Scotty heads the back, eloquently calling the practically unconscious Nash a “little bitch” as he goes, while security and backstage personnel FINALLY clear the ring area…..and we’re down to three…..

…..and after eliminating Big Sexy, Owen Hart decides to take a back seat, dropping to the mat and rolling under the bottom rope to the outside…..Jim Ross is appalled by that, stating that Owen is the freshest man in the ring, and if he really believes that he belongs in the Wrestlemania main event then he should be in the ring proving it…..

“…..Stone Cold Steve Austin is in there on one leg, and he’s fighting till his dying breath…..and you can say what you will about the attitude of Ahmed Johnson, but he’s backed down from no-one here in the WWF…..not even the mighty Undertaker…..”

“…..the ‘mighty’ Undertaker Jim Ross? He didn’t look so mighty earlier this evening…..and as far as Owen Hart is concerned, you don’t win all matches by tackling them head on…..a TRUE champion uses his mind as WELL as his wrestling ability, and that’s exactly what Owen Hart is doing here…..”

…..Rick Rude may have a point…..I mean, Owen Hart DOES want to go onto Wrestlemania to compete for the World Wrestling Federation Championship…..and in a match where you lose by being tossed over the top rope, if you’re not in the ring, you can’t get tossed…..

…..Ahmed Johnson and Steve Austin don’t care…..one leg or not, Steve Austin will fight anyone, and he and Ahmed have certainly had their battles in the past…..

…..the two men punch and kick each other all over the ring…..Austin might only have one good leg, but he’s got two good hands, and he uses them to their full effect…..Ahmed has TWO good legs though…..and he uses one of them to kick Austin’s leg out from under his leg (sorry Owen) and then goes about trying to muscle the Texas Rattlesnake right over the top rope…..

…..which is when Owen re-enters the ring…..sneaks up behind the battling pair…..and tips BOTH men over the top rope……

Eliminated: Ahmed Johnson (15:16) (by Owen Hart)

Eliminated: Steve Austin (21:16) (by Owen Hart)

…..or so he thinks…..

…..Ahmed Johnson is eleiminated, no question about that…..but Steve Austin catches himself and comes to rest on the apron, rolling back into the ring behind a celebrating Owen Hart…..standing…..

…..and waiting…..

…..for Owen to turn around…..and when he does, the Hart Foundation man is met by a double bird…..a kick to the midsection (that Austin sells as painful to deliver……sell the leg brother)…..

…..and a Stone Cold Stunner…..

…..the force of which propels Owen right back up to a standing position…..

…..and Austin grabs him by the back of the head…..

…..and launches him right over the top rope and down to the floor…..

Eliminated: Owen Hart (09:52) (by Steve Austin)

Winner: Steve Austin (21:52)
Rating: B+

…..and the bell rings…..and the crowd erupts…..because The Rattlesnake has done it…..one year after being screwed over by his then friend Brian Pillman, Steve Austin has secured the opportunity to potentially challenge the Loose Cannon at the main event of Wrestlemania…..for the World Wrestling Federation Championship…..

…..a weary (and limping) Steve Austin mounts the turnbuckles to salute this crowd, before embarking on a beer bash of epic proportion…..

…..and as his adoring public looks on, this great crowd aren’t the only ones watching Stone Cold celebrate…..because out walks Vince McMahon, Brian Pillman, Ric Flair and Arn Anderson onto the entrance ramp…..and none of them look particularly happy by this development…..THEN the rest of the Hart Foundation make their way out to console Owen…..and none of THEM look particularly happy by this development either…..

…..Stone Cold Steve Austin looks delighted though…..and as he drains another beer he notices his audience on the entrance ramp watching him…..he calls for another beer…..pops it…..places it on the mat…..

…..and flips all eight men a double bird with a smile on his face, before going back to toasting the crowd, as we come to the end of an action packed Royal Rumble, with The Rattlesnake standing tall…..

…..goodnight folks…..
