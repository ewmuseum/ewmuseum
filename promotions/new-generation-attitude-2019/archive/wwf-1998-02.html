WWF Raw is War: Week 1, February 1998

Posted 21 October 2019

Week 1, February 1998

Myriad Convention Center (Oklahoma City, Oklahoma)

ATT: 15,000

TV: 38.61

We’re opening up this evening with not only the owner of the World Wrestling Federation (Vince McMahon) but his chosen champions too…..because the WWF Tag Team Champions Ric Flair and Arn Anderson are by his side, while the World Wrestling Federation Champion himself, The Corporate Champion, Brian Pillman leads the way……and that’s not to mention the still surprising sight of Mankind bringing up the rear…..

…..the quintet enter the ring, where McMahon addresses the conclusion of last weeks show, a conclusion which saw the announcement that both Bret Hart and Steve Austin’s respective #1 contender status would be on the line at No Way Out…..Steve Austin’s Wrestlemania main event shot (a result of his Royal Rumble victory) would be up for grabs when he faces Owen Hart, although to get this opportunity (and in an effort to ensure that Brian Pillman would only have ONE challenger at Wrestlemania) Bret Hart had to put his OWN guaranteed title shot up for grabs, against a man who agreed to take HIS title shot (should he win) the very next night on Raw is War, Big Sexy, Kevin Nash…..

…..McMahon says that he HAD intended on giving the WWF Champion the night off at No Way Out, while his numerous potential challengers fought it out amongst themselves, HOWEVER, being the fighting (Corporate) Champion that he is, Brian Pillman has DEMANDED to face Kane, and so (against his better judgement) Vincent Kennedy McMahon hereby makes that match official…..the Corporate Cannon vs. The Big Red Machine…..for the World Wrestling Federation Championship…..

…..Vince hands the microphone to Pillman, who cackles like the lunatic that he is, telling Vince that his “better judgement” would be better reserved for the safety of his employees…..

“…..because Kane, never let it be said that THIS king rested on his laurels…..I buried your big brother like the faux phantom phenomenon that he is…..the king of the underworld is DEAD at the hands of the crowned prince of chaos…..and in his stead, the NEW Ruler of the Revolution ascended to the throne…..they say that heavy lies the head that wears the crown, but when you’ve carrying a revolution on your back, there is really no weight that’s too much to bear…..”

…..Pillman says that those who fail to learn from history are doomed to repeat it, well Kane has clearly learned NOTHING, because the last time he was in alone with Brian F’n Pillman, he limped away only half the man he was when he arrived…..

…..Pillman unhooks the WWF Title from around his waist and drapes it over his shoulder, telling Kane that the prize he seeks is an unobtainable desire…..a desire that will see him fade into obscurity…..just like Paul Bearer, just like the Undertaker…..

“…..and just like anyone else that believes they have what it takes to topple this tyrant…..

…..Bret Hart, Owen Hart, Kevin Nash…..even my old pal Stone Cold Steve Austin…..each and every one of you believe you are walking a path from despair to immortality…..unfortunately for you, that path winds its way through MY Kingdom, and if I have to scorch this earth with fire to eradicate YOUR path, then that’s exactly what I will do…..”

…..Pillman says that Kevin Nash is otherwise engaged tonight (with a date with the Million Dollar Corporation set for later this evening) but Bret Hart is free…..so Brian invites him (and Vince McMahon mandates) to get an up close and personal view of what Brian Pillman is capable of, when the two join forces later tonight, to face the combination of Stone Cold Steve Austin……and Kane……

Rating: A*

Faarooq vs. Scorpio


…..we air a quick recap video featuring Faarooqs journey from the leader of the Nation of Domination to his violent ostracisation from the very group he founded, before we come back to ringside, where he looks to gain a measure of revenge against Nation member Scorpio…..

…..these two men will be clashing in a tag team encounter at No Way Out, in a match that will also feature Bradshaw and Ahmed Johnson…..funnily enough it was Scorpio’s loss LAST week to Bradshaw that forced that tag team match into fruition (a fact that Ahmed Johnson didn’t seem to be too pleased about) and in what can only be punishment for that loss, it appears as though he’s not afforded the luxury of having any Nation members at ringside with him tonight…..

…..probably could have used them as well…..because he lost here tonight too…..

…..these two had a pretty decent match actually, but after not competing inside a WWF ring since late December of last year, Faarooq was desperate to prove something…..and he did…..finishing off Scorpio with the Dominator for the one, two, three…..

Winner: Faarooq
Rating: B

…..Scorpio might not have been without the Nation throughout the duration of that match, but they’re here now…..and Ahmed Johnson doesn’t look impressed…..

…..Kama, D’Lo and Mark Henry surround three sides of the ring (cutting off any escape route for Faarooq) while Ahmed Johnson climbs through the ropes on the fourth side…..he drags Scorpio up to his feet, telling him that “that’s twice your punk ass has let me down”, before making it crystal clear that there had better not be a third time…..

…..Faarooq looks around at the four Nation members that he USED to be team mates with, before staring long and hard at Ahmed Johnson (the man who usurped his position) telling him to call off the henchmen and fight him like a man…..

…..a request that Ahmed simply smirks at, before calling the rest of the Nation inside the ring with him…..

…..the last time Faarooq found himself in this position, he got his ass kicked…..

…..last time though he was all alone, this time it looks as though he has some back-up, because Bradshaw and (surprisingly considering their history) Nelson make their way out to stand side by side against the Nation…..

…..of course, they’re still at the disadvantage, because no matter how big Nelson is, five against three still gives the Nation the numbers to do some serious damage here, and Ahmed knows it too, because he’s not taking his men anywhere…..

…..which is when Booker T makes his way out from the back…..

…..Booker has a match with Jeff Jarrett upcoming at No Way Out, his first opportunity to capture singles gold when he challenges for the European Championship…..with that being said though, he DOES have the Nation to thank for that opportunity, even if he WAS unaware of that fact at the time…..

…..Ahmed has been courting Harlem Heat to join him in the Nation for some weeks now, so when Booker tells the former World Champion to take his Nation to the back, WITHOUT laying so much as a finger on Faarooq Bradshaw or Nelson, Ahmed surprisingly acquiesces…..

…..he doesn’t go COMPLETELY without word though, he lets both Faarooq and Bradshaw know that he’ll see their asses at No Way Out, before letting Booker know that they’re only leaving here right now as a courtesy to him, but that courtesy will only extend so far…..the clock is ticking…..their invitation to the Nation WILL expire before much longer…..

“…..tick tock, tick tock…..you want that European Title, you’d better take it…..by any means necessary…..”

Rating: A

…..speaking of ‘any means necessary’, Bret Hart has gone to very extreme means to ensure that his brother gains another opportunity at challenging for the WWF Title (going so far as to put his OWN title opportunity on the line to do so) and the Hart Foundation are here now, alongside Michael Cole, who has a few questions for the group…..

…..he first asks Bret how confident he feels going into No Way Out? After all (had he so chosen) he could have been challenging Brian Pillman TONIGHT for the WWF Championship, instead, he may find himself with no title shot at all should Kevin Nash defeat him in just 20 days time…..

…..a pretty big gamble to take…..

…..Bret says that it’s no gamble whatsoever…..he says that there is NOTHING that he wouldn’t do to ensure that his brother also gets the opportunity to fight for the richest prize in the sport, especially if it means getting to take out Kevin Nash one more time…..

“…..I’ve beat him before…..I’ve defeated him FOR the World Wrestling Federation Championship…..WE defeated the Wolfpac at Survivor Series…..so you tell me Michael Cole, where’s the gamble?…..”

…..Owen agrees, telling Cole that Bret Hart could defeat Kevin Nash in his sleep…..and as for him, he’s going to show Steve Austin, that throwing someone over the top rope doesn’t make you World Champion calibre…..

“…..after all, even Al Snow was lucky enough to toss a few bums at the Royal Rumble, do you think HE should be in the main event of Wrestlemania? Don’t make me laugh…..”

…..Owen says that he’ll earn HIS title opportunity the old fashioned way, by making Steve Austin submit to the most devastating hold in wrestling, the Sharpshooter…..then, when Bret beats Nash, the Hart Brothers will go to Wrestlemania, take Brian Pillman out of the equation, and on THAT night, the better man will bring the WWF Title home to Canada where it belongs…..

…..that takes us to the only Hart Foundation member to currently hold gold, the WWF Intercontinental Champion Chris Jericho…..Bret and Owen will both be competing in number one contender matches at No Way Out, but Chris Jericho, he’s going to be in an actual title match, putting the Intercontinental Championship on the line against Rey Mysterio…..

…..Jericho says that he didn’t really have much choice…..he felt bad for the little guy…..

“…..I mean, I took his division…..I beat up his loser friend Bore-Us Malenko Jr, there’s not a lot else for little Rey-mundo to do…..the LEAST I can do is get him a decent payday, because a record number of Jerichoholics from around the world will be hitting that order button, to see THEIR hero…..me…..defend MY Intercontinental Championship, as only I can baby…..”

…..as for toning, Jericho and Test will be giving the world a little preview of what’s in store for Mysterio at No Way Out, when they face Rey and Dean Malenko in Tag Team action……and as Test (loudly) informs us, Rey could t have picked a worse partner, because it’s going to be business as usual, when Dean Malenko finds himself on the losing end of a match against Chris Jericho…..because that loser couldn’t beat Chris Jericho OR Test on his best day…..

…..that’s four confident Canadians right there…..let’s go back down to ringside…..

Rating: A*

Matt Hardy vs. Raven


…..where Matt Hardy is set to face the self proclaimed “Deity of the Damned”, Raven…..

…..while Raven has been visible on virtually every episode of Raw in the last few months, in terms of actual IN ring action, he’s been pretty sporadic, in fact, he’s had more matches on tv in these last two weeks that he’s had in the previous two months…..

…..he should have a bit of ring-rust then right?…..

…..well if he has, it’s not noticeable here tonight…..granted, Matt Hardy isn’t anywhere close to a walkover, but when the time comes, he takes the same trip that the man ACCOMPANYING Raven to ringside (Jake Roberts) took at the Royal Rumble…..Evenflow DDT…..short trip, bad landing…..

…..two matches…..two weeks…..two wins for Raven…..

Winner: Raven
Rating: B

…..Raven rolls off Matt Hardy and shuffles backwards into the corner, where Jake Roberts hands him a microphone (before taking up a protective stance in front of his one time enemy) as Raven begins to speak…..

…..he says that too often people are quick to rush into folly…..a temporary solution in an impatient world…..

“…..people have abandoned the infinite patience of man, only to seek the instant gratifications of temporary addictions…..be that physical, emotional or spiritual, rarely do you people remain comfortable enough in your own heads to withstand the solitude that comes before reward…..

…..forever moving forward, never arrive at your destination…..always searching, but blind to what you seek…..never does it occur to you to just stop…..to stay…..to wait…..never does it occur to you that the thing you truly desire, will eventually find YOU…..

…..I stand before you a patient man…..I have little need for material possessions, for meaningless interaction…..for instant gratification…..instead I wait…..I wait for you…..find me…..come to me…..for I can set you free…..Quote the Raven…..Nevermore……”

…..any idea what Raven is going on about? No, neither do the commentators…..he clearly has something (or rather SOMEONE) on his mind though…..although who that is remains to be seen…..let’s go to the back, maybe the Million Dollar Corporation will talk a bit more sense…..

Rating: B+

…..who are standing by with Dok Hendrix…..

…..Ted DiBiase isn’t happy, telling Dok that last week, both The Nation of Domination AND The Wolfpac took money out of HIS pocket…..and NO ONE takes money out of the pocket of the Million Dollar Man…..

…..he says that because of the influence of Ahmed Johnson’s gang of thugs, not only did his Corporation take home the losing share of the purse last week, but Jeff Jarrett now needs to put their (much valued) European Title on the line against ANOTHER street punk in Booker T…..

…..but worse than that “which is the only reason you Nation street rats are currently getting a pass” was that Kevin Nash and The Wolfpac took it upon themselves to unleash a vicious, unprovoked assault (“from behind mind you, because otherwise this wouldn’t have happened”) on the remaining Corporation members, Rich Steiner and Southern Justice…..

“…..now apparently, Vince McMahon seems to believe that the appropriate punishment for this crime, was to reward Kevin Nash with a chance to become the number one contender to the WWF Championship…..WE on the other hand, believe that we will unleash our OWN brand of justice…..tonight…..”

…..Jarrett takes a turn on the microphone, telling Dok that it doesn’t matter who gets put in front of him, they all leave the same way……on their back, and on their way to the emergency room…..

“…..in fact, since everyone has a price for the Million Dollar Corporation, we decided (in a rare display of charity) to pre-purchase Booker T’s treatment facility…..so Booker, and you can thank us later, once No Way Out is all said and done, you’ll be spending the night in your very own hospital bed……right next to your brother ha ha…..”

…..but that’s then, this is now…..and now, Rich Steiner and Southern Justice are going to take the Wolfpac out…..permanently…..

Rating: B+

The Wolfpac vs. The Million Dollar Corporation (w/Ted DiBiase)


…..and that match is next…..

…..Kevin Nash was NOT a happy boy last week (as the Corporation found out first hand) but he appears to be in a MUCH better mood tonight…..in fact, all three Wolfpac men look particularly pleased…..and why wouldn’t they…..Nash has the chance to become number one contender at No Way Out, Scott Hall gets to gain a measure of revenge when he faces Scott Steiner (the man that cost him the Intercontinental Championship at the Royal Rumble) and Sean Waltman, well…..he’s still employed?…..I jest Sean, I jest (please don’t sh!t in my bag)…..

…..the Million Dollar Corporation on the other hand are far from happy…..they pretty much spent the best part of last week getting beat up on all sides, and they’re looking to make a statement here tonight with the Wolfpac…..or rather MORE of a statement…..because say what you will about Ted DiBiase’s group (and the fans often do) ever since Jeff Jarrett captured the European Championship back in late October of last year, they have developed somewhat of a vicious streak in an effort to ensure that the prize remains in their possession…..and if you don’t believe me, ask Ken Shamrock…..Chavo Guerrero…..Psycho Sid…..oh right, you can’t…..because the Corporation took them out…..

…..now, with all that being said, the Corporation hasn’t had to contend with The Wolfpac…..tonight they do, and honestly, it doesn’t go well for them…..collectively Kevin Nash, Scott Hall and Sean Waltman have won every single Championship there has been to win here in the WWF (including the European and now defunct Light Heavyweight titles thanks to Waltman) so they’re no pushovers…..and as (a decent) match breaks down, it’s Kevin Nash that picks up the big victory, finishing off Rich Steiner with the Jackknife Powerbomb for the one, two, three…..

Winner(s): The Wolfpac
Rating: B

…..with a decent victory in the bag, Kevin Nash grabs a microphone to ask Bret Hart if he liked what he just saw?…..because that right there, that’s what’s waiting for him at No Way Out…..

“…..Bret, me and you we’ve gone around and around and around again…..in fact, to borrow a line of yours, had Scott Steiner not screwed me out of winning the Royal Rumble, we could well have been having this match at Wrestlemania instead of No Way Out…..but instead, I’M going to be heading on to Wrestlemania with the strap, while your dumb ass is sat back in Calgary shovelling snow from your driveway…..”

…..Hall takes the stick, to tell Big Kev that he wasn’t the only one to get screwed over by Scotty Steiner at the Rumble, but it’s all good, because at No Way Out, he’s going to take that muscled up meathead, and do things to him that his head freak Sable really isn’t going to like…..

“…..in fact, the only thing that Big Poppa’s going to be able to Pump, is gas….down at the little back water town he’s gonna run off and hides in after The Bad Guy embarrasses him on live Pay Per View…..”

…..obviously Big Poppa Pump has something to say on the matter…..because he makes his way out onto the stage (along with Sable and a microphone) to tell Scott Hall that the only embarrassment around here, is how many times that the Genetic Freak has singlehandedly kicked The Wolfpac’s ass…..

“…..HELL BOY, I SHOULDN’T EVEN BE WASTING THE SWEAT OFF MY BALLS TO KICK YOUR ASS ONE MORE TIME…..SEE THE WAY I SEE IT, IT SHOULD BE ME IN THAT NUMBER ONE CONTENDERS MATCH…..KEVIN NASH, HE AIN’T DONE WORTH SH!T AROUND HERE SINCE HE STOPPED CARRYING SHAWN MICHAELS BAGS AROUND FOR HIM…..

…..SEE, WHEN YOU THREE LITTLE B!TCHES WERE RUNNING AROUND PLAYING AT BEING BIG BAD WOLVES, BIG POPPA PUMP WAS OUT HERE WITH THE WORLD TITLE AROUND HIS WAIST, AND I AIN’T NEVER GOT NO REMATCH EITHER…..AHMED JOHNSON DID’NT GIVE ME NO SHOT, THE UNDERTAKER DIDN’T GIVE ME NO SHOT…..BRIAN PILLMAN SURE AS SH!T AIN’T GONNA GIVE ME NO SHOT…..BECAUSE EVERYONE KNOWS THAT WHEN YOU STEP INTO THE RING WITH SCOTT STEINER AND YOU GOT A TITLE I WANT, YOU’RE GONNA LOSE THREE THINGS…..YOUR BELT, YOUR MANHOOD…..AND YOUR WOMAN TOO…..”

…..typical rambling promo from Scotty there…..but he has a point, he never DID get a rematch at the big one after losing the WWF Title to Ahmed Johnson at Summerslam, now here (some six months later) and he’s not had a shot at the World title since…..no wonder he’s pissed…..

…..he tells Hall that if his guts are as big as his mouth, then he’ll leave his little girlfriends down there in the ring, walk up that aisle, and face Steiner like a man right now…..

…..obviously Hall has no problem with that, telling Nash and Waltman to wait right there while he slaps this jabroni around a bit, before climbing through the ropes and heading up the aisle…..

…..of course he only gets so far before he hears a commotion behind him, turning around to see that the Million Dollar Corporation have jumped Nash and Waltman back in the ring…..

…..Hall turns back and heads towards the ring, but he’s blindsided from behind by Scotty who comes rushing down the aisle with that lead pipe in hand, crashing it across the bottom of his back, then launches him hard into the steel safety barricade…..

…..Nash and Waltman are currently doing their best to hold the Corporation at bay, but four on two odds aren’t great…..FIVE on two are even worse, when Scotty enters the ring to join in the fun…..

…..it might be Hall that’s getting the first crack at Steiner at No Way Out, make no mistake though, Big Sexy still wants to get his hands on the man that caused his elimination from the Royal Rumble match, so as Big Poppa Pump beats on Waltman, Nash launches each member of the Corporation to the outside one by one (and looking like an absolute beast as he does) before heading straight for Scott…..

…..only to be cut off by Sable, who puts herself between the two men…..

…..Big Sexy hesitates for a second, clearly reluctant to lay his hands on a woman…..unfortunately Sable has no problem laying her hands on HIM, as she quickly drops to her knees and whacks Nash right in the balls…..she’s turned into quite the little minx has our Sable…..

…..with Nash doubled over in pain, Steiner quickly capitalises, driving the man to the mat and locking in the Steiner Recliner, forcing Nash to watch as the Million Dollar Corporation set up a table, take hold of Waltman…..and drive him straight through the wood as they have with so many others these last few months…..

…..Scott Hall finally recuperates enough to come diving into the ring (steel chair in hand) but it’s too little, too late, and as Steiner, Sable and The Corporation bail, all he can do is check on the condition of his fallen comrades…..

…..we saw Rich Steiner try and patch things up with his brother recently the only way he knows how…..by offering him lots of money…..it didn’t work then, but Rich (and Deebs) are nothing if not persistent, and again (as the group stands at the top of the aisle) Rich holds out a wad of cash to his brother for “a job well done”…..again, Scotty doesn’t look interested, saying that he’d beat the Wolfpac up for free, any day and twice on Sunday’s…..

…..but he does take the money…..

…..or rather, he doesn’t object when SABLE takes the money…..after all, she’s a girl with expensive tastes…..so while it’s safe to say that Scott Steiner is no where near a member of the Million Dollar Corporation, it seems that he CAN at least tolerate standing beside his brother without knocking his teeth down his throat…..

…..so that’s something I guess…..

…..I suppose we’d better go to the back…..

Rating: A*

…..where a nervous looking Michael Cole is currently traversing the bowels of the building, not on a bear hunt, but on a Mankind hunt…..

…..he makes his way through storage area’s and backstage rooms, saying that ever since the deranged one returned at the Royal Rumble, every fan he’s seen has been asking him the same question…..why would Mankind help Brian Pillman of all people, become the WWF Champion…..especially after their history…..well, Michael Cole is going to find Mankind, and he’s going to ask him that same question…..

…..why?…..

“…..why?…..well I’ll tell you why Michael Cole…..all you had to do is ask…..”

…..Cole almost jumps out of his skin as he almost trips over a seated (and rocking) Mankind, who doesn’t even look up at him as he speaks…..

…..he says that he’s heard the question himself…..many, many times in the last few weeks…..but while he shouldn’t have to explain himself to these fans, to Michael Cole, to ANYONE, he says that he’s answered anybody that’s asked him that question the same way…..

“…..why not?…..after all, if you can’t beat them…..join them…..”

…..Mankind says that for all his best efforts, for all his sacrifices, his suffering, his pain…..for everything that he put his body through as Mankind…..Dude Love…..it didn’t matter…..no matter what he did, Brian Pillman won…..

“…..I could not beat him Sam I Am…..I could not beat him in a match, I could not beat him in the back…..I could not beat him bell to bell, I could not beat him in a cell…..I could not beat him here or there, I could not beat him anywhere…..I could not, would not beat that man, I could not beat him Sam I Am…..”

…..Dr. Seuss aside, Mankind says that after trying and trying and trying, the little engine that could realised that he was the little engine that COULDN’T…..and all those fans that were cheering his name, that were chanting his name as he damn near killed himself…..all those fans that “welcomed” Mankind into their hearts…..they all just…..forgot……

…..Mankind says that back in October, when he crash landed on that mat from the top of the hell in a cell structure, he was off tv…..he was in no condition to get out of his hospital bed, let alone perform…..he was out of sight….and he soon realised that he was out of mind too…..

“…..’thanks for the memories Mankind’…..’thanks for giving your body Mankind’…..’thanks for putting your life, your career, your livelihood on the line to entertain us Mankind’…..’but, what have you done for us lately?’……’DANCE MONKEY, DANCE’…..

…..well I’m done dancing…..I’m done being nothing more than a glorified crash test dummy for these people to ‘ohh’ and ‘ahh’ about…..I’m done, putting MY body on the line for YOUR approval…..I’m done…..I’m just done, being on the losing team…..if you can’t beat them, join them…..may as well break my back for a buck rather than the fickle admiration of these fans that forget you in a heartbeat…..

…..because Mr. McMahon didn’t forget me…..Mr. McMahon made sure I was paid while I was recuperating…..Mr. McMahon made sure my medical expenses were covered…..and when Mr. McMahon called me up and asked if I thought I’d be ready to come back for the Royal Rumble, I said ‘for you Mr. McMahon, I’ll make myself ready…..I’ll join you’…..

…..if you can’t beat them Michael…..join them…..join them……join them…..”

…..Cole does try to get some follow up questions, but I think Mankind has gone for now…..he’s completely shut down as he rocks back and forth on the concrete…..

…..let’s go back to ringside…..

Rating: A

Dean Malenko & Rey Mysterio vs. Chris Jericho & Test


…..for some Tag Team action…..as (perennial loser) Dean Malenko teams up with Rey Mysterio, to face the Hart Foundation pairing of Chris Jericho and Test…..

…..Dean Malenko initially entered the World Wrestling Federation with TWO goals in mind…..capture the Light Heavyweight Title, and defeat the man that he claimed was the best Light Heavyweight competitor in the world, Rey Mysterio…..a claim that Chris Jericho took offence too…..

…..to be fair to Malenko, Jericho wasn’t even competing in the Light Heavyweight division at this point (and had even challenged for the WWF World Title by now) but still, the young Canadians ego is obviously a fragile one, and Chris Jericho is a petty, petty man…..how did he deal with this slight? By putting his career up for grabs against the fate of the ENTIRE Light Heavyweight division…..he won the subsequent match, and just like that, Dean Malenko’s dreams of becoming the champion of that division faded away, because Chris Jericho TOOK that Division away……

…..since then, Chris Jericho has gone from strength to strength (winning the WWF Intercontinental Championship at the Royal Rumble) while Dean Malenko has somewhat stagnated (losing in his effort to become WWF European Champion at the Royal Rumble) and got taunted and ridiculed by Jericho along the way, so much so that Malenko’s one time dream opponent has stepped up to try and shut the Hart Foundation man up, challenging him to put the Intercontinental Championship on the line at No Way Out…..

…..and the ever arrogant Chris Jericho accepted…..

…..which brings us to this tag team encounter…..it was good…..it was really good actually, especially for a tv match…..for all the mocking that they have been on the receiving end of, Malenko and Mysterio completely dominated the opening minutes, getting Jericho out of the way quickly before turning their attention to the largest man in the match, Test…..

…..it’s eventually Test that turns the tides, and it’s Jericho’s opponent at No Way Out that gets to play the face in peril, with the two Hart Foundation members working him over until a Test Running Big Boot misses and the bigger man finds himself all wrapped up on the top rope, leaving Malenko to receive the hot tag…..

….he is hot too…..in fact he’s on fire, as he works over the leg of Test in between fighting off the offence of Jericho….in fact, he looks like he’s going to shut Jericho up once and for all by getting the win…..

…..except he doesn’t…..

…..because Chris Jericho is the best in the world at what he does…..and what he does is run his mouth…..from a safe distance…..

…..either way, Malenko gets sidetracked into a shouting match with Jericho on the outside, which means that this time Test’s Running Big Boot DOES connect…..and three seconds later, Dean Malenko just lost another match…..

Winner(s): Chris Jericho & Test
Rating: B

…..Jericho jumps up and down in joy as the bell rings, before snatching up his Intercontinental Championship (and a microphone) from the time keeper and rushing up the aisle (where his partner is now waiting) and jumps into Test’s arms in celebration…..

…..Test holds on just long enough for the scene to look as ridiculous as was intended, before setting the Intercontinental Champion down, where Jericho pulls out the microphone to mock Dean Malenko just a little bit more…..

…..he tells Mysterio that he warned him about picking a loser for a partner…..he didn’t listen…..now here he is, a loser as well…..

“…..see Rey-Mundo, that’s the difference between you…..and me…..I, Chris Jericho, your Intercontinental Champion, Hero and general Paragon of Virtue, I only associate myself with WINNERS…..while you, you hang around with two-bit losers like Bore-Us Malenko Jr…..Bore-Us is a loser….his father was a loser…..his mother was a loser….his brother is a loser…..even his Great-Aunt Gretchen is a loser…..and at No Way Out, when you get in the ring with me little guy, you’re going to be nothing more, than a big old loser too…..because you will never…..eeeeeeeever……take this Intercontinental Title…..from me……THANK YOU, GOODNIGHT!!…..”

…..with that Jericho waves to his adoring fans (who are booing the crap out of him) before heading to the back, leaving the losers in the ring…..

…..Mysterio tries to help Malenko up, telling him not to listen, and that this loss doesn’t matter, nut Malenko isn’t interested…..he shrugs Mysterio off, telling him that EVERY loss matters, before angrily climbing out of the ring himself and heading to the back too…..

…..someone is clearly letting Chris Jericho get inside his head…..

Rating: B+

…..from there we head backstage, to what looks to be a very frustrated looking Steve Austin trying to get something (ANYTHING) out of the Big Red Monster, Kane…..

…..clearly Kane isn’t answering him (and judging by his complete silence since his arrival here in the WWF, can’t answer him) but that doesn’t stop a p!ssed off Steve Austin from hollering at him…..although by the look of Kane (and the way he’s pulling on that one black glove of his) it looks like he’s had about enough of Austin’s attitude…..

…..fortunately, before matters can escalate, Dok Hendrix walks into shot, looking to find out what’s going on, and get a few words on tonight’s main event…..

“…..what’s going on? I’ll tell you what’s going on…..Old Stone Cold is in here tryin’ to find out whether or not he’s got two opponents tonight, or three…..because the way I see it, if this red sumbitch beats Brian Pillman at No Way Out (and he just might) then he’s got a Wrestlemania date with Steve Austin to look forward too…..

…..thankfully most of Sean Waltman’s hair is still in tact, but he’s not a happy wolf…..Jeff Jarrett might have a European Title shot lined up for later this evening, but from the look of it he’s still got the Wolfpac to worry about…..

…..we’ll see how that goes later on…..

Rating: A

…..from there we head to the back for another brief cut scene…..the Undertaker and Kane are still looking for Raven, but much like earlier they are having little luck finding him…..for someone that was so insistent about the Deadman coming to him, he’s not making himself easy to find…..

…..again Taker and Kane toss the furniture (and staff) around in their hunt, but the Deadman is clearly getting increasingly impatient about their inability to find the Deity of the Damned…..

…..he tells Kane that SOMEONE is going to feel his wrath tonight…..preferably Raven, but if the bird boy wants to play hide and seek then maybe they will have to broaden their search…..

…..maybe they should look for Mankind too…..

…..Kane is happy with that (I think, it’s hard to tell what with the mask) after all, Paul Bearer has already warned Mrs. Foley’s Baby Boy that Kane is coming for him…..he nods his head at his brother and the two continue their search…..

…..with TWO targets now instead of one…..

…..that’s if they find them…..

Rating: N/A

…..from there we go to a sit down interview that was recorded earlier in the day, as Jim Ross gets up close and personal with Dean Malenko, to discuss Malenko’s challenge to Chris Jericho……

…..JR says that this is no normal challenge…..yes, Dean Malenko will be getting a shot at Chris Jericho AND the WWF Intercontinental Championship (at Wrestlemania no less) but in order to do so, he WILLINGLY put his career on the line…..

“…..which means that if you win, you become the World Wrestling Federation Champion…..but if you LOSE…..that’s it, you’re done, you’re going home…..and Dean, with all due respect, you haven’t exactly been lighting it up around here with victories…..why would you not only accept these terms, but actually be the one to set them?…..”

…..JR says that Chris Jericho is one of them most self-confident (to the point of arrogance) superstars here in the WWF…..he would likely have eventually agreed to face Malenko again…..why put it all on the line?…..

“…..why?…..because I have to…..”

…..Malenko says that he put his career on the line because he has to know…..he HAS to…..this will be the most high pressure, high stipulation match he’s ever competed in and he NEEDS to know that he’s up to the task…..

…..Malenko tells JR that he came to the WWF with the goal of proving himself to be the best Light-Heavyweight competitor in the world……he wanted to prove himself against the best Light-Heavyweight wrestlers in the world…..he never got to do that…..and now, thanks to Chris Jericho, he never will…..

…..he says that Chris Jericho, in one night, changed Dean Malenko’s entire career projection…..he took away everything Malenko had been working towards…..

…..and then he laughed about it…..

“…..Chris Jericho didn’t even WANT the Light-Heavyweight Title…..he just didn’t want anyone else to have it…..he carried that Championship Belt around like a piece of trash and then discarded it the second he became the Intercontinental Champion…..he crapped on the Light-Heavyweight Title…..he crapped on the Light-Heavyweight Division…..and he crapped on me…..

…..he crapped on me…..my family…..AND my career…..I’ve never doubted my ability a day in my life Jim, but Chris Jericho had my doubting myself…..CAN I defeat Chris Jericho?…..CAN I become Intercontinental Champion?…..CAN I do it on the grandest stage of them all?…..

…..CAN I?…..

…..I think I can Jim Ross…..in fact, I KNOW I can…..but if I can’t…..then I shouldn’t be here…..if I can’t…..

…..then I’m going home…..”

Rating: A

…..oh look, it’s Savio Vega…..

…..poor old Savio hasn’t really been relevant (or used regularly) around here since, oh…..I want to say early to mid 1996…..

…..he was (at one point) an early foil of the Nation of Domination, even going on to wrestle for the vacant WWF Intercontinental Championship back at Wrestlemania XII against (then Nation Leader) Faarooq and (current Nation Leader) Ahmed Johnson in a triple threat match…..

…..funnily enough, it would have been around that time that his path would have crossed with his opponent tonight…..because he’s a former Nation of Domination member himself…..FORMER being the operative word, because it was just last night that Scorpio was violently evicted from the group…..

…..I guess that makes him a babyface now…..

…..might explain the dancing…..

…..considering he WAS only evicted from the Nation last night (violently…..I did mention that right?) Scorpio is in a surprisingly good mood here…..the crowd is somewhat into him and he certainly appears to be enjoying pandering to the fans again…..he looks good here, and while this was Savio’s first outing on Raw in some time, he doesn’t get the result that I’m sure he was hoping for…..instead he gets to count the lights after getting squashed with a Scorpio (450) Splash…..

Winner: Scorpio
Rating: B

…..it was short and sweet, but Scorpio has his first official win as a Non-Nation member…..and Michael Cole wants to find out how that feels, making his way out from the back to join the victor in the ring…..

…..Cole talks briefly about the attack that he suffered last night at the hands of his so-called brothers, before asking Scorpio where he goes from here…..after all (as Ahmed pointed out last night) the Light-Heavyweight Division (a Division that Scorpio spearheaded in the early days as it’s first Champion) is no more…..

…..Scorpio says that just like Ahmed Johnson, Micheal Cole is too short sighted……he says that he was never a Light-Heavyweight wrestler……he was just a wrestler…..who happened to be a Light-Heavyweight…..

“…..one or two little losses (and a Division being dissolved) and the Nation wanna kick me to the Curb?…..that’s ok Michael……the Nation can keep on underestimating me…..AHMED JOHNSON can keep on underestimating me…..that just means it’ll be that much sweeter when I whip his big ass…..”

…..Scorpio says that where he goes from here, that’s pretty easy to answer…..he goes to war……and he goes to war AGAINST the Nation…..one of them…..two of them….three, four…..all of them, it doesn’t matter…..all he has to do now is find some tag team partners…..

“…..if only the Nation weren’t so damn likeable Michael Cole…..where ever will I find anyone that would even WANT to fight them?…..”

…..looks like Scorpio already has some partners in mind…..because as Faarooq, Bradshaw and the big man Nelson make their way out, he has a huge smile on his face…..

…..the three men join Scorpio in the ring, not for more promo time though…..they just show a little bit of solidarity with the man and bark threats at the Nation of Domination…..looks like Scorpio has a (temporary) Nation of his own…..

Rating: B+

…..before the four men head to the back, and we get to the reason Michael Cole was out here in the first place…..

…..what? You think we sent him out here just for a Scorpio promo?…..

…..Cole says that he initially came out here because he has an introduction to make…..he says that as the World Wrestling Federation continues to prove that it is THE world wide leader in sports entertainment, it’s attracting more and more members of the fighting community…..

“…..first we had the worlds most dangerous man Ken Shamrock…..then the worlds strongest man Mark Henry…..now I want to introduce you to a man so adept at the martial arts, that his entire body has had to be registered as a lethal weapon……ladies and gentlemen, this…..is Steve Blackman…..”

…..he doesn’t LOOK like much of a lethal weapon…..in fact (apart from the impressive physique) this Steve Blackman fella looks rather bland…..black hair, black beard, black martial arts bottoms……Black(man) by name, black by nature it would seem…..

…..he does have some supercool looking sticks with him though…..

…..he gets into the ring and runs through a brief (rather boring…..he’s not much of a talker) history and introduction…..basically informing the crowd that he first wrestled for the WWF a decade ago, before contracting malaria (and dysentery) which left him practically bed bound for two years…..Blackman says that his ultimate goal was always to get back to the WWF though….and after spending years rebuilding his body, his muscle mass AND his skills (discovering a talent for Escrima and Tae Kwon Do) he’s finally able to step back into the ring…..

…..which is exactly what he’s in the WWF to do…..

…..Michael Cole points out that since Blackman has bought those Escrima Sticks with him, why doesn’t he show us what he can do with them? Blackman agrees, but only gets a few seconds into a display…..

…..before Savio Vega rolls back into the ring…..

…..I guess Savio has been nursing his ribs on the outside this whole time after getting squashed by Scorpio, but he’s not having this……he snatches the microphone from Michael Cole, saying that he has had virtually no screen time in months, but this little Karate Kid wannabe just walks in off the street and gets on Raw just like that?……

…..Savio doesn’t think so…..

…..he tosses the microphone down, before shoving Blackman in the chest (as well at rather poorly attempting to do the classic Karate Kid Crane Kick) telling the newcomer to get out of here…..

…..Blackmans not going anywhere…..so Savio shoves him again…..and again…..and again…..and then finally swings a right hand…..

…..which Blackman dodges…..

…..and then he cracks Savio upside the head with one of those sticks…..

…..Savio goes down like a sack of guano, and Blackman simply rolls him out of the ring as the lights go down (to reveal that this sticks glow in the dark damn it) and he’s finally able to give his rather impressive display…..

…..ok, enough of that…..let’s go to the back…..

Rating: B

…..where Kevin Kelly is attempting to get a brief word from Steve Austin…..

…..Kelly says that tonight, Vince McMahon has made his feelings (and his wishes) crystal clear…..he doesn’t want the Rattlesnake anywhere near the main event of Wrestlemania…..he doesn’t want Stone Cold anywhere near Brian Pillman…..and he most certainly does not want Steve Austin anywhere near the WWF Championship…..in fact, tonight…..Ric Flair and Arn Anderson have been tasked with taking the Rattlesnake out of commission once and for all…..

“…..is that right?…..well let me tell you something son, the only way that you’re getting Steve Austin out of that WWF Title Match at Wrestlemania, is to put me six feet underground in a wooden box…..and hell, I’d probably dig my way outta that, crawl my way to Wrestlemania, Stun Bret Hart, Stun Brian Pillman, and STILL walk out as the World Wrestling Federation Champion…..

…..Ric Flair and Arn Anderson, they’ve tried to put Steve Austin out before and the only thing they ACTUALLY managed to put out was their crooked old backs…..I don’t care who you put in front of me Kevin Kelly…..Arn Anderson, Ric Flair, that masked freak Mankind, Brian Pillman, Bret Hart…..I just don’t give a damn, because the fact remains the same…..Stone Cold Steve Austin is walking into Wrestlemania as the toughest summbich on the planet and he’s walking out of Wrestlemania with the World Wrestling Federation Title around his waist and that’s all I gotta say about that…..

…..so I suggest you straighten your little bow tie (you look like a stupid summbich by the way) straighten up your stupid little bow tie and walk your ass back on to Vince McMahons office, kiss his ass….and then tell Ric Flair and Old Man River to walk their geriatric old bodies to the ring…..”

…..Austin pauses for a moment to pick up a steel chair…..

“…..because their ass belongs to Stone Cold…..”

…..Kevin Kelly might have more questions to ask, but the only thing he’s going to get if he sticks around here is an ass-whipping…..so with that being said, maybe we should go down to ringside…..

Rating: A

…..for a return match from last night…..except this time the roles have been reversed, as Booker T puts his newly won European Championship on the line against former Champion Jeff Jarrett…..

…..Jarrett has been quite vocal already tonight, apparently believing that had it not been for the involvement of Sean Waltman, then he would still be standing here as Champion…..he’s already dealt with the Wolfpac, so winning his title back should be a doddle…..

…..right?…..

…..yeah, it won’t be that simple…..

…..Booker T might not have won the title last night the way he wanted to, but rest assured the former Harlem Heat man is a VERY deserving champion…..Booker has risen up the ranks here in the WWF at a remarkable pace and has been putting on some excellent performances week in and week out…..

…..these two men had an excellent match last night, they have a great match here too…..it’s not marred in quite so much controversy either, because a (clearly) confident Jeff Jarrett leaves the help in the back (with the exception of Ted DiBiase) while Booker T simply doesn’t want any help…..

…..he didn’t really need it either…..Jarrett (and Deebs) may have controlled a good portion of the match, but at the end of the day it was Booker who walked out with a victory, kicking his opponent off during a Figure Four Leg Drop attempt (sending him crashing into DiBiase on the apron) and gets met with a kick to the gut, followed by a hard ax-kick to the back of the head…..and three seconds later, Booker T retains his Championship…..and he does it as clean as a whistle.

Winner: …..and Still WWF European Champion…..Booker T…..
Rating: A

…..Jarrett decides not to stick around, telling a nearby camera that this was just a fluke win for Booker……Jarrett had expended too much energy taking apart the Wolfpac…..this was Sean Waltman’s fault…..AGAIN…..

…..yeah, keep telling yourself that Jeff…..

…..in the meantime, Dok Hendrix has made his way out in order to get word from Booker T on the challenge levied by Stevie Ray…..

“…..the question is Booker T…..will you put that title on the line against your own brother?…..”

…..Booker is quick to tell Hendrix that of course he will…..

“…..you know Dok, people make too much about the ‘brother vs. brother’ thing…..me and Stevie, we grew up fighting for everything we have, I’ve fought my brother more times than I can count…..if Stevie Ray wanted to fight me for THIS title, then all he had to do was ask……instead of that he went out and he stabbed his REAL brother in the back for a couple’a Wannabe G’s…..”

…..Booker says that he would have been HONOURED to face his brother at Wrestlemania…..even more so to be doing it for the European Championship…..but it seems like Stevie Ray doesn’t have any honour…..

“…..Stevie, you want a shot at this belt sucka, you GOT a shot at this belt…..and I’ll tell you what…..you wanna bring Ahmed Johnson…..Kama…..D’Lo Brown…..bring them too, bring Mark Henry…..because they might be a Nation…..but 110th Street is 110th street, and ain’t no Nation in the world that can stack up to that…..you sold out your brother for your ‘brothers’, now your ‘brothers’ can watch your brother whoop you like your daddy, you dig that sucka?…..”

…..I don’t know if anyone digs that…..or even understands it…..but it sounds like it’s official……Booker T will be putting the European Championship on the line at Wrestlemania…..and he’ll be defending it against his own brother Stevie Ray……

…..oh brother…..

…..let’s go to the back…..

Rating: A

…..since Dok Hendrix is already in the ring, we go backstage to Michael Cole, who’s standing by with the members of the Hart Foundation…..

…..Bret Hart looks to be in pretty bad shape (courtesy of taking the ride through the announce table last night) but he says it was all worth it, because he’s going to the main event of Wrestlemania, which gives him about five weeks to recuperate…..plenty of time…..

…..Cole says that since Chris Jericho has a match booked with Dean Malenko and Bret has his WWF Championship opportunity, that just leaves Test and Owen without any Wrestlemania dance partners……which is when the Intercontinental Champion cuts him off…..

“…..woah, woah, woah…..hold on there Michelangelo…..first of all, Test will be PLENTY busy at Wrestlemania…..he’ll be where he always is, right by my side, guarding this sexy body from all the gutter skanks in the crowd that are desperate to get a taste of some prime Canadian Bacon…..

…..and for the record, Chris Jericho (your hero and mine…..paragon of virtue) doesn’t just have A match…..he has THE match…..the match that’s going to see Bore-Us Malenko Jr finally pack up his boring, sensible clothes in his boring, sensible suitcase…..and run back home to Great Aunt Gertrude Malenko with his tail between his legs…..because Chris Jericho is going to embarrass him…..humiliate him…..defeat him…..and retire him…..mark it on your calendar folks, because after Wrestlemania you will never……eeeeeeeever…..see Dean Malenko again…..”

…..as for Owen (who actually looks pretty down in the dumps after losing to Steve Austin last night) Bret says not to worry about Owen…..

“…..Owen might have got screwed over last night Cole (Editors Note: He really didn’t Bret) but everyone knows that Owen Hart is twice the man that Steve Austin is…..and it’s only a matter of time before he’s wearing gold again in the World Wrestling Federation…..as for Steve Austin, judging by what Vince McMahon said earlier this evening, Steve Austin won’t even MAKE IT to Wrestlemania…..so Brian Pillman, that’ll just leave you…..and me…..

…..Brian Pillman, everyone knows that you’re Vince McMahon’s chosen Champion…..his little stooge…..and trust me, I know better than most what it means to be screwed over by one of Vince McMahon’s pets…..you might have Vince McMahon on your side…..Ric Flair…..Arn Anderson…..Mankind…..all of them, nothing but mercenaries…..while Steve Austin, he’s got NO ONE on his side…..”

…..Bret pauses for a moment to look around the room, smiling at Jericho and Test, patting Owen on the back and telling Cole that he…..he has FAMILY by his side…..

“…..family by my side….and at Wrestlemania…..I’ll have the WWF Title around my waist…..”

Rating: A*

…..we go to commercial just before our main event, but before we do we see a brief 30 second split screen of Steve Austin walking down a hallway with a steel chair in hand, while Ric Flair and Arn Anderson walk down a separate hallway on the other side of the screen…..tyre iron in Arn’s hand…..and then when we come back, the glass shatters, the crowd explodes…..it’s main event time…..

…..Vince McMahon has certainly piled the pressure on his WWF Tag Team Champions here…..he wants Steve Austin taking out…..he wants Steve Austin taking out tonight…..and if he DOESN’T get what he wants, The Nature Boy and The Enforcer will have to go one better than Brian Pillman, because they will have to defend those Tag Team Titles against FOUR teams…..

…..not the best odds…..

…..anyway, back to this match…..

…..if Steve Austin truly wants to become the next WWF Champion, then he’s going to have to know how to defeat a champion (to be the man…..you know the rest) and despite his advancing age, there’s no doubt that Ric Flair is one of (if not THE) most decorated World Champion in recent history…..he’s also got a hell of an incentive to take out Austin here (not to mention the assistance of The Enforcer in his corner) but that doesn’t help him initially, because The Rattlesnake is all over him in the opening portion of the match…..

…..Flair knows how to sell (clearly) and bumps and feeds every punch that Austin throws (and there’s a lot of them) before finally retreating to the outside…..where Stone Cold follows him, continuing the offence and even putting Anderson down to the concrete with a running clothesline…..

…..the Nature Boy finally gets a reprieve thanks to his tag team partner though, because as Austin looks to deliver a piledriver right there on the arena floor, Anderson draws the Rattlesnakes attention just long enough that Flair is able to backdrop his way out of it and it’s Austin that eats the concrete…..

…..now Flair goes to work, ‘Woooooooo-ing’ like a madman as he works over the knee that he and Arn previously assaulted back at the Royal Rumble…..Vince McMahon wants Stone Cold out of commission and he wants him out of commission tonight, so Flair stretches, torques, tweaks and bends the knee, as well as distracting the official so that Anderson can reach under that bottom rope to slam the bum knee off the ring apron and the ring post…..

…..Austin has vowed that these two (horse)men are going to have to literally put him six feet under to stop him tonight, and even that won’t stop him from going to Wrestlemania…..so try as he might, Flair just can’t keep Austin down…..bum knee and all, the Rattlesnake keeps fighting back, eventually levelling the Nature Boy with a Thez Press and a flurry of fists…..

…..Anderson rolls into the ring behind Austin, but the Rattlesnake sees him coming, kicks him in the gut and then drops him with a Stunner…..Flair is groggily (and slowly) getting back to his feet, but the Rattlesnake is waiting…..and he’s waiting with a second Stunner…..

…..which doesn’t connect…..because Mankind comes rushing out from the back, spins Austin around…..and locks on the Mandible Claw…..

…..that’ll be a disqualification…..

Winner: Steve Austin (via Disqualification)
Rating: A

…..hey, McMahon didn’t say that Flair had to WIN here…..just that Steve Austin had to leave the arena in no condition to perform at Wrestlemania…..if Mankind keeps his fingers lodged into the gullet of the Rattlesnake much longer then he’s not going to be in any condition to do ANYTHING, let alone perform…..and while Anderson might still be recovering from the affects of that Stunner, Ric Flair is heading to the outside to pick up a steel chair…..

…..:::::!!!!!GONG!!!!!:::::…..:::::!!!!!PYRO ERUPTS!!!!!:::::…..

…..but after a rather frustrating evening for the Deadman and his brother (after failing to find Raven) The Undertaker and Kane are here….and they’re here for Mankind…..

…..Paul Bearer made it known that the Big Red Monster had his sights set on the Masked Mankind, so as he steps over the top rope that’s exactly who Kane sets his sights on, grabbing Foley by his scraggy hair and dragging him free of Austin…..

…..with Kane otherwise occupied, the Deadman turns to Flair in order to work some (Raven related) frustrations out of his system, knocking the former multi-time World Champion on his ass with a series of blows…..

…..Anderson is back to his feet and charges across the ring (picking up that discarded chair on his way) and smashing it across the back of the Deadman…..it barely phases the Undertaker though, who’s head snaps around like a B-Movie Monster to glare at the Enforcer…..

…..that clearly didn’t work…..

…..if at first you don’t succeed (etc, etc) so Anderson backs up a few steps and comes again, this time looking to smash the chair into the skull of the Undertaker…..who gets a Big Boot up, smashing Arn’s own chair into his face…..

…..Flair has snatched up Arn’s tyre-iron in the meantime and he stalks up behind the Undertaker…..but Steve Austin isn’t down…..and despite the Mandible Claw he just sustained, he’s certainly not out either, spinning Flair, kick to the gut…..Stunner…..Flair is done……

…..Arn reaches under the bottom rope and drags Flair to the outside, just as Kane sends Mankind tumbling through the ropes as well to the floor…..and with The Undertaker, Kane and Stone Cold Steve Austin standing tall in the middle of the ring, Vince McMahon’s Corporate Guns decide that the best option is to make a hasty retreat…..

…..unfortunately for them, Steve Austin looks to be very much still in condition to perform at Wrestlemania…..looks like our WWF Triple Threat Championship Match is still very much set……and now, because of that, so is the Fatal Four Way WWF Tag Team Title match…..

…..goodnight folks…..we’ll see you next week…..

Rating: A*

Final Rating: A*
