WCW Monday Nitro: Week 1, August 1997

Posted 31 May 2019

Week 1, August 1997

Orlando Arena (Orlando, Florida)

ATT: 16,515

TV: 18.05

Announce Team: Tony Schiavone, Eric Bischoff and Mike Tenay

It’s Titans of Wrestling time, as the dastardly heel group make their way down the aisle in mass……and they don’t look in a particularly good mood……at least Russo and Hogan don’t…..and if Hollywood Hogan isn’t happy then nobody is happy…..the group climb into the ring where Russo tells these fans to first of all, shut their mouths……and second of all, open their ears……because the boss has something to say…….obviously that generates a fairly large chorus of boo’s, but Russo continues regardless…….

……he looks around at the people surrounding him, before saying that he thought he had made himself very clear…..that where it comes to the Titans of Wrestling, he expects results……last week he was fairly confident that The Enforcer and Jean Paul Levesque could get the job done…..beat Sting and the Warrior……and get rid of those two face painted morons…..but no……and now it’s going to be down to Hollywood and The Macho Man to do the job that those two couldn’t…..fortunately, Hogan and Savage are the two biggest icons in professional wrestling…..bar none……and they will easily wipe the floor with the mute and the space cadet…..

”…….but that’s not the point bro……Hollywood Hogan and The Macho Man Randy Savage shouldn’t be having to clear up YOUR mess……so Ray Traylor…..you get another chance to redeem yourself tonight, because I’ve booked you in the main event…….against Sting…….”

…….The Enforcer seems happy enough with that……Levesque less so, asking what he’s supposed to do? Russo tells him that if he can’t take care of the big boys, he’s arranged a slightly easier opponent for him…..Diamond Dallas Page…….see if Levesque can’t snuff that little problem out before it gets any bigger…….if he can handle it that is…….if not……..well, there are plenty of Titans in waiting……all Vince Russo has to do is pick up the phone and make it happen……because like he said……unless your name is Hollywood Hogan or Randy Savage……pretty much anyone is replaceable…..

……Levesque looks far from pleased with that public dressing down……but he keeps his mouth shut…..for now……as Hogan takes the mic…….he says that just leaves one more piece of business tonight…….

”…….and that’s the Warrior dude……see, that face painted freak show is getting too big for his boots brothers…..and it’s time he was taken down a peg or two…….and like Mr. Russo said, it’s time to earn your colours tonight…….I want the Warriors head served up on a silver platter……I want him bloody……I want him battered……I want him broken into a thousand pieces man…….I want the Warrior……or MAYBE I’m going to be looking through Mr. Russo’s little black book, and finding someone that CAN give me what I want…….”

……clearly all is not rosy in the Titans garden right now……and that can only spell problems for WCW.

Rating: A

VS.

Big Van Vader & Bam Bam Bigelow vs. Mike Awesome & Gideon Cain (w/Missy Hyatt)


We open up tonight with Tag Team action……as the monsters of WCW continue to line up in an attempt to get their hands on Mike Awesome……tonight it’s Vader and Bam Bam Bigelow’s turn……but Awesome isn’t alone…….he’s got a little bit of back up in the form of the gentleman who debuted last week……

……Mike Tenay shows us why they call him the Professor, informing the audience that he spoke to Missy Hyatt just today, and he can confirm that this young man’s name is Gideon Cain……and just like Mike Awesome, he wants to prove that he is the next generation of big man, by systematically taking apart ANYONE that says differently……..(incidentally, did you know that young Horshu/Luther Reigns here wrestled on the Indys as Max Impact? Inspector Max Impact to be precise…….do you know how badly I wanted to keep that piece of crap name just so I could give these two the team name of Awesome Impact……buttttt, Russo is in control here, and we all know what he thinks of “outdated gimmicks”……and since Luther Reigns is so 2003, Gideon Cain it is)…….

……considering that young Gideon here is pretty much fresh out of the Power Plant, this match wasn’t half bad…..obviously Cain was the weak link (performance wise we had Vader A*, Bigelow A, Awesome B+ and then Cain with a D+) but being that it was a Tag Team match we were able to hide his limitations pretty well…..and the match would probably have been even better recieved, had the main eventers won…….

…….as it was however, a p!ssed off Giant made his way out…..with Lex Luger trying to keep the man calm……as the two made their way down the aisle…….that caused Vader to completely take his eye off the match……which left his partner all alone to eat an assisted Powerbomb combination from Awesome and Cain……and as Mike Awesome made the cover, this one was in the books.

Winner(s): Mike Awesome & Gideon Cain
Rating: B

Again, while the combination of Vader and Bam Bam Bigelow, and the pairing of The Giant and Lex Luger SHOULD be focusing their attention on Mike Awesome and Gideon Cain, it’s each other that they are pushing and shoving…….and a full on brawl almost erupts here……with Bigelow furious that the Giant stuck his nose into the match…..but also somewhat peeved that his partner left him high and dry……and as tensions flare and tempers erupt in the ring, Awesome, Cain and Missy make their way triumphantly up the aisle, with huge smiles on their faces as we go to a commercial for Road Wild, which is less than two weeks away.

Rating: A

VS.

The Renegade vs. Konnan


It’s time for The Renegade to once again entertain his dozens of fans……this time against Konnan……The Renegade actually picked up a victory last week……this week not so much…….as the same masked man that assaulted him following his victory over Craig Pittman makes his way out again tonight, once again with his advisor in tow…….distracting The Renegade long enough for Konnan to lock on the Tequila Sunrise, giving his opponent little choice but to submit.

Winner: Konnan
Rating: C-

And just like last week, the action doesn’t stop just because the Renegades match has, as these pair enter the ring, and the man clad in green proceeds to assault our face painted hero…….this week as the assault takes place, the associate takes a microphone……declaring himself to be James Vandenberg, the mouthpiece and the mastermind behind this man…..Mortis…..a man who is going to systematically dismantle the entire WCW……starting with The Renegade…….why the Renegade?……because James Vandenberg strives for nothing short of purity and perfection…….and this man here, is nothing more than a second rate attempt to recapture the essence of another…….a Warrior clone built on deception and lies…..unpure……unworthy and unwanted…..and Mortis is here, to cleanse the mistakes of the past…….

…….with that Mortis drags The Renegade to his feet, before positioning him for the Flatliner and driving his skull into the mat, as Vandenberg looks on laughing in the background…….someone scrape the Renegade off the mat, because he’s done folks.

Rating: D

VS.

Rick Martel vs. Earl Robert Eaton


Rick Martel will be challenging Lord Steven Regal for the WCW Television Championship in just 13 days at the Road Wild…..with the agreement in place that it shall be one on one, with the Blue Bloods remaining in the back…….with that being said, it’s the Earl that Martel is facing tonight……and much like at Road Wild there is to be no outside influences…..because while The Duke, Douglas Williams does accompany Eaton to the ring, the official is quick to remove him from ringside and send him back to the locker rooms……much to the outrage of Earl Robert…..

…….these are two veterans of the Ring right here, and while neither man can exactly be accused of being in their prime, they’re still good enough to put on a decent showing……this is all about setting Martel up to face Regal however, and the former Model picks up the victory win the Boston Crab to build his momentum going forward.

Winner: Rick Martel
Rating: B-

Mean Gene is out to shill the Hotline next, urging all the great WCW fans to call right now for ALL the rumoured new arrivals around here…….before introducing WCW United States Champion Eddie Guerrero down to the ring……

……Eddie makes his way out with a sneer on his face and the US Title strapped firmly around his waist, standing arrogantly beside the Gene…….who says that Eddie has been on quite the roll as of late…..what’s next for Eddie Guerrero?…..

…….Eddie says he’s been on more than just a roll……he’s been on fire…..so much so that he can’t get no competition around here…..everyone is avoiding him esé……Gene scoffs at that, stating that as long as he has that United States Title around his waist, he sincerely doubts that ANYONE is going to be avoiding Guerrero……in fact, he’s probably got half the locker room gunning for him……..

”……..is that so?……where are they then esé?……do you see a line that I don’t?…….do you see Diamond Dallas Page rushing to try and regain this belt?……you see Chris Benoit?……Sting?…….Lex Luger?……you don’t see nobody vato……you know why?….because everybody knows that as long as Eddie Guerrero has got this belt, they don’t have a chance……..”

…….Eddie says that it’s getting boring……so much so, that he’s offering an open challenge right now to anybody in the back…..Gene is taken back by this revelation, asking…..

”……an open challenge?……”

“………an open challenge…….”

“………to anyone in the back?……..”

“……..that’s what I said esé………”

“……..for the title?………”

“……..for a CHANCE to have a shot at the title……..”

……..Eddie says that anyone that wants to come out here right now and face him, bring it on…….and if they win, he’ll even give them a shot at the US Title at Road Wild…..which is when some peculiar music begins to play…….

………as this masked unknown makes his way to the ring to a rather unenthusiastic ovation…….Eddie looks unimpressed too as the new comer hits the ring, asking Gene who the hell this is? Gene has no idea…….and does the sensible thing……ask……..to which the new arrival announces (in an unusually high pitched voice) that his name is Willow the Wisp……a jobber name if ever you heard one…….and Eddie obviously agrees, telling an official to get out here and ring the bell so they can get this over with…….

Rating: B+

VS.

Willow the Wisp vs. Eddie Guerrero


Oy Veh…….there may be no Yiddish people in this match but I can’t think of a better term for the crowds disappointment at an open challenge being accepted by a masked nobody…….they get even more apathetic as Eddie cockily assaults this newcomer…..who barely puts up any fight what so ever……until he does……out of nowhere……with a sudden flurry of moves that look oddly familiar……that take Guerrero completely by surprise……and after taking the US Champs head off with what looks suspiciously like a Whisper in the Wind, Willow picks up an utterly surprising and upset victory, after hitting Guerrero with a Swanton Bomb……

Winner: Willow the Wisp
Rating: B

Eddie was taken completely by surprise there……and clutching at his ribs he complains loudly that he wasn’t ready……restart the match, he wants a do over……obviously the official was never going to do that…..instead he raises Willow’s hand in victory……

……a clearly irate Guerrero tells the newcomer that he got lucky……and he may have a shot at the United States Title at Road Wild, but there’s no way that a nobody like him can defeat Eddie Guerrero twice…….Willow simply cocks his masked head at Eddie……before reaching up to pull of his mask………

”…….IT’S JEFF HARDY!……JEFF HARDY IS HERE IN WCW……WHAT A MONUMENTAL NIGHT FOR THIS GREAT SPORT……”

……alright Schiavone, calm down……although in fairness the crowd do pop big for the former WWF star (who incidentally is a MUCH bigger star in WCW than he was in the WWF……how much bigger? Auto push says Upper-Midcard……that’s a huge step up from the Lower-Midcard he was Auto-Pushing up north)…….

…….Guerrero looks on in shock……saying no, no, no……he’s been duped…..conned……this is highly unfair…….unfortunately a deal is a deal……and Jeff Hardy WILL be facing Eddie Guerrero in just 13 days at Road Wild, for the WCW United States Championship.

Rating: B+

VS.

Bill Goldberg vs. Alex Wright


It’s that time again……and the announcers are really starting to get all aboard the Goldberg Train…..with Bobby Heenan in particular impresses by the big guys recent form……tonight’s victim is Alex Wright……and you know the formula by now…….Spear……Jackhammer……Splat…..

……I don’t think Mr. Wright was actually too pleased at doing the job here……in fact, it looked suspiciously like he was considering telling the Booker man “Nein”……at least until Goldberg cracked his knuckles in his direction, the young German soon piped down then……he even gave the rookie his best match to boot…….your winner, Bill Goldberg.

Winner: Bill Goldberg
Rating: C

VS.

Diamond Dallas Page (w/Kimberly) vs. Jean Paul Levesque (w/Buff Bagwell)


It’s time for Jean Paul Levesque to prove his worth around here, as Vince Russo has tasked him with taking out Diamond Dallas Page tonight before the man becomes too much of a problem…..and while Page is accompanied by the gorgeous Kimberly, Levesque also has an accomplice…..although it’s not any of the Titans of Wrestling……it’s Buff Bagwell…..although after the Titans (and mainly Tripl….oops, Levesque) handed him a victory last week, it looks like Buff is keen to return the favour……although I’m sure he wouldn’t say no to entry into the exclusive group……

…….this match was pretty good, and pretty much went exactly how you would expect…..Bagwell interfered on numerous occasions (when he wasn’t too busy actively flirting with Kimberly) and it seemed like DDP was fighting a loosing battle against two men………

………at least until Chris Benoit (the man on the receiving end of Levesques Pedigree last week) made his way down to deliver a good old fashioned beating to Bagwell……

…….the distraction from which was enough to take Levesques eye off his opponent…..giving Page plenty of time to measure him up for the Diamond Cutter and dropping the man the moment he turned back around…….and even though it took some convincing (and protecting…..AND keeping strong) Levesque puts Page over, for possibly the biggest win of his career so far.

Winner: Diamond Dallas Page
Rating: B

Not that he gets to celebrate for long…..because the Enforcer is out from the back, nightstick in hand to nail Page right in the ribs……Levesque doesn’t sell the Diamond Cutter for long (obviously) and is soon back to his feet to join in on the three on two assault of DDP and Benoit……until suddenly the crowd erupts…….

……because Sting is out…..and he’s standing in the entrance way, bat in hand……

……not that he looks particularly happy about having to make the save……but at least Page and Benoit haven’t been going out of their way to help him after his little “leave me alone” speech a month or so back……he also appears to be checking behind him for someone…..and since The Warrior is nowhere to be seen, you’ve got to assume he’s wondering where he is……

……eventually he’s seen enough……and while he doesn’t exactly run, he certainly walks briskly to the ring, where he nails Bagwell in the kidneys with the bat, holding off Levesque and Traylor just long enough for Benoit and Page to regain their composure long enough for the pair to get back into the fight…….as the two men brawl all the way to the back with Levesque and Bagwell, leaving Sting and Traylor all alone to focus on their match……which is up next.

Rating: B+

VS.

Sting vs. Ray Traylor


So it’s Sting against Ray Traylor……The Enforcer, The Big Bossman, Big Bubba, The Boss, The Guardian Angel……damn, this guys had more gimmicks than Brutus Beefcake……anyway……Russo is looking for Traylor to take Sting apart here tonight after taking the pinfall last week that granted Sting and The Warrior their shot at Hogan and Savage at Road Wild…….Russo is going to be disappointed……because he ate the loss tonight too……after Sting dropped him on his head with the Scorpion Death Drop.

Winner: Sting
Rating: B

Sting picks up the victory…..but his music is cut off by the arrival of Vince Russo, who makes his way out on the ramp……he’s not happy, telling Traylor to get his ass to the back, he’ll deal with him later, before turning to Sting……he tells the Stinger that he must be feeling pretty pleased with himself……he got the win last week……he got the win tonight……and he and Warrior have got The Mega Powers of Professional Wrestling at Road Wild…….

”……well I’ve got news for ya bro…..that’s where your fairy tail comes to an end……because you might THINK you want Hogan…..you might THINK you want Savage……but what you don’t understand bro, is that you simply can’t hang with the Mega Powers of Professional Wrestling……and Hollywood and The Macho Man, they’re gonna year you apart……”

“……..is that right?…….you mean like Ray Traylor tore me apart just now?…….I tell you what, since you’re so confident there…..how about you put your money where your mouth is……..and at Road Wild……if I pin Hogan……if I make him submit…….Hogan, not Savage…….if I beat the WCW Champion…….I get Hogan next month……and I get a shot at that World Title…….”

……the crowd pops for that…..Russo doesn’t look convinced however…….and Sting sees it too, asking him what’s the matter, not so confident after all?……Russo weighs the options up for a minute, before regaining his cocky composure, telling Sting he’s on…….

”……if by some miracle you can beat the biggest icon in professional wrestling, Hollywood Hogan in that match……then you get a shot at the title…….BUT…….but bro, if Hollywood Hogan beats YOU……then you don’t get a shot at that title for as long as it’s around Hollywood’s waist……and believe me, that’s going to be for quite some time……”

……Sting doesn’t take quite as long as Russo to answer…..but he still pauses for a beat before finally agreeing……..to which Russo looks surprisingly happy……telling Sting that he just made the biggest mistake of his life……because it’s not just Him and Hogan out there……it’s Savage and The Warrior too……and The Warrior……he might not be in any condition to help……

”…..after all, it’s like they always say…..if you want something done right, do it yourself……”

…….with that the big screen, the Turnertron if you will flickers to life…….to show Hollywood Hogan and The Macho Man standing over the seemingly unconscious body of The Warrior somewhere in the back……Hogan starts cackling like a madman, telling Sting that he’s just signed his own death warrant…..because at Road Wild, he’s going to find out exactly what the Warrior Dude just found out…….and that’s the fact that you just don’t mess with the Mega Powers of Professional Wrestling jack……

…….Savage pulls out a can of spray paint, proceeding to spray T.o.W across the massive back of the Warrior in black paint…..as the show comes to an end on a close up of Sting’s now worried expression…..just 13 days away from Road Wild.

Rating: A

Final Rating: B

Final Thoughts

Absolutely nothing to report this week…..other than the fact that Jeff Hardy has really helped the WCW locker room by being such a pleasure to be around……only for Hollywood Hogan to undo all his good work, by turning half the locker room against the Giant…….good god Hogan, first Savage and now The Giant

WCW Monday Nitro: Week 2, August 1997

Posted 1 June 2019

Week 2, August 1997

The Cajundome (Lafayette, Louisiana)

ATT: 11,694

TV: 18.18

Announce Team: Tony Schiavone, Eric Bischoff and Mike Tenay

We are just six days away from Road Wild…..and just six days away from Sting and The Warrior facing off against the Mega Powers of Professional Wrestling…..Hollywood Hogan and Macho Man Randy Savage…….both of whom are making their way out now, along with the rest of the Titans of Wrestling…….and after the assault of the Warrior last week, and Sting’s agreement that should Hollywood defeat HIM at Road Wild in that Tag Team Match, then Sting will not be granted a WCW Championship Match for as long as Hogan has the belt, the WCW Champion appears to be in a very good mood……obviously the fact that Russo agreed that should Sting defeat Hogan in that tag match he gets a WCW Title shot next month, isn’t even factoring into his mindset right about now……

……the group enter the ring as a unit and its Vince Russo that takes the microphone, saying that all is good in the camp of the Titans…….the Warrior has been taken out……Sting is GOING to be taken out……the Nasty Boys are going to regain the WCW Tag Team Titles from the Road Warriors (if they know what is good for them)……and to top it all off, as a VERY special treat tonight…….Hollywood Hogan, the biggest Icon In all of Professional Wrestling…..is going to lace up those boots, and put the WCW Championship on the line, for the entertainment of all you morons in the crowd…….

”…….see, while Hollywood will be in Tag Team competition on Sunday, the old men sat on the WCW Championship Committee have DEMANDED that Hogan put the gold on the line tonight……..but those old farts didn’t speculate WHO our man Hollywood had to face…….so I’ve signed one of the hottest free agents in the sport to face Hollywood…….a man who has held Championships in every promotion he’s been in bro……..a man, who once he passes his initiation tonight, will be the NEWEST member, of the Titans of Wrestling……….”

”…….Curt Hennig……”

…….the rest of the Titans stand applauding as the former Mr. Perfect makes his WCW debut…..the name might be different but there’s no mistaking that cocky swagger (and gum swat) as the newest member of the WCW roster makes his way down the aisle and in to the ring, where he shakes hands with all of the Titans……

……Russo pulls a contract out of his pocket, telling Hennig that here is his (very lucrative) future right here……a brand new WCW contract just waiting for his signature……

”……which I will hand to you personally to sign…….AFTER tonight’s main event……because Hollywood will be putting the title on the line…….and you know what you have to do…….right?….”

……Hennig tells Russo not to worry, he knows EXACTLY what he has to do tonight…..because he’s here to add a little bit of perfection to the Titans of Wrestling, before giving a little wink and shaking Hogans hand, as Tony Schiavone bemoans the fact that Hennig is clearly going to take the dive tonight, giving Hollywood an easy victory just six days away from Road Wild.

Rating: A

VS.

Jeff Hardy vs. VK. Wallstreet


Our first match of the evening sees WCW newcomer Jeff Hardy in action, fresh off his surprise reveal and victory over Eddie Guerrero last week……a victory which grants him a shot at the WCW United States Championship at Road Wild, a fact that Eddie Guerrero is none to happy with…..but then that’s what happens when you go around making open challenges……

…..Hardy’s opponent tonight is perennial jobber to the stars VK Wallstreet, a solid if unspectacular performer who will give anyone a decent match, which is exactly what this was….and as Schiavone fawns over Hardy, telling us what a coup it was to land this talented youngster, Jeff puts his opponent away with the now familiar Swanton Bomb.

Winner: Jeff Hardy
Rating: B

Hardy mounts the second turnbuckle to celebrate in front of these fans that have obviously taken to him……..which is when the United States Champion comes skulking down the aisle like the Repo Man……he slowly rolls into the ring and unhooks his title belt from around his waist and clutching it like a weapon, slowly advancing on the unsuspecting Jeff Hardy…….

…….the crowd does their best to warn Jeff, and like a true pantomime villain Guerrero places his finger on his lips, encouraging the fans to quiet down…..Hardy obviously got the message however, because quick as a flash he steps up to the top rope and comes flying off with a Whisper in the Wind that almost takes Guerrero’s head off……

……Eddie drops the US Title as he rolls to the safety of the outside, leaving Jeff alone in the ring to pick up the strap and hold it high in the air for these approving fans….as Schiavone tells us that if Jeff Hardy can defeat Eddie Guerrero one more time at Road Wild, he will be the new WCW United States Champion.

Rating: B

VS.

The Renegade vs. Scott Norton


Straight back to the action now, as The Ultimate Warrior Lite, everybody’s favourite paint covered performer (What? Don’t laugh…..there’s a kid out there somewhere that loves……) the Renegade, goes up against the true Sherman Tank of a man, Scott Norton and his hugely barrelled chest…..

……I’d like to say this was a pretty even affair, but that would be a lie…..Flash beat the snot out of the Renegade (ha, suck on that Renegade you stroppy little sh!t) with little real retaliation from the (Big Baby)face….and when Renegade DID finally begin to show a little bit of offence, he was quickly distracted by the arrival of Mortis and his manager James Vandenberg, who make their way down the aisle…..

…..not that Norton really needed the assist, finishing off the hapless Renegade with an inverted shoulder breaker for the three count.

Winner: Scott Norton
Rating: C-

In somewhat of a reoccurring theme, the masked Mortis makes his way into the ring, with his microphone wielding manager following him in…..Mortis drags the Renegade to his feet, and holds him in place so that Vandenberg can look the man in the eyes as he tells him that he warned him this would happen…….he warned him that imperfections would no longer be tolerated here in WCW…….Vandenberg reaches out and rubs his hand over the painted face of the Renegade, attempting to wipe the red and yellow colours (what’s left of them anyway) away, telling him that he is nothing but a cheap imitation…..and imitation is NOT the most sincere form of flattery…..it is merely the instruments of the unoriginal…..before finally telling Mortis to finish the imposter off for good…..

…….Mortis spins his victim around and sets him up for what appears to be a Flatliner……accept the Renegade has apparently had enough of the constant abuse and attacks, breaking free from the hold with a series of elbow strikes that rock the masked man, before unloading with a number of right hands that back his attacker up against the ropes……

……Renegade backs up and sends Mortis flying over the top with a running Clothesline, before turning to focus his attention on Vandenberg, who immediately bolts and joins his man on the outside…..as the two would be attackers retreat to the back, leaving the Renegade to beat his chest in the middle of the ring.

Rating: D

VS.

Rick Martel vs. The Duke Douglas Williams


Last week it was Earl Robert Eaton stepping into the ring with veteran Rick Martel……this week it’s his partner, the Rookie Duke, Douglas Williams who has the pleasure of tangling with Martel, less than one week before the former Model meets the head of state of the Blue Blood family, his Lordship, Steven Regal (even though technically…..and I may be wrong…..I think a Lord would be below and Earl…..and a Duke above them both but hey ho) for Regal’s Television Championship……

…….Regal has guaranteed that both Eaton and Williams will not be a factor in their Road Wild clash…..just as Eaton and Regal are not a factor here tonight unfortunately for Williams, because he really could have done with the help…..maybe then Martel wouldn’t have had him submitting to the Boston Crab……and for Rick Martel, that’s two Blue Bloods down, one to go……the one with the Television Title.

Winner: Rick Martel
Rating: C+

To the back we go now…..and from Television Title implications, the focus switches to the Tag Team Title’s, as Champions The Road Warriors are backstage with Mean Gene…..Gene says that it would appear that Vince Russo has set his sights on bringing those WCW Tag Team Championships back into the gold of the Titans of Wrestling……and he’s made it clear that the Nasty Boy’s position within the group may be at stake should they fail to win at Road Wild…….and that in itself should make for some very desperate….and very dangerous opponents……

…….Animal doesn’t agree, shouting at Gene that the only two dangerous men in that match, are standing right here…….he says the Road Warriors are the Tag Team Champions, and the Road Warriors are going to stay the Tag Team Champions, no matter how desperate the Nasty Boys are……”tell ‘em Hawk”……

”…….WELLLLLL…..MEAN GENE……..IF YOU ASK ME…..THE ONLY THING DANGEROUS ABOJT THE NASTY BOYS……IS THEIR BODY ODOUR…….IN FACT, I’M A LITTLE JEALOUS OF THEM GENE…….”

“………jealous?…….of the Nasty Boys?……”

“…….JEALOUS…….YOU KNOW WHY?…….BECAUSE AFTER ME AND ANIMAL KNOCK THEIR NOSES SIDEWAYS ACROSS THEIR STINKING FACES, THEY’RE NEVER GONNA HAVE TO SUFFER THE STENCH OF THEOR PUNGENT ARMPITS EVER AGAIN……BUT THEN, AFTER THIS MATCH…..THEY’RE NEVER GOING TO SUFFER ANYTHING…..BECAUSE WE’RE GONNA PUT THEM DOWN LIKE THE MONGREL DOGS THEY ARE……..UHHHHH……WHAT A R………”

…….hold that thought……..

……or that catchphrase at least…….because Knobbs and Saggs, the Nasty Boys come flying into shot with a couple of steel chairs…..that they smash across the backs of the Road Warriors with a sickening crash…..before showing EVERYONE just how desperate and dangerous they are, as they each lay about ten shots each on both Hawk and Animal respectively, before throwing the steel to the ground, telling Hawk and Animal that one way or another, those Tag Team Titles are coming home with the Nasty’s this Sunday night.

Rating: B

VS.

The Soul Train (w/Teddy Long) vs. Mike Awesome & Gideon Cain (w/Missy Hyatt)


Time for Tag Team action now…….and as the Soul Train took on the intimidating combination of Mike Awesome and Gideon Cain, we actually learned something pretty interesting in this match…….well, two things actually, but one of those has obviously been a note before that I missed……because Missy has managed Awesome during a match before now, I just obviously didn’t notice the note that the duo had some good chemistry as a pairing…….which is fortunate, because Awesome really does need a mouthpiece……..but not only that, we found out this evening, that Mike Awesome and Gideon Cain have some EXCELLENT chemistry as Tag Team Partners…….which is even better…..because while I DIDN’T have plans with them as a full time team, they were clearly going to have to team on occasion…….although with this Tag Team display, maybe they will find themselves as more of a permanent fixture in the Tag Team ranks in the future…….and that excellent chemistry, is what probably helped the pair tonight, after finishing off the enormous Ice Train with an assisted Powerbomb.

Winner(s): Mike Awesome & Gideon Cain
Rating: B-

…….there’s no tags in this one, it’s tornado rules as these four men proceed in beating the holy hell out of each other, and the action spills out to the floor practically instantly (leading to Bobby Heenan remarking that WCW moving the announce desk from ringside and putting it way up here out of the way is the best thing they ever did)……hold that thought though Bobby….because while these men start off battling at ringside, it’s not long until they are going all over the building…..INCLUDING up by the announce desk (where Bobby Heenan throws down his headset and gets the hell out of dodge until the coast has cleared)…..

…….the Nasty’s get more offence in here than they have in their last two meetings with the Road Warriors combined, as they use all manner of weapons at hand to nail Hawk and Animal…..Knobbs even drills Hawk with a Piledriver to he concrete floor…..although he probably should have known better….because everyone knows Hawk is (bizarrely) immune to piledrivers, and he bounces right back to his feet…..Knobbs charges at the now standing Hawk, but gets backdropped straight through a nearby table…..

…….that takes Knobbs out of the equation long enough for Animal to get the upper hand on Saggs, and both Warriors toss the lone Nasty Boy back into the ring….Animal hoists the Challenger up onto his shoulders, and Hawk comes flying off the top as the Road Warriors deliver the Doomsday Device to their opponent……Animal makes the cover, and three seconds later the Champions retain…..and as far as the Titans of Wrestling are concerned, the Nasty Boys are in big, big, trouble.

Winner(s): ……and Still WCW Tag Team Champions…..The Road Warriors
Rating: B

The match is over and the Titans of Wrestling are out immediately…..surprisingly however, they don’t give the Road Warriors a second glance, marching straight past the WCW Tag Team Champions who look as surprised as anyone, clearly expecting a fight….when it’s obvious that Hogan and co. aren’t here for them however, they simply shrug their shoulders and head to the back, as a rather uneasy looking pair of Nasty Boys find themselves surrounded…..

…..Knobbs and Saggs tell the Titans to calm down, telling Russo that they nearly had the match won (they really didn’t) and that they just need one more chance…..Vinnie Ru is having none of it however, grabbing a microphone and telling the Nasty Boys to…….

“……shut up……SHUT UP…….I think I’ve made myself pretty clear around here…..this is the TITANS of Wrestling…..not the three time Losers of Wrestling……the Elite…..the best of the best…..you wanna stand shoulder to shoulder with Hollywood bro, you need to be pulling your weight…..and Boys, you just ain’t cutting it…….”

……Knobbs clearly realises that he’s fighting a losing battle with Russo and turns his attention to Hogan, telling him that they’ve been friends for years, that must count for something…..much like Russo however, Hogan isn’t interested, telling Knobbs that…….

“……the Titans of Wrestling isn’t about friendship brother…..it’s about success……Hollywood Hogan and the Macho Man Dude, we’ve been up and down that road a thousand times, we’ve fought, we’ve laughed…..but more importantly we’ve made money together brother…..lots of money…….and right now, you’re not making us money, you’re just making us a laughing stock, and Hollywood Hogan doesn’t find that funny…..the Macho Man doesn’t find that funny……and you’d better believe that the Warrior doesn’t find that funny dudes……”

……to be fair, this new Dark Warrior doesn’t look like he finds anything funny, as he stands looking rather intense in the background……Russo takes the microphone, telling the Nasty’s that they had one job…..bring home the Tag Team Gold……and they couldn’t even do that……

“…….but it’s ok, it’s ok…..you live and you learn…..see sometimes, name value alone doesn’t cut it anymore……sometimes, those past accomplishments are all you have left…….and sometimes……you need to put the old dogs down, to bring the new blood in……..because while this group right here before you features the biggest names in wrestling EVER, we’re not just about star power……we’re about building for the future……out with the old, in with the new……blood in……..blood out……..”

…..which is when two familiar looking blonde haired gentlemen come bursting into the ring, nailing both Knobbs and Saggs from behind…..Tony Schiavone is totally blown away by these new arrivals, and while he is careful not to mention them by name, is is certainly not shy about alluding to their former identities……

…..the rest of the Titans stand back and watch the beating unfold…..some with glee like Ray Traylor who is clearly delighted at seeing some good old fashioned violence…..some with disinterest like the Warrior, who is standing there as though all of this is beneath his time…..either way it’s obviously our new arrivals initiation, and as the two newcomers hop to the outside to pick up a couple of steel chairs, it looks like it is indeed going to be blood in for the blondes……and blood out for the Nastys…….because as the now former Titan Tag Team stand to their feet, the two former employees of Titan Sports each raise a steel chair, swinging simultaneously at first Knobbs, then Saggs…..a Con-Chair-Co if you will……

……the Nasty Boys both slump to the mat, and each man has indeed been busted wide open….Russo grabs the microphone again, reminding everyone that he TOLD them that all he had to do was make one phone call, because everyone is replaceable…..

……Traylor rips the Nasty Boys Titan shirts off, rolling the pair onto their fronts, before Hogan hands the two newcomers a spray-can each……and in a now familiar scene, the show ends with a close up of two battered and bloodied bodies…..with three letter painted across their backs in black paint…….T……..o……..W……..goodnight fans.

Rating: A*
