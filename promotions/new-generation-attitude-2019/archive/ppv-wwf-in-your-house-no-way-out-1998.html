A New Generation…..a new attitude? Pay-per-view

WWF In Your House: No Way Out 1998

Posted 18 January 2020
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.

The blog’s images are not served; the posts’ text is given whole, as the author wrote it.

Week 3, February 1998
Tacoma Dome (Tacoma, Washington)
ATT: 23,000
PPV BUY: 1.22
Announce Team: Jim Ross, Jerry “The King” Lawler & Ravishing Rick Rude

…..it’s Pay Per View Sunday here in WWF Land, which means The King has polished his Crown specially and two become three, as Jerry Lawler joins the Raw is War pairing of Rick Rude and Jim Ross at the commentary table, to welcome us to WWF In Your House: No Way Out…..

…..the trio welcome us to the show, promising a blockbuster night on this final outing ahead of Wrestlemania…..and after tonight is over, we should have a much clearer view of who will be heading into that much coveted Wrestlemania Main Event, because we have THREE matches tonight with World Title ramifications, including our WWF Title match later this evening in which the Corporate Cannon, Brian Pillman, puts the gold on the line against the brother of the man he took that Championship off to begin with, the Big Red Monster…..Kane…..

…..then there’s the man who’s Wrestlemania status shouldn’t even be up for discussion, because he won himself a guaranteed spot in the main event by virtue of winning the 1998 Royal Rumble…..that man being Stone Cold Steve Austin…..

…..Steve Austin’s Wrestlemania spot is very much up for discussion though…..because while Vince McMahon won’t out and out strip The Rattlesnake of his prize, he has no qualms in putting him into a situation to LOSE that prize…..and tonight, that prize is indeed on the line, when Steve Austin goes one on one with Owen Hart…..

…..and then there’s our third match…..the match in which Bret Hart puts his own guaranteed title opportunity on the line, against a man he knows very well…..Big Sexy, Kevin Nash…..this is a match that (should Bret win) will see the Hitman slotted into our Wrestlemania Main Event, giving us the possibility of our first ever triple threat Wrestlemania main event…..

…..such won’t be the case should Big Sexy pick up the victory though…..because if Kevin Nash defeats Bret Hart here tonight, then TOMORROW night on Raw is War, we will see the WWF Title defended, as Nash goes one on one with whoever walks out of this event as the World Wrestling Federation Champion…..

…..oh…..

…..and that match is next…..

Rating: N/A

WWF Championship: #1 Contenders Match

…..it’s not often that you start a Pay Per View with a match featuring TWO former WWF Champions, but that’s exactly what we’re doing here, as Kevin Nash and Bret Hart face off one more time, with The Hitman’s #1 Contenders spot on the line…..

…..obviously these two men know each other very, very well…..they spent the best part of 1996 and 1997 locked in a bitter feud that saw both men cost each other matches, title opportunities, blood…..it’s even seen alliances shift, with Kevin Nash (and the KLiQ/Wolfpac) going from dastardly heels to fan favourites, and vice versa for The Hitman (and The Hart Foundation)…..in fact, with this match right here, that marks the fourth consecutive year that these men have met on a Pay Per View stage, stretching back to the King of the Ring 1994…..

…..like I say, they know each other very, very well…..

…..with that being said, we perhaps know what to expect when these two meet…..power from Big Sexy, while The Hitman does his best to chop that big redwood down to size…..remarkably (considering the stakes) both men come out alone for this one, but then when you consider that Scott Hall, Owen Hart and Chris Jericho all have matches of their own to worry about (while the last time we saw Sean Waltman he was still wearing a neck brace from the assault at the hands of the Million Dollar Corporation) that’s perhaps not much of a surprise…..even so, Jim Ross is sceptical…..

“…..because King, you know as well as I do that where Bret Hart goes, the Hart Foundation go…..and after putting it all on the line here tonight to ensure that his baby brother gets another opportunity to become the WWF Champion, you’ve got to assume that Owen Hart is keen to repay the favour…..”

“…..if Owen Hart has any sense at all JR, he’ll be staying in the back to prepare for his own match…..because take it from me, the best way for Owen Hart to repay the favour to Bret, is by taking Steve Austin’s Championship opportunity right out from under him…..”

“…..not to mention the fact that Bret Hart KNOWS he can beat Kevin Nash Lawler…..he knows it because he HAS beaten him…..and Jim Ross, you can take it from me, when you have taken a man’s title away from him before (just like Bret Hart did in 1995) you know that you can beat them ANY time…..”

…..while Rick Rude’s words there are perhaps true, it was actually losing the WWF Championship to Bret Hart back at Survivor Series 1995 that actually kickstarted the Kevin Nash we all know today…..as the babyface World Champion Diesel, Nash had perhaps lost his killer instinct…..he lost his edge…..but it came back with a vengeance on November 19th 1995, where “Diesel” delivered quite possibly the first ‘table break’ in WWF history, propelling The Hitman off the apron and through the announce table, as well as assaulting Hart post match and Jackknifing him for his troubles…..

…..and THAT’S the Kevin Nash we have tonight…..because after constantly getting shortchanged in his efforts to become a two time WWF Champion (not to mention Scott Steiner costing him the Royal Rumble victory) Big Sexy is on the attack from the second the bell rings…..and while this match isn’t being held under no-disqualification rules, the referee is certainly being lenient with the competitors, because Nash COULD have been disqualified a number of times, leading JR to question whether the official has received a mandate from Vince McMahon…..

“…..because Vince McMahon made it clear that he does NOT want his Corporate Champion involved in a triple threat match at Wrestlemania…..a victory for Kevin Nash tonight guarantees that we won’t see that happening…..”

…..and as Nash drives Hart spine first into the ring post on the outside, you have to wonder if that’s the case…..although when you consider the fact that if Kevin Nash DOES win, the WWF Champion will have to meet him tomorrow night on Raw, that triple threat possibility maybe doesn’t sound so bad…..because Nash is in a bad, bad mood here…..

…..eventually (after taking an almighty ass-kicking) Bret is able to catch a breather, taking out the knee of the near seven footer with a chop block, before focusing his offence on the knee of Big Sexy…..he kicks, stomps, wrenches and stretches the limb of Nash, as well as wrapping it around the ring post a number of times, but Big Sexy will not be denied…..and even on one leg he hobbles his way back into this one, seemingly marching straight towards a shot at that World Title…..

…..there’s even a throwback to that 1995 match between the pair…..

…..because as Bret tries to escape the onslaught of Nash rolling under the bottom rope looking for a rope break, Big Sexy charges across the ring and shoulder barges Bret right off the apron…..

…..where he crashes through the announce desk once more…..

…..the Hitman lands hard as he explodes through the wood, and as he lies broken under a heap of table fragments and monitors, Jim Ross urges the official to end the match…..

“…..because by god, whether you like Bret Hart or not, he’s a human being that just got broken in half…..”

…..it looks like the official is considering it too…..which is when the rest of the Hart Foundation come rushing out from the back in an effort to stop him…..

…..finally (after a lot of persuasion from Owen, Jericho and Test) the official starts his mandatory ten count, deciding to give Bret at least a fighting chance of saving this match, while Kevin Nash stares down at the carnage he caused…..

…..which means that neither he, nor the official (who’s busy counting to ten) sees the arrival of Big Poppa Pump Scott Steiner…..who slides into the ring behind Nash with that lead pipe in hand…..and as the Hart Foundation help Bret out of the wreckage, he nails Big Sexy right in the back of the head with that lead pipe…..

…..Nash drops to the mat unconscious (as Steiner flips him a middle finger and disappears just as quickly as he arrived) and despite the protests of the official, the Hart Clan rolls Bret under the bottom rope (at the count of nine) leaving the Hitman with little to do but to wearily drape one arm over the chest of his opponent…..

…..and three seconds later, Bret Hart is heading to Wrestlemania…..

Winner: Bret Hart
Rating: A

…..the Harts leave Kevin Nash out of it in the middle of the ring, while Owen, Jericho and Test help a battered and beaten Hitman up the aisle…..Test leads the way, keeping a close eye out for other Wolfpac members, while Owen and Jericho each drape one of Bret’s arms over their shoulders, walking the victor to the back…..

…..they get to the entrance way and are met by Michael Cole, who comes rushing out from the back looking for some words…..he almost gets more than he was bargaining for too when Test advances on him, but Bret calls the Bodyguard off telling Cole to ask his questions…..

…..Cole says that by hook or by crook Bret Hart is going to the main event of Wrestlemania, but remarks that this display (and his victory as a whole) was hardly reminiscent of a Champion…..

…..Bret is clearly in a lot of pain, but he has enough left in him to tell Cole that he is the total epitome of what a champion is…..he has the will, the desire AND the heart of a champion…..he just got driven through a damn table and he STILL kept fighting……

“…..and now…..just like I told the world I would…..I WILL be going to the main event of Wrestlemania…..and later tonight, when Owen kicks the crap out of Stone Cold Steve Austin…..so will my brother…..and THEN, whether it’s Kane or Brian Pillman, that WWF Championship is guaranteed to come back to where it belongs…..

…..Canada…..”

…..it’s just about that time that Kevin Nash starts to stir…..rolling over onto his front with a p!ssed off look on his face, clearly seething as he begins to get to his feet…..Jericho clocks that first, telling his team mates that the Big Bad Wolf is about to huff, puff and blow a gasket…..

…..Test shoves Cole to one side, telling him that the interview is over, as the Hart Foundation quickly disappear to the back and we go to a promo package highlighting the history between Faarooq, Bradshaw…..and the Nation of Domination…..

Rating: A

…..because our tag team match is next…..

…..it’s safe to say that Bradshaw has been looking forward to this one…..he had to earn it too…..because after weeks of trying (and failing thanks to Nation interference) Bradshaw finally picked up a victory against a Nation member…..Scorpio…..meaning that he got to pick this match AND his opponents…..

…..when he announced that he was choosing to face Scorpio and (the leader of the Nation) Ahmed Johnson here tonight, that in itself wasn’t overly surprising…..after all, he’d already defeated Scorpio once and had been trying to get Ahmed back in the ring for weeks now…..what WAS surprising, was his choice of tag team partner…..because while Johnson clearly thought that Nelson would be the logical choice (given that the two had teamed previously) it was actually Faarooq that Bradshaw chose…..

…..that’s ‘Former Leader of the Nation of Domination’ Faarooq by the way…..

…..Faarooq who hadn’t been seen since his violent excommunication from the Nation, courtesy of Ahmed Johnson’s hostile takeover…..

…..and he’s been waiting for his revenge ever since…..

…..this match was pretty much what you would expect…..smash-mouth offence with a sprinkling of aerial offence from Scorpio…..it wasn’t great, but there was plenty of heat behind it…..Faarooq and Bradshaw came out strong, with Scorpio in particular getting tossed around like a rag doll…..they didn’t walk over Ahmed Johnson quite so easily (after all, you don’t become a WWF Champion by being a walkover) but after disposing of Scorpio on the outside there was only so much the Walking Armageddon could do against two men…..

…..of course, with that being said Ahmed was never going to be down for long…..and the fact that Kama was out there on ringside helped to cause enough of a distraction that Ahmed was able to blindside Bradshaw and give the Nation the advantage…..

…..not that it was a LONG advantage (to be fair, this match itself wasn’t overly long, we have a LOT to get through tonight after all) and while Ahmed did well when he was the legal man, Scorpio got flattened with a big lariat that (had Bradshaw been able to capitalise on) would probably have lead to the victory…..

…..as it was it lead to the hot tag…..

…..and Faarooq came in swinging…..

…..the former Nation leader went straight after the CURRENT Nation leader…..and while Faarooq was dismissed from his position for essentially running from Nelson, he’s certainly not running here…..at least not away anyway…..he IS running straight for Johnson though…..

…..Faarooq is able to not only hold off Ahmed, but Scorpio too (who gets backdropped straight over the top rope and to the floor) long enough for Bradshaw to regain his senses and rejoin the action…..

…..it all got a bit messy from there…..

…..the match descended into a brawl…..and it was a brawl that saw Bradshaw link up with Scorpio inside the ring, while Faarooq and Ahmed battled it out on the outside…..and ON the outside, Faarooq used the Nation to his own advantage, reversing an Irish Whip attempt to send Ahmed Johnson crashing into Kama…..

…..that was OUTSIDE the ring…..

…..INSIDE the ring Bradshaw decided to utilise that Clothesline from Hell a second time…..this time turning Scorpio completely inside out…..and with both Ahmed and Kama down on the floor, there was no one to break up the pin attempt as the official counted one, two and three…..

…..this one is over…..

Winner(s): Faarooq & Bradshaw
Rating: B

…..victory in the bag, Faarooq and Bradshaw head to the back, as Mark Henry and D-Lo Brown make their way out to join the rest of the Nation in the ring…..

…..Ahmed doesn’t look particularly happy at this decision and he grabs a microphone from ringside, before telling Kama to get Scorpio back to his feet…..

…..he tells his Nation Brother that while he is a lenient leader, his patience will only stretch so far…..he says that it was Scorpio’s loss that lead to this match even happening tonight…..then he lost to Nelson…..THEN he lost again tonight…..

“…..again…..that’s three for three…..now when the Light Heavyweight Title was a thing around here, you actually had a use…..you had a purpose…..but now, what use are you now boy?…..three matches, three losses…..if this was baseball you’d be out…..unfortunately for you this ain’t baseball…..and you ain’t out…..

…..you DONE…..”

…..and with that, Kama almost breaks Scorpio’s neck with a brutal Clothesline to the back of his head…..

…..then the beatdown begins…..

…..this isn’t a new sight…..this has been the way that the Nation has dealt with failure since it’s inception…..Bobby Knight…..Nelson…..even their one time leader Faarooq learnt that when you outlive your usefulness to the Nation, they discard you in the most violent way possible…..

…..Scorpio doesn’t stand a chance here…..in fact, after the initial attack he doesn’t even have chance to defend himself as he is stomped mercilessly into the mat…..things are looking VERY bad for Scorpio…..

…..so bad in fact that even Bradshaw and Faarooq have seen enough…..and they come rushing out from the back with steel chairs in hand…..

…..and they aren’t coming alone…..they have the Half Ton Monster, Nelson beside them…..

…..the trio hit the ring looking to put a stop to the punishment…..luckily for Scorpio their mere presence was enough to do the trick, because as Bradshaw, Faarooq and Nelson enter the ring, Ahmed calls off the troops, telling them that they made their point, leaving the trio to tend to Scorpio in the middle of the ring, while the Nation make their way to the back…..

…..one member lighter than when they entered…..

…..talk about sore losers…..

Rating: A

…..speaking of sore losers…..we quickly head backstage from there…..and Kevin Nash is a VERY sore loser…..

…..we aren’t in the room with The Wolfpac, but we don’t need to be either…..we CAN see the trio courtesy of a long shot from the hallway through their open locker room door, but even if we couldn’t see it we would certainly hear it…..because Kevin Nash is currently going berserk…..

…..chairs are getting thrown…..lockers are getting dented…..a monitor comes flying through the open doorway…..and there are a few choice words being thrown about as the censors have to go into overload…..

…..eventually a chuckling Scott Hall comes walking out of the room (followed by a neckbrace wearing Sean Waltman) and as the pair clock the camera, he tells its operator that he “might want to back it up mang…..because Big Kev, he ain’t in a good mood Chico”…..

…..that might be the understatement of the century…..because as Hall and Waltman walk out of shot, telling the camera that they’re going to give Kev a little bit of time to cool down, an almighty crash is followed by Kevin Nash screaming Scott Steiners name…..

…..Big Poppa Pump might have made a very big mistake…..

…..let’s follow Hall and Waltman’s example and give Big Sexy a bit of alone time to cool down shall we…..

…..to ringside we go…..

Rating: B+

…..where it’s time for another match…..this time one that’s had much less build than our previous two matches, but one which promises to be no less intense, as after spending months cast in little more than a speaking role (during his feud with Jake Roberts) Raven looks to continue his recent winning ways against former Intercontinental, European and Tag team Champion, The British Bulldog Davey Boy Smith…..

…..Raven has his eye on SOMEONE here in the WWF…..quite who that is has t been made clear yet, but it’s definitely NOT Davey Boy…..the Bulldog on the other hand definitely has his eye on both Raven and Jake Roberts, after a post match assault from Raven resulted in The Snake choking the life out of Davey with an electric cable…..

…..while we’re talking about Jake, The Snake accompanies Raven to the ring for this one (as he has done since Raven finally crushed his spirit at the Royal Rumble) a fact not lost on Davey Boy, who looks distracted by the presence of Roberts on a number of occasions throughout the match…..

…..this was another good match, that could perhaps have been great given a little more time and build…..as it was, Davey Boy completely dominated the opening portion of the match, until Jake’s presence paid dividends that is…..Bulldog spent a few moments generally assaulting Raven on the outside (including dropping him face first on the ring steps) but as he rolls his opponent back into the ring, Raven grabs at the leg of the official, distracting him from the fact that The Snake nailed Davey Boy with a throat thrust to that already weakened wind pipe…..

…..from there Raven went on the attack, with a lot of his offence focused on that throat area…..he even tests the officials patience, choking Davey Boy numerous times on the ring ropes…..he fully utilises Jake Roberts too, often tossing the Bulldog to the floor (and distracting the official) so that the Snake can get his licks in…..

…..eventually though Davey Boy is able to regain the control of the match, powering out of an Evenflow attempt with a backdrop, before levelling Raven with a running Clothesline…..both men stay down as the official delivers the mandatory ten count, but both are back to their feet by eight, with that little bit of breathing room being all that The Bulldog needed…..he begins trading right hands with his opponent, eventually driving Raven back into the corner where he delivers a series of shoulder thrusts to the midsection…..

…..Bulldog whips Raven hard (chest first) into the opposite corner and charges in behind him…..Raven was anticipating that though and uses to ropes to attempt to leap out of the way…..

…..problem with that, was that he leapt straight into position for the Running Poweslam…..

…..Raven literally lands on the shoulder of Davey Boy and the Bulldog isn’t about to waste an opportunity like that…..fortunately for Raven, Jake Roberts hops onto the apron and grabs the ankle of Raven, pulling him free…..Bulldog spins around looking to catch hold of his opponent again…..spins right into a kick to the gut and an Evenflow DDT…..

…..short trip…..bad landing…..Raven wins…..

Winner: Raven
Rating: B

…..Bulldog is done…..Raven isn’t…..

…..Jake Roberts joins his colleague in the ring, handing him a microphone as Raven shuffles backwards into the corner…..Raven lifts the microphone to his lips but pauses for a minute, instead motioning towards Roberts…..

…..Jake looks down at Raven with a sick smile on his face, before turning to focus on the Bulldog…..Davey Boy is still down from that Evenflow, but that’s not going to stop Jake, who pounces on him like a wild animal, delivering a savage attack…..

…..that on its own would probably be enough, but not for Jake…..Roberts briefly heads to the outside where he collects a steel chair…..a steel chair that he tosses down onto the mat before dragging Davey Boy back to his feet…..

…..and DDTing him right into the unforgiving steel…..

…..NOW Raven decides to speak…..he says that the biggest threat to face humanity isn’t war…..it isn’t famine…..disease…..it isn’t other people…..

“…..it’s distraction…..distraction is the threat to end all threats because distraction is the procrastination of genius…..distraction is that which separates the ‘can do’s’ from the ‘can do…..later’s’…..distraction is the lynchpin of failure…..Davey Boy Smith, tonight, you were little more than a distraction…..a distraction from my true calling…..a distraction from my destiny…..a distraction that’s over…..Bulldog, it’s ok….I forgive you…..you’ve paid for your sin…..

…..but now that the distractions are over, the end game is in sight…..I know you hear me calling to you…..don’t allow distractions to derail your journey home to the dark…..come to me, for I can set you free…..

…..come to me…..

…..or I’ll come to you…..

…..Quote the Raven…..

…..Nevermore…..”

Rating: A*

…..from there we head backstage, where Dok Hendrix is standing by with the man that will be challenging Chris Jericho next for the Intercontinental Championship…..Rey Mysterio…..

…..Rey isn’t alone though, he’s standing here with the man who defeated him this past Monday night, Dean Malenko…..although as Dok Hendrix reminds us (and motions towards a nearby monitor for his guests to watch the replay) it wasn’t clean…..

…..Rey and Malenko watch the footage, first of Test taking out the knee or Mysterio, followed by Dean locking on the Texas Cloverleaf to give him the submission victory…..and yes, while it was a tainted victory, it’s clear from the footage that Dean Malenko had no idea that Test had weakened his opponent…..

…..Rey (who does appear to have his knee heavily wrapped up) doesn’t hold a grudge though, telling Dok that obviously Malenko isn’t going to be celebrating that win, he has too much integrity for that…..Chris Jericho and Test handed him that victory and tonight, Rey is going to get his own revenge, by taking the WWF Intercontinental Championship…..

“…..they HANDED me the victory?…..what, you don’t think I could have defeated you otherwise?…..”

…..Mr. Malenko clearly has a little bit of a chip on his shoulder here…..after weeks and weeks of taunting, belittling and being out and out insulted by Chris Jericho, Malenko isn’t going to give that win up easily…..

…..he tells Rey that no, that’s not how he wanted to beat him…..but he DID want to beat him…..and that’s exactly what he did…..now tonight, Malenko wishes Rey all the luck in the world, but as far as he is concerned, if Rey loses, then Malenko beat the number one contender and should be next in line for an Intercontinental Championship match…..if Rey WIN’S…..well, then Malenko just beat the Intercontinental Champion…..and should STILL be next in line for a shot…..

“…..so good luck…..but win, lose or draw, I’m coming for that belt…..and I’ll show Chris Jericho…..YOU…..and anyone else that doubts me…..that Dean Malenko is anything but a loser…..”

…..yeah, good luck Rey (I guess) let’s go down to ringside…..

Rating: B+

…..where it’s time for that match…..our first Championship match of the evening, as Rey Mysterio does indeed challenge the ‘Ayatollah of Rock’n’Rolla’ Chris Jericho for the Intercontinental Title…..

…..at one point this match would have been for the WWF Light Heavyweight Championship…..but of course, Chris Jericho put an end to that when he (rather spitefully) retired that title AND the entire division, simply because Dean Makenko had the nerve to call Rey Mysterio the best Light-Heavyweight in the business…..

…..who is or isn’t the best Light-Heavyweight in the business is redundant now, but Chris Jericho has certainly transcended that division regardless…..he was already well on his way to the top on his own, but after joining forces with the Hart’s AND taking an up and comer of his own under his wing (in Test) he is a genuine bonafide star, even defeating Scott Hall to capture this Intercontinental Championship to begin with…..

…..he’s also picked up a few tricks along the way…..such as how to dissect an injured leg…..

…..Bret (and Owen to a fashion) have been chopping guys down to their size for years now, usually because that opponent is bigger than them…..Rey Mysterio is never going to be accused of being bigger than ANYONE…..but he is quicker than most…..take out the leg and you take away his speed…..

…..you also take away 90% of his offence…..

…..it’s that speed and agility that gave Rey the advantage in the opening portion of this match…..Jericho is quick…..he’s certainly agile…..but Mysterio was running circles around him…..

…..was…..

…..because after rolling Jericho back into the ring, Mysterio looked to capitalise with a springboard attack from the apron…..unfortunately Jericho captured the officials attention just long enough for Test to clip Rey’s ankle as he leapt, causing the challenger to tweak his knee on landing…..

…..from there Jericho went to work and Cocky Jericho came out to play…..kicking and stomping at Mysterio’s leg between taunts and two footed shuffles, our Intercontinental Champion has changed quite a bit from his white meat babyface days…..Rey attempts to mount an offence a number of times, but every time he does Jericho (or Test behind the officials back) is there to cut him off, usually by again taking out the knee…..

…..Jericho gets a little bit overconfident after a Lionsault connects, choosing not to cover his opponent in the conventional manner, instead laying on his back with his arms crossed nonchalantly behind his head…..that nearly bites him in the ass though, because Mysterio has enough to hook the arms of the Champion and roll him into a crucifix type cover that very nearly secures him the match (and the Intercontinental Title) before a flustered Jericho finally gets his shoulder up at 2.99999999…..

…..with Jericho furious at almost being embarrassed, the Champion goes on the attack hard…..but it also makes him prone to mistakes and a second (missed) Lionsault attempts sees him hit nothing but canvas and from there, Mysterio mounts his comeback…..

…..he mounts it on one leg (still favouring the knee) but even with one wheel Mysterio can do more than most people could do with two…..he rocks Jericho with all manner of one legged manoeuvres, and very nearly picks up a victory after reversing a Powerbomb attempt into a Hurricanranna…..in the end though, the end of the match followed much the same story as the rest of the match…..

…..because after finding himself momentarily distracted by Test (while perched upon the top rope) Rey flew across the ring towards his opponent, who (thanks to the time bought by the distraction of Test) is able to move at the last second…..

…..Rey hits the mat hard, cries out in pain clutching at his knee…..and as Jericho locks on the Lion Tamer in the middle of the ring, Mysterio eventually has little option but to tap out…..

…..and the Hart Foundation are two for two…..

Winner: …..and Still WWF Intercontinental Champion….Chris Jericho…..
Rating: A

…..although the fact that his opponent has tapped out doesn’t seem to be good enough for Mr. Jericho, because as Test climbs into the ring (with the Intercontinental Championship over his shoulder) this Paragon of Virtue (your hero and mine) is showing no sign of releasing the hold…..

…..the official does his best to drag Jericho off of Rey, but all he gets for his troubles is grabbed by the scruff of his neck by Test and tossed roughly to the mat as Mysterio cries out in pain…..

…..as Dean Malenko comes rushing out from the back…..

…..the Man of 1000 Holds seems to have found a new one…..it’s a move mainly utilised in Mexico, called ‘El Wacko Los Backo’…..it’s not exactly a legal move either, because it involves getting a steel chair and swinging it as hard as possible towards your chosen victim…..

…..that’s exactly what he does here…..he doesn’t connect though, because while Malenko swings for Test first, Test just manages to avoid it before bailing from the ring…..

…..seeing his Bodyguard flee (and the chair wielding Dean Malenko) Chris Jericho decides that he’s inflicted enough punishment and HE bails too before Malenko can take his frustrations out on him, snatching his title from Test as the pair back up the aisle…..

…..Dean Malenko made it clear earlier that he was coming for that Intercontinental Championship…..if it wasn’t clear then, it is now…..because with Jericho watching on from the entrance way (arm raised…..title raised) Dean grabs a microphone to make the challenge official…..he wants Jericho…..and he wants the Intercontinental Championship…..

…..and Jericho finds that hilarious…..

“…..I mean, really?…..come on ‘Bore-Us’, I just squashed your little buddy there (a round of applause for Little Rey-Mundo by the way folks…..he tried, he really tried…..such a little trooper) I squashed you AND Rey a few weeks back on Raw (sure, Test was in the match too….but I did most of the work) I mean, why would I face you again?…..

…..let’s look at the facts ‘Bore-Us’…..you couldn’t win the European Title at the Royal Rumble…..I took the Light Heavyweight Title right from under your nose…..I took the Light Heavyweight DIVISION away from you…..we beat you on Raw…..let’s face it, the only time you’ve won a match recently was against Junior there, and THAT was only because I helped you win…..

…..you ‘Bore-Us’ are a LOSER…..Chris Jericho is a winner…..you want a shot at MY title, what do you have for me eh? Just stepping in the ring with you makes my stock drop quicker than Grandma Malenko’s Underoo’s after two glasses of cheap wine…..what can you offer that could POSSIBLY make me want to face you? And don’t say the Light Heavyweight Title, because that was a gift damn it…..c’mon ‘Bore-Us’…..whaddya got?…..”

…..what does he have? Obviously (as Jericho pointed out) not a lot…..although he does have one thing…..something only he can offer……c’mon Dean, whaddya got?…..

“…..my career…..”

…..ohhhh, that got Jericho’s attention…..

…..Malenko says that Jericho is right…..he DOESN’T have a lot to offer, but what he does have is his pride…..his spirit…..and (despite Jericho’s constant belittling) his self belief…..

“…..because no matter what you say…..what you do…..how you do it…..Chris Jericho, I KNOW I can defeat you…..and I’m willing to put EVERYTHING on the line to prove it……face me…..face me for THAT title…..and if I can’t beat you…..if I can’t become the WWF Intercontinental Champion…..I’m done…..you win…..it’s that simple…..”

…..Jericho loves that idea….LOVES it…..so much in fact, that he says the whole world needs to see that…..

“……so I’ll do you one better…..you’re on by the way……but I’ll do you one better…..it will be Chris Jericho, WWF Intercontinental Champion Extraordinaire, Ayatollah of Rock’n’Rolla, Paragon of Virtue, your Hero and mine…..against Bore-Us Malenko Jr……title vs. career…..

…..at Wrestlemania…..

…..from the sight of (a beer drinking) Steve Austin celebrating we head backstage, where a rather grim faced Vince McMahon (along with his chosen Corporate Champions) is standing by with Dok Hendrix…..and as Austin’s music can still be heard playing out in the arena, Hendrix says that the Chairman’s Night could yet go from bad to worse…..because while Flair and Anderson may have retained the tag team titles earlier this evening, McMahon’s Crown Jewel (Brian Pillman) may not have to worry about the fact that both Bret Hart and Steve Austin will be two thirds of the first ever WWF Title Triple Threat Match at Wrestlemania…..because he has to defend that title right now……

…..against the Big Red Monster…..Kane…..

…..McMahon is silent for a moment, before telling Hendrix that he didn’t get to the position of power that he’s in today without knowing how to plan for all eventualities…..he says that Brian Pillman defending the World Wrestling Federation Championship in a triple threat match at Wrestlemania is hardly an ideal situation, make no mistake…..

“…..it WILL be Brian Pillman defending the World Wrestling Federation Championship in a triple threat match at Wrestlemania, because tonight (just as he was against his brother at the Royal Rumble) Brian Pillman WILL be victorious against the so called “Big Red Monster” Kane……”

…..Pillman cackles at that, telling Dok Hendrix that his victory tonight is an absolute inevitability…..

“…..see Kane might be big…..he might wear red…..but I’M the monster that the monster checks under his bed for…..Kane, you may envision yourself wearing this WWF Title around your waist, but your vision is obviously as damaged as your fire scarred face, because this isn’t the age of the boogeyman…..it’s the age of the anarchist…..it’s the reign of the crowned prince of chaos and you WILL kneel before the Ruler of the Revolution…..”

…..Pillman says that he ended the phenomenon of the Undertaker…..’Undertaker Red’ will find out that what big brother couldn’t do, baby brother will NEVER do…..because NOTHING and NO ONE is going to take this title from him…..not Kane…..not Bret Hart…..and certainly not Stone Cold Steve Austin…..

…..THIS Stone Cold Steve Austin…..the Stone Cold Steve Austin that walks into shot, sweat soaked and beer drenched following his match (and celebration) before coming to an abrupt stop and face off with Brian Pillman…..

…..there’s an awkward moment as the two men stare each other down, before both men break out in the most insincere smiles possible, as Austin pats Pillman on the shoulder and walking off…..but not before offering one simple sentence…..

“…..good luck out there…..partner…..”

Rating: A

…..which brings us to our main event and WWF Championship match…..

…..we already know that the World Wrestling Federation Championship will be defended in the main event of Wrestlemania in a triple threat match…..we know Bret Hart is going to be in that match…..we know Steve Austin is going to be in that match…..now it’s time to find out who is going to join them, as Brian Pillman puts the gold on the line against Kane…..

…..you could argue that Kane was the logical first challenger for the Corporate Cannon on the basis that it was his brother The Undertaker that Pillman took that title from in the first place (and we haven’t seen or heard of the Undertaker since incidentally) but in reality, whether Pillman had taken the title from the Undertaker, Bret Hart or the Brooklyn Brawler, Kane would have STILL been on a collision course with Brian Pillman, thanks to the Cannon’s “Pillmanization” of the Big Red Monsters ankle back in December…..Pillman put the Monster down and out of commission and now it’s time to pay for that attack…..

…..Brian Pillman may display the demeanour of a confident man, but that confidence is rocked within about three minutes of this match starting, because no matter what he throws at Kane, the Big area Monster seems completely unphased by it…..he drives a series of right hands into the masked jaw of the challenger, but Kane barely registers the blows landing before almost taking Pillman’s head off with a single blow…..

…..from there on in Kane (for lack of a better word) unleashes hell on Mr. McMahon’s Corporate Champion…..he literally throws him about the ring like a rag doll as he unleashes weeks and months of pent up aggression on the WWF Champion…..and Paul Bearer (who’s accompanied Kane to ringside) loves every minute of it…..after all, he too has felt the pain of Brian Pillman’s steel chair attack, quite literally getting beaten unconscious by the animalistic Loose Cannon…..

…..speaking of getting beaten unconscious, Kane is going a good way to returning the favour here to Pillman…..in fact the commentators (even the pro-heel Rick Rude) are questioning what, if anything the WWF Champion can do to halt the attack of his challenger, as Kane chokes the life out of Pillman in the corner…..he was probably risking disqualification to be fair, and had Paul Bearer not been there at ringside to remind Kane that the best way to hurt Pillman was to take his title, Kane would have probably continued to illegally squeeze the life out of him…..

…..he does break the hold though, and as Pillman desperately gasps for breath he rolls to the outside, where his first course of action is to deck Paul Bearer…..that ticks off Kane, who makes his way to the floor only to get his ankle (the one time crushed ankle) chopped out from under him….apparently the Nature Boy has been passing on his tips…..and NOW the Champion takes control…..

…..briefly…..

…..because although Pillman gets SOME offence in here, it comes to a screeching halt when the Corporate Cannon returns to his Light-Heavyweight roots, flying across the ring towards Kane with what looks suspiciously like an Air-Pillman, only to get caught mid-air by the Big Red Monster, who wraps a hand around the throat of Pillman, lifting him high and then driving him to the mat with a huge Chokeslam…..

…..then Kane runs a thumb across his throat…..looks like it’s going to be Tombstone time…..

…..which (much as it did the last time Kane had Brian Pillman in this position) brings out Flair and Anderson from the back…..

…..the last time they tried pleading with Kane not to do it…..it didn’t work then so they try a different approach here…..mainly Flair (who’s practically hanging off the official) and Arn distracting the referee, so that Mankind can slide into the ring behind Kane, undetected…..where he knocks Pillman free with a running blow to the back of the challengers head…..a running blow with that Tyre Iron of Arn Anderson’s in his hands…..

…..THAT puts the monster down…..

…..Mankind isn’t done though…..he scratches and claws at the mask of Kane until he has enough room to slide his fingers underneath, driving his fingers down the gullet of the Big Red Monster with the Mandible Claw…..

…..Kane is half out of it from the tyre iron blow, with the Mandible Claw just being the icing on the cake…..holding the Big Red Monster up at a dead weight Mankind shoves him backwards into the waiting arms of Brian Pillman and exits the ring, as the Champion drops the Challenger with the Time Bomb Hangman’s Neckbreaker…..

…..Flair and Anderson finally leave the official alone long enough for him to turn around to see Brian Pillman making the cover and in a rare sight (and with a LOT of help) Kane’s shoulders are pinned to the mat for the one, two, three…..

…..and NOW we know what our Wrestlemania Main Event will be…..

Winner: and Still WWF Champion, Brian Pillman
Rating: A

…..once the triple threat match was made a guarantee thanks to Bret Hart defeating Kevin Nash, and Steve Austin was announced as the second entrant after defeating Owen Hart, did anyone REALLY think that the third man was going to be Kane?…..

…..probably not…..but as Flair and Anderson raise the battered and beaten Champions arms in the air, there’s really no doubt now…..because it WILL be the Corporate Cannon Brian Pillman defending the WWF Title against Steve Austin AND Bret Hart at Wrestlemania…..and Kane? Well, it looks like Kane could be about to join his brother on the absent list, because with the Monster still down, Pillman orders his troops to put the boot in…..

…..and put the boot in they do…..

…..Mankind is particularly savage here as he works his hands back under the mask to lock in the Mandible Claw, while the Nature Boy and the Enforcer get their kicks in too…..and as Brian Pillman slides into the ring with steel chair in hand, it looks like things are about to go from bad to worse for Kane…..

…..:::::!!!!!GONG!!!!!:::::…..

…..at least until the lights go out…..

…..:::::!!!!!GONG!!!!!:::::…..

…..and the gong does it’s thing…..

…..:::::!!!!!GONG!!!!!:::::…..

…..and the lights come back on…..

…..to The Undertaker standing smack bang in the middle of the ring…..

…..Pillman’s eyes almost bulge out of his head when he sees the Phenom standing in front of him…..but Taker doesn’t just stand there…..he explodes as he swings at Flair, Anderson and Mankind, eventually driving all three men from the ring before grabbing at Pillman wildly…..

…..Pillman has bailed though…..he wants no part of the Deadman here…..and he backs his way up the ramp clutching the WWF Title to his chest, as his three cohorts join him in the aisle…..

…..the Undertaker looks like a man with revenge on his mind, and as he points at Pillman (before running a finger across his throat…..perhaps indicating Brian Pillman’s fate) the announcers state that the WWF Champion already has his hands full with TWO Wrestlemania opponents…..the last thing he needs is The Undertaker out for blood……

…..:::::!!!!!GONG!!!!!:::::…..

…..and the lights go out…..

…..:::::!!!!!GONG!!!!!:::::…..

…..and for a second time the gong does it’s thing…..

…..:::::!!!!!GONG!!!!!:::::…..

…..before the lights come back on again…..

…..to reveal Raven standing behind the Deadman…..steel chair in hand…..

…..the commentators proclaim that Raven has been calling out to someone for weeks now…..was the Undertaker that someone?…..

…..with Pillman and his partners staring past the Deadman it doesn’t take long for The Undertaker to realise that someone is standing behind him, so he turns around…..

…..and gets smashed straight over the head with the steel chair…..

…..it rattles the Deadman, no question about that, but it doesn’t put him fully down…..only down to a knee…..unfortunately that puts him in perfect position for Raven to hook the head and drive him into the mat with an Evenflow DDT…..

…..into the steel chair…..

…..and that DOES put the Deadman down…..right next to his brother…..

…..Raven rolls over and props himself up on his knees, looking down at the Undertaker for a moment, before pulling out a microphone from under his jacket…..and as Pillman looks on from the aisle, the show closes with a shot of Raven resting one hand on the head of the Deadman…..and delivering one closing line…..

“…..come to me…..or I’ll come to you……Quote the Raven…..Nevermore…..”
