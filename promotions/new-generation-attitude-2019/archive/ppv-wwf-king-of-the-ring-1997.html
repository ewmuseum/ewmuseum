A New Generation…..a new attitude? Pay-per-view

WWF King of the Ring: 1997

Posted 29 May 2019

The blog’s images are not served; the posts’ text is given whole, as the author wrote it.

Week 3, June 1997

Carrier Dome (Syracuse, New York)

ATT: 50,000

PPV BUY: 1.52

It’s time for the 1997 King of the Ring tournament….and what a night it should be…..the show starts with a promo video highlighting the events previous winners…..Bret Hart…..Owen Hart…..Mabel…..before glass shatters and we are treated to a highlight reel of Stone Cold Steve Austin winning last years King of the Ring, while a voiceover tells us how winning on this night can propel a star…..to superstardom……especially because the winner will go on to main event Summerslam and compete for the World Wrestling Federation Championship……

…..before we go down to ringside, where Jim Ross and Ravishing Rick Rude are waiting to welcome us to the show. They discuss the magnitude of this event, citing that superstars can be made in ONE night…..and if you don’t believe them, just ask the winner of last years King of the Ring, Stone Cold Steve Austin…..who tonight has the opportunity to climb to the very top of the mountain, to become the World Wrestling Federation Champion…..although according to Rick Rude, it will be the Corporate Cannon Brian Pillman walking out of here tonight with the WWF Title around his waist…..

Rating: N/A

VS.

King of the Ring: Quarter-Final Match

Kevin Nash vs. Ahmed Johnson (w/Jim Cornette)

Our opening contest, is our first quarter-final match of the evening, and it sees two men with more on their mind than simple glory in the King of the Ring……because while BOTH men want to win the tournament…..and BOTH men want the opportunity to become WWF Champion, both men also have their sights set on two other of tonight’s competitors…..the Heartbreak Kid Shawn Michaels, and The Undertaker……

……if you were expecting some kind of technical masterclass from these two, then you’re watching the wrong match…..these two don’t know a wrist lock from a wrist watch…..what they do know however, is how to pound the crap out of your opponent…..they’re both big, they’re both strong…..and while Nash does have the height advantage, he’s severely hampered by that bum knee, which sees him limping heavily down the aisle, and is a big flashing bullseye for Ahmed to attack…..

…..and attack he does, as basically as you can, kicking the knee out from under Nash and stomping it hard on the mat. Ahmed is strong we know that…..but I think we sometimes forget just HOW strong he is……this is a man that’s bodyslammed Kokina….and with much more finesse than some people….(I’m looking at you Lex Luger)…..so the sight of him manhandling Big Sexy shouldn’t come as a surprise, but it’s still an impressive sight none the less…..

……Nash does manage to claw his way back into the match a few times, even attempting to slam Ahmed, but each time he tries a high impact move his knee buckles and Ahmed is straight back on the offence….Rude even remarks that the best thing Nash could do here is concede the match and head home for a few weeks to fully rehab that knee, but Jim Ross says that if there’s one thing Kevin Nash is not, it’s a quitter…..and as if to prove his point, Nash chooses that exact moment to mount the first comeback of the match that doesn’t see his knee give way…..

……it’s not the best comeback in the world, mainly consisting of punching Ahmed square in the face…..but it’s effective…..and as the adrenaline starts to pump, Nash is able to put more and more weight on that bad leg, until finally he signals that he’s going to try for the Jacknife……of course Ahmed has something in his arsenal that Nash doesn’t……Jim Cornette…….who hops up onto the apron to distract the official away……

……from the fact that Shawn Michaels has come running down the aisle, sliding into the ring behind Nash, and in a Page right out of the Nature Boy’s playbook, chop blocks the knee of his former friend, before bailing quick as a flash, just as Ahmed wriggles free and nails Big Sexy with a Big Low Blow, before locking him to the mat for the one, the two and the three.

Winner: Ahmed Johnson
Rating: B

As medical personnel are assisting Kevin Nash in the ring, we head to the back where Dok Hendrix is standing by with Raven and the Flock…..DeMott, Daniels and the Dogs of War stand strictly behind their fearless leader, who is asked by Dok, exactly why he seems hell bent on driving Sid to the very point to psychosis? Raven looks at Hendrix in silence for a moment, before shaking his head and telling his interviewer that his simple mind couldn’t possibly begin to comprehend what Raven is doing for Sid……

”……everything I do…..everything I have done since my arrival here in the WWF has been for the betterment of others….Psycho Sid has already fallen down the rabbit hole into a pit of insanity…..and yet these people cheer him…..Psycho Sid is the living definition of the word paranoid……and yet these people cheer him…….Psycho Sid is on a fools errand, searching for angels in places that only monsters exist……and yet when I try and guide his path, these people jeer me…..and still cheer him……well what about me……WHAT ABOUT RAVEN?…….

……Psycho Sid is seeking a sanity for himself…..a peace within a storm…..he’s searching for the darkness with a flashlight…..or to put it in words that even you can understand, he’s seeking something that simply doesn’t exist…..Sid may well be damaged beyond even my repair….and yet these people continue to give him false hope…..because they cheer him…..and they jeer me……a prisoner locked inside the cells of his own delusions…..for his sake, I hope I can free Sid…..before they come to take him away……Quote the Raven…….Nevermore…..”

……Dok asks Raven exactly what he means by that…..before who takes him away? But Raven simply walks away, leaving Hendrix looking rather uneasy left alone with the Flock…..before he quickly sends us back down to ringside.

Rating: B+

VS.

King of the Ring: Quarter-Final Match

Psycho Sid vs. Raven

Our second match of the evening sees Psycho Sid facing off against a man in Raven who seems determined to to tip the scales of Sid’s sanity from Psycho all the way over to “One flew over the Cuckoo’s Nest” crazy…..and he’s doing a good job of it so far, as just last week he raised Sid’s paranoia to such a level that the Big Man actually struck a member of the audience…..and was arrested for his troubles……Sid was since released without charge (obviously, since he’s here) and spent time visiting with the gentleman that he assaulted…..but when it comes to this match, Psycho Sid’s head is anywhere but in the game……

……and if you’re Head is not in the game, Raven is the last person you want to be facing…..because the man is a master manipulator, and his game plan is obvious tonight…..rattle his much larger opponent into making a mistake…..because it’s safe to say that in a straight up fight, Sid has the size and the strength to put pretty much anyone down……but sometimes all the strength in the world can’t help you against an actual evil genius, and even though Raven comes down to fight this match alone, one by one the Flock slowly start to appear…..

……it starts with Christopher Daniels making his way through the crowd and taking up position in the front row……that rattles Sid…..followed by Bill DeMott doing the same…..and that makes three bodies that Sid’s attention is divided by……and when the Dogs of War appear too, Sid is completely distracted…..talking quietly to himself in between sudden fits of maniacal laughter…..and when all four Flock members climb over the safety rail to ringside, Sid snaps and drops to the floor, looking to take some of his paranoia out on them……

……before he can reach them however, all four men climb back over the barrier and re-take their positions in the front row and now Sid doesn’t know what to think……he eventually gives up and decides to refocus his attention on the match, but it’s too little too late, because as he climbs back into the ring he’s met by Raven and an Evenflow DDT……and just like that, this match is over.

Winner: Raven
Rating: B

The ring announcer goes to declare Raven the victor, but Raven has other matters to attend to first, rolling out of the ring to snatch the microphone from the startled employee, before sliding back under the bottom rope and sitting down in the corner….he waits for the big man to stumble to his feet, and as Sid looks about ready to snap, Raven simply raises the microphone to his lips, and announces in the most monotone way possible that…..

”……they’re coming to take you away ha ha…..”

……before tossing the microphone to one side and rolling to the floor, as a group of five men in white coats come charging down the aisle…..Sid stares at the oncoming tribe with confusion, but Rude clearly knows what’s up, stating that it looks as though the Funny Farm are here to claim a new patient….and Sid soon realises this as well…..especially when they circle him and it’s clear that one of the men has a straight jacket with them…..

…..they try reasoning with Sid, telling him that they’re only here to help him…..they’re not going to hurt him etc…..but Sid is understandably not particularly eager to comply…..and when he shuns these gentlemen’s advances, they warn him that they are authorised to take him by force if need be…….and that’s when Psycho Sid lives up to his name, absolutely snapping and beating the crap out of everyone, before delivering five big Chokeslam, one to each orderly, before bolting from the ring and heading to the back, eyes darting everywhere as his paranoia continues to grow…..as we go to the back ourselves…..

Rating: B

……where Scott Hall and Sean Waltman are attempting to restrain an extremely p!ssed off Kevin Nash, who is doing his hardest to ignore the advice of the medical personnel in the room, screaming that he’s going to rip Shawn Michaels smug little face right off and use it to wipe his ass with…..

…..Hall and Waltman are doing their best to calm their buddy down, but even on one leg Nash doesn’t look like he’s ready to call it a night…..Nash finally relents long enough to allow the terrified looking doctor to attend to his knee……but after Shawn Michaels cost him not only a shot at becoming the King of the Ring, but a shot at the WWF Championship at Summerslam too, quite how long Mr. Nash is going to be prepared to sit on his ass is another matter….but for now, it’s back to ringside…..

Rating: B

……where the Bizarre One, Goldust is making his grand entrance, along with his entourage Marlena and Marcus……the flamboyant trio enter the ring, where Goldust uses a microphone to declare…..

”…..’None of you will get my crown, I leave you nothing and I wish you plague’…..The Lion In Winter: 1968……”

…..before announcing that tonight is the night that Goldust finally gets his crown…..and what a radiant princess he shall make adorned with sparkles and jewels……so why doesn’t Cactus Jack come down here……so that Goldust’s Crown Jewel Marlena can embarrass him once more, before Goldust sits his golden derrière upon the throne of the WWF…….

…….obviously Cactus does come out…..he has a match after all……telling Goldust that he doesn’t know too much about being a King…..although he’s worn a few barbed wire crowns over the years……he DOES know however, that behind every great King……is a Queen…….

……and Marcus’s mascara clad eyes almost pop out of his head, as his former valet Sable makes her way out from the back to accompany Cactus to the ring……..and neither Goldust nor Marlena look particularly pleased to see the buxom blonde either…..but she’s here, and as Jim Ross remarks that this looks suspiciously like Beauty and The Beast, this one is underway….

Rating: B+

VS.

King of the Ring: Quarter-Final Match

Cactus Jack (w/Sable) vs. Goldust (w/Marlena & Marcus)

These two “gentlemen” have been feuding in and off now since shortly after Wrestlemania….Cactus found solace in the fans, and finally felt accepted in a world that has always shunned him…..and Goldust didn’t like that…..but then other than himself, Goldust doesn’t seem to like much……although he would certainly like that crown on his head and the Gold WWF Championship around his waist…..but to accomplish either of those things, first he needs to get past Cactus Jack…..

……Cactus in the other hand has got to contend with not only Goldust, but the presence of Marlena and Marcus on the outside……although he eliminates the threat of Marcus halfway through the match….because after the make up clad stylist of Goldust got too close to Sable one too many times, the blonde haired beauty slapped the blusher right off his face, before Cactus drive his skull into the concrete with a Double Underhook DDT, that takes him out of commission for the rest of the match…..

…..and this was a good match too….and it probably could have been great had it not been for time restraints…..Goldust has always been one of my most consistently reliable workers, and to be fair, so has Cactus…..and both men work their asses off here with a great back and forth battle…..but in the end, it’s the blonde on the floor that makes the difference……and no, not Marlena……although she does play her part, climbing up onto the apron looking to deliver another stinging open handed slap to the furry face of Cactus, as Goldust holds his opponents arms behind his back……before she can land the blow however, Sable yanks her down to the floor, and in the ensuing confusion Cactus is able to catch hold of the Bizarre One, driving him into the mat with the Double Underhook for the one, the two and the three.

Winner: Cactus Jack
Rating: B+

Cactus celebrates his victory as Goldust lays unconscious at his feet, when suddenly he is spun around, coming face to face with a furious looking Marlena, who proceeds to berate the amused looking Cactus……Marlena tells Jack to stop smirking that stupid toothless smirk, before she embarrassed him again in front of the whole world…….Cactus’s toothless smirk doesn’t get any smaller, so Marlena swings her arm back, ready to deliver another slap to the face…….but it’s a slap that never comes……because Sable is in the ring, and (clumsily) grabs hold of Marlena’s arm, spinning her round, and slapping the taste out of her mouth with an open handed blow of her own…….it’s not smooth, it’s not pretty…..but it pops the crowd, and really that’s all that matters, as the Beauty and the Beast celebrate Cactus’s victory, as we head to the back……

Rating: C+

…..where Dok Hendrix is once again standing by……this time with the former World Wrestling Federation Champion, the Heartbreak Kid Shawn Michaels…….along with the beautiful Sunny, The Nature Boy Ric Flair and The Enforcer Arn Anderson…..Dok says that in a few short minutes he’ll be walking out there to compete in his quarter final match……but if it wasn’t bad enough that his opponent is the Undertaker, it seems as though Kevin Nash has some unfinished business with the Heartbreak Kid…..the entire group laugh at that, with Michaels telling Dok…….

”……you’ve got to be kidding me little man……first of all, I don’t sweat the Undertaker……never have, never will……you saw the Royal Rumble right? The so called Phenom flat on his back while the Heartbreak Kid has his hand raised?…..”

“……you mean the match you won by count out, after Kane…………..”

“……don’t interrupt me……if you wanna talk so much answer me this…..the 1997 Royal Rumble, Shawn Michaels vs The Undertaker…….who won?……”

“………..you did………”

“…..that’s right……and…..who lost?…..”

“…………………the Undertaker…………..”

“……right again…….so, the Heartbreak Kid Shawn Michaels has got no problem handing the Deadman another loss to carve on his tombstone……and as for Kevin Nash…….”

……Shawn puts on a pronounced limp and hobbles around the set…….

”…….if Kevin Nash left NOW, he still wouldn’t make it down to the ring before I’d kicked the Deadman’s head of his shoulders and hopped, skipped and jumped all the way to the King of the Ring Title……and the WWF Title at Summerslam…..and if the rest of the little Puppy Pack want to try and stick their noses in my match……well, let’s just say I’ve got a little bit of back up of my own……..”

……Flair lets out a big Wooooooooooo, as Michaels and company leave, and Dok He drip sends us back down to ringside.

Rating: B+

VS.

King of the Ring: Quarter-Final Match

The Undertaker (w/Paul Bearer) vs. Shawn Michaels (w/Sunny)

It’s not very often that you’re going to see The Undertaker vs. Shawn Michaels plonked in the middle of a Pay Per View Event…..or ANY event for that matter……this is a main event match whichever way you look at it, and yet here we are, not even half way through the show and it’s time for The Phenom vs. The Icon…..as Shawn so kindly reminded us, these two have faced off before, back at the 1997 Royal Rumble…..but what Shawn DOESN’T want you to remember, it the fact that the Undertaker absolutely destroyed him for 95% of the match……and it was only the interference of the KLiQ……AND Kane……that finally saw the Heartbreak Kid victorious……and then only by Count-Out……

……considering the fact that to win the King of the Ring tournament you are essentially going to have to wrestle three matches in one night, the aim of the game in these quarter final matches, is to get the win as quick as possible to conserve your strength for your upcoming battles…..the only problem with that is that neither Shawn Michaels, nor the Undertaker are the calibre of opponent that you can defeat in five minutes, means that whoever manages to advance in this one is going to be in for a very long night……

……Shawn Michaels might have claimed not to sweat the Undertaker earlier this evening, but he was soon sweating his ass off, because much like at the Royal Rumble, The Deadman dominated the Boy Toy for a good portion of this match…..so much so in fact that it took Flair and Anderson making their way down the aisle and The Enforcer distracting the official so that Flair and Michaels could Double Team the Deadman to stop the onslaught…..and from there on in, it was Michaels in control…..or at least as in control as anyone can be against the Undertaker…..especially when Flair and Anderson continue to interfere at opportune moments…..

……the Undertaker has took a lot of offence in this…..and it looks as though Michaels believes that the Deadman is done, as he backs up onto the corner, where he starts to tune up the band……..

…..until the sounds of the Wolves howling distracts him…..and Kevin Nash comes hobbling out of the back…..with Scott Hall and Sean Waltman still trying to talk the angry giant out of doing anything to further injure himself…….

…..Shawn turns and sees the limping seven foot figure of Kevin Nash, pointing him out to Anderson and Flair, before cracking up laughing and hobbling his way around the ring mocking Big Sexy……..until he turns around and hobbles right into the waiting arms of the Undertaker, who spins him upside down and plants him head first into the mat with the Tombstone Piledriver……and three seconds later, Shawn Michaels King of the Ring journey comes to a screeching halt, courtesy of the distraction of a Diesel Truck.

Winner: The Undertaker
Rating: B+

Nash still doesn’t look happy, but he’s at least placated slightly by the fact that Michaels just got dropped on his head, and finally relents to his partners requests to head to the back, while Arn, Flair and Sunny check on Michaels, as we go to a quick promo video highlighting the Hart Foundations recent change in attitude, as well as all the build up to our next match, Bret and Owen Hart vs. The New Age Outlaws, for the WWF Tag Team Championships.

VS.

WWF World Tag Team Championship Match

The Hart Foundation (c) vs. The New Age Outlaws


It’s now time for our first title match of the evening, as the Hart Foundation put the gold on the line, against the cocky young upstarts, the New Age Outlaws…..the Harts haven’t been their usual selves as of late….bemoaning the state of the business, questioning the decision of the fans to cheer for certain individuals….even going so far as to leave their Brother In-Law the British Bulldog to fend for himself as Billy Gunn and the Road Dogg assaulted him……hardly behaviour becoming of two men who pride themselves on being Canadian Heroes…..

…..as for the Outlaws, they have been on quite a tear as of late, and thanks to the generosity and unselfishness of two legends in Mr. Perfect and Barry Windham, both men have seen their popularity skyrocket to a point that they aren’t just worthy Challengers to the Harts, but a real threat to their Championships…..they also represent everything that Bret and Owen hate…..they’re rude….there crude….they’re cocky and arrogant……but then so far they have every right to be, because they’ve been backing that cockiness up with victories…..

…….the match itself was outstanding……quite possibly even the match of the night on a card filled with huge match ups…..Owen, Billy and the Road Dogg worked their collective asses off, but once again it was the Hitman putting on the performance of the match, proving that even at almost 40 years old there’s life in the old dog yet……he even played the role of face in peril tonight, a job usually reserved for Owen, selling masterfully for his two opponents, who in turn came out of this one looking like a million bucks……Billy in particular, who many are saying put on a true main event calibre performance…..

……eventually however the Hitman was able to make the tag to his brother, and Owen hit the ring….and his opponents….like a house of fire, using speed and agility to leave the Outlaws rocking, and buy the Hitman enough time to recover and finally get back into the action himself, rolling back the years with a plancha over the top rope to The Road Dogg in the outside……back in the ring Owen comes flying across the ring and attempts to catch Billy with a spinning heel kick…..but Gunn ducks it and it’s the official that eats the back of Owens boot…..Owen stays on the offence, trying to catch Gunn with a quick kick, but the Bad Ass catches the foot…..before the back of his head catches the other foot as Owen puts him down with an enzuguri…..

……now Bret’s down, James is down, Gunn is down………..the official is down……..but Owen isn’t…..he does look to take advantage of the situation however, collecting a steel chair from the outside and standing poised over the fallen Billy Gunn……..

……..when suddenly the British Bulldog comes charging down the aisle, snatching the chair out of his hands…..Owen looks shocked briefly, but stands aside, obviously believing that Davey is here for retribution from the Outlaws attack on Raw, as he invites him to nail Gunn in the back……Davey however tosses the chair to one side, before berating Owen for leaving him stranded just six days ago to get his ass kicked by the Outlaws…..and that was AFTER intentionally costing him a place in the King of the Ring Tournament……Owen tells Davey to quit being such a big baby, before face palming him out of the way, turning to look for the discarded chair……and that’s when Davey scoops him up…..and drives him hard into the mat with the Running Powerslam, before turning and walking to the back……Billy Gunn and the Official both come to their senses at about the same time, and Gunn pounces on the helpless Hart, connecting with the Famouser and making the cover for the one, the two…….and the three……and just like that, we have brand new Tag Champs.

Winner(s): …..and New WWF Tag Team Champions…..The New Age Outlaws.
Rating: A

The Hitman joins his brother in the ring to find out what the hell has just happened…..and a very groggy Owen informs him that the British Bulldog ”…..just screwed us out of the damn belts…..they screwed us again Bret!…..” Bret slams his hand on the mat in frustration as the now FORMER Champions watch The New Age Outlaws wall to the back with the Tag Titles raised high in the air, before Bret helps his brother to his feet and then explodes to the outside where he snatches up a microphone…..announcing that……

”……this is bullsh!t…..this was a World Wrestling Federation Tag Team Championship match…..does that mean nothing around here anymore?……we defended those Titles with pride and honour, but apparently ANYONE can just walk down the aisle and stick their noses into CHAMPIONSHIP matches, and SCREW the most honourable Champions in this once great company’s history OUT of their titles……and not only that, but screw all these fans out of seeing their HERO’S victorious at the same time…..”

…..to be fair, these fans aren’t exactly sympathetic to the Harts cause…..Bret and Owen’s constant finger wagging and disapproving judgement is beginning to wear a little thin…..but Bret seems oblivious to that, stating that if that’s the way things are going to be around here, then maybe it’s time for the Hart Foundation to get with the times…..

”……because clearly, nice guys finish last around here…..there’s no more honourable men, only cheats, liars and scumbags…….”

…..Bret says you don’t have to look any further to see a cheat and a liar……than the British Bulldog……he says that Davey Boy Smith would have NOTHING in this world if it wasn’t for the Harts…..and this is how he repays them? They tried to instil some good old fashioned Canadian values on the Bulldog……and he still screwed them just like everyone else…….well enough is enough…..maybe it’s time that the Hart’s gave everyone a taste of their own medicine….before tossing the microphone down in disgust and heading to the back, leaving Jim Ross to question what exactly Bret means by that.

Rating: A

VS.

King of the Ring: Semi-Final Match

Cactus Jack vs. Ahmed Johnson (w/Jim Cornette)

Our first Semi Final match of the evening see’s Cactus Jack (without Sable this time) facing off against Ahmed Johnson, with Ahmed fresh off the biggest win of his career thus far earlier in the evening, defeating former WWF Champion Kevin Nash, although be it by dubious means…..Cactus picked right up where he left off earlier in the evening as well, putting on an outstanding display here in ANOTHER match that COULD have been great if the two men had say 8-10 more minutes to wok with……

……as it was, they worked with what they had…….and both men did what they do best…..Ahmed threw Cactus around and beat him to within an inch of his life, and Cactus took an insane amount of crazy bumps to make his opponent look like a killing machine….with Jim Cornette his usual annoying self on the outside, taking particular pleasure in the abuse Ahmed is dishing out, given his and Cactus’ previous history…..

……he even interjects himself into the match towards the end of the match, possibly even saving the match for his client, draping Ahmed’s foot over the bottom rope to break the count following a Double Underhook DDT….Jack drags Ahmed to his feet and sets him up for a second DDT attempt, but Johnson powers free, lifting Cactus up almost in a backdrop, before holding him up at the very hight of the hold and tossing him forward in a modified flapjack, before running through him with a huge clothesline….

……the two men begin pounding on each other, landing (stiff) rights and lefts, before Cactus manages to drive the bigger man into the corner, Irish Whipping him accords the ring and charging……but Ahmed moves and Cactus hits nothing but turnbuckle, driving the wind out of himself as Ahmed spins him around, delivering the Pearl River Plunge right in the middle of the ring…..and it’s Ahmed Johnson that advances to the finals of the 1997 King of the Ring.

Winner: Ahmed Johnson
Rating: B+

VS.

King of the Ring: Semi-Final Match

The Undertaker (w/Paul Bearer) vs. Raven

We waste no time in going straight to our next Semi Final match, because after a quick promo package hyping up Summerslam we are ready for The Undertaker vs. Raven…..and this isn’t the first time these two men have met…..they had an OUTSTANDING match several months back at the tail end of a much too short programme…..this match was nowhere near the quality of that one, but then (in the story of the night) they didn’t have a great deal of time to work with……

……Raven showed once again that he’s more than comfortable hanging with the big boys, and he absolutely worked his ass of in this one, performing at the very highest standard…..and it’s safe to say that these two are going to clash again at some point…..and probably in a far more extended programme than their previous one…..but for tonight we are going to have to stick a pin in that, because the Undertaker has other things on his mind……like the fact that he will finally get his hands on Ahmed Johnson tonight……because after laying out Raven with a Tombstone Piledriver, the 1997 King of the Ring Finals are set…….and it will be Ahmed Johnson……vs. The Undertaker.

Winner: The Undertaker
Rating: B+

With our final set, we are surprisingly greeted to the arrival of the Nation of Donination……and they’re wearing 3/5ths of the gold around here…..as Scorpio has his Light Heavyweight Championship around his waist……The Rock has the European Championship over his shoulder……and Kama and Nelson are each holding one strap of Faarooq’s Intercontinental Championship…..holding it high over the Nation’s heads like a banner as they march to the ring……

…..the group climb between the ropes and stand en-mass as one, before Faarooq brings out a microphone……asking the crowd if they can tell him what’s wrong with this picture? He says it’s funny that even though the Nation of Domination hold three of the five Championships here in the World Wrestling Federation, somehow those are the ONLY titles NOT being defended tonight…….why?……

”……..why is the King of the Ring Title on the line, but the Light Heavyweight Title isn’t…..why were the Hart Foundations Tag Team Titles on the line, but The Rock’s European Championship isn’t……..and why do you have THREE men competing for the WWF Title……and NO ONE competing for the Intercontinental Title? I’ll tell you why…….because the WWF Intercontinental Championship is owned by MY black ass, that’s why……”

……Faarooq says says that even with Championship Gold around their waists, the Nation of Domination STILL don’t get any respect…..the Nation of Domination STILL don’t get to perform on Pay Per View…..and even with Championship Gold around their waists, these scumbag fans in attendance STILL boo the Nation of Domination…..because they’re all a bunch of New York trash, who like their Champions to have a slightly paler complexion……….he continues spewing venom at the fans……but the fans seem to be concentrating on something else……..as two figures are making their way through the crowd…..not that we can see who they are, because they are surrounded by rowdy New Yorkers…..although by the time they reach the front row, it’s clear exactly who they are……..

…….because Booker T and Stevie Ray, The Harlem Heat, are here in the building…..and (for some strange reason) they have a microphone of their own……they don’t actually climb over the barricade…..instead they stay in the crowd, mobbed by these (surprisingly) rowdy fans, who are clearly pleased to see them……

…..Booker tells Faarooq that they’ve had to sit back now for MONTHS and listen to the crap he’s spewed out of his mouth, but he and his brother are here and they’re here in the WWF to tell the Nation of Domination one thing……and that’s the fact that…..

”…….Sucka, these people don’t boo you because you’re black…….these people boo you because you’re a jackass…….”

…….obviously the crowd pops huge for that, but Booker ain’t finished….telling Faarooq that not only is he a jackass, he’s a whiny little b!tch and someone needs to shut his mouth for good……

…..obviously seeing as there’s five of them and only two Harlem Heat members, Faarooq feels pretty brave, telling Booker that if he wants to shut his mouth so bad, then why don’t they come in this ring and try and make him…..and to their credit it looks as though Booker and Stevie are considering it…..before they can hop the rail however a group of security guards come running out from the back to halt their advance, with Booker finally telling Faarooq that it’s all good, they’re just going to hang in the crowd with these great fans…..great fans who despite what Faarooq May claim, are more than happy to cheer the newest arrivals to the WWF, regardless of the colour of their skin.

Rating: A

VS.

Head on a Pole Match

Al Snow & Chris Candido vs. Edge & Christian

It’s time for our most unusual match of the evening, and quite possibly the first (insert object here) on a pole match since the Big Boss Man fought Nailz way back when……and if it’s NOT the first pole match since then, well then the rest were obviously so bad I have erased them from my own memory……Al Snow has been on a free fall since his confidante and companion Head was….well, Headnapped for lack of a better term….by Edge and Christian……such a free fall in fact that his reluctant partner Chris Candido is even keen to reunite Head and Snow, just to raise Al’s questionable behaviour back up to a level he can live with……

……Edge and Christian have gone through somewhat of a metamorphosis as of late too, with Head apparently bringing out a personality and charisma in the pair that was previously unseen, and as such, these two teams have been slowly chugging along, getting themselves over with the crowd as they go, and what was originally planned to be a silly bit of filler between the King of the Ring Semi and Final match, actually turned out to be a pretty decent match……

……Candido must be secretly relieved that he is part of this odd couple, because he’d hit a bit of a roadblock in his WWF career, finding himself lost in the shuffle of a pretty packed Midcard….and Al Snow’s “entertaining” antics have certainly breathed new life into Candido’s ability on the mic, because while he’s still not the best talker, he’s certainly improved since the inception of this new team, and in all honestly, must have one eye on the new WWF Tag Team Champions, the New Age Outlaws……

…..but anyway, the match…..the rules were pretty simple…..climb the pole, get Head……use Head……win the match……and that’s exactly what happened, with those dastardly Canadians getting their just desserts……with Al retrieving Head……but being reluctant to use her as a weapon it was Candido snatching her from his hands and cracking Christian in the skull with her that granted them the pinfall victory.

Winner(s): Al Snow and Chris Candido
Rating: B

VS.

King of the Ring: Final Match

The Undertaker vs. Ahmed Johnson

So it all comes down to this…….the 1997 King of the Ring, and the man set to main event Summerslam, will be either Ahmed Johnson…….or the Undertaker…..although both of those facts may well be lost on these two……who have been looking for an opportunity to tear each other apart since Ahmed cost the Undertaker a victory against his brother Kane back at Wrestlemania……Undertaker as payback for that loss, while Ahmed Johnson simply wants to prove that he’s the Biggest Bad here in the WWF…..and what better way to prove it, than by taking out the Deadman…..especially if by taking out the Deadman he becomes the King of the Ring in the process…..because just ask Steve Austin what that title does for your career…..

……and if you don’t think Ahmed was serious when he told the world that he couldn’t wait to get his hands on the Phenom, you have to look no further than the entrance of this match…..because Ahmed Johnson is out of the Ring and running up the aisle to start brawling with his opponent before the Deadman gets so far as ten feet from the curtains….(which Rick Rude likes, stating that the Undertaker has used that elaborate entrance as an intimidation factor for years now……well, Ahmed Johnson isn’t intimidated by the Deadman, and he’s proved that right there)……

…….the Undertaker shrugs off his hat and coat and fires right back with rights and lefts as these two men brawl down the aisle and around the ringside area before the official can even ring the bell to get this one underway…..in fact Jim Ross goes as far as to say that if these two men don’t make it into the ring soon, this official may well just throw this whole match out……

…….fortunately that doesn’t happen, and the two men do finally manage to roll into the right, still laying into each other with rights and lefts as they go…..and now this one DOES finally get underway……but to be fair, the match isn’t anymore pretty than the pre-match, as this was just an out and out fight from start to finish…..neither man backing down, neither man giving an inch…..and neither man letting a punch in the face stop them from delivering one of their own…..given the amount of action involved, (that and the ridiculously large number of bouts scheduled for tonight) this wasn’t an overly long match…..but it was long enough for the crowd to get into it……and it was a good match……hampered slightly by the finish……..

…….which saw Jim Cornette hop up onto the apron, just as the Undertaker wraps his big hand around the neck of Ahmed, looking to Chokeslam him straight to Hell……because of Cornette’s distraction however, the official failed to see Ahmed deliver a stiff low blow to the Deadman (his second of the night, Kevin Nash is still talking in falsetto) and he also failed to see Ahmed pick up his managers strategically placed ever-present tennis racquet……which means he also fails to see Ahmed drive said racquet straight into the throat of the Undertaker, who drops to the mat frantically clawing at his larynx…..

……Jim Ross screams that Ahmed Johnson has just crushed the windpipe of the Deadman……and he may be right, because the Undertaker certainly appears to be having difficulty breathing……difficulty made even harder when he has the wind driven out of his body by a huge Pearl River Plunge from his opponent……..and three seconds later, Ahmed Johnson has done the unthinkable.

Winner: …..and 1997 King of the Ring…..Ahmed Johnson
Rating: B+

Ahmed Johnson stands victorious as a team of medical personnel rush to the ring to tend to the Undertaker…..and Rick Rude remarks that we have never seen the Deadman in this predicament…..and as he rolls around on the mat, seemingly unable to breathe, he may well be right……because it seems that even the power of the urn is unable to heal a crushed windpipe……and it’s quite a surreal sight to see the Phenom of the World Wrestling Federation stretchered out of the arena, breathing apparatus attached…..unable to leave the ring without help……and a helpless Undertaker is a sight that NOBODY thought they would ever see…..

……Dok Hendrix joins Ahmed and Cornette in the ring, along with the ceremonial crown, robe and sceptre…..as he questions the lengths that Ahmed Johnson will stoop to in order to win this match…….Cornette tells Hendrix (and the World) that there is NOTHING that Ahmed Johnson won’t do to prove that he’s the baddest man in the World Wrestling Federation…….and there is NOTHING that Ahmed Johnson won’t do at Summerslam…..to become the World Wrestling Federation Champion……

……and as King Ahmed regales himself in his Royal outfit, Cornette says that no matter who wins tonight’s Championship match, they’re still going to be a loser……because just like Kevin Nash found out…….Cactus Jack found out……..and yeah, even the big bad Undertaker found out…….when you get in the ring with Ahmed Johnson…….(Ahmed snatched the microphone from his manager before he can finish…..

”……when you get in the ring with Ahmed Johnson……whether you’re Scott Steiner…..Steve Austin…..or Brian Pillman……it doesn’t matter……because……YOU’RE GOING DOWN……”

……and with that, a disgusted Jim Ross sends us to some pre-recorded words, from the three competitors in tonight’s main event.

Rating: A

Not that they are anything groundbreaking these comments…..it’s simply a quick sound-bite from each man stating why they are going to be walking out of here tonight as the WWF Champion, giving us just enough time to clear the Ring area of Ahmed and his coronation gear……it was still surprisingly well received considering the short length of it……either way it did its job….which was to prepare us for our main event…..next.

Rating: B+

VS. VS.

WWF Championship Match

Scott Steiner (c) vs. Steve Austin vs. Brian Pillman


It’s main event time……and if you’re Brian Pillman, it’s time to sit back and watch, as Stone Cold Steve Austin and Big Poppa Pump Scott Steiner attempt to tear each other apart…….and it’s interesting (planned actually) that these three should meet here tonight, almost one year to the day that the World Wrestling Federation fans sat up and took notice of each man……Scott Steiner in his career making display against then WWF Champion Shawn Michaels…….Steve Austin as he defeated the British Bulldog, Rowdy Roddy Piper and Bret “The Hitman” Hart in the same night to capture the King of the Ring Crown…….and Brian Pillman, who in a sign of things to come, screwed over so-called friend Bret Hart, costing him the match and kickstarting the real Loose Cannon character that has lead to him becoming the number two heel in the entire company, second only to Shawn Michaels…..

…..and for Brian Pillman, the match went right as you would expect……well……almost……(but more about that later)…….because the moment the bell rang Steve Austin and Scott Steiner absolutely flew at each other, picking right up where they left off these past few weeks…..and the crowd love it, regardless of the fact that these are two of the most popular wrestlers in the World Wrestling Federation (in fact, despite the fact that Scott Steiner is the WWF Champion, Steve Austin has just cemented his place as the number one babyface around here, and the number two guy in the entire company behind only….again…..Shawn Michaels)….

…..obviously both Austin and Steiner know how to brawl, they’ve been doing it for practically a month straight now after all……and they inflict some serious damage on one another both in and out of the ring…..and considering that both men’s original problems lay with Brian Pillman, the Corporate Cannon has seemingly been forgotten here…..so much so that he actually takes up situ at the commentary table for a while, revelling in the punishment that his competitors are inflicting each other….while at the same time discussing the finer points of the Hangman’s Neckbreaker that he has adopted as his own with Ravishing Rick Rude, the man who made that move famous…..

…….obviously it was only a matter of time however before Brian Pillman’s crowing got the better of him…..because with Austin and Steiner laying blows on each other in front of the commentary table, both men suddenly stop and turn…….and as the Corporate Cannon’s eyes bulge out of his head……and he actually mutters an audible ”….oh sh!t….” over the headset……Austin and Steiner both reach over the desk and drag Pillman over the top of the table to the floor……where they take it in turns to beat the living crap out of him, bouncing him around like a pinball…….Pillman tries to escape back into the ring, but he only makes it as far as the apron, before Austin hooks his ankle to halt his escape…….and with nowhere for him to go, Scott Steiner comes charging across the ring, sending Brian flying off the apron and crashing through the announce table with a big shoulder block, that takes Pillman out of the equation…….

…….with the big mouth of the Corporate Cannon finally shut up for two minutes, their temporary truce is forgotten, as Steiner and Austin resume their battle……..and a match that was shaping up to be a true classic, was suddenly derailed by its ending…………

……because out of nowhere, Bret and Owen Hart come sprinting down the aisle…..each with a steel chair in hand…….and they blindside both Austin and Steiner with the folded weapons, knocking both men down to the mat, where they proceed to continue to hammer them with the steel…..of course these triple threat encounters are generally no disqualification, so there’s not a lot the official can do about it…….and out of the wreckage of broken wood on the outside crawls Brian Pillman…….

……he pauses for a moment when he sees the Harts, but as Bret indicates towards a practically unconscious Scott Steiner, a huge grin breaks out on his face, and he slithers into the ring, before covering the WWF Champion for the one…….

……the two……

……and that’s when Bret Hart crashes the steel chair off the back of Pillman’s head……and with all three competitors down……and much to the disapproval of the crowd…..the official has little option but to throw this one out……

Winner: No Contest (19:43)
Rating: B+

Bret and Owen aren’t done there…..they continue to punish all three men with another series of steel chair shots, before they finally toss the chairs to the ground as Bret picks up a microphone…..asking this rowdy crowd if they are happy now……

”…….ate you satisfied? This is what you like to see right? Violence……..unsportsmanlike behaviour……title matches ruined by outside interference?…..this is the way things are now right?….”

……the crowd are not amused, and they begin hurling abuse…..boo’s…..and finally trash at the ring, as the Hitman continues to run them down……

”……what, it’s only alright when it’s happening to us? But the minute your precious Scott Steiner or Steve Austin get screwed over, then we’re the bad guys? Steve Austin has screwed the Harts over more times than I can count, and yet you morons cheer him like he’s the second coming of……well……me…….

…..you remember me right? The man that carried this company on his back when things were at their worst……when people were jumping ship, following the money and heading down south, it was ME that stepped up, came out here every week…..and made sure that you ungrateful b*stards had the BEST wrestling product to watch…….as Hogan left……as Savage left……as the Warrior left…….the British Bulldog even left……it was Bret Hart that strapped that WWF Championship around my waist and came out here to be a hero for you…….for your children……and what do I get? Screwed over every time my back is turned……

…….where was Scott Steiner in those dark days? Where was Steve Austin? I’ll tell you where…..down in WCW with Brian Pillman…..with Ric Flair and Arn Anderson…..I was here…..Owen was here…..and yet it’s Scott Steiner that you cheer…..it’s Steve Austin that gets treated like royalty…….and as always, it’s Bret Hart that gets screwed……you cheer for the people that were actively trying to put the World Wrestling Federation out of business……you cheer the people that curse…..that flip obscene guestures……well cheer this…….” (Bret and Owen both flip the crowd of simultaneously) ”……..and as for the cursing, I’ll tell you people the same thing I told Kevin Nash…….I don’t need your help……I don’t need your approval…….I don’t need you……..so screw each and every one of you……”

……and with that shocking statement, the show comes to a close with a sight not seen for almost ten years……a World Wrestling Federation crowd booing the crap out of a smirking Bret Hart, as Jim Ross signs off, telling us to be sure and tune into Raw tomorrow night…….goodnight folks.

Rating: A*

Final Rating: B+

Final Thoughts

So that was King of the Ring……and while the event was a huge success, I’m not going to lie, I was a little disappointed with it…..and in fairness I only have myself to blame. The show was so packed to the gills with potential huge matches that it could have been amazing, but I way overbooked the card and didn’t give half of the guys enough time to go out there and really showcase what they can do……there was a few angles that should have been cut…..and ideally the Al Snow/Candido/Edge/Christian match could have been done with being dropped from the card entirely, but that storyline needed to reach a conclusion, and the four men involved deserved for that to be on Pay Per View……but a pole match…..what was I thinking……I only made that stipulation to begin with to shut Vince Russo up, who’d been banging on to book any kind of pole match for months now…..and how does he repay me? By running off to the competition…..god damn Judas that he is……and the Legion of Doom are no better, both coming up to mehours before the show demanding their release……and you know what, I’m so sick of their sh!t that I gave it to them……bye Hawk……bye bye Animal…..go and cause problems for someone else, because I have Raw to book.
