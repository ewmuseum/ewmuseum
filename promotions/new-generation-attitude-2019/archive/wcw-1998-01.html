WCW Monday Nitro: Week 1, January 1998

Posted 14 June 2019

Week 1, January 1998

Orlando Arena (Orlando, Florida)

ATT: 16,467

TV: 22.48

Announce Team: Tony Schiavone, Bobby Heenan and Mike Tenay

Ladies and Gentlemen we are one night removed from arguably the biggest night in World Championship Wrestling’s history…..the night that WCW stood up and hit back against Vince Russo, Hollywood Hogan and the Titans of Wrestling…..the night that Diamond Dallas Page and Chris Benoit proved that they deserved to share that ring with the very best…..the night that Randy Savage and The Warrior learned that you can only push Shawn Michaels so far…..and most importantly, the night that Sting stepped back into the ring and bought the WCW Championship back to where it belongs…..and TONIGHT…..Tony Schiavone promises us that after months of silence, we ARE going to hear from the WCW Champion……Sting….l

…..the still images from last night conclude and the pyro erupts to signal the start of the show, and we go straight to a jubilant announce team of Mike Tenay, Tony Schiavone and Bobby “The Brain” Heenan, who run down all the highs and lows of WCW Starrcade 1997, before welcoming us to WCW in 1998…..

…..Schiavone is on fine form as he openly laughs at the Titans of Wrestling’s poor showing last night…..losing both the WCW Tag Team and WCW World Championships, Ray Traylor’s defeat to Sid Vicious….Savage and Warrior didn’t lose, but they didn’t win either…..in fact, the only Titan to taste victory last night was Buff Bagwell, and even he fought a one legged man in Rowdy Roddy Piper…..all in all it was a good night for World Championship Wrestling…..and a very BAD night for the Titans of Wrestling…..

…..speaking of the Titans of Wrestling…..

Rating: N/A

…..here they come now…..and they don’t look to be in a very good mood…..in fact, there looks to be a good deal of tension between them all (except for Buff Bagwell, who is in exceptionally high spirits after his victory last night) so much so that they barely even acknowledge each other as they make their way down the aisle…..

…..Vince Russo takes a microphone first, and he first of all tells this braying crowd to shut up if they want to hear what the Titans have to say…..I think it goes without saying that this crowd does not shut up…..in fact the jeering gets even louder, so much so that Russo finally gives up and hands the microphone to Hollywood Hogan…..

…..Hogan says that last night was a total miscarriage of justice…..he says that he was forced to defend HIS world title against a man that was not only fired a few weeks previous, but against a man who lost the right to challenge for that same title, when Hollywood pinned his shoulders to the mat one, two, three back at Road Wild…..

“…..Eric Bischoff…..you know, just like everyone in this arena knows, Sting ain’t fit to lace Hollywood’s boots up brother, and the ONLY reason that he won last night was because I wasn’t prepared to face him…..I was prepared for the biggest fight of my life against The Giant…..a REAL challenger, when Sting stuck in the back door like the no good snake that he is…..”

…..and not only that says Hogan, but he was trying to prevent the Titans of Wrestling from imploding before his very eyes…..because with the exception of Buff, every single Titan let him down last night…..Copeland and Cage lost the Tag Team Titles to those two midcard jabroni’s DDP and Chris Benoit…..Ray Traylor got his butt handed to him by Sid Vicious…..twice…..

“…..and you two…..the supposed other two thirds of the Holy Trinity of Professional Wrestling, you couldn’t even beat a Titan Reject and a scrawny little jobber who was ‘Rocking’ in the opening matches while Hulk Hogan was selling out Madison Square Garden…..”

…..erm…..this probably isn’t the best night to be giving Randy Savage a dressing down…..the Macho Man went through a true war last night, when (along with his partner The Warrior) he stepped in (and out) of the ring with Jean Paul Levesque and Shawn Michaels……and after blood was spilled and rules were stretched to the very limit, the official finally threw the match out and all four men found themselves getting a lift downtown courtesy of Washington DC’s finest…..

…..of course, the fact that Savage and Warrior WERE arrested, means that they weren’t around to make an appearance in the main event…..another fact that Hogan is quick to point out…..and that’s when Savage snaps…..

…..he tells Hogan that he and Warrior might not have won their match, but unlike Hollywood, they didn’t lose either…..and unlike Hollywood, Randy Savage and The Warrior put JPL and Michaels through hell…..

“…..unlike you brother, brother, brother…..who just rolled over and handed the title to the Sting man with barely a fight, yeah…..”

…..the Mega Powers look to be on the brink of exploding one more time here as Hogan and Savage begin to bicker amongst themselves, finally breaking out into a shoving match that Vince Russo tries to break up (unsuccessfully) only to be put on his ass by the original loose cannon of professional wrestling, Randy Savage…..

…..finally it’s The Warrior that brings a halt to the arguing, putting himself between the two men as Russo grabs a microphone again…..

…..he tells everyone to calm down…..step back and take a breath…..Russo says that tempers are frayed tonight, but he’s going to get back to the Status Quo around here…..

…..first of all, the Tag Team Titles…..Copeland, Cage AND Traylor are going to take care of that fluke loss from last night, and they’re going to take care of it TONIGHT…..when they have their championship rematch…..

…..speaking of championship rematches…..in four weeks time, at WCW’s latest event ‘Souled Out’, it’s going to be Hollywood Hogan and Sting one more time…..except this time, it will be Hollywood with his hand raised in victory…..

…..as for the Macho Man…..since he and Warrior can’t take care of business under the confines of a regular match, they’re going to put the two of them in there with Shawn Michaels and JPL one more time, but THIS time, they’re going to throw out all the rules…..call it no holds barred, call it a streetfight, call it whatever you want……but the only way that match will end will be by either pinfall or submission…..

…..that placates the Macho Man a little bit…..in fact, everyone looks a damn sight calmer after those announcements…..Russo isn’t done though, telling Buff Bagwell that he hasn’t forgotten about him…..and since he was the only Titan to win last night, he gets a very special match here tonight…..he gets the main event…..and since Mr. ‘Too Good for WCW’ is now under contract, that means that Vince Russo gets to book his matches…..so tonight, it’s going to be the true sexy boy of WCW, Buff ‘The Stuff’ Bagwell, going one on one…..with Shawn Michaels…..

“…..hold on…..hold on…..I hate to break up the party, but…..I’m going to break up the party…..”

…..well if it isn’t the public face of the WCW Championship Committee, Mr. Eric Bischoff himself…..

…..Eric tells Russo that he hates to burst his bubble…..and while all those matches sound great “they really do” there are just one or two problems…..

“…..mainly with those title matches you just booked…..you see, they’re not going to happen…..”

…..Russo goes to shut down Bischoff, but Eric cuts him right off…..he tells him that while he thought it prudent to wait until tonight to make those matches official, certain other individuals didn’t…..in fact, two parties took Eric to one side last night within ten minutes of Starrcade concluding and, well……

“…..long story short, Diamond Dallas Page and Chris Benoit, they’ve already agreed to put the WCW Tag Team Titles on the line tonight against The Public Enemy…..and as for Sting…..he’s already been challenged to a match at Souled Out…..and it’s a challenge that’s been accepted…..these two men have a lot of unfinished business to take care of and at Souled Out, Sting will be putting the WCW Championship on the line……against The Giant…..

…..oh, those other matches sound great though, so well done with that…..goodnight gentlemen…..”

…..and with that Bischoff simply leaves…..and the Titans of Wrestling’s brief good mood rapidly turns sour again, as the tension resumes and we go to a commercial for Souled Out as the commentators revel in the Titans internal bickering, while reminding us that Sting will speak…..later tonight…..

Rating: A*

WCW World Tag Team Championship Match

Diamond Dallas Page & Chris Benoit (c) (w/Kimberly) vs. The Public Enemy


So we’re back…..and we have the first title match of the evening, the first title match of 1998 and the first title defence for new champions Diamond Dallas Page and Chris Benoit…..

…..Page and Benoit have spent the best part of the last six months embarked in a bitter feud with the Titans of Wrestling, and while you’ve got to assume that neither man had tag title aspirations at the start of that feud, they’ve got to be happy to have finally delivered a huge blow to the Titans by taking the gold away from the Totally Awesome Twosome…..

…..that being said, their celebration last night was cut short by the arrival (and subsequent beatdown at the hands of) The Forgotten, who it would seem have title aspirations of their own…..DDP and Benoit were left laying last night, but they are here now and they have no problem putting their newly won championships on the line…..

…..Public Enemy are perhaps not the first team that come to mind when you think of tag team championship material, but at least they ARE an actual tag team, unlike our current champions….so while Page and Benoit are (much) bigger names on paper, they are much bigger names in singles action, while Johnny Grunge and Rocco Rock have been a regular tag team for quite some time now…..so if we are in fact talking about “on paper” maybe it’s the challengers who are the favourites…..

…..either way, they lost…..

…..the Public Enemy are a big, rough, tough, brawling team, but that wasn’t enough to overcome their own stupidity, and as Grunge placed Benoit on top of the ever present table that the PE like to carry with them, Rocco Rock spring-shot himself over the top rope…..Chris Benoit moved…..Rock crashed and burned through the wood…..and DDP finished off Grunge with a Diamond Cutter in the middle of the ring for the one, two, three.

Winner(s): ….and Still WCW Tag Team champions…..Diamond Dallas Page and Chris Benoit.
Rating: B

That’s Title defence number one in the bag, and Mean Gene makes his way out to get a word from the celebrating Champions…..

…..Gene shills the hotline (obviously…..1-900-909-9900) telling us that all the news, results and rumours from last night are there (kids get your parents permission before calling) before turning to congratulate Page and Benoit, although he does prefix it by saying that while they must be pleased to be standing here as the Tag Team Champions of the world, and even more pleased to have taken those titles away from the Titans of Wrestling in the first place, their celebration was somewhat bittersweet, after it was cut short by the arrival of the Forgotten…..

…..Page agrees…..he says that after six months of ALMOST defeating the Titans of Wrestling…..after six months of being one second away from proving that they could hang with the best of the best, only for another Titan member to cheap shot them and cost them the victory…..after six months of even beginning to doubt themselves, these WCW Tag Team Titles make it all worth it…..

“…..and then three monkeys like the Forgotten come along, and it all begins again…..”

…..Benoit takes the mic, telling Gene that he’s been saying for months that he prefers to do his talking in the ring rather than on the microphone…..Kevin Sullivan, Bam Bam Bigelow and Big Van Vader, they obviously just like jumping people while their backs are turned…..well Chris Benoit, he prefers to meet his problems head on…..

“…..if it’s head on you want Chris, believe me, we can do head on…..”

…..well, wouldn’t you know it…..it’s The Forgotten…..

…..Kevin Sullivan leads the way, microphone in hand as he walks down the aisle along with Big Van Vader and Bam Bam Bigelow…..

…..Sully tells Benoit and Page that last night was only a fraction of what the Forgotten are capable of…..he says that Bam Bam Bigelow and Big Van Vader joined his crusade for two reasons….one, because they were promised that Kevin Sullivan would lead them down a path littered with gold…..and two, they just really, really, REALLY like to hurt people…..something that the WCW Tag Team Champions are about to find out…..

…..Diamond Dallas Page obviously takes that threat very seriously, because as The Forgotten climb up onto the apron he tells Mean Gene to take Kimberly and get to the back, before joining his partner in a preemptive strike, rushing at the two bigger men as they climb between the ropes…..

…..it wasn’t a bad idea, because it DOES give them a brief advantage…..an advantage that is eventually cut short as the three on two advantage comes into play, and then (much as they did last night) the Forgotten proceed to beat the holy hell out of the Tag Team Champions…..

…..looks like Mean Gene did get Kimberly safely to the back…..but it doesn’t look as though she’s staying there…..because here she comes again, and she’s bringing a BIG surprise with her…..the mammoth Sid Vicious…..

…..Sid (much like Page and Benoit) is fresh of a victory over one of the members of the Totally Awesome Trinity last night, where he solidly defeated The Enforcer Ray Traylor…..that win obviously whetted the big man’s appetite for fighting, because even with the three Forgotten members standing tall, he marches straight down the aisle and steps over that top rope into the ring…..

…..Bigelow charges at Sid (but eats a Big Boot) and so does Sullivan (who eats a right hand) before Vicious comes face to face with his one time tag team partner, Big Van Vader…..

…..the two men stare each other down menacingly, before finally exchanging blows…..and it’s Sid that begins to get the upper hand in this slugfest, at least until Sully and Bammers come in from behind, nailing Sid with clubbing blows to his back…..

…..before we can have another three on one beatdown however, DDP and Benoit rejoin the fray, as that three on one beating changes into a pier six brawl…..a brawl that is only finally ended when a wave of security comes rushing out from the back, and we go to commercial break…..

…..it looks like the Forgotten are far from finished with the in demand tag team champions…..

Rating: A*

Mean Gene still looks somewhat shaken by the sudden arrival of the Forgotten during his last interview, but being the consummate professional that he is he’s out here again to (not only plug the fact that Sting will speak this evening, but) introduce his next guest, the former Television Champion (and Television Championship thief) Lance Storm…..

…..Storm makes his way out, but he’s not alone…..he’s accompanied (as he often has been in recent weeks) by the Amazing French Canadians, Jacques Rougeau and Carl Oulette, as all three men join Gene in the ring…..

…..Mean Gene says that after last night, surely Lance Storm is now willing to accept the fact that Bill Goldberg is the WCW Television Champion, after defeating Storm in the very match that Lance demanded……a submission match…..

…..Lance Storm has been somewhat delusional as of late, going so far as to steal away the Television Title belt (after losing it to Goldberg at Mayhem) and proclaiming himself STILL the rightful Champion…..

…..he’s a little bit delusional here too (probably not helped by the presence of the AFC’s, who appear to be equally delusional) as he tells Gene that he doesn’t know what match HE was watching, but Bill Goldberg did NOT make him submit last night…..

…..Gene is a little bit confused by that, telling Storm that the whole world saw it, and the undefeated Goldberg is still exactly that…..undefeated…..Lance Storm lost…..

…..Rougeau and Oulette pipe up here, rowdily shoving Gene around as they too proclaim that Lance Storm never submitted…..a Canadian would never quit…..Storm invites Gene to show him right now up on the big screen, a shot of the submission hold that Bill Goldberg applied to him…..

“…..was it a Chickenwing Over the Shoulder Crossface? A three quarter Facelock? An STF? A step-over Armbar?…..what was it Gene?…..what about something a bit simpler…..a side headlock?…..”

…..Storm says that they won’t show it on the big screen because there is nothing to show…..Bill Goldberg neither applied, or knows, a single submission hold…..and even if he did, Lance Storm would have known how to reverse it…..he says that Bill Goldberg is only the Television Champion today because of crooked American officiating, but that’s fine…..he says that Goldberg can hold on to the Television Title, because Lance Storm is moving on to bigger and better things…..maybe since it was biased US officiating that cost him the TV Title, Storm will go and take the US Title…..maybe he’ll take the WORLD title…..he hasn’t decided yet…..but any titles he DOES win, he’ll win by utilising real SUBMISSION holds, just like he’s going to do in his next match…..

“…..because I’m a professional WRESTLER…..from Calgary…..Alberta, Canada…..home of all the worlds superior athletes…..so Gene, send me my opponent…..”

Rating: B+

Scotty Riggs vs. Lance Storm


And who is that opponent? Well it’s one time American Male (although he’s actually still American….and still a Male, but you know what I mean) and former Tag Team Champion, Scotty Riggs…..

…..Scotty Riggs isn’t exactly a regular on prime time television…..sure he gets his run outs on the WCW Saturday Nights of the world, but appearances on Nitro are few and far between…..Scotty is a decent worker…..he’s not great, he’s not bad, but if you’re looking for a half decent match then he’s a solid enough hand to put in there…..he has a great look, but he’s never really connected as a singles competitor, although perhaps the fact that he’s playing a pretty boy babyface is working against him, and a stint as an arrogant narcissistic heel would at least get him a reaction from the crowd…..granted, he’s not the greatest promo in the world, but then neither was a certain Shawn Michaels at one point when he was first cutting his teeth as an arrogant singles star…..

…..anyway, that’s a thought for another day (note to self, push Riggs…..eventually) because today Riggs has one job, and that’s to DO the job…..Storm really goes all in on the submission style with this one, stretching poor Scotty with all manner of holds (while still allowing the Amazing French Canadians at ringside their opportunities to interfere) before finally going for his bread and butter hold, the Canadian Maple Leaf……to which Scotty Riggs wastes no time in submitting to……your winner (by submission) Lance Storm.

Winner: Lance Storm
Rating: B

From there we go backstage, where the tensions are still running high within the camp of the Titans of Wrestling…..

…..Vince Russo is currently getting an ear bashing from Hollywood Hogan, for the fact that it’s going to be the Giant competing for the WCW Championship at Souled Out, not Hollywood himself…..and he is NOT pleased about it…..

…..Hogan says that Vince Russo is here in the Titans of Wrestling, to make sure that Titan Business gets taken care of…..and more importantly, to make sure that Hollywood Hogan is taken care of…..Hogan says it’s bad enough that he couldn’t rely on Randy Savage and The Warrior last night, without having to worry about the fact that he can’t rely on Vince Russo either…..

…..a comment that sets the Macho Man off…..again…..

…..he snaps at Hogan that if he has to rely on ANYONE to hold on to the WCW Title, then maybe it’s not Hollywood that should be carrying the Titan flag…..

“…..maybe it’s not Hollywood that should be out here looking for a title shot, yeah…..The Macho Man and The Warrior Man, we didn’t ask for no help from no one, no way, no how last night…..we beat Shawn Michaels and that punk Levesque to a bloody pulp, and we didn’t need no help to do it, you dig?…..”

…..so maybe…..maybe it should be Randy Savage looking for a shot at that World Title…..Sting might have the Giant at Souled Out, but once Savage and Warrior destroy Michaels and Levesque, their dance card will be empty in time for, oh I don’t know…..Superbrawl?…..

“…..maybe it’s not Hollywood time…..maybe it’s Randy Savage time…..maybe it’s The Warrior Man’s time…..maybe it’s just ANYONE time…..anyone but Hollywood, yeah…..”

…..rather predictably, Hogan doesn’t agree…..and again an argument breaks out between the two men…..an argument that the Warrior doesn’t even try to break up this time……does he agree with Savage?…..

…..the heated words finally reach breaking point…..there’s pushing, there’s shoving, there are accusations…..there are threats……

“…..hey…..hey!…..HEY!……THIS TOTALLY SUCKS!!…..”

…..apparently it’s going to be Adam Copeland and Christian Cage that are the voices of reason, and they look mortified that their two hero’s are about to go to war…..

…..Copeland says that they all had their problems last night, they were all let down…..they all had their losses…..

“…..well apart from Buffy the Haggis Slayer here…..because you totally kicked Roddy Piper right in the bagpipes, that was awesome…..”

…..Cage says that even THEY were let down…..they were totally relying on The Enforcer being out there at ringside to cheer them on…..he says that Ray Traylor is like their own one man cheerleader…..he totally gets them super hyped up for their matches, but he wasn’t there when they needed him…..

“…..you totally got your ass kicked by Sid Vicious, and that’s totally NOT cool…..BUT…..we forgive you Big Ray Ray…..partly because we’re pretty damn nice guys, but mainly…..because we’re bro’s…..”

…..Copeland says that no one wants to see this…..no one wants to see the Mega Powers explode…..he says that Hogan and Savage are two of the biggest names in all of Wrestling, they can totally put this behind them…..he says that the Titans had their set backs, but they just have to remember that they’re ALL bro’s…..

“…..I know we’re all totally bummed right now, but Buff, he’s gonna Heartbreak Kick Shawn Michaels ass later…..and Macho and Warrior, they’re going to finish the job at Souled Out, because that’s what we do…..we totally overcome…..we are the Titans of Wrestling…..the best thing to happen to wrestling since, well, Hulk Hogan and Randy Savage…..”

…..Cage says that he and Adam, they’re gonna show the world what it means to be part of the Totally Awesome Trinity…..and that being a Titan of Wrestling means facing your problems head on…..

…..with that Cage and Copeland stop for a moment to break out that (Totally Awesome) Mega Powers handshake, before heading out of the room…..

“…..come on Big Ray Ray…..we’ve got some dogs to neuter…..”

Rating: B+

WCW Television Championship Match

Bill Goldberg (c) vs. Fit Finlay


We go back down to ringside now, where we have our second title match of the evening, and it’s Bill Goldberg putting the Television Title on the line, against the Fighting Irish, Fit Finlay…..

…..it’s not going to be as cut and dry here as many people might think…..Fit Finlay may not have made quite the impression that the undefeated rookie has here in WCW, but Finlay is a veteran of the sport who has wrestled the world over…..he’s a tough, tough man who loves nothing more than a tough, tough fight…..

…..I guess he’s in there with the right opponent then…..

…..it’s funny, Bill Goldberg has been the WCW Television Champion for little over one month now, and he HAS defended that title numerous times since winning it…..but this is the first time he’s come out on tv with the actual physical belt…..be kind of ironic if he lost it on it’s very first outing then right?…..

…..he didn’t lose it…..

…..Finlay put up one hell of a fight, but just like everyone else he fell victim to the Spear/Jackhammer combination, to see Bill Goldberg retain his Television Title, and retain his unbeaten streak, walking out this evening at 57-0.

Winner: ….and Still WCW Television Champion…..Bill Goldberg
Rating: B-

As Bill Goldberg moves one win closer to his next big milestone victory, we air a promo video for Sting, before the commentators again remind us that we will hear from our brand new WCW Champion later this evening…..at least they try to…..before their words are abruptly cut off by the music of the Titans of Wrestling…..as the Totally Awesome Trinity make their way out…..

…..Copeland and Cage lead the way, with Big Ray Ray….sorry…..I mean, with The Enforcer Ray Traylor bringing up the rear…..nightstick at the ready…..

…..Copeland takes a microphone, and (after he and Cage have done that totally sweet Mega Powers handshake) he tells the crowd that they are out here to show the world that the Titans of Wrestling “always totally come back bigger and better than ever”…..he says that tensions are running high right now in the Titans of Wrestling, but no one worry…..because the Mega Powers will NOT be exploding anytime soon, and everybody’s two favourite hero’s and icons (Hogan and Savage) will totally put their differences behind them soon…..

“…..but in the meantime, me and Christian, we’re totally out here to take care of business…..because last night, we were robbed of our WCW Tag Team titles…..we were totally unprepared…..”

“…..and frankly distraught Adam…..”

“…..and frankly distraught, after the beating our good bro Big Ray Ray took at the hands of Sid Doofus…..and we were robbed, because two no good mangey mutts stuck their totally ugly snouts into our business…..”

…..Copeland says that he’s heard a rumour that the Pitbulls are here this evening…..and if that’s the case, they should totally get their butts out here so that the Totally Awesome Trinity can rub their noses in the mess they made…..

“…..so monkeys in the back…..play their funky music…..and release the hounds……”

…..and the Pitbulls music does indeed begin to play…..

…..and the Pitbulls DO come out…..

…..I guess…..

…..kind of anyway…..

…..because what ACTUALLY comes out, are two leather clad midgets (dressed in as dangerously close to S&M gear as Turner executives would allow) being lead out on collars and dog chains by a very ugly, VERY obese lady(?) who isn’t wearing a great deal either…..

…..the ‘Pitbulls’ climb into the ring, where Copeland tells them that they totally have some explaining to do…..he tells the two mangey mutts that last night, they cost the best tag team in WCW the tag team titles…..they cost the Totally Awesome Trinity the tag team titles…..and they totally cost the Titans of Wrestling the tag team titles…..“and you DON’T want to be costing the Titans of Wrestling ANYTHING now, do you?”…..

…..’Madusa’ speaks for the ‘Pitbulls’, telling Copeland and Cage that she’s sorry….very sorry….she says that the last thing they want to do is upset the Titans of Wrestling…..and they will do ANYTHING to make it up to them…..

“…..well first of all, thank you Madusa, for having the decency to admit what a totally stupid move that was……….you look great by the way, did you drop a few pounds?…..”

…..’Madusa’ almost blushes at that remark, fluttering her eyelashes (on her good eye….the lazy one doesn’t do much) at Copeland, before Cage snatches the microphone…..he tells Copeland to stop flirting with ‘Madusa’…..he doesn’t want to go there…..

“…..I mean, I’m not saying she’s easy, but she’s totally responsible for more merry men than Robin Hood…..”

…..Cage says that it’s nice and all that they’re sorry…..but now, they’re totally gonna kick their butts anyway…..

…..and that’s exactly what they do…..they beat the snot out of the two ‘Pitbulls’, before finally finishing off poor little ‘Pitbull #2’ with the Pitbulls own Superbomb from the second rope…..

…..job done, Copeland tells Big Ray Ray to take out the trash (which The Enforcer does, throwing a ‘Pitbull’ over each shoulder and taking them to the back, as Copeland and Cage celebrate their epic victory…..

…..although they do look kind of annoyed that the Pitbull’s music is playing and not theirs…..

…..of course that might have something to do with the fact that the REAL Pitbulls (along with their manager Madusa) are making their way out from the back and running down the aisle…..

…..they hit the ring and go straight after the former tag team champions, revenge on their mind after Cage and Copeland took out Anthony Durante with a Con-Chair-To a few weeks back on this very show…..

…..with The Enforcer not there to watch their backs however, Copeland and Cage seem to be less eager to face of against the real Pitbulls than they were to face their pint sized counterparts, and before either man can lay a finger on the Canadians, the Totally Awesome Twosome quickly bolt from the ring and scarper to the back, leaving Madusa to grab the discarded microphone to promise Cage and Copeland that her Pitbulls are coming for them one way or another……as we go to commercial.

Rating: A

And we’re back…..and we are back down at ringside, where the current WCW United States Champion (and Papi of Perfection) Eddie Guerrero is making his way out…..he’s not alone though…..as he has the newest member of the WCW roster by his side, his nephew…..Chavo Guerrero Jr…..

…..the two Guerrero’s climb into the ring, and Eddie is clearly brimming with confidence following his victory over The Rock last night, a victory that (he is quick to point out) makes him the current longest reigning champion here in WCW…..and therefore the BEST champion in the world…..

…..Eddie says that at the rate they’re going, they’re going to have to rename this the ‘Eddie Guerrero Championship’, because he’s rapidly running out of opponents to defend it against…..he says that there is no champion in wrestling today who has a laundry list of who’s who under their belt in title defences…..no champion except Eddie Guerrero that is…..

“…..Órale Vato…..you can even forget about Bill Goldberg little streak esè, because the only streak that matters in wrestling today, is Eddie Guerrero’s US Title winning streak…..”

“…..Eddie Guerrero, the only streak that matters around here is that streak of yellow running up your back, that big yellow streak of p!ss running down your leg…..”

“…..and that big brown streak you’ve got in your underoo’s, because the thought of going one on one with the great one makes you wanna straight up crap your pants…..”

…..you had to have known that the Rock would have something to say after getting his brains rattled with a set of brass knuckles last night (brass knuckles that Chavo Guerrero Jr. supplied) and here he is, larger than life and microphone in hand as he walks straight down the aisle and into the ring…..

…..Eddie doesn’t look particularly pleased at this development, telling Rocky that he’s had his shot…..he’s had his chance…..and just like that other fat Samoan Rikishi, he lost to Eddie Guerrero…..

“…..just like Jeff Hardy, Diamond Dallas Page, Chris Benoit…..they all got titles now esé, but they couldn’t take mine…..just like Rick Martel, Brad Armstrong….BOB Armstrong…..just like Mr. Not-so-Perfect Curt Hennig…..just like YOU holmes…..everyone goes down to Eddie Guerrero esé…..”

“…..woah, woah, woah…..say that again…..”

“…..everyone goes down to Eddie Guerrero esé…..you line them up and the Papi of Perfection knocks them now like a piñata holmes…..”

“…..let me get this straight…..do you actually think that YOU beat the Rock?…..”

“…..that exactl…..”

Rating: A

Kid Kash vs. Scott Taylor


…..if WCW Monday Nitro was to be classed as an ‘A’ Show, then I would personally consider WCW Thunder a ‘B+’ show…..not as important as a Nitro, but certainly more relevant than a WCW Worldwide or (1998) Saturday Night…..just enough marquee matches and storyline development to make it necessary viewing, but also somewhere you can give some of the lesser seen stars some much needed exposure…..

…..hence our next match…..

…..we are showcasing the Cruiserweight division, and its Kid Kash facing off against relative newcomer Scott Taylor…..both men very capable in the ring, but unless they were in the middle of a Cruiserweight Championship programme, unlikely to be featured predominately on Nitro…..

…..of the two, Kid Kash (some personality issues no withstanding) is the more likely to one day hold that. Cruiserweight gold…..the former David Jericho, who’s passing resemblance to the cult hip-hop/rap-rock star Kid Rock (who’s becoming more of a household name after signing a new deal with Atlantic Records last year) garnered him not only a new name, but an entirely new (cocky) gimmick still has some way to go to reach his full potential, but in this junior division he has star written all over him…..

…..which was why he won…..

…..it was a decent enough match, with both men looking good, but in the end Kash picked up the pinfall with an Double Underhook Piledriver that he’s christened the Moneymaker…..

Winner: Kid Kash
Rating: C

…..we air a brief promo package next, highlighting the recent attacks that have been inflicted upon the Samoan SWAT Team of all people, by a number of as yet unknown assailants, ending in Gary Hart’s challenge last week, for the attackers to meet the SST right here tonight in Tag Team action…..

….from there we come back live to the arena, where the Samoans have made their entrance, and Hart once again has a microphone…..he says that his team is here as promised, but after weeks of sneaking around and jumping people from behind, where are these mystery men?…..

…..ask and you shall receive…..because it doesn’t take long for two black clad figures (masks and all) to emerge from the crowd and climb into the ring…..I guess we have a tag team contest next…..

Rating: C+

The Samoan SWAT Team (w/Gary Hart) vs. ??? & ???


…..the SST were somewhat void of direction in WCW until the re-emergence of their one time manager Gary Hart, who’s set these Wild Samoans back on the winning track, with presumably ambitions of Tag Team gold…..

…..more recently however they have been somewhat distracted by the arrival of these mystery men….sometimes one…..sometimes two…..occasionally three…..but no matter how many the number, the result has usually been the same…..the Samoans have been left laying…..

…..that’s perhaps why they are wilder than usual tonight, going after their masked opponents with savage intent…..they look to have the advantage too…..and surely it’s only a matter of time before they both defeat and unmask these mystery men, particularly when the action spills to the floor……

…..which is when a further two men emerge…..one who leaps over the crowd rails, one who knocks the rail down to get to ringside (because he’s HUGE…..there’s no way he’s climbing over anything) and once again the SST are the victims of an assault…..this time four on two…..and this one is over…..

Winner(s): The Samoan SWAT Team (via disqualification)
Rating: C

…..but the assault is not…..as these four masked men continue to assault these (now near helpless) Samoans…..

…..they use the ringside environment to their advantage as they crash Fatu and Samu into ring barriers, steel steps, ring posts and aprons, before they finally roll the duo back into the ring…..

…..the three (slightly) smaller attackers continue to stomp the downed SST, while the larger man (who is clearly the ringleader) leans back in the corner and watches, gesturing silent orders with his arm motions…..

…..finally with Samu and Fatu down, two masked men make their way to the top rope, where they come flying with duel splashes, that completely finish off their opponents…..

…..the larger man walks to the centre of the ring as his three generals surround him, and in one fluid motion, all four men slowly remove their masks…..

…..and Tony Schiavone is shocked (shocked I tells ya) when it reveals four more Samoans underneath…..one of whom we know VERY well, because he is former WWF Champion (and former Son of Samoa) Kokina…..

…..Dusty says that he knows these Samoans…..he says that he and the SST go wayyyyy back daddy…..he says that these men aren’t just Samoans, they’re family…..in fact those two at the back (Ekmo and Smooth) are as family as family can be…..because Ekmo is the brother of Fatu, while Smooth is Samu’s brother…..

…..not that family seems to mean much right now…..because all four gentlemen are currently standing over the SST with a look close to disgust on their faces…..

…..which is when (a very wary looking) Mike Tenay climbs into the ring with a microphone…..asking these Samoan’s one simple question…..

…..why?…..

…..is it a simple answer? Maybe, but apparently not an answer we are getting today…..Kokina is apparently the mouthpiece of the group, but he just tells Tenay that the sins of the fathers are not about to be visited on the sons, but rather the sins of the savages are about to be visited on themselves…..

“…..things change, times change…..the world turns…..and nothin stays the same…..you either change with it, or watch your past be destroyed by your future…..”

…..not a simple answer at all then…..or any answer at all really…..let’s go to commercial…..

Rating: B

The Road Warriors vs. Lance Storm & Lord Steven Regal


…..Lance Storm has been on anything BUT a one man campaign to take humiliate the American Patriot, Hacksaw Jim Duggan…..in fact, its regularly been a three, four, even six man campaign, with the Amazing French Canadians and (more recently) The Blue Bloods getting in on the fun…..

…..of course, we ARE predominantly in America…..and while Jim Duggan is an American Patriot, the Road Warriors are American Originals…..and they took Storm’s USA bashing VERY personally…..

…..hence this match tonight…..

…..not Hawk and Animal vs. Earl Robert and Duke Douglas……not The Road Warriors vs. The Amazing French Canadians…..no, instead, these American Originals want the two heads of State…..the leaders of their nations…..so tonight, it’s The Road Warriors, Hawk and Animal, against Lance Storm and Lord Steven Regal…..

…..this is WAY out of Storm and Regals comfort zone…..you’re talking about two technical, mat based wizards here…..Hawk and Animal are big, burly, bar-room brawlers, who quite possibly don’t even know how to tell the time, let alone know a wristwatch from a wrist lock…..which is why they completely dominate the opening portion of the match……

…..Storm and Regal (do cheat) but finally manage to isolate Hawk, cutting off the ring and isolating the body part…..it doesn’t look as though it’s going to be enough though…..or at least not until the Amazing French Canadians AND the Blue Bloods get involved, with Jacques Rougeau tossing Lance Storm a (Canadian Original) Hockey Stick, which he uses to wrap around the back of Hawks head, giving him the one, two, and the three…..

…..it’s not a clean victory…..

…..but a victory it is none the less…..

Winner(s): Lance Storm & Lord Steven Regal
Rating: A

…..the Storm and the Canadian, as well as the Blue Bloods stand over the fallen Hawk for a moment, before shaking hands with one another like the civilised servants of Her Majesty that they are…..

…..although they’re clearly not THAT civilised…..because it looks as though they are about to deliver a six on two beating…..and Storm still has that hockey stick in hand…..

…..he’s not the only one who’s got wood though…..because here comes Hacksaw…..and before the dastardly heels even know he’s arrived, he nails Storm across the back with that 2×4……Hoooooooooo…..

…..he starts swinging wildly then…..and (once the Road Warriors get back to their feet) it’s not long before Her Majesties Mercenary’s bolt from the ring and rush up the aisle out of harms way……

…..Hacksaw grabs Old Glory and waves it high in the air, as Road Warrior Hawk snatches a microphone up from ringside…..he tells Storm and Company (rather loudly) that this is nowhere near over…..he says that one way or another, they’re going to war…..and with Hacksaw on board, the three of them want three of ANY combination of the six of them…..and they want them next week on Monday Nitro…..

…..what a rush indeed…..

Rating: B+

Blitzkrieg vs. Hayabusa (w/James Vandenberg)


…..the Cruiserweight Division is showcased again now, this time as the current Number One contender, Hayabusa faces off against another masked man, the extremely agile Blitzkrieg…..

…..Hayabusa is clearly no slouch either…..and as well as agility, he possesses something that Blitzkrieg doesn’t…..presence…..there is clearly something special about this young man, something that James Vandenberg is going to exploit in an effort to bring home the Cruiserweight Championship, and FINALLY eradicate Jeff Hardy once and for all…..

…..of course, that might be easier said than done, because if Jeff Hardy has proved anything as of late, it’s that he possesses a never say die attitude, and he’s developed quite the knack for overcoming the odds…..and if Monday night is anything to go by, he’s also riding a huge wave of confidence after managing to defeat Mortis on Nitro…..

…..but enough about Jeff Hardy, this is about Hayabusa…..and Hayabusa soundly defeated Blitzkrieg rather easily here, finishing him off with an impressive Phoenix Splash…..

Winner: Hayabusa
Rating: B-

…..Vandenberg (and the rest of his congregation) join Hayabusa in the ring, where this somewhat sinister minister grabs a microphone, to deliver the message that Jeff Hardy so rudely interrupted on Monday Night…..

…..he says that Mortis may have fallen at the final hurdle on Nitro…..Tajiri may have failed him at Starrcade…..Shinzaki might have come up short in his efforts to liberate the Cruiserweight Championship from the clutches of the un-pure…..but Hayabusa…..Hayabusa is set to be his avenging angel…..his fiery sword…..his purifier…..

“…..young Jeffery, I applaud your spirit…..and was it not for the fact that you appear unable to rise above the need for the approval of these moronic fans, maybe it could have been YOU at my right hand…..but be it naivety…..ego…..or plain stupidity, you are determined to position yourself in my way…..so be it…..because at the aptly named Souled Out, my angel of death will finally irradiate YOUR soul…..as we bring the Cruiserweight Championship home, to the Vestibule of Vandenberg…..”

Rating: B

…..from there we go backstage, where Mike Tenay is standing between two men who are in action next…..Tag Team action that is, when they face off against the Forgotten combination of Bam Bam Bigelow and Big Van Vader…..Curt Hennig and The Rock…..

…..Tenay says that this past Monday night, things got a little heated between the two of them following Curt Hennig’s defeat to Lord Steven Regal……TONIGHT, they will absolutely have to be on the same page if they want to have any chance of defeating their opponents…..because Vader and Bigelow are two big, big men, who will utilise ANY miscommunication to their advantage…..have they put that tension behind them?…..

…..Rocky fields that question….telling Tenay that he better than anyone knows exactly what the Forgotten are capable of……

“…..see this ain’t the Rock’s first rodeo with the Forgotten Mike Tenay…..no, no, no, no, no…..The Rock, he’s faced the Forgotten before…..he’s gone one on one with the Crypt Keeper Kevin Sullivan…..The Rock beat the cobwebs out of his beard and shoved a pentagram up his candy ass…..The Rock, he’s stepped into the ring with Bam Bam…..slapped him so hard upside his head that he put those stupid flames out…..the only thing left on Bam Bam’s skull now is the Rock’s handprint…..

…..but Pebbles there…..the big man…..the bigger man…..Big Van Vader…..see the Rock, The Rock didn’t beat Vader…..sure the Rock kicked his candy ass…..The Rock bounced him around that ring and left him laying like the big sack of brontosaurus crap that he is…..but the Rock didn’t win, and the Rock didn’t win because of one man…..Eddie Guerrero…..”

…..Rocky says that he and Vader have some unfinished business, but it’s about to be finished tonight…..because he’s going to hit Bam Bam with the People’s Elbow…..he’s going to drop Vader with the Rock Bottom…..and then at Souled Out, FINALLY, The Rock will lay the smack down on Eddie Guerrero’s Candy Ass, to become the NEW WCW United States Champion…..

“……IF YA SMELLLLLLLLLL……”

“…..hold on there ‘Rocky’…..”

…..hold that thought…..because Curt Hennig has something to say…..

“…..because believe it or not, it’s not ALL about you…..YOU’RE going to do this…..YOU’RE going to do that…..YOU’RE going to beat the Forgotten…..YOU’RE going to become the United States Champion…..maybe YOU should remember, that YOU only have that US Title shot to begin with, because I made sure that Chavo Guerrero didn’t interfere in your match again…..

…..maybe YOU should remember, that this is a TAG TEAM match tonight…..and maybe…..maybe the next time you leave me out there to get my ass jumped by Eddie Guerrero…..maybe YOU should remember, that the special enforcer in YOUR US Title match at Souled Out……is ME…..”

…..Hennig tells Rocky that he might think that this is HIS show…..but if the so-called ‘Great One’ isn’t there for Curt Hennig tonight, then maybe Curt Hennig will have to rethink his priorities at Souled Out…..

…..so I guess (in answer to Tenay’s initial question) it’s safe to say that the tension between The Rock and Curt Hennig is still very much there…..

…..let’s get to the match…..

Rating: A*

The Rock & Curt Hennig vs. The Forgotten


…..talk about the wrong team to be facing when you’re already having ‘difficulties’…..The Rock and Curt Hennig would have a hard enough time defeating Big Van Vader and Bam Bam Bigelow on any other given day, let alone on a day when tensions between the two are somewhat high…..

…..realistically, this is a match that neither The Forgotten nor The Rock can afford to lose…..after all, Vader and Bam Bam are just three days away from their first title match in over six months (when they face DDP AND Chris Benoit for the WCW Tag Team Titles) while The Rock, he’s got his United States Title rematch with Eddie Guerrero coming up…..picking up a loss here would seriously derail the losing man’s momentum…..

…..The Rock touched on it a minute ago, but he also has the added pressure of the fact that he has never defeated Vader…..a fact which has clearly ignited a fire under the People’s Champ…..

…..for what it’s worth, Hennig and Rocky actually work pretty well together…..especially in the opening moments of the match…..neither man is exactly what you would call a Cruiserweight, but both are considerably quicker than Vader and Bam Bam and they use that speed advantage to take control of the match…..

…..eventually however, it’s Rocky that gets to play the face in peril, as The Forgotten (with a little help from Kevin Sullivan) gain the advantage and cut the ring in half…..

…..to his credit, Hennig clearly wants the win here tonight, and he frantically cheers his partner on from the corner (in between breaking up pinfall attempts) and when Rock DOES finally make the hot tag, he hits the ring like a house of fire…..

…..he somehow manages to fend off both Vader and Bammers long enough for Rocky to get back into the fray…..and then a four way brawl breaks out as the match spirals out of control…..

…..finally the baby faces seem to garner the upper hand, with Rocky sending Bammers flying through the ropes, as Hennig looks to be going for the impossible…..he’s hooking Big Van Vader for the Hennig Plex…..

…..which (as Sullivan hops up onto the apron to distract the official) Eddie Guerrero slips into the ring behind Rocky, and launches his Souled Out opponent into the back of Curt Hennig, just as Curt was about to drive Vader to the mat with the Hennig Plex…..

…..Eddie quickly rolls out of the ring (and hides out of sight on the floor) as Hennig turns around…..and Curt is furious that The Rock appears to have broken up his Hennig Plex attempt, asking what his problem is, before shoving Rocky in the chest…..

…..Bigelow hits the ring now and takes out Rocky from behind, as Curt shrugs his shoulders at his partners current predicament, instead turning back to face Vader…..

…..who kicks him in the gut…..

…..and Powerbomb him to the mat…..

…..for the one, two, three…..

…..the Forgotten win…..

Winner(s): The Forgotten
Rating: B+

…..and they don’t stick around either…..with their victory in the bag their work here is done, so Sully leads his men to the back, presumably to begin working on their game plan for Sunday night…..

…..Hennig drags himself back to his feet using the ropes, as Rocky rolls back into the ring…..and Curt’s mood right now is anything from perfect…..

…..he again shoves the Rock in the chest, wanting to know why he stopped him from going for the Hennig Plex…..was it because the Rock’s ego didn’t want anyone other than himself getting the victory?…..Rock doesn’t like the shove in the chest, but he still protests his innocence, telling Curt that it was Eddie Guerrero that caused the problem…..

…..Curt smiles at that, before theatrically searching the ring, telling Rock that unless Eddie Guerrero has suddenly mastered invisibility, he has a hard time buying that (because Eddie is indeed nowhere to be seen) before again shoving Rock in the chest, telling him that he warned him about this…..Rock can only take so much though, and he shoves Hennig back…..

…..and then things get a little heated…..

…..because Curt slaps Rocky in the face…..

…..and Rocky slaps him right back…..

…..and then the pair begin trading blows…..

…..as security rushes the ring to separate them…..

…..and the camera catches sight of Eddie Guerrero smirking in the crowd (cleverly disguised with a fans hat hiding his mullet) as we go to the back…..

….although as Tony Schiavone points out, The Rock might want to request a different enforcer for Souled Out…..

Rating: A*

…..where Shawn Michaels and Jean Paul Levesque are in the locker room, as Shawn puts the finishing touches on lacing up his boots…..

…..the two men are discussing their upcoming match this Sunday with the Titans of Wrestling…..with Shawn telling Levesque that no matter what, make sure he stays in the back tonight…..

“…..because I don’t want ANYTHING coming between me, and me kicking Randy Savage’s teeth…..especially that match being cancelled because of Lex Luger…..after all, I shouldn’t have ANY trouble in finishing off old Flexy Lexy…..”

…..both men laugh at that, before Levesque taps Shawn on the shoulder conspiringly, telling him that he can level with him……DID he lay out Luger on Nitro?…..

“…..because, well…..I know it wasn’t me…..”

“…..well it wasn’t me…..”

“…..well it certainly want me…..”

“…..I’m telling you, it wasn’t me…..have I ever told a lie in my life?…..”

…..the two men laugh again at that, as Shawn stands up and slips on his entrance jacket, telling Paul not to worry…..he’ll be right back after he’s kicked Lugers teeth down his throat…..

…..and with that he struts out of the room, leaving the announcers with one question…..

…..was it Shawn Michaels that assaulted Lex Luger on Nitro?…..

…..our main event is next…..

Rating: B+

Lex Luger vs. Shawn Michaels


…..and it’s a main event that’s kinda been coming for a few weeks now…..ever since Shawn Michaels Superkicked Lex Luger in the face a few weeks back on Monday Nitro…..

…..sure the pair have faced off in Tag Team competition since then, but that match was kind of inconclusive considering the fact that the Titans of Wrestling interjected themselves into it…..since then they’ve teamed together (which didn’t go so well) and just three days ago on Nitro, Luger was found unconscious in the back, with the words “Clique Rules” conveniently spray painted on the wall behind him…..

…..now, both Shawn Michaels and Jean Paul Levesque have denied responsibility for this attack…..but neither man has been particularly convincing, nor were they overly concerned when they heard that Luger had been taken down…..all in all I’d say this match has been a long time coming…..

…..Luger is straight up on fire for this one too…..he’s put in some strong performances in the last few months has our Lex and tonight is no exception, as he runs through Michaels with a series of clotheslines…..it’s clear that Michaels thought that he would take Lex out in no time tonight, but he’s soon finding out that The Total Package isn’t anybody’s pushover…..and with Jean Paul Levesque barred from ringside, Michaels could be in real trouble here…..

…..speaking of Levesque, we cut away to shots of him watching the match on a monitor intermittently through the contest, and he’s clearly frustrated that Shawn isn’t putting Luger away quite as easily as they both thought he would…..with a no holds barred match against Randy Savage and The Warrior on the horizon in just three days time, he could do without his partner taking too much of a beating here…..and as Luger sets up what looks to be the homeward stretch, he bangs his hand on the table in frustration, snatches something up from the floor…..

…..and storms out of the room…..

…..not to ringside I hope…..

…..back in the ring Luger picks up a near fall after nailing Michaels with that trademark forearm, before signalling to the approving crowd that he’s going t po go for the Torture Rack…..

…..and that’s when the Turnertron switches to a shot of the production area, where JPL has a video technician by the scruff of his neck, telling him to “play the damn tape I just gave you”…..

…..which he does…..

…..the tape in question? Well, it appears to be CCTV footage from outside the locker room of Lex Luger, which (from the time and date stamp) looks to be from this past Monday Night…..it shows Luger entering his locker room, followed a few moments later by the Titans of Wrestling’s Buff Bagwell, who pauses at the doorway, peeks inside…..places a black bag on the floor (out of which he pulls a bat) and then rushes into the room…..

…..there’s no shot of what happened INSIDE the room, but I’m sure we can draw our own conclusions…..especially as a few moments later, Buff re-emerges from the room…..puts the bat back inside the bag…..and pulls out a can of spray paint…..

…..back inside the ring Lex Luger completely taken his eye off his opponent to watch this footage…..and as the Turnertron flicks off (taking with it any doubt that Shawn Michaels WAS in fact an innocent man) he turns back around…..

…..and gets kicked right in the mouth…..

…..Michaels drops down on top of Lex…..

…..the official counts three…..

…..and this one is all over…..

…..Jean Paul Levesque may have been barred from ringside, but it looks as though he still found a way to influence the outcome of this match…..and as Shawn gives (the still flat on his back) Luger the Crotch Chop, he rolls to the outside, where he’s met by JPL…..and as the two friends exit stage left (loudly informing a nearby camera that they TOLD us they were innocent) they let Savage and Warrior know that they’re going to take them out for good at Souled Out…..

…..meanwhile in the ring, Lex is only just coming around…..it’s a kind of good day/bad day scenario for The Total Package…..on the one hand, he just lost a pretty high profile match…..but on the other hand…..

…..he at least now knows who to direct his anger at…..

…..Buff Bagwell…..

…..we’ll see you Sunday fans…..

Winner: Shawn Michaels
Rating: B

Final Rating: B
