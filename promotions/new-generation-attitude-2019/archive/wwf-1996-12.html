Week 1, December 1996

Key Arena

ATT: 14,868

TV: 18.88

The show opens in much the same way as last week….with the owner and CEO of the World Wrestling Federation, Mr. Vince McMahon strutting his way down the aisle. He waits for the crowd to die down before cutting right to the chase…..he says that last week, he gave Kevin Nash seven days to sign his new and very much improved contract…..swallow his pride…..and walk down that aisle to shake the hand of the Heartbreak Kid Shawn Michaels…..and ensure that those KLiQ dollars keep on rolling in….so first things first, he brings out the KLiQ….

…..who once again make two separate entrances…..one for Hall, Waltman and Credible…..and a second, spotlight driven entrance for the WWF Champion Shawn Michaels (and Sunny who is hanging off his arm).

Vince spends a few moments fawning over Michaels, while the rest of the KLiQ stand around looking disinterested…..clearly it’s only the money keeping them at Michaels side right now….before breaking the bad news that before they get down to reuniting the Black and White express, there is the small matter of In Your House to discuss…..and the fact that Michaels will need to defend the WWF Championship at the event….

“…..Vin man, relax……I’m Shawn Michaels….the Showstopper….the Main Event…..you line them up, and I’ll knock them down…..”

Shawn says that he knows who won’t be challenging him at the Pay Per View…..it wont be either Bret or Owen Hart…..it wont be Steve Austin or Brian Pillman…..and it wont be Big Pile of Crap Scott Steiner, or whatever stupid nickname he’s given himself…..Vince says no, before telling him that at In Your House, he’s going to be defending the WWF Championship against Ahmed Johnson.

Surprisingly Shawn doesn’t bat an eye at that…..saying that it doesn’t matter who steps between those ropes….because with the KLiQ by his side, there’s no one here in the WWF…..or ANY company…..that can take this Title from around Shawn Michaels waist…..

“……so let’s get this show on the road…..Kev, Big Man…..walk yourself down that aisle…..give your old buddy The Heartbreak Kid a big Million Dollar Handshake…..and lets get the band back together…..because a great front man is only as good as his backing group…..and the best front man in all of professional wrestling, doesn’t like to be kept waiting…..”

….::::!!!!GLASS SHATTERS!!!!::::….

……well that’s not Kevin Nash…….it is however an extremely p*ssed off Stone Cold Steve Austin……and a slightly unhinged Loose Cannon Brian Pillman……and considering the reaction these two men were garnering just a month ago, the crowd explodes for them here as they march down the aisle.

Michaels doesn’t like the look of that, and orders Hall, Waltman and Credible to get to the floor to keep them away from him….at first it looks as though the KLiQ aren’t planning on going anywhere, but McMahon barks that if they want to keep their lucrative contracts, then they had better protect his prize asset…..the WWF Champion. The trio finally head to the floor to meet the charging Austin and Pillman…..and a wild brawl ensues…..a wild brawl that almost sees the former Tag Team Champions waylay the KLiQ and storm the ring…..but McMahon calls out an army of security from the back, who are able to contain the Loose Cannon and Stone Cold…..before eventually managing to handcuff the pair and drag them back up the aisle, kicking and cursing the whole way. Hall, Waltman and Credible are battered and bruised, but thankfully, much to Vince McMahons relief, Shawn Michaels is unharmed. The close call however has left the owner flustered….demanding that Austin and Pillman be escorted to the nearest law enforcement venue, where he wants them detained for Shawn Michaels safety….before letting Kevin Nash know that he has until the end of Monday Night Raw, to show up and do the right thing.

Rating: A*

VS.

Marc Mero (w/Sable) vs. Mr. Perfect

Mr. Perfect made it two for two last week, because after finding himself on the winning side at the Survivor Series, he once again had his hand raised in last weeks Tag Team match…..can he make it the Perfect trifecta tonight? Not if Marc Mero has anything to say on the matter…..especially since he found himself on the losing side of both of those wins. Perfect stops via the commentary table on his way to the ring to greet Raws newest announcer Ravishing Rick Rude…..who first informs us that He and Mr. Perfect are close friends who go way back….and secondly, calls the least impartial match since Bobby Heenan’s tour de force during the 1992 Royal Rumble match.

Jim Ross questions whether or not there are some conflicts of interest involved now that Mr. Perfect has returned to active competition, considering that he is supposed to be concentrating on guiding the career of Billy Gunn…..but Rude says no…..Mr. Perfect can be the Perfect Executive Consultant AS WELL as being the Perfect in ring competitor…..surprisingly Billy Gunn is not out there with Perfect tonight….but we do have a surprise arrival…..

…..as after a close, hard fought battle…..Jesse James of all people makes his way down to ringside…..and behind the back of the official, trips the leg of Mero, who stumbles straight into the Perfect-Plex and the one, two, three.

Winner: Mr. Perfect

Rating: B

The match is in the bag, and although he continues his Perfect return…..Mr. Perfect is somewhat perplexed by the reason for the assist of Jesse James….James is unphased however hopping into the ring to raise the arm of the victor…during which the cameras pick up the young man’s reason for being there….he’s looking for an executive consultant of his own to take him to the very top. Perfect considers it for a moment, before smiling and telling Jesse James that they should talk in the back.

Rating: B+

Mr. Perfect and Jesse James aren’t the only ones heading to the back….because we go there ourselves now, where Dok Hendrix is standing by the Rikishi and Kokina, the Sons of Samoa. Dok begins by asking how Rocky Johnson is following the devastating betrayal by his own son…..their view on the man recently renamed The Rock…..and their chances of capturing the WWF Tag Team Championships from the KLiQ, especially since the Nation of Domination seem hell bent on making sure that doesn’t happen.

Rikishi says obviously Rocky Johnson is a broken man about now…..so is his mother Ata Maivia, and the whole Samoan community….but you can be sure of one thing…..”The Rock”…..or whatever he wants to call himself now…..he’s gonna get his…..along with the rest of the Nation of Domination…..right after the Sons of Samoa take those Tag Titles home to the Islands…..Rikishi says there has never been a better time to be facing the KLiQ…..he says money talks for now, but Shawn Michaels ego is gonna tear that whole group apart….and Rikishi and the big man Kokina, they’re gonna be there to pick the bones.

Rating: B

VS.

Rey Mysterio vs. Kane (w/Jim Cornette)

Oh boy…..Rey Mysterio truly does have the heart of a lion…..and while he may be the current WWF Light Heavyweight Champion, he clearly has his eye on bigger things…..much bigger things…..because they don’t come any bigger or badder than Kane. Mysterio requested this match up off the back of Kanes unprovoked attack during last weeks Light Heavyweight Championship match, but he’s bitten off far more than he can chew here…..because Kane has him out powered, out weighed….and frankly out matched….because nothing that Mysterio does has an impact on the Big Red Monster. Mysterio to his credit will never say die….and he doesn’t back down….and in all reality his is kicking out of moves when it would really be in his best interest to just stay down…..in fact, Kane brutalises the little luchador so much…..

……that Chris Candido eventually makes his way down the aisle…..and after pleading with Mysterio to just stay down, eventually throws the towel in for the Light Heavyweight Champion. The official sees the white object….and while Candido has no authority to quit for Mysterio, clearly he agrees with the sentiment…..because he calls an end to the match and the punishment. Rude questions why Candido would care what happened to Rey Mysterio, but Jim Ross informs us that it may be a case of protecting his investment….because Chris Candido has a shot at that Light Heavyweight Championship coming up at In Your House…..and an injured Champion can’t defend the title.

Winner: Kane (by stoppage)

Rating: B

It’s promo time here again tonight….as Chris Jericho makes his way down to the ring. Jericho is a prime example of what Vince McMahon wanted the “new generation” to be when he first debuted the concept a few years prior…..he’s young….he’s hungry….he can work….he can talk…..the dirt sheets love him….the fans love him…..and when he was given the ball and the chance to headline his first Pay Per View against the WWF Champion, Shawn Michaels, he took that ball and ran with a match of the year performance.

Unfortunately for him, he performed so well in there with Shawn Michaels, that the Heartbreak Kid decided that he needed to remove Jericho from the title picture…..which is exactly what he did, by bringing Jericho’s former tag Partner, Lance Storm into the WWF to keep Lionheart’s eye off the top prize…..and apparently that’s exactly what he’s done…..because Jericho is out here to let Lance Storm know that WWF President Gorilla Monsoon, has sanctioned a match between the pair at the upcoming In Your House event.

Jericho says that ever since Lance Storms arrival, he has done thing but cheap shot and attack Jericho from behind…..and while he may have eliminated Lionheart from the Survivor Series match, that was only because Jericho had a mountain to climb, facing four men by himself. He tells Storm that he’s not entirely sure what his problem is…..

“…..I never double crossed you, I never stabbed you in the back….my only crime, was wanting to compete in the BEST wrestling company in the world…..and the fact that I got signed off my own back…..and YOU had to bought in by Shawn Michaels, because the Boy Toy knew that it was only a matter of time until that WWF Championship was around MY sexy waist……and that’s fine……because once I’ve finished with you at In Your House, I’ll be moving on to the Royal Rumble, where I’ll outlast 29 other superstars to main event Wrestlemania…….”

Jericho tells Storm that if you’ve got any sense in that head of yours….you should concentrate on showing people WHY you deserve to be in the WWF…..because Lance really are one of the best technical wrestlers on the face of the earth……..unfortunately, he’s no Chris Jericho…..and at In Your House, Lionheart will prove just that.

Rating: B

While Jericho is heading to the back, we go to a quick segment with Dok Hendrix, who’s standing by with big Psycho Sid. Sid is still smarting from being on the receiving end of a number of Jeff Jarrett guitar shots now…..the first of which arguably cost him the WWF Championship, while the second ensured that he was eliminated from the Survivor Series match…..obviously Sid is not a happy camper, basically telling Jarrett to……

“……count your days……count your dollars……and count your blessings……because they’re all gonna come crashing down around you, when you step into that ring…..with the master……and the ruler…..of the world…..see Jarrett, you might have sold your soul to the Million Dollar Man…..but your ass belongs to me…..”

Sid says he doesn’t know where, he doesn’t know when, but somewhere down the line, Jeff Jarrett is going to pay for his actions.

Rating: B+

VS.

Jerry Lynn vs. Christopher Daniels

It’s time to showcase the light heavyweights now…..and while Jerry Lynn has had a few opportunities to showcase his talents….most prominently when he challenged Sean Waltman for the Division’s Championship….Christopher Daniels has been more involved in The Flocks affairs to fully show what he can do. Both men pull out all the stops tonight, and it’s a good little match with lots of back and forth action…..

…..the firing of Hunter Hearst Helmsley a few months back has also played out in Daniels favour….because he is able to revert to his usual finishing manoeuvre, the Pedigree style Angels Wings…..which he uses here tonight to dispose of his opponent.

Winner: The Fallen Angel, Christopher Daniels

Rating: C+

As The Fallen Angel celebrates a hard fought victory, he is joined by a figure walking down the aisle…..Sherlock Snow and Doctor Head, still dressed in their ridiculous attire. Snow gets into the ring and congratulates his “team mate”, but says he has other pressing matters to attend to……he says that last week, he was rudely interrupted before he was able to finish storytime with Sherlock Snow (bay-bay)…..he says that when they left off last week, their fearless hero Dude Love was about to make his first on screen debut……in “The Loved One”……before indicating to the video screen……

……where a grainy old home movie is displayed…..and the “plot” (if it can be called that, is told in a series of flashbacks, showing a young Mick Foleys long journey….beginning with a failed suicide attempt (using a gun that shoots ping-pong balls) brought on after Mick propositions what he thinks is a woman but is actually a man. Eventually, at the urging of his friends, Mick decides to “get back into wrestling,” using “The Masked Debater” as his gimmick……Snow pauses the video for a moment to answer Head, who appears to have a question…..

“…..The Masked Debater?…..you don’t get it?……….Do I have to spell it out for you?……Say “The Masked Debater” three times fast…….faster……..now you get it……yes I know it’s juvenile, but can we move on?……thank you…..”

….after a supposed run of successful matches (which we don’t actually see), The Debater is introduced to some female wrestling fans, and he turns on all his charm to get the girls…..it doesn’t actually go according to plan however, and the ladies end up laying a beatdown of the Debator. Knowing that suicide didn’t work last time, Debater decides he has only one choice….he has to contact The Grand Lizard of Wrestling.

He finally tracks down and introduces himself to The Lizard…..who is less than impressed with the hapless Debater. He showers his would be student with a series of insults (including the academy award worth line, “I wouldn’t let you shake the urine off the tip of my penis”) Eventually however, the Lizard agrees to train him, but he’ll have to do it the Lizard’s way…..and he rips Debater’s shirt off, revealing a heart-shaped tattoo (drawn on with magic marker) squarely in the middle of Mick’s chest. The Lizard then presents the Debater with his “first pajama top,” and Dude Love is born…..

…….which unfortunately is all we are going to see tonight….because Raven is out with the Flock (and a rather sheepish looking Mankind) in tow….and he’s not amused…..he tells Al that he told him to stop this nonsense last week……he’s telling him again now for a final time…..Raven doesn’t want Dude Love…..he doesn’t need Dude Love…..and he certainly doesn’t want to waste his time with someone as useless and pathetic as Dude Love…..

“…….I stared death himself in the face last week…..and he WILL seek vengeance…..I don’t need a pathetic shell of a man living in a dream world…..I need a man so evil and depraved, that even Satan himself allowed his escape from the depths of hell, because sometimes, even the Devil has limits to his depravities…..”

Raven tells Al to get to the outside with the rest of the Family…..because there is a reckoning coming, and they need to be prepared…..it looks as though “The Loved One” will have to wait…..

Rating: A

VS.

Savio Vega vs. Raven

Savio Vega gets a rare appearance on Monday Night Raw tonight, as he looks to go one on one with the Maestro of the Misunderstood, Raven. These two actually gel really well in the ring, with complimenting styles that lead to a pretty good, old fashioned, down and dirty brawl. It’s a shame that Savio has been spinning his wheels as of late, because he’s a useful hand to have about the place….and he rarely fails to put out a decent performance…..in the end tonight however he was no match for Raven……and the Shepherd of the Flock was able to finish him off with the Evenflow DDT.

Winner: Raven

Rating: B

Raven rolls Savio to the outside, where the Flock surprisingly stand back and let him leave. Instead of celebrating however, Raven simply drops to his knees with his arms outstretched, crucifix style…..almost as if he is waiting for something…….or someone…..

….::::!!!!GONG!!!!::::….

……well, if he was waiting for the Deadman to make an appearance, it looks like he has gotten his wish…..because The Undertaker is here. He begins his slow walk down the aisle, only to be met at ringside by a wall of Flock members…..Bill DeMott, Doink, Daniels and the Dogs of War. Al Snow has made no effort to join his partners…..but Mankind is front and centre like a shot, blocking the Deadman’s path. Raven however tells his followers to move aside and let him pass, making sure to tell Mankind that he “….couldn’t stop him before…..Mankind isn’t going to stop him now……we both know who COULD stop the Undertaker……but Mankind would rather see me suffer, than release his inner demon…..so move…..”

The Flock part like the Red Sea….and Mankind reluctantly steps to one side (as a smattering of a Dude Love chant breaks out) leaving the Undertaker free to enter the ring. He walks towards Raven, who is still on his knees….arms outstretched……The Undertaker takes a microphone, declaring that Raven clearly wanted to dance with the devil, well here he is…..so dance. Raven slowly stands to his feet, only now he has a smirk on his face, causing the Undertaker to question “…..what’s so funny, boy?…..”

“…….what’s funny, is the fact that your ego is such, that you can’t even comprehend that you are nothing more than a pawn…..a nonentity so insignificant to the existence of greater men, that you are nothing more than an afterthought……the phenom of the World Wrestling Federation, reduced to a quivering wreck by his baby brother…..the conscience of the WWF, staring into the eyes of one without such an affliction……

……your entire presence is of little consequence to me…..I don’t fear the reaper…..nor do I particularly have any desire to face the reaper…..but for this fleeting moment you do serve a purpose…..you’re a needs to an end…..the precursor to the punchline…..you will be the conduit through which my ultimate desire will flow……in essence, YOU Deadman…..will bring me what I want…..you will bring me Jack…..or you will destroy me…..so it is written, so it shall come to pass…..a destiny not written by my hand…..nor by yours……..but by his……”

With that Raven turns and points down to Mankind, saying that for once, neither the Undertaker, nor Raven is in control of their own futures……fate has landed that role in the hand of Mankind…..and HIS choices alone will dictate the path of things to come. The Undertaker however seems uninterested in Raven’s ramblings……he’s here to fight…….and he snatches the leader of the Flock by the throat. Raven’s followers (with the exception of Al Snow) jump onto the apron, but Raven holds up a hand, telling them to stand down “……what happens here happens because of YOU Mankind……and all of the world shall bear witness to your failings…..”

Which is when Raven is chokeslammed straight to hell as the Flock (again with the exception of Al Snow) look on in horror. The Undertaker picks up a microphone and tells Raven that vengeance is coming for him at In Your House…..and the world will see just who it is that is insignificant…..before dropping the microphone on his chest, then turning and heading to the back, leaving Raven flattened on the mat…….but with a huge smile on his face, causing Jim Ross to remark that “….that’s one sick puppy right there folks…..who may have gotten more than he bargained for….because at In Your House, Raven will be going one on one with the Deadman…..”

Rating: A*

We are back with Dok Hendrix, who this time is standing by with two men who are both challenging for gold at In Your House…..Scott Steiner will be facing his treacherous brother Rich Steiner, with the Million Dollar Corporation members Intercontinental Championship on the line, while Ahmed Johnson has the opportunity of a lifetime, when he goes one on one with the Heartbreak Kid Shawn Michaels, for a shot at the World Wrestling Federation Championship.

Dok asks both men what they think their chances are going into the event. Ahmed says that he’s worked his whole life to get this shot…..and his whole life has been a battle since the day he was born…..so make no mistake, at In Your House….when Ahmed Johnson finally gets his chance to take home the WWF Title…..he’s gonna grab it with both hands……because Shawn Michaels…..you’re going down.

Scotty is a little (a lot) more intense…..screaming into the microphone that….

“……AT IN YOUR HOUSE, BIG POPPA PUMP IS GONNA SHOW THE WORLD WHY HE’S THE GENETIC FREAK OF THE STEINER BROTHERS…..RICK, YOU SCREWED OVER YOUR OWN BROTHER FOR MONEY…..WELL I DON’T NEED A MILLION DOLLAR MANAGER, BECAUSE I’VE GOT A MILLION DOLLAR BODY, WITH THE LARGEST ARMS IN THE WORLD….AND I’M GONNA POP YOUR HEAD LIKE A PIMPLE….THEN I’M GONNA TAKE THAT INTERCONTINENTAL TITLE ALL THE WAY TO THE ROYAL RUMBLE, PICK TWENTY NINE OTHER PIPSQUEAKS UP OVER MY HEAD…..AND TOSS THEM ALL THE WAY INTO THE SUN….BECAUSE I’M STRONG…..AND I CAN THROW PEOPLE A LONG WAY…..

…..THEN I’M GONNA GO ALL THE WAY TO WRESTLEMANIA, BECAUSE ONE WAY OR ANOTHER, I’M TAKING BACK THAT WWF TITLE THAT I WON FAIR AND SQUARE…..THEN YOU’RE GONNA SEE AN INTERCONTINENTAL BELT HANGING OVER THIS PEAK (Steiner flexes his huge left bicep) AND THE WORLD WRESTLING FEDERATION BELT HANGING OVER THIS PEAK (Steiner then flexes the right) AND THEN WHEN I’M DONE WITH THAT, I’M GONNA TAKE SHAWN MICHAELS OLD LADY (Steiner then looks down at his crotch area) AND HANG HER RIGHT OVER MY……..

Dok Hendrix cuts in right there with a worried look on his face, telling Steiner that they’re out of time, because their match is up next.

Rating: A

VS.

Scott Steiner & Ahmed Johnson vs. Shawn Michaels & Rich Steiner (w/Ted DiBiase)

There is gold galore at ringside for this one, with the WWF Champion and the Intercontinental Champion both involved in the match, and Scott Hall wearing one half of the WWF Tag Team Championships on the outside, along with Sean Waltman, Justin Credible and Sunny who have accompanied Michaels to the ring. Vince McMahon has also taken it upon himself to sit at the commentary table, where he is confident that Kevin Nash will soon make his triumphant return, to shake the hand of Shawn Michaels.

As for the match itself, Shawn Michaels picks right up with Rich Steiner where he left off with his KLiQ brothers……directing traffic, keeping himself out of harms way….and generally being an obnoxious p*ick….so much so that it’s Steiner who takes the majority of the beating that Ahmed and Scotty dish out in the opening minutes…..at least until Ahmed finds himself on the receiving end of the punishment, then Michaels is much more willing to interject himself into the match.

Eventually however, Johnson is able to make the hot Tag to Big Poppa Pump, and once again Rich Steiner finds himself battling without a Tag Team partner…..so much so that eventually…(and not for the first time in recent weeks)…..Steiner decides enough is enough….rolls to the outside to collect his Intercontinental Championship….and walks out of the match and to the back, leaving Michaels all alone……until some familiar music begins to play…..music that we haven’t heard for quite some time……

……because Kevin Nash is here, but it’s not the KLiQ’s theme that he’s making his way out to….it’s the old Diesel music…..and it throws Shawn Michaels for a loop…..so much so that Ahmed Johnson is able to catch hold of the WWF Champion, and nail him with the Pearl River Plunge for the one, two, three……..and just a few weeks away from their In Your House clash, Ahmed Johnson has picked up a huge victory over the Heartbreak Kid Shawn Michaels.

Winner(s): Ahmed Johnson & Scott Steiner

Rating: B+

Shawn Michaels is out cold, flat on his back in the middle of the mat, as Kevin Nash steps over the top rope and into the ring…..the rest of the KLiQ (and Vince McMahon) are in like a shot, as Nash stands tall over the fallen Michaels…..before slowly reaching into his pocket and pulling out a piece of paper, which is presumably his new and improved contract. He reaches into McMahons blazer and brings out a pen, before dropping to one knee…..rolling the WWF Champion over…..and signing the contract on Shawn Michaels back, before tossing the piece of paper in Vince’s direction. He then abruptly rolls The Heartbreak Kid over again……picks his limp lifeless arm up off the mat……and shakes the hand of the unconscious Champion. Nash then stands to his feet, stares a hole through each KLiQ member…..then abruptly turns and leaves without uttering a single word, as the show comes to a close.

Rating: A*

Final Rating: A

Foxtel are one of the smallest channels in Australia, but they were the only network that would touch us a year ago…..in January however we will be moving Raw to Channel 10……one of the biggest channels in Australia…..and at Prime Time too….it shows how much we have grown as a company in the last 12 months.

Week 2, December 1996

Week 2, December 1996

Fleet Centre

ATT: 18,000

TV: 19.71

The show opens up to the now familiar sight of Vincent K McMahon and the KLiQ standing in the centre of the ring…..the only slight variation on the last two weeks is the fact that Kevin Nash is once again back in the fold……unlike the rest of the group however, who are wearing those sweet black and white shirts, Big Sexy is wearing a Shawn Michaels shirt, while standing separate from the rest of the group, leaning up against the turnbuckles in the corner.

McMahon welcomes us to the show, before saying that while last weeks reunion between Kevin Nash and Shawn Michaels didn’t exactly go the way he planned…..it did at least happen……

“…..so ladies and gentlemen, I give you…..your heros…..the number one merchandise sellers in the WWF…..and the reason you’re all here tonight……..The KLiQ…….”

Shawn Michaels stands glaring at Kevin Nash for a moment, before whispering something in Vince’s ear…..

“…….let me rephrase that……Ladies and Gentlemen……I give you……the reason for the ratings……Shawn Michaels……and The KLiQ……”

Michaels smirks at Nash, before taking the microphone off Vince. He begins by first of all telling Kevin how nice it is to see him back……

“……and that’s a great shirt you’re wearing there…..it really suits you…..and I just want to say, no hard feelings buddy…..I know things got a little bit strained between us recently…..but when you’re as close as us, that’s bound to happen…….oh……and I’ve even got you a gift……since I know what a “Bad Ass” you are……and how much you like to fight…….I’ve got you a match tonight……in fact it’s gonna happen right now…….when Big Daddy Cool, Big Sexy Kevin Nash……goes one on one with old mumblemouth himself, Ahmed Johnson……”

Nash smiles and asks Shawn if he means his In Your House World Title opponent Ahmed Johnson? Shawn pauses for a moment, before channeling his inner “Home Alone” and placing both hands on his face in shock, telling Nash that it never occurred to him, but yes…..what a coincidence…..that very same Ahmed Johnson.

Big Sexy has the same fake smile plastered across his face that the Heartbreak Kid has, telling Shawn that as long as Vince McMahon there keeps paying the big bucks, Kevin Nash has no problem kicking anyone’s ass…….before staring Shawn in the eye, repeating “…anyone….”

“…..ok then……glad we got that cleared up……and since I was kinda…….’indisposed’ last week……how about that handshake…..pal?…..”

Nash smiles and opens his arms wide, telling Shawn that he can do one better than that……

“…..come here buddy…..”

…..before wrapping his arms around Michaels in a big bear hug…..that was a little too tight and a little too long not to awkward……before letting go and ruffling the champs hair.

Vince McMahon finally breaks the tension, announcing that due to the Million Dollar Intercontinental Champion Rich Steiner abandoning the match last week…..and leaving the WWF Champion Shawn Michaels alone and possibly risking serious injury…..The Heartbreak Kid will have the opportunity to extract his revenge tonight……when it’s Champion vs Champion…..non-title obviously……as Shawn Michaels faces Rich Steiner…….and since Shawn is THE main event…..it will be IN the main event……and since Ted DiBiase’s men obviously can’t conduct their business in a professional manner, it won’t just be Steiner that suffers…….because Jeff Jarrett will be facing Psycho Sid at In Your House……because Vince McMahon always gets what he wants……

……but that’s not all……because to ensure that we don’t have any rude interruptions like last week…….McMahon has also hired extra security to keep Stone Cold Steve Austin and The Loose Cannon Brian Pillman OUT of the building……so Ahmed Johnson, get your ass out here so we can get this show started.

Rating: A*

Regardless of the Dude’s comedy value, there is no doubting Mick Foley’s ability when it comes to matches of a more Hardcore variety……and Bill DeMott, while not in the Dude’s league, is equally adapt when it comes to brawling in the aisleway. Being that Foley is somewhat of a self-confessed Christmas fanatic, it must have broken his heart to be brawling around the staged Christmas gifts that are scattered around the arena…..but not as much as when Bill DeMott began to beat him with a fully decorated Christmas Tree.

The two men brawled up and down ringside, into the crowd and back again….but in the end, if this was supposed to be punishment for the Dude then it didn’t really work….because he had a blast….but non more so than when he rammed an oversized Santa Hat, fully over the head of DeMott….blinding him long enough to connect with a double arm DDT on the stage to pick up a pinfall victory…..much to the delight of the fans.

Winner: Dude Love

Rating: B+

Up next we have Lance Storm making his way down to the ring. He talks about being a man of his word, because like all great Canadians, there is nothing he values more than honesty…..he says that he vowed to defeat Chris Jericho at In Your House and that’s exactly what he did….he eliminated Chris Jericho from the Survivor Series…..he’s beaten Chris Jericho at every opportunity…..and proved without a shadow of a doubt, exactly who the Marty Jannetty around here is…..

Storm isn’t much of a talker however, which is why his promo time is kept short and sweet….and it’s not long before Chris Jericho makes his way out to show Lance how it’s done. He tells Storm that he’s seen amoeba with more personality than him, but worry not folks, because Chris Jericho, the personification of personality is here to save the day. He tells Lance that maybe he’s right…..that is twice now that he’s got the better of Jericho…..and as a congratulations, he’s going to play Secret Santa tonight……because he’s got his old friend a gift…..a Christmas surprise…..a Christmas Surprise opponent…..

……it’s Marty Jannetty…..(fresh out of his second stint in rehab)……and he doesn’t look happy that Lance Storm has been using his name as a veiled insult…..looks like we are about to find out who the real Marty Jannetty is around here.

Rating: B+

VS.

Marty Jannetty vs. Lance Storm

Marty Jannetty is one week out of rehab……and he’s already doing better than the last time he came out, because he was no showing work within a day then, meaning he got a second stint in the dry house on the WWF’s Dollar…..that means in the course of 12 months, Marty has spent 11 of them in rehab…..hopefully this second stay straightened him out…..because he won’t get a third….he’ll just be out of here.

As for this match, there was a little bit of ring rust on Marty’s side of things…..fortunately Lance is a good enough worker to mask that…..and it helped that Jericho remained at ringside to keep the crowd rowdy…..his presence worked in Jannetty’s favour too….because Jericho was able to prevent any blatant cheating from Storm, the likes of which cost Jericho the match at In Your House….in the end though, Jericho being at ringside possibly motivated Lance even more….because as Marty attempted to connect with a Super Kick reminiscent of the Sweet Chin Music, (which Jim Ross tells us is dubbed “Into the Void”…an homage to the Black Sabbath song) Storm was able to catch Jannetty’s foot and convert it into the single leg Boston crab….and just like that, Marty’s comeback moment was ruined by Canada’s own Lance Storm.

Winner: Lance Storm

Rating: C

We come back from commercial break, and we have gone from one Canadian to another……..two others actually……..and a Brit……..because despite Bret Harts insistence that he was going home, here he is….along with brother Owen and brother in-law Davey Boy Smith. The whole Hart Foundation get a great reception….. it none more so than the Hitman. Bret thanks the fans for the reception, before saying that he’s not here to try and take up too much of everyone’s time….but he’s been sat at home…..honouring the stipulation to the Survivor Series match….because despite the fact that Vince McMahon screwed over him and his brother, Bret is a man of his word…..but then he turns on his television, and what does he see? Vince McMahon allowing Steve Austin and Brian Pillman to enter the 1997 Royal Rumble match…..so basically, if Vince isn’t going to adhere to the stipulation, then neither is Bret…..and neither is Owen…..and they want into the Rumble too……

……that brings out the man himself…..Vince says that he may be feeling generous tonight…..which is why the British Bulldog WILL be in the Royal Rumble match….but he’s not feeling generous enough to just put Bret and Owen in like that….but since it IS Christmas, how about this…..at the Royal Rumble….The Hart Brothers team up to face Kevin Nash and Scott Hall…….non-title obviously……and if you win that match….THEN you will be entered into the Royal Rumble match later in the evening…..of course, they will be pretty beat up if they do make it to the match….but that’s the best he can do……Merry Christmas.

Rating: A*

VS.

Ho Ho Ho Handicap Match

Al Snow vs. The Dogs of War

It’s time to continue Raven’s punishment OF the Flock, WITH the Flock….it didn’t work out so well earlier in the evening, with Dude Love defeating Bill DeMott…..Al Snow however, (being the perpetrator of Mankinds transformation into Dude Love) has definitely got the more severe end of the punishment….as he has to face both of the Dogs of War in a ho ho ho handicap match. When you take into account the fact that this was a handicap match, the trio…..well, mainly Al Snow to be fair…..were able to make the match entertaining enough (with Al making the hot tag to Head at one point) but vicious enough from Wrath and Crush, to ensure that the story of Al’s suffering is told.

Eventually after holding his own for as long as physically possible against two animals like the Dogs of War…..Al Snow is put out of his misery with a big Double Chokeslam…..and then another for good measure…..and much to the displeasure of the crowd…..Al Snow is defeated.

Winner(s): The Dogs of War

Rating: B

With his vengeance delivered, Raven makes his way down the aisle to stand over his fallen follower. He picks up a microphone and drops to his knees beside Al Snow….

“……unfortunately….our sins will always be more easily remembered than the good we do….you may THINK, that by freeing that moron Dude Love, that you have saved Mick Foley from the monster that resides deep within himself…..but in reality, you’ve only delayed the inevitable……understand this…..I WILL have what I want…..I WILL have what I need…..I WILL…..have Cactus Jack…..and your good deed…..will not go unpunished…….

Raven motions to the Dogs of War, who drag Snow to his feet and hold him in place…..

“……..for no matter what I do, people only remember my so called sins…..because they are too blind to see that EVERYTHING I do….is for the greater good…..you were lost….walking aimlessly in the dark….until I bought you MY light to follow…..I validated your existence in the WWF….I took you from the bottom….and raised you to heights you could previously only dream of…..you may not like the way that I did it….because like I said, our sins will always be more easily remembered than the good we do…….but whether you like it or not….everything I did I did for YOU…..well no more…..Al Snow I cast you out…..and examples need to be made….for your actions……”

With that Raven clutches Snow by the head…..and drives him skull first into the mats with the Evenflow DDT. Snow is down and in no condition to fight back….which gives Raven ample time to drop to the floor, pick up a steel chair and slide back into the ring, dropping the foreign object down in the centre of the mat. He drags Snow back to his feet…and again positions him for the DDT….this time poised over the steel chair…….

……which is when Dude Love comes running down the aisle….he comes to a halt at ringside, grabbing a microphone and pleading with Raven not to do it…..but that just infuriates Raven more…..he asks The Dude why….why after everything that Raven has given him….does he insist on trying to end Al Snows suffering? “….what about me?…..WHAT ABOUT RAVEN?……”He tells the Dude that he knows how to end this…..he knows what he has to do……but the Dude shakes his head, telling Raven that “…..that bird has flown daddio….now the Dude is in the driving seat….and this cool cat is hard to de-rail…..” he says that even if he wanted to, he couldn’t get rid of the Dude now……but Raven simply replies that he will…..soon……and then he drives Snow head first into the steel with the Evenflow.

Dude Love drops to the mat to check on Al, but Raven drags him by the hair to his feet…..telling him that there is little cause for his false concern, when it’s YOU that has bought this suffering upon him. He then orders the Dude to the back, telling him that Al Snow has been excommunicated from the Flock….and his affairs are no longer of interest to Dude Love, Mankind“……or anybody else that’s lurking inside that damaged head of yours….”

Rating: A*

Following that we head backstage….and apparently to the Locker Room of the KLiQ. It’s surprisingly relaxed for once…..everyone is wearing a smile….Sunny has a very skimpy fur lined, red and white Mrs. Claus outfit on….and Shawn Michaels has a big sack at his side. He first of all thanks each KLiQ member for their “services” this year….stating that Shawn Michaels is nothing if not a humble World Champion. He reaches down into the sack and retrieves for elegantly wrapped boxes, which he hands out to each man. Nash looks down at the box, before asking “…..nothing for Sunny?….” Shawn just smiles at that, saying what Sunny wants, Sunny gets….all year round…..and all night long…..in fact, she had a beautiful pearl necklace….just this morning.

The four men unwrap their gifts to reveal expensive looking matching gold Rolex watches. The KLiQ seem impressed by this….but Shawn says that’s not all…..he has another gift for his good buddy Kevin Nash….he says that this is not for what Nash has done….but for what he’s going to do……and that is to ensure that the Hart Brothers are NOT in the Royal Rumble…..then Big Sexy will be entering the Royal Rumble match himself…..where he will make sure that a suitable winner comes out of the event. Nash smiles and takes the gift, telling Shawn that he can count on it…..Kevin Nash will personally guarantee…..that the right man…..will win. The two men stare each other down for a moment….before Nash finally unleashes a huge smile, opening up Shawn’s second gift…..a signed and framed 8×10 picture of Shawn Michaels. Big Sexy looks blankly at the picture, before smiling and telling the Heartbreak Kid that he will cherish it.

That’s not it though folks…..Shawn Michaels is the Champion that keeps giving…..because he points over to a door at the end of the room….telling the KLiQ that there are four more gifts, right in there….that desperately need unwrapping. With that the door swings open…..and four very scantily clad women are waiting inside. Michaels tells the boys to enjoy the festive treats…..in fact he knows for a fact that the blonde on the end is called Holly…..but in the mean time, the World Wrestling Federation Champion has a match that he wants to watch…..because Scott Steiner is up next…..and that match has a Royal Rumble ramifications.

With that the Heartbreak Kid leaves, with Sunny on his arm…..and Hall, Nash, Waltman and Credible get to their feet and walk into the opposite room and into the arms of the waiting ladies……..which is when the door abruptly slams shut……and some god awful sounds are followed by a series of crashes, bangs and cries of pain…….

…..before it suddenly swings open, just as abruptly as it closed…..and out walks Stone Cold Steve Austin and The Loose Cannon Brian Pillman…..each sporting a mangled chair. Behind them you can see the four KLiQ members each laid out on the floor, clutching at various body parts. Austin simply shoves the camera man to one side and exits the room…Pillman on the other hand stops, giving the camera that wide eyed maniacal look, before proclaiming…..

“……sorry booker man…..Steve got bored…..you know how it is……”

Rating: A*

VS.

WWF Intercontinental Championship: Christmas Cage Match

Rich Steiner (c) (w/Ted DiBiase) vs. Scott Steiner

After that rather lengthy scene, the ring crew have finally got the big blue cage set up….because it’s time for our main event of the evening. Self preservation has been the name of the game for Rich Steiner since becoming the Intercontinental Champion….and that self preservation has seen him walk out on a match against Psycho Sid…..the elimination Match at the Survivor Series…..a tag team match pitting himself and Shawn Michaels against Ahmed Johnson and Scott Steiner…..a Champion vs Champion match against Shawn Michaels…..and in his latest title defence, just last night against his opponent tonight, Big Poppa Pump…..but tonight for once….walking out of the match is actually the key to victory….because this is a Steel Cage match…..and there are only two ways to win….climb over the top or walk out the door.

It’s all on the line for both men here…..a loss for Rich Steiner means the end of his Championship dream….while a loss for Scott Steiner means not only does he lose the chance to call himself the Intercontinental Champion….but he also loses out on the chance to call himself WWF Champion….because unless he walks into the Royal Rumble with that Intercontinental Championship around his waist…..then he doesn’t walk into the Royal Rumble at all.

Despite the fact that these two make much better partners than they do opponents….(they just don’t click at all)…..it didn’t stop them from pulling an amazing performance out of the bag last night…..and it doesn’t stop them tonight either….because this was just as good, if not better than last nights bout. The main story of the match was Rich Steiner’s desperate attempts to escape….while Scott made some half hearted attempts, but clearly would rather be spending his time bouncing his brothers head OFF the cage, than trying to escape from it…..

…….although the one serious attempt at exiting the ring via the cage door, is hampered by the arrival of Rich’s Million Dollar Counterpart Jeff Jarrett…..who comes running down the aisle to slam the cage door into Big Poppa Pumps face as he was inches away from victory. It’s not the only input he has in the match either…..foiling numerous attempts by the Big Bad Booty Daddy to escape…..

……so much so that Psycho Sid (who clearly has unfinished business with Jeff Jarrett, comes running down the aisle to chance old Double J away.

That just left the Steiner Brothers….who continued to abuse each other….until Rich finally decided that he was going to head to the top rope, looking for his patented Bulldog….or should that be predictable Bulldog? Because Scotty clearly knew it was coming….and swept his brothers legs out, crotching him on the turnbuckle…..before nailing him with a big top rope Frankensteiner. In a normal match this would be all over…..one, two, three…..but this ain’t a normal match….and Scott still needs to escape…..so he climbs….and climbs……as DiBiase enters the ring looking to drag his client out of the cage…..and he succeeds……but about a second later than Scott Steiner landed on the floor…..this one is over…..and in our last show of the year we have a rarely seen title change on TV……and a brand new Intercontinental Champion.

Winner: ….and NEW WWF Intercontinental Champion…..Scott Steiner

Rating: A

The match is over and Scott Steiner is handed his first official singles title of his World Wrestling Federation career, which he returns to the ring with, climbing to the top of the cage to celebrate for his cheering fans……which is when his music is cut short…..and the Heartbreak Kid Shawn Michaels makes his way out from the back with a microphone in hand. He congratulates Steiner for finally winning a title that’s on his level…..but proclaims that if he thinks he achieved anything spectacular then he’s going to be severely disappointed. Michaels walks down to ring side, but ensures that he keeps the cage between himself and Steiner, who is encouraging him to step into the ring……

“…..no, no, no big boy…..I don’t need to step inside that ring….I’m just here to give you some friendly advice…..you make the most of that shiny gold belt….hold on to it real tight….and you might be heading into the Royal Rumble, but that don’t mean jack…..because even if you do win….and you make it to Wrestlemania……I’ll just beat you again like I beat you before….hell, I’ve beaten everyone there is to beat…..I don’t even HAVE an opponent for the Royal Rumble because I’ve knocked you all down one by one……Bret Hart, Jerry Lawler, Owen Hart, you and Steve Austin in the same night….Bret Hart again…..Chris Jericho….Psycho Sid…..Brian Pillman…..Ahmed Johnson…….one by one you all fall down to the Icon….the Main Event…..the Showstopper……there is nobody left to beat…..and that is why, at the Royal Rumble……I think I’m going to take the night off………..

….::::!!!!GONG!!!!::::….

…….suddenly the lights go out………

….::::!!!!GONG!!!!::::….

……..and when they come back on, the Undertaker is stood at the top of the entrance way……and here is a man (Jim Ross informs us) that Shawn Michaels has never beat…..in fact, he’s never even faced him…….Michaels looks like a deer caught in the headlights as the Deadman stands motionless at the top of the aisle…..he can’t go backwards, because Steiner is there….he can’t go forwards because the Undertaker is there…..and he doesn’t have any back up, because unbeknown to him, Steve Austin and Brian Pillman were there to take out the KLiQ….and as the final show of 1996 comes to a close, the Undertaker makes the universal “belt” motion to his waist…..indicating that maybe Shawn Michaels won’t have the night off at the Royal Rumble after all……..we’ll see you next year folks……

Rating: A*

Final Rating: A

Final Thoughts

So that was that…..and 1996 was in the bag…..and while our final Raw of the year was really supposed to be a lighthearted way to end the year, it was really well received….far better than I was expecting considering the only real marquee match was Steiner vs Steiner. Scotty was originally penned in to win the Intercontinental Championship at the Royal Rumble event….in what was always going to be a cage match due to the “story” or Rich Steiner constantly taking the deliberate count-out route in his matches….the cage was going to be there to prevent that….but then I figured that a cage match and a Royal Rumble match on the same show was a bit of an overkill….so I moved it to Raw…..plus it gave the fans a feel good moment to finish the year with a bang, because TV title changes are few and far between for us, and they have really been behind Scotty for the last six months or so….especially with his current character change.

Marty Jannetty is back from rehab again….I’m hoping his demons are behind him because he could prove to be a useful hand….he wasn’t overly pleased about jobbing to Lance Storm on his first night in, but I’ve been burnt with Marty before….if he wants to start picking up some wins around here then he needs to prove that he can stay clean and sober first.

Anyway, while we are done for the year, WCW still have Starrcade to get through…..lets see what ridiculous ideas they come up with for this one……
