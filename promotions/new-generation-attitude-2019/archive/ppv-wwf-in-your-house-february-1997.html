A New Generation…..a new attitude? Pay-per-view

WWF In Your House: February 1997

Posted 26 May 2019

The blog’s images are not served; the posts’ text is given whole, as the author wrote it.

Week 4, February 1997

Carrier Dome (Tri State)

ATT: 50,000

Buy: 1.45

We open up tonight’s event with the traditional voice over video package, featuring the highs and lows that have lead to tonight’s action……the creation of the Corporate Cannon Brian Pillman at the Royal Rumble…..the Hired Monster, Kane’s methodical destruction of everything and anything put in his path by Vince McMahon…..the rise of Scott Steiner and Steve Austin, not to mention the unlikely emergence of Marty Jannetty as the Number One Contender to the WWF Championship……finishing up with a shot of Marty standing tall, after picking up the victory in this past Monday Nights Tag Team contest, before we head down to ringside where Jim Ross and Ravishing Rick Rude officially welcome us to the show……

……Jim Ross goes straight into hype mode, excitingly telling us that tonight, for the first time ever(?) we are opening a WWF Pay Per View, with a WWF Championship Match, because, as Rick Rude gleefully informs us, Shawn Michaels has arrogantly claimed, that while HE might be the Main Event, Marty Jannetty is anything but……and because of that, the Heartbreak Kid has refused to let his former partner compete in the nights final match, instead he’s going to curtain jerk like the scrub that he is, and then when Shawn is done with Jannetty, he’s going to go to the back, kick his feet up, and watch the man that he will be defending the Championship against next month……in the main event of the biggest show of the year……Scott Steiner, get his ass kicked and his title taken, at the hands of Brian Pillman…..

Rating: N/A

VS.

WWF Championship Match

Shawn Michaels (c) (w/Sunny) vs. Marty Jannetty


So here we are……and it’s time for the Rockers to collide once more…..had this been five years ago, no one would have batted an eyelash at the sight of Shawn Michaels and Marty Jannetty opening up a Pay Per View…..but this isn’t five years ago…..this is 1997…..and seeing Shawn Michaels in any match other than the main event is going to take some getting used to…..although should Shawn retain his Championship tonight, we won’t have to get used to it for very long…..because he will have a date in the Main Event of Wrestlemania, against the 1997 Royal Rumble winner, Scott Steiner…….

…….the two men make their customary entrances, before they come face to face in the middle of the ring, and the realisation that The Rockers are currently competing for the biggest prize in all of professional wrestling begins to sink in…..but then the match starts, and all my previous concerns about the quality of this one disappear, because Shawn Michaels puts on the match of his life…..not that Marty didn’t play his part, but make no mistake, this was the Heartbreak Kid show…..I don’t know if it was the fact that he’d been bumped down to curtain jerker, or if he’d just got a point to prove that he can have a good match with anyone, but this could have easily closed the show…….

…..Marty has been pretty beat up as of late, mainly at the hands of Vince McMahons Hired Monster Kane…..but picking up the victory on Monday night……(where he and Chris Jericho defeated Shawn Michaels and Lance Storm)…..has given tonight’s Challenger quite the spring in his step…..and the Champions cocky demeanour soon disappears as Jannetty takes instant control of the match, countering everything that Michaels attempts and generally getting the crowd fired up, to the point that Shawn has to bail to the outside to seek comfort from Sunny. Marty follows him to the floor and HBK quickly hides behind his valet, before shoving the beautiful blonde towards the Challenger……..who catches her in his waiting arms and delivers a big kiss to the horrified KLiQ member, who pitches a fit and tries to slap the taste of her lips of his……but Marty ducks and as Shawn was stalking up behind his opponent, it’s him that eats Sunnys open hand…….

…….back inside the ring and Shawn finally manages to take control of the match after Jannetty crashes and burns on a cross body attempt, and he slows the match down, dismantling his opponent while taking the time to arrogantly inform him that the last time he was relevant in wrestling, was when he got his head slammed through the Barber Shop window, courtesy of the Heartbreak Kid……it’s safe to say that Marty is never going to reach the heights of his former partner, but if there’s one thing he does well, it’s sell a beating and draw sympathy from the crowd, and luckily Shawn is happy to dish out that beating…….

…….Marty manages to draw a few hope spots out to keep the match alive, and to be fair, it’s his strongest showing since his return from his second stint in rehab, because he manages to kick out of everything that the World Champion can throw at him……and even manages to move out of the way of Michaels patented elbow drop to finally get himself back into the match…….the crowd has been red hot for the whole match, but they really come to life as we go into the home stretch, and almost explode out of their seats when Marty hits the Into the Void Superkick…..and we may well have had a new Champion had Sunny not been there to place Shawn’s foot on the bottom rope to break up the count…….

…….Marty drags Michaels to the middle of the ring and goes for the cover again, but Shawn has had too long to recover and manages to get the shoulder up…..Marty slaps the mat in frustration, but he’s going to try again, backing up into the corner where he begins to tune up his own band…….Shawn slowly struggles to his feet but before Jannetty is able to let fly, Sunny again reaches into the ring, hooking onto his ankle to stop the Superkick before it’s even begun……Marty finally manages to kick his leg free and turns to face his opponent, but that’s all the time Shawn needed, and Marty walks straight into the Sweet Chin Music…..and three seconds later, despite Jannetty’s best effort, Shawn Michaels is STILL your WWF Champion, and is surely now heading onto Wrestlemania to meet the Challenge of Big Poppa Pump Scott Steiner.

Winner: …..and STILL WWF Champion…….Shawn Michaels.
Rating: A (!)

Shawn may have finally put the Rockers to bed once and for all here tonight, but it doesn’t look as though he is finished with his former partner…..as he drags Marty to his feet by the hair and tosses him roughly to the outside. Shawn follows and drags the beaten Challenger up the aisle way until he finally comes to a stop at the elaborate In Your House set. He drags Marty to the window on the side of the House and raps on the glass, before again grabbing Marty by the hair, telling him that he put an end to the Rockers by sending him through a window……tonight, he’s putting an end to Marty Jannetty by putting him through another window…….before slamming his opponents head through the (gimmicked) glass……..

……….Marty stays slumped half in/half out of the window, before finally sliding backwards and collapsing to the floor with blood pouring out of a laceration in his head, as Sunny hands Shawn a microphone…….he points down at Jannetty, saying that if this is the best Challenge that the WWF has to offer, then he may as well take the title home and retire now……because there is NOBODY in the entire World Wrestling Federation that can take this belt from around his waist……and that includes Scott Steiner…….because by the end of tonight, the only thing that Scott Steiner will be in any condition to take, is another ass-kicking from the Heartbreak Kid Shawn Michaels.

Rating: A

VS.

The Legion of Doom, Marc Mero & Ahmed Johnson vs. The Million Dollar Corporation


It’s time for a little eight man Tag action now…..as Marc Mero joins Ahmed Johnson and The Legion of Doom in their on going battle with Ted DiBiase’s Million Dollar Corporation……The Corporation have tried to make the most of their numbers advantage in recent weeks with beat downs and attacks, tonight however the numbers are even, Sable is even on the outside to counteract the presence of Ted DiBiase…….

…….the power in this one is mainly on the side of the face Team, with Ahmed, Hawk and Animal all knowing their way around a gym……brawn doesn’t always win out over brains however, and the Corporation are still able to take control of this one…….but predictably it’s not so long before Hawk is able to make the hot Tag to Animal, and then all hell breaks loose…….

…….the official quickly loses control of the match and it descends into an eight man brawl…..and with so much chaos going down, it’s easy to miss the odd infraction of the rule here and there…..and even a huge infraction of the rules……like when Jeff Jarrett obliterated a guitar over the head of Marc Mero……which is enough to give the Corporation the very tainted victory.

Winner: The Million Dollar Corporation
Rating: B-

We go to the back now, where Jerry Lawler is standing by with Raven and the Flock…..conspicuous by his absence however is the Tie-Dyed figure that is Dude Love…..Lawler voices the same question that is on everyone’s mind……where is the Dude? Raven looks at Lawler like he was something he’d just stepped in, telling him to worry less about the whereabouts, and worry more about the safety and well-being of Rey Mysterio…..he says that if Lawler crawled out from under that Paper Crown of his and experienced life in the real world for one day, he would realise that “where” and “when” are empty statements……the real world operates in the “here” and the “now”…..and here and now, Raven is about to tear apart Rey Mysterio piece by piece……and as far as Dude Love goes, when the “here” and the “now” dictate it, he will be there…….because if he’s not, it’s Rey Mysterio that will pay for the concurrences of his actions.

Rating: A

VS.

Rey Mysterio vs. Raven


If I was shocked by how good a match Shawn Michaels and Marty Jannetty had earlier, I was even more shocked by the quality and the reaction to this one…..because while Marty Jannetty is nowhere near on the level of Shawn Michaels, Rey Mysterio is arguably even lower on the totem pole….and Raven is no Shawn Michaels……although saying that, the last time Raven competed in one on one competition on Pay Per View was back in December when he took the Undertaker to the limit in one of the best matches that evening……I thought a lot of that was down to the Deadman, but here we are again, another Pay Per View match, another great performance……so maybe Raven is actually better than people give him credit for…….

………Raven may have been surrounded by the Flock for his earlier interview, but he’s surprisingly alone here…..and that includes Dude Love, who despite Ravens orders to be at ringside, appears to be nowhere to be seen……as I said before though, this was a fantastic match, and the crowd was clearly rooting for the underdog, looking for Mysterio to finally shut the mouth of the man who has spent more time talking here in the WWF than actually wrestling…..

……..and Rey certainly takes the Master of the Macabre to the limit, because while Raven May have competed against Light Heavyweight Jerry Lynn on Monday Night, Rey Mysterio is really a competitor unlike anything Raven has faced before…..the speed and agility of Mysterio really gives him the edge in this one, but the aggressive streak of Raven gives him control of the match, after shoving Rey off the top rope, all the way down to the floor below……and on the outside is where Raven excels…..and he really punishes the youngster, bouncing him off the ringside furniture……

……back inside the ring and Raven continues the offence, telling a nearby camera that Rey Mysterio can thank Mick for this pain……before continuing the attack……he even sets up a steel chair up in the middle of the ring, despite the officials warnings……and sends Rey into the ropes, looking to connect with a drop toe hold to the chair……Mysterio is able to counter it however into a spinning DDT, and it’s Raven that eats the steel…….the official removes the chair and the two men continue to battle, with Mysterio now creeping back into control as he resumes the high flying offence that gave him so much success earlier in the match……

…….unfortunately they don’t call it “high risk” for nothing……and as Mysterio comes comes flying off the top looking for a splash, Raven rolls to one side, leaving his opponent with nothing to connect with but canvas…..he then drags Mysterio to his feet, before quickly finishing him off with the Evenflow DDT for the victory.

Winner: Raven
Rating: A (!)

Raven looks directly into a nearby camera again, declaring that “this blood is on your hands Mick”, as he drops to the outside to pick up a steel chair. Back in the ring and he screams “WHAT ABOUT ME MICK? WHAT ABOUT RAVEN?”, before unleashing a series of crashing blows across the back of little Rey. He drops the chair and rolls Mysterio over, kneeling over his chest and laying in punch after punch to the little luchador, before finally relenting…….

……..although clearly he isn’t done, as he drags Rey’s lifeless body from the mat, opens up the steel chair, and places it over Rey’s head………before positioning him for what would be a lethal Evenflow DDT……….

……..which is when Dude Love finally makes an appearance, as he comes running down the aisle……he slides into the ring and pulls Mysterio out of Ravens grip, pulling the chair off his head and tossing it to one side. Raven looks utterly disgusted at the show of emotion from the Dude……telling him that he wouldn’t have had to do this if Mick had just let his true self out……but the Dude says that he doesn’t have to do this full stop…….then turns to check on Rey……….

……..which is enough for Raven…..who picks up the discarded steel chair and smashes it across the back of the Dude……who turns around just in time to receive a shot straight between the eyes, busting him wide open……The Dude drops to one knee and Raven absolutely nails him with another crashing blow to the head which finally puts the Coolest Cat in the Room on his back. Raven drops to his knees, and much as he just did with Mysterio, begins raining blows onto the open wound of the Dude, screaming “WHAT ABOUT ME? WHAG ABOUT RAVEN?”……..before dragging The Dude to his feet, telling him that he asked for one thing…..and Mick couldn’t even give him that……..before dropping Mick skull first onto the steel with an Evenflow……

…….Raven exits the ring, leaving a trail of bodies behind him…….and gets half way up the ramp before the voice of Dude Love stops him…….except it doesn’t sound like the Dude at all…….it doesn’t sound like Mankind either……..in fact it sounds damn right deranged…….

”…….stop……stop right there you narcissistic son of a bitch…………”

……Raven stops in his tracks and turns to face the Dude……except the Dude doesn’t look like he’s all there…….there’s a light gone out behind those eyes, and as the blood streams down his face he slowly pulls himself to his feet………

”……you think this is a joke…..you think this is what you really want?…….you want Jack?…….if you want Cactus Jack?………”

………and The Dude rips off his tie-dye to reveal an entirely new shirt underneath…….

”………..you got Cactus Jack…………and he won’t ‘have mercy’…….and you won’t ‘have a nice day’…….because he’s going to drag you through broken glass, all the way down to the gates of hell…….and put you out of your misery once and for all……….Bang! Bang!………”

………and finally…….finally Raven has got what he wants……..and he may be smiling now……..but whether he’s still smiling when he faces the full madness of Cactus Jack is anyone’s guess.

Rating: A*

After that revelation we head backstage where Dok Hendrix is standing by with the Hart Foundation. He says that the Harts have displayed a meaner streak as of late…..and to be fair it has wielded some success….unfortunately, as long as Shawn Michaels is still the WWF Champion, and Sean Waltman and Scott Hall are the Tag Team Champions, that’s success is not going to translate into Title opportunities…..Dok says that tonight,, the Harts are up against the Nation of Domination……who DO have a title opportunity coming up at Wrestlemania…….what exactly do the Harts have to gain with victory tonight?……..

………Bret says that the biggest thing on the line tonight…..is pride……pride at showing the world that the Hart Foundation are THE dominant group in the WWF……pride that the Hart Foundation will finally shut the mouth of the Nation of Domination……and pride in the knowledge that while Vince McMahon handed the Nation a Championship opportunity on a silver platter, it’ll be then that are screaming out in agony, as the Harts trap them in the Sharpshooter…….because one way or another, the Harts WILL find a way to get back into title contention…..one way, or another……

Rating: B+

VS.

The Hart Foundation vs. The Nation of Domination


This feud has been brewing (pretty one sided to be fair on the half of the Nation) for close to a year now……Faarooq has been using the Harts as an example of one group getting all of the opportunities, while the Nation has been held down…..and for the most part Bret and Co have let the insults go unpunished…..it was only when the Nation cost Bret and Owen the chance to enter the 1997 Royal Rumble that the Harts really turned their attention to Faarooq and his gang……and they have been using every trick in the book as of late to put one over on the NoD………

…….tonight, it’s Bret, Owen and Davey facing off against Faarooq, The Rock and Kama……although as usual, Nelson and Scorpio are at ringside……it’s a rarity that a Bret Hart match isn’t one of (if not THE) best matches of the night…..but while this match was very, very, good, it was still probably only the fifth best match this evening…..part of that may have been down to the relative inexperience of the Rock, who although improving at a rapid rate, is still a solid midcarder……couple that with Kama, who while slightly more over than the Rock, is still Kama…..and then there’s the finish of the match itself……or rather, the aftermath of the finish of the match…….

…….,because the finish itself was fairly straight forward. After a decent back and forth battle that saw BOTH teams stretch the rules to their limits, Bret Hart was able to wrap Kama’s long legs up in the Sharpshooter…….Faarooq tried to break it up, only for The Bulldog to send him tumbling to the outside……then The Rock tried to break it up, only for Owen Hart to tackle him and lock in a Sharpshooter of his own……..and finally, with Davey Boy holding off the other Nation members, Kama had little option but to concede defeat, submitting to the trademark Hart hold……..but that wasn’t the end of the action……..

Winner(s): The Hart Foundation (via Submission)
Rating: B+

…….because the bell rings…….and Kama and The Rock are still screaming in agony from the duel Sharpshooters…….but Bret and Owen show no sign of breaking the hold……in fact, if possible, they hold on even tighter……in the meantime the Bulldog has picked up a steel chair from ringside and is holding Faarooq, Nelson and Scorpio at bay with wild swings of the steel every time they try to enter the ring……

……..this goes on for a good few minutes, as the bell rings more frantically, and the official tries to drag both Bret and Owen off of their opponents…..but the Harts are relentless…..and Davey manages to clip both Nelson and Scorpio hard enough to keep them on the floor……Faarooq finally gets into the ring, but the Bulldog is waiting and he doubles him over with a chair shot to the gut…….before HE applies a Sharpshooter of his own to Faarooq…….

……..it’s about this time that the official decides enough is enough, and informs the timekeeper to announce that the referee has reversed his decision, and has awarded the match to the Nation of Domination. The Harts don’t appear overly concerned by this announcement however……and finally they release their victims, before heading to the back, leaving the entire Nation laying.

Winner(s): via reversed decision……The Nation of Domination
Rating: A*

VS.

Chris Jericho vs. Scott Hall


Next up we have a match brought about, mainly on the back of Chris Jericho eliminating Scott Hall from the Royal Rumble back in January……apparently the Bad Guy didn’t take to kindly to this, and had Vince McMahon arrange this match for him……Chris Jericho however, arguably had eyes on a different opponent, one Mr. Lance Storm…..according to McMahon however, the only time Jericho will be getting Storm any time soon, will be if it’s on the grandest stage of all…….

……..considering the ridiculous reason for this match, it was again, very, very good…..excellent in fact, and that was even with the hampering of Scott’s agility from a tendon problem that’s going to be bugging him for another year yet……but if Chris Jericho continues to put on performances like he did here, and like the one he put on against Shawn Michaels, it’s only a matter of time before he finally breaks through that upper midcard glass ceiling and becomes a genuine main eventer……unfortunately for him tonight, Scott Hall is ALREADY a genuine main eventer…….and you don’t get to the top without knowing how to play dirty……….

……..which is why when Sean Waltman at ringside decides to distract the attention of the official, Lance Storm once again makes his way down to ringside…….tripping Jericho as he runs the ropes, sending him staggering straight into Scott Hall……and straight into the Razors Edge.

Winner: Scott Hall
Rating: A

It takes a while for Jericho to finally come to his senses enough to realise that Lance Storm has just cost him ANOTHER match……but the fact that he sees the man himself standing at the top of the aisle way soon helps the penny drop……and in a rather uncharacteristic display, Chris Jericho frankly looses his s*it…….throwing a Shawn Michaels sized temper tantrum in the ring as he slams his hands on the mat and kicks out at the ring ropes, before dropping down to the floor and snatching a microphone from the timekeeper, who he shoves roughly to the floor………

………he paces around the ring for a moment, before pointing up at Storm, telling him that he’s had enough of this…..and he’s had enough of him……and if McMahon wants it done on the grandest stage of all, then Chris Jericho will gladly put Mr. Monotony in the Liontamer…..live and in living colour at Wrestlemania…….and finally put him, and his stupid face behind him once and for all………Storm doesn’t say much in response……in fact he doesn’t say anything……he simply nods his head in acceptance and walks to the back, leaving Jericho still furious in the ring.

Rating: A

Following that we head to the back for a quick segment with Vince McMahon, who tells Dok Hendrix that what he has just witnessed, that unprofessional behaviour from Chris Jericho, is exactly why Vince is proud to call Lance Storm a member of his roster…..Dok is rather none committal in his response, simply asking Vince what the folder in his hand contains……

……McMahon smiles and says that it is the contract for a WWF Tag Team Title shot, that he will personally deliver to the winner of our next match, the over the top rope, Tag Team Battle Royal……Dok asks if that’s the same rather underwhelming Battle Royal that was announced a few weeks back……with the less than stellar combatants? Vince says yes, before catching himself and saying that every team in that match is a viable threat to the KLiQ…..and he will be proud to personally hand them their hard earned shot at the prestigious WWF gold.

Rating: B+

#1 contenders Tag Team Battle Royal

Featuring: The Harris Boys, The Headbangers, Tatanka & Savio Vega, Bob Holly & Bart Gunn, The Bushwhackers, The Conquistadors, The Executioners, The Orient Express, Power & Glory…….and various other people you have never heard of…..

I shan’t bore you too much with this one……all you need to know is that Vince McMahon, Scott Hall and Sean Waltman joined us at ringside……and it was a pretty dull and uneventful affair, because the crowd couldn’t care less who wins and who loses……although, surprisingly it was the masked Conquistadors that came out victorious, finally eliminating the Headbangers in what was arguably their strongest showing since the 1988 Survivor Series…….and those ladies and gentlemen, are your new number one contenders.

Winner(s): The Conquistadors
Rating: B-

True to his word, Vince McMahon (along with the WWF Tag Team Champions Hall and Waltman) climbs into the ring with contract in hand. He walks over to congratulate the Conquistadors, raising each mans hand before collecting a microphone…….he congratulated them once more, before declaring that by signing this contract here, they have a guaranteed Championship opportunity……so how does tomorrow night, live on Monday Night Raw sound to them?…..

…….the two masked men huddle together for a moment, before finally coming up for air and shaking their heads……..

”……..you don’t want your shots tomorrow night? Ok I can understand that, you need time to prepare when you’re up against two premier athletes like Scott Hall and Sean Waltman……..so how about, in one weeks time? Is that long enough to prepare?……”

…….again the masked men huddle, and again they come up shaking their heads……this time however, Vince appears to be getting a little annoyed…….

”…….I think maybe something is getting lost in translation here…..I mean, I don’t speak Spanish……but you know what I’m saying right? When….do…..you…..want…..your……match?.. …”

…….again the Conquistadors huddle, this time however when they come back up, one man takes the microphone from Vince, and in a heavily accented Spanish accent, simply says……

”……Wrestlemania…..”

……Vince actually chuckles at that, stating that unfortunately, the Tag Title opportunity at Wrestlemania has already been granted to the Nation of Domination…….Scott Hall however stops Vince……and tells him (in between fits of laughter) to go ahead and book a triple threat match, because after all……

”……these two jabroni’s are never gonna be at Wrestlemania again…..”

……Vince doesn’t look overly pleased at the prospect of the Conquistadors heading to the biggest show of the year, but finally relents, handing the contract to the gold clad duo, stating sign the contract, and the Wrestlemania match is yours…….

…….which is exactly what the two men do, before handing the legally binding contract back to McMahon……who looks down at the paper in front of him……..and his eyes go so wide they look like they are about to burst out of his head……..and that’s about the same time that the Conquistadors remove their masks………..

……..to reveal Bret and Owen, the Hart Brothers…….Vince is going ballistic……and Hall and Waltman are screaming that the Harts can’t challenge for their titles……but Bret simply snatches the microphone away from Vince……telling him that the contract they just signed, means that they CAN challenge for those titles…..and they WILL challenge for those titles…..and they’ll see the KLiQ…..and the Nation of Domination……at Wrestlemania……

Rating: A*

VS.

Steve Austin vs. Kane (w/Jim Cornette)


Things are about to break down now…..we’ve had it all tonight…high flying action, technical masterclasses, shocks, surprises……now it’s time to fight…..now it’s time to brawl……now it’s time, for Stone Cold Steve Austin to go one on one with Kane……and if you want headlocks and take downs, you’re watching the wrong match……if you want a red hot crowd however, and action that spills out the ring, in the ring, and everywhere in between…..then this one is most definitely for you……

…….and you don’t have to wait for long…..because the fists start to fly from the moment the bell rings…..most people would think twice about going toe to toe with Kane….in fact, most people would think twice a out being in the same building as Kane, let alone fighting him, but then Stone Cold Steve Austin isn’t most people…..he is in fact (according to Jim Ross on commentary) one of the toughest S.O.B’s in all of professional wrestling…..and Jim may well be right, because Austin manages to drive Kane backwards with right hands, before clotheslining him over the top and down to the floor……

…….except, as he is known to do, Kane lands on his feet….reaches into the ring and drags Austin to the floor by his ankles, then drives him into the steel steps at ringside. He drives Austin into the ring post, then charges at him, but Stone Cold moves and it’s Kane that eats the steel…..and that was much the tone of the match…..each man would hit a move, try a move, miss a move….neither could really sustain any prolonged period of offence, although in fairness as the match progressed, it was Austin looking the worse for wear out of the two men, because whatever Stone Cold threw at him, the Big Red Monster just kept coming…..

……the match goes on and Rude declares that the longer this match goes, the better chance we have if seeing Kane pick up the victory…..because McMahon’s Hired Monster can take everything that Steve Austin can throw at him….and then some…..Austin on the other hand already looks the worse for wear, and there’s no way that he can continue to take this onslaught…..Jim Ross disagrees however….stating that Austin can and will find the chink in Kane’s armour…..and when he does, he’ll finally slay that Big Red Dragon once and for all (way to mix your metaphors Jim)…….

…….the pinfall attempts are few and far between so far, with the two men more interested in hurting each other rather than pinning their opponent, so all the official has really had to do thus far, is keep out of harms way and try not to get hurt……unfortunately, by the end of the match, even that wasn’t possible……because as Austin sidestepped a Big Boot attempt, the referee took the full force of Kane’s foot right in the face, knocking him out cold…….

……..with no official, Austin and Kane continue their battle on the outside, which Kane gets the better of, hoisting Austin high up in the air with a Chokeslam, slamming Stone Cold down so hard on the steel entrance ramp that Austin actually bounces slightly, before rolling down towards ringside………..and that is when the lights go out………..

….::::!!!!GONG!!!!::::….

………and the crowd erupts……….

….::::!!!!GONG!!!!::::….

…..because when they come back on…….

The Deadman is stood at the top of the entrance ramp……and he has Kane already in position for the Tombstone……and as the official slowly regains his composure in the ring, he drives his Baby Brothers skull into the steel…..before stepping back to admire his handy work…….

…….the referee may have missed the Tombstone, but he now sees that both Austin and Kane are on the floor, and so begins his mandatory ten count……..fortunately for Austin, he is able to drag himself back inside the ring by nine…..Kane however, isn’t moving, and much like his brother at the Royal Rumble, is counted out following the interference from his sibling.

Winner: Steve Austin (via Count-Out)
Rating: A

The Undertaker still stands over his fallen brother, only now he has a microphone……he tells Kane that at the Royal Rumble, he stuck his nose in the Deadman’s business, costing him the WWF Championship…….then he had the gall to challenge him to a Last Man Standing match………the goal of which is to keep your opponent down for a count of ten……..Taker says that by his reckoning, Kane has been down for a lot longer than ten seconds right now……and that was one Tombstone…….imagine how long you’re going to be down for at Wrestlemania, when the Undertaker finally buries the past once and for all……..

”………because I accept your challenge baby brother…….and at Wrestlemania……you will…….Rest……..in……….Peace……..”

………and with that, the Deadman turns and walks to the back……but no second is he out of sight, than the Big Red Monster sits bolt upright and snaps his head around in the direction his brother went……leaving Jim Ross to question, was Kane really out for the count? Or simply playing possum to give his brother a false sense of self confidence……I guess we will find out one way or another…….at Wrestlemania.

Rating: A*

VS.

WWF Intercontinental Championship Match

Scott Steiner (c) vs. Brian Pillman


And so it’s time for the main event of the evening…….and a rather historic main event to say the least, as it is the first time that the Intercontinental Championship has been higher up the card than the WWF Championship ever on US soil……and the first time it has headlined a Pay Per View since Bret Hart and The British Bulldog battled it out in Wembley back at Summerslam 1992……and everyone knows how good that match was…….so the pressure was on for tonight’s competitors to deliver something special……..and deliver they did………because this match was more than worthy of its main event status…..

……..these two have already met once for the Intercontinental Championship…..that match however was on Monday Night Raw, and ended in disqualification after Shawn Michaels decided to kick Scott Steiner’s head off with the Sweet Chin Music…..hopefully tonight brings a more conclusive finish……

……truth be told, both of these men have surpassed the Intercontinental level….in fact Brian Pillman is arguably the second hottest heel in the WWF right now behind Shawn Michaels….and is easily the number three or four guy in the whole promotion, with only HBK and the Undertaker considered bigger stars, while he and Austin fight it out for the third and fourth spot….so it would come as no surprise to see these two men tangling for the WWF Championship somewhere down the line……tonight however, it’s all about the prestige of calling yourself the Intercontinental Championship…….and Scott Steiner has vowed to take that title all the way to Wrestlemania……

……Brian Pillman on the other hand, doesn’t even have a match booked for Wrestlemania yet…..you’ve got to believe though that walking into the biggest show of the year as the WWF Intercontinental Champion would guarantee him a pretty marquee match. Because of that there was a lot on the line for both men here…..and they fought their hearts out……Steiner used his superior strength to overpower his smaller opponent, but while Scotty is surprisingly agile considering his size, he’s got nothing on the Corporate Cannon, who uses his speed, agility…..and frankly bizarre character to gain the upper hand on the defending Champion…..and as the match goes on, a lot of the smarks predictions that Steiner is sure to drop the title tonight (due to his title match at Wrestlemania) looks to be a good bet….because Pillman certainly has the Champion in trouble……

…….Big Poppa Pump isn’t headlining the Grandaddy of them all for nothing though, and just when it looks like all is lost, he almost takes Brian’s head off with a hard Steinerline…..and from there on in Steiner goes on the rampage……so much so……

………that Vince McMahon, clearly still seething from the Hart’s back handed way of earning themselves a title shot at Wrestlemania earlier tonight, makes his way out from the back…..along with Scott Hall and Sean Waltman……..clearly eager to ensure that it’s his Corporate Cannon that walks out of here this evening with the Gold, not Scott Steiner………

……..of course, speaking of the Harts……they haven’t been shy about getting the jump on their opponents recently……..and before the Tag champs can interject themselves into the match, Bret and Owen come running out from the back, sending Hall and Waltman tumbling to the ground……and Bret even gives Vince a shove that puts the owner of the WWF on his ass……that’s a lot of action transpiring on the outside……which is probably why the official fails to see………..

………Stone Cold Steve Austin come flying over the safety barrier…….Austin slides into the ring and stands behind Steiner and Pillman, who both turn round at the same time…….the competitors do a double take as they see Stone Cold waiting……..before Austin flips Pillman the double bird…….kicks him in the gut………..and drops him with a Stone Cold Stunner………

……..Austin and Steiner lock eyes, before Stone Cold finally tells Steiner to “……cover him then you stupid son of a bitch……..” before hopping to the outside……..Steiner shrugs his massive shoulders, before calling out to the official as he makes the cover……and walks out of In Your House with his Intercontinental Championship in tact.

Winner: …..and STILL WWF Intercontinental Champion…….Scott Steiner
Rating: A

The match is over, and Big Poppa Pump’s final Pay Per View match before Wrestlemania is in the books…….the action is still going on however, as the Harts and The KLiQ brawl their way back to the locker room, as Pillman and Austin spill into the crowd, biting and clawing their way up the stairs until they disappear into the depths of the building……Scotty is still standing tall on the corner turnbuckle with the Intercontinental Title hanging proudly over his shoulder. He hops back down and turns to head over to the other corner………….

…….when he gets hit right in the jaw with some Sweet Chin Music……Shawn Michaels has made his way out from the back, and pounces on the fallen Steiner immediately, nailing him with blows and generally throwing out insults. He backs off for a second…..long enough to pick up the WWF Championship……and long enough for Scotty to pull himself back to his feet using the ring ropes……..which is when the Heartbreak Kid runs through him, smashing the World Championship into the skull of his Wrestlemania opponent……

……….Scott goes down……and he goes down hard, as Michaels stands arrogantly over him. Shawn raises the WWF Championship in the air for a moment, before stopping…….reaching down to pick up Steiner’s Intercontinental Championship……he looks at the two belt, before breaking out into a huge grin as he hoists both Gold Titles overhead……Shawn Michaels may not have been the main event tonight…..but when the show came to a close, he still managed to make sure that the spotlight shone only on him……we’ll see you tomorrow night folks, because the road to Wrestlemania is officially underway.

Rating: A*

Final Rating: A*

Final Thoughts

Well……after an surprisingly good Monday Night Raw, we only went and put on possibly the greatest Pay Per View in my (admittedly) short reign here in the WWF……the fact that Shawn and Marty opened up the show with such a barn burner came as a surprise to everyone…..well, everyone except Shawn I suppose, who was quick to let out a loud “Follow that” as he parted the curtains……fortunately for us, virtually everyone took Shawn’s cocky exclamation as a challenge, because “follow that” they certainly did…….Raven and Rey blew expectations out of the water, (and both got big praise after the show for putting on the match that they did, and both men’s stock rose as a result…..in fact Raven could probably be classed as one of our main eventers after this showing tonight), Jericho and Hall put on the match of both their careers considering how ridiculously their match came about…….Austin and Kane, Steiner and Pillman…..this show was packed to the gills with amazing matches……and if I’m honest, it worries me slightly……I’m a little bit concerned that we have shot our load a month early…….if anything I’m concerned about Wrestlemania being able to “follow that”……hopefully I’m wrong though……I guess we will find out in a months time……
