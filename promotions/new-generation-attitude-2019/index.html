Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

A New Generation…..a new attitude? is one author's blog rebooking the World Wrestling Federation week by week, begun in March 2019 and, by its own title, played in Total Extreme Wrestling 2016. The wing portrays the WWF: its cast is real wrestlers and its shows are the promotion's own, from the Monday night broadcast of the fourth week of January 1996 onward, in the diary's telling. In its opening posts the author writes himself in as an Englishman in New York who answers letters for WWF Magazine until, in October 1995, Vince McMahon promotes him to the booking team, with Vince Russo hired to stand in as his public face.

Each show is a script. A header gives the game week, the arena, the attendance and the television rating (or the pay-per-view buy rate), then each segment and match follows in turn, with a winner line and a letter grade for every match and an author's section of notes on contracts, injuries, rehab stints and the ratings war with WCW. The monthly pay-per-views each take a post of their own, from the 1996 Royal Rumble through WrestleMania XII, King of the Ring, SummerSlam and Survivor Series to WrestleMania XIV. Posts headed "The Stars of…" in August 1996, September 1997 and January 1998 list the champions, the roster by rank and each belt's history.

The diary widens as it goes. From June 1997 it also writes up WCW Monday Nitro and the WCW pay-per-views, and from January 1998, after a post in which the author phones Paul Heyman to strike a private arrangement between the WWF and ECW, it adds WCW Thunder and ECW Hardcore TV. The belts are the diary's own: the WWF Championship, Intercontinental, European, Light Heavyweight (retired at SummerSlam 1997) and Tag Team titles, five WCW titles and three ECW titles, each with the reigns its own lists date by the month.

The last post, dated 15 February 2022, is WrestleMania XIV, in the fourth week of March 1998 on the diary's calendar, and it ends with Stone Cold Steve Austin as WWF Champion. It carries no closing note. When the blog was collected on 6 October 2026 nothing newer stood on its front page or in its sitemap: the diary is dormant, four years and almost eight months after its last post.

This federation’s fiction portrays the WWF, the WCW and the ECW.
