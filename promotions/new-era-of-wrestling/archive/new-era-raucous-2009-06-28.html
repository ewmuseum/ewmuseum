"WFW:NE RAUCOUS, 06.28.09!"

LIVE from the ALLTEL Arena in Little Rock, Arkansas on June 28, 2009! WFW:NE comes roaring to life with its first RAUCOUS after the merger! Six matches, including two championship defenses, are amongst the jam packed show. While both New ERA of Wrestling World Heavyweight champions will be in the ring in Little Rock, only one title will be on the line! Rocko Daymon will defend his World Heavyweight championship in the ring against "the Phenom" Shawn Hart! His co-World Heavyweight champion, Larry Tact, will face the man whom tagged with in the Battle Bowl Mania tournament to crown the first ever World Heavyweight champion... five years and two months after Larry Tact became New ERA's first World Heavyweight champion ... he will be going one on one with Spectrum! All this .. AND MORE ... as WFW:NE presents RAUCOUS from Little Rock!
MATCH #1 OTTO PEARL versus BLACK DRAGON MATCH #2 - Non Title - ALEX WYLDE versus STEVE JOHNSON (c) MATCH #3 - Television Championship - CAMERON CRUISE versus MR.
