New ERA of Wrestling — Roster

"The First"

posted 17 March 2015
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
WRESTLER INFORMATION NAME: The First AGE: 25 HEIGHT: 6'0 WEIGHT: 198 lbs ALIGNMENT: Heel APPEARANCE: Thin, wiry build, hair about as long as Sheamus' only not spiked up, dyed black and a bunch of other colors, wears black most of the time, his ring gear consists of a black wife beater, black gi pants, black ring boots, with his right hand taped up. He wears black and white face paint in various patterns and designs.
PERSONALITY: Pretty much a nut takes the sport seriously but has a very weird world view since he has been led to believe he's 6,000 years old and the oldest soul in the world.
BACKGROUND / HISTORY: Wrestled in UWC, currently in EPW and A1E, a kid from Salem, worked in his dad's garage fixing cars till he got bit by the wrestling bug.
HOMETOWN: Salem, Massachusetts ENTRANCE MUSIC: “Happy Birthday” by the Birthday Massacre RING ENTRANCE: Normally runs out from behind the curtain, screaming at the fans, hopping up and down acting like a nut, high fives/fist bumps anyone he can get his hands on, gets to the apron and will hop from the apron over the top rope to the second rope inside the ring and stand there posing for photos.
New ERA CHAMPIONSHIPS: New ERA Champion (x2) WRESTLING STYLE: High Flyer, reckless, could be thought of as a spot monkey. MOVES: 1. Poison Mist 2. Handspring Elbow into the Corner 3. Spear after Whipping Opponent to the Ropes 4. Hurricanrana 5. Tope 6. Footsweep 7. Swinging Neckbreaker 8. Fujiwara Armbar 9. DDT 10. Running Dropkick to an Opponent sitting up/propped up in the Corner SETUP MANEUVER: Shining Wizard PRIMARY FINISHER: Cut The Thread FINISHER DESCRIPTION: Front Flip Legdrop off the Top Rope SECONDARY FINISHER: Soulbreaker FINISHER DESCRIPTION: Carlito's Backstabber MANAGER: Muse
