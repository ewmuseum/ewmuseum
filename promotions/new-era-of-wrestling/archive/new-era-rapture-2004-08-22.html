LIVE from the Cox Convention Centre in Oklahoma City, Oklahoma on AUGUST 22nd, 2004! ​ RAPTURE hits the airwaves as New ERA's secondary show becomes a reality! And what a show it should be! With three new members of the New ERA roster debuting against more seasoned New ERA stars, it will give everyone a chance to see the future of wrestling! And to top it all off ... World Champion Jean Rabesque will be in the ring, as he takes on CHAOS .. who defeated Rabesque to win the Television title before! The match is a non-title affair, but that doesn't mean that things won't get out of hand! Make sure to join us, Sunday August 22nd as RAPTURE COMES TO OKLAHOMA!
