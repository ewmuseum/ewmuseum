http://fwrestling.com/host/neweraofwrestling/images/television/past/new_RAUCOUS.jpg LIVE from the Fleet Centre in Boston, Massachusetts on January 12th, 2006! New ERA rings in the New Year with its 01.12.06 edition of RAUCOUS coming from the Fleet Centre in Boston, Massachusetts! With the crowning of a New World Heavyweight Champion in the Phantom Republican, New ERA embarks on unchartered territory after the year plus long reign of former champion Jean Rabesque. The show promises to be a thriller as seven action packed matches are scheduled on the card. A dark match between Trevor Cane and Matthew, the Hardcore Kid, will entertain the fans as RAUCOUS is about to go on the air .. and will lead into the seven scheduled matches that will air on DEMAND. The Insurgent takes on newcomer 'Iceman' Kevin Kearns, in the opening bout. Insurgent, coming off his one card suspension after his antics at International Intrigue, looks to regain the momentum he had before the event on his quest to overthrow the company. Kearns, whose been seen around New ERA studios mocking Jason Tripp, is a great addition whose looking to step into the limelight of New ERA of Wrestling and reclaim his past glory.. Rocko Daymon, who surprised the world by showing up at RAPTURE, takes on Dan 'the Dragon' Taylor in a double debut match. The gloves will come off as Victoria Hawke, enraged at being overlooked in the women's division, butts heads with the latest addition to the roster in Foxx. Also scheduled on the match is the anticipated return to action of Alex Borden, a former Television Champion, as he takes on the debuting Steve Johnson, a WFW superstar who has signed on to New ERA to expand his dominance. A triple threat is scheduled to determine a new #1 contender to the Television title as Jason Payne, Cameron Cruise, and Mr. Entertainment battle .... with the added pleasure of having current TV Champion MWG as the special referee! John Doe will get his chance to win the P©X title from Chaos, who he defeated at the 09.19.05 edition of RAUCOUS, as those two will be bound together by a leather strap ... and in the main event, after the explosive conclusion to the World Title match, a non-title triple threat survival match between The Masked Man, Jonathan Marx, and World Champion The Phantom Republican will sure to be the highlight of the evening. All this and MORE as New ERA of Wrestling storms into Boston .... and into 2006! So tune in on 01.12.06 for New ERA RAUCOUS!
