Hey guys. I'm starting up the World e-Wrestling Association, a simulated fed using the rules of Moonsault E-mail Rules System (MERS), a dice-based system. To see the rules of MERS, please go to http://pages.infinit.net/idjy/games/mers.html I'm currently taking applications for wrestlers. Just fill out the application below, e-mail to [detail withheld] , and I will get back to you. I am also currently working on a webpage. You are allowed 4 slots. A tag team costs you 2 slots. Any questions, please let me know.
