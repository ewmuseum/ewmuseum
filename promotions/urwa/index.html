A real-wrestler simulator fed run solo by one AOL operator, staging four named pay-per-views across a single autumn season with eight championships, seven rival stables, and a cross-generational roster that could put Lou Thesz across from the Undertaker. Written by AI, 2026

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The Universal Rampage Wrestling Alliance introduced itself on 18 August 1997 as a fed that “uses the legends from yesterday and the champs of today,” deciding results on the Wrestling Rampage simulator rather than roleplay: “you don't have a roleplayer like other fantasy feds…you choose your guys, you agree to your matches, and we do our talking in the ring, the way it used to be.” It opened its roster to any real professional wrestler from any era — “one of the only feds where you can choose a Lou Thesz just as easily as you can choose the Undertaker” — and closed charter membership at midnight on 12 September, the first twenty-four entrants each choosing one singles wrestler, one tag team and a wild-card entry, run solo from one operator's AOL member page.

Its first card, Ground Zero, ran 14 September 1997 at the Ice Palace in Tampa, Florida. Four battle royals decided its own first title challengers before four title bouts crowned its first champions: Bret Hart's gut-wrench pinned Nikita Koloff for the TV Title; Sting and Lex Luger beat the Gangstas for the International Tag Titles; Andre the Giant's suplex beat Frank Gotch for the International Title, “giving the Heenan family it's first championship here in the URWA” and, in its own broadcast's words, “an omen for things to come”; Rick Rude and Tully Blanchard took the World Tag Titles from the Outsiders two falls to one; and Vader's Vader Bomb pinned Bruno Sammartino for the first World Title.

By its 26 October Halloween Hell card the promotion was staging a faction war: a new heel stable, the Titans of Hell, debuted behind a bald-headed Owen Hart promising the fed “when you start running rough-shod over the URWA, you go to hell.” The same card made Andre the Giant, submitting Gorgeous George for the World Title while he still held the International, “the first man in URWA history to hold more than one championship”; four of its wrestlers, self-styled the Boy Toys, quit the NWO on the air, accusing the group's own boss, Eric Bischoff, of favoring “his bunch of losers” over them; and a storyline neck injury, disclosed backstage after the main event, stripped Bret Hart of the TV Title he had just defended, opening an eight-man tournament for it.

Five nights later, at its 31 October Bad Dreams card in Albuquerque, the World Tag Titles changed hands three times on the same bill: the Road Warriors lost them to Cactus Jack and Mortis, who lost them the same night to Rocky Maivia and his surprise mystery partner, Ernie “The Cat” Ladd, who lost them in turn to the Steiners — already the reigning International Tag Team Champions, and now the first team in URWA history to hold two tag titles at once. Andre the Giant defended the World Title three times on the same card, against Ahmed Johnson, Sting and Fred Blassie.

Its last surviving cards, both dated 22 November 1997 out of Vancouver, British Columbia, show the World Title in Hulk Hogan's hands against Great Sasuke and set up an eight-way “World Series of Survival” tournament pitting stables including the NWO, the Heenan family, the Titans of Hell and its own recurring Rare Oddities stable (Wrath, Kain, Meng and Mortis) against one another. No result of that tournament is in its surviving record, and neither is a closing notice; the promotion's own record stops there.
