URWA Arena Report, 22 November 1997

22 November 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
URWA Arena Report 11/22/97-- B.C. Place, Vancouver, B.C., Canada Bout 1: (20 min) Meng vs. Williams-- Williams gets the pinfall after a slingshot at 5:30, as Williams gets another impressive victory. Bout 2: (20 min) Andre vs. Abdullah-- in a wild brawl, Andre gets the pin at 13:25 with a big squash. Bout 3: (30 min) Sting/Luger vs. Rude/Blanchard-- Heenan brought the Brainbusters to ringside, but the NOD made the loudest statement, as Ahmed attacked Rude spraying a can of mace right into Rude's eyes! A blinded Rude gouged Luger to get at 17:54. Bout 4: (20 min) Henry vs. Raven-- Raven scores another win with a DDT and pinfall at 17:56. Bout 5: (20 min) Inoki vs. Zeus-- Inoki scores the upset, landing a karate kick to get the pin at 18:42, making a remarkable rally when it appeared the Japanesse legend was beaten. Bout 6: (20 min) Giant vs. Sid-- Owen and Bigelow brought Sid to the ring in this crucial match. But, as Giant had Sid in a bearhug late in the bout, Owen would distract the ref, and Bigelow entered the ring to attempt to blindside the Giant, but the Giant quickly got out of the way, as Bigelow and Sid ran into each other! After Bigelow was tossed, the Giant choke slammed Sid for the pin at 18:44. Bout 7: (30 min) Bulldogs vs. Von Erichs-- Both teams went to a 30-minute draw in a clean match. The Von Erichs subbed for the Henry/Ahmed duo, as each had singles bouts this evening. Bout 8: (20 min) Goldberg vs. Backlund-- Goldberg was in serious trouble late in the time limit, but survived long enough to gain a draw. Bout 9: (30 min) Bam Bam/Taz vs. Whipwreck/Putski-- Owen and Warlord were in the Titans corner for this bout, but Putski and Whipwreck would fight gamely, earning a time-limit draw. Bout 10: (20 min) Annilhilator vs. DDP-- Page and the Sheik went right at it, and Page would be DQ'd at 2:26 for a choke, which brought out Sid, who attacked DDP, but security broke up this situation. Bout 11: (20 min) Perfect vs. Kain-- Kain would get a stunning quick pin with a suplex off the 2nd rope at 4:56. Bout 12: (20 min) Ahmed vs. Bossman-- Bossman knocks out Ahmed for the pin at 18:37 with some brass knuckles. Bout 13: (30 min) Sabu vs. Rey Misterio Jr.-- Sabu scores a beautiful flying head scissor/cradle combo to et the pin in 17:08 in another spectacular bout. Bout 14: (30 min) Headbangers vs. Maivia/Ladd-- Maivia would be DQ'd for a choke at 18:40, as the Headbangers get a huge win over the former champs. Bout 15*: (30 min) 2-Cold vs. Owen, Non-Title Match-- Owen would yank down 2-Cold by the hair to cause a DQ at 8:50, despite protests from Sid and Bigelow. The NOD then jumped 2-Cold, Sid, and Bigelow until security could break things up. Comissoner Blom then signed a rmatch with the title on the line for Manic Monday in Owen's hometown of Calgary. Bout 16: (45 min) Chynna vs. Misty Blue Simms, Ladies Title-- Misty retains her title after a clothesline at 17:37. Bout 17: (45 min) Rhodes vs. Morales, International Title-- After a lengthy scientific bout, Dusty gets the pin at 36:42 after a Dusty Driver, with both men shaking hands as Dusty is given his toughest challenge, and the Vancouver crowd gives both men a standing ovation. Bout 18: (60 min) Hogan vs. Sasuke, World Title-- Hogan gains the pin at 14:17 after a legdrop in an easy defense. However, as Bischoff was climbing into the ring to celebrate, Kaufmann came up and nailed Bischoff with a hubcap!! Bischoff was knocked out, and the NOD would attack Hogan until security would arrive to break things up. Hogan wasn't injured, but as Kaufmann celebrated in the ring, unaware that the NOD had already left. Nash grabs Kaufman and jacknifes him thru a ringside table!!! Kaufman was taken away by a strecther only as a precaution, but very luckily for Kaufman, he was uninjured.
