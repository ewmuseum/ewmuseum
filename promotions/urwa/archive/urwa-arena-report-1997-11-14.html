URWA Arena Report, 14 November 1997

14 November 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
URWA Arena Report 11/14-- Cow Palace, San Francisco Bout 1: (30 min) Eliminators vs. Blanchard/Rude-- despite a Total Elimination on Blanchard late in the bout, this would end in a 30-minute draw. Bout 2: (20 min) Benoit vs. Mr. Perfect-- Benoit ended this technical bout..by throwing powder in Perfect's eyes (????) for a DQ at 15:55. Bout 3: (30 min) Bagwell/Janetty vs. Sandman/Dreamer-- Dreamer missed a plancha from the top rope, and Janetty was alert enough to get a cover for the pin at 10:48. Bout 4: (20 min) Backlund vs. LaParka-- After a long rant about the sexual morality and vissitiudes of the San Francisco crowd, Backlund scores the pin after a piledriver at 10:19 on the chairman of the URWA. Bout 5: (20 min) Sabu vs. Rey Misterio Jr.-- These two high-flyers went at it in a wild bout, with many near three-counts, but none in this time limit draw at 20:00. Man, I'd love to see these two go at it again!! Bout 6: (20 min) Morales vs. Goldberg-- Goldberg went to bodyslam Morales, but a cradle gives Morales another win by pinfall at 10:41. Bout 7: (20 min) Blassie vs. Bigelow-- only Piper and Lawler came out with Blassie, will Dudley, Warlord, Sid and Owen brought out Bigelow. And, with Blassie in serious trouble, Lawler goes to the apron and whacks the nearby Bigelow with his nearby crown! Meanwhile, Blassie applys his swinging neckbreaker to score the pin at 12:58. Bout 8: (30 min) Meng vs. Warrior, TV Title Semifinal #1-- someone do a drug test on Meng, as he matches Ultimate Warrior hold for hold, and gets the pin at 17:52 with his Mafia Kick in an amazing upset. Bout 9: (30 min) Sammartino vs. Sid, TV Title Semifinal #2-- Owen, Interrogator, and Dudey accompanied Sid in this crucial semifinal. Bruno took a big risk early in the bout and went for a top rope jump, landed it, and scored the pin at 4:26..could Bruno finally strike gold tomorrow? Bout 10: (30 min) Andre vs. Michinoku/Sasuke-- Andre would get the pin with a Big Squash on Taka at 14:14, as Andre looks primed to get back into the title picture. Bout 11: (30 min) Simms/Nakano vs. Moolah/Kai-- Nakano would score the pin on Moolah after a 2nd rope suplex at 27:46. Bout 12: (20 min) Liger vs. Lynn-- Liger was DQ'd for use of a foreign-object at 7:18 in this cruiserweight bout. Bout 13: (No Time Limit) Road Warriors vs. Demolition vs. Gangstas, 6-man battle royal-- this got started with Smash shoving Hawk to Animal, eliminating Animal at 2:27. Meanwhile, Smash would eliminate Hawk at 6:54. Smash again would cause some chaos as he shoved Jack into Said, and Said was eliminated at 10:32. Jack was so incensed he did the same thing, shoving Ax into Smash, tossing him out at 10:56. Meanwhile, Ax would toss Nu Jack out for the victory at 13:01. Bout 14: (45 min) O. Hart vs. 2-Cold Scropio, Cruiserweight Title-- Owen came into the ring all alone, and as 2-Cold came out he was attacked by Sid, the Annihilator, Bigelow and the Interregator, ending when Sid powerbombed 2-Cold, but security would break things up, and the Titans were restrained from ringside. But, the damage was done, and Owen becomes a Cruiserweight Champion at 16:25 after a Sharpshooter. Bout 15: (45 min) Ahmed/Henry vs. Ladd/Maivia, International Tag Titles-- Ladd gets an elbow drop on Maivia to score the pinfall at 23:36, as Ladd and Maivia continue to make their mark here in the URWA. Bout 16: (45 min) Gorgeous George vs. Dusty Rhodes, International Title-- Bischoff and the Outsiders brought Geroge out to see if he could do a title double only Andre has done, the International and World titles. but, a busted-open George would be DQ'd for a bite at 16:23. Bout 17: (2 of 3 falls, 60 min) Van Damn/Candido vs. Steiner Brothers, World Tag Titles-- First Fall: Scott pins Van Damn after an airplane spin at 21:34. Second Fall: Sunny hands Candido a roll of quarters, and Candido was DQ'd for attempting to use this on Rick at 29:30, as the Steiners turn back another challenge. Bout 18: (60 min) Hulk Hogan vs. Zeus, World Title-- Bischoff and Lawler brought Hogan out, while Sid, Owen, Warlord, and Interrogator did the same with Zeus. At the 18 minute mark, Interrogator would toss brass knuckles to Zeus, but Hogan would intercept them, and nail Zeus...with no effect. Hogan would need two legdrops to finish Zeus off in 22:38, as Zeus came close to winning the title, but Hogan had just a bit more tonight. Want to see more?
