URWA Arena Report, 23 October 1997

23 October 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
URWA Arena Report 10/23 Municpal Auditorium-- Nashville, Tenn. Bout 1: (20 min) Orndorff vs. Warlord-- Warlord was a late replacement for the suspended Big Dick Dudley, and Orndorff would chalk up another win with the piledriver at 14:43. Bout 2: (20 min) Mortis vs. Steve Williams-- That cut Mortis had suffered Saturday once aain busted open on him, as blood was running down his masked area..Williams scores the pin with the Oklahoma Stampede at 17:11. Bout 3: (20 min) Bundy vs. Mr. T-- This bout was all Bundy, as he wins after a 2nd rope suplex at 8:21. Bout 4: (30 min) Owen/Bigelow vs. Headbangers-- Owen and Bigelow fancy themselves as tag contenders, but would be DQ'd by the worst tag team in the league as Owen gives Thrasher a thumb jab at 18:33..Headbangers get a win! Bout 5: (20 min) Meng vs. Arn Anderson-- A kick to the groin after an exhausting battle gives Meng the pin at 18:07. Bout 6: (20 min) Steve Austin vs. Barbarian-- Austin goes to the top rope for a splash to score the pin on Barbarian at 7:26. Bout 7: (20 min) Lex Luger vs. Ahmed Johnson-- The NOD came out to support Ahmed, and Mark Henry would toss brass knuckles to Johnson, causing his DQ at 10:27. Bout 8: (20 min) Blassie vs. Sammartino-- The NWO would bring Hulk Hogan and Outsider Nash out to ringside for this bout..Bruno's power kept the classy one off balance..and he'd eventually be DQ'ed for a choke at 15:45. Bout 9: (20 min) Gracie vs. Mil Mascaras-- Gracie didn't come out with any members of the NWO, as he and Mascaras put on a good technical bout, going to a 20-minute draw. Bout 10: (30 min) Brody/Studd vs. Blackjacks-- Lanza would hoist Studd up in a piledriver to give the Blackjacks an unexpected win at 23:31. Bout 11: (20 min) Douglas vs. Hack Myers-- Hack gave Shane LOTS of trouble tonight, but Douglas would pin Myers after a clothesline at 17:42. Bout 12: (No Time Limit) Kain vs. Undertaker, Casket Match-- The Undertaker, after a Tombstone Piledriver, puts Kain in the casket for the pin at 15:52 Bout 13: (No Time Limit) Benoit vs. Backlund, Submisson Match-- Backlund makes Benoit submit to the chicken wing after some superb technical wrestling at 30:02. Bout 14: (45 min) Nakano vs. Divine, Ladies Title-- Nakano would hit Divine with a roll of quarters, busting her open, and would get the pin at 15:18 after a scoop slam.. Bout 15: (30 min) Rude vs. Bret Hart, TV Title-- Andre and Tully came out to help Rude, while HBK and Lawler would do the same for Hart...the bout would have no interference, but Hart would score the pin with a small package after a flurry of pin attempts at 14:39. Bout 16: (45 min) Lawler vs. Andre The Giant, International Title-- Hart, Hogan, and HBK were all at ringside for this bout..Lawler gave it a good try, but Andre came away with the win after a press slam and pinfall at 13:22. Bout 17: (2 of 3 falls, 60 min) Road Warriors vs. Rock-n-Roll Express, World Tag Titles...Blassie and Piper and the Outsiders came out to support the Rock-n-Rolls in this one.. First Fall-- Aminal would be busted open, and Owen Hart would toss Morton a roll of quarters to nail Aminal, but Animal would score the pin after a gouge at 39:35 had blinded Gibson for a moment. Second Fall-- Morton would even things up at a fall each with a cross-body on Aminal at 52:35. Third Fall-- Both teams would battle thru the remaining 7 1/2 minutes without a fall, so the bout ends in a 1-1 stalemate.. Bout 18: (60 min) Vader vs. Gorgeous George, World Title-- Piper, HBK, Hart, and Blassie all came out to help George in this one..about 8 minutes into the bout, a masked man who very much looked like he had Hulk Hogan's build came up from behind while the ref and George were in a heated discussion to whack Vader in the arm with some type of club...as Vader yelped in agony..George then went to work on that arm, and then nailed Vader with a flying kick...could George do the unthinkable? Then George did his famous rolling hiplock on a weakened Vader..could he do it? 1---2---THREE...A STUNNING UPSET! Gorgeous George is the new world champion with a pinfall at 17:17..as the NWO celebrates in the ring the fans pelt the ring with some serious debris..Vader would be very livid, but after he calmed down, a examinaton proved that Vader only had a deep bruise on his forearm and will not have to sit out..
