URWA Arena Report, 24 October 1997

24 October 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
URWA Arena Report 10/24 The Pryamid, Memphis, TN Bout 1: (20 min) Benoit vs. Sabu-- Sabu and Benoit fought an excellent technical battle, but Sabu would be DQ'd for a bite at 18:37. I'm sure whoever the Cruiserweight champ is, Sabu will be heard from.. Bout 2: (20 min) Zeus vs. Williams-- This bout would go the distance, a time-limit draw...Williams remains unbeaten so far in the URWA.. Bout 3: (20 min) Sting vs. Meng-- Meng continues to look sharp here in the URWA, as he pins Sting after his Mean Side Slam at 11:51. Bout 4: (20 min) Hulk Hogan vs. The Barbarian-- Piper and Lawler would bring Hogan to the ring, while the Barbarian came out all by himself..this would be all Hulk Hogan, and Lawler would toss some brass knuckles to Hogan and Barbarian went out like a light..giving Hogan the pinfall at 6:56..but, while Hogan flexed for the crowd..out came a very angry Vader! He'd work Hogan over and put the Vader Bomb on him! Lawler came in, and was quickly clotheslined, as was Piper--with a clothesline that NEARLY took his head off..MERCY! VADER IS ONE ENRAGED MAN TONIGHT! Bout 5: (20 min) Piper (?) vs. Backlund-- Paramedics were still tending to Piper as Backlund came out, and there'd be no way Piper would be able to go in his first of two bouts tonight, so Comissoner Blom called out Stevie Richards to substitute..Backlund would rant about the unkempt condition of the URWA, but Richards would do the Memphis crowd a favor and dropkick him from behind, and the bout was on..and Richards put up a good show, but a bloody Backlund applied his famous Crossface Chickenwing appled for over 90 seconds finally made Big Stevie Cool tap out at 19:53. Bout 6: (30 min) Sandman/Dreamer vs. Road Warriors, No DQ-- Animal would put a flying tackle on Dreamer to score the pin at 22:10. The Extreme Duo put up a game effort, but couldn't dethrone the URWA's hottest tag team.. Bout 7: (20 min) Sammartino vs. Taz, No DQ-- Sammartino dominated most of the way, but Taz would play possum as Sammartino went for a Thesz press, and Taz pulls off a major upset getting the pin at 8:48! Bout 8: (30 min) Wright/Inferno vs. Candido/Van Dam-- Candido was counted-out at 23:04 after Disco gulliotine his throat over the top rope, knocking the wind out of Candido as he fall to the floor for the 10-count.. Bout 9: (20 min) Gracie vs. Whipwreck-- Blassie and Lawler came out to give Gracie some support in this one..Gracie is given trouble from Mikey, but a Ju-Jitsu Choke ends matters by a submisson at 18:30. Bout 10: (30 min) Outsiders vs. Gangstas-- The Rock-n-Roll Express would bring out the Outsiders for this bout..and Hall would be quickly DQ at 1:30 for a face rake on Mustafa..Hall begun to rough up the referee..when the Road Warriors came out, and a donnybrook followed..Hall would be hoisted up by Animal, as Hawk did his top-rope clothesline, the Doom Smash, while the Gangstas held the Rock-n-Rolls at bay..then security arrived, but the Destruction Crew struck again! Bout 11: (20 min) Lawler vs. Michinoku-- HBK would second Lawler for this bout..but it wouldn't matter, as a face rake causes Lawler's DQ at 12:03..then, to make matters worse, out comes Vader again, to rough up both HBK and Lawler..it was Lawler's turn to get the Vader Bomb, despite boos from the partisan Memphis crowd. Bout 12: (20 min) Piper vs. Flair, No-Interference Match..Piper was feeling much better by the start of this bout, and true to his word, Piper brought out no one..but Flair went berzerk, and was DQ'd for a really blantant hair pull at 4:54. Bout 13: (20 min) Blassie vs. Mascaras-- Bret and HBK brought the Classy One to ringside..Blassie had Mascaras in real trouble late in the bout, but couldn't capitalize in time to beat the time limit, a 20-minute draw.. Bout 14: (60 min) Williams/Anderson/Midnight Express vs. 2-Cold/Henry/Kaufmann/Ahmed-- The NWA beats the NOD in this battle as Eaton borrows Jim Cornette's tennis racket on Ahmed to get the pin at 12:51. Bout 15: (No Time Limit) Rude vs. Warrior, Cage Match-- Rude pulls off the upset after smashing Warrior's face into the cage for the pinfall at 4:51. Bout 16: (30 min) Rock-n-Roll Express vs. Owen/Bigelow, one-fall, non-title match-- Out came the Outsiders and Hogan to assist the RNR's tonight in this one-fall non-title bout..and they'd turn away this challenge as both men combine for a double dropkick on Owen for the pin by Morton on Hart at 18:43. Bout 17: (45 min) Andre vs. Yokozuna, International Title-- Andre, the last of the original champions, would have his title run come to an end, as Vader would come down yet again and whack both the Barbarian, who was planning to interfere, with a chair, and would do the same to Andre..knocking him cold..Meanwhile Yokosuna would mount the top rope and do his famous "BANZAI!" and drop his girth on an unconscious Andre for the pin and the International Title at 15:06, as now Ric Flair awaits Yokozuna Sunday in Dallas, while Andre and Vader will be the headliners in the 20-man battle royal tomorrow in Little Rock for the World Title Shot. Bout 18: (60 min) Gorgeous George vs. Blue Meanie, World Title-- Hogan, Piper, and the Outsiders would come out with the "Human Orchid" himself, the new World Champion tonight in his defense..did they fear another Vader assault? On the other hand, could anyone imagine the Blue Meanie the new world champion? Well, after the first 12-13 minutes, Blue Meanie as champ had possibilites, but Hogan would toss George his NWO spraypaint, knocking Meanie out cold, as George's first title defense goes down as sucessful with a pin at 16:16.
