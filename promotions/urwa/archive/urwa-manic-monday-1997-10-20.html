URWA Manic Monday, 20 October 1997

20 October 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
URWA Manic Monday 10/20-- Freedom Hall, Louisville, KY Bout 1: (20 min) Outsider Nash vs. Honky Tonk Man-- Scott Hall and Hollywood Hogan would come out to the ring to give moral support to Nash. And Nash didn't seem to care much about a win tonight, as Hogan would bust HTM open with a chair behind the ref's back. Nash would then be DQ'd for doing the same a little while later at 4:51. Bout 2: (20 min) Farooq vs. Mark Henry-- Farooq showed tremendous strength suplexing Henry off the 2nd rope for a pin at 14:44. Bout 3: (20 min) Dick The Bruiser vs. Royce Gracie-- this time, the NWO would bring out Lawler, Piper, and the Rock-n-Roll Express to protect Gracie. But, Bruiser would score a major upset by using a power piledriver to get Gracie pinned in only 9:22. Comissoner Blom then came out to announce 9 bouts for the Halloween Hell PPV this Sunday in Dallas. More on that in the Ringside Report. Bout 4: (45 min) Cruiserweight Semifinal: 2-Cold Scorpio vs. Mikey Whipwreck-- Scorpio advances to the finals after pinning Whipwreck with a two-arm toss in a classic bout at 20:41. Whipwreck nearly had 2-Cold pinned on numerous occasions. Bout 5: (45 min) Cruiserweight Semifinal: Taka Michinoku vs. Chris Benoit-- Benoit moves to the finals after a press slam at 17:16. But as Benoit was accepting the kudos of the Louisville fans, out came Royce Gracie to drill Benoit with a chair, knocking Benoit out with a concussio, but he'll be able to wrestle his bouts this week. I'm sure the Crippler will be feeling that one! Bout 6: (30 min) TV Title: Bret Hart vs. Diamond Dallas Page-- After DDP made his way to the ring, out came Bret Hart, and before HBK and the Rock-n-Rolls could accompany him, Bret got clobbered by the Quad Threat, Sting, and Lex Luger..what a mess! Hart, showing the URWA that he is a true champion, refused any medical attention until after the bout. And even though the NWO was a non-factor in this bout, Hart would score the pinfall after a gut wrench suplex at 18:08.
