URWA Arena Report, 3 October 1997

3 October 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Arena Report 10/3, Hartford Civic Center, Hartford, CT Bout 1: (30 min) Road Warriors vs. Eliminators-- The Roadies are rising in the tag ratings, and they beat the Eliminators easily, as Animal pins Kronus after a Triple Crossknee in just 7:25. Bout 2: (20 min) Sandman vs. Cactus Jack-- Sandman would be DQ'd for use of his Singapore Cane at 18:47. Bout 3: (30 min) Sammartino/Race vs. Hansen/Anderson-- This was another quick brawl that sees Arn Anderson DQ'd for an eye-poke on Bruno at 1:31. Bout 4: (20 min) Dick The Bruiser vs. Roddy Piper-- In a wild brawl, Bruiser would smash Piper with the chair at 13:11 for a DQ.. Bout 5: (20 min) Terry Funk vs. Shane Douglas-- Terry Funk would lose and be DQ'd for use of a branding-iron at 9:27. Bout 6: (20 min) Chris Benoit vs. Mortis-- Mortis hang in there in this bout, and despite a furious rally from Benoit, the bout went to a 20:00 draw. Bout 7: (20 min) Dan Severn vs. Wrath-- In the last minute of the bout, Wrath lands a piledriver to get the win at 19:12. The Rare Oddities are on a roll.. Bout 8: (20 min) Ric Flair vs. Tully Blanchard-- Flair would put Blanchard in the Figure Four with only seconds to go...Blanchard couldn't hold on and submitted at 19:45... Bout 9: (20 min) Terry Gordy vs. Shane Douglas-- Douglas was woozy after his bout earlier with Funk, and a Gordy Splash gives Terry his first singles win after a Gordy Splash and pin at 16:40. Bout 10: (20 min) Bruno Sammartino vs. Ox Brody-- Sammartino would get the win in this one, having not broken a sweat in his first bout, getting the pin after a Brutal Body Slam at 16:10. Bout 11: (20 min) Crusher Lisowski vs. Paul Orndorff-- Big John feared the worst as in the last 30 seconds, Orndorff got caught in a Crusher abdominal stretch. But, Orndorff with all his might held on for the 20:00 draw. Bout 12: (30 min) Alex Wright/Disco Inferno vs. Richards/Meanie-- This bout would go all the way to a 30-minute draw with action going back and forth. Bout 13: (No-Time Limit) 3-Man Battle Royal: Lawler vs. Ahmed vs. Mahoney-- In a very quick bout, as 3-Man Battle Royals tend to be, Ahmed would body slam Mahoney out of the ring in just 47 seconds. Vader and Nikita Koloff would came out to attempt to distract Ahmed, and it would backfire to eliminate Lawler via a clothesline at 2:04. Lawler was furious a Vader and Koloff for their interference. Bout 14: (No-Time Limit) Non-Title Cage Match: Sid vs. Giant Gonzales-- Giant would dominate that bout this time, despite having King Kong Bundy in his corner, and won by espacing the cage at 10:53, giving the Giant a mandatory title shot next week. Bout 15: (60 min) Ladies Title: Candi Divine vs. Bull Nakano-- Nakano suprised everyone in Hartford by attacking Divine with a suicide dive thru the ropes, and Divine would not make it back in to the ring for a count-out Nakano win at 4:35. Bout 16: (2 of 3 falls, 60 min): World Tag Titles: Outsiders vs. British Bulldogs-- Lawler and Hogan accompanied the Outsiders to ringside this evening.. First Fall--after a grueling 34-minute opening fall, Nash took the easy way out, smashing Dynamite Kid with the chair at 34:31 to give the Bulldogs a 1-0 lead via a DQ. Second Fall--just minuters later, a forearm smash gives Dynamite Kid a quick three-count on Scott Hall to secure the World Tag Team Titles at 39:25!! (under URWA rules, as long as the deciding fall ends in pin or submission, the title changes hands)
