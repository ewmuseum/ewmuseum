URWA Arena Report, 21 November 1997

21 November 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
URWA Arena Report 11/21/97, Key Arena, Seattle, WA Bout 1: (45 min) Ahmed/Henry/2-Cold vs. Dreamer/Warrior/Benoit-- late in the bout, Dreamer lands a DDT to give him the pin on Ahmed at 44:48. Bout 2: (20 min) Andre vs. Morales-- Heenan brought Andre to the ring for this battle, but Pedro would fall prey to the big squash as Andre wins again at 16:57. Bout 3: (20 min) Sid vs. Kain-- Bigelow and Owen would bring out Sid, and Sid would beat Kain cleanly, winning with the Power Bomb at 15:09. Bout 4: (30 min) Van Dam/Candido vs. Eliminators-- The Elims were DQ'd at 20:43 for constant illegal double-teams on Candido. Bout 5: (20 min) Meng vs. Abdullah-- Butcher splashes Meng off the 2nd rope to get the pin at 9:55, a good debut for the Butcher. Bout 6: (30 min) Bulldogs vs. Sting/Luger-- Both teams battled back and forth, but neither side could score the pin for a 30-minute draw. Bout 7: (20 min) Flair vs. Piper-- George, Blassie, and Bischoff brought Piper out for this bout. Piper would be outmatched most of this bout, and was DQ'd at 12:49 for an eye-poke. Bout 8: (20 min) Goldberg vs. Blanchard-- Heenan and Bockwinkel brought Tully out for this bout, and Heenan would toss Blanchard a foreign object..but Blanchard would be caught red-handed for the DQ 13:23. Bout 9: (20 min) Backlund vs. Perfect-- these two masterful technicans fought hard to a 20-minute draw. Bout 10: (20 min) Douglas vs. Whipwreck-- Douglas scores the pin on Mikey at 12:12 with an elbow drop. Will the Franchise find a home in a URWA camp in December? Bout 11: (20 min) Kaufman vs. Nakano-- As Kaufman came out, Bossman would nail Andy from behind with...what looks like the Comissoner's briefcase..Bossman grabbed the mike.."Kaufman, you punk, the Commish wanted me and my friends to show you what he thinks of your battle-of-the-sexes challenges!" As Bossman finished off his statement, the Wright Brothers came out to whip Kaufman with a chain...the East Tennessee Dog Whuppin! Ron finished it off by dropping his cigar lighted end first on Kaufman's belly...yikes! They then took Kaufman and dragged him into the ring. Nakano would dominate this bout, winning with a shoulder-ram at 9:19. Then, as the weary Kaufman was getting up, the Sheik came down and lit his face up with a fireball! Kaufmann yelped in pain, as security came down to help Kaufman to the locker room. Kaufman had about a bad a night as you can legally have in the URWA. Bout 12: (No Time-Limit) Rude vs. Arn, Cage Match-- A busted-open Rude scores the win at 18:24, after knocking Arn out cold bashing his head into the cage. Bout 13: (30 min) Putski vs. Owen Hart, non-title match-- Owen gets the pin at 10:24 on Putski with a forearm uppercut. Bout 14: (30 min) Sammartino vs. George, TV Title-- Lawler, Piper, Blassie, and Bischoff all came out with the Georgeous one for this chance to acquire the TV belt. Lawler would nail Bruno with his crown early on, but a bloody Sammartino still beats George at 21:09 with a two arm toss. Bout 15: (No Time Limit, No DQ, No Countout) Interrogator/Bigelow vs. Outsiders, International Tag Title-- Bischoff and Blassie would stay out with the Outsiders, while Taz and Sid looked after the Titan duo. Nash would nail Interrogator with a foreign object to score the pin in 11:51 to settle all doubt as to who the real International Tag Champs are. Bout 16: (45 min) Austin vs. Dy. Rhodes, International Title-- the Dusty Driver gives Rhodes yet another sucessful International title defense over Stone Cold at 17:01. Bout 17: (2 of 3 falls, 60 min) Road Warriors vs. Steiners, World Tag Titles-- First Fall-- Hawk would get a pin on Steiner with a neckbreaker at 40:35. Second Fall-- Rick DQ's himself to get his team to keep the Tag Titles with a thumb jab on Aminal at 57:20. The Roadies will get a rematch, however. Bout 18: (60 min) Blue Meanie vs. Hulk Hogan, World Title-- Bischoff was all alone in bringing out Hollywood. In a upset, Hogan would be DQ'd trying to use a foreign object on Meanie at 11:58. Comissoner Blom will sign a No-DQ Match for their next encounter. Many in the Seattle crowd were laughing at Hogan, taunting him with "Hogan fears Meanie..."
