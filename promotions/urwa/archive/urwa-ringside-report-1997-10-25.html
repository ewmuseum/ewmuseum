URWA — Ringside Report

URWA Ringside Report, 25 October 1997

25 October 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
URWA Ringside Report with J.J. Anthony Fans, here's the very latest on tomorrow's exciting PPV spectacular, Halloween Hell, out of Dallas, Texas's Reunion Arena.. and my predictions.. 1. World Title: Gorgeous George vs. Andre The Giant Look for Andre to win the title here, if the Gorgeous One dosen't get DQ'd first.. 2. For the World Tag Titles: The NWO's Rock-n-Roll Express vs. Road Warriors, but this bout is 2-out-of-3 falls, no DQ, no Time Limit The Roadies have given the Rock-n-Rolls all they can handle..and with no clock to hide behind, look for the Roadies to win.. 3. For The International Title: Yokosuna vs. Ric Flair Can Ric Flair chop the big 600 pound Sumo down to size?...I tend to think so.. 4. For The International Tag Titles: The Outsiders vs. The Steiners Nash and Hall get a chance to snap back into some gold..but the Steiners should hold them off.. 5. For The TV Title: Sid vs. Bret Hart, Inside A Steel Cage! Both men have plenty of heart, no pun intended, but I look for Bret to turn away Sid here.. 6. Bruno Sammartino vs. Sandman Bruno got a dose of reality in being upset by Taz..I don't look for Bruno to have that problem again.. 7. Rude/Blanchard vs. Bruiser/Crusher If Blanchard's head is in the game after a SAVAGE attack on Baby Doll, look for Heenan's duo to win.. 8. For The Cruiserweight Title, 2-Cold Scorpio vs. Chris Benoit This one is too close for call, but I'll give the edge to 2-Cold..as Benoit's taken a beating this week.. 9. In A Special Triangle Match to unify the Ladies and "Intergender Titles", in the first bout, Andy Kaufman goes against Candi Divine, with the winner getting Ladies Champion Bull Nakano. These 2 bouts will be no-time limit and no-DQ. And, if either lady is the winner, the Intergender Title may never be "claimed" in the URWA by Kaufmann. But meanwhile, should Kaufmann win both bouts, the Ladies Title will offically become the Intergender Title. Nakano will prevail..as she's been dominant in the ladies field 10. Cactus Jack vs. Sabu in a special No-DQ match! I'll take Cactus Jack with the special rules.. 11. Owen Hart vs. DDP Owen to snap into the Diamond.. 12. Kain vs. Barbarian.. Kain should prevail here Also, let's quickly go over the rules for Bad Dreams Halloween Night.. 1. At Manic Monday, 10 men (one of which will be a free-agent) will be chosen to get any challenge they choose on the Bad Dreams PPV..the 10 men will then be assigned a number from 1 to 10. 2. For instance, say Rick Rude will be one of the the men chosen: He can go after any championship, singles, tag, or 6-man or challenge anybody he wishes..but if Rick chooses, with or without help, to go after a championship, he must state the title he wishes to go after..if he states a person, he gets that person, but by the time Rude gets to him, he may not be champ, and therefore, no championship will be at stake 3. The World Champion is limited to two defenses, all other champions are limited to three..so the luck of the draw comes into play.. 4. If any titleholder loses his championship after his first defense, he is entitled to a rematch on the next available card.. 5. 1 man from each of the 24 stables who isn't a champion will be chosen for a 24-man battle royal in the opening bout..the winner meets the World Champion in the main event.. That's all for now folks, see you at Oklahoma City Monday with our next Ringside Report..
