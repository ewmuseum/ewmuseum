URWA Manic Monday, 22 September 1997

22 September 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Manic Monday--9/22 Charlotte, NC--Independence Arena Bout 1: (30 min) Ladies Tourny Quarterfinal: Chynna vs. Bull Nakano-- Even the powerful Chyna couldn't mount an offensive on the Japanese star, and Nakano gets the pin after an airplane spin at 9:55. Bout 2: (30 min) Ladies Tourny Quarterfinal: June Byers vs. Madusa-- In a mostly technical bout, June Byers ruins it by hitting Madusa with a foriegn object to get the pin at 12:56. Bout 3: (20 min) ????? vs. Taz-- Many wondered who the mystery man would be as Taz made his way to the ring..Lou Thesz then came up the runaway to grab the mic..."Taz...you think I'm your opponent? ..Um..look behind you." and there he was--ROYCE GRACIE, the UFC star.. this was almost all Gracie, with the Brazilian putting a Ju-Jitsu Choke on Taz for a submisson at 12:19! Bout 4: (20 min) Ric Flair vs. Shane Douglas-- another interesting bout saw Flair have some trouble with Shane, but Flair would pin Douglas with a slingshot suplex at 14:51. Bout 5: (30 min) TV Title: Bret Hart vs. Stan Hansen-- Despite Terry Funk's berating of Hart constantly throughout the bout, Hart gets the win after a guillotine over the top rope for a pin at 17:38. Terry Funk, branding iron in one hand, towel in the other, took any liberty in calling Bret an "Egg-Sucking Dog" and a coward for not defending his belt against Funk this weekend. Bout 6: (40 min) International Title: Andre The Giant vs. Lex Luger-- Luger was the chosen opponent for Andre this evening...and after Luger intercepted a roll of quarters from Bobby Heenan to clobber the Giant with them..Bobby then grabbed the timekeeper's beverage, threw it on Andre to revive him, and Andre got up in time to hoist Luger up in a press slam for the pin at 18:42. Andre, with some quick thinking from the Brain, retains the belt yet again..
