URWA Arena Report, 18 October 1997

18 October 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
URWA Arena Report 10/18/97-- Riverfront Coliseum, Cincinnati, OH Bout 1: (No Time Limit, No DQ) 6-Man Semifinal: Undertaker/Demolition vs. Hogan/HBK/Hall-- as announced by Eric Bischoff, HBK would take Nash's place in the bouts tonight so Nash could concentrate on winning the I-C title tonight, and the NWO trio was flanked by Piper and Lawler. But interfernce would backfire as Lawler would bring a chair and hold it on the apron as a battering ram, Smash reveresed, but HBK put on the brakes..and Smash would hoist HBK up for a side slam, and the ref gets a quick three-count for the pin at 19:57. Bout 2: (No Time Limit, No DQ) 6-Man Semifinal Dory Funk/Ric Flair/??? vs. Wrath/Kain/Meng-- Flair would introduce his partner, fellow Horseman OLE ANDERSON for this 6-man semifinal..this bout would rage on and on, and Dory would go to the top rope for a jump at the 50-minute mark, but miss! Kain would make the cover at 50:31, as the Rare Oddities move to the finals later tonight. Bout 3: (20 min) Terry Funk vs. Paul Orndorff-- a quick boot to the gut gives Orndorff a quick pin over Terry Funk at 13:59 after Funk had a spinning-toe hold got Orndorff to the ropes for an escape. Bout 4: (20 min) Lawler vs. Sabu-- Piper, Hogan, and the Outsiders woud bring Lawler out to the ring..and Sabu would go berzerk, getting DQ'd for a gouge at 8:56. Bout 5: (20 min) Kaufmann vs. Divine, Intergender Non Title Bout-- the NOD would bring Kaufmann to the ring for this bout. Kaufman had Candi in an airplane spin, and then started to celebrate with the NOD and gloated for several moments, and Kaufmann then motioned for the airplane spin..Candi would cradle Kaufmann and score the pin at 3:57. You sure you want to claim the Intergender title, Andy? Bout 6: (30 min) Owen Hart/Bam Bam Bigelow vs. Brawler/Hopper-- this was a nasty brawl, ending when Hopper was DQ'd for a choke on Bigelow at 4:02 after numerous infractions of the rules by both parties. Bout 7: (No Time Limit, No Count-Out, No-DQ) Earthquake vs. Big Dick Dudley, Loser Must Serve a 7 Day Supsension-- Comissoner Blom came out and said since Earthquake did not agree to a 14-day suspension for the loser..this bout would be only for a 7-day suspension.. Earthquake hoisted the big man in a piledriver and would score the pin at 10:20. See you in a week, Richard.. Bout 8: (30 min) Tommy Dreamer/Sandman vs. Studd/Bruiser Brody-- this was a rough and rugged battle...the time limit would expire ending as a 30-minute draw. Bout 9: (30 min) Crusierweight Quarterfinal: Alex Wright vs. 2-Cold Scorpio-- Wright tried to pearl-harbor 2-Cold, but missed..Scorpio then brawled with das Wunderkind, gullotining him over the top rope. Momentum carried Wright outside of the ring, and was counted out at :58. Bout 10: (20 min) Undertaker vs. Meng--in this preview of the 6-man finals...Meng would bring out some brass knuckles to get the pin on Undertaker in a shocking upset at 14:43. Bout 11: (30 min) Candido/Van Damn vs. Headbangers-- Candido scores the pinfall after a cross-body block on Trasher at 19:09. So it appears the Quad Threat's Housecleaning has been justified. Bout 12: (60 min) Bret Hart/Gorgeous George/Blassie/Piper vs. Freebirds/Race/Sammartino-- the Outsiders came out to accompany the NWO foursome to the ring.. George was DQ'd for a thumb jab on Harley Race at 9:57. Bout 13: (45 min) Ladies Title: Bull Nakano vs. Miss Jacqueline-- Nakano gets the pin in a grueling bout after making Jacqueline submit to a half-Boston Crab at 18:42. Bout 14: (45 min) International Tag Title: Brusier/Crusher vs. Steiners-- Rick hits Lisowski with a Steiner Line to get the pin at 34:47, as the Steiners retain the International Tag Belts.. Bout 15: (45 min) International Title: Andre The Giant vs. Kevin Nash-- the entire NWO came out with Nash,, while all of the Heenan clan came out with Andre..by far, this was Andre's toughest challenge..Nash would kick Andre in the groin..and Nash would put Andre in the Nash Knife, and come very close to getting a 3-count..Hall then whacked Andre with a chair..and Nash would come off with a forearm smash..only to get another 2-count..Andre would clear the cobwebs off..and land a massive headbutt on Nash and get the count of three at 20:37...and the focus would be on Blanchard, as HBK would put Sweet Chin Music on him, knocking him out cold! Blanchard would suffer a concussion, but be OK for next week.. Bout 16: (No Time Limit, No DQ) 6-Man Tag Titles: Undertaker/Demolition vs. Wrath/Kain/Meng-- Wrath would put a big boot on the Undertaker, knocking him out at 45:34 and Wrath gets the pin, securing the World 6-Man Belts for his Rare Oddities.. Bout 17: (2 of 3 falls, 60 min) World Tag Titles: Road Warriors vs. Rock-n-Roll Express-- Vader and Backlund came out with the Roadies, while the Express came out with the Outsiders..and Eric Bischoff.. First Fall-- The Roadies had the Rock-n-Rolls reeling, and Gibson would be DQ'ed for poking the eyes of Hawk at 18:22. Second Fall--After 34 minutes of exhausting action, the Rock-n-Rolls even the score after Gibson clotheslines Hawk after he had Morton rolled-up at 52:22. Third Fall-- Both teams went all out in the last 7:38, with Hall throwing a vial of some kind into Gibson, which was...POWDER...and he fired it into Animal's eyes for a DQ at 59:14, with the Roadies winning 2 falls to 1, but not the title due to a deciding fall DQ..the Roadies will get another day..Bischoff would walk behind his four men into ganging up on the Roadies, only to run into Andre The Giant! The Giant was screaming at him, and would slam Bischoff thru a table...OUCH!! Bout 18: (60 min) World Title: Ox Brody vs. Vader-- Vader would play with Brody for a good 15 minutes, and foiled Bundy's interfernce...stomping on Bundy's hand, sending brass knuckles flying into the crowd! Andre then came out, and hit both men with chairs..knocking both out. Vader got to his feet first, and nail Brody with the Vader Splash for a pin at 16:45.
