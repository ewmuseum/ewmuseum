URWA Ringside Report, 3 November 1997

3 November 1997

URWA Ringside Report with J.J. Anthony Well, Undertaker was more than willing to give Andre the Giant a rematch, which foils any terrorist attacks planned on Undertaker's men..or so we think..but the question is..who will be the world champion when Andre comes around? With Undertaker favoring a bad back, Yokosuna could easily win the casket match, and the next night, and Vader could regain the title the following night...setting up a possible Vader/Andre battle next week.. The International Tag Title Tourny got off to a big bang tonight with Rocky Maivia and Ernie Ladd pulling off another upset on the powerful Road Warriors to advance to the semifinals against Dick The Bruiser and Crusher...and one of those teams could be the cinderlla story of this tournament. And, with one week to go before our next Acquistion Period, it appears Cactus Jack will be the hot commodity. Team Hardcore has put out some angry noises concerning Cactus, and I'm sure many of the URWA's factions will give him a hard look. That's all for tonight...talk to you Thursday from San Diego as the URWA invades Southern California and Nevada..
