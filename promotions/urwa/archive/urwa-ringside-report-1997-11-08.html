URWA Ringside Report, 8 November 1997

URWA Ringside Report by J.J. Anthony Well, the card is set for Manic Monday November 10th in Reno World Title at Stake: (60 min) Hulk Hogan, TV Champ vs. Vader 4-Way Dance: Outsiders, Demolition, Road Warriors, and Eliminators, winners get a International Tag Title shot next week Mystery Challenge: (20 min) Baron's Mystery Man vs. Big Bossman Special Tag Team Russian Roulette Match: Comissioner Blom/??? vs. Andy Kaufmann/???, no-DQ, no-time limit, must pin both men to win Ladies Title: (45 min) Mildred Burke vs. Madusa (20 min) Royce Gracie vs. Great Sasuke -- as soon as the results for the November 10th Manic Monday are posted, the monthly acquistion period begins. -- WSOS matches for Thanksgiving Eve: Heenan Family (TBA) vs. NWO (Hogan, Outsiders, George, Gracie) Titans of Hell (TBA) vs. Boy Toys (TBA) Horseman (TBA) vs. ??? -- Rumors are abdundant as to who the Comissoner has decided on to tag up with in the all important Russian Roulette match Monday..names we heard..Cowboy Bill Watts, Roddy Piper, Big Bossman, Verne Gagne, and Nick Bockinkel..remember, if Blom's team loses, Blom must appoint a brand new commissoner, and Kaufman will be allowed to make his Inter-Sex belt an offical URWA title. -- That's all for now, I'll talk to you this Monday in Reno..right now, I have to catch a cab to UNLV for the Holyfield-Moorer heavyweight fight.. TAXI!!!!!
