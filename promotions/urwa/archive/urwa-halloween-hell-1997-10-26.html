URWA Halloween Hell

26 October 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
URWA Halloween Hell PPV 10-26-97, Dallas, TX, Reunion, Arena J.J. Anthony starts the PPV in center ring, welcoming everyone to the PPV spectacular..when the lights go out..and the Video Wall flashes one word over and over in red.."HELL"..and suddenly a spotlight casts down..on a bald-headed Owen Hart..who has a hand-held mic on him.."That's Right J.J., welcome to Dallas everyone..and we welcome the URWA to the Titans of Hell!..then out would come, all in black tights with a red stripe down the sides, and all very bald: Warlord, Interrigator, Bundy, Bigelow, Zeus, Big Dick, Bloody Ox, the Natural Disasters and Sid..Owen resumed his comments: "That's right NWO, when you start running rough-shot over the URWA..you go to hell..and the Titans of Hell will pay you a visit before the night is over...and only we know when! HA HA HA HA HA.." GOOD LORD...things are getting very ugly here in the URWA.. Bout 1: (20 min) Kain vs. Barbarian-- Kain would set the tone for the evening..by getting DQ'ed with an eye poke, giving the Barbarian his first win by DQ at 4:26. Bout 2: (20 min) Owen vs. DDP-- Owen didn't bring his minions out for his bout with DDP..and probably should of, as DDP puts the Diamond Cutter on for the pin at 18:23. Bout 3: (30 min, No DQ) Cactus Jack vs. Sabu-- Jack held Sabu up near the ropes..WAIT--who's coming out from under the ring..it's Terry Funk! Funk has his branding out and swings..Sabu DUCKS! Cactus gets nailed with the branding iron! Sabu goes for his Moonsault while the Hardcore Duo reels in shock..nails it! 1-2-3..Sabu wins the no-DQ bout by pin at 16:41. And while Cactus heads back to the locker room after a discussion (which I wouldn't call heated)..out cames Meng...superkick on Cactus Jack! Security rushes to the fray and breaks things up.. Bout 4A: (No Time Limit, No DQ) Andy Kaufman vs. Candi Divine-- the NOD is out in full force for the bout..but Divine dosen't come out alone..Misty Blue Simms, Dump Matsumoto, Jackie, Wendy Richter, Reggie Bennett, and even Chyna are out at ringside in an attempt to keep the NOD at bay, and with "Sisters Are Doing It for Themselves" playing in the background, they lead Divine to the ring..every time NOD tried to interfere, 3 or 4 ladies would surrond and subdue whoever tried to intervene..meanwhile, both persons gave their all in this bout..and a clothesline gives Kaufmann the upset at 18:12 by pin.. OH NO--Kaufmann's one bout away from desacrating the Ladies Title! Bout 4B: (No Time Limit, No DQ) Kaufmann vs. Bull Nakano, Ladies Title-- Divine, though, disappointed, goes out to ringside..and this time, June Byers, Mildred Burke, and Lelani Kai, who make the ladies forces go up to 10 aginst 3 NOD's..they were determined not to let Kaufmann go away tonight with their title..and Nakano went wild, like a woman posessed..9 minutes later...Nakano hoisted Kaufmann up for an airplane spin, Ahmed climbs to the apron..Jackie nails him with a chair right in the head!...on the other side, Mark Henry tries to interfere..Misty Blue gets up on the apron and tosses Henry a chair..What?? OWWW!! Misty Blue dropkicks Henry into the chair, or should I say, the chair into Henry...Henry is staggered..Moolah whacks Henry in the knee with a chair! Meanwhile..Kaufmann is staggered from the airplane and falls flat on his back..Nakano just puts her foot on Kaufmann for any easy pin at 9:20! Nakano keeps the Ladies Title! Bout 5: (No Time Limit, No DQ) 2-Cold Scorpio vs. Benoit, Cruiserweight Title-- after 18 minutes of great technical wrestling, Scorpio smashes Benoit with a chair, and is the first-ever Cruiserweight Champ by pin at 18:38. Bout 6: (45 min) Bruiser/Crusher vs. Rude/Blanchard-- After 17 minutes, a press slam Blanchard tried on the Brusier fails, and Bruiser crashes down on Tully for the pin at 17:37. Bout 7: (30 min) Sammartino vs. Sandman-- Bruno dominates, and pins Sandman after a splashin just 3:00, which makes many wonder when Bruno will get the key title-shots? Bout 8: (No Time Limit) Bret Hart vs. Sid, Steel Cage Match-- Sid would be brought out by the rest of the Titans of Hell..who surrounded the cage..and as Bret came out..LOOKOUT! Rude kicks Bret in the knee..and...a Rude Awakening! Oh my, it dosen't look good for Bret...what's up with that? Rude tosses Hart into the cage and the match begins..where's the NWO? Meanwhile, once Bret entered the cage, he became a man posessed...and started to choke out Sid to where Sid was nearly unconscious..Bret climbs the top of the cage, and sees the Titans surrounding the floor, now joined by Kain..as Hart needed the floor, he did a cross-body block on the masses below! Hart offically wins by exiting the cage in only 4:29..out comes the Rock-and-Rolls to help Hart..and Bigelow and Owen come up from behind to NAIL them with chairs! Sid, who had exited the cage thru the door now has Bret, and 5 Titans (the Diasters, Brody, Dudley, and the Interrogator) have Bret by the hair..Warlord's pull the mat back...what's this..oh no..A QUINTUPLE SPIKE POWER BOMB...OH MY!! Bret is OUT COLD!! Security rushes in as all of the men SPIT on Bret before they can all be subdued..the paramedics come out and have to wheel Bret off! Bret may have won tonight, but his 2nd TV Title run may be in DIRE jeopardy..and where was the rest of the NWO during all of this? Meanwhile, the video wall was showing a HEATED ARGUMENT between Bischoff and HBK over the future of some URWA title shots, as it appears HBK was lobbying Bischoff for a World Title Shot! As the rest of the NWO subdues Bischoff and HBK..HBK leaves the locken room furious, comes out while the cage is being dismantled..looks like he has some words: "It is obvious to everyone that Bischoff has his favorites, and the boy toys here are just being used. He only wants us here to keep the titles and let the glory shine on his bunch of losers, cause look around. 3 of the 4 belts here are with the boy toys. When push comes to shove, Bischoff dosn't care about us. And we have noticed the lack of respect that we are getting as part of the nWo. Well, we don't need them to win matches for us, we can do that fine on our own. Thanks for nothing Mr. Bischoff, and we would like to apologize to the whole of the URWA and ask to be taken back. If you won't have us, then what the hell, we will stay on our own. We can handle it! Any nWo guys who want revenge? For once in your lives, be men about it, don't attack us from behind, challenge us from the front, be it 1 on 1, 2 on 2, or even 4 on 4, that is fine, we will be there!" WOW-- ANOTHER MAJOR SUPRISE...the Boy Toys QUIT the NWO!! Or, were they double agents serving their own purposes? Bout 9: (45 min) Outsiders vs. Steiners, International Tag Titles-- the NWO, still reeling for HBK's major announcement, didn't bring anyone out to ringside for this bout..and it would be costly as Rick pins Hall after a Steiner Line at 27:35..after the bout, with the Heenan family looking on, Tully Blanchard come out to NAIL Nash from behind with a chair, while on the other side of the ring, Flair would do the same to Hall. Then, Blanchard would bring out...SPRAYPAINT! They paint "Horsemen" on Hall's back, and "Forever" on Nash's back...the rest of the NWO comes out to make the save.. Bout 10: (45 min) Ric Flair vs. Yokosuna, International Title-- The Heenan Family assembled at ringside to keep the NWO from interrupting this bout.. Flair actually got the big man of his feet with his agility, and put the Figure 4 on Yokosuna...Yoko would find the ropes, but an Irish Whip by Flair...and a Flair Chop right to the forehead with all Flair had left knocked Yoko out cold! Flair would then score the pin...and WOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO!!! Flair is the new International Champion by pin at 22:37! Bout 11: (2 of 3 falls, No Time Limit, No DQ) World Tag Titles: Rock-n-Roll Express vs. Road Warriors-- HBK would be the only one to come out with the Roadies this evening..the NWO wasn't there..but this would be a dogfight for the World Tag Titles. First Fall--Hawk lands a clothesline with Aminal had Morton hoisted for the pin at 21:21. Second Fall-- HBK would try to toss a weary Morton a roll of quarters, but Hawk would intercept and blast Morton with it...cover...1...2...THRRRREEEEEEE!! Roadies are the new World Tag Champs, 2 falls to 0 at 50:34. Bout 12: (60 min) World Title: Georgeous George vs. Andre The Giant-- Despite numerous attempts for a DQ from George and the NWO, Andre would bust George open with a massive Headbutt, and then a backbreaker with the Heenan family all yelling to the Gorgeous one.."GIVE UP!" makes George SUBMIT the championship at 11:17, as Andre becomes the first man in URWA history to hold more than one championship in his career.. But there was unfinished business.. While the Heenan family celebrated in the ring, the video wall came on, with Comissoner Blom back in the locker room, saying that Bret Hart had suffered a major neck sprain and concussion which will put him out of comission for 6 weeks. Therefore, Bret will not be able to meet URWA rules about a championship being defended once every 30 days, so sadly, Bret will be stripped of his TV title, and a 8-man tournament to determine the new champ starts tomorrow..and Sid will be banned from this tournament. in addition, when Bret is well, he is entitled to a TV title shot when he goes off IR December 7th. Also, Terry Funk will be in the tournament, since he was scheduled for a title shot..along with 7 other non-title holding contenders...Lastly, the Boy Toys may name a replacement for Bret during the 6 months he's away..
