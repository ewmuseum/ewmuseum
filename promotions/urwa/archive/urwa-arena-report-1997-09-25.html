URWA Arena Report, 25 September 1997

25 September 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
URWA Arena Report 9/25/97 Greensboro, NC Bout 1: (30 min) Ladies Title Tourny Quarterfinal: Fabulous Moolah vs. Dump Matsumoto---Moolah pulled out all the stops to win tonight as she blast Dump with a roll of quarters to get the pin at 12:42. Bout 2: (30 min) Freebirds vs. Headbangers-- This one went down to the final seconds as Gordy landed a sucker punch on Mosh to get the pin at 29:51. Bout 3: (20 min) Sting vs. Bobby Heenan-- Sting was dominating when Andre The Giant showed up at ringside. Sting hoisted Heenan up for a press slam when Andre came up to the apron and shoved Sting, so that Heenan landed on top of him. The ref scored the 3-count, and Bobby Heenan, by pinfall at 11:21, has done it again! Bout 4: (30 min) Bulldogs vs. Furnas/LaFon-- What started out as a scientific battle ended with all 4-men brawling outside of the ring for a double-countout at 13:47. Security had to be called to break this one up, and many here at Greensboro wondered what caused this bout to brake down... Bout 5: (30 min) Harley Race/Bruno Sammartino vs. Midnight Express-- Harley was in for the majority of the bout, and paid the price as Eaton landed an Alabama Jam on Race at 25:32. Bout 6: (20 min) Steve Austin vs. Nikita Koloff-- Koloff choked Austin over and over while the ref was distracted by a brawl amongst the spectators at the Greensboro Coliseum. Austin then, weakened, couldn't bridge his way out of a test-of-strength that had Austin's shoulders to the mat for a three-count at 15:19. Bout 7: (20 min) The Giant vs. Stan Hansen-- The Giant pins Hansen after a failed bodyslam by the badman from Borger at 18:12. Bout 8: (20 min) Chris Benoit vs. Sabu-- Right before the bell Austin, DDP, Sting, Luger, Blue Meanie, Taz, and Stevie Richards surrond the ring. Benoit went wild and was DQ'd for a choke at 3:26. Then the 7 men jumped on Benoit and beat him senseless!! Benoit suffered a minor rib pull and will be able to continue to wrestle. Bout 9: (30 min) Eliminators vs. Richards/Meanie-- While Sabu went to the back, Richards and Meanie climb in to face the Eliminators, with the remaining 5 men at ringside. While Richards and Meanie were hamming it up, they seemed to be unaware that the bell had rung. Saturn and Kronos come up to do a Total Elimination on Richards for a pin in a new record of 28 seconds! Austin, DDP, Sting, Luger, and Taz protest, but the Eliminators come away with the win. Bout 10: (20 min) DDP vs. Gracie-- Sabu, and Richards and Meanie resume their ringside position as DDP entered to face shootfighter Royce Gracie. Gracie beats the odds and pins DDP with a flying forearm at 13:37. Bout 11: (20 min) Ric Flair vs. Hulk Hogan-- This back and forth bout couldn't be decided in the time-limit, a 20:00 draw. Bout 12: (No Time Limit) Steel Cage: Rick Rude vs. Shawn Michaels-- Rude storde into the cage first and yelled: "CUT THE MUSIC! What I'd like to have right now is for all you fat, out-of-shape, Greensboro Greaseballs to keep the noise down while I show the URWA who the Boy Toy really is..HIT THE MUSIC!" Michaels would enter with all his showmanship, and the battle was on! Rude would be busted open early, but looked like the winner after he hit HBK with the Rude Awakening. Michaels desperately pulled Rude off the cage. And went for Sweet Chin Music...fake Rude out...and kicked him right where it hurts! OUCH!! Michaels easily exited the cage for the win at 15:52. Bout 13: (2 of 3 falls, 60 minutes) World Tag Titles: Bruiser/Crusher, challengers vs. Outsiders, champions..Lawler and Hogan came out with the Outsiders at ringside.. First Fall-- At the 20 minute mark, a masked man, who didn't have Gracie's or Thesz's phyiscal build, came in and blasted Nash with a chair. A Crusher Bolo gave Lisowski the pin on Hall at 38:44. Second Fall-- Depsite a strong rally from the Bruiser/Crusher duo, the bout ended with Bruiser and Crusher a fall shy from winning the tag titles...which entitles Bruiser and Crusher to yet another rematch. Bout 14: (60 minutes) World Title: Taz, challenger vs. Vader, champion..Taz's dojo of Sabu, Meanie and Richards came out with Luger, Sting, Austin and DDP for this bout...Taz put on a tremendous effort, but Vader wins after a scoop slam and pin at 19:15.
