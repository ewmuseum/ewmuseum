URWA Arena Report, 8 November 1997

8 November 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
URWA Arena Report 11/8/97, Plaza Hotel Ballroom, Las Vegas, NV Bout 1: (30 min) 2-Cold/Henry vs. Wright/Inferno-- Henry pins Wright with a Gorilla Press at 18:10 as this tag team scores its first win. Bout 2: (20 min) Zeus vs. Wrath-- While Vandeburg had the ref distracting, Meng comes in to attack Zeus as Wrath immoblizes his arms..Meng goes for a Mafia Kick..Zeus ducks..and Meng connects on Wrath! Zeus shoves Meng aside, and pins Wrath at 11:51 with a power piledriver. Bout 3: (20 min) Blanchard vs. Douglas-- Heenan and Andre came out to assure The Franchise didn't have anything up his sleeve, and a top rope jump by Blanchard would miss, and Douglas would score a quick pin before the Heenan Family would interfere at 13:15. Bout 4: (20 min) Backlund vs. Bigelow-- Backlund was viciously attack by Owen, Dudley, Bundy, and Sid as this bout was about to start..and Backlund would get DQ'd for trying to pull on Bigelow's tites at 13:53. Bout 5: (20 min) Benoit vs. Dynamite Kid-- Benoit is DQ'd for using brass knuckles on Dynamite at 11:45. Bout 6: (20 min) Whipwreck vs. Sasuke-- this replaced a scheduled Doink/Benoit match that will be rescheduled for next week. Sasuke would score the pin at 7:25 after a spectacular Flying Head Scissors! Bout 7: (20 min) Ahmed vs. Taz-- Ahmed would score the pinfall with a flying cross body on Taz at 15:11. Bout 8: (20 min) Sabu vs. Janetty-- Sabu would be unjustly DQd at 4:24 for a gouge as Janetty gets his first singles win. Bout 9: (No Time Limit, No-DQ) Heenan vs. Kaufman-- Kaufman was no match even for the Weasel, as a suplex gives Heenan the pin at 9:49, and after the bout Heenan was hoisted onto Andre's shoulders in victory! Bout 10: (30 min) Sting/Luger vs. Dudley/Warlord-- with Owen and Sid ringside, Dudley and Warlord held Sting and Luger to a 30-minute draw. Bout 11: (30 min) Demolition vs. Outsiders-- Ax was tossed out of the ring just past 19 1/2 minutes in, and out came Mortis and Wrath to prevent Ax to get into the ring, and, as the ref signaled the count-out, Meng attacked Nash from behind going after Nash's injured knee. Security quickly broke things up, but the Outsiders get the win by count-out at 20:07. Bout 12: (45 min) Simms/Richter/Burke vs. Madusa/Nakano/Kai-- This 6-woman tag replaces a cage bout between Mortis and Kronos that will be held next week. But all 6 women fought vailantly, but the result was a 45-minute draw. Bout 13: (No Time Limit Steel Cage Match) Bundy vs. Sandman-- Bundy went for his famous Avalanche, but missed, and Sandman would escape the cage at 10:22. Bout 14: (20 min) TV Title: Hogan vs. Steamboat-- Hogan came out with Lawler, Piper, and the Outsiders..and Steamboat would come out alone despite several Titans being subdued by security as plan sneak attack was foiled. Hogan would miss his pattened legdrop, but after Nash comes up to the ring apron to nail Steamboat with a roll of quarters..and a pair of clotheslines by Hogan gives him the pinfall at 17:10. Bout 15: (No Time Limit, No DQ) International Tag Title Final: Eliminators vs. Maivia/Ladd-- while the Eliminators came out Meng snuck up and put a Tongan Death Grip on Kronus..but security came in to save Kronus from any serious damage. Meanwhile, this bout went on and on..until Ladd would scoop slam Kronus and score the pin at 43:02 as the Ladd/Maivia duo scores a International and World Tag Team double, something only the Steiners had previously done, and winning the tournament despite being the number 8 seed. Bout 16*: (45 min) Flair vs. George, International Title..Hogan, the Outsiders, Lawler, and Blassie came out with George..but the quintet got into a scrap with Heenan's family, so Georgeous George was forced to go it alone..out came Backlund again, as he tried to toss a roll of quarters to George, Flair intercepted, but was also caught red-handed for a DQ at 11:43, so George will get a title-shot... Bout 17*: (2 of 3 falls, 60 min) Rock-n-Roll Express vs. Steiners, World Tag Titles-- Dudley, Owen, and Sid when after the Rock-n-Rolls prior to the bell, but security quickly broke things up. First Fall-- Meng and Kain tried to toss the Rock-n-Rolls some numbchuks, but Scott would intercept and blast Gibson with it to score the pinfall at 30:45. Second Fall-- in nearly similar fashion as to what had occured Monday night, this time, Scott was DQ'd for using brass knuckles at 50:55, tieing the bout at one fall each. A third fall would not be reached by the end of the time limit, so this bout also ends in a 1-1 draw. The Rock-n-Rolls now get a 90-minute time limit for their next rematch. Bout 18: (60 min) Perfect vs. Vader, World Title-- Tully and Rude tried to attack Vader as he made his entrance, but Vader held his own until security arrived..about 11 minutes into the bout..Andre nailed Vader solidly in the ribs while Heenan had the referee distracted, and Vader would favor his ribs the rest of the bout, and Perfect kept hope of an upset alive, until a power bomb gives Vader the pin at 21:43.
