URWA — Livewire

URWA Livewire, 17 September 1997

17 September 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
URWA LIVEWIRE with J.J. Anthony Welcome everyone to the inaugural edition of Livewire! Lots of stuff to talk about on our very first show.. As you know, at URWA Ground Zero..our first five champions were crowned as this "league-of-dreams" kicked off. Vader proved the URWA will be "Vader Time" by turning back the challenge of Bruno Sammartino. Andre The Giant proved his superiority over early-century star Frank Gotch. And, Bret Hart proved he was the excellence of execution, giving Nikita Koloff a wrestling lesson. In the Tag Divison, the NWO was just too sweet for Rick Rude and Tully Blanchard, and Sting and Lex put away those ever-dangerous Gangstas.. But, the Rude/Blanchard duo got revenge by pushing back the challenge of the Gangstas for a World Tag Title Shot at URWA Fall Brawl at Washington, while Frank Gotch shocked the world, winning the World Title shot against Vader. Now, we look forward to tomorrow's card in Jacksonville--and what a card it will be! Stan Hansen, who fought brillantly to a 20-minute draw against Bret Hart on Manic Monday, will get a shot at immortality, taking on Vader for the World Title. Vader had this to say after Sunday's championship performance: "It's hard to be humble when your so good. I thought the URWA was where the big boys were. I guess I was wrong. VADER FEARS NO ONE." And old rivalry will be renewed for the International title when Andre The Giant faces Hulk Hogan. We got comments from both men.. Hollywood: "WELL YA KNOW SUMTHIN' ...that battle royal, dude, was just a setback..because NOW...HA HA HA...I get my hands on that BIG NASTY GIANT..and I KNOW I'm gonna take that International Title and defend it against those jabronis Gotch and Dreamer..." Andre:Hulk Hooogaaaaan,when i am through with squeezing your scrawney little neck,i will still be champ and hulkamania,will DIIIIIIIIIEEEEEEEE::all of the heenan family laughs::.. Wow...I think Andre's living in a time warp...Hulk's in the NWO now.. The other title bout should be a classic as Sting and Lex Luger want to prove their fighting champs by putting their belts up against the Steiners..this should be a great one, and I wouldn't be suprised if this goes the full 45-minute time limit. The undercard presents some extraordinary matches...how about Dory Funk, Jr. getting in the ring and having to look across..and see Terry as his adversary? Or, the legendary Lou Thesz going up against Randy Savage? And what will happen when the Sandman hooks up with Stone Cold Steve Austin? In other bouts..Tommy Dreamer locks up with Diamond Dallas Page in a battle of two fan-favorites who'll scratch and claw for a win here in the URWA. The British Bulldogs will duke it out in a tag-team collison with the Midnight Express. Rick Rude will battle another heartthrob in URWA-land..Shawn Michaels. In another battle of long-time rivals, Harley Race and Dusty Rhodes lock-up. How will Sabu's agility do against the agression of Arn Anderson? We'll find out Thursday. And, last, but certainly not least..Mr. Wonderful, Paul Orndorff goes up against Bruno Sammartino..All this on ONE card!!! Comissoner P.J. Blom and his staff of advisors have been working the phones trying to finalize matches this weekend as the URWA hits the road. Friday in Savannah, GA and Saturday in Columbia, SC leading up to Manic Monday. And believe you me, fans..I'm sure when Manic Monday reaches your homes from Charlotte Saturday, Ric Flair, the Nature Boy himself, will be warmly welcomed! And Finally, the tournament to crown the new Ladies tourny will be in high-gear as both Bull Nakano and Chynna advance to what will be an incredible bout on URWA Manic Monday in Charlotte. In other news, there have been rumblings of a non-tournament special grudge match in the ladies divison Friday between Cindi Divine and Madusa. However, Madusa has been unusually quiet this week..probably in preparations for her bout tomorrow against Lioness Azuka.. That's all for this week! Tune in next Wednesday for a complete Fall Brawl preview..
