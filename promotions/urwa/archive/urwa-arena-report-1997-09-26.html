URWA Arena Report, 26 September 1997

26 September 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
URWA Arena Report 9/26--Richmond, VA Bout 1: (45 min) Ladies Semifinal: Bull Nakano vs. June Byers-- A scoop slam gives Bull Nakano a trip to the finals by pinfall at 16:50. Bout 2: (30 min) Road Warriors vs. Headbangers-- As Ironman blared, Sting and Luger came up from the runaway and clobbered Hawk and Animal with steel chairs, adding a couple of whacks for good measure. They tossed the Warriors in to the Headbangers, and this bout was underway..and despite being weakened, the Warriors we able to come away with a time-limit draw against the Headbangers at 30:00. Bout 3: (30 min) Meanie/Richards vs. Midnight Express-- Sweet Stan would get the DQ for using Jim Cornette's tennis racket on Stevie Richards at 3:26. Bout 4: (45 min) Outsiders/Hogan vs. Bulldogs/Dory Funk Jr.-- The NWO came out in all it's glory--with Jerry Lawler as well, at ringside....in a back and forth battle, Davey Boy Smith pins Scott Hall after a suplex off the 2nd rope at 32:33...So, not only do Brusier and Crusher get title shots next week, the Bulldogs will as well. Bout 5: (20 min) Taz vs. Lawler-- Lawler bended the rule-book early and often, most notably wrapping a small chain around his fist to clobber Taz. Lawler went up to the top rope for a fistdrop, but Taz got away, and got the cover at 15:43. Bout 6: (20 min) Cactus Jack vs. Sandman-- both men pulled out all the stops, but Cactus would be DQ'd for a gouge at 12:25. Bout 7: (10 min) Arn Anderson vs. Gracie-- Arn Anderson was the first to step up to Gracie's 10-minute challenge. A cradle by Gracie out of a AA bodyslam gives Gracie the pin in 7:18. Bout 8: (20 min) Lou Thesz vs. Terry Funk-- This up and down bout ended with both men going the distance, a 20:00 draw. Bout 9: (30 min) Stan Hansen vs. Nikita Koloff, NO-DQ match-- A powerful Lariat give Hansen the pinfall over Koloff in a grueling bout, in 22:57. Bout 10: (30 min) Furnas/LaFon vs. Rhodes/Severn-- This bout would go the distance, and was a good techincal bout, 30:00 draw. Bout 11: (45 min) Orndorff/??? vs. Heenan/Rude/Andre-- Orndorff came out sans mystery partner and then introduced him...ROWDY RODDY PIPER!! Heenan started screaming.. BH: Wait just a #^*&#^%^ Minute!!!! Piper is not a wrestler here in the URWA, he can't be here!! RP: Yes I can!! There's no rule saying any wrestler of the 3300+ eligible to compete can't if he's not in the stable. Comissoner Blom confirmed the loophole.. BH: Well, let's just get his vertically-challenged fanny out here... Comissoner Blom: Ladies and Gentleman here in Richmond, Rowdy Roddy has signed a 30-day contract here in the URWA..he will be a free agent, and anyone who would like to challenge him in the next month, Piper will have no choice but to accept..Referee--- RING THAT BELL!! And just like that, the bout was on! Orndorff strategy would backfire, as Piper would be DQ'd for a thumb jab on Andre at 30:58. Bout 12: (NO Time-Limit) Sid vs. HBK, cage match-- Sycho Sid would power-bomb a busted-open HBK, and get the escape at 19:52.
