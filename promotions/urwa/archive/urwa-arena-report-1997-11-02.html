URWA Arena Report, 2 November 1997

2 November 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
URWA Arena Report 11/2 America West Arena, Phoneix, AZ Bout 1: (30 min) Sting/Luger vs. Midnight Express-- Jim Cornette came from behind on knocked Luger silly with his tennis racket, as Lane scores a quick pin at 11:22 to get the win. Bout 2: (20 min) Race vs. Ahmed Johnson-- Ahmed scores the upset as Harley is DQ for a blantant face-rake at 14:14. Bout 3: (20 min) Hogan vs. Douglas-- As Hogan came to ringside, the entire Heenan family came out to block Hollywood's path..Hulk then whisted loudly, and he was joined by the NWO...and after several tense moments, security scurried both parties away.. meanwhile, Hogan dominated, smashing Douglas with a chair, viciously busting him open, and legdrop later seals the Hogan pin at 11:25. Bout 4: (30 min) Outsiders vs. Flair/Blanchard-- Bockwinkel and Heenan brought the Horseman out, while Piper and Lawler brought out the Outsiders...this bout would end in a 30-minute draw as all 8 men would get into it after the bell, and security again cleared up the fracas.. Bout 5: (20 min) Backlund vs. Raven-- Raven got so tired of Backlund's mumbling long words that he tried to smash Bob with a chair and was DQ'd at 14:47. Bout 6: (30 min) Bruiser Brody/Studd vs. Freebirds-- this wild brawl ended when Gordy unloaded some brass knuckles on Brody, knocking him out cold for the pin at 14:14. Bout 7: (30 min) 2-Cold/Henry vs. Demolition-- Before the bout, 2-Cold was Pearl Harbored by Taz, who slipped the Kata-Hajime on him, yelling to give Sabu a Cruiserweight title shot, and security quickly got in to break things up, and also to keep a very angry Henry from going at it with Taz. Both teams battled vailiantly to a time-limit draw. Bout 8: (30 min) Rock-n-Rolls vs. Headbangers-- The Bangers have been on a tear as of late, and were able to score a 30-minute draw on the former world champions in this contest. Bout 9: (30 min, No DQ) Benoit vs. Sabu-- Benoit gets the win with a elbow drop and pinfall at 17:34. Bout 10: (20 min) Rude vs. Perfect-- Andre and The Barbarian assaulted Perfect before the bout, as the security would be called again to break up this assault...the bout would be evenly thought, as both men went to a time-limit draw. Bout 11: (20 min) Meng vs. Taz-- Taz would score the win after a series of clotheslines to get the pin at 16:11. Bout 12: (20 min) Zeus vs. Morales-- Zeus would be DQ'd for a gouge on Morales at 9:15 as the Latin star continues his winning ways.. Bout 13: (20 min) Yokosuna vs. Giant-- Use of brass knuckles by Yoko would be the difference in this bout, and as Giant was down, a Banzai drop and elbow gives Yoko the pin at 17:16. Bout 14*: (20 min) Sid vs. Doink-- Owen, Bigelow, Bundy, and Dudley came out and assulted Doink before the bell..and Sid would toy with Doink until a press slam ended matters at 18:36. Bout 15: (45 min) Madusa vs. Nakano, Ladies Title-- Late in this bout, Nakano tried to bring a toaster to use on Madusa, but Madusa qucikly kicked her and as the toaster dropped, Madusa blasted her with it! A running shoulder ram on a woozy but still standing Nakano makes Madusa the new ladies champion by pin at 19:43. Bout 16*: (No Time-Limit, No DQ) TV Title Finals: Bruno vs. Hogan-- Hogan came out for his second match of the night, and was flanked by the entire NWO...the Heenan Family came out and before you knew, a 9-on-6 brawl was on..as Andre was single-handedly taking care of 4 of the NWO-ites..and bodyslammed Bischoff on a ringside table..but Piper and Lawler kept Heenan's men from getting at Hogan. Security again was called out to send both parties' thugs to the dressing rooms..Bruno had the early edge, but Hogan poured it on, scoring the pinfall with a Big Boot at 18:41, as the Hulkster wins his first URWA title. Bout 17: (2 of 3 falls, 60 min) Steiners vs. Bagwell/Janetty, World Tag Titles-- First Fall-- Rick pins Buff after a Steiner Line at 17:27. Second Fall-- Bagwell wins this fall by DQ after Rick hits him with loaded headgear at 27:06. Third Fall-- This would go the remaining 33 minutes for a 1-1 time-limit draw, ensuring Bagwell and Janetty a future title shot. Bout 18: (60 min) Undertaker vs. Andre The Giant, World Title-- this bout was a doozy, and about 18 minutes in, the ref was knocked out. Bockwinkel blasts UT with a chair, only to see UT unfazed by Nick's assualt! Barbarian tried to launch an assualt from the top rope...UT catches him and mid-air and throws him out into the third row like a sack of potatoes..then, the Undertaker did the unthinkable..he hoisted the massive Giant up into a Tombstone...but immediately favored his back..UT ignored the pain long enough to Throat Thrust Andre while he was down, and score the pin for the World Title at 19:40, as once again, we have a NEW WORLD CHAMPION..the Undertaker, but as the celebration in Phoneix continued, collapsed! Paramedics would have to assist Undertaker leaving the arena, but he had the precious World Title on him..
