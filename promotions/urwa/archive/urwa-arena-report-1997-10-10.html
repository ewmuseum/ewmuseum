URWA Arena Report, 10 October 1997

10 October 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
URWA Arena Report 10/10 Maple Leaf Gardens, Toronto, Canada Bout 1: (30 min) Wrath/Meng vs. Bundy/Sid- The Quad Threat made their presence known at ringside. Mosh would hold Meng in place until Sid come off the top rope for a kneedrop, and a pin gives Sid and Bundy the win at 17:42. Bout 2: (30 min) Furnas/LaFon vs. Von Erichs-- These two teams would battle all the way to a 30-minute draw, with some good action.. Bout 3: (20 min) Farooq vs. Koloff, all parties cuffed to ringside--Rocky, Ahmed, and 2-Cold were cuffed to ringside on one side, while Vader and the Roadies were cuffed on the other. Farooq wins with a Dominator Slam and pin at 13:17 in a back-and forth match. Bout 4: (20 min) Hansen vs. Dreamer-- Hansen dominates, and pins Dreamer with a lariat at the 18:08 mark. Bout 5: (20 min) Benoit vs. HBK-- Benoit gave HBK all he could handle, and the bout went to a 20-minute draw. Bout 6: (No Time Limit) Richards/Meanie vs. Inferno/Wright-- Disco submits to a step-over knee break by Richards at 29:53. Disco's knee will be fine though, only a slight strain.. Bout 7: (20 min) Heenan vs. Brooklyn Brawler-- Heenan continues to have some extraordinary luck, as Brawler went for an atomic drop, something in his back gave way, and Heenan scores the three-count at 17:25. Bout 8: (20 min) Hogan vs. Piper-- out came the NWO with Hogan, and Piper knew tonight would be an upholl battle. But Piper fought hard enough and had Hollywood in some trouble at the bell, signaling a 20-minute draw. Bout 9: (20 min) Sasuke vs. Malenko, Cruiserweight Tourny-- this bout ended in a 20-minute draw, eliminating both from the Cruiserweight Tourny Bout 10: (20 min) Sabu vs. Brian Christopher, Cruiserweight Tourny-- Jerry Lawler came out to interfere on Brian's behalf, taking Sabu's feet out from under him during a suplex for a Christopher pin at 14:21. Bout 11: (30 min) Freebirds vs. Mortis/Wrath-- The Birds and the Oddities would go to a 30-minute draw. Bout 12: (30 min) Von Erichs vs. Headbangers-- Kevin intercepted a microphone and bashed Mosh with it, earning Von Erich a DQ at 12:49. Were the Bangers trying some Heavy Metal singing in the ring? Bout 13: (No Time Limit): Mortis vs. Benoit-- A Benoit clothesline gets the pin at 17:15 in the battle of wreslters in the 2nd bouts. Bout 14: (Cage Match) Sandman vs. Raven-- A busted-open Sandman would smash Raven's face into the cage and escape with the win at 19:32 in a bout the could have gone either way. Bout 15: (Cage Match) DDP vs. Koloff-- Koloff seemed pretty upset losing to Farooq earlier in the evening, busting open Page before escaping at 14:46. Bout 16: (45 min) International Tag Titles: Sting/Luger vs. Steiners-- In this rematch, it was Scott's turn to go balistic, choking Sting out and earning a DQ at 10:23. Comissoner Blom came out to promise Sting and Luger a no-DQ, no-time limit return bout for next Friday night. Bout 17: (20 min) TV Title: Giant Gonzalez vs. Sid-- The Quad Threat came out to ringside, and Meng would come out to accompany Gonzalez. Amazingly, with Douglas preventing Meng from getting involved, Sycho hoisted up Giant in a Power Bomb for the pin at 3:55..Hmm, maybe Sid does rule the World, he'll need all the help he can get against the Hitman Monday.. Bout 18: (2 of 3 falls, 60 min) World Tag Titles: Bulldogs vs. Rock-n-Roll Express-- First Fall-- Smith would get his famous Power Slam reversed into a cradle by Morton for an incredible pin at 28:31. Second Fall-- Time would run out before the Rock-n-Rolls could score a second fall, though Smith was in a bad way at the bell and was nearly pinned.
