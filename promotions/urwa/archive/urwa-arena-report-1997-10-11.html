URWA Arena Report, 11 October 1997

11 October 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
URWA Arena Report 10/11-- Corel Center-- Ottawa, Canada 1. (20 min) Taka Michinoku vs. ???? -- The mystery man scheduled to make his appearance in the 4-way dance Thursday makes his appearence in this bout.. and the mystery man behind the curtain in Ottawa tonight was no one other but former Horseman OLE ANDERSON!! But, Michinoku, a serious threat to the Cruiserweight title, pulled off the upset, cradling up Ole for the pin at 16:51, fleeing the ring before Ole could exact his revenge. 2. (20 min) Chris Benoit vs. Madusa, non-sanctioned "Battle of the Sexes"-- Benoit wanted to take it easy on the Ladies' star..but a few slaps changed the Cripper's mind and a Crippler Crossface puts Madusa away by submission at 10:23. 3. (30 min) DDP vs. T.L. Hopper-- Toilet Match-- well, this was Hopper's kind of match, and he wins in 19:41 as he stuffed Page's head in toilet water for a 5-count..TL went for his plunger, but URWA security came in to save Page from drowning.. 4. (No Time Limit) Gracie vs. Hart vs. Rude-- Bret Hart was the first out of here as Rude throws him out at 3:56. Gracie would then toss out Rude at 10:04 to take the victory. My oh my, does Gracie look pumped for his world title bout with Vader tonight.. 5. (30 min) Meng vs. Terry Funk, No-DQ Match-- Meng is on a roll here in the URWA, as a Superkick puts Terry down for the pin at 16:20. 6. (20 min) Blanchard vs. Janetty-- The NWO would come out to ringside, doing some scouting on the newcomer Janetty maybe? As Kevin Nash said to J.J. Anthony, ringside reporter, "Hey, the NWO is always looking to expand." Meanwhile, Blanchard pins Janetty with a power piledriver to get the pin at 4:28. 7. (30 min) Road Warriors vs. Freebirds-- The Roadies would dominate, but only get a 30-minute draw on the reeling Freebirds.. 8. (20 min) Orndorff vs. Undertaker-- This battle would spill outside on numerous occasions, and Orndorff matched the man from the darkside hold for hold, gaining a 20-minute draw. 9. (20 min) Mil Mascaras vs. Alex Wright, Cruiserweight 1st Round-- Mascaras would miss a plancha off the top rope, and an exhausted Wright would score the cover at 12:27. 10. (20 min) 2-Cold Scorpio vs. Jerry Lynn, Cruiserweight 1st Round-- Jerry Lynn went berzerk, getting the DQ for a choke on Scorpio at 16:18. 11. (30 min) Demolition vs. Blackjacks-- Ax would get busted open in his debut, but both teams would battle to a 30-minute draw in the debut for Demolition.. 12. (20 min) Hart vs. Meng-- Hart would pin Meng after some NWO interference from Outsider Nash at 15:13, when Nash, who came out in crutches, whacked Meng in the face with them! Could the NWO be looking for some boy toys in their stable too? 13. (45 min) Midnights/Arn Anderson vs. Rock-n-Roll Express/HBK-- There was a brief delay as URWA Security came out to escort Hogan, Lawler, and the Outsiders away.. Meanwhile, this bout would see no interference, but Eaton would be DQ'd for a thumb jab at 29:34 on HBK.. 14. (20 min) Bobby Heenan vs. Mr. Perfect-- This was almost a Perfect domination, and just for good measure, Lawler of that blasted NWO came out to bash Heenan with a chair! Heenan ended up submitting to a crucifix (he really didn't submit, he seemed to have been unconscious, and the ref ruled him unable to go on) at 8:23. 15. (30 min) Rude/Blanchard vs. Eliminators-- Rude and Blanchard seems concerned for their fallen comrade, but would go to a 30-minute draw against the rugged Eliminators. 16. (45 min) Road Warriors vs. Sting/Luger-- The long rest between appearances seemed to work for the Roadies, as a flying clothesline gives Hawk the pin on Sting at 36:39. The Road Warriors continue to look impressive in URWA land, and the fans are getting pretty upset with both title teams not granting the Roadies their just due..a title shot.. 17. (60 min) Ladies Title: Misty Blue Simms vs. Bull Nakano-- Nakano would win after a press slam and pin on Misty at 16:16. Misty Blue did give all she had here tonight, and I think a rematch may be in the cards down the road. 18. (60 min) World Title: Royce Gracie vs. Vader-- Vader did retain his title with the Vader Splash at 12:23..but fans, Gracie pulled out all the stops and was twice able to secure two-counts on the champion. Gracie would be busted open, but his show of determination tonight made an impression on the Ottawa crowd.
