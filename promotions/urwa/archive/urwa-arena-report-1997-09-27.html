URWA Arena Report, 27 September 1997

27 September 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
URWA Arena Report 9/27 Norfolk, VA Bout 1: (45 min) Ladies Title Semifinal: Emma Waldek vs. Fabulous Moolah-- Moolah went for a bodyslam, and Waldek countered with a cradle to score the pin in 13:43, with Waldek and Nakano now ready to face off in the final Monday. Bout 2: (10 min) Paul Orndorff vs. Royce Gracie-- Although Orndorff dominated most of the bout, Gracie was able to come away with the time-limit draw in 10:00. Bout 3: (30 min) Sabu vs. Arn Anderson-- This was given a special 30-minute time limit, considering their last bout was a draw. Both men gave all they had, but Arn lands the DDT to get the pin at 19:29. Bout 4: (20 min) Lou Thesz vs. Dory Funk, Jr.-- For the second night in a row, Thesz could only muster a time-limit draw at 20:00, this time with Dory Funk Jr., who was in deep trouble as the bell rang. Bout 5: (30 min) Blackjacks vs. Bruiser/Crusher-- The Brusier and the Crusher have proved their a top-flight tag team, but couldn't get a pin against the tough and rugged Blackjacks for a time-limit 30:00 draw. Bout 6: (30 min) Rock-n-Rolls vs. Richards/Meanie-- In another suprise, the heavily favored Rock-n-Roll Express were held to a time limit draw against Richards and the Blue Guy, despite a late attempt to pin Meanie by Robert Gibson in which the bell rung before the cover could be made. Bout 7: (45 min) Road Warriors vs. Sting/Luger--This special tag team attraction saw a sneak attack on Sting and Luger by 5 men...Bundy, Sid, Shane Douglas, and the Headbangers all attacked before the bell. As security helped Sting and Luger to the ring, out came the Road Warriors...A gutsy effort by Sting and Luger to stay in the ring over 37 minutes before a stunned Sting was hoisted on Hawk's shoulders for an Animal Doom smash for the pin at 37:04. Bout 8: (20 min) Sid vs. Giant Gonzales-- With three members of the Quadruple Threat and King Kong Bundy looking on, a clothesline gives Sycho the victory after a pin at 17:17. Bout 9: (20 min) Meng vs. Raven-- Late in this bout, Mengh went for a press slam, but some slightly bruised ribs cause him to give way, Raven winds up on top for the pin at 17:46. Meng's injury's will not prevent him from continuing, but it looks like those ribs will need some taping. Bout 10: (20 min) Ox Brody vs. Dreamer-- This bout saw a lot of brawling..but a swift kick to the head gives Ox Brody the upset pin at 11:19. Bout 11: (30 min) Von Erichs vs. Gangstas-- Late in this bout, a draw looked like a certainty, but Nu Jack yanked Kerry down by the hair for a DQ win for the Von Erichs at 28:59. Bout 12: (30 min) Hulk Hogan vs. Bobby Heenan/Rick Rude, handicap eliminaton match-- Hulk had 30 minutes to defeat both men, and he'd only need 20...Heenan was counted-out as, after a legdrop, Hogan thrw him into the 3rd row..Heenan couldn't get back in time and was eliminated at 17:21. Rick Rude was pinned following a clothesline at 19:51. Bout 13: (No Time Limit) Steve Austin vs. Jerry Lawler, cage match--this was a total bloodbath!! Both men were busted open after about 12 minutes, and Royce Gracie would drop by toss a chair over the cage to Steve to wack Lawler with, and Austin would escape at 17:47. Bout 14: (45 min) International Tag Titles, Furnas/LaFon, challengers vs. Steiners, champions-- as the Steiners came out, they were jumped by Hogan and the Outsiders, leaving them laying, Furnas and Lafon, came down from the ring, and tossed the Steiners in so the match could get underway. Rick would pin LaFon after a Steiner line for the win at 20:07. Bout 15: (60 min, best 2 of 3 falls, no-DQ) World Tag Titles: Mortis/Wrath, challengers vs. Outsiders, champions: First Fall--A neckbreaker gives Nash a quick pin on Mortis in just 3:52. Second Fall-- Wrath nailed Hall with brass knuckles to get the equalizing pin at 13:49. Could the Rare Oddites pull of a major upset tonight? Third Fall-- Nash pins Mortis after blasting him with the chair at 37:14 for the deciding fall
