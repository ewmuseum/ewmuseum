URWA Arena Report, 2 October 1997

2 October 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Arena Report, 10/2/97 Meadowlands, E. Rutherford, NJ Bout 1: (20 min) Dan Severn vs. Blue Meanie-- In a rare scheduling error, the BWO had to each wrestle twice tonight..Da Blue Guy suprisingly took it to Severn, but the bout ended a 20:00 draw.. Bout 2: (30 min) Rude/Blanchard vs. Freebirds-- In a battle of top tag teams, P.S. Hayes gets the DQ for a hair-pull on Tully at 27:27.. Bout 3: (20 min) Koloff vs. Sandman-- The NOD (Farooq, 2 Cold, Rocky, and Ahmed) would surrond the ring at about the 7 minute mark. As Nikita made a move to the top rope, Farooq would shove him off, and a quick Sandman cover gets the pin at 13:44. But, the Road Warriors would come out to make the save. Bout 4: (20 min) Orndorff vs. Race-- Race puts Mr. Wonderful to sleep with a Sleeper at 19:07. Bout 5: (20 min) Non-Title Bout: Bundy vs. Andre-- As Andre came out--POW! Hack Myers, Ox Brody, and Sgt. Slaughter all came out to attack the big man...and would them toss Andre in to Big Bundy.. but a woozy Andre would stay unbeaten here in the URWA as he pinned Bundy with the Biq Squash at 14:20. Meanwhile, Slaughter's gang of thugs would go at it again, attacking Heenan at ringside, and Slaughter put a cobra clutch on him until Andre made the save. Bout 6: (30 min) Midnight Express vs. Hopper/Brawler-- Amazingly, the Brooklyn Patrol would pull out all the stops, attempting to using chairs several times in the bout...but couldn't get any better than a 30-minute draw on the Express.. Bout 7: (30 min) Gangstas vs. 2 Cold/Rocky Maivia-- Another very even tag bout would go the distance for a 30-minute stalemate. Bout 8: (20 min) Flair vs. Anderson-- Flair wasted no time disposing of Double A..pinning him after a chair-smash in only 4:01. Bout 9: (30 min) Meanie/Richards vs. Mahoney/Myers-- In Meanie's second bout of the night, he'd be the decidng factor, as Meanie missed a top rope jump, and Mahoney gets the win by pin at 16:33 Bout 10: (No Time Limit) Road Warriors vs. Headbangers-- NOD interference would back fire as Trasher was accidentally chaired by 2-Cold, and a Neckbreaker by Hawk pins Mosh at 17:11. Bout 11: (No Time Limit, I Quit Match) Taz vs. Meng-- In an absoultely incredible bout, Meng would win after applying a Nerve Hold on Taz in an amazing 1:03:15. Taz would be severely busted open in this bout, and Meng would suffer a minor leg injury..This bout had chairs, forks, gouges, bites, looked like a Mike Tyson buffet! Taz got his Tazmission on several times in the first 30 minutes, but Meng wouldn't tap out!! Bout 12: (Cage Match) Benoit vs. Richards-- This was almost all Benoit as Richards, fatigued from his earlier bout, was busted open badly, and Benoit just made Big Stevie Cool suffer until he finally escaped at 16:13. Bout 13: (30 min, No DQ) Mr. T vs. Hogan-- Hogan dominates this bout, pinning Mr. T after a piledriver at 5:54. Bout 14: (30 min) TV Title: Gracie vs. Sid-- Gracie tried his UFC-Style attack on the big man, but Sid was too big and too strong tonight as the Power Bomb and pin at 12:13 gives him the TV Title! Bout 15: (2 of 3 falls, No-DQ) World Tag Title: Outsiders vs. Bruiser/Crusher-- First Fall-- Lisowski would be counted-out in the opening fall at 23:31 after Hall would toss him out of the ring. Second Fall-- Lisowski would come right back and get a legdrop to pin Hall at 28:33 to even the match. Third Fall-- A chair-smash puts Dick The Bruiser as the Outsiders finally turn back their challenge with the pin in this fall at 38:18 and the match, 2 falls to 1.
