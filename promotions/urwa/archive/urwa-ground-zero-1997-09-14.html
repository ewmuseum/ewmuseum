URWA Ground Zero

14 September 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
URWA Ground Zero--9/14/97 Ice Palace--Tampa, FL On to Ground Zero..where tonight, the URWA's first champions will be crowned..but before that, not just one battle royal, or two, or three, but four! Bout 1: Singles I (13)--Hogan, Sammartino, Koloff, Catcus Jack, D. Funk Jr., A. Arnderson, Raven, Benoit, Austin, Taz, Andre, Shawn Michaels, Thesz-- Chris Benoit was the first eliminated at :46 by the Raven. Bruno then tossed out Arn Anderson at 4:02. Nikita Koloff gets the field down to 10 at 8:11 by tossing out Taz. Then it would be Austin's turn to be tossed, thanks to Bruno at 10:16. Lou Thesz got a huge roar for tossing out Shawn Michaels at 13:32. Dory Funk Jr. tossed out Cactus Jack at 16:24 to get the field down to 7, and Bruno would toss out Raven at 17:40 to make it a 6-man field. Another huge roar went out as Hulk Hogan made a suprise early exit at 27:19 from Dory Funk Jr. Bruno then eliminated Thesz at 28:34, and Dory at 29:25. A massive Headbutt by Andre on Nikita Koloff eliminates him at 30:41, but the Russian earns the TV title shot. It all comes down to Andre and Bruno, and Andre had been ganged up numerous times thru out the bout. It looked like Andre was going to win, but Bruno got a sudden burst of power and hoisted the big man over at 37:38 to win the World Title shot! Andre will have to settle at a bout for a shot at the International Title. Bout 2: Tags I (7 teams)-- Outsiders, Road Warriors, Eliminators, Bulldogs, Sting/Luger, Richards/Meanie, Rock-n-Rolls-- Hawk was the first one eliminated, by Sting at 1:14..and Animal followed right behind, eliminated by Luger at 4:31. Kronus would be tossed by Smith to get the field down to 11 at 7:23. Robert Gibson soon follow being tossed out at 8:38. Over 20 minutes later, Stevie Richards would be next out 30:18. Dynamite Kid would then be eliminated by Sting at 36:04. Nash then tossed out Luger at 37:19. Just 23 seconds later Saturn would exit via Hall at 37:42. Then it was Hall's turn out at 38:55, thanks to Davey Boy Smith. And then Stinger would eliminate Davey Boy at 40:33. With 4 teams still in the running (Sting/Luger, Meanie/Richards, Rock-n-Rolls, Outsiders) the Meanie would be thrown out 46:19. Ricky Morton ended any chance of a Rock-n-Rolls title shot as Nash throws him out at 52:01. And Sting would be eliminated by Nash at 56:05 to put the Outsiders in to a World Tag Title shot later in this card, with Sting and Luger to go for the International Tag Titles. Bout 3: Singles II (14)-- Savage, Race, Vader, Terry Funk, Flair, Hansen, Dreamer, Sandman, Rhodes, DDP, Sabu, Heenan, Bret Hart, F.Gotch-- 16 minutes would pass before Bobby Heenan would be the first man out at 16:07 by Stan Hansen. Hansen struck again as, Dusty Rhodes was eliminated at 19:43. Vader would score an elimination on Sabu at 24:39. DDP would get rid of Stan Hanson at 27:09. With the field down to 10, Bret Hart would eliminate Funk at 31:38, and Randy Savage then eliminated Dreamer at 32:52, and then got DDP eliminated at 36:10. Ric Flair then tossed out his long-time foe Harley Race at 36:59. Bret Hart then took care of Savage at 38:14. Then, Vader would toss out Flair at 39:26. With 4 men left, Sandman would just miss title contention by being tossed out by Vader at 42:16. Vader then took care of Bret Hart at 48:47, giving Hart a TV title shot at Nikita Koloff. Could early-century star Frank Gotch pull of the upset on Vader? No...as Vader tossed out Gotch at 52:05 to earn the World Title shot against Sammartino, and Frank Gotch will have a tough task in trying to beat Andre The Giant for the International title.. Bout 4: Tags II (7 teams)-- Freebirds, Midnight Express, Von Erichs, Gangstas, Steiners, Rude/Blanchard, Bruiser/Crusher-- trouble began early for Pauly Ace's team, as Crusher accidently tossed out Dick The Brusher at 4:35. Scott tossed Kerry Von Erich out at 4:58, but Kevin would toss out Tully Blanchard at 7:49. Hayes had Crusher Lisowski tied up, and Gordy accidently clotheslines Hayes out at 9:48. Crusher would then toss Rick Steiner out at 11:26. More freakiness would occur, as Nu Jack accidentally tossed out fellow Gangsta Mustafa Said at 21:16. Nu Jack then took care of Bobby Eaton at 24:57, and then would eliminate Scott Steiner at 29:03. Crusher would eliminate Stan Lane to make this a 5-man field at 30:16. Rick Rude would then eliminate Gordy at 33:07. Nu Jack then took care of Kevin Von Erich at 36:29. Rude would then within 30 seconds would toss out Lisowski (38:28) and Nu Jack (38:51) to claim the world tag title shot. The Gangstas would gain the International Tag Title shot shortly. Bout 5: TV Title (1 fall, NO DQ, No Time Limit): Nikita Koloff vs. Bret Hart -- The Excellence of Execution claims the first belt in URWA history, as he dominated Koloff, pinning the Russian after a flying clothesline at 7:21. Bout 6: International Tag Title (1 fall, NO DQ, No Time Limit) Sting/Luger vs. Gangstas-- This bout would be a lot more competitive than the first title bout, with Luger pinning Mustafa after two forearm smashes at 29:22. Bout 7: International Title (1 fall, NO DQ, No Time Limit) Andre The Giant vs. Frank Gotch-- Gotch tried his technical expertise on the big man, with no success...Andre's famous suplex puts Gotch down for a three count at 13:18. Giving the Heenan family it's first championship here in the URWA..an omen for things to come? Bout 8: World Tag Title (2 of 3 falls, NO DQ, No Time Limit) Outsiders vs. Rude/Blanchard-- Rude and Blanchard came out first and Rude grabbed the mic to yell out "CUT THE MUSIC! What I'd like to have right now is for all you fat, out of shape, Tampa Bay trailer trash... keep the noise down while me and Tully prepare to give the Heenan family some more gold-- HIT THE MUSIC!" The Outsiders would come out to more boos, and the crowd had no clue who to root for here.. First Fall-- This fall went back and forth, and Hall would try a top rope jump, but Rude evades, and gets a quick 1-2-3 at 30:03. Second Fall-- The Outsiders wasted no time in drawing even, as Nash nailed Rude with a chair to set up the pin at 37:52. Third Fall-- This fall would go back and forth with several near falls in the 27 minutes this fall went, Rude went for a body press, but Hall got his knees up, and Hall pinned Rude at 1:05:06 to give his team the 2 falls to 1 win... Bout 9: World Title: (1 fall, NO DQ, No Time Limit) Bruno Sammartino vs. Vader-- What a bout to decide the URWA's first world champion..Vader dominated this championship match up, but Sammartino tried to rally late, but a missed dropkick by Bruno set up a Vader Bomb for the pin at 17:43, making Vader the first world champion. Congrats to Batt 27, JJ 29, TexRang 193, Gurgle, and myself-- you own URWA's first champions!
