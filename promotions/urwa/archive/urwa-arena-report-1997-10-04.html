URWA Arena Report, 4 October 1997

4 October 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
URWA Arena Report 10/4, Providence Civic Center, Providence, RI Bout 1: (20 min) Sandman vs. Meng-- In the opening bout, a piledriver for Meng gives him the win over Sandman at 7:52 via pinfall Bout 2: (30 min) Headbangers vs. Eliminators-- Mosh gets "totally eliminated" and Kronus, with the cover, puts the Eliminators back on track with a pin at 24:31. The Headbangers have had a tough go of it in the URWA.. Bout 3: (30 min) Andre/Heenan vs. Piper-- Roddy Piper walked into some major troule tonight, getting busted open..but got the moral victory for yanking Andre's hair at 17:08, for a DQ.. Bout 4: (30 min) Brusier/Crusher vs. Hopper/Brawler-- A kneedrop off the ropes giving Bruiser the pin on Brooklyn Brawler at 12:26. Brawler did manage to bust Crusher open in the bout, however, as Brawler was passed some brass knuckles by the massive King Kong Bundy..making the crowd wonder how Crusher would do against Double A later on.. Bout 5: (30 min) Midnight Express vs. 2-Cold/Rocky Maivia-- The Road Warriors was present at ringisde, and when Stan Lane was DQ'd for a hair pull on 2-Cold at 6:21, the Roadies beat the stuffing out of the NOD's top tag team, while the Midnights left in disgust.. Bout 6: (20 min) Wrath vs. Hansen-- This bout was marred by a freak accident. Both Hansen and Wrath's heads came together...and while both were knocked out Hansen scored the pin at 5:50, as he had landed on top of Wrath.. Bout 7: (20 min) Thesz vs. Raven-- A small package gives Thesz the win at 15:13 over the tough opposition of Raven. Bout 8: (60 min) Vader/Koloff/Road Warriors vs. 2-Cold/Rocky/Farooq/Ahmed-- After some furious tag action, Vader would Power Bomb 2-Cold to get the pin at 27:05. Bout 9: (No Time Limit) No-DQ, Falls Count Anywhere: Sgt. Slaughter vs. Mr. T-- This was no the wild brawl everyone expected as Slaughter lands a 2nd rope-clothesline for the pin on T at 8:59. Bout 10: (20 min) Crusher Lisowski vs. Arn Anderson-- Anderson showed a patient but yet aggressive attack worked out, as Lisowski was DQ for a hair-pull at 18:53. Bout 11: (20 min) B. Hart vs. T. Funk-- Funk took the easy way out, being DQ'd for a gouge at 18:04. Bout 12: (20 min) Dy. Rhodes vs. Steve Austin-- A very painful Hammerlock kept Austin's shoulders down for the three-count as The Dream moves up in the URWA Ladder with the pin at 15:35. Bout 13: (45 min) International Tag Titles: Steiners vs. Sting/Luger-- Rick Steiner got so steamed at Sting that he took off his amateur-style ear protectors and started bashing Sting with them, earning Sting and Luger the DQ at 9:54, plus a return title shot next week.. Bout 14: (2 of 3 falls, 60 min) World Tag Titles: Bulldogs vs. Rude/Blanchard-- First Fall: Rude's pinned after a flying tackle at 33:45.. Second Fall: Time would expire before another fall would be scored, so even though the Bulldogs retain the tag titles, Rude and Blanchard gain yet another shot.. Bout 15: (60 min) World Title: Vader vs. Balls Mahoney-- despite some inference from Ahmed Johnson, Vader made quick work of Balls, scoring the pin after a power bomb in just 8:47.
