URWA Manic Monday, 13 October 1997

13 October 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
URWA Manic Monday 10/13 Joe Louis Arena, Detroit, Mich Bout 1: (20 min) Candi Divine vs. Madusa-- A chop right to the temple gives Madusa the pinfall at 16:19 in this Ladies Grudge Match. Could Madusa gain a Ladies Title Shot against Bull Nakano sometime soon? Bout 2: (20 min) Ox Brody vs. Royce Gracie-- Gracie dominated, but the action spilled out to the floor where Brody took the ringside bell and bashed Gracie with it for a count-out upset victory for Brody at 6:18. Bout 3: (30 min) Outsiders vs. Road Warriors-- With the NWO not present at ringside for this bout, The Road Warrior dominated with Hawk landing 4 neckbreakers in a 5 minute span to get the pinfall at 23:07. Meng then came in to put his Nerve Hold on Nash for a few moments for good measure. Bout 4: (20 min) Shawn Michaels vs. Dean Malenko-- The NWO, except for Nash and Bret, came out for this bout, and HBK seemed more confident then ever, getting a pin after an amazing flyind head scissor-roll up combo at 8:23. Bout 5: (20 min) TV Title: Bret Hart vs. Sid-- All of the NWO would come out to lead Bret into the ring, and even before Sid could get to the ring, the Rare Oddities would attack...Gonzales went after Sid and would viciously pummel him, while Bundy was triple-teamed by Mortis, Wrath, and Meng. Despite the appearence of Andre The Giant, who was held off by all 7 NWO co-members. Meanwhile, back inside the ring, as the time limit winds down, Hart gets a quick snap suplex on Sid..and a three-count gives Bret Hart the TV title a second time, the first URWA member to hold a second title, at 17:40. Bout 6: (45 min) International Tag Title: Steiners vs. Bruiser/Crusher-- Scott would be DQ'd for a bite on Crusher at 18:41 as the Steiners continue their viciousness in an effort to retain their title, Meanwhile, Gracie came out and started to attack Dick The Bruiser! WHATS GOING ON?? While Bruiser had Gracie in a chokehold, out came Hogan to drill Bruiser with a chair, busting him open. The rest of the NWO and Gracie would gang up on Crusher, until the Heenan Family and Ric Flair came out to send the NWO scurrying for cover. Meanwhile, the NWO stormed the URWA broadcast booth, saying the remaining member of the Throwbacks, Lou Thesz, according to Hogan, "has been bought and paid for his slot in the lineup, brother! The NWO is gonna be looking for same new blood, and Gracie will be a perfect fit!" We'll have to see who the NWO hires in the coming days..Bruiser and Crusher, it appears, have been fired, but they go get a return bout for the International Tag Titles in their future as free agents.
