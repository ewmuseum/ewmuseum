URWA Ringside Report, 16 October 1997

16 October 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
URWA Ringside Report with J.J. Anthony Hello fans, and welcome to the Ringside Report for October 16th.. --There is celebration in the NWO camp tonight as the Rock-n-Roll Express easliy defeated the British Bulldogs in two straight falls, in less than 20 minutes. But, by inheriting the World Tag Titles, the Bulldogs may have done themselves a favor, as the Road Warriors will be the next tag team to get a title defense. How good have the Road Warriors been? Standings wise, the Roadies as a tag team have racked up 43 points, against 29 for the Rock-n-Rolls, and they gained 16 of those tonight, getting 6 points for the 2-fall sweep and a 10 point bonus for winning the tag belts. So, it wouldn't be impossible for the Rock-n-Rolls to break Royce Gracie's record for the shortest championship reign in URWA history, currently at 3 days. -- Vader and Andre The Giant continue to dominate the singles divison here in the URWA, and many wonder how long can we go on before we see the first ever title vs. title match for Vader's World Crown. Could it be at Bad Dreams, or will we have to wait until "When Champions Collide" in December to see these two dominate forces lock up? Vader gets a stiff challenge tomorrow from Shane Douglas, and where Shane goes, the Quadruple Threat seems to follow. -- And speaking of the Quadruple Threat, how long wil Sycho Sid and Shane go before they jettison the Headbangers? Mosh and Trasher have been stinking up the joint, already at the tag-divison low -17 points, as the second-worst tag team right now, Furnas and LaFon and the Brooklyn Patrol are nine points better at -8 points, but these teams have only had two bouts go to a decision. -- Many are appalled at the choice of the Nation of Domination to let comedian Andy Kaufmann of "Taxi" fame join this group of athletes. And even though Comissoner P.J. Blom has stated that Kaufmann's so-called "Intergender Title" will not be recognized by the URWA as a sanctioned championship, it hasn't stopped Candi Divine from taking on Kaufmann. Rumor has it Bull Nakano, the reigning Ladies champion, is so livid that she has appealed to the Commish thru an interpreter to have herself battle Kaufmann on the Manic Monday on the 20th from Freedom Hall in Louisville. -- And, congratulations goes to Denver, Colorado for being named the site of the "World Series of Survival" Thanksgiving Eve at McNichols Arena, home of the Denver Nuggets of the NBA, and the 1996 NHL champs, the Colorado Avalanche. This card will feature eight 10-man eliminaton bouts, with all survivors meeting in a battle royal. The winner then gets a title shot against the World Champion that night. This has been the Ringside Report, and we will see you ringside.
