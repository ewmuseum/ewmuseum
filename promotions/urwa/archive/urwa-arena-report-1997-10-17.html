URWA Arena Report, 17 October 1997

17 October 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Note: Sorry if you see another Arena Report later on dated 10/17, the one later down in the list is actually 10/16 URWA Arena Report--10/17 Gund Arena-- Cleveland, Ohio Bout 1: (20 min) Arn Anderson vs. Paul Orndorff-- Double A and Orndorff went right at it, and Anderson would be called for a DQ for a choke in only 1:58. Bout 2: (30 min) Meng vs. Cactus Jack-- No DQ-- Meng had been on a tear as of late, but this much was right up Cactus's alley, with Catcus hitting Meng with a garbage can to get the pin at 13:21. Bout 3: (30 min) Road Warriors vs. Gangstas-- Hawk would pin Nu Jack after a Flying Hawk leaping splash in the turnbuckle at 23:25. Many think the Roadies will take the World Tag Belts in Cincinnati tomorrow.. Bout 4: (30 min) Bundy/Sid vs. Midnight Express-- Jim Cornette would be a role in this bout as his infamous tennis racket would help Eaton knock Bundy out cold, as the Midnight Express get another pin at 24:06. Bout 5: (20 min) Chris Benoit vs. Bob Backlund-- As Benoit made his way to the ring, Sting and Luger would come out and assault Benoit with a chair? What in the world??--I didn't know Sting and Luger had a squabble with Benoit...DDP and Austin held security off for a while...as Benoit was pretty badly roughed up, but would shake off the cobwebs after Mr. Backlund dominated the first 5 minutes, and after an even battle to kick Backlund in the groin for the pin at 16:09. Benoit gets Taka Michinoku in a crucial Crusierweight Semifinal Monday. Bout 6: (20 min) Royce Gracie vs. Steve Williams-- The NWO would come out for the bout, flanked by Hogan, Bret Hart, and HBK..Williams would toss Gracie outside of the ring, and then before the NWO entourage could get to Dr. Death, Death picked up Gracie and sent him sailing into the crowd! Fans scurried out of the way, but a staggering Gracie couldn't make it back in to the ring, as Williams wins his URWA debut against stiff opposition at 10:51 by count-out. Bout 7: (20 min) Kain vs. Zeus-- Kain showed tremendous power Chokeslamming Zeus, and then tossing him out of the ring for a count-out at 6:56. Bout 8: (20 min) Mark Henry vs. Rocky Maivia-- Maivia grabbed Henry down by the tites, and the ref caught it for a Henry win by DQ at 3:58..Man, these refs mean buisness. Bout 9: (30 min) Cruiserweight Title: Mikey Whipwreck vs. Brian Christopher-- Jerry Lawler would come out to throw his son his crown, but the ref would see it, and Whipwreck makes the semifinals Monday against the Alex Wright/2-Cold winner via DQ at 11:54 Bout 10: (30 min) Ahmed vs. Farooq, No DQ, No Countout Match-- Ahmed would score the pin on Farooq after his famous Pearl River Plunge at 13:56. Bout 11: (20 min) Undertaker vs. Giant Gonzales-- the crowd pulled for the Undertaker, despite Gonzalez dominating most of the way. The Undertaker then showed tremendoous strength, pinning the Giant with a slingshot manuever for the pin at 14:44. Bout 12: (20 min) Big John Studd vs. Big Dick Dudley-- Dudley missed with a body press, giving Studd the pin on a cradle at 9:21 in the battle of the big boys.. Bout 13: (2 of 3 falls, 60 min) Hulk Hogan vs. Antonio Inoki-- Outsider Hall, Piper, Georgeous Geroge, and Bischoff all came down to ringside.. First Fall-- Hogan, knowing his stamina may be suspect later, went for a quick fall with the use of brass knuckles Hall threw in to the big man, but the ref would DQ Hogan and keep him from using them on Inoki at 1:02. Second Fall-- Hogan, now trailing 1 fall to 0, still went all out, risking a DQ on several instances, but would dominate, scoring the legdrop for the pin at 19:12. All even now at 1 fall apiece.. Third Fall-- Hogan's size and brawling ability was too much for Inoki tonight, as two legdrops at 35:33 gives Hogan the pin and the 2 falls to 1 match victory. Bout 14: (30 min) Outsider Nash vs.Tully Blanchard (if Nash wins, Nash vs. Andre tomorrow, if Tully wins, Bischoff vs. Heenan and Bischoff vs. Andre in two 10-minute unsanctioned bouts) even before Nash got to the ring, Meng came out an attacked Nash quickly before URWA security could arrive, Hall would come out and offer some "security" for his buddy Nash. And after Tully came out, Eric came out flanked by 6 of Cleveland's finest to prevent his interference in this bout...and after 13 minutes of close action, Rick Rude came out to throw Tully a chair...but, the ref would call for the bell, as the DQ rule was not waived in this bout..and Kevin Nash gets Andre The Giant tomorrow night, with perhaps the International Title on the line thanks to a Blanchard DQ at 13:42. Bout 15: (No Time Limit) Ultimate Warrior vs. Mortis, cage match-- The Warrior came jogging down to ringside only to run-in to a chair from Lex Luger..he and Sting would work the Warrior over...What is this, open season on the URWA tonight from this foursome of Sting, Luger, Austin, and DDP?? Austin and DDP again held security off as long as they could...Mortis would then feel the wrath of the Warrior...as Mortis would get seriously busted open, as the spectators could see blood running down underneath his green mask. The Warrior wins his URWA debut by escaping the cage at 18:42..it looks like Mortis will need stitches from a nasty gash, and would get some immediate medical attention.. Bout 16: (No DQ, No Time Limit) Sting/Luger vs. Steiners, International Tag Titles... The Steiners would clean house, as they would turn back interference attacks from DDP, Austin, and Wrath to get a concusive pin on the Sting/Luger duo, as Rick pins Sting after a wicked Steiner Line at 11:52. Bout 17: (45 min) Jerry Lawler vs. Andre The Giant, International Title-- Outsider Nash and Hulk Hogan came out as the NWO continues to come out in bunches..Heenan would bring Andre to the ring. Lawler would use his agility to keep Andre off balance, as the crowd was amazed to see Andre reeling..but a quick thumb jab gets Andre the DQ at the 5:22 mark. Lawler knew he'd get another shot at Andre next week..and Nash, Hogan, and the King were jubliant...after a quick consultation between the NWO members, Heenan, walking behind Andre, was yanked by the hair by Kevin Nash and brought to the ring. Despite the Gund Arena crowd throwing debris into the ring, Nash put Heenan in a Nash Knife..with Hogan helping out to "spike" Heenan..OUCH!..Heenan was down, and would not get up, and the Heenan Family came down to ringside in utter despair! The early prognosis is that Heenan had suffered some minor neck damage, and will be out of wrestling action for 3 weeks, but will still be able to manage..who would replace Heenan as an active member of the Heenan Family? Bout 18: (60 min) Vader vs. Shane Douglas, World Title-- with Douglas already in the ring, and with Vader approaching, Owen Hart and Zeus attacked the champ before the much called-upon URWA security could make a save..but all the interference would do is make the World Champ mad!! Vader would dominate the Franchise and would destroy those in the Quad Threat who tried to interfere..with Sid, Van Damn, and Candido all getting clobbered at one point or another...then, a Vader Splash off the top rope would pin Shane at 14:45, as Vader retains his world belt...as Vader left the ring, Andre The Giant blocked the runaway..MERCY! We could see the much-talked about battle of champions right here!! But Andre would say some words to Vader before letting the World Champ go peacefully..
