URWA Arena Report, 9 October 1997

9 October 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
URWA Arena Report 10/9/97-- Montreal, Canada-- Molson Center Bout 1: (20 min) DDP vs. HBK-- A very close opening bout ends when Page, reeling, regains his composure long enough to put on a Diamond Cutter for a pin at 18:06 Bout 2: (20 min) Ric Flair vs. Bret Hart-- Both sides played it close to the vest, and the bout went to a 20 minute draw. Bout 3: (20 min) Dan Severn vs. The Giant-- The Giant goes wild, and was DQ'd for smashing a chair on Severn in only 3:01. Bout 4: (20 min) Sammartino vs. Dory Funk Jr.-- Bruno goes to work on Dory's ribs, and makes the Amarillo legend submit at 11:10 after a series of bearhugs. Many think Bruno deserves some title shots despite some key losses, and many think the current champs may be afraid to put their gold against the Pittsburgh superstar. Bout 5: (No Time Limit) Brooklyn Patrol vs. Midnight Express, no time limit, no-DQ, no countout-- Despite Lane being busted open earlier in the bout, but a foreign object puts Brawler out for the count for a Lane pinfall at 29:38. The Patrol brought bats to the ring, but URWA security intercepted them at ringside. Bout 6: (20 min) Dean Malenko vs. Farooq-- The NOD came down to keep trouble from occuring, and didn't interfere, and probably wish it did. Malenko scores the pin at 16:26 with a catapult maneuver..the Iceman looks primed for the Cruiserweight tournament. Bout 7: (30 min) Meanie/Richards vs. Rock-n-Roll Express-- The double drop kick on Meanie gives Morton the pin while Gibson keeps Richards at bay at 10:19. The Rock-n-Rolls may be another team to watch in the title picture. Bout 8: (20 min) King Kong Bundy vs. Hulk Hogan-- Hulk kept Bundy guessing all day, and Bundy gets a DQ attempting to use a foreign object at 9:19. Bout 9: (20 min) Crusierweight 1st Round: Royce Gracie vs. Rey Misterio Jr.-- In a great scientific bout, Gracie gets the submission after a Ju-Jitsu Choke at 17:03. Bout 10: (20 min) Cruiserweight 1st Round: Scott Putski vs. Mikey Whipwreck-- In a very unsportsmanlike move, Mikey takes out brass knuckles to get the pin at 16:23. Bout 11: (No Time Limit) Road Warriors vs. 2-Cold Scorpio/Rocky Maivia, No-DQ Streetfight-- As this bout went all over the Molson Centre, out come Rocky and Farooq to interfere, and as Maivia held Animal in place Farooq went to club him with a 2x4, Aminal slipped out and Farooq nailed Rocky..meanwhile Hawk puts a neckbreaker on Scorpio for the pin at 31:34. Bout 12: (20 min) Mr. T vs. Paul Orndorff-- Orndorff is your winner after T gets DQ'd by a choke at 9:18. Hey, the URWA refs mean business! Bout 13: (30 min) Outsiders vs. Blackjacks-- A top rope jump from Nash on Mulligan secures the pin at 24:14. Could the Outsiders be back on the title trail? Bout 14: (30 min) Cactus Jack vs. Sandman, No DQ-- Sandman drapes the Singapore Cane across Cactus throat and scores the pin after a hard fought bout ending at 21:42. Bout 15: (60 min) Sting/Austin/Luger/DDP vs. Raven/Dreamer/Von Erichs-- Austin gets a hold of some powder and hurls it into Dreamer's eyes for a DQ at 19:02. Bout 16: (No DQ, No Time Limit) Rare Oddities (Gonzales/Mortis/Wrath/Meng) vs. Quad Threat (Sid/Douglas/Headbangers)--King Kong Bundy was also present at ringside for the Quad Threat.. Sid would throw Mortis out of the ring, and Bundy would land a couple of pancake splashes on Mortis to insure the count-out at 41:22. Bout 17: (2 of 3 falls, 90 min, No DQ) World Tag Team Title: Rude/Blanchard vs. Bulldogs-- First Fall-- This would be a wild opening fall, with both Dynamite Kid and Blanchard getting busted open, as a face slam to the canvas by Dynamite on Blanchard scores the fall at 36:09. Second Fall-- Davey Boy would intercept a roll of quarters Heenan would try to toss Tully, nailed Tully with them for a pin at 1:15:07 for a two-fall sweep. Bout 18: (60 min) World Title: Vader vs. Flair-- An even bout was ruined when Flair hits Vader with a chair, earning him a DQ at 16:20, despite some interfernce on Flair's behalf from the NOD.
