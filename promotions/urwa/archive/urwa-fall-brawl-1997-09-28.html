URWA Fall Brawl

28 September 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
URWA Fall Brawl 9/28/97-- Capital Center, Landover, MD Bout 1: No-DQ, No Time-Limit: Road Warriors vs. Blackjacks-- Despite an early run-in on the Road Warriors by DDP, Austin, Luger, and Sting....Hawk pulled off a flying clothesline, sending Mulligan to the floor, dazed, and out for a 10-Count at 20:45. Bout 2: (20 min) Sean Michaels vs. Shane Douglas--Douglas would be on his way to the runway, when Bloody Ox Brody would attack him with the chain..Sgt. Slaughter came out to fend of Brody and lead HBK to ringside. These guys would go all the way to a 20:00 draw, and Sid and the Headbangers would attack HBK after the bout..Slaughter, who took his time in trying to fend off the Quad, did...only to put a Cobra Clutch on an unconscious HBK!! Bout 3: (20 min) Ric Flair vs. Bobby Heenan-- Rude and Blanchard accompianied Heenan to the ring..A powerful Flair Chop gives Flair the easy pin at 6:52. But here comes DDP, Austin, Sting, and Luger to work over Flair...Lex racked Flair while the others worked him over, although Flair would be OK.. Bout 4: (45 min) Rock-n-Rolls vs. Sting/Luger--After nearly 40 minutes of action, out came DDP and Austin, yet again, to distact Ricky Morton. A piledriver later gives Sting the pin on Morton at 40:15. Bout 5: (30 min) Sycho Sid vs. Hulk Hogan-- The Quad Threat came out to give Sid moral support, and this bout was all Sycho Sid. Hogan, to avoid being pinned, DQ'd himself with a gouge at 18:15. DDP, Austin, Luger, and Sting, would came out..Hogan in disgust would leave..and a 8-man brawl was on..The Quad Threat kept Sid from being hurt until security could arrive.. Bout 6: (30 min) TV Title: Arn Anderson vs. Bret Hart-- Arn Anderson was still warming up, when Bret snuck up from behind to Pearl Harbor AA...A gut-wrench a short while later gives Bret the second-quickest pin ever in URWA history, :31, and the quickest title bout ever.. Bout 7: (45 min) International Tag Titles: Steiners vs. Freebirds-- Gordy would lose it, and smash Scott with a chair for a DQ at 36:04. Freebirds blow a big chance at some gold tonight... Bout 8: (45 min) International Title: Steve Austin vs. Andre The Giant-- Despite run-ins by Page, Luger, and Sting..which Andre single-handedly fended off...Andre wins after a suplex at 19:49..but as Andre left, the Giant came up from behind and POW!--nailed Andre with a chair..Andre suffered a deep gash on his forehead that will need to be stitched up.. Bout 9: (2-3 falls, 60 min) World Tag Title: Outsiders vs. Rude/Blanchard-- Rude told the sell-out crowd that he wouldn't do his usual talking of trash tonight, and the crowd roared it's approval..the NWO brought Hogan and Jerry Lawler to ringside..Heenan came out to try and get the Family some more gold.. First Fall-- Blanchard and Rude blow a great shot at a fall when Blanchard is DQ for a hair pull on Hall at 19:21. Second Fall-- The Outsiders broke all the rules in this fall, using brass knuckles, using Lawler's crown on Rude..but as the battle went outside of the ring, Blanchard bodyslammed him on a table! Hall would be counted-out at 44:21. Third Fall-- Hall would then be DQ'd for using Hollywood's bandana on Rude at 46:50..And once again, the Outsiders lose a match, but not the title..On the other hand, Blanchard and Rude do get a rematch on Saturday. Bout 10: (60 min) World Title: Sammartino vs. Vader..despite suffering some bruised ribs..Vader would get the pin after a Vader Pummel at 22:53...
