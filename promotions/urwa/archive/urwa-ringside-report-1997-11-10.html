URWA Ringside Report, 10 November 1997

10 November 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
URWA Ringside Report with J.J. Anthony Tonight in Reno, Nevada..once again the World Title changed hands as Hulk Hogan moves up from TV Champion to World Champion in a memorable 35-minute classic that could have gone either way. Meanwhile, I hear it on good authority, now that stables can hire and fire this week, that we will be seeing some new faces, including, from what I have heard, an up and comer from one of today's big-3.. Match Update on World Series of Survival: 1.NWO (Piper/Outsiders/George/Gracie) vs. Heenan Family (Andre/Rude/Barbarian/2 unnamed opponents) 2. Malenko/Misterio/Douglas/Candido/Van Damn vs. Technical Trauma (Road Warriors/Bulldogs/Inoki) 3. Titans of Hell (Zeus/Interrogator/Earthquake/Sid/???) vs. Boy Toys (HBK/Rock-n-Rolls/Steamboat/???) And now a Comissoner's team of Blom, Sheik, and 3 Mystery Men challenge Kaufman and any 4 mystery men he can think of... Still room for 4 matches at WSOS Thanksgiving Eve...there will also be 10-midget eliminaton match and a 10-women eliminaton match added on top of a the 8 matches featuring 80 URWA superstars!!! Remember, the survivors move on to a battle royal, and the winner meets the World Champion that night! Talk to you this Thursday at Sacramento for the first round of our second TV title tourny..
