URWA Ringside Report, 1 November 1997

1 November 1997

URWA Ringside Report with J.J. Anthony Tonight in Flagstaff, Andre The Giant, suffering a damaged knee in an NWO assault earlier tonight, lost a non-title bout to Vader...meaning these two mastadons will lock up next Friday night with the World Title on the line....trainers for Andre are still unsure of the extent of Andre's injuries, as doctors have been struggling to find X-Ray equipment to deal with Andre's size. Meanwhile, Comissioner Blom has gotten into a war of words with Andy Kaufman of the NOD. It seems Andy once to bring in a new intergender belt to the URWA, the Inter-Sex belt. The Commish, who has seem this scam unfold one time to many, is having nothing of it, and the war of words between Kaufman and Blom is heating up. Kaufman claims to have insiders in Rampage Towers in Largo, Fla....so this should heat up very nicely. And lastly, we are 3 weeks away from the World Series of Survival...where teams of 5 strive of survive...the NWO has issued the first challenge, as the team of Hulk Hogan, the Outsiders, Gorgeous George, and Royce Gracie issue a challenge to the Bobby Heenan family.. That's all this time, see you in Los Angeles Monday as thse bouts take place: World Tag Title: (2 of 3 falls, 60 min) Steiners vs. Rock-n-Rolls TV Title: (?? min) Steamboat vs. Bruno/Hogan winner Int'l Title Quartefinal: (30 min) Road Warriors vs. Maivia/Ladd Int'l Title Quarterfinal (30 min) Outsiders vs. Bruiser/Crusher (20 min) Rude vs. Douglas Plus A 10-Woman Battle Royal, winner gets a title shot next week
