URWA Arena Report, 7 November 1997

7 November 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
URWA Arena Report 11/7/97, Anaheim Pond, Anaheim, Cal. Bout 1: (30 min) 2-Cold/Henry vs. Von Erichs-- Kerry weakened 2-Cold after a Clawhold, and scored the pinfall after a snap mare at 20:49 Bout 2: (30 min) Natural Disasters vs. Tully/Rude-- With the Heenan Family present, Andre would help Tully brain-busting Typhoon while Heenan, Barbarian, and Bockwinkel were mixing it up with Warlord and Bigelow..the ref quickly got back and scored the three-count on Typhoon at 21:41. Bout 3: (30 min) Outsiders vs. Candido/Van Dam, winner gets World Tag Title shot-- all the Titans, Sting/DDP, and Austin came out and worked over the Outsiders, until the rest of the NWO came out, and security quickly broke things up..Nash was down, favoring his knee, but not severe enough to be stopped..meanwhile, Nash was DQ'd for trying to gouge Van Dam at 18:17, so Candido and Van Damn will get their well-deserved title shot next Friday. Bout 4: (20 min) Lawler vs. Meng-- Piper, George, and Blassie came out with Lawler, while Meng, one-third of the 6-man champs, comes out alone..but a kick to the groin gives Meng the pin at 15:15. Bout 5: (20 min) Bossman vs. Michinoku-- A side-slam by Bossman makes his debut sucessful by pin at 13:21. Bout 6: (20 min) Owen Hart vs. Steamboat-- Doink and Honky Tonk went after Owen before the bout, but their attack was foiled by Warlord and Interrogator..Steamboat was tossed out of the ring, and as a brawl between the two continued..Steamboat just ducked into the ring in time, as Owen was counted-out at 7:24. Bout 7: (30 min) Rock-n-Rolls vs. Bundy/Bigelow-- Doink and Honky came out again with shovels, but Bundy quick ducked, and Warlord, Interrogator, Owen, and Dudley all came out to attack this duo until secuirty came out.. a flying forearm puts Bundy away by pin with Morton making the cover at 19:56. Bout 8: (20 min) LaParka vs. Sabu-- Sabu missed a cross body onto a nearby table, and La Parka brings Sabu in for the pinfall at 14:19. Meanwhile, Andre The Giant come out and went berzerk on both man, taking La Parka and heaving him into the seventh row before security would come out to subdue the big man. Bout 9: (45 min) International Tag Semifinal: Maivia/Ladd vs. Bruiser/Crusher-- Bruiser would be DQ'd for a bite on Maivia at 11:48, setting up a Maivia/Ladd vs. Eliminators final tomorrow. Maivia did suffer some damage to his ribs, but will be able to continue. Bout 10: (20 min) Blue Meanie vs. Mr. Perfect-- even the inept Meanie, a last second replacement for Undertaker, couldn't find answers to Perfect's outstanding technical style..and Meanie was pinned after a 2nd rop legdrop at 15:47. Andre and Heenan came out to rough up Perfect, but again security was on the job, and Perfect was able to get away unscathed. Bout 11: (20 min) Backlund vs. Rhodes-- again, Backlund would put his famous Crossface Chicken Wing on, but time would run out before a submission could be scored. Bout 12: (30 min) Dreamer/Sandman vs. Midnight Express-- despite some interfernce from Sting, Luger, DDP, and Austin, the Midnight Express get the win as Eaton lands his Alabama Jam on Dreamer for the pin at 20:36...but Benoit and Warrior would rough up Eaton before security (who deserves OT for their efforts tonight) broke yet another fracas up. Bout 13: (20 min) Austin vs. Warrior-- Dreamer, Sandman, and Benoit all came down to help Warrior, as Dreamer would toss Warrior some brass knuckles, Austin was intercepted, but right after the ref had a conversation with Sandman on the apron, he caught Austin red-handed and DQ'd him at 17:44...Austin kick the ref and Stunned him!!!! Uh-Oh, I'm sure Austin will be in the commish's office over that one.. Bout 14: (No Time Limit) Cactus Jack vs. Kain, No-DQ Sudden Death Match-- Catcus was beaten to a bloody pulp, and Kain would KO Cactus after a chokeslam to get the win at 17:13. Bout 15: (20 min) Steve Williams vs. Hulk Hogan, TV Title-- Bischoff and the Outsiders came out with Hollywood, who was wearing a new T-shirt..."BREAKING ALL THE RULES TO KEEP THE TV TITLE", and again, Hulk was DQ'd after a face rake at 5:26. Meanwhile, the Heenan clan came down the aisle, but security held off the Heenan family. Bout 16: (45 min) Flair vs. Sid, International Title-- The first half of the bout was very scientific, as Tully was shown at several times watching on from the back. Then, with both men throwing caution to the wind, Ric put Sid in a series of Figure-4s to make Sid submit at 28:35. Meanwhile, the Natural Disasters were shown roughing up Tully! Did the Titans double-cross the Horsemen?? Earthquake put his splash on Blanchard before Flair could get to the locker room with the chair to make the Disasters flee. Bout 17: (2 of 3 falls, 60 min) Steiners vs. Bagwell/Janetty, World Tag Titles-- before this bout could get underway...Sting, Luger, Austin, and DDP began to assualt Bagwell and Janetty, and yet again, security broke things up.. First Fall-- Scott pins Buff after a flying tackle to gain the 1-0 lead at 23:10. Second Fall-- Buff then was DQ'd for a thumb jab on Scott to give the Steiners a two-fall sweep at 35:23. After the bout, the Heenan family attack Bagwell and Janetty, and security again had to do some restraining. Why would Heenan's thugs go after Bagwell and Janetty--they lost!! Bout 18: (60 min) Vader vs. Yokosuna, World Title-- The Heenan family again made it's mark in this bout as Andre bashed Yoko with a chair, a Vader Splash later, and Vader wins the World Title a second time at 17:39, becoming the first man to be World Champion twice. Many were curious as to why the Heenans would help Vader, since this win would give Vader the lead in the race for the best overall wrestler in the URWA.
