URWA Arena Report, 19 September 1997

19 September 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
URWA Arena Show, 9-19-97 Savannah, GA Bout 1: (20 min) Ladies 1st Round: Misty Blue Simms vs. Fabulous Moolah-- Moolah wasted no time in this one, bodyslamming Simms to get the pin at 10:44. Many thought this would be a battle, but Moolah dominates this one. Bout 2: (20 min) Ladies 1st Round: Dump Matsumoto vs. Wendy Richter-- In a shocking upset, Matsumoto uses a foreign object on Richter to get the pin at 12:20, setting up a Moolah-Matsumoto quartefinal. Bout 3: (30 min) Rock-n-Rolls vs. Midnight Express-- Jim Cornette did not show up at the onset of this bout, but he would make his impact known as he came out shortly after the start of the bout. Meanwhile, Eaton was DQ'd for a hair pull on Gibson at 12:50, as the Rock-n-Rolls dominated tonight..The Midnights were very motivated to call it a night.. Bout 4: (20 min) Bobby Heenan vs. Harley Race-- Race was so enraged, wanting to get back as his former manager, that he went to illegal tactics 6 times, and the ref bent over backwards to keep the bout going, but even he had had enough...Heenan with an unlikely win, by DQ at 7:54. Bout 5: (20 min) Hulk Hogan vs. Frank Gotch-- Gotch had entered to ring only to be met by Stevie Richards and the Blue Meanie, asking if Gotch was going to join the BWO..when Gotch started to refuse..Taz came up behind him and locked him in a Tazmisson for nearly a minute until security arrived. Was the NWO and BWO working together?? Gotch got a few minutes rest, but would be able to continue..Gotch fought vailantly for nearly 15 minutes, but Hollywood was too much tonight, getting the pin after a piledriver at 15:19. As Gotch left, he was holding his left shoulder..more details on the injury report at the end of the bout descriptions.. Bout 6: (30 min) Road Warriors vs. Blackjacks-- this bout was an all-out war as the Road Warriors, with "Iron Man" blaring from the P.A. system, attacked the Blackjacks before the opening bell! Animal would be DQ'd for a poke of the eyes in just 4:57 on Blackjack Lanza, for another shocking upset! Bout 7: (20 min) Ric Flair vs. Randy Savage-- With the NWO music blaring, Savage brought a suprise to ringside, as Hollywood Hogan came down to advise the Macho Man. Flair looked a little worried, but came down to ringside with his usual fanfare. Flair dominated the bout early and often, with the Nature Boy getting a submisson on the Macho Man at 18:27. Flair took off before Hollywood could get a hold of him, and gave the crowd a big "WHOO!"...it was revealed later the the Macho Man suffered a serious arm injury, more details on that below.. Bout 8: (20 min) Cactus Jack vs. Shane Douglas-- Cactus would be busted open in this bout, and scored the pin after a vicious punch knocked Shane out cold, scoring the pin at 16:36. Bout 9: (30 min) Freebirds vs. Headbangers-- A running power slam gives Gordy the pin on Mosh at 18:42 as the Freebirds dominated this one..Mosh and Trasher weren't able to mount any meaningful offense on badstreet U.S.A. Bout 10: (30 min) Eliminators vs. Gangstas-- The Elims get an impressive win here as this bout went back and forth, with Mustafa being busted open on a missed Flying Headbutt at the 22-minute mark. Kronus would pin Nu Jack after using brass knuckles at 25:44. Bout 11: (No Time Limit) CAGE MATCH: Raven vs. Stan Hansen-- The first of two cage matches saw an all out war between Raven and Hansen, with Raven getting busted open as his head was rammed twice, hard, into the cage, before Hansen escapes at 18:38. Bout 12: (No Time Limit) CAGE MATCH: Arn Anderson vs. Tully Blanchard-- this was one of the better bouts of the evening, as both men gave their all, but Anderson's DDT would knock Tully out for the Enforcer to escape at 21:10. Bout 13: (45 min) International Title: Lou Thesz, challenger vs. Andre The Giant, champion..The Giant again dominated, and won after his famous Big Squash over in one of the corners made Thesz collapse. Andre scored the pin at 16:17. But as Andre left the arena, Lex Luger appeared at the runway. And while Andre was staring him down, Sting came up from behind to knock Andre out with Sting's International Tag Belt..before more damage could be done, out came the Heenan Family..and Sting and Luger fled.. Bout 14: (2 of 3 falls, 60 minutes) World Tag Titles: Dick The Bruiser/Crusher Lisowski, challengers, vs. The Outsiders, champions.. Fall #1-- This bout would see the rule book go out the window early and often! Lisowski would be DQ'd in the first fall for use of a foreign object at 5:47. Fall #2-- Bruiser and Crusher would come back and even things at 1 fall each when Dick The Bruiser would bust Hall open, and pin him after a monkey flip at 18:35. Fall #3-- The match then got completely out of hand, with Nash getting the DQ for an eye-poke at 20:05, so Bruiser and Crusher win in 3 falls, but don't win the titles. By scoring a pin in the second fall, Bruiser and Crusher get a world tag title shot at a later date. INJURY REPORT In the 5th bout, Frank Gotch suffered a shoulder injury, and will be out 6 weeks.. In the 7th bout, Randy Savage would suffer an arm injury, and will be out 10 weeks.. In the 12th bout, Tully Blanchard suffered some bruised ribs, and will be able to wrestle, with them taped, for 3 weeks...
