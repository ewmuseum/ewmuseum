URWA Arena Report, 18 September 1997

18 September 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Bout 1: (20 min) Ladies 1st Round: Lioness Asuka vs. Madusa-- Asuka and Madusa held nothing back, as Asuka had been busted open by a foreign object from Madusa 12 minutes in..Madusa poured it on from there, ending the bout with a piledriver and pin at 15:41. Bout 2: (20 min) Ladies 1st Round: Mildred Burke vs. June Byers-- This battle of two old-time ladies legends was another mat classic. Some woman with a mask run in to clobber Burke, and Byers gets the pin for the quarterfinals at 14:40. Bout 3: (20 min) Dreamer vs. DDP-- This was another fantastic bout, but DDP dominated near the end of the 20 minute time-limit, and he brings back the Diamond Death Drop to get the pin at 19:40. Bout 4: (30 min) Bulldogs vs. Midnight Express-- The Bulldogs brought their mascot, the loveable Matilda, to the ring, and when Cornette came in to introduce the Midnights, Matilda run wild after him!! Cornette ran for the refuge of the arena floor to say: "LADIES AND GENTLEMEN, the team who will kick a$$ and take names in the URWA, Beautiful Bobby and Sweet Stan, the MIDNIGHT EXPRESS!" This bout would see the Bulldogs get the duke as Smith landed a press slam to get the pin on Eaton at 10:48. Bout 5: (20 min) Sandman vs. Steve Austin-- Another intriguing bout saw Stone Cold rise to the occasion, scoring a pinfall after a top rope elbow and pin at 11:18. And just for good measure, Austin acted like he wanted to shake hands with Sandman after the bout, and he hit the Stunner on him! Bout 6: (20 min) Rick Rude vs. HBK-- Rude came out with Bobby Heenan, still in his neck-brace..and Rude, said, you guessed it: "CUT THE MUSIC! What I'd like to have right now is for all you, fat, out of shape, Jacksonville Jackasses...keep the noise down, and show you who the real boy-toy in the URWA is...HIT THE MUSIC!" This bout would go back and forth, with the bell ringing to signify that the 20-minute time limit had expired for a the draw. This is a bout I'm sure we will see again with an expanded time-limit! Bout 7: (20 min) Dusty Rhodes vs. Harley Race-- In another back and forth bout, Dusty would be busted open early in this bout after a chair smash about 6 minutes in. Race bended the rules frequently in this bout, leading to a DQ after a choke at 14:19. The American Dream was bloodied, but would be in the win column tonight. Bout 8: (20 min) Randy Savage vs. Lou Thesz-- This bout was another good one, Savage went to an aerial attack, while Thesz kept appling basic technical holds. A back slide ended this one as Thesz gets the pin at 12:01, much to the chargin of Savage and the NWO. Bout 9: (20 min) Sabu vs. Arn Anderson-- This card continued to have some intriguing and riveting battles in it, and this bout was no exception. Arn's toughness and Sabu's agility made this a bout to remember. And, once again, the bell would ring before a winner was found for a 20-minute draw, with very little cheating. Bout 10: (30 min) Blue Meanie/Stevie Richards vs. Von Erichs-- The BWO was outmanned in this one, and Meanie took the easy way out, landing a thumb jab and a foreign object to earn the DQ on Kevin at 12:50. Bout 11: (20 min) Bruno Sammartino vs. Paul Orndorff-- This bout went down to the final minute, with Orndorff in serious trouble..And with time running out, Sammartino went to a bearhug on his foe, and Orndorff, perhaps unaware of how much time remained, submitted at 19:45. Bout 12: (20 min) Dory Funk Jr. vs. Terry Funk-- This was the bout many in the crowd anticipated most. This bout was a very scientific affair, ending once again with a 20-minute draw, neither man really lost his cool nor cheated in a very intriguing bout. Bout 13: (45 min) International Title: Hollywood Hulk Hogan, challenger vs. Andre The Giant, champion-- the turning point of this bout came when Heenan handed Andre a roll of quarters to bust open Hollywood 10 minutes in. Andre took control from there, scoring the pin after a press slam at 15:22. So Andre retains his International belt. Bout 14: (45 min) International Tag Titles: Steiner Brothers, challengers vs. Sting/Lex Luger, champions.. so many bouts tonight are bout of the night candidates, and this was also no exception, this bout would be a very scientific affair until the 40-minute mark, as Rick picked Luger up to guillotine his throat over the top rope. Luger's momentum carried him outside, and was counted-out at 42:39. So the Steiners get a win over the Luger/Sting duo, but must wait another day to claim the belts. Bout 15: (60 min) World Title: Stan Hansen, challenger vs. Vader, champion...Vader toyed with the smalller but tougher Hansen throughout the bout, ending it with a Vader Pummel, repeated forearm smashes to Hansen's head and body, for a pin at 17:41. So all 3 champs in action retain their belts tonight.
