URWA Ringside Report, 23 October 1997

23 October 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
URWA Ringside Report with J.J. Anthony Tonight in Nashville, the URWA World Title picture was thrown in TOTAL CHAOS in what has to be considered the biggest upset by far in the 6 week history of URWA, as Vader was pinned by Gorgeous George of the NWO, with help from someone very much resembling Hollywood Hulk Hogan..which means it could be George and Royce Gracie, two NWO members, battling for the world's championship Sunday at Halloween Havoc..WAIT! I see Royce down the hallway, I am going to try to get a word with him...Royce..Royce Gracie..can I prevail upon you for some words this evening? RG: Why sure JJ.. JJ: Mr. Gracie, you look a little too happy to be battling a fellow NWO member Sunday in Dallas...what's causing all this? RG: Well, after talking it over with our NWO co-members, I will be giving up my World Title shot in the interests of NWO unity.. JJ: WHAT?? You've got to be kidding me.. (JJ spots Comissoner Blom walking to the camera location) JJ: Comissoner Blom, I know you heard what Mr. Gracie has just said.. CB: I did J.J., so Royce, are you sure you want to do this? RG: Absolutely P.J., I could always go after the Benoit/2-Cold winner and become the Cruiserweight Champion.. CB: OK, here's what we will do..the top 20 overall excluding those with titles after Friday Night's bouts will be put in a battle-royal Saturday..the winner will get the title shot against Gorgeous George... JJ: OH MY!! CB: And should a Flair or a Sid be the winner, or someone else who has a slot in the Dallas Halloween Hell lineup, the runner-up will be allowed to take his place.. JJ: Well, this should prove interesting... Folks, here is the revised Halloween Hell line-up 1. World Title: Gorgeous George vs. Saturday's 20-Man Battle Royal Winner 2. For the World Tag Titles: The NWO's Rock-n-Roll Express vs. Road Warriors, but this bout is 2-out-of-3 falls, no DQ, no Time Limit 3. For The International Title: Andre The Giant vs. Ric Flair 4. For The International Tag Titles: The Outsiders vs. The Steiners 5. For The TV Title: Sid vs. Bret Hart, Inside A Steel Cage! 6. Bruno Sammartino vs. Sandman 7. Rude/Blanchard vs. Bruiser/Crusher 8. For The Cruiserweight Title, 2-Cold Scorpio vs. Chris Benoit 9. In A Special Triangle Match to unify the Ladies and "Intergender Titles", in the first bout, Andy Kaufman goes against Candi Divine, with the winner getting Ladies Champion Bull Nakano. These 2 bouts will be no-time limit and no-DQ. And, if either lady is the winner, the Intergender Title may never be "claimed" in the URWA by Kaufmann. But meanwhile, should Kaufmann win both bouts, the Ladies Title will offically become the Intergender Title. 10. Cactus Jack vs. Sabu in a special No-DQ match! 11. Owen Hart vs. DDP 12. Kain vs. Barbarian.. This Saturday, I'll give you my Halloween Hell picks, plus the complete rules for Bad Dreams, our TV Special Halloween Night in Alberquerque!! Until then, I'll see you Saturday Night in Razorback Country, Little Rock, Arkansas...SOOEY!!
