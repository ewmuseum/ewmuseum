URWA Manic Monday, 10 November 1997

10 November 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
URWA Manic Monday 11/10/97 -- Lawlor Events Center, Reno Nev. Before the first bout, a coin toss was held, won by Comissoner Blom who elected Kaufman must reveal immediately his mystery man...and it is KEN SHAMROCK! Bout 1: (20 min) Gracie vs. Benoit-- Bischoff and Lawler watched Gracie's back for this one..and as time was running out Benoit executed a beautiful headbutt of the top rope, but the bell rang to signify the end of the 20-minute time limit. Bout 2: (45 min) Mildred Burke vs. Misty Blue Simms, Ladies Title-- This match was kept scientific for the most part, concluding when Simms scored a pin on Burke at 18:45 after a two-arm toss Bout 3: (20 min) Kevin Sullivan vs. Big Bossman-- Bossman wins by DQ at 14:19 after Sully smashed a chair Heenan and Rude brought in into Bossman's face, knocking Bossman out cold. Security kept a 3 on 1 situtaion from getting worse. Bout 4: (No-Time Limit) 4-Way Dance: Outsiders, Eliminators, Road Warriors, Demolition, battle royal rules..Nash would throw Animal out at 2:31, while Hawk threw out Kronus at 6:34, and right behind Hawk was Nash tossing Hawk out at 6:36. Saturn then threw out Ax at 7:46. Nash took care of Smash a short time later at 8:33, and the Outsiders then eliminated Saturn at 11:45. The Outsiders were the winners! Bout 5: (No-Time Limit, No DQ) Russian Roulette Tag Match: Comissoner Blom/??? vs. Kaufman/Shamrock-- with Kauffman and Shamrock out, Blom revealed his suprise...OH MY--HAS BLOM GONE OFF THE DEEP END?? IT'S THE SHEIK...and look what Blom has with him...his familiar brown briefcase..Sheik and Blom run in to the ring, and here we go!! At the 15-minute mark, Shamrock had Blom in a bad way, and barely got the tag to the Sheik, and the Sheik took it from there...eventually wrapping a chain around his fist to bash Kaufmann to score the pin at 35:48. Then after Shamrock look like he would eliminate Sheik, Blom went into his briefcase and tossed Sheik a roll of quarters, and Sheik blasted Shamrock with them to score the pin at 38:16. Giving the Blom/Sheik team a clean sweep!! Bout 6: (60 min) World Title: Hogan vs. Vader-- As promised, Hogan did not come out with the NWO, and Vader also came out all alone..there was a mutual show of respect from both men, as the bout was scientific the first 20-25 minutes. But, Flair came out to sit down at ringside at the 8-minute mark and after a few minutes when Hogan and the ref were jawing, Flair jabbed the front of the chair at Vader's knee. And while the ref didn't see, security did, and Flair was spirited away...the bout then went past the 30-minute mark..Vader would go for his Vader Splash..but Hogan kicked out at 2..Hogan came right back with a chop and a forearm smash, knocking Vader off balance..Hogan quickly covered..only for a 2 count..then Vader went to a quick boot to Hogan's gut..only a 2 count there! Vader was still prone shocked he couldn't pin Hogan for the 3..Hogan quickly got up and scored the legdrop! 1-2-3!!!! Hogan wins the world title in a memorable bout at 35:11. How refreshing it was to see a battle reach a conclusion in the ring, and not be dictated by gang warfare..
