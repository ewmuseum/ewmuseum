URWA Arena Report, 20 November 1997

20 November 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
URWA Arena Report 11/20/97, Rose Garden, Portland, OR Bout 1: (20 min) Yokosuna vs. Warrior-- Yokosuna was just too big for the Warrior, and a Banzai Drop ends matters at 15:21. Bout 2: (30 min) Van Damn/Candido vs. Headhunters-- a flying body press gives Van Dam the win over Headhunter Malheim at 16:19. Bout 3: (20 min) Benoit vs. Andre-- with Heenan at ringside, Benoit's agility get Andre confused in the first 10 minutes, but Andre soon took control and wins with the Big Squash at 15:41. Bout 4: (30 min) Rude/Blanchard vs. George/Blassie-- Heenan came out with Bockwinkel and Barbarian with the Heenan Family, while Bischoff and the Outsiders brought out Georgeously Classy. And the NWO duo would get the W with Blanchard getting called for a choke on Geroge for the DQ at 23:06. Bout 5: (20 min) Vader vs. Morales-- Vader gets the pin with a Vader Splash at 16:46 in a mostly one sided bout. Bout 6: (30 min) Henry/2-Cold vs. Richards/Raven-- Henry pins Richards with a bone-crushing Suplex to get the pin at 17:20. Bout 7: (20 min) Meng vs. Steve Williams-- Williams crunched Meng with the Oklahoma Stampede, and when Meng kicked out of it, and Meng bickered with the ref, Williams cradled Meng up for the pin at 11:18. Bout 8: (20 min) Ahmed vs. Flair-- Kaufmann tried to toss a chain to Ahmed, but Flair would intercept and nail Ahmed with it for the pin at 12:42. Man, I wouldn't want to be Kaufman in the NOD locker room. Bout 9: (30 min) Interrogator vs. Suillvan/La Parka, Handicap Match-- Interrogator wins after a Side Slam at 26:28 over both men who nearly scored the win. Bout 10: (30 min) Sting/Luger vs. Furnas/LaFon-- Furnas would DQ'ed after trying to choke a bloody Sting with the turnbuckle rope at 27:47. Bout 11: (20 min) Inoki vs. Annihilator-- Inoki scores the pinfall on the Annihilator after his special karate kick at 14:03. Bout 12: (No Time Limit) Sabu vs. Rey Misterio Jr.-- In this no time-limit attraction Sabu gets the win with his Sabu Dropkick at 22:34 by pin. Bout 13: (20 min) Backlund vs. Bagwell-- Backlund makes the Buffster submit to a Crossface Chickenwing at 17:19. Bout 14: (No Time Limit) Zeus vs. Kain, casket match-- Zeus would nail Kain in the face with brass kncuckles and then smash a busted open Kain into the casket before stuffing him into it at 25:01. Bout 15: (No Time Limit) Bundy vs. Dreamer, cage match-- Bundy would leave Dreamer a bloody mess, escaping thru the door at 10:26. Bout 16: (No Time Limit) Heenan vs. Kaufman, cage match-- Kaufmann came out charging, but a clothesline by Heenan had Kaufman down and hurt...Heenan did some quick stomps and escpaed the cage in an amazing :29. Bout 17: (45 min) Race vs. Rhodes, International Title-- Race was DQ'd for a hair-pull at 20:58, so Rhodes keeps his title. Bout 18: (60 min) Giant vs. Hogan, World Title-- Bischoff, Lawler, and the Outsiders brought Hogan out to the ring, but the Giant had the upper hand all thru the bout, giving Giant a DQ win at 19:48.
