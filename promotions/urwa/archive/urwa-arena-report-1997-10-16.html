URWA Arena Report, 16 October 1997

16 October 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
URWA Arena Report 10/16/97-- Market Square Arena-- Indianapolis, IND Note: The UT-Gonzalez will be held tomorrow due to the fact I had Gonzalez down with a tag bout earlier in the card. Bout 1: (No Time Limit) 6-Man Title Prelim Round, Group A: 12-Man Battle Royal, Sid/Zeus/Bundy, Cactus Jack/Eliminators, Outsiders/Hogan, Blassie/Lawler/Piper...Kronus was the first thrown out by Sycho Sid at 1:35. Freddy Blassie would accidentally bump Cactus into Saturn, eliminating Saturn at 6:34. Zeus would throw out Lawler at 9:27, and Lawler would geat whacked with a loaded neck brace from Bobby Heenan as he was leaving, before security could intervene, while Blassie and Piper took care of Bundy at 13:37, Piper and Blassie tried to hold Zeus in place, while Hall got out some type of foeign object, only to miss and bust open Piper...Zeus then took care of Hall, throwing him out at 19:23. Zeus then eliminated Hogan at 20:13. Sycho Sid came up from behind to Power Bomb and throw-out Piper at 28:04, and while Sid was celebrating, Nash would throw Sid out a few short seconds later at 28:09. Blassie then eliminated Zeus at 28:59, to leave two NWO-ites in the ring. Bischoff came out to toss a coin to see which team would go to the 6-man Semis, which was won by Outsider Nash, sending the Outsider/Hogan trio on to the semifinals. Bout 2: (No Time Limit) 6-Man Title Prelim Round, Group B: Flair/Gracie/Dory Funk, Dreamer/Sandman/Warrior, Heenan/Rude/Blanchard, Road Warriors/Backlund-- Flair and Dory jumped the Ultimate Warrior and threw him out right at the bell, at :00. Rick Rude then tossed the Sandman out at :26, Backlund would then toss Rude out at 4:03, with Backlund being next out via Dory Funk at 5:41. Flair would toss Animal out at 11:26, and Dory would be accidently bumped out by Flair at 11:50. Gracie then took care of Dreamer at 12:13, and Bobby Heenan would be eliminated by Hawk at 15:02. Gracie suddenly jumped Ric Flair, as Royce finally revealed his NWO colors despite promises to honor his prior agreement, but Flair would get the last laugh, eliminating Gracie at 15:26, and Hawk at 15:51, leaving it down to a Flair-Blanchard battle, and Flair would win at 18:42. Flair, Dory Funk, and Royce Gracie will advance, but I'm sure we'll hear a ruling from the Commish as to the status of this shaky team. Meanwhile, as Gracie was heading back to the dressing room, he was jumped from behind by Antonio Inoki and the British Bulldogs, totally upset with Gracie's betrayal. But the crack security staff of the URWA prevented injuiries. Bout 3: (No Time Limit) 6-Man Title Prelim Round, Group C: Sting/Luger/Austin, Mr. T/Brooklyn Patrol, Undertaker/Demoliton, Orndorff/Blackjacks-- Smash gave Luger his walking orders at 4:57, while the Undertaker threw out Mr. T at 9:21. Blackjack Mulligan would take care of Stone Cold by eliminating him at 12:39, while Sting threw out the sewage known as T.L. Hopper at 14:44. Brawler would eliminate Orndorff at 16:46, but he fell into the deadly grasp of the Undertaker, who would work him over before tossing him out at 17:34. Smash then eliminated Mulligan at 19:13, while Undertaker would be tossed by Sting at 19:39. Lanza would bump Smash into Ax, accidentally elimating a furious Ax at 22:32. Lanza and Smash then struck a deal to team up and eliminate Sting at 24:33. But while Lanza celebrated, Smash would double-cross him, tossing him out at 24:56 as Demolition and the Undertaker advance. Bout 4: (No-Time Limit) 6-Man Title Prelim Round, Group D: Ahmed/2-Cold/Mark Henry, Meng/Kain/Wrath, Brusier Brody/Studd/Sabu, Malenko/Misterio/Disco Inferno-- Disco would get the first trip out of the ring via Wrath at 2:48, then Wrath would take care of Sabu at 4:02. Brusier Brody would take care of Meng at 4:53, but Wrath would send Dean Malenko sailing at 10:41, Studd would toss Misterio out like he was a rag doll at 11:29. Wrath and Brody locked-up, with Wrath tossing Brody aside at 14:19. Scropio got a running start to take care of Wrath at 17:33. Mark Henry would then accidentally collide with Ahmed Johnson, sending Ahmed out at 18:46. Studd then took care of 2-Cold for tossing out Brody by tossing him out at 20:22, and then would take of Mark Henry at 22:27, and then after a six-minute battle, a fresher Kain took care of Big John Studd as the Rare Oddities prevail at 28:58. After our 9th bout, Comissoner Blom will draw the semi-final pairings.. Bout 5: (30 min) Eliminators vs. Brusher/Cruiser-- Lisowski would reverse a power-piledriver as Saturn would be pinned at 21:10, as the Bruiser/Crusher duo would get their first free-agent victory. Bout 6: (No Time Limit, No DQ, No Count-out) Headbangers vs. Gonzales/Mortis-- Sycho Sid of the Quad Threat came out to toss brass knuckles to Trasher, but Gonzales intercepted and would conk out Trasher for the pin at 13:17. Bout 7: (20 min) Rude vs. Buff Bagwell-- After the opening moments of this bout, Hogan, Blassie, and Gorgeous George came out...only to scout Bagwell it seems, as Rude get his Rude Awakening for the pinfall at 12:47. Bout 8: (20 min) Shane vs. Meng-- the rest of the Quad Threat came out to attack Meng on the runaway as the Headbangers assist Sid in putting a Spike Power Bomb on Meng...OUCH! But, Meng would get his senses, reveresing momentum on a catapult to pin Douglas at 18:11, and escaped before the Quads could attack. Bout 9: (30 min) Cruiserweight Title Quarterfinal: Gracie vs. Benoit-- Bischoff, Hogan, and Lawler brought out Gracie to ringside, but Gracie would illegally choke out Benoit for the DQ at 17:31, and Gracie would let Benoit know that he had bigger fish to fry other than the Cruiserweight Title, as Hogan and Lawler came in and gave Benoit a nasty beating, before security made the save. Then, Comissonner Blom came out to do the drawing for the 6-Man Tag Semifinals: In one semifinal, Hogan/Outsiders vs. Undertaker/Demolition In the other, Flair/Dory Funk Jr./??? (Flair and Funk will be allowed to chose another partner) vs. Kain/Wrath/Meng Bout 10: (20 min) Rocky Maivia vs. Great Sasuke-- Rocky put a shoulder-breaker on Sasuke in the final moments, but the bell would ring to declare the bout a 20-minute draw. Bout 11: (20 min) Farooq vs. Austin-- As the bell signaled the start of this bout, the Nation of Dominaton came down to ringside.. Farooq would get a late pin thanks to an Austin rib injury and a two arm toss at 19:48, just before the time limit was to expire. The NOD came out....to ATTACK FAROOQ...as the NOD does a little housecleaning.. Bout 12: (30 min) Road Warriors vs. Furnas/LaFon-- The Road Warriors continue their reign as the #1 tag contenders, as Hawk pins LaFon after a neckbreaker at 14:15. Could we see the Roadies strike gold this weekend? Bout 13: (30 min) Blackjacks vs. Midnight Express-- Jim Cornette would toss Bobby Eaton a roll of quarters and nailed Lanza out cold, as Eaton pins Lanza at 21:53. Bout 14: (No Time-Limit) Battle of the Horseman: Tully vs. Arn vs. Flair vs. Ole, 4-Way Battle Royal-- Arn started out by tossing out Ole at 4:32, while Flair would be tossed out by Arn at 8:06 after Flair was gouged in the eyes by Arn, then after a 12-minute battle Tully would get the victory at 20:41 after kicking Arn in the groin. Bout 15: (No Time-Limit) Wrath vs. Sid, cage-match-- Wrath would make his way to the cage first, and was again assualted by the rest of the Quad Threat as the rammed Wrath into the constructed cage..Sid came out and toyed with Wrath, busting him open and then escaping after putting Wrath in the Power Bomb at 14:16. Bout 16: (20 min) Andre The Giant vs. The Giant, non-title match-- A big chance tonight for the Giant to get a title shot should he win tonight, but The Giant would blow it, smashing Andre with a chair for a DQ at 11:50. Bout 17: (30 min) Bret Hart vs. Taz, TV Title-- Eric Bischoff would grab the mic and say the even though Bret could easily specify this would be a non-title bout, Bret would agree to put his title on the line tonight. And, without any interference, Bret gave Taz a wrestling lesson, pinning Taz after a cross-body off the 2nd rope for the pin in just 6:54. Bout 18: (2 of 3 falls, 60 min) Bulldogs vs. Rock-n-Roll Express, World Tag Titles...the new look Rock-n-Roll Express brought out Lawler, Hogan, HBK and Gracie to ringside, as things looked bad for the champs First Fall-- A flying cross body gives Morton the first fall on Dynamite Kid at 18:42. Second Fall-- After showing the Rock-n-Roll could score a clean fall, they would throw away the rulebook in the second as Gracie would toss brass knuckles to Morton and he quickly used them on Davey Boy, busting him open, a double dropkick and cover by Morton gives the Rock-n-Rolls the fall and the WORLD TAG TITLES in just two falls at 19:38..
