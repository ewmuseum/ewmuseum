URWA Manic Monday, 29 September 1997

29 September 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
URWA Manic Monday 9/29..Corestates Spectrum..Philadephia, PA Bout 1: (20 min) Sycho Sid vs. Lex Luger-- Bundy was in Sid's corner tonight, but it would backfire as Bundy's interference gives Luger the DQ win at 6:25..Sting would come out and make the save..but out came Douglas and the Headbangers to foil the save and again leave Sting and Luger laying.. Bout 2: (30 min) Eliminators vs. Brusher/Crusher-- In a tough bruising battle, neither side scored a pin in the time limit for a 30:00 draw..good effort by all 4 men in this brawl.. Bout 3: (No Time Limit, No DQ) Ladies Title Final: Bull Nakano vs. Emma Waldek..could Waldek, the underdog, score the title winner here..Waldek had Nakano in trouble, but a suplex off the 2nd rope gives Nakano the title at 15:55 in a hard fought brawl.. Bout 4: (30 min) TV Title: Royce Gracie, challenger vs. Bret Hart, champion--with Terry Funk and Cactus Jack at ringside, Bret Hart was off his game, and Gracie dominated until a right hand puts Hart out for a pin at 18:42!! Royce Gracie is your new TV champ!! Bout 5: (60 min) No-DQ 8-Man Tag: Sabu/Taz/Richards/Meanie vs. Hogan/Lawler/Outsiders-- In the battle of NWO vs. BWO..an unlikely upset occurs as Taz gets a piledriver on Nash for a pin at 22:59..Meaning right here, next week..Taz and a partner of his choices get the Outsiders in a World Tag Title Match..
