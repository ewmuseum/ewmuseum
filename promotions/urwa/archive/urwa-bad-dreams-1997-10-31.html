URWA Friday Night Fury: Bad Dreams

31 October 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
URWA Bad Dreams 10/31/97 The Pit-- Alberquerque, NM Bout 1: 24-Man Battle Royal: Outsider Hall, Harley Race, Blackjack Lanza, Cactus Jack, Dynamite Kid, Stan Lane, Tommy Dreamer, Hawk, Dusty Rhodes, DDP, Bruiser Brody, Barbarian, Robert Gibson, Fred Blassie, Sid, Morales, Wrath, Big Dick Dudley, Owen Hart, Disco Inferno, Mark Henry, Earthquake, La Parka, Mr. Perfect...This one all began with Disco being toss out by Hawk at 1:12, followed seconds later by Diamond Dallas Page at 1:35, as Hawk was a house-of-fire! Sid took care of the Barbarian at 4:05. Dreamer waved bye-bye to his chance due to an Earthquake at 5:17. Dusty Rhodes eliminates Stan Lane at 10:10, while Blassie gives Race has exit at 11:47. Sid throwa out La Parka at 13:46, while Mark Henry does the same to Dusty Rhodes at 14:36. Hall throws out Wrath at 21:21, and Dudley would take care of Lanza at 27:04. Blassie would polish off Dynamite Kid at 27:54, and then Perfect eliminated Owen at 29:10. Brody takes care of Big Dick Dudley at 30:25, while Sycho Sid would be suprisingly tossed out by Hawk at 35:15. Catcus would take care of Pedro Morales at the 44:32 mark...while Bruiser polishes off Hall at 47:48. Perfect shows suprising strength eliminating Mark Henry at 52:18, and Brody sends Cactus packing at 54:46. Gibson gets a little tag-team revenge on Road Warrior Hawk, tossing him out at 57:58. As the field went down to four past the one hour mark, Gibson was easily tossed by Earthquake at 1:00:25, and would ditch Perfect seconds later at 1:00:49. Then Blassie took care of Bruiser Brody at 1:01:12. And instantly Typhoon got up to the apron to bicker with the ringside refs...lowering the top rope while talking to the refs on the floor, and while his back was turned, Blassie gave Earthquake a swinging neckbreaker and shoved Earthquake out for the world title shot at 1:01:59. Bout 2: (20 min) Buff Bagwell vs. Rick Rude-- these two went right at it, but Rude would get the DQ at 1:55 for a thumb jab. Bagwell didn't choose a title shot, but gets the W. Bout 3: (No Time Limit, No DQ, No Count-Out) Dean Malenko vs. Ric Flair, International Title-- with the Titans from Hell watching at ringside, both men battle scientifically for the first 15-20 minutes, but Flair reversed a snap suplex to get the painfall at 23:04. As Warlord came down to interfere, he met Blanchard who knocked him out with a chair! Bout 4: (60 min) Road Warriors vs. Mortis/Catcus Jack, World Tag Titles-- for the first time in URWA history, the World Tag Titles was defensed in a one-fall match...and, as it turned out, as Hawk went to the top rope, out came Wrath to shove Hawk off, and Mortis would get the cover for a title-winning pin in just 8:45, as Mortis and Cactus Jack become, shockingly, the new World Tag Champs! Bout 5: (45 min) Rock-n-Roll Express vs. Steiners, International Tag Titles-- this bout didn't last long, as Steiner would take his headgear off and nail Morton with it for a DQ in :29. The Rock-n-Rolls will get another title shot, but, the Steiners have a chance at a simultaneous double against Cactus Jack and Mortis later on. Bout 6: (60 min) Ahmed Johnson vs. Andre The Giant, World Title-- Ahmed would give Andre serious fits as he survived over 26 minutes before a Pearl River Plunge attempt by Ahmed failed, and a back drop and pin later, Andre retains his World Title at 26:07. Bout 7: (45 min) Taz vs. Ric Flair, International Title-- Taz was as tough as they come, but Tully came out yet again and nailed Taz with a chair while he was perched on the top rope...Flair quickly got up to suplex Taz off the 2nd rope for yet another win at 17:20. Bout 8: (60 min) Rocky Maivia/mystery partner vs. Mortis/Cactus Jack, World Tag Titles--many speculated as to who Maivia's mystery partner was, would it be Maivia's family? A football player? One of his former NOD mates? Well, as Maivia came down to the ring..he introduced...ERNIE "THE CAT" LADD!!!..a shocked Cactus Jack and Mortis came to ringside, and after a well-fought match, Cactus Jack went for a backbreaker, but Ladd shifted his weight and scored the three-count at 24:43, as now Ladd and Maivia are the new World Tag Champs...as Mortis and Cactus Jack now set a mark that can only be tied, winning and losing a title in the same night! Bout 9: (45 min) Brian Christopher vs. 2-Cold Scorpio, Cruiserweight Title-- John Kronus orignally drew the #8 spot, but since Kronus was MIA...a drawing amongst the free-agent was held, and Brian Christopher drew the replacement position, and he elected to challenge 2-Cold for the Cruiserweight belt. Many thought Christopher had the title sewn up, but B.C. lost it, choking out 2-Cold, and getting DQ'ed at 14:16. Bout 10: (60 min) Steiners (International Tag Champs) vs. Maivia/Ladd, World Tag Titles-- In this first ever title vs. title match, the Steiners against prevail as Rick loads up his headgear, takes it off, and nails Ladd for the pinfall at 27:14, as now the Steiners become the first time to hold both tag titles at once, the first team to have two titles of any type at once, while Ladd and Maivia tie the Mortis/Cactus Jack mark for winning and losing the tag titles in one night. Meanwhile, the Steiners will have to vacate the International Tag Titles, and a 8-team tournament will be held next week to find the new champions. Bout 11: (60 min) Sting vs. Andre The Giant, World Title-- Sting used his agility, but a massive headbutt by Andre retains him the title by a pin at 13:50. Bout 12: (60 min) Fred Blassie vs. Andre The Giant, World Title-- Hogan, Lawler, Piper, and the Outsiders came out with Blassie, while the Heenan clan came out with Andre..even a tired Andre could suplex Blassie as the Giant retains the title at 13:19.
