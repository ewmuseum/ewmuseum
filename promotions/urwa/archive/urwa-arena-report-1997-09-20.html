URWA Arena Report, 20 September 1997

20 September 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
URWA Arena Report 9-20-97 Columbia, SC-- McGuire Center Bout 1: Special Ladies Tag Match: (30 Min) Lionness Azuka/Misty Blue Simms vs. Wendy Richter/June Byers...this special tag bout ended with Asuka going for a body press, but June got her knees up at the last second, and Byers would cover for the pin at 16:44.. Bout 2: Ladies 1st Round: (20 Min) Reggie Bennett vs. Candi Divine..this would be an up and down bout, with both ladies giving their all..but the bell would ring, a 20:00 draw, before a winner was found here..so both woman are out, and the Waldek/Martel winner goes on to the semifinals. Bout 3: Ladies 1st Round: (20 Min) Emma Waldek vs. Sherri Martel.. Emma hoisted Sherri in the airplane spin to get the pinfall at 16:13. Waldek go into the semis to meet the winner of the bout between Moolah and Mastumoto. Bout 4: (20 Min) Bobby Heenan vs. Paul Orndorff...as Orndorff awaited in the ring, out came Heenan..in a neck-brace...and as he grabbed the mic he said, "OK, Orndorff, and the rest of you humanoids, listen up! Under doctor's orders, tonight, I'm gonna have to pull out of our bout. But I got a BIG suprise for you tonight in a non-title night, I give you the eighth wonder of the world..ANDRE THE GIANT!.." Andre came down to the ring and said "HEY! Wonderful..show the folks you're no coward. Beat me, and I'll give you a bout for the title, IF YOU CAN.." Orndorff looked to the crowd, and agreed, but Andre went to the sneak attack! Orndorff got his head clear, and once again, was very close to gaining a draw..but Andre hoisted Paul in the backbreaker for a submisson at 19:36. Give Mr. Wonderful his due, in his two bouts against much more superior talent, he nearly came away with two draws.. Bout 5: (20 Min, No-DQ) Shawn Michaels vs. Steve Austin-- In this No-DQ brawl, Austin brought back his WCW move, the Stungun, to get the pinfall at 17:56..Austin seems to be on a roll here in URWA-land.. Bout 6: (20 Min) Hulk Hogan vs. Tommy Dreamer-- This would be all Hollywood Hulk Hogan, as Lawler came out to ringside with Hollywood..Lawler would distract Dreamer right before bell-time, and Hogan took advantage, tossing him out of the ring. Lawler than beat the stuffing out of Dreamer, and tossed him back in..Hogan would use Jerry's crown to knock out Dreamer..then pinned him with the legdrop at only 4:22.. Bout 7: (20 Min) Rick Rude vs. Taz-- Rick Rude came out by himself, and he started into his usual pre-bout statements, which I won't repeat..this is a family show, ya know..Rude pulled off the upset with a Rude Awakening to get the pin at 11:17. Bout 8: (20 Min) Chris Benoit vs. Sid-- Both men made their URWA debuts..but Sid would dominate, quickly pinning Benoit in a power-bomb in a URWA record 59 seconds..better luck next time, Chris.. Bout 9: (30 Min) Gangstas vs. Headbangers-- Sid would enter in to this bout to interfere against the Gangstas, power-bombing Nu Jack...but Said, at the same time, threw powder in the face of Thrasher..causing the ref to call this bout a DOUBLE-DQ at 13:20. Bout 10: (45 Min) Outsiders/Lawler vs. Road Warriors/Nikita Koloff-- This would be an up-and-down battle, with Lawler missing a forearm smash, and Animal getting the cover at 33:08. Bout 11: (20 Min) Ric Flair vs. Bruno Sammartino-- the winner of this bout would be the #1 contender for title contention in the singles divison...and Bruno would get the win here after an airplane spin and pinfall at 15:14. Bout 12: (45 Min, No-DQ, Chicago Street Fight) Steiner Brothers, challengers vs. Sting/Lex Luger, champions...with the Steiners already in the ring, and Sting and Luger on their way..the Road Warriors came from behind to attack Sting and Luger, leaving them laying..this battle would go all over the place, but Stinger would miss a body press, and Rick gets the cover at 11:24 to make the Steiner Brothers the NEW International Tag Champs...what a way to end our card, with the first title change in URWA history..
