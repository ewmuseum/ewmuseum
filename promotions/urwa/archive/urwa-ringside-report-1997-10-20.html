URWA Ringside Report, 20 October 1997

20 October 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
URWA Ringside Report with J.J. Anthony Without further ado, ladies and gentlemen..let's run down the 10 bouts out of 12 that have been signed for Halloween Hell this Sunday in Dallas.. 1. In the Main Event, For The World Title: Royce Gracie vs. Vader 2. For the World Tag Titles: The NWO's Rock-n-Roll Express vs. Road Warriors, but this bout is 2-out-of-3 falls, no DQ, no Time Limit 3. For The International Title: Andre The Giant vs. Ric Flair 4. For The International Tag Titles: The Outsiders vs. The Steiners 5. For The TV Title: Sid vs. Bret Hart, Inside A Steel Cage! 6. Bruno Sammartino vs. Sandman 7. Rude/Blanchard vs. Bruiser/Crusher 8. For The Cruiserweight Title, 2-Cold Scorpio vs. Chris Benoit 9. In A Special Triangle Match to unify the Ladies and "Intergender Titles", in the first bout, Andy Kaufman goes against Candi Divine, with the winner getting Ladies Champion Bull Nakano. These 2 bouts will be no-time limit and no-DQ. And, if either lady is the winner, the Intergender Title may never be "claimed" in the URWA by Kaufmann. But meanwhile, should Kaufmann win both bouts, the Ladies Title will offically become the Intergender Title. 10. Cactus Jack vs. Sabu in a special No-DQ match! Some quick thoughts on the matches already signed for Halloween Hell: --Gracie, even though he's had a cold spell as of late in the URWA, gave Vader his toughest test for the world title. And, how big a factor will the NWO play in this one? -- The Rock-n-Rolls could very easily go into Dallas as challengers as the Road Warriors and Rock-n-Rolls meet again in Nashville Thursday for the World Tag Titles.. And: -- Rumor has it that there may be a reunion amongst the Horseman here in the URWA, with more details on that as they occur.. That's all for now, fans, join us Thursday in the Music City of Nashville for the next Ringside Report..
