Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

World Wrestling Asociation — misspelled in its own name from the start — introduced itself in a recruitment post on 29 September 1996: “yOU GUYS WANTS COMPETITION??? COME IN THE WWA!!!” It dated its own opening to that 8 August, staffed a front office of a president and vice president, and put its whole philosophy in one line: “THE MOST YOU ROLEPLAY, THE MOST YOU WILL WIN!!!”

By its own account it fielded 37 singles wrestlers and 11 tag teams, and had already crowned five champions before its first served card: Prisonner 109 held the World Heavyweight Title, The Ringmaster the Internet Title, Jay “Crash” Clark the Xtreme Title, Evil Ones the World Tag Team Titles, and The Rising Sons the Xtreme Tag Team Titles. Its shows ran three formats a week: Monday Night Carnage (“Raw style”), a Saturday Megastars card (“Superstars style”), and a Sunday Xtreme! card where, in its own words, every match that night was No Holds Barred.

Two ring-named commentators worked most cards: Little Dom, billed face, and Handsome JB, billed heel — Little Dom called all three of its weekly shows, Handsome JB joined him on Monday Night Carnage and Sunday Xtreme!, and a third, neutral voice sat in on Carnage and the Saturday Megastars card alike. New talent came in on a standing application — up to three wrestlers or tag teams per person, no more giants over seven feet, no powerbomb finishers, and, already fielding thirty-seven wrestlers of its own, a hard line against real ones: “I DON'T ACCEPT ANYMORE REAL WRESTLERS.”

How it fared after this one post isn't in the surviving record — no card, result or further document survives beyond this founding announcement.
