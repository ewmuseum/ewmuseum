Introduction The X-tremely Violent Wrestling Organization(XVWO) originally opened on December 28, 1997, but officially closed on February 1, 1998. It re-opened on
April 4, 1999, after over a year on the shelves. While our name implies that we are violent, we are actually more of a traditional federation. Hardcore matches will be fought every now and then, but for the most part wrestlers will fight pure scientific matches. We are booked, and the result of a match is determined by role-playing. (See Part 3.)
2. Rules ***The XVWO will employ a "Three Strikes and You're Out" rule. This means that for the first time you break a rule, you will be given a warning. On your second warning, you may be stripped of any titles you hold. On your third offense, you will be thrown out of the federation. This applies to all rules except the role-playing rule. The role-playing rule has its own set of consequences. NO EXCEPTIONS.
The Role-playing Rule: You must role-play at least once per week, and you are recommended to role-play at least twice per week. If you fail to do one role-play per week, first I will job you unmercifully. (I may even job you to
Monty Goldturd or Iceberg. See Part 7 about those guys.) Then, if three weeks have passed and you still haven't role-played, I will do one of two things:
(a)Just flat out fire you, or (b)Sign you into a "Loser Leaves XVWO" match. There are other rules for this federation. Read carefully...and adhere to the rules. 1. Cursing is allowed in role-plays...but to an extent. Anything that you are not allowed to say on TV, don't bother to say...either find a different word or use a mark like [CENSORED], [bleep], etc.
2. A handler is not allowed to have any more than 2 wrestlers. (NOTE: I will let it slide if you have one singles wrestler and two wrestlers that wrestle
STRICTLY as a tag team.)
3. You cannot complain to me about a loss. I explain the system by which matches are decided in Part 3. 4. You may not use other wrestlers in a flash without their permission. By this I mean, you can't just beat up a wrestler in a flash. Other wrestlers may be used as "props", meaning they merely appear somewhere, but that's about it.
5. You are allowed to talk about the President in a flash, but you can't attack the Prez...saying "I hate you, Stephenson!" is okay, whereas "Freddy picks up
Stephenson and puts him in the Rack" is not.
6. You are not allowed to enter "real" wrestlers or other figures that most people have heard of...i.e., you can't be Lex Luger or Richard Nixon. You may use a "real" wrestler's finisher, but unless it's the name of a regular move, you have to rename it. (i.e., the "Soviet Suplex" is acceptable; the "Rock
Bottom" is not.)
7. Attacks are allowed...send your idea to me, but don't make it too wacky. For example, coming out and hitting someone with a chair is okay; blowing someone's head off with a bazooka isn't. Also, I may turn down an attack if I have something planned for the wrestler whom you want to attack.
Okay...follow these rules and everything should be just fine. 3. How Matches Are Decided Matches are decided STRICTLY by how hard you work for the match. Specifically, here is how I determine the winner of a match.
1. Each role-play you send in is judged on a scale of 1-10, based mainly on content. -A role-play that is 10 lines or less gets a 1, and a role-play less than 20 lines can get no better than a 5. -Other than that, length has little relevance...a 100-line role-play can get a low score if it makes no sense, and a 30-line role-play can get a high score if it gets your point across.
2. The scores of your role-plays are totaled up and averaged. If you have done 2 or more role-plays(even if they are 5 lines long) this number is multiplied by 2. -The rules recommend that you do 2 role-plays per week; this is why. 3. If you send in a strategy for the match, one point is added to your score. -A strategy is a short summary of what you want to do in a match. Its main purpose is to help me write the match, but it can help you win a close match. Also, angles do not play a role in the actual result of a match. For example, say that Johnny Green has a match with Seth Porter, and Willy Jackson wants to start an angle with Johnny Green. This will not affect the winner of the match:
if Green out-flashes Porter, he'll win by DQ or Jackson's interference will backfire. If Porter out-flashes Green, Jackson's interference will lead to
Porter getting the pin.
4. Weekly Events Monday: Monday Night Massacre-Monday Night Massacre is a show that alternates between commentary and summary format. The two announcers, Rick Winston and
Steve Falcon, give commentary between the matches, but the matches are given in summary format. Usually, every wrestler in the federation will have a match on
Massacre.
Tuesday: The Black Sand Show-The Black Sand Show is the federation's flash show, hosted by Black Sand, himself a former wrestler and one of our announcers. The show airs on Tuesday, Thursday, and Sunday.
Wednesday: Off day Thursday: The Black Sand Show Friday: Friday Night Fury-Our main weekly show, which is in full commentary format. The announcers on the show are Rick Winston and Tom Stephenson. It will usually feature the federation's top wrestlers. There will generally be about six televised matches; all other matches will be dark matches. Televised matches will be determined by (1)the rankings, and (2)role-playing.
Saturday: Weekly Update-This is a show that recaps the week's events, and looks at matchups for upcoming shows. Hosted by Black Sand.
Sunday: The Black Sand Show(Weekend) 5. Titles XVWO World Heavyweight Title: This is the federation's major title. A wrestler must be ranked in the top five contenders to challenge for it. It must be defended at least once per month.
XVWO Television Title: The federation's secondary title. Any wrestler can challenge for it, and it must be defended each week on Fury. A wrestler must be a good role-player to hold onto it for a long time.
XVWO World Lightweight Title: The federation's title for lightweights(under 235 pounds). Its holder must defend it twice per month. You must be under 235 pounds to challenge for it.
XVWO World Tag Team Titles: The federation's tag team titles. Any team may challenge for them. They must be defended at least twice per month.
6. The J.O.B. Order Monty Goldturd-He is a 'roided monster who tries to make quick work of his opponent...but usually fails.
Iceberg-He has one of the most spectacular entrances in all of wrestling, but generally it's longer than the match.
