Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Superslam began as one promoter's answer to a gap on the calendar: a single night built on no federation's own continuity but on the accumulated best of many, chosen by application for how well a wrestler could carry a promo and a strat rather than for what banner he wrestled under.

Its second night ran at the Air Canada Centre in Toronto under a host federation's own broadcast team, its ring announcer and its commentary booth borrowed for the evening the same way its wrestlers were.

A six-way pregame scramble decided the tournament's final seed, and eight first-round matches followed it, ending in a submission, a no-show forfeit, and a squash finished in seconds.

The night closed on a war of attrition between two heavyweight champions, one escaping a finishing hold once before submitting to it a second time, to the loudest ovation of the evening.

A second and third night were promised before this record breaks off: a champion still to be crowned, a ladder match still to be fought, and a hall of fame with one name in it, waiting on a second.
