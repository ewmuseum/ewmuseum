Superslam 2 - Host Federation and Venue Announced, and More

7 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Congratulations to the I-Slash, IWF/WOW, as they will host Superslam 2. SS2 will take place from the Air Canada Centre in Toronto, Ontario, Canada. And just one other quick note. I have plenty of apps in already with people wanting to be in the tournament. If I could get 8 or 9 more, we could do a _HUGE_ 32-man tournament. So get some damn apps in, everybody! The deadline is September 22. Thanks!
Pete ******************************************************************************** Note: Send all applications to [detail withheld] WHO ARE YOU? My name is Pete, and I handle "Deadly" Derek Irvin and the tag team Debonair in such feds as BSCW, NEO, and GWC/MPW. I've been e-wrestling for almost two years now, but I've really only been off the crappy, two sentence match feds and in the good feds for a year now.
WHAT IS SUPERSLAM? [IC] Superslam is an independent wrestling supercard started by former wrestler and Superslam promoter Chris Chronic in late 2001. The first
Superslam, held in April of 2002, was a great success, due to the unbelievable matches that took place in the Fleet Center in Boston,
Massachussetts. A location for Superslam 2 has yet to be announced, as officials are ironing out the details. What _IS_ for sure is that
Superslam 2 will be a night to remember, a gathering of the best talent around for some of the best wrestling ever seen.
WHAT IS SUPERSLAM? [OOC] Superslam is a supecard, bringing together the best talent in all of e-wrestling. It is going to become _THE_ supercard of all supercards if I have my way. The first Superslam was a tremendous success, and I only look to make the show bigger and better over the next few efforts. Superslam 2 is going to be huge, make no doubt about it.
WHY SHOULD I PARTICIPATE? Fun. Duh, that's what it's all about. So whether you have fun my getting some bragging rights, or by RP-ing and stratting against some of your friends, Superslam is going to be a blast. I remember that the first ever time my character, Derek Irvin, got any attention was in
[name withheld]'s Masters of the Ring VI. So whether you're an unknown looking for attention, or one of the darlings of the RSPWF rankings, participate in Superslam and have a good time.
WHAT ARE THE DIFFERENT ELEMENTS OF SUPERSLAM? Glad you asked. Or, glad I asked, I guess I should say. First and foremost, there is the Superslam tournament. Last year's winner was
Brian Taylor, one of the most underrated men in e-wrestling. This year the next champion will be crowned. I still haven't ironed out all the details, as I'd rather vary from the traditional 8 or 16 man tournament setup this year and give SS2 something that sets it apart from other supercards. Some people have suggested giving each match a gimmick, and I've had a few other ideas, but we'll see. One thing is for sure, however, and that is a new Superslam champion _WILL_ be decided this year.
Next, we have the 5-Star Showcases. These are the mega-matches from the world of e-wrestling. Last year Ben Tuckwell defeated Kirk McLean in one _HELL_ of a pure wrestling match, and Spikyjim defeated Loaded in a bloody, brutal, explosive battle. This is to give the fans some dream matches that they either wouldn't otherwise see in e-wrestling, or to settle longstanding feuds, as in Spikyjim/Loaded's case.
Last year, there was a $25,000 ladder match, which I found to be rather fun. I'd hope to have another at SS2.
And finally, the match. The final match of Superslam 2. It will pit the Superslam 2 champion against the Superslam 1 champion, Brian
Taylor, in a matchup to see who will be the first man ever entered into the Superslam Hall of Fame.
HOW DO YOU DECIDE WHO WINS EACH MATCH? Well, that's done by me. So if you have any grudges after the show, hold them against me. Several people did last year. ;)
First of all, you'll do three promos. This is the bulk of what will be used to determine who wins and who loses. It's not about quantity, it's about quality. Whoever does the most consistent, best work will most likely win. I'd say the RP's determine about two-thirds of what happens at Superslam. The next big chunk is through strats. Simply put, you need to strat if you want to win. Besides, it helps your character come across the way you want he or she to, so why wouldn't you? And finally, in case of virtual ties, optional pre-show promos will be taken into consideration. These are very short prematch backstage promos that you will write before the show.
WHAT ELSE DO I NEED TO KNOW? Well, after you do your promos, then it's my time to shine. Okay, our time to shine. Last year at Superslam I wrote about 15 of 18 matches, or something insane like that. This year I'd really like your help.
So, I will alot plenty of time for handlers to write matches, and for myself to write matches, and we will put out one hell of a product.
Take a look at last year's Superslam if you have any doubts. The quality of the card speaks for itself, and is the one thing I pride myself on. You can have a tournament full of all the big names and big matches you want, but if the writing sucks, it _SUCKS_. Superslam won't suck, guaranteed. In fact, it'll be the best supercard out there. Writing will not factor into who wins and who loses, but will be greatly appreciated.
If you have a gripe with me after Superslam 2 about how your character was portrayed, what happened, etc, please email me and I'll talk to you about it.
I have not ironed out an exact schedule yet for Superslam, but I will do so instead after the application deadline. All I know is that it won't be as compact as the first Superslam, in effort to make life a little bit easier for all those involved. You'll have more time to write promos and do strats, and I'll have more time to write matches.
Rest assured, Superslam is a card that _WILL_ be out on time, just like last year's show. I will post a link soon for those of you who want to see the Superslam 1 results.
And finally, I need a host federation for Superslam 2. Last year's host was Blood, Sweat, and Chairs Wrestling, and I'd like to have a different host federation this year. The host federation will have their announcers do the matches, their backstage interview guys do the interviews, and have their fans in the audience cheering for their wrestlers. So all interested fedheads, contact me.
