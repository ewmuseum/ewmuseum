Superslam 2 - A Note From the Promoter

28 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Since I'm the guy who runs Superslam, I figured I should make a post here. First off, the predictions seemed to cross the line of what predictions should actually be - if you wanna give opinions, fine, but there's no need to be a downright asshole to people publicly. However,
I received emails from Andj and Walls saying they apologize for what got stirred up on RSPWF about SS2. Was Frank right in defending guys like Lane and Jim? Damn right he was. Just do me a favor and settle this shit through email or IRC or whatever.
See,I don't want Superslam 2 to be about flame wars, OOC battles, politics, or all that bullshit. I'm gonna do my best to put out the best damn supercard that's out there, period. And in the end, who cares what anyone thinks of your character? Well, except me, of course, since I can _JOB YOU ALL_~!
*Pete cackles* [detail withheld]
