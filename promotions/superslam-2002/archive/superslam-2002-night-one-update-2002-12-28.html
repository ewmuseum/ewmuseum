Superslam 2 - Update! (Spoilers!)

28 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
If you haven't read night one of Superslam 2 yet, and intend to, then go back now... -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Results of night one of Superslam 2! In a pregame show match, Steve Liermann defeated Ryan Delaney, Syko,
Stevie Ray Murdoch, Steve Jensen and Seth Alexander when he pinned
Alexander.
-In the opening match of the show, Spikyjim defeated Liermann in a first round tournament match, after hitting the Living End in a terrific match. -Randy Love made Jeff Keenan tap out to the Lovelock in less than three minutes in their first round tournament matchup. -Robert Kellan survived a good outing from Vile Vince Viper, hitting a three point stance lariat and the Lonestar Lariat for the pinfall. -In the fourth first round tournament match, Simon Underwood pinned Allen Donelson in a great matchup, after piledriving Donelson on the floor outside the ring and hitting the Transcendant Exposure back inside the ring for the pinfall.
-Despair made Chad Allen tap out to the Human Torment in just a few minutes to move on to the quarterfinals. -"Bastard Deluxe" Andy James made Mike Steele tap out to an STF, after Steele's wife was jumped by rival Ryan Delaney. -Ktulu destroyed Chance Branigan in a matter of seconds, to move on to the quarterfinals. -Corey Irons beat Justin Arcola when Arcola didn't show up for the match. Irons called out anybody from the back, and second night opponent Despair came out, sparking a brawl between the two men.
-And, in an amazing main event, Rosco Riggins made Ben Tuckwell tap to the Checkmate...words can't do this match justice...you simply have to see it! With that, we move on to night two of Superslam! We'll see _four_ quarterfinal matches in the Superslam 2 tournament! In match number one, the hardcore, psycho, blue-haired legendary freak himself takes on the super dick, the asshole, the "Bastard Deluxe!"
Who the hell will the fans root for when _SPIKYJIM_ takes on _ANDY
JAMES_? Match number two will feature the super-popular and arguably fastest-rising star in the business, as the "Extreme War Machine"
_COREY IRONS_ takes on one of the most underrated superstars in our game today...a high-flying, hardcore, brooding psycho who wants to prove to the world that he _is_ the real deal..._DESPAIR_!
Match number three will feature two of the hottest wrestlers in our sport today...the family man, the good guy, the rough and tough Texan himself, _ROBERT KELLAN_ will step into the ring with one of the most vicious, brutal, battle-tested warriors our sport has ever seen, in
_KTULU_! Ktulu _annihilated_ Chance Branigan at night one...will
Kellan simply become another one of Ktulu's victims? Or will Kellan's awesome mix of agility and big-man _power_ be too much for Ktulu to handle?
Match number four will feature a great contrast of styles between two men who know each other _very_ well...the creature of the dark himself, _SIMON UNDERWOOD_ will step into the ring, looking to expose the blonde-haired, good-looking, good-natured" "Nature Boy" himself, when he takes on _RANDY LOVE_ in quarterfinal action! Love had an easy time with Jeff Keenan in round one, but Underwood is _far_ from being a pushover...who will move on to the semifinals?
We'll also see a triple threat match featuring representatives of the three teams that will take each other on in _FED WARS_ at night two!
And with all the teams at ringside, you know this one will be _CRAZY_! And finally, the _MAIN EVENT_. The biggest match Superslam has ever had, right here, _tonight_! We will see the man many consider
_unbeatable_, the monster himself, _REMBRANDT_! And his opponent? The former GLCW world heavyweight champion and tag team champion, the
"Spiteful Superstar," the man who pulled off the "Mad Hatter" trick, the master of the Zero Heat Kick~! himself..._TRIPP SHADE_!!!
What a night it'll be...you won't want to miss a minute of it!
