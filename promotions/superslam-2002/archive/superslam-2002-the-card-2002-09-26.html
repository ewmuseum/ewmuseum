Superslam 2 - live, from the Air Canada Centre in Toronto, Canada ********************************************************************************************
Okay folks, the card for Superslam 2 is below. Thanks to all who applied, sorry I couldn't accept everybody. Here's how the schedule will work.
Sunday, October 6 - first RP is due at midnight eastern time (These
RP's will be assembled into an RP packet that will be sent out the following day.)
Sunday, October 20 - second RP is due at midnight eastern time, same format as the first RP. Strats for Night One are also due.
Sunday, October 27 - short pregame show oncards and RP's are due. Match assignments will be sent out to writers. Hopefully, around November 10, Night One will come out. Depending on how many writers I have, I'll update you then on when the rest of the card will come out.
So, your responsibilities are: 2 regular RP's. They can be held anywhere, yadda yadda yadda. RP's will be judged based upon quality, not quantity. They will be composed into RP packets. This is the most important part of what you do.
Strats: For each night your character is involved in Superslam, a strat will be expected. A strat is composed of an entrance, a general strategy, some spots, reaction to win and loss, and anything else you'd like to add. The better the strat, the better your character will be portrayed.
Pregame show RP's/oncards. These should be short, one per every night of Superslam your character is involved in. Have your character in or around the Air Canada Centre, whether it be him arriving or in his locker room or doing a backstage interview. Guys that have Five Star
Showcase matches can have oncards earlier on in nights they are not participating to pump their match, if they'd like.
So, you have until October 6 to do that first RP. Send everything to
[detail withheld] . If you have any questions, would like to get the app for your opponent, or need to know anything, just write me back. I will try to be getting out a full roster for you guys to see later on, with in depth detail about each wrestler, but if you have any questions, just ask. Thanks!
