EPW Advertisment, 2 October 1997

2 October 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
{ Fade in... } { SCENE: The screen opens with a shot of a handsome man standing in front of the camera and cradling what seems to be a solid gold title in his arms. Behind him, is a large piece of white canvas. On the canvas, you clearly see the what is written on it in HUGE black letters. EPW. Coughing a little to deepen his voice, quickly fixing his tie, then flashing a sparkling smile to the audience, he begins... } [name withheld] : Hello, my name is [name withheld]. I am president of the EPW, an upcoming fed in the wrestling world. The fed has yet to open, but will be starting in the near future. We, here at EPW, are the _future_ of wrestling. ( Points to the belt. ) Do you want a shot at this? ( Holds up belt ) Do you want a shot at one of the most prized titles in the world today?? Do you want to be remembered as a _LEGOND_? If so, try your ability. Try your skill. Put yourself to the test and see if you have what it takes to make it... Or are you just another one of those over rated jobbers who nevers gets up and does anything?? { Drops the belt } If you want a shot at _this_, a shot at the _glory_ and _envy_ of every man in the wrestling world today, then step up to the plate and take it. I _dare_ you... The belt is right here in front of me. ( Pauses ) All you have to do is sign your name on the dotted line and you could be the very next EPW champion. As of now, everyone has a shot at the belt in the title tornement. Still, only you can make it happen... There is no reason why not too. So don't be lazy, get back in shape, get off you retired a** and get moving into the EPW. EPW -=- Extreme Pro Wrestling -=- EPW Join Today!!! Contact [detail withheld] to Join ( Flashes over the screen. ) Wrestlers and Writers needed!!! { Fade to Black... }
