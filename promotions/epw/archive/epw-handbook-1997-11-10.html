This fed will be a booked fed aka an RPing league. The rules and regs for RPing will inclosed in there own speacial section, so I really do not have the time to go into them now. Well, all I can say is RECRUT and enjoy your time here at EPW. CARDS AND TITLES!!!!! -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- Cards: SUNDAY NIGHT SHOWCASE: Every Sunday we will have Sunday Night Showcase. This will be the weeks big card having 4-7 matches and maybe a few darks. On most nights one of the titles will be defended and a few big things will happen. WEDNESDAY WARZONE: A smaller card having only two or three live matches and a bunch of darks. In this we will start and push angles for the big Sunday Night Card. No titles will be defended. Usually used for rookies to appearances. Titles: WORLD HEAVYWIEGHT TITLE: The most prestigous title that the EPW has to offer. If you can achieve the honor of holding this title, then you are sure to be at the forefront of the wrestling world. Must be defended once a month or stripped from the holder. If a holder is stripped, then the Northeastern Title holder and a man picked by the league officals, usually the top ranked contender, will fight it out. NORTHEASTERN TITLE: The second biggest title in the world of singles wrestling. With this title you are ranked second in the federation next to the champ. Must be defended once a month. If stripped, then the number one contender and a person decided by the president will fight. Usually either a person on a hot streak or the number two contender. WORLD TAG TEAM TITLES: The most prestigous title that a tag team can win. The only title avalible to tag teams and probubly one of the hardest titles to asertain. Most be defended once a month of the number one and two contending tag teams will fight for the right to be called champion. _RP RULES_: ( Layout for rules created by MLWO. Changed by [name withheld]. ) -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- - A wrestler is allowed as many roleplays a week as they feel they need to do. They do not have to be done in one day, but they must be in by 4:00 PM on the day before the card to count. All could be done and sent on your last day. This will definitely put an emphasis on quality. - Wrestlers may also do one character development roleplay (Sorta like a look into their history) that will NOT affect their matches in any way. Therefore, they don't count :) Still, they are a good way to show past actions and give everyone a better picture of why your character is the way he is. - Also, you may do one In Ring interview. These are like the interviews where your wrestler comes down on the card, into the ring (hence the name in ring), and does an interview. If you do not want to do an in ring, you can do a short rp that will be put in a card for pre-match comments. Usually do not affect a match unless my disgression sees differently. - Lastly, angles and strats will now partially affect the outcome of the match. In an extremely close match, I will go to the angles to see if something would make my decision better, especially if it saves me from declaring a draw.
