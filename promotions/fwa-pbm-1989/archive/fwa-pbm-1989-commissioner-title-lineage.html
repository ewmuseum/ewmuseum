FWA Commissioner Title Lineage

The alliance's own title-history spreadsheet keeps a "Commissioner Title Archives" section for each territory listed below: interim titles the alliance's own commissioner created between a territory's ordinary championship reigns, later permanently retired. Two of the Georgia Elite Wrestling territory's own titles are stated outright, on the same spreadsheet, as spin-offs of two titles from the earlier, unrelated IWF play-by-mail game; their earliest holders carry the exact billed names of that game's own former champions.
