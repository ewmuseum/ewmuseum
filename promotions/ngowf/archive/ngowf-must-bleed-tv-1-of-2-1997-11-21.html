[NGOWF] Must Bleed TV (1 of 2)

21 November 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
THE NEW GLOBAL ONLINE WRESTLING FEDERATION PRESENTS THE PREMIERE OF MUST BLEED TV -Live! From The Norfolk Scope in Norfolk, Virginia- (We see the capacity crowd in The Scope as the amazing fireworks go off. A video screen is showing memorable events from the old Global Online Wrestling Federation. As the clip montage ends, the words "We're Back" come up on the screen. The camera zooms in to NGOWF commentators Al Hunter and Nicholas Van Buren.) NVB: Hello everyone! I'm Nicholas Van Buren! AH: And I'm Al Hunter. NVB: We're back with a vengeance, folks! AH: Let's cut the chit chat and get some underway with some fast and furious wrestling action! Take it away, Mr. Ring Announcer!!! Ring Announcer: This match is set for one fall with a 30 minute time limit. It's a first round match-up in the NGOWF World Tag Team Title Tournament! Already in the ring, at a total combined weight of 465 lbs, from parts unknown... THE MIGHTY GLADIATORS!!! AH: I wonder if these masked guys are related to The Conquistadors. ("Sweet Home Alabama" by Lynyrd Skynyrd begins to play over the loud speakers.) Ring Announcer: Their opponents... coming down the aisle with their manager Elsie May... from Tuscaloosa, Alabama... at a total combined weight of 457 lbs... "GENTLEMAN" THOMAS BRODERICK AND "BAYOU" BILLY BURMEISTER... THE SOUTHERN HEARTBREAKERS!!!!! AH: Elsie is looking ravishing tonight. NVB: They aren't real, Al. AH: I know, I know. NVB: The Mighty Gladiators not even waiting for the bell! They're already pounding on Broderick and Burmeister! AH: The Heartbreakers are fighting back! A Broderick dropkick sends Gladiator #1 over the top rope! Burmeister bulldogs #2!! Cover... 1.......................................... KICKOUT!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! NVB: Billy Burmeister not wasting any time. He picks Gladiator #2 up for a bodyslam! Down he goes! Tag to Broderick! AH: Broderick is mounting the top turnbuckle! FLYING LEGDROP!!!!!!!!!!!!! NVB: Broderick tags Burmeister. Broderick got Gladiator #2 in position for a piledriver. AH: And Burmeister is perching himself on the top turnbuckle. NVB: SPIKE PILEDRIVER! OUCH!! THAT'S GOTTA HURT!! AH: Burmeister covers Gladiator Part Deux for the pin... 1.......................................... 2.......................................... 3!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! NVB: Impressive!!! AH: The Southern Heartbreakers score the pinfall and advance in the World Tag Team Title Tournament. Ring Announcer: The winner of the match at three minutes and twenty seven seconds... THE SOUTHERN HEARTBREAKERS!!!!!!!!!!!!!! NVB: Thomas Broderick and Billy Burmeister are a step closer to becoming the very first NGOWF World Tag Team Champions. AH: The ring is cleared so let's head back down to ringside for our next match. Ring Announcer: This next match is a first round match for the NGOWF World Tag Team Title Tournament. It's set for one fall with a 30 minute time limit. ("Rock & Roll Hoochie Coo" by Rick Derringer begins to play throughout The Norfolk Scope.) AH: Oh God! It's Rick Derringer! I hope to hell that they don't play "Real American" too. Ring Announcer: Coming down the aisle... from Saratoga, New York... at a total combined weight of 505 lbs... JOHNNY CARWASH & FREDDIE SUNSHINE... THE ROCK & ROLL BRIGADE!!! AH: Carwash and Sunshine. They sound like a law firm for clowns. ("More Human Than Human" begins to play over the loud speakers.) Ring Announcer: Their opponents... from Miami and Fort Lauderdale, Florida, respectively... at a total combined weight of 550 lbs... BRU-DAWG & PRIME... DAMAGE CONTROL!!!!!!!! AH: Look at these guys. They're monsters!!! NVB: It looks like Bru-Dawg and Johnny Carwash will be starting the match. AH: They lock up. Kick to the midsection by Bru-Dawg! NVB: Another one!! AH: SNAP SUPLEX BY BRU-DAWG!!! NVB: Bru-Dawg picks Carwash up by the hair. Standing dropkick!! Wow!! AH: Bru-Dawg has an incredible amount of agility. NVB: Bru-Dawg tags out to Prime. AH: Here comes the big guy. NVB: Prime picks Carwash up by the neck. WAIT!!! Here comes Freddie Sunshine!! AH: Prime has HIM by the neck now!! OH MY GOD!!! DOUBLE CHOKESLAM BY PRIME!!!!!!!! NVB: Unbelievable, Al!! He did that with an amazing amount of ease!! AH: Prime picks Sunshine and throws him over the top. Prime shifts his attention back to Carwash. He scoops Carwash up... BACKBREAKER!!! NVB: I heard Carwash's bones snap all the way over here. AH: Prime has Carwash like a rag doll. NVB: Why doesn't he just finish him off? AH: Maybe he wants to break a few more bones, Nick. NVB: Prime has Carwash in position for what appears to be a piledriver!! NOOOOO!!! AH: POWERBOMB!!!!!!! NVB: Prime lifts Carwash up by the hair and tosses him into the corner. AH: That crazy S.O.B. wants Carwash to tag Sunshine in. NVB: Sunshine is ignoring his own tag team partner! AH: He's afraid of Prime!!! HA HA HA!!!!!! NVB: Prime is going over to their corner. He's got them both by the hair!!! HE RAMS THEIR HEADS TOGETHER!!! AH: I'd say that counts as a tag, Nick. HA HA HA HA HA!!!! NVB: Prime pulls Freddie Sunshine in through the ropes--- AH: And he clotheslines Carwash over the top rope!!! THIS MAN IS SOMETHING ELSE!!! NVB: NECKBREAKER BY PRIME!!! AH: Yet, he doesn't go for the pin. He just picks Sunshine up by the hair. Belly-to-Belly Suplex!!!!!!!!! NVB: Prime picks Sunshine back up again. Prime tags Bru-Dawg in. Prime is perching Freddie Sunshine on his shoulders. AH: Bru-Dawg climbing to the top turnbuckle... NVB: FRANKENSTEINER BY BRU-DAWG!!!!! AH: I've been doing my homework, Nick. They call that "High Tide". NVB: Who are you? Mike Tenay? AH: Bru-Dawg covers him and hooks the leg. 1.......................................... 2.......................................... 3!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! NVB: He got 'em!!! Ring Announcer: The time of the match... Four minutes and seventeen seconds... your winners... DAMAGE CONTROL!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! AH: Let's keep the ball rollin'. On to our next match! NVB: Alright folks. Here we go! Some more action! AH: Time for the first round matches in the NGOWF World Heavyweight Championship Tournament. Ring Announcer: This next match is set for one fall with a 20 minute time limit... already in the ring... weighing in at 265 lbs... from California... here's SWEET PEA!!!!! AH: Mr. Pea blessed himself with a classic professional name. ("Burning Bridges" by E.L.P. pipes through the arena's loud speaker system.) NVB: Here comes the baddest man in the land, Al. Ring Announcer: His opponent is from Dundee, Scotland... weighing in at 261 lbs... "DANGEROUS" DOUG MILNE!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! AH: Who? NVB: One of the meanest S.O.B.'s that I've ever seen. He was in the original GOWF and he hauled some major ass in his matches. AH: I suddently feel awfully sorry for Mr. Pea. Speaking of whom, there he goes!!! He's attacking Doug Milne and there's the bell!!! NVB: Major mistake by Sweet Pea. AH: Sweet Pea sends Doug Milne into the ropes! NVB: Sweet Pea misses with a clothesline! AH: But Doug Milne didn't!! Milne's clothesline almost took his head off!! NVB: Milne helps Sweet Pea up. Irish whip into the corner by Milne! AH: Clothesline into the corner by Milne! Sweet Pea is down! NVB: Milne mounting the ropes. Sweet Pea is slowly rising to his feet again. AH: MILNE LAUNCHES AND MISSES WITH A FLYING LARIAT!!!!!!!! NVB: Sweet Pea ducked in the nick of time! AH: Pea now climbing to the top turnbuckle. NVB: But Milne is already back on his feet! Milne levels him with a punch and Sweet Pea lands crotch first onto the top turnbuckle!!! OH MAN!!! He's gonna do it!!!! AH: What??? What??? What in the hell are you talking about, Nick?? NVB: DANGEROUS DROP!!!!!!!!!!!!!! Milne almost decapitated him with that!!!! AH: Milne rolls him onto his back and covers him. 1.......................................... 2.......................................... 3!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! NVB: "Dangerous" Doug Milne hasn't lost any of his killer instinct since I last saw him! Ring Announcer: The time of the match... three minutes and fifty seconds... your winner... "DANGEROUS" DOUG MILNE!!! AH: Milne showing an impress--- NVB: What's this??? AH: It's "Bruiser" Bob Todd!!!!!!!!!!! NVB: Bob Todd has come out of the crowd and he's heading towards the ring. AH: Nick, do you have any clue what's going on? NVB: Bob Todd was supposed to do an interview with me later on this evening. I guess he wants to say what he has to say now. AH: He just swiped the microphone off of the Ring Announcer. NVB: Back in the original GOWF, Bob Todd had an intense feud with "Dangerous" Doug Milne. Let's see what he has to say. Bob Todd: Well well well... what do we have here? You certainly get around, don't you Douglas? First, I see your tired ass upon my return to UEW and now this. Have you been following me?? HAHAHAHAHA!!! NVB: Bob Todd playing with fire here. You anger Doug Milne enough and you end up with a huge fireball!! BT: Enough joking. I'm here to make a challenge. I don't want to wrestle you. That's for damn sure. If you want me, you can have me in UEW. I have no need for this bloody second rate organizations and I as sure as hell won't wrestle here! But I've been training someone. I've been training him to follow in my footsteps. I've already had the pleasure of kicking your ass. Now I want him to have that same satisfaction. NVB: Bob Todd has a protege?? AH: I wonder who it is. BT: Now I know all of this information is too much for your brain to handle at once. That's why I'm giving you a little time to think about it. Don't take too long, ya git! Or my man may have to just find you and beat you on his own terms!!! Either way, your ass is gonna be fried! NVB: With that said, it's time for us to take a break!
