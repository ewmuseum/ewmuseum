All These Feds... They ALL SUCK!

30 November 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Ya know what. I`ve been around on this RSPWF Board for longer then a year (under different screen names) and the past few months have been nothing but crap on here. Not all of `em however. Just the ones you can actually get into. I was in NGOWF for a few weeks and it was okay... at first. But after I saw a card it sucked. The president of the place is a loser. Only in one way. And it goes like this: Me and this other guy, Lucien Champion, had a pretty good RPing battle that was one of the better ones I`ve had in the last month. We were pulling out some pretty awesome RPs. Then I see the card. SHIT. Our two RPs together had more quality and more length then the damn card. Then About two or three months ago I joined NeCW. Now this was a Great Fed. And I do mean GREAT. But About a week after it was confirmed that I did join I found out that I lost a match I never knew about. I was pretty upset and quit. Which is something I honestly Regret. But NeCW Sucks in that way. You should join NeCW if you can. But the ________________________ (bottom line) is that I have a challenge for all you Federations out here. Gimmie what ya got. I`m not in any feds right now and I need to be in one... Maybe two. I`ve held alot of AOL fed titles such as: CIW Tag Titles, CIW Norh American Title, IEUW TV Title and the ULW World Title. I need a fed that has been around for at least a week. Must have some good competition because I haven`t had much in awhile. I`ve never been in a real totally Hardcore federation so I think I might wanna try one of those out. If you need some one to bring in alot of heat, some talent and anything else, I`m a good investment. And as for this "God" of E-wrestling, I`d like him to KISS MY ASS!
