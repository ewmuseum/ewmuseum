The NGOWF Wants You!

12 November 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
NEW GLOBAL ONLINE WRESTLING FEDERATION ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ I. Introduction Hello, I'm [name withheld]. Some of you may remember me as the president of the gone but not forgotten GOWF. I'm back and I'm relaunching a brand new fantasy wrestling fed. I hope you all take time to read this. I intend on making the NGOWF one of the best fantasy feds around today! II. Rules The New Global Online Wrestling Federation is a ROLEPLAYING fed. That means that you must roleplay in order to achieve success here. There will be no sims or dice used here. ORIGINAL WRESTLERS ONLY! No carbon copies of real wrestlers either. I don't want a dozen "Stone Cold" wannabes running around the NGOWF talking about the asses they're gonna kick! :) THREE wrestlers per member! This can be in any kind of combination. Flashes must be sent to me. If you use the mailing list in order to send out flashes yourself, you will be suspended from the NGOWF for two weeks. III. Titles There will be three singles titles and one set of tag team titles. They are: *NGOWF World Heavyweight Title* The most important title... defended at least once every two weeks. *NGOWF International Title* The stepping-stone to the World Heavyweight Title... defended at least once a week. *NGOWF Television Title* Defended every week at GOWF Monday Night Carnage. *NGOWF World Tag Team Titles* Self explanatory... defended at least once a week. IV. Flashes, Cards, & PPVs There will be two TV cards every week, two weekly interview shows, and a PPV every month. The TV cards are- *NGOWF MONDAY NIGHT CARNAGE* Our Monday night wrestling extravaganza! PPV caliber matches every week! Anything can happen during "Monday Night Carnage"! *NGOWF MUST BLEED TV* Forget "Must See TV"! We have "Must Bleed TV"! It's our Thursday night wrestling show where anything can happen! Interviews, grudge matches, and plenty of suprises! The interview shows are- *NGOWF PRESENTS SCUTTLE'S CORNER* Legendary GOWF, WFW, LAW, and UEW manager Fred Scuttle dishes about news concerning the NGOWF. Comings, goings, feuds, injuries, and PPVs will be discussed! Tons of interviews and flashes featuring many NGOWF superstars! *BETWEEN THE ROPES WITH THE PREZ* Your's truly talks about what's going on in the NGOWF. Plenty of interviews and flashes! -PPVs- Pay-per-views will be announced at least a week and a half before they occur. V. When Will It All Start? No definate date has been set for the NGOWF's launch. Things will get underway as soon as we get enough members. If you're in a fantasy fed currently, publicize the NGOWF! :) VI.
