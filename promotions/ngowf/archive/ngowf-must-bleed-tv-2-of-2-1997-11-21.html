[NGOWF] Must Bleed TV (2 of 2)

21 November 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Nicholas Van Buren: We're back and ready for some more action! Al Hunter: Time for our second first round NGOWF World Title tournament match. Ring Announcer: The next match is set for one fall with a 20 minute time limit... already in the ring... from Wilkes-Barre, Pennsylvania... weighing in at 223 lbs... JIMMY MULVEY!!!!!! ("Sexual Healing" by Marvin Gaye starts to play over the loud speaker.) AH: What is this??? Ring Announcer: His opponent... coming down the aisle... weighing 244 lbs... from Miami, Florida... "THE DOCTOR OF AMORE" BRADLEY FINE!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! AH: Who is this fruit loop? He has the words "I Love You" on his behind! Oh good God... he's calling for the mike! Bradley Fine: I know you all love me. I don't feel like getting my new haircut all messed up so how 'bout we do this... ummm... Jimmy? Both of us know that I've got the superior skill as well as the superior physique so why don't you just forfeit this match? You can write it off as a learning experience. I am, as you know, one of the greatest in the business today. What do you say? NVB: DROPKICK BY MULVEY!! AH: I guess that answered Bradley Fine's question. Jimmy Mulvey is mounting the ropes!!! NVB: Fine is to his feet. AH: FLYING DROPKICK FROM THE TOP ROPE!!!! NVB: Mulvey covers him! 1............................... NO!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! AH: Mulvey barely getting the two count. NVB: Mulvey sends Fine into the ropes with an Irish whip! AH: Mulvey with a high cross body!! He's got him down! 1............................... 2............................... NO!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! NVB: Another kickout by Fine! AH: Mulvey mounting the ropes once again. Bradley Fine is on his feet but he's dazed. NVB: Flying bodypress from the top rope by Mulvey!! AH: Fine caught him in mid air!!!!!!!!!!!! NVB: RUNNING POWERSLAM!!! AH: Fine picks Jimmy Mulvey up by the hair. NVB: SWINGING NECKBREAKER!!! Fine covers him! 1............................... 2............................... FINE LIFTS HIM UP!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! AH: Fine is biting him!!! Poke to the eye by Fine! NVB: Fine sends him into the ropes! AH: FINE CATCHES MULVEY IN A SPINEBUSTER!!! NVB: Fine is climbing to the top. AH: FLYING FISTDROP ONTO MULVEY!!! There's the cover. 1............................... 2............................... HE LIFTS HIM UP AGAIN!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! NVB: What's the deal with this guy? AH: Fine pulls Mulvey back to his feet. He's got him in position for an airplane spin! NVB: NOOOO!!! DIAMOND CUTTER BY FINE!!! He covers him again. 1............................... 2............................... 3!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! AH: Fine scores the pinfall!!! NVB: He could've scored the pinfall earlier but he chose to humiliate Mulvey. AH: I would've made the same choice if I was him. Ring Announcer: The time of the match... two minutes and thirty-nine seconds... your winner... "THE DOCTOR OF AMORE" BRADLEY FINE!!!!!!!!!! AH: God no! Fine has the mike again! Cover your ears!!! BF: "The Doctor of Amore" does it once again. Another one bites the dust. All who face me will succumb to the move of all moves... THE LOVE FOOL!!! Thank you for enjoying my match, everyone! I love you all! Goodnight! NVB: Fine is certainly a loving person. AH: Yeah, I'd love to have him neutered. NVB: Have no fear, fans, another match is here. Let's get to ringside for the pre-match introductions. Ring Announcer: The next contest is set for one fall with a 20 minute time limit. (Sounds of thunder and lighting vibrate through The Norfolk Scope.) AH: There's a storm a-comin', Nick! Lock up the cows in the barn! Ring Announcer: Coming down the aisle... from Parts Unknown... weighing 290 lbs... here is DEATH ANGEL!!! NVB: What a strange individual! ("Triumph" by The Wu-Tang Clan pumps through the arena loud speakers.) Ring Announcer: His opponent... hailing from Buffalo, New York... weighing in at 313 lbs... AKMED JACKSON!!!! AH: Jackson not wasting any time at all! He clotheslines The Death Angel to the mat!! NVB: Akmed gets The Death Angel in position. Can he actually do this to a man who's 290 lbs??? AH: Oh yes he can! HE POWERBOMBS THE ANGEL!!! NVB: Akmed got him up again and he goes for a suplex! NO NO WAIT!!! AH: Akmed places him onto the top turnbuckle! Now he's climbing to the top himself! NVB: What's he gonna do? AH: Akmed demolishing The Death Angel. NVB: There he goes!! TIGER BOMB FROM THE TOP ROPE!!! AH: Jackson really took The Angel for a Plunge Into Hell!!! NVB: The cover... it's over! 1............................... 2............................... 3!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! AH: Akmed Jackson defeating The Death Angel in record time! Ring Announcer: The time of the match... one minute... your winner... AKMED JACKSON!!!!!!!!!!!!! NVB: Earlier this evening, there were three dark matches. They were also first round match-ups for the World Heavyweight Title tournament. Here's Lance Satchel with the recap. Roll the footage. -------------------------------------------------------------------------- ------------------------------------------------ LANCE: The first of the two dark matches was Dark Blood against Dr. Death. It was a virtual see-saw match-up. Both men having similar styles proved to make this quite a challenging match for both competitors. At the 18 minute mark, Dr. Death scored the win with The Decapitator. The next dark match had Lucien Champion facing "The Stud Muffin" Jerry Rebo. Rebo was no match for Champion. After Rebo's initial good start with several aerial maneuvers, Rebo was roughed up by Damian Mazura and Francois Lefebvre outside the ring. Mazura rolled Rebo back into the ring and Champion executed the Super Kick for the pinfall victory at the 3:22 mark. The final dark match had "The Underdog" Jason Giordano facing "Postal" Paul Kruppa. Kruppa went postal on Giordano from the get-go eventually hitting the referee with a chair in the process. Giordano wins via a disqualification at the 4:55 mark. -------------------------------------------------------------------------- ------------------------------------------------ NVB: Alright folks! We're back and it's time for another match-up! AH: Prophet will be facing "Insane" Greg Gatling in what will prove to be one helluva match. NVB: Gatling is already in the ring so let's go to the Ring Announcer for the pre-match introductions. Ring Announcer: Ladies and gentlemen, this match is set for one fall with a 20 minute time limit. Already in the ring... hailing from wherever the hell he wants... weighing in at 279 lbs... "INSANE" GREG GATLING!!!!!!! His opponent, coming down the aisle--- AH: THE SOUTHERN HEARTBREAKERS HAVE JUMPED PROPHET!! NVB: Prophet was on his way to the ring when Broderick and Burmeister jumped him!! They're beating on him with a steel chair!!! AH: Those chairshots are taking their toll on Prophet's right leg!! NVB: Prophet is in some serious pain!! Wait... here comes Damage Control!!!! AH: Damage Control trading blows with The Southern Heartbreakers!! NVB: Prophet is still on the arena floor clutching his leg! The referee is calling for the bell. AH: The match didn't even start! Ring Announcer: The winner of the match... as a result of a count-out... "INSANE" GREG GATLING!!! AH: What? The match didn't even start!! Prophet is definately getting screwed in this decision. Who's the referee? Ray Charles? NVB: Damage Control assisting Prophet to the locker room as the referee raises Gatling's arm in victory. AH: As Gating himself heads to the locker room, let's head to the ring for another match. Ring Announcer: This next match is set for one fall with a 20 minute time limit... entering the ring... from Los Angeles, California... weighing in at 425 lbs... SAINT BRUNO!!!!!! ("Pure Massacre" by Silverchair begins to play throughout the arena.) NVB: Bruno's a big boy. Ring Announcer: His opponent... from Lyme, New Hampshire... weighing in at 250 lbs... JOEL GUTMAN!!! AH: Bruno outweighs Gutman by almost 200 lbs. NVB: There's the bell! They lock up!! AH: Bruno gets sent into the ropes! Savate kick by Gutman! Didn't even faze Bruno in the least! NVB: Gutman runs into the ropes! Bruno picks him up! PRESS SLAM!!!!! BRUNO DROPS HIM RIGHT ON HIS FACE!!!!!!! AH: The big guy picks Gutman back up! He scoops him up for a slam! NO!!!!!!! NVB: SMALL PACKAGE BY GUTMAN!!! 1............................................................ 2............................................................ 3!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! !!!!!!!!! GUTMAN PINNED BRUNO!!! AH: What an upset!!! Gutman scrambles out of there after the three count!! Bruno can't believe it!! NVB: Gutman comes out of nowhere and scores the pinfall over Saint Bruno! Ring Announcer: The time of the match... one minute and three seconds... your winner... JOEL GUTMAN!!!!!!!!!!! NVB: What a way to end tonight's telecast!!! AH: This Monday, the NGOWF premieres what will ultimately become a Monday night wrestling tradition.... MONDAY NIGHT CARNAGE!!! NVB: Let's see what the line-up for this Monday's Carnage is. -------------------------------------------------------------------------- ------------------------------------------------ THE NEW GLOBAL ONLINE WRESTLING FEDERATION PRESENTS THE PREMIERE OF "MONDAY NIGHT CARNAGE" -Live! On November 24th From The Hershey Park Arena in Hershey, Pennsylvania- FEATURED CARD: Two World Tag Team Tournament First Round Matches: Little & Large (Mikey Dobson & Saint Bruno) .vs. The Witt Brothers (Derek Witt & Brett Witt) The Fujiwara Corporation (Masahiro Yama & Taka Saito) .vs. The Boys of Summer (Doyle Kiosky & Jake Snyder) Three World Heavyweight Title Tournament Quarter-Final Matches: Joel Gutman .vs. "The Underdog" Jason Giordano Akmed Jackson .vs. Dr. Death "Dangerous" Doug Milne .vs. "The Doctor of Amore" Bradley Fine -THE MAIN EVENT- OVER THE TOP ROPE BATTLE ROYALE FOR THE TELEVISION TITLE -------------------------------------------------------------------------- ------------------------------------------------ NVB: Until next time, folks, that concludes tonight's edition of "Must Bleed TV"! It's goodnight from me. AH: And it's goodnight from him. Both: Goodnight!
