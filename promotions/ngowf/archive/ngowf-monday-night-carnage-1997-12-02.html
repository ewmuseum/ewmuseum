[NGOWF] Monday Night Carnage

Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
THE NEW GLOBAL ONLINE WRESTLING FEDERATION N G O W F PRESENTS "MONDAY NIGHT CARNAGE" -LIVE from The Spectrum in Philadelphia, Pennsylvania- ("Thunderkiss 65" plays as a video screen in The Spectrum displays many of the memorable moments from NGOWF's past events... Joel Gutman scores a suprise win over the much larger Saint Bruno in the first round of the World Title tournament... "Insane" Greg Gatling executes the Gatling Gun on NGOWF President [name withheld]... "Bruiser" Bob Todd reveals Saint Bruno as his new protege... and Lucien Champion winning the battle royal to become the very first NGOWF Television Champion. Cut to NGOWF commentators Nicholas Van Buren and Al Hunter at the broadcast table at ringside.) NVB: Hello fans and welcome to NGOWF's Monday Night Carnage! I'm Nick Van Buren. The man sitting next to me is Al Hunter. AH: We've got a night of grrrrreat wrestling action ahead of us! Let's head to the ring for our opening contest! Ring Announcer: Our opening contest is set for one fall with a 20 minute time limit. ("California Love" by Dr. Dre and 2-Pac begins to play over The Spectrum's loud speakers.) NO!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! PROPHET KICKS OUT!!!! NVB: There's still some life left in the big boy! AH: It's still early in the contest, Nick. Prophet is far from being finished. Pea is still getting in position for the moonsault! AH: Pea jumps off the top turnbuckle! Prophet catches him in mid-air!!!!!! NVB: OH MY GOD!!! Tombstone piledriver by Prophet!!! AH: Prophet now heading to the corner. He mounts the second turnbuckle! NVB: ELBOWDROP!!! Prophet connects!! AH: Prophet now pulling Sweet Pea to his feet! He sends him into the corner! NVB: Prophet charges after him! STINGER SPLASH!!!!!! AH: Not wasting any time whatsoever, Prophet sends him into the other corner! Sweet Pea gets thrown into the turnbuckle face-first!!! Sweet Pea stumbles back dazed! NVB: Prophet's got 'em!!! It's THE AWAKENING!!!!!!!!!!!! NVB: As the paramedics stretcher the defeated Sweet Pea away from ringside, let's head back to the ring for yet another match. Ring Announcer: Ladies and gentlemen... our next match is set for one fall with a 20 minute time limit. AH: I've heard some not so nice things about this "Blazer" guy who's in our next match. Dark Blood better watch himself or he'll be leaving The Spectrum via a stretcher like Sweet Pea. ("Carnival of Carnage" by Insane Clown Posse begins to play throughout The Spectrum.) Ring Announcer: Coming down the aisle... from Detroit, Michigan... weighing 265 lbs... DARK BLOOD!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! NVB: What's this? AH: Barry Blades has emerged from the crowd! He's jumped Dark Blood outside of the ring! I can't believe this! I've heard this guy was the unpredictable sort! NVB: Blades now bouncing Dark Blood's head off of the guard rail like a basketball! AH: Not even a minute has gone by and Dark Blood is already busted wide open! NVB: A fan has handed Barry Blades a chair! OUCH!!! He smacks Dark Blood upside the head with it!!! AH: There's a permanent dent in the chair now, thanks to Dark Blood's head! NVB: Normally, that sort of action would result in an immediate disqualification. NVB: Cover by "The Blazer"! 1.................................................... NO!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! DARK BLOOD GETS HIS RIGHT SHOULDER UP!!!!!! Blades covers Dark Blood!!! 1.................................................... 2.................................................... 3!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! He put up quite a valiant fight against this crazy individual. AH: He may have put up a fight but, in the end, it was Blades who scored the pinfall! Already in the ring... from Detroit, Michigan... weighing 297 lbs... DR. DEEEEATH!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! AH: The good doctor is here and he's ready to operate! ("Keep The Heads Ringin'" by Dr. Dre pumps through the speaker system of The Spectrum.) Ring Announcer: His opponent... he's from Brooklyn, New York... weighing in at 240 lbs... ("Sexual Healing" by Marvin Gaye begins to play throughout The Spectrum.) AH: Oh no... not him!! AH: This is gonna be a wild one, Nick! NVB: Damian Mazura, the bigger of the three members of The Axis Powers, will be starting the match for his team.
