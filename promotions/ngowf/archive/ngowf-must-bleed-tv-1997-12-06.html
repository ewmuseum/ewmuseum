We see Francois Lefebvre winning the six man tag team elimination match... Prophet demolishing Sweet Pea in singles competition... Dr. Death scoring a suprise upset victory over "Gold Tooth" Benny Ramirez... and finally, NGOWF President [name withheld] presenting Damage Control with the NGOWF World Tag Team Titles. AH: And I'm too damn important for these cheesy introductions! NVB: He's Al Hunter. Welcome to NGOWF's Must Bleed TV! We have an action packed card planned for you tonight!
