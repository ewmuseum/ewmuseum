Ahh yes, King of Death. What a wonderful PPV for Windy City RAW. The first
King of Death tournament was held in 2001 -- who would forget that fateful match between Dead on Arrival defeating all three members of the Freaks of the Industry to become the first Windy City RAW hardcore champion! This tournament looked to hold the same amount of fevor. While DOA, The Freaks of the Industry, and most of those who participated in the original tournament are no longer members of this federation, the tradition of greatness remains. Though the Hardcore Belt is currently sitting on a shelf somewhere in the WC-RAW building, the stakes of this match go much higher as the winner of this tournament gets a shot at the world title.
High stakes indeed. Buck Madison and Stan Willis welcomed everyone in TV land to Windy City
RAW's second King of Death tournament. Right off the bat, Madison and
Willis discussed the "alliance?" of War and "Ironman" James Loyal -- two
Windy City RAW superstars. Is this the beginning of a power group? Or is it just the start of a respectfull relationship between the two wrestlers? Who knows -- though Willis thought it wouldn't matter -- "both of them suck" -- that both wrestlers "couldn't get it done in the ring, what makes you think they can become a team and win?"
Madison: There's always strength in numbers, Stan. Willis: Pssh. Group a bunch of weaklings together, and what do you have? A bunch of weaklings! War and Loyal are nothing. War is a FORMER champion
that -- Madison: That had his belt stripped from him by Byers. Willis: War is still a former champion -- he doesn't hold the belt now, does he? No. He's the former champion. End. Of. Discussion.
After a lull in this discussion, both commentators agreed to disagree -- so to speak -- about this topic. As it stood, ladies and gentlemen, the current federation champion is none other than "The Real Shit" Jamal Byers, who, as
Madison pointed out, would be facing War in a Parking Lot Match. Both wrestlers would be out in the parking lot of the UIC Pavillion, duking it out amongst the cars and the debree out there for the World Heavyweight
Championship. It would also mark the first time these two combatants faced each other following the hideous display of power as War was stripped by commissioner Byers of the championship. Would War have his revenge or would
Byers find a way to keep the belt around his waist? As Madison stated, those answers would be found out later tonight. But first thing first -- The King of Death tournament!
As Madison was quick to point out, this tournament would be one for the ages as well -- first off, they were having a heated exchange between newcomer, so to speak, Bel Biggz with WC-RAW veteran Shawn Borders. The good money was that these two -- Biggz and Borders -- would be squaring off in the finals.
However, this is Windy City RAW and anything could happen! However, the first match would be a very important match as Rage settles an old score with The Bard.
