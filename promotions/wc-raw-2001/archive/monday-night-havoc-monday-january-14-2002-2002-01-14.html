Monday Night Havoc -- Monday, January 14, 2002

14 January 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Results from Monday Night Havoc Monday, January 14, 2002 United Center Chicago, Illinois [ Before Windy City RAW logo slams onto the screen, the waiting audience is caught off guard as "Hero Of The Day" by Metallica blasts over the speakers. A man recently departed from WC-RAW steps back thru the curtains. "Ironman" James Loyal... one of the main followers of Liermann's XHCW .. A man few ever expected back in WC-RAW makes his way to the ring to the sound of boos. He is wearing an Ironman
XHCW t-shirt, black leather jacket, blue jeans, and a large fiberglass black full knee brace around his left knee. He climbs the steps and walks to the center of the apron. He turns around and lifts his arms high in the air as the music continues to play. Loyal steps inside the ropes and takes a mic. He paces the ring as boos continue from the crowd. ]
Loyal: I know that I am the walking enemy. A few monthes ago I was one of the main men to lead an invasion upon the great fed of WC RAW. In what seemed like no time XHCW dominated and left WC RAW in its wake. I showing the man of morals that I am left WC RAW to follow Liermann back to XHCW.
I was wrong. Since that time all that could go wrong has went wrong. I lost my XHCW World Heavyweight Title to The Dehibilitator -- Liermann's new wrecking machine. After a 2 month long feud. A feud in which I gave all of my soul all of my heart all of my life... I came out with nothing.. but pain. I was disrespected, injured, and replaced. My service was done and he had moved on. Last week after my last heart breaking 3rd loss to Dehib... I hobbled into Liermann's office cluthching the walls after nearly having my leg destroyed by his monster. I look right in his eyes and I told him exactly what I thought of him. Unsuprisingly enough he didn't seem too phased as I have said he had moved on.
That night I realised that I had to move on as well. As I said to
Liermann I was spinning my wheels in XHWC. Maybe I should come back to a fed that more fits my good hearted personality. A fed that fought for its own survival and thru it all retured. I loved my time in WC RAW.... and I hope to return. Douglas Coleman... I am sorry. I am sorry for listening to that raving egomaniac Steve Liermann. I was blinded as so many others have been in the past. Bt I am ready to work for you... and all I need is a chance.
Respect is earned.... Liermann forgot that. But I have not, thank you all for your time. And its good to be back.. if only for tonight. Hopefully... it can happen again.
[ Loyal places the mic on the ground and shows a sign of respect to the crowd as he slowly climbs over and leaves.. the sound of the crowd changes to a mix of cheers and boos which gradually builds as he exits behind the curtain. ]
[ The Windy City RAW opening commences shortly afterwards -- someone anti-climatically as the audience, stunned by what they have just witnessed, try to figure out what just happened as they work themselves into anticipation of the first match. Even Buck Madison and Stan Willis seem a bit perplexed -- here was a main stream, hardcore XHCW...what the hell is he doing here in WC-RAW? Especially apologizing? Madison applauded the young man and even ventured to welcome him back to Windy City RAW while Willis called him a "chicken- s*** coward who couldn't cut it in XHCW so he had to try to crawl back to WC-RAW." Willis and Madison then set about the task of outlining the four-match card and informing people that the card for
Virtual Brutality had not been booked yet, though they did know that at least one match will be booked tonight. Before they could finish talking, "I Disappear" by Metallica came over the speakers, announcing the arrival of none other than The Sexy Ninja. ]
The Sexy Ninja Brett White v. Jeremy Walters Ninja came out to his usual pop while the crowd remained silent with the arrival of Walters, a man they haven't seen or heard much of.
Ninja sits in contempt on one of the turnbuckles as Walters swaggered into the ring. The match opens with Ninja taking an early advantage with Walters apparently showing that he wasn't in the same league as
Ninja, working extremely stiff. Finally, it looked like Ninja was just going to dominate the match when Walters apparently awoke from his slumber and released a torrent of moves on the unsuspecting
Ninja, hitting him with a series of impressive offensive moves that would have taken anyone down, but Ninja refuesed to be pinned. As the match grew longer, it became increasingly clear that Walters was becoming annoyed with Ninja's inability to cooperate and just quit as
Ninja attempted to rebound time and time again. Walters picked a battered Ninja up in a power hoist suplex and slammed him into the top turnbuckle, stomach first. White writhed in pain as Walters went for the pin, only to get a very close two. While Jeremy agrued, rightfully so according to Willis, Ninja somehow found the strength to stand. He mounted a last ditch effort and went for his patented roundhouse kick. However, Jeremy turned around just in time to duck but the foot carrened, slamming into the face of the referee, sending the man out of the ring and to the ring apron.
The spin-kick took every ounce of strength that Ninja could muster and he collapsed in a corner of the ring, exhausted. Jeremy was relentless and charged into the corner, picking up Ninja and standing him in the corner while Walters viciouslly assulted Ninja with a punches, kicks, chops, and elbows in a blinding fury of speed and power. As Jeremy backed away, Ninja stumbled out of the ring to catch a ghetto blaster from Walters. Jeremy looked down and smiled as it became evident that Ninja was bleeding from a cut on his forehead.
However, Walters wasn't finished. He slid out of the ring and took out a steel chair and set it up on the bottom turnbuckle and then picked up the limp carcass that was once the Sexy Ninja and hit his
East Village DDT on the Sexy Ninja, using the chair to amplify the pain, so to speak.
It became clear to everyone that Ninja was in need of some serious medical attention but Walters was not going to stop his assault. He picked up the battered Ninja, standing him up in the middle of the ring and slammed his loaded shoe into Ninja's temple, dropping him like a ton of bricks. He then quickly hit his East Village DDT in the middle of the ring, and the clamped on his Keening Claw, holding it on the defenseless Ninja as the crowed booed unmercilessly. The
Referee finally staggered to his feet and saw the carnage in the ring and called for the bell, but it wasn't enough for Walters as he continued to hold the claw in place, long after the bell sounded, despite the obvious pain he was causing Ninja. A team of paramedics and security people rushed into the ring and the security men charged
Walters, tackling him and in turn forcing Walters to release his hold and dragging him out of the ring while paramedics attended to the unmoving body of The Sexy Ninja. Walters, however, wasn't easy to sedate, and battled against the five men security team like a rabid wolverine, blasting security forces over the railings and catching one security member and hitting his East Village DDT on him, this time on the steel steps! Finally, more members of WC-RAW security showed up and Walters was forced out of the ring area. Willis exhonerated the newcomer while Madison claimed the man deserved to be locked up.
Winner: Jeremy Walters, via Submission Stone v. SuperDeadly SuperDeadly showed himself to be in prime condition for this match as he attacked Stone with a vengance. Stone, however, battled back, rebounding off a slow start to show Windy City RAW that he deserved his high ranking in the RSPWF and quickly dismantled the high-flying
SuperDeadly, taking a quick advantage. However, Stone underestimated his opponent and soon paid the price, taking a little too much time to showboat and allowing SuperDeadly to recover. SuperDeadly exploded for a huge onslaught of offense and looked to be in prime condition to win the match, but The Bard suddenly appeared, dragging his US
Championship with him. He slid into the ring and coldcocked
SuperDeadly as the ref became distracted by an observant Stone. The opportunist Stone quickly covered the unconcious SuperDeadly and scored the victory.
Winner: Stone, via pinfall [ Backstage, a sweating Bard steps away from the entrance ramp and is confronted by his manager, former WC-RAW hardcore Champion, Rage. ]
Rage: What the hell was that? What the hell are you doing running into someone else's match like that?!
The Bard: I do believeith that the time was right And that it wasith the proper course of action. SuperDeadly should pay for making me look The part of a fool. Rage: Where the hell did you get a boneheaded idea like that? That's not the way I taught you. You need to be focused on Borders, tonight, next! I will not operate like this.
The Bard: Then perhaps you have outlived your usefullness. [ The Bard walks off camera with Rage, stunned, watching The Bard walk off as the screen fades to black and heads to commercials. ]
[ Windy City RAW returned from the commercial break to see the former
World Heavyweight Champion Khan walking down the hall with Tony
Gravano. Though much of what they said was muffled -- thanks to horrible mike placement -- but it was overheard that Tony Gravano was there just to protect Khan from someone described as "short, blondheaded, stocky, and horrifically deformed. Basically, a deformed elf." ]
WC-RAW US Championship Match The Bard w/ Rage v. "Same Old Savage" Shawn Borders As Borders waited in the ring, the US Champion The Bard made his way to the ring with a bunch of pompesity, pointing to Borders and making a "breaking" motion with his hands, much to the dismay of Rage, who shuffled behind the champion he helped train, shaking his head and rubbing the back of his neck. Finally, after a long, drawn out pagent, The Bard entered the ring and The Bard taunted Borders, who stood stoically silent. Referee Jonathan Beachem then announced for the match to begin. The two wrestlers circled each other when suddenly, The Bard motioned that he had to tie his boots, and knealed down to tie his laces. Beachem took particular interest, watching The
Bard and making sure the knot was secure, then helping him tie the other boot. While this was going on, Borders waited patiently, obviously annoyed, when from the left side audience seating slithered out Commissioner Jamal Byers, who grabbed the US Belt and proceeded to climb the apron. He jumped on the apron and slammed the belt into the back of Borders, sending him to the mat. The Bard then stood up and the match "resumed" with The Bard covering Borders and registering the three count. An enraged Rage stood shouting at The
Bard as he held up his hands, making out like it was a hard-fought victory. The Bard shrugged and exited the ring with Rage still standing on the apron. Rage climbed through the ropes to check on
Borders and looked up just in time to catch Byers, The Bard, and
Beachem all shaking hands on the entrance stage admist a cascade of boos.
Winner: The Bard via pinfall [ It's almost time for the match of the year, War Vs. Khan. War is shown in his dressing room casually getting ready for his match against Khan. War is shown putting on his elbow pads. He is still cool, calm, and collected from what we can tell. War finishes with his elbows and puts on the tape. Once War is done with that, he places the world title around his waist and patiently awaits what he likes to call, "Go time." War sits down, and looks at the camera. His eyes show nothing but calmness. The crowd is near deafness as they wait for this long anticipated bout between two massive egos, er, a clash of the titans. War speaks. ]
War: Isn't it a little late to be asking for your last requests Khan? What? You're not longer your super cool, calm, and collected self, now aren't you? You're just waiting for the inevitable. Me, beating you. That's what's going to boil right down to it at the end of this night. Hell, even your girlfriend, Cassandra, knows that you're going to lose. Did you see her face? What? She was actually concerned with the fact that you're going to lose, and go down hard. Making these drawn out excuses to her is just signifying that you're scared of me.
You're afraid to losing to me. ME! You insult me, my credibility as world champion and when it's almost go time, you're scared. Just plain pitiful. What? You think that's some sort of joke about you being scared? Do you think that I'm making this up? What? Look at yourself. Even when you're giving your usual monotone promos you look nervous. And why do you want me to forfeit the match? Are you terrified of being beaten? The fact that you're not going to win the world title scares you. Mortifys you, Khan. Khan, tonight, I'm going to make this nightmare, a reality. When I slam my boot into the side of your skull and pin you, the nightmare will come true. You won't be the world champion after tonight. Oh and uh, by the way, I am by no means a paper champion. That's your former role. If you don't believe me, just ask Moose, or Coleman, or just about any other person and they'll give you the same answers. Tonight, my Kick of Fear will solve all your problems. Trust me.
[ FTB ] WC-RAW World Championship Match War v. Khan Khan, the challenger for the world title, came to the ring first, accompanied as always by the lovely Cassandra. He was wearing a blue terry robe. He enters the ring, slapping the hands of the fans as he struts to the ring. He climbs through the ropes and hands Cassandra his robe and gets prepared for the match. War then makes his way to the ring, the world title hanging on his right shoulder as he glares into the ring, a look of determination oozing from his eyes. Slowly he saunters down the aisle, ignoring the crowd and their outstretched hands. Methodically, War walks up the stairs and slides through the ropes, never taking his eyes off Khan. Periodically, Kahn glances up the aisle, as if expecting someone to run down to the ring. War glances back, shrugs, and motions that it it's just Khan and War in the ring. The bell sounds and the two superstars circle each other in the ring and then lock horns like two young moose in the wild. Both men seem equally match and neither are able to gain the upper hand and they release their holds and glare at each other. They go for another move, but War changes it, slamming an elbow into Khan's jaw.
War continues working on Khan, sending him into the ropes and swinging wildly for a clothesline, but Khan ducks and hits War with a standing dropkick and the crowd erupts as Khan gains the upperhand.
The advantage is shortlived as War stops the offense with a brutal
DDT. War goes for the quick pin but only gets a one count.
This seesaw battle continued throughout much of the match with neither wrestler able to garner a 3-count nor were they able to take any definative edge on their opponent. The match drags on and Khan does everything in his power and within the rules, and sometimes outside of them, to regain his title, but after several near falls, the match continued. About midway through the battle, Khan switched tatics and concentrated on the legs of War, locking him up in a
Boston Crab but War never surrendered his title, even though Referee
Jason Nicholson kept right on top of things. Nicholson then counted to five, but Khan refused to give up his hold. This is where we pick things up with Buck Madison and Stan Willis...
BM: ...Khan's refusing to release the Boston Crab on War. SW: That coward! He's not following the rules! BM: Khan keeps looking at the entrance area -- what's he looking for? SW: There! Nicholson is finally getting Khan off the Boston Crab. Khan's probably wondering why his "boy" isn't coming out of the backstage area to beat down on War -- did you see that lead pipe
Gravano had?
BM: That definately was a lethal weapon. But Tony is still back there, like he's waiting for something
SW: Or someone. BM: Nicholson, meanwhile, is chewing Khan out for his actions during the last few minutes and War is getting to his feet, but very gingerly. It appears that Khan will soon become the 3-Time World
Champion.
SW: LOOK OUT WAR! BM: Yeah...Khan pushes Nicholson out of the way and gets set up for his SnakeCharmer -- How I've missed that kick -- sheer poetry in motion!
SW: Poetry? More like a horror story! War doesn't even know it's coming! I can't believe the crowd is cheering for this guy! Khan's about to hit a defenseless man!
BM: Defenseles? You didn't seem to mind when Walters went after Ninja the way he did. SW: That was differnt -- Ninja deserved it! BM: Yeah, everyone deserves to be wheeled to the hospital. War staggers off the ropes... BM (Simultaneously): SNAKECHARMER! SW (Simultaneously): KICK OF FEAR! BM: Whoa -- I can't believe what I just saw! Both wrestlers just slammed their finishers into each other and are now unconcious in the middle of the ring!
SW: War wins! BM: What? How do you figure? SW: Easy -- War hit his first! BM: ...You know, I didn't miss you at all...why did Coleman even bring you back? SW: Again easy -- he felt one of us needed some charity and since I'm the best one of the two, da Prez decided to bring you back and get you out of that car you live in.
BM: Admist your innane comments, Nicholson has begun the countdown and has already gotten to 4.
SW: War's getting up! Look! BM: Yep -- he's rolled over. Good for him. 7! SW: WAR! GET UP WAR! 8... BM: Khan staggers to the second rope...if he gets up he wins...nope, he's out... 9... SW: Khan has fallen...and he can't get up! BWAHAHAHAHA! BM: Yep...War is doing so much better. SW: War just needed a nap. 10! [ The Ref calls for the bell and the match is announced as ending in a double knockout! ] BM: Unbelieveable! The clash of the titans ends up in a draw! SW: Khan was lucky, that's all! BM: Lucky my ass! He dominated the match! War was a little too overconfident in the match. SW: He was just playing with him. Look, War's on his feet while Cassandra is checking on Khan. BM: War walks over to Cassandra...Cassandra doesn't even notice that
War is there...Cassandra! Look out!
SW: KICK OF FEAR! KICK OF FEAR! Cassandra flies out of the ring! WOO HOO! BM: How the hell can you be happy about that? Your "champion" just beat up a defenseless woman! Khan jumps to his feet but War is already walking up the aisle, his world title in his hand!
SW: Now THAT's a champion! Khan couldn't carry his jock! BM: Khan slides out of the ring and checks on his beloved Cassandra, all the while watching as War walks up the stage.
SW: That little wench didn't need to be in the ring anyways! BM: On that disturbing note, Windy City RAW is out of time. Until next week, I'm Buck Madison, for a disturbing Stan Willis, good night folks!
