Windy City RAW — Card Archive

Monday Night Havoc — Monday, September 3, 2001

4 September 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
LIGHTS! PYRO! MUSIC! FLAMING GIANTS FANS (yes the ones from San
Francisco!) Scenes from Resurrections flash across the with bits of commentary. The Iron Man match goes into the most replays, a couple from the cage match, and the highlights end with the Sexy Ninja saving Coleman at the end of the pay per view. Buck and Stan begin gushing over the PPV and tonights HUGE card. Buck states that there are more matches for tonight than on the PPV and even though most of the wrestlers participated in the event, there should be a lot of action, especially with the debut of more XHCW wrestlers and two new comers.
Just as Stan begins to speak, "Yukon" Jack Daniel's music his the crowd and he strolls down to the ring with a mic in his hand. YJD begins spouting off about Hannibal and the rest of the WC-RAW roster when Hannibal's music kicks in. The fans jumped to there seats only to see Hannibal walking down with a wet bar? Hannibal opens it up and shows all the liquor inside. With YJD's mouth salavating, Hannibal proposes a different sort of challenge. A Last Man Standing Drinking contest. YJD accepts and the crowd roars as the two stars walk off to begin their contest.
Alex Dunvegan vs. Stone The first true match of the night pitted Former Heavy Weightchamp
Alex Dunvegan against the new comer Stone. Alex had a pretty fair reception and as Stone came down, the crowd seemed impartial.
Cassandra and Stone's valet Rox didn't seem to fond of each other and jawed the whole night. Alex was still hurting from the PPV yet pulled out some surprising moves like starting out with a monkey flip and then into a series of suplexes and powerbombs. His one missed move, an inverted powerbomb let Stone take advantage of the match. He hit text book drop kicks and an amazing brain buster that left both Buck and Stan speachless. Only once did he jepordize his chances when
Cassandra went after Rox. Stone slid to the outside and was about to give Cass a powerbomb on the cement. Dunvegan saved her, but barely.
Alex had a little offesive mounted until Stone hit a knee shot off the ropes to Alex's sore midsection. Stone wasted no time and hit his
Stone Crusher, an amazing 360 5 star splash from the top rope. After that it was all academic. After the match Stone collected Rox and spit as Cassandra as she slid into the ring to check on Alex.
Winner: Stone by pin Time: 7:25 Rating 7/10 The Sexy Ninja vs. The Juice Buck was excited about this next match as the "Saviour" Bret White came down to the ring, pumped and prepared. His hand is still bandaged from his oath and he just waited for "The Juice" to come on down. The Juice got a small fan following started with his into music from Oasis. SN took advantage of the rookie and schooled him getting three very early near falls. The Juice turned it on though surprising
Ninja with a reverse hurricanrana he calls "The Slay" and almost got him to tap out to a half boston crab the Juice calls "Straight
Shooter". Juice took it to the air getting massive cheers as his high impact moves took the breath out of SN. When it looked like SN was taking back control, Juice hit his Juicer finisher, a twisting, flipping stunner that could rattle even Hannibal's messed up melon.
Winner: The Juice by pin Time: 8:13 Rating: 7/10 After two good matches the WC-RAWTron jumps to Hannibal sitting across from Yukon, two empty bottles of vodka on the table behind the ref. Hannibal has a glazed look in his eyes while Yukon still looks, well, sober. The ref has a half empty bottle of black label Jack
Daniels and is filling up the shot glasses. Buck and Stan both tell the kids watching at home not to follow Hannibal's and YJD's method.
<Commercial for Alcholics Anonimus> "The Rat Bastard" Angus O'Grady vs. Michael Grunge Back from the appropriately timed commercial, XHCW star Angus O'Grady and Grunge are already in the ring and ready to square off. Angus started the match off with a low blow followed by a DDT. Grunge fired back with a round of snap suplexs and a massive clothesline that brought both men outside the ring. Some at ringside by where Angus landed got a whiff of something they didn't like, his BO. The ringside street brawl kept getting broken up by Grunge and O'Grady as they would roll into the ring and then back out breaking the count.
Finally back in the ring, O'Grady nailed a piledriver and when Grunge stood up, he hit his patented heart punch called The Plague Punch.
Guess who the winner is? Winner: Angus by pin Time 10:32 Rating: 8/10 Fire & Brimstone vs. The Bouncers Just before this match, the Ring Announcer stated that the Lance Ammo match against Moose was canceled because of Ammo's stay in the hospital, thus giving Moose the win. Then F&B were introduced followed by the Bouncers. The Bouncers came out and saw that Fire and
Brimstone were in the ring and they looked pissed. Fire and Brimstone didn't stand a chance, but then neither did the Bouncers. As the
Bouncers were giving both men a taste of a ring bar room brawl Moose came running full steam from the back and GOREd Brimstone out of his boots. Fire went to attack Moose, got a big boot from him and an
Irish whip into a GORE!! Stan was going nuts. The Bouncers just stayed back until both F&B were down. When Moose's back was turned to leave, Alex and Marshall attacked. They pounded down on Moose.
Alex left Marshall and pinned the still destroyed Brimstone for the win. When Alex got up to get Marshall, Moose GOREd him right into the turnbuckle. Moose proceded to pound away at the two tag teams until security came down and overwhelmed the massive superstar to the back.
Winner: Unoffically Moose Time: 5:02 Rating: 5/10 Sabbath vs. Bel Biggz The darkness that is Sabbath still sent chills down the spines of the fans especially after the hell that he went through at the PPV. Bel
Biggz came down to the ring focused expect for spying some of the nicer looking ladies at ringside. Once he was inside, though, Sabbath was his only focus. This wasn't a wrestling match, it was controled chaos! Both men landed massive moves and blows, though Sabbath seemed to absorb most of the punishment like he did at the PPV against
Anubis. As the match reached an amazing ten minute mark with only two, thats right, two near falls, Biggz hit a single arm DDT out of nowhere and followed it with a Big Splash from the top rope. Biggz thought he won it there, but Sabbath barely kicked out at 2 and 1/2.
Biggz picked up the dazed Sabbath and hit his Bel-trasm finisher. He grogily got up and left the ring with his arm raised.
Winner: Bel Biggz by pin Time 13:44 Rating: 8/10 The WC-RAWTron cut back to YJD and Hannibal. Both men now have a glazed look to them and there are empty bottles on TWO tables now. A bottle of Hot Damn sits on table and both men have their shot glasses full and in hand. They both slam it down and put down the shot glasses. The ref pours two more shots and calls out that the current bottle is out. Both men finish off the shoots as both end up tossing cookies as the next match begins.
"The Classic" John Sebastian vs. The Bard Just on principle and the fact that he is XHCW, John Sebastian got booed to high heaven. The Bard received the biggest cheer of the night as this underdog came down to the ring, a little wobbly still from the iron man match. The Classic had at least one fan in Stan, who praised every move he made. The Classic hit nice power moves like a powerslam and a pulling pile driver. But the Bard, out of freak luck, caught Sebastian just right with the weapon Sebastian was going to use on the Bard outside the ring. Rage hobbled down to the ring and yelled at the Bard to get Sebastian in the ring and cover his "Lanky Ass!!" The Bard did and......ONLY GOT A TWO COUNT!
Sebastian came back with stiff rights and lefts rocking the Bard. Rage had enough and got up on the apron. The ref started yelling at
Rage as The Classic hit his classic finisher. When Sebastian saw what
Rage was doing, he shoved the ref out of the way and got nailed by
Rage. Rage climbed into the ring and started landing rights and lefts on to Sebastian. The ref saw everything and DQed the Bard as Rage was yelling at the Bard as to this is how it should be done. Sebastian waited patiently and hit his Classic finisher one more time. He left to a chorus of boos. The Bard left with Rage yelling at him all the way to the back.
Winner: "The Classic" by DQ Time: 9:06 Rating 6/10 Anubis vs. Greco This is a war, as Buck put it, dating back to the XHCW days. Both men ignored the cheers and the boos as they came down to the ring. Greco and Anubis circle each other after the bell rings. Anubis starts off smartly, moving in when Greco gives him just the slightest opening.
His speed caught Greco off guard and Anubis hits nice martial arts combos, rocking the Iron Man of Wrestling. Whenever Greco went in for the attack Anubis gouged and sucker punched Greco, further aggrivating Greco. After a series of reversals and blocked blows,
Anubis rests on the ropes. Greco, in a mad dash, nails a vicious clothesline sending both men outside. Anubis went for a chair shot after he took Greco down with a spinebuster to the cement floor, but
Greco ducked it and hit the chair back into Anubis. But Anubis's armor saved him from that damage. The chair to the face was only a distraction though as Greco quickly went after Anubis, landing a vicious drop onto the guard rail. Anubis quickly removed his helmut and looked to be gasping for air. Greco's drop dented the armor into
Anubis's solar plexus. Anubis finally got it pulled away enough as
Greco kept pounding away and rolling in and out of the ring to break the count. Greco kept up the attack against Anubis's armor, making
Anubis remove it piece by piece until he was just wearing the shin protectors. Greco moved it back into the ring when Anubis hit the
Cutting Edge II out of nowhere. Greco kicking out barely. Anubis hit a Yakuza kick along with the Michinoku Driver dazing Greco even more.
Anubis went for another pin and got another two count. Anubis began getting frustrated and then POW!! Greco shot up with the force of a rocket and nailed Anubis with an uppercut from Australia to the
Arctic. Both men are down and the Ref began his count. It ended at 6 after Greco got up. Greco stumbled to Anubis and locked on the
Manacle. The ref checked and received no reaction, probably from the vicious uppercut. The ref rang the bell. Buck and Stan thought this match was pay per view quality and gave Anubis a lot of credit for fighting through the pain and the breathlessness he suffered.
Winner: Greco by KO Time: 16:52 Rating: 10/10 Tag Team Championship Match Purgatory (c) v. Randy Hudman & Reese Whattington Hurst This match was almost a complete bore after the last one. Purgatory came out with the belts on their shoulders. Reese and Randy looked pumped. The match itself went back and forth, with both teams getting two near falls a piece before each member saved the other. Reese hit his Pop Quiz and then, in an instant of tag team chemistry, Randy timed a top rope leg drop while Reese hit a back breaker. Randy made the cover and just as easily as Purgatory won the belts, they lost them.
Just after the match the WC-RAWTron changed: [total blackness] female voice: Where am I? Where is everyone? I'm all alone here. What's going on? Reece, are you there?
Help me, I scared. Reece. [The scene fades in with Amanda Hurst tied to a plain wooden chair that is in the middle of a room with green walls and a wodden floor, and nothing else. She is wearing flannel pajamas and is blindfolded. This remains for a few seconds with her crying then fades to black, her crying lingering a few seconds after.]
Reese, after seeing that, just dropped his newly won title. As the scene cuts to commercial, Randy is helping Reese calm down.
<Commercial for a bunch of crap you would only by high or drunk, speaking of which.> Before the final match of the night, Buck and Stan begrudgingly go back to check on the status of the Last Man Standing Drinking contest. What they see is Hannibal and YJD both passed out. The ref proceded to go over what was consumed, the entire wet bar and about
500 from the bar on Rush Street they are at. The ref procedes to say that they did not finish the last bottle and he is going to get drunk. The scene fades from the big screen to the ref taking a big ole swig from the last bottle and sitting down. Buck and Stan say that because both participants were passed out, the match was a draw?
6 Man Tag Team Match "The Franchise" Khan/"Same Ole Savage" Shawn Borders/War vs. "Sadistic" Steve Liermann/Scotty "The Body" Fitzpatrick/Sean "Bohemian" Lehoist Both Buck and Stan were ready for this match. It turned out to be much more. The XHCW team used the trick of the trade of double teaming and blocking off the ring and dominated most of the match, almost taking Khan out. But War and Borders made the save and cleaned house. War hit World War III and Borders took out Liermann. Borders even rubbed the WC-RAW title in Liermann's face at the request of
War. Khan was being handled by Lehoist because of the beating he received at the beginning of the match. War came back into the ring along with Borders just in time for the rest of the XHCW members to storm the ring, with the exception of the passed out YJD and Greco.
What was left of the WC-RAW group came out and the match was thrown out. Buck and Stan sign off as the battle in the ring begins to turn toward the XHCW.
