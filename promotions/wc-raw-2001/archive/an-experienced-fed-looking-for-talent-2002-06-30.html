WC-RAW: An Experienced Fed looking for talent

30 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.

— Part 1 of 2 —

Hey guys, this is the ever-posting, e-fed exclusionist extraordinaire, [name withheld]. And today, I have an open proposition for you. To join Windy City RAW, one of five feds in the RAW family currently living, breathing and prospering. Why join WC-RAW? Well, if you're looking for an open RP system with a fedhead ([name withheld]) who is willing to help you with angle ideas, while letting you have complete character control (no matter how unorthodox he is,) then you're in luck. Half of our roster are 5 years or over vets, and the other half is brand new talent. So whether you're looking for experienced angle driven players, or something fresh and new, Windy City RAW is the e-wrestling federation for you. (Cool! I rhymed!) So yah, that's my pitch. For us vets, it's great for characters you wish to try out, angles you've dreamed up but cant get done in other feds, and for just having a good time without the threat of having your nads chopped off if you're busy and need a week for mental health. And for you new guys? This is THE place to start. In all of the RAW feds, there are no favorites. If you roleplay, you progress. If you roleplay really well? You get pushed. And if you have questions, or haven't quite been able to fit in? You got guys like Doug, I, Joe and Matt who can help you get on your feet. Hope you'll join Windy City RAW. Great times for old, new, and the experimental. -[name withheld] [Wondering why I'm doing this and not Doug? He's not able to get on google groups, so I'm posting for him.]
