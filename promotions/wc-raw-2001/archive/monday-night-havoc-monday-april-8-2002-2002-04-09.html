Monday Night Havoc April 8, 2002 Monday Night Havoc opens, uncharastically, with a shot of the arena. No opening monologue, no opening skit, but, instead, the start of "My Way" by
Limp Bizkit starts coursing out of the speakers. The crowd lets out a string of boos as he makes his way to the ring and the most powerful man in Windy
City RAW doesn't seem fazed. He enters the ring and stands in the ring as he produces a microphone and begins to address the crowd.
President Douglas Coleman: Hello and welcome everyone to WINDY CITY RAW! The crowd goes for the cheap pop but there are still some boos. DC: Before we kick this show off, I have some questions that need to be answered. Marcus Wilson, come down here.
The start of "Weapon of Choice" starts pouring out of the speakers. The crowd remains relatively unsure of where the new commissioner, Marcus Wilson stands. Though he has shown flashes of smart and unbiased thinking, the fans realize that the man is a long time friend of President Douglas Coleman -- the man who, as the fans think, sided with the enemy at Extreme Measures:
Annihilation. Wilson makes his way to the ring with a smile beaming on his face and looking rather...odd in his new beige suit. The suit fits great, but the man doesn't fit the suit. He climbs up the steps and eases into the ring, his tie held against his chest. He stands in the middle of the ring, hearing the boos as well as the cheers from the crowd. He nods as the music begins to die down.
DC: Marcus, Marcus, Marcus. It's very good to see you. It's been so long since we stood together and, if I recall, this is the first time you and I actually stood in the same ring. How's your family?
Marcus Wilson: Fine. DC: That's good to know. You, no doubt, wonder why I brought you out here tonight? MW: I have my...ideas. DC: Good. Now, I brought you in to this federation because you are a fair and reasonable man.
MW: Thanks. DC: And despite the concerns of the Dragon Syndicate, I brought you in here because I knew you'd be a team player.
President Coleman stops and looks up at Wilson. DC: You...are a team player, right? MW: But of course. The crowd lets out a torrent of boos as Coleman smiles. DC: I knew you'd see it my way. MW: You're way? DC: Yes. My way. Boy, you really had us fooled back there. But you did good. So what that Walters gave up the Tri-State Championship -- he won the world belt. Now, with you on my side, Windy City RAW will be --
MW: On your side? DC: Yes. I'm fighting for Windy City RAW, Marcus. I've got punks like "Ironman" James Loyal, Reese Whattington Hurst, Matthew dee and the former
WAR telling me how to run my federation. It's time that you and I joined forces to shut them up.
Coleman extends his hand to welcome Marcus Wilson. DC: Join The Dragon Syndicate, Marcus! Let's bring Windy City RAW back to the top! Wilson just stands in the ring, staring at the hand. He raises his head and looks at Coleman.
MW: Like hell I will. The crowd goes NUTS! Wilson is turning his back on Coleman?! DC: I'm sorry? MW: You say you're trying bring the federation back to the top and I'm trying to do the same thing, but for you to attack "Ironman" James Loyal,
Hurst, and WAR is WRONG Coleman. Those guys are the ones who made this federation. You're going to destroy Windy City RAW! I'm out to save it.
Coleman stands stunned. His best friend... DC: How can you do this to me, Marcus? You were my best friend! MW: No. Douglas Coleman was my best friend. Something is wrong with you,
Doug. You're blinded by the power you have created. Stand with me, Coleman, you and I can right the foundation of this federation.
The crowd watches in anticipation. Coleman walks to the corner of the ring, very distraught.
DC: I can't believe it. You of all people, Marcus. You, the man I trusted the most in all the world...
Coleman turns and looks at Wilson. DC: You're just like everyone else. You're trying to steal my federation from me. You're trying to take over the thing I put my blood, my sweat, my tears into! You're just like Loyal. You're just like War. YOU'RE JUST LIKE
LIERMANN! You're XHCW Filth! You, Loyal, Biggz...ALL OF YOU ARE SPIES FROM
LIERMANN! MW: Liermann? That foul stench is gone from here, Coleman. Loyal, Biggz, and myself all came to this federation because they were tired of Liermann's tyrannical reign.

MW: But I have one more thing to say to you -- I hope you brought your wrestling attire. The crowd settles down... DC: What? MW: Haven't you wondered just WHO would be Walters wrestling partner? DC: Just who did you have in mind. Wilson smiles.. MW: YOU! WHAT?! What the hell is Wilson thinking? The crowd erupts as Coleman stands, completely taken by surprise. He staggers a step back before regaining his composure.

A stunned and shocked Buck Madison and Stan Willis went down the card, focusing on the new revelation that Jeremy Walter's parter for Havoc was none other than President Douglas Coleman. Tag Team Match Marcus Wilson & Bel Biggz v. Jeremy Walters & Douglas Coleman Wilson and Biggz made their ways to the rings first -- seperately. They stood patiently in wait as Coleman's music came over the speakers and he appeared on the stage with the rest of the Dragon Syndicate. I believe it was the first time in WC-RAW history that the crowd actually booed President
Coleman. He didn't seem fazed, however, as he made his way to the ring wearing his old school suspender-type wrestling attire -- an obvious link to
Coleman's olympic wrestling roots. The tension was thick as the four wrestlers stared down each other in the ring. The referee got some simblance of civility and Biggz and Walters started the match.
It was evident early on that Walters had no desire to play fair with Biggz. Annoyed at having to face Biggz in the ring with Coleman, a man who never wrestled in the ring professionally, Walters simply didn't give Biggz a chance to breathe. Walters also wanted to make sure that Biggz wouldn't get his chance to take the World Championship Belt from him. Coleman rarely saw any action in the match as Walters concentrated on beating Biggz to a pulp.
Coleman, however, did come in periodically to give Walters a quick breather and to hit a few kicks in on Biggz. However, it was during one of those breathers that turned out to be a crucial mistake.
Haven taken a beating for much of the match, Biggz finally snapped out of it, slamming the unsuspecting Coleman in the stomach with a vicious kick to the midsection. A bulldog later, Biggz and Wilson was in control.
It appeared that Wilson wasn't going to spare Coleman anything, slamming
Coleman with spectacular moves from his long arsenal. Wilson and Biggz worked effectively as a tag team, tagging in and out and making Coleman suffer. Biggz climbed the top rope, going for what appeared to be a missle dropkick but Walters was there, pushing the would be challenger off the top rope. As the ref admonished Walters, Biggz writhed in pain as Coleman slowly got to his feet and walked over to Walters and tagged him in. Biggz crawled to Wilson, diving for the tag but was dragged away at the last minute by

Yes, ladies and gentlemen, "The Master of Insanity" Hannibal Payne had returned to Windy City RAW!
Payne dashed for the ropes and just about got into the ring when Baxter grabbed him and pulled him off the ropes. Payne blocked two punches from

Ninja started to duke it out on Payne but Payne was able to keep them off.

Wilson lept off the ropes and quickly went to the to Payne's aid, focusing solely on Payne as the crowd went wild. Coleman, terrified of the madman Payne backed slowly to the opposite corner of the ring, wanting to get away from Payne in every way possible and not paying attention to the opponent in the ring.
Biggz stood ready... Coleman backed into Biggz and he instantly knew he was in trouble. Slowly, the President of Windy City RAW turned around, the fear multiplying in his eyes. He turned to look at Biggz and quickly he found himself hoisted
up....BEL-TRASM! 1... 2... Walters dove into the ring to make the save.... 3!!!!! TOO LATE! Walters slammed into Coleman instead! BEL BIGGZ WAS GETTING A TITLE SHOT AT SYMPHONY OF DESTRUCTION! Walters with the Windy City RAW Championship on the line.
