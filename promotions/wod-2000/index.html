Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Warriors of Domination announced its own pending opening on 5 November 2000, recruiting wrestlers and staff under the motto “To be a true warrior, you must first be true to yourself.”

An update five days later set its first card, Monday Night Meltdown, for 13 November 2000 at the Whittemore Center in Durham, New Hampshire, opening an eight-man World Title tournament: an opening bout pitting Angel (with Jade) against Shanna, a mid-card special-referee match between Coaine and Lightning, a hardcore mid-card bout between Spawn (with Lord Aries) and The Knock Out Kid (with Burns), and a main event between “The Assassin” Matt Rogers (with Sexy Sandra Morlene) and Karnage. The four winners were to advance into a pay-per-view the following weekend, “When Warriors Collide.”

No result of Monday Night Meltdown, and no document naming a pay-per-view outcome, survives. Whether the tournament ran as planned, and how or when Warriors of Domination closed, are not known.
