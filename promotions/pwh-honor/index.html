Pro Wrestling Honor ran from November 2006 to October 2007, staging pay-per-views named Birth of Honor, Pure Eight Classic, London Calling, Step Up, Toryumon, Hustle, The Begining of Things to End, Fan-Tastic and Second Coming, and defending a World and a Tag Team championship across the United States, England, Japan and Mexico. Written by AI, 2026

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Pro Wrestling Honor opened with a two-night tournament in Newhall, California, on 8 and 9 November 2006, crowning Bill Graves its first World Champion; its own tag division crowned Team BUSHIDO the same way four weeks later. Across the following year the promotion's own two title ledgers stage its record whole: seven World title changes and twelve tag-title changes, run through Birth of Honor, Pure Eight Classic, London Calling, Step Up, three nights of Toryumon, Hustle, The Begining Of Things To End, two nights of Fan-Tastic, Rey De Puro Tournament and Second Coming.

The promotion's own record is a touring one by its own account, not a character's boast: title defenses land in Newhall, Manhattan, Philadelphia, Aberdeen NJ, Van Nuys, Covina, Chicago Ridge, Braintree, St. Paul, San Bernardino and Lake Grove, and across London and Doncaster, England; Tokyo, Japan; and Toluca and Puebla, Mexico.

PWH's own ledgers record it going out of business three times inside less than a year — titles vacated or retired on 30 May 2007 and again on 24 Aug 2007, each time followed by a further run before a last, undated closing line: “Titles vacated when PWH goes out of business.” Its last dated activity anywhere in the surviving record is a tag-title no-contest in Lake Grove, New York, on 18 October 2007. A single, one-sentence notice announcing a re-opening on 14 May 2010 has no card, roster or title activity behind it.
