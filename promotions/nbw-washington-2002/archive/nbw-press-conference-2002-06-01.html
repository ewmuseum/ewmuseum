NBW Press Conference

1 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Fade in.] [From a bird's eye view we find ourselves looking down at a rather large, and elaborate setting for a room crowded with what appears to be lower-class media figures. Going to a side angle, as if we were watching a basketball game, we are able to see more then before. Many faces come and go as the camera work continues. Soon, we are brought to a picture that has evaded us - the stage, along with podium, and chairs. With this view, we come to realize that we are in the meeting room for a press conference. We can still hear the voices, and movement of the press; however, they aren't paid to much attention as we focus solely onto the stage, and now, the New Blood Wrestling logo. What a beauty it is.] [After a few more moments pass, about a dozen people walk onto the stage. Everyone who walks out is dressed differently - Some maintain a more business like attire wearing a suit and tie, while two or three others wear jeans, shorts, Nikes, Jordans, etc. All with the exception of one person, the rest have a seat. That man walks over to the podium, placing himself behind it. He loosens his tie, clears his throat and begins to settle the crowd…] BP : Ladies and gentlemen… [Soon all the talk from the crowd is silenced. They know what they need to do if they want to keep their jobs making just enough to support them, along with their apartment in the slums, and 1978 Ponto.] BP : Thank you, and while I am giving thanks I would like to thank all here from the Media, and our distinguished guest. We have all been called here to Washington, D.C. We have all been called here to hear a special announcement from New Blood Wrestling president and owner, Alexander Raymond. Before asking any questions we ask that you wait until Mr. Raymond has finished with everything he has to say. Now, instead of beating around the bush, I would like to welcome Mr. Alexander Raymond. [With that said, possibly the young looking man on stage stands up. As he stands, he walks over to the man who was just speaking, shaking his hand, and the two exchange a few gingerly words. Those setting on the stage begin in a small clap of hands, while those in the crowd begin to flash their cameras.] AR : Thank you, Brian Perkins. [The man who has just been identified as Brian Perkins, goes to a nearby seat, finding his place.] AR : Ladies and gentlemen thank you for coming here tonight, to the nation's capital, to hear what I, one young man has to say. [Pause.] AR : As many of you may or may not know, my late father, Christian Raymond, was making all sorts of business arrangements at his time of passing in late December. Now, as his one and only son, I felt it necessary to see my father's dreams fulfilled. And, that is what has brought us hear tonight. As you might be able to tell by looking at his son, my father was not a very large man. However, that did nothing for his burning desire to be a professional wrestler. But as the years went by, his window of opportunity closed. And, as that very window closed, his pockets opened, and that is when he began making all of this happen. And now, Dad, your dream is now becoming reality. Six months ago, I began speaking with many of my fathers' business associates, and today, they set behind me. [Raymond raises his left arms, with a large smile on his face, as a way to acknowledge those on stage with him. Cameras flash with every action the young Raymond makes.] AR : These ladies and gentlemen setting behind me form the executive committee of New Blood Wrestling. They, along with myself have gone out and got some of the best available talent. They, along with myself have worked many nights, with food or sleep. And now that all of this is coming true, it will continue. Now, the work really begins. Now, the New Blood can flow. With our up and coming superstars, and with the first card date sat for June 15th, 2002 we will begin our legacy. The show is entitled "Superstar Showcase: Genesis". This is it. This is the day that the work will all pay-off. This is the day that wrestling gets a new competitor. And, more importantly, this is the day N.B.W. strikes a dagger into the wrestling world's flesh so a little blood can flow. Ladies and gentlemen, this is the beginning… Yes, the beginning. [Raymond pauses, with a confident look on his face. The media isn't amused with his babble - They've heard it all before. But, the owner is a rookie himself. Talk about going straight to the league! Taking a step out of the proverbial "lime light" Raymond addresses the crowd.] AR : Questions? [Suddenly, many hands shoot into the air, to the liking of President Raymond. Alexander is a little overwhelmed, however. Regaining his composure, the Yale graduate points out to a young lady in the media.] RP1 : Phoenix McAlester from the Wrestling Insider. Raymond, you said that you have signed some of the best up and comers in the game care to drop a few names? [Raymond smirks before replying.] AR : We have talent from the North, South, East and West, Ms. McAlester. From wrestling veterans like "Extreme War Machine" Corey Irons to "The Odyssey" Dante Madison. Also, with men like "The Only Star" Eric Dane and "Jester" Chad Allen who was most recently on Wrestlebowl IV. Not to mention newcomers Memo Machado, and "Too Hot" Scottie Foxx our roster is growing daily, and looking extremely talented from viewing their N.B.W. tryouts. PMA : Are you in contract negotiations with major stars like Chris Courtade, or Mike Waldrop? AR : No, Phoenix, that isn't the audience we wanted to target. While I believe a man like Mike Waldrop is one of the best I have ever seen; we found a problem. [Pause.] Everyone has seen him. There is so much talent out there in the wrestling world today, that we didn't want to send him contract offers. We felt like our talent is the "cream of the crop," and now, they will get the exposure that they deserve. Next question, please. [Raymond looses his tie while waiting on the next question.] RP2 : Howard Finley here with W.o.W., World of Wrestling. Raymond, New Blood Wrestling is going to be right here in Washington, D.C.; how do you see things working out here in the Nation's capital? AR : As you know, this was my fathers' hometown, born and raised. So, it is only fitting to give back to his hometown. And, it is the nation's capital, name a better place to run an organization! And, if there is a better place, N.B.W. will pay them a visit sooner then later. And to further support my answer, look at the other 'top' promotions sale rates while visiting the capital! Washington, Maryland and Virginia have the best fans. That is for sure. Now they will have nonstop action, and double the satisfaction of wrestling right nearby. [Another reporter fire away!] RP3 : Ryan Parker, RSPWF. Mr. Raymond do you have a line-up for the first show? Title matches? Anything special we could tell your fans? AR : Good question, I like that. Yes, Ryan, you can tell the fans that all matches will be scheduled at the show. The first show will be held in an impromptu style for the first card. However, I can tell you that all of the New Blood talent will be in action on that night. RP : Special matches? AR : I'm shooting for the stars, Mr. Parker. That is all I can say for now. [Standing up is Brian Perkins, he rushes over to the podium.] BP : Ladies and gentlemen, thank you for your time, we look forward to seeing all of you again. But, for tonight, that is all the time we have, as we are set to meet with possible talent. Thank you. [And with that, we fade. That's it folks, think what you wanna think, but know that June 15th is the landmark day. Be there!]
