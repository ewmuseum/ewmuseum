Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The Fantasy Wrestling League announced itself “off and running” on rec.sport.pro-wrestling.fantasy on 19 July 1995, run on the WLS 5.1 simulator with separate men's and ladies leagues and title matches staged as specialty matches. Entrants were barred from copying a real wrestler, a clone, or a real wrestler's own name, could enter more than one wrestler apiece, and could even field a whole stable; applications closed two days later, on 21 July.

On 28 July its president, also billed as a founding member and president of the Mutants of C.W.U.S.A., reported that the results of the league's own World Title tournament were delayed: his computer had crashed mid-tournament, and he promised the results would be posted “tomorrow.”

No further FWL document survives. Whether that World Title tournament was ever completed, and how or when the league itself ended, is not in the surviving record.
