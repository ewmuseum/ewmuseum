BLAZE UPDATE 12.23.2001 Hello all BLAZE participants and observers, The Silent Winds "REBIRTH" card has finally been released after a five month hiatus period. This is completely unprofessional and I sincerely apologize for my actions. My actions may have greatly reduced the enjoyment, interest, and entertainment value of the game for many who have put their trust in me to produce a consistent, quality e-wrestling product and for that I'm sorry. I showed no consideration for anyone's feelings, despite the events of 9-11-2001 and other issues in my personal life. Rather than leave everyone hanging on the status of the card and future of the promotion, I should have been more honest about how I was feeling and made it clear that I was taking some time away from the game to resurrect my passion and motivation. I couldn't force myself to write, and I was somewhat depressed with the direction of my life, so my passion and desire for e-wrestling was non-existent, however after time to reflect I feel very rejuvenated. I'm ready to bring BLAZE back to life and develop it into the best women's e-wrestling promotion on the internet and with your help I feel we can eventually achieve that goal. 2002 will be a year to remember for BLAZE and the way I want to kick it off is with the Silent Winds "MASSACRE IN SHIBUYA" PPV on Saturday January 26th. The reason I'm running the show this late in the month of January is because of two factors. Factor #1: With Christmas upon us and New Years Eve around the corner, everyone most likely will be spending time with family and friends and that's more important than writing RP's. Factor #2: I myself will be out of town the second week of January, 8th-13th, so I won't be writing anything that week. That means the deadline for RP's on the next Silent Winds card will be Sunday January 13th, MIDNIGHT! This will provide people with plenty of time coming off the Holiday's to RP so please do so. After January I will run two shows a month. Remember, the whole key to success in BLAZE is providing consistent/quality work for your wrestler. I reward consistency so keep in mind that at times you may not be able to write a masterpiece RP, but if you are consistent I will push your wrestler. The handlers with wrestlers at or near the top in BLAZE, present the most consistent/quality work. Although I have been experimenting with different formats/systems in regards to match writing, I have decided to stay with the format I'm using now and find various ways to perfect it. Most of the format I'm using now was inspired by [name withheld] RINGST*RS and [name withheld] JIWWA (Japan Independent Women's Wrestling Association). Hopefully with more experience and learning, I can one day capture the superior level of Joshi Puroresu action that Chris and James represented in their work. As far as the BLAZE webpage is concerned, hopefully Niels and I can get that completed by the end of January. For those handlers who haven’t send me a picture of their wrestler, please do so. I want to enhance the visual aspect of the wrestlers in BLAZE as much as possible. Finally, everyone have a save, peaceful, Holiday and a happy New Year! [name withheld], BLAZE -------------------------------------------------------------------------- ------ NEWS AKIRA KAHN RETIRES! By ASUKA KANSAI, AP Joshi Puroesu Writer TOKYO, Japan (AP)- Akira Kahn, one of the best ever Joshi Puroresu wrestlers in the sport will officially retire from active wrestling Saturday January 26th, 2002 after 8 years in the business. Kahn, who is known throughout Japan as "The Phenomenon" will wrestle her final match against former 3WU Champ "Beauty Star" Angel Saito on the Silent Winds "MASSACRE IN SHIBUYA" show. After speaking with Kahn earlier this week she provided the reason for her departure. "When I first got into this business, my ultimate agenda was to do whatever I could to advance the concept of Joshi Puroresu. I feel I have exhausted all my abilities inside the ring and now is the proper time to step down and promote Joshi Puroresu exclusively as CEO of BLAZE. I'm at peace with what I have accomplished in this sport. Everything, from becoming world champion in both Japan and the United States, to the controversial MillenniuM faction I created in a few United States promotions, all the way to the legendary angle I once ran in the IFWF (Independent Female Wrestling Federation), the various students I've trained in the Hellfire Dojo and the countless number of wrestlers I have beaten around the world. I wouldn't trade any of those experiences for the world. What I have learned over the years, is women's wrestling needs strong ambassadors to promote the craft and as fast as women's promotions come and go the craft has suffered tremendously. This has always been a male dominated business, but I believe with my heart and soul women's wrestling can reach and maintain a powerful presence in the wrestling mainstream and that's what I want to accomplish with BLAZE." After being asked whether or not she would ever return to the ring Kahn stated, "I'm very secure with decision to retire and I don't see myself returning for any reasons. It's a chapter in my life that will be closed forever". There had been speculation that Kahn would retire near the end of the year, but now that speculation will become a reality. Kahn's career includes three World titles, two World tag team titles, various other titles, and many memorable moments, from her MEGA heel status in the United States to her uncanny wrestling skills in Japan. More important, Kahn over the years developed a image that has earned her respect from fans, media, and other wrestlers around the world. Her presence in inside the ring will be missed. -------------------------------------------------------------------------- ------ MAIN EVENT- SPARROW YOKOTA VS TARA SMITH AT "MASSACRE IN SHIBUYA" ANNOUNCED! By TONY CASTRO AP, Joshi Puroresu Reporter TOKYO, Japan (AP)- BLAZE CEO Akira Kahn announced today that "Tornado" Tara Smith will defend her BLAZE Triple Crown Women's Universal Championship against BLAZE International Women's Champion, SPARROW Yokota at the Silent Winds "MASSACRE IN SHIBUYA" 1/26 Club ATOM show. The match will only be for the 3WU Championship and not be the title versus title match as many would expect. Both women have been very successful in BLAZE and have yet to wrestle each other which makes this match intriguing. The undefeated SPARROW Yokota can make history by becoming the first wrestler to hold both 3WU and IW titles simultaneously if she is able to beat Tara Smith, however that's a task easier said than done. Tara Smith on the other hand, is now faced with her one of the toughest challenges of her career. If she defeats Yokota, can she truly say she's pound for pound the best wrestler in BLAZE? Many fans and media say no, because she has yet to wrestle Dalbello Rage, Yori Kenyuki or upcoming Hellfire Dojo star "Mocking Bird" Nina Grimsson, but I say yes. I don't know if I can find a harder worker in BLAZE than Tara Smith. If she beats Yokota it will be a testament to her superior skill and ability. The official contract signing for the BLAZE Triple Crown Women's Universal Title match between Yokota and Smith will take place December 28th, 2001 in the ballroom of the Royal Park Hotel. -------------------------------------------------------------------------- ------ RANKINGS BLAZE Triple Crown Women's Universal(3WU) Champion- "Torando" Tara Smith BLAZE International Women's (IW) Champion- SPARROW Yokota 1.
