Part I: BLAZE 1.1 What is BLAZE? =============== BLAZE is a heavily booked influenced wrestling promotion [e-wrestling] structured to capture the various elements and stress the ultimate experience of Joshi Puroresu [women's wrestling] in Japan. Joshi Puroresu is a different form and concept of pro wrestling than what is usually seen in the United States. A fast and highly intense level of skill, workrate and perfection of craft are emphasized over promos, and T & A. Clean pinfall or submission endings to a given match are greatly embraced over count outs, disqualifications, and run-in endings. Angles are not as common as in the United States, however BLAZE has been receptive to different sorts of angles. BLAZE works in a unique wrestling style of shoot, hardcore, lucha libre, pro, and martial arts, and seeks to always display the respect, honor, heart and fighting warrior's spirit in it's matches. 1.2 Progression Beyond All Standards ============================ Progression Beyond All Standards is the motto of BLAZE. To understand this means one must understand the root or foundation of professional wrestling. When professional wrestling was created it started out with a hypothesis. But now it's a gospel truth that emphasizes laws and principles which must not be violated, so in e-wrestling when handlers get into it whether they handle wrestlers, or become Fed Heads they become product of that truth. It doesn't seem to matter if you want to do things in a different or creative way in regards to the writing of a match, the structure of a promo/ roleplay, or the advancement of a angle. If you don't conform to that gospel truth than your wrestler/ character is not pushed or your promotion is frowned upon and to me that is a travesty. BLAZE encourages honest, consistent, creative expression. The more creative and consistent you are in the development and evolution of your wrestler the more your wrestler will succeed. In the realm of BLAZE everyone from the bookers to the handlers seek to transcend all standards in a effort to maximize the best possible product. 1.3 The Path of Elite Advancement ========================== The concept behind the philosophy "The Path of Elite Advancement" comes from the notion that a wrestler must be empowered to become the best in the promotion and this leads to the elite path where your wrestler is then crowned CHAMPION. In BLAZE all the wrestling doctrines, patterns, forms, and styles, mean absolutely nothing without clarity and commitment. To excel among the best your wrestler/ character has to be empowered with passion before advancement can be properly obtained. Part II: A Booked E- Joshi Puroresu Promotion 2.1 History ========= Wrestling great Akira Kahn and NRJ [New Rising Japan] CEO Takashi Koshinaka, had a meeting on October 5th, 1999 to discuss concerns regarding the evaporating state of women's wrestling in Japan. The result of that meeting was the formation of BLAZE, with the agenda of preserving the purity and beauty of the Joshi Puroresu craft for the benefit of future generations BLAZE held it's virgin show at the Korakuen Hall on December 10th, 1999 and featured 1st round matches of the 3WU Championship tournament. At Madison Square Garden in New York City on December 19th, 1999, "Beauty Star" Angel Saito was crowned the 1st ever 3WU Singles Champion with a victory over "Tornado" Tara Smith in the finals. The promotion went on to run shows throughout 2000, then on January 8th, 2001 BLAZE officially merged with "Izumimax Productions", a licensing, distribution, and production company which bolsters the distribution rights to programs produced by Fuji Television Network Inc. 2.2 How matches are booked ====================== Who wins matches in BLAZE are determined and based on the three golden aspects of promos/ flashes, current angle[s], and strats. If wrestler [A] shines in all three aspects over wrestler [B], then wrestler [A] will win the match. 2.3 Application ============ Name: Height: Weight: Hometown: Alignment: Theme Music: Wrestling Attire: Ring Entrance: Profile/ Biography: Wrestling Style: Championships Held: Move List: [At least 15, no more than 30] Finishing Maneuver :[Description if necessary] Alternate Finishing Maneuver : 2.4 Promos/ Interviews ================== Every two weeks, BLAZE will release a flash show featuring interview segments by the wrestlers. Triple Crown Women Universal [3WU] Singles Championship - The recognized World Title of BLAZE. 2. BLAZE International Women's Championship - The secondary singles championship of BLAZE. 3. Triple Crown Women's Universal [3WU] Tag Team Championship 4. World Women's Wrestling Independent [3WI] Tag Team Championship - Tag Team championship of the J.I.W.W.A, but is defended on BLAZE Silent Winds shows 3.2 Title contention =============== Contention for each title is based upon a wrestler's win/loss record versus the quality of the opposition thus, just because wrestler A has ten [10] wins does not mean she will get a title shot, especially if all her victories were against wrestler B. If a handler feels his/her character deserves a higher ranking, he/she has the right to have their case heard before the Booking Committee. The same goes for the right to demand a title shot. Some guidelines are to be followed however. The Triple Crown Women Universal [3WU] Singles Championship - To be defended on almost every card against a Ranked challenger within the top 5. BLAZE believes in the notion that every champion should be a fighting champion. There are no dead weight champions in BLAZE. These are mandatory defenses. The BLAZE International Women's Championship - This title also will be defended on almost every card against any opponent ranked in the top 10. The Triple Crown Women's Universal [3WU] Tag Team Championship - This title will be defended once [1] every month against any ranked team. However, once every two months, the champions MUST defend their titles against the 1st ranked team in the division. 3.3 Ranking Matches ================ Any ranked competitor in BLAZE has the right to challenge for a higher ranking no more than 2 spots above her current position in a "Ranking Match". This a wrestler ranked at License 6 may challenge either License Position 5 or License Position 4. If the challenger wins the match she then assumes the losers License Position. The loser in turn assumes the winners old License Position in the rankings. 3.4 Junko Shiratori Memorial Ascension Cup ================================= A annual tournament held in the honor of the late J.I.W.W.A wrestling star Junko Shiratori. The tournament selects the most elite [8] applicants per year. The winner of the tournament receives the Ascension Cup and a guaranteed shot at the 3WU Championship. 3.5 Year End Triumph Awards ======================= At the end of each year [Triumph] Awards will be given out to honor the accomplishments of the wrestlers.
