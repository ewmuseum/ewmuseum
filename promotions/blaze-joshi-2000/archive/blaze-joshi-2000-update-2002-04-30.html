BLAZE UPDATE 04.30.2002 Hello all BLAZE participants and observers, Nearly all the applications on the current BLAZE roster have been restructured and move set expanded. Everyone now will have 32 total moves including the finishers. I was going to add a damage percentage rating to each wrestler's move, but decided against it as that would take the challenge out of strategizing for certain moves a wrestler may have. I have created a Yahoo E Group website and will use that as the BLAZE website at this point. It's more important to have some form of a website rather than not have one at all. The address to the new BLAZE website is http://groups.yahoo.com/group/BlazeAlpha/ I have all the roster applications located in the files section and I have 3 photo folders of "The Goddess" Akiko Yamazaki, Miyuki Ohmura, and "Beauty Star" Angel Saito located of course in the photo section. I hope everyone enjoys it. If you have 5 or more pics of your wrestler, please send them to me through email and I will create a photo folder for them. If you need help with pics, let me know and I will be more than happy to help you. The updated rankings and card lineup for the next BLAZE show is listed below in the event section of this email. All RP'S and STRATS for the next show are due MAY 10TH, 2002 MIDNIGHT CT! This will be our second stop on the "Burning Spirits" tour. Until next time Peace!
