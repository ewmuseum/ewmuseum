BLAZE UPDATE 08.01.2002 Hello all BLAZE participants and observers, In a brief bit of news, the BLAZE website has been updated with small adjustments to ALL the wrestler applications on the roster. "La Femme Diabolique" Josie Saito has recently signed with BLAZE and her application has been added to the website. Also a profile for Silent Winds Host Asuka Kansai has been added to the website. The BLAZE FAQ, STRATEGY TEMPLATE, and APPLICATION documents have been updated as well. To find all the updates, please look in the files section of the website.
