BLAZE UPDATE 05.04.2002 Hello all BLAZE participants and observers, All applications regarding the current BLAZE roster have been updated to the BLAZE website. Also the FAQ, Strat Template, and Application files have been added to the website. Please remember that all RP'S and STRATS for the next show are due MAY 10TH, 2002 MIDNIGHT CT! If you need any help with RP and STRAT concepts/ideas, please let me know. I would be more than happy to help you. To the handler of Lida Yanagisawa, I'm not sure if I still have your email address. If you read this in the "rec.sport.pro-wrestling.fantasy" forum, please email me ASAP. In a final bit of news, the JIWWA (Japanese Independent Women's Wrestling Association) from now on will be running cards through BLAZE, but both promotions will still be two separate entities, however BLAZE wrestlers are welcome to compete on JIWWA cards and JIWWA wrestlers on BLAZE cards. Expect the JIWWA KAMIKAZE HEARTS 2002 "VIRGIN BRAWL" show to released either the first or second week in June right before the BLAZE SILENT WINDS "JUNKO SHIRATORI MEMORIAL ASCENSION CUP" show. Until next time Peace!
