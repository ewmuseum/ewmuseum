BLAZE UPDATE 05.24.2002 Hello all BLAZE participants and observers, There has been a few slight changes regarding two matches on this upcoming "Endangered Species" card. The 1st Silent Wind match between TaJu and Tara "Sunburst" Marshall has now been made into a "Triple Threat Match". The third wrestler involved in the match will be a new signed talent to BLAZE "Indian Princess" Dasha Tarsem. The second slight change will see the "Japanese Electrified Barbed Wire Steel Cage Death Match between "Burning Cherry Blossom" Rekka Sakura and "The Goddess" Akiko Yamazaki now be made into a standard BLAZE match. On different note, I have been working on a match project that I will introduce to everyone in the near future called "MATRIX Evolution 2.0". I'm still putting together all the groundwork for it, however when it's completed, look for it to be a lot of fun. I also need to remind everyone that RP'S and STRATS for the next show are due MAY 31ST, 2002 MIDNIGHT CT!
