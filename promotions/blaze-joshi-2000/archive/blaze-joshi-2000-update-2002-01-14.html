BLAZE UPDATE 01.14.2002 Hello all BLAZE participants and observers, I have just returned from visiting some family in Michigan and I wanted to see if everyone was squared away in regard to RP's and strats for "MASSACRE IN
SHIBUYA". The deadline was yesterday, however I have only received RP's and strats from a limited amount of people who have wrestler's participating on the card. For those who haven't sent me anything, if there is a problem of some sort please email me ASAP and perhaps something can be worked out. I want to make this a fun and enjoyable experience for everyone so everyone's participation is very important and appreciated. If anyone has questions of any kind please feel free to email me.
[name withheld], BLAZE NEWS TOP 100 WOMEN WRESTLER'S OF 2001 Its the end of the year, and about this time, all the polls start to come out. There's the Top 200 Wrestler List and the Top 50 Tag Team list. If I remember correctly there was a Top 100 Women Wrestler list but this year,
I've yet to see a Top Women's poll. So this year, I have set out to do one myself in order. This is not an "official" poll by any means and it shouldn't be taken as so. This is just for fun.
Its simple. Make a list of the top ten women wrestlers of the year with their full name and the feds that they are in and send the ballot to
[detail withheld]
. For every first place vote, 10 points will be awarded. For every second place vote, 9 points will be awards. For every third, 8 points and so on.
There are a few ground rules of course. 1. NO VOTES FOR MEN- As funny as it was having Rick Styles being voted as the Top Female Wrestler in the 2001 Year End Awards, it takes away from the poll. Any ballot that includes a male wrestler will be instantly disqualified.
2. All ballots need to be sent in by February 15th. Anything received after that will not be counted. 3. All votes need to be sent to [detail withheld] 4. Any vote from a free e-mail service such as Hotmail will be discounted to help avoid spamming. However, if its the only email you have, send me an email and we'll work out something.
5. Please do the complete Top Ten. A Top Eight or a Top Seven is acceptable but anything less than a Top Five isn't. 6. This is a serious poll and much like Rule #1, any joke that is obviously meant to "mess with" the poll, will be disqualified. Should be a good poll and I look forward to your votes. Thanks Kyle Lee ALESSANDRA AMBROSIO NAMED SIREN OF THE WEEK! In Erexim, a small town in southern Brazil, Alessandra Ambrosio was born on
April 11, 1981. Despite her travels around the world, this tiny town is the place she loves the most. "I'll never forget my little city! I could talk a whole day about it!" The close-knit Erexim is the kind of place where everyone knows everyone else. She says growing up there was "like being part of one big family. "Which is exactly why Erexim is so proud to see one of their own starring in an international GUESS? campaign.
Currently a full fledged jet-setter, Alessandra regularly flies between her New
York apartment, to the European fashion shows and back to Brazil where she is busy building a house. Alessandra's career started when she enrolled in a local modeling course which led her to compete in Elite's Look Of The Year contest.
After the contest, she signed a modeling contract with Elite and they passed along some of her Polaroids to GUESS?. Before long, she and her friend, fellow
Brazilian Ana Beatriz Barros, were booked for the Millennium GUESS? campaign.
In addition to her GUESS? work, expect to see even more of Alessandra in the upcoming Revlon Cosmetics campaign. Alessandra cites her favorite pastime as spending time with her boyfriend of 2 1/2 years, Jayson Lee, a model and club promoter. Despite their hectic schedules the two always manage to find time to see each other. She says her favorite day is spent sandboarding with her boyfriend, then lying on the beach listening to Brazilian singer Caetano Velozo and drinking coconut water. After modeling, she plans to study at the university and develop a certain project (sssh! it's a secret) that she is sure will make her an even bigger success.
Alessandra's advice to others who would like to pursue modeling is simple: "Take care of yourself, be healthy, and always believe you can be successful in anything you truly want."
