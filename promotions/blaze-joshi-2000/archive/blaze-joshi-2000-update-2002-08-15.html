BLAZE UPDATE 08.15.2002 Hello all BLAZE participants and observers, Well, the Silent Winds "EYE OF THE STROM VOL.1" card should now be in everyone's mailbox. Considering this was the first experimentation into the screenplay format, I feel the card came out decent, however there is STILL much room for improvement. One thing that immediately comes to mind that I see I personally need to improve on is dialogue and story structure. I felt the dialogue I provided for my characters, Asuka Kansai, Tony Castro, SHIRI,
Temptress Kuzumi, Mizuki Muto, and others non BLAZE related was too rigid and lacked development of character. I have been recently studying dialogue more closely and I have came up with some ideas on how to the dialogue of all my characters in the near future. I want to make sure everyone can understand and emotionally connect with my characters, so that is something I'm trying the accomplish with my writing. In regard to the storylines, I believe with the past card and the next card, a solid foundation will be created from which to build. Personally, I have all kinds of ideas that can be used to build the storyline to an incredible cinematic level. I'm sure you all have ideas too, and I would be more than happy to work with all of you toward turning those ideas into a reality.
Ok, with that out the way, I want to turn my attention to the next card, Silent Winds "EYE OF THE STROM VOL.2". Because of Bob and [name withheld] (handlers of
Tara Smith and Nina Grimsson), going on vacation and Rocko Rok (handler of
Madison Stone), getting married, (congratulations again Rocko), there will be NO title defenses on this next card. The title defenses will resume the card following this next one, which would be the Silent Winds "EYE OF THE STROM
VOL.3" card. The deadline for RP'S and STRATS for the next card is SATURDAY, AUGUST 31ST, 2002 MIDNIGHT! CST! All the matches scheduled for the next card are listed below.
