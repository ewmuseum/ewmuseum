Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The United Kingdom Wrestling Alliance's surviving record is four recruiting notices running from 25 November 2001 to 20 January 2002, each pointing to the same GeoCities address, and each accepting only original characters rather than real-wrestler portrayals.

It ran one card a week, Wednesday Night Wreckage, alongside a pay-per-view or house show every two weeks, and on 15 December 2001 opened a house show to outside talent from other federations as a proving ground for prospective UKWA wrestlers. Its earliest notices set out four titles — Heavyweight, Tag Team, British and Light-Heavyweight — and by 2 December 2001 it had added a Women's Championship and a gimmick Challenge Belt, modelled, in its own words, on a cross between the WWF Hardcore Title and Smoky Mountain's 24/7 belt.

By 20 January 2002 it was recruiting again after cutting what it called “a lot of poor talent,” still pointing to the same address.

No card, roster, title result, or closing notice of its own survives beyond these four notices.
