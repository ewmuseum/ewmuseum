Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Two posts on the same July day announce the same idea twice: a message-board promotion "born, out of a dream," open for anyone willing to fill out an application. Within a week it had booked a stadium in Liverpool for its first live event and drawn a first-round hardcore tournament bracket around a small stable of invented brawlers -- a masked figure called Death Master, a tag pairing in Fire Hazard and the Flying Cat, a rival billed only as Fatwah Sam.

The card grew a second attraction as it went: a newcomer named Chris "the Grappler" Cogan, introduced feuding with the promotion's own president -- a wrestler who worked the microphone one week and the ring the next, putting his own bye into a world title tournament on the line against him inside a cage.

That tournament, staged for a first pay-per-view billed as Ignition, is the last thing the held record shows: an eight-man bracket with two wrestlers already through on a bye, set against fresh names -- Frost, Time Bomb, Union Jack, a pair of arrivals fighting under the banner of "Representatives of Hell," and another pair styled "The Psycho Brotherz." Whether either tournament ever reached a finish, this record does not say.

What survives in between is the ordinary noise of a small promotion trying to stay fed: a near-daily run of recruitment calls, complaints about a roster stacked with more heels than faces, and a running joke about the initials themselves, floated more than once as standing for something considerably sillier than "Alliance."
