Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

A federation calling itself the FWA posted a recruitment notice to rec.sport.pro-wrestling.fantasy on 1 June 2000, its own president asking for “wrestlers, writers, anybody,” hosted at an Angelfire address of its own.

The post set out what it wanted in a recruit — roleplayers who could “use some kind of fonts,” and writers able to turn out results — and closed with the plain arithmetic of a federation still building itself: “the more we have, the better we are.”

No further post, roster, or result survives; the recruitment notice is the whole of its own surviving record.
