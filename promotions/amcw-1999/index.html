Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

American e-Mail Championship Wrestling's surviving record is its own FAQ, version 3.0, which states the promotion was created on 1 May 1999. Its founder had just closed L.A.W., spent time following EMWC as “a backstage fan, learning the card styles, looking at the talent,” and then, encouraged by everyone he called, opened this promotion instead.

It staged itself out of its own AMCW Hall in Queens, New York, though its cards also toured arenas and high schools around the country, across three weekly programs: WIRED on Saturdays, hosted by Dave Van Ryan and Adam Rotten, carrying the promotion's own angles and title defenses; Ringside on Wednesdays, a taped house-show recap hosted by Lance Caruso; and Ring Report on Mondays, a rankings-and-rumors update hosted by Rick Storm.

Four championships were named — the Heavyweight, Juniorweight and Television titles and the Tag Team Belts — none yet assigned to a signed wrestler, alongside a promised summer flagship card, AmericanMania, and cards running every two or three months through the rest of the year.

How those plans played out isn't in the surviving record; the FAQ itself, dated 28 April 1999, is the last surviving document under this promotion's own name.
