VWWF Looking for TOP-NOTCH free agents

8 March 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hey guys.. The VWWF has been around for about 4 1/2 months now, and over that time we have grown leaps and bounds. We started as a fed with all real wrestlers, but, as time has passed, created wrestlers are taking over the fed. Our ultimate goal is to have ALL created wrestlers with the exception of maybe 2 or 3 real guys...
We have a very good quality set of mid-card werstlers (TV, US, Lightweight titles), but we are stille in need of two BIG-TIME Free
Agents who can roleplay VERY well and become a main-eventer in our fed. In my estimation, we only have maybe 2 or 3 main eventers, and
I'd like to have a couple more...Only serious applicants need apply
Here is some info about our fed:
1) We are role-play based. Those who roleplay well WILL be pushed and be pushed hard. This, along with a good gimmick, is what will take you to the top. 2) There are no freaky "Pit of hell death matches". We consider ourselves a realistic and reputable fed. We wil have no part in these ridiculous matches or any other type of "overboard" hardcore matches.
We will have some hardcore matches now and then, but they will not rule the fed. 3) Storylines, storylines, storylines.......I can't say enough about this. We will listen to you when you give us ideas for angles/storylines for your wrestler, but PLEASE don't email us every week asking to interfere and destroy the World Champion...it just doesn't happen like that. One thing that REALLY impresses me is when someone is able to adapt and go with the storylines we put out. If we do pull some kind of major story with you, you should be able to roleplay and sell that angle.
4) We do book our matches. You will only get matches that you deserve. Requests by bad roleplayers to fight for a World Title will be ignored. We want everything to flow well, and sometimes your request just doesn't fit in with what's happening.
5) As you can see, we are not a little kiddy fed. Me and my Co-Commissioner are college students at Princeton University and
University of Houston. We do things fairly, and we push people who deserve to be pushed. We are looking to hire another person in the near future to help with hyping up angles/ commentary, etc...
To join, go to our web page and fill out an application! http://butchmac.digitalchainsaw.com/vwwf/ [name withheld] VWWF Commish http://butchmac.digitalchainsaw.com/
