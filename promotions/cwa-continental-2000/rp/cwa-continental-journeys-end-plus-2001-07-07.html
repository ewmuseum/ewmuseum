CWA (Continental, 2000) — Roleplay

CWA Journey's End Plus

7 July 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Todd Michaelson: Hello CWA fans, this is Todd Michaelson giving you some very quick late breaking news after the PPV! Journey's End has come and gone and as a result we have two new champions in Shane Russell the CWA Traditional
Champion as well as Jasen Casen the CWA North American Champion! We would also see the return of one Max Boulder attacking hometown hero Mark Blaze and former
World Boxing Champion Marvelous Marvin Hagler! Chainsaw Charlie successfully defended the World Title against Frat Boy Perry Tanhammer in which many are saying in controversial fashion! Larry Gionet went for Charlie only to nail
Frat Boy instead.
Todd Michaelson: In the other bouts, Anthony Davis defeated The Psychotic One with the help of his new buddy Sean Harris! Ace Fireside defeated Chris
Natural. Mark Blaze defeated Buddy Costello in the Brockton Brawl. Kevin
Candido defeated Larry Gionet in their match as well as Street Punk defeating
THP with the help of Kevin Candido! But what lingered on the minds of the fans was if the Chainsaw Charlie-Joe Snow pact as well as the Larry Gionet-Frat Boy pact was still in tact sort to speak. Many questions still remained to be answered but Joe Snow has decided to speak his mind in front of the whole world and give his thoughts on the world title match!
{ The scene opens up inside the locker room area of the Hartford Civic Center directly after the greuling main event match between Chainsaw Charlie and Frat
Boy Perry Tanhammer. The camera turns and focuses on the locker room of the
"Snow Dogg" Joe Snow. The door opens and shows Joe sitting in his locker room with a shit eating grin on his face. Joe stares directly into the camera and begins to speak. }
Joe Snow: I bet you are all wondering why I came in and helped Chainsaw Charlie retain his CWA Title. I bet you are all thinking that I did it because Charlie and I are "friends", that I did it because of the long history we have and the respect that we have for each other.
{ Joe turns his face away from the camera for a moment lets out a brief chuckle and turns back towards the camera. }
Joe Snow: Well if that is the case then you had better think again. You see
Charlie 2 weeks ago you and I stepped into this ring to fight for the CWA
Title. In the end you were the one that scored the pinfall, you were the one that walked out with the belt and you proved once again that you deserve to be a champion. But I ask you this Charlie, do you deserve to be the CWA Champion?
Do you deserve to have that CWA World Title around your waist. Let me answer that question for you. NO!! You do not deserve to represent the CWA and call yourself it's champion.
{ Joe gets a sudden look of seriousness on his face. He takes a couple of deep breaths and begins to speak again. }
JS: Charlie let me take you back down memory lane. Let me take you back to a point in the CWA history that I'm sure you and the fans have forgotten.
{ Joe Snow gets out of his seat and walks across his locker room where there is a television set with a VCR hooked up to it. Joe turns on the television and
VCR and turns back towards the camera. }
JS: Those of you who do not wish to have your eyes opened to the truth about your CWA champion should now leave the room because what I am about to show you wil shock and completly repulse you.
{ Joe bends over and presses the play button on the VCR. The image on the television becomes Chainsaw Charlie with the CWA Title around his waist and a sledge hammer in hand. The video proceeds to play and shows Chainsaw Charlie removing the belt from his waist and dropping it on the ground. Charlie flips off the camera and smashes the sledge hammer into the CWA Title. Charlie raises the sledge hammer over his head and slams it down onto the CWA World
Title. Charlie smashes the title four more times and then proceeds to spit on it. Charlie picks up the demolished title, holds it to the camera and says
"Thats what I think of the CWA." At this point Joe Snow bends over and stops the video. Joe looks up at the camera with a look of dissapointment. } JS: Bring back memories Chuckles?? Do you even remember that?? WELL I DO!!! The image of you repeatedly smashing that title has burned a hole in my heart since the whole CWA/WWC thing started. Charlie you made your decision and now
I am making mine. You decided to turn to the WWC and completly abandon and desecrate the CWA when it looked like things weren't going to well here. You decided that the grass was greener on the other side and you turned your back on me, you turned your back on the fans and you turned your back on the CWA.
Now the last few months you have come back and played to the fans and acted as if nothing ever happened and expected everything to be perfect. The fans forgave you, the suits in the front office accepted you and everybody was happy. So you came back and went after the CWA's top heel Perry Tanhammer.
You beat him for the belt and went back to calling yourself champion. { Joe Snow wipes his face with his hand and walks around the locker room a few times to calm himself down. Joe walks back to his seat, sits down and begins to speak again. }
JS: When you won that title everything went back to the way it used to be. The fans still cheered you, they still bought your T-Shirts and once again I was cast back into your shadow. You walked around saying that you were the CWA champion and that anybody who dared to challenge you would be guarenteed to bleed. Well tonight Charlie, I am steping up to the plate. I am here in this ring to call you out and challenge you to a match at the next Pay Per View for the CWA World Title. I will step in this ring with you and do what somebody should have done a long time ago, stand up for the CWA and take back the CWA
World Title. Maybe by challenging you I have just signed my death warrant, maybe I am guarenteed to bleed but ya know what Charlie, I frankly don't give a damn. I have sat back and watched you parade around here with that belt like you are the god of the CWA. I have busted my ass for this company and I am not going to sit back and watch you disrespect this organization anymore.
JS: Now, earlier tonight night when I interfered in your match and helped you retain the title. I was not because we are friends, it was not because I respect you it was because I made a promise to myself that I would be the man that would end your reign of terror on the CWA. So Charlie if you are the fighting champoin that you are then meet me at the Pay Per View, put the title on the line and prove to me that you truly are deserving of forgivness for the disrespect that you have caused this great organization. And when the time comes and you and I step foot in that ring I may be guarented to bleed but all of the blood will be worth it. Because I have given my blood, my sweat and my tears for this company and I will do whatever it takes to make sure that the
CWA Title ends up around the waist of the one man that will represent this
great company until the end, and that man is me, the SNOW D-O-DOUBLE G JOE SNOW!!!! { Joe Snow gets up from his seat and walks towards the shower as the camera fades to black. The screen then cuts back to Todd Michaelson.}
Todd Michaelson: Joe Snow has now officially challenged the CWA World Champion
Chainsaw Charlie for a world title match for our next PPV in July entitled
Blood, Sweat and Tears on July 29th. We will keep you updated on Charlie's reaction to Joe Snow's challenge! I have the lineup for this friday's CWA show
Traditon!
July 6th, 2001 Main Event: Chainsaw Charlie/Joe Snow vs Larry Gionet/Frat Boy Perry Tanhammer CWA North American Title match: Jasen Casen (Champion) vs Street Punk (Challenger) CWA Traditional Title match: Shane Russell (Champion) vs Chad Yaro (Challenger) The Psychotic One vs Sean Harris A.J. Darkk vs Anthony "The Animal" Davis Scotty Harrison vs Adam Mitchell
