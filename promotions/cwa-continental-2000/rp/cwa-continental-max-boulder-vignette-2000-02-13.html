CWA vignette: “The Bulldozer” Max Boulder

13 February 2000
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[detail withheld] This is the addy of my fed.  Check it out, if it interests you apply. Chuck T (aka Commish Dick) [name withheld] < [detail withheld] > wrote in message news:[detail withheld] ... With the recent closing of ACCW, Pash Kavana needs a new federation that he can call home! I've included a sample roleplay for my other character, Max
Boulder because I don't have one of Pash's handy. If you need some statistics on Kavana, let me know and I'll send them to you.
Here is what I am looking for in a fed: ** Although I doubt there are any on RSPWF, absolutely NO simmed feds! ** Flash packets only. I don't care how often they come out but no roleplay boards or open mail roleplaying! ** I could care less if you have been open for 3 days or 3 years. How organized your fed looks will decide for me. It's not much to ask, if you meet the requirements and are interested in
Kavana,
either send me the URL to your fed or your promotion's handbook/guide/FAQ.
Here is the sample of Boulder: [The shot opens up to a black screen, the entire screen is pitch black (except the snow from the crappy reception because you are a cheap bastard and won't buy a better television set) until a mans voice can be heard while the words appear on the screen before you.]
Voice Over: Once in a period of time, comes something so grand that words themself cannot even explain the moment. C W A, this Thursday on
Tradition
a man so great that he is already headlining the card comes to town. C W A,
"The Bulldozer" has arrived! [The shot now cuts to a construction site where powerful machinery is hard at work digging up and moving dirt, all the while the other members of the constuction crew stand scratching their asses, instead of doing their job. As the camera pans across the site, it stops at a very well built man wearing a black T-shit with the words
"Bulldozer Boulder"
sprawled across it.] Max Boulder: Let me introduce myself CWA, and I'm only going to do this once because after Thursday my name will go down in the history books. My name is "The Bulldozer" Max Boulder. Now the President of this fine establishment made a wise choice when he handed me my freshly signed contract. Because now, not only do you great fans have the chance to watch the greatest performer in
CWA history but 9 unlucky wrestlers will have the dishonor of being Bulldozed this Thursday! [Max motions for the cameraman to follow him a bit as the noise from the trackers and such are begining to be picked up on the camera.]
Max Boulder: Now I could stand here and go over the list of wrestlers and what I am going to do them like everyone else seems to love to do.
But you see, that isn't like my style. I'm not here to bore the hell out of you, and I would be lying anyway. Because when I get in the ring, I am just going to open up on everybody. There is no gameplan in a battle royal. I am just going to go in there and wreak as much havoc as I can.
[Max takes his hardhat off and whipes his brow once, showing just how hot it is at the present time.]
Max Boulder: Now it's a great pleasure to be in the main event of not only my first CWA match, but my first professional wrestling match all together but there is something more on the line here. From out of the ten men entered to do battle this
Thursday, two will walk away with a chance to become CWA's first North
American Champion. There would be no greater feeling in the world than to walk out of Tradition knowing that
I could be the first man to hold that gold title! [Max checks his watch to check what time it is, realizing that his lunch break is almost over he begins to wrap things up.]
Max Boulder: The clock is ticking down to this historic event, so you guys better be geared up to be hit by the BULLDOZER!
