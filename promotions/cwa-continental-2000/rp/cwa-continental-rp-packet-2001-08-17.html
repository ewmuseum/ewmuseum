Fireside: TPO you got my attention you freak, and now I have no reapect for you not that I had any before, I had you beaten, but Adam Michell had to to put two cents in, you got hte cheap victory, that monitor hit my head like a damn brick, and you took advantage..Congrads on a cheap victory, I am not done with you or Adam Mitcell just yet, the chapter in this book is coming to a close. But not until I say so!!
(Fireside tries to stand but gets dizzy very quickly and sits back down) Fireside: Now this week its THP the most famous big man around, I been seein you at the start of my career until now, and I am impressed, but not enough to think I can't beat you!!! You got your jack knife, and I got the Fireside
Bomb and you are not to big to feel it big man...I fear no man, and I don't lay down, you beat me you will know you been in a fight..I beat you another upset, some would say, in the Fire I am blazing to the top...See you in the ring...and one more thing Want some COme GeT Some!!!
Scene Fades... -------------------------------------------------------------------------- -------------------------------------------- {The scene opens up to The Motor City Bar and Grill in Detroit, Michigan. The
Psychotic One is sitting in a booth. He is kicked back with his feet up on the table and a beer in hand. He is watching the large screen tv that is on the
All Wrestling Channel.}
TPO: You know, Tradition was a great night for myself. Ace Fireside, you just thought I was a laydown kind of guy didnt you? You thought that just because you beat me at Blood Sweat and Tears, that you could beat me in a rematch didnt you? Well, who looks so stuid now? You look that stupid now. Hell, I was paid off to lay down for that match. And let me tell you, it was quite profitable to lay down for your punk ass, but now, I dont have to do that anymore. I want you, in the Thunderdome Cage at the Next PPV. I dont care where or when it is, PPV, me, you, Thunderdome Cage, see if you can beat, the undefeated streak of 13-0.
Now, it seems that I am matched up with Adam Mitchell on Friday Night. Oh this should be loads and loads of fun. You know Mitchell, you should just turn around and go home. Now that I can unleash the power of the darkside, rather than lay down for punks around here, you are going to be the next victim to fall to the Psycho Drop. Now, as I have just finished watching all the interviews and crap from the idiots in the CWA, I see you are no where to hide.
In fact, I noticed that none of the so called bad boys of the CWA were on it. No THP. No Fratboy, Perry Tan Hammer. Hell, no one showed up to thier interview spots tonight I see. Does this really surprise me. NO! You are all afraid of the power that I posess. I am calling each and every one of you out, right here right now. There is not a man here that can compete with the likes of me. Adam Mitchell. I will make an example out of you on Friday Night at
Tradition. I will beat you from pillar to post, and when thats all done, I will take you to the back. To the bathrooms, the concession stand, to the suvenier stand, and finally out to the parking lot. From there, I will put you through windshields. I will put you through fences, I will crack your head on the concrete. I am here by making our match and parking lot fight.
Step two. Anyman at Tradition that has the balls to step forward and face me after that in any kind of match possible, I will beat the hell out of them two.
I dont care if it is THP, Shane dumbass, Frat Girl, I mean Boy, Chainsaw
Charlie, Candido, or even Gionet. I will beat the hell out of anyone of you.
Why? Because I can! Ace, PPV, me and you. Thunderdome Cage. I will walk out, 14-0. Watch your back Friday Night. Adam Mitchell, your toast in the parking lot. Maybe I can find your car and put your ass through your own windshield. CWA Chumps, after
Mitchell gets his ass handed to him, its anyone of you that feels froggy to jump up to my challange. I will kick your ass and there is nothing anyone of you can do about it.
