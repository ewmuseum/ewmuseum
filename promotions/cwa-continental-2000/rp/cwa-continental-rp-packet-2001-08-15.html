CWA (Continental, 2000) — Roleplay

CWA RP Packet

15 August 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Scene opens up with Shane still at the gund arena being looked at by medics... they have just finished stiching him up and are now putting a banaged around his head from the beating he took earlier in the night at the hands of THP...
he pushes the medic aside when he sees the camera...) Shane: You proud of you self THP? You walk around saying the old THP is back... Who the hell cares! When i get you in the ring, just like Tanhammer you will be yelling for mercy tapping out... You see what you did? (he points to the stiches) six stiches in my head from you dropping me on it.... but you know what? I am still Champion and I am still walking... So your ass is next!
Speaking of my Title, AJ Darkk, what the hell are you thinking? Keving Candido pins you then is beating the crap out you, so being the nice guy that I am jump in and save
you, then you attack me from behind... Why? What have i done to you? No problem, you want a shot at my title? You got it boy but be prepared to tap out just like Adam Mitchell and Perry Tan
Hammer... Infact if you got the guts lets make this a hardcore match pinfalls count anywhere... It's time for the world to see a new Shane Russell... a two hundred and eighty pound hard core Champion... A J time to take your medicine boy!
(He stands up grabs a back and heads out the door) THP I notice you have a match with AceFirside, if i was you boy I would watch my back cause you never know who just might come down and get you!
and last but not least Kevin Candidio... some has been trying to set me up. well Kevin you did not get the job done boy, consider youself on the list of people I will take out... I don't know what your problem is but if you want some I am not a hard man to find...
It's like this, time for playing is over boys get ready to be LOCKED DOWN! (Scene fades as he enters a car) -------------------------------------------------------------------------- -------------------------------------------- (Scene cuts to Raleigh, North Carolina the site of the CWA's next Tradition card, we see A.J. Darkk standing outside the hotel waiting for someone. A limo pulls up as Todd Michaelson hops out and him and A.J. shake hands. The two along with a cameraman go to the hotel room of A.J. Darkk. When we arrive at the room A.J. lays on the bed, the cameraman stands at the foot of the bed and
Michaelson sits in a chair next to the bed.)
Todd Michaelson: So A.J., I would first like to talk about your loss to "StarKiller" Kevin Candido. AJ Darkk: What's there to talk about? The man is an incredible performer, I feel that I put up a good fight against one of the greatest in the business and that in itself is a victory for me.
TM: Now what about your opponent at Tradition, Shane Russell? AJ: What about him, yeah I think he's great, if he wasn't then he wouldn't be
Traditional Champ now would he? He made Frat Boy Perry Tanhammer tap out, now that's pretty good. But as for this week Shane, you have me, you have "The
Dream" you have AJ Darkk. The Dream Weaver, The Dream Machine, you won't see them coming. I will tell you the same thing I told Scotty Harrison, don't bother watching tapes because I never repeat you wonder why they call me the
"Sensation of Innvoation" that's why. You have the lock up, good for you, that's great but you will not get it on me. You better start polishing that belt because come Tradition that belt is as good as mine.
TM: So you are pretty confident in this match? AJ: Yeah why shouldn't I be, I am not this shot in the dark wrestler who has one shot of victory and that's good enough for them, but not me. So Shane-O I will see you at Tradition, I hope you are ready.
(AJ gets up and leaves) -------------------------------------------------------------------------- -------------------------------------------- [Backstage at a CWA House Show. The scene opens in the backstage locker room. Chad Yaro is sitting on a bench unlacing his boots after an easy victory over some local wannabes.
There is a bandage around his elbow, with some blood stains... a result of a senton bomb sending his opponents through a table!
Yaro pulls of one boot and drops it into his bag. As he starts to undo the lace on his other boot you see a pair of New Rock boots standing next to him. Slowly Yaro looks up at their wearer... Chainsaw Charlie!]
Chad: Hey Charlie! How's it goin? Did you see me destroy those 2 out there? Charlie: Bad man! Some dumbass in the back spilt coffee all over my booking sheet and I can't tell what the outcome's supposed to be at our match on Tradition this week!
Chad: Yeah I was confused about that. I mean, OK I lost to Snow and didn't get the CWA World Title.
Charlie: And I lost to Gionet... fluke that it was. What is this? Some punishment for losing our matches?
Chad: But the fans love us! They won't know who to cheer for the most. Charlie: So am I laying down for you, so that you get another chance at the gold? Or are you gonna job to me ending my losing streak? Cmon man - I'm a former champ! I can't start losing matches each week... Think about it.
Chad: But its my time. Word in the back is that if I keep my workrate up, and the great vignettes and interviews... I'll soon be CWA World Champ!
Charlie: This sucks! I mean... not that you could be World Champ or anything. I mean you've got a great arsenal of moves... the Chadton bomb, and all the rest... But I've got the Chainsaw Cutter... the Chainsault, the
Chainsaw Massacre! Moves that for years have got me victories!
Chad: So what're we gonna do? I don't know if Gionet, Snow or Perry are meant to do a run in... Have you spoken to them?
Charlie: No way! I can't stand to be left in a room with any of them. I don't know what's going on. The prez won't answer my calls... The road crew are avoiding me... Are you sure you don't know what's going on?
Chad: I'm as much in the dark as you are!!! Well do you want to go through tonight's routine? Or shall we work it out in the ring?
[Charlie and Chad start whispering as the scene fades to black.]
