CWA (Continental, 2000) — Roleplay

CWA Roleplay Packet

31 May 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The party to end all parties!] [Forget Shawn Michaels' post-Wrestlemania XII drink!] [Cuz Chainsaw Charlie is the NEW CWA World Champion!] CWA WORLD CHAMPION /\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\ [CHAINSAW CHARLIE] \/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/ CWA WORLD CHAMPION [Scene opens in a very plush nightclub, host to the CWA Celebration Party! Hordes of celebrities are mingling around... Kid Rock has the bar crew working overtime, J-Lo is on the dance floor, Geri Halliwell is persuading the DJ to play her latest hit single and Dino from Fear Factory is trying to persuade some of the dancers to star in his latest "home movie"!
In a specially cordoned off area, Chainsaw Charlie, new CWA World Heavyweight
Champion and conqueror of Frat Boy is sitting with close friends, a selection of wrestlers including Joe Snow, Frantic, Kevin Candido, Mad Dog Mortimer, CWA
President Mike Taylor, Kevin Beck and others. 100 lucky CWA Magazine winners are also there, clutching onto their freshly signed Price Of Glory programs, and photographs with their extreme icon!
The camera pans around the club and you can see a few unhappy people - THP,
Jasen Casen and Sean Harris are stood at the bar nursing their drinks and wondering why they weren't allowed access to the executive area.]
Charlie: Well this is it! A toast to almost 5 years of blood, sweat, tears and chainsaws! [Everyone raises their glasses/cans of beer and shouts "Charlie!"] Charlie: Thank you! On Sunday night I overcame the odds, I achieved the impossible, I defeated my arch-nemesis Frat Boy to become the CWA World
Champion for the second time! Now the first time I beat Candido for the belt; well that was shrouded in controversy... Not on Sunday!!! It was the Chainsaw
Cutter heard around the world that knocked Frat Boy from his perch at the top of the ladder!
That match was definitely one of the most enduring, psychological matches I've ever participated in! I'm not going to BS you... Frat Boy does bring out the best, or worst in me! He is "that damn good"... but as I've proved; I'm better!
Perry, wherever you are drowning your sorrows. I know you'll do one of three things - One. You'll bow out and disappear from wrestling for another few months, preparing your next attack!
Two. You'll demand a rematch immediately. Well guess what? FINE BY ME! You want a ladder match for the title? TLC? Hell in a Cell? Scaffold? Triple
Tower of Doom? Candido Carnival rules? It really doesn't matter because I'll accept! Did you hear that! I'm ready for you! I've overcome the demons, I've shattered the illusion that you are have no weaknesses! You can be beat! And
I'll do it again, with pleasure!
Three. You'll lay low, working other matches, taking the easy road for a while as you concoct your latest devilish scheme to get back the gold! I just hope you can survive the pain and agony of watching me successfully defend my title day after day whilst you stand in the shadows.
At Tradition they've booked a special 6-man tag match... to clear up the chaos at the end of the main event! Now Fratty & THP teaming up makes sense. Adding
Larry Gionet to it though? Here we have the weakest link! On Team Chainsaw however we have Kevin Candido, former CWA World champion on more than one occassion and the Blood Angel Street Punk! OK, analysts would probably never dream up such a team but it works! Street Punk and I have the hardcore and extreme edge over them, and Candido and I are more athletic and are masters of the mind games!
What do you guys bring to the table? A defeated world champion? An uncontrollable powerhouse? And someone who should have quit years ago in his
"glory days"! Each one of them with egos that could fill this club 100 times over!
On Friday we won't need to divide and conquer. Oh whilst I'm on the subject, I want a few of the new guys to watch the CWA
World Champion at work! Jasen Casen, I'll give you credit to call me out before a world title match! That kinda reminds me of me... but I soon learned the hard way that you have to have the skills and workrate to back up your threats and verbal tirades! I guess we'll see over the next few months if you "have what it takes"!
The same goes for Sean Harris. You call yourself the Perfect 10? Isn't that the name of some top-shelf magazine? Is that what you bring with you to the CWA?
Well it'll take more than fancy names, friends in business suits and I see a lot of hungry new stars... Damien, Yaro, Mitchell, Casen, the list goes on... we'll just have to see just how badly they want to be right where I am
RIGHT NOW!!!! [Suddenly Mike Taylor stands up and raises his glass!] Mike Taylor : Charlie! That was an excellent main event at Price of Glory, and our buy-rate went off the scale! I have full faith that you can lead the CWA into a new exciting era! We're building on tradition and with an intake of new talent, this federation continues to grow from strength to strength!
So after such a stellar PPV match, the CWA have arranged a little surprise for you... Hit it! [Suddenly Linkin Park arise from the stage and break into "One Step Closer"! The crowd goes insane and everyone including Charlie races to the dance floor which soon turns into a giant moshpit!!!]
-------------------------------------------------------------------------- ------------------------------------------------- (Scene opens in a bar near the ice palace in Orlando, Florida. Mike Philips is standing next to a huge man in black leather pants and a shirt that simply says
"The Animal". Mike lifts the mic up to his face to talk but is quickly stopped by "The Animal")
(Anthony Davis) Why are you interrupting my drinking time? Did I ask you for an interview? No, but while you're here I do have a couple things to say.
(Mike Philips) First of all, what.... (Davis grabs the mic) (Anthony Davis) I said that I have a couple things to say, not you. First of all, and the most important matter at the time, my match against the psychotic one. What kind of a name is that? The Psychotic One. I can see how being put on your back match after match can drive you crazy, but you shouldn't show that off. The name really doesn't matter though. What matters is the big piece of crap that calls himself that. Do you really think you're worthy of wrestling "The Animal"?
You're about to go face to face with the greatest man in this business today. If I were you I would just admit deafat, tuck your tail, and run. Once that bell rings i'm gonna whoop your ass from rope to rope, turnbuckle to turnbuckle, guard rail to guard rail. Once the carnage is over and you're on your back screaming in pain, you'll know why they call me "The Animal".
(Mike Philips) Why exactly do they call you "The Animal"? (Anthony Davis) Why exactly does everybody call you the little jackass? Cause you're always pointing your little mic in everybody's face askin questions. They call me the animal because I am an animal. I have absolutly no concern for anybody except for myself. You think if somebody taps out that I'll stop applying the hold? To me tapping out is just a signal to make it hurt even more. Do you really think that after the ref counts 1...2...3 and raises my hand that I'm gonna stop.
Think again! I don't stop untill my opponent is on his way to the hospital, only conscious enough to feel the pain I just put him through. That is why they call me the animal, just like the alligator that doesn't stop attacking untill it's prey is dead, I won't stop untill my prey is on a stretcher. And most of all I won't stop untill I'm wearing all of the gold in this fed. So you people like ironman jordan jones, the jester, and anyone else with a title, you'd better watch your back and look out because i'm coming for you.
(Mike Philips) Sounds like you're a little full of yourself, do you really think you can beat everybody in this fed? There is some real talent in this company.
(Anthony Davis) Real talent? Are you serious? You havent seen talent yet untill you've been in the ring with Anthony "The Animal" Davis, untill you've gone the distance and survived a match with me, untill you've felt "The Ragin Climax" and been put on your back by the greatest wrestler alive.
(Mike Philips) What about the other guys like Joe snow, Ace fireside, Street Punk, Sean Harris,... (Anthony Davis) What was that last one? (Mike Philips) Sean Harris (Anthony Davis) "The Perfect Ten" Sean Harris? (MIke Philips) Yeah, that's him. (Anthony Davis) I haven't seen him since we were both in the ACW. We had some great matches and litteraly blew the roof off of that fed.(R.I.P. ACW employees and fans) He might be the only other guy with some talent in this fed, but it doesn't matter because noone, and I mean noone has even a fraction of the talent that I posess.
(Mike Philips) You sound pretty full of yourself (Anthony Davis) I am that damn good, and you know what else? I"m that damn sick of you and your dumb questions, this interview is over.
(scene fades) -------------------------------------------------------------------------- ------------------------------------------------- {Kevin Candido is in his apartment chilling and watching a tape of his Price of Glory match.}
KC: I cannot believe that I did not see that coming. How could I be so stupid. I have to say out right now that Kevin Candido got his ass kicked. Yes I admit it
I got my ass kicked. You hear me THP you kicked my ass!!! I hope you hear that because that is the first last and only time you are going to see me that humble. GET THAT!! LAST TIME!! IN FACT THAT IS THE
LAST TIME YOU ARE EVER GOING TO KICK MY ASS!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! That six match on Tradition you better watch your ass. There is one thing that I have always heard… when two enemies fight its war, when they get together to fight a common cause its trouble for you. And when you THP made it so Charlie and me team up you just signed your last rights. Nuff said!!
KC: After that match I had to go somewhere for guidance. I thought I had nowhere to go until I met up with my religious advisor Father Mike. He told me that in order to get anywhere in life you have to set goals. So I have I call them the “Candido Crusades”
And I have two crusades that I need to accomplish before I become as they say “A Happy Camper” Candido
Crusade 1, destroy THP and everything that he stand for. Yes THP one of my goals is to star kill your ass straight to Hell. Even if it takes me aligning with an enemy to do so. Crusade 2 GRT MY CWA TITLE BACK.
NEED I SAY MORE. KC: And THP I leave you with this. You have been weighed, you have been measured, and you have been found wanting. In what world could you possibly beat me again ?
{Camera Fades} -------------------------------------------------------------------------- ------------------------------------------------- {Scene} The scene opens in a hotel room. Its a huge room, across the room is a couch and sitting on the couch there is a man, its Sean Harris! He is sipping on a drink and has his arm around his woman Lauren. They are talking about something and then there is a knock on the door. Sean says something to Lauren and then places his drink on a coaster and heads to answer the door. he gets to the door and looks through the peep hole. He draws his head back and gives a sigh. He opens the door and Mr. Carter busts into the room yelling about something. Sean goes back and sits down as Mr. Carter settles down and then starts to tell Sean whatever big news he has
{Mr. Carter} Mr. Harris you are never going to believe what I just saw. I was down at the CWA offices clearing up the rest of your paperwork when whoelse but
ACE FIRESIDE was on the TV flapping his mouth about how bad he is going to run you into the ground! He said there was no way you were going to survive this match with him. It had to be one of the funniest things I have ever witnessed.
I had to come right here to let you know about it. {Sean Harris} I thought I told you that I didnt want to be inturrupted tonight, not for anything. This was suppose to be a special night for me and my baby. I didnt want anything to ruin it but here you come barging in here telling me about something that Ace Firstone or whatever his name is said. I dont really care what he has to say. I dont want to hear from him I dont want to know him.
I just want to go into the ring, destroy him, and then move on to my bigger and better things.
{Mr. Carter} Yeah Mr. Harris I know that you didnt want to be inturrupted but I thought that this was some important business that needed top be taken care of.
I know that you dont need to know this guy. After your match its not going to make a world of difference who Ace Fireside is or anything like that. I just thought that you wanted to be notified when you were being talked about. I know that you dont like it when people talk bad about you.
{Sean Harris} Awwwwww c'mon Carter. You know me, I am a shining star. I am the
Perfect human. There isnt anything that can hurt me. He can run his mouth about what he is going to do and how he is going to do it. What matters is what he can do in the ring. Yeah you can talk crap all you want about whats gonna happen and how everything is going to go your way and then all of the sudden you get your words thrown right back into your face. Unlike Mr. Firestone, I am not like that. What I say goes, and what I say falls through. I am not going to go into a war of words with this guy. I know that I am better and I know that I am going to take him down easier done than said! I could sit around talking about what I want to do but that wouldnt get us anywhere. When the time comes
Saturday Night I am going to make my way to the ring and I am going to put an end to his mouth! If he wants to talk crap, if he wants to "say" what is going to happen than that is all good and well. He can daydream about beating me and being better than me, but thats not whats going to happen. Let him have his time to be someone, because after Saturday Night he isnt going to be able to do much of anything.
{Mr. Carter} Yes Mr. Harris I understand. This shouldnt be a problem for you at all. You are the best. I just am trying to look out for you thats all.
{Sean Harris} Carter its all good man. I know that you are doing it all for the better of my career! I just know that this first match isnt going to be anything to worry about. You want to worry about something? How about working on getting me in line for a title shot. You go find who I need to fight, who I need to go through, whatever. You just go do it and make me happy. You know thats what I am in the buisness for, I am looking for gold, thats all that matters. This Ace Firestone guy is going to be a stepping stone. After I hurdle him I am into the mix of things and I am going to have to ready myself for the next matches to come. Its going to make or break me and you know as well as I do, I NEVER break. I am hard as rock. So why dont you go do that while me and..... hey, where did she go?
{Scene} Sean notices that in the conversation of him and Mr. Carter Lauren had left her position on the couch and wandered off somewhere. Sean is looking around when the bedroom door opens and Lauren motions for Sean to come join her. Sean smiles and then gets up.
{Sean Harris} Carter, im glad you are looking out for me but, you know what I am capeabloe of doing and you know whats going down Saturday. All it takes is time, and I am the champ. So, why dont you go work on that while I go work on..
Well you know. Its good having you around Carter, you keep me updated. {Mr. Carter} Well thats what I am here for, and have fun.... Dont tire yourself out for Saturday, you have a match!
{Sean Harris} Ha Ha, you know me. I am ready for anything. See ya later man. {Mr. Carter} Ha, alright man. Later. {Scene} Mr. Carter laughs and then picks his stuff up. He leaves the room as
Sean locks the door behind him. Sean pops his neck and smiles as he walks into the bedroom. The lights go out and Sean laughs as the scene slowly fades into black and nothing more is heard.
