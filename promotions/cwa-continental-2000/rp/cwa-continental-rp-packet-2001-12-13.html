CWA RP Packet

13 December 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Scene opens up hours before Tradtion, you see Shane Russell and "Agent" Rodney
Wright get out of a car... Kevin Candido is no where to be seen....Rodney
Wright is in his suit and Shane who is also in a suit has the NA over his shoulder... They both grab some bags from the back seat and enter through a door... they walk down a hall where they run into Kevin Candido... from there all three enter through a door that says TTBA on it...)
Shane: Ok lets get our plan down tonight, cause I am about to get revenge for what Chad did to us last week... so don't even sweat it Kevin... If the Pres had some balls this would be a Title vs Title match so i could walk away with two titles, but this company plain sucks.... Now Chad you thank your lucky stars that this is not for your newly won World Tilte, cause if it was I would kick your sorry ass like i did last week and walk away World Champion... That's ok through, cause after i beat you I will get a shot and when i do i will walk out the new Champ...
Now i sure wish they wold get me some comp for this belt (he taps the NA title) Cause right now there is none... the CWA lines them up and I keep
knocking them down I think maybe tonight i will show the world just how good I am..and maybe just maybe sombody with some balls will step up and make me break a sweat defending this belt...Larry G don't think i have forgotten about you, tonight you see what the TTBA are all about the hard way... They call you a legend, well were about to make you a legend...
*he smiles as the camera fades to black* -------------------------------------------------------------------------- -------------------------------------------------- Gargoyle: Again they run. Again they hide. CWA releases that Shaun FX just so happens to decide to up his contract desires to the point that the CWA lets him go. They explain it as that his ego got the better of him and he turned his back on the CWA. Well, I say that's a bunch of crap. As soon as he challenged me to see who really is hardcore, he then started to find out who the Gargoyle really is and he got scared. He found that I may not specialize in Tables, Ladders, and
Chairs, but Oh My, when it comes to pure wrestling and fighting, no one is more hardcore than me. There is nothing bigger than the fight, nothing like conquering another obstacle, nothing like breaking bones and tearing flesh. What people got wrong now a days is that hardcore wrestling isn't about foreign objects, its about two men tearing each other apart with their bare hands, caring nothing for themselves and less for each other. Tears swells, sweat pours and blood pools under them. That's hardcore, and to hell with them.
And Shaun just wasn't ready for that. Much like people like Chainsaw Charlie, Shaun FX was going to hide behind toys instead of having a good match. But then he heard that I don't care about toys, that my hands were more than enough, that my strength is more than enough, that my heart is more than enough for a punk like Shaun, or anyone else like him.
Gargoyle: So he ran, ran from the man that is his biggest fear, ran from me. He ran like a coward from a beating of a life time, and I hope he never comes back. Punks like him make me sick. If he ever does decide to come back, I'm not waiting till I get him in the ring, I'm taking him out as soon as I lay my eyes on him. The only question will be, how long will he be in traction...
Gargoyle: And now for you Kyler. Your not going to run from me, are you boy? I mean, you talk trash just like Shaun, try to get attention from some slut that cared more about the vibrator option on her cell phone than your manhood, do you have the balls to stand up to me? The only person you are going to humiliate at our match is yourself. I don't have bad matches, but I do give horrendous beatings. Battling me is not being put on the back burner, Kyler, its a test, one that I'm sure everyone knows your going to fail with flying colors.
You want to know why? Because while you can't tell when someone really doesn't care whether you live or die, I am the true meaning of hardcore and I will decide whether you live or die.
No one survives the Death Dive. No one survives me. -------------------------------------------------------------------------- -------------------------------------------------- (Camera pans in Ace Fireside is backstage coming out to ring side...The pyro blasts as he comes from behind the curtain and the crowd goes wild as the peoples champion comes to the ring, he hi fives the fans as he rolls into the ring and grabs the mic)
Fireside: ole Ace was sittin back stage and thought to himself maybe I should go out and address the people, and let you know exactly where my head is...I won't take up a lot of your time, but I guess all I got to talk about is Rick Taylor, I have rambled on and on about this guy...,who don't get me wrong is a great wrestler, but he needs to learn about respect. have I done alot scene hitting the singles route, yes..Have I beaten many great competitors, yes....You the people respect Fireside, but others do not.
Sometimes you have to teach respect and a kick to my head, tells me Mr. Taylor you need to be taught some, right here in my ring...With the people behind me how can I lose? See ya in the ring....
Want SoMe CoMe GeT SoMe!!!! -------------------------------------------------------------------------- --------------------------------------------
