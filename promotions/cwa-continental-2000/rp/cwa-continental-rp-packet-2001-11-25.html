The camera fades out to a black screen with this lettering written in blood on it. THE WHEEL OF TIME TURNS AND AGES COME AND GO, WHAT WAS, WHAT WILL BE AND WHAT IS WILL YET FALL UNDER THE RAINMAN. Pouring rain on the screen washes away the blood on the screen and soon it is all black. ********************************************************************** ********************************************************************** A cold wind blew in from Belfast Lough, driving rain across the city. Jason
Devlin moved along a narrow street between tall warehouses, relics of the
Victorian era, mostly boarded up now. He stood on the corner, a small man, no more than five feet eleven, wearing a trench coat and an old rain hat.
He was on the waterfront now. There were ships out there at anchor, their riding lights moving up and down, for there was a heavy swell driving into the docks. There was a sound of gunfire in the distance. He glanced in the general direction, lit a cigarette in cupped hands, and moved on.
There was an air of desolation to the whole area, examples of the devestation caused by twenty-five years of war everywhere, and his feet crunched over broken glass. He found what he was looking for five minutes later, a warehouse with a peeling sign on the wall that said MURPHY & SON --IMPORT & EXPORT. There were large double doors with a small Judas gate for easy access. It opened with a slight creak and he stepped inside.
It was a place of shadows, empty except for an old Ford van and a jumble of packing cases. There was an office at the far end with glass walls, one of two panes broken, and a dim light shone there. Devlin removed his rain hat and ran a hand through his hair. Devlin climbed into the van and into a hi-tech editing booth. He started flipping a few switches and all throughout the world screens flickered and Devlin appeared on the screen.
"I'd like to say Good Morning to the million of viewers, actually probably closed to billions of viewers. Anyways, I'd just like to remind everyone out there to go out and watch the CWA and to watch me Jason "The Rainman" Devlin defeat this newcomer, Mike Broughton. I thought long and hard about accepting a match with a newcomer with no experience here. Then decided that for my first match back, maybe i should just take it easy, and ease myself back into the
CWA. Make sure i'm as good as I was when I left. Mike, I'm sorry you have to fight me for your first match, I don't completly understand why you would challenge me, but you'll find out why, no one, and i mean no one, challenges the Rainman. Come Tradition Mike, you will be regretting the day you decided to enter the CWA. And now, i'm out."
The screen flickers goes all fuzzy and then resumers to normal programming. Devlin leaves the warehouse and dissapears into a cloud of mist. ********************************************************************** -------------------------------------------------------------------------- ------------------------- [Gargoyle sits in his locker room.
