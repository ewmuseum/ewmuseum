Gargoyle: The Gargoyle soared from the spires and crashed violently to its prey on the ground, a little bird who knew not what it had found. But in the end it knew, as the shadow of the diving predator not only put a chill down the little birds spine, but into his very soul...
Gargoyle: And you know what Nighthawk, I didn't even have to go for my Death Dive to finish you off. That's why I took the chair to your sorry ass, I wasn't done yet.
I wasn't satisified with my win. No, I wanted my pound of flesh, and I got it. And there I was, the victor standing over the fallen, and what happens?
Some punk decides to challenge me? Some punk thinks he can ruin my fun by calling me out from the back?
Gargoyle: Well guess what, Shawn, you didn't ruin anything. In fact, you made my night. Now I know who I'm going to destroy next. You want extreme? How about I take a chair and beat the crap out of your ass, then scoop up that crap and I make you eat it? How would you like that Shawn? Would that be extreme enough for you?
Gargoyle: But maybe I won't do that to you. You know why? I've heard things Shawn. I've actually have heard things about you, you dumb ass kid. I've heard you like the pain, Shawn. I've heard you like to grab men Shawn and have them hurt you. Well you know what, I don't want to be a part of your little homo-erotic S&M sessions you try to get from the men like Chainsaw
Charlie, but I will bring to you the beating of a life time. I hope your up to the task Shawn. Nighthawk wasn't and look at him now...
No one survives the Death Dive. No one survives me. -------------------------------------------------------------------------- -------------------------------------------- [Darkness.] VOICE: Gargoyle? WHO THE HELL IS GARGOYLE?! [Fade in to the interior of a fancy-looking restaurant. We catch one Johnny
Kyler sitting at a table, while sitting across from him is a lovely brunette woman babbling away on her cell phone. Kyler, appearing quite disturbed, just talks to her in spite of the fact she's just outright ignoring him.]
JK: I mean, Hell, I've already beaten two schmoes in this stupid federation, and I'm stuck in the opening match, only to be followed by the likes of Jason

WOMAN: Yeah, I know! That's so unbelievable! JK: See?! You know what I mean! I swear man, it's all a part of the conspiracy against me here in the CWA! Christ -- first they tried to get
Shane Valentine against me, and obviously, he screwed _that_ up -- and now, they're trying to get friggin' _Gargoyle_ on me?
Well, try your best, punkasses -- you ain't gonna bring down the man, yo! 'Cause I am Johnny-friggin'-Kyler -- the roughest, toughest, rootingest-tootingest, baddasest-ass mofo in these here parts!
[A snarl appears on Johnny's face as he nods at the camera, affirming the fact that yes indeed, he's the "baddassest-ass mofo" in the CWA.]
JK: Hey, Gargie, you know what I'm gonna do to you? Huh? Do ya? I'm gonna make like friggin' Michael Madsen on you, man. Just like in Reservoir Dogs.
You ever seen that movie? I hope not -- because then, you'll know that I'm gonna CUT YOUR FRIGGIN' EAR OFF~!
[...] JK: ...just kidding. I'll just humiliate you and show you _exactly_ why this card feels like a tour where the Rolling Stones are opening up for Limp
Bizkit. WOMAN: [_still_ talking on the phone] Oh yeah, dearie, that's right! JK: Chicks dig the Ky-man. Now Rick Talylor as the peoples champion when you nailed me in the jaw you nailed the people and for that you must pay or at the very least be taught a lesson..You by the way fought a good match up until you kicked me the head that is...If you ever need my help or a guy in your corner I will be in it, but, I said but...we have to settle this between us man to man in that squared circle. Want Some Come Get Some!!! -------------------------------------------------------------------------- -------------------------------------------- /\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\ [CHAINSAW CHARLIE] \/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/ Charlie: Jason Devlin! The rainman! Well I'm the bloodman! And when we fight, it'll sure be raining blood alright, boyo!
Now the Irish have a saying. If I remember it correctly, it goes something like this: "They don't know what they want, but they'll fight to the death for it". So now I know what motivates you Devlin. I've seen what you do in the ring, and I've seen your moody vignettes.
The only difference is that I DO know what I want, but I'll still fight to the death for it!
You see, there are a couple of nobodies fighting over the CWA World Title and it needs a SOMEBODY like me to give the title the prestige it deserves.
But the powers that be won't give me a title shot until I prove myself. So first I have to defeat you Devlin! See where I'm going with this....
Now I know you have a penchant for guns... for explosives... for baseball bats, and all of that. But there's something you need to use with them -
Your arms! And that'll be pretty difficult IF I'VE LOPPED THEM BOTH OFF WITH
MY PATENTED DIAMOND-TIPPED CHAINSAW!!! Talking of 'armless people in the CWA! Didn't I warn you Mr World, you stupid masked freak!
I told you to watch out... but you didn't listen! And you paid the price!!! What I did to you at Tradition was just an insight into the pain and punishment to come. You think you're making friends backstage? Guys that have your back! Well you'll need 'em when I next get my hands on you which I predict will be very... very... soon!
Mr World... Mr Devlin... You're both, guaran-damn-teed to bleed!
