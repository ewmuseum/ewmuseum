CWA (Continental, 2000) — Roleplay

CWA Roleplay Packet

29 May 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
{Scene} The scene opens up in an office building. There are poeple moving about holding papers talking on cell phones and doing other various things. Its loud in the room and there are bright overhead lights. The front doors have people pouring in and out of them. Finally the camera focuses on a group of people standing in the far corner of the room. There are two men and a girl. One of the men is big, the man next to him is smaller and is wearing an expensive suit and tie. He is holding a briefcase. The woman standing there is beautiful. She has on a tight pink dress with long blonde hair. The bigger man is talking on the cell phone and the smaller man is talking to the woman. The camera listens in.
{Man in Suit} You know if we just go get on the private jet we can be there before you know it. I mean its not like this match is going to be anything big for our man. Its going to be easy! Just likes his days in the old FWF. He is going to roll through the competition and run the federation in no time. This man is a killer. He can't be stopped. We have nothing to worry about. There isnt a man alive that can beat him. I have already phoned ahead to the hotel to make reservations for his stay, and ours as well. We have nothing to worry about. One hundred percent or not there is no stopping him.
{Pretty Girl} You think I dont know that he is the best around? I have been by his side throuhg his career and I have watched him take out man after man and win title after title. I know what he is capable of doing and I know that he is the best. I am just worried about this fever that he has had the past few days.
It worries me that he can be seriously hurt if goes out there and wrestles with a high temperature. You do know that you can get seriously hurt dont you?
{Man in Suit} Look, the card isnt until saturday night. I am sure that with plenty of rest he is going to be GREAT by the time of his match. I am sure that he will be fine, dont worry about it. The man is a hoss.
{Pretty Girl} Well alright. I guess. We do have a long trip though. I mean if we take the jet we can get there in about a few hours.
{Man in Suit} You see, everything is fine. We have nothing to worry about. Now, as soon as he is done on the phone we can get out of here.
{Scene} The big man continues to talk on the phone while the other two still talk. Finally the man gets off the phone and then starts talking to the other man and the girl. They begin to walk towards the door and out the door as they walk down the street a little bit to where a limo is waiting to take them to the airport. On the side of the limo reads CWA Transportation. They all get in and inside the car a man is waiting for them with a camera and a mic.
{Man in Suit} Hey now just wait a minute. I thought that I told the CWA that we didnt want a man in our face twenty four seven bothering our man. He has been sick and we need him to be stress free if he is going to be fighting at one hundred percent by Saturday! Who are you??
{Mike Phillips} Well I am the interviewer. I thought that you guys would want some air time.
{Man in Suit} Well we dont. Who do you think you are? You cant just walk up to us and expect to get a word or two. Thats not how we work here!
{Mike Philllips} Well who ARE you? {Mr. Carter} Just call me Mr. Carter. I tend to all the buisness that needs to be taken care of with my man here. If you want an interview you come to me. I take care of everything so I take the load of stress of my mans back.
{Mike Phillips} And who is this beautiful woman? {Mr. Carter} Well, if I were you I would be careful about how you talk around her. This woman is my mans girl. Her name is Lauren, she has been by his side ever since his career has started.
{Mike Phillips} And your man is? {Mr. Carter} And my man is? What is wrong with you man. Were you born yesterday! This man is going to be the NEXT CWA Champion. He is the only man in the CWA that is PERFECT. If you think you are going to find another man like him, you better start looking somewhere else. He is UNSTOPABLE, and the whole
CWA is going to learn that VERY quickly. The man that I speak of has accomplished everything he has ever set out to do. He has been training to compete in the highest ranks of wrestling and finally his training is done and he is ready to come to action. The CWA looked to be the best start for him. He is going to come out of the gates ready to fire it up. The man I talk about is an ICON, the man I talk about is the BEST, the man I talk about is none other than The Perfect Ten.... Sean Harris! And he is going to be the most used name around here in the CWA soon enough, you can guarantee that.
{Mike Phillips} Well thats very nice to hear. You do know that your MAN is facing off against Ace Fireside right?
{Mr. Carter} Ah yes Ace Fireside. The first victim of many for Sean here in the
CWA. This is going to be a very unique match because it is the first match of
Seans pro career. All of his years in the indies is going to finally pay off as he steps into the ring toe to toe with a CWA superstar for the very first time.
This is going to be a day that no one will forget. I am going to unleash my man on him and its going to be a day of reckoning. I know that Mr. Fireside, wherever he is, is going to soon see that what he is going against is no man.
He isnt mortal, he is a Perfect human beign. He was molded from the very same material as the gods and there isnt anything that can hurt this man. We dont know much about this Fireside guy but that isnt going to effect him. Wether we are familiar with him or not makes no differance. He is going to be beaten into the mat and he is going to be put down for the three count. Then maybe we will put the point across to the CWA that this man right here is the future title holder. He will be there soon enough, mark my words!
{Mark Phillips} Well does your man ever talk? {Scene} The camera looks at Sean Harris and he looks right back. He flashes a cocky smile and then he begins to speak for the first time!
{"The Perfect Ten" Sean Harris} Yeah I do talk. And when I talk everyone better listen beacuse I am the most important person that you are going to hear from when you are doing an interview with me. What I say is what matters, I could have Mr. Carter sit around and talk for me all night but then that wouldnt really be getting the point across. He takes care of everything else, and I do the fighting in the ring. What he does is makes sure that I am getting the best, because I am the best. And when Saturday Night comes up, I am going to be showing the CWA who is the best. I mean I come into this federation and I am on a bonus match. Well that doesnt show me that I am thought well of here. I am going to have to change that. I am going to have to make sure that I show them
I belong. What does the president think? I am some cocky badass who is all talk and wont produce a thing? Well when he sees what I do to you my friend, he is going to fall out of his chair. When he sees the talent I have he is going to know immidiatley that this man deserves a shot. Not necessarily a shot at the title, but a shot to be someone. Not just a bonus match at the end of some card. I am better then that, I am Perfect. And there isnt anyone who can prove me wrong. If you want to see then just come and try me. I am willing to fight anyone anywhere anytime. I have the most uttermost confidence that there isnt a man in this world that can defeat me one on one. And this Ace FIreside guy, he is going to be the first hand experiance of why I am the master of the ring.
When the lights go out and "California Love" hits the PA System, Ace you better get ready for the fight of your life. When you step into the ring with an ICON like myself, you are signing your life away. You see, when it all comes down to it I have everything going for me. I have the looks I have the body and I have everything that I need to be a STAR! All I need is competition for me to BLOW away, and you are going to be the first on a long list of wrestlers that I destroy and put out of the buisness!
{Mike Phillips} No one has even seen you fight? How do we know that you arent full of it?? {"The Perfect Ten" Sean Harris} I am not going to tell the people what to think, I am going to show them. I am going to show the CWA and the world what I am made of. You dont have to like me, you dont have to listen to me, but you do have to put up with me. I am the Perfect Human. I have everything, and I am going to make Ace Fireside a prime example of what being a lower class human is like. When I stomp him into the mat and make a point, the rest of the CWA will shudder at me. They will know what true perfection is when they see me, because thats exzactly what I am. I am perfect and I am going to show just that to the
CWA Hands Down! Everyone just better make sure to watch the card Saturday, because wether you like me or not I am here and I am going to make everyones life a living hell here in the CWA! It is all going to take time, but not that long of a time. Ace Firside, you are just the first of many. The first of many to get SPIKED! And when you get SPIKED, you arent getting up! After the Spike comes the victory. I will pin you and at that moment the CWA is going to watch the future champion walk out of the ring, and walk out victorious. The first victory on the road to a title reign. You may doubt me and mock me know but you will all change your outlook on it. You will all see who the man is, and you will all see that I what I say I am...... PERFECT! Thats all I have to say right now. You can guarantee there will be more because I am not one to sit back and be silent. I have a lot to say and soon everyone will know that. Ace
Fireside just better hope he is better than me, which I can say, isnt very likely. If he walks into that ring thinking I am a new comer who will be a walk in the park, think again. I am the new wave of wrestler. I am the ELITE... I am
PERFECT! And soon the CWA will know that to be a fact, because I will be running the show......
{Mr. Carter} You heard the man the interview is over. Turn the camera off and let my man get his rest. He has a match to be resting up for. Here Sean, have a drink. You need something to cool you down.
{Scene} Mr. Carter hands Sean a bottle of cold water as he drinks some of it. Lauren cuddles with him in the car as he kicks back to relax. The camera slowly fades to black with Sean drifting to sleep.
-------------------------------------------------------------------------- ------------------------------------------------- (The scene opens with an unknown man hammering away near a heated oven the flames rise and fall as camera pans in closer...The man lets his face mask it is Ace Fireside)
Fireside: This is a different spot for old Ace, now its all singles action for me..And (bang, bang, bang) this is what I am going to do to any man that gets in my way, Hammer the crap out of them so that they don't dare get back up. This is directed at you Sean Harris one jackass in a nation of millions!!! You see I have to prove to every one I can hold my own, and beat any bodies ass that gets in my way...I don't like to be an after thought on any card, and you my friend are just the lucky piece of crap for me to start my climb to the top of the heap called the CWA....I won't say I will do this and that to you I am just here to win the match and move on, but bring all the fight you can boy I will be waiting and I will drop the hammer....(bang, bang, bang) Scene Faids...
-------------------------------------------------------------------------- -------------------------------------------- (The scene opens to a small house/cabin near the lake it is around 6:00 and the sun is still up, the camera enters the house and walks to one room, there are numerous artworks around. The house is lit with the sunlight of the open windows of the house. IMJ is seen napping in a recliner, he holds his newly acquired
North American Title close as he sleeps. The camera move towards ever so slightly, the camera man accidently taps the recliner with his foot. IMJ awakens, grabs the Camera Man by his shirt then hits him in his stomach, IMJ then realizes what he's done and gets up with haste)
IMJ: What are you doing waking me up guy? CM: (Out of breath) I didn't mean to, I just was here for an interview. IMJ: Ah well I guess you probably would want an interview with the new ever-reigning North American
Champ. So I'll grant you one. What do you want to know? CM: Well I was wondering about your thoughts on your last match in which you won the North American Title.
IMJ: Well it was a tough match but the Iron Man persevered. I can't even remember who I faced but the road behind is road traveled that I won't travel again so let's speak about the road ahead.
CM: Well you face Chris Natural at Tradition how are your chances you think? IMJ: Chris Natural, I've never heard of or heard from this joker. Who is he? My chances in my opinion are about 99.5% win and the other .5% a DQ or no contest.
Chris, what about you is so Natural? Is it your natural ability to step in the ring and fall on your back for 3 seconds every match or your Natural ability to get beat by IMJ in the center of the ring?
Well whatever it may be, it won't be enough, because I'm stronger than you, I'm better than you, I'm the champ and gosh darnit people like me. So Christopher I hope you attempt to get ready for our match, yes attempt because you'll never be prepared for the wrath of the Iron Man!
CM: This will be your first title defense ever here in the CWA how does that make you feel?
IMJ: It may be my first CWA title defense but it's not my first ever title defense, I've defended numerous titles in numerous matches. Chris Natural is at the very bottom of my list of worthy competitors and the least of my worries. Also this may be my first title defense but I can gaurantee it won't be my last.
Whether I defend this belt, the Traditional title belt, or the World title belt I will give it everything and never give up until I win. Whether I face Tanhammer or the frickin Brooklyn Brawler it doesn't matter because I WILL win.
CM: pretty confident, do you think that could be your downfall? IMJ: Heaven's no. Confidence is like Armour. If you wear very little confidence you will be killed quickly, and if you wear to much armour you can't move and will be immobilized during the fight, but if you wear just the right ammount of armour then you are protected angainsts short stabs and can move just fine. I have the just right ammount of armour and that will help me get this quick job done by beating
Natural all over my ring. CN: Well strong words from IMJ, can he back them up? We can only wait. (Scene fades to a Code Red Mountain Dew commercial.)
