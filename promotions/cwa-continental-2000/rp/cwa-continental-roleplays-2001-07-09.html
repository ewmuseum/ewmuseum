CWA Roleplays

9 July 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I meant to send this when I sent it to the fed but it slipped my mind. I'll be posting the packet I am sending tonight as well so enjoy!
(Scene opens up to a bright sunshine. The camera then focuses on a ranch in the distance. There are three horses in the pasture. One brown, and two black. The camera then focuses on the ground as the camera is walking to the ranch. All of a sudden there are two feet in the camera's view. The cameraman stops points the camera up and the face of Chad Yaro appears. He is wearing a white bandana, and black oakley sunglasses with red lenses. Yaro spits and then looks into the camera.)
You see, ever since the PPV people have been asking me, why Chad why? And my answer to all of them is, I have to do what is best for me. I've been letting people push me around for far too long in this business. I realize now that instead of trying to make friends, I have to do what is best for Chad Yaro. Now
Jasen I know that we have had a rough past with maybe even a rougher future, but that is a risk I am willing to take with you. You know me better than anyone in this business and I with your help am going to get to the top of this business.
(Chad starts walking to his yellow Jeep Wrangler, he tells the cameraman to jump in.Chad gets in closes the door, pulls down on his bandana to fix it, spits out the window, puts the Jeep in drive and spins the tires and speds off into the sunlight. Chad asks that the cameraman to shut the camera off. The cameraman agrees. The camera turns on again and is pointed at a run down barn.
Chad gets out of the Jeep and walks inside. Chad opens the padlock and opens the two large wooden doors. Chad pulls the cord and the lights go on. Inside this run down shack there is a wrestling ring surrounded by chairs. Chad walks over and steps into the ring.)
This is where I spent six years of my life everyday from 3:00 to 12:30 at night, everyday after school from the time I was a junior in high-school all the way through the time I finished college. This is where I taught myself how to wrestle, I taught myself how to take bumps by trial and error. My parents did not approve of me buying a ring or of me buying this barn but I did it anyway, because I knew this is what I wanted, wrestling has been a part of my life ever since I saw my first match when I was 2 1/2 at the Boston Garden. I have been wrestling ever since. No one has ever been in here, you are the first to experience the route to the CWA that Chad Yaro took. You see this ring stained with blood, that is from bumps gone bad, in this ring alone I have had
12 stitiches in my head, 14 in my leg and 20 in my foot. I have had a partially separated shoulder which I only went to the doctors for after I could not move it any longer. But that didnt stop neither did my four concussions.
Now Shane Russell, you have something that I want, no that I need. You have the
CWA Traditional Title, the only thing standing in my way of accomplishing my lifelong goal of becoming World Champion. The six years that I spent in this ring and the numerous injuries that I have suffered have well prepared me for my match with you and it will hopefully steer me right to win the Traditional
Title.
(Yaro squats and takes a back bump.) So Shane you think that you are going to come into our title match and take me lightly? Go ahead because you will be in for a rude awakening when I nail you with the "Yaro Special". So Shane-O sleep tight. Make sure you bring it ALL.
Because I want it all or nothing at all. Shane choke on that and I will see you at Tradition.
-------------------------------------------------------------------------- ------------------------- (The Scene opens up after Friday nights's show, Shane with the title over his shoulder is drinking champaine, well mostly getting it poured on him by other wrestlers... He is very excited as the camera gets closer he turns toward it and speaks)
Shane: Finally I have the prize, but I know winning this was easy, keeping it is not going to be so easy... I will be the best Champion the CWA has ever had, I won't duck people like our beloved World Champion... I will take on everybody and defend this Title evry week...
(He wips champaine out of his eyes) Shane: In fact this week i get Chad Yaro... Now Chad I can only hope you are prepared , cause to get this title from me, you will have to pry it from my cold dead hands...I have the speed, i have the moves, and most of all... I have the fans...The fans are what make this and me a star and I give them all the thanks... Now back to you Chad... I don't know alot about you but I am sure i can get some tape on you. so from now until next week I will focus on you and only you...i can only hope you do the same....
Shane: I also notice some things going on that i just don't like, for one Anothony "The Animal" Davis, Sean Harris running in on guys from behnd and attacking them is just not kewl... TPO You ever need some help just let me know...
(He lets out a laugh as an unknown wrestler pours more champaine on him, and he again wipes it from his face)
Shane: Well as you can see I still have a few more hours of celibraiting to do, but it will only last one day, as then i get prepared for Chad Yaro... getting ready Chad cause hell is coming to everybody who tries to take this from me...
(The turns back to the party at hand as the scene fades to black) -------------------------------------------------------------------------- ------------------------- -==-The arena turns black-==- -==-The fans in the arena cheer-==- -==-A red spotlight hits the entrance way-==- -==-A voice speaks-==- Hate Me, Love Me Cheer Me, Boo Me Remember Me, Forget Me Most of All Fear Me -==-A burst of pyro goes off-==- -==-”Papercut” by Linkin Park plays-==- -==-A dark figure emerges from the smoke-==- -==-The figure is wearing baggy sweatpants, sweatshirt, and a bandana-==- -==-The figure has a mic in hand and is ready to speak-==- CWA- Continental Wrestling Alliance. I am AJ Darkk, I am the man who is raved by many, mocked by few. I am often imitated never duplicated. You never know where I am coming from or where I've been.
All you know is that you are next All you can do is pray. -==-AJ takes off his hood and reveals his face-==- -==-AJ has a lip ring on the middle of his bottom lip-==- -==-He takes off his bandana to show his bleach blonde hair-==- -==-AJ walks down the aisle way to the ring-==- -==-AJ walks around to the side of the ring and grabs a chair-==- -==-He puts the chair in the ring and sits in it-==- You know about six months ago I sat in the Shelby Rec Center in Shelby,North
Carolina and watched some no talent rejects wrestle in front of maybe 40 people for about 20 dollars. I told myself that was not going to be me. I was going to make it into the CWA. So day after day I trained harder and harder until I called up CWA headquarters and set up a tryout. Well lets just say that the CWA now knows why people back home called me the sensation of innovation. So
Anthony "The Animal" Davis you may have someone watching you back, well that's the difference between us you need someone I don't, listen to me I may lack experience in this business but I have learned one thing from my time in this business, it's not the size of the dog in the fight it's the size of the fight in the dog. So Animal when you get hit with the Dream Machine it will be all over. So save the drama for your mama and bring all of your ass to Tradition cuz I wanna kick it all over the arena.
