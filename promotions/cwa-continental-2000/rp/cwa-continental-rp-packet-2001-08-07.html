CWA RP Packet

:: Scene cuts to the CWA training facility located in Brockton,MA. We see AJ
Darkk in one of the six different rings. AJ is running the ropes, looks like he is working very hard because he has completly soaked his white wife beater and his hair is very wet. He grabs a towel off of the top turnbuckle whips his forehead, puts the towel around his neck, bends down and grabs the bottle of water from underneath the ropes. Just then he notices the camera as it gets closer to him. He lifts the second rope up and lets the cameraman in, AJ takes position sitting on the top turnbuckle pad. ::
AJ Darkk: I went into Blood, Sweat and Tears very,very nervous. I wasn't nervous because of Scotty Harrison no no, I wasn't nervous about what Scotty may do to me, I was nervous about what I may have done to Scotty. Well Scotty I want to congratulate you, you put up one hell of a match. I very much enjoyed that match and I hope it's not the last time we battle. Now onto bigger and better things, "StarKiller" Kevin Candido, you say I should fear you? I fear no one, fear is only a four letter word to me and trust me Kevin it is not in my vocabulary. You feel sorry for me because you lost last night and you are a crybaby so you are feeling sorry for me because of what may happen to me. You lost deal with it, geez I had so much respect for you but now you sound like a whiny little bitch. You should be the one fearing me my freind. You ask yourself, "where does the starkiller go from here?" I say the starkiller goes to Cleveland and gets his ass kicked AJ Darkk style. Just like I told Scotty, don't bother watching the tapes because you will never get the same AJ Darkk.
You wonder why they call me the sensation of innovation trust me it's not just a marketing thing.
::AJ walks over to the calendar, he looks at it:: AJ Darkk: Friday August 10, I am counting down the days. I will be here training night and day for this match. Ever since everyone heard about me challenging you, they ask me "AJ are you crazy, this man is great, he will eat you for supper" well yes Kevin you are great and you probably could eat me for supper but not at Tradition, if you think for one second that you are coming into this match and just gonna make quick work of me just because I am a rookie, just because I am only 20 years old. I don't think so, I have left too many things behind to lose at Tradition, I turned down an acedemic scholarship to a top notch college just so I could train to wrestle, my parents didnt approve of me skipping school to wrestle just like they didn't approve of me buying the wrestling ring when I was 16. So Kevin you may have been a big dog in this business but believe me when I say this, you may be the
"starkiller" but you are no AJ Darkk. When it's all said and done and all the smoke has cleared, it will be my hand being raised in victory and your body they will be scrapping off the mat. I know how you like quoting famous people well wrap your mind around this one pal, "some are born great, some achieve greatness, and some have greatness thrust upon them" which one are you? I guess we will find out at Tradition won't we?
::aj goes leaves through one of the hallways and goes to the locker room. the camera cuts to a slim jim commercial or something like that::
-------------------------------------------------------------------------- ------------------------- THE HOTTEST PROSPECT....... The former World Champion that I am....... A Current TV Champion...... Also a former International and Tag Team Champion.... And let's not forget the Awesome Twosome! So after facing "tough" competition in the likes of.. Joe Snow Chad Yaro Kevin Candido Street Punk Who do I get? You guessed it! Jason fucking Devlin! Well Devlin, guess what? At Tradition..... I'm gonna end your fucking career! THP The Hottest Prospect [The scene opens up with the picture of a back of a map. It appears someone is holding the map and as the camera pans out, it's a map of Ohio, and in particular, Cleveland. The map is thrown down to the floor and as that happens, we see THP's face staring into the camera.]
THP: Cleveland? So we're going to Cleveland huh? That's right, that's what the whole world's been waiting for. A CWA show in Cleveland, Ohio. Oh yeah, you know it. That's like Vince taking his troops to Taiwan to put on a show for all those crazy fucks who make our American goods and get paid seven cents an hour. Hey, why do you think it all says made in taiwan?
[He pauses and turns and picks up a paper] THP: Anyways, this says right here I've got a match with Jason Devlin. Now to me, this has GOTTA be a misprint. It's got to be a mistake, an error, you name it. It's wrong. How can THP be working a match against Jason Devlin? Is this
CWA Jakked or Metal we're taping for here? I mean c'mon....I'm at the most dangerous state of my career, and you're gonna put me with Jason Devlin? Give me a break....no, give me a fucking break!
[He snaps] THP: I'm tired of this shit. All of this shit the CWA continues to pull over my head, your head, everyone's head. They continue to book corporate puppets as the top superstars in the CWA, and they continue to avoid and stay away from those men that they fear, like myself! The CWA and it's powers that be, they fear THP! They fear me and the man behind me because they know that what I want I get, and whatever I do, they have to learn to deal with! I've wanted
Chainsaw Charlie and they never gave it to me! I wanted Frat Boy and THP to be a dominant heel alliance and what did they do? They got in Frat Boy's ear and talked him into turning for the 1157th time of his career. And now I'm still gunning for the World Title and they've got me side tracked fueding with a has been or make that, "could have been" Frat Boy and wrestling in meaningless matches against Jason Devlin!
[He pauses and shakes his head in disgust, throwing down the paper] THP: So they want to play like that? Fine! I know they fear me and eventually I'm going to get to them because I'm going to hurt each and every bit of talent that they put in my way to try to slow me down! Jason Devlin, you're in the wrong place at the wrong time and you know what boy? At
Tradition, we're going to see the end of your career! You thought the jacknife through a table, in a dumpster or on the turnbuckle was bad....just wait till what I have in store for you! As for you Frat Boy...I will find your ass at
Tradition. The Gund Arena is a dump and it's not a big place and mark my words, I will you find you. You better hide in your ice cream truck and listen to "Mr. Speed" by Kiss you little bitch because when I get my hands on you..you too will burn at the hands of the Hottest Prospect!
[He pushes the camera lens and the camera sharply turns to the corner and fades to black.] -------------------------------------------------------------------------- ------------------------- THE MAN... THE MYTH... THE LEGEND!!! {"Running With The Devil" by Van Halen plays as The Living Legend Larry Gionet comes to the ring to a chorus of boos from the crowd. He wears his trademark
Larry Gionet T-shirt under his blue denim jacket. With a smile that oozes confidnece, he enters the ring with his mic in hand.}
Larry Gionet: CWA...WHO'S THE FUCKING MAN? {The fans become angrier and the boos become louder but for once Larry Gionet doesn't seem to care.}
Larry Gionet: You can boo me for all I care because it doesn't change the fact that I pinned Frat Boy Perry Tanhammer in the middle of this ring! He lost his match against his ally THP and Joe Snow so you decide to get pissy and turn on your buddy! Then I knew that was when I would strike! I struck at your boiling point, where you would kill anything that moved! I attacked at your most vulnerable state and you fell prey to That's Legendary which lead to your defeat! But you are old news brother. There is one man on my mind and thats
Chainsaw Charlie!
{The crowd pops for his name.} Larry Gionet: Oh yeah your beloved hero! Whats the matter Charlie, feeling a little down that you lost your title! You better not be worrying about that damn title you lost and you sure as hell better not be worrying about those men that came down during your match because you have something much more severe to worry about and thats me! You see don't look for a wrestling match outta me
Charlie this time around. I'm out for blood! Your own damn catchphrase is gonna come up and bite you in the ass because you my friend are guranteed to bleed! Chainsaw Charlie, my mission has no end! My mission of pain doesn't end until I am the last man standing and there is NO ONE LEFT! Prepare for it
Charlie, because at Tradition, it will be YOUR...END!
{"Running With The Devil" by Van Halen plays as The Living Legend Larry Gionet leaves the ring as the fans are jeering him.}---------------------------------------------------------------------
------------------------------ { The screen opens up to video footage from CWA Blood, Sweat, and Tears. The first footage to be shown is Michael D Taylor telling Joe Snow that the World
Title Match has a no rematch clause. The screen goes black and the footage of the hodded men attacking Chainsaw Charlie and Joe Snow winning the World Title appear. Joe Snow is shown raising the World Title as the screen fades to black. }
{ The screen re-opens inside the jam packed CWA Arena. "Bodies" by Drowning
Pool blasts over the PA system as Joe Snow walks out on to the rampway with the
CWA World Title in one hand and his Barbed-Wire Kendo Stick in the other. Joe raises the World Title in the air as the the crowd explodes in a chorus of boos. The Crowd begins throwing bottles, cups and anything that they can get their hands on at Joe as he walks to the ring, climbs the turnbuckle and points his kendo stick towards the crowd but then flips them off. Joe jumps back into the ring rips the mic from Rick Collings hands. }
Joe Snow: Blood, Sweat, and Tears, the greatest night in the history of professional wrestling. No longer do we have to suffer through boring promos from Chainsaw Charlie about how he is the Hardcore Icon and how he is the CWA
World Champion. Now it's my time to shine. No longer do I have to stand in
Chainsaw Charlies shadow. No longer will I be held down, no longer will I be held back, and no longer will I be denied what is rightfully mine. I have busted my ass for this company since day one and until Charlie came in I was on my way to the top of the business. I was the CWA North American Champion and I was making my run for the World Title until the suits in the back decided that it would be a good idea if they gave my spot to Charlie and just left me as
Charlies "friend".
{ The crowd starts chanting "CHARLIE CHARLIE" as Joe just sits in the ring and laughs. } Joe Snow: Go ahead chant all you want, call for your hero but he ain't coming. Oh no, you see last night we made sure that Charlie wasn't going to be around for a long time. That brings me to my next point, the masked men. Everywhere
I go people have been asking me who the masked men are. Are they enemies
Charlie has made in the past, are they new people looking to make a name for themselves or are they just some of Joe Snow's old friends. Well folks, tune in to Tradition and I might give you some answers. But on to the question that's on everybodys mind. Why did Joe Snow turn his back on Chainsaw Charlie?
Crowd: YOU SOLD OUT, YOU SOLD OUT Joe Snow: Call it selling out, call it backstabbing or call it whatever the hell else you want, but the bottom line is the CWA World Title is around my waist and not Charlies. But let me take you back in time to right after I made the challenge to Charlie. Roll that video tape.
{ Joe turns his body towards the TraditionTron and motions towards the crowd to watch the screen. The image on the screen changes from the CWA logo to a picture of Charlie standing in the ring. The video starts playing and Charlies promo can be heard through the arena. }
{ Charlie: Joe, so it appears you didn't like the way I "mistreated" the CWA
World Title in the past? Well I'll hold my hands up to that! Some of what I did was unforgivable. However you have to understand something. I had to do
WHATEVER it took to win the title! I had to unleash the demons within me. You're one of the best hardcore wrestlers in the business today... you'll sacrifice your body each and everynight for the fans... and the business! BUT, are you ready, are you prepared to sacrifice your SOUL to be the BEST??? }
{ The video footage stops playing and the camera goes back to Joe Snow standing in the ring with the mic in his hand. }
Joe Snow: There is all of your answers right there. Charlie said it himself, he said to do WHATEVER it took to win the title. He told me to sacrifice my SOUL to be the BEST and at Blood, Sweat and Tears that is exactlly what I did. Last night I sacrificed my soul, I did what I had to do to climb to the top of the mountain. And now that I'm up here nobody is giong to be able to knock me off.
Not Candido, not Larry Gionet, Not Frat Boy, Chad Yaro, Michael D Taylor and sure as hell not THP or Chainsaw Charlie. Last night was just a small sample of what I am capable of. I did what I had to do to win the title and now I will do whatever it takes to make sure that this belt right here stays around my waist.
And if anybody back there wants to stop me, just say the words and get in the ring, but I gurantee that all of your Bodies will hit the floor.
{ Joe drops the mic in the middle of the ring and starts walking back up the ramp towards the locker room. He picks up some of the bottles and cups that the fans threw at him earlier and throws them at the fans in the front row as the screen fades to black.}

Still no-body comes out. The atmosphere is electric. You could cut it with a knife. As is something life-changing was about to happen. And it is...

Charlie: I guess you'll all notice that something is missing. Not my trusty ole patented diamond-tipped chainsaw.
Got that right here. Sure I've lost the CWA Heavyweight Title! But that's only temporary, I ASSURE each and every one of you! No - what's really missing, and I don't believe can every be restored is my respect for Joe Snow.
OK, so he achieved his childhood dream... BUT HE COULDN'T DO IT BY HIMSELF! Nope - it took some gimp in a mask to interfere and cost me one of the most important matches of my career.
Well now I'm telling each and every one of the guys in the back, that Chainsaw Charlie will be launching his own investigation into whoever was behind that attack. There'll be questioning... interrogations...
searches... It'll make the Spanish Inquisition seem like a few simple questions. And when I find that treacherous, cowardly, spineless excuse for a human being... well they'll need more than a mask to hide their face cuz once I'm through with them, they're gonna make the Elephant Man look like a Boy Toy in comparison.
No one is exempt. From AJ Darkk and the new guys, right up to Mr CWA President himself... You'd better prepare. Because each and everyone of you will have to look me in the eye, and tell me that you had nothing to do with what went down at BS&T. And if I so much as detect that you're lying... well it'll be the bathroom scene in Scarface all over again!
Gionet, I don't know why you chose the short straw to face me on Friday. Wrong place, wrong time, and definitely wrong attitude! I have to take you down Larry - I >HAVE< TO! You know the end result. You have no control over that. All you get to choose is whether it's over in 60 seconds or if you end up in a wheelchair again.
Now Frat Boy & THP. I saw your little vignette the other week... going on and on about how "this IS the CWA".
I'm glad you realise exactly where you are! You're in the jungle... you know the rest! THP! The only reason you hang out in the gym, day in - day out, is to watch old guys in the shower!
You want a piece of Charlie? You didn't like me with the belt? Well explain to me why you weren't in the Death Cell... the Main Event at BS&T? Oh but you can't can you. THP, Candido's been running circles around you. You've embarassed yourself trying to get noticed. Why can't you admit that you don't have what it takes in this sport. Industries, revolutions have a tendency from time to time, to weed out all the excess crap. That's you! You're just like a Dot.com, now a Dot.BOMB! Hang up your tights, go start a restaurant somewhere, I don't give a <beep>... Don't go away mad - just go away!
Frat Boy... all I've got to say is that the race for the title is on. I've got the headstart! N'uff said!
As for my rematch... You can take it as gospel that it will happen! But I'll address that at Tradition!
Worry not. When this is over, when it's all said and done... The last few days will be forgotten like an unpleasant dream and once again CHAINSAW
CHARLIE WILL BE YOUR CWA WORLD HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPION!!! And if you don't like that? Well you're GUARAN-DAMN-TEED TO BLEED!
