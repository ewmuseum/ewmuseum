CWA RP Packet

13 July 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The scene open in the CWA center Ace Fireside is busy working out asthe camera approaches he looks up and a smile spreads across his face)
Ace Fireside: After the other night my blood burn and ole Ace knows what has to happen next..Some Said I could not makeit as a single because now no one would have my back, but I got a new sayin don't trust anybody!! I will watch my own back from now and blaze a trail of hell all the way though the CWA...
{Ace takes a gulp of something out of a bottle and stands, looking into the camera) Fireside: As for the Natural, more like a Natural piece of crap, you put the hit out on me thought I would be a push over, but what you didn,t know, I been singles before hell I been an undeated TV Champ before..should have done your research a lot better. Being half of the greatest tag team in the history of wrestling yea that was great, but its time for me to be one of the greatest single wrestlers of all time.
If you want some more hell come get aome I got all you handle and more!!! It anit nice, it anit pretty, its FIRESIDE!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! -------------------------------------------------------------------------- ------------------------------------------------- [As the crowd sits in there seats waiting for the next match, the lights suddenly dim to a flat darkness among the arena. Some fans take out lighters and do the traditional lighter routine..some sit in awe wondering what will emerge from the curtains. Some sit in their seat..not knowing what to think or whom to guess will come from behind the curtains. Then..as thoses infamous red, yellow, and white strobe lights filter throughout the arena..the knotches of the PA system are turned up..and Godsmack's "Greed" screams over the PA system like a blind lady has just seen a ghost. The fans immediately jump out of there seats..and erupt in cheers..but then, after moment of waiting in anticipation, the music stops and there is no Casen to be found. Alas, the tron comes on and a cameraman stands there..looking to the ground. Then, two feet appear..and finally the cameraman looks up..to see the NEW North American Champion standing there. He is wearing a white bandana and oakley glasses with red lenses. He begins to speak..in a somewhat girlish voice]
"Hello..my name is Chad Yaro..and..and..I have been trying to get to the top..an SHUT UP!" [Casen rips the oakley's off and immediately steps on them, crushing them into pieces. He rips the bandana off and tosses it to the ground before spitting on it]
"You know Yaro..you have some balls showing up at the pay-per-view to help me win..I didn't ask for your help, I didn't want your help, and most importantly
I didn't NEED your help. You see Yaro..this is just one big mind game you are trying to play on me. You drive up to a run-down shack where you used to live and say "Oh blah blah this and I have taken bumps and had stitches and blah blah blah"..Well cry me a river you fat fuckin baby..because you don't know what it's like to grow up jumping from foster home to foster home..not knowing who the hell your mom is or where the hell your dad is. You don't know what it's like to be abused by a drunk! YOU DON'T FUCKIN KNOW! You don't know what it's like to be thrown out of your house at age 13. To wonder the streets without food or shelter. To sit in the pouring rain without clothing..knowing that it is just a matter of time until you die. YOU DON'T KNOW! So why don't you save your little whiny story for some rich kid who would actually have some pity for you! Let me tell you my story. 13 years old..sitting on the ground in front of the Providence Civic Center in the middle of a fuckin blizzard watching all these wrestling fans walking in to see Bret Hart vs. Bob Backlund.
Some stranger..some man..reaching out his hand and pulling you up. Looking coldly into you eyes and saying "I know how you feel"..taking you in to that infamouse building to get a glimpse of the fantasy..a feel of the electricity..and a sight of the talent. It was a night...I would never forget.
You see..as I saw those two men brawl..the tears in their eyes..the fear in their movements...the glamour in their hearts..I knew that I wanted to be one of them. I will stop there in the story because I don't want to bore my fuckin fans!"
[Casen calms down a bit before continuing on] "Yaro..I want to show you where I trained my heart out...where I got my chance..where my life completely..BEGAN."
[Casen hops into his black 2001 Montero Sport and drives down the long road..heading into Providence. He pulls slowly into an alley, and hops out of the suv.]
"Yaro..I got my start right here. This is where my life began. Right on the streets of Providence. My trainer was my conscience. My mentor was my mind.
Nothing could stop me. No one could hold me back. I would run 5 miles a day..averaging 5 minutes per mile. I would do 700 jumping jacks, 400 push ups, and over 1000 sit-ups a day. I would invite ANYONE to my alley..anyone to my house..and I would kick the living shit out of them until they were completely and utterly knocked unconscious. All that training..all that blood..all that sweat..and all those..wait I don't wanna sound like some fuckin loser from the
World Wrestling Federation who retired before 50. That fuckin mook wasn't determined...and I would NEVER, EVER in my LIFE want to become a mirror image of that man [OOC: This is a reference to Mick Foley, however these are not my real thoughts. These are only thoughts to up the role play. Thanks] Yaro, I have ruined something truely valuable to you..and I am not talking about my
North American Title. I am talking about this..."
[Casen steps to the rear of his Montero Sport and looks at the camerman. All of a sudden the tron switches from the camera looking at Casen to another camera..a camera inside the run down barn that Yaro trained in. The canvas reads, in big red letters "We've only just begun..."]
"Yaro..it's time..." [Casen hops into his Montero Sport then and takes off out of the alley, leaving the camerman there by himself. The crowd watches on]
-------------------------------------------------------------------------- ------------------------------------------------- CWA WORLD CHAMPION /\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\ [CHAINSAW CHARLIE] \/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/ CWA WORLD CHAMPION [The CWA are in town, ready to put on another sell out show. An extra long limo pulls up in the parking lot behind the arena and security rush to protect whoever's inside. The door opens and a leather clad figure climbs out - the CWA World Champion, Chainsaw Charlie!
CWA President Mike Taylor's PA rushes out to meet the hardcore legend, looking apparently flustered.]
PA: Charlie! Mr Taylor wants to see you in his office right away! Its about the forthcoming PPV!
Charlie: I don't have time for Taylor. I've gotta get to the ring and cut a promo about my buddy Joe Snow!
Tell Taylor we'll talk WHEN I'M READY!!! [Charlie storms past her so quickly she drops her papers on the floor.] PA: Oh my! [Scene cuts to the arena, where the thousands of fans are all chanting "Charlie! Charlie!". Pyros criss-cross the entranceway and then a airbomb explodes in front of the TraditionTron! Out of the smoke a patented diamond-tipped chainsaw rises high in the air and Linkin Park's "One Step
Closer" BLASTS out of the PA system.
The CWA World Championship belt is raised up high in the air and the extreme icon strides swiftly to the ring! Climbing the turnbuckle, chainsaw and title held up to the heavens, the crowd reach fever pitch! Charlie places both of his most important possessions down in the middle of the ring and is passed a mic.]
Charlie: Well the Journey has certainly reached its END for Frat Boy!!! Perry, it's over!!! You're looking at the last man standing! You're looking at the CWA World Champion! You're looking at Chainsaw Charlie! Despite interference left right and centre, I proved that in our eternal feud, I AM the better man! That's gotta eat you up inside like cancer, but only YOU can deal with it!!!
Now lets move onto my road partner, Joe Snow! [Crowd cheers, but there are a few angry faces throughout the crowd.] Charlie: Joey, you had some cutting words to say to me at the end of the PPV! [He reaches down and places the CWA World Title over his shoulder.] Charlie: Joe, so it appears you didn't like the way I "mistreated" the CWA
World Title in the past? Well I'll hold my hands up to that! Some of what I did was unforgivable. I admit that! Maybe I loved the controversy that people like Frat Boy embrace every living day.
However you have to understand something. I had to do WHATEVER it took to win the title! I had to unleash the demons within me... And as much as it pains me to say this, I don't think you'll ever understand that.
You're one of the best hardcore wrestlers in the business today... you'll sacrifice your body each and everynight for the fans... and the business!
BUT, are you ready, are you prepared to sacrifice your SOUL to be the BEST??? Because if you aren't - there are plenty of newcomers to the CWA who WILL do
ANYTHING to stand where I am now - the best in the business, King of the
Hill and Champion of Champions! Mitchell, Yaro, Russell! All of these are proving themselves!
Do they have what it takes? Are they going to be the next headliners? That's down to them. What you have to concern yourself with is will they beat you to the spotlight?
We've got a tag match against Frat Boy & Living Corpse Larry Gionet at Tradition! A perfect opportunity for you to prove to me, and more importantly YOURSELF, that you have what it takes!
Then and only then will I consider putting up the CWA World Title against you! Perry. Larry. Friday night, Joe and I will be bringing the chainsaw and barbed-wire singapore cane with us. All you have to do is show up, get to the ring on time, and make your peace with your God because we're going to introduce you to him!
YOU'RE BOTH GUARAN-DAMN-TEED TO BLEED!!!
