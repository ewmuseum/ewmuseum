CWA RP Packet

10 August 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
********************************************************************** The camera fades out to a black screen with this lettering written in blood on it. THE WHEEL OF TIME TURNS AND AGES COME AND GO, WHAT WAS, WHAT WILL BE AND WHAT IS WILL YET FALL UNDER THE RAINMAN. Pouring rain on the screen washes away the blood on the screen and soon it is all black. ********************************************************************** Devlin wanders out of a side street and walks silently down the lane. As the rain starts to slowly fall, he ducks into a covered doorway and lights a cigarettes. When he finishes it, he walks into the pub across the street and sits at a table in the back. The old man behind the bar walks up and asks what he'll have.
"A bottle of Krug champagne, non vintage, old man, and make sure it's of the grape mix." The bartender brings the bottle and a glass as the CWA cameramen show up.
"THP. I'm glad your calling me a rookie, with no experience. What you may not know is that I'm not all that new around here, or new to some of these people. I've fought Chainsaw Charlie, Gionet, Streetpunk and others. I've won some and for extenuating circumstances, i've lost the others. Do not take me lightly ole boy. I was born a soldier, and you my son, wouldn't last a bad saturday night in Belfast."
The screen goes dark as Devlin finishes his glass of champagne and lights a cigarette. ********************************************************************** -------------------------------------------------------------------------- ------------------------------------------------- {The scene opens up to an old abondoned warehouse. The outside is very rundown. Windows are broken and boarded up. Siding is falling off. There is yellow police tape blocking the entrance. Upon entry, the place is dimly lit.
There are some old wooden bleachers that surround an old, rickity wrestling ring. There is a hold in the ceiling of the building that the sun is shining through that makes it look like there is a spotlight shining down on the ring.
In the ring is a huge man, about 7'3, 325 pounds. He is wearing a black trench coat. He has long black hair in dread locks. His face is plae with many scars across his face.}
TPO: Ace Fireside, how does if feel to have a nice confidence booster. In fact, every wrestler that has faced me in the ring, how does it feel to have a huge confidence booster? You see, I came in here, and really didnt take things
seriously. I was very lax on how I responded to the CWA. Now, Ace, you really think I am nothing more than a comic book character? Well, Ace, you will find out as well as Adam Mitchell just how much of a badass that I am. I am the DEVIL INCARNATE. I am far from a comic book character.
You can continue thinking that if you wish, but you will be sorely mistaken. I am going to take you and Adam to the edge then instead of letting you two fight over who goes off the edge, I will put both of you over myself.
What happened at Blood, Sweat, and Tears was not me. That is not what I do. I dont job for anyone. I was brought in here to whoop on some select few people, but then was told that if I didnt job, that I would be fired. I am telling everyone, NO MORE. Ace and Adam, I am going to give the both of you a Double
A, ass whoopin that you will never forget. This is the beginning. The beginning of the Era of Destruction. And there is nothing anyone can do about it.
