CWA (Continental, 2000) — Roleplay

CWA RP Packet

6 August 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The T H P Shoot IS COMING! But for now! You Must Settle For This! After the pay-per-view went off the air! The Hottest Prospect had a few things to say! [The scene opens up at CWA Blood Sweat and Tears in the Georgia Dome in Atlanta. The main event has just concluded and Joe Snow and Chainsaw Charlie have both left to head to the back. Matt Chapen and Chris Poleski have entered the ring to thank the fans for being there and telling them to have a safe ride home.]
[All of a sudden, THP storms out from the back. The crowd that's starting to leave turns and cheers and begans to stop their motions. THP walks out to the ring as the arena lights are on and people are standing through the stairways to get ready to go home.]
[Everyone has stopped and a "THP" chant breaks out from a small portion of the crowd. Others don't know how to react, some are boo'ing, a few cheering but mostly a lot of commotion between the fans watching THP enter the ring.]
[THP pulls out a mic and looks around. He begins to speak in a loud type of voice] THP: Now I know that we're not on tv anymore and this pay-per-view's already gone off the air, but I've come out here to say a few things and get a few things off my chest!
[Some fans begin to sit down] THP: I know you expect me to come out here and apologize, for what I did to
Kevin Candido and what I did to all his fans. Well you know what!? I ain't apologizing for shit!
[That comment gets a mixed reaction from the crowd.] THP: Joe Snow, let me tell you something boy. You won the CWA World Title and defeated me in the same night. Boy, I guess this was your lucky night!
But Joe, just remember, you won't be holding onto that title for very long! After you won the three way with me and Perry, I snapped. I was ready to jacknife your ass three times. But no, instead, Frat Boy Perry Tanhammer made the biggest mistake of his career when he nailed me with the microphone.
Frat Boy, I think you've outstayed your career! I helped you screw over Kevin Candido for the title and that's how you repay me? Well Frat Boy, i got a little message for you. THP IS BACK!!!!!!!!!!!
[The crowd cheers] THP: That's right.......I'mmmmm back!!!!!!! [The crowd continues to cheer. We hear more "THP" chants] THP: Kevin Candido, what I did to you was not only what I said it was, it was what Joe Snow and Frat Boy made me do to you! So if you want to look for revenge, you find those two men for driving me to the edge of taking out your career! OUR WAR IS OVER! I have beaten you once again and Kevin, you go elsewhere because I've got other plans right now!
[He pauses and paces back and forth] THP: Chainsaw Charlie, you couldn't hold on to the title could you? You couldn't even retain your title against Joe Snow in a singles match? How pathetic is that!? Charlie...I'm going to get that World Title, but like
I've said for the past two months, I'm going to get you first!
[The crowd continues to cheer and react to THP] THP: So this is how it's gonna be huh? This is the new THP? The THP of old? The THP that the CWA decides to have Frat Boy and Joe Snow work a shoot over on me so I can get my ass pinned on pay-per-view, and have to do a job...TO JOE SNOW NO LESS! It's bullshit!
[He pauses] THP: But that's okay, because Snow, your time is coming! Your reign will be short! Charlie, you and I will meet! And Frat Boy Perry Tanhammer, I'm going to end your career you little piece of shit! I think I'm gonna give old Mad Dog a call and see if he saw the shit you pulled tonight!
[He pauses one more time] THP: Frat Boy, you don't fuck with me! I thought you realized that..especially in the state I've been in the past few months! But obviously, the delusional Frat Boy would rather stir up trouble than to stay clear of me and logically save his own ass! But Perry, it's too late for that, because you're gonna get the shit kicked out of you! MARK MY WORDS!
I'mmmmmmmmmmmmmm BACK!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! ["King Nothing" plays as THP walks out of the ring towards the back as the crowd gives THP a mixed, but very loud reaction. Some fans begin to leave as the scene fades out]

Blood, Sweat, & Tears. He appears to be a little stressed out and upset at the same time.}
Kevin Candido: DAMN IT!!! DAMN IT!!! DAMN IT!!! {Pauses} KC: A great man once said, “I stand up next to a mountain and I chop it down with the edge of my hand!”
But in this case I stood up next to a mountain, and got the ever loving shit kicked out of me. Yes once
again I admit it I llllll… I llllllll… I llloooosssss… I LOST there I said it. {A production assistant comes into the room} PA: Mr. Candido your limo is here to pick you up. KC: Tell him to wait PA: Okay {He leaves} KC: As I was about to say where does the Starkiller go from here. I just do not know where to go from here but mark my word, It may not be tonight, It may not be at Tradition but one of these days YOU WILL GO DOWN!!
It may be in a match, but not necessarily against me. Watch your back because I want revenge and I will get revenge. KC: I was told that I am going to fight A.J. Darkk. A.J. I feel so sorry for you. The fact that I can not fight THP again also makes this worse for your wares.
A.J. do me a favor fear Kkevin Candido. Fear me please. Fear me for what is going to happen to you, because I cannot fight THP.
KC: Now if you please turn off the camera so that I may get changed and get to my limo so that I do not miss my flight.
{Camera Fades as your hear Candido yell} KC: GOD DAMN IT!!!! -------------------------------------------------------------------------- ------------------------------------------------- * "Man in the Box" by Alice N' Chains blares over the P.A. system as Chad Yaro walks out to a deafening chorus of cheers. Chad is wearing a pair of tearaway mesh pants, and a white CWA logo T-shirt. He stands atop the entrance way, raises one hand above his head and the pop from the crowd was amazing. Chad proceeds to walk to the ring. Chad signals for a mic and addresses the crowd. *
Chad: Last night I came here and did exactly what I said I was going to do and that is beat Anthony "the animal" Davis.
*Crowd POPS* Chad: Anthony, your size and strength was nothing to be compared with the complete package that Chad Yaro brings to the ring each and every night. But I must admit we had one hell of a fight last night. But in the end it was the
Hilton Head Special that guided me to the win. And I am also aware that two congratulations are to be said, one being to someone I know very well and that is the Northeastern Champion, Jasen Casen, and the other goes out to the new
CWA World Champion, Joe Snow. But Joe you better start polishing that belt since after Tradition it will be mine.
*Crowd POPS* Chad: Joe, you are a great wrestler, but I am still Chad Yaro. So Tradition takes place in Cleveland, Ohio. The home of the Cleveland Cavaliers and the same place where we lost one of wrestlings greatest competitors, Owen Hart. So just like the Lakers did to the Cavs, Snow I am gonna kick your ass and take home the title. Joe you had a tough match last night at Blood, Sweat and Tears but don't think for a second that I am going to go easy on you because Chad
Yaro doesn't play that way, I go 100 percent at all times. No breaks. Snow I will be training nonstop for this match because this is what I have worked so hard for, I have worked so hard to win the World Title, and I know that the people will help me win it as they are what gives me the drive to compete and put it all on the line. So Joe, you feeling lucky? Go ahead, roll the dice, take the chance.

THE WHEEL OF TIME TURNS AND AGES COME AND GO, WHAT WAS, WHAT WILL BE AND WHAT IS WILL YET FALL UNDER THE RAINMAN. Pouring rain on the screen washes away the blood on the screen and soon it is all black. ********************************************************************** A cold wind blew in from Belfast Lough, driving rain across the city. Jason
Devlin moved along a narrow street between tall warehouses, relics of the
Victorian era, mostly boarded up now. He stood on the corner, a small man, no more than five feet eleven, wearing a trench coat and an old rain hat.
He was on the waterfront now. There were ships out there at anchor, their riding lights moving up and down, for there was a heavy swell driving into the docks. There was a sound of gunfire in the distance. He glanced in the general direction, lit a cigarette in cupped hands, and moved on.
There was an air of desolation to the whole area, examples of the devestation caused by twenty-five years of war everywhere, and his feet crunched over broken glass. He found what he was looking for five minutes later, a warehouse with a peeling sign on the wall that said MURPHY & SON
--IMPORT & EXPORT. There were large double doors with a small Judas gate for easy access. It opened with a slight creak and he stepped inside. It was a place of shadows, empty except for an old Ford van and a jumble of packing cases. There was an office at the far end with glass walls, one of two panes broken, and a dim light shone there. Devlin removed his rain hat and ran a hand through his hair. Devlin climbed into the van and into a hi-tech editing booth. He started flipping a few switches and all throughout the world screens flickered and Devlin appeared on the screen.
"I'd like to say Good Morning to the million of viewers, actually probably closer to billions of viewers. Many of you are wondering why I was out watching AJ Daark. Maybe wondering how I know him, or some skeptics might be wondering if I know him. That is for time to tell. Today i'm here because I have an upcoming match with THP. A formidable opponent. Better then me, and worse. With his new style we've been seeing, he is definatly crazier, taking more chances, however that leads to mistakes. And I don't make mistakes, I only capitalize on others. I just hope he's up to the challenge. Now onto
Ace Fireside who defeated me a week back at Tradition. That was a good match, that could have gone anyway. I have no regrets, i might have done some things differently, but i have no regrets, and if you ever need somebody to watch your back. i'm here. One last word. THP, your going down, expect to feel the LIGHTNING.
The screen flickers goes all fuzzy and then resumers to normal programming. Devlin leaves the warehouse and dissapears into a cloud of mist coming off the river. **********************************************************************
