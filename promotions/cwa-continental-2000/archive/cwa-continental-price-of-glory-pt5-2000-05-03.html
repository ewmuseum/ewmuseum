CWA Price of Glory Part 5

3 May 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Matt Chapen: We just whitnessed one hell of a battle between Mighty Ryan Kelly and Kevin Candido in which Mighty Ryan Kelly won the match! But before that we were interrupted by Aidan McKeenit who prefers to be called the "Natural One"!
We will have to find out what he is all about! Now following this tag team title match they must come back to fight in a hell in a cell!
Rick Collings: This contest is a triple threat elimination match for the vacant CWA Tag Team Championship! First coming down the aisle, Ace Fireside,
Rick Frost...ATTITUDE ADJUSTMENT!!!
{"Ride The Lightning" by Metallica plays as Attitude Adjustment come to the ring to a wave of cheers from the crowd.}
Matt Chapen: Attitude Adjustment have made a great impact here in the CWA and look to make their mark as the first CWA Tag Team Champions!
Rick Collings: And their opponents, the CWA Hardcore Champion Mark Blaze and
Derek Stryker!!!
{"Bring The Pain" by Method Man plays as The Untouchables come to the ring a chorus of boos from the crowd..}
Chris Poleski: Look at these Untouchables Matt. They have taken down every team that has been put in their way and they are my pick to walk out the tag team champions of the world!
Rick Collings: And their opponents...Terry Rogers, Dylan Donnelly...THE ULTIMATE BACKFIELD!!! Matt Chapen: Terry Rogers goes for a back suplex, BUT RICK FROST FLIPS OVER! Rick Frost nails Terry Rogers with an atomic drop! Rick Frost takes Terry
Rogers down with a forearm to the back. Rick Frost tags out to Derek Stryker!
Derek Stryker climbs to the top turnbuckle, but Terry Rogers nails him in the stomach! STRYKER FALLS ONTO THE TOP TURNBUCKLE! Terry Rogers tags out to
Dylan Donnelly! Both teams making the quick tags preventing Stryker from making one!
Chris Poleski: This is just a conspiracy I know it! Dylan Donnelly and Terry
Rogers whip Derek Stryker into the ropes. They attempt to hit Derek Stryker with a double kick to the midsection, BUT STRYKER COUNTERS WITH A DOUBLE
CLOTHESLINE! Dylan Donnelly goes for a clothesline, but Derek Stryker COUTNERS IT WITH A CRUCIFIX! One... Two... Kickout! WHAT A FLYING BULLDOG BY STRYKER! Derek Stryker goes for a Canadian backbreaker, but Dylan Donnelly COUTNERS WITH A BACKDROP!
One... Two... Kickout! Matt Chapen: Dylan Donnelly tags out to Terry Rogers! MARK BLAZE ENTERS THE
RING AND LAYS OUT DYLAN DONNELLY! COME ON REF! DEREK STRYKER THROWS ROGERS
OUT OF THE RING! Mark Blaze comes over to make it two-on-one! This match is much more than just those CWA Tag Team Titles, its about pride! Its about recieving bragging rights to be the number one tag team in the CWA! Mark Blaze goes for a spinning leg lariat, but Terry Rogers DUCKS OUT OF THE WAY AND BLAZE
HITS HIS TAG TEAM PARTNER! Chris Poleski: ITS OK! It was just an accident! Terry Rogers hits a Northern
Lights suplex on Derek Stryker! Terry Rogers goes for a moonsault, but Derek
Stryker GETS THE KNEES UP! Derek Stryker hits Terry Rogers with a diving elbow
smash! DEREK STRYKER SETS UP A TABLE ON THE OUTSIDE! Derek Stryker whips Terry Rogers into the guardrail! Derek Stryker sets up Terry Rogers on the table! STRYKER COMES FLYING OFF THE TOP WITH A SPLASH THROUGH THE TABLE! THE
CROWD IS LOVING IT! How can they not?
Matt Chapen: Stryker is showing the burning desire in him to be the co-holder of the CWA Tag Team champions! Derek Stryker throws Terry Rogers back into the ring! Derek Stryker runs into the ropes. Stryker comes back, BUT BOTH ROGERS
AND STRYKER GET HIT WITH A DOUBLE CLOTHESLINE! Both men trying to get to their tag team partners! ROGERS TAGS RICK FROST! FROST NAILS STRYKER WITH A PUNCH!
ANOTHER ONE! Rick Frost runs into the ropes. Derek Stryker hits Rick Frost with a shoulderblock! COVER!
One... Two... Shoulder up! Rick Frost throws Derek Stryker out of the ring! Chris Poleski: This is a very dangerous place for Rick Frost to be! Didn't he see Stryker put Rogers through a table just moments ago? Rick Frost is handed a chair AND RICK FROST HITS HIM WITH IT! HOW VICIOUS DID THAT SOUND? PURE
STEEL MEETING SKULL! Rick Frost reenters the ring and Derek Stryker follows him back in with the chair! Rick Frost tags out to Terry Rogers. GERMAN
SUPLEX ON DEREK STRYKER! One... Two... Thr...Kickout! Matt Chapen: Terry Rogers tags out to Dylan Donnelly! Dylan Donnelly nails
Derek Stryker with a kick to the midsection. Dylan Donnelly tags out to Rick
Frost! Rick Frost whips Derek Stryker into the ropes. Derek Stryker hits Rick
Frost with a kick! Derek Stryker FINALLY MAKES THE TAG TO THE CWA HARDCORE
CHAMPION! Mark Blaze goes for a Hotshot, BUT FROST COUNTERS WITH A LARIET! In turn, Mark Blaze counters it with an armbar submission! What quick thinking on the part of the CWA Hardcore champion!
Chris Poleski: DAMN RIGHT MATT! Rick Frost is struggling to reach the ropes! I THINK HE IS TAPPING OUT! ASK HIM REF! Rick Frost is inching his way towards the ropes and reaches them! Mark Blaze runs into the ropes! He comes back AND
HITS A FLYING HEADSCISSORS ON RICK FROST! MARK BLAZE JUST NAILED RICK FROST
WITH THE STEEL CHAIR! BLAZE WITH THE COVER! THAT'S GOTTA BE IT!
One... Two... Kickout! Matt Chapen: Rick Frost tags out to Ace Fireside. Ace Fireside goes for a monkey flip, but Mark Blaze blocks it. Mark Blaze whips Ace Fireside into the ropes. Ace Fireside comes back WITH A ROLLING KICK! Ace Fireside tags out to
Dylan Donnelly! Dylan Donnelly hits a Hotshot on Mark Blaze. Dylan Donnelly
goes for a kick to the midsection, BUT BLAZE COUNTERS WITH A LEGSWEEP! Mark Blaze tags out to Ace Fireside and Ace Fireside tags out to Rick Frost! Rick
Frost hits a DDT on Mark Blaze! Rick Frost runs into the ropes AND LEVELS HIM
WITH A CLOTHESLINE! Chris Poleski: Come on Mark! Rick Frost kicks Mark Blaze. Mark Blaze punches
Rick Frost. Mark Blaze whips Frost into the ropes AND CONNECTS WITH A
POWERSLAM! BEAUTIFUL! HE MUST BE FINISHED! One... Two... Shoulder up! HOW DOES HE KEEP KICKING OUT? Matt Chapen: He is not giving up that quick! Attitude Adjustment want to leave Price of Glory as the champions! Ace Fireside places Mark Blaze on the turnbuckle. BELLY TO BACK SUPERPLEX!
One... Two... DEREK STRYKER MAKES THE SAVE! Mark Blaze tags out to Terry Rogers. Ace Fireside runs into the ropes. Ace Fireside hits Terry Rogers WITH A DIAMOND NECKBREAKER! NICELY DONE! One... Two... Three!!! Rick Collings: The Ultimate Backfield, HAVE BEEN ELIMINATED! Chris Poleski: Well the Ultimate Backfield have to hit the showers! Now its
The Untouchables and Attitude Adjustment! Mark Blaze tags out to Derek
Stryker! Derek Stryker whips Ace Fireside into the turnbuckle. Derek Stryker runs shoulder-first into the corner, BUT FIRESIDE LIFTS THE KNEE! Ace Fireside punches Derek Stryker! DEREK STRYKER PUNCHES ACE FIRESIDE! THIS CROWD IS
GOING INTO A FRENZY! Ace Fireside kicks Derek Stryker! Ace Fireside tags out to Rick Frost. Rick Frost whips Derek Stryker into the ropes AND TAKES HIM
DOWN WITH A DDT! One... Two... Kickout! NO! NO! NO! ITS NOT OVER! Matt Chapen: Derek Stryker tags out to Mark Blaze! Rick Frost goes for a clothesline, but Mark Blaze counters it with a belly-to-belly suplex! In turn,
Rick Frost COUNTERS WITH A FACERAKE! FROST BITE! ITS OVER!
One... Two.. Three!!! Rick Collings: The Untouchables HAVE BEEN ELIMINATED! Therefore, your winners
AND NEEEEEWWWWWW CWA TAG TEAM CHAMPIONS...ATTITUDE ADJUSTMENT!!!
Matt Chapen: My oh my! After one brutal matchup, Attitude Adjustment beat both the Ultimate Backfield and The Untouchables! Wait a second! TWO MEN ARE
BEHIND AA! Frost and Fireside turn around AND GET BLINDSIDED! TWO MEN HAVE
JUST ATTACKED THE TAG TEAM CHAMPIONS! WHO WOULD DO SUCH A THING?
Chris Poleski: Some pretty smart guys Matt! One has the mic! {After Nitroglycerin attacks the Tag Team champions, Havoc grabs the microphone.} Havoc: I knew since the first day I signed with the CWA, there was going to be a bunch of crappy people that was going to try and down
Nitroglycerin. But guess, what, that shit ain't gonna happen. Not while me and "The Natural One" are here!
{Havoc drops the mic as he and Aidan "The Natural One" McKeenit leave the ring to the disproval of the crowd.}
Matt Chapen: How dispicable can two men be? THIS IS JUST WRONG! Someone get help for Attitude Adjustment!
