CWA (Continental, 2000) — Card

CWA Price of Glory Part 3

30 May 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Matt Chapen: We have returned and we just saw a debut of Buddy Costello and the return of Mark Blaze.
Chris Poleski: Due to that confrontation, Blaze and Costello will fight at this Friday's tradition!
Matt Chapen: That should be a great matchup! Coming up next we have a rivalry that has raged on for a good year or so. A grudge match a year in the making and it all comes to a halt tonight at Price of Glory!
Rick Collings: This contest is schedueled for one fall with a 30 mintue time limit! First coming down the aisle, being accompanied to the ring by
Cherry...JASEN CASEN!!! {Godsmack's "Greed" plays over the Public Announce System as Jasen Casen makes his way down to the ring.}
Rick Collings: And his opponent...CHAD YARO!!! {Yaro's music plays as he also heads to the ring.} Matt Chapen- These two men waste no time starting this match off, their trading blows in the center of the ring.
Chris Poleski- These men have been waiting to get their hands on one another. Matt Chapen- Yaro takes control and whips Jasen Casen into the ropes, and drops him with an elbow.
Chris Poleski- These guys will be the future of the CWA! I'm telling you Matt. Matt Chapen- They both are very talented competitors. Yaro sets up Jasen Casen for a suplex, but Casen blocks it and nails Chad Yaro with a beautiful snap suplex of his own!
Chris Poleski- Yaro is quick to his feet, but Jasen Casen runs into the ropes and hits Yaro with the clothesline!
Matt Chapen- Casen continues his assault with a hotshot! Yaro's neck snapped off that top rope!
Chris Poleski- Cherry at ringside cheering her man! She's a hottie! I'd like to get a piece of that...
Matt Chapen- Calm down Chris. Chris Poleski- Sorry, sorry. Matt Chapen- Casen picks up Yaro for another hotshot, but Chad Yaro COUNTERS IT WITH A THESZ PRESS! Chris Poleski- Yaro follows it up with an assault of rights to the head of
Jasen Casen! Matt Chapen- Yaro plants him with a powerbomb! Yaro on the apron now. SLINGSHOT SPLASH MISSES! Chris Poleski- Jasen Casen rolled out of the way just in time! Matt Chapen- Now Jasen Casen is climbing to the top turnbuckle, but Chad Yaro is up and falls on the ropes. Casen is crotched on the top!
Chris Poleski- Oh man! That'll stop any offense Casen had going. Matt Chapen- Chad Yaro up on top as well now! BELLY-TO-BELLY SUPERPLEX BY CHAD
YARO! What a move!
One... Two... Kickout! Matt Chapen- Yaro quick to get up and hits an nice Arabian Moonsault off the top rope! One... Two... Kickout! Chris Poleski- Yaro just can't keep Casen down. Matt Chapen- Chad Yaro whips Jasen Casen into the ropes, Jasen comes back ONLY TO GET A SPINNING MULE KICK TO THE HEAD! Matt Chapen- Chad Yaro picking up Casen! This could be the end. SCOOP REVERSE DDT ON JASEN CANSEN! COVER! One... Two... Three!!! Rick Collings: Here is your winner...CHAD YARO!!! {"Man In The Box" by Alice in Chains plays again as Chad Yaro's hand is raised and he celebrates in the ring. Jasen Casen and Cherry angerly leave the ring to the backstage area. Before going behind the curtain, Jasen Casen gives Yaro an angry look.}
Matt Chapen- Chad Yaro hits the Scoop Reverse DDT and picks up the win.
Chris Poleski- You can bet things aren't over between these two. Matt Chapen- Well, both these men definately have a bright future in front of them in CWA!

— Part 3 of 8 —
