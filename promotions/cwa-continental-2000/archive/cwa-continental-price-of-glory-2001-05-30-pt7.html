CWA (Continental, 2000) — Card

CWA Price of Glory Part 7

30 May 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Rick Collings: This contest is schedueled for one fall with a 60 minute time limit and is for the CWA North American Title! First coming down the aisle...IRONMAN JORDAN JONES!!!
{Jordan Jones's music plays as he heads down to the ringside area. The crowd is booing him loudly}
Rick Collings: And his opponent...STREET PUNK!!! {"Iron Man" by Black Sabbath plays as Street Punk comes to the ring to a mixed reaction. He is holding his trusty signapore cane.}
Rick Collings: And their opponent...FRANTIC!!! {"Same Old Song And Dance" by Aerosmith plays as Frantic comes to the ring as the fans go crazy.}
Matt Chapen- It looks like Frantic and Jordan Jones are gonna start this match off. {Ding, Ding, Ding} Matt Chapen- Jones, who was bad-mouthing the crowd, is hit from behind by
Frantic with a big dropkick.
Chris Poleski- I'm just so glad that Jordan Jones is the new North American
Champion. Matt Chapen- The match just started! He isn't champ! Chris Poleski- He will be! Matt Chapen- Frantic picks up Jones and drops him with a brainbuster! Frantic quickly makes the tag to Street Punk.
Chris Poleski- Of course Frantic wants someone else to the do the work for him! Matt Chapen- Street Punk charges Jones, but Jordan Jones hits him with a back elbow. Jones goes down low and drives a knee into Street Punk. Jones throws
Street Punk out of the ring!
Chris Poleski- That was not a good idea. Matt Chapen- Street Punk is known all around the world for his hardcore, aggressive style. This might not be a smart move on the part of the rookie!
Jones quickly goes to work on Street Punk, but Punk whips Jordan Jones into the guardrail! Street Punk follows up with a big knee on Jordan Jones! And Punk with a punch right between the eyes of Jones.
Chris Poleski- Get back in the ring Jones! Matt Chapen- Street Punk picks up Jordan Jones and drops him with hotshot on the steel guardrail!
Chris Poleski- What is Frantic doing? Matt Chapen- Frantic off the ropes, SOMERSAULT BODY BLOCK ON STREET PUNK! Jordan Jones is behind Frantic, and pushes him into the ringpost! Chris Poleski- Good thinking Jones! Matt Chapen- Jordan Jones dumps Street Punk back into the ring, and heads up top! Chris Poleski- What's he doing! Matt Chapen- BIG ELBOW SMASH ON STREET PUNK!! One... Two... Shoulder Up! Frantic is now on the apron, and Jones tags him in! Matt Chapen- Frantic is groggy, and Punk puts him down with a spinebuster! Street Punk tags Jordan Jones back in! Chris Poleski- That's not fair! Matt Chapen- Jones to the ropes, BUT FRANTIC CAUGHT HIM IN AN AIRPLANE SPIN! Jordan Jones is trying to break the hold and Frantic drops him hard on the mat with a flapjack! Frantic off the ropes for a leg lariat, but Jordan Jones sits up! That let Jordan Jones get a breather! Jordan Jones makes the tag to
Street Punk!
Chris Poleski- Yeah! Just rest champ. Matt Chapen- These men know each other very well. And they brawl it out in the center of the ring.
Chris Poleski- Yeah! Just kill each other. Matt Chapen- Punk, who has a huge size advantage takes control. And whips
Frantic into the turnbuckle. Frantic stumbles back and Punk lays him out with a
HUGE release german suplex! Punk sends him into the ropes now, and catches him coming back with a gutwrench powerbomb! What a move!
One... Two... Jones breaks up the count! Chris Poleski- You the man Jones! Matt Chapen- Punk sends Frantic to the ropes again, BUT FRANTIC HITS HIM WITH A
HURRICANRANA! Frantic slides to the ropes now, AND MAKES THE TAG! Jones quickly in and picks Punk up INTO A TIGER DRIVER! COVER!
One... Two... Kickout! Jordan Jones makes the tag to Frantic. FRANTIC JUST GOT OUT! Chris Poleski- Good thinking by Jordan Jones! Matt Chapen- Frantic slowly gets in. He runs into the ropes AND NAILS THE BLOOD ANGEL WITH A BACKSPIN DDT OUT OF NOWHERE! One... Two... Thr...Kickout! Street Punk gets up but gets knocked silly with a leg lariat! Street Punk is in trouble! One... Two... Kickout! Matt Chapen- Frantic runs into the ropes again and misses with a shoulderblock,
Street Punk hits Frantic with an elbow. Frantic goes down. Punk has just slowed this matchup down. The more Frantic is off his feet and can't pull off the aerial attacks, the better for Street Punk to win the title. Punk sends
Frantic into the ropes once more, BUT FRANTIC NAILS HIM WITH A DROPKICK! Punk quickly makes the tag to Jordan Jones!
Chris Poleski- You better run Frantic! Matt Chapen- Jordan Jones misses the clothesline, AND FRANTIC HITS HIM WITH A SUPERKICK! One... Two... Shoulder Up! Chris Poleski- That's it Jones! Matt Chapen- Frantic sets him up, AND FRANTIC HAS THE FRANTIC STRETCH ON JONES! Jones has nowhere to go! The ref is asking him, BUT STREET PUNK BREAKS IT UP! Chris Poleski- Punk wants to be the winner of this match! Matt Chapen- You are right about that! When its every man for himself, thats the mindset you must have!
Chris Poleski- Too bad Jones will still come out victorious. Matt Chapen- Jordan Jones whips Frantic into the turnbuckle, but Frantic reverses it. JORDAN JONES GOT RAMMED INTO STREET PUNK! FRANTIC WITH A SMALL
PACKAGE! One... Two... Thr...JORDAN JONES REVERSES IT! HE'S GRABBING THE TIGHTS! One... Two... Three!!! Rick Collings: Here is your winner and new CWA North American Champion...IRONMAN JORDAN JONES!!! {Jones's theme music plays again as he quickly heads to the back, holding the
North American Title.}
Chris Poleski- I told you Matt, I told you! Matt Chapen- Jordan Jones comes out the winner, but I'm sure he'll run into
Street Punk once again!
