Matt Chapen: What an unbelievable night its been so far, and now we've got a
Falls Count Anywhere match!

THP, and now THP is going to show Candido the error of his ways!!!

THP somehow manages to keep his balance and he's standing on the ring apron!
Chris Poleski: For a big guy he's got great balance! Candido grabs THP by the back of the head and hits him with a forearm to the face! He goes for another - but THP counters dropping to the floor, guillotining Candido on the top rope!!! That almost beheaded Candido!

Frat Boy he's been downright sick!

Candido manages to get a shoulder up! This match was almost a non-starter! Matt Chapen: Candido's just being dominated by THP's size and power! THP pulls up Candido, only to drop him with a backbreaker! He follows it up with an elbow drop, right onto Candido's chest! Candido's trying to pull himself up but
THP's kicking him in those ribs! THP now grabbing Candido's legs... kick to the midsection. He doesn't let go as he falls back and slingshots Candido into the ringpost!!!

THP hooks the leg for the pin...

Candido pulls himself up now and he grabs THP and rams him head first into the safety rail! THP continues to bleed here as Candido takes him and rakes him across the top of that rail!!
Raking his head, driving it deep against the top of that steel rail! And now
Candido takes THP back into the aisle, and he whips him into the opposite side of the rail AND THP GOES UP AND OVER THE RAIL AND INTO THE CROWD! OH MY!!
Chris Poleski: That final few rows of fans there in the aisle seats really getting more for their money here as Candido goes over and pulls THP up here and he PICKS HIM UP AND SLAMS HIM BACK DOWN ONTO THE AISLE WITH A SUPLEX!

BODY DROPS CANDIDO ONTO THE CHAIR!!! THP SITS DOWN WITH THE COVER!

He picks him up and holds him up high, BUT CANDIDO KICKS THP INTO THE CHEST AND BOTH MEN GO CRASHING DOWN!! And now both men are down and out as they both try to get up!
Matt Chapen: What a contest between these two men here as both men slowly get to there feet here! Candido grabs that chair though and THP charges in, BUT
