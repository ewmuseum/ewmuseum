{"I Can't Drive 55" by Sammy Hagar plays as the camera shows the capacity crowd at the Ice Palace in Tampa Florida. The fans looked pumped for another exciting night of CWA action. The camera zooms on Matt Chapen and Chris
Poleski.} Matt Chapen: Hello everyone and WELCOME to CWA Tradition! We are live at the
Ice Palace in Tampa Florida! We have 3 title matches here tonght as Rick
Taylor tries to take away the CWA Traditional Title from Ace Fireside! Shane
Russell defends the CWA North American Title against THP and the main event features Chad Yaro CWA World Champion defending that title agianst Kevin
Candido! Rick Collings: This three way dance is an elimination match with a 30 minute time limit! First coming down the aisle...JEFF KANE!!!
{Jeff Kane's music plays as he comes to the ring to a round of cheers from the fans in attendence.}
Rick Collings: And his opponent...JOHNNY KYLER!!! {"Creep" by Radiohead plays as Johnny Kyler slowly makes his way to the ring as the fans boo him.}
Rick Collings: And their opponent...A.J. DARKK!!! {"Dream On" by Aerosmith plays as A.J. Darkk comes to the ring as the fans cheer him on.} Matt Chapen: Tieup by Kyler and Darkk but Kane kicks A.J. in the midsection and levels Johnny Kyle with a clothesline. He whips Darkk into the corner, but
A.J. Darkk reverses it! Darkk runs at Kane but A.J. picks him up and he lands on the top turnbuckle.
Chris Poleski: JEFF KANE WITH A CROSS BODY ON JOHNNY KYLER! Darkk runs at
Kyler but Johnny picks him up and over his head AND DARKK DROPKICKS KANE TO THE
OUTSIDE! Darkk connects with a shoudler block. A.J. runs into the ropes, leapfrog by Kyler. Johnny Kyler goes for a monkey flip but A.J. jumps over him. Kyler ducks the clothesline. Tilt-a-wirl, NO JOHNNY KYLER COUNTERS WITH
A HEADSCISSORS! What speed and agility by this young superstar.
Matt Chapen: This is one fast paced matchup and the fans are eating it up! Jeff Kane tries to sneak behind Kyler but gets a kick to the midsection for his troubles! Kyler puts Kane up on top, but here comes A.J. Darkk! TOP ROPE
HURRICANRANA ON JEFF KANE! A.J. Darkk just sprung off Johnny Kyler's shoulders to execute that maneuver. That was incredible! DARKK WITH THE COVER!
One... Two... Kickout! Chris Poleski: Johnny Kyler up on top and connects with a missle dropkick on
Darkk. KYLER WITH THE COVER!
One... Two... Shoulder Up! Johnny Kyler whips A.J. Darkk into the ropes and nails him with an awesome tilt-a-wirl backbrekaer! JEFF KANE WITH A DROPKICK TO THE KNEE ON
KYLER! Nicely done!
Matt Chapen: Indeed. Jeff Kane is that kind of wrestler that chooses his spots very carefully. Kyler ducks the spinkick, Kane ducks the clotheseline.
JEFF KANE WITH A VICIOUS SUPERKICK TO THE FACE ON DARKK! He went to hit Kyler but Johnny ducked just in the nick of time.
One... Two... JOHNNY KYLER BREAKS IT UP! Johnny Kyler picks up Darkk and spikes him with a piledriver! One... Two... Thr...JEFF KANE BREAKS IT UP WITH A DROPKICK TO THE FACE! Chris Poleski: OUCH! He throws Johnny Kyler up and over the top. He picks up
A.J. Darkk AND DRILLS HIM WITH A BRAINBUSTER! He makes the cover!
One... Two... Three!!! Rick Collings: A.J. Darkk HAS BEEN ELIMINATED! Matt Chapen: Now it comes down to Johnny Kyler and Jeff Kane! Kyler comes back in the ring but gets a stiff kick to the ribs! Jeff Kane goes for a spinkick but Johhny ducks it. GERMAN SUPLEX RIGHT ON HIS HEAD!
One... Two... Thr...Kickout! Johnny Kyler saying its over. He picks him up in a tombstone, but KANE REVERSES IT!
Chris Poleski: He just got nailed! Jeff Kane on second turnbuckle. 2ND ROPE
MOONSAULT MISSES! Johnny Kyler with a small package. HE HAS THE TIGHTS!
One... Two... Three!!! Rick Cillings: Jeff Kane HAS BEEN ELIMINATED! Therefore your winner of the match...JOHNNY KYLER!!!
{"Creep" by Radiohead plays as Johnny Kyler celebrates his victory. He rolls out of the ring as Jeff Kane looks pissed off.}
Matt Chapen: Well Johnny Kyler with the win here on Tradition. A tainted win but a win nonetheless.
Chris Poleski: What do you mean a tainted win? He beat him fair and square! Matt Chapen: He pulled the tights! Look from the right, ITS RICK TAYLOR! Taylor slides into the ring and lands a right hand on Jeff Kane! Darkk with a left hand. Both men just trading blows. Jeff Kane whips Taylor into the ropes
BUT GETS NAILED WITH A FLYING FOREARM AND ROLLS TO THE OUTSIDE! Kane trying to collect himself. Taylor runs the opposite ropes and goes flying off WITH A
SOMERSAULT PLANCHA! Chris Poleski: What is the meaning of this? A.J. DARKK IS RUNNING TO THE
RING! Kane and Taylor duking it out on the outside. A.J. Darkk is on the apron, ASAI MOONSAULT ON THE BOTH OF THEM! Ace Firesie coming from the other side! He turns around A.J. Darkk and kicks him in the midsection, FIRESIDE
BOMB! FIRESIDE BOMB ON THE OUTSIDE! Matt Chapen: This is insane! Ace Fireside is the last man on his feet and what a way to start off Tradition!
