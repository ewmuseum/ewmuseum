CWA Tradition Part 5

CWA North American Title match! Adam Mitchell vs Shane Russell Despite both men being heels, Adam Mitchell was the favorite in this matchup. Adam Mitchell came in with a house of fire and scored many near falls in the opening minutes of the contest. Shane Russell gained control of the matchup when Mitchell went for a clothesline but the champion ducked and nailed the challenger with a vicious lariet! Shane Russell went for the Lockout, but Adam
Mitchell quickly grabbed the ropes. Shane Russell went up on top but Adam
Mitchell countered with a dropkick to the midsection for a two count. Russell countered a bodyslam into a small package for the two count! To the disapproval of the crowd, Nighthawk made his way to the ring. Adam Mitchell countered a short arm clothesline by ducking the move and hitting the Marskman!
He went for the cover and just had the three count before Nighthawk pulled the referee out of the ring! He is arguing with the ref as Mitchell pulled
Nighthawk up on the apron! Shane Russell from behind but Mitchell ducks and
Russell nails Nighthawk sending him to the floor! Adam Mitchell with a school boy for the two count! Adam Mitchell ran into the ropes and got a forearm to the head by Nighthawk who got back to his feet! Mitchell fell to the ground as
Shane Russell applied the Lockdown! With nowhere to go Adam Mitchell tapped out!
Winner: Shane Russell
