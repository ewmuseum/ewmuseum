CWA (Continental, 2000) — Card

CWA Tradition Part 5

11 May 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Matt Chapen: Welcome back fans and... {"King Nothing" by Metallica plays as Jon Massey comes to the ring with a serious look on his face.}
Chris Poleski: What is the meaning of this? Matt Chapen: I don't know! Let's find out, Jon Massey is in the ring! Jon Massey: I came out here for one reason and one reason only! I have a certain matter to discuss with one THP! So I know you can hear me so come out here!
Chris Poleski: He is calling out his friend The Hottest Prospect! Matt Chapen: I don't know what to think of this! Massey's tone of voice seemed like something was wrong!
{"King Nothing" by Metallica plays as THP and Frat Boy Perry Tanhammer come to the ring as the fans boo them like there is no tomorrow.}
Chris Poleski: Looks like we got company! Matt Chapen: He only called out THP, why is Tanhammer here! Jon Massey: Hello, Perry, THP! This is just between me and THP so stay out of this Tanhammer! THP, you were banned from that world title match between Kevin
Candido and the World Champion Perry Tanhammer! You were instructed to stay away but you didn't!
THP: Whoa there Jon, hold the phone! I was instructed by Taylor to stay away from the ring and NOT the match which made it perfectly legal! So why I am out here talking to people who have been foolish, let me discuss one Kevin Candido!
{The fans cheer at mention of his name.} THP: Perry and I proved that each and everyone of you are just like Kevin
Candido, a bunch of gulliable, vulnerable fools! But Candido, here is something that is no joke, if you have the balls why don't you and I have a little falls count anywhere match at Price of Glory May 27th?
Matt Chapen: THP just challenged Kevin Candido to a falls count anywhere match! Frat Boy: Oh and Candido, you can get any partner you oh so desire because just like everybody else in the CWA they will go down!
THP: And now we come to my buddy Jon Massey. {THP and Frat Boy Perry Tanhammer look at Jon Massey. He has an uneasyness in his eyes.} THP: Jon, I never needed you before in my career and sure as hell don't need you know you fucking prick! All you ever did was ride on my wave of success!
Well I ain't gonna take it no more Massey! {THP drops the mic and kicks Jon Massey in the midsection and drops him with the Jacknife Powerbomb. Frat Boy Perry Tanhammer applauds.}
Matt Chapen: WHAT?! WHAT DID HE DO THAT FOR? Chris Poleski: Because he was a pest thats why! Matt Chapen: THIS IS NOT RIGHT! Tanhammer picks the unconscious Jon Massey up! No he wouldn't! He has him set up! HE NAILED HIM WITH THE HANGOVER!
Both men stomping away at Jon Massey THP's former friend! This was a setup! {"Battleflag" by Lo-Fidelity Allstars plays as Kevin Candido walks onto the ramp. He has a mic in hand.}
Chris Poleski: THAT'S KEVIN CANDIDO! WHAT THE HELL DOES HE WANT? Kevin Candido: THP, as for your falls count anywhere match at the PPV, you got it! Oh and this little beat-down of Jon Massey, yeah real classy! I just got through with talking to Michael D. Taylor and becasue of your actions he has ordered for this tag team match to have the title on the line! Oh and Perry,
I'd like you to meet your opponent for Price of Glory and my tag team partner!
{"One Step Closer" by Linkon Park plays as Chainsaw Charlie stands side by side with Kevin Candido. Perry Tanhammer is going nuts. THP is trying to calm him down a bit.}
Matt Chapen: ITS CHAINSAW CHARLIE! He is teaming up with Candido tonight with the title on the line! If Frat Boy still has the belt, he must defend it against the Hardcore Icon in 14 days! This is unreal! Fans we will be back!

— Part 5 of 8 —
