CWA (Continental, 2000) — Card

CWA Tradition Part 5

12 August 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Matt Chapen: Welcome back fans and so much has transpired tonight and it looks to be only getting crazier as each match passes by!
Chris Poleski: You are telling me! I mean in a few minutes THP is gonna tear apart Jason Devlin!
Rick Collings: This contest is schedueled for one fall with a 30 minute time limit! First coming down the aisle...THP, THE HOTTEST PROSPECT!!!
{"King Nothing" by Metallica plays as THP makes his way to the ring to a mixed reaction from the fans.}
Matt Chapen: At Blood, Sweat and Tears he beat his rival Kevin Candido in a great matchup. He is more vicious and more bitter ever since losing to Joe
Snow so Devlin must be ready to dish it out!
Rick Collings: And his opponent...JASON DEVLIN!!! {"Highway To Hell" by AC/DC plays as Jason Devlin comes to the ring as the fans cheer him on.}
Matt Chapen: THP with a kick to the midsection. Punch to the back of the head of Devlin followed up by a punch to the face! THP wasting no time in inflicting as much pain on Jason Devlin as possible! THP whips him into the corner AND NAILS DEVLIN WITH A BODY SPLASH!
Chris Poleski: Jason Devlin is getting his ass handed to him on a silver platter Matt just like I thought he would! THP whips him to the other side, but Devlin reverses it. THP jumps on the second turnbuckle and comes off with an elbowsmash! What power and agility being showcased by the monster
THP! Matt Chapen: He hasn't been this vicious in a long long time Chris! THP runs at Jason Devlin, but Devlin back body drops him AND THP LANDS ON HIS
FEET! Devlin with a vicious right hand that makes the Hottest Prospect a little sluggish! Devlin off the ropes AND HITS A SPRINGBOARD CLOTHESLINE ON
THP! THP drops to the outside!
Chris Poleski: The best thing for Jason Devlin to do is get the hell out of there because once THP gets his hands on him he will be a dead man! Jason
Devlin climbing up on top here. He is mantaining his balance, AND FLIES OFF
WITH A HIGH CROSS BODY ON THE BIG MAN ON THE OUTSIDE! Matt Chapen: I bet THP wasn't expecting this from Jason Devlin one bit! The minute you underestimate your opponent is the minute that can cost you the matchup in a matter of three seconds! Devlin whips THP into the guardrail,
BUT THP REVERSES IT AND DEVLIN HITS THE STEEL! Chris Poleski: He gave the man too much time to recover and it cost him there! THP with a right cross on Jason Devlin! THP throwing Devlin back into the ring. THP knows that he must beat the man inside of the ring and not on the outside. THP whips Jason Devlin into the ropes, BUT DEVLIN
REVERSES IT! Jason Devlin jumps at him BUT THP CAUGHT HIM! BIG SPINEBUSTER SLAM! Matt Chapen: Any momentum that was building in Devlin's favor was squashed with that spinebuster slam! THP is a very smart wrestler, he picks his spots carefully. THP going to the outside! He throws Rick Collings out of the way! Hey what did he ever do to you THP? HE GRABS THE STEEL CHAIR!
Chris Poleski: HA HA! We can all say goodbye to Jason Devlin! He slides into the ring with the chair just as Jason Devlin is slowing getting to his feet!
{CRACK!!!} Matt Chapen: THP just walloped him with that steel chair! One... Two... Thr...Devlin's foot is on the rope! THP showing a little sign of fustration there. He thought he had him beat! THP runs into the ropes with that steel chair, he swings, but Devlin ducks! Devlin with a kick to the midsection causing the chair to be dropped!
Chris Poleski: JASON DEVLIN HAS A HOLD OF THAT STEEL CHAIR! {CRACK!!!} Chris Poleski: Jason Devlin just nailed THP with that steel chair! THP IS
STILL STANDING! THP just barely holding on. Jason Devlin throws the chair to THP!
{CRACK!!!} Matt Chapen: DEVLIN JUST DROPKICKED THAT CHAIR IN THE FACE OF THP! One... Two... Thr...Kickout! Devlin puts the chair on the face of THP. Devlin ascending to the top! {SMACK!!!} Matt Chapen: FLYING ELBOW OFF THE TOP ROPE ONTO THAT STEEL CHAIR! Devlin with the cover! One... Two... Thr...Shoudler Up! Chris Poleski: YES! Keep on fighting THP! You can't lose this one buddy! Devlin throws the chair out of the ring. Jason Devlin going up on top again and is waiting for THP!
Matt Chapen: What is he doing! THP trying to shake off those cobwebs! He makes his way over to Jason Devlin, AND DEVLIN GRABS THE HEAD! Tornado DDT,NO!
THP GRABS THE LEGS OF DEVLIN! POWERBOMB! Jason Devlin almost hits the tornado DDT, but THP counters with a variation of the powerbomb! He should finish him off here!
Chris Poleski: He ain't done with him yet Chapen! THP picking up Jason Devlin, he has him between his legs. POWERBOMB! THP DROPPED HIM WITH THE
JACKNIFE! Now its over Chapen! THP with the cover!
One... Two... Three!!! Rick Collings: Here is your winner...THP! {"King Nothing" by Metallica plays as THP celebrates his victory and looks over
Jason Devlin who is knocked out cold.}
Matt Chapen: THP got the win and a good one. Jason Devlin gave him a good fight, but THP got the best of him!
Chris Poleski: Someone is coming from the aisleway. ITS JOE SNOW AND PERRY
TANHAMMER! Yes get him boys!
Matt Chapen: NO! From behind Chad Yaro tackled the CWA World Champion to the ground! Tanhammer doesn't even know it! Frat Boy in the ring and is looking at THP! He turns around to see Joe Snow getting beat on by Chad Yaro! THP is loving it and Perry Tanhammer is in shock!
Chris Poleski: This is not fair Chapen not at all! Frat Boy runs at THP but gets a boot to the face for his troubles! Tanhammer gets back up but gets nailed with a clothesline knocking him out of his boots!
Matt Chapen: THP is setting up for it! He is looking for that Jacknife! THP picks him up off the ground BUT TANHAMMER NAILS HIM WITH A LOW BLOW! THP falls to the mat in agony! Chad Yaro has left to the back and Snow is out. Perry
Tanhammer slides out of the ring like a theif in the night. Tanhammer helps
Joe Snow to his feet and they retreat to the back before anymore damage can be done!
