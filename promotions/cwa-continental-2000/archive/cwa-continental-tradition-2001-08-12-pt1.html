CWA (Continental, 2000) — Card

CWA Tradition Part 1

12 August 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
{"I Can't Drive 55" by Sammy Hagar plays as the camera shows the jam packed CWA crowd in the Gund Arena. The camera then shows Matt Chapen and Chris Poleski.}
Matt Chapen: Hello CWA fans and WELCOME to CWA Tradition! We are live at the
Gund Arena in Cleaveland Ohio and getting ready for an action packed...
{"King Nothing" by Metallica plays as THP comes to the ring to a mixed reaction from the crowd with some booing him while others cheering him on.}
Chris Poleski: Looks like THP decided to make an appearance before his match tonight! Matt Chapen: We were to kick things off with Street Punk vs The Assasin but that will be later on as THP has a lot on his mind I am sure!
THP: FRAT BOY...GET YOUR ASS OUT HERE RIGHT NOW! {The crowd goes nuts knowing that hell is gonna break lose.} Chris Poleski: THP just called out Frat Boy! {"Mr. Speed" by KISS plays as Frat Boy Perry Tanhammer walks to the ring as the fans boo him like mad.}
Matt Chapen: Frat Boy is in the ring! This could get out of hand for sure! Frat Boy Perry Tanhammer: I'm here THP what do you want? THP: You damn know why I called you out! You made one pretty stupid mistake by turning on me! NOBODY FUCKS WITH THP! I should have gone on to face
Chainsaw Charlie in that death cell and beat him for it! But it didn't come out that way now did it Perry? Oh no, you had to be some cocky little shit!
Because of you Frat Boy I got pinned by that no good son of a bitch Joe Snow! Joe Snow being the world champion just boggles my mind! He's just lucky that he ain't facing me yet!
{"Bodies" by Drowning Pool plays as Joe Snow stands on the rampway with the CWA
World Title drapped around his shoulder and a mic in his other hand.}
Chris Poleski: The CWA World Champion is in the house! Joe Snow: THP, did I just hear you say it boggles your mind about me being the
CWA World Champion?
THP: Yeah asshole! Joe Snow: Now THP, don't be going crazy over this situation with me being the
World Champion, because being the man that I am, I'm gonna make it crystal clear to you and the entire world why I stand as your CWA World Champion!
Matt Chapen: What in the hell is he talking about? Chris Poleski: I don't know but I am listening! {Joe Snow starts walking towards the ring.} Joe Snow: THP how can you not know something that is right under your nose? The reason I am the CWA World Champion is because OF THAT MAN RIGHT THERE! {Joe Snow points to Frat Boy Perry Tanhammer in the middle of the ring as the fans explode in a chorus of boos.}
Matt Chapen: I SHOULD HAVE KNOWN FRAT BOY WAS BEHIND THIS! Chris Poleski: This is great! THP is pissed off! Joe Snow: But wait thats not all folks! As you all know at Blood Sweat and
Tears, there was a second hooded man who assisted me in my title victory and quite honestly I want to know who it is! I am sure Frat Boy want to know who it is right Perry?
{Frat Boy Perry Tanhammer nods his head and smiles.} Joe Snow: And I am sure all these toothless hicks in Cleaveland Ohio want to know who the hooded man is!
{As he finishes, Larry Gionet climbs through the crowd.} Chris Poleski: THAT'S LARRY GIONET! Matt Chapen: He is the third man! Larry Gionet is behind THP on the apron! THP doesn't see Gionet. Chris Poleski: BUT CHAINSAW CHARLIE DOES! Matt Chapen: Where did he come from?! Gionet turns around AND GETS NAILED
WITH THE CHAINSAW CUTTER ONTO THE FLOOR! The crowd is going crazy! THP turns around to see Gionet on the floor unconscious. He turns around AND JOE SNOW
CHARGES THE RING! Frat Boy runs at THP BUT GETS A BIG BOOT FOR HIS TROUBLES!
Joe Snow getting behind THP! {SMACK!!!} Chris Poleski: JOE SNOW JUST NAILED THP WITH THAT WORLD TITLE BELT! Joe Snow is stomping away at THP, BUT HERE COMES CHAINSAW CHARLIE INTO THE RING! HE HAS
THE CHAINSAW! Matt Chapen: FRAT BOY AND JOE SNOW FLY OUT OF THE RING! The were a few seconds away from getting sliced with that chainsaw! THP is slowly getting to his feet and Frat Boy as well as Joe Snow are helping Larry Gionet up the ramp from getting that Chainsaw Cutter off the apron onto the floor!
{Chainsaw Charlie turns towards THP who is up with the diamond-tipped chainsaw.} Chainsaw Charlie: You...I still don't trust you! If you want a piece of that no good backstabbing maggot over there you are more than welcome to it! BUT if you get in MY way, you my friend are GURANTEED...TO...BLEED!
{"One Step Closer" by Linkin Park plays as Chainsaw Charlie leaves the ring to a round of cheers from the crowd. THP looking at Chainsaw Charlie as he leaves the ringside area.}
Chris Poleski: Oh man! This is totally insane! Matt Chapen: What a way to kick off Traditon! We have to take a commerical break!

— Part 1 of 9 —
