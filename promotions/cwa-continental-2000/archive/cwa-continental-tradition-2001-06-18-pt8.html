CWA (Continental, 2000) — Card

CWA Tradition Part 8

18 June 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Matt Chapen: We have returned and lets get this main event underway shall we! {"Mr. Speed" by KISS plays as Frat Boy Perry Tanhammer comes walking to the ring as the fans boo him like there is no tomorrow.}
Chris Poleski: Awesome, we have some company! Matt Chapen: Looks like Perry Tanhammer has decided to join us for commentary. Frat Boy Perry Tanhammer: That's right Chapen, the real world champion, has decided to come down here and give my insights on this matchup at hand!
Rick Collings: This contest is schedueled for one fall with a 30 minute time limit! First coming down the aisle...THE HOTTEST PROSPECT!!!
{"King Nothing" by Metallica plays as THP comes to the ring as he gets a round of boos from the crowd.}
Frat Boy Perry Tanhammer: There's my pal there THP! Him and I go way back. Joe Snow is nothing but some little peon that is trying to rain on our parade of domination!
Matt Chapen: Yeah and I am sure you will not get involved right! Frat Boy Perry Tanhammer: Of course not! I am here to watch a good matchup and I will not get in that ring during the match!
Chris Poleski: See Chapen, Perry Tanhammer is a man of integrity and honor! This man is presidential material! You'd have my vote Perry! Frat Boy Perry Tanhammer: Thank you for those kind words Poleski. You should be nice to your collegue Mr. Chapen!
Matt Poleski: Oh brother! Rick Collings: And his opponent...JOE SNOW!!! {"Break Stuff" by Limp Bizkit plays as Joe Snow comes to the ring with his signapore cane in hand as the fans cheer him on. As he enters the ring, he points to the people who are cheering him.}
Matt Chapen: They lock up. The Hottest Prospect hits a stomachbreaker on Joe
Snow. THP WITH A GORILLA PRESS ON SNOW!
Frat Boy Perry Tanhammer: Look at the power of this man! You just can't beat that! One... Two... Kickout! The Hottest Prospect goes for the body avalanche, but Joe Snow counters it with an elbowsmash! Joe Snow throws The Hottest Prospect out of the ring. Joe Snow whips The Hottest Prospect into the guardrail. JOE SNOW
GRABBING A CHAIR! Joe Snow sets up the chair. DDT ONTO THE STEEL CHAIR!
Frat Boy Perry Tanhammer: What the hell! That's a fucking disqualification ref! Chris Poleski: I know right! They're brawling inside the ring area. THP has a steel chair!
{CRACK!!!} Matt Chapen: Hey that should be a disqualification there! Frat Boy Perry Tanhammer: What are you talking about Chapen? I didn't see anything! Chris Poleski: Me neither! Matt chapen: The Hottest Prospect goes for a high knee, but Joe Snow ducks out of the way. Joe Snow whips The Hottest Prospect into the guardrail. Joe Snow throws The Hottest Prospect back into the ring. Joe Snow goes for a vertical suplex, but The Hottest Prospect blocks it. THP WITH A FALLAWAY SLAM! THP just toying with Joe Snow!
One... Two... Shoulder Up! Chris Poleski: Joe Snow hits The Hottest Prospect with a dropkick! SNOW WITH
ANOTHER ONE! Joe Snow leaves the ring AND HAS A CHAIR! THP IS DOWN! Joe Snow runs into the ropes and springs off the chair. SNOW WITH A FROG SPLASH ON THE
BIG MAN! GET UP! One... Two... Shoulder Up! Frat Boy Perry Tanhammer: What is this referee blind? He is letting Joe Snow use chairs! Chris Poleski: Where does Taylor get these refs anyways! Joe Snow whips The
Hottest Prospect into the ropes. The Hottest Prospect goes for a Gorilla
Press, but SNOW SLIDES DOWN HIS BACK!
Matt Chapen: Joe Snow climbs to the top turnbuckle, but The Hottest Prospect nails him in the stomach. Joe Snow falls onto the top turnbuckle and into the ring! THP coming to the outside!
Frat Boy Perry Tanhammer: Here you go pal! Matt Chapen: You said you were not gonna get involved! Frat Boy Perry Tanhammer: I said I was never gonna get in that ring during this match! See thats a big difference! Haha!
Chris Poleski: THP swings at Snow with the chair but he ducks! Snow goes for a clothesline, BUT THP DUCKS OUT OF THE WAY! Tanhammer is distracting the referee from the apron!
{CRACK!!!} Matt Chapen: THP just nailed Snow with that steel chair and the referee didn't see it! Don't let it end like this damnit!
One... Two... Thr...KICKOUT! BY GOD HE KICKED OUT! THP whips Joe Snow into the ropes AND
HAS HIM IN A BEARHUG! THP squeezing the life out of Joe Snow!The Hottest
Frat Boy Perry Tanhammer: Come on you son of a bitch TAP OUT!
Chris Poleski: Snow just broke that hold with a facerake! STREET PUNK IS MAKING HIS WAY TO THE RING WITH THAT CANE! Frat Boy Perry Tanhammer: Wait a minute! What is he doing here? He is not supposed to be here!
Matt Chapen: Neither are you! Chris Poleski: Shut up before he punks you out Chapen! Matt Chapen: The Hottest Prospect whips Joe Snow into the turnbuckle. The
Hottest Prospect charges into the corner, but Joe Snow lifts his leg. He runs at THP, BUT HE NAILS HIM WITH A CLTOHESLINE! THP picks him up FOR THE
POWREBOMB! NO JOE SNOW IS PUNCHING HIS WAY OUT OF IT AND FALLS ON TOP OF HIM! DOUBLE LEG HOOK! One... Two... Three!!! Rick Collings: Here is your winner...JOE SNOW!!! Chris Poleski: WHAT?! {"Break Stuff" by Limp Bizkit plays as Joe Snow has his hand raised in victory as the fans are going nuts!}
Matt Chapen: JOE SNOW WITH THE UPSET VICTORY! THP can't believe it! Chris Poleski: I can't either! Frat Boy Perry Tanhammer: That's it I gotta take care of some business! {Frat Boy Perry Tanhammer throws his headset down and climbs into the ring.}
Matt Chapen: THP is livid! HE JUST NAILED JOE SNOW FROM BEHIND! AND HERE
COMES PERRY TANHAMMER! ITS TWO ON ONE! Both men stomping away at him! BUT
HERE COMES STREET PUNK WITH THE CANE! {CRACK!!!} Chris Poleski: Street Punk just nailed Frat Boy with that cane BUT GOT SPEARED BY THP! JOE SNOW COMING BACK TO HIS FEET! Matt Chapen: Joe Snow with a dropkick on Tanhammer! DROPKICK ON THP! Joe
Snow WHIPS FRAT BOY INTO THP AND BOTH GO DOWN! Larry Gionet has made his way to the ring. Larry Gionet is up on the top rope. Snow goes to face him AND
GETS NAILED WITH A FLYING CLOTHESLINE BY THE LIVING LEGEND! Chris Poleski: There ya go! Larry Gionet looking to the crowd AND HE SEES
CANDIDO! GIONET IS RUNNING INTO THE CROWD! THESE FANS ARE GOING NUTS! THP has Snow again! He is gonna nail him with the Powerbomb! Street Punk is starting to come to. THP HAS HIM UP!
{CRACK!!!} Matt Chapen: Street Punk just nailed THP with that cane! THP rolls to the outside. FRAT BOY THREW HIM TO THE OUTSIDE! Frat Boy looking for something.
HE HAS THE STEEL CHAIR! Joe Snow goes to swing at Tanhammer but Tanhammer ducks! {SMACK!!!} Chris Poleski: HOME RUN! Frat Boy Perry Tanhammer is the only one left in the ring! {"One Step Closer" by Linkin Park plays as Chainsaw Charlie runs to the ring as the fans go crazy. Frat Boy Perry Tanhammer is waiting for him.}
Matt Chapen: HERE COMES THE WORLD CHAMPION! Everyone is out and the only two men left are Frat Boy and Chainsaw Charlie! Both men are unloading with a flury of fists on each other! But here come the officials! I don't think a ring can contain these two explosive forces! Who will be the last man standing at the PPV? Will the reign of Chainsaw Charlie end or will the journey end for
Frat Boy Perry Tanhammer? The only way to find out is watch Journey's End June
24th live on PPV! For Todd Michaelson, Rick Collings and Chris Poleski, this is Matt Chapen saying good night everyone!
