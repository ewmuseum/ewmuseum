CWA (Continental, 2000) — Card

CWA Price of Glory Part 1

3 May 2000
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
{"Blaze of Glory" by Bon Jovi BLASTS as the camera zooms in a the crowd in attendence! You can hear the fans cheering in anticipation for tonight's PPV!
You can see some signs saying "SNOW + CHARLIE=HARDCORE ICONS!", "WWF FEARS
CWA!', and "KURT N. JERKER IS MY HERO!" The camera zooms in on Matt Chapen and
Chris Poleski.}
Matt Chapen: CWA fans, WELCOME TO CWA PRICE OF GLORY! This is gonna be quite an event! The tag team titles will be decided as Attitude Adjustment face The
Ultimate Backfield as well as The Untoucables!
Chris Poleski: We will also see Gary Pate and Street Punk finally get some revenge against Chaisaw Charlie and Joe Snow in a double ladders match! Larry
Gionet will challenge Assasin for the North American Championship!
Matt Chapen: Yes and who can forget the main event! What has been billed as a
Candido's Carnage Carnival! Three matches stretched out and by the end of the night, either Kevin Candido or Mighty Ryan Kelly will walk out as the CWA World
Champion! Todd Michaelson is in the ring right now!
Todd Michaelson- Ladies and Gentlemen. It is now time to bring out my guests. At CWA Price of Glory they will be taking on Chainsaw Charlie and Joe Snow in a
Double Ladders Matchup...
{Todd Michaelson is cut off as "Revolution Is My Name" by Pantera starts up as everyone in the arena starts booing immediately. "Twisted" Gary Pate emerges from the curtains wearing a black t-shirt, camouflage pants, and black combat boots. He holds his arm up for the crowd, who responds with a wave of jeers. He looks back at the curtains as the crowd quiets down again. When they are just about silent, The Blood Angel himself steps out of the curtains, holding his singapore cane in the air. The shit really hits the fan this time. He is wearing his new Street Punk 4:20 'I'm Gonna Cane Your Ass' t-shirt. (Available for $19.95 in Medium, Large, or XL) Black cut-off shorts, and some black and blue Airwalk sneakers(apparently for sneaking). They hop in the ring and hold their arms in the air once again, only to recieve many boos and a little thrown garbage. But they don't seem to care, or notice.}
Todd Michaelson- Hello gentlemen.
Street Punk- What's up man. Todd Michaelson- Tonight you two take on Chainsaw Charlie and Joe Snow in a
Double Ladders matchup.
Gary Pate- Yeah we sure will. Todd Michaelson- Um... Do you have any thoughts on that matchup? Street Punk- Thoughts? What's there to think about. When me and Gary get our hands on this singapore cane or Charlie's precious chainsaw, things are gonna get bad.
Todd Michaelson- But what if they get a hold of either of those weapons. Gary Pate- Don't be stupid, Michaelson. The Blood Angel and The Twisted One are the most hardcore men CWA has to offer. Yeah we put some new guys over last week, but that means nothing. The bottom line is, tonight, Chainsaw Charlie and
Joe Snow are gonna bleed like never before.
Todd Michaelson- Yes but.. Street Punk- Uh-Uh... Like never before. {Todd shakes his head} Todd Michaelson- So guys, do you care to make any predictions for the matches at Price of Glory.
Street Punk- Yeah I think we're supposed to do that anyway. Todd Michaelson- Well Ian Walsh is taking on Jay Moxely. But who are newcomers. Gary Pate- H'm.. It's a tough call. I say someone will win by countout. Todd Michaelson- Why do you say that? Gary Pate- Because whoever is knocked down first will probably run out of the ring crying. {Street Punk and Gary Pate slap hands and both start laughing.} Todd Michaelson- How can you say that? Both men could be big stars in CWA. What kind of prediction is that?
Street Punk- A good one. And plus, we can make or break those guys. We put them over last week, there's your one break. But I won't be so nice of a fella next time.
Todd Michaelson- Well. Gargoyle is taking on Tony Starks. Street Punk- I say Stevie Kane runs his gimp ass down to the ring. Todd Michaelson- So are you saying Gargoyle will win or there will be a DQ? Street Punk- I say Tony Starks wins. Todd Michaelson- What? It would be two on one of Stevie Kane came down to the ring, and Gargoyle is plenty mad already.
Street Punk- Yeah. But Gargoyle and Stevie Kane would probably start hugging, only to be laid out by Tony Starks.
Gary Pate- Man that would be funny. Street Punk- (Laugh) Yeah it would. Todd Michaelson- I guess I don't need to ask you about your match. Street Punk- But while we're on the subject, what are we doing following Gargoyle and Tony Starks? We should be in the main event, or atleast right before. Gary Pate- You know why we're mid-card? Street Punk- Why? Gary Pate- Because damn Charlie and Joe Snow are holding us back. Street Punk- Your right man. We need some REAL competition. Todd Michaelson- I'm sure that's why... Street Punk- So what else in on that card Michaelson? Todd Michaelson- Well. There's a three way tag team matchup for the CWA Tag
Team Titles. Street Punk- Ah yes. Gary, what do you think? Untouchables? Gary Pate- Yeah. Street Punk- Yeah, we have to go with the Untouchables. They are pretty impressive. I mean, Attitude Adjustment is one of the best around, but their just not making that big of a mark in CWA, like they have done other places.
Todd Michaelson- I can't believe you said that. Street Punk- Wha? Todd Michaelson- That was the most non-negative thing you've ever said, this interview is over, let's leave on this note.
{Todd Michaelson slides out of the ring shaking his head in disbelief as Street
Punk and Gary Pate watch on with wondering eyes. They finally leave as
"Revolution Is My Name" starts up again.} Chris Poleski: You see that Matt, those are very determined men! They are gonna go to the top!
Matt Chapen: Um, yeah whatever you say pal! Lets get to our first matchup! Its a grudge match featuring Gargoyle and Tony Aftermath Starks! Rick Collings: This contest is scheduled for one fall with a 30 minute time limit! First coming down the aisle, being accompanied to the ring by the CWA
Hardcore Champion, Mark Blaze...TONY "AFTERMATH" STARKS!!!
{"Bring The Pain" by Method Man plays as Tony Aftermath Starks and Mark Blaze come to the ring to a round of boos from the crowd.}
Chris Poleski: Tony Starks looks pretty intimidating and ready to take down
Gargoyle! He is undefeated ya know!
Rick Collings: And his opponent...GARGOYLE! Matt Chapen: Aftermath whips Gagoyle into the ropes, but Gagoyle reverses it, AND HITS STARKS WITH AN ELBOW! One... Two... Kickout! Gargoyle runs into the ropes. Aftermath misses with a kick AND GETS
NAILED WITH A VICIOUS LARIET! That almost decapitated Starks!
One... Two... Shoulder up! Chris Poleski: Aftermath executes a forearm to the back on Gagoyle! TONY
STARKS DELIVERS A RIGHT HAND TO GARGOYLE! Starks runs the ropes, and comes back WITH A FLYING ELBOWSMASH! What agility by the big man! That is what
Starks must do! He must keep the momentum in his favor as well as keep his winning streak alive here in the CWA! Aftermath goes for a chokehold, but
Gagoyle blocks it! HE THROWS AFTERMATH OUT OF THE RING! Gargoyle goes for a choke slam, but Aftermath blocks it AND TAKES HIM DOWN WITH A DDT ON THE
CONCRETE! Matt Chapen: TONY STARKS KNOCKS GARGOYLE INTO THE RINGSTEPS! Tony Starks is keeping this man grounded so he cannot come back! I have never seen Aftermath
so aggressive in my life! Aftermath throws Gagoyle back into the ring! Aftermath uses a series of punches on Gargoyle! Listen to those stiff shots by
Tony Aftermath Starks! Aftermath whips Gargoyle into the ropes, BUT GARGOYLE
REVERSES IT! Gagoyle misses with a shoulderblock and Aftermath hits him with a clothesline!
Chris Poleski: Aftermath goes for a roundhouse right, but Gagoyle blocks it! DEATH DIVE! COVER! COME ON TONY GET UP! One... Two... Three!!! Rick Collings: Here is your winner...GARGOYLE!!! Matt Chapen: That was a very quick match in which Gargoyle took the win and destroyed Stark's winning streak! Stevie Kane is celebrating in the ring with
Gargoyle, BUT HERE COMES MARK BLAZE! KANE AND BLAZE ARE POUNDING AWAY AT EACH
OTHER! STARKS HAS GARGOYLE BY THE THROAT! NO NO! Chris Poleski: CHOKESLAM THROUGH THE TABLE BY STARKS! YEAH! THAT'S WHAT HE
GETS! AFTERMATH GRABS STEVIE KANE AND IS CHOKING THE LIFE OUT OF HIM!
{The lights in the arena go pitch dark as "Soul Bossa Nova" plays. The lights flicker back on as the mystery man slowly comes to the ring with a brown hooded robe on.}
Matt Chapen: IT'S THE MYSTERY MAN! LOOK AT BLAZE'S FACE! HE DOESN'T KNOW WHAT TO THINK! Chris Poleski: He is in the ring! He takes his hood off, AND MARK BLAZE LOOKS
LIKE HE SAW A GHOST! HE AND AFTERMATH ARE HEADING FOR HIGH GROUND!
Matt Chapen: WHAT A LOAD OF COWARDS! Mystery man: What's the matter, YOU FEAR OTTO? YOU FEAR THE UNKNOWN! Chris Poleski: SOMEONE IS IN THE RING! Kick to the gut, DDT ON OTTO! He has grabbed a mic!
Mystery Man: Hey Otto, Ashes to Ashes, Dust to dust, is there a soul I can trust? {Azmyth drops the mic and leaves through the crowd.} Matt Chapen: Who in the hell was that? This man by the name of Azmyth just drilled Otto with a DDT! This makes no sense at all!
Chris Poleski: Nothing makes sense anymore! This is the CWA we are talking about here!
