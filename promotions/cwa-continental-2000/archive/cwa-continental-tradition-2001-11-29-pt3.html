CWA Tradition Part 3

29 November 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
{Just as Mr World is about to leave the locker room for his match, Chainsaw
Charlie walks into the room. In the background you can hear a "Charlie!
Charlie!" chant as the extreme icon puts an arm up against a locker, blocking Mr World's path.}
Charlie: Off so soon? Mr World: You? What do YOU want? Charlie: Just to give YOU some advice. Mr World: How could you possibly give ME some advice? I AM Mr World after all! I know everything, I AM everything. What can YOU offer?
Charlie: Well, its so simple even YOU would understand it. Be careful out there... ANYTHING >could< HAPPEN!
Mr World: Ha! Oh >I< get it! You're upset because I'VE got a match and YOU have NOT! That's what it's all about isn't it!
Charlie: Oh I have more! You're going up against Joe Snow. That's right Joe
Snow! Mr Joe Singapore-wrapped in Barbed Wire-can Snow! Ex CWA World Champ!
Ex-Chainsaw Charlie tag team partner! Ex back-stabbing, two-timing, sewer-dwelling, under-handed, lacks-morales-and-testicular-fortitude, CWA superstar!
But still he is Joe Snow! Mr World: I KNOW! [Mr World pushes past Charlie and walks out as Charlie stares at him...] Charlie: Can't say I didn't warn the World!!! Joe Snow is walking out of his locker room but Agent Rodney Wright is blocking his way. Joe Snow is getting irritate with him telling him to get out of his way. Rodney Wright knees him in the gut and rams his head into the locker!
Agent Rodney Wright smiles as Joe Snow lays unconscious on the ground. Mr. World walks around the corner and sees Joe Snow knocked out. He smiles at
Rodney Wright as he is walking back to his locker room. He turns around only to have Chainsaw Charlie attack him from behind! He is pounding away at Mr.
World and finishes his assault by saying: "See I told ya to watch your back...BITCH!" The announcers recap what just happend out there and announced that there would be no Mr. World-Joe Snow match as a result of what just occured. They say that
the next match will be Johnny Kyler vs "Shotgun" Shane Valentine! "Shotgun" Shane Valentine vs Johnny Kyler This becomes the show stealer of the night. Both men tore into one another at a fast pace. The end came when Valentine went for the sharpshooter but that was blocked. Kyelr goes for the Kyler Driver but Valentine lands on his feet.
Valentine pushes him into the ropes chest first into a roll-up but Kyler quickly reverses it pulling the tights for the cheap victory. Shane Valentine looks on in fustration as Johnny Kyler walks to the back with a cocky smile on his face.
