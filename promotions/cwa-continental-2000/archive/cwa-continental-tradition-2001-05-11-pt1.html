CWA (Continental, 2000) — Card

CWA Tradition Part 1

11 May 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
{"I Can't Drive 55" by Sammy Hagar plays as camera shows the fans at The World
Arena in Denver Springs, Colorado. The fans are pumped and ready to see some
CWA action. The camera then zooms in on Matt Chapen and Chris Poleski.}
Matt Chapen: Hello CWA fans and WELCOME to CWA Tradition! We are live at the
World Arena in Denver Springs Colorado where we have 7 matches for you tonight!
Including the tag team grudge match between THP and the World Champion Frat Boy Perry Tanhammer vs Kevin Candido and a partner of his choosing! Chris Poleski: I wonder who that punk is gonna choose for a partner! Hell it won't matter since its gonna be a walk in the park for THP and Frat Boy!
Matt Chapen: Ok fans it is time for Hia taking on Shane Russel... Chris Poleski: That it is both men are in the ring so lets get this under way... Matt Chapen: Both men lock up and, oh man! Russel just send Hia flying across the ring like a baby...
Chris Poleski: Well he does out wieght him by 70 or so pounds, that is why you will see this young star Hia use his speed to get the win...
Matt Chapen: He better, but instead he goes to lock up again, NO Hia with a kick real low! Chris Poleski: That stops anybody, big or small... Matt Chapen: Hia now springs off the ropes and drops kicks the knee of Shane... Not a bad move right there...
Chris Poleski: He has to break the big man down to his size... Matt Chapen: And that is what he is doing now as he repeatedly kicks and stomps the knee of Shane...Hia now going to the top! Oh man legdrop right on the face of Shane, and the cover!
One... Two... Matt Chapen: Big kickout as Hia goes flying across the ring again... Shane trying to get to his feet with help of the ropes, Hia rushes him and Shane flips him over the top...
Hia lands on the apron and grabs Shane's head and drops down! Chris Poleski: Great move by Hia, and another cover! One... Two... Matt Chapen: And another kick out by Shane... This guy is huge! Chris Poleski: Yea and Hia now on the outside grabing a chair! Matt Chapen: Oh come on this is not needed.... OH MAN SHANE JUST FLEW OVER THE TOP ROPE! Chris Poleski: And he sent that chair right into the face of Hia... Both men slow to get to there feet, hey wait a minute! it's Adam Mitchel, what does he want? Matt Chapen: I don't know but as both Shane and Hia slowly slide back into the ring,the two begin to trade rights and lefts... Shane getting the upper hand, but Hia again goes low!
Chris Poleski: I love that move, and Hia not sees Adam staning on the ramp way... Matt Chapen: Hia again slides out of the ring, grabs a mic and slides back in... Hia Tao: What you stupid American... you looking to get your ass kicked like this one behind me?
{Adam pulls out a mic of his own.} Adam Mitchell: As a matter of fact I am, but lets do this at the PPV! Hia Tao: We don't have to wait till Price of Glory let's do this right now! {Damien comes from behind and blind-sides Adam Mitchell from behind with a chairshot.... Hia Tao looks on and is laughing. He turns around only to get scooped by Shane
Russell and dropped with a spiked pile driver.} Chris Poleski: What a cheap shot! Matt Chapen: That is his prelude to his finisher and there it is, his version of the STF he calls "The Lock down"
Chris Poleski: He has it in the center of the ring! Matt Chapen: And Hia taps out, this one is over! Rick Collings: Here is your winner...Shane Russell!!! Matt Chapen: Shane picks up the win, wait a minute Damian slides in the ring and exchanges rights and lefts with Shane!
Chris Poleski: Damian will show him a thing! Matt Chapen: Here comes security to break the two up, lets take a break!
