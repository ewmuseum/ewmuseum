CWA Blood Sweat and Tears Part 6

6 August 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Matt Chapen: We just saw a mat wrestling clinic in Shane Russell and Adam
Mitchell! They just can't put on a bad match against each other! We are just moments away from that triple threat match to see who takes on Chainsaw Charlie later on tonight but before we do Todd Michaelson is in the back with Joe Snow!
{The camera shows Todd Michaelson standing next to Joe Snow} Todd Michaelson: I am back here with Joe Snow the man that will go into a triple threat here tonight against Frat Boy Perry Tanhammer and THP to see who will fight Chainsaw Charlie in a death cell match for the CWA World
Championship! How do you feel right now? Joe Snow: You know Todd, that world title match was to be mine. There was no
THP or Frat Boy Tanhammer standing in my way to getting there, but that has changed. I have never backed down from a fight in my life, but if I have to shut those two up for good to get Charlie in that Death Cell so be it!
Todd Michaelson: Michael D. Taylor said to you that this is do or die for Joe
Snow, how do you respond to that?
Joe Snow: You're damn right its do or die! If I get to Chainsaw Charlie and don't beat him there is no rematch for me agianst him. Tonight I must fight as if this were my final match of my career! Without that mentality then I've already lost! Frat Boy, THP, you have no idea what you have gotten yourselves into! You're judgement day is now and you won't like the outcome!
Todd Michaelson: Well there you have it fans, Joe Snow is moments away from that triple threat match! Back to you Matt Chapen and Chris Poleski!
{Todd Michaelson smiles as the camera fades to black. The camera then shows
Matt Chapen and Chris Poleski.}
Matt Chapen: Joe Snow is ready and he has to be! Let's get to the ring introductions! Rick Collings: This contest schedueled for one fall is a triple threat match! First coming down the aisle...FRAT BOY PERRY TANHAMMER!!! {"Mr. Speed" by KISS plays as Frat Boy Perry Tanhammer comes to the ring as you hear jeers from the crowd.}
Chris Poleski: Frat Boy is the most controversial man in America today! Can he pull off a win and get another shot at Chainsaw Charlie that he wants so bad?
Rick Collings: Next coming down the aisle...THP, THE HOTTEST PROSPECT!!! {"King Nothing" by Metallica plays as THP makes his way to the ring as the fans boo him like there is no tomorrow.}
Matt Chapen: THP has expressed in anger about how bad he wants Chainsaw Charlie and about how Joe Snow does not deserve a shot at Chainsaw Charlie's
World Title! Well tonight he may very well get both!
Rick Collings: And their opponent...JOE SNOW!!! {"Break Stuff" by Limp Bizkit plays as Joe Snow comes to the ring with his signapore cane in hand. He climbs to the top rope and points the cane to the crowd that is cheering him.}
Matt Chapen: All three men staring each other down in the ring. Side headlock by Snow, side headlock by THP! Joe Snow throws THP into the ropes in which he moves AND THP NAILS FRAT BOY! Collar-elbow tieup with Joe Snow and Perry
Tanhammer. Tanhammer reverses it into the turnbuckle. Perry Tanhammer with a vicious chop! SNOW NAILS THP! Tanhammer chops Snow to save his friend. He must remember that their are no friends when its for the title!
Chris Poleski: Snow and THP tie up. Left jab by snow followed up with a kick to the midsection. THP fires back BUT FRAT BOY HITS HIM WITH A SCHOOL BOY!
One... Two... Kickout! Frat Boy ducks a clothesline attempt by THP and Joe Snow nails him with a right hand! Joe Snow has him in a boston crab. THP trying to reach those ropes AND FRAT BOY NAILED JOE SNOW WITH A FLYING FOREARM! THP hits Snow with an elbow drop. Frat Boy follows up with an elbowdrop of his own! FB and
THP showing that cohesive unit that they are! They are like a well-oiled machine Matt!
Matt Chapen: And for Joe Snow that is bad news! FB and THP look at each other. What do they have planned here? THP picks Frat Boy in a back suplex position AND THROWS HIM ONTO JOE SNOW WITH A LEGDROP!
One... Two... Shoulder up! Both men stomping away at the Snow Dogg! Tanhammer and THP looking for their next plan of attack. THP NAILS FRAT BOY WITH A BACK
SUPLEX! This is every man for themselves not two on one! Chris Poleski: What is he doing? It must have been an accident! THP leaving the ring now and its Joe Snow and Perry Tanhammer in the ring. Both men have quite a history here in the CWA! Joe Snow with a hangman's neckbreaker! THP seems to be looking for a weapon of some sort. Joe Snow is on the apron,
SLINGSHOT SENTON SPLASH ONTO PERRY TANHAMMER! COVER! One... Two... Shoulder up! THP HAS A STEEL CHAIR! Matt Chapen: Joe Snow picking up the wobbling Tanhammer! {CRACK!!!} Chris Poleski: THP JUST THREW THAT STEEL CHAIR RIGHT AT JOE SNOW! THP was right on target! Joe Snow slowly to get up and THP has that steel chair in his hands!
{CRACK!!!} Matt Chapen: THP WITH A CHAIRSHOT TO THE HEAD OF SNOW! THP throws the chair aside AND HERE COMES FRAT BOY! THP ducks the clothesline and nails Frat Boy with a kick to the gut followed by a DDT! THP ascending to the top rope. He is not known for his high risk moves! Joe Snow comes up and starts pounding away at the big man! THP is leaning back trying to avoid anymore right hands.
Frat Boy is starting to come to. He is trying to get to Snow, BUT JOE SNOW
PUSHES OFF THP AND HITS A MOONSAULT ON FRAT BOY PERRY TANHAMMER!
Chris Poleski: That was unbelievable! Joe Snow used THP's shoulders as a catapult to nails Frat Boy! THP from behind with a left jab on Joe Snow! THP
SLAMS SNOW ONTO FRAT BOY AND MAKES THE COVER ON SNOW! One... Two... Kickout! THP grabbing the steel chair, BUT FRAT BOY DROPKICKS THP RIGHT IN THE FACE! Cover by Perry Tanhammer! One... Two... Shoulder Up! JOE SNOW COVERS TANHAMMER! One... Two... Kickout! Matt Chapen: Elbowdrop by THP on Snow followed up by a fistdrop onto Joe Snow by Frat Boy Perry Tanhammer! FB motioning to THP. Joe Snow is inching his way to that steel chair! THP picking up Joe Snow, BUT SNOW WITH A CHAIR SHOT TO
THE RIBS! JOE SNOW IS BACK ON HIS FEET! {CRACK!!!} Chris Poleski: Come on! Thats a god damn DQ! Matt Chapen: There are no DQs! Joe Snow nailed Perry Tanhammer with a chairshot to the head! THP trying to put down Joe Snow with some stiff right hands but he keeps getting up! Both men on their feet! They're trading blows!
Frat Boy seems to be going up on the top turnbuckle here! Joe Snow with a
European upercut AND FRAT BOY FLIES OFF THE TOP WITH A MISSLE DROPKICK ON JOE
SNOW! The tide has came back into the favor of Frat Boy!
One... Two... Thr...THP BREAKS UP THE PIN! Chris Poleski: UH OH! This could be trouble! THP THROWS FRAT BOY OUT OF THE
RING! THP running the opposite side ropes. SUICIDE PLANCHA BY THE BIG MAN!
HOLY SHIT! I never thought I'd ever see of all people THP pull off a suicide dive! Matt Chapen: You said it! Joe Snow coming to the outside of the ring. HE HAS HIS SIGNAPORE CANE! {CRACK!!!} Matt Chapen: Cane shot to the head of Frat Boy! {CRACK!!!} Matt Chapen: Cane shot to the head of THP! Joe Snow throws Frat Boy Perry
Tanhammer over the guardrail and back into the ring! THP still trying to recover on the outside. Joe Snow with a textbook vertical suplex!
One... Two... Thr...THP BREAKS UP THE PIN! He pulled Joe Snow's leg off before he could get the three count! He saved Frat Boy but more importantly his shot at becoming the CWA World Champion!
Chris Poleski: Oh man thank god Joe Snow didn't get the win! THP back into the ring nad he picks up Joe Snow. Frat Boy on the top turnbuckle, SPIKE
PILEDRIVER BY FRAT BOY AND THP! Frat Boy on the apron! THP pulls the ropes towards him and FRAT BOY NAILS SNOW WITH A SLINGSHOT LEGDROP!
Matt Chapen: This is not looking good for Joe Snow! I hate to say it but I don't like Joe Snow's chances! Joe Snow is hanging on the ropes. Frat Boy is pointing to Joe Snow and THP is applauding! Frat Boy runs for the clothesline but JOE SNOW BACK BODY DROPS FRAT BOY TO THE OUTSIDE! THP wasn't even aware of it he was busy taunting the fans. Joe Snow from behind THP! JOE SNOW HITS THE
SNOWFALL! JOE SNOW WITH THE COVER! One... Two... Three!!! Rick Collings: Here is your winner and #1 contender for the CWA World Title...JOE SNOW!!! Matt Chapen: Frat Boy tried to save THP but couldn't make it in time! Chris Poleski: I can't believe it!! Joe Snow with a pin on THP!!!!! After a hard fought match which was basically a handicapped match Joe Snow wins it!!
Matt Chapen: Look at Frat Boy!!! He is livid!!!! Frat Boy can't believe what just happened!!!!
Chris Poleski: And neither can THP!!! THP slowly getting to his feet now and he can't believe what just happened!!!!
Matt Chapen: THP and Frat Boy both stunned at the unexpected victory of Joe
Snow!! Chris Poleski: They had it made!! Matt Chapen: THP kicks the ropes in anger, Frat Boy slides into the ring. Both of these men extremely upset.
Chris Poleski: Frat Boy's got a mic! Frat Boy: Snow, your pasty little ass got lucky tonight. You know and I know and The Hottest Prospect knows that what happened right here in this ring not 5 minutes ago...was a fluke!!!
{The crowd boos.} Frat Boy: Snow, you know and I know and The Hottest Prospect knows that on any other day, hell even on your best day you couldn't beat me one on one, let alone THP and I in a three way!! Snow you sick piece of disgusting crap, I will not stop until I get my hands around your throat and squeeze the life out of your puny body. Snow so help me I will go through you, Charlie anyone who
Taylor want's to throw at me to get my hands on the CWA World Title.
{Frat Boy collects his thoughts as he starts to calm down. THP applauds as the crowd boos.}
Chris Poleski: I think the fans are telling Frat Boy just what they think about these two. Frat Boy: And finally......Snow......you know.....and I know...and more importantly you know THP..that I would be heading on to the Main Event if it wasn't for YOUR...SORRY ASS!!!!!
Matt Chapen: What?!?!?! Frat Boy: THP time and time again you and I have teamed together, time and time again we have faced all the odds and come out on top. But THP time and time again you have failed to hold up your half of the deal and this time you pay...and you pay in full!!!!
Chris Poleski: Frat Boy just nailed THP with the mic!!! The crowd not knowing what's going on here who to cheer and who to boo!!!
Matt Chapen: Apparently Frat Boy is blaming the victory of Joe Snow on THP!!!! Chris Poleski: Frat Boy pounding THP into the corner!!! He has a right to blame
THP, he's the one who got pinned!!!!
Matt Chapen: But where was Frat Boy!!! THP was in there alone only because he wasn't thrown to the outside like a little pansy!!!
Chris Poleski: Frat Boy lining up THP....HANGOVER!!! HANGOVER RIGHT INTO THE
MIDDLE OF THE RING!!! THE CROWD IS GASPING IN AWE OF WHAT JUST TRANSPIRED!!!
WHO TO CHEER FOR!?! Matt Chapen: Frat Boy pounding on THP but here come CWA officials!!! Chris Poleski: Where were they before?!?! Matt Chapen: CWA officials pulling Frat Boy off of THP, he is hell bent on madness here tonight!!!
Chris Poleski: Frat Boy wants Charlie, but more importantly he wants that title!! Matt Chapen: Frat Boy fighting against the officials he's knocking them left and right!! BUT HERE COMES JOE SNOW!!!!
Chris Poleski: The Snow Dogg is heading to the ring!!! He slides in the ring he's up and he just speared Frat Boy!!!!!
Matt Chapen: Joe Snow unleashing a fury of punches on Frat Boy!!! He's really putting it to him!!!
Chris Poleski: The CWA Officials pull Snow off of Frat Boy and Tanhammer rolls to the outside!!
Matt Chapen: Snow really throwing some fire into those rights and lefts, this man is pumped for his world title shot!!!
Chris Poleski: THP being helped to the back as the Snow Dogg is on fire in the ring!!! Matt Chapen: Snow calling Frat Boy to come back into the ring Frat Boy slides in and here they go again!!!!!
Chris Poleski: Frat Boy and Snow trying to get at each other but I think Frat
Boy is slowly but surely making more enemies then he can handle!!!
Matt Chapen: Officials escorting Snow backstage as Snow continues to verbally assault Frat Boy!!!
Chris Poleski: Frat Boy wanting Snow to come back in calling him out, begging him to come back to the ring. But who is that who just ran by security!??!?!
Matt Chapen: It's Larry Gionet!!!! The Living Legend has just charged past security and he is in the ring with Frat Boy!!!! THIS MATCH IS UNDERWAY!
