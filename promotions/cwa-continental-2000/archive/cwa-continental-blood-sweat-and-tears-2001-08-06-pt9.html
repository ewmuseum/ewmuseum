CWA Blood Sweat and Tears Part 9

6 August 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Chris Poleski: Well fans both Jasen Casen and Sean Harris fought their way out to the ring, and neither man wanted to wait for their enterances! Now this match is under way…Jasen with the upper hand thus far as he continues to kick and stomp Sean Harris despite the crowd booing…
Matt Chapen: The crowd not liking either man in this match but love the moves both men will show…
Chris Poleski: Jasen off the ropes and drops a running leg lariat and goes for an early cover…
One… Two… Matt Chapen: That was a close call there… Chris Poleski: That it was and Jasen like a cat hops to the top rope, high risk move coming up!
Matt Chapen: NO! He missed the frog splash as Sean just rolled out of the way… Sean now picking Jasen up and big power bomb! But that took every thing
Sean had and he can’t make the cover…
Chris Poleski: I think he would have gotten him there to… Matt Chapen: Sean back to his feet as is Jason, Sean with a right hand, and a return right from Jasen… Another by Sean who seems to be getting the upper hand… Sean sends Jasen into the ropes and nails him with a boot to the midsection… Sean off the ropes and hits a swinging neck breaker… and here’s a cover!
One... Two... Th… Chris Poleski: Oh man I thought it was over! Matt Chapen: So did Sean… Sean back to his feet, picks Jasen up and nails a big right hand… Seam then sends Jasen into the ropes again… Jasen comes back ducks a clothesline, hits the ropes again and nails a flying forearm… oh man, Sean back to his feet but is quickly nailed by a well place dropkick…
Chris Poleski: The crowd letting Jasen know they don’t care for him… Matt Chapen: And he is wasting time by doing some show boating here… Jasen finally turns his attention back to Sean who is getting back to his feet…
Jasen with a right hand that Sean blocks it and returns one of his own… another right and another block and return right by Sean Harris…Jasen in some trouble here…
Chris Poleski: He has him right were he wants him now…
Matt Chapen: Sean sends Jasen into the ropes, Sean with a tilt-a-whirl slam!
No Jasen counters in midair with a hurricaranna! Oh my what a counter… Chris Poleski: it was a great move but can he follow it up? Matt Chapen: He is doing just that as he picks Sean up and sets him on the top rope… Chris Poleski: What is he going to do with him? Matt Chapen: Well there is only one place to go! Jasen with a DDT! Chris Poleski: Talk about being planted... Matt Chapen: Here is the cover! One... Two... Three!!! Rick Collings: Here is your winner and still CWA North American Champion...JASEN CASEN!!! {"Greed" by Godsmack plays as Jasen Casen has his hand raised and victory and raises the CWA North American Title in the air for all to see.}
Chris Poleski: He got him! Matt Chapen: What a match that was… Chris Poleski: So many things going on tonight and we still have that death cell main event!
