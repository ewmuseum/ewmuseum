CWA (Continental, 2000) — Card

CWA Journey's End Part 2

7 July 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Matt Chapen: We got a blockbuster announcement at the beginning of the PPV regarding the CWA Traditional Title match and Ace Fireside just defeated Chris
Natural.
Chris Poleski: Ace Fireside got lucky but next time around he won't be! Matt Chapen: I am being told Todd Michaelson is in the back! {The camera shows Todd Michaelson in the back with Joe Snow.} Todd Michaelson: I am here with Joe Snow, and I gotta ask you Joe, why are you here? You are not schedueled to fight.
Joe Snow: I'm just here to watch the great CWA action. Todd Michaelson: You had an impressive win over THP last friday, but lets backtrack for a second. Two weeks ago, you had a match with your friend
Chainsaw Charlie and came within seconds of winning the CWA World Title and you lost. Are you bitter towards Charlie?
Joe Snow: No comment. This interview is over! {Todd Michaelson puzzled looks at the camera.} Todd Michaelson: Well there you have it folks. Joe Snow not really giving an answer. Back to you Matt and Chris!
Matt Chapen: Welcome back fans and we are ready for two big men, as Anthony "The Animal" Davis takes on The Psychotic One in a Hardcore match that is sure to get match of the year nominations… Chris Poleski: It should be a great power match and I am sure somebody will not walk out on their own two feet…
Matt Chapen: Ok fans we are ready, so let go to the ring for the introductions… Rick Collings: This contest is a Hardcore match scheduled for one fall… Coming to the ring first, weighting in at 330 pounds Anthony "The Animal" Davis!! {The lights go out, all of a sudden there is a huge explosion and "American Badass" by kid rock starts to blast over the speakers. blue spotlights start flashing all over the arena as Anthony Davis steps out on to the entrance ramp. The lights come back and TPO is standing behind him!}
Chris Poleski: TPO is behind Davis and he doesn’t know it! Matt Chapen: TPO Spin him around and nails a big right hand. He knows it now…TPO sends The Animal into the rail headfirst…
Chris Poleski: No rules in this match so anything goes just like that low blow from The Animal…
Matt Chapen: The Animal in control sends TPO into the rail, then sends grabs him and belly to belly on the carpet! No padding there fans… The Animal picking TPO up and sends him hard into the side of the ring! The Animal with a head of steam, but TPO gets the boot up and The Animal nails it face first!
Chris Poleski: TPO now pulling out a trash can… {CRACK!!! } Matt Chapen: He just nailed Davis over the head, and now throws him back into the ring…TPO walks up the steps with the can, wait a minute Davis with a drop kick into the can that hits TPO and sends him crashing to the outside…
Chris Poleski: Great move and Davis now on the outside and he sends TPO into the steel steps! Matt Chapen: Davis now with a chair! {WHACK!!! } Chris Poleski: Man did you hear that? {WHACK!!! } Matt Chapen: A second shot to the back of TPO! Davis now throws TPO back into the ring and enters behind him… Davis now stomping on the injured back of TPO… Davis picks TPO up and sends him into the ropes… Davis tries to run right through him but TPO simple catches him and BELLY TO BELLY SUPLEX!
Chris Poleski: And TPO calling for the Psycho Drop! He is going to try and end this! Matt Chapen: TPO on the top rope! {CRACK!!!} Chris Poleski: Sean Harris just nailed TPO with a chair shot! Davis now on the top for his Leg drop from hell!
Matt Chapen: He nails it! One... Two... Three!!! Matt Chapen: Davis gets the big with but had help and wait a minute Sean now stomping TPO! TPO back to his feet {CRACK!!!} Chris Poleski: What a shot and TPO is out cold! Matt Chapen: Fans as security clears the ring! Well fans we have lot’s more action to come but let’s go to the back where Shane Russell is standing by!
{The camera changes to the of Shane Russell.} Shane: Well Adam, are you ready? In just a few moments you and I will step into the ring to see who the number one contender is for the Traditional Title… The open contract will be raised high above the ring and we will fight, with anything we can get our hands on to get it... Are you ready
Adam! I hope so boy cause it is not about that contract for me… For me it is just about…. REVENGE!!! {The camera fades to black.}
