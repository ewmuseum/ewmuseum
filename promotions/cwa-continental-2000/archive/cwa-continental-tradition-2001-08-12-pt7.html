CWA (Continental, 2000) — Card

CWA Tradition Part 7

12 August 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Matt Chapen: We welcome you great fans back to CWA Tradition! If you have just joined us Larry Gionet got a big victory over the former CWA World
Champion Chainsaw Charlie and smacked him in the face! Chris Poleski: No doubt about it, he showed to the world why he is the true living legend of professional wrestling!
Rick Collings: This contest is schedueled for one fall with a 60 minute time limt and is for the CWA Traditional Title! First coming down the aisle...FRAT
BOY PERRY TANHAMMER!!! {"Mr. Speed" by KISS plays as Frat Boy Perry Tanhammer makes his way to the ring as the fans jeer him.}
Matt Chapen: You are looking at a man who is as cocky as anything but he can still get the job done!
Chris Poleski: You're damn right and that is why he is walking out of the Gund
Arena as the new CWA Traditional Champion!
Rick Collings: And his opponent, he is the current CWA Traditional Champion...SHANE RUSSELL!!! {Shane Russell comes to the ring with mic in hand and starts singing his own rap called Battle as he high fives the fans.}
Matt Chapen: Frat Boy is going right after Shane Russell! Russell caught him and is riding on Tanhammer! Perry Tanhammer trying to get out of the hold.
Tanhammer with a nice sitout by Perry, but Russell is all over him showing why he is the CWA Traiditonal Champ!
Chris Poleski: Russell has never faced Perry Tanhammer before and Tanhammer is more experienced of the two combatants! Tanhammer with a bridge trying to keep his shoudlers off the mat. That's smart fighting right there by Frat Boy Perry
Tanhammer! Russell sweeps the leg from under him!
Matt Chapen: Perry is fighting with everything he has. For these opening minutes of this contest it has been all Shane Russell! Tanhammer rolls through and twists the arm of the Champion, but Russell reverses it! Shane with a belly to belly suplex!
One... Two... Kickout! Russell stomping at the head of Tanhammer. If the occasion arrives,
Shane Russell can take his game up a notch if the CWA Traditional Title is at stake!
Chris Poleski: Russell whips Tanhammer into the ropes, but he reverses it. Shane jumps over Tanhammer. Monkey flip countered, Tanhammer coutners the block by Russell, but Shane has that wrist lock in tight! Tanhammer with a standing switch followed by a headlock. Shane throws him into the ropes,
Tanhammer comes back WITH A CARTWHEEL KICK to the face of the Champion! He hooks the leg!
One... Two... Kickout! The challenger just to quick for the champion right there! Matt Chapen: Whenever Tanhammer is in trouble he finds a way to get on the defensive and turn the tide back into his favor! That is what has made him the superstar that he is today I will give him that much! Shane Russell being picked up. PERRY TANHAMMER DRIVES HIM TO THE MAT WITH A TIGER DRIVER!
One... Two... Thr...Kickout! He almost had the win there! We would have had a new CWA
Traditional Champion!
Chris Poleski: He should be the champion right now if the ref didn't register such a slow count! Perry Tanhammer telling the fans what he thinks of them!
Tanhammer places the champion on the top turnbuckle! Frat Boy setting him up for a superplex, BUT SHANE RUSSELL THROWS HIM DOWN AND HE FALLS FLAT ON HIS
FACE! Matt Chapen: Things are not looking up for the challenger here! Perry Tanhammer getting back on his feet! He doesn't seem to know where he is! RUSSELL WITH A SUNSET FLIP OFF THE TOP! NO, TANHAMMER WITH A SIT DOWN! One... Two... RUSSELL ROLLS THROUGH! One... Two...Perry gets out of it! TANHAMMER WITH A ROLLUP! One... Two... Kickout! Chris Poleski: Oh man! Tanhammer runs at Russell and the champ ducks the clothesline! GERMAN SUPLEX BY THE CHAMPION!
One... Two... Thr...Kickout! The champion kicking away at Perry Tanhammer in the corner! Shane Russell showing us a more vicious side of him tonight! Matt Chapen: Russell taking a few steps back and runs at Tanhammer, BUT PERRY
NAILS HIM IN THE GUT! He turns around Russell and places him on top! He is signaling for the Hangover! Frat Boy is gonna win the Traditional Title right here! He looks to the crowd!
THP: HEY FRAT BOY! Matt Chapen: THAT'S THP! If THP was looking to get Frat Boy's attention he just did! He is not even paying attention to Shane Russell! RUSSELL NAILS
TANHAMMER WITH A BULLDOG! Shane Russell has it clamped on! THE LOCK UP! HIS
VERSION OF THE STF! THP is walking to the ring! He is in and is watching Frat
Boy agonize in pain! He has the mic!
THP: Come on Frat Boy, tap out you son of a bitch! Chris Poleski: No don't! Matt Chapen: Frat Boy Perry Tanhammer is trying to reach the ropes but its too far! Perry Tanhammer taps! TANHAMMER TAPS!
Rick Collings: Here is your winner and still CWA Traditional Champion...SHANE RUSSELL!!! Chris Poleski: No no NO! Matt Chapen: This is indeed an upset! Shane Russell defeated Frat Boy Perry
Tanhammer! THP in the ring and raises the hand of the CWA Traditional
Champion! What a great show of sportmanship on the part of THP! Chris Poleski: THP holds on to the arm of Russell AND DRILLS HIM WITH A SINGLE
ARM DDT! HAHA! So much for sportsmanship huh?
Matt Chapen: What is going on? Frat Boy trying to get up while holding his neck, BUT THP GRABS HIM! THP with a kick to the gut! THP PICKS HIM UP! NO
DON'T DO IT! JACKNIFE POWERBOMB TO THE OUTSIDE! GOOD GOD! Chris Poleski: That was awesome! THP picking up Shane Russell and puts him between his legs! JACKNIFE POWERBOMB ON THE TRADITIONAL CHAMPION!
{"King Nothing" by Metallica plays as THP smiles and leaves the ringside area as the fans are still in shock.}
Matt Chapen: THP just took out both Shane Russell and Frat Boy Perry Tanhammer! Chris Poleski: THP is crazy and I like that! He doesn't trust Shane Russell and has no mercy for Frat Boy!
Matt Chapen: All I have to say is that THP said it before the old THP is back and quite frankly that is not good for anyone in the CWA that stands in his way! We will return with the CWA North American Title match!

— Part 7 of 9 —
