CWA (Continental, 2000) — Card

CWA Tradition Part 1

6 May 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
{"I Can't Drive 55" by Sammy Hagar plays as the camera shows the jam-packed crowd in Madison Square Garden. They are pumped for an exciting night of CWA action. The camera then shows Matt Chapen and Chris Poleski.}
Matt Chapen: CWA fans WELCOME to CWA Traditon! We are live at Madison Square
Garden in New York! Man it feels good to be back!
Chris Poleski: It sure does! We proved that nothing could take the CWA and what doesn't kill us makes us stronger!
Matt Chapen: Very well said Chris! Tonight we have some unbelievable matches to present to you the fans for our return and we know you are gonna love it!
First we are gonna start off with a killer matchup between Kevin Candido and
THP to determine who will fight the CWA World Champion later on tonight in the main event!
Chris Poleski: When you talk about a war, when you talk about two guys that absolutely despise each other, you have to mention THP and Kevin Candido! Both men have a score to settle with the other and I can't wait!
Rick Collings: This contest is schedueled for one fall with a 30 minute time limit! First coming down the aisle...KEVIN CANDIDO!!!
{"Battleflag" by Lo-Fidelity Allstars plays as Kevin Candido makes his way to the ring to a chorus of boos from the crowd.}
Matt Chapen: When you talk about cockiness this is the man! I am suprise he doesn't have the world champion watching his back!
Chris Poleski: Hey now, Kevin Candido can fight his own battles! Rick Collings: And his opponent, being accompanied to the ring by Jon Massey...THP, THE HOTEST PROSPECT!!! {"King Nothing" by Metallica plays as Jon Massey walks behind the big seven footer as the fans go crazy.}
Matt Chapen: KEVIN CANDIDO ATTACKS THP BEFORE THE BELL! Kevin Candido whips
The Hottest Prospect into the ropes and Candido connects with a clothesline!
Kevin Candido goes for a piledriver, but The Hottest Prospect blocks it. You can't piledrive a 300+ man like that! The Hottest Prospect executes a clothesline on Kevin Candido. THP with a high knee to the face of the star killer!
Chris Poleski: The Hottest Prospect throws Kevin Candido out of the ring. The Hottest Prospect whips Kevin Candido into the guardrail. The Hottest
Prospect throws Kevin Candido back into the ring! Get up Kevin, don't let him get the best of you!
Matt Chapen: The best thing for Candido to do is get the hell out of there! The Hottest Prospect whips Kevin Candido into the ropes, but Kevin Candido reverses it! The Hottest Prospect goes for a big boot, but Kevin Candido ducks out of the way. SWINGING NECKBREAKER ONTO THP! Kevin Candido throws The
Hottest Prospect out of the ring. Candido with a full head of steam, FLYING
CROSS BODYPRESS ONTO THE BIG MAN! Candido whips THP into the guardrail! Kevin
Candido reenters the ring!
Chris Poleski: Thats it Candido, keep on him! When THP is off his feet, he loses 90% of his offense! Kevin Candido whips The Hottest Prospect into the ropes. Kevin Candido hits The Hottest Prospect with a dropkick! Kevin Candido nails him with a side suplex! With all of his might he picked the big man up in a side suplex! Now thats gutsy!
One... Two... Kickout! Matt Chapen: I thought Candido had the victory there! Kevin Candido goes for a Hotshot, but The Hottest Prospect counters it with a lariat! The Hottest
Prospect runs into the ropes and Candido nails him with a flying forearm!
CANDIDO WITH THE COVER! One... Two... Kickout! Kevin Candido takes The Hottest Prospect down with a kick to the midsection. Kevin Candido whips The Hottest Prospect into the turnbuckle.
Kevin Candido goes for a piledriver, but The Hottest Prospect counters it with
A SLINSHOT INTO THE TURNBUCKLE! The Hottest Prospect hits a fallaway slam on
Kevin Candido. Wait a minute! FRAT BOY PERRY TANHAMMER IS MAKING HIS WAY TO
THE RING! Chris Poleski: YES! Finally some prestige in this place! Kevin Candido goes for a kick to the midsection, but The Hottest Prospect counters it with a lariet! THP WITH A TILT A WIRL SIDESLAM!
One... Two... Thr...Kickout! Matt Chapen: The Hottest Prospect goes for a clothesline, but Kevin Candido ducks out of the way. Kevin Candido hits The Hottest Prospect with a shoulderblock. Kevin Candido nails The Hottest Prospect with a dropkick. Make that two! Candido is on the verge of victory! FRAT BOY IS ENTERING THE RING!
THP is gonna get screwed! He kicked Candido! WHAT THE?! The referee has called for the bell!
Rick Collings: Here is your winner by disqualification...KEVIN CANDIDO!!! {"Battleflag" by Lo-Fidelity Allstars plays as Kevin Candido has his hand raised in victory as he looks at Perry Tanhammer in confusion.}
Chris Poleski: He hit Candido but why? Matt Chapen: I don't know but Frat Boy is trying to calm him down! He did get a title shot thanks to Frat Boy!
Chris Poleski: See friends do that for each other! Matt Chapen: I guess so, I mean he is the CWA World Champion. He does have the power to pick who he faces!
Chris Poleski: You're damn right Chapen! Matt Chapen: Kevin Candido seems to have cooled off. Both men are leaving together and heading to the backstage area.
Chris Poleski: You see thats cohesiveness for ya! Matt Chapen: Thats true, Candido looked like he wanted to go to blows with the
World Champion but he would have none of it! Fans we have to take a commerical break!

— Part 1 of 7 —
