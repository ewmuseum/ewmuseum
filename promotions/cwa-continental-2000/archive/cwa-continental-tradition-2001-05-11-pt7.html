CWA (Continental, 2000) — Card

CWA Tradition Part 7

11 May 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Matt Chapen: We have returned and Frantic gets the win in what became an emotionally charged match for one Chad Yaro!
Chris Poleski: Yes thats true. Cherry is not letting Yaro forget what he could have had! Matt Chapen: But that doesn't mean she has to rub it in his face! Chris Poleski: Yes she does! Matt Chapen: Anyways, coming up next Larry Gionet gets his shot to become the
CWA Traditional Champion as he takes on Al Basler!
Rick Collings: This contest is schedueled for one fall with a 60 minute time limit and is for the CWA Traditional Championship! First coming down the aisle, he is the challenger..."THE LIVING LEGEND" LARRY GIONET!!!
{"Cum On Feel The Noize" by Quiet Riot plays as The Living Legend Larry Gionet comes to the ring to a chorus of boos.}
Chris Poleski: These two have a major history together in various promotions including the CWA! Most of the times Gionet has pulled off the win and I see
Al Basler leaving the arena beltless tonight!
Rick Collings: And his opponent, he is the current CWA Traditional Champion...AL BASLER!!! {"Awake" by Godsmack plays as Al Basler comes to the ring with the CWA Traditional Title around his waist.} Matt Chapen: Al Basler is perhaps the fightingest Traditonal Champion we have ever had in the CWA! He won't go down without a fight!
Chris Poleski: Well ain't that just fine and dandy Chapen! Al Basler whips
Larry Gionet into the ropes. Al Basler misses with a clothesline. Larry
Gionet misses with a clothesline. BASLER NAILS HIM WITH A FLYING
SHOULDERBLOCK! Al Basler nails Larry Gionet with a clothesline. Al Basler whips Larry Gionet into the ropes, but Larry Gionet reverses it. Larry Gionet misses with a clothesline. But Basler takes the challenger down with a rolling elbow! Come on Larry! Larry Gionet rolls to the outside. There ya go Gionet, take a time out!
Matt Chapen: There are no timeouts in wrestling! Al Basler getting the better of Larry Gionet in the early goings of this matchup! Larry Gionet comes back into the ring and gets stomped on! The Tradtional Champion making sure Gionet doesn't try anything sneaky. Al Basler places Larry Gionet on the turnbuckle.
WHAT A TOP ROPE FRANKENSTEINER! One... Two... Kickout! INSIDE CRADLE BY THE LIVING LEGEND! One... Two... Shoulder up! Larry Gionet whips Al Basler into the turnbuckle, but Al Basler reverses it. Larry Gionet comes back AND ROCKS THE CHAMPION WITH A KICK TO THE
MIDSECTION! Chris Poleski: Larry Gionet goes for a shoulderbreaker, but Al Basler counters it with an elbowsmash! FIGURE FOUR BY THE TRADTIONAL CHAMP! Larry Gionet is valiantly trying to break the hold! Larry Gionet tries to escape the hold.
GIONET'S SHOULDERS ARE ON THE MAT! GET UP LARRY! One... Two... Shoulder up! Larry Gionet tries to fight the pain! Larry Gionet sitting up! He is pounding on Al Basler which makes him break the hold. Good thinking by the true living legend of professional wrestling! Larry Gionet runs into the ropes. Larry Gionet goes for a Western lariat, but Al Basler COUNTERS WITH A
BELLY TO BELLY! What quick reflexes by Basler!
Matt Chapen: Indeed! He is not the Traditonal Champion for nothing! Al
Basler whips Larry Gionet into the ropes, but Larry Gionet reverses it! Al
Basler hits Larry Gionet with a kick. Al Basler goes for a figure-four leglock, but Larry Gionet kicks him off! He tried to work more on the leg of
Gionet, but Larry Gionet would have none of it! Larry Gionet nails Al Basler with a shoulderbreaker. Larry Gionet hits Al Basler with a clothesline. Larry
Gionet chops Al Basler. Basler responds with a right hand AND ANOTHER ONE!
Chris Poleski: Hey those are closed fists ref! Al Basler goes for a guillotine choke, but Larry Gionet blocks it. Larry Gionet goes for a Death
Valley Driver, BUT BASLER COUNTERS WITH A SMALL PACKAGE!
One... Two... Kickout! Al Basler attempts to place Larry Gionet on the turnbuckle, but Larry
Gionet blocks it. Al Basler whips Larry Gionet into the ropes, but Larry
Gionet reverses it! BASLER BOUNCES BACK WITH A CARTWHEEL KICK! That almost took Gionet's head off!
One... Two... Kickout! Al Basler keeps him down with a flying legdrop! One... Two... Kickout! Matt Chapen: Neither man want to give this up! When a title is on the line, the CWA's athletes pull off all the stops to leave the crowd breathless! Larry
Gionet executes a legsweep faceslam on Al Basler! The challenger now in control of this Traditional Title contest!
Chris Poleski: Larry Gionet waiting for Al Basler to get up almsot like stalking his prey! Basler is up AND GETS CRUSHED WITH A WESTERN LARIET!
Matt Chapen: Gionet thinks he has this match won! He slowly runs into the ropes. Larry Gionet goes for a splash, BUT BASLER GETS THE KNEES UP! Larry
Gionet wasted too much time! MAD HATTER IS WALKING TO THE RINGSIDE AREA!
Chris Poleski: What is he doing here! Get him out of here ref! The referee telling Mad Hatter to get out of here! Good job ref!
Matt Chapen: Al Basler picks Gionet up, BUT LARRY GIONET NAILS HIM WITH A LOW
BLOW! Gionet sees him! Larry Gionet is screaming at him to get in the ring!
AL BASLER ROLLS HIM UP! One... Two... Three!!! Rick Collings: Here is your winner and still CWA Traditional Champion...AL BASLER!!! {"Awake" by Godsmack plays as Al Basler raises the CWA Traditonal Title above his head as the fans go crazy. Larry Gionet directs his attention to Mad
Hatter obviously irrate.}
Matt Chapen: Larry Gionet can't believe it! HE IS GOING AFTER HATTER! Chris Poleski: Larry Gionet pounding away at Mad Hatter! He rams his head into the ringsteps!
Matt Chapen: Larry Gionet picking up Mad Hatter! Where is he taking Hatter? OH NO! Chris Poleski: Yes he is gonna suplex him onto the steel ramp! HE JUST SUPLEXED HIM ON THE COLD HARD STEEL! YOU GO LARRY! {Larry Gionet looks over to the ramp and sees a table setup. His eyes light up and grins from ear to ear.}
Matt Chapen: He wouldn't! Chris Poleski: Yes he would! He is gonna put Mad Hatter out of his misery and put him through that table!
Matt Chapen: He grabs Mad Hatter! HE IS GONNA GERMAN SUPLEX HIM THROUGH THAT TABLE! Chris Poleski: YEAH! This will show him! Matt Chapen: He has him up, BUT MAD HATTER FLIPS OVER AND HOLDS ON TO DEAR LIFE! {CRASH!!!} Chris Poleski: OH MY GOD! Matt Chapen: MAD HATTER JUST NAILED THE ASYLUM ON GIONET THROUGH THAT TABLE! Gionet is not moving and Mad Hatter is just starting to come too. The officials are attending to Larry Gionet and Mad Hatter under his own power is slowly making his way to the back.
Chris Poleski: WHO CARES ABOUT HATTER! HE SHOULD BE FIRED! HE COULD HAVE KILLED GIONET! Matt Chapen: It was a matter of self-defense! The officials are helping Larry
Gionet and we have to take a break! But when we come back its time for the main event!
