CWA Tradition Part 3

6 May 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
{The backstage area shows Mad Hatter walking down the hallway. As he turns left he is attacked by Larry Gionet! Larry Gionet starts beating on the Mad
Hatter! He rams his head against the garage door. With an evil look in his eye, Gionet wispers to Mad Hatter: "I'm not through with you punk!." He leaves
Hatter battered and bruised as the camera fades to black.}
Matt Chapen: What was that? Larry Gionet just assaulted the Mad Hatter for no reason except he is not done with him yet! The Jester and Madhatter get the win over the former CWA tag team champions Attitude Adjustment! Their victory would be short-lived as the devious duo of Jester and Hatter would destroy
Attitude Adjustment!
Chris Poleski: That was just incredible! Rick Collings: This triple threat contest is shcedueled for one fall and is for the #1 contnedership of the CWA Traditonal Championship! First coming down the aisle...MAD DOG MORTIMER!!!
{"Dr. Feelgood" by Motley Crue plays as Mad Dog Mortimer walks to the ring as jeers from the crowd fill the arena.}
Matt Chapen: Mad Dog is quite the manipulator. Will he sneak his way to victory tonight! Chris Poleski: Mortimer would do no such thing! He is a man of integrity! Rick Collings: And his opponent...THE LIVING LEGEND LARRY GIONET!!! {"Cum On Feel The Noize" by Quiet Riot plays as The Living Legend Larry Gionet calmy makes his way to the ring as the fans boo him.}
Matt Chapen: Larry Gionet, despite his attitude is a very gifted athlete in the CWA! He is a verteran of the sport and that could be an advantage for him in this matchup!
Rick Collings: And their opponent...CHAD YARO!!! {"Man In the Box" by Alice And Chains plays as Chad Yaro comes to the ring along with an applause from the capacity crowd.}
Matt Chapen: The fans love Yaro! Chad Yaro whips Larry Gionet into the ropes, but Larry Gionet reverses it. Larry Gionet goes for a shoulderbreaker, but
Chad Yaro slides down his back! Chad Yaro runs into the ropes and Gionet misses with an elbow! YARO NAILS HIM WITH A SUPERKICK!
One... Two... Shoulder up! Chris Poleski: Chad Yaro whips Larry Gionet into the ropes, but Larry Gionet reverses it. Larry Gionet hits Chad Yaro with a backdrop! Larry Gionet tags out to Mad Dog Mortimer. Mad Dog Mortimer whips Chad Yaro into the ropes.
Chad Yaro misses with a clothesline, BUT HE NAILS HIM WITH A JUMPING ELBOW THRUST! Yaro on the apron, SLINGSHOT SPLASH ONTO THE BIG MAN! One... Two... Shoulder up! Matt Chapen: Chad Yaro runs into the ropes, BUT GETS PLANTED WITH A CLOTHESLINE! Mad Dog Mortimer makes the tag to Gionet! Larry Gionet goes for the STF, but Chad Yaro blocks it. Chad Yaro goes for an over-the-shoulder stomachbreaker, but Larry Gionet counters it with a sunset flip! Nice athleticism by Gionet!
One... Two... Kickout! Chris Poleski: Larry Gionet takes Chad Yaro down with a legsweep faceslam. Larry Gionet tags out to Mad Dog Mortimer. Mortimer and Gionet trying to keep the rookie grounded so he will be easy for the kill! Mad Dog Mortimer whips
Chad Yaro into the ropes and Mortimer levels him with a SPEAR! He throws him out of the ring! Mad Dog Mortimer knocks Chad Yaro into the ringpost. Mad Dog
Mortimer whips Chad Yaro into the guardrail. MDM seems to be in control at the moment! Mad Dog Mortimer goes for a flying hip attack, but Chad Yaro counters
IT WITH A LOW BLOW! DQ HIM REF! Chad Yaro whips Mad Dog Mortimer into the guardrail.
Matt Chapen: He can't disqaualify him from the outside! Chad Yaro runs at
Mortimer, BUT HE GETS A BOOT TO THE FACE! He throws him back into the ring and tags out to larry gionet! Chad Yaro chops Larry Gionet AND ANOTHER ONE TO THE
LIVING LEGEND! YARO WITH A SPINNING HEADSCISSORS! Chris Poleski: Chad Yaro goes for a front-layout suplex, but Larry Gionet blocks it. Larry Gionet takes Chad Yaro down with a shoulderbreaker. Larry Gionet goes for a jack-knife power bomb, but Chad Yaro counters it with a backdrop!
One... Two... Shoulder up! Chad Yaro goes for a headlock takedown, but Larry Gionet throws him off. Larry Gionet goes for a Western lariat, but Chad Yaro COUNTERS WITH A
CRUCIFIX! One... Two... Kickout! Matt Chapen: Chad Yaro runs into the ropes AND YARO CONNECTS WITH THE BACK
HEEL KICK! Chad Yaro makes the tag to MDM! Mad Dog Mortimer hits Larry Gionet with an inverted power bomb.
One... Two... Kickout! Chris Poleski: Mad Dog Mortimer throws Larry Gionet out of the ring. They're brawling inside the ring area. Mad Dog Mortimer whips Larry Gionet into the guardrail. Both men duking it out! Get him Gionet! Larry Gionet hits a
Western lariat on Mad Dog Mortimer! There ya go!
Matt Chapen: Larry Gionet whips Mad Dog Mortimer into the guardrail. Larry
Gionet throws Mad Dog Mortimer back into the ring! HE GOES FOR THE COVER!
One... Two... In the ropes! Larry Gionet whips Mad Dog Mortimer into the ropes and hits the clothesline. Larry Gionet goes for a splash, BUT MORTIMER GETS THE KNEES UP!
Mad Dog setting Gionet up! HE NAILS HIM WITH THE BITE! One... Two... Thr...CHAD YARO BREAKS IT UP! Chris Poleski: MAD DOG IS FURIOUS! HE IS ARGUING WITH YARO AND GIONET ROLLS HIM UP! One... Two... Thr...KICKOUT! Gionet makes the tag to Yaro! Chad Yaro goes for a jumping elbow thrust, but Mad Dog Mortimer ducks out of the way. Mad Dog Mortimer runs into the ropes, BUT YARO OUT OF NOWHERE WITH A MOONSAULT BODYBLOCK!
One... Two... Three!!! Rick Collings: Here is your winner...CHAD YARO!!! {"Man In The Box" by Alice And Chains plays as Chad Yaro gets his hand raised in victory. Mad Dog Mortimer looks furious.}
Matt Chapen: CHAD YARO PULLED OFF AN UPSET! Mad Dog is furious! He runs towards Chad Yaro, BUT HE SLIDES OUT OF THE RING!
Chris Poleski: LARRY GET OUT OF THERE! Matt Chapen: Too late! Mad Dog whips Larry Gionet into the ropes AND CONNECTS
WITH A BIG BOOT TO THE FACE! He picks Gionet up perhaps looking for the Bite!
HE NAILED HIM WITH THE BITE! Gionet is out cold and Mad Dog exits into the crowd. {The camera points to the TraditionTron where Jasen Casen is pacing back and forth in the backstage area.}
Chris Poleski: Chad Yaro is walking up the ramp and is watching the traditionTron! Looks like Casen wants to talk!
Jasen Casen: Yaro, remember me? You damn right you do! A few years back you and I formed an alliance to take out all that opposed us! But who would know that a year would make all the difference in the world Chad! It was one year ago in this very arena in Madison Square Garden that you turned your back on me! You wanted to fly on your own and be your own man! I never forgot the betrayl I felt as my best friend left me hanging high and dry! I sure as hell never forgot that night and damnit I will personally make sure you never forget it either!
Matt Chapen: What does he mean by that? Chad Yaro looks confused! Jasen Casen: You see Yaro, I got a little suprise for you. But we are waiting until you have had your little wrestling match later on tonight! So why don't you go back there and relax because when that final bell rings, I have a bombshell to unleash on you! Oh and one more thing, good luck BITCH!
{Jasen Casen storms out of the room as the screen fades to black. Chad Yaro heads to the back in confusement and curiosity.}
Chris Poleski: What could possibly Casen have in store for Yaro? Matt Chapen: I don't know but it doesn't look good! We have to take a commerical break
