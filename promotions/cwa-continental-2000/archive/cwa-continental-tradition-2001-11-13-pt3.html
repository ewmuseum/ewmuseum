CWA (Continental, 2000) — Card

CWA Tradition Part 3

13 November 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Matt Chapen: We are back and if you just joined us, Johnny Kyler attacked
Shane Valentine in the back while he was getting an interview and Rodney Wright defeated Joe Snow with the help of his cronies!
Chris Poleski: I am gonna tell Wright you said that! Before we go to the Mr. World-Gargoyle match we have the priviledge of seeing a press conference held today by Mr. World's associates right now!
Matt Chapen: Oh geez great! [A static camera angle, the sense of a room full of people who aren't really
"buzzing" with excitement. Anticipation is not at a particularly high level.
There is a podium, and behind that a plain white backdrop.] [Three men come from off camera, left. Each larger than the one before him. They are each dressed in matching outfits, black suits, white shirts, black masks.] [When they stop at the podium, you notice the small differences in each man's outfit. The leader wears a silver tie, and his mask has silver stitching. The second largest, much larger than the first, but signifcantly smaller than the last has a black tie and black mask stitching. The final man, who could just aswell be a large bear in a man's suit, has a green tie and matching mask stitching. They all also have brushed alluminium tie tacks shaped like the letter "N" "F"]
Mr. Gray: Hello, I am Mr. Town, I'll be reading a prepared statement from Mr. World, there will not be a question and answere period after the statement. Thank you. "Mr. World is pleased to have found a forumn in the CWA. He thanks both Mr. Beck and Mr. Taylor for bringing him on board. He is looking forward to facing
'Gargoyle' on Saturday's Tradition. Mr. World is sure that 'Gargoyle' will put up a fight, however he is just as sure that Saturday's matchup will be the begginning of a long streak of victories here in the Continental Wrestling
Alliance.
On a more serious note, 'Chainsaw Charlie' you've claimed you have no competition in the CWA. No one here is on your level. Mr. World is here now, stop complaining and decide on a time and a place. Mr. World would rather not wait too long before adding your name to the long list of casualties.
That is all.' [All three men exit as the entered. You now notice that, Mr. Town has a brushed alluminium case handcuffed to his left arm. As they leave, the previously bored reporters excitedly yell questions, as flashbulbs burst throughout the room. "WHO IS MR. WORLD?" "WHAT'S IN THE CASE?" "WHAT IS MR.WORLD'S RELATION
TO BECK AND TAYLOR?" "WHO ARE YOU GUYS AND WHY CAN'T MR.WORLD SPEAK FOR
HIMSELF?"] Matt Chapen: Well we just saw that press conference and don't really know what to make of it. In any case we see the wrestling debut of Mr. World right now!
Rick Collings: This contest is schedueled for one fall with a 30 mintue time limit! First coming down the aisle...GARGOYLE!!!
{"Scum of The Earth" by Rob Zombie plays as Gargoyle storms to the ring with a sinister smirk on his face. The fans boo him but he just shrugs off their jeers and enters the center of the ring waiting for his opponent.}
Rick Collings: And his opponent...MR. WORLD!!! {Smoke fills the entranceway, lit from below by a red light. Suddenly, the sillouete of an athlete is in the smoke the crowd erupts and then as suddenly as he had arrived, the masked man emerges and stomps down the aisle. He slides under the ring ropes, rolls and pops up to his feet in the center of the ring.
The maskes man, moves to the corner shakes out his neck, before turning to face the center of the ring, prepared for battle.}
Chris Poleski: Well, both men are in the ring and we are ready to start. (Before Chris could get done speaking, Gargoyle attacked Mr. World from behind when he was acknowledging the fans response to him.)
Matt Chapen: Gargoyle looks very aggressive here tonight, and the attack from behind only shows that he will take any route to a victory tonight.
Chris Poleski: Don't count out Mr. World yet. (Gargoyle has Mr. World up for a powerbomb, and World sneaks out the top and hits a textbook standing dropkick and Gargoyle hits the ground hard.)
Chris Poleski: Gargoyle rolled to the outside. World is now going out after him, double axehandle off the apron onto Gargoyle.
(Gargoyle goes stumbling into the steel guardrail.) Matt Chapen: This is gonna be a dogfight. OUCH!! Gargoyle is clotheslined over the railing. Mr. World has got to capitalize on this but is not, he has his back turned to his opponent, a big no-no at this level in the business.
(Mr. World turns Gargoyle around and suplexes him onto the concrete floor.) Crowd: CWA, CWA, CWA, CWA, CWA, CWA, CWA, CWA. Chris Poleski: That's gonna leave a mark. Mr. World just got introduced to the concrete floor. Both men are struggling to get to their feet. As the chants from the crowd continue.
(Mr. World is the first one over the railing. He rolls into the ring as the referee begins to administer the count on the outside. Gargoyle falls over the guardrail and grabs a steel chair and brings it into the ring with him.)
Matt Chapen: He's gotta chair. (Just as Gargoyle is ready to wail World in the head with the chair the ref pulls it away. Gargoyle turns around and stares down the ref as World turns him around and hits a DDT.)
Chris Poleski: Lights out for Gargoyle!!! One Two... Matt Chapen: They are not done yet. (World picks him up and hits a rocker dropper.) One... Two... Thr... Matt Chapen: Kicked out again. (Mr. World goes over and picks up the chair and lays it down. He turns around to be kicked in the gut and its a Butterfly bomb on the chair. Mr. World is squriming around.)
One... Two... Foot on the ropes..... Chris Poleski: Oh boy! He Kicked out. (The ref is now reprimanding Gargoyle for the use of the chair as Mr. World stumbles to his feet. He hits Gargoyle with The Authority.)
Matt Chapen: THE AUTHORITY, THE AUTHORITY. He goes for the cover. One... Two... Three!!! Rick Collings: Your winner, Mr. World. {"Down With The Sickness" by Distubed plays as Mr. World celebrates his victory in the ring and grabs a mic.}
Chris Poleski: Mr. World is gonna speak! Keep it down Chapen! Mr. World: You just saw right there in the middle of the ring what I can do to the greats of the CWA! So where is your beloved Hardcore Icon Chainsaw
Charlie? I'll tell you where he is, he is sitting at home trying to found out where he lies and where his spot is here in the CWA! He sees the future of this business rising to new heights like never before, while dinosaurs like him sink lower and lower into never never land. Face it Charlie, you don't have it in ya anymore. Nothing will save you from becoming just a skeleton of your former self and becoming your own worst enemy! Now...
{"One Step Closer" by Linkin Park plays as Chainsaw Charlie comes to the ring with a mic in hand. He enters the ring, raises his right fist in the air as white pyro shoots behind him. Mr. World looks quite annoyed.}
Matt Chapen: CHAINSAW CHARLIE IS HERE! Chris Poleski: This is crazy! Mr. World: Hey what the... Chainsaw Charlie: SHUT UP! You've spewed enough shit already now its my turn to talk! You wanna say how you are my competition and gonna show me what real talent is all about huh? Who exactly are you Mr. World? To me and to everyone else you are nothing but a second rate rookie that can't hang with the best the
CWA has to offer.
Mr. World: Well, Charlie... {Mr. World looks around the arena, and then, he finally stops at Charlie himself.} Mr. World: I don't really see the best out here. Chainsaw Charlie: You wanna be a wise ass, huh? Well, let's see how hardcore
I can get! {Mr. World attempts to nail Charlie, however, Charlie ducks it, spins around, and begins wailing away at Mr. World with right hand after right hand! A flurry of officials dart towards the ring to seperate the two men as Mr. World spears Charlie to the mat and begins punching him.}
Matt Chapen: ALL HELL HAS BROKEN LOOSE HERE! Officials are trying to keep these two apart! Fans we have to take a break!
