CWA Blood Sweat and Tears Part 1

6 August 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
{You see Joe Snow walking in the hallway of the Georgia Dome. Michael D. Taylor sees him and stops him.} MDT: Hey Joe there is something I need to discuss with you. Joe Snow: Sure Taylor what's up? MDT: Well Snow, a lot of people have been saying that you are not a worthy competitor to Chainsaw Charlie's world title! Since you lost that world title match to him people have said that you shouldn't be in the main event!
Joe Snow: What people? MDT: Mainly Frat Boy Perry Tanhammer and THP! Joe Snow: I don't give a damn what THP or Frat Boy think! MDT: Look I know you don't Joe but why don't you prove them wrong tonight! Since Frat Boy Perry Tanhammer and THP have been running their mouth this whole time about you not being worthy and how they should be the world champion, I say we put the three of you in a triple threat match with the winner getting
Chainsaw Charlie in the main event tonight! It won't be just any match Snow its gonna be a death cell world title match where we enlcose the two competitors in a steel cage filled with weapons. Then we will get the true meaning of blood, sweat and tears!
{Joe Snow smiles at the thought.} Joe Snow: I like that I like it a lot! MDT: But there is one catch Joe Snow, if you win that triple threat tonight, there is a no-rematch clause in that contract so if you lose you don't get
Charlie again for that world title! Its do or die Snow DO OR DIE!
{Michael D. Taylor leaves the hallway as Joe Snow nods and lets out a half smile before the screen fades to black.}
{The CWA logo bursts on the screen as the opening riffs of "Hang Tough" by
Tesla plays.} # Took a shot to the chin# # Looks like you just can't win# # In this do or die situation# # Its harder than it seems# # To survie keep alive and make your dreams #Make your dreams come true# {The camera shows the Adam Mitchell/Shane Russell match at CWA Price of Glory. Adam Mitchell climbs for the belt but Shane Russell shoves him off the ladder and into the announcers table. The screen then cuts to THP picking up Kevin
Candido and dropping him with the Jacknife Powerbomb. The screen flashes into
Frat Boy Perry Tanhammer nailing Larry Gionet with the Hangover.}
#You gotta give it your best shot# #Give it all you've got# #Oh you gotta hang tough# {The screen shows Jasen Casen and Chad Yaor hitting simulatanious superkicks on
Sean Harris and Anthony Davis from Tradition two weeks ago. The screen fades off into Ace Fireside hitting the Fireside Bomb on The Psychotic One to get the victory two weeks ago on Tradition. In a flash, you see Tradition this week where THP flat out on his back and Kevin Candido is up on top and hits the Star
Killer.}
# You stay locked away inside your room# # Cause you don't know what this world is coming to# # A Hummingbird hums because he don't know the words# # The piper will play because he doesn't know what to say# # He's a lot like you yeah!# # Oh he's a lot like you!# {The clip shows A.J. Darkk on the Traditiontron smiling as Scotty Harrison is in the ring visibly upset from Tradition three weeks ago. The screen goes dark then the screen comes on with Larry Gionet hitting That's Legendary on Frat Boy
Perry Tanhammer. The screen cuts to Chainsaw Charlie getting double teamed as
Joe Snow looks on the rampway.}
# Hang tough# # You gotta give it all you've got# # You gotta hang tough# {The screen shows the world title match a month ago on Tradition. Joe Snow goes for the Snow Fall but Chainsaw Charlie backs up into the turnbuckle and hits the Chainsaw Cutter on Joe Snow for the win. Joe Snow extends his hand to
Chainsaw Charlie and accepts it as their raise their hands in a sign of mutual respect. The screen fades and cuts to Matt Chapen and Chris Poleski at ringside.}
Matt Chapen: HELLO EVERYONE and welcome to CWA Blood Sweat and Tears! We are live at a sold out crowd at the Georgia Dome in Atlanta Georgia! Tonight all three titles will be on the line as Adam Mitchell takes on his nemesis Shane
Russell in a rematch for the CWA Traditional Title! Sean Harris will do battle with Jasen Casen to snare the CWA North American Title!
Chris Poleski: Let's not forget what just transpired a few moments ago. Michael D. Taylor told Snow that he should prove himself! Tonight we see a triple threat match between THP, Frat Boy Perry Tanhammer and Joe Snow to see who takes on the CWA World Champion Chainsaw Charlie in a death cell match for the title! Its anyone's ballgame Matt!
Matt Chapen: You are damn right abou that! Tonight its do or die for Joe Snow because if he gets to Charlie win or lose there is no rematch in store! Let's get to our ring announcer Rick Collings for the ring introductions to our first matchup of the evening!
Rick Collings: This contest is schedueled for one fall with a 30 minute time limit! First coming down the aisle...THE PSYCHOTIC ONE!!!
{"Dragula" by Rob Zombie blasts over the PA system as The Psychotic One makes his way down to the ring as the fans boo him.}
Chris Poleski: TPO is one bad man Chapen. He is one guy that I would not want to be in an empty alley with!
Matt Chapen: Ace Fireside does has his work cut out for him but I know he can get the job done!
Rick Collings: And his opponent...ACE FIRESIDE!!! {"Princes of The Universe" by Queen plays as Ace Fireside makes his way to the ring with a round of cheers from the crowd.}
Matt Chapen: TPO with some right hands. He whips him into the turnbuckle and nails Ace Fireside with a splash! The Psychotic One whips Fireside into the ropes. Fireside ducks the clothesline AND NAILS HIM WITH A FLYING FOREARM!
TPO back up but Ace Fireside hits a swinging neckbreaker! Cover! One... Two... Kickout! Chris Poleski: Ace Fireside whips him into the turnbuckle. Ace Fireside goes towards TPO, but TPO pulls him to the outside! He rams him into the guardrail and Ace hits hard. The Psychotic One picks him up and THROWS HIM INTO THOSE
STEEL STEPS! TPO throwing him back in. Ace Fireside down and The Psychotic
One is on the apron. SLINGSHOT SPLASH BY THE BIG MAN!
One... Two... Shoulder Up! Matt Chapen: TPO putting the boots to a hurting Ace Fireside! TPO going up on top. He flies through the air, BUT FIRESIDE DROPKICKS HIM IN MID-AIR! What an athletic move by Ace! Fireside blocks a punch by TPO! Another block by
Fireside! Ace Fireside kicks him in the midsection! DDT, no TPO nails him in the stomach and just saved himself from going head first into the canvas!
Chris Poleski: TPO with a standing switch INTO A VICIOUS GERMAN SUPLEX! That twisted Ace Fireside apart!
Matt Chapen: TPO is looking to finish Ace Fireside off here with the powerbomb! He picks him up but Ace slides down his back. Kick to the midsection by Fireside! FIRESIDE BOMB! FIRESIDE BOMB!
One... Two... Three!!! Rick Collings: Here is your winner...ACE FIRESIDE!!! {"Princes of The Universe" by Queen plays as Ace Fireside has his hand raised in victory.} Matt Chapen: Ace Fireside with a win from out of nowhere! Chris Poleski: That lucky son of a bitch! Matt Chapen: Ace Fireside continues his streak here in the CWA! Wait a minute, someone is coming from the crowd!
Chris Poleski: ADAM MITCHELL HAS HOPPED THE GUARDRAIL! He grabs Ace Fireside and nails him with a short arm clothesline! Adam Mitchell picks him up AND
DROPS HIM WITH A FISHERMAN BUSTER! Matt Chapen: What in the hell is wrong with him? He has snapped! Mitchell grabbing both legs of Ace Fireside! HE HAS HIM LOCKED IN A SHARPSHOOTER! Look at his eyes Chris he is like a god damn madman!
Chris Poleski: Yes I like this! This is the new and improved Adam Mitchell! This is the man that is gonna win the CWA Traditional Title! Matt Chapen: Officials are scrambling in the ring trying to restrain Adam
Mitchell! Fans we understand someone is in the backstage area!
{The camera Street Punk entering the arena waving to some fans. As he turns the corner, Damien blindsides him from behind.}
Damien: Come on get up you son of a bitch! {Damien starts putting the boots to Street Punk.} Damien: Blood angel my ass! {He spits on him and walks away. The camera fades to black. The camera then cuts back to Matt Chapen and Chris Poleski.}
Matt Chapen: What in the hell has gotten into people lately?! Chris Poleski: Everyone is realizing that in this world that its every man for themselves Chapen! You can't trust anyone!
