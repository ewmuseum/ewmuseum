CWA Price of Glory Part 2

30 May 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Matt Chapen: We have returned and saw a great opening contest between Larry
Gionet and Mad Hatter...
#Old school lives forever# Chris Poleski: Who is this? {With that phrase, the fans get on their feet to see who is about to walk down the aisle. They don't know who it is, but they want to see. "Joker" by the
Steve Miller Band begins playing throughout the arena as the newcomer, Buddy
Costello steps out in front of the PPV audience. Nothing special. Just the tune and the fans. No lights, no lasers, no fireworks. He's a newbie, c'mon.}
Matt Chapen: Buddy Costello, the old schooler, out here now. He's already trying to make a name for himself.
{Buddy slides into the ring, and takes the mic from the ring announcer. Buddy looks at the crowd, smiling innocently.}
Buddy Costello: Ah know what y'all are thinkin'. Y'all are thinkin', "Who in the heck is this interrupting our pay per view?" Well, quit thinkin' that, quit askin' yourself that question, and start makin' sure y'all are gonna'
remember this date. Why? {Buddy stares right into the crowd.} BC: Because this date ... is gonna' live in infamy. Matt Chapen: Infamy? BC: Tonight is the night that a new legend is born, y'all. And Ah'm glad y'all are gettin' tah see it.
{Buddy smiles as he receives a decent pop, then gets a scowl on his face.}
BC: Naw, forget that! Ah wish Ah was in Tampa or Los Angeles, wrestling
_real_ people. Not these never-could-be's. {HEEL POP!} BC: Ah'm too good for this place. The CWA, Price of Glory ... worthless if Ah ever heard it. Ah'm the savin' grace of this god forsaken pay per view, and this god awful promotion!
{Another heel pop for the newcomer. BC: Look at my face, remember it ... cause it's the face you'll see when you see that World Title. Ah'm gonna' own this place, and nobody ...
{With that said the arena all of a sudden goes black..and "Bring the Ruckus" By
The Wu Tang Klan begins to pump through the arena's PA system. Then as Buddy turns to look at who is gonna come out of the entrance way, a man hopes out of the crowd and slides in the ring. The man stands in the ring at about six foot three and a muscular build. He has on a pair or baggy Khacki pants with brown
Timberlands under them. He also has an unbutton Trot Nixon baseball jersey on with a Red Sox hat on backwards. The unknown man has two silver hoop earings to go with his silver chain, the tips of his jet black hair are barely showing under his cap and he has black chin goatee.The man pulls out a mic.}
Matt Chapen: You know what Chris that man looks kinda familiar. Chris Poleski: Your right, but it can't be so lets listen to what he has to say. Man: Boy, you just made the second biggest mistake of your life, the first is when you came out here and trash talked this fine federation.
{ The man turns towards the crowd to recieve their applause.} Man: Now I know what your thinking. You think I am some newbie that is trying to make a name for himself by confronting you, but you are deeply mistaken if you think that. See this isn't my first stint in the CWA and I know a couple of the people in the back might remember me since I was once the hardcore champion here. Yes thats right this is the return of Mark Blaze.
{Mark Blaze again turns to recieve the cheers from the crowd.} Matt Chapen: I knew it was him. Chris Poleski: No you didn't. Mark Blaze: Ok now that I got my personal information out of the way I am gonna answer your question on why such a god of a man came down here to talk to the likes of you. And to answer it I say this, you talk about getting the world title around your waist and that we are not good wrestlers here. Well I say this, I am challenging you a match on the next CWA event. Ohh and to show you how nice I am I will let you pick the stipulations. So Buddy sleep with one eye open because the Blazen one is on the prowl and you are gonna get burned....Now thats the LAST WORD!
{With that said Blaze throws done the mic and goes back to were he came from in the audience, while recieving a big applaud from the crowd.}
Matt Chapen: Well it looks like one Mark Blaze has shut this new comer and its about time!
Chris Poleski: All he was doing was telling the fans the truth and they couldn't take it! Matt Chapen: Truth my ass!
