CWA (Continental, 2000) — Card

CWA Journey's End Part 1

7 July 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
OOC: This was the CWA's latest PPV that was sent out during the week. Check it out and feel free to critique it. Enjoy!
{The camera shows Michael D. Taylor at his desk.} Michael D. Taylor: CWA fans, I have an announcment that will change the landscape of this PPV. Due to circumstances beyond our control, both Chad Yaro and the CWA Traditional Champion The Jester can't make it here tonight! So now the title is vacant. To make sure our fans go home happy, we WILL have a CWA
Traditional Title match tonight! The ladder match that was to be for the #1 contendership for the CWA Traditional Title is now for the title itself between
Shane Russell and Adam Mitchell. A jorney that was to begin for this two men comes to an end as they go for the gold and one man will walk out on top!
{"Handle This" by Sum 41 plays as the camera shows the fans in the Hartford
Civic Center going crazy waiting in anticipation for the PPV. The camera then shows Matt Chapen and Chris Poleski at ringside.}
Matt Chapen: Hello CWA fans and welcome to CWA Journey's End! We are live at the Hartford Civic Center in Conneticut! We have the last man standing match between Frat Boy Perry Tanhammer and the CWA World Champion Chainsaw Charlie!
We also have Jasen Casen challenging Ironman Jordan Jones for the CWA North
American Title! And what about that blockbuster announcement by CWA President
Michael D. Taylor?
Chris Poleski: I can't believe it! As a result of neither Jester nor Chad
Yaro being here the match has been called off! Therefore Adam Mitchell and
Shane Russell will do battle in their ladder match for the CWA Traditional
Title! This PPV is starting out with a bang!
Rick Collings: This contest is schedueled for one fall with a 30 minute time limit! First coming down the aisle...CHRIS NATURAL!!!
Matt Chapen: Chris Natural and Ace Fireside have been in quite a war as of late and it all comes to a stop tonight! Will Fireside stand on his own as a singles competitor?
Chris Poleski: I have to give the advantage to Chris Natural since he has done it longer! Rick Collings: And his opponent...ACE FIRESIDE!!! {"Princes of the Universe" by Queen plays as Ace Fireside comes to the ring as the fans cheer him on.}
Matt Chapen: Ace fireside with a dropkick to the face of Chris Natural. Natural in the corner and Ace Fireside is pounding away on the turnbuckle! Fireside throws him to the outside. Chris Poleski: FIRESIDE WHIPS HIM INTO THE FIRST ROW! Ace picks him up INTO A
SIDE RUSSIAN LEGSWEEP ON THE GUARDRAIL! Ace Fireside throws him over the guardrail and what is he doing now?
Matt Chapen: He seems to be pulling the guardrail closer to the ringside area here. I don't know what he could have in store! Chris Natural is on the apron and Fireside is going after him. Ace Fireside goes for a russian legsweep, BUT
CHRIS NATURAL HANGS ON! He hanged onto the ropes and Ace Fireside landed hard onto that steel guardrail!
Chris Poleski: Chris Natural thows him back into the ring AND HE GRABS A STEEL
CHAIR! Natural setting up that chair as Fireside is trying to get to his feet.
Chris Natural whips him into the ropes AND NAILS FIRESIDE HEAD FIRST INTO THAT
STEEL CHAIR! His head just collided with steel Matt!
Matt Chapen: Chris Natural is continuing his assault! He pounds Ace's head into the turnbuckle in which he falls. NATURAL IS STOMPING HIM IN THE CORNER!
Chris Natural grabs the legs of Ace. BACKWARDS SITDOWN POWERBOMB! COVER! One... Two... Kickout! Chris Poleski: Ace Fireside on his knees but Natural nails him with a punch to the eyes. Chris Natural whips Fireside into the ropes and goes for a hiptoss
BUT FIRESIDE COUNTERS WITH A BACKSLIDE! One... Two... Kickout! Fireside going for a jawbreaker, BUT NATURAL COUNTERS WITH A REVERSE
DDT! Nicely done!
One... Two... Thr...Kickout! Matt Chapen: Chris Natural throws him to the outside. Chris Natural hops onto the guardrail!
{CRACK!!!} Matt Chapen: Ace Fireside out of nowhere with a chair to the face. Chris
Natural crotched on the guardrail dazed! Ace Fireside on the guardrail.
SUPERPLEX OFF THE GAURDRAIL ONTO THE FLOOR! UNBELIEVABLE! Chris Poleski: Chris Natural wisely rolls back into the ring. Ace Fireside runs back in and whips Chris Natural into the turnbuckle. The impact sends him stumbling foward. Fireside to the opposite ropes INTO A SWINGING NECKBREAKER!
One... Two... Kickout! Ace Fireside WITH A SLINGSHOT INTO THE TURNCUCKLE INTO A SCHOOL BOY! One... Two... Thr...kickout! Matt Chapen: Irish whip into the ropes reversed by Natural. Chris Natural's hiptoss is blocked. Bodyscissors by Fireside is counters with an inverted powerbomb! NO FIRESIDE BOMB! FIRESIDE BOMB!
One... Two... Three!!! Rick Collings: Here is your winner...ACE FIRESIDE!!! {"Princes of the Universe" by Queen plays again as he has his hand raised in victory as the fans give him a good ovation.}
Matt Chapen: Ace Fireside with the impressive victory over Chris Natural! He is gonna make it far here in the CWA!
Chris Poleski: Yeah he got lucky. I say Fireside's days are numbered!

— Part 1 of 8 —
