CWA (Continental, 2000) — Card

CWA Tradition Part 3

18 June 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Matt Chapen: This night has been wild and we are only to our second match! Chris Poleski: Yes, we just found out that Perry Tanhammer will challenge
Chainsaw Charlie in a last man standing match for the CWA World Title! That's just awesome!
Rick Collings: This contest is schedueled for one fall with a 30 minute time limit! First coming down the aisle...ADAM MITCHELl!!!
Matt Chapen: Adam Mitchell will face the man he turned on Shane Russell in a ladder match to see who becomes the #1 contender for the CWA Traditional Tite!
Rick Collings: And his opponent...MARK BLAZE!!! Chris Poleski: They lock up. Mark Blaze attempts to place Adam Mitchell on the turnbuckle, but Adam Mitchell blocks it. Mark Blaze throws Adam Mitchell out of the ring. Mark Blaze knocks Adam Mitchell into the ringsteps!
Matt Chapen: Mark Blaze whips Adam Mitchell into the guardrail. This seems to be where Blaze does the best! He was a former CWA Hardcore Champion! Mark
Blaze hits Adam Mitchell with spinning headscissors. Mark Blaze hits a backbreaker. BLAZE DROPS AN ELBOW ON MITCHELL! Mark Blaze may have been a hardcore wrestler in the past but he is showing us he can be technical as well.
Mark Blaze knocks Adam Mitchell into the ringpost. Chris Poleski: Mark Blaze throws Adam Mitchell back into the ring. One... Two... Kickout! Mark Blaze uses an inverted atomic drop on Adam Mitchell. Mark Blaze uses a monkey flip on Adam Mitchell. Mark Blaze goes for flying headscissors,
BUT MITCHELL THROWS HIM OFF! SHANE RUSSELL IS COMING TO THE RINGSIDE AREA!
Matt Chapen: I guess he is keeping an eye on his opponent for this sunday! Adam Mitchell runs into the ropes and Mitchell goes for a flying clothesline,
BUT BLAZE DUCKS OUT OF THE WAY! BLAZE ROCKS HIM WITH A DDT! Nicely done there!
One... Two... Thr...Kickout! MITCHELL JUST SAW RUSSELL! He doesn't look happy! Chris Poleski: Of course not! Its a distraction! Mark Blaze runs into the ropes and connects with the shoulderblock! Mark Blaze goes for a double underhook suplex, BUT MITCHELL COUNTERS WITH A BACKDROP! NO, BLAZE COUNTERS
WITH A SUNSET FLIP! One... Two... Three!!! Rick Collings: Here is your winner...MARK BLAZE!!! Matt Chapen: Blaze with a nice victory over Adam Mitchell! SHANE RUSSELL
PULLS ADAM MITCHELL FROM THE RING! HE IS BEATING THE LIVING HELL OUT OF
MITCHELL! Chris Poleski: Adam Mitchell wasn't prepared for this! Shane Russell throws
Adam Mitchell over the guardrail AND WE HAVE A BRAWL INTO THE CROWD!
Matt Chapen: Speaking of, BUDDY COSTELLO JUST RAN INTO THE RING FROM THE
CROWD! He nails Mark Blaze with a bulldog! Buddy Costello picks Blaze up AND
DRILLS HIM WITH THE DDT! Chris Poleski: ACE FIRESIDE IS RUNNING TO THE RING! Matt Chapen: Fireside is up on the top and Costello is talking trash to the fans! FIRESIDE JUST NAILED BUDDY WITH A MISSLE DROPKICK! The referee has rung the bell AND THIS MATCH IS UNDERWAY!
