CWA Blood Sweat and Tears Part 8

6 August 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Matt Chapen: The action is getting hotter and the action is getting more fierce as the night rolls on! In just a few moments THP will take on his rival
Kevin Candido!
Rick Collings: This contest is schedueled for one fall with a 30 minute time limit! First coming down the aisle...THP, THE HOTTEST PROSPECT!!!
{"King Nothing" by Metallica plays as THP comes to the ring as the fans give him a mixed reaction from ringside.}
Chris Poleski: THP must be in one foul mood tonight! Not only did he lose his match against Frat Boy and Joe Snow to get his shot at Charlie but he got turned on by Frat Boy!
Matt Chapen: You are right about that! THP is probably in a more vicious state now more than ever and thats what Kevin Candido has to look out for!
Rick Collings: And his opponent...KEVIN CANDIDO!!! {"Battleflag" by Lo-Fidelity Allstars plays as Kevin Candido makes his way to the ring as he gets cheers from the fans.}
Matt Chapen: There's the bell and these two men are already going at it!! Chris Poleski: THP and Candido locking up and THP with the power here nails
Candido with a short arm clothesline! Candido quickly back on his feet though and these two men feeling each other out once again, not to eager to lock up again is Candido.
Matt Chapen: I don't think many people are too eager to lock up with THP. Chris Poleski: Well Charlie better be since THP is going to demolish that little peon Snow and go on to take on the World Champion.
Matt Chapen: I wouldn't count out Snow like that. Chris Poleski: I wouldn't give him too much credit. Matt Chapen: Candido now locking up with THP again but using his quickness to take Candido over with a nice hip toss, THP getting to his feet but
Candido kicks his knee out from under him. Candido now choking THP. Chris Poleski: Look, blatantly cheating. Candido is nothing but a no good cheat. Matt Chapen: THP with a low blow. Chris Poleski: THP with a fine reversal to such an illegal move. But look
THP is setting him up!!!! THP is gonna try to end this match quickly!! No
Candido back body drops THP.
Matt Chapen: Candido pulling THP up and a stiff kick to the head on the part of Candido, THP straggles back and Candido bounces off the ropes and nails
THP with a big dropkick that sends THP leaning against the ropes. Candido charges and with a big clothesline sends THP out to the floor.
Chris Poleski: But look at THP he's still standing!! Nothing can stop this man I tell you...nothing!!!
Matt Chapen: OH AND KEVIN CANDIDO WITH A FLYING HIGH CROSS BODY OFF OF THE
ROPES ONTO THP AND BOTH MEN GOES CRASHING THROUGH THE DANISH ANNOUNCERS
TABLE!!!! BOTH MEN DOWN!! Chris Poleski: What in the hell was that table doing there?!?!! Matt Chapen: Candido getting back up now and THP is still shaking the cobwebs out. Candido pulls THP to his feet, Candido whips THP into the post!
And a big chop by Candido!! Crowd: WOOOOOOOOO Chris Poleski: Count em out ref!! Matt Chapen: And another chop by Candido!! Crowd: WOOOOOOOOOO Matt Chapen: THP grabs Candido and slams his head into the ring post!! THP setting Candido up against the post now and a loud chop by THP!!
Crowd: WOOOOOOOOO Chris Poleski: THP giving Candido a little bit of his own medicine!! Matt Chapen: THP chops Candido again..NO CANDIDO MOVED AND THP JUST NAILED HIS
HAND ON THAT STEEL RINGPOST!!!! Candido grabs THP's arm and slams his hand into that ringpost again! BUT THP NEARLY TAKES CANDIDO'S HEAD OFF WITH A HUGE
CLOTHESLINE!!!! Chris Poleski: HA HA!! Matt Chapen: Candido laying on the floor and I think his mind is about 8 rows into the crowd!!! Man what a shot by THP!
Chris Poleski: Candido learning how futile it is to take on The Hottest Prospect!! Matt Chapen: THP pulling Candido to his feet now and rolling him into the ring. THP climbing up onto the apron and going over the top rope. Candido staggering to his feet now. THP grabs him and throws Candido against the ropes. THP WITH THE BIG BOOT!! NOOO Candido slides underneath THP's leg and grabs his foot!! CANDIDO TAKES OUT THP'S LEG AND THE BIG MAN FALLS FLAT ON HIS
FACE AND CANDIDO WITH AN ANKLE LOCK!!!! THE REF CHECKING THP!!! THP SCREAMING
IN PAIN!!! BUT HE WON'T GIVE UP!!! Chris Poleski: Of course not, he's The Hottest Prospect!!!! Matt Chapen: THP reaching for the ropes!!! But they might as well be a mile away as Candido pours on the pressure!!!!
Chris Poleski: I think Candido is using an illegal move ref!!! Matt Chapen: It's an ankle lock!! Chris Poleski: But it's in illegal ankle lock!! REF CHECK THE HOLD!!!! Matt Chapen: THP reaches out and finally gets the ropes and the ref telling
Candido to break the hold!!!
Chris Poleski: Ignoring a ref's commands!!! That's a DQ!!! DQ REF DQ!!!! Matt Chapen: Candido releases the hold but he grabs THP's foot and drags him back towards the middle of the ring. BUT THP KICKS HIM OFF!!! THP slowly getting to his feet as Candido recovers quickly. Candido comes after THP again but THP with a low blow!!!
Chris Poleski: Amazing strategy on the part of THP!! Matt Chapen: The ref warning THP. Chris Poleski: Oh so Candido can get away with an illegal ankle lock but THP doesn't get the same!?!?!
Matt Chapen: THP with Candido in the corner now, THP with multiple elbows to
Candido's head. Ref warning THP again as THP switches from elbows to the head to knees to the midsection.
Chris Poleski: Wearing down his opponent, beautiful THP beautiful!! Matt Chapen: The ref now giving THP another warning and THP arguing with the ref!!! But LOOK AT CANDIDO!! CANDIDO HOPS UP TO THE TOP ROPE!!! HE WAS
PLAYING POSSUM!!!! THP TURNS AND A BIG FLYING LARIAT ON THE PART OF CANDIDO!!!! CANDIDO BACK UP AND IT LOOKS LIKE THIS MIGHT BE THE END OF THP!!!! CANDIDO GOING TO THE TOP!!! IT LOOKS LIKE IT COULD BE STARKILLER TIME!!! Chris Poleski: BUT CANDIDO ISN'T THE ONLY ONE WHO CAN PLAY POSSUM!!!! THP DIVES
FOR THE ROPES AND CANDIDO IS CROTCHED ON THE TOP ROPE!!!! BOY THAT THP!! WHAT A
GENIUS!!! Matt Chapen: THP is up and he looks pissed!!! Candido sitting helplessly on the top rope!!! THP looks like he is setting him up for a Superplex!!!! BUT
CANDIDO WAS WAITING FOR HIM!!! CANDIDO GRABS ONTO THP'S HEAD AND NAILS HIM WITH
MULTIPLE KNEES TO THE FACE!!! THP STAGGERS BACK AND GOES DOWN!!! CANDIDO ON
TOP!!!! STARKILLER!!! STARKILLER!!!! NO!!! THP MOVED!!!!!! THP ROLLED OUT OF
THE WAY JUST IN TIME!!!! AND BOTH MEN LAYING ON THE MAT!!!! Chris Poleski: HA HA THP OUTSMARTED HIM AGAIN!!!! Matt Chapen: Looks to me like THP rolled over to cover up his busted nose! Chris Poleski: Never! Matt Chapen: Needless to say both men slowly climbing to their feet here. Candido charges THP!! THP WITH A BIG BOOT!!! NO CANDIDO DUCKS IT!!! CANDIDO
BOUNCES OFF THE ROPES AND THP GOES FOR A CLOTHESLINE BUT CANDIDO DUCKS IT HOOKS
THP'S ARM AND FLOATS OVER!! HE'S SETTING UP THP FOR A SUPLEX!!! BUT THP BLOCKS
IT!!! THP WITH A BIG KNEE TO THE MIDSECTION OF CANDIDO!!! THP PUTS CANDIDO
BETWEEN HIS LEGS!!!! Chris Poleski: HERE IT COMES!!!! Matt Chapen: THP PULLS CANDIDO UP!!! JACKKNI....NO CANDIDO IS POUNDING THP'S FACE WITH RIGHTS AND LEFTS!!! THP IS STAGGERING!!! Chris Poleski: Ah..NO!!! Matt Chapen: AH YES!!! THP ABOUT TO CRUMBLE!!! NO!!! THP CHARGES THE CORNER AND
DROPS CANDIDO WITH A JACKKNIFE POWERBOMB INTO THE CORNER!!! DID YOU SEE
CANDIDO'S HEAD BOUNCE OFF THAT TOP TURNBUCKLE!?!?! Chris Poleski: HA HA HA HA IT'S OVER!!!!! THP! THP! THP! Matt Chapen: He's not through yet!!! THP drags Candido into the middle of the ring and sets him up again!!! AH COME ON THIS IS UNCALLED FOR!!!
Chris Poleski: No, he's calling it...see HA HA JACKKNIFE!!!!!! Matt Chapen: THP with the non-chalant cover.. One... Two... Three!!! Rick Collings: Here is your winner...THE HOTTEST PROSPECT!!! {"King Nothing" by Metallica plays as THP has his hand raised in victory. He grabs the referee and throws him to the outside.}
Chris Poleski: Yeah you tell him THP! Matt Chapen: Candido and THP tore up the Georgia Dome tonight and THP won this match viciously I might add! We have seen a new attitude in THP and that might not be good for anyone that crosses paths with him!
