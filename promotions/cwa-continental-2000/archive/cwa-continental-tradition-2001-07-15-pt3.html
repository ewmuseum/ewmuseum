CWA (Continental, 2000) — Card

CWA Tradition Part 3

15 July 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Matt Chapen: We are back and A.J. Darkk with an impressive debut beat Anthony
Davis! But that is not the whole story as another newcomer Scotty Harrison burst out on the scene!
Chris Poleski: I don't know whats going on but I understand Tood Michaelson is with A.J. Darkk right now.
{The camera shows A.J. Darkk sitting in a chair with Todd Michaelson standing next to him.}
Todd Michaelson: A.J. what just went on out there with Scott Harrison? What past do you have with him?
{A.J. Darkk stands up and throws the chair across the room which makes Todd
Michaelson back off a bit.}
A.J. Darkk: What gives you the right to come in here and ask me a question like that? My past is none of your damn business Michaelson! So why don't you go back to your office and tell your little croonies that you didn't get the scoop on A.J. Darkk! So get the hell out of my locker room before I throw you out!
{Todd Michaelson walks out of the room while A. J. Darkk looks visibly pissed off.} Matt Chapen: Well it looks like we won't get ourselves an answer tonight! Rick Collings: This contest is schedueled for one fall with a 30 minute time limit! First coming down the aisle, being accompanied to the ring by Anthony
The Animal Davis...THE PERFECT 10 SEAN HARRIS!!!
{"California Love" by 2Pac blasts the loud speakers and my guy comes out with his escorts. He just gets into the ring and raises his arms in the air as the fans boo him.}
Rick Collings: And his opponent...THE PSYCHOTIC ONE!!! {Thunderstruck by AC/DC begins to play as the lights fade to blue. TPO steps out of the curtain in his usual all black. Black t-shirt, black jeans, black boots, and black duster. The crowd boos very loudly. TPO slowly walks down to the ring. He climbs the steps then steps over the top rope. He stands in the center of the ring as the lights come up and the music fades.}
Matt Chapen: Sean Harris hits a flying clothesline on TPO. Sean Harris takes him down with a dropkick! Sean Harris whips The Psychotic One into the ropes, but The Psychotic One reverses it. HARRIS TAKES HIM DOWN WITH A FLYING
SHOULDERBLOCK! Chris Poleski: Sean Harris whips The Psychotic One into the ropes, but The
Psychotic One reverses it. The Psychotic One hits Sean Harris with a clothesline.
TPO takes him down with a backbreaker. He goes for the cover! One... Two... Kickout! The Psychotic One hits an elbowsmash on Sean Harris. The Psychotic
One hits a back suplex on Sean Harris. The Psychotic One whips Sean Harris into the ropes AND NAILS HIM WITH A CHOKESLAM! He just chokeslamed the 265 pounder!
One... Two... Kickout! Matt Chapen: The Psychotic One runs into the ropes and TPO misses with a kick. The Psychotic One misses with an elbow. Sean Harris hits The Psychotic One with a back elbow. Sean Harris whips The Psychotic One into the ropes, but The
Psychotic One reverses it! The Psychotic One goes for a chokehold, but Sean
Harris counters it with a facerake.
Chris Poleski: That was a desperation move by the perfect 10!Sean Harris executes a series of punches on The Psychotic One. Sean Harris runs into the ropes but TPO PICKS HIM UP INTO A GORILLA PRESS! What power by the Psychotic
One!
One... Two... Kickout! TPO whips him into the ropes and The Psychotic One goes for a powerslam, BUT HARRIS COUNTERS WITH A SWINGING NECKBREAKER! MOONSAULT BY THE
PERFECT 10! What agility!
One... Two... Kickout! Matt Chapen: Sean Harris goes for a right jab, but The Psychotic One ducks out of the way. Sean Harris hits The Psychotic One with a left jab! He couldn't escape that one! Sean Harris whips The Psychotic One into the ropes and Harris misses with a kick. The Psychotic One goes for a lariat, but Sean Harris counters it with a crucifix.
One... Two... Kickout! Chris Poleski: Sean Harris goes for a DDT, but TPO COUNTERS WITH A BACKDROP! The Psychotic One whips Sean Harris into the ropes. The Psychotic One hits
Sean Harris with a clothesline!
Matt Chapen: The Psychotic One goes for a roundhouse right, but Sean Harris counters it with an armbreaker. Sean Harris attempts to place The Psychotic
One on the turnbuckle, but The Psychotic One blocks it. Sean Harris runs into the ropes AND TPO NAILS HIM WITH A POWERSLAM! This one could be all!
One... Two... Shoulder Up! Sean Harris whips The Psychotic One into the ropes, but The
Psychotic One reverses it. TPO WITH A BIG BOOT! TPO picks him up, POWERBOMB!
One... Two... Three!!! Rick Collings: Here is your winner...THE PSYCHOTIC ONE!!! Chris Poleski: Yes! TPO got the victory over that punk Sean Harris! I guess
Harris wasn't as perfect as he thought he was!
Matt Chapen: What's this? ACE FIRESIDE IS RUNNING TO THE RING! TPO IS TELLING HIM TO BRING IT! Chris Poleski: Ace Fireside with some right hands to the big man! He whips him into the ropes, but TPO reverses it! Fireside ducks the clothesline AND
NAILS THE PSYCHOTIC ONE WITH A CARTWHEEL KICK! Matt Chapen: The Psychotic One rolls out of the ring and isn't keeping his eyes off of Ace Fireside!
{"Princes of the Universe" by Queen plays as Ace Fireside stands in the ring looking at TPO who is mad as hell.}
Chris Poleski: Why on Earth would Ace Fireside go after TPO? What does he want a death wish?
Matt Chapen: All I gotta say is that kid has a lot of guts and a lot of heart
Poleski! Fans, Todd Michaelson is in the back with Shane Russell.
{The camera shows Shane Russell with the CWA Traditional Title drapped around his shoulder as Todd Michaelson stands beside him.}
Todd Michaelson: Shane, we all heard from a very intense individual in Adam
Mitchell at the start of this program. What is your response to his remarks as well as his challenge to a rematch?
Shane Russell: You know Todd, I'd never intentially try to cripple another human being to become champion because I have a hell of a lot more class and integrity than to stoop so low to pull something like that! It seems to me that one Adam Mitchell is a little insecure! I bet he's feeling a little insecure that he lost to the better man and his only way to retaliate is to lie to everyone else that it wasn't his fault that he lost! As for the challenge
Mitchell, you should know that I don't back down from anybody so you got your rematch. But just remember one thing Adam, when you lose at the PPV, leave your excuses at home!
todd Michaelson: Tonight, you have your first title defense against Chad Yaro, your thoughts?
Shane Russell: Chad, unlike Adam I see a little class in you. You deserve this shot at the Traditional title that was granted to you at the PPV! I know that you will give it your all tonight, but its not gonna be enough Yaro! I respect your abilities Chad Yaro, but this is the CWA Traditional Title we are talking about, MY TITLE! You are gonna have to kill me to take it from me
Chad! I'll see you in the ring!
{Shane Russell leaves the room as the screen goes back to Matt Chapen and Chris
Poleski.} Chris Poleski: Haha! Shane Russell is gonna be in a world full of hurt when the PPV rolls around!
Matt Chapen: Shane Russell has worked hard to get where he is Poleski and I think that belt will stay around the waist of Russell! We will return!
