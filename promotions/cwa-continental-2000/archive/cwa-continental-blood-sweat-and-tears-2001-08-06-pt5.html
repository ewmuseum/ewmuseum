CWA Blood Sweat and Tears Part 5

6 August 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Matt Chapen: In just a few minutes we have the CWA Traditional Title match! It was at Price of Glory where these two men got a shot at the title in a ladder match when it was not even supposed to go down! So now with Shane
Russell as champion, Adam Mitchell looks to take it away!
Rick Collings: This contest is schedueled for one fall with a 60 minute time limit and is for the CWA Traditional Championship! First coming down the aisle, he is the challenger...ADAM MITCHELL!!!
{Adam Mitchell's theme plays over the PA system as Adam Mitchell comes to the ring to a chorus of boos from the crowd.}
Chris Poleski: I like this kid. He knows what to do in that ring and with the right moment he could walk away tonight as the champion!
Matt Chapen: There is no doubt Chris that this is gonna be a show stealer for sure! Rick Collings: And his opponent, he is the current CWA Traditional Champion...SHANE RUSSELL!!! {Red fireworks go off as he raps his own rap called "Battle" while walking to the ring he will greet fans going around the ring.}
Matt Chapen: Both men tie up and Russell with a headlock takedown. Mitchell with a legscissors, but Russell kicks out and both men are back on their feet.
Shane and Adam in a test of strength but Russell hits him with a legsweep and goes for the cover!
One... Kickout! Shane Russell trying to put away the match early and retain his title but its too early to be trying to do so! Adam Mitchell trying to grab the legs from a croouched position but Russell quickly moves out of the way. Adam
Mitchell showing us his amateur background!
Chris Poleski: He is also showing a world-wide audience why he deserves to be the CWA Traidtional Champion! Shane Russell with a hammerlock on the challenger but Adam Mitchell puts the champ off his feet with a snapmare takedown. Very good ring presence of mind by Adam Mitchell here. Mitchell trying to get on the shoulders of Shane Russell, BUT RUSSELL BRIDGES HIS LEGS!
That is some thinking on the part of the CWA Traidtional Champion! Matt Chapen: No doubt about it Chris! Adam Mitchell tried to push down on the shoulders of Shane Russell so they could be on the mat to administer a count by the referee but Russell quickly bridged his body with his legs to prevent it!
Russell tries to roll through, but Adam Mitchell keeps him grounded with an armbar. Adam Mitchell to fast for Russell this time around. Russell grabs a hold of Mitchell's left leg, BUT MITCHELL ROLLS THROUGH WITH A FIJI ARMBAR!
Adam Mitchell trying to hyper-extend the left arm of the Champion to make him say uncle! Russell's shoulders are on the mat! The ref makes the count!
One... Two... Kickout! Chris Poleski: Shane Russell was one second away from losing his Traditional
Title! Russell's shoulders couldn't take the pressure and it almost cost him!
RUSSELL PUSHES THE OTHER LEG OF MITCHELL INTO A COVER! One... Two... Kickout! With Shane Russell's arm still open he grabbed the left leg of Mitchell and pushed upwards. That was incredible! Adam Mitchell grabs a hold of the ropes before anymore damage could be dished out!
Matt Chapen: Both men get back on their feet and Mitchell hits Shane Russell with a knee to the midsection. He whips him into the ropes and connects with a hiptoss. Mitchell throws him into the ropes again. Mitchell with a japanesse armdrag COUTNERED BY A MONKEY FLIP! MITCHELL LANDS ON HIS FEET! Adam Mitchell ducks the clothesline! Russell goes to grab mitchell, but Mitchell grabs the legs! He is going for a boston crab BUT RUSSELL SPINS AROUND AND THROWS HIM
OFF! Shane Russell has good lower body strength!
Chris Poleski: Shane Russell nails him with a dropkick! Mitchell grabs Russell catching him in a northern lights suplex! One... Two... Thr...Kickout! Mitchell was so close! He was one second away from becoming the new CWA Traiditonal Champion! Mitchell with a dropdown toe hold on Russell, he turns him around AND WRAPS THE LEGS AROUND THE NECK OF THE CHAMPION!
Matt Chapen: Adam Mitchell grabbing a hold of the right arm of the Champion. Shane Russell trying to kick out! Neither man look to be slowing down at this point because they are doing whatever it takes so when that final bell rings they have their hand raised in victory! Shane Russell able to turn around and has the legs of Mitchell! SHANE RUSSELL WITH A BOW AND ARROW! The tide has quickly turned to the favor of Russell. Shane Russell with the strength of his legs PUSHES UPWARD PLANTING THE SHOULDERS OF MITCHELL ONTO THE MAT! This could be all!
One... Two... Kickout! Chris Poleski: That was so close! Adam Mitchell grabs the ropes and gets himself back up to that vertical base! Adam Mitchell with a thumb to the eye of Shane Rusell. Adam Mitchell grabs a hold of Rusell AND HITS A MONSTEROUS
BELLY TO BELLY! With all the power that was left in Adam Mitchell he threw the traditional champion halfway across that ring with the belly to belly!
Mitchell with the cover! One... Two... Thr...Kickout! Adam Mitchell whips Russell into the ropes and HITS A HIGH CROSS BODY! One... Two... Thr...Kickout! SHANE RUSSELL KICKED OUT AGAIN! Matt Chapen: Both men want this title bad! They want it so bad that they can taste it! The fans are on their feet applauding the efforts of these two men!
Mitchell setting up Shane Russell for a vertical suplex, but he plants him on the top rope! He hooks the arm over INTO A SUPERPLEX! The challenger rolls over into the cover!
One... Two... Kickout! Adam Mitchell going for an abdominal stretch, but Russsell grabs both his arms from behind! Both Russell and Mitchell going for a backslide. Shane
Rusell with the advantage but Mitchell flips over! Mitchell grabs Russell from behind. Rollup, NO RUSSELL ROLLS THROUGH! THE LOCK IN! SHANE RUSSELL HITS
THE LOCK IN! Adam Mitchell with nowhere to go! Adam Mitchell trying to reach those ropes but he can't do it. MITCHELL TAPS! MITCHELL TAPS! ITS OVER!
Rick Collings: Here is your winner and still CWA Traditional Champion...SHANE RUSSELL!!! {Shane Russell has his hand raised in victory as he holds the CWA Traditional
Title high in the air.}
Chris Poleski: That was an incredible matchup! Matt Chapen: Indeed it was! Shane Russell put it away with the Lock In! Both careers of these men look bright!
