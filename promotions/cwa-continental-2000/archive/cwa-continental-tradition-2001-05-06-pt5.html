CWA Tradition Part 5

6 May 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Matt Chapen: This match is underway! Gargoyle now sends Charlie into the rope, and nails him with a clothesline, Gargoyle on the top rope now, Charlie back to his feet! Only to be level with a flying clothesline… Gargoyle again picks him up and this time send him to the floor…
Chris Poleski: Uh oh this is about to get ugly…Gargoyle out after him grabs a chair… {CRACK!!!} Matt Chapen: Oh man did you hear that? Man oh man that has to hurt… Chris Poleski: Well look at the welt it left on the back of Charlie, man! Matt Chapen: Gargoyle now measering him and waits for Charlie to get to this feet… {CRACK!!!} Matt Chapen: Right over the head and Charlie is busted wide open. The ref letting this one go as he is pleading for Gargoyle to get it back into the ring…And as if on cue he throws Charlie back into the ring. Listen to this crowd they do not like what they have seen thus far.
Chris Poleski: Who cares Gargoyle is showing that there is not a man in this building who can beat him.
Matt Chapen: Gargoyle setting Charlie up for a powebomb! Oh man he nailed it and the cover!
One... Two... Matt Chapen: No he kicked out! Man that was close… Chris Poleski: I thought it was over right there… Matt Chapen: Gargoyle now with a side headlock, but Charlie fighting out with forearms to the kidney, then another…Gargoyle breaks the hold, Charlie runs into the ropes and runs right through Gargoyle…
(Crowd cheers) Matt Chapen: Gargoyle back to his feet and is nailed with a clothesline sending him right back down… Charlie again runs into the ropes Gargoyle gets up, turns around and leapfrogs Charlie… Charlie hits the ropes again and nails a double arm
DDT just as Gargoyle ducks his head! Oh man! The cover! One... Two... Thre… Matt Chapen: No Kick out! Chris Poleski: That was to close for the Champ… Matt Chapen: Yea we almost had a new Champ right there. Charlie now picking the
Champ up and throws him to the outside…
Chris Poleski: Oh no! Charlie is getting his chainsaw! Come on ref! {Charlie grabs his chainsaw much to the delight of the crowd but is stopped in his tracks with a low blow.}
Matt Chapen: Oh man that will stop anybody… Chris Poleski: Great move by the Champ. That chainsaw is dangerous…Gargoyle now with a bleeding Charlie and slams his head into the rail. Gargoyle now throws Charlie back into the ring… Gargoyle calling for his Death Dive Bubba
Cutter!
He his this and it is all over… Matt Chapen: He has it set up and… NO Charlie with the reverse into his Chainsaw cutter, NO wait! Another reversal and Charlie nails the Death Dive! One... Two... Three!!! {Crowd boos.} Rick Collings: Here is your winner and still North AmericanChampion...GARGOYLE!!! {"Scum Of The Earth" by Rob Zombie plays as Gargoyle rasises the CWA North
American Title high above his head as the fans boo some more.
Chris Poleski: Gargoyle with a successful defense of his North American title over Chainsaw Charlie.
Matt Chapen: An exhausted Chainsaw Charlie Chris Poleski: What are you talking about he had plenty of time to rest! Matt Chapen: He has like 30 seconds!! Chris Poleski: That should be plenty for an athlete who is as well conditioned and as tough as Charlie claims to be.
Matt Chapen: Gargoyle cheated!! He came out here and attacked him directly after a match when Charlie is supposed to get 15 minutes to rest!
Chris Poleski: Gargoyle cheated!?!?! What about Charlie!!! Has his friend
Joe Snow come in and try and take out Gargoyle while he's not looking! I mean really!
Matt Chapen: What are you talking about!!! Wait..Gargoyle has a mic. Gargoyle: CWA, once again I prove why I am the most unstoppable man in this sport today!!! ::Crowd boos:: Oh shut up! Here below me are two of your most precious little men, Chainsaw Charlie and Joe Snow crushed under the might of Gargoyle!!! ::Crowd boos louder:: I've said it before and I'll say it again...NOONE SURVIVES THE DEATH DIVE!!!!! There is not one damn miserable little speck of a man in this arena or backstage that can even come close to me!! I am invincible!!!!!! For now I am the North American champion, but soon I will go and I will claim what is rightfully mine. The CWA World
Championship title does not deserve to be on anyone other then the most powerful force on the face of the earth!! So all you other pathetic contenders in the CWA, know this, Gargoyle is an unstoppable machine, I will go and crush and maim and destroy everyone who steps in front of me, everyone who gets in my way until this federation is MINE. NOONE CAN STOP
ME!!! {"Same Old Song and Dance" begins to play and the crowd erupts as Frantic walks out onto the entrance ramp. He has a mic in his hand and smiles down at the ring. The crowd continues to cheer wildly as Frantic waits for them to calm down. The music stops and Frantic raises his hand for silence, the crowd calms down to see what the former North American champion has to say.}
Frantic: Gargoyle..............you have something that belongs to me.
Gargoyle: Oh really little man? And what might that be? Frantic: MY title, doesn't deserve to be anywhere near your greasy little ass. Gargoyle: Is that so shrimp? Then why don't you come down here and get it from me? Frantic:.........Gladly. {The crowd erupts as Frantic throws the mic down and begins to head towards the ring. He takes off his shirt and throws it into the crowd he begins to take off his watch when Gargoyle comes back on the mic.}
Gargoyle: Stop right there tiny. I know getting into this ring and battling me for this title is exactly what you want. So you know what....SCREW YOU!!!
{Frantic looks at Gargoyle confused.} Gargoyle: From this moment on, I believe I will just take a leave of absence. And I'm taking your precious little title with me!!!!!!
Matt Chapen: WHAT??!?!?! HE CAN'T DO THAT!!!! Chris Poleski: Come on, the guy just finished wiping the floor with Charlie and Snow, he deserves a vacation he is the NA champion you know!
Matt Chapen: But he's the North American champ!! He can't just take a vacation in the middle of it!!
{Frantic now has a mic.} Frantic: You think you can just leave? You have an obligation to these people to defend your title!
Gargoyle: SCREW THESE PEOPLE!!! I don't give a shit about them!!! I just give a shit about me! And right now I feel like pissing you off in the worse way possible. And we all already know that me kicking your ass isn't going to do it because I just did that last time we met!!!!
{"I Can't Drive 55" blasts and the fans erupt again. Both Gargoyle and Frantic look up to the entrance to see Michael D. Taylor come out. He has a mic in his hand and let's the crowd calm down.}
Chris Poleski: What does HE want? Matt Chapen: Probably the same thing we all want, to see Gargoyle knocked down a peg!! Michael D. Taylor: Gargoyle, it has come to my attention that you want a leave of absence from the CWA...is this true?
Gargoyle: {Smiling} Ya damn right it is. Michael D. Taylor: Good....it's granted. {The crowd boos as Gargoyle smiles and nods his head. He applauds the President as Frantic looks on in outrage.} Michael D. Taylor: But....the North American Title isn't going with you. {Gargoyle stares in amazement at the President as it's Frantic's turn to applaud.} Gargoyle: What are you saying? Michael D. Taylor: You want to leave? Fine..but you are stripped of the North
American title.
{The crowd goes nuts as Frantic applauds louder and grins at Gargoyle.} Gargoyle: WHAT?!?!?! YOU CAN'T DO THAT TO ME!!! Michael D. Taylor: Just did. Gargoyle: Fine then, I'm not leaving {to Frantic} Come on pipsqueak let's teach you another lesson in humiliation.
{Frantic heads towards the ring.} Michael D. Taylor: Hold up Frantic. Sorry Gargoyle, it's not negotiable..you wanted your leave of absence, I gave it to you. I can't undo a done deal.
Gargoyle: You can't!! I am the North American Champion I do have certain rights as that champion!!
Michael D. Taylor: Yes you do, and as such I will give you this. After your leave of absence which I will determine how long that is. You can come back, and when...sorry....IF you do return you can have a "Whatever, Whenever,
Wherever" match. Gargoyle: A what? Michael D. Taylor: If you return, whoever the North American champion is at that time must grant you a match. Whatever kind of match you want, wherever you want that match to be and whenever you want it to happen. But for now, you are no longer the North American champion. In fact at Price of Glory there will be a three way match for the vacant North American Title!! Three men will meet to determine who is worthy of holding that title. Frantic, you of course will be granted a slot in that match. {The crowd goes nuts as Frantic smiles and nods.} Seeing as how you were the previous champion that's only fair. But as for your other two opponents, we will have to see who they are.
I have four men in my mind that I think are worthy of the shot. They will have to battle it out to see who gets the other two. Those four men are Joe
Snow {Crowd cheers}, Kevin Candido {Crowd Boos}, Jordan Jones {Crowd cheers} and The Jester {Crowd boos}. Between now and Price of Glory those four men will battle for those two extra spots. But as for you
Gargoyle, like my father used to say....pack your shit...and get. {"I Can't Drive 55" plays again as Taylor exits.} Matt Chapen: I DON'T BELIEVE IT!!! Chris Poleski: WHAT A BUM DEAL!!!! GARGOYLE SHOULD GO AND TAKE TAYLOR'S HEAD OFF!!! Matt Chapen: What?!?! Chris Poleski: Gargoyle was just trying to get a few days off and Taylor has to butt in!!! Matt Chapen: Well Gargoyle got his few days, and more!!!! Without the CWA NORTH AMERICAN TITLE!!!! Chris Poleski: I can't believe it. Matt Chapen: I can't either!!! A three way for the North American Title!!!!! Fans we will be back!!!
