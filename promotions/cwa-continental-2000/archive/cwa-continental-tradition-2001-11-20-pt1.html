CWA (Continental, 2000) — Card

CWA Tradition Part 1

20 November 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The camera shows the outside of the Dunkin Donuts Center in Providence, Rhode
Island. A limo pulls up that is in camera's view. Out comes Agent Rodney
Wright then CWA North American Champion Shane Russell, followed by the CWA
World Champion, Kevin Candido. All three are all smiles until five men charge at them with lefts and rights. Frat Boy and Larry Gionet throw Shane Russell onto the hood of the car. Joe Snow throws Agent Rodney Wright into the garage door. Kevin Candido gets a double suplex by THP and Chad Yaro! Shane Russell tries to get up, but Frat Boy and Larry Gionet double clothesline him. Larry
Gionet is pounding away at Shane Russell at the roof of the limo while Frat Boy goes to work on Rodney Wright. Frat Boy brings towards the limo and goes to smack his head against the trunk but he blocks it and shoves him into THP who was working on Kevin Candido. Joe SNow nails Wright with a DDT! THP and Frat
Boy turn around and start having a shoving contest! Before they can go to blows, Joe Snow and Larry Gionet hold back Frat Boy while Chad Yaro and an official holds back THP. All 5 men leave as officials are attending to Kevin
Candido, Shane Russell and Agent Rodney Wright.
The announcers welcome us to CWA Tradition and recall with what just happened in the parking lot. Matt Chapen then talks about the shoving match between
Frat Boy and THP going in detail about their history together. We get into our first matchup.
Adam Mitchell vs Shotgun Shane Valentine The opening contest of Tradition proved to be a fast paced affair from bell to bell. The match was full of near falls and false finishes. The end came when
Shane Valentine went for the Double Barrel Burst, but Adam Mitchell kneed him in the gut catching him with the Downward Spiral at the 11 minute mark. Shane
Valentine rolls out of the ring as Adam Mitchell comes to the outside and throws Rick Collins out of his chair and grabs the chair he was sitting in. He throws it in the ring and grabs a mic. Mitchell says that for two weeks he has been attacked from behind and he's sick of it. He wants to face his attacker once and for all face to face man to man. You see Nighthawk walk to the ring.
He has spiked black hair and pale white skin. He has tattoos on his body that represent various occult things. Nighthawk has a black goatee and a mean looking smile. The crowd is stunned but Mitchell isn't phazed. Mitchell wacks
Nighthawk with the chair, but Nighthawk just smiles at him. Adam Mitchell goes for another shot but Nighthawk ducks it and grabs the throat of Mitchell forcing him to drop the chair. Before he can pick him up for a chokeslam, Adam
Mitchell kicks him below the belt and hits the Downward Spiral onto the steel chair. With a smile on his face he leaves the ring.
Winner: Adam Mitchell Interview: Jason Devlin Todd Michaelson interviews Jason Devlin. He says he has waited a long time to show the CWA what he can do as a wrestler. He says he has waited a long time to find that certain person that can push him to the limit and bring out the best in him. He found that person in Mike Broughton. He knows about his background and accepts his challenge. Devlin sets the date for next Tradition.
