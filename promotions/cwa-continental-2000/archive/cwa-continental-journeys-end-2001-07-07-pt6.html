CWA Journey's End Part 6

7 July 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Matt Chapen: Kevin Candido just defeated Larry Gionet in one epic battle! Chris Poleski: Speaking of Larry Gioent he is in the back! {The camera shows Larry Gionet walking down the hallway. Larry Gionet turns a corner and is at the locker room of Frat Boy Perry Tanhammer. He opens the door and you see Perry Tanhammer doing some pushups.}
Frat Boy Perry Tanhammer: Hey Larry, what's up? Sucks that you lost your match huh? Larry Gionet: You damn right it sucks! Where the HELL were you when I was getting my ass kicked huh?
Frat Boy Perry Tanhammer: I was here in my locker room. Come on Larry I have a big match ahead of me and much preperation must go into fighting a last man standing match with the CWA World Title on the line!
Larry Gionet: Yeah thats all fine and well but I had the chance to beat a former world champion and get myself in the title picture! So whats the deal, you don't care about your friends anymore is that it?
Frat Boy Perry Tanhammer: Woa bro, take it easy pal. I just thought you could handle it all yourself since you are the true living legend of professional wrestling.
{Larry Gionet just shakes his head in fustration and heads out of the room.}
Frat Boy Perry Tanhammer: What did I say?
{Frat Boy Perry Tanhammer looks perplexed about what went happened but soon continues his workout. The camera goes back to Matt Chapen and Chris Poleski.}
Matt Chapen: Interesting developments with Gionet and Tanhammer. Can they get along?
Chris Poleski: Of course they can get along! They are simply the best and even simple the best have their arguments!
Matt Chapen: Oh brother! Rick Collings: This contest is schedueled for one fall with a 30 minute time limit! First coming down the aisle...THP, THE HOTTEST PROSPECT!!!
{"King Nothing" by Metallica plays over the PA system as THP slowly walks to the ring as the fans talk some trash as he heads to the ring.}
Rick Collings: And his opponent...STREET PUNK!!! {"Iron Man" by Black Sabbath plays as Street Punk comes to the ring with signapore cane in hand as the fans cheer him.}
Chris Poleski: Street Punk nails The Hottest Prospect with a kneelift. Street
Punk executes an inverted atomic drop on The Hottest Prospect. Street Punk whips The Hottest Prospect into the ropes and he takes the big man down with a clothesline!
Matt Chapen: Street Punk runs into the ropes and throws him to the mat with a hiptoss. Street Punk nails The Hottest Prospect with a fistdrop. Street Punk uses a back suplex on The Hottest Prospect. STREET PUNK WITH A BOSTON CRAB!
Street Punk throws The Hottest Prospect out of the ring. Street Punk hits The
Hottest Prospect with a clothesline! He has been dominating this match!
Chris Poleski: Street Punk whips The Hottest Prospect into the guardrail. Street Punk goes for a stomp, but THP ROLLS OUT OF THE WAY! THP nails him with a clothesline! The Hottest Prospect knocks Street Punk into the ringpost. The
Hottest Prospect takes Street Punk down with a fallaway slam. The Hottest
Prospect hits Street Punk with a high knee. The Hottest Prospect throws Street
Punk back into the ring. THP IS BRINGING THE TABLE INTO THE RING!
Matt Chapen: The Hottest Prospect goes for a clothesline, but Street Punk counters it with a hiptoss. Street Punk climbs to the top turnbuckle, BUT THP
THROWS HIM TO THE MAT! The Hottest Prospect whips Street Punk into the ropes and he takes him down with a high knee! THP SETTING PUNK UP ON THE TABLE! THP
IS UP ON TOP! {CRASH!!!} Matt Chapen: THP WITH AN ELBOWDROP THROUGH THAT TABLE! One... Two... Shoulder Up! Chris Poleski: The Hottest Prospect whips Street Punk into the ropes, but
Street Punk reverses it. The Hottest Prospect hits Street Punk with a clothesline. The Hottest Prospect whips Street Punk into the turnbuckle. The
Hottest Prospect charges into the corner, BUT STREET PUNK LIFTS HIS LEG! Punk balancing on the top rope! MISSLE DROPKICK INTO THE FACE OF THP!
One... Two... Kickout! Street Punk gives him a forearm smash, but The Hottest Prospect only stares at him. The Hottest Prospect hits a clothesline on Street Punk.
Matt Chapen: The Hottest Prospect goes for an eye gouge, but Street Punk blocks it. Street Punk takes The Hottest Prospect down with a kneedrop.
Street Punk hits The Hottest Prospect with a fist to the midsection INTO AN IMPLANT DDT! One... Two... Thr...Shoulder Up! Street Punk whips The Hottest Prospect into the ropes AND
NAILS HIM WITH A CLOTHESLINE SENDING HIM UP AND OVER THE TOP! They're brawling inside the ring area. The Hottest Prospect knocks Street Punk into the ringsteps.
Chris Poleski: The Hottest Prospect goes for a stomachbreaker, but Street Punk blocks it. Street Punk hits The Hottest Prospect with a kick to the midsection and knocks THP into the ringsteps. Street Punk whips The Hottest Prospect into the guardrail. He throws him back in! Street Punk chops The Hottest Prospect.
THP FIRES BACK! The Hottest Prospect whips Street Punk into the ropes. The
Hottest Prospect misses with a clothesline. Street Punk goes for a clothesline, but THP has him up IN A GORILLA PRESS SLAM!
One... Two... Kickout! Matt Chapen: The Hottest Prospect whips Street Punk into the ropes and takes him down with a backddrop! The Hottest Prospect chops Street Punk. Street
Punk with a right hand! Street Punk whips The Hottest Prospect into the ropes,
BUT THP REVERSES IT! Street Punk hits The Hottest Prospect with an elbow.
Street Punk whips The Hottest Prospect into the turnbuckle, but The Hottest
Prospect reverses it. The Hottest Prospect charges into the corner. The
Hottest Prospect hits a stomachbreaker on Street Punk!
One... Two... Shoudler Up! Chris Poleski: THP with a kick to the gut. HE HAS HIM SET UP! ITS POWERBOMB
TIME! THP taunting the crowd and he picks him up! WHAT?!
Matt Chapen: ITS KEVIN CANDIDO! HE JUST GRABBED THE LEG OF THP KNOCKING STREET PUNK ONTO THP! One... Two... Three!!! Rick Collings: Here is your winner...STREET PUNK!!! {"Iron Man" by Black Sabbath Plays as Street Punk celebrates his victory.}
Chris Poleski: Where did he come from?
Matt Chapen: The instant replay of the match is showing that when Street Punk and THP were brawling, Kevin Candido snuck in from the crowd and under the ring which allowed him to pull his leg from underneath him to give Street Punk the win!
Chris Poleski: Kevin Candido raising his arms in the air like a big shot! Yeah you better run! THP is gonna kick your ass! Matt Chapen: Calm down Chris!
