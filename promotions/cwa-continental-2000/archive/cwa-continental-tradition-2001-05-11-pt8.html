CWA (Continental, 2000) — Card

CWA Tradition Part 8

11 May 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Matt Chapen: We have returned and what a main event this will be! A tag team grudge match where the CWA World Title is on the line! Frat Boy Perry
Tanhammer better have siad his prayers because this could very well be his last night as CWA World Champion!
Chris Poleski: Oh stop being so negative Chapen! There is no way he is gonna lose this match!
Matt Chapen: Oh yeah Chris, well we got Chainsaw Charlie and Kevin Candido that are gonna be gunning for him and his title!
Chris Poleski: Exactly and those two will bicker about who pins the champion if it ever got to that point which it won't!
Rick Collings: This tag team contest is schedueled for one fall and is for the
CWA World Title! First coming down the aisle...THP, THE HOTTEST PROSPECT!!!
{"King Nothing" by Metallica plays as THP slowly walks to the ring as he hears jeers from the crowd.}
Matt Chapen: THP, what a no good son of a bitch! I thought he couldn't get any lower than he already has! He turned on his best friend Jon Massey and powerbombed him! The World Champion would assist in this brutal attack!
Chris Poleski: He deserved it! All Massey did was ride on the THP express throughout his career!
Matt Chapen: Now that is not even half true damnit! Rick Collings: And his opponent, he is the current CWA World Champion...FRAT BOY PERRY TANHAMMER!!! {"Mr. Speed" by KISS plays as Frat Boy Perry Tanhammer walks to the ring as he wears the CWA World Title around his waist. The fans boo him like there is no tomorrow.}
Chris Poleski: Now here is a man that has some class! He has brought dignity back to the CWA World Title that it was lacking when was in the possesion of the star loser Kevin Candido!
Matt Chapen: You are nothing but a hypocrite Poleski! Rick Collings: And their opponents! First coming down the aisle...KEVIN CANDIDO!!! {"Battleflag" by Lo-Fidelity Allstars plays as Kevin Candido makes his way to the ring to a mixed reaction from the crowd.}
Rick Collings: And his tag team partner...CHAINSAW CHARLIE!!! {"One Step Closer" by Linkon Park plays as Chainsaw Charlie comes to the ring with his diamond-cut chainsaw. As he enters the ring he raises the chainsaw high in the air as white pyros explode around him.}
Matt Chapen: Perry Tanhammer whips Chainsaw Charlie into the turnbuckle. Chainsaw Charlie comes back and rocks Perry Tanhammer with a kick to the midsection. Chainsaw Charlie and Kevin Candido whip Perry Tanhammer into the ropes and nail the champion with a kick to the midsection! The Hottest
Prospect enters the ring and throws Kevin Candido out of the ring! Chris Poleski: Perry Tanhammer and The Hottest Prospect whip Chainsaw Charlie into the ropes and level charlie with a double elbowsmash! Perry Tanhammer tags out to The Hottest Prospect. The Hottest Prospect whips Chainsaw Charlie into the ropes, but Chainsaw Charlie reverses it! The Hottest Prospect hits
Chainsaw Charlie with a high knee! Just look at the dominance THP has over
Charlie! Its amazing Matt simply amazing!
Matt Chapen: You can't put away Chainsaw Charlie easily! The Hottest Prospect leaves the ring. HE HAS A BASEBALL BAT! THIS CAN'T BE GOOD!
{SMACK!!!} Matt Chapen: Chainsaw Charlie is busted wide open! The referee throws it out of the ring! The Hottest Prospect goes for an eye gouge, but Chainsaw Charlie blocks it. Chainsaw Charlie tags out to Kevin Candido! TANHAMMER ENTERS AND
LAYS OUT CHARLIE! DAMN HIM! The Hottest Prospect and Perry Tanhammer whip
Kevin Candido into the ropes and drill him with a double clothesline! The double-teaming continues for this devious duo! The Hottest Prospect goes for a stomachbreaker, but Kevin Candido blocks it!
Chris Poleski: Kevin Candido goes for a kick to the midsection, but The Hottest Prospect counters it with a dragon screw, BUT CANDIDO COUNTERS WITH AN
ENZIGUIRI TO THE FACE! Kevin Candido throws The Hottest Prospect out of the ring!
Matt Chapen: Chainsaw Charlie takes The Hottest Prospect down with a back suplex. Charlie can play dirty when he needs to! Kevin Candido goes for a piledriver, but The Hottest Prospect COUNTERS WITH A BACKDROP! THP trying to get some breathing time is setting up a table! The Hottest Prospect whips
Kevin Candido into the guardrail. The Hottest Prospect throws Kevin Candido back into the ring! The Hottest Prospect tags out to Perry Tanhammer! Perry
Tanhammer and The Hottest Prospect whip Kevin Candido into the ropes and take down the star killer with a double backdrop! Things are not looking up for the
Star Killer!
Chris Poleski: No there not! I told you so! Perry Tanhammer goes for a cobra clutch suplex, but Kevin Candido COUNTERS WITH AN ARMDRAG TAKEDOWN! Kevin
Candido hits a swinging neckbreaker on Perry Tanhammer. Kevin Candido takes
Perry Tanhammer down with a gutwrench suplex! This could be it! We could have a new world champion! KICK OUT PERRY!
One... Two... Shoulder up! Matt Chapen: Perry Tanhammer with all his might just got the shoulder up before the three! Perry Tanhammer runs into the ropes and the champion misses a clothesline. Perry Tanhammer hits him with a shoulderblock. Perry Tanhammer tags out to The Hottest Prospect. Candido nails him with a dropkick! Kevin
Candido off the ropes AND CONNECTS WITH A FLYING CLOTHESLINE! HE MAKES THE
COVER! One... Two... Kickout! Kevin Candido tags out to Chainsaw Charlie! Chris Poleski: Chainsaw Charlie and Kevin Candido whip The Hottest Prospect into the ropes. They attempt to hit The Hottest Prospect with a double kick to the midsection, BUT HE FLATTENS THEM WITH A DOUBLE CLOTHESLINE! THP is no match for either man! The Hottest Prospect goes for an eye gouge, but Chainsaw
Charlie blocks it. Chainsaw Charlie throws The Hottest Prospect out of the ring. Chainsaw Charlie hits The Hottest Prospect with a chop. Kevin Candido comes over BUT GETS LAID OUT BY THE CHAMPION! What good teamwork! The Hottest
Prospect goes for a fallaway slam, but CHARLIE COUNTERS WITH A FOREARM SHOT!
Matt Chapen: THP stumbles back INTO A CANDIDO DROPKICK! Chainsaw Charlie throws The Hottest Prospect back into the ring! Chainsaw Charlie brings the table into the ring. Chainsaw Charlie runs into the ropes AND GETS CLOBBERED
WITH A BIG BOOT! The Hottest Prospect tags out to Perry Tanhammer. Chainsaw
Charlie goes for a bodyslam, BUT HE COUNTERS WITH A SMALL PACKAGE!
One... Two... Kickout! Chris Poleski: Perry Tanhammer runs into the ropes BUT CHARLIE NAILS HIM WITH
A CLOTHESLINE! Chainsaw Charlie sets up Perry Tanhammer on the table. BELLY
TO BELLY THROUGH THE TABLE!!! CHAINSAW CHARLIE MAKES THE COVER! NO NO!
One... Two... Thr...Kickout! TANHAMMER JUST KICKED OUT! THANK GOD! Chainsaw Charlie runs into the ropes, but the champion connects with a shoulderblock! Nicely done!
Matt Chapen: Tanhammer on the top rope! What is Tanhammer going for here A
MOONSAULT! HE IS PULLING ALL THE STOPS TO KEEP HIS TITLE! Perry Tanhammer goes for a moonsault, BUT CHARLIE GETS THE KNEES UP! His knees banged against the midsection of the champion! Chainsaw Charlie picks him up! CHAINSAW
CUTTER! CHAINSAW CUTTER! THIS IS IT! WE HAVE A NEW WORLD CHAMPION!
COOOOOVER! One... Two... Thr...THP JUST PULLED CHAINSAW CHARLIE OUT OF THE RING! HE LEVELED HIM WITH A CLOTHESLINE FROM HELL! Chris Poleski: Kevin Candido making his way to the top! Listen to these fans! HE NAILED THE STAR KILLER ON THE CHAMPION! But he is not the legal man! Matt Chapen: That right, so he can't pin him! HERE COMES THP FROM BEHIND! Chris Poleski: He has Candido set up FOR THE JACKNIFE POWERBOMB! HE NAILED
IT! Tanhammer is starting to show signs of life!
Matt Chapen: Chainsaw Charlie is sneaking behind The Hottest Prospect! THP is proud of his accomplishment! He turns around and CHAINSAW CUTTER ON THP!
CHAINSAW CUTTER! THIS FANS ARE GOING CRAZY! Chris Poleski: Wait Frat Boy rolls Charlie up! HE GRABS THE TIGHTS! One... Two... Three!!! Rick Collings: Here are your winners...FRAT BOY PERRY TANHAMMER AND THP!!! {"Mr. Speed" by KISS plays as THP pulls Perry Tanhammer out of the ring as he grabs the CWA World Title. They look at Kevin Candido and Chainsaw Charlie in the ring.}
Matt Chapen: Chainsaw Charlie was robbed! He had the victory won! Chris Poleski: Yeah right, Tanhammer had this win in the bag! Matt Chapen: Yeah right! This is a god damn travesty! Chainsaw Charlie has grabbed the mic.
Chainsaw Charlie: Tanhammer, I got your ass at Price of Glory so why don't you keep that World Title nice and shiny because come May 27th, it will be yours no more and you are GURANTEED TO BLEED BITCH!
{Chainsaw Charlie drops the mic as he stares at Frat Boy Perry Tanhammer.}
Chris Poleski: OH MAN!
Matt Chapen: This has been a wild and crazy night here at Tradition! For Rick
Collings, and Chris Poleski, this is Matt Chapen saying GOOD NIGHT EVERYONE!
