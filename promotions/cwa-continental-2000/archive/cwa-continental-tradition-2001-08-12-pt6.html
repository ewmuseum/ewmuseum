CWA (Continental, 2000) — Card

CWA Tradition Part 6

12 August 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Matt Chapen: We have returned and THP got the win over Jason Devlin in a great matchup! After the bout Perry Tanhammer and Joe Snow tried to run down to the ring but the champion was tackled to the ground by Chad Yaro his opponent for tonight!
Chris Poleski: Yeah and poor Tanhammer was left all by himself! THP tried for that Jacknife but Frat Boy nailed him with a low blow!
Rick Collings: This contest is schedueled for one fall with a 30 minute time limit! First coming down the aisle...THE LIVING LEGEND LARRY GIONET!!!
{"Running With The Devil" by Van Halen plays as The Living Legend Larry Gionet makes his way to the ring as the fans boo him like there is no tomorrow.}
Matt Chapen: Larry Gionet looking more confident than he has in a while!
Chris Poleski: Gionet has the mic! Larry Gionet: CWA, none of you saw it coming did ya? I know Chainsaw Charlie sure as hell didn't see it coming! How did it feel Charlie to get smacked into that steel cage and see your hopes and dreams of retaining that title that you cherish so much go down the tubes? How did it feel Charlie to see your best friend Joe Snow turn on you so he could become the CWA World Champion and step out of your shadow? I bet you felt like shit!
Matt Chapen: Just horrible! Chris Poleski: Shut up! Larry Gionet: You see Joe Snow, Frat Boy and I dreamt this whole thing up! And isn't it ironic that Frat Boy and I have been at each others throats at
Blood Sweat and Tears! You see kiddies, this was all about the master plan!
Each and every one of you fell for it hook, line and sinker! There is nobody that... {"One Step Closer" by Linkin Park plays as Chainsaw Charlie stands on the rampway enterance with a mic in hand.}
Matt Chapen: Looks like Chainsaw Charlie decided to ruin Gionet's little speech! Chris Poleski: He is just pissed that he got outsmarted! Chainsaw Charlie: Well if it isn't the living lackey Larry Gionet! {The crowd laughs at the comment and starts chanting "Larry's a lackey!" Larry
Gionet is starting to get pissed.}
Larry Gionet: I AM NOT A LACKEY! Chris Poleski: Damn right, Larry is NOT a lackey! Chainsaw Charlie: See I knew that you wouldn't do such an eleborate plan all by yourself! You had to get too bed buddies to do your dirty work! You have the CWA World Champion Joe Snow, the young strong back to carry out such a thing! Then there is you, the brains behind this whole ordeal to make it work.
And who can forget Frat Boy Perry Tanhammer, the only guys with the BALLS to even dare to carry this out! And if you were wondering if your boys are,
Taylor has banned them from ringside, so its just you and me brother! Sorry for you punk but you are GURANTEED TO BLEED!
Matt Chapen: CHAINSAW CHARLIE RUNS TO THE RING! Both men slugging it out here on Tradition! After Gionet was revealed as one of the masked men he made this personal with Chainsaw Charlie! Gionet caught in the corner. Charlie goes for a punch but he ducks out of the way. Chainsaw Charlie is begging Larry Gionet to come at him. He's playing mind games with the true living legend!
Chris Poleski: You wanna talk about mind games, you are looking at the mastermind Larry Gionet! He set this all up! Charlie and Gionet tie up with
Chainsaw Charlie pushing him into the turnbuckle! Chainsaw Charlie with some fists of fury. Come on ref thats a DQ! Larry Gionet falls to the mat and
CHARLIE IS STOMPING AWAY AT HIM! Larry Gionet rolling to the outside! He's crazy Matt I'm telling ya!
Matt Chapen: Well I wouldn't be exactly myself either if I got stabbed in the back by my ally! Larry Gionet rolls back in and Charlie hits him with a side headlock takedown and goes for the cover!
One... Two... Kickout! Chris Poleski: You can't beat the true living legend of professional wrestling like that! No way no how! Larry is starting to get back to his feet. With the headlock still applied, Gionet throws Charlie into the ropes and gets hit with a shoulderblock! The former world champion runs the opposite ropes, hiptoss blocked by Charlie. Chainsaw Charlie with a backslide!
One... Two... Kickout! Chainsaw Charlie whips Larry Gionet into the ropes, but Gionet reverses it. OVERHEAD BELLY TO BELLY! GIONET HOOKS THE LEGS!
One... Two... Shoulder Up! Matt Chapen: Chainsaw Charlie running into the ropes but gets knocked down with a dropdown toe hold! LARRY GIONET IS BOUNCING CHARLIE'S HEAD LIKE A BASKETBALL! Larry Gionet is trying to do anything he can to defeat the hardcore icon! Larry Gionet wrapping the legs around Chainsaw Charlie in what looks to be a triangle hold. The referee checking to see if its a choke and its not.
Chris Poleski: Of course he wouldn't cheat Chapen. I mean what kind of man do you think Larry Gionet is? He is one of the most technical wrestlers of this business! Charlie quickly grabbing the ropes. Sure Chainsaw take the easy way out why don't ya!
Matt Chapen: What easy way out? What else was he supposed to do? Chris Poleski: He should have taken the pain like a real man! He should follow Larry Gionet's example!
Matt Chapen: I surely doubt he will and God help us all if he ever does! Chris Poleski: Whats with the bad-mouthing Chapen huh? Anyways back to the match up at hand. Both men are back on their feet and Chainsaw Charlie whips
Larry Gionet into the corner ramming the knees into his midsection! Don't give up Larry! Chainsaw Charlie grabs his head and rams it into the turnbuckle. Oh no!
Matt Chapen: Gionet's head bounces off the turnbuckle AND CHARLIE WITH A SCHOOL BOY! One... Two... Thr...Kickout! Larry Gionet is helping himself up on the ropes. Chainsaw
Charlie grabs him from behind with a wristlock, BUT GIONET NAILS HIM WITH A LOW
BLOW! The referee didn't even see it! Where is the justice?
Chris Poleski: Oh the justice is when Larry Gionet pins Chainsaw Charlie in the middle of that ring for the one, two, three! The living legend hits him with a reverse atomic drop FOLLOWED UP BY A CLOTHESLINE!
One... Two... Kickout! Gionet runs into the ropes and hits a swinging neckbreaker on Chainsaw Charlie! Gionet hooks the legs! One... Two... Shoulder Up! Matt Chapen: Larry Gionet whiping Chainsaw Charlie into the ropes but he reverses it! CHARLIE WITH A BULLDOG!
One... Two... Kickout! Chainsaw Charlie keeping Gionet down with an elbow! The hardcore icon's momentum is growing. He picks up the living legend and plants him with a single arm DDT! Charlie hooks the leg!
One... Two... Thr...Shoulder Up! Chris Poleski: Charlie picking up Gionet sensing the end! NO! GIONET THROWS
HIM INTO THE TURNBUCKLE! There ya go Larry! Charlie is holding his shouler!
The impact of that just hurt his shouler. Gionet picks him up FOR THAT'S LEGENDARY! NO! CHARLIE LANDS ON HIS FEET! Matt Chapen: Charlie goes for the Chainsaw Cutter but Larry Gionet throws him into the ropes! Gionet with a kick to the midsection, he picks him up AND
NAILS HIM WITH THAT'S LEGENDARY! Chainsaw Charlie is out cold!
One... Two... Three!!! Rick Collings: Here is your winner...THE LIVING LEGEND LARRY GIONET!!! {"Running With The Devil" by Van Halen plays as The Living Legend Larry Gionet celebrates his victory.}
Chris Poleski: I knew he could do it! He didn't need outside help like you thought he would Chapen!
Matt Chapen: You were right Chris! Larry Gionet has the mic and stands over
Chainsaw Charlie!
Larry Gionet: Charlie...THAT...is why you're not legendary! {Larry Gionet drops the mic and slaps Chainsaw Charlie in the face and the fans booing Larry Gionet like crazy.}
Matt Chapen: That good for nothing son of a bitch! Fans when we come back the
CWA Tradional Title will be on the line!
