CWA (Continental, 2000) — Card

CWA Tradition Part 5

15 July 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Matt Chapen: We have returned and its been a crazy ride! TPO just attacked
Ace Fireside in the back while THP powerbomed Kevin Candido through the announcers table! What else could happen next?
Chris Poleski: I don't know but I am loving this! Matt Chapen: I have gotten word that Joe Snow is in the back and wanted some airtime! { The scene opens up inside the locker room of Joe Snow. Joe is sitting down on a bench near his locker as he gets ready for his tag team match later tonight. Joe is sitting with his head down staring at the floor. He continues to look at the floor as he slowly begins to speak. }
Joe Snow: Chainsaw Charlie, tonight you and I step into the ring with Larry
Gionet and Perry Tanhammer. Now it's obvious to me that Michael D Taylor is just doing this to stir the pot and see how far he can push me. He knows about the problems that you and I are having, he knows all about the controversy and he damn sure knows just how bad I want to kick your ass. Now don't get me wrong chuckles, when we step in that ring tonight I will be your partner, not because I like you, not because I respect you, but because I have nothing but hatred and contempt for Larry Gionet and his little cronie Perry Tanhammer.
{ Joe takes a deep breathe, runs his hands through his hair and begins to speak again. } JS: Tonight I wil od the same thing that I have done since day one. I will walk out to that ring and take care of business by putting my body on the line.
I will go out there and prove to Michael D Taylor, I will prove to Chainsaw
Charlie, but most importanly I will prove to myself that I am worthy of the title shot and that I am worthy of being the next man to walk around with the
CWA title around my waist.
{ Joe takes another deep breathe, but this time he raises his head and stares directly into the camera. }
JS: Charlie, I want you to look me in the eyes, I want you to remember this face. Because at the next pay per view when you are laying on your back, this will be the face that you see standing over you as the new CWA Champion. You want me to sell my soul, you want me to do whatever it takes to win the title.
Well chuckles if thats what you want then thats what you will get. But don't come crying to me when you don't like the results.
{ Joe closes his eyes, takes a few breathes of air and lowers his head as the screen fades to black. }
Matt Chapen: I haven't seen Joe Snow more focused in my entire life Chris! He wants to not only prove to the CWA that he is worthy of being in the main event but also to himself!
Rick Collings: This contest is schedueled for one fall with a 60 minute time limit and is for the CWA Traditional Title! First coming down the aisle...CHAD
YARO!!! {"Man In The Box" by Alice And Chains plays as Chad Yaro comes to the ring as the fans cheer him on.}
Rick Collings: And his opponent, he is the current CWA Traditional Champion...SHANE RUSSELL!!! {Red fireworks go off as he raps his own rap called "Battle" while walking to the ring he will greet fans going around the ring.}
Chris Poleski: Shane Russell takes Chad Yaro down with a bodyslam. Shane
Russell with a dropkick on Chad Yaro.
One... Two... Kickout! Shane Russell hits Chad Yaro with an elbowdrop. One... Two... Shoulder Up! Matt Chapen: Shane Russell runs into the ropes BUT YARO HITS AN ENZIGUIRI TO
THE FACE OF THE CHAMPION! Chad Yaro whips Shane Russell into the ropes, but
Shane Russell reverses it! SPINNING MULE KICK BY THE CHALLENGER! Chad Yaro continues the assault with an over-the-shoulder stomachbreaker.
Chris Poleski: Chad Yaro is on the apron. SLINGSHOT BODYBLOCK! One... Two... Kickout! I'll admit this kid can fly when he is given the chance! Shane
Russell has to keep this match on the mat to stop Chad Yaro's momentum! Chad
Yaro takes Shane Russell down with a front-layout suplex. Chad Yaro whips
Shane Russell into the ropes, but Shane Russell reverses it. Shane Russell whips Chad Yaro into the turnbuckle, but Chad Yaro reverses it. Chad Yaro runs shoulder-first into the corner. Chad Yaro goes for a headbutt, but Shane
Russell blocks it. RUSSELL WITH A SPINNING LEG LARIET!
Matt Chapen: Shane Russell uses a vertical suplex on Chad Yaro. Shane Russell gets a front facelock on Chad Yaro. Chad Yaro is writhing in pain. The referee checking the arm of Yaro!
He lifts it... it falls! He lifts it...it falls! One more time and he's out! He lifts it again...IT STAYS UP! Matt Chapen: CHAD YARO POWERING HIS WAY OUT OF THE HOLD! There is no quit in this kid I'll tell ya that much! Chad Yaro nails Shane Russell with a back heel kick. Chad Yaro goes for a front-layout suplex, BUT SHANE RUSSELL
COUNTERS WITH A KNEELIFT! Chris Poleski: Shane Russell throws Chad Yaro out of the ring. RUSSEL JUMPS
AT HIM WITH A FLYING BODY PRESS! Shane Russell whips Chad Yaro into the guardrail. Shane Russell goes for a vertical suplex, but Chad Yaro blocks it.
Chad Yaro hits spinning headscissors on Shane Russell. Chad Yaro whips Shane
Russell into the guardrail. YARO WITH A DOUBLE-UNDERHOOK PILEDRIVER!
Matt Chapen: Chad Yaro whips Shane Russell into the guardrail. Chad Yaro goes for a German suplex, but Shane Russell counters it with a backward kick!
Shane Russell knocks Chad Yaro into the ringsteps. Shane Russell reenters the ring. Shane Russell goes for a clothesline, BUT YARO COUNTERS WITH A BELLY TO
BELLY! FRANKENSTEINER BY THE CHALLENGER, COVER! One... Two... Kickout! Chris Poleski: ANTHONY DAVIS IS COMING TO THE RING! Looks like he wants to check out some CWA action! Chad Yaro goes for a cobra clutch, but Shane
Russell counters it with an armdrag takedown. Shane Russell goes for a clothesline, but Chad Yaro counters it with a hiptoss. In turn, Shane Russell counters it with a lariat. Shane Russell hits Chad Yaro with an elbowdrop.
Shane Russell places Chad Yaro on the turnbuckle. TOP ROPE FRANKENSTEINER! THE
CHAMPION WITH THE COVER! Anthony Davis looks on.
One... Two... Thr...Kickout! Matt Chapen: Shane Russell whips Chad Yaro into the ropes, but Chad Yaro reverses it. YARO WITH A JUMPING ELBOW THRUST!
One... Two... Kickout! ANTHONY DAVIS IS ON THE APRON! Chad Yaro runs at him, but Davis jumps off before Yaro can grab him! SHANE RUSSELL WITH A GERMAN SUPLEX FROM
BEHIND! One... Two... Three!!! Rick Collings: Here is your winner and still CWA Traditional Champion...SHANE RUSSELL!!! Chris Poleski: Shane Russell retains his Traditional Title against Chad Yaro thanks to the assistance of Anthony Davis!
Matt Chapen: What was the meaning behind that? Chad Yaro had that match won! Hopefully we will find out in the weeks to come! When we come back Jasen Casen defends his CWA North American Title against Street Punk!
