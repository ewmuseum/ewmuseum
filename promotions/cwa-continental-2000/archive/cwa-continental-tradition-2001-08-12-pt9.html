CWA (Continental, 2000) — Card

CWA Tradition Part 9

12 August 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
{The camera shows the backstage area where Frat Boy is talking to Larry Gionet and Joe Snow.}
Frat Boy Perry Tanhammer: Hey guys, where were you when I was getting powerbombed to the floor by THP?
{Frat Boy looks at Larry Gionet.} Larry Gionet: Hey man, I was still hurting from my match with Chainsaw Charlie. {Frat Boy shakes his head and looks at Joe Snow.} Frat Boy Perry Tanhammer: And how about you world champ? Joe Snow: Perry I need to give everything I have out there tonight! Frat Boy Perry Tanhammer: Whatever. Well I am out of here guys, the doc want to check my neck.
Joe Snow: Take it easy Perry. Larry Gionet: Later bro! {You see Larry Gionet and Joe Snow chatting as the camera cuts back to Matt
Chapen and Chris Poleski.}
Chris Poleski: What happened there? Matt Chapen: I don't know! It seems that Perry was a little upset over the fact that his friends were not there for him during his match. Whatver the case, this rivalry between Frat Boy and THP has become personal!
Rick Collings: This match is scheduled for one fall and is for the CWA World
Heavyweight Title! Introducing first, the challenger. Hailing from Hilton
Head South Carolina... weighing in at 280 lbs..."The Prospect" CHAD YARO!!!!!
[Alice In Chains's "Man In a Box" blasts as Yaro comes out to a great reception! He hi-5's a few fans on the way to the ring.]
Chris Poleski: It'll take more than a few hi-5's to win the CWA World Title! Yaro's going to learn that the hard way toni... What the? [Suddenly Rage Against the Machine's "Bulls on Parade" kicks in. Chapen and
Poleski look at each other, clearly confused as they start frantically checking through their notes. ]
Matt Chapen: This isn't part of the programme. What's going on? [A platoon of security guards, dressed in black shirts and pants march out to ringside. They surround the ring, each one looking intimidating.]
Chris Poleski: I know that there's always the possibility of fans invading the ring before, but this is overkill!
Rick Collings: And his opponent. The NEW CWA World Heavyweight Champion... JOE SNOW!!!!! ["Bodies" by Drowning Pool plays as Joe Snow comes to the ring with kendo stick in hand and the CWA World Title around his waist as the fans boo him like there is no tomorrow. He looks at the guards curiously as he climbs into the ring and raises the kendo stick high in the air.]
Matt Chapen: This is a great opportunity for Chad Yaro! If he could unthrone
Joe Snow tonight, it would turn the entire federation on its head! Chris Poleski: Yaro's good, but he's the rookie compared to Snow! He doesn't even give Snow a chance to prepare before flying at him... Flying forearm takes
Snow down and Yaro runs into the ropes. Snow flips up and leapfrogs over Yaro who misses with a shoulder tackle! Yaro rebounds off the ropes... and Snow catches him in the face with a pinpoint dropkick!
Matt Chapen: That caught Yaro right in the face, but the youngster gets back up to his feet. Snow quickly applies an armbar, trying to wrench Yaro's arm off! He follows it up with a side kick to the face. Yaro's stunned and Snow whips him into the ropes, but it's reversed... Running Knee by Yaro and Snow goes down!
Chris Poleski: Yaro quickly pulls up Snow and drops him with a brainbuster DDT! Snow's seeing stars and Yaro's going up top. Don't tell me Yaro thinks he can finish Snow off this quickly?
Matt Chapen: I'm sure Yaro know's what he's doing! Remember Snow is a hardcore brawler. The longer this match goes, the more it'll be in Snow's favour.
Yaro leaps off - frogsplash! BUT SNOW GETS HIS KNEES UP!!! Chris Poleski: I knew it! Too soon! Snow's up and he's got a nasty look on his face! He pulls Yaro up by his goatee and slaps his face! Yaro gets whipped into the turnbuckle and Snow follows up with a flying splash! He knees Yaro in the midsection and then... WHAT A CHOP!!! That's gonna leave a mark!
Matt Chapen: Snow now chopping away at Yaro's chest! He's chopping meat and
Yaro's chest is going bright red! Snow now distracting the ref, then headbutts
Yaro square in the face! That might have broken Yaro's nose! Snow hips Yaro into the opposite turnbuckle who hits hard and rebounds... straight into a flying headscissors!
Chris Poleski: Blood's dripping from Yaro's nose as Snow scoops him up... spinning piledriver! Snow's showing the world why he's the new champion! He pulls up
Yaro only to bring him down with a side russian legsweep! Snow now on the second rope, waiting for him to get to his feet. Yaro's on spaghetti legs and Snow leaps over him and AND CATCHES HIM WITH A STUNNER! What a move!
Matt Chapen: Yaro's down and Snow makes the cover... One... Two... Yaro gets a shoulder up. Chris Poleski: Snow's visibly upset! He's stomping a hole through Yaro who's trying to pull himself up using the ropes. Snow's really showing a mean streak here! Its gotta be after years of being held out of the spotlight by Chainsaw
Charlie!
Matt Chapen: What? More like Joe was jealous of Charlie! I'm still disgusted at how Snow could turn his back on the fans, and his longtime friend!
Chris Poleski: It's simple! Joe had to choose between his "friend" and the fans or the gold. No contest! Snow snapmares Yaro out of the corner and follows it up with a kick to the spine! Snow pulls up Yaro by the neck and spins him around! He's got him set up for a suplex... but Yaro manages to block it. Snow tries
again... but Yaro still blocks it! Reversal by Yaro who front suplexes Snow across the top rope!
Matt Chapen: Desperation move by Yaro but it paid off! He follows it up with a guillotine legdrop off the second rope, bringing Snow back into the ring!
Yaro pulls up Snow and drives an elbow into his guts! Snow's doubled up as
Yaro runs into the ropes... scissor's kick catches Snow on the back of the head and he goes down hard!
Chris Poleski: Yaro seizes the moment and rolls him up in a small package... One... Two... Thr... No! Snow kicks out! Matt Chapen: I think Snow is finally realising the serious trouble he's in! Yaro wastes no time pulling him back up and sends Snow shoulder first into a turnbuckle! Snow looks hurt, as Yaro spins him around... Full Nelson Bomb!
That's gotta do it! Yaro hooks the leg... One... Two... Again Snow kicks out! Chris Poleski: Snow's resilient. He's up on his feet and Yaro kicks him in the guts! He whips Snow into the ropes... Clothesline, but Snow ducks it! Snow with his own kick to the gut! He's setting up Yaro for a powerbomb... He nails it... No! Yaro countered into an arm-drag! Both men back to their feet and Yaro whips Snow into the ropes... Spinning heelkick by Snow takes down
Yaro! Snow has Yaro by the legs... he's looking around at the crowd... and stamps down on Yaro's lower abdomen!
Matt Chapen: Snow now sets up Yaro for a slingshot into the turnbuckle! Yaro flies through the air... but blocks it, landing on the second rope. He leaps off backwards, driving an elbow into Snow's midsection! Snow's winded and
Yaro hooks the leg..
One... Two... Thr.. Snow barely managing to get the shoulder up in time! Chris Poleski: Yaro's getting anxious. He scoops up Snow and slams him into the mat! Yaro pulls up Snow and whips him into the ropes...great Japanese armdrag by Yaro! He pulls up Snow and places him over his shoulder... RUNNING
POWERSLAM!! Yaro with another cover... One... Two... What? Yaro just pulled up Snow. He's saying he's not finished with him! Matt Chapen: That was a mistake for sure! Yaro throws Snow into the ropes! Snow rebounds straight into a stinging chop to the chest! Yaro with another chop!
Whooo! And another! He whips Snow into the ropes...Snow ducks a kick and runs into the ropes... clothesline by Snow but Yaro ducks it! Both men run into the ropes... SUPERKICK BY SNOW!!! He caught Yaro right on the chin and dives onto him for the pin... This one might be over!
One... Two... Three! Noooo! Only a two! Chris Poleski: Snow pulls up Yaro... spinning DVD!!! Yaro's down and Snow's climbing the turnbuckle. Looks like he's going for a SnowSault! But Yaro's up!
Yaro's on his feet and nails Snow with an Irish-uppercut! He should be disqualified right there!!!
Matt Chapen: Well the ref didn't see it! Snow got planted on that turnbuckle and now Yaro's up on the second turnbuckle. He's got Snow hooked up... SUPERBACK SUPLEX!!!! This has to be it! If Yaro can cover Snow we've got a new CWA World Champion! Yaro's climbing the opposite turnbuckle. He's signalling for the Chadton Bomb! Hey what the...?
Chris Poleski: It's Larry Gionet!!! He's seen enough and is racing down to ringside! Gionet's the difference maker! He's ready to...Noooo! The security guards are holding him back! Gionet's trying to fight them off but there're too many of them! Eight of the guards hold back Gionet as Yaro dives off the top rope... Chadton Bomb! Chadton Bomb! He nailed it!
Matt Chapen: This one is over! We've got a new CWA World Champion for sure! One... Two... Thre...Noooooo! That was unbelievably close! I don't know how Snow managed to kick out in time!
Chris Poleski: Gionet's making his way back to the ring entrance. He's looking at the ring in shock, shaking his head. I guess his plans have been messed up. Yaro now has Snow up and rocks him backwards with an uppercut! Snow's barely with it and Yaro... HILTON HEAD SPECIAL!!! Snow's down and Yaro smells blood!
Matt Chapen: Yaro scoops up Snow and whips him into the corner! What impact! Snow's back arched and Yaro charges at him... BUT MISSES!!! He collided with the turnbuckle with tremendous speed... he may have dislocated his shoulder with that move! Snow sidestepped him and Yaro's injured. Snow spins him around...SNOW FALL!!!! From outta nowhere - SNOW FALL!!!
Chris Poleski: Snow with the cover... One... Two... Three!!!!!! He did it! I told you he would! Snow's still CWA World Champion! Rick Collings: Ladies and Gentlemen. The winner of the match... And still CWA
World Champion - JOE SNOW!!!!!!!
Matt Chapen: Snow's getting to his feet and is asking for his title! Yaro's still down on the mat. I don't care what you say, that was a very close match! Hey, now those guards are leaving. They're filing out of here single file to the back. Will someone tell me what that's all about?
Chris Poleski: Snow's up on the turnbuckle with his title and kendo stick! He looks very pleased with himself, as well he should be! That was a great title defence.
Matt Chapen: Well its been another crazy night here on Tradition. For Chris
Poleski and myself, thank you and good night!
