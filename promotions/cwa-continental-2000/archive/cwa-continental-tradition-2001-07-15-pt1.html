CWA (Continental, 2000) — Card

CWA Tradition Part 1

15 July 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
{"I Can't Drive 55" by Sammy Hagar plays over the PA system as the fans get fired up for an exciting night of CWA action! The camera then shows Matt
Chapen and Chris Poleski at ringside.}
Matt Chapen: CWA fans welcome to CWA Tradition! We are at the aftermatch of one of the best PPVs in recent memory by the CWA Journey's End! At that spectactular card we saw two titles change hands where Shane Russell defeated
Adam Mitchell in a ladder match for the CWA Traditional title and Jasen Casen defeated The Jester for the CWA North American Championship thanks to Chad
Yaro!
Chris Poleski: But perhaps the greatest travesty of them all, Frat Boy lost his shot at being the CWA World Champion thanks to his ally Larry Gionet!
Matt Chapen: In a somewhat controversial move, CWA President Michael D. Taylor signed the tag team main event tonight putting Larry Gionet and Frat Boy Perry
Tanhammer against Joe Snow and Chainsaw Charlie! The question is who will
co-exist and who will not? Chris Poleski: I don't know but we'll find out tonight! Matt Chapen: I understand later on we will hear from both Joe Snow and Larry
Gioent regarding tonight's main event! This should be a great opening match!
CWA Newcomer Scotty Harrison gets the chance to perform in front of millions of fans, and make a name for himself!
Todd Michaelson: This opening match is scheduled for one fall. Introducing first, making his CWA debut and weighing 289 pounds - SCOTTY HARRISON!!!!!
[The lights in the arena fade and a red strobe begins to flash in the darkness. "Don't Fear the Reaper" by Blue Oyster Cult begins to play throughout the building. A laser from the ceiling shoots into the ring and spells out "Fear the Fate". Finally a large explosion sends a giant fireball in front of the entrance to the locker room. As the flash fades,
Harrison is standing in the entrance with no expression on his face. He makes his way silently to the ring.
As he enters the ring, he walks to the far turnbuckles and sits on the top rope to await his opponent.
Chris Poleski: But he's going up against Adam Mitchell! This guy has already made a name for himself!
Todd Michaelson: And his opponent, weighing 239 pounds - ADAM MITCHELL!!!!! [The lights go out and spotlights race around the arena trying to get a glimpse of Adam Mitchell! Suddenly he appears, rushing through the crowd behind Harrison. He leaps up onto the apron and shoves Harrison off the top rope into the ring before stepping through the ropes!]
Matt Chapen: Sneak attack by Mitchell! Looks like he's not going to go easy on the new guy! He whips Harrison into the ropes - big back bodydrop!
Harrison flips back to his feet, only to get clotheslined back down! That almost took his head off!
Chris Poleski: A great show of aggression by Mitchell! He runs into the ropes... rolling necksnap!! I wonder which part of Harrison's body he's concentrating on!?! Harrison's getting to his feet - and catches Mitchell with a thrust to the throat!
Matt Chapen: Mitchell staggers backwards and Harrison nails him with a right to the head! He follows it up with series of jabs, and drives his knee into
Mitchell's midsection! He whips Mitchell into the ropes - armdrag takedown!
Nicely done there and Harrison follows it up with a legdrop on Mitchell's arm! Harrison's looking pretty good here now and the crowd are slowly warming to him!
Chris Poleski: We'll see! Harrison pulls up Mitchell and twists that arm! Mitchell goes for a shortarms clothesline but Harrison ducks it, and takes
Mitchell down with back suplex! Harrison now on the second rope - Senton bomb connects! He hooks the leg...
One... Tw.. Nooo! Kickout by Adam Mitchell! Matt Chapen: Adam Mitchell does NOT look happy! He gets to his feet, but Harrison's ready for him! He whips Mitchell into the corner turnbuckle and charges in after him! Flying splash connects! Harrison hooks Mitchell by the arms - double underhook suple... no, he uses the double underhook to place
Mitchell on the top rope! What's he going to do now?
Chris Poleski: I don't know but it doesn't look good! Top rope powerbomb!!! Matt Chapen: He calls that the Equalizer! This could be over as Harrison makes the cover! One... Two... Th, Noo! Mitchell gets a shoulder up! Chris Poleski: Of course! Mitchell's got bigger things on his mind in the
CWA than just beating up newcomers! I see lotsa gold in line for Adam
Mitchell in the future! He gets to his feet and barely dodges a side kick from Harrison! Harrison rebounds off the ropes - flying double ax, but
Mitchell counters into a bearhug slam!!! Matt Chapen: That took the wind out of Harrison and he's struggling to get to his feet! Chris Poleski: Mitchell's waiting for him... SPEAR!!! He speared the air out of Harrison and hooks the leg!
One... Two... Harrison barely kicks out! Matt Chapen: Mitchell pulls up Harrison by the neck and twirls him around... and charges Harrison shoulder first into the turnbuckle. He pounds on Harrison in the corner... then whips him into the opposite turnbuckle! Mitchell charges in - but Harrison gets up both feet to Mitchell's face! Mitchell falls backwards onto the mat and Harrison dives on him with a double elbow to the face! He hooks the leg...
One... Kickout by Mitchell! Chris Poleski: Both men get up and start exchanging blows! Mitchell has Harrison by the hair and whips him into the ropes! Dropkick by Mitchell and Harrison's caught on the ropes allowing Mitchell to assault his chest with a series of chops!
Harrison's chest is going bright red from the blows! Matt Chapen: Mitchell runs into the ropes and a flying crossbody sends both men over the top rope to the outside! Harrison took the brunt of that as
Mitchell landed on him! Mitchell gets up first and whips Harrison hard into the guard rail! Harrison's doubled over in agony, as Mitchell whips him straight into the ring steps! What impact!
Chris Poleski: Mitchell now rolls Harrison back into the ring and then jumps up onto the apron. Springboard elbow drop hits the mark and Mitchell hooks the leg! He's got him now!
One... Two... Three! Nooooo! Harrison powered out! Where'd he get the strength to do that? Matt Chapen: Scotty Harrison is showing a lot of promise here! Mitchell pulls him back up and DDTs him hard into the mat!
Mitchell now on the second rope waiting for Harrison to get to his feet... Flying bulldog connects! Mitchell runs into the ropes, leaps over Harrison's body into the other ropes - Springboard Moonsault!!!
Chris Poleski: BUT HARRISON GETS THE KNEES UP!!! Dammit! I thought Mitchell had him there, but Harrison got his knees up in desperation and caught
Mitchell in the chest! Mitchell's rolling around the ring in agony... AND HARRISON SINCHES ON A FULL NELSON BODY SCISSORS! Matt Chapen: He calls it the Injection!!! And Mitchell's getting an Injection of PAIN right here!!! He's gonna tap! He's gonna tap! He's... Noooo! Mitchell makes it to the ropes and the ref's calling for the break!!! Harrison won't break the hold and the ref's starting to count... One... Two... Three... Fou... Finally he breaks the hold!!! Chris Poleski: He should be disqualified anyway!!! Matt Chapen: Yeah right! Harrison pulls up an injured Mitchell and piledrives him into the mat! What else has Harrison got in mind for
Mitchell? He scoops up Mitchell and slams him hard into the mat! He scoops him up again and places him over his shoulder! Harrison now running round the ring with Mitchell over his shoulder!!! He's gonna slam him in the...
Noooo! Mitchell slips out the back door! Harrison turns around and... Chris Poleski: CRYSTAL METH!!! CRYSTALL METH!!! There it is!!! From outta nowhere, Adam Mitchell nails his finisher and hooks the leg!
One... Two... THREE!!!!!!! HE GOT HIM!!! Matt Chapen: Scotty Harrison never saw it coming!!! Well I don't care what you say Chris, that was still a damn impressive debut by Scotty Harrison! He may not have the experience yet but he makes up for it with intensity and determination! I expect we'll see great things from him in the future...
Chris Poleski: Shut up Chapen! Mitchell's got something to say! Adam Mitchell: At Journey's End, I had the chance to become the CWA Traditional Champion since Chad Yaro and The Jester didn't have enough balls to show up for their own damn title match! You wanna know why I lost? It wasn't because I don't have the talent because I have that. It sure as hell wasn't because I wasn't strong enough because I could break his back in two if I wanted to. It was for the mere fact that he had to shove me off the ladder to the outside where I went through a table! I only got a little banged up but I could've been crippled out there! Does trying to cripple people make you more of a man Shane? Does trying to end a man's career just so you can proclaim yourself as champion make you feel like some tough guy? Let me give you a reality check my friend, you are nothing but a piece of shit in my book! You couldn't beat me on my worst day let along my best. So I propose to you this
Russell, you and me one on one for the last time at the July 28th PPV Blood
Sweat and Tears! Let's find out and see if you truly are the man that you say you are and accept my challenge!
Matt Chapen: Adam Mitchell looked very serious and emotionally driven in that interview! Shane Russell didn't try to cripple him!
Chris Poleski: How do you know Chapen? Have you even been put through a table? Matt Chapen: No I haven't! Chris Poleski: Alright then! Matt Chapen: Fans we will be back after these messages!
