CWA (Continental, 2000) — Card

CWA Tradition Part 3

12 August 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Matt Chapen: Welcome back fans and we had a pier six brawl on our hands! Chris Poleski: That was a wild and crazy brawl! Damien and Street Punk were going at it while Jasen Casen and The Assasin duked it out!
Rick Collings: CWA fans welcome our special guest commentator...SHANE RUSSELL!!! {Shane Russell comes to the announce with the CWA Traditional Title as the fans cheer him on.}
Matt Chapen: We welcome the CWA Traditonal Champion here at ringside! Shane Russell: Thanks its a pleasure to be here! Rick Collings: This triple threat match is schedueled for one fall with a 30 minute time limit! First coming down the aisle, TPO...THE PSYCHOTIC ONE!!!
{"Thunderstruck" by AC/DC plays as TPO comes to the ring to a chorus of boos from the crowd.}
Matt Chapen: TPO lost his match at the PPV and tonight he looks to do anything to get a victory over Fireside!
Shane Russell: He is a real dangerous man and will do whatever it takes like you said Matt to get a victory!
Rick Collings: And his opponent...ACE FIRESIDE!!! {"Princes of The Universe" by Queen plays as Ace Fireside makes his way to the ring to a round of cheers from the crowd. With a mic in hand he looks to the crowd.}
Matt Chapen: He is gonna speak? Chris Poleski: I guess so! {Ace Fireside steps up to the mike as the crowd cheers loudly, he looks out to the crowd and smiles as he raises his arms, than the smile fades.}
Ace Fireside: I found out something tonight that a lot of you fans more than likely didn't know..I am an undefeated since I went single here in the CWA, but I don't care about records all I care about is giving 100% in that ring for you fans...The wins will come and tonight I face TPO one more time, but
I already proved what a non-threat he is! That brings me to Adam
Mitchell..lets see how big you are facing me face to face, then we will see who the best wrestler is when I put you in the Firesidebomb it will fel so good, to me anyway...See you in the ring punks...Want some Come get some!!!!
Matt Chapen: Fireside looks ready to me! Rick Collings: And their opponent...ADAM MITCHELL!!! Matt Chapen: Tieup by TPO and Mitchell. Ace Fireside with a kick to TPO and one on Adam Mitchell for good measure! Ace Fireside chopping Adam Mitchell in the corner. Fireside with an armtwist and irish whips him into the turnbuckle, but he reverses it! Mitchell bounces back, Fireside jumps towards him but Mitchell catches him and he lands on the top rope. HIGH CROSS BODY ON
TPO! NICELY DONE BY FIRESIDE! One... Two... Kickout! Chris Poleski: Adam Mitchell throws Fireside in the air, BUT FIRESIDEDROPKICKS
HIM SENDING HIM TO THE OUTSIDE! Its now Fireside and TPO in the ring. These two fought at Blood Sweat and Tears where Ace picked up the victory. Fireside with a headlock, but TPO throws him into the ropes! Shoulderblock by The
Psychotic One. TPO into the ropes again, Ace with a leapfrog. Ace Fireside ducks the clothesline, goes for a hurrancanrana, BUT TPO CATCHES HIM INTO A
POWERBOMB! NICE! One... Two... Shoudler up! Shane Russell: That was a rookie mistake by TPO by not hooking the legs! He could have had the victory there!
Chris Poleski: Quit you! Shane Russell: Don't make me come over there Poleski! Matt Chapen: Both of you stop it! Adam Mitchell finally coming back into the ring. He just did some smart thinking earlier. He stayed to the outside while
Fireside and TPO duked it out. Now he is the more refreshed of the three which gives him a huge advantage! Adam on the apron AND HITS A SOMMERSAULT LEGDROP
ON FIRESIDE! Mitchell with the cover!
One... Two... Kickout! Adam Mitchell clamps on the figure four! Ace Fireside is screaming in pain. TPO is just watching this!
Chris Poleski: UH OH! Not anymore! TPO picks up Adam Mitchell by the head and throws him to the outside. TPO stomping away at Fireside. He is still bitter about his loss last sunday and who wouldn't be! TPO rams Ace's head into the lower right turnbuckle. He whips him into the other side. TPO placing Ace Fireside onto the top turnbuckle!
Matt Chapen: This is not looking good for Ace Fireside! TPO pounding on Ace, but Fireside is firing back with some right hands KNOCKING TPO DOWN!
Fireside is looking at Mitchell! ACE FIRESIDE WITH A SOMMERSAULT PLANCHA TO
THE OUTSIDE ON ADAM MITCHELL! Listen to this crowd they are going crazy! They love this action!
Chris Poleski: They just can't get enough Matt, they love the CWA! Fireside and Mitchell and brawling at ringside. TPO is going up on the top rope. What in the hell? TPO GOES FLYING OFF THE TOP WITH A DOUBLE CLOTHESLINE! Ace
Fireside and Adam Mitchell got knocked off their feet!
Shane Russell: I may not like this kids attitude but The Psychotic One is one strong, powerful guy that should go far here in the CWA!
Matt Chapen: Man the bodies, are flying everywhere here at the Gund Arena! Ace
Fireside picking himself up with the guardrail. TPO whips Adam Mitchell into
Fireside. The double teaming continues. Fireside ducks AND ADAM MITCHELL GETS
BACK BODY DROPPED OVER THE GUARDRAIL! TPO running at Fireside. Ace ducks before hand and gets a kick to the face! TPO saw that coming for sure! TPO throws him back in!
Chris Poleski: Adam Mitchell is climbing his way out of the folded chairs at ringside and is crawling back into the ring! TPO looking to finish off Ace
Fireside with the powerbomb! MITCHELL WITH A DROPKICK TO THE KNEE OF TPO!
FIRESIDE LANDS ON THE PSYCHOTIC ONE! One... Two... Thr...MITCHELL BREAKS IT UP! Fireside is not happy about this. Mitchell whips
Fireside into the ropes, but Ace reverses it INTO A TILT-A-WIRL BACKBREAKER!
One... Two... TPO breaks up the count with a stomp to the head! Matt Chapen: TPO with a clothesline to the back of Fireside! Mitchell gets up and gets a boot to the face for his troubles! Both men trying to get up BUT
GET DOUBLE CLOTHESLINED UP AND OVER THE TOP ROPE! This TPO is a monster!
Chris Poleski: You damn right he is! Mitchell and Fireside are brawling right next to us Matt and TPO is laughing his ass off! He is letting those two beat the living hell out of each other while he stays refereshed. That's the tactic of a very smart man Chapen!
Matt Chapen: I can't argue with you there! Ace Fireside is getting the advantage over Mitchell! FIRESIDE RAMS MITCHELL'S HEAD INTO OUR ANNOUNCERS
TABLE! Ace Fireside is on our table telling Mitchell to bring it on! OH UH!
TPO IS COMING OUT OF THE RING! Mitchell with a right hand. Fireside responds with a right hand! Ace with another right! TPO coming onto the table! He runs at Ace BUT FIRESIDE DUCKS THE CLOTHESLINE AND HE HITS MITCHELL! Mitchell just went flying off the table!
Chris Poleski: TPO turns around... {CRASH!!!} Matt Chapen: FIRESIDE BOMB THROUGH THE TABLE! Fireside has looked to have hurt himself! As the table crashed, his head hit one of the monitors! Both men seem to be knocked out! Adam Mitchell finally getting back to his feet and looks at the carnage! Mitchell is trying to pick up Ace Fireside but he is dead wood right now! Mitchell dragging Ace Fireside's limp body from the wreckage and throws him back into the ring!
Chris Poleski: TPO is slowly showing signs of life! He is starting to roll over towards the apron! ADAM MITCHELL MAKES THE COVER!
One... Two... Thr...TPO PULLED HIM OUT! HE JUST THREW MITCHELL INTO THOSE STEEL STEPS AND
ROLLS BACK INTO THE RING! TPO hooks the leg of Ace Fireside!
One... Two... Three!!! Rick Collings: Here is your winner...THE PSYCHOTIC ONE!!! {"Thunderstruck" by AC/DC plays as TPO has his hand raised in victory and spits on the fallen Ace Fireside.}
Matt Chapen: That was a great matchup! Shane Russell: No doubt about it! Although TPO showed little respect to Ace
Fireside just a few moments ago!
Matt Chapen: Oh I agree completely! Chris Poleski: Bunch of pansy-asses! Matt Chapen: Whatever you say Chris! We have to take a break!
