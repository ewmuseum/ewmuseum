CWA Tradition Part 6

13 November 2001

Chris Poleski: Oh this is going to be history in the making... Kevin Candidio is about to become world Champion...
Matt Chapen: Well there is the bell and this one is under way...They lock up and Kevin with a rake to the eyes for the advantage... Kevin sends Chad into the ropes and nails a backelbow... Kevin quickly off the ropes and drops a leg right over the face of the Champion... quick cover!
One Two Chris Poleski: The Champ out on two, I think Kevin needs to keep that up if he is to win...
Matt Chapen: I agree and i can only hope that neither Shane nor Rodney Wright will get involed...Kevin picks Chad up but is nailed with a right hand, the
Champion came to fight that is for sure... Chad with another right that sends
Kevin into the corner... Chad charges into the corner, but Kevin puts his boot up and Chad runs right into it face first... Chad stumbles out and now Kevin charges... Chad with a power slam!
Chris Poleski: Cover! One Two Matt Chapen: Kevin kicks out on two and Shane Russell was about to make a save...The ref letting Shane know has to stay on the outside.. Chad in control now as he picks Kevin up and sets him up and nails a belly to belly suplex!
Chris Poleski: This is not looking good for Kevin...Chad picks Kevin up and nails a neckbreaker! Chad now stomping and kicking on the back of Kevin's head... He must be setting him up for the Hilton Head Special!
Matt Chapen: I think your right and Kevin back to his feet and thrown hard into the corner... this time Chad Charges and nails a spear as Kevin had no where to go....Kevin stumbles out of the corner and lands face first in the middle of the ring, and this crowd is loving every bit of it...
Chris Poleski: What do these idiots know? Kevin has him right were he wants him... Matt Chapen: Chad in complete control picks Kevin up, SMALL PACKAGE! One Two Thr... Chris Poleski: Oh come on ref count to three! He had him! Matt Chapen: I thought he might have gotten him also but he Champion kicked out and is not kicking the hell out of Kevin Candidio...Mad Dog looking on with a smile on one side of the ring as both Shane Russell and Rodney Wright do not look so happy... Chad picks Kevin up and sends him into the ropes, Kevin reverses it and Chad goes into the ropes, then falls flat on his face as he comes of them... What the hell? Chad gets up and begins to yell at Shane
Russell who nails him with a right hand...
(At this the crowd erupts as Mad Dog runs around the other side and beginst to nail Shane with rights and lefts... Mad Dog grabs whips him into the Steel post, but Shane reverses it and send Mad Dog right into a very stiff side kick from Rodney Wright...They both pick Mad Dog up and Power bomb him through the anoucers table!)
Chris Poleski: My Gawd Mad Dog just got powerbomb through out table! Matt Chapen: and inside the ring Kevin is fighting back as both men exchange rights and lefts... Kevin getting the best of the two send Chad into the corner, reversal by Kevin and Chad goes into the opposite corner right in to the ref!
Chris Poleski: And the ref is out cold, Uh oh Shane in the ring and charge
Chad, Chad with a belly to belly suplex on Shane... Kevin gets up and another belly to belly suplex...
Matt Chapen: The World Champion holding his own, and Oh man Rodney Wright with a spin buster just as Chad turned around!
Chris Poleski: Oh this is great theTTBA'S rule man... Matt Chapen: And now the triple team is on as Shane picks Chad up with a huge choke slam! Rodney on the outside now and what the hell!
Chris Poleski: Oh this is getting two good, Rodney with a table as Kevin goes to the top rope!
(With Kevin on the top rope, Rodney Wright and Shane Set up and place the World
Champion on a table... Kevin then nails the "Star Killer"! Shane drags the ref into postion as Rodney clears the table...The crowd is booing like no tomorrow)
One Matt Chapen: No not like this! Two Three! Rick Collings: Here is your winner and NEW CWA WORLD CHAMPION KEVIN CANDIDIO!! Chris Poleski: And they are not done! As all three now kick and stomp at the former Champion.....
Matt Chapen: This is really getting out of hand...Kevin now with a chair across the head of Chad...
WHACK! Chris Poleski: CHAD YARO JUST GOT NAILED WITH THAT CHAIR! HERE COMES JOE
SNOW! HE IS POUNDING AWAY AT THE WORLD CHAMPION! Get off of him!
Matt Chapen: Snow tearing apart Candido! HERE COMES RODNEY RIGHT FROM BEHIND! He whips Joe Snow into the ropes and nails him with a big boot to the face! Rodney Wright picks him up and throws him to the outside. Chris Poleski: Russell back into the ring now BUT LARRY GIONET COMES IN FROM
BEHIND! Gionet with a right hand AND ANOTHER ONE! Larry Gionet goes for a clothesline, but the North American Champion ducks and PLANTS HIM WITH A
SPINEBUSTER! Matt Chapen: TTBA seems to be in control! What the? TANHAMMER IS UP ON THE
TOP ROPE! WHERE DID HE COME FROM! Tanhammer off the top with a FLYING
CLOTHESLINE ON THE CHAMPION! RIGHT HAND ON RUSSELL! LEFT HAND TO WRIGHT! He runs into the ropes, Wright and Russell duck the clothesline. DOUBLE
CLOTHESLINE SENDING TANHAMMER UP AND OVER THE TOP! Shane Russell helping the newly crowned CWA World Champion to his feet. Fans we are out of time. For
Rick Collings, Todd Michaelson and Chris Poleski, this is Matt Chapen saying goodnight everyone!
