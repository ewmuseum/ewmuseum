CWA (Continental, 2000) — Card

CWA Tradition Part 2

13 November 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Matt Chapen: Welcome back fans and we just witnessed a great 3 way dance between A.J. Darkk, Johnny Kyler and Jeff Kane. Jeff Kane put A.J. Darkk away with a brainbuster then Kyler sealed the win with a small package on Kane!
Chris Poleski: After that contest all hell broke loose between Ace Fireside,
A.J. Darkk, Jeff Kane and Rick Taylor!
Matt Chapen: Indeed it has. It has gotten very personal between these 4 men! I am being told that Todd Michaelson is in the back with our new CWA superstar
"Shotgun" Shane Valentine!
{The camera shows Todd Michaelson with CWA signee Shotgun Shane Valentine.}
Todd Michaelson: Shane Valentine on behalf of myself and the CWA I would like to welcome you to the Continental Wrestling Alliance!
"Shotgun" Shane Valentine: Thanks Todd, its great to be here! Todd Michaelson: I gotta ask you, what are your long term plans here? "Shotgun" Shane Valentine: Well just like every aspiring wrestler in this business I want to win gold but I know I have to prove my worth here in the CWA and just take it one step at a time.
Todd Michaelson: Very well said, now Shane, I gotta ask... {Before Todd Michaelson can finish his question, Johnny Kyler bursts into the room. Shane Valentine and Todd Michaelson look annoyed.}
Todd Michaelson: What... Johnny Valentine: Shut up! Only talk when you are spoken to Todd! Now Todd, why are you not interviewing me? I just won a 3 way dance out there and I too am a superstar in the making!
Todd Michaelson: Well I was told by CWA President Michael D. Taylor to interview Shane Valentine.
Shotgun Shane Valentine: Yeah man, you'll get your time dude. So please leave so I can have mine.
{Johnny Kyler looks fustrated but calms down a little bit.} Johnny Kyler: Fine man, I'll go. {Johnny Kyler starts walking out of the room but then turns around and attacks
Shane Valentine from behind. He is stomping away at Valentine who is trying to defend himself! Officials are scrambing to get Kyler off of Shane Valentine.}
Johnny Kyler: Wait for my time huh? I DON'T THINK SO! I make my own damn time!
{Johnny Kyler storms out of the room as the officals check on Shane Valentine. The camera zooms back to Matt Chapen and Chris Poleski.} Matt Chapen: What was the meaning of that? Chris Poleski: He wanted some of the spotlight too, but NO Shane Valentine was trying to hog it!
Matt Chapen: No he wasn't, he just got here! Rick Collings: This contest is schedueled for one fall with a 30 minute time limit! First coming down the aisle..."AGENT" RODNEY WRIGHT!!!
{"Thunderstruck" by AC/DC plays as Agent Rodney Wright comes to the ring being accompanied to the ring by Kevin Candido and Shane Russell as the fans boo him like there is no tomorrow.}
Rick Collings: And his opponent...JOE SNOW!!! {"Heretic Anthem" by Slipknot plays as Joe Snow comes to the ring with the signapore cane in his hand. He climbs to the top turnbuckle raises the cane high in the air and flips off the crowd.}
Matt Chapen: Here we go as Wright and Joe lock up and Wright showing his power as he just threw Snow across the ring.
Chris Poleski: No way Joe wants to traded power with this man, he will need to use his speed if he wants any chance of winning.
Matt Chapen: Well with the NA Champion Shane Russell and Kevin Candido at ringside he may need more then that.. They lock up again this time it's Wright with a poke to the eyes right in front of the ref to, now Wright with a pick up and a big slam...Wright off the ropes and drops an elbow, but Snow moves out of the way... Both men back to their feet, and Snow with some rights and lefts sending Wright back to the ropes..Snow sends Wright for the ride, Wright comes back and is nailed right in the face with a drop kick... He better check his teeth.. and Snow going to work with boots to the back of the head of Wright...
Chris Poleski: Wright needs to keep the pace slow, he can't match speed with
Snow... Matt Chapen: Snow picks him up and slams him into the corner, Snow with right hands, Snow sends Wright to the opposite corner, Wright charges out and runs right through Snow...
Chris Poleski: That is the power this man brings to the TTBA... I want to know who is going to stop this group?
Matt Chapen: I am not sure anybody can right now, Wright now picks Joe up and tosses him to the outside... Wright with the ref as Shane Russell pick Joe up and drops him over the rail... Kevin now sends him hard into the steel steps!
come on this is not fare at all, no way Joe can beat three men! Chris Poleski: Russell tosses him back in and the ref didn't see a think, I love these guys...
Matt Chapen: Yea go ahead and kiss up now, Wright with the easy cover! One... Two... Thre... Matt Chapen: He kicked out! Chris Poleski: What! How! Matt Chapen: I don't know but Wright is letting the ref know he did not like that count...Wright now picks Joe and sends him into the ropes, Joe reversas it and send Wright into the ropes, Snow with a clothesline takes Wright down...
Snow off the ropes again, and nails Wright just as he gets to his feet... Wright in trouble... Chris Poleski: And Russell and Candidio are not happy at all... Matt Chapen: Snow with the crowd behind him comes off the ropes, Wright sides steps him and sends him over the top rope to the floor...the ref over to check on him and here come Wright causeing the ref to push him back and Candido going to work on Snow once again... come on!
Chris Poleski: Snow agian sent head first into the steel steps... Russell picks
Snow up slides him into the ring ... I think Snow is out cold...
Matt Chapen: Well Wrigth who picks him up and places him on the top turnbuckle,
Oh man a hard right and if he wasn't out he is now.. Wright climbs up and sets up a superplex!
Chris Poleski: No a top rope DDT! One... Two... Three!!! Rick Collings: Here is your winner "Agent" Rodney Wright! {"Thunderstruck" by AC/DC plays again as the crowd boos as both Shane Russell and Kevin Candido enter the ring.}
Chris Poleski: What a huge win for "Agent" Rodney Wright... Matt Chapen: Are you kidding? It took three men to beat Joe Snow..This happens in out main event and Kevin Candido will win the World Title... Don't go away fans we will be right back.
