CWA Tradition Part 4

12 August 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Matt Chapen: Fans, we are back and Shane Russell is still with us at ringside to give us his comments on tonight's action!
Rick Collings: This contest is schedueled for one fall! First coming down the aisle...A.J. DARKK!!!
{"Dream On" by Aerosmith plays as A.J. Darkk comes to the ring as the fans boo him.} Shane Russell: This is one athletic kid, but I think his ego can get into his head at times and that doesn't make a good champion!
Chris Poleski: Oh pipe down, I bet Darkk could beat you on his worst day! Shane Russell: Is that so jackass? Chris Poleski: Excuse me? Matt Chapen: Guys please! We have a match! Rick Collings: And his opponent...KEVIN CANDIDO!!! {"Battleflag" by Lo-Fidelity Allstars plays as Kevin Candido makes his way to the ring to a round of cheers from the crowd.}
Matt Chapen: Both men staring each other down. Collar-arm tieup and Kevin
Candido hits a japanesse armdrag. A.J. Darkk rolls to the outside. A.J. Darkk making Kevin Candido wait for him, and that could be a smart move on his part.
Chris Poleski: That's right, make him aggitated so much that he loses his focus at hand! Darkk rolls back in. Both men in a test of strength.
Arm-winger by Darkk. Kevin Candido trying to gain some speed to get out of the move, but A.J. Darkk takes him back down! Its Darkk's ring experience that is keeping A.J. in control of this matchup!
Matt Chapen: A.J. wrenching on that hammerlock. Candido grabs the head of
Darkk and hits him with a snapmare takedown. Both men on their feet. Kevin
Candido runs into the ropes but Darkk knocks him down with a clothesline!
Darkk grabbing the leg of Kevin Candido, but Candido kicks him off. Kevin
Candido would have none of it!
Shane Russell: Kevin Candido has been in the CWA from the start and he knows what he is doing in that ring!
Chris Poleski: Kevin Candido off the ropes AND HITS DARKK WITH A JUMPING DDT! One... Two... Kickout! Candido picks up A.J. Darkk, but Darkk nails him with a low blow! A.J. Darkk hooks the arm and hits a vertical suplex. One... Two... Shoulder up! Matt Chapen: Kevin Candido back up, but Darkk nails him with a dropkick and
Candido rolls to the outside! A.J. telling Candido to come get some! Kevin
Candido comes back in and gets nailed with a spinebuster! A.J. Darkk drops the leg. A.J. hooks the leg!
One... Two... Kickout! Chris Poleski: Come on ref that was a three! That was a slow count and you know it! Where does Taylor get these referees? A.J. Darkk whips Candido into the corner. Darkk goes for a flying forearm, BUT CANDIDO MOVES OUT OF THE WAY!
A.J. Darkk bounces off that turnbuckle like a basketball INTO A CANDIDO BELLY
TO BACK SUPLEX! Cover!
One... Two... Shoulder Up! A.J. Darkk grabbing onto the rope to pull himself back onto his feet. But here comes trouble because Candido is running at him! A.J. ducks and backbody drops him over the top, but Candido lands on his feet!
Matt Chapen: What balance by Candido! A.J. Darkk following Kevin Candido on the apron. Candido with a right hand knocking him down on one knee! A.J.
Darkk retaliates with a left jab! Candido with a vicious chop. A.J. Darkk with a clotheline! Kevin Candido is near that corner post which could spell trouble! A.J. Darkk runs at Candido, BUT CANDIDO GETS HIS FEET UP! A.J.'s head connected with the top turnbuckle! Kevin Candido grabs the visibly hurt
A.J. Darkk and throws him back into the ring! Looks like he is signaling for it! He is up on top! HE JUST HIT THE STAR KILLER! THE SHOOTING STAR PRESS!
He nailed the star killer on Darkk! Candido with the cover and he hooks the legs! One... Two... Three!!! Rick Collings: Here is your winner...KEVIN CANDIDO!!! {"Battleflag" by Lo-Fidelity Allstars plays as Kevin Candido has his hand raised in victory.}
Chris Poleski: Darkk put up one hell of a fight but just came up a little short! Candido was able to hit the Star Killer for the victory!
Matt Chapen: But what is the meaning of this? CANDIDO IS STOMPING AWAY AT
A.J. DARKK! He already won the match damnit!
Shane Russell: That's it! Matt Chapen: Shane Russell just threw his headset down AND CHARGED INTO THE
RING! Shane Russell just shoved Kevin Candido off of A.J. Darkk! KEVIN
CANDIDO CAN'T BELIEVE IT! He shoves Russell. RUSSELL SHOVES BACK! Chris Poleski: Uh oh! This is gonna be trouble! Both men yelling at one another! A.J. Darkk is back on his feet AND DROPKICKS THE KNEE OF SHANE
RUSSELL! YES! Matt Chapen: What in the hell? Shane Russell just saved Darkk from getting beat on and this is the thanks he gets? Candido and Darkk taking out the CWA
Traditional Champion with stomps to the head! Its was a damn setup! This is not right damnit!
{"Iron Man" by Black Sabbath plays as Street Punk runs to the ring as the fans go nuts.} Chris Poleski: WHAT THE HELL?! Matt Chapen: STREET PUNK HAS CHARED THE RING AND IS TAKING THEM BOTH ON! Candido gets hit with a clothesline! Darkk gets clotheslined for his trouble! Candido blocks a clothesline by Punk and dropkicks the blood angel in the face! He leans towards the ropes. Candido and Darkk whip Street Punk into the ropes and nail him with a double clothesline!
Chris Poleski: Shane Russell is getting up! He charges at A.J. Darkk and clobbers him from behind! Oh come on what did he ever do to him huh?
Matt Chapen: He just turned on him thats what he did! Russell and Darkk slugging it out in the ring while Candido is taking it to Punk! Kevin Candido is picking up punk! Oh no! POWERBOMB ONTO THE TURNBUCKLE! Strret Punk just got nailed. Kevin Candido getting some bitter sweet revenge to what happened at the PPV in his own little way by taking it out on Punk! A.J. Darkk just got the advantage and clotheslined BOTH HIM AND RUSSELL OVER THE TOP ROPE! This place is just crazy! We will be back fans!
