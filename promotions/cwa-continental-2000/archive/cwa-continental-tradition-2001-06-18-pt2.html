CWA (Continental, 2000) — Card

CWA Tradition Part 2

18 June 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Matt Chapen: We are back fans. If you have just joined us, Chainsaw Charlie was brutally attacked by Frat Boy before even getting into the arena.
Chris Poleski: Yeah thats what he gets, by screwing Frat Boy out of his championship! Matt Chapen: He never did such a thing! Todd Michaelson is in the backstage area. {The camera shows Todd Michaelson in the back with a microphone in hand.}
Todd Michaelson: Fans I am in the back approaching the locker room of the CWA
World Champion Chainsaw Charlie. I will try to get a word with the champion in just a minute.
{Todd Michaelson knocks on Chainsaw Charlie's locker room door.} Chainsaw Charlie: Its open. {Todd Michaelson opens the door to see Chainsaw Charlie sitting on a chair with his head bandaged up.}
Todd Michaelson: This might not be the best time but, you were just attacked by Chainsaw Charlie as the show began, what are your thoughts?
{Chainsaw Charlie gives Todd Michaelson a crazed look. He picks up the chair he was sitting on and throws it against the locker.}
Chainsaw Charlie: You wanna know how I feel huh? Well I feel a little pissed off. Tanhammer your time's up and I have a little proposition for you when I get in that ring!
Matt Chapen: CHAINSAW CHARLIE IS COMING TO THE RING! {Chainsaw Charlie storms out of the room and it shows him heading to the enterance way. As he goes through the curtain, "One Step Closer" by Linkin
Park plays as Chainsaw Charlie makes his way to the ring to a roar of cheers from the crowd. He has a mic in hand.}
Chris Poleski: Chainsaw Charlie does not look like a happy man right now! Chainsaw Charlie: TANHAMMER, I like everyone else am growing tired of your little mind games! What you did to me earlier tonight put me one step closer to the edge my friend! If you thought you were safe from me before YOU'RE DEAD
WRONG! You should have thought twice about blindsiding me Perry, for now your days are numbered and your time is almost up! Frat Boy, I know you can hear the sound of my voice, so why don't you come out here right now, you will want to hear what I have to say!
Matt Chapen: What is he talking about? Chris Poleski: Who knows, he has taken one to many shots to the head! {"Mr. Speed" by KISS plays as Frat Boy Perry Tanhammer walks to the ring as the fans boo him like there is no tomorrow. He has a smile on his face.}
Frat Boy Perry Tanhammer: Alright, I know what this is about. Since I Perry
Tanhammer beat you within an inch of your life, you are handing me the CWA
World Title due to injury!
Matt Chapen: WHAT?! I don't think so! Chris Poleski: I bet that is exactly why! Chainsaw Charlie: How can a man so devious be so stupid! You see, you may have beaten me badly out there and got me bandaged up, but it looks like you didn't get the damn job done now did ya?
{The fans pop huge.} Chainsaw Charlie: Perry, this is about the CWA World Title match you and I will have at the PPV. You know I was thinking, sure we can have a regular match at the PPV, but I say we need to top that classic encounter we had at
Price of Glory! I did a lot of thinking and since you seem to like to take me out and leave me for dead, why don't we have ourselves a last man standing match for the world title!
Chris Poleski: Oh my God! Chainsaw Charlie: What do you say Perry, its up to you! {Frat Boy Perry Tanhammer is looking at the fans in disgust.} Frat Boy Perry Tanhammer: What do you think this is a duel to the death? Everybody knows that I am the most technically sound athlete in the whole CWA! I don't condone such violent acts in a wrestling match! {The crowd starts chanting: BULLSHIT! BULLSHIT! BULLSHIT!} Chainsaw Charlie: Well, it looks like the "most controversial man in America" doesn't want his title shot! How many people think that Perry Tanhammer is nothing but a piece of chicken shit!
{The fans cheer in approval.} Frat Boy Perry Tanhammer: You know the fans were right for once, this is bullshit! For me to stoop so low is outrageous, but I will do whatever it takes to win back my title! So you know what you got your match! When you are down on the mat staring at the celling when the ref counts down your shoulders for the one, two, three you will know why they call me the real world champion!
{"Mr. Speed" by KISS plays as Frat Boy Perry Tanhammer drops the mic and leaves the ring as Chainsaw Charlie looks on.}
Chris Poleski: A very hesitant Frat Boy accepts the challenge of Chainsaw
Charlie! Matt Chapen: That will no doubt tear down the house live on PPV! We will be back!
