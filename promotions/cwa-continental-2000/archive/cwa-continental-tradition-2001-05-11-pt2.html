CWA (Continental, 2000) — Card

CWA Tradition Part 2

11 May 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Matt Chapen: Fans, we saw a great match between Shane Russell and Hia Tao where Russell got the victory!
Chris Poleski: Yes and Hia Tao would set up Mitchell by challenging him to come to the ring as he came to ringside it was great! Damien attacked him from behind and Shane Russell brawled with Hia Tao! It was wild!
Matt Chapen: If this is any indication of what the night will be like, we are in for a crazy night of action! Up next we have The Mad Hatter vs Street Punk, this one should be nuts!
Chris Poleski: Yeah both of these guys are complete psychos. Rick Collings: This contest is schedueled for one fall with a 30 minute time limit! First coming down the aisle...STREET PUNK!!!
{"Iron Man" by Black Sabbath plays as Street Punk makes his way to the ring with his signapore in hand as the fans boo.}
Matt Chapen: Punk looks ready for this battle. :::"The Thing That Should Not Be" plays over the PA system. The arena goes dark as a spotlight shines down on the entrance and in that spotlight stands a man nearly completely enveloped in shadow. He is wearing a large black trenchcoat and a black top hat. He raises his eyes at the ring and begins to walk towards it. The fans reach out for him but he doesn't even acknowledge their existence. He walks up the stairs and into the ring and stares at his
opponent::: Chris Poleski: My...what a intriguing entrance. Matt Chapen: Caught my attention. Chris Poleski: Definitely Matt Chapen: There's the bell and already Street Punk attacking Hatter!!! :::Street Punk begins to pound on Hatter who hasn't even removed his trench coat. Punk throws Hatter into the corner and starts nailing him with viscous rights and lefts. Hatter trying to block as best he can. Punk continuing his assault before Hatter grabs him by his throat with both hands steps forwards and turns and throws Punk by his throat into the opposite corner. Punk lands on his back and sits up quickly looking at Hatter like he just slapped his mother. Hatter stares at Punk smiling an evil smile. He removes his trench coat and throws it over the top rope. Never taking his eyes off of Punk.
Hatter crouches a bit and raises his hand. He signals for Punk to bring it on:: Matt Chapen: A lot of history between these two men. Chris Poleski: I wouldn't be surprised if there were a few deaths between these two men. ::Punk gets back to his feet and charges Hatter, the two men start battling back and forth. Throwing rights and lefts as the crowd gets into it. Both men pummeling each other back and forth all over the ring. Finally Punk grabs Hatter and throws him against the ropes. Hatter bounces back and Punk throws a clothesline but Hatter ducks. Hatter bounces off the opposite ropes and nails Punk with a flying clothesline of his own. Hatter hops on Punk and begins to beat him with his two fists. The ref signals Hatter to get off but he doesn't Finally Punk gets his two feet up and kicks Hatter right in the chest. Hatter topples back and Punk quickly gets to his feet and spears
Hatter to the mat. Street Punk now pummeling Hatter:: Chris Poleski: Is this even a wrestling match? Matt Chapen: This is a straight up ass whoopin that's what this is. ::Hatter pushes Punk off of him and clips Punk's knee. Punk goes down to one knee as Hatter gets back up. Punk up now fighting through the pain. Hatter kicks Punk in the gut and drops him with a snap suplex. Hatter bounces off the ropes and drops and elbow on Punks head. Hatter back up now and he pulls
Punk to his feet. Punk gets thrown against the ropes but Punk reverses and throws Hatter into the ropes. Punk follows and clotheslines Hatter right over the top rope and to the floor. Punk quickly follows and pulls Hatter up. Punk takes Hatter and throws him into the stairs. Hatter sitting on the ground with his back to the steps. Punk picks up a chair and sends a baseball swing towards Hatters head but Hatter ducks. Street Punk clobbers the steels steps as Hatter trips up Punk. Hatter grabs the chair that Punk dropped and slams Punk in the head with it. Hatter rears back and nails Punk again. Punk beginning to bleed from his forehead. Hatter turns the chair sideways and goes to crush in Punks head but Punk grabs Hatter's leg and brings his legs up to wrap around Hatter's waist. Punk brings Hatter down with some sort of leg scissors and puts on an ankle lock. Hatter doesn't scream out in pain. Instead he pushes himself up onto his hands and then uses his free foot to kick Punk in the jaw. Punk holds on and Hatter kicks him again. Punk let's go of the hold. Hatter rolls over and climbs to his feet as Punk does the same::
Chris Poleski: It's like these two are just going move for move. Matt Chapen: I told you they had history!! ::Hatter waves for Punk to bring some more on. Punk slides his foot under the chair that was laying on the floor and kicks it up at Hatter. Hatter goes to block but Punk runs in and clobbers him with a clothesline. Punk pulling Hatter to his feet and throws Hatter into the ring post. Punk grabs
Hatter again and throws him into the ring. Hatter who has now also been busted open is trying to get to his feet while Punk slides into the ring.
Punk kicks at Hatter repeatedly trying to keep him down. Hatter grabs Punks foot and nails him right in the crotch with a low blow. Hatter climbs to his feet and levels Punk with a viscous European uppercut. Punk quickly back to his feet though. Punk looks at Hatter and smiles, he then waves him on.
Hatter spits out some blood and comes at Punk. The two men start battling back and forth once again. This time Punk gets in more hits and takes the advantage. Punk picks Hatter up and drops him with a body slam. Punk goes to follow up but Hatter is back up. Punk charges Hatter but Hatter drops a shoulder and back body drops Punk up and over onto the mat. Hatter pulls
Punk up and throws him into the corner. Hatter charges but Punk moves out of the way and Hatter puts his shoulder right into the ring post. Hatter backs out and Punk lays him out with a back suplex. Both Punk and Hatter laying on the mat::
Matt Chapen: This is...this is...I don't know... Chris Poleski: You said it right the first time, this is just a straight up ass whoopin. I have a feeling these two will meet again.
::Punk getting to his feet now along with Hatter. Punk grabs Hatter and knees him in the face. Punk throws Hatter against the ropes and goes for the back body drop but Hatter jumps it. Hatter bounces back and Street Punk drops to the mat and Hatter goes over him. Hatter bounces off again and Punk goes for a clothesline but Hatter ducks it Hatter bounces off the ropes,
Punk turns around..ASYLUM!!!!! HATTER JUST NAILED PUNK WITH THE ASYLUM AND IT'S OVER!!!! One... Two... Three!!! Rick Collings: Here is your winner.......THE MAD HATTER!!!!! {"The Thing That Should Not Be" by Metallica plays over the PA system as Mad
Hatter is trying to catch his breath.}
Matt Chapen: My goodness!!! WHAT A BRAWL!!!!!! Chris Poleski: Mad Hatter is directing his attention to the TraditionTron! Matt Chapen: LARRY GIONET IS ON THE BIG SCREEN! Larry Gionet: Mad Hatter, I saw you come in here to the CWA trying to make an impact! Trying to rub off of MY spotlight! Ever since I have known you
Hatter, you have never been in the same league as the true living legend of professional wrestling! You always cried to the brass about not getting your fame or recognition, well Hatter its time I made your ass famous and it happens at Price of Glory. I will show the world why I am exactly what I say I am and thats simply the best.
{The TraditionTron fades to black as Mad Hatter has a mean look in his eye.}
Chris Poleski: Well it looks like Gionet is gonna take on Mad Hatter at Price
Of Glory!
Matt Chapen: Apparently so, Mad Hatter isn't backing down by any means Poleski. That will no doubt be a match to watch! Fans we will be right back!
