CWA (Continental, 2000) — Card

CWA Tradition Part 4

15 July 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Matt Chapen: We have returned and TPO just defeated Sean Harris! After the matchup Ace Fireside ran to the ring and started brawling with the Psychotic
One and sent him out of the ring!
Chris Poleski: Not only that but Shane Russell accepted the challenge of Adam
Mitchell! Matt Chapen: Speaking of Ace Fireside, Todd Michaelson has caught up with him! {The camera shows Todd Michaelson and Ace Fireside in the parking lot.} Todd Michaelson: Ace Fireside, earlier tonight you took down one of the biggest men in the CWA the Psychotic one. My question to you is why?
Ace Fireside: You wanna know why Todd. All my career I have been told that I would only be a tag team wrestler and could never make it in the singles ranks!
I defeated Chris Natural at the PPV and decided to pick on the biggest man in the CWA today! Sure he may have the height and weight advantage on me, but I am out here to prove the old cliche true: It ain't the size of the dog in the fight its the size of the fight in the dog! So...
{SMACK!!!} Matt Chapen: ITS THE PSYCHOTIC ONE! HE JUST NAILED ACE FIRESIDE WITH A LEAD
PIPE! Referees are trying to restrain The Psychotic One but he is swinging the lead pipe at them!
{Todd Michaelson runs away to safety as The Psychotic One throws the pipe down and leave. The officials check on Ace Fireside as the camera goes back to
Chris Poleski and Matt Chapen.}
Chris Poleski: Ace Fireside got exactly what he deserved! Matt Chapen: He didn't deserve that! Ace Fireside will get his revenge! {"King Nothing" by Metallica plays as The Hottest Prospect comes to the ring to a chorus of boos from the crowd. He has a mic in hand and looked extremely angry by the look on his face.}
Matt Chapen: What is THP doing here? This is not on the lineup! Chris Poleski: Shut up Chapen, THP is about to speak here! THP: Ever since Journey's End, the only thing on my mind has been to beat the living shit out of Kevin Candido! I looked for him at the PPV and he left the building with his tail between his legs in fear! I drove in circles around this damn arena looking for ya but no such luck! So Candido, I'm right here in this ring! I ain't leaving until Kevin Candido gets his ass out here! Come on
Candido!
Matt Chapen: THP CALLING OUT CANDIDO! Candido cost him his match at Journey's
End! {"Battleflag" by Lo-Fidelity Allstars plays. The crowd looks to the enteranceway to find no Kevin Candido.}
Chris Poleski: Where the hell is Candido? THP: Thats right I knew it! The big shot Kevin Candido didn't have enough guts to show up and face the music!
{You hear a roar from the crowd as you see Kevin Candido right behind THP.}
Matt Chapen: CANDIDO IS BEHIND THP!
{THP turns to the Traditiontron to see Kevin Candido right behind him.} Chris Poleski: And here they go! Matt Chapen: THP with a right hand, but its blocked! CANDIDO WITH A RIGHT
HAND, AND ANOTHER ONE! Candido nails THP with a dropkick!
Chris Poleski: Come on THP! Candido whips THP into the turnbuckle! Candido running towards THP, BUT THP NAILS HIM WITH A BIG BOOT TO THE FACE! THP throws
Candido into the turnbuckle and is giving him a boot choke! THP throws him to the mat!
Matt Chapen: Kevin Candido is gasping for air here! THP picking Candido up! Come on now this is enough! You have proved your point! Chris Poleski: I don't think he is done yet! He is looking at the table! Matt Chapen: No way! Chris Poleski: THP lifts him up in the air for the powerbomb! {CRASH!!!} Matt Chapen: OH MY GOD! THP JUST POWERBOMBED KEVIN CANDIDO THROUGH THAT ANNOUNCERS TABLE! Chris Poleski: Hey that's what you get when you mess with THP! Matt Chapen: Somebody get some help for Kevin Candido! He could be seriously hurt! Fans we have to take a break!

— Part 4 of 7 —
