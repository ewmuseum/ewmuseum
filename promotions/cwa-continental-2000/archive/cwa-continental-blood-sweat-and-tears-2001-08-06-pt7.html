CWA Blood Sweat and Tears Part 7

6 August 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Matt Chapen: Tanhammer nor Gionet looking to hold anything back! Tanhammer blocks the punch of Gionet. Perry unloading away with right hands. Headlock by Tanhammer but Gionet throws him into the ropes, and nails him with a shoulderblock. Perry Tanhammer rolls to the outside. Tanhammer taking his time, but he gets back in the ring but Gionet is there to meet him with a kick to the midsection!
Chris Poleski: LARRY GIOENT RAMS TANHAMMER'S HEAD INTO THE TURNBUCKLE! Gionet now stomping away at Perry Tanhammer in the corner. Larry Gionet picks him up and drops him with a textbook vertical suplex! Larry Gionet is talking trash to Perry Tanhammer! Gionet picking up the fallen Tanhammer, BUT PERRY NAILS
HIM IN THE STOMACH! Rake to the eyes by Frat Boy. Now Perry rams Gionet's head into the turnbuckle! TANHAMMER POUNDING AWAY AT GIONET IN THE CORNER!
Some retribution from earlier in the match! Matt Chapen: Perry Tanhammer whips him to the other turnbuckle. He runs at
Gionet, BUT LARRY GIONET CAUGHT HIM AND DROPS HIM WITH A STUN GUN ON THE TOP
ROPE! Tanhammer wobbles back into a back suplex!
One... Two... Kickout! Gionet looks to be waiting for Perry Tanhammer to get up. Frat Boy still shaking off the effects of that stun gun into a back suplex.
Chris Poleski: Clothesline blocked by Perry INTO A SAMBO SUPLEX BY FRAT BOY! What a move! One... Two... Kickout! Tanhammer with a facetwist! Perry Tanhammer whips Larry Gionet into the ropes AND NAILS HIM WITH A DROPKICK TO THE KNEE! Perry Tanhammer dragging
Larry Gionet to the ropes. He is on the apron, GUILOTINE LEGDROP ONTO THE LEG
OF LARRY GIONET! He got that knee area good!
Matt Chapen: Perry Tanhammer grabbing that injured leg AND CLAMPS ON THE
FIGURE FOUR! Tanhammer putting pressure on that injured knee of Larry Gionet.
Gionet trying to find a way to escape. Perry Tanhammer is giving him hell. LARRY GIONET WITH A THUMB TO THE EYE! That will force the hold to be broken for sure! Larry Gionet leaning against the turnbuckle to help himself up.
Tanhammer goes after him but GIONET NAILS HIM WITH A KICK TO THE GROIN! Chris Poleski: Larry Gionet turns him over into the turnbuckle and starts ramming his soulder into Tanhammer. Those shoulder thrusts can be brutal!
Gionet with a fullarm drag twist followed by a standing wristlock INTO AN
ARMBAR! What a maneuver! Perry Tanhammer flips over on his feet and throws
Gionet onto the mat. TANHAMMER WITH A CODE RED ARMBAR SUBMISSION! Gionet quickly grabbing a hold of the ropes!
Matt Chapen: Both Tanhammer and Gionet back on their feet. Perry Tanhammer whips Larry Gionet into the turnbuckle, BUT GIONET REVERSES IT! Larry Gionet runs at Perry but Tanhammer nails him with a boot to the face. He grabs the head of Gionet! ITS HANGOVER TIME! THE HANGOVER TORNADO DDT! NO GIONET LANDS
ON HIS FEET! Kick to the gut by the living legend! Larry Gionet picks him up,
THAT'S LEGENDARY! TANHAMMER IS OUT! One... Two... Three!!! Rick Collings: Here is your winner...THE LIVING LEGEND LARRY GIONET!!! {"Running With The Devil" by Van Halen plays as Larry Gionet looks at an unconscious Perry Tanhammer in the ring as he has his hand raised in victory.}
Matt Chapen: After all the war of words and after all the attacks, Larry
Gionet picked up the victory in a great matchup between these two legends! I don't think we have seen the last of these two competitors!

— Part 7 of 10 —
