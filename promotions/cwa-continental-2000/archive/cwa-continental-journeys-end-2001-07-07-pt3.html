CWA (Continental, 2000) — Card

CWA Journey's End Part 3

7 July 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Matt Chapen: Fans just moments ago we saw Anthony the Animal Davis put away the Psychotic One with help from Sean Harris. By the looks of things, a new alliance of sorts has formed so the CWA better be on the lookout!
Chris Poleski: Speaking of being on the lookout, we have Todd Michaelson in the back with Kevin Candido!
{The camera shows the backstage area with Todd Michaelson next to Kevin Candido.} Todd Michaelson: Kevin Candido, you have a match with Larry Gionet tonight on
PPV. The question is, are you ready both mentally and physically?
Kevin Candido: Todd, I have never been more ready for a matchup in my life. Nothing is gonna stop me from kicking his ass from pilar to post! Todd Michaelson: What about the injury you suffered at the hands at Larry
Gionet on Tradition, that doesn't sidetrack you at all?
Kevin Candido: You know Michaelson, I maybe only 85% physically but I am here
200% mentally for the self-proclaimed living legend of professional wrestling.
As for his boys, they better stay out of my business or else I will have some backup of my own!
Todd Michaelson: Any finals words? Kevin Candido: Larry Gionet, you don't know what you have gotten yourself into my friend. You will fall just like everyone else that has fallen before me
Larry. I will be in that ring victorious.
{The camera cuts back to Matt Chapen and Chris Poleski.} Matt Chapen: Kevin Candido perhaps in one of the biggest fights in his life in the sense that if he beats Larry Gionet, he knows inside if he still has it to become the CWA World Champion once again! Ladies and gentlemen, now we'll go to a live sattelite feed to Brockton, Massachucetts, where the Brockton Brawl between Buddy Costello and Mark Blaze will take place!
Chris Poleski: It's not fair, dammit! Buddy Costello isn't a neanderthal like
Mark Blaze. He shouldn't even be in this match, first of all.
Matt Chapen: Let's go to the Marciano Stadium. [Open up to the Marciano Stadium, where Buddy Costello and Mark Blaze are standing toe-to-toe in the ring.. suddenly, some music hits, and out comes none other than MARVIN HAGLER!]
Chris Poleski: Who the Hell is that? Matt Chapen: It's Marvin Hagler! For you non boxing fans out there, Marvin
Hagler captured the WBF heavyweight title from Tommy Hearns in 1980. And look at that shirt... he's the special guest referee!
Chris Poleski: WHAT?! [Costello glares a hole right through Hagler, who just smirks at the old school hero. Hagler signals for the bell to ring.]
Matt Chapen: OH! And Buddy Costello with a cheap shot to Mark Blaze! Costello is taking the match right from the beginning!
Chris Poleski: Of course he is! He's Buddy Costello, dammit! {SMACK!!!} {WHOOOOO!!!} Matt Chapen: Knifeedge chop by Costello! {SMACK!!!} {WHOOOO!!!} Matt Chapen: And another! Chris Poleski: Yeah, you go, Buddy! Whoo! Matt Chapen: Now, Costello whips Blaze to the other side.. Costello comes in.. but Costello eats Blaze's boots! Chris Poleski: Ew. Matt Chapen: It's a figure of speech. Um... Blaze now begins wailing away at
Costello's head with right hands! Right hand, right hand, roundhouse right!
[POP!] Chris Poleski: Blaze can't treat Costello like that! And why isn't Hagler doing anything about this?
Matt Chapen: It's a Brockton Brawl- anything goes! And Blaze just knocked
Costello out of the ring! This match is officially in the proverbial no-man's land...
Chris Poleski: Great. Come on, Buddy! [The crowd pops big time as Mark Blaze takes a chair and winds it up, getting ready to hit Costello!]
Chris Poleski: Hey, Hagler! Do something! Matt Chapen: BLAZE IS GONNA CAVE IN COSTELLO'S HE- NO! Costello just low blowed Blaze, dammit!
Chris Poleski: Eye for an eye, Matt. Matt Chapen: Now, Costello takes the chair from Blaze- {CRACK!!!} {HUGE HEEL POP!} Matt Chapen: MY GOD! Costello just slammed the chair over Blaze's back! Chris Poleski: See, now they've created a monster in Buddy Costello! He used to be a nice little boy...
Matt Chapen: Oh, please... and now, Costello is winding up that damn chair agai- {CRACK!!!} Chris Poleski: WHOO! Matt Chapen: Dammit! The chair got dented on Mark Blaze's head... and I think he's busted open! Look at that sadistic grin on Costello's face.. and now,
Costello just tossed Blaze over the guardrail and this looks to get even more intense! And Hagler's not even doing anything, what's his role here?
Chris Poleski: That's what I said! Matt Chapen: At any rate, Buddy Costello and Mark Blaze are in this literal sea of humanity... and they're fighting it out something fierce!
{SPLASH!!!} Matt Chapen: BLAZE JUST SMASHED A CUP OF SODA OVER COSTELLO'S HEAD! Chris Poleski: Blaze is a hoodlum, dammit! Matt Chapen: And now- {SMASH!!!} Matt Chapen: GOOD GOD! Blaze just slammed a chair right over Buddy's head! And now, Buddy is busted open! Blaze is now taking the old school hero back to the ring! Chris Poleski: This has been one huge disaster, Matt. Matt Chapen: Damn right it is. And now, Blaze is pulling a table out from under the ring.. and it doesn't look good at all for Buddy Costello!
{POP!} Chris Poleski: Come on, Buddy! Get up! Matt Chapen: Buddy is on the table... and Blaze has ascended the turnbuckles! He's going for the Blazen Glory all the way from the top rope, through the table on the outside! Good god!
Chris Poleski: THIS CAN'T HAPPEN! {CRASH!!!} {HO-LY SHIT!" "HO-LY SHIT!} {HO-LY SHIT!" "HO-LY SHIT!} Matt Chapen: GOOD GOD ALMIGHTY! BLAZE MISSED! BLAZE MISSED! Chris Poleski: THANK THE LORD! Matt Chapen: Buddy Costello is one lucky man! Buddy is still dazed, but he manages to throw Blaze back into the ring.. Buddy makes the cover! One, two-
NO! BLAZE GOT THE SHOULDER UP! Chris Poleski: NO WAY! HAGLER MADE A SLOW COUNT! Matt Chapen: And apparently, Costello thinks so, too... he's arguing with
Hagler! Costello just left the ring.. and he's got a chair! He's got a chair!
Chris Poleski: He's gonna smash in Blaze's good-for-nothing head once and for all! Bwahahaha!
Matt Chapen: No, dammit, no! Blaze might be seriously injured from missing that Blazen Glory... but this might end it for Blaze! Costello winds it up- {POP!} Chris Poleski: WHAT THE HELL IS HAGLER DOING?!? Matt Chapen: Marvin Hagler just took the chair from Buddy Costello! Costello shoved him- OH MY! HAGLER WITH A RIGHT HAND! HAGLER WITH A RIGHT HAND! AND
BLAZE ROLLS UP COSTELLO! One... Two... Three!!! [The crowd pops as "Bring the Ruckus" by Wu-Tang Klan begins playing and Marvin
Hagler raises the hand of Mark Blaze in victory.]
Matt Chapen: Mark Blaze has won this contest and a brutal one it was! What the hell someone has just ran from the crowd! He is in the ring now! Who is that?
Chris Poleski: THAT'S MAX BOULDER! THE FORMER CWA NORTH AMERICAN CHAMPION! He clotheslines Mark Blaze! Max Boulder picks him up in a gorillar press position AND THROWS HIM OVER THE RING!
Matt Chapen: Max Boulder is back in the CWA and just beat the tar out of Mark
Blaze in front of his hometown! HAGLER WITH A RIGHT HAND! ANOTHER RIGHT HAND
TO BOULDER! These fans are loving it! Hagler running into the ropes. Marvin
Hagler misses with the clothesline, BULLDOZER! BULLDOZER! Max Boulder just hit his finisher on a former boxing world champion! These fans do not like it at all!
Chris Poleski: This is awesome! What better way to make a return that to attack the hometown heroes?
Matt Chapen: Its a cheap way damnit! Look at Boulder's face, he is proud of himself! He will get what is coming to him in due time! I have just been informed that Chad Yaro is in the building! What is he doing here? Todd
Michaelson is going to try to get a word with him right now so let's go there!
{The backstage area shows Chad Yaro walking in the back. Todd Michaelson runs to Chad Yaro to try to get a word with him.}
Todd Michaelson: Chad Yaro.. {Chad Yaro keeps on walking.} Todd Michaelson: Chad I want to get a word with you. {Chad Yaro turns around and acknoleges him.} Todd Michaelson: You don't have a match tonight, so whats the deal? Chad Yaro: Yeah Todd, I know all about it. Let's just say I got a little business to take care of and everyone will see me later on tonight!
{Chad Yaro takes off as the screen goes back to Matt Chapen and Chris Poleski.}
Chris Poleski: Chad Yaro showing a little bit of an edge tonight I see. What business could he be talking about?
Matt Chapen: I have no idea Chris but we are sure to find out as this night goes on!
