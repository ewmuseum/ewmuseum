CWA (Continental, 2000) — Card

CWA Tradition Part 2

12 August 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Matt Chapen: Welcome back fans and if you just joined us, we had an explosive interview to start up the show! Larry Gionet and Frat Boy were revealed as the
2 hooded men that helped Joe Snow become the CWA World Champion!
Rick Collings: This falls count anywhere match is schedueled for one fall with a 30 minute time limit! First coming down the aisle, making his CWA return...THE ASSASIN!!!
{"Perfect Crime" by Guns N Roses plays as The Assasin comes to the ring to a chorus of boos from the crowd.}
Chris Poleski: At the PPV, this man made his return and in a big way! Matt Chapen: Yeah by destroying an already bruised and battered Street Punk! Yeah that was real brave of him! Rick Collings: And his opponent...STREET PUNK!!! {"Iron Man" by Black Sabbath plays as Street Punk slowly walks to the ring as the fans cheer wildly.}
Matt Chapen: Street Punk runs into the ropes and gets hit with a clothesline! The Assasin picks him up and whips him into the ropes. What a vicious powerslam by The Assasin. Right jab to the jaw of Street Punk. Assasin running into the ropes and Street Punk ducks the clothesline AND HITS ONE OF
HIS OWN! Chris Poleski: Street Punk picks the Assasin up and drops him with a vertical suplex! Assasin quickly goes to the outside. Assasin going towards the back!
You see thats not cowadice, that's smart fighting! Matt Chapen: Smart fighting my ass! He knows that Street Punk is gonna kick his ass so he is trying to run away with his tail between his legs! STREET
PUNK IS RUNNING AFTER HIM! GO GET HIM STREET PUNK! Chris Poleski: The Assasin right a right hand knocking Street Punk down! Assasin grabbing a steel chair! {SMACK!!!} Matt Chapen: ASSASIN JUST THREW THAT STEEL CHAIR INTO THE FACE OF THE BLOOD ANGEL! Chris Poleski: What kind of name is that, Blood Angel? He better prey that he has some guardian angel is watching over him because he won't be saved from the ass kicking that is being handed to him!
Matt Chapen: You were just cheering this guy not too long ago! Chris Poleski: Well times change and people change Chapen! Matt Chapen: Both men are brawling towards the back where there is a stack of tables! Assasin nails Street Punk with a boot to the face! The Assasin is taking one of those tables! He leans it against the others. This can not be good for the blood angel!
Chris Poleski: Hell no it won't! Haha! Assasin with some stiff punches to
Punk! I bet he busted his nose with those punches! The Assasin whips Street
Punk towards that table BUT STREET PUNK REVERSES IT!
{CRASH!!!} Matt Chapen: THE ASSASIN WENT FLYING THROUGH THAT TABLE! Assasin is down and
Street Punk nails him with a kick the head. Street Punk follows up with some stiff right hands. NO! THE ASSASIN GOES DOWNSTARS! Assasin in control now
AND HE IS TAKING THEM OUTSIDE OF THE ARENA CHRIS! Chris Poleski: This is a very fitting enviornment for both men! They are known for their hardcore wrestling styles. THE ASSASIN RAMS THE HEAD OF STREET
PUNK INTO THE WALL OF THE ARENA! There is no padding there its all solid! The
Assasin continues the assault with a right cross to the jaw! The Assasin getting out of the way making Street Punk come to him!
Matt Chapen: Punk blocks a punch by The Assasin AND LANDS ONE OF HIS OWN! Assasin fires back!j Assasin is taking them through the side door BACK INTO
THE GUND ARNEA! THEY ARE COMING BACK THIS WAY CHRIS! Assasin trying to ram
Punk's head into the guardrail, BUT STREET PUNK BLOCKS IT! HE RAMS THE HEAD OF
ASSASIN INTO THE COLD HARD STEEL! Chris Poleski: Street Punk throws The Assasin over the guardrail! Street Punk stepping back. He runs at Assasin, BUT THE ASSASIN BACK BODY DROPS HIM!
{CRASH!!!} Chris Poleski: STREET PUNK JUST GOT BACK BODY DROPPED THROUGH THAT TABLE! Matt Chapen: Street Punk must be hurting bad! He throws him back in and goes for the cover!
One... Two... Kickout! DAMIEN IS WALKING DOWN THE AISLE! He has no business here! Assasin with a left hook. Punk fires back with a right hand! Assasin with a knee to the midsection and throws Street Punk to the outside! DAMIEN IS PUTTING THE
BOOTS TO STREET PUNK! HEY STOP THAT! Chris Poleski: He can do whatever the hell he wants Chapen! Street Punk coming out of the ring and its a double team! Its like Blood Sweat and Tears all over again! I love it!
Matt Chapen: I don't like it at all! Who is coming to the ring? THAT'S JASEN
CASEN! What in the hell? JASEN CASEN ATTACKING DAMIEN! HE THREW DAMIEN INTO
THE STEEL STAIRS! He nails The Assasin with a punch for good measure!
Chris Poleski: This is not right Matt! Street Punk throws The Assasin back into the ring! Street Punk picks him up! POWERBOMB! PUNK JUST HIT THE
POWERBOMB ON THE ASSASIN! Casen and Damien are preoccupied on the outside! Punk hooks the leg! One... Two... Three!!! Rick Collings: Here is your winner...STREET PUNK!!! {"Iron Man" by Black Sabbath plays as Street Punk looks what is happening on the outside.}
Matt Chapen: Street Punk just won the match, but Damien just dropped a powerbomb onto Jasen Casen! Damien celebrating on the outside and Street Punk running the opposite side ropes! STREET PUNK FLIES OUT OF THE RING AND HITS A
SUICIDE PLANCHA ON DAMIEN! The Assasin rolling out of the ring and duking it out with Jasen Casen! We have a brawl on our hands between these four men!
The officials are running to the outside trying to break up this chaos!
