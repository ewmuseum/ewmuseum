CWA (Continental, 2000) — Card

CWA Tradition Part 4

11 May 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Matt Chapen: We have returned and that was a classic matchup between Damien and Adam Mitchell! Not only did we have that match going on, but Hia Tao and
Shane Russell were tearing down the enteranceway with their brawl!
Chris Poleski: In the end Damien hit a phenominal diamand dust to put away
Adam Mitchell! Matt Chapen: As a result of the actions that have taken place and what happened just moments ago, Michael D. Taylor has signed yet another match for the May 27th PPV Price of Glory! It will be Adam Mitchell and Shane Russell taking on Damien and Hia Tao in a tag team grudge match! Won't that be a match for the ages!
Rick Collings: This contest is schedueled for one fall and is for a spot in the CWA North American Title three way dance at Price of Glory! First coming down the aisle...THE JESTER!!!
Rick Collings: And his opponent...IRONMAN JORDAN JONES!!! Chris Poleski: Both men in an arm elbow tie-up! The Jester goes for a front-layout suplex, but Jordan Jones blocks it. Jordan Jones hits a double underhook suplex on The Jester. Jordan Jones goes for a flying cross body press, BUT THE JESTER COUNTERS WITH A RUNNING POWERSLAM! BEAUTIFUL!
One... Two... Shoulder Up! The Jester whips Jordan Jones into the ropes and The Jester goes for a side suplex, but Jordan Jones blocks it. Jordan Jones runs into the ropes and
The Jester hits Jordan Jones with an elbow! Jones staggers back but The Jester is staying on him. The Jester whips Jordan Jones into the ropes, BUT JONES
NAILS HIM WITH A CLOTHESLINE! Just when Jester thought he had this match in control, Jordan Jones rebounds with a clothesline!
Matt Chapen: That is what to expect here in the CWA! Jordan Jones with a hiptoss on The Jester. Jordan has The Jester in a headlock! Some very good mat wrestling on the part of Jordan Jones! He is wrenching on the neck of
Jester as he gets to his feet! Jester throws him into the ropes. Jester leapfrogs over Jordan Jones and sends him crashing down with a dropkick!
One... Two... Kickout! The Jester executes a snap suplex on Jordan Jones. Chris Poleski: The Jester whips Jordan Jones into the ropes AND PLANTS HIM
WITH A JUMPING DDT! That's gotta be it!
One... Two... Kickout! Matt Chapen: Jordan Jones is not giving up so easily! He wants that title shot just as bad as The Jester wants it! The Jester goes for a back suplex, but Jordan Jones counters it with a facerake. GERMAN SUPLEX BY THE IRON MAN!
One... Two... Shoulder up! Jordan Jones runs into the ropes AND JORDAN NAILS HIM WITH A
LARIET! Jester got turned inside out with that lariet. Jordan Jones whips The
Jester into the turnbuckle, but The Jester reverses it. The Jester charges into the corner, but Jordan Jones LIFTS HIS LEG! HIGH CROSS BODY ON JESTER!
JORDAN HOOKS THE LEG! One... Two... Kickout! Chris Poleski: OH TOO CLOSE! Jordan Jones goes for a headlock takedown, BUT
THE JESTER THROWS HIM OFF! The Jester goes for a side suplex, but Jordan Jones blocks it. Jordan Jones with an armdrag takedown! Jordan Jones goes for a swinging neckbreaker, but The Jester counters it with a side suplex, BUT JORDAN
JONES LANDS ON HIS FEET! Jester turns around into THE RIVERS OF JORDAN! As
Jester turned around, he grabbed the legs! He has that elevated sharpshooter held on tight! JESTER IS TRYING TO GET TO THE ROPES! HE CAN'T DO IT HE TAPS!
JORDAN JONES WINS! Rick Collings: Here is your winner...IRONMAN JORDAN JONES!!! {Jordan Jones snatches the mic from Rick Collings.} Jordan Jones: CWA fest your eyes on the newest CWA superstar to turn this promotion upside down and make an inpact! Why am I so confident? I just beat the living hell out of The Jester to earn my spot in that North American Title match at Price of Glory! Not only that, but I too have a match with your so called Traditional Champion Al Basler at the PPV! But you must be asking yourselves: "But Jordan, TWO title shots at the PPV, thats great!" Well guess what piss-ants, you ain't getting the pleasure of seeing me twice in one night, you pieces of shit ain't worth it! So Al Basler, why don't we have our little title match next week on Tradition?
{"Awake" by Godsmack plays as Al Basler stands on the enteranceway with the CWA
Traditional Title around his waist and a mic in hand.}
Al Basler: Jones, I have had enough of your crap! I told you we would settle the score at the PPV, but if you are so hell-bent on having our match next week, then fine I ain't back down from a fight! See you in a week boy!
{"Awake" by Godsmack plays as Al Basler leaves the ramp and to the backstage area as Ironman Jordan Jones smiles.}
Matt Chapen: Well Jordan got his wish! He gets Basler next week in what should be a hell of a matchup! Fans we will be back!
