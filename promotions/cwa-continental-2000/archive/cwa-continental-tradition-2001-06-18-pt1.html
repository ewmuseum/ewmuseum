CWA (Continental, 2000) — Card

CWA Tradition Part 1

18 June 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
{"I Can't Drive 55" by Sammy Hagar plays as the camera shows the CWA crowd. They look pumped for another exciting night of CWA action. The camera then shows Matt Chapen and Chris Poleski.}
Matt Chapen: Hello CWA fans and WELCOME to CWA Tradition! We are just 9 days away from our next CWA PPV Journey's End where the rematch of Frat Boy and
Chainsaw Charlie for the CWA World Championship will happen! Speaking of, I have been told that both men are here tonight! What is gonna happen?
Chris Poleski: I am sure both men will be watching the back of their respective allies as THP takes on Joe Snow! THP is gonna squash him for good!
Matt Chapen: I understand a limo has pulled up to the arena! {The camera shows the limo slowly coming to a stop at the arena. A man opens the door and its Chainsaw Charlie.}
Matt Chapen: The CWA World Champion has arrived Chris! Chris Poleski: Yeah so what. Matt Chapen: PERRY TANHAMMER JUST BLINDSIDED HIM! He is stomping the head of the champion!
{Chainsaw Charlie trying to protect himself gets kicked in the ribs by Frat Boy
Perry Tanhammer.}
Frat Boy Perry Tanhammer: You got my gold you son of a bitch! You better watch that title with your life, because at the PPV its coming home to daddy!
{Frat Boy Perry Tanhammer walks away as officals attend to Chainsaw Charlie. The camera goes back to Matt Chapen and Chris Poleski.} Matt Chapen: What the hell did he do that for? Chris Poleski: Its easy Chapen, he wanted to have an edge over the champion. Matt Chapen: Not so smart if you ask me. Rick Collings: This contest is schedueled for one fall with a 30 minute time limit! First coming down the aisle...CHRIS NATURAL!!!
Rick Collings: And his opponent...SHANE RUSSELL!!! Matt Chapen: They lock up. Chris Natural goes for a neck snap, but Shane
Russell blocks it. Shane Russell goes for an eye gouge, but Chris Natural blocks it. Chris Natural whips Shane Russell into the ropes, but Shane Russell reverses it. Shane Russell hits Chris Natural with a clothesline. Shane
Russell runs into the ropes and he misses the elbow! NATURAL WITH A BACKDROP
SENDING HIM TO THE OUTSIDE! Chris Poleski: This is where neither man wants to be! This is where its the most dangerous! Both men duking it out! Shane Russell with a dropkick on
Chris Natural. Shane Russell knocks Chris Natural into the ringsteps. Chris
Natural bounces back and gets hit with a devestating clothesline! Shane
Russell whips Chris Natural into the guardrail. Russell may be the stronger of the two, but Chris Natural is quicker! Shane Russell goes for a roundhouse right, but Chris Natural blocks it. Chris Natural goes for a backspin DDT, but
Shane Russell blocks it. Shane Russell hits Chris him with an inverted atomic drop! Russell re-enters the ring waiting the arrival of Natural!
Matt Chapen: Shane Russell whips Chris Natural into the ropes BUT NATURAL HITS
A BACKSPIN DDT ON RUSSELL! Its that quickness you were talking about earlier
Chris that could earn Natural a victory here! Chris Natural goes for a clothesline, but Shane Russell counters it with a crucifix!
One... Two... Kickout! Shane Russell goes for a flying cross body press, but Chris Natural ducks out of the way. Chris Natural whips Shane Russell into the ropes, but
Shane Russell reverses it! Shane Russell hits Chris Natural with a clothesline.
Chris Poleski: Shane Russell goes for a bodyslam, BUT NATURAL COUNTERS WITH A SMALL PACKAGE! THIS COULD BE ALL! One... Two... Kickout! Matt Chapen: Shane Russell whips Chris Natural into the ropes. Shane Russell misses with a clothesline. Chris Natural misses with a shoulderblock. Chris
Natural goes for a European uppercut, but Shane Russell blocks it. Shane
Russell takes Chris Natural down with a double-axhandle chop! RUSSELL WITH A
NORTHERN LIGHTS SUPLEX! One... Two... Thr...Shoulder Up! Chris Poleski: Shane Russell whips Chris Natural into the ropes, but Chris
Natural reverses it. Natural comes back AND GETS HIT WITH A SPINNING LEG
LARIET! Shane Russell attempts to place Chris Natural on the turnbuckle, but
Chris Natural blocks it. Chris Natural goes for a European uppercut, but Shane
Russell COUNTERS WITH A BACKSLIDE!
One... Two... Kickout! Chris Natural goes for a full nelson faceslam, but Shane Russell counters it with a reverse cradle!
One... Two... Thr...Shoulder Up! THAT WAS TOO CLOSE! RUSSELL WITH A TORNADO DDT! One... Two... Three!!! Rick Collings: Here is your winner...SHANE RUSSELL!!! Matt Chapen: Shane Russell gets the pinfall victory here on Tradition! Chris Poleski: Chris Natural should've gotten the win tonight, he is the more talented of the two.
Matt Chapen: I will admit he is a talented performer, but Shane Russell got the best of him tonight.
Chris Poleski: Bah, what do YOU know about talent? Matt Chapen: Regardless of my wise-ass collegue, I am very proud of all our talented men here in the CWA! Shane Russell is leaving the ring and someone is behind Chris Natural!
Chris Poleski: OH NO! Matt Chapen: ITS ACE FIRESIDE! Fireside with a right hand AND ANOTHER ONE! Ace Fireside levels him with a clothesline! He picks him up, the fans know whats coming up next!
Chris Poleski: FIRESIDE BOMB! NO! This was not right! He attacked poor
Natural from behind! What the hell!
Matt Chapen: He attacked him while he was facing him! Fans we will be back!

— Part 1 of 8 —
