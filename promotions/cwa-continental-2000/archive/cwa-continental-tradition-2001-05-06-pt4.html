CWA (Continental, 2000) — Card

CWA Tradition Part 4

6 May 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Chris Poleski: Welcome back. Chainsaw Charlie and Joe Snow are already in the ring ready for this hardcore battle for the number one contendership for the
CWA
North American Title! Matt Chapen: But before we do so, we have an interview with Adam Mitchell. {The TraditionTron shows Todd Michaelson interviewing Adam Mitchell.} Todd Michaelson: So Adam Mitchell how does it feel to be in the CWA? Adam Mitchell: Well ya know... {All of a sudden, Hai Tao attacks Adam Mitchell. He is pounding and kicking away at the motionless Mitchell. He drapes the Japanesse flag on Adam
Mitchell. He threatens Todd Michaelson as the screen fades to black.} Matt Chapen: What in the hell was the purpose of that? Chris Poleski: Hai Tao needed to make an impact here in the CWA! What better way than to rid the CWA of another newcomer in Adam Mitchell!
Matt Chapen: Mitchell will be far from done I can gurantee you that! Joe Snow and Chainsaw Charlie have formed quite a team over the last few weeks. I'm not too sure what the CWA Board was thinking booking this match between two friends. Whatever happens though, this matchup will be extreme and not for the faint-hearted!
Chris Poleski: I hope these two tear each other apart! It'll be the best for everyone; especially the World Champion - FRAT BOY!!! Oh what??? They're shaking hands in the middle of the ring?
I wanna puke!!! Matt Chapen: It's a sign of respect... something you wouldn't know anything about! They lock up and Joe quickly applies an armbar! Charlie with the reversal, he sinches it in...
Chris Poleski: Hiptoss by Charlie, but Joe rolls with it and takes down Charlie with a dragon-whip takedown! Charlie rolls quickly to his feet as Joe launches a pinpoint dropkick at his face - and it connects! Charlie's down as Joe advances on him...
Matt Chapen: Monkey flip by Charlie! But Joe Snow lands on his feet. He runs into the ropes, then launches a clothesline at Charlie - who leapfrogs over him.
Both men run into the ropes! Charlie with a rolling clothesline... but Joe
Snow cartwheels out of the way!!! This is amazing! They both stop and stare at each other, grinning... And the crowd is loving this! A great show of respect!
Chris Poleski: Gimme a break! Joe Snow catches Charlie with a side kick and follows it up with a succession of elbow strikes! Charlie staggering back now... Joe scoops him up - hard bodyslam! Joe now on the second rope...
legdrop across Charlie's throat, and the cover! One... Easy kickout by Charlie! Matt Chapen: Joe's waiting for Charlie to get to his feet... Superkick!!! But
Charlie ducks it, spins Joe around and kicks him in the midsection! Chainsaw
Charlie now with a front suplex on Joe planting him unceremoniously across the top ropes!!! Charlie slides out onto the apron. He sets up Joe - Gutwrench powerbomb to the floor!!! Charlie with the cover
One...
Two... Joe gets the shoulder up! Chris Poleski: Now this is more like it! Would you do that to a "friend"? Matt Chapen: No, but I know YOU would! Charlie reaching under the ring now and he pulls out a table. He's setting it up across the ring and the security barrier as Joe starts to get to his feet! Charlie sees Joe and charges at him with a shoulder barge... only to get sent colliding into the ring post after a drop-toe hold!!! That could have seperated the hardcore legend's shoulder!
Chris Poleski: That's terrible! Snow has to realise that to get anywhere in this business he's got to get agressive! Its the only way! Tread on them before they tread on you!!! This is more like it! Joe grabs a chair from a fan and slams it into Charlie's shoulder!!!
{CRACK!!!} Matt Chapen: Joe now working on that shoulder, with the chair and boots! That's gonna leave some horrible bruises for sure!
Chris Poleski: It'll be more than bruises that Charlie'll need to be worrying about! {CRACK!!!!} Joe just waffled Charlie with that chair sending him rolling backwards onto the hard concrete floor. Joe throws the chair into the ring and and now he's looking under the ring for something. Why? You've got him down! Don't give him a second to recuperate!
Matt Chapen: Charlie's rolling back into the ring... that shoulder injury looks to be bothering him! Joe rolls in after him... and he's got his infamous
Singapore Cane, wrapped in barbed wire!!! Chris Poleski: As they say, "Business Just Picked Up!!!" Matt Chapen: Isn't that copyrighted? Chris Poleski: Who cares! Oh beautiful! Look at that long line of crimson Joe just left on Charlie's back after that cane shot! Charlie's T-shirt's torn in half and Joe's winding up for another shot...
{CRACK!!!!} That's it! OPEN HIM UP!!! Matt Chapen: Charlie's a bloody mess already!!! He's crawling into the corner as Joe Snow climbs the turnbuckle, cane in hand!! Chris Poleski: What a coward! Look at Charlie cowering in the corner! Where's your extreme icon now? Joe's up the top, waving that bloody cane and the crowd are loving it, just like me! He leaps off and...
Bzzzzzzzzzzzzzzzzzzzzzzzzzzzzzzzzzzzzzzzzzzzzz!!! Matt Chapen: WOW!!! Charlie wasn't hiding! He was setting up his patented diamond-tipped chainsaw! Not cowering as you put it... but playing possum!!!
As Joe leapt at him, Charlie swung his chainsaw and cut the cane in half!!! A few more inches and we'd be off the air, and Joe Snow would be in 2 pieces! Chris Poleski: He he. A few more inches make all the difference! I should know! Matt Chapen: Oh purlease!!! Joe's in shock, and Charlie capitalises whipping him into the ropes. Joe Snow rebounds... AND CHARLIE FLIES THROUGH THE AIR NAILING
HIM SQUARE IN THE HEAD WITH THE CHAINSAW HANDLE!!! That's gonna be one for the replay reels right there!!! Joe's cut open, and Charlie's back in control!
Chris Poleski: Charlie now with a snap suplex on Joe Snow. He pulls him back up, whips him into the ropes... Tornado DDT on the chair!!! Charlie with the cover!
One... Two... Kickout by Joe Snow!!! Matt Chapen: Charlie pulls up Joe and hoists him up in the air with a high vertical suplex!!! All the bloods running down Joe's face... and Charlie drops him onto the chair with an incredible Brainbuster!!! Another cover by
Charlie...
One... Two... Thr... Noo! Only a two! Joe barely managed to kick out of that one! Chris Poleski: Charlie pulls up Joe and pushes him into the ropes... Joe rebounds, straight into a belly-to-back suplex onto the chair! Charlie places the chair over Joe's face... and now he's climbing the turnbuckle!!! I don't know what he's planning on doing up there but if he nails it, then Joe Snow may need reconstructive surgery!
Matt Chapen: Somersault legdrop by Charlie... and it connects!!! That's gotta do it! He just drove that chair into Joe Snow's face! Bit of a cocky pin by
Charlie... One... Two... Noooooooooo! Still Joe manages to kick out! How'd he do that? Chris Poleski: He wants the NA title!!! Serves Chainsaw Charlie right for not hooking the leg!! Snow's struggling to get to his feet and Charlie's climbing the turnbuckle.
He's looking to finish off Snow! Oh no... Joe just fell into the ropes, causing
Charlie to be crotched on the top turnbuckle!!! Man - that's gotta hurt!!!
Matt Chapen: Joe Snow's climbing that turnbuckle and punches Charlie square in the face. They're dangerously high up there slugging it out on that turnbuckle.
Both men are bleeding profusely... and what else can you expect from two of the
CWA's best hardcore wrestlers? Charlie swings a right... but Joe Snow ducks it and....
OH MY GOD!!! Chris Poleski: APOCALYPSE OFF THE TOP TURNBUCKLE DOWN THROUGH THAT TABLE! Joe
Snow's top-rope DVD is devastating in itself... but through a table?!
Matt Chapen: Chainsaw Charlie has been broken in half!!! Joe Snow with the cover! This is it! One... Two... Three!!! NOOOOOOOOOOOOOO! Only a two!!! Somehow... Somehow... Charlie kicked out!!! Chris Poleski: Unbelievable! Cmon ref!!! What're you playing at??? Matt Chapen: Both men are slowly starting to move. Joe Snow's first up, and stomps on Charlie for good measure. He rolls him into the ring, then climbs up onto the apron... Springboard elbow drop!!! Great move but Charlie's starting to get to his feet. Joe hooks him up and takes him down with a side
Russian legsweep!!!
Chris Poleski: That won't finish off Charlie. Joe needs to pull off something special! Joe sets up that chair in the middle of the ring... It looks like he's going for a Triple Jump Moonsault! He runs into the ropes, leaps from the chair onto the top rope and here comes the moonsau... NOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO!
Matt Chapen: CHAINSAW CHARLIE LEPT TO HIS FEET, CAUGHT JOE WHO WAS IN MID-SAULT
AND DROPPED HIM FACE FIRST ON THE CHAIR WITH A FACE BUSTER!!! Charlie with the cover...
One... Two... Three!!!! He did it!! Rick Collings: Here is your winner and will face Gargoyle for the North American Title...CHAINSAW CHARLIE!!! {"Come With Me" by Puff Daddy Plays as Chainsaw Charlie raises both hands in the air in victory as the fans go crazy.}
Chris Poleski: Dammit! Joe was so close! This means Charlie gets to face Gargoyle for the CWA North American title. HERE COMES GARGOYLE! HE IS NAILING CHARLIE BEFORE HIS MATCHUP! Matt Chapen: HERE COMES JOE SNOW TO HIS RESCUE! Gargoyle ducks the clothesline! DEATH DIVE ON JOE SNOW! SNOW IS OUT AND GARGOYLE IS UNLOADING ON
CHALRIE! FANS DON'T TOUCH THAT DIAL!
