Matt Chapen: Fans, we have been on a rollar coaster of a PPV thus far and... [The lights in the arena slowly die out and even though they have no idea what's going to happen next, the fans pop huge. Folk Implosion begins to play over the arena speakers.] Matt Chapen: Oh come on! He has shoulder-length, multi-colored hair that hangs over his face and is dressed like he stepped out of the latest Abercrombie and Fitch catalog. He takes a few steps forward and then stops and stares out into the crowd through his red tinted sunglasses. He gets a few cheers and catcalls from some women in the crowd, but it's far from a huge pop. He pulls a mic out from behind his back and holds up a hand to silence the crowd.]
#I'm the one natural one, make it easy# #We can take it inside# #I can have it cause I act like I love it# #It's a matter of pride# #Yeah, we can take it# #Good excuse on an endless spree# #Good because we made it# #Your woman's falling down, you may as well crash with me# [The man, who we now can tell speaks with a slight Scottish accent, asks for the music to stop playing and the music cuts abruptly as the man continues to stare out into the crowd. He pulls off his shades and pushes back his hair to reveal a pair of dark brown eyes and a grin that goes from ear to ear. He raises the mic to his mouth and begins to speak.]
Aidan: Ladies, gentlemen, mark this date down and always remember it, because today is the day that a man entered the CWA and single-handedly revolutinized not only it, but the entire wrestling world as a whole. How's that you ask? It's quite simple, actually. Something that has long been missing and conspicuous by it's absence in the CWA is talent, and that, my friends, is what I, Aidan McKeenit, plan to bring to the proverbial table.
Chris Poleski: YES! He is gonna bring some much need talent into this promotion! Genius!
[There must be a few so-called "smart fans" in the crowd tonight, because they begin to cheer at Aidan's words. The less informed members of the crowd still sit silent, deciding to listen to the rest of what he has to say before passing judgement.]
Aidan: Thank you, thank you. [He begins to walk a little further down the aisle towards the ring.] You see, unlike some of the more established
"stars" here in the CWA, I don't claim to be "mighty" nor do I claim to be an a "hardcore" icon...
[He's cut off by the huge face pop that comes from the crowd at the mention of the nick-names of their heroes. Aidan stops mid-stride on his way to the ring and a shocked look comes across his face as he stares into the crowd.
He frowns and shakes his head as if he were disagreeing with the crowd's approval of the men. He tries to silence them after a few more seconds.]
Matt Chapen: Who does he think he is? This is crazy! Where is securiry? Chris Poleski: WIll you please shut up Matt Aidan is trying to talk here! Aidan: [holding up his hand to the crowd again.] Wait, wait, I'm not done yet. Let me finish...[the crowd slowly settles down and let's him continue]
I'm not here to take your hard-earned money and I'm not here to try to confuse your already febble minds. No, I'm here to entertain you, and not through the use of some flashy gimmick, but through the tried and true methods of hard work, drive, and ambition.
[A few cheers come from the crowd, but the large majority seems to have already turned against him. A slight chant of "asshole" can be heard, but it quickly dies out.]
Aidan: You boo, but never before has any federation had the good fortune and luck to have someone as talented as myself grace it's squared circle, and that's no lie. I'm literally the total package.
[more boos come from the crowd.] No, it's true. It really is. I mean, I've managed to entertain you people with mere words, and that's no small task, considering 99% of you have the attention span of a Peruvian fruit fly. And if it's true what they say about actions speaking louder than words, then you're all in for a treat when I finally step through the ropes to claim my first victim. And, I hate to brag, but look at me...you won't find any blokes in the back who look even half as good as this. I've got looks, talent, and charisma, three things that make promoters drool, and the competition fear for their existence. Aidan McKeenit is on a runaway train to the future and I plan to drag this place with me. So, in closing, fans of the CWA, consider yourself lucky, and Pres., consider yourself blessed, because with me at the fore front of your company, your future will be so bright you'll have to wear shades.
[He puts his glasses back on and smiles an evil smile.] Aidan: The Natural One has spoken... [He tucks the mic back into the back pocket of his brown cargo shorts and raises his hands into the air. The crowd is booing him heavily now, much to
Aidan's obvious dislike. His music hits again and he walks backstage.]
