CWA (Continental, 2000) — Card

CWA Tradition Part 8

12 August 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Matt Chapen: We have returned and Shane Russell made Frat Boy tap out to the
Lock up to retain the CWA Traditional Title!
Chris Poleski: He would not have one if it was not for THP! THP not only
Jacknifed Perry Tanhammer onto the floor but powerbombed the CWA Traditional
Champion as well!
Rick Collings: This contest is schedueled for one fall with a 60 minute time limit and is for the CWA North American Title! First coming down the aisle...DAMIEN!!!
Matt Chapen: Damien has been quickly making a name for himself here in the CWA and looks to become the new CWA North American Champion!
Rick Collings: And his opponent, he is the current CWA North American Champion...JASEN CASEN!!! {"Greed" by Godsmack plays as Jasen Casen makes his way to the ring with the
CWA North American Title around his waistas the fans boo him.}
Matt Chapen: They start up with a tieup, and Damien with a rake to the eyes. Damien with a right hand, followed by a chop in the corner! CASEN FIGHTS BACK
WITH A RIGHT HAND! Lethal left hand by Damien. Casen with a right AND ANTOHER
ONE! He whips him into the corner. Casen bounces back INTO A BOOT TO THE
FACE! Chris Poleski: This man Damien is so unpredicable and dangerous you don't know what to expect from him! Jasen Casen set for the ride and Casen blocks the hiptoss into a backslide!
One... Two... Kickout! The North American Champion grabs the leg of Damien. He spins him around AND GETS LEVELED WITH A CLOTHESLINE!
One... Two... Shoulder Up! Matt Chapen: DAMIEN IS CHOKING CASEN! The referee making the count! One... Two... Three... Four... Damien breaks the hold! Damien whips Casen into the ropes. Casen ducks the boot! He ducks the clothesline! JASEN CASEN WITH A FLYING FOREARM!
One... Two... Thr...Kickout! Chris Poleski: Both men back on a vertical base! Jasen trying to duck the kick of Damien but Damien stomps him for his trouble. He picks up the champion and plants him with a T-Bone slam!
One... Two... Kickout! CASEN WITH A DROPKICK TO THE KNEE OF THE BIG MAN! Matt Chapen: He must keep Damien down on the mat if he wants any shot at winning this matchup! Jasen with a legbar into a single leg crab! Damien too close to the ropes and he grabs them forcing the champion to release the hold!
He must be getting fustrated now! Chris Poleski: If he gets fustrated now, then he could cost himself the title! Jasen Casen with a front facelock! Casen locking that arm into a standing switch, but Damien reverses it! Damien turns him around and DROPS HIM RIB
FIRST INTO THE ROPES! The champion just hanging there. DAMIEN HITS A
GIOLITINE LEGDROP! What agility for a big man! He hooks the leg! One... Two... Kickout! Damien with a scoop and a slam! Damien going up on top! This is unlike Damien to take a high risk move! Damien off the top WITH A FLYING
HEADBUTT! NO JASEN CASEN MOVES OUT OF THE WAY! One... Two... Shoulder Up! Matt Chapen: That was a close one! Jasen Casen whips Damien into the turnbuckle. He runs at Damien, BUT DAMIEN PULLS THE REF IN THE WAY AND THE REF
GETS KNOCKED DOWN! Using the referee as a sheild, Damien avoided a flying forearm in the corner by the CWA North American Champion!
Chris Poleski: No way, the ref is just too damn clumsy! Damien gets out of the ring and HE GRABS THE CWA NORTH AMERICAN TITLE! Jasen Casen is checking on the referee! Damien is back in the ring with the title in his hand.
{SMACK!!!} Matt Chapen: DAMIEN JUST NAILED JASEN CASEN WITH THE CWA NORTH AMERICAN TITLE! Casen is out! Damien is reviving the referee! Don't let it end like this! One... Two... Three!!! Rick Collings: Here is your winner and NEW CWA North American Champion...DAMIEN!!! Chris Poleski: Yes he did it! Let the celebration begin! {"Perfect Crime" by Guns N Roses plays as The Assasin comes to the ring grinning ear to ear.}
Matt Chapen: Well Assasin looks happy that his ally Damien won the Traditional
Title! That damn cheater!
Chris Poleski: Hey he is not a cheater! The title belt was there so why not use it! Matt Chapen: Whatever! Damien and Assasin are beating the tar out of the former CWA North American Champion! JASEN CASEN IS TRYING TO FIGHT BACK!
Casen with a right jab to the face of Damien! Assasin from behind with a punch to the back of the neck sending him down again!
Chris Poleski: Well ain't that a shame! Casen is getting his ass kicked! Matt Chapen: Its a disgrace! Damien won the match and should have just left him alone! STREET PUNK IS RUNNING TO THE RING! Street Punk in the ring. Punk nails Damien with some right hands! Street Punk levels him with a clothesline!
Jasen Casen rolling out of the ring. Chris Poleski: Street Punk ducks the clothesline of The Assasin. Punk with a kick to the gut and HITS HIM WITH A DDT! Street Punk turns around AND GETS
PLANTED WITH A LARIET! Thats it Damien!
Matt Chapen: Jasen Casen is leaving while Street Punk is getting beat on! Chris Poleski: Why should he care? Matt Chapen: Because he just saved him that's why! Damien and The Assasin have the upper hand on Street Punk. Jasen Casen is almost at the curtain, but stops! Jasen Casen turns around AND GOES CHARGING TO THE RING! THE FANS ARE
GOING NUTS! Jasen Casen nails Damien with a lef jab! Casen with a right hand followed by a discuss punch! Street Punk getting the upper hand on The
Assasin! Assasin leaning on the ropes! STREET PUNK AND JASEN CASEN SENDING
HIM UP AND OVER THE TOP WITH A DOUBLE CLOTHESLINE! Street Punk raises the hand of Jasen Casen and the crowd is loving it! When we return its time for our main event!

— Part 8 of 9 —
