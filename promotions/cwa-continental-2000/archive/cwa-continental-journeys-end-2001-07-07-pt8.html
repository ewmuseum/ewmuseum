CWA Journey's End Part 8

Matt Chapen: We are just moments away from the main event fans! Just moments away before Frat Boy and Chainsaw Charlie tear each other apart to see who deserves or more fittingly who can survive as CWA World Champion!
Chris Poleski: I still have to go with my pick Frat Boy! Matt Chapen: I have just been told that THP is in the backstage area again! Oh no! {The camera shows THP walking in the back. He spots a road agent.} THP: Where the HELL is Kevin Candido? Road Agent: I..I... THP: Whats the matter prick? Answer me damnit! Road Agent: I was told that he left the arena sir! THP: Is that right? Road Agent: Yes sir. THP: Well since I can't kick Candido's ass right now, you are a suitable replacement! {THP kicks the road agent in the gut and nails him with the Powerbomb!} THP: That's nothing compared to what Kevin Candido has coming to him! {THP storms out of the arena. The camera goes back to Matt Chapen and Chris
Poleski.} Matt Chapen: That sick son of a bitch! Chris Poleski: What, that road agent was in the way! Matt Chapen: Ugh. Let's just get to ringside shall we? Rick Collings: This contest is schedueled for one fall with a 60 minute time limit and is for the CWA World Title! First coming down the aisle, he is the challenger...FRAT BOY PERRY TANHAMMER!!!
{"Mr. Speed" by KISS plays as Frat Boy Perry Tanhammer makes his way to the ring as the fans boo him like there is no tomorrow.}
Chris Poleski: My boy Tanhammer. No one can touch him and he is not gonna let a little scruffle with his friends get in the way of becoming a two time CWA
World Champion!
Rick Collings: And his opponent, he is the current CWA World Champion...CHAINSAW CHARLIE!!! {"One Step Closer" by Linkin Park plays as Chainsaw Charlie comes to the ring with the CWA World Title around his waist as the fans go crazy. He comes to the ring and raises his fist in the air as white pyro blasts behind him.}
Matt Chapen: Chainsaw Charlie hands the referee the CWA world title which may be his last here tonight. Both men circling one another. Who is gonna make the first move and get the early advantage in this matchup? Remember you not only need to get the pinfall on your opponent, but you need to keep him down for a ten count administered by the referee in order to be declared the winner and the CWA champion!
Chris Poleski: You are right about that and I am confident that it is going to be Perry Tanhammer. Despite what is going on with Gionet and THP he will come out of this contest the champion! Both men charge at one another, AND ITS A
TEST OF STRENGTH! Come on Perry!
Matt Chapen: Perry Tanhammer getting the upperhand in this knuckelock, BUT THE
CHAMPION IS POWERING HIS WAY OUT OF IT! Oh and its broken up by a high knee by the challenger Tanhammer! Irish whip by Tanhammer. Charlie rebounds off the ropes and is armdragged into an armbar by the challenger. Frat Boy really laying down the punishment on the champion in the early moments of this contest!
Chris Poleski: No doubt about it Matt, this man is hungry and just like a shark that smells blood, he will attack until there is nothing left. Tanhammer has that armbar tight. Break him in two!
Matt Chapen: Charlie on one knee. He is trying to get to his feet! The fans are rooting him on. NO Tanhammer hits him with a drop toe-hold. Chainsaw
Charlie perhaps wisely rolling to the outside. Chainsaw Charlie taking a powder on the outside on the outside to stop the momentum of the challenger.
Chris Poleski: Frat Boy laying some stiff rights onto the champion. Tanhammer throws Chainsaw Charlie over the guardrail. These two men are brawling in the crowd for the biggest prize in the CWA the World Title! PERRY TANHAMMER HAS A
STEEL CHAIR! Matt Chapen: This doesn't look good for the world champion! CHARLIE WITH A
DROPKICK TO THE KNEE ON FRAT BOY! CHAINSAW CHARLIE WITH THAT CHAIR!

Frat Boy Perry Tanhammer looking for a weapon. HE GRABBED CHARLIE'S CHAINSAW! Chainsaw Charlie getting up to his feet. Tanhammer shoves him towards the hood of a black lexxus! Charlie's ribs landed hard on that front of that car!

THE CHAMPION REVERSES IT! They are back in the crowd!
Chris Poleski: ITS LARRY GIONET! LARRY GIONET IS IN THE CROWD! Gionet is beating the living hell out of Chainsaw Charlie! Perry Tanhammer grabs a steel chair from the scattered chairs. Tanhammer swings...
{CRACK!!!} Matt Chapen: JOE SNOW JUST DROPKICKED THE STEEL CHAIR INTO THE FACE OF FRAT
BOY PERRY TANHAMMER! FROM OUT OF NOWHERE! Joe Snow and Chainsaw Charlie simulatiniously whip Larry Gionet and Frat Boy into the guardrail and follow up with a double clothesline sending them both over!
Chris Poleski: Joe Snow looking under the ring. HE HAS THE LADDER! JOE SNOW
