CWA (Continental, 2000) — Card

CWA Tradition Part 2

15 July 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Matt Chapen: This next match coming up should be an interesting one. It pits the 212 pound cruiserweight A.J. Darkk against the 320 pound powerhouse Anthony
"The Animal" Davis. Darkk is still looking for his first win since entering the CWA, and hopes it will happen tonight.
Chris Poleski: He will too. Mark my words. This kid has it coming to him. I like what I see and can't wait for him to make his mark here.
Matt Chapen: He is a fine talent, his attitude could use a little work though. Lets get to the ring. Rick collings. Coming to the ring first, weighing in at 212 pounds, A. J. DARKK!!! {"Dream On" by Aerosmith plays over the P.A. system as A.J. Darkk comes to the ring to a chorus of boos from the crowd.}
Rick Collings: And making his way to the ring second,m weighing in at 320 pounds, Anthony "The Animal" Davis!!!
{The lights go out, all of a sudden there is a huge explosion and "American
Badass" by kid rock starts to blast over the speakers. blue spotlights start flashing all over the arena as Anthony Davis steps out on to the entrance ramp.
The lights come back on as Davis sticks his left arm in to the air and flashes the wolfpac sign. He starts making his way to the ring slapping hands w/ the audience members on his way down. He steps over the top rope and enters the ring. He throws both arms in to the air as a row of blue fireworks shoot off behind him. He takes off his vest and tightens his elbow brace as he gets ready to fight.}
Matt Chapen: These two are ready to go, and there's the bell. Not surprising,
A,J, Darkk comes out with the first offense, These competitors really go at it. A few punches from Darkk isn't going to get the desired effect against
Davis, who shrugs it off and lands a monster right of his own that sends Darkk reeling. Davis right there, whip to the ropes, and there's a big lariat that floors Darkk.
One... Two... Kickout! I don't know how effective Darkk's quick offense will do to a Davis, who's built solid as a rock. Davis with the boots, not letting Darkk get any recovery time.
Chris Poleski: That's the way you do it, Matt. You get in there and you do what needs to be done.
Matt Chapen: I've seen and called enough wrestling matches in my day to know how it works. Davis now, picking Darkk up. whips him to the corner. Darkk lands hard, and Davis follows up with a charge and a clothesline. Darkk was just sandwiched between a turnbuckle and a hard place there.
Chris Poleski: Don't try to be funny Matt, it only makes me look bad. Matt Chapen: God forbid you look bad. Davis nails him with a DDT! One... Two... Shoulder Up! Davis now with a firm lead, but while he was turned, Darkk was able to climb up the turnbuckles, and jumps off hitting a missile dropkick that takes davis off his feet. over for the quick cover.
One... Two... Kickout! Matt Chapen: Almost a victory, but not quite. Davis now looks to end it right. Motions to the crowd, listen to the cheer from the crowd. He's picking
Darkk up, looks like he's going for his Ragin Climax powerbomb. Davis, picking
Darkk up, he's going for it. Darkk with some quick shots while up there.
Stuns Davis a little. Darkk, jumping over the shouldres, takes Davis with him halfway. HE'S SET UP FOR THE DREAM MACHINE!! HE HITS IT!! WHAT A COUNTER!!
One... Two... Three!!! Rick Collings: Ladies and gentlemen, the winner of the match, A.J. DARKK!!!! Chris Poleski: I told you he was gonna pull it out, Didn't I?? Matt Chapen: You were right Poleski. This young superstar with a very impressive showing tonight!
{The lights in the arena fade and a red strobe begins to flash in the darkness. "Don't Fear the Reaper" by Blue Oyster Cult begins to play throughout the building. A laser from the ceiling shoots into the ring and spells out "Fear the Fate". Finally a large explosion sends a giant fireball in front of the entrance to the locker room. As the flash fades,
Harrison is standing in the entrance with no expression on his face. He looks at the ring where A.J. Darkk is standing.}
Chris Poleski: What is the meaning of this? Matt Chapen: I don't know but look at A.J. Darkk, he looks like he has seen a ghost! His eyes are open wide and he looks to be in shock!
Chris Poleski: Do Harrison and Darkk have some sort of past that we don't know about here?
Matt Chapen: Scotty Harrison leaves the enteranceway to the back. A.J. Darkk can't believe his eyes! Hopefully we can get some answers tonight in this program! Fans we will be back!
