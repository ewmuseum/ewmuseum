CWA (Continental, 2000) — Handbook

[CWA] Players Guide v. 1.0

29 May 2000
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
A. About CWA? CWA stands for Continental Wrestling Alliance. CWA is built on molding itself to the needs of the handlers. If there's something you like, let us know.
Something you didn't like? Let us know. A certain direction you want your character to go? We're more than happy to hear it.
The CWA's main goal is too have active involvement from all the handlers. We give everyone a fair shot, it's the handlers job to take advantage of that. If you roleplay on a regular basis(there is no guideline for roleplaying in CWA) then you will go places. It might be right away, or it might be a little while but we will notice your efforts. And if you send in strategies, then your character will be more accurately represented during their matches.
The CWA is run by [name withheld] and [name withheld]. We started CWA because we were tired of being in feds where the fed-head calls all the shots, and you sit around for weeks at a time waiting for late cards. And when you do hear from the president, it's just a lame excuse about his grandpa passing away... Again.
(This has happened to us before.) B. Joining the CWA To join, fill out an application which is at the bottom of this player's guide. When you fill out an application, if you are excepted, you will not be put on a waiting list. Instead you will be put right into the action, it's your responsibility to make sure you stay there, or to get even higher in the rankings.
C. CWA Titles CWA World Heavyweight Title- The belt to hold. If you hold the CWA World Title, you are the man to beat.
CWA North American Title- The secondary belt in CWA. More than likely, if you hold the CWA North American Title, you will be granted a shot at the World
Champion. CWA Hardcore Title- Yes we do have a hardcore championship. If not under any other sort of gimmick, the belt will always be defended in No Rules Matches.
CWA Tag Team Titles- The tag team championship of CWA. Holding these belts signifies you are the No. 1 tag team in the league.
D. CWA Weekly Events The CWA has 1 weekly show, Friday Night Tradition. Usually Tradition has around 5-7 matches, featuring many of the CWA's superstars. Tradition is a great forum to get angle's and in-ring's out with other handlers. Tradition is written in play-by-play format.
Sometimes, in the week before a PPV, CWA holds another show. CWA Wednesday
Night Warfare. It is written in summary format.
A Flash Packet will be sent out before every card containing all of the flashes or roleplays for that particular show.
E. CWA PPV's The CWA has one PPV per month. The PPV are usually named with the show. An example is, our latest PPV had a Gauntlet Match as a main event. So the PPV was appropriately titled, King of Gauntlet. See how simple this is?
F. How does CWA work? The CWA is a booked league. So a percentage of match outcomes are based on storylines, and an even bigger percentage is based on roleplays, and work you put into the league. Remember, strategies may not help but they can't hurt.
Remember guys, without your roleplays and input CWA has nothing to build on and nothing to go with. And plus, your input makes us feel like we're doing something right. :-)
One thing though. Even the best roleplays will find their characters losing a match or two. But please don't come complaining about it. Especcially new guys.
Do you think that you can come in and rule the federation right away? But for you honest new guys who don't mind paying dues, there is hope for all. We've had guys come in and win a title their first week. If we think you'd be good, we might bring you in as a big start immediately!
G. Matches/Angle's If you want to challenge for a match, send out a challenge through a roleplay or flash. If the president thinks it's a good idea, it's done. He may change some things (Challenging the World Champion to a Flaming Electrified Cage Match is one thing that may be changed) but your request will not go un-noticed.
Since angles are an important thing in the CWA, it is very important to know the other handlers. The best way for an angle to work out, is too plan thing's out with the other handler, that you are doing the angle with, and send the idea's back to the president. And be shy about getting to know the president's either.
H. CWA Employee's The Broadcasters- Matt Chapen- The Jim Ross of CWA. "The Voice" has been with CWA since the start. Not biased in matches, just likes to watch good wrestling and have a good time.
Chris Poleski- Chris Poleski is the most biased man you will ever meet. Can't stand babyfaces or anyone that won't do anything remotely bad or illegal in the ring. His one wish is to watch all faces in the ring be destroyed by a single heel. He's a dreamer.
Interviewers- Todd Michaelson- Recently signed with CWA. Since he is the only interviewer in
CWA. Not only is he in charge of in-ring interviews, but he also has to spend the rest of his time in the back looking for a juicy interview. He loves his job, but sometimes will go to far to get a story.
Other important figures- Rick Collings- The CWA's ring announcer, not often acknowledged, but the show would be boring without him.
