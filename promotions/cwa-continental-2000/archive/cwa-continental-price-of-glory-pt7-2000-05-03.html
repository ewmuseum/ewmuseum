CWA Price of Glory Part 7

3 May 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Rick Collings: This contest is scheduled for one fall and is for the CWA North
American Championship! First coming down the aisle, he is the challenger..."THE LIVING LEGEND" LARRY GIONET!!!
{"Cum On Feel The Noize" by Quiet Riot BLASTS through the PA system as Larry
Gionet comes to the ring to an applause from the audience.}
Chris Poleski: Look at this punk Gionet! He thinks he is the best but his legendary status will end tonight when The Assasin keeps the gold!
Rick Collings: And his opponent, he is the current CWA North American Champion...THE ASSASIN! {"Voodoo" by Godsmack plays as The Assasin comes to the ring with the CWA
North American Title around his waist.}
Matt Chapen: Look at the arrogance of this man! He has tried to put down
Gionet with every chance he got! Larry Gionet is looking for some serious revenge!
Chris Poleski: Larry Gionet runs into the ropes. Gionet comes back AND NAILS THE CHAMPION WITH AN ELBOW! Larry Gionet goes for a double armbar submission, but The Assasin blocks it. The Assasin whips Larry Gionet into the ropes AND TAKES HIM DOWN WITH A CLOTHESLINE! THERE YA GO ASSASIN!
Matt Chapen: The Assasin locks Larry Gionet in a Boston crab! Larry Gionet tries to escape the hold! He is trying to get to the ropes and he does! The
Assasin executes a backbreaker on Larry Gionet! The Assasin takes Larry Gionet down with a side suplex! The Assasin punches Larry Gionet. The Assasin chops
Larry Gionet! The Assasin is pummeling Gionet!
Chris Poleski: GIONET COMES BACK WITH A PUNCH! LARRY GIONET HAS HAD ENOUGH! Larry Gionet goes for a shoulderbreaker, BUT THE ASSASIN SLIDES DOWN HIS BACK! ABDOMINAL STRETCH! Nicely done by the North American Champion! Gionet grabs the ropes! If Assasin keeps this momentum up he could very well retain the CWA
North American Title here tonight AND HE WILL DAMNIT!
Matt Chapen: That will be seen to believe! The Assasin nails Larry Gionet with a WITH A FLYING FISTDROP! The Assasin runs into the ropes AND NAILS
GIONET WITH A CLOTHESLINE SENDING HIM OVER THE TOP! Look at that, Jen is taunting Gionet! What a...
Chris Poleski: Hey come on Matt, be nice to Jen! The Assasin goes for a swinging neckbreaker, but GIONET COUNTERS WITH A SIDE SUPLEX! Larry Gionet reenters the ring and Assasin following him back in now! Gionet making a cover! CHEATER!
One... Two... Kickout! Matt Chapen: LEGSWEEP FACESLAM BY THE CHALLENGER! One... Two... Thr...Shoulder up! WAIT A SECOND! JEN IS DISTRACTING THE REFEREE! THE ASSASIN HAS THE CWA NORTH AMERICAN TITLE! HE TAKES A SWING AT GIONET BUT MISSES! Chris Poleski: Larry Gionet grabs the title! HEY THAT'S ILLEGAL! NO HE JUST HIT THE ASSASIN WITH IT! COME ON REF! Matt Chapen: The referee turns around! GIONET HAS HIM COVERED! One... Two... Thr...KICKOUT! HE JUST KICKED OUT! Chirs Poleski Larry Gionet whips The Assasin into the turnbuckle, but The
Assasin reverses it! WOO HOO! He puts him up on top, ASSASIN'S DROP! THE
ASSASIN WITH THE COVER! One... Two... Three!!! Chris Poleski: GOOD NIGHT! HAHAHA! Rick Collings: Here is your winner, and still CWA North American Champion...THE ASSASIN!!! {"Voodoo" by Godsmack plays again as The Assasin and Jen celebrate in the ring.} Matt Chapen: The Assasin successfully retains the CWA N.A. title in a very fast paced match up with Larry Gionet and I am sure Gionet will have his day in the sun!
Chris Poleski: Oh stop making excuses for him! Hey look its Kevin Candido on the ramp! Matt Chapen: That sure is! He could be our next CWA World Champion! He and
Assasin took out Chainsaw Charlie and Joe Snow earlier on tonight but Candido looks angry at Assasin right now! He shakes his head and returns to the back!
What's the deal here? Chris Poleski: I don't know, I mean they seemed fine before! Maybe we will find out tonight!
