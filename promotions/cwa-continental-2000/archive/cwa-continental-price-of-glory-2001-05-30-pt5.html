CWA (Continental, 2000) — Card

CWA Price of Glory Part 5

30 May 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Matt Chapen: We have returned from an excellent tag team contest that ended in controversy. Shane Russell and Adam Mitchell has victory in hand before
Mitchell turned on his tag team partner and leaving him for dead! Chris Poleski: Russell was only using Mitchell to get his way in the CWA! Matt Chapen: Thats not true and you know its not! Rick Collings- This contest is schedueled for one fall and is for the CWA
Traditional Title! First coming down the aisle, he is the challenger...THE
JESTER!!! [The Jester's music plays and the crowd pops for him as he walks to the ring.] Rick Collings- And his opponent, he is the current CWA Traditional Champion...AL BASLER!!! [Basler's music plays as he also makes his way to the ring.] [Ding, Ding, Ding] Matt Chapen- These men waste no time going at one other. Basler hits The Jester with a right. The Jester kicks Al Basler. Jester with a chop now. The Jester picks up Basler and nails him with a big powerslam.
Chris Poleski- These men are in it to win. Matt Chapen- The Jester has Basler up and drops him back down with a side suplex. Irish whip by The Jester, Basler off the ropes, The Jester lifts him up and DROPS HIM DOWN WITH A SPINEBUSTER SLAM! Quick cover!
One... Two... Kickout! Chris Poleski- Too early. You can't put Basler away like that. Matt Chapen- The Jester has Basler in a front-face lock, but Al Basler PUTS HIM OVER WITH A NORTHERN LIGHTS SUPLEX! One... Two... Shoulder Up! Both men back to their feet, but The Jester with rake to the face of Basler! Chris Poleski- Smart tactic on taking down the Traditional champion! Matt Chapen- The Jester with a shortarm clothesline, NO! Basler counters it with a crucifix! Backslide!
One... Two... Kickout! Chris Poleski- Basler had the presence of mind to pull The Jester over with that move. Matt Chapen- Basler sends The Jester into the ropes and takes the challenger down with a clothesline of his own!
Chris Poleski- Basler is heading up top! Matt Chapen- BASLER WITH A FLYING LEGDROP! COVER! One... Two... Shoulder Up! Chris Poleski- The Traditional Champ not letting up one bit. Matt Chapen- Al Basler runs toward The Jester, but the Jester with a sidekick to the face of Basler! Now The Jester runs into the ropes and drills Al Basler with a kneedrop to the face!
Chris Poleski- Oh! His face! Matt Chapen- The Jester locks Al Basler in an armbar submission. But Basler reaches the ropes. The Jester picks up Basler, and sets him up for a piledriver, but Al Basler COUNTERS WITH A BACKDROP! Basler sends The Jester to the ropes now! Basler misses a clothesline and The Jester catches Al Basler in an abdominal stretch!
Chris Poleski- The Jester trying to stretch out the Traditional Champion and get himself a submission victory and the title!
Matt Chapen- Al Basler is valiantly trying to break the hold. He makes it to the ropes. The Jester grabs a hold of Al Basler and nailed him with a piledriver! He hit it that time! Al Basler clutching his neck after that move and The Jester makes the pin!
One... Two... Kickout! Chris Poleski- I didn't think Basler was gonna kick out! The Jester now bad-mouthing the Champion! Yeah give it to him!
Matt Chapen- The Jester goes for a belly-to-belly, but Al Basler counters it with a lariat! THAT NEARLY KNOCKED HIM OUT OF HIS BOOTS!
Chris Poleski- The Jester better get some tape to stick his head back on his neck! Matt Chapen- Al Basler throws The Jester out of the ring! Basler off the opposite ropes, SUICIDE DIVE TO THE OUTSIDE!
Chris Poleski- I don't know who got the worst of that! Basler or The Jester. Matt Chapen- Well the Jester is on his feet, and throws Basler back into the ring. The Jester lays out Basler with an Implant DDT. The Jester heads up top now, MOONSAULT ON BASLER!
One... Two... Three!!! {Ding, Ding, Ding} Rick Collings- Here is your winner and new CWA Traditional Champion...THE JESTER!!! {The Jester gets his hand raised in victory as he holds the CWA Traditional
Title draped around his shoulder. Al Basler leaves the ring fustrated.}
Matt Chapen- The Jester is the new Traditional Champ! Chris Poleski: I told you the Jester would leave the Traditional Champion! Matt Chapen: You we're correct on that assumption! Al Basler has nothing to be ashamed of. He fought a great match with The Jester but he proved to be the better man tonight.
Chris Poleski: Damn right! Matt Chapen: The Jester making his way up the ramp AND CHAD YARO IS ON THE TRADITIONTRON! Chad Yaro: Jester, congratulations on your title victory tonight! You better keep that title close, because that Traditional Title is gonna come home to the
Prospect in due time! You see the last time I fought for the title I got screwed by Jasen Casen, but seeing that he is out of my way, NOTHING is gonna stop me! Jester, I don't know when and I don't know where, but you and I will meet in that ring for your title! You feeling lucky Jester, then roll the dice and take the chance!
{The TraditionTron fades to black.} Chris Poleski: How dare him! Nobody threatens the Traditional Champion like that! Chad Yaro will get whats coming to him!
Matt Chapen: The Jester better be watching his back, because Chad Yaro is looking to take that title away from him!

— Part 5 of 8 —
