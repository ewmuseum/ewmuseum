CWA Blood Sweat and Tears Part 3

6 August 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Matt Chapen: Moments ago we say a high-flying epic battle between two former tag team partners in A.J. Darkk and Scotty Harrison! In the end Darkk countered a ligerbomb into a facebuster for the win!
Chris Poleski: But more importantly, why was Jason Devlin out there in the first place? He wasn't even schedueled on the card!
Matt Chapen: Who knows Chris! I am being told that Frat Boy Perry Tanhammer and THP are in the back.
{The scene shows THP and Frat Boy in their locker room.} Frat Boy Perry Tanhammer: Bro I can't believe it actually worked! Not only by telling the world that Joe Snow was not a worthy competitor the president of this company did something right for a change, he booked us in a triple threat match with that no good piece of shit Joe Snow for a shot at Charlie later on tonight!
THP: Perry, we gotta tear apart Joe Snow limb from limb until there is nothing left! Once he is done and finished we will know who truly is the better man!
Frat Boy Perry Tanhammer: Yes we will THP, yes we will! {The screen cuts back to Matt Chapen and Chris Poleski.} Chris Poleski: Haha! I smell a little bit of a plan set up by THP and Frat
Boy to stop Joe Snow from taking on Chainsaw Charlie!
Matt Chapen: This is not looking to well for one Joe Snow! He must give it all he has in order to take on the champion and become champion!
Rick Collings: This hardcore match is falls count anywhere! This contest is schedueled for one fall with a 30 minute time limit! First coming down the aisle...STREET PUNK!!!
{"Iron Man" by Black Sabbath plays as Street Punk comes to the ring with his signapore cane in hand as the fans cheer him on.}
Chris Poleski: Street Punk is known as one of the most hardcore wrestlers to even grace a squared circle but you know Damien has that mean streak in him that won't die! Damien is my pick to win this match!
Rick Collings: And his opponent...DAMIEN!!! Matt Chapen- Damien enters the ring, and Punk immediately goes to work on him. Chris Poleski- I'm kind of afraid of what might happen in this match Matt. They guys are both insane.
Matt Chapen- Punk whips Damien into the ropes, and slams him back down! Punk lifts Damien back to his feet, and throws him into the turnbuckles. Punk now going to work on Damien in the corner.
Chris Poleski- This match is going pretty safely thus far. Matt Chapen- Street Punk pulls Damien out of the corner, and dumps him outside. Chris Poleski- Alright, I jinxed it. Matt Chapen- Punk off the ropes, SUICIDE DIVE TO THE OUTSIDE!! {Ooh!} Chris Poleski- Oh man! Matt Chapen- Damien moved out of the way, and Street Punk ended up flying headfirst into the steel guardrail.
Chris Poleski- Damien has the downed Punk by the waist, is he going for the
German Suplex? Matt Chapen- Damien has him in the air, and drops him back neckfirst onto the guardrail! Chris Poleski- Somebody's going to end up in the hospital. Matt Chapen- Damien has a chair now. {CRACK!} Matt Chapen- Did you hear that Chris? Chris Poleski- Damien just dented that chair across the back of Street Punk! Matt Chapen- Damien wedges the chair under the bottom rope now, and slides back into the ring. Street Punk is standing right behind that chair. Damien off the ropes, BASEBALL SLIDE!
Chris Poleski- That chair just crushed the face of Street Punk. Matt Chapen- This match has only begun, and if this is a show of what's to come, then someone may end up in the cemetary!
Chris Poleski- Don't try to be like me Chapen. Matt Chapen- Damien goes under the ring now. He's pulling out some wood! Chris Poleski- They might as well bust out some tables sooner than later. Matt Chapen- Damien setting up that table outside of the ring. And pulls Street
Punk up to the apron now. Damien has Punk in a standing headscissors, he lifts him up!
{CRASH!} Matt Chapen- BIG PILEDRIVER THROUGH THE TABLE BY DAMIEN! Chris Poleski- What a move! Damien is making the cover. One... Two... Kickout! Matt Chapen- I knew this match was No DQ, but I didn't know it was Falls Count
Anywhere! Chris Poleski- Just let these guys beat the hell out of each other wherever they want Chapen.
Matt Chapen- Damien sends Punk back into the ring, and pulls out some more tables. Chris Poleski- Damien has one... two... three tables in the ring! I can't believe what we're seeing so early on in this match!
Matt Chapen- Damien is seting one table vertically in the corner. Chris Poleski- But Street Punk is up! He's behind Damien. Waistlock, {CRACK} Chris Poleski- GERMAN SUPLEX! Matt Chapen- Street Punk suplexed Damien right on one of those other two tables that were in the ring! They weren't even set up! Damien's head cracked that table!
Chris Poleski- Settle down Chapen. It's still good, it's only a little crack. Matt Chapen- But that took all of what Punk had, and he rolls to the outside. Damien must have shook off that move because he is up now, with Punk in his sights. Chris Poleski- Damien off the ropes again, over the top rope. [Damien jumps over the top for a body splash, but is caught by Street Punk. The force sends them both backwards and Damien is hotshotted on the guardrail.]
Matt Chapen- Oh god! His neck could be broken! Chris Poleski- What force Damien hit that steel with. This match should be stopped. Matt Chapen- I doubt either of these men are going to let that happen Chris. Chris Poleski- Yeah, your right. Matt Chapen- Both men slow to get up here. Street Punk is the first to his feet, and he dumps Damien back into the ring.
Chris Poleski- Looks like Street Punk is planning to put Damien through the table that's still in the corner now Matt.
Matt Chapen- It appears so, both men are on the opposite side of the ring. Street Punk picks up Damien. {CRASH!} Matt Chapen- RUNNING POWERBOMB THROUGH THE TABLE!! Chris Poleski- What a mess these two are making! Matt Chapen- Well, I think it may get messier because Street Punk is looking under the ring for something... What the hell does he have?
Chris Poleski- Are those? No way. Those can't be, florescent lightbulbs! Matt Chapen- I think your right Chris! Street Punk has four florescent lightbulbs! Chris Poleski- This is insane Matt. Street Punk takes one of the lightbulbs into the ring. Matt Chapen- Damien is on his knees. Chris Poleski- He should just stay down! Matt Chapen- Street Punk has that lightbulb in his hands. He winds up! {POP!!} Matt Chapen- Right between the eyes! There's glass in the ring! What the hell!! Chris Poleski- Matt, I've only heard about things like that in backyard feds. I never thought I would see that in CWA!
Matt Chapen- Damien is begin to bleed profusely from the head Chris. And the worst part is, Street Punk is back out of the ring and has those three other light bulbs in his hand.
Chris Poleski- What's he doing? He's grabbed two chairs from the fans. And sets them up facing each other. Now he lays those three lightbulbs on the seats!!
He's make a bridge of lightbulbs! And he's gonna drop Damien right through them from inside the ring!
Matt Chapen- Damien is up though. And Street Punk is on the apron! Damien with a dropkick! Street Punk is flailing! He's gonna fall! He's gonna fall!
{POP!! CRASH!!} Matt Chapen- Oh my god! Street Punk just fell through those lightbulbs! And he landed on the glass!!
Chris Poleski- Jesus Matt! Look at his back! The blood has soaked the back of his shirt! Matt Chapen- This is out of control! What else can these guys do to each other!? Chris Poleski- We may find out right here Matt. Damien sets up that last table in the ring. And now he's putting the cracked table on top of it!
Matt Chapen- Will that table hold him? Chris Poleski- I think he forgot that he cracked it Matt. He's climbing up ontop of those two tables! He's gonna crush Street Punk on the outside!
Matt Chapen- Street Punk is still on that glass! If Damien lands on him it's gonna be all over!
Chris Poleski- He's gonna jump Matt! {CRACK! CRASH!!} Matt Chapen- I told you that table wouldn't hold him! Chris Poleski- I can't believe that! That top table cracked and Damien fell through both of them!!
Matt Chapen- Street Punk doesn't even know what happened! He doesn't know how close he came to a hospital trip!
Chris Poleski- Punk is slowly sliding into the ring. He doesn't know what the hell is going on.
Matt Chapen- He doesn't care though. He throws his arm over Damien's shoulders. One... Two... Thr... NO! Kickout!! Matt Chapen- I thought Street Punk had him there Chris. Chris Poleski- We he's not letting up. He puts Damien on the top turnbuckle. Matt Chapen- Fireman's carry on top, DEATH VALLEY DRIVER ON THE BROKEN TABLES!! Chris Poleski- It has to be over now! One... Two... Kickout! Matt Chapen- Damien is just not gonna give up Chris. Chris Poleski- A man can only take so much Matt. Matt Chapen- Punk sends Damien into the ropes. He's going to back-body drop him on the broken tables!
Chris Poleski- No! He telegraphed it, Damien picks him up! Powerbomb on the table!!! Matt Chapen- Damien may be back in this thing Chris. Both men are down though. Chris Poleski- Jesus Christ Matt. Let them catch their breath! Matt Chapen- Damien to his feet, and he pulls Punk him. Damien on the top rope. Tornado DDT on the table!! No, Street Punk slides it and sidewalk slams Damien on the tables!!
Chris Poleski- Will someone please move those broken tables! They're just chunks now! Matt Chapen- The referee kicks the tables out, but it looks like Street Punk already has a new plan. He is taking Damien out into the crowd now. Where are they going?
Chris Poleski- Street Punk and Damien are brawling into the crowd. Punk knocks him down. He has something from the crowd. What is it?
Matt Chapen- It looks like a table to me Chris. Which can't spell anything good for Damien.
Chris Poleski- Street Punk lays Damien on the table, and lays a couple more shots into him. Now he takes off again.
Matt Chapen- Where did Street Punk go this time Chris? Chris Poleski- I don't know, but Damien is still out on the table. Where the hell is he. Matt Chapen- ... Uh, Chris. Chris Poleski- What? Matt Chapen- I think I see him. [The commentators look up above where they last saw Street Punk to the balcony of the arena. He is looking down at Damien on the table, about 20 feet below him.]
Matt Chapen- He must be 20, 25 feet up there! What is he thinking? Chris Poleski- I don't know if he is thinking anymore Matt. Matt Chapen- He is up on the balcony with his back to the crowd. WHAT THE HELL IS HE DOING?! {CRASH!! POP!!!} Matt Chapen- OH MY GOD!! Chris Poleski- THEY'RE DEAD! THEY'RE BOTH DEAD!! Matt Chapen- STREET PUNK JUST MOONSAULTED OFF THE BALCONY! 25 FEET IN THE AIR
BUT DAMIEN GOT UP AND HIT A MODIFIED DIAMOND DUST ON STREET PUNK!
Chris Poleski- DAMIEN MAKES THE COVER!! One... Two... Three!!! Rick Collings: Here is your winner...DAMIEN!!! Matt Chapen- It's over Chris! Damien wins it! Chris Poleski- But at what cost! Damien is slowly getting to his feet! Matt Chapen: Damien is motioning for someone to come out! Chris Poleski: We got some company! Matt Chapen: ITS THE ASSASIN! The former CWA North American Champion has returned to the CWA! He has a steel chair in hand!
{CRACK!!!} {CRACK!!!} Chris Poleski: Assasin just nailed Street Punk with two chair shots to the back of the brusied and battered Street Punk!
Matt Chapen: This is totally uncalled for! He has already won the damn match! Chris Poleski: He wants his presence felt here Matt! He wants the world to know what happens when you mess with Damien!
Matt Chapen: Assasin drops that chair and grabs Street Punk! Damien is right next to him and THEY NAIL HIM WITH A DOUBLE POWERBOMB ONTO THAT STEEL CHAIR!
Somebody get some help for Street Punk! He could be seriously hurt!
