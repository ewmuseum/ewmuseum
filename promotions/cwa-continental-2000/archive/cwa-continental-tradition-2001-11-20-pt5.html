CWA (Continental, 2000) — Card

CWA Tradition Part 5

20 November 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The announcers discuss the great North American Title match that just took place including Joe Snow chasing off Kevin Candido to the backstage area. They go backstage where Joe Snow and Kevin Candido are tearing each other apart. A referee comes to the scene and rings the bell. This World Title match is on!
CWA World Title Match: Kevin Candido vs Joe Snow Joe Snow rams Kevin Candido into the garage door. He repeatidly slams Kevin
Candido's head into the door. Joe Snow is looking around and spots a chair.
He goes to slam Kevin Candido with it but he ducks and the garage door makes a loud sound. Joe Snow turns straight into a superkick for a two count. Kevin
Candido is in control of this matchup with a bodyslam followed by a legdrop.
The momentum shifts Snow's direction as the challenger reverses an irish whip into the wall. Snow pulls Kevin Candido by the hair into the hallway of the arena.
He drops candido to open a door and gets a big boot by Rodney Wright from out of nowhere! He puts Kevin Candido on top of him, but he kicks out. Chad Yaro comes from behind Wright with a dropkick to the back of the head followed by a reverse DDT. Joe Snow picks him up with a powerslam. Snow goes for the cover but Candido gets the shoulder up. Joe Snow is running towards the enterance way with Kevin Candido in a headlock while Chad Yaro is taking care of Rodney
Wright. He throws Candido into the guardrail. He runs at him but Candido reverses it into a backbody drop. The security guard is pushing the fans away for their safety but attacks Joe Snow. He takes off the shades to reveal its
Shane Russell! Candido and Russell are pounding away at the challenger and throw him over the guardrail. Kevin Candido is running to the ring as Shane
Russell throws Joe Snow back into the ring. He covers him but he gets the shoulder up!
Shane Russell goes on the apron. Kevin Candido goes for a suplex but Joe Snow flips over landing on his feet. He shoves Kevin Candido into Shane Russell who falls to the floor! Joe Snow gets a school boy but Kevin Candido kicks out!
Shane Russell is furious! Russell has a steel chair in his hands. Joe Snow runs into the ropes but gets a chair shot to the back. With Shane Russell cheering on Candido he climbs to the top signaling for the star killer! From out of nowhere, Larry Gionet knocks Kevin Candido off the top! Both men are down and Shane Russell pulls Larry Gionet off the apron onto the floor. Shane
Russell throws Larry Gionet into the crowd were those two brawl. Both men trying to get to their feet. Joe Snow lands a right on Kevin Candido with
Candido nailing a left on Joe Snow. Both men duking it out. Joe Snow ducks a clothesline. He goes for the Snow Fall, but Kevin Candido pounds him in the head! Kevin Candido nails him with an inziguiri to the back of the head!
Kevin Candido going up on top and he hits the Star Killer! He covers Joe Snow for the victory! Kevin Candido celebrate in the ring as Shane Russell and
Larry Gionet duke it out and Agent Rodney Wright is taking his brawl with Chad
Yaro on the rampway! The announcers say goodnight as the screen fades to black.
