CWA (Continental, 2000) — Card

CWA Tradition Part 5

18 June 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Matt Chapen: We are back from the commercial break and I think Mark Blaze is gonna be on a rampage right now!
Chris Poleski: I have just been told that Mark Blaze is destroying everything in sight! Lets go back.
{Mark Blaze is walking down the hallway.} Mark Blaze: Hey Buddy where are ya? {He spots Todd Michaelson.} Mark Blaze: Hey Todd, where is Costello's dressing room? Todd Michaelson: Second door on your right. Mark Blaze: Thank you. {Mark Blaze approaches the dressing room door of Buddy Costello. He backs up a few steps and kicks down the door. Buddy Costello is in fear.}
Mark Blaze: Don't speak Costello! You see you got this thing all wrong, its not you coming to get me its ME coming to get YOU!
{He unloads with a flury of lefts and rights on Buddy Costello. He rams his head into the wall. Mark Blaze picks him up and throws him against the lockers as the fans go nuts. Buddy Costello is knocked out as Mark Blaze leaves the locker room. The camera then goes back to Matt Chapen and Chris Poleski.}
Matt Chapen: Well it looks like Mark Blaze got what he wanted!
Chris Poleski: He has no compassion! Blaze always just goes and jumps to conclusions. Matt Chapen: I think Blaze was right on this one! Rick Collings: This contest is schedueled for one fall with a 30 minute time limit! First coming down the aisle...CHAD YARO!!!
{"Man In The Box" by Alice and Chains plays as Chad Yaro makes his way to the ring as the fans cheer him on.}
Chris Poleski: Chad Yaro gets his shot at glory as he takes on The Jester live on PPV but sorry for him, I think his dream is gonna get crushed!
Rick Collings: And his opponent...KEVIN CANDIDO!!! {"Battleflag" by Lo-Fidelity Allstars plays as Kevin Candido comes to the ring to a nice ovation from the crowd.}
Chris Poleski: Kevin Candido takes Chad Yaro down with a chop. Kevin Candido with a dropkick on Chad Yaro. Kevin Candido takes Chad Yaro down with a waistlock suplex!
One... Two... Kickout! Kevin Candido whips Chad Yaro into the turnbuckle, but Chad Yaro reverses it. Chad Yaro goes for a front-layout suplex, but Kevin Candido blocks it.
Kevin Candido goes for a Hotshot, BUT YARO COUNTERS WITH A LARIET! Matt Chapen: CHAD YARO GOING UP ON TOP! Chad Yaro goes for a moonsault bodyblock, BUT CANDIDO MOVES OUT OF THE WAY! Kevin Candido nails Chad Yaro with a swinging neckbreaker. Kevin Candido throws Chad Yaro out of the ring.
Kevin Candido executes a side suplex on Chad Yaro. Both men very focused tonight in getting a win to give them momentum going into their respective matchups!
Chris Poleski: Kevin Candido hits an elbowsmash on Chad Yaro. Kevin Candido goes for a swinging neckbreaker, but Chad Yaro counters it with a side suplex.
YARO WITH AN IZIGUIRI TO THE FACE OF CANDIDO! He throws Kevin Candido back into the ring. Yaro on the apron, SLINGSHOT BODYBLOCK! UNBELIEVABLE!
One... Two... Shoulder Up! Chad Yaro takes Kevin Candido down with a moonsault bodyblock. One... Two... Kickout! FRANKENSTEINER BY YARO! One... Two... Kickout! Matt Chapen: Chad Yaro going for another slingshot bodyblock, but Kevin Candido ROLLS THROUGH WITH THE MOVE! ONE TIMES TO MANY YARO! One... Two... Shoulder Up! Kevin Candido nails Chad Yaro with a gutwrench suplex! One... Two... Kickout! Chris Poleski: Chad Yaro whips Kevin Candido into the turnbuckle, but Kevin
Candido reverses it. Kevin Candido goes for a gutwrench suplex, but Chad Yaro blocks it. Chad Yaro goes for a Frankensteiner, BUT CANDIDO COUNTERS WITH A
VICIOUS POWERBOMB! THIS HAS TO BE IT! One... Two... Shoulder Up! Matt Chapen: Kevin Candido whips Chad Yaro into the turnbuckle, but Chad Yaro reverses it. Kevin Candido comes back and rocks Chad Yaro with an elbow! Kevin Candido whips Chad Yaro into the ropes, but Chad Yaro reverses it. Kevin Candido hits Chad Yaro with a shoulderblock. Kevin Candido goes for a kick to the midsection, but Chad Yaro counters it with a legsweep. YARO NAILS
HIM WITH THE SCOOP REVERSE DDT! One... Two... Three!!! Rick Collings: Here is your winner...CHAD YARO!!! {"Man In The Box" by Alice And Chains plays as Chad Yaro gets his hand raised in victory.} Matt Chapen: Chad Yaro with one impressive victory over the Star Killer! Both men shake hands in a nice example of sportsmanship!
Chris Poleski: If Candido were smart he would have attacked Yaro from behind but NO! Matt Chapen: Kevin Candido leaving the ring. Chad Yaro in the ring AND HERE
COMES THE JESTER! The CWA Traditional Champion in the ring! Chad Yaro ducks the clothesline and nails him with a dropkick! The Jester leaned against the ropes AND YARO CLOTHESLINES HIM OVER THE TOP ROPE!
Chris Poleski: Chad Yaro up on the top rope! SOMERSAULT PLANCHA OFF THE TOP
ON JESTER! CHAD YARO IS BEATING THE HELL OUT OF JESTER! SECURITY GET OUT
HERE! Matt Chapen: Oh pipe down! Kevin Candido is going up the ramp, BUT GIONET
NAILS HIM WITH A CLOTHESLINE! Kevin Candido is down and out. LARRY GIONET
RAMS HIS KNEE INTO THE FACE OF CANDIDO! HE NAILED ANOTHER ONE! THIS IS CRAZY!
Gionet heading to the ring. What is going on here? Chris Poleski: Larry Gionet looks pissed! He is asking for a mic. Larry Gionet: Since I just showed the world why Kevin Candido is nowhere near to the level that I am, Street Punk GET YOUR ASS OUT HERE so I can make you famous in front of the entire fucking world!
{Larry Gionet drops the mic waiting in anticipation for Street Punk to come out.} Matt Chapen: LARRY JUST CALLED OUT STREET PUNK! Looks like he's ready for a fight! {"Iron Man" by Black Sabbath plays as Street Punk runs to the ring as the fans are going crazy!}
Chris Poleski: Looks like we don't have to wait for his response! Matt Chapen: This match is starting up right now!
