CWA (Continental, 2000) — Card

CWA Price of Glory Part 4

30 May 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Matt Chapen- Hello fans, we are live from The Target Center in Minneapolis, MN what a show thus far...
Chris Poleski- That's right and with more great action still to come including the World Title and TV Title matches!
Matt Chapen- But right now it's tag team action as Hia Tao and Damien take on
Adam Mitchell and Shane Russell...
[Scene changes to Rick Collings in the ring] Rick Collings- The following contest is schedueled for one fall with a 30 minute time limit! First coming down the aisle...HIA TAO!!!
(Crowd boos as Hia Tao's music plays and he makes his way out.) Rick Collings- And his tag team partner...DAMIEN! (Damien's music plays as he heads to the ring, joining his partner.) Matt Chapen- Last week these two really did a number on Shane and Adam. Chris Poleski- That was nothing! Wait until you see what they have in store for them tonight!
Rick Collings- Their oppenents, coming to the ring first...weighting in at 285 pounds...He hails from Denver Colorado!!! SHANE RUSSELL!!!
(Crowd explodes with cheers as fireworks go off, Shane walks out with his hands raised erupting the crowd again.)
Rick Collings- And his tag team partner... ADAM MITCHELL!!! (Mitchell's music plays as he joins the 3 men already in the ring.) Matt Chapen- There you see Shane with a bandage on his head from the attack last week. Chris Poleski- Both Hia and Damien have planned all week for this, just wait you will see! Matt Chapen- All four men in the ring now as the bell sounds and this one is under way... Damien and Shane Russell will start this match off. These two really went at it last week. As they goes to lock up... Damien quickly goes down stairs with a boot. Damien with a right hand and knocks Shane Russell down with a quick suplex.
Chris Poleski- What a team Damien and Hia Tao make! Matt Chapen- One move does not make a team! Damien now off the ropes and nails a clothesline as Shane was getting to his feet, a tag to Hia and up to the top he goes... Leg lariat right on Shane! Cover right away!
One... Two... No! Big kick out by Shane. But Hia in control now. Chris Poleski- Watch this guy fly... Matt Chapen- Fly he can, as he goes right back to the top rope and somersault plancha knocking Shane to the mat once again. They need to get this big guy down and thus far they have done just that.
Chris Poleski- Hia is amazing, I have never seen a guy move so fast! Matt Chapen- He certainly is fast... Hia now picking Shane up and sends him into the neutral corner. Hia follows in, but Shane get the boot up and Hia runs into it. Shane runs off the ropes but is nailed with a knee to the back by
Damien.
Chris Poleski- Good team work! Matt Chapen- Shane turns around and nails Damien. Sending him to the floor! Shane turns back around and is nailed with a spinning leg kick! Another quick cover by Hia! One... Two... Another big kick out by Shane! Shane need to tag in Adam. He can't possibly take to much more...
Chris Poleski- What skill! Hia Tao back to the top again! Matt Chapen- Hia with a crossbody on Shane! NO! Shane caught him! Fall away slam! Cover by Shane!
One... Two... Damien in to break the count! Shane might have had him right there and this one would be over!
Chris Poleski- What great thinking by Damien to break up the count and watch his partners back.
Matt Chapen- Shane going for the tag but is cut off by a dropkick to the knee from Hia! Chris Poleski- Yes! They are taking the big man apart! Matt Chapen- You have to wonder why they are working on the bigger man instead of Adam who is much smaller... At any rate, Hia pulls Shane back to his feet and this crowd now getting behind Shane.
Chris Poleski- That won't do him any good. Matt Chapen- Hia with a right, but it's blocked. Shane with a right hand of his own. Hia returns one and Shane with another, and another! Adam Russell is off the apron and is getting a chair from ringside!
Chris Poleski- No. Disquailfy him. Matt Chapen- Shane has the upper hand now. Russell off the ropes and runs right over Hia! Shane going for the tag to Adam now.
[Shane Russell moves toward his corner, extending his hand for his partner. The referee's back is turned as he is dealing with Damien, who is trying to get into the ring. Shane now notices his partner has a steel chair from ringside.
Obviously, neither man has the same idea about what this chair is for.] {WHACK!} Matt Chapen- WHAT THE HELL! Chris Poleski- Surprise Russell! Ha ha! I told you Matt! Matt Chapen- Adam just nailed Shane in the head with a steel chair! Hia on the top rope and he nails Shane Russell with a 450 splash!
One... (crowd erupts into boos) Two... Three! [Ding, Ding, Ding] Rick Collings- Here are your winners... HIA TAO AND DAMIEN!! {More boos from the crowd as the three men celebrate in the ring} Chris Poleski- Yes they won it! Just like I said! Matt Chapen- But why? Why did Adam turn his back on his partner? Chris Poleski- Because he was holding him back! Come on, you could see this coming for weeks get with it Matt...
Matt Chapen- Adam now walking away disgusted with Shane Russell, as Hia and
Damien continue to pound on him.
Chris Poleski- This looks just like last week doesn't it? I told you they had a plan and you just saw it!
Matt Chapen- Hia Tao picks up Shane Russell, and Damien has grabbed that steel chair. {WHACK!} Matt Chapen- That shot busted Shane wide open! CWA officials are out now to put a stop to this beating.
Chris Poleski- Oh come on let them continue! Matt Chapen- The whole thing was a set up from the begining and you knew about it! Chris Poleski- Well, not really... But I did see Hia and Damien talking to Adam the other day!
Matt Chapen- Fans lets get ready for the next match as the paramedics help
Shane to the back.

— Part 4 of 8 —
