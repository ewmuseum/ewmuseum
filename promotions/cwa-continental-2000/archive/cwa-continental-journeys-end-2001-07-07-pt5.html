CWA Journey's End Part 5

7 July 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Matt Chapen: What an incredible ladder match we just saw for the CWA Traditional Title! The end came when Shane Russell shoved Adam Mitchell off the ladder into the announcers table which allowed him to win the belt!
Chris Poleski: Adam Mitchell was robbed of the belt! I am sure this war between Russell and Mitchell is far from over!
Matt Chapen: Right now we have Todd Michaelson in the back with the living legend Larry Gionet!
{The camera shows Todd Michaelson next to Larry Gionet.} Todd Michaelson: I am here with Larry Gionet who will take on Kevin Candido in just a few moments. Larry, what do you have to say to your opponent Kevin
Candido?
Larry Gionet: You know what, when I first started here in the CWA, nobody gave a damn about me just like they never gave a rat's ass about you Candido!
Everybody told me that I didn't have what it took to survive in this business! Everybody said that I was way past my prime, but they are all sadly mistaken! You always say how you are gonna star kill people, well its time I made my legacy known and it starts tonight when I seal you're fate!
Todd Michaelson: There you have it. Larry Gionet looking to take out Kevin
Candido tonight! Back to you Matt.
{The camera cuts back to Matt Chapen and Chris Poleski.} Matt Chapen: A very dangerous Larry Gionet looking to make an example out of
Kevin Candido tonight! Can he get the job done?
Chris Poleski: Of course he can get the job done, he is the true living legend of professional wrestling!
Rick Collings: This contest is schedueled for one fall with a 30 minute time limit! First coming down the aisle...THE LIVING LEGEND LARRY GIONET!!!
{"Running With The Devil" by Van Halen plays as The Living Legend Larry Gionet makes his way to the ring to a chorus of boos from the crowd.} Rick Collings: And his opponent..."STAR KILLER" KEVIN CANDIDO!!! {"Battleflag" by Lo-Fidelity Allstars plays as Kevin Candido comes to the ring as the fans cheer him on.}
Matt Chapen: Larry Gionet and Kevin Candido circling one another! Both men psyching the other out, both men waiting for the first move. Collar elbow tieup. Both men out of position. Gionet presses Candido against the turnbuckle. Chop by Gionet, CANDIDO FIRES BACK! Right hand by Gionet,
CANDIDO WITH ONE OF HIS OWN! Chris Poleski: Its a slugfest! Matt Chapen: Gionet with a right hook but its blocked into a hiptoss! Candido with an armbar, but Gionet quickly grabs the ropes! Very good presence of mind by Larry Gionet. Larry Gionet on his knees but the star-killer nails him with two fists to the eyes sending him down!
Chris Poleski: Get up Larry! Matt Chapen: Uppercut by Candido. Kevin Candido going up on top here. What is he going for? Gionet back to his feet. GIONET ON THE TOP TURNBUCKLE! Both men trading blows on the top. Candido knocks him down again.
Chris Poleski: Candido is taking flight! Top rope legdrop MISSES! Larry
Gionet rolled out of the way just in the nick of time!
One... Two... Kickout! Matt Chapen: Kevin Candido runs into teh ropes, but Gionet catches him. HE
JUST DROPPED HIM RIB FIRST ONTO THE ROPES! Candido is back on the apron, BUT
GIONET DROPKICKS HIM TO THE OUTSIDE! Gionet to the outside and picks Candido up. NO! CANDIDO JUST GOT DROPPED RIB FIRST ONTO THE STEEL GUARDRAIL! Gionet is not only trying to win this matchup but to cripple Kevin Candido!
Chris Poleski: That is very smart on the part of Larry Gionet. The more he works on those ribs, the better chance of that referee stoping this contest!
GIONET RAMS CANDIDO'S HEAD INTO THE GUARDRAIL! That must have rattled his brain a bit! Matt Chapen: Larry Gionet on the offensive tonight and he has to be. Larry
Gionet rolling into the ring waiting for Candido. Candido slowly rolling in but Larry Gionet puts the boots to the falling Candido! LARRY GIONET WITH A
DRAGON SLEEPER! CANDIDO IS PUNCHING AWAY AT GIONET! Larry Gionet is forced to break the hold.
Chris Poleski: Come on ref that was a loaded fist! Larry Gionet gets up BUT GETS KNOCKED DOWN WITH A LARIET! One... Two... Shoulder up! Kevin Candido runs into the ropes but Gionet catches him. TORNADO DDT OUT OF NOWHERE BY KEVIN CANDIDO! One... Two... Kickout! Matt Chapen: Candido going up on top. He is going for another hgh risk manevuer! HIGH CROSS BODY! NO LARRY GIONET ROLLS THROUGH WITH THE MVOE!
One... Two... Thr...Kickout! Larry Gionet is looking towards the enteranceway! Who si he looking for? Gionet directs his attention back to Candido. Candido misses with the clothesline! THAT'S LEGENDARY, NO KEVIN CANDIDO REVERSES IT!
Chris Poleski: Kevin Candido is going up on top. NO! HE NAILD IT! HE HIT THE STARKILLER! COVER! One... Two... Three!!! Rick Collings: Here is your winner...KEVIN CANDIDO!!! {"Battleflag" by Lo-Fidelity Allstars plays as Kevin Candido has his hand raised in victory.}
Chris Poleski: Gionet was screwed! You saw it Chapen and so did everyone else! Matt Chapen: Candido reversed it and hit the star killer he was not screwed out of the win! Larry Gionet looks irrate and he is heading for the back!
That was indeed a great match!
