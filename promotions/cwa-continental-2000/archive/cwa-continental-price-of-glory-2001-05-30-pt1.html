CWA (Continental, 2000) — Card

CWA Price of Glory Part 1

30 May 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
OOC: I meant to post this after I sent it to the fed but got a little pre-occupied. I know my last Tradition was not the best in the world, but this
PPV sure makes up for it! So here is the CWA PPV Price of Glory!
{"Blaze of Glory" by Bon Jovi plays over the PA system as the fans in the
Target Center go crazy. The fans are ready for an exciting night of action for
Price of Glory. The camera then zooms in on Matt Chapen and Chris Poleski at ringside.}
Matt Chapen: CWA fans we welcome you to our PPV Price of Glory! We are live with a sold out crowd at the Target Center in Minneapolas Minnesota! We have 7 action packed matches for you tonight with every title on the line here tonight! The Jester challenges Al Basler for the CWA Traditional Title. For the vacant CWA North American Title, Street Punk does battle with Frantic and
Ironman Jordan Jones! Lets not forget the main event as Chainsaw Charlie goes to win the World Title for the thrid time as he does battle with Frat Boy Perry
Tanhammer for the biggest prize in our sport the CWA World Title!
CHris Poleski: Perry Tanhammer has to deal with this punk for years and I say its about time he puts him out of wrestling for good tonight!
Matt Chapen: We will have to see! Rick Collings- This contest is scheduled for one fall with a 30 minute time limit! First coming down the aisle... He is "THE LIVING LEGEND" LARRY
GIONET!!! ["Running With The Devil" by Van Halen jams as Larry Gionet makes his way to the ring. The fans boo him loudly.]
Matt Chapen: Larry Gionet has been a thorn in the side of The Mad Hatter and
Hatter looks to take him out tonight!
Rick Collings- And his opponent...THE MAD HATTER!!! ["That Cannot Be" by Metallica plays as The Mad Hatter makes his way out. He recieves a good pop from the crowd.]
Matt Chapen- They lock up. Hatter takes control and whips Larry Gionet into the ropes, but Larry Gionet reverses it. Gionet drops Hatter with an elbow.
Chris Poleski- As usual, Gionet is quick to take control of the matchup. Matt Chapen- Gionet whips Hatter back into the ropes, but The Mad Hatter nails him with a kick to the midsection. He follows up with a pumphandle slam! Larry
Gionet trying to get back up but is knocked back down with another dropkick from Hatter!
Chris Poleski- Come on Larry, don't let this guy outsmart you! Matt Chapen- Hatter swings for Larry Gionet, but Gionet blocks it and hits a right hand of his own! Larry Gionet runs into the ropes AND NAILS HATTER WITH A
CLOTHESLINE! Larry Gionet climbs to the top rope.
Chris Poleski- What is Larry doing here? Matt Chapen- Gionet jumps but HATTER MOVES OUT OF THE WAY! COVER! One... Two... Kickout! Matt Chapen- The Mad Hatter goes for a suplex, but Gionet turns in mid-air and lands on him! Very quick thinking on the part of the Living Legend!
One... Two... Shoulder Up! Chris Poleski- Come on Larry! Matt Chapen- Larry Gionet runs toward Hatter, but the Mad Hatter nearly takes the head off of Larry Gionet with a spinning leg lariet!
Chris Poleski- Gionet will rally back! Matt Chapen- Hatter puts Larry Gionet in a standing headscissors. He tries to lift Gionet up, but Larry Gionet blocks it. Larry sends Hatter over with a back-body-drop. He whips him into the turnbuckle now, and follows up with a big shoulder-tackle. NO! Hatter out of the way. The Mad Hatter plants Gionet with a
DDT! The Living Legend is in trouble here!
Chris Poleski- He is not in trouble! He will turn the tide in this matchup with his wrestling experience and submission specialty!
Matt Chapen- Hatter with a kick to the gut, POWERBOMB WITH AUTHORITY! He hit it that time! Mad Hatter with the cover!
One... Two... Kickout! Matt Chapen- The Mad Hatter usually one to go to the air in his attacks is keeping this match on the mat! Hatter locks Larry Gionet in a waistlock, but
Larry stops him with a back-kick. And takes him down with a belly-to-belly suplex of his own!
Chris Poleski- I told you! Larry is a master of technical wrestling! He is clearly in control of this matchup.
Matt Chapen- You are right about that for the moment! This is one incredible opening matchup fans! Its a back and forth battle! Gionet locks The Mad Hatter in an armbar submission! Gionet trying to pull his arm out of the socket!
Chris Poleski- Look into the eyes of Larry Gionet! He knows no mercy! Matt Chapen- The Mad Hatter finally reaches the ropes! Larry Gionet picks up
Hatter and quickly throws him out of the ring! Larry Gionet follows him out and knocks The Mad Hatter into the ringpost.
Chris Poleski- Let us not forget that Larry is also a Master of Hardcore! Matt Chapen- Larry Gionet whips Hatter into the guardrail, but Hatter reverses and Larry hits hard! Hatter throws Larry Gionet back into the ring! Larry
Gionet trying to shake off the cob-webs from that whip into the guardrail. The
Mad Hatter whips Larry Gionet into the ropes. Hatter gets down, and Larry jumps over him off the other side. Larry Gionet misses with an elbow! Hatter comes back, but he misses a clothesline now! Larry Gionet hits The Mad Hatter with a clothesline of his own!
Chris Poleski- You can't keep a good man down. Matt Chapen- Gionet pulls Hatter to his feet and again sends him into the ropes. The Mad Hatter ducks the clothesline! THE ASYLUM!! OUT OF NOWHERE!
HATTER WITH THE COVER! One... Two... Three!!! Rick Collings: Here is your winner...THE MAD HATTER!!! ["That Cannot Be" by Metallica plays again as The Mad Hatter makes his way back to the locker room, victorious.]
Matt Chapen- What an upset here Chris! Chris Poleski- Upset? I'm angry! I can't believe this! Larry was robbed! Matt Chapen- Larry Gionet can't believe it either! Larry Gionet will live to fight another day!

— Part 1 of 8 —
