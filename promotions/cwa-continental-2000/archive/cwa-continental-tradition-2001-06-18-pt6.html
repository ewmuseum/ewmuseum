CWA (Continental, 2000) — Card

CWA Tradition Part 6

18 June 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Matt Chapen: STREET PUNK WASTING NO TIME IN BEATING ON GIONET! Street Punk nails Larry Gionet with a headlock takedown. Street Punk goes for a forearm smash, but Larry Gionet blocks it. Larry Gionet goes for a jack-knife power bomb, but Street Punk COUNTERS WITH A SMALL PACKAGE!
One... Two... Kickout! Street Punk uses a chop on Larry Gionet. Street Punk nails Larry
Gionet with a side suplex!
Chris Poleski: Street Punk goes for a bodyslam, but Larry Gionet blocks it. Larry Gionet nails Street Punk with a Western lariat. Larry Gionet whips
Street Punk into the ropes BUT PUNK HITS HIM WITH A SHOULDERBLOCK! Street Punk nails Larry Gionet with a punch. Street Punk uses a roundhouse right on Larry
Gionet! Come on ref those are closed fists! Street Punk chops Larry Gionet.
Larry Gionet chops him back! Gionet continues the assault with a legsweep faceslam! Gionet in control of the contest and goes for the cover!
One... Two... Shoulder Up! Matt Chapen: Street Punk goes for a back suplex, but Larry Gionet counters it with a bulldog! GIONET WITH A SPLASH!
One... Two... Kickout! Chris Poleski: Larry Gionet runs into the ropes and Gionet misses the shoulderblock. Punk connects with a dropkick to the face! Cover!
One... Two... Kickout! Matt Chapen: Street Punk hits Larry Gionet with a fist to the midsection. Street Punk whips Larry Gionet into the ropes. Larry Gionet misses with a clothesline. Larry Gionet goes for a Death Valley Driver, BUT PUNK HITS HIM
WITH A DDT! The living legend just got drilled with a DDT! Who is coming down the aisle?
One... Two... Kickout! Chris Poleski: THAT'S FRAT BOY PERRY TANHAMMER! Street Punk whips Larry
Gionet into the turnbuckle, but Larry Gionet reverses it. STREET PUNK BOUNCES
BACK WITH A CLOTHESLINE! One... Two... Shoulder Up! FRAT BOY IS UP ON THE APRON! The referee is ordering him to get down. STREET PUNK NAILED FRAT BOY PERRY TANHAMMER WITH A LEFT HAND! Larry
Gionet with a school boy! HE HAS THE TIGHTS!
One... Two... Three!!! Rick Collings: Here is your winner...THE LIVING LEGEND LARRY GIONET!!! {"Running With The Devil" by Van Halen plays as The Livng Legend Larry Gionet celebrates his victory in the ring as the fans boo him.}
Chris Poleski: Yes that was the best! With the assist of Frat Boy Perry
Tanhammer, the true living legend of professional wrestling got the victory over Street Punk.
Matt Chapen: Larry Gionet rolling out of the ring and shakes the hand of Perry
Tanhammer! Street Punk does not look like a happy man! He is not keeping his eyes off of Perry Tanhammer.
Chris Poleski: Heh, what a sore loser! Matt Chapen: SORE LOSER?! Chris Poleski: Someone coming from the crowd, ITS THP! THP enters the ring and ATTACKS STREET PUNK FROM BEHIND! He whips Street Punk into the turnbuckle.
Matt Chapen: Street Punk has nothing left! THP chocking him out with his boot while Gionet and Frat Boy are applauding! This is just sick!
{You hear the roar of the crowd as Chainsaw Charlie and Kevin Candido slowly make their way down the ramp with steel chairs in their hands.}
Chris Poleski: NO ITS CHARLIE AND CANDIDO! Matt Chapen: GIONET AND TANHAMMER DON'T EVEN SEE THEM! Chris Poleski: Don't turn around! Matt Chapen: Tanhammer and Gionet are looking around to see what the comotion is about from the crowd.
{CRACK!!!} {CRACK!!!} Matt Chapen: GOOD NIGHT! PERRY TANHAMMER AND LARRY GIONET JUST GOT THEIR CLOCKED CLEANED! Chris Poleski: THP just saw what happened and stopped what he was doing! THP
IS GOING AFTER CHARLIE AND CANDIDO BUT THEY LEAVE FROM THE CROWD! What a load of cowards!
Matt Chapen: They are not cowards! They were getting some bitter sweat revenge from what happened earlier tonight! Tradition is just starting to heat up! When we come back from break, the CWA North American Championship is on the line as Jasen Casen takes on Jordan Jones!

— Part 6 of 8 —
