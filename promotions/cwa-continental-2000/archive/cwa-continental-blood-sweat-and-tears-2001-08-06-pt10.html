CWA Blood Sweat and Tears Part 10

6 August 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
{The ring crew begins setting up the steel cage for the next match, which prompts some buzzing from the crowd.]
Matt Chapen: Well, it looks like we're getting ready for main event between none other than Chainsaw Charlie and Joe Snow for the CWA World Title.
Chris Poleski: That's right, Matt... it'll be the Death Cell, the most brutal structure in CWA history... or something. Anyway, the Death Cell will have weapons inside the ring that each man is free to use.
Matt Chapen: And ladies and gentlemen, Joe Snow will be living out his boyhood dream here... to be come a world champion. To finally "be the man," so to speak.
Chris Poleski: Oh, great. You had to play out the "boyhood dream" aspect, didn't you? That's just pure crap, Chapen. Charlie's going to brutalize the
Hell out of Snow in this match.
Matt Chapen: That remains to be seen, Chris. {"Break Stuff" by Limp Bizkit plays as Joe Snow makes his way to the ring with his kendo stick. He comes to the ring and climbs to the top turnbuckle and points to the crowd that is cheering him.}
{"One Step Closer" by Linkin Park plays as Chainsaw Charlie comes to the ring with the CWA World Title around his waist and his diamond-cut chainsaw in his hand. He enters the ring and climbs to the top turnbuckle, raises the chainsaw in the air as white pyros shoot behind him.}
Chris Poleski: Hell yeah! There's our world champion! The man who's not a
[BLEEP] like Joe Snow! He doesn't give a damn about any stupid boyhood dream;
he's just in there to kick some ass, and by God, next in line is Joe "I'm Gonna
Live Out My Boyhood Dream of Finally Being a Champion, Wah, Wah" Snow!
Matt Chapen: Boy, that was a mouthful. Chris Poleski: Indeed... but 'tis true. {DING, DING, DING!} Matt Chapen: There's the bell, and the match is finally underway! Snow goes for a shot with that kendo stick, but Charlie ducked it!
Chris Poleski: Haha! Foo'! Matt Chapen: And now, Charlie just picked up a kendo stick of his own- "CRACK!" Matt Chapen: GOOD LORD! What a shot! Chris Poleski: He splintered it over Snow's back! {As Snow writhes in pain and edges towards the corner, Charlie rears back with his kendo stick again...}
"CRACK!" Chris Poleski: There ya go, Charlie! Teach that punk a lesson! Matt Chapen: It's Snow's kind of match... but Charlie is taking charge here! Wait, Snow is fighting back- he hits Charlie with some right hands! And another! Chris Poleski: C'mon, Charlie! "CRASH!" Matt Chapen: WOW! Snow just _slammed_ Charlie's face right into that chain-link steel cage! Chris Poleski: That's gotta be tearing his face to shreds! Matt Chapen: Indeed... and Snow's got Charlie again! "CRASH!" Matt Chapen: And Snow sends Charlie to the adjacent side of the cage! Chris Poleski: "Adjacent"? Don't be using no f'n big words on a CWA broadcast, young man! {The crowd pops big-time for Snow as Charlie collapses to the canvas. Snow quickly makes the cover!}
One.. Two.. Matt Chapen: NO! Charlie kicked out! Chris Poleski: Thank God! I would've had to live through some kind of stupid, melodramatic celebration...
Matt Chapen: Still, you can't deny the fact Snow would be a fighting champion. He's proving it here against the current CWA World Champion, Chainsaw Charlie! {Snow brings Charlie back to his feet... but Charlie nails him with a low blow, which garners a heel pop.}
Matt Chapen: LOW BLOW! Chris Poleski: But there's no DQ, so, hah! Matt Chapen: I know that, moron! And Charlie hits a _brutal_ DDT on Snow right onto a steel chair! He goes for a cover!
One.. Two.. Matt Chapen: NO! Snow got the shoulder up... and look at Charlie! He's smiling... what the Hell is he smiling at?!
Chris Poleski: He sees blood, Chapen; Snow's busted open from that DDT! Matt Chapen: That spells bad news for Snow- "CRACK!" "CRACK!" "CRACK!" Matt Chapen: OH GOD! Charlie just hit Snow three times with that kendo stick! Snow is reeling! Chris Poleski: Show 'em what being a champion's all about, Charlie! This isn't some kind of fantasy dream bull[BLEEP]! Show 'em!
Matt Chapen: Oh, please. And now, Charlie is picking up what looks like a
STAPLE GUN, for crying out loud!
{HARDCORE POP!} Chris Poleski: Come on, Chapen... you know the fans want to see it, I want to see it... and even _you_ want to see it, Matt!
Matt Chapen: No way! Oh, man... Charlie's got Snow cornered... but Snow's blocking it! Charlie's trying to staple gun Joe's face, but he's blocking it!
{The crowd pops big-time as Snow kicks Charlie in the nuts and takes the staple gun!} Chris Poleski: NO F'N WAY! Matt Chapen: Yes! Snow's got the staple gun- "SNAP!" Matt Chapen: SWEET JESUS! JOE JUST PUT A STAPLE RIGHT INTO CHARLIE'S FACE! AND NOW CHARLIE IS ROLLING AROUND, JUST WRITHING IN PAIN! Chris Poleski: STOP THE MATCH! {The crowd pops as Snow lifts the staple gun in the air, signaling that he's going to staple Charlie again!}
Matt Chapen: Joe wants to live out his boyhood dream... and there's no limits! Charlie is crawling towards the ropes- "SNAP!" Chris Poleski: AAH! He just stapled Charlie in the _ass_! Matt Chapen: Indeed he did! And Charlie is in real pain now! And Snow spikes an elbowdrop right into the sternum of Charlie! Cover!
One.. Two.. Thr... Matt Chapen: NO! The ref says it's a "two"! Can you believe it?! Chris Poleski: Yes! Charlie's a resiliant champion! That punk Snow'll never live up to his legend, dammit!
Matt Chapen: Snow is busted open... and so is Charlie! This is becoming a bloodbath real fast... and now, Snow is taking Charlie-
"SMASH!" Matt Chapen: -and smashes his head right into the steel cage wall! {The crowd begins cheering as Snow begins scraping Charlie's face across the chain-link cage wall repeatedly!} Chris Poleski: THAT is one of the reasons why Snow wouldn't make a good champion! The man is sadistic, dammit!
Matt Chapen: My God, the skin is peeling off Chainsaw Charlie like the skin off of an apple!
Chris Poleski: You're absolutely disgusting, Chapen! How dare you?! Matt Chapen: You're the one who wanted- nevermind. At any rate, Charlie is just pouring a fountain of crimson from his forehead now! And now, Joe Snow hits a
_brutal_ German suplex on Chainsaw Charlie!
{The crowd pops as Snow begins ascending the turnbuckles... but he hasn't stopped there. He continues climbing until he reaches the top of the cage.}
Chris Poleski: What the Hell is that maniac doing?!
Matt Chapen: I can't believe it! Joe is going for a Superfly Splash! Chris Poleski: What if he misses this? Matt Chapen: I have no idea, Poleski... but we'll just have to cross our fing- "SLAAAAM!" {HUGE POP!} Matt Chapen: GOOD GOD, HE DID MISS! CHARLIE MOVED OUT OF THE WAY JUST IN THE NICK OF TIME! Chris Poleski: I knew it! I knew it! What a fool! Boyhood dream, my ass! Matt Chapen: And now, both men are down... but Charlie is showing some signs of life! Chris Poleski: He's still hurting from that German suplex, though! Dammit, look at the blood pouring out of his head!
Matt Chapen: And now... Charlie drapes an arm over Joe Snow! One.. Two.. Thr.. {HUGE POP!} Matt Chapen: NO, NO, NO! UNBELIEVABLE! Chris Poleski: WHAT THE HELL?!? Matt Chapen: SNOW GOT THE SHOULDER UP! SNOW GOT THE SHOULDER UP, FOR GOD'S SAKES! WHAT A KID! Chris Poleski: That was a three count, I saw it! Blah! Matt Chapen: Wait... wait a minute... what's this? {The camera catches two masked men making their way to the ring. The fans seem to be confused by all this.}
Chris Poleski: I have no idea who those two are, that's for sure... Matt Chapen: Those two masked men are just standing there, watching this, as
Chainsaw Charlie has a steel chair-
"THWACK!" Matt Chapen: -and _slams_ it right into the ribs of an already-injured Joe
Snow,
dammit! Chris Poleski: That's what he deserves, the bastard. Matt Chapen: The chances of Joe becoming the CWA World Champion don't look good here, Chris... it looks like he might've broken some of his ribs after that missed splash from the top of the cage... and that chairshot isn't doing him any favors, either.
Chris Poleski: You're damn right, Matt... and that's why I'm gleaming. Chainsaw Charlie is the best CWA Champion we've ever had, and it's going to stay that way. {Charlie brings a heavily dazed Snow to his feet and sets him up in the corner. The crowd starts a chant of "SNOW! SNOW! SNOW!"} Matt Chapen: Listen to these fans! They're trying to encourage Joe Snow! Chris Poleski: Heh. Like that'll work. Matt Chapen: And Chainsaw Charlie is going for the proverbial kiss of death! He's going to cave in Snow's head with that steel chair- no! Snow moved out of the way! Snow picks up a baseball bat-
"SMACK!" Chris Poleski: NOOOO! Matt Chapen: What a comeback! Snow just nailed Charlie right in the shoulderblade with that baseball bat... and it looks like it was wrapped in barbwire!
Chris Poleski: That's even worse! Matt Chapen: Hey, wait a minute! The masked men are entering the cage! They're entering the cage! Chris Poleski: What the Hell? One of them just gave Snow a kendo stick and the other just gave Charlie a baseball bat-
"CRAAASSSSH!" Matt Chapen: What in the Hell?! The masked man just slammed Chainsaw Charlie's face right into the cage! What the Hell is this?
{SHOCKED POP!} Matt Chapen: And Snow nails Charlie with the baseball bat that Charlie had in his hands! No! What the Hell is this?
Chris Poleski: SNOWFALL DDT! SNOWFALL DDT! Matt Chapen: Joe hit the Snowfall! One.. Two.. Three!!! {DING, DING, DING!} Rick Collings: Here is your winner and NEEEEEEEEEWWWW CWA World Champion...JOE SNOW!!! {"Break Stuff" by Limp Bizkit plays as Joe Snow is handed the CWA World Title and he raises it high in the air. He is trying to catch his breath after that brutal and bloody battle.}
Matt Chapen: I can't believe it... what the Hell just happened?! Chris Poleski: Joe Snow is your new CWA World Champion, that's what happened! Matt Chapen: Those men who were just presence have left for the back! Chainsaw Charlie is down on the mat and Joe Snow is our new CWA World Champion! I don't know what to think of all this! This has been a night of chaos! Fans for Rick Collings, Todd Michaelson, and Chris Poleski, this is Matt Chapen saying GOODNIGHT EVERYONE!
