CWA (Continental, 2000) — Card

CWA Tradition Part 6

6 May 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
{The Camera shows Frat Boy Perry Tanhammer talking with Kevin Candido.} Kevin Candido: I want THP and I WANT HIM NOW! Perry Tanhammer: Woa there killer, you will get your chance in the near future! You gotta concentrate on our CWA World Title match! We gotta show all those smart marks why we are the most powerful force today and why we are the best wrestlers this world has ever seen! As the old saying goes, may the best man win and the best man will win the CWA World Title!
{Perry Tanhammer extends his hand to Kevin Candido in which he accepts it. Perry Tanhammer pats Kevin Candido on the back as he leaves. The camera goes back to Matt Chapen and Chris Poleski.}
Matt Chapen: Welcome back fans and what a night this has been so far! After
Chainsaw Charlie got the victory over his ally Joe Snow, he got viciously attacked by the North American Champion Gargoyle!
Chris Poleski: In which it was easy pickings for the North American Champ! The guy decided to take a vacation and Taylor gets all bent out of shape! I mean come on now!
Matt Chapen: Oh stop it! At Price of Glory we will have a 3 way dance for the vacant CWA North American Title! Whomever it is we don't know! All we know that Frantic is in it and the rest will be decided in the coming weeks. I have been informed that since Larry Gionet was not pinned, he is in the CWA
Traditonal Title match that has now become a three way! This should be quite interesting! Rick Collings: This contest is schedueled for one fall with a 60 minute time limit and is for the CWA Traditonal Title! First coming down the aisle...THE
LIVING LEGEND LARRY GIONET!!! {"Cum On Feel The Noize" by Quiet Riot plays as The Living Legend Larry Gionet makes his way to the ring.}
Chris Poleski: Larry Gionet is as traditonal as they come and there is no doubt in my mind that he walks out of here the champion!
Matt Chapen: I would have to disagree with you there! Al Basler has been on a roll as of later and doesn't look like he is slowing down for anybody!
Rick Collings: And his opponent...CHAD YARO!!! {"Man In The Box" by Alice And Chains plays as Chad Yaro comes to the ring as the fans cheer him on.}
Chris Poleski: Chad Yaro got the win over Mad Dog but to me that was a fluke! Lighting won't strike twice for this rookie! Matt Chapen: He is a young wrestler and will go into this match with 110% of his blood, sweat and tears!
Rick Collings: And their opponent, he is the current CWA Traditonal Champion...AL BASLER!!! {"Awake" by Godsmack plays as Al Basler makes his way to the ring with the CWA
Traditional Title around his waist as the fans go crazy.}
Chris Poleski: Al Basler runs into the ropes and Yaro nails him with a dropkick! Chad Yaro takes Al Basler down with a standing moonsault! YARO WITH THE COVER! One... Two... Kickout! SLINGSHOT LEGDROP ON THE TRADITONAL CHAMP! One... Two... Shoulder up! Matt Chapen: Chad Yaro nails Al Basler with a headlock takedown. Chad Yaro tags out to Larry Gionet! Larry Gionet goes for a Western lariat, but Al
Basler counters it with a crucifix!
One... Two... Shoulder up! Basler is so quick in that ring and thats why he is one of the best technically sound men in our sport! Al Basler makes the tag to Yaro!
Chad Yaro goes for a front facelock, but Larry Gionet blocks it. Larry Gionet hits Chad Yaro with a jack-knife power bomb!
One... Two... Shoulder Up! Larry Gionet goes for a splash, BUT YARO ROLLS OUT OF HARMS WAY! One... Two... Kickout! Chris Poleski: Chad Yaro nails Larry Gionet with a fistdrop. Chad Yaro runs into the ropes AND TAKES HIM DOWN WITH A CROSS BODY!
One... Shoulder up! Chad Yaro tags out to Al Basler. Al Basler nails Larry Gionet with a flying fistdrop!
One... Two... Shoulder up! Matt Chapen: Basler tags Yaro back in! Very smart move on keeping yourself out as much as possible! It keeps you fresh in there! Chad Yaro whips Larry
Gionet into the ropes. Larry Gionet misses with an elbow. YARO WITH A MULE
KICK! Chad Yaro takes Larry Gionet down with an armdrag takedown. Chad Yaro tags out to Al Basler! THE MAD HATTER IS MAKING HIS WAY TO RINGSIDE!
Chris Poleski: Al Basler whips Larry Gionet into the ropes and Larry Gionet nails him with a powerslam! Gionet follows up with a splash!
One... Two... Kickout! Larry Gionet goes for a jack-knife power bomb, but Al Basler counters it with a frankensteiner!
One... Two... Thr...Kickout! Al Basler attempts to place Larry Gionet on the turnbuckle, but
Larry Gionet blocks it. Al Basler goes for a spinning leg lariat, but Larry
Gionet ducks out of the way! Larry Gionet goes for a Death Valley Driver, but
Al Basler blocks it.
Al Basler runs into the ropes. GIONET NAILS HIM WITH A CLOTHESLINE SENDING HIM TO THE OUTSIDE! Matt Chapen: Larry Gionet whips Al Basler into the guardrail. GIONET HITS HIM
WITH THAT'S LEGENDARY ON THE CONCRETE FLOOR! He throws him back in! GIONET
COULD HAVE THE TITLE WON! One... Two... Thr...CHAD YARO BREAKS UP THE PIN! Chad Yaro goes back on the apron, BUT LARRY
GIONET THROWS HIM INTO THE RING! I guess that is a legal tag! MAD HATTER
GRABBED GIONET! He is pounding him on the outside!
Chris Poleski: Meanwhile in the ring, Chad Yaro places Al Basler on the turnbuckle. BELLY TO BELLY SUPLEX BY THE CHALLENGER!
One... Two... Thr...Kickout! Matt Chapen: I THOUGHT HE HAD HIM! WE COULD HAVE HAD A NEW CWA TRADITONAL
CHAMPION! had Yaro whips Al Basler into the turnbuckle. Al Basler comes back, but is met with a clothesline! FRANKENSTEINER BY THE CHALLENGER!
One... Two... Thr...Shoulder Up! Chad Yaro uses a moonsault bodyblock on Al Basler. One... Two... Thr...In the ropes! With Basler's last bit of strength and awarness he was able to put that foot on the rope!
Chris Poleski: Chad Yaro whips Al Basler into the ropes. Al Basler misses with a shoulderblock. Al Basler hits Chad Yaro with a clothesline! Al Basler goes for a figure-four leglock, but Chad Yaro blocks it. Chad Yaro goes for a slingshot bodyblock, but Al Basler rolls through with the move INTO THE LIGHTS
OUT DDT! HATTER JUST NAILED LARRY GIONET WITH THE ASYLUM ON THE OUTSIDE!
One... Two... Three!!! Rick Collings: Here is your winner, and still CWA Traditional Champion...AL BASLER!!! {"Awake" by Godsmack plays as Al Basler has his hand raised in victory as he holds the CWA Traditional Title in his other hand.}
Matt Chapen: That was unbelievable! Yaro thought he had that match won but within a second, Al Basler turned the tide and won the match and retaining the title!
Chris Poleski: Ironman Jordan Jones is on the top turnbuckle! GET HIM KILLER! Matt Chapen: Jones takes flight, BUT GETS A FIST TO THE MIDSECTION FOR HIS TROUBLES! LIGHTS OUT DDT BY THE TRADITIONAL CHAMP! Chris Poleski: That cheater! Al Basler has the mic! Al Basler: Jordan Jones, we had an issue that never got settled! I say we settle this once and for all at Price of Glory! You wanna take this title from me boy, you gotta KILL ME FOR IT!
{Al Basler drops the mic and leaves for the back as Chad Yaro stands in the ring.} Matt Chapen: Al Basler means all business tonight and defended his title! He will defend it again at the May PPV! Chad Yaro is wating for Casen's suprise!
{Jasen Casen appears on the TraditionTron with a grin on his face.} Jasen Casen: HAHA, Yaro you have been waiting for this moment! Waiting nervously in that locker room to see exactly what my big suprise is! I won't keep you in misery any longer, but then again maybe its just beginning! Come here baby!
{You see Cherry walk into the room and hugging Jasen Casen.} Matt Chapen: THAT'S CHERRY! YARO'S EX FLAME! Jasen Casen: Look who it is Chad, your old main squeeze! The one that you left high and dry in the middle of that ring just like you did to me "friend!"
Got anything to say babe? Cherry: Hey Chad you miss me hon? I wanted to come out to show you what a real man can do! Casen is the real deal and can satisfy me in more ways than one and thats a lot more than you could ever do Yaro! So I say why don't the three of us get together! Let's have a little date with destiny on May 29th at
Price of Glory!
Jasen Casen: Come on Yaro, what do you say? Chad Yaro: I ACCEPT! As for you Cherry, don't even think about getting in my business babe, because if you do I promise you that you WILL regret it for the rest of your life!
{Cherry looks disgusted as her and Casen leave the room as the screen fades to black.} Chris Poleski: Another match signed for Price of Glory! Casen and Yaro with
Cherry in the middle of it! This is great!
Matt Chapen: When we come back its time for the main event! Frat Boy vs Kevin
Candido for the CWA World Title!
