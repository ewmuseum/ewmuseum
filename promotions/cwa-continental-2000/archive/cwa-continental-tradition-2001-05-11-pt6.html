CWA (Continental, 2000) — Card

CWA Tradition Part 6

11 May 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Matt Chapen: Fans this has been a great night thus far! In an epic battle,
Jordan Jones earned himself the right to take on Frantic and whomever else earns the shot next week to determine who wins the CWA North American Title at
Price of Glory!
Chris Poleski: But before he gets to Price of Glory, he challenged the Traditonal Champion Al Basler to a match next week on Tradition. He was the very opponent that he was to face at Price of Glory but tonight changed all that!
Matt Chapen: But who can forget the vicious attack laid out by THP and Frat Boy on Jon Massey! It was just henious! We now know that Kevin Candido's tag team partner is Chainsaw Charlie and Tanhammer's opponent at the PPV! Coming up next the man who has seen his fair share of controversy here in the CWA. That is the former North American champ
Frantic! Chris Poleski: That's right..former. And he shouldn't be getting a chance at
Price of Glory. He should have to go up against the TRUE North American champion, my main man..Gargoyle!!
Matt Chapen: Well some say that Gargoyle never officially lost the North American title, and that maybe..just maybe Frantic had something to do with that. Chris Poleski: I think he had EVERYTHING to do with it!!! Matt Chapen: Well Frantic about to face a man that most people think should be the number one contender to the North American title, and that's Chad
Yaro. Chris Poleski: I personally feel that Chad Yaro is being overlooked by the
CWA brass in this matter. He SHOULD be in that three way for the NA title!
Matt Chapen: Well we will have to see what develops because here comes Chad
Yaro. {"Man In the Box" by Alice And Chains plays as Chad Yaro comes to the ring as the fans cheer.}
Chris Poleski: And here comes the FORMER..and I mean FORMER North American champion. ::"Same Old Song and Dance" begins to play. As the drum beats hit fireworks shoot off into the air and the crowd goes nuts. Frantic walks out amidst the smoke and raises his hand in the air as the crowd gets louder. He begins to walk down the ramp slapping hands the whole way. Frantic jumps up onto the apron and then hops over the top rope and lands on the second rope on the inside. He raises his hand again as the crowd cheers.::
Matt Chapen: There's the bell and here we go! ::Frantic and Yaro circle around the ring a bit before Yaro and Frantic lock up. Yaro comes out of it with an armbar on Frantic. Yaro turns it over again and levels Frantic with a clothesline. Frantic back up to his feet quickly though as Yaro bounces off the ropes Frantic drops to the ground as Yaro hops over him and bounces off the opposite ropes. Frantic back to his feet he lines up and goes for a superkick but Yaro ducks it and bounces off the ropes again. Yaro charges at Frantic but Frantic starts to back up towards the ropes. Frantic leans back against the ropes and brings up his legs.
Frantic grabs Yaro's head with his legs and uses Yaro's own momentum to send him over with a hurricanrana to the floor!!!::
Matt Chapen: My god!! It's moves like that that makes Frantic such a fearsome force in this federation!!
Chris Poleski: Yeah that move looked almost as good as...WHEN GARGOYLE DEATH DIVED FRANTIC!!! HA HA HA HA Matt Chapen: Frantic landed on his feet on the outside. He hops back up onto the apron, bounces off the second rope and nails Yaro with a moonsault!!!
Frantic is so quick and his luchador training certainly comes in handy when he is dealing with some of these smaller guys in the CWA!!
Chris Poleski: Yeah but it doesn't mean crap against a powerhouse like Gargoyle!! Matt Chapen: Would you shut up with that! Chris Poleski: No! Matt Chapen: Frantic now on his feet and pulling Yaro up. ::Frantic rolls Yaro back into the ring. Frantic hops over the top rope and goes to pick Yaro up again but Yaro nails Frantic with an elbow to the midsection. Yaro then stands and drops Frantic with a DDT. Yaro gaining momentum as he drops another elbow down on Frantic. Yaro pulls Frantic up to his feet and bounces him against the ropes. Frantic comes back and Yaro nails Frantic with a stiff forearm but Frantic doesn't go down! Yaro grabs
Frantic and pulls him up for a suplex but Frantic floats over and pushes
Yaro into the ropes and rolls him up!!:: One... Kickout! Matt Chapen: Frantic with a quick rollup trying to catch Yaro off guard!! Chris Poleski: Kind of like when Gargoyle caught Frantic off guard when he took his North American title!!!
::Frantic and Yaro lock up, Yaro throws Frantic into the corner and comes charging in. Frantic moves and Yaro slams into the turnbuckle. Frantic jumps up onto the top turnbuckle and is setting up for the Doubledeetee but Yaro pulls him off of the turnbuckle and into the ring. Yaro goes to grab Frantic but Frantic kicks Yaro in the head. Frantic back up and Yaro charges nice hip toss by Frantic but Yaro is right back up. Yaro charges again and
Frantic catches Yaro and nails him with a belly to belly suplex. Frantic pulls Yaro up and throws him towards the corner. Yaro jumps up onto the top turnbuckle and jumps off and nails Frantic with a viscous spinning kick to the head!!!::
Matt Chapen: What a shot to the head by Yaro!!! Chris Poleski: Frantic is going to wake up thinking he's still the North American champ!!! ::Yaro goes in for the kill when suddenly the Traditiontron lights up:: Jasen Casen: Hey Yaro!! :::Yaro doesn't pay attention:: Jasen: I said..HEY YARO!!!! ::Yaro looks at the Traditiontron and you see Jacen Casen smiling:: Jasen: Hey there Chad..remember me? Matt Chapen: Who the hell is this? Chris Poleski: Beats me. Jasen: Yeah Yaro I know you do. It's me...your old buddy Jacen Casen. Chris Poleski: Kasey Kasem!?!?! Matt Chapen: Jacen Casen you dolt! Jasen: I know it's been awhile Yaro but I have someone here who wants to see you again. :::Jasen moves aside and you see Cherry come onto the screen. He lipstick is a bit smeared and her hair is russled:::
Matt Chapen: THAT'S CHERRY!!! CHAD YARO'S EX-GIRLFRIEND!! JUST LEAVE THE GUY ALONE DAMNIT! Cherry: There were some things I didn't get to tell you last week so let me say them now. You know Chad, I just wanted to let you know that I really appreciated everything that you did for me. But I think I've found someone...better suited for my needs. You were good Chad...but Jasen's better.
:::Chad stands in disbelief in the ring as Frantic gets back up. Frantic looks at the Traditiontron and then at Yaro. Frantic walks over to the turnbuckle and hops up and sits down on the top turnbuckle as Chad continues to watch the Traditiontron::
Jasen: So you see old buddy, just letting you know that...well...that your going to be very lonely tonight...and for a lot of nights to come.
::Jasen stops speaking as a bra lands on his head:: Jasen: Whoa...well Chad, I think I need to go. Got um...business to attend to. See ya bro! ::Jasen smiles and the Traditiontron turns off. Chad Yaro is standing in the ring trying to keep his rage in check. His face is red with anger. He turns and sees Frantic sitting in the corner::
Matt Chapen: I think Yaro is looking for someone to take some aggression out on. Chris Poleski: I think he just found him. Matt Chapen: This doesn't look good for Frantic. Chris Poleski: Yeah, then he'll have two people to fear!! Chad Yaro and Gargoyle!! ::Chad Yaro blindly charges the corner as Frantic stands up on the second rope. As Yaro gets closer Frantic grabs his head and knees him in the face.
DOUBLEDEETEE!!!!! FRANTIC NAILS YARO WITH THE DOUBLEDEETEE!!!!!:: Matt Chapen: Frantic was waiting for him!! Chris Poleski: I wouldn't be surprised if Frantic is behind Jacen Casen as well!!! Matt Chapen: Frantic with the cover!! One... Two... Three!!! Rick Collins: Here is your winner...FRANTIC!!! Chris Poleski: Frantic again with a carefully laid out plan to defeat his opponent!! What a cheat!!
Matt Chapen: Frantic had nothing to do with any of that! He was just smart and waited for Yaro to come after him!
Chris Poleski: Yeah I bet, Frantic is up to something. Matt Chapen: No he isn't your just trying to stir things up. Chris Poleski: We'll see. Matt Chapen: Fans we will be back!!!
