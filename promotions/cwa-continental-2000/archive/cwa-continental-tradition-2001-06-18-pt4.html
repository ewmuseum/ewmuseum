CWA Tradition Part 4

18 June 2001

Matt Chapen: ACE FIRESIDE WITH A NECKSCISSORS! Ace Fireside hits Buddy Costello with a flying sunset flip! One... Two... Shoulder Up! Chris Poleski: Ace Fireside goes for an inverted power bomb, but Buddy Costello blocks it. Buddy Costello whips Ace Fireside into the ropes and
Costello misses the clothesline. Ace Fireside hits Buddy Costello with a shoulderblock. Ace Fireside goes for a rana, BUT COSTELLO COUNTERS WITH A
TIGER DRIVER! NICE! PUT HIM AWAY! One... Two... Kickout! Ace Fireside runs into the ropes AND NAILS HIM WITH A DROPKICK! Matt Chapen: Ace Fireside uses a short clothesline on Buddy Costello. Ace
Fireside goes for a chinlock deathlock submission, but Buddy Costello blocks it. Buddy Costello goes for the Doctor Bomb, but Ace Fireside counters it with a backdrop! Buddy Costello needs to keep on Ace Fireside if he wants to win this match! If he keeps him grounded then he has a chance!
Chris Poleski: Buddy Costello whips Ace Fireside into the ropes BUT FIRESIDE NAILS HIM WITH A SUPERKICK! One... Two... Kickout! Ace Fireside whips Buddy Costello into the ropes. Ace Fireside misses with a clothesline. Buddy Costello goes for a back heel kick, but Ace
Fireside counters it with a cradle suplex! Ace Fireside goes for an elbowdrop from the second turnbuckle, BUT BUDDY MOVES OUT OF THE WAY! BIG TIME MISTAKE
BY FIRESIDE! One... Two... Kickout! Matt Chapen: That almost cost him the match like you said. This has to be a warmup for ace since Costello and Natural have similar wrestling backgrounds.
Ace Fireside goes for a figure-four leglock, but Buddy Costello blocks it. Buddy Costello whips Ace Fireside into the ropes. Ace Fireside misses with a kick. Buddy Costello misses with a shoulderblock. Ace Fireside goes for a legsweep faceslam, but Buddy Costello counters it with a faceslam!
Chris Poleski: Buddy Costello runs into the ropes. Buddy Costello hits Ace
Fireside with a shoulderblock.
One... Two... Thr...Kickout! Matt Chapen: Buddy Costello runs into the ropes and Fireside drills him with a clothesline! Fireside picks him up FOR THE FIRESIDE BOMB! HE NAILED HIM WITH
IT!
One... Two... Three!!! Rick Collings: Here is your winner...ACE FIRESIDE!!! {"Princes of the Universe" by Queen plays as Ace Fireside celebrates his victory.} Matt Chapen: Buddy Costello is has left the ring to head to the back. He had a great fight with Ace and will have many more! Ace Firside leaving the ring to high five some fans at ringside!
Chris Poleski: LOOK! Matt Chapen: CHRIS NATURAL FROM BEHIND WITH A CLOTHESLINE! He whips Ace
Fireside into the guardrail! Fireside bounces foward INTO THE BELLY TO BELLY
BY NATURAL! Chris Poleski: This is awesome! Chris Natural stomping a mudhole in Ace
Fireside, making him not forget what he did to him!
Matt Chapen: Chris Natural runs into the crowd before officials could get to him! Go figure! We understand Mark Blaze is in the back!
{Mark Blaze walks into his locker room.} Mark Blaze: What the hell?! {He sees some writing on the wall that says "I'm going to get you!"} Mark Blaze: That son of a bitch! {He punches a locker to vent out his fustration as he leaves the room.} Matt Chapen: Looks like Buddy Costello is playing mind games with his opponent for the PPV!
Chris Poleski: Who said its him? Matt Chapen: I do! Costello wants to get it in the head of Mark Blaze that his days are numbered and I wouldn't want to be Costello right now! We will be back!
