CWA (Continental, 2000) — Card

CWA Tradition Part 7

15 July 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Matt Chapen: We are back and Jasen Casen had a successful title defense against Street Punk! The more interesting story is that Chad Yaro helped out
Jasen Casen again when he nearly got his head taken off by Sean Harris!
Chris Poleski: Damn Yaro, he can't keep his nose out of other people's business! {The camera shows Jasen Casen walking down the hall and bumps into Chad Yaro.}
Jasen Casen: Listen Chad we have to have a little talk! I don't know what you are trying to pull with helping me out!
Chad Yaro: I ain't pulling nothing Casen! You don't gert it do you? Anthony
Davis tried to take my ass out and Sean Harris tried to take your damn head off out there. Anthony Davis and Sean Harris are in this together. YOu see they know we don't see eye to eye and they want that! If we let our past get to us, they are gonna tear us apart piece by piece until the both of us are gone from this sport! For now we have a common enemy. We can either watch each other's back and beat these boys at their own game just this once or we can go down the road we are going and let them eat us alive! Its your choice!
{Jasen Casen nods.} Jasen Casen: You're right! Davis, Harris, I'm sick and damn tired of your mind games and I say Chad and I finish you boys off! Next week on Tradition let's have a tag match: Chad Yaro and Jasen Casen against Anthony Davis and
Sean Harris! What do you say boys?
Chad Yaro: Hey fellas, you feeling lucky? Then roll the dice and take the chance! {Chad Yaro and Jasen Casen shake hands as the fans go nuts. The camera then goes back to Matt Chapen and Chris Poleski.}
Chris Poleski: What did we just see there? Matt Chapen: I think we saw perhaps just for one time, an unstoppable force that is going to wreck havok on Anthony Davis and Sean Harris!
Rick Collings: This contest is schedueled for one fall with a 60 minute time limit! First coming down the aisle...THE LIVING LEGEND LARRY GIONET!!!
{"Running With The Devil" by Van Halen plays as The Living Legend Larry Gionet comes to the ring as the fans give him a mixed reaction.}
Rick Collings: And his tag team partner...FRAT BOY PERRY TANHAMMER!!! {"Mr. Speed" by KISS plays as Frat Boy Perry Tanhammer makes his way to the ring as the fans boo him like there is no tomorrow.}
Rick Collings: And their opponents. First coming down the aisle...JOE SNOW!!! {"Break Stuff" by Limp Bizkit plays as Joe Snow comes to the ring with his signapore cane in hand as the fans cheer him on. He enters the ring and climbs on the top turnbuckle and points to the crowd with his kendo stick.}
Rick Collings: And his tag team partner, he is the current CWA World Champion...CHAINSAW CHARLIE!!! {"One Step Closer" by Linkin Park plays as Chainsaw Charlie comes to the ring with the CWA World Title around his waist and his diamond cut chainsaw in his hand. He enters the ring and raises the chainsaw in the air as white pyros shot in the air as the fans go crazy.}
Chris Poleski: They lock up. Joe Snow hits Larry Gionet with a belly-to-back suplex. Joe Snow goes for a bow-and-arrow submission, but Larry Gionet blocks it.
Larry Gionet goes for a shoulderbreaker, but JOE SNOW COUNTERS WITH AN ELBOWSMASH! Joe Snow goes for a dropkick, but Larry Gionet side-steps and Joe
Snow only hits air. Larry Gionet whips Joe Snow into the ropes. Joe Snow goes for an Asai moonsault, BUT GIONET NAILS HIM WITH A DROPKICK IN MIDAIR!
Matt Chapen: Larry Gionet is a seasoned veteran and has a few more years experienced over Joe Snow! Larry Gionet goes for a jack-knife power bomb, but
Joe Snow blocks it. INSIDE CRADLE!
One... Two... Kickout! Larry Gionet whips Joe Snow into the ropes, but Joe Snow reverses it. Joe Snow misses with a clothesline. SNOW WITH A SINGLE ARM DDT! Joe Snow showing that he is more than a hardcore wrestler!
One... Two... Kickout! Chris Poleski: Joe Snow tags out to Chainsaw Charlie. Perry Tanhammer enters the ring and lays out Joe Snow. Perry Tanhammer nails Chainsaw Charlie with a football tackle. Larry Gionet goes for a legsweep faceslam, but Chainsaw
Charlie COUNTERS IT WITH A FACESLAM! Larry Gionet and Perry Tanhammer whip
Chainsaw Charlie into the ropes and nail him with a double kick to the midsection. Gionet makes the tag to Tanhammer! JOE SNOW THROWS GIONET OUT OF
THE RING! Get Snow out of there ref!
Matt Chapen: Snow didn't want Larry Gionet trying anything funny! Chainsaw
Charlie and Joe Snow whip Perry Tanhammer into the ropes AND CONNECT WITH A
DOUBLE CLOTHESLINE! Both men seem to be working considerably well! Chainsaw
Charlie executes an inverted atomic drop on Perry Tanhammer. Chainsaw Charlie whips Perry Tanhammer into the ropes. Perry Tanhammer goes for a football tackle, but CHARLIE COUNTERS WITH A DDT! COVER!
One... Two... Kickout! Chainsaw Charlie whips Perry Tanhammer into the ropes but Tanhammer nails the champion with an inziguiri to the face! Perry Tanhammer tags out to
Larry Gionet!
Chris Poleski: Larry Gionet goes for a Western lariat, BUT CHARLIE COUNTERS WITH A POWERSLAM! One... Two... Kickout! Chainsaw Charlie hits Larry Gionet with a backdrop AND GIONET FALLS
OUT OF THE RING! Perry Tanhammer comes over to make it two-on-one. Perry
Tanhammer nails Chainsaw Charlie with a DDT. Larry Gionet whips Chainsaw
Charlie into the guardrail. SNOW LAYS OUT PERRY TANHAMMER!
Matt Chapen: Joe Snow grabs a chair. Joe Snow takes a running start and springs off the chair. DROPKICK BY THE SNOW DOGG! Joe Snow goes for another dropkick, but Larry Gionet moves out of the way. Snow went for that one to many times! Larry Gionet takes Chainsaw Charlie down with a Western lariat.
He throws him back in. GIONET WITH THE COVER! One... Two... Kickout! INSIDE CRADLE BY CHARLIE! One... Two... Kickout! Chris Poleski: Chainsaw Charlie goes for a vertical suplex, but Larry Gionet blocks it. Larry Gionet goes for a shoulderbreaker, but CHARLIE SLIDES DOWN
HIS BACK! Chainsaw Charlie goes for a short clothesline, but Larry Gionet ducks out of the way! Gionet tags to Frat Boy! Joe Snow enters the ring and lays out Larry Gionet. Chainsaw Charlie and Joe Snow whip Perry Tanhammer into the ropes and take him down with a double clothesline!
Matt Chapen: Chainsaw Charlie runs into the ropes and Charlie hits Perry
Tanhammer with a kick. Chainsaw Charlie goes for a back suplex, but Perry
Tanhammer counters it with a facerake. Perry Tanhammer goes for a football tackle, but CHARLIE COUNTERS WITH A KNEELIFT! Chainsaw Charlie tags out to Joe
Snow. Larry Gionet enters the ring and throws Chainsaw Charlie out of the ring. Larry Gionet hits Joe Snow with a shoulderbreaker. Perry Tanhammer goes for a flying bulldog, BUT SNOW THROWS HIM OFF!
Chris Poleski: Joe Snow goes for a Death Valley Driver, but Perry Tanhammer counters it with a DDT. Perry Tanhammer throws Joe Snow out of the ring.
Larry Gionet goes for a Death Valley Driver, BUT SNOW COUNTERS WITH A BACKDROP! Perry Tanhammer whips Joe Snow into the guardrail. Matt Chapen: Chainsaw Charlie takes Perry Tanhammer down with a short clothesline. Joe Snow knocks Perry Tanhammer into the ringsteps. JOE SNOW HAS
A ROAD SIGN! {SMACK!!!} Matt Chapen: Larry Gionet comes over and NAILS CHAINSAW CHARLIE WITH A CLOTHESLINE! Perry Tanhammer whips Joe Snow into the guardrail. Perry Tanhammer throws Joe Snow back into the ring! Chris Poleski: Perry Tanhammer tags out to Larry Gionet. Larry Gionet goes for a shoulderbreaker, but Joe Snow counters it with an elbowsmash. SNOW TAGS
THE WORLD CHAMPION! Joe Snow tags out to Chainsaw Charlie. Chainsaw Charlie and Joe Snow whip Larry Gionet into the ropes and hit him with a fist to the midsection! Chainsaw Charlie goes for a snap mare, but Larry Gionet blocks it.
Larry Gionet whips Chainsaw Charlie into the ropes, but Charlie reverses it! THE CHAMPION WITH A CLOTHESLINE OVER THE TOP! Charlie with a running start! MOONSAULT PLANCHA! Matt Chapen: They're brawling inside the ring area. Chainsaw Charlie whips
Larry Gionet into the guardrail. They're brawling inside the ring area. Joe
Snow goes for a clothesline but he ducks and nails him with a lowblow!
Chainsaw Charlie goes for a right hand, but Larry Gionet blocks it. He throws him back in! Gionet has Charlie set up! THAT'S LEGENDARY! HE NAILED THE
CHAMPION WITH THAT'S LEGENDARY! One... Two... Thr...JOE SNOW SAVED CHARLIE! The world champion was one second away from defeat! Larry Gionet goes for a jack-knife power bomb, but Chainsaw Charlie blocks it. Chainsaw Charlie whips Larry Gionet into the ropes, but Gionet hits him with a shoulder block! Gionet leaving the ring and he has a steel chair!
Chris Poleski: He makes the tag to Perry Tanhammer! Tanhammer whips Chainaw
Charlie into the ropes but CHARLIE REVERSES IT! TANHAMMER GETS RAMMED INTO
GIONET! GIONET DOESN'T LOOK PLEASED! Matt Chapen: HE IS GONNA BLAST TANHAMMER IN THE HEAD WITH THAT STEEL CHAIR! He swings at Tanhammer! {CRACK!!!} Matt Chapen: Tanhammer ducked and Larry Gionet nailed Chainsaw Charlie instead! Perry Tanhammer with the cover!
One... Two... Three!!! Rick Collings: Here is your winner...THE LIVING LEGEND LARRY GIONET AND FRAT BOY PERRY TANHAMMER!!! {"Mr. Speed by KISS plays as the referee raises the hands of Larry Gionet and
Frat Boy Perry Tanhammer.}
Chris Poleski: I can't believe they did it! Larry Gionet and Frat Boy won the match! Matt Chapen: Frat Boy is one lucky son of a bitch I'll tell ya that! Frat Boy nearly got decapitated with that steel chair! Joe Snow has left the ring and is heading to the back!
Chris Poleski: LARRY GIONET AND FRAT BOY PERRY TANHAMMER ARE STARING EACH OTHER DOWN! Matt Chapen: Gionet and Frat Boy are gonna explode! Chainsaw Charlie is knocked out cold and Joe Snow is just looking on the rampway! What in the world is going on here? Fans we are out of time! For Rick Collings, Todd
Michaelson and Chris Poleski, this is Matt Chapen saying goodnight everyone!
