CWA (Continental, 2000) — Card

CWA Tradition Part 4

13 November 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Matt Chapen: Welcome back fans and we just saw a great match between Mr. World and Gargoyle where Mr. World put him away with the Authority.
Chris Poleski: Not only that but things got personal as Mr. World verbally assaulted Chainsaw Charlie which nearly came to blows.
Matt Chapen: Its indeed an intense rivalry that is reaching its boiling point. Chris Poleski: We're going back to the back, where Adam Mitchell is running loose!!! {Cut to Mitchell in the back, breaking down doors and looking for his attacker from last week...}
Adam Mitchell: Attack me up front you little (bleep).... {Adam Mitchell slams open a door and barges in on the CWA World Champion Chad
Yaro....} Adam Mitchell: Where is he?! {Chad Yaro remains quiet....} Adam Mitchell: I see how it is...Yaro, you'll get yours. The entire CWA has theirs coming. No talent pieces of shit you are......
{Adam Mitchell walks out and down the hall,spoting his attacker who begins to run as MItchell starts the chase.......]
Adam Mitchell: Run from me now you little prick! {Nighthawk runs out some exit doors and Mitchell gives chase. Mitchell is out the door and is met with a baseball shot to the midsection and one across the back before Nighthawk runs again and out of the camera's view....}
Matt Chapen: That little son of a... Chris Poleski: Hey Matt you need to cool yourself down man! Rick Collings: This contest is schedueled for one fall and is for the CWA
Traditional Championship! First coming down the aisle, he is the challenger...RICK TAYLOR!!!
{"Only for the Weak" by In Flames starts up over the PA, and Rick Taylor emerges from the entranceway, wearing his wrestling gear and a black t-shirt with the sleeves cut off. He heads to the ring, not paying much attention to the fans. Taylor makes it to the ring and pauses on the ring apron. He faces the crowd and drags his thumb across his throat to a huge pop before entering the ring.}
Rick Collings: And his opponent, he is the current CWA Traditional
Champion...ACE FIRESIDE!!!
{"Princes of the Universe" by Queen plays as Ace Fireside comes to the ring with the CWA Traditional Title around his waist.}
Matt Chapen: Both men circling each other. Rick Taylor goes for a thrust kick but Fireside wisely backs away. Fireside goes for a spinkick but Taylor ducks.
Both men tieup, headlock by Fireside countered by a Taylor throw into the ropes. Ace Fireside nails him with a shoulderblock. The Traditional Champion with a snapmare continued BY A STIFF KICK TO THE SHOULDERBLADES!
Chris Poleski: Good lord! Did you hear that Matt? That was vicious. Ace
Fireside picks up the challenger and whips him into the turnbuckle but Rick reverses it. Rick Taylor runs at Fireside but gets a boot to the face for his troubles! Ace Fireside runs towards Rick Taylor AND GETS CAUGHT WITH A
POWERSLAM! WHAT IMPACT BY THE CHALLENGER! One... Two... Kickout! Matt Chapen: Rick Taylor is kicking away at the Traditional Champion. Rick
Taylor sends Fireside for the ride and connects with a picture perfect dropkick! Rick Taylor goes for a kick, but Ace Fireside counters with a dragon screw! Fireside grabs the leg of Taylor AND LOCKS ON A REVERSE FIGURE FOUR!
Rick Taylor agonizing in pain here. One... Two... Shoulder up! Rick Taylor fighting to get out of the hold! He grabs a hold of the ropes forcing Ace Fireside to release it.
Chris Poleski: That was a great showing of Rick Taylor's presence of mind in that ring. Ace Fireside picks up Rick Taylor. What is he doing here? ACE
FIRESIDE WITH A RUNNING DEATH VALLEY DRIVER! COVER BY THE CHAMPION!
One... Two... Thr...Kickout! Rick Taylor with his last bit of strength kicked out at the last second! Fireside runs into the ropes, leapfrog by Taylor. Ace goes for a cross body, but Rick counters with a tilt a wirl INTO A HANGMAN DDT! HOLY
SHIT! Matt Chapen: This crowd is stunned! The CWA Traditional Champion just got flattened like an accordion! Rick Taylor hooks the leg!
One... Two... Thr...KICKOUT! The challenger grabs Ace from behind and flips him over. ACE
FIRESIDE LANDS ON HIS FEET! FRIESIDE WITH A DROPKICK TO THE BACK OF THE HEAD
OF TAYLOR! One... Two... Kickout! Chris Poleski: This is one intense battle for the CWA Traditional Title! Ace
Fireside whips Rick Taylor into the ropes but Taylor reverses it! Rick Taylor with a leapfrog, but Ace Fireside holds onto the ropes before he went flying at him. Fireside ducks the clothesline. Kick to the gut INTO THE FIRESIDE BOMB!
Ace Fireside hooks the leg! One... Two... Three!!! Rick Collings: Here is your winner and still CWA Traditional Champion...ACE FIRESIDE!!! {"Princes of the Universe" by Queen plays as Ace Fireside has his hand raised in victory and has the CWA Traditional Title high above his head as the fans go crazy.}
Matt Chapen: Ace Fireside picks up the victory here on Tradition. He is helping up Rick Taylor to his feet.
Chris Poleski: What is he doing? Matt Chapen: Ace Fireside extends his hand out to Rick Taylor and he accepts it! Rick Taylor nods to Fireside as he rolls out of the ring. Nice example of sportsmanship right there. Fans when we return the North American Title is on the line as Shane Russell defends against the 7 footer, THP!
