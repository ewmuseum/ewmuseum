CWA (Continental, 2000) — Card

CWA Tradition Part 7

18 June 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Matt Chapen: For those who has just joined us, thanks to the help of one Frat
Boy Perry Tanhammer, Larry Gionet put away Street Punk for the victory. As
Gionet and Tanhammer looked on, THP attacked Street Punk from behind. While their backs where turned Kevin Candido and Chainsaw Charlie got some much needed revenge and nailed both Larry Gionet and Frat Boy Perry Tanhammer with steel chairs!
Chris Poleski: Those two will get what's coming to them at Journey's End I know it! Rick Collings: This contest is schedueled for one fall with a 60 minute time limit and is for the CWA North American Championship! First coming down the aisle, he is the challenger being acoompanied to the ring by Cherry...JASEN
CASEN!!! {"Greed" by Godsmack plays as Cherry followed by Jasen Casen comes to the ring to a chorus of boos.}
Matt Chapen: Jasen Casen, looking to get an advantage mentally on Ironman
Jordan Jones by defeating him for the title so he comes into the PPV as champion!
Rick Collings: And his opponent, he is the current CWA North American Champion...IRONMAN JORDAN JONES!!! Chris Poleski: Jordan Jones runs into the ropes and Jones hits a clothesline! Jordan Jones hits Jasen Casen with flying headscissors. Jordan Jones goes for a headlock takedown, BUT CASEN THROWS HIM OFF! Jasen Casen whips Jordan Jones into the ropes and the challenger nails him with an elbow! Jasen Casen goes for a vertical suplex, but Jordan Jones COUNTERS WITH A SMALL PACKAGE!
One... Two... Kickout! Matt Chapen: Jasen Casen goes for a bodyslam, but Jordan Jones counters it with a facerake/ Jordan Jones goes for a tiger suplex, but Jasen Casen blocks it. Jasen Casen whips Jordan Jones into the turnbuckle, but Jordan Jones reverses it. Jasen Casen comes back, but is met with a clothesline! Both men giving it their all to see who is the better man! Jordan Jones goes for a fireman's carry, but Jasen Casen blocks it. Jasen Casen runs into the ropes and Casen hits a CROSS BODY!
One... Two... Kickout! Chris Poleski: Jasen Casen climbs to the top turnbuckle, but JONES THROWS HIM
TO THE MAT! Jordan Jones whips Jasen Casen into the ropes and nails him with a dropkick! Jordan Jones whips Jasen Casen into the ropes, but Jasen Casen reverses it. THE CHALLENGER WITH A VICIOUS CLOTHESLINE SENDING HIM UP AND OVER
THE TOP! Look at Cherry cheering her man on! What a nice girl ain't she?
Matt Chapen: Yeah right! Jasen Casen knocks Jordan Jones into the ringsteps. Jasen Casen nails Jordan Jones with a piledriver. Jasen Casen reenters the ring. Jasen Casen goes for a DDT, but Jordan Jones COUNTERS WITH A NORTHERN
LIGHTS SUPLEX! One... Two... Thr...Kickout! GERMAN SUPLEX BY THE CHAMPION! One... Two... Kickout! Chris Poleski: Jordan Jones takes Jasen Casen down with a double underhook suplex! This has to put him away!
One... Two... Kickout! Matt Chapen: Jordan Jones whips Jasen Casen into the turnbuckle. Jordan Jones runs shoulder-first into the corner, but Jasen Casen lifts his knee. JORDAN
JONES WITH A TOP ROPE BULLDOG! One... Two... Three!!! Rick Collings: Here is your winner and still CWA North American Champion...IRONMAN JORDAN JONES!!! Matt Chapen: What an unbelievable matchup we just saw! The two men that will fight for that title just gave it their all tonight in that ring. Jasen Casen has nothing to be ashamed of because he busted his ass and if this is any indication of what lies ahead for Journey's End, I believe that we are in for a classic!
Chris Poleski: He may have busted his ass but he is pissed! Jasen Casen dishing out some rights hands to Jordan Jones! THE CHAMPION FIRES BACK WITH
SOME CHOPS OF HIS OWN! Matt Chapen: Jasen Casen knocks Jordan Jones AND IS LAYING LEFTS AND RIGHTS TO THE CHAMPION! THE CHAMPION IS BEING BATTERED! Chris Poleski: Officials pulling Jasen Casen off of Jordan Jones! Jasen Casen is like a house of fire in there Chapen!
Matt Chapen: Yes he is and its that fire and determination that could very well take him to the CWA North American Championship! Fans, when we come back its time for the main event! THP and Joe Snow coming up NEXT!

— Part 7 of 8 —
