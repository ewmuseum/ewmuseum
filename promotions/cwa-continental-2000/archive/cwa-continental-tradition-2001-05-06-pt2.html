CWA (Continental, 2000) — Card

CWA Tradition Part 2

6 May 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Matt Chapen: We are back! If you have just joined us, Frat Boy cost THP the matchup so that Candido would get the world title shot!
Chris Poleski: That is gonna be a great matchup in my view! Rick Collings: This tag team contest is schedueled for one fall with a 30 minute time limit! First coming down the aisle...THE MADHATTER AND THE
JESTER!!! {"The Thing That Should Not Be" by Metallica plays as The Mad Hatter and The
Jester make their way to the ring as the fans boo them.}
Matt Chapen: Jester and Hatter making their Traditon debut as a tag team! How confident can they be without knowing who their opponents are?
Chris Poleski: Pretty damn confident! Look at these guys! They are athletically sound! Nobody can beat them!
Rick Collings: And their mystery oppoennts, Ace Fireside and Rick Frost...ATTITUDE ADJUSTMENT!!! {"Princes of the Universe" by Queen plays as Attitude Adjustment come to the ring to the roar of the crowd. Jester and Hatter can't believe it!}
Chris Poleski: ATTITUDE ADJUSTMENT ARE BACK! NO! FIRESIDE WITH A RANA ON HATTER! One... Two... Kickout! Rick Frost enters the ring to make it two-on-one. Ace Fireside and
Rick Frost whip The Mad Hatter into the ropes and they take him down with a double dropkick! Ace Fireside goes for a belly-to-back suplex, but The Mad
Hatter counters it with a go-behind. The Mad Hatter goes for a spinning leg lariat, but Ace Fireside DUCKS OUT OF THE WAY!
Matt Chapen: Ace Fireside goes for a flying kneedrop, BUT HATTER ROLLS OUT OF
THE WAY! The Mad Hatter makes the tag to The Jester. The Jester whips Ace
Fireside into the ropes AND CONNECTS WITH A VICIOUS SPINEBUSTER SLAM!
One... Two... Kickout! Chris Poleski: The Jester goes for a snap suplex, but Ace Fireside counters it with a vertical suplex! Ace Fireside tags out to Rick Frost. The Jester throws
Rick Frost out of the ring. The Jester whips Rick Frost into the guardrail.
Both men duking it out here! Rick Frost hits a hiptoss on The Jester. Rick
Frost whips The Jester into the guardrail. FIRESIDE WITH A ROLLING KICK ONTO
JESTER! Nice move! Rick Frost goes for a back suplex, but The Jester counters it with a facerake, INTO A MADHATTER SHORT ARM CLOTHESLINE! Good team work!
The Jester with a belly-to-back suplex on Rick Frost. Matt Chapen: Fireside gets back up AND NAILS MAD HATTER! AA NAIL JESTER WITH
A DOUBLE DROPKICK! Rick Frost knocks The Jester into the ringsteps. Rick
Frost hits a kneelift on The Jester. Rick Frost enters the ring and tags out to his tag team partner! Hatter has entered the ring! HE LAID OUT RICK FROST!
FROST IS THROWN TO THE OUTSIDE! The double-teaming of Jester and Hatter continue! Chris Poleski: These two are a well oiled machine! The Jester and The Mad
Hatter whip Ace Fireside into the ropes and connect with a kick to the midsection! The Jester goes for a flying dropkick, but Ace Fireside ducks out of the way. Ace Fireside runs into the ropes and takes him out with a mule kick! GERMAN SUPLEX BY FIRESIDE!
One... Two... Thr...The Mad Hatter makes the save! Matt Chapen: The Jester and The Mad Hatter hit Ace Fireside with a double snap suplex. JESTER WITH AN IMPLANT DDT!
One... Two... Thr...Rick Frost makes the save! The Jester tags out to The Mad Hatter. HATTER AND JESTER NAIL FIRESIDE WITH A DOUBLE POWERBOMB! One... Two... Thr...Rick Frost makes the save again! Chris Poleski: Ace Fireside attempts to place The Mad Hatter on the turnbuckle, but The Mad Hatter blocks it. Rick Frost entering the ring! Ace Fireside and Rick
Frost whip The Mad Hatter into the ropes AND DROP HIM WITH A DOUBLE DROPKICK!
Rick Frost goes for a bodyslam, but The Mad Hatter counters it with a small package! One... Two... Shoulder up! Matt Chapen: The Jester goes for a vertical suplex, but Rick Frost blocks it. Rick Frost executes a dropkick on The Jester. Rick Frost takes The Jester down with a kneelift. MAD HATTER IS ON THE APRON! GET HIM OFF THERE REF! Frost is going after Hatter, he drops to the floor AND ACE FIRESIDE STARTS EXCHANGING
BLOWS WITH MAD HATTER! Rick Frost turns around INTO A SUPERKICK FROM JESTER!
One... Two... Three!!! Rick Collings: Here are your winners...THE JESTER AND MAD HATTER!!! Matt Chapen: The Jester and Mad Hatter just got the win just barely! Chris Poleski: They had it in control! Matt Chapen: Yeah right! JESTER AMBUSHES RICK FROST! THE MATCH IS OVER
DAMNIT! The Jester is leaving the ring. He is heading this way!
Chris Poleski: Hit Chapen Jester! He was talking trash! Matt Chapen: No I wasn't! The Jester throws Rick Collings out of his seat and grabs the chair! NO!
Chris Poleski: I like this! He is in the ring with that steel chair! He stands above the unconsios Rick Frost!
{CRACK!!!} Matt Chapen: He just nailed Rick Frost in the back! No don't tell me he is gonna do it again!
{CRACK!!!} Matt Chapen: THAT SICK SON OF A BITCH! Chris Poleski: Come on this guy is great! Matt Chapen: ACE FIRESIDE BREAKS FREE OF THE BRAWL AND IS ON THE TOP ROPE! He is going after Jester! He takes flight!
{CRACK!!!} Chris Poleski: YEAH BABY! Ace Fireside just tried to save his partner and look where it got him?
Matt Chapen: Ace Fireside went for a flying clothesline and got a chair shot to the head! Mad Hatter is in the ring and The Jester is giving him instructions! NO HE WOULDN'T!
Chris Poleski: YES HE WOULD! He has the head of Ace Fireside. He runs to the top turnbuckle. HE NAILED THE ASYLUM OFF THE TOP ROPE! BEAUTIFUL!
{"The Thing That Should Not Be" by Metallica BLASTS as The Mad Hatter and The
Jester stand in the ring triumphantly. The fans boo them like there is no tomorrow.}
Matt Chapen: I hope they are real proud of themselves! They just took out the former CWA Tag Team Champions and are loving every second of it!
Chris Poleski: I love it too its great! Matt Chapen: Somebody get some help for these men! Fans we must take a break!
