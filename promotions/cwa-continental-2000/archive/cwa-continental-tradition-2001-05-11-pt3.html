CWA Tradition Part 3

11 May 2001

Subj: CWA TRADITION PART 3 Date: 5/6/2001 To: [detail withheld] Matt Chapen: We are back fans! Street Punk just had one hell of a war with
Mad Hatter in which Hatter won the match with the Asylum!
Chris Poleski: His victory was short lived because The Living Legend Larry
Gionet appeared on the TraditionTron to challenge him for a match for Price of
Glory which Hatter accepted! Poor soul!
Matt Chapen: Damien is in the ring now and awaits his opponent Adam Mitchell! Rick Collings: And his opponent...ADAM MITCHELL!!! {The camera looks to the enteranceway but no one comes out.} Chris Poleski: Whats the deal! {Adam Mitchell runs from the crowd and stands on the apron while Damien is still looking towards the enteranceway.}
Chris Poleski: BEHIND YOU DAMIEN! Matt Chapen: Damien turns around INTO A SPRINGBOARD DROPKICK TO THE FACE OF
DAMIEN! Adam Mitchell off the ropes and connects with a clothesline! Adam
Mitchell goes for a back suplex, but Damien gets behind him and nails him with an atomic drop! Damien from behind with a sleeperhold! Damien showing his old school training! Adam Mitchell grabbing a hold of the ropes just in the nick of time!
Chris Poleski: Damien hits a back suplex on Adam Mitchell. Damien goes for a short clothesline, but Adam Mitchell ducks out of the way! Adam Mitchell goes for a European uppercut, but Damien COUNTERS WITH A BACKSLIDE!
One... Two... Kickout! Matt Chapen: Damien runs into the ropes and hits Adam Mitchell with an elbow! Damien is using his power to his advantage against Mitchell! If Adam Mitchell wants to win this matchup, he should use his speed to take the big man down to a vertical base! Damien hits a backbreaker on Adam Mitchell. Damien goes for a vertical suplex, but Adam Mitchell blocks it. Adam Mitchell goes for a vertical suplex of his own, but Damien reverses it! Damien making Mitchell think about it. DAMIEN WITH A JACKHAMMER IN STEREO!
One... Two... Kickout! Damien goes for a side suplex, but Adam Mitchell blocks it. Chris Poleski: Adam Mitchell nails Damien with an enzuigiri to the face! One... Two... Thr...Kickout! Adam Mitchell goes for a vertical suplex, but Damien COUNTERS
WITH A SMALL PACKAGE! What quickness by the big man!
One... Two... Kickout! Adam Mitchell with a discus punch! Matt Chapen: Adam Mitchell whips Damien into the ropes and Damien misses with a clothesline, BUT MITCHELL WITH A SLEEPER! Damien trying to get to the ropes!
DAMIEN HAS HIM UP AND BACKS UP INTO THE TURNBUCKLE! Damien taking a few steps back. He is gonna squash Mitchell! He runs at Adam, BUT MITCHELL GETS THE
FOOT UP! He is up on the top, SUNSET FLIP!
One... Two... Kickout! {Shane Russell is shown in the backstage area watching this match on a monitor.} Matt Chapen: Shane Russell seems to be watching this match closely. Chris Poleski: Yeah what is that cheater up to! {Out of nowhere Hia Tao runs into the locker-room and nails Shane Russell from behind.} Matt Chapen: WHAT IS THIS ABOUT NOW! Chris Poleski: Tao is teaching this punk a lesson or two! Matt Chapen: Russell is fighting back against Tao! Nails him with a left and a right! Chris Poleski: Come on Tao! Matt Chapen: RUSSELL THROWS HIM THROUGH THE DOOR INTO THE HALLWAY! Russell goes to grab Hia Tao, but he nails him with a low blow! Hia Tao whips him into the wall! Hia Tao is in controll and is SENDING HIM TO THE ENTERANCEWAY! We have a brawl in the ring and a brawl on the enterance way!
Chris Poleski: Meanwhile, Adam Mitchell goes for a Russian legsweep, but
Damien counters it with an elbowsmash! Damien whips Adam Mitchell into the ropes, but Adam Mitchell reverses it AND TAKES HIM DOWN WITH A FLYING
CLOTHESLINE! Adam Mitchell whips Damien into the turnbuckle, but Damien reverses it! Mitchell jumps on the turnbuckle AND ROCKS HIM WITH A BACK ELBOW
IN ONE MOTION! Get up Damien! Don't let this prick beat you!
Matt Chapen: Damien getting back to his feet and Mitchell off the ropes INTO A BULLDOG! COVER! One... Two... In the ropes! Adam Mitchell whips Damien into the turnbuckle. He runs towards
Damien but he kicks him in the head! Damien up on top, what is this? DAMNIEN
WITH A DIAMOND DUST! Cover!
One... Two... Three!!! Rick Collings: Here is your winner...DAMIEN!!! Matt Chapen: Damien with a victory. He spots Shane Russell! Damien with a burst of speed, SUICIDE DIVE INTO A SPEAR ON RUSSELL!
Chris Poleski: HAHA! Hia Tao coming in to join the attack! He rams Russell into the guardrail! Damien on the other guardrail. What is this?
Matt Chapen: DROPKICK OFF THE GUARDRAIL ONTO RUSSELL INTO THE OTHER GUARDRAIL! DEAR GOD! Chris Poleski: How do your ribs feel Russell huh? HAHA! Matt Chapen: This is nothing to laugh about! The man is hurt! Somebody help him! Chris Poleski: Hia Tao and Damien celebrating. Job well done guys! Adam
Mitchell is climbing the top turnbuckle!
Matt Chapen: NO WAY! He is airbourne! FLYING CROSS BODY ONTO DAMIEN AND HIA
TAO! THESE FANS ARE GOING CRAZY! Adam Mitchell is brawling with Hia Tao!
Shane Russell is coming to! Damien goes for a right but its blocked! He goes for another but Russell ducks out of the way and nails him with a left jab!
Here comes the officials! Chris Poleski: Come on! Let them go for crying out loud! Matt Chapen: Order must be restored Chris! Fans this has been wild and we have to take a break!

— Part 3 of 8 —
