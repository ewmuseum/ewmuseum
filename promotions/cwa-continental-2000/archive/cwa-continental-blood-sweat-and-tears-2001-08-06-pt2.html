CWA Blood Sweat and Tears Part 2

6 August 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Matt Chapen: Fans moments ago Street Punk was just attacked by Damien and officials tried to get him to his feet but he just pushed them away and I hear he is walking towards the office of Michael D. Taylor! Let's get a camera over there!
{The camera is shown in Michael D. Taylor's office. Taylor starts talking on the phone and Street Punk barges in.}
Michael D. Taylor: Hey Street Punk, can you hold on just a minute? Street Punk: I don't know if you saw it a few minutes ago but Damien was trying to take me out! But as you can see he didn't get the job done! I want his ass and I want his ass tonight!
Michael D. Taylor: But Street Punk we have time constants for this PPV. Street Punk: Don't give me that time constrants bullshit! You just had a triple threat match signed for later tonight! I am not asking I am DEMANDING
Damien tonight!
Michael D. Taylor: Fine you want him tonight you got him! Street Punk: Oh and one more thing, I want him in a hardcore rules falls count anywhere match!
Michael D. Taylor: You got it! Street Punk: Thank you! {Street Punk leaves as Michael D. Taylor picks up the phone again. The screen fades to black then cuts back to Matt Chapen and Chris Poleski.}
Matt Chapen: We have another match for tonight's PPV! Chris Poleski: That's right Street Punk fights Damien tonight! Rick Collings: This contest is schedueled for one fall with a 30 minute time limit! First coming down the aisle...A.J. DARKK!!!
{"Dream On" by Aerosmith plays over the P.A. system as A. J. Darkk slowly comes to the ring and takes in the boos from the crowd.}
Matt Chapen: Darkk, a very impressive athlete. He exposed to the world his past with Scotty Harrison and made this fued personal! Harrison looks for the ultimate revenge tonight!
Rick Collings: And his opponent...SCOTTY HARRISON!!! {The lights in the arena fade and a red strobe begins to flash in the darkness. "Don't Fear the Reaper" by Blue Oyster Cult begins to play throughout the building. A laser from the ceiling shoots into the ring and spells out "Fear the Fate". Finally a large explosion sends a giant fireball in front of the entrance to the locker room. As the flash fades, Harrison is standing in the entrance with no expression on his face. He makes his way silently to the ring.}
Chris Poleski: Both men staring each other in the ring. You can feel the emotion in their eyes and in this arena! Darkk with a headlock, spung around into a dropdown toehold. Harrison with a front facelock COUTNERED BY A
STANDARD WRITSTLOCK! Harrison flips over, landing on his feet and hits a hiptoss. Both men going for a test of strength but Darkk kicks him in the midsection.
Matt Chapen: Darkk goes to irishwhip Harrison, but Scotty holds on to the ropes! Darkk with a chop and whips Scotty Harrison into the ropes. Scotty ducks a clothesline AND ANOTHER ONE! Scotty Harrison getting a burst of speed.
He slides under Darkks feet and whips him into the ropes. Darkk with a back body drop BUT HARRISON LANDS ON HIS FEET! Scotty with some thrust kicks to the back! Harrison takes him down with a snap mare AND NAILS HIM WITH A KICK TO
THE SPINE! Did you hear that?
Chris Poleski: That was sick! Harrison with the cover! One... Two... Kickout! A.J. whips him into the ropes and connects with a dropkick to the face of Harrison! The momentum is shifting to the favor of this rising superstar!
One... Two... Shoulder Up! Darkk throws him into the turnbuckle AND RUNS AT HIM WITH A
CLOTHESLINE IN THE CORNER! A.J. Darkk whips him to the oppostie turnbuckle BUT
HARRISON JUMPS ON THE TOP! Matt Chapen: Harrison with a moonsault and lands on his feet! Darkk catches the leg and ducks the inziguiri attempt by Scotty! Both men know each other so well since they were former tag team partners and champions together for a number of years! Scotty Harrison ducks the clothesline AND HITS THE INZIGUIRI
THIS TIME! Darkk lands hard on the mat! Scotty Harrison with a camel clutch.
Both men are known for their high-flying escapades but Harrison is showing that he knows how to mat wrestle as well. Darkk trying to find a means of escape to the ropes but he is way too far. Darkk grabs a hold of one of Harrison's leg and hits a single leg takedown INTO A CAMEL CLUTCH OF HIS OWN! Very nicely done and the crowd is applauding the efforts of these two men!
Chris Poleski: That was a very smart move by A.J. Darkk when you are not close to the ropes you gotta attack a part of your opponent's body and he did just that! Come on A.J. break him apart! Scotty Harrison grabs a hold of the ropes.
Matt Chapen: A.J. Darkk applied that move to close to the ropes thus allowing an opening for Scotty Harrison!
Chris Poleski: That may be true but look who is on the recieving end of an asskicking right now! A.J. Darkk with a bodyslam. Darkk is ascending the top rope. He is looking to hit one of those high risk maneuvers that he is known for. Darkk flies through the air with a GUILOTINE LEGDROP! Scotty Harrison looks to be holding his mouth. I think the heel of Darkk's foot connected with the mouth of Scotty Harrison! Good job A.J. Darkk with the cover!
One... Two... Kickout! Matt Chapen: A.J. Darkk whips Scotty Harrison into the turnbuckle, but he reverse it! Scotty runs INTO A BOOT! Scotty Harrison fell hard to the canvas.
A.J. Darkk on the second turnbuckle and hits a vicious elbowdrop! He goes for the cover AND IS USING THE ROPES FOR LEVERAGE!
One... Two... Thr...Kickout! He tried to cheat his way out of victory this time but it didn't work in his favor this time!
Chris Poleski: Sometimes you gotta do what you gotta do to win a match Chapen! Darkk with a reverse chinlock but Harrison throws him into the ropes. Darkk ducks the clothesline and hits a perfect tilt-a-wirl backbreaker! Cover!
One... Two... Kickout! Harrison whips Darkk into the ropes and hits a sleeper on Darkk! A.J. trying to get to the ropes but no avail. A.J. Darkk with a jawbreaker! Darkk jumps on the top AND HITS A SPINNING LEG LARIET ON HARRISON SENDING HIM
TO THE OUTSIDE! Harrison thought he had the advantage in that exchange with the sleeper but Darkk turned the tide and now he is in control!
Matt Chapen: Both men so quick so fast! How do we keep up with this action? Darkk with a full-burst of speed and nails Harrison WITH A SUCIDE PLANCHA TO
THE OUTSIDE! Darkk rams him into the apron! Scotty Harrison holding those ribs. A.J. Darkk back in the ring. What is he going for? A.J. DARKK WITH A
SLINGSHOT SPLASH ONTO SCOTTY HARRISON ON THAT CONCRETE! I don't know who got hurt more on that encounter. Darkk throws Harrison back into the ring and goes for the pin!
One... Two... Thr...Kickout! Chris Poleski: A.J. Darkk going for a powerbomb, BUT HARRISON COUNTERS WITH A HURRANCANRANA! NO DARKK COUNTERS WITH A SUNSET FLIP! One... Two... Thr...Kickout! Scotty Harrison grabs him into a MAHESTRIAL CRADLE! One... Two... Thr...KICKOUT! Scotty Harrison looks ready to put away A.J. Darkk away! He picks him up in a RUNNING LYGERBOMB! NO A.J. DROPS HIM FACE FIRST WITH A
FACESLAM! Darkk with the cover, he hooks the legs!
One... Two... Three!!! Rick Collings: Here is your winner... A.J. Darkk!!! {"Dream On" by Aerosmith plays as A.J. Darkk has his hand raised in victory.}
Matt Chapen: A.J. Darkk pulls off the win here at Blood Sweat and Tears! Both men gave it their all and the fans are showing their respect to these two athletes!
Chris Poleski: Face is Chapen, Darkk is the better athlete like I said! Matt Chapen: Well A.J. Darkk was the better man tonight! Chris Poleski: Told ya so! Matt Chapen: Scotty Harrison has rolled out of the ring and is heading to the back! What's this? Jason Devlin is looking at A.J. Darkk at the rampway!
Chris Poleski: What is going on? What does Devlin have against Darkk? Matt Chapen: I wish I knew that answer to that question! We will have to find out in the weeks to come!
