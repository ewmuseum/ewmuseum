CWA Price of Glory Part 2

3 May 2000
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Matt Chapen: Well fans this should be a great match between two rookies in Jay
Moxley and Ian Walsh! Let's get to the ring!
Rick Collings: This contest is an I Quit match! First coming down the aisle..."ASSHOLE" IAN WALSH!!!
{"Asshole" BLASTS through the PA system as Ian Walsh comes to the ring sporting an evil smirk. The crowd boos him like there is no tomorrow.}
Chris Poleski: Ian Walsh, this man has so much confidence in himself! What can't you be like him Matt?
Matt Chapen: What's that Chris, being AN ASSHOLE? Chris Poleski: Now now, you better watch it or else Walsh will kick your ass! Rick Collings: And his opponent...JAY MOXLEY!!! {"We're Not Gonna Take It" by Twisted Sister blares over the PA system as Jay
Moxley storms to the ring.}
Chris Poleski: These two men are having a stare down in the middle of the ring. Jay Moxley steps back and starts taunting Ian Walsh! Ian Walsh quickly grows tired of Jay Moxley and nails him with a bautifully executed dropkick.
Matt Chapen: Ian Walsh now going for a right hand but Jay Moxley ducks and hits him with a fishermans suplex. WHAT A MOVE!!!!
Chris Poleski: WHERE DID HE FIND THE STRENGTH FOR THAT? Moxley now picks Walsh up by his hair and goes for a vertical suplex, Walsh reverses it with a kick to the stomach and a DDT! THAT WAS IMPRESIVE, you gotta admit that Chapen!
Matt Chapen: You are right that was impressive, but he must make Moxley say I quit! Walsh picks him up and hits him with another DDT. Walsh obviously working over the head so that he can Moxley submit! Walsh whips Moxley into the ropes and nails him with a vicious clothesline!!!
Chris Poleski: COME ON WALSH KICK HIS ASS!!!! Matt Chapen: Calm down Chris, remember your blood pressure! Moxley now mounting some offense as he comes back with a series of lefts and rights! HE
IS JUST POUNDING AWAY AT THE ASSHOLE! Moxley whips Walsh into the corner.
Moxley now setting him up on the top, WHAT'S HE GOING TO DO NOW? Chris Poleski: TORNADO DDT!!!! HE JUST NAILED HIM WITH A VICIOUS TORNADO
DDT!!! Moxley now hooking Walsh in a Boston Crab. YOU CAN SEE THE PAIN ON THE
FACE OF WALSH! Moxley now leaning back and applying more pressure!
Matt Chapen: HE'S TRYING TO BREAK HIM IN HALF!!! IAN WALSH IS JUST SCREAMING IN PAIN!! Now Walsh is trying to inch his way towards the ropes. He is almost there, can he reach it. YES, Walsh grabs a hold of the bottom rope. MOXLEY DOES NOT WANT TO BREAK THE HOLD! Chris Poleski: Moxley gets off and picks Walsh up by the hair. He whips him into the ropes and follows up with a vicious dropkick. HE ALMOST TOOK HIS HEAD
OFF WITH THAT ONE! Now Moxley is dragging him to the center of the ring. Good thinking here, I guess he learned his lesson from the Boston Crab. Now he locks in a FIGURE FOUR LEG LOCK! NICLEY APPLIED! He has it locked in and there is nowhere for Walsh to go.
Matt Chapen: IAN WALSH IS TRYING TO REVERSE THE HOLD!!! HE IS SUMMONING ALL
HIS ENERGY AND IS ACTUALLY REVERSING THE HOLD!!! YES, IAN WALSH HAS JUST
REVERSED THE FIGURE FOUR LEG LOCK!!! WHAT GUTS, A SIGN OF DETERMINATION!!!!
Chris Poleski: Moxley now trying to inch his way to the ropes. Moxley is almost there. YES, MOXLEY HAS REACHED THE ROPES, HOW'S THAT FOR
DETERMINATION!?!?!? Matt Chapen: Back in the ring Ian Walsh is picking Jay Moxley up. Walsh now setting Moxley up for a piledriver here. YES, WHAT A PILEDRIVER! Now Walsh is setting Moxley up for a Camel Clutch. Walsh has it locked in, but it looks like he might be a little to close. Moxley trying to fight the pain here.
Chris Poleski: YES, MOXLEY HAS DONE IT, MOXLEY HAS REACHED THE ROPES. GOOD JOB MOXLEY! Matt Chapen: You were just rooting for Walsh! Chris Poleski: NO WAY MOXLEY IS GOING ALL THE WAY! Matt Chapen: MAKE UP YOUR DAMN MIND! Now Walsh is picking Moxley up but
Moxley reverses it and grabs Walsh around the waist and hits him with a devestating Belly-To-Belly Suplex! WHAT A MOVE!!!!! I think thought took a lot out of both men. Moxley now stomping on the leg of Walsh. HE'S TRYING TO
BREAK WALSH'S LEG. Now he grabs him and hooks him in a texas cloverleaf.
MOXLEY WITH THE TEXAS CLOVERLEAF HERE ON WALSH. MOXLEY COULD CAUSE SERIOUS DAMAGE HERE. YOU CAN SEE THE PAIN ON THE FACE OF WALSH! Chris Poleski: WHATS WALSH DOING?!?! WALSH HAS JUST PUSHED MOXLEY OFF. WALSH
SHOWING TREMENDOUS LEG STRENGTH THERE. Both men now standing up, how are these men standing, they have inflicted a tremendous amount of punishment on each other here. Moxley now charges at Walsh but Walsh just nailed him with a superkick. MOXLEY IS OUT COLD!
Matt Chapen: It could be over here, Moxley is out cold and Walsh senses it. WALSH SMELLS BLOOD, HE SENSES MOXLEY IS AT HIS WEAK POINT. WALSH MOVING IN FOR
THE KILL. WALSH HAS JUST LOCKED IN THE OCTOPUS HOLD. IT'S THE WORLD RENOWNED
OCTOPUS HOLD, NOBODY ESCAPES THE OCTOPUS HOLD!! MOXLEY IS TAPPING, MOXLEY IS
TAPPING OUT! HE TAPPED! Rick Collings: LADIES AND GENTLEMEN, YOUR WINNER.........IAN WALSH!!!!! Matt Chapen: WOW, What an incredible match we've just seen here folks. We'll be right back folks!
Chris Poleski: No no! Walsh has the mic! Ian Walsh: Look at that! Just another loser that got in the way of me Ian
Walsh! Do you think that I was worried about him, Ha NO CHANCE! I fear nobody! There is no one good enough nor tough enough to shut this asshole up!
As a matter of fact, if there is anyone out there in the back that thinks they can shut me up why don't you come on down RIGHT HERE RIGHT NOW!
{The GloryTron starts flashing SURE FIRE! Out from the curtain is a man that is about 6'5", and 235 pounds. He has Blonde hair with a buz cut. This man wears Black and Red tights that say SURE FIRE Across the back and has M.G. on his boots.}
Matt Chapen: Look at Ian Walsh! He doesn't know what to think of it! He looks insulted! Man: For those of you that don't know me, I AM MITCH "SURE FIRE" GATLIN! As far as you go Asshole, you can sing your song all you want, but I never back down from a challenge! You will wish you never crossed my path! You will wish that you never opened your fucking mouth in front of millions of people because
I am gonna go down there and kick your sorry ass brother!
Ian Walsh: COME ON BRING IT ON BITCH! {Mitch Gatlin runs to the ring.} Matt Chapen: WALSH AND GATLIN ARE DUKING IT OUT HERE AT PRICE OF GLORY! BOTH MEN ARE EXCHANGING BLOWS AND HERE COMES SECURITY! Chris Poleski: LET THEM GO! Matt Chapen: THIS IS GETTING OUT OF CONTROL! GET THESE MEN OUT OF HERE! [name withheld] "You wont find a bigger Al Isaacs mark" Handler of Street Punk Member of TAW as Street Punk VP of TAW as Mr. Rage Ex and soon to be again member of WWC as Street Punk Has been in more crappy stables that Ed Leslie has had crappy gimmicks.
