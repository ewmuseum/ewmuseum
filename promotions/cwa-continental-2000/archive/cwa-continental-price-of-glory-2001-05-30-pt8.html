CWA Price of Glory Part 8

30 May 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Matt Chapen: We are back fans and this has been an explosive PPV! THP just beat Kevin Candido in his fall count anywhere match and says Charlie better watch himself! Ironman Jordan Jones also became the new CWA North American
Champion!
Chris Poleski: He sent a message to Charlie saying that his man Frat Boy is still gonna be the CWA World Champion by nights end and I tend to agree with him! Also I would like to congratulate Ironman Jordan Jones on his victory.
Matt Chapen: With that being said, lets get down to ringside! Rick Collings: This contest is schedueled for one fall with a 60 minute time limit and is for the CWA World Title! First coming down the aisle, he is the challenger...CHAINSAW CHARLIE!!!
{"One Step Closer" by Linkin Park plays as Chainsaw Charlie comes to the ring with his diamond cut chainsaw. As he enters the ring he climbs to the top turnbuckle and raises the chainsaw in the air as white pyros explode behind him.}
Matt Chapen: Chainsaw Charlie has fought for everything he has earned. He is looking to gain the CWA World Title for a third consequitive title reign!
Chris Poleski: Don't bet on it Chapen, remember THP lurks in the shadows if anything fishy were to go down!
Rick Collings: And his opponent, he is the current CWA World Champion...FRAT BOY PERRY TANHAMMER!!! {"Mr. Speed" by KISS plays as the fans look to the enteranceway. There is no sign of the World Champion. The music stops as the fans are confused.}
Matt Chapen: Where is the champion? Is he afraid? Chris Poleski: He doesn't have to fight a hasbeen like Charlie! Matt Chapen: Yes he does, or else he would be stripped of the title and Chainsaw Charlie would be crowned the champion! {"Mr. Speed" by KISS starts up again as an ice cream truck drives into the rampway. It stops and the driver's door opens to show the CWA World Champion
Frat Boy walking to the ring.}
Chris Poleski: FRAT BOY JUST DROVE THAT ICE CREAM TRUCK INTO THE TARGET ARENA! Matt Chapen: I don't believe it! Older fans would remember that the ice cream truck would be used as a weapon in many classic Frat Boy matches! Frat Boy and
Chainsaw Charlie stand face to face!
Chris Poleski: Frat Boy lays down the World Title drawing the line! Who is gonna make the first move?
Matt Chapen: Slap by Frat Boy! CHARLIE WITH A SLAP TO THE CHAMPION! Chainsaw
Charlie unloading with fists to the eyes. Chainsaw Charlie irish-whips him into the ropes, but Frat Boy reverses it! Charlie ducks the clothesline and takes him down with a clothesline of his own! Frat Boy Perry Tanhammer rolls to the outside.
Chris Poleski: Thats right Tanhammer, take a breather! Matt Chapen: This must be hurting the mindset of the champion being dominated in the opening minutes of this world title match! Frat Boy making Chainsaw
Charlie wait for him. He comes into the ring and Chainsaw Charlie stomps him on the way in. Chainsaw Charlie runs into the ropes. Leapfrog by Tanhammer,
Charlie jumps over the champion! Tanhammer ducks the clothesline, into a cobra clutch suplex! NO! CHAINSAW CHARLIE LANDS IN MIDAIR!
One... Two... Kickout! That caught Perry Tanhammer by suprise with our first near fall of this main event!
Chris Poleski: Chainsaw Charlie going for a leg, but Frat Boy sees him coming and catches him in a headlock! Frat Boy is unloading a fury of right uppercuts to the nose of the challenger! That has to bust his nose up real good!
Matt Chapen: Tanhammer with a knee to the abdomen area of Charlie! Charlie is hurting and now the champion is taking some shots at the fans. He can't be focusing on the fans, you got a title to prevent Chainsaw Charlie from taking from your cold, hard hands! Chainsaw Charlie helping himself with the ropes.
Tanhammer goes back to charlie, BUT CHARLIE NAILS HIM WITH A DROPKICK TO THE
KNEE! That sends the champion down fast. Chainsaw Charlie runs into the ropes. Perry Tanhammer slow to get up and gets nailed with a flying forearm!
Frat Boy wisely rolling out of the ring. Charlie off the ropes, BASEBALL SLIDE
INTO THE CHEST OF THE CHAMPION! WE HAVE A BRAWL ON OUR HANDS!
Chris Poleski: This is getting wild Chapen! Both men going into the crowd! Left hand by Charlie, right jab by Tanhammer! Frat Boy whips Chainsaw Charlie into the guardrail! Charlie grabbing a chair from a fan! He is setting it up.
What is he doing here? Matt Chapen: Chainsaw Charlie taking a few steps back and takes a running start springing off the chair! NO FRAT BOY MOVED OUT OF THE WAY! Chainsaw
Charlie landed rib first into that steel baracade and the champion throws him over the guardrail.
Chris Poleski: Frat Boy in the ring now yelling at Chainsaw Charlie to get up! Chainsaw Charlie is holding his rib cage and slowly rolls into the ring. Frat
Boy with a fist to the head. Chainsaw Charlie can barely stand! Frat Boy irish whips Chainsaw Charlie into the ropes and hits a drop toe hold.
Tanhammer with a bow-and arrow submission! Frat Boy trying to make Charlie tap out! Come on Charlie say it!
Matt Chapen: Chainsaw Charlie doesn't quit! Frat Boy trying to stretch out
Chainsaw Charlie into submission but Charlie is a fighter to the very end! The referee is asking Charlie if he wants to give it up but he shakes his head no!
He wraps his legs around the ropes! Frat Boy releases the hold. Both men back to a vertical base AND ITS A SLUG FEST! Frat Boy with a right, Charlie with a right hand of his own! Single leg takedown by Frat Boy! Tanhammer has the leg of Chainsaw Charlie, BUT CHALRIE HITS THE INZIGUIRI!
Chris Poleski: NO! Chainsaw Charlie stepping over the ropes to the apron. He is waiting for Frat Boy to get up. Charlie leaps into the air and connects with a springboard shoulderblock!
One... Two... Kickout! Chainsaw Charlie throws Frat Boy into the turnbuckle! HE HAS HIM IN
THE TREE OF WOE! Charlie to the outside, HE HAS THE STEEL CHAIR!
Matt Chapen: The challenger putting the chair to the face of the champion. This can't be good! He takes a running start towards Tanhammer! {CRACK!!!} Matt Chapen: OH MY GOD! DROPKICK TO THE FACE INTO THE CHAIR OF PERRY TANHAMMER! Frat Boy falls onto the canvas! His title is in serious jeopardy! One... Two... Thr...Shoulder Up! HOW DID HE KICKOUT?! Chainsaw Charlie obviously fustrated with Frat Boy. What can he do to put this man away?
Chris Poleski: You can't put him away! Tanhammer wants to keep his title around his waist just as badly as Chainsaw Charlie wants to take it from him!
Chainsaw Charlie picks up Frat Boy, BUT TANHAMMER THROWS HIM TO THE OUTSIDE! With a grab of the clothes, Frat Boy may have just saved him some time! Matt Chapen: You damn right he did! Frat Boy outside of the ring and grabs a hold of Charlie! OVERHEAD BELLY TO BELLY SUPLEX OVER THE GUARDRAIL! Chainsaw went flying into a row of chairs! Frat Boy Perry Tanhammer grabbing something under the ring! HE HAS A TABLE! He is setting it up between the ring and the guardrail! He is looking to do anything to keep his title!
Chris Poleski: Desperate times calls for desperate measures Chapen! Frat Boy back into the ring and Chainsaw Charlie crawling on the table to get into the ring! Frat Boy runs into the ropes, but stops! He saw Chainsaw Charlie get up. BOTH MEN ARE ON THE TABLE!
Matt Chapen: This is not safe at all! That table could break! Tanhammer going for a northern lights, BUT CHARLIE BLOCKS IT! Charlie spins around...
{CRASH!!!} Matt Chapen: OH MAN! JUMPING SWINGING DDT THROUGH THE TABLE BY CHARLIE! Both men are hurt and look to be hurt badly! Charlie is grabbing the head of Frat
Boy. Where is he taking him?
Chris Poleski: HE IS TAKING HIM WHERE THE ICE CREAM TRUCK IS! Chainsaw Charlie is running towards the ice cream truck ready to ram Frat Boy's head in it! He goes to throw his head into the front of the truck but Frat Boy blocks it AND RAMS CHARLIE'S HEAD INTO THE FRONT OF THE ICE CREAM TRUCK! FRAT BOY
CLIMBING ONTO THE TRUCK! Matt Chapen: Frat Boy trying to run away from Charlie! CHAINSAW CHARLIE MET
HIM! Frat Boy is unloading with rights to the chin of Charlie. CHARLIE
WALLOPS HIM WITH A HAYMAKER! Chris Poleski: Chainsaw Charlie is climbing to the very top of the truck! He is a maniac! FRAT BOY IS CLIMBING UP TO! GO GET HIM CHAMP!
Matt Chapen: Both Frat Boy and Chainsaw Charlie are on the top of that truck! TANHAMMER AND CHARLIE WITH A FLURRY OF FISTS! Both men want that world title so bad! THP IS CLIMBING UP THE SIDE OF THE ICE CREAM TRUCK! Charlie with a kick to the midsection, CHAINSAW CUTTER! Chainsaw Charlie rolling Frat Boy to the front side of the truck! THP FROM BEHIND!
{CRACK!!!} Matt Chapen: He just nailed him with that steel chair! He is laying the boots to Chainsaw Charlie! Frat Boy grabbed the chair and is heading towards the ring! What a coward! He has him set up here the powerbomb! THP READY TO
POWERBOMB CHARLIE! NO! Chris Poleski: Out from the crowd, ITS STREET PUNK! THP DOESN'T SEE HIM! THP raising his hands in the air. DROP HIM ON HIS ASS THP! STREET PUNK ON THE TOP
OF THE TRUCK AND CATCHES THP BY SUPRISE! {CRACK!!!} Matt Chapen: STREET PUNK NAILED THP WITH THAT STEEL CHAIR AND HE WENT FLYING
OFF THE SIDE OF THE TRUCK! Frat Boy is back in the ring now waiting for
Chalrie! Chris Poleski: Street Punk just helped Chainsaw Charlie up! He points to Frat
Boy AND GIVES CHAINSAW CHARLIE THE CHAIR! He wants him to go get him!
Chainsaw Charlie is off the truck and is running towards the ring! Charlie enters in the ring. Looks like we are gonna have a duel on our hands!
Matt Chapen: TANHAMMER AND CHARLIE DUELING CHAIRS! Frat Boy swings the chair, but Charlie ducks out of the way! Charlie swings and misses! Frat Boy with a chair shot to the midsection!
{CRACK!!!} Chris Poleski: YEAH! Frat Boy cracked that chair over the back of Chainsaw
Charlie! He is looking to finish him off!
One... Two... Thr...KICKOUT! Frat Boy whips him into the turnbuckle! Tanhammer with a vicious chop! MAKE THAT TWO! Perry in control. Chainsaw Charlie dazed in the corner and Tanhammer has that chair!
Matt Chapen: He runs at Charlie, BUT HE MOVES OUT OF THE WAY! Chainsaw Charlie kicks him in the midsection forcing Tanhammer to drop the chair! CHARLIE WITH THE CHAINSAW CUTTER ONTO THE CHAIR! CHARLIE WITH THE COVER! One... Two... Three!!! Rick Collings: Here is your winner and new CWA World Champion...CHAINSAW CHARLIE!!! {"One Step Closer" by Linkin Park plays as Chainsaw Charlie has his hand raised in victory. Street Punk enters the ring and hands the CWA World Title to
Chainsaw Charlie as the fans go crazy.}
Matt Chapen: What a great moment in CWA history! Through the hell that Frat
Boy and Charlie put each other through, Chainsaw Charlie is the new CWA World
Champion!
Chris Poleski: THP DECKS STREET PUNK FROM BEHIND AND THROWS HIM TO THE OUTSIDE! THP kicks Charlie in the gut! He's got him this time! Matt Chapen: KEVIN CANDIDO IS RUNNING TO THE RING TO MAKE THE SAVE FOR CHARLIE! NO! LARRY GIONET JUST ATTACKED KEVIN CANDIDO FROM BEHIND! Chris Poleski: Gionet and Candido are brawling in the aisleway! Frat Boy has found Street Punk on the outside here and is pounding away on him AND THP HAS
CHARLIE AND HE PICKS HIM UP!!!!!!!! THP JACKNIFES CHARLIE DOWN TO THE MAT!!! MY GOD! And now THP picks up that World Title belt! Matt Chapen: What's he going to do!?! Chris Poleski: THP looking at the title, and HE THROWS IT DOWN ONTO CHARLIE!! And now THP laughing! LOoks like he ruined his celebration! Matt Chapen: This is heinous Chris! Frat Boy backs off of Punk here while
Candido breaks free of Larry Gionet and both Candido and Punk entering the ring to check on Charlie. THP steps over the top rope and to the outside and he high fives Frat Boy! And now, THP and Frat Boy walking to the back as Gionet watches Candido from the aisle! Meanwhile, Candido and Punk trying to help the
fallen champion up! The battle lines have been drawn! Fans this has been an incredible PPV! For Rick Collings and Chris Poleski this is Matt Chapen saying GOODNIGHT EVERYONE! Continental Wrestling Alliance Copyright 2001 All Rights Reserved ®
