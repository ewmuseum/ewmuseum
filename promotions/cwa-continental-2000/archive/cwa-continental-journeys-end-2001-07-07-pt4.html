CWA Journey's End Part 4

7 July 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Matt Chapen: We just witnessed an unbelievable Brockton Brawl! Not only did we see Buddy Costello get knocked out by Brockton's own Marvin Hagler, but we saw the return of our first CWA North American Champion Mas Boulder!
Chris Poleski: And what a return it was! He not only brutally attacked Mark Blaze but took out hometown hero and a former heavywieght Champion in Marvin Hagler. It just can't get better than this! Matt Chapen: I understand that Todd Michaelson is in the back ready to interview one of the participants in this ladder match Adam Mitchell.
{The camera shows Todd Michaelson with mic in hand standing next to Adam Mitchell.} Todd Michaelson: I am here with one man that will fight for the CWA Traditiona Title tonight Adam Mitchell! So Adam, how does it feel going into tonight, you got your shot for the title when you were originally planned to fight for it later on?
Adam Mitchell: You know it was great getting the chance at having a shot to become the #1 contender for that title, but now the reward is even sweeter tonight since I get that shot tonight on PPV!
Todd Michaelson: You have any words for your opponent Shane Russell? Adam Mitchell: Of course I do moron! I will finish off what I started at Price of Glory Shane and my long joruney ends tonight as I am crowned the new CWA Traditional champion! Todd Michaelson: Back to you guys! {The camera cuts back to Matt Chapen and Chris Poleski.} Matt Chapen: A very focused and determined individual in Adam Mitchell! Let's head to the ring with Rick Collings for the introductions!
Rick Collings: Ladies and Gentlemen, it is time, for the ladder match to determine who will become the new CWA Traditional Champion!
Matt Chapen: This should be an hellacious matchup... Rick Colllings: First coming down the aisle...SHANE RUSSELL!!! Chris Poleski: Shane Russell has absolutely, positively no chance in hell of winning this match.
Matt Chapen: I beg to differ, Shane Russell has a great chance of winning this match. Rick Colings: And his opponent...ADAM MITCHELL!!! Chris Poleski: There's the man! That's my man and the man I bet a ton on, Adam Mitchell!!! Matt Chapen: Mitchell is an outstanding competitor and he'll give Russell everything he has. Russell will give everything he has to Mitchell. That's what makes this match such a dynamic matchup .
Chris Poleski: Yeah, yeah, yeah, Mitchell's going to win and that's all I got to say. Matt Chapen: Mitchell and Russell with the lockup, Mitchell to a side headlock, Russell with a push off, leapfrog by Mitchell, Mitchell coming back, ducks Russell's clothesline, Mitchell coming back, Russell catches him with a beautiful dropkick. Mitchell to his feet, Russell off the ropes, Mitchell with a superkick that takes down Russell.....
Chris Poleski: Yeah, beautiful savant kick by Mitchell right there. Mitchell grabs Russell's legs, oh yes, baby the-eliminator-danny-williams punch to Russell. Russell rolling around in pain!!! Mitchell with a kick to the midsection. Make it two. He's kicking Russell to the outside. Yes, a third kick and Russell rolls to the outside. Russell to his feet....
Matt Chapen: Mitchell off the ropes, going for the slide kick, Russell moves to his right, Mitchell slides through. Russell with a right. Make it two. Irish whip into the steel steps by Russell. Mitchell hit hard with the knees there.
Russell charges at Mitchell, Mitchell with a hotshot into the ringpost. Russell bounces back. That was sick.
Chris Poleski: No, Matt, that was freakin awesome!!! Mitchell with a slingshot in the same ringpost and Russell is bleeding slightly from the nose. Mitchell rams Russell's head into the mat. Mitchell with an irish whip into a shortarm clothesline.Mitchell is the MAN!!!
Matt Chapen: But why hasn't he gone for the ladder yet? Chris Poleski: To wear him down, you idiot!! It's the perfect plan, Mitchell will pound, and beat, and pummell until Russell can't walk, then Mitchell will walk up the ladder. Perfect, just like that vertical suplex onto the floor!
Matt Chapen: Mitchell walking over to Rick Collings, what is he doing? He's pushing Rick Collings out of his seat..
Chris Poleski: He grabbed Cowling's chair. He's walking toward Russell. He raises the chair up....
Matt Chapen: Russell with a right to the midsection. Now a right to the face. Mitchell dropped the chair. Another right by Russell. Russell on a roll. Chris Poleski: Oh, nice move by Mitchell with the rake of the eyes. Russell staggers back, hangman's neckbreaker on the chair. Mitchell with some stomps on
Russell. Mitchell to the ring apron, a chair is tossed to Mitchell. Matt Chapen: What, is he doing? Chris Poleski: Mitchell runs, Mitchell leaps, MITCHELL CONNECTS WITH A FLYING CHAIR SLASH ELBOWDROP ON SHANE RUSSELL ON THE OUTSIDE. MITCHELL IS GOD, Y'ALL!!! Matt Chapen: What an athletic move!!! Mitchell must have leaped a good six or seven feet to connect with Russell!!!!
Chris Poleski: Now look, Mitchell spits on Russell. And now Mitchell is going for the ladder. Mitchell has finally started to make his move....
Matt Chapen: But Russell is stirring here on the outside. Mitchell slides the ladder into the ring. Mitchell into the ring, Russell rolls in. Mitchell with a diving axehandle to keep Russell grounded. Mitchell with repeated elbows to the back of Russell's head here. That's just not right.
Chris Poleski: But it's legal, and in a ladder match, everything is legal. Mitchell is playing within the rules! Matt Chapen: Mitchell with an irish whip, OH MY GOD, WHAT A DROP TOE HOLD BY ADAM MITCHELL. RUSSELL'S HEAD HIT THE LADDER HARD. AND I MEAN HARD. Chris Poleski: What an awesome move. Mitchell pulls Russell over the ladder, to the top rope, WHAT A TOP ROPE LEGDROP!!! MITCHELL IS DESTROYING RUSSELL.
Matt Chapen: Russell might be out. Mitchell brings Russell up, irish whip into the ropes, Mitchell ducks his head too soon and Russel with a knee to the face.
Mitchell up, staggering, BULLDOG ON THE LADDER BY RUSSELL. Mitchell is clutching his face. Russell to his feet, with a sense of urgency and energy here, brings Mitchell to his feet, HANGMAN'S NECKBREAKER ON THE LADDER. That was rough, yo!
Chris Poleski: Mitchell needs a comeback here. Russell. drags Mitchell up, big time DDT there by Russell and Mitchell might be out. Russell brings the ladder and stands it up.
Matt Chapen: I think Russell made a miscalcuation and isn't near enough to the CWA Traditoina Title! Chris Poleski: Mitchell to his feet , Russell leaps off, MITCHELL DROPKICKS RUSSELL IN THE FACE!!! RUSSELL IS DOWN. MITCHELL IS KING!!! Matt Chapen: High risk, high pain there for Russell. Can Mitchell capitalize!!! Chris Poleski: Mitchell can do whatever he wants. Mitchell grabs the ladder and is slamming it into Russell. That has to be painful.
Matt Chapen: Your right, that looks definitely painful. Mitchell props the ladder in the corner. He brings Russell to his feet, irish whip, reversed by
Russell....OH MY, MITCHELL TURNED UPSIDE DOWN AND IS CAUGHT IN THE LADDER!! Chris Poleski: Yeah, Mitchell's hanging by a leg in a modified tree of woe. Russell charges, dropkick into Mitchell's face!!! That has to hurt. Matt Chapen: Mitchell slanks down and out of the ladder. Russell takes the ladder and sets it to the side. He picks up Mitchell and pushes him into the corner. Russell plummeting Mitchell into the corner. Mitchell slumps down in the corner. Russell with a good stiff kick for good measure...
Chris Poleski: I think Mitchell might be out. Matt Chapen: I think your right. Russell sets the top of the ladder near Mitchell, oh no, not this Shane!! Chris Poleski: I can't watch!!!! Matt Chapen: OH MY, SHANE RUSSELL SLIDE KICKS THE LADDER INTO ADAM MITCHELL'S, UH, "REGION" Chris Poleski: Oh my god, the poor boys! Matt Chapen: Russell is standing up as Mitchell is shaking in pain!!! Russell grabs the ladder and sets it right below the title. Mitchell is still down.
Russell looks at Mitchell and begins to climb. Chris Poleski: Mitchell's to his feet! He' s over to where Russell is, SIT-OUT POWERBOMB BY MITCHELL. RUSSELL IS DOWN!!! Matt Chapen: Mitchell is down as well. Can he capitalize!!! Chris Poleski: Mitchell to his feet first, right, blocked by Russel. Make it two. Now three. Russell punching Mitchell to the ropes, irish whip by Russell,
RUSSELL BACKBODY DROPS MITCHELL INTO THE LADDER. The ladder crashes to the mat. Matt Chapen: Mitchell crashes to the mat. Chris Poleski: Russell pulls the ladder onto Mitchell... Matt Chapen: RUSSELL WITH AN ASAI MOONSAULT ON MITCHELL AND THE LADDER!!! Athletisicism at its finest!!! Chris Poleski: Mitchell might be done here! Matt Chapen: I think your right. Russell props the ladder up. He's climbing. Mitchell's up, oh no, LOW-BLOW ON RUSSELL. Mitchell to the top rope, MISSILE DROPKICK TAKES OVER RUSSELL AND THE LADDER!!! Chris Poleski: BEAUTIFULLY DONE ADAM! Matt Chapen: Both men are down on the canvas. Russell stirs first. Russel to his feet, Mitchell on his knees, Russell comes over, Mitchell with a right to the midsection and eye rake again! Mitchell with a Impaler and rolls to the outside. He grabs the chair. He rolls back in and sets the chair down. He picks up Russell.
Chris Poleski: Vertical suplex on the ladder by Mitchell. He rolls it over into a brainbuster on the chair. Russell might be out!!!
Matt Chapen: Mitchell hopes so. Mitchell climbs the ladder. Russell stirs. Russel to his feet, Russell off the ropes, Russell pushes the ladder..... Chris Poleski: OH MY GOD, OH MY GOD, OH MY GOD!!! Matt Chapen: ADAM MITCHELL JUST WENT FROM THE TOP OF THAT 15 FOOT LADDER ALL THE WAY THROUGH OUR TABLE!!!! HE'S OUT. I DON'T SEE HIM BREATHING!!!! Chris Poleski: OH MY GOD, OH MY GOD, OH MY GOD!!! Matt Chapen: Russell looks at Mitchell. Mitchell has to be broken in every part of his body!!!!
Chris Poleski: Oh my god... Matt Chapen: We get the point. Russell climbing the top of the ladder!!! The fans are on the feet!!! Russell reaches, RUSSELL GRABS THE BELT! RUSSELL WINS.
SHANE RUSSELL IS THE NEW CWA TRADITIONAL CHAMPION! Rick Collings: Here is your winner and new CWA Traditional Champion...SHANE RUSSELL!!! Chris Poleski: Adam Mitchell was destroying Shane Russell. A lucky move by Russell gave him the victory. Matt Chapen: Adam Mitchell is still not moving here!!! Chris Poleski: What a great match!!! Matt Chapen: Indeed it was, indeed it was.
