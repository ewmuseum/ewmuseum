CWA (Continental, 2000) — Card

CWA Tradition Part 5

13 November 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Matt Chapen: We are back and Rick Taylor took Ace Fireside to the limit but in the end Ace Fireside picked up the victory retaining his title!
Chris Poleski: Coming up next we got the greatest CWA North American Champion of all time Shane Russell taking on that big goof-ball THP!
Matt Chapen: Um I wouldn't be calling him a goof-ball. He could kick your ass! Chris Poleski: I'd like to see him try. Rick Collings: This contest is schedueled for one fall with a 60 minute time limit and is for the CWA North American Championship! First coming down the aisle he is the challenger, THP...THE HOTTEST PROSPECT!!!
{"King Nothing" by Metallica plays as THP makes his way to the ring to a round of cheers from the crowd.}
Rick Collings: And his opponent, he is the current CWA North American Champion, being accompanied to the ring by Agent Rodney Wright...SHANE RUSSELL!!!! {"Welcome To The Jungle" by Guns N Roses plays as Shane Russell runs to the ring followed by Agent Rodney Wright.}
Matt Chapen: Shane Russell runs into the ropes and jumps over THP. THP with a hiptoss to take the champion down! THP whips Russell into the ropes and picks him up! Look at the power of the 300+ pounder. RUSSELL WIGGLING BACK ON HIS
FEET! He pushes THP into the ropes and rolls him up!
One... Two... Kickout! Both men circling one another seeing who will strike next. THP going for the midsection but Russell grabs the left arm of THP!
Chris Poleki: That's it Russell mobilize the big man! Shane Russell with a headlock take down. What a great showcase of talent by the CWA North American
Champion! Shane Russell working on that arm of THP. Break the bloody end of it man!
Matt Chapen: You are nothing but a sick, twisted individual Chris! I don't know why I even deal with you. THP quickly grabs onto the ropes. Its his height that was able to reach for those ropes quicker. Both men are now back to a vertical base. Collar-elbow tieup. Russell with a headlock but THP counters with a belly to back suplex. Great thinking by the 7 footer. THP picks up Shane Russell grabbing him from behind but Russell nailing him with some elbows to the face. Shane Russell trying to pick up THP but he can't!
Chris Poleski: Come on Shane you can do it! Shane going for it again and YES HE PICKED THE BIG MAN UP WITH A GUTWRENCH SUPLEX! Matt Chapen: Unbelievable! I think that took just as much out of Russell as it did THP. Shane Russell slowly making his way to THP. He draps an arm over
THP! This could be all!
One... Two... Thr...Shoulder up! Shane Russell is looking fustrated! You know he must be asking himself what he has to do to put this big man away. Russell is up and is daring THP to get up. THP helping himself up with the ropes. Shane Russell with a right hand followed by a forearm to the face of the challenger!
Chris Poleski: Yeah! Russell you got him where you want him! Shane Russell runs the opposite sides of the ropes and runs towards THP, but THP NAILS HIM
WITH A BIG BOOT TO THE FACE! He ref that was a loaded boot!
Matt Chapen: It was not a loaded boot. THP picks him up here, looks like ITS JACKNIFE TIME! HE PICKS HIM UP! {CRACK!!!} Chris Poleski: Oh thank God! Matt Chapen: AGENT WRIGHT JUST NAILED THP WITH A STEEL CHAIR! The referee is calling for the bell.
Rick Collings: Here is your winner, by disqualification...THP! By CWA rules, a title cannot change hands by disqualification therefore still the CWA North
American Champion...SHANE RUSSELL!!!
{"King Nothing" by Metallica plays as Agent Wright and Shane Russell are doing a number on THP.}
Chris Poleski: That's right stick it to the man! Matt Chapen: This isn't right. THEY ARE TRYING TO END HIS CAREER! {CRACK!!!} {CRACK!!!} Matt Chapen: WRIGHT JUST NAILED THP WITH TWO MORE CHAIR SHOTS TO THE BACK! He throws the chair down and is high fiving Shane Russell. What a damn disgrace!
Wait a second! HERE COMES MAD DOG MORTIMER AND THE CWA WORLD CHAMPION CHAD
YARO! Wright and Russell slide out of the ring! Chad Yaro grabs a mic!
Chad Yaro: Hey Russell, Wright, where is your almighty leader Candido? I came here to defend the CWA World Title and I don't want to wait any longer! Come on Candido you SON OF A BITCH!
Chris Poleski: Hey he can't talk to Candido like that! {"Battleflag" by Lo-Fidelity Allstars plays as Kevin Candido makes his way to the ring as Rodney Wright and Shane Russell applaud and Mad Dog Mortimer rolls out of the ring.}
Matt Chapen: When we come back, its Yaro and Candido NEXT!
