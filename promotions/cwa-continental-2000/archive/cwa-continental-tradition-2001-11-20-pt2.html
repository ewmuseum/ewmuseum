CWA (Continental, 2000) — Card

CWA Tradition Part 2

20 November 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Chris Poleski announces to the fans in attendence and the television viewing audience that as a result of what occured in the parking lot, an impromptu
match has been set up tonight between Frat Boy Perry Tanhammer and THP! We then are taking to our next match. Johnny Kyler vs Rick Taylor Johnny Kyler was looking to keep his winning streak alive against Rick Taylor by finishing him off early with a crescent kick to the back of the head for a two count. The crowd stayed hot for this match with many reverse pins. Rick
Taylor turned the tide in the match with a devestating german suplex for a near fall. Kyler quickly got back in control when Kyler countered a top rope move with a dropkick to the face. Johnny Kyler dazzled the crowd with a Pheonix splash only for a two count. The end came when Johnny Kyler went for a spin kick but Rick Taylor ducked and hit his Cobra Clutch Back Suplex for the three count. Johnny Tyler quickly retreated to the back. Jeff Kane ran in and hit a superkick on Rick Taylor from out of nowhere. A.J. Darkk comes into the ring.
Jeff Kane picks up Rick Taylor on his shoulders and A.J. Darkk comes off the top with a super hurrancanrana! Ace Fireside runs from the crowd and climbs up on top and hits a top rope clothesline on A.J. Darkk to the crowds delight.
Jeff Kane turns Ace Fireside around into a DDT and hits The Sensation off the top. Matt Chapen plugs their matchup later in the evening and wonders if the
Champion will be able to make it.
Backstage: Johnny Kyler Johnny Kyler is holding his neck. He hears a knock on the door and he yells at him to come in. Shane Valentine opens the door and tackles Johnny Kyler to the ground! He starts pounding on his neck that he was nursing a few minutes ago.
Officials rush to the scene to take Valentine off of Kyler. Interview: THP THP tells Todd Michaelson that its been quite some time when he's been able to kick Frat Boy's ass. THP says how they ruled the wrestling business like no other and tonight they are gonna tear into one another like no other. He ends the interview by saying his end is near.

— Part 2 of 5 —
