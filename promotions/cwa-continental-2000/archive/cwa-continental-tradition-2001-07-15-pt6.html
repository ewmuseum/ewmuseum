CWA (Continental, 2000) — Card

CWA Tradition Part 6

15 July 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Matt Chapen: We have returned fans and Chad Yaro was within seconds of winning the CWA Traditional Title before Anthony Davis come in and distracted him!
Chris Poleski: Well if Chad Yaro just stayed focused on Shane Russell he would have maybe become the CWA Traditional Champion!
Matt Chapen: We have been told Larry Gionet is in his locker room.} {The camera shows Larry Gionet sitting in a chair in his locker room staring at the camera.}
Larry Gionet: The whole worlds been wanting to know when I was going to break my silence once and for all. They've all been asking themselves, did Larry
Gionet really mean to hit Frat Boy Perry Tanhammer with a steel chair or was it an accident? Knowing Perry like I do, he would think I meant to hit him!
Despite our little argument backstage after my match, what happened in the main event was a complete total accident! You see Tanhammer, eventhough you weren't there for me during my match, I was there for you during yours! Over the past week I had a lot of time to think of what went down at Journey's End and I thought to myself, why should I be there for Perry when he wasn't going to do the same for me in return? You see Tanhammer, I was a team player with you, but NO MORE! I don't need your mishaps and screw-ups on my back! I am my own back up and my own man! I am only doing this tag match tonight because we are contractually obligated to. So here's a warning Frat Boy, don't even get in my way or else you will suffer the same consequences like Joe Snow and Chansaw
Charlie will!
{The camera fades to black. You then see a shot of Matt Chapen and Chris
Poleski.} Matt Chapen: Well I guess we have a somewhat change of heart from Larry Gionet! Chris Poleski: I don't even know what to think about all this! Matt Chapen: Well it sems to me that both teams tonight are not on the same page and it will be interesting to see who will be at each other's throats first!
Rick Collings: This contest is schedueled for one fall with a 60 minute time limit and is for the CWA North American Title! First coming down the aisle, he is the challenger...STREET PUNK!!!
{"Iron Man" by Black Sabbath plays as Street Punk comes to the ring with his signapore cane in hand. As he enters the ring he raises the signapore cane in the air to the approval of the crowd.}
Rick Collings: And his opponent, he is the current CWA North American Champion...JASEN CASEN!!! {"Greed" by Godsmack plays over the PA system as Cherry walks down the aisle. I emerge from the curtain onto the stage, with mic in hand, ready to cut a promo. Then, without hesitation, I steps slowly down the middle of the ramp, avoiding the fans and their boos. I turn right, and step to the ring steps.
Walking up them I grab a hold of the ring post and step to the apron before ducking into the ring between the middle rope and top rope.}
Matt Chapen: Jasen Casen takes Street Punk down with a dropkick. Jasen Casen hits Street Punk with a kneedrop. Jasen Casen whips Street Punk into the ropes, but Street Punk reverses it. Street Punk with an inverted atomic drop! Street
Punk whips Jasen Casen into the ropes and Street Punk goes for an inverted atomic drop, but Jasen Casen blocks it.
Chris Poleski: Jasen Casen goes for a bodyslam, but Street Punk counters it with an elbowsmash! Street Punk goes for a kick to the midsection, but CASEN
COUNTERS WITH A DRAGON SCREW! Jasen Casen hits Street Punk with a headlock takedown. Jasen Casen very quick and seems to be catching Street Punk off guard! Jasen Casen whips Street Punk into the ropes and Punk misses with a clothesline. Jasen Casen hits Street Punk with an elbow. Jasen Casen on the top rope, FLYING BODYPRESS!
One... Two... Kickout! Matt Chapen: Jasen Casen goes for a punch, but Street Punk blocks it. Street
Punk whips Jasen Casen into the ropes, but Jasen Casen reverses it.
CLOTHESLINE BY THE CHALLENGER! One... Two... Kickout! Street Punk runs into the ropes and nails Jasen Casen WITH A FLYING
FOREARM! Street Punk showing the CWA why he is one of the best!
One... Two... Kickout! {The camera spots Athony Davis and Sean Harris watching the match on a monitor in their dressing room. You see a smile on their faces. Then the camera comes back to Matt Chapen and Chris Poleski.}
Chris Poleski: Anthony Davis and Sean Harris looking at this match closely! Street Punk goes for a powerslam, BUT JASEN CASEN COUNTERS WITH A LARIET! PUNK COUNTERS WITH A CRUCIFIX! One... Two... Shoulder Up! Matt Chapen: Anthony Davis already got in the business of Chad Yaro so what do they want to do know? Street Punk goes for an armbar submission, but Jasen
Casen blocks it. Jasen Casen whips Street Punk into the turnbuckle, but Street
Punk reverses it. Street Punk charges into the corner. Street Punk with a right hand. Casen fires back with a chop! Jasen Casen whips Street Punk into the ropes AND NAILS HIM WITH A HOTSHOT!
Chris Poleski: JASEN CASEN WITH A BELLY TO BACK SUPLEX! The champion picks him up into a piledriver!
One... Two... Shoulder Up! {The camera shoots back to the backstage area where Chad Yaro is watching the match in his locker room and seems to be nodding in approval. The camera cuts back to the ring.}
Matt Chapen: Now Chad Yaro is watching this match closely! At the PPV Chad
Yaro did help Jasen Casen win the CWA North American Title so perhaps he wants to make sure he keeps it! Jasen Casen whips Street Punk into the ropes, but
Street Punk reverses it. Street Punk hits Jasen Casen with a backdrop AND
FALLS OUT OF THE RING! Matt Chapen: Street Punk whips Jasen Casen into the guardrail. Street Punk knocks Jasen Casen into the ringsteps. Cherry comes from behind, but Street
Punk nails Cherry! Jasen Casen whips Street Punk into the guardrail. Punk stumbles foward INTO A SUPERKICK! Jasen Casen throws Street Punk back into the ring.
Jasen Casen goes for a Northern Lights suplex, BUT STREET PUNK COUNTERS WITH A SWINGING NECKBREAKER! Chris Poleski: Street Punk runs into the ropes, AND CASEN HITS HIM WITH A
HIPTOSS! Jasen Casen whips Street Punk into the ropes, but Street Punk reverses it. Street Punk misses with a clothesline. Jasen Casen misses with an elbow.
STREET PUNK DRILLS HIM WITH A DDT! One... Two... Kickout! Matt Chapen: Jasen Casen executes a gutwrench suplex on Street Punk. One... Two... Thr...Kickout! Jasen Casen goes for a spinning leg lariat, BUT PUNK DUCKS OUT
OF THE WAY! Street Punk runs into the ropes AND CASEN NAILS HIM WITH A
DROPKICK! NORTHERN LIGHTS BY THE CHAMPION! COVER! One... Two... Three!!! Rick Collings: Here is your winner and still CWA North American Champion...JASEN CASEN!!! {"Greed" by Godsmack plays as Jasen Casen celebrates his victory.} Chris Poleski: That was a great matchup! Jasen Casen showed us all why he deserves to be the CWA North American Champion!
Matt Chapen: Street Punk is walking to the back. {The camera then cuts to the backstage area of Sean Harris and Anthony Davis.}
Anthony Davis: Haha! This is our chance! Go get Casen Harris! TAKE HIS ASS
OUT! Matt Chapen: Oh no I knew they were up to something! Anthony Davis is laughing at this like its some damn joke!
{As Anthony Davis sits down in the chair, you can see someone coming into the rooom.} Chris Poleski: ITS CHAD YARO! CHAD YARO JUMPS ON ANTHONY DAVIS! He is pounding the living hell out of him! HE JUST BASHED DAVIS'S HEAD INTO A LOCKER
DOOR! {The camera cuts back to the ring again.} Matt Chapen: Meanwhile back at ringside Sean Harris has run into the ring AND
THE FIGHT IS ON! BOTH MEN TRADING LEFTS AND RIGHTS! Casen ducks the clotheline, Harris ducks the clothesline! Sean Harris with a DOUBLE ARM DDT
ONTO THE CHAMPION! Cherry runs out of harms way. Sean Harris is going to the outside. HE IS LOOKING FOR A STEEL CHAIR!
{The camera shows Anthony Davis knocked out cold and Chad Yaor looks at the monitor to see Sean Harris grab a steel chair from the ringside area. Chad
Yaro runs out of the room. The camera cuts back to the ring.}
Chris Poleski: Is Yaro coming this way? Matt Chapen: I would think so! HARRIS IS IN THE RING WITH THAT STEEL CHAIR! He is waiting for Jasen Casen to get up! {"Man In The Box" by Alice and Chains plays as Chad Yaro runs to the ring. Sean Harris then directs his attention to Chad Yaro.} Matt Chapen: Yaro is here! Sean Harris wants to rip his head off. Jasen
Casen is slowly getting to his feet!
Chris Poleski: Sean Harris swings at Chad Yaro but he moves out of the way! {CRACK!!!} Matt Chapen: JASEN CASEN DROPKICKED THAT CHAIR INTO THE FACE OF HARRIS! SEAN
HARRIS IS KNOCKED OUT! Chad Yaro and Jasen Casen nod at each other and leave the ring. This is a very interesting situation! We will have to see what develops! When we come back its the main event with Frat Boy and Larry Gionet taking on Joe Snow and the CWA World Champion Chainsaw Charlie!
