CWA (Continental, 2000) — Card

CWA Journey's End Part 7

7 July 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Matt Chapen: Street Punk just had a war with THP. THP was going for the powerbomb, but Kevin Candido prevented it which allowed Street Punk to get the win! THP is in the backstage area. Look out!
{THP approaches the locker room of Larry Gionet and breaks down the door! Larry Gionet gets startled for a second.} Larry Gionet: Hey THP, how goes it? THP: That S.O.B. Kevin Candido cost me my match! I want his ass right now! Where is he? Larry Gionet: Why the hell would I know? {In a fit of rage, he picks up Larry Gionet and shoves him against the locker. Larry Gionet is in shock.} THP: Don't get smart with me asshole! I don't care what I have to do Candido will not make it out alive!
{THP walks out of the room. The camera goes back to Matt Chapen and Chris
Poleski.} Matt Chapen: This alliance of sorts between Frat Boy, Larry Gionet and THP is crumbling right before our eyes Chris!
Chris Poleski: No its not! THP was just a little tipped off thats all! Matt Chapen: In any event, we are now set for the CWA North American Championship match! Lets go down to ringside! Rick Collings: This contest is schedueled for one fall with a 60 minute time limit and is for the CWA North American Title! First coming down the aisle, he is the challnger being accompanied to the ring by Cherry...JASEN CASEN!!!
{"Greed" by Godsmack plays over the PA system as Cherry walks to the ring followed by Jasen Casen to a chorus of boos.}
Rick Collings: And his opponent, he is the current CWA North American Champion...IRONMAN JORDAN JONES!!! Matt Chapen: JASEN CASEN IS POUNDING AWAY AT THE CHAMPION! Casen whips him into the turnbuckle. Jones bounces back, Jasen Casen into the ropes AND NAILS
HIM WITH A SLINGSHOT SIDEKICK TO THE HEAD OF JORDAN JONES! Very nice!
One... Two... Kickout! Chris Poleski: Jordan Jones still on the mat. Jasen Casen hops on the apron. HE NAILS HIM WITH A SOMERSAULT LEGDROP! One... Two... Kickout! Jordan Jones thrown to the outside. Casen with a full head of steam. SOMERSAULT PLANCHA ONTO JONES! Matt Chapen: Jasen Casen whips Jordan Jones into the guardrail, BUT HE REVERSES IT! The champion finally getting some offense in this matchup! Jordan Jones nails Jasen Casen face first into the apron. Casen thrown back into the ring. The tide has turned in favor of the champion.
Chris Poleski: Irish whip by Jones, reversed by Casen. DROPKICK TO THE KNEE
BY THE CHAMPION! If you take out Casen's knee you take out 90% of his offensive moves. Jordan Jones tying up the legs of Jasen Casen. SURFBOARD
STRETCH! The champion stretching out the challenger! He quickly throws him off. Jones continues the assault with some stomps to the head.
Matt Chapen: Jones goes to get Casen, but he kicks him in the head. Jones responds with another vicious stomp to the head. Jones leaving the ring and he is looking for a chair! Jasen Casen getting to his feet. JASEN CASEN DIVES AT
JONES BEFORE HE CAN USE THE CHAIR! Casen with some stiff punches to the head and throws him inside the ring with the steel chair!
One... Two... Kickout! Chris Poleski: CASEN WITH A STEEL CHAIR TO THE FACE OF JONES! Jasen Casen going up on top. He is taunting the crowd. JONES IS UP! He catches Casen IN
A TOP ROPE FRANKENSTEINER! One... Two... Thr...Kickout! Matt Chapen: Jones goes to pick up Casen, but Jasen responds with a legsweep! Nice presence of mind by the challenger. Jasen Casen nails him with a hotshot FOLLOWED BY A SCHOOL BOY! One... Two... Thr...Kickout! Bodyslam by Casen. Jasen Casen going up on top. BIG TIME FROG SPLASH! COVER! One... Two... Thr...Kickout! Chris Poleski: Jasen misses the clothesline. CARTWHEEL KICK BY THE CHAMPION! What's this? CHAD YARO IS COMING TO THE RINGSIDE AREA! What is he doing here? Matt Chapen: I have no idea! He said he had business to take care of! Jordan
Jones goes for a German suplex, but Jasen Casen counters it with a go-behind.
In turn, Jordan Jones counters it with an elbowsmash. Jordan Jones goes for a flying dropkick, but CHAD YARO SHOVES HIM CROTCHING HIM ON THE TURNBUCKLE!
Jasen Casen has him set up. THE JASEN DRIVER! One... Two... Three!!! Rick Collings: Here is your winner and new CWA North American Champion...JASEN CASEN!!! {"Greed" by Godsmack plays as Jasen Casen has his hand raised in victory as he holds the CWA North American Title in one hand and Cherry in the other.}
Chris Poleski: YES! Jasen Casen has done it! Matt Chapen: He did but at what price? Chad Yaro has left for the back and all I can ask is why? Casen and Yaro were mere rivals just a month ago. I just don't understand it!
Chris Poleski: All you need to understand is that Jasen Casen is your new CWA
North American Champion end of story!
Matt Chapen: Well I can't argue with you there! In just a few moments we have our last man standing match for the CWA World Title and I can't wait!

— Part 7 of 8 —
