Chris Poleski: YES CANDIDO DID IT! HE IS THE NEW CWA WORLD CHAMPION! I TOLD YA SO! Matt Chapen: I guess you are right! You said that Kevin Candido would win the title and he did! Mighty Ryan Kelly has the mic now!
Mighty Ryan Kelly: Kevin Candido, you and I put each other through hell tonight! Through our 3 matches, you proved to me and the entire world that you were the better man tonight! For that I respect you!
{Mighty Ryan Kelly extends his hand to Kevin Candido and Kevin Candido accepts it to the crowd's approval.}
Mighty Ryan Kelly: You know I have been thinking about this for a long time and it is the right thing to do! So all you fans in attendence, savor this very moment! Each and everyone of you watching at home better have set their
VCRs because this is the last time you will ever see Mighty Ryan Kelly in a wrestling ring again!
{The crowd is in shock and a silence hovers over the capacity crowd.} Matt Chapen: WHAT?! Mighty Ryan Kelly: You heard me right! I am retiring from the sport of professional wrestling! For all you smart marks out there, I am not getting some phony injury to cover up my retirement, No this is the real deal! I have told myself since day one that I would never become washed up and just hold on to my glory days until it destroyed me! I would rather leave this industry on top of the world than rock bottom! I know this may be hard for many of you to understand, but I must do it! I want people to remember me as the top dog rather than a man than just a faded memory to every wrestling fan! Throughout my wrestling career, I have had some very memorable matches, meet some great people and most importantly made some good friends! In a sport where there are many acquaintances and very few friends, I made 3 very good ones right in my home state Massachusetts! So without futher or do, I give you The Living
Legend Larry Gionet, Al Basler and Mad Dog Mortimer!
{"Cum On Feel The Noize" by Quiet Riot BLASTS through the PA system as the
Living Legend Larry Gionet comes to the ring to a loud ovation from the crowd.}
Chris Poleski: Larry Gionet may have lost his match up to Assasin, but he is saying good-bye to a friend in Mighty Ryan Kelly!
{"Cum On Feel The Noize", cuts into "Stitches" by Orgy as Al Basler comes down the aisle.} Matt Chapen: Al Basler, a man that has been a force to be reckoned with in the wrestling world! He retired a year ago and is here tonight to wish Mighty Ryan
Kelly the best!
{"Stitches" cuts into "Dr. Feelgood" by Motley Crue as Mad Dog Mortimer gets a great reaction from the crowd.}
Chris Poleski: This must be quite a homecoming of sorts for Mad Dog Mortimer! He is paying respect for MRK but seeing a few old friends as well! Mighty Ryan Kelly: First off, Mad Dog, Bill thank you! Too all the fans, thank you all for supporting me through my career as a professional wrestler!
