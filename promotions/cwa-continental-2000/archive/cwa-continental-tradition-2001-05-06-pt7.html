CWA (Continental, 2000) — Card

CWA Tradition Part 7

6 May 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Matt Chapen: This ought to be a very interesting main event. Both men are hardly what you call fan favourites! You'll remember that it was only a short while ago that Frat Boy turned on Kevin Candido during a three way matchup beating him for the CWA World Title.
Chris Poleski: Wasn't that great?!!! Someone finally outsmarted the Starkiller! Who else but my man, Frat Boy! Matt Chapen: Your man? Is there something you're not telling us? Chris Poleski: What? Oh shut up!!! I tell you, if Frat Boy comes this way I'm gonna make sure he stomps you into the ground!
Matt Chapen: Yeah right!!! Anyway, this match is underway and Frat Boy's extending his hand to Candido. Like he's gonna shake it?! You can't trust him an inch!
Chris Poleski: Well he's not listening to you! They shake hands... and Frat Boy drives a knee into... No! Candido was ready for it. He grabs Frat Boy's knee,
Fisherman Suplex!!!
One... Two... Frat Boy kicks out!!!!! Matt Chapen: That could have been one short match, and one helluv'an upset for the World Champion! Both men get up and Frat Boy kicks Candido in the shin.
He follows it up with a double arm DDT! Chris Poleski: Great offence by Frat Boy! That's why he's the CWA World Champ! Frat Boy runs into the ropes... cocky elbow drop hits the mark! He grabs
Candido's left foot - Spinning Toe Hold! What a vintage move!!! Candido's in agony and Frat Boy spits in his face! Candido tries to reach for Frat
Boy... and he rolls him up in a small package!!!
Matt Chapen: Great counter by Candido... One... Two... Frat Boy kicks out!!! Chris Poleski: Candido's up and he looks primed! Frat Boy's struggling to get up and Ooooh! Low blow by the World Champ! Candido falls backwards onto the mat as Frat Boy grabs him by the legs! He slingshots Candido into the turnbuckle with amazing force!!! Candido rebounds... straight into a hangman's neckbreaker!!! Frat Boy with the cover...
One... Two... Candido manages to get a shoulder up! Matt Chapen: Frat Boy wasting no time, pulls up Candido and whips him into the ropes. Candido ducks a clothesline, rebounds off the ropes and catches Frat
Boy square in the face with a flying forearm!!! Frat Boy starts to get up and Candido knocks him back down with another clothesline!
Chris Poleski: Candido with a sit-down tombstone!!! He hooks the leg.. One... Two... Th... Frat Boy got a shoulder up!!! Matt Chapen: Candido's really taking it to Frat Boy. He climbs the turnbuckle, waiting for Frat Boy to get up. Everyone knows that Candido is deadly in the air! Frat Boy is staggering around... that tombstone must have messed up his equilibrium! Candido flies off - Flying Hurricunrana!!! Candido's got Frat Boy's shoulders pinned to the mat!
One... Two... Frat Boy barely kicked out of that one! Chris Poleski: The pressure's on Frat Boy right now! Candido pulls him up and.... he slapped him right across the face! What an insult! Frat Boy shoves Candido back only to be clotheslined over the top rope!!! No wait - Frat
Boy managed to hang on as he went over the top... he regains his balance and grabs Candido by the head!
Matt Chapen: Frat Boy charges with Candido in a headlock ready to ram his skull into the turnbuckle! Candido puts on the brakes, and its Frat Boy who collides headfirst with the turnbuckle!!! Candido holds onto Frat Boy..
looks like he's going to suplex him back into the ring! Up goes Frat Boy... Chris Poleski: But he escapes the hold, landing behind Candido! He grabs him... BLUE THUNDER PIN!!!! One... Two... Thre.. Noooooooooooooo! Oh so very close!!! I thought that was it! Matt Chapen: Frat Boy wastes no time in whipping Candido into the ropes...Tilt-a-whirl Slam!!! He scoops up Candido... and carries him over to the turnbuckle!?! He's got him tied up in the Tree-of-Woe! Candido's helpless as Frat Boy unleashes his fury wish punishing left and rights!
Ouch! Now whilst Candido's caught upside down, Frat Boy's standing on his groin!!! Chris Poleski: That's it! Jump up and down! Oh why's he stopped??? Frat Boy backs up and charges in... Baseball slide straight into Candido's face!!! He may have dislocated Candido's jaw with that move!!
Matt Chapen: Well he's not spitting teeth just yet! I have to agree with you -
Frat Boy's sending a message to Kevin Candido and the rest of the CWA!
"Mess with me and you will get badly hurt!". Chris Poleski: Frat Boy grabs Candido and free's him from the Tree-Of-Woe only to drive his knee into the mat, with authority I might add!! Frat Boy flips over Candido and stomps on his midsection! And now he's got him in a leglock in the middle of the ring!!! This is it!!!
Matt Chapen: Candido's in agony. He's trying to crawl towards the ropes, but
Frat Boy's got it locked in tight. If he increases the pressure he could pop a joint, or dislocate Candido's knee. I hate to say it but this is excellent strategy. Frat Boy knows Candido's a high flier and he's grounding him by crippling his leg!
Chris Poleski: Candido now slowly edging towards the ropes.... I don't think he's gonna make it before he gives up! He's.... he's... he grabs the ropes and the ref's calling for the break!
Matt Chapen: Candido's struggling to get up and Frat Boy nails him with a manhattan drop! Candido's down in obvious agony, and Frat Boy drops both knees onto his chest! Frat Boy with mounted punches and Candido's struggling to block them... Finally he flips Frat Boy off him and tries to get to his feet. Candido's bleeding from his nose and lip and Frat Boy is closing in for the kill.
Chris Poleski: Candido tries to irish whip Frat Boy into the turnbuckle, but its reversed and Candido crumples into the corner. Frat Boy's on the crowd's case as he prepares for... a running splash in the corne... No wait!!! The ref was checking on Candido who pulled him in the way of Frat Boy's splash!!! The ref was sandwiched between the two!
Matt Chapen: Wait till you see the replay but Frat Boy's chin collided with the top of the referee's head as he crashed into him. Frat Boy's down and Kevin
Candido collapses on top of him.
Chris Poleski: But there's no ref to make the count!!! Thank God! Matt Chapen: What??? You could count to 100! This is a travesty! Candido can't believe it. He pulls up Frat Boy... Tiger Driver!!! He's got Frat Boy in a pinning position.
Chris Poleski: But the ref's still out!!! What's the point! Candido looks frustrated, and Frat Boy's starting to come to. Frat Boy getting up but he's struggling, trying to hold onto Candido for assistance. This doesn't look good....
Matt Chapen: No it doesn't!!! It was a ploy! Frat Boy just clocked Candido in the chin with a set of brass knucks!!! What a cheat! He's lucky the ref is still coming to, or he'd be DQ'ed!!! Candido is out and Frat Boy places him over his shoulder. This can only mean one thing...
Chris Poleski: That's right Matt... He's climbing the turnbuckle. It's Hangover time baby!!! Look at the grin on Frat Boy; he know's its over!!! He's got
Candido in position... But Candido pushes him off!!!
Matt Chapen: It's not over yet!!! Candido's getting ready for the Starkiller! We're gonna have a new CWA World Champion! Now who's that coming down to ringside? Chris Poleski: It's THP!!! He want a firsthand glimpse at the new CWA World
Champion! Matt Chapen: The ref's pulling himself up using the ropes as THP reaches the ring. He climbs up onto the apron. Is he whispering some advice to Candido?
Chris Poleski: NO! HE JUST PUSHED CANDIDO OFF THE TOP TURNBUCKLE!!! Candido lands badly in the ring... AND FRAT BOY COVERS HIM...
One... Two... Three!!!!! Matt Chapen: I don't believe it! Frat Boy retains the CWA World Championship!!! What in the world was THP thinking helping Frat Boy? THP now stepping over the ropes... He pulls up Frat Boy who looks up at the big man... AND HI-5's
HIM!!!! Chris Poleski: They were in it together!!! What a setup! What a team! Frat Boy and THP together!!! The CWA better watch out!!! There's no-one who can beat this team! NO ONE!!! Frat Boy has grabbed the stick! Frat Boy Perry Tanhammer: Candido, did you really think that you could be my tag team partner, you insignificant piece of crap! You'd be nothing without me,
NOTHING! You would have never been the World Champion without me! If I didn't carry you along with me, you would have never been in the main event of anything Kevin! I drugged Doonovan Moore so you could pin him to become the champion! Do you really think I did this for you? HELL NO! It was all a setup with THP to get me the World title and turn the CWA upside down! I organized the whole damn thing! You were just a pawn to use strategically and then sacraficed when needed! The CWA is never gonna be the same again!
{"Mr. Speed by KISS plays as Frat Boy Perry Tanhammer wears the CWA World Title around his waist and raises THP's hand in triumph as the crowd is in shock.}
Matt Chapen: This is still so shocking! What an amazing Tradition show this has been!!! Plenty of surprises, unbelievable matches and quite a few shocks!
