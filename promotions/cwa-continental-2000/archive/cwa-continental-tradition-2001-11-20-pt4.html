CWA (Continental, 2000) — Card

CWA Tradition Part 4

20 November 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
CWA Traditional Title: Ace Fireside vs Jeff Kane The announcers discussed Jeff Kane taking out Ace Fireside earlier in the night and debating whether he is 100% right now. The champion held his own in the early goings of the contest. Ace Fireside countered an irish whip by the challenger and nailed a devestating flying shoulder tackle for a two count.
Fireside whips Kane into the ropes, picks him up but gets hit with a hurrancanrana. Jeff Kane takes his time going up to the top and goes for The
Sensation, but Ace Fireside moves out of the way! Ace Firside kicks Kane in the gut and goes for the Fireside Bomb but Jeff Kane backbody drops him!
Fireside counters with a sunset flip. Jeff Kane reverses and hooks the legs for the three count! Jeff Kane becomes the new CWA Traditional Champion! Jeff
Kane is celebrating and head to the back.
Interview: Jeff Kane Todd Michaelson congratulates the newly crowned CWA Traditional Champion. He says he laughs at everyone who said he wouldn't be anything in this business.
He pinned the champion cleanly in the ring proving that he doesn't need to cheat in his matches and proving that he is the better man. Before he can finish, A.J. Darkk walks up to Jeff Kane and congratulates him. He explains that what he did out there helping him was only becasue he hates Rick Taylor and despises Ace Fireside even more. Darkk says what this all boils down too is that title and nothing will be able to save him from Darkk beating him for it.
CWA North American Championship: Shane Russell vs Frat Boy Perry Tanhammer
The announcers recap the chair shot Frat Boy Perry Tanhammer took thanks to his former ally Larry Gionet. They have gotten word that Frat Boy Perry Tanhammer has a concussion and is unable to perform tonight. Shane Russell heads to the ring with Kevin Candido and grabs the mic from Rick Collings. He says it looks like that Tanhammer can't wrestle tonight because he got a little bump on his head thanks to his ex-friend Gionet. He apologizes to his "fans" for not being able to perform tonight but it seems like he has no opponent to face. Just as he goes to leave, "Bombshell" by Powerman 5000 plays as Larry Gionet stands on the ramp to the roar of the crowd. Larry Gionet says he knew exactly what he was doing. He was not only getting rid of the Frat bitch, but doing so would give him Frat Boy's place in the North American Title match. Shane Russell is irrate at this. Larry Gionet then brings out Joe Snow to even the odds! Larry
Gionet stormed into the ring like a house of fire. The fans were treated to a sequence of mat-wrestling holds that kept the fans hot. Shane Russell reversed a whip into the ropes. He grabs Gionet but Larry countered with a spinging DDT for a two count.
The tide quickly turned as Shane Russell countered a flying forearm into a spinebuster for a near fall. Russell goes for a kick to the gut, but Gionet grabs the leg, turns him around into The Legacy! He almost gets the three count but Kevin Candido put Russell's foot on the rope! Larry Gionet is yelling at Candido. Joe Snow chased Kevin Candido to the backstage area.
Gionet re-directs his attention to Shane Russell. He goes to pick up Shane
Russell, but Russell grabs the trunks sending Gionet's shoulder into the middle turnbuckle. Russell with a drop down toe-hold into the Lock Down! Larry
Gionet tried to fight it, making his way to the ropes but it proves to be too much and he taps.
Winner: Shane Russell
