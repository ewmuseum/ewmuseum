CWA Price of Glory Part 6

3 May 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Chris Poleski: WOW what a tag team encounter! Eventhough The Untouchables lost the match it still was a great match especially the end!
Matt Chapen: That new tag team seems like they have a big chip on their shoulder attacking the tag team champions! Just horrible!
Rick Collings: This contest is the second of a possible three matches and is a Hell In A Cell match! First coming down the aisle, the challenger...THE
STAR KILLER KEVIN CANDIDO!!! {"Blackflag" by Lo Fidelity Superstars plays as Kevin Candido comes out to a chorus of boos. His head is bandaged from the fall he took earlier, and many of the crowd can't believe that he's able to walk to the ring.}
Chris Poleski: Look at Kevin Candido! He took a thirty foot fall through four tables earlier but he wants the CWA World Title so badly he's going to wrestle in an unforgiving cell. Now that's class!!
Rick Collings: And his opponent and CWA WORLD HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPION...MIGHTY RYAN KELLY!!! {Its Murder Remix plays through the PA system as Mighty Ryan Kelly comes to the ring to a great response from the crowd. He climbs into the cell and the bell rings.}
Matt Chapen: This one's underway and Candido irish whips Kelly into the ropes... Great spinning heel kick takes down the champion!
Candido pulls him up quickly and charges with Kelly ramming his head into the steel mesh. Kelly bounces backwards onto the mat and lands on him hard with a jumping legdrop! Candido knows he has to win this match if he's got a chance of winning the World Championship
Title! Cover by Candido... One... Easy kickout by Mighty Ryan Kelly! Chris Poleski: Candido pulls up Kelly and slams him down to the mat! He stomps on him before pulling him back up! He hoists Kelly over his shoulder and charges towards one of the cell's walls! Kelly slips out the back at the last moment AND GERMAN SUPLEXES CANDIDO!
One... Two... Candido gets a shoulder up! Matt Chapen: That was close! One more second and Candido's chances of winning the title would have been shattered!
Kelly pulls up Candido and whips him into the ropes... NORTHERN LIGHTS SUPLEX!! Well executed, and Candido just bounced off the mat! Mighty Ryan Kelly is a machine! He is without a doubt the best technical wrestler in the CWA if not the world today! See what I mean - hanging powerslam into a cover!
One... Two... Thre, Noo! Candido kicked out! Chris Poleski: Technical, schmechnical! Attitude's what its all about and
Candido's got more of that than Kelly, or you can handle!
Kelly whips Candido into the ropes, but he reverses it - SPINEBUSTER!!! Now you're talking! Candido drops a knee into Kelly's throat and now he's puting on a Texas cloverleaf. No - he flips over into a bridge pinning Kelly!
One... Two... Kelly gets a shoulder up! Matt Chapen: Kevin Candido whips Mighty Ryan Kelly into the corner AND CHARGES AT IT WITH FULL FORCE!! Now he's setting up Kelly in a tree of woe!!! Kelly's caught upside down and the challenger's raining down kicks to his chest and face! Candido backs up and races in... BASEBALL SLIDE TO THE, NOOO! KELLY
SHOWED GREAT ABDOMINAL STRENGTH PULLING HIMSELF UP WHILST STILL HANGING UPSIDE
DOWN AND CANDIDO CONNECTED GROIN FIRST WITH THE TURNBUCKLE! Kelly falls from the turnbuckle onto Candido and the ref starts to count...
One... Two... Th, Candido kicked out!!! Chris Poleski: Candido's hurt and Kelly's climbing the turnbuckle. He's waiting for Candido to get up and catches him sqaure in the face with a missile dropkick! Kelly pulls Candido up and smashes his face into the cell wall! He grabs Candido by the back of the head and races to the other side of the cell, again smashing his face into the steel! That's reopened a wound
Candido suffered earlier and bloods seeping through that bandage!
Candido's getting to his feet as Kelly leaps to the top turnbuckle... Flying crossbody!!! But Candido rolls through with it, and now he's covering Kelly
One... Two... Kelly kicks out again!!! Matt Chapen: Great athletic move by Kelly, but Candido had the presence of mind to reverse the momentum and make the cover.
Both men are up now exchanging blows. Candido charges at Kelly with a lariat, but its sidestepped! Kelly atomic drops Candido and follows it up with a PUMP HANDLESLAM!!! He hoists up Candido putting him on a turnbuckle. We've already seen one Mighty Suplex tonight. Will we see one off the top turnbuckle??? Surely that'll end Candido's chances! This is it!
He's got him hooked... Chris Poleski: But Candido's fighting him off! He leaps off with Kelly... jumping backbreaker off the top!!!! That could have snapped Kelly's spine in half for sure!!! Candido now with the cover...
One... Two... Three... No! Only two! Damn I was sure he had him! Matt Chapen: Mighty Ryan Kelly's hurt bad and Candido's taking full advantage! He hooks Kelly by the legs... SLINGSHOT INTO THE CELL WALL!!!
Now Kelly's busted open as he hits the mat.. STANDING MOONSAULT FLIP BY CANDIDO! He hooks the leg... One... Two... Kelly gets a shoulder up!! Chris Poleski: Why won't he stay down? Candido's getting agitated now and rightfully so! He flips over Kelly and drops an elbow into his spine! Again he hooks the legs... INVERTED POWERBOMB SCRAPING KELLY'S FACE DOWN THE CELL
WALL! That was brutal! Kelly's hanging on the ropes, trying to support himself as Candido runs into the ropes... He leaps onto Kelly's shoulders, then twists, into a sunset flip!!! This has to be it...
One... Two... Th, Noo! Again only two! Matt Chapen: Candido's giving it his all trying to pin the World Champion but he's just not getting the job done. Candido's in the corner now waiting for a groggy Kelly to get to his feet... SUPERKICK!!! He caught Kelly square on the chin with that kick! Now he's trying to open the cell door?
Its padlocked shut for God's sake! I don't know if he was going to try and get out for some reason, but that door won't be opened until this match is over. Kelly's still down and Candido's climbing the turnbuckle. He's setting himself up to... Wait! Kelly flips to his feet and launches himself at Kevin
Candio... LEAPING ARMBAR OFF THE TOP!!! WHAT A MOVE!!!
Chris Poleski: That almost wrenched Candido's arm out of the socket! He's got the armbar on tight, but I don't think Candido'll submit. I know he won't!
Kelly's applying the pressure but Candido's shaking his head. He won't lose it like this! No way! He's crawling over to the ropes... AND HE GRABS THEM!
Break the hold Kelly! Good he did! What's Kelly doing? He's climbing the ropes and the mesh... Does he think he's spiderman or something? There's no way out of the top...
ELBOW DROP FROM THE CELL WALL ONTO KEVIN CANDIDO!!! Kelly with the cover... One... Two... Three, Nooo! Candido managed to get a shoulder up! Matt Chapen: That was almost a suicide move by Mighty Ryan Kelly but it almost paid off! Both men are trying to get to their feet, with blood dripping down their faces. Candido smashes an elbow into Kelly's jaw and follows it up with a European uppercut! That staggers the champ and Candido goes for a suplex!
No - its reversed into a Falcon Arrow! Candido's down and Kelly's climbing the turnbuckle!!! Now he's climbing up the mesh... is he going for another elbow drop? Candido's still down and KELLY'S CLIMBED TO THE CELL CEILING AND
HE'S... HE'S USING THE CEILING TO HANG ABOVE CANDIDO!!! He's a good 15 to 20 feet above Candido!!! What in hell's name is he going to do????
Chris Poleski: It looks like he's gonna drop a Legdrop all the way down onto
Kevin Candido!!! Don't do it!!! Candido's starting to get up and... KELLY
LETS GO!!! HE DROPS AND... CANDIDO'S UP!! KELLY LANDED ON CANDIDO'S SHOULDERS -
AND CANDIDO NAILS HIM WITH AN ELECTRIC CHAIR DROP!!! What a move!!! Candido now staggering over to the corner... he's climbing to the top... STAR KILLER!
STAR KILLER! HE NAILS IT AND MAKES THE COVER... One... Two... Three!!!! Rick Collings: Ladies and Gentlemen, the winner of the hell in a cell match... KEVIN CANDIDO!!!!!
Matt Chapen: That truly was an excellent match, and Kevin Candido's win means that the final part of the Candido Carnage Carnival, the ladder match will take place!
