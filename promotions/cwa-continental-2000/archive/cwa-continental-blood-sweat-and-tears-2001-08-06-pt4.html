CWA Blood Sweat and Tears Part 4

6 August 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Matt Chapen: CWA fans we just witnessed a brutal hardcore match between Street
Punk and Damien! Both men put their bodies on the line and Damien came out on top just barely!
Chris Poleski: Barely is right! Street Punk went for a moonsault 20 some odd feet in the air, but Damien was able to catch him in a modified diamond dust for the win!
Matt Chapen: We also saw the return of The Assasin! We knew they talked in a bar but never knew that an alliance would form in the CWA!
Chris Poleski: Everyone better look out! Rick Collings: This contest is schedueled for one fall with a 30 mintue time limit! First coming down the aisle...ANTHONY "THE ANIMAL" DAVIS!!!
{The lights go out, all of a sudden there is a huge explosion and "American
Badass" by kid rock starts to blast over the speakers. blue spotlights start flashing all over the arena as Anthony Davis steps out on to the entrance ramp.
The lights come back on as Davis sticks his left arm in to the air and flashes the wolfpac sign. He starts making his way to the ring slapping hands w/ the audience members on his way down. He steps over the top rope and enters the ring. He throws both arms in to the air as a row of blue fireworks shoot off behind him.}
Chris Poleski: This man is a pretty gifted athlete that is a powerhouse yet can fly in the air if necessary! I say Davis as my pick!
Rick Collings: And his opponent...CHAD YARO!!! {"Man In The Box" by Alice and Chains plays as Chad Yaro makes his way to the ring as the fans cheer him on.}
Matt Chapen: Well folks this one is off the ground here as Yaro takes on the much larger Anthony Davis.
Chris Poleski: Larger and more talented Anthony Davis I'm sure you meant to say. Matt Chapen: Yeah right. Davis and Yaro lock up here and Davis grabs Yaro by the throat AND DAVIS HEAVES YARO OVER THE TOP AND TO THE OUTSIDE!!!
Chris Poleski: A show of power there by Davis!! Matt Chapen: More like a slap in the face of Yaro by Davis! Yaro laying on the outside here more out of shock then pain. I don't think he knew Davis could do something like that!! But wait Davis has a hold of the ropes...AND
A SLINGSHOT OVER THE TOP BY DAVIS AND HE DROPS A BIG ELBOW ON YARO ON THE
OUTSIDE!!! MY GOD WHAT AMAZING AGILITY FOR A BIG MAN!!!! Chris Poleski: I'm telling you Davis has Yaro outclassed in every way!! Matt Chapen: It would appear so as Davis pulls Yaro to his feet here AND THROWS HIM INTO THOSE STEEL STEPS!!! Yaro goes tumbling into and over those steel steps as Davis is just dominating this match!!
Chris Poleski: He's going in for the kill!!! Matt Chapen: Davis stalking Yaro now BUT YARO KICKS THE STEEL STEPS INTO DAVIS!!!! DAVIS IS STUNNED!!! YARO JUMPS UP ONTO THE LOWER HALF OF THE CHAIRS
AND NAILS DAVIS WITH A SPINNING KICK!!! DAVIS STAGGERING!!! LOOK AT YARO!! YARO
UP ON THE APRON NOW AND NAILS DAVIS WITH A FLIPPING NECKBREAKER OFF OF THE
APRON!!!!!!! Crowd: CWA!!! CWA!!! CWA!!! Chris Poleski: ILLEGAL MOVES!!! COME ON REF!!!! COUNT EM OUT!!!! Matt Chapen: There is one thing that Davis doesn't have over Yaro and that is speed!!!! Chris Poleski: And lack of talent! Matt Chapen: Yaro is back up now and he hops up onto the apron again and MOONSAULT OFF OF THE APRON!!! BUT DAVIS GOT HIS KNEES UP!!!!! Chris Poleski: But Davis has the brains baby!!!!! Matt Chapen: Davis pulling Yaro back up by the hair here. AND A BIG GORRILLA
PRESS BY DAVIS!!! AND DAVIS THROWS YARO THROUGH THE SECOND AND TOP ROPE BACK
INTO THE RING!! MY GOD WHAT POWER!!!! Davis climbing back into the ring now and
Yaro is slowly getting to his feet. You have to admire the heart and determination in Chad Yaro!!
Chris Poleski: Too bad it's overshadowed by his lack of intelligence, HEY
YARO YOU'VE MET YOUR CLUBBER LANE!!! STAY DOWN ROC STAY DOWN!!! HA HA HA HA!
Matt Chapen: Your just a RioT tonight Poleski. Davis in the ring and nails
Yaro with a running clothesline!! Davis smiling now as he pulls Yaro back up. Davis picks Yaro up....OH SNAKE EYES IN THE CORNER!!! NO!!! YARO SLID
BACK AND HE HAS DAVIS IN AN INVERTED HEADLOCK!!!! YARO PULLS DAVIS UP!!!
HILTON HEAD SPECIAL!!!! THIS ONE'S OVER!!!! Chris Poleski: NO NO NO NO KICK OUT DAMN YOU!!! One... Two... Three!!! Rick Collings: Here is your winner...CHAD YARO!!! {"Man In The Box" by Alice And Chains plays as Chad Yaro climbs to the top turnbuckle and raises his fist in the air as the crowd applauds.}
Matt Chapen: Chad Yaro and Anthony Davis poured out an impressive match tonight but Chad Yaro got the win with the Hilton Head Special!
Chris Poleski: He got lucky tonight, but luck doesn't last forever Chapen! Matt Chapen: He is a youthful talent and has a bright future here in the CWA! Coming up in just a few moments, the CWA Traditional Title is on the line!

— Part 4 of 10 —
