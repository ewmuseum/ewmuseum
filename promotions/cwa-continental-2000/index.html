Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The Continental Wrestling Alliance ran a weekly Friday Night Tradition broadcast and, in the week before a pay-per-view, an occasional Wednesday Night Warfare summary card, sending handlers a Flash Packet of that week's roleplays ahead of each show. A February 2000 vignette bills a newcomer's debut match as the promotion's first-ever title bout, for a brand new North American Championship.

Its own Players Guide named four divisional belts — a World Heavyweight Title, a North American Title, a Hardcore Title and a Tag Team Title — and described a booked league where storyline direction and handler roleplays both shaped match outcomes, with one pay-per-view named for its own main event each month.

That structure produced Price of Glory, an eight-part supercard built around a Hell in a Cell defense of the World Heavyweight Championship, a triple-threat elimination match for a vacant Tag Team Title, a North American Title defense, and a ladder match that crowned a new World Heavyweight Champion after its reigning titleholder announced his retirement from the sport on the same broadcast, thanking three ringside well-wishers by name for years shared at earlier promotions.

No document survives past that May 2000 Players Guide, and nothing in the held record explains how much longer the promotion continued.
