A GeoCities-hosted fed billing itself "WCW" on every title and show, risen "out of the ashes" of a named predecessor (the DWF) in 1998, running Zeus-simulator-generated weekly cards and named PPVs across a roster built almost entirely from real wrestlers' own names and gimmicks. Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Wrestling Champions Of The World opened its own home page framing its own origin: "Out of the ashes of the DWF arises a new league Wrestling Champions of the World(WCW)." Every title and card on its own site is self-billed "WCW" throughout, a name it never once expands to "World Championship Wrestling" anywhere in the corpus.

Its own current-champions snapshot — the fullest surviving record of its title lineages, since no dated history page survives for any of its twelve belts — names Cold Blooded Mike Bauce World Champion. Its dated weekly-show logs and PPV recaps otherwise show the belt changing hands repeatedly across its run.

Its own largest surviving document is Halloween Havoc, a 35-match Battle Bowl elimination tournament run from the Pontiac Silverdome — preliminary tag matches funneling into a single-elimination battle royal, closing on Mystik's running lariat over Goldberg for the win.

Its own roster runs overwhelmingly on real professional wrestlers' and recording artists' own names and gimmicks — Kane, Taz, Undertaker, Kevin Nash under three of his own separate ring names (Kevin Nash, Diesel, and Vinnie Vegas), Goldberg, The Rock, and members of Bone Thugs-n-Harmony among them — each staged under a per-promotion portrayal, never merged with any other wing's own use of the same real figure. Two titles changed hands on the Halloween Havoc card itself: Saturn won the European Championship from Warrior and still held it as of the fed's own last surviving snapshot; Brett Clark won the Television Championship from Casper in an Inferno Match, setting him on fire for the finish.
