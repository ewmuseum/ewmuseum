[PWO] Fever #2 Lineup!

2000-10-21
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The camera shot, well opens up to nothing. And a voice over kicks in.] VO: Will "Mr. Excitement" Alex Extreme get his revenge? [A clip from the first edition of Fever just five days ago of Alex Extreme being lured into the ring by Victor V. Hill. Extreme then tackles Hill and nails a few right hands when then in the ring is former IIWF Cruiserweight
Champion "Badboy" Randy Acorn, chair in hand. He then smacks it over the back of Alex's head!
We then freeze on this, a surge of light comes from the middle of your screen... and then we are back to black.]
VO: What will happen when the next best thing in wrestling, Mark Marleau collides with former SCRA superstar Pedro Kozaro?
[The clip on last weeks Fever then comes into play with Pedro Kozaro being chased through the crowd by Fang. Then a fan from the crowd cracks a steel chair over Kozaro's head!
A few seconds later to be identified as Mark Marleau. Fade once again back to black.] VO: Dillon de Stride is finally out of jail... what does he have in mind for
Edward Winston III? What does Co-President Tommy Grayson have in store for
Solozzo Bracci?
Will Eric Cooper successfully defend his Mid-Atlantic Title... will the Wasp be victorious in a triangle match of doom? There's only one way to find out. As the MacInTrash Incorporated Presents...
[I'm lazy, so all of the opening graphics EXPLODE onto your screen!] FEVER! Live from the PWO Center Stage along the magnificent mile in Chicago,
Illinois! FIRST MATCH: It's going to be a wild ride when both "SWEETNESS" STAN DANIELS and CODY 'SILVER' SPARKS collide in there first match in PWO history. Both men need a victory to establish themselves in the Premier Wrestling Organzation, but which one will pick it up?
SECOND MATCH: It's going to be three men in one ring when THE WASP, "SHOTGUN" SHAWN CRIMSON, and JASON "THE BANDIT" BLAKE square off in the Center Stage. The Wasp rolled up
Crimson last week on Fever after Blake distracted Shawn with a chair. Then
Jason came into the ring and knocked out both Shawn and the Wasp! A three way war you do NOT want to miss.
THIRD MATCH: CHRIS KING and "THE ELITE SENSATION" ERIC EMERSON will try to establish themselves in the win column next week. Emerson is quite pissed after losing the PWO Mid-Atlantic Title match to "Five Stars" Eric Cooper. Eric thinks it's rightfully his, but there's no way Chris King will let that thought roll quietly into the night.
FOURTH MATCH: Speaking of the PWO Mid-Atlantic Championship, "FIVE STARS" ERIC COOPER after making his return to wrestling will defend his prestigious belt against
"FIRST-RATE" KEVIN KLASS. Klass knocked off Chris King last week, while Cooper defeated Emerson and became the first ever Mid-Atlantic Champion the PWO has seen. Can the youngin Klass top the legend Cooper?
FIFTH MATCH: SOLOZZO BRACCI will fight an opponent TO BE ANNOUNCED later on Fever. Bracci after dropping a contest against "Simply Golden" Jeff Keenan needs a win in hopes to stay in the race for some gold. However, who will Bracci just be facing on Fever? It just might affected his chances...
SIXTH MATCH: DILLON DE STRIDE is back from jail and he's teaming up with STEVE HOUSTON to face his arch nemesis EDWARD WINSTON III along with his cousin SCOTTY DRAKE.
Winston pressed charges against Dillon landing him in jail. However he'll be out and wanting his revenge. Steve Houston also has a bone to pick with Scotty
Drake who purposely superkicked him allowing Winston to scrape up a victory last week. A tag team war NOT to miss!
Dillon de Stride & Steve Houston vs. Edward Winston III & Scotty Drake SEVENTH MATCH: PEDRO KOZARO former SCRA and IWE superstar, will go head to head with who many say is the future of wrestling today... MARK MARLEAU. Last week on Fever, Fang charged into the ring and sent Kozaro running... into a chair shot from Mark who was in the crowd! Marleau quickly signed a PWO contract afterwards and
Pedro wants his revenge. Since Kozaro's match was striken from the record by
PWO Co-President Dan "Windy City" Stevens, this is technically a double debut your not missing for the world!
MAIN EVENT: A SIX MAN TAG FRENZY! On the last edition of Fever VICTOR V. HILL and "BADBOY" RANDY ACORN defeated "MR. EXCITEMENT" ALEX EXTREME and "INTERNATIONAL EXCELLENCE" TYRONE PARKER... by the actions of Parker! He used the ropes to spring onto Randy to keep him away from Extreme so he wouldn't ruin the match... but Alex was however climbing the ropes and he fell right onto his crotch. He fell, and Hill rolled him up. However this time around FANG is with
Parker and Extreme and Hill and Acorn promise a MYSTERY MAN that'll blow you away! Six men, a truly blockbuster main event.
VO: Plus much more! It's Tuesday the 24th, live on the Wrestling Network. Be there, or be square!
