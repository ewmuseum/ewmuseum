[PWO] Upcoming Events!

2000-11-30

SHOW: Fever! (Show #6, Fever #4) WHEN: December 9, 2000 WHERE: PWO Center Stage; Chicago, Illinois TICKET COST: $30.00 each TICKET AVAILABILITY: SOLD OUT! CHANNEL: The Wrestling Network. FIRST MATCH: [Mid-Atlantic Division Singles Contest] Oh, how sweet it is! "SWEETNESS" STAN DANIELS ventures back into the squared circle this time against the seemingly unstoppable "FIRST-RATE" KEVIN KLASS.
Kevin a undefeated youngster quickly rising up the ranks of the PWO, can defintely prove himself by toppling Stan Daniels on Fever. However, Stan doesn't go down to easy... will Kevin be able to put the icing on the cake for a chance at Eric Cooper again?
SECOND MATCH: [ELITE Division Contest] Martin Wyngarde, and the 'Age of Rage' Jack Hammer aka WYNGARDE INTERNATIONAL are in live action against the dynamic duo of VICTOR V. HILL and "INTERNATIONAL
EXCELLENCE" TYRONE PARKER. Hill is quite possibly the most hated individual in the PWO after his brutal attack on Alex Extreme sidelining him for up to 8 weeks. Tyrone Parker has been sloppy lately since his turn on longtime friend
Extreme, and his double DQ against Fang shows for it. Maybe he'll turn his in-ring abilities around this week, but don't expect Wyngarde International to just lay down and die.
THIRD MATCH: [ELITE Division Singles Contest] "BADBOY" RANDY ACORN is in live action once again this time against rising star
"SIMPLY GOLDEN" JEFFREY KEENAN. Acorn has been doing quite well so far, but not as well as the undefeated Jeffrey Keenan, who with a win against Randy Acorn here, could put him in Elite Title position in no time. This is a crucial make or break match for these two high flyers.
FOURTH MATCH: [ELITE Division Singles Contest] PEDRO KOZARO after knocking out PWO Co-President Dan "Windy City" Stevens during the main event last week with brass knuckles, and winning the #1 contendership to the vacant PWO Elite Heavyweight Championship is back in the squared circle with Double Dee Ess, the "Tomorrow Man," DILLON DE STRIDE.
Dillon is fresh off a beating on Edward Winston III, and things are looking bright for him. However, Kozaro has proven himself to be quiet a spoiler throughout the years and well, he just might prove that to all of us again.
MAIN EVENT: [Mid-Atlantic Division Singles Contest] In a non-title match, "THE ELITE SENSATION" ERIC EMERSON ventures off to the ring to take on his arch rival, the PWO Mid-Atlantic Champion: "FIVE STARS"
ERIC COOPER. Eric was proclaimed #1 contender last week, and the PWO front office wants to build up a little heat before the big Pay-Per-View title match between these two. There's quite some fire behind Emerson right now, who believes the Mid-Atlantic Title belongs to him since he was the MMOW Sooner
Champion, and that when the MMOW and ASCW merged to form the PWO, the ASCW forfeited there secondary belt. Now, Emerson has a chance to prove that he really deserves that hunk of gold.
PLUS: - New Vice President, "Mr. Main Event" Chris Douglas will be in the house! - So will Gabriel Whitecross, who last week was on a rampage. - Another update on "Mr. Excitement" Alex Extreme's physical condition. - And don't just think Co-President Dan Stevens is going to let Pedro Kozaro get away with his cheap shot last week on Fever. He's always got a few tricks up his sleeve!
And who knows what else could just go down kids? --- SHOW: Blackout (Show #5, Blackout #2) WHEN: December 6, 2000 WHERE: PWO Love Shack; New York, New York TICKET COST: $12.00 each TICKET AVAILABILITY: SOLD OUT! CHANNEL: The Wrestling Network. FIRST MATCH: [ELITE Tag Team Division Contest] Martin Wyngarde and 'Age of Rage' Jack Hammer, the team known as WYNGARDE
INTERNATIONAL step into the limelight of the PWO against the rookie sensation
"The Answer" Spencer Pryce and a old EWC veteran, "Die Hard" Darren Shaw.
Together, they form the SUICIDE KINGS, and this one should be one helluva war! SECOND MATCH: [Mid-Atlantic Division Singles Contest] "SWEETNESS" STAN DANIELS after a loss against "The Elite Sensation" Eric Emerson on Fever, looks to bounce back against the big round CANTO LOPE [J]. Daniels nearly became number one contender to Eric Cooper's Mid-Atlantic Title last week, and this could be a good bounce back victory for the big lovable Stan.
THIRD MATCH: [Mid-Atlantic Division Singles Contest] CHRIS KING made a unexpected heel turn on the fans last week, in a desperate attempt to jumpstart his future here in the PWO. He looks to capture his first win against "BAD BAD" LEROY BROWN [J] the baddest man on the South Side of
Chicago. And in the whole damn town.
FOURTH MATCH: [Mid-Atlantic Division Singles Contest] TERRENCE STEELE and "LOADED GUN" CHRIS TUCKER battled to a draw on Fever last week. Now, they will collide again, and this time will we finally have a check in the win column for one of these wrestlers?
MAIN EVENT: [ELITE Division Singles Contest] "BADBOY" RANDY ACORN and Victor V. Hill last week sent Alex Extreme out of the
PWO for up to 8 weeks. Randy might be celebrating right now, but when he crosses the path of the legend that'll never die FANG in the squared circle?
You'll never know what'll happen! Fang is still upset of the interference from
Gabriel Whitecross costing him a win on Fever. Now, Fang looks to get vengance for a dear friend. This one you cannot miss!
