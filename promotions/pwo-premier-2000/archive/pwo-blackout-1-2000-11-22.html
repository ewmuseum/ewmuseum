Kevin Klass gets back to his feet and stomps away on the leg of "The King of
Extreme" before wrapping him up in a figure-four. King starts to feel the pain and Klass starts yelling at him... "Who's the king now, baby?!"
With the crowd cheering him on, Chris King starts to turn the figure-four over, much to the dislike of Klass. He eventually does turn it over and into a scorpion deathlock, but Klass is too close to the ropes and grabs for them.
King keeps the hold on as long as he can while the ref counts to five before breaking. King keeps up the onslaught with a facebuster and a cover for his second near-fall of the match, but "First-Rate" Kevin Klass proves his stamina with a kickout. Chris King, now getting frustrated, sends Klass into the ropes again.
As Klass comes back off the other side, King sidesteps and propels Klass over the top rope to the outside. Mega-pop from the crowd!
The suicidal King climbs the ropes... Klass starts to get up... only to be met with a flying bodypress from King right to the outside! plancha right over the top and onto Klass, who had just managed to get up. King pulls Klass up and whips him into the guard-rail. King runs in with a shoulderblock, but Klass lifts his boot, causing King to run right into it.
Both men trading rights and lefts now, and Klass is busted open! This one should have been on Fever!
Chris King throws Kevin Klass back into the ring, and goes for a two-handed choke slam. Klass, however, has other ideas as he goes for a low-blow, stopping King in his tracks. With King doubled over, Klass lifts him up in a piledriver and drops him down hard. Klass now with a cover... 1... 2... And a kickout by King.
Both men now, back to their feet, trading punches again. King gets the upperhand and goes to whip Klass into the ropes. Klass reverses it, and King ends up receiving a boot to the midsection.
Kevin Klass, exhausted, manages to wrap Chris King up in a fisherman suplex he calles the Klassic Plex. Klass takes him over, keeping the leg hooked... 1... 2... 3! It's all over! The fans give a large heel pop as
Klass gets his arm raised in victory.
