Introduction =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= Hey guys. Well, the latest Theater is out, and if I do say so myself, I think it was a pretty good card. We've got some new faces in here, and things are starting to pick up nicely. I hope you guys are enjoying this, because I know I'm having a blast. The website is back up at [detail withheld] That's it for now. Enjoy the update. Angelo. =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= 2. News =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= + The NJWA Television title changed hands last night as "The Rush" EJ Drivven defeated former champion Jack Jones. "The Deathdealer" Tommy Ganz stood at ringside towards the end of the match making comments towards both Drivven and his newly aquired gold. + The mystery wrestler was revealed to be El Cool Arrow's manger, Johnny Riot in disguise. After El Cool Arrow interfered in the match, tension between he and The Newage Tyrant rose to a new level. These two will be sure to be at each others throats. =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= 3.
