LIVE! From the NJWA Arena in Atlantic City, New Jersey! LIVE! Saturday, December 1, 2001 ..................................................................... Theater of War NJWA Arena, Atlantic City, New Jersey December 1, 2001 Saturday, December 1, 2001, will be the date of the second NJWA Theater of War! After the action on the first show, we can only imagine what will happen during this show. There are only two matches scheduled so far, with more being scheduled later this week.
In the first match of the evening.... "The Rush" EJ DRIVVEN vs. "Dangerous" WILLIAM DEVONSHIRE * These two men will do battle in a dual newcomers matchup. The bruising, powerful wrestling style of EJ Drivven and the high impact attack of William Devonshire is sure to present a great contest for the fans. The winner of this match is sure to be on their way to big things in the NJWA!
STONE HARRIS vs. "Mr. Muscle" JIM VICKENS * These two competitors were scheduled to wrestle on the last edition of Theater of War, but do to the attack of Jake Adams, the match was cancelled and awarded to Stone Harris. But tonight, Jim Vickens attempts to extract revenge and get that win back as he takes on
Stone Harris! And in the main event... -- OVER THE TOP ROPE BATTLE ROYALE -- FOR THE NJWA TELEVISION TITLE JACK JONES - "The Deathdealer" TOMMY GANZ STONE HARRIS - "Mr. Muscle" JIM VICKENS - BRICKHOUSE BLACKBURN JAKE "the Devil" ADAMS - "The Rush" EJ DRIVVEN "Dangerous" WILLIAM DEVONSHIRE * These men will enter the ring at the same time. From then on out, they will do all that they can to send their opponents over the top rope while keeping themselves inside the ropes. There are eight wrestlers scheduled to participate, and tonight, the winner becomes the first ever NJWA Television champion!
