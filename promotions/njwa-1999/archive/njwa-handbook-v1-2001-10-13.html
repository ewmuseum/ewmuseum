INTRODUCTION =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= Hello, My name is [name withheld] and I have been involved in e-wrestling for nearly six years. This handbook is the basis for mynewest e-wrestling venture, the New Jersey Wrestling Association.
The plan is simple: a semi-bi-weekly league. Sounds odd, but it will hopefully work just fine. The schedule goes like this: the main wrestling card, "Theater of War", takes place every other Saturday. Each week, there will be a shows called "This
Week in Wrestling," additional house shows, and a role-play packet. More information regarding each boradcast can be found further into the handbook. If you would like to gather more information regarding theleague, visit the NJWA website at
[detail withheld] . Thanks, [name withheld]. =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= 2. WHAT IS NJWA? =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= NJWA stands for New Jersey Wrestling Association, which is an e-wrestling league. You particpate by creating a wrestler, role-playing his character, and reading the hand-written cards. I will pit your wrestler against others and you will set up angles and storylines with other handlers in order to get as far as possible in the organization.
If you do well enough, you can guide your wrestler to the top: the Heavyweight championship! Even though the name implies that we are based out of New Jersey, we do travel acrossed the country, holding our cards in sometimes large venues.
=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= 3. BROADCASTS =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= There are several broadcasts that the NJWA airs: - Theater of War: the live, bi-weekly wrestling shows which airs from different venues each time. There are anywhere from six to ten hand-written matches on most episodes. The show is hosted by
Martin Andrews and Jeremy Cecil.
- This Week in Wrestling: a weekly broadcast consisting of all of the happeneings in the NJWA. Included are a preview of the next
Theater; a review of the previous Theater; and much more. Hosted by BJ Carter and Maria Pontech.
- House Shows: these happen almost randomly and aren't considered NJWA "broadcasts" in the sense that they are for further character development, and not in any show format. They recap house show action which can help build angles and characters.
- Flash Packet: a collection of role-plays collected each week from the handler's. This is sent out once a week. =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= 4. TITLES =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= There are two titles sanctioned by the NJWA, the Heavyweight and
Television championships. Both are singles titles.
- NJWA Heavyweight title: the number one title, the champion of this belt must defend the title at least once a month. They can be considered "the best" in the league at the time.
- NJWA Television title: the bearer of this title is the work horse of the promotion, defending the title each week. =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= 5.
