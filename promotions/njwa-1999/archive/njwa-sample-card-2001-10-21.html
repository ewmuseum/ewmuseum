LIVE! From the NJWA Arena in Atlantic City, New Jersey! LIVE! Sunday, October 21, 2001 ..................................................................... Tonight, on Thursday, October 18, 2001, the New Jersey Wrestling Association present a sneak peak at its finest action. Two live wrestling matches in front of a sold-out NJWA Arena in Atlantic City, New Jersey. In the first match of the evening.... FORMER NAW HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPION "Mr. Muscle" JIM VICKENS vs. JAKE "the Devil" ADAMS * "Mr. Muscle" Jim Vickens hasn't been seen in a pro wrestling venue in nearly two years. He has changed a bit, but he's still the wrestler that he was a few years ago. Jake "the Devil" Adams is a native of Philadelphia, Pennsylvania... the type of guy you don't want to meet in a dark alley. Tonight, the two will do battle in the ring. It's going to be the perpetual good vs. bad when these two collide! -- IN A FALLS COUNT ANYWHERE MATCH -- STONE HARRIS vs. BRICKHOUSE BLACKBURN * Stone Harris has been seen in several promotion over the past year, including MVW, MPW and GWC. He has only wrestled three matches in the past four months... but as a favor to his dear friend, NJWA
President Angelo Sica, Stone is wrestling here tonight! His opponent was released from prison seven months ago, and has since tried to straighten his life up. The two will do battle in a falls count anywhere match. Anything and everything _will_ happen!
All of this and more, tonight, on NJWA's Sample Card!! =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= [Boom! Boom! Boom! The sound of fireworks echoes throughout the NJWA Arena. The fireworks create a cascade of colors which dance on the roof top.
Amidst the cheers from the crowd, you can here Steppenwolf's "Born to be Wild" pulsate from the loud speakers. The camera pans the crowd, standing and yelling for the show to start. There's something in the air... the fans can sense it... you can tell that this is something new. The dawning of a new era, so to speak, in wrestling... and there isn't anybody in the crowd any more excited about the start of the
NJWA than the number one commentator, Martin Andrews.
The camera cuts to ring side where the two NJWA announcers, Martin Andrews and Jeremy Cecil, sit. Clad in a black pin stripe suit with the NJWA name blazoned on the right breast pocket, Martin looks like a million bucks. His brown hair combed perfectly, and his wire-framed glasses rest snuggly on his nose. His partner in crime, however, is a different story.
In a brown leather jacket, blue jeans and a dirty white tee-shirt, Jeremy looks as though he's worn the same outfit for several days. A cigarette rests between his lips and black sunglasses on his nose,
Jeremy slouches down in his chair as Martin Andrews stands behind the announcing table, enjoying the view.]
MA: Hello everybody, and welcome to Atlantic City, New Jersey! Welcome to the NJWA Arena... welcome to the NJWA! JC: Marty, sit down. You're blocking the ring. [Martin looks back, tries to rebuttal, but decides to merely sit down.] MA: Sorry, Jeremy, but I am a little bit anxious to get this going. This is incredible! "the Devil" Adams take on former North American Wrestling champion "Mr. Muscle" Jim Vickens. JC: I can't wait for Adams to get out here... he's my kinda guy. MA: And in our main event, you will see Stone Harris take on relative wrestling newcomer, Brickhouse Blackburn in a falls count anywhere match!
Not much is known about Blackburn, Jeremy... all that we do know is that he was convicted on attempted murder charges some eight years ago.
JC: And now they're lettin' him in the ring. What kind of world do we live in? How can this barbarian be allowed to get into the ring with civil people? Although... I do hope he hands Harris his ass on a silver platter. I can't stand that goodie-two-shoes
Stone Harris. Gets on my last nerve. MA: Alrighty. Folks, let's get this one underway. We're going to give the floor to the ring announcer, Hansen Wilson, to introduce our first match... take it away, Hansen!

Harris, not paying any attention to the fans near ringside who try with all of their might to touch the big man.]
MA: Brickhouse Blackburn, ladies and gentlemen. Wow, he is a big fella. JC: I'll say. And compared to Stone Harris, who's what, 6-foot-2? I can't wait to get this one underway.
