Upcoming Event =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= Theater of War NJWA Arena, Atlantic City, New Jersey November 17, 2001 On Saturday, November 17, 2001 the New Jersey Wrestling Association presents the first episode of Theater of War! The show is guaranteed to bring excitement and attitude! Make sure you join us to see the action. In the first match of the evening.... JAKE "The Devil" ADAMS vs. BRICKHOUSE BLACKBURN * After the sample card on October 21, Jake Adams was walking around the back of the arena when Brickhouse Blackburn crossed his path. Jake Adams did not appreciate the words of Blackburn and the two soon began to brawl! On Theater of War, the two will vent their frustrations in an all-out battle! STONE HARRIS vs. "Mr. Muscle" JIM VICKENS * Stone Harris has found his fire once again. The man who has been troubled in finding his path has once again given a shot in professional wrestling, and to sharpen his skill is wrestling once more in the NJWA! He will take on the most peculiar "Mr. Muscle" Jim Vickens in what is sure to be a barn burner! And in the main event... "BROKEN DREAMS" Bobby Durango vs. "THE DEATHDEALER" Tommy Ganz * The two men have a similar outlook on life, although their backgrounds are incredibly contrasting. With their straight forward attitudes, these two men should bring the house down in a very exciting match! These two are sure to give the fans their moneys' worth! =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- 8.
