Introduction =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= Well, the first card is under our belts. Hopefully the rest will basically write themselves. :) Anyway, I think that it was a good way to set up the league. I'm going to continue to use a couple of my characters until the league fills out more. We're up to four handlers... :) It'll grow as we put out more quality cards. The lineup for the next Theater of War is further down the Update, but it is not the _final_ schedule. There will be matches and wrestlers hopefully added before the actual lineup is revealed on This Week in Wrestling this Saturday, November 24, 2001. That reminds me, let me try to explain the schedule of shows. This Saturday there is no Theater of War since it is bi-weekly. Instead, there is a This Week in Wrestling. You are allowed to send in flashes for that if you wish. They must, however, be to me by 10:00 AM Eastern time on Saturday morning. That gives you plenty of time. The Thrusday before Theater, I will send a packet of any flashes that I have received since then that weren't aired on This Week. They must be to me by 10:00 AM on the Thursday before Theater. I will periodically send out house shows. You aren't expected to flash for these and they have very little effect on your wrestlers ranking. They are merely a way to keep your ideas fresh and the angles developing. If the schedule is confusing at all, e-mail me and I'll try to explain it to you a little bit better. Besides that, if there is anything you want to say or ask, send me an e-mail or catch me on AIM. My screen name is [detail withheld]. Enjoy the update. Angelo. =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= 2. Upcoming Event =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= Theater of War NJWA Arena, Atlantic City, New Jersey December 1, 2001 Saturday, December 1, 2001, will be the date of the second NJWA Theater of War! After the action on the first show, we can only imagine what will happen during this show. There are only two matches scheduled so far, with more being scheduled later this week. In the first match of the evening.... "The Rush" EJ DRIVVEN vs. "Dangerous" WILLIAM DEVONSHIRE * These two men will do battle in a dual newcomers matchup. The brusing, powerful wrestling style of EJ Drivven and the high flying attack of William Devonshire is sure to present a great contest for the fans. The winner of this match is sure to be on their way to big things in the NJWA! And in the main event... -- OVER THE TOP ROPE BATTLE ROYALE -- FOR THE NJWA TELEVISION TITLE "Broken Dreams" BOBBY DURANGO - "The Deathdealer" TOMMY GANZ
STONE HARRIS - "Mr. Muscle" JIM VICKENS - BRICKHOUSE BLACKBURN
JAKE "the Devil" ADAMS - "The Rush" EJ DRIVVEN
"Dangerous" WILLIAM DEVONSHIRE
* These men will enter the ring at the same time. From then on out, they will do all that they can to send their opponents over the top rope while keeping themselves inside the ropes. There are currently eight wrestlers contracted to participate, but more are expected to sing up. And who wouldn't want to? The winner after all becomes the first ever NJWA Television champion!
