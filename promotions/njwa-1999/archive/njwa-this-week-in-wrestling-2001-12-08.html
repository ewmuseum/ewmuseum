This Week in Wrestling - December 8, 2001

8 December 2001

Y T | | | | |j l| | | | \_/\_/ l__j\_j_____j\___j l__j l_____j____j__j__j___,_j S A T U R D A Y - D E C E M B E R 8 , 2 0 0 1 [We open to a bird's eye view of the Atlantic City skyline. The streets are busy with cars whizzing by, the casinos illuminate the boardwalk busy with pedestrians, and the moonlight shows us the ocean gently lapping on the shoreline. The camera shifts closer to the buildings, getting a good look at what the logos on the sides say. We quickly pass by the Showboat and Harrah's, taking in the heavily lighted signs. Finally we come to rest at a large, but short building with the NJWA logo attached to the side. At this moment,
"Swan Dive" by hed (p.e.) begins to play in the background, as the scene becomes intertwined with an interior shot of the NJWA Studios.
A large white banner serves as the backdrop for the stage where Maria
Pontech and BJ Carter sit. BJ Carter is decked out in a flamingly bright red tee-shirt and black wrap-around sunglasses as Maria is dressed in a more professional attire consisting of a black sweater and glasses which rests on her Polynesian nose. Her sandy blonde hair is tied back in a pony tail.]
MP: Welcome everybody to This Week in Wrestling! I am Maria Pontech, and beside me is my colleague, BJ Carter. Tonight we will update you on all of the latest happening in the NJWA and preview for you the next edition of Theater of War. But first, I have to ask, how are you feeling today, BJ? I heard you had a rough night last night.
BJ: Yeah, these Atlantic City girls really know how to party... you ever have a cement mixer? A peculiar drink to be sure, but plenty of fun. From there we downed a couple of Kamikaze's and then came the BJ Shots. After a few of those I blacked out... Next thing I remember, I was waking in a bed with Chips Ahoy! bedsheets next to a Malaysian chick named Akmar. She smelled like sugar cookies,
Maria... sugar cookies!
MP: Good grief. You really should control yourself a little more, BJ. You're going to wind up with diseases if you sleep around with women. BJ: How very Kabalarian of you. I'm not worried about diseases and such, Maria, I've got an immune system built like the walls of
Jericho...
MP: That doesn't even deserve a response from me, BJ. You are quite an ignorant fellow, that's for sure. If you would spend half of the time looking around you as you do spouting off ridiculous comments about people, you'd be a half-way decent individual.
But instead, you speak of things which you have no true knowledge of and come off looking like an ass. Case in point, if your immune system is built like the walls of Jericho, you wouldn't be able to listen to a orchestra of trumpets without breaking out in a rash of enormous proportions.
BJ: Quite feisty tonight, eh? MP: You aren't worth my time anymore. Let's move onto a review of last week's Theater of War, shall we?
_______ __ | _ \-----.--.--.__|-----.--.--.--. |. l / -__| | | | -__| | | | |. _ 1_____|\___/|__|_____|________|------------------------------. |: | | Theater of War - December 1, 2001 | |::.|:. | NJWA Arena in Atlantic City, New Jersey | `--- ---'------------------------------------------------------------' MP: What a show we had for you last week, folks. You saw the debut of
"The Rush" EJ Drivven, the rematch of Stone Harris and Jim
Vickens, and the eight-man over the tope rope battle royal for the
NJWA Television title. Here's a run-down of the show:
1. "The Rush" EJ Drivven defeated "Dangerous" William Devonshire by pinfall 2. Stone Harris defeated "Mr. Muscle" Jim Vickens by pinfall 3. Jack Jones won the eight-man battle royal to claim the NJWA Television title BJ: You know what would've made the night better? MP: What is that, BJ? BJ: Some haka dancers from New Zealand. All we need are three or four of these guys. Get some minstrel paint, a couple of piu piu skirts and we're set. You'd see the ratings go up, that's for sure.
MP: You simply amaze me sometimes, BJ. But let's get back to the review of Theater. The night started off with the double debut match between "Dangerous" William Devonshire and "The Rush" EJ
Drivven. These two went at it tooth and nail, with EJ Drivven prevailing after the referee's three count.
BJ: He didn't stop there, though. Drivven took it to Devonshire,
Adrenalizing him for a second time, dropping him on his neck like a slinky. Mom always told me back on the farm, "Billy Joe, don't ya go playin' with that goat now, ya hear? Ya know better than that."
MP: What does that have to do with the EJ Drivven/William Devonshire match? BJ: Simple. Dear ol' ma told me such things to keep from getting my ass handed to me by that buck. See, our goat, named Demetrius, was one sick son of a bitch... he'd take out your knee if he thought you were too close his alfalfa hay. But you know what? I liked that goat. Wouldn't take crap from anybody without handing out whoopins. Best damn Nigerian Dwarf goat we had.
MP: I still fail to see how that ties into the match at hand. BJ: Maria, I think of EJ Drivven as my old buddy Demetrius -- except for the roman nose, and pendulous ears -- evil, sick, and demented. He'd hurt you just for the sake of hurting you.
Devonshire on the other hand, is like my young self: naive and headstrong. We all told him that Drivven would come out to maim him, but he didn't listen. Like a kid touching the hot stove -- or in this case, a kid playing with a chocolate Nigerian
Dwarf. Drivven sure did hand out his own whoopins last Saturday. And Devonshire paid for it by being taken out on a stretcher. MP: After the show, words has it that William Devonshire burst into the office of NJWA President Angelo Sica and took himself out of the league. Nick Williams overheard parts of the conversation, saying that he heard Devonshire cite unsafe working conditions as his main reason for departure. Some believe that he had a certain problem with a few of the names that had recently been signed to
NJWA contracts, however. What ever the case may be, we wish
"Dangerous" William Devonshire the best of luck in whatever he does next.
BJ: Let's get to the battle royal already. MP: Patience, my dear BJ. First we need to discuss the rematch of
Stone Harris and "Mr. Muscle" Jim Vickens. This matchup was signed to the inaugural edition of Theater of War, but due to Jake
"the Devil" Adams injuring Jim Vickens, the match was called off and the victory awarded to Stone Harris. President Angelo Sica announced on our last episode of This Week in Wrestling, that the match would take place on December 1... which is exactly what occurred.
BJ: Nobody knew if Adams was gonna come down to the ring or not to interfere, and luckily for Jim Vickens, he didn't. Jake Adams would've beaten him ugly if came down to the ring.
MP: After a series of hangman's noose neckbreakers, Jim Vickens finally stayed down for the three count which gave Stone Harris the victory. After the match, President Angelo came down to the ring to make announcements regarding the battle royal -- in particular the resignation of Bobby Durango and the addition of
The Newage Tyrant.
BJ: I don't know what Angelo was thinking allowing The Newage Tyrant into the ring for this match... the guy's off his rocker! He has less of a clue than Martin Andrews does. Do you realize how utterly ridiculous that is? How is this guy signed to a contract?
MP: BJ, the comments are really not necessary. Try to contain yourself. BJ: LET MY PEOPLE EAT! [Maria sits back in her chair and tosses a quizzical look to BJ Carter. Carter just glares back, increasing the enmity.] MP: Fine, let's speak about the battle royal. What a match it was,
BJ. We saw eight of the finest NJWA superstars enter the ring on last Saturday night, and only one came out on top, Jack Jones.
With the help of his brother, Duncan Jones, at ringside, Jack was able to push "The Deathdealer" Tommy Ganz and "The Rush" EJ
Drivven over the top rope and down onto the floor. BJ: Duncan is the only reason that Jack won that match... not that I'm complaining. I sorta like Jack.
MP: However, Tommy Ganz was not too pleased with the outcome of the match. Afterwards, Tommy stood on the outside at the bottom of the entrance aisle as brothers Jones celebrated in the ring with the title over the shoulder of Jack. Tommy Ganz merely held his hands to his hips and shook his head in anger and disbelief.
BJ: Ganz must feel cheated. He has a long history in the wrestling business -- his family is a huge name around the Boston wrestling scene, and actions like this don't sit well with Tommy. He's a firm believer in the whole wrestling with pride idea... and the way that Jack Jones won the battle royal must've really stung all kinds.
MP: And let's not leave out EJ Drivven, who in all rights, was the most impressive force in the ring during the match. EJ eliminated
Jake Adams, Stone Harris, Jim Vickens, and Brickhouse Blackburn.
He was, without a doubt, the man that most thought would come out on top with the title in his firm grasp. However, Jack and Duncan had other plans. EJ Drivven was lost in the celebration after the match, not waiting around to see what the Jones brothers were doing, but rather headed towards the locker room.
BJ: I'd be a little more pissed than he was. He did eliminate half of the wrestlers in the match, after all. He shoulda been kicking some ass, making his imprint made in the skull of Duncan.
MP: EJ Drivven will surely get his chance at Jack Jones on the next
Theater of War as the two headline the card in the Jack Jones'
first Television title defense. Which brings us to... _______ __ | _ |----.-----.--.--.__|-----.--.--.--. |. 1 | _| -__| | | | -__| | | | |. ____|__| |_____|\___/|__|_____|________|-------------------------. |: | Theater of War - NJWA Arena - Atlantic City, NJ | |::.| Saturday, December 15, 2001 | `---'----------------------------------------------------------------' MP: ...the preview section of the show. On Saturday, December 15,
2001, the NJWA brings you the third episode of Theater of War. As we've discussed earlier, Jack Jones won the battle royal by eliminating EJ Driven and Tommy Ganz at concurrently. EJ Drivven has been awarded a title shot against Jack next Saturday. Let's go to the lineup:
1. NJWA Heavyweight Television championship match: Jack Jones [c] vs. "The Rush" EJ Drivven 2. El Cool Arrow vs. "The Deathdealer" Tommy Ganz 3. The Newage Tyrant vs. Mystery Opponent MP: What a night we're going to have for you folks! We've been promoting it all night, but surely, the match of greatest proportions on the card for this Saturday would be the Television title contest. Besides that, we have NJWA newcomer El Cool Arrow taking on "The Deathdealer" Tommy Ganz and The Newage Tyrant taking on a mystery opponent! Any guesses on who the mystery opponent might be, BJ?
BJ: Margaret Fuller? MP: That is quite absurd, BJ Carter! BJ: What? The show _is_ called Theater of _War_. Who else would you expect to show up? MP: [sighs] Let's go over the matches, no? ---------------------------------------------------------------------- The Newage Tyrant vs. Mystery Opponent ---------------------------------------------------------------------- MP: In The Newage Tyrant's singles debut, he will take on a mystery opponent. Word is circulating backstage about who it is -- and BJ
Carter here seems to think it's going to be a female war correspondent -- but nobody is certain on who will show up.
Whoever it is, this match is sure to be an action-packed contest, as Newage Tyrant is an incredible athlete.
BJ: Yeah, he showed it in the battle royal... MP: BJ! BJ: Sure, why not? [Maria sighs again right before nailing a tremendous backhand to BJ's chest, causing this rough-and-tumble Canadian to pull back, nearly knocking his chair off balance.]
---------------------------------------------------------------------- El Cool Arrow vs. "The Deathdealer" Tommy Ganz ---------------------------------------------------------------------- BJ: In the seconds match of the night, we're gonna see El Wheel Barrow take on The Pokerdealer. Actually, the sounds like that time I spent a weekend on a Hopi Indian reservation in Kykotsmovi,
Arizona, spent my life savings on the roulette wheel, bought a bag full of Kachina dolls and had shooting practice outside of the tee-pee. Who knew they were so uptight about those figurines?
MP: Let's hear from El Cool Arrow. [The picture opens up to darkness. There are few muffled noises heard. Finally...the camera lens is taken off. The image is blurry and you can't make out the picture until it focuses. When it finally does, you recognize the blue aberration as El Cool Arrow. He's grinning madly.]
ECA: Hola! Como estas? [El Cool Arrow steps back into full view. He's wearing his usual luchador garb. The gold and blue attack. He places his hands on his hips and smiles broadly. He's in what looks like a basement. The production is shoddy, and looks to be entirely homemade. The light is very dim. On the walls, you can see such things as "Corazon", "ECA", and "El Leone" spray painted in gold. There is a lone stool to the right of ECA.]
ECA: The Leone of brass has returned. The battle for justice will always rage on, and the corazon will always win. The corazon of the arrow. The cool arrow. El Cool Arrow.
[ECA flexes for the camera] ECA: I look around this Jersey turnpike of a mess, and I see nothing. Nothing that compares to the legend. Nothing that compares to the myth. Nothing that compares to _El Cid_. The battle will be short. It will be quick. And it will be _divisive. And it begins at the War of
Theaters.
[ECA shakes his head a bit at the camera, disapprovingly.] ECA: And come this Theater of El Cool Arrow, I face the one...Tommy
Ganz. A weak little man no doubt. A man of brevity and weak, malleable copper no doubt. Malleable. A _malleable_ little boy.
You know what Ganz? [He attempts to sing. It sucks.] CRY ME A RIVER! [El Cool Arrow throws back his head and cackles with glee. He crosses his arm in the process.]
ECA: [He runs a finger down his face] Tear! [He then flips off the screen] ECA: You say you're keeping the family name alive? Well let me tell you about the Ganz family name. First, you spell it wrong. Then, you bring up Boston? I played in Boston! Every instrument! I've been to Boston. I won seventy eight world championships in
Boston! I _own_ Boston! And not _once_ did any Gunz or Ganz or
Gunn or Gum ever beat _me!_ I am the leone de brass corazon, I am the Cid of Boston, and at this Theater of the Arrow, I'm gonna have a tea party all over your crying, bleeding naranja!
[ECA stops suddenly, and coughs a bit. He calms down a bit.] ECA: Allow me to demonstrate. [He steps out of the picture, only to return with a doll. It's an old
"My Buddy" doll. He sets it down on the stool next to him.]
ECA: This is you Tommy Ganz. A little, little boy. Hi Tommy Ganz! How are you today? [Silence] ECA: I said, How are you today? [pauses] Hola!? Como estas!? [Silence] ECA: How are you today! Answer me little boy! You speak when told by the arrow. The cool arrow. El Cool Arrow, the one who is going to break your little, four letter, last name in half with a stick next week! _ANSWER!!_
[Silence. ECA steps out of the screen and returns with a baseball bat. He smashes the doll off the stool and continues beating it on the ground. The low production of this work makes it uncomfortable, as well as ridiculous, to watch. ECA drops the bat and stands back up infront of the camera. He smiles once again.]
ECA: The battle for justice has just begun. My first step in the climb for El Leone de Muerto, will be to topple the master of the tearjerker, the protector of vengence, the insulator of the alphabet, Tommy Ganz. You say you want pay back? After the
Theater of the War, you will see just why I am El Cid. The true luchador. The hero. The legend. What are you Ganz? The family freakin name.
[El Cool Arrow shakes his head, bearing a malicious smile.] ECA: Oh Ganz. You ridiculous little man. I piss on your family name. [Fade.] MP: Wow, another interesting character. BJ: What is the deal with these hysterically retarded people? First it was The Newage Tyrant... and now this clown. I swear, I can't work under such conditions!
[BJ stops for a second seemingly in mid-thought before a grin appears on his rough face, peeking through the hostility he feels for his co- employees.]
BJ: Now that I think of it... this could be fun. MP: I don't like when you say things like that, BJ. Last time that happened I ended up in a Philadelphia rat-infested sewer with a head of lettuce tied to my ankle. Remember that?
BJ: Ha, ha. Yeah... those were good times. Why don't we do things like that anymore? MP: Because I don't trust you. BJ: Good call. Good call. ---------------------------------------------------------------------- NJWA Television championship match: Jack Jones [c] vs. "The Rush" EJ Drivven ---------------------------------------------------------------------- MP: In what is the biggest match so far in NJWA's short history, Jack
Jones and EJ Drivven will go toe-to-toe in a tremendous match!
Jack Jones wants to start off his title run with a successful defense against EJ Drivven, and The Rush wants to seek revenge on
Jack Jones -- and brother Duncan -- for taking him out of the battle royal last week!
BJ: Don't you ever get tired of repeating yourself? I know I tire of hearing it. MP: Shutup, BJ. Let's hear from the challenger, EJ Drivven! [From the silent darkness, a demonic laugh ruptures the calm. Ironically, the sound is no more than a whisper ... but the intent that lies behind it sends twisted fragments of unease down the spine.
Despite the subdued volume, the laugh is commanding, deliberate and morbidly hypnotic as it peels over and over, trapped in a seemingly endless chamber of echoes. Eventually, the cackle morphs into a form of free flowing spoken word. The voice remains muted, but the intensity behind it rockets to higher realms.]
"Whiplashed straight to Hell was he ; A Devonshire by name, but a coward by nature. Faster than anyone could spit, he was gone. His bags were packed, citing 'unsafe working conditions' as he hauled his useless carcass out of the door. It was lies ... all lies ... It's not the NJWA that is unsafe ..."
[From the raven tapestry, the hulking 6'5" frame of one EJ Drivven then oozes into prominence. His grim chin-goateed countenance is mostly obscured by the matted tendrils of long black hair, while his heavily muscled body is covered merely by a pair of black denim jeans and scuffed Dr. Marten boots.]
EJD: ... It is *I* that is unsafe, unstable and is severely detrimental to your health. Y'see, I take great pleasure in the pain I inflict upon others ... And this truth is a constant within me that will never change, irrespective of whether my path takes me to salvation or damnation.
[Drivven raises his partially bowed head, fraction by practiced fraction until his piercing blue eyes glare straight into the prying camera lense. A wide grin of disturbed satisfaction creases the seething mask of his all-too-pale facade.]
EJD: Of course, at the last Theater of War event, it wasn't all adrenaline and glory for the Chaos Warrior that is I. In fact, thanks to the attentions of a putrid piece of spineless sh-*bleep* named Duncan Jones, I was unceremoniously denied the
Television gold that had *my* name written all over it ... Thanks to that little runt, his brother was able to topple me from my position of dominance and flip me to the concrete ; ripping from my grasp the accolade that *everyone* knows should belong to me.
[An exaggerated scowl clouds his complexion. However, true to this man's schizophrenic nature, the expression almost instantly falls into another malevolent smile.]
EJD: However, in their wisdom, the NJWA powers have attempted to rectify the screwjob ending that tainted the previous Main Event by setting me against Jack Jones in a TV Title match. This time, it's one on one ... This time, no win will be given on a ridiculous stipulation ... This time, the victor is going to be the one that beats the other to a plasma-ridden pulp, before pinning his shoulders to the mat for the inevitable 1-2-3 ...
... And y'know what? ... [Unbidden, the unsettling Joker-esque grin widens. Drivven's facial features warp to a greater extent, further highlighting the unmistakable 'man unhinged' aura within the sadistic behemoth.]
EJD: ... I like my chances. This time, Jack, it matters not to me if you involve your whole f*bleep*-in' family in this fight, because you can be assured that the result of the equation will definitely remain the same ;
[As insidiously as he had arrived, the supposed mouthpiece of Jacob's
Eternal Sin melds back into the darkness in a fascinating chameleon-like manner. Naturally, the rasping whisper that is his haunting voice continues to resound.]
EJD: ... My fist, your face ... My fury, your decimation ... Your gold, *MY* *DAMN* *GOLD*!!!
Jones, your descent into my realms of 'Spiral Aggression' has begun ... [End.] BJ: He still scares me, Maria. MP: Indeed, very strong words from EJ Drivven for not only Jack, but
Duncan Jones as well. EJ Drivven feels cheated from the
Television title, and as well he should. Determined and headstrong, EJ Drivven goes into this coming Theater of War with one thing on his mind: victory. He will surely not be finished until he holds that title over his head, or he takes Jack Jones out indefinately.
BJ: I will take my chances that Drivven's going to win... he one mean son of a bitch. I compared him to my goat Demetrius, earlier, and it was a good comparison I feel. I don't lie, Maria... never have, never will. Uh-uh.
MP: Ahem. Now we have prerecorded comments from the Television champion himself, Jack Jones!
[We fade in from black upon a shot of the NJWA Television Championship. Just the gold, the only gold that is currently floating around NJWA. Of course this shot wouldn't be complete with out none other than Duncan Jones.
Duncan Jones? Yes.. he's got the title.] DJ: You see what's it about? What the name of Jones stands for? It stands for winning, it's stands for kicking everyone's ass that stands in their way. See, I may not have been born with the height or size, but EJ Drivven.. my last name is Jones, and that means that if you get into my face.. I _too_ will kick your ass!
But let's not get ahead of ourselves. Because standing in between of me just going out there and showing the world how truly sad you really are, is my brother Jack. And from what I've heard, he's not too happy about already putting the title on the line. You're not going to like Jack when he's pissed off.. not at all.
[Just then Duncan flashes the $1,089 smile.] DJ: So here's what I think you need to do EJ. Stay away, stay very far away.. because you do not want to take the trip to Jonestown to meet Jack. It's not a place for a person like you.. not at all man.
My best advice is not to even show up at all, but if you are dumb enough to take the trip... well, it is widely known that you might make it to Jonestown on a bus, but you're sure as hell to leave it on the slab.
[A $1,089 smile, but this time with some change to bring it up to
$1,089.47!] DJ: We don't play nice there! [Fade as Duncan again hoists the title up onto his shoulder, very damn proud of what his brother won.]
MP: Duncan Jones doing the speaking for his brother Jac-- BJ: Doesn't Jack do things for himself? He has Duncan talk for him, he had Duncan win him the Television title... the man needs to stand up on his own two feet, if you ask me. The NJWA needs prominent figures standing tall for it, not some big bulky thug who can't speak! We need orators, damnit! And seeing-eye dogs!
And for the love of god, will somebody please get me a black coffee! Is that too much to ask?!
MP: You're starting to get on my nerves, BJ. Lucky for you, we're out of time. Otherwise, a national audience might see me kicking your ass...
BJ: Grrr... I'd like that. Would you wear thigh-high leather boots? MP: Folks, we're out of time. I want to thank for you watching tonight! For this pathetic excuse of a man, BJ Carter, I for myself, Maria Pontech, we want to wish you a good night!
[The scene slowly fades to black as Maria Pontech gets up out of her chair and walks off of the set. The screen silhouettes in a black cascade as we go off the air.]
