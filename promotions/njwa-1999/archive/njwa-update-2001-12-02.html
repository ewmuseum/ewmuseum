Upcoming Event =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= Theater of War NJWA Arena, Atlantic City, New Jersey December 15, 2001 After the first two editions of Theater of War, the bar was set. The NJWA will have to produce its finest quality wrestling in order to outdo the the first two cards. Three action packed matches are signed, with more matches being added a possibility. In the first match of the evening.... THE NEWAGE TYRANT vs. ???? * The Newage Tyrant will make his singles debut in an NJWA ring against a mystery opponent. After an impressive outting in the battle royal, Tyrant looks to make a name for himself here in the NJWA. Who will be the man to take on Tyrant? Only time will tell. The Newage Tyrant will have yet another difficult task on his hands as he tries to prepare himself for a match against an unknown competitor! EL COOL ARROW vs. "The Deathdealer" TOMMY GANZ * El Cool Arrow will debut in the NJWA by taking on NJWA superstar Tommy Ganz. Ganz was one of the final three in the ring for the Television title battle royal, and surely wants to make up for the shortcoming by defeating El Cool Arrow in his debut match. And in the main event... -- NJWA TELEVISION TITLE MATCH -- NJWA TELEVISION CHAMPION JACK JONES vs. "The Rush" EJ DRIVVEN * In Jack Jones' first title defense, he has himself a hell of a challege. EJ Drivven sent William Devonshire to the hospital and almost won the battle royal last Saturday. He will attempt to extract revenge on Jones for eliminating him on Saturday by taking his Television strap! =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- 8.
