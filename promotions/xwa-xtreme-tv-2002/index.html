Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

XWA taped a weekly television program it called Xtreme TV, hosted on a shared server inside the Primetime wrestling family, running an open roster-application board alongside its own card previews and taping recaps through the spring of 2002.

Its surviving record centers on a single-elimination World Title tournament, run across successive tapings from a rotating string of Connecticut and New York venues — the Armory in Kingston, the Hudson Valley Civic Center in Poughkeepsie, the University of Western Connecticut in Danbury, and the Foxwoods casino in Ledyard.

Its own owner-president, "Boda," narrated the promotion's own news in the third person across its surviving rows, announcing his own promotion mid-tournament to the presidency of a related federation, NTICW, while naming a successor to be revealed live on Xtreme TV.

Its held record runs from a 25 March 2002 taping through a 20 May 2002 card preview; no closing notice survives.
