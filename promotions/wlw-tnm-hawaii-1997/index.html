Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The World League of Wrestling advertised itself inconsistently across its own recruitment posts: its earliest surviving ad, 17 April 1997, claimed it had “been running since the early 90's and has been recently opened to the public,” while its August posts claimed “over 6 years experience” and then, the same month, “over 7 years” — inflating too inconsistently across posts to stand as a founding date. Every version of the pitch agreed on the essentials: original characters only, decided by the TNM v6.2+ simulator “to ensure no biased results,” with real wrestlers barred by name though characters merely resembling one were allowed.

Its own rules let a runner field up to four characters, pushed according to how much that runner actually participated, with experience gained through submitted “flashes” and title wins; it planned heavyweight, cruiserweight, tag-team, and six-man-tag divisions, and for matches its simulator couldn't itself resolve, it ran a modified version of a WWF roleplaying game.

Its one surviving in-fiction document, a roleplay dated 24 July 1997, introduced the Dark Shadow Corporation, an elite, mafia-styled stable of fourteen wrestlers dressed in matching pinstripe suits and led by ring leader Eric Youngblood, who pitched it as more selective than its real-world model — “unlike the nWo with Bagwell, Norton, Vincent, etc., there are no mid-carders in it” — and said he hoped to recruit the group's reach into other federations as well.

No card, result, or further roster document survives. The promotion's last dated post, 12 September 1997, carries nothing beyond a signature and a homepage link.
