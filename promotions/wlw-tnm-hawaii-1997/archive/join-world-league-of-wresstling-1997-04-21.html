***JOIN*** World League of Wresstling

21 April 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The WLW is looking for the top wrestling superstars in the world to compete in the highest level of competition - the WLW! Think your character has what it takes, then join the ranks of the WLW. ONLY ONE CONDITION: NO REAL CHARACTERS ALLOWED, however, you can create a character that acts like a real wrestler, but cannot use the name. We like creativity and your characters push is based on the degree of participation of the runner. Check out the home page for the WLW at: Http://www2.hawaii.edu/~evanc/wlw.html It runs on TNM 6.2+! But, for matches not able to run on TNM, I used a modified version of the WWF role-playing game. E-mail me for more info. We are always hiring, and there is no limit to the amount of runners in the league. -- [name withheld] WLW Co-President http://www2.hawaii.edu/~evanc/wlw.html [detail withheld]
