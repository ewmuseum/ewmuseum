UBC Dual Combat Tournament

23 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
No specific date set yet, but we're getting closer. Maybe it's about time that I explain to ya'll exactly what's going down. It's going to be big. REAL big. We will start off UBC with a giant tournament. How big? 64 fighters. How will it be run? Format: Team-tournament -- a team of two fighters will face another team in tornado competition until one man is knocked out or submits. Single elimination, winning team is the new Dual Combat Champions. Rules? The tournament will be based on RPs, but we'll make it fair. For two-handler teams, it'll be two per person, 4 total for the team is the maximum. For one-handler teams, they may post up to four if they wish. RPs must be at least 30 lines long, no maximum limit. Standard fare, don't use people in your RPs without permission, don't attack or kill UBC crew, keep the cursing at least that it's not all you say. What's the X-factor? Glad you asked. You see, the UBC understands that for whatever reason, some people just plain suck at applications. It happens. When the pressure is on, people may perform well. That's how we'll run the tournament. Because why would we have it so big to start off? Out of the 64 people who start, some of them will not be employed by the end of that round. The first round, the qualifying round, will be where the UBC workers will prove to us that they should indeed be there. RP-quality and segments (that they wrote) will be taken into account. Great workers who lose to great workers will not be dismissed. Likewise, a no-shower who is on a winning team will not be allowed to stay. We're giving everyone a tryout in the first round. No-shows and those with a severe lack in writing quality and workrate will be sent to PWA. After we see the applications, we'll extend invitations. Accepting the invitation means that you agree to the rules of the tournament. I think it will definitely mean something and get people working if their [potential] jobs are on the line. If you can't work an RP-based tournament, please do not accept the invitation. After the qualifying tournament, everyone who is left will be added to UBC's opening roster. And from there the tournament will be finished. Teams? You may form your own once I list the participants, or you may request that I pair you with someone. If that happens, please do not complain about your partner. When will this happen? I hope to give everyone a week's notice as soon as I go through the applications and send out the invitations. Anything else? I'll be posting more updates as I can. Thanks for keeping up with UBC and don't worry, it's coming along nicely. Bye!
