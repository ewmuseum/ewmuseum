Jurisdiction Is Up

7 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Well, we had one helluva time at the Jurisdiction show. Afterwards, we went into the parking lot, off camera, and got a few reactions from the fans. And here are what a few of them had to say: Jeffery Gila: Tonight, was defenitely historical, and by the ways things turned out, I expect ALOT of great things to come from the UBC! This was defenitely worth the wait and worth every penny for what I paid for my seat! Spencer Gansin: All I can say is that the show was unbelievable! I was on my feet the whole night, and not just because there were no seats down on the floor level, but because it was that damn good! Jessica Alvarez: My boyfriend was going on all night, he was excited and so was I. I can't wait to go to the next show, because we have already gotten our tickets for the show in ALbuquerque, and we will be there on the floor, just like we were tonight! Stay tuned with UBC, because next week, we will have one helluva show, featuring fighters that are making their debut, along with a star making his return to the fighting zone!
