First Announced UBC Tourney Teams

23 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
This is a tentative list, still with much slots left to fill. Also, this will indeed be a 64-man tournament (32 team slots open). Quite a goal, I know, but hey, any slots left to fill by the start of the tournament will be taken up by the Suburban Ninja Clan. And they can fill 20 slots, though that won't be necessary as it is. :p Some teams wish their names not to be revealed yet, in which case I'll only use their team name. A reminder about the tournament, the first round is a qualifier. Applicants are invited to enter the tournament, and they have one shot to impress myself and the UBC staff or they will be sent to PWA. So, not everyone will make it past this round. Quality and workrate, not wins and losses, will ba taken into account when deciding who to keep. A great RPer and segment writer beaten by another great RPer and segment writer won't be sent away just because they lost the first round. Likewise, someone who no-showed and their partner squeaked them past with a win for the team will not be staying, even though their team won. So just put out your best work if you're in the tournament. No half-assing it. Your jobs aren't secured yet. :p As for the rest of you, you're free to speculate who the mystery applicants or obscure teams are, and make tentative predictions about who will advance past the qualifying rounds and actually make it onto the UBC roster. 1) Johnny Nightmare and Black Lotus 2) Tommy Grady and Mystery Partner (TBA) 3) Malik Roland and Devin Shadow 4) Heart Throbs (returning tag team, one handler) 5) High and Dry (names withheld, new characters, familiar handlers) 6) Forever Chaos (names withheld) 7) Politically Incorrect (hint, it's not the former PCW/FSW tag team senators.) 8) Honor and Ambition (names withheld) 9) Alan Ackart and Brett Wayne That's it to announce today. I'm waiting for some confirmations of invitations extended and then there will be more names to list. Applications are still open, if you want to join then just drop me a line and you'll be added to the tryout list!
