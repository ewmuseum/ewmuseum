Jurisdiction Inching Closer

5 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
As Saturday, December 7th grows closer and closer, the usual feeling amongst fighters and staff alike is that of nevous. Xavier, Ehren and President Kron all have one helluva show entitled to earning everyone's respect and drawing everyone's attention. The card is as well finalised and what a great one it is! We also have received a final word that the contract for next week's Jurisdiction WILL have a Premier Event featuring PosterBoy and Chris "AK" Carter, in a match to claim the name: "UBC's Original Gangsta/Thug". With the loser of the match required to begin competing under a NEW gimmick! UBC provides the goods as Jurisdiction claims it's spot on TV's top 10 list, all this Saturday!
