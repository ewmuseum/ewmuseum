Another Jurisdiction Hits!

21 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
That's right, UBC fans, another Jurisdiction episode jumped off the air waves and into your TV, live from Denver, Colorado. It hit, and it hit hard! This week's Jurisdiction was the first episode of the "Kron Era", as President Kron came out and made his first appearance, stating some dominant comments. We saw the debut of Statuz Quo's cousin. For those of you who don't know, Statuz Quo is a PCW superstar, which basically tells you this new UBC fighter, Red Vizionz, is no damn joke. Red Vizionz is the newest member of Sudden Violence as he assisted Violent B and Chris "AK" Carter in an all out assault to MrSynnerster. Plus, THE trophy was awarded! Tommy Grady and Brett Wayne battled eachother and it was everything we thought it would be. Go check out UBC Jurisdiction!
