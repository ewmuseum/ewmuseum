UBC Title Divisions

23 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Okay, more news. This time about the UBC title divisions. There will be 4. 3 singles divisions, will be three ranked levels of competition. Lowest level will have 55% of the fed working there. Middle division will have 35% involved. Upper-card is projected to have 10% of the people involved. These numbers are predictions and people's actual talent may shift things around slightly. The singles divisions will all be run based on an angle format. I trust you all know what that is. :p The team division will be run based on RPs. Angles may help decide what happens in a match, but outcomes are determined by RPs. The reasoning? If there are two people in a team, surely they can come up with a good RP or two between them. I won't institute RP limits into this division (except for the opening tournament), nor will I nitpick about if there is a one-handler team or a two-handler team. Just make sure that if you're a one-handler team that you're confident enough to handle yourself against two opponents. And two-handler teams, if you are facing a one-handler team, just try to be reasonable and don't swamp them with more RPs than one person could ever try to beat. Now, the first month will be run in the tournament for the team belts. This will let the singles division directors see who goes where. After the tourney, business will be conducted as any other fed in WT. The handlers will drive UBC. The staff's job will be to coordinate it, but what don't expect or want to have to jump in every minute to come up with angles for people. There are plans, and definite fan and handler interest in a seasonal "Street Fighting Division" to be run a little further into UBC's span. More on that when it happens.
