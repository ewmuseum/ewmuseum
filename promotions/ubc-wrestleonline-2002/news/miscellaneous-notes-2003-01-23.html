Miscellaneous Notes

23 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Homebase: The UBC will have a new homebase. Everyone get your bags packed, because the UBC is now an international federation! I've come to the conclusion that the fighting aspect of this federation needs more than just to be emphasized. It needs to be viewed as legitimate competition just as much as wrestling, something that can't happen as long as it's viewed as dingy and "underground". So, I scoured the world looking for hospitable environments for fighters in the mixed martial arts circuit... *Spins globe* And I came across a city that is renowned for its appreciation of MMA fighting as a honorable, respectable form of physical competition. *Places finger on the globe* And that city is... *Globe stops spinning from friction, finger stops over particular city. Removes the finger and reads the name of the city.* Uh... Sa, Sapa-- Right! UBC's new homebase, and the place where all of its shows will be held, is Sao Paolo, Brazil! It's the largest city in South America, and the third largest city in the world. ____________________________________ All our shows will be held in the same arena. [b]Stadium: Cicero Pompeu de Toledo Capacity of 80,000 people Built in 1960[/b] ______________________________________ This arena is where all our fights will be held, fought in a cage-enclosed ring. There is a mat. That's it. This ring is design to be for in, plain and simple. There will be no high-flying attacks, the holes in the cage are too small to climb up. No interference, that cage is too tall to jump. And the cage itself isn't so solid as wrestling cages. They give a little if pressed with weight. The cage is to contain the action, not to be used as a weapon. The ring is a circle with a diameter of 20 feet. There is a single door that is locked by the ref from the inside once the ref and all participants have entered the cage. The door will only open when someone wins by submission or knockout. ________________________________ Some people have asked if I, as [name withheld] (IC [name withheld] in PCW, or OOC [name withheld] just made into a character) will be running the UBC on television. Truth be told, the character I created is so simplistic he doesn't matter, but I'll introduce him anyway. *A guy in a black suit walks out.* Say hello to Thomas "T.P." Parker. He worked in the UBC offices as a clerk until the restructuring. Why is he in charge? He bought the place by dipping early into his trust fund. That's right, unlike some other presidential figures, T.P.'s financial future rides on how well the UBC performs, which gives him a little more accountability for the product than others who will be fine if it tanks or not and therefore do whatever just to fulfill their egos. So what about him? I'm actually wanting people to say that, because T.P. will rarely ever be on camera. T.P. is a fedhead like Russell Simmons is a producer. You know he's running things, but at most you'll see him on the program is at the end saying "Thanks for coming." That's the kind of IC fedhead that T.P. will be. He's fine staying off-camera and letting the fighters be the stars of the show. While the fighters all off-camera answer to him, he feels that there's no reason why he needs to be an on-camera presence. He saw how President Kron got more press than some other the former UBC workers, and didn't think that helped anything. So T.P., for a fedhead, works in the background, and no one on-camera will be in an ownership position, nor will there be anyone representing the ownership (ie, commissioners, enforcers, etc.) ____________________________ Miscellaneous Notes: 1) The UBC, being a fighting federation based in Brazil, declares itself official "neutral ground" in WO. That means that all the times that people from different federations wanted to kick the crap out of each other but never had any place where you could go and settle those issues in the ring, the UBC will be that arena. We'll dedicate three to five PPV spots to matches between wrestlers from other federations in the WO. We'll provide the matchwriters, the match judges, the RP board... all you guys have to do is come here and RP. Yes, UBC is a WT fed. It's neutral ground in that we're a fighting federation based in Brazil. We're not near anyone to immediately answer to them, and even so, we're not in the same business as the other federations, so if they want to come here and wrestle, we'll allow it. And as for the other issue of if outside wrestlers will come here and overshadow our workers... well, it's hard to account for relative or overall fame at this point. But, given the business of being a fighting fed (and allowing people to wrestle here, even though our fans may not be wrestling fans as well), we obviously wouldn't book these matches higher than being the first matches on the card. And the exact number wouldn't only be on the basis of first-come, first-serve. We'd evaluate things like drawing power, relative importance to the wrestlers (ie, matches just made up on the fly without any significance with a win or loss wouldn't be accepted), and the number would also depend on how many well-built feuds we have going into the PPV. I'm a believer in PPV's not having random matches. Every match should be important and in some way climactic for those involved. Otherwise people will naturally overlook it and in that case what's the point of having people waste time RPing for it, or having matchwriters spend time on that as opposed to something else that was well-put together coming in? So if there aren't many angles coming into the PPV, we'd accept a few more outside matches than normal. Still, ideally we'd be looking for a UBC-dominated card since it is our PPV. 2) If anyone is loaned to UBC for extreme lack of workrate, you'll be placed on working probation here. What that basically means is that you will be required to put in at least one in-card for every show we inform you about for two to three weeks. Missing even one without advance notice means that you'll just be sent back to PWA. After the probation period, you'll be placed into an RP-based division to get you back up to speed with that. You'll be disqualified from winning any UBC titles, but if your fedheads are impressed with your turnaround your "loan term" here could be relatively short. 3) The will have matches written that damn near will seem like something out of Tekken or Dead or Alive. It has to be that way to allow for all the different fighting styles I'm seeing come in. Matchwriters will get bored if they're just writing clinched knees to the head every week. 4) I'm working on an RP-based singles division, will gauge reaction to it. It'll be a couple of months off, but it will take advantage of Sao Paolo as a base city. In fact, I'll post a poll about that a little later.
