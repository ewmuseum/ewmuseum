UBC Needs A Roster!

20 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I'm not lying. UBC is opening and we need you, the e-fedders out there, to help us out by, well, applying! We'll go through the free agent boards and other places as well, but we would also like some good ol' fashioned applications. What are we looking for? Interesting characters to work in a fighting federation. What's that mean? I'd like this roster to be just as interesting and diverse as the other feds in WrestleOnline. PCW/RUSH, OSW, PWC, PURE... all of them have a greatly diverse cast of characters. That's what UBC wants, as well. It can't all be a federation of bare-bones "guys who want to fight," because it'll get old fast. The more interesting and unique the character, the better! But, being a fighting fed, we're looking for characters to battle in a fighting format. Ever seen PRIDE? UFC? That's the kinda feel we're going for here. You're looking to the wrong place unfortunately if you're expecting to see piledrivers, vertical suplexes, or hurricanranas anywhere in this federation. It'll be a fighting federation and run as such. So we'll take anybody but in order to survive here, they have to be fighters within one of these subcategories: Strikers -- Duh, they punch and kick, using their limbs to inflict blows. Though they can attack downed opponents, they mostly do their fighting from a standing base. We'd like strikers, and diverse strikers at that. (IE, specializing in a particular martial art such as Muay Thai, Kung Fu, Tae Kwon Do, Jiujitsu, Tai Chi, so on and so forth) Wrestlers -- Not the professional wrestlers, but amateur wrestlers. These guys use wrestling takedowns to get their opponents off their feet, and prevent them from counterattacking.... And then they beat the crap out of them. Freestyle wrestlers or classically trained Greco-Roman wrestlers are welcomed. Judo would also count in a wrestling category since it is a takedown-based martial art. Submissionists -- Bare bones fighters who focus on wrenching opponent's joints and bones in such a way that they'll be forced to tap out. Submissionists are often wrestlers as well, but they can exist separately. You can obviously be a mix of these, but you can make your character better and more realistic if they are easily defined in one group. What to put in the application? Well, there is a link under the main menu on the website, but if one can't use that, here is a list of things that they can manually send to [detail withheld] Name (duh) Height Weight Biography (the longer, the better, but please don't send a novel. :p) Fighting Style (Pick one or a mix of the two above) Finishing Move (This is optional; truth, be told rarely do fighters actually have a go-to move to win a fight, but if you desperately want a finishing move then I won't stop you.) Gimmick (Like wrestling, the UBC would like interesting gimmicks/characters; this should be simply explained in at most two lines.)
