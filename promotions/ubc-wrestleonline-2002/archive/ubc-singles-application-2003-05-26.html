Singles application (2003)

26 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is The fed’s own singles application form, as the Wayback Machine captured it on 26 May 2003: the questions it put to a new wrestler’s handler and to the character (fighter information, gimmick, moves, appearance for the poser artist, entrance, biography) and the sample roleplay it required. The form’s entry boxes, navigation and images are not carried.
This is the singles application for the UBC. Give us a short explanation of your character's gimmick. Give a few basic wrestling moves your character uses. Explain your fighter's most detailed appearance for our poser artist to create your picture. Give us 3 poses that you would like for your character to be posing in whenever they are rendered. signature.move Describe how your signature move will work. finishing.move Describe how your finishing move will work. Give a detailed description for the entrance of your wrestler for our match writers to use in the shows. Please note that we do not use arenas and that you will be fighting in a warehouse. Your character's biography. backstageINFORMATION If you are accepted into the UBC, we need a password that you will be using to access the backstage part of the UBC website. Please provide that password in the right-hand field! sampleROLEPLAY Be sure and make this one of your best works because this is mainly how we decide on your status with UBC.
