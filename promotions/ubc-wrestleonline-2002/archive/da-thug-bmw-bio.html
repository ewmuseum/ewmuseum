Da Thug Biography Biography Chris [name withheld] aka Da Thug was born on February Ninth, 1977, in Compton, California. While most children his age went to sleep with a soothing bedtime story from there mom or dad, Chris was forced asleep with the not so soothing sounds of police sirens and gunshots. Life isn't always a fair place, and Chris knows this better than the mass majority. Through Chris's entire childhood, his parents were unhappy and fought with eachother constantly. The non-stop battling between his parents wasn't good for Chris, but he dealt with it, just like most children in his situation, who live in the ghetto, would. See, Chris didn't live with his actualy parents, because as a newborn, he was left in a dumpster outside the hospital as soon as he was born. A black couple were walking out of the hospital after receiving word that they were unable to have a child... And there it was, little baby-Chris crying in a trash can. When Johnesha and Tryone found Chris, they took him. It wasn't til a year later that they legally registered him and adopted him as their child... Little did Chris, or anyone else know, that this was the beginning of a special life, this was the beginning of a life who belongs to someone who will haunt the world when he gets older, this was the beginning of Da Thug's life. By the time Chris was seven years old, the year was 1984. Chris had already began to understand the concept of this cold process known as life. He started to realize how things were, he started to understand how the game is played and how life in the ghetto was. In school, Chris was always being rejected because he was the 'white boy', he was the 'cracka', and just like his very own life, he learned to adapt to it. The toture continued til' he was ten years old, a boy by the name of Sean Jackson moved to Compton from Harlem, New York. Being a reject himself, Sean quickly made friends with Chris. Chris and Sean grew up together and have been through several things together, they were like brothers... But again, Chris's white skin came into play as a set-back. On a rainy September evening, Chris came bursting in through the front door of his house in a rage. When his mother, Johnesha, asked him what was wrong, he replied, 'I'm sick of being white, I'm a fuckin' reject, Mah! Why am I like this?'. Johnesha told him, 'Chris, don't let this get you down. I know it's hard for you sometimes, just hold your head up and you'll be alright'. So Chris did exactly that, or at least he tried to as long as he possibly could. The years went on, Chris still lived in the same apartment, Sean still lived in the apartment across the street from him, and they were still best friends. But now, Chris and Sean were sophomores in High School and things were a lot more serious and deep than they were when Chris and Sean were children. Things are much different. Now, if you were to insult a gang or a person, you don't get jumped, you get shot. Now if you steal money, you don't get jumped, you get mobbed. Now, girls aren't bitches, they're hoes, and you're no longer a boy, you're on your way to being a man. After very much abusive behavior and a massive criminal record, Chris's parents began to get concerned. His dad, Tryone, said 'Don't worry, Johnesha, he's jus' livin' da ghetto life, stop sweatin' it. I went through it, my daddy went through it, now it's Chris's turn'. They days went on, another season flew by, and Chris's gangsta-life behavior didn't get any better, in fact, it only got worse. Now, Chris was husslin', pushin', fightin', and shootin'... All in a day's work. Then on the tenth of December, Chris and Sean were seen walking down their street around midnight, wearing all black. An hour later, 1:00 AM, Chris came running home with blood covering him, his loud panicing and breathing woke his mom up and she rushed in the room to see what the noise was. When she got in the room, she was shocked, she bursts into tears of panic. It turns out, Chris and Sean went to rob somebody with some friends from a local crip gang, because at this point in time, Chris had earned the respect of his high school and peers. Back to the fact, Sean was shot by a rival gang... And killed. The rest of the crips were hit with bullets, they also hit the rival gang with some of their own counter-fire. Chris was unarmed so he ducked under a nearby car after Sean was hit. But it didn't end there, oh no, not by a long shot. Chris decided to go get his 45 magnum and go back after the wounded rival gang members who managed to escape... And at the age of 17, Chris was now considered a murderer. He had killed the rival gang members with shots to the head. 'Momma, they killed my boy, they killed Sean! I had to get them muthafuckaz back!', said Chris. Tyrone, Chris's father, woke up and was informed. Both Chris's parents were depressed and shocked, so they gave Chris a last hug, $300, and a bag to pack his clothes in. They cared too much for Chris so they told him to run before police investigate. So Chris said his final goodbyes to his parents and told them he'd call them when he gets a chance. Chris then managed to travel over to southern LBC, where he rented a broke-down, shitty apartment from a friend of a friend's. With nothing to do, Chris began drinking alcohol by the gallon, and was a sever alcoholic for eight monthes of his life. One night, Chris was rushed to the hospital from an alcohol binge, he almost died. This was a cold awakening for him, so he decided he wanted to try and turn his life around. He began working out after stealing some weights and equipment from a nearby gym. More monthes went on, Chris turned 20 years old and had stopped drinking so much, he was then at the point where he was actually able to control it. So Chris began making several attempts to contact his parents... But the phone number was disconnected and they were no longer at the apartment. Chris believes that they were kidnapped and possibly murdered by that same rival gang, but doesn't know for sure. Although the guilt is great, Chris manages to ignore it and lodge it in the back of his head from day to day, which very well may be a reason to what happened next... Chris was diagnosed with a disease known as Chronic Violence Syndrome, or 'CVS'. Due to the overwhelming stress and problems in his life, he snapped because he couldn't hold his problems in any longer. A chemical imbalance in his brain is the source of the disease, however, there are no side effects or physical changes. Basically, the disease acts up sometimes and lays low sometimes, not much is known about this complex mental disease. Chris began going to a monthly councelor appointment to try and solve the problem, which is very unlikely. At the age of 21, Chris was now strong and in very good shape, he was mentally stable, or at least from what everyone says, and he needed something to do to get him out of the projects. So one day on a hot June day, Chris was walking down a street a few blocks away from his house when he saw a wrestling gym, lead by a former World Wrestling Entertainment superstar. Chris instantly walked in and signed up, he was unsure at first, but was very thankful he signed up after a few months of wrestling. Years went by, Chris was wrestling for several indy feds, and then came across Big Money Wrestling. Chris had many great matches and feuds, especially against a man known as Statuz Quo. In the year 2002, Da Thug decided it's time to leave BMW, he has set a big enough legacy there, as one of their recognized legends. Chris has now signed with the UBC... Other Moves Flowing DDT, Scoop Slam, Snap Suplex, Powerbomb, Flipping Arm Drag, Flipping Splash Off Top Rope, Half Crab, Boxing Combo, Swinging Neckbreaker.
