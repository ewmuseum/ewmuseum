Xavier Morningstar Biography Nicknames X, Xav. Hometown Las Cruces, New Mexico Birthday July 26, 1978 Height 6'5' Weight 275 Wrestling Style Technical / Brawler Finisher Move A Fallen Star Finisher Description Xavier stands behind the victim facing towards them. He hooks both the victim's arms and places their head inbetween the victim's shoulderblades. Morningstar then turns around in a motion so they are now facing the opposite direction and still holds onto Finisher Setup Move The Morning After Setup Description Xavier Morningstar stands facing the victim. He bends the victim down so they are bent facing in front on the his body. Xavier reaches around the the victim's body with his arms and lifts them up, spinning the victim in front of the his body. Morningstar Other Moves Name: Xavier X’tension Move: Arm Breaker, Fireman's Carry Description: Xavier lifts the victim up so they are laying stomach first across the his shoulders (a standing fireman's carry). Xavier then releases the victim's legs and throws them towards the his back. Morningstar grabs on the victim's arms and holds it across their should. Xavier sits down as the victim falls to the mat and impacts the victim's arm on his shoulder Name: Blackout Move: Face Driver, Reverse Description: Morningstar stands behind the victim, who is usually kneeling, he steps over the shoulders of the victim and then drops down to his knees, driving the victim's face into the mat. Name: Ankle Lock Move: Ankle Lock, Reverse Description: The victim is on their stomach, the Xavier grabs one leg by the ankle pushing it forward so the knee is bent and twists at the victim's ankle. Name: Adrenaline Rush Move: Back Breaker Rack, Back to Back Description: Xavier lifts the victim up over his shoulder so that the victim is across his back and their upper back is across his shoulder. Morningstar tucks the victim's legs around the his hips. From there he either hooks the victim's arms or applies an chinlock on the victim, applying pressure to the upper back of the victim by pulling down. Entrance Music 'Money Aint A Thing' by Jay Z, and Jermaine Dupri
