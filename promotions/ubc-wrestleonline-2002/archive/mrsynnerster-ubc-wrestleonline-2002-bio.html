MrSynnerster Biography Hometown Charleston, South Carolina Birthday 11/3/75 Height 6'5' Weight 265 Biography Unlike many of the other wrestlers, William Thornbush AKA MrSynnerster and his twin brother Malcolm grew up in a normal upper middle class African American household. Malcolm was the athletic one. He excelled in football, basketball. You name it he could play it, but his real love was wrestling. He was hoping to one day make it big in the pros. While Malcolm was doing the athletic thing, William was all about academics, especially science and medicine. Even though Malcolm and William were total opposites, they shared a love for the martial arts. Malcolm loved it for it’s physical discipline and William loved how it teaches you to became one with the mind body and spirit. After graduating high school, William went on to college, while Malcolm went on to train for wrestling. While Malcolm was training he met up with a guy from Charleston named Roger Merchant. The two became friends and after finishing training formed a tag team called the Chucktown Playaz and traveled the independent circuit. One day Roger and Malcolm were doing a show in Columbia, South Carolina, which was the same town as William’s college. Since it was in town, William and his fiancée Rosalind Stevens decided to attend the event. As soon as William arrived at the arena, one of the Security guards mistook him for Malcolm, and escorted William to the to the back. Backstage a nervous Roger Merchant paced back and forth. Malcolm’s plane was late, and they had the opening match against the tag team champions, Psi-Co Path. Realizing, he didn’t have much time, Roger convinced William to stand in for Malcolm. Roger offered to do all the work; he just needed someone to watch his back. After several minutes of convincing, William agreed to stand in for Malcolm. Dressed in his street clothes, William followed Roger to the ramp way. Redman’s Time for Some Action blasted through the Carolina Coliseum. As soon as the Chucktown Playaz stepped through the curtains the crowd erupted as the hometown heroes made their way to the ring. The SWF champions, Psi-co-Path made their way to the ring, and the match was on. Roger did as promised fights a majority of the match and getting his ass handed to him. The Psi-co Path team had the match won, until they made one fatal mistake. Being the cocky bastards they were, they allowed Roger to tag in William. Since William had no real knowledge of wrestling holds or moves, he did the best he could with his martial arts training. William was able to turn the tide of the match with a series of take downs and well placed kicks and in one final move William surprised one of the Psi-co Path team members with a choke hold, which is now known as the Burning Sensation. The man quickly tapped out. The Chucktown Playaz were the new Southern Wrestling Federation Tag Team Champions!!!! William stood on the step rope and held the belt high over his head. He knew he couldn’t keep it, but for one brief moment he was a champion. It was at that moment that William realized he didn’t want that feeling to end and he wanted a belt of his own. William went on to up his degree in Pre-Medicine, but instead of going on to Medical school, he opted to go into wrestling school. Much to the dismay of his parents, and friends, and even his brother Malcolm. The only person that supported William’s decision was Rosalind. Just before William finished up college, Malcolm and Roger landed a contract with the XCW. They were sent to the ACW, which was the farming league for the XCW. There they were going to sharpen their skills before advancing to the big league. Malcolm took up the name Cerberus and Roger took up the name Pluto, and they changed the name of their team to the Lordz of the Netherworld. The Lordz of the Netherworld dominated the tag team scene as well as many singles matches. The hot steak caught the eye of the XCW owner Oklahoma. Lordz were given offered a handicap match against another superstar Firestar. The winner of the match received a spot in the XCW. Due to a miscalculation, the Lordz lost the match, and was sent back to the ACW. After the lose Malcolm was never the same. He started doing drugs, fucking around with the wrong crowd. Just really making a mess of his life. Roger tried to help him, but couldn’t get through to him. Roger asked William to help out Malcolm, but William couldn’t at the time. William was trying to finish up school, he was trying to training for my wrestling career, and he was planning a wedding with Rosalind. William didn’t have time to watch after his brother and all his mistakes. Looking back, William has never been able to forgive himself for being so selfish. Time went on, and William finished graduated college, graduated from the wrestling school, and had a try out lined up with the XCW. The day before he try-out, William received a call from his brother Malcolm. Malcolm wanted William to meet him at his house for a talk about the future. Thinking Malcolm wanted to wish William luck, William rushed to Malcolm’s home where he found the house in shambles. There was broken furniture everywhere. There were a couple of people crashed out on the floor and laying across pieces of furniture. It almost looked like the aftermath of some wild party or orgy-gone aerie. William finally found his brother crashed out on a couch. In one hand, Malcolm held a half-empty bottom of Mad Dog 20/20. In the other hand he held a gun. After a long conversation, Malcolm shot William in the head. William was able to look up, just long enough to see Malcolm shot himself in the head as well. After being shot, William woke up in a mental institution claming to hear the voice of his dead brother in his head. With his mind so clouded, William looked for something, anything he could to focus on. That one thing was wrestling. William lost contact with his family, friends, and even his fiancée Rosalind. William lived, breathed, ate and slept wrestling. Soon William was accepted in the XCW. He was sent to the ACW to hone his skills and donned the name MrSynnerster. During his stint in the ACW MrSynnerster became the first ACW Television Champion only to lose it a week later to Rob Van Dam. This was the only lose MrSynnerster ever had in the ACW, but unlike Malcolm, William’s lose did not make him lose his grip on reality. In fact it strengthened it. Slowly but surely, William was becoming his old self again. The ACW/XCW Empire began to crumble and from the ashes rose the CWA. Like many of the former ACW/XCW wrestlers, William migrated to the new league. During his time off William married Rosalind. While in the CWA William shirked off the brooding tortured soul gimmick in favor for a very unconventional comedy role. This new form of promo cutting lead William AKA MrSynnerster to became the longest reigning and most popular World Heavyweight Champion in CWA history. Tiring of too much backstage politics William retired from wrestling. William soon began missing entertaining the fans on a weekly basis. Gaining encouragement from an unlikely source, William decided to come out of retirement hoping to make a fresh start in a new Federation. Other Feds GWO,ACW,XCW,CWA,NEW Other Fed Awards GWO Tag Champ, ACW TV Champ, CWA World Champ, NEW Regional Champ Wrestling Style Technical Finisher Move The Burning Sensation Finisher Description Cobra Clutch/ Full nelson type Submission move. Sometimes is modified into a Tiger suplex pinning combination. Finisher Setup Move The Whoa Setup Description (Similar to Brock Lesner’s F5 but when Synnerster swings his opponent around he falls forward like a reverse suplex instead of falls backwards like a DDT.) Other Moves Spinning Spinebuster (Arn Anderson style), frog splash, Dragon Suplex, Pain killer (armbar submission), floating armdrag (Ricky Steamboat style), Various martial arts kicks, punches and chops. T-bone Suplex, gutwrench falling powerbomb, Jumping tornado DDT, Springboard elbow in the corner.(Great Muta style, not Chyna.), Firemen carry suplex (Angle slam), top rope neckbreaker (Buff blockbuster), flatliner, electric chair drop Entrance Music Sinner by Drowning Pool
