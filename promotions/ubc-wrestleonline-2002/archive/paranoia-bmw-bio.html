Paranoia Biography Nicknames Noia, Para, That one guy with red hair... Hometown New Orleans, Loiusiana Birthday 05/19/80 Height 5'11' Weight 227 Biography Dan Atchley was born in New Orleans on May 19th, 1980. He was raised by his father, because mother died while giving birth to him. His father continuously blamed Dan for the death of his wife, and abused him for it. Dan never fought back, out of fear. This went on for 16 long years, till one day Dan had enough of it. He shoved his dad into the wall and punched him square in the face. His father just looked at him in shock, stood up, and walked away. Dan couldn’t seem to figure out why his father suddenly stopped, and just went upstairs to sleep. He was awoken suddenly in the night to a loud noise, and just thought someone outside was just messing around. He went back to sleep and in the morning awoke and went downstairs to a horrid smell in the house. He saw a trail of blood leading from the kitchen into the bathroom. He followed it and found his father dead in the bathroom, with his chest ripped open by a shotgun, which was laying clutched in his father’s hand. Dan freaked out and ran back upstairs to his room, where he proceeded to smash anything he could find. He jumped out the window, falling 2 stories and landing in a bush below. He smashed his head open and snapped his knee. He hobbled out of his yard, where he wandered the streets for a week, till one day he went to a hospital to fix his knee. He spent almost 2 weeks in the hospital waiting for his knee to recover, until they started asking him a bunch of questions, about how he did it, where he was from, and where his parents were. Suddenly he freaked out and stabbed the nurse with a needle, and fled from the hospital. He went back to his house, which was deserted, and cleaned out by the cops and looters. His father’s body had been removed, but the stench of death was still in the air. He went up to his room, which was empty except for his old TV, which he found in the closet. He plugged in the TV and switched it on. The first thing that came on was wrestling. He sat there for a week, just watching wrestling when it was on and sleeping on the hard wood floor of his room, slowly sinking into self-pity. He decided then that he wanted to do something with his life. Inspiration came when he saw the high-flying style of the wrestlers on TV, and decided that before his 17th birthday, he was going to be a student in any wrestling school he could find. Within 3 days of his deadline, he convinced a school to accept him. He spent almost a year learning and training, and then was ready to show his high-flying skills to the world as Paranoia.
