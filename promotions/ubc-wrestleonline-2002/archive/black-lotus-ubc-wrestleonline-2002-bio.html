Black Lotus Biography Nicknames The Assassin Hometown A small village in Japan Birthday July 14, 1982 Height 5'6' Weight 165 Biography Black Lotus was born in a small village in Japan. The village is manly his family. Black Lotus was trained in Martial arts from the time he could walk. The clan of the Lotus is a very mysteries one. They have lived in the mountains for many years. Some believed that his clan died off. At one time clan Lotus was used as Assassins and was hunted down one by one. They were good at what they did and some how end up vanishing from the hunters. Black Lotus wanted to show that his clan was still alive so he set out for Tokyo. He did some jobs for some of the darker people that lived there. The times are different now and the Tokyo police started looking for him. The elders of his clan found out and told him to leave Japan for his own safety. Black Lotus did just that. He called some of the people he worked for in the past and they got him out of Japan. He hid on a ship for many days. When he finally came out of his hiding place, he found they were in the US. He got off the ship and called a number he was given. Some men came and got him. They took him to meat some people. That’s when he found out the he was no longer a free man. He now had to do jobs for the people till he payed off the money he owed them. Still working for them, they have sent him out to fight people for money. Black Lotus is not trying to make a name for him self so he can pay them all they want. All he wants right now is to be a free man.
