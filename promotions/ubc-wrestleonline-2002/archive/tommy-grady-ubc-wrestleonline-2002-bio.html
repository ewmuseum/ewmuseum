Tommy Grady Biography Nicknames The Drunken ICON Hometown Huntington Beach, CA Birthday March 1, 1983 Height 6'4' Weight 265 Biography Tommy is a very interesting man. After growing up as a highly touted quarterback for his high school football team, Tommy was knocked unconcious with a vicious hit during a game. A severe concussion left him with extreme mental problems that he refused to have checked. Now he is left in the care of a family friend (known to Tommy as Aaron Everage, but known to many others as Artie Sacromanti, mafia hitman). Tommy is oblivious to what Everage does for a living and often finds himself confused by it, but doesn't bother seeking more. As far as the severity of his head injuries, we often find ourselves in the midst of them. Tommy is not the assertive type. He's a large specimen of a man, but he is not the fighting type. However the competitive side of him (the Tommy of old) always gets him into things that he doesn't think he can accomplish.. but seems to do it when the time calls for it. During interviews we find ourselves in his thoughts -- seeing what the competitive side of Tommy Grady is saying in Tommy's thoughts -- usually about an upcoming opponent. However, some times we catch Tommy drifting about some off the wall things that really don't make any sense. Another aspect of Tommy Grady is the edge of his sanity. He's not a smart human being at this point in his life. That one hit has handicapped him for a long, long time. But along with his stupidity come a drop off point. Tommy does reach a point where he goes absolutely insane. Like any man, Tommy can be pushed over the edge -- but when Tommy is pushed, he has no sense for what is legal, morally right, or considered SANE. If someone messes with Tommy enough, pushes him too much, angers him much -- Tommy will do everything he can to inflict as much pain as possible. It has recently been discovered, however, that alcohol brings back the old Tommy Grady. A drunken Tommy Grady is the most dangerous Tommy Grady possible. With his friend, and narrator, as his emotional anchor, Tommy has found a way to discover his old self. Beware of the drunken icon. He is as threatening as they come.
