Devin Shadow Biography Nicknames 'The Shadow' Hometown Vagrant all of his life (Born in Chicago, Illinois) Birthday February 22, 1979 Height 6' Weight 213 Biography A lifetime of living with no destiny spanning two decades… … and it all began with a paperback copy of “The Catcher In The Rye”. On the cold night of February 22, an Italian man in his late forties by the name of Wendall Valentino was driving through the snow covered streets of Chicago. Wendall was on his way to a job interview, one of which could have changed his unfortunate status of being homeless, living out of his Chrysler Station Wagon for the last few years. As he turned a corner in a rundown part of the city, his eyes caught the sight of a woman on her back, holding her stomach and screaming in pain. Blood stained the white snow all around her. Wendall stopped the car, and got out to see what was happening. The woman told him that she was pregnant, and that she was about to give birth. Wendall could tell that there was no time to get an ambulance, so he stayed with her until the ordeal was over. The result was a baby boy. Wendall cut of the umbilical cord with a knife he had been carrying for him for quite some time. He then took off his jacket, worn out from all the use over the years, and wrapped up the newborn. He then put him into the arms of the woman, who smiled and kissed the baby on the forehead. Wendall thought that this would pretty much be the end of this astonishing event, but he just before he left, he realized that the woman was still bleeding, and that it wouldn’t stop. He was about to leave, and call an ambulance, but the woman told him to stay with her, and allow her to explain what had happened. Her name was Anticlea Stillwater. She had grown up in Chicago with her family, a father by the name of Arien Stillwater and mother named Doris Stillwater. Her father had been a struggling poet who tried his hardest to make a life for himself by writing, but who was forced to feed his family by taking a job at a factory instead, while her mother worked in a cheap department store down the street. The family was poor, but happy to live together none the less. When Anticlea was only seventeen, a fire engulfed the family’s small little home, killing her father and her mother. She too almost died in the blaze, but instead, she survived by what she called a miracle from the Lord. Anticlea had been trapped in a corner, with the flames surrounding her. Just when she was about to be overcome by the fire, the floor gave way, and she fell onto a bed on the story below. Quickly, she rushed to the first floor window of the room and climbed out to safety. Anticlea survived, but was homeless and left alone with no family to turn to. She would be forced to use prostitution to survive for the next few years. When she was reached the age of 22, she realized that she was pregnant with the boy Wendall had just helped bring into the world, and that she had no idea who the father could be. And all the time she had been telling Wendall the story of her life, he had been writing all the important points down on the inside over of a book he had been reading – The Catcher In The Rye. He wrote down the names of her father and mother, her name, and the fact that she didn’t know who the father was. Anticlea Stillwater asked the complete stranger who had saved her son two more things. She first asked him to make sure that he son would be taken care of. He promised her on the spot. She then asked him to name the boy the name she had always wanted to give her son – Devin. Wendall wrote down “Devin Stillwater” in the book and the date of his birthday, and then looked down at her. The last thing that she did was looked into the eyes of her son, cried one last tear, and then died, her arms still holding the baby. Wendall called the hospital and then left the scene, taking the newborn Devin Stillwater. A few days later he went to the hospital, and told them the baby had been left in front of an abandoned building and that he was lucky to have found him when he did. The hospital gave the newborn his birth certificate and all the information to go with it. When asked what they wanted to do with the baby, Wendall asked them if it would be all right if he kept Devin. They agreed, not knowing his homeless status, and he left with the newborn in his arms. Years would pass. Wendall kept Devin as healthy as he could, and the newborn baby grew up to be a boy, a boy to a teenager, a teenager to a young man. He taught the boy everything that he knew. He taught him how to read, how to do math, but most importantly, he taught him how to survive. All that time Wendall had never found a decent job for a home. He had performed numerous odd jobs, and kept traveling across the country, never stopping in one place for too long. When Devin reached the age of eighteen, the two men had found out that Wendall had cancer, and didn’t have much longer to live. Devin decided that Wendall would stay in a hospital in the city of Detroit until his death, and that Devin would work at a near by factory to make sure he could afford it. So for a couple months, Devin worked in the factory every morning of everyday, and would then sleep in an armchair that sat beside Wendall’s hospital bed. Devin had found out about the cancer in late December. Wendall Valentino, the only father Devin ever had, died on the morning of February 22, 1998, the same day that Devin became nineteen. Only seconds after Wendall’s heart rate flat lined, Devin left the hospital, climbed into the station wagon that had been parked for over two months, and left Detroit behind. For the next few years, all that Devin did was get into cheap fights for a few underground feds. He fought, got the money, and spent it all on alcohol and any drug anyone could give him. He had earned himself a nickname - The Shadow. They called him this because he would fight, and then never really be seen again. Things went on like this for years. Devin Stillwater walks the Earth, not really searching for anything. All he caries with him is a copy of “The Catcher In The Rye”, trying to forget about a life that he never lived. Wrestling Style Brawler Finisher Move The Shadow Effect (Not the official name, but rather what it has been known to be called) Finisher Description An onslaught of hard punches to a standing opponent, finished off with an uppercut directly to the chin Finisher Setup Move Jumping Piledriver Setup Description Jumping Piledriver Other Moves Clothesline, DDT, Jump Kick, Russian Leg Sweep, Choke Slam, Sleeper Hold (Basically anything he can do to win) Entrance Music 'Light My Way' by Audioslave
