Regulations

22 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is The fed’s own Regulations page, as the Wayback Machine captured it on 22 May 2003 (our capture, lot 0163). The page’s navigation, images and pop-up links are not reproduced; its text is.
Here at the Underground Brawlers Club, we have set rules for how we like to run the show. If you do not obide by these rules as they are outlined, your contract could be placed in suspension. With an exception in the Consequences list, your contract could as well be terminated, pending the nature of the situation. Please, read these rules and follow them at all times to ensure your performance in UBC goes well. 1. The first and most important rule, is there will be absolutely no racist remarks made towards anyone. If you fail to acknowledge this rule, you will automatically be put on indefenite suspension. 2. If you don't like the way your fight turned out, complain to the writing staff on the 'Complaint' forum. Do not complain on ANY other boards other than that one. 3. The way our matches are decided are 45% angle/storyline wise and 55% roleplay quality. If your roleplay wasn't very good, don't expect the angle/storyline way to pull you through, you will be jobbed for a poor effort in roleplaying. 4. Although we don't look on the sole quantity of your roleplays, there is a 25 line limit to your roleplays. If this requirement is not met, it is dealt with in a very precautious manner, meaning we do deal with the people who fail to meet our requirements. 5. DO NOT attack any fighters, any staff members, any fans, referrees, animals or practically anything else that there is to attack without sole consent from the booker and the brawler being attacked. 6. If property isn't yours then leave it alone. Do not mess up anything that doesn't belong to you. 7. DO NOT roleplay until you have been officially accepted into the UBC. If you do, then you will automatically be rejected, meaning your contract deleted. 8. Don't join the fed as any real person/wrestler and do not base your character on any real wrestler. This is a fantasy fight club and we will keep it in that manner. 9. If you are unable to roleplay for one of your fights, then let one of the staff members know in advance instead of just not showing up. We will job you very harshly. 10. If you sign up as two completely different brawlers and they aren't for a tag team, then you are to abide by these rules for both characters. All of these rules will apply to the both of them. 11. You are to state your contract when signing up for the UBC as being between 4 months to 24 months. Developmental contracts are currently not available, as we do not have any branch-offs for UBC. If you happen to leave the UBC or if you are fired before your contract is up, we reserve the right to your characters' name, all likenesses belonging to him and everything else between him and the company to do with as we wish. 12. Before any of the roster members can represent the UBC in any type of PTC tournament or anything else involving one sole competitor, they must have wrestled at least 2 matches within the UBC. If not, then they will not be granted permission in representing us.
