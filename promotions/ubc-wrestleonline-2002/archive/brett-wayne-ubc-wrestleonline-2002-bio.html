Brett Wayne Biography Nicknames The Insane One Hometown Jonesboro, AR Birthday 02-14-1976 Height 6'6 Weight 321 Biography On Febuary 14, 1976 Gary Wayne and Terra Wayne brought birth to a little boy in a Emergancy Room in Jonesboro, Arkansas. This boy happened to be the one and the only Brett Wayne. Bretts father Gary owned a training facility for boxers and wrestlers. His father trained young men to become the best they can be. Years past and Brett grew bigger and bigger until eventually he was big enough to compete in Pee Wee Boxing Leagues for Teenagers. Brett was 10 when he took place in his first boxing match. Normaly you had to be atleast 16 years of age or older but after Brett showed them a demostration of his strength both physicaly and mentally they gave him the ok to box. So the officials put him up against the best they had for his first match to really see if he was good enough to compete in the league. Everyone doubted the 10 year old but it didn't seem to bother Brett of what the others thought of him. Its December 11, 1986. Bretts first match against the top boxer in the league. Millions tuned in to HBO to see the young 10 year old box. Once the boxing match started it ended. The match only lasted 12 seconds. The winner Brett Wayne. Everyone was stunned to see the power of the young boxer, they started praising him. Brett was disgusted at the people looking at him and talking about him. Instead of celebrating his first victory he went to his fathers training facility and worked out. Brett become the Pee Wee Undisputed Champion in a matter of weeks, he held on to the title for years until he finally resigned and gave the title up at the age of 14. The year 1990, January 31 the day of his fathers birthday. This day changed Bretts life entirely. Brett recieved a phone call about 1 pm saying that he's father was in a bad car accident. Brett rushed to the emergancy room as quick as possible only to see his father with cuts and scratch's all over his body. He's eyes were sweld shut. Pieces of glass were stuck in his face from the shattered windshield. Blood covered the poor mans face. About 6 pm Bretts father Gary, died leaving him only with his mother and him. Days past and Brett became stranger and stranger each day. He started fights everyday at school. He was caught doing drugs on the school property. He even went to school drunk a few times. Brett just wasnt the same. The school counsler annaylized that Brett had gone Insane because of his fathers death. She recommended to his mother she indeed get him help but Terra denied the fact that Brett was insane so they moved from the town of Jonesboro Arkansas to a smaller town. Walnut Ridge, Arkansas. This was a new start for the two and it seemed to help a little but Brett would still have mad spells where he would explode on someone. Four years past as Brett made many new friends at the local high school of Walnut Ridge. He played football, baseball, and basketball. He was known as the best defense of player in both basketball and football. He was also the most feared in both sports. Brett didn't care to much about baseball although he did play it, only because it took his mind off of his father but time would come when a mental picture of his father would pop up in his head and he would go off hurting someone pretty bad. He was suspended from school several times until eventually he was going to fail so his mother decided to homeshchool him. Brett starts his homeschooling on his junior year. He seemed to stay out of more trouble this way although he did miss playing basketball and football but Brett new it was better off the way it was. Brett finished homeschooling a year earlier and went to college. He had lots of offers in both basketball and football but Brett seemed to find something else better then both of those sports. WRESTLING, Brett seemed to love the sport of wreslting after his first year of wrestling for ASU, Arkansas State Univeristy. He continued wrestling and became one of the best in this sport also, the main reason was because he had a size advantage on most of the wrestlers. He won many awards and became well known to all the professional wrestling organizations. Brett recieved many phone calls from different wrestling organizations but he could only join one. On the day of Bretts 21st birthday he had received a phone call from the XWC President offering him a very good deal. Brett accepted. He first couple of months in the XWC was hell. Brett had finaly met people the same size as him. He was stunned to see them just as big. Brett lost his first 5 or 6 match's before winning his first, after that Brett seemed to pick up more wins match after match. Running over wrestler after wrestler. He won the XWC Television Title and then few months later he won both the Tag Team Title and World Heavyweight Title. It had seemed things were going very well for the young superstar of XWC until the President called Brett telling him the the XWC will be shutting down. Brett started making phone calls after phone calls trying to find another organization willing to take him so he could support his mother. After weeks of calling he had no luck. Weeks past and Brett sat in his house thining of what he could do next. Suddenly his phone rang once again. He answered it and Brett heard a voice the XWC President. He told him about a small organization called PoW. Brett got in touch with the PoW President and signed a contract for one year. Brett had now belonged to the PoW. He wrestled winning each title slowly one by one, the year went by fast for Brett Wayne it seemed and his contract was done with. Now it was up for the higher organization to see if they liked what they seen. Days past and Once again Brett received another phone call. Brett answered it and was in shock as he heard the words PIW go through one ear and out the other. PIW was a well known federation throughout the world and Brett had just been offered a contract. Brett met up with the President of PIW and sign the officiall papers regarding him and the PIW. He started out from the very bottom as he tried his best to make it halfway to the top but he didn't succeed until he met the man he will never forget. LLB LLB and Brett became good friends and eventually won the Tag Titles of PIW. LLB taught Brett things he would have never learned and if Brett wouldn't have met LLB he wouldn't be where he is at today. Beleive it or not LLB made Brett Wayne although Brett help LLB throughout his career also. Brett had suddenly made a name for himself until something tragic happened. The PIW CLOSED! Brett did not see this coming. He had once again went Insane. A year or two past for Brett as he had a small job working for a farm trying to provide for his mother. One day Brett had came home from work early, as he pulled up to his driveway he seen a Jet Black Limo parked in the middle of the road liked he owned it. Brett walked in only to reveal and Bald Man wearing expensive clothing. The man introduced himself revealing him as no other than Xaiver Mourningstar. Xaiver offered Brett a chance to join the UBC. Suprised and Excited Brett gladly excepted the offer. Now Brett is remaking a name for himself as he continues to bleed for the UBC.
