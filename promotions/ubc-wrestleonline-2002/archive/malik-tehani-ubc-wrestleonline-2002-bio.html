Malik Tehani Biography Nicknames The Great Imhotep Hometown Kinshasa, Democratic Republic of Congo Birthday 11-12-74 Height 6'3' Weight 268 Biography Malik Tehani comes from a powerful political family in The Democratic Republic of Congo. At a young age he decided that sports was his calling and wrestling especially. For many years his father had him trained by some of the best amateur wrestling coaches in Africa, when Malik turned 20 his father thought it was time for him to really test his limits so he had him entered into the Professional African Wrestling Championship. After the two week event came to the finals Malik Tehani was in the finals against a tough young man from the streets of Swaziland by the name of Amir Mubootu, after a hourlong battle the match was declared a draw and both men earned a mountain of respect for the other. Malik's father took Amir in after that day and began to train Amir and Malik as a strong tag team, a dominate tag team that will bring honor and pride back to the land of Africa.
