Before e-mailing any of the staff about something, please read this F.A.Q. list and see if your question is answered here. What can I do about it? General »1. Who owns the UBC? Wrestleonline.org owns UBC. Prior to this, both Ehren Sims and Xavier Morningstar co-owned UBC. »2. How long has the UBC been open? Since November 24th, of 2002. »3. What does UBC stand for? UBC stand for Underground Brawlers Club. »4. Can I be a part of the UBC staff? If there are staff spots left open we would be more than happy to see you sign up. Go to the Staff page and check for availabilities. Joining »1. How can I join? You can join by going to one of the above drop down menus and click on the Singles/Tag Team application. »2. Can I join without filling in the sample roleplay? Absolutely not. If you send in the app without the sample roleplay, then it will in fact be ignored! »3. What if I don't put a contract length on the form? Then you will be contacted for information on your contract. You will not be allowed to take part in any action without having your contract finalized. »4. Can I join as a real person? Absolutely not. Everything within the UBC is fantasy and nothing is ever copied or represented after any real material or person. »5. Can I join as more than one person? In most cases no, but if we feel that you will do your best to represent both characters as seperate people, then we may make an exception. If you do sign up as two wrestlers, then you will be treated as two different wrestlers, as if there were two seperate handlers handling them. Matches »1. How do you determine match winners? Sometimes it is by roleplay qualities and sometimes it is by angles. If you are a champion and your contender out-roleplays you, yet we see that the contender was just on a lucky-streak, we may decide to fall forth with the angle option, in turning the match out into a DQ or something along those lines. »2. How long do my roleplays need to be? Anywhere above 50 lines. We don't like to be too strict with this rule, as we feel that you all know what is and isn't acceptable. »3. I don't like how my match turned out! What can I do about it? If you have reasonable questions, talk to staff, otherwise talk to the hand. Bitching about it in public will only lead to suspension or possible contract termination.
