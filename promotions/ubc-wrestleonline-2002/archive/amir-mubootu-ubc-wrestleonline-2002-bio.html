Amir Mubootu Biography Nicknames The Swaziland Assissin Hometown Somewhere in Swaziland Birthday 12-19-77 Height 5'11' Weight 248 Biography Amir was born on the streets of Swaziland to poor parents. With a population of a little more than one million people and with a little more than ten percent of those people being infected with AIDS or HIV his odds of survival where not good from the start. He lost both his parents to AIDS while he was still young and had to fend for himself for many, many years. While trying to survive on the streets of Swaziland he learned that life can be cruel and he told himself that he would one day make his parents proud of him. He got himself a job in the city's only gym and learned very basic training schedule. He got the owner of the gym to let him sleep in a back room while he worked there. After a few years Amir grew strong and became famous for being able to beat anyone in ammeter wrestling styles. He was able to get entry into a local wrestling tournament and to his surprise he came in third out of 32 people. This drove him to train harder and worker longer to earn enough money to get a better trainer, he did this and was able to enter Africa premier wrestling tournament he breezed thru to the finals defeating his opponents in mere minutes. During the finals he wrestling a man by the name of Malik Tehani, their match was one for the ages. After an hour of pure wrestling the referee and ring judges decided to call the match a draw. After the match Malik's father, a local politician approached Amir and offered him the chance of a lifetime, train at the best gym in Africa with his son Malik and become the world's best tag team to date, Amir accepted the offer and now it time for them to show what they can do.
