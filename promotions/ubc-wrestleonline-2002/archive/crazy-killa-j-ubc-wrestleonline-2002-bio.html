Crazy 'Killa' J Biography Nicknames Crazy Jay, Killa, J, CJ, Ckj Hometown Rota, Spain Birthday 5/18/1978 Height 7'1 Weight 320 Biography 22 Year old Crazy 'Killa' J is a rap/wrestling superstar who likes to battle in the ring and on the mic. Killa J had first started writing and preforming rap at the age of 14. He had gotten into his battles so much that he ended up brawling with the opponents. Killa J was known as a fearsome brawler who got offensive over little things and did not allow any one to run over him. he became a big fan of wrestling at the age of 15 and decided to go to wrestling school. After a few years of Practicing rap and wrestling, he was ready for the ultimate challenge. At the age of 17, he released his first rap album called 'Tha Darkness Devours Upon Me'. The Cd was a hit and he was famous for his music. At the age of 19, he joined the first federation called the XCW which was battling another rival fed called the EWF for total controll. He decided to go with the XCW as they were the underdogs of the battle, he then took the federation under control after backstabbing some EWF'ers. At the time, the jobber was on every one's hate list, but no one knew that he would become one of Wrestling's most hated heels ever around. After three months in the XCW, he had won a Battle Royal match for the XCW World Heavyweight championship. He had became the first ever and only ever XCW Champion. After a year and a half of being champion, the federations met on one last PPV to fight for all, In the end, the XCW won the fight but both business went out and Killa J was a Free Agent. For a few months, he was in his hometown of Rota Spain training and producing more music. After Those months past by, Killa J got an offer to many smaller federations and he ended up going on a tour for all of them on try outs but he only ended up signing with a few of them. A year later he bought a federation called the IWF and ran it well for a few months, after that the corrupt fed was sold to another owner. Killa J then signed with the EWA and lasted there for a few months before getting into an argument with the Talent and ending up leaving. Later that same month, a Federation called the SWF(now called the XWE) had given him an offer, he eventually accepted it and started wrestling for them. After a year and a half, the Federation had died out in popularity and His contract was comming to an end. Last Month, the contract was expired and Killa J was out on the look for a new challenge, thats when he came accross the UBC. He has just signed with the UBC and has plans to make a huge impact to the federation and is hoping for a long and successful run.
