What this is The fed’s own Class Rankings page, as the Wayback Machine captured it on 14 February 2003 (our capture, lot 0163). The page’s navigation, images and pop-up links are not reproduced; its text is.
These are the UBC rankings according to rank A. The lower a star is, the lower down the ranking he is, which the lowest is 1 A. The higher and more talented a person is, then the higher up the ranks they will be, which the highest is 5 A. This is also affected by the amount of work put into UBC from you. If you are really good, and you give very poor effort in doing anything around UBC, expect to be around 3A or 2A.
