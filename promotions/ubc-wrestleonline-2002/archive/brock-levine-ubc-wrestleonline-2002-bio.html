Brock Levine Biography Nicknames The Regulator, The Demoniacal Sultan, The Diabolical Galant Hometown Athens, Georgia Birthday 11-03-76 Height 6'7' Weight 275 Biography Greed... Hate... Pain... Struggle... Suffer... Death... Sorrow... These words are the only words that can describe the story of the life of Levine. Born Brock Paul Levine to parents Michael and Melissa Levine on November 3rd, 1976, just outside of the Athens area in Georgia. His father Michael was an archatect and his mother was a school nurse, in a little nursery a few blocks away of Brock's house of birth. Born in the middle class style family, Brock was a very normal kid, he was completely perfect and nothing more could have been asked from him. This all would change when this little boy turned the age of 7. It was the season of snow, and Brock was out at a buddies house while his parents went Christmas shopping for him, beind an only child. The parents sadly rode a red light and crashed into another family of people, Michael and Melissa Levine were found dead within minutes time. Of course, this killed the boy Brock, he went to many foster homes because he had not relatives close to him. Brock was completely insane, he was sick and twisted and no parents could control him even at the age of 7. He raced through foster homes like no tommorow and none of the foster parents were suitable for him. This young boy never acted normal because he simply couldn't, he saw the other kids at school with a big family all proud and happy and he was just crushed. This was a young boy facing the harsh reality of mental pain. This little boy was shipped out to live with his grandmother in Minnesota where she found him on her doorstep with a woman, of social services telling her that she needed to take care of her grandson immeditally. She accepted him, being a widow was questionibly very kind and gentle with the young boy, she knew what he was going thru and to be harsh and mean to this boy would be a sin, to a christian lady who had a huge loss in her life aswell. Brock and his grandmother meshed, they loved and adored eachother for years to come, and made it thru school with flying colours. Brock was a very big kid, he joined the high school hockey team where he was talked upon by the WHML (Western Hetmisphere Hockey League) for kids playing at the age of 16 to 21, this was a step behind the pros. Brock was a very happy kid, after many years forgetting about his parents death he played for the WHML and was likely to have a spot in NHL ice. He also found another love, he got a girlfriend, her name was Brittany. The two seemed inseperable, they adored eachother and their relationship ran strong for a few years even. At this time you could expect Brock being a very enthusiastic and happy kid, a future right infront of him, a girlfriend and amazing school grades, but soon enough, took a bullet to the back when his grandmother passed away... He knew this day would come, but he never expected it now, things were going too good for him it seems, and god had a way of telling him to slow down. The note of this in Brock's head took all of his hapiness and threw it down the drain. Brock and Brittany were going strong, of course the two were not as close anymore, Brock kept away from her alot due to his grandmother's death, once he recouperated, he was going to ask her to marry him. He was heading to her house when he found his girlfriend laying with a bullet to the back of her head, another tragedy and death within this young man's life, he couldn't take it. He needed something to stimulate him, he needed to get his anger out somehow and no better place than wrestling. Fighting people was illegal so he decided to do this. Brock was hard pressed for money, he didn't have much but he managed to go thru a wrestling school and this got him votes within the indies. Making little money he survived, somehow, the owner, Joe Cappelo was a very good man, he got Brock many spots in the ring, and made sure to keep an eye on him. Brock was an impressive machine, nothing could stop him within the ring, he took all of his hate, pain, and suffer out upon his opponents and the fans loved it. Brock is a young, impressive machine, he takes everything out of himself and throws it at others, he is locked up within emmotional pain and he found away to make it all disinagrate.
