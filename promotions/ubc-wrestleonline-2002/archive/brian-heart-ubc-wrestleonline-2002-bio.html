Brian Heart Biography Nicknames lil Heart, lil kid, lil boy Hometown Brooklyn, New York Birthday March 26, 1982 Height 6'1 Weight 205 Biography Born and raised in Brooklyn, New York the young boys learned how to fight, run, and 'fly'. They were always popular with the ladies and were quick with their hands and feet when fighting also. Then they were at a gym one time when they were 15 and 10, Chris being 15 and Brian being 10 they saw a wrestling event, they always loved wrestling but never seen one personally. Then they got even more into it. They fight one day and get spotted by the Northeast Wrestling Promotor and become professional wrestlers. Then they dominated the tag team scenes. They left for more compition and prowdly looking.
