The Superstar Biography Nicknames Supes Hometown Hollywood, California Birthday March 23rd, 1978 Height 6'1' Weight 245 Fed Titles None to Date Fed Awards None to Date Biography Remember being a little kid? With that countless question, ' What do you want to be, when you grow up? '. Of course you do, we all have. Common answers were ' A Fireman, or a Cowboy' As you get older those ambitions fade, now those firemen turn into The President and A Police Officer. The glory era begins and now all the kids want to become models, actors, professional athletes. But finally, reality sets in. Not all of us can become what we want to be. We all don't grow up becoming rich and famous, half of the time we don't even like our job. But we do what we have to do to make a living. So what seperates those that become and those that continue to dream? Here is the story, of just such a man... John Burton, son of Thomas and Jessica Burton. Younger Brother to Michael and older brother to Heather Burton. Born on March 23rd of the year 1978 at approximately 11:07 at night in Beverly Hills, California. John was born with many great opportunities to him, with his parents money and social status. They could buy anything John's little heart desired. John grew up like most young boys, having a love for sports among other things. One of his fondest things growing up was going across town to his Uncle's house and watching television with him. Why would he go across town to watch television with his uncle on his small twenty inch television when he had a great entertainment set at his own house is beyond all. But with the sports buff that his Uncle was, he spent countless hours over his Uncles. John wasn't really close with his mother, and the only time he spent with his father was when he was gett scolded at, for wasting his life. Watching their favorites, Boxing, Wrestling, Kick Boxing, and of course martial arts contests. There was just something about those combat sports that had a hold on John. Even though John was into such things, he didn't partake in any of them. As it showed later down the road. As John was coming home from his Uncle's house he was confronted by a group of kids. Telling him to give them his money or pay the price. John refused, and he did just what they said.. Paid the price. As the group attacked him. Taking his money, along with his pride. After the incident, John went running straight home. He ran up to his father and explained the whole story. The only thing his father did was swipe John, and told him that he was an embarassment. After that as quick as he made it home, he went running right back to his Uncle's house and told him about everything. His Uncle just gave him a big hug, and said that he would take care of everything. John smiled and figured his Uncle was going to take care of everything like he said, but not exactly. His Uncle came back two hours later and asked John to come with him. John figured his Uncle had his stuff back and was going to get an apology from the others. They then hoped in his uncle's car and they took off onto the road. They then made it to a house in which they both approached. There was a man standing at the door, as he looked at John and just told him ' Lets Begin'. John had a confused look on his face. As they entered they followed the man until they went into the basement. John looked around to see as if this was a training camp of some sorts. The man then said ' Come at me'. John just looked at his uncle who just nodded his head. John seemed confused, as he just chased after this man who swept John as he came to him. John came up with tears in his eyes. Asking what is this all about? ' I thought you were going to take care of this!' screamed John to his Uncle. ' I did' replied his Uncle, right before he walked upstairs. John just looked at this man, who nodded to John as he approached him. For weeks, John hadn't seen a television or even his family for that matter. He was always training, living with his new Master, Kinja. But finally the day came when John was approached by the same group that mugged him before. It started off the same way, but ended totally differently thanks to Kinja. After his battle with the group he went running to Kinja and his Uncle giving them the good news. Both men asked if John was to go home and end it all. John thought about it long and wise, but John decided to stick with the martial arts. John was no longer the rich priss boy. He no longer relied on his parents for everything, he took a stand on his own. The years went on, as well as the training. John grew, in size and skill. And he became quite the handsome man. Being rather big with the ladies, John became cocky at a young age. Through the years John achieved much. Reaching the status of the black belt in martial arts, winning various trophies and tournaments that were offered to him. He finally decided that he should take it to the next level. Within weeks of planning things out John moved into the ranks of the KBL otherwise known as the Kick Boxing League. As his skill grew, so did his popularity. It grew so much, that John decided to take time off from the KBL to persue movie offers that were made to him. Starring in 3 roles of action movies, John became a superstar overnight. From the KBL to Hollywood. The fans were in an uproar for him. After movies he did talk shows, guest appearences and so on. Finally John returned back to the KBL, but he was no longer the same man. He was arrogant, cocky, and all for himself. Who no longer wanted to be called by his birth name, but after his transaction to Kick Boxer he stated he will only be known to as The Superstar. Ironically enough this did not change the fans opinion of him, needless to say that is not how the board felt. They were just waiting for The Superstar to slip up and slip up he did. Some incriminating evidence of prostitution and gambling came up on The Superstar. And the board gave him the decision, leave KBL or face the consequences. So The Superstar did the smart reliable thing, and he left the Kick Boxing League. Now out of a job, The Superstar was not sure just what he should do. With Kick Boxing done, should he go back to being a full time actor? He went back to his roots, remembering sitting down with his Uncle each day watching television. Finally it seemed so clear to The Superstar, but would this new transversion make Superstar realize the errors of his way? ... Probably not.
