'Another day of Insanity' Pt 5

2 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12-02-02 Brett Wayne has lasted one day in the Nut house where is brother sent him in high hopes that he would be cured of his insanity but little did he know. Brett Wayne seems like he doesn't want to be cured of his Insanity but why? No one really understands Brett, one minute he is fine and then the next minute he flips out, like he has split personality's. The main question is, can Brett really survive 'Another day of Insanity.' The scene picks up inside the padded room with only four walls for Brett to look at. Brett is seen using the wall as a punching bag. Brett then turns and looks at the camera at the top corner of the wall as everyone watch's him from the main control room. 'You guys like watching this' Brett says looking up at the camera. Brett looks like his has lost it even more than before he arrived at the Nut house. He shakes constantly as he continues to speak. All of you are 'Pathetic' getting a thrill of watching me slip further and further each minute I'm in here. You all think this is just a game for me, well your all wrong. This isn't just a game for me to go stroke some heads, this is my life, this is what I do for my living and it fits me perfect. I have no regrets in joining the UBC, I was born to do this and now I have people trying to prevent me from being a UBC fighter by locking me up in this room. I'm going to give you all watching one word of advice if you step one foot inside 'My room of Insanity' then you'll be laying on the ground. The scene goes to the Main control room as everyone watch's Brett continue to speak, suddenly a voice comes from outside the room. What the hell are you guys doing to my Brother. Mike then appears at the door way, everyone stunned and don't know how to reply to the smaller version of Brett Wayne. 'You guys get a kick out of watching my Brother flip out. Well I brought him to this place to get better not to get worse so I SUGGEST YOU DO YOUR GOD DAMN JOB!' Mike becomes extremely pissed and starts throwing moniters off there stands. You will not watch my brother anymore! Mike continues thrashing the place as the gaurds just stand there. 'Now take me to that fucking room' Mike shouts as he points them to lead the way. That start walking down the halls of the Nut House as Mike begins looking around at all the old people. What the hell was I thinking bringing my brother to a place like this? Mike ask himself. They make it to the door and Mike hears Brett still talking to the camera. I'LL KILL EVERYONE ONE OF YOU MOTHERFUCKERS! Mike sits there motionless for a minute listening to Brett shout at the camera. 'Ok open the door now.' The guy just stands there not listening to Mike. 'Did you not hear what I just said open the damn door.' Brett suddenly stops talking after hearing mikes voice. 'MIKE, is that you!' Brett shouts. Mike shoves the gaurd out of the way and opens the door. When the door is completely open Mike looks up and see's Brett right there in his face. The gaurds take off running as Brett laughs. 'There pathetic' Brett says laughing. Mike sits there thinking to himself 'Now what have I done, he's simply more Insane then when I first put him in here. God please help us' Mike then looks up at Brett and says 'Get your things packed we are leaving this place' 'I'm not leaving this place now' Brett says. Mike looks up at him in shock. 'What, you actually want to stay here!?!? Forget it your going back to house with me so I said get your damn things packed right now' Brett looks down to the ground and walks off, Mike follows him and they reach Brett's room where Gramps lays in the bed smiling as Brett walks into the room. 'Hey gramps' Brett says as Gramps jumps up out of bed and looks at Brett then looks over at Mike, Mike shrugs his shoulders as Gramps looks back over at Brett. 'One day, very impressive. It usually takes one week for people to come out of there cured' Gramps says scratching his head. 'Oh let me correct you on that, I'm not cured. You can't cure what is impossible to cure, thats a fact. I'm just 'Simply Insane' and thats all there is to it.' Brett says as he begins packing his bags. 'Where are you going, it isn't time for you to leave' says Gramps as he walks over to Brett pulling his hand away from his bag. Mike suddenly walks over and pulls Gramps back, Brett then goes back to packing his bags. 'He is leaving today, this place is only making him worse' Mike says keeping Gramps away from Brett. Brett turns and looks at gramps 'I just want to thank you for helping me out Gramps.' Gramps stands there speachless and confused. 'Ok...I'm finished' Brett says as he walks half way to the door then he stops and looks at Gramps. 'Thanks old man' Gramps stands there still speachless and motionless and watchs Brett walk out of the room as Mike follows. They make it to where the control room is and Brett see's the place is trashed and just smiles as he walks past it. They make it outside and The moment the sun hits Bretts eyes and covers them up. 'Damn, I almost forgot what the outside world was like' Brett says getting in the car. 'You were only in there for a couple of days, how could you forget' Mike ask getting in the car. 'When your in that room, you don't think about the outside world, you only think what is on your mind and Johnny Nightmare was on my mind. Thats all I thought about and come Saturday everyone will know what true power is all about. Mike turns and looks at Brett as he starts the car and smiles. 'Oh and I though I should tell you things have changed in the UBC, some roster changes. People are getting fired left and right because there not doing there job and I'm sure saturday they will announce more to be fired so I suggest you keep working hard so you don't get fired.' Mike says as he drives off. Brett smiles and looks at the scenery, then turns and looks at Mike. 'I'll keep up the hard work Brother, I will be the best known fighter there is before its all said and done. Just watch and See. We will be famous.' Brett says as he leans back his seat and closes his eyes. After a few hours of riding around, they pull up to there house, mike then shakes Brett a little bit telling him to wakeup. Brett opens his eyes and see's where they are at. 'We're home, Finally I can go get in my nice soft bed' Brett says getting out of the car as he grabs his bags. Mike walks up and unlocks the door and walks in as Brett follows. Brett gets inside and drops his bags and looks around. 'I am going to go get me some good sleep now that I'm home' Brett says as he walks to his bed room and closes the door behind him. 'I guess I'll get some sleep also.' Mike says walking to his bed room and closes the door behind him. -Fade 2 Black-
