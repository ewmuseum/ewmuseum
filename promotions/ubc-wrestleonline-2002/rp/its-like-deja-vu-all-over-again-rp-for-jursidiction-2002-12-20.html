Its like deja vu all over again... (rp for Jursidiction)

20 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12-20-02 {Amir Mubootu and Malik Tehani are sitting in them Mississippi home that their boss has bought for them to live in while they are in UBC doing his business. Both men are sitting on the porch in their chairs when a commerical of the next UBC show comes on with a quick run down of who is on the card. Amir looks at Malik and just shakes his head as the commerical goes off.} Amir Mubootu: Didn't we just face Dangerous Revolution? Malik Tehani: Yep. Amir Mubootu: And didn't we just mop the floor with them? Malik Tehani: Yep. Amir Mubootu: Then why are we facing them again? Malik Tehani: That's an easy one. From what I hear those cry babies whined to the powers that be and got themselves a rematch. I don't understand it myself but hey if they want more of the same this week that's more than fine with me. I mean we need another easy wind don't we? Amir Mubootu: I don't know about you Malik but I didn't come to UBC for easy wins. Malik Tehani: Yeah you came to UBC cause the Boss paid you to. Amir Mubootu: Well, that's right but beside that I came here to prove myself. I mean once this business deal is done I want to explore the world of singles fights. Malik Tehani: Whoa there big guy. I thought we where in this together for the long run. You make it sound like you don't enjoy our team. Is that the true? Amir Mubootu: No, no that ain't it I just want to prove my talents is all. Malik Tehani: Welll if that's the case why not set up some singles matches in the coming weeks. I mean I wouldn't really mind as long as it didn't interfere with The Boss' business of course. Amir Mubootu: Of course it won't. Malik Tehani: Well that's good to hear. So do you think we need to teach Dangerous Revolution a lesson? Maybe break a bone or two? Amir Mubootu: Hmmm, sounds interesting. Maybe then Sean and Steve will understand when it comes to team fighting there is nobody that can hang with us, nobody. {Amir and Malik both look at each other and get big grins on their faces. Just then the phone rings and Amir picks it up. Its their boss.} Amir Mubootu: Yes sir we are leaving for Denver in about six hours and everything is under control to sir. With this rematch we will show UBC that The Kingz of Africa are and will be a force to reckon with. Everything is coming along to for your confrontation with Chris Carter but his buddies are always around what shall we do about that?..... Understand sir, by any means necessary, I understand.... Good bye sir. {Amir hangs up the phone but Malik just stares at him for a moment and shrugs his shoulders. It seems Malik wanted to talk to him for a moment.} Malik Tehani: Hey man I wanted to talk to the boss for a moment. Amir Mubootu: You know how he gets sometimes its always business with him. Besides he was on the phone in his plane and the connection was breaking up. We need to get started packing we only got a little while to go before we need to get to the airport. Malik Tehani: Yeah Denver should be great this time of year. Tons of snow, sub zero temperatures, frostbite I can't wait! Amir Mubootu: Malik you need to great the good side out of this trip. We take on step closer to the boss' goal and one step closer to becoming the people in UBC will fear. {With that Amir and Malik get up and go inside their home to start packing for their trip to Denver Colorado.}
