Meeting Up With An Old Friend (RP #1 for Jurisdiction)

18 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12/18/02 [The scene opens up in an underground fighter’s training arena in Detroit, Michigan. The camera cuts straight to Violent B, who’s wearing nothing but a pair of workout shorts. He has tape on his fists, and on his feet. He’s working on a punching bag. He’s punching the bag and beating it with a combination of punches and kicks. Violent B then hit’s a hard hitting punch-kick combo. He then bends over and uppercuts the bag, making the bag fly to the other end of the gym. The bag explodes and dust flies everywhere. Violent B just shrugs and wipes off the dust on his shoulder. He then puts on that cocky smile of his. He turns and walks over to the workout ring. All the men in his way move out of his way, as he walks into the ring. There are two people sparring in the ring. Violent B walks up and puts his arms in between the two men. Then they stop and look at him, he punches both of the in the face, knocking them out.] Violent B - “You guys call this a workout. Come on! If I can just walk into the ring and punch you, knocking you out, then you don’t deserver to be here. Come on!” [Violent B then kicks the men out of the ring.] Violent B - “Does anyone wanna step in the ring with me!?” [Violent B puts his arms out and gets ready for a challenge. All the people in the gym just look at him, and no one steps into the ring.] Violent B - “Come on! ANYONE!? Where’s the challenge!?” [No one replies to Violent B’s challenge.] Violent B - “You guys are a buncha pussies!” [Violent B then steps out of the ring. He walks into the locker room. He opens up his locker and pulls out his clothing. He puts his baggy black jeans over his shorts, and puts his black t-shirt on. He then grabs his spikey necklace and puts it on. He then heads for the door. As soon as he gets to the door, a man walks up to him. The man is about 6’2”, and he looks like he’s mad.] Man - “You the one who called everyone in here a buncha pussies!?” [Violent B once again puts on his cocky smile.] Violent B - “Damn right that was me! Why, what are you gonna do about it?” [The man looks somewhat familiar. Violent B then realizes who it is.] Violent B - “Snake? Is that you?” [The man puts a smile on his face. He has short black hair, and a small beard. He has a leather jacket on, and some black leather pants on. His almost black eyes glare in the light.] Snake - “Didn’t think you’d recognize me with my short hair. But I guess you haven’t lost your mind, from all the fights you’re in. So how have you been B?” [For once, Violent B has a normal smile on his face.] Violent B - “I’ve been pretty good. How about you? I haven’t seen you since I was in the “SHOOTERS” which is about 3 years. I like the haircut. So how have you been?” Snake - “I’ve been pretty good. I like you’re haircut too. I remember you’re long hair. It didn’t look too good. I like what you have now though.” Violent B - “Thanks. So have you toughened up yet?” [Snake puts a smile on his face.] Snake - “I should ask you the same thing. The last time you and me tried to fight each other, you beat me, but barely. I’ve toughened up a lot since then.” [Violent B gets his cocky smile back.] Violent B - “Yeah, well so have I. Know what, I think we should step into the ring and have a little fight. What do you say?” [Snake gets a cocky smile that looks almost like Violent B’s.] Snake - “Why the hell not? It’s not like I have anything better to do. Let’s go.” [Snake and Violent B then make their way to the ring. Violent B takes off his shirt and his necklace. Snake takes off his jacket, revealing a black wife beater. His arms are covered with tattoos of snakes running up and down them. Snake steps into the ring, and so does Violent B. They begin to warm up in the corners. After the warm up, they step into the middle of the ring. They stare at each other. They both have the cocky smiles on their faces. They both put their arms out and punch each others’ hands.] Snake - “This is gonna be good. I can tell you’re a lot stronger than before. I can’t wait to see how you’ve improved since the last time we faced each other when we were in the “SHOOTERS”.” Violent B - “I can see that you’ve been working out more. I like the tattoos. I can’t wait to face you again. It’ll be like old times, when you can me used to train together in hopes of beating each other. This is gonna be good.” [The scene fades with the two about to fight.] TO BE CONTINUED….
