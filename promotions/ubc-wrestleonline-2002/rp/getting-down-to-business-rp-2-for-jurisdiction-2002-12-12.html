Getting down to business...( RP #2 for Jurisdiction)

12 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12-12-02 {Malik Tehani and Amir Mubootu are in their hotel sweet which is just minutes away from The Pit, the site of this week's UBC show. Both are pacing back and forth in the room waiting for the man who paid them to come to UBC and wreak havoc. Just then there is a knock on the door, both men look at each other before heading to the door. As Amir reaches the door there is a young man standing their with a letter. Amir takes it and hands the young man a fifty dollar bill for his troubles. He opens the letter and scans over it before shaking his head and handing it to Malik. He reads it and crumples it up before lighting it on fire and throwing it into the garbage. } Malik: So what's the plan now, I thought he was gonna be here this week. Amir: Look Malik you know as well as I do that he operates on his own schedule and it isn't up to try and figure out why he does what he does. We are just here to do his bidding. Besides this week it looks like we got big plans. We got our first match against Dangerous Revolution, we got big plans for the future, I mean how can it get any better? Malik: That's one thing I always liked about you, you always look at the positive of things. But I don't think all this is a positive, I mean think about it if all goes well we will be making some enemies at The Pit that will probably follow us our UBC career. Are we really ready for that? Its one thing to do one man's bidding when he's here to back us up but we don't know when he will be showing up so until that time its us against the world and the last time I looked UBC has a nice size roster, and our team is just you and I. Amir: And the boss, don't forget the boss. {Just then the phone rings in their room and both of them are startled for a moment. Amir picks up the phone.} Amir: Hello? Um, yes sir both of us are here, we just thought you would be here too.... Um I understand, hold on one-second. He wants me to switch over to speakerphone so he can talk to both of us. Okay sir you are on speakerphone. The Boss: Okay fellas sorry I couldn't make it to New Mexico but I am in Singapore looking to add some more talent. I am in talks with several pit fighters right now and this is something I couldn't break away from. Amir: Um, sir I was just wondering why you need more muscle? The Boss: Amir I understand your concerns but you have to understand that I always have a backup plan just in case you and Malik don't get the job done. Malik: You doubt us sir? The Boss: Its not that I doubt you but, I don't want anything to go wrong, and just in case it does... Well, let's just hope everything works right. Now I have no doubt both of you will go to great heights in UBC but right now it ain't about what I think you two can do, its what I am paying you to do. I hired you two to destroy a former rival of mine and anybody who stands by his side. I hired to you clear my path in UBC so when I do decide to arrive I will have nothing standing in my way to the championship. Malik: So basically we are here to do your dirty work is that right? The Boss: Basically Malik yes. Now I understand the way you two might be feeling thinking that I am just using you but that's not the case, I see great potential in you and believe both of you have what it takes to be greater than either of you have ever imagined, but don't get confused you two work for me until the job is done after that you can do whatever you feel is right for you. Now let's get down to business. Amir: Excuse me boss, but I got some questions for ya if you don't mind. The Boss: Shoot Amir I am all ears. Amir: Okay boss I am all for doing is needed to be done, but what type of guarantee do we have that we won't feel the repercussions of what we are about to do. I mean we are about to make a lot of enemies. The Boss: Amir I am sure you are gonna be able to think this one out. If you make enemies don't you think you can make friends just as fast? I mean you two have great charisma so go out there and get some allies! Amir: Can't you just buy us some? The Boss: Amir do you like the money I pay you? Amir: Well of course I do! The Boss: Well then I suggest you stop asking me questions that are of no concern to me. You two have been paid so do what I am paying you for! After your match I need for you two to make a great, great statement and trust me when I show up then people will understand why you two are there. Malik: You are coming this week? The Boss: Yes it was all in the letter I had delivered to you, you haven't received it yet? Malik: Um, yeah we received it but, there was a little problem with it. The Boss: What's the problem? Malik: Um, um Amir burned it. The Boss: Amir you are wearing my patience thin. I am paying you to take care of the fine things not to fly off the handle and act without thinking. Don't make that mistake again. {Malik looks over at Amir and just shakes his head.} Amir: Sorry boss, I won't make that mistake again. The Boss: Let's not hope so. Now I get to get back to my meeting and I will meet you two at The Pit Saturday night, let's pray for the impact I want to make. {With that The Boss hangs up the phone and Amir and Malik just stare at each other.}
