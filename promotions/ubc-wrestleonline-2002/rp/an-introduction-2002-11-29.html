An Introduction

29 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11.29.02 Introduction: America is a very interesting place, to say the least. Only in America can you find pimps with feathered hats who can get away with driving hoes around in their '84 Buicks while wearing a plumb-color suit. Only in America can the mass majority of people suffer through money problems and every day issues as ten percent of the nation drives a Mercedes and gets away with their selfish habbits. In America, when children out-grow positive behaviors and grow into something worse (usually influenced by their peers), their parents begin to get angry and look around for someone to point their fingers at. Often times, the people who get fingers pointed at them are either Rappers, Rock Stars, or Celebrities. The sad thing is that the parents never stop and point the finger at themselves, god forbid they take responsibility for their own actions, right? Follow through in this journey with Da Thug and witness the reality that some people have ot live with every day... The date is Monday, November 11th, Da Thug is asleep as it's nearing eight in the morning. The scene opens up in a pitch black, dark room. Morning's sunlight begins to enter through a pair of dirty, beaten down blinds. The chirping of birds can't be heard, the sound of children playing isn't ringing in the air, and it damn sure doesn't smell like flowers and fresh water. This is Compton, California. This is the hometown of professional wrestler, Da Thug. The light overgrows the blinds in power and breaks through, fully, into the room. The light shines directly in Da Thug's eyes, causing him to enter the beginning stages of waking up. Thug's eyes begin to roughly blink as his tired body begins moving around, appearing as if life has just been recovered. At last, Da Thug lets out a grunt of awakening as he feels the epiphany of a hang over, due to last night's outrageous partying and drinking. Da Thug manages to get out of his brokedown bed and walk across the hard wood floor to open the blinds even more. The smoke stained, yellow blinds lifts to the ceiling and the rest of the sunlight rushes into the room, revealing all of the disfigurements and problems with the room and it's contents. Sitting back down on his bed, Da Thug reaches for his TV remote. To no surprise, the remote batteries are dead. After letting out a sigh of frustration, Thug gets up and tiringly walks over to his TV and turns it on. The TV screen flashes to life, Thug turns the channel to five, where the 8 o'clock news is on. Tracy Levstied(News Reporter)-'In a deadly shoot out earlier this morning, three teenagers were arrested for the murder of a forty year old woman. The motive is not yet clear, but police and investigators are working on the case, as we speak. Now, let's go to Russ Devensburg who is out on the streets right now getting peoples' general reaction to the event.' Russ-'Well, Tracy, I myself, am not too shocked about the whole thing. I mean, think about it, we report this type of thing at least five times a day. What makes this one so different from all the rest, you ask? Well, it's simple. When it involved juveniles, the issue tends to be much bigger. Yet in this case, the shooting is blamed on hometown, legendary Rap Artist, Dr. Dre.' Da Thug, sick of watching already, throws his shorts at the TV screen after removing them. He gets up and walks over to his closed, where he then puts on a pair of jeans and a new white 'wife beater'. The old, worn down closet doors squeak to a shut as Da Thug walks into the next room of his apartment. The camera cuts to the bathroom mirror, where Da Thug walks in the room and ducks down so that his face is near the fosset of the sink. He turns the rusting knobs to a comfortable setting and a small, but fair amount of water begins leaking out of the sink and into Da Thug's cupped hands. Thug gently closes his eyes and raises the water to his face, in attempt to clean up and get rid of his remaining hang over. Satisfied, Thug leaves turns the water off and leaves the room. Squeaking is heard by the rotting stairs as Da Thug travels down them, still not feeling one hundred percent. Hungry, Da Thug walks into his kitchen and opens up his fridge to find nothing but a half empty bottle of 80-proof Vodka, and an old peice of steak. Without even bothering to think about it, Da Thug takes the Vodka and previously cooked steak into his living room and then sits down on his couch. Noticable holes and 'cig' burns lie on the couch's surface. Da Thug turns his TV on to MTV, just to find some wannabe Rapper trying to make it, yet will only have one good hit before the mere end of his pathetic career. Just then, Da Thug's cell phone rings... So he picks up. Da Thug-'Wussup, this is Thug...' Jerry (Da Thug's Agent)-'Hey, Thug, it's me. Jerry.' Da Thug-'oh, wus' crackin'?' Jerry-'Not much at the moment, just thought I'd call on you and check how you're doing. Afterall, that is my job, isn't it?' Da Thug-'Yeah, I guess so... I'm doin' aight, kinda hungry and I need some cash cause I'm runnin' low on food and Vod---, nevermind.' Jerry-'Thug, you're still drinking aren't you?... Ya know, man, you really need to stop this nonsense. You could be huge! You could make it big time, but you are so damn foolish. Instead of going out and looking for a promotion to attempt to sign with, you sit at home and drink your life away like you have no cares in the world. May I ask, what the hell is wrong with you? Is that brain of your's functioning properly, or has all that alcohol finally taken it's toll on you?' Da Thug-'Jerry, chill out! Like I told you before, I've been lookin' for a damn job but I can't find one ANYWHERE... I've looked up and down, high and low, but it's jus' out of my reach or somethin'. And don't start lecturin' me 'bout how drinkin' is bad and all that other bullshit, I know what I'm doin'. Be real, Jerry, this is my reality and this is all I have. I ain't into wearin' suits like you, I can't afford tons of limos and cars like you, and I can't get away wit' counceling other people because I have problems of my own.' Jerry-'Thug, that's besides the fact. Okay, so you drink... There is nothing I can do about that except tell you how it's ruining your life, slowly but surely. I'm only supposed to be helping your profession-wise, and hell, I'm even failing to do that the right way. Cause look, if I was doing my job properly, you would be working somewhere right now... And you're obviously not.' Da Thug-'Aight then, Jerry, I'ma make a deal wit' you. If you can manage to get me a job, I'll quit drinkin', or at least I'll try to quit. But otherwise, this is my life and I'ma live it the best and easiest I can. Shiiit, pretty soon I'll be out hustlin' the streets to get money so that I can pump some food in my stomach. That's startin' to seem like the only way out, nowadays.' Jerry-'Okay, at least you're giving a chance with some effort, instead of just giving up and going to the streets again. I can't guaruntee anything, but I will promise you, I will try my absolute hardest to get you into a top notch free agents list. In the meantime, why don't you start hitting the gym every day. I'll call and reserve you some appointments for noon every weekday at that gym down the street from your house. Okay?' Da Thug-'Aight, thanks for helpin' me, Jerry. I really appreciate this, dogg... I mean it.' Jerry-'No problem, that's what I'm here for. You and I go way back, you can count on being able to trust me by now, Thug. But I'll let you go, I'll call you if anything comes up. See ya.' Da Thug-'Aight then, peace.' Da Thug hangs up the phone, with ease. He then lifts the bottle of Vodka to his mouth after finishing the peice of steak. His attention is directed back to the TV set, he changes the channel from MTV to 'Prime Time TV', where a review of the current PCW World Champion is going on. Da Thug-'Ole' burnt ass muthafucka, get this shit off my TV.' Da Thug carelessly switches the channel. After realizing that nothing interesting is on TV, Da Thug gets up and grabs his cell phone while heading out his front door. Thug opens it and sits on his front step after dialing a number. Outside, it looks as if rain is in the forcast. Clouds begin hovering closer and closer over the city of Compton as the Pacific breeze lightly floats past. Da Thug proceeds in holding the cell phone to his ear... J.J. (Other Line, Thug's Best Friend)-'Yo?' Da Thug-'Wussup playa? It's Thug.' J.J.-'Ah, sup Thuggy? Nuttin' much over here, I'm jus' tryin' to figure out what to do tonight. I ain't tryin' to be home alone tonight, I got the urge to party like whoa.' Da Thug-'I feel ya, I feel ya. Man, it's only eleven in da mornin' and I'm already bored out my mind. Ever since Big Money Wrestling 'temporarely closed', I been out of work and it's startin' to annoy the shit out of me. My agent is tryin' to tell me he's lookin' for anotha place for me to work, but I'll believe it when I see it. Anyways, I'm 'bout to bounce on over to the gym, you wanna come wit' me?' J.J.-'Oh fo sho! I'll go anywhere, haha, shiiit. Oh, by the way... You gotta any food at yo crib?' Da Thug-'What kinda question is that? Fa real, you know me. When was the last time I had more than twenty five dollars?... Be Real.' J.J.-'Haha, true, I musta forgot who I was talkin' to. I mean, Thug... Having food at his crib?! Yeah right! But naw, I'm jus' fuckin' wit' you. So you gonna come pick me up or do I gotta walk?' Da Thug-'What do you think?' J.J.-'Uhhh... Beats me, why you gotta play like that? Jus' be straight up and tell me, damn, fool.' Da Thug-'Man, c'mon, you know I'm only playin'. I'll be over in about five minutes, you better be waitin' outside cause this ain't no damn taxi service. Aight?' J.J.-'Aight then, sounds coo wit' me. Holla.' Da Thug-'Aight, Peace.' With that said, Da Thug hangs up and places his cell phone in his right pocket. He walks back into his living room, throws on a black 'doo-rag' and grabs his car keys. Walking from his living room to the parking lot, Da Thug jumps in his car and takes off with his gym bag in hand. Scene Fades... The scene opens back up in front of another apartment, still located in Compton. Da Thug's friend, J.J., approaches the car and opens the door. Stepping in the passenger side, he slaps Da Thug a five. They begin driving to the gym... A few moments of silence intakes Da Thug and J.J., until Da Thug notices a large purple pelt on J.J.'s left eye. Da Thug-'Oooh, shit, what happened to you?' J.J.-'What chu talkin' 'bout?' Da Thug-'Don't try and play it off, haha, you know damn well you got a huge ass bruise on your eye... Who stuck you? Haha.' J.J.-'Man... Why do you gotta bring this up? Fa real. You coulda jus' left it alone... And if it matters, I got mah ass jumped the other day. No big thang.' Da Thug-'No big thang?! You're a lil' sucka, that's what you are. Gettin' jumped and callin' it no big deal, pfft! That's pretty fucked up, J.J.' J.J.-'Well what am I supposed to do 'bout it? Carry a strap? See, I'm tryin' to get out of this lifestyle, not back up in it even more. That's the thing that you're failin' to realize, dogg.' Da Thug-'Aay, I ain't failin' to realize shit. You think I don't know how it is to get jumped and not be able to do anything 'bout it? Please! I'm white, don't forget that. I grew up in Compton, don't even try to act like I don't know how that goes. That's been my life since I was a lil' kid, I'm jus' lucky cause I don't let it happen anymore. When you're the only white boy in the city, you get real messed up every day... You don't jus' get jumped, you get punked the hell out of.' J.J.-'That seems too fake to me, how do you jus' not let it happen? From what I know, it's gonna happen to yo ass whether you think you can or can't help it. So tell me... Some G rolls up on you and points a strap in ya face, and he's supposed to drop it jus' because you claim you won't let it happen?... Naw, haha, that's fucked up.' Da Thug-'You know what? I'ma drop this right now before you get me pissed off at you. I know I'm right, and you don't like what I have to say, so jus' drop it. Leave it as what it is.' J.J.-'Aight...' Da Thug takes a right and pulls into a parking lot that belongs to the gym. The worn down tires screech to a stop as Da Thug and J.J. step out of the car and begin walking to the front entrance of the gym. They enter through the front door to find an extremely large gym, containing various peices of work out equipment. Da Thug and J.J. walk over to the registry counter... Da Thug-'Oh wussup man?!' Big John (Gym Owner)-'Haven't seen you forever! Where have ya been lately?' Da Thug-'Everywhere, man. My life has hit rock bottom and it's time for me to bring it back up to the level it used to be at.' Big John-'Oh really? I was wondering where you went to. You used to be here each and every single day, and then all of a sudden, you just stopped coming one day... Things have changed so much. All the guys you use to work out with are gone.' Da Thug-'What do you mean, gone?' Big John-'Well, one day, the SWAT broke the door in and bombarded everyone in the place. They never gave a valid reason, they just locked em' up and took off... Just like that. It was awkward, I still have no clue what it was about, still to this very day.' Da Thug-'Whoa, that's crazy... But anyways, I'ma be here every day from now on. I brought my friend, J.J., wit' me today... That's aight isn't it?' Big John-'Oh, no problem! Don't bother paying, you're a regular around here.' Da Thug-'Damn! Thanks...' Big John-'Oh, don't mention it.' J.J. and Da Thug continue to walk over into the main section of the massive gym. Iron is pumping everywhere as various machinary is being put to use by many people. The sleek white walls look like they have recently been painted, and the shinny floor looks like it's been buffed beyond recognition. J.J. walks over to a tricept machine next to Da Thug, who is bench pressing. Warming up, they begin talking. J.J.-'So, exactly how hard was is growing up in Compton with white skin?' Da Thug-'Ya know, you ask the oddest questions sometimes, haha... Anyways, it was twice as hard for me as it was for you. Because unlike you, I didn't fit in. I couldn't jus' join a gang because I was a white boy. I couldn't ask a girl out wit' her keeping a straight face til' I had finally earned my respect. It was the hardest thing I've ever experienced... But now that I look back on it, I wouldn't have it any other way. This is me, this is my history, and if it was any different, I wouldn't be the violent-minded prick that I am today. But havin' that problem, it created much more problems in my life. Like my alcohol addiction, my love for Vodka is like your love for chicken, haha. And another thing I don't regret about it, is the disease I got.' J.J.-'Disease? What you got AIDS or some shit? Ugg, playa, don't touch me no more! Haha.' Da Thug-'Naw, it ain't even like that. It's called Chronic Violence Syndrome... The doctors say it's a chemical imbalance that people get from only the most hectice lifes, which fits my spazzin' ass perfectly, if you think 'bout it. Ever since I was a lil' fuckin' kid, I been havin' these VIOLENT ASS thoughts flowin' through my head. It's not your average type of thoughts, I'm talkin' 'bout thoughts so sick that it makes the word 'mean' look nice... For example, have you ever thought about rapping your ex wit' a knife, choppin' her head off wit' the same knife... Than beatin' her coarpse wit' the head and stuffin' it in the trunk, and finally drivin' off like you're in some Brady Bunch movie?' Silence brushes over J.J. as the sickening thoughts of what Thug just said set picture into his mind. J.J.-'Thug...' Da Thug-'What?' J.J.-'You're nasty as hell! I always knew you had some type of thang for violence n' shit... But that's jus' out of proportion, right there.' Da Thug-'Dogg, I already been havin' councelin' for this shit... I know I have a problem, and I have been tryin' to take care of it. But sometimes it comes in handy, it gives me the balls to do whatever I need to... And I like it. Nuttin' make me feel better than stickin' someone in the face wit' a metal object.' Just as Da Thug is expressing himself, a police car parks itself outside on the front curb. A police officer walks into the gym and heads directly toward Da Thug. Da Thug and J.J. both stop working out, 'fore they know what the near future holds for one of the two... If not both. J.J.-'What da fuck did you do this time, Thug?' Da Thug-'Shiiit, I haven't done anything wrong in a long time. What 'bout you?' J.J.-Besides not paying Child Support and stealin' my neighbor's cable, I'm innocent.' The police officer walks closer and then approaches Da Thug with a pair of handcuffs. Officer-'Chris [name withheld], you're under arrest for the influenced murder of a young man earlier last week...' J.J. gets upset and the officer warns him to back off as the handcuffs are done being placed on Thug. The police officer begins to escort Da Thug out of the building. Da Thug-'J.J.! Call Jerry and tell him I need a bail.' On Thug's way out, Da Thug turns and looks the officer in the eyes and gets a sick grin on his face. The officer looks at him in confusion, unsure of the criminalistic thoughts that could be swirling through Da Thug's screwed up head... Scene fades as Da Thug begins laughing histerically. Outroduction: As proven in the footage you just witnessed, the wrong people are often wrongly accused of crimes they didn't commit... Or did they? Regardless, Da Thug is going to jail, but J.J. is attempting to get bail money from Jerry (Thug's agent). A question comes to mind... Did Da Thug do it?... Knowing Da Thug, something extremely sick had to have been going through his mind at the time of his arrest. You're soon to find out what the mind of a violent killer looks like... Look no further than the mind of Chris [name withheld], Da Thug...
