'Oops!' Part Uno

29 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11-29-02 -Johnny NightMare is walking back and forth. He walks through the doorways but being to busy on the phone he slams his head into a pole. He drops to the ground and gets up slow and looks into the camera a bit confused. He then remembers something and then speaks.- Johnny NightMare: God bless the midgets. Hangliding? Not funny. A midget hangliding? Funny! Playing bumper cars? Fun. Midgets in bumper cars? Fun for the whole family! A drunk falling down? Whatever. A drunk midget falling down? Where’s my video camera?! Porn? Entertaining. Midget porn? A party waiting to happen! Still not convinced? Would the Oompa Lumpas have been nearly as creepy-cool if they were full-grown men? Hell, no. Would anyone have watched 'Willow' if the title character wasn’t a midget? Never. Who would have sung to Dorothy on her start down the yellow-brick road? A regular group of singers? I think not. Who would point out 'de plane, boss, de plane?' Some shmuck in a tux? Of course not. I have no idea where my fascination for midgets stems from but I know, thanks to conversations with the other staff member and countless other conversations with other people, I’m not the only one. What I do know is that when I see a midget I just want to grab it and put it front of a funhouse mirror so I could see what they would look like if they were normal. Some midgets are more memorable than others but no one can deny the fact that they are intrigued by midgets. All normal people have tried picturing the mechanics of midgets having sex with a normal sized person at one time or another. Breast feeding? Annoying at a restaurant. A midget breast feeding? Priceless! Checking yourself for breast or testicular cancer? A health precaution done once a month. Having a midget doctor check you for breast or testicular cancer? A story for the grandkids! The world needs more midgets. I really mean it. I’m not trying to mock them. We need more midgets the same way I believe we need more jolly fat people. I can’t help but get all warm whenever I see a midget or a jolly fat person. Regular guy with a body covered in thick, black hair? Icky. Midget with a body covered in thick, black hair? Side show attraction! Beyond the Oompa Lumpas and the Popeye-wannabes the Lollipop Guild, midgets have shaped out lives in other ways. What’s a happy thought? Santa’s Workshop? Elves are almost always played by midgets. Who saved Obi Wan from being blown away before landing on Tattooine in Episode One? R2-D2. Who originally played R2-D2? A midget! And what about Mini-me?! Did you know midgets can give birth to full-sized people? It’s true! Wrap your mind around that physics issue! In conclusion: Like beer, sex, drugs, rock & roll, cigarettes, daytime TV, 'OZ' on HBO, and cheerleading competitions on ESPN2 life is more fun with midgets! Raymond Mercy walks from behind the camera and looks at Johnny. He checks his pupils and gives Johnny some smelling salt. Johnny grabs it from Mercy and eats it. Mercy takes a step back and then he whispers some stuff to Johnny and then Johnny nods. Mercy walks back to behind the camera. Johnny NightMare: Yeah thats right I am cool as hell. Ha...Oompa Lumpas are cool. Those little fellows dancing and singing, its like broadway. Did you know that Umpalumpas come from Lumpaland, but Willy Wonka secretly transported them to live and work safely in his factory. Lumpaland had creatures called Horn Swagglers, Rotten Vermicious Kinids, and Swangdoodles that would eat 10 Umpas for breakfast and think nothing of it. That is amazing. Raymond Mercy(from behind the camera): What the hell happen to this guy, he is a damn accident. Wait...thats it Johnny NightMare is an accident. 'The Accident' Johnny NightMare. Bianca come here. The camera turns as Bianca approaches Raymond Mercy. Raymond Mercy: Bianca, Johnny has hit his head a little too much. So you need to watch him close and make sure he focuses on his match whether you make it sound like a comic book, whatever just make sure you do something to make him talk about it ok? Bianca: Sure. Raymond Mercy: Ok your flight for Las Cruses leaves in about a hour so make sure he talks about his match during sometime during that flight or right now. I have to leave. Raymond Mercy leaves the scene which is now confirmed as an airport. Bianca: Johnny you have to talk about those bad guys. Bless and Wayne. Johnny NIghtMare: I don't want to. Bianca: But if you don't then you are going to get the shit beat out of you and maybe even fired. Do you want that to happen. Johnny NightMare: No... Bianca: Ok then you better talk about those bad guys. Johnny NightMare: But Bless looks scary. Bianca: Thats because he is ugly not scary. Johnny NightMare: Like the wicked witch of the west. Bianca: Sure... ok Bless is ugly like the wicked witch and what about Wayne. Johnny NightMare: Brett Wayne is nothing but a mere amobea on my shoes. Bianca: Johnny what was that. Johnny NightMare: Why Bia, that was me talking yet again. About my opponent on Jurisdiction. And that Bobby Bless is nothing more then a mere moron, he hasn't spoken once because he cowers at the name Johnny NightMare. Bianca: Johnny talk about Bless again. Johnny NightMare: He scares me like the wicked witch of the west. Bianca: What about Wayne? Johnny NightMare: He is ugly like a mean monkey. Bianca: Johnny... when we get to Las Cruses you are getting checked on by the trainers. Maybe even a professional. Now come on we have to go on our flight. --Johnny NightMare grabs Bianca's out held hand and Johnny starts to pull away like a little child. They go to a gate and wait in line as Johnny starts to poke some of the people in line. The scene fades to black.--
