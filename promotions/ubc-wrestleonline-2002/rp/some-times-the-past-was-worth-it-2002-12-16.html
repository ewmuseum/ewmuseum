Some Times The Past Was Worth It

16 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: The Summer of '92 “Hey kid, what’s your name?” “Devin.” “Ok Devin, I’m Ben. You know how to play, right?” “Yep. Never get sick of playing.” Well, he was sure he wouldn’t be if he had ever played before. “That’s good. Now guys, we can’t lose this freakin’ game. I’m not giving Jake any of my fucking money. Never. We can beat him… them, if we stick together.” “That mother fucker is too big man. Look at him. He could have killed Tim right then.” said a boy in a black T-shirt and denim jeans. “Who cares? He sucks. We can beat the piece of shit. Trust me.” “Yeah Paul, fuck him. Kid’s a pansy.” said another kid, wearing a Yankees cap and a red Nike T-shirt. “Look. Sam. You just run down there and I’ll throw it to you. Jesse and Paul. Block Flannigan and Nick. Devin?” “Yeah?” “You think you can take Jake?” “Is that the big guy?” “Yeah that asshole. You think you can?” Devin looked over at him, staring with both fear and readiness. His mind asked him what he was doing. Are you insane, Devin? You can’t take him. Look at him. He would snap you like a toothpick. Just forget this stupid game. It’s not going to matter. You won’t even see these guys again. Just leave now and forget it. Forget him. Forget them. Forget it. “Yeah sure. I think I can take him.” “Ok man. Don’t try anything. Just make sure Sam gets in. That’s all.” “No problem.” Ben turned around and stood behind Jesse and Paul, they’re bodies preparing for the task put before them. Devin’s legs were waiting in anticipation for the signal to go. To run. To take out his target. If he didn’t succeed at this… “Hut. Go. Go.” He rushed towards the other side, and ran towards Jack himself. The bulk of a boy stood tall as he moved towards him, like a mountain daring a hill to take its place. Devin’s breath rose up from his lungs, and into his brain. His muscles were sucking in all the energy that they could muster. His eyes targeted. His legs threw his body up in the air. His arms readied his impact. And with the strike through the air, the massive young man fell to the ground, and Devin came crashing on top of him. Devin looked up, and saw Sam running by. Sam ran all the way with the ball, and then threw his hands into the air. The victory had been taken. The memory had been embraced. Devin’s smile was unmatched.
