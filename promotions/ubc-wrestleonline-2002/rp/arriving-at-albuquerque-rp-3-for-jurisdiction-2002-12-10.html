Arriving at Albuquerque (RP #3 for Jurisdiction)

10 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/12/02 [The scene opens up at an airport in Albuquerque, New Mexico. The doors to a plane open up, and a whole bunch of people floor out of the door. Among them is UBC Fighter Violent B. Violent B is wearing a black t-shirt and some black baggy jeans. He has his usual spikey necklace on, and he has his spikey hair up. Violent B walks out of the center of the people. He walks up to one of the flight attendants. He begins to speak.] Violent B - “Hey, where do you pick up your luggage?” [The man points over to a big machine that’s spinning all the luggage. Violent B just nods and walks over there. He stands next to some of the people there, waiting for his bad. One of the men standing next to him begins to talk to him.] Man - “So, what are you doing in a far out place like Albuquerque?” [Violent B then looks down at the man. He’s about 5’10”, and he has a chunky build to him. He’s wearing a white t-shirt and a pair of overalls. He has shoulder length dark hair that’s bald on top. You can see his belly sticking out of the t-shirt, and he’s very hairy. Violent B just shrugs and looks at him.] Man - “I’m here because UBC is here, and I came to see the fight! Why did you say you were here again?” Violent B - “I didn’t say anything. I’m here because I’m one of the UBC fighters. I’m Violent B.” [The man just looks at him.] Man - “If you’re in UBC, how come I haven’t seen you on there then?” [Violent B gets a pissed off look on his face.] Violent B - “Because I’m new there. Don’t give me this crap!” [The man steps back in confusion.] Man - “What are you talking about!?” [Violent B gets an annoyed look on his face, and he rolls his eyes.] Violent B - “You see, you’re just like the rest of the fans. I mean, no one knows who I am. I’ve been everywhere. I used to be one of the biggest stars in BMW, and I’ve been a big superstar in other places too, but the fans forget. I’ve been screwed everywhere I’ve went. The people that know me know my ability. I’ve fought and beat the best there are in the places I’ve been. Now all you fans probably think I’m just another joke. Another superstar that’ll be gone in a week. I’m sick of that shit! You need to realize that I’m gonna make a lasting impression at Jurisdiction. I’m gonna make everyone realize just who the hell I am. None of you are gonna be able to forget me, when I spread my violence through UBC. I don’t care what you think, I don’t care what the rest of the fans think. I don’t care what the staff thinks, and I don’t care what my fellow fighters think. All I care about is getting to the top and earning the respect I deserve for the skills I have. I’ve been through it all, yet no one even pays attention to who I am. I’m sick of you fans trying to act like I don’t exist. Get ready to see what I’m made of at Jurisdiction, when I take out one of the best in the business, Brett Wayne. He can talk all the shit about me that he wants, but it doesn’t mean shit in the ring. When you see what I can do, and when he sees what I can do, all of you will have no choice but to fear me. I’m a sick, twisted, violent son of a bitch, and I don’t feel like being at the bottom of the ladder anymore. I’m coming up, and everyone is gonna realize that when it’s all said and done, Violent B will be at the top of UBC, waiting for a real challenge.” Man - “Shut up! That’s never gonna happen!” Violent B - “You just watch it happen!” [Violent B then punches the man in the head, knocking him over. Violent B then begins to kick and stomp him. He gets into a mount position and punches the guy in the face as hard as he can, breaking his jaw and knocking him out. Violent B stands up and sees his bag coming. He grabs the bag. He then begins to walk off. He then turns around, spits on the man, and points in his face.] Violent B - “YOU FUCKIN’ TAKE THAT! You’ll see what I’m made of at Jurisdiction, and you’re NEVER forget my name!” [Violent B then turns around, and walks out a nearby door, waiting to go to his hotel. The scene fades.]
