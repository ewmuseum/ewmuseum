The Revolution Begins Part 2

12 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Friday 13th (....Hanger and Wonder are sitting on the couch talking as the Television Crew left around half an hour earlier. Hanger has a Jim Bean and Cola in his hand and Wonder has a Bundaberg Rum and Coke in his as they are talking about their match on Jurisdiction this week...) [--/--Steve Hanger--/--]: Did you see that just before man. Those Kingz of Africa clowns are being controlled by strings by some jackass who wants a clear path to the championship when he arrives. [--/--Sean Wonder--/--]: Sounds as thought their boss is a bit of a pussy to me. But hell lets not worry about their boss Steve because we are going to take them out and show them that without a shadow of a doubt we are the greatest team in UBC. I am going to prove to the world that I am capable of becoming a big star. I may be new to this sport but I am far from naive. [--/--Steve Hanger--/--]: I wouldn't have selected you as my partner if I didn't feel you could get the job done Sean but realise this. You have a big task to live up to as far as being my Tag Team Partner. Sure we are best friends but inside The Pit its gonna be different. PS could only stand being a partner of mine for so long before he had to leave and Icestorm was just a clown that soon felt my wrath and Macavedi has stood the test of time but he wasn't able to join us here because of legalities he is involved in at the moment. [--/--Sean Wonder--/--]: You can count on me Steve because I will not let you down at all. I know that the Dangerous Revolution name is one that is filled with great history from XFW and IHW and I hope to continue the great name of this group here in UBC with you. [--/--Steve Hanger--/--]: I'm glad that you realise that Sean because that is the most important thing that you needed to have learnt and I didn't need to teach it to you which proves to me that you are exactly what I am after in terms of someone that can back me up in UBC because I can assure everyone that I will one day be the Greatest Ultimate Brawler there ever was. I can assure you Sean that everyone in UBC and all of its pathetic fans will remember Steve Hanger whether they want to or not. [--/--Sean Wonder--/--]: And I can promise you Steve that everyone will see why I am a Natural Wonder of The World. (....Hanger reaches over to the remote and turns the Television on and sees highlights of The Brotherhoods last adventure on the town which was well documented on the UBC Website...) [--/--Sean Wonder--/--]: Certainly looks like the bosses had fun. But Steve I need to ask Ehrena and Xavier are the big dogs in charge but they are also a team so do we treat them with caution or just leave them alone. [--/--Steve Hanger--/--]: Well Sean, The Brotherhood are a team and if the time calls for us to fight them then we will but we are also not going to go out and pick a fight with them because we do not want to be out picking fights when at the time it has no way of concerning us. [--/--Sean Wonder--/--]: But doesn't that relate to The Kingz of Africa and their boss?? [--/--Steve Hanger--/--]: No that is a different kettle of fish altogether because those guys think that they can overule their little vendetta on our time then I don't think so. You see we have been given a spot on the card and a nice high spot as the 4th Match on the card which is two matches below the Preimer Event which means that Ehren and Xavier have high hopes for us and I WILL NOT have those Kingz of Africa clowns ruining our chance for a good first impression by letting their boss overrun my I mean our time. [--/--Sean Wonder--/--]: Because of the notion of you only get one chance to make a good first impression?? [--/--Steve Hanger--/--]: Exactly. We let the whole world know that we where here last Jurisdiction and warned The Brotherhood and The Kingz of Africa that we where not to be taken lightly and now this week on Jurisdiction we get to back up our words with our fists and I can assure you that we will deliver and The Kingz of Africa and their boss will not take us lightly. I will make this promise to them now and that is if their boss wants to make a statement during my, I mean OUR time then I am going to make sure that it the only statement made is me slamming my fist into his face because of the fact that no one messes with our time and gets away with it. No one and that stands for everyone in UBC. [--/--Sean Wonder--/--]: But Steve I am excited at this because on Jurisdiction we get to debut the Final Riff for all the world to see and I don't reckon it would seem any more fitting than to deliver it onto Amir and Malik because once those two have been dealt with our path to UBC glory will only have just begun. [--/--Steve Hanger--/--]: I couldn't have said that any better myself. (....Suddenly the Phone rings in a high pitch tone as Hanger stands up and walks over and answers it. He takes down some notes from the call as Sean looks on waiting to hear what the call is all about. Hanger puts the phone back down on the receiver and speaks to Sean....) [--/--Steve Hanger--/--]: Looks like we got ourselves a gig buddy. [--/--Sean Wonder--/--]: Sweet mother of Jebus man. Where at? [--/--Steve Hanger--/--]: At the local Strip Club. (....A childish grin like he just saw porn for the first time comes across Wonder's face...) [--/--Sean Wonder--/--]: I know I just said it but Sweet Mother of Jebus. Tits and Ass man and we get to play. Looks Like I'll get a chance to show the ladies why they call me Dr Love. (Starts singing) I am Your Doctor Love. They Call me.....Dr Love, I've got the cure your thinking of. [--/--Steve Hanger--/--]: If you say so buddy, If you say so. (....Hanger walks off as Wonder is still singing to himself. He notices Hanger is gone and shoots out the room as the camera fades to blakc...)
