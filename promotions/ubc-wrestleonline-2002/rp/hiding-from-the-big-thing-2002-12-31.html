::: Hiding from the 'Big Thing' :::

31 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12.31.02 ...It was all on the line. Guts, glory, and most importantly - revenge. But then the Ultimate title came into play. And now it's about much more than that. UBC is beginning a legacy. One of power and tradition. It's in its early stages, but to be known as the first ever champion in the Underground Brawler's Club -- what an honor. And that's what Tommy Grady is faced with come Saturday. He has two talented opponents, but he isn't backing down. And he's quite the workhorse. Granted, a sober Tommy doesn't really have a good idea of what is going on, but he senses the importance. Why does the sober Tommy matter anyways? The fearsome Tommy Grady will be the one to show up at Jurisdiction. And he'll be as ready as he can possibly be. To shut up all the nay-sayers. Keep them all in line. A new era is about to begin, boys and girls. And it will be one hell of a reign... :::Scene opens with an image of a house. It is not exactly the nicest house. The outside needs a paint job, with peeling white paint there currently. There are three steps leading up to the front door. We cut to the inside of the house, where it seems quite calm. Suddenly two legs are seen walking. The camera backs away a little bit to reveal Abner Estrada making his way somewhere within the house with a joint flopping around in his mouth, unlit. He walks over to a door and gives it a knock. He waits and looks confused. He knocks again, still no response. The third time he knocks and just walks into the door. There is no one in there. Abner looks around for a moment. He opens the door to a closet and looks around. He closes it and looks around before taking the joint out of his mouth. He looks at the bed for a minute and the camera follows his gaze. Two legs are seen sticking out from under the bed and Abner shakes his head. He walks over and pulls the legs out from under the bed to reveal Tommy Grady::: Tommy Grady - AAAAAAAAH!.. wait.. YOU are the big thing? Abner Estrada - Hey, now -- I've been on a diet.. what the hell are you under the bed for? Tommy Grady - Aaron said I should get ready for a big thing.. but he said he'd tell me more when he go home.. Abner Estrada - A big thing? Tommy Grady - He said it was really big.. Abner Estrada - You sure it was physically big and not just.. news or something? Tommy Grady - Big news? You don't think I'm gonna make a guest appearance on Teletubbies, do you? I wrote them a letter and they-- Abner Estrada - TOMMY.. putting a letter in the mail box that says Teletubbies with no address doesn't work.. Tommy Grady - But it's Teletubbies.. it's like Santa Claus. They get it somehow! Abner Estrada - Dude, I think you need this weed more than I do.. Tommy Grady - I had to pick weeds all weekend.. it's part of my allowance from Aaron.. Abner Estrada - But it's your money he's giving you.. Tommy Grady - That's what I said, but he hit me in the head with a shoe and called me a slut.. Abner Estrada - That's rude.. Tommy Grady - That's what I thought.. jerko.. :::The voice of Brett Wayne is heard from the TV of the next room::: Tommy Grady - It's the big thing! :::Tommy slides back under the bed::: Abner Estrada - Relax.. it's just a big ego.. Tommy Grady - Oh.. whew.. so it's not the big thing, then? Abner Estrada - It's falsely large, but I don't think that's the big news from Aaron.. Tommy Grady - The wait is killing me.. Abner Estrada - Take a hit at this, you'll relax then.. Tommy Grady - I don't smoke.. I don't want black lungs, buddy.. thanks.. Abner Estrada - It will give you the big news quicker.. Tommy Grady - Nuh uh.. Abner Estrada - Uh huh.. Tommy Grady - Liar.. liars go to hell! :::Abner lights up his joint and takes a big whiff. He blows it out and relaxes. He holds it out and Tommy grabs it. He looks at it and sniffs the smoke. Suddenly his eyes glaze over and he stares into the air with a goofy smile::: Abner Estrada - Did you just get fuckin' high off the smell? Tommy Grady - Wha man? Come back down, man.. quit floatin', man.. Abner Estrada - That's crazy, dude.. high off smell.. quit hoggin' the shit.. give it to me.. !!!HIGH!!! :::Tommy's smile suddenly fades off his face. He looks down at the ground and Abner takes the joint out of his hand::: Tommy Grady - What the fuck was that? Abner Estrada - It's my weed.. Tommy Grady - You don't snatch things from me. You don't take things that I have in my grasp. You shoulda learned that after Brett Wayne stole the Ultimate Brawler's Trophy from me just a week ago, asshole.. Abner Estrada - Oh dude.. you blacked out! Sweet.. Tommy Grady - The only blackouts that are gonna happen, Abner, are the blackouts that will occur on Saturday night.. two opponents.. Chris Carter and Brett Wayne.. two opponents that clearly don't even -- whoa.. !!!REALITY!!! Abner Estrada - Whoa.. Tommy Grady - Dude.. that shit rules! Abner Estrada - Why do you think I spend my McDonalds paycheck on it, man? :::The door is heard::: Tommy Grady - AH! AARON HAS THE BIG THING! Abner Estrada - *peeking into the other room* He's alone.. Tommy Grady - What if the big thing is invisible?! Abner Estrada - Dude....... that would be awesome.. Tommy Grady - For YOU.. Aaron Everage - What the hell's goin' on in here? Tommy Grady - AAAAAAAAAH! Aaron Everage - What the hell is wrong with him? Abner Estrada - He is scared of the big thing.. Tommy Grady - I've seen Harry Potter! I know about the invisible cloak, you big thing! Go away before I swing randomly with my pillow! Aaron Everage - Tommy, it's big NEWS.. not a big THING.. Abner Estrada - SEE! Tommy Grady - OOOOH! Aaron Everage - Geez.. Tommy Grady - I've seen Lord of the Rings, too -- If I see a stone in here I'm gonna kick its ass! Aaron Everage - Sit down and shut up, Tommy.. you may need a drink.. Abner? :::Abner takes another hit of his weed and nods towards Aaron, leaving the room with a big grin::: Tommy Grady - It's okay.. I already had a Hi-C juice box.. Aaron Everage - I'm talking hard stuff.. Tommy Grady - You can't drink hard stuff, silly.. Aaron Everage - Don't ever say silly again.. Tommy, this is big.. BIG news.. you're gonna need to be held down for this, believe me.. :::Abner returns with a tall, skinny shot glass filled to the rim with what looks like taquila::: Tommy Grady - Is that orange Hi-C? Aaron Everage - Uh.. yeah.. Tommy Grady - Mmmmmm.. :::Tommy quickly drinks it::: Tommy Grady - BAAAAH! GROSS! :::Tommy spits::: Aaron Everage - What's wrong? Tommy Grady - Dude.. the expiration date must be years over! Aaron Everage - Oh uh.. we'll check on that.. sorry..... about that.. Tommy Grady - It's okay.. *spits* .. gross.. :::Tommy looks at the ground in a daze for a moment before looking up with a gleam in his eye::: !!!INTOXICATED!!! Aaron Everage - You okay, Tommy? Tommy Grady - I'm fine, who's askin? Aaron Everage - I have great news for you, Tommy.. Tommy Grady - Oh yeah? You're gonna move your ass back into your pants instead of wearing it on your neck? Aaron Everage - .. uh.. no.. that's not it.. Saturday, Tommy.. it's no longer first blood.. it's for the Ultimate title.. Tommy Grady - Ultimate title.. on the line? The biggest accomplishment one man can gain is going to be up for grabs? Despite my so called loss to Brett Wayne a week and a half ago, I get my shot at the title? Wow. That is some great news.... for me. For Brett, on the other hand.. and for that other out of the blue fighter in Chris Carter -- that's the kiss of death. Sure.. they'll go down in history as the first two losers in a UBC title match.. but they'll never receive the glory of becoming the first UBC champion ever. The legacy is beginning to be written. The legend is on its second page, and as UBC goes trucking on -- it's first stop will be with Tommy Grady. I will win that Ultimate championship. I will be the victor come Saturday night. Sure you can call in backup. You can call in your mother or your father or tag team partner or stable mate. Sure you can do that. And sure, I may not have the backing that the two of you have. But I have faith that Kron will want this thing done right. I have faith that there will be a champion determined this Saturday night and it won't be out of a screwjob one way or another. You see, UBC wants a champion with class. With spirit. With the will to go out and compete day in and day out for the rest of their career. And I'm going into my young career VERY ready. Already contending for a championship in my first appearance. My first month. While people like Brett Wayne and Chris Carter -- with histories.. they are fighting to even stay in the same realm as I'm in. Well, guess what, my friends -- you don't compare. You don't now and you never will. Brett, you and I fought a hard match, but who won? Who TRULY won? I did. I had the heart when it came down to it. I had the drive deep within my very soul. And when we meet for the Ultimate Championship, all the Brawlers trophy busines will be in the past. That doesn't matter now. I don't care about you screwing me out of a victory. I don't. It went into the record books as a draw and that's the way we'll keep it.. but none of that matters this Saturday. It was first about revenge, but now it's about gold.. now it's about becoming the first UBC champion. And nothing is going to stand in my way. Especially not Chris Carter. Who do you think you are anyways? Do you deserve a shot at the championship? I mean, I'm seriously asking because the only time I've heard of you is when it was announced that I was going to be facing you in a first blood match.. yet that's been changed to a title match. You don't even deserve to face ME.. much less with the Ultimate Championship lying in the balance. But why does it matter? It doesn't. Tommy Grady will walk out of Jurisdiction the champion whether you like it or not. There isn't a damn thing you or the rest of Sudden Violence can do about it.. :::Tommy shakes his head as if the alcohol is wearing off::: Tommy Grady - Now, as for now.. I'll be going. But don't you dare take your eyes off the prize. Forget about Brett Wayne, and Wayne -- forget about Carter. You two need to team up on me if you want the slightest chance at either of you taking home the championship. Because when it's all said and done.. one way or another.. you'll be countin' the lights.. and that ain't a promise.. that's a guaran.. damn.. tee! Aaron Everage - Now Tommy, you have to get ready.. cuz THIS is the big thing.. !!!SOBER!!! Tommy Grady - What is? Aaron Everage - The match.. Tommy Grady - I know, dude.. the match Saturday is big.. but I already knew that.. Aaron Everage - *sigh* Why'd you have to sober up? Tommy Grady - I don't know what that means, but I was just out cold like a fish on an ice hook.. woo! Aaron Everage - What does that mean? Tommy Grady - Fish.. ice hook.. self explainatory.. Aaron Everage - Right.. well, your MATCH this Saturday.. is for the Ultimate championship.. Tommy Grady - NO WAY! Aaron Everage - Yes way.. Tommy Grady - NO WAY! Aaron Everage - Yup.. Tommy Grady - No... WAY! Aaron Everage - Shut up! Tommy Grady - Okay.. Aaron Everage - Ultimate title on the line, man.. Tommy Grady - Dude.. I need to stay concious for this one.. I haven't gotten to fight a fight concious yet.. Aaron Everage - No! The last thing we need is a concious Tommy Grady going into a title bout.. Tommy Grady - Cuz I'm crazy when I black out? Aaron Everage - You're a superstar when you black out.. Tommy Grady - I still lead a Christian life when I black out, right? Aaron Everage - Uh... sure.. Tommy Grady - Sweet.. blacking out rules, then! Aaron Everage - Yup, it rules.. and it pays the bills.. Tommy Grady - Bill who? Aaron Everage - Bills.. Tommy Grady - Who are the Bill's? A band or something? Aaron Everage - I think that invisible big thing just walked in the front door.. Tommy Grady - AAAAAAAAAAAAH!! :::Tommy slides back under his bed and Aaron laughs a bit. He stands up and walks out of the room. The camera scans over to show Tommy's feet hanging out from under the bed again. It slowly pans to the window and fades to black::: ...Detemined. That's what Tommy Grady is. And come Saturday night it will be obvious that Chris Carter and Brett Wayne both don't stand a chance against the destined-to-be champion. Tommy Grady will leave the first Ultimate Champion on Saturday. He'll go to sleep a champion for the first time in his career. He'll wake up with something accomplished for the first time in years. He'll live his life, head held high. UBC is the future of this business.. and to be first champion.. that's what this sport is all about.. the Ultimate champion...
