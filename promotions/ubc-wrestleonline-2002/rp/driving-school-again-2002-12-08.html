Driving School Again

8 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12-08-02 (After UBC officials get words that UBC superstar, Krisis, is at a local bar in Minneapolis, Minnesota. They waste no time rushing a UBC interviewer to hunt Krisis down. Over the past few weeks, Krisis has rarely been seen, even by his fellow family members and his friends. His in ring abilities have also been 'below par' since the closing of BMW. What is the deal with Krisis? It's almost as if he's disappeared...) Bartender - Hey Krisis, would you like it straight or with coke in it? Krisis - I want that shit straight. Gimme it hard too. (The bartender pours some bacardi in to a shot glass and slides it to Krisis across the bar table. Krisis grabs it and slams it down hard.) Bartender - Damn man, you don't look to good. Maybe you should go home and get a nice sleep. You need to call a cab because you can't drive like this. You're piss drunk off your ass after four beers, three bacardi cokes, and after six shots of straight bacardi. Krisis - No no no, I'm fine. I don't need no cab. I ain't got no money for a stupid cab ride. Bartender - Alright, whatever you say. I can't say I warned you. You know why you have no money, that's because you spend it all here at the bar to get yourself drunk. Whatever, later. Krisis - Yeah, whatever. I'll talk to you later. (Krisis stumbles off the bar stool and stumbles outside. He walks over to his 2000 Black Hummer and climbs in. He starts the engine and pulls out of the parking lot. He starts to speed down the highway and then enters the freeway. He steps on it and in the distance, there is a police officer lurking in the dark, behind a bilboard sign. Krisis speeds past him as the police officer checks his speed on the speed radar gun. Krisis is speeding past the speed limit so the police officer starts to chase Krisis down. Krisis starts to slow down and pulls over to the side of the road. The police officer pulls up behind Krisis and gets out of his squad car and walks up to Krisis's Black Hummer.) Krisis - Yes, Mr. Officer? Police Officer - Do you know how fast you were going? Krisis - Dahhhh....ummm.... gee... let me think.... 55 miles per hour? Police Officer - No sir, you were going 95 in a 70 speed limit zone. And what it seemed to me is that you were all over the road. You can't seem to stay in the same lane and keep on going straight. Krisis - Oh, I have a driving problem like that. I can't stay in the same lane for some reason. I just have to be all over the road. I feel more safer that way. Police Officer - Ok son, step out of the truck. (Krisis steps out of his Hummer) Police Officer- Now, place both of your hands on the truck. Do you have anything that you could stab me with or shot me with? Krisis - Nope, I'm all clean. (The police officer checks Krisis for any weapons that might be on him) Police Officer - Alright, extend your arms and touch your nose with your right hand. Krisis - No problem. (Krisis attempts to touch his nose with his left hand when the police officer told him to touch his nose with his right hand.) Krisis - That was easy, can you please give me something that's more challenging? Police Officer - Alright, put your feet together and keep your arms extended and walk in a straight line, heel to toe. (Krisis tries to walk in a straight line, heel to toe but stumbles a few times.) Police Officer - I've seen enough. How many drinks did you have tonight, sir? Krisis - Well, let's see. I had a beer and a few bacardi cokes, that's all. Police Officer - Sure, turn around and place your hands behind your head, please. (Krisis does the the officer said to do. The officer takes out his cuffs and takes one arm at a time and cuffs Krisis. The officer opens his back door and places Krisis in the back. The officer gets on his radio and calls for a tow-truck to pick of Krisis's Hummer and take it to the in-pound lot. The officer speeds off to the nearest detention hall for Krisis to sober up in. After they arrive at the detention hall, the officer places into the 'Sober up room' and goes into his office and runs a record check on Krisis. Krisis has no criminal background, but a lot of DWI's. Krisis is going to have his driver's license revoked and he's going to have to take the driver's training class over.) The Next Day (Krisis awakes from being drunk and has a little hang over. The officer lets Krisis free but Krisis doesn't walk of the detention hall without a driver's license. Right before Krisis left, the police officer told him that he'd have to take the driver's training class over if he wants to have a driver's license. Krisis walks home and calls 'Sears Driving School' and gets all set up for his driver's training class. A few days after that, Krisis wakes up at around 9:30 a.m. and goes to the bus stop. The bus comes and Krisis gets on and tells the bus driver where he's going. After about 10 minutes, Krisis arrives at Sears department. He gets off the bus and walks into Sears. He slides up the escalator and walks down the hall and walks into the driver's training classroom and sits down.) Teacher - Ah sir, you have to check in and register. Krisis - Uggg... alright. (Krisis gets up and walks up to the front to check in and register.) Teacher - What's your name? Krisis - Krisis Teacher - Okay, you're gonna have to sign here, here, here, and here. Did you pay over the phone or do you want to pay here, right now? Krisis - No, I paid over the phone. (Krisis signs the paper where he's supposed to and hands it back to the teacher.) Teacher - Alright, I just have one question for you. How old are you? Krisis - 23 Teacher - Damn, usually I have 17 year olds as the oldest but you, by far out beat them. hahaha. Krisis - Hahaha, funny. Not. (Krisis turns around and walks back to where he was sitting. He gets comfortable and gets ready to listen and learn.) Teacher - Alright, lets get started. First, before we get into the good things, let's talk about drinking and driving. Never, and I mean NEVER drink and drive. If you do and get caught, you'll get your license revoked and get into deep trouble. Krisis - Hey, how come no one told me this before? I mean, I got pulled over for being pissed drunk off my ass and then I lost my license and now I'm here, learning about it. What's up with that!? Teacher - Well Krisis, it's common sense NOT to drink and then drive. You must be pretty stupid to do that. If you keep doing that, then you're putting yourself under crisis. Krisis - No, I don't put myself Under Krisis, I put people like you Under Krisis. Oh and it's Krisis with a big K to you. Teacher - No Krisis, I don't spell or say crisis with a Big K. It seems like to me that you're familiar with 'Big K' K-Mart stores because you're wearing somethng that would come from that 'Big K' store. Now if you'll execuse me, let me teach this class and teach the other people that actually want to learn about drinking and driving. Krisis - They call me Krisis, bitch! Teacher - Execuse me? Krisis - Yeah, you heard me bitch. They call me Krisis! Teacher - Okay...? (Krisis gets up and runs toward the teacher at full force. Krisis jumps up and dropkicks him square in the head. The whole class takes a huge gasp in shock.) Krisis - (Yelling in the teacher's face, over and over again) They call me Krisis, bitch! They call me Krisis, bitch! They call me Krisis, bitch! (One student stood up and spoke what was on his mind.) Student - Hey, that was very un-called for! I wanna learn something here. I didn't pay $359 for this bullshit! Krisis - You know what!? I didn't pay $359 to listen to him run his mouth about me and my finisher! Student - What's your finisher? Krisis - Here, lemme show you. Get up on the table. (The student climbs up on the table and Krisis follows him. Krisis turns the student around and grabs his head and plants the Under Krisis on the Student, right through the table. The other students gasp in shock as Krisis puts that one student Under Krisis.) Krisis - Anybody else wanna know why they call me Krisis.... bitches!? (One student says something) Student - Yeah, I would like to know why EXACTLY they call you Krisis, bitch. Krisis - Alright, if you say so. (Krisis stands on a nearby steel chair and executes a moonsault on the student.) Krisis - THAT'S why they call me Krisis, bitch! Oh and let me tell you all what the definition of Krisis is. It means 'Breaking all the fuckin' rules' and that's exactly what I did here. Now if you'll excuse me, I have to get my driver's license back so I can drive again! (Krisis walks to the teacher's desk, stepping over the three knocked out bodies on the floor and grabs his driver's license. He walks out of the class and calls for a cab. He gets a cab, drives to the inpound lot and gets his 2002 Black Hummer back. He pulls out of the drive-way of the inpound lot and speeds off as the scene fads to black.)
