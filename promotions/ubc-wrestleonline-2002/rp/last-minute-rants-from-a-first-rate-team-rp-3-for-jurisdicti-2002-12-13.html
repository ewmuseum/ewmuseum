Last minute rants from a first rate team...(RP #3 for Jurisdiction)

13 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12-13-02 {The scene opens with nothing but a fuzzy shot coming from the camera slowly the picture fades in and you see Amir Mubootu and Malik Tehani with their K. O. A. shirts on and slight smiles on their faces. } Malik Tehani: Well low and behold it seems Dangerous Revolution had finally graced us with their presence and frankly I am unimpressed. I thought maybe, just maybe when you finally spoke that something intelligent would come out of your mouths. Amir Mubootu: Well that's what happens when you think Malik. You see I could have told you from the beginning that they aren't about nothing. A lot of hot air if you ask me. Not to say they don't have talent, just not enough to hang with us. Now boys you made one very, very big mistake you decided to try and talk down to us. You decided to try and prove yourself against what will soon be the greatest team in UBC The Kingz of Africa. Well, I am sorry to be the first to tell you two that that fantasy just won't happen. We are better, bigger, stronger, faster and smarter than you on our worst day! But come Jurisdiction it won't be our worst day it will be our best. The day The Kingz of Africa debut in UBC, the day the master plan begins to unravel and the day that the Dangerous Revolution learn a little manners! Malik Tehani: Sean Wonder, Steve Hanger do you really want to go thru with this? I mean the world of hurt that is coming your way really isn't worth it is it? I mean you two sound like you where big names in other federations, not that that really mean anything but still you two sound like you had potential there. So why don't you do the right thing and bow out of this match. You see in UBC we don't put on a 'show,' we put on fights. We sweat, bleed and break bones that the sick pleasure of our fans! Are your really ready for all that? I really don't think so now Sean let me speak to you personally, you consider yourself a 'Natural Wonder of Nature?' You can't me serious! Its a wonder of nature they your pathetic life has lasted this long and now you want to put all that on the line for what? Money, fame, fortune? Its hard for a man to spend any money when he is laid up in a hospital bed! Amir Mubootu: Easy Malik, we still got a day to go before we get to prove to them and the world that. But it seems our opponents want to look past us and towards the The Brotherhood. They want to act like we don't exist and try and concentrate on the UBC bosses. Well, friends that is a cardinal sin, you never ever look past your next opponent! When you try and look towards the future you tend to skip what's directly in front of you! And right now The Kingz of Africa are staring you right in the face and haven't flinched yet. Ehren and Xavier might be the bosses but this week they shouldn't be your concern surviving The Kingz should be! {Amir and Malik lose their grins and get more serious looks on their faces.} Amir Mubootu: Now unfortunately you two also decided to speak about our employer. Why oh why would you want to do that? If you two would have left well enough alone then we would have just beaten you and moved on, but now we have special orders to teach you two a lesson for speaking on matters that you know nothing about! You had the chance to just come into this match, fight a good fight and lose like everyone knows you will do but you want to try and make your way to the top by going over us? That my friends is something that just will not happen! Malik Tehani: Sean, Steve let this first brutal beating in UBC be a lesson to you and everyone else that you can't come into UBC and say this and that and claim that you are the best, you must prove yourself night in and night out. The Kingz of Africa will do just that and since it has to start with the so called Dangerous Revolution then so be it! But it won't end there, it won't end till we decide that its time to move on! We decide when this ride is over, and boys as those American rappers like to say, ' We gonna ride til the wheels fall off!' {Amir looks over at Malik for a moment a little confused by his last statement.} Amir Mubootu: Um, Malik what on earth are you talking about? Gonna ride til the wheels fall off? Since when did you start listening to that type of music? Malik Tehani: That's not the point, it gets to the heart of the matter. Just thought I would try to adapt to our new home. But we are getting off the subject now aren't we? We can talk about my new taste in music another time. Besides what about our theme song? Have you listened to it? Amir Mubootu: Well no but.... Anyway where were we at? Malik Tehani: Beating Dangerous Revolution, Kingz of Africa best team going, blah, blah blah. Amir Mubootu: Oh yeah. Dangerous Revolution you till will come to sit at the head of the table in UBC but your time just ain't right now. Malik Tehani: And Dangerous Revolution like the great Xzibit once said, 'My sixth sense tells me you ain't worth six cents!' {Amir just looks at Malik who has a big smile on his face and shakes his head as the camera fades to black.}
