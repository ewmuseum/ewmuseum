Out of the Corner of my Envious Eye

28 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/28/02 “No No. It’s ok. It’s only me.” Devin’s eyes glanced at the shadowy figure walking towards him. Her voice, a lullaby in the nightly hour, was some what beautiful, but mysterious. He knew at once, before the neon glow of the street lights lit her face, who she was. “I just wanted to thank you for what you just did for me.” Her smile drowned him in a shower of self pity. Her eyes cursed him with love that he could never have. Devin wanted to hold her. He fought the urge. And it took so much strength to do just that. “It was not a problem. I don’t care who he thinks he is. No woman should be treated that way.” She stopped, standing on the other side of the street, her splendor a shimmer in the darkness of the sidewalk. He stood their, staring at her. “I don’t blame him though. I know that he loved me. I guess he just took our break up a little too drastically.” Devin realized that this guy, whose face he had just damaged and his spirit he had just crushed, had been lucky enough to say that once, this beauty that stood before him had once been his. He could say that he had touched her. Kissed her. Embraced her. Devin couldn’t say that. He only wished he could. Your beauty So stunning in its entirety I wish I could feel you Keep this love deep inside of me “He really isn’t a bad guy, Darrel. He just doesn’t want to let me go that easily. I don’t blame him either. He is such a stubborn guy.” “Well… I was only doing what I thought I should…” “My name is Nicole.” Nicole – the epitome of magnificence. “Mines is Devin.” If only you were mine Then maybe I could exist Hold onto you forever Denude this bliss “Thank you Devin.” She said, her voice echoing down the empty street. She started to slowly cross the street, her eyes never steering away from his glance. Devin started to walk towards her, and found himself longing for her. To feel the sensation of her touch. The welcoming of her warmth. The passion of her body. Ironically, they met in the middle of the street, standing in between the white streak of a line that separated the two sides, in the only spot where to two parallel street lights met. “I’m really grateful that you helped me like that. You didn’t have to risk yourself like that Devin.” “I had to.” “Why?” His heart filled with love, as she threw her arms around his neck. “I just had to. I didn’t want… want to see you get hurt.” “Well… thank you.” She pulled him, and then, as Devin’s heart reached its climax, she kissed him. He was at first caught by surprise, but then almost immediately pulled her with him. And now Through all this anticipation And all this desire You are my only salvation There they were, in the middle of the city lights, the moon their only witness. Their passion was shared only between two souls. Their love only a stint that would last momentarily. Maybe, if he had been a different person with a different life, this moment could last forever. But, as he took in all this love from all this beauty… Then, all of a sudden, I started to cry. I couldn’t help it. – Page 179 of Chapter 23 He took in one last moment from this embracement before letting go of his grasp. She looked at him, a smile glowing on her face. But when she saw his tears shining in the street light, her smile turned into one of sadness and despair. “What’s wrong Devin?” “I… I love you.” Devin turned around, and walked away. He wiped his tears off his cheeks, and then never looked back. He could hear Nicole weep in the wind, but he didn’t want to see her again. He knew that if he did, his heart ache would be too much to bear. Part of him wanted to turn back. Part of him wanted to run back to her. Part of him was never going to let her go. But Devin knew one thing. A shadow can never love. You were my everything My love’s only entity Now you are a memory My soul’s envy When Devin returned to his car some hours later, no one was in sight. He would get into the car, find the copy of “The Catcher in the Rye, take his black ballpoint pen, and write the name “Nicole” on page 22. That was his birthday. That was the day that had changed his life. And she was something that he never wanted to forget. Devin sat there, the tears long since dried, and watched the world. And realized that he missed her already.
