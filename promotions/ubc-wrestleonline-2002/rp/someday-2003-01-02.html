Someday

2 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 01.02.03 The holidays, Devin decided, were overrated. They passed by every year, with families and all the people celebrating and cherishing their annual arrival, and then they were gone. Just added memories to mourn over while one slowly died. Now, with the sight of people walking the streets alone and with their same, dreary faces wearing a hole through the atmosphere, the world had gone back to normal once again. The car drove smoothly through the streets of the once lively city. Traces of confetti and balloons lay lifeless along the sidewalks and the roads. The people, cheering and enjoying themselves one evening a few days ago, were now silent and unspoken, dragging themselves along their mundane routines. Inside, Devin was sort of relieved not to have taken any part in their little once a year event. Counting away another year of his life so that he can enjoy being that much closer to dying? What purpose would that really serve? Instead, he walked the streets of Phoenix, staring into the eyes of all that he passed. Some taunted him, while others smiled at him, while other checked him out, and while others got pissed off at him. None of that mattered. He didn’t do it to feel superior, or to give them any pity. He did it because some part of him wanted to be one of them. Deep down, hidden in a costume of decadence and hate, shined a little boy’s soul, begging for someone to take his hand and lead him out of the cave. When the countdown started, and a million voices crowded the air, Devin was sitting in his car, watching the world around him slowly build into an explosion of happiness. There he was, in the center of everything, staring out a windshield into humanity’s heart. All the people hugged each other, kissed their loved ones, sand together in one voice, and celebrated the coming of their new year. All the hate and violence in their hearts was gone. They were no longer apparent. Only the New Year was clear. Nothing bad was going to happen. Only good. And as he sat there, part of him hating everything that ever was and another wishing that he could be apart of that same everything, Devin thought about his career. Where would it lead him? Would it give him any recognition? Would he truly gain anything for beating the life out of other guys? A title maybe. A few fans to cheer his name. Sure, it was insignificant, but maybe it was the first step to being apart of them. As of now, he was driving to his next show. His next fight. Probably his first really important one too. This fight would decide the number one contender to a title, which was just what Devin needed. A life. Sure, he thought that he had one already, but he found out that it was nothing. Now he wanted everything. Maybe then he could be something. Maybe then he could be someone. Maybe then he could celebrate a holiday someday. You never know – Page 52 of Chapter 7 ---- Back
