' A day of embarassment ' Pt 1

17 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12-17-02 The mourning sun shines through the blinds as the sound of birds chirping is heard, the camera drafts down to an alarm clock that says 7:00 am. A couple of minutes later the phone rings and continues to ring as Brett lays motionelss in the small bed. Moments later Mike opens the door with a pissed off look on his face and shouts at Brett... Mike: Answer the God Damn Phone so I can get some fucking sleep. Mike continues yelling as Brett still lays there motionless, Mike finally gets fed up with Brett and walks over and answer the phone himself. Mike: (Shouts).... Hello! Person on the Phone: Hello, son is that you? Mike: (Shocked).... MOM?!?! Person on the Phone: Mike....Oh thank god its you! I'm glad that women told me where to find you two...Brett is with you isn't he? Mike: Yeah, he's right here next to me asleep. Where are you at mom? MoM: (Hesitates for a Minute).... Umm...Well...I'm in jail again... Mike: (Shouts).... WHAT! AGAIN! MoM: Shhh!!! Don't wake Brett up please! Brett quickly raises up from his bed, he yawns as he turns his head only to see Mike on the phone. Brett throws the covers off of him and onto the floor as he gets up out of bed, he turns and looks at Mike. Brett: Who's on the phone at....(Turns and looks at the clock) ....7 am in the mourning? Mike: Its our mother... Brett: Oh really....(Confused)....what does she want on 7 am in the mourning? Mike: Well she says she is in jail again. Brett: (Gets Angry)....WHAT?!?! AGAIN?!?! Mike: Yep! Brett: What the hell did she do this time? Mike: (Thinks to himself for a minute)....Umm well you know, I don't know myself. Brett: Give me that damn phone! Brett quickly grabs the phone out of Mike's hands as he puts it up to his ear. Brett: Mother... MoM: Hi son, how are things going? Brett: DON'T CHANGE THE FUCKING SUBJECT, WHAT THE HELL ARE YOU DOING IN JAIL! MoM: (Pauses) Well it isn't all excatly my fault... Brett: Well who's fault was it then?!?! MoM: Do you know a guy named Tommy Grady? Brett: (Laughs)....Tommy Grady yeah I know that fool. MoM: Well he got me put in Jail. Brett: (Shouts) HOW?!?! MoM: Well you see I was working hard and he came along asking for a hose that squirted water and I said I had one he offered to buy it for 15.25 and I said that wouldn't cut it and he started getting angry with me, he scared me Brett. I didn't know what to do...but then a police officer came along and asked what the deal was and Tommy Grady told the officer I was a whore fucking for money and you know me son I'm not that kind of person but then the officer hand cuffed me and through me in the back seat of the police car. They both hurt me son and Tommy Grady called me a WHORE and I'm not one. Brett: (Gets Very Angry)....That motherfucker called you a whore! Why that son of a bitch....(Looks over at Mike)....Grady called our MoM a WHORE and got her thrown in jail. Mike: Well thats what she is, she does fuck for a living you know... Brett:(Covers the phone up and whispers) Shhh...I'm trying to make her feel better damnit, play along. Mike: Oh...ok....(Smiles) Brett: (Uncovers the phone)....We'll kick his ass for you mom, won't we Mike? Mike: We sure will don't worry about a thing mom. MoM: Good! So now can you boys come get me out of Jail? Brett: What right now? MoM: Yes right now... Brett: (Moans)...Ok where at? MoM: Albuquerque, New Mexico. Brett: WHAT?!?! You expect me to go all the way back to Albuquerque, New Mexica to pick you up... MoM: Umm, yes I do you are my son and if you love me then you will. Brett: (Moans)....Damnit we'll be there tomorrow but this is the last god damn time I'm going to come get your ass out of jail. If you get put back in there one more time then.... MoM: (Interrupts)....I know I Know you'll never come and get me again, thats what you always say. Brett: Damnit I'm on my way!....(Brett hangs the phone up) Mike: Now what? Brett: We're going to go get mom so get ready. Mike: Damnit you know how embarassing this is going to be walking into the police station after losing to two amature fighters. Brett: Its going to be embarassing just to be seen with her. Mike: Damnit can I stay here? Brett: No where leaving now, call and order us some plane tickets, make sure its a round trip. Mike: Ok fine... Mike pulls out his cell phone from his back pocket and dials a number as he walks out of Brett's room, Brett begins packing some of his things up as he begins talking to himself. Brett: Damn that Tommy Grady! He'll pay for this! He may be laughing right now but come saturday I'll be the one looking down at him laughing. Tommy Grady is nothing compared to me! I can't beleive he made it this far, how can that fool be undefeated! Oh wait....I know why. He hasn't faced anyone better than him yet but he will Saturday and when he does I'll own his ass. Grady's winning streak will end next week and that will prove who the real man is in this business. Brett finishes packing his things and walks to his door, before opening it Mike opens the door connecting with Brett's face. (SMACK) Brett: (Holds his nose).... Damnit that hurt! Mike: (Laughs)....Sorry bro! Brett: Yeah you better be now what the hell do you want this time? Mike: Well I have the tickets ordered and there waiting on us at the airport and our limo is waiting on us outside. Brett: (Continues holding his nose)....Well lets go then, I'm ready to get this shit over with. Mike: This is going to be embarassing. Brett: Your telling me! Mike: Lets just hope this doesnt go on the news! Brett: No shit! Brett walks out of his room as Mike follows shutting the door behind him. They walk down the flight of stairs and outside the hotel where the limo awaits on there arrival. Brett: (Looks at the Limo)....That is a shitty limo man! Mike: Sorry its the only one Xaiver had left to spare I guess. Brett: Look at that shit, the front two tires are spares, the back rims are rusted making it look like someone shit on them. Look at the damn windows Half of them are tinted and the other half isn't and look at the god damn paint job. Its not even all the same color. Mike: Quit complaining, its just something to get is to the airport. Brett: Damn thats going to be embarassing. Brett walks over to the limo and opens the door, it makes a screeching noise as he opens it, Brett stands there a frowns for a minute then jumps in the limo. Mike follows and shuts the door after getting in. The limo drives off out of site as it squicks.
