Flare Up In Here

1 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Jan. 1,2003 [The segment opens in a murky hallway. We can't see much. We then hear loud noises. Something is ringing out like blind gunshots in the night. The camera round a corner to reveal a ring. Some young kid is bumping in it. He's taking back bumps, front bumps, belly bumps. Finally, the kid stops to take a breather and a drink from his water bottle. The kid looks up to see the camera. The kid just glances at it, and then begins to bump again. The camera man continues walking past the ring, only to reach a work-out room. There is someone in there. This person is doing some bench presses. The man stops, and grabs a few weights. He lifts them up and does some hammer curls with them. Finally, then man stops doing that after a few reps and goes to work on his abs. He does some crunches, and some bicycle crunches. The man finally stops. He gets to his feet and grabs a drink. The man looks off the mirrors in the room to see a camera man standing right behind him. The man turns around. The man is somewhat of a medium build. He has a goatee, and black hair. The man is still sweating after his work-out. Finally, someone else walks into the room. Someone with a UBC microphone. We have no clue who this man is, but he seems to be working with the UBC. The man walks up to Flare. This little interviewer is about 5'5', 140lbs. Flare shares a small grin, and awaits the man's first question.] Man - Charles, you're here in the UBC, how does it feel? [Flare glances down at the man. He looks around at the work-out room, then takes another swig from his water bottle. Charles swallows the water and looks down once again. The man begins to shake a little, perhaps scared of Flare, perhaps just nervous. The man holds the mic. to his mouth and proceeds to ask Flare again.] Man - Charles... ummmm... Charles.... how does it feel to be here in the UBC? [Flare stares the little man. Flare takes another gulp of the water. The stare from Flare continues to scare this little man. The little man lifts the mic. up so that Flare can talk into it. Flare finally speaks up.] Flare - Well, let me tell you something, if you hadn't been paying any sort of attention, you woulda realized. You know what you woulda realized? You would have noticed that I, Charles Flare was already in the UBC. I've been in the UBC since day one, jackass. [The man looks blankly at Flare. This little announcer must know nothing about the UBC, but yet he manages to get a job with them. The man holds the mic. to his mouth yet again. He looks up at Flare, and proceeds to ask the next question.] Man - Ummmm... sorry, but I don't remember anyone working for the UBC under your name, Charles. Sorry. [At this point, Flare begins to look frusterated. Almost at the point of explosion. Flare then calms down. The man holds the mic. to Flare's mouth once again. Flare stares down the little man once again. It's almost too painful for even the cameraman to watch. The little man looks scared, he looks fearful of Flare. What would Flare do to this little man? Would he harm him?] Flare - What the hell. You don't remember anyone under my name? You don't recall the best damn announcer this side of the e-wrestling world has ever seen? HUH? No? Why not? You new to the business? HUH? Answer me! [The man brings the mic. back down to his mouth to answer.] Man - I'm sorry, I really am. I'm brand new, just leave me alone, please. [Flare looks at the man and grabs the mic. away. Flare then pushes the little man out of the camera's view. Flare then begins to talk.] Flare - Honestly, what the hell was that. The UBC sends in some jackass newbie to fill us in on my situation and what happens? He doesn't know who I am? I mean, COME ON! You honestly think that these people don't know of me? They don't know who I am? Well, if they don't, then screw you. Because you need to realize that I am the next upcoming thing on this whole damn roster. You think that this little UBC has some huge stars, wait, just wait until I begin to excersize my pain. Wait until I begin hurting others, just wait, because when I come around, there wont be ain't no stoppin' this train. [Flare takes a drink of water. He then puts the bottle back down and begins to talk once again.] Flare - Just because I ain't wrestled one single match here in the UBC doesn't mean that I don't know how to win. It doesn't mean that I don't know how to kick ass, and it doesn't mean that I don't know how to have a good time. Hell, I may be Ehren Sim's little protege, but let me tell you something, he's damn lucky that he picked me up. Imagine what Sims would be like without me. He would be boring, washed up, and lame. No one wants to see that! That's when I come in. Me, the man of so many different opinions. But, really, folks, that's what it's all about. Opinionating. [Flare grabs his water bottle and chugs down the remaining bit of water that remains, he then tosses it into the garbage. Flare then begins to speak once again.] Flare - You can go off and send these no-talent, no-knowledge bums to interview me, but i'll keep throwing em' out on their ass. They're not worthy to talk to me if they don't even know my name. But soon, the whole entire UBC roster will know my name, they'll realize that they signed the wrong man. They'll realize that pain isn't just pain anymore. Because i'll hurt you so many times, you'll have to invent new ways to scream. There will be no stoppin' me. [The little man walks over to Flare and grabs the mic. away. Flare doesn't know what to do know. He looks like he might just snap on the interviewer. Flare walks over to the little man, and grabs him by the collar of his shirt.] Flare - What the hell is your problem, little man? If you wanted the mic., why didn't you just ask politely? Now, you've left me no choice. No choice but to hurt you. You understand, little man? No one disrespects Charles Flare more than once. And these wrestlers will learn that too, they'll soon realize that it'll be all about respect in a hurry. [Flare goes to punch the man right in the face, but some off-camera crew run in and break it up. Flare is slowly pulled away from the interviewer. Flare backs off and plays along. The crew let him go. Flare then runs at the interviewer and smashes him right in the face with a huge right hand. The interviewer falls to the ground. Flare then clearly goes a bit insane and begins shouting.] Flare - RESPECT! RESPECT! YOU'LL ALL LEARN ABOUT IT! YOU'LL ALL LEARN! [Flare then grabs a weight and tosses it at a mirror, shattering it upon impact. Flare then makes his exit by walking into his locker room area. You can still hear Flare yelling as he walks in.] Flare - RESPECT! [The camera then pans over the the interviewer. From what we can tell, the interviewer is a bloody mess. Possibly a broken nose, maybe something else. The crew begin to help the interviewer up. The camera man then pans over to the broken mirror shards.] [WHAT DOES THE FUTURE HOLD FOR FLARE AND THE UBC?] ---- Back
