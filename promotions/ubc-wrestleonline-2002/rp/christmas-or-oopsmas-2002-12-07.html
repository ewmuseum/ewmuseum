Christmas or Oopsmas??

7 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12.7.02 Johnny NightMare sits wearing a white suit with his hair spiked. He sits behind a desk and seems to be normal. That is until he speaks. Bianca sits in the background doing some sprucing up of stuff in the background. Johnny NightMare: As the planet earth month of December arrives each year, you humans have a strange 'holiday' which you prefer to 'celebrate'. This strange occasion is known as 'Christmas'. I have yet to meet this 'Christ' person that the holiday references, but should I meet him, I guarantee that he shall kneel before OOPS! Should he not, he will pay for his defiance with his life. Then again, most humans have informed me that this 'Christ' is nothing more than a fabrication of your puny minds, and that the 'holiday' is all about commercialism and greed. Well at least it is an evil holiday, so I approve of that. But the name of the holiday must go. From now on, you shall refer to it as 'OOPSmas'. Anybody heard speaking the name 'Christmas' shall be killed without hesitation. Now then, while this 'Christ' character is a farce, I have heard many tales of a man by the name of 'Santa Claus' who travels to your homes every Christmas to bring you 'presents' for being 'good little boys and girls'. WHO IS HE TO JUDGE WHETHER OR NOT YOU HAVE BEEN GOOD OR BAD??? I DEFY YOU SANTA CLAUS! Johnny holds up a picture of Santa Claus lying in a pool of blood and surround by police tape. As you can see, I heard that this 'Santa Claus' was making an appearance at a local mall. So I met him there, and ended the matter. I must ask you humans a question however: Why do you choose to celebrate an obese man in a hideous suit who has the stench of cheap liquor on his breath? The odor was so strong that even I, the great Oops, chose to cover my nose while ending his life. Revolting. Your Santa Claus is dead and I roasted his reindeer over an open fire. From this day forth, I am your new Santa. SANTA OOPS! As your new Santa, I have decided to get into the 'Oopsmas spirit' by participating in some of the very same rituals that you, my slaves, partake in each year. The first ritual is the lighting of a 'Oopsmas Tree'. Though the electrical light bulbs which you string across a tree may light it up slightly, nothing lights up a tree better than my powerful eye-lasers.Kneel before me Oopmas Tree! I have never seen a tree that glowed so brightly. Therefore, I declare my tree to be the greatest Oopsmas tree EVER. Kneel before it! Next on the list, I noticed that you humans appear to be obsessed with using a product known as 'ginger'. I had a slave, who claimed she was an expert on the creation of 'gingerbread houses', help me with the creation of one. Constructing the house proved to be quite easy, but that is to be expected when you are the ruler of all that exists. I must say, I felt that her gingerbread house was not a worthy tribute to me. It even had a Santa Claus picture on it. Naturally, I killed her for her defiance. My gingerbread house, however, was flawless. Rather than use mere 'icing' as she chose to use, I used tar. I believe the tar gives my gingerbread house the viewer a feeling of 'helplessness' and 'impending doom', both of which fit in perfectly with the holiday season. Once again, your ruler has done it. I, Johnny Oops NightMare have created the greatest gingerbread house EVER. I wasn't going to stop there though. Since I had proven to be a bread of ginger master, I decided to make what you humans call a 'gingerbread man'. Now, surely none of you are worthy of the title of 'man' in my eyes, so I have created the world's first 'gingerbread slave'. Behold! Johnny tosses down the picture of the dead Santa and now picks up a picture with a gingerbread man with a sad face and no legs. Johnny NightMare: Look at the hopelessness in his eyes! The fear! This gingerbread slave knows who his rightful leader is... OOPS! Furthermore, I'm sure you have noticed that my gingerbread slave has no legs. This is my merciful way of allowing the slave to live, so that he will always be able to kneel before me. If you have no legs, you cannot stand. If you cannot stand, you can only kneel. If you only kneel, I shall allow you to live. Any gingerbread slave that dares to grow legs shall receive the ultimate punishment... CONSUMPTION! And finally, since you human slaves were so accustomed to having the defiant Santa Claus visit you each year, I have decided to take his place. I, Santa Oops, shall make a list. I shall check it twice. I shall find out who has been naughty or nice. Santa Oops is coming to town. For those of you who have kneeled before me, I shall give you the gift of your very own gingerbread slave. For those of you who haven't been kneeling, I shall drop a 3-ton boulder on your homes and then use the flesh of your bodies to create more gingerbread slaves! Remember my slaves, the only way I will deliver you these gingerbread slave treats, is if you continue to dedicate your life to me as you...MUAHAHAHHAHAHHA...MUAHAHAHHAHAHA Johnny continues to laugh and Bianca who was listening to Johnny in the past bit slams him in the head with a vacuum. Johnny falls to the desk and then Bianca pulls out a cell phone. Bianca dials her cell phone and then puts it to her her and converses. Bianca: Hi, Dr. Johnson? Trainer: Yes. Bianca: Johnny was just talking about taking over Christmas... Trainer: Oh...how many times have you hit him. Bianca: 46 including the one I just did because he was scarying me. Trainer: I see. Well do you have the bag I gave you when we last met? Bianca: Yea. Trainer: In it is some southwestern rice. Bianca: What!?! Trainer: Yes southwestern rice. That is an advancement in the curing process. Bianca: Ok. So what should I do? Trainer: Give it to him every so often. Maybe twice every 4 days. Bianca: Ok. What about the freaky Christmas rants? Trainer: Hopefully it will go away. Bianca: Alright bye then. Trainer: You know my number so if the rice doesn't work call me. Bianca hangs up and puts the cell phone in her pocket. Johnny still lays on the desk out cold. Bianca goes back to cleaning after kissing Johnny on the head. The scene goes to some public access commercials and Johnny is sitting at a table while Bianca is cooking on the stove. Johnny has tons of crayons by his hand and is coloring in a Christmas colouring book. Johnny NightMare: Bianca..I DON'T LIKE CHRISTMAS!! Bianca: Why not hunny? Johnny NightMare: Well The Charlie Brown Christmas Special. Ooh!! I loathe this cloying TV show. I hate everything about it. I have never liked 'Peanuts' - Charles Schultz is a brain-dead unfunny pedophile - the staying power of this strip, and its spin-off TV specials further reinforces my concept of humanity as destructive sheep. The music of this show is enough to make me flee the room, if not the house; the adenoidal voices of the characters gets my molars grinding; the 'message' is depressing and shallow. In a just world, dedicated culture enthusiasts would, at their own expense, buy up all extant prints of this abomination and hold a celluloid bonfire: I would find this much more heartwarming than that horrible final sing-song around that crummy little tree. Hey, I can wish too, can't I? Bianca: I guess you can. But Johnny why do you hate Christmas so much, you used to love it. Johnny NightMare: Rudolph the red nosed reindeer. The TV show, with cheap puppets and lame-assed music, just pads the nasty underlying message out to half an hour: what kind of message does this send to kids? The only way to make the vicious spiteful herd like you is to be of use to the power structure (Santa) - you're 'loved' if you're useful, shunned if you're different: moral --- 1) different is bad; 2) society is shallow and venal and you should still want to belong. No thank you. I have been disturbed by this story since I was small --- I didn't fit in then and didn't want to either. Bianca: I guess your right... Here Johnny have some rice. Bianca hands Johnny a bowl of southwestern rice and Johnny starts to eat. He starts to eat faster and faster until he is all done. Then Bianca takes away the bowl and gives Johnny back his book with crayons. Johnny looks at them confused. Johnny NightMare: Bianca, since when do we have crayons? Bianca: You told me to/// Bianca turns around and smiles. Johnny NightMare:And why aren't we decorated for Christmas? Such a lovely holiday not celebrated for? Bianca's smile brightens. Bianca: Oh we will decorate right after me and you have a...well a...wrestling match...yeah a wrestling match in the bed room. Johnny's grin appears. Johnny NightMare: Ok right away...and this wrestling match will be a naked match right? Bianca: Of course. Johnny NightMare: Nice. Bianca: But lets hurry please! Johnny hops up and grabs Bianca and lifts her up. He runs around a corner and the scene fades to black as the couple...well have se...a wrestling match in their room.
