MEET THE PRESS

9 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12/09/02 MEET THE PRESS . . . MEET THE PRESS . . . MEET THE PRESS . . .MEE THE PRESS . . . . The scene opens inside a large auditorium. The room is packed with reporters and other various members of the sports media. At the front of the auditorium is a stage. On top of the stage is a large wooden podium with a microphone, and behind the podium sits four chairs. There is a commotion from the crowd as Rosalind Thornbush (Mistress Rose), William Thornbush’s agent; Gene E. Prince and Gene’s assistant Anastasia Morgan walk into the room. All three are dressed in business attire. Gene and Anastasia take a seat while Rosalind goes to the podium. Camera flashes go off as Rosalind waits for the crowd noise to cease. Once the rabble settled, Rosalind begins to speak . . . Rosalind: “Unfortunately, my husband William Thornbush will be unable to attend today’s press conference.” Some members of the audience begin to get rowdy. Rick Metz, the sports reporter from the STATE newspaper, and stereotypical antagonistic evil reporter, stands up and says . . . Rick: “What’s this all about? Synnerster is the one that called this press conference.” Rosalind gives Rick Metz a ‘Sit your ass down’ look. Rick quickly takes notice and sits down without saying a word. Rosalind sucks her teeth and rolls her eyes and continues . . . Rosalind: “As I was saying before I was so rudely interrupted, my husband MrSynnerster will not be able to make it today in person, but we have him here via Satellite.” A large view screen descends from the ceiling. Once the screen is lowered a headshot of William appears on the screen. He has a shit-eating grin on his face. William: “Good afternoon ladies and gentlemen. Sorry I couldn’t be there in person. I had to scare up a little dinner.” Rick Metz stands up and says : “William. Considering we know you so well and from the looks of things this is going to just be a cheap rip off of the press conference from Queen of the Damned why don’t you just skip the theatrics and get to the point. I mean must you always do a promo where you refer to some pop culture movie, television show, or comic book. Your antics were funny when you were in the Canadian American Wrestling Alliance, and it was even amusing in the New Era Wrestling Federation, but now you’re just yesterday’s news. This is the UBC. Isn’t it time for something different.” William: “What is your name?” Rick: “Rick Metz.” William: “Well then Mr. Metz, I was going to entertain you and bring some happiness into your pathetic life Mr. Metz, but since you don’t want that Fuck you then. Cut the cameras off.” The screen goes blank. There is a loud murmuring amongst the crowd. Rick Metz is still standing there with a stunned look on his face. Many reporters’ frantically beginning scribing things down on pieces of paper and speaking into mini tape recorders. Suddenly the screen comes back on. This time William has a sour and evil look on his face. The crowd quiets back down. William: “Are you happy now Mr. Metz?” Rick is still in shock of the prior events and says nothing. William: “Were you expecting me to end this press conference did you Mr. Metz?” Rick slowly shakes his head no. William: “Would you say I shocked you?” Rick nods his head in the affirmative. William: “Good. Now sit your ass down. And just to let you know (His voice gets high pitched.) “I was just bullshitting. And you know that mannnnnnnn. The crowd laughs. William: “Mr. Metz maybe the reason you don’t like my pop culture refers is because you’re just too dumb to get them. So maybe you should do like that other wrestling league and get the ‘F’ Out!” People in the crowd begin to applause. William: “And to the rest of the audience I’d like you to enjoy this next Pop Culture movie rip-off I’m about to do.” The screen goes black as suddenly the sounds of a drum pounding come from the back of the room. Everyone turns around to see a hood wearing man in a red hooded sweatshirt (Sidenote: Make your own Adam Sandler joke here.) gray joking pants, and black Sneakers. On the front of the sweatshirt was the name ALI in black letters. The man has an Africa drum strapped around the front of his chest. The man pulls off his hood to reveal himself to be William Thornbush. William bags on the drums with his hands and yells. . . William: “The CHAMP is here!” (Drum drum drum drum drum) “The CHAMP is here!” William continues to drum and chant on his way to the stage. Rosalind takes a seat in one of the chairs, allowing William to stand behind the podium. He puts the drum down beside the podium. William: “For those of you wondering. I was ripping off this movie right here. William points to the word ALI on his shirt. William: “I know some of y’all like Mr. Metz here are kinda slow on the uptake so I have to help you out when I can.” The Crowd laughs. William: “Now I want to get down to business and get a few things straight. Even though last week was an off week for me, I still I proved that Brad Douglas is not on my level. What does this make Mr. Douglas? The 3rd, 4th time you’ve lost to me. I saw your little promo the other day. All I can say is you damn right you’re cursed. I bet you have 000 the mark of the loser on burned on the back of your head. Damn it, why must I be forced to waste precious moments of my life talking about that douchbag. Next question.” A tall, dark hair, a Caucasian gentleman wearing a dark blue suit stands up with his hand in the air. William points to him and says . . . “Yes sir. Your question.” Man: “Clark Kent from the Daily Planet. What are your plans here in the UBC?” William: “That’s a SUPER question Mr. Kent?” Kent: “Thank you. I consider myself somewhat of a super kind of man.” William: “I’m sure you do. And to answer your question, I’m here to make the UBC my playground. I am called many things. The Ambassador of Entertainment because I entertain my fans. The SINNERS as I like to call them. I am called the Clown Prince of wrestling because if you haven’t noticed I like to joke and have a good time. But most importantly, I am called the The Postman because I always deliver 5 star matches. You all saw that Saturday when I took a worthless slug like Mr. Douglas, and turn the situation into a high quality match, and I’m going to do the same with Mr. Noid.” Kent: “Mr. Noid?” William: “Yes. Mr. Noid. As in Para-noid.” Kent: “Do you call everyone by their last name?” William: “Yes I do. He made fun of his own damn self there. And that’s about enough out of you. (People snicker.) “Anyone else have a question?” A young black haired Caucasian man with a goatee and glasses stands up. William points to him signaling the man to ask his question. Man 2: “Gregory Helms from PWI magazine. Citizen Synnerster, in your last you said Xavier Morningstar begged you to join the UBC, Wuz-up-wit-dat?” William: “Oh, it’s true, it’s true. I knew Mr. Morningstar from back in the day, so when his brainchild the UBC was being conceived I was the first person he called to join. At first I said no thanks. I have too many other projects going on right now. I can’t do it. A week later he asked again, and again I turned him down. From there things got bad. He started showing up at my other job and at my home. It was getting ridiculous. Then one day he showed up on his hands and knees crying like a feigning crackhead looking for money. He told me he’d suck my dick if I joined the UBC. I told him I’d join up if he stops stalking me. He agrees and the deal was made. Then he asked if he could suck my dick anyway, and I slammed the door in his face.” Helms: “So Citizen Synnerster, do you think it’s wish to make fun of your current employer?” William: “Funny you should mention that. X called me yesterday and said he thought my last promo was funny except for the part about him. He said other presidents may have let me get away with making fun of them, but he’s not going to stand for that. I told him to kiss my ass, and he giggled like a little schoolgirl and said he’d be over in a second. I hung up the phone on him. Moral of the story is I don’t give a rat’s behind if I piss off Xavier or his girlfriend Ehren. What’s the worse they can do to me? Fire me? I have more jobs than the Jamaican family from In Living Color. Helms you look familiar? We’re you the kicker for the Miami Hurricanes?” With a stunned look on his face, Helms curses to himself. Helms: “Shit, my cover is blown. MrSynnerster is more diabolical than I thought. Must rethink game plan.” Helms spins around, strikes a pose and runs out the room make whooshing noises along the way. A short chunky gray haired man stands up and asks. . . Man 3: “Kent Brockman from the Springfield Gazette. What do you think of your opponent for the upcoming match Saturday?” William: “Ahh yes. The Ragin’ Cajin Mr. Noid. I’m sorry, but with a name like Paranoid does not seem to strike fear into my heart. What the fuck is this guy paranoid about? Is he paranoid that he’s going to get his ass handed to him by me? Is he paranoid that I’m out to get him? Is he paranoid that he will tap out to the Burning Sensation? Or maybe he’s paranoid that after the fight with me, Ehren and Xavier or going to use his lifeless body for unspeakable sexual debauchery? All these things will happen, so I guess Mr. Noid has a reason to be paranoid. Next question. Rick Metz stands up one last time and says “MrSynnerster hasn’t this whole press conference just been another example of your use of pop culture references and humor to get over on the crowd. William: “And what would you rather I do Mr. Metz. Do an interview in my trailer park home, and whine about being in hell. Oh maybe I should walk the means streets of my suburb home and talk about being an O. G. from the hood. Oh maybe I should do an interview that rivals the charisma of a Test interview. Or maybe I should quit wrestling all together and because a bitter sports critic like you.” Rick Metz: “Maybe you’re just using your weak jokes to make up for your lack of wrestling skills.” William jumps off the stage into the crowd. He grabs Rick Metz by the collar of his shirt head butts him then bites off his ear and spits it into the crowd. Rick falls to the ground clutching his ear. William returns to the podium, grabs the mic and say. “This is ludicrous. I’m the greatest fighter of all time. I’ll eat your children Rick. This interview is over.” William backs away from the podium and he and his friend begin to take their leave as people continue to call out William’s name and ask questions and cameras continue to flash. The scene ends. . . .
