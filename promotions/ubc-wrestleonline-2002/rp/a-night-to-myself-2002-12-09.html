' A Night to Myself '

9 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12-9-02 The stinch of mexican sweat floated around in a small bar just down from 'The Pit' at Buquerque, New Mexico were the next Jurisdiction will take place. The room was very hot, very humid, and very crowded. Suddenly the crowd gets silence as they stare at the figure stepping inside the bar. The short mexicans look up at the huge man as he walks straight to the bar as the figure walks the floor creeks and dust shoots slowly off the ground after each step. The man is Brett Wayne, he's head is still bandage up from the falls to the conrete and his hands still taped up from the shatter glass he landed on. Brett sits down and points to a bottle of whiskey sitting on the top shelve. The mexican bartender slowly gets the bottle of whiskey and hands it to him, Brett pours the whiskey in a dirty shot glass and takes a shot, he then slams it hard back onto the bar. Thats just what I need to ease the pain. Brett pours another shot as everyone attention is still on him. He turns his slowly to look at the people behind him and then turns it back taking the other shot. What the hell are all those dirty mexicans looking at? Is it my cuts on my arms... Is it the bandage on my head... Or is it the tape around my hands... Or is it the fact that they're just scared of me... Who knows about those damn mexicans, I would ask one of them what there problem is but It wouldn't matter. I wouldn't understand a word they would say anyways, besides I don't want any trouble tonight its only been two days since the last fight all I want is one more shot and I'm out of here. Brett pours one last shot and drinks it quickly, he then stands up and throws an american twenty dollar bill down on the bar and turns around. The bartender says something that you can't understand and moments later a man the same size as Brett walks out and blocks the exit. I don't want to fight damnit, I just want to leave and get a good nights rest so I can go back to my hard work but Nooo! I have a bunch of stinky mexicans wanting to cause problems. Brett continues to talk to himself as he stares eye to eye at the Mexican Monster. Brett then takes off his shirt revealing the cuts, scratch's, and bruises on his back. He moves his kneck side to side popping it. He pops his knuckles and makes a fist but the other man backs off revealing the exit after seeing the battle marks on Bretts body. Brett lets out a deep breath as he picks his shirt back up whiping the dust off of it and he slips it back on and walks past the man staring at him the whole time. When he makes it outside the warm humid air blew swiftly against him as he walked. Dust crowded his vision as lightning echoed through the warm humid air. Rain begins to slowly pour down from the black clouds in the sky as the lightning strikes once again making the earth rumble. Brett continues walking against the wind as he continues talking to himself. These storms don't scare me just like I wasn't scared of that damn mexican back there. Just like I'm not scared of Violent B and Brad Douglas. Violent B and Brad Douglas will see who the true fighters are coming this saturday. They both should be scared of me, I'm even scared of me sometimes. I can't control what I do in a fight, thats what makes me the most dangerous in the UBC. Everyone seen what happened in that warehouse at Las Cruces, NM. It was 'Simply Insane' Now I can just see me and Nightmare on the same side. We will dominate the UBC and the time will come for me and him to prove it. We will take down anyone who disagrees with us or gets in our way. We will make sure of it... Brett continues speaking to himself until a car pulls up next to him as dust follows making the it hard to see the driver, a familer voice is heard as Brett opens the door. You ready? The dust blocks the view of the person as Brett nods his head yes and jumps in the car slamming the door shut. The car drives off out of site.
