Out of the Corner of my Wrathful Eye

28 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/28/02 The whole thing was sort of funny, in a way, if you thought about it, and all of a sudden I did something I shouldn’t have. – Page 134 of Chapter 17 The larger guy swung at Devin, but he simply ducked and then used all his strength to see if he could take this abuser to the ground. He was able to do just that. Then, for what appeared to be forever but was merely a few seconds, everything was in slow motion. The guy’s breath shot out of him in a big hurry. A few laughs emanated from the spectators across the street. The girls were watching with a sense of relief, yet a sense of awe and pity. His friends were about to do something, but then, as Devin’s eyes caught them in their advancing steps, they stopped. His face showed the fiery rage bubbling inside of him, and they didn’t want any part of it. With her lying on the ground, staring at Devin, he knew that she appreciated what he had just done. This man, who didn’t know her, was protecting her. As he looked back down at this guy, he saw that he caught his breath and was about to fight back. It was too late to just get up and walk away now. Someone was going to have to get hurt. And, if Devin had anything to say about it, it was going to be… “Don’t touch her again you sick son of a bitch!” This hatred. This black. Your sin. My wrath. Devin’s fists smashed the guys face. Once. Twice. Thrice. Blood splashed out from his lips, mixing with his saliva on the pavement of the sidewalk. Devin only stared down, witnessing the agony that he had just caused. Wrath had just taken its course on this guy. Devin got to his feet, and helped her up to hers. She looked up at him, physically thanking him with the glow in her eyes. He was all serious and hate filled only seconds ago. Now he was able to block all that out, and smile back at her. She returned that smile with one of her own. Out of the corner of his eyes, flashes of blue and red ripped through the black sky. Devin turned around, through the parking lot of make shirt automobiles, and ran towards the buildings off in the distance. He ran, and ran, and then, after a good three blocks of distance between him and the law, ran some more. He finally stopped behind an alley of what appeared to be a rundown, old building. He stopped, leaned up against the wall, and caught his breath, his misty gasps emanating in the darkness. The city was silent, with only the occasional human sound echoing through the air and the hum of the wind traveling through the sky. His thoughts drifted back to his actions, but he wasn’t upset. Devin knew that what he had just done was all right, and God would understand. Love at first sight, he thought of it. Maybe a little to naïve for someone of his stature, but that didn’t matter. Fuck ‘em. Devin turned to walk away, when all of a sudden he heard the sounds of foot steps behind him, getting closer in their emanation. And as he turned…
