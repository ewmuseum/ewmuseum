'Another day of Insanity' Pt 1

28 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11-28-02 Have I gone Insane? Why Can't I get ahold of myself? Do I need help? Is there anyone out there who can? Who can answer my questions? Brett continues to ask himself these questions as Mike sits next to him just watching him and worried not knowing how is brother will act when he gets in a match. 'He's going Insane, he could kill someone if he doesnt snap out of it.' Mike says to himself as he stands up and shakes Brett. 'Snap out of it man, your scaring me' Brett looks up at Mike 'Snap out of what, what am I in to snap out of' 'Oh my, he really has lost it' Mike sits back down and begins thinking to himself. 'What the hell should I do now, he's gonna snap like he did at that steak house, theres no doubt about that but I'm just afraid he'll kill someone with him acting like that' Mike looks at Brett and he is still sitting there asking those same questions. Have I gone Insane? Why Can't I get ahold of myself? Do I need help? Is there anyone out there who can? Who can answer my questions? 'I can't handle this shit anymore' Mike gets up and is about to leave then finally Brett snaps out of it. 'Hey, where are you going' Brett says turned around looking at Mike. Mike looks down to the ground then looks back up at Brett 'I don't know, away from you thats for sure' Mike walks out as Brett sits there scratching his head 'What did I do? No telling about him, he is losing it I think' Brett gets up and walks to kitchen. 'Lets see what to cook, I'm kind of hungry' Brett stands there and thinks for a minute. 'I'll just order some pizza, I dont feel like cooking' Brett grabs his cell phone and dials a number... 'I'd like two large pizza's with everyone you have on it' ....................................................................................... 'That will be fine, how much?' ........................................................................................ 'Twenty even, ok I'll have a check written out for you when you get here, thanks' -CLICK- 'Pizza sounds good right now, they better hurry with it.' Brett walks back to the living room and sits down turning on the tV with the remote. 'Wonder if there is anything good on' Brett clicks through the channels not finding anything decent, he throws the remote down and gets up. Damn tV, what the hell did we get it for if nothings ever on it. The sound of a door opens and Mike walks in 'Look what I found' Mike pulls on a paper and on the front page is Virus in the steak house with his hand on that young waitress. 'You see what you have caused, we probley have a law suit on us now' Brett throws the paper on the ground stomping on it. 'I'll kill that women if that happens' Mike looks up at Brett and just stares into his eyes as Brett stares back. 'What you have a problem with that' Brett sayed with anger running through his veins. Mike see's something is going through Bretts mind but he can't quite tell just yet. Mike starts asking Brett questions. 'What the hell is going through your mind right now' Brett turns around and looks at MIke. 'Well.....alot of things actually. One of them is about the match next saturday and the other is my pizza I just ordered. Mike looks down to the ground trying to think of something else to ask him. Mike then looks back up at Brett and puts his hand on his shoulder 'Now damnit I know something is wrong, now what is it' Brett smiles and looks at Mike 'Nothing is wrong with me Bro, I'm just fine trust me, I may go off sometimes but thats only when people piss me off and I just go nuts, Im not called 'Simply Insane' Brett Wayne for nothing. -KNOCK-- -KNOCK- 'Oh that must be the pizza' Brett hurry and runs to the door and opens it as he opens the door the Pizza guy drops the Pizza's. 'Damnit I've had a very bad day I'm sorry' says the Pizza man. Brett's face starts turning red again 'You dropped my god damn pizza's, now thats going to cost you' Brett grabs the guys shirt collar and picks him up off the ground. 'You should've been more careful' The pizza guys starts screaming for help and Mike hears the poor guy and runs over to help. 'Oh no not again, BRETT put him down now' Mike tries and tries to get him to put down the poor guy but he doesnt listen. 'I'm fucking starving and you dropped my pizza damnit' Brett says as he is about to throw the poor guy down on the ground. 'Oh so thats what happened, its only a damn pizza and besides its still good...look' Brett puts down the guy and looks at the pizza. 'Hey it is still good, sorry guy. Here take this' Brett hands the guy a twenty and the guy runs back to his car. 'I bet i scared him to death' Brett smiles and picks up the pizza putting it back into the box and walks to the table and sits down as Mike follows. 'You have to quit that shit, scaring people like that' says Mike. Brett acts like he doesn't hear him as he stuffs the pizza down his throat 'this is some good stuff' he says as he continues to stuff the pizza down his throat. Mike finally gives up as he walks back to his room and locks the door while Brett is still sitting there eating his pizza, he then gets up and gets him a glass of water and goes back to stuffing pizza down his throat pizza. -Fade 2 Black-
