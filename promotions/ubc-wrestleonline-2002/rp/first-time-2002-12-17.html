First Time

17 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
A long white limo pulls up outside the wharehouse and the 5ft6' driver gets out of the 20 feet long limo and walk along side the 20 foot limo and opens the door and out steps the 'Livin Legend' Troy Mclean. Troy gets out and looks around at the rough looking wharehouse. After he is finished looking around he heads inside the wharehouse and the driver lifts out Troy's kit bag from the rear of the limo and follws Troy inside the wharehouse. They get to the entrance and a security guards ask Troy Mclean for they ID to get access to the wharehouse and the backstage area. Troy starts chatting to the sercurity guards. Troy Mclean:: Hey you guys dont you know who i am? Security guard:: Yes i know Troy who you are but we are stepping up security at all wharehouses all over America for your saftey Troy its you who we are trying to keep safe you know. We wouldnt like nothing to happen to the Livin Legend himself. Troy Mclean:: Well guys i have a security card but this is my Bulter and he wouldnt have a card but will he be able to gain access to the wharehouse via my pass? Security guard:: Im sorry Troy but your bulter wont be able to get access unless Xavier Moringstar or President Kron or Ehren Sims gives him access to get inside the wharehouse, Sorry! Troy Mclean:: Oh... well shite happens sorry, Paul but you will have to go back to the limo because you cant get access to the wharehouse i will sort you out with an access card to get you into these can of places so i dont have to carry my own bags!! Troy takes the kit bag from the bulter and shows the security guards his backstage pass. They give him access but they check his kit bag for security reasons. They find nothing and Troy heads into the main building. He meets a few UBC superstars on his way to his locker room. He makes his way down a corridor turns right through another corridor and finally finds his locker room. Troy stands outside and looks at the door and just thinks how long he has waited to get back to this business. Troy walks inside and sets down his kit bag and gos for a shower then comes out and opens his kit bag and lifts out some clothes. He lifts out a pair of royal blue track suit bottoms and puts them on, he then looks through the bag and lifts out a back t-shirt with has UBC at the top and in the middle it says Beat Down in green writing and has Troy Mclean printed on the back of the t-shirt. He then closes the kit bag and puts it in the locker conpartment. Then opens the door and walks out again looking at his name printed on the door. Troy makes his way towards the fighting ring. Troy gets to the black curtain leading to the ringside area and he gets a flash back to the match 6 months when he got the cartilege injury and left him out for six months when his was part of BMW. Troy turns back towards his locker room and a black figure appears. The figure starts talking to Troy. Black figure:: Troy you will have to go out there sometime and get rid of your fear! What are you scared of anyway you wouldnt have to fight just look around get the feeling of the place before Jurdiction this Saturday night. You have a great career in front of you just go out there! Troy walks out to get the feeling of the wharehouse because Troy has never done anything like this before! Troy walks into the ring and walks around a little bit and climbs up on to the top rope *pretends to salute to the crowd with clunched fist and that crowd go wild and starts chanting his name around the wharehouse! Then Troy gets off the top rope and looks area again walk back up the ramp nad throught o the backstage.Again the black figure appears.... Black figure:: See Troy i told you before you went out there that you had nothing to fear. You will make big in the UBC just like you did in BMW but now the UBC is the best place for you Troy! Troy Mclean:: My next worry will be when i have to step in to the ring for my first match back my from my injury. Black Figure:: Troy never worry you will be fine just put to the back of your head and you will be fine! The black figure disappears just as quick as it appears and Troy walk off thinking about getting into the ring for his first match back and first match in the UBC with Nick Walker. He gets to his locker room andd lifts out his kit bag from the locker and heads out of the wharehouse and gets into his limo and shouts to the driver... Troy Mclean:: Get me out of this place!
