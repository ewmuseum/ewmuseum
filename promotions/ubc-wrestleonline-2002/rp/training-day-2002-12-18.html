Training Day

18 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12/18/02 The scene opens with Anastasia Morgan, Rosalind and William Thornbush driving down I-26 in Anastasia’s dark green Infinity. It is early in the morning. The sun is just now beginning to break over the horizon giving off an orangish red light. Driving the car is Anastasia dressed in a loose white UBC tank top with a black sports bra underneath, black biker shorts with a big white stripe down the sides, white socks and white Nike running shoes. Her jet-black is tied in a ponytail with a white scrunchy. William is slumped over half asleep in the passenger seat wearing gray nylon pants with a white UBC T-shirt, gray MrSynnerster sweatshirt from his CWA days, over the Tee, black Adidas and a white headband. Rosalind is stretched out in the back seat watching a portable combo TV/VCR. She is dressed in a black sports bra, black gym shorts, and white Keds shorts. The current program Rosalind is watching is a tape of the most recent UBC interviews. As she watches the interviews Rosalind says, Rosalind: “Does anyone else work in the UBC besides Tommy Grady and Brett Wayne? What happened to everyone else? Maybe you should be fighting one of them just so you could have some competision.” William: “I can’t worry about Wayne Grady. I have 2 other people on my shit list I have to deal with first. ” Anastasia: “What are you stressing about? You’re just wrestling two jobbers. You’re a former world champion. Winning this thing should be no problem.” William: “I’m not exactly worried about it. It’s more of, I want to make a really good impression. I wanna show Xavier Morningstar he can’t push me around. First he hires me, then tries to censor what I say. Fuck that. I’ll show him who really runs the show.” Rosalind: “I’m surprised he hasn’t said anything about you yet!” William: “That’s because he knows better. Morningstar knows I’ll bitch slap him. Then again he’d probably like that sort of thing. But enough about him. Turn up the radio.” William turns on the radio. Butterfly by Crazy town is play and soon Anastasia his bobbing her head and dancing to the music. Rosalind does the same in the backseat, while William leans back in his chair and goes to sleep. After about an hour drive the Infiniti slowly crawls down an old gravel driveway. Old farm equipment and broken down cars align the sides of the driveway. William: “Are you sure we’re at the right place?” At the end of the driveway were impaled baby doll heads on wooden stakes. Many heads with one eye missing. Anastasia: “Oh yeah. We’re in the right place.” Rosalind: “You have got to be kidding.” Sitting atop a hill is an old rundown shack of a house. The foundation is crumbling, so the house is slanted. Beside the house is a small pen with two barking pit bulls in it. Camouflage netting adorns the rooftop and well as a couple of armless and legless baby doll bodies hanging by nooses from the gutters. A tattled American flag flaps in the morning breeze. An old rusty barbwire fence surrounds to entire yard. William: “Damn this looks like something straight out of a horror movie.” Anastasia: “No kidding. Ummm . . . I’m going to stay in the car. I don’t wanna slow you down.” Rosalind: “You, honey, you go handle your business.” William: “Oh hell no. If this is a horror movie, you know the black man always gets it first.” Anastasia: “Yeah, he goes just minute before the nerdy white girl is hatched to pieces too.” Rosalind: “Excuse me, but the nerdy white girl always saves the day in a horror movie. Can we say Nancy from Nightmare on Elm Street. And the chick from the Scream movies was pretty nerdy too. Hell even a couple of brothers have been living through some of the new horror movies, but name one horror movie where the sista lived.” Anastasia: “Damn, you got us on that.” Rosalind: “That’s right. So you two go, and I’ll keep the car running.” The trio sits in the car for about 15 minutes debating on whether or not to go inside. Finally after much deliberation, Rosalind won her case, and William and Anastasia make their way up to the porch and knock on the door. William knocks on the door causing in to fall in. William: “Ain’t that a bitch.” Anastasia: “This is messed up. How does Rose always win these kind of arguments?” William: “I ask myself that same question sometimes.” They walk inside the house. The floor is so unsteady they are afraid they are going to fall in. The furniture inside is old and dusty, but it still looked like someone occupied the premises. Off in the distance, they could hear water dripping in the sink. William: “I don’t like the looks of this.” Anastasia: “You’re not the only one. Where the hell is this guy. He knows we’re coming.” They venture upstairs to the first door on the left. In the room they find someone lying in the bed with the covers pulled over their head. William:(whispering) “See who it is.” Anastasia: (whispering) “Fuck that. We’re here for you. You see who it is.” William:(sighing) “Okay.” (In his regular voice) “Hello? Uncle Pete, is that you?” The person in the bed doesn’t move. Anastasia: (Pushing William) “Go wake him up.” William gets a little closer. William: “Uncle Pete?” The person still doesn’t move. William reaches out to shake the person, but something grabs William’s leg. He looks down to see an arm coming from under the bed grab him. William is pulls off his feet, hitting the ground with a thud. As soon as he’s on the ground a man dressed in army fatigues with a black eye patch over one eye rushes from under the bed on top of William. Anastasia runs out the room screaming. The man puts his hand over William’s mouth. His other hand is a shiny silver hook, which he holds to William’s throat. Man: “Who sent you boy?” William: Mumble mumble Mumble(Will can’t talk because the man’s hand is over his mouth.) Man: “What do you say boy?” William: Mumble Mumble mumble.” Man: “Speak up boy, I can’t hear you.” William: “Mumble.” (Sighs and looks down at the man’s hand. The man removes his hand from William’s mouth.) “Thank you. Are you Uncle Pete?” Man: “How do you know my name boy.” William: “I’ll take that as a yes. Now get the fuck up off me. I’m MrSynnerster not Nightcrawler. I don’t like having another man on top of me. (Will pushes the man off of him. When the man hits the floor his hook gets caught in the floorboard. William gets to his feet looks at the man and says) “Need a ha. . ahh . . . let me help you. The man gives William an evil look. William helps the man to his feet. Then the man walks back and forth looking William up and down. As he walks his peg leg make a loud thumping sound. Uncle Pete (the man): “So you’re suppose to be the big wrestling superstar I’m suppose to train.” William: “I am more than just a super star. I’m an ICON! I am the Ambassador of Entertainment!!!” Uncle Pete: “What makes you think you’re a superstar? What have you ever done? Have you ever took a man’s head and scraped his face against the concrete.” William: “Nope.” Uncle Pete: “Have you ever powerbombed a man through a glass table?” William: “Can’t say I’ve done that one either.” Uncle Pete: “Have you ever stapled a man’s lips shut.” William: “That would be a no also.” Uncle Pete: “Have you ever knocked a man up, and jerked off on his face?” William: “Now that one is just straight up gay there.” Uncle Pete: “Well what the hell have you done.” William: “I’ve started a group named with a derogatory name. I’ve got a moved named after something you get from a diseased hooker. I make fun of The owner of the UBC, and I plan on continuing that. I’ve made fun of Karl Malone and have an ongoing feud with Kobe Bryant, and I seem to be a mainstay on our Stone Cold impersonating Color analysis’s Talk show. Oh and I have a Don King looking agent, and I seem to continually have fights with his fat ass niece Meka. That about sums me up.” Uncle Pete: “That sucks. What kind of wrestler are you? You’ll never get anywhere with that.” William: “Please, must I show off the T-shirts again? I’ve already promised the people in the UBC that I would not brag about being the first ACW television champion. Or brag about being ½ of the GWO tag team title, or brag about being the great CWA HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPION in history. Or talk about being the current NEW Regional Champion and INA Tag Champion. Don’t make me go back on my word. I’ve worked hard to get where I am. No one has hand. . ahh given me anything.” Uncle Pete: “I hate a smart ass.” William: “The feeling is mutual.” Uncle Pete: “But I’m going to whip you into shape. Make you a lean mean fighting machine. And do you know how I’m going to do it?” William: “I don’t know. You got me stump. . .ahh I have no idea.” Anastasia and Rosalind slowly walk into the room. Rosalind has a stun-gun in her hand and Anastasia has a tire iron. Anastasia: “I ahh, run. Umm. Back to the car to ahh,to get some help. Yeah, that’s it. Get some help.” Rosalind: “See honey. We we’re going to leave you hanging. William: “Uncle Pete. Meet my brave associates, Rosalind and Anastasia.” Uncle Pete looks at Anastasia with a funny glare in his eye. Uncle Pete: “I know exactly what we’re going to do to whip you into shape. Y’all got a car.” Anastasia: “Yeah why?” Uncle Pete: “Let’s take a little trip.” After a short drive the three end up in front of a hunting and fishing supply store in Lynchburg South Carolina.(Bet you’ll never guess how it’s name Lynchburg. And yes that is lynching as in hang.) Outside of the store are varies animal skins. There is an advertisement for shotgun shells in the window. A group of very rural looking men standing out front talking. All the men are dress in varies overalls, plaid shirts and hats with pictures of deer fish and rebel flags on them. William: “What the fuck are we doing here?” Uncle Pete: (With an evil grin on his face.) “You’ll see. Let’s get out the car.” As everyone gets out the car)“William did you ever read Kurt Angle’s book?” William: “A small piece of it. Then I was cured of my insomnia” Uncle Pete: “Well in the book, Angle talked about how he run uphill with his trainer on his back to get in shape.” William: “Man, I’m not running with you on my back. You’re hook might. . . never mind.” Uncle Pete: “No we’re not going to do that. You’re just going to run from here back to my house while these two little ladies keeps me company. (With his good hand, Uncle Pete grabs Anastasia’s butt.) Anastasia: “Hey, keep your meat hooks. . . I mean your hand. . . umm I mean. Don’t touch me.” Uncle Pete looks at Rosalind as he is about to grab her butt, but Rosalind give him an evil look and says. . . . Rosalind: “Don’t even think about it Captain Ahab.” Uncle Pete: “Don’t let the hook scare you baby. I can still hang with the best of them. William: “How is that going to get me into shape? You only live ten miles away. I run fifteen a daily basis.” Uncle Pete: “Oh this isn’t going to be as easy as you think. Yella girl, give your husband a kiss. And get in the car.” Rosalind kisses William on the lips, then gets behind the wheel of the car. As Rosalind and William were kissing, Uncle Pete whispers something to Anastasia. Anastasia: “What? Are you crazy?” Uncle Pete: “That’s why they call me Crazy One Eyed Uncle Pete. Just do it so we can go. It’s almost time for Judge Joe Brown, and I wanna get home to watch. Anastasia: “Okay if you say so.” Anastasia grabs William by the collar of his shirt, pulls him to her and gives him a long lingering kiss. While she is doing this the men group of men look on with contempt and anger. Anastasia releases from the kiss and gets in the car. Uncle Pete: (Sitting in the passenger side of the car with a big smile on his face.) “I told you it wasn’t going to be that easy. See you at the crossroads son. Let’s go, cuties.” The car speeds off down the road. A shocked William stands at the end of the road for a moment. William looks at the camera and says William: “It does take a genius to realize that a white woman kisses a black man in a town called LYNCHburg equals trouble.” (William looks back towards the store to see an angry mob coming towards him. He looks back at the camera.) “If I haven’t said it before, I’ll say it now. Oh this is some serious up bullshit! (He takes off running with the angry mob in pursuit.) The scene switches to a couple of hours later. Uncle Pete is sitting in an old rocking chair watching The Andy Griffith show on television. Anastasia and Rosalind are cuddled up on the couch taking a nap. A tattered and battered William stumbles into the room and collapses on the floor. Uncle Pete: “Did you have fun?” William: “I hate you more than anyone has ever hated anyone in the history of existence.” Uncle Pete: “What happened?” William: I had to fight off Bubba, Joe Bob, Billy Bob, Billy Joe, Joe Bob Billy Bob, and the rest of the gang before I could make it home.” Uncle Pete: “So you’d say you fought off what, 9 guys?” William: “More like 15 to 20. Uncle Pete: “So you fought off fifteen to twenty angry rednecks in Lynchburg South Carolina, and lived to tell about it. Son I’d say you’re ready for your match now.”
