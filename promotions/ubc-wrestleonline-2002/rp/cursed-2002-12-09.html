Cursed

9 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12/9/02 'Fuck that,Stitches are for little bitches. I swear don't put thoes things in my head.' Brad Douglas yelling at some helpless person like always.We wonder why he is such a dick to everyone. It seems as if Mr.Synnerster was able to win the fight with Brad,But he was not capable of makeing him shut up. 'Your not trying too help me out,Just fuckin' put some super glue and tape on it.' 'Bradley,Your bleeding quite badly.' 'What the fuck do you think,I got dropped on my head.Yeah,I'm gonna bleed when i get dropped head first on the concrete.' 'Did you have a concussion ?' 'I don't fucking know, I'm not the doctor that's your job.So you tell me if i have a damn concussion.' 'Calm down Bradley,Your only makeing things worse by yelling.' 'No,Your makeing things worse by running your mouth,Shut your mouth and stop my head from bleeding.' The doctor only gets a second of silence before Brad's cell phone starts to ring. 'What the hell are you talking about,Violent who? now i have to team with this Violent c person.Well that's not that bad who are we fighting?' All that can be heard from the outher end of the phone is mumbles. 'Nightmare & Wayne,This has gotta be a joke.Hey look call me back in 5 minuets.' Brad turns around and looks at the doctor 'Are we done?' 'You know Brad your not in any shape to fight next week.I don't have to sign your medical release after the way you spoke to me.' 'This is great,I'm being blackmailed by a fucking doctor!' 'No,Blackmail would be if i said..I want to be in UBC or else i will never clear you to fight Wayne and Nightmare.' 'Oh ok well then sign my papers,So i can walk out these doors and go home for a couple of days.' 'You know,You seem to know what your doing here Bradley.So i'm going to give you the benifet of the doubt and let you walk.Just go home and relax for a little while thoe ok?' 'Yeah yeah,Whatever just let me go now.' The doctor walks over and picks his clip board from the table.He signs the papers and gives Brad his copy of the work release. *Later* Brad Douglas is sitting in his black impala,In the hospitial underground parking lot.Talking on his cell phone to that same person as before. 'Look evan,I know what im doing and i know that Violent Q is not capable of carrying his own weight.Brett Wayne is not shit,Don't worrie he will not even be able to get near me or i'll slap him like the bitch that he really is.' 'All im sayin Brad is don't trust any of these mother fuckers.Don't trust your team mate and for sure as hell don't trust your opponents.' ' I don't trust anyone that has anything to do with UBC.From the owners to even the man who calls the matches.Fuck them all i don't need them,So it don't hurt me to not trust them.' 'How's your head doing by the way,Mr.Synnerster droped you pretty hard i heard.' 'Yeah i guess,He's a pussy thoe.I was getting up and the ref said i was out cold.Nah,I was only getting started.I was gonna break that bitch in half.' 'Yeah he's yesterdays trash thoe,What about your up comeing fight?' 'What about it,Im going home to sleep for a few days,Then im going to fly out and whoop the hell out of all three in this fight.' 'Will the owners fine you for attacking your teammate?' 'Pussy,Evan your a pussy.Do you really think i care? I got more money then UBC as a whole.I'm the king of every ring,Wrestling or Fighting rings! If they wanna fine me,Or suspend me then feel free to.But im going to prove my point at this jurisdiction.' 'Well Xavier and the rest are former figthers you know.' 'I Know,But they know not to step near me or they will get slapped down like bitches also.This is my ring even if the ref did screw me or not in that last fight.As a matter of fact im going to slap not 3 but 5 people this week.The first 3 being Violent Q,Brett Wayne,Nightmare,Mr.Synnerster,And that mother fucking ref that screwed me over last night.' Fin
