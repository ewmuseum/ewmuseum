'Another day of Insanity' Pt 7

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11-04-02 It seems Brett will never be cured of his Insanity or atleast that what it looks like so far. Yesterday Mike tried putting Brett on disability, when Brett heard this he asked 'How am I disabled' and Mikes answer was 'Mentally.' Brett suddenly erupted with anger as Mike tried to explain. Brett didn't care he simply went off on Mike. Mike tried just walking away but Brett chased after him, causing only pain to himself. Brett fell to the ground and busted his head open on a hard conrete basketball court and then was rushed to the hospital. Now the question of the day is...How will Brett turn out after his sudden bump in the head. Will he become worse or will this bump on the head finally Cure Brett of his Insanity. The scene goes to Brett being rushed through the hospital doors as Mike is following right behind his brother as he is laying on the stretcher. Mike is suddenly stopped by a nurse as she hands out some papers for him to fill out, Mike ignores her is he continues to watch his brother stroll down the hall until he is out of site. 'Sir, please fill out these forms' says a nurse holding a clipboard with papers and a pen on it. Mike looks down at the papers and looks up at the women and snatchs the clipboard from the womens hands. Pathetic, my brother is bleeding to death and I have to sit here and fill out this stupid forms. 'I'm sorry sir but its our policy' the nurse replies. Fuck your god damn policy! I'm not signing this shit I'm going back to where my brother is. Mike throws down the clipboard and runs down the halls as he looks for Brett. The sound of men and women screaming is heard coming from each room as Mike runs past the rooms. Mike finally find Bretts laying on an operating table, he just sits there and watch's Brett as the doctor's operate on his forhead. Damnit they better not fuck up or I'll have there ass. Mike starts getting impatient as he starts banging on the window, the doctors stop just long enough to turn around and look a Mike beating on the window then they turn back around and continue on Brett. Moments later footsteps are heard coming up from behind Mike. Sir, step back from the window please. Mike turns around only to see two police officers behind him. He simply ignores them as he turns back around and continues watching his brother. SIR, please step away from the window. The officers continue to talk to Mike as Mike just keeps ignoring them. The two officers look at each other and nod. They begin walking towards Mike slowly as Mike continues watching his brother. 'Don't come any closer or I'll be forced to defend myself.' Mike says still watching his brother. All I want is to make sure he is ok, then I'll do whatever you ask. The two police officers stop and waits for them to finish operating. Mike stands there for hours watching and waiting, finally one of the doctors walks out. 'He will be fine now, he just needs some rest.' says the doctor as he walks off. Mike lets out a sound of relief as he he turns around a looks at the officers. Now what do you two pigs want? We need you to sign these forms. One of the officers hands out the same clipboard from before as Mike just laughs. 'This is really pathetic.' Mike says as he grabs the clipboard as he starts walking towards Bretts room. Mike enters room 169 where his brother Brett lays there in his bed sound a sleep. Mike smiles as he sits down and fills out the forms. Mike reads through all the questions before repling. Do you have insurance? Yes/No Mike laughs and says to himself 'It seems money is there only concern.' Mike takes a few more minutes to finish filling out the forms, then he drops the clipboard on the ground. I need some rest. Mike gets up and walks over to the small sofa and lays down. Moments later he is sound asleep. 5 hours later... Mike opens his eyes and see's an empy bed. 'Wha...where is he?!?!' Mike panics for a minute and gets up, Brett then walks through the door. 'Your up, finally.' Brett says with a bandage wrapped around his head. 'Where the hell have you been' Mike ask. 'I was hungry so I went and ate something. There food is really nasty here. I'm never eating here again' Brett says as he jumps back in his bed. Mike sits back down and takes a deep breath then begins speaking again. Are you still going to be able to fight Saturday? Brett smiles 'Of course! Why wouldn't I. This is just a little scratch, nothing big.' 'Well I'll just have to ask the doctor about that, if he says it is ok then it will be ok but if he says it isn't then your not fighting saturday.' 'Ok go ask him, I'm sure he'll give me the ok.' Brett says as he smiles. Mike gets up and walks out of the room, he stops for a minute only to see an old women throwing up in a trash can. 'Nasty.' Mike continues walking as he looks around for the doctor that stopped and talked to him earlier. After minutes of searching he finds him sitting in his office. Mike knocks on the door and walks in. 'You came in with the guy who busted his forhead right' ask the doctor. 'Yea, thats why I came to talk to you about also. I just came to ask if it was ok for Brett to fight in his match Saturday' Mike ask. 'Fight...' the doctor looks confused. 'Yea fight, he is in the UBC. Its a fighting federation, thats his job.' When he asked if he could go back to work he didn't mention fighting. Oh you already talked to him? Yea a few minutes ago, he came in and asked if it was ok for him to go back to work so I filled out the papers that said it was ok and gave them to him. Wha?!?!? Your shitting me! Mike shouts. 'Now what the hell is going to happen.' Mike says to himself as he runs out of the doctors office and runs back to Bretts room. 'So what did the doctor say?' Brett ask with that same smile on his face. 'Fine go ahead and fight saturday but if you get hurt or permantly injured don't blame me this time. I'll just sit back and say I told you so.' Mike says as he sits down disgusted. 'I'm not going to get hurt' Brett laughs. If anyone is going to get hurt it is going to be Bobby Bless and Nightmare. You don't have to worry about me, I'll be fine by saturday. I just need my rest and I'll be ready to go stroke some heads. Brett sits there and smiles as Mike frowns and says to himself 'He'll never learn' They have a moment of silence between each other for a few minutes. Then Mikes cell phone rings and he answers. Hello? .............................................................. Yea he's right here hand on just a second. Mike gets up and walks over to Brett 'Its for you' Mike says as he hands the phone to Brett. 'Hello' .............................................................. Oh, I havent heard from you in a few days, how are things. .............................................................. Good to hear that. .............................................................. I'm fine, just a little bump on the head. .............................................................. Oh yea I'll be there saturday what about you? .............................................................. Good thing because those BMW boys are starting to annoy me, we need to take care of them and show them who the real men are in the UBC. .............................................................. Ok, sounds good so I guess I'll see you saturday. .............................................................. Good luck in your fight two. -CLICK- Brett hands the phone up and tosses it to Mike. 'Things are still going as planned, you see I have everything under control.' Brett says looking at Mike. Mike laughs 'Lets hope so.' -Fade 2 Black-
