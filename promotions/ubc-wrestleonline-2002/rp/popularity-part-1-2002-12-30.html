Popularity? Part 1

30 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12/30/02 The Scence opens as Chris Heart and Brian Heart are thier apartments in Brooklyn, New York. They are lieing on the ground and thinking of things to do. Chris Heart: Damn...I am bored as hell. Theres gotta be somethin here to do we live in the best city in the world. We gotta find somethin. Brian Heart: For real man. hmm... They start thinking of what to do then Brian turns in the T.V. and a UBC commericial is on. It's main theme of the commericial is for Juristiction on Saturday. They watch the commerial then it shows the card for the match. It says a couple matches then it says that there will be a dual combat title match with Kings of Africa vs. Heart Throbs vs. Krisis & Violent B. They look at each other. Chris Heart: Finally little bro, it's time to show all dem bitches at UBC that we ain't no fuckin joke. Brian Heart: Hell yeah! But today is tuesday and that is Saturday. Chris Heart: Damn, I guess we stuck bored again. We should prepare but where should we start? Brian Heart: Wanna go to the gym? Start lifting weights and practicing our high flying moves? Chris Heart: Alright, but I don't want to be noticed as a UBC wrestler I don't want people to see us before the warehouse. We will get mobbed by fans and hataz. Brian Heart: Alright...I guess hoods might help. Chris Heart: Yeah lets get ready. Chris and Brian start getting ready, it's 10:00 AM and they are ready to go to the gym. They walk out of the apartment covering they heads with sweater hoods. They head towards thier X5 BMW. Chris unlocks the car and they get in. And Chris starts the car. Chris Heart: Lets go... They get spotted in the car and 3 cars fallow them yelling thier name and the number of cars keep multipling as more and more people see them. Brian Heart: So much for not being seen... They laugh and continue they're way to the gym. When they get their they sign a couple dozen autographs before they walk in and begin to workout. They start lightling weights and the commercial comes on again. Now everyone sees the commerical then one UBC fan says, look they are right over there. And the fan points to the Heart Throbs. They all rush over to the Heart Throbs. Chris whispers to himself: Oh boy... Brian looks at Chris and says: I guess this is a good thing. They start to sign autographs, and they meet so many people. Then a whole bunch of girls walk in and say look its the heart throbs. Then all the girls rush over to the heart throbs. Girl: I heard you guys were here. Chris: Oh yeah? From who? Girl: Everyone knows now! Chris and Brian look at each other and Chris says: Damn...in a funny voice. They sign autographs and they must have signed over 200 already and thought to themselves damn we are popular huh? The numbers get greater and greater then they are done...and signed to many autographs to count. Finially we can work out now. They start using the stare master at the highest level to improve thier speed. Chris: I am kind of tired now, all those autographs. Got my forearms sore. Brian: Yeah me to Chris: We will go home soon first lets practice on in the boxing ring. Brian: Again thats twice as hard as a wrestling ring... Chris: So, we have a title shot we need to get ready. Brian: True, true... They do some moves and kind of purposley hurt themselves then when they are finished they walk out side to thier X5 BMW and see it. It has Heart Throbs, New York loves you in red letters with a heart painted on it on thier navy blue car. Chris: Damn my car... Brian: Thats pretty cool though huh? And it was free. Chris: True but now how we gonna keep away from the fans? They get in the car and start it up and are about to go home. Chris: if you think about it thats pretty tight. New York loves you? thats tight. Brian: Yeah, I guess we are more popular then I thought. Chris: Yeah but one thing...we have been in many efeds and not this much and we were well known here but we weren't caught and we haven't had a ubc match yet. Brian: YEah but UBC everyone watches so they probrobly had more people that knew us. Chris: True...true... They arrive at the outside of the apartment and in the parking lot it has the heart throbs logo. A heart with HT in facny letters in it. Chris: WOW! nice art. Brian: Graphitti dedicated to us! Chris: YEah! They take out some sprayspaint and autograph thier names with the spray paint. Chris: But who did all this...who got the people fallowing us? Who got told everyone we were back in Brooklyn? Who? Brian: I dont know... They enter the house thinking and they take a shower and watch some TV before they go to bed. TO BE CONTINUED.... ---- Back
