Chat with the Prince

17 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12/17/02 The scene opens with William Thornbush (MrSynnerster) stepping into the elevator inside the Summit Towers in downtown Columbia South Carolina. The Summit towers is a twenty story building that houses many different companies and buildings, including the office of Gene E. Prince, William’s faithful Agent. William is dressed in a white polo shirt, baggy Tommy Hillfiger jeans, and a red pair of sneakers. His dreadlocks are pinned up in a red sack hemp hat and his round shaped sunglasses sit on the brim of the hat. William steps into the elevator and pushes the fourth floor-button, and takes the trip skyward. Soon the elevator dings upon reaching the 14th floor. William steps off the elevator into the reception area of Gene E.’s office. William’s first thought is “Damn, Gene E. is living Ghetto Fabulous.” The reception area is painted an ugly bright green color. The reception desk is an old school teacher deck that looks like it’s way past its better days. In front of the desk on one side of the wall are two chairs. One is a metal with the back gone and the other a wooden chair with one of the legs mended together with gray duck tape. Oh the other wall is an old red couch with red duck tape covering up the rips and spring coming out of one of the cushion. Sided the couch are two half-dead ferns. Sitting beside the desk is a young African American woman reading a Vibe magazine with Busta Rhyme on the cover. The woman is dressed in a tight blue shirt with a diamond shaped peek a boo window on the front. William can easily tell that the woman’s over ample abundance of breast is too big for the small bra she is trying to wear. Unfortunately to go along with the woman’s big bosom is a big stomach, so it’s almost are to tell when her chest ends and her belly begins. On every finger the woman has an oversized gold ring, and around her neck hangs a thick gold chain with a big Auk medallion. Her hair is in thick braids with red yarn weaved into it. She is wearing a really bright red lip stick, and too much makeup, but it doesn’t help cover up her face. “Damn this chick is ugly.” William thinks to himself. William walks up to the desk and says ”Excuse me. I’m here to see Mr. Prince.” The receptionist is so into her magazine, she doesn’t even notice William. William says a little louder this time. “Excuse me. I’m here to see Mr. Prince.” Without looking up, the receptions rolls her eyes, sigh, and turns the page of her magazine. William thinks to himself, ”I know this bitch is not acting like this. You can’t be fat, ugly AND mean. That shit don’t work. If you’re fat and ugly, you gotta make up for it with a nice personality.” William puts his hand in front of the magazine, blocking the woman’s view, and says ”I’m here to see Mr. Prince.” The lady sucks her teeth and replies, “I know you did not put your hand in my face like that. I will . . .” Her words get caught in her throat as she looks up at William. “Oh shit. It’s you.” Her demeanor changes from sassy secretary to star struck fan. “I have got to get a picture of this. Rhonda is not going to believe you were here. The woman reaches into her desk and pulls out a Polaroid camera. Then she walks over to William, puts her arm around him and uses her other arm to hold out the camera and take the picture. As she stands beside he waiting for the picture to develop, William notices the woman’s skirt is too short and too tight for her large body. The woman reaches over her desk to grab a pen, which causes her skirt to rise up, revealing that she is wearing a green thong. William thinks, ”Shit, I didn’t know they made thongs that damn big.” The woman looks out of the corner of her eye and sees William looking at her behind and says, “I see you looking at my ass. I know you wanna be all up in this.” It takes everything inside William, not to say something smart or mean. He literally has to bite his pierced tongue to keep silent. The lady hands William the pen and the pictured and asks, ”Would you sign this for me please. Make it out to Meka.” William signs the picture To my favorite Synner, Meka. MrSynnerster Then he hands the picture back to Meka. She reads the message and frowns up her face. Meka: “Who is MrSynnerster?” William: “I am.” Meka: “You mean like the X-men character?” William: “No like the wrestler. My wife gave me this name a long time ago.” Meka: “Shit I thought you were Busta Rhythms. You ain’t nobody.(Puts her hands on her hips, and gyrates her head in a circular motion as she speaks) “And what the fuck are you doing starring at me ass if you married. What you thought you could get with me on the side.” William: “Bitch I don’t wanna get with your fat ass. I was just amazed that made thongs in whale size.” Meka: “I know the fuck, you didn't just say that. I’m going to kick your ass now.” William: “Whoa, calm down there free Willy. It’s not that serious. I might be a wrestler, but I’m a very non-violent person outside the ring. Meka: “That’s fine then. You just stand there and I’ll kick your ass.” Before William knew it, Meka punches him in the face. William (Saying to himself) “This bitch did not just punch me.” Meka punches him again. William: “Lady you are pushing it. Why do I feel like Chris Tucker in Rush Hour 2?” Meka tries to punch William again, but William catches her fist. William: “Calm down Barney. This isn’t called for.” Meka lunges at William knocking them both onto the couch. Once down, Meka starts biting William’s shoulder and tries to bite a chunk out. The biting quickly turns to kisses on William’s neck. Luckily Gene E. Prince walks into the room. Gene: “What the hell is going on here.” Meka quickly jumps up, straighten up her clothes, and casually says “MrSynnerster is here to see you Gene.” Gene helps William to his feet. Gene: “Come on. We’ll go talk in my office.” William walks past Meka, who reaches out and grabs his butt. William turns to look back at Meka. She smiles and blows him a kiss. William rolls his eyes, and follows Gene down the hall to his office. The Scene changes with Gene E. Prince and William Thornbush walking into Gene’s office. Gene is dressed in a white shirt with a black and red UBC novelty tie and black pants. His hair is still sticking straight up in the usual cone shaped Afro. Gene’s office is decorated in a neo-metropolitan style, with large oak desk and plush black leather chair for Gene. Along one wall is a black oak bookcase with books of various subjects. Against another wall sits a black leather couch with a large picture window behind it. In front of the couch is a glass top coffee table with a couple of magazines sitting on top of it. Along another wall is a big screen TV and the last wall are adorned with pictures of Gene E with various clients. William plops down on the couch, while Gene takes a seat in the chair behind his desk. William: “Gene, I have two questions for you. One, what is up with your secretary, and two why does your front office look like hell and you’re big ballin’ back here in your office. Gene leans back in his chair and props his feet on the desk before saying, ”To answer your first question, Meka is what happens when you hire family. She’s my wife’s niece, and all the women in that family are crazy. That’s why my hair sticks up. My wife got mad at me one day and threw a toaster at me when I was taking a shower. I was lucky it didn’t kill me.” William sits up on the couch, and scratches his shoulder where he was bite. William: “I think she gave me rabies. Has she had all her shots? Gene: “You’ll be alright. You’re lucky she ate before you got here, or she might have chewed off your arm. You should see her ex boyfriend. He asked her to go down before feeding her, and now he’s one of the Sopranos and I’m not talking about Tony’s family. So consider yourself lucky. What I wanna know is why do you look so down?” William: “I’m pissed off. Did you see what the jackass Xavier did?” Gene: “Yes, but .” (Pauses for a moment.)”Actually if we’re going to have a meeting we have to do this thing right.” (Presses a button on the phone on his desk)”Ms. Morgan, would you step in here for a moment. I have a client.” Moments later a young Caucasian curvy woman walks in the room carries a silver tray. She places the tray on the desk. The woman processes to sprinkle some brown colored leaves on a small piece of paper, then rolls the paper up like a cigarette, licking the end of the paper so that it sticks together. The whole time she is doing this, William glares at her body. Her long jet-black hair is pulled back in a ponytail and she is wearing a pair of metal-framed glasses. She is wearing a nice red lipstick that William wishes was on his shirt instead of Meka’s. Her body is covered in a black business suit, with a knee length skirt. As William looks at her legs he notices the last of what appears to be a set of tiger paw track tattoos going up the inside of her thigh. William chuckles to himself as he realizes the woman reminds him of the Baroness from the old GI Joe cartoon. Ms. Morgan finishes rolling up the paper and hands it to Gene. Ms. Morgan: “Will that be all Mr. Prince?” Gene: “Yes. Thank you Anastasia.” Ms. Morgan turns to walk away, William watches Anastasia as walks out the door, stopping once to look back and give William a coy smile. Once she’s gone Gene says, “Um, Um, Um. Fine and she can roll a hell of a fatty. You want a hit of this?” William: “No thanks.” Gene: “Come on now. Don’t be like that.”Gene leaves his desk and sits down beside William on the couch. ”Take a hit. I smoke with all my clients. Come on now. It’s Wednesday. Your match isn’t until Saturday and you ain’t got shit to do.” William: “Who are you? Smoky from Friday?” Gene: “Nope. I am the Gene E. and this is me working my magic.” William: “Fine if this will shut you up. Give it here.” William takes the joint, puts it to his lips inhales the smoke in his lungs, holds it for a bit, then coughs it out.” Gene: “Slow down, baby. Don’t hit that shit that hard. You smoking like you’ve done this before.” William: “I have smoked a couple of times back in college. But our stuff wasn’t this strong.” Gene: “I had this stuff imported in from Jamaica. The home of the good shit. Only problem is that sometimes it’s laced with something else. But hey that just adds to the high. Anyway getting back to what we were talking about. What’s the problem my man.” William:(Taking another puff of the joint and passes it to Gene) “That bitch Xavier Morning-sickness put me in a handicap match against Violent B and Chris Carter. What the fuck is that about? Is Mr. Morning-sickness upset because I exposed him for being an ass ranger?” Gene: “Don’t worry about that. You can handle what were their names again?” (Inhales the smoke and passes it back) William: (takes another hit and passes it) “Violent B and Chris Carter. Violent B? Isn’t he the skinny member of the Insane Clown Posse? Violent B, stick to rapping and getting your ass kicked by Emienm. And Chris Carter? He’s the guy that wrote the X-files. Well Mulder and Scully won’t be there Saturday to help his ass. Shit why was I even trippin’. I can beat both their asses, and still have enough energy to crack off a few more Morning-breath jokes.” Gene: “I never said you couldn’t handle those two. BUT a little training doesn’t hurt. Which is why I set this up.” (puffs the blunt and passes it.) Gene gets up from the couch. Goes over to his desk drawer, retrieves a business card, and hands it to William. Gene: “That’s my Crazy Uncle One Eyed Pete’s card. He’s trained all my wrestling clients.” William: “What the hell are you talking about? I’m your only wrestling client.” Gene: “Exactly. He’s training you. You’re my only wrestling client, therefore Uncle One Eyed Pete will have trained all my wrestling clients.” William looks at the card then flicks in against his fingers, and takes a nice long hit of the weed. William: “Oh hell no. I’m not dealing with anymore of your crazy relatives. Dealing with Meka every time I come see you is more than enough for me.” Gene: “My Crazy Uncle One Eyed Pete is not crazy.” William: “How are you going to say some shit like that. Of course he’s crazy, hence his name being CRAZY Uncle one Eyed Pete.” Gene: “Don’t talk about my Crazy Uncle One eyed Pete. He’s a veteran. He gave his arm and leg to the cause. Literally. Which reminds me, don’t talk about his hook. He’s sensitive about that shit, and I know how you are Mr. Funny man. William: (With a falsely innocent look on his face.) “Whatever do you mean? I’m the nicest guy you ever met.” Gene: “I’m just saying. Don’t try to be funny. You’ll end up with a peg leg in your ass William takes another hit and starts laughing hysterically. Gene: “What’s so funny?” William: (Smokes and passes) “I don’t know.” (Begins looking around the room.) “Do you hear that?” Gene: “Hear what?” William: “I can hear my brain cells dying. (Puts his hands over his ears.) “Shh. Stop screaming in my ear.” Gene: “Who the fuck are you taking to?” William: “My brain cells.” Gene: “Man this is the last time we. . . (Pauses and closes his eyes.) Oh my God. I can’t see. I can’t see. William: “Your eyes are closed. Gene: (Opens his eyes.) “Oh thank God.” William: “I have to get out of here.” (William stands up and walks into the closet closing the door behind him. Several minutes later he walks back out of the closet.) “That elevator wasn’t going anywhere. (William leans against the wall, and slides down until he’s sitting on the floor.) “I think I’ll sit here instead.” Gene: “This has to be one of the most productive meetings I’ve ever had. (Both men laugh hysterically) “Don’t forget about your training tomorrow with uh. With uh. Shit, I can’t remember who you were suppose to train with.” William: “That’s okay. I can’t remember who I am right now.” The two laugh uncontrollable again as the scene fades out.
