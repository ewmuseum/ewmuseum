The Beginning

29 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Nov. 29, 2002 **The scene opens up to a wideshot of the beatiful horizon just over Daytona Beach. It is obvious that is is early evening and the weather is just beginning to get cooler. There are a few sea-dooers in the water getting in their last ride of the day, and people packing up their blankets after a great day of tanning. The camera pans down to show a silhouette of a man lying on the sand. The man is fairly slim, and his mid-length, dark hair is flowing in the wind. The camera takes a loop and turns to face him, and he is revealed as Raine Knights. He looks into the camera with a slight smirk on his face.** Raine: Beatiful isn't it, just beatiful. The palm trees, the dark blue ocean, and most of all, this face your looking at. Breathtaking. But don't be fooled, although I may look harmless, you really don't want to see me when I step into the ring. Ugly. Much like that. **Raine points down the beach to show a rather heavy set woman taking off her towel to reveal a tiny thong. Raine turns away in disgust and tries to continue without getting distracted.** Raine: It may be hard to believe, but you will most likely rather stare into that fat can than look at Troy McLean's face after Jurisdiction. I'm the new guy around here, which means I should not be underestimated, or overestimated, but I HIGHLY suggest you don't underestimate me. You want proof? See for yourself. **The camera pans down the beach where there are a few fairly large men sprawled out, onconsious on the sand.** Raine: They were the ones that looked at me and my size, and automatically decided that they can just do and say whatever they want to me, and look where they ended up. Voice: Raine! **Raine looks back to see two scantilly clad women standing next to a 1983 Porsche Carrera. Raine waves and tells them that he will be there shortly.** Raine: Looks like I have to wrap this one up quick. Troy McLean, I don't know you, and after Jurisdiction you will not know anyone, as I will knock you around that ring for so long that you will forget your mother's name. This will all be done with True Perfection, and it will never happen any other way when you go toe to toe with Raine Knights. Now if you'll excuse me. **Raine hops up to his feet and strolls over to the car. He opens up the doors and the two women step in. The camera zooms in closer as Raine slips on a pair of shades and gives the camera the wink and the gun. He drives off into the distance as the camera fades to static.**
