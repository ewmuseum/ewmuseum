' A day of embarassment ' Pt 2

18 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12-18-02 The scene cuts to a commercial airline as the sound of jets landing and taking off is heard. The scene then drafts inside the airport where people ramble throughout the building. Tommy Grady's voice can be heard as the camera quickly flows up to a tV screen sitting in a corner allowing the people waiting for takeoff to view. The tV shows Tommy Grady and his interview with the press, moments later the crowd of people begin laughing after hearing Tommy and his jokes. After a few minutes of laughing the camera the drafts back to the exit of one of the flights, crowds of people exit before Brett Wayne and Mike Wayne make there exit. One of the guys notices Brett as he laughs... Man: (Points towards Brett)... Look everyone its Brett Wayne!!! Everyone turns to look at Brett as the continue to laugh, Brett scratch's his head wondering what the commotion is all about. Brett: (Confused)... What are all of you laughing about. The man points to the tV sitting in the corner, Brett see's Tommy Grady as he starts running away from the press. Brett: What the hell has he done now? Man: He just set up a press conference about your mother... Brett: (Interrupts)...MY MOTHER?!?!? Man: Yes your momma, the 'WHORE!' Brett: What did you just say old man?!?! Man: Well she is, she does fuck for a living so thats considered a WHORE. Brett: I'm warning you, you old fucker don't call my mom a whore or else... Man: Or else what you going to kick my ass just like you kicked Brad Douglas's ass...(Begins Laughing) Brett: Ok, thats it your ass is grass you old fucker. Brett begins walking toward the old man but Mike steps in the way preventing him from going any closer. Brett: What the hell are you doing Mike....He's talking about OUR mother you know, you just going to let him stand there and talk shit about her like that... Mike: Well he does have a point you know... Man: Yeah so she is a WHORE! Mike: BUT THAT DOESN'T MEAN YOU CAN CALL HER THAT TO OUR FACE YOU OLD FUCKER! Mike quickly turns around and hits the old man causing him to go crashing to the ground. Brett: (Laughs)...Who's laughing now you old timer. Brett and Mike finish laughing as they begin walking towards the luggage area, on there way they notice various of people staring at them as the chuckle under there breath. Brett: I wonder what the hell is going on now, this shit has hit the ceiling. Mike: Oh Shit! Look at that...(Points towards another Television)...Its on the god damn news now! Brett: So thats what that bastard was doing, damnit he just keeps making my life miserable. Mike: We have to get this shit straightened out. Brett: How in the hell are we going to do that? Mike: Well the only thing I know to do is setup our own press conference sometime... Brett: And what in the hell will we do once we've setup our own, get embarassed MORE! Mike: No, No, No! I have a plan...(Smiles) Brett: Oh yea...(Smiles)...and what is this plan of yours. Mike: I'll tell you later but for now lets get our bags and get the flock out of here before we have to whoop up on some more old people. Brett: Yeah that sounds like a good idea. Brett and Mike pick up there luggage and make there way down the esclader to the first floor. They continue walking as they see a limo sitting outside the the Airport. Once arriving outside they notice this limo nicer then the one they had before. Brett seemed happy as he jumped into the limo with Mike following him. The camera fades into the Limo were Brett and Mike begin to set up there press conference. Mike: (Picks up Cell phone and dials a number)... Yeah this is Mike Wayne, Brett Waynes brother. We seen that little press conference that Tommy Grady through together earlier and we want to throw our own together can you have one setup for us later on today.... ..................... Mike: Is our mother going to be there...(Looks over at Brett as Brett shakes his head yes)... Umm....yeah she'll be there she has a few words she would like to say also...(Listens for a minute)... Good we will see you at six o clock then...(Hangs cell phone up) Brett: So..... Mike: We have to head straight to the police station and got mom out of jail then head over to the local park for our press conference. Brett: Ok sounds good to me...(Knocks on the glass)... Driver take us to the local Police Station. There driver nods his head and makes a U-Turn in the middle of the street as he speeds up hurrying to the police staions. Brett: I'm dreading going and getting mom out, you know how many police officers will laugh at me and you... Mike: Correction Bro! Just you going in there I'm staying right here. Brett: WHAT!?!? Mike: I did my part getting the press conference together, now you go and do your part by getting mom out jail. Brett:(Lets out a deep breath)...Oh man this is going to be embarassing as hell. Mike: Look at it this way, atleast we are getting mom out of jail...(Smiles) Brett sits there for a few more minutes before arriving in front of the police station, the limo stops and Brett moans as he opens the door. Brett: Here goes nothing! Mike:(Chuckles)...Good Luck! Brett slowly steps out of the limo as everyone is staring wondering who he was, directly after stepping out of the limo every begins laughing, Brett ingores them and enters the police station. He walks up to the front desk as he begins talking to the officer. Brett: Excuse Me! I'm here to get my mother officer...(Looks at name tag on the officers shirt)... Taylor. Officer Taylor: (Looks up noticing who the man is and begins laughing)...I was wondering when you was going to show up, you here to get that whor...(Corrects Himself)...that mother of yours? Brett:(Brett gets slightly angry)...Tell me how much the damn bail is! Officer Taylor: Well lemme see here...(Looks down at a sheet of paper)...that will be 1500 dollars right now. Brett: WHAT! 1500 DOLLARS! You've got to be joking. Officer Taylor: No I'm not sir, 1500 dollars or your mother stays. Brett: I can't beleive this shit(Brett pulls out his wallet and pulls out wod of cash and counts it making sure its 1500 dollars, he then throws it on the table)...I want a receipt for that. Officer Taylor: So you not as dumb as what Grady thinks, I actually thought you was there for a minute but I guess you proved me wrong. Brett: Write me my god damn receipt right now and go get my fucking mom! Officer Taylor points to another officer signaling him to go get his mother from the back, he writes him his receipt and moments later Brett's mom comes walking to the front with a look of excitement on her face. MoM: Oh Son! You came! The entire police station begins dieing out lauging. MoM: What did I say something? Brett: Damnit lets get the fuck out of here...(Brett walks out of the building quickly) MoM: Wait for me...I'm Cumming son! She walks out of the police station as everyone continues laughing, she walks down the steps to the Limo and she gets inside. MoM: Mike!!!! Mike: (Frowns)...Hey mom.... MoM: How have you two been? Mike: Fine... Brett:(Interrupts)...FINE AFTER ALL THE SHIT WE BEEN THROUGH MIKE YOU SAY FINE! Mike: Ok maybe things haven't been fine but hey...look on the bright side man, we got mom back and now we have a press conference to go to. Brett: I still havent heard what your plan is? Mike: Well we still like another 10 minutes before we arrive I'll explain it to you on the way. The scene fades to the outside of the limo as it drives off from the police station. The camera fades away and 10 minutes later it fades back to the limo pulling up to the local park. Brett, Mike, and there MoM gets out with a huge smile on there faces as they walk up to the stand where the mic is. Brett: (Taps Mic)...Testing...(Smiles)...Its all yours mom. MoM: Ladies and Gentlement I have come here to state to each and everyone of you that I Vicky Wayne is not a whore nor will I ever be. These kids do know who there father is so that means I am not a whore. Although I do like to have sex every so often but that is MY personal business not any of you little dick reporters or you loose pussy bitchs!(She begins getting out of line, Mike puts his head over her mouth and pulls her back as Brett steps up to the Mic smiling) Brett: She kinda got out of line there and I'm sorry for you people having to witness that but we came out here today to state that my mom is NOT a prostitute. Tommy Grady made the whole story up. It was nothing but lies. Tommy Grady is laughing right now but come saturday ladies and gentlemen of the press WE will be the ones laughing. My brilliant brother has come up with another fantastic idea that is going to schock UBC fans from across the world and you can bet your ass it involves all three of us plus another fighter that cannot be named at this time...(Smiles)... Brett: Also Tommy Grady has been saying I am scared of him, well its time to let the truth be known. I Brett Wayne...The Insane One of UBC is NOT scared of Tommy Grady nor will I ever be. Tommy Grady is just a thorn in my hand, those are easly taken care of and he will be easly taken care of. Brett: Now for all you Tommy Grady fans out there I have only...(Counts to 2 on his hand)...two words for you... Vicky: (Interrupts)...EAT ME!!!! Brett: (Quickly covers the mic up)...Shut her up damnit! Mike quickly puts his hand back over her mouth and shuts her up. Brett: Now what was I saying... Female Reporter: You have two words for them... Brett: Oh yea, umm well...(Thinks for a Minute)... I forgot but anyways the fact is Tommy Grady will be defeated this Saturday on Jurisdiction, now is there any questions you would like to ask? Hands go soring through the air as camera's flash one after the other and reporters begin talking constantly. Brett: Well I guess not so its shall be our time to leave, all of you take care and enjoy my show this Saturday...(Smiles as they walk back to the limo and jump in quickly leaving the reporters behind)...
