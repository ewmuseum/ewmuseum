Introduction to Cade

14 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12-14-02 ~ Scene ~ A dimly lit room…white plaster walls bathed in the soft yellow glow of a single bulb at the end of the chain. Sounds echo through the room…sounds of flesh on flesh, but not in the pleasurable manner. This sound is loud, constant, always followed by the whimper of a man, which of course is followed by another sound of flesh on flesh. We pan around, to find a man tied to a chair in the center of the room, his hands bound behind his back, legs tied at the ankles. His plain white t-shirt is covered with a crimson stain, blood dripping from his lips, nose, and other cuts across his face. Another man, a large well built bull of a man, stands in front of him, bringing another hard right hand down across the other’s face, eliciting another stream of blood to splatter on the concrete floor below. 'Jesus, Cade…don’t kill him. If he’s dead, we’ll never get the money.' This voice is from the darkness just beyond our view. Cade doesn’t turn around, but brings yet another thunderous right hand down across the tied man’s face. The man whimpers again, causing a scowl to cross Cade Roberts’ face. Cade steps back away from him, blood covering the knuckles of his right hand, as he turns his head towards the shadows and the man hidden within them. 'When the fuck did you become my personal advisor, Jack? You’re a lousy courier, and an ever worse ball buster, so just shut your damn mouth and let me do my job. Vito didn’t hire me for my looks, asshole, he hired me because I can get what he wants, his money. And if this low life doesn’t start answering my fucking questions…' Cade turns, bringing another right hand downward, this one directly atop the collarbone, the sound of a * crack * heard rather loudly before the man screams out in pain. '…he’ll never get out of this chair alive.' 'Damn, man, calm down. I was just making a simple observation, you don’t have to jump down my throat about it. All I was trying to say is, if you break his fucking jaw, he can’t talk. Punch him in the chest or something.' Cade laughs, a rather dark humorless laugh, as he straddles the man in the chair, sitting down upon the man’s knees. Cade rests his arms over the man's shoulders, causing a hissing intake of air due to pain from the broken collarbone. Cade gets right into the man’s face, ignoring the blood. 'Now…you owed five grand by yesterday afternoon. Mr. Manelli was nice enough to loan you the money, and he expected prompt payment in return. Now I’m gonna ask you one more time…where is the money?' The man stutters and stumbles over his words, pleading with Cade to give him just one more day and he’d have it. Cade listens through the man’s tears and sobs and pleads for mercy and an extra day, before standing up to tower over the man again, a disappointed scowl playing on his lips. Slowly, and deliberately, Cade produces a stainless steel .45 Ruger Redhawk from behind his back. He holds the gun up to eye level, allowing the light of the naked bulb to reflect of the steel plating. He drops the barrel of the gun, pointing it directly at the forehead of the man in the chair. 'Mr. Manelli hates late payments, Mr. Ferrera. You owed five thousand by YESTERDAY, and that time has passed. I’m afraid your contract has been terminated, and you can consider this the foreclosure on your life.' Cade holds the gun steady as Mr. Ferrera promptly pisses all over himself, dark urine running down his pants leg as the tension lies thick in the air. Suddenly, Cade pulls the trigger, and…* click *… 'This is your final warning. Since your going to be three days late, there’s going to be a sur-charge for my bill collecting services. At 12 noon tomorrow, a payment of NO LESS than 6 grand is due on Mr. Manelli’s desk. I expect you to pay it in full…don’t make me have to come find you. Am I understood?' Mr. Ferrera nods quickly, as Cade slowly puts the gun away. Ferrera breathes a loud sigh of relief, which causes Cade to bring his hand down to grasp the broken collarbone, a scream parting the lips of Ferrera from pain. Cade smiles wickedly, and then releases the grip. He and the shadow man exit through the only door in the room, leaving Ferrera to find a way to untie himself alone with his broken collarbone. ~ End Scene ~ :: Welcome to Las Vegas :: ~ Scene ~ The sleek red Porsche 911 just seemed to glide down the interstate, passing cars left and right as if they didn’t move at all. Cade sat at the wheel, the drop top down, and the wind whispering over the top of the car like a long lost lover. His button down shirt was open, flapping in the wind, as his gloved hands stayed locked upon the steering wheel, his eyes forward and on the road as he headed south, to a place he should not be. To this day, he still had not figured out the whole affair, or how it started. All he knew was that he was in love with Mr. Manelli’s wife, Jackie…and that no matter the consequences, one way or the other, they would both leave this life in search of a new one. ~ Flash ~ Jackie: 'Oooh…oh yes, Cade…' She moaned in her seductive manner, as she knew Cade enjoyed, to heighten both their pleasures as they lie naked and entangled upon the large bed. Jackie tilted her head back, closing her eyes tightly as she felt Cade filling her, her legs wrapped tightly around his waist, her nails digging deep into the skin of his back. Cade grasped her ass tightly, pulling upwards as he drove downward into her, the sweat making them both shine in the fading moonlight coming through the windows. When it was over, they lay together, her head upon his well-toned chest, as the silence echoed throughout the room…until she broke it. Jackie: 'Cade…what are we going to do? We can’t continue to meet like this, Vito will figure it out eventually.' Cade: 'Don’t worry about Vito. If he weren’t so preoccupied with how much money he has in the bank, he’d be taking care of you like he should. We’re gonna get out of here, Jackie, I promise you. A few more jobs for Vito and I’ll have enough money to take us to Europe, to places where even Vito’s Italian connections couldn’t find us. Just give me a little more time.' Jackie sat up, propping herself on one elbow as her she looked down upon him with soft green eyes. Jackie: 'I love you, Cade. I just hope the time you need isn’t the time Vito needs to figure us out.' Cade: 'You have to trust me, Jackie.' Jackie smiled, crawling atop him, her firm breasts pressed fully against his chest, as she swiveled her hips atop him. Jackie: 'Speaking of time, lover…I think it’s time we moved on to round two…' ~ Flash ~ The Porsche pulled up to the doorway of the cabin, coming to an almost silent halt upon the gravel drive. The house was dark, and as Cade exited the car, he took note of it with a scowl. Jackie should have been here before him; it wasn’t like her to be late. Cade cautiously looked over the grounds, looking for any sign of something that shouldn’t be, but finding nothing. This did not however ease the tension in his mind, as the feeling of something being wrong crept through his system like a plague. No one knew where this place was; it was well hidden off the road, basically out in the middle of nowhere. There were no signs, no roads, no way of knowing the place even existed, which is why Cade had liked it so much, it meant that when he wanted to be alone with Jackie, that it was guaranteed. But the feeling persisted as he opened the door and entered into the cabin. Cade: 'Jackie?' His voice went unanswered as he tossed the keys down upon the coffee table. He flipped the switch to the free standing lamp, illuminating the room in a soft glow…a glow that immediately revealed the blood stains across the carpeted floor. Cade withdrew his gun in an instant; turning back and forth as he scanned what rooms could be seen with the lamplight. Easing forward toward the bedroom, Cade kept his eyes on the blood, a trail as if someone were drug through the house while bleeding. Cautiously he entered into the bedroom, letting his eyes dart back and forth around him, looking for any sign of attack that might come his way. As his eyes focused forward upon the bed, he realized someone was lying in it, the covers completely pulled up over the body to hide it from view. Cade flipped the switch to turn on the bedroom lights, and as his eyes focused again on the bed, he could see the blood dripping off the female fingertips of the body that lie underneath the covers. Cade eased forward more, the truth already known, but the need to make sure overwhelming him inside. Reaching forward with his right hand, he slowly removed the covers, and then turned away quickly, eyes squinted shut, but the sight burned into his mind like a branding iron. Jackie lay on the bed, or at least what was left of her. She had been gutted, a knife or sword cutting her from her crotch all the way to her throat, her insides spilled out onto the bed. Her head had been beaten in, her skull crushed as gray brain matter oozed out along with the crimson fluid. His stomach wrenched, but he fought down the vomit as his body shook with rage and agony, and as he stood there, a voice reached his ears with bad intent. 'Cade, Cade, Cade…how long did you think this little love affair could go on? You think I was just gonna let you pork my wife and then forget about it like it never happened? You should know me better than that.' Cade slowly opened his eyes, to find himself facing down not only Vito, but Jack, Carl, and two more faceless henchmen, all with iron drawn and pointing his way. Cade kept the Ruger at his side, but his finger twitched back and forth across the trigger as the rage spread through him. Cade: 'I’m gonna fucking kill you…' Vito: 'Not likely, my friend. You see…your nothing but a puppet, Cade, someone I sued to do my dirty work so that I could keep my hands clean. But the moment you started fucking my wife, the plans to replace you were already in the works, this just accelerated it. Ha…you wanna know the truth Cade? I never liked the bitch in the first place. She was already dead, I thought maybe tossing her off a balcony or even a simple shot to the head would do. But I know how much she meant to you, Cade…so I had to disfigure her, make it hurt…' Cade: 'YOU SON OF A BITCH!' Cade raised the Ruger, but the men were ready for it. Jack’s shotgun blasted out, and Cade felt the hot lead penetrate his skin as the impact drove him off his feet and backwards onto the bed. Blood gurgled up in his throat, splashing out over his lips and running down his cheek as Jack walked over to him with a smile, the faint sound of Vito’s laugh echoing in his ears. Jack was saying something, but it sounded like he was talking from the other end of a long tunnel, but he was smiling…and even though he knew he was already dead, the rage burned one last time inside of him. Jack: '…told you to never mess around, Cade. How many tim…' Jack’s last words were blasted away by four fast shots from the Ruger, two blowing huge chunks of flesh out of Jack’s back as they penetrated his chest, one missing, and the last one driving upwards through his chin as his brain exploded onto the roof above. Cade: 'Fuck you Jack…' He laughed as the darkness over took his eyes, but was able to watch as Carl walked up to him, pumping two more slugs into his chest with a small .38, but feeling no pain…then all went black. ~ End Scene ~ :: Rebirth :: ~ Scene ~ Cade had survived that night…saved by a mysterious man who lived in the desert like a hermit. Cade never knew how, or why, but the old man had somehow gotten him to a hospital and then out again before the police could question him. All the old man had told him was that he had taken him out right after surgery, and brought him back out here to the desert to recover in peace. Cade now stood out in the open sand, looking out over the waste of the desert as the hot sun beat down upon him. Slowly he dropped his eyes to the skin of his chest and the bullet scars that adorned it. He gently ran a finger over these; the pain of what happened over a year ago still enough to bring a tear to his eye. He could not save her, and she had trusted him to deliver her away from this place, this life, and into something happier. He had failed. But as he raised his eyes back to the desert around him, he promised HIMSELF something…that no matter how, when, or why…Vito would pay for her death. He would at least do her that favor; he would avenge her death so that maybe her soul could be at peace. 'You going to stand out here all day?' Cade turned slowly to face the old man, who had silently sneaked up behind him while he was immersed in the past and thoughts of revenge. Cade just looked upon him for a moment, this elder oriental man in his long black robe, his jet-black hair done up in a long winding ponytail. Cade couldn’t help but smile, this man seemingly so far out of place in this wasteland, yet right at home with the living death. Cade: 'I was just remembering someone, Master Kenshu.' Kenshu: 'The girl perhaps? I know that revenge weighs heavy on your heart, Cade-son…but do you think you’re truly prepared to face them again?' Cade: 'I don’t think I have a choice anymore. Don’t get me wrong, I appreciate all you’ve done for me here, Master Kenshu, but it has been a year, and it’s time to have their own poison shoved down their throats.' Kenshu: 'I understand your situation, but your skills are not yet complete, they need to be fine tuned, meshed within your soul, only then can you get that which you seek.' Cade: 'What more is there to learn? You have taught me everything you know, but I can’t put these skills to the test unless I can get my hands upon someone…and that someone needs to be Vito Manelli!' Kenshu: 'In time, Cade-son…all in time. But for now, I have another proposition for you, something that will help you hone the skills that you will need to seek your revenge. Come.' Cade looked slightly confused as he followed Kenshu back into the house…the same house at which Jackie had died over a year ago. He followed Kenshu into the living room where Kenshu pointed to the television and a program that was on. Cade followed his finger, watching as a man was suplexed out of his boots and then covered for a pin. Cade: 'WRESTLING?!' Kenshu: 'You speak it as if it were some disease. Yes, wrestling. Where else could you fine-tune your skills on such a regular basis? This would be perfect for you, Cade-son. You could regularly compete in matches, and this weekly repetition of fighting will greatly enhance the skills of which you have learned.' Cade scowled slightly at the television screen, but he could see the logic in which Kenshu spoke of. He would never admit it, but he had kept up with wrestling as a kid, even catching it a few times as he got older, but it was something he kept hidden from everyone he knew…or everyone he USED to know. Cade slowly turned back to face Kenshu, bowing low in respect before speaking again. Cade: 'Alright, Master Kenshu…I will do as you ask me to. But know this…I WILL have my revenge, and Jackie’s death will NOT go unpunished.' Kenshu: 'I know, Cade-son. Come…we have to make some…how do you say it…connections, to get you into the proper federation.' Cade nodded slowly as he followed Kenshu into the back of the house. The scene fades to black on the television screen and the program which still plays upon it…but the sport of wrestling was about to get a new face, one who cared nothing for the sport, but sought to avenge a love that was lost in violence, by any means necessary. ~ End Scene ~ Fade Out
