Dear Mr.Douglas

2 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12/2/02 The camera roamed the small villa, capturing the personal furnishings, showing a man of little taste.The camera made it's way over to a desk where a journal was sitting, it' cover saying 'the book of your wrestling god'Thus telling us who's book it was. With more questions than answers, the camera zoomed in with curiosity to todays date in time.... Dear Diary, It has been a little over 5 months since i left N.E.W.Thoes basterds diden't even know what to do with me.They booked me in no win situations.Causeing my career to hit rock bottom,Causeing my life to be effected in way's that it has not since my days in FWO.Or even back to my days as a child,Thoes days should be compared to nothing in my life being where i am right now. My career has always been a roller coaster and after walking out on N.E.W because they were not useing me to my full value.Night in and Night out it was me faceing Mr.Synnerster,That's what caused my depression.Not being in the ring with him,But always haveing to make him look good.It's hard makeing some one so bad look so good.The Question i'm always asking myself is how is it that the average pro wrestler today can not even carry his own weight in the company. Mr.Synnerster may hold a couple of victorys over me,But the whole world knows that I, Brad Douglas am the greatest wrestler ever!. Heh,Yes i know that's a bould statement with the likes of Kodiac Vic Creed and the Boston strangler still wrestling.But sure they could not last in the long run with me,The king of every ring. Hell,Who am i fooling by saying thoes lines every second that i have a shot.Everyone already knows what im capable of doing,I know what im capable of doing,Shoulden't that be enuff.Why is nothing ever good enuff from me,Nothing is ever good enuff.Not good enuff for the fans or for the promoters or even myself.Maybe i need to get back into control of where im going in this sport. Maybe it's time that the wrestling world actully starts to respect me rather then just blowing me off untill i completely destroy my competetion,Then they jump on the band wagon.Maybe i should just say fuck everyone and stop careing about what they think.Maybe it's time i just do thing my way. Focusing on the words 'MY WAYl' in the latter, the camera zoomed out.
