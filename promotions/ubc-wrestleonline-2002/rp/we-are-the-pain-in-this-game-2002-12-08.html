'We are the pain in this game'

8 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12-8-02 Steam flows through the air of the scorching hot locker room at Las Cruces, NM. The sound of water flowing from the shower fossit is hard as the mixture of blood and water is seen flowing down the back of Brett Wayne as he has his hands prompt up on the wall, blood runs down the wall from bretts cut up hands as he lets the water hit the top of his head. He stands there motionless for minutes as he continues to let the water slowly run down his cut up back from where he fell through the sheets of glass. His arms are bruised from the hard falls onto the concrete. The camera rotates around and gets a shot of Brett's black and blue face, dried up blood is sticking to his face as the warm water runs down slowly burning the cuts and scratch's. He continues to stand there with his back turned to the camera. I am the debt that cannot be paid. I am the pain in this game. If you don't beleive the words I speak then ask Evil One or Johnny Nightmare. If you call me out then you call out Pain. All these cuts, scratch's and Bruises you see right now is nothing compared to what I gave to Nightmare and Evil One. Evil One thought he could stand up to me, he though he would end 'My pain and suffering' well... HE DIDN'T! It seems I have inflicted the 'Pain and Suffering' into him. And for Johnny Nightmare... Me and him had a long conversation after our fight. He seemed to like my skills and I came to respect him. We made an agreement to have each others backs whenever needed and I will keep my end of the bargin but... Johnny if you happen to turn your back on me then you will receive even more pain then what I gave to you last night. Brett removes his hands from the wall as you can see an outline of his hand from the blood. He raises his hands eye level and picks the glass out of his hands. As he slowly pulls the glass out blood squirts out little at a time once he removes the glass he throws in on the ground and cups his hands letting them fill up with warm water. Then he splatters the warm water onto his face as blood from his hand and his face runs down his body remove only small portions of blood away. Now the Sudden Violence. Whoever these people are they will not ruin the UBC. Sudden Violence has to make there appearance sooner or later and when they do.... Brett turns around revealing the rest of his battered body as he begins laughing. You will regret coming to the UBC. I will make sure of that and so will Nightmare. We are the 'Pleasurably Physcotic.' We love giving and receiving pain. Its hard to beat the unbeatable. You all seen the things we did to each other when we fought against one another. Imagine what we will do together. We have the... SIZE SPEED STRENGTH AND THE SKILLS We have it all, no one is going to stand a chance against us and I MEAN NO ONE! Brett turns back around facing the wall and finish's up cleaning the blood off of his body. Once finished he opens the curtians and grabs a white towel. Blood still runs down his body on to the tower leaving blood stains on the towel. He then steps out and walks into the next room where his fight clothes are laying down on the ground as puddles of blood are leading from the door to his clothes to the showers. He walks over to his locker and opens it reaveling his fresh clothes. He then sits down infront of the locker as he put his elbows on his knees and leans over as he continues speaking to the camera. Now for all of you fighters out there who want some of this. I only have one thing to say. 'Come and Get It' Brett stands up as he pulls a black sleeveless shirt out of his locker and slips it on. He drops the towel and pulls out a pair of boxer briefs and slips them on. After putting them on he gets out a pair of kackies and slips them on over the boxer briefs. He then pulls out a pair of socks and sits back down, he slips both pairs of socks on and then slips on his white pair of shoes laying down underneith the chair he is sitting on as he starts to stand back up there is a knock on the door. -KNOCK - KNOCK- Brett walks over to the door slowly missing the puddles of blood on the floor. He gets to the door and opens it. His brother Mike is standing there smiling. You ready, I have everything loaded up and ready to go. Brett nods his head at Mike as Mike begins walking off, Brett turns around and takes one last looks at the locker room filled with his blood, his swear, and his Pain. When finished he flips the light switch and closes the door as he walks off, making the scene pitch black.
