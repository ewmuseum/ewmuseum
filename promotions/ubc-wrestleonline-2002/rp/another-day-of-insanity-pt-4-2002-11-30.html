'Another day of Insanity' Pt 4

30 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11-30-02 Previously Mike had taken Brett to a nut house for Brett to stay until his match Saturday. Mike hopes this will some how teach Brett to control his actions for when he gets angry. His first day in the Nut house was nothing but trouble for Brett, he thought he could handle things his way but was taught a lesson by his roommate 'Gramps.' Brett then was taken away by the Gaurds dressed in all white to a place yet to be indentified. The scene now picks up to the gaurds strapping Brett in a Straight Jacket. 'Damnit get this fucking coat off of me, I'm going to go Insane if you leave me in this damn coat' Brett says as he struggles to get out. The gaurds sit there and laugh at him as he struggles. 'That isn't all, wait and see were the real fun is' says one of the gaurds. They begin dragging him down the hall, he kicks and screams trying to get loose. Brett kicks the walls, knocks things off tables and so on still trying to get loose. They finally arrive to there destination. 'This is the place where you will be staying for a day or two' says another gaurd. Brett begins staring closely as one of the gaurds begins opening the door slowly. When the door opens Brett looks inside, the moment he see's its nothing but a padded room, he begins losing it. 'No....No damnit No!' Brett begins screaming he then breaks loose and takes off running down the hall. One of the gaurds is running right behind him, the gaurd then dives tackling Brett is he has to hold him down until the others arrive. 'Let me up damnit, let me up' Brett still screaming struggling to get loose. 'Hurry your asses up, I'm losing my grip damnit' Says the gaurd holding down Brett. Three others arrive and help pick him up as he still fights to get loose. They get him back to the entrance to the room and throw him through the door, slamming it shut behind Brett. Nooo! Damnit I can't handle this shit! Brett says banging his hand up against the door. -SOMEONE LAUGHING- Someone laughing can be heard outside the door. 'I told you that you don't mess with me' says the voice of Brett's room mate Gramps. 'Until you learn to control your actions, you will stay in this room so I suggest you start thinking of a way to learn how to control your actions.' Gramps says as you can hear his foot steps walk off as he continues to laugh. 'That son of a bitch, God I have to get out of this jacket thats for sure' Brett says as he try's to rip through the jacket. 'Damnit its not working, shit what have I got myself into. Brett says as he still sturggles to get the jacket off. Brett continues struggling for a few more minutes before giving up, he then walks to a corner and sits down. 'How is this supposed to help me, its doing nothing but making me go Insane and where the hell is my brother, he better come visit me soon enough.' Moments later Brett lays down. 'I'll take me a little nap' Brett says closing his eyes. Few minutes later Brett is sound asleep laying down in the padded room with only four walls surrounding him with no windows. Moments later the sound of a door opening can be heard. 'Take the jacket off of him and leave his food beside him while he's still sleeping' says an unknown voice. The camera turns to a few gaurds standing by the door as two more begin taking off his jacket nice and slowly as another one lays down his food beside him. They finish and Brett eyes begins opening as the gaurds run out of the room quickly, Brett jumps up running to the door as they shut it. 'Damn you stupid sons of bitch's you'll pay for this' Brett says as he notices they took off the jacket. 'Atleast the assholes took off this damn jacket and left me some food' Brett looks down at the food and laughs 'Are they just teasing me, that isnt enough food to feed a damn dog' He walks to the food and sits down 'Its better than nothing I guess' Brett says as he starts eating his food. Few minutes later he finishes and lays back down as he begins thinking to himself. 'Damnit I need to be working out or something instead of being in this place. I have a big match next saturday and he expects me to be locked up until the match. What is he thinking.' Brett continues thinking to himself for hours and hours, he eventually gets bored and begins doing some push ups. 1 . 2 . 3 . 4 . 5 He continues counting and he eventually makes it to three hundred and he quits and takes a breather. 'That felt damn good, time for some Crunches now' brett says getting down and laying on his back. 1 . 2 . 3 . 4 . 5 He does another three hundreds of these also and he stands up looking around at the walls. 'This isn't going to be so bad after all' Brett says looking around. He punchs the wall once and smiles 'This will do just fine for a punching bag, those people think the can cure me...ha, once insane always insane. You can't cure that problem I have, thats what makes me who I am, thats what makes me a fighter.' Brett puts an evil grin on his face and begins punching the wall, the scene fades as Brett starts laughing as the camera fades to black.
