Date: 1/01/03 Lord of the Ring . . . Lord of the Ring . . . Lord of the Ring . . . Lord of the Ring . . . Lord of the Ring . . . Lord of the Ring . . . Many thought we’d seen the last of MrSynnerster when he suffer a major beat down from Red Vision and Company. Many thought he was gone from the UBC, but that is not the case. The story you are about to read took place after the show and explain the whereabouts of MrSynnerster. There are some things you just DO NOT do. For instance, you DO NOT take a giant dump while at a funeral wake. You clinch your cheeks, and fucking hold it in. Just because ONE mothafucka is dead, doesn't mean the entire room has to be! You DO NOT curse in church. 'Why the FUCK NOT?' Because! You’ll end up at Xavier Newborne’s house AKA Hell, and who the fuck wants that? You DO NOT, and I repeat DO NOT do is fuck with MrSynnerster. And most importantly, you DO NOT stay up till Midnight on a weekday, and go out on the town! The scene begins in downtown Charleston South Carolina. The date is Dec. 17, 2002. The time is 11:45. Chuck town, as it is called by South Carolinians is colder than a witch’s tit in a metal bra. William Thornbush tries to grab Rosalind’s breast as the set up is written, but she swats his hand away. (Go figure.) Anyway, he’s colder than all get out. There is even a light snow on the ground. This is unusual for Charleston. Since there is snow on the ground that means everything is shut the fuck down. Everything that is except for The Movie Theater. A banner the size of Rhode Island hangs above the theater, promoting 'LORD OF THE RINGS: THE TWO TOWERS!' A line has formed outside the closed doors. It's like a costume ball, or something. Who the fuck wears a HAT that big, and a stupid blue bathrobe outside! Is that a guy in a Knight outfit? Earl Lee is right. There is not God, and the world went to hell in a hand basket, At the end of the nerdy line, about 30 people in length... we spot a baroness looking hottie dressed up like an elf, a guy dressed like a hobbit, and the ONLY 2 NEGROES in the line, One male and one female. Unfortunately, the two blacks are also dressed in LOTR grab. The man as a hobbit, and the woman in a black cloak, which covers her face when the hood is up. The black man looks like he spent the previous night shooting up heroin with those BAGS UNDER HIS EYES. I'm guessing, he has to work tomorrow. If you want names, the Elf is The Anastasia Morgan, The Hobbit is Anastasia’s date, and the two token blacks are William and Rosalind. All have came out for tonight's cinematic piece. Rosalind: “Why the hell are we out here freezing our Asses off in the cold. William has a match Saturday. He doesn’t need a cold. We could be at home right now. Sleep in a warm bed.” William: “That’s what I’m saying. Don’t get me wrong. I wanna see this movie just as much as the next guy but . . .” (William stops in mid-sentence to look up at the sea of costumed freaks standing in front of him.) “Okay, maybe I don’t wanna see it as badly as everyone else here tonight. But don’t you think I should be at home resting. I have a big week of training ahead of me. After my last Jurisdiction show, I really have to put on a better show. Red Vision as so payback coming his way. No one is using me to make themselves look good. Vision calls himself a pimp. Well I’m about to show him, pimpin’ ain’t easy. It’s time to stop talking and whip some ass. And I’m not trying to whip any ass standing out here dressed like this.” William looks down at his outfit. Stupid khaki vest, puffy pirate shirt, TIGHT KHAKI BIKER SHORTS... and wonder why the fuck can't he wear shoes?! William looks up. William: “I looked like the damn Crocodile hunter.” (pointing to Anastasia’s date) “And, whatever your name is. Why the fuck do you have furry feet? The guy looks down at his own outfit. Leather... animal fur and the clippings from his last haircut strapped to his feet with sparkle glue. Guy: (Answering with an attitude)“My name is Lance, but you may refer to me as FRODO! William looks at the camera and rolls his eyes. Then he stomps on Lance's 'hairy' feet!! Lance winces in pain, as he holds his sore foot in his hand and hops around. Rosalind: “William, that wasn’t nice.” William: “This is not a week to be nice. This is not a week to joke and be jovial. If I didn’t have you around, I’d be on a killing spree right now, starting with Xavier.” William slowly begins to caress Rosalind’s cheek. William: “She is Ever beautiful, Ever caring, Ever loving. She is my soul mate, my dream girl. She is the one person that can make me complete. Without her, I can only wander the world aimlessly. In the very depths of my soul, I know her all too well. For it is there that we are forever together. My love for her is never-ending. I would willingly give my life for her in a moment. Anastasia: “Aww. That’s kinda sweet in a creepy sort of way.” William: “Your boyfriend is wearing his own hair on his feet, and you’re calling me creepy? Anastasia: “He’s not my boyfriend. He’s my next-door neighbor. He’s the reason we’re dressed up like this. He said we had to dress up like characters from Lord of the Rings and we could get in the special screening.” William: “So it’s Lance’s fault we’re dressed like this?” William pushes Lance, who is still hopping on one foot. The push causes Lance to fall to the ground. Lance: “Eek, you’ll make me loose my ring.” William: “You’re ring’s not the only thing you’re about to lose.” Lance: “Help me my hobbit brothers. The king of Human’s has gone to the darkness.” William: “God help me.” Rosalind playfully pushes William, Rosalind: “Help him you big lug. Don’t take your anger for Red Vision out on the little nerd boy.” William reaches down to help up Lance, when someone grabs William by the back of his neck. The size of and the strength of the hand made if feel like the hand of God. The guy might not be god, but his name begins with a G too! A big tall NERD dressed like Gandolf grabs and pulls William up in the air then drops him back down. William looks the man up and down. William: “Who are you? Tommy Grady’s father. I mean Brother. I mean, what am I talking about. They’re the same person.” Gandolf': “Stay BACK ye Human King of Evil! Any further attacks on my hobbit friend, and ye shall meet my big brown stick.” William: “Riiiiiiiiiiiggggggggggghhhhhhhtttttttt. I got your big brown stick, right here buddy.” William grabs his crotch. Gandolf takes his massive hand and wraps it around William’s neck. Gasping for air, William looks at the camera and says. “I bet he gets his hand strength from masturbating to the unedited version of the Hobbit cartoon. Kinda like how Xavier Newborne has strong hands from masturbating to gay porn.” Gandolf squeezes tighter causing William’s eyes to bulge out. The line breaks up into a circle, with a bunch of 'Hobbits', 'Humans', 'Elves', 'Dwarves', 'Gandolfs', and 'TROLLS' cheering on the Middle EARTH SHOWDOWN! Gandolf lets out a giant laugh, as William’s hand begins to grow large like an inflated balloon. Soon Gandolf let’s out a loud scream and releases his hold on William’s neck. Gandolf begins hopping around on one foot. Standing beside him is Rosalind with an evil grin on her face. Rosalind: “Luckily the Ring Raves can wear whatever kind of shoes they want. Even nice heavy combat boots.” William: (While catching his breath) “Thanks baby.” Rosalind: “Whose your bitch.” The two giggle, then kiss. William looks at the camera. William: “See Mr. Newborne. That’s pussy talk. That’s some shit you know nothing about. Just remember. While you’re out paying for young boys to fulfill your little sick fantasy’s. I’ll be at home sleeping with my wife.” Rosalind: “Unfortunately dear. Not tonight. The movie is like 6 hours long. The sun will be coming up by the time we get out of here.” William: “Man, Rose, you just had to piss on my parade.” Rosalind: “Speaking of pissing on parades . . . look out.” Gandolf is now charging towards William, but to Gandolf’s surprise he is met with a right hand to the face. Followed by another one. William throws up a peace sigh to the Middle Earth wanna-be’s and gives The Wizard Gandolf and Karate Thrust to the throat. The gust of wind coming off the blow knocks over a pair of Dwarves, and an elf standing too close to the action! Hobbit 1: AGH! An ogre?! HERE?! LETS HEAD FOR THE HILLS, GENTS! William: “Oh for fuck sakes..” The group of hobbits pull out their tiny SWORDS, and raise them high! A loud 'HURRAH!' is heard, as they rush down the line, pushing and shoving! They run down the sidewalk, and make a sharp left turn. William shakes his head. Lance jumps up and down, trying to get a glimpse of the heads of all the TALL people... seeing if the doors have opened. Lance: “I don’t wanna miss the previews.” William: “Mothafucka, I don't get it. What's tha BIG FUCKIN' DEAL? We could've dressed up like this, and stay at home! Shiiiiit, we could hold our own shitty battles with wooden spoons! Hell if we wanna see a ring. I have my wedding band on right now.” Lance: “But those aren’t the rings we have to see. This is the second part of the Lord of the Rings Trilogy, you guys! HOBBITS VENTURING TO DESTROY THE EVIL RING! We have to see it! Math Time: Nerds + LOTR 2 Opening = HUGE MOTHERFUCKING RIOT! The doors of the Theatre swing open in unison as the huge crowd of Costume Nerds stand in awe for... a few silent seconds. The silence is QUICKLY broken as a war cry is shouted by a band of Orc/Geeks as they rush towards the door... A BAND OF HOBBITS cut them off, crashing into them with plastic shields and swords drawn! The fight begins as Hobbit and Orc punch away the tinfoil armor of their foes... BUT ARE THEN JOINED BY SEVERAL ELVES WITH PLASTIC BOWS DRAWN! Sticky arrows are fired, hitting Orcs and Hobbit alike, causing further chaos and destruction. Our heroes... push forward. William: “Why, this action is better than the last Jurisdiction show.” As William and company make their way through, a Nerd dressed like Gollum flies past yelling for his mother! William just shrugs then sticks his arm out... CLOTHESLINING THE NERD FOR NO GOOD REASON! Hey, he did say this was a week of pain. Lance tries to tunnel under the legs of several fighting Orcs, as Rosalind stands... totally unaffected by the fighting and nerds, looking confused. Rosalind: “Lance what the fuck are you doing now? Lance: BLAH! US HOBBITS ARE TUNNEL PEOPLE! DIIIIIIG SAM! DIIIIIG! William is handed a tiny sword from WHO KNOWS WHO. He examines the sword then says. “Kicking ass. Even Geeky nerdy ass is good training for Phil Payne.” William dives at the line of grappling Middle Earthlings! He goes tumbling onto the ground, with the several dweebs with glued on ears and feet! Lance manages to get out the other end of the 'leg tunnel', and rolls into the doors of the theater, throwing his arms at Rosalind and Anastasia.. signaling them to HURRY. Rosalind looks at Anastasia, she is about to say something but Ana stops her. Anastasia: “Don’t say it. I already know. I fucked up.” Rosalind: “Big time.” Rosalind and Anastasia proceed to walk alongside the sidewalk turned BATTLEFIELD. All of a sudden, an ELF grabs for Rosalind’s arm. Rosalind: “What the fuck?” Elf : THE ELVES WILL REMAIN IMMORTAL!! The elf reaches up and grabs Rosalind by one of her braids. Rosalind: “Oh hell no he did.” The camera pans over to Anastasia as her face grimaces as Rosalind inflicts pain too graphic for the camera to see. Rosalind walks back into the camera shot and dusts herself off. Rosalind: “When will muthafucka learn not to fuck with a black woman’s hair. That shit will get you hurt. Just watch and see what happens to Phil Payne. I owe his ass a couple of times for pulling my hair. Rosalind and Anastasia walk into the theater. Lance greets Anastasia with a hug, but she brushes him off, as William finally runs into the theater, CLOSING THE DOORS BEHIND HIM! William grabs a sword, and tucks it between the two door handles, LOCKING IT! Lots of banging is heard on the doors, from the outside as William turns to this friends...huffing and puffing. William: “ ......*SNORT*..... Damn, it feels good to be a human. Lance: “Are you sure it was a good idea to lock everyone else outside?” Rosalind: “FUCK Lance! I ain't ever seen so many crazy white people at one time, and I use to live near a country western bar. I think it's pretty fuckin' smart to not want to share a room with THEM for' 3 hours. William. . . get away from the window. Stop provoking them.” The camera pans over to William he is standing at a window giving the disgruntle middle earthers outside DX crotch chops. Rosalind walks over to William and pulls him away from the window. Rosalind: “Stop that. That’s not nice.” William: “Why not? I’m just promoting World Peace.” Rosalind: “How are you promoting world peace.” William: “The world would be a much happier place if everyone was getting a little (DX crotch Chop) PIECE.” Rosalind: (Hangs her head) “How did I know that was coming.” William: “Think about it. If everyone was getting some ass on a regular basis, they wouldn’t be uptight. Take Red Vision for example. Clearly the man is sexually frustrated. I know if I wasn’t getting any like him. I’d be a pissed off man, myself.” Rosalind: “Are you condoning his actions?” William: “Oh hell no. I’m still going to reign down on him with fiery and anger. I’m just saying I know where he’s coming from. That still isn’t going to excuse his ass from this beat down I got waiting on him.” Lance runs up, popcorn in hand. Lance: (whining) “Come on guys. We’re missing the previews.” William to Rosalind: “I see someone else that has a beat down waiting on them too.” Rosalind: “Damn skippy.” The two look at the camera flash a peace sign and walk away as the scene fades into William and company sitting in the balcony of the movie theater. William and Lance are both sporting beards. William looks at the camera and says, “Now you know sinners. The reason you haven’t seen me is because we’ve been locked up in this movie theater. And can you believe that anger mob is still out there. It’s been 2 weeks, and those fuckers are still there. But don’t worry. I got Lance working on digging a tunnel out of here, so I’ll be ready for Saturday match. So I’m sorry Mr. Vision, but you won’t be walking away with that title. It’s coming to me. Now if you’ll excuse me, I have to find a way out of here.” William flashes a peace sign and the scene fades out. . . . ---- Back
