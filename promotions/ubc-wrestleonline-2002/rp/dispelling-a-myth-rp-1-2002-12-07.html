Dispelling a myth... (rp #1)

7 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12-7-02 {The scene opens with a close-up shot of some dense jungle vines and trees. You can hear monkeys shrieking in the background and also you can hear a lion roaring. As the camera breaks thru the vines you see a stereotypical African village with children running around and women standing around a fire beating corn into a mash and singing some type of tribal song. Some of the men of the village are walking in from the jungle with a gazelle strapped to a pole. As they drop the gazelle down by the women they begin to join in singing. Several young girls can be seen next to what looks like the chief of the village's hut. The chief walks out of the hut and stretches for a moment before ducking his head back in and grabbing a spear and his head dress. The village chief walks towards the women with a very large head dress that is made out of a lion's mane. He begins speaking in Swahili when suddenly the camera frame freezes for a moment before it begins to bubble and melts away. The screen is blank for a moment before its replaced by a waist up shot of Malik Tehani and Amir Mubootu. Both men are wearing black sleeveless K. O. A. shirts and have an angry look on their faces. Malik Tehani is the first to speak.} Malik Tehani: Now wasn't that a nice picture of what some people think Africa is all about? Half naked women, villages out in the middle of nowhere and a bunch of lions and tigers and monkeys running around! Well, as nice as that sounds to some of you out there the Kingz of Africa are here to set the record straight! Amir Mubootu: That's right Malik! Now we have come to UBC to, how shall we say correct some mistakes of the past for a dear close friend. of ours who has some more pressing matters than soil his hands with the dirty work. We are in UBC to dominate each and every person or team that might stand in our way! What is the pressing matter I am speaking of, what is it that The Kingz of Africa wants in UBC! Well-- Malik Tehani: Whoa, Whoa Amir now you know the deal, nothing gets revealed till he wants it revealed. Until that time lets just bide our time and take care of the business in front of us which is the team of Dangerous Revolution. Sean Wonder, Steve Hanger both of you have made a very, very big mistake. You decide to bring it upon yourself to try and make a joke out of the Kingz of Africa? Did you really think we would let that pass without saying a word about it? Did you think that your words would go without being noticed? Well, my very uneducated Australian friends that is one mistake you will live to regret! Amir Mubootu: You two will have a lot to answer for! At The Pit you will learn why we are the Kingz are the most feared team in UBC. But you ask how can a team that hasn't displayed their talents be the most feared? Well, that's easy we have looked over the roster from top to bottom and nothing and I mean nothing impresses us! So be ready for a war Dangerous Revolution cause that's exactly what you are gonna get! Malik Tehani: Dangerous Revolution you want to trade verbal jabs till we meet at The Pit in New Mexico? Well, that's fine with me lets begin shall we? {Both Malik and Amir turn around for a moment and you can see on the back of Amir Mubootu's shirt it says 'The Swaziland Assassin' and on the back of Malik's shirt it says 'The Great Imhotep' the slowly turn back around and have slight smirks on their faces.} Malik Tehani: I see the great team of Dangerous Revolution is from the great place of Brisbane Australia. A great place I hear, but correct me if I am wrong but ain't Australia just a filthy prisoner colony from years ago? Didn't England drop off its works criminals and other low life's that they didn't want on their shores on that God forsaken island so many years ago? So basically the people of Australia ain't nothing more than descendants of common criminals and thugs. But thru the years the bloodthirsty edge that those criminals has dwindled to nothing but a whimper. So ask yourself do you really want a piece of this team that stands before you? Amir Mubootu: Sean, Steve unlike my educated friend here I couldn't care less where you are from, I really don't care what you have to say, I just want to get you two heathens into my domain and show you why they call me the 'Swaziland Assassin.' You see I really don't about what style people claim to have I just love to see the look of disbelief on their faces when they give me the very best they can and I am still standing. I thrive of pain, receiving it and giving it, the more I bleed the stronger I get. So Dangerous Revolution I really hope you are ready for what you are about to receive! Malik Tehani: Easy there big guy don't waste all that aggression on speaking to a camera we got more than enough time to do what is necessary to this team and still pay a little visit to the person we are really here for. Sean, Steve let this be your first warning that we are not the team you want to try and cut your teeth on we got more going for us than you will ever know and when we do finally meet at The Pit you will understand no matter how much you fight it you will be the first team to bow to the Kingz! {Malik and Amir get slight grins on their faces as the camera fades to black....}
