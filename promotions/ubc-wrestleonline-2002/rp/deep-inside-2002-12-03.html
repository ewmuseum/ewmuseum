Deep Inside

3 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12/3/02 [Memories fade after time, but some remain imprinted in our minds. A movie that forever scars our minds...] [No one could ever understand me...or where I came from. What I went through to become what I am. And I don't expect them too. But at the same time, I want to give some insight into my world. Into what I live daily...but more importantly...what fuels me to win.] 'Mike?' 'Hmm..' 'You awake?' 'No, I'm just talking to you...while asleep...' 'Oh, right...' [Mike rolls over in his bed, to face the love of his life. That beautiful woman he met 2 years ago. When she fell into his life...or rather...he fell into hers, after trying to show off with his skating skill, and wiping out directly at her feet...he felt like the world could never be bad again.] 'What's up?' 'I dunno, I was just thinking.' 'About?' 'You.' 'Oh reeeally??' [She giggles and slaps him lightly on the arm] 'Not that way! I'm being serious! Lately you've been...quiet.' 'I have??' 'Yes...and it's coming up on the anniversary...' 'Oh...right...' [Silence. Oddly enough can be the most deafening noise known to man.] 'And??' 'And what? It's the anniversary, that's all. Nothing special.' 'C'mon Mike...don't shut me out on this one. We've been through a lot...' 'Look...I don't want to talk about it right now...it's 1 in the morning, and we'll talk later...ok?' 'Fine...' [Jade rolls over and goes back to sleep. Mike...whose now awake and thinking, can't help but stay awake. All he can see is what happened.] [FLASH] [Through the rain, and above the thunder and flashes of lightning, a scream pierces through the air, cutting everything and everyone that hears it.] 'Enter the Pain...' [The flash of a knife blade shone through the night, as the lightning flickered off it, illuminating it's cruel deed. Another scream....blood....] 'Enter the suffering...' [Her muffled screams, and sobs, all combined into one as tears streaked her face. He slowly drained the life from her body, as he rocked with her body...] 'End it...' [Finally, her body went limp...losing all life. A sudden surge of energy shoots through me and I charge him. His knife comes at me, and catches my shoulder...cutting deep, but not deep enough.] 'Struggle...' [The knife comes close to me...but somehow I reverse it, driving it into his heart....he goes limp, and the night is over..] 'Realization...' [Cradling her lifeless body in my arms, my eyes glaze over, staring at the morbid scene. His body lie still in the corner, the silver and red knife blade still stuck in his heart...she's dead, he's dead...but it's over...] 'All at fucking 13 years old...' [Sister in his arm...Father with a knife in his chest...he can't take it. Kissing his sister on the forehead, he grabs his bag and leaves. Never to return....] 'That was eight years ago...come December 7th. The night of my debut match in UBC....' [Pity..] 'All emotions will be unleashed...poor Shadow. It's his fault, and I have nothing against him, but it's like a transformation....' [It will happen...beware...] [Fade]
