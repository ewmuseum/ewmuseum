'My Story'

29 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11-29-02 In the middle of the night a figure is seen walking down the street, as the rain pours down and the wind blows the man suddenly stops. He turns and faces the camera, the only thing that can be seen is his red eyes facing the camera. Can he take the pain away,is he the one. I need someone to, perhaps your the person who will take my pain away for good. Evil walks closer to the camera and a portion of his face can be seen now, he then grabs a knife from his back back and starts cutting his chest. You see, I have problems feeling my body, I feel no pain, I only hurt inside. Long time ago I was once like everyone else until I lost the person I loved most. God took her away from me so that there is when I joined the Evil side. He gave me the strength and power that I would have never had before and took away my feelingsbut he forgot to take away one of my feelings and that feeling is the pain I feel when I think of my Love One, that is why I ache so much, that is why I want to leave this world but they cursed me, I can't kill myself or I would. Only one person can do it but I do not know who it is or where he is. I may not ever find this man but then again I may find him. Its a small world now days and anything could happen. Evil steps out of the shadow to reveal his entire body. Back to the story of the Women I once loved....We were together all the time, even when I wrestled she was right there at ringside with me cheering me on. We never fought with one another we always got along with each other. She was really my true love. Evil has a tear running down his face but he doesnt realize it. She went to go see her grandmother in the hospital, and I was supposed to meet her there after my match but half way into the match I had a feeling that she was in trouble, I just walked out of the ring without even saying a word. I left the arena speeding to the hospital to where she was supposed to be but when I arrived she had not been there all night long. I was going insane yelling, screaming, punching holes in the wall, I absolutely went nuts. I then suddenly blacked out and when I woke up She was right there beside me looking down on me like an angel. I just sat there and staired into her eyes, I went out to reach for her but there was nothing there my hand just went right through her. Then moments later her mom came running in there crying, I asked her what was wrong and she sit there still crying, then It all came into picture. I knew then she had gone out of my life, that really was an angel I seen and God had taken her away from me. I just lost it after that, I went home without even saying a word to anyone, I sat down in our chair where she would always sit in my lap and think about the good times we had, then I suddenly rememered she had gone away and that there is when things changed. I wanted to be with her again so I decided to try and commit sucide. I walked out in the middle of the street, it was foggy and you couldn't see a thing. Soon enough a vehicle of some sort had to come and soon enough it did, and I didn't move. The car didn't see me until it had ran smooth over me, I was dead or atleast thats what they thought, I even thought that for a minute but I the seen a man in a black cape. He exact words were.... You see what God does to people like you, he destroys them leaving you behind suffering but I can take all that away if thats what you want. I actually beleived him, so I agreed. When I came out of my daze I was still laying in the middle of the road, people were staring at me asking me... Are you alright? I didn't say a word, I just stood up and walked off and went inside my house. I didn't feel any pain from the car hitting me so I tried something else, I took a knife and starting cutting my chest, still couldnt feel anything. I thought he really did it but then I seen my loved one picture hanging on the wall and suddenly I screamed in pain, I was hurting inside because she was still gone and now I still ache in pain when I see her face. Only two things ease my mind of her. Those two things are..... Relieving others of there pain and suffering and Fighting. Those are my only two things I ever do anymore and thats what I'll do until that one person ease's my own pain and suffering. Evil One walks away with a few tears running down his face as he continues thinking of his loved one, he then walks into the shadows into you see his figure
