:::Where Are the Flags?:::

9 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12.09.02 ...Oh boy! Does it ever feel good to be a part of a winning team. Ya gotta feel bad for Jurisdictions losers, including Outcast. It's gotta hurt to go home in pain and as the ultimate loser. Take Outcast, for instance. Tommy beat him down so bad he's been forced to take a break. Step away from the sport. It was more than he had bargained for. Now Tommy continues to climb to the top while Outcast sits on his couch, thinking about the horrible mistake he made by agreeing to take Tommy on in his opening bout. This is a mans business. And the next person to learn that lesson is gonna be Krisis. You made a big mistake by acting like a man, Krisis. But I respect that. You just saw Tommy whipe the floor with Outcast, but you had the coconuts to step up to him moments after. That's all fine and somewhat respectable. The fact is, on Saturday -- it won't look like such a good decision when your lying on a hospital bed with a flat line... :::Scene opens showing a theme park from a fair distance away. The camera cuts to the inside of this park where we see Tommy Grady standing in a line. Aaron Everage is right behind him in this line with Abner Estrada trailing behind him. Tommy has a goofy grin on his face as he gets nearer and nearer to the front of the line. He crosses his arms across his plain white shirt. He pauses a moment and adjusts his sunglasses before turning to Aaron::: Tommy Grady - I'm so glad you found free tickets just laying on the ground! Aaron Everage - That's somethin', huh? Tommy Grady - Of course it's somethin'.. anything is somethin'.. I'm somethin', you're somethin', Abner is somethin'.. this hairy, stinky man with some sort of crader on his neck in front of me is somethin'.. Aaron Everage - Alright, alright.. enough.. we're all somethin'. I get it, Tommy.. Tommy Grady - Good, I'm glad I could be of help! :::Tommy hands his ticket to the ticket clerk and she rips it::: Tommy Grady - You asshole! Aaron Everage - TOMMY! Get the fuck in there.. :::The ticket clerk seems offended as Tommy snags the ticket from her, looking angry as he walks into the park::: Tommy Grady - She ripped my ticket! Now what am I supposed to do? Aaron Everage - You're already in the park.. you don't need the ticket anymore.. Tommy Grady - ..... oh.. :::Aaron sighs::: Tommy Grady - So where are these six flags? Let's check 'em out so we can get on our way.. Aaron Everage - It's not six flags, Tommy. Six Flags is just the name of this place.. it's a theme park.. Tommy Grady - What's the theme? Aaron Everage - Six Flags.. Tommy Grady - Haven't heard the six flags theme.. I've heard the Tommy Grady theme.. I GET STUPIFIED! It's all the same you say, live with it but I don't get-- Aaron Everage - Not a theme song.. nevermind, Tommy.. you know that game you play on the computer where you build a rollercoaster? Tommy Grady - And I make too many loopity loops and my loopity loop runs into the ground? Aaron Everage - Right.. that's what this place is.. Tommy Grady - They've used my plans?! DAMNIT! That was MY creation.. Aaron Everage - No, this is another persons creation.. Tommy Grady - That bastard! Aaron Everage - Well, he had the money to create his idea.. and now we get to ride his rides.. Tommy Grady - Wait a minute.. we're actually actors in the game? Where can I type my complaint to the game player? I don't appreciate not being asked to be a part of his little game.. I have a career, you know.. what would Kron say? Aaron Everage - No. This place is real.. you aren't in a game, Tommy.. geez, must I spend my entire life explaining things to you? Tommy Grady - Would you? That would rock my world! Aaron Everage - Normally I'd say I need a beer, but I think I'd rather you just have it.. Tommy Grady - AH! Aaron Everage - :::jumps a little bit::: WHAT!? Tommy Grady - MRSYNNERSTER! HIDE ME! HE'LL BORE ME TO DEATH! Aaron Everage - Tommy, relax.. it's unscooped crap from that horse.. Tommy Grady - Oh.. I thought he was just holding hands with his mom.. Aaron Everage - No.. the horse is just stepping in it's own business.. Tommy Grady - Lucky for me, huh? Aaron Everage - Sure thing.. Abner Estrada - I'll go get us a couple beers.. Aaron Everage - Please do.. :::Tommy stops and stares at something with mouth open::: Aaron Everage - What's the problem now, Tommy? Tommy Grady - They let Bugs out of cartoon world! There he stands right before my eyes! I've never met a celebrity before, Aaron.. Aaron Everage - You met Snoop Dogg once.. don't forget him.. Tommy Grady - I don't count that cuz he just said izzle over and over again with different letters.. shizzle bizzle kizzle mizzle for dizzle.. then he blew green smoke in my face and I went unconscious for a while.. and I woke up with a black eye lying on the ground.. it was like that fake wrestler guy that spits green mist into your face except this was for real.. and stinkier.. Aaron Everage - Fo' shizzle.. Tommy Grady - Don't tell me YOU caught the izzle, too.. Aaron Everage - No.. you said fo' dizzle.. it's fo' shizzle.. Tommy Grady - I'm sorry I'm not down with the LBC ebonic lingo, Aaron.. now if you'd excuse me.. I have a child stealing my favorite television stars attention when he should be talking to me.. if you don't mind.. Aaron Everage - Don't embarass me, Tommy.. :::Tommy turns and laughs as if he thinks Aaron is kidding. Aaron shakes his head as Tommy approaches the man dressed in a Bugs Bunny costume. Tommy nudges a little boy away from Bugs::: Tommy Grady - What's up, Doc? :::Bugs waives::: Tommy Grady - Now you do it.. :::Bugs shrugs::: Tommy Grady - OH! I get it.. you can't talk out of cartoon world.. that's cool.. :::Bugs nods his head::: Tommy Grady - It's an honor to meet you.. I always liked it when you fooled that Fudd dude.. that ROCKED! :::Bugs nods his head again::: Tommy Grady - Well, it was a pleasure, dude! :::Bugs gives Tommy the thumbs up. And Tommy turns away::: Aaron Everage - How'd it go? Tommy Grady - He's a major lame-o.. that made Snoop Dogg seem like meeting the yellow teletubbie.. Aaron Everage - Do you realize you idolize a show for children? Tommy Grady - Teletubbies?! They have lessons for ALL ages.. it depends on your perspective.. with one as narrow as yours you'll never learn anything.. Aaron Everage - You're driving me crazy.. :::Abner walks up balancing three cups of beer::: Aaron Everage - Thank God! Tommy Grady - I know.. thank God for the teletubbies.. Aaron Everage - No.. beer.. thank God for beer.. Tommy Grady - I don't think God appreciates you thanking him for alcohol.. it was probably created by satan, actually.. :::Aaron hands Tommy a beer::: Aaron Everage - If you drink it within ten seconds I'll buy you an ice cream cone.. :::Tommys eyes get big and he instantly puts his cup to his lips and chugs it down, with a little dripping off the side of his mouth. He slams the cup down. Aaron hands him another drink and Tommy goes after that one, as well. As soon as he downs them both he breathes hard::: Tommy Grady - Did I qualify? Aaron Everage - Oh yeah.. you qualified.. Tommy Grady - WOO HOO! ICE CREAM! Aaron Everage - I mean.. no you didn't qualify.. sorry.. you were a second off.. :::Tommy narrows his eyes. Obviously the alcohol has hit the veins::: Aaron Everage - Tommy? Tommy Grady - Don't Tommy me, asshole.. you promised me some fuckin' ice cream and you'll give it to me or else I'll stick my foot so far up your ass I'll make a foot puppet outta you, do you understand me, Aaron? Aaron Everage - .... excellent.. Tommy Grady - Excellent? What, you think this is a game? You think I'm joking? Aaron Everage - No.. there's no joke.. Tommy Grady - Well, that's good to hear, Aaron.. cuz I can only take one joke at a time. And right now I'm dealing with the biggest joke of all -- Krisis. The man isn't even civilized, for Christ's sake. He attacks any human being he has the opportunity to attack. He doesn't use common sense. He reminds me of a person I once saw at a freak show.. he had the ability to bend enough to stick his head near his ass. Well, that's ALMOST Krisis.. except he's doing a three sixty right INTO his ass. He doesn't know what the hell is goin on and it's all coming down right in front of him. Maybe he now realizes his mistake. Maybe he now knows that Tommy Grady is the wrong guy to mess with. I can't blame him. Maybe he should have thought about that before he sealed his fate in a match on Jursdiction. But sure enough, I'll meet him in just a few short days. And when we meet face to face, Krisis.. I'm gonna teach you a new meaning to the word pain. Something you've never felt before. You can watch tape from my Outcast match.. you can see my tendencies.. my faults.. you can even see my strengths.. but strategy is something that won't help you one bit.. I see you acting like a fool on national television.. jumping on anyone you get the opportunity to jump on.. I can handle that. I can handle watching you do that, because while you're doing that I'm laying in wait. Preparing myself for Saturday.. preparing myself for another victory on my way to a fat payout and the Ultimate championship belt. So while you're still wrestling around in your jeans, challenging for a UBC pipeline championship -- I'll be bling blingin' it with the Ultimate Championship around my waist. Krisis, you're nothin! And that is exactly how I'm gonna treat you come Saturday. I'll hold back nothing.. I have no sense of what is right and what is wrong. I have no set of mental boundaries and I don't plan to create them for your sake. Come Jursdiction, my friend.. you'll be regreting the day that your fat disgusting welfare ridden mother brought you into this world.. the same way that I despise her for making us put up with your embarassing antics that would embarass any man -- knowing that you pack the same lunch we do. There's a rude awakening lying and waiting for you to come down the aisle.. step into my world.. and it's only a few days away, my friend.. :::Aaron's arm sticks out, handing Tommy an ice cream cone::: Tommy Grady - Thanks Aaron.. Aaron Everage - Not a problem.. Tommy Grady - What do you say we go try that Mr. Freeze ride.. Aaron Everage - It would be an honor.. Abner Estrada - With all of Tommy's talk of green smoke earlier I'm in the mood to toke it.. Tommy Grady - Drugs are bad.. Abner Estrada - So is Bill Clinton, but everybody thinks he's so great.. Aaron Everage - Good point.. Tommy Grady - Very good point.. Abner Estrada - So you wanna toke it with me, then? Tommy Grady - Me peeing into a cup for UBC seems to give it away, for some reason, when I do.. so I'm out.. Aaron Everage - Do you realize how much more money we'd have if you didn't waste it? Abner Estrada - Yeah.. crazy isn't it? Aaron Everage - Jerk.. Tommy Grady - Right.. just like I.. ME.. Tommy Grady is gonna do to Krisis at Jursdiction.. jerk him one way.. jerk him another.. give him a black eye.. fractured jaw.. bruised hip.. anything it takes. There will be blood.. there will sweat.. and your tears will be covering the ground when it's all said in done. Don't say I didn't tell you so, Krisis.. you WILL be defeated.. and that ain't just a promise.. oh no.. it's a guaruan.. DAMN.. tee.. :::Tommy nods for the crew to follow him. The camera stays put and focuses in on Tommy walking away. Aaron is noticably talking to Tommy as they walk as Abner trails the two of them fumbling through his pocket. The camera slowly fades to black::: ...So what did we learn if we're Krisis? We have learned that Tommy Grady.. the Tommy Grady of old.. is lying in wait.. inside the less intellegent Tommy we know now. And that Tommy is preparing at all time for his competition. So if we are Krisis, are we scared? Can ya milk a cat? No. At least I don't think so -- the fact is, though.. as long as alcohol runs through the veins of our Drunken ICON there is no way that he can be defeated. He is simply the most overpowering human being in the UBC. So c'mon.. try and put a licken on him, Krisis. Just try him. You'll fail and you'll fail miserably.. in your own blood...
