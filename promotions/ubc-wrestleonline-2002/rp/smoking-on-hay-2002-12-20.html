Smoking on Hay

20 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12/20/02 SMOKING ON HAY IN THE MIDDLE OF THE BARN . . . > SMOKING ON HAY IN THE MIDDLE OF THE BARN. . . > SMOKING ON HAY IN THE MIDDLE OF THE BARN. . > SMOKING ON HAY IN THE MIDDLE OF THE BARN. . . > SMOKING ON HAY IN THE MIDDLE OF THE BARN. . . > SMOKING ON HAY IN THE MIDDLE OF THE BARN The scene opens with a wide-angle view of down town Charleston South Carolina. In the middle of the shot is William Thornbush, also known to the wrestling world as MrSynnerster, running down the cobblestone road. William is dressed in a pair of orange nylon pants, an orange CLEMSON TIGERS basketball jersey with a blue short sleeve shirt underneath, and blue Nike shoes. William stops running and begins walking to cool down from his jog. Out of the corner of his eye he notices the camera, so he turns his attention to that direction. William: “hello sinners. Good to see you again. I just thought I’d come out here for a little jog. You know, keep myself fit for Saturday’s match. I may make fun of Violent B and Chris Carter, but even I’ll admit they are decent wrestlers. If they can stay focused they may actually have a chance of beating me.” William immediately doubles over laughing. William: “Shit I was hoping I could keep a straight face when I said that. Oh my God that was funny. The Wanna-be gangsta’s beating me? Not gonna happen. Even on my worse day, I’m still better than them on their best day. Do they even know who they are wrestling, because I have yet to hear them mention my name. Not that it matters. I can never stay awake long enough to watch an entire promo done by Mr. B or Mr. Carter. That’s what’s got me out here now. I got bored watching the latest UBC promos. So I figured I’d come outside, since adventure seems to follow me. I bet just minutes from now something interesting is going to happen.” As soon as those words come out of William’s mouth, the camera pans to off in the distance, where an old burgundy colored convertible Cadillac with the top down is driving down the road towards William. Cypress Hill’s I WANT TO GET HIGH blares from the car’s stereo. As the vehicle gets closer to William, the driver flips a button causing the front of the car bounces up and down. The camera quickly pans back over to William. William: “See what I mean. For those of you not familiar with Charleston. A car hitting switches in not a common occurrence. Which only proves what I just said. Adventure seems to follow me.” The car pulls up beside William. The hydraulics lowers the car almost to the ground. Driving the car is Eddie Guerrero wearing a pair of baggy Kaki pants, a white wife beater and a short sleeve green Hawaiian shirt unbutton. In the passenger seat is Chavo Guerrero dressed in similar attire. Sitting in the backseat are Rey Mysterio Jr. and John Cena. Rey is wearing a pair of blue jean overalls and no shirt, and oddly enough his red Spiderman looking mask. Cena is wearing a black Eddie is my favorite wrestler shirt, baggy blue jeans, and a black hat turned around backwards. Eddie: “What’s up homes?” William: “What’s Up Eddie.” (William and Eddie give each other a pound.) “What’s up Chavo.” (William and Chavo give each other a pound.) “What’s up Rey Rey.” (William and Rey give each other a pound.) “What’s up John.” (William starts to give John a pound, but stops just before their fists touch.) “What the hell is John Cena doing with y’all?” Eddie: “Cena’s our bitch, homes.” William: “Well that would explain those gay light blue trunks he wears to the ring.” Cena: “Hey, my nana made those trunks for me.” Chavo: “Shut up bitch. We didn’t give you permission to speak. Chavo reaches into the backseat and smacks John in the back of the head, which causes John to start crying. Cena: “I just wanted to fit in.” Chavo: “You stop that crying, before I give you something to cry about.” John wipes away his tears and tries not to cry anymore. Eddie: “White boys. What can you do with him huh? You know what I’m sayin’ homes.” William: “I know what you’re sayin’.” Rey Rey: “Damn, I wish we had Kidman with us again. At least he’d let us ass fuck Torrie while he videotaped on his Kidcam. What good is Cena?” Cena: (With tears in his eyes) “Why do you say such hurtful things?” John begins to weep loudly. Eddie and William both hang their heads and give each other a ‘ain’t this some shit’ look. Chavo shoot Rey an evil look. Chavo: “You see what you did, Rey. You know this bitch cries like a beauty pageant winner.” Rey: “What am I suppose to do?” Chavo: “Say you’re sorry or something.” Rey lets out a long sigh, shakes his head in disgust and then half-heartedly pats John Cena on the back. Rey: “It’s okay homey. I’m sorry. You’re just as cool as Kidman.” Cena: “Am I really as cool as Kidman?” Rey: “Hell no. Did you not just hear me say Kidman let us ass fuck his real life Barbie doll Torrie. Now stop that damn crying before I give you something to cry about.” John goes back to crying loudly. Chavo: “That’s it. Get yo’ ass back in the trunk.” Chavo and Rey man handle John Cena out of the backseat and stuff him in the trunk of the car. Meanwhile Eddie asks William, “What are you up to homes?” William: “Nothing much. Just getting in a little training for my match Saturday against Dumb and Dumber.” Eddie: “Yeah, that’s going to be a tough one there homes. They might give you a run for your money.” Rey and Chavo stop beating up on John and there is a moment of silence as everyone stares at Eddie for a second before busting out laughing. Chavo looks at the giggling John Cena and asks, “Did we give you permission to laugh?” Cena: “No.” Chavo: “That’s right. Get yo’ ass in the truck.” Chavo and Rey throw John Cena in the trunk and slam the trunk lid. Eddie continues talking to William. Eddie: “Those punita Violent B and C Squared don’t stand a chance against you homes.” William: “I know. But they can’t be any worse than Brad Douglas or that guy that didn’t showed up. Then again most of the people in the UBC are losers. Have you seen the fucker with the goofy glasses doing the DX crotch shot. What’s he’s name, Tommy Grady. Someone please tell him DX died serveral years ago, and it’s ghost is not to be resurrected. Someone tell Mr. Grady, if he wants a point in the L column, I’ll be happy to give it to him. Damn, just thinking about that goofy smile he has on his face makes me want to puke.” Eddie: “Shit, you got that right homes. Hey, you wanna roll with us. We’re about to head out and see what kind of trouble we can get into. Maybe go by my grandfather’s farm and tar and feather John Cena.” William: “I don’t know. I really should be training. The Wanna be Gangsta’s might put up a bigger fight than. . . ahh fuck it. We’ve already done that joke twice. Let’s go.” William jumps in the backseat where John was sitting. Eddie hits the switch, the hydraulics lifts the car, and they roll out. The scene changes to car being parked outside of an old red burn. Eddie just finished rolling up a blunt and takes a hit of the blunt. Immediately afterwards he starts coughing heavily. The coughing gets so bad he falls to the floor of the car. Chavo: “That must be some good shit man. Uncle Eddie hasn’t cough like that since back in the day, yo.” Chavo takes the blunt from Eddie, tokes the fatty and ends up on the floor coughing like Eddie. As Chavo coughs to the floor, he hands the blunt to Rey. Rey: “I was reading up on the UBC the other day. Why are your wrestling two men. I mean Wuzupwitdat?” William: “You’re stealing material from the Hurricane now?” Rey: “No, I’m parodying Hurricane. Just like you do.” William: “Point take.” Rey: “Since you’re a guest you can have a hit before me.” William: “Nah man. I don’t do that shit.” Rey: “What you don’t smoke man?” William: “I did a couple of times back in college. But I don’t do that shit now. It kills brain cells. You’ve seen my promos. I don’t need to kill anymore brain cells.” Rey: “Come on Synnerster. Stimulate your mind. God put this here. For you and for me. You can smoke this and get inside Billy McStoners head.” William: “Alright alright. I’ll smoke it if it will shut y’all up. Damn this is the second time this week someone has tried to get me to smoke with them.” Eddie and Chavo slowly get back in their seats as William takes a hit. Eddie: “Don’t hit it so hard homes.” William: “I know what the fuck I’m doin’ Man. I’ve been smokin’ since I was 2.” Eddie: “Shut up.” William takes a hit. Then starts to feel funny. William: “What’s in this?” Eddie: “It’s sssssssssssssssssssssss Gold dust.” As soon as that was said, William starts to spaz out and crazy himself like he has bugs crawling all over him, then he jumps out of the car and runs around. Meanwhile Chavo asks. “Who’s got muchie duty?” Eddie: “Cena usually has it, but he’s still locked in the trunk, so Rey has to go, since he made that bitch cry.” Rey: “Alright alright. What does everyone want?” Chavo stands up in the car and says “Get some sour cream, onion chip. With some dip, man. Some beef jerk. Some peanut butter. Some Hogan Daas ice cream bars. A whole lot of them. Chocolate, make sure their chocolate. . . (Eddie gives Chavo a crazy look, but Chavo pays it no mind and continues on.) Some Popcorn, bread, popcorn bread, Graham Crackers, Graham crackers with the little marshmallows. Make some S’mores, Also some celery, grape jelly, Captain Crunch with the little crunch berries. We need pizzas. Yeah, two big pizzas with everything on them And water. Lots of water. And ahh. . . some Funyons. Yeah.! Yeah!” Chavo continues to say yeah, and then begins whining about missing Pepe the horse. Rey: “Anything else?” Eddie: “Oh yeah. Give me a box of condoms. And um, what’s that stuff. I use to eat it all the time back in the day. Oh yeah. . . pussy.” Rey: “Alright I’m out. What are we going to do about MrSynnerster?” William goes running pass the car dressed in just a pair of boxers. Eddie: “I don’t know, but he scares me.” The camera continues to follow William on his wild run. The scene changes to a shot of William sitting in a chicken coop trembling then fades out to a shot of William sitting on a park bench talking to the camera. William: “I was in Grandpa Guerrero chicken coop sweatin’ like a slave. It took Rosalind to come get me out, and I ain’t been right ever since. That’s why I’m like fuck Eddie Guerrero.” (William looks around) “Wait a minute. What was I talking about again?” A voice off camera says in a slow staggered way. “I . . . don’t know I was axin’ you about your shoes. Those look like real comfortable shoes.” William: (Looking at the person off camera.) “Who are you?” The camera pans out to show a man sitting beside William on the park bench. He is dressed in a gray suit with a white shirt. His brown hair is in a short crew cut, and in his lap sits a box of candy. Man: “My name is Forest. Forest. .G. . .” William: “Stop. Don’t go there. (William looks back at the camera.) “See what I mean. I just ain’t right. I got some ass to go kick. Catch you later Sinner.” William throws the camera a peace sign, then gets up from the bench and runs off camera. The camera continues to focus on the Man in the gray suit. Man: “Mama always said MrSynnerster’s promos are like a box a chocolates. You never know what you’re going to get until you bite into it. Bye.” The man waves like a little child, and the scene fades out.
