Loyal?

30 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/30/02 It doesn’t feel like it’s been that long. It feels like only yesterday since Brad Douglas was the man in the wrestling world.Hype Wrestling world champion 2 times,Not too mention the last ever world champion before the company closed it's doors.That was supposed to be the end of that great run that was Douglas's wrestling career. He had little success in little indy federations that he once had.Bradley paved the way for alot of men in this sport today.But as the pormoters say Brad 'got lost in the system' Many ask was it simply because he was sick and tired of wrestling.Or was it because wrestlign was sick and tired of him? 'Talent does what it can, genius does what it must.' Brads voice sounded enthusiastic as he thoes words. Brad's bodyguard standing tall behind him as he walks around the clean streets of Lyon,France. A young man in his late teens attempts to talk to Brad.But the large bodyguard pushes his palm into the chest of the young man. 'What's your buisness here' grumbles the large man. 'Geez,I just wanted to talk to my idol.I could not belive my ears when i heard Brad returned to Lyon.Can i speak to him?' The bodyguard lowers his head so that Bradley can wisper something to him. 'Fisrt off...Brad would like to be called 'The champ' and second don't refer to 'the champ' as 'HIM'.He finds it as an insault from a peon like you.' The young man has a look upon his face as if he just found out that his father was really his brother. 'Kid,What is it you wanted to ask me,'The champ' Brad Douglas?' Brad says while holding his head up high. 'Bradley..I mean 'The champ' i just wanted to say that it is my honor to meet you.You'r like my favorite wrestler ever.' Brad looks down at the kid with a frown on his face. 'OMG,YOUR LIKE MY FAVORITE WRESTLER!, you know how many times i've had to hear thoes fucking words.It's the part that some snot nosed punk liek you is saying that.You know nothing about wrestling history.Do you even know who Rick Rude is?' 'No,Mumbles the you man.' 'You call yourself a wrestling fan.You don't know shit about wrestling history.Atleast my older wrestling fans know there stuff.Go home and study you stuff kid.' Brad signals his bodyguard to get the kid out of his sight. 'Well Tito,That's enuff of this little fresh air break.Take me home.As Bradley say's that they start to walk back towards there limo witch is quite visable now. Scene fades into the back of the limo shortly after. 'My Ego...HAHAH i don't have a ego thoe brandon.' The limo driver looks back at Brad and rolls his eyes. 'Brandon,You don't have to watch over me.I don't have a large ego,Well maybe at times i do.But im able to because im a 24 year old icon in this industry.Yeah i know UBC does not take any shit from anyone....Chill out man.Hey look i gotta jet so call me back in about half a hour.' Brad hangs up his cell phone 'Anyways,As i was saying.That's whats wrong with the wrestling fans today.They're just in it for the moment,when someone they think looks cool is the champ.Then they stop watching the sport.Reminds me of someone i once used to call my friend.William Thornbush also known as Mr.Synnerster we were friends early in N.E.W he helped me when i first started working for that company.Then when i started to get bigger then him he stabbed me in the back. Oh well,What does not kill me only makes me stonger right? So now they book me and William in UBC.This means i get to beat the hell out of him like i always wanted to in N.E.W.Let's just hope im not too outrageus for the rest of UBC' Black
