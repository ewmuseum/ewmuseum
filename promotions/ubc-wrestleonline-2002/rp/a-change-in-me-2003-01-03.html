-|- A change in me -|-

3 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6.6.6 (OOC: Red Vizionz is going through a gimmick change, I'm turning his all goth so he goes through different styles before he finds the perfect one.) -|- Heavy breezes cast on thick vapors propelling curtains in dance on sultry winter night. Electrified air sets emotions afire, felt heavy on the skin, with a foreboding of doom, yet without really knowing. As a storm brews on dark horizons, announcing its approach with a light show of electricity. A low rolling symphony of percussion follows, a crescendo portent of violence building. Exciting even at risk of possible destruction. Disaster or promise, merely a twist of fate, flashes of lightning cast flickering shadows. -|- 'Christopher...' Knowing that hell is on the horizon, words seep from his lips at a monotone only elaborating the fear at which is to come, 'Ravnos Lestat III is awaken.' 'I know my brother, that's why we must finish this spell to cast him away from this deminsion for good. If he returns, the state of well being will be tarnished forever.' Sprinkling red sand in a cirle around them at which red candles lit with blue flames blend in with darkness from far making it an eerie dark. -|- Rain falls in heavy drops making a thin sheet before the tomb which lies Red Vizionz, great grandson of the great Lestat. Cobwebs sewing themselves from wall to wall appearing ancient, vacant of spiders. Another rumble shakes the heavens as a streak of lighting crawls through dark clouds pregnant with rain. -|- 'He wants our blood Christopher! He will not rest until he is bathing in our precious blood Christopher! Don't you understand?! God can not save us as he is a Vampire too! Our souls will be rung of spirit and our bodies will be sucked dry! I can feel his presence, I can feel his piercing teeth and cold breath while he curesses my neck!' 'SILENCE! What is this you say about our Lord?! You will be damned for fate will never forgive you!' In total dismay Jonathan could ever let such foul spewing words enunciate off his dry tongue. 'Christ the Vampire. He was a magician in ancient Palestine. The Romans tried to kill him. ... Only they didn't know to drive a stake through his heart. So he has lived ever since, appearing to people who are weak. Whoever accepts his kiss gets sucked into the whole trip and becomes a mindless zombie wandering around trying to suck in the living by saying things like 'Jesus is the answer.'' His theory made Christopher think, such questions getting closer to answeres, such sterness present in his tone. 'After all, who else but a vampire god would have his disciples drink his blood and eat his flesh in return for everlasting life? ...' -|- The sound of thick rock sliding over another reigned in sound through the tomb. Bats flew, rats ran, signifying death. Out, rising from the shot dust creating a slight fog, Red's hand creeps up to life. Smelling the sweet scent of crimson liquid arosed him, made him want more. Made his teeth itch. Bones cracking resulting in moving from stealth from over a thousand years. Vision of light crimson mixed with cynnism, Ravnos noticed how quickly the pendulum had swung since the beginning of his hibernation. Things were different, people didn't believe anymore, revenge... just ripened! The paradox of unquestioned blood shed will be accomplished without contradiction, Red was finally awaken, and thirsty. -|- 'Your soul has has become unpure Jonathan. Do not let darkness devoir your soul for thou's blood will crave anothers! Do not give into Johnathan, the light, or grace will never accept you. Neon skies will crumble your body within, if you do not help preform this ritual, no more will the world be safe!' Citing truth, Christopher tries to not let Jonathan make the wrong decisions. 'Who feels my pain?! Only one, the one they say feels nothing the one they say uses 'mind control'. Who feels my sorrow? He does, the one they call cruel, the one they fear and do not understand I tell you this, he knows me more than society, more than family, friends without having met or spoken to me. I know he understands, he has undergone real anguish, he truly feels my pain...' Just then Jonathan's face contorts into blackened veins crawling with rabid evil up to his hair line, or lack of. But a hood hides that to whom, Christopher, already knows and stays calm while blowing all the candles out. 'It is to late... Evil approaches...' *ARG! Blood stains the walls and paints them dark red while creeping down them with a forbidden emphasis. -|- A sudden *thud* signifies Christopher's shell, drained clean of blood, hitting the ground lifeless. Almost with day light vision, Red stalks his prey in Jonathan, circling him as if he were a vicious wolverine volture. -|- 'Are you ready to die?' Slied, it came out of Red's mouth with such smoothness that made your skin crawl and your adrenline pound at your heart. Mumbled laughter made Red stop in the darkness, his teeth insert back from it's previous state of slaughter. 'You're nothing but a simple vampire with a history you choose not to carry on its great burden. It should have all been me!' Through pitch blackness, Jonathan's words slightly being repeated back by the walls of the temple. 'You're weak, and you will stay weak, for blood will never grasp your veins! Freeze in hell... Brother!' -|- Everything suddenly went completey white for Red Vizionz, it was his allowance for his evil intensions. -|- ---- Back
