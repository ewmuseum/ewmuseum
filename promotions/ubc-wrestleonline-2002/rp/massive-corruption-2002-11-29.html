Massive Corruption

29 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11.29.02 Introduction: So the vicous cycle of Da Thug and his legal problems has already started up again, obviously by watching Da Thug's last BMW promo, you'd know that. Now besides the fact of possibly being wrongly accused, Da Thug recently had his court trial for his so called 'crime'. In the state of being accused of influencing a murder, Da Thug never did find out the result of his court trial. Something does't seem right about the whole issue, a possible conspiracy maybe? In any case, Da Thug is still in prison, it's about 2:00 AM and he is asleep... The dark, cold walls of Da Thug's prison cell surround him while in a deep sleep on his hard, roughed up prison bed. He is wearing a beater and some black shorts. His orange prison uniform is folded, lying on a shelf next to his sink. The cell is a place nobody on earth would want to be, but it's the place criminals are sent for the crimes they committed. Often times, prisoners just wish they could die and leave this life of hell behind them, and it's hard to blame them. But remember, they all got themselves where they are today, or so it's said. Da Thug remains sleeping, the camera peeks over to the cell accross the hallway, another miserable prisoner is lying in a sleep... Just like every other cell in the entire massive building, prisoners sleeping in misery. Footsteps from down the hall interupt Da Thug's deep sleep, Da Thug wakes up and rubs his face as the steps get closer and closer to his cell area of the hallway. The darkness of the night makes it difficult to identify the character's appearance, but it's a male wearing black and a black ski mask, that's all that can be made out. Then, this mysterious character pulls out a long, metalic object, smacks the prison lock and the scene fades... The scene opens back up on the streets of Compton, California... Something isn't right, there is just an eerie feeling that doesn't sit well in everyone's stomach. Even walking down the street, you can tell something's wrong. As a man drives by in his car, the scene zooms into his car to listen to the radio. Scribbling noises are heard as part of an animation... Then the bad news is broken free. News Reporter: Ladies and gentleman, lock your doors, watch your children, and keep an eye out with a phone because we have horrible news. Last night, around the estimated time of two PM, something very terrible took place at the Compton County Prison. Police and detectives are still extremely baffled to how this happened, but they're working on it. Well, I guess from what we're being told, fifty prison guards and a worden were brutally murdered... But it doesn't stop there! Folks, please cover your children's ears, we want to prevent a public riot... ALL prisoners in the entire prison were released free! Including recent convict, Chris [name withheld], AKA Da Thug! The Coast Guard, National Army, several Police Stations, and a few military crews are on their way to protect everyone. But please, lock your doors and remain safe. The scene then animatedly zooms out of the man's car and goes back to the street where cameras then focus on a busy street, a huge amount of people are panicing and rioting the streets. There are many people dead in this world, and many who still need to be killed... The scene opens back up in an alley filled with trash and random assortments of that type. Da Thug is walking down the streets, looking over his shoulder every now and then, in a paranoid state of mind. The last thing he wants is to have to go back to prison. Ten minutes later, Da Thug arrives at his house, he kicks the front door in, due to the fact that he has no key from being in prison so long. Da Thug locks his door and attempts to screw back in the damage he caused from the blast of the kick. The frist thing he does is walk upstairs and get a change of clothes... He notices that his house has been searched and all his alcohol is removed. Entering his closet, Thug grabs a pair of black jeans, a black Southpole T-Shirt, and a black doo rag. He snatches his 'ice' off a rack and places it on his wrists and around his neck. Now walking downstairs, Da Thug goes for his phone, he has a few phone calls... However, nothing important. Thug picks up the phone and calls his agent, Jerry. Jerry: Hello?... Jerry speaking. Da Thug: I'm very angry at you, muthafucka... Jerry: (In Fear) Wh...Who...Whoo is this? Da Thug: You abandoned me, Jerry. You shouldn't have done that... Now you have to die... Jerry: Uhhh...uhhh...Thh...Thug? Da Thug: (Sickly Laughing) Haha, yeah bitch, it's me. Except I'm not your client anymore, now I'm your worst fuckin' nightmare! Jerry: Bu...Bu...But, you're supposed to be locked up? Da Thug: Oh, I take it you didn't hear? Hahahaha, now you're really fucked. Jerry: Hear what?! You got released?! Da Thug: No, Jerry. I didn't get released, haha. I'm not quite sure myself, but tune into the news or radio, the city has a code red alert. Have you ever heard of the expression, 'Jailbreak!'? Jerry: Oh god no... This can't be happening! Let me hear the news on TV first, please don't go anywhere. Jerry listens to his TV... Moments pass as he hears the frightful news for the city of Compton. Jerry: Good lord, I never thought a day like this would come. Da Thug: Shiiit, neither did I, but I'm damn glad it happened. Now I'm back, muthafucka, prepare for a life of hell. You ditched me, Jerry. You bailed out on me like a cheap sell out, now you're gonna pay the price in full. You were supposed to be my agent, and we even became friends, then you do somethin' this stupid? What the fuck would motivate you do to this? This is somethin' that will cost your life, it better have been worth it. Jerry: Please no, Thug, please don't kill me. Can't I just pay you with money, I'll give you all you need! Sorry, I really am. Da Thug: Money?!... HAHAHAHAHA! You think money will save your ass?! You've got to be fuckin' kiddin' me, Jerry. I thought you were a lot smarter than this. You of all people should know that money doesn't mean jack shit to me anymore. I've been broke my entire life, why would it matter now. I'm a professional fuckin' wrestler, I can get all the money I want! Jerry: Please no, Thug. Please don't kill me, I got a family! Please don't kill me, I'm too young to die. I'm very sorry. Da Thug: Stop apologizing! You make me sick, Jerry. I know you have a family, and that makes it that much more exciting. I think after I slit your throat and beat your coarpse with your chopped off head, I will make a trip to your taped up wife and give her some meat, if you know what I mean! Hahahaha! And wait, I forgot about your baby... Hmmm... Maybe I could just smack him over the head and cause brain damage... No no no, too brutal for a child. Let's see here, hmmm... I could intoxicate his ass with all my leftover drugs, then I could 'accidently' drown him in the bathtub! That's it! Hell yeah! Jerry: Oh god no... I can't believe this! That's it you sick freak, I'm taking my family and moving out of state! Yeah! That's what I'm gonna do, you coward. Come get me, by the time you get here, I'll be long gone! Da Thug: My, my, my, Jerry. You're such a man, you're so brave and daring, not! Well, go right ahead, pack your bags and take your precious family. But you can bet your ass that I will GET YOU before you leave! You know me, Jerry, I get what I want and I'm capable of doing anything. Be rest assured to hold your family close all day, because I'm comin' for you... Be afraid, Jerry. Be VERY fuckin' afraid......... Peace. Da Thug hangs up on the extremely frightened former agent, Jerry. On the other line, Jerry drops the phone and orders his family to start packing immediatlly. Da Thug grabs a gun from his bed upstairs and gets in his Mercedes Benz, outside his house. The engine roars to life as he tears out of the street, nobody knows where he will go, we'll just have to wait and see... The scene then fades and opens back up on a public TV behind the glass of a store on a busy street. It's on channel four news... News Reporter: Folks, we have striking news! Already, one of the escaped prisoners, Chris [name withheld], has murdered a family of three! The family was on their way to the airport, when Chris rammed their car off the side of the road and into a ditch. Chris then proceeded to beat the father, Jerry (39), with a baseball bat in the head. Jerry was announced dead ten minutes ago. Chris then punched their three year old child in the head and threw him into on coming traffic like a frizbee, if you will. And the horror doesn't stop there... Sadly. Chris then cracked Jerry's wife, Sandy (33) in the back of the head with the same baseball bat. He then bent her over and raped her in the ditch with traffic witnessing the horror! This is shocking! We're getting more reports of prisoners committing crimes! We're being loaded with work, stayed tuned after this break. The scene fades to complete darkness with a picture of Da Thug's face, sickly laughing... And laughing... And laughing... Outroduction: As you can tell, pent up anger is a very bad thing. And as of right now, the whole city of Compton, California is on red alert with hundreds of escaped prisoners. The law is on it's way, but will they be able to stop the madness?... Also, Da Thug appears to be a changed man, he seems to have gone back to his 'roots'. It's amazing what prison brings out in a person, and you're about to find out.
