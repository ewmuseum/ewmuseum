Missing in Action (Old school style)

19 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12/19/02 Almost a week after the big event, Jurisdiction that is, and things are heating up pretty good in the UBC. The last we heard from Brad Douglas he was in Albuquerque, New Mexico, at the site of where he and Violent B burried Brett Wayne and Nightmare.As of now days have passed since he has been seen and people aren't worried about him, but they're wondering about him. As of now nobody has heard from him and not one person knows of his whereabouts. Even his girlfriend, Kelly, she called some UBC wrestlers and staff members wondering where he is and they haven't heard from him either. They all know he wouldn't ditch a flight back home to see his girlfriend. So they kinda have a feeling he's ok and kinda have a feeling he's not, but nothing to worry about none the less cuz the majority of their thoughts is that he's ok. Right now a scene cuts in on a camera and it shows a concerned Erika Timmons walking up to the Brad Douglas Estate. This takes place in Lyon,France, and the camera is a bit shaky as the camera man is trying to adjust the lens right so the scene is a clear nice view. Then as the lens becomes clear it shows the Brad Douglas Estate again like from the first Douglas promo ever. Erika is walking down the long narrow road that leads to his house and she passes the statue that has him raising the title over many titles at his feet. She raises her eyebrows as she walks past it and then walks more down the long narrow road. They obviously got past security but that wasn't shown on camera as the camera was shut off at that time. Just to get a visual picture of this, Erika Timmons is wearing a tight pair of silky pants that go a little below her knees, a tight purple shirt showing her nice figure, sandles on her feet, and her hair's done nicely (I'm not too good at describing womens hair damnit!). As she looms closer to the house the house becomes more visual and we can see it clearly. From outside of the golden gates you can't see the house but as you get closer the house gets bigger, and bigger, and so much bigger than imaginable. Erika walks confidently towards the house and all of a sudden as she's a few feet away from the doors she hear's a crackling behind her. She turns around and see's a few deer running through the woods that surrounds the house. She then giggles a bit and turns around back towards the house. She looks kind of exhausted from the walk to the house all the way from the gates. The warning of now knocking on the door and ringing the bell because the door is made of solid gold, that's what she noticed. She then puts her finger up to the bell and rings it. She waits awhile and a video camera then turns towards her and on the speaker a voice is heard and it's Kelly, Brad's girlfriend..... Kelly: Hello and welcome to the Brad Douglas Estate, are you Erika Timmons? Erika: Yes I am, I'm here because you asked me to come here, remember? Kelly: Of coarse I remember. Sorry for asking you who it was, I can't see the monitor that shows who's outside too well from here. I'll get the door in a second, ok? Erika: Ok..... Erika then stands there and waits in the beating hot sun and she then looks at the camera man. Erika: Hey Russ, do you have my sunglasses on you? Camera man (Russ): Ummmm, hold on a second, let me check here. The camera becomes shaky as Russ searches to see if he's holding Erika's sunglasses. He then steady's the camera... Russ: Sorry, I don't have them on me. I set them on the dashboard in the van when you handed them to me while I was getting the camera out. Sorry again. Erika: Russ, don't worry, it's alright. Kelly then opens the golden door from the inside and invites them in. Kelly is wearing a tight white belly shirt that shows her belly piercing, a tight pair of short short pleather shorts, no shoes or socks on her feet, and her hair's tied up in a bun. As they enter the house they go to the left out of many possible places to go and they enter a library. They pass the library and come into a very comfortable looking living room with a nice big T.V. set with the movie 'Gone in 60 Seconds' on. Let me explain what this room looks like.... As you first enter the living room you are surrounded by windows showing a cliff to go over that shows acres and acres of woods and wild life. It's a nice red carpet with a bear rug thrown in front of the stone marble fireplace. There's no fire on as it's too hot for one. The furniture all matches and over to the right is a reclinable chair, in the center is a cough that surrounds all of the center and part of the left. And on the left side is also another reclinable chair. The furniture is all white and very soft as when you sit it soaks you in. Kelly then sits down on the couch and shuts off the movie and sets the remote back down on the glass coffee table with nice designs all over it. Pictures and the such are around the room too but that's not important. Russ, the camera man, then positions himself in front of Erika on the couch as Kelly then sits down next to her on the couch. Erika: Wow, this is an incredible house. From on T.V. I saw the pool room and backyard and entranceway, but that's it. This living room is just so comfortable.... Kelly: Thanks, Brad does all of the decorating himself. He's very picky when it comes to his house. But he lets me live here and some of his pets are upstairs. He's just gotta have a German Shepperd and Golden Retriever for some odd reason. The Shepperds name is Bandit and the Retrievers name is Heidie, if you ever see them call them by those names. Erika: Kelly, what's the real reason you called me here in the first place? Kelly now gets a serious looking face and kind of looks concerned. Kelly: I didn't expect this camera to come with you, but I just needed someone to talk to. You know, a girlfiend, and Brad says you're a good person to talk to. Speaking of Brad, do you know where he might happen to be? Erika: Sorry Kelly but I haven't got the slightest clue to where he might be. Kelly: He calls me every night at around 10 to talk to me and he didn't even call last night. That's what has botherd me, and I actually am concerned. Erika: If I knew where he was I'd definately tell you. You don't need this much stress and weight on your shoulders. I kind of know what you're going through Kelly, same thing happened to me once a few years ago. My husband was in the wrestling business and I didn't hear from him in a long time, but he kept contact with that federation and not me. We got divorced so I'm not really worried about him.... Kellyy: I'm glad we can talk like this Erika, I should invite you over sometime for dinner. Brad throws huge dinner parties and stuff like that sometimes..... Just then the phone rings and the cordless phone is right on the coffee table in front of Kelly. Kelly gets very surprised and quickly picks it up and answers it.... Kelly: Hello?.......Oh my God where are you?!?!.......Huh?......You're all the way over in Tampa Bay?......In two days you have to be in Denver, Colorado you idiot!.......Whatever......I don't feel like looking in the library........I won't be able to find that book probably........no.....i said NO!......Fine, just for you then, ok?......Bye.... Kelly hangs up the phone and sets it on the coffee table again. Erika: Is that who I think it was? Kelly: Yes it was, Brad's all the way over in Florida! What an idiot! Now he wants me to find a phrase in this one book he reads and call him back up on his cell phone and tell him what page it's on. I don't know why, don't ask me.... Kelly then goes into the library and screams. Not a bad scream, but a good one as she yells 'BRAD!!!' A few minutes pass and Erika is still seated in the same spot as much as she wants to get up and look what happened. Then from the doorway walks Brad Douglas and he's carrying Kelly and she's holding a dozen roses. He smiles and sets her down on the recliner and then sits next to Erika. Erika is smiling seeing the romance between the two and then Brad looks at the camera. He looks back at Kelly with a confused look and Kelly knows what he's thinking and replies to his thoughts.... Kelly: I asked them to come here Brad, so don't worry. I was really worried about you and wanted someone to talk to, and you said Erika's a good person to talk to. I didn't know about the camera but it doesn't bother me. Brad: Awwww, you were worried about me, hehe. I haven't seen you in almost 2 weeks because I've been on the road with the UBC, so I wanted to see you on my way to the next show which is in Denver. I'm leaving later on tonight because I have to get there early, you know how it is babe. I couldn't stand not seeing you, and let me tell you this.....the house is in damn good condition... Kelly: Well, I haven't really done much (laughs a bit). The maids have done all of the work around here, I just cook my dinners and clean the dishes that I make. Other than that they keep care of everything around here. You pay them for the work they're required for, so why should I do their work? Brad: Good point... Kelly: I know it is, it's a good point because I said it and you love me. Brad: Don't push it babe... Brad gives a little chuckle and Kelly gives him a look, but it's a serious/humorous look. Brad then turns towards Erika.... Erika: Well, Jurisdiction is mearly two days away and you're an interviewer for UBC. You should really ask me some questions and let me answer them since that's what I do on my off days from the UBC. Come on, let's start a promo for the hell of it.... Erika then opens her purse and grabs out a few cards and searches through them. She searches and searches and searches until getting back to the start of the cards and then gets out her calendar book. She looks through that and then dissapointingly looks at Brad... Erika: It looks like I'm booked and got some other interviews to settle firstly. Sorry, but I can't. I barelly had time to come and talk to Kelly, but I found time for her. I don't have much time for you because I have to get going soon... Brad: Oh come on, you've never ever interviewed me and you can ask some simple questions since you don't have any written out yet. Just think off of the top of your head, it won't take that much time, trust me. Right Kelly? Kelly: Erika, let me give you one key word of advice.....run! Brad: Don't listen to her....come on, it won't take long. I've never asked you a favor before, and this is a fun one! Just for a few minutes, come on..... Brad then gives her puppy eyes and puffs out his bottom lip looking like he's gonna cry. This obviously hits a sweet spot in Erika and here's what she says to that.... Erika: ..........Ok, but only a few minutes. After that I must get going, ok? Brad: Perfect! Here, let me get ready for this.... Brad then ties his hair up in a tail and keeps his custom made sunglasses on. He then takes off his black shirt he was wearing and underneath it is a tight white wife beater. He keeps on his blue jeans that are a bit ripped up and he's wearing his usual black Red Wing boots that come up to the lower part of his calf, but we can't see that cuz his jeans are covering that. He then stand up and looks out the windows that show the magnificent nature scene. He views over the cliff right before his eyes and the tree's that spread out for acres and acres. You can see some animals running around and Brad owns all of this, even the animals. He shoots down one deer in the winter every year but that's only for sport and he makes deer jerkey out of it instead of just throwing it away. And with the deer heads he gets them stuffed and put up on the wall. But as he comes back from the window he sits back down next to Kelly and Erika is ready for the interview/promo.... Erika: You ready? Brad: Yep, let's go... Erika: Alright, hey Russ, is that camera on? Russ then starts to luagh behind the camera. Russ: It's been on for the past God known how long. Just start it whenever you want... Erika: Alright, I'm ready.....Hello UBC viewers from all around the world. I'm sitting here with none other than the master at what he does best, Brad Douglas. This man has done it all and is about to do some more this Saturday against Johnny nightmare. Alot of rumors are going around for this matchup but before we get into that, let me start things off with what he talked about yesterday. He seems to have some problems with Xavier morningstar and Ehren sims. Brad, what do you have to say about these people? Brad: First off let me talk about the boss himself,Xavier, or to many people X. To me he's simply known as a backstabber! The backstabbing comes from widely known experience as he gave me a contract about a month back, or less than a month....whichever. But he gave me a contract and was being extremely friendly to me. I see what he does to people, he sucks them in and then sucks them dry. He did this to me! He got me into the UBC with one hell of a contract and then I was officially on the UBC roster and into several matches. In each one of these matches I bled and busted my balls to give UBC what it needed, and that was some ratings. Oh, and trust me, ever since I got here the ratings have gone up. Xavier, I wouldn't mind getting in the ring with you since you're an owner and also a wrestler here in the UBC. You may think you can get your way around me, but I'm not like all the others you've screwed over. I don't get screwed over and let you come by and do it again. You got away with it with the help of your friends, but that's it...no more! If you mess with Brad Douglas then you can't ever go back to the way things were. I have seen the way you treat everyone and I am very displeased. Brad: Now we have Ehren sims, our paths have not crossed yet.Let's just keep it that way ok Ehren. Brad: Last but not least of the three would have to be Brett Wayne. The future World Champion that wins his belt's with the help of many people. he wouldn't make it in UBC on his own and he knows damn well that's true. He also knows that if I come into the title range with him then he doesn't have a chance of winning. Xavier sure as hell can't stop me from getting to you Brett, so watch your back also. You're at the top of your game and still using that big mouth of yours to down your opponents. When you down your opponent it doesn't sound right, but when I do.....everyone listens and they listen in silence. They hear the words I have to say and they know what I say is true. 'Who is they?' you're probably asking yourself. My fans and my opponents would be 'they'. My opponents speak all tough and bad and the such, but they know deep down inside that I will be victorious and almighty no matta what. the thought of you as the champion is a disgrace to UBC, so Xavier might wanna reconsider his plans to make you champion. Brad then calls in his maid, Nancy, and sits back from getting caught up in the promo... Nancy: Yes Mr. Douglas? Brad: Would you please get me a glass of water and get these two young ladies whatever they want. Nancy: Comming right up Mr. Douglas. What would you two ladies like? Kelly: I'd like a BLT sandwhich and a glass of grape juice. What about you Erika? Erika: I don't need anything right now. I'll just pick up something while heading towards Denver. Nancy then goes and comes back with Brad's glass of water quickly then goes towards the kitchen to make Kelly's BLT and get her glass of grape juice. Then Erika and Brad continue with the interview/promo.... Erika: Are those all your thoughts on Xavier,Ehren and Brett Wayne? Brad: Those are all my thoughts for now. We'll just have to wait till next time. Erika: Just for fun let's go over who you think will win at Jurisdiction, and let's get your thoughts on those matches. I'll mention the match then give me your thoughts... Brad: It's alright, I know the matches on the card and I'll just go over them here quickly. I always know all the matches on every card for some odd reason. So here's my thoughts on the first match.... Eldar Markovich VS. Makoto Kenobi: I really don't know ither man that much,Ok truthfully i've never heard of them before. I'm giving this match to Eldar Markovich....the winner will be Eldar. Brad Douglas Vs. Johnny Nightmare: Let's see here. I am more experienced, better, and will just flat out wipe the ring up with his face. The winner of this is an easy one and I know for a fact who it will be....the winner will be me,Brad Douglas Krisis VS. Raine Knights: I'm thinking Krisis is gonna win this one. Raine, I like you man, but no offense.your in this match, and I've been in the ring with you before. Through past experiences with you, you flat out suck!....the winner will be Krisis. DR VS. KOA: the winner will be KOA. Gutter Rat VS. Darcy Crisis: This match is a no brainer, I mean, come on! Gutter Rat lacks skill and doesn't even know what talent is. This match is going to be easy for Darcy Crisis......the winner will be Darcy Crisis. Mr.Synnerster VS. Violent B and Chris carter: As much as i think it's funny that Chris Carter and Violent B sleep together,I've never won against Mr.Synnerster in our 4 fights.Well....the winner wil be Mr.Synnerster. Brett Wayne VS. Tommy Grady: I don't care about ither of these bastards.I've never seen Grady in the ring,So being that i hold a victory over Brett Wayne.My money is on Tommy Grady. Erika: I was about to ask if you're afraid that someone will take your predictions the wrong way but I guess you got there before me. Well, I really wanna know your thoughts on Tommy Grady and Brett Wayne. I wanna know what you think of them in case you do face them one day... Brad: I will win. Erika: Ok, WHEN you win. What are your thoughts on them? Brad takes a large gulp from his glass of water then sets it down on the corner table at the corner of the couch.... Brad: Tommy Grady, let me get to you firstly.You have a big mouth I haven't really been impressed by you that much. Actually I've laughed everytime a promo of you comes on the T.V. When I see you talk it reminds me of a person I once knew in the FCW, his name was Blaze Mcory. He spoke words that really didn't make much sense which is much like you. You will face me if you beat Brett Wayne and that's it, no f*cking way out! You may like the fact that you're getting a shot at the man to beat in UBC, but you won't like the fact after seeing who you're going up against for it. Tommy, you aren't much in the UBC and you never will be.... Now onto Wayne, even though I don't know you I have a bit of respect for you. I've seen your promo's and I'm rahter impressed. But do you have what it takes to take out 'The King of every ring'? I think not! You put on a hell of a match last week but in the end,I beat you and Johnny Nightmare. Call it cocky,Call it a EGO, what ever the hell you want.But this ego gets me wins and I'm proud to be called the cockiest son of a bitch in the business today. If it wasn't for that then who knows where I'd be. Hell, I'd be in the same spot cuz of all my great talents but that's besides the point. The point is that if you beat Grady, then you will lose to me.Because the road to the gold runs right in front of me,Got to get threw me to get to the gold. Brad then takes another sip of his water and sets it back down on the corner table at the corner of the couch. Nancy then walks in and is carrying Kelly's BLT sandwhich and grape juice on a tray. Nancy then sets it down next to Kelly on a small little table and Kelly starts away at it automatically, she must be hungry. Nancy: Need anything else Mr. Douglas? Brad: No, I'm ok. Brad then takes a last sip of his glass of water and hands it to Nancy. Nancy takes it to the kitchen to be washed as Erika and Brad continue with the interview/promo... Erika: Well Brad, it's been really fun but I gotta get going. Brad then gives her a mean glare... Brad: Listen here Erika, you asked me the damn questions and I answerd them. The polite thing to do would be to sit there and shut the f*ck up while I finish answering your question! Kelly then goes over and nudges Brad in the arm. Brad: What? Kelly: Apologize to her now for using that tone of voice and swearing at her. Brad: I did? Kelly: Yes honey, you did. Brad: Well then, sorry Erika for raising my voice and swearing towards you. I didn't even realize what I was doing at the time, hah, sorry 'bout that. Erika: It's ok, I get it all the time from wrestlers. Kelly: Well my man shouldn't. And if he does then he better say he's sorry or he gets a nice slap to the face from me. Since he said he's sorry, like I knew he would, then his face is clear from my slaps. Right Brad? Brad: Damn, you're learning too well from me. Kelly: This isn't your kind of talking, it's mine. Brad: Damn, you're fiesty, hehe! Looks like I'm gonna have a fun night. But as you said,You have to get going now. Brad then escorts Erika and Russ over to the door and he opens it for them because nobody is to touch that door without his permission. They walk out of the door and as they reach the bottom of the steps Brad yells at Russ, the camera man. This saturday your all about to see something never before seen in the short history of UBC.
