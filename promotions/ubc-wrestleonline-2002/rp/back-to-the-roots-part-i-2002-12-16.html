Back To The Roots - Part I

16 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12.16.02 It was a Sunday Night, I was driving my black 2003 Mercedes SLK Benz. I could see traffic tearing through the dark highway lights as I was driving with a face of nothing. No emotion, facial expressions, or sensitivity could be seen or felt. I continued driving… What the fuck? Why does life have to be so damn hectic? Ever since I was a young child, I’ve always had to do things I didn’t want to. Things like tryin’ to reason with stupid people. Things like tryin’ to cover up for other people’s mistakes to cover my own ass. I’m sick of it all. But then, I jus’ kinda faded away into a damn drunken joke. This isn’t me, yet I live this life each and every day. It’s my body, it’s my mind, but it feels so different every morning I wake up and get out of bed, or wherever the previous night took me. I try to dig deep inside myself and figure out why I feel this way, but jus’ like the rest of my life, all that’s left at the end of the day is unanswered questions. I guess alcohol really has shaped my life, even though I told people it would never get to that point. Maybe I was wrong? Even so, I’m too stubborn to admit it. Never will I admit that alcohol has taken over my life, impacted my decisions, and fucked wit my mind. SCREEEEECH! Chris Carter slams on his breaks and begins “taking off a layer of rubber”, so to speak. Carter roars his Mercedes past a white car while flipping the driver off and yelling. Seconds go by, Carter’s mind clears of the thoughts and the anger leaves his head… See, just like that, STUPID FUCKIN’ PEOPLE. And if I woulda got out and shot his ass, it would have been all over for me and my career. On the other hand, I am a very fortunate person. That isn’t the only time I’ve come close to losing it. Hell, the past ten years have been like this. For some odd reason, I’ve been lucky enough to hold myself back, though. Not sure how much longer I can keep doing this, holding myself back all the time, drinking half way to death every other night. Then I put it all behind me and go out and fight for UBC each Saturday. This schedule and the elements are really beatin’ on my body. Yet I continue to do it. Why? Because I can’t help it anymore, this is basically the only way for me to get by while keeping my sanity. I’ve tried counseling, believe me, I’ve tried that shit before. Did it work? Hell no. Explain to me, how would someone else know my problem if I don’t know them my own damn self? Well, that’s what I tried to get across to them, but they’re the “professionals”, so that was jus’ pointless. Straight up. Seriously who am I tryin’ to fool? Have I lost it? I haven’t had a SERIOUS battle in quite some time now. My last serious fight was against a man known as “Ice”, it was jus’ he and I in a street fight. A large prize was on the line, something I only wish I could ever hold again. And let me tell you, I beat his muthafuckin’ ass so badly that neither he or I will ever forget that match. But now that I’m able to look back on it, it doesn’t feel right for some reason. I mean, not only did I take his soon to be wife from him, but I took his championship and ruined his name. This is another thing, why do I feel guilty? Because I went out and got mine? Because I did something that most people wish they could? I shouldn’t feel guilty at all, but I do. This is part of the new me, the weak me. This is part of me that needs to change. No, this is a part of me that is changing right now. I’m sick of this shit. From here on out, I’m getting’ back into the mean ass, vodka guzzlin’, ass kickin’, career endin’ muthafucka that I was known as before I came to UBC. From here on out, watch your fuckin’ backs and lock your doors because the games are over and nobody is in a playin’ mood. Chris Carter’s cell phone rings, he delays speaking and picks up the phone. Violent B: Hey, Chris, it’s me. What’s goin’ on? Chris “AK” Carter: Nuttin’ much man, jus’ thinking. Hey, listen… Do you think I’m a pussy? Violent B: No, why? Chris “AK” Carter: No no no, honestly. I won’t get pissed off at you, jus’ be real wit me. Violent B: Chris, I was being honest. Is something bothering you? Because it sure as hell seems like it. You can tell me, we go way back. Chris “AK” Carter: Yeah, somethin’ is damn sure botherin’ my ass. I’m sick of bein’ so nice to everyone and puttin’ up wit people’s bullshit. Violent B: Whoa, slow down! Since when were you nice?! Chris “AK” Carter: Good point… Well, I’m goin’ back to my roots, it’s time to go back to the old me. And seein’ as how we got a handi cap match at Jurisdiction. I got somethin’ on my mind. I need to get my name back in business and my blood flowin’ again. The violence is back, B, and it’s here to fuckin’ stay. Now here is my plan for Jurisdiction… TO BE CONTINUED
