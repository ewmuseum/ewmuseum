Violence in the Gym (RP #2 for Jurisdiction)

10 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12/10/02 [The scene opens up in a gym just outside of Detroit, Michigan. The gym is filled with strong looking men, working out and lifting weights. The camera is roaming around. All of a sudden, the doors in the gym burst open, and Violent B, wearing a trench coat, black baggy jeans, and black boots, with his spikey necklace on, walks in. All the men in the gym stop what they’re doing and there’s a sudden hush. Violent B just looks around. He walks past everyone and walks into the back of the gym, into the locker room. The camera follows him. He walks up to his locker, opens the locker, and takes off his jacket. He puts the jacket in the locker. He’s wearing a black t-shirt. He then takes that off and stuffs it into the locker, revealing his “ripped” physique. He then takes off his pants, revealing a pair of black workout shorts. He puts his jeans into the locker. He then walks out of the locker room and into the gym. All the men have resumed what they were dong. Violent B then walks into the back of the gym and walks to the bench press machine. He sees that someone is working out on it. Violent B walks up to him. The man is pretty “ripped“ himself, but not quite as physical as Violent B. He‘s wearing a white wife beater and some shorts. He has dark hair and a bushy beard.] Violent B - “Excuse me, but could you move so I can work out.” [The man on the machine, obviously not knowing who Violent B is, looks up at him.] Man on Machine - “Why the hell should I move for you!? I was here first asshole. Go find yourself another machine to work out on!” [Violent B doesn’t look too happy.] Violent B - “NO! You don’t seem to understand! You see, I wanna work out on this machine, and I’m not moving until you get your ass outta the way so I can work out!” [The man then looks angry. He puts the weight down and gets up. He gets into Violent B’s face. The man is about 3 inches shorter than Violent B.] Man - “Who the hell do you think you are!? You wanna talk to me like that!? I’ll show you what happens when people talk down to me.” [Violent B just gets a cocky smile on his face.] Violent B - “Well, it’s kinda hard not to talk down to you when you’re shorter than I am.” [The man gets even more angry.] Man - “That’s it buddy! You’re goin’ down!” [The man then throws a wild punch at Violent B. Violent B ducks the blow and the man loses his balance and falls onto another man that’s working out. The other man gets up. He’s bigger than the man that ran into him, and looks similar. He looks to be about the same height though, but his body is physically bigger from a more intense work out. He looks at the man that fell on him.] Second Man - “What the hell are you doing Frank!?” Frank - “That guy right there made me run into you John. He’s trying to push me around.” [John looks up at Violent B with a look of anger. He points his finger at him.] John - “What the hell are you doing!? You can’t push around my little brother. Who do you think you are!?” Violent B - “I’m Violent B. Who the hell are you?” John - “We’re the Olson Twins! And we’re gonna give you a beating!” [Violent B begins to laugh to himself.] Violent B - “The Olson Twins! HAHAHAHAHAHAHAHA! You guys sure sound intimidating now! HAHAHAHAHAHA! Which one of you is Ashley?” [The Olson Twins look to be getting angrier with each word Violent B says. They look angrier and angrier. Violent B is still laughing at them.] John - “You think our names are funny!? What about you Mr. [In mocking tone.] VIOLENT B!” [Violent B then stops his laughter and gets pissed off.] Violent B - “If you knew how I was brought up, and knew what I’ve been through, you’d understand my name. I earned my name by bringing violence to the table. I’m a sick, twisted, violent son of a bitch! That’s where my name came from. I wasn’t named after some little girls named Mary-Kate and Ashley. So why don’t you shut your mouth before I have to do it for you!?” [The Twins look even more angry. They walk up and both of them get into Violent B’s face.] Frank - ‘You started something you can’t finish little man. Why don’t you just turn around and get outta here before you get hurt!” John - “Yeah, we don’t wanna have to break you!” [Violent B just laughs to himself once again.] Violent B - “So, you’re calling me little man now. Look at you! I’m bigger and badder than you’ll ever be. You two might have strength in numbers, but you can’t beat me. You don’t know me. You don’t know my past. And you don’t know my fighting abilities. You think you can take me out. Well then why don’t both of you try?” [With the, both the Twins throw punches at Violent B. Violent B ducks Franks punch, but John’s punch connects to Violent B’s face. Violent B’s head turns on impact. Violent B then whips his head back and looks John in the eye. He has a pissed off, determined look in his eyes. John then realizes that he can’t win by himself. Franks then throws a punch that hits Violent B in the back of the head. Violent B turns around and stares at Frank with the same look. Franks looks scared as well. Violent B then punches Frank in the face with a right hand, sending Frank into the wall. He then turns around and hit’s a clothesline like punch on John, sending him to the ground. The men all over the gym crowd around the watch the fight. Violent B then mounts over John and begins to punch him in the face. John is screaming out of pain and trying to escape, but the bigger Violent B doesn’t let him move. He cocks back his arm and gives John one hard right to his jaw, knocking John unconscious.] Man in Gym - “OH DAMN! I HEARD THAT SHOT!” Another Man - “DAMN! I wouldn’t wanna meet up with this guy in a dark alley!” [Violent B then gets up and sees the Frank is starting to get up. He grabs a 20 pound weight. He throws the weight. It flies over to Franks and hits him in the head. Frank goes down right away, unconscious to the floor. Violent B then spits on John. He looks up at everyone in the gym.] Violent B - “Anyone else wanna fight!? Maybe you wanna stop me from working out!? Come on!” [Every man in the gym shakes their heads no to Violent B’s challenge. Violent B then gets that cocky smile on his face.] Violent B - “Alright. Then get both these pieces of shit outta here before I do the same to you as well!” [A couple of the men drag John and Frank out of the gym. Violent B then lays down on the bench, grabs the weight, and begins to work out, starting what he came to do before he was so rudely interrupted. He begins to do repetitions with the weight, tightening his biceps, as the scene fades.]
