- Time to Party -

2 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 01-02-03 The scene is faded black but the sound of music is heard as well as the voice of Brett Wayne... Brett Wayne - Its been nearly two weeks since my last fight at Denver, Colorado. I came out victorious like I always do, I proved myself once again just like I will prove myself this coming Saturday. Brett Wayne - Things have been said about me in the past two weeks...rumors have been spreading around that I might not be able to make it back but I was just on Christmas Vacation ...(Smiles)... and you idiots actually think Brett Wayne would just get up and quit after winning this UBC Trophy ...(Laughs)... yeah right! Brett Wayne will never quit, I will reign supreme over everyone and I'll just prove my worthyness once again this Saturday but first I must do something, something that every fighter should do in the UBC...lay back and have fun! The scene opens up to a huge mansion at the end of a block, the side of the streets are filled up with parked vehicles as the sound of music is heard coming from the mansion. The scene then fades into the house where a few hundred people dance, drink, and smoke a few joints. Smoke flows through the air like fog as the sound of music gets louder and louder as the camera gets closer and closer to the the back of the house after it makes it past the few hundred people the camera then turns to Brett Wayne and Mike Wayne sitting down on a bench with a beer in there hands talking to one another... Brett Wayne - You gotta love these frat parties! Mike Wayne - Its been years since my last frat party man...(Thinks to himself)...I remember when we through our first frat party...man was that exciting! Brett Wayne - (Laughs)... Yeah it was exciting, almost as exciting as fighting. Mike Wayne - Yeah fighting is pretty exciting expecially when your the best they have to offer... Brett Wayne - Yeah...I am the best they have, If it wasn't for me where would UBC be right now anyways? Mike Wayne - Prolly down in the gutter, you are UBC and everyone knows it. Brett Wayne - Everyone doesn't know it or they would bow down to me when they see me but instead these useless fighters like Chris AK Carter think there better than me so they want to step up to the best and TRY and prove themselves worthy but they end up failing like everyone else who comes across my path. Mike Wayne - They all fail, they can't handle Brett Wayne...(Laughs) Brett Wayne - (Laughs also)... Your right there bro! Tommy Grady couldn't handle it either, he got put in his place, he was Equallized...(Laughs Again) Mike Wayne - Oh Shit! ...(Puts a serious look on his face)... I forgot to tell you that this Saturday is for the UBC Title! Brett Wayne - WHAT?!?! How could that be I'm the only one with the UBC Trophy no one else besides me has one so WHAT THE FUCK?!?!? Mike Wayne - Beats me man...I was confused at first to but look at it this way it'll be easy for you to win it, look who your going up against. Brett Wayne - Your right there, Chris Carter doesn't even deserve to get a shot, he's only fought the pathetic jobber fighters he hasn't faced anyone of my quality yet but he will soon see I am bigger and better than what he thinks of me to be just like Johnny Nightmare saw as well as Tommy Grady. There all jobbers to my eyes, I'm the Best and I'll always be the best! Suddenly three teenage boys walk into the room with a beer in each of there right hands and the boy in the front has a joint in his mouth... Mike Wayne - Man that joint looks good, lets go take a hit off of it... Brett Wayne - Man just because everyone else in the UBC are a bunch of druggies doesn't mean we have to be, drugs do nothing but slow down your mind making them weaker in battle. Drugs just kill the fools and get you out of shape and that is something I myself cannot aford I must stay in perfect form in order to be the best so Mike if you want to go to a hit off of it go ahead... Mike Wayne - (Thinks to himself)... Well thats just your opinion I'm craving a hit of that damn joint... Mike jumps up quickly and runs over towards the 3 guys with the joint and jumps in the middle of them... Mike Wayne -Hand me that Damn joint boyz and lemme show you how to smoke that thing right...(Mike takes the join away from the guy quickly and takes a look at it)... God Damn Boyz! Who the hell roled this shit.... Guy - Umm...I did why? Mike Wayne -Because you can't role worth a shit but hey its still good...(Smiles as he takes a puff)...Yeah......man this is some g00d shit! Guy - God damn old man don't smoke all my shit... Mike Wayne - OLD MAN!!! You stupid fucking kid! You ain't getting your shit back now just because of your fucking comment! Mike walks back over to the bench were Brett sits as he continues to drink his beer, Brett then stands up... Brett Wayne - Ok I'm out of beer now I'm going to go get another one...you want one? Mike Wayne - Nawww man....I'm c00! I have what I want right here...(Smiles and then enhales the joint)... Brett walks out of the room and into the kitchen area where crowds of people stand and talk to each other, smoke fogs up the room and Brett begins coughing... Brett Wayne - (Coughs)... Fucking smoke, don't these kids know that that shit is bad for you... Some African American - What did you say cracker...(Pokes Brett on the shoulder making him turn around) Brett Wayne - I said that this shit right here...(Pulls the join out of the African Americans mouth)...is bad for you...(Throws it down on the ground and stomps on it)... Now anymore god damn questions... The same African American - You stupid white junky that was some good weed you just put out and now its time to put you out...(Whistles and 2 other African Americans come up from behind Brett)... Brett Wayne - Damnit your just as stupid as Tommy Grady and Chris Carter...you think by making the numbers uneven that you can still take me on...(Laughs)...you three fuckers don't even stand a chance, I'll have you knocked out before you can say Chicken! The Same African American - Oh Yeah Cracka! Well take this you stupid mofo! The black guy swings at Brett but Brett dunks causing him to hit the black guy behind him Brett laughs as he turns around and walks over to where the keg is and he begins refilling his cup up with beer... Brett Wayne - Ok that was just pathetic...(Finishes filling up his beer)...you have one more chance to hit me before I leave you wanna take it? That Same Damn African American - Hell Yeah Cracka I'm gonna take that last hit on y0 ass! Brett Wayne - Well come and take it brotha...(Immitates the African American) The Black guy yells as he swings once again but Brett dodges the punch causing the black guy to stumble down to the ground... Brett Wayne - (Laughs)... Happy New Years man! Brett smirks and walks out of the kitchen and back into the room where his brother sits there layed back with a joint in his hand... Brett Wayne - You still not finished with that god damn joint? Mike Wayne - Aww yeah mon....this is a different one, you want a puff? Brett Wayne - No damnit I'm not not fucking druggy! Mike Wayne - Yo man I'm sorry Bro just asking, so anyways what took ya so long... Brett Wayne - Oh it was nothing, just some retard acting all big and bad... Mike Wayne - TOMMY GRADY?!?!? Brett Wayne - No..No no tthat retard. Mike Wayne - Oh well shit you got me all excited for nothing! Brett Wayne - (Laughs)...Whatever...what time is it I wanna watch the fucking ball drop this year instead of laying past out on the ground. Mike Wayne - Oh fuck I forgot about that we gotta find us some women to kiss!!! Brett Wayne -Ha...your right lets go find us two to kiss on... Mike Wayne - Hell yeah! Brett and Mike stand up and walk quickly into the living room where the dance area and the tV is located, suddenly everyone stops dancing as the DJ begins speaking... DJ - Ok ladies and gentlemen the time is almost here, the New Years is almost HERE...(Everyone screams)...its just seconds away so lets start counting down... 20 19 18 17 16 15 Brett Wayne - Shit just grab a fucking women I guess...(Brett turns and looks at his brother and he already starts making out with a women)... Well shit... 14 13 12 11 10 Brett Wayne - Time to find a women... 9 8 7 6 5 Brett walks over to a women and grabs her and spins her around before he makes his move he pauses and notice's the familer face... Brett Wayne - MOM!!! Vicky - Oh hey son didn't figure I'd see you at a frat party! 4 3 2 1 0 Before they countdown reach's to zero Brett turns around and grabs a big chunky black women and begins kissing on her moments later that same god damn african american walks up... That Same God Damn African American - What the hell you doin kissin on my gal Cracka!...(He then realizes who the guy is)...Aww if it isn't the little Cracka who thinks he is hot shit, well let me tell you something boy! Vicky - Boy....you calling my son a fucking boy, I'll show you who the little boy is YOU STUPID ..........(Brett grabs her before she spits out the last of the sentace) Brett Wayne - Ok thats enough its time to go now, MIKE hurry your ass! Mike Wayne - Wha...(Notice's that Brett is carrying his mom)... What the fuck is she doing here... Brett Wayne - You fucking tell me?!?! Mike Wayne - I have no clue, lets just get her out of here before we have another problem like last time... Brett Wayne - Thats what were doing jackass! Mike Wayne - Oh well I'll meet you outside in one moment I need another joint! Brett Wayne - Hurry your god damn ass I'm not going to wait forever... Brett hurries out the front door as Mike snatch's a joint out of someone's mouth as he runs out the front door following Brett they then jump in Bretts camera and burn rubber leaving.
