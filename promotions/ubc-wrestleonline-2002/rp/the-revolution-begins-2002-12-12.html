The Revolution Begins

12 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Friday 13th (....The Camera opens up in a home garage which is cluttered with various musical instruments including drums and guitars. There are boxes sitting up against the wall contain various CD from various different genre's. Other than the boxes and the guitar the garage is derelict with leaves scattered on the ground as the door to the garage is up just a tiny bit. Suddenly a roar of an engine is heard as coming screeching to a hault in the driveway. The smoke from the tyre's can be smelt as the garage door is lifted by a remote as the beeping noice is heard. The door rises to the top as it clang's as the car drives in and is parked in what was once a near empty garage. Out of the car steps Steve Hanger and Sean Wonder both are wearing their own customized Dangerous Revolution pictures which reveal their faces on the front and the Words The Revolution Just Got Dangerous on the back. They slam the car doors shut and the car is looked as it beeps twice as both men walk into the house where Wonder walks into the fridge to get something to drink and Hanger starts talking to Wonder....) [---Steve Hanger----] So you ready for this dude. (....Wonder has just finished drinking and now is eating and is talking with his mouth full....) [---Sean Wonder---] Ready for what Steve?? [---Steve Hanger----] To beat the living hell out of those weird ass son of b*tches The Kingz of Africa. [---Sean Wonder---] Yeah, those two are a bit on the loony side but no worry because at Jurisdiction they will be in our Jurisdiction and we will take them out no questions asked. (....Hanger walks over to the answering machine and plays it and it informs him that an UBC Television Crew will be arriving anytime soon to help conduct a Interview for the upcoming Jurisdiction....) [---Sean Wonder---] Dammit dude I was gonna get a bit of practice in and then hit the sack, those son of b*tches better not be here to long. [---Steve Hanger----] Sean, this is what we want. We want Television time so everyone in the UBC knows that we indeed mean business and that we have the power to take down anyone, anywhere at anytime. (....Suddenly a knock is heard against the wooden door as Wonder looks pissed off as he throws the food and drink into the fridge as Hanger rushes him to sit down. Hanger walks over and turns the door knob as it opens and instead of the television crew that they where expecting a busty blonde is standing at the door....) [---Steve Hanger----] Well Hello. May I ask what it is exactly you would want. [---Blonde---] Well I have this camera crew out my place that said they where here to interview me from some place called UBC but I have never head of it but since it sounded like they where from some wrestling/fighting company i figured they actually might be looking for you. [---Steve Hanger----] Ah, yes. Tell those clowns to get over here and if anyone like that bothers you again don't hesitate in letting us know. [---Blonde---] Ok I will and don't worry I won't hesitate. (....The blonde winks at Hanger as she walks off as Hanger stands at the door and watches her walk away as he turns back towards Sean with a massive grin on his face...) [---Sean Wonder---] Dude I think your in. (....After about ten minutes we are back in the Living Room where The Camera Crew counts down Sean and Steve as they get ready to speak into the camera...) [---Steve Hanger----] Hello to all you peons in Television land and all of the rat faced fans of UBC. I do hope that every single one of you are ready for a Revolution and a Dangerous one at that because that is what you are all going to be instore for at Jurisdiction because whether you like it or not. You see the Kingz of Africa believe that they can match us in a war of words yet I have yet to face someone alone who can match me in a war of words let alone both men and Sean. [---Sean Wonder---] Steve is dead right. Because whether they like it or not the Kingz of Africa are going to be looking at the roof of The Pit whether they like or not. You see after the end of Jurisdiction they are going to need to by a stairway to get into the Warehouse because after we humilate them and make them look like little b*tches then there is going to be no way that Ehren and Xavier are going to waste time and money in letting those two in UBC. [---Steve Hanger----] Now Malik you want to see the bloodthirsty edge of Australian's has worked its way down to a whimper well instead of telling you that it hasn't I am going to prove it to you inside the pit because inside the Pit I am going to make you bleed and I am going to make you look like a third rate competitor which will match you nicely since you come from a third world country. Hell everything about both of you is third rate compared to everything about us because we are simply first class. [---Sean Wonder---] I love the little things on the back of the shirts you have. Do they make you seem big and important having the words assassin and great on the back of your shirts because that is all they must do because frankly the shirts are ugly and I doubt they are for merchandise because no one in their right mind is going to want to go out and buy one of those shirts because frankly no one would want to be caught dead in one of those. [---Steve Hanger----] Sean stay on track. Now if you two think I fear you then your dead wrong. I have competed against more bloodthirsty animals and tougher people than you and I have came out on top and to be frank Amir the last person that called themselves and assassin I took out with relative ease and you will be the same because I urge you to give us all you got because it will all end in Sweet Dreams for both of you in the middle of the pit. [---Sean Wonder---] And you will both be the first to fall victim to a Natural Wonder of the World. (....Steve and Sean smile as they fall down onto the couch and look into the camera as they laugh evilly as the camera fades to a black...)
