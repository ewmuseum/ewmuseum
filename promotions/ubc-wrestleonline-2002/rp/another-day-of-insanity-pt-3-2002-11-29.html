'Another day of Insanity' Pt 3

29 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 02-29-02 As you know, Brett Wayne has been having problems with his actions here lately. When someone says something wrong to him or something he doesn't want to hear Brett flips out and loses his mind. His brother Mike has been thinking of a way to teach him to control his actions. After days of thinking Mike finally came up with a suggestion..... 'Are you ready yet' Mike ask looking at Brett. 'Where are we going' Brett ask looking back at Mike. 'I already told you its a suprise so no more questions hurry up and get ready' Mike says as he walks out to his car. Brett finishes getting ready and walks out to the car. They drive off slowly. While driving down the highway, Brett begins thinking about his match saturday, moments later they arrive at the place Mike wants to show Brett. 'What is this place' Brett ask. 'Its a place for you to stay until your match next saturday' Mike says walking to the entrance. 'Oh I see what your doing, this place is a nut house isnt it?' Brett says standing there not moving an inch. 'No its not a nut house, its simply a place for you to stay until Saturday so you don't do anything stupid and get locked up, you do want to fight Saturday don't you.' Mike says staring at Brett. Brett scratches his head and thinks for a moment, then he starts walking towards Mike. 'I guess your right, besides how bad can this place be.' Mike leads the way through the doors as Brett follows, when walking in Brett see's old men and women in wheel chairs and with cains and so on. 'Your putting me in a place full of old shits.' Brett says watching the old people walk very slowly. He then walks over to an old man with a can, he gets right in front of him and looks him in the eyes. 'How you doing sir.' He ask the old man. The old man speaks slowly 'Get out of my face you...you shithead' Brett sits there and laughs, he gets out of his face and looks around to see if anyone is watching, he sees no one is looking at him so Brett pushes the old man down on the ground. 'Listen here you old fucker, I'm the man of the house now, so you bow down to me, you got that' Brett says looking down at the old man. 'Sonny Boy, you ain't no man. You just another Insane person coming to the nut house, you won't last one day in here with all of us old people. You go Insane.' The old man says as he starts getting back up to his feet. Brett begins to speak then Mike interupts 'Yo Bro, your all signed up now, lets go to your room. Brett gives the old man a go to hell look as he walks off. 'This way' Mike says walking to Bretts room. While walking Brett continues to view the scenery, he sees more old people sitting down smoking cigs, drinking coffey, and wachting 'Dukes of Hazards.' They make it to Bretts room, when they walk in an old guy is laying in bed, Brett sees him and and stops. 'What the hell are you doing in my room' Brett says standing right beside the old man. They old man looks up at Brett and gets up slowly, he walks around Brett for a minute and laughs. 'Your a Figher, what has the world come to these days' 'Who the hell are you' Brett ask as his face starts turning red. 'Calm down young one, I'm the person you'll be rooming with for the next week so you better get used to it because there isnt a damn thing you can do' says the old man. 'Oh, is that right. I bet I can do something about it.' Brett says as he stands up tall. 'Go ahead and try, but you'll find out the hard way you ain't shit in this place.' 'We will see' Brett says as Mike try's to sneak out of the room. 'Where are you going' Brett says seeing Mike trying to sneak out in the corner of his eyes. 'I have to go take care of some business, you have fun in here for the next week. I'll be back in a couple of days to visit you, oh yea and here take this' Mike hands Brett $50 as he walks off. 'Thanks alot' Brett says as Mike walks off out of site. Brett then walks back to his room and starts unpacking his bags. 'Whats your name old man' Brett says still unpacking his bags. 'I've had so many names, just call me gramps' says gramps as he sits back down. 'So how long you been in here gramps' Gramps laughs and lays back down 'To long' he says closing his eyes. Brett laughs and finishes unpacking his bags, he thens turns around seeing the old man is asleep. 'This is going to be a very long week' he says as he walks out of the room. 'I'm hungry, wonder what they have to eat around this place' Brett says wondering around. He then walks up on a nurse taking care of an old man. 'Where is the food at around here' Brett says tapping the nurse on the shoulder. The nurse just ignores Brett as Brett gets frustrated and walks off. 'She is a Bitch' he states as he sees an Ice Box in the corner of the room. He walks over to the Ice Box and opens the door. He see's nothing but baby food for the old people with teeth. 'You have to be kidding me' He says closing the door. 'There has to be a water fossit around here somewhere, I'm thirsty' he continues to walk around and he comes up on a water fountain. 'Finally' he says taking a drink from the water fountain. When he finishes he makes his way back to his room, when he enters the room he smells an awful smell. 'What the hell is that' he looks around and he see's gramps laying in his bed. -FART- 'Nasty, its the old fucker. I can fix that problem real quick' Brett raps the old man up in sheets and carries him outside the door and lays him down. He then finds some air freshoner laying around and sprays it everywhere. 'That should do it' Brett says pulling off his shirt and pants. 'Lets see wheres that book I brought' Brett looks around for a minute and he finally finds his book named 'How to rid of Nightmares.' Brett turns on his lamp on his side of the room and turns off the bright light shining in the middle of the room and begins reading as he has a big smile on his face. About an hour later, someone starts beating on the door, Brett gets up and goes to the door, when he opens it gramps is right there... 'There he is, take him away' Four or Five big guys walking in dressed in all white and start tackling Brett. 'What the hell is this shit' Brett says as the men start tieing him down. 'I told you, that you'll find out the hard way you can't do anything you want around this place' Gramps starts laughing as they carry him away. When Brett is out of site, Gramps turns the light off and goes back to bed. -Fade 2 Black-
