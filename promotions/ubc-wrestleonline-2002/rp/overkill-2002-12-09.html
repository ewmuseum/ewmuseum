Overkill

9 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12.09.02 Introduction: Facts are facts, history can't be erased or modified. And the fact is, Chris Carter recently murdered his former friend and agent, Jerry. But does Chris even care? Does it matter to him at all? Does killing even mark somewhat of a notch on his guilty conscious? Maybe, maybe not. You're promised to find out in this promo. Chris is undergoing several changes, which the majority are more than obvious. Even after killing Jerry and his family, the way he did, Chris has the nerve to go to Jerry's funeral! It'll definitely be interesting, 'nuff said. Scene opens up at the front gate of a graveyard, cars are swarming back and forth. It's some time around 7:45 PM, the sun is setting and the evening air is arriving into the atmosphere. A crowd of about fifty people or so, are standing in a section of the graveyard. A section where a new family of headstones and people have been added to the tragic masses. All wearing black and mourning, Jerry's family and friends stand by as a priest leads the final goodbye ceremony. As this is all going on, a very familiar vehicle and character arrive in the parking lot. This man is no stranger to TV Cameras, this man is the killer of Jerry and his family. This man is Chris 'AK' Carter. This is the man who ended Jerry's life, and now he's showing up at his funeral… Chris Carter steps out of his Mercedes, wearing a black doo rag, a black leather jacket, silver jewelry, and black, baggy jeans. Chris Carter walks a bit more until he gets to Jerry's funeral, along with Jerry's friends and family. He stands next to a couple people, crying, and puts his head down, as if he's thinking about something. The talking goes on as more tears are shed… After a few more minutes of the final respect, a quiet laughing noise can be heard. This laughter rudely interrupts all the sorrow and mourning. The laughter grows, louder and louder. It's coming from Chris Carter. Everyone is now shocked and very angry. One man walks up to Chris and confronts him. Tall Man-'You sure got a lot of nerve, buddy. Coming here to a damn funeral of someone we care about and then having the balls to laugh! Who the hell do you think you are? Where do you get off laughing at the death of such a great man?' Chris Carter-'A Great man?… Hahaha, I'm sorry, but no. Jerry Latimer was not a great man. He was a damn fool. He was a coward, a scared lil' bitch.' Gasps come from the crowd. Tall Man-'Excuse me?!' Chris Carter-'What? You didn't hear me? Do I need to repeat myself or are you clear on what came out of my mouth?… Wait, before you answer… I wanna let you know, the only thing that comes out of my mouth is the truth. Deal with it… Jerry was not a great man, he was anything but a great man. Ya see, he and I were really good of friends at one time, and it remained that way for many, MANY years. But recently, he turned on me. He made the biggest mistake of his life time. That's jus' a mistake that he won't ever be able to repay me. But, it's cool, it's aight. Because the price he has payed, is greater than anything he could have repayed me with. Chris silently walks up to Jerry's headstone and begins speaking to it quietly. Chris Carter-'Here we are again, Jerry. I never thought we'd speak again, but I guess I was wrong. Ya know, things didn't have to go down this way, they could have been different… Much different. But no, you chose your fate. You chose it to be like this. I never asked for anything in my life, just someone to trust and have my back. But damn, man, you couldn't even do that for me. You and I, we go way back, it seems like since the beginning of time. And you jus' threw it all away like the selfish fool that you are. Jerry, it was your damn fault you ended up here. I trusted you, I was locked up, and I trusted you. All you had to do was get some of my own damn money and bail me out, and you didn't do that. Instead, you left me their for dead. You were thinkin' I would never get out and that you'd be safe for the rest of your life. Now look at you, you and your wrong ass self. You wouldn't be six feet under if you had never tried to turn your back on me. But that's the thing. People who turn on me never get away with it, and I mean never. It doesn't matter if you steal, if you cheat, if your hurt me, it doesn't fuckin' matter. People who fuck with me don't get away with it, and you're jus' another one who had to find out the hard way…' Chris stands up and takes a breath of air, then rubs his face with his hands and looks back at Jerry's grave. Chris Carter-'Well, man, that's about all I have to say to you. It's your fault you ended up this way. You and your family. Over these past few years, things have been changing me. I'm not the same person I used to be. But in the end, it doesn't matter, it's all good and there are no problems. The fact of the matter is, I'm still breathing. I'm still walking… And you're not. See you in hell.' After speaking those words, Chris Carter gets up and shoots off a cruel look to Jerry's family and friends. He then walks away, with that same Vodka in his hand. He walks from the gravesite to the parking lot, where his car is parked at. Police lights corrupt the calm air as a flashlight shines in Chris's eyes. Chris stops and hides the Vodka bottle behind his back. Police Officer #1-'Hello sir, what are you doin' around here, so late?' Chris Carter-'I… Ahhh… Had a funeral to attend to…' Police Officer #2-'Care to inform us of who's funeral this was?' Chris Carter-'Yeah, no problem. Jerry Latimer.' The two police officers look at eachother without saying a word, in silent communication. Police Officer #1-'Care to show us some ID?' Chris Carter-'Ahhh, yeah, no problem. Let me see if I can find it…' Chris Carter pulls out his wallet, at the same time the police reach for their guns, unnoticeably. Chris pulls hands it over to one of the police officers. He inspects it for a little bit and then hands it back to Chris.' Police Officer #1-'Alright, well, I know you've done something tonight. I just don't have any proof to bust you. So I suggest you get the hell on up out of here.' Chris Carter does exactly that. He walks away from the police and towards his Mercedes. He opens the door and turns his headlines on then starts up the engine. The car roars to life and pulls out of the parking lot. As the car pulls out, a small amount of blood can be seen, left from the tire tracks. The scene then cuts to the front mirror in Chris's car, a wickedly scarey shot of Chris's eyes in the mirror is shown as the scene fades. Outroduction: In another odd twist of events, Chris Carter proves to be even more unpredictable than we thought we was. With all the strange things he's been doing as of late, you can only wonder, what's next? What does the future hold? And is Chris finally over the edge? Has he gone psycho? Personally, I wouldn't doubt it he becomes the next 'Ted Bundy'. In any case, there is a rampage started, a murderer is on the loose. Rest assured, your streets will never be safe again.
