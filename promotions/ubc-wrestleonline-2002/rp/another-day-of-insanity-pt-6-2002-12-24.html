'Another day of Insanity' Pt 6

24 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12-24-02 Days have come and gone for Brett Wayne. He has had many experaince in the past couple of days. He is now just four days away from his Main Event Match against Bobby Bless and Johnny Nightmare. Brett seems not to have any worries now about his match next saturday on Jurisdiction but why? A few days ago he was scared he would lose but now it seems 'The Insane One' has no worries what does Brett have up his sleeve for the fans of UBC. Only time will tell. The Scene goes to Brett standing in the middle of a basketball court on a rainy day. Brett thinks to himself about his future in the UBC, how will his career turn out. Out of no where certain things have come to Brett's attention. He seems to be confident about his upcoming match but he doesn't seem as confident about his career. Is he afraid of Tommy Grady ruining his career or is it the guys from BMW. Brett continues to think about these questions as he goes and picks up a basketball from a rack in the courner of the basketball court. He dribbles for a minute then holds the ball in his hand and start talking to himself. 'Why do people have to interfere in others business' Brett says walking towards the basketball goal. I already know how things are going to turn up this Saturday on Jurisdiction, well I did know how things were going to turn out but now... Brett stops walking toward the goal and scratches his head and continues to speak. But now that Tommy Grady and the boys from BMW have popped into the picture I have no clue how things will turn up. Maybe Tommy is right maybe he will end my career. Brett pauses for a minute and slaps himself across the face. What the hell am I thinking, why did I say that. Why would I let Tommy Grady end my career, he is nothing but trash. I've never heard of him so why should he be a problem. God whats wrong with me? Brett starts dribbling the basketball as he gets next to the goal. Tommy Grady should not be a problem for 'The Insane One.' and neither should the boys from BMW when.... Brett suddenly stops and shuts his mouth. I better shut up, I almost opened my big mouth but anyways the BMW boys don't stand a chance with me. They can talk all the trash they want but I still no who the very best is. Brett tosses the ball up into the goal as it slides around the rim and falls through. Then suddenly a voice is heard coming from a distance. Hey Bro! I've been looking for you. Bretts brother Mike walks up to him and hands him a sheet of paper. 'Whats this' Brett ask while reading the letter. 'Well you see, I was going to put you on disability because....' Mike says as Brett interupts him quickly. 'What the fuck, disability for what, how am a disabled' Brett suddenly gets angry. 'Well...I'm just trying to help you thats it.' says Mike. Brett walks away from Mike and then turns around. 'I absolutly will not be put on disability.' Mike looks at Brett and smiles 'Think about the extra money you will make' 'I DON'T WANT ANY EXTRA MONEY!' Brett shouts. Brett takes the basketball from the ground and punts it, the ball sores through the air until it finds itself bouncing off a windshield as the car alarm goes off. 'Why do you think I'm disabled' 'Mentally' Mike says softly looking down at the ground. 'Mentally, MENTALLY. I'm not mentally ill god damnit' Brett says as he starts slapping himself across the face. 'DOES IT LOOK LIKE IM MENTALLY ILL TO YOU! Mike sits there and frowns as Brett continues to hit himself. 'Just trying to help, sorry for getting you upset' Mike says as he starts to walk off. 'Get back here right now!' Brett says. Mike continues to walk off, Brett begins to run towards him but Brett then finds himself laying on the ground. Mike turns around seeing Brett knocked out on the ground and starts laughing. 'Thats funny, haha' Mike suddenly stops not thinking it is funny anymore as he see's blood running onto the wet concrete court. He pulls out his Cell phone quickly and dials 911. 'I need an ambulance at the City Park's basketball court immediatly' Mike says as he hangs up his cell phone. Mike kneels down and picks up Bretts head try to stop the blood from gushing out of his head. Moments later the ambulance pulls up, the medics rush and get the strecther out from the back of the ambulance and the rush over to Brett still knocked out from his fall. 'Lets get him on the strechter and hurry to the hospital' says the male medic. They pick him up off the ground carefully and lay him on the strecther as Mike follows them to the ambulance. They get Brett in the ambulance carefully as Mike jumps in the back. 'Is he going to be ok?' Mike ask the femal Medic. 'He'll be fine, just have to stop the bleeding real quick then he can rest at the hospital for a day or two.' says the femal medic. The camera fades as the Ambulance takes off leaving black marks behind them as they drive off out of site. -Fade 2 Black-
