Sometime The Past Is Remembered

16 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12.16.02 As the warm breeze rushed though the window, and the slightly cool night gave the world the right atmosphere of comfort, the pages of his novel danced with its windy partner. His eyes scanned the words, reading and taking into heart the same letters his eyes had seen a million times before. His bible was split up into pages. The pages themselves spilt into chapters. Those chapters were all that he needed in his life. The mind of Devin was lost, and no words or actions could wake him from his daze. His hand turned the pages, one by one, as they had before. The words came springing out again, as they had in the past. When he was old enough to read those words and understand what they were expressing, he was hypnotized by them. Lost in their mesmerizing lyrics of life and enthralling in their passive frame. They were always there for him. Always in his mind. Always ready to whisper the wisdom into his ear. And as his eyes met the corner of page 154… Then I went in the park. Boy, was it dark. – Page 154 of Chapter 20 …written in blue pen were three words, of a past that he never could remember but never wanted to forget. The book in his hands wasn’t there anymore. Neither was the wind or the world. Only his mind, the past, and that day in Central Park in New York City.
