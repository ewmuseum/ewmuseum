::: Not HER Again :::

17 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12.17.02 ...What a jealous idiot this Brett Wayne is, huh? I mean, Tommy Grady is a hell of an athlete but I'd hardly call two wins a row a winning streak. And it's pretty easy to be undefeated with his competition.. so one may be convinced to follow the 'wise' words of Brett Wayne and believe that Tommy Grady will not have a chance come Saturday night. Or you could use listen your own 'wise' words that are engulfed within you, screaming out how pathetic Brett Wayne is. 'I'm better than you', 'I'm gonna beat you'.. wow.. some intimidating stuff, my friend. Don't know if Tommy will even want to face you after that stuff. I mean, you're a freakin' animal! I hope you noticed the lack of enthusiasm in that.. and why is that? Because you are nothing to afraid of. You are nothing to even be nervous about. You have faced worse lackluster opponents than Tommy Grady and you're one and one. Am I wrong?... :::Scene opens up showing a prison. There are reporters crawling all over the scene outside the front gates with a podium set up and several microphones attached. Apparently this is a big announcement soon to be made. Well, there is Tommy Grady leaning against the fence. He has his sunglasses on as he looks around the area. He takes a deep breath and makes his way towards the podium. He walks up to the mic and taps it::: Tommy Grady - Is this thing on? :::Tommy smiles::: Tommy Grady - *singing* Who let the dogs out? OOF! OOF-OOF-OOF-OOF! :::Tommy smiles again and looks around to see no one smiling::: Tommy Grady - Uh.. props to Baha Men for that last song.. Reporter - When will the press conference be held on this latest big arrest, sir? Tommy Grady - AH! Exceptional question, my good man. It begins.. PRONTO! Reporter - You called this? Tommy Grady - I did, and I'll tell you why! Another Reporter - Better be good! I'm missing the dog with AIDS press conference for this.. Tommy Grady - Oh, it's a hell of a lot better than dogs with AIDS! It's a prostitute.. Reporter - A HO?! Awesome.. in New Mexico, nonetheless.. Another Reporter - I see the headlines now.. Cops Nab Hoe from New Mexico! Tommy Grady - That's not even the punch line.. Reporter - Don't tell me it's that Brett Wayne's mom again.. Another Reporter - That's getting old.. Yet Another - So is her poon.. :::Everyone looks at the man::: Yet Another - Poon.. le.. poodle, I mean.. uh.. Tommy Grady - You mean this story has already broke? Reporter - Don't get me wrong, it's a great story, but unless there's a new twist it's nothing more than second page news.. Tommy Grady - She offered to squirt water for money! Another Reporter - Squirt water?! I see the headlines now.. Hoe from New Mexico is Fountain of Whore-ography! Reporter - That won't fit.. Another Reporter - It's so good we'll MAKE it fit! Tommy Grady - Now, I'll be taking questions on my mental state after that whore-able.. *laughs* get it? WHORE.. able.. :::Reporters laugh at Tommy's dumb joke::: Tommy Grady - .. incident that occured last night.. Another Reporter - ME! OH! ME ME! PICK ME! Tommy Grady - You, Mi.. Another Reporter - You? Tommy Grady - You're Mi right? Another Reporter - No, I'm me.. Tommy Grady - That's what I said.. go, Mi.. Another Reporter - Go you? Tommy Grady - Are you Yu? Another Reporter - Of course I am.. Tommy Grady - Then why'd you say you were Mi? Another Reporter - ... that's a DAMN good question.. Tommy Grady - So your name is Yu, not Mi? Another Reporter - I'm me.. Tommy Grady - Just ask your damn question you confused human being.. Another Reporter - Alright, well -- TOMMY.. Brett's mother has been known.. uh.. from.. what I understand.. NOT by first hand experience by any means or anything.. *spaces out with a grin* .. OH YEAH.. she's been known to bend over and howl like a dog while thrusting.. did that happen to you and if so -- you lucky bastard.. Tommy Grady - No.. she did show me her veigny thighs.. All Reporters - AH! Yeah.. Tommy Grady - You know what I'm talking about? All Reporters - US? No no.. Tommy Grady - Okay, next question.. Yet Another - Was she a screamer or more of a uh uh uh uh type girl? Tommy Grady - I didn't take her home, guys.. I just was verbally raped by this woman.. Yet Another - You did not take her home? Tommy Grady - Let me say it again -- I did not.. have.. sexual relations with that woman.. Ms. Wayne.. Another Reporter - Let me get this straight.. you did NOT pop the rolls royce into park? Tommy Grady - Don't have a rolls royce.. Reporter - You didn't stick the hot dog in the hairy ant hole? Tommy Grady - That's a waste of food.. Yet Another - Tommy, you didn't.. how should I say.. add some sausage to the omlet? Tommy Grady - I always add sausage.. with a slice of bacon.. All Reporters - BACON! Of COURSE! New Reporter - Hot off the presses! Dog humps womans nostril, spooges in her eye! Press conference in one hour! :::A ruckus is heard::: Reporter - But what about Brett? Tommy Grady - He won't be joining us today.. Another Reporter - Oh yes he is.. he was spotted about ten minutes away about.. *looks at watch* ten minutes ago.. Tommy Grady - AAAAAAAAAAAAAH! :::Tommy takes off running. The camera shows him running away from the reporters, who look on in confusion. The camera cuts to an area further away from Tommy as he continues to run. He is now in a commercial area and he runs up to the side of a restuarant where people are sitting on the patio. Tommy runs up to a couple eating::: Tommy Grady - *out of breath* Can I.. *gasp* have a.. *deep breath* drink of that? Lady on Patio - Uh.. no.. Tommy Grady - I'm gonna.. *breath* .. DIE here.. Lady on Patio - This is expensive.. I-- :::Tommy gives up the nice act and grabs ahold of what appears to be an iced tea. He drowns it quickly, until there is nothing but ice in the glass. He slams it on her table and her mouth is open in anger. The other woman sitting across from her look confused::: Lady on Patio - How DARE you drink my tea, you overgrown bully! :::A look comes over Tommy Grady. The transformation? But he didn't have any alcohol!::: Lady on Patio - You owe me another Long Island Iced Tea! :::The look continues to take over Grady::: Tommy Grady - I don't owe you jack shit! Lady on Patio - Well, I.. NEVER! Tommy Grady - Never had a date? Never had a mirror? I'd believe both of those cuz you're so damn ugly! :::The lady doesn't even say another word. He mouth just drops and she looks at her friend. Tommy snickers and walks away::: Tommy Grady - Ya never know when somethin' is gonna hit ya.. the question, Brett, is are you ready? From listening to your words -- apparently not. I was hoping we wouldn't have to do things the hard way.. but you're so hard-headed when it comes to getting 'your overly deserved' shot at the first Ultimate Brawlers Trophy.. What makes you think you're so deserving? You expect favors from the front office.. from possibly even Johnny Nightmare himself? Wake up, what goes around.. comes around. Speaking of coming around.. you'll find by the time you do on Jurisdiction -- it'll be too late. You'll just end up being another disgruntled UBC fighter that thought his time at the top was just a fingertip away. Your chance has come and gone Brett, but mine.. *slight laugh*.. mine hasn't even begun. The fact is, Brett -- at six foot four, two-hundred and sixty-five pounds -- I'll be more than just a fight.. It's that kind of stupidity that no doubt caused you to lose to Brad Douglas, a shameful loss. You can keep telling yourself that I'll be easy to get past.. the man on top of this company.. Krisis couldn't do it.. he couldn't come close! Nor could numerous others who thought they could beat me at ANYTHING in the past. Do you take the Underground Brawlers Club as a joke, Brett? Well I'm not laughing.. and in case you caught a glimpse of yourself in one of the dressing room mirrors -- I'm not exactly sure what's amusing you either. Just when you think you've got everything figured out it all changes and you're in unfamiliar territory.. yeah, well -- no shit, but that's the nature of the business we're in. You can't have everything figured out.. you can't back on anything -- you can't even trust anyone other than yourself. Those that are truly great though deal with it and succeed regardless of those odds.. and you're looking at one of them right now. You want competition? Be careful what you wish for Mr. Wayne, because you'll be getting plenty of it on Saturday Night Jurisdiction. Forget Brad Douglas -- the only person you need to be paying attention to is me. I hold your number Brett, and the only thing you can do is point your finger at the bookies for being dumb enough to listen to your challenge. I've dominated at everything I've ever done, and nothing changes as I enter in the world of fighting for money -- moving closer and closer to the top every second of the day. Time devourer, your time just expired -- it's time to step aside and let TOMMY GRADY carry on his journey towards the promised land. I've layed it all down on the table for you.. it's time you took it all in and realized that you don't matter as much as you think you do. It's all about me and achieving what I was told I never would -- becoming the Ultimate champion. I only see you as another obstacle, another naysayer who wants to try and come between me and my destiny. Well that's not gonna happen.. Cuz any way you cut it.. whether it's in the back or in a cage or just out in the middle of that damn fighting zone.. your ass will fall to a ending that is Simply Degrading.. mark my words, Brett.. you'll be counting the lights! :::Suddenly Tommy shakes his head and stumbles backwards.. falling onto the floor. He shakes his head again and pulls himself back up to his feet::: Tommy Grady - Whoa, dude.. that was the best blackout EVER! :::Ring ring::: Tommy Grady - *answering his cell* Yo yo dis is da Grado, how can I help yo.. Voice - I see you.. Tommy Grady - Who is this? Voice - Look to your right.. Tommy Grady - *looking to his left* OH NO! You're a moped?! Please don't run over me, I'll be good for Christmas, I promise! Voice - You're OTHER right, stupid! Tommy Grady - Watch yo mouth, Jerko! :::Tommy looks to his right and sees the car of Aaron Everage::: Aaron Everage - Come get in the fuckin' car.. :::Tommy looks angry at the way Aaron talked to him and he hangs up the phone, walking in Aaron's direction. He opens the door to the car and hops in::: Aaron Everage - I've been drivin' around lookin' for you all day and you've been walking around in THIS shit hole? Tommy Grady - GROSS! I was in a shit hole? I didn't even notice.. thanks for savin' me, Aaron! You da man! Aaron Everage - What the hell have you been doing? Tommy Grady - Well, after I got home last night I decided to hold a press conference in the name of Momma Wayne.. Aaron Everage - The whore? Tommy Grady - You're being too nice.. the dirty stinky ass whore.. Aaron Everage - And.. you did this why? Tommy Grady - Because it's only the most creative idea ever to be created! Aaron Everage - I think even shitting into a bucket was a better idea than that.. Tommy Grady - Aw shucks.. Aaron Everage - Let's go home.. :::Tommy shrugs and slumps his shoulders. The camera shows the car driving away into the distance until the image just shows a hill. Slowly the image fades off to black::: ...The day is getting closer and closer, Brett.. I can almost hear the boos now. But the cheers are even louder. Cheers of Jurisdiction when Tommy Grady knocks you out like a cold fish. Makes you scream like a little girl. Mike, Johnny.. NOBODY is gonna be there to save you on Saturday. And that will be your downfall...
