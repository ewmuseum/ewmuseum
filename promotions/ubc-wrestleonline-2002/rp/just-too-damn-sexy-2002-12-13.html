Just too damn sexy!

13 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12/13/02 **The camera flashes on to show a beatiful New Mexico silhouette from the inside of a snazzy jet black limo. The limo is loaded with a T.V., minifridge, you name it. Unsurprisingly, Raine Knights can be seen sitting down on the plush leather seat. He is staring out the window at the beauty which surrounds him, as he wipes his wire frame Oakley's with his silk shirt sleeve. He slowly looks back to see that the camera is on, and flashes a cocky smile that could piss off a monk.** Raine: Even in my personal limo, the UBC cameras still manage to get a shot of me. I guess you guys just can't get enough of me. I know Troy McLean couldn't. **Raine takes a second to snicker at the memory of him sending Troy McLean out of the UBC the hard way. He presses a button, which triggers the driver window to open. He then addresses the driver.** Raine: Hey, you, greaseball. How long until we get there? Driver: About a half an hour, and if you wouldn't mind Mr. Knights, I prefer Lenny. Raine: Yeah whatever. Hey greaseball, tell you what, you get me there in half the time, I'll buy you a new facecloth. **The driver sighs.** Driver: Yes Mr. Knights. **Raine smiles and nods, and closes the driver window. He then turns around to face the camera once again.** Raine: OK, what was I talking about again? Oh yeah, Troy McLean. Heh, well, what is there really to say, I destroyed him, now he's gone, and that's that. Now what's this I hear about me getting a dark match this week? What the hell is up with that?! I'm World Championship material, and the UBC bigwigs think that they can just throw me in the opener with some bitchface Convict character. Hey, bitchface, while you weren't getting pooned up the ass, I hope you spent some time in that jail gym, because your gonna need all the strength possible to even last two seconds with 'True Perfection', and that's not just a guess, that's a perfect certainty! **Raine throws on his Oakley's again as the camera fades out.**
