Friend Against Friend (RP #2 for Jurisdiction)

19 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12/19/02 [The scene opens up in the Underground Fighting Warehouse in Detroit, Michigan. Violent B is standing in the ring, getting ready to fight his long time friend and member of the “SHOOTERS”, Snake. They both look like they’re ready to fight. They are in each other’s faces. Snake then pushes Violent B. Violent B then pushes Snake back. Snake then gets pissed off and throws a punch. Violent B gets nailed in the face with the shot. Violent B’s head turns to the side. Violent B whips his head back and he has an angry look on his face. Violent B’s look of anger than turns into that cocky smile. He puts his hand on the punch wound.] Violent B - “Nice shot Snake, but it’s gonna take A LOT more than that to beat me.” [Snake gets the same smile.] Snake - “I don’t doubt it.” [Violent B then throws a punch, which hits Snake in the head. Snake turns his head in pain. He turns hit face back to Violent B, with a look of anger on his face. Snake then throws another punch, but Violent B blocks the punch and throws another punch, which Snake ducks. They begin to fight. They throw punches at each other, but each punch is blocked. Violent B then throws a hard kick which hits Snake in the ribs. Snake bends over. Violent B grabs his head and attempts to lift his knee into Snake’s face, but Snake sees this coming and avoids the knee. He then hit’s a stiff uppercut to Violent B’s jaw. Violent B tumbles backward and Snake lifts his leg up for a roundhouse kick, but Violent B catches the foot with his hand. He then lifts up his leg and kicks Snake into the corner. Violent B then notices a trickle of blood coming out of his mouth. He wipes it off.] Violent B - “You certainly have improved since the last time we fought. You might have drawn first blood, but you definitely didn’t win the fight yet.” [Violent B then runs up to the corner with a clothesline type arm into the corner. Snake flies up. Violent B then throws Snake off the ropes and hit’s a knee to Snake’s gut, sending him flipping over and landing hard on the mat. Violent B then gets into the mount position and begins to hit punches to the face of Snake. Snake then reverses it and begins to punch Violent B. They roll over and over until they hit the ropes. They both get up. They charge at each other and lock up. While in the lock up, they exchange trash talk.] Snake - “So far you’ve been lucky. I’ll give you more than you can handle!” Violent B - “Yeah, you just think that!” [Violent B then pushes Snake away. Snake flies and hit’s the mat. Then he gets up, Violent B runs at him and attempts a spear, but Snake moves and Violent B goes headfirst into the corner. He falls to the ground. Snake points and laughs at him.] Snake - “You ALWAYS do that!” [Violent B gets up, shakes it off, and starts to throw punches at Snake. Snake dodges them all. Snake then throws a punch at Violent B, which hits him in the jaw. Violent B instantly shakes it off and continues the fight. Violent B hit’s a knee to Snake’s ribs. Violent B puts Snake’s head between his legs. Violent B lifts him up and power bombs him to the mat. Violent B looks at one of the men in the warehouse, and tells him to come in and count the 3. The guy rolls in. Violent B pins Snake. The guy comes down and counts. 1.……2.….Snake kicks out.] Violent B - “I knew that wouldn’t be it yet. I know this Snake’s got A LOTTA fight in him.” [Violent B picks Snake up, and Snake punches him in the ribs. He pushes Violent B into the corner and begins to pummel him. He grabs his head and begins to hit his head into the corner. Snake then grabs Violent B and suplexes him. He then rolls into the mount position and begins to his rights to Violent B’s face. Violent B rolls into the ropes and they break the fight up. Violent B gets up with an angry look on his face.] Violent B - “So you think you can do that to ME! NOW YOU PISSED ME OFF!” [Violent B, with an angry look on his face, runs and spears Snake to the mat. He then begins punching him in the face with furious anger. He picks Snake up and puts him into the corner. He then pounds his head in with right hands. He grabs him and hits an overhead belly-to-belly suplex. Violent B then jumps up and signals that the fight is over. He picks Snake up and puts his head between his arms. He picks him up into the vertical suplex position and hits “The Violent Drop”. He then rolls over and pins Snake. The man acting as referee counts. 1.…2.…3! Violent B gets up and gets into Snake’s face.] Violent B - “WHAT THE HELL DID I TELL YOU!? I TOLD YOU I’D BEAT YOUR ASS!” [Violent B then gets up and throws his arms in the air, and the men in the warehouse cheer for him. Snake begins to stir. Violent B turns around to see him. Snake gets up, dizzy and confused. Violent B then walks up to him.] Violent B - “I told you I’d beat you. Who’s the toughest now!?” [Snake has a look of disappointment on his face. He then looks up at Violent B.] Snake - “You did beat me. You truly are tougher than I am.” [Snake then puts out his hand. Violent B shrugs. Violent B then shakes his hand. Violent B walks out of the ring. He puts on his shirt, his necklace, his jacket, and walks out of the warehouse as the scene fades.]
