Precious Thoughts

3 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1/03/03 So calm, so at peace… here tranquility. In my mind… my gentle, peaceful mind. Nothing to do… nothing to say. Just relax. Images, patterns… all the colors. My mind begins to take off, thoughts enter my head. At first… a river. A small river, surrounded by trees on either side. Nature’s beauty around my as I float gently down the river. The noises around my, the frogs, birds… a feint noise in the distance. Music… so peaceful. And the smells… fresh, awakening smells. The leaves, the plants, the water. The water… so clean, sparking off the sunlight. I place my hand down into the water. So cool… clean. I bring it up and wipe my face. My dirty face. I rub my hands over my face… making it clean again. Then I lay back. My head resting against the soft wood of my boat. I stare up to the sky above my, the sun blinding my slightly for a moment, before my eyes settle. Further down the river… still floating… still resting. Thoughts in my head, knowing I have no worries, I have no cares. So calm, so at peace… I close my eyes again. Relaxing, the colors return again. Images, patterns. I think, my mind takes off again… I look around me. The grey stone walls… the metal… the fire. The heat burns my face as I begin to walk forwards… towards the other men. The clanging of the metal upon metal as they work. I screams of pain… the crying… the shouting. I cover my ears and turn away… then pain. A sharp whip across my back. Orders for me. More orders. Shoved towards the fire… spat upon… mocked. I fall to my knees… I drop my head in my hands. This torture… this pain. I start to cough… I open my mouth coughing… sweat running over my lips. My gut wrenches… my mouth tightens… blood spews out. I cough again… more blood. Still being whipped… mocked… spat upon. I look up… towards the light. The light so far away… so far away. Sweat running down my face… tears rolling across my cheeks… blood dripping from my mouth. I close my eyes once again. And I relax… the images again. Patterns and the colors. I begin to calm again… My eyes suddenly shoot open. Question shoot through my mind. Where am I? Who am I? I feel faint… but only for a brief second. I look to my left and see off in the distance two figures speaking to one another. I guess that means I must wake from my slumber. . . The scene opens with the UBC rookie interviewer Jimmy Daniels nervously pacing back in forth in front of the camera mumbling to himself, “I can do this, I can do this.” The Camera cuts away to the inside of the UBC Underground area. The sight of Saturday’s Justification show. In just a few short hours it will be packed wall to wall with screaming fans. But for now it is nothing more than a deserted building. Well almost deserted. One lone person sits in the middle of the ring. The person (MrSynnerster) is lying perfectly still on his back with his arms across his chest as if he were in a coffin. He is dressed in black leather pants, a black shirt and a black duster jacket. His dreads are fanned out around his head like a peacock’s tail and his top hat is leaning up, so that it covers the top part of his face. MrSynnerster is lying perfectly still and silent. The only sounds being made are the echoing footstep of Jimmy and the cameraman. Once the two reach the ring, Jimmy back at the cameraman, and asks, “Is he sleep?” Camera Man shrugs his shoulders, making the camera move up and down. “I don’t know. Go check on him, dude” “Me?” Jimmy exclaims. “What about you?” Cameraman: “Dude my job is just to hold the camera. You gotta do all that other shit, dude.” Jimmy: “Shit, okay. Hold this.” He tosses the cameraman the microphone, and gives the ring a funny look. He puts one foot on the ring apron, then puts it back down. He puts it on the apron again, then puts it back down. The Cameraman sighs, “Do you need some help?” Jimmy: “Umm . . . Yes.” The camera put down, so all the viewers can see is the bottom of the ring. In the background they here the cameraman say, “There you go” and a loud thud. The camera is picked up and the views see Jimmy laying face down on the mat beside MrSynnerster. “Did you have to throw me? Jimmy groans. Cameraman: “My bad dude. I didn’t know you were that skinny. This camera weights more than you do. Yo dude, Synnerster still hasn’t moved. Is he dead? Jimmy: “Hold on. I’ll check.” Before Jimmy and sit up, MrSynnerster pounces on him like a bird swooping down on its prey. MrSynnerster grabs Jimmy by the collar of his shirt and lifts him up off his feet so that they are face to face. MrSynnerster: “What do you want little man?” Jimmy doesn’t say a word. He simply faints and crumples to the ground. MrSynnerster looks over at the cameraman and smiles. MrSynnerster: “I still got it.” Cameraman: “Got what?” MrSynnerster: “That psychotic glare that makes little piss-ant faint.” Cameraman: “Who said you ever lose it. Your psycho Joker smile scares me, dude. You kinda reminds me of a demented Dennis Rodman. I'm surprised you haven't found your way into my nightmares.” MrSynnerster: “Thank you. I’ll take that as a compliment.” Cameraman: “Dude, what are you doing here?” William: “It’s a long story, but I’ll be happy to tell you. It all started earlier today when I was cutting a promo.. . .” Harps begin to play, and the screen goes wavy as a flashback occurs. . . The camera fades in on an empty pool hall. Well, almost empty. Partially obscured by the other tables and the lights hanging above them a figure can be noticed moving around a table lining up for a shot. The camera weaves its way through the tables for a better look at this lone individual. As the camera approaches the individual's identity is not defined as he has his back to the camera leaned over the table preparing for another shot. The camera turns back to the figure that stands up after his shot. He then pauses and turns around to face the camera confirming his identity. None other than MrSynnerster. MrSynnerster: “Throughout history men have strived to achieve greatness and gain everything they can. Which indeed is a noble pursuit. However, for everything one man obtains there is another standing nearby who wishes to have what has been earned by that other individual. It has occurred over and over again all throughout time, one man questing for that which another possesses. MrSynnerster takes some chalk and redusts his cue stick. MrSynnerster: “But what defines a man is how he chooses to go about acquiring that which he desires. Some men choose to prove their valor and will put themselves to task and work hard to obtain that prize. But not all men possess the sense of righteousness to set out on such a quest. For every man who stands up for what he believes in and fights for what he wants, there is another man who would rather take the short cuts and lie, cheat, and steal rather than fight face to face. MrSynnerster turns back to the table and moves around to line up another shot. MrSynnerster: “And thus is the predicament laid before me now. You see, I have something that someone else wants. But yet, that individual does not have the guts to come up to me face to face and confront me. No, instead they chose to come from behind and nail me when I was involved in a heated match. MrSynnerster takes his shot and stands up looking into the camera. MrSynnerster: “That’s right Mr. Vision is jealous of yours truly. The man who should happy to be hired by the UBC, but yet he can't be happy with what he has. No, instead he is caught in a fit of jealousy because I have something he doesn't. But what I have is not something tangible. Not something he can just steal away from me. No, instead what he wants is something that the only way he can get it is take me completely out of the picture. William moves around the table considering his next shot. MrSynnerster: “You see Mr. Vision knows that I was hand picked by Xavier Morningstar. Yes, I constantly tease of Mr. Morningstar, but he knows it’s all in good fun. Xavier knows that’s just how I am. Xavier did not put me in a handicap match to make me look bad. He did it because he knew I could take out two men with no problem. But there were forces out there, I shall not mention that had other ideas in mind. And after it was all said and done, Mr. Red Vision decided to use me to make a big splash in the UBC.” William fires off another hard shot across the table. MrSynnerster: “I hate to be the bearer of bad news Mr. Vision, but when I’m finished with you, your big splash will be more like a big belly floop into a shallow pool. You see not only am I going to get my revenge on you for what you did, but I’m going to be rewarded with gold once the job is done.” MrSynnerster sinks another shot leaving just a couple balls left on the table. MrSynnerster: “But unlike you Mr. Vision, I am not going to sneak up from behind you and try and take you out. No, rather I was and still am going to stand up, confront you face to face. You have no idea what you’ve gotten yourself into Mr. Vision. You have no idea.” MrSynnerster sinks one more shot leaving just the 8 ball left on the table. MrSynnerster: “Tomorrow Mr. Vision, you will be put on the injured list. Tomorrow you will find yourself laid up in a hospital bed. Plaster will cover your body, holding the bones in place as they hell. I have watched the last Justicfication show over and over again, Mr. Vision, and each time I get more and more anger. Anger at what you did to me. Tomorrow you will be the outlet for my frustrations over the events of last time. Mr. Vision, facts are facts, as talented as you are, you can't hack it in the ring with me. As fast and as quick as you are, as technically sound as you are, it isn't going to make a bit of difference in the ring with me. You see little birdie, tomorrow I'm going to clip your wings and ground you. There will be no running around the ring, playing that hit and run game that has won you some matches in the past. Tomorrow you will be grounded, and once there dear bird boy, you are going to feel the pain that every man in the UBC fears. Mr. Vision, tomorrow you’re going to feel the Burning Sensation. And I’m not talking about the one the whore gave you. You see Mr. Vision, in your pain I will find release for my frustrations. MrSynnerster leans over and sinks the 8 ball then drops his cue on the table. MrSynnerster: “Tomorrow. Red Vision, will just be left red.” Out of the darkness a voice says, “What the heck is this?” MrSynnerster’s agent, Gene E. Prince steps out of the shadows. He is dressed in a gray business suit with a white shirt and gray tie. His hair is in the usual Don King-ese cone shaped Afro. MrSynnerster: “What are you talking about?” Gene: “I’m asking what are you doing?” MrSynnerster: “I’m cutting a promo.” Gene: “That’s pretty obvious, but something missing.” MrSynnerster: “What?” Gene: “This is a MrSynnerster promo, but yet, I haven’t seen a single joke. No funny lines. No parodies. No nothing. Wuzupwitdat?” MrSynnerster: “You’re stealing lines from the Hurricane now?” Gene: “I had to. You haven’t stolen anyone’s lines. What’s up with all the seriousness. You’re even calling yourself MrSynnerster today, instead of William. MrSynnerster: “As I said yesterday, this is not a week for jokes. It’s time to get serious.” Gene: “I knew you were going to say that. Which is why I’d like you to meet two of my new employees.” From the shadows steps two men. One a tall muscular black man dress in a black turtle neck, black jeans, black duster, and round black sunglasses. He head is shaved bald. The other man is a short skinny blond man with his shoulder length hair in a ponytail. He is dressed in a black T-shirt with UBC on the front, and baggy blue jean cargo pants. When MrSynnerster and the white guy look at each other they both exclaim. Both: “You!!!” Gene: “You two know each other?” MrSynnerster: “Yeah. That’s Lance Ryden. He’s the fucker that made us dress up for that stupid Lord of the Rings premiere the a few weeks ago.” Lance: “What the deal son.” Lance holds his hand out for some dat. MrSynnerster looks at Lance’s hand, then rolls his eyes. Lance: “Oh, I see how it is. You go leave me hangin’.” MrSynnerster: (looking at Gene) “Gene, why is he here.” Gene: “He’s my new cyber junkie. He’s going to be working on the Gene E. Website as well as the MrSynnerster website.” Lance: “Don’t worry playa, I’m gonna hook yo’ sight up. Fo’ shizzle my nizzle.” MrSynnerster: “Why are you talking like that?” Lance: “I’m just trying to fit in. You know what I’m sayin’. Ya’ll my nig. . . (Gene, MrSynnerster, and the other black man heads whip towards Lance.) never mind.” Gene shakes his head in disgust and sighs, “White folks.” (Point to the black man in the duster) “Oh and William, this is your new trainer Mr. Laft.” William looks Mr. Laft over. William: “Has anyone every told you you look like either Morpheus from The Matrix, The Angel of Death from the Crossroad’s video, or Shaft.” (William looks at the camera) “As you can see, the narrator is calling me William instead of MrSynnerster now, so you know I’m about to act stupid.”(singing) “Who is the man that looks like the angel of death?” Lance: “Laft.” William: “You damn right. Who is the man that’s going to help train Mr. Synnerster?” Lance: “Laft!” William: “I can dig it.” Lance: “I sure can dig it. Suuuuuuuuuuccccccccccckkkkkkkkaaaaaaaaaaa!!!” William: “Damn it Lance. That’s Booker T. Not Laft.” Lance: “But I like Booker T. He’s a bad mother. . .” William: “Shut your mouth.” Lance: “But I’m just talking about T. You know what I’m sayin’, G.” Mr. Laft: “Are you two done yet?” William: “I suppose we are.” Mr. Laft: “Good then it’s time to get started.” Mr. Laft begins to pace back and forth. Mr. Laft: “I too heard what you said earlier, MrSynnerster. And I’ve seen what Mr. Red Vision has done to you. With my training methods we are not going to let that happen again. Correct me if I’m wrong, but the other night you went to the premiere of Lord of the Rings.” William: “You are correct.” Mr. Laft: “And correct me if I’m wrong, but you also locked out the entire costumed clowns waiting in line to see the movie.” William: “Again, you are correct sir.” Mr. Laft: “It’s safe to say that those people are probably still pretty pissed off. After all they are fan boys, and you did keep them from their PRECIOUS movie.” William: “Yeah, I’m pretty sure I have a price on my head in the LOTR fan boy community.” Mr. Laft: “I bet that price would double if they knew you had this.” Mr. Laft reaches into his pocket and pulls out a gold ring. Mr. Laft: “This was the ring used in the Lord of the Rings movie.” He puts it in William’s pocket. Mr. Laft: “Meet us back at Gene’s office for the next phase of your training.” Mr. Laft, Lance, and Gene walk off leaving William alone in the pool hall. Suddenly in darkness, several big eyes begin to appear. And one word echoes through the hall. PRECIOUS. William looks at the camera. William: “This is worse than the time I had to fight off a town of redneck.” William makes a mad dash for the door, and the scene goes wavy returning to the United Center. William: “And that’s why I’m here now. I’m hiding out from the fan boys.” Jimmy: “Do you still have the ring?” William: “Yep. Right here.” William holds up a necklace with the ring attached to it. Jimmy’s eyes grow big. Jimmy: “My precious!” William looks at the camera. William: “Here we go again.” William makes a run for the door as the scene fades out. . . . ---- Back
