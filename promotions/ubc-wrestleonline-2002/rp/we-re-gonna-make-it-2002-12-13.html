We're Gonna Make It

13 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Saturday 14th (...The camera opens up and fades into a shot of Hanger and Wonder standing in an abandoned warehouse which is littered with boxes filled with nothing but junk and an old conveyor belt is sitting in the middle of the warehouse. Hanger and Wonder are sitting on the converyor belt and are wearing Jeans as well as their Dangerous Revolution T-Shirts. Hanger is the first to speak into the camera...) [--Steve Hanger--]: Some people just don't seem to understand. You two claim to bigger and smarter than all the rest well my buddy Sean here I believe is the biggest man in UBC he is standing at 6ft 9 and weighs a solid 285 pounds. You see you two come from a third rate country and your a third rate competitor's their is no denying that first fact and I can assure both of you that by the end of Jurisdiction it will be proved to everyone that along with being from a third rate country you will be shown off as third rate competitors. [--Sean Wonder--]: Now Malik you seem to be a very diluted man don't you? You seem to think that you are going to put me The Natural Wonder of Nature in a hospital bed? I very much doubt that but because of those stupid and idiotic words I am going to take my anger out on you because you seem to think you are better than me and frankly Malik it offended me. It made me feel like I was being pissed on by a little kid which is exactly what you are Malik and that is exactly how you are going to be treated inside the Pit. [--Steve Hanger--]: Malik it does seem to me that you are the stupid one of your team. You seem to think that we are here because we wanna be Show Offs and that we don't belond in UBC because it is a place where people fight. Well I hate to tell you this but I think I can hold up my fair share on the fighting end of the scale. You see I have fought twice in a Hell On Earth Match, I have been looked inside a Glass Hell In A Cell and I fought for my life inside of those and I survived and inside The Pit will be nothing more than a walk through King's Cross for me. [--Sean Wonder--]: Once again you provided us with a simple mis-interpratation of ours words you see we weren't looking past you and towards the brotherhood. We where just simply stating that The Brotherhood exisited and if the time came we would fight them. Through all of these words however we are not looking past you and we are not going to take you two lighty because we are going to give to both of you every single thing we have got and by the end of the night A Dangerous Revolution will have begun at the expense of Kingz. (...Both Hanger and Wonder get a sadistic smile before they go back to addressing The Kingz of Africa...) [--Steve Hanger--]: Like I told you both before your 'Employer' is going to have his words heard on my time and that is a personal insult to me because from what I have heard due to my various sources your employer doesn't belong anywhere with the same breathing space as me let alone inside the Pit where I will be standing after you two are knocked unconcious on the ground as all the world will see that you are both nothing more than two men who talked big but couldn't live up to your expecations and fell to a Revolution. [--Sean Wonder--]: Now you want to try and sound cool and hip by relating to rap music but it seems as though you just don't have a clue when it comes the music world because although you claim that you will continue to go through people after you believe you will beat us when it will be a well known fact at the end of the Jurisdiction that you won't be able to beat us and the wheels will have come off of your ride a little early because to quote the undisputed Kings of Heavy Metal we are going to 'Seek and Destory'. [--Steve Hanger--]: Not to mention while the fans will be standing in line to see the show tonight they will be talking about The Dangerous Revolution dethroning the Kings because we are going to celebrate our victory in the only town that will be able to warrant a debut victory celebration of a Dangerous Revolution victory.......DETRIOT ROCK CITY!!!! (...Both men launch into a quick chorus of Detroit Rock City before Hanger looking back the camera seriously has some final words.....) [--Steve Hanger--]: Kingz of Africa the time has come for a Dangerous Revolution to be swept over UBC and you both shall be the first victims in a long list of many. See You inside THE PIT!!!! (...Hanger and Wonder laugh evily as the camera fades out their picture but the audio of their laugh still rings through the darkness creating an eerie sensation...)
