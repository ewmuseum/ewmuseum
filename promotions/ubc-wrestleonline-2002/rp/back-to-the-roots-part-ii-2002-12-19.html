Back To The Roots - Part II

19 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12.19.02 The scene opens up in darkness. It’s black, but not completely. The only light in the room is shining on a small, wooden poker table. The air is filled with cigar smoke and there is a deck of cards lying on the table. A few bottles of beer and vodka lay assorted around the edges of the table as two men are sitting at the table. Violent B: So how exactly is this gonna go down? This seems like something we haven’t done in a long time, I just wanna make sure we go through this with no mistakes, period. Ya know what I mean? Chris “AK” Carter: Hell yeah I know what you mean, dogg. See, UBC is like an entirely new environment to Sudden Violence, we’re the newbies here. But the sad thing is, we’re already the ones who are dominating. And soon enough, we’ll have our reputation back. The reputation that was once feared through out the world of wrestling. The reputation that officially made us cold blooded, mean ass muthafuckaz. B, trust me, we’re gonna get that back. We’re claimin’ what’s ours and takin’ our spot back. Violent B: Damn straight, I couldn’t say it better myself. Chris, I know how you feel sometimes. Hell, not sometimes, all the time. I’m sick of not being a legend, I’m sick of not being given the proper respect I deserve too, just like you. All these new damn faces like Tommy Grady, Synnerster, Brett Wayne, Johnny NightMare. Shit man, the list could go on and on. I can’t stand it. It makes me fuckin’ sick to my stomach to watch these guys run around and act like they’re even close to our level. It’s time to beat them back down to the level they belong at. It’s time to show these little fuckers who the damn bosses are. I’m so down with whatever you have planned. Chris “AK” Carter: That’s good, B, that’s very good. Because what I have planned, is unlike anyone has ever seen before. I mean, yeah, sure, people can laugh and crack jokes now, but when Jurisdiction hits… BAM! Something so sick, so heinous, so violent will happen to Synnerster at the hands of you and I, that the UBC fan base will never be the same again. Not only will the fans be terrified to watch another Chris Carter or Violent B match, but the damn roster will be terrified to step in the warehouse with either one of us. And that, B, is exactly what my goal is. This is the first step in taking back what should rightfully belong to us, and that is respect. Violent B slaps a few cards down, takes a hit of the cigar, and looks back up to Chris. Chris takes a slam of Vodka mixed with Lemonade, then responds back to Violent B with a few cards of his own hand. Violent B: Ya know what I just realized? Chris “AK” Carter: What would that be? Violent B: Poker is a lot like real life. If you seriously think about it, they are practically identical. Chris “AK” Carter: How the hell do you figure? Violent B: Alright, in poker, everyone starts out even. But over time, the best rise to the top. It’s not necessarily about who’s the best, all the time. Sometimes people like to use the saying, “Nice Guys Finish Last”, and damn that is true. I think that is probably why you, myself, Krisis, and Paranoia never decided to be nice to people. Cause the fact of the matter is, we never NEEDED to be nice to anyone. A lot of people have to kiss ass and make lots of friends to benefit themselves and progress in life, but no, not us. We needed the four of eachother and we then had the world at the tip of our fingers. Chris “AK” Carter: I feel ya, that’s reality right there. Only if every other stupid muthafucka in the UBC could see that. We got guys calling themselves synnersters and making public service announcements, we got guys like Tommy Grady and Brett Wayne who act like one of their matches are the biggest fights in the history of the world. But when it comes down to it, it’s all about us. Sure, ain’t nobody fuckin’ realize that jus’ yet, but it will come soon enough. All the disrespect and bullshit we’ve had to put up with in our stay here at UBC so far, it will all be put to a brutal holt at Jurisdiction. After we do this, nobody will have shit to say. And if they do, we’ll jus’ talk ot good ole’ President Kron for another handi cap match, hahaha. Violent B: Hell yeah, that’s what I’m talkin’ about! But still, you haven’t told me exactly what we’re gonna do… Care to inform me, or are you just gonna keep me wondering? Chris “AK” Carter: Of course I’m gonna tell you, otherwise it wouldn’t work, haha. Aight, I want you to stick in there wit me, stay wit me, it’s gonna be hard to understand and you might think it’s a lil’ too much at first, but jus’ remember what we’re doing this for. Aight, here is the deal… Chris Carter takes out a piece of paper and begins writing… Moments go on as the pen is still scratching along with the paper. Finally, Chris slides the paper over to Violent B and VB begins to read it silently… Violent B’s eyebrows cock up and he gets a grin on his face. Violent B: Ohhh, I like this! Chris “AK” Carter: Keep readin’, B, it gets much better. Violent B continues to read… His face then gets very pale and he slowly looks up at Chris. Chris is grinning and Violent B is still silently shocked. (The End)
