The Bar Fight (RP#3 for Jurisdiction)

19 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12/19/02 [The scene opens up in a dark alley in Detroit, Michigan. It’s nighttime, and it’s dark. The only light is the light from the street lights. You can hear police sirens in the background, screams, gunshots, and the sound of breaking glass. The alley is dark. All of a sudden, a figure begins to walk out from the darkness. That figure is Violent B. He’s wearing a black leather jacket, his spikey necklace, his baggy black jeans, and his boots. He has fingerless leather gloves on his hands. His silver blue eyes glare in the light of the street lights. He’s looking directly into the camera. He then walks by the camera without paying any attention to it. He walks into the middle of the road. A car driving by stops to avoid hitting Violent B. The person in the car begins to honk at Violent B. Violent B just flips him off and continues to walk. He walks across the street. Across the street is a bar called, “The Pit”. Violent B walks into the bar, and the camera follows him. The bar is full of smoke and drunk men. In the back of the bar, men are playing pool and getting drunk. There are men at the bar table, ordering beers. There are also some half naked women trying to get money and give pleasure. Violent B ignores them and walks up to the counter. He walks up to the bartender.] Violent B - “Give me a beer.” [The bartender walks into the back of the bar to grab a beer. He walks back out and gives Violent B the beer. Violent B whips out a $5 bill and gives it to the man. He then cracks the beer open and slams it. He crunches up the can the throws it. The can flies back and hit’s a guy in the head. The guy turns and looks at Violent B, looking pissed off and drunk. He walks up to Violent B and pushes him.] Drunk Man - “You just hit me with a beer can you son of a bitch! What the hell is wrong with you!?” [Violent B, pissed off that the man pushed him, gets in his face.] Violent B - “How can you just come up and push me, you drunk ass piece of shit!?” [The drunk man gets pissed off as well.] Drunk Man - “I’ve had enough of you. I’m gonna beat your ass and throw you outta here!” [The drunk man throws a punch, buy misses. Violent B just shrugs and throws a hard right hand to the face of the drunk man. The guy falls over and lands on another man. The other man gets up and begins to punch him. All of a sudden, a big bar fight breaks out. Everyone in the bar is fighting with each other. Men are picking up bar stools and hitting each other with them, hitting bottles of beer over other’s heads’, and fist fights are breaking out everywhere. Violent B sees that some of the men are charging at him. He punches the first guy in the face. He then grabs the nearest beer bottle and cracks it over someone’s head. The person that owned the bottle then gets in Violent B’s face. Violent B then punches him in the face and throws him out a nearby window. Violent B then grabs a stool and cracks it over some guys back. He then picks up a pool stick and cracks it over someone’s dome. Violent B then grabs the nearest man and throws him out a window as well. Violent B sees a fat man running at him. Violent B charges and spears the man. Violent B then gets sick of the fight. He grabs a stool and throws it into the crowd of fighters. He then grabs another and throws it. He grabs another and throws it. Each stool knocks out a couple of people. Violent B then runs and hit’s a double clothesline on two of the men fighting. He then runs up and spears another man. He then grabs two guys by their heads and cracks their heads together. All the people in the bar are knocked out accept for Violent B and the bartender. Violent B then walks up to the bartender. He pulls some money out of his pocket and gives it to the bartender.] Violent B - “Here’s some money for the fight I started.” [Violent B then walks out of the bar, with everyone knocked out. When he gets out, he notices the UBC camera.] Violent B - “What the hell do you want!? Oh. You wanna talk about the match I have this week at Jurisdiction against MrSynnerster. Yeah. Sudden Violence is on the rise, and me and Chris “AK” Carter have to face him. To tell you the truth, I don’t need Chris to beat MrSynnerster, but hey, it’s an extra bonus. Now, Chris promised that there would be a SEVERE beating this week. I’m not gonna disappoint him. Synnerster, what me and Chris are gonna do to you will be unimaginably painful. I don’t care if you’re some legend here in UBC or not, you’ll still end up getting a beating from me and Chris. We’re gonna make that bar fight I just had in there look like a walk in the park. You can say anything, talk shit, do whatever you want. But you better be prepared for the beating you’re gonna receive at the hands of me and Chris this week at Jurisdiction. Later.” [Violent B then walks off into the darkness, as the scene fades.]
