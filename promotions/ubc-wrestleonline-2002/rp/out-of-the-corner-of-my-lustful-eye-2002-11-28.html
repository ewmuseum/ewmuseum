Out of the Corner of my Lustful Eye

28 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/28/02 Parked outside the theater, staring at the little people cramming themselves inside, Devin stared, lost in thought and wonder all at the same time. The window was open, the slight chill traveling through the air and down his spine. The streetlamps hummed with a rhythm of crickets in an open field. The moon glowed with its ivory smile. Ahead of him, life went on in its consistent motion. Devin realized that he had done this too many times before. He would just sit, watching and admiring all that they were. It wasn’t so much that he wished he could be like them. That wasn’t it at all. If anything, he was glad that he wasn’t like them. They were scum. He wasn’t. He was just… “Nothing.” A sad truth. At least scum had some purpose. He didn’t have any. Devin didn’t really know what it was about seeing them. Maybe he never would. But none the less, he stared. His mind, a passion of words echoing all at once. Appreciating. Respecting. Desiring. Braving. Watching. Waiting. Missing. Craving. I wait for you. Lost in my mind. Crying these tears. I’ll never find. A beautiful blond woman, who Devin imagined was in her early twenties, ran by the front of the station wagon. Devin, his arms crossed across his blue T-shirt, looked over and glanced at her. He saw the brown in her eyes. He could reach out and feel her dirty blond hair blown by the wind. Her smile was enough to reach down into the pit of his heart and make it beat with hope. His soul sprung from his mind and embraced her. His thoughts drifted into that of love. He imagined himself kissing her, and taking her as his own. Making love to her would be an honor beyond words. Feeling the beat of her heart against his being a dream. The glow of her eyes and the smile on her lips a present from God to good to be true. My eyes – observers of my lust. My soul – crying out for savior. My mind – searching for love. My mouth – craving the flavor. And, if only for a moment, her eyes looked over and glanced into him. The eyes of God wouldn’t have made such an impact. He wanted to jump out and hug her. He didn’t dare. The smile on her face turned to one of curiosity. The split second he looked at her, he could feel a sense of desire run through him, and wondered if she felt the same way. Devin concluded that she didn’t. A smile returned to her face as her friend, a brunette at first glance, walked up to her. The two of them, followed in close by two others, walked towards the theater. His heart sank back down, and his body went back to its original position. Well, it did, for the few moments that it could. As she was just about to walk across the street, three men walked up to her and her friends. Devin’s unemotional face turned to that of interest. His hands gripped the steering wheel. His eyes locked onto her back, with the corner of his eyes watching those men. His first thought was that he was her boyfriend. Yeah, that was probably it. But when he grabbed her, and pulled her in as she fought him, he realized that he wasn’t. And when Devin saw him slap her across the face, throwing her to the ground… “Hey you mother fucker!” Devin shot out of the car, and started walking towards him. The tall, muscular, white guy in about his late twenties, smiled sarcastically, and prepared to beat the life out of this weak, defenseless little nobody. Devin’s steps never faltered. He walked with a mission. He walked to save her. He was now the Catcher. Then, all of a sudden, I got in this big mess. – Page 90 of Chapter 13
