'Hope You Like Pain'

11 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12-11-02 Brett layed in bed in a motel room just outside of 'The Pit' at Albuquerque, New Mexico. Brett is still bandaged up from his hard falls to the cold conrete ground, his hands are still taped up from the glass that he went crashing through. Laying on the dresser next to him is a bunch of empty whiskey shot glasses and pill bottles. Brett picks up the pill bottle and turns it upside down as if there were still pills in the bottle. He gets furious and throws the bottle across the room. God Damn, I'm out of my Pain Killers! He sat there in pain, as he starts shivering. He finally gets up and starts pounding on the wall with his fist. Moments later Mike comes running through the door. What is it?!?! Mike stands there in his pajama pants and shirt, with no socks or shoes on. His hair is sticking up in places but it doesn't bother him right at the moment, he continues to ask Brett whats wrong. Damnit tell me what the fuck is up!?!? Brett turns around as he head begins bleeding once again. He looks at Mike and points at him telling him to leave the room. Mike starts walking backwards slowly as he continues to stare into Brett's eyes, he then stops and walks back into the room. Ok now I understand, Here take this! Mike throws Brett another bottle of pills as he turns around and walks off. Brett pops off the lid on the pill bottle and begins dumping the whole bottle of pills into his mouth. He walks over to the dirty shot glasses laying on the dresser and sits two of them up properly, he bends over and grabs a bottle of whiskey from underneith the bed, he twist the lead off the whiskey bottle and begins pouring the shot glasses full. He picks up two of the full shot glasses and takes each one, one by one swallowing the mouth full of pills. He stands there for a minute shaking constanly after few more minutes pass he calms down. The door opens back up and Mike walks through the doors with his cell phone in one hand talking on it and some type of papers in the other. He hands the papers to Brett. What is this? Brett looks down at the papers and begins reading. Mike finishes with his phone call and walks over to Brett pointing to a specific part on the paper. Read that right there. Brett looks down at the papers, when he finish's reading he smiles. So he's the first person on our list. Mike nods his head and walks out of the room. This shouldnt be to hard to take care of, he'll be taken care of in a matter of weeks but first we have two fighters who beleive they can take me and Nightmare down so we must once again prove ourselves to the world and to Brad Douglas and Violent B. 'That we are the Pain in this Game'. Brad thinks he's just going to sit and watch me and Nightmare pound away on on Violent B. Brett laughs. That boy is dumber than he looks. He thinks its original Tag Team match. Well I guess I need to inform him that it isn't an ordinary wrestling tag team match. Which means we just ain't going to let him sit there and watch Violent B get pounded, we're going to pound him into the ground also beating the both of them an inch away from your pathetic little lifes. I hope them two like Pain because I love giving it! Suddenly Mike comes running through the doors once again. Your ride is here so hurry your ass! Brett hurries and slips on his clothes as Mike walks back to his room. Brett looks out the window as he slips on his jeans and we see a Jet Black Limo parked outside waiting on Brett. Guess its time to talk business once again. Brett walks off through his bedroom door and out of site.
