| Life of a Pimp |

31 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12.31.02 Let me take ya'll back to a time where there were no dogs, only cool catz. A time where huge afros, and the juicest of jerry-curls where fashion. A time where if you weren't bumpin' James Brown, or had the moves of Micheal Jackson, you were not black. Boom-boxes with the bass cranked to his fullest, took place along shoulders of passengers, who loved to walk by knowing they were the shit. A time where a Cadillac was only drove by a pimp, and wasn't a Cadillac unless filled with hos or had hydraulics. This era I behold to be the greatest era; 1970s - 1980s. White hippies stinking the elevators with fragrance that poisoned noses with a foul stinch of dirt; it was one of the era's flaws, but nevertheless, that didn't keep us soul brothers down. Oh no my soul brothers, and sistaz. See my father had ran a neighborhood in Atlanta called Piru for quite sometime. It was a Indian turned white turned black which came with ghetto, neighborhood. There were no gangsters. You were either a pimp, ho, whore, or Black Panthers wannabe. But that's another story, let's get into mine and then we'll talk again >|< It was a sunny day outside as the rays of light glistened off my shinny red Cadillac. Rolling on nothing short but twenties that spun even after I stopped, nothing topped my hydraulics as I cruised down the street leaning all to the left while I turned a right corner. I had to patrol my 'hood. Make sure another pimp, wasn't pimping my shit, if ya dig what I'm saying. I took a glance at my small gold stop watch with diamonds planted in it's crust at which was linked by a chain to my fly dress pants; it was 7:48pm. Afternoon time, almost about time to colect my debt. By this time, my hos where only waking up and beginning to get ready. So I thought I'd stop at a friends house, it wasn't my best choice. >|< 'What is up my soul cat' Raising my fist to unify power to my friends. 'Yo Red, we got sum hot new shit we want you to sell on ya block.' Freddy, one of my main men, insisted on this new drug. 'Now I don't want to effect my people, I want to educate and progress my people. No new shit hits my block with out me knowing it won't kill them off.' I made sure my people would be protected. 'It's a new drug all the other pimps just got they hands on. I had it was making those niggaz across the sea bug out when they hit it. It's called coke.' He reached into he plaid dress coat pocket with the silk inner layer and pulled out a small bag containing it's poison. 'I'm not so sure about that. I don't need my people turnin' on me in shit. You try it out, and then we'll see what comes in store.' Seating back and crossing my legs before me. 'Well...' Hesitating, 'Since this is your block and you want to know what it does, why don't you try it out?' >|< Freddy poured a line of coke across the glass table that finished off his living room. >|< I looked at the line of coke as Freddy's boys handed me a straw. 'I got a better idea, why don't you try it.' I wasn't that stupid to put myself in that situation. 'What? So now you don't trust me? Look Red, when your dad died, it was me who was there for you! No one else!' Sweating faced, 'And that's how it's gonna be? After all these years, you don't trust me?' Freddy pulled out a cloth, padded his face in several places, then stuffes in back in his outer coat pocket. 'Fred. I don't know what you trying to pull on me but this pimp ain't having it. I've been in the game to long Fred, why you two timing me like this?!' >|< For a couple of minutes, there was a pause between Freddy and I, a pause that only was heard before battle. >|< '...I hoped it didn't have to come down to this. See, this is the reason I had to kill your father! He didn't know how to run shit! When it came down to the big one, he choked like a ho on a fat dick!' Smirking towards the end. 'You know it, I know it, hell your own people know it. It's time for you to step down.' 'YOU SON OF A BITCH!' Quickly showing them my pimp cane was of no ordinary cane, I pushed a button on the side and out popped the top, replaced with a blade. 'I'MA CUT YO BITCH ASS UP!' Slicing him across the jaw, he fell to the floor bleeding as his crew pulled away AKs and began spraying. >|< I drove for cover behind a couch, before noticing my light of savor, and diving out the window. >|< 'No! Let his ass go. We got bigger shit in store.' Giving pause to his crew. >|< Only if they knew... I was on to them before they even knew how to talk. I am the pimp of this shit, and if they thought for one second they could foul a pimp... heh, they already fouled themselves. BOOM! The place was bugged from the get-go. Police showed up on the scene sometime later, but no trace. - My red Cadillac pulled up to a corner around 9:00pm. >|< My black tented window crept down stealth like to an approaching ho, 'You got my money bitch?' A true pimp wouldn't let the day end without an collection.
