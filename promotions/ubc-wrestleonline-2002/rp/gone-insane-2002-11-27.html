'Gone Insane'

27 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11-27-02 Brett is seen walking down the streets of Memphis Tennessee with his brother Mike following. Brett had alot of things on his mind at the time, he had his debute match coming up and he didnt know what to do. Mike felt there was something wrong with Brett so he walked up behind him and tapped him on the shoulder. 'Whats wrong' asked Mike as Brett continued walking. It had look like Brett was ignoring Mike but he really didn't hear a word he said. He was thinking about the match on Jurisdiction. Mike starts getting frustrated as Brett continues to walk and not say a word. 'I'm nervous Mike' Brett says still with his head down as he continues to walk, not looking where he is going. 'Nervous about what' Mike seemed confused, Brett had been so confident thathe couldn't be beat and now he says that. 'My match, I know I've talked alot of shit lately but when It comes right down to it, I don't know how I will do. I'll just forget everything I've been practicing. It always happens, I don't know why but it just does. My mind just goes blank and I'm standing there all alone it seems' Brett seemed like he was scared. he finally stops and he turns and looks at the little girl standing beside him and continues walking. Mike didn't know what to say so he had to think of something up quick so he could cheer up Brett, something pops into Mike's head and he starts speaking 'Thats just it, your afraid of being alone. Don't worry about a thing. You will do just fine trust me, your my brother I've known you for a long time so I believe I would know' Brett stops and turns around and faces Mike, staring him into the eyes 'Your Right! Why am I so damn nervous, its only Bless and NightMare. Mike says in his mind 'Oh man, he is going to choke in that ring. I know it. Damnit I though I trained him better than that. I don't know what else to say or teach him, is there anything else? Hell who knows! I have to do something though or he will be put to shame. He has great talent and he is a huge guy, he has all the right tools but I need to get him to use them the right way but HOW?!?! What are you thinking about bro? Mike looks up at him quickly as he makes a quick reply 'Nothing...Nothing at all' Mike knew he was in trouble. 'You hungry' Brett says to Mike as Mike seems disopointed at himself. 'Yea sure, what do you want to eat?' says Mike. Brett thinks to himself for a moment 'Umm...how about that steak house down the road' 'Ok..lets go' Mike says as they both take off walking down the street, few minutes later they arrive at the steak house. You can smell the food from outside the building making you even hungier. 'I love the smell of steaks' Brett raises his head to smell the food. 'QUIT THAT! You look like a big idiot doing that, I'm sure you have some fans in here, you dont want to make a fool out of yourself' Mike says as his face turns red. A female waitress walks up to the Brett and Mike and ask 'Smoking or Non' Mike and Brett both shout at the same time 'Non' 'Follow Me' says the 5'5 about 125 lbs waitress,who has one of those bubble butts. They follow the young good looking waitress to there steats, as they sit down the waitress says 'Here's your menue' she hands them a menue of there food and walks away. 'How rude, she didnt even tell us her name' Mike says look down at his menue. 'I'm not hungry now' says Brett looking up at Mike. 'What the hell! Five minutes ago you was, what is it now. Is it the match again?' shouts Mike. Brett picks up his fork and looks down at it and stares, not saying a word. The waitress walks back and ask 'You two boys ready to order' the waitress notice that Brett wasnt the same as he first walked in. She turns around and looks at Mike 'Your order please' Mike not catching on to the young waitress but finally realizes what she had said 'Oh yea! I would like two t-bone steak, mash potatoes and a side order of fries to go with that' Mike looks over at Brett 'What do you want?' Mike ask kind of feeling sorry for Brett now. 'I just want a cheeseburger and some water' Brett says still looking down at that fork as he twirls it around slowly. 'Damnit Brett, get ahold of yourself! You are the Best there is, thats a fact. When you get in that ring the only thing that needs to be on your mind is winning and if you think of winning then you will win' Mike just made up something in his head again trying to cheer Brett up. Brett looks up at Mike and smiles, still not saying a word all he does is smile, Mike is confused once again not knowing what the hell is going on in Brett's head. Mike sits there and thinks to himself 'He's going Insane, One minute he is down then the next he smiles. What the hell is going on with him.' Brett sits there still smiling, the Waitress walks back with there food 'Here you go' She says as she hands Mike and Brett there food. The young waitress looks down at Brett, Brett see hers in the corner of his eyes and he looks up at her and just smiles. 'Can I ask you one question pretty lady' Brett says with that same smile on his face. 'What is it?' says the waitress. 'What is your name' Brett kind of scares the young women as she takes a step back, Virus then grabs her army and yanks her back 'I ASKED WHAT YOUR NAME WAS' the women scared to death right about now, Mike doesn't know what to do, he is kind of terrified of Brett right at the moment but Mike can't seem to let the young women be scared to death so he stands up and pulls her back whispering something into her ear 'Make up a name and give to him' Mike lets go of her as she tells Brett her name 'Tonya' She says studdering. 'Tonya I'm Brett Nice to meet you' he says looking up at her then turns his head and looks at Mike and just smiles. 'You Simply Insane Brett you need help you know that' Mike speaking before thinking he than realizes what he just said. 'I know I need help but there is no one out there big enough to help me' Mike scratch's his head wondering what that comment just meant. He then sits back down and starts eating. 'I thought you came here to eat steak' Mike says just trying to make conversation. 'Thats what I fucking ordered but the dumb bitch brought the wrong one out' Brett stands up angry he starts walking up to the women but Mike gets in front of him. 'What the Hell is your problem' Mike starts getting angry. Brett looks out him and smiles 'I don't have a problem little brother' 'Wha?!?!Little Im your big brother damnit not your little' Mike starts shouting at Brett. 'Well look at me and look at you, I beleive I am bigger than you, so that makes you my little brother' Brett seems to be calm now as he mind fades off the food. 'Lets just get out of here and go home damnit, your scaring everyone' Mike pushes Brett out of the Steak House. 'Lets go home' says Mike. Brett sits there not hearing what Mike said and out of no where Brett screams something 'I'm Simply Insane' Everyone turns and looks out him like he is a dumbass, Mike's face turns red again. 'Damnit I'm leaving without you' Mike takes off walking and eventually Brett follows, Brett finally catch's up with Mike. 'Ok I beleive I am ready now' says Brett. 'Ready for what?' Mike ask confused once again. 'Ready for that damn match, Bless and Nightmare has nothing on 'Simply Insane' Brett Wayne' Mike looks up at Brett and nods. 'You have lost it, Maybe this will change the way you feel during a fight or atleast lets hope' Mike and Brett continue to talk each other until they finally get out of site. Fade 2 Black
