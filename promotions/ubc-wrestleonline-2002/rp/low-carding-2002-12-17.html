Low Carding??

17 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12/17/02 [Fade In] [A gentle breeze blows across the streets of Philadelphia, tussling up old newspapers and other pieces of trash. His footsteps create an even rhythm as he walks casually down Main Street.] [Eventually he turns the corner into a tatoo parlor, called simply, 'Eds.' Inside is rather run down looking, with a couple of chairs, with tools resting on tables next to the chairs. A large man with tatoos covering his body and a black goatee, assumedly Ed, appears from the back upon hearing the doorbell.] 'Heeey Mike! Good to see you again my friend, what can I do for ya??' 'Need a Tat Ed.' 'No kidding, I thought you wanted a burger and fries.' 'Heh, good to see you again too.' [He shakes Ed's hand and takes a seat in the chair that Ed motioned him to. Ed sits on his stool, and picks up his tools, prepping them for action.] 'What'll it be?' 'Opposite shoulder as last time, with a flaming switchblade, pointing down my shoulder.' 'Hmmm, why?' 'My nickname? Switchblade?' 'Ah...right...heh, forgot about that. How's the biz goin' these days??' 'Not to bad, I'm movin' up in NGWA pretty good, got me and Chris a World Tag Shot in the alliance comin' up.' 'Niiice, against?' 'The Birdz' 'Ah, good team. Anything else??' 'In a fight club again.' 'Nice! Just like the old days eh??' 'Yea, I missed the good stuff. No rules, all out fighting.' 'What's the place called?' 'Underground Brawlers Club. UBC for short. Good fighters, I lost my first fight.' 'Against who?? You never lose!' 'Goes to show nobodies unbeatable. Devin Shadow was the guy. Heckuva fighter.' 'I'll hafta come watch one of these events.' 'Yea, that'd be cool.' [Ed starts up with his needle, carefully outlining the tatoo] 'What about this week? Who'd you fight??' 'I had this week off, didn't hafta fight. I fight this coming week. Shane Matthews or something.' 'Shane who??' 'Yea, exactly.' 'Heh, that bad eh??' 'Well, to tell you the truth, I have no idea who he is. He could be another Devin Shadow, or he could flop out like a Chris Carter. But then again, Carter has backup...so maybe Shane has backup...who knows.' 'Carter's a pussy...he's still in the game?' 'Yep, he is. Still 'ghettoing up' the place.' 'Ugh, dude makes me wanna hurl.' 'It's like Eminem....part two.' 'Hahaha, seriously.' [Finishing off the outline, he grabs a color cartridge, and goes to work filling in the tat.] 'Almost done...this'll be an easy one. Not bad though.' 'That's why I come to you Ed...you're the best.' 'Aw, I think I'm gonna tear up here...' 'Shut up...' 'Heh, k.' [About 15 minutes later...] 'You know the drill, let it sit, wipe it down..yada yada.' 'Yep, thanks Ed.' 'Good luck this week kid...I'll be rootin' for ya.' 'Thanks.' [And out he goes...]
