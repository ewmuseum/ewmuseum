---I Depend On No One---

9 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12-09-02 The sound of water leaking from pipes is heard hitting the cold wet concrete floor. Its very cold, so cold you can see your breath when you breathe. The rats are even to cold to move from one spot to another but one man isn't. The man who took a brutal beating three days ago at Jurisdiction. The man who was slammed on the conrete a numerous amount of times the man who knocked out Johnny Nightmare. This man is no other than The Evil One. His red eyes glow into the camera as he breaths the camera fogs up on revealing Evi'ls glowing red eyes. I feel none of this, I feel nothing. These scars I just recieved three days ago are nothing. Everyone may think I took a beating just because I was pounded into the ground but mentally I wasn't hurting. I was loving every single minute of that beating. There were only for parts of that fight that I remember. One was Johnny Nightmare hitting me with a steel chair over the back of my head. I also remember picking up the huge monster Brett Wayne and throwing im onto the hard conrete floor but all that doesn't matter. It will soon be another forgotten memory just like most victories are forgotten after a short period of time. What matters is the Future, its time to quit dwelling on the past and focus on the future. Its time to start a new life and I might as well start it in the UBC. I have an upcoming match with Devin Shadow, a man I know nothing about but I garauntee it will not be an easy fight for shadow just like it wasn't easy for Brett and Nightmare who now are teamed up with one another. Why do they need each other if they believe to be the best, just to make things easier I guess but how will you learn in life it things are always to easy? It seems everyone deponds on someone else but why? You should depond on you and you alone, when your in the Fight Zone its just you and your opponent. Your body gaurd won't be able to help you there expecially when your in the Fight Zone with me. That goes for everyone of you fighters out there listening. I'm always waiting, I'm always ready, no matter what kind of shape I'm in I'll be there. So Devin Shadow prepare to meet your you destiny... ME! The camera continues to fog up as Evil Breaths until his red glowing eyes cannot been seen anymore.
