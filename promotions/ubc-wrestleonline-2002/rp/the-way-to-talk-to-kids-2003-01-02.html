The Way To Talk To Kids

2 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 01.02.03 (The scene opens up in a local elementary school called, North Park Elementary. The school is located in the outskirts of Minneapolis, MN. North Park school officials called Krisis the other day to have him come in and talk to the little kids about how drugs are bad and that they should never use and abuse them. Krisis agreed to it and the day came for Krisis to come talk to the kids. Krisis pulls up in the school's parking lot and gets out. He walks into the school and walks to the office.) Krisis - Hey, I'm here to talk to the little kids. Secretary - Ah yes. You're supposed to go to room 109 and the teacher's name is Mr. Mars. Krisis - Thank you, you have a good day. Secretary - Good day. (Krisis walks out of the office and then heads down the hall to room 109. He knocks on the door and Mr. Mars opens the door. Krisis walks in, looking at all the 25 little 5th graders sitting in their seat. Krisis takes a few steps in and then stands infront of the whole class, with all eyes on him.) Mr. Mars - Class, this is Krisis. Some of you may know him from the wonderful world of professional wrestling. Krisis - That's right, I'm Krisis. (Krisis turns around and writes 'Mr. Krisis' on the chalk board. Someone knocks on the door and then Mr. Mars answers it. He steps out and someone tells him that there is an emergency down stairs. He tells Krisis to watch the class and talk to them. Mr. Mars shuts the door and then Krisis begins to talk to the kids.) Krisis - Alright, Mr. Mars had an emergency and he'll be back in a few. Now, let's get to the point why I came here today. I came here to talk to you about drugs. Do any of you know what drugs are? (A few kids raise their hands.) Krisis - Umm... how about you. (Points to Jonny.) Jonny - Are they candy? Krisis - In some ways, yeah. Krisis - (Points to Billy.) You? Billy - Are they toys you can play with? Krisis - They're not toys, but you can play with them. They're actually pretty fun to play with. You can smoke them, stick them, sniff them, you can do practically everything with them. (The class giggles a little bit.) Krisis - So what I'm trying to tell you all, DO DRUGS! When you're about my age, you'll understand. (The class starts to laugh.) Krisis - Do you guys know any jokes? (Mike raises his hand.) Krisis - Ah, you got a joke? Come on up here and tell it to me and the whole class. (Mike gets up and walks to the front of the class.) Mike - Knock, knock. Krisis & the class - Who's there? Mike - I don't know? Krisis & the class - I don't know who? Mike - I don't know, how the fuck am I supposed to know!? (Krisis and the class burst out in a loud laughter.) Krisis - That's a funny one, I'll have to remember that! Go sit down and listen up everyone. Did you guys do the pledge of allegiance to the flag yet? The Class - No. Krisis - Well, shit. Stand up and place your right hand over your heart and look at the flag, and repeat after me. I pledge allegiance to the flag. The Class - I pedge allegiance to the flag. Krisis - Of the United States of America. The Class - Of the United States of America. Krisis - And to all the Krisis-head across the nation. They'll repect me and cherish me will all the will power that they got and if they don't, they will die. (The class is speechless for a few seconds and then they begin to repeat what Krisis just said.) The Class - And to all the Krisis-head across the nation. They'll repect me and cherish me will all the will power that they got and if they don't, they will die. Krisis - Alright, sit down. Now onto the next topic. Remember this, the system was designed to bring people, like me, down. Don't EVER forget that, okay? And don't ever forget to do drugs! The Class - Okay. (Mr. Mars walks in the classroom.) Krisis - Back so soon, ay? Mr. Mars - Yeah, the emergency is all taken care of. Krisis - Okay, I'd like to thank you for letting me come here to talk to all these wonderful kids. Mr. Mars - No problem, anytime. Krisis - Alright, I think my job here is done. What did I tell all of you to remember? The Class - The system was designed to bring people like you down, and do drugs! Krisis - That's right, all of you guys have a good day now. Peace. (Krisis walks out of the classroom and walks down the hall. He exits the building and gets in his 2003 Black Hummer and speeds off as the scene fades to black.) ---- Back
