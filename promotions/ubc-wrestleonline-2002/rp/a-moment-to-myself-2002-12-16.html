A Moment To Myself

16 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12-16-02 I just sat there motionless in the locker room after my fight with Violent B and Brad Douglas. How could this have happened I asked myself as I continued to think about the fight. The locker room was smelly from the sweat floating swiftly through the air. The floors were drenched in sweat as Brett was kneeled over with his elbows on his knees with his head down as he continued to think to himself. The walls had a few spots of dried up blood splattered on them from previous fighters who bleed from the hellacious fights. The lockers were rusted over and dusty, you can tell that many fighters have been in this locker room just from the looks of but Brett doesn't seem to be affected by the looks and smells of the room. Brett gets up out of his chair and nods his head as he dodges the dried up blood on the floor as he walks to the shower area. Damn them, they will pay. Violent B and Brad Douglas will pay for what they done here tonight, they've embarrassed me in front of alot of UBC Fans, they tied me up and kicked the shit our of Johnny Nightmare, it isnt his fought we lost. Its my fault for letting the two bastards tie me up, its my fought because we lost. I would just walk right up to Violent B and beat him senseless but come to find out he is in Sudden Violence which makes matters a bit more complicated now. Brett slowly makes his way to the shower as he pulls the dirty shower curtian to the side, he drops his pants that is covered in sweat, he bends over and twist the nob and turns the shower on, once the water slowly falls from the shower fossit steam slowly flows smelly air, it covers the mirrors with dampness and sensors out Bretts privates as he jumps into the shower. And not only do I have to deal with Sudden Violence but I have Tommy Grady next week on Jurisdiction, that isn't going to be easy but I'll get the job done. I'm promising myself right now I'll get the job done, None of them have seen my true potential just yet but Tommy Grady will be the first to see it. I haven't gave it 100% in the Fight Zone yet, I haven't even came close to giving it 100%, only because I thought I couldnt be beat but I learned from two fighters who I could have easly defeated that anything can happen in the UBC. The tides can turn in a matter of minutes, you can be knocked out in a matter of seconds, and you can lose in an instant...Thats how it is and thats how it will always be no matter what anyone else thinks thats just the way it is. Brett places his hands on the wall as the warm water slowly falls from the shower fossit onto his head causing his yellow afro to fade black. He sits there for a minute enjoying the warm water running down his body, he then grabs a bar of soap and starts rubbing himself clean of all the dirt and sweat on his big bulky body. He finishes with the soap and rinses his body off he then reach's down and turns the knob turning the water off as he steps out you hear the sound of water draining down the drain. He grabs a towl from the rack next to the shower and dries himself off, he wraps the towel around him and walks back to his locker as he dodges the blood spots on the floor. He then opens his locker slowly and grabs a pair of boxers and his solid white pants, he slips the both of them on and grabs a white tank top and slips it on. He sits down in his chair and relaxes for awhile before putting on his shoes. When he finish's he goes back to putting his elbows on his knees with his head leaned over as he continues thinking to himself. Moments later the door quickly opens as Mike runs through the door... Hey Bro, you ready yet?!?! Mike says before he notices the look on Brett's face, he frowns and quickly comes up with something else to say. Hey if your Ashamed about your lose tonight against Violent B and Brad Douglas, don't be. You have nothing to be ashamed about, everyone wins and loses its all apart of the game. Make up for this week by going and beating the shit out of Grady next week. Take your anger from this week out on Grady next week, use all your emotions on Grady next week and prove to everyone that your the best there is in the UBC. Brett looks up at Mike a smirks, he then slowly stands up and grabs his bag out of the locker and puts the strap on his shoulder and walks past Mike as he smiles. Mike stands there and laughs to himself... Everyone is going to be a bit suprised next week on Jurisdiction, I gaurentee it! Mike smiles as he flips off the light switch making the dirty locker room pitch black, Mike then walks out closing the door behind him causing the scene to fade...
