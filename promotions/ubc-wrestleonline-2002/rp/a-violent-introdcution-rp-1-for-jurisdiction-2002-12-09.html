A Violent Introdcution (RP#1 for Jurisdiction)

9 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12/9/02 [The scene opens up in a big underground fighting arena in Detroit, Michigan. There are some very big, strong looking guys practicing and sparring in the ring. The two men in the ring are practicing what looks like kickboxing in the ring. One of the men hits a spin kick on the other and knocks him to the mat. The man falls over and looks like he’s out. The other fighters in the arena cheer and laugh at the fallen man. Two of the other fighters grab the man and drag him out of the arena. The man in the ring raises his hands in victory.] Winning Fighter - “I’M THE MAN! YEAH!” [The man then begins to dance around the ring, air boxing and acting like the king of the world. He looks into the crowd.] Fighter - “Next victim.” [Another fighter steps into the ring. He begins to warm up. The fighter that was already in the ring starts to laugh at him. The fighter that just stepped into the ring doesn’t look to happy. He then charges at the first fighter, and runs into a hard punch to the face. The first fighter then begins to hit some kicking combos to the sides of the other fighter. The first fighter and hits a roundhouse kick on the other fighter. The man falls to the mat and looks to be unconscious. The fighter once again begins to gloat in victory.] Fighter - “Come on! This is weak! Where’s the competition!?” [The doors to the arena then get thrown open, and a figure walks in. He has a black trench coat on. The doors slam behind him.] Figure - “So you think that you’re something because you can beat these pathetic excuses for fighters? You can’t say that you’re anything until you beat me in a fight.” [The fighter in the ring then begins to laugh. He gets a cocky grin on his face.] Fighter - “HAHAHAHAHAHA!! You think you can beat me! This I gotta see!” [The figure then begins to walk to the ring. When he gets there, he grabs the bottom of his coat and pulls it off. He throws the coat on the ground. He’s wearing a black t-shirt, a pair of baggy jeans, some black boots, and a spikey necklace. He also has his fists wrapped with tape. He pulls off his shirt to reveal a chiseled physique. He has a tattoo of a skull on fire with a sword through it and snakes coming out of it’s eyes on his left bicep, and he’s “ripped”. He takes off his spikey necklace. He’s about 6’4”. He has spikey dark hair, silver blue eyes, and a goatee on his chin. He looks up at the fighter with a smile on his face. The fighter looks at him as well.] Fighter - “So hot shot, you gotta name?” [The figure replies.] Figure - “The name’s Ben Danielson, but you can call me Violent B.” [The fighter in the ring begins to laugh.] Fighter - “Violent B huh? What kinda name is that!? You sound like some wannabe that thinks he’s violent.” Violent B - “So you think I’m a wannabe. Well, you’ll see why they call me Violent B when I get in the ring and show you the violence that gave me the name.” [Violent B then slides under the bottom rope and gets into the ring. He stands up and looks into the eyes of the fighter. The fighter, with that cocky smile on his face, just laughs in Violent B’s face.] Fighter - “So are you gonna attack me or what?” [Violent B puts a smile on his face.] Violent B - “Actually I think I’ll let you attack first. I don’t wanna end this fight too early.” Fighter - “Okay, whatever you say!” [The fighter then charges at Violent B. He swings a punch at Violent B. Violent B ducks his head and dodges the punch.] Fighter - “So, you think you’re good because you’re fast enough to dodges that punch. I’ll show you what it’s like to fight me.” [The fighter then charges at Violent B once again. He throws random punches, but Violent B dodges all of them. The fighter tries to back Violent B into the corner, but Violent B ducks under the fighter and gets back into the middle of the ring.] Fighter - “Come on [In mocking tone.] VIOLENT B!” [The fighter then runs up to Violent B and hits a hard punch to the face of Violent B. Violent B turns his head and the guys starts to laugh. Violent B then whips his head around and has a pissed off look on his face. He stares into the eyes of the fighter. Violent B then wipes blood from his lip. The fighter gets wide eyed at the look on Violent B’s face. He looks into Violent B’s eyes. Violent B notices that the fighter has a scared look in his eyes.] Fighter - “How did you take that punch!?” [Violent B then charges the guy. He hits him with a tackle and takes him down to the mat. He then begins to violently punch the man in the face. He hits rights and lefts and rights and lefts, and beats the man until his face is bloody and he has bruises all over his face. The guy spits out some teeth and some blood. Violent B then sees that he’s still conscious. Violent B then cocks back his right hand and hits a hard punch to the face. The guy falls over unconscious. Violent B stands up and spits some blood out of his mouth. He wipes the blood off his lip and steps out of the ring. All the fighters in the arena are shocked. The best fighter in the arena was just beaten severely by Violent B. All the men clear out of his way as he walks over to his coat. He grabs his shirt, puts it on, puts on his spikey necklace, and puts on his trench coat. Violent B then walks towards the door. He opens up the doors. Before he leaves, he looks back at all the fighters, who are still shocked at what they saw.] Violent B - “I told him that I’d show him why they call me Violent B. Now he knows, and all of you know. I think I’ve proven my point.” [Violent B then walks out of the arena and the doors slam. The scene fades.]
