A Night At The Movies!

1 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12/1/02 (The scene opens up in the lobby of a local moive threater in Mineapolis, MN. Krisis is seen talking to some girls that are standing in line at the consession to get some pop and popcorn.) Krisis - So ladies, what movie you guys gonna see? Girls - Triple X... Krisis - Ah, that's cool. I'm seeing that one too. I heard it's pretty good... Girls - Yup... (In dis-interest) Krisis - So, you would pretty ladies mind if I sat by you guys? Girls - Ummm... no. Krisis - Well why not? Girls - Because our boyfriends are sitting by us... (Rolls eyes) (Their boyfriends come from behind the corner and walk up to the girls) Boyfriends - Hey baby, who's this? Krisis - (Talks very fast) They call me Krisis, bitch! Girls - Oh we don't know, he's some loser that was hitting on us. Boyfriend - Hey, no one hits on my girlfriend! Who do you think you are!? Krisis - Well some people call me Krisis, bitch. If you don't mind, I'd like to be called Krisis... bitch. Boyfriend - Did you just call me a bitch or my girlfriend? Krisis - I called all you bitches a bitch. Here, all of you stand right here. (Krisis moves them into position as he climbs on top of the counter and does a 450 splash onto the two couples.) Krisis - (Looking down at the couples) Yeah, what now bitches! That's why I'm called Krisis, bitch! (Krisis steps over the couples and walks up to the counter. He tells the clerk what he wants to eat and drink for the movie he's about to see. He then proceeds to the theater where the movie is being shown. Krisis hands over his ticket to the ticket taker at the door and walks in. He finds a seat in the packed theater and sits down and awaits for the movie to start. The previews are being shown as of right now.) Krisis - What the fuck, when the hell is this damn movie gonna start!? (The woman sitting behind Krisis, along with her two young kids scolds Krisis) Woman - Hey, watch your mouth! I've got two kids behind you that heard what you said! Krisis - Well I'm ssssoooorrrryyyyy! I didn't mean to offend you, I'm just getting very impatient! Woman - Yeah you better be! Krisis - (Screaming in her face) AHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHH! (The woman just shuts her mouth and leans back to her seat. The previews end and the movie starts. About a 1/3 of the movie passes and Krisis is almost falling asleep. Suddenly a loud explosion from the movie rocked the theater and woke up Krisis. Krisis sits up in his seat and starts to eat popcorn really fast as he's wide awake and into the movie. Suddenly one of the girls gets hurt in the movie.) Krisis - (Yells loudly) Ah, that's fuckin' bullshit! People in theater - SSSSHHHHHHH!!!! Krisis - Hey, this isn't everyone versus Krisis! People in theater - Who cares! (Krisis's jaw drops in amazment.) Krisis - I thought you guys liked me! People in theater - Ummm... we don't even know who you are!? Krisis - Oh my god, how can you not! They call me Krisis, bitches! People in theater - Why would they call you Krisi--- Krisis - Zip! People in theater - Let us tal--- Krisis - Ssshhh! People in theater - Hey, sto--- Krisis - BLAH! (Krisis continues to interrupt the people as they try to talk. The people get pissed off and just stop talking. Krisis smiles and starts to watch the movie again. As the movie progresses, the main character (Vin Diesel) gets hurt in a scene. Krisis starts to cuss and throw popcorn at the screen as the people start to get annoyed but they don't say anything because it will make matters worst. As Krisis is cussing and throwing popcorn all over the place, the two couples walk into the theater finally after being assulted by Krisis.) Krisis - Well hey, look who droped in! (The couples walk by looking at Krisis with a look of dis-like on their faces. Krisis just smiles and continues to watch the movie.) Krisis - Hahahahaha.... (Laughing at the couples.) (The movie ends and the credits roll as the lights come on. Krisis stands up along with the other people in the theater. Krisis stands by the door and prevents the people from walking out.) Krisis - Everyone, If I could get your attention please! (Everyone looks at Krisis.) Krisis - Before you leave, you'll have to pay a small fee of five dollars to me for you people to walk out here. If you don't, I'm gonna have to do something about that. (Some people whip out their wallets and pay Krisis five dollars. Once the two couples come up to Krisis, they don't pay) Couples - We ain't paying you anything! Krisis - Uh oh... not a good thing to do. (Krisis runs back into the theater where the seats are and climbs to the top and and jumps off and does a 450 splash into a five star frog splash into a shooting star press onto the two couples.) Krisis - That's what happens to people who don't pay, and that's why people call me Krisis, bitches! (The everyone then pays Krisis and then the two couples pay Krisis and then walk out. Krisis collects the money and then walks out of the movie theater as the scene fades to black.)
