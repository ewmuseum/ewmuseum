Cursed P.t 2

10 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12/10/02 Brad Douglas was laying on his large bed, holding an icepack on his forehead. His duffle bag was packed beside him, and he was just getting back from last weeks fight, when the room stopped spinning.It was rumor that the medics were taking Brad out to the ambulence, but he refused, and even assaulted one of them. Now he could barely remember what happend. 'How am I ever gonna win the Ultimate Brawler Championship if I can barely handle Mr.Synnerster? Do I even belong in this company?' Brad looked around the room as much as he could without moveing his head. 'Oliver,Where is my drink?' Oliver was Brad's High school girlfriend,Shocking that he could actully hold on to something that long.She walked into the room with a bottle of miller mgd beer.Alcohol was something Brad stuggled with ever since he broke into Wrestling years ago. 'Thanks hun,Hey can you fluff my pillow for me' 'Brad,It's not enuff i get you your beer and wait on you hand and foot.But now you want me to fluff your pillow.No that's a little to much there bucko,Fluff your own damn pillow.' Oliver walks out of the room as Brad lie on the bed without moveing a inch. 'Oh well,Im just fine laying here watching televison,Drinking my beer,and laying on this broken down pillow!.Ohwell that's what i get for letting Mr.Synnerster Break my head open like this.You know what happend the last time some one did a number like this on to me? I took them to court and took all there money.But i was young and dumb then,This time around im just going to take it all out of Nightmare and Brett wayne.And if he gets into my way i'll even knock out Violet J,Or whatever he calls himself this week. I wonder how long i can do this,It's been this way ever since i started wrestling,Now it's comeing with me over into Fighting.I never pay attention to whom im fighting and i trash talk them every chance i get.Well this time around i think im going to take a different approach to this fight.Since Violent J is so hardcore,Im going to let him fight both men,Leaveing Wayne and Nightmare tired,Then i will come in and BOOM then they start to plumit into a dark Dsend.Yes your all hearing me right,Im telling everyone my game plan for this match.And that's letting Wayne and Nightmare wear themselfs out by beating Violent J within a inch of his life.Then Boom i come in and clean house and pick up the win.Sounds like one hell of a plan to me,Shall be a great one at that' Brad flips the television off and looks over at the camera once again. 'What do you all expect me to show you how i sleep also? Yeah so get the hell out of my house.' Fin.
