Let me Introduce myself

3 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12/3/02 The scene opens with a shot of the living room, in the river front condo of William and Rosalind Thornbush known to the wrestling world as MrSynnerster and Mistress Rose. As the camera pans around it reveals everything from the black and gold nevo contempo lamp to the German created bookcases with various medical and literary books on each shelf. The camera continues panning around until finally settling on a black leather couch where two people sit reading various pieces of paper. In front of the couch is a glass coffee table with several more pieces of paper and envelopes scattered about. One person, William, a tall muscular dark skinned African American man with long black dreadlocks, is sitting with his right leg draped over the armrest of the couch, body stretched out and resting on his left elbow. He is dressed in a garnet colored University of South Carolina sweatshirt, blue jean cargo shorts, white socks and black sneakers. Around his neck hangs a silver necklace with a Celtic cross charm. On the ring finger of his right hand sits a silver ring with funny writing, and on that same finger on his left hand sits his white gold wedding band. Sitting beside him Indian style is Rosalind; a light skinned African American woman with short black braided hair. She is wearing a gray Converse College sweatshirt and black gym shorts. Her body is adored with a silver chain bracelet around her ankle, a silver necklace with a simple silver cross charm, a silver hoop navel belly ring hidden under her shirt, and a white gold wedding ring and band on her left hand ring finger. Her fingernails and toenails are painted dark red, almost the same color as the USC shirt. Both are reading various pieces of paper and pointing out things to each other. William looks up from the letter he is reading towards the camera. William: “Oh hello. How are you doing? Mine name is William Thornbush. But y’all will soon know me as MrSynnerster. And this lovely lady beside me is my wife slash valet slash . . .” Rosalind lifts her head up from the paper she is reading, turns towards William, cocks an eyebrow and says . . . Rosalind: “Excuse me?” With a surprise look on his face William turns his attention away from the camera towards Rosalind and replies . . . William: “What?” Rosalind: “Valet? You make me sound some fluffy piece. Some ring rat. Some glorified wrestling hoochie.” William: “What are you doing?” Rosalind: “I’m just informing the people of who I am, and sharing some witty back and forth banter with you.” William: “Yeah, but you’re interrupting the flow of the promo. You can’t do that yet. We need to let everyone know who we are first, before we can do the witty banter thing.” Rosalind: “Why. This is who we are. We are the witty banter people. We are the people that thumb our noses at the rules and do things our way. We are the most entertaining couple in sports entertainment today. We are the best there is, the best there was, the best there ever will . . . . Opps, sorry. I got off on a tangent. Please continue on with your little promo. I’ll just play the Chyna to your Triple H from the DX days.” William: “You can’t do that either.” Rosalind: “Do what?” William: “You can’t mention wrestlers from other organization. See it’s right here.” William grabs on of the pieces of paper off of the glass coffee table and points to something written on it. Rosalind reads what’s on the piece of paper. Then giving a look like a child just caught doing something she is not suppose to be doing, Rosalind looks at the camera and says . . . Rosalind: “My bad.” (Turns her attention back to William and says . . .) “Maybe I do need to shut up before I get you into any more trouble. Go ahead with your promo, just don’t call me a Valet.” William: “I’m sorry then. How about this. (Turns his head back towards the camera.) This is my wife slash partner in crime Rosalind Thornbush. (Turns back to Rosalind.) Is that better?” Rosalind: “Much better.” William: (To Rosalind) “Thank you.” (Turns his attention back to the camera.) “As I was saying. I am William Thornbush, and this is Rosalind Thornbush. But to the wrestling world we are MrSynnerster and Mistress Rose. Now I could go into all my old accolades and what now, but really what’s the point. I mean back when I was the World Champion in the CWA, I use to hate for new guys to come in and talk about all the things they used to do, but never really amount too much in the new fed. So I’m here to let y’all folks in the UBC know that I will not brag about being the first ACW television champion. You won’t hear me brag about being one half of the GWO tag team champions. You won’t hear me brag about being the current NEW Regional Champion. Y’all will not even hear me brag about being the longest and most successful World Champion in the CWA. Nope. I’m not going to do it. All that matters is what I do here in the UBC.” Rosalind taps William on the shoulder and says . . . Rosalind: “Sorry dear, I don’t mean to interrupt you. But is it just me, or is it hot in herrrre?” William: (Turns towards Rosalind) “Yeah it is hot in herrrrrre. So take off all your clothes.” Rosalind: “Ahhhhhh, no, I don’t think so. I don’t need to get naked to get over. I’ll just take off this hot ass sweatshirt.” Rosalind takes off her Converse College sweatshirt to reveal a white T-shirt with the words FIRST ACW TELEVISION CHAMPION in big black letter and a big black arrow pointing to William on the front. William: “Comfortable?” Rosalind: “Yes. Thank you. You should do the same.” William: “I think I will.” William stands up and takes off his USC shirt to reveal a gray T-shirt with the words GREATEST CWA WORLD CHAMPION on the front of it. As William sits back to relax again, he runs his hand over the words drawing attention to them. William: “You’re right this is much better. But I think these pants are too big for me. Could you head me a belt dear?” Rosalind: “Sure thing.” Rosalind reaches off camera and returns with the NEW Regional title. William puts it around his waist and pats it a couple of times. William: “So where was I?” Rosalind: “You were saying you’re weren’t going to brag about your past Championships, like the ACW Television title or the GWO tag team titles or the CWA world title or even this NEW Regional Title you are currently wearing.” William: “Oh yeah. Thank you dear.” (Turns his attention back to the camera.) “Anyway, it’s not going to happen people. There will be no talk of any of my many title reigns. It’s all about the present. And at the present time I see I have a match Brad Douglas. All I can say is what the fuck? Hey, can I say fuck?” Rosalind: “Fuck if I know. I didn’t read the rules.” William: “Me either. I just signed up for the UBC because I was tired of Xavier Morningstar stalking me. Don’t let Xavier fool, you people. He may try to appear smooth and all that shit. But he was at my house crying like a little bitch every day. Every morning he was at my doorsteps with a dozen roses, a box of candy, and a contract to the UBC. I swear my neighbors thought either I was gay, or Rosalind was cheating. It was a sad sight. But I digress. MrSynnerster, the Ambassador of Entertainment, the clown prince of wrestling, the mailman is here in the UBC. Let us all rejoice and be glad in it. And for my first act in the UBC, I shall smite the plague before it begins, and that plague is Brad Douglas. William shakes his head in disgust then says . . . William: “Mr. Douglas, I saw your little promo the other day. Excuse me but when the hell were we ever friends. I do not like you. I never have and I never will. In fact if memory serves me right. I have beaten you every time we’ve stepped into the ring. Okay okay, so now we’re fighting in a warehouse instead of a wrestling right. Do you really think that’s going to make a difference Mr. Douglas? I’ve said it before and I’ll say it again, you suck worse than a buck tooth hooker with a nervous twitch.” Rosalind taps William on the shoulder and William turns towards her. Rosalind: “You know he may have improved since last time.” William: “You know you’re right.” The two stare silently at each other for a moment, before both busting out laughing. Rosalind: (Laughing with tears in her eyes.) “Oh shit. I crack me up sometimes.” William: (Laugh.) “Oh god. Girl you are too funny.” They both laugh for several more minutes before William finally regains his composure. William: (To Rosalind.) “But seriously, I guess a little training wouldn’t hurt. Call Gene and tell him we’re on our way over. I’m tired of sitting around here. (To the camera.) “Well folks, now you know who we are. Oh don’t expect us to be sitting around talking to the camera all the time. We just did that today to get you acquitted with us. Rose and I are all about the adventure, just like the one we’re about to go on. Let’s get this party started. Peace out.” William and Rosalind both make a peace sign with their fingers towards the camera. As they start to get up from the couch, the scene fades out . . ..
