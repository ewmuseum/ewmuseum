::: Taken Advantage Of? :::

29 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12.29.02 ...So what? Brett Wayne can invite his prostitute mother to do away with Tommy Grady and still win a fight? I think we all saw the fight. I think we all know who the victor should have been.. and I think we were all disappointed with the outcome of the fight. An unconcious Brett Wayne was laying on the ground. Eyes shut. In a completely different world while he had his mother use a foreign object to defeat Tommy Grady. It took three people. For Tommy to beat Brett.. it took one. He may be bigger at stature -- but Tommy is bigger at heart. And that's truly what matters when it comes down to who has the guts and glory to go out a winner. This thing is far from over, buddy. You may have STOLEN the Ultimate Trophy, but this saga is FAR from being over... :::Scene opens with an image of a hotel. Mountains are seen in the background topped with white snow through the eerie upper fog. It is morning. In fact, the morning after Tommy Grady was so embarassingly - not a word, but truly fits - defeated by Brett Wayne for the first ever UBC Ultimate Brawlers Trophy. The camera cuts to the inside of the hotel and we are no inside of the room with Tommy Grady. He is sitting in his bed with his legs crossed. He has his hands pushed together and his head bowed as if he were praying. Aaron Everage walks in from the bathroom and looks at Tommy. He shakes his head::: Aaron Everage - Tommy, what the hell are you doing? Tommy Grady - Repenting.. Aaron Everage - What are you repenting for? Tommy Grady - You said that when I blacked out last night -- I got screwed.. and I was saving myself for marriage.. I made God cry.. Aaron Everage - I'm not talking about that kind of screwed Tommy.. you screwed out of victory! Tommy Grady - So I'm still a virgin? Aaron Everage - I don't know.. you didn't lose it last night, as far as I know.. Tommy Grady - My future wife will be SO happy to hear that.. Aaron Everage - When are you gonna find someone dumb enough to marry you? Tommy Grady - I was thinking about going to one of those remedial schools and scoping for one.. those chicks dig muscles and unintellegent conversation.. it rocks! Aaron Everage - Perfect.. Tommy Grady - So tell me a tale of last night.. Aaron Everage - No tale about it, Tommy.. You knocked Brett unconcious.. then his mom showed up and sprayed you down with a water hose. Doctor had to give you mouth to mouth to make you spit out the water.. ya almost drowned! Tommy Grady - Gross! A doctor put his lips on me?! Aaron Everage - On your mouth! Tommy Grady - NO! Aaron Everage - It was a chick.. Tommy Grady - A baby chicken got a doctrite? Aaron Everage - NO! A GIRL.. Tommy Grady - Oh.. well did she get a doctrite at remedial school? Aaron Everage - Uh.. that question must have slipped my mind.. Tommy Grady - So you're saying that a chick doctor took advantage of me, giving me mouth to mouth, and might have gone to remedial school? Aaron Everage - It's rather doubtful.. Tommy Grady - That freakin' RULES! Aaron Everage - What does? Tommy Grady - ...... I forgot.. Aaron Everage - How could you forgot? You were just thinking about it? Tommy Grady - I glanced at the clock and it said Bob.. Aaron Everage - What.... WHAT the hell are you takling about? Tommy Grady - It's 8:08.. it says BOB in clock lingo.. Aaron Everage - Clock lingo? Tommy Grady - You know.. 11:34 is hell upside down.. you know the lingo.. Aaron Everage - Why.... the HELL would that throw your thinking off track? Tommy Grady - It only happens twice a day, dude.. it's like taking a pee, you have to treasure each minute.. Aaron Everage - We obviously have different priorties.. Tommy Grady - What are yours? Aaron Everage - My what? Tommy Grady - NOW who's the one who's forgetful! Always bites ya in the butt, huH? Aaron Everage - Well what you mean 'what are yours'.. what are my priorities? Tommy Grady - Drrrrr.. my name is Aar-- Aaron Everage - Shut the fuck up.. my priorities are taking care of my business and taking care of you.. your father has been like a brother to me or else I'd sink your ship right now.. Tommy Grady - Dude, you must have me mixed up with Captain Ahab or Hook or somethin' cuz I don't even have a boat.. I have a TOY boat, but that's for my baths.. and it's plastic and won't sink soooo.. you couldn't do it anyways.. Aaron Everage - I'd do you in! But -- Tommy Grady - You'd do me in the what?! You are a sick mothertrucker! Aaron Everage - Good LORD, Tommy will you let me finish my damn sentence before you cut it off? Tommy Grady - I'm not gonna cut ANYTHING off, man.. do you think I'm a sicko? First in the butt.. now this? Aaron Everage - Tommy.. I'm going to tell you this very nicely. Go back to praying or whatever you were doing.. and just keep your mouth shut.. just don't open it.. PLEASE.. Tommy Grady - Hushpuppies? Aaron Everage - Right.. 1.. 2.. 3.. hushpuppies.. :::Tommy tucks his lips into his mouth with it sealed as if to say he's done talking. Aaron turns the television on to reveal Al Gore talking on a talk show::: Tommy Grady - Al loses! Aaron Everage - Al wasn't playing! Tommy Grady - He didn't specify it before he started talking.. you have to say you're not playing if you don't want to be counted.. Aaron Everage - Tommy, he's on TV.. he doesn't know we're playing.. Tommy Grady - If I have a fight and I'm asleep, do I get a free pass? No.. Aaron Everage - That's not even REMOTELY the same.. Tommy Grady - You are what you eat.. Aaron Everage - What are you TALKING about, Tommy? Tommy Grady - If you don't get it now, Aaron... I'm afraid you never will.. Aaron Everage - You get all the wrong things, Tommy.. Tommy Grady - I got the Ultimate Brawlers Trophy, didn't I? Aaron Everage - NO, Tommy.. that's what I've been saying! Brett screwed you out of the trophy while you were unconcious.. Tommy Grady - You mean I was actually inside of the trophy? Aaron Everage - What? You should have won it, but Brett screwed you out of it.. Tommy Grady - Well, how did I get in there to begin with? Aaron Everage - Tommy.... you were never inside of the trophy.. Tommy Grady - Let me get this straight.. you said I got screwed, but I'm still a virgin.. and I got knocked out of the Brawlers Trophy, but I was never in it? Aaron Everage - In a sense, yes.. I can't believe I actually have to explain this to you.. Tommy Grady - I don't even think Albert Einstone would have figured this one out.. Aaron Everage - Einstone? .. wow.. Tommy Grady - I know, he was really smart.. Aaron Everage - Yeah.. Einstone was a smarty.. Tommy Grady - I LOVE smarties! If eat a lot of 'em they give me a headache though.. ouch.. headaches suck.. Aaron Everage - YOU give ME a headache.. Tommy Grady - Did I beat you up or something? Aaron Everage - No. You're just a nuisance.. Tommy Grady - New.. sense? Aaron Everage - Stare at the wall.... Tommy Grady - What are you-- Aaron Everage - STARE.. at the wall.. please.. Tommy Grady - Wh-- Aaron Everage - STARE! :::Tommy looks confused and turns around staring at the wall::: Tommy Grady - Aaron, I don't know what's so great about the wall. I mean, it's a nice paint job and everything but it looks like you wiped a few boogars in a couple of places. You could have at least wiped them on the bottom of a chair or something.. :::Sound of rubber pealing out is heard and Tommy turns and Aaron is no where to be seen::: Tommy Grady - Aaron?.... aw shucks.. must have gotten an android.. ...ANNOYED!... Tommy Grady - What was that? :::Tommy looks around with a confused look on his face. His eyes lighten up as he walks over to a bottle of Beefeater vodka sitting on the counter with a shot glass lying next to it::: Tommy Grady - I like beef.. Abner Estrada - *walking into the room* You talkin' to yourself? Tommy Grady - No.. er..... I think not.. but maybe so.. Abner Estrada - What's that? Tommy Grady - I think it's for beef eaters.. Abner Estrada - What makes you think that? Tommy Grady - It's called Beefeater.. Abner Estrada - Good observation if ya ask me! Does it have any marijuana residue on it? I'd be happy to sniff it off for ya if so.. Tommy Grady - I don't know.. Abner Estrada - I'll sniff it off anyways.. :::Abner grabs the bottle and takes a big whiff before coughing::: Tommy Grady - You okay? Abner Estrada - I think I just sniffed ass crumbs.. Tommy Grady - Off this bottle? Abner Estrada - Wait a minute... this is the bottle I stole from Brett Wayne's locker room.. Tommy Grady - You stole it? Abner Estrada - I did.. Tommy Grady - Stealing is a sin.. it's in the ten commandments! Abner Estrada - Um.... sorry? Tommy Grady - Don't apologize to me.. I think we both know who you should apologize to.. Abner Estrada - ..... okay..? Tommy Grady - Hold on a minute.. my brilliance just picked up something.. why would ass crumbs be on a bottle of Beefeater in Brett Wayne's locker room? Abner Estrada - That IS a brilliant question.. Tommy Grady - Comes with the brilliance.. :::Tommy pours himself a shot of Beefeater. He throws his head back and downs the shot. He shakes his head and spits a bit::: Tommy Grady - What the hell? This tastes nothing like beef! THOSE LIARS! !!!INTOXICATION!!! Abner Estrada - Liars suck.. Tommy Grady - What the fuck do you know, Abner? Abner Estrada - Huh? Tommy Grady - Do you think you know everything? Do you think that everything revolves around you? Hate to burst your bubble but you're dead wrong.. and people like Chris Carter and Brett Wayne who think they can even COMPARE to Tommy Grady? They're just as wrong. But the sad thing is, they're to the point where they can't apologize and back away. They've been forced into a position where it's kill or be killed. First blood match. Which is rather disappointing considering, compared to the usual UBC matchups, this is a lighter. How many times has a UBC fighter bled? More than a dozen. I've bled in both of my matchups. As has Wayne. And this 'AK' thug.. well, I don't pay attention to the UBC gutter 'talents', but I'm sure he's bled as well. So does Kron think he's doing some sort of amazing feat by having a first blood match? I don't know what's going on, but the air just doesn't smell right. There's a sour smell in the air. And it's not Brett Wayne's jock strap or a Chris Carter promo. Those are more of a rotten smell. I sense something not right. But what can I do? I'll keep an eye on the entrance, an eye on Brett Wayne.. and a finger on Chris Carter. I haven't been in this business long, but I don't think that gives anyone a right to disrespect me the way I'm being disrespected. Or even Brett Wayne for that matter.. :::Estrada gets in Tommy's way and Tommy gives him a firm shove, forcing Abner to fall back into a chair::: Tommy Grady - This Chris Carter? Where in the blue hell did this guy come from? Comes from pancakes and sweat towels to main event? From fighting in a two on one match with a pathetic human being in MrSynnerster to Tommy Grady? If it weren't so funny it would be insulting. Chris, you've gotten to enjoy my skills the past couple of weeks. You've gotten to see my talents displayed in an absolute beating of Outcast.. then Krisis.. then Brett Wayne.. if it weren't for Wayne's slut mother I would be holding the Ultimate Brawlers Trophy in my hand.. but the fact that I don't doesn't make me any less dangerous. I'm fuckin' lethal, my friend. You've never had the displeasure of meeting a guy like me face to face. And if you have met someone like me, then ya must have gotten your ass handed to you. Cuz that's exactly what's gonna happen come this Saturday. No bandaids or bandages are gonna keep you from gushing your pathetic little blood all over that fighting zone. You are nothing. You've never been a damn thing and you'll never BE a damn thing. And don't blame yourself, blame your mother for producing a pathetic human being like yourself.. :::Estrada laughs and Tommy gives him a glare that shuts him up::: Tommy Grady - And as for YOU, Brett.. this is the first time I've gotten to confront you since the shit you pulled last Saturday night. I hope you're god damned proud of yourself. But your worst nightmare has come true. You can't avoid me. I whooped you all over the fighting zone a week and a half ago and there ain't a damn thing that's changed since then. It was no fluke that I had you unconcious on two different occasions. It's no fluke that if the referee had paid attention to you instead of that whore you call a mother -- I would be the current Ultimate Brawlers Trophy holder. I would be the man with the inside track to the Ultimate championship belt. And the anger that's boggled up inside of me isn't going to go away. It's going to worsen more and more.. until Saturday. And on that day you'll feel the complete wrath that you, yourself, have created. You crossed a line. A line that many people are afraid to cross with me. Took some balls, Brett. Took some major balls. And we'll see if you have the balls to nut up and and take the beating that is coming to you. You may wanna get a few pointers from your mom on how to take it like a bitch, cuz there ain't a damn thing you can do to avoid my onslaught.. Abner Estrada - Well, he could not show up.. Tommy Grady - And not get a paycheck? I think he'd rather date a female.. Abner Estrada - I don't know if it's THAT big of a longshot.. Tommy Grady - He'll be there, Abner.. and when he is there he'll be seeing the bottom of my foot.. and a close up of my fist.. all.. night.. long.. and THAT.. !!!SOBER!!! Tommy Grady - ........ Abner Estrada - Tommy? Tommy Grady - ... was one.. AWESOME blackout.. Abner Estrada - You blacked out, dude? Tommy Grady - Oh.. hella blacked out! Big time hella blackout! Abner Estrada - That Beefeater fucked you up.. Tommy Grady - I had sex with the Beefeater? Abner Estrada - I mean it got you shit faced.. Tommy Grady - GROSS! :::Tommy runs into a nearby restroom and walks out with a bit of a smile::: Tommy Grady - You must have shit on your eyeballs cuz there's no shit on MY face.. Abner Estrada - That beefeater knocked you out.. Tommy Grady - I KNOW, DUDE! Abner Estrada - Beefeater rules.. Tommy Grady - It was pretty gross.. Abner Estrada - .. still rules.. Tommy Grady - Blackouts rule.. Abner Estrada - Oh yeah! Tommy Grady - Christina Aguilera rules.. Abner Estrada - I'd hit that.. Tommy Grady - If you hit her I'd knock you the fuck out! Abner Estrada - I mean I'd tax and wax it and flip it over and pax it.. Tommy Grady - I have no idea what you said, but if it had to do with her being hot, I agree.. Abner Estrada - Definitely.... Tommy Grady - ....... Abner Estrada - ........ Tommy Grady - So... Abner Estrada - So, uh.. wanna go smoke some weed? Tommy Grady - Ehren doesn't allow me to.. plus it's against the law.. Abner Estrada - In the Bible it says it's legal.. Tommy Grady - It.. DOES? Abner Estrada - I heard.. like.. something 4:20 or something.. maybe Mary Jane 4:20.. Tommy Grady - Mary Jane wrote in the Bible? Abner Estrada - ...... uh.. yeah? Tommy Grady - I'll go look that up.. Abner Estrada - No.. I'll do it.. and give it to you.. Tommy Grady - Thanks, dude.. Abner Estrada - Anything for a fellow rocker dude! Tommy Grady - You calling me racist? :::Abner looks at Tommy confused before turning around and walking out of the room. Tommy shrugs and opens the fridge, searching through it. The scene slowly fades to black with the image of Grady pulling out an egg::: ...And that's how it is.. Brett, Chris.. it's first blood this weekend. Hardly any guts.. barely any glory.. yet so much is on the line, especially when it comes to Brett Wayne and the Ultimate Brawlers Trophy. Chris Carter? Just another pawn in the game. Just another obstacle to overcome. But don't think for a moment Tommy is going to overlook you.. he's gonna be prepared for you. The day to day Tommy may not be preparing -- but all along, a certain -- DRUNKEN Tommy is preparing. And he'll be ready come Saturday. You both have quite a feat to accomplish. I must say I don't envy your position. But that's why you fight and I just talk, right? I have a feeling Tommy will be at one hundred percent come Saturday.. and that's definitely bad news for the likes of the other participants in this matchup. First blood won't take long.. and it won't be a scratch. One of you two will be gushing.. and Tommy Grady will have his hand raised in victory.. while the loser is forced to count the lights...
