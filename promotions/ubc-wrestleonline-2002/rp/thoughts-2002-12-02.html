2 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12-02-02 A dark figure is seen standing in a dark cold ally way in the middle of the night. Rats running around looking for stuff to feed on. Old men and women digging in trash looking for food to eat. Cats and Dogs looking for food to eat. The figure begins walking to the camera as you can see his red eyes getting closer and closer. Soon enough Evil One reveals himself to the camera. You see this. Evil points to the old men and women digging in the trash. These people are suffering, no home to stay in, no family to be with just them and there trash. People like this deserve better, they deserve to be more than just trash. They need to be in a place far more better than this one. Shall I be there taker or should I let god decide to take them. Why should I let god decide, he took what I loved most so I'll take what he loves most. Instead of simply putting them out of the misery, I'll just make them feel horror for the rest of there lifes. Evil walks up to the old man digging in the trash and looks at him straight in the eyes, his eyes start glowing as the old man begins to shake. Thats right fear me, because everyone should. I will bring all fear to people who do not fear me and you old man fear me, so your life is spared. Evil walks off slowly as the old man faints and falls to the ground. He walks up on an old women on the ground eating old hamburgers invested with mold and magits. Disgusting isn't it, the way people eat these days. She should be put out of her misery but I do not have the time right now to be messing with these kinds of people. I only have one person on my mind as of right now. Thorn Wilson, he is a silent one. I do not know much about him and I hope he is the one I have been looking for but if he so happens not to be the one then I'll simply put him to sleep for good. Evil begins walking again only to find himself at a dead end, he turns around and looks down the long dark ally way, he decides to sit down in the corner of the dead end. He begins bobbing back and forth. I will bring Hell to UBC. I am Hell, and I am everyones Taker. If you do not bow down to me then you pay for your sins and I know everything. Bobby Bless pretends to be god but he lacks the power. He only has his words. He says he has the 'Book of Bless' which is another sin he has brought to himself for there I will make him pay. Johnny Nightmare also thinks he is god but he also lacks the power and strength. He is nothing more then pathetic human. He will soon perish by the hands of me. Evil quits bobbing back and forth as he gets on his hands and knees stairing at the camera as his Red eyes glow. And for Brett Wayne who beleives to be the Insane. Whether he is insane or not is not the picture. Brett Wayne ha committed many sins in his lifetime. I see it in his eyes. He wants to be the Very Best he can be, but he has to sin to be the best. Whether he is the best or not it doesn't matter I will soon have him backed up in a dead end much like the one I'm sitting at now and then thats when I'll end his career for good. Evil stands up and begins walking back through the ally way mumbling to himself, he continues walking until only his shadow can be seen then soon enough he walks off into the darkness from where he came.
