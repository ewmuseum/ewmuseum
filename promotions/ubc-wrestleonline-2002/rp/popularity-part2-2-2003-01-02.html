Popularity? Part2 2

2 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1/2/03 The scene opens as Chris and Brian Heart wake up from the mystery popularity day. It's their second day back in Brooklyn and everyone already knew they were here. They don't know how though, they don't see how that could be. They were trying to hide from the public for a while, but everyone knew they were in Brooklyn... Chris Heart: Ah bro, last night was weird man... Brian Heart: Yeah, I still don't see how everyone knew we were back...not even mom and dad know... Chris Heart: Yeah, I wonder how Stephanie's doing.. Brian Heart: lil sis? Hmm...haven't heard from her in a while... Chris Heart: Yeah...lets go watch TV... They turn on the TV and the first thing they see is a big commercial saying that the Heart Throbs are back in Brooklyn. Chris and Brian begin to think... Chris Heart: Don't even worry about it...lets go workout our match is in two days Brian Heart: okay, I'll get the keys. They get ready and leave the house into thier car with the new paint job (thanks to the fans) and they are about to go to an old friend of theirs. His name is Salvadore Juliani (Sally J for short). When they arrive there are some people in the bar. Chris Heart: Wazzah Sally J! Brian Heart: Hey Sall Sally J: Hey fellas, long time no see. Chris Heart: Yeah, been bussy... Sally J: Yeah, I know your all ova da TV. Chris Heart: Yeah i know, but who the hell putt us on TV. Sally J: Ah, anyways what can I do for ya. Chris Heart: You still got that ring down in the dungeon. Sally J: Yeah, why...need to use it? Brian Heart: Yeah Sally J: Ok, hold on let me take care of these guys. Sally J takes care of the customers and one of them say.... Customer: Ah, its the Heart Throbs... Chris and Brian say Hi. Customer: I know the girl that got you all this publicity, but I forgot your name...she is a girl and very beautiful. Chris Heart: REally...? Customer: Yeah Sally J gets his keys and they go in the basement to the wrestling ring and they practice for a long while. They when they are done they go home sore...and thinking about how they could figure out who did this...then they go to sleep. ---- Back
