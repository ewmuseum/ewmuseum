Date: 12.08.02 ...Nearly twenty four hours after his sweet, sweet victory to open up his UBC career -- Tommy has returned to the airwaves. Now don't get too excited quite yet. Let me free my mind right quick, in recap. Outcast wanted to save his words for the ring. He fought a hell of a match, but in the end he was shown that he just doesn't have the talent -- OR charisma to defeat Tommy Grady. Not a knock towards him. He'll be a successful UBC athlete. He may even get the number one contendership to some bottom feeding title, but he was obviously out of his league when he stuck his nose through Tommy's door and got popped right the fuck out ... Mission two is Krisis. It is awfully sad that Tommy has to go down rather than up, but I guess if you mess with Tommy -- ya gotta pay the consequences. It doesn't matter if it is spilt coffee or a boot in the ass, Tommy takes it all the same. And come Jurisdiction he's gonna be ready to make it victory.. part deux... :::Scene opens with an image of the inside of a hotel. Tommy and Aaron are walking behind Estrada on their way to their hotel room. Estrada leads them up a flight of stares as Tommy and Aaron carry their own bags to the top. Estrada takes a right and they Aaron and Tommy both follow close behind. Abner stops in front of a door and opens the door without a key entrance, for some odd reason. They all three step into the room with their bags hanging on their shoulders.. with the exception of Estrada, of course. Tommy sets his stuff down on a bed and Aaron does the same on another bed. Tommy to look out the window at the city of Fort Worth, Texas.. he speaks::: Tommy Grady - This land is about as abandoned as Krisis’s bedroom.. Aaron Everage - No.. it’s even WORSE.. Tommy Grady - Dude.. that’s IMPOSSIBLE! Aaron Everage - Nope.. it’s VERY possible.. in fact.. it’s TRUE.. the only person that enters Krisis’s bed is himself. There are precisely NO humans wondering these streets.. Tommy Grady - Wow.. I’ve never BEEN to waste, country, Tommy.. Aaron Everage - It’s not too exciting.. Tommy Grady - Surely there is SOMETHING to do here.. Aaron Everage - Nothing besides riding horses.. Tommy Grady - You mean beastiality is legal here? Aaron Everage - They ride em.. like.. for fun.. Tommy Grady - For fun or not.. it’s still a sin! I bet Bush legalized it, didn’t he? Aaron Everage - NO, Tommy.. beastiality is NOT legal here in Texas.. Tommy Grady - So there are a bunch of rebel outlaws who are doing it? Let’s go track em down, Aaron! Aaron Everage - No, Tommy.. listen.. here.. there are things called cowboys.. do you know what those are? Tommy Grady - The result of a cow and a man having sex? Aaron Everage - No.. not even close, Tommy. A cowboy is a man who rides horseback around.. he usually wears old western gear.. mainly the most well-known one would be the cowboy hat.. You have a few of those at your home, don’t ya, Tommy? Tommy Grady - Cowboy hat? Um.. nnnn.. :::looks at Aaron::: YES! Yes I do have one.. so these guys get on the back of a horse and just ride around? Aaron Everage - Correct.. Tommy Grady - Sounds like a major case of blue balls to me.. Aaron Everage - In most cases it is.. Tommy Grady - Ya’know what? Aaron Everage - What, Tommy? :::Tommy looks at the ground for a moment.. then looks back up at Aaron::: Tommy Grady - I have a game plan for the pay per view.. Aaron Everage - Oh, you DO.. do you? Tommy Grady - Yup.. and I think it ROCKS! Aaron Everage - :::to himself::: Oh boy.. :::looks at Tommy::: Lemme here it.. Tommy Grady - Okay.. :::deep breath::: Here it goes.. I was thinkin’ that Krisis must be a little intimidated since he didn’t appear on UBCTV yesterday.. so I was thinkin’ that maybe we could just make fun of him for being quiet and afraid ‘n stuff.. what do you think? Aaron Everage - That’s all? Tommy Grady - Well.. :::thinks::: Yeah.. unless you have somethin’ to add? Aaron Everage - NO! Not at all, Tommy.. have a ball.. Tommy Grady - To-NIGHT? I don’t have time to prepare for a ball, Aaron.. I have a match to concentrate on.. geez, man. And to think I was about to call you a genious.. do you WANT me to lose this match? Aaron Everage - :::sigh::: No, Tommy.. Tommy Grady - You are an ignoramus! Aaron Everage - :::gasp::: Tommy Grady - What’s wrong? Did you wet yourself cuz I’m so intimidating? Aaron Everage - No.. I was just amazed that you used a word that was over three consonants long and it.. :::gulp::: Made.. SENSE.. Abner Estrada - :::from the other room::: Without alcohol, mind you! Tommy Grady - It’s amazing the vocabulary you can acquire by listening to a Brett Wayne promo.. that Wayne character has quite a mind on him, ya’know? Aaron Everage - Coulda fooled me.. Tommy Grady - Fooled you with what? Aaron Everage - Calling him intelligent.. Tommy Grady - I wasn’t even trying to fool you, dude. Quit bein so paranoid.. Aaron Everage - :::sighs and shakes his head::: Sometimes I wonder how I became associated with such an idiot.. Tommy Grady - :::whispers::: I know.. have you been listening to my narrators intros lately? He might as well just get on screen and take a dump the whole time.. that’s how much I get out of him.. ...Heeeeey.. are you talkin' about me?... Tommy Grady - No, chubbs.. why don’t you go eat something and quit budding into our conversations.. ya boob! Aaron Everage - Wow, Tommy.. that was another good.. ORIGINAL one.. Tommy Grady - Thanks.. I came up with it whenever I was suckin on your mom’s last night.. OOOOOOOOOH! Aaron Everage - That was VERY uncalled for, Tommy.. VERY uncalled for.. Tommy Grady - Yeah.. I know. It was funny though! I’m a funny man. You should take lessons from me, ya’know that? Maybe you could get back into this business if you worked on entertaining the people in your promos instead of gag 'em.. Aaron Everage - What the hell’s that supposed to mean? Tommy Grady - You’re about as funny as Krisis is talented.. bitch.. Aaron Everage - You think I’m THAT unhumorous? Tommy Grady - ‘fraid so, dude.. Aaron Everage - Why am I even listening to you, for God’s sake. You have about as much intelligence as an Ethiopian foreign exchange student.. Tommy Grady - Wow.. isn’t that in Asia? Those people are ALWAYS smart.. Aaron Everage - No.. it’s a third world country.. Tommy Grady - You mean, they found life on another planet and I didn’t even KNOW about it? Maybe THAT’S why everyone in the UBC is so good now.. Abner Estrada - :::walking in::: Back from the beer run! WOO! Tommy Grady - Sweet! I can drink one of those buddys that make me wiser and black out again.. just like I did when I stomped a hole in that flake Outcast.. :::Tommy drains a beer extremely quickly and shakes his head, letting out a sigh. He belches loudly::: Aaron Everage - You’re hopeless, Tommy.. purely hopeless.. Tommy Grady - No no, Aaron. Like I said.. Krisis is hopeless when it becomes Saturday evening. Yeah.. you heard me. How it was yesterday is how it's gonna be a week from that date. How dare you comfront me. How dare you even think you have the right to speak to me, much less ask if I 'knew who you were'. Well, guess what? I didn't know, I didn't care. And I still don't care -- but why the hell not accept a match that I know I'll win. One that, really, poses no threat towards me whatsoever. Krisis, I am a hell of a specimen. I have the charisma. I have an aura that superstars dream of having. You have a stinch the shit is jealous of. You see, that's the difference. I'm great, I'm going places -- towards the Ultimate title. What about you? Huh? You don't care about titles. You run around beating down every day people and the fact is -- they feel sorry for you. I feel sorry for you watching it. Humorous, yes.. but intellegent? Hardly. Intimidating? Not at all. And the fact that you don't intimidate me is the catapult to the fact that on Saturday night, you'll be suffering, bleeding, and pleeding. Because it will be impossible to escape my wrath, my friend. You should have hit the paved road the moment you looked into my eyes. You had just watched me destroy... DESTROY Outcast and moments later you want to get in my face? Big mistake. Did you forget who you're messin' with, Krisis? I'm Tommy Grady. Not Brett Wayne. Not Paranoia. Not some push over slob that is fighting for bread crumbs. Not some passionless idiot fighting for the lesser things in life. I'm after gold. I'm after silver. I'm after green. And I'm after red -- and that red is gonna be your blood on the damn ground come Saturday night. You're not gonna escape it, my friend. I've had my first taste and I'm ready for my next.. :::Tommy cracks a smile::: Aaron Everage - What's going through that sadistic mind of yours, Tommy? Tommy Grady - Dude...... I totally just blacked out! Aaron Everage - Huh? Tommy Grady - Blacked out. Like... see black.. out.. Aaron Everage - What happened to Tommy? Abner Estrada - Didn't have enough beer to keep him buzzin'.. Aaron Everage - Damnit.. please, load his ass up! Tommy Grady - Where are you taking my ass? Aaron Everage - No where.. Tommy Grady - You better tell me where you're taking my ass! It's not leaving without me.. and you definitely aren't loading it onto something.. especially a trunk. My ass needs air to keep from breaking out.. Aaron Everage - Please, Abner.. Tommy Grady - No, Abner. Please don't! Aaron Everage - Shut up, Tommy.. no one is gonna touch your ass.. Tommy Grady - See how I intimidated you there? Man, I'm good! Aaron Everage - Right. Nobody mess with Tommy.. Tommy Grady - DAMN RIGHT! :::Aaron flicks Tommy on the throat::: Tommy Grady - OW! That fuckin' hurt, JERKO! Aaron Everage - Right.. I just wanted to make sure you heard me tell you you're intimidating.. Tommy Grady - Damn right! GRRRR! :::rubs his throat::: Damn, Aaron.. you need to cut your nails or something, dude.. it feels like I just got probed through my asophagus.. Aaron Everage - El wimpo.. Tommy Grady - El jerko.. Aaron Everage - You belong in a home.. Tommy Grady - I AM in a home.. it's your home, did you forget? And you said I'M dumb.. HA! Aaron Everage - Get him out of here.. Tommy Grady - Abner? What'd he d-- Aaron Everage - ABNER! Get him out of here.. Tommy Grady - Mmm.. me? Abner Estrada - C'mon Tommyboy.. I got some new Shrek coloring books to play with.. Tommy Grady - Dat'll do donkay.. dat'll do.. Abner Estrada - Right.. c'mon.. :::The camera shows Grady leaving the room with Abner leading him, Tommy talking about how much he likes Shrek. The camera then focuses in on Aaron, who shakes his head. He snaps open a beer and takes a drink, staring into space. He shakes his head one more time and the scene fades to black::: ...Tommy is ready. He's prepared. Don't be intimidated, Krisis. That's the best position you could be in. Look at this Tommy. Look at the coloring book Tommy -- because he is completely different from the Tommy you're gonna meet at Jursdiction. Be prepared for a brawl, because that's what has been booked.. and that's for damn sure what's gonna happen...
