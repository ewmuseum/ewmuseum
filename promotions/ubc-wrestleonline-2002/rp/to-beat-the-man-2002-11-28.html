:::To Beat the Man:::

28 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11.28.02 :::He got it. He actually got what he asked for. Tommy must be one happy chap. The Ultimate Brawlers Trophy is within his grasp and step one begins with Outcast in just over a week. Tommy has been training feverishly over the past few days, laying low -- awaiting the release of the Jurisdiction card where good ole Tommy can showcase his talents to the entire world. As long as there's no trivia questions involved, I'd tend to tilt Tommy's way in this one. There's no doubting Outcast is an athletic, intimidating man. But there's no intimidating Tommy Grady. Not because he's brave, but because he's too dumb to know when he SHOULD be intimidated. Point of the matter is, Outcast, you've avoiding flapping your jaw for far too long. Now it's come to the point where you put up or shut up. You better think long and hard about where you wanna go in this business because to get there you have to get by Tommy on December 7th::: :::Speaking of Tommy, lets take you to him now. Ah, wait. Wrong promo. That guys looks like a penis with a hairy tip. Surely this isn't an Outcast promo -- AH! Wait. There's Abner. I guess that's Tommy dressed up as the Cowardly Dull Outcast. But why is he dressed up like that? Surely he has something better to do than dress up as his opponent. Although, I guess this IS Tommy we're talking about. Tommy, as Outcast, and Abner turn left and duck into a bar. The camera quickly cuts to the inside of the bar where Tommy and Abner walk towards a couple of stools. They both take a seat::: Abner Estrada - I'm tellin ya.. this will put you over the edge.. if you drink like Outcast.. you will DOMINATE him next Saturday! Tommy Grady - But I'm not much of a liquor drinker, Estrada.. Abner Estrada - Trust me.. Ya smell that? It's VICTORY! Tommy Grady - *sniffing* Smells like a mixture of B.O. and Medicated Hand Cream to me! Bartender - What'll it be? Abner Estrada - Give him some of that Jack Daniels.. Tommy Grady - Whoa whoa whoa.. I thought we were drinking alcohol.. Abner Estrada - It's whiskey.. Tommy Grady - I'm not in the mood to get frisky, Abner.. especially not with a man! Abner Estrada - NO! Whiskey.. Damn good whiskey.. the kind that Outcast drinks all the time.. Tommy Grady - This is the stuff? Abner Estrada - I'm sure it is.. it messes ya up real bad.. for a long time. Outcast can take it.. be a real man! Tommy Grady - If Outcast can do it.. Grady-cast can do it two times better.. Abner Estrada - Well he takes five shots of this crap, man.. I dunno if you can take that much.. Tommy Grady - HA! Baby boy, put it on me! Abner Estrada - *shrugs* Alright, then.. :::The bartender gives Tommy a strange look before filling ten shot glasses full of Jack Daniels. He places them all in front of Tommy and Tommy looks at em. He picks one up and tosses it back. He makes a disgusted face and shakes his head quickly. He looks at Abner::: Tommy Grady - Gimme somethin else to drink! Abner Estrada - Okay.. *hands Tommy his beer* :::Tommy takes a drink without thinking and swallows. He realizes it's beer and struggles for somethin else to drink. He grabs a mans left over bloody mary and takes a drink of it. He realizes it has alcohol in that, as well. Tommy finally takes another shot of Jack Daniels and stands up to his feet. He looks at Abner::: Tommy Grady - Dude.. we better go.. Abner Estrada - What? Only two shots? *cough*weak*cough* Tommy Grady - *taking another shot* THERE! Abner Estrada - You're still one behind him! :::Tommy quickly takes two shots one after the other and slams the glasses down. He looks at Abner and nearly falls over. He catches himself before he goes down and looks at Abner::: Tommy Grady - I probably outta go to the bathroom real quick.. Abner Estrada - Probably so.. :::Tommy steps out of the area and walks through the mens restroom. The camera, of course, follows to pick up the action. Another picture of Tommy Grady sitting on top of a toilet seat. Ya'know.. it's either a prison or a toilet::: Tommy Grady - *obviously intoxicated* Hey there man! Ya know what's goin on now? What's goin on now is me gettin prepared for my match.. I'm trainin hard. Livin like a Outcast man would live.. lemme tell you.. I'm havin a ball. Yeah.. actin like an idiot and not carin. I get to be as immature as I want cuz I've been drinking alcohol. Woowee! Look at me.. how about I go grab a retired sitcom star and make him be my bodyguard. Then I can go to hell.. grab someone as sadistically boring as I am.. and bring 'em back to earth with me! I can be just like Outcast now that I'm drunk.. right? Am I right.. *Tommys eyes rest at ease and looks into the camera seriously* What? You think I can't HOLD my alcohol? Ya see, there's ANOTHER difference between Outcast and me. I'm not as big of a pussy when it comes to shots. Whether it be at the doctors office.. whether it be at a local bar.. or whether it be for a title.. I give it my all. I don't step down from a challenge.. I step up and take it like a man. Something that Outcast, I KNOW, can't do.. Well, let me tell you somethin, Outcast.. Grady-cast, had he been real, would totally beat your pathetic little ass. Which leads me to believe that despite the name mix around -- the original.. Tommy Grady.. would beat it just as bad.. if not WORSE. Bein' you is great and all.. but it just doesn't add up to being me. You see.. I am talented. I give a certain entertainment value to the brawling world. I make the boxing world a better place for people like YOU who try to become something in this business, Outcast. And no matter how you slice the cake.. you are still gonna have the same type of pie. Which means that you can go anywhere in this federation, Outcast.. but you can't perform above your ability. You can either dish out a big piece.. or a small piece.. but that doesn't change the fact that your pie is pussy pie. And pussy pie means that despite your victories.. despite your accomplishments.. Tommy Grady will be the winner at Jurisdiction.. and here is your reasoning. You see.. no man in the Underground Brawlers Club is exactly good with dealing with.. 'pussy', for say. But one man is fully capable of that. And that is yours truly. Yes I can tame that pussy pie.. hell I am the one that cooks your damn'd pussy pies for ya. I know the recipe better than any man in this place -- so when you try to bring that piece of crap so-called talent to the ring against me? You know just what's gonna happen. When I walk into the arena, Nathanial.. you'll be standin up.. and when I leave? You'll be layin down. One way or another.. just as sure as the night will come.. Tommy Grady will leave you layin on your ass at the end of Saturday Jurisdiction.. and THAT'S a guarun'damn'tee.. ::::........good God. That actually made sense. We've found the secret ingredient to his intellegence::: Abner Estrada - You alright, Tommy? :::Abner has entered the restroom. Tommy steps out of the stall and looks at Abner::: Tommy Grady - Hell yeah, I'm alright.. what'd you think I was? An Outcast? Abner Estrada - Nope.. but I do think you are in charge of getting pops out of jail.. Tommy Grady - Nah.. Everage has it taken care of.. :::To Tommys surprise his cell phone begins to ring. Tommy pats himself up and down until he finds it. He pops it out of his pocket and puts it up onto his ear. He begins to speak::: Tommy Grady - Hello? ...... Tommy Grady - Who IS this? ...... Tommy Grady - Ya don't say.. ...... Tommy Grady - YA DON'T SAY! ...... Tommy Grady - Well, by God.. ya don't say! :::Tommy turns his phone off and slides it into his pocket.. turning back towards Abner::: Abner Estrada - Who was it? Tommy Grady - He didn't say.. :::Abner looks a bit confused as Tommy nods towards the door. As they walk out they see Aaron Evearge and his associate Rhett Bomar walking right outside the window. Aaron's black BMW is sitting in front of the bar as Tommy goes ahead of Abner. Abner is still trying to figure out what was going on with Tommy. Tommy is outside and still dressed as Outcast.. So I would assume that Aaron would fall for it::: Tommy Grady - Hey, Ev! You seen Tommy around? Aaron Everage - It's YOU! Tommy Grady - ARG! Aaron Everage - AH! Tommy Grady - Hey Ev.. Aaron Everage - What? Tommy Grady - Watch this.. :::Aaron looks at Tommy. He rips his wig off::: Aaron Everage - AH! Rhett Bomar - AH! Both - Tommy? Tommy Grady - Indeed.. great disguise, huh? Aaron Everage - You've been in the UBC as two people and didn't tell us? Troy Huntley Dude! Everyone thinks you're gonna be the first champ! Aaron Everage - You TOTALLY spoofified me, man! All along I thought that you were-- Tommy Grady - Guys.. I'm not REALLY Outcast.. :::They raise their fists and look at Tommy::: Aaron Everage - Then.. who ARE you? Tommy Grady - Just Tommy.. Aaron Everage - Aw shucks.. Tommy Grady - You're disappointed? *sniff* YOU'RE A JERKO! *sniff* Aaron Everage - Quit being melodramatic. You're drunk.. put him in the car.. and make sure he dangles his head out the window.. Tommy Grady - I'm not a dog, Aaron! Aaron Everage - No, but you're dressed as a prick -- that's even worse.. Tommy Grady - Hu hu.. that's kinda funny.. :::Aaron cracks a grin for a minute before snapping into a serious state. Abner leads Tommy to the back of the BMW and they both duck into it. The door closes as the rest of the men duck into the car. The doors close and off they go ::: So there ya go. Tommy actually seemed brave and somewhat intellegent with alcohol streaming through his veins. I suppose we'll have to keep that in mind the next time. But fair warning be to Outcast: Tommy isn't your average two year old. He's a big'en. Don't cover your pie hole too long or else this guys confidence may continue to rise. And a confident Tommy isn't want you want at Jurisdiction. Bet ya bottom dolla!:::
