Beyond Fucked Up

29 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11.29.02 Introduction: Previously on 'Thug-Vision TV', Da Thug was one of many prisoners to be mysteriously broken out of prison by an unknown character. After breaking out, he called his lying agent, Jerry, to find that his suspisions were correct, Jerry had ditched him and left him in the cold. Da Thug, by the end of the day, followed through with his promise and killed Jerry along with his wife and baby. But this is a new chapter, a new day, what chaos will go down today? The scene opens up in a dark, rainy alley of Compton, California. Da Thug is walking through the alley with a bottle of vodka in his hand, a gun in his pocket, and nothing on his mind. Footstep after footstep, Da Thug meanders down the alley with no seemingly place to be heading to. The usual night-life sounds can be heard coming from the city of Compton, no surprise. Da Thug takes yet another repetitive drink of his Vodka and realizes that the content in the bottle is nearing empty. Just like a car on low fuel, Thug begins searching for somewhere to get more. A rather large alcohol shop can be seen down the street quite a ways. Da Thug keeps moving his legs, progressing down the long, dark street. A dark figure is seen down the street, walking towards Da Thug. Closer and closer, the figure turns out to be just another regular person walking down the street, like Thug. A couple more minutes pass as Da Thug finally arrives at the Liquor Store. He enters through the front door aftering dropping his empty Vodka bottle. Clerk: Hey, what can I get ya? Da Thug doesn't answer, he just looks at him for a minute... Thug then rubs his face and eyes with his hand and looks back at the clerk. Da Thug: Uggg... Can I get some 80-Proof Absolut Vodka? Clerk: Wow, man, are you sure? You don't look so well, you could really mess yourself up with drinking like this. Anyone know you're here? Like family or anything? Da Thug: Dogg... Fuck you, gimme somethin' to drink. Reluctantly, the clerk grabs what Thug wants and then slides it over the counter. Da Thug throws his wallet at the clerk and walks off the the vodka, drunk off his ass. Da Thug walks out to the front of the store and begins drinking the vodka, straight! A wave of sweat goes over Thug's face as his eyes glaze over. Da Thug's blood pressure is now eigth times over the legal limit and there is actually a chance of death if he drinks too much more! Thug gets up off the curb and falls over onto a trash can. He stumbles back on to his feet and then attempts to cross a busy road. On the other side of the road, Da Thug see what appears to be the 'Pilsbury Doy Boy' and Sesame Street's 'Big Bird' having a fist fight! PDB pops a huge right fist to Big Bird's face and begins tickling his stomach. Big Bird grabs a nearby rock and bashes it over PBDB's head, Big Bird then yells across the street to Thug, 'The letter of the day today is, D! As in, Damn I fucked him up! HEHEHEHE!'. Big Bird then skips across the street and is hit by a speeding semi truck. Big Bird flies at least seventy feet through the air and then disappears out of Thug's imagination. Da Thug sees a silver chain on 'Barney The Dinosaur', who is standing in the middle of the street jerking off. Da Thug, with a confused, dazed look on his face, walks into the street. Cars slam on their breaks and screech to a stop, as their horns begin blasting into the air. Da Thug begins swinging at Barney to steal his chain. But in reality, Da Thug is just swinging at the air and stumbling over, due to the alcohol's effects. Finally, Da Thug falls over and passes out in the middle of the street. Someone calls the police in concern, which is rare in Compton. Minutes later, a police car shows up, along with an ambulance. Da Thug is properly diagnosed and is put in the ambulance and rush to the nearest hospital. Female Medic: Wow, I haven't seen somebody this drunk in a long time! The last time was... probably when my dad found out my sister was pregnant. Pfttt... That was a disaster! Male Medic: Haha, yeah, I bet it was. Poor guy, check and see if he has any ID on him... The female medic leans over and begins searching Da Thug's pockets for ID, but finds nothing. Male Medic: Damn! This guy... He just looks so familiar! I swear that I've seen him somewhere before... Wait a minute! He's a pro wrestler! That's it! Female Medic: That would explain why he has nothing better to do than get drunk, I guess. What, they only work maybe once a week? Male Medic: Yeah, I guess so. This isn't uncommon among superstars like him, depression just gets to them. Da Thug's body begins violently shaking and his eyes become blood shot. The medics jump out of their seat and begin tossing equipment around. Male Medic: What the hell!? Check his heartbeat. Female Medic: It's high... Very high. The scene begins to swirl into a faded shade of black and red. Da Thug then has a flashback of his childhood... This scene opens up with Thug and his dead best friend, Tyrone, back when Thug was around 17. It was dark and in the middle of a busy street. Slow motion effects show a bullet blasting hinto his best friend, Sean's chest. Sean flies back and leaps to his death bed on the cold, wet pavement. The scene slowly reopens with Thug shooting the rival gang members with a 9mm. Cap after cap, the gun busts the bullets into the air and towards the gang members. Thug's inner voice begins calling him a murdered. The scene opens back up in the hospital, Thug is back to the current time as the flash backs are over. They are pumping his stomach to rid the alcohol. Da Thug begins screaming as he begins shaking again... The scene fades with a clear blurr and Thug having severe physical problems on the hospital bed with doctors and nurses rushing all over, around him. Outroduction: It's clearly obvious that something is very wrong with Da Thug, but what? Is it something to do with his guilt and mental well-being? Or is it deeper, like the alcohol? Tune in next time as 'Thug-Vision TV' is scheduled to bring another promo in the near future.
