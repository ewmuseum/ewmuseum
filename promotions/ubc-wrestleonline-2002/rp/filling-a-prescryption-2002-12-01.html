:::Filling a Prescryption:::

1 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12.01.02 :::Silence speaks words. Nothing has to be said to show your fear in a given situation. To show that, after all -- you don't really have the guts you were so adament about having. So is the case with Outcast. Sure, he may show up for Jurisdiction and be the man -- but where are the guts for glory? Where are the men that used to stand up for themselves day in and day out. Aparently those days are gone. And now we're stuck with big muscles with little persona. But that's okay. Really, all that matters is that we poor alcohol down Tommy's throat and let him do the rest. He's a big mouth oozing of charisma just waiting to get out of that half dead brain of his. He's used to jealousy from grammar ridden, psycho flakes like Brett Wayne. He's used to having people beg for his partnership, just trying to be a part of something special. But Tommy is his own man. He'll knock you on your ass before you even realize what hit you. I don't expect you to be scared. I know the balls that everyone in UBC must have to be complete brawlers. And respect is given. But, like I said -- I don't expect you to be scared. However, when good ole Tommy makes you start countin' the lights.. I wouldn't be surprised if a little fear crept into your lifeless skull::: :::But forget about fear for a second. We've discovered the root to Tommy's charisma. His fury. The root is found within alcohol. Now previously, Abner Estrada was forced to find an every day looking sort of guy to pick up his drug 'prescription'. Tommy was taken to the site where Abner was to pick up the prescription. As we see now, a black BMW. We slowly zoom in from the side. The window is cracked. A crushed up beer can comes flying out the window. The door opens to the car. Tommy Grady stands, enormous, gazing across the landscape. There sits a large house upon a hill with stairs leading to it starting directly in front of Tommy and continuing about thirty yards to the front doorstep. Trees surround what is seen of the house as it sits about forty yards wide and two stories tall. Tommy takes a deep breath and seems to be concentrating before Abner yells out of the BMW::: Abner Estrada - Before you head out guzzle this down.. :::Abner tosses Tommy a bottle labeled Gatorade::: Tommy Grady - Okay.... *chugs the bottle* ... blah! Do they make 'Gatorade Shit' now? Abner Estrada - That wasn't Gatorade.. it was one fourth water and three fourths Beefeater vodka.. Tommy Grady - Didn't taste like beef to me.. so why do I have to fill your prescription with this rich guy, again? Abner Estrada - He doesn't want to be caught.. you see there's this thing in life called law.. Tommy Grady - Where a Jurisdiction finds you guilty or not guilty.. Abner Estrada - Atta'boy, Tommy.. Tommy Grady - WOO! :::Pats himself on the back. Tommy steps out of the car and walks towards the front of the mansion. A man opens the door as Tommy approaches. This man is pretty damn big. Compared to Tommy.. the man appears to be 6'11'.. maybe 350 pounds of muscle. He's stacked. Tommy takes off his glasses and appears to be taken back by the size of this mansion. He follows the bodyguard slowly.. looking around the mansion as he walks. Finally the bodyguard motions for Tommy to speed up and he does so. He quickly strides towards the kitchen area where there is a table. Tommy turns the corner and the rich dude stands.. shaking Tommy's hand with a half cocky smile on his face. He motions for Tommy to take a seat and he does so. The man recrosses his ankle over his knee. Tommy mocks the man.. trying to fit in, more than likely. Hell, who wouldn't.. filthy rich bastard surrounded by two men that equal three of you if put together. Quite threatening.. but why is Tommy here in the first place.. and what IS 'here'? Welp.. we're about to find out, ladies and gentlemen.. However.. that Rich Dude has quite an accent. It's hard to understand::: Tommy Grady - Nice place ya got here.. Rich Dude - Ya fook'n rot! Tommy Grady - Um.. Rich Dude - Dowin' to bees'neese! Tommy Grady - Huh? Rich Dude - Bees'neese! Dowin' to eat! Tommy Grady - OH! Business.. alright.. um.. Abner W. Estrada sent me down here to have his prescription filled out.. and.. Rich Dude - GEEVE EAT TO ME! Tommy Grady - Damn dude, calm da' firetruck down! Rich Dude - NO! Yooooou com don'! :::Tommy gives the man a look of confusion. He shakes his head and 'geets don' to bees'neese'. He hands the paper over the table to the rich dude. The rich dude takes a look at it and nods his head.. he turns and speaks a different language to one of his bodyguard dudes and then the bodyguard dude scattles out of the room like the cool, stupindous dude that he is.. alright.. nuh of that. He comes back with a sack of something::: Rich Dude - And.. some'ting fo' yo'sef? Tommy Grady - Uh.. I wouldn't mind an ice cold coke, or somethin.. Rich Dude - Coke eat ease! :::Tommy smiles at one of the bodyguards nervously. The other comes back shortly with another sack. The rich dude glares over at Tommy. He pulls out a paper rolled up and points it at Tommy::: Rich Dude - You won-uh.. 'smoke eat up wit' me?' Tommy Grady - I'd rather no-- :::Tommys memory catches him as he remembers the words that Abner Estrada spoke to him just a week ago. 'Be like your opponent and you will succeed'. With those words.. Tommy speaks to himself.. not meaning for anyone to hear him. The camera, however, catches his words::: Tommy Grady - *quietly* .. well.. Outcast DOES appear to be smoking SOMETHING.. but it ain't cigarettes.. and I definitely won't be suckin any of it to be like him.. :::Tommy shrugs and looks up at the Rich Dude::: Tommy Grady - Sure.. why the fiztuck not! :::The rich dude smiles and passes the 'cigarette' over to Tommy. He takes it a holds it up to his mouth::: Tommy Grady - I've never damaged my body like this, so you'll have to take it easy on me.. :::Rich dude smiles again and shakes his head. He lights the end of the 'cigarette' and Tommy inhales. He is taken back by the taste of this shit. His eyes get wide as Tommy blows the smoke out::: Tommy Grady - Damn dude! This is the shiznit! Rich Dude - Eat da SHEET! Tommy Grady - I'm not really into eating cotton.. Rich Dude - NO NO! The SHEET! Tommy Grady - Mmhm.. that's what ya said. Gimme that shit back.. Rich Dude - No! You geet one heet! Dat's eat! Tommy Grady - Just one more, man! C'mon.. we're homies! Rich Dude - I s'pose.. ONE more! :::Tommy grasps the 'cigarette' and takes a big smoke of it. The rich dude appears to be angry as he snatches it back::: Rich Dude - You son of a beech! Tommy Grady - No, sir. My pa is Robert Grady.. why? Rich Dude - No! You's EASE a beech! Tommy Grady - A beach? Um.. I suppose if you mean sexy by beech.. Rich Dude - You hit my mary-g-wana too much! Tommy Grady - Sir, I never had sexual relations with ANY woman by that name.. and if I did without knowing.. how can I help it? I mean, with this body I-- Rich Dude - AH! DAT'S EAT! YOU E-DER PAY UP OR I TAKE YA'OUT! Tommy Grady - Are you hitting on me? Rich Dude - No! I not hit on you! I take you out! Tommy Grady - Well, normally I don't accept invitations, but.. I like you.. as long as we don't go to McDonalds.. they always screw up my order.. plain means NO ONIONS, DAMNIT! Rich Dude - Geeve me my fookin mon-e! Tommy Grady - OH!.. uh.. how much do I owe you? Rich Dude - Yo' o-der cost twenty five hundred dolla's.. Tommy Grady - Good LORD that's a lot of money! Rich Dude - Abna' Estrada o-der very much mary-g-wana! You o-der very much coke. 'Dat equal very much moo-lah! Tommy Grady - *to camera* Luckily he gave me a blank check.. *to the Rich Dude* Who should I make it out to? Rich Dude - T.D. Lord.. Tommy Grady - Yes, Lord.. HAHA! :::Tommys comedy is not mutually felt::: Tommy Grady - *handing the check to T.H. Lord* And here ya are! Rich Dude - You take eat easy on deese sheet, ok? Tommy Grady - How'd YOU know I'm a coke-a-holic? Gosh.. :::Tommy shakes the mans hand once more before turning away. The two men carry his sacks for him towards the front door. Tommy walks through the front door and has his bags packed into the passenger side of his green '96 Neon. The bodyguard closes the door and Tommy hops into the driver side. Looking into the camera he begins to speak as he begins to pull out of the driveway::: Tommy Grady - It's amazing how expensive this stuff is nowadays. I mean.. for one, this Mary Wana prostitute must work wonders if her and a pack of coca-cola cost two and a half thousand dollars. If that ain't a lot of money.. I dunno what is! But people like Outcast are probably used to paying that much for soliciting sex and buying.. company. But I don't have that problem. Outcast is the man with the problem. Not only does he have social problems, but he has some major head problems, as well. And now I see that his problems have just gotten worse. Tommy Grady has been launched in front him now. He must step into the ring with the stallion. This poor world must be out to make you suffer, my friend. You just can't catch a break. Picked to go all the way to the world title, but you face an absolute road block. Ah well.. I don't really like you anyways. I'm not sure if it's the way you act, the way you talk, the way you look.. hell it may even be the way you smell.. I'm not too sure. But let it be said -- I don't like you. And come Saturday there's no shimmy, jive, or shake that you can do to escape the punishment I'm gonna be layin' on you. Say your prays.. make sure you're right with God -- cuz come Saturday.. you'll see me. You'll be able to admire me for a just a moment -- especially my extremely sexy phisique, and then you'll be countin' the lights! And there ain't a damn thing that you or Damien can do about it. :::Good Lord! (no pun intended) The alcohol worked again::: Tommy Grady - This past week has really been testing my patience. I mean, I was told to hold my tongue until I got a response back from you, Outcast, but it just appears there's no fight in you. Then I told myself, well -- it's probably not him. He's probably trying to cut a promo but he's just so damn ugly that the camera lens keeps breaking.. then I thought -- that's pretty unlikely. So then I thought, well he's got the mind of a little scared child -- maybe that yellow patch on his head is big bird trying to get out of his poor, pathetic soul. Nope. Not even big bird is that frightened. He has a hairy elephant as a friend, for God's sake. Then it came to me. That's just it. There's nothing wrong. The only thing wrong is that you're angry at the world because you're very first match in the UBC is against the cream de la crim. The best of the best. You don't want your first match to be a talley in the loss column. After your silent treatment -- your attempt at intimidating the UBC wrestlers into being afraid of you. Well, guess what. From day one I've been after you -- what does that tell you? You don't intimidate me. You don't even intimidate Brett Wayne, however pathetic he is. So if you want to keep your jaw strapped, and your teeth clinched -- that's fine with me. My confidence is oozing and there ain't a damn thing that you can do about it, Outcast. So if I don't see your ugly mug on UBC Television before Jurisdiction -- it'll just be like smashing an ugly little spider. But the ugly little object's gonna be your face.. and it's gonna be a hell of a lot more brutal than one stomp. See you at Jurisdiction, pal.. :::Tommy turns on his turn signal and cuts in front of a jeep. He is taking an exit on the right side of an interstate. He looks back at the camera and sums up this interview::: Tommy Grady - Well.. I gotta get this stuff back to Abner. But Outcast.. you really are gonna get it this Saturday. For all the hatred that I've built up for your character and for the pain your lack of promos have caused me.. I DARE ya to doubt me. You WILL get the ass kickin of a lifetime Saturday.. And, folks.. I tell no lie. So don't doubt it.. cuz it WILL happen.. and that ain't a promise.. it's a guaran.. DAMN.. tee.. :::The camera slowly turns to the front of the truck. The scene slowly begins to fade as Tommy drives off into the distance with a long road ahead of him ::..
