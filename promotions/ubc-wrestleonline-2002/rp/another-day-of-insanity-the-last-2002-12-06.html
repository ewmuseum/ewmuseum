'Another day of Insanity' The Last...

6 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12-6-02 Brett Wayne is Truely 'The Insane One' of UBC. The past few days was the prove of his Insanity. There is only one day between Brett and his debute match at Jurisdiction. Will today be different for 'The Insane One' or will things still be the same. The scene opens up inside an unfamiler house. With boxes of different things packed away in them are scattered throughout the house. The camera turns and shows a couple of pictures of two little boys in a wrestling ring. One boy is a couple inches bigger and wider than the other. The bigger boy in the picture is wearing a familer pair of glasses and shorts. The smaller boy wears only tight red pants with small black boots. The camera then turns around to a door being opened. 'I think this will be a new start for me and you' says Brett as he carries a couple of boxes stacked on one another and his head still bandaged up from his fall. Mike is seen with a suitcase in each hand and he is holding some papers of some sort in his mouth. 'Let me help you out' Brett says as he lays down the boxes softly and runs over and grabs the suitcases from Mike. 'How much did this place cost?' ask Mike as he starts looking through the papers. 'Nothing right now, I charged it to Xaiver.' Brett smiles as he turns and lays down the suitcases. 'Does Xaiver know about this?' Brett looks up and smiles 'No not yet but I'm sure he will when the bill arrives in his mail.' 'He'll fire your ass for doing shit like that' Mike gets angry as he walks around looking throughout the house. 'Maybe...but I doubt it, I might get a little ass chewing but oh well' Brett smiles and follows Mike. 'This is a very nice house though and plus its in the hometown of UBC.' Mike says as he starts walking upstairs. They both walk upstairs and look around as they see dust and spiderwebs covering the place up. They walk into the bathroom seeing that the tub is crusted over and so is the toilet. 'This place needs some major cleaning up' says Mike. 'Yea it does but It will have to wait, first we need to clear out a room and bring in our equipment, I need to get straight to work, I have a very important match tomorrow night and with my head the way it is I need all the practice I can get. 'I can't beleive your still going to fight with your head the way it is, you Insane.' Mike then realizes what he said 'Oh thats right you are 'The Insane One' stupid of me to forget.' 'Thats right Insane is my middle name, now enough chit chat lets go unload our equipment' Brett says as he begins walking down stairs. The stairs crack as Brett walks down each one of them, the house shakes as Mike moves around upstairs. 'This house needs some repairing also' Brett shouts to Mike. The sound of Mike walking down the stairs is heard as Brett walks outside. 'What do we get first?' Brett ask as Mike walks outside. 'Just wait for a minute, I called some guys they should be here in a minute to unload all of this stuff. You just go get a little rest before you start going at it all night long.' 'Ok...wake me up in about three or four hours.' Brett says as he walks back into the house. Mike begins unload a couple of things as he thinks to himself. I hope things do turn out different in our new home, maybe this is what we need was just a small change. Maybe now Brett will think more about his fights than the people that surround him. Who knows about that kid anyways? A 2001 red Dodge pickup pulls up with a black brush gaurd on the front. Three men step out and walk towards Mike. One man where white sleeveless shirt with black mesh shorts and a backwards hat. The middle guy isn't wearing a shirt and only has orange mesh shorts on with a gold necklace and black sunglasses on. The last guy is the biggest and he only wears a black tanktop with black mesh shorts and a black backwards hat with a gold chain around his kneck. 'Hey guys, glad you could make it in time. I need these things unpacked and inside quickly.' Mike says as he walks in. 'You heard the boss lets go guys.' says the biggest guy. The three men begin unloading the equipment as the scene fades to Brett laying down in his bed. Brett seems to be talking in his sleep. GET UP! NO DAMNIT NO! I WON'T BE DEFEATED NEVER! Brett toss and turns as he kicks the covers off of him as they fall in the floor. -SCREAMS- Brett jumps up quickly and starts feeling his body. 'Its just another Nightmare, easly forgotten' Brett says as he lays back down. Why do guys like Nightmare think there simply the best. I'm the one that has the right to think that, look around you'll see I'm the most dedicated and hard working fighter there is in the UBC. I just want some god damn respect from people like Nightmare and by god I'll get in come tomorrow everyone will see Nightmare respect me, oh yes! Everyone will see the true power of UBC tomorrow night, I'll make sure of it. Brett lays back down and the scene fades as the sound of people walking up and down the stairs can be heard.
