============================= GCWF Info and Application v.1.1 ============================= =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= Introduction =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= Hello and thank you for reading this packet of information about the GCWF(Global Championship Wrestling Federation) =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= Rules and Regulations =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= #1.-This is a "booked" league that means that you have to roleplay.You must roleplay at least once a week or you'll be thrown out. #2-E-Wrestling is supposed to be fun.So if you have a problem with a member,tell me about it. #3-If you have to go on vacation,e-mail me to tell me so I don't book any matches for you. =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= GCWF Show Information =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= There are 2 shows a week in the GCWF.There will be House Shows now and later.House Shows is a card in which all matches are summarized since pro wrestling house shows aren't televized neither is the GCWF's House Show.There will be a special rarely like a card on Thursday,etc,etc.Here are the 2 shows to take place in the GCWF.... -GCWF Monday Extravaganza- (This show is purely "Nitro-esque"it's a normal show with many suprises the glitz and glamour of WCW Monday Nitro.Also takes place in the big name arenas...Written By:[name withheld]) -GCWF Saturday Night Special (PURELY HARDCORE!!!!!!!Takes place in the GCWF Arena in Hartford,Connecticut.Truly without a doubt exciting and EVERYTHING HAPPENS!!!!Written By:[name withheld],[name withheld],and [name withheld].) =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= GCWF Championships =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= There are 3 titles in the GCWF.................. GCWF World Heavyweight Championship GCWF United States Heavyweight Championship GCWF Television Championship GCWF World Tag Team Championship =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= GCWF Pay Per View Events =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= There's a pay per view once a week.We do everything original!We don't have the same PPV name after each year unless it's one of the big cards...... The First GCWF Pay Per View will be..... GCWF Brawl In Boston! LIVE!In The Fleet Center July,27th,1997 {NOTE:The GWA and ECWF merged together to form the GCWF.} [{The Main Event}] GCWF World Heavyweight Title Match! ECWF World Heavyweight Champion "Kold Blood"Kevin Kage vs. GWA World Champion "Mr.Hall of Fame"Tommy Van Dam =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= Roleplays =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= Roleplays will be sent out once we get about 10 or more in an update with "Prime Time Wrestling Update".This league is BCCed(Blind Carbon Copy)so you have to send the roleplays to me so I can send it out to the organization.This way,your mailbox doesn't fill up with stuf. =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= GCWF Staff =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= GCWF Co-President-[name withheld](RJGMS [detail withheld] ) GCWF Co President-[name withheld]( [detail withheld] ) GCWF Vice President-[name withheld] aka Brad B.Extremely ( [detail withheld] ) GCWF Head Match Writer-Now Hiring GCWF Match Writer-Now Hiring GCWF Match Writer-Now Hiring =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= Application =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= You may have 2 Singles and a tag team!
