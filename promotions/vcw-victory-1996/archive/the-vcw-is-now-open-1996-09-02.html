Victory Championship Wrestling — Founding Thread

"The VCW is now Open!"

2-3 September 1996
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hello fellow handlers! I would like to invite you all to join the VCW, better known as Victory Championship Wrestling. We won't be starting just yet, since there is still some planing to do. Not much mind you. We should kick things off with our first card in about 2 weeks time.
We are a very seriouse e-wrestling league. As you can see, we won't start till we got everything ready. Our staff now has 5 writers, so cards won't be late. (Well not always, since it's almost impossible to get cards out always on time.) That's why our weekly card doesn't have a scheduled release date. It comes out during the week from either
Monday to Friday.
In addition to the weekly card, we also have an interview show, that will come out a few days before the card. Thw show will have your flashes, and line-ups for the next couple cards.
Depending on how many handlers join, these will be the VCW belts: - The World VCW Heavyweight Title - The World VCW Light-Heavyweight Title (Only if enough light-weights are in the league) - The World VCW Online Title - The World VCW Tag Team Titles - The World VCW Online tag Team CHampions (should there be enought tag teams) -Another possible belt would be an international belt, if enough non north-americans are on the VCW roster. What else, oh! Our method of determining the winners. Most leagues use one method of deciding a winner. The VCW is a bit different. We use 3 different styles, all rolled into one. You can say we are a simmed/booked/dice kind a league. Not only that, but we use money as well. (not real money, since this is a free league, but you aready knew that). The booking is there if you have a match, and your opponant does not role-play. No sence in simming there. The dice is for how succesful a sneak attack ends up, and how much your fined for doing that. And the simming is there for...well duh! ;)
You want more of a background? Well, the president (that would be me) has had over 2 years of e-wrestling experience. I think after watching and being in so many feds that don't even last a month, I should do something about it, hance the VCW!
If your interested in joining what could be a great fed, please don't hesitate to visit our web site, and read the VCW Handbook, and fill out an application. Our offical web site is located at:
http://www.axess.com/rudedog/vcw/ As a word of advise, please read the Handbbok carefuly, so you can understand how everything at the VCW works. The Handbook is rather long, but easy to understand, even for the beginners of e-wrestling.
Hope to hear from some of you! Thanks for your time! Marcus Prime [detail withheld]
