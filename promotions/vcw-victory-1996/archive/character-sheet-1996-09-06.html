Victory Championship Wrestling — Character-Creation Form

"Say wa? VCW? What ya talking about?"

6 September 1996

That's right, the Victory Championship Wrestling, where victory means everything! But before I get to the sheet for you to fill out, I want to be clear on a few things. I know how a lot of you are very picky about what new feds to join, in fear of them folding in a month or two. How do I know? Simple, I'm the same way. But the VCW is not the same as any other new fed out there.
We got 6 members on our writing staff, now how many first time feds can say they have that? All are great writers and extremly excited to bring you great cards. We have one card a week, but with 11-13 matches on each.
The VCW means business! We have been planing the VCW for over a month now, and we are ready to set our mark in the e-wrestling community.
The NCW, NCWF, FoPEW and the FWLI are great feds, and it's in our best effort to make the VCW just as good as they are.
Here's the sheet to fill out, but please also go visit the VCW webpage
(URL at the end of the sheet) where a handbook has been made with all the rules, and info about the VCW. Another small note, even if you might know what this sheet is based on, the VCW is far from only being a simmed fed. We use booking methods and dice as well. The handbook as all the 411 about that.
Standard Format (Please use this format when submitting wrestlers) Your E-Mail: Wrestler Name: Tag Partner: (if in a tag team) Tag Team Name: (optional) Primary Skill: (one of the 11 listed just below) Secondary Skill: (one of the 11 listed just below) Class: (Brawler, Technical, etc...) Alignment: (Face/Heel/Neutral) Hometown: Height: Weight: Age:: Entrance Music:: Description: Ring Entrance, any pre-match rituals, Special traits or characteristics, etc...
History: past feuds, feds wrestled in, etc... Manager: Desc physical/attitude, etc... (optional)) General Strategy: optional Now for the attributes: - You have a total of 125 attribute points - No attribute can be over 50 points - Your primary skill must be at least 25 points - Your secondary skill must be at least 20 points, but not more than the primary one. - All other attributes must be less than your secondary points. Strength: Toughness: Conditioning: Intelligence: Cheating: Brawling: Technical: Mat Wrestling: Aerial: Quickness: Teamwork: (Total - 125 pts) Moves (10-20) Half from classs Finisher (1) Please include description of the move, what it is, location in the ring it is executed from, etc...
Notes: Any other info you may want to include. (optional) *if submiting a tag team, please fill out 2 sheets. One for each member. The VCW webpage is located at: http://members.tripod.com/~vcw/ Hope you all decide to sign up! [name withheld] [detail withheld]
