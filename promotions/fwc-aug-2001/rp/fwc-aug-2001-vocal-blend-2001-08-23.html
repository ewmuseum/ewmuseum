Basic contents are included in this room: Double-beds off to the side, recliner in the center of things, and a bolted down television directed towards the television... As the camera pans out, Jack Armstrong is seen lying facefirst on his bed. Somehow, realizing the camera is on he sits up, though his head is lowered solemnly.]
JA: Somebody finally knocked me off... And of all people it was Trevor Smith! [Jack ruffles his messy hair while adjusting the collar of his disgusting tee-shirt, you can obviously tell what he ate for breakfast, lunch, and dinner just by looking at the contents on his shirt. Ugh...]
JA: Yeah, I'm a little messy right now but I have more important things to worry about. Like my reputation at hand..
Pretty soon, people are gonna be stepping up trying to knock me again... My reputation as untouchable is diminishing and it's all because of that
punk-bitch Trevor Smith! [Jack continues looking down but it seems as if a fire ignited in his voice.] JA: Because of Trevor, people are gonna be attacking me.. hoping to gain a little fame off my bad seed. Bitches are smart too. Trying to loot the building before it collapses completely, I can't say I blame then except I do! Because
I'm that goddamned building! Oh Trevor, there's gonna be hell to pay Saturday night.
[Jack pauses for effect.] JA: Saturday night... I'm gonna make you scream like your mama behind the mailman's truck. [Jack lets out a sigh as the scene fades out.] --------------------------------- Trevor Smith --------------------------------- [The scene slowly increases in clarity ... it's evening time somewhere outside of Orlando, a breeze from the local river sends a candy wrapper skittering across the pavement; the howl of an ambulance echoes in the street.
A figure is seen walking down this vacant road, alongside the river as something draped across his shoulder glitters and reflects what little light is left of the evening... He pauses, nodding at the camera and looking out over the river.]
VO: Come over here... I want you to see this. [The man rubs his bald head and as the camera zooms in it's quite apparent this is the man that dethroned the former Armstrong champion.. Trevor Smith.]
TS: [Dangling the title above his head.] Jack Armstrong's pride and joy. [Trevor stares directly into the camera lens, smiling wickedly.] TS: My hair was once many of the things I took pride in and you took that away from me, Jack.
And did you ever think I wasn't going to retaliate? You didn't honestly think I was going to take it up the ass and smile about it, did you? [An ominous smile breaks across his face.] TS: I hope my little victory Saturday sent you an ego-check. If not.. maybe this will.. [Trevor winds back, title in hand and flings it into the river!] TS: Consider this title officially retired. Up next: Your career, Jack-O. [Static.] --------------------------------- "Big Willy" William Christopher --------------------------------- [The shot fades in, uncovering none other than FWC's pimp-extraordinare, "Big
Willy" William Christopher. Decked out in atleast ten lbs. of gold, a silk
T-shirt which puts emphasis on his fat belly and of course to lovely hoe's hanging off his nut-- err... arm. As the camera fades out a FWC backdrop is noticeably evident.]
WC: The Doc iz upset 'bout me costin' him his 'lil match at Scorcher and he should be, yo. [William chuckles while his belly shakes for a good ten seconds.] WC: Yup. I hear dat punk moanin' and groanin' bout dis and dat and I tink ta myself being da smart pimp dat Ah am..
[Big Willy taps his skull as he nods.] WC: I hear Spence tellin' me bout dis big congregation... [Big word.] WC: And I says ta mahself, I says.. maybe me and dat overgrown freak should dance so I tells Spence and he be like, "Fa sho, Will." So now we goin' ta dance..
[The Big Will pauses, possibly thinking of what to say next.] BW: But first I got 'dat bald midget, Trevor Smith.. I'm gonna eat dat sucka for breakfast then maybe have mah way with his momma.. heh heh.
Trevor, Saturday Scorcher, you're goin' ta have dah biggest pimp on yo hands... [Close-up on the pimp.] BW: Can ya handle it? [The girls giggle as the scene fades to black.] --------------------------------- Dr. Killjoy --------------------------------- [Scene fades in to the FWC Interview set where moments later, Dr. Killjoy drudges through, his mind consumed with thoughts of random acts of violence and heart-surgery. The Doctor has a large bandage on his head where he was
Adrenalized by E J Drivven... he looks irate as he begins pacing back and forth.] Kj: Too _F'N_ long. [Killjoy sneers at the camera while rubbing his fists ominously.] Kj: Wilson tells me I have to wait _ATLEAST_ two weeks before William and I can wrestle officially in a ring..
[The Doc sighs though oddly, looks up at the camera with a grim smile.] Kj: Though Saturday, I get to have a little appetizer to hold me over.. and that appetizer comes in the form of Jack Armstrong. Which should be loads of fun for me...
I saw his loss at Scorcher and I have to say, I'm pretty ashamed of him. He's s'possed to have Mr. T sized muscles and loses his title to who? Trevor Smith.
Man, I think I'm gonna have more fun tearing Jack apart then my actually match with William.
[Killjoy rubs his week-old stubble thoughtfully.] Kj: Naw... [The Doctor chuckles as the scene fades out.] --------------------------------- The Crash --------------------------------- [Distraught, dressed from head to toe in "Crash" Merchandise, Casey Robinson stands stationary in one of the FWC Headquarters' corridors. He appears to be
THINKING?!?! ...After a few dull seconds, an unknown page walks onto the screen.]
P: HEY BUDDY! CR: Hmm. [Crash scratches his chin.] P: What's botherin' you Crash-boy-oy? CR: Well... where's gas come from? P: Petroleum. CR: Nah. Your bum gas. Y'know. P: Huh? CR: Where_does_gas_from_your_bum_come_from? P: Uhh... [The page stares a blank look at Casey. A moment passes, and Robinson brushes past, forgetting the page is even there. He's pretty preoccupied, but manages to turn and address the camera:]
CR: I'd say things are pretty hectic around here. Spence sure doesn't run a tight ship. Always holed up in his office if you know what I mean! *WINK*
Speaking of, did you notice KJ's recent pleasure-bath with the group of transvestites? Oh, that's right... Private video only! 21.95 folks, call me.
I'll arrange it. ...Wait. I don't want to steal any of Big Willy's thunder. Speaking of, did you hear about Trevor shitting thunder? He took that bugger Jack-O Armstrong the
(BEEP) OUT!
But lets get to my issues. My degenerative father decided it time to sick my overconfident assclown brother on me. And let me tell you Fletchie, it's payback time. No more nookies!
[Fade.] --------------------------------- "Sexy" Fletch Johnson --------------------------------- [The camera opens up outside of the Ft. Love Airport in just outside of the
Dallas / Fort Worth area in Texas. The crowds are busy coming and going as the summer is coming to an end; kids are eager to get back to college, parents are eager to get back to homes with their screaming children awaiting them, and where "SexY" Fletch Johnson is just eager to get the hell out of Texas. Sure he was born and raised in the Dallas area, and lived there his entire life, but honestly, who can take that sort of heat every single day? Well,... Despite the tried and true warrior that "SexY" is, he just can't take it. So with air plane ticket in hand and FWC duffel bag slung over his shoulder, he is seen getting out of a cab, throwing a pair of twenty dollar bills into the cab.]
"Sexy" Fletch Johnson: "Hey buddy, thanks for the ride, keep the change." Cabbie: "But Meesta. Meesta-----" "Sexy" Fletch Johnson: "Just call me a good Samaritan, I didn't mean to cut you off mid-sentence, but
I really would love to chat with you more, my good Iranian man, but I really must be catching my plane to...ick...Tampa Bay!"
[Fletch turns around to walk away as the cabby starts swearing.] Cabbie: "Mother of Allah! That goat lover owed me 54 dollars! Keep the change my ass!" "Sexy" Fletch Johnson: "You know, you try and help some people get somewhere in their lives and what happens? They start swearing in you in Persian or what not. Definitely not nice, nor pristine.
Fletch is here, back in his hometown. Actually, Crash, it's OUR hometown, huh? Heh. It's weird to be talking about_US_as family, but then again, who would've ever thought our paths would've crossed again. After the way you stormed out of the house when you couldn't take the_HEAT_anymore, I never thought I would see you again. I had heard that you were around, and I heard that you were actually trying to make a name for yourself as a wrestler. Boy, were they right when they said_TRYING_to_MAKE_a name for yourself.
Speaking of heat. Being here in Texas actually reminds me of the FWC locker room. Full of heat. But the heat in the locker room isn't like this sort of heat. No, not at all. The locker rooms are usually air conditioned, at least mine is. But the heat back their is more like the humidity. You could cut the proverbial heat with a knife. I go back to the locker room and guys just start to glare, and you know what... They NEED to be looking at me. You know why?
Because I actually am a wrestler. These guys are fools. These guys, they're some of worst looking wrestlers, skill wise, that I have ever seen. And that is why, I am issuing, an open challenge. Not just to one wrestler...TO ALL THE
WRESTLERS.. You see, I don't care how many matches I have to wrestle to get my brother in the ring, I will wrestle them, and that is not going to be a problem. I know that for every wrestler that is in the back that thinks they can beat me, there is maybe one ounce of talent, and the rest is just pure adrenaline rushing. Now don't get me wrong, adrenaline can lead to many good things in life, many of us remember the first time we got to first base, in Crash's life, it seems like yesterday, BECAUSE IT WAS YESTERDAY, and we remember the adrenaline rush that gave us the balls to do it. We credit the adrenaline with most of the reasoning behind it, but more than anything else, it is the thing that puts you over the edge. The only problem is, it will only get you so far. So when the guys step into the ring with me and are expecting to have the adrenaline push them over the edge, and over me in a match, they are going to be in a world of hurt.
Especially because "Sexy" Fletch Johnson has a one track mind and that is getting to my brother. You know what Crash? I hope you got the balls to step out during the open challenge, because it is going to be Fletch's time to shine brotha', and you know what else?
I'm gonna' have momma bake us up a whole big batch of blue berry muffins, just for you little brother."
[Fletch chuckles and then walks off to his plane as the camera slowly fades to black.] --------------------------------- "The Rush" E J Drivven --------------------------------- [From the cloying, oppressive gloom the voices ring out. The phrasing of the
"exchange of views" between the two is well-practiced and filled to the brim with a very genuine dislike for one another.]
"YES! Drivven is standing behind Killjoy and the Doc doesn't even know it! He's still inviting Big Willy to come down!"
"Obviously his finisher wasn't as effective as he thought." [An unholy crash of a deadweight hitting unforgiving canvas then rises into being. The swirling boos/cheers of the unseen audience are momentarily forced into the background as the impact reverberates around the void.]
"MY GAWD! E J DRIVVEN JUST EXECUTED A SNAP BACKDROP DRIVER!" "He just Adrenalized Killjoy." [In a manner that is very similar to a mantra being vocalized by the masses that worship, a multi-harmonized count takes priority over all.]
ONE!!! TWO!!!! THREEEEEEEEEEEEEEEE!!!! [... And from this audio-only chaos, there comes a gradual visual clarity as the figure of the aforementioned EJ Drivven is seen. His ripped 6'5" frame is standing in front of the generic FWC backdrop, with his head bowed. It is an effect that only gives more emphasis to the long, dampened straggles of twisted black hair that obscures every facet of his facial features.
Low and familiarly guttural his musing are.] EJD: Last week, the FWC saw first hand just a sample of the furious violence that defines the one named "The Rush". Of course, some may say that were it not for the intervention of others into my match, then I would have been resigned to the realms of humiliating defeat ... To they that find it necessary to agree with this form of delusional falsehood, I say this;
[In a split second of almost supernatural speed, the man-monster from Parts
Unknown snaps his head upwards to confront the camera. His piercing blue eyes are afire with savage intent, as his chin goatee serves to more effectively underline the sneer that sits unwavering upon his lips.]
EJD: You're living in a f*BLEEP!*-ing DreamLand, sh*BLEEP!*-sticks! As was seen in the last Scorcher, I not only got up from a patented 'Kj Chokeslam', but I duly sent that pukeass to the nearest chiropractor with the destructive effects of my
Adrenalized Driver. [Unable to control himself, Drivven, clad in his usual black denim jeans/Dr. Marten boots ensemble, begins to pace back and forth along the soundstage. Now only choosing to glance in the direction of the lens upon his every turn, EJ continues on with his "venting".]
EJD: ... and now the 'powers', in their infinite wisdom, have made it clear that I am to fight at their next card. This time it is against a character called The Gremlin.
However, knowing this leads me wonder if the man in question happens to be the pseudo-legendary warrior from the EMWC, or but a pretender to a name that is synonymous with warring excellence.
Well, I promise you this, Gremlin; If you are indeed the man who is the cornerstone to 'Extreme entertainment', then I will extend you every respect before I deliver my unrelenting 'Spiral Aggression' upon you ... But if you are merely an irreverent imposter, then ...
[In a whirl of hostility that ceases his lateral movement, the lead vocalist of
Jacob's Eternal Sin glares at the lens one last time. In response, the view pans forward to gain a tight headshot of the snarling Drivven. His voice is now no more than a whispered hiss of undiluted rage.]
EJD: ... you're an instantaneous dead man walking; no 'ifs' 'ands' or 'buts'. Life is pain ... And pain is defined by Jacob's Damnation ... [From here, a return fade to ominous raven is duly realised.] [End.]
