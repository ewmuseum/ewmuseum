Jack Armstrong is perched upon a chair within the FWC Interview Set. Armstrong is clad in wrestling tights and a sleeveless white shirt. A grin set upon his face, he gently taps the Armstrong Championship on his lap.]
JA: *wink* It's your favorite FWC Champion, Jack-O Armstrong. And let me start off with this: Mismatches are commonplace, and often times evident before they even begin.
Take for example, Julio Cesar Chavez. Chavez was over the hill, a shadow of the man he once was. But he insisted he fight Kostya Tszyu did he not? ... Oh, you're not familiar? Let me rehash the fight for you.
[Jack clears his throat.] Chavez's reasons for the fight: "to go out with pride." Instead, he went out black and blue, full of bruises, and with a loud whimper that echoed through the arena. His flesh nothing more than goo, he was pummeled unmercifully by

It was one of many classic mismatches that are so prevalent today. But you're asking yourself, Why's Jack-O talking 'bout this? I'll tell you.
[Armstrong squares his shoulders and raises his arm, pumping his bicep.] I'm billing Mr. Clean versus Jack Armstrong as the mismatch of the century ... and my reasons are apparent. [Jack flexes his arm once more as the scene fades to black.] --------------------------------- Trevor Smith --------------------------------- [A slight increase in visuality, and Trevor Smith is the focus of the shot, wiping his face with a damp towel. His surroundings: the FWC Training Facility.
FWC Wrestlers, some known, others forever to be left to tend to their meaningless existence, surround him, each one focused entirely on the lifting and lowering of dumbbells and other odd-looking machines.
Trevor is clad in his normal wrestling attire, a look of exhaustion apparent upon his face. Nevertheless, he rubs his cleanly shaven head for good luck, and faintly smiles addressing the camera.]
TS: They say it's not the big that eat the small, but it's the fast that eat the slow. [Wink.] TS: Now, just because we're not eating dinner, and I'll have whatever you're drinking by the way, it still applies. Sure, Armstrong's a muscle bound freak who's constantly jakked up on steroids ... but he's just big, and you know the bigger they are, the harder they fall. Whereas, I'm fast. I eat lightning, and shit thunder.
[Trevor's face lights up with a broad grin.] TS: Now where've I heard that before? ... Awe, anyway. The synthetic hormone himself, Jack Armstrong and I will square off in armwrestling competition, main-event style, for the Armstrong Championship this Saturday on Scorcher.
I've been denied once, not again. The second times the charm right? ... Maybe it's the third, but no matter. [Pause, and then in all the seriousness that Trevor can muster: TS: Jack ol' boy, let's swing some cats. But that's second. My eyes always be lookin' to the future, y'know I'm a business man... but I can't be overlookin' this Sexy Fletch fella.

Creased khaki's, polished shoes, a button-down shirt and of course his bloody apron. His usually tidy hair is a mess and there are various rips scattered about his apron.]
Kj: That bastard William Christopher! We had an agreement. I specifically told him that I wasn't into kinky stuff, and what does he do?

--------------------------------- "Sexy" Fletch Johnson --------------------------------- [The camera opens up outside of a large house that we all know and love. It belongs to the wrestling legend, known as "Sexy" Fletch Johnson. People in the wrestling world have always known Fletch Johnson to be one of the heavy party-types as well as someone who was...at least in his latest outings...a
"has been". There was no doubt that for a period of four years, this man was the number one man in the lightweight circuit and on top of that, he was damn good against wrestlers twice his size. But he gained a little weight, all muscle, while rehabbing from a torn ACL and a strained MCL, and moved out of the light heavyweight title scene. He was forced to wrestle big men every day and that took a toll on his body, his paychecks and his fans. Fletch started to shy away and stay in his home more, throwing more parties and having his way with more women. He was ready to give up, and one day, he did. He retired from active competition. That didn't last very long. From someone who had competed all his life, 24 hours a day, 7 days a week he was competing. And then, a good friend of his, gave him a call. "Join FWC, and you might just find someone that you haven't seen in a long time." CLICK. Just like that he knew what was going to go down. His brother... Flash forward to today. The camera weaves through the well-kept yard and up to the front door, and then just cruises on in, because when you are "Sexy" Fletch Johnson, no one is going to screw with you.
They go up to his bed room and you can see him pulling stuff out of a chest of drawers and throwing it into a duffel bag.]
'Sexy' Fletch Johnson: "Through out my 8 year wrestling career, there have been many things that I have accomplished. I am a former World Champion 4 times over. I held two titles at one time in FWWF, and defended them both, sometimes on the same evenings.
Not many people can say that they have been able to meet my accolades and even less will_ACTUALLY_ever get there. And somehow, by some crazy twist of fate, I landed on the footsteps of the FWC Headquarters and when I told them what I wanted, they were more than willing to give me, "Sexy" Fletch Johnson, a contract.

Casey, being so upset, dropped my dad's last name and took my Mom's maiden name...just like the little bitch that he is. Mama's boy. --------------------------------- "The Rush" E J Drivven --------------------------------- [Claustrophobic, and heavily infused in an overwhelming ambience of dark-edged distortion, the struck guitar chord does ring out. It pierces the all encompassing visual blackness; its detuned growl showing no signs of dying away into a more comfortable sense of silence.
With this form of wordless introduction seemingly gathering in intensity, the gloom clears somewhat to reveal the emerging frame of a physically gifted 6'5", 256lb male entity.
He is a very muscular caucasian with long dark stringy hair trying its level best to hide the sneer of contempt that currently sits upon his less-than-welcoming face. A well trimmed chin goatee only serves to further emphasise this man's wholly malevolent expression, as he steps purposefully towards the prying camera lense.
As he approaches, attention is drawn to his clothing apparel, which merely consists of a pair of black denim jeans and heavy Dr. Marten boots of the same hue. Soon, the entire perspective offered unto us shows nothing but a tight "head to chest" shot of this brooding and seething behemoth, as he finally halts his forward motion. The simple "stencil-esque" ink rendition of fractured skull upon his left pectoral muscle rising and falling with his every deep breath, the man possessing probably *the* most piercing blue eyes known to modern civilisation speaks for the first time. His voice is low, almost guttural in its delivery.]
Entity: My name ... is E J Drivven ... Some people call me "The Rush" ... [His breathing now becoming more pronounced, Drivven continues his catharsis of venom.] EJD: *I* am a man of few uttered words. Instead, I prefer to demonstrate my unholy inner contempt for all and sundry in a more creative and altogether more "destructive" manner.
[The hint of a sadistic grin then flits across his formerly grim facade. This only intensifies the aura of unease that surrounds the by-now-silent scene.]
EJD: Y'see, if I'm not pouring the contents of my blackened soul into the framework of a creation that vaguely resembles a song, then I'm raining my fury down upon those that fancy themselves as Professional Wrestlers ...
Yeah, to put succinctly; I get my jollies from either making loud, offensive music *or* pummelling the living pi*BLEEP!* outta those puke-inducing a*BEEP!*holes who think that they're tough ...
[Drivven raises his heavily taped right hand into the air, until the back of his aforementioned hand attains a resting place that partially obscures the right side of face. His fingers slowly clenching into a tight fist, the man-monster hailing from "Parts Unknown" allows himself a brief cackle of dark amusement.]
EJD: ... And Dr. Killjoy ... You now stand in my way, Sh*BLEEP!*stick. The FWC Powers has seen fit to push your useless carcass into me in a warring stance. To this, all I have to say is ...
[Unbidden, the entire presentation falls into a complete shade of raven. However, Drivven's hate-addled voice continues to spew forth from the void.] EJD: ... So long, f*BLEEP!*-wit. You can consider yourself "Adrenalized". Hope you enjoy your preordained decimation, victim ... Because one thing is for sure ... I definitely *_WILL_*!!! ... Your descent into my realms of
"Spiral Aggression" has begun ... [End.]
