MVW Nostalgia: Tommy Rifles vs. the Professor

8 March 1998

Enjoy. =) Keep in mind, it was 1998, my writing was a bit cheesey. =) ______ _ _ _ _ _ | ___ \| | | | || || | v^| | _ | | | | | || || |v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^ | || || |\ \/ /| ||_|| | Main Event | || || | \ / | |___| | Tommy Rifles vs. the Professor |_||_||_| \/ \______| Written by [name withheld] Mohawk Valley Ring Wars v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^ DAVID FERRY - The following _main event_ contest is scheduled for one fall, and has a 30 minute time limit! [huge pop] Introducing first ... [The camera focuses on the center of the ring where David Ferry is standing, smiling. He gets his cue and then begins to speak towards the camera, again] DAVID FERRY - He hails from Chicago, Illinois, standing in at 6'1", and weighing in at a mere 228 pounds, here is "The Ass Kicker" TOMMY RIFLES !!! [The crowd begins to cheer loudly as "IRON MAN" by Black Sabbath quickly comes on over the loudspeakers. The crowd stands up, clapping, and begins to look towards the entrance way as Rifles walks out to a _HUGE_ ovation. Rifles quickly begins walking, stopping at the top of the aisle way, looking as cocky as he's ever been before. He has on long black tights with the words "ASS" going down his right leg and "KICKER" going down his left leg. He makes his way to the ring, keeping his eyes on some fans and quickly leaps up on the apron way and then slingshotting into the ring. He then walks to his corner with a smile on his face and then the bell rings as Rifles walks towards the center of the ring.] MARTIN GRIMMER - Folks, the man known as "Ass Kicker" is going through some rough times in the MVW right now. A _tough_ count out loss to Kiljoy last week and loses Shane Slaughter and some others. This man keeps getting deprived of the rewards he deserves. DEVON CANNONS - That's right, but tonight he has a shot to get a win over a man that's standing in his path. A man that he hates to the core, at this point. The _leader_ of the Citadel of the Superior. That being the Professor. Rifles is not a happy man, for good reason. MARTIN GRIMMER - If you'll remember correctly, Rifles was coming out to 'save' Professor from then-Citadel members Shane Slaughter and Jim Greed, what he didn't know was that Professor was the newest member of the faction. The rest is history, as Professor beat Rifles to a pulp. DEVON CANNONS - But tonight, this man who thinks he 'kicks ass' will get a shot at Professor. And if he can keep his mind off "Legacy" Paul Kiljoy long enough, he may have a shot at winning. A heated match to say the least. ["It will be a long Time" by the Offspring cues up over the sound system as the crowd instantly hops up to their feet and leans over towards the aiselway, awaiting the entrance of the leader of the Citadel.] DAVID FERRY - And _his_ opponent, hailing from the upper side of Connecticut, he weighs two hundred and fifty pounds and stands six feet three inches tall, here is the leader of the Citadel of the Superior ... THE PROFESSOR !!! [The Professor parts the curtains and begins to walk down to ringside with a cocky smile on his face. He is wearing his familiar yellow spandex trunks with a "Citadel of the Superior" sweat shirt with XXL underneath the title. He struts to the ring cockily, ignoring the boos coming form the crowd. They give him a larger chorus of boos than usual as he is the leader of the Citadel of the Superior.] MARTIN GRIMMER - Professor not even in the ring yet! Rifles hits the ropes, and a quick over the top rope plancha suicida onto Professor before the bell! [huge crowd pop] My god almighty! What a move! He flattened Mr. Citadel into a pancake and this crowd loved every second of it! DEVON CANNONS - He attacked him before the bell! Come on ref, disqualify him! Rifles pulling the Professor up again and he's unloading with a barrage of right hands. One after another. Professor is getting rocked [pop] and rocked [pop] and rocked [pop]. Another right hand by Rifles sends Professor into the gaurdrail! [pop] MARTIN GRIMMER - Tommy Rifles is letting his emotions get the better of him in this contest as he's just pummeling the MIT graduate. He pulls him up again and tosses him head first into the [crash] ring steps! And he throws the steps over and _again_ pulls Professor up. He slams Professor onto the steps! Vengeance! DEVON CANNONS - Rifles needs to see a doctor or something. This man is completely out of control. What's wrong with him? Could he possibly want a peice of Professor _that_ bad? MARTIN GRIMMER - He could and he _does_. Rifles is kicking him in the ribs now! Professor is on all fours and getting brutally kicked in the ribs over and over again. If he doesn't go to the hospital, it'll be quite a suprise to me. Rifles lifts him up into a front waistlock. [thump] And a hot shot onto the ring apron! [pop] Jesus Christ! DEVON CANNONS - This MVW crowd is RILED for RIFLES! And he's just pouring it on. He doesn't stop. He won't stop. He can't stop. This guy is insane. He's a man on a mission. Professor getting back up to his feet, now, and he's bleeding from the mouth. Rifles rolls him into the ring and follows him in. MARTIN GRIMMER - [ding, ding, ding] Haha, there's the opening bell. We could hear the closing bell very shortly if this onslaught continues. Rifles with an irish whip into the corner and Professor hits hard, shaking the ring. Rifles with a running start, he does a cartwheel handspring elbow avelanch! [big pop] Another high impact move by Tommy. DEVON CANNONS - Rifles slowly stepping out of the corner and watching Professor suffer in there. He grabs Professor by the arm and whips him to the other side, now! He follows it in with a big lariat. Enough, already, Rifles! Go for a pin! What's he trying to do? Put _another_ Citadel leader on the injury list? MARTIN GRIMMER - One can only hope. "Ass Kicker" Tommy Rifles is a house of fire in there, so far, tonight. He grabs Professor again and puts him in a front waistlock. Huge belly to belly suplex by Rifles and he took all the air out of Professor with that. Follows up with a big elbow drop to the sternum. He covers. One! Two! No, not yet. DEVON CANNONS - No, not yet, but damn close, I'll give him that. Rifles coming off a win over Steve Kline and a loss against Paul Kiljoy. He's taking it to the man who gets an Oneida County title shot at Shattered Dreams. That being the Professor. And what a match that will be. Two of my favorites, locking it up. MARTIN GRIMMER - Those two are your favorites? It figures, bro. Rifles grabs him once again by the arm and locks him into a hammerlock. Professor is screaming as Rifles applies pressure. Rifles trying to lift him up! He gets him up an inch off the ground but he can't get him high enough for that patented Hammerlock Suplex that he likes to do. DEVON CANNONS - Professor swings back his other elbow and hits Rifles in the bridge of the nose! Rifles drops the hammerlock and Professor grabs him by the hair. Front chancery into a fisherman's suplex! One! Two! _Professor_ almost had him that time. He's fighting back, now. MARTIN GRIMMER - But can he take Tommy Rifles? DEVON CANNONS - Most definatly! If Rifles came in acting all crazy because he wanted a fight, he's definatly going to get one. A Citadel member lays down for nobody, 'cept maybe your daughter. You should know that by now, Martin. MARTIN GRIMMER - Ouch. You need to close that hole you call a mouth right now, because there's action going on in the ring. Professor grabs Rifles and hits him with a quick European uppercut. He backs him into the ropes, and whips him to the other side. Professor with a jumping shoulderblock! Almost in a clothesline type fashion, he blasted Rifles. DEVON CANNONS - He blasted Rifles? You're making it sound like he robbed a bank. Rifles couldn't take care of Paul Kiljoy, and now he can't take care of the Professor. It all falls into place. Professor with a scoop and a slam. And he locks on a dragon sleeper! [big pop] MARTIN GRIMMER - Hey! This is the VanDamager, dragon sleeper, which Rifles used to use as a finisher! And now Rifles is being forced to fight out of his own hold! Professor using this to get a little rest for himself, and Rifles is feeling the effects as he kicks his feet and hands! DEVON CANNONS - Listen to all these Professormaniacs! What a great guy, this Professor has become. And Rifles is in some trouble, and he knows it. The referee is checking on him, not yet. It'll be done with soon enough. MARTIN GRIMMER - I'm not so sure about that, Devon, Rifles has always got a whole lot of fight in him. Professor has it synched in good, but if anybody knows a reversal for this hold, it's Rifles. He's used it for years. And now Rifles is trying to bridge out of it! Professor standing up on his feet! Rifles in a bridge situation ... DEVON CANNONS - REVERSE DDT! [big crowd pop] [The crowd pops as Professor hits this devastating hold. Both men lay on the canvas in a heap as the fans go crazy, waving signs and yelling as loud as possible.] MARTIN GRIMMER - A jumping reverse Class Dismissed DDT! Good Lord! It's all over! Professor inching over, slowly for a cover. He's taken his share of punishment in this match as well. Especially in the first minute or so. Professor with a cover. One! Two! Rifles slumps his foot up onto the bottom rope! Great knowledge of his ring positioning! DEVON CANNONS - Oh god, that was pure luck! Rifles just threw his foot up into the air, and it happened to fall onto the bottom rope. Professor pulling him up again and ... Rifles with a groinal uppercut! [big pop] MARTIN GRIMMER - OH MY GOD! Professor gets a big _peice_ of his own medicine! Rifles hammered him with that uppercut to the lower extremities! But Paul Kiljoy is on his way down to the ring! God does this man hate Rifles! Professor slumped over! Rifles charges a clothesline ... Professor sidesteps and sends him reeling through the ropes and out into the gaurdrail! [boo] DEVON CANNONS - Dirty tactics by Rifles really didn't pay off too much! Professor swung the momentum around and sent him outside into the railing. That _had_ to hurt! MARTIN GRIMMER - And now Professor is distracting the referee! Kiljoy has that MVW Television Championship, again! Rifles up to his feet ... [_CRACK_!] Oh _NO_! [The crowd begins to boo as Rifles hits the pavement and stops all motion. Kiljoy begins screaming in his face, and then rolls him back into the ring.] MARTIN GRIMMER - Say it ain't so! Kiljoy tortures Rifles again! Rifles laying on his back in the ring, now, blood pouring out of his head! Professor pulls him up and hits him with an uppercut to the groin of his own! [boo] CLASS DISMISSED DDT! ONE! TWO! THREE! DEVON CANNONS - He did it! And all by his lonesome, too! [ding, ding, ding] MARTIN GRIMMER - AH NO! THE PROFESSOR STOLE THE WIN! KILJOY'S OBSESSION WITH RIFLES HAS GONE TOO FAR! DEVON CANNONS - Kiljoy tosses David Ferry out of the ring! Count the win for the Professor! Just one more on the road to the ultimate showdown! The battle of battles! Shattered Dreams ... Professor vs. Scott Storm! My two favorites! Best match in MVW history, I promise! MARTIN GRIMMER - Kiljoy has a mic now! What's he gonna do now, insult him while he's down? [Camera switches solely to Paul Kiljoy. He's still wearing his 'LEGACY' robe. A bloody MVW TV belt hangs from his hands] "LEGACY" PAUL KILJOY - Well Tom, things are working out just as I saw them weeks ago. You're lying in a pool of blood. You've lost to me, you don't got a belt, and you're getting your butt kicked every week. And me? Well, I just made myself some new friends. Hit the music. [" Nothing Else Matters" by Metallica plays over the loudspeakers.] MARTIN GRIMMER - THAT'S SOLITUDE'S MUSIC! WHAT'S GOING ON? [Solitude calmly walks down the aisle. He wears a black leather vest over a sleeveless "Seclusion" shirt. Solitude's black tights are made of glassy latex. His boots are black and laced all the way to the top in tight succession. Sinewy strands of black hair sprawl down the extent of his face. His face painted a pale white with a black tear streaming from his left eye. Solitude steps into the ring and with no emotion whatsoever, walks slowly to the corner.] DEVON CANNONS - Looks like Kiljoy just made some new friends, idiot. "LEGACY" PAUL KILJOY - _Now_ we see what was meant to be. The Citadel is upon us. Oh wise one, the man who just brought Tommy Rifles from school and back ... enlighten us. [The Professor stands up and wipes the sweat off of his face. He breathes heavily into the microphone.] THE PROFESSOR - See, [catches his breath] I told everybody that the MVW was in for a surprise tonight, and unlike Rifles I _always_ deliver on my promises. Tonight the Citadel becomes as strong as it ever has been. I have added the last of the unbeaten wrestlers to the Citadel ... who also happens to be the MVW TV Champ. The legacy lives on ... [Huge heel pop] THE PROFESSOR - But I was not done! No stable is complete with only two men. I ... [Kiljoy looks puzzled.] THE PROFESSOR - Wait, we added another member to the Citadel. The most demented, insane, suicidal, mysterious, outré ... I could go on and on. The next great MVW star who will finally get the respect he deserves, I give you Solitude! [The Professor passes the microphone to an anxious Solitude.] SOLITUDE - Oh how the tides have changed. Just a few weeks ago, I held Kiljoy in my grasp. Wanting to exert every living breath out of his body. Wanting to rip him in two to prove a point. [looks at Kiljoy] Seems now, that all I want to do is thank this man for giving me a path to walk. Thank him for sheltering me when others mocked and wanted to take me down. Thank him for bringing me out to embrace the legacy. [looks at Professor] And to you, Professor, the man with the moniker of education and knowledge. Under your tutelage, I hope to learn the trade. And perhaps, become the prized student. To gain that knowledge and hopefully uncover the mysteries of my past. [Paul Kiljoy picks up the limp body of Tommy Rifles and sets him on the top turnbuckle. He delivers a merciless Birthright to a chorus of boos. Solitude walks over and begins mercilessly stomping the life out of Rifles. Kicking him in the face, the chest and the midsection.] "LEGACY" PAUL KILJOY - Now _that's_ what I'm talkin about! This is the _new_ Citadel! And I am the LEADER! We have formed to _dominate_ the MVW! One man at a time! Feel Rifles' pain, MVW! Sense the Legacy has fallen upon you! [He passes off the microphone and starts kicking Rifles] SOLITUDE - Rifles? [whips the hair out of his face and arches his left eyebrow] He was just the _first_ victim.... [Solitude passes the microphone to the Professor] THE PROFESSOR - Nobody messes with the Citadel, like my good friend said ... Rifles is only the first of _many_ to come. Soon we will run this league, and trust me my friends it will not be a democracy. The New Age had arrived MVW. The Citadel is back! [All of a sudden, the lights of the arena flash out, and a low, resounding bass floods over the crowd. You know deep down that there is something familiar about this music. Before you can really think about it, however, a low, yet firm voice spread through the PA system.] VOICE - So, the Citadel is back in the MVW, and now boasts the wrestler Solitude. [You know it...you know the mans voice! All of the fans begin to throw up their hands into the air, and the lyrics of "Hey Man...Nice Shot!" rip through the sound system...] VOICE - Well I guess that I didn't know that until tonight...so my ass BETTER call somebody! But...since I lack a phone at the moment, why don't I just come out there and make sure I have my facts straight! [You see a large man come walking out of the locker area. He is wearing a black T-shirt and blue jeans, and he is carrying a mic. On the front of his T-shirt is the logo "EWA - FOREVER" and on the back you see the man's name spelled out in thick, white letters: "DAEMON DEMPSEY"] "DAEMON" DRAGON DEMPSEY - Well, well, well. The big man Solitude has decided to come out here and beat on a man that cannot even stand on his own two feet! You're a strong man Solitude [smirk] a strong man! [Dempsey continues to the ring, and you see the faces of Kiljoy and Solitude show their shock. You see that they try to say something into their mic, but it has been turned off.] "DAEMON" DRAGON DEMPSEY - Don't bother wasting your breath gentlemen. We have heard quite enough from you tonight. [Dempsey reaches the ring, and leaps in under the bottom rope. You see Kiljoy makes a move to get closer, but Dempsey locks his gaze on him, and he stops...] "DAEMON" DRAGON DEMPSEY - I don't have a problem with you Kiljoy...at least not yet anyway. So do us all a favour and shut that thing you call a mouth! ... (((((MASSIVE CROWD POP))))))) ... [Dempsey turns his gaze on the big man Solitude...] "DAEMON" DRAGON DEMPSEY - So, Solitude, I have a little proposition for you. What say you quit beating on people that are lying on the mat and try your luck against somebody like me? Or do you prefer to flaunt your [smirk] skills to people who just lie down and take it? ... ((((((MASSIVE POP)))))) ... [You see a whole bunch of MVW officials come running into the ring, and they stand between Dempsey and the members of the Citadel. Solitude and Kiljoy look absolutely ticked right off, and Dempsey is just smiling.] "DAEMON" DRAGON DEMPSEY - So let me know, Solitude. Let me know if you have the balls to step into the ring with the Dragon. Let me know f you can find the strength within you to fight a man who is actually standing up and punching back. Let me know, Solitude, and I will face you ANYWHERE and ANYTIME you want! [The crowd is going nuts...] ... (((((((((((((((MASSIVE POP)))))))))))))))))) ... [Dempsey looks directly at Solitude, and spits on the mat.] "DAEMON" DRAGON DEMPSEY - That is, of course, if you are man enough? [Dempsey throws down the mic, and "Hey Man, Nice Shot" screams through the speakers again. Dempsey leaves the ring keeping his eyes on Solitude, who is trying to push back the people in between him and Dempsey. Dempsey smirks at him, and leaves down the walkway, slapping the hands of all the fans standing by the metal runway cage who are screaming his name. The copyright information hits the TV screen. Fade to black.] [name withheld] [detail withheld] MVW President "Number three, never trust nobody, your moms would set that ass up, properly gassed up. Hoodied and masked up, shit, for that fast buck .. she'll be layin in the bushes to light that ass up." - B.I.G.
