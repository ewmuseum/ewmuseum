Editing may occur, but in some way shape or form your news will occur in this column in the next update! =) xxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxx PRESIDENT'S MEMO! xxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxx MVW! Before long, it'll be more than just _us_ saying that name. Mohawk Valley Wrestling is about to make it's enormous return to the e-wrestling world, and without you guys, it wouldn't be possible. I just wanted to take some time and thank each and every one of you for your support in MVW's return. I've had a lot of helping hands reaching out to me since I decided to restart. A number of you helped me recruit and were timely with sending in your applications. You know who you are. Thank you. =) Also, I was thinking about the eight man tournament at The Ascension, and it brought to mind a few memories and a few thoughts. I really took a look at the situation and decided that I may have made the wrong decision by having a tournament, but the decision stands. The reason I feel it may have been the wrong decision, is because I realized that with an eight man tournament, seven walk out dejected. Seven people will recieve a loss on their record one way or another. I just want to reiterate the fact that losing in the tournament will _not_ disqualify you from getting a push or anything of the sort. And if you're knocked out in the first round, that doesn't mean you're going to end up a jobber. Stick it out, stay loyal ... the rewards will come in time. That's all, Briggs xxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxx ROSTER! xxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxx ANGEL SAGRADA (5'5", 140 lbs, Heel) - The young Angel is very cocky and very, very arrogant. He hides his bitterness and redemption underneath his mask of his arrogance and "high-and-mighty" attitude. He enjoys to slap you around a bit with his words and to bring you down. Let's put it this way, he gets a natural high from being a rude and aggrivating to whoever or whatever's around him or associated with him. His attitude, however, can be backed up with athletecism. The diminutive luchador has been at it since a very young age, and brings absolutely an absolutely unbelievable repetoire of moves to the table. "DIRTY" DICK LEBARON (6'4", 278 lbs, Heel) - "Dirty" Dick LeBaron is a brash loudmouth who loves nothing more than to get into a good scrap. At 50, he's seen it all, done it all, and is not impressed by the younger generation of grapplers. In fact, he has taken it upon himself to prove that the young guns can't handle an old cannon such as himself. And seldom has he been proven wrong. LeBaron has a solid build, characterized by a barrel chest, strong though relatively undefined biceps and triceps, a prominent keg-like stomach, and a set of knobby knees. M-DUB (6'1", 221 lbs, Heel) - M-Dub has competed in many federations, under many different "gimmicks" given to him by various promoters, trying to cash in on current crazes. He's a very cocky man with a very bad meanstreak trying to make a living and become the 'next big thing' in Mohawk Valley Wrestling. Not to be mistaken for another, M-Dub sports a unique appearance. His long black dreadlocks hang in front of his face. He also wears a black goatee with a matching moustache. M-Dub is ready to take what he feels is his, and will stop at nothing to top the MVW. MITCH "SURE FIRE" GATLIN (6'2", 235 lbs, Heel) - Mitch seems to think he is gods gift to wrestling calling him self "The New Breed Of Wrestler". He does not like all the older wrestlers in the sport, and feels they should step out of the spot light and retire. Although he's careful, Mitch likes to put his body on the line in all of his matches. He could be a future MVW star, just ask him. PLEX (6'2", 300 lbs, Face) - The attractive, natural athlete in Plex shines for the world to see. Although he may be a bit on the conceited side, Plex has a good handle on morals and virtue. His chiseled physique is an astounding sight to see, and the fans seem to eat up his every motion. If Plex can get his mind of the females and the glory of the professional wrestling scene, he will be a force to be reckoned with in the MVW. Coming off an injury that occured on the set of his latest released movie, Plex, real name Jason Williams, seems to be in the best shape of his life. It's do or die for this young man, and chances are 'die' won't be his choice. RAGE (6'8", 299 lbs, Heel) - Used to work as a security professional for CHAOS!!! wrestling, trained with workers at NPW (Northeastern Pro Wrestling) shows and traveled to Midwestern Pro Wrestling since NPW closed its doors. Rage had a torturous childhood, and enjoys easing his own pain by inflicting it on others. He's very bitter and vengeful about life and society in general. He's very methodical, doing as much damage as possible to the weakest area he can find. He is also very cold and callous, believing no one's pain and suffering is equal to his own past. He is willing to hurt anyone and everyone to get the respect or fear he feels has been witheld from him. RONNIE BLACK (6'1", 225 lbs, Face) - No Fear. For some, that statement is a cool thing to say, or simply a logo on their t-shirt. But for Ronnie Black, it's a way of life. This African American competitor has dealt with adversity in his life and overcome it. He believes to the very depths of his soul that no one and nothing can prevent him from reaching his goal - being the very best. He will never give up, never stop, never surrender - whether it's in a fight, or with a friend. His father, an ex-Marine (in Vietnam), taught him the meaning of Semper Fi - Always Faithful. Loyality is extremely important to him, and if nothing else, he'll always have his integrity. RORY BRADEN (5'7", 186 lbs, Heel) - Rory's an angry, bitter young man.  He's got a chip on his shoulder bigger than you could believe.  He resents anyone who's bigger than he is - and that's mostly everyone.  He's got a big mouth and likes to run it, but he likes to back it up even more.  The kind of guy who goes looking for a fight.  He doesn't make friends very easy.  Sticks to himself.  Hot tempered.  Very, very hot tempered.  The slightest thing can get him going.  Also has a fondness for alcohol.  Never wrestles drunk, but before or after a match, he frequents bars.  In general, he's a hard edged individual ... very no frills, no nonense.  A fighter in the truest sense of the word. SNOW (5'9", 160 lbs, Heel) - Snow has no wrestling history whatsoever. Just a crackhead found off the street, signed to a MVW contract only to fill roster space. Snow is a easy going guy to get along with. But, the little guy has a temper. When enraged, he can be one bad dude. STEVEN ERICKSON (6'2", 205 lbs, Face) - He received his first ego check in Mark Stone's WWO. He was the laughing stock of the Cruiserweight division, and the source of many insults. That was until he visited PAIN (Physicians Advocating Innovative Neoprocedures), they gave him his 'happy pills' though in return he was to do their dirty deeds, he tired of this and de-nounced his friendship with PAIN and no longer needs the pills. Now he is even more dangerous then ever, and facing him could mean a free trip to the emergency room. STONE HARRIS (6'2", 241 lbs, Face) - Born to a stock broker and a Japanese woman, Stone was brought to the United States by his father while his mother stayed in Japan. At the age of twenty two, he decided to find his mother. After arriving in Japan, he found that his mother had died two years prior. Distraught and without money, Stone started wrestling, believing that he had found his calling. He stayed in Japan to wrestle until the promotion closed it's doors. Confused on what to do next, Stone took his savings and flew home looking for work. He thinks that he found his way in the MVW. XYZ (5'9", 202 lbs, Heel) - XYZ is a Japanese competitor who saw fit to come over to America to try his skills. Now competing for Mohawk Valley Wrestling, he is hoping to get a firm grip on American wrestling. XYZ is a brash, outspoken individual who is highly critical to everyone. A man that will never quit when the goin' gets rough and a man that can push buttons on people like no one else can imagine. xxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxx CONTACT LIST! xxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxx Guys, I realized that you need some way of contacting one another for angles and soforth.
