MVW Nostalgia: Paul Kiljoy vs. Scott Alexander

4 January 1998
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
RSPWF, Here's the second in the series of nostalgic MVW matches I'll be posting. This one boasts "Legacy" Paul Kiljoy and "Stunning" Scott Alexander putting their respective Television Titles on the line against one another in a MVW Ring Wars classic. This one was written by [name withheld] and took place on January 4th, 1998. Give it a look. Enjoy, Briggs ______ _ _ _ _ _ | ___ \| | | | || || | v^| | _ | | | | | || || |v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^ | || || |\ \/ /| ||_|| | MVW and NPW TV Titles | || || | \ / | |___| | Scott Alexander vs. Paul Kiljoy |_||_||_| \/ \______| Written by [name withheld] Mohawk Valley Ring Wars v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^ MARTIN GRIMMER - Folks, up next we have a Television title match that's sure to be a crowd pleaser. "Legacy" Paul Kiljoy is making his first MVW Television title defense here, and "Stunning" Scott Alexander has put up his Northeastern Pro Wrestling Television title, as well. The stakes are high, folks, as only one man will walk out with both belts, the other man with well ... nothing. DEVON CANNONS - Kiljoy's out to humiliate Alexander, flat out. He wants to walk out of this joint with two title's to his credit and leave Scott flat on his face as a a failure. Both of these guys are flat out egotists, and they can't stand the thought of the other one's superiority. In my opinion, that just makes for one hell of an intersting matchup. MARTIN GRIMMER - Well, fans, we're ready to get underway, so let's do just that! DAVID FERRY - The following match is set for one fall with a fifteen minute TV time limit and it is for the MVW _and_ NPW Television titles! [pop!] Introducing first, he is the NPW Televison champion ... [The arena goes from black to pitch black, not a light to be seen. Strobe lights begin to roam around the arena, before stopping in a spotlight formation at the ring entrance. "Breathe" by Prodigy plays over the loudspeaker. A man, aprox. 6'0 tall, steps through the curtain and into the spotlight. A pyro displays happens behind him as he steps out, and the NPW and MVW superstar walks down the aisle.] DAVID FERRY - Introducing ... standing 6'1" and weighing in at 232 lbs ... from Basking Ridge, New Jersey, here is the one and only "Stunning" Scott Alexander [Alexander walks down the aisle. He has on White, Black and Gold wrestling tights that have the words "Simply Stunning" on the back. He wears a black leather "Polo Club" logo jacket to the ring. He also wears silver Oakley's. His fiancee and manager, Veronica, is wearing a stunning blue sequin dress, with a slit up the leg going to her upper thigh. Alexander walks up the stairs, leans on the ropes to let Veronica in the ring. He lets go, then leaps over the top rope, climbs to the second turnbuckle, and gives the Polo Club sign. He takes off the jacket to reveal a clean cut physique. He drops the jacket to the ring attendant, takes off his glasses, and gives them to Veronica, who gives them to one of the lucky fans at ringside, the new owner of a pair of $200 Oakley's.] MARTIN GRIMMER - This man has put up the NPW Television championship without hesitation -- say, what's the ordeal with that? Is this match sanctioned by NPW, too? Because I haven't heard anything frmo Mike Langan or from Dean Wright about this. But if Alexander decided to put up his title, I'm guessing that Langan and Wright really can't stop him. What a magnificent thing it would be to _unify_ these to glorious titles. Is _that_ possible, Devon? DEVON CANNONS - What the [BLEEP] are you asking me for, you're the technical one. All I know is that Alexander's got a hottie on his side -- I think I'll pick him for that. Kiljoy's got nobody, not even an ugly slob. MARTIN GRIMMER - Your predicting a winner based on Veronica? DEVON CANNONS - I'm a prophet, what can I say? Besides, Veronica sure beats the hell out of that ... that _thing_ that "the Original" Sean Sabre carries around. What does he call it again? Sherry or something? Ack, I wouldn't touch that with my 1 ft. pole. MARTIN GRIMMER - You're 1 ft. pole? I thought the saying goes ... I wouldn't touch that with a _50 ft_ pole ... DEVON CANNONS - [sick grin] MARTIN GRIMMER - Oh god, Devon. That was horrible, I'm going to be sick. Anyways, folks, just for a little reminder, this match _cannot_ go very long. Because of the MVW TV time limit. It's at fifteen minutes which is just enough time for two MVW superstars to get their feet wet in a matchup. Both men won't even break a sweat in that time, most likely. DAVID FERRY - Introducing next, his opponent, the reigning MVW Television champ ... ["Born To Rule" by Queenstryke plays over the loudspeakers.] DAVID FERRY - Hailing from Boston, Massachusetts, standing 6'3'' and weighing 244 pounds, here is .... "LEGACY" PAUL KILJOY!!!! ["Legacy" Paul Kiljoy emerges from the curtains as the crowd bursts out in boos. His diamond-studded robe sparkles in the limelight, the back showing the word 'Legacy' in drawn out stones. Kiljoy is being led on a throne by a handful of preliminary grapplers. He seems to think Alexander is one of them carrying him.] PAUL KILJOY - C'mon Scottie! Lift it up higher on your end! That's not _stunning_ enough! [Kiljoy looks up and sees "Stunning" Scott in the ring and his face shows a look of mock surprise. He hops down off of his throne and enters the ring. Ding, Ding!] MARTIN GRIMMER - Can you beleive the gall and the aarogance of this "Legacy" Paul Kiljoy? Arriving to the ring on a throne as if he's some kind of King or Lord. It just amazes me how highly Kiljoy really does think of himself. The guy needs to be smacked back into reality because Scott Alexander is certainly no pushover and he's going to do everything in his power to take Kiljoy out. Bottom line. DEVON CANNONS - You can bet on that, but will it work? Kiljoy has pinned Alexander before, you always forget to make mention of that. Kiljoy, as much as you hate him, is a well schooled athelete. Even _Alexander_ admits that. Now all that's left is for you to suck up your pride and admit it as well. MARTIN GRIMMER - Yeah, sure, he's good. Anyways, an 'interesting' entrance from Kiljoy ... collar and elboe tie-up, and it's Scott Alexander who comes away with the advantage. Single leg takedown! He's twisting on the leg, now, but Paul reverses his positioning and sends Alexander sprawling. Both men back to a vertical base -- oh! Kiljoy just rammed a thumb to the eyes of "Stunning" Scott and used the distraction to bulldog him to the canvas. DEVON CANNONS - It didn't keep the man down for long, though, Grimmer, because Alexander hopped right back up to his feet after the bulldog. He's charging at Legacy and Legacy sidesteps and Alexander goes face first into the corner. Crafty and cunning is Paul Kiljoy. He rolls him up ... One! Kickout at one by "Stunning" Scott Alexander. MARTIN GRIMMER - Both men quick to their feet again and Kiljoy throw a boot to the midsection. Alexander catches it and sweeps out the other leg. Kiljoy down, Alexander has both legs, and he's setting up a Texas cloverleaf ... no! He's not going to get it on! He's got the legs grapevined but Kiljoy does a sit up and he's in the air, now! Alexander's holding him up, I've never seen anything like that ... DEVON CANNONS - High angle leg grapevine powerbomb by "Stunning" Scott Alexander! And yes, I _did_ just make up that move name! He's still got the legs tied up and he steps over into a Texas Cloverleaf! What a spectacular combination by Alexander but Kiljoy is already in the ropes, hence Scott must break that hold. Honestly, I'm not an Alexander fan but that was amazing. _Amazing!_ MARTIN GRIMMER - Kiljoy already back up and he's holding the back of his head, Alexander advances on him and Kiljoy spits in his face! Alexander riled up and he runs right into a rake of the face. A rake of the back and now a poke to the eyes. Kiljoy taking advantage of this referee's visual impairment. Kiljoy with a scoop and a slam. DEVON CANNONS - Now those are the types of things you need to do to win a match. Especially since he doesn't have a valet like Veronica -- three words, Martin. Va va voom! MARTIN GRIMMER - [sighs] Yes, whatever, Dev. Waistlock from behind from Legacy ... German suplex! The cover, one! Two! Thr-NO! Kickout at the last second by Alexander. He rolls back to his feet and strikes back with a lariat, sending the MVW Television champ reeling. A dropkick placed right on the knee, now, and Scott Alexander is mounting an offense finally. DEVON CANNONS - Why? All he needs to do is have Veronica hop up on the apron, show some thigh, and while Kiljoy -- and everybody else in this arena, ourselves included -- get a hard on, he can simply roll up Legacy and walk out with a new title. MARTIN GRIMMER - Devon, you're a brilliant strategist. [mumbling] Jesus, what a moron! DEVON CANNONS - Thank you, thank you. MARTIN GRIMMER - Alexander with a side headlock on tight, but he's send into the ropes. Kiljoy drops down and Scott hops on over and keeps running ... back on the rebound ... BELLY TO BELLY! Kiljoy caught him in mid run and just turned him right over. Paul's grabbing the legs now, twisting them every which way -- ways they were made to bend and ways they weren't! He's got a Boston Crab about to be turned over, now, and he sits down on it. DEVON CANNONS - Now, see, if Alexander was smart, he'd have payed off the ref, who could rake Kiljoy's eyes and get him a fast count. One, two, three, new champ! Just like that. MARTIN GRIMMER - Uh huh. Kiljoy releases the hold and brings Alexander to his feet. He irish whips him into the ropes, sidesteps ... hooked him in mid run again! A shoulder toss takedown by Kiljoy sends Scott Alexander crashing to the mat. DEVON CANNONS - Look who's been simply stunned _now_. MARTIN GRIMMER - Alexander is ticked off, now, at his lack of offense. He's back on his feet and he's going to work with punches, not even letting Kiljoy follow up on his move! Rights and lefts, and this technical show has broken down to a brawl. Sorry, purists, but this one's about to get ugly! DEVON CANNONS - Alexander's goin' ape[BLEEP], now, and he's smacking Paul's head into the turnbuckles rapid fire like -- oh, baby, look at this! He chucks him over the top rope and Kiljoy lands in a heap back in his little throne. The jobronnies left it right there on the floor, what a set up! MARTIN GRIMMER - Alexander is climbing up to the top rope ... oh my. Kiljoy is coming to his senses and trying to stand up in the seat of the throne -- I don't think he sees Scott. DEVON CANNONS - He will in a second. Legacy turns around ... flyin' monkeys, look at "Stunning" Scott Alexander! He leaps off ... spinning ... cockscrew f[BLEEP]in' plancha into Kiljoy! The throne tips over into the aisleway and the MVW crowd is lovin' every second off it. He's royal! He's royal! MARTIN GRIMMER - What in the world are you talking about. Stupid imitation chant. DEVON CANNONS - Sorry, Martin, I got carried away. MARTIN GRIMMER - The referee's not even bothering to count them out, 'cause he knows how much both of these men want to win. Kiljoy's startling to move from underneath the throne -- is that a piece of the armrest? He's grabbed a broken piece of the wooden arm rest. Alexander is back up as well, and he's setting up a superkick behind Legacy. Jaw smack comin' up ... NO! Kiljoy ducks it and Alexander turns back around ... OH MY! Paul just swung the piece of armrest like a bat and it nailed Alexander upside the head. DEVON CANNONS - It's going, it's going ... it's gone! Goodbye, home run, Kiljoy! MARTIN GRIMMER - Alexander's forehead is cut open, but Kiljoy isn't done yet. He tosses him back into the ring ... Paul's going for the top rope. He's stationed up there, waiting for Alexander to roll over onto his stomach. He does and Kiljoy leaps -- BOOT TO THE FACE! Alexander, laying prone on the mat, stuck up his foot and Paul landed right on it! Oh my! DEVON CANNONS - Legacy, meet the dentist! MARTIN GRIMMER - Alexander's now the one heading to the ropes, but on the inside. He's standing on the top rope, ready. He leaps with an axe handle, but Kiljoy catches him! He's got a bear hug on and he sticks him back up on the middle rope. He adjusts his grip -- fisherman lock. THE BIRTHRIGHT! HE HIT IT! The cover, the count ... One! Two! Three! DAVID FERRY - Ladies and gentlemen, the winner of this match as a result of a pinfall, and thus the MVW _and_ NPW Television champion ... "LEGACY" PAUL KILJOY! DEVON CANNONS - Rats. Hey, he's grabbed the ring mic! PAUL KILJOY - WHO'S THE STAR NOW, EH? WHO'S STUNNING NOW? [He thrusts the belts into the camera] MARTIN GRIMMER - Folks, I can't beleive it! "Legacy" Paul Kiljoy has just won the NPW Television Championship right here on MVW TV. He's celebrating in the ring and the crowd is booing him. Now what's going to happen? Is he going to take a trek down to NPW and have to defend that thing, there? History has been made on MVW TV! PAUL KILJOY - THE LEGACY CONTINUES! DEVON CANNONS - It certainly does because right now, "Legacy" Paul Kiljoy is the NPW TV champ _and_ the MVW TV champ! Good lord, he did it. And with no assistance from anybody or anything. Kiljoy was the better man, here, and now he's going to be the TV champion in not only Franky Luciano's MVW, but in Dean Wright's NPW as well. Congrats go out to Paul for winning a great matchup on Ring Wars. [Kiljoy exits with both belts as Alexander is assisted out of the ring by Veronica.] MARTIN GRIMMER - With that, folks, we'll be back with your main event. It will be an MVW Oneida County Heavyweight Championship encounter between the champion "Superstar" Scott Storm and his first challenger, this evening, "the Original" Sean Sabre.
