Those matches consist of Plex taking on Fallen, Steven Erickson taking on Rory Braden, Dick LeBaron against Ezra Martin, Mitch Gatlin and Ronnie Black, Donovan Keltrane meeting Veyliam Kai, Rage against Reginald Worthington-Smythe, and Snow rounding up the evening in a match with Stone Harris. [POP!] CHRIS WINTERS: In Binghamton, New York at the War Memorial, on Monday, February 19, 2001, a champion will be crowned. The rest of the tournament will take place on that evening, and I'm expecting that it will be one of the greatest professional wrestling events to come along in a _long_ time. Now that I've made that announcement, I'm leaving. [Winters steps off the stage and begins to walk towards the exit. He mutters, "These god damn people stress me out", loud enough to hear. Rich Williams takes the podium again.] RICH WILLIAMS: WOW! Fang has returned to MVW and the tournament has been announced. Now, it's time for our last superstar to introduce himself. Now, ast but certainly not least, everyone please welcome a young man who Mohawk Valley Wrestling is incredibly hopeful of his potential... ["Maybe" by Pushmonkey begins to be played over the PA system. The reporters and fans at the press conference not already sitting begin to stand and look around as a spotlight shines on a young man, about 5 feet, 11 1/2 inches tall and tipping the scales at about 225 pounds walks arm and arm with a beautiful goddess of a woman, a buxom blonde, each dressed to the nines.] RICH WILLIAMS: Please welcome... along with the lovely, the ravishing... ASTRA DEVINE. He is OWEN NEO! [Flashes from cameras flicker at a record setting pace, the camera pans up the body of the young couple, showcasing the amazing style of Devine's dark blue and black dress and Owen Neo's tuxedo. He tugs on his lapel slightly, smiling like a man who has just begun to live his most amazing fantasy and dream. His light brown hair is cut fairly short and his brown eyes are seemingly piercing, yet alluring. Devine lets out the same glow and magnetic appearance, as she pushes her radiant curly blond locks behind her shoulders as the two slowly ascend to the podium.] [The music slowly fades out and the crowd calms down after a moment of the pomp and circumstance surrounding Neo's entrance. He looks out towards the crowd for a moment and takes a deep breath. He looks towards Astra for a moment. She nods. He nods as well before pausing, then speaking.] OWEN NEO: For over a decade now, I've spent my days engulfed within the sport of professional wrestling. Call me a crazy kid with dream, but I can still remember the first time I ever flipped through the channels and my remote stopped on professional wrestling. [Neo gazes into the sky, with a europhic look upon his face.] OWEN NEO: If I remember correctly, Jake Roberts was wrestling. He was a master inside the ring. He tortured his opponent, worked him for every ounce of energy his opponent had, as well as the audience. When Jake Roberts was on television, or in front of that crowd, not a single person who watched him could say that they didn't remember every move he made. I couldn't stand it. Wrestling had me hooked. Roberts wasn't the only one, there were others. [The crowd rumbles for a bit, feeling a bit nostalgic before Neo continues.] OWEN NEO: On that note, perhaps I should let everyone here know who I am. My name is Owen Neo and I've been a professional wrestler for six years. Six long years of trials and tribulations. Putting long hours in the gym, busting my ass in and outside that ring. I've made it my every desire to take that fixation, that OBSESSION, I've had on the sport of professional wrestling and make others feel the same way I do. I want children to turn on their television sets, their eyes glued to the screen... and then when the commercial hits and the time comes where they'd normally get up to get a drink or a snack they find themselves sitting in the same position they have been sitting in, unable to move. I want everyone to quiver, to see the magic, to feel the wonder that I felt--- that I feel every time I'm around this sport. [Neo smiles slightly and shakes his head a bit.] OWEN NEO: You know, I want to take that history that I've lived to see, that I've lived to love, that I've had to CHANGE in order to survive through--- and make my own history. I'm not here just to be another wrestler. I'm not here to be a victim. I'm not here to find my dreams crushed and destroyed. I'm not here to fail. I've worked too long, too hard, and too much to watch this ONE opportunity blow up in my face. [Neo pauses for a second in a bit of reflection.] OWEN NEO: That's a funny word--- Opportunity. Funny, I thought the day I passed out flyers, set up rings and chairs and got to hang out with wrestlers was a great opportunity. I thought the day I first stepped into the ring to train was a great opportunity. I thought having my first match was a great opportunity. That's what life is all about--- the chances we are given, the chances we take. [Neo pounds his hand on the podium, putting emphasis upon his every word, keeping everyone in attendance's attention.] OWEN NEO: ... to me, finally moving up and getting recognition is not a chance, not an opportunity, but a destiny that I have come to fulfill. [Owen Neo takes a deep breath upon taking a step back from the podium. He closes his eyes and basks in the roar of applause that comes from the crowd of fans and reporters in attendance.] RICH WILLIAMS: Thank you, that was Owen--- [Within seconds of the applause, Neo flies from the stage onto the sea of reporters beneath it, scattering all in attendance like roaches fleeing for cover from the light. A lone figure stands above the fallen Neo on the stage as wrestlers, announcers, and security in attendance attempt to keep him from causing any harm.] RAGE: You don't GET IT! [The monster known as Rage throws the podium aside, causing the wrestlers and officials around him to scatter, just as the reporters did. The fallen Owen Neo can barely rise to his feet when Rage dives off the stage onto him, ricocheting Neo's head on the concrete below.] [The hustle and bustle of the people in attendance doesn't seem to stop any of the chaos. Rage pounds away viciously on the head and face of Neo before he begins to pound the back of his head repeatedly into the concrete. The beautiful ravishing Astra Devine, who was with Neo in attendance attempts to stop the beast, latching onto Rage's arm as he pulls back.] ASTRA DEVINE: Please, stop! Why are you doing this? [The beast stands from his mounted position clenching the hand of the helpless woman in his massive fingers. His eyes seem to glaze over for a second as he stares at her before tossing her aside the moment he hears Neo begin to speak.] OWEN NEO [groggy, attempting to stand]: Leave.... h-h-her... alone.... [Security surrounds the men before Rage can make his way back over to Neo. Rage proceeds to pick up a chair and swing it wildly, knocking a camera man over and destroying a video camera in the process. As the crowd about them is momentarily distracted, Rage whacks Neo over the head with another chair laying about, knocking Neo up against the stage.] [Rage begins to wail away again on the prone Neo, whacking away at him with a chair and his fists. He leeches onto the light brown locks of the young man and tosses him onto the stage like a rag doll. Rage follows him up, leaping onto the stage like a gazelle and tears at Neo and his tuxedo, pulling him to his feet. For a moment Rage pauses, staring at the groggy young man dead in the eye before spatting in his face.] [Rage suffocates Neo, pulling him in close and then proceeds to lock him up and lift him up towards the heavens. More officials arrive, attempting to stop the big man from dropping the defenseless Neo, but only adding fuel to the fire.] [Time seems to stop as Rage begins dropping Neo, the beast leaps off the ground and delivers a head and arm brainbuster onto a chair on the stage, causing the stage to break and begin to collapse upon impact. The crowd that still remains surrounding the incident, whether as voyeurs, officers, or sadists attempt to scatter as smoke and dust fill the open air.] [The scene grows silent as the smoke begins to clear... a lone voice is heard.] RAGE: Ashes to ashes... dust to dust... [The camera zooms in on the destroyed stage. The black T-shirt worn by Rage is now torn and a grin is seen from ear to ear across his anguished face. He lifts his now blood soaked hands from the ground and glances at them. He chuckles at the sight.] RAGE: ... only Rage is Absolute. [Rage stands and walks away from the scene. The sound of sirens and screams echoes through the air. The camera zooms in on the fallen hero, Owen Neo. His face coated with blood, his body laid across boards and barriers. His body leaps in a convulsive movement as he loses consciousness one more time.
