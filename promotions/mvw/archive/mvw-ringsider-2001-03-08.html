MVW Ringsider — 8 March 2001

8 March 2001
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The camera opens up to a flaming Mohawk Valley Wrestling logo. A clip from last week shows Rory Braden and Stone Harris exchanging punches on the ropes, both worn down from battle. A lightning bolt strikes the screen and Donovan Keltrane comes out knowhere with a shoulderblock, knocking both men out of the ring to become the Empire State Champion.] VOICE OVER: Donovan Keltrane wins it! Donovan Keltrane is our new Mohawk Valley Wrestling Empire State Champion! ["Epic" by Faith No More begins to play, as the MVW logo rises to the screen once again, followed by the logo of ...] -------------------------------------------- |Mohawk Valley Wrestling's | | ___ _ _ _ | || _ \(_) _ _ __ _ ___(_) __| | ___ _ _ | || /| || ' \ / _` |(_-<| |/ _` |/ -_)| '_| | ||_|_\|_||_||_|\__, |/__/|_|\__,_|\___||_| | | |___/ | | Episode #1 March 08, 2001 | -------------------------------------------- [Cheesey music plays in the background as the camera cuts to the a studio setting. A large black news casting desk lies in the center of the scene with a blue MVW logo stretched across the front. Two men sit behind the desk in sport coats and ties. The man on the left has blonde hair hair and brown eyes. He has a small face, but is a little pudgy. The man wears a black suit with an MVW insignia on the left breast pocket. He speaks first.] CURTIS KEENAN: Hello, Mohawk Valley Wrestling, I'm 'Cunning' Curtis Keenan and it is my pleasure to bring to you, the first ever edition of Mohawk Valley Wrestling Ringsider! Tonight, we will take a look at many of the happenings in MVW. DANNY ANGEL: And of course, I'm the most respected journalist in Mohawk Valley Wrestling history, Danny Angel, and I, _too_, will help take a look at what's been happening. Why didn't you mention me, Curtis? [Keenan looks over to Danny Angel, who has a long, broad jaw and small, beady eyes. He shakes his head 'no' at him.] CK: Simply because you get on my nerves. DA: That wasn't nice. |-|-|-|-|-|-|-|-|-|-|-|-|-|-|-|-|-|-|-|-|-|-|-|- Last Week On ... Ring Wars! |-|-|-|-|-|-|-|-|-|-|-|-|-|-|-|-|-|-|-|-|-|-|-|- CK: Now, at the very top of the broadcast, you saw what transpired in the main event of last weeks Ring Wars. After suffering a defeat from Stone Harris just tens of minutes before, 'Dark Heart' Donovan Keltrane walked out of the arena as the MVW Empire State Champion. DA: I don't like Keltrane, a whole lot, but I have to hand it to him, he really gave it his all in that battle royal. Stone Harris and Rory Braden were punishing eachother with rights, and he slithered in like a lizard and dumped them both over the top. CK: And as the show went of the air, neither man had come to terms with what happened. They couldn't have been happy men. But the big story is, Stone Harris holds a clean victory over our ES Champ, and that's a _big_ notch under his belt, and it should put him into MVW Title contention, if not the #1 contender. DA: And you can't forget that Harris also came within _inches_ of pinning Owen Neo, just two weeks ago. CK: If it wasn't for Rage's interference on Harris, he may have walked out of that match as the champion. Many speculated he intentionally caused the disqualification because he feels he can take the title from Neo himself. DA: That would be a task, but if anybody can do it, it'd be Rage. He's damn near unstoppable. CK: But that wasn't all that happened on Ring Wars, Dan. You witnessed history as 'Dirty' Dick LeBaron pinned Mitch 'Sure Fire' Gatlin, sending him home without a contract. It was a loser leaves town contest. DA: It sure was. Gatlin gave it everything he had, but in the end, it was LeBaron who stays. CK: Lets take a look a clip of the closing moments from that particular altercation, shall we? ------------------- FLASHBACK: Dick LeBaron v. Mitch Gatlin ------------------- [The camera fades to black, and then fades in to a shot of Mitch Gatlin hammering away at LeBaron with a series of right hands.] DANNY ANGEL: Gatlin going right after him, though, he's not letting up. at him laying in those right and left hands shots on the face of LeBaron! RICH WILLIAMS: He is relentless. Gatlin, now, picks LeBaron up to his feet. Armwringer by Gatlin -- shortarm clothesline! Whoo! He really blasted him. Gatlin, drops for the cover again! One! Two! And LeBaron muscles out. DA: These two both seem to want it _bad_. RW: But they can't both have it. LeBaron getting to his feet, and Gatlin takes him over with a quick gutwrench suplex. He strained himself on that, but it was effective, regardless. DA: He knocked the wind out of big Dick. RW: Gatlin grabs LeBaron, and hooks him for a DDT. He jumps! And PLANTS him! Man alive! He put LeBaron's head DEEP into the canvas! That should be it! Gatlin rolls him over! . ONE! . . . . . . . . . TWO! . . . . . . . . . . KICKOUT! He kicks out! Dick LeBaron fighting for his MVW career! DA: Good God! How he kicked out of that one is beyond me! Gatlin planted him _viciously_ into the mat! RW: And Gatlin is frustrated! [Gatlin curses a few times, it's bleeped out. He kicks the bottom rope, and pushes the referee.] RW: He'd better take his hands off the official! He's flirting with a huge fine if he crosses the line! The official admonishing him, Gatlin pushes him out of the way as LeBaron gets up and he hammers him with a diving forearm! Gatlin launched himself and drilled LeBaron again! DA: He caught him good! RW: And Gatlin with a few stomps! He picks LeBaron up again, and LeBaron fights back with a right to the midsection! And another! LeBaron is fighting back! He rakes the eyes! Kicks him in the gut and DDTs him! LeBaron with a DDT of his own, now! One! Two! No! Gatlin kicks out! DA: And Gatlin gets right back up for more! RW: But LeBaron chops him down to the canvas. LeBaron, now, irish whips him into the corner and follows him in with a monsterous clothesline! [Gatlin stumbles forward, but LeBaron pushes him back into the corner.] DA: He suprised us all by coming back and fighting with such fury! LeBaron may have been playing opposum! RW: And LeBaron drills him with a big shoulder to the midsection. And again! And again! LeBaron grabs Gatlin by the hair and drags him to the center of the ring! [Gatlin fights back with a few right hands. The crowd begins to stir, becoming excited.] DA: And Gatlin's coming back, now! He hits a few right hands on the big man! RW: And Gatlin hooks him for a suplex! He lifts -- no! LeBaron puts on the brakes! And LeBaron lifts him up for a suplex, now, he's got him high in the air, just holding him there! [LeBaron holds Gatlin high up there, as the crowd begins to cheer.] DA: What's he waiting for?! RW: LeBaron with a show of strength! And ... . . . . . BRAINBUSTER! LEBARON HIT HIS BRAINBUSTER! HE COVERS! . . . . ONE! . . . . . . . . . . TWO! . . . . . . . . . . . . . THREE! [HUGE POP!!] RW: GATLIN IS NO LONGER ON THE MVW ROSTER! LEBARON WINS IT! LEBARON TAKES THE VICTORY! DA: WOW! LEBARON COMES BACK AND HITS HIS BRAINBUSTER FOR THE WIN, AFTER A TORTUROUS MATCH! ------------------- AND WE'RE BACK! ------------------- [The camera cuts back to the studio, where Keenan and Angel sit, patiently. The camera focuses in on the pair.] CK: Gatlin gave it a hearty effort, but he just didn't have enough inside of him to get the pinfall victory on the veteran 'Dirty' Dick, and thus was released by Mohawk Valley Wrestling. He will be missed. DA: You're not kidding. CK: Another major story from Ring Wars was Steven Erickson pulling out his first victory, against M-Dub. M-Dub is very frustrated with his position in MVW, thusfar, and he's struggling in the rankings. Erickson is also down on his luck, but this may be the boost he needed to start an upswing. DA: Erickson employed a different style than we're used to, during that matchup. Instead of his usual technical jibberish, he stuck to a much more effective brawling style, and that's what helped him pick up the win. The big story, however, happened in the back, when Ronnie Black got into his face. CK: The footage Mr. Angel is referring to was not aired on Ring Wars. In the locker room, Erickson and Black had a slight altercation. We'll let you see that for yourself. Lets roll the footage. This took place shortly after Erickson picked up the victory over M-Dub, take a look. ------------------- EXCLUSIVE: Trouble Backstage! ------------------- [Steven Erickson approaches the camera, absolutely exuberant after upset victory over M-Dub. Sweat glissens off his face, and a warm smile greets the camera. He continues walking as he speaks.] SE: What did I say, huh? M-Dub, I said I was going to give it to you, and that's exactly what I delivered! You take th... [Walking backwards, he inadvertantly bumps into someone who also has his back turned.] SE: Hey, watch it, dude... [Erickson turns around to face a perturbed Ronnie Black, especially considering what Steven Erickson said about him earlier.] SE: Oh... [They stare each other down, neither flinching, neither looking away. Finally, Erickson smiles slyly, but Black continues looking straight into his face, set stern, no words, no expression. Erickson walks away smiling, the tension broken. Then, inaudible to the camera, Erickson says something to Ronnie Black.] RW: What'd he just say, Danny? DA: I don't know, the camera didn't seem to pick it up! Ronnie doesn't appear to be too happy about it, whatever it was! [But, it obviously wasn't inaudible to Black. A mouthed profanity comes from his lips, and then suddenly he charges at Erickson, viciously grabs him by the back of the neck, and slams him headfirst into a nearby door!] RW: Good god! Ronnie Black just hammered him! [Erickson immediately counters, perhaps sensing the attack, or planning it, with a shot below the waist and a football tackle down to the floor. They roll around, exchanging punches, until security rips the two men away from each other.] RW: And here comes Boomer Sinclair and the rest of the security team, and they pull the two apart! Both of these men are acting like savages, and it's not their usual behavior! [Erickson, being pulled back, manages a muffled sentence.] SE: This is _not_ over! [Black once again tries to get to him, but is held back too tightly, and cannot get through the barrier of security. Fade to black.] ------------------- AND WE'RE BACK! ------------------- CK: Obviously, Erickson said something that was totally inappropriate. I'm not too sure what it was, but I don't even want to venture a guess. Ronnie Black is _not_ a happy man, right now. DA: Well, both of these men recently picked up their first victories. Black seems to be handling his losing streak much better than Erickson, however, but every man has a boiling point. This was just the spark that exploded the powder keg. Erickson's gonna kill him. CK: That remains to be seen. In other action from that evenings Ring Wars, XYZ picked up a victory over Fang, who's been on quite a losing streak, lately. DA: Fang just can't seem to get it together since his return to MVW, and XYZ is on a bit of a tare. He's one of the premier cruiserweights in MVW, and he really took it too Fang, showing him a thing or two about lightweight wrestling. CK: The veteran, Fang, was in a foul mood, afterwards. DA: He can't win! CK: Well, he just needs to re-find his niche. And the final important story from Ring Wars, this week, involved the Television title match. It was scheduled to be Patrick 'Heat' Seeker, our Commissioner, taking on the champion, Hawiian Crippler. Things worked out a little differently, though. DA: Thanks to Pat's power. [grin] CK: Yeah. Seeker decided to pull back his challenge for the title, offering the shot to Ted Bruce and Snow. And that match will appear on next week's Ring Wars. Before we go over what we may and may not see next week, I think we should take a look at this week's updated 'condendership' rankings, recently compiled by President Winters himself. DA: I never recieved a memo, on this! |-|-|-|-|-|-|-|-|-|-|-|-|-|-|-|-|-|-|-|-|-|-|-|- Mohawk Valley Wrestling's Rankings! |-|-|-|-|-|-|-|-|-|-|-|-|-|-|-|-|-|-|-|-|-|-|-|- CK: First, lets take a look at the breakdown on the MVW Title ranks for contendership. MVW HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPION: Owen Neo [5-1-0] 1. Stone Harris [3-1-0] 2. Rory Braden [4-1-0] 3. Rage [4-2-0] CK: There's three major contenders to Owen Neo's title, and there's no big suprise, here. Stone Harris was propeled to number one contender after giving Neo a run for his money two weeks ago and defeating the Empire State Champion on Ring Wars. He deserves the spot, and he's worked hard for it. DA: Don't forget, he defeated Rage a couple weeks ago, as well, and he has another match with Rage on this week's Ring Wars. If he can get by that, he'll most likely keep his slot. CK: The number _2_ contender is Rory Braden. He's another one who really worked hard to get where he is. His one loss came to him at the hands of the champion, Owen Neo, at Ascension. That may be the best match since the return of MVW. DA: That's what many people are saying. The match was absolutely excellent. Braden held his own, but his lack of wrestling skills led him to defeat. He couldn't get out of Neo's small package. Had he escaped, we may be singing a different song, here. CK: And lastly, the number three contender is Rage, who's another one who's worked hard. He took himself all the way to the finals in the MVW title tournament, and he will _not_ be forgotten when it comes down to it. A couple more victories, and he may be looking at a shot, real soon. His match this week with Harris is very important. DA: True dat. CK: So that's the MVW Title situation. Next on the list, is the second most important. The Empire State title situation. It should be heating up, very shortly. MVW EMPIRE STATE CHAMPION: Donovan Keltrane [4-2-0] 1. Dick LeBaron [3-2-0] 2. Patrick Seeker [2-0-0] 3. Ronnie Black [1-3-0] CK: There you see, there are three major contenders to the title held by Donovan Keltrane. At the top of the list, is Dick LeBaron. He put up an impressive showing at the battle royal, but his key to being the number one contender to that title is the fact that he put Mitch Gatlin out of MVW, no two ways about it. DA: Very true. That was a _major_ victory for LeBaron, and the veteran is very deserving of the slot. CK: Second, is Patrick 'Heat' Seeker, the MVW Commissioner. He's had his way with his only two opponents so far, Reginald Worthington Smythe and Ronnie Black. He hasn't wrestled a whole lot of matches, but the one's he wrestled, he proved that he has what it takes to be in contention. DA: Certainly. Seeker is one of the best. The third contender, though I don't like very much. Ronnie Black. How'd _he_ pop up on the list? CK: Very simple. Ronnie Black took Seeker to a high quality match and almost had him beat. Also, Black had an impressive showing as a dark horse participant in the battle royal. He made it into the final four before being eliminated by Braden and Harris. He had the Empire State title in his sights. DA: Eh, I guess I can see it, now. MVW TELEVISION CHAMPION: Hawiian Crippler [1-0-0] 1. Snow [3-2-0] 2. XYZ [2-2-0] 3. Ted Bruce [0-2-0] 4. Steven Erickson [1-2-0] 5. M-Dub [1-4-0] CK: The Television Title division is perhaps the most saturated. You have five guys who are all legit contenders, for their own reasons. Although they don't have sparkling records, they're doing well, here. DA: Snow is the #1 contender? CK: He sure is. Snow currently holds a victory over Stone Harris, and has proved that he actually can wrestle. Nobody expected it, but he's showed us he can do it. XYZ? Same story. He's an exceptional talent, and when he's on, he's on. Snow and XYZ are two of the better cruisers I've seen. DA: And then there's Ted Bruce! I love that guy! CK: Ted Bruce hasn't done a whole lot, yet in MVW, but he worked hard in the four way for the TV Title, and showed us he can wrestle. If he gets his game face on, he's worthy of the number three contendership. He gets his shot this week, along with Snow. DA: Steven Erickson, another quality wrestler. CK: Yes, he is. Erickson, recently having problems with Ronnie Black, is still struggling to find his way in MVW, but he's another one with a lot of ability, but not a lot of wins. DA: And M-Dub, one of my favorites! [grin] CK: He took Owen Neo to two _close_ contests, but came up short on both occasions. M-Dub is getting serious, now, and hopes to move up in the MVW. CONTENDERS FOR CONTENTION: 1. Fang [4-6-0] 2. Ezra Martin [1-2-0] 3. Reginald Worthington Smythe [1-2-0] 4. Plex [1-3-0] 5. Veyliam Kai [0-3-0] CK: And then there's give guys who aren't really of note. Fang, who is a former Oneida County Champion, has lost a step since he arrived in MVW. I don't know what the hell happened to him, but he's not the Fang I remember. Ezra Martin could use a boost, and the other three -- well -- I don't even know what to say. They're just roster space. DA: You're right. I'm suprised. You actually said a couple things I agree with. NEWCOMERS: x. James Branchini [0-0-0] x. Mr. Majesty [0-0-0] x. Ryan Delaney [0-0-0] CK: And then there's the three who are yet to debut, but we're looking forward to see what they have to offer. They are all unproven in MVW, but Winters wouldn't have picked them up if they didn't bring anything to the table. We'll have to see where they fall. DA: I met Majesty, I like him. He's a humerous guy, in a weird sort of way. Branchini? He's different. And Delaney? Well, he's ... he's ... CK: What, a freak? DA: You said it, not me. CK: Now, lets take a look at what's in store for next week! |-|-|-|-|-|-|-|-|-|-|-|-|-|-|-|-|-|-|-|-|-|-|-|- Next Week On ... Ring Wars! |-|-|-|-|-|-|-|-|-|-|-|-|-|-|-|-|-|-|-|-|-|-|-|- CK: Here's the lineup -- Ronnie Black/Steven Erickson v. Veyliam Kai/Ezra Martin [Wild Card Tag Match] James Branchini v. Fang XYZ v. Mr. Majesty Snow v. Ted Bruce v. Hawiian Crippler (c) [TV Title] Dick LeBaron v. Donovan Keltrane (c) [Non Title - Empire State] Stone Harris v. Rage Mystery Man v. Owen Neo (c) [Non Title - MVW] DA: Lookin good! CK: Nobody really has an easy match, this week. Two guys who haven't been getting along, Ronnie Black and Steven Erickson, were paired together 'randomly' by Chris Winters to take on the combination of Veyliam Kai and Ezra Martin. DA: I'm not too sure they're going to be able to work as a cohesive unit. CK: It remains to be seen. Then, we've got the newcomer, James Branchini stepping into the ring with the slumping Fang. Fang has lost a step, but I would never count him out. It's an MVW rookie taking on an MVW veteran. DA: Fang sucks, now. CK: Well ... perhaps you're right. And then, a battle between the Japanese sensation, XYZ, and Mr. Majesty, another man who is yet to make his MVW debut. I don't know what Mr. Majesty brings to the table, but he'd better bring a lot. XYZ is stiff competition. DA: I heard a rumor about that match. CK: What's that? DA: Can't tell, it's a secret. CK: Jackass. And then there's the Television Title match. Hawiian Crippler, who we haven't heard from in a while, will walk to the ring as the champion, to take on Ted Bruce and Snow. Both of those guys are excellent wrestlers. Crippler has allies, but he will have to do this alone. Who's your pick? DA: For some reason, I see Bruce coming out on top of this, but I wouldn't count Snow out. Crippler is sketchy, lately. He's been nagged with minor injuries sustained at the hands of Rage. I hope he puts up a fight. CK: We'll see. Also on the card, number one contender to the Empire State Title, 'Dirty' Dick LeBaron will get a chance to take on the champion, Donovan Keltrane, in a non-title match. DA: It will be neat to see how they match up, they've got very different styles, and it should turn out to be a quality match. If they both put in their best effort, it could be match of the night. CK: If LeBaron can pick up that victory, I see no reason he wouldn't be granted an immediate title shot against Keltrane, so this could be an important match to him. He's getting up there in age, and he may not have too many opportunities like this left in him. DA: And then, there's Stone Harris going at it with Rage! Rumor has it, the winner of that one will go on to Shattered Dreams to get the title shot against Neo! CK: Wow! Is that really a rumor, or did you make it up? DA: Can't tell, it's a secret. CK: Jackass. Stone Harris holds one victory over Rage, and it would be miraculous if he could pull off another. Rage caused him to lose his match in the first round of the title tournament at Ascension and has been a thorn in Harris' side ever since, simly because Harris is a friend of Owen Neo's. DA: That's the way it goes. Speaking of Owen Neo, he finally gets a one on one match with that mystery guy. Who the _hell_ is that, anyhow? CK: Your guess is as good as mine. Owen Neo will go one on one in a non-title match with Rage's masked friend, and there's a good chance the guy will be unmasked on Ring Wars. On top of all that, Chris Winters promised us all that he has an announcement that will change MVW for the better. DA: What could it be? CK: I'm not sure, I know he's been busy all week on the phone, trying to contact a handful of new talents. He told me a couple of names, nobody major, but quality wrestlers. I think he just wants to increase the ratings. DA: Yeah, they've slumped. CK: Well, I hope we've shed a little light on some of the happenings in MVW. We've run out of time. Thank you for watching, for Danny Angel, this is Curtis Keenan ... goodnight, everybody! DA: I can say goodnight myself ...
