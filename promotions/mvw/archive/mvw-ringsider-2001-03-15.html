MVW Ringsider — 15 March 2001

15 March 2001

RORY BRADEN IS OUT WITH A CHAIR! [***THWACK***] RW: BRADEN WALLOPS RAGE WITH THE CHAIR! DA: WHAT?!?! RW: RORY BRADEN is out here to save Owen Neo!! BRADEN WITH THE CHAIR AND KELTRANE GOES FROM THE TOP TURNBUCKLE TO THE FLOOR! Jesus! He hit the floor hard! DA: BRADEN HAS JUST TAKEN OUT RAGE AND DONOVAN KELTRANE WITH A STEEL CHAIR! What the hell?! RW: BRADEN now just whacking the chair repeatedly over the skull of Jason Patrick... Patrick is ROCKING--- and BRADEN drops the chair --- F-BOMB! BRADEN JUST DOCTOR BOMBED Jason Patrick THROUGH A TABLE! WELCOME TO THE MOHAWK VALLEY! Good lord! A large black news casting desk lies in the center of the scene with a blue MVW logo stretched across the front. Two men sit behind the desk in sport coats and ties. The man on the left has blonde hair hair and brown eyes. He has a small face, but is a little pudgy. The man wears a black suit with an MVW insignia on the left breast pocket. He speaks first.] CURTIS KEENAN: Hello, Mohawk Valley Wrestling fans, I'm 'Cunning' Curtis Keenan, alongside the notorious 'Dastardly' Danny Angel, and we're here with another edition of MVW Ringsider! DANNY ANGEL: Yeah, and tonight we're going to look at a few things that are going on in the world of MVW. Some good, some bad, some ugly. CK: The clip you saw at the top of the show, was the one that shocked the MVW World, folks. Rory Braden came to the aid of Owen Neo, after Neo _begged_ for his assistance at the top of the show. DA: Neo groveled like the whiney little rat that he is, and Braden, being a man with integrity, decided to step up and help him. I'm not sure it was a very bright move, though, Curtis, because now he's got three dangerous men _very_ angry with him. CK: I don't think Braden cares. |-|-|-|-|-|-|-|-|-|-|-|-|-|-|-|-|-|-|-|-|-|-|-|- Last Week On ... Ring Wars! |-|-|-|-|-|-|-|-|-|-|-|-|-|-|-|-|-|-|-|-|-|-|-|- DA: Well, if not, then he needs his head examined, because he was just signed to take Rage on ONE ON ONE on the supercard, Shattered Dreams in _two weeks time_. And you can bet, after this, Rage will NOT take it easy. CK: But that's not all that transpired on Ring Wars, Danny, because there was a whole host of other action. We saw something that we weren't expecting. The _end_ of the relationship between President Winters and former Commissioner, Patrick 'Heat' Seeker. DA: They had a disagreement, and it quickly turned to violence. It's a good think Winters had Branchini there for him, because Seeker was about to lose his cool. CK: Lets take a look at the highlights of that situation. ------------------- FLASHBACK: Seeker/Winters Confrontation ------------------- [pop!] PS: So, Mr. Winters, you wanna pull rank around here? You want to treat me like one of your puppets? Well brother, you got another thing coming. You think I am pissed about you throwing a wrench in my plans? It aint that, I was going turn on Bruce and Snow eventually anyway, so who the hell cares about that crap. You made the mistake of crossing ME, the "ice storm" decided to play with fire ... and you will soon learn that when you play with fire... [Seeker looks into his eyes with a menacing glance, directed straight at President Winters.] PS: ... you get burned. [Seeker stares at Winters, and begins walking towards him. Winters backpedals a bit.] RW: And Seeker is walking towards the president! He's got him backed into the corner! Seeker is right in his face, and Winters is now curled up in the corner! [Winters raises the mic to his face, as he's figeting, trying to avoid contact with Seeker.] CW: I thought you might do this, so ... [A body flies into the ring and hammers Seeker with a chair from behind.] RW: And somebody just hit Seeker with a chair! The man is laying in a few quick stomps as Winters rolls under the bottom rope and makes his escape! Winters is taking off! DA: That's .. that's James Branchini! The newcomer who beat Fang earlier on! RW: Your right! It is Branchini! He hits Seeker with a couple more stomps, and now he's taking off! Seeker is back up and he chases him out of the ring! Branchini is over near Winters, and what the hell connection do _they_ have?! [Branchini and Winters hug.] RW: Wow! It didn't take Winters too long to find a new right hand man! But when Seeker gets his hands on either one of them, we're in for something BIG! [Seeker mouths the words 'piece of shit' at Winters and Branchini as they take off to the back. Seeker kicks the chair out of the ring. He stares into the crowd, and they give him a meager pop.] ------------------- AND WE'RE BACK! ------------------- DA: See that? Patrick Seeker should have known at the Winters _always_ has a backup plan. He's careful and cunning, and he's never going to let a measley Commissioner get one up on him. CK: Some would agree with that statement. But Winters clearly provoked Seeker by altering his booking decision, and not telling him about it. On top of it, Winters blamed Seeker for the entire miscommunication. DA: Like I said, good thing he had Branchini there. CK: And James Branchini, a newcomer to MVW, must have thought he could easily get on the president's good side by watching his back. He whacked Seeker in the back with the chair and allowed just enough time for Winters to roll out of the ring into the safe haven of the outside of the ring. DA: This situation's going to _explode_ as two big matches have come out of it. Seeker taking on Winters on Ring Wars, and Seeker taking on Branchini on Shattered Dreams. CK: Winters _must_ have some sort of ace up his sleeve. He's been challenged a number of times on Ring Wars, and never has he accepted a match for his own company. This leads me to believe that he's got something in mind. DA: I don't know about that, he's a stand up guy. If he says he's going to fight, he's going to fight. CK: We'll find out in due time. But also on Ring Wars, was one of the best MVW matches ever, perhaps. Stone Harris and Rage took it to a fifteen minute time limit draw, Danny, and those were fifteen _helacious_ minutes. DA: I can't believe Harris actually lasted fifteen minutes in the ring with Rage. CK: Each man took the best the other had to offer, but it wasn't enough to eliminate a competitor from the match. But since Rage and Harris had a draw, Harris was left in the number one contender's slot, and will face his good friend Owen Neo for the MVW Title at Shattered Dreams. DA: I'm not so sure that's fair. I think Rage deserves the match. It was stated at the beginning that the winner of that would most likely be the one to recieve the shot. And it was a draw, so isn't Rage just as deserving? CK: It all depends on how you look at it. Lets take a quick look at the closing moments of that contest. ------------------- FLASHBACK: Stone Harris v. Rage ------------------- [Both wrestlers are exhausted ...] RW: Harris grabs him in a front chancery, and lifts him up to his feet! He hooks the near leg -- FISHERMAN'S SUPLEX BY STONE! ONE! TWO! NO! RAGE POWERS OUT! RAGE POWERS OUT! DA: You think he's gonna quit so easy? RW: Easy?! This match has been NOTHING easy for these two wrestlers! They are fighting their hearts out, seeking glory! Both men back to their feet again, and Harris with an irish whip -- no! Reversed by Rage! Rage catches him -- . . . ABSOLUTION! GOD DAMNIT! THE ABSOLUTION! . . . . ONE! . . . . . . . . . . . . . . TWO! . . . . . . . . . . FOOT ON THE ROPE! HARRIS WAS SAVED! HARRIS GOT HIS FOOT ON THE BOTTOM ROPE, AND THIS ONE IS STILL NOT OVER! THIS CONTEST WILL CONTINUE, EVEN AFTER THE ABSOLUTION! DA: Shit, shit, shit, shit, shit! How the hell did he get his leg up there? I'm shocked that he's concious! RW: Who said he is? Rage, now, is a little bit pissed off! He grabs Harris and brings him up to his feet! Rage sets Harris up on the top turnbuckle! Rage looking for a superple -- HARRIS with a right hand! He staggers Rage back! DA: What the hell?! HARRIS HOOKS HIM! RW: HE LEAPS OVER HIM WITH A HANGMAN'S NOOSE NECKBREAKER! HE HIT THE HANGMAN'S NOOSE OFF THE TOP ROPE! FOR THE LOVE OF GOD, IF THIS ISN'T THE END, I DON'T KNOW WHAT IS! . . . . . . ONE! . . . . . . . . . . . . . . TWO! . . . . . . . . . . . . . . RAGE KICKS OUT! RAGE KICKED OUT OF THE HANGMAN'S NOOSE FROM THE TOP! GOOD GOD ALMIGHTY! DA: YEAH! YEAH! YEAH! RW: BOTH MEN ARE EXHAUSTED, AND THEY'RE LAYING DOWN ON THE MAT, NOT KNOWING WHAT TO TRY AGAINST EACHOTHER NEXT! I DON'T KNOW WHICH ONE IS DAMAGED WORSE! [Both men lay on the mat, half concious.] RW: I CAN'T BELIEVE WHAT WE'RE WITNESSING! DA: Both of them are getting back up! I can't believe their getting back to their feet! RW: Harris and Rage are both standing! And Harris with a right hand! [Rage staggers.] RW: And Rage returns the favor! [Harris staggers.] RW: And Harris with a right! [Rage staggers.] RW: And another! And another! And ANOTHER! [Rage falls, Harris collapes onto the mat.] RW: It's all a matter of who crawls over to make the cover! this has been an absolute war from start to finish, and -- [ding, ding, ding] RW: What the hell?! [The crowd erupts into boos, as the referee signaled for the bell.] ------------------- AND WE'RE BACK! ------------------- CK: And we left off, right there, with the referee signaling the bell because the time had run out, and you've got to believe there will be another day for these two men to meet. A third contest. DA: I hope it's sooner than later. Actually, it'd be nice if Harris snagged the belt from Neo at Shattered Dreams, and Rage got his shot the next night! CK: That's wishful thinking on your part. Another thing we have to mention, is Steven Erickson and Ronnie Black. After the pair defeated Veyliam Kai and Ezra Martin in a wild card tag, they _once again_ broke into a brawl. DA: Because Ronnie Black is nothing but a weak little baby. He can't wrestle his way out of a wet paper bag, Erickson's sick of it. Erickson got the pin in that match, remember? CK: Well, after Ronnie hit the Fade to Black, Erickson tagged himself in and made the cover on Martin, which infuriated Black. The two took eachother on in the center of the ring, and were broken up. DA: They should have let them fight. CK: And of course, in other matches, James Branchini defeated Fang, who is no longer with the company, fired by Mr. Winters. And Mr. Majesty, who people seem to enjoy, defeated XYZ in his debut match. DA: Yeah, I'm a Mr. Majesty mark. That guy makes me laugh my ass off, Curtis. And he can _wrestle_ on top of it! CK: He's certainly got a bright future ahead of him in MVW, if he keeps up the good work, but the way he won the match was not exactly what I'd call fair. He had a handful of tights. DA: You do what you gotta do. CK: And one more _huge_ thing to occur on Ring Wars -- Snow captured the Mohawk Valley Wrestling Television Title in his three way match with Bruce and Crippler! And during the match, Crippler's ankle was broken! DA: That's right, after a Bruce dragonscrew legwhip, Crippler's ankle was twisted in an awkward direction. I don't think we'll be seeing him for a while. CK: And Snow capitalized on Bruce while the referee was taking Crip to the medics in the back, and Bruce and Snow had a WAR with the VCR that was at ringside. Both men hammering eachother, but Snow coming out with the better of the exchange. Snow hit a double arm DDT on the VCR for the cover, the count, and the TV Title. I'd congratulate him, but I'm not sure he knows what the word 'congratulate' means. DA: That was a very rude statement, Curtis. Snow is an exceptional talent, regardless of his life outside of MVW. That guy can fly. He must watch a LOT of lucha tapes. CK: And the other big news from Ring Wars -- DA: Why are you changing the subject? CK: -- is 'Dirty' Dick LeBaron scoring the upset win on the Empire State Champion in a non title encounter. Dick LeBaron defeated Donovan Keltrane, in what was an EXCELLENT match. He turned a suplex into a brainbuster, and got the pin. Keltrane was very upset. DA: I'm happy for ole Dick. CK: And I think that covers all our Ring Wars bases, except for the unveiling of the Mystery Man. He turned out to be a grappler that we don't know. Jason Patrick. DA: Patrick is _excellent_. CK: He took Neo to an outstanding match, before being disqualified for the interference of Rage. Patrick is looking forward to a bright future in MVW, and hopes to damage Owen Neo as much as possible in his stay, here. DA: Quality goal. CK: But I heard a good friend of his, Chase Firebird, isn't very happy about it. DA: Too bad. CK: Chase, if you don't know, works behind the scenes in Mohawk Valley Wrestling, and subs for missing athletes on house shows, ect. He's grown to be close friends with Patrick, and is disappointed in his actions. We'll see what develops out of that. But before we do, we have more Ringsider to come! Next up? |-|-|-|-|-|-|-|-|-|-|-|-|-|-|-|-|-|-|-|-|-|-|-|- Mohawk Valley Wrestling's Rankings! |-|-|-|-|-|-|-|-|-|-|-|-|-|-|-|-|-|-|-|-|-|-|-|- DA: Who hoo, my favorite! CK: First, lets look at the contenders for Owen Neo's MVW Heavyweight Championship ... MVW HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPION: Owen Neo [6-1-0] 1. Stone Harris [3-1-1] 2. Rory Braden [5-1-1] 3. Rage [4-2-0] DA: Looks like last week! CK: As you can see, there's been no major changes to the Heavyweight Title situation. Since Rage drew Stone Harris, their positions stay the same. Stone Harris, however, will be getting his title shot at Shattered Dreams as Rage takes on Rory Braden. The winner of the Rory Braden/Rage match is certainly going to be catapulted to number one contender status. DA: Perhaps. That is, if the title stays in the hands of Neo. If Stone takes the title, however, you'd have to assume Neo will automatically take the number one contender position until he recieves a rematch. CK: That's a very good point, Dan. Now, the Empire State division: MVW EMPIRE STATE CHAMPION: Donovan Keltrane [4-3-0] 1. Dick LeBaron [4-2-0] 2. Patrick Seeker [2-0-0] 3. Ronnie Black [1-3-0] 4. Steven Erickson [1-2-0] DA: Once again, reminiscent of last week! CK: Very true. Donovan Keltrane, who was defeated in a non title encounter by Dick LeBaron, remains your champion. He will, however, be forced to defend the title against LeBaron at Shattered Dreams. DA: And look! Erickson makes a jump! CK: A rather large jump. Steven Erickson is quietly gaining steam in MVW, and due to his hot feud with Ronnie Black, both are working their way up the ladder. Those two, of course, will meet at Shattered Dreams as well. DA: And Seeker? CK: Pat Seeker has proved himself as an _excellent_ wrestler, but you have to believe if he loses his Shattered Dreams match against Branchini, he may work his way back down that list. DA: And if he wins? CK: He could have an ES title shot in his future. MVW TELEVISION CHAMPION: Snow [4-2-0] 1. M-Dub [1-4-0] 2. Jason Patrick [0-1-0] 3. James Branchini [1-0-0] 4. Mr. Majesty [1-0-0] 5. XYZ [2-3-0] DA: Some say the TV Title division is getting stale. CK: But I beg to differ. You've got three _great_ new talents trying to climb the ranks. M-Dub mysteriously recieved the number one contendership postition with no real explanation. He did, however put up two VALIANT efforts against MVW Champion Owen Neo, and a valiant effort against Rage, so that could have something to do with it. DA: When you've giving the MVW Champ and the number 3 contender to the belt runs for their money, you've gotta think you deserve a shot at the TV strap. CK: And he will get his short, providing Snow is able to retain his strap against Ronnie Black on Ring Wars. DA: And what about Majesty, Branchini, and Patrick? CK: Three _excellent_ newcomers. Majesty debuted with a victory over the number five contender, XYZ, which put him in the number four slot. Branchini relieved Fang of his position in MVW, which has put a smile on the face of Winters, and Patrick took Neo to an _excellent_ match on Ring Wars. They're all looking good. DA: And XYZ? CK: He has Ryan Delaney to worry about. Delaney claims he used to idolize XYZ, until XYZ came over to America and changed his style. It will be interesting to see how they match up. CONTENDERS FOR CONTENTION: 1. Dante Madison [0-0-0] 2. Ryan Delaney [0-0-0] 3. Scott Sexton [0-0-0] DA: And of course, the three newcomers. CK: Dante Madison, who is a second generation athlete, will be making his debut on Ring Wars. Delaney has his match against XYZ, which could make or break his career early on, and Scott Sexton will meet Mr. Majesty at Shattered Dreams. They all are recieving opportunities, it's just a matter of who takes it up. MVW TAG TEAM CHAMPIONS: Vacant 1. Delta Destroyers [0-0-0] DA: Should we even comment on this one? CK: Winters is busily negotiating contracts to bring teams into MVW. Until he does that, we may not see too much tag team action occuring. And THAT was the MVW Contendership Rankings. Remember, this all changes on a weekly basis, but NOBODY is ever out of the running for a title, if they play their cards right. |-|-|-|-|-|-|-|-|-|-|-|-|-|-|-|-|-|-|-|-|-|-|-|- Next Week On ... Ring Wars! |-|-|-|-|-|-|-|-|-|-|-|-|-|-|-|-|-|-|-|-|-|-|-|- CK: Here's the lineup -- Ryan Delaney v. XYZ 'the Odyssey' Dante Madison v. Mr. Majesty Ronnie Black v. Snow [c] [TV Title] Patrick 'Heat' Seeker v. 'Ice Storm' Chris Winters Jason Patrick & Rage v. Owen Neo & ??? DA: This week looks like it could get VERY interesting. CK: Certainly. Ryan Delaney will get his match with XYZ, which he basically pleaded for to the front office. He wants to teach XYZ a lesson for letting him down. DA: Delaney says he was an XYZ fan, until XYZ came to the states. Now he wants to teach one of his idols a lesson in wrestling. It should be an interesting match to watch. CK: And then, Dante Madison makes his debut taking on Mr. Majesty, who really turned some heads in his debut last week against XYZ. We don't know too much about Madison, other than the fact that he comes from a wrestling family, and is a _wrestler_. DA: But we do know Majesty kicks ass, he showed us last week. CK: But he also won with a handful of tights. I'm sure the ref will be looking out for Majesty during the match. Hopefully, the match will be smooth and clean. And you can't forget, on Ring Wars, Ronnie Black gets his television title shot against Snow. That should be intriguing. DA: It will be interesting to watch Snow beat his ass. And it will also be interesting to see how M-Dub and Steven Erickson react to the match. Of course, Erickson would love his match against Black at SD to be a TV Title match, but would he ever resort to helping the helpless Black? CK: We'll find out Monday. And M-Dub, who has Snow for the TV Title at Shattered Dreams, would obviously be interested in Snow retaining, so we could see two men helping their future opponents. That's an interesting prospect. And then, on top of all this, you have former Commissioner Patrick 'Heat' Seeker taking on President Chris Winters. DA: I'm suprised Winters accepted the match, but I expect it to be good. CK: Hmmm ... and something I just found out on the top of the show, guess who Owen Neo's partner will be for Ring Wars, Danny? DA: Who? CK: Rory Braden. DA: WHAT?! CK: Owen Neo and Rory Braden will TEAM UP to take on Jason Patrick and Rage. After Neo's begging, last week, Braden answered him back by saving his hide. And now, the two are teaming up, but can they get along? DA: Why would Braden help him? It just doesn't add up. CK: If you recall, the two have history together. Neo beat Braden in the MVW Title Tournament, and that's the only scar on Braden's record. I'm sure Braden feels that if he helps Neo, there may be a title shot in his future. DA: That match would be earthshattering. CK: Well, that's about all the time we have for this week. Stay tuned on Monday for Ring Wars, and NEXT WEEK on MVW Ringsider, we will go over SHATTERED DREAMS! Goodnight, everybody, and thanks for watching!
