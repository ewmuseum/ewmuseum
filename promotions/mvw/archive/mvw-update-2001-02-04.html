Sunday, February 04, 2001 xxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxx In this release: - News - President's Memo - Roster - Contact List - Rankings - Recent Results - Schedule - Upcoming Cards - Quote of the Week xxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxx NEWS! xxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxx - Word on the street is future MVW wrestler SNOW has developed some sort of problem involving an addiction. Editing may occur, but in some way shape or form your news will occur in this column in the next update! =) xxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxx PRESIDENT'S MEMO! xxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxx MVW! Big changes. Ring Wars for the 19th has been canceled! It has been replaced by Night Two of MVW: The Ascension, and the brackets have been released! The tournament has been changed from a one night eight man tournament to a two night sixteen man tournament. It might be wise to wait until the Press Conference has passed to turn in your first tournament roleplay. That way, you can get a better idea of what your opponent is all about. The first roleplay show will come out on Friday the 9th, and the following Friday will be the second roleplay, so you will have _two_ chances to roleplay for the tournament. Unfortunately, I'm not going to be able to allow match strats for these tournament matches, because to allow time to write them all. Think about it, if I couldn't write at least _part_ of the first round matches ahead of time, the cards would never come out. So keep that in mind, folks, don't bother writing them up. The tournament will be based on _RPs_ alone. Also, don't forget to put together your piece for the press conference, but don't mention your opponent in your statement, just introduce yourself. Because, in character, your guy doesn't know he's in the tournament, yet. Get it? Got it? Good. Questions, comments, complaints are always welcome. Send them in my direction, if need be. =) That's all, Briggs xxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxx ROSTER! xxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxx "ANGEL OF CHAOS" FALLEN (6'1", 224 lbs, Face) - Fallen, real name Robert Taylor, lived a normal life just like every kid. He was just average, and that's it. He had friends, he had money, he had fun, he basically had a life. Later, though, he felt the urge to change his ways of living. He thought maybe he should try a new lifestyle, and so he did. That is where Fallen was born. He is a dark man, who has no allies. He trusts nobody, he befriends nobody. Fallen wants nothing to do with anybody else in this world, and that is just the way he likes it. He is a man of many words, but he always knows how to kick some ass. ANGEL SAGRADA (5'5", 140 lbs, Heel) - The young Angel is very cocky and very, very arrogant. He hides his bitterness and redemption underneath his mask of his arrogance and "high-and-mighty" attitude. He enjoys to slap you around a bit with his words and to bring you down. Let's put it this way, he gets a natural high from being a rude and aggrivating to whoever or whatever's around him or associated with him. His attitude, however, can be backed up with athletecism. The diminutive luchador has been at it since a very young age, and brings absolutely an absolutely unbelievable repetoire of moves to the table. "DARKHEART" DONOVAN KELTRANE (6'2", 223 lbs, Heel) - A perfectionist to the core, Donovan has always had the best and has always forced himself to be the best. Despite the vain, uncaring exterior he presents to the world, Donovan has always been especially dedicated to his training. He is somewhat of an egoist, believing himself to be one of the most beautiful creatures walking the earth. Because of this, and his appettite for destruction, he has determined that beauty is strength, and vice versa. He remains a perfectionist, and during matches he will ridicule his opponents for making mistakes - and then make them pay for their insolence in attempting to fight against him. "DIRTY" DICK LEBARON (6'4", 278 lbs, Heel) - "Dirty" Dick LeBaron is a brash loudmouth who loves nothing more than to get into a good scrap. At 50, he's seen it all, done it all, and is not impressed by the younger generation of grapplers. In fact, he has taken it upon himself to prove that the young guns can't handle an old cannon such as himself. And seldom has he been proven wrong. LeBaron has a solid build, characterized by a barrel chest, strong though relatively undefined biceps and triceps, a prominent keg-like stomach, and a set of knobby knees. EZRA MARTIN (6'4", 250 lbs, Face) - After graduating college, Ezra decided that computer engineering wasn't exactly what he wanted to go into, but instead, go into some sort of ministry. After being unable to find any positions and working as a computer engineer simply to have a job, Martin took on the hobby of wrestling, where he was soon discovered and hired as Ezra Martin, Ezra being one of Martin's favorite Bible characters. Now, he can live his dream of being in the ministry field, as he ministers to both fans and wrestlers. M-DUB (6'1", 221 lbs, Heel) - M-Dub has competed in many federations, under many different "gimmicks" given to him by various promoters, trying to cash in on current crazes. He's a very cocky man with a very bad meanstreak trying to make a living and become the 'next big thing' in Mohawk Valley Wrestling. Not to be mistaken for another, M-Dub sports a unique appearance. His long black dreadlocks hang in front of his face. He also wears a black goatee with a matching moustache. M-Dub is ready to take what he feels is his, and will stop at nothing to top the MVW. MITCH "SURE FIRE" GATLIN (6'2", 235 lbs, Heel) - Mitch seems to think he is gods gift to wrestling calling him self "The New Breed Of Wrestler". He does not like all the older wrestlers in the sport, and feels they should step out of the spot light and retire. Although he's careful, Mitch likes to put his body on the line in all of his matches. He could be a future MVW star, just ask him. PLEX (6'2", 300 lbs, Face) - The attractive, natural athlete in Plex shines for the world to see. Although he may be a bit on the conceited side, Plex has a good handle on morals and virtue. His chiseled physique is an astounding sight to see, and the fans seem to eat up his every motion. If Plex can get his mind of the females and the glory of the professional wrestling scene, he will be a force to be reckoned with in the MVW. Coming off an injury that occured on the set of his latest released movie, Plex, real name Jason Williams, seems to be in the best shape of his life. It's do or die for this young man, and chances are 'die' won't be his choice. RAGE (6'8", 299 lbs, Heel) - Used to work as a security professional for CHAOS!!! wrestling, trained with workers at NPW (Northeastern Pro Wrestling) shows and traveled to Midwestern Pro Wrestling since NPW closed its doors. Rage had a torturous childhood, and enjoys easing his own pain by inflicting it on others. He's very bitter and vengeful about life and society in general. He's very methodical, doing as much damage as possible to the weakest area he can find. He is also very cold and callous, believing no one's pain and suffering is equal to his own past. He is willing to hurt anyone and everyone to get the respect or fear he feels has been witheld from him. REGINALD "STALLION" WORTHINGTON-SMYTHE (6'4", 250 lbs, Face) - Stallion does the haughty upper class routine, with a sneer and a raised eyebrow. But he doesn't put anyone down, he knows not everyone can be a gentleman and a consummate professional like him, but he doesn't hold that against them. In fact he believes that if his fans watch him closely enough, some culture may rub off on them. RONNIE BLACK (6'1", 225 lbs, Face) - No Fear. For some, that statement is a cool thing to say, or simply a logo on their t-shirt. But for Ronnie Black, it's a way of life. This African American competitor has dealt with adversity in his life and overcome it. He believes to the very depths of his soul that no one and nothing can prevent him from reaching his goal - being the very best. He will never give up, never stop, never surrender - whether it's in a fight, or with a friend. His father, an ex-Marine (in Vietnam), taught him the meaning of Semper Fi - Always Faithful. Loyality is extremely important to him, and if nothing else, he'll always have his integrity. RORY BRADEN (5'7", 186 lbs, Heel) - Rory's an angry, bitter young man.  He's got a chip on his shoulder bigger than you could believe.  He resents anyone who's bigger than he is - and that's mostly everyone.  He's got a big mouth and likes to run it, but he likes to back it up even more.  The kind of guy who goes looking for a fight.  He doesn't make friends very easy.  Sticks to himself.  Hot tempered.  Very, very hot tempered.  The slightest thing can get him going.  Also has a fondness for alcohol.  Never wrestles drunk, but before or after a match, he frequents bars.  In general, he's a hard edged individual ... very no frills, no nonense.  A fighter in the truest sense of the word. SNOW (5'9", 140 lbs, Heel) - Snow has no wrestling history whatsoever. Just a crackhead found off the street, signed to a MVW contract only to fill roster space. Snow is a easy going guy to get along with. But, the little guy has a temper. When enraged, he can be one bad dude. STEVEN ERICKSON (6'2", 205 lbs, Face) - He received his first ego check in Mark Stone's WWO. He was the laughing stock of the Cruiserweight division, and the source of many insults. That was until he visited PAIN (Physicians Advocating Innovative Neoprocedures), they gave him his 'happy pills' though in return he was to do their dirty deeds, he tired of this and de-nounced his friendship with PAIN and no longer needs the pills. Now he is even more dangerous then ever, and facing him could mean a free trip to the emergency room. STONE HARRIS (6'2", 241 lbs, Face) - Born to a stock broker and a Japanese woman, Stone was brought to the United States by his father while his mother stayed in Japan. At the age of twenty two, he decided to find his mother. After arriving in Japan, he found that his mother had died two years prior. Distraught and without money, Stone started wrestling, believing that he had found his calling. He stayed in Japan to wrestle until the promotion closed it's doors. Confused on what to do next, Stone took his savings and flew home looking for work. He thinks that he found his way in the MVW. VEYLIAM KAI (5'11", 215 lbs, Face) - Noted is Kai's ability to not give away feelings, he hides all emotion, most of the time. When he does release emotion, its crazy, intense, raw, pure.. good stuff. hes quick on his feet, and also has a great sense of balance, but Kai isn't the greatest of wrestlers. He's somewhat green, and knows few moves, but is looking to add to his arsenel in his quest to capture the Mohawk Valley Wrestling Title. XYZ (5'9", 202 lbs, Heel) - XYZ is a Japanese competitor who saw fit to come over to America to try his skills. Now competing for Mohawk Valley Wrestling, he is hoping to get a firm grip on American wrestling. XYZ is a brash, outspoken individual who is highly critical to everyone. A man that will never quit when the goin' gets rough and a man that can push buttons on people like no one else can imagine. xxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxx CONTACT LIST! xxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxx Guys, I realized that you need some way of contacting one another for angles and soforth.
