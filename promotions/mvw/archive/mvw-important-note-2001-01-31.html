Hi, guys! Just a few quick items of note. Firstly, the following will be our last call for applications. We still need a handful more to fill out the roster. All applications must be in my box before February 2nd. This is _imperative_. We don't have a lot of time before the press conference, and the cards will be starting up shortly. Introduction xxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxx FROM THE ASHES (OOC) After spending the last full year in the grave, Mohawk Valley Wrestling is about to make it's un-ignorable return to the e-wrestling world, only this time, things will be different. In the past, Mohawk Valley Wrestling was an organization with stiff competition and an entertaining atmosphere. It's major problem was it's noticeable lack of organization and consistency. This time around, things have changed. I, [name withheld], promise to do all that I can to make this an entertaining and fun organization to compete in. I, [name withheld], promise to work to achieve greatness for the organization that once fought to be one of the best regional organizations on the net. All it takes is a flexible roster and a couple of writers, and Mohawk Valley Wrestling will be on it's way back to the top where it belongs. Past superstars such as Stigmata, "Legacy" Paul Kiljoy and "Keen" Kurt Orr are gone, but not forgotten. Today is for the stars of the future. Any man who sets foot in the MVW has his shot to make it to the top, and any man with the drive and determination to get there, will get his due. THE RETURN A former professional wrestler has decided to stake his claim in the wrestling world by rising Mohawk Valley Wrestling from the ashes. This man is "Ice Storm" Chris Winters. In the past three years, Chris Winters has not only been an active professional wrestler, but also a fan. He felt there was a severe lack of good solid wrestling, and is obsessed with obtaining success in his future endeavor. Laying his neck on the line, Winters purchased the rights to Mohawk Valley Wrestling, along with the equipment necessary to open shop, from former owner Frank R. Luciano. Eager to open up the organization and set up shop, Winters scheduled an eight man _one night_ tournament for the Mohawk Valley Wrestling Heavyweight Championship. This tournament will take place on Saturday, February 17, 2001. THE ASCENSION On Saturday, February 17th, a champion will be crowned. Mohawk Valley Wrestling will finally return to the wrestling world after a lengthy absence. The brackets to the one night tournament will be announced in due time for preparation. xxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxx 2. How To Succeed xxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxx HOW TO WIN MVW will run on a closed flash system. Roughly fifty percent of your match will be decided by your weekly roleplay. Like I said, each MVW competitor will be allowed one roleplay per week. The importance of a roleplay is enormous. You must use your weekly roleplay to talk about upcoming matches, push angles and perhaps most importantly ... develop your character. Please, no novels. Get to the point, send it in, and be done with it. Approximately twenty-five percent of your match will be decided by strats. For each match you have, you are expected to send in a quick little shimmy telling us your basic strategy, a couple of high spots, and a bump or two you'll take. That's all. The last twenty-five percent of your matches will be decided by momentum and participation. This is to the staff's discretion. A wrestler who is participating in a major angle, has compiled a decent record and roleplayed for the match has a better chance of winning than a newcomer, but upsets will occur on occasion as well, I'm sure. CONTRACTS Each wrestler will be required to sign a contract for a said number of weeks, normally ending with a supercard or a Pay Per View. The fewest number of weeks contracts will be allowed to be signed for is ten weeks. Clarification: If you don't plan to stay in the MVW for at least a few months, don't bother joining. If you sign our contract, we are legally allowed to use your character on any Mohawk Valley Wrestling broadcast until the time on your contract is up. If there are extenuating circumstances, we can discuss, but generally this rule will not be flexible. LONG TERM GOALS By no means will a wrestler be counted out of title contention due to his record. Through hard work and participation, any wrestler can receive a push. Kissing ass will not help you in your attempts to attain championship status, but good roleplaying, lots of participation and cooperation will help you on your way. Your win-loss column is important, but it is not the say all end all of your status with the organization. One loss will not kill you, neither will fifteen. And anyone who complains about jobbing for the sake of an angle with either be jobbed again for their attitude or simply canned, no ifs ands or buts. Seniority helps your standing in the organization, so if you suck up your losses and take them like a man, continuing your angles and having fun at the same time, you may be pleasantly rewarded. xxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxx 3. Cards xxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxx MOHAWK VALLEY RING WARS Each Monday, Mohawk Valley Ring Wars will come to you from a different location on the East Coast. Formerly taking place in Gyms and Bars across the Mohawk Valley, Ring Wars will now be held in more desirable locations, due to the money Chris Winters pumped into the organization. Ring Wars will house four to six top notch MVW matches. The majority of these matches will be written in full play by play form, but if people aren't willing to help with the writing, some will be shortened slightly in a highlight form. This will not take away from the action, as it will only shorten the load a small amount, but it will be helpful to your president to prevent burnout. If your match ends up in highlight form, try not to be disappointed. It would be more disappointing to you if the card never came out at all. This is just one of the measures being taken to make sure MVW doesn't fold or take hiatus, presidents need a break once and a while, too. HOUSE SHOWS Also each week, on Thursdays, MVW will hold a house show. This house show will be written in summary form, and should be mentioned by your wrestler in some way shape or form in a roleplay. Strats will not be necessary for house shows, the matches will be decided by your heat, current angles and roleplaying alone. This is just to give you a little something extra to read each weak, and it will beef up the rankings a bit to give you a better idea of how you're doing in the organization. Also, the house show will be a good way for just about everybody in MVW to get a match each week, so you don't feel cheated if you're not included on a weekly Ring Wars Broadcast. SUPERCARDS Roughly once every two months, MVW will broadcast a grand supercard. Supercards will normally be broadcast on Sundays, and should house the conclusions of old angles and feuds and the start of new ones. This will be where most of the major title matches will occur, along with the start or end of a major push. The conclusions of Supercard matches will not be based on your roleplaying for that card alone, but will also take into effect how you've done since the last supercard. Everything will be taken into consideration to decide these matches. xxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxx 4. Titles xxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxx MOHAWK VALLEY HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPIONSHIP A title that has been in the hands of many a great competitor. The MVW Heavyweight Championship (obviously) is the major title for this organization. It will take much skill and dedication to achieve this goal. Former champions include Shane Slaughter, Stigmata, and more. MOHAWK VALLEY TELEVISION CHAMPIONSHIP The Mohawk Valley Television Championship is our secondary title. Like the MVW Title, it will take skill to achieve this status. Once held by "Legacy" Paul Kiljoy, it has been known to boost a wrestler from high mid card to the main event status. The TV Champ will generally not be a man to bet against. MOHAWK VALLEY TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP This will be the cream of the crop in MVW tag teams. When this title is introduced into the organization depends on how many tag team applicants, we receive. xxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxx 5.
