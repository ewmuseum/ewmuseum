MVW: GoodFella's Press Conference

3 December 1998
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The latest commercial for "Durex" condoms fades to black, and the cameras open up and come into focus. The camera captures an image of a small room, with a stage in the front a few feet high off the ground. On top of the stage, is a podium with the "Mohawk Valley Wrestling" logo in the center, and the "MVW" flag on the wall directly behind it. Curtains line the sides of the stage.] [Standing within the "pit" down in front of the stage are various members of the national, local, and wrestling media. Reporters are seated, holding their notebooks and pens, ready to take notes at a moment's notice while photographers jockey for the closest spot to the stage so they may get the best photographs.] [The curtains begin to flop, and then part as MVW owner [name withheld] emerges and walks up to the podium. [name withheld] is stylish dressed, wearing a white and blue pin-striped long sleeve Tommy Hilfiger button up shirt, black tie, and black J.Crew pants.] AB: Ladies and gentlemen of the media, thank you for coming today. MVW is back, in a major way, as you can tell by our recent shows as well as recent free agent signings. I have a feeling that MVW is on the threshold of greatness, and it's only a matter of time before we hit our stride. But the purpose of this press conference is not to boast about my organization. Rather, it is to announce a recent free agent signing with MVW. I am very pleased to have this group of individuals, rather a family actually, enter this organization. So, without further adieu, please welcome..... ..... led by their manager GOODFELLA ..... ..... "SIR" POLO STETSON ....... and..... ..... "Mr. eWrestling" ...... ..... "DESIRE" ..... BRANDON ...... BLOOOOMMMM! [The media rises to its feet and gives a standing ovation the only way members of the media can. Flashbulbs begin clicking even though the trio has not even entered the room yet.] [All of a sudden, a voice booms over the PA System, drowning out [name withheld] and bringing the media to a stand still as everyone's attention is focused upon the entrance.] VO: Ladies and gentlemen, everyone knows that _I_ give the introductions for the N.E.W.! [As "The Dope Show" by Marilyn Manson plays over the loudspeakers, the curtain parts and out steps "The GF Family"...] VO: That's right, it is I, the savior, the creator, and the leader of the greatest eWrestling family in the history of this sport.... [The flashbulbs flicker and illuminate the room with flashes, much like a strobe light. GoodFella leads his family, up to the podium. GoodFella hands Bloom the wireless mic so GoodFella can speak into the podium's microphone as "The Dope Show" by Marilyn Manson fades.] GF: Please allow me to introduce first..... weighing in at an unbelievable and very impressive 235 lbs., and hails from Manchester, England..... here is... ... da' only _TRUE_ royalty in eWrestling.... .... everyone drop to your knees, bow, and give thanks for the privilege of being honored with his presence.... .... SIR ..... ..... POLO ...... ...... STETSON!!!!! And his tag team partner.... The man known affectionately by its citizens as "Mr. New York City"..... ... this is "The Devil's Advocate".... .... everyone get happy .... .... here is..... ..... "Mr. eWrestling" ..... ... ( : [A nod, a wink, and a smile] : ) .... "Desire" Brandon Bloom! [The media cheers as GoodFella smirks devilishly. His facial bones seem to be made out of granite, with an iron-like jaw and a face that has seen its share of days in the ring. His eyes are a cold blue, and his faded gray hair is slicked back. For a man in his mid-thirties, he still has an impressive physique.] [Polo Stetson stands beside his uncle. Stetson has long, straight, silky black hair which is in a pony tail, which is tied with a ribbon. His facial features are clean cut, and there isn't a blemish, wrinkle, or mole on his "pretty boy" face. He is wearing a real fancy white turtleneck sweater, black boots, and long black tights that say "Stetson" down each pant leg in white lettering. Stetson is also carrying a polo stick in his right hand.] [Bloom stands to the left of his father. But, how can you tell its Brandon Bloom? Because..... .... the tall black top hat that would make Abe Lincoln jealous... .... black tights with black boots that say "Mr. eWrestling" on the right boot, "Mr. NYC" on the other.... .... a long-sleeve white button-up collared shirt that barely covers his torso due to so many of the top buttons left unbuttoned.... .... the solid oak cane that he twirls in his right hand.... ... the face; smooth and tanned, with a pierced eyebrow and two loops in his left ear... the Oakley sunglasses that cover his eyes.... the fine eyebrows, the strong cheekbones, and the dimples- one in his chin, and one on each cheek that flashes whenever he smiles. These facial features are enough to make James Dean jealous.... ... the body; tall and lean. Somewhere beneath the shirt, a thin crop of chest- hair rests over the tight and toned chest, leading down to the pierced belly button... for a body that would make Sylvester Stallone jealous.... .... this leads to the crotch that would make Dirk Digler jealous.... ..... oh yes.... ..... it is definitely "Desire" Brandon Boom.] GF: And it is true, ladies and gents, we have signed with Mohawk Valley Wrestling. This marks the return, and the first time in a year that we all have ventured out into another organization besides National Extreme Wrestling. Our goal here is simple. MVW Tag Team Championship. And it will be ours. Those of you who are familiar with N.E.W. know that when I guarantee something, it happens. So, not only do I guarantee that my boys will become the MVW Tag Team Champions..... .... they will do so in less than a month. [This bold prediction sends the media into a frenzy. Photographs snap zillions of pictures while the reporters jump to their feet and shout out various questions.] GF: [holds his arms up and motions for the media to settle down].... one at a time, one at a time. You, the blonde right there with the nice jugs... go ahead. [reporter]: Lisa McDonald of the New York Times. GoodFella, what is your relationship with N.E.W.? GF: Simple. There is none. I retired from my job as President of N.E.W., a job I held for a year and a half out of N.E.W.'s two-year existence. It was a move I wanted to do way back in April of this year, but Mark Mitchell aka Steven Miller aka the worst President in eWrestling's history fu-..... excuse my French, I mean screwed things up, so I had to return to save the day. The worst mistake I ever made was putting that fool in charge. He's so clueless he couldn't find his ass with both hands. [Crowd laughs.] However, this is all I'm going to say about N.E.W.. For those of you who had questions about the N.E.W. and myself, forget about em'. _BUT_, I will have something to say at the next MVW show about National Extreme Wrestling. You'll just have to turn in to find out..... .... [cheap plug].... and if you want to see what GoodFella has to say about National Extreme Wrestling, email [detail withheld] to see what "The GF Family" has to say. [Reporters jump back to their feet and yell out their questions.] GF: How about you Wade Keller? I see you anxious to ask me something. Go ahead. KELLER: What role will you be playing at MVW's next show? GF: And give the surprise away? C'mon Wade...... your reaching, buddy. I will tell you this much. We will be in attendance. And it will be a debut that MVW.... and N.E.W..... will never forget. But right now, what I want to do is give my boys the chance to speak. So, Polo, if you will..... [GoodFella moves to the side while his British-born nephew walks up to the podium.] "SIR" POLO: Cheerio to all you bloody blokes out in the media. This marks the return of the only man in eWrestling with royalty in their blood to Mohawk Valley Wrestling. Those familiar with my name knows that I reached the finals of the MVW Heavyweight Championship Tournament held earlier this year. But now, I am here, with my uncle and with my cousin, to bring some class.... some royalty.... and some sophistication to this bloody promotion which is over-run by barbarians and louses. And once again, "Sir" Polo Stetson will dominate MVW. Do I do this for money? No. I am richer than your bloody Fort Knox, and I have more wealth than the Queen of England. Will I do this for fame? I already had this. Will I do this for women? Look how beautiful I am..... look at the women I've dated, pure royalty all of them: Fergie before she gained 60 pounds, the late Princess Diana before she seperated from Prince Charles, and all of the Spice Girls. [The media laughs.] Will I succeed? Of course I will. I'm royalty; I have the best trainers throughout the world sculpting this beautiful body and teaching me the fine arts of Greco-Roman wrestling. And for the competition of the MVW? You bloody immigrants.... you bloody imbred trailor-park bastards..... you middle-class, blue collar, working blokes.... you are _ALL_..... [Polo raises his right hand up against his forehead, and extends his thumb and index finger to form the shape of a "L"] .... LOS-SAHS! [The crowd laughs again.] "SIR" POLO: And now, it is time for my American cousin... the _ONLY_ American with any sorts of class.... to speak. Cheers! [Stetson steps aside, and Bloom struts up to the podium. The enormous Abe Lincoln hat is over-sized for his cranium, making his look like a little kid with his daddy's hat on. Sunglasses wrap around his face, covering his eyes, but the pierced right eyebrow sticks out above the glasses. Bloom smirks, exposing the dimples in his cheeks and chin as well as his pearly white and straight teeth.] "Mr. eWrestling": No one can believe it. No one thought this would happen. There would be no way hat the life and blood of National Extreme Wrestling would ever leave. No one thought that its former Heavyweight Champion would cut any ties with N.E.W.. No one thought that its savior would want no part of National Extreme Wrestling any more. Well, I am here today to tell ya that it is true. That's right, I.... ... "the Devil's Advocate" ..... ..... "Mr. N-Why-Cee"...... ..... and "Mr. eWrestling" ..... ( : [A nod, a wink, and a smile] " ) ... "Desire" Brandon Bloom ..... .... who is unquestionably the _BIGGEST_ star N.E.W. ever had, has left the promotion that he put on the map. And now, once again, I help to put another promotion on the map: Mohawk Valley Wrestling. But this time, I am not doing it alone. Oh no...... I brought my family with me. Bloom Sr. and my cousin from England, the true Prince, Polo Stetson. We're here not to take over, but to dominate. We're not going against tradition, or seeking to destroy our boss. Infact, I think Mr. [name withheld] is one helluva a cool guy. [Bloom looks over his shoulder at [name withheld], and gives him a thumbs up, a nod, a wink, and a smile.] We're here simply to put this promotion on higher level of status, and to dominate every division and title this promotion has. And there'll be no stopping us. With GoodFella's brains, Stetson's money, and with my talent, this is the greatest stable or family in the business today. And remember, that this interview was brought to you courtesy of.... ...... "Mr. eWrestling" ...... ( : [A nod, a wink, and a smile] : ) .... "Desire" Brandon Bloom. [Bloom steps away from the podium, and GoodFella steps up as the flashbulbs of cameras click away.] GF: I have one last thing to say before this press conference is over. I'm bringing the dope show to the MVW. Can you handle it? Boys, let's go..... ["Dope Show" by Marilyn Manson begins to play over the loudspeakers as "The GF Family" leave the press conference room. Photographs get their last shots of the Family while reporters try to get in last second questions. The Family ignores them all, and arrogantly walk out the door and leave the room.] [Camera fades.]
