MVW Nostalgia: Tony Houston vs. Lord Foul the Despiser

24 December 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
RSPWF, To add to the already rediculous anticipation for MVW's return (wink), I will be posting one nostalgic MVW matchup each day until the supercard, MVW: The Ascension. This may help the RSPWF in an indirect way. We haven't had a whole lot of on-topic posts, lately. Maybe a few people will be given some nice reading material by this. Anywho, the match is between "Cold and Calculating" Tony Houston and Lord Foul the Despiser. It took place on December 24, 1997 at the annual MVW supercard known as Blizzard Blast. It should provide a decent read, and perhaps get you back into the MVW spirit, although please excuse the ocassional cheesey comments and lame spots. [The crowd, on the tape, is still in awe and surprise as they try to place the familar voice with the seemingly familar face as the man talks on the microphone looking down at Foul, spitting on him.] VOICE OVER - You two skipped town and that lil' fed folded. Well, Lord, I ain't folded what I wanted ain't folded, and that's to whoop your sorry disappearin' 'n reappearing ass. [This man rolls out of the ring and storms up the aisleway and nails Foul across the back of the head with a chair, sending him to the floor as well as the medical unit. The camera then cuts to show him delivering a forward throatbreaker to Jimmy Jimmons. Cut to a throatbreaker on Lord Foul, then David Ferry, then Hollywood Blonde, then Mr. Popular, "Stylin" Tommy Blayze, Vinnie Rescigno, [name withheld] and numerous other PTWL, NEW and INWI opponents. The screen then explodes back to the Utica Memorial Auditorium in Utica, NY. The crowd is pumping.] [return to arena] MARTIN GRIMMER - Wait a minute, here. That wasn't the video package that I prepared. DEVON CANNONS - [laughs] But that certainly was a hell of a show! Now lets get down to ring announcer David Ferry for the introductions. Ferry's felt the Cold Drop before, he'd better stay out of Houston's way this time or he could be in for some troubl -- ["2112 Overture" by Rush hits the PA as the crowd goes wild. A purple blanket of haze envelopes the entire isleway. In an instant, Lord Foul the Despiser steps out through the curtain with a horrid grimace on his face. He slowly makes his way to the ring, not stopping to acknowledge the fan's cheers. He stares at all of the wrestlers in the ring, who seem to be peirced by the glare. Foul makes his way up the ring steps and enters the ring eclipsed in a large purple and blue robe, covered with jewels which represent fallen opponents of the past. Once in the ring, Foul removes the robe and then remains in a full black leather bodysuit, with a crimson five pointed star on the chest, the symbol of the lords. His silver hair is tied back in a ponytail. Foul's build is average. Foul stares down at the ring as the crowd cheers, seeing only purple and the men in the ring.] DAVID FERRY - Introducing first, weighing in at -- [No music plays as "Cold and Calculating" Tony Houston opens up the curtain and storms in, staring at his opponent in the center of the ring. Houston isn't in his wrestling gear as he wears a pair of authentic Tommy Hilfiger jeans, a black "Ringmaster" T-Shirt, a red and blue Tommy Hilfiger bubble jacket with a leather pair of black $200 M_Gear boots. His hair is brushed back and faded to a professional style. His eyes ... his eyes, reflect not only intensity, but appear to be filled with pure and utter hatred. Houston climbs up into the ring to a pop.] DAVID FERRY - I'd appreciate it if next time you'd wait for your introduct -- DEVON CANNONS - COLD DROP! [pop] Houston with a Cold Drop on Ferry and Ferry rolls to the outside holding his throat! Foul jumps on Houston from behind and rains down a few right hands to the back of the head! Houston turns around and hammers him with a kick to the midsection ... COLD DROP! [pop!] He hooks a leg! One! Two! No! I don't know how, but Foul kicked out of that one! MARTIN GRIMMER - Houston tosses Foul to the outside near Ferry and Houston drops through the ropes and goes right after him. Houston lifting him up in a bodyslam position ... drops him head first on the gaurdrail! [pop] Houston with a few boots to the side and the ribs and he tosses [crash] Foul into the ring steps! He's really tearing into him! DEVON CANNONS - Houston with a headbutt and he tosses Foul into the crowd. Houston follows him over and picks up a chair, he's going to nail him with it! Houston's my pick for the winner in this one, folks. This guy's frickin _awesome_! Quite a violent display early on! MARTIN GRIMMER - Houston grabs a chair and rails Foul right in the side of the head. Foul goes back after him and he ducks a chair this time and boots Houston in the midsection. Foul with a double underhook and a [mega pop] Viledriver on the concrete! It's a little early for that, but what the hell, right? We're here at a supercard and they've both thrown their finishers in the first three to four minutes of the contest! DEVON CANNONS - They both want to get this overwith quickly, and rightfully so! Foul drops the chair on the back of Houston's head and stomps the chair a few times viciuosly! He pulls Houston up and they brawl right into the crowd! ["Houston!" chant] Houston hates them, but they love him. Go figure, they're from Utica. MARTIN GRIMMER - Those fans are clearing out a circle and Houston and Foul are having an all out war. They're brawling like school kids with rights and lefts! Foul rips off Houston's Tommy Hilfiger Bubble Jacket and tosses it back into the crowd! Some happy fan just got a $300 coat! Houston's donned in just a pair of jeans and a t-shirt with boots! Where's his wrestling gear? DEVON CANNONS - They're throwing punches again and Foul pulls Houston's shirt over his back and Houston's tangled in it! He can't see! Foul backs up and nails him with a running kneelift ... Houston [pop] flies back and hits the back of his head on a chair! I'm tempted to start an MVW chant! Foul runs up and nails him with a hard boot to the ribs! Foul grabs a fan's coffee and Houston gets back to his feet! MARTIN GRIMMER - Foul's gonna blind him! No! Houston with a quick boot to the midsection and a slap to the face! Foul drops the coffee on the concrete! Houston picks up a chair and hammers him over the head with it. Foul is staggering once again. He doesn't know where he is! A fan hands Houston what looks to be a broken off chair leg! Houston nails Foul with it with all his might! DEVON CANNONS - Foul won't go down! Houston whacks him iwth it once more, and again, and again. If that doesn't make him drop ... Houston nails him in the groin with it [pop] and he hits him with a second Cold Drop of the night! Foul's on his back and he's not moving and now Houston is just soaking up these cheers! This match is an all out war! MARTIN GRIMMER - The Uticans are lifting Houston up ... Mohawk Valley crowd surfing for the fans in the blue seats! That's dangerous! Here comes MVW Cheif of Security, Boomer Sinclair, and he's trying to get the fans to drop Houston. I don't think Houston gives a damn what the hell Sinclair things, though. Foul is attempting to regain his composure out on the concrete and he's on his hands and knees shaking his head. DEVON CANNONS - Foul's taken quite a beating so far in this matchup, I'm not sure how much more of this he can take. Sinclair finally gets Houston down but he doesn't _want_ to be down! Houston grabs Sinclair by the shirt! He might give him a Cold Drop! [pop] No! Foul nails him in the back with something. What the hell was that? MARTIN GRIMMER - Looked like a crow bar, to me, but I'm not too sure where the hell he'd get a crow bar from in the middle of the crowd. Sinclair is trying to make some room for Foul and Houston but these damn fans keep trying to get a peice of the action. That's why crowd fighting isn't a good idea ... Foul is hit with a drink! Foul turns around to face the assailant and Houston dives and clips out his knee! DEVON CANNONS - Sinclair is ordering Houston to move the fight back to the ring, and he obliges, suprisingly. Houston lifts up Foul onto his back and dumps him over the railing. Houston follows him over ... OUCH! Uh oh! [pop] Foul with a groin shot and he slams Houston into the ring apron head first! Houston staggers back and Foul begins choking him out with a handful of electric cables! Houston's flailing his arms and he's in pain! MARTIN GRIMMER - But there's really nothing he can do about it! Foul's got his right knee on the middle of Houston's back and he's pulling back on those cables as hard as he can. This one is anything goes, I guess, because the referee doesn't seem to give a good god damn what the hell they're doing. Fans are throwing sodas at Foul and for once Foul isn't the crowd favorite in a matchup. Not like he cares, or anything. DEVON CANNONS - Foul drops the chokehold and stomps the back of Houston's neck. And he stomps him again. Foul pulls Houston up by the neck and Houston decks Foul with a right hand. Foul counters it with a block and a right hand of his own! Foul with a chop to the chest [whooo] and another [whooo] and he slams Houston head first into the post. These two are slowing down already. MARTIN GRIMMER - Foul rolls him into the ring and follows him in. Foul instantly goes on the attack and he locks Houston into an octopus hold! Houston may be in some serious trouble, here, his neck is being wrenched by that horrible move. He's flailing his arms once again and now Foul is obviously in control. This is a very high impact matchup, folks, one of these guys may end up hurt. DEVON CANNONS - No, one of these guys _will_ end up hurt. MARTIN GRIMMER - There's a ninety nine percent chance you're right, Dev. Houston is trying to find a possible solution to get out of this move, and what he's doing right now isn't working. The ref checks in with Tony, and Tony doesn't want to give up. Foul is screaming at the ref to ask him again, and the ref does. But once again, there's no answer for the ref to indicate him to give up. Foul, out of frustraion, pops Tony Houston's neck good, with that forearm planted hard to the nape of the neck. DEVON CANNONS - Foul is really showing frustration in this match, as you mentioned, he comes off the ropes, flips, and catches Houston's neck as he flips, and goes forward with it! My goodness, that had to be painful. What am _I_ saying? Shit, that was the worst case of whiplash I've EVER seen. Foul is out to kill this man, no doubt. MARTIN GRIMMER - I beleive that was a jumping neck snap. Something out of the ordinary for the methodical Foul to do. But he seems to be very angry. Foul grabs him again and wrenches his neck to the side and buts his thumb in the side of Houston's neck for an Asiatic spike! This is another very dangerous move. With one slip move he can dislodge Houston's adam's apple. DEVON CANNONS - That's not a very pleasant thought. Foul has that wrenched on good and Houston swings his arm up and hits Foul with a thumb to the eye! Foul drops the hold. Houston gets to his feet and he's holding his neck. Foul stalks him and Houston scores with a boot to the stomach! [pop] MARTIN GRIMMER - He grabs Foul and DDTs him down to the canvas! One! Two! Nearfall by Houston! He almost had him. Foul gets up again and he's stunned. Houston gets a running start and drills him with a swinging neckbreaker! The tide has turned completely in just a few seconds! Houston with a legdrop and a cover! One! Two! Kickout by Foul again. DEVON CANNONS - Nothing's working for either man. MARTIN GRIMMER - Houston with a right had to the head and he lifts him up and nails him with an inverted atomic drop. Houston follows it up with a running left arm lariat [pop] and Foul is down again! Houston on a roll! DEVON CANNONS - Houston off the ropes ... kneedrop to the head! There's a cover! One ... two ... thr -- oh, hell no. Foul kicks out. This has been a close one in and out, as both men are trying to hurt each other. And the Uticans don't care. They just love the action. Houston with a blatant chokehold on Foul! The ref gets to two, before Houston releases the hold. He had four seconds. Those extra two can be crucial, if Foul gets up. But Foul is picked up, and put in between his legs! MARTIN GRIMMER - This COULD be a powerbomb ... he picks him up, and releases him into the _turnbuckle_! My God! These two men are risking their bodies, just for the match that has never happened. They're gonna be HURT before the battle royal begins, and that's a true fact. But if they have enough to go on, let 'em fight till they drop dead. This _HAS_ been a good match so far, and it looks to be a better one, as Houston knees away at the lower lumbar area of Foul. DEVON CANNONS - Houston grabs him again and nails him with a head and arm suplex and now it's _Foul_ with the hurt neck. He landed directly on that area and his right shoulder could be in some pain, too. Houston with an elbowdrop. And another. And another quicker one. He covers again. One! Two! Kickout by Foul again. Houston's removing his belt! This could get sticky! MARTIN GRIMMER - Oh god, Devon, don't be disgusting. Houston whips Foul a few times and wraps the black leather belt around the neck of Foul. A bit of a payback for earlier on when Houston got choked out by the steel cables for the Audio\Video equipment. Houston's pouring on the pressure and the whole Utica Memorial Auditorium is on the edge of their seats. DEVON CANNONS - For good reason. [fans begin to stamp their feet] MARTIN GRIMMER - Possibly. Foul is reaching for a rope, and trying to loosen that belt at the same time, but everytime he tries, Houston pulls him back to the center of the ring! Houston lets go now, and begins whipping him like he's a whipping boy! And listen to those horrible smacks! Goodness, Foul's gotta be in pain right about now, with that tight leather on his body. Pain's being dealt tonight, folks. No doubt. Houston throws the belt off some where, and connects with a elbow, right on the top of the head! DEVON CANNONS - Got any BC? Lord Foul the Despiser is going to need it, after he wakes up in the morning. Legdrop by Houston. And there's a cover! One! Two! No! Kickout by Foul! This is _too_ close. How far will these men go, to win a simple match? We're about to see. Houston climbs to the top, and balances ... oh my [bleeping] goodness. A diving elbowdrop onto Lord Foul. And people thought this was the worst time to do things like this ... boy, were they wrong. MARTIN GRIMMER - An incredible move by Tony Houston! Something we've never seen out of the guy. Houston gets back up again and stomps him a few times and Houston pulls Foul up. Foul with a quick shoulderblock to the midsection of Tony Houston! [pop] Foul gets up and double underhook's Houston's arms! Viledriver? No! Houston grabs Foul's legs and a double leg takedown ... Houston grapevines the legs .. SMALL PACKAGE! .. ONE! TWO! THREE! HE GOT HIM! [mega pop] DEVON CANNONS - How the [bleep]?! MARTIN GRIMMER - Tony Houston has won this contest and he rolls to the outside wiht his arms raised high in victory! Foul is stunned as he gets up to his knees and runs his hands through his silver hair! Tony Houston has won this matchup, but I'm sure this is just the first battle of a full scale war! DAVID FERRY - Here is your winner ... "Cold and Calculating" Tony Houston! [pop] DEVON CANNONS - Well, that's all from this one. What a match, and what a close call. Houston pulls it out by a hair, and now he's got a whole lot of bragging rights to boast.
