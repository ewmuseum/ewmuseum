MVW: STIGMATA -- Mercy.

6 December 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The camera opens to Stigmata perched, sitting calmly, on what seems to be a cliff. The sky is a dark blue, the moon is a glistering yellow, the howls of the wild are ever so wicked and intense. A man with a cause sticks out, in deep thought. His hassick blows in the wind, dirt rustles up, getting caught in the heavy wrinkles of the old garment. With a heavy outburst, he speaks.] [STIGMATA] Awaiting. Awaiting, awaiting my judgement. I have reached the ultimate limit of my patience with your deliberate sinfulness. I have gone beyond mercy and love to patiently await your return to grace. I have graciously given my all for years, and still you persist to be sinful. The wonders that await those who live according to mine and the Lord's commands and those who do His will are beyond anything you could imagine. Yet over two-thirds of all souls will be lost forever, forfeiting the magnificence of life eternal with Jesus in heaven. Are you aware, or do I have to shred you even more? God lets you try, God grants you free will, it's been long enough. It's time to use force. Thomas, are you aware? Are you _prepared_ for the judgement? I'm giving you a chance, although, it's too late for your body to be saved, your soul can be saved. Follow me, follow our law, and you shall be freed. As God tries to provoke you back into the faith with his words, I laugh, I laugh, he does his job, I do mine. It's time. [Stigmata rises to his feet, the wind blows in a fury, the hate is in the atmosphere, is it time?] [S] You freely choose to follow evil, to commit every kind of horrific sin. You close your eyes, your ears, and your hearts to me. Your ignore me until it becomes a natural part of your life. You make no contact with God until you become self-reliant. But, _WOE_ to you when the day of judgement arrives. Oh, how you will feel my wraith. Your punishment will be eternal without end, because you neglected me, God, his parents, his angels, his saints. Thomas, all that money and glamour of yours, it will be your downfall. Living the good-life, posing for the magazines, being fed like a king. But be assured now, there is only one king, the king of kings. Nobody is worthy of the wealth which they benefit from the sport of pro-wrestling, Thomas. Thomas.. you wine and dine, you are treated with respect, this clouds your mind, greed, all the seven deadly sins.. This locks you from God, this locks you from listening to me. You just blow this off, continuing to use vulgar, continuing to degrade me, which in turn, degrades God. God hasn't left you yet, Thomas, and maybe.. just maybe.. he will lay his hand down and save you from the pits of _my_ destruction. You lived in constant sin, neglected prayer and acts of kindness, and had no respect for he who created you. Your kind has killed life, infants, elderly, weak and sickly, -- animals to the point of extinction. Polluted the air He gave you, water, and his earth. Yes, you've crucified your own God over and over again, "...for what you do to another you do to Him...," you and your kind. If I vanished forever.. Your suffering would never end, Thomas. "For as your give, so shall you receive." His day of wraith is very rapidly approaching. Mine is all ready here, oh yes, I'm more of a mere cleansing then what he shall do. The blood of all his innocents cry out in agony for revenge and he will answer to the cries to the poor. Some say, this is how the rich get richer, and the poor get poorer. Charity, I bet Rifles' has never done anything to help, he's a demon, a devil, and he will be struck. I am a just man. Each person will be properly repaid for the life he or she has lived, for all the graces that were squandered. Beware, sinners. I am aware of your EVERY thought, word and action. I know your evil motives. RETURN. Satan and hell await to devour your souls. RETURN. Repent today, or be lost FOREVER. The time of mercy is now.. Will you accept my mercy? Amen. [End.]
