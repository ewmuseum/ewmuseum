Progress!

27 January 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hi! Just wanted to let you all know that the recruiting process is going really well so far, for MVW. Just twenty four hours since the announcement of our return, we've recieved a good number of applications. As you can see by the following schedule, we will be getting the ball rolling on the seventh of February. From there, the days leading up to The Ascension will be very busy. If you want to be a part of our starting roster, it's imperative that you get those applications in soon. We'll be closing the roster with twenty singles.
