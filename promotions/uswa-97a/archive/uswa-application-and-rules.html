You Haven't Proven Yourself Yet Until Your Prove Yourself in the USWA!!!

11 May 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
USWA UNITED STATES WRESTLING ASSOCIATION **Here is the app. A few things before you start. We at the USWA would like to have a variety or guys and to do so we will limit the certain amount of guys we have that duplicate a pro wrestler. For example we will not have any more then one Undertaker rip off runing around this fed. ( No Grim Reapers). If you need help think up a guy it is okay to ask the Prez or VP( Only made up guys).Now to the app.** E-Mail Address: Wreslters Name: Height: Weight: Hometown: Good/Bad: Ring Attire: Theme Music: Gimmick: Manager: Finishing Move: What's is the set-up move (Move before actual Finisher): What kind of Wreslter is yours? 1. Amateur Wrestler 2. Top-Notch Brawler 3. All-Rounder 4. Stiff Martial Artist/Grappler 5. Basic High Flyer 6. Basic Brawler 7. Luchador 8. Submission Wrestler 9. High Flyer/Good Tech 10.US Standard Worker 11.Tech Skilled Brawler 12.Tech/Brawling 13.Brawler/Aerially Skilled 14.Daredevil Flyer DO NOT SEND THE APP TO ME, SEND THE APP TO USWA [detail withheld] 1. Role Play. I know you guys have no trouble with this so I'm not going to even worry about it. Let me just say though, the person who role plays a lot and really gets into his character is most likely to win matches. Also don't just send a Role Play in. Make the Role Play mean somthing. Don't just talk. Have somthing to say in your Role Plays. Anyone who doesn't send in role plays with be jobbed and thrown out forever!!!!!!! ** Role-Plays should be sent to me and me only. Please do not hit reply to all.** 2. Sneak Attacks. Send me sneak attacks too!! 3. Maximum limit for wrestlers now is.......2 per screen name. I'm sorry, I know that some of you have more than two, but this will just crowd the league with your wreslters. 4. How do I make a match? Easy simply write who your wrestler wants to wreslte and on what card, and I'll try to book it. Mostly I'll make the matches. 5. How are the matches determined? Well I believe that the best Role-Players should win but also to keep the fed cool I will do things certain ways to form angles. But if you Role-Play and Role-Play good you will do better over all. 6. When are the cards? We have two play by play card each week. Sunday Night Slam and Thursday Night Mayhem. Sunday's card will consist of about 6 or 7 matches and Thursday's card will have 4 or 5 matches on it. 7. What about the belts? 1- USWA World Title 2- USWA United States Title 3- USWA Pro Title 8. What are this feds Features? The USWA will try to give its wrestlers and members the best that it can give. We will have our own webpage, where you can get bios and others great news on the other wrestlers and the fed in general. Plus for those of you who have America Online we will be opening a chat room. ( More news on that later.) Plus the best feature I will felel that we have is two hard working kick ass presidents. The wrestlers in this fed will feel just like they are in the WWF or WCW. USWA Inc. Prez: USWA [detail withheld] VP: USWA [detail withheld] REMEMBER DO NOT SEND YOUR APP TO ME! SEND IT TO USWA [detail withheld]
