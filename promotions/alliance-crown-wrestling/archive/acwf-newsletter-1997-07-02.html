[ACW]f Newsletter 7/2

2 July 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Alliance Crown Wrestling Newsletter, 7/2/97 Results from the House Show in Kitchener, Ontario, 7/1/97: -Ariel Terror dominated Molly O'Rourke, putting her away with the Terror Bomb. O'Rourke was knocked unconscious. -Diana Silk beat Violet Evans in a matchup of fan favorites. Silk defeated Evans using the Silk Experience Side Suplex. Evans, an up and coming grappler, who bears a striking resemblance to Sandra Bullock, had to be helped from the ring. -Roxy Heller destroyed Iris Sweet. Heller never let up throughout the match, as is characteristic of her style. Heller Powerslammed Sweet twice before applying the Hellrazer Powerbomb. Sweet, who looks uncannily like Moira Kelly, had to be hospitalized with a concussion. Heller, a Racquel Welch lookalike, is gunning for Athena Crisis's Alliance Crown Title Belt. Here, she had too much savvy and experience for the young Sweet. -Morgana Quest beat Teri Moan in a grudge matchup of two old friends, now bitter enemies. Quest missed the top rope leg bomb when Moan rolled out of the way. Quest was immobilized by the pain, and Moan capitalized, landing her Full Body Moan Full Nelson Body Slam, but Quest was able to reverse Moan's lazy pin and gain the win. Quest, a dead ringer for Kathy Ireland, wants Dominique Thunder's Super Queen Title. -Desiree Maze upset Holly Harte when Harte went for a Top Rope Superplex. Maze reversed the hold and Harte hit the mat hard with the back of her head. Maze hit a top rope elbow and covered the curled up, unmoving Harte for the win. -In the main event, Athena Crisis, often mistaken for Frederique, defended her Alliance Crown Title against tough veteran Jenny Love, who could do stunts for Julia Roberts. Crisis and Love have a long history together, often teaming, often feuding. Crisis wore a sea-green one piece suit, with black boots and sheer tights. She sported black half-gloves, and wore the title proudly. Love wore a crimson one piece outfit, studded with metal disks. She wore red matching boots, and dark tights. Love attacked Crisis before the bell, but Crisis was able to counter. Crisis gained the early advantage with a series of hip tosses and gut punches, doubling Love over in pain. Love reached for a handful of hair, and, dragging her head forward, drove her fist into Crisis's full, pale lips. Crisis was spitting blood... [...] Jenny Love had Athena Crisis in trouble, pinning her face down on the mat, and stomping on her neck. Crisis moaned in pain. Hair covered her face. Love administered several leg drops to her head and to the small of her back. When Love paused her attack to go to the top rope, Crisis didn't move... [...] Athena Crisis was making a comeback, but she was still groggy from Love's fierce attack. She grabbed at the lacy bodice of Love's outfit, and hit her with a shoulder block to the gut. Crisis whipped Love to the ropes, and ducked under a clothesline. But Love came right back with another clothesline, and Crisis was down again. Love went for the pin, but Crisis just managed to kick out. Love slammed the mat in frustration... [...] Crisis was in control after Love's plancha to the outside missed. Love went down in a heap in a pile of metal chairs, and was unresponsive to the referee's count. Crisis went to the outside after breaking the referee's count, dragged Love onto a table, and hit her with a piledriver. The table split, and Love's head was driven into the cement floor... [...] Bleeding and semi-conscious, Love sucuumbed to the Crisis Control Standing Frankensteiner. Athena Crisis held her retained title aloft, and paraded around the ring. She wiped the perspiration away from her forehead, and combed at her wet, matted hair. She was dizzy, but managed to return under her own power to the locker room area. Love was not so lucky, and had to be carried in a stretcher from the ring, accompanied by Teri Moan and Cat Francis. In other news, Janet Smolenski informed the ACW Commissioner's Office that she wishes to be known from now on as "The Ultimate Beauty." Her finishing maneuver will now be known as the Beautiful Dreamer Cobra Clutch. The Ultimate Beauty grew up in Poland, and moved to this country under the auspices of a modeling contract. She was married to a prominent rock star, before becoming involved in professional wrestling and arriving at the premier ladies's wrestling federation, the ACW. Finally, some bad news. Although the Commissioner's Office has tried to suppress this information, the ACW Newsletter has learned that the ACW is once again in dire financial straits. The future of the organization is in doubt. After last year's bankruptcy, the Commissioner's Office was able to overhaul and reorganize the company, hoping that its improved hierarchy could forestall the bad financial management that had plagued the ACW in the past. However, lack of a steady TV deal and low attendance at house shows across the country have led to more money woes. The ACW's source has informed us that unless things pick up dramatically, not only are the tentatively scheduled PPVs (the DreamGirl Show, the Tag Team 10000 Tournament, the Super Queen Competition) in trouble, but so is the organization itself. The ACW could fall apart. There are rumors a large company is interested in using some of our wrestlers for promotions. However, this remains to be seen. We will have to wait and see what transpires in the coming weeks. In the meantime, our female athletes will continue to compete and improve, and show that we are the greatest female wrestling organization in the world. Until next time...
