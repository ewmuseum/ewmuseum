[ACW]f Newsletter.7/4

4 July 1997

[ACW] Newsletter, July 4, 1997 Happy Independence Day to all our great fans and viewers. Thank you for supporting the ACW, and for reading our Newsletter. Results from House Show in Memphis, TN, 7/3: -Seneca Smith beat Ariel Terror with the Aussie Side Power Bomb. The tall Australian was too much for Terror in this matchup of two heels. Smith has made no secret that she’s after Athena Crisis’s Alliance Crown Title Belt, as are many of the ACW stars. However, Smith will first have to overcome Jacqueline LaRue. Furthermore, her recent surprising loss to Crystal Vice will cost her in the rankings. -Natasha Cruise dominated Sugar Suarez, finishing her off with the Cruise Control Butterfly Piledriver. -Sabrina Wild defeated Brittany Haven with the Wild Thing Superkick. After the match, Wild went to a house mike and reiterated her belief that she is the most beautiful woman in the ACW. When she was booed by ACW fans, Wild lost control of herself. Whipping the dazed Haven into the ropes, Wild delivered another Superkick. Wild dragged the stunned Haven up by her hair and threw her into the corner. Wild hit another Superkick, and Haven was knocked out. But Wild was not finished. She dragged Haven up to her knees, and thrust the house mike into her mouth, demanding that Haven call her "the most beautiful woman in the ACW". Haven only moaned. Wild, the foxy, conniving blonde, choked and repeatedly struck Haven with the mike, and then threw her out of the ring. Wild announced that she would wrestle anyone who denied that she was the most attractive and sexy woman in the ACW. Even though she is one of the more petite and slender wrestlers in the ACW, Wild said she feared no one. Haven was taken from the arena by paramedics. -Mona Sheer beat Stella Still with a figure four leglock. Still, the blonde, former supermodel, reached the ropes, and Sheer broke the hold, but Teri Moan, who was at ringside, hit Sheer with a foreign object. However, the official keenly spotted this infraction, and disqualified Still. Still and Moan beat on Sheer out of frustration. Mona Sheer was in trouble, but her sometime partner and former friend Athena Crisis rushed out for the save. Then Cat Francis came out of the locker room area, and a brawl threatened to ensue. However, members of the Commisioner’s Booking Council came to ringside broke up the confrontation. Crisis helped Sheer back to the locker room. Crisis, of course, was still to face Cat Francis later on in the card. -During this break in the action, Natasha Cruise returned to the ring, and, grabbing a house mike, disputed Sabrina Wild’s earlier notion that she was the most beautiful woman in the ACW. Cruise said that she herself was more beautiful than Wild. Cruise, the former Catwoman actress, said that Wild’s experience as a "bitchy goddess on a prime-time soap was hack-work", and that Cruise could do "everything better than that tramp." Sabrina Wild came down the entrance tunnel, and began jawing with Cruise, but didn’t force a confrontation. Cruise called Wild a coward, and challenged her to a match. Wild left the entrance tunnel without responding. Should Wild respond, we will report to you immediately. -After Natasha Cruise left, Irene Pain faced off against Iris Sweet. As you recall, Pain’s suspension was lifted by the ACW Commissioner’s Office last Monday, when it was announced that Pain’s recent overdose should not affect her in-ring status. This was a questionable and controversial decision, given Pain’s history of drug problems, and her run of mediocre matches. The Memphis ACW fans booed Pain mercilessly, chanting "Druggie" at several points during the match (Pain pressed her hands to her ears). One fan went so far as to throw ziploc bags of white flour at Pain. Of course, this fan was ejected from the arena. After the match, Pain complained bitterly about this treatment, claiming that Memphis fans couldn’t appreciate "talented black wrestlers." The former runway supermodel clashed with fans after the match, spilling a few beers along the way. None of this seemed to affect her wrestling; she thrashed Sweet soundly, putting her away with the Painkiller Chokeslam. The Commissioner’s Office will surely keep a close eye on this volatile situation. -ACW Champion Athena Crisis retained her title over Cat Francis. Francis had to be hospitalized with bruised ribs. After the match, Stella Still and Teri Moan came to ringside and attacked Crisis. However, Mona Sheer returned the earlier favor and ran to the ring for the save. It seems that a temporary truce is extant between Crisis and Sheer, the onetime tag team champions, and on and off bitter rivals. The rivalry is rumored to date back to their days as models for the same lingerie catalogue. -Finally, Sable Price faced off against Dominique Thunder for Thunder’s Super Queen Belt. Price, the red-haired former government agent and physician, wore white ring attire, ribbed at the waist, and belted with a blue sash. Thunder came to the ring accompanied by Toni Velvet and Amber Rose. By the way, we wish all the best to Velvet, who recently suffered through a divorce from her baseball player husband. Good luck, Toni. Thunder wore her trademark silver one piece outfit. Neither competitor waited for the bell to ring before attacking. Thunder fended off a few roundhouse kicks, and landed a knee to the gut. Thunder applied a headlock, twisting Price’s head in slow circles. She claimed that Price was pulling her swimsuit, but when the referee went to check, Thunder sucker punched Price in the mouth... [...] Price fought back, and hit a series of sliding leg bombs. She poured it on, stomping on Thunder’s throat, chest and thigh. Thunder rolled out of the ring, and went for a walk to clear her head. Price clearly wanted to pursue Thunder, but the sight of Velvet and Rose at ringside deterred her. Thunder grabbed Price’s leg when she strayed too close to the apron, and dragged her to the outside. Thunder kneed Price in the back and jumped back into the ring. She distracted the referee for a moment, and Toni Velvet and Amber Rose stomped on Price... [...] Sable Price battled the wave of blackness. Thunder had hit her with a piledriver and spinebuster in quick succession, and she was in real trouble. Price swung wildly at Thunder, who evaded one punch and absorbed another, reversing it into a Dragon Sleeper Hold. Thunder’s muscular arms closed around Price’s neck, and Price cried out, knowing that she was losing consciousness.... [...] Price was nearly helpless, lying supine in the center of the ring. She had been fortunate that her foot had slipped under the ropes, otherwise the match would have been over. Thunder was simply too big and fast, although not as talented as Price. Thunder went for the cover, but Price kicked out at two. Thunder pulled Price up slowly. Thunder thrust Price’s head between her legs. She squeezed, and Price’s knees buckled. Sable Price knew that Dominique Thunder was going for the Thunderbomb, and that if she hit it, the match would be over. Price stumbled forward purposefully, and, summoning her last reserves of strength, hit Thunder in the forehead with an over the shoulder kick. Thunder went down, but was she hurt or just surprised?... [...] Price was finally mounting an offense, though it might have been too late. She was weak, and dizzy, and her punches to Thunder’s gut didn’t seem to have much effect. Price realized she had to try her finishing maneuver, though Thunder was heavy for the Horizontal Reverse Piledriver. Price paused to adjust her tights, which were bunching at her inner thigh, and Thunder suddenly capitalized, landing a forearm to the chest. After a series of European Uppercuts, Thunder hit a brainbuster, which virtually finished Price... [...] To punish her, Thunder went to the top rope and hit the Lightning Crashes Splash. After a few slaps to the face, and some showboating, Thunder put Price on the top rope turnbuckle and hit the Total Domination Superplex. Price was unconscious and helpless, and Thunder could have pinned her, but instead she humiliated the beaten Sable Price by hitting her with the ThunderBomb one-arm powerbomb. She went for the cover, but, to add to the humiliation, dragged her up at two and hit another ThunderBomb, this time going for the pin and winning the match. Dominique Thunder retained her Super Queen’s Title over Sable Price. Later that evening, through a spokesperson from the hospital, Sable Price demanded a rematch. Thunder has yet to repond. Finally, more details are emerging regarding the investor group who could bail out the ACW. It is a manufacturing company, whose product is some type of accessory for women. Our source in the Commissioner’s Office informs us that the nature of the product lends itself to being endorsed by the talent in the ACW. Therefore, this group is *not* another wrestling federation, which was originally though to the be the case. As a result of these rumors, our first television show taping in over four years will be expanded to a full half-hour, instead of a series of three, five-minute commercial style broadcasts. The ACW looks forward to announcing their sponsor as soon as negotiations are complete. This mystery company might be taking on a financial lodestone, as the ACW has had its share of financial woes in the past five years, including, of course, the extensively disseminated bankruptcy hearings. Here’s hoping that this is the answer to the business side of ACW, so that the Commissioner’s Office can focus on bringing our fans the best product in the Women’s Wrestling business. Our next report will be following our first television taping. Also, results from house shows in Palomina, North Dakota, and Wabash, Wisconsin. See you soon. Until next time...
