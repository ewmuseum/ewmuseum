[ACW]f Newsletter July3

3 July 1997

Alliance Crown Wrestling Newsletter, 7/3/7 The ACW finally has some good news to report. A small cable station in the Minneapolis-St. Paul area signed a contract this morning to carry ACW telecasts for a year. The ACW Commissioner's Office is hopeful that this exposure could lead to more and greater things. For those fans in the greater Minneapolis region, we will be carried on the Foreign Languages Network, once a week on Friday at 2am. The show will be rebroadcast Sunday at 4pm. Please tune in to see the greatest in Ladies's Professional Wrestling. Ratings will be included in our next issue. Results from the House Show in Saltbox, Ohio, 7/2/97: -Satin Fantasy defeated Monica Tremble by countout. Tremble, who once sang country western music, a dark-haired, slender young woman, was holding her own against the stronger German. Tremble went for her finishing maneuver, the Sunset Flip, but Fantasy stepped out of the way, and Tremble flew out of the ring, hitting her head on the floor. Fantasy, a beautiful, tall, blonde former supermodel from Germany, hit Tremble with her Spinning German Suplex onto the floor. Tremble was unable to return to the ring, and in fact could only lie limply on the hard concrete floor. -Irene Pain whipped Brandy Watson. -Candy Marvel, wearing a form-fitting orange two piece, defeated Lola Soto, wearing a web-like black one piece, trimmed with silver. Marvel and Soto are among the smaller wrestlers in the ACW. However, Marvel makes up for her lack of size with deception and intelligence. Marvel, a former lifeguard, skilled in lifesaving techniques, made short work of the less experienced Soto, a buxom Spanish-speaking actress from Mexico. Marvel rendered Soto unconscious with the quick application of the Candy Kiss Swinging Neckbreaker, the Sweet Nothing Jawbreaker, and and Marvelous Body Scissors Sleeper Hold. Marvel's rock star husband was believed to have been in the crowd, though this is unconfirmed. -Tawny Cruel destroyed top contender Mona Sheer. Cruel continues to trumpet herself as number one contender for Athena Crisis's Alliance Crown Belt, though this is not borne out by the computerized rankings. Cruel's loss to the red-headed Sable Price, a former physician and government agent investigating the paranormal, was costly in her pursuit of this title. Cruel, wearing a sky blue bikini outfit with matching boots, wore sunglasses to the ring, which she used against Sheer, a former lingerie supermodel, when the referee wasn't looking. However, this figured little in the outcome of the match, which Cruel won easily with the Cruel Connection Northern Lights Bomb. Sheer was wearing a set of lingerie from her old catalogue-a matching red bra and panties, with white leggings and red booties. This loss will be costly for Sheer. -Crystal Vice, another former lingerie model, defeated Helen Stone, an aging actress who has starred in an action movie about a deadly weapon, a virus movie, a golf film, and a humorously cool gangster film. Vice, a blond, medium sized woman, defeated Stone with her Vice Squad Armbar Crucifix Pin. -Finally, Dominique Thunder retained her Super Queen's Title when she beat Samantha Blush. Thunder hit both her Lightning Strikes Top Rope Splash, and the Thunderbomb One Arm Powerbomb. Thunder, a well-endowed, black, young supermodel, who has appeared in commercials with a prominent basketball player and a puppet, was booed by the crowd of just under 500. In a post-match interview, Thunder stated that her ultimate goal is to win the ACW Title before the year is over. Last year's Award Winning Top Newcomer has been destroying every woman in her path, and it won't be a surprise if she gets a title shot against Athena Crisis very soon. In other news, there is a very definite rumor circulating around the Commissioner's Office that a large company is planning on stepping in and sponsoring future ACW events. This would not only relieve the financial pressure on this organization, but it would also guarantee greater exposure, should this company elect to use ACW athletes in its advertising. Details are sketchy, but we will have them for you as soon as they become available. Brief Rankings: 1. Athena Crisis. Alliance Crown Wrestling Champion. 2. Dominique Thunder. Super Queen's Title Holder. 3. Sable Price. 4. Seneca Smith. 5. Tawny Cruel. 6. Honey Devine. 7. Mona Sheer. 8. Jenny Love 9. Roxy Heller 10. Cat Francis The Tag Team Titles are vacant.
