An all-women's newsgroup federation that filed a daily newsletter three days running, then went quiet. Written by AI, 2026

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Alliance Crown Wrestling presented itself, in the three daily newsletters that are its whole surviving record (2–4 July 1997), as “the premier ladies's wrestling federation” — an all-women's league running house shows across small-town North America, its titles headed by Athena Crisis's Alliance Crown Title and Dominique Thunder's Super Queen's Title, with its Tag Team Titles standing vacant.

Its own newsletter narrated a company in crisis and then a company in recovery, inside the space of three days. The first issue broke word that the ACW was “once again in dire financial straits” after “last year's bankruptcy,” with its tentatively scheduled pay-per-views — the DreamGirl Show, the Tag Team 10000 Tournament, the Super Queen Competition — and the organization itself in doubt. The second reported a rescue: a Minneapolis-St. Paul cable station had signed a year's contract to carry ACW telecasts, weekly on the Foreign Languages Network. By the third, a mystery investor was confirmed — not, as first feared, a rival promotion, but a manufacturer of “some type of accessory for women” — and the ACW's first television taping in over four years was expanded from three five-minute spots to a full half hour on the strength of it.

The in-ring record over the same three nights ran a rivalry between Sabrina Wild and Natasha Cruise over which was “the most beautiful woman in the ACW,” a controversial return from suspension for a wrestler with a history of drug troubles that Memphis fans met with open hostility, and, in the final night's main event, Dominique Thunder retaining her Super Queen's Title over Sable Price after a match the newsletter recapped move for move, closing on a demand for a rematch its own record leaves unanswered.

The newsletter promised a fourth issue, to follow the ACW's first television taping and carry results from further house shows. No further issue survives. Whether the cable deal, the mystery sponsor, or any of the three nights' rivalries ever played out beyond 4 July 1997 is not something ACW's own record answers.
