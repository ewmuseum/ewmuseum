Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

IWC opened in May 2008 out of Long Island, New York, an angle/roleplay federation whose owner, in the fed's own bylined interview, traced his ambition back seven years to a falling-out over pacing with an unnamed prior company, Platinum Wrestling, and set out to build something slower and more deliberate instead.

Its own account is modest by design: “currently not big enough to tour large scale arenas and coliseums,” it announced a home at the Suffolk County Community Center and a Cablevision Public Access deal for its weekly show, Fusion. Five wrestlers — Inferno, Anton Steele, Evenflow, Wild Thing and Ian Crosby — make up the whole of its surviving roster, and its own Road to the Gold tournament opened its first round on 4 June 2008 with three further, page-less entrants (Johnny Price, Xander Dolcievsky, Dominic Chance) filling out the bracket.

No result survives for that round or for anything after it; the record breaks off there.
