PCW match: Akiyama v. Miyagawa (PCW TV Title)

March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Akiyama v. Miyagawa (PCW TV Title) Written: Clay: Coming to the ring weighing in at 339lbs, he is one half of he tag team champions, Akiyama! 'Nothing' by Headsnap plays as Akiyama makes his way to the ring. Clay: And his opponent weighing in at 230lbs, from Anaheim, California, he is the current TV Champion and the newly crowned World Champion, Miyagawa! 'One Step Closer' by Linkin Park hits as Miyagawa makes his way to the ring to the cheers of the crowd. Dave: The World and TV Champion is ready to defend his TV title against one half the Tag Team Champions! Boda: A short reign for Miya because I will win my title back on Tuesday. Jeff: Still longer then 3 days. Dave sighs. The bell rings as Akiyama raises his hands in the air, offering Miyagawa a test of strength. Dave: It wouldn't be a wise move from Miyagawa to accept this test of strength, as you'd have to say that Akiyama is the stronger of the two. Boda: Miya has not strength. Miyagawa takes hold of Akiyama's hands with his own, and the two push against each other in a test of strength. Boda: Just like I said he’s a pathetic weakling. Akiyama slowly gets the advantage on Miyagawa and has managed to start pushing him down towards the mat. Dave: Just like I said before, Akiyama is definitely the stronger of the two. Boda: Did you hear me arguing with you? Dave: That's true. Akiyama has managed to pin Miya on the mat by pushing him down while holding his hands. The referee counts this as a pin, 1...Miya kicks out straight away. Dave: Akiyama managed to get Miyagawa in to a pinning predicament via that test of strength, but Miyagawa kicked out straight away. Jeff: It would of been pretty sad if that jobber of a World Champion, Miyajobber, had lost to Akijobber straight away. Miya and Akiyama both get to their feet. Aki grabs Miya's arm and irish whips him in to the turnbuckle as hard as he can. Miya smacks it and goes stumbling backwards in to the arms of Aki, who lifts him up in to the air and gives him a belly-to-back suplex. Dave: Imagine what it would mean if Akiyama can defeat Miyagawa tonight, even if the world title is not on the line. Boda: It would mean nothing because Miyajobber will lose the title to me on Turmoil and Akiyama can’t touch a monster like me. Dave: Not only would it prove that he's capable of fighting world champions, but it also will mean that he'll be a double champion by holding both the tag team titles and the TV title at the same time. Jeff: (Sarcastically) And being a double champion is special, especially since the last double champion we had was....well the latest double champion is currently holding the World and TV Titles, and that's Miyajobber. And if Miyajobber is a double champion, then being a double champion must really mean a lot! Aki goes over to Miya, who is slowly getting to his feet, and puts his hand around Miya's throat, and lifts him in to the air and choke slams him. Dave: Akiyama, just a couple of minutes in to this match, has choke slammed the world champion! Jeff: It just goes to show what a joke Miyajobber is as a world champion. He's letting Akijobber chokeslam him in TV Title matches! Aki then kneels down and pins Miyagawa, 1...Miya once again kicks out straight away. Dave: It goes to show, that despit Jeff's belief's, Miyagawa is a deserving world champion, who doesn't plan on giving up. Boda: I would be champion if it wasn’t for Mexi screwing things up. Jeff: All it showed was that Akijobber delivers pussy chokeslams! Akiyama lifts up Miyagawa again and gives him another choke slam in to the mat. Dave: Another choke slam by Akiyama to the world champ! Boda: That’s it Aki soften him up. Dave: I'm surprised that Miyagawa hasn't been able to fight his way out of getting chokeslammed. Duke: The reason he hasn't been able to fight out is because Akiyama has been deliviring them so quickly, surprising Miyagawa and not giving him enough time to even think about fighting out. Akiyama kneels down again and pins Miyagawa, 1...kick out! Dave: I can't believe this! Miyagawa kicks out once again on just a one count! He just won't quit! Boda: It would make life so much easier for all of us if he would just quit. Dave: And that's not going to happen! Akiyama starts to get in the ref's face about slow counting, but the ref denies he was counting slowly. Dave: Akiyama is starting to feel the pressure of this big match as he gets in the referee's face about the speed of his counting. Boda: That's what happens when you're in big time matches and things aren't going your way. Jeff: And you would know wouldn’t you Boda. Boda: Shut up! Akiyama turns his attention back to Miyagawa who starts to throw fast and furious kicks at. Dave: Akiyama is releasing his aggression with some fast and powerful kicks. Jeff: Speaking of releasing, I wish these two jobbers were released so we wouldn't have to put up with them! Akiyama refuses to give Miyagawa the opportunity to get to his feet as he continues to hit his body with kicks. Dave: Akiyama is relentless in his attack on Miyagawa. Boda: Who doesn’t want to kill Miyajobber? Akiyama stops kicking Miyagawa, and then gives him an elbow drop to the back of his neck. Dave: An elbow drop to the back of Miyagawa's neck, from Akiyama. Boda: I don't know what's worse, two choke slams or an elbow drop to the back of the neck. Dave: Why don’t you ask Mexi what he preferred earlier when you were Boda Bombing him? Boda: Are you mocking me Kern? Akiyama then signals to the crowd for Crescent Moon Knocker. Dave: No, no why would I mock the greatest champion in PCW ever. Jeff: Now he’s mocking you. Boda: Shut up! I’m not as bad as Miya, has he mounted an attack yet? Jeff: What have I been saying this whole time? Miyajobber is an embarrassment to the belt! Dave sighs. Akiyama gives Miyagawa Crescent Moon Knocker. Boda: It's all over! Akiyama has hit Miyagawa with Crescent Moon Knocker! Miyagawa's going to lose the TV Title with out even getting in one offensive move! Dave: Akiyama is going to do something that not many thought he would, and that's win the TV Title from the newly crowned World Champion! Akiyama pins Miyagawa .......... 1 .......... 2 .......... kick out! Dave: Miyagawa just kicked out from Crescent Moon Knocker. Boda: Impossible!! Jeff: Any one can kick out of that jobberific move! Akiyama bangs his hands on the mat and yells out in frustration. Dave: Akiyama can't work out what it will take to put away Miyagawa. Miyagawa slowly gets to his feet as Akiyama yells out in rage. Miya walks over to Aki and goes to attack him, when Aki spots Miya and as quickly as he can, he gives Miya a low blow sending him back down to the mat. Dave: Miyagawa just did what some would say is impossible, and kicked out of Crescent Moon Knocker. But as he got to his feet, he was taken down by a low blow from Akiyama. Boda: HA HA HA!! Dave: That’s not right. Akiyama laughs as he walks over to Miyagawa. He picks up Miyagawa, and once again gives him Crescent Moon Knocker. Dave: That's two! Akiyama has just given Miyagawa a second Crescent Moon Knocker in this match! Boda: Miyagawa may of been able to kick out of the first Crescent Moon Knocker, but he's got no chance of kicking out of the second. I guarantee you. Dave: I'm going to have to agree with you their. No mortal could possibly kick out of two Crescent Moon Knocker, not even the PCW World Champion, Miyagawa. The referee counts as Akiyama pins Miyagawa. .......... 1 .......... 2 .......... Kick out! Dave: Oh my goodness! The impossible has just happened! Miyagawa has kicked out of Akiyama's finsher not once, but twice! Boda: That’s impossible!! Dave: The question has to be asked, is Miyagawa human? I said before that no mortal could kick out of Crescent Moon Knocker twice in a match, but Miyagawa has done it! Jeff: No, he's not human. He's a jobber! Dave: No jobber can do what Miyagawa has just done. Miyagawa gets to his feet as Akiyama just stares at Miyagawa in rage, not able to believe what has happened. Dave: Akiyama doesn't know what to think any more. Having some one kicking out of your finisher not once but twice in a match, even if it is the PCW World Champion, is something that no one can fully understand how or why it happened. Jeff: If I was Akijobber, I'd understand it. Because I'd realize that I'm a jobber and therefore have a jobberific finisher which means that it can be kicked out of easily! Akiyama takes hold of Miyagawa's head and starts to get in his face and yell at him. As Akiyama is yelling at Miyagawa, he is caught off guard by an elbow to his heart from Miyagawa. Akiyama lets go and stumbles backwards. Dave: Miyagawa is back in this match as he hits Akiyama with an elbow! Boda: He's going to need to do more then hit an elbow, as you know as well as I do, that an elbow by Miya is going to do nothing against anyone! Dave: I think he's got a lot more planned then the elbow! Miyagawa slaps Akiyama across his chest a couple of times before giving him a punch to his head. This sends Akiyama falling in to the turnbuckle which he slumps on. Dave: Miyagawa is taking control of this match! Miyagawa runs up to Akiyama and gives him a missile drop kick to his face, as Akiyama holds his head in pain while still slumping against the turnbuckle. Boda: No Miya is winning! Dave: I noticed that, he's delivering moves to Akiyama at a high paced rate. Miyagawa jumps on to the top of the turnbuckle and starts to punch Akiyama's head as the crowd counts. Crowd: One...Two...Threee...Four... Dave: Like we said before, Miyagawa has a lot of energy and is using that to his advantage. Crowd: ...Five...Six....Seven... Jeff: Why do these idiots in the crowd think that we don't know how many time Miyajobber has punched Akijobber? Why do they have to tell us? Dave: I think they're counting along because it's amusing. I don't think that they're trying to tell everyone else how many times Miyagawa has punched Akiyama. Jeff: What ever, they're still idiots! Dave sighs. Crowd: ...Eight...Nine...Ten! Miyagawa jumps off Akiyama who falls to the ground. Dave: Miyagawa has taken complete charge of this match. Boda: All luck, just like his win Tuesday. Miyagawa lays a couple of kicks in to Akiyama, and then picks him up. He Irish whips him in to the ropes, but the referee is in the way and Akiyama goes flying in to the ref, knocking the ref to the outside. Dave: The referee has just been knocked to the outside! Boda: Gotta go! Jeff: I guess this is the point in the match where we can expect complete chaos! After hitting the ref, Akiyama continued to bounce in to the ropes thanks to Miyagawa's irish whip. When he rebounds off the ropes Miyagawa grabs him and locks him in Therapy. Dave: Therapy locked in! Boda makes his way calmly walking up the ring steps and stepping over the ropes. Dave: Not many people have been able to escape from Therapy. Akiyama tries to get to the ropes, but can't as Miyagawa tightens the hold. Dave: If Akiyama does tap, the ref won't even be able to see it as he's on the outside. As Miyagawa tightens the hold some more, Akiyama taps, but the referee is knocked out on the outside and can't see it. Dave: Akiyama is tapping and Miyagawa should have this match won, but the referee can't see it as he's on the outside. He doesn’t have much time to keep the hold locked as Boda nails Miya from behind breaking up the hold and quickly slides out of the ring as he spots Xavier Kannon charging down to the ring. Dave: Indeed he is. Jeff: I'm regretting having to watch this contest of jobbers! Boda meanwhile innocently strolls back over to the announce table. Dave: What is he doing here? Jeff: Hopefully to rid us of these jobbers! Dave: That wasn’t fair and you know it Boda. Xavier Kannon slides in the ring as Miyagawa starts to yell at Xavier, asking him what he's doing here, when Xavier smacks Miyagawa's face with a bottle of Snapple he was holding behind his back. Dave: That son of a bitch has come down here and taken out Miyagawa with a bottle of snapple! Boda: Even his own stable doesn’t like him Jeff: Just like I said, when the ref gets knocked out, the chaos begins! Miyagawa's face is covered with glass as blood begins to pour from his face. Boda: This is too great. Dave: I hope Xavier has realized just what he's done, having glass in your face could be a career threatening injury. Xavier Kannon smashes the remainder of the bottle over Miyagawa's head as Jeremy Howard runs down to the ring. Dave: Jeremy Howard is here, and he's going to take out that piece of trash, Xavier Kannon. Boda: Krayzie can’t even take out the trash that is piling up in his kitchen let alone Xavier Kannon. Jeremy Howard slides in to the ring and starts to yell at Xavier Kannon, asking him what he was thinking. Xavier Kannon just laughs at Jeremy Howard, and spits on him. Dave: Xavier Kannon just spat on Jeremy Howard. Jeff: Good on him! Dave: I think it's time that Jeremy got Krayzie on Kannon! Jeremy Howard looks at Kannon in disgust, and then kicks him in his stomach and grabs him, throwing him over the top rope to the outside. Dave: Jeremy Howard is teaching Xavier Kannon a lesson about respect! Jeff: The only lesson Howard can teach any one is how to be a jackass! Jeremy Howard jumps over the top rope and lands a suicide dive on Xavier Kannon. Dave: What a move by Jeremy Howard! Boda: Who cares about those jobbers Jeff: That's for sure! In the ring Miyagawa is still bleeding heavily with glass in his face, while Akiyama is slowly getting to his feet after recovering from therapy. Dave: Akiyama is back up, and it appears that he has an easy road to victory now as Miyagawa has been hurt pretty badly from that Snapple bottle to the head. Boda: I don’t think Aki can lose this now. As Akiyama gets to his feet, Darkstar jumps the barricade and slides in to the ring with a baseball bat. Dave: Darkstar is here to help Miyagawa! Boda: That’s the same trick they used on me. It’s all an act the tension was to cause a distraction so DarkStar can slip in unnoticed. They even had Mexi and Walden in on it too on Tuesday. Darkstar cracks the baseball bat over the back of Akiyama's head sending him crashing to the mat in a heap. Boda: See I told you so. Dave: It would appear so, and that certainly was a vicious shot with the baseball bat from Darkstar. Darkstar then throws the baseball bat to the outside and picks up Miyagawa and goes to put him over top of Akiyama, when Miyagawa punches Darkstar in his stomach because he thinks that it's Akiyama due to the fact that he can't see anything as his eyes are covered with blood. Dave: Miyagawa just thought that Darkstar was Akiyama. Boda: All an act I tell you. Miyagawa continues to throw punches at Darkstar thinking that he's Akiyama. Darkstar, not a man to accept getting attacked even if it is an accident, starts to punch Miyagawa in his face causing the glass to rip through the skin more and more blood pours from Miyagawa's face. Dave: Darkstar has just attacked Miyagawa! Asylum is falling apart! Jeff: Wake up Kern it fell apart on Turmoil. Darkstar punches Miyagawa again causing him to fall back to the mat as the glass rips more of his skin. Darkstar then picks up Miyagawa and gives him the Pressure Drop. Dave: Darkstar just gave Miyagawa the Pressure Drop! Boda: An act! AN ACT I SAY~! Darkstar then slides out of the ring, picks up his baseball bat, and leaves through the crowd. While he does this Howard is beating up Kannon on the outside. Dave: This is a sad site to see, Asylum members are destroying each other. Jeff: It's a great site to see if you ask me! The referee has now managed to drag himself back in to the ring, and has crawled over to where Akiyama and Miyagawa are. Akiyama has managed, with the last of his energy, to put his hand over top of Miyagawa. Dave: The referee is back in the ring, and Akiyama is pinning Miyagawa! Boda: Ha! Dave: Well we thought it was safe to say that a few other times in this match, but Miyagawa proved us wrong. But with the condition that Miyagawa is in right now, I know that there is no way even he'll kick out this time around. The referee counts the pin .......... 1 .......... 2 .......... 3! THE WINNER OF THIS MATCH AND NEW TV CHAMPION: AKIYAMA! Winner: Akiyama Dave: I never thought I would see the day. Jeff: Miyajobber did the job. View Akiyama 's Biography Back
