Bernard Colt Biography Real Name Bernard Daniel Colt Hometown Fayetteville, North Carolina Birthday 12-19-1965 Height 6'2' Weight 252lbs. lbs Biography I remember when America was… America was beautiful. When you started the day, roused from bed by your mother, who cooked you bacon and eggs, and who dressed you, making sure you were ready to go to school. You went out to the friendly bus-driver, who drove you to your safe, non-ghettoized, non-politically correct school, and you learned about heroes. American heroes. How we destroyed the Nazis Grandpa fought against in The Big One. How we were the biggest and the best EVER. Then, we went home, and Dad came home and read the paper while the news was on. And there wasn’t a bunch of colored folks stereotyping themselves into the roles that the rednecks down in Georgia placed them in, dancing around like fools, preaching multi-culturalism and mixed marriages. No, we were more concerned with how the Commies were nearly over the edge, ready to launch the missiles, living on the bring of a world war, a war we Americans, with our clean living and morals and good ole American know-how, we would win. But no, somewhere along the line, that all changed. We got more worried about how some Third World douche bag country was doing, and we sent our boys over there to ‘keep the peace’, all the while Russia is selling their nukes off to rag-heads who wanna bitch and cry about how we tread on their holy land, or whatever those Arabs think. No, we got more worried about how some dumb nigger basketball player is driving around in a big old Sports Utility Vehicle which only makes the towel-heads over in Saudi Arabia richer, on how his extravagantly lavish house is, or how he beat up his wife or got caught with a pistol. Celebrity murders become more important than how we’re gonna keep the Commies from taking everything. We bargain with the Palestinians, so we can keep the kikes and the rag heads killing each other. Why, when the World Trade Centers were bombed, I read in the papers on how college students were trying to evade the draft. Saying they don’t wanna fight for the freedom their grandfathers fought for. Their daddies or their granddaddies were dying in rice fields so their spineless ass successors could go to college, and they spit in the face of all that. They piss on the flag, the flag I carried on my rucksack out of a C-130 as I jumped into Grenada with the 82nd Airborne. They wanna piss all over it, they wanna piss all over the memories of their ancestors, our forefathers. They wanna let the Commies come in and run everything. They wanna put hardworking Americans like me out of work, calling me a warhawk and a capitalist pig. I thought about shooting them. I thought about walking onto a university, where these hippy kids wanna talk about lifting the sanctions on Iraq, let the ragheads eat when they stupid camel jockeys won’t even revolt against their Commie leader. They wanna talk bad about our President, the son of a good President, George Bush, who did more for the military than that adulterer we had in office ever did. I think about buying a .38 caliber Smith and Wesson revolver. If I could, I would take an M-249 Minami automatic rifle. 5.56mm round, full metal jacketed rounds, 200 round belt drum. I would take it and walk into a university and shoot all those hippies, make them pay for their lies and their disloyalty to the United States. It’s treason, you know. That’s what they teach them. They’re not proud of their country. They don’t care. They could give a shit less. All they want to do is party and get drunk and fornicate. They don’t think about their country like me and my Dad do. They don’t care. And I want to shoot them, but that’s against the law. And I’m better than some ghetto miscreant. I’m not a psychopath. So now I have to fight them, an American Hero, punishing them by proxy on a wrestling show. You gotta think of your demographics. They taught me that in school. Impressionable young men, who are ripe for the military, because we need them in uniform, not being homosexuals or hippy protestors, the kind that spit on my Dad when he got off the plane from Saigon. I’ve got to be the example. An American Hero, like my GI Joes I had when I was a kid. I’ll teach them all. The way a real American is. I’ll teach them all.
