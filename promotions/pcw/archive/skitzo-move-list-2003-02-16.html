Skitzo — PCW move list, February 2003

16 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is The move list of Skitzo’s page on PCW’s own site as the Wayback Machine captured it on 16 February 2003: his ten named moves with their descriptions, the list of his other moves, his trademark weapon and his Ultra Special Move. The page’s navigation, headshot and site banner are not carried.
See No Evil Hear No Evil Speak No Evil - Triple Classic Powerbomb Bayou Buster – Death Valley Bomb Freak Out – Mexican Stretch Bomb Cerebral Assassination – Jacknife Powerbomb into the Turnbuckle Skitzophrenia – Running Crucifix Powerbomb Mind Game Over - Wheelbarrow Face First Powerbomb Brain Strain - Underhook Over The Shoulder Powerbomb Pride of Louisiana - Reverse Face First Crucifix Powerbomb Minds Over Matter - Crossed Arms Canadian Back Breaker Rack Powerbomb Schizophrenic Slap Attack – Series of Bitchslaps and Chop Blocks done in succession Others: Spear, Hangman’s Neckbreaker, Nestea Plunge, Stiff DDT, Fisherman Buster, Full Nelson Camel Clutch, German Suplex, Electric Chair Drop, Stiff Piledriver, Rear Naked Choke Drop Trademark Weapon: 20' Black Rubber Dildo - Skitzo will use this in matches at times of frustration. ALWAYS GUARANTEES A WIN FOR SKITZO! Ultra Special Move Gatorlicious - This move is a last resort for Skitzo, and can only be used when he is on the brink of defeat. Taking a bottle of Gatorade: The Official Drink Of PCW, Skitzo consumes the contents and proceeds to no-sell punches and kicks from the Opponent, waving his finger menacingly before his foe before flexing his muscles intimidatingly and suddenly dividing and splitting into 12 separate individual Skitzos. The Skitzos then proceed in beat fuck out of the opponent, executing most all of Skitzo's arsenal of moves before merging back into one Skitzo and finishing the opponent off.
