Cowabunga Connection Biography Biography It was on a fateful night that 'Totally Tubular' Tommy's father, 'Righteous' Randy, was killed in the ring with his manager, 'Far Out' Frank. Ever since learning his fathers death Tommy has been wanting to become a professional wrestler in the worst way, but unfortunately he was stopped by his deep need to surf. Tommy surfed every single day and soon became one of the best in the world, winning every single award you can in surfing. At the age of 22 he decided to hang up his surfboard for competition and focus on other ventures. That was playing Yahtzee with bodacious babes and making gnarly green. Tommy went on to open his own private beach and cafe chain called 'Tubular Tiki'. He then marketed his own cologne called 'Tubular' and his own children's foundation for slow children, 'Tubular Tykes'. Success was his, but there was something missing from his need to be complete... Tommy found his wrestling dream again and with his best friend, 'Heavy Metal' Harry, he embarked on his dream by being drafted into the Primetime Wrestling Academy where he worked as hard as he could to be the best, and eventually entered the NTICW, with the greats such as Xavier Kannon, Drake Maxwell, The Rush, Pharaoh, and Jay Sylo. Eventually it crumbled and Tommy faded away into the sunset... Then life was breathed into the man again. NTICW was given a second chance and Tommy was ready to conquer his fears and become the best they had ever seen. Harry began training with Tommy as he ventured through this new NTICW taking out every opponent he faced. Now with his former manager the Tubular One is ready to get bodacious, gnarly, and righteous as the COWABUNGA CONNECTION IS BORN!!!
