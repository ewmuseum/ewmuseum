Rent-A-Hero — PCW biography (November 2001)

RENT-A-HERO Real Name: Jason Kidd Nick Name: "That Masked Guy" "PCW'S Favorite Hero" "Rent-A-Dork" by his enemies Catchphase: "SAME RENT-A-TIME....SAME RENT-A-CHANNEL" "HALT EVILDOER!!" "HOLY (Insert Word Here)" "Krypton's Other Last Son" Character Handler: Carl W. MSN Account: None AIM Address: [detail withheld] Theme Song: "Superhero Man" by El Domino Age: Unknown (Rumored to be 21) From: Metro-Gothamoplois-City, Krypton Height: 6'4 Weight: 221lbs. Wrestling Style: An eratic mix of lucha-libre and technical style. Is he sees the oppourtunity for a high spot,9 times out of 10, he will take it, no matter the risk. Signature Moves: The Spiderman Splash-(Top Rope Leap Frog Crossbody Block), The Final Fantasy-(Sleeper Hold Neckbreaker), Superkick, Sonic Spin-(Reverse Tornado DDT), The PS1-(Plancha Sault 1-Cannonballrana from the top rope *ya know, the one Lita does*), Swinging Neckbreaker, Moonsault, Top Rope Missile Dropkick, limitless amounts of suicide dives to the outside, Butterfly Armlock, Japanese Leg Crab, 450 Splash, Twisted Metal Black-(A Full Nelson Into A Stepover Face Drop), The Bizzaro Bomb-(Sitout Front Face Suplex), Finisher Move: The Gravity Defier-(Our hero grabs his opponent in a Diamond Cutter set up and runs up one of the turnbuckles, flipping off backward when his foot hits the top one and driving his opponent down with an elevated reverse DDT) Set-Up Move(s): The PS2-(Plancha Sault 2)-Our hero sits his opponent on a turnbuckle facing the audience like he's going for a super backdrop, but he climbs up and performs a reverse top rope hurracanranna, driving his opponent square on the head unlike the front face version & The Sinistro Series-(Three left jabs combo followed by a spin into a split and a clothesline to end it off, usually a HULK UP move when he's making a comeback or ready to finsih the match) Current Team: On and Off Partners with fellow hero Big Alan Steele Past Teams & Groups: CAN'T YOU READ GOOD CITIZEN, NONE! Title History:Longest Reigning PCW Rising Star Champion. (46 days) Awards History:Holds longest PCW title reign ever. Description:Handsome man with blonde hair, streaked with other colors on some occasions when he's in the mood. Muscualr build for his size, as can be seen by his form fitting bodysuit costumes, and light blue eyes. Pearly white smile and no tattoos or piercings, quite clean cut. Boyish good looks. He has and endless assortment of video game and superhero replica costumes that he wears and is never caught without wearing hsi coveted mask...it is a rarity whenit ever comes off. History:In a world filled with such negativity...a voice of purity and courage shines through that being none other then the PCW's very own Rent-A-Hero. Sticking adimantly to his story that he was bron in Metro Gothamopolis City, he was raised by a loving family who didn't understand why the heck he was wearing a superhero mask at age 2. as depicted in some of his early pictures. Growing up, our hero always had a facination with heroes and the feats they pulled, vowing when he grew old enough, he would become a hero to the masses, often making his parents and friends laugh when he said that was his goal for the future. Rent-A-Hero was often watching lucha-libre tapes and wrestlign with his friends, tryign to pull off the feats and stunts he'd seen on carttons and wrestling tapes as he honed his skills as a little Rent-A-Hero in training. In high school, laughter was often the first reaction of many upon seeing him coming to school in one of his many assorted superhero outfits, the kids teasing him for his naive personality and belief that "Santa really did eat my oatmeal last year". Despite many beliving he was clinically stupid...he gradutated from school among the top in his class, making money for college at UCLA by starting a chain of "Rent-A-Hero" services in the Los Angeles area as they provided services such as cat saving, old lady walking, purse snatching stopping, and motivational speaking, although most people immediately laughed after the speeches due to the silly costumes. Although the buisness failed, that did not daunter out hero as he finally graduated college, taking a major in Physics and a minor in Criminlogy since "Superheroing 101" and "Looking Good In Costumes 321" was never a curriculum. With a failed buisness, a degree, and little or no money in his pocket, our hero thought as to what he could do for a living when it came to him one day while he was sitting on the couch in fear of eviction....he turned to Primetime Championship Wrestlign and knew deep inside that a hero was needed in that federation, a hero that would be a role model for all the kids while still having enough self depreciation to hock cheap wares and T-shirts...Rent-A-Hero knew this was the job for him. And so our hero foudn himself in the infamous PWA wrestling school, where he was picked up in a record 6 days(probably so they can see how long an idiot can last before flopping in a larger fed) and wrestling in the greatest federation in history...PCW. And as they say, "the rest is history" and so...when you see a signal, more like a flashlight with "RAH" written on the face, and you hear the phrase "HOLY SHIZZNITTLEBAM" be fore-warned that your hero has arrived and he's handing out justice in one size fits all incrimantes!
