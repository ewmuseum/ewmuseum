The Mack Ed — PCW biography (November 2001)

Ed Real Name: Ed Nick Name: The Hardcore Legend Catchphase: "I've got 'it', baby!" Character Handler: Ed MSN Account: [detail withheld] AIM Address: [detail withheld] Theme Song: "Food for the Brain" by One Minute Silence Age: 28 Home Town: Bakersfield, California Height: 6'3" Weight: 253 Wrestling Style: Hardcore Signature Moves: Crescent Wrench - DDT where Ed throws himself outward during the fall, twisting his opponent's neck in the process. Spinesnapper - Submission where Ed kneels on his opponent's back and pulls his victim's right arm and leg together. Looks painful, mainly because it is. Lunging Clothesline - A basic clothesline, except Ed throws all his weight behind it and goes into a roll. Guillotine - A top-rope leg drop to either the throat or chest, depending on whether it's through a breakable object or not. Running Dropkick - Ed bounces off the ropes, jumps as high as he can, and throws his feet out. One of the "best-looking" moves in his inventory. Finisher: Brink of Insanity (Sky High Facebuster) Set-Up: Clutch Clothesline Other Moves: Standard - Standing arm bar, suplex, sidewalk slam, Manhattan drop, piledriver, Sambo suplex, side kneeling powerbomb Rear - Bulldog, backdrop, full nelson, hard German suplex Running - Falling neck drop, face crusher, bulldog, Yakuza kick to stomach Turnbuckle - Ten Punch (natch), superplex, super back drop, powerbomb into turnbuckle Flying - Double axe handle, flying clothesline, diving elbow drop Misc - Suicide dive through ropes, guillotine from apron, suplex to outside, running-diving clothesline from apron to outside Current Team: Old School Past Teams & Groups: Team International, Media Title History: 3x Extreme Champion Awards History: 2x Hall of Famer Description: Ed's almost thirty years old, but in the best shape of his life. While he doesn't posess a completely chiselled physique, his arms are significantly thicker than they've ever been, and he could probably get away with not wearing a shirt to the ring. Of course, when you dive onto steel-barbed wire-nail chairs for a living, you probably need the extra protection. Ed has shoulder-length sand-blond hair, and his bangs hang in disarray above his eyebrows. He also has a thick goattee, shaved under the nose to give it a sort of crescent shape. He also has thick eyebrows (partially concealed by his hair) and dark brown eyes. In the ring and out of it, Ed wears loose denim shorts, an unbuttoned, single-colored dress shirt, and a white T-shirt underneath. The only alternation in his style is the color of his outer shirt, which range from black to bright orange. Valet: Confidence Real Name: Unknown Debuted: August 2001 Valet Description: Often described as "the auburn haired beauty", Confidence is a slender, yet well-formed young lady with a flowing mane of auburn hair that comes down to the nape of her back. A small curl often hangs down in front of her left ear, just to the side of her wide-set teal eyes. Her face is vaguely heart-shaped, with a sharp, somewhat exotic beauty, and full, pouting lips. When she first debuted, Confidence wore only a tiger-striped bathing suit, but nowadays she wears more commonly accepted clothing. She seems to favor V-neck blouses and tight skirts. Well, maybe she doesn't, but Ed does. History: In 1992, Ed became a professional wrestler. He worked in various indy leagues for six years, gradually becoming known for his hardcore prowess and ability to take bumps that would make Mick Foley cringe. Finally, he signed on with Ultimate Fantasy Wrestling, broke into the mainstream, and then floundered when the company went face-first into a wall. Along with several of his friends, he joined the brand new PCW, and has been an off-and-on member ever since. Ed has had two Extreme Title runs so far, one as himself and one as his pop-star alter ego, "The Mack". Following a disagreement with the Phantom Booker, Ed left PCW to strike out on his own...and ended up working at McDonald's for several months. Finally, he began wrestling in indy leagues again, and in that time has dramatically improved his physical condition. Soon after his return, Ed won the Sea World Brawl, and then claimed the PCW Extreme Title for a third time. He held the strap for a month, and then dropped it to Clyde, who he was forced to team with in the Lethal Lottery. Unfortunately, he didn't seem to get on well with Bethany and the rest of the group. Following an incident involving the demonic eight-year-old and the Ream Team, Ed snapped, obliterating everyone he could get his hands on before passing out. Soon after, he brutally attacked Jon Kano, seemed to be back to normal, and then went completely off the deep end. Since then, Ed has gained several companions. First among them was the British Voice, a sarcastic and somewhat bitter "spiritual helper" who's helped Ed do everything from drive cars to avoid deadly sleep rays. Following the Red October PPV and the disruption that came in its wake, Ed finally decided to step up to the plate. He called Ivan Stanislav out, and since then has been relentless in his efforts to cause the Russian Bear pain. Ed doesn't seem too intent on winning the World Title -- he just wants to make Ivan hurt.
