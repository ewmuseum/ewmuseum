Ellie Hall Biography Federation Awards PCW Commissioner (Current), PCW Extreme Champion, PCW Valet of the Year 2002. Biography Eleanor Hall has landed on her feet. Many women in wrestling, who have no skills in the ring at all are stuck on the arm of a wrestler with the sole task of keeping their breasts pushed up while on camera, not for Ellie~! When she came to PCW she was simply Xavier Kannon’s valet, tempted into wrestling for the TV exposure she couldn’t get with her zero acting skills... And she both relished and revelled in it. As Xavier rose through the ranks she followed him, their close relationship leading to a marriage live on PCW PPV. But there were not many wishing her well, as she is quite honestly a bad human being.. Looking down on those who have less then her... And trying to ruin those who have just as much. But through a bizarre series of events she has now found herself in the role of PCW Commissioner, an although everyone knows it’s only so her husband can book his own matches, she, as always, is oblivious. But while the Xavier/Ellie~! relationship shows no signs of faltering, we can only expect Ellie~! to continue her reign of mild annoyance over PCW.
