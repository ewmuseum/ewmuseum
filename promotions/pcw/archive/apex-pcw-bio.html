Apex Biography Real Name Unknown Hometown Silver City, TX Birthday February 16, 1984 Height 5'11'' Weight 217 lbs Biography Apex’s past is blurry and unknown due to the fact he doesn’t speak much about it. All the information that is known about Apex was submitted by fans and people who have watched his career, or know a bit about him personally. Apex was born February 16, 1984, in Silver City, Texas; Apex’s parents are both unknown. He dropped out of school at the age of sixteen and formed an aggressive punk band called Hope Against Hope. When Apex wasn’t playing in bars with his band he was jumping off houses, landing in broken glass, and getting hit by foreign objects. Apex started backyard wrestling at the age of fourteen and continued to do so till he was eighteen. Apex was then picked up by a local wrestling promotion called the Longhorn Wrestling Council. Apex wrestled in the LWC for about a year, and became one of the best wrestlers in the small promotion. The LWC folded within a year; however, Apex was the only wrestler in the LWC to be inducted into the LWC Hall of Fame. After LWC folded, Apex had nowhere to go, so he packed what belongings he did have, left the band, and headed for the East Cost. While heading to New Jersey to check out a new talented promotion called Pro Wrestling Circuit, Apex wrestled on various cards around the Southern States. Apex was now a known name throughout the independent wrestling scene; however, Apex still continued on up toward New Jersey. After putting down numerous promotions and beating his body day in and day out, Apex strongly made his way to New Jersey to watch a few PWC events. It didn’t take long to make up his mind; Apex knew that PWC would be the perfect promotion. After many days of trying to contact the PWC, Apex finally got through, and was signed within a week.
