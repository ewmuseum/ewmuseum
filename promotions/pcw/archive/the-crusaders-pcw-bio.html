Tom Lexian for all that is good and right in the world. “The Crimson” Thinh Alexander Date Of Birth: October 14, 1974 Height: 5’11’’ Weight: 200 lbs Age: 27 years Hometown: Saigon, Vietnam Current Residence: Piedmont, California He is a young, yet very strong man. He is proud of his heritage, but because he has so many stereotypes against America, he has chosen to wear a mask to conceal himself in the ring. Even when out of the ring, when his appearance and racial ethnicity are plain to see, he wears sunglasses to hide the distinctive asian eyes that he possesses. He continues to try to find his ultimate self through his experience in and out of the ring, and believes becoming the top of the sport is the key to doing so. His secret is that he is a vampire, but one that has his soul and fights for the good of all humanity. 2) Appearance: Thinh Alexander is average size, with a rather average build. He has very broad shoulders, with short black hair which is parted to the side. He has a clean shaven face, and usually wears dark black shades when he’s in public. His costume includes a dark red mask with black shades around the eyes, and a very dark red body suit. On his chest is a large letter, “C” and he has black boots and gloves. 3) History: Thinh Alexander was born on October 14, 1974. He was the son of a United States officer, and a very prominent freedom fighter in the South Vietnamese army. It was their love that brought him life, and it was their lifestyles, that drove them apart. Just a year after he was born, his mother died during rogue bombings by the North Vietnamese army. His father was crushed, but unwilling to allow his son to go on without a mother. He would wind up moving his son from the war riddled country of Vietnam over to Japan, where the United States was slowly gaining influence. It was here that Thinh gained most of his knowledge of the martial arts, developed a love for art and music, and even picked up English, would be important later. As a child, Thinh stayed mostly to himself, although he had a few close friends. There were many stereotypes about Asians in Japan who were not of Japanese descent. Thinh hid his true identity, instead going by Masato Alexander in order to conceal his Vietnamese heritage and create a Japanese persona for himself. He would rise through the ranks of his school adeptly, graduating from high school with secondary honors and going to college at the University of Tokyo. Although he displayed a love for his studies and an energy to succeed, he still had no idea what he wanted to do as a career. His father worked as an educator. He was a former solider, with a very limited education himself, so going back to the United states would have meant a very poor future for his son and himself. Although he had a background devoid of any real skills, his ability to read and write English fluently proved an incredible asset for a country that was looking to educate their citizens. He would wind up following Thinh to every school he was enrolled in, even up to the University level where he was a professor of American studies. Thinh actually attended many schools in his scholastic career. Namely, four different elementary schools, three different high schools, and two different universities before he was handed a bachelors degree. The fault was Thinh’s and Thinh’s alone. He never truly found a school he liked, and was constantly badgering his father to move him to another. His father got tired of the constant moving, but did as his son wished. It was mostly Thinh’s fault, who continually was unsocial with the other children. It began as a problem of language, since all of the school children knew Japanese, while Thinh, naturally, only knew Vietnamese. The language gap distanced Thinh in every elementary school he was in. That bridge was eventually gapped, as Thinh learned Japanese, as well as English from his father. Still, his individualistic nature continued to keep him from making close friends. His university life was rocky at best. He was strangely attracted to the subjects of philosophy and psychology, and that lead him to believe that it would be the career of his future. An incident in one of his philosophy classes Sophomore year, however, would scar that entire idea. He got into a shouting match with his professor about the idea of a perfect family, and Thinh chastised him for being such a narrow minded man. Thinh was reprimanded for his words and disrespect for his instructor, but not expelled. Thinh would do the University one better, and transfer out to a smaller college. There, he strayed away from the subject matter he used to love, and instead went back to music and the arts. He was a casual clarinet player, and he began to hone his skills in music courses, as well as feeding his appreciation for the arts. He soon decided to be an Arts History major, with a minor in music. He graduated three years later with his bachelors degree, and began working on his doctorate degree at the University of Tokyo once more. It would be his fathers love for the United States, however, that would bring Thinh and his father back to the United States. His father kept Thinh in Asia for the simple fact that he was afraid that Thinh would not be accepted in a country that had warred so much with people of Asian descent. But his father made no bones about it that he had a lot of family there and wanted to return one day. With American feelings towards Asians improving, and his own age catching up with him, he decided to return to the United States and rejoin his family. He returned to much fanfare, as most of his loved ones thought he was dead or had been lost in action. They were equally surprised by his grown up son, who had an amazing array of knowledge and a great working vocabulary with English. Still, Thinh’s nature kept him distant from his father’s family. Things slowed down for Thinh, who loved the arts, but was soon growing wary. He had learned the martial arts, albeit very little, and wanted to engage in an activity that would spur on both his physical and mental states. He tried martial arts in the states, but found that it was more about flash and dash instead of becoming one with yourself. It was that this time that he met up with several independent wrestling stars, who had come to learn martial arts to try to spruce up their wrestling careers. They invited Thinh to see some of their shows, and so he did. He did, and he fell entranced by it. The action, the story, the drama…it all appealed to him. It was here that he realized that he wanted to wrestle, to be a wrestler, to hopefully find himself in the ring. His father was disappointed in his decision, and felt partly responsible for taking his son from his higher education and throwing him into a new situation. But he wound up supporting his son, even finding a school for Thinh to train at. As always, Thinh would wind up joining several different schools and leaving, and by the time he was ready to wrestle professionally, he hadn’t even stayed in one place long enough to graduate. Still, he had enough knowledge to wrestle and to fight. He worked several indy feds, with his father as his manager, until they finally got a call that would change Thinh’s fledgling career. He was called over to Realm Wrestling, where he was being given a chance to tell his story all over again. Thinh signed the contract, and brought with him his heart, his soul, and a mask to hide his true identity. But the truth would be revealed someday. “Night Shard” Robert Yarney 5’5’’ 240 lbs Richmond, California Face Hardcore Robert Yarney is a quiet man who lives a relatively boring life. But what he held back was his extraordinary powers. Those powers were his telekinesis, the ability to move objects with his mind, and his ability to cloak himself at night. He is otherwise a very lonely man and has little confidence, but when he becomes Night Shard and is teamed up with the other Crusaders of Light, his self worth becomes apparent. 2) Character Appearance: Robert Yarney is fairly overweight man, with a gut that tends to stick out a bit and a receding hair line. He was black hair with a bowl like bald spot. His costume includes an all black body suit, with a yellow utility belt and a yellow lightning bolt on his chest. His face mask is a black strip of material covering his eyes. He often has a bewildered look on his face, but is known to smile every now and then. Character History: Robert Yarney was born in Topeka, Kansas, where he was raised by a farmer father and a homemaker mother. He had two older brothers, both who followed in their father’s footsteps and became farmers themselves. Robert had other plans, however. He moved out of Kansas and to New York where he wanted to pursue his acting career some more. New York, unfortunately, was not ready for Robert. He was ostracized from the business because his lack of good looks and general acting ability, so he moved to California, too embarrassed to go back to Kansas. He had an affinity for numbers and became an unassuming accountant for the firm, New Wave Incorporated, an industrial company. He lived a rather simple life, going to work, coming home and eating dinner, and then going to sleep. The dark secrets of his life, however, was that he was born with the ability to be a telekinetic, meaning he could move objects with his mind. He also could cloak himself in the night so no one could see him. While living this life, he would eventually meet up with Legend Inc. and The Crimson, who would ask him to join the Crusaders of Light to fight injustice in PCW. Move Set Coming Soon!
