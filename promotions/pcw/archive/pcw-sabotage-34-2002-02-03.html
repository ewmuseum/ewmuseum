Sabotage 34 3 Feb 2002 Melbourne Sports & Entertainment Centre, Melbourne, Australia A sabotaged city and the Melbourne Sports Centre We get our usual opening to Sabotage. The wrestlers running around in a sabotaged city. Among the wrestlers highlighted are Flaymin Ayin, El Diablo, Tigerclaw, Aireborn Tyler, and Miyagawa. We cut to the inside of the Melbourne Sports & Entertainment Centre in Melbourne, Australia. The Aussie crowd is going nuts. But because of their lack of knowledge, they didn't bring any signs.
