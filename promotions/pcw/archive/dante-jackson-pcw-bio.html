Dante Jackson Biography Real Name Dante Jackson Nicknames 'The Messiah', 'The Prodigal Son', 'DJ' Hometown Houston, Texas Birthday August 26, 1978 Height 6'4' Weight 220 lbs Biography Entrance Music: The horns begin to blare as the insignia of the Lyrical Messiah plaster upon the PCW Tron as Tupac's voice breaks in, 'Ambitionz Az A Ridah' by 2Pac cuts in as a few pyro sets from opposites sides of the stage blasts upward through the arena as Dante parts through the entrance way, hopping on the mic before performing a customary flow or rap about one of his opponents, only hearing the instrumental of the song before tossing the microphone away and sliding into the ring, jaw jacking with the opponent, before the bell rings. It all began, the untold legacy, the writings on papyrus for the stories of life in Brooklyn, New York... ...on the date of August 26th, Pamela Jackson found herself clutching her stomach as the zooming of the neon blue Mercedes Benz her husband Eric had gotten for her 25th birthday. No care for trying to be a cautious driver, Eric kept the car in gear with his emotions on high alert as his wife of only a year was about to give birth to their child. Reaching the hospital about fifteen minutes past the original ignition of the car's journey, Eric opened the driver side door in a flash, running towards the other side opening the passenger side door for his pregnant wife. Turning his head, he saw a not used wheelchair parked on the side of the building closest to his Mercedes, a smiled grew on his face as he rushed over and grabbed the wheelchair, helping his wife out of the car. Rolling her into the George Washington hospital emergency room, he saw lab coat doctor's take the wheelchair out of his hands and left him to wait in time. He lifted up his arm and watched the look of his silver Rolex watch. The little hand was stuck on a three, the long hand on a seven. Looking behind him looking at the sky, a light blue covered with blank pure sky and a blazing sun made him tell the time. As he paced around the hospital waiting room, he continued to run his fingers through his wavy hair. An hour later, his son was brought into this world. Walking into the room, a smile of glee grew on his face as he saw his seed in his wife's arms sucking on his thumb. He kissed his son on the head and told him, 'One day, you'll be something.' Dante Jackson, born August 26th, 1978 at approx. 4:35 P.M. From brith, it wasn't sure if Dante actually would function like everyone in normal society. He seemed educated as soon as he left the womb. His mother, a regular school teacher in Manhattan and his father, a well known disc jockey and writer at the local newspaper brought him up on three things, law of education, law of professionalism and social working with girls, and music. Every Saturday night, his mother after finishing grading papers would play Marvin Gaye, The Temptations, Earth, Wind, & Fire, etc. They would jam out, and with his father disc jockeying nightly, music found its way into his mind daily, probably the cause of his feeling for music today. Studies at school were no different with a college graduate varied teacher for a mom as Dante managed to keep himself occupied as a child growing up around the rough sections of New York. When about in the second grade, Dante was mostly getting into trouble for bet boxing and banging on the table recreating music from people like Grandmaster Flash. About this time, his teacher Mrs. Felinstien happened to catch Dante in a curious situation. Thinking he was nothing more than a young hooligan whom had no care for school work, Dante happened to be sent to the principal's office. Sitting down in the left most chair, farthest away from the wood door with the glass outline in the middle stating his name with the use of drawn print, Dante looked down at the wheat linoleum on the floor, starring at a scurrying cockroach travelling down the floor with a piece of bread crumb in its mouth. A large shadowy foot stood over the cockroach, almost hitting it for an inch away. The cockroach ran as fast as it could, trying to be the car beating the tracks. Hitting only floor, the foot placed itself just movements away from the foot of Dante. 'I've never been to the principal's office before.' 'Don't worry, its no big deal since you're young and they don't play attention to young boys like you.' Dante looked up, to see a older boy, but not by that much looking directly at him with his head on the lime green painted wall. Blinking and turning his head, Dante just wondered at the person he was talking to, in sheer amazement that you could get in trouble, without the repercussions to follow. 'Cool, I'm Dante.' 'I didn't ask you for a name short man, I just told you some knowledge you needed for the brain.' Dante chuckled to himself as he looked up once more at the boy, a scar on the left side of his chin and a budding mustache hairs on his upper lip. The boy was a bit taller than Dante, about 4 feet flat on his heels. 'Malik Bishop, come in.' The principal calls his name out, Malik gets up from the chair and looks directly at Dante and shrugs. Not before long, Dante once again beings to beat box and begins to add lyrics to the beat box causing the secretary to bring in Dante towards the principal as well. Sitting him right next to Malik, the principal laid down some guidelines which the both of them knew they wouldn't follow and soon after the two of them hit it off, eventually becoming friends. At age ten, Dante got a turntable mix set for his birthday, joying over it non-stop. Rap was slowly getting its name out there and in New York, there would be nothing better than a freestyle battle at the nearest club spot. For his birthday, Dante got one more special gift for his birthday. His father happened to know certain members of the club scene for a 33 year old and really got to know a particular club owner, Marcus Ganier so well that Dante was able to enter the club just for this special occasion. It was like an odyssey through his eyes, bodies shaking, music going on, people sweating to the beat - music heaven! Looking up towards the main stage, Dante saw a young rapper by the name of LL Cool J on stage, preforming for a Def Jam recording party. When he was finished with his set, the crowd went nuts, chanting his name. Right then and there, Dante knew what he wanted to become. School years passed him by, grades steadily at the same recognizable pace they originally were. Nineteen Ninety Four brought Dante at fourteen, in the first year of his teenage days Dante was a popular kid, fitting in nicely with other school boys and girls, because of his way of talking. So informative, so comical it won him a lot of friends and admirers mainly many girls calling the Jackson household when Dante was supposed to be in the bed. When one late Saturday night, Dante ventured off into a house party his friend Malik had set up since his parents were gone for the weekend. Walking in with a smile on his face, Dante could only let his emotions run wild on this night, no restrictions to hold him down. Dancing with fine girls, freestyling on the microphone to mass cheers, drinking for the first time and almost getting drunk while at it. This was a fast life, the basement room packed with strobe lights and numerous people from Stonewall Jackson Intermediate dancing to the groove of the Notorious B.I.G. On this night, Dante could have lost it right then and there, but walking up to Malik as he danced with his arms tightly around the waist of one Latin American girl who looked so African American, she probably could pass for it any day of the week. 'This party is just...' 'Just what? You at a loss for words or something kid?' 'Loss for words? Nah, its just that girls wanna get hype with me and...' 'And what?' The Latina took her head and crocked it backwards, so it would be laying on Malik's shoulder her eyes squarely looked on the face of Dante as he smiles a bit towards her. He remembers something as he places his hand into his pocket, pulling out a Trojan and raising his eyebrow at it. Stuffing it back into his pocket, Dante smiles to the Latina, while Malik continues to dance with the sway of his hips. Moving away, Dante spots a young African American girl with a white complexion towards her skin to make her look lighter enough not to be black, but dark enough not to be white. Using his cocky grin, Dante uses his charm and casually walks over to her, whispering in her ear as Jodeci begins to play over the DJ's record. He nibbles on her earlobe, kissing down her neck and behind her ear. Smoove as a fox, as Dante continued his play of swooning girls to his feet. Soon it leads to the bedroom and well...I'll leave the rest up to imagination. 'Tracy Ellison.' The name was written on his hand along with numbers as she kissed on on the cheek passionately, before their tongues intertwined and danced for what seemed like an eternity. Malik walked up behind him as Tracy went to walk home. 'How the hell you did that kid?' 'Skill, all skill.' 'Whatever son, it's not like I care though, you can hit honey passed the nighttime hour into daylight, but you know yo' pops is gonna kill you if you don't hit that house.' 'Nah, I'm going through the back, anyway what happened to that girl that had her ass on your pants, dancing real close with?' Licking his lips in a playful fashion, Malik whispered into Dante's ears which caused Dante to laugh and using their hand shake, Dante ventured back towards his house, playing like he was sleep when he hit the bed, fully clothed. After graduation of high school, and more girls as well, Dante thought of settling down to have kids with Tracy, but his parents, mainly his 45 year old father told him, 'You either go to college, or become one of them faggots in prison.' Hearing that, Dante enrolled himself in Penn State University, Tracy not far behind him as she went to Pitt. Using psychology as a major, although it wouldn't do good streetwise, nor a bachelor in business could propel him into a job that fitted him the way he liked it. So on weekends, or in case of holidays in which there were no classes, Dante went back up to New York and continued massing legions of fans while still being one to keep his promise towards Tracy. On a particular night in 1997, Dante's sophmore year he went to the Hammerstein Ballroom just for kicks and a night out with his friends. What he didn't know was that they were going to a wrestling show. Extreme Championship Wrestling, an upstart to the WWF and WCW brands happened to be in town and putting on a great show for the month of August. Looking at the outlook of a Taz/Sabu match, the extreme usage of body language and place of violence it made Dante's eyes widen, not an instant love for the sport although he did have the body for it. He went back to Penn State that night, in question of where his priorities lay. After graduating in late 2001, Dante found that second jobs are applicable towards the rap world, looking back at articles of Biz Markie, 'I'm going to be a wrestler.' First story, a change over into the actual professional wrestling world, also known as your initiation. Primetime Wrestling Academy, and really you can ask if it were a short stay because less than a month their and four grades earn you a promotion. Not a graduation, but a promotion. Dante thought it sucked as well, one day before getting an inch closer towards a Class title shot. He welcomed open arms towards the XWA, another like the RSW where improvement is needed for a greater profession and job. RSW wasn't that very high, just about as short as his PWA experience with only one match under his belt. Rumors had it that Dante was going to carry the company on his back, but those weren't been proven yet and so hasn't success. In RUSH wrestling, only two months ago Dante Jackson made a feverish splash into the circuit, quickly gaining a name for himself as one of the better competitors in the wrestling world. When himself and Statuz Quo happened to think about what would become of themselves after Crossroads, they began to plan. At Third Dawning, the two performers Dawned to the entire PCW world as they both made the jump, shoving it in RUSH's face and even being over RUSH as a partner in crime Rush Mason owns the federation itself. Both men take it up for the tag team ranks, Playaz Inc. coming in and bringing the old ruckus that they performed earlier in RUSH. Soon that they actually thought, they happened to get better and better as the weeks progressed, before long at Red October they had done what only Da Westsidaz had accomplished - Black Tag Team Champions. Now, they're pushing for more than 42 days at the top, wanting to set a Big Allan Steele like high for the Tag Team titles, whether it come soon or late. Describe a personality of him and then you get a mixture, a hybrid if you will, of a cool, collected on top of his game Chris Benoit and the cocky, suave charm of Don Juan. Or if you want his third occupation, music is the key in the world today and Dante follows it with his heart, being one of the biggest acts on the scene today. Intensity in the ring is at a boiling point, outside its a different story because when he's put to work, you get the total thing, a raw and polished gem to the eye of man. Why he choose a chance to pursue both careers, he wishes he could change every thought, but he's in the game now and you can't deny him of that.
