Fusion 92 27 Sep 2002 James H. Hilton Coliseum, Ames, Iowa. Brandi's office encounter with Nathan A young man with rugged features, a tight muscle shirt, and the looks of a young college football player stands nervously in front of a door. He reaches up as if to knock, shakes his head, and reads the sign taped to the door which says, "I'M BUSY, AND YOU'RE UGLY. GO AWAY!". He takes a deep breath, knocks, and hurries inside. Inside, Brandi is filling out some paperwork at her desk. She looks annoyed to be bothered. Brandi: Can't you read the sig… She looks up and is shocked to find the handsome man standing on the other side of her desk. Brandi: Nevermind. The sign doesn't seem to apply to you. Is there something I can help you with? Young Man: No, not really. I was just sent here by Mr Kannon to tell you that it's almost time for your announcement. Brandi grins and stands up, walking slowly towards the nervous man. Her high heels click against the linoleum, each step sending a chill up his spine. Brandi: You seem awfully nervous for some reason. Something wrong? He fidgets in place and shakes his head. Brandi giggles. She unbuttons the top button on black blouse, and sits down on the edge of her desk, crossing her right leg over the left. Brandi: Are you sure? You seem kind of uncomfortable. Young Man: I'm…I'm…fine! Brandi: What's your name? Young Man: Nathan. Brandi gets up and runs a finger up Nathan's chest towards his chin. She grins and pats him lightly on the cheek. Brandi: Well, thanks for the message…handsome. Brandi quickly walks past him and out into the hallway. The door is barely able to close before she bursts into giggles and hurries off down the empty corridor. Brandi: …handsome.
