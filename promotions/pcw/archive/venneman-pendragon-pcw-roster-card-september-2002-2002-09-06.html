Venneman Pendragon Real Name: Venneman Pendragon Birthdate: 2/18/76 Hometown: St. Cloud, Minnesota Height: 6'5' Weight: 265 lbs. Nicknames: 'The Legend' Status: Neutral Entrance Music: 'The Small Hours' by Metallica Information Wrestling Style: Technical Powerhouse Set Up Move: 'The Sword in the Stone' abbrv. SIS (A hard spear to a standing opponents back. A surprise move, it can be hit from anywhere. Also a setup to his finisher, the Last Crusade. ) Finisher: 'The Last Crusade', 'Fall of a Legend' ('The Last Crusade'-The move normally follows the Sword in the Stone, unless the SIS is done as a last ditch defense. Venneman firsts lifts his oppoenent into a torture rack position. From here he slams them down onto one knee in a devestating shoulder breker. Afterwards he lifts them back up and down into a backbreaker which he holds for submission upon his knee. 'Fall of a legend'-Reverse DDD. Opponent is hoisted onto Venneman's shoulders, face up. Venneman puts one hand on the throat, one hand on an upper leg and performs the DDD, driving opponents face into the mat.) PCW Title History: Former Rising Star Champion, TV Title Holder (Current) PCW Award History: Other Federations: FWA, NCWA, BSN, CCW Other Federation Awards: FWA Kaiser Champion, Imperial Champion...NCWA World and International Champ at same time....CCW Trans Atlantic Champion at feds Closing. Handler Information Handler Name: Davis AIM Name: [detail withheld] MSN Name: Quotes "Prepare...FOR THE PAIN!!!"
