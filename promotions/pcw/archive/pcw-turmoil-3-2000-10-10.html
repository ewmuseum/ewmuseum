Turmoil 3 10 Oct 2000 Parker Field, Richmond, Virginia Introduction Turmoil opens with a music and video package of some PCW wrestlers posing throughout a ruined city. Wrestlers highlighted are Troy Brown, Jason Chase, Johnny Raindance, Some guy with a mask no one has ever seen before, Soy Boy, Dillan Meyers, Ryan Knox, Scott Naket, Kremmen, Doug Troy and Meanstreak. Once the opening is finished there is a video recap of Meanstreak's confrontation with Johnny Raindance last week on Fusion. That is followed by a collage of all 25 eliminations from the Carnival of Carnage Battle Royal for the PCW Extreme Title. It closes with Ryan Knox hitting Kremmen with DJ Raw's Yellow Hummer, then hitting the PCW Extreme Champion Scott Naket with Future Shock. The scene goes live to a helicopter shot of Parker Field, home of the Richmond Braves, the Atlanta Braves Triple-A farm club show a jam packed outdoor stadium. They circle each other again, and Knox again tries to strike Naket with the bat. Naket ducks the swing and steps forward in toward Knox, grabs his shirt and delivers a quick headbutt. Knox staggers back and leans against the ropes, rubbing his forehead and getting visibly upset at the sight of Naket's blood over him. Naket rubs his own forehead, smearing the blood further up into his hair and over his face, and grins evilly as he charges Knox and clotheslines him over the top rope, sending him and the riot policeman he landed on sprawling into the guard rail. Naket climbs through the ringropes and down the stairs, then picks up the stairs and charges Knox, connecting with his head. Knox goes down hard, holding his head. Naket reaches under the ring and takes out a fire extinguisher and as Knox climbs to his feet Naket pulls the trigger and engulfs Knox in the fine white mist. Knox swings his barbed-wire-wrapped fists blindly, and actually flukes a hard blow across Naket's right cheek. Naket howls in pain, and his cheek starts bleeding profusely. Knox regains vision and charges Naket, knocking him to the floor. Knox sits astride Naket and rains blows on his head and face, causing Naket to bleed further. Knox gets up and goes over to the corner of the ring, lifts the curtain and reaches underneath. He pulls out a saucepan, looks at it in disgust and throws it over his shoulder. A pot plant, same. A screwdriver, Knox shoves that into the pocket of his jeans. A kitchen skillet. Knox grins and takes it and heads back for Naket. Knox rains blows on the blood-soaked Naket with the kitchen skillet. Naket covers his head with his hands and howls with each blow. Knox picks up Naket and clotheslines him across the throat with the skillet. Knox throws the skillet into the crowd, then charges at Naket again, who somehow ducks and backdrops Knox onto the announcers table. Naket grabs a chair and waffles Knox across the head with it. WHACK!. WHACK!.. WHACK! WHACK! Knox rolls off the table and onto the floor, between the table and the guard rail. Naket re-enters the ring, and seeing that Knox is still down from the multiple chairshots, demands that the referee start to count him out. The referee shakes his head and slaps his hand three times, indicating to Naket that the decision must be by pinfall. Naket glares at him for a second, kicks him in the gut, then locks him in a rear headlock and hits the HOTT DROP on him. The referee lies motionless in the ring. Supernova Goes Pop hits the PA as the lights start to strobe. Meanstreak runs in and heads straight for Naket. He kicks the Extreme Champion hard in the gut and turns him over onto his back in At Death's Door, right on the floor outside! Meanstreak howls in triumph, then returns up the rampway to the locker rooms. Long minutes pass with all three men out. Finally, Ryan Knox starts to stir from under the table. He gets up and sees that Naket is still down after Meanstreak's vicious attack. Before he can take advantage of the situation, one of the riot police breaks ranks and picks Knox up from the floor under the table and places him on the table. The officer of the law then gets up on the table with Knox, and lifts him up on his shoulders, and drops him down in a Tombstone Piledriver, shattering the table and sending them both sprawling onto the floor! Naket comes to and gets back to his feet. He staggers over to Knox, picks him up and drags him by the hair over to the ring, and rolls him back in. Knox drops to the mat and lies motionless, out cold from the piledriver through the table by the mystery riot policeman. Naket picks up the ring steps again, and throws them into the ring. They bounce into the corner with a clatter. Naket goes under the ring and pulls out a table, and slides that into the ring as well. Naket sets up the table, and then lines up the steps next to them. Ignoring the fallen referee, Naket picks up Knox, and positions him on the steps, facing away from the table, and signals again for the Hott Drop. Naket with a HOTT DROP on Knox off the ringsteps through the table! Naket covers Ryan Knox and, seeing that the referee is still out, calls to the riot policeman, who comes in and counts to three himself 1.2.3! Naket and the policeman get to their feet. The riot policeman hands him the Extreme Title belt, then raises Naket's hand in the air. Naket, his head now completely covered in blood, accepts the title belt, and he and the riot policeman leave the ring together and go out to the back, with the riot policeman supporting Naket as he walks up the ramp. As they get to the top, the Riot policeman removes his mask, revealing the man to be Revenant. He takes off his flack jacket to reveal a sHs tee shirt! EMT's arrive and take the referee and Ryan Knox to the back on stretchers. Winner: No contest Time: 6:59 Dave: Well, I am afraid we are out of time.
