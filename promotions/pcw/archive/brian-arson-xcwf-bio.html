Brian Arson Biography Real Name Brian Arson Nicknames Really Hot Guy, Guy Who Is Really Hot Hometown Santa Barbara, CA Birthday 6/14/78 Height 6'4 Weight 245 lbs Biography At twenty-eight years old, Brian is financially set for life. Even prior to his successful wrestling career, Brian had plenty of money. His father, James K. Arson, owns one of the largest law firms in Santa Barbara and could have retired years ago, but he loves money and making millions more only makes him happier. It was quite apparent that James spoiled Brian tremendously. He was never around to be with his son so he thought making up for it by other means would be just as well. Brian’s mother had been killed in a car accident when he was only seven and this left him in the hands of multiple nannies throughout the years of his childhood. Not being taught differently, Brian liked to flaunt his money and was incredibly over confident in himself when he got into middle and high school. This turned many people away from him, but if one thing could be said about him, it was that he was very intelligent, and if one didn’t know this about him, after one meeting, Brian would make sure he did. Whether it be by bribe or force, Brian Arson gets what he wants. Many have stood strong and proud, or at least attempted to, but the situations they were in never were resolved nicely, for Arson is a dangerous man, a very dangerous man. What is the true reason that Arson is known underground throughout the West Coast as being so powerful? Only time can answer that question, but the time is not now. He leads a seclusive life that has only been revealed to a select few, a life of crime, a life of secrecy. A life which he now brings to PCW...
