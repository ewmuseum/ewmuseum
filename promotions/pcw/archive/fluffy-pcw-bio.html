Fluffy Biography Biography 'Fluffy sleeping one day, then men take fluffy away on big green shiny bird. Fluffy sleeping and wake up inside bird. Fluffy and men got eaten by it. Bird fall asleep and Fluffy climb out in strange place with big rocks. Fluffy look for food but no animals. Fluffy find dead ones and begin to eat. Then another man come, but he not like other men. Fluffy like him. Man call man Rezz. Rezz call Fluffy Fluffy and tell him to speak. Now Fluffy watch Rezz fight men in big bright places with fences like Fluffy had to break to get to chicken food. Bad men hurt Rezz, so Fluffy do things to bad men. Fluffy hungry.' To put this in words you can understand, I found Fluffy in a butcher shop, sucking the blood from a carcass. My guess from what he tells me is that some people in an airplane found and sedated him, then it crashed and he made his way into the city. I remember a story about a plane crash on the news a few days before meeting him. Then some dog came in, a little poodle. Fluffy pounced on it and stuck his tongue into the neck, he sucked the blood THROUGH his tongue, like some kind of sharpened straw. Then the owner came in seconds later and saw her little poodle laying on the floor with some monster sucking the blood out. I had no choice but to copy Fluffy and shut her up. After we were both done feeding, we dumped the bodies in with the rest of the carcasses. We figured nobody would go looking there, not with the smell. It would all be incinerated or something, anyway. Just before I shoved the dog in, I noticed it had a collar with a nametag. In honour of the night and the events, I named him Fluffy. And that's how a vampire and a Chupacabra became best friends. Real Name Fluffy The Chupacabra Nicknames That little grey thing, the other grey rights protester Hometown Puerto Rico Birthday ??? Height 3' 5 (truly, about 2', but the spines extend another foot) Management Style Supreme Freakness Weight Unknown, guessed around 75-90 Special Moves Jumping around, usually colliding with the victim. Other Tactics UFO: (Unhygienic Falling Obscenity) From the top turnbuckle or if floating, Fluffy performs a vertical splash on the opponent, hitting the target's face. In short, he lands butt-first on their head. Cranium Clamp: Fluffy sits on the opponent's head, digging in with the claws on both his hands and feet. The opponent usually runs around screaming until Nerezza blindsides them. FLUFFY SUCKS!: When the referee is distracted, Fluffy hops into the ring, sits on the opponent's shoulder and his sharpened tongue shoots from his mouth into the victim's neck. He drains some of their blood to make them dizzy and disoriented, then hops back out. You won't see this too often. Kaniff Neckbreaker: Fluffy will use his squirrel-like agility to run up an opponent's body, ducking and weaving, sometimes spiralling up until he reaches the victim's head. He then runs in circles around their head and either grabs a piece of hair or holds the sides of the head with his hands, then sits down on the shoulder and spins the head quickly, attempting to pull a few muscles in the neck. Fluffy will also use the following techniques to distract Nerezza's opponents: Dancing. Because nothing's more distracting than a Chupacabra doing the Macarena. Flying around. Except for this. When the rubbery spines on Fluffy's back quiver rapidly, he floats in the air like a helicopter. He's able to move with all the precision of one too, flying in circles and even figure-of-8 motions. If nothing else, he usually gets a pop from the crowd for this. Attacking/Stealing property. If an opponent brings any items of clothing, weapons or other accessories to ringside, Fluffy will pick them up and either run off with them, or parade around the outside of the ring wearing them. He's an attention whore, really! Entrance Music Hellraiser - Ozzy Osbourne & Black Sabbath
