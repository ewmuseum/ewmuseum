Big Daveylicious Biography Real Name [name withheld] Nicknames The Enchilada Of Champions, Mentor Of Professional Wrestling, Deity of Entertainment, Dynasty of Charisma, Man With The Ego Bigger Than Texas, Man With 1,001 Ways Telling You How He Is Gonna Kick Your Ass, Big D, PCW's He-Man Woman Hater Hometown Miami, Florida Birthday December 19,1978 Height 6'5 Weight 247 lbs Biography On September 14th, 2001 a career was ended and a career would start on Fusion with a win. Big Daveylicious ended the PCW career of the Green Mofo and began his own. A month before he was in negotiations of a possible PCW contract after leaving Five Star Wrestling as a former FSW World Heavyweight Champion. Big D vowed to the world that he would not end up like the other ex-FSW stars that had entered the PCW before him. After defeating numerous PCW stars the former FSW star joined in the Thugs For Hire with the likes of Badge, 'Polish Punisher' Tom Walzack, and Tim Walden. The group was just as the name put it. Other members of the PCW roster would hire them with a good amount of money and attack their enemies for them. Big D’s run with the Thugs For Hire gave him a feud with former NTICW World Heavyweight champion, Big Poppa Plump. Big D would be the cause that took away the word 'Big' away from the Plumpster causing him to be Poppa Plump. Soon after Plump would become the Big Daveylicious’s tag team partner for the Lethal Lottery tag tournament for the PCW World Heavyweight title. 'Mr. Extravaganza' carried the team to the third round of the tournament but they lost due to Plump. Later on Big D would end the PCW career of Plump during a Berlin Wall match for a shot at the Rising Star title at Red October. As for the Thugs For Hire business wasn’t going too good. Finally, during Alliances they broke their alliance and went their separate ways. Big D’s separate way led him to becoming the 'PCW’s He-man Women Hater'. He would have various battles with the woman roster trying to prove they were not meant to be in the ring. He beat all three of them known as Sassy, Kya Brady, and Brandi. Brandi was the toughest woman for Big D to beat in an Extreme match with interferences of Rent-A-Hero and Woman protesters. Later on, Big D would cost Brandi of her Extreme title to Hank in an Fatal Fourway Elimination match for the Extreme title. After, the month of January. All the women were fired from the PCW. Leaving Big D with no more women to fight. Proving his point that women fighting in the PCW is a place were they don't belong. Big D would join in the ranks of the tag division. Bringing in a friend from the FSW, Mike Frampton and ending the career of 'Amazing' Mike Storm in the process. Big D and Frampton became the Sensation of Anihilation. Being part of the S.O.A he costed Core the Extreme title to Karl LeBlanc. On April 7th of 2002 at Spring Fling. After 7 months of being in the PCW, Big Daveylicious wins the Extreme title in a 6-Pack Elimination match. It was not only a night of redemption but a night of sleeping with three naked women! After his first title defense which was a no show his own tag team partner betrayed him into tricking him into a match against him nearly costing him his title. An irrate Big D destroyed the SOA by ruining Mikes important matches. The two tag partners later fought each other at PCW Supremacy in a Pole match to determine who is the Extreme champion and the best FSWer in the PCW. The better of the two won which was Big D. Soon after he won his match he was fired like the rest of the PCW roster at the pay-per-view. Costing him to beat Dan Hollywoods 41 Extreme title regin. His regin lasted 36 days. He was later rehired to be apart of the 'new' PCW. After all his hard work for the title you bet he is irate. Frustrated about the title being taken away from him he went back to the place that made him the FSW. Leaving the PCW as the longest staying former FSW star. After, staying with the FSW for nearly half a year. He along with his stablemates of the Supreme Court destroyed the FSW as planed. Now, he's back in his new home with his new girl, Delily.
