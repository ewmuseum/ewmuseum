Turmoil 100 29 Oct 2002 Flaminio Arena in Rome, Italy Venneman Pendragon discovers hip-hop at the record store The scene opens inside what appears to be a busy record store. Young kids, well, teenagers, fill the aisles looking at their favorite musics, debating whether or not to buy or go home and simply download what they want on their PC. Where dost thou….Ahem…I mean….Where da rap at? The fellow chuckles before replying after he gets a good look at the getup Pendragon has donned. He smiles and points towards aisle three. Pendragon nods and limps off. As he goes, the fellow gets on the phone…. Man: Yo, M…I thought I’d give ya a ring…You have GOT to come down here and see this…….Cool…Peace. The man hangs up and continues to watch the rather large, white, “thug”. Pendragon has sidled down and now stands directly in front of a rack of rap Cds. He picks up this one and that, trying to decide on one that he can sample. Venneman: Hmm….Redman….Eminem….Tupac? So many odd names to choose from…He then spots it. In a dusty area of the rack reserved only for the worst of the worst, he notices a man on the cover of a CD doing much what he is doing. Vanilla Ice. He picks up the CD and smiles…. Venneman: This fellow looks like he knows what be going DOWN! He walks over to the headphone that has the scanner built in so he can take a listen to the music and puts them on, running the CD beneath the scanner. A quizzical look comes over his face before he smiles and begins to bob his head. I a voice that is not quite accustomed to the style, he starts to, um….Sing… Venneman: Alright stop….Collaborate and listen…. He then looks up at the counter and begins to get loud…. Venneman: YO! Where all da good music be at in this joint? I mean, ‘Nilla’s tight and all, but I gotta keep it REAL! He starts to strut around the store, making a rather large scene of himself, when a loud voice rings out… YO V! What the fuck you doin here? The sot bounces over to show Rush Mason standing in the door, an unfriendly smile upon his face… Rush: This some kind of JOKE? Venneman looks at the man and instantly his visage is one of pure anger… Venneman: YOU!!! It ain’t no joke…You tried out my way, and found it hard…I’m just seein’ how you be living… Rush lets out a laugh at the wannabe in front of him, before his face turns angry… Rush: Well….This here’s my motherfuckin place, and you AIN’T welcome! Cds fly everywhere when they hit and Pendragon bounds to his feet. Rush jumps up to and the two men start trading punches, toe to toe. Rush moves in close, grabs Pendragon and slings him across the room. There is no rope around this ring, so Pendragon’s momentum carries him across the way and he crashes through the window in the front of the store. Mason walks calmly after and just as he reaches Pendragon, who is climbing up from the ground, glass falling off if him, Mall security arrives….Lots of it. Six men to each combatant. They break up the scuffle and escort the two men, who still fight to get away, in opposite directions… Rush: Your ass is mine, white boy! Venneman: I am sure thou wishest thou might have my ass!
