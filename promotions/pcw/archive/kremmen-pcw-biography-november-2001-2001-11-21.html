Kremmen — PCW biography (November 2001)

21 November 2001
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Real Name: Kremmen von Smackdown Nick Name: Kremmy Catchphase: Do not talk over me! Character Handler: Ross MSN Account: [detail withheld] AIM Address: [detail withheld] Theme Song: O'Fortuna - Carmina Burana (used as the theme to the movie "Excalibur") Age: 29 From: Melbourne, Australia (residing in Washington DC) Height: 6'3" Weight: 275lbs Wrestling Style: All-rounder Signature Moves: Taser (aka Cattle Prod) to the guts Brainbuster Suplex German Suplex Belly to Belly Suplex Sidewalk Slam Dragon Screw Legwhip Double-arm DDT Scoop Slam Ringpost ankle lock Dragon Screw Legwhip :P Reverse Neckbreaker Titlawhirl Backbreaker Finisher Move: X-Kremment (Vertical DVD - dropping opponent hard on the top of their head instead of shoulders) Set-Up Move: Kick to the gut Current Team: Management, with PCW President Tyler Nelson, Unstoppable Eugene and PCW Heavyweight Champion Ivan Stanislav Past Teams & Groups: Team CEO, with Tyler Nelson for the Lethal Lottery tournament Title History: First PCW Heavyweight Champion, at PCW Dawning Awards History: First inductee into the Hall of Fame Winner of Match of Champions Winner of first 32 man singles tournament, culminating at PCW Dawning Co-finalist in the 64 man singles tournament, culminating at March Madness Co-finalist with Tyler Nelson (Team CEO) in the 32 team Lethal Lottery Tournament Won first ever Main Event in PCW - Friday Fusion, September 22, 2000 Co-awarded feud of the Year for 2000, with Meanstreak First competitor to break the $10,000 mark in the now defunct money earnings system. Description: TBA History: TBA Record: Singles: 16:7 (1 on 1, triple threat or fatal four way) 4 Man Tag: 4:2 6 Man Tag: 1:0 8 Man Tag: 1:0 Total: 22 wins, 9 losses Match History: Alliances - November 18, 2001 Jeremy "Krayzie" Howard defeated Kremmen in a Last Man Standing match. Howard also pinned Kremmen in the Alliances main event. Fusion - November 16, 2001 Kremmen defeated Jeremy "Krayzie" Howard in a Straight Jacket match. Turmoil - November 13, 2001 Kremmen, Tyler Nelson and PCW Heavyweight Champion Ivan Stanislav defeated Snow and The Blood Brothers when Kremmen pinned Snow. Sabotage - November 11, 2001 In a Four Corners, Four man per team Tag Team Match, (Ivan Stanislav, Kremmen, Tyler Nelson and Edgecrusher) and (Jeremy Howard, Ed, Miyagawa and Timo Bolamba) and (Akiyama, Kenji Hirose, Itagaki Freeze and Ketsueki Karasu) and (Rent A Hero, The White Mexican, Big Alan Steele & Chris Chase) went to a no-contest. Fusion - November 9, 2001 Kremmen and Jeremy "Krayzie" Howard fought to a no contest. Turmoil - November 6, 2001 The team of Kremmen, Tyler Nelson, Edgecrusher and PCW Heavyweight Champion Ivan Stanislav went to a no-contest with Ed, Miyagawa and Jeremy Howard after Clyde interfered in a classic clusterfuck ending involving lots of fire!! Sabotage - November 4, 2001 Kremmen defeated PCW's President Trashcan Man, who was later fired and replaced by Tyler Nelson Turmoil - October 30, 2001 Ivan Stanislav defeated Meanstreak (c), Kremmen and Tyler Nelson for the PCW Heavyweight Title after all three men joined forces and along with special referee Edgecrusher, attacked Meanstreak. Sabotage - October 28, 2001 In an impromptu match, Tyler Nelson defeated Kremmen which allowed him to compete in the PCW Heavyweight Title Match on the following Tuesday Turmoil. Red October - October 21, 2001 1. Kremmen and Tyler Nelson defeated Jeremy Howard and Rent-a-hero to advance to the finals of the Lethal Lottery Tournament for the vacant PCW Heavyweight Title. 2. In the final of the Lethal Lottery tournament, Ivan Stanislav and Meanstreak defeated Kremmen and Tyler Nelson when Meanstreak pinned Tyler Nelson. 3. Kremmen defeated Tyler Nelson to become the #1 contender for the PCW Heavyweight Title. Fusion - October 19, 2001 A match between Kremmen & Tyler Nelson, Meanstreak and Ivan Stanislav, Kid Wonder and Snow and Jeremy Howard and Rent-a-hero resulted in a no-contest. Sabotage - October 14, 2001 Kremmen, Tyler Nelson & Edgecrusher defeated Big Alan Steele, Meanstreak and Ivan Stanislav, allowing Kremmen and Tyler Nelson to advance to the fourth round of the Lethal Lottery tournament for the Vacant PCW Heavyweight Title. By winning this match, Team CEO won their tournament bracket. Sabotage - October 7, 2001 Kremmen and Tyler Nelson defeated Boda and Continental Champion "The Malice Man" Duke Williams to advance to the third round of the Lethal Lottery Tournament for the Vacant Heavyweight Title. Turmoil - October 2, 2001 1. Kremmen defeated Nikita Morozov in the Main Event in a match for the rights to use O'Fortuna - Carmina Burana as an entrance theme. 2. Kremmen and Tyler Nelson defeated Timo Bolamba and Kramer WildWing to advance to the second round of the Lethal Lottery Tournament for the Vacant Heavyweight Title. "Dream Card" - Fusion - June 29, 2001 - Kremmen wins inaugural Match of Champions, featuring all twelve former PCW Heavyweight Champions. This match featured the eternally cool "finger poke of death" by Frisco on Stewart Hix, and a Gurney Ride of Doom on Jeremy "Krayzie" Howard! Sring Fling - April 2001 - Kremmen defeated Kid Wonder and "The Scholar" Howard Porter in his official retirement match. March Madness - March 4, 2001 - Kremmen defeated Super Duper Masked Jobber King (Kid Wonder under a mask) but is defeated by Meanstreak and Frisco to be eliminated from the final round of the 64 Man Heavyweight Title tournament. Turmoil - February 27, 2001 - Kremmen defeated Trashcan Man and moves onto the finals of the Heavyweight Title Tournament at March Madness Fusion - February 23, 2001 - Kremmen defeated Tyler Nelson - the leader of Media - in the third round of the Heavyweight Title tournament. Fusion - February 16, 2001 - Kremmen defeated Hardknocks Chris Allen (aka Boda) in the second round of the 64 man tournament for the Heavyweight Title. Fusion - February 10, 2001 - In one of his darkest career moments, Kremmen and mystery partner Reverend are defeated by Scott Kilborn and Revenant after interference from Hardknocks Chris Allen (aka Boda). Turmoil - February 6, 2001 - Kremmen defeated Boca del Inferno in the first round of the 64 Man Tournament for the vacant PCW Heavyweight Title Turmoil - January 30, 2001 - Kremmen makes his return to Turmoil, giving Scott Kilborn the Gurney Ride of Doom and giving him a lecture in "Old School" philosophy! Fusion - December 22, 2000 - Kremmen is eliminated by Meanstreak, who goes on to win the match and take the Heavyweight Title for the second time. Decimation - December 17, 2000 - Kremmen partners with Meanstreak, Reverend and Doug Troy to take on Kid Wonder, Alan Kriegman, Johnny Raindance and Trashcan Man in the main event. The four men are victorious and go on to Fusion to battle it out for the vacant Heavyweight Title. Fusion - October 27, 2000 - Kremmen lost the PCW Heavyweight Title to Meanstreak after being gored by a bull ridden to the ring by Meanstreak, and being the recipient of a calf deposited on him courtesy of a Meanstreak DVD. Dawning - October 22, 2000 - Kremmen defeated Meanstreak, Doug Troy and Reverend to become the first PCW Heavyweight Champion. Fusion - October 20, 2000 - Doug Troy and Reverend Jon Hull receive the Gurney Ride of Doom! Kremmen goes to a no-contest with The Joker due to interference by Meanstreak. Turmoil - October 17, 2000 - Kremmen gives a rare in-card interview, and is attacked by a defibrilator-weilding Doug Troy afterward. Kremmen later returns to give Joker, who had been attacked by rabid Koalas, one of his three career Gurney Rides of Doom! Brawler and Main Man are the recipient of a unique Double Gurney Ride of Doom! Fusion - October 13, 2000 - Meanstreak goes on the Gurney ride of Doom after an attack from Kremmen and HAC`! Turmoil - October 10, 2000 - Kremmen defeated Big J in the third round of the Heavyweight Title tournament, despite suffering severe injuries at the hands of Meanstreak. Fusion - October 6, 2000 - Runner up in the Carnival of Carnage, which was won by Scott Naket after Kremmen was run down in the first in-card appearance by the infamous Yellow Hummer. Turmoil - October 3, 2000 - Kremmen defeated Blood in the second round of the Heavyweight Title tournament. Meanstreak aborts attack on Kremmen to launch a fatal attack on Freak of Nature. Turmoil - September 26, 2000 - Kremmen defeated Freak of Nature (handled by HANK~!) in the first round of the 32 man Heavyweight Title tournament. Fusion - September 22, 2000 - Kremmen defeated The Third Degree in 13:57 (The Main Event of PCW's first Card)
