Background Nathaniel Shannon 'Shane' Crockett was born on April 1st, 1979 in Los Angeles, California to David and Maria Crockett. Shane was born into a wealthy family, one of the most influential wrestling clans in the world at that time: The Crocketts of the Carolinas. Moving to Charlotte when he was only three, Shane was quickly captivated by the intense competition his uncle, Jim Crockett brought to the table. At an early age, Shane could be found wondering through the locker rooms backstage at NWA events, and even found himself on the road quite often. Shane was an average student in school, but his athletic prowess was well documented. Showing a passion for baseball and wrestling, Shane was involved in many sports during his childhood, and eventually moved back to California with his mother after his parents divorced in 1992. The Crockett namesake slowly faded from memory, as Shane quickly became one of the top High School baseball prospects in California.
