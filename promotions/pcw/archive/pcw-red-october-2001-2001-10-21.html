Red October 2001 21 Oct 2001 Moscow, Russia A doctor's office and a bone marrow match The scene fades in from black to reveal the Queen of PCW, Brandi Wilson, once again waiting alone in what appears to be a doctor’s office. Brandi shifts uncomfortably around in her chair attempting to become comfortable, but neither the design of the cold leather chair nor the recent series of events will allow the young lady to gain any measure of comfort. Just when one could make the mistake of thinking that some intern in the PCW production truck popped in the show opening from the past week’s Tuesday Turmoil or that this is a rare instance of déjà vu, those illusions are quickly dispelled as the camera strafes the coffee table in the waiting room, to reveal Russian language fashion magazines strewn across the tabletop. Brandi’s shaking hand slowly reaches out grabs one of the magazines. The once lovely Brandi sighs nervously as she pages through the magazine looking at the photos, but it is all to clear her mind is not on the women or clothing shown in the magazine, but halfway across the room to the pediatric intensive care unit in Los Angeles, where her gravely ill infant son continues to struggle for life. Spying a glass of water sitting among the magazines, Brandi’s hand shoots forth and shakily brings the glass to her lips. Suddenly, a side door to the room springs open, prompting a gasp of surprise from Brandi, who accidentally spills her water in the process. The camera pans up as a black haired man with a thick goatee and mustache, wearing a white doctors coat steps through the doorway. Brandi does not look up at the doctor as she futilely tries out of sheer nervousness to mop up the water she spilled with a single, tissue. Doctor: Brandi, never mind the water. I will send in an orderly to clean it up. I am Dr. Potrenko. Brandi continues to use her now deteriorated tissue to attempt to clean the water, not even looking up at the doctor. Doctor Potrenko: Brandi, I have good news. Breaking out of her trance, Brandi looks up at the doctor and for the first time in recent memory a smile coupled with a look of hope crosses her tired features. Brandi: Have you found a bone marrow match for Caleb. Potrenko smiles widely, then approaches Brandi. He leans down, grabs her by her hands, halting her rapid but failed attempt to soak up the water she spilled. Potrenko then pulls Brandi up. Portenko: Yes, we have found a match. Brandi lets out a scream of relief and excitement, and leaps into the doctor’s arms, hugging him tightly. After a few moments of joyful crying, Brandi releases her grasp of Dr. Potrenko. Brandi: Who is the match, Doctor? Who is my baby’s father? A look of concern crosses Dr. Potrenko’s face and his smile fades away, as beads of sweat begin quickly dotting his forhead. Brandi: Doctor? The camera pans in on the troubled look on face of Dr. Potrenko for a brief moment, before shifting back to the inquisitive eyes of Brandi Wilson…..as the scene fades to black.
