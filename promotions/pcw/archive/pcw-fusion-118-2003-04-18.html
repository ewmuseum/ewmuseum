Fusion 118 18 Apr 2003 The Allen County War Memorial Coliseum, Fort Wayne, Indiana Semore Smoke's release from custody The scene opens to what seems to be a local jail location. In fact, for those of you have seen the Semore Smoke promos, it’s the same jail where Smoke was being held for his charges. The “Recorded Earlier” logo soon appears, as the scene fades into the front part of the jail, where you see Semore Smoke walking through the door, un-cuffed and looking confident… Semore Smoke: Who did you say bailed me out? The person behind the counter looks up at Smoke as he steps into the room and the person behind the counter scrambles to get the release forms ready for Smoke to sign… Man: Just some stranger, said he wanted to stay anonymous. Smoke nods his head as he signs his signature on a form that was set in front of him… Semore Smoke: Could have been AJ Trinidad, that asshole would even bail me out if it meant a chance at getting his poor precious kangaroo back! Ha! Smoke breaks out into a fit of laughter. He finishes signing everything the man handed to him and turns around to walk out the door. He opens the doors to the outside and smells the air… Semore Smoke: Ah! The smell of freedom… with a bit of that victory cream on top! Smoke walks through the door and slams it shut behind him…as we cut into the usual Fusion opening. Black and white stills open the show, then our lovely translucent lightning strike occurs, which sends us into real-time full-color highlight reels, showing the past week in PCW action. Highlighted on the reel are Adam Stryker, El Romantico, Jeremy Howard, and DarkStar. “48 HOURS FROM SPRING FLING IS WHERE YOU’LL FIND US, FANS!! Now having gone through everything in the normal Fusion routine, including Dave Kern’s stupid opening comment to fully commence the event, the cameras open inside the arena, where the opening pyro is just finishing off. The camera heads right down to the announce table, where Dave and Jeff Marx are both sitting dutifully…well Dave is, while Jeff picks his nose. Right after March Madness last year, I had a chance to go after Kid Wonder and put that self righteous, egotistical son of a bitch in his place! Jeremy pauses, letting his words sink in. Jeff: Um, no. Jeremy Howard: Of course, it took Nelson firing me to save you. But, how did it feel when I ruined your celebration in Hubbardville? How did it feel tasting the sweet pain of a Krayzie Death Slam in front of all of your pathetic followers? HOW DID IT FUCKING FEEL!?!?!
