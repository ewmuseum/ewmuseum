Darkness Continued! — PCW in-card segment

March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Darkness Continued! Written: March 2002 [We cut backstage to El Diablo standing against his locker room door with his eyes staring directly into the camera.] El Diablo: It's like it's the end of the world, only it's just you and me left, we are at opposite locations unlike that fateful night. What you did to me was unforgiveable, it was like stabbing me in the heart, only I couldn't die, I wanted to, I really wanted to but no blood would stem, and the blood of my pain is still on your hands, and that blood frrightens me. [El Diablo slowly slides his back down the door so that he is now seated with his knees opposite his chin.] El Diablo: It is an abyss of all I ever believed in, sucked out from me and still you don't give a damn. It's lead into my heart and into my head, but I can't die, I can't give you that pleasure, I won't give you the pleasure knowing that you are responsible for this demise. It disgusts me at how you continue like nothing happened, and like you don't care, maybe you forgot. Jeff: What is he talking about? Dave: This man is sick. Jeff: WHAT IS HE TALKING ABOUT? SMACK! Duke: Don't yell when ur sitting next to me, hoss. [El Diablo starts running his hands through his hair.] El Diablo: Illuminati, the all seeing eye, I never forgot, it's brooded within me for oh so long, and now it's time to unleash these feelings of hatred towards you, just like you rejected and hated me, it's time for me to draw you near, my lamb, then reject you and show the hate that you once showed me. A thousand lonely suicides will not cure anything, my friend, you will see the end, I promise. [El Diablo stands up and switches the locker room lights off, leaving the room in darkness.] El Diablo: Shadows of abyss, heathens of my soul. It frightens me to know I can feel like this, that I can harbor this much hatred for one human being within me, it's enough to cause nightmares, mind nullyfying nightmares. [El Diablo shrieks loudly and kicks the door like he is being tortured.] El Diablo: Wake me, oh please wake me. [He shrieks again letting it be known of his torture. And continues to talk like a voice in pain.] El Diablo: It's you in my nightmares, it's you that is giving me all these restless nights, that's why I need to cease this heartbeat, I need you away from me. [He shrieks once more and only the outline of his body can be seen due to the lights being out.] El Diablo: GET AWAY! [He shrieks again and a loud thud is heard as he drops again to the floor.] El Diablo: It blinds my sight, it kills a little bit more of my soul each time you come near, I need you gone and nullified, just go, get away, this hatred hurts too much. [The lights switch on and El Diablo has blood stemming from his arms as the scene fades out.] View El Diablo 's Biography
