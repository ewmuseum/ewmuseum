Silvio Rochelli Biography Real Name Silvio Rochelli Nicknames None Hometown New York City, New York Birthday September 20th, 1964 Height 6' 4' Weight 278lbs lbs Biography Silvio Rochelli's history is not a well-documented, as he often chooses to conceal past events of himself. This is to keep from being blackmailed by malicious businessmen who wish to use his past against him in order to achieve an objective they may have. What has been found out over time is that Rochelli was born in Naples, Italy. His father was not present for his birth as he stayed in New York, working to increase his hundreds of thousands of dollars into millions and becoming the epitome of the businessman. But, his mother, staying close to family, chose to fly to her hometown of Naples and Silvio was born surrounded by relatives of the Rochelli family. Two weeks after his birth, his mother and the young baby Silvio returned to New York and finally united him with his father. He was schooled in New York at a very expensive private school but one of which the name may never be known. Along the way, while Rochelli grew up, his father constantly reminded him that the empire of millions would one day be his. And as he grew up, Silvio found himself educated in the basic skills taught in grade school and taught how to maintain the business in which his father ran, as he was to inherit it upon his father passing on. At the age of 19, Silvio met a cute girl with brown hair by the name of Abrielle Graziano. They married after only 5 months of dating. At the age of 20, the fateful day came when Silvio's father passed away and the 'torch' was passed. At first it was rough but being well-educated, Silvio quickly grabbed ahold of the reins and piloted the plane like a well-trained veteran. His mother died when he was 24. On the eve of thier 4th wedding anniversary, the young couple of Abrielle and Silvio Rochelli (now 23 years old), were surprised by doctor's information that Abrielle was finally pregnant. After 9 months of waiting, Abrielle gave birth to a little miracle whom they named Geena. Geena is currently 17 years old. Silvio Rochelli is now 40. Surprisingly, this is all the information that has been gathered from his past thus far..
