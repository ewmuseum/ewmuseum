Hanami 'The Ahtumoth' Aneyuge Biography Real Name Hanami Aneyuge Nicknames The Ahtumoth Hometown Unknown Birthday Unknown Height 6'0' Weight 226 lbs Biography Appearance A completely black mask covers the Ahtumoth’s face and we can see a small part of his blonde hair come out at the top. From what we can see on his face at the uncovered parts of the mask he is very fresh faced and is in good condition because he is still relatively young. On the top half of his body he wears a completely black shirt, which covers everything except his hands, which are wearing thick black gloves. His bottom half is nearly exactly the same as he wears pitch black tights and black boots over the top of them which reach up to his black kneepads which are visible. History Although the Ahtumoth's birthplace is unknown, it is known that he grew up in Cairo in Egypt where his father kept him. His mother died during his birth and his father never moved on since that. His father was one of the top scientific researchers in the area looking for lost artefacts of Egypt. After coming across a very rare discovery one day his father made enough money, which allowed him to retire his job and look after his son. The discovery was an underground tomb of an unknown pharaoh. Inside the tomb where all his possessions including gold and silver jewellery, statues and exotic jewels. One piece caught his eye though, which was a small cup. It was only made of a single piece of wood and had been carved excellently. At the age of 12 Hanami received the cup as a birthday present and he set out to break the code, which had been encrypted, on the bottom of it. After years of research Hanami finally broke the code. The message simply stated, 'Find the second cup and you will be complete'. As Hanami read this his eyes lit up. He knew from that point on that he had to find the second cup. He was hit by a set back though when his father died and his research company was dragged into the ground by its opposition. Hanami was left with no family and no home. His friends were still there with him through his time of trouble. He made a promise to himself that he would find the second cup in honour of his father and take down the opposition that killed his company. On his first expedition he went with his friends to the tomb of Calutil. When they arrived the rival company who had somehow found out about the second cup attacked them. That is when Hanami decided that he would take no risks anymore and become as stealth like as he could. He covered his face and all his flesh and became 'invisible' as he called it. With money low Hanami decided that he would need something to build up some wealth. After watching a local wrestling program at one of his friend’s homes it was his intention to become a professional wrestler. Though all his friends were also low on funds they put all there money together to allow Hanami, now known as The Ahtumoth, to travel to the United States where he could try and make a name for himself in the world of Pro Wrestling. After getting to America he found residence in Tampa Bay, Florida with a man who knew his father, David Tellor. Hanami checked out websites on the Internet for martial arts. After finding clubs with styles in Ju-Jitsu and Karate he joined and soon become one of the most impressive fighters in the state of Florida taking the State Championship in Ju-Jitsu. Till this day he has been searching for a federation. Could this be The Ahtumoth's crowning moment?
