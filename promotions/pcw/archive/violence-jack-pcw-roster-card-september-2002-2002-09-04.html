Violence Jack Real Name: Bruce Shanahan Birthdate: August 8, 1971 Hometown: Boston, Massachusetts Height: 5'11 Weight: 233 lbs. Nicknames: VJ; V-Jack; The God of Extreme; Boston's Bastard Son; Baron of Brutality Status: Heel Entrance Music: 'The Truth Hurts' by Pro-Pain Information Wrestling Style: Hybrid of hardcore and brawling, with some technical expertise evident. Commands a very direct and relentless style of street fighting and 'hardcore' utilization of weapons and dirty tactics, but has been known to utilize strategic methods as the situation warrants (ground high flyers, take out legs of big men, etc) and usually also aims to weaken the neck of his opponents in preparation for either of his finishing techniques. He possesses an almost inhuman stamina and can endure insane amounts of punishment, and often uses this 'talent' as a perpetual Rope-A-Dope to exhaust his opponents before putting them away. He's the proud type that will pass out before ever submitting, regardless of the consequences. Set Up Move: N/A (VJ has no typical set-up move, per se; his finishers all tend to hit spontaneously from virtually nowhere, and rarely are preceded by a particular setup maneuver. In the rare instance he uses the Carlisle Legacy he tends to follow it directly with either a Detonator variation or the Hi-Jack Brainbuster, but other than that has no normal setup. ) Finisher: Detonator; Hi-Jack Brain Buster; Jack in the Box ( 1. Detonator - Hangman's Neck Breaker that lands more like a reverse Stunner, impacting the back of the victim's head off VJ's shoulder while at the same time whip-lashing their neck. VJ, over time, has developed a barrage of variations to make the maneuver executable from essentially any situation and he has labeled each of these variations with 'code names', as follows. a) Detonator Omega - Lifts opponent into a vertical suplex. Drops opponent down behind him in a suplex, keeping them semi-horizontal as they fall and landing the Detonator (combination suplex/Detonator). Also has been performed as a reverse Osaka Stunner where opponent lands on their feet, immediately brought down in the Detonator. b) Detonator Beta - Lifts opponent onto his shoulders in an inverted fireman's carry (his opponent facing skyward). VJ twists around, pushing the victim's body so that it swings out straight behind his own body. VJ drops the opponent into the Detonator. c) Detonator Delta - Sits on top rope, locks standing opponent in a front face lock as if for a Tornado DDT. Instead he flips over his foe, landing in the Detonator. Resembles a reverse Diamond Dust. d) Detonator Gamma - Lifts opponent into a crucifix powerbomb, then drops them so they plummet straight down and into the Detonator Detonator Charlie - Standing behind his opponent and near the ropes, VJ locks them in the reverse 3/4 bulldog position. He then springboards his feet off the ropes, twists around and drops his opponent in a Detonator. This version resembles the Stratusfaction popularized by Trish stratus, except performed as a springboard hangman's neckbreaker variation rather than a springboard bulldog. 2. Hi-Jack Brainbuster - VJ faces a stunned or wary opponent, twisting the arm and ducking behind them. He then hoists them into the air, opponent facing skyward across his shoulders, and falls to his side dropping them directly on their head or their head/neck. AKA: Judgement Slam 3. Jack in the Box - Scissors and traps the near arm of a prone opponent laying on their stomach, then uses both hands to lock on a chinlock and pulls back for submission. AKA: Crippler Crossface. NOTE: He rarely uses this move anymore, primarily only in submission matches.)
