Syko the Goblin Biography Real Name Well, if he had one, don't you think I'd use this space legitimately? Nicknames The Goblin, jobber, janitor hunter, Duck Falconer, 'PULL!', and 'GET OUT, YOU SICK FREAK!!' Hometown Dirty trashcan found behind a dumpster, located in the heart of... Birthday He just turned 20. Not that theres any proof, or birth certificate to prove it. Height 5' Weight 129 lbs Biography Deep in space... Where no man can hear you scream... There is only one... One man... Who can save the universe... FROM STUPIDITY!! Okay, so that isn't his origin, and he is the epitimy of stupidity. Let's face the facts, and THEY CLEARLY STATE that Syko grew up in the world as a rejected bum, not really caring about anything. Not wanting to allow himself to sucumb to the cowardous heathens that purged the world around him, he moved from ally to ally, refusing to perish. Falling deep into the hole that was his life, he spent up until the age of... 11 rotting to nothing. At age eleven, he saw something he had never seen before... Something... LIKE NOTHING HE'D EVER SEEN BEFORE!! Wrestling programs! Racing to the nearest indy fed he could find, he begged the owner for a job, and got one! He became an eleven year old hot dog vender... A MASKED ONE! Many failed run-in assaults on the workers of this fed resulted in his termination, but he trained hard, and got into more feds... He was small now, but soon he would grow. GROW LIKE NEVER BEFORE! He'd get stronger... better... he'd.... unforutionatly, the servere training at that age ruined his growing bones, thus stunting his growth eternally. Soon after, someone called him a Goblin and it stuck. Thus, he is a vengeful lad, and the rest is history! Other Feds EHWF, WfnW, OCWF Other Fed Awards Nothing. Quotes Enter the Goblin!
