Pharaoh Biography Real Name Titu 'Horus' Xansiv (Not yet legally changed to include 'Horus') Nicknames The Egyptian Madman, King Tit (by enemies) Hometown Cairo, Egypt. Birthday 4th of April 1975 Height 6, 2 Weight 229lb lbs Biography Titu Xansiv grew up in a hostel near Cairo. He was told at an early age that his parents were dead, and was never given any sort of explanation through which they died. For whatever reason, he showed little emotion, and went about his young life just like the rest of the children at the inn, never complaining, and never showing interest in anything outside of the orphanage. As the years went by, he was labelled as one of the more intelligent teens at the orphanage and picked up the language of English with flying colours. He was commemorated on many aspects of learning and enjoyed to know he was much more gifted than the others about him. At the age of 15, Titu strolled into the small and fairly poor library the place had to offer and took an immediate interest in ancient history, or more to the point, in the history of the ancient Egyptians. He looked on through his motherland’s history and took a great curiosity in their beliefs and ritual. At the age of 17, the people who looked after the place began to worry about Titu’s mentality. For almost a year he had prayed to Gods and deities of his county’s past and couldn’t seem to let it go of the ritual he had become so enrolled in. He had become so obsessed with Egypt that his grasp of reality was slowly slipping away. It wasn’t long before professional help was brought in from America. Doctors and specialists in the field of physiology attempted to bring the boy’s sanity back, but failed miserably. The only thing these men and women managed to do was spark a new interest in Titu. One of the methods they had used to try and bring him around was for him to watch television, something Titu had rarely seen before. The doctors believed if they could divert his attention elsewhere, his condition would gradually decrease and one of the programmes they showed him on a constant basis was wrestling, possibly America’s greatest sport. After the docs had left, the youthful ‘prince of Egypt’ as he began called himself, embarked on challenging the other boys at the inn to wrestling matches. The people he offered on, however, were much bigger than him, and slowly but surely Xansiv began to devise ways through which he could pick apart the larger foe’s anatomy until they were beaten. At the age of 20, Titu left the place he had learnt to call home for years and travelled into the heart of Cairo. He sought out a job as a waiter at a small, scruffy café near the Nile’s banks and made what money he could. Once he had enough cash, and this took him almost 3 years, he left Egypt to go to America. His interest in wrestling was not faltered through this time and upon arrival in the states he snapped up an application form for an academy in pro wrestling named PWA. There he learnt how to wrestle, cut a promo, and work the crowd, and build an on-air past for himself. PWA described the young Titu Xansiv as an ancient Egyptian King who had travelled through time to wrestle in the PTC, the company funding the academy. Not knowing of ‘The Pharaoh’s’ weak grasp on all things real, PWA only helped to further push Titu’s mental state into oblivion and soon he considered himself the real deal, a genuine Pharaoh from ancient Egypt. Although PWA did this to the young man, the Primetime Wrestling Academy was an invaluable help to Xansiv and before long he was off on his way to a development federation called NTCIW. In NTICW, Xansiv developed his in-ring persona even more so, and was billed to feud with Xavier Kannon, the organization’s biggest draw. Within a few months the federation closed its doors and Xansiv made his way to PCW, the supreme king of all wrestling promotions... It has been almost two years since Titu Xansiv first stepped foot in PWA, and since then he has lived quite the brilliant career. Along the way he has picked up the PCW tag team titles with Akira Akiyama, and the PCW TV title. And good things continue to come to Xansiv as he continues to stalk PCW, the fans backing him all the way... Other Feds NTICW, PWA, PURE.
