Miss Moonlight Biography Real Name Confidential, to protect her alter ego Nicknames Goes by Tsukiko-chan...though it's no indication to her real name whatsoever!, Missy Moony (cutie nickname), The Moonlight, the Mistress of the Moon Hometown Tokyo, Japan Birthday 1984 / 02/ 29 Height (177.5cm) 5'10 Weight (75kg) 200 lbs Biography Gimmick Breakdown: Miss Moonlight is a college student by day, heroine by night, pro wrestler by free time(how about that for ambition?). Miss Moonlight - AKA Naomi Kawaii, was born surprisingly February 29, 1984 in Kamakura Japan to Kibino and Manabu Kawaii. Although wanting a son, Manabu still loved his daughter very much and treated her as if she were the son he never head. Manabu was a police officer, and night after night he would come home and tell his little girl stories about his street work. From an early age Naomi was influenced by the neverending battle of good and evil. Her father began teaching her police grade Karate and Judo for self defense at an early age, and by the age of 9 she was alreadya pro. By 12 she got her black belts from accredited schools in the area. While all this was going on her mother managed to balance out her tomboyish tendancies with good old fashioned feminism. Naomi was a beautiful budding young woman, and her mother was determined to groom her to be a very fit bride. After graduating from high school, Naomi got accepted into Tokyo University and moved to the big city. By graduation, all of her physical activities with judo, karate, kendo, weightlifting, and amateur wrestling (after school activities to beef up college applications) left Naomi at a sculpted size of 200 pounds lean; but her mother's inherited feminine graces and grooming made her a stunning beauty. Living in Tokyo, she got a first hand look at the crime that plagued the fair city. Driven by her childhood installment of heroism from her father, while her own personal quips about protecting the innocent and her friends, she dons the mask and uniform to combat crime as....MISS MOONLIGHT! Miss Moonlight Uniform: Black with white trim; a black bandana style mask, a tie around mask that covers the upper portion of her face, ending at the bridge of her nose. Below that she wears a black skirt with black panties with crescent moons on them, black knee and elbow pads, black heeled boots reaching mid calf, and a black sleeveless shirt with a slot cut Other Feds TWL Other Fed Awards TWL Women's Champion, TWL Jr Heavyweight Champion, winner of the Starlight Festival Tournament.
