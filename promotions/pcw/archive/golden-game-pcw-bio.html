Golden Game Biography Biography Golden Game started off in PWA 7-8 months ago. That's where Kevin Klaymore and Juan Borr first met. They then went to EW, where they were roommates. The men began a friendship, and worked together in the ring. It was the typical big guy, small guys team. The two formed a tag team from there. They didn't have any tag team matchups in EW, but when it sloced and they moved to NTICW, the history began. In NTICW Golden Game main evented, and won the tag team titles in there first match. They did it in dramatic fashion in a tag team TLC match. The men defended there belts and retired them when NTICW Closed. The two men recieved the call to move up to PCW. When in PCW, they won a few matches and lost a few matches. Meanwhile both men had single carreers. The men put them aside and concentrated on Golden Game. That's when they won the PCW Tag Title from Freeze and Hirose. They then defended it for a respectable time until they lost it to AFL-CIO. The two men split up and went back to singles wrestling for awhile. Juan won the cruiserweight title, and after that reign International Incident began. Kevin decided to take some time off, and Golden Game has been on hold. Just a few weeks ago the team united once again. Golden Game is back and ready to lead the Tag Team Title Division to it's peak and become 2 time Tag Champions.
