The Schmoopies Biography Fed Titles 2x PCW Tag Champions Biography Tessa and Chris first met back in WIW:NG. Their first personal experience with eachother was not all too good for Tessa being that she was driven through a table by Chris because of Chris' extreme hate for Erik Erratic and Tessa's alliance with him. As time went on though Chris found himself developing a weakness for Tessa after teaming with her to face DWO's Terry Woods and Kristi Kiss is a grueling match inside the asylum. After losing Chris decided to get a little revenge not on Kristi and Terry but on Tessa for 'being the weaker link'. In doing this he convinced Tessa that he was only out there to congradulate her for her hard work but instead drove her head into a cake and then raising a steel chair high above her head ready to strike at any moment. But finding feelings for Tessa emerging he couldn't bring himself to strike her and instead left. These feelings continued on for several weeks until WIW's Pay Per View Retribution rolled around and cemented the relationship between Chris and Tessa after Chris had helped Tessa gain the Intercontinental Championship and Tessa had helped Chris defend his World Title succesfully by screwing Erik Erractic over. When WIW was forced to close in December Tessa and Chris made their way over to PCW where their relationship continued to grow. First with the couple adopting a child named Evan along with the children that were given to Tessa after the death of her brother, Christa and Alex. They moved to Tessa's house in Chatham MA, after Chris proposed to Tessa in January. Soon after the proposal Chris and Tessa found themselves some gold after defeating P.W.A. for the Tag Team Championships. They lost the championships a week later to P.W.A. after Nerezza swereved against the crowd and reunited with Gaz Green, leaving Chris beaten and Tessa ending up bitten by Nerezza. Tessa and Chris soon redeemed themselves though by regaining the Tag Team Titles for a second time after defeating Clyde and Darkstar.
