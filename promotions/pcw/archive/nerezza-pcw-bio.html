Nerezza Biography Real Name Nascosto Ireul Nerezza (formerly Matt Jay) Nicknames 'The Living Nightmare' Hometown Akihabara, Japan (Born in Wales, UK, turned into a vampire in Detroit, Michigan) Birthday 'Re-born' May 29th, 2002 (March 3rd, 1976) Height 6'7' Weight 350 lbs Biography In-Ring Attire: Simular to D-Lo Brown, black vest/tights combination with a huge electric purple 'N' on the front and 'Nerezza' going down the sides of the legs. His boots are also black with three buckles on the sides. He wears purple elbowpads and kneepads, with purple wrist tape. He wears his coat and sunglasses to the ring but removes them before the match. Out of ring attire: Sleeveless red ankle length coat, shiny and reflective (simular to Edge's style but with no sleeves). Black T-Shirt that usually has anime or hair metal bands on them. A piece of armour sits in his chest, shiny like plastic and purple in colour. It starts at the neck and covers to the solarplexus, then splits and covers the ribs. It's held in place by the orange diamond-shape shoulder pads on his coat. He also wears blue stonewash jeans, boots like his in-ring attire (like motorcycle boots with 3 buckles on the sides) and a leather belt with a silver custom buckle that has 'Nerezza' engraved into it. Purple fingerless gloves with orange wristbands and his trademark red circular 'Ozzy' glasses complete the outfit. Ring Entrance: Yoko Kanno's 'Eyeball' screeches over the PA system, usually making the recipients cover their ears as the song is little more than a noise of drumbeats and glassy scraping. The Jumbotron displays a grainy, low-quality image of Nerezza with his hair over his face amongst a fog, slowly walking towards the camera. As he reaches the camera, fog pours from both sides of the jumbotron and covers the stage below. When it clears, Nerezza emerges from it and crawls on all fours to the ring with his hair still covering his face. Did you know?: A few facts about Nerezza: A stake (or any other object) through the heart will disable him until it's pulled out. He casts a reflection, but he sees his vampire form in a mirror. Sunlight doesn't kill him, but blinds him for a few minutes. He hated garlic long before he was made a vampire. Silver causes him to itch slightly. He has wings, but won't show them through vanity. He dislikes heavy noise. He only needs to drink blood to keep his skin human-looking and to fuel his vampire form. He survives by feeding on negativity. Human sadness, anger, pain, etc. He can't read minds, only hear thoughts when he chooses. If someone thinks in a non-english language, he can't understand them. He can't hear something if the person isn't thinking it. Holy objects such as crosses, churches, holy water and so on have no effect on him, as he was agnostic in his former life. The rumour about holy items started on medieval times, when everyone was more religious. As Christianity faded, so did the strength these items possess. Seeing that neither Nerezza nor the person using the item have faith, it's powerless. A servant of God using one may have more effect. Nerezza has to breathe only to speak or smell. Choke-holds simply hurt his neck. His bite doesn't turn someone into a vampire. To do that, he has to first drink the victim's blood, where it mixes with his own. If the victim drinks this hybrid blood, they will turn into a vampire. They will have wings, but will need to feed on blood. The elements of hearing thoughts and feeding on negativity are the results of Nerezza drinking the blood of a different species of vampire back in his early PWA days and as such, are inherited. He can't pass this on to his offspring. Nerezza's second form is his true shape. All the extra body mass is kept as body fat. This gives him his chubby appearance. His arms reach down to his ankles in his vampire form, but he can extend them to twice their normal length in either shape. He prefers to do so in his vampire shape, seeing it gives him even more reach. Nerezza speaks a little japanese and is a heavy animé fan. Most of Nerezza's moves are named after metal & rock or videogame references. Nerezza stole his old scythe from the set of a cheap vampire movie. He was hired as a stuntman for it and stole the prop when he was underpaid. He now carries a metal one which he can take apart and fit into a small case. The original hangs on the wall of his home in Akihabara. Nerezza was born in Wales. He lived with another wrestler, Ethan Wright in Canada, then moved back to the UK to live in Reading after the collapse of his upstart wrestling promotion. He then moved to Detroit, Michigan to study under Hessian in PWA, lived with Gaz Green in Las Vegas for a while before finally getting a new home in October in Akihabara, Japan. Nerezza considers his greatest loss being from Matt Blitz in the 3rd Golden Turnbuckle Tournament, on the grounds of Matt being a holy man. Nerezza jokingly calls Hessian 'Father' or 'Daddy' on occasion. Nerezza has been mistaken for Ozzy Osbourne. Biography'My past is irrelevent. What you see before you now is the result of a childish fantasy, ironic, twisted by the hand of fate. I stand as testament to truth behind one of man's oldest myths. From the writings of a depraved novelist comes the proof that WE exist. As a child, and as a man, I believed so deep in their existance that I lived my life as one. Now, I live my unlife as one.' These are the exact words Nerezza quotes when he is asked about his life. The man born Matt Jay in the UK grew up training as a wrestler and suffered much ridicule for his gothic look and his choice of pastime. The money Matt made was spent on his appearance, from buying clothes, contact lenses and prosthetics to look like the object of his obsessions - a vampire. The trade paid off and soon, his look captured the attention of small wrestling promotions throughout the United Kingdom and eventually Canada. Furthermore, he was even given the opportunity to run a federation when the owner of the now defunct DWA retired. 'Nerezza', as he had chosen for his stage name, spent his entire life savings on the deal. His poor business skills forced him to sell the company on. After a brief hiatus, Nerezza then set his sights higher - Primetime. After enrolling in Primetime Wrestling Academy under his real name to hide his past, Matt Jay's shame grew and his concentration shattered. His enthusiasm was shortly rekindled by a kindred spirit in the shape of a man named Gaz Green, who shared the former Nerezza's love for causing mental anguish and confusion. The two mind-game warlords became friends and gradually, Nerezza's true spirit emerged once more. Then one night, the unthinkable happened. Nerezza was kissed by a girl. More precisely, bitten hard on the lip. Throwing his crazed companion aside, Nerezza retreated to his shared home with Gaz and overnight, he changed. No longer able to walk in sunlight without the protection of strong sunglasses and now possessing all kinds of new abilities, Nerezza was soon hunted down by the council that governed the vampires of Detroit. Taking orders and advice from the city's record keeper, Rab, he was slowly taught more and more about his kind. Eventually, he learned his most valuable lesson - the ability to change his body from the human shape into a huge hulking behemoth with arms that scrape the groud and immense strength. However, he was bound from publically showing his powers. Not conforming to his limits, Nerezza killed his mentor and declared himself excommunicated from the council's laws. Gaz and Nerezza were then cast out once more from NTICW after illegal use of weaponry. Shortly afterwards, they spoke with a man who promised them the confusion and mayhem they craved so much, whereby their next step was Primetime Championship Wrestling... Nerezza once had the ability to become a huge monster, but has since found that merging the two shapes together has been more beneficial for him. He also has a pet of sorts, a Chupacabra named Fluffy, who's information can be found in the Allies Section. Truly, he isn't human. Clearly, he isn't humane. Obviously, he is Nerezza.
