Akiyama — PCW roster card (November 2002)

Akiyama Real Name: Formerly Akira Akiyama; goes by Akiyama Birthdate: March 3, year undeclared Hometown: Rampaging through Japan Height: 6'10 Weight: 339 lbs. Nicknames: The Official GOD OF PCW, PCW's GOD OF TAG, God of Celestiology, Celestial Being, Neo Void God, Lord of the Aura, The Darkness Eternal, The Absolute Destiny Apocalypse, nVg Status: Heel Entrance Music: LONG ENTRANCE: A eerie vibration begins to reverb off the walls as darkness fills the arena… Godsmack’s “I STAND ALONE” rocks through the speakers, silver fireworks raining down from above onto the stage. At that point, the Neo Void God leaps out from under the stage while silver fireworks shoot up, landing in a crouched position with his hand slamming into the metal stage loudly. Cyclonica joins by his side, the two of them showered by silver sparks from above. Then the two Celestials walk down the ramp, Akiyama clenching and unclenching his fists. He leaps up onto the apron and leaps over the top rope, landing in another crouched position with his fists raised at his face. Cyclonica climbs in the ring and sits on the top turnbuckle with her legs crossed, glaring at the opponent or the crowd before leaning back and flipping over the top rope and landing on her feet on the outside. Akiyama then stands to his feet for battle. SHORT ENTRANCE: “I STAND ALONE” by Godsmack rocks the speakers as Akiyama leaps up onto the stage amidst silver fireworks shooting up from the ground. He lets out a roar before running to the ring, sliding in on his knees, slamming his palms into the mat and jumping to his feet as Cyclonica walks down behind him. Information Wrestling Style: Celestial Style, the style of the nVg devised by him to utilize his Celestial abilities as well as the mortal techniques of battle man have devised over the years, including Martial Arts mastery, Pit Fighting, Technical, Speed, Power, Brawling, Aerial, and Hardcore techniques. Taunts are irrelevant, and only the fight is necessary. Set Up Move: Set Up/ Secondary Finisher: HAND OF FATE© (Akiyama exhibits his amazing power and strength with a devastating Kung Fu Straight Punch to the mouth of his opponent. The strike seems almost unstoppable, going through anything and anyone to hit the desired target. The mere force of the blow has been known to deliver concussions, knock out teeth, shatter ribs, break bones, and damage vital organs. ) Finisher: =©= The Absolute Destiny Apocalypse (ADA) =©= (”Reverse DVD into single-arm Spike Jackknife”---Akiyama grabs the waistband of the opponent with left hand, his right hooking around and grabbing the throat. Then he lifts him onto his shoulders in a Reverse DVD position. The NVG then spins him off his shoulders forward, his hands still holding the waistband and letting go of the throat, and drives the head/neck/shoulder area into the mat with the imposing force of his single powerful arm. The impact on the neck and shoulders causes the victim to flop over onto their stomach out cold.) PCW Title History: 2 Time PCW Tag Team Champion (w/ Pharaoh, and then HIMSELF!), PCW Akivision Chamion (TV Champion), PCW Extreme Championship, PCW Cruiserweight Championship (Via The Divine Prophetess, Cyclonica's possession) PCW Award History: ONLY individual to possess the PCW Tag Team titles ALONE...making him THE GOD OF TAG Second Longest PCW Tag Team Title reign w/ Pharaoh (43-42 days) -Void Banishments: Official (Permanent): Itagaki Freeze Ketsueki Karasu Avalanche Gaijin Kenji Hirose Derek Casey Guy Leslie Random biker Ripper Ryckman Carmoon Lance Bell Drake Maxwell Sushi Crew (2 for the price of 1) Mr. Morozo (Sushi Crew manager) Unofficial (Not completely Permanent): Rush Chyna Stephanie McMahon Martha Stewart Pamela Anderson Kid Rock Eddie (dog from Frasier) Sir Authur Browberry (NTICW) PCW Hall of Famer Frisco The End Jay Sylo Other Federations: Tokyo Wrestling League Other Federation Awards: Multiple TWL Grand Champion, TWL Hardcore Champ, TWL Regional Champion, and winner of the '99 Voltage Fighter tournament.
