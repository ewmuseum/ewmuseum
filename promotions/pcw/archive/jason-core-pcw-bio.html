Jason Core Biography Biography <u><b>Description:</b></u> J.C. is a large man, by normal standing. His body is very well-defined...he's huge from years of athletic training. Definition like Goldberg, only hold the 'roids. His rugged good looks (definitely a handsome man) are framed by dark brown hair pulled back into a 4-6 inch ponytail. His eyes are dark (brown? green?) and are usually resolute, depressed, or a 2:1 mixture of the two. I'd go with the mixture. His face is much the same, expcept add two cups of wry humor, and a dash of anger/cynicality. As for his in-ring appearance, J-Core comes to ringside wearing his t-shirt of the week/month, whatever that may be. The shirt's gone by the time the match starts, it's thrown to a kid in the audience. Apart from that, he wears black tights (J-Core on the rear) that only go to mid-thigh, showcasing his tree-trunk legs. His knees are encased in black braces, covered in black kneepads. He also wears black elbowpads, and black boots. His wrists get wrapped with white tape, as do his individual fingers, aside from index and thumb. Outside of the ring, Jason dressed in what could be described as 'cheap preppy casual chic.' Flannel shirts, t-shirts, baseball-style shirts, blue jeans or track pants, sneakers, Vans, leather jacket...there, now you've got a mental picture. You're welcome. :) <u><b>T-Shirt Of The Week:</b></u> The official PCW Malice Crew t-shirt. Black t-shirt with the Malice Crew logo on the back, on the front, it has the determined faces of it's four members, and one of the Malice Crew credos: 'Down, but never out, hoss.' Pick one up today. <u><b>Ring Entrance:</b></u> The lights dim to half, as Jason's silhoutted profile fills the entrance tron-screen thingy. Music hits, and smoke billows out of the entrance (More like a haze of war, than a big poofy Edge cloud). On 'Resolute...' Jason emerges from the entrance to a large crowd pop (ideally, naturally). <u><b>History:</b></u> <b>-Taken from the <i>Centre Daily Times</i> from State College, PA</b> <font size='4'>Local Wrestler Begins Again</font> Most pro wrestlers don't get their 'start' at the age of 34, but then again...most pro wrestlers aren't Jason Core. Most of you remember Core as the phenom back in his days wrestling for Penn State. As a freestyle wrestler, he took home NCAA Division IA gold in both his junior and senior years ('88-'89, '89-90) and was one of the brightest prospects in a pro-wrestling world starting to realize amateur potential. Sadly, Jason's status as a 'can't miss' prospect, well...missed. After a few months on the amateur circuit, a vicious ACL tear, combined with the popping of his patella tendon in his left knee, caused him to take an early retirement from independent pro wrestling. After a year and a half of rehab and surgery, the fickle business of pro wrestling had passed him by... Jason was devastated, but he began to start over. He moved to the small town of River Plaza, New Jersey...and worked as a paralegal for a law firm down there. There, he met, fell in love with, and married Jenna Estep (Yeah, you heard me...). Jenna Core gave Jason a new lease on life, and he continued to rehabilitate, and was able to lead a normal life, as if no injury had ever hampered him. Unfortunately, life in an office did not suit Jason, and he was prone to mild fits of depression, despite his good situation. Something was missing in his life... In the summer of 1996, Jason suffered another terrible tragedy, as a drunk driver slammed into the side of his Camry, and fatally injured Jenna, and he mangled his right leg in the accident. Insurance took care of most of the fiscal damages, but Jason was left...simply...devastated, at the loss of his beloved wife. For a while, he sat in a cycle of depression. Finally, an old friend, and his family managed to pull him out of his sadness, and he began to live life anew. In 1997, with several loans, he opened up the Core Wrestling School (CWS) in Detroit, Michigan. Finally, Jason could have the professional part of his life to be what he truly wanted, in the business he loved. Jason trained some of the best and brightest of the 'Attitude' generation of sports entertainment...with a strong emphasis on promos as well as the physical aspect of wrestling. Strongly against 'spot wrestling' Jason's school was famous for showcasing strengths, and masking/healing weaknesses. The wrestlers that emerged from the school are some of the top stars in the field today. With the 'Attitude' generation, came some true 'Attitude.' Jason refused admittance to a certain student, as the school became more elite at the end of 2000. The angry student set fire to the CWS building in downtown Detroit. Once again, all Jason's hopes and dreams became ash. Jason returned to State College for a little while, but the familiar settings of Happy Valley stifled him. He packed up and moved out to Tallahassee, Florida...and moved in with a high-school friend and physical therapist. With his assistance, Jason decided to rehab his body, and prepare to achieve his lifelong dream, a career as a professional wrestler...for the purpose of gaining enough finances to rebuild his wrestling school, as well as to achieve his dream. He rehabilitated for nine months, until he was in 'performance' condition again, then toured with a few independent federations, quickly picking up a head of steam with a combination of natural physical charisma, and excellent microphone skill. Yesterday, Jason was finally signed to a conditional PCW contract, fully entering him into the 'big time'. He has had four major knee surgeries, a fused vertebrae, and several broken bones and concussions over his amateur and professional wrestling career. Yet none of these have prevented him from making the jump into the career he's always wanted. Jason's a man filled with hope, determination, and desire...and nothing in this world can stop him now... <u><b>The Core Wrestling School Students</b></u> -<i>Kyle 'Velocity' Lamen</i> <font size='1'>(Former PCW Cruiserweight Champion/CCW Independent Champion; 20 years old, injured, and a prodigy of sorts.
