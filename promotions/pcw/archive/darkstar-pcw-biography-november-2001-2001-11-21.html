The Jobber is lifted up so they are now upside down, DarkStar suspends jobberboy by keeping their arms trapped. DarkStar holds their arms and grabs their legs. He then sits down, dropping the jobber on their head/back of the neck.] Special High [DarkStar stands face to face with the victim and grabs the victim's head and places it under their arm. He throws his body out backwards and drops to the mat on their stomach, driving the victim face first to the mat.] Static Cling [DarkStar lifts the opponent up over his head. DarkStar pivots and slams the opponent down back first into the mat.] Tearchaser [DarkStar stands face to face with his opponent. The opponents arms are crossed under their chest, DarkStar lifts the victim up so they are horizontal to the mat. He then sits down and drops the opponent back first to the mat while still holding their arms. ] Lollirot [The opponent is picked up for a body slam but then is dropped abruptly on his head. DarkStar then hooks the victim's tights and lifts them in the air as so they are upside down, similar to an inverted facelock suplex. DarkStar then lets the victim fall forward and across DarkStar's body. DarkStar kneels down so the victim lands back first across their knee.] Disfiguration [DarkStar stands behind the victim. DarkStar reaches under one of the victim's arms with one hand and places it behind the victim's neck. DarkStar charges forward and drives the Victim face first into the mat.] Crossbearer [DarkStar applies standing head scissors and grabs the victim around the waist. He lifts the victim up so they are sitting on his shoulders, facing the opposite direction of the attacker. The attacker pushes the victim fowards slightly so they fall straight down off the attackers shoulders but not down as if in a powerbomb, straight down as if to their feet or stomach. As the victim is falling to the mat, DarkStar applies 3/4 face lock (basically, the victim's head on the attacker's shoulder withthe attacker forming a headlock around the top of their head as DarkStar has his back to the victim) and drops to the mat, impacting the victim's chin into his shoulder.] Fadeaway [DarkStar bends his opponent over or applies a standing head scissors and grabs the victim around the waist. DarkStar lifts him up so they are over his shoulder. From there, he places both his arms under the arms of his opponent and extends their arms out as far as they can. DarkStar pulls down on the victim's arms/upper body, flipping the victim's body over so they are falling face first towards the mat. On the way down, the attacker hooks the victim's head in a front face lock and falls to the mat, driving the victim face/head first into the mat.] High Flying Sacrifice Theory [DarkStar, insanely I might remind you, does a corkscrew suicida with a backflip onto his opponent, he has no problems with doing this from high distances] Crystal Method [DarkStar climbs to the top rope, holding his opponents head in a inverted face lock, he spins around, outside of the ring, pulling the opponents neck across the top rope backwards, can be done during most high spots] Thorned Halo [DarkStar springboards off an open chair and huracanranas the opponent head first into the open chair that he jumped off.] The Prayer Wheel [This move is generally executed near the ropes when an opponent is recovering from a previous blow. When the opponent is getting up, DarkStar vaults to the 2nd rope and propels himself backwards simultaneously hooking the opponents head dropping into a DDT.] Martyr Complex [No matter where in the ring or outside of the ring, DarkStar pounces on his opponent, doing a full backflip in mid air before landing on his opponent in a horizontal bodypress] Springboard Suicida [DarkStar vaults to the middle of the top rope and front somersaults onto an opponent on the floor. Has been known to get a great deal of height and cover large distances with this move.] Target Audience [DarkStar runs the length of the top rope to the turnbuckle, when his opponent regains composure he leaps, spinning his opponents body around into a facebuster] Ny-Quil [DarkStar goes to the top rope as his opponent is lying on the ground, he jumps off doing a frogsplash like tuck, but then at the last second turns it into a Swanton Bomb] Letter to God [DarkStar climbs to the top rope or any other high perch and leaps, spinning his body in a corkscrew, normally this isnt impressive but the rotation DarkStar gets can reach up to 1080 degrees or more, he then lands on his opponent] Submission Stretch Plum [Used as a tribute to Reckless Youth, the man that taught DarkStar everything, this is his primary Submssion finsiher, DarkStar stands behind the opponent. He reaches around the opponents body with one leg so it is around their side and between their legs, hooking the leg on the same side as DarkStars leg. DarkStar forces the opponent down into a sitting position, with the attacker's leg still positioned around the opponents body and inbetween their legs and applies an inverted face lock. From there, DarkStar twists in the direction opposite their leg.] My Plague [The Opponent is on their stomach. DarkStar stands on the back of their knees and locks their ankles behind his legs. DarkStar grabs both their arms, pulls back, sits down and rolls onto his back, lifting the opponent off the ground so they are facing upwards. Tuesday Turmoil: 08-07-01 with Clyde vs Mike Marvelous and Genetic Perfection Johnny Smith Winner: DarkStar and Clyde PCW New Dawning: 08-19-01 (TV Title match) vs Mike Marvelous (Champion) Winner: DarkStar won the TV Title Friday Fusion: 08-25-01 (TV Title Defense) vs Big Alan Steele Winner: DarkStar Friday Fusion: 08-31-01 with Harle Quinn vs Kid Wonder and Amanda, Jack Valentine and Mrs Valentine, Dan Hollywood and Buffy Vegas Winner: Dan Hollywood and Buffy Vegas after Buffy pinned Amanda Sunday Sabotage: 09-03-01 (TV Title Defense) vs Jeremy Johnson Winner: DarkStar Friday Fusion: 09-07-01 (Handicap Match) with Stryfe vs Big Alan Steele Winner: Big Alan Steele pinned Stryfe Sunday Sabotage: 09-10-01 with Stryfe vs Big Alan Steele and Rent a Hero Winner: DarkStar and Stryfe PCW Back to Old School: 09-16-01 (Briar Patch Match for TV Title) vs Big Alan Steele Winner: Big Alan Steele won the Title Tuesday Turmoil: 09-26-01 (Tag Team Title Tournament Match) with Stryfe vs AJ and Jay Storm Winner: DarkStar and Stryfe Tuesday Turmoil: 10-04-01 (Tag Team Title Round Two) With Stryfe vs Da Westsidaz Winner: Da Westsidaz Protagonists: Clyde, Vampir Nosferatu, Tyler Nelson, Kremmen Antagonists: Chris Chase, Big Alan Steele, Rent a Hero, Dan Hollywood, Kid Wonder History: Born in downtown Philadelphia as Gavyn Drazail, the Darkstar as we know him was never suspected. Drazail was a very normal child... up til a point. 1987 - 6 Years Old Drazail went to see his first wrestling match, a WWF Event in Philadelphia, beginning at that point he began practicing moves against his then 14 year old brother, sadly for his brother, Drazail beat his ass everytime Rumor has it, DarkStar perfected the Pressure Drop on his brother, almost ending his football career. 1992 - 11 Years Old The normal child Gavyn Drazail began changing, his father was a heavy alcoholic and a sposal abuser, the emotional scaring Gavyn went through during his first 11 years created another 'entity' in his mind, he felt that the only way he could fight back against his father and the various tragedys in his life was to repress it, this brought out the Darkstar... [Side Note: Darkstar - at 11 he was sent to a psychiatrist and never spoke, he then overheard his doctor say to his parents 'He is not crazy, nor is he depressed, he just has a a little dark star inside him that has the need to claw its way to the top, its nothing serious and he should grow out of the stage in less then a year'] 1993 - 13 Years Old Upon his graduation of eight grade, Drazail ran away from home for the first time, citing abuse from his father and mental abuse from his mother, Drazail went to a local wrestling school, Pennsylvania Wrestling Association and Wrestling School, and met with the few wrestlers on the card, after showing his honed moves they mentioned that he should look into wrestling, shortly there after his parents found them and removed him from the area 1994 - 14 Years Old Drazails father dies, this is the final wedge droven between him and his mother, through the years emotions no longer ran on the scarred psychie of Drazail, and he could show no saddened emotion for the passing of his father, after what he was put through, there was no reason for him to. After his mother noticed this, the final fuse was lit and Drazail turned towards the darkside... he embraced the gothic sub culture, latching on to acts such as Marilyn Manson and Bauhaus, he regressed deeply til his alter Darkstar persona was taking a head. 1995 - 15 Years Old His band, Angelus Errae [latin for 'when angels lose their way'] recieves what can only be called a break, being signed to NG Records, a young indie recording label, although this was short lived Gavyn met a main influence in his life... Talena. Talena at the time was in a band called Frequency.08, though the band was short lived [much like Angelus Errae was] The two hooked up and shared a unmatched love for wrestling 1996 - 16 Years Old The straw that broke Drazails back was finally laid down, in a rage of frustration he packed his things and left, leaving his mother behind in Philadelphia, he took Talena with him for the travels, not knowing where it would take him, Gavin tagged along as Talenas new band, did a brief tour of the east coast, and at some point... his travel brought him to the New Dimension Wrestling School, just outside of Fayetteville, North Carolina Also, Drazail formed his new band, Dread Wait, in the months of touring. New Dimesnion Wrestling School [1996-1997] After wandering into the New Dimension Wrestling School in mid 96, him and Talena were hit with a overwhelming feeling of a second calling, Drazail quickly signed up and Talena signed on as his valet... during this time Drazail met his closest friend [and sometimes most bitter enemy] Aaron Rossdale (The Insane Kidd) 1997 - 17 Years Old Drazail began developing his alter ego, Darkstar, into a character, the character slowly took over, much like his new found friend Aaron Rossdales character the Insane Kidd took over, the two [now labelling themselves as teh 'Insane Asylum'] began marketing themselves to the various wrestling federations, quickly graduating from the New Dimension and going onto ECW together Extreme Championship Wrestling [1998] 1998 - 18 Years Old The Insane Assylum quickly drew a huge following, jumping from the Indys to ECW in less then a year, this would be one of Darkstars three stays in ECW, and Insane Kidds only stay before moving where the grass was greener Very quickly however, the pair drew the Tag Team Titles away from the Dragons in a match that propelled both teams careers. Shortly thereafter Insane Kidd and Darkstar parted ways for greener pasteurs Global Proffessional Wrestling [1999] Darkstar made a stay in the independent GPW before its closing in the same year, in the four months there Darkstar dominated the league, winning the TV Title before the closing Extreme Championship Wrestling\Canadian Wrestling Association [1999] Darkstar split time in the ECW and CWA in the last few months of 1999, he reached even more independant glory by crossing over the CWA Hvyweight title into ECW, defending it against mid carders and some high caliber talent before dropping the title after beating King Diamond and the Angel in a three way dance for the TV Title, after losing it to The Master he left the ECW. Four Twenty Alliance [2000] Extreme Fantasy Wrestling [2000] In 2000 Darkstar pulled double duty, he helped propel the fledgling FTA to new levels along with other talent in the federation, almost capturing the world title and holding both the TV and Hardcore titles for runs In the EFW however, he met up with the Insane Kidd and his new tag partner the Extremist [at the time the pair was High Incident] as he was double timing it Darkstar helped transfer some EFW talent to the FTA, it ended in a merger... the FTR Four Twenty Revolution [2000-2001] the FTR started with the Triple Threat of FTR [Extremist, Darkstar, Insane Kidd] tearing up the ranks against the Corporation and the Dawg Pound, eventually however, Darkstar grew to big and was ousted from his standing in the Assylum, but this only propelled his career, going on to win every title with the only exception being the World and Tag Team Titles... that is until him and Extremist met in the biggest main event of the year, he defeated his old friend and finally reached the pinnacle of the wrestling world, after losing the title in a screw job, he took a much needed vacation, when he came back he was changed... for the better? Alligning himself with the Corporation he lost the true love he once had to his arch enemy, but now.. his time for revenge is upon... Primetime Championship Wrestling [2001] Double duty time for Darkstar, after hitting the big time with the FTR, Darkstar hit the BIGGER Time with the PCW, the number one fed in the world, after a meeting with the president he was booked in a match against three other new comers... In recent days however, DarkStar has been through many changes, losing four straight since his original burst onto the scene, he and Talenas relationship strained to far and shattered like spun glas, consoling himself in a bottle of alcohol, he met his current love... Harley, a sociopathic harlequin, cant beat that now can we. Along with Harle, they plan to grip the PCW with a new found Insane-streak, hoping that somehow DarkStar can find his first taste of gold through her. But still, the thought of Talena lingers in the back of his mind... September 2001 DarkStar, after finally tasting a win of PCW Gold at PCW New Dawning, has found himself in the spotlight, too bad he can only barely hang on to it all... Finding out about your true father can be hard to deal with, it makes you feel better if you find out that your true father is really the King of the Dream realm, well guess who DarkStars real father was. His entire history as he knows it is now obsolete, for it was all a plan Destiny had for him to take the throne, however, DarkStar did not work with the plan as it was supposed to go, for he takes this new found power under his own terms... but he did find something out... DarkStar is the Seventh Son, of the Seventh Son, of the Seventh Son, and anyone wiht any mythological background at all knows that this means he has powers greater then this earth... how he deals is something none of us, not even his lover Harle, can know... Somehow, he has dealt with it with a new resolve, destroying any boundary that anyone had between good and evil, one day he uses his power to get revenge, the next he uses his power to give a wrongfully killed girl new life... he can see pain caused in this world without flinching, he can help people when he chooses to... but when will he really choose to? Those with the power decide what happens... and right now, he has the power Constants: People Joyce Drazail (Mother) - Alive, Havent spoken with her for many years, since running away from home. She came into his life and has never left it, ever since they met all those years ago. They broke up after a misunderstanding and havent spoken since, she managed him for most of his career. Insane Kidd - DarkStars first real 'friend' on the wrestling circuit Willow the Whisp - Independant Wrestler that got him a start in FWA. Reckless Youth - Trained DarkStar during his early years in wrestling. Davey Havok - Possibly DarkStars biggest 'famous' friend. Things Shai Hulud - One of two bands that helped give DarkStars original band, Angelus Errae, a start in the business. AFI - The other band. Places 325 Arch Street, Philadelphia, PA - DarkStars estate in Philadelphia, a converted church, built around its 17th century gothic design of course. QXT's - A Gothic Industrial nightclub in south Philly that DarkStar frequents, he constantly can be seen there during days off. The Dream Realm - A Realm of make believe which DarkStar rules over, the place where he bridges the gap between make believe and reality.
