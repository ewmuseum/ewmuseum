Hiro Biography Biography Hiro's pending arrival to the PTC was not a well kept secret. He briefly signed with PURE but was promptly removed from the roster due to a contract problem with another wrestling company. He tried to make a come back in RUSH but his stay there was also short lived due to conflicts with management and contract problems with another wrestling company. Hiro hopes that the 'third times the charm.' Finally getting past all the red tape Hiro throws his hat back into the world of the PTC, with his good friend and tag partner Edward Lambert AKA BloodAngel, with hopes of fulfilling his hype and proving to himself and the rest of the PTC that he can hang with the 'big boys' of the PCW. Throughout Hiro's young life three things have been a constant. One, the martial arts. For seventeen years Hiro has studied various styles ranging from Chinese Boxing to Tae Kwon Do. He has earned a black belts in Shoto-kan (3rd Dan), Tae Kwon Do (2nd Dan), Jujitsu (1st Dan) and has earned the equivalent of the 'Golden Gloves' in Chinese Boxing. A competitive streak runs deep in Hiro. From a small game of Chinese Checkers to the fighting world Hiro is fiercly compatitive. When people say that he should play for fun he replies, 'Don't you find winning fun?' Hiro plays to win but that doesn't mean he breaks the rules. He believes that cheating is the easy way out and that cheats the person out valueable lessons that can be gained from losing. Notice he doesn't like losing but it wont kill him if he does. The last constant in Hiro's young life are the strong ties of tradition and honor. Two things Hiro hold dear. In a constantly changing world Hiro knows that one must have some ounce of stability to remain sane. Hiro believes that he can effectivally mix old world traditions and new world thinking together to bring harmony into his life. Number three is an ongoing project. The martial arts gave Hiro the opportunity for a bigger challenge, the world of wrestling. That is why Hiro is here in the PCW, to embrase the talent the PTC and PCW has to offer and to see if he has what it takes to hang with the PCW's big boys. The years of constant martial arts training and diet gave Hiro a very lean, defined and muscular body. This was ideal for the speed and finess needed to be one of the best in the martial arts. When Hiro came into the wrestling ring he quickly found out that he would be shoved around by the larger and stronger wrestlers if he didn't put some weight on. So that is exactly what he did. His lightly tanned body, smooth and blemish free complexion, clean cut black hair, warm hazel eyes, friendly and sincere smile mixed with his 'Adonis-esque' body make Hiro a natural fan of the young and old. Not to mention the ladies. In the ring Hiro likes to keep it simple and a little traditional. Fingerless white sparing gloves cover his hands, white tape covers his wrists and a pair of white Adidas mesh shorts cover his thighs. Three red stripes run down the side and a small Japanese flag is sewn on his right hip. His feet are covered by white Adidas tennis shoes with the same red stripes. To add some color his ankle cut socks are a bright red. Can you tell that he has Japanese pride?
