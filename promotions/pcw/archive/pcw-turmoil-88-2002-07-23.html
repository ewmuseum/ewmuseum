Turmoil 88 23 Jul 2002 The USF Sun Dome, Tampa, Florida The CEO and the Dark Age The picture is black, but for some reason we can tell that something is there. There is just that....aura. After a few seconds a dim light begins to crack from somewhere at the bottom of the screen, seemingly from behind some sort of object. It's an eerie light, diffused somehow. As the light's intensity slowly increases we can just start to make out a desk from which the light is coming from behind, and the form of a person sitting behind it. The light increases just enough to make out the evil features on the face of the CEO of PCW, Tyler Nelson. Darkness shrouds the rest of the room as Nelson begins to speak, his voice decidedly different than the cocky tone we are used to. It's darker....more subdued. Tyler: The Dark Age continues its evil grip on Primetime Championship Wrestling. With each passing day the hold grows tighter, the fear in everyone's heart continues to grow, the pain administered by my Dark Knights continues to wreak havoc on the rest of the roster. Many have tasted the wrath of the Dark Age in the last several weeks since I unleashed pure hell on PCW. Those who were foolish enough to challenge the Dark Knights have met their demise with haste, not having enough time to beg for their souls. There is a short pause as the CEO moves forward somewhat, bringing his face and body more into light. His pale looking skin has replaced the carefully manicured tan he once wore. Gone is the Armani suit which Nelson once wore, replaced by what looks to be a pair of leather pants and no shirt. He sits in the high-back leather chair that he always does, but cock-eyed with his leg slung over the arm. A cold stare greets us as Nelson begins to speak again. Tyler: With everything that we have managed to do, there is still much work left to be done. As the Dark Knights continue their rampage through the PCW locker room, it gives me great pleasure to know that the likes of Duke Williams, Legend Inc, Xavier Kannon, Frisco, Big Alan Steele, and even The Phantom Booker are all living in fear of the fate that awaits them. Those men are scared, and rightfully so, of what my Dark Knights have in store for them. Clyde, Darkstar, and Syko......my Dark Warriors....have done everything that I could have ever asked of them and more. A sick, half smirk slides across Nelson's lips as he thinks about the carnage his minions have inflicted. As if on cue, three forms step forward from the darkness revealing themselves to be those men whom the CEO just named off. Clyde, the PCW Continental Champion Darkstar, and Syko the Goblin flank Nelson from left to right respectively. All three have sinister smirks on their faces, Darkstar with the Continental Title draped over his shoulder. Tyler: This weekend will be no different, as more souls will no doubt be laid to rest at the hands of the Dark Age. And while they are going above and beyond the call of duty in the evil deeds, I feel the need to add to my Army of Darkness. I want more Dark Warriors to do my bidding, to destroy all those who dare to oppose me. This is why I have coaxed one of the greatest Warriors ever to step in the ring out of his wrestling slumber. I have lured a monster from his den and he will be in Jamaica on Sunday to inflict pain and punishment the likes has rarely been witnessed by human eyes. This is a warning to everyone in PCW....the Dark Age will continue to wreak havoc and terror over any soul we so see fit to. Anyone who is brave enough to stand up to my Dark Warriors will be stricken down with extreme prejudice and serve as a sacrifice to the Devil himself. The greatest Warrior of them all is coming, and he along with my Dark Knights will be an unstoppable force which will sweep across PCW like the wildfires of Hell, wiping out all who stand in our way. This is your only warning. The CEO begins to chuckle, along with his Dark Age cohorts. The light that had been illuminating them ever so slightly begins to fade as their laughs increase in volume. Soon the picture is black but their laughing haunts our ears still for several seconds afterwards, then slowly fades out to silence.
