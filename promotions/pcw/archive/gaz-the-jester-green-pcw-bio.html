Gaz 'The Jester' Green Biography Real Name Gareth Green Nicknames The Emerald Menace, Prince Of Pranks, King Of Komedy and Lord Of Laughter Hometown Las Vegas, Nevada (The City Of Sin) Birthday 23 - 07 - 79 Height 5' 11' Weight 226 lbs Biography Description: From the confines of the ring Gaz Green is usually sporting velvet green, three-piece business tuxedo accompanied by dark sunglasses, sheltering his eyes. His natural hair colour would be a medium brown but since his transition into the wrestling industry we have been accustomed to him dyeing it a pale shade of green and held in a fashionable spike. During promotional segments and in-ring performances Gaz would be show casing his trim figure with a cruiserweight physique and adorning his wrestling attire. Nothing would appear out of place, again his hair would be firmly held high and his glasses will still be present. The Jester wears emerald green wrestling tights with his insignia present on either leg accompanied by the black Jericho style shin-guards. Current Angle: The Jester is currently one half of the tag team 'Pranks With Attitude.' The team is comprised of Nerezza and himself, based around two obscure characters with very different out-looks on the wrestling industry. A team of contradiction Gaz Green is the laid back half whilst his partner Nerezza is more up tight with a serious tone to his attitude. Entrance: The area becomes bathed in complete darkness, silencing the crowd. As the seconds countdown the PA system hits with Off - Springs Original Prankster and a torrent of fireworks fall creating a wall. The video wall flickers to life and shows various images of Gaz 'The Jester' Green accompanied by his insignia. The fireworks simmer down unveiling the host with his back to the crowd in a crucifixtion pose before he pivots on his foot and descends to the ring under the crowds reaction. History: Like all backgrounds, Gaz Green had an abusive childhood. Always felt like a black sheep amongst others of his age he would shelter his anger and frustration, remembered only as the class clown, the butt of everybody’s jokes. During wrestling’s popularity surge in the midst of the mid 80's, Gaz Green found himself taking a keen interest and soon began the initial steps in his short career. At every opportunity Gaz would prepare physically and mentally for his desired career, enhancing his strength through fitness training whilst remaining an active teenager. Even this newfound hobby didn't discourage his tormentors as the abuse continued but with great power, comes great responsibility and it was by this code he lived and prevailed. Eventually his days as a student expired and he found himself baring the question, ‘What next?' Would he set off to College and further his education or follow his heart, taking the biggest gamble of his life since the wrestling profession either makes or breaks a person. To the disbelief of his parents Gaz opted the latter option and decided to gamble his life in the make, break situation because if this weren’t the case then this story would have surely ended. The Jester's life would again take a turn for the worse as employment within the industry almost immediately ceased since the arrival of specified training complexes that only catered for those willing to part with their hard earned pay. Many people slaved away for their opportunity including Gaz Green who became renowned for working odd jobs, desperately trying to salvage his dream but as always popularity soared and in turn so did the required tuition, making the peak of the mountain almost unobtainable. By a twist of fate it was one of these odd jobs that earned his first foot through the door. On a cold winters night, nothing unusual and certainly a typical day in the life of Gaz Green he began work. It was during this time Gaz Green became a bar tender for a local social club that was barely kept afloat, even with its dedicated members but this night would change his life forever. The punters sat in the smoke infested room, drowning their sorrows when suddenly a mountainous man clad in a long flowing trench coat walked in minding his own business happy enough to sit quietly quenching his thirst and taking the weight off his legs. Again nothing out of the ordinary, but this mans very presence only spurned the regulars into frenzy as one particular fellow stepped forward with a showcase of disrespect. It came to fluition that the stranger was a wrestler on tour and that the clubs regulars wanted to test his patience with a shoot of their own. Abuse was thrown and things were said but the stranger refused to react even from the occasional nudge but as tempers began to flair it was the quick thinking of one man that separated the two parties, namely Gaz Green. It was Gaz who stepped from the shadows and turned the tables by executing a simple hammerlock, preventing any major misunderstanding thus breaking the ice and possibly an arm. The stranger who was later unveiled as Double MJ offered the young Gaz Green the chance to work in a real wrestling environment but only as a lowly ring constructor. Obviously he accepted and for many months he assembled the ring for shows, until the promoter came to him with a proposition with regards to a new angle he had in mind. The angle required Gaz to take a number of bumps from the company’s biggest heel, which never once disheartened him. The night came, there was a flood of spectators watching this heel trash talk both members of the active roster and staff alike then through his show of frustration he took out his anger on the lowly construction boy. It began as a complete white wash, Gaz being thrown about like a mere doll, taking a number of large bumps but it was an attempted cross body that almost cemented his name to the roster. His quick thinking took the wrestler off his feet but the mistake of ascending the top rope signalled the end as the shocked amateur regained his vertical base and threw his smaller work mate through the air and onto the canvas. An electric reaction followed but it was the final foot through the door thought the badly bruised Gaz Green, nursing his wounds. Gaz trained hard for his opportunity and grasped it with both hands, but one thing was still missing, an alias and so began the era of a man known as New-Blood. New-Blood became a prominent part of the company and his ascension through the ranks was rewarded with a dream partnership between himself and the man who made this dream a reality, Double MJ. A dream partnership turned sour and soon become a blatant nightmare as the team crumbled within with a series of bad matches and poor communication, topped off with an angle based on the team splitting. Through the split came bad blood, as the promoter’s next act was to feud the former partners with a series of high profile matches and a finale at their forth-coming pay-per-view. New-Blood holding very little stroke with the company never once realised the turmoil of the back as many wrestlers were shipped off for financial reasons and it was days before his showdown with Double MJ that he realised his opponent had terminated his contract and signed with an up and coming promotion known as the DWA (Dark Wrestling Alliance). The next twist in this tale came when New-Blood was told he was surplus to requirements and his services were no longer needed, again a road block had been put between himself and his dream. Well a number of weeks past and a fateful telephone call put the dream back on track. It turned out that the owner of the DWA, a Dave Time had been following the tag team turmoil between Double MJ and New-Blood. His idea was to sign the unemployed Gaz to a contract and resurrect their feud with the DWA with an eventful finale, reforming the team through mutual respect. The two individuals once again became friends and both had successful title reigns within the company, most notably was New-Bloods historic Double Gold Championship era as he adorned both the Hardcore and Light Heavyweight titles. It was during this time New-Blood acquired his arrogance and his new egotistical attitude, only accepting main events or high profile matches that would benefit his character. The pressure of performing finally took its tole as a new stipulation was brought in, which stated that New-Blood would have to defend one of his titles every week. Still at the top of his game, New-Blood began showing signs of paranoia and his backstage antics were not winning him any popularity awards pushing him to the brink of career suicide. As his career began to fade, waiting for the final nail in the coffin, New-Blood was offered an opportunity to join Primetime Wrestling Academy with a view to furthering himself. Without so much as an hesitation he accepted but the company did however feel the character of New-Blood had ran its course and they were looking for him to undertake a change of gimmick. Thinking back to his abusive childhood and what he had been lacking since stepping into the wrestling profession, he concluded that he had lost the fun element and decided to dedicate his new gimmick to comedy. And so the era of Gaz 'The Jester' Green begins....
