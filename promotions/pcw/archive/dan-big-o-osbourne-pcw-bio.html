Dan 'Big O' Osbourne Biography Real Name Dan Osbourne Nicknames Big O Hometown Intercourse, PA Birthday 6-9-1978 Height 6'1' Weight 255 lbs Biography Born of the Earth, bronzed in the Flames of the Heavens, touched by the Gods. This is Dan Osbourne. This is a brief and unworthy history. Dan was destined for greatness from birth. With academic and athletic excellence throughout his scholastic career, Dan was also Class President, President of his Fraternity, and always the most popular. After graduating with a Masters in both Anatomy and Sexual Education at the age of 18, Dan was more than qualified for his first career. Adult films. Dan so loved women that he worked as an adult film star for free. After a couple years, Dan tired of the life, and especially the strange looks it drew from good people. Dan searched out something new, something fresh. He went to Canada and there he saw the most amazing thing: Professional Wrestling. While in a state of euphoria, Dan went to his normal location where he collected his thoughts and made his life decisions… a nearby strip club. There he met a man. Nay, more than a man. Dan met his destiny that night. His destiny’s name was Mikey and he was a wrestling trainer. After many drinks, discussions, arguments, and bets, Mikey agreed to train Dan in the ways of the wrestler. Soon after, Dan made his way to the training facility, PWA where he quickly graduated with honors. Soon Dan found himself in the PCW2. After a decent showing, Dan was crowned the Rising Star champion. Then the world changed. PCW2 was dismantled and a select few were invited into the ranks of the Granddaddy of them all, PCW. Then Dan disappeared. Like a ghost, or a dream, he was gone. Dan has now been rediscovered and he has made his return, but for what purpose? Quotes I’ll leave you lying with a warm, sweet AFTERGLOW!
