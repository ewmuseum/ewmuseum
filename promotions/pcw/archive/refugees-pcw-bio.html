Refugees Biography Biography The Refugee's story begins in the Shak-I-Kot Valley in northwestern Afghanistan. Their fathers had chosen to break away from the King's rule and form the first Afghan tribe since the King was appointed ruler of Afghanistan. They were not a peaceful people and often got into fights with Kabul's Army. Ali and Qaboos grew up knowing that they would one day be important to the Afghan World. Little did they know that they would not only be important, but also be heroes. When they were young teens in 1979, war broke out with the Soviets. The Nuristani Tribe found itself aligned once more with Afghanistan but this time they were fighting not only for pride but for freedom, hence The Refugee's nickname Freedom Fighter's. Though they rarely got involved with much action, they played an important role in guarding the prisoners. They were armed at all times and learned how to use weapons. After that there was peace for the most part until the year 1996. Ali and Qaboos were appointed tribal leaders two years prior to their meeting of Mullah Mohammad Omar, the Taliban leader. He told them he would allow them to keep their cave but they were to avoid Red Cross workers. They willingly accepted and so the Taliban rule began. September 11th was a normal day in Afghanistan. The Nuristani's had no knowledge whatsoever of the attack and to this day have mixed feelings about it. This however triggered the attacks against al-Qaeda forces taking refuge in Afghan cities. And so sparked another saga in the troubled times of Afghan living, air raids. The first bombs dropped were far away from the Nuristani tribe but they still startled the members who watched as great balls of fire rained down on Kandahar. Ali and Qaboos feared that the tribe had to flee the valley and so they fled to the worst possible place, the Tora Bora caves. After heavy bombing began Ali and Qaboos began to worry and selfishly they saved their own lives and watched helpless as their entire tribe was swallowed into a great inferno which raged across the Shak-I-Kot Valley. Ali vowed that day to take his anger out on America. The Refugee's were taken to Professor Sergei Koroylov in Eastern Russia. He was the son of a former Soviet scientist and had the ability to create nuclear weapons. He gave The Refugee's shelter for a bit but turned out to be against them altogether and he sent a man to take them to a prison in Cuba. Lucky for them they escaped and reached México and from there an easy walk to America. Soon after arriving, The Refugee's found themselves in Genesis Wrestling but after only two wins they left for Primetime Wrestling Academy. There they rivaled PCW star The Freak and former NTICW stars El Lunes Mascara and Karep. They were in Boda's class. Boda's alliance with The Refugee's helped them greatly throughout their career. After being their Headmaster for less than one week he promoted them to his XWA. XWA was filled with talent at the time and The Refugee's helped form the failed stable, Global Milk Anonymous which ended soon after it started due to Livewire and Steve 'Milk Man' Carnage leaving for RUSH. The Refugee's stuck around XWA long enough to win the Tag Team titles from Jonathon Drachen and Cege Hayabusa but were promoted by Boda, once again, to NTICW. NTICW was a wake-up call to The Refugee's. They were shown that they didn't have the experience or talent that Limerick or Methodical Disorder had.
