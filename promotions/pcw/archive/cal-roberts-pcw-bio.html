Cal Roberts Biography Real Name Calvin Roberts-Kowalski Nicknames The Buzz Hometown Detroit, Michigan Birthday April 12th, 1975 Height 6'1' Weight 262 lbs Biography Born, Calvin Roberts, on 4/12/75 to two teenaged parents. He was left and raised in a foster home until, at the age of 7, he was adopted by a grizzled pro-boxer, Jim Kowalski. This was good news to the boy who had always wanted a father. The bad news would outweigh the good, however, as Jim was hard on the boy. Always expecting the best from him in his athletic pursuits, and more importantly in the gym and ring. He trained with his father, and was being groomed as a boxer. However, one day he saw the spectacle of what was pro-wrestling on a television set in the gym. The boy immediately made it his goal to become a pro-wrestler, but was scorned by his father to stay away from something he could not handle. On March 17th, 1988 after coming home from school. Calvin would come into his adopted father's gym to find Jim dead in his office. Not knowing what to do, Calvin took up street fighting, amateur boxing, and anything else he could do to support himself. Jim had not left the gym to anyone, and it was demolished with the next year. Then one day on April 12th, 1993, Calvin discovered a pro-wrestling school. He would train at 'Slam Fest Gym' for the next 6 months before making his pro-debut as 'The Buzz' Cal Roberts. He quickly gained noteriety for being a vicious competitor in the ring. Now that he was making a good amount of money by wrestling up to seven days a week on the independent circuit, The Buzz began to seek something else...women. One was not enough however. He went after anything and everything that was beautiful. However, he made it a habit to be gone before the next morning to avoid any emotional attachment. He continued his career until 1998 when he began to get a few big breaks including some work for two national US promotions, and for Japan. His name now meant something more. When someone said the name 'The Buzz', tickets were about to be sold. Since then he continued to work in hopes of finally landing a spot in the top promotion, PCW. PCW would eventually shut down, and Buzz was content with independent work until he received a call in early November. The call was from PCW officials offering him a spot in the revival of PCW. By now 'The Buzz' Cal Roberts is definately ready to handle Primetime, but can Primetime handle him? As it would turn out 'The Buzz' Cal Roberts has been a huge part of PCW during its revival, and has developed strong rivalries with both Skylar Green and Inphino Blitz. He even managed to hold the PCW World Title for a week after only seven weeks in the promotion. No small feat by any means. Now he was going for the belt once again, but was going to attempt to earn it himself. However, he was becoming sidetracked by the PCW Continental Champion, Skitzo. Would Buzz achieve his goal? Other Feds ECFED, SWC, UWC, TRW, WRF, EWWF, ECWF, and more. Other Fed Awards Many...Various Hall of Fame noms, #25 on the PWI 500 list in 2002, Wrestler of the Year in quite a few promotions. Quotes Hey beautiful, would you like to become one with The Buzz? 'Now Can You Handle That!?
