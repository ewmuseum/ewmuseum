Turmoil 73 12 Mar 2002 The Lloyd Noble Center, Norman, Oklahoma Badge tied to a chair while Bethany and Miss Cleo torment him Fade in. In a small room a maelstrom blares. An unending cacophony of noise, tearing through the brain and ripping what remains of sanity from a man's mind. Through the haze of unconsciousness this blaring sound finally rips............becomes more clear. It becomes a familiar noise............and a familiar voice. "Wake up dipshit!" Badge: Wha.........? The young man known as Badge opens his eyes, looks to his left to find an odd sight, a little girl clanging a pair of cymbals right next to his head. Bethany: I said wake up goddamn you! Badge: Ugh........... Badge attempts to move, only to find he cannot in the slightest. A glance down reveals why............he sits tied to a chair by several lengths of very constricting rope. Bethany: Look at me when I'm talking to you! With eyes red and bleary the former Extreme champion raises his head to face his tormenter. Badge: Knew I shouldn't have slept with that 27th chick............but dammit she wanted me.............. Bethany: Eyes forward nimrod! Badge: Uh............ Badge follows the orders of the tiny terror, and looks forward............into the glow of a TV with a VCR attached. On it plays a familiar movie, the ever depressing Schindler's List. Bethany leans in close..........slowly she crawls onto Badge's lap, eying him and smiling evilly. Bethany: We have plans for you Badge............you're going to be the start of a new revolution. The little girl taps the former cop on his head once.........twice.............thrice in rapid succession. Bethany: But first we need to shatter that poor wittle brain of yours. So you get to sit here and watch the Nazis torture and mutilate those poor Jews for endless hours. Have fun Badgey wadgy................ And with that Bethany hops off Badge's lap...............cackling maniacally she makes her way from the room, as Badge stares blankly at the television.............. Fade out The normal opening to Tuesday Turmoil rolls. The city is in ruins as it burns. The people all run around while this goes on. Among the wrestlers highlighted are The Rush, Charlie Loc, Jobbers Inc., Big Alan Steele, and Miyagawa. Now is a better time than ever to kick the American PCW while they’re down. I want you to begin my mission tonight. Get Kevin Klaymore and Flaymin Aymin, go out there, and make your country proud Juan, I know you can do it. (Chef gives Juan a pat on the back, as Juan goes running out of the locker room, fully determined to succeed.)
