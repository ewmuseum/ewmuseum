Jordan X Shabazz Biography Height 5'11 Weight 226lbs Biography The man who goes by the name of Jordan X Shabazz was actually born Jordan Andrew Copeland. his middle name is taken from his older brother Andrew Copeland, who wrestles in the OSW under the name of Drew Hostyle. Jordan was born in Brooklyn, New York, but after his father left as a child, he was forced to be put up for adoption. He was adopted by a white family in Toronto, Ontario Canada. And Jordan lived in Canada for most of his life. He got good grades and was a very respectful young man. When Jordan graduated from High School, his adopted parents sent him away to The University Of Toronto, perhaps the best University in all of Canada. Jordan studied Broadcast Journalism and graduated with honors. After graduating from University, Jordan took all the money he had saved up from working in a Nestle chocolate Factory, and he moved out on his own. He was at a lost. He didn't know what to do with his life, he got a job with a local radio station called Kiss 92 FM. After spending a few months there, Jordan realized he wasn't liked by some of the people working there. After getting into an altercation with one of the DJs Jordan physically assaulted him and accidentally killed him. They tried Jordan for involuntary Manslaughter. That's where he met his friend, and current manager, Jake Jackson. Jordan was facing a life sentence in prison, but he convinced his manager that it was Self-Defense, and Jake Jackson, used his brain, and he made sure Jordan got off by paying some of the jury members. Jordan walked out of court a free man, and that began the bond that those two men share to this very day. After getting out of court, and starting over again with a clean slate, Jordan decided to move back to New York. This time not in Brooklyn, but in Queens. After seeing his brother Drew Hostyle for the first time in over 15 years, the two men shared a bond. Drew was a professional wrestler, and he encouraged Jordan to join the profession. Jordan did. Jordan was trained by legendary wrestling superstar Johnny Rodz. Jordan learned all the tricks and secrets of wrestling. Jordan first wrestled for a small Indy fed called the ESW. The ESW was based out of Boston, Jordan wrestled under the guise of Jordan Crazy there. His tag team partner was his long time friend, Chris Green, who worked under the name of Chris Crazy. They were given Jake Jackson as a manager, together they were The Crazy Boyz. A tag team that used the 'One white Brother, one black brother' gimmick. They were over huge in the ESW. They slowly began to prove that they were very talented and could hang with the big boys on the Independent scene. Jordan and his tag team partner Chris, won the tag team titles and became the leaders of a weak tag team division. The ESW suffered through numerous financial problems and closed and reopened it's doors regularly. Jordan couldn't deal with the unpredictability so he left. This time he went to New Jersey to work for a federation called the ICW. The ICW (Which is now in the PTC) was a federation where Jordan wrestled there as Jordan Dragon, and he teamed with Mikey Richards. Together they comprised Extreme Impact. They went on to win the ICW tag team titles in their first match in the promotion... exactly, that tells you how weak their tag team division was. Jordan Dragon and Mikey Richards hung around for a bit looking for a feud, but when they didn't find one, Jordan picked up his stuff, and he went back to the ESW. The ESW was in a resurgence. They hadn't gotten back several of the stars that had made the ESW a desirable organization to work for, one of those included several time ESW World Champion, Rich Sherwin, who now wrestles with Ryan Moore in The Pretty Cool Guys here in the PCW. It wasn't long again before the ESW closed, this was something that hit Jordan right in his heart. He couldn't take the unpredictability of the ESW, you have a job one minute, the next, you don't. 'The Golden Boy' John Knight, long time ESW Television Champion, decided that it was time that he make his own wrestling organization. The PCW's Scorpion was also working there. It was called the HWF, and had it's home base in Queens, New York, using the Elks Lodge (AKA The ECW's Madhouse of Extreme) as their home arena. The federation was drawing near sell outs in really small arenas. Jordan wrestled there as 'Sick' Rick Jordan. He was pushed as one of their top stars, selling out the 500 seat Murphy Recreation center in their second show, and selling out their first non-televised event. After advancing to the finals of the World Title tournament, John Knight closed the federation. Citing money problems as the thing that did him in. After leaving the HWF, Jordan decided to try and work his magic in a Fedwars federation. First he wrestled for the EDW, a federation made famous by former PCW Extreme Champion, Big Daveylicious. Jordan wrestled as ‘Violent’ Chris Flash. Jordan had a few friends in that federation, Jason ’Jay Jay’ Jameson, and Josh Sutton included. Jordan didn’t last a single match. He left the federation before he was booked, for reasons that have never actually been made public. From EDW, Jordan went to Soldout Wrestling. Where he wrestled there for a short while under the guise of ‘Canadian Icon’ Jordan Case. Again, he didn’t wrestle a single match in the federation, due to the fact that Jordan was told that wrestling in $W would destroy his credibility, and in the future, hurt him when he went out looking for a job in a bigger federation. Jordan left $W, without so much as a goodbye. He told Vice President, Victor Blood, that he was going, and was on the receiving end of a nasty tirade. You’d think the man had learned his lesson, but Jordan was nothing more than a fedwhore at this time. Next he went to an uprising promotion called the GWO. They had former PCW World Heavyweight Champion, Stewart Hix there, they had former Soldout Wrestling Cruiserweight Champion, The Little Great One there, Stevie C, former EWWL Champion of the world was there. Known fedwhore, Galuda there. Former WWA World Champion, and current PCW wrestler, Violence Jack was there as well. A lot of talent was in that federation. Jordan was brought in as a huge free agent and signed a long term big money contract with the federation. He was primed to feud with “Devious” Devon Ace, and Stewart Hix in a three way feud over the GWO World Heavyweight Title. The federation wasn’t run too well, paychecks were always late, and Jordan couldn’t stand some of the people backstage. The politicking became to much for Jordan, and after wrestling two matches in the GWO, he picked up his stuff, and left. Next on Jordan’s list of federations that he worked for, was Steve Cash’s EWWL. Extreme World Wrestling League was based in the city of Chicago, and was home to such talents as Josh Sutton, Stevie C, Grim, former PCW star, Billy Blaze, former Soldout Wrestling star, Devin Dawson, and the NTICW’s Carl Cash. Jordan wrestled there with his old time tag team partner, Chris Crazy. They made quite the splash in the EWWL. Working their way up in the tag team defeating several superstars on their way up. After winning a Number one contenders match against a tag team called ‘Uprising’, Jordan and Chris were primed for a shot at Billy Blaze and Harrison James, the tag team champions at the time. Axl Young, and Sean Senn, the two wrestlers that were defeated by Jordan and Chris Crazy, were bitter. They didn’t think that Jordan and Chris were talented enough to carry on a feud with the tag team champions, and Senn and Young were two talented grapplers. So they began to threaten the EWWL with the possibility that they were going to tear up the prospective contracts that they had in the works, and leave. So with the PPV coming up, the EWWL decided the make a rematch between The Crazy Boyz and Uprising, in the friendly confines of a steel cage. Uprising won the match after handcuffing Jordan and Chris’ manager, Jake Jackson, to the steel cage, and brutally beating Jordan and Chris Crazy. Jordan and Chris didn’t have a problem jobbing to Axl Young and Sean Senn, what they did have a problem with was when Harrison James came down the ramp after the match, and said the match at the Pay Per View for the tag team titles would be a three way dance with Uprising, The Crazy Boyz, and Harrison and Blaze. Chris and Jordan were visibly upset in the ring. They started screaming obscenities and asking to speak to AC Carter, the owner of the federation, and the World Champion. After having a meeting with AC Carter, and realizing that he wasn’t going to change the Pay Per View match up, in which The Crazy Boyz would job to Uprising again, Chris, Jordan, and their manager, Jake Jackson, all walked out of the federation. The EWWL had very close ties to a promotion in Japan called Burning Spirit Pro Wrestling. Jordan decided to split from his good friend, Chris Crazy, and go to Japan with Jake Jackson. Jordan wrestled his first match on the promotion’s return Pay Per View, ‘Resurrection.’ Jordan wrestled against a student of the business, Atsuka was his name. He just went by Atsuka. Jordan won the match via submission with a sharpshooter. After that match, Jordan was entered in the Junior Heavyweight Title tournament, their Junior Heavyweight Championship was called the Revolution Title. Jordan worked hard and won his block of the tournament. IN the semi-finals, he lost to a wrestler named DRAGON. Jordan went on to do several tours with the company before leaving for good and moving to America to wrestle for the PWA. After spending a month and a half in the Academy, Jordan got promoted to the NTICW. In the NTICW, Jordan started off as a nobody, he wasn't known, and he wasn't given his just desserts. Then, one day, he was listening to his radio, and he heard The Phantom Booker on PTC Malice. Jordan called in, and the rest was history. Jordan screamed, ranted, and raved, then he used his radio show popularity to ride his way through the NTICW. Jordan is a regular guest of the Phantom Booker and Brain Chapman on the PTC's Sunday radio show. While in the NTICW, Jordan feuded originally with Mark Makonie. The two men engaged in an intense feud which included such gimmick matches as a Billy Club match, a no DQ match, and burn the flag match. The latter match was the last match Jordan ever had in the NTICW, he was defending his Extreme Championship against Makonie. In a brutal 30 minute classic, Jordan prevailed, defeating Mark Makonie for a second time, while losing zero times to Makonie. That ended their feud, and Jordan's career in the NTICW. The next week, Jordan called into the PTC Malice show like normal, and he begged Xavier Kannon for a job on air. He smothered Xavier with compliments, and after making Jordan look like an ass. He was given a chance to become Xavier Kannon's lackey. Jordan was supposed to lick somebody's ass for the PCW contract, he was willing to do it, until he realized that the person who's ass he was supposed to kiss was Neo Nazi, and black man hater, Mark Makonie. Jordan immediately refused, which was going to induce a beating he wouldn't soon forget...But, thankfully, Duke Williams came out to make the save. After Duke and Jordan cleaned house, Duke offered Jordan a chance to become a member of the Malice Crew, Jordan immediately accepted and joined Duke Williams, Rush Mason, Jason Core, and Legend Inc. in that stable. Jordan originally wrestled under the name of Jordan Crazy, but he changed his name to be one with himself, and to remember his African roots and to be closer to his idol, Malcolm X. Jordan became a Civil Rights Extremist after being stopped in Miami, Florida for a traffic violation. Jordan felt that the stop was racially motivated, so he began to fight for his people, and a hatred for racist white people developed. Jordan was one of the few talented wrestlers who were signed by the PCW after it's famous roster firing at Supremacy 2002. Jordan X Shabazz recently started N.I.G.G.A, the Negroes International Growing Group Association, it's like the NAACP, Jordan helps African Americans everywhere. Jordan's hatred for most all white people is evident, he usually has a few white friends for the simple fact that they have only been nice to him. If he doesn't know a certain white person, he'll hate them. And hate them with a passion. His uncontrolable anger is obvious too, he can go crazy when it comes to the simplest of things, he isn't afraid to accuse racism over anything. Jordan X Shabazz has recently left the Malice Crew and now teams with Thug roC and Rush Mason in 'The Uprising.' A stable that fights against the suppression of African American males and females. </font> Finisher Move By any means necessary
