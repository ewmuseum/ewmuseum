Jeremy Johnson Biography Real Name Jeremy Johnson Nicknames JJ Hometown Charlottesville, VA Birthdate 1st April, 1983 Height 6'0" Weight 200lbs Biography Jeremy Johnson was born in Charlottesville, VA on December 20th, 1983. Soon after his birth, his father left the family, leaving his mother, brother Scott and himself alone to fend for themselves. They eventually grew into a state of poverty and were forced to live in the projects of Charlottesville. Over the years, Jeremy grew so depressed that he would take anyway out. This forced him to change drastically into a happy-go-lucky character named JJ, which made him very unstable. As a child, he was very interested in wrestling, and decided to pursue it as a career. He soon did, and that's how he found PCW at age 18. When his career first started, he had very little luck, losing a great deal of his matches. But he didn't care. He was a crazy character, who often talked to a teddy bear he kept around him named JJ Bear. After about 6 months in PCW, JJ met a woman who would soon become his steady girlfriend, Lisa. They grew very close.....and that's when everything started to go wrong!! His own multiple personalities began to destroy his happy equilibrium. One in particular was one called Evil JJ. JJ was almost ruined. Until he finally seeked psychiatric help. A theropist by the name of Arnold McDonwald helped JJ to realize who he really was and get in touch with the person he was many many years ago. Jeremy returned. McDonwald helped to cure Jeremy's insanities after just one session! And soon after, Jeremy realized his passion for movie production and film directing. Which brings us to the present, where Jeremy now pursues a career in independent films, while wrestling in PCW on the side. And that, my friends, is how 'The Visionary' became the person he is today. --------- Jeremy Johnson was originally a wrestler in the PCW like a couple of the other new refs. He decided that the movie making business was more for him so he left. A few months past and he realised that he didn't have the budget to make a decent movie, so he wanted to get a summer job. Miyagawa hired him as an interviewer for PURE, but that didn't last long, but after someone mentioned to Duke Williams that he could get JJ to count his enemies out of the ring, Mr Johnson was hired on the very same spot.
