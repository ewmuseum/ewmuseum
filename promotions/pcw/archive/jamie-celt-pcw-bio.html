Jamie Celt Biography Real Name Jamie Celt Nicknames Celt,Celtic Kid Hometown Boston, MA Birthday 02-09-83 Height 5'11 Weight 224 lbs Biography A child growing up inside the heart of Boston City is usually not a controversial or in anyway suspicious thing. Though one Jamie Celt grew into a family that were indeed controversial and suspicious, he was born into the mighty Celt family. And more specifically, he was born into the family of Anthony Celt, the most powerful man in all of Boston. One may ask what is so controversial about a man that has power, dresses elegantly, has a respectable wife and children, lives in a mansion in Boston and has a place in the Boston City council. Well, Anthony Celt is and has been since the passing of his father, Peter Celt, the leader of Boston’s most feared yet most dignified gang, the Celtics. The Celtics span from ancient Irish origin and the gang contained many members. Anthony had the power to control Boston because no one dared stand up to such a power. So Jamie Celt grew up, like a normal child. Only instead of learning how to ride a bike he learned how to drive a get away vehicle, and instead of his first word being ‘Mama’ or ’Dada’ it was ‘Kill’ and ‘Now!’ etc. By the age of five Jamie could use an m-45 handgun with pinpoint precision, like it was a child’s toy, which technically speaking, it was. As life went on for Jamie he learned all the lusts and louts of being a gangster. By the time he was 15 he had done more drive-by’s than he could count, and he had seen so many gang wars it was like it was an everyday thing to him. Gangsterism came naturally to him, he was very good at it. With all of his father’s wealth Jamie learned self-defence tactics and was taught by the very best teachers money could buy. Life seemed to be perfect, despite his parent’s conspiratorial ways they still loved Jamie and cared for him. Jamie was also good at athletics and quite a smart boy. Though he did not wish to peruse a college education despite his parents pushing him to do so. Yes, indeed, life would seem good for a boy like Jamie, yet there was something missing. The lust of gangsterism didn’t seem enough for him, he searched and searched for what was missing and soon he found it. One night when the Celtic gang was invading a Triad bar they under covered and underground fighting league, Jamie watched men half kill each other for no more than $100 a fight, it seemed crazy, and someone as rich as Jamie would never need to go that distance to obtain money. Yet, one night he went to that same underground fighting league went into the circle with another man twice his size and got absolutely obliterated, he was killed, though Jamie did get a few good shots in, he should not prevent the man he was facing from nearly killing Jamie. Most would think such a thing would be on bearable, but for Jamie it was the perfect adrenaline rush, he wanted more, and would go through hell and high water to get it. So he trained, his parents spent more money a trainers, training him in the art of Martial Combat, he great increasingly stronger and even started beating some of the beastly men in the fighting leagues. But soon his perfect life was to come to an end… when his parents told him he must get a career instead of wasting his life on the streets, Jamie was shattered when he heard this, he couldn’t make a decent living out of fighting, he only did that for the rush. So after nearly a year of contemplating, he found the answer… professional wrestling. He joined an academy and worked his way up… he eventually got into a development federation known as XWA where he went on to NTICW, though it closed due to an apparent ‘curse.’There he had won the NTICW World Title andmade quite a name for himself. So, if Jamie wanted to continue his wrestling career, he would need to find a federation, and he hoped he had found that in…Primetime Championship Wrestling.
