PCW match: Pharaoh v. Ben J Pailthorpe v Bobby Stormbringer v Big Daveylicious (#1 Contender to the Television Title)

22 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Pharaoh v. Ben J Pailthorpe v Bobby Stormbringer v Big Daveylicious (#1 Contender to the Television Title) Written: March 22, 2002 Dave: Coming up next is the Four Corners match that’ll earn one of these men a shot at the Television title! Jeff: You mean the Akijobber title, don’t you? Duke: The NVG is set to face off against his former tag partner at Spring Fling for this Akiyama...Television title with Jay Sylo as the special guest referee. There’s something foul afoot with that match. Clay: The next match is a four way match for the #1 contender’s spot for the Akivision title! Dave: What? Clay: Sorry, Akiyama threatened to banish me if I didn’t say that. “Girls, Girls, Girls” by Motley Crue hits the speakers. Big Daveylicious comes walking down the ramp to a small pop from the ladies. Clay: First, coming down to the ring, weighing in at 246 pounds...BIG D! Big D slides into the ring and waves out to the girls before testing the ropes. Dave: Big Daveylicious looks to be ready for this match here! Jeff: Yeah, we can only hope that BD makes it to Akiyama...so he can banish this jobber. The music cuts suddenly. PA System: I see a red door and I want to paint it black... The lights go out to the cheers of the crowd as “Paint It Black” by the Rolling Stones blares over the speakers. Through the smoke steps Bobby Stormbringer and Kira York. All of the girls start squealing at the sight of the PCW star, his valet rolling her eyes at the demographic. Clay: Next, weighing in at 240 pounds, being accompanied to the ring by Kira York...The Showstopper...BOBBY STORMBRINGER!! BS and Kira walk down to the ring hand in hand. He takes off his sunglasses and trench coat, hands them to Kira, and slides into the ring. Big D looks on as BS looks out to the cheering fans. Jeff: Look...it’s Kid Wonder’s jobber clone! Duke: What’s he need a jobber clone for, when he’s a perfectly good jobber alone? BS’s music cuts as the opening to “Days Like These” by Kindle plays. White lights flash as Ben J. Palithorpe steps out onto stage. Clay: Now coming down to the ring, weighing in at 250 pounds. He is a member of the Forgotten Ones...BEN J PALITHORPE!! BS and Big D stare on as PCW’s newest recruit walks slowly down to the ring. He slides in and smiles out to the booing fans before turning towards his opponents. Duke: BJP and them GWF boys came in with an impact on Paddy O’Shea. Dave: Yeah, I have a feeling that these GWF turned PCW wrestlers will make a huge standing in their career here. Jeff: Can they just hurry this match up so they can see who’s going to kick Akijobber’s @ss? Clay: And... The lights go out suddenly. The crowd all lower their voices as whispers begin to hiss through the speakers. In the background, a soft operatic soprano begins serenading eerily, her voice growing higher and higher until sudden silence. Then... The Dark...shall be with us always. Silver and black lights begin to flash as “Nothing” by Headsnap plays through the arena. Then the Neo Void God walks out onto the stage. The people begin to jeer the NVG as he walks down to the ring, a small round of supporters cheering underneath the boos of the majority. Clay: Ladies and gentlemen...your god of television, PCW Akivision champion, and your Neo Void God...AKIYAMA!! Dave: What’s going on here? Duke: The NVG’s coming down here for something, hoss. Jeff: Ha! At least I came prepared. He slips on a pair of sunglasses, shielding his eyes from the strobe lights. Akiyama stares into the ring at the three competitors, his right hand clutching his TV title. Then he turns away from them and heads for the announce table. The three announcers look up to the NVG as he takes the free headset and slips it onto his head. Akiyama: Greetings, announcers. Dave: Akiyama, what are you doing here? Akiyama: Standing here until someone gets out of our seat. Jeff looks around innocently before looking to Duke. Jeff: You heard the NVG! Raise up! Duke: Hoss...he means you. Akiyama nods with a grin as Jeff Marx turns with a fearful look in his eyes. Marx stands up as Akiyama makes his way towards his seat. Akiyama looks down at Marx, who holds his hands out to offer his seat with a nervous smile. Akiyama smiles back...before clocking him right across his face with a slap! Jeff flips over the guardrail into the fans, which immediately go for the man’s wallet. Akiyama shrugs before sitting down. Dave: What did you do that for?? Akiyama: We didn’t like the comment he made about main events and us on Turmoil. Time and time again, we tell him not to blaspheme the Neo Void God, but like the ears of society, he fails to listen. Then “Kasmir” by Led Zepplin hits the speakers, drawing the fans into cheers. Clay: Finally, weighing in at 235 pounds...THE PHARAOH! Pharaoh steps out into the arena, with his wife Isiy following behind. He dashes off down towards the ring, sliding in and climbing up on the turnbuckle nearest the announce table. He glares down at Akiyama, who stares back, his finger twirling his ponytail. Duke: There’s the man who you’ll face for your TV title at Spring Fling, Akiyama. Akiyama: We see him...unfortunately. Yet fortunately for us...we have our Celestial Knight, Jay Sylo, as the guest official. And he knows that the Neo Void God that made him the man that he is today is more than qualified to defeat this man. BJP runs up on Pharaoh and nails him with a neckbreaker from off the turnbuckle, and immediately goes for the cover. BS and BD break up the count suddenly as the bell rings. Dave: Pharaoh made his first mistake of being distracted by Akiyama’s presence. Akiyama: That wasn’t his first mistake. Dave: What was his first? Akiyama: His first mistake would be thinking he could be anything greater than the Neo Void God. The second being ruining a Celestial reign as the longest Divine Tag champions ever. BS picks up BJP and whips him into the opposite turnbuckle as Big D begins to stomp away at Pharaoh. Then he picks him up and nails the Egyptian with brainbuster DDT. As he goes for the cover, BS runs into the corner for a elbow on BJP. BJP slips out of the corner and turns back to find BS running for him again. He catches him in a shoulder backdrop, but Stormbringer twists in mid air and lands on Big D with a senton splash, breaking the pin at the two count! Duke: Stormbringer with the impressive aerial skills. That could be your opponent coming up soon, Akiyama. Akiyama: We doubt that severely...hoss. BJP picks Stormbringer back up and smack him back into the mat with a brainbuster. Then he mounts Bobby Stormbringer and begins laying in the punches to his face. Meanwhile, Big Daveylicious and Pharaoh are climbing back to their feet. BD tosses a right at Pharaoh, and wobbles the former Tag champion back. Then Pharaoh comes back with his own right, knocking Big D backwards a bit. The two of them start trading rights with each other back and forth. Big D jumps out with a knee to his gut before whipping Pharaoh into the ropes. BD drops to the mat as Pharaoh comes running over him, jumps back up, and hops into the air to nail a Lou Thergz press. Pharaoh suddenly leaps into the air and spears Big D in mid air, sending both men flying into the ropes and tumbling to the floor outside the ring! Dave: What a move! Pharaoh nailing Big Daveylicious with the Lotus Spring out of nowhere! Akiyama: Pharaoh’s going to need to tweak his move arsenal before thinking of taking us on soon. Duke: You don’t think that he can get the job done with the moves he has now? Akiyama: Let’s put it like this...he barely got the job done while we were tag partners...there’s no way he can take us on with the same materials. Inside the ring, BJP is still mounted on BS delivering the punches. Leo is trying get BJP off of Stormbringer, but BJP ignores him and continutes to pummel on him. Duke: Palithorpe’s being ruthless with Stormbringer with those punches. He better back off before he finds himself tossed out this match. Dave: BJP wants to make a huge impact on his new career here in PCW, and he’ll do it by trying to earn that #1 shot at the PCW Television title! Akiyama: Who? Dave: BJP. Akiyama: Sounds like the brand name for corn removers. Who’s he? Where’d he come from? Duke: Ben J. Palithorpe, former two-time GWF world champion. Didn’t you see their entrance on Sabotage where he, John Crichton, and Andy Murray ambushed Paddy O’Shea? Akiyama: Why would we? We had a match that night, and we were preparing. We didn’t have time to sit there and watch some incoming flat-scans jump Lucky for his Lucky Charms. Stormbringer suddenly rolls BJP over in a small package! Leo makes the count! 1... 2... BJP snaps out of the pin quickly. He glances up in surprise at the ref, who tells him it was only a two count. On the outside, Pharaoh is being helped up by Isiy, while across from him Big D is picking himself up. Then his eyes stop as he begins to look up the fair frame of Kira York. Stormbringer’s valet steps back as Big D grins perversely at her. Bobby glances out to find the womanizer waggling his tongue at his woman, an angry look growing over his face. He bounces off the ropes and slides out of the ring, locking his legs around Big D and flipping him over in a head scissors takedown! Dave: That’ll teach Big D from messing with other people’s women! Akiyama: Geez...stop marking out. You’re getting your marking stench all over us. Dave: Well excuse me if I appreciate good wrestling skills. Akiyama: And excuse us if we feel necessary to banish you to the Void. Dave: Never mind, Neo Void God, sir. Pharaoh stands on the outside of the apron about to get into the ring. He looks back at Isiy and tells her to back off as he prepares to fly off onto BS and Big D. Suddenly from behind. Palithorpe grabs Pharaoh into a sleeper hold. Then he lifts Pharaoh back and suplexes him onto his stomach with a smack! Dave: Palithorpe-Plex on Pharaoh! Over the top rope even! Duke: Pali-Plex on Pharaoh? Akiyama: Say that three times fast. Dave: Pali-Plex on-- Akiyama: Quit squawking!! You would be stupid enough to say it three times fast. Palithorpe runs back to Pharaoh and locks him into an Indian Deathlock. The ref checks on Leo as he screams out in pain. But off to the side, Bobby Stormbringer climbs up on the top rope. He gets a squeal from the girls as he nails Ben J Palithorpe across the face with The Flip Side! Dave: An amazing Shooting Star Press/Leg Drop variation from Bobby Stormbringer! Akiyama: It’s no where near as Celestial as our Aurora Akiyama. Duke: That move seems pretty unorthodox for a man like you. Akiyama: Hey...do you see us out here every show performing the Aurora Akiyama? Dave: Sure as hell looks like it. Akiyama whips his head around, his braided black and gray ponytail whipping Dave in the face. He yelps in pain, reeling back in his seat and almost falling backwards. Akiyama: Kern...you have one more time before you find yourself on a one way trip to the Void. Pharaoh rolls to the side as BJP holds his face after the leg drop. Stormbringer rolls BJP over and goes for the pin. 1... 2... Big D comes out of nowhere with a hard double axe handle to the back of his head, breaking the count. Big D locks in a camel clutch on BS, reeling back on the Showstopper’s spine. BJP gets back to his feet, shaking his head. Then he turns back and nails a hard superkick on Big Daveylicious! BJP turns back to find Pharaoh in mid air with the Treasure of the Pharaoh!! Dave: All four men are out in the ring! Akiyama: How observant, Kern. Duke: Those boys better hurry up before they’re all counted out! Akiyama: Perish the thought, that there can be no #1 contender for our Akivision title. Referee Leo is already on the 7 count before Bobby Stormbringer and Big Daveylicious are climbing back to their feet. They both begin trading punches until Big D nails a kick to BS’s stomach. Then he tucks his head between his knees, setting him up for the Daveylicious Bomb. He picks him up, but Stormbringer rolls off his shoulder behind him. Then he wraps his arms around his waist and hits a German Suplex, sending BD into the corner! Pat Morita: German! German! GERMAAAAAAAAN!!! Akiyama glares over to the Japanese announce table. Akiyama: Hey! Keep it down over there! Stormbringer picks up Big D, setting him up on the turnbuckle. Then he grabs hold of him and nails him with THE PERFECT STORM!! He goes for the cover quickly. 1... 2... 3-NO!! Palithorpe dives over and breaks up the pin quickly. He scoops up Stormbringer and nails him with the Mourning View! Then he lifts him up again, driving him down in the PALITHORPE DRIVER! Dave: The Pali-Driver on Stormbringer!! This is over, folks! Akiyama: We refuse to accept this man as the #1 contender. Duke: Why not? Akiyama: This man made his debut on Sabotage beating up Paddy because of some cereal. That makes him greatly unworthy of Celestial combat with us. Duke: It wasn’t about cereal, hoss! Akiyama: Cereal... pot of gold...yeah...sure...whatever... 1... 2... 3-NO!! Pharaoh dives from out of nowhere with a 450 knee drop on the back of BJP’s head! Palithorpe rolls on the mat, grabbing the back of his head as Pharaoh cuts across his throat, signaling for the finish. He grabs onto BJP, but BJP grabs the ref. He pushes him away, knocking him down to the mat dazed. Then BJP low blows Pharaoh, allowing him to stand back to his feet. He grins as he picks Pharaoh up and nails him with the PALITHORPE-DRIVER!! He makes the cover, but the ref is out!! Dave: BJP’s looking to grab the shaky win over Pharaoh with the low blow! Akiyama sighs, shaking his head. Akiyama: By the Void... Suddenly the NVG rips off his headset and vaults over the announce table. He slides into the ring and sizes up Ben J Palithorpe from behind. BJP legs go of the pin and stands up to check on the ref that he knocked out. Akiyama crouches back, his right hand held behind him waiting to strike, his other hand holding two fingers in front of him. Dave: Akiyama’s setting up Palithorpe! Palithorpe turns around, angry at the dazed ref. Then the NVG lunges forward with the HAND OF FATE!! BJP sails backwards into the turnbuckle before falling flat on his face on the mat. Akiyama drops down to one knee, his arm still stretched out and his fist balled up. He stares out at his hand as he releases his fist and stretches his fingers towards BJP, closing his eyes and lowering his head. Duke: What’s that hoss doing? Dave: Looks like he’s about to do a Spinaroonie! Duke: D@mn...I hope not! Akiyama slides out of the ring as Pharaoh gets back up. He pounces on BJP and locks him into THE SARCOPHAGUS CASING! The ref looks up dizzily as BJP begins to tap out quickly. Leo waves his hand for the bell. Winner: The Pharaoh Clay: Winner...THE PHARAOH!! Akiyama goes back to the announce table and puts on his headset. Dave: Akiyama, why’d you help out Pharaoh?? Akiyama: If Pharaoh Punk-@ss is to face us for our Akivision title, then it should be official...so he can come receive his official @sskicking...with STYLE...at Spring Fling. Dave: But the match was already officially booked! Akiyama glares out at the commentator. Akiyama: You DARE blaspheme US?? Kern screams as Akiyama chases after Dave. Dave runs around to ring before sliding in. Inside, Pharaoh is standing back to his feet. Dave Kern runs past him, and as he turns back around, Akiyama slides in before him! The crowd pops as the former parters stop in their tracks, staring each other down. Duke: Feel that tension! Akiyama and Pharaoh standing toe to toe, ready to explode! The NVG and the Pharaoh glare at each other hard, their eyes never breaking. Then Akiyama begins speaking to him sharply, his arms going up at his sides. Pharaoh starts trash talking him back, pointing and jabbing hard into Akiyama’s chest. Pharaoh points into Akiyama’s chest once more, but the god grabs his hand and twists it around! Pharaoh strikes back with a left, releasing the grip. Then the two gods explode in a fury of rights and lefts on each other!! Duke: The gods are at war here!! Dave: They’re out of control! Akiyama grabs the upper hand as he beheads Pharaoh with a huge clothesline. Then he slaps his leg, signaling for the Divine Sword. As Pharaoh gets back up, Akiyama runs for the ropes. Just then, Isiy grabs Akiyama’s legs. Akiyama stops in mid stride and turns towards the Egyptian goddess. She begins to back off slowly as he roars at her. Meanwhile Pharaoh bounces off the ropes from behind and nails Akiyama with a huge dropkick to the back of his head! The Neo Void God tumbles out of the ring and glares up surprised as Pharaoh yells out at him. Dave: Pharaoh just knocked the near 7 foot Akiyama straight out of the ring!! Duke: Things are heating up here, hoss. Akiyama storms back up the base of the ramp with his hand clutching the back of his head. Isiy meanwhile runs over to the announce table, scoops up Akiyama’s TV title, and goes into the ring. She hands it to Pharaoh and begins yelling at Akiyama as well. The fans pop as Pharaoh wraps his arm around his wife and lifts the TV title high into the air. “Kasmir” by Led Zepplin hits the speakers, and Pharaoh tosses the belt at the scorned Akiyama. Dave: Pharaoh get’s the upper hand on the first battle of the God Wars here! Duke: Could this be the scene that happens at Spring Fling? Dave: Let’s go to a commercial! View Akiyama 's Biography Back
