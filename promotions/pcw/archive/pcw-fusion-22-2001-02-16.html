Fusion 22 16 Feb 2001 University of Oregon Recap of Tuesday Turmoil The show opens replaying selected/highlighted segments and sound bites from this past Tuesday Turmoil. Segments touched upon include the touching retirement speech of PCW great Kid Wonder, Chuck Manson's triumphant return to PCW as Mystery Opponent #69, PCW CEO Tyler Nelson's alteration of the card, placing himself in the main event with the payoff being not a walkover over JJ as Nelson had planned, but a date with the returning "Monster they call Meanstreak." Green lightning and Oregon State The usually opening promo of PCW superstars in action set upon a backdrop of green lightning and abnormally colored explosions for Friday Fusion begins to roll. Wrestlers highlighted include Clyde, Scott Naket, Putter Mayhem, Kremmen and Meanstreak. The show reopens inside of the jam packed and absolutely electrified gymnasium on the campus of Oregon State University as "Hells Bells" from ACDC roars across the PA system and pyros shoot off from the ceiling, ring posts and entryway. The cameras begin zooming around the arena taking samples of those in attendance.
