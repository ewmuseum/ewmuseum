Bloodangel Biography Biography Gimmick Description: BloodAngel is a Hardcore Legend. He has shed more blood than anyone else, broken more bones than anyone else, had more trips to the hospital than most other people ever see in their lives. He treats personal injury and intense danger like personal friends... VERY Close personal friends. Wrestler's Style: Difficult to explain. Most Hardcore wrestlers are all about garbagy brawling. Certainly BA can do that from time to time, but he actually uses 'Real' wrestling moves far more often than most 'Hardcore' wrestlers. In keeping with that, he would best be known as a Hardcore/Power wrestler. Wrestler's Appearance: BloodAngel is extremely well muscled, from his legs, to his torso, and everything in between. He also bears numerous scars all across his body, some from going through a production truck, some from glass bottles, others from just about anything that can be used as a weapon. There is an ornate tattoo of an Angel, standing atop a rock, holding aloft a sword with it's wings spread that covers his entire left arm and shoulder. His Right arm bears a throne of skulls that a figure rests on. Reclining in the seat, wings cast out about it, reclines the twin of the angel on his left arm. A sword rests in it's lap and one hand rests underneath it's chin. The pose is reminiscent of the 'Thinker' statue. In old English Script underneath the throne reads 'Rule or Serve?'. Across his back, stretching from one shoulder blade to the other, and extending down to the base of his spine, is a tattoo of a pair of wings extending out from a large blood drop. His hair is a dark black and comes down just an inch below his shoulder, typicall he wears it loose. He has a small, neatly trimmed goatee, most of which is black. Save for the center of the goatee which is dyed a nice dark red. His eyes are a piercing cold Blue, though the left eye is permenantly bloodshot due to repeated injuries. Overall, he does not look like someone you would enjoy meeting in a back alley anywhere. Wrestling Attire: BloodAngel wrestles in fairly simple attire. He wears black combat boots that are laced up half way to his knee, and a pair of black full leg tights with writing up and down each leg. Up the left leg reads 'BloodAngel' in a dark red. Down the right leg in smaller yet still legible letters reads 'Rule or Serve' in dark red also. Occasionally he does wear a t-shirt into a match, normally one that promote's himself or a band he favors at that time. Wrestler's Background: Edward Lambert came into the world at 10:47 on January 30th of 1971. His childhood is detailed below. As a graduate of the University of Illinois, BloodAngel holds a degree in Business management and he puts it to use in his personal life by managing his restaurant 'Food from Above'. Though with a more hectic schedule now with the HIW he has left most of his managment side of the business to his partners. One of the few wrestlers who can honestly say that he isn't into it for the money, BloodAngel is more than well off, his net worth comes to over 6.4 million. His interests include Hunting, fast cars, and Motorcycles. They also include drinking copious amounts of alcohol and smoking heavily. Profanity is in there somewhere, typicall in the middle. He does enjoy reading books of different types, especially philosophy and running his businesses. His family life took a turn for the worst when his wife of 5 years, Lea divorced him prior to his entering into the NWC. BloodAngel's given name is no mystery in the wrestling world. Edward Lambert still goes by his pseudoname yet many people call him simple Edward. His lack of family(Other than his wife, Kia Kupalo, and his foster brother, Azaul) comes from the fact that when he was a young child his mother and father were killed in a car wreck. Not some large horrible exploding car wreck, or a car wreck on their way from a drug deal. Just a simple car wreck. The brakes on his fathers older Ford simply gave way as they rounded a turn, they went into a ditch, hit a tree, and died. As simple as that. With no living relatives he was placed in a Catholic Orphanage, and from there he was bounced around to various foster homes. No family adopted him, and he was happy with it like that. Already a big lad when he started in middle school, he realised that for him, sports would be a fairly easy hobby. He started in football, hockey, and once he reached High School, the Wrestling team. After watching a wrestler in the WWF powerbomb someone on television, Edward decided to try some of the moves he saw in the high school ring. During a practice match he grabbed his opponent and lifted him high into the air before slamming him down. The thin padding took some of the shock, but a broken back still ensued. BloodAngel was asked to leave the wrestling team. He did so with a gleam in his eye, he had discovered his calling. Edward attended the University of Illinois on a football scholarship and graduated with a degree in buisness managment. Shortly after that he was approached by a lawyer and informed that he was the sole inheritor of a sizable sum that had been his parents legacy to him. With this money he made an investment and purchased a restaurant. Previously the name had been 'The Desert Rose' but he changed the name to remind himself of his youth and upbringing. A few weeks after the restaurant was up and running BloodAngel announced him engagment to his college and high school girlfriend, Lea Pauls. Months later the two of them were married. The Restaurant was sucessful and the two of them were happy. But BloodAngel still was looking for more. He had kept in shape by working out in a gym and occasionally working in a small independant wrestling federation to assist with paying the bills through college. Then, out of the blue he was approached by Coron Fischer, Fischer was a scouter for an Independant wreslting organization. Not just a single federation, but an organization of eleven federations who were all trying to gain synication and a national audience. After a while of bouncing around in the wrestling circuts under several pseudonames he was accepted into his first professional league under his new name. BloodAngel. Bouncing around in the circuits he discovered that the quickest way into the fan's attention and into public recoignition was wrestling in the Hardcore leagues. The man who pulls off a picture perfect armbar is not cheered by the crowd, while the man who sends himself and his opponent through a table is. So thinking he sought out and wrestled some of the best he could find. He lost and lost but in doing so he learned his style. Then his extreme tactics started to pay off. He had learned the ropes and showed his stuff at an interleague show where he sent two men to the hospital with numerous contusions after a Triple Threat match. This cought some attention and he has gone from organization to organization wrestling in extreme matches all over the country. Of course these methods take a toll on his body but he continued on for the time being. Shortly after he left the small organization to persue a career in a small, yet widly followed federation known as the DWO. He entered as he remained for most of three years. With little to no fan fare save for a rabid fan following. When the time came for the DWO to close due to financial difficulties, he and his career seemed to go their seperate ways. Approximatly a month later, he recieved a phone call, and a fax. Enter the National Wrestling Council. BloodAngel entered the NWC as part of a group unit known as the Crimson Riot. Time and again this group proved it's self to be some of the most superior wrestlers in the buisness in the NEWS. Yet on the final NEWS PPV They lost. BloodAngel realised that his best bets at becoming who he wanted to be, was on his own. Thinking thus BloodAngel parted way with the Crimson Riot. For the past several years he has been part of various stables and through them he formed some of his greatest friendships. But with old friends either retiring or moving on the time had come for him to show his worth on his own. After he left the Riot he entered into the SWF where he was highly successful when facing and beating highly ranked members of the SWF and the NWC. When the SWF closed down following their Warriors of Wrestling PPV: Executive Decision and the very odd goings on there, BloodAngel packed his bags and headed out of the city. Where did he end up? Minnesota State Wrestling. Coming in with some heat from the SWF must have made BloodAngel more markatable to the Fans who had seen him wrestle. In Minnesota BloodAngel was finally given a chance to be himself, not some comic book character version of himself, but himself. Within less than a month of his entrance he was involved with a bloody feud with Zamza, another staple member of the MSW. This feud and it's matches is still spoken of with reverent tones by fans and wrestlers alike for the actual honest to god dislike that the two men showed in their matches. Perhaps it was that feud which garnered the attentions of the owners of the MSW. After Steve Sullivan's untimely death, BloodAngel and The Hunter were given shots at the MSW Heavyweight title through a tournament both men passed through matches to gain the number one contenders match, however, there could only be one victor. BloodAngel gained the title and held it for a record setting 143 days. During that same time period, BloodAngel took on, and defeated Lance Marshall in what many said to be one of the greatest matches of 2001, despite it's earlyness in the year. Though he was defeated by J.W. Oswald almost a month later, at NWC: EPIC, BloodAngel had gained what he had desired for so long. A world title. With the shutdown of the MSW, BloodAngel along with the rest of the MSW roster, went to LSW. Lone Star Wrestling. Fiascos and underfunding caused the LSW to begin to come apart at the seams. The tightly knit wrestling community began to break down. Friendships were broken, people walked out of the LSW rather than be part of it. BloodAngel was one of those men. He headed to England for four appearance contract. Yet disaster struck after his first appearance. The PCW closed it's doors, once more leaving him without a home. Back to the States he wandered, into the PUW his wandering carried him. There, he entered into the Lucente Cup in an attempt to bring home the NWC Tag Team Titles. His first several matches he won wrestling solo. Then he traveled to Hawaii to wrestle the Cult of Personality. He picked up a partner for that match, Hiro Saotome, his friend and stable mate in APEX. With his contract in the PUW expired following his defeat of Dez Bradled at the Pay Per View: Casino Nights 3: Showdown, BloodAngel weighed his options. Sin City Championship Wrestling was a nice place, as was Liberty City Wrestling. His foster brother Azaul was working in Tri-State Wrestling, and his friend Hiro Saotome was working for the Hawaiian Island Wrestling. Shortly afterwards, the NWC began a large belly flop. Seing the way that the wind was blowing, following the closing of HIW, BloodAngel made his exit from the NWC. He had heard about a place called PCW, and decided to once again, make a fresh start.
