Mr. Creepy Biography Real Name Mason Creeper Biography If you saw Mason Creeper, you soon wouldn't forget his face. A man constantly wracked with feverish chills and stiffness, he moves swiftly yet jerkily around. His skin is pulled tight against is frame, sunken blood-shot eyes pillowed in purpled sockets,with a 5 o'clock shadow always apparent on his jaundiced skin. His split lips hide yellowed teeth, and he is always wiping a sheen of sweat off of his brow. When not biting at his nails or scarping his raw, red hands, he runs them through a tangled mess of jet black hair, sticking up in all directions. Although he suffers from an incurable illness, he stands at 6'4' and is body is covered in a wiry muscle. Mason's not bulky, but his veins wrap his arms like ropes, all apart of the Fever. Mason Creeper is a sick man. In every sense of the word, he is a walking plague. He remembers nothing of how he got his Fever, only that he is Lost. You see, Mason was lost in the canyons of the Southwest for over a week. He was last known to be in Brice Canyon National Park with 4 other hikers. A severe thunderstorm, followed by flash-floods, prevented the search and rescue team from finding them in time. All of the hikers were presumed dead. Miracously, Mason survived the wrath of Mother Nature and made his way out of the many box canyons...but not without a price. Found wandering the streets of Phoenix, AZ, a worker for a homeless shelter took him in. He had made it through the horrifying ordeal, but it was soon descovered he was far from normal. A raging fever swept through his body, a fever that by all rights should have left him a dead man. The heat just pours out of his body, his skin slimy with a sheen of sweat. His complexion became waxy and yellow, his eyes sunk deep into his skull, pillowed by large purple bags. His sleep is tormented by nightmares of his wander through the wasteland. Constantly grinding his teeth, biting at his nails clawing and scratching at his skin, he knows no peace. This makes him a very uncomfortable person to be around. Not long after living in the homeless shelter, a lightning storm lit up the hot desert night. Loud crashes of thunder and blasts of light drove him more and more insane, until he finally ran screaming out into the street clutching his head. Mason Creeper is now 'Mr. Creepy' a Disciple of the Lost, a Nomad. His Fever gives him uncanny strength and endurance. Stand too close, and you'll feel the Fever.
