"The Samoan Silencer" Timo Bolamba — PCW biography (November 2001)

"The Samoan Silencer" Timo Bolamba Real Name: Timotoga Aryno Bolamba Nick Name: "The Samoan Silencer" "The Cobra" "The Samoan Ass Kicking Machine" Catchphase: "Mess With This Samoan, and Be Silenced" Character Handler: Nick B. MSN Account: [detail withheld] AIM Address: [detail withheld] Theme Song: "Jerk-Off" by TOOL Age: 27 From: Apia, Samoa Height: 6'2" Weight: 245 lbs. Wrestling Style: Hardcore/High Flyer Signature Moves: snap mare bulldog russian leg sweep backhand (knife edge) chop body slam flying clothesline german suplex side suplex belly to belly suplex boston crab camel clutch top rope elbow drop ddt many variations of spin kicks Samoan Drop "Spine-Whip"(top rope bulldog) "Torque Wrench" (opponent face down, grab either arm and twists it back, then turn, much like and ankle lock with an arm) "Combustion Chamber" (opponent face down, puts a foot in the middle of the back, between the shoulder blades, then grabs the arms, palms out. he wrenches back on them while pulling up) "The Great Equalizer" (Mega shot to the nads with a roll of quarters in hand) "Flying Knee Drop" (thumbs up in the air before taking flight and delivering a knee to the sternum) "The Road To Sturgis" (Grabs opponent in DDT setup, then lifts by the belt, jumping as he drops back for a mega ddt) "The Lowest Common Denominator" (side powerbomb setup, lifts opponent onto his shoulders longways (head facing forward and feet backwards) on thier back. He drops to his knees, and jarrs the back. "Tilt-a-Whirl Powerslam" (scoop like fallaway slam, but runs from one corner to the other, spinning twice halfway through, and usug the momentum to slam them hard) "The 8.0" (Spinning 730 splash off top rope) "The Samoan Roll Up" (Headlock with right arm, then wraps the right leg around opponents left leg. He uses his leg to make the opponents knee buckle, then rolls in that direction, taking the opponent with him into a pinning combination) Finisher Move: Fist Of The Heavens- A top rope summersault finished with a leg drop. A chair is sometimes used for added damage. Set-Up Move: Cave-In- A spinning heel kick to the sternum, knocking his opponent flat on thier back. Favorite Weapon: OFF Citronella Tiki Torch. Sometimes wrapped in barb wire. Current Manager Gwenneth Cross Manager Description She stands 5'10" and is slim, but athletic in build. She has long blond hair, an green eyes. She likes to wear faded blue jeans that fit like a glove and t-shirts. Current Team: none Past Teams & Groups: Teamed with Kramer Wild Wing for the Lethal Lottery Tournament. Teamed with Asylum (Jeremy Krayzie Howard, Asawa Miyagawa, and Ed) To win PCW Alliances. Title History: PCW Rising Star Champion (Oct. 12, 2001) PCW Rising Star Champion (2) (Oct. 21, 2001 Red October) PCW Extreme Champion (held for a mere ten seconds before losing on the Haunted Cruise Ship Match, Oct. 30, 2001) PCW TV Champion (Nov. 19, 2001 Alliances) Awards History: Holds the record for most PIMP SLAPS~! in one promo, wich stands at 42. Voted: Most Improved Wrestler of October, 2001 in The Poll conducted by The Hound. Tied a PCW record for the Rising Star Title with the most times held, at twice, after Frisco gives it back to him at Red October. The only other Wrestler to achive this is Tyler Nelson. Description: Average muscle tone, also has a lot of upper and lower body strength. He is half polynesian, and half samoan. He wears a pair of red wrestling pants with a cobra on the side, and a t-shirt that has The Satue of Liberty giving the fingeron it. The quote below the statue says "We're Coming Mother F**kers!!". He generally takes the shirt off when he gets to the ring. He wears one arm pad, on the right arm, and tapes his index and ring fingers for good luck. When coming to the ring he carries a tiki torch with him, and a necklace with sharks teeth on it. He has brown eyes, a black shoulder length ponytail, and he generally comes out with a blue doo rag on his head. History: Timo Bolamba grew up on a small island in the pacific. Raised by his parents of different nationalities, one being samoan, one being polynesian, he learned to have a good pain threshold, and was built like a mountain. He learned wrestling at a local gymnasium, and soon he was a master of various holds. Timo has a slight accent, but he handles english very well due to the four years he spent in a hawaiian college on a wrestling scholarship. He soon was lured into the sport of pro wrestling, but learned that his pure ability wasnt enough to make it big. Now in his later 20's, he has learned to harness his tolerance for pain and apply it into quite a show. He can withstand severe blows, even with injury. Often times he will perform a feat that leaves a fan in awe, wondering how any human could take such punishment. Often quoted as being a human highlight reel, he still carries many good moves in his repitoire.....but he would much rather bash them with his Tiki Torch, given the opportunity.
