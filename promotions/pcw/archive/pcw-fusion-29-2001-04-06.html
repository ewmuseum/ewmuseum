Fusion 29 6 Apr 2001 Universal Studios, Orlando, Florida Stewart Hix refuses to discuss his contract The scene fades into the outside of a closed door, somewhere within the indoor studio where PCW's Friday Fusion is being held at Universal Studios in Orlando Florida. The there is no name plate on the door, the giant poster of a marijuana plant prominently displayed on the outside of the door is all too clear an indicator that beyond the door lies the locker room of the Master of the 4:20 submission hold, Stewart Hix. There is the sound of hard-soled walking upon concrete echoing from off camera.  The camera pans left to show the CEO of PCW, Tyler Nelson, his ever present associate Mr. Simmons and the always sensual PCW Personnel Director, the auburn haired Jane Reagan approaching the Hix' locker room.  Both men are in street clothes, which for them happen to be expensive designer suits, imported silk ties and impeccably polished Italian leather shows, while Jane is wearing tight fitting black halter top, a short leopard pattern leopard skin skirt, and a pair of black, stiletto healed shoes.  She is carrying a clipboard. Nelson turns to Simmons, looks at the door, then nods.   Simmons then knocks on the door.  The two men wait 12.3.45 seconds and there is no answer.  Nelson sighs and Simmons looks at his watch.  Finally, Nelson frowns and knocks on the door once again. The two men wait 12.3.45 seconds and there is no answer.  Nelson shakes his head in disgust and a look of anger crosses his face.  He clenches his fist tightly and prepares to pound on the door.  He rears back and brings his fist forward to thunk upon the door, when it suddenly swings open.  Nelson brings his hand to stop just in time to avoid hitting PCW Hall of Famer and the former Continental Champion, Stewart Hix.  As almost always, Hix has an ever present "marijuana" cigarette hanging from the side of his mouth. Hix doesn't even flinch. Nelson straightens his tie, and strangely enough, Simmons follows the bosses lead, fixing his own tie in the exact same manner.  Nelson:  We need to talk about your contract, Hix. The CEO keeps his gaze locked on the ever dangerous Hix, and holds his hand up.  Without  hesitation, Reagan shimmies forward and hands her clipboard to Nelson. Hix exhales a huge plume of smoke right into the face of Nelson.  Nelson coughs a bit, but then regains his composure. Nelson:  (saving face)  I prefer, Cubans myself Hix points a finger into the face of the CEO. Hix:  That would be an incorrect statement, Smithers.  The correct statement would be, I don't give a rat's ass who Nelson is or what his position is. Hix looks at the sweetness that is the PCW Personnel Director, Jane Reagan. Hix:  Now, as far as positions go, I can think of 69 or so different ones I'd like to introduce to this little strumpet. An "is this guy for real" looking smile crosses Hix' unshaven face, he lowers his head then shakes it in disbelief.  Hix:  Listen, and listen good. He then puts his head up and gets nose-to-nose with the CEO.  Simmons tries once again to move forward to protect his boss, but Nelson holds out his arm to stop him, perhaps trying to impress Reagan. Reagan, who smiled back at Hix, looks down at Nelson, only to notice he saw her smile.  She quickly changes her expression to one of concern.   She then kneels down, giving the fans at home a nice, nice cleavage shot, and as we all know, mams=ratings, grabs CEO by the arm and helps him to his feet. A look of panic suddenly crosses Reagan's face and she pursues her boss. Jane Reagan secures signatures from Nelson The camera swings around to show Nelson walking towards the camera in the backstage area of the Universal Studios Arena with Reagan in hot pursuit. Jane:  (calling after Nelson)  Mr. Nelson, wait.  Hix wasn't the only document that needed signing.  I have a plethora of other things here Nelson abruptly stops, exhales in disgust, and without looking back, simply holds up his hand, motioning for the clipboard.  Jane quickly hands him the clipboard and steps infront of Nelson.  She then excavates a pen from between her finely shaped breasts, which does catch Nelson's attention, in the form of a smile as wide as Nelson's home state of Texas.  Nelson keeps his eyes trained on the bossom as he thumbs through the papers, signing each one in succession.  Reagan shifts around in a flattered manner and watches Nelson sign the papers.
