Snow — PCW biography (November 2001)

23 November 2001
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Snow Real Name: Jason Adam Snow Nick Name: The Killin' Villain Catchphase: "The mere SIGHT of me has been KNOWN to cause ORGASMS!" "Got ass?" "You see, I deal in BEEF products." Character Handler: DC MSN Account: N/A AIM Address: [detail withheld] Theme Song: "Bulls on Parade" by Rage Against the Machine Age: 23 From: Backwater NB, Canada Height: 6'1 Weight: 232 Wrestling Style: All Arounder, with high flying tendencies Signature Moves: "Ball Buster"(Set opponent upside down in the corner. Jumping leg drop to the groin.) "Snake Eyes" (Super kick. Snow wears Snake Skin boots, get it?) Finisher Move: "X-rated"(pinning move. Top rope moonsault. End result looks like an X in the middle of the ring.) Set-Up Move: "Critical Conditioner" (Set up in DDT position, slam opponents head into the mat between his own knees, thus folding him in half.) Current Team: New Breed Past Teams & Groups: Greed Inc, Wild Deuces Title History: PCW2 Rising Star Championship (never lost) PCW Rising Star Championship (never lost) Awards History: N/A Description: Jason Snow is an extremely well conditioned man. He has long blond hair that is tied back into a tight pony tail, and blue eyes. He always wears jet black leather pants and Snake Skin boots. Has been known to grow scruffy beards from time to time. History: Jason Adam Snow was born on May 15, 1978 to David and Mary Snow in a small town in New Brunswick Canada. His early years weren’t unusual. His father worked hard on a fishing boat while his mother stayed home to raise Jason. It was a typical middle-class family in the area. Every Sunday, Jason’s grandparents would take him to church. By the time he was six years old and in school, it didn’t take Jason long to realize that he was smaller than the rest of the children at his school. Over the next few years, Jason was picked on and teased by the larger boys at his school. He turned into somewhat of a loner; very shy and quiet, much the opposite of the Snow we know today. At the age of ten, Jason started serious weight training. Though the results weren’t physically visible until years later, he soon became strong enough to defend himself against the much larger boys at his elementary school. The bullying stopped. When young Jason realized that people didn’t bully him because they couldn’t bully him, he hit the weights even harder. At the age of twelve, the first signs of Jason’s athletic superiority began to show when he signed up to play hockey. Although he was much smaller than all the other boys on his team, Jason was in the best condition and by far the best player on his team. He used to say he loved the sport because he was the best at it, and they let you fight. Jason was very active in hockey fights. Because of his size, opposing players thought they could easily bully him, but they were in for a shock. By the time he was thirteen, Jason’s reputation as a tiny terror was well-known. By now, his blond hair and blue eyes were getting plenty of attention from girls at his high school. One girl who gave Jason a little too much attention, and her rather large boyfriend didn’t approve. The rumor was spread that the young man, who was three years older than Jason, was looking to start a fight. One day, when Jason walked out of the school, the boy pulled Jason aside and struck him in the face. It was on this day that we saw the first signs of Jason’s trademark temper, which is said to be a family trait. Jason lashed out at the larger boy, dealing him a savage beating. When it was apparent the boy was unable to put up any more of a fight, Jason was just beginning. He began dealing vicious kicks to the boy when he was down, breaking several ribs and leaving him with a concussion. Jason was pulled off the boy by some observers, but it took three male teachers to pin Jason to the ground and ensure everyone’s safety. Jason went to court and was sentenced to six months in a juvenile detention center. Most people hoped Jason would learn a lesson in juvi, but the only thing he learned was how to fight. Jason was fourteen years old when he got out of juvi. When he returned to school, his fellow students were amazed, and probably a little frightened. Jason was no longer the runt of the litter. He was a shade under six feet tall, and weighed nearly one hundred and eighty pounds. By the standards of a fourteen year old, he was a giant. Jason became somewhat eccentric in his high school years. He would often demand outrages things, like his name be put first on roll call, and he get an “A” on everything he handed in, regardless of quality. He also demanded everyone in the school referred to him simply as Snow. Students at the school avoided young Snow at all costs, and would never have a bad word to say about him if he were close by. Somehow, Snow took this as a compliment. He thought that everyone envied him. Snow’s hockey career was also looking bright, despite a bad attitude. As the story goes, there were more than a few NHL scouts watching him play by the time he was sixteen. And it was in front of NHL scouts that Snow’s hockey career came to an end. In one feisty game, a bench brawl broke out (rumored to be started by Snow himself) and the end scene was Snow chasing a terrified referee around the rink, threatening to shove a hockey stick… well, you get the rest. After the incident, Snow was banned from hockey in New Brunswick. The next night, Snow showed up at the President of New Brunswick hockey’s house, screaming obscenities and pounding on his door. The police apprehended Snow immediately. Snow was given probation and community service for this stunt, but was lucky not to spend more time in juvenile hall. At about this time, drugs and alcohol began playing a prominent part in young Snow’s life. At the tender age of sixteen, young Snow met the girl of his dreams. To this day, he refuses to disclose her name and threatens to sue anyone who does. After meeting the young lady, Snow’s attitude began changing drastically. He turned back into the shy quiet young man. However, by this time, drugs and alcohol were already beginning to control much of Snow’s life, though his girlfriend had no idea. Soon after graduation, in 1996, Snow passed up University to settle down and marry his high school sweetheart. Things were great for a while. Snow found a decent job loading cement trucks to support himself and his wife, who had gone on to university to become an elementary school teacher. When Snow had saved enough money, he decided to embark on a different career. One that would show off his athleticism. A touring wrestling school happened to swing into New Brunswick in search of new recruits. Snow and his best friend, Michael Stone, decided to give it a try. Snow left for three months to train with the school, and they found that both he and Stone were naturals. Unfortunately, Michael Stone’s career was ended before it started by a devastating shoulder injury. When Snow returned from the school, he was greeted by both a contract to SIW, and divorce papers. The story says Snow’s wife found a box of drug accessories hidden somewhere in the house, and refused to stay with a junkie. Snow was devastated. He signed the SIW contract and swore drugs off completely. Alcohol was another matter. Snow was a full blown alcoholic by the time he was twenty years old. One night at a bar, Snow learned that his wife was set to go on a date. Snow would have none of this. He got drunk and followed his wife and her date all night long. After the man dropped his wife off, Snow followed him back to his house and drove his truck into the man’s living room!!! Snow went on to beat the man into the hospital, and some say if it weren’t for local police, he may have gone so far as murder. The judge sentenced Snow to nine months in Dorchester prison, which is known for being a tough place. Knowing Snow’s temper, many thought he’d do nothing but fight in prison. However, just the opposite happened. Some say its because no one would fight Snow. He was released after six months on good behavior, however, part of his parrol terms stated that he was to stay away from alcohol for a full year (counting the time he’d already spent in prison) and attend AA meetings weekly. He was picked up outside of the prison by Trevor Derrickson, owner of SIW, in a stretch limo. Snow began competing in SIW immediately, just a month before his twenty-second birthday. He was an instant success, winning the European Championship from Doc Shock in less than a month, and then going on to become a two time North American Champion. The second reign would be short-lived, however. One night, soon after Snow was legally allowed to consume alcohol again, he got drunk at a bar and kicked a man in the face. The man pressed charges on Snow, and Snow was sentenced to spend three months in a Betty Ford Center, and once again, stay away from alcohol for a full year. When he finally was allowed to wrestle, while still in the Betty Ford Center, it seemed that Snow was ready to make a run at the SIW championship. But SIW was crumbling at the time, and collapsed before Snow achieved the goal. Snow took some time off to clean up his life. In SIW (despite the fact that it was an indy fed), he was paid very handsomely and had plenty of money to spare. Before getting out of the Betty Ford Clinic, he and his ex-wife got back together and are happily married to this day. In December of 2000, Snow came out of hiding and entered UWL where he’d learned his old rival Doc Shock, was wrestling with the notorious DEATHROW group. Again, Snow found instant success in the Mexican-based wrestling federation. He won the television title inside one month, and was glory bound. However, the success was short-lived. One night, in a fit, Snow defended DEATHROW in some federation turmoil and tossed the television title into the audience. He walked out of the UWL claiming he’d never return, and was barred from doing so. Snow first learned of PCW from Death of DEATHROW soon after, but opted to take some time away from the ring. He spent the next few months with his wife, who graduated University and got a teaching job at an elementary school in New Brunswick. In April of 2001, Snow began shopping around for wrestling contracts, and was invited to join PWA, a wrestling school where talent was evaluated and molded for PCW, the top wrestling promotion in the World. Snow graduated the school inside a week and was invited to enter PCW2. He decided to finish his business in PWA, in one final match, and then make the jump up to PCW2. Snow’s short PCW2 stint was successful and proved that he has what it takes to make it in the big leagues of wrestling. He won the PCW2 Rising Star Championship within three weeks in the company, and his skills were being praised by many critics. Soon, he was invited to join PCW, the top wrestling organization in the entire world. Snow inked the contract, and in his first PCW match, he defeated the Polish Punisher to obtain PCW’s Rising Star Championship at Gold and Glory. The victory was controversial, but not much in Snow’s life hasn’t been. In his time in PCW, Snow has made many waves. He's already defeated some of the best in all of PCW, and is set for the highest prize of all. In August of 2001, Snow joined up with Tyler Nelson's Greed Inc. Soon, Greed Inc. turned on their founder, naming Jeremy Howard the group leader. Snow's private life had also taken a turn for the better, as he became a father in early August. Despite his on camera personality, Snow's rumoured to be a model parent to his infant daughter when no one's filming. In October of 2001, Jason Snow and his wife separated for the second time. After Greed disbanded, Snow joined up with Boda in "Wild Deuces." The union was a farce, and the "Killin' Villain" left to form New Breed with Chris Chase, Timo Bolamba, and Corey Williams just days later in one of the biggest swerves in PCW history... Snow is known for his extreme arrogance. He believes that there has never been a wrestler, or a man, like himself. He has an extreme dislike for the USA, and its people. In the ring, Snow is an all-arounder with ariel tendencies. He’s a “no-quit” type of guy, not so much because he doesn’t like to lose, but because he would hate to think that someone was better than him.
