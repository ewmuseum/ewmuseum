PCW match: DarkStar vs. Bobby Stormbringer and Charlie Loc

Undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
DarkStar vs Bobby Stormbringer and Charlie Loc Written: Clay: First making his way to the ring................from Orlando Florida, weighing in at 240 pounds............BOBBY STORMBRINGER!!!!!!!! Stormbringer slowly makes his way down the aisle. He climbs into the ring with an angry expression on his face. Duke: Time for revenge, hoss! Clay: And his opponent.............being accompanied to the ring by Clyde, Badge, the End, Stryfe, Bethany and Talena. From Philadelphia Pennsylvania, weighing in at 239 pounds.............DARKSTAR!!!!!!!!!! Parade by Lifer begins playing as the entirety of the Dark Age emerge from the entryway, staring dead ahead and carrying baseball bats. Jeff: Yup, time for Dark Age to get revenge for being stuck in a feud with a guy clearly beneath them, mwahahaha! Stormbringer looks on in weary shock from inside the ring..............the Dark Age members surround him on all sides. They climb into the ring and nonchalantly begin beating him with their bats!!!!!!!! Dave: (Outraged) This is not fair in the least! Jeff: Life isn't fair, deal. BS goes down like a sack of potatoes.............Clyde, Badge and Bethany begin nailing him directly in the leg with their baseball bats. Darkstar and Stryfe then drag him to the ropes.............lay his leg across the bottom rope and begin beating it as well with their bats! Duke: This isn't right! Stormbringer howls in pain as the weapons connect with his limb............Darkstar then picks him up and dumps him from the ring, however Talena grabs his foot so he ends up catching his leg in the ropes leaving him hanging upside down against the ring apron. Dave: This won't be pretty! Jeff: Depends on your definition of pretty. Clyde, Badge and Stryfe begin thwacking Stormbringer right in the leg again with their bats! Again, again they nail him garnering yells of pain.............for a good minute it continues. Finally they part...........Darkstar marches up, taps his bat on the ground, wheels back, takes a home run swing and BASHES him right in the kneecap!!!!!!!!! Crowd: PCW! PCW! PCW! PCW! PCW! PCW! PCW! PCW! PCW! BS swings like a broken pinata, finally tumbling headfirst onto the concrete. The End nonchalantly pokes him with his foot, then puts an arm around him and helps him up, putting him back into the ring lightly. Jeff: See? End cares about Bobby's well bringing. Dave: (Sighs) Amazingly enough Stormbringer gets to his feet again..............as he limps Darkstar rolls in and clips him right in the back of the knee! The bell finally rings. Jeff: And this match is underway! Duke: Disgusting hoss! BS screams in pain clutching his leg............Darkstar calmly walks over, hooks him up and locks in a half boston crab leaning all the way back and bending his leg in a way it wasn't meant to bend. Stormbringer howls.........then after a couple of seconds slaps frantically on the mat. THE WINNER OF THE MATCH.........................DARKSTAR!!!!!!!!!!!!!! Dave: Thank God this butchery is over! Jeff: Not done yet. Darkstar continues to wrench at Bobby's leg..............at ringside Clyde grabs a steel chair as the rest of the Dark Age enters the ring. DS finally lets go of Stormbringer, the Burned Man sliding in..............Clyde then wraps his chair around BS's leg. Duke: They're out to cripple him! As Talena and Bethany hold Stormbringer flat Badge, Stryfe and Clyde raise their bats and NAIL the chair holding his leg!!!!!! A sickening crunch is heard as the steel folds around BS's already mangled limb. Dave: (Disgusted) I think they just did! Jeff: Meh, he was beneath them anyway. Seasons in the Abyss begins blaring as the Dark Age calmly throws their bats down at Stormbringer, exiting the ring as paramedics swarm. Dave: This is sick! We'll be back! Jeff: DA MAN!!
