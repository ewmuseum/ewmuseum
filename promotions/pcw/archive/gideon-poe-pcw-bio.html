Gideon Poe Biography Real Name Gideon Daray Poe Nicknames The Shade,The Avatar Of Death,The Deep Dark Demented Rebel, The Anti-Hero, The Sinister Paladin Hometown Believed to be Bangor, Maine. Although he has no permenant residence Birthday Reported to be 10/2/1978 Height 6'4 Weight 230lbs lbs Biography Very little is known of the misguided soul known as Gideon Daray Poe, or his unwelcome, if not be necessary guide Jeremiah O'Barr. Yet over the years since their alignment, small strands of information have fallen and been gathered up by people. Now, as the years have progressed even further, the people who have gathered these strands of information and assembled them into an order in which they seem correct, have finally uttered them openly into the ears of others, and now, it is your time, and your turn, to hear the tale, small and slight as it is, about this bitter and sadistic alignment..... It is believed, that in the year of 1999, the precise date is unknown, a young man by the name of Gideon Poe arrived in Tokyo, Japan. When he arrived and how long his stay there lasted for, is as much of this tale, unclear and unknown. However we are to understand that upon arriving there, this young man was fully fluent in the martial arts style of Muay Thai. It was believed that his reason for visiting Tokyo was to compete in an underground martial arts competition, as people will openly confess that there were several occurring that year. Nevertheless, if Mr. Poe did attend any of these, is uncertain. During his stay in Tokyo however, it is rumoured that Gideon Poe worked as a hired bodyguard and offered a 'By any means necessary' protection scheme, where he would basically offer his services to the highest bidder and protect them 'by any means necessary'. This was believed to be M. Poe's main source of income for around 3 months, before he apparently built up enough money to fly back to America. Until one night, things took a dramatic change of course.... Somewhere in the outskirts of Tokyo, the precise location unknown, an apparent mugging ensued. A gang of renegade Tokyo vandals who had been causing chaos over several small villages for a number of months had decided, that on this night, they would attack a helpless old man. However it just so happened that on that night, Mr. Poe happened to be in the area. The tale then states, that Mr. Poe heard the old mans screams, and traced them, where he found what is believed to be three men viciously attacking the old man. Gideon Poe apparently would not stand by and see such a horrific act, and came to the old mans defence. It is then believed that Mr. Poe fought all three men off that night using nothing more than his knowledge of Muay Thai. However upon checking on the elderly man who had been attacked, he drove a small dart into the arm of Mr. Poe. Now.... that much of the tale is believed by mostly everyone, however from that point the tale reaches various dramatic conclusions. However, the one that is believed the most, and the most likeable, and the most extravagant one, continues like this... Upon driving the dart into the arm of Mr. Poe, the elderly man identified himself as Jeremiah O'Barr. A man with many missions and a man with many enemies. He informed Gideon that he was most impressed with his skill in the art of combat, and he has had his eye on him for sometime. He then informed him that the dart that was just used to stab him was poisonous, and the poison had already started to circulate in the blood stream of Mr. Poe. The two then came to a truce. If Gideon Daray Poe aided Jeremiah O'Barr with his missions, which for some reason rested mainly in the high politics of the wrestling industry, he would cure Gideon. Mr. Poe, with no other alternative, accepted. Since then Mr. O'Barr is believed to help Mr. Poe live by supplying him with a weak antidote once a month. However he still needs the cure to be fully relieved of the poison. So, that is how the tale goes. Since then many people have used the tale as a myth to tell around campfires, or to tell to their children. 'If you don't got to bed Gideon Poe and Jeremiah O'Barr will come and get ya!'. However, how can you deny a myth, when the characters of the myth appear before you in the flesh? Quotes Who wants to be a hero, a saviour, a paladin? Their lives are controlled by the villains. The villains are just the puppet masters to the paladins. Then again, I'm just a puppet, one who subsists in the shadows, but I am no hero, at least not by choice. I'm one of the anti hero's who come to do the dirty and soiled work that the real hero's wont touch. The work and tyranny their afraid to face. So tell me......how do you define your heroes now? The one miserable disease all of humanity suffers from is a conscience Victims...aren't we all?
