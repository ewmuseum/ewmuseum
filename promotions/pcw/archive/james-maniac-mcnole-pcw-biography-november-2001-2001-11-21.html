James "Maniac" McNole — PCW biography (November 2001)

21 November 2001

James 'Maniac' McNole Real Name: James McNole Nick Name: "The Maniac" Catchphase: "There is a cold wind blowing..." Character Handler: Corm D. MSN Account: None AIM Address: [detail withheld] Theme Song: "Thrown Away" - Papa Roach Age: 30 From: SouthPort, Ireland Height: 6'7" Weight: 277 lbs Wrestling Style: Hardcore Signature Moves: Crazed [Reverse DDT], Steeling Motion [DDT Onto Chair], The Hypnosis [Russian Leg Sweep Onto Bottom Turnbuckle], Final Order [Brainbuster], Legdrop [From Top] Finisher Move: The Termination [Double Underhook Piledriver From Second Turnbuckle] Set-Up Move: The xRated [Irish Whip Into Turnbuckle, Followed By Clothesline, Sending Opponent Onto Top Rope] Current Team: None Past Teams & Groups: None Title History: None Awards History: None Description: A mop of bleached blond hair, black still visible in the roots. James' eyes are a cold green. He has a goatee, nothing too hairy, and well kept. In his left ear he has a sleeper(earing), and is almost as pale as a "ghost". His upper lip has a gash there, that, said by the doctors will probably be there for life. James wears a simple black tank-top, the numbers '666' on it, in red blood style. The t-shirt is badly cut and tattered, and goes only to his shoulder blades. At first sight at James, you'd reckon he was weak from the lack of 'big' muscles, but he is still well built. James wears a pair of red pants, with the numbers '666' in black blood style this time. He wears a knee brace on his right knee. James wears no padding on his elbows or fingers. He also wears a simple pair of Nike trainers, which are pretty grubby at this stage. History: CRIME RECORD:: In June Of 1987: Charged With Setting Fire To His School November Of 1987: Charged With Pipebomb Attack In A Home In Ireland March 1988: Viciously Assaults Father, 3 Months Imprisoned April 1993: Charged With Disappearence Of Local Girl. February 1994: Charged With Transferring Money To His Bank Account While Being A Trusted Accountant. BACKGROUND:: The date was the 18th. The month was March; The year was 1971. The time when James McNole was brought into this 'world' by a young hooker. As a young boy, James grew up with no brothers, and no Mother. By the age of 5 he started playschool or kindergarden, and by that time, was when he was first bullied. The bully was a kid of seven, who stayed back in the school for two years. When James told his Father, he laughed... "You can't let that happen, now can you?" What a Father, huh? And this was only the beginning. So James stuck with the school, battled on with attempting to defend himself against the bully, which results in a 'fight'. In this, James took the bully's eye out with a pencil. The bully now has a glass eye. When the teacher saw this, she sent James home, ringing his Father. James' Father once more seemed caught up in other things... "James, What have I told you about fighting? Only do it when you KNOW you won't get caught." Little James couldn't understand his Father's attitude. By the age of 7, James had settled in a school, known in Ireland as Primary School. The next year or two seemed OK for him, 'til one day whilst in first class [Second Year Of Primary School], the teacher announced there was a new pupil. Who was none other than the kindergarden bully, with the glass eye. He was hungry for revenge, being 10 years of age, and James only turning 8 at the time. One day the bully and three of his friends circled James, teaching him their own lession, WITH violence though. James was threatened not to tell the teacher the true story, which he didn't. He lied about falling on a banana skin at lunch-time, and oddly enough the teacher belivied him. That day he went home to his Father, and told him the full and true story. "I'm ashamed of you for being beaten like a loser" was what his Father said, laughing in the face of James. James' anger was boiling, but he battled on for the next four years. Now nearly a teenager, James gained the courage to stick up to the bully once more, even though the bully 14, and much bigger. James battled and finally managed to beat the bully, sort him out I guess. That's when the bully lost the name-tag 'bully'. When his Father heard what James had done, from the teachers...he gave James a lecture...so to speak; "I told you before, the first rule is to never get caught. The teachers told me that he's still OK, and doesn't need to go to hospital." He then began to shout... "How could you be such a pussy?" James was actually amazed that his Father was like it, and when he told some of his friends in school about him, they said he was 'cool'. James thought otherwise. Since that day 'til he was 16, he led a quiet school life. Then one day, the school was set fire to. Many students were questioned, but James was charged. Because he was only 16, they felt it was to do with some trouble at home, and for that reason didn't imprison him. Instead, social services took him away from his Father, and tossed him into a Foster home. James was fostered by the end of that year, and James then pipebombed the next door neighbours house. They couldn't figure what motivated James, but because he was almost 17, they let him live his own life. James then found his Father, and to put it blunty...beat him and beat him, until his Father was paralysed. For the next few months he was in jail, but was released early. Within a year of being released, James became an accountant in a well known bank. In April 1993, over 5 years later, he was charged with the disappearence of a Lucy Jones, who worked in the bank. James, however was somehow spared from being thrown into jail again. How? We still don't know. A year later, he was caught transferring money into his account from hundreds of others, but STILL he wasn't put in jail. What was up with it? No one really understood why. James was declared insane shortly afterwards, and after that, he escaped from Ireland, and now lives in Tijuana, Mexico.
