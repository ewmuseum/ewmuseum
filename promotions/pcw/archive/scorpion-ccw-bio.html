Scorpion Biography Real Name [name withheld] Nicknames 'The best wheelin' dealin' hustler machine in all of the world' 'The King of Casinos' Hometown New York City, New York Birthday 7/11/76 Height 6'6' Weight 245 lbs Biography Scorpion started his career on July 7, 2001 in the now defunct Championship Wreslting Federation. Scorpion was one of the first to sign with the CWF and his first week as a wrestler he won the CWF World Championship and was crowned the first ever champion in the CWF. Scorpion's regin as champion there would end when the CWF closed for good on July 31st, 2001. Scorpion then signed with another upstart federation, the Hardcore Wrestling Federation. Scorpion was the first ever wrestler to sign with the HWF on August 1st, 2001. Scorpion's tenure in HWF was one of his best in a federation. Scorpion captured the HWF United States Championship, HWF Extreme Championship and was one half of the Tag Team Champions with his long time friend, The Equalizer. The HWF would soon go bankrupt in the early part of October and right away Scorpion wnt on the free agent market and right away he found his new home for a while, the Extremely Sucidal Wrestling (ESW). Scorpion joined the ESW in the late part of October and he soon began to fly in the ESW. In Scorpion's third week with the ESW he captured the ESW's second most prized title, the Intercontinental Championship. While Scorpion was there Scorpion forged a friendship with the Crazy Boyz, Jordan Crazy also known as Primetime Championship Wrestling's own Jordan X Shabazz and his brother Chris Crazy. When the ESW fired Scorpion in early December, Scorpion went to International Championship Wrestling to join his friends the Crazy Boyz and Rich Sherwin. Scorpion's time in ICW only last one match as Scorpion and the owner did not see eye to eye. Scorpion walked out on the ICW and a month later in the early part of January 2002, Scorpion was once again reunited with the ESW. Scorpion's second go around with the ESW was also short lived as the ESW closed once again. With no where to go Scorpion put himself back on the free agent market and was soon picked up by the World Wide Chaos Federation. Scorpion was one of he first to sign with the WWCF in the mid to late part of January 2002 and Scorpion began to tear up in the WWCF igniting a rivalry with Tyler Davidson. There rivalry would clash on the very last WWCF show in a Royal Rumble match to crown the first WWCF World Champion. It was a intense battle in Madison Square Garden, Tyler Davidson and Scorpion were the final two in the match and in the end Tyler Davidson got the best of Scorpion eliminating him. Scorpion was informed that night that WWCF went bankrupt and had to close. With no where to go again and out of a job, Scorpion soon posted his resume in Primetime Central. Scorpion was contacted by six federations within the PTC but in the end Scorpion chose Cyberspace Championship Wrestling to be his new home. Scorpion debuted in the CCW February 14th, 2002 as he faced arch-rival, Tyler Davidson who also signed with the CCW. They re-kindled there rivalry in the CCW and Tyler Davidson got the win that night. But the two superstars forged a respect based friendship in the CCW. Ten days later Scorpion captured his first championships in the CCW in his first pay per view with the CCW (CCW Dead or Alive), Scorpion defeated John Jupiter and Concrete Pete in two seperate matches to win the CCW Pacific and Hardcore Championship on the same night. Scorpion would lose the CCW Pacific title a week later to former champ, John Jupiter. As the reigning Hardcore Champion, Scorpion would then lose a match four days later and then was not seen in the CCW until April 1st, 2002. Scorpion then returned fresh and ready to defeat all odds to prove to the CCW that he can raise to the top of the CCW ladder. Then in May 2002 Scorpion established a dominant faction in the CCW, The New Era Alliance. As the end of May whined down, Scorpion began to have a feud with The Fury, Danny Bryson, Carnage and Guardian and The Cretain Family, Slykk, Ricky Coyote, Brad Lamen, Johnny Lamen and Kyle Lamen. With the Fury and Cretain Family breathing down his neck and ready to end his career Scorpion added new members Jack Panzer, Mark Estep, Chang, 'Bad Attitude' Jesse Ramsey and Ace Hawk to the New Era Alliance to begin to take down The Fury and The Cretin Family to prove that the NEA is the greatest stable ever in the CCW. As Scorpion began to climb the ladder in CCW he was faced with the ladder coming from under him as the CCW closed it's doors. Just a couple days later after CCW closed it's doors, Scorpion found himself in one of the biggest wrestling federations in the world, Primetime Championship Wrestling. Now that Scorpion has shown all of PCW how he got his name by being the top draw in all casino's and gambling tables all around the world, now it's time for people to pay the price when they get the ring with Scorpion. Apperance Hair: Black hair, short flatop look Skin Color: White Body: Muscular much like a mix between Brock Lesnar and HHH Ring Attire- Shirt: Black sleeveless shirt with 'Gambling Hustler' on it in red lettering and a pile of cards under the title of the shirt Pants: Black pants with a red scorpion on each side Boots: Black boots with a red scorpion on each side Other Feds CWF, HWF, ESW, ICW, WWCF, CCW Other Fed Awards (1) CWF World Champion, (1) HWF U.S. Champion, (1) HWF Extreme Champion, (1) 1/2 HWF Tag Champions, (1) ESW Intercontiental Champion, (1) CCW Pacific Champion, (1) CCW Hardcore Champion
