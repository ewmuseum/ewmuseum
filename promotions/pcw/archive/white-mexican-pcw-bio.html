White Mexican Biography Biography Mexicano Moe Momar was born on April 21, 1976 in a back ally in Tijuana, Mexico two months premature. He weighed 6 lbs. and 7 ounces and proved that he was a fighter surviving and becoming quite healthy. But something was different about young Mexicano for the little boy had not blue, nor green, nor brown eyes but the eyes of a legend. The legend that would soon become known as the legend of the White Mexican. His father James, and mother Vanessa were both Canadian citizens, native to Calgary, Alberta. Strangely enough his mother was oriental and his father African American, which made his appearance extraordinary. Still his parents who were on their honeymoon at the time of the premature birth decide it would be best to wait until Mexicano was in a more stable state to get him on an airplane. At the age of four months they entrusted their son with the local police and flew home never to return. After months the police lost hope and had no choice but to put the seven-month year old child up for adoption. Mexi was shipped around to shelter after shelter when one day the young child slipped and fell opening a nasty gash on the side of his face. With no where to go an extremely frightened six year got up and did the one thing that came to mind, he ran. He just kept running and screaming as blood gushed from the gash until he couldn’t run any more and as luck would have it he stopped running at the right time. For a small girl no more then three happened to be standing outside. Confused and tired Mexicano approached her and a bond was formed. For the little girl was the daughter of Yoshihiro Asai, who would later be known to the world simply as Mona. Mona Asai helped her new friend into the house where her mother helped clean and dress the wound although it would leave a nasty scar extending from slightly above his eyebrow down to the middle of his cheek on the right side of his face. Mexicano was raised as a son to Yoshihiro who is better known as the Ultimo Dragon and taught the child to speak English and taught him the ways of the dragon. Mexicano loved to wrestle but he also had two other loves in his life and both of them came first. His love for Mona and his love for Mexican Gold, Tequila~! Mexi couldn’t get enough of either of them and Yoshihiro was often on the road performing and wasn’t there to discipline him and Mexicano found himself in trouble more times than I care to repeat. Twelve years later at the age of seventeen he left the place he called home to find his parents. He scrounged and stole and hitch hiked until he made it to Calgary, Alberta. With only a set of names he searched everywhere for his long lost parents seeking any of a thousand answers, which sadly were answered before he got the chance to ask one. Mexicano found his parents and they were the same place they had been for seventeen years in the Calgary Ross Cemetery. A sense of relief and sadness both filled his body, as he was ecstatic to find that his parents hadn’t abandoned him and yet deeply saddened that his parents were dead. Upon further investigation Mexicano found that they had been killed in a plane crash, the very plane that Mexicano would have been on and shared his parents fate. For this he prayed a prayer of thanks to his parents for making the decision that would spare his life and he vowed to change his ways and make something with his life. It was at this time that Mexicano noticed something wrong and soon realized he had lost all feeling in his body. Scrounging up what money he had Mexicano went to see a local doctor who informed him that his years of drinking had let to his nervous system shutting down. So now determined to be successful and with a high tolerance to pain Mexican knew only one option and that was wrestling. Mexicano managed to catch on in a small independent federation called the UXWF. He wrestled there under the name of Mexicano until he caught the eye of Colin Thomas and was signed to a deal to wrestle in Electronik Championship Wrestling. This is where he started to wrestle under the name of White Mexican. Mexi learned to wrestle splitting time in ECW and the Primetime Wrestling Academy. Mexi secretly was attempting to run ECW into the ground as he jumped to PCW and successfully managed to run ECW out of PTC. Meanwhile in PCW The White Mexican was climbing the ranks and captured the PCW Cruiserweight Title in a Bra and Panties match. He held the title for 38 days before the HIT ended his reign. He continued to flirt in both the Cruiserweight and Tag divisions with his partner B.J. Mac until he won the Cruiserweight Title for a second time defeating Chet King. This would be the start of a feud that would stretch for several months to come. Nine days later at Red October thanks to the brass knuckles of Tim Walden and the bisexual distractions of Cindy Bi and Mona at ringside Chet managed to defeat Mexi and regain the Cruiserweight title. Mexi and Chet would split ways until the December edition of Playboy was released and Mona was featured sparking jealousy from Cindy Bi. In a confusing series of events Mexi and Chet got dragged into the catfight and would develop a hatred for each other. The two had many epic battles as enemies but perhaps their best battle came as friends. When Mona’s true side showed and she admitted to using Mexi for his money and filed for divorce. Chet and Mexi forgave each other and Mexi built up trust with Chet and appeared on his talk show, which the producers demanded that Mexi be made a co-host and so the Chat King with Mexi Wrestling Show was born. On that show they agreed that whoever won the title on Turmoil would defend it against the other at Shanghaied 2 and that set the stage for one of the most shocking events in Mexi’s career. Chet King and the White Mexican faced off for PCW’s second highest title, the Continental Gold. Both men were being dubbed as too good for that title yet it would not stop either men from giving there all to win it. Chet King did the unthinkable in not only making the Mexican scream out in pain but making him tap out to win the match and retain the title. Unlike their previous battles both men stood and shook hands before Mexi uttered three words that would change his career forever, “I own NTICW.” His left shoulder had been ripped from the socket and would require time off to heal and Mexi announced on Turmoil that he would inDEED take time off to oversee the reopening of NTICW. What he didn’t count on was that a week later he would be fired from PCW and join his ex-wife on the unemployed list. Mexi accepted the fact and left to run his federation but on the opening show Mona reared her pretty little head and screwed Mexi yet again. Mexi had spent a show getting to know his employees and playing the good owner when during his address to the fans Mona and her lawyer Mr. Petercum showed up to spoil the party. With the signed divorce agreement stating that Mona gets every major property owned by Mexi during their marriage Mexi has no choice but to hand NTICW over as Mona promptly had his ass thrown out, however he did manage to win the commissionership in an improptu match Still wanting to wrestle in the only federation that mattered to him Mexi's secret plan was moving along nicely and a trial was set. Mexi was taking Tyler to court for wrongful dismissle and he would win that case thus reinstating him in PCW and straight into a heated battle with Tyler Nelson that would lead to both men being arrested during a match March 1 on Friday Fusion.
