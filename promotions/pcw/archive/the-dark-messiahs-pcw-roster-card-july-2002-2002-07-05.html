AIM Name: [detail withheld] MSN Name: [detail withheld] Bio Every person in this world has two sides to them, a good side and an evil side. Now most people are able to hide it, but not The Dark Messiahs... Daniel Steel's and Mike Stone's life started normally, they were born, they both had great parents and great childhoods. It was at the age of 14 that things began to change... One night whilst driving home Steel and his family took a side road through the forest as so to get home faster. However the car's batteries seemed to fail and with no source of light it was only a matter of time until the car ended up piled into a tree. After the crash Steel woke up with his head bleeding on the car bonnett. He checked behind him to find both his parents dead. Now Steel was all alone in the world, in a forest he had never been in before. Was he scared? Your damn right he was! Now as for Mike Stone, he was on the run from the police, after attacking his teacher with a flip knife. His parents already dead from an Arsonic attack, had sent him over the edge. Stone kept running until he found himself in the same forest as Steel, and just like Steel he was scared... Both boys were running from their world, but no matter how far they ran they couldn't escape. Something had gone wrong in their heads, what? They did not know, but they could feel it. Both boys soon came upon a cave. Steel was the first to arrive, but was soon joined by Stone. They did not have much time then to introduce each other as out of nowhere jumped a gray wolf. Both boys were yet again running, but this time they were running for their lives... It was a few miles before both boys grew tired, and decided they would just face their fate, they couldn't go anymore. The wolf pounced but something inside them both snapped, and before they knew it they were both beating down on the wolf with rocks and their fists. Blood began to pour from the wolf's beaten body, but this did not stop the boys, they had a taste for it and continue to beat away. A new side had emerged from the boys' personas, a much darker and evil side, The Dark Messiahs were born... Eventually both boys calmed down and stopped. However the strange thing was they didn't even remember what had happened. Something had taken control of them, but they had no idea what it was. The boys were not alone anymore, as they had found each other, and they knew where their detiny lied...in wrestling! So began over 10 years of training. Both boys grew up living violence, feeling it pump through their viens. They were ready, and they knew the place where they wanted to be...the PCW!!! Now they are here.
