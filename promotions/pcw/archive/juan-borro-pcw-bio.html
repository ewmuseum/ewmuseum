Juan Borro Biography Biography Juan Borro is 22 years old and is a notional icon. He is well respected throughout his country and loved by many. He was drafted into soccer at 17, being the youngest drafted player in Portugal ever. Throughout the years he's grown into a world icon, with loads of money and publicity. He is currently on The Portuguese National team, but does not feel like he's completed what he's set out to do. So far he's completed allot, coming up from a young childhood in which he was very poor, and an orphan. He managed to work his way up all on his own. His final goal is to prove himself not only as a world-class soccer player, but a World Class fighter. In recent events he's proved he is the world class fighter we set out to be, with shocking stats, superior to many wrestlers. Juan Borro was in PWA for one week before graduating to EW. In EW he had one match where he easily won, and formed a tag team with Kevin Klaymore. The two men were than transfered to NTICW where in there first match they won the NTICW Tag Team Titles in a TLC Title Match. The young but talented Juan Borro is going to continue his streak, it may only be two matches without a loss, but here in PCW he will make sure to make that much higher. And the team that walked away with the NTICW Tag Team belts retiring them. The Golden Game have now gone on to win the Tag Titles in PCW for the first time. Juan then went on to win the cruiserweight title, as the two men decided to try singles wrestling. Juan fought in some of the most grueling matches of his career in the division. The Golden Game got back together, but it was not for long. International Incident was started by Juan as a way to give back to the International world. Kevin Klaymore represented Spain but soon departed. The rest of the men will try their hardest to show the world the power of a united group of International wrestler’s with so much diversity and style. Old Description: He has a faded goatee, with sloppy short brown hair and brown eyes. He has a small cut under his right eye, and has a slight tan. He wears a Portuguese soccer jersey, nike shorts, red socks, and gold cleats. New Description: Juan is now with a full grown goatee, and a cut right below his left eye. Juan wears a bear skin coat into the ring, smoking a cuban cigarre. He's wearing black dress pants and a red velvet shirt. His shoes are still the same old golden cleats. Other partners: El Romantico: The mysterious Spaniard who always seems to get Juan's back ina time of need.
