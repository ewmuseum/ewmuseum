Vilas Lavagna Biography Real Name Vilas Lavagna Hometown Bangkok, Thailand Birthday July 12 1973 Height 5ft 4 Weight 165 lbs Biography Vilas Lavagna’s life started with the church and would end when he didn’t care anymore. Vilas grew up with his own thoughts and his own beliefs in the world. Leaking his ideas out into the world would get him banished and at the age of five that was the last thing he needed at the time. He just went about his life and carefully kept his ideas to himself, but of course he had to worship with his parents or have the chance of getting caught. Vilas was one of those people who didn’t draw attention to him self-but had an answer for everything. He was very stubborn when it came to church and Gods to worship and finally just quit acting all together. His parents first thought he was just trying to cause trouble in an act to get attention, so that is what they gave him. For the next three years, Vilas could not escape from his parent’s hold and had to go along with their plans. Vilas need time to himself, so he had to choose an activity of his own. Vilas had to take part in some form of art class. His writing was not the greatest and painting was out of the question, so he decided to take up the art of martial arts and learn how to channel his thoughts through his body. Vilas became interested fast in Martial arts and quickly took to the study as much as he could. But there was always something missing. He had forgotten his own ideas in the process and this brought them all back. By the age of fourteen he was back to his old ways and this got the attention of the church. They banished Vilas from their care and his parents quickly did the same kicking Vilas on his own. So Vilas picked up and left and headed his own way. The martial arts club sponsored him in various tournaments that gave a steady steam of money so he could afford to live. During the time with no competition Vilas became very interested with a more risky style of competition. The Mexican style of Lucha Libre interested Vilas and was perfect for his already different life style. Vilas first stayed in Thailand to learn the mental side of competition. Vilas moved out of his house and into the temple where he trained. The other students were not as giving as the teachers were, as they didn’t want any none believers in their group. Vilas ended training alone and stayed 100% focused on the task at hand. Vilas spent five years in the temple before moving out and representing Thailand in annual fighting competitions. But after only a year of the standard martial arts, Vilas decided to continue his interest and make the move to Mexico and learn lucha libre. With more then fifteen years under his belt with martial arts and lucha libre Vilas moved to Puroresu Ultimate Ring Entertainment and the world of professional wrestling. His stint there was very short, with only one match under his belt in the two weeks there in which he won. Now at twenty-nine, Vilas moves to the fast going United States and more importantly Primetime Championship Wrestling. With only two weeks of real wrestling experience Vilas may look like an under dog, but with fifteen years of training and mental experience he makes even some of the best look weak. He may not have a lot of past to talk about, but to Vilas past means nothing. All that matters to Vilas is what he has learned and what his opponent’s will learn from him. His views may very well get in the way of his goals but most of the time, Vilas sees something he wants and never stops till he has achieved it. The reality is Vilas uses his mind as a weapon and his body as a tool. A combination the allows Vilas to live the way he likes to. Different and always on the edge of something new.
