Kevin Klaymore — PCW biography (November 2001)

Name: Kevin Klaymore Nick Name: "The Bomb" Catchphrase(s): "Women want me, men want to be me hell everybody loves me. I am everything that you can never ever be, and that is why I am The Bomb!" "Ready or not here I am!" "The Bomb is tickin' so run like hell!" Character Handler: Kevin S. MSN: [detail withheld] AIM: [detail withheld] Theme song: Rock the Party by P.O.D. From: Detroit, MI Height: 6'4'' Weight: 269 lbs Wrestling Style: All round: All martial arts mixed with shoot fighting and street fighting skills. Common Moves: sleeping neckbreaker, Jacknife powerbomb, cobra clutch, ankle lock, spinning Fishermans suplex, The Klaymission (figure four leg lock combined with a death lock), death lock, gorrila press slam, Northern lights driver, Military press slam, vertabreaker Finisher: The Klaymation (realease verticle suplex) Set-up: Super Kick from Hell (lock and load superkick) Tag Finisher: Golden Bomb (Kevin sets an opponet up for a power bomb, Juan gets on the top rop and as Kevin holds him up. Juan gets on the top rope and gives him a super kick in mid air forcing the opponet down with a superkick into a powerbomb.) Stables & Tag Teams: Golden Game with Juan "Golden Toe" Borro Circle of Justice with Mexi, Juan, Rent-A-Hero, Chris Chase Title History: NTICW tag team champions/with Juan Borro , PCW tag team champions/with Juan Borro Awards: Special K cereal endoursement from a ladder match in EW Appearance: Kevin Klaymore is well built an amature body builder and has the perfect tan. He has short dirty blonde hair (the Gladiator haircut). He comes to the ring wearing leather pants, white wrist tape, a black elbow pad on his right arm, army boots, a brilliant Platinum necklace, a sterling silver chain on his leather pants. He also has a tattoo on his left arm ,barbed wire going aound the arm except in the middle there's a dragon. He has a six pack abdominal region and a 29" Python. Bio: Kevin grew up in the suburbs in Detroit where only the rich live. He attended the best school in the State which is in Detroit. He was captain of the football team and led his team to an undefeated streak for four years and won the National Championship. When he graduated he was voted most sexiest male, hottest male, cutest guy, best kisser, best smile, most flirtacious, most perfect hell he was best everything. He aws even offered full ride scholarshos to every college in the world. Yet he went to the orient to study the martial arts and to become a wrestler. After his training in the orient he returned to the States to study wrestling skills under the great Killer Kowalski, and after his training there he went to the famous dungeon where most of the legends learned at under the great Stu Hart. After about a couple of years he returned to the States in Venice Beach to become an amature body builder. After taking his skill and physique into new heights for about two years he wanted to follow his dreams to become a profesional wrestler. With his physique fighting ability and his power to control the crowd at any given moment and his 27 years under his belt this young SuperStar is definately a force to be reckoned with. When arriving to the PWA his first bout was against the Ice Man Nikita Morozov and defeated him in an upset. He remained in PWA for several months moving up in the ranks. After graduating to the GAW where he only stayed a week before he watched it go bankrupt. He returned to the PWA to brush in up on his skills. After a few months of moving up in Venoms class ranks he received the call to EW. Before recieving the call Kevin had a depression problem, and resorted to drinking. By the time he lost his Acid Title shot, he went to the local bar and drank too much. By the time he was found he receved alchohol poisoning and was dieing. Upon being rushed to the hospital for treatment Kevin was revived and kept under close surveliance. During his time at the hospital Piranha came down to encourage his frreind to hang in there cause it wasn't his time yet. When Kevin fully recovered he vowed to never drink again. After all he almost lost his life. During his time in EW he made his mark as a hard worker. When he arrived there he met his new room mate whom would become a very good freind Juan "Golden Toe" Borro. After looking at the Immortals card he was in a ladder match for the new and improved image for Special K cereal. During the show he helped Juan out allowing him to gain a victory and Juan returned the favor which gave Kevin the win and creating the Golden Game. While Juan and Kevin were on vacation they received a call from NTICW president letting them know that they have moved to NTICW starting the following Monday. Upon their first match where they showed that they were hard workers, and thus they received a tag title shot. While looking more closely at the card they noticed that the match has four other teams and was a TLC match. After training hard they gained a victory winning the tag titles and retiring with them. Due to the fact that NTICW closed down when they got the call to their child hood dream, PCW. Shortly after arricing to PCW Kevin and Juan noticed that many of the teams cheated to win. After Kevin was beaten unmercifully by the stable Xxplosive, he wanted to make every match a fair one. Upon realizing he wasn't the only one who wanted that he soon formed and alliance with Juan "Golden Toe" Borro, Mexi, Rent-A-Hero, and Chris Chase to form the Circle Of Justice. The new stable was very succesful in the beggining, however before the Red October PPV Klaymore was injured by several disgusting chairshots to his knee forcing him to drain water from his knee and miss several weeks. Upon his time out he began to train hard to make himself new and improved much like Special K cereal. When he returns he will go back right where he left off making his way through the ladder of success. Gaining spots up the contender ladder for Tag and singles competition. During grueling battles The Golden Game got their shot at the titles. They defeated the japanese fighters, whom got backstabbed by their own members, to gain the win for the titles. KEvin is defiantely a force to reckon with in PCW.
