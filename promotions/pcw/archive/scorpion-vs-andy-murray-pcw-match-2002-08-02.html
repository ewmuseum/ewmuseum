PCW match: Scorpion vs. Andy Murray

2 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Scorpion vs. Andy Murray Written: 8-2-02 “Check” by Zebrahead hits over the PA system in the PCW arena and all the fans stand and give a mixed reaction for Andy Murray as he steps through the curtain and into the arena… Clay: The following contest is for one fall! Now entering the arena, weighing in at 270 pounds, from Glesga, Scotland, Andy Murray! Jeff: Next match is going to be boring! Dave: No it isn’t, these two PCW stars are proving themselves every day and I think this will be one of the best matches tonight. Jeff: I wipe my ass with what you think, Dave. **Smack** **Smack** Duke: Both of you shut up or there will be more of that! Andy walks down the ramp and looks to have a purpose in mind. He enters the ring and walks around the referee playing with the crowd… Dave: Andy Murray might win this thing, but Scorpion seems to be on a role, he proved himself to me at Wrestle Vessel in that Sand Castle match. Jeff: Ah shut up. Scorpion won’t win this thing, if I had my way neither one would win this match. Duke: I’m actually looking forward to this, Andy Murray is an established star here and Scorpion is a former CCWer who is taking PCW by storm. Dave: True. “Enter Sandman” By Metallica hits over the PA system and Scorpion comes running down the ramp towards the ring. Clay: Coming down the ramp, weighing in at 245 pounds, from New York City, New York, Scorpion! Jeff: Look at Clay run as Scorpion runs in and throws rights and lefts at Murray. Dave: Don’t be picking on Clay now, he’s a great ring announcer. Duke: You pick on Clay and you have to answer to me. Jeff: Ok sorry, geez, just trying to have some fun. Micelli calls for the bell as Scorpion lays in a few rights to Murray and backs him up to the ropes, he grabs Murray and goes to whip him across the ring into the ropes, but Murray reverses and when Scorpion comes back Murray hits him in a huge powerslam, he goes for a quick cover… Micelli: 1…2….. 2! He kicked out! Jeff: Andy Murray might be bigger then Scorpion, but he isn’t going to put him away that quickly. Dave: For once I agree. Duke: Yeah, even I know that as well. Murray gets up and grabs Scorpion by the hair pulling him to his feet. As Scorpion gets to his feet he kicks Andy in the mid-section and hooks him in a front face lock. He then picks Murray up in a vertical suplex and drops him back hard on his back. He gets up as the crowd starts to chant for him for picking up the bigger wrestler so easily… Crowd: Scorpion! Scorpion! Scorpion! Scorpion! Scorpion! Scorpion! Dave: Jeff, even you have to admit that was a little impressive there. Jeff: Yeah I guess you’re right then. Duke: Jeff respects someone who isn’t a heel! This is the day I’ve been waiting for! Jeff: Shut up! Duke: You want me to slap you again? Jeff: No! I’m sorry. Scorpion gets up as the surprised Murray gets up with his back to Scorpion, he turns around to Scorpion kicking him in the mid-section again, and then hooking his arms behind him and hitting a Double Arm DDT… Dave: Royal Flush DDT! That could be it! Scorpion pins the fallen star… Micelli: 1…2….. 2! Jeff: I don’t think so, Andy Murray may not be my favorite star, but it’s going to take a lot to take him out. Duke: Wow, Jeff is just so agreeable today for some reason. Dave: Yeah I know what you mean. Scorpion doesn’t seem to be at all surprised by the kick out and he just gets up and walks over to the turnbuckle, he climbs to the top rope… Dave: Scorpion is going to fly! Duke: Yeah, Scorpion is a pretty good high flyer! Jeff: I hope it ends with some blood! Scorpion looks at Murray who’s staggering up to his feet, he jumps off the top rope with a cross-body attempt, but Murray turns to see it come and ducks out of the way and sticks a stiff kick up that lands Scorpion right in the middle of his chest, knocking him down hard and the air being knocked out of him. He lays there trying to get his breath back as Murray shakes the cobwebs out from the DDT he took, he picks up Scorpion after shaking them out and stands him on his feet in front of him. He hooks his arms around Scorpion and lifts him in the air with a Belly-to-Belly suplex, as they land he keeps the cover on Scorpion and Micelli gets down to count… Micelli: 1…2….. 2! Murray gets up and looks at the referee with a crossed look in his eyes and then picks Scorpion up. He drags him over to the ropes and whips him through the ropes to the floor outside. He walks out on the apron and drops down to the floor. He starts to lay in kicks to the back of Scorpion as they start to battle up the ramp. Murray picks Scorpion up, but as if it were out of desperation, Scorpion hooks Murray in a T-Bone Suplex and throws him onto the steel ramp, Murray lands hard on the back of his head and falls limp. Micelli counts… Micelli: 1!!!!! Jeff: Woo! Finally some action in this match! Dave: Oh shut up! Duke: I wonder if this match will even end in this ring now… Micelli: 2!!!! Dave: I hope it does. Jeff: I hope it doesn’t. Eddie Mandrake! It’s Eddie Mandrake coming down the ramp! Mandrake walks down the ramp and picks Murray up he then drags him towards the ring. Micelli looks at Mandrake with a confused look on his face but he continues to count as Mandrake rolls Murray into the ring. Micelli: 3!!!! Mandrake looks behind himself as he sees Scorpion down as well, he walks back and grabs Scorpion and throws him under the bottom rope as well… Jeff: Mandrake attacked Scorpion last Wednesday on Turmoil after his match. Now he’s helping Scorpion by continuing this match… what’s going on? Dave: I think Mandrake just wants Scorpion to finish his match. Micelli looks at the two stars as they get to their feet. Scorpion doesn’t realize that Eddie Mandrake is at ringside as he gets to his feet. Murray walks over to Scorpion and whips him across the ring into the ropes. Scorpion comes back with a hard clothesline, and then he spots Mandrake at ringside. He looks at Mandrake hard with a huge stare and the two of them start to throw insults back and forth at each other. Murray comes up behind Scorpion and rolls him up in a schoolboy! Dave: Schoolboy! Micelli: 1…2……..3! Andy Murray’s music hits over the PA system again as Clay announces the winner… Clay: The winner of this match, Andy Murray!!! Dave: Eddie Mandrake just cost Scorpion this match! Jeff: What do you mean? All Eddie did was help continue the match, it isn’t his fault that Scorpion wanted to take his attention off Murray just to talk to him. Dave: It is his stupid fault he shouldn’t be at ringside! Duke: I actually blame Mandrake as well. Jeff: Damn faces overcoming me! Scorpion looks around as if he can’t believe he lost, and he starts to bang the ring with his fist as he realizes why he lost. Andy Murray exits the ring and heads up the ramp. Mandrake looks up at Scorpion and shoots him a cocky wink, followed by a smirk as he heads back up the ramp toward the back. Scorpion follows him with his eyes to the back and then starts to head back there himself, steaming mad from what just happened. Dave: I think these two are just getting started with each other, but right now we have some more action in the back! View Scorpion 's Biography Back
