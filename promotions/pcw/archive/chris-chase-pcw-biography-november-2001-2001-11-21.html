'The Messiah' Chris Chase Real Name: Chris Chase Nick Name: 'The Messiah' Catchphase: And you can Justify...that Character Handler: Chris L. MSN Account: [detail withheld] AIM Address: [detail withheld] Theme Song: One Vision by either Queen or Type O Negative (Even though the latter is mostly in German) Age: 22 From: Calgary, Alberta, Canada (Born in London, England) Height: 6'2" Weight: 229lbs Wrestling Style: High Flying Technical Signature Moves: Springboard Shooting Star Press Suicida or "Lone Star" Execution: Once often used by a Wrestler known as Jody Fleish, I now use this move as my trademark move. It's basically executed by running to either the ropes or turnbuckle, and using the momentum used by that to launch myself into a Shooting Star Press, which is a variation on a body press where the wrestler executes a backflip whilst going fowards. It either brings the opponent down to the mat, or can be use on a downed opponent. Inverted Swinging Bulldog: a variation on the famous Bulldog attack. Known to Chase as 'The Epitomizer'. Execution: I apply an inverted face lock on the vicitm. I then reach over with my far arm and turn around so the victim is in sort of a side head lock facing upward and I am now facing 180 degrees opposite of what I was originally facing. I then fall and spin to the side, flipping the victim over and driving them face first into the ground. In one word - OUCH! Flying Somersault DDT: A souped up version of the standard DDT. Known to Chase as "Pure Justification". Used by Villano VI Execution: I usually stand on the second rope behind the victim. I then apply an inverted face lock on the opponent. I then jump off the second rope and do a forward flip over the victim's body. I land on my back and drive the victim head first into the mat on their face or top of their head. Half Crab: Chase's under-used submission attack, often only used lightly but to good effect. Execution: Can be set up one of two ways. If the victim is on their back, I usually grab one of their legs, step over the victim's body turning them on their stomach and pull back applying pressure to the victim's back. If the victim is on their stomach, I grab one of the victim's legs and pull backwards applying pressure to the victim's back. 3/4 Bulldog Powerbomb: A rarely used move known to Chase as "A Cut to the Chase". Execution: I bend my victim over into a powerbomb set up or apply a standing head scissors and grab the victim around the waist. I then lift my victim up so they are sitting on my shoulders, facing the opposite direction of me. I push the victim fowards slightly so they fall straight down off my shoulders but not down as if in a powerbomb, straight down as if to their feet or stomach. As the victim is falling to the mat, I apply 3/4 face lock (basically, the victim's head on my shoulder with me forming a headlock around the top of their head as I have my back to the victim) and drops to the mat, impacting the victim's chin into my shoulder. Finisher Move: Double Vision - Can be one of three variations. The first is the original version, an Elevated Cradle Neckbreaker set up on the second/top ropes. The second is the 'Valiant Vision' variant, a standing Cradle Neckbreaker. The third is a Swinging Tornado Cradle Neckbreaker, where the Cradle is formed around the neck and arms of his opponent whilst kicking up and spinning around after running/bounced off the ropes. Secondary Finisher: Leap of Faith - A Barrier Senton Bomb. Current Team: High Impact w/ Dustin Downey Past Teams & Groups: None Title History: PCW2 Continental Title Awards History: Description: INSERT WRESTLER DESCRIPTION HERE History: On the 1st January 1979, a young boy by the name of Chris Andrews was born into this world, in London General Hospital. He was born to English parents, Peter and Geraldine Andrews, a middle class family. He was born at a weigh of 8lbs, and was born two weeks late. A year after he was born, he was injected into tragedy, as his parents were killed in a Car Crash after a Christmas Party; A drunken driver had collided with their car head on. Chris never knew his parents, and a month later he was taken to Calgary, Canada, adopted by a young couple by the names of Bob and Susan Chase. To him, these were his real parents, he never knew his past before that, and all he knew was that he was called Chris Chase. As the years rolled by, Chris became stronger and more athletic as he took up an interest in Soccer at school. A naturally gifted player, his coach said that he would go far. On his 16th birthday, he was taking a walk in downtown Calgary, when a strange, tall man approached him. The man later turned out to be Sean Stevens, a professional Canadian Wrestler, who had given up Wrestling to coach it. He handed Chris a card, and walked off without saying a word. Two days later, Chris showed up at Sean's Wrestling School, a converted office block, located in the posh area of Calgary. Known for his acquired gift of the gab, he was a hit with the young women in this part of the town and they often watched through a window at him wrestling. He realised that this was his calling, he was a naturally born Showman. (This will be replaced eventually)
