PCW match: Snow (Rising Star Champion) vs. Ivan Stanislav - non-title match (L)

17 June 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Snow (Rising Star Champion) vs. Ivan Stanislav - non-title match (L) Written: June 17, 2001 Dave: Well, in that case, let's head to our next match as the PCW newcomer, Snow, takes on Ivan Stanislav. Clay Clayborne: The following contest is scheduled for one fall. Already in the ring at this time weighing in at 232 pounds…he is the current PCW Rising Star Champion…SNOW!! Dave: Rising Star Title not on the line in this match. The Soviet National Anthem begins to play as the red flag of the old Soviet Union appears on the jumbotron. The massive Russian bear Ivan Stanislav makes his way out lead by Alexi Ruslan. Jeff: (saluting) Kill him Comrade! Clay Clayborne: And his opponent….hailing from Arkhangelsk, Russia and weighing 400 pounds…he is the leader of the Red Army…IVAN STANISLAV!! The huge Russian slowly makes his way to the ring and climbs in, stepping over the top rope. Dave: Snow made a strong showing at the pay per view, but he may be in a little over his head in this one. Jeff: Comrade Stavislav will crush this capitalist bug. The bell sounds and Stanislav methodically paces toward Snow. Ivan lunges forward to grab his opponent but Snow uses his quickness to escape. Dave: A smart strategy by Snow to stay away from the big Russian Bear. Jeff: BAH! It is only a matter of time before Comrade Stanislav catches this coward and snaps his neck like twig. Dave: Jeff, have you forgotten how to speak proper English? Jeff: English is secondary language to native Russian. Dave: You’re not from Russia you idiot. Jeff: SILENCE INFIDEL! Dave: (sigh) Once again Stanislav stalks Snow and traps him in a corner. Ivan lunges forward and again Snow is able to duck under and escape. Ivan gets frustrated and roars in anger. He starts after Snow again, backing him into a corner. Ivan reaches for Snow and once again he tries to duck underneath the massive arms of the Russian, but this time Stanislav grabs Snow by the hair and tosses him back into the corner. Snow crashes into the turnbuckles and his head snaps back from the impact. Stanislav raises his hand in the air and brings it down across the chest of Snow with a mighty chop that echoes throughout the arena. Crowd: Oooooh! Dave: He might have cracked Snow’s sternum with that blow. Jeff: I told you that Comrade Stanislav would catch the little rabbit and destroy him. Stanislav grins as he raises his hand again and lands another vicious chop across the chest of Snow. Snow’s chest is beet red with broken blood vessels as Ivan grabs one of his arms and whips him across to the opposite turnbuckle. Snow almost flips up and over from the force of the impact and then stumbles out a couple of steps…right into the grasp of Stanislav. Ivan grabs Snow by the neck with one hand and easily lifts him high into the air. Dave: IVAN HAS SNOW UP! WHAT IS HE GOING TO DO WITH HIM? Jeff: KILL THE CAPITALIST PIG COMRADE! Ivan starts to drive Snow down for a chokeslam, but in what can be called nothing less than desperation Snow hooks Ivan’s head in a front face lock on the way down. SNOW PLANTS IVAN STANISLAV WITH A DDT OUT OF THE CHOKESLAM! Dave: WHAT A MOVE!! Jeff: What the…?! GET HIM COPRADE! Snow goes for a cover …1…Ivan powers out and sends Snow flying halfway across the ring. The Russian gets to his feet and looks none too happy. Snow looks up wide eyed at the monster before him and leaps to his feet. Snow charges Stanislav and begins to throw a barrage of wild right and left hands. Ivan simply takes the blows and smiles sadistically. Dave: Those shots are having absolutely no effect on Stanislav! Jeff: Of course not. Snow realizes that he is getting nowhere and looks up at Ivan who leers down at him and then picks him up high above his head in a gorilla press position. Ivan parades around the ring as Snow looks around wondering where he is going to land. Then Ivan removes one hand and hold Snow in the air above his head with the other. Dave: What a feat of strength by the massive Russian monster!! Jeff: My Comrade cannot be stopped, especially by this puny Canadian. Ivan holds Snow up for a few more moments before unceremoniously dropping him straight down to the mat. Snow lands on his face and writhes around in pain. Ivan looks down on Snow and then out to the crowd, laughing. Then Ivan crouches down to one knee and lets out a mighty roar, pounding his chest and mocking Meanstreak. Crowd: Streak!! Streak!! Streak!! Dave: Does Stanislav have a death wish? Jeff: I do not wish to comment at this time. Ivan then reaches down and pulls Snow to his feet. Ivan spits in Snow’s face and then whips him into the ropes. Snow ducks under a huge clothesline attempt and bounces off the other side. Ivan turns around just in time to catch a flying forearm that seems to stagger him somewhat. Snow bounces off the ropes again and hits Ivan with the same maneuver, making him move back a few more steps. As Stanislav seems somewhat dazed, Snow goes to the outside and sets up on the apron in between the corners. He leaps up on the top rope and springboards himself towards Stanislav, hooking his legs on the huge Russian’s head and taking him over with a Hurracanrana! Crowd: HOLY SH*T! HOLY SH*T! Dave: DID YOU SEE THAT MOVE?! SNOW JUST TOOK IVAN STANISLAV OVER WITH A HURRACANRANA!! Jeff: There must be some water or sweat on the mat that Comrade Ivan slipped on! That could not have really happened! Snow looks stunned but quickly makes the cover…1…2…Ivan roars and presses out of the pin, sending Snow across the ring once again. Dave: I think Ivan’s pissed. Jeff: DAMN right he is! This infidel Snow will pay for his crimes against the Red Army. Ivan kips up to his feet and makes his way towards Snow. Snow tries to hit Stanislav with a low blow, but the big Russian no sells and grabs him around the neck. Ivan pulls Snow to his feet and gets him in a front face lock. Dave: I think we all know what’s coming. Jeff: KILL THE INFIDEL, COMRADE!! Ivan hoists Snow up with ease and then NAILS HIM WITH THE RED SCARE!! Ivan walks over and places a foot on Snow’s chest as a cover…1…2…3!! Winner: Ivan Stanislav Dave: Snow gave it his best shot but came up shot against the mighty Russian named Stanislav. Jeff: Snow should be glad Comrade Stanislav allowed him to live and defend his title another day instead of killing him and taking his worthless crap title. View Snow 's Biography
