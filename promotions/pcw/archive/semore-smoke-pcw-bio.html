Semore Smoke Biography Real Name Simon Seymour Smoke Nicknames Smokey, Smoke Hometown Atlantic City, New Jersey Birthday April 5, 1976 Height 6'2'' Weight 225 lbs Biography Semore Smoke was born Simon Seymour Smoke on April 5, 1976 at Johnston Memorial Hospital in Smithfield, North Carolina. His mother was a 17 year old hooker, and his father a new navy recruit who would go off for a training sequence, but never come back, as his ship was hit by a live torpedo. Semore grew up alone trying to raise money for his mother and him as she worked as a street walker, and he doubled as a drug dealer. At the age of ten they moved to Wilson, North Carolina to look for a better life, which they didn't find. Semore continued his work as a drug dealer and did some inside jobs for the local Trench Coat Mafia, which was the biggest gang in the small town. Semore continued to go to school as he did his deals, and even graduated, which is against what Smoke had said in the past in his biographies. Semore Smoke graduated High School and went on to his first love, the Army. Semore Smoke participated in his JROTC program in High School and later would enlist in the Army. Since he was in JROTC he was enlisted as a second Lieutenant. He would join the 82nd Airborne, his life long dream was to jump out of an airplane over France, something he had wanted to do since he read aobut D-Day in World History. Well, Mr. Smoke's attitude wasn't right for the Army. Not too long after his training and his assignment, he was dishonorably discharged for using illegal substances on the base, or better known as marijuana. Well, after the his discharge he continued his old studies at ECU, or Easter Carolina Universary, which is in Greenville, North Carolina, there he got his History Major in just four years, at the latter of the four years he began to train to become a professional wrestler, and even set up a case where a judge 'made' him wrestle. Semore later enrolled into Primetime Wrestling Academy, and did triple duty as he trained there, learned more in DRWF, and tried to work his way through the rankings of MSCW at the same time. In MSCW he won the MSCW TV Championship after becoming the number 1 contender after losing to Paul Cline in the MSCW Survivor. Semore Smoke would have a short two week reign as the TV champion, but it's enough to put his name in MSCW history. He later left the federation on mutual terms, and the fed later fell. He left DRWF and started to concentrate on his Primetime Central career more then the other career. He graduated PWA after five months of work and went onto DWF, which never got off the ground. After the disappointment which was the DWF. He stayed around in the hearts of PTC for about a month or so before he was invited to join NTICW in Febuary of 2002. Semore jumped at the opportunity, and soon became a star in the NTICW. He was the first World Champion of the new era, and can always say that he is one of the best ever to come through the ranks of NTICW. After working in NTICW for four months he was forced to move on. Owner, Chris Sloboda, closed NTICW and Tyler Nelson bought out his contract. Now Semore Smoke is in PCW hoping to work up to the status where he was in NTICW. But will the PCW Superstars accept him? The answer soon became yes after he began to win matches. On August 14, 2002 he realized his calling and auditioned for a part on the Comedy Central Premium Blend show for stand-up comedians. He was automatically accepted and is now a stand-up comedian within the PCW. Just days after his debut on Premium Blend as a stand-up comedian, Semore Smoke won the PCW Rising Star Championship at Third Dawning. After he lost the Rising Star championship to Anarchy, he's been stricken with a kind of sad feeling. He's put his comedian career on hold for a short while and is doing small spots in contest. After his Comedy career went down the drain, and his loss in the Cannonball Run race because of Skylar Green, Smoke went onto have an almost career ending injury to his spinal cord. After surgery he was out for rehab for several months and during those months, several weird stuff happened. News came to Smoke that his father was in New Jersy, dead in New Jersey. Smoke had never known his father so he left for New Jersey to find out more. His father's name was Michael Bordon, an Italian business man in New Jersey. But it later became obvious that Mr. Bordon was the boss of a major organized crime ring. And with Smoke as his only son, he became heir to his thrown on top of New Jersey. Semore Smoke returns to PCW after surgery with new heat behind him. Hell bent on finding the person who killed his father and on winning what he wants, the PCW World title, he's out to take over PCW and the world of organized crime, all at the same time....
