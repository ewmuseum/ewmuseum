Hank — PCW history (November 2002)

24 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
History Hank~! was born in Toronto, Ontario, Canada, but ran off at age thirteen because his parents were a couple of dicks who only tried to control every move he'd make. He has one sister by the name of Shana. He had to leave her behind and let her make up her own mind. She soon did at age sixteen, Hank was seventeeen by that time and already in Seattle doing what it took to get the rent money, as long as it was legally acceptable. He managed enough money to manage the weekly visit to the gym to keep in shape and start to practice and perfect his fighting style. He was noticed by some wrestling scout by the name of Al Stoval. Al saw a drive and determination in Hank that he didn't see in too many that trained under him. Al and Hank got to talking over a couple rounds of Coke and Beer and Hank expressed how much he loved wrestling and so wanted and wished someday he could be like some of the greats. Steamboat, Snuka, Andre, Triple H, Rock, he said that he could visually see himself doing the wrestling business and doing it with all the heart he could throw at it, which always met 120%. It was enough for Al to start his training for fifty dollars a month and work him day and night until he was ready for real competition. His first couple of matches were rocky and ended up in a loss, but then Hank started to understand the business and what it stood for and what he had to do to actually pick up the win. Hank then started carrying a 3-foot long steel pipe to the ring and picked up on the heel gimmick, to at least get him started. He even picked up a new wrestling name in Chris Ontario, 'The Chosen Canadian'. He became PHCW TV and Hardcore Champion. He then got his big break for the UWF, as The Ultimate Warlord opened it for business and signed him on the spot, but said that Chris Ontario had to change, that the Canadian gimmick has already been played. So Chris Ontario changed to the babyface The Drummer where he had so much success, then moved to NZW where he and his bestfriend back in Seattle, Ganker Ransolver formed the tag team Team Nirvana and captured the NZW Tag Titles and NZW World Title. He's moved around fed to fed but now he's found himself in PCW where he's had mostly downtime and not much luck, until Shang-Haied 2 where he captured the PCW Extreme Championship and his career seemed to have shot up with so much success. Hank's love life now hasn't been like his wrestling career as of late. He's been through around two managers who just couldn't hack having a wrestling boyfriend who put them second. He finally met Janelle Stout, who had a respect for him and his career. They are still together to this day. She stands beside her man Hank until the bitter end. Hank has three to four true friends aside his girlfriend Janelle. Those being Josh, Ganker, Sephiroth, Jarrod on occassions. Josh is by Hank's side 24/7 and it kind of gets on Hank's nerves, like wise with Josh, so it works out. Ganker is his partner and has no choice, but to be by Hank's side most of the time, unless he's in the hotel with Janelle.
