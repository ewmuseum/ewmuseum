Fusion 13, 15 December 2000

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Fusion 13 15 Dec 2000 Arco Arena, Sacremento, California Turmoil main event highlights The show opens by showing highlights of the "main event" from Turmoil. First we see Doug Troy with a dented chair, followed by a laid out Kid Wonder with tied up valets. Then we see The Reverend Jon Hull come out to the ring. Next we see Alan Kreigman laid out in the hallway as Billy Blaze puts the boots to him. Jon Kano continues by whacking Herbert D. Dorkiman with a chair and taking him away. Then Meanstreak comes out with Chuck Manson and hits him with At Death's Door. Then, as Meanstreak addresses the crowd, Johnny Smith's music plays. As he comes out, he is promptly taken out by a kendo stick. Then we see a masked man pick up the belt and reveal himself to be the first World champion, Kremmen. Meanstreak goes to attack but is intercepted by Johnny Raindance. We then watch as all hell breaks loose and we go to the opening of Fusion. Fusion kicks off The usual green lightning filled show opening for Fusion begins. Wrestlers highlighted are Johnny Vain, Archangel, Meanstreak, Doug Troy, The Blood Brothers, and Trashcan Man. Ed and Anguish arrive The scene switches to backstage, where a large red semi truck is pulling into the arena. The passenger side doors of the truck open, and a skinny man with unkempt black hair and wearing a light sweatshirt hops out of the truck, stumbles forward, and bumps into a car. Following him is Ed, holding Bob and with the Extreme Title on his shoulder. Ed: Thanks, Cal! The driver of the truck leans over and waves. Cal: No problem, Ed. Good luck in yer match! With that, the passenger door slams, and the truck backs out of the lot. Ed turns to his friend, who is still struggling to keep his balance. Ed: Are you gonna be okay, Anguish? Anguish: I'm fah...fin...I'm okay. With that, the little lunatic tries to stand straight and nearly falls over backwards. Ed catches him with his free arm, and helps guide him into the arena. The two enter the huge parking garage, and immediately notice a garbage truck with "I love you, Optimus Junior!" scribbled on the back. Angy's eyes go wide. Anguish: I wrote the...that! Ed sighs. Ed: I knew I shouldn't have let you drink that seventh bottle of tequila... Anguish: Viva la tequila! With that, the little man doubles over, clutching his stomach. He starts to shake, and before Ed can stop him, throws up all over the driver's side door of the truck. Ed: Oh, for God's... Now look what you've done! Anguish: Haha! That's hila...hilary...uh...funny! Hahaha! Ed sighs again and grabs his friend's shoulder, pulling him away from the truck and into the arena. Marx: Oh man, what a sick way to start the show. Smith: Angy looks cute when he's sick A loud slap can be heard, then some whimpering. Kern: Well, tonight promises to be a huge night as we are just 2 days away from Decimation. And, coincidentaly enough, all the men in the big main event get the night off. Marx: All of them except for Johnny Raindance. He has to fight the man that rocks more than anything, JON KANO! He smiles widely, content, and places the razors in his pocket. Next he reaches for the shampoo, but before he can pop the cap open, Johnny G has noticed Ned at ringside. Johnny quickly rushes over to Ned and grabs the bottle of shampoo. With a yank, he takes the shampoo from Ned's hand. Ned, upset, slides into the ring and tries to get the shampoo back. Johnny turns around and launches a superkick at Ned, but Ned ducks and lunges for the bottle of shampoo. Contact. The two grapplers fall to the ground and try wrestle the shampoo from the other. They roll around for a second when Johnny starts to gain control. He grins as his hands take control of 3/4ths of the bottle, and he tries a hefty pull to wrest the bottle from Ned's possession. Ned responds by tightening his grip on the bottle even more, perhaps a little too much. The pressure created inside the bottle pops the lid and a stream of shampoo squirts into Johnny G's eyes. His hands let go of the bottle and go to his eyes as he yells in pain. He frantically tries to get the soapy substance out of his eyes as Ned tries to get as much spilled shampoo as possible back into the bottle. While Johnny G stumbles around the ring, blinded, he starts throwing punches in random directions, hoping to make contact with Ned's jaw. Unfortunately, his aim isn't what it used to be, so no dice. Ned finishes putting as much shampoo as possible back into the bottle before standing up. Ned sees Johnny trying to rub the shampoo out of his eyes, shrugs his shoulders, gets behind Johnny and rolls him up into a small package. 1, 2. 3!
