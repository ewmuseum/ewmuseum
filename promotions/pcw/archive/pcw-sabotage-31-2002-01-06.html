Sabotage 31 6 Jan 2002 The Alamo Dome, San Antonio, Texas The Spurs in the front row and a city in ruins Primetime Championship WrestlingFrom the Alamo Dome in San Antonio, TexasJanuary 6, 2002The normal opening to the show reels. It involves wrestlers running around in a sabotaged city. Among the wrestlers highlighted include The Pharaoh, Frankie Bones, Twisted Metal, Afwa Kilmanjaro, and Vampir Nosferatu. Tim Duncan, David Robinson, and the rest of the bunch sit in the crowd, in the front row, in the seats that their millionaire asses couldn't pay for. Suddenly, the lights in the arena black out and the crowd can be heard panicking and shouting, adamant not to miss any action in this excellent main event. Camera flashes go off as some of the crowd attempts to light up the arena for just a single solitary second, but to no avail. Jeff: Damned lights! Nelson won’t be pleased at the light coordinators! Duke: If I were president this wouldn’t be happenin’ hoss! The crowd suddenly goes quiet and watches on as in the middle of the ring, an orange glow appears. The glowing light grows and spreads across the ring. Through the light we can make out that Clyde has climbed down from his perch on the top turnbuckle and Howard has gotten to his feet. Vampir is also in the ring now as the three men throw aimless punches in the blinding light. The glowing light soon envelops the whole mat, or so it seems, when it suddenly shrinks back into a little smudge in the middle of the ring. Jeff: U.F.O.? Duke: What the hell? Dave: I’m stumped… Jeff: So I’ve heard… Duke: Quiet you two! Dave: We’re commentators, we’re not supposed to be quiet! The light under the mat begins to pulsate and “throb”, until finally with an immense BOOM! KICK! -WHAM! -KABOOM! The center of the ring explodes, and huge, lapping flames rise up out of the middle of the ring. With the explosion the lights in the arena return to full beam, and “I Am Hated” begins playing throughout the arena. The song ends abruptly. The flames dance high into the sky, as JKH, Vampir Nosferatu and Clyde look bewildered. Duke: Hell this isn’t the place or the damn time for a BBQ! Jeff: What’s going on? Dave: The ring just exploded… Jeff: I CAN SEE THAT YOU FIEND!!! The flames get higher, and the three competitors stand ready for whatever happens next. With that in mind, an immense black shape begins to emerge from the flames, until it is high in the ring. Thinking quickly, Vampir Nosferatu turns and knocks Krayzie to the mat with a hard elbow, and stalks him as he struggles to get up whilst clutching his running nose. Dave: VAMPIR SPLIT KRAYZIE WIDE OPEN!!! Duke: He gave JKH a nosebleed, stop bein’ so damn traumatic! Jeff: For sooth, what doth thou mean…eth? SMACKAMATE-KAMATE-KO! Jeff: Ouch… In the ring, Vampir is kicking JKH to the gut, while Clyde watches the huge figure in the flames. It suddenly steps out and grabs Clyde by the throat, and proceeds to Chokeslam him hard to the mat. However the huge figure pulls Clyde back up and again chokeslams him, this time down into the flame pit. The figure jumps down after him and the flames pounce into the air. Jeff: AND DON’T COME BACK!!! Duke: Looks like the devil took Clyde back to Georgia… Dave: What? Duke: Nothing…vote for Duke! JKH leg sweeps Vampir and nips up. He grabs Vampir by the hair and pulls him to his feet. A swift kick to the gut doubles Vampir over, and JKH sets him up for the Krayzie Death Slam. Dave: KRAYZIE IS GOING TO DEATH SLAM VAMPIR INTO THE FLAME PIT!!! Jeff: YOU MUST BE KIDDING ME! Duke: No I’m not kidding………I am this rugged and handsome! Jeremy Howard signals for the Death Slam, but in a split second Vampir maneuvers his feet onto the ropes and launches Krayzie back, and into the flamepit. Dave: VAMPIR NOSFERATU JUST SENT THE KRAYZIE ONE STRAIGHT TO THE DEPTHS OFHELL!!! Duke: What’s he doing now, hoss? Vampir stands before the flame pit and tilts his head back. He slowly raises his arms, and from the depths of the flame pit arises the huge figure again. Vampir motions for it to step out, which it does, only to reveal itself to the masses. Dave: Who the hell is…oh wait it’s…Hessian? Jeff: HESSIAN!? A JOBBER JUST SENT CLYDE AND KRAYZIE TO HELL!!! Dave: Vampir Nosferatu and Hessian are working together??? Duke: That can’t be good hoss… Dave: Oh come on they’re both Satanists, you should’ve seen it coming but you didn’t! Hessian looks at Vampir and bows his head, then as Vampir reaches out and points to the flame pit, Hessian backs up and steps down into the flames. For a moment there I nothing, then suddenly out of the flames two dark objects appear. They fly up into the air and crash onto the mat in front of Vampir, who waits as a very scalded and singed Clyde and JKH slowly make it to their feet…and the flames die down to nothing. Jeff: Well I’ll Be Damned… Duke: Most likely hoss! Dave: I DO NOT BEEEELIEVE IT! Jeff: What? Dave: After all that commotion the World Champion and Clyde are still on their feet! Howard and Clyde look at each other, then over at their attackers. Howard leaps at Vampir and Clyde charges Hessian, taking both men down to what’s left of the mat. An all out brawl erupts in the ring as Howard and Vampir roll around fighting while Clyde and Hessian do the same. Dave: OH NO!! Jeff: OH YES!! Down the ramp come Azreal Cain and Jonathan “Scarecrow” Davis, followed by Bethany and Violator. They sprint down the ramp and hit the ring, kicking and stomping away on Vampir and Hessian. Jeremy gets to his feet and gets nailed by Clyde with a DDT!! Dave: THIS IS CHAOS!! Jeff: It’s not gonna get any better I’m afraid....LOOK!! Two more figures come charging down the ramp. Dave: IT’S DRAKE MAXWELL AND THE REVEREND JON HULL!! Duke: Thinks are looking extremely dark for the champion. Maxwell and Reverend hit the ring and immediately start to lay waste to Violator and Bethany, sending them to the outside. Reverend then grabs Scarecrow and starts to do battle with him. Drake spears Cain off of Vampir and they spill out to the floor. Clyde turns around and is eye to eye with Vampir. Those two start to battle once again as the crowd screams and gasps. Dave: WE HAVE TOTALLY LOST CONTROL!! Jeff: Has there even been a pin attempt yet? Duke: Nope. VP-RK: Hey.....gimme a break. Reverend hits Scarecrow with a clothesline that sends him backwards over the top rope. Vampir hits Clyde with the CUBAN UPRISING, then sends him outside the ring through the ropes. Dave: JEREMY HOWARD IS ALL ALONE WITH THOSE MONSTERS!! Jeff: That’s what happens when you don’t have any friends. The crowd gasps as Vampir, Reverend, Maxwell, and Hessian all move in on the World Champion. Jeremy backs into a corner, throwing fists out at the air to try and fend off his attackers. The gasps from the crowd turns into a massive eruption as Miyagawa comes springing out from the back. Dave: HOWARD DOES INDEED HAVE FRIENDS!! Jeff: I’m sorry, but if I had to rely on Miyajobber to bail me out of an ass-kicking that is one beatdown I would have to take. Miya hits the ring and is immediately met by the massive Hessian who intercepts the goggled one. Reverend, Vampir, and Drake all move in closer to Howard as Miya fights to help him. Suddenly from over the guardrail comes Gaijin, who slides into the ring and jumps on Drake Maxwell. Dave: IT’S GAIJIN!! Jeff: So? Dave: IS GAIJIN PART OF ASYLUM OR DOES HE HAVE A BEEF WITH DRAKE MAXWELL?! Gaijin and Maxwell take their fight to the outside where they get tangled up with Azreal Cain. The three of them battle over the guardrail and into the crowd. Reverend and Vampir lunge at Howard and start to beat him down to the mat. The two men kick and stomp the World Champion mercilessly as he tries to fight back to no avail. Dave: I DON’T KNOW WHAT THE HELL IS GOING IN HERE?!?! WE HAVE REACHED A POINT OF NO RETURN!! Jeff: What? Dave: Hell, I don’t know. This is madness. Clyde rolls back into the ring and attacks Vampir from behind, nailing him with a REVERSE DDT!! Reverend spins around, which allows Jeremy to get to his feet and hoist the Reverend up and nail him with the KRAYZIE DEATH SLAM!! Jeremy celebrates over Reverend, but fails to notice Clyde behind him until it’s too late. The Burned Man spins Howard around and kicks him in the gut, then nails him with a PILEDRIVER!!! The nearly unconscious Howard lies limply over Clyde as the referee drops to make the count......1........2.......3!! Winner: JEREMY “KRAYZIE” HOWARD!! Dave: WHAT A TURN OF EVENTS!! DARKSTAR COMES OUT AND SCREWS CLYDE OUT OF THE WORLD TITLE WHILE SAVING JEREMY HOWARD!! Jeff: This can’t be! DA MAN had this thing won! Gaijin slides in the ring as well and the four men stand triumphant in what is left of the destroyed ring. Dave: COULD THIS BE THE NEW MAKEUP OF ASYLUM?! Jeff: NOOOO!! THESE GUYS ARE ALL JOBBERS!! Dave: WE'RE OUT OF TIME!! HOPEFULLY WE'LL HAVE SOME ANSWERS FOR YOU ON TURMOIL!! GOOD NIGHT!!
