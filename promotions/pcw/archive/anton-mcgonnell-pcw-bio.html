Anton McGonnell Biography Real Name Anton McGonnell Biography (Please Note: This Biography is done in the form of a roleplay.) Life is a strange thing. But youth is even stranger. A simple boy with nothing but a simple life. So many young boys can say that. Even Anton McGonnell, well at least he used to be able to say that. You see the thing that people say makes the world go around, and the thing many people would kill, rob or do some other terrible deed is what can effect people’s lives the most. The thing that people hate but cannot do without, money seems to be the source of some ridiculous and sometimes evil misfortunes and situations. Anton McGonnell lived a normal life in a normal village in Ireland. He was happy and had a well off family. He had a father who was a teacher, a mother who was a Bank Worker, and had four older sisters. He sometimes felt lonely without any brothers and none of his friends really lived that close to him, but he didn’t mind. He was happy and content with his house, it was yellow and was very big, it had a beautiful living room with many luxurious ornaments. He liked his own room, which was covered with sports posters. He loved his school as well; it was a small school about two miles away from him. He liked it because they had lots of sports teams and he was played for of all of them, except the netball team. Which consisted of all girls. So really he didn’t have anything to complain about. He was good at school and was one of the top students. He had a good home with a good family and lived an average boys life. He never really thought anything amazing would happen in his life, but that was before his mother got a call from Anton’s auntie in New York. She was very sick and her husband had recently died. She had two children, one was three years old and the other was eleven months old and she couldn’t cope on her own anymore. She was on her deathbed and needed support. So in a wild and out-of-the-blue decision the McGonnell family headed over to New York to support Anton’s Auntie. They flew over on January the seventh 1994. Anton was six at the time. He was beginning to realise the thrills and heartaches of life. Like any young child his age he was curious and wanted to know about everything around him. When they got to America they realised what an amazing place it was. It truly was the land of opportunity. Everything you needed was so close and it was the perfect place to raise a young family. So as time went on Anthony and Sheila, Anton’s parents, decided to stay, with the agreement of all the family. Anton who had been living a normal boys life since he had come out into this wide world was finally having a taste of adventure. Which he just couldn’t help loving. When he was in New York for two months he joined the local school. He loved it. He made friends instantly. Because of his good sporting ability and sense of humour everybody liked him. Anton couldn’t believe how well things were going for him. Things had never been so great and at the age of eight he had settled in and loved every moment of his life. Every flicker of amusement and every twinkle of laughter made him enjoy it even more. He considered himself to be the world’s luckiest boy. He had also found a love for something in particular, wrestling. Everyday when he came home from school he turned on the television in his kitchen and watched the first wrestling show he could find. He watched it all day and in breaks he practised the moves, and his parents noticed he was very good at it, although they found it very dangerous. Then one day he heard his friends in school talking about it and he was over the moon. He couldn’t believe that these boys that he had known for over two years had never mentioned to him their love for wrestling, but he was thrilled that they did. Never before had he dared tell them in case they didn’t like wrestling and made fun of him. That was when things really began to pick up for him, everyday after school his friends then came over to his house and they watched wrestling together, and at the breaks they all played wrestling together. They had multiple tournaments, which Anton always seemed to win. He had amazing Technical and High-flying manoeuvres, and he had recently started Martial Arts classes. His friends admired him for this and saw a talent in his ability but it was just to early to tell how good he could be. So as time past Anton’s life got better and better. At the age of ten he had captained both his school soccer and basketball teams to cup glory. This had made him very popular with his friends. He was new to the sport of basketball just two years previous but because of his talented sporting ability he learned fast and soon became very good at it. While he was making a lot male friends he also had gotten a lot of female friends. And one in particular, she name was Alicia. She had golden blonde hair that came past her shoulders and was about 5’4 at the time, only an inch smaller than Anton. Anton and her liked each other instantly but neither had the guts to say anything. Finally then one of Anton’s friends asked her to go out with Anton and when she accepted this made Anton even happier, which he didn’t think was possible. But that all was soon to change… Now at the age of thirteen, Anton’s friends still came to his house and watched wrestling. Then one day after an enjoyable event of ‘PCW Turmoil’ his friends watch a commercial on television. It said “If you believe in your wrestling abilities why don’t you try and make it in the big leagues. The PWA (Primetime Wrestling Academy) is the world’s leading wrestling academy for future wrestling stars. This is not to be taken lightly this is where the best of the best start and it is tough at the top.” A deep voice said with a sense of fear detected. The number and information was on the screen and Anton and his friends decided to take this information down on some paper. So when they took it down a PWA Banner came up on the screen and under it said ‘The best get a PCW Contract, and remember, it’s tough at the top’ they all thought this was cool and after a long discussion about it they decided to have a little fun. One of them would send in their information to the PWA and see if they get a response. They wouldn’t lie about it, they would tell the truth about their physical appearance, experience etc. So when Anton drew the shortest straw he was the one that would send in the info. So when they had finished and put it in the mail they had all had their laugh for the day so Anton’s friends went home. But that night in bed Anton had a bad feeling, he felt that something was wrong, not a big thing though. How wrong he was because what was about to happen to him was no small thing. So now, two months later he was walking home from school with a soccer ball tucked under an arm and a dirty blue soccer jersey on and is in a very good mood after scoring the winning goal in his soccer match today. He whistles down the street towards his house and looks around the neighbour hood; the houses are all different shapes, sizes and colours. Anton appreciates this, like he appreciates most things. He thinks that is what makes the world so interesting. He looks at one that is two stories high, had a while paint coat on it and has a beautiful big garden, then he looks at a small yellow bungalow. Then he gets to his own house, which is a one and a half story house that has a pale yellow coat and a small but neat garden. He notices that there is no one home so he decides to do his good deed for the day and take in the mail. He opens the door of the house with his spare key and puts the mail on the kitchen counter. He then goes over to the telephone and presses down multiple numbers and leans against the wall beside him and waits for an answer. When he gets the answer we can only hear Anton’s side of the conversation. “Hey Andy, are you coming over to watch Fusion today…?” Says Anton in an enthusiastic voice. “Heh, yeah, well thanks I thought it was a good goal too…” “Well, okay, I will see you in half an hour then, see you” Anton then puts the phone down and makes several similar phone calls to various other friends. He then sits down and turns on the television, he then notices the mail, and he decides to go over and check it out. He picks up five pieces of bulky paper and sits back down on the couch. He looks through the letters and notices that three of them are over –due bills. Anton had known for a long time the his parents where in big debt and their family was practically bankrupt because since coming to America Anton’s mother or Father had never managed to get Green Cards, which guarantee American Citizenship. So in other words they couldn’t really get proper jobs. Anton always had known that one day they would have top go back to Ireland because of these crippling debt, but he never let these thoughts get him down from his ‘dream-life’ so he decided to let his parents handle it all. As he continues to look at the mail he finally finds one that makes his heart sink through the ground. There on this while piece of paper in the right-hand corner of it is a PWA logo. Anton couldn’t believe it. He was blinded with multiple thoughts, he didn’t know which one to believe, he didn’t understand. What could this mean, surely he was not accepted into the academy, he was only thirteen for god sake. How could this be possible? His hands were shaking at a rapid pace and his knees had suddenly gone weak. This couldn’t be what he though it was. Anton kept trying in his head to get this realised in his head. He tried to make it sink in that this could not be an acceptance letter. Anton just stared at the front of the letter for what seemed like hours. Then he was interrupted by a knock on the front door. He put the letter down and walked through the kitchen, while still shaking. When he opens the door it reveals five boys that look the same age as Anton. They are all smiling but when they see the look on Anton’s face there faces turn puzzled. “What’s wrong Anton?” A small boy with brown hair and wearing a brown jumper asks Anton this. Anton then lets them in and fills them in on the letter. They stare at him. There faces flushed from its colour. They are all now as white as ghosts. “It can’t possibly be what you think, can it?” one of them says anxiously. They sit there for a long time discussing what it could be. “It is probably just an acknowledgement letter, yeah that has to be it” They all agree that this must be the reason. “ So are you going to open it?” asks Andy, a tall boy who has blonde hair and big ears. After much persuasion Anton finally agrees to open the letter. He slowly takes the ‘wrapping’ of it as his hands shake like never before. The room is quiet and the six boys are all staring in anticipation. Anton then takes out two things. One is a sheet-long letter and the other is a twenty-sheet contract stabled together. He takes the single piece of paper first and his hearts sinks all the way down to hell. It is indeed an acceptance letter. “This can’t be right, it just can’t, I can’t be a wrestler, I am 13 years old for god sake” says Anton with tears filling his eyes. Anton waits in his couch for half an hour with his friends trying to comfort him in the most ‘manly’ way as possible. When his parents come home he fills them in on everything. He tells them about the joke, then about the letter. They stand in front of Anton, their eyes filled with fear, anger and shock. “How could this happen…? “It must be a mistake…” “What were you thinking of…” The repeated these things over and over as they walked anxiously around the room. Finally they told Anton to go outside with his friends, while they read over the contract and letter, and since Anton was already in enough trouble he obeyed instantly. All of them were still in shock, especially Anton. But the other boys were not shocked enough to stop them having a soccer match. A few other of Anton’s friends had arrived and been filled in on today’s ‘Goings on’. Everybody had then forgotten about it and started to have a game of soccer on the street, all except Anton who sat on the wall and watched the match, although he wasn’t really concentrating on the match that much, as he watched the ball flypast him he was thinking about the PWA. How could he possibly become a wrestler at thirteen? All of them guys were one hundred times stronger, faster and more resilient than him. He would probably end up dead. As much as he tried to convince himself that it would all blow over and the PWA Staff would realise it’s a mistake, but it wouldn’t work. He had a strong feeling in his stomach that this time two weeks his life would be turned up side down. About two hours later when it was beginning to get dark his parents called him in as he said good-bye to his friends and the replied good luck to him. He walked into the house and saw his parent’s faces. He didn’t know what to think by their expressions. It was nearly as if they were about to plead with him. He sat down in a seat beside them. His brown hair was in perfect position and he was still wearing his dirty Blue soccer jersey. He looked puzzled. “So, what’s going on?” he asked in a quiet voice. “Well Anton, you mother and I have read over this contract several times and it seems as if it is Iron-steal, which means there is no way out with out our consent. If we want you in PWA they can do nothing about it.” His Father says as-a-matter-of-factly. “So what, I mean that’s a good thing. Now with your consent I can just tell them I don’t want to be in it. “ Anton says this in s curious voice. His mother and father have a face on as if to tell him that the next thing they were going to say would be difficult. “ Well you see Anton. You know the trouble we have been going through financially. And we just can’t cope anymore. We have tried everything but it just won’t work. If we don’t get sufficient funds soon we will have to give up our home and all our worldly possessions and we will have to move back to Ireland. And at this stage we will probably suffer there too. So, by the looks of your contract here it could mean that not only will we be able to vanquish our crippling debts, but we will also be able to live in luxury. The money you get is amazing and that is only a starting of bases and is the academy as well.” Anton’s face fell expressionless. He could not believe it. Had his parents just asked him to risk his life so they could live a life of luxury? How could this be? These two people were his parents. The people who had raised and loved him all his life. He could not bring himself to believe that they had just asked him what he thought they had asked him. He rose from his seat in a fit of rage. “ Are you serious? You expect me to get into the ring with guys that have been doing this for years that are the best in the business. How can you actually mutter those words to me? This is the most outrageous thing I have ever heard.” Shouts Anton at the top of his voice. He then storms out of the room and into his own bedroom. He lays there for what seemed like hours pondering the thought of fighting against professional wrestlers, sat the height of their career. This wasn’t just wrestling; it was fighting, fighting to prove their worth. They wanted to win so badly they would do just about anything, even cripple a helpless thirteen-year-old boy. But his parents would never ever ask him to do something like this unless it was their complete last option. Their last grasp on life, how could Anton let them down? For once he would have the chance to be known and maybe become a legend. He would be an idle to boys his same age. He has always dreamed of this but never actually thought it could ever happen. He then went back out to the kitchen on calmed mood. His mother and father were still sitting there. They were in complete silence and knew that their last hope had vanished. “Mum, Dad, I’ll do it. I no it will be hard but if it means this much to you I will give it a shot. Hey and maybe I’ll get lucky and become one of the best someday.” His mother and father stared at him in a delighted cascade of shock. His mother went over to him and flung her arms around his shoulders. She was in a fit of tears and his father had as smile on his face that seemed to stretch up to his eyes. Anton lay that night in bed wide-awake. Earlier his parents had been on to PTC Officials and apparently that Iron steal contract was meant for one of wrestling top guys but it got mixed up with the Application files and Anton ended up receiving it. But since it was an Iron-Steal contract there was nothing they could do. Anton would have to become a PWA Wrestler. He knew that his life would never be the same again. He would rarely see his friends and he would have to train vigorously everyday. He would live a completely different life and everything would be different, some for the better, some for the worse. But whatever way he looked at it he now knew that he was no longer Anton a kid. Now he was Anton The Kid.
