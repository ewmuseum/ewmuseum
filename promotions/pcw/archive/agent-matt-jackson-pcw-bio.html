Agent Matt Jackson Biography Real Name Christian Michael MacManus Nicknames The Chameleon, The Hitokiri Hometown Columbia, MO Birthday August 22, 1977 Height 5'9' Weight 202 lbs Biography Not a lot is known about Matt's past. Most of it is in a hole so deep and dark that only the top dogs in Washington can get to it. Even they have some problems getting past all the red tape that surrounds it. Even less is known about his childhood. The only people that would actually know are his parnets and to the best of Matt's knowledge they are both dead. The little that is known is that he was born in Dublin Ireland as Christian MacManus and that name no longer exists to even Matt himself. The little that is known about Matt is mostly rumors and stories that have been leaked. Most of the rumors started about five years ago when two missions happened back to back. The first one in Paris, where Matt was shot and a lot of people were 'said' to be involved. This led to Matt wanting to leave the CIA, so one of the biggest opperations went down in Hong Kong to make sure everyone knew that the famed agent Christian Michael MacManus was dead and this caused the birth of Matthew Joseph Jackson, son to a wealthy family. Everything was all right, nothing too special happening, until ends started not to meet and Matt got the call to return to the place he hated most. Other Feds PWA Other Fed Awards PWA Graduate
