Frisco Real Name: Francisco Cassius Valerius Nick Name: Frisco Catchphase: "I'm the man you hate to love." Character Handler: Vince MSN Account: [detail withheld] AIM Address: [detail withheld] Theme Song: Beethoven's "Ode to Joy" Age: 28 From: Buenos Aires, Argentina Height: 5'11 Weight: 227 Wrestling Style: High Flyer/Technical Signature Moves: 630 Senton, Shooting Star Legdrop, Cerebro Asesino (fireman's carry into DDT), The Migraine (vertical brainbuster onto knee), Alesicoaster (gorilla press into DDT) Finisher Move: F-Bomb (inverted powerbomb, done in many variations) Setup Move: A hard knee to the back. Secondary Finisher: Endorphin Rush (dragon suplex from the top turnbuckle) Current Team: None Past Teams & Groups: DAMN Title History: PCW World Heavyweight Champion Two Time PCW Continental Champion PCW Television Champion PCW Cruiserweight Champion PCW Extreme Champion Awards History: PCW Hall of Fame Inductee Winner of the 1st 64-man World Title Tournament, defeating finalists: Kremmen, Super Duper Masked Jobber King, and Meanstreak at March Madness. Winner of the 64-man Continental Title Tournament, defeating finalists: Jeremy Howard and Ivan Stanislav at Spring Fling. 2001 PCW Wrestler of the Quarter-Year (January-March) 2001 PCW Wrestler of the Half-Year (First Half) Description: Frisco has a medium sized frame with well-defined muscles. He has a light golden brown complexion and jet black, slicked back hair. His handsome face consists of full lips, piercing grayish-green eyes, perfect teeth, and a thin, black goatee. He has a tattoo of a panther on his left bicep and the ace of spades on the underside of his right forearm. He usually wears expensive FRISCACEMANI~! suits, fine jewelry, and dark sunglasses, or on the more casual days, labeled clothing of whatever merchandise he's shilling at the time. In the ring, he wears black velvet pants with matching open shirt, and black patent leather boots. History: COMING EVENTUALLY!
