Skitzo Biography Real Name William Williams Nicknames Mr. Psycho, The Mental One, The One Man Gang Hometown Bayou D'Arbonne Lake, Louisiana Birthday 05-09-1975 Height 6'3' Weight 241 lbs Biography Skitzo’s past is a detailed yet strange one. Born in 1975 to Cletus and Marjory Williams, William “Bill” Williams was an only child, and grew up hunting and selling alligator skins with his father in the Bayou. By Age 7, Cletus could afford to send Bill to school, however Bill was immediately the centre of attention once jibes of “Hey Billy Willy got no edumacation!” and such were thrown at him. A year or two of this tore away at Bill’s mind for months on end, until one day four children found themselves victim of a beating by Bill with sticks and stones, he broke their bones. Bill Williams found himself immediately ejected from school, and upon telling his father and mother of the jokes thrown upon him, the giggles with which they replied sent young Bill on a rampage – if that’s what burning your house down can be called these days – which sent the family into turmoil. Bill stole what money he could from Cletus and Marjory and ran away, aged 10. Bill denounced his life, threw away his history, and made up a new one. Finding a local orphanage, Bill was taken care of and looked after for many years, eventually gaining a good education and finding work in the Bayous and market places. One of few incidents, which led to his current state of affairs, came in 1991 when Bill was 16. During a run to the Bayou to hunt for some Crawfish, Bill was attacked by an Alligator, which took a good bite at his torso, only to come away with one eye left and a broken jaw. The townsfolk were drawn by Bill’s tale, and the scar brought more attention to Bill, until one day young Jimmy Joe Jack – Bill’s best friend and resident dumbass – stated that Bill should take to Memphis and make his claim to fame as a ‘rassler! Being a hick, Bill liked the idea too, and simply shrugged and took up and left off for greater things. Memphis brought much wisdom to Bill, he learned what cars were and what they do when you hit them hard, but he also learned about the Wrestling business in a little old academy run by a big old wrestling promoter. By the time he was 25, Billy Williams was a consistent and hard-working wrestler at the academy. News came of his abilities and background, and friends advised Bill to join up a mainstream Academy called the Primetime Wrestling Academy. Bill was hesitant, but in 2001 he soared to the top of PWA, winning a Class Championship in the Class of Jack Valentine and engaging in a neat little feud with Ritchie Syxx. When the time came, Bill Williams, a.k.a. “Skitzo”, graduated and went through Eternal Wrestling and NTICW before finally landing in PCW with a tag partner from NTICW by the name of Syko. For a few weeks, ‘Xxplosive’ as they were known wrestled with the big names, until Bill “Skitzo” Williams was admitted to the “Silenced Bayous” Mental Asylum in the Bayou D’Arbonne, Louisiana. Why he was admitted, no one knows, being that the schizophrenic thing was just a gimmick, but in the year that followed, Bill Williams took a turn for the worse. 343 days in Solitary Confinement changed him for the worse. All there was to do to keep him going was exercise, Morse code through the pipes and…talking to himself. Subconsciously, Williams made up a friend to talk to, who for the 343 days would listen to Bill’s thoughts and hopes…and share his nightmares. As each day passed Bill became more and more paranoid, so much so his gimmick of “Skitzo” became a nickname given to him by the guards and patients at the Asylum. On the day of his release, Bill Williams was no more, for he had entered the Asylum no more than a man, and left nothing more than an animal. His alter ego kept at bay, and Skitzo’s only means of living came from the one thing he knew how to do – Wrestle. PCW maintains his sanity, wrestling stops him from going over the edge. But a few weeks upon entering the PCW, Skitzo quickly resumed his alliance with Syko The Goblin, only for the pair of weirdos to be split up abruptly as Syko was removed from PCW, and while things for the Goblin were going downhill, Skitzo managed to obtain the PCW Soda Endorsement contract at an Alamo Match during Alliances 2K2! The new era of Gatorade was upon the people, and one of Skitzo's career highlights for sure! The Mental One had arrived! Following Skitzo's big PCW Alliances win, he found himself gunning for Pranks With Attitude after a small argument, perhaps even a misunderstanding, about Gatorade, Tag Titles, and possibly someone's mother. In a match where Skitzo was scheduled to tag with a mystery partner, it was discovered he was facing Pranks With Attitude in a tag team with himself and...himself! During the actual match, Nerezza drove his boot into the spine of Skitzo, the blow of which paralyzed Skitzo and actually killed him, reducing his number of split personas to 86! After the gruelling, action-packed battle that barely finished before the time was up, Skitzo got his own back after being beaten by Gaz Green and Nerezza by devastating them, throwing them into a pit of horse manure through some thick sheets of glass inside a 600lbs cage with a crane! One thing was for sure after this devious act, Skitzo and P.W.A. were at war! And indeed after a couple of weeks of fussin', feudin' and fightin', Skitzo found himself at the hands of Statuz Quo, and indulged in a racial war that ended with the clubbing of Quo with a 20' black rubber dildo...the legacy continued...
