PCW match: Trigger vs. Duzza

24 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Trigger vs. Duzza Written: 5/24/02 “Dead On Arrival” by X-Raided hits and Trigger walks from the back. Clay: The following contest is scheduled for..... A hush of anticipation spreads through the building... Clay: Wait for it... Dave: This is getting ridiculous! Clay: Nearly there.... Jeff: SAY IT DAMMIT!!! Clay: ONE FALL!!!!! Crowd: ONE FALL! ONE FALL! ONE FALL!!! Clay: Introducing first, from Portland, Oregon.... Weighing 230 pounds.... TRIGGER!!! Jeff: From Only Fools & Horses? Duke: Behave. Like anyone from outside the UK will get that joke anyway. As Trigger slides into the ring, “Harlem World” by P Diddy and Ma$e hits and Duzza walks from the back. Clay: His opponent.... From Harlem, New York... Weighing 238 pounds.... DUZZA!!! Jeff: ARGH DUZZA!!! *SMACK* Duzza slides into the ring and the pair of them immediately start trading rights. *Ding* *Ding* *Ding* Dave: This one is underway early here, with Duzza taking control. Duzza backs Trigger into the corner and whips him across the ring but as he charges in, Trigger explodes out of the corner with a CLOTHESLINE THAT NEARLY BEHEADS the native of Harlem. Jeff: HAHA!!! Duzza’s a retard! Dave: Er... Jeff, Trigger is a face... Jeff: I mean... Er.... DAMN TRIGGER!!! Duzza gets up and staggers back at Trigger who picks him up AND PLANTS HIM WITH A SPINEBUSTER!!! 1............2.....SHOULDER UP!!! Dave: Duzza is in trouble early here... Trigger is all over him! Trigger pulls him up and hoists him over in a VERTICAL SUPLEX then floats over for a cover. 1..............2..........NO!!! Jeff: DUZZA! HE IS LIKED BY THE FANS, DON’T GIVE THEM ANY SATISFACTION!!! Duke: That your moto for Women too.... Leave them unsatisfied. Jeff: Ask Kern’s Mom! Dave: This isn’t NTICW you know! Duzza is pulled up and Trigger gets him set for a Powerbomb BUT DUZZA LIFTS HIM UP AND OVER WITH A BACKDROP!!! Trigger gets right back up but Duzza CATCHES HIM WITH A BELLY TO BELLY SUPLEX!!! Jeff: Come on Duzza.... Urgh.... Can’t believe I just said that! Dave: That suplex knocked the wind out of Trigger there... Duzza pounces on Trigger and unloads right hand after right hands into the newbies head before dragging him up and planting him back down with a HANGMAN’S NECKBREAKER!!! 1....................2........SHOULDER UP!!! Dave: Duzza has Trigger stunned here.... Can he capitalize? Jeff: Who cares. Duke: A little enthusiasm? Jeff: WHO CARES?!?!?!?!? That better? Duzza scoops Trigger up onto his shoulder but Trigger rolls forward AND PLANTS DUZZA’S HEAD INTO THE MAT WITH A DDT!!!! Dave: Trigger just pulled something out of the bag there!!! Jeff: Hmmmm... Both men down... Duke: Huh? Trigger groggily rolls over and drapes an arm over the chest of Duzza……1……..2…….3!! Winner: TRIGGER! The referee pulls Triggers near lifeless arm in the air to signify the win. Dave: Who’s that?!?!? Suddenly a figure hops over the guard rail and rolls into the ring. The referee turns around only to be grabbed by the head and tossed over the top rope. Dave: That’s.... That’s.... GOEREN!!!! Jeff: YES!!!! Duke: He just threw the ref from the ring! Dave: He’s left with Duzza and Trigger!!! Jeff: LET THE JOBBERS HIT THE FLOOR! LET THE JOBBERS HIT THE FLOOR! LET THE JOBBERS HIT THE FLOOR! LET THE JOBBER HIT THE...... FLOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOR!!!!!! Dave: SHUT UP JEFF!!! Jeff: FUCK YOU MARX! I’M MARKING OUT! Trigger pulls himself up onto his knees but Goeren just looks down at him before NEARLY DECAPITATING HIM WITH A ROUNDHOUSE KICK!!! Jeff: FUCK ME SQUARELY UP THE ASS THIS IS MAGNIFICENT!!! Dave: ....... Duke: ...... Jeff: Figure of speech. Goeren grabs Trigger and sits him on the top rope before KNOCKING HIM OFF AND DOWN TO THE FLOOR WITH A JUMPING SIDE KICK!!! Jeff: AS I SAID... LET THE JOBBERS HIT THE FLOOR! Dave: What the hell does he want.... Is he even signed? As Goeren looks down at Trigger with a smirk on his face, he suddenly turns and looks at Duzza who is slowly coming too. He strolls over and grins as he delivers a low side kick RIGHT TO DUZZA’S GROIN!!! Jeff: HAHA!!! HE JUST STERILISED DUZZA!!! Duzza collapses but Goeren catches him and pushes him back to the ropes where he ties Duzza’s arms in them then rolls to the outside. Jeff: Oh.... PCW just got better!!! Dave: What is he doing now!!! On the outside Goeren grabs a chair and folds it up, nailing Trigger right on the back of the head before sliding into the ring and taking some steps back from Duzza and holding up the chair. Jeff: YES!!! DO IT!!! Goeren then charges at Duzza and WAFFLES HIM WITH THE CHAIR TO THE HEAD SO HARD THE SEAT FLIES OUT AND INTO THE CROWD!!! Jeff: Oh.... This is a GREAT day!!! Duke: What the fuck does he think he is proving? Jeff: How superior he is!!! Goeren looks at the chair, through the hole where the seat was and right at Duzza before he then swings it side on right into Duzza’s throat. Dave: DAMMIT he could have broken his larynx!!! Jeff: We should be so lucky! Dave: But then what will Duzza do if he wants to do an in-card... HE won’t be able to speak... Duke: Yeah Dave.... Whatever.... As Duzza struggles, gasping for breath he frees himself and rolls straight to the outside, grabbing his throat and trying to get air into his lungs. Goeren just casually tosses the chair out of the ring and smirks. Jeff: LET THE JOBBERS HIT THE FLOOR! Oh this RULES!!! Dave: This is sickening... Goeren calmly adjusts his suit and dusts himself off after the brutal attack, staring out at the crowd as the jeers echo in from the stands. He stands in the centre of the ring, his arms outstretched towards the crowd as his gold suit sparkles from the arena light above. He turns towards the announcer's table and motions for Jeff Marx to approach the ring and hand him a nearby PCW microphone. Dave: WHERE ARE YOU GOING?! Jeff: Business calls. Jeff drops his headphones down and heads toward the ring. Goeren leans between the ropes and politely takes the microphone from Marx, slipping him a 20 dollar bill for his good deed. Dave: You SUCK Jeff! Goeren paces about the ring, the same demented smile painted across his face as he addresses the crowd in his thick German accent. Goeren: Now kiddies, I can guarantee that every single one of you dirty mouth breathers out there must be absolutely joygasmic over the fact that I prevented those two yokels from further tainting this show. He continues to pace about the ring, as he runs his hand through his slicked back blonde hair. The crowd boos him unmercifully. Goeren: If it is one thing that I cannot stand in PCW, it is ugly and pathetic ignorants that plague the federation and hold it down from the inside. If I ran Goeren Enterprises in the same manner, I would be scrounging the dumpster behind Wendy's for food like so many of the hacks in American society today. Is there any question to why PCW saw its ratings drop before the roster revamp? If you continue to infest the federation with disgusting lowlifes, of course the fans will turn away from you in droves. Jeff: Tell that to The Phantom Booker! Dave: Who in the hell does this guy think he is? Jeff: He’s Goeren! Duke: He’s a piece of shit. Goeren: That is why your International Megastar has decided to grace you all with the presence of a true representative of athletic talent. I'm not here to entertain my urchin fans or revolutionize Primetime Championship Wrestling, I am here to save it...and mold it...into my own image. The old guard has failed to produce satisfactory results in regards to creating a media uproar that the PCW once had, and now the PCW needs yours truly and the company at my disposal as its wrestling salvation. Dave: Oh, so this jackass is the savior of PCW? Puh-lease! Jeff: He saved us from having to endure more of that jobber match! He’s on a roll so far! Goeren: Goeren Enterprises was built from the ground up by my wealth and personal ambition, and I personally take on the responsibility to bring forth the same order to PCW. No need to thank your Sensation Not From This Nation kiddies, I understand that there hasn't been a true legend and hero in PCW for you all to look up to in a long time. Dave: WHAT?! No heroes? What about Doug Troy? Or Ed? Hell, even Kid Wonder? Jeff: They’re all in OSW you moron! You just made his point for him. Dave: Shit! Goeren: Now for those ugly hacks who miraculously got rehired and have the smallest bit of desire to cross my path, why don't you take a good look at what I did to these two undesirables and realize that crossing Goeren is probably the worst career decision any wrestler could ever make. If I have to cripple all of the 'Triggers' and 'Duzzas' of the world in order to make PCW and its fans understand what they owe to me, then by God this will not end until I have what I desire. I no longer wish to see a PCW controlled by the blind consumers, it is about time that the men with insight and ambition reclaim the reigns of control. Dave: This guy is sick. Jeff: He has a vision, Dave. Follow the dream! Goeren: Regrettably, the only language some of your Jerry's Kids understand is violence, but I have no remorse about making an example out of every wrestler backstage until they realize the greatness of yours truly...and my precious company. So kiddies...make sure you keep spending those joygasmic dollars of yours and just sit back and watch your Megastar...Goeren...downsize the rabble...that beleaguer PCW. Goeren drops the microphone, giving his suit another quick adjustment as he gives a gracious bow out towards the crowd. His eyes light up as if he remembers something, as he quickly snatches up the microphone one more time. Dave: What now? Goeren: One more thing, you can get a head start on your worship of yours truly by visiting my official website. You can even start to order my merchandise and memorabilia on www.GoerenIsBetterThanIAm.com, and get 25%...no...15% off your first purchase! Where else can you get an officially licensed Goeren toaster? Or better yet, ensure the human race's purity by not spreading your inferior seed by purchasing a 12-pack of Goeren condoms! You can't get a better deal with Goerenobillia! Vielen Dank und gut ist! Goeren tosses the microphone down once more, flashing his bright smile out towards the crowd. He calmly jumps off the ring apron, making his way over towards the announce table at ringside. He reaches inside his sports coat and hands out free samples of Goeren Enterprises shampoo to Dave, Duke and Jeff. Each individual packet has his corporate logo imprinted on the packaging. He firmly shakes each of their hands before turning away and makes his way backstage in style. Jeff: GOEREN...... FUCKING...... RULES!!! Dave: Goeren has a problem... Duke: Shampoo looks nice though... Dave: *Sigh* Lets go to commercial... View Trigger 's Biography
