Turmoil 16 9 Jan 2001 The NBAC Arena in Buffalo, NY The mystery man unmasked The events that took place on Turmoil are shown in a nifty looking highlight reel. The endings of all the matches the mystery man interfered in are shown. Everything from the Wulf/Archangel match to the Kreigman/Naket match. In the end, it shows the man interfering in the Doug Troy/Reverend match and eventually revealing himself to be MEANSTREAK., (now known as WAR).
