Turmoil 120 8 Apr 2003 The Delta Center, Salt Lake City, Utah Matt Jackson demands help from Xavier Kannon The scene cuts backstage where Matt Jackson is standing outside the office of Xavier Kannon. He is wearing a grey suit and his face is still rather bruised from the attack at the hands of Lacuna Debris at Fusion. He knocks on the door and the begins to pace outside, hoping that Kannon isn’t busy. Xavier: Come in. Matt pulls the office door open and steps inside. Xavier is stilling at his desk, looking over some papers. Matt pulls out a chair and sits down in front of the desk. He kicks his feet up on Kannon’s desk and places his head in his hands, as he leans back. A smile curls on his lips. Matt: I am not to pleased Xavier, but now that I know you are going to be able to help me, I feel a lot better. Xavier: What exactly are you getting at Jackson? I am busy, so if you could please just hurry this along. Kannon’s eyes are now locked onto the man sitting across from him. Matt: Well at Fusion Lacuna Debris viciously attacked me and of course I am fine, she couldn’t hurt someone, as great as myself. The thing is she busted up Haley pretty bad with that shotgun and now she is sitting in the hospital. The smile fades off of Matt’s face and is replaced by a look of anger. Xavier: You want me to do exactly what? Matt: I want you to fire her! She is interfering with the operation and making it difficult to continue. Without Haley I have no one to take notes on all the great things I am doing and there isn’t anyone to cheer me on. The Scientologist is seemingly unamused by this waste of time and starts to look a little angry he was pulled way from what he was doing for this. Xavier: I brought you in to do a job and I expect you to do it. That is what I am paying you for. So do the job and everything will be fine. The PCW will be nothing soon enough and then she will be fired. Taken a back by his inability to help Matt jumps up from his seat and slams his fist on the desk. Matt: You are going to sit here and do nothing? Xavier: Look I am very busy with Howard and getting ready to free myself of this place. So if you want to do something, then by all means do something, but I can’t be bothered by this waste of time. Rattled by Kannon’s lack of help, Matt turns on his heels and storms out of the office. Slamming the door behind him and walking back out into the hall. He begins pacing up and down the hall, still angry. Matt: If you won’t help me I will take matters into my own hands Kannon. I will make sure those bitches start to hate the name Matt Jackson. With those words, he pulls a small black cell phone out of the inside of his jacket pocket and dials a number. I want to make sure they both suffer for what they did to me. Man: Yes sir, everything should be in place shortly. I will let you know if there are any complications with it.
