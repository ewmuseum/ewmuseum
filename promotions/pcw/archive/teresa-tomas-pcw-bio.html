Teresa Tomas Biography Real Name Teresa Jane Tomas Nicknames Top Rope Temptress, 'The' American Woman Hometown Nashville, Tennessee Birthday 6-9-75 Height 5'7 Weight 140 lbs Biography Daughter of a well respected US Navy Captain; Teresa was what you would call a Navy brat. Growing up, she always hung out with the boys. She found the girls were boring and too wimpy. This of course labeled her as a ‘tomboy’ and eventually, she did become ‘one of the boys’ so to speak. She is known to be tough and stubborn with a temper that can become violent at time. Only the few closest to her has seen her more softer, gentler side as well as what the public sees her as. Due to past experiences in the business field, she knows the rules of the game and takes nothing from anybody, thinking it best to work for what she wants. Besides her wrestling career, she also sponsors her own monster truck with a life long friend and ex as her driver. The two people she calls family are her brother Eric and her mother who both live in her home state of Tennessee just outside of Nashville. Now at the age of 27, she knows her life isn’t even half over and expects to live it to its fullest. Entrance The shadow of Teresa stands at the top of the entrance ramp while red and blue lights blink like strobe lights throughout the arena. Suddenly the music to 'Comin' Through' by Killer Dwarfs blares through the speakers. Teresa comes strolling down the ring wearing her usual camouflage leather pants with black halter-top. Over her shoulders she wears a black leather jacket with both the American and Rebel flag crossing each other. At the top of the jacket written in silver. American Pride and at the bottom is written Southern Heritage . She strides down the ring giving a few high fives and a couple of fingers.
