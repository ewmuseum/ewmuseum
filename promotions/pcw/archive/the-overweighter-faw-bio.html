The Overweighter Biography Nicknames The American Lardass, The Fatman Waddlin Hometown New York Birthday 13/02/77 Height 7'2'ft Weight 535 lbs Biography Bio The Overweighter was born in New York, America. His parents were both English, His father die in America when he was 1 years old. Him and his mother stayed in America, His mother married again and The Overweighter grow up believing that his step-father was his father. His step-father was an overweight biker, The Overweighter started to grow up and acted like his step-father. The Overweighter’s family couldn’t afford to send The Overweighter to school, so The Overweighter was uneducated. When The Overweighter turned 15 years old he was 380lbs and 6’7”ft. He was told by his mother who his father really is, when he was 18 and The Overweighter went to England to meet some of his family he has never met. They told him that his father was one of the funniest men alive, they showed The Overweighter his very own joke book he made and The Overweighter started to act more like his real father. He started to make up his own jokes, he was becoming more and more immature. when he got home to his mother, she straight away saw his father. His step-father gave him his motorcycle, and The Overweighter started to get interested in wrestling. His mother and step-father died in a house fire, The Overweighter was now living on the street and had no where to go. He got a job as a comedian and after a few weeks wreaths of show’s he had enough to get an apartment. He started to learn how to wrestler, while he was a comedian. He got fired from his job and owed money for his wrestling lessons. One day a man showed up on his doorstep, it was his brother he didn’t know about and he has been living in England. His brother was called Candy Cane, he was a wrestler for a small federation. He started to live with The Overweighter and started to teach him how to wrestle for free. The Overweighter joined the small federation, him and his brother was raking in the money. They both brought a house together, The Overweighter was 24 years old, nearly 500lbs and nearly 7”ft. The Overweighter was doing pretty well for himself, when one day Candy Cane seriously injured his knee in a car accident. Candy Cane couldn’t wrestler anymore, it was just The Overweighter bringing in the money. The Overweighter won $10,000,000 from the lottery, started his own business called BiGG Mouth’s Burger Bar. He spent all $10,000,000 on the business, It slowly went out of business because of Mc Dave’s. He was forced to sell it to Mc Dave’s, he promised himself that before he dies he will buy BiGG Mouth’s Burger Bar back. The Overweighter and Candy Cane was running low on money once again, there was a wrestling tournament called The Golden Turnbuckle Tournament and Candy Cane showed The Overweighter all he could before the tournament started. The Overweighter was now 7’2”ft and 535lbs. The Overweighter signed up for the tournament, he won his first few matches and got to his bracket final. He lost to Totally Tubular Tommy, he went home with no money and with out a job. When one day he got a phone call from the FSW Owner offering him a contract, he accepted. FSW closed down before The Overweighter could settle down and went without a job for a long period of time. He started to run out of money again, thats when he applied for PCW. Not thinking he will be accepted he went looking for a job, he worked in a fast food resturant but he got fired on the first day for eating all the food. When he got home that day he found a letter from PCW accepting him to the federation. Is currently working in PCW and hopefully he wont get fired like in the fast food resturant. --==What Overweighter Looks Like==-- The Overweighter is 535lbs and 7'2, he is possible the heaviest wrestler in WO. Overweighter likes to dress in bright clothes and looks like a fool. But he still wears black jeans no matter what other things his wearing. --==His face==-- Overweighter's face is long but still very fat. His checks are huge and has a massive smile on him. He has a beard about a 1mm long but it more looks like he hasn't shaven for a couple of days. His eyes are brown and he has a nose which is boney and big. --==His body==-- Overweighter's body is very big. His chest size is 64 inches. He has a waist size of 67 inches. His belly does look alot bigger that his chest but it's not really. Overweighter has more rolls that a supermarket and isn't afraid to show it off. He has huge breasts they are so big they make playboy models mad. His legs are very chunky they are basically tree trunks. --==His Hair==-- Overweighter likes to think of himself as a rocker and he has long hair. but it's not long enough to headbang. It looks really funny when he decides to comb his hair back. It normally looks scruffy, like he has never combed it ever.
