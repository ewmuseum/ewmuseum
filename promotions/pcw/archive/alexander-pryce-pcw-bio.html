Alexander Pryce Biography Real Name Alexander Pryce Hometown Huntington Beach, California Birthday 1/1/82 Height 6'2 Weight 210 lbs Biography Throughout his life, Alexander has aspired for Pop Super Stardom. Having admired astounding performers such as New Kids on the Block, Michael Jackson and Ricky Martin, Alexander's dream was to one day perform at the Grammy's lip-syncing a duet with Madonna for all the world to see. The journey to stardom started at a young age for Alexander. While other kids were watching The Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles and He-Man cartoons, Alexander worked effortlessly to become a member of the Mickey Mouse Club. Alongside, Britney, Justin and Christina, they peformed together for years, entertaining millions of children around the country. But whereas, the afformentioned people rose to become Pop Superstars in their own right, Alexander failed. But the determination for success shines brighter than ever. Managed by his over-bearing and short tempered mother, Marie Pryce, Alexander is inspired to prove his critics wrong and blaze his way to the top of the music industry. Both Alexander and Marie view wrestling as a way to gain exposure and win over an older audience of male fans who have traditionally shunned the artistic form of enchantment known as 'Pop Music'. He has the looks... The Hairstyle... The dance moves... And the brai... well, at least he can dance... All that remains is a music deal and the world would be his.
