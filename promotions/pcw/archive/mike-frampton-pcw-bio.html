Mike Frampton Biography Biography Mike Frampton also known as Punisher 2000 was born on a cold November night around 5 PM. He was born in 1977 to a mother in Dallas. He had no brother. His father was an alcoholic and treated Mike with no respect. He hit Mike alot until he was 3. When Mike turned 3 the family took a trip from Dallas Texas to Toronto Canada. While there Mike's father took him to an orphanage and left him there. This started the transformation. Mike grew up an isolated boy. He didn't fight until he met another kid. His name was, Rob. Rob and Mike never got along. They fought the usual school yard fights and sometimes wound up spending time in Juvi. Mike and Rob then found peace with eachother. Well, peace when they both met Teddy. Teddy was a rough kid. He liked pushing people around and had his own group of bully friends who liked to fight alongside him. Mike and Rob knew that they were going to have to cross paths, especially since Mike lived across the street from Teddy. That is when Mike and Rob began collecting an army, a gang if you will. The street war was about to begin. It was a warm summer day when the band of youths lead by Mike and Rob met up with Teddy and his friends. A bloody battle ensued. People were stabbed and hit with chains. 15 year olds fighting for their lives was such a site to see. In the end only 2 men were left standing. Mike and Teddy. Rob, Mike's ally was stabbed and left to die in the street. After watching his friend die, Mike Frampton left. P2K was born! Mike grew up into his teens playing in a rock band called Satan's Den. They jammed together and one guy in the band became an important figure in Mike's life. More important than he knew, for the person he was only friends with was in reality his own flesh and blood. His friend and him joined a wrestling school. The two learned the ropes from Sully, the same man who trained Edge. Mike and his friend Sean were to become Punisher 2000 and Rain 2000, the 2000 Squad. The team only lasted a few months. Then Sean and Mike split apart. Sean never to be seen again. Until now. Recently Mike and Sean met again, in the comfort of Five Star Wrestling. They soon became aware that they were brothers, but neither knew that the other was his former band member and tag team partner. But then it all made sense and Mike put it together. TJ Real, Sean Frampton and Rain 2000 were all the same person. Mike's sanity was returned. Mike 'P2K' Frampton forgot Punisher 2000, or at least, is trying.
