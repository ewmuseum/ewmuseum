The Sushi Crew Biography Biography Ryu went to a little japanese school in Osaka, Japan when he was a little kid he kept very much to himself he had not many friends and mainly sat in the corner reading untill a young boy Otoshi came to his school they met and instantly became friends for life and did everything together, they joined PWA and went to NTICW and clamimed the gold there and held it for a long period of time untill losing it to Methothecal disorder at the PPV then they met with the still mysterious Cult leader known only as Mr Morozo and became the champs once again, Joined Mr Morozo's cult called the 'followers of the hidden star' when NTICW closed was recruited to PCW and now he we are Descriptions. Ryu: Medium height, nice build not much muscle, long brown hair tied back into a ponytail, wheres a long dark brown cloak that covers most of his body, has long arm bits that cover his whole arm and dangles to about his knees, his whole face is White with little shades of Black circled around his eyes, his eyes are a slight redish colour and shows no emotion on his face, has his hood over his head Otoshi- Shorter than Ryu by about 12 inches, much smaller build then Ryu, skinnier, has short brown hair that has blonde tips dyed into it, spiked up, he has his hood down, a whiter face then ryu with darker rings of black around his eyes has bluish lips, that are frowning. both men has there arms crossed acrossed their chest looking slightly down to the ground. Mr Morozo- a tall, muscly man with a black Mask covering his whole face, his hands holding each other like a hand shake, he is smiling, he is wearing a black t-shirt with a white logo in the middle and a leather jacket with Brown pants and long brown shoes Finisher Name Raw Sushi
