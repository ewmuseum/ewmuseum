Fusion 61 30 Nov 2001 The Igloo, Pittsburgh PA Blond rage in the weight room The scene fades in from black to a shadowy and somewhat Spartan weight room with the sounds of Sure Shot from the Beastie Boys serving as a musical backdrop. Streams of dim light illuminate the room enough that it can be made out that a heavily muscled, caucasian man is lying on weight bench, pressing what looks to be about 400 lbs, before to the bar, high to the sky, up then down. Up then down… Up then down… The scene suddenly shifts from the weight bench, to a chin up bar, where the same man does chin up after chin up, while sweat glistens off his massive bicepts and perfectly defined, hairless, pecs. The bad lighting in the room prohibits identification of the man, but a glimpse of short-cropped blond hair can be made out upon his head. Again the abruptly cuts to the same man doing sit up after sit up, with his hands clenched behind his head, and his tattoo-less arms covering the sides of his face. The camera focuses in on his perfectly defined six pack, which with ever crunch of a sit up becomes all that much more defined. Once again abruptly shifting, we catch a side shot of the individual doing pushups, giving us a full view of his 6’2 or so body. The camera swings around to a frontal view were catch another look at the man’s short blond hair, as Sure Shot from the Beasties begins to wind down. Just as the song’s base and drums fade out, leaving nothing but the dominant flute from the song, the subject of this montage looks up into the camera. A thing beam of light flashes across the man’s face, but the only thing that can be made out are the man’s blue eyes… …crystal clear, rage filled, blue eyes. The usual opening for Friday Fusion begins to role, featuring PCW wrestlers in action upon a backdrop of green lightning and explosions. Wrestlers highlighted are Clyde, Vampir Nosferatu, DarkStar and Tyler Nelson. The scene fades into the arena section of the Igloo in Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania as pyros shoot off from the ringposts, ceiling and entry way andCherub Rock from the Smashing Pumpkins hits the PA system.
