Sabotage 1 11 Mar 2001 The Palestra on the Campus of the University of Pennsylvania Friday's highlights and the road to Sabotage The show starts by showing highlights of the events that took place Friday night. We see Meanstreak lay out all the members of The Franchise just before they recieve their much deserved pink slips. We see Chef win his match to become the first ever PCW European champion. The return of "The Mack" and his victory over Chris Allen and Joe Cross is highlighted. Finally, the ending of the main event where Meanstreak annihilates everybody, flipping all the DAMN members over in a car, is shown; followed by Lord Crom Griffin Dagoth chokeslamming Meanstreak. He raises his arms triumphantly in the air as we fade into the beginning of the opening. Welcome to the Palestra The opening to PCW Sunday Sabotage starts to the music of "Sabotage" by the Beastie Boys. We see many people running through a sabotaged city. Wrestlers highlighted are Miyagawa, Lord Crom Griffin Dagoth, Guy Leslie, Da Westsidaz, and Reverend. We get an outside shot at the University of Pennsylvania where snow covers the ground as it does along most of the east coast. We then shift inside The Palestra on the campus of the ivy league school. The cameras pan through the crowd to show all the rabid PCW fans that have shown up for this new project. First we spot NHL star Eric Lindros being beat down by a bunch of angry men. Then we spot a couple of nerdy girls holding what appears to be an old Think Tank sign with Alan Kriegman, Howard Porter, PHD, and Nick Taylor pictured. Finally we see a young man wearing an "Edge" shirt and holding an "I Came to See the REAM TEAM!" sign. This leads us to our trio of announcers, Dave Kern, Steven Smith, and Jeff Marx.
