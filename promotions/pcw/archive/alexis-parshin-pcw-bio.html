Alexis Parshin Biography Height 6'10" Weight 355lbs Biography <b>Physical Description:</b> Alexis is a very tall, Arab man. His face is clean shaven, and his short black hair isn't cut short enough for millitary style, but close. His entire upper body is very large, especially his arms. His legs are thick, but they have been injured in wars and required surgery, so he wears knee braces on both knees, but under his camoflouge. Alexis wears a large beige t-shirt with the Uzbek flag on half, sliced in the middle, showing the Afghan flag on the other side. He then wears camoflouge pants and large combat boots, memorable from his days and a soldier, and his current job as a guerilla general. <b>About Raghab Karszat:</b> <i>Raghab Karszat</i> is small, pudgy, Muslim man who is more involved in his own personal needs than that of Alexis, but he would never say so, for a deep fear of the huge man. He will often cheat to gain a win for Alexis, but if he is distracted by something that is of value or an oppurtunity for himself, he will leave ringside. Deep down, Alexis would rather not have him around, but he puts up with the pudgy man anyway. <b>Ring Entrance:</b> 'The Martyr's Blood' plays over the loudspeaker, its opening fast-paced guitar riffs echoing around the arena. Then, the half-Uzbek, half-Afghani flag comes up onto the large screen and the name Alexis Parshin comes across the screen in huge red letters. Then, as the guitar riffs contine to speed up, the immense Uzbek and his associate, Raghab Karszat walk out, Alexis bellowing at the top of his voice. He walks down to the ring, not even bothering to listen to the fans and steps over the top rope, while Raghab stays outside, mostly for lack of being able to climb up to the ring. <b>Match
