"The New Legend" Stephen Blood — PCW biography (November 2001)

21 November 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
WRESTLER NAME: "The New F~cking Legend" Stephen Blood REAL NAME: REALLY Stephen Blood HOMETOWN: Cleveland, Ohio CURRENT RESIDENCE: Milwaukee, Wisconsin HEIGHT: 6' 3" WEIGHT: 283 lbs FINISHING MANEUVER: The Endpoint FINISHER DESCRIPTION: Stephen hoists his opponent onto his right shoulder, facedown, then throws his own body to the right, driving his opponent's neck, head and shoulders into the mat like an Emerald Fusion slam SIGNATURE MOVE: The Crash Bomb CRASH BOMB DESCRIPTION: Stephen picks his opponent up to perform a powerbomb, then flips them forward and slams them down into a facebuster SIGNATURE MOVE: The Vicious Cycle VICIOUS CYCLE DESCRIPTION: Stephen leaps to the top turnbuckle, spreads his arms, then crosses them over his chest as he delivers a Shooting Star Press SIGNATURE MOVE: The Stephenflow DDT STEPHENFLOW DESCRIPTION: Stephen yells, "Assclown!" at the top of his lungs, kicks his opponent in the crotch, then sweeps backward with a snap flowing DDT STYLE: Brawler/Technician OTHER MOVES: Death Valley Driver, Jumping Piledriver, Hanging Vertical Suplex, Top Rope Spinning Wheel kick, Suicide Dive Through Ropes, Smashing Head With Section Of Guardrail, Slingshot Spinebuster, Top Rope Moonsault, Flurry Of Punches In Corner, Low Blow, Sak-Fu Stomp, Multiple Chair Shots PHYSICAL DESCRIPTION: Stephen is very well-built and bears many scars from his years on the streets. He has many tattoos, most notably the one bearing the word "SINNER" in Old English lettering across the upper part of his abdomen. A sickle-shaped pattern can also clearly be seen on the side of his neck, as can a set of tribal howling wolf heads on each of his biceps. If he happens to not be wearing gloves, the word "Forgiveness" is on his left hand and "Vengeance" is on his right. In addition to his tattoos, he also has both ears and his right eyebrow pierced. His head is shaved bald and he has a "Jeff Hardy" style for his facial hair. His ring attire consists of baggy black vinyl pants, black combat boots, a flannel tied around his waist, black fingerless gloves and a black bandanna with skulls and roses adorning it tied "biker-style" on his head. Away from the ring, he has a tendency to keep his "street tough" image, often sporting black t-shirts, black leather Harley Davidson jackets and other such garb. ENTRANCE: The arena lights dim low and video clips of Stephen's past matches spliced with parts of "Ninja Scroll" and "Crying Freeman" play as red strobe lights flash and "Bad Brother" by The Infidels and Juliette Lewis blares throughout the arena. Stephen Blood steps out in his ring attire with wife Stacey in tow. He is carrying a six-pack of either Killian's or Heineken and when he steps out to the entrance ramp, he breaks one from the ring, raises it to the crowd, cracks it and pounds it. As he walks to the ring, he cracks and pounds another. He then leaves his beer in the corner so he can enjoy the rest later. MANAGER: Stacey Blood, Stephen's wife, was once Stacey Blitsch of WSL Rollerjam fame. She is a very voluptuous vixen with an incredible athletic build and cantaloupe-sized knockers. She always has a deep tan and very long, silky blonde hair. When she accompanies Stephen to the ring, she typically wears black vinyl pants, a Stephen Blood baby tee and a white short-sleeved collar shirt. FUN FACTS: Stephen is a brawler and a drinker, plain and simple. He is in PCW more or less just for beer money. He loves cursing people out as he pounds them down, often making up really cool-sounding words on the spot. For instance, he considers each and every one of his opponents to be a "No-talent ASSCLOWN" and vows to "Knock the living piss out of those piss-lapping PISSCLAMS!" BACKGROUND: Stephen grew up on the streets of Cleveland. After briefly running with gangs and the like, he was able to find legitimate work at the docks doing hard labor, usually loading and unloading boats and stacking pallets. One day someone started an idea of an after-hours "fighting tournament". Entrance was twenty bucks and the winner would keep all the entrance money. Stephen figured why the hell not--he'd had nothing better to do, anyway. His harsh upbringing and street survival tactics took him through each of his opponents and he actually found himself the winner out of forty men and 800 bucks richer. Word got out to the surrounding areas--more guys wound up entering and more money was involved. As soon as Stephen had amassed roughly 15 grand, he hopped a bus to Lima and began training under Al Snow so he could get paid for doing the one thing he knew he was good at--beating people up. When he was ready, he signed on with Primetime Championship Wrestling, where he has come into his own. He had floundered around the singles cards for a brief moment before the untimely death of Judas Steele. The funeral marked a turning point in Stephen's career. A battle royal was held throughout the cemetary for the coveted PCW World Tag Team Championship Belts. Stephen found the first atop the weather vane on the church belltower. His longtime friend Brock Newbludd was able to find the second in the casket of Steele. That day went down in PCW history as the birth of one of the greatest tag teams ever, The Blood Brothers, who would go on to be four time tag team champions and the Tag Team Of The Year 2000. After that fourth loss, Stephen beat his long-time partner and friend Brock for the coveted PCW Extreme Championship before losing it to Mike Extreme just days later. But that singles championship showed Stephen that he had the ability to go far in singles competition. He has been considering a feat that would top his record four-time tag championship reign. And for Stephen, that means one thing--acquiring every single PCW title in existence.
