PCW match: Mock Match: Snow vs. Chris Chase (World Champion) (W)

12 April 2002

Mock Match: Snow vs. Chris Chase (World Champion) (W) Written: April 12, 2002 Clay: Coming to the ring first PCW’s former Heavyweight Champion… Snow!!! 'Right Next Door to Hell' by GnR blares and Snow paces to the ring to the jeers of the crowd. He tosses his shirt on the turnbuckle and rotates his arms as he waits for Chris Chase. Clay: And now introducing PCW’s World Heavyweight Champion… Clay is cut off as “One Vision” by Queen hits and Chris Chase comes charging down to the ring. Dave: Chase is wasting no time! Chris slides into the ring and stares at Snow thrusting the World Title into the air and then handing it to the ref. As he turns around Snow is there to with a Superkick nailing Chase up and back and down to the mat. Jeff: SNAKE EYES!!! Snow quickly jumps on top and covers Chase as Leo Micelli counts the pin 1..2..3! Winner: Snow! Dave: What the HELL?!?!?! The crowd is silent in shock as Snow jumps up to his feet raising his hands in victory. Jeff: That was better then expected. Duke: Hold on a second, show that reply again except in slow motion Hoss. The production crew responds and even gets close up on the kick. Snow stares at the jumbotron as the reply clearly shows that Snow’s kick was about a foot away from Chase’s face and the crowd starts to boo loudly. Dave: IT WAS A PHANTOM KICK!!! THIS WAS A SHAM!!! Chase gets to his feet and walks over to the corner grabbing the World Title belt and handing it to Snow as the debris and garbage starts to litter the ring. View Snow 's Biography Back
