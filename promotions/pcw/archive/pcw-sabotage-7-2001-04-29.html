The scene then shifts to replays of Meanstreak being hauled off to the slammer by no less than 15 law enforcement officers, enhanced by a looping soundtrack of Meanstreak's parting ROAR enhancing the image of him being attacked with mace and tazers, then being shackled and dragged away to eventually be incarcerated in the Pittsgrove Federal Penitentiary, Brownsville, Texas.  This is followed up by some stills of Trashcan Man holding the PCW Heavyweight Title aloft in his 15 seconds of fame, Tyler Nelson becoming the second two-time PCW Heavyweight Champ, and Stewart Hix outsmarting him and making away with the title with the assist from Jake Keeton and Slade, then closing out with a slow-motion replay of Doug Troy in pursuit of Stewart Hix but being taken out by the Red Ford pickup.then the scene fades to black.
