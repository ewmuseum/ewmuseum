Heaven Rayne Biography Height 5'7" Weight 131lbs lbs Biography Heaven Rayne grew up thinking that she was born on September 3, 1982 in New York City, New York. She was an only child and lived with both of her parents. Previous to Heaven’s birth, her parents had lived in a run-down apartment in a bad part of New York City. Neither one of them had a stable job, and as a result they lived very tight, scraping for every need. By the time Heaven was born her father had landed a decent job and could support the family, and his job transferred them to Las Vegas, Nevada, where Heaven was raised as a baby. Heaven had a pretty normal childhood, and she was happy. She was close to both of her parents, until she was about nine or ten years old. By that time, her father was a full time lawyer, and he was becoming very successful. Most would consider that great, since ten years previous he was living in a dilapidated tenement with no real job. But the farther up the success ladder he would climb, the more drive he had to continue climbing, and the higher he climbed the less he was home. Heaven hardly saw him after she was in the fourth or fifth grade. It saddened her to lose the closeness she once had with her father, but she held the pain inside, and continued on living as if she didn’t notice any changes. He worked when he didn’t have to, sometimes not coming home until two or three o’clock in the morning. Heaven knew this because she was curious and actually stayed up a few times, just to find out exactly when she’d see his red sports car pulling in the drive. He was no longer there to tuck her in under her pink covers and read her stories at bedtime, and he was never there when she got home from school to ask how her day was. The only time Heaven saw her dad was at breakfast in the mornings before school. It was like he became an entirely different person once he began to succeed in his career….Heaven went from spending all her spare time with him, and watching wrestling with him in the evenings to seeing him read “The Wall Street Journal,” at the breakfast table. And it hurt to see her dad when she did, because he would act like he didn’t even know her. He was always in his own world, and after awhile, Heaven just had to adapt. Heaven was the kind of child who always adjusted well to everything anyways. Besides she had Mick. Mick moved in next door from Heaven when they were both three years old, and they immediately began partners in building mud pies and sand castles in the sandbox. They were best friends from day one and they became inseparable. From building mud pies, to learning to ride bikes, to starting school, to roller skating, to swimming, to catching fireflies, to playing video games to swinging in the park, to catching chicken pox, to having their first kiss….Heaven and Mick did everything together. Having Mick next door, Heaven’s childhood never had a dull moment. When Heaven and Mick started kindergarten they met a new friend. Her name was Sarah, and she was the pretties little dark headed girl with big brown eyes and movie star smile. Everybody liked Sarah, it was hard not to, and pretty soon Sarah, Mick, and Heaven became like the three Musketeers. From kindergarten all through school, the three of them stayed close. They would all meet over at Heaven’s house and watch professional wrestling on cable television twice a week. Heaven always said it was her dream to become a professional wrestler and be like the greats she watched on TV. In fourth grade, a new boy named Jason transferred schools, and ended up in Heaven’s class. Heaven had a secret crush on him, and by the seventh grade they started going steady. By then Jason had been in the same class with Heaven, Sarah, and Mick all at least once, so he knew all of them, and they all got along. When Heaven and Jason started going steady, he started joining them all to watch wrestling as a part of the group. Wrestling was a common interest among the friends; however, it became less important to Heaven as she became more serious in her relationship with Jason. By the time Heaven and Jason were fifteen, Heaven had decided to give up her dream of pursuing a wrestling career, because marrying Jason was more important. Although they were only fifteen, they loved each other very much and had deep commitments. After all, they had been together for two years. They planned to get married and settle down after they graduated. A few superficial years went by, and Jason and Heaven stayed together. By senior year of high school they were involved in a long distance relationship of five years. They became each other’s world. Heaven was happier than she had been in her entire life…in a few months she’s be graduating and planning her wedding. Of course she’d go to college, but she and Jason would do that together. Finally, graduation came…walking down the aisle in that blue cap and gown, Heaven felt so accomplished! Having her boyfriend, and two best friends there with her made it even more significant. It was like she had always dreamt. Sarah was the class Valedictorian, and she had to get up on stage to give a speech which brought Heaven to tears with its beauty. Three weeks after graduation, June 17, 2002, Jason was driving Heaven, Mick and Sarah in his red Mercedes Benz Convertible with white leather interior and no top. They were riding through Las Vegas, Nevada at ten o’clock at night. They were coming back from a party of a previous classmate, and they were all four drunk. Why they let Jason drive drunk is a mystery, besides the fact that the other three were just as drunk and had no access to common sense. They were all laughing, joking, and having the time of their lives. The excitement of partying and drinking was exaggerated by the multicolored blinking lights flashing crazily throughout the city known as the “Sin City.” At this point, they had so much going for them…Sarah was the valedictorian and on her way to Harvard University, Mick was a high school quarter back and was on his way to play football for UCLA, Heaven and Jason were planning on getting married and getting degrees at a local college. They were at the ecstasy of the most exciting times in their lives…but in an instant that all changed! Suddenly the tires of the Benz began to squeal immensely on the black paved road…glass was broken and shattered, screams of utter fear were heard. Heaven started to vomit uncontrollably before all went black. She woke up three hours later alone in a lonely cold hospital room, with two broken legs and a mild concussion, wondering what happened. Heaven’s injuries were very mild, making her the luckiest of all. It was actually a mystery to the doctors that Heaven’s injuries were so slight considering the type of wreck she was in and the condition of the other passengers. Jason was killed instantly, there was nothing the doctors could do for him at all, and he was announced dead on the scene. Mick was put into intensive care for a while but eventually died as well. Sarah, the beautiful valedictorian who once held such a promising future was put into a coma to which she never recovered. To this day, two years later, she is still in the coma. The day Heaven got home from the hospital; she was propped up on the couch with her legs in casts. Her mother had to talk to her and break the horrible news to her about her friends. After a few minutes of the conversation, Janet Rayne, Heaven’s mother decided it was time to just say what had to be said. 'Heaven,' her mother said irrationally as though she had been waiting for the right chance to speak for a very long time. 'Jason hasn't called you to ask how you are doing....,” Heaven’s mother said trying to get the nerve up to tell about the tragic death of her boyfriend. 'I know, I'm beginning to get worried. Have you heard anything about him? What about Sarah or Mick? Maybe...you could wheel me next door to visit Mick later. I bet he needs some cheering up. I know I do. I'm already tired of lying around. 'Honey, I don't think you are ready to visit Mick yet. He isn't going to be how you expect.' 'Mom, don't be silly! Mick and I have been through everything together. We've lived next door to each other since we were three. He sat with me for four whole days when I had the chicken pox. (Heaven laughs at the memory) And, then he caught them from me. He had them for a whole week. Luckily, it was the summer, so I got to sit with him.' 'Heaven, Mick is still at the hospital. He's in intensive care, and the doctors say if he makes it, he may never be able to walk again,' her mother blurted out all in one breathe and starts' to cry. 'OOH my gosh!' Heaven reached up and placed her hand over her mouth as the salty tears begin to roll down her tan cheeks. 'Mick!....Mom you have to tell me the rest. I know you have more news...I can see it in your eyes. What is it? Is it Sarah? Is she hurt, too!?” 'Well...Baby, I’m sorry...but, Sarah is in a coma.' By this time, Heaven was too shocked to say anything. She could only stare blankly as her mother's face grew even more twisted. Her eyes squinted into tiny slits as she forced her mouth to open wide enough to push out words that would bring pain. Finally, she was able to speak, 'Heaven, Jason was injured the most severely. There was nothing the hospital could do for him he just......' Before her mother could finish, Heaven realized what her mother was saying and almost went crazy with emotions, not wanting to believe the truth. 'No, I don't fucking believe you! You hate Jason and all because you think I'm growing up too fast! Mother, I'm eighteen, and I have graduated now! Your precious little girl has grown up! So face it, and stop blaming Jason!' Heaven stopped shouting, and instantly broke down. She knew what her mother said was the truth, and that she was only pushing these accusations on her to ignore the dose of reality, but it was the only thing she could say. In the months that followed, Heaven became extremely depressed, and wanted very much to die. Her mother had great concern for her and made numerous counseling appointments. It didn’t work, because Heaven didn’t want to talk to people any more. The person she was closest to and wanted to spend her whole life with was dead, along with her two best friends in the whole world. Heaven’s heart grew cold. She hated people, life, and even God, because she felt he cursed her. She stopped going to church regularly and basically forgot all the good things she ever experienced and every good thing she was ever taught. However, by the time her legs came out of the casts, Heaven was passed the “I want to die too stage,” and she eventually wanted to live again. In fact, she wanted to live so bad that she wanted to live for herself and only herself. She became selfish, and narrow minded. Even though she decided to live, she still didn’t want to have much to do with people. She became withdrawn. She did visit Sarah at the hospital and talked to her in hopes that she could hear her. Other than that, she didn’t talk much to anybody. Maybe it was because she felt guilty for the deaths and injuries of her friends, since Heaven got drunk for the first time the night of the wreck, when usually she was the one who didn’t drink and could drive her friends safely home after parties. Maybe she thought by visiting Sarah she was somehow making up for it a little bit. Or, maybe it was just because Sarah was the only one who couldn’t talk back to lecture her about her new way of life. By the time Heaven was finished with her physical therapy, she had decided that she had had enough of the town, and enough of her life. She wanted to get rid of all the bad memories it held, and she decided to move away. She decided to move somewhere quaint, like a suburb, where there would be no loud cars and blinking lights to bring back pain. Heaven also decided that since Jason was gone and there was no one else with whom she cared to spend a life with, she would indeed pursue her childhood dream of being a professional wrestler. So said a few words of goodbyes, packed her belongings and moved to a small suburban town, just out side of Nevada, four hours away. Heaven bought a little house just big enough for her and, a little white and black kitten which she named Cloe to keep her company. Heaven had just begun to settle down and regain happiness and contentment, when her mother paid her a visit one rainy day. It was then that she told Heaven the truth…. She said that in 1980 she had to have a full hysterectomy, a surgery which involves removing the uterus. Because of this she was unable to bear children. The bills she owed to the hospital were one of the reasons she and Heaven’s father were so poor in those days, living in a rundown apartment in New York, besides the fact that they didn’t yet have good jobs. Nevertheless they very much wanted children, and prayed continuously for the money to adopt. On September 3, 1982, she was walking home alone late at night. She was mugged and beat up in a dark alley, blacked out. When she awoke there was a lady in white standing before her who claimed to be an angel. She said she had saved her life and brought her a miracle. The miracle was a three month old baby girl, her name…was Heaven. The angel said that the baby she held in her arms was an angel, just brought from heaven as a blessing. She told Heaven’s mom that she was to take and raise the child as any other. The child had a calling to be an angel but would not know of it until she came of age. Angels do not grow old, so as a result, when Heaven came to the age where she was a fully developed adult, her aging would be halted. Because of this she told Heaven’s mom that she had to tell Heaven the truth as soon as she became an adult. She said that after she told Heaven that she was an angel, the rest of it would all fall into place; she would eventually believe it and have some reconciliation of her time before coming to Earth. When Heaven’s mother told her all of these things that the angel in white had told her twenty years previous, Heaven was shocked. Later that day, the same angel that her mother spoke of, came to visit Heaven. She told her that everything her mother said was the truth. She also told her not to grieve over the lost of Jason any longer, because he never really existed. She explained that Jason was a lost soul from another realm, and was given the choice to come to Earth for a chance to grow up, and serve the purpose of teaching Heaven Rayne the power and beauty of true love. When it was almost time for Heaven to discover the truth about herself, Jason’s spirit would descend, and go to paradise, instead of the lost realm he came from. Another purpose Jason served while on Earth was keeping Heaven occupied so she wouldn’t fall in love with a mortal. The rule is…if she ever falls in love with a mortal she will lose all powers and will no longer have a calling. She also will be sentenced to eternity as a lost soul. As of now, it’s been a few weeks since Heaven found out that she is an angel. She is just now starting to believe it, and she is still sort of testing the waters so to speak. She is not real sure what powers she beholds, and she’s not sure of her specific calling, other than she is to help others. For now…it’s all left unanswered. Quotes Taking you past Cloud Nine...
