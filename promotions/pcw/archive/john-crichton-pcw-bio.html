John Crichton Biography Height 6'2" Weight 232lbs lbs Biography Crichton joined the army straight from school at the age of 17, coming up to his 18th Birthday. He learned alot in his experience there and 8 years into his army career he was chosen for the special unit. There he was taught various hand to hand combat techniques and was an exeplorary soldier. Then in the Tenth year of his career, the army chose him for a scientic experiment, which Crichton knew would end in the Inevitable death of Millions. His conscience got the better part of him and escaped the army. The experiment had the best chance of success used specifically on Crichton's DNA, so they need him to complete the experiment. A ruthless mercenary going by the name of Mr Fleming was hired to track down Crichton and bring him back ALIVE. Due to Fleming's apparent 'inefficiency' at capturing Crichton, the govt. hired the rogue band of 'superheroes' known as The Thunderbolts to take over the case. Within 24 hours Crichton was once again in Military custody. They took him to a remote military base and subjegated him to the super soldier tests. Aeryn had managed to acquire the Avengers help and together they staged a daring rescue attempt to take Crichton back. Whilst the Avengers distracted the soldiers, Aeryn snuck off to find Crichton. In the end she did find him, but not before the tests had been carried out. Apparently, the tests went well the scientist believe 'It was the best we could have hoped for' . They didn't know how right they were. Just before Aeryn sprung Crichton free, she was shot, by none other than Mr. Fleming. Enraged, Crichton broke out of his shackles, rendered Fleming unconscious, then destroyed the army base with him in it. However, the The Thunderbolts managed to save Fleming's life. Aeryn Sun died in Crichton's arms that day...the death tipped him over the edge and he vowed from that day forth that he would get revenge upon all of the people who had done him and Aeryn wrong over the past years. This was tipified when he found out that Fleming was still alive... Crichton no longer cared about other people's perceptions of him...he just did what had to be done...reap revenge...some people thought this to be cruel...some thought this callous...for him, he was just paying old debts. The public condemn him...Superheroes are wary of him...Villains are scared of him...he is the 'Chief Anarchist' John Crichton Quotes 'The Chief Anarchist' John Crichton will kick your ass at the drop of a hat; Now ain't that a bitch!!!
