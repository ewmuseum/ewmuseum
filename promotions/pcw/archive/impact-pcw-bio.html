Impact Biography Real Name Drew Driven Biography Drew Driven was born on a sunny day in California, on the fourth of May. Growing up as a little boy he saw his first wrestling match while on a trip with his dad to New York City. What was the event? None other then the first WrestleMania. Drew was so fascinated with this sport called wrestling that he made a pact to himself that one day too, he will be a wrestler, and the greatest at that. Drew then went back home and told all his friends about this event. His friends were also fascinated, but they fascination could not match up to Drew's After several more years in elementary and junior high school, he attending high school and joined the wrestling team. His first two matches were total blow outs, as he lost quickly and gained the name Impy (for being a Wimpy) after falling to a arm drag take over. But Drew did not prevail as he continued his damned hardest to be better. Before anyone knew it, he gained pounds of pure muscle and easily over came his fellow competitors. Drew quickly became the captain of his wrestling team, and then went to the State wrestling tournament, only to lose in the semi finals to a spactacular wrestler. After his loss, Drew became even more determined to win, but still had that thought in the back of his mind. Becoming the best professional wrestler ever to enter the squared circle. Drew graduated High School and got a full scholorship to Dartmouth. He was overjoyed and soon joined the wrestling team here. While in Dartmouth, he studied the human anatomy and after graduating five years later he thought that it was time to achieve his dream. Drew then went down to Louisville and learned the basics at OVW, where he was promoted to the second level, only to be promoted to the third level the next day. (The third level is where he actually gets to compete with other OVW wrestlers and gets taped for TV.) There, he took up his name, Impact and started his career to stardom... While in a match, Impact received a stiff punch or two to both of his eyes causing him to reduce his vision. He went on and tried to use a plancha spot where his opponent would utilize a cross body from the ring to the outside, but just then, he lost his vision for a split second, and this resulted in a blotched spot that broke his opponent's legs. Impact was quickly realeased, and his heart for the sport was too. After several years of torment, he decided to come back to the sport, this time to a organization called the UEF. In the UEF he met the greatest mentors and tutors any man can make. He gained the tag titles with Trash and then the International Title a few months after. He then saw an ad for PCW Diamond in the Rough tourney and was excited to have a chance at becoming a superstar in the biggest organization out there... PCW. Impact was accepted gladly and was given the number 2 seed in the third bracket. He managed to beat his first opponent and from there it was all history. But in the semi finals, once more, he lost again but came out with high hopes at the chance to still sign with PCW. PCW surely did sign Impact a few days later, nearly accomplishing his goal of becoming the greatest wrestler in the world... Impact is now known for his nearly impossible yet very innovative moves. He finishes his opponent's with his ImpACTION maneuver, a move that ends up with the head on the mat for the opponent. He takes fans breath's away with his high flying Shooting Star variants and for his heart stopping ImpACTOR kick.
