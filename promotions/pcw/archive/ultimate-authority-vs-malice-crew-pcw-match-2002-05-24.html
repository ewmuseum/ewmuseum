PCW match: Tim Walden, Mark Makonie, Tyler Nelson, and Xavier Kannon v Duke Williams, Rush Mason, Jordan X Shabazz and Jon Kano

24 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Tim Walden, Mark Makonie, Tyler Nelson, and Xavier Kannon V Duke Williams, Rush Mason, Jordan X Shabazz and Jon Kano Written: 05/24/02 Dave: Well here we are folks, it’s Main Event time! Jeff: Meh... How dare the Ultimate Authority have to share the spotlight with the Jobber Crew!!! Duke: Well... Time for me to get into practice... *SMACK* Duke: I still got it!!! “The Olympic Theme” hits as Big Alan Steele, complete with the PCW World Title belt comes out to the ramp... dressed in his regular non-referee attire. Jeff: He can’t even be bothered to dress in the proper gear. How disrespectful. Dave: When was the last time you were respectful to anything? Jeff: I’m respectful to Mr Nelson. Duke: That’s sucking up, hoss. Not being respectful. Clay: The following match is a stable tag team match... heading to the ring at this time, the special guest referee for this match and he is the Primetime Championship Wrestling World Heavyweight Champion... BIG ALAN STEELE!!! “Honky Tonk Heroes” by Walyon Jennings blasts out around the arena as Duke gets from his chair and Jordan X Shabazz, Jon Kano and the Rush make their way down the aisle. Clay: On their way to the ring, weighing something I can’t be bothered to add up... THE MALICE CREW!!! All 4 men meet in the ring and ready themselves. As Big Alan Steele doesn’t actually remove the belt from his waist and stands in the middle of the ring, ready to mediate this match. Jeff: Damn bloody Jobber Crew idiots. Dave: You can be Shabazz and Duke have some payback planned from Supremacy. Jeff: Meh. “Money, Power, Respect” by the Lox then hits as Tyler Nelson leads out Xavier Kannon, Tim Walden and Mark Makonie. Jeff: DA MEN!!!! Dave: I should have known these parasites would stick together. Clay: Their opponents, the ULTIMATE AUTHORITY!!! The U.A go to slide into the ring but see the M.C lunge for them and as one they back off and Big Alan Steele pushes them all back. Jeff: Good tactics. Dave: More like cowardice. They hop up onto the apron and Walden steps into the ring, on the opposite side Kano is left in the ring and charges at Walden right away and takes him down WITH A KANO-SPEAR!!! *DING* *DING* *DING* Jeff: Hey, the match hadn’t started yet!!! Dave: Now it has!!! Kano pulls Walden up to a huge cheer and claps Tim’s throat in his giant hand before lifting him with ONE ARM AND CHOKESLAMMING HIM!!! Dave: AMAZING POWER!!! Jeff: DAMN IT WALDEN!!! Crowd: KANO ROCKS~! KANO ROCKS~! KANO ROCKS~! Kano reaches down and picks the groggy Walden up before lifting him then PLANTING HIM INTO THE MAT WITH A POWERBOMB!!! Jeff: STOP THE MATCH!!! Dave: Kano is carrying on from Supremacy!!! Kano raises a giant arm to a huge pop and drags Tim up by the hair before SENDING HIM FLYING WITH A KANO-PLEX!!! Jeff: This is horrible, Kano must be on drugs or something! Dave: You can’t say that!!! Jeff: Just did. Kano drops down and covers Tim as Big Alan Steele starts to count. 1.............2......MAKONIE BREAKS IT UP!!! Kano gets right back up and stares at the Nazi who broke up the pin. The man who Rocketh the Mosteth pulls Walden up and tosses him into the U.A corner and urges Makonie to tag in. Jeff: HAIL HITLER!!! Dave: YOU CANNOT SAY THAT ON NATIONAL TV!!! Jeff: ZEIG HEIL! ZEIG HEIL!!! Makonie tags in and smirks at Kano who then turns around and tags in Jordan Shabazz and Makonie’s smirk fades. Dave: HERE WE GO!!! Shabazz and Makonie charge at each other and trades right hands before Mark is knocked right down with a huge haymaker. Jordan waits for Makonie to get up before knocking him back down with a clothesline and calling for more. Jeff: DON’T LET ADOLF’S DEATH BE IN VAIN MARK!!! Dave: You’re pathetic!!! Mark charges Jordan who drops his head but Makonie rolls over him and delivers a low blow allowing him to lift Shabazz up and over with a belly to back suplex. Big Alan Steele is distracted by Xavier Kannon at that point and doesn’t notice it. Jeff: GOEBELLS WOULD BE PROUD OF YOU MARK!!! Dave: Would you SHUT UP!!! Jeff: Hey, PCW needs a 3rd Reich! Mark keeps the waist lock on and pulls Jordan back up to hit a Side Russian Legsweep before tagging in Kannon. Jeff: DA COMMISH!!! Dave: Er... Jeff.. He isn’t Commissioner anymore. Jeff: I’m in Denial... Sue me. Dave: Denial.. Hmm.. Want me to crack a bad Pharaoh joke? Jeff: The Pharaoh is a bad joke. Kannon storms in and starts to put boots into the back of Jordan’s head before dragging him up and hitting an Inverted Stunner before covering. 1..................2.....KICK OUT!!! Xavier hurriedly gets Shabazz back up and lifts him in a vertical suplex before dropping him down onto the ropes and back over with tremendous impact, Kannon floats over and hooks the leg. 1.................2....SHOULDER UP!!! A frustrated Kannon pulls Shabazz up and whips him into the corner before charging in with an avalanche splash but Jordan moves out of the way and Xavier eats buckle. Jeff: NO! TELL HIM TO GET BACK IN THE CORNER AND TAKE THE SPLASH!!! Dave: Jeff... This is live. Jeff: Then tell the crowd to like it or else than! Jordan waits for Kannon to stagger out before hitting an inverted atomic drop, causing the ex-Commish to grab down low and wince in pain. Shabazz then bounces off the ropes and sends him flying back with a knee lift to the face. Jeff: End the show.... This doesn’t need to be seen!! Dave: Oh.... It does!!! Shabazz walks to his corner and tags in Duke to a massive cheer and the President waits for Kannon to rise. Jeff: Kannon’s plying possum!!! Dave: We’ll see... Xavier turns only to nearly get beheaded with a running clothesline. Dave: Possum you say? Jeff: BAH~! Duke pulls Xavier back up and whips him into the corner, following him in with another huge clothesline. H waits for Kannon to stumble out before taking him over with a powerslam and cover. 1...................2......KICK OUT!!! Duke drags him up and kicks him in the ribs before planting him with a DDT and covering. 1................2......BROKEN UP!!! Nelson runs in and drops a knee to the back of Duke’s head but Williams is up and makes a charge at Tyler only to be met with a right hand from Makonie as the U.A all set upon Duke. Dave: HEY! This is meant to be a tag match!!! Jeff: Rules are there to be broken!!! From the other side of the ring the Malice Crew charge to the rescue, Shabazz knocking Makonie down with a spear and Kano planting his huge foot into Walden’s face. Jeff: DAMMIT! THE JOBBER CREW RUIN EVERYTHING!!! Dave: Kannon and the Rush, Nelson and Duke, Jordan and Makonie, Kano and Walden! All 8 are in the ring!!! Suddenly the camera cuts to the enterence ramp where Ryan “Streak” Stevens is standing. Jeff: What does that ripoff want? He starts to stroll down slowly towards the ring when suddenly another man appears behind him walking, and another and another and another... Jeff: Dave... ok... who the hell are they? And don’t shout it out like you normally do. Dave: IT’S BLOOD ANGEL, THE SUPREME MACHINE, RICKY THUNDER AND HIRO SAOTOME!!! And you know who he is! The person that Dave is referring to is last... Jeff: THE PHANTOM BOOKER? WHY THE HELL IS HE HERE? GO BACK TO THE DEPARTMENT STORE YOU GOT YOUR WOMAN’S STOCKINGS FROM! The seven men stand at the edge of the ring while the action in the ring draws to a close with all eyes on what these four want. Nelson walks around the side of the ring and starts having a go at Booker. Booker nods towards the Supreme Machine, and the only CCW Hall of Famer socks Tyler straight in the jaw! The crowd goes crazy! Jeff: NO! YOU CAN’T DO THAT TO THE BOSS! NO!!!! Dave: You can, and now he’s laying the boots into Nelson! Jeff: NO! Saotome and Thunder run around one side of the ring and Blood Angel and Ryan Stevens run around the other, trapping Ultimate Authority in the middle, beating them down with the advantage of the surprise. Jeff: NO! All the while, not a single blow is being layed on any of the members of the Malice Crew, who are standing around rather dazed at the extra support and the UA taking the beatings of their lives. Dave: I don’t know what the hell is going on here, but I’m sure we’ll find out on Turmoil. The PCW2002 logo appears in the bottom left of the screen as The Phantom Booker gets a microphone.. Booker: No one is above MY word! Dave: The Booker is Back TWO HUNRED PERCENT!! View Jordan X Shabazz 's Biography
