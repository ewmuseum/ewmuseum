El Diablo Biography Real Name Raul Diaz Nicknames Diablo, The Mexican Devil, The Lord of Mexico, The Pinnacle Of Perfection, Apex Of Excellence, Summit Of Success Hometown Puebla, Mexico Birthday 05/28/74 Height 5'9 Weight 223 lbs Biography Poser Details --------------- .Skin colour and overall look to be Mexican! .Shaved short dyed red hair. .Dyed blue chin goatee. .Muscular body- in proportion to his weight. .Black elbow pad on right arm. .Red and blue ringpants .Further add ons to be detailed later (Sky agrees with this) El Diablo was born Raul Diaz in Puebla, Mexico on the 25th June 1974. It wasn't a rich family he was born into but it was generally a better standard of living than that of most Mexican's. From the age of 6 El Diablo began his love for wrestling. He'd watch all of the Mexican wrestling on Television and dream that one day it would be him. El Diablo wanted to live out his dream, so he began to train by himself inside his bedroom, and soon his brother Pedro would help him and his father Raul Sr., his mother Salma was never too happy with the idea of her son wrestling, but his Dad worked in a jail, so the family was used to controversy. By age 8 he had become rather good at technical wrestling and brawling, and now he wanted to learn to fly like his heroes on Television. There wasn't much help he could be given here from his family, so he would practice many highflying moves by himself, by jumping off garages onto mats etc. This didn't go uninjured, as he broke his arm on at least 3 occasions, dislocated finger joints, a broken leg and strained neck. But nonetheless it was worth it, as El Diablo gained the highflying skills he had only dreamed of. By the time he was 13, he was quite the wrestle but still had a lot to learn in wrestling and in life. When he turned 15, he got involved with the wrong people and ended up with a crack addiction that would dominate his life for over year, and limit the skills he had just learned in wrestling. It was only when a friend of his, Jose, died of a heart attack at 18 that he realised he had to quit his addiction and leave the gang for good. When he reached 16 he had successfully quit his drug habit and was once again wrestling. When he turned 17, he was welcomed to train at a small Mexican promotion's academy, to tweak up his skills and get him as refined as many other cruiserweights and luchalibres out there. After spending a year in the academy he was pulled out and booked in his very first match, under his real name of Raul Diaz. His debut went well and after that he was offered a full time contract for this Mexican company in which he promptly signed, and wrestled in over 2 years until tragedy struck. When driving home with his brother Pedro, when he was just 19 years old, a car driving the wrong way made Pedro have to swerve out of the way and crashed into a tree hard and the car flipped. The man that caused the crash did nothing to help except phone the ambulance and leave. Raul and Pedro were both taken to the nearest hospital. Raul had minimal injuries but Pedro was critical. Despite all the prayers, Pedro died three weeks later and his parents blamed Raul for it. Raul was distraught as him and Pedro were always so close, he promptly resigned from his wrestling company and for the next 2 years resided in the bedroom that him and his brother had once shared. When he was nearing 22, he finally left the room, but with a new attitude to life. Instead of wrestling as Raul Diaz, he would now wrestle as El Diablo or 'The Devil'. He donned a mask and lived a strange lifestyle after this, which cost him most of his friends and definitely his family as he turned his back on religion after the death of Pedro. He trained hard for 4 years and wrestled in many independent federations in Mexico until finally getting his big shot in America, the land of opportunity as the PWA (Primetime Wrestling Academy) signed him. El Diablo was only there a short time before being moved across to EW (Eternal Wrestling) and forming a 'Super Group' called 'Freaks By Choice' with Agent Orange, Skitzo and Syko. His tenure in EW however didn't last long as the company collapsed with managerial troubles. 'Freaks by Choice' then went across to NTICW (New True Internet Championship Wrestling) to dominate, but this again did not last long as it folded again due to management troubles. This again left El Diablo on the scrap heap, until he was appointed as the Head of the PWA. Shortly after this El Diablo entered PCW (Primetime Championship Wrestling) the premier federation in the whole of the United States. With his career in PCW finally taking off, he managed to team in Illegal Aliens with Frisco for a short while which pushed his career slightly and he met his now wife, Angela when in Chicago, but despite all his success came more troubles. He began questioning who he was and became stranger than ever before. But despite all these troubles became stronger than ever mentally and physically. He became PCW Rising Star Champion on December 7th with victory over Gaijin which kick-started his career in PCW which had previously been plummeting. Soon, El Diablo became a real patriot to Mexico and was one of the founders of the new power stable in PCW, 'International Incident' with Flaymin Aymin, Akiyama, The Pharaoh, Juan Borro and Kevin Klaymore, a team of International stars looking for respect and domination of PCW. On Sabotage (26th Feb 2002) El Diablo decided he had, had enough of International Incident and would make his own way in PCW, how this would go was unknown, but The Mexican Devil is always known to take risks. He soon found himself in a bitter rivalry with Flaymin Aymin over several weeks, and at Spring Fling 2002 he lost a match meaning he had to lose his mask forever. Soon enough though the El Diablo and Flaymin Aymin feud heated up, as it was revealed that Aymin was the man driving the opposite car that resulted in the death of Diablo's brother Pedro and in all his anger this resulted with the complete destruction of Sunday Sabotage, maybe forever gone from Television. Then at the following PPV El Diablo ended what Aymin started, by defeating him in a brutal unsanctioned streetfight, that saw his wife leave him. Then there was a change in El Diablo! He somehow wanted to become a film director, and his film would be called 'The End' starring The End. But it all went sour when The End assaulted him. This started another heated rivalry for El Diablo, which ended with victory in another streetfight. Now 28, Diablo was sick and tired of Akiyama and began to challenge him constantly, which resulted in another sick rivalry. Diablo would fight his heart out but it was all in vain as despite his best efforts he could not take down the Neo Void God. The Lord of Mexico took a short break and in this time rekindled his love with Angela and his love for wrestling. Soon following this he found himself and former PCW Champion Jason Snow at each other's throats. This feud was manic and in a severe twist of fate saw El Diablo turn his back on the fans that had made him when he 'screwed' Snow out of victory at Red October and a spot in the World Title Main Event. Following Red October, it was clear how much El Diablo had changed, in attitude and in morals, highlighted by his paranoia and hatred for everyone except his beautiful wife Angela. During his change of attitude, El Diablo exiled the 'corrupt' Pharaoh from PCW. Then came along PCW World Champion Clyde, where El Diablo developed a sick and twisted love for the World Title. Mind games ensued quickly between the two, and despite El Diablo's best efforts he could not capture the Title at Alliances. Another defeat at Decimation lead the Mexican into despair where he went on strike for over a month before finally bowing out, due to the state of corruption in PCW. Other Feds PWA, EW, NTICW.. Quotes ONE! MORE! TIME! I am The Pinnacle of Perfection, I am The Apex of Excellence and I am The Summit of Success. Wait For It!!!!! They're all watching us! This is my film, now get out of it! Shadows of abyss, heathens of my soul. Through time it will be realised that their is only one true darkness, El Diablo. Some call it fate, I call it being the best. Who?
