DarkStar Biography Real Name Gavyn Psyman Drazail Nicknames The Dream Lord, The Mourning Star Hometown Philadelphia, PA Birthday 4-11-81 Height 6 Foot 3 Inches Weight 239 lbs Biography From Talena's mind Talena loved her husband. She loved every single one of his personalities. Today she sat at the kitchen table at the Drazail estate in Philadelphia, nursing a bowl of Captain Crunch, she was with her lover, DarkStar. He was dressed in his normal morning attire, black pajamas with a black fur robe, almost identical to Talena's. The difference was the sling his arm was in to take the stress off of his dislocated elbow and shoudler. The black sling was a annoyance to him, he had never speant injury time away from wrestling. Sure, he had been fired once or twice, died once, even lost his smile before (a term he would rip your skull off and feed it to his ferrits if he ever heard in reference towards him) but an injury that kept him away from his past time wasn't easy to comprehend. Talena herself didnt understand how he felt, but she was always good at empathy, its what she does. The dealing with people and knowing how they think, thats why Talena loved her second sight. The ability to feel emotion and see the future (however shortly into the future it really was) was something she loved. Thats how she knew she would meet DarkStar, through second sight. The day of the concert where she saw him she had known she would meet him, vaguely, its how she was presented things. It was why she was generally certifiable. She, however, was proof that just because you were insane didn't mean you couldn't be a genius. They had been through alot together, Talena and DarkStar, It all started that day. DarkStar had a rough childhood, he was born... and that was easy enough,. His family consisted of his mother, Joyce, a woman who cared more about herself then anything else, and his father, Stephen, who cared more about a beer bottle then anything else. So the young Gavyn Drazail took a back seat to the interests of his family. He went to school, and did fine for a while, until the nightmares began. See, Drazail was abused alot at home, not just physically, his father abused him and his mother physically, but mentally also. He was constantly reminded that he wasn't going to amount to anything and that he was an accident, he was made sure that it was realized noone cared if he lived or died. This all got to him, he began to have nightmares, and these nightmares began to manifest themselves in his head, he didn't quite understand it. His fragile psyche (he was about 13 at the time) began to shatter. The more he was picked on at home and in school, the more violent he got. In 8th Grade his Mother and Father decided to take him to a child psychologist. He made the simple statement of Gavyn Drazails split personality, he had issues at home and with himself, therefor he seperated them into two seperate entities, one was Gavyn Drazail, kind and caring, the other was a 'dark star' in comparison to his light, it was violent and dark. Drazail loved the word. DarkStar. It rang off of his tongue. From there it got worse. DarkStarDrazail graduated 8th grade and became a High School student. High School breeds contempt. He was even more of a outcast, he had no friends, even the outcasts wouldnt hang out with him because of the way he dressed and acted. Gavyn Drazail became a smaller part of him until one day, it was gone. DarkStar became the owner of the man that was Gavyn Drazail. It was his Junior year in High School when he finally lost it. He was jumped in the hallway at school, thrown into lockers and slammed onto the floor roughly. He casually stood up, his mouth bleeding, and ripped his assailents tongue out. Only in self-defense, of course. That was the final straw. His mom and his dad snapped, pulling him out of public school and sending him to a private one, hoping to know his sanity would return. It didn't, and it never had a chance to. His father, after years of alcohol abuse, died of liver failure when Drazail was 17. His mother, even after all the abuse, was distraught. The funeral was a scene where well-wishers refused to mention his bad tendancies. This sickened DarkStar. He hated fakeness. He hated everything to relate to the human race. He was as misanthropic as anyone could possibly be. He stood up, walked to the microphone, and told every sickening detail of how his father REALLY was. People cried, people argued, people got mad. Most of all, his selfish mother. She cried not for her dead husband, but for her son and how he embarressed her. She screamed and yelled with fury. DarkStar never cared, he let it roll off his back like rain water. He laughed at her, spat towards the casket, and walked out of the church. Noone came after him. Now, you may be wondering what Talena was doing in this time, since she is the focus of this.. Her life wasn't as nearly as eventful. She was 10 when she realized her powers, and she coped with them easily. She accepted it all, its hard to understand why. Maybe because she was Canadian. She took every problem in stride and never cared, eventually, she too ran away from home after her parents threatened to send her away to a all girls school. She never saw her parents again, knew she would never see her parents again, and new she would never care. This is when fates began to cross. This is when Destiny stuck his hand in and turned the page of the book. DarkStar had nowhere to go, he lived on the streets of Philadelphia for a time until he got a minimum wage job at Zipperheads, a gothic industrial store on South Street. He lived in the apartment above, lieing about his age the entire time. During his time there, he met many beautiful women. His gothic attraction (he had long since dressed the way he thought, dark) brought on as many phone numbers as you can count. He was a very romantic now 18 year old, and started to date a few. All ended horribly. His first real love was Sandy, she stayed by his side for almost a year, til someone better came along of course. DarkStar didn't take that lightly. He cursed under his breath and wished them both dead. A week later they died after falling into the Delaware. Remorse still, however, was not in DarkStar vocabulary. After the terrible break up (and news of their deaths) a band by the name of the Deftones came through town. Across from Zipperheads, DarkStar showed up to support them (they weren't quite a big name yet... key being yet). The Deftones were Talena's favorite band. They met in line on a snowy December, much like this day, and it all began. Talena always had thoughts of this boy, although she didn't know his name. They noticed eachother, his blue hair stuck out, they talked, and they kissed. It was magic. Too bad there was no fairytale. They parted ways, thinking they would never see eachother again. Days, weeks, months went by. They still didn't. DarkStar met a new girl, a girl that would change his life forever. Her name was Courtney. She had no heart. She hurt him more then it should be possible to hurt another human. She cheated on him, belittled him, then when he tried to crawl back to her, it ended. He was hurt, terribly. She could care less. However, Talena cared. When the preveiously stated female was in her house sleeping on a warm spring night, Talena arrived in Philadelphia. Quietly she snuck into Courtneys house and drew a long, silver blade, she gently... almost reverently, plunged it deep into the sleeping girl numerous times. Fate made itself known even more now. DarkStar was at the door. He had decided he would give it one last chance with her, he would throw caution to the wind and beg for her to take him back. He realized the door was open, he pushed it lightly, the creak would resonate in his heads for the rest of his life. He saw the glint of the knife, he saw the crimson drip to the floor, he saw his ex lover laying on her stomach, the life sputtering from her body as her blood coated the bed in a macabre finger painting. A normal man would have screamed. He would have cried. He would have grabbed the assailent and tried to get revenge. DarkStar was no normal man. He saw Talena and he saw the crimson and he saw the macabre art, and his teeth were revealed, smiling, almost laughing. Talena dropped the knife and ran to him, they laughed and smiled and hugged together in the house, his ex still bled and couldn't speak, nor could she move, nor could she react. If she had the strength to turn around she would have seen a sight that could disturb even the mind of Rodan. DarkStar took Talena right there, their relationship began on the floor of a dieing girl. It was beautiful in its sickness. Anyone elses story would have ended here, with enough death and heartbreak to choke a stable of purebred horses. Not this one. For even after all this, DarkStar was barely an adult. Punk wasn't even trendy by the time he met his true love, and the next four years spoke multitudes in shaping the lives of DarkStar and Talena. The couple had love, but that was it. They had no money. Talena was mildly successful in a past music offering, and DarkStar had his writing. Neither of that was enough to support them for too long. By chance however, DarkStar met up with a wrestling promoter through one of his oddjobs at the Troc. He saw him bounce a few drunkards and loved his style, not necessarily his bulk or his talent, but his style, the way he handled himself. He gave his card, he was a trainer at New Image Wrestling in North Carolina, a former independant wrestler under the moniker 'The Insane Kidd'. It was the only thing DarkStar was ever skeptical about, but he did it. He and his love travelled to NC to find this place. Sure, this isn't the typical way people find wrestling, no, most stories see people become wrestlers beccause they are naturally violent or insane or whatever unoriginal thought these peoples characters breathed. DarkStar was just a 19 year old kid with split personalities and some sort of psychic ability who needed some quick cash. How often do you see that? Anyway, he made a name for himself on the indie scene mostly do to his spotwork, nothing fundamental, just the ability to ignore pain and do stuff noone had seen before. His wrestling career on the independant scene is not important. Whats important is what was going on in his personal life. In late 1999, DarkStar finally returned home. He didn't know why. He wouldn't learn why for a few years to come. He did however, and he returned home to find a new family. His mother had reformed and remarried, to a Truck Driver from New Jersey, they had had a son named Gryphon, DarkStars half brother. Everything was alright, DarkStar started to fade from memory, Talena and Drazail's love became more of a natural thing instead of supernatural, more earthly and believable. Gavyn stopped wrestling and began to work at Hot Topic, training to be a manager, it wasn't as lucrative as wrestling. It was, however, much more normal. It crashed down quickly. It was March of 2000. Gavyn Drazail and Talena had just taken a walk on the beach in celebration of yet another month together. At about 2am, the pair returned to Drazails house. ...Or what was left of it. For tragedy and heartbreak can never leave someone cursed by it, So on that night, Gavyn watched his house burn. He was stopped by firemen galore and told the terrible news, noone survived. It was arson in the first degree, a random act of violence, they had the perp caught and he was in police custody. Why had he been brought so close to his family again just to have it ripped out of his chest, along with his own heart? Why? This was the question he asked himself as he grabbed the firemans axe. Why? This was the question he asked himself as he drove it into the arsonists chest. Why? This was the question he asked himself as he was shoved in a mental institution on a court order, to stay out of jail. Why? This was the question he always asked himself, as DarkStar took over again. Talena never caught the sanity bug, she had dual sides to her that ran deeper then DarkStar even could, partly though. She was a genius. Using her legal knowledge a loophole was found that released DarkStar into her custody. She took full responsibility for his actions. These actions led him to one place though. The doors of Primetime Incorporated in beautiful Atlantic City, New Jersey. In the office of The Phantom Booker, signing his career to the greatest league in Sports, PCW. What a strange trip it had been. One that won't end for a long, long time. Ever since then, DarkStar hasn't been able to balance the happiness with the responsibility. He is still insane, although more dynamic. Shortly after he signed with PCW, it was all made sense to him through the Gods. Gods he never believed in and still doesn't like. It all happened because DarkStar, in truth, was the link between reality and fantasy, he existed because humanity existed, he was the seventh son of the seventh son. His father? Destiny. His mother? Despair. The burnt house, the loss of family, it was all because he needed to be insane to be effective. He was the controller of Dreams, the Sandman that you were all told about as children. He was a blatant rip off of a Neil Gaiman comic book seri-... Er. Heh. Sorry. However, he existed. This was his job. To be a 'filter' that kept the rest of the wastes of skin and matter from losing it, he kept the nightmares out of this realm. He did it thanklessly, he wrestled to support himself, and he enjoyed every last second of his insanity and reponsibilty. For the first time in his life, his life had a meaning. Even Harle Quinn wouldn't be able to bring him down from this maniacle euphoria, he had his wife, he had his job, he had his powers. All Talena needed, she thought to herself while watching her lover try to eat a grapefruit one handed, was him. Even as important and successful as he was, she would still be by his side if he were flipping burgers. Love, true love, knows no bounds and cannot be stopped. Take that as advice from slaves to its cause, and take it to the heart. Because you never know when you will be going in a blaze of glory... ... Or when everything will be gone in a blaze.
