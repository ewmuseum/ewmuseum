Ariz Ona Biography Real Name Ariz Ona Nicknames Zona, The Riz, The Rizmaster Hometown Skull Valley, Arizona Birthday 12/28/79 Height 6'4' Weight 187 lbs Biography Bio Ariz Ona was born on December 28, 1979. He was raised in a single parent home by his mother, Madison (Maddie) Ona. Ona's father left Maddie after he found out that she was pregnant with Ariz. When The Rizmaster was younger, he was told that his father was a pro wrestler, but his mom never told him the father's name so Ona does not really know who his dad is. Ariz was an isolationist during his childhood and adolescence. He would always stay away from the other kids. After high school, Zona tried to make it on his own as a drummer. It took him a long while to get a gig, but he was finally able to with a band called Los Diablos, which he is still with every so often. Ona and the other four members of the band, Mex Iko, Cal E. Fornya, and Sid Nghe, have all became great friends. Not too long after joining the band, Ariz happened to bump into my girlfriend Carmen Cordova, known to all of everyone in the wrestling world as Misery. After Maddie's death, Ona took a trip to Japan, where his life was forever changed. Ona saw a wrestling match for the first time while in Japan, and decided to become a professional wrestler. After a few short weeks in PWA and then WWCF/RSW, Ona made it to the big time, NTICW. However, soon after coming to NTICW, Ona's relationship with Misery soured. Ariz decided to dump her because he had believed that she had cheated on him with Eric Tomas, the brother of his archrival, Teresa Tomas. Soon after, Ona won the NTICW Cruiserweight Title, only to disappear soon afterward. Now after a short stint PWA, where he started his career, The Rizmaster has been signed by RUSH. What will happen now that he is in RUSH? Only time will tell... Poser Description Ariz is somewhat tanned (thanks to the Arizona sun). He is also kind of muscular. He has short brown hair and brown eyes. He wears black sunglasses, a white t-shirt (preferably with either a picture of the Arizona flag or a cow skull on the front), jeans, and white tennis shoes.
