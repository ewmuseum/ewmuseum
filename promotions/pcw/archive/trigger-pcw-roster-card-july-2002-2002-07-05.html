AIM Name: [detail withheld] MSN Name: [detail withheld] Bio As a young adult, Trigger became mixed up in some trouble with the law, mainly due to his outstanding anger problems. Life in JDH was good for Trigger in many ways, blocking out the past and opening his eyes to a brighter future. A new door opened for the Infinite Warrior, when on probation he met Dan Archer, a simple man who would become Trigger's mentor. Dan taught Trigger to express his anger into an interest or hobby, and introduced him to the sport of wrestling. From here, the Infinite Warrior triumphed, finding great success in many federations. No sooner did he bring this glamerous career to Trigger, however, Dan disappeared. Trigger felt alone in the world once again, but over years of hard work and dedication was able to move on, making new allies (and enemies as well!). At 28 years of age, Trigger had accomplished much, and felt that it was time to end his legacy. But then a though occured to him. Wouldn't it be best to carry on his legacy for many years to come? With the help of a former foe, Trigger began to train three young men, determined to mould them into warriors like himself. These 'Warriors In Training' have rekindled Trigger's energy, his life, and most of all his need for wrestling. Sights set on the future, the Infinite Warrior refuses to be stopped, and continues to shine with the brighest of the stars. As he goes on into new realms of his career, Trigger's legacy will live on, and that is plainly because 'he is a warrior.'
