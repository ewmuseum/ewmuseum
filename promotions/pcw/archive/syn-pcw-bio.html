SyN Biography Real Name Stephen Y. Neirlinger Nicknames SyNner, German Mastermind, The REAL Juggalo Hometown Germany Birthday July 20th, 1973 Height 6'1 Weight 322 lbs Biography SyN led a pretty normal life, born and raised in Germany. SyN wasn't part of a loved family, his Mom was a hooker. SyN's mom was hardly ever around, leaving SyN to raise himself when he was old enough to do so. One day, Stephen's mom couldn't take it any more and sent SyN packing on an immigration trip to Boston. Only 11 when he was sent on his own, SyN learnt things the hard way, eventually getting enlisted into a foster home. Stephen stayed in the foster home until he was sixteen, when he got a real job. SyN found his passion in wrestling, where he earned a living. SyN’s happy-go-lucky attitude led him to the top of NTICW’s ranks, earning a title shot at Scott Taylor. While all this was happening, SyN was sent on a special detective job, look for a guy by the name of Nick Ramsey. When SyN found Nick, a final battle ensued. To make things even more juicier, SyN found out that Nick Ramsey, was his brother, SVEN! The battle ended with SyN being knocked unconscious from a gun shot and Sven getting away. Waking up from being hurt in the hospital, SyN became different. It was like he wasn’t there. SyN remembered his passion for wrestling but nothing else. SyN went out again, looking for another job in a fed. Instead of a job though, SyN found a cult. The Hare Krishna cult didn't care about there members. All they cared about was throwing there members out to waste. It was too late, SyN was hooked on the cult. When he wasn't with the cult, SyN wrestled in indy feds. Almost every day, SyN goes into the Hare Krishna praying centre to booth #17, where he speaks with the mysterious brain-washer behind the booth. SyN hears what he wants to hear, which gives him confidence. But, inside, SyN is nothing but a demented soul who has a short temper. Then one day, SyN broke away from the cult. While in the missionary booth, SyN realized PCW was becoming to much and he wouldn't be able to visit the Krishna. The missionary gave SyN a new life. Be a juggalo.
