Turmoil 27 3 Apr 2001 The Gymnasium, South Carolina University Soy Boy grabs a doughnut The show opens to show PCW Hall of Famer Stewart Hix walking through the backstage area of the gymnasium at South Carolina University, in Columbia, South Carolina. Hix is in street clothes, which for him, means he is wearing a pair of worn jeans and a black T-shirt which reads "I'm a Weed Monkey" on the front and he is carrying a gym bag. Hix continues to walk through backstage area until he comes to an intersection of hallways.  He rubs his chin for a moment, trying to determine which way to go, then apparently has a better idea.  He spies a nearby table and puts his gym bag on top of it, and begins rummaging through its contents.  After a moment of searching, his smile grows larger, as he pulls out what appears to be a marijuana cigarette from the bag. Hix slips the joint into the side of his mouth and pulls out a lighter.  He begins to toke up the joint and inhales deeply.  After..1....2.....3....4....5 seconds, he then exhales and plume of white smoke. Hix: (musing, looking at the joint) Now THIS is definitely a Soy Boy~! Suddenly, a chair enters the scene from off camera and smashes Hix square in the back of his weed-loving melon.  Hix staggers forward as the camera pans out, to reveal the entire area and the fact that there are not one, but two masked men, wearing expensive suits, wielding chairs in the arean. Hix continues to stumble forward, but the second man nails Hix in the with his chair, causing the stoner-turned-wrestler to double over.  The masked assailant then tosses his chair at his feet, and follows by nailing Hix with a face buster, down onto the steel chair.  Hix is out cold. The two masked men walk over to Hix and then high five each other. Masked Man #1:  That was too DAMN easy. Masked Man #2:  DAMN straight! Masked Man #1:  Let's get him up and outta here. Masked Man #2 nods and grabs Hix by the arms, while MM #1 grabs the former Continental champion by his feet, and they begin to carry him down the hall.  After about five seconds, the come to a door on the right hand side of the hall, which is ajar.  Both men continue carrying Hix as MM #1 backs through the door, and MM #2 follows.  The camera follows the two men through the door. Once the camera passes through the threshold, it becomes apparent that the masked men have exited the building into the parking lot of the gymnasium.  Just outside the door, a black Cadillac sits idling, with the trunk popped open.   The two men carry Hix' still motionless body around to the trunk. Masked Man #1:  OkOne.Two.Three. With that, the two men toss Hix into the trunk and slam it closed.  Masked Man #2 thumps on the trunk three times, and the Caddy then squeals off.  Masked Man #2:  Well, that oughta even the odds up a bit! Masked Man #1:  DAMN straight, brotha! The two man chuckle and high five.  The camera pans over to view the tail lights of the Cadillac, as it squeals out of the parking off in the distance, as the scene fades to black.
