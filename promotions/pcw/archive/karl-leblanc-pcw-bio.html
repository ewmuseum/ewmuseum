Karl LeBlanc Biography Biography LeBlanc grew up in the heart of cajun country. He was abused by his father, and thus had to learn to defend himself. He was able to hone his skills by fighting other kids in town, as well as the occasional run-in with gators in the swamps. He is a tough, hard-nosed, reckless, African American with some obvious Cajun bloodlines. He had a long juvenile record which was expunged when he turned 18. He then received a football scholarship at LSU, where he earned Freshman All-American honors. His sophomore year he was convicted of murdering 3 white supremacists as well as his father. He continued to kill even in prison, and was then sent to Amaranth Penitentiary. A new, state-of-the-art maximum security prison complex in Truth or Consequences, New Mexico, where he is currently serving a life sentence. The last-resort prison houses the nation’s most dangerous inmates. LeBlanc earned the nickname 'Bayou Butcher' by the national media, but prefers to be called “Swamp Dawg.” In an effort to raise money for the new multi-million dollar prison system without raising taxes, the state has taken it upon themselves to 'exploit' a number of these inmates by placing them in the large market of the increasingly popular world of professional wrestling. The government is very hush-hush about this project as the risk factor is obviously high. Officials are working with wrestling bookers and their creative writing staff to portray these convicts as mere 'characters' to avoid fear from spectators. Not even the other wrestlers know of the truth behind these 'real life' convicts. They too believe it is all a gimmick. Though he had no choice, LeBlanc was more than happy to participate in this top-secret project. The 'Swamp Dawg' was raised in Louisiana by his grandmother, Madame Bordeau, an alleged practitioner of VooDoo. Karl LeBlanc & Orpheus Grant (0-1-0) <b> Wrestler Description:</b> LeBlanc is a muscular, barrell-chested, African American with very dark skin. He has a flat-top haircut, black goatee, tattoos covering his arms. He wears orange prison pants, black combat boots, black fingerless gloves and usually dark sunglasses. He has high cheekbones and is always scowling. Very mean, tough appearance. LeBlanc is unpredictable and uncontrollable. He is not a trained wrestler... he is a self-taught killer. Mostly a brawler with raw power moves, yet displays a vast array of devastating submission holds; LeBlanc is surprisingly agile for his massive frame. <b> Ring Entrance: </b> Arena goes dark. Search lights pan the crowd, sirens wail throughout the arena, as “Wanted Dead or Alive” by Bon Jovi echoes through the building. Smoke fills the entrance way. 'The Warden' Jack Riley, carrying a billy-club, emerges from behind the curtain. He is followed by a 10-man entourage of armed prison guards. The 6'9' LeBlanc towers above them all. He wears orange prison-issue pants, black combat boots, black fingerless gloves, black tank-top, and dark sunglasses. He shuffles to the ring shackled by his wrists & ankles. At ringside the guards line the perimeter of the ring as Warden Riley unshackles the massive, black, monster. LeBlanc climbs the apron and steps over the top rope into the ring. He climbs the turnbuckles and raises his fists in the air.
