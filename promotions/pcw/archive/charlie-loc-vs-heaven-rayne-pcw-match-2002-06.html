PCW match: Charlie Loc vs. Heaven Rayne

June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Charlie Loc vs Heaven Rayne Written: Back at the announce table, Jeff Marx bangs his head on the table. Jeff: Jobbers*pound*jobbers*pound*jobbers*pound*…. Duke: Hey, that looks like fun hoss! Duke grabs Jeff by the back of his head and starts to ram it even harder into the table. Dave can’t help but smile. Jeff: OUCH!! YOU REDNECK SON OF A BASTARD!! Dave: Heheheh…..we’re going to head to the ring for our next match. Duke stops banging Jeff’s head into the announce desk and Marx rubs his head with a grimace on his face. Clay: The following contest is........ Clayborne is interupted as Heaven Rayne and Charlie Loc tumble out of the entryway, exchanging claws and punches! Dave: They aren't even waiting for the introductions! Loc gains the advantage, rolling on top of Heaven and bouncing her head off the steel ramp. He stands, starts to make his way to the ring but Rayne gets up and spears him in the back, causing them both to tumble to the ring! Dave: Wow Rayne is really holding her stuff against Loc! Jeff: Meh, Loc’s letting her get away with all this! Just wait, he’ll put her away when the time comes! You’ll see! Duke: Well I suppose the Hoss proved a point by pulling the upset on Fusion last week but…. Jeff: Didn’t you hear me 'LOC HAS IT WON'! Heaven Rayne leaps up into the air and nails Loc with a standing Drop Kick really getting the crowd working as she quickly hooks Loc’s leg for the pin attempt. 1…. 2…. Kickout! Loc kicks out and then springs back onto his feet in true Bruce Lee style fashion as a look of concentration crosses over Heaven’s face. The two circle each other before leaping into the air and grappling. Dave: I’m surprised Borro hasn’t made a 'guest' appearance yet! Jeff: Probably passed out somewhere backstage….. Dave: How dare you talk about the Cruiser Weight Champion like that! Suddenly the lights dim and 'GOOOOOOOOOOOOOAAAAAAAAAAAAAALLLLLLLL~!' appears on the JumboTron and Juan 'Sunshine' Borro’s entrance music and video are played. As the lights flicker back on the entire ring is surrounded by the Portugal Soccer Team as Juan is on the entrance ramp holding a mic and a forty in the other hand. Duke: You spoke to soon Hoss! The crowd roars in approval as Juan looks down at his feet and smiles before picking up his Cruiser Weight Title which he had just dropped in the darkness. He raises the mic to his lips as Rayne and Loc stop fighting in the ring and begin to look at the angry Soccer Players surrounding them. 'Sunshine' hiccups as he tries to speak and he nearly drops his Title yet again but shakes his head and speaks into the mic at the two competing in the ring. Sunshine: You know what? Both of you are going to pay for messing with me tomorrow night! That’s right! *HiCcUp* No one messes with the mighty Moonshine…I mean Sunshine! The crowd bursts into laughter as do Duke and Jeff at the announce booth as Borro throws the mic down onto the floor and stumbles around a little before pointing at Loc and Rayne in the ring and beginning to swear in Portuguese. Jeff: Haha, it looks like your Cruiser Weight Champion is making a fool out of himself! Duke: The mighty mighty moonshine hoss! Jeff: You can say that again! Borro smiles at the crowd as Loc and Rayne begin to look around at the fans before they finally stand back to back in the ring as the Portugal Soccer team begin to advance in through the ropes. All 11 men stand in one giant circle around Loc and Rayne as 'Sunshine' tries to run up the ramp but trips over himself….causing yet another outbreak of laughter from the fans. Jeff: This is great! This is what I call Quality Tv! I can’t stop laughing~! Dave: SHUT UP! 'Sunshine' picks himself up and runs into the ring sliding underneath the bottom rope and confronting Rayne and Loc in the middle as the Soccer Team draws a tighter circle around the two. Sunshine: Heh..laugh at me now, but come Gold and Glory when I see your bodies broken over a table you won’t be laughing. I mean look at me and look at yourselves. Jeff: Okay, one drunk and two people who aren’t drunk, is he trying to prove a point. Dave: I don’t know why Juan even comes out here like this, he’s in no shape to be on television or to be in a ring. Duke: Funny thing is Juan has been kicking all their asses either high or drunk, imagine the damage he can do if he’s clean. Dave: never thought of it that way. Sunshine: Well you see I’m a legend and you two are nothing. I’m a two time Cruiserweight Champion and neither have you have ever even laid a hand on this belt. YOU ARE BOTH BENEATH ME. Loc snickers and turns around, but is met by Fernando Couto who looks at Loc and cracks his knuckles. Loc backs off and goes back to the center. Jeff: Are you kidding me, why are Loc and Heaven intimidated by a bunch of foot fairies, PCW must have some real weak people. Dave: I wouldn’t say that seeing as Couto along with Pinto of the Portuguese soccer team, are known for manhandling referees. Jeff: Okay so you just proved, Loc and Heaven are equivalent to a referee when it comes to wrestling. Duke: Why don’t you shut the hell up, even if those 11 guys were midgets with no hands, they could still kick your ass Jeff. Sunshine: You two may think I’m some type of a whattaya call it, JUNKY yea, but I’m not. I lost something I loved and I’ll never get it back. Now I’m going to make sure you two lose something you love, that being your lives. Dave: Juan is getting a little out of hand here. Duke: Juan has gone insane a long time back, and any man in this mental condition can be extremely dangerous. Jeff: You would know, your one of them. Sunshine: Now it’s time for both of you to pay. Loc makes a break and spears through Pauleta and out of the ring, Heaven is left all alone, and each player surrounds her even closer. Jeff: Loc has a brain; he got the hell out of that place. Dave: Now poor Heaven is left there with twelve men ready to take her head off. This is going to be completely uncalled for. Duke: Forget beating her up, twelve Portuguese men and one women, this looks more like a gang rape then a brawl. Dave: Heaven needs helps. The men back her into the turnbuckle and start to laugh. Juan pushes through the crowd and gets face to face with Heaven Rayne. Two players come over and hold Heaven’s arms, while the drunks Juan laughs in her face. Heaven backs away and coughs. Heaven: Nice Breath! Jeff: Ooh Sunshine needs a breath mint. Juan touches the lower lip of Heaven gently and then goes in and spits right in her face, leaving her to scream out loud. Dave: That’s disgusting. Duke: The real Juan Borro would never do this. Juan mocks Heaven, when suddenly running into the ring from the crowd is what seems to be KRIS BURDEN! Dave: Kris Burden IS HERE!!!!!!!!!!! Jeff: Oh great, now this goofy jobber is going to try and be the boyfriend savior. Burden comes out and fights through the group of Portuguese soccer players, Burden finally gets to Juan and clocks him to the floor. Burden grabs Heaven and picks her up getting ready to leave the ring when Loc comes and gets in the path of Burden, and then Burden gets a tap on the shoulder. When he turns around he’s met with a Golden Goal right to the face, dropping Heaven to the ground hard. Dave: Loc just helped Juan in his assault, this is sick. Jeff: That’s what Burden gets for trying to be the big hero. Everyone knows heroes never win in the end it’s the truth. Duke: At least Burden made an attempt to save her, you would have just stood their and ran away like a little woman. Jeff: I sure would have and damn proud of it. Juan laughs as all of the Portuguese soccer team stomp away at Burden and Heaven. After two minutes of the assault Juan signals for the men to exit the ring, and let Heaven and Burden be on their own. Sunshine: Now you two lovebirds could be together forever, have fun. Juan stumbles on his way to the back, but finally gets out of the ring. When Loc sees this, he runs into the ring and jumps on Heaven to make the pin. ……..1 …………..2 ………………….3 Dave: Loc has just stolen this match after leaving Juan and his Portuguese soccer team do all the work. Jeff: And do you actually blame him for that. Duke: A win is a win. And come Gold and Glory any three of these people will give it their all just to win that Cruiserweight title. It should be one of the major highlights this Sunday. Dave: You two have no morals, but I’m still looking forward to the match on Sunday. Let’s cut to a commercial break. (Commercial: PCW is brought to you by Gold and Glory, LIVE and only on pay per view…..This Sunday, June 30th from the Rupp Arena in Lexington Kentucky….call your local cable or satellite operator NOW!)
