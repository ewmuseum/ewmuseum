Height 6'2 Weight 221 lbs Biography There was something never quite right about Rent-A-Hero growing up. Obsessed with comic books and the grand stature of superheroes in particular, our young hero began his quest at the early age of four, his dream to become a superhero for the millions around the globe. His parents, often thrown off and having no understanding of his passion, just did not seem to get why he felt a full body Green Lantern replica spandex costume seemed so right for school to young Hero, but they dared not question or stop their son. The kids at school found his antics incredibly weird and amusing...the norm for him beign rescuing the school nerds so their milk money would be intact. His antics grew more in spectacle as he grew older, graduating from high school and vowing to start a 'Rent-A-Hero' business so that anyone could Rent-A-Hero nearby. His college stay was short yet educational, still believing a 'blow job' required a suit and tie and previous experience because of his childish and virgin nature, he was quite upset 'Superheroing 101' and 'Catchphrases 098' weren't available as courses. He did however, acquire a degree in Chemistry stating that,'I wanna make a GIGANTON GROWTH POTION TO USE SO THAT I MAY CRUSH ALL EVILDOERS UNDER THE BOOT HEEL OF JUSTICE!!' He tried to make his Rent-A-Hero business a reality, but people just weren't willing to pay for villain fighting, cat saving, felonious plan foiling, and grandman walking. Unemployed and without income to pay for his spandex and video game habit...he turned to the only other thing he could which would garner him worldwide support and the chance to fight evil...PROFESSIONAL WRESTLING!! Where else do you get paid to slay the evilest of villains all while recieving cheers from a fanbase, and the merchandside sales didn't sound bad either! So with some spandex costumes, a dream, and thousands of the silliest catchphrases this side of Krypton, our hero set out for the world of wrestling and hoped to reach it's highest valley....becoming the first Kryptonian World Champion to be exact. Plus he believes superheroes are discrimnated against in wrestling and their should be at least one on every roster. Rent-A-Hero, backed by the support of millions of his 'Rent-A-Fans' went on to become one of the most popular stars in his PCW tenure before disappearing off the map into the obscurity of PWA...stories claiming that our hero was looking to hone his skills and become upgraded for the new era of crime fighting he would face. In his PWA tenure was where he met now current OSW superstar, Amy Campbell....consumating the team of Heroes 4 Hire, a popular indy team who disbanded quite abruptly due to Rent-A-Hero getting to the point of breaking his code when amongst Amy...that being not to take his Kryptonite rod and put it in her reactor!(or whatever that means.) Disappearing faster than the Invisible Woman, Rent-A-Hero has come back stronger and with a purpose....to decimate all the looming evil that has now swept into the PCW!
