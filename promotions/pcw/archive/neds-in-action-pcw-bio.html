Neds in Action Biography Fed Titles Andy - 1x PCW Rising Star Champion Biography One day, something weird happened. Andy Murray was born. Another future Ned was brought into the world by a couple of Nedly parents, living in the fine city of Glasgow, home of the Neds. Andy Murray was born into a poor family living in a small Glasgow apartment, like many others around him. He lived a poor life early on, neither of his parents had a job (with them being Neds and all), meaning that they had to live on a very small Unemployed Benefit. However, it wasn’t much of a problem in reality, because all they ever ate was fish, chips & Deep Fried Mars Bars, they even fed that to Andy. As Andy grew up, he went through school where he made friends with a fellow group of ‘Wee men’ (See the NEDucation RP for further info on The Wee Man). They stuck together to keep themselves out of trouble, and offered each other fashion tips. Well, they exchanged names of ‘quality’ tracksuits, if that counts as fashion tips. Like so many other ‘Wee Men’, Murray and his group (led by Andy and his now PCW manager Rab) hid behind The Big Man most of the time, and were lucky enough to escape the wrath of the normal public on many any occasion. Andy left school aged sixteen, as he described it as ‘Pure pish min’. He then moved into an apartment with his best friend, Rab, where they still live today. As Andy’s attitude and accent continued to develop, he turned into a fully fledged Ned, enjoying his Nedly life to the fullest. Andy’s parents live in the same apartment block as Andy, as do most of his other early school friends. Andy still pays visits to The Big Man, but he is far less reliant on him than he was as a Wee Man. A supporter of Glasgow Rangers Football Club, Andy now plies his trade as a professional wrestler, trying to, in his words, ‘Show they chubes who’s boss an’ stuff’. Indeed. Still confused?
