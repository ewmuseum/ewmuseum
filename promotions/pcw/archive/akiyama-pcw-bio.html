Akiyama Biography Real Name Akira Akiyama Nicknames Akiyama, Aki, and a few others... Hometown Jahana Castle, Sado Island, Japan Birthday March 3, year undeclared Height 6'10 Weight 309 lbs Biography Akiyama's known for wearing tight black vinyl multi-zippered pants and black silver buckled boots along with a black mesh see through muscle shirt. His waist length black and silver hair is tied back most of the time in a tight ponytail with a yellow ribbon. His handsome face and almond eyes are often found emotionless, yet a bit of depression can be found as well. Black tape is wrapped around his forearms and hands. His fingernails are black; not painted, but as a result from an attack from Zeus. His origins are one shrouded in mystery. Where some say that he's just some psycho freak who thinks he's a dark god, others claim that he is indeed the ruler of the collective negativity of the universe! The tales never come across as fact or fiction, and only Akiyama himself will be the one to declare his background. If it's a analysis of the NVG you want, then listen closely. A chiseled mountain of a being who defies the laws of gravity and logic with amazing aerial and acrobatic maneuvers only capable from smaller cruiserweights. He claims that everyone, including himself, are 'Children of Battle', and that everyone is joined together on another level when combat ensues. Sure, he's eccentric, complex, stylish, and deeply intelligent. Sure, his motives are unknown. Sure, you'll never know when he'll snap into the VOID TRANCE and go on a banishment rampage. Sure, he seems to be quite dark and unemotional sometimes...but once you take some time out to talk to him...you'll see that he's not so bad...once you get past his dialogue and brooding demeanor...
