Shawn Mosely Biography Biography Shawn Mosely was born in Philadelphia where he would live the first year and a half of his life . Mosely was born into a rich family who eventually moved to Orlando Florida where they hoped for Mosely to get a good upcoming of his life . Shawn was somewhat spoiled when he grew up but his parents never over did it .Eventually Mosely got older and got interested into sports . At the age of 14 he played Basketball , Soccer , Tennis , and Wrestled for his High School . A very competitive kid he practiced every sport hard but took a liken for wrestling . He took Soccer and Tennis out of his schedule leaving basketball and wrestling . His junior year in high school he was one of the top stars on his basketball team and the best wrestler in the state of florida at one point . His senior year never happened . His mother died in a car accident involving his father also . The father survived with bruises and cuts as Shawn Mosely was all in a dark corner in his mind as this went on . He never played basketball and wrestled his senior year . The summer after school closed Shawn Mosely moved to Palm Beach , Florida hoping to get the good outta life . It was there he realized how lucky he was with his money and athletic ability . He eventually became to cocky thinking he was better then everyone . He had friends but was it for his money or an actualy friendship . Wih his cocky attitude Mosely brought himself to this wrestling camp in Southern Florida after deciding to get into wrestling again . . .a different kinda wrestling . It was there a guy by the name of Joe Armstrong noticed his athletic ability and started to train . For the past year now Shawn Mosely has been guided by Armstrong and is now ready to step into the real ring with his cocky attitude and great athletic ability . Moselys career starts off in Fans Wrestling Online [fWo] . He pretty much picks apart his first foru opponents with ease . At 4-0 he goes into the pay per view for the Heavyweight Title . He ends up getting screwed over in the end as Shane Lawless is the first man to ever beat him . Shawn then leaves fWo as he was afraid of an action of him getting screwed over would happen again . Mosely has jumped from little fed to little fed until he met . .Five-Star Wrestling [FSW] . A top wrestling organization that will provide Mosely fame and competition . Mosely started off slow as he had to wait two weeks before getting his first match in FSW . His first match came at the pay per view against S. Sterling Silver . Probably the biggest feud he has had to this point . The match was a rafter death match which at the end hurt both men . Sterling speared Mosely 40 feet down to the ground as they went threw two tables . The match was declared a 'no contest' . Shawn was then sceduled in a PrimeTime title match with Sterling Silver at the ppv . Shawn goes on and wins the PrimeTime title as it becomes his first belt win of his career . He then holds the belt for an FSW record 49 days before he is finally beaten . Kay Kash beat him and Silvers in a triple threat match . Shawn has battled his way back up to the #1 contender and recieves a shot at the title Sunday , August 19 , 2001 . Shawn retired from wrestling after a World Title bout with Stefan in FSW and went into a great depression where he left America for South America. He was founded in Jacksonville, Florida 7 months later by James Creon and Scott Nolan.
