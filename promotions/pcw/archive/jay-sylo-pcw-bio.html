Jay Sylo Biography Biography <b>Ring Entrance:</b> The entire arena turns as black as midnight. It stays this way a few seconds until the sound of a child praying can be heard. In the back ground the sounds of classical music can be heard until a great explosion of fire crosses the stage. 'My Plague' by slipknot blasts through the arena as Jay Sylo pushes his way through the curtian and out onto the stage. After letting otu a snarl he moves down to the ring area, enters the ring, and stands ready to fight.<br> <b>Bio:</b> Jay Edward Sylo, born November 15th 1978, born to Julia and Michale Sylo. Jay was born on a stormy night, he came out 6 lbs. 7 ounces. <b>Born-5:</b> Jay was a normal little boy, he liked to play with his friends, watch cartoons, and play with his toys. He loved his family very much and all and all they were an average middle class family. But then one night a robber snuck into their house and killed both of Jay's parents. He found them dead the next morning...this is how Innocents becomes lost. <b>6-10:</b> Jay went to live in a group home. The headmasters hated him it seemed but Jay kept to himself. He didn't have very many friends and this was the time when he knew he was diffrent. <b>11-18:</b> Jay lived with his grandmother and attended school. He had no other family besides her, the two became close but once Jay left for college...she passed on. <b>19-To Now</b> Sylo attended the University of Georgia...he played football and also worked nights as a bouncer. Pretty soon Sylo was fired due to complications at the club. Then shortly after graduation he decided to become a wrestler, from there he went to the PWA and soon came into the NTICW. After the closing of that he went to the GWF and finally the PCW where he remains.
