Bob Vurdo Biography Real Name Roberto Vurdo Nicknames The Mayor Hometown Amber Hill USA Birthday 17-12-1973 Height 6'2 Weight 245 lbs Biography Bob Vurdo grew up in the Vurdo family a healthy young boy. He didn't know about his fathers dark side or the business until his brothers death. Although his father (Vincent) was considered a dark man and was feared by most of America, he was a very good father to Bob. He taught Bob many lessons and values and by the time Bob was ten he was a well mannered young man. At the age of seventeen Bob began finding out about the business and what his father did for a living. He finally confirmed what his father did for a living through his brothers death. His brother was killed during a feud with another family. His brother was just an object used by the other family to get at Vincent. His brother was shot several times including twice in the head. They then dumped his dead body outside the Vurdo families' mansion. His brothers name was Anthony and he was just twenty five when he died in 1997 Bob was twenty two at the time. Bob knew something was up and in the next few years he gradually found out about the business. The respect the community had for the Vurdo family started growing quickly through Vincent Vurdo. Vincent was brilliant in the way he ran the Vurdo family and they became very wealthy and powerful. They then decided to make a move from New York to Amber Hill which was a quieter town with a lot of potential for the Vurdo family. They began running things in Amber Hill and started making a lot of money. The other families heard about this and many tried to move in on the Vurdo's territory but none where successful. In 2000 Vincent Vurdo was found dead in a railway tunnel, he had suffered multiple strikes with a bludgeon weapon to the back of the head Vincent was fifty one years old at the time. They never found out who killed Vincent Vurdo and its always in the back of Bob's head, he feels he must one day find the murderer and make him pay. Bob was twenty five when this happened but by then he knew a lot about the business as his father was teaching him. With Vincent's death the Vurdo family needed a new leader and when they watched the will taping of Vincent the Vurdo family was shocked it was Bob Vurdo. Everyone within the Vurdo family doubted Vincent's decision as Bob was only twenty five at the time and was unmarried. Bob also had only known about the business and how things worked for three years. Despite everyone's doubts Bob was determined to prove himself as a worthy leader of the Vurdo family. Within three months he did this, a rival family found out about the new leader in Bob and thought the Vurdo family was weaker than ever. They came and tried to take over Amber Hill but where slaughtered very quickly by Bob Vurdo's men. Bob made a lot of good decisions in the feud which eliminated the rival family very quickly. Bob also did a few revolutionary things, things that were never done within any family before. These revolutionary ideas where well liked by the family and they worked well. Bob became a pioneer in the world of the Mafia. However there was one thing that was beginning to become a problem. Bob didn't have a wife or a girlfriend he was single. At the age of twenty seven people started wondering when he was going to have children and get married as he would need someone to take over the business one day. Bob never really had a strong relationship with any female other than his mother. Bobs mother (Maree Vurdo) was Bob's only blood family member alive. She was always a very influential part of Bob's life and it didn't change when he took over the business. Bob loves his mother more than any other person on earth and is very over protective of her. Mrs. Vurdo is the only person Bob loves and is a very important part of him that he can't afford to be taken away.
