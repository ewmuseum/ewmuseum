Savanda is never serious about anything until it comes to kids, and her brother Stephen Carson. Then and only then, does her Lady Cat personal drop Bio: Let's see what can I tell you about me? I was born in Andalusia, AL on May 26. When I was three, my parents got a divorce. I went with my mother and Stephen (my brother) went with my father. Me and my mother moved out to Atlanta, GA and she remarried to a man named Thomas Walker. Me and him don't get along much. I lived with them until I was sixteen, then I moved back to Andalusia to live with my Aunt and Uncle who were also raising my brother. My father had got his big "break" in his wrestling career and just dumped Stephen off. Stephen continued to work out thou. He has a bit of a mental problem and can get quite violent at times. I admit I was scared of him at first. We didn't really talk to each-other until one day when I arrived home on a date. This jerk just wouldn't take no for a answer. Needless to say, Stephen scared him off and completely totalled the guys car. Since then we've had a pretty close relationship. Unfortunately, my aunt and uncle died in a car accident. I've been taking care of Stephen ever since. Well, I noticed Stephen was really into wrestling, so I decided to get him "more" involved. Lucky for us, Stephen was still pretty popular at my dad's old wrestling hangouts. After a few training sessions (a breeze for my brother) one of the talent scouts we called Uncle Ror decided to give us a boost. We entered into the ACW (Alabama Championship Wrestling) when Stephen turned 25. Stephen was doing great and getting the notice that myself and Uncle Ror had hoped for. Uncle Ror planted the idea of a name for Stephen and I really don't want to get into that story again *l*. After ACW, we traveled to around a bit with a group of guys in got involved in BCP (Brother's Professionals Challenge) in traveling organization that booked matches in different cities with up and coming wrestlers. It was basically a organization that went out and found new talent. We stayied with them for two years. After that, we took some friendly advice from dear Uncle Ror and entered the WWC (World Wrestling Profession) were he become three time International Champion and World Wrestling Profession Heavyweight Champion. After three years, followed by the company being sued and closed, I got a phone call from my friend Victoria Lorraine (Bunny) about a great new wrestling organization called CCW (Cyberspace Championship Wrestling). Well he never won any titles but Tag team. So, I managed Bunny, Jim Klown Bowe, my brother, Kelly Bowe, and now Akiko. All of them winning championship gold of some kind. Well, back to me. *L* CCW started having some serious problems, and CEO's began quitting. Stevens offered me the position of Talent Coordinator. Soon, the commissioner quit, and the rest is history and I don't want to talk about it. *blows kisses* Goodbye babez
