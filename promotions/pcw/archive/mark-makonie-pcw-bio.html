Mark Makonie Biography Real Name Markus Makonie Nicknames The Little German Tank Hometown Pasadena, CA Birthday 7-12-82 Height 6' Weight 220 lbs Biography Mark was born on July 12, 1982 to Norm and Medusa. When Mark was little he was just a normal kid going through grade school. He dressed like the normal clothes and did the normal activities that other kids did. Mark played a little bit of YMCA youth soccer but that was all for sports. Going through middle school he had friends but was not a popular kid, but later only found out that his friends were using him for his homework skills. He tried out for the basketball team but he got cut because there was a couple of African American kids that were awesome who made the team. So going into his freshman year in high school Mark decided to try the game of chess, he discovered his talent at a summer camp in Pasadena. He was really good and it and had a natural ability to play the game. All through high school Mark was teased constant for his apperance and because what he did for fun. He was in the drama club, chess club, and the band playing the tuba. Going out of high school Mark was accepted into a college down in Florida. After taking up the sport of wrestling, Mark had to find a way out of all of this teasing. Rooming with an African American in college did not help much because of the constant teasing from him and his friends. Mark then met a man Jax, who brought Mark in to the Neo-Nazi world where he stands now. Now having his wrestling dreams come true with joining PCW Mark is just wrestling to take out his anger and violence somewhere that it is not banned, and that is wrestling.
