Ebola III Biography Real Name Kevin Sandusky Nicknames 'Bola, E-3 Hometown Tucson AZ Birthday Jan 17th, 1983 Height 6'1' Weight 215 lbs lbs Biography Kevin Sandusky was born in Tucson AZ in 1983.. Even before his birth, he was a center for controversy in the wrestling world.. His father, Mark Sandusky, was an up and coming face in the Arizona based SWA.. His mother Rachel Doyle was a 17 yr old girl that worked for the promotion as a seamstress.. Unfortunately for all involved, Mark was already married.... Before Kevin's birth (infact shortly after his conception), Mark was forced out of the SWA by Rachel's father Jack, and her brother Joey (who at the time was a teenage phenom sidekick to 'Mighty' Mark Sandusky) in a move that crippled Mark's marriage and US career.. A divorced, depressed, and desperate Mark Sandusky traveled to Japan in hope that he could find success without the specter of his past hanging over him.. In Japan, Mark was forced to play the 'ugly American' heel.. Isolated in the foreign environment, Mark began to live his gimmick.. The 260 pound Sandusky swelled up to 320 pounds in a lifestyle that was encompassed by the 'Triple S'.. Sake, Sapporo, and Steroids... 'Mad' Mark was a legitimate draw for a time, until his temper started to wear through.. He was pegged as a risky prospect with a penchant for hurting other workers, but was still one of the top 5 'overseas' heels in Japan.. Kevin's uncle Joey continued to work in the SWA... Joey became a father of sorts to Kevin during his early years, as Rachel and Kevin continued to live in the family's home in Tucson.. Joey watched Kevin with a close eye, praying that his father's immoral tendencies wouldn't be passed on to the next generation.. But as with all young men, the future loomed large for Joey Doyle.. Desperate to have a career in the wrestling industy, was forced to leave the US where his cruiserweight style wasn't appreciated, and went to the only place where he could find work.. Japan.. For years Joey and Mark avoided each other in Japan.. Joey being forced into a role as a 'Masked Luchadore' and Mark as a mid-level 'monster-heel'.. The situation was a powder keg, and the fuse was lit in April of 1989 at the Tokyo-Dome.. Mark and Joey were booked against each other.. The Japanese bookers of the event had no idea of what they had done.. They were was shocked and disgusted as Japanese fans and the rest of the world at what came to pass.. What was supposed to be a filler match to bring about a top-level face run-in, turned into a bloodbath... Mark pounded away at Joey mercilessly, until the younger and quicker Joey brought his speed and expertise to bear.. Joey destroyed Mark's knee with a straight kick that bent Mark's knee at a 90 degreee angle, outward.. Worse yet, Joey unmasked, exposing himself as a caucasian and not the Mexican luchadore he was billed as.. Both Mark and Joey left Japan disgraced; both labelled as stupid Americans not professional enough to to what they were told... Ok, back to young Kevin... While this was playing out on the far side of the Pacific, Rachel Doyle fell in love with a part-time SWA trainer, Jose Gonzales.. They eventually married.. Young Kevin was emmersed in the business from the time he could walk.. He showed alot of promise, but also the egotistical arrogance that was so much a part of his father.. He was smug and cocky, and remained that way until one night, Jose set him down and told him the entire story of Rachel and Joey Doyle, Mark Sandusky, and how Kevin came to be... From that night on, Kevin's demeanor changed.. He was focused not to become the wreck of a man that his father became.. He also vowed to right the wrongs that had preceded him.. To prove that a man can overcome his own circumstances and prevail.. But to do that, Kevin went to the one place where he would have to work the hardest to overcome the past.. Japan.. In Japan, Kevin became one of the breakout stars of the new PURE promotion, climbing the ranks and garnering the full support of the initially hostile Japanese fans. As PURE's finacial situation crumbled, Kevin realized he had achieved his goals in Japan. He had overcome the adversities left for him, and he decided to look for work in the states, seeking acceptance not for his father or uncle but for himself. Now Kevin fights for his own career and to expose the American fans to the traditions of true lucha and puroresu, to which he owes so much. Other Feds PURE v1.0, RUSH Other Fed Awards n/a
