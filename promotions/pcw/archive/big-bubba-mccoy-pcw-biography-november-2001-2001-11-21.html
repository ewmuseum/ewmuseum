Big Bubba McCoy — PCW biography (November 2001)

Big Bubba McCoy Real Name: Bubba McCoy Nick Name: Bubba Mac, Big Bad Bubba Catchphase: Damn! / Ohh! Now you dun pissed me off! Character Handler: James P. MSN Account: [detail withheld] AIM Address:[detail withheld] Theme Song: Click Click BOOM! - Siliva Age:38 From: Huntsville Alabama Height:6'6" Weight: 245 lbs. Wrestling Style: Brawler, Powerhouse, Innovator Signature Moves: Bull Dog, (All Versions) Alabama Jam, (Super Shoulder Breaker)Oklahoma Slam, Finisher Move: Round-Up (A cross of the Indian Deathlock and the Tazzmision) Set-Up Move: Branded (Top rope Implant DDT) Manager:'The Hardcore Dwarf' Logan Creed Age: 35 Description: Logan is rough looking little man, like a walking rock. Has deep brown eyes and a long brown grayish beard, and short messy hair to match. He wears jeans and a black shirt of some kind, often matching Bubbas. Brown Midget size trench coat.(Concealing various weapons) Dips tobacco and always has a mouth full. Has a nasty habit of spiting on people after a match. Current Team: None Past Teams & Groups: None Title History: None Awards History: None Description: A well built man, Not Meanstreak built, but A good size. More of a Mountain Man build, Short Black scraggly hair with some gray around the ears and though out, Black Hoganish Mustache, and a Matching Goatee, This as well has streaks of gray in it, the rest of his beard is in a constant 5 o clock shadow. A scar on the side of his face, under the left eye, (not ugly gory type) but an old wound; from his pit fighting days. Brown eyes Missing his right tooth next to his K-9 tooth Has a barbwire tattoo on his right shoulder with a Rebel flag under that On his back right shoulder blade a Skull wearing a Cowboy hat and smoking a cigar. A few others on his arms and chest as well but those are the more prominent. A big old scar on his left shoulder blade. History: Grew up in Alabama, Where he was a troublemaker in high school. Found the fast money of underground pit fighting. Worked that scene for a long time Became a 4 time champion longest reign lasting almost a year, Retired from that as the Champion, Moved onto wrestling, Joined WCWF back in the day. Had a memorable feud, with Will Stone, Witch bubba was taken out. (Bubba still believes there was backstage working on this grooming Stone to be a World Champion.) Became the head commentator for WCWF’s WNW. Did that for a while then left. Tried a comeback to the ring, but that did not pan out well; not liking the way WCWF was heading he left. Returned to Wresting in the GWF as their Head Commentator. Did this for a while. After undergoing a state of the art back operation, Bubba now finds his self fully healed, and in the best shape of his life. The operation restored full movement to his back. Returned to in ring competition, by entering the PTC Global tournament. Bubba went to round three and lost in what he sees as his best match to date. Bubba then went to wrestle in GWF, and returned to WCWF, Wanting to Prove he is a Champion. Then came Diamond in the Rough. Bubba entered and, Lost. Bubba was defeated in another grueling match with a man half his age, showing something to the PCW mysterious Phantom Booker. So now Bubba enters into the PCW. Fully healed he is ready to give it one last shot. To be able to go out on Top instead of being remembered for the guy who lost to Will Stone. Who? Yep that’s the point.
