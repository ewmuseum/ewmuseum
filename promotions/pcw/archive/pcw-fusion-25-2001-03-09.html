Fusion 25 9 Mar 2001 The E-Center, Camden, New Jersey The Franchise and Meanstreak in the parking lot The scene opens in the back parking area of the E-Center in Camden, New Jersey, just before Friday Fusion begins. A camera remains trained on a wide open area in the middle of the lot, where four men stand proud and tall. They're all of similar height and as they step into the light, we see them to be the group claiming to call themselves "The Franchise" consisting of John Storm, Shane Andrews, Jason Cross, and Drake Diamond walk towards the camera view. They pass by a few cars, running their fingers along them and speaking about their new contracts in PCW. John drops something and bends over. He leans down to pick it up, only to be met by a huge black boot thudding to the ground before his eyes. He looks upward as the camera follows his gaze. Meanstreak stands before him. John Storm: Uh...hi... The beast jerks his head to his left in which Storm takes a look. There, parked and standing out like a sore thumb, is Streak's nearly junked Mitsubishi 3000 GT. John Storm looks back up and yelps. Meanstreak: Ya touched my car...all of yas touched my car... The monster leans downward and grips Storm by the throat. The camera switches back to the front of the other three men as they continue to talk, not seeming to take notice of the occurrings behind them. Streak walks forward, clenching Storm in his right hand by the throat and dragging him across the ground. He growls and Shane Andrews turns around. He gasps and tells his companions to stop. They do so and turn around to see what he's making such a big fuss about. All three of their jaws drop to the floor as they watch Meanstreak whip the body of John Storm through the air like a rag doll. He lands horizontally across his other three members, taking them all down. The beast approaches them, kicking Storm off and grabbing Andrews and Diamond by the throats. He, too, pulls them up and holds them into the air. Meanstreak: Only I can touch my car, ya lil f#cks... The beast slams their heads together and lets them drop to the floor. Jason Cross, by now, has pulled hismelf free from the group and is crawling on his belly across the parking lot floor. Streak sees him and lunges forward, bringing his entire weight down onto his body. Cross screeches under the 340 pounder's weight and tries to catch the wind that had just been knocked out of him. Meanstreak pulls himself to his feet and picks Cross up. He slings him over his shoulder and walks back to the other three members of the "Franchise". Leaning down and grabbing Diamond and Andrews by the back of their shirts, he drags them forward, kicking Storm ahead every time he gets within the monster's walking path. Streak drops Cross off his shoulder and lets the other two go. He bends down and pulls them atop eachother, stacking them in a big pile. The monster's fists leave from the view, strapped up in heavy white tape from his bleeding knuckles and the camera focuses on the heap of bodies. A second later, the beast returns. He perches atop the hood of his car, draggin both Andrews and Cross up with him. He holds them both by the collars. Meanstreak: Ya touched my car...ya four punks...touched MY car. He rakes their faces across the jagged glass and hops off the hood. Andrews and Cross are both pulled to their feet. Streak roars and bends down, grabbing them both around in a huge bear hug. Meanwhile, Diamond and Storm have gotten to their feet, seeing their friends in trouble. Meanstreak, however, sees them as well. He squeezes hard, pressing his enormous arms inward, and growls as both Andrews and Cross scream in pain. Their limbs immediately descend downward like puppets. The minute they do so, the beast drops their limp bodies to the ground and charges at the approaching Diamond and Storm. He lunges at Storm, drilling him across the face with a punch that pops the neck of the man audibly and spins him around. This leaves him open to be clocked with another punch, this time to the base of his back. Storm falls to his knees as Streak stands up. The beast grabs the man's hair in his hand and brings his knee swiftly and forcefully into the back of his head. The camera switches to Storm's blank face and gaping mouth as he falls forward and hits the concrete. Diamond looks up at the beast, trying to reason with him and give a good reason as to why Meanstreak shouldn't tear him apart like he did his friends. Drake Diamond: Please don't hurt me!!! Meanstreak kicks the unconscious body of Storm out of the way and clenches his fist tightly. He backhands the smaller Diamond across the face, blood fleeing from his mouth and splattering across the pavement. He hits the floor and Streak jumps up, bringing his body feet first onto the back of the rookie PCW employee.
