Fusion 6 27 Oct 2000 Maple Leaf Gardens, Toronto, Canada My TV title belt is bigger than yours! The show opens showing sHs members Scott Naket, The Flaming One and PCW Television champion Revenant exiting a limosine outside of Maple Leaf Gardens in Toronto. Scott Naket is wearing an sHs T-shirt with a Canadian flag on it, showing off his Canadian heritage. Scott Naket: Don't worry Revenant, we got your back to make sure the NUO doesn't intefere in your title match tonight. Revenant: Well just make sure you guys don't get out numbered and overwhelmed because the NUO, though a bunch of nobodies, out numbers us 2 to 1. Flaming One, you got that. The Flaming One just nods. Scott Naket: Well, now that Seraph is with a member of sHs, that should even out the odds a little. Just as the trio is about to enter the arena, Revenant begins digging through his gym bag. Revenant: Sh!t! I left something in the limo. I'll be right back. Revenant sprints off towards the limo while Naket and the Flaming One enter the arena. As they enter the building, there is suddenly a blur of motion and Scott Naket and The Flaming One find themselves confronted and surrounded by a dozen police officers in swat gear. The scene is chaotic, as the camera man is shoved out of the way by police rushing in to apprehend Naket and the Flaming One. There are innumerable commands being screamed at the pair as the police grab apprehend Scott Naket and drag him off, leaving the Flaming One standing their alone. The camera gets a close up of The Flaming One's face. His eyes glaze over as he watches Naket dragged away by police. He seems confused and doesn't know what to do, so he sits down on the floor and begins to drool. The camera shot goes back to the parking lot where Revenant is shown digging looking around for something in the trunk of the limo. He picks up a tire iron from the trunk, shrugs and tosses it in his gym bag. He reaches up and closes the trunk and then the camera shot is full of white mist.
