Xavier Kannon Biography Real Name Christian Xavier Kannon-Hall. Nicknames Gold Patron Meritorious, Wrestling's Only Scientologist. Hometown Hubbardville, Wisconsin. Birthday 25/12/74 Height 6' 1 1/2 Weight 229 lbs Biography All around the World, religion is causing problems. Petty disputes by people of differing faiths to full on war between nations. Millions have died, many more will... All due to religion. PCW, all be it on a smaller scale, has also suffered due to a certain person’s religion of choice. Namely Xavier Kannon and Scientology. Kannon has made no secret of his faith, and he knew how to use it to his advantage. Every attack against him must have been due to his religion. Any small mistake by a referee was due to their deep rooted prejudices against him... Even the PCW President was not spared his rantings of oppression. This belief that he was being looked down on spurred Kannon on not only in the ring, but out of it too. He knew if he had backstage power he could mould the minds of the fans, and he even went as far as naming Scientology the official religion of PCW. Still, it didn’t stop. As long as their is a non-Scientologist in the crowd he will berate them, as long as there are no referees who follow Scientology he claim to have been cheated. Does he honestly believe it? Who knows, but he is a cunningly clever man and it would be no shock to learn that Xavier played PCW like a game. But with his wife, Ellie~! as PCW Commissioner and a 50% stake in PCW along with the Phantom Booker, it is a game he could easily win. Other Feds RSW, PWA. Other Fed Awards RSW World Champion, PWA Graduate, PWA Top Student x2. Quotes Peon. So while you’re trying to decide if you should be on ReVolution or Grey’s Anatomy, Wade, I’ll be there ready to beat you so damn hard that you’ll flashback to Daddy getting the belt out and his drunk pals bruising your ‘Bama… and by ‘Bama, I obviously mean shithole. I’m not just a cunt, Wade. I’m THE cunt. Oh, who am I kidding?! Kids, what I did to Rhine last week? DO IT AT SCHOOL! Some kid is picking on you because you’re brighter? STAB THEM IN THE EYE! Getting bullied because your spiritual enlightenment leaves you dismissing your classmates as mere troglodytes? STAB THEM IN THE EYE! Big brother or sister hassling you at home? STAB THEM IN THE EYE! Worked for me! You don’t even need a broken heel… you can use so many household objects. A chopstick, a toothbrush, a pen, be resourceful! I don’t want you to lose heart, Jono. I may have taken you eye, I may have taken your dignity, and when I’m through with you tonight, I’ll have taken away any credibility you had as SCCW Universal champion. But, y’know, I hear that in the Kingdom of the Blind, you’re really someone! I Dislike You A Lot, So Should You. Can You Feel The RUSH? Peon.
