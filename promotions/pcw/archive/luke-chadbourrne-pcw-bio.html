Luke Chadbourrne Biography Real Name Luke Chadbourne Nicknames 'The Ladies Man' Hometown Bakersfield, CA Birthday 10/01/82 Height 6'0' Weight 236 lbs Biography Luke Chadbourne was born on October 1, 1982 in a small college town in central Wisconsin by the name of Stevens Point. He lead a very average life. He always did well in school and had lots of friends. His father was an business man and owned his own car repair and body shop, which would make him millions by the time Luke was 15. His mother was a great lawyer but didn't get much practice in the small town, so family decided to move to California. On January 3, 1998, The Chadbourne's pack up and head out west to the mid-upper class town of Bakersfield, California. The move would devastate Luke. He had his lose group of friends back in Wisconsin and now he was forced to leave them. When Luke started school in California, he was slow to make friends. There was only one guy who was willing to hang out with him, and that kid's name was David Kurtenbach. Now this is when all the trouble started. With David's young anarchist influence, Luke started smoking marijuana, drinking heavily and using cocane and heroin in excess amounts. Then on February 19, 2000, Luke would get one hell of a wake up call. His parents had died in a fatal car crash. The bitter irony is that the car crash that took his parents' lives was caused by a drug driver. And as if this wasn't enough reason to get clean, his parents will stated that to inherit the fortune that the family had earned of 6.4 million dollar, he had to be drug free. Luke would get drug free, but not stay that way. Luke to this day is alcohol free and doesn't do heroin or cocane. He still smokes marijuana and now he has picked up the habit of smoking cigarettes. Now at the age of 21, Luke has decided to make something out of his life that he almost wasted on drugs and alcohol. That's where PCW comes in. Now its time to make history.
