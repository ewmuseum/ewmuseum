Rudolph von Hackenmann Biography Real Name Rudolph Alkamatra Biography Rudolph von Hackenmann was born in Vienna, Austria to a lower class family who lived in the bad section of the city. When Rudolph was just one year old, his house was burned to the ground by some hooligan children who loved to play with fire. To save Rudolph from certain death, his father grabbed him out of his crib while he slept and threw him out of a window, right on to some trash. His father and mother were trapped inside the house, and they died. Rudolph was found crying in the garbage a few hours later by a little demon child, who showed himself to be more mature than he looked. He brought the child to the police station, where officers identified him as Rudolph Alkamatra. They soon discovered that his parents had been killed in the fire, and immediately shipped him to us, here at the Voltkrieger Home. He lived here with us for one continuous year, when a middle class family adopted him. He returned after only a few days, when the new father claimed that Rudolph had thrown a beer bottle at him. All I could do was laugh in my head. Rudolph continued to live here, after possible guardians read up on his past experiences, including breaking the side off of his crib, hitting his mother in the face with a baseball, and many, many more... He was here for most of his time until he was fifteen years old. In early May, a rather scary man by the name of John von Hackenmann came into the place, wearing soiled science clothing... The first thing that ran into my head was that it was the man who lived in that castle up on the mountain. But, I soon remembered how foolish of an assumption that was, since they found the body of the owner up there, dead from natural causes almost ten years ago, and since then, the place had been boarded up. Anyway, back to Rudolph... John took Rudolph, kicking and screaming, with him. We here at the home never heard from Rudolph or John again. Almost two months later, John was found dead in the castle that I had been thinking about, and Rudolph had killed him. Strangled him... I saw Rudolph again later, after I wrote this first section, and he almost killed me too! Him and that... blasted robot... ---Amadeus Greicher <b>Description</b> Attire: White laboratory coat with black buttons and long sleeves, black pants, black gloves, and black boots. Face: Rudolph has sunk eyes, a thin nose, and small lips. His hair is black, poofy, and scruffy. His eyes are dark brown, almost blackish. He has no outstanding facial hair, but he has black whiskers where a beard, mustache, and sideburns would be. His eyebrows are very thick and bushy, and his forehead is wrinkly. Skin Color: White, almost albino color.
