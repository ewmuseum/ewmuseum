Jon Kano Biography Real Name Jonathan Kano Nicknames 'The Man Who Rocketh the Mosteth' 'He Who Rocks' 'The REAL LEGEND' Hometown Las Vegas, Nevada Birthday February 19, 1976 Height 6' 10' Weight 304 lbs Biography Jonathan Kano was born on February 19, 1976 to the loving parents of Marcus and Jennifer Kano at Our Lady of the Worthless Miracle Hospital in Las Vegas, Nevada. He was their second child. At an early age, Jon Kano showed tremendous athletic ability as his older brother, Ken, did. Ken was two years older than Jon and set many records in baseball and basketball at Las Vegas High School, only to be shattered by Jon when he entered high school. Ken and Jon were as close as two brothers could be and Jon followed in Ken's footsteps after graduation by enlisting in the Army. Both Kano children were demolition men. They built and defused bombs for the U.S. Army and both achieved the rank of Specialist. Everything was great. That was until Ken was killed in a freak accident during the summer of 1996 while defusing a bomb. Jon was devastated. Following his brother's death and his discharge from the Army, Jon bounced from job to job and finally enrolled in a wrestling school. There he met his wrestling mentor, The Sparrow (of FWF fame). Jon learned his new found trade very well and very quickly. He has since wrestled across the globe, capturing gold on five continents. In May 1998, Jon Kano founded Kano Enterprises, Inc. This corporation funds several real estate developments across the state of Nevada and has made Kano and several stock holders very rich men. In October of 2000, Jon Kano debuted in Primetime Championship Wrestling. He has held three titles since his debut and has gained the respect and admiration of fans and wrestlers alike. Recently, Kano has had one goal and one goal only, to survive. He is one of the few true OLD SCHOOLERS in PCW left in the sea of new talent in PCW. Kano has christened himself the 'REAL LEGEND,' and is hell-bent on keeping his place in PCW as a future hall of famer. At PCW Third Dawning, Jon Kano returned after a short retirement from injuries suffered at the hands of Corey Williams, the self proclaimed, 'King of the New Era.' It was touted at Old School versus New School. These two men had feuded for several months in 2002, culminating at Third Dawning in a retirement match. Both men fought valiantly for their careers, and on that day, Jon Kano was victorious, forever cementing his place as an Old School hero and a REAL legend of PCW. After Third Dawning, things had changed in PCW. Xavier Kannon was hell bent on ridding PCW of all of the old guard. This resulted in Xavier Kannon selling PCW in January of 2003 to a man who would do his best to destroy PCW, Syko the Goblin. Jon Kano had enough of the antics of Xavier Kannon and Syko the Goblin and walked out of the company he helped create, PCW. He returned to the wrestling ring a few weeks later, but was never the same. He just tended to go through the motions, exploding with fury inside of the ring, but had no flare outside of the ring. Jon Kano retired from Primetime Championship Wrestling on March 16, 2003, at March Madness. He had won four PCW titles. He was one of the most popular and recognized PCW personalities and faces in PCW history. Following a heartfelt farewell from Kano at March Madness, Jon Kano was informed that he was now a HALL OF FAMER.
