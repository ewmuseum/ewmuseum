El Diablo Real Name:Raul Diaz Nick Name:Diablo. The Mexican Devil. Your Saviour. Catchphase:"Sweet Dreams" "Mommy once said..." "666" "I am Your Saviour. Character Handler:Richard MSN Account:[detail withheld] AIM Address:[detail withheld] Theme Song: "Stanislaw" by Liberty 37 Age: 23 From: Puebla, Mexico Height: 5'9 Weight: 207lbs Wrestling Style: Luchadore Signature Moves: Lunasault(Moonsault), Bomba(Sen-ton bomb), Rana Chaputeo(Frog Splash), Hurrican Superior De La cuerda(Top Rope Hurricanrana), Vuelo Cruz Cuerpo(Flying Crossbody), Barrabraza(Arm bar), El Fantastico(Tarantula), Suplex Estupendo(Superplex), Estrangulacion Triangulo(Triangle Choke) STF, 360° Chaputeo(360° Splash), Hurricanrana, DDT. Finisher Move: Mephistopholes (Top Rope Flipping Spear) Set-Up Move: Ultra Loco (Juve/Michinoku Driver) Current Team: Past Teams & Groups:Illegal Aliens w/Frisco Freaks By Choice w/Syko, Skitzo, Agent Orange- EW, NTICW Title History: Awards History: Description: Long black hair that runs part way down his back. Bare Chest- defined and quite muscular, toned stomach, defined arms. Devil tattoo on his left shoulder. Muscular legs. Red and black mask(cross between Vader's and Mankind's masks) Black and white ringpants with "El Diablo" on the back of it in red blood dripping font. Black kneepads. Black and red boots. History: Grew up in Puebla, Mexico. Began watching wrestling at age 6, and decided he wanted to become a wrestler at age 8. His Dad, who ran a jail, started to train him until he was 16years old and soon a Mexican Wrestling Promoter signed him to a contract, and was taught many high flying moves, and eventually debuted there at 18 years old as Raul Diaz, his real name. But a few months later, he was involved in a car crash that could have ended his career, with his brother Pedro Rafael Diaz. Raul Diaz(El Diablo) prayed to God for him to heal, as Pedro was in a critical condition, but Pedro died a week larer. Raul then quit the Mexican promotion and wrestling and hid himself away in the room that he and his brother once shared, only coming out for meals and other neccessities. Whilst hiding away in the room, he still practiced wrestling, and as he was in there, he also gave up his faith and never again went to church. He started having nightmares about the devil, but soon came to a realistaion that Satan was speaking to him while he was sleeping, and Raul soon began listening to him. He left the room at age 19 and began wrestling again, but with a different attitude as El Diablo, and believes in sacrificing himself to the devil. El Diablo was hired aged 22 by PWA but was soon moved to NTICW and when that closed was placed in charge of PWA before being hired by PCW.
