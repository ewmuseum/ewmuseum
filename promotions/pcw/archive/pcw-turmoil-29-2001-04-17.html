Turmoil 29 17 Apr 2001 The Gulf Center, Gulfport, MS Tyler Nelson's interrupted path to the ring The scene fades into the backstage area of the Gulf Center in Gulfport, Gulfport, Mississippi.  The camera suddenly pans right, to reveal none other than the CEO of Primetime Championship Wrestling himself, Tyler Nelson striding confidently down the hallway towards the camera, with his trademark evil smirk on his face. The smirk, however, is the only thing that appears to be "usual" about the appearance of Nelson on this night, as the black and white vertical stripes of a referee's shirt, rather than his normal $3000 suit ensemble. Nelson continues to strut towards the camera, when suddenly there is a flash of movement from right to left from off camera as someone has stepped into the path of the CEO.  The shot shifts to a new camera angle to reveal the source of that movement is Stewart Hix, one of the competitors for tonight's world title match where Nelson will act as the referee. Hix is dressed in his usual attired, consisting of a black T-shirt and a pair of old, torn blue jays.  As always, a "marijuana" cigarette hangs from the Master of the 4:20's mouth. Nelson is momentarily started by the sudden appearance of Hix, but quickly regains his composure. Nelson:  Step aside, Hix.  Go talk to Da Westsidaz if you are looking to purchase a dime bag. Hix exhales a billowing cloud of smoke directly into the CEO's face and a sneering smile crosses his face.  Nelson blinks, then lets out a slight cough. Hix:  Relax, I just wanted to have a little chat before my match tonight and make sure you plan on calling things "right down the middle." Nelson smirks at Hix. Nelson:  Don't allow the few functioning brain cells you have left to fill with worry.  I am going to be completely neutral.especially since I hate both your guts! Nelson folds his arms defiantly as Hix takes a huge hit of his joint and shakes his head. Hix:  I figured you say that Suddenly, a chair erupts into the scene and crashes down upon the skull of Nelson, dropping the CEO like a sack of potatoes. The camera quickly pans out to reveal Jake Keeton as the assailant. Hix immediately jumps on the downed CEO and locks on his debilitating 4:20 submission maneuver while Keeton stands guard.  The CEO screams out and begins to tap on the concrete for mercy, but this only makes Hix' grin grow wider and seems to encourage him to tighten his grip. Hix locks on the hold for a good 20 seconds before Keeton grabs him by the back of his shirt.  Keeton : Come on man!! Someone's coming! Hix releases his hold on Nelson then stomps him in the small of his back once for good measure.  He and Hix then run off, with their chuckling voices trailing behind them, as Nelson lays motionless on the concrete floor.
