Chris Storm Biography Real Name Chris Storm Nicknames The Perfect Storm Hometown New York City Birthday Unknown Height 6'4 Weight 243 lbs Biography Chris Storm has always wanted to be a wrestler. He loved the sport and everything about it. Chris watched WIW and loved Genesis. Genesis was a mean gothic character and was the dominate heel. Well years later Chris decided to be a wrestler. He went down to New Orleans where Genesis lived and begged Genesis to train him. Finally he gave in and trained Chris. It was a horrible time for Chris. For two years Genesis tried to break his body and love for the sport. In his soul, Chris grew to hate Genesis. But Chris kept on with his training because he loved the sport. Finally Chris was able to get into the WIW. He had Genesis in his corner and won small titles. Chris got into trouble though after winning the TV title and started having behavioral problems. Chris was fired and public humilated. Having no place to go he started to tour in Japan. It is there that Chris Storm because a Shinto believer. He even studied the martial arts of the shrine called Teal Chi. Finally after a year Chris begged to be let back into the WIW, claiming he was a new man. They allowed him in and he once again gained more fame. He rose to a mid card level. But Chris once against must with trouble as he suffered a bad knee injury while training for a US title defense. Chris was once again side-lined for a year. He recently returned and now has had even more success in the ring. He is also having large success out of the ring. Chris was caught having sex with a co worker in WIW:NG, the reporter Kim Simpson, and it almost instantly launched his porn career. He now has multiple porn movies and has been on the cover of multiple magazines including Playgirl. He also has realizes his dream of being the WIW:NG World Heavyweight champ. Chris is being stated as one of the pillars that made WIW:NG a network league in PTC. Chris has also begun a serious relationship with WIW:NG and former XCWF wrestler, Tessa Windsor. The two are openly dating on and off camera. Chris Storm is now looking to use his success in the PCW league to test his limits. With Tessa Windsor by his side, he has the strength and the will to do whatever it takes to make it to the top. *It should be noted that Chris knows nothing of his childhood really.
