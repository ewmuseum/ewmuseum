One day, he couldn't take anymore of the boring television, consisting of weak promo after weak promo, and crappy matches. He quit his job, and trained vigorously in many wrestling schools. It was under the tutelage of legendary wrestler Dory Funk Jr that Badge would develop into a top talent in the Indies. Badge and his mentor decided that it would be a huge addition to Badge’s training if he would train in Mexico. While there, Badge learned the ropes of luchadore wrestling, and he learned how to counter the much quicker wrestlers. Badge was not complete in his training however. He needed something that would bring him much needed notoriety. He begged his teachers to teach him some high-risk moves, but they argued about the dangers of a man his size flying across the ring. After a lot of pleading and hard work, one of his teachers finally taught him one of the most difficult moves to master, the Swanton bomb. When he got back to the states, and showed off his new abilities, he was immediately watched by many of the top scouts in professional wrestling. Word spread widely in the wrestling scene about a 7'0 wrestler that could perform a Swanton bomb. It was then, that Badge would be scouted by PCW. The Phantom Booker thought that Badge had potential to be a big name in PCW so he signed Badge to a contract. Popo as he was called then, still needed something that would bring him to the top of his class. In a meeting with PCW management, The Phantom Booker himself came up with the name Badge, and his transformation was complete. Still green when signed by PCW, he was sent to the Neutral Zone Wrestling Academy, then later was sent to Primetime Indy Circuit. When PCW announced the opening of PCW2, management decided that the massive Badge would be one of the building blocks of PCW2. Badge’s PCW2 debut was not as he had hoped. He lost his first few matches, but it did not stop him. He was determined to make it into the PCW, so he worked hard, and finally won his first match at the hands of Livewire. Even with the hard work, Badge was not moving up the ranks until a man calling himself “Gods Booker” took over the helms of the PCW2. God’s Booker liked what he saw in Badge, and he convinced him to keep working hard. This hard work paid off, as he would go on a huge win streak. None of the other wrestlers could handle the mixture of his size and agility. Badge would continue this winning streak until he would be attacked by a man wearing an Easter bunny costume. The culprit finally revealed himself to be an Amish Revolutionary calling himself Jacob Yoder. Badge and Jacob Yoder would continue their feud through their entire time in the PCW2. The hatred between these two would fuel them into putting on amazing matches, and their feud would be the backbone of many PCW2 shows. The rumors of this heated battle flew through PCW, and caught the eye of PCW CEO Tyler Nelson. Nelson thought that Badge and Jacob Yoder would make vital contributions to his new formed stable GREED INC. During a match at Gold and Glory between Badge and Frisco, GREED INC would assist Badge in pulling of an upset over one of the most respected wrestlers in the history of PCW. The fans were outraged that Badge and Jacob Yoder would put aside their hatred for each other in order to get a few more zeroes in their paychecks. The loss of Yoder’s Amish support would cause him to retire, but Badge’s fans had already deserted him so he was unaffected. Badge and Nelson would be the backbone of GREED INC and would carry them to become one of the most dominating forces in the history of PCW. Nelson would begin to lose his motivation, and Badge and the rest of GREED INC would send him a message by attacking him at a PCW show. Once GREED INC lost the direction of Nelson. GREED INC began to have problems. Badge and newly recruited member Jeremy Howard would argue over control over GREED INC. Howard ordered GREED INC to attack Nelson during a match between the two, but Badge refused. Instead, Badge came to Nelson’s aide thus costing Howard the match. This outraged Jeremy Howard and it ultimately lead to Badge’s removal of the company he helped form, GREED INC. Having lost all of the financial support GREED INC was supplying him, Badge decided to go into business of his own. He formed Badge Protection, and now looks to be one of the PCW’s brightest superstars heading into the future.
