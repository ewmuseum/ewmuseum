Kid Krazie Biography Real Name Kid Krazie duh! Nicknames The Springboard Kid Hometown Houston, Texas Birthday June 22, 1980 Height 6'1' Weight 215 lbs Biography ***Krazie Poser Apperance*** Krazie has a California tan, athletic build with six pack, chisled chest and toned arms. His head is shaved on the sides and back with short light brown spiked hair on top with blonde tips. He has light brown, normal shaped eyes. He has his left eye brow pierced, and his left ear pierced twice along with his bottom lip. He has a bleached blonde goatee on his chin that leads to under his bottom lip. Krazie wears khaki colored cargo shorts, black knee pads, and black shin high wrestling boots with white laces. He has a dragon tattoo on the right side of his chest and the words KRAZIE tattoo'd in old english in an arc above his belly button. ***Poser Entrance Pose*** Krazie has his feet on the bottom bar of the shopping cart. His hands are clutching the handle/bar of the shopping cart. He is smiling. (Kinda like a kid jumping on a shopping cart after a running start. Kinda! :b ) ***Lu-Lu*** Lu-Lu is a steel shopping cart that is dented in the side and front. It has a red ribbon tied around the front cage of the cart into a bow tie. The front right wheel is loose as it squeks and wobbles as it is pushed. ****Back Ground***** Kid Krazie while walking out from his neighborhood Randall's grocery store slips in a puddle causing him to crash down to the concrete ground. Upon waking from his state of unconciousness it is noticed he has experienced some head trauma. As he walks from Randall's that fatefull afternoon with amnesia he sees a dented shopping cart sitting next to Randall's dumpster. He begins talking with this shopping cart as he finds out she is 28 years old and her name is Lu-Lu. She is originally from Kroger Island and she stands approximately 3 feet tall weighing 11 lbs. of soaking wet steel. They walk out of Randall's parking lot together that fatefull day man and cart together at last. His past is clouded in confusion after the fall he took. Maybe some day his memory will be back but until then don't look at his past look at the present and maybe even a little of the future!
