Xavier Kannon's Bachelor Party Written: 28th June 2002 ************ We cut to the outside of a bar, somewhere in Lexington, Kentucky. “Last Night” appears in the bottom right hand side of the screen. Suddenly bursting onto the screen in a daze of bright woven fabrics is current PURE interviewer and former PCW jobber, Jeremy Johnson. His shirt today would have Steven Smith weep... mainly because it would hurt the homosexual OSW announcer’s eyes that much. JJ: I GET TO GO TO A REAL BAR! Alexander Miyagawa dressed as closely to Television’s Angel as possible walks onto the screen, along with Syko, dressed in what appears to be an umpalumpa suit meeting Star Trek... yes... he is wearing a complete silver ensemble. In fact, he looks rather neat and tidy compared to JJ, but still... very... very... very... did I say very? What? Very bright. Suddenly into the screen comes KREMMY~! Making his return to PCW television after a length absence in his rocket powered disabled wheelchair... Kremmy: KREMMY~! Miyagawa: Yes Kremmy, you get to see the strippers too. JJ turns to Miyagawa. JJ: Tell me you didn’t say that. Tell me you did not just say that. Miyagawa: Whoa, and to think had you used that gimmick in PCW, you might not have spent more time on your back than Jenna Jameson. Kremmy: KREMMY~! JJ frowns for a second and then remembers that he’s going to be watching woman’s breasts bounce up and down for the best part of an hour, with someone else’s money. JJ: BREASTS! I’M GOING TO SEE REAL BREASTS! Miyagawa: Now just remember, if Natalia asks, we went to a quite wine bar. There were no women of any type. Syko: So it was a gay bar? Get JJ to tell her, then she’ll believe you. JJ looks at Syko annoyed. JJ: What did you mean to imply by that. Syko points at the large rucksack with JJ Bear poking out of the top of it that JJ has on his back. Syko: Perhaps that you are a homosexual? JJ: Right, that’s it! WHAT COMES AFTER L?! Syko: I believe “Ow, my nose, you bastard!” comes after that. JJ: What? Syko jabs him in the nose, reeling JJ back in pain. JJ: Ow, my nose, you bastard! Syko: See, I told you. And so on this cold dark night in Kentucky, stand the wrestler, the jobber and the goblin... Kremmy: KREMMY~! Oh, and Kremmy too. And together these four men wait here for the man who is to be married... the scientologist. Miyagawa: Ok, let’s run over the main things to do tonight... JJ: SEE BREASTS! REAL BREASTS! Kremmy: KREMMY~! Miyagawa: Nonononono. JJ, Syko and Kremmy all look disappointed and Miyagawa picks up on that. Miyagawa: We are going to see breasts, just that’s a side effect. JJ: BREASTS! REAL BREASTS! Kremmy: KREMMY~! Syko: Bah, we all know you prefer el cocko more than the breasts. JJ points at Syko threateningly. Syko: Did they teach you that in gay school? JJ: Look shut up, or else I WILL set JJ Bear on you. Syko: What a threat. I feel totally put in my place now. Miyagawa: coughs gently, returning all attention to him. Miyagawa: I have all three of your fake ID’s. Unless you don’t want to see the breasts, I would be quiet. Suddenly silence ensues from the other members of the “weird clique”. Miyagawa: Ok. I have set in motion a plan. A great plan. A great humiliating plan. A massively humiliating great plan. A totally... Kremmy: KREMMY~! Miyagawa: Indeed Kremmy. This plan will totally humiliate Xavier Kannon and will prove that one thing once and for all... at the end of the night Xavier Kannon will go home and think to himself... my word... I was humiliated... and you know why? Because... Miyagawa’s... Kremmy: KREMMY~! Miyagawa: Ok, Kremmy too... Excellent. JJ: So what’s the plan? Miyagawa: First of all we meet Mr Kannon outside of the strip bar. Then we start on... the plan. JJ: But what’s the plan. Miyagawa: The plan is on a need to know basis. Your roles are simply to watch breasts all evening and drink beer. Syko: I like this plan. JJ: Me too. Syko: No you don’t, you want to go to that Gay bar we passed earlier. JJ: I warned you before, and now I am going to kick your frigging ass. Miyagawa coughs quietly, but no one notices. He coughs again. Nothing. He slams JJ’s and Syko’s heads together and suddenly he has their attention. Miyagawa: Remember the ID situation people. Now onwards, to the strip bar. Syko: And the breasts! Kremmy: KREMMY~! And we see one teen idol, one Japo-American, one goblin and one KREMMY~! Walk/drive off down the street towards a strip bar and of course... the breasts. KREMMY: KREMMY~! **************** The cut into what appears to be a red hallway where No Doubt’s “Hey Baby” is playing over quite a good intercom system. Miyagawa seems to be leading the group of five down the corridor with Xavier Kannon right behind him wearing pretty much the same as Miyagawa except for what appears to be a white t-shirt with “Soon to be Married” written on the front in massive black text. A young woman walks up towards Miyagawa... not wearing much (the woman, jackass, the woman. NOT MIYAGAWA. Kapiche?) Woman: Hi there. Miyagawa: Hi there. I’m famous. My friend here is also famous. In fact, we’re all famous. So thus we would like to sample your most overpriced cocktails and then perhaps we could get some lapdancers for my good friend Mr. Kannon here. Miyagawa slaps Xavier on the chest, who is actually looking quite impressed with the set up. The five get escorted through to a large open seating arrangement where they can easily watch the girl stripping on the stage. They all sit down and JJ takes JJ Bear out of his bag and sits him next to him on the leather couch. Syko peers at the woman taking her bra off on stage. Syko: Hey JJ, is that your sister. JJ looks at him and suddenly stamps on Syko’s foot. Syko: Hey! And then pokes him in the eye. Syko: OW! JJ: I warned you. You kept pushing me, and I kept warning. That was a step too far. The lady comes back with a plethora of cocktails and puts them down on the table in front of the boys. Miyagawa leans forward holding out a credit card. Miyagawa: Charge everything on this. It’s my shout boys, anything you like, and charge it all to that. Pretty Much Everyone around that table: YEAH!!!!! Kremmy: KREMMY~! Miyagawa: And lets get say five jugs of beer as well, and get us some women! We’re famous don’t you know. Woman: Actually I do recognise you from somewhere... but I can’t place you... and the soon to be wedded as well... Xavier: I’m Gold Patron Meritorious Xavie... Miyagawa: My name is Rhett Locke. This is... Xavier: Hey, what do you mean Rhett Lo... Miyagawa leans over and whispers quietly in Kannon’s ear which the camera just picks up, but the young lady doesn’t. Miyagawa (whispering): Would you rather the headlines read Rhett Locke in Strip bar fiasco or Xavier Kannon in Strip Bar fiasco? Kannon nods in agreement. Kannon: Sorry Rhett, I was like... fighting earlier and got a bump on my head. I’m actually Sebastian Toomes. My friends here are **points to Syko** Corg, **points to JJ** Kid Wonder and of course my long time compatriate **points to Kremmy** Meanstreak. Miyagawa: Meanstreak? Kremmy: KREMMY~! Miyagawa: Erm... yes... he’s still bitter because he lost the first PCW Title to Kremmen... thus... Kremmy... of course... JJ (interrupting): Do we get to see breasts yet? Kremmy: KREMMY~! Miyagawa starts drinking some sort of cocktail. Syko: Actually it’s amusing why Meanstreak over there thinks of Kremmen when breasts are mentioned. Usually when I think of breasts I think of Jon Hull. Miyagawa spits out half the drink at the joke while JJ looks at Syko. JJ: You think of men when you see breasts? And I’m meant to be the gay one? Several underdressed dancers make their way over towards the table which receives some rather heavy watching from those at the table. Kremmy: KREMMY~! Especially Kremmy. And we leave our five-some of Rhett Locke... Sebastian Toomes... Corg... Kid Wonder... and Meanstreak for the moment, but soon to return. ********* We return to the strip bar where Syko is up dancing with a woman and Kremmy is getting a lap dance from a young Asian lady. Kremmy: KREMMY~! KREMMY~! KREMMY~! Miyagawa: Sheesh... go get a room already. Suddenly an extremely heavy breasted woman walks up to the three seated men who all love up her and then at both of them. Suddenly she looks at the large teddy bear sitting next to JJ. Woman: Wow, what a cute bear. She leans over JJ’s face to stroke the top of the bear, placing her breasts in JJ’s face who remains quite silent. Kremmy: KREMMY~! The woman pulls back and picks up the bear and moves it’s head near her ear as if it’s talking to her. Woman: Apparently I’ve got to take a Kid Wonder to a private room for a private dance. She reaches out her hand and JJ sits there doing nothing. Xavier: Yo, Kid Wonder **smacks JJ around the back of the head** That’s you dumbass. JJ almost leaps to his feet and is virtually pushing the heavily breasted woman along so he and JJ Bear can get a private dance. Miyagawa: Don’t worry Sebastian, I’ve already booked you a private dance. Xavier: Really Rhett, who have I got. Miyagawa: Oh... someone you’ll recognise. Silence hits for a brief second. Xavier: I don’t want your mail order slag of a wife to strip for me. Pregnant women just really don’t do it for me. Miyagawa: Funny. Although Ellie did offer to take her top off for me earlier for ten bucks. I turned her down. Both men continue smiling at the woman around them. Xavier: Well Rhett, you keep talking like that and you won’t remember the rest of the night through having a concussion. Miyagawa: Now, now, Sebastian. There’s no need for violence. Yet. Let’s save that for the family and friends brawl at the wedding shall we. Xavier: If you make it to the wedding Rhett, of course. Miyagawa: I’m the best man, Sebastian. Of course I’ll make it to the wedding. Why wouldn’t I? Xavier: That would be telling Rhett, that would be telling. Both Kannon and Miya smile and wave at Syko having fun with three women on the stage... and a bottle of baby oil... and a feather boa. A young lady partially dressed walks over to the duo still sitting on the couch. Lady: Hi there, we have a special dance for a Xavier Kannon. Is that either of you? Miyagawa: That’s my friend here. I booked it earlier under the name Tyler Nelson. Lady: Ok, thanks. If you would like to come this way sir. Xavier gets up. Miyagawa: Kannon, don’t forget your wallet. Xavier: Huh? Miyagawa hands Kannon’s wallet back to Kannon. Xavier: What are you doing with my wallet. Lady: This way please. Xavier looks at Miyagawa suspiciously and shoves the wallet into his pocket before being led away by the girl. Miyagawa (in hushed tones): What’s up Xavier? You thought that was MY credit card earlier? ********************* We return to the strip club where Jeremy Johnson is standing by the bar looking pretty dishelved... and smoking a cigar. Miyagawa walks over to him looking at him a little warily. Miyagawa: You ok man? JJ doesn’t reply, but instead he removes the cigar from his mouth and blows a perfect smoke ring before replacing the cigar back in his mouth. Miyagawa: Erm... right. We’re just about ready to get going... they’re closing up tonight. We’re just waiting for Kannon’s “Special” dance to finish. JJ nods at him. We cut into a small white room where Xavier Kannon is sitting on a single chair. “Rock DJ” by Robbie Williams begins to play over a small speaker and out from a door appears a fair maiden, dressed as a middle eastern dancer with a covering over her face to disguise her. We cut to back to the bar once again where Miyagawa, JJ, Syko and Kremmy are all standing. Miyagawa: Shouldn’t be much longer now. Syko: What shouldn’t? Miyagawa: The screaming. The running. Shouldn’t be much longer now. Syko: Huh? The camera cuts back inside the small white room where the fair maiden is down to simply the face covering, the bra and the panties. The camera cuts behind the dancer as she removes the bra and Kannon is smiling with fun. Xavier: I was dragged here by a man who I think of pretty much as my enemy. And I’ve got to admit... I’m having a lot of fun. I expected to really HATE this. I’ve got to see your face.
