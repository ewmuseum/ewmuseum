Manuel Labor Biography Real Name Manuel Labor Nicknames Manny, Manuel Hometown Tijuana Mexico Birthday 5/11/78 Height 5,6 Weight 160 lbs Biography Manuel was raised by his parents in Tijuana, Mexico with one goal in mind, to become a pro wrestler. His father throughout the years had become a cult figure in South America for his daredevil moves and laid back 'Fonz' like attitude. Manuel wanted this and his parents wanted it for him. His father though did not live to see Manuel grow past his teens. In the biggest match of his career, for the Jnr Heavyweight Title his opponent screwed up big style. This was the belt that allowed the holder a ticket to PURE, the syndicated fed of American Television. This was going to be Manuels Fathers big chance but instead looking up at the lights that night he breathed his last breath. Manuel was in the crowd that night and the image haunted him for many years. So much so that he could not even stay in Mexico, he moved to America and as he had no qualifications got the only job he could. Manuel Labor, the son of the Lucha Legend was a janitor! A lowly janitor working for a company who had various contracts all across America. As coincidence would have it Manuel was handed the contract for PCW. On his first night there Manuel was unable to work, he sat outside in his car looking at the fans and the wrestlers, thinking about his father. As if a lightining bolt had struck him though the next day, Manuel was in the gym before work, at work he was studying the wrestlers and began to realise his potential that his father had seen in him so many years before. Labor just wants his chance, a chance to prove himself, to make himself a household name and most importantly a chance to honour the one man he respects the most, his father. All this though is but a pipe dream just now as he is still only a PCW janitor, dissed by the likes of Walden and Skitzo. One day though the Lucha Legend will get his shot and the PCW cruiserweight division better be ready for some hard work. DESCRIPTION Manuel has a short black crew cut, with a 12 o'clack shadow (stubble). He is not full of muscles but he is quite well built from endless hours of scrubbing floors. As attire he usually wears a pair of faded Blue jeans and Nike sneakers. His t-shirt is black with red writing, on the front it says 'HARD WORK ?' but on the back it says ' NO MANUEL LABOR!' The red shirt is sleeveless and the trainers are black. Other Feds none Other Fed Awards none Quotes Code 147, serious liquid spillage!
