Sabotage 4 1 Apr 2001 The Raleigh Sports and Entertainment Complex, Raleigh, North Carolina Moby's Machete and the highlight reel The techno sounds of Moby's "Machete" play in the background as the opening highlight package is shown. Our first shot is that of Chet King capturing his second title in PCW by winning the PCW Shazbot Crusierweight Title. Next, we see Tyler Nelson nailing one of the Phantom Phive with the TV title belt leading to a Media victory. That's followed up by the first PCW appearance by Bo Raider who went on to punk out Tyler Nelson. We watch Meanstreak and Slade battle in their match from Friday.
