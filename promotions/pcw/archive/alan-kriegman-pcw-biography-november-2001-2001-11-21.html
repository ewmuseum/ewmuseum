Alan Kriegman — PCW biography (November 2001)

21 November 2001

Alan Kriegman Real Name: Alan Kriegman Nick Name: Evil Supergenius Catchphase: "Evil Rules.", "Muahahahahaha..." Character Handler: Dan P. MSN Account: [detail withheld] AIM Address: [detail withheld] Theme Song: "Sympathy for the Devil" as performed by Guns'n'Roses Age: 37 From: Pleasantville, New Hampshire Height: 6'2" Weight: 230 lbs Wrestling Style: Martial Artist. Signature Moves: The Kriegafix (Crucifix Powerbomb), Superkick, Cheating, Use of Gadgets, Various Martial Arts Kicks, Figure Four Leglock. Finisher: Getting MediEVIL Finisher Description: Crippler Crossface Secondary Finisher: The End Secondary Finisher Description: Cradle DDT Arch Nemesis: Ed. Valet: Barbara Bush-Kriegman, his Wife. Weapon of Choice: The PalmPilot...OF DEATH~!!! Enemies, Rivals: THE Multiple Man, Professor Herbert D. Dorkiman, Ed, Trashcan Man. Friends, Associates: Dillan Meyers, "The Scholar" Howard Porter, Six Finger Cisco (PWA), Frisco. Current Team: The First Couple of PCW w/Barbara Bush-Kriegman. Past Teams & Groups: Goovil w/Dillan Meyers. The Think Tank w/"The Scholar" Howard Porter, Professor Herbert D. Dorkiman, and Nick Taylor. The Phantom Phive w/The Phantom Booker, LACKoSTRESS, Scott Naket, and Seraph. Think Tank: The Next Generation w/"The Scholar" Howard Porter, Professor Herbert D. Dorkiman, and "The Noble" Wellington Littlebrook. Old School w/The Reverend, Ed, Trashcan Man, Miyagawa, and Brock Newbludd. Title History: PCW Continental Champion (2-Time). PCW Tag Team Champion w/Dillan Meyers. PCW World Champion. Awards and Honors: First-Ever PCW Continental Champion. First-Ever PCW Tag Team Champion w/Dillan Meyers. PCW Hall of Famer. Former PCW President. The Official Greatest Tag Team in PCW History w/Dillan Meyers. Winner of Fedwars 4-Corners Match against Kremmen (PCW), Big Alan Steele (CCW), and Reeco Suave(ICWO). The First Couple of PCW w/Barbara Bush-Kriegman. Only World Champion in PCW History to defend the title at Consecutive PPVs. Description: Always sporting stylish, italian made, evil-looking black suits even when he's wrestling, Alan Kriegman dresses to kill. With his hair slicked back to show off his forehead (he refuses to admit his hairline is slowly receding), a black goatee, and a sinister grin, he is the epitome of what an evil super genius should look like. History: Who is Alan Kriegman? A flamboyant but impeccably groomed evil supergenius, who seems more at home as the bad guy in a spy movie than in the wrestling business. His appearance holds true to his lifestyle though, as he is the retired (at the ripe age of 35) founder of an international "special interests" group, whose interests (according to Kriegman) are so special, that if he told you, he'd have to kill you. The Past When the group was brought down by U.S. special agent, and long-time rival, Kennedy Smith, Kriegman's career in attempting world-domination was suddenly cut short. So what did he do? He retired from his industry and decided to live-out the American dream. He bought a house in a suburban neighborhood, complete white-picket fencing and kids playing ball in the street. While his suburban life with longtime partner Barbara Bush-Kriegman (his wife) was fulfilling, something was missing. He missed the action of his former life, the violence, the destruction. Living in the suburbs provided many interesting opportunities to terrorize his neighborhood, it simply wasn't the same. Whether he was switching neighborhood children's cartoon tapes and comic books with pornography, secretly killing and stuffing his neighbor's annoying pets, or holding barbeques that ended with firey tragedy, Alan Kriegman was the evil king of his neighborhood. He then realized that all along, during his days of trying to take over the world, that it wasn't world-domination he was after, it was the chase. After every failed attempt, he kept trying because he loved playing the game. Being the undisputed winner (to him) is no fun because there is no excitement when you know you cant be beaten. It was after having this epiphany that he discovered the one lifestyle that fitted his needs. Physical competition, more specifically, violent physical competition. But violent competition of what kind? Martial arts was out of the question, as he is a grand master in several forms. Ultimate Fighting wasn't an option, as it was too obscure. Pit fighting? Too uncivilized. Finally, one day, he considered pro wrestling. It was perfect for him! He could express himself physically in the ring, and with his super-slick...super-evil personality he could express himself on the mic! Using his house in suburbia as base of operations for his mission to take over the world of pro- wrestling, he started to collect title belts. One federation at a time, he'd join, beat everyone on the roster (with the help of his wife who'd serve as his manager), suck the life out of the organization, and move on to the next company. Then he found PCW. It begins... Alan Kriegman was one of the first wrestlers signed by PCW. Early in his career he feuded with the somewhat crazed champion of good, Dillan Meyers. Later, he would go on to join forces with Meyers to form the most dominating tag team in PCW history in Goovil. At PCW Dawning, he and Dillan won the finals of a tournament to crown the first-ever PCW Tag Team Champions. The Think Tank Later that night at PCW Dawning, with the help of PCW President Professor Herbert D. Dorkiman and Nick Taylor, he would win a four-corners cage match to become the first-ever PCW Continental Champion. After winning two titles in one night, Alan Kriegman joined forces with "The Scholar" Howard Porter, PHD, and Nick Taylor to form The Think Tank. In typical Kriegman fashion, he turned on his super hero cohort soon afterwards. Birth of a Rivalry. The birth of The Think Tank brought about what is considered to be one of he greatest rivalries in PCW history. To promote stable unity, Kriegman moved his genius companions into his home in Pleasantville. What he did not consider, though, is the defiance that Think Tank would meet after their rise to power. At the time, The Think Tank was in control, unchallanged...that is, until the return of a Hardcore Legend by the name of Ed. Ed took it upon himself to bring down Alan Kriegman and The Think Tank's reign of terror over PCW. But he couldn't do it alone. Along with Troy Brown, The Chef, and Two Ton, Team International, the one challenge to The Think Tank's throne, was born. The War Next Door The Think Tank vs Team International is known to many as "The War Next Door". In order to fight the Kriegman-led Think Tank, the Ed-led Team International moved in righ next door to the Kriegman residence. The War Next Door had begun! Team International waged war with food products, while The Think Tank relied on their intellects and technology to fight back. TTT got sniped with M&Ms, bombarded with cans of Dr. Pepper, drenched in paint, and suffered many other forms of humiliation. In return, Alan Kriegman led The Think Tank into battle as they raided the TI residence on numerous occasions, hired Emril to go after The Chef and Bertha, nearly leveled the TI house by causing Two Ton to fall through the roof, and waged war in numerous other fashions. Alan Kriegman even successfully managed to infiltrate Team International HQ on night and steal "Bob" (Ed's imfamous barbed-wire nail-studded chair of destruction). The War Next Door would go on for several weeks, until it was brought to an end, not with a bang, but a whimper. In one of his many evil schemes, Alan Kriegman hired Doug Troy to plant a colony of deviously hungry African ants in Ed's front yard. Days later, the Team International household was reduced to shambles, and TI was forced to pull out. The Think Tank was victorious, but the rivalry between Alan Kriegman and Ed sill goes on to this day. Alan Kriegman coninued his diabolical ways in PCW, becoming a two-time Continental Champion and feuding with the likes of Stewart Hix, his good twin Frisco, Scott Naket, and Jon Kano along the way. The End...? Once Media took over PCW, Alan Kriegman's services were no longer needed. He was fired. The fans, who claimed to hate to love Kriegman, were outraged. Due to a massive letter writing and streaker campaign, Kriegman was directly placed into the PCW Hall of Fame. The Phantom Phive Not satisfied, Kriegman would later team up with his nemesis, The Phantom Booker, to plot the "re-taking-over" of PCW from Media. One can only believe that his evil mind was behind the plot to get himself named President of PCW during the Phantom Booker's attempt to retake PCW. Think Tank: The Next Generation It was during Alan Kriegman's reign as PCW President when the evil supergenius hatched what his most diabolical scheme ever. It began with Alan setting himself up to be "fired" from PCW a second time. Using his cunning genius, Alan Kriegman not only got himself, the PCW President "fired", but PCW's Commissioner Scott Naket as well. With these two positions vacant, this left them open to be claimed by PCW's original President and Commissioner, Professor Herbert D. Dorkiman and "The Scholar" Howard Porter respectively. With the old Think Tank regime back in command, PHD and Porter re-hired the evil supergenius. With Professor Herbert D. Dorkinan as President, "The Scholar" Howard Porter as Commissioner, "The Noble" Wellington Littlebrook's riches, and Alan Kriegman as the mastermind and leader, The Think Tank was born anew. Think Tank: The Next Generation lived! Evil Super Champion Shortly after his return, a match was booked. Alan Kriegman and "The Scholar" Howard Porter vs PCW World Champion Stewart Hix and Slade. Earlier that night, the match was changed by CEO Tyler Nelson into a 4-Corners match for the PCW World Championship. It was a long, violent battle. Slade turned on his stablemate Stewart Hix, and Kriegman even broke a hold that would have won Howard Porter the title. In the end, it was Alan Kriegman who emerged the winner, and new PCW World Champion! It was his finest hour, his moment of glory. The PCW World Championship was his, the fans were roaring, and his enemies had been smited. Alan Kriegman was the King of PCW. End of a Friendship Kriegman's moment of glory only lasted for so long. Moments following his victory, his long time friend "The Scholar" Howard Porter turned on him in a jealous rage. Later that month at PCW Supremacy, Kriegman successfully defended his World Title against Porter in a match refereed by their stablemate Professor Herbert D. Dorkiman. Following the match, PHD shocked the world when he turned on Porter, ending another long-term friendship. Alan Kriegman was still PCW World Champion, but at what cost? Think Tank: The Next Generation was no more. Dark Age Alan Kriegman's reign as Evil Super Champion ended after 30 days, at the hands of the PCW-2 World Champion, Stryfe. Throughout the entire match, Kriegman was utterly dominant, proving his worthiness as World Champion. It was not until the intervention by Clyde, Darkstar, and Vampir Nosferatu that Stryfe was able to stop the superior Kriegman's momentum. The encounter almost ended Kriegman's very life, after being thrown off the top of the Jumbotron by Clyde. Barely able to stand on his own two feet, Stryfe was thrown atop the fallen Kriegman by his new counterparts. Three seconds later, there was a new PCW World Champion, Stryfe. PCW's "Dark Age" had begun. Vengance For several days following his first brush with Dark Age, Alan Kriegman was comatose, lying brain dead in a hospital bed. Somehow, he managed to pull out, and immediately undertook the task of getting back at Dark Age. In his return match against Clyde, Kriegman managed to make DA's leader tap out, something which had been considered impossible until then. His war on the satanic jobbers continued, forcing Stryfe to submit in the famous "World Champions Match" at the PCW Dream Card. Continuing his crusade, Kriegman fought a bitter rivalry against Vampir Nosferatu, scoring multiple wins over the vampire in the process. To this day, Kriegman credits himself for destroying the credibility that Dark Age strived to gain for themselves. Successful in his battle against the group who had wronged him so, Alan Kriegman moved on. Goovil: The Greatest Tag Team in PCW History In the weeks following his war with Dark Age, Alan Kriegman decided the time was right for him to regain the place in the spotlight he once held. He and Dillan Meyers, who had long since buried the hatchet and became best friends, won the right to call themselves "The Official Greatest Tag Team in PCW History", defeating The Blood Brothers, Da Westsidaz, and The Media Assassins in a 4-Corners TLC Match for the official title. The question of whether Goovil or The Blood Brothers was the better team had been answered. Goovil was the greatest tag team in PCW history. Treading Water Though he had scored multiple big wins since losing the World Championship, there was no dispute to the fact that Alan Kriegman was getting nowhere fast in PCW. He had come within inches of winning the Carnival of Carnage 2, but was eliminated after being buried under a pile of eliminated Russians. He nearly defeated Ivan Stanislav for the Continental Championship, but was denied victory due to Red Army interferance. He even suffered a massive upset at the hands of Russian jobber Nikita Morozov (again, thanks to Red Army interferance). In short, the evil supergenius was in a rut. This lackluster period came to an end when an opportunity presented itself, in the form of "Old School". Back to Old School When 5 of the biggest names in PCW history formed together in a stable appropriately named Old School, it seemed only right that Alan Kriegman, who had been in PCW since it's birth, join their ranks. And so he did. Following a win over Vampir Nosferatu and John Jupiter, Kriegman was saved from a post-match attack at the hands of Dark Age by Old School, and was asked to join the group. Naturally, he accepted. A few weeks later at PCW Back to Old School, Alan Kriegman turned on his stablemate Trashcan Man, costing him the PCW World Championship! A Return to Normalcy Since his return to heelhood, Alan Kriegman has gone back to his old, dastardly ways. If you ask him who is responsible for the destruction and disbanding of Old School, you're more than likely to hear him say "Me". He possibly is, too. Trashcan Man has disappeared since Kriegman cost him the title, and following a singles defeat at the hands of Kriegman, The Reverend has once again gone into retirement. With Old School out of the way, Alan now finds himself in the middle of a rivalry with the enigmatic THE Multiple Man, possibly the only man on the roster who poses a threat to Kriegman. "But what's puzzlin' you is the nature of my game..." More than anything else, Alan Kriegman loves the spotlight. He's turned face for it, turned heel for it, and even turned on his friends for it. Wherever chances for attention, glory, and the limelight are, you'll find the Evil Supergenius. A PCW Legend, Alan Kriegman always has us guessing. What does he have in store for us next? Only time will tell...
