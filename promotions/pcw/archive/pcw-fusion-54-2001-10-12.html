Fusion 54 12 Oct 2001 The Auschwitz Auditorium, Auschwitz, Germany Clyde and the Auschwitz Museum The scene opens as black and white footage of the Nazi leader Adolph Hitler and of the German SS before shifting to Nazi soldiers rounding up Jews and putting them on trains flash across the screen. The sounds of what appear to be an old movie projector can be heard from off camera. Shots of lines of Jews wearing huge, yellow stars on their clothing to signify their ethnic and religious beliefs are shown being corralled into camps and of men, women and children being tattooed with crude and barbaric identification numbers. The black and white footage continues with the hopeless and helpless faces of almost skeletal concentration camp detainees shooting across the screen in rapid fire succession, before cutting away to the same emaciated victims first marching in line, then doing hard labor, before cutting away to several chilling shots of the nude and motionless bodies of the dead lying in mass graces. Focusing in one of the horrifically twisted faces of the dead, stark white words “The End” appear in the center of the screen. Suddenly, the screen goes black for a moment, just before it fades to whitish gray in a someone confusing moment. The camera begins panning back, finally revealing the situation, as it becomes apparent the images we just witnessed were not directly piped into the PCW telecast, but were those being projected onto a movie screen from the presentation theatre at the Auschwitz Museum, located inside of the Nazi run WWII Auschwitz concentration camp, in Auschwitz, Germany.
