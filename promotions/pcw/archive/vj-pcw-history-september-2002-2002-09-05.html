VJ — PCW history (September 2002)

5 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
History VJ grew up as your standard street tough in the slums of Boston, with most of his origin shrouded in mystery as he prefers to keep his past clandestine. This much IS known: After years of scrounging for food and battling to survive, he discovered a small pro wrestling school across town and began to train there, in addition to his usual hobby of weight training and attempting to further his education through reading and self-motivated study. Wanting out of the street lifestyle and fueled by hate for the elitists of society who had held him down for so long, VJ headed for Japan and broke into the wrestling scene. For years he fought some of the most insane individuals in the most brutal matches ever conceived before finally packing up and returning to America in some smaller independent organizations. Eventually he signed onto rising promotion the WWIWF and soon after the CIWF. He eventually rose to stardom and held every title in both organizations at one time or another before heading to even greener pastures. To this day, wrestling is his life....no romance and no other occupation. He is determined to prove he's the best and rise above his beginnings, and ultimately crush those who he best believes epitomize the 'social aristocrats' who persecuted he and his peers.
