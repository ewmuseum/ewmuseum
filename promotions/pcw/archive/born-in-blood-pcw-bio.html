Born in Blood Biography Biography Both Christian and Ethan were born in Midland, Texas a mere two months apart. Their father, Donny McCain, was a arrogant asshole who chose to leave Christians mother while she was pregnant for the girl he had been dating for longer and was also pregnant. Ethan's mother, Dianna, was a rich mans daughter and therefor Donny decided that it was better for him and the dreams he had to stay with the rich one. The lives of these two boys couldnt have been any different. Ethan grew up the spoiled kid, typically, as his parents had plenty of money. Both from his mother being from a rich family and from the fact that his father, asshole that he is, was also a very good business man who used the money his wifes family fronted him to gain a good finacial business. As Ethan grew up he became a very fine athlete, especially in wrestling, and his father pushed him very hard to uphold the family name. Meanwhile Christian grew up very different. His mother loved him but often had to work two jobs just to make the ends meet. Everything they got they got through hard work and sacrifice. Along the way Christian also became a very fine athlete and as fate would have it, an excellent wrestler. The two boys wound up on the same teams, of course, but never got along. They both dreamed of one day becoming professional wrestlers. After they finished with their schooling and went out into the independants they gave it everything they had to move up. Nothing seemed to be getting them anywhere. Then one night at a small show in Dallas fate would play a role in the boys lives once again. The small town promoter decided to place them as a team for that nights show. Despite not liking the idea the boys displayed a chemistry that hadnt been seen in that region in a while. After the match the promoter decided that they would team for good, as it was the only way they would ever get over as wrestlers and make it in the big leagues. They balked at first because neither liked the idea of relying on the other one. Finally they decided that to get their feet in the door and have a chance to succeed and live thier dreams they would have to learn to trust one another and get along. In the ring anyway....
