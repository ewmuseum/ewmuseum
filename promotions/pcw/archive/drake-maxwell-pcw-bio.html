Drake's main remembrance of his childhood was his parent's murder/suicide, which is very much detailed during any of his promotional videos. Through out his life, Drake lived the hard life. Without his mother or father, Drake fended off of his grandparents and found himself caught up in violence for most of his life. Ever since the beatings he received as a child, Drake has taken out all of the anger and frustrations onto his opponents. Since high school, Drake has been entrapped in a web of controversy and pain. He has had some relationships before, but only one happened to stay during it all, Amy Falan stayed during it all, his newfound quest, wrestling, the works. Drake found wrestling during another one of his usual pit fightings, his manager at the time trained him for the dangers of fighting one on one with the dirtiest players in the game. Drake had his amazing abilities and showed them off during these fights. Soon Drake was receiving telephone calls from wrestling promoters all across the world. He only accepted one, Primetime Wrestling Academy. Drake flawlessly went through the PWA with ease, he went through the Academy for 5 weeks gnarring high grades and only going through one match in his entire time. A few days after the match, Drake ended up graduating from the PWA and into one of Primetime Central's prominent feds: NTICW. Drake, who by this time has come up alot with his style of innovation took NTICW by storm finding himself within the finals of the Tower of Doom World title Match at Halloween Bash 2k1. Drake's own selfishness caused him to take a nasty fall 20 feet to the ground below knocking him unconscious, making him 3rd in the entire proceedings behind The Tormentor and the eventual World Champion- Xavier Kannon. Drake took no more of the tolerance of the broken and wasteless and began a reign of attacks proclaiming himself 'The Avenging Angel', Drake took his attacks to the extreme including a usage of weapons such as kendo sticks, cars, and even piercing units! Drake ended up finding himself embroiled into a feud with the World Champion and Main Eventing the latest NTICW PPV-Redemption. Drake took maybe the hardest road to the title, even wrestling for a grueling 45 minutes in a Redemption Tag Match ending up second behind Kannon once again. Fed up with Kannon victories, Drake found himself on the victory side, winning the NTICW World title, a title he held for 1 week 2 days. Minutes into the dawn of Monday, the news broke out to the wrestling community- NTICW had been shut down. Siezing all titles in a mass raid of the now condemed NTICW Plaza, PCW Executive Producers wondered what to do with the now defunct title belts and the best thing that they came up with was simply: burn them. And on December 11,2001- The NTICW titles were burned by Tyler Nelson on Tuesday Turmoil ending some slim hope of NTICW reform.Outraging some members of NTICW, Drake Maxwell saw this at home in Detroit looking onward at his television screen. Soon a rage befited him and he then just drove out of Detroit and towards the next PCW venues insearch of maybe landing a contract. He got one December 14th 2001 just a month and exact day after his 20th birthday, this was his Christmas present towards himself and now he found himself back into a wall lead by the general of his NTICW pain: Xavier Kannon. Although it only happened once inside of PCW, Drake didn't pay attention that much to Kannon and found himself riding amongst the dark ranks of PCW, although falling short in some places he brought it right back up with victory, including one over Jeremy Howard - the PCW World Champion. This was his test and he passed, now going through the higher ranks every other match like butter on toast. Drake got this surreal spot he once thought he would never happen to get, and now he opens to continue writing his stories in the book of history and the walls of fate while breaking the dreams of his opposition...
