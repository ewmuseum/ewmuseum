Joe Mac Biography Real Name Joseph Mac Nicknames The Real American Hero, The Isolator. Hometown Parris Island, South Carolina Birthday 01/24/1975 Height 6 ft Weight 220 lbs Biography Joe Mac, the Marine, was born in Parris Island, South Carolina. When he was a year old his family started to move from base to base because he was serving in the military. While moving around he was taught Discipline, Respect, Honor, Courage, Commitment, and Combat Training. When he turned 18 he joined the United States Marine Corps and climbed through the ranks and joined Marine Special Forces Recon. After 10 years of service he retired from the Corps and joined the DWF. After working in the confines of the DWF for almost three trimesters of a year, he has seen and done much in the wrestling world including the federations of the IWF, ECFed, and MWA. He has come in contact with some of the best and the worse and destroyed them all, but now it is time for the very best, and a chance to imbrace in the glory of combat once again. Other Feds DWF, IWF, ECFed, LWF, MWA Quotes 'I will Awaken you to your Weakness.' 'I am one Sexy Beast.' 'I am absolutley Flawless.'
