"The Darkness" Akiyama Real Name: Unknown Nick Name: The Darkness, The Voltage Fighter, The Neo Void God, NVG Catchphase: "Screw that." "I am the Absolute Destiny Apocalypse" "Fighting against me is like f^cking for virginity!" "Do you know what I hate more than anything in this world? It's that---!" "I am the darkness that you hold close to your heart." "I'm Akiyama...Aki--frickin--yama." "I have no name...call me No Name if you wish." "Shonomama shine!!!" (Japanese= Die as you are!!!) Character Handler: Akira MSN Account: [detail withheld] AIM Address: [detail withheld] Theme Song: "High on your Own Supply" by Apollo 4-40 Age: Unknown From: Japan Height: 6'10 Weight: 369 lbs ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ ***Manager*** Name: Omina Height: 5'9 Weight: 129 lbs. Age: Unknown Description: Coming Soon ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ Wrestling Style: Kuro-Doragon-Waza (Akiyama's own martial art style developed by himself), along with mastered skills in technical, hardcore, powerhouse, and aerial. His fighting stances include both the drunken boxing stance, and a slouching stance where his arms lower and a glazed look enters his eyes. This one is called Void Trance, and he tends to go out of control in this stance, blazing a trail for pain with blinding punches and kicks. Signature Moves: -Error of Cyclonication: Dragon Screw into a Spinning Toe Hold -Cold Fusion: A spiraling Emerald Fusion. -Dark Waterfall: A springboard Samoan Drop used consistently as a counter move. -Neo Gothic Headbutt: A corkscrew headbutt available for use within two positions; the first from the top rope and the second from a standing position to the midsection of the opponent. -Cresent Moon Knocker: Akiyama turns his back on his opponent, bends backwards, and locks his hand around their throat. Then he reels them forward as he flips back with a back kick to the area between their eyes. Capable of delivering to two opponents at once. -Neo Void Tribulation: A falling sitdown Chokeslam. Capable of delivering to two opponents at once. -Neo Void Wave: A Diving Senton -Neo Void-Sault: A aerial move to the outside where Akiyama bounces off the ropes, cartwheels forward, and then hits a corkscrew splash to the opponent outside. -Crucifixion of the Damned: A Samoan Drop performed from the top turnbuckle in a Neo-Gothic Wave(corkscrew swanton bomb) fashion. -Neo Void Hammer: A corkscrew springboard axe kick with a twist. -Eternal Vengeance: Multiple Dragon Suplexes followed by either a pinning Dragon Suplex or a releasing Dragon Suplex. -Guillotine's Fate: Akiyama sets up for the Scissors Kick, and delivers a kick to the throat simultaneously along with the kick to the back of the skull. -Trinity Bomb: Triple Powerbomb Combination -Vortex Drive: Gorilla Press into a Michinoku Driver. -360 Suplex: Akiyama sets victim up for a powerbomb, lifts, but when opponent reaches peak of the lift, Akiyama tosses him over his head behind him, causing the victim to flip in the air and land onto his back. -Crash Landing: Flapjack into a Samoan Neckbreaker. - Akifixtion: Akiyama picks opponent up by his arms with their backs facing each other. From there Akiyama pulls outward on the opponent's arms, attempting to rip their arms from their sockets. -Cyclonica's Flight: Giant swing to the outside. (locks arms around opponents legs and swings around five times before releasing him, sending him flying over the ropes to the floor outside. -Divine Sword: Running boot to the face; the impact causes the opponent to flip backwards and land onto their face(Test's Big Boot fashion). -Path of the Righteous: "Old School" style walk on the ropes, except Akiyama goes from one turnbuckle to the next before executing a roundhouse kick to the victim's face. Fall of Darkness: A counter maneuver where Akiyama stoops down, catches oncoming attacker over his shoulder, stands back up, and slams victim headfirst into the mat (something like a High angle Spinebuster). Capable of delivering to two opponents at once. - Black Rainbow Smasher: A counter for when Akiyama is Irish whipped. Opponent whips Akiyama to the ropes, but Akiyama hops on the top rope, Moonsaults back at the opponent, grabs the opponent's head during the peak of the Moonsault, and delivers a Diving Reverse DDT. Capable of delivering to two opponents at once. - Aurora Akiyama: Akiyama flips off the top turnbuckle like he's performing a shooting star splash. After completing the backflip, he performs a Five Star Frog Splash onto the opponent (Shooting Five Star). Standard Non-trademark Moves: Front moves= Crossed Arms Powerbomb, Belly to Back Powerbomb, Argentine Pumphandle Backbreaker/Neckbreaker, Belly to Back Pumphandle Brainbuster Suplex, Body Press Forward Toss, Stalling Brainbuster DDT, Half Belly to Belly Suplex Back moves Half Nelson Suplex, Pumphandle Slam, High Angle Backdrop, Tiger Suplex Turnbuckle moves Top Rope Neo Void Tribulation, Super Fisherman DDT Aerial moves Diving Knee Strike, Top Rope Roundhouse Dropkick, 'Low Down' Frogsplash (call it the LDF), Elbow Dive to heart, Diving Fist to throat Running moves Flying Forearm Smash, Rotating Jumping Knee Strike, Running Reverse Thrust Kick, STO, Neckbreaker Drop Counter moves: Bearhug Front Slam, Reverse Armbar, Pulling Walk Slam, Backflip to Pancake, Standing Fireman Carry Double Team moves: Symphonic Harmony: Superkicks to adjacent sides of the victim's head (conchairto capabilities if performed with 4 people) Double Neo Void Tribulation Plummeting Nightmate: Akiyama takes associate and performs the Eletric Chair Drop onto a prone opponent. High Angle Backdrop: Associate grabs opponent like he's performing a backdrop, but Akiyama runs up and grabs both of them. Then he hoists them both up into the air and performs a double backdrop of sorts. Migrane's Birth: Akiyama performs a Super Camel Clutch onto victim, where associate(s) follows with repeated 'Tajiri' style kicks to the face. Finisher Move: Absolute Destiny Apocalypse - Akiyama takes his left arm and locks in a front headlock. From there, he reaches over with his right arm and underhooks the opponent's left arm. Then he lifts him into the air vertically while rotating the victim by pulling on his left arm until their faces are level with each other's chests. Finally, he swings the victim back down horizontally until the victim's head is between his knees, and then drives the back of the head into the mat with the force. (An elevated flowing Black Dragon Smasher, but please refraim from calling the move that :P). Can be preformed in a quick, fluid motion. Secondary Finisher Move: Akiyama Lock: Akiyama locks in a inverted reverse Full Nelson Submission hold from a standing position. Then he drops down with the move locked on in a neckbreaker fashion, converting it into a sitdown inverted reverse Full Nelson Submission hold. Set-Up Move: 1) Danse Macabre: A high angle super kick. 2) The Light of Darkness: Akiyama grabs opponent in a Tongan Death Grip, and releases the dark energy within him to electrocute his opponent's nerves until he passes out in a heap at his feet. Can be delivered to two opponents at once. Taunts: 1) Akiyama stands there and stares into his opponent. (done before the match) 2) Akiyama "poser" stance...growls low in Japanese. 3) Folds his arms over his chest and shakes his head slowly. 4) Holds his hand to his head and begins to laugh insanely. (Void Trance signal) 5) Holds palms together in praying fashion and bows head. (also done after victory) Weapons of Choice: Kendo Sticks of diffent varieties (flaming, razor wired, elecrified, steel plated, etc.) Current Team: Kakumei Kurayami (Revolutionary Darkness)...Akiyama, Itagaki Freeze, Kenji Hirose, Gaijin, Botan Otonachi... Past Teams & Groups: None Title History: PCW Extreme Champion (PCW Red October, Oct 21, 2001): Scaffold Match victory over Isawa Miyagawa, Edward, and Clyde Awards History: -Winning Soccer Riot Match @ Millenium Stadium, Cardiff, Wales Description: Akiyama's ring attire is a short-sleeved tight black see through mesh shirt, shiny black leather tights, and knee high boots of the same shiny depth. His hair is a colored mix of black and brown, unkempt and hanging over his face. A black and white bandana spans across his forehead like a headband. A black and dark blue facemask covers all the lower parts of his face. Black shades are often worn, though his dark black eyes are sometimes considered darker. Black tape stretches across his arms all the way to his elbows. History: The story of Akiyama is nothing sweet and pleasant. He was born around the same time as Inphino Blitz in a rivaling household of martial artists. It was his father that killed both of Inphino's parents so long ago. In an effort to eliminate the Black Dragon Clan in the future, Akiyama's father sent him off into the wilderness to train under a legendary Drunken Master. Only 4 years old at the time, he wandered through the forests only surviving on berries and any animal that he killed along the way. He did not find the drunken boxing master until he was ten years old. By this time, Akiyama was a savage animal that was strangely large for his size. He was already five foot one and one hundred and twenty five pounds when he was found thrashing around with a pack timber wolves. The master took in the child and trained him to harbor his feral side while organizing it with his Drunken Master Style. It wasn?t until Akiyama was sixteen when his master up and left him for dead after a sparring match. Akiyama left the wilderness and traveled into the city. It was there where he found the life of wrestling. He was already two hundred pounds and nearly six foot one at his age, and he was a shoe in for the career. He joined an Indy league of the Tokyo Wrestling League called the Z Training Grounds. Here, he learned to develop his patented Akiyama Lock among other moves devised from wrestling and drunken boxing. By the age of eighteen, he had earned the ZTG master?s title. He returned to the wilderness to show his master his accomplishments, and when they got into a heated debate, Akiyama killed him in the Akiyama Lock. Satisfied with his newest accomplishment and a new sense of entertainment, he left to return to the city. That?s when he met Inphino Blitz. He was leaving his dojo when he crossed his path. Akiyama looked at him and something within him told him that he was powerful. Akiyama wanted to destroy this man by all means. He looked down at the blood on his hands and erupted into a mighty roar! Akiyama charged at Inphino with full intention of killing him. Inphino saw him early and merely brought him down with a reverse armbar. That?s when all of this madness began. Blitz ended up in TWL along with Akiyama. They feuded for years on and off, with Blitz winning or a draw. Finally, Blitz left for unknown reasons, but Akiyama didn?t want him running off so easily. Wherever Blitz entered a fighting tournament, Akiyama was always right behind him. There was no escape from him in Akiyama?s eyes. Then, he overheard an argument between Inphino and some other guys calling themselves the Void Elements. Inphino left, and Akiyama took his opportunity. Inphino went on to PWA, and later PCW. Akiyama used his resources to track down the Void Elements, leading him all the way to the deserted Takazawa Castle. He was captured, beaten to near death, and brought before the Daughter of Darkness, Hinageshi. She glared down at him and saw something within him that very well may have been the same darkness that they all possessed. She trained him in the ways of the Void, before finally being baptized within the Void. When he came back from the darkness, he was more than man?he was proclaimed the Neo Void God, equipped with a Blood lust stance of insanity he slips into randomly during battle called the Void Trance. From there he plotted revenge upon Blitz and was moments before executing it, only to find him dead already. In a fit of rage, Akiyama slipped into the Void Trance and nearly destroyed a nearby Buddhist temple. Akiyama decided to start the Neo-Gothic Revolution, the movement of the new dark ones that depended not upon the devil, but upon their own inner darkness and strengths. Recently, he came to realize the power the Void had over him with some black powdered drug, and freed himself from their clutches. With his new ally, Botan Otonachi, Akiyama starts off with a fresh mind and a new beginning...and he will use it to become what he has always wanted to be...an individual...despite the psychological and emotional hardships littering his path...
