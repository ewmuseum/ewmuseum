Politically Incorrect Biography Biography <b>Appearances:</b> William S. Powell: Powell is a fairly good looking young politician of the age of 27. He has short, close cropped blonde hair and blue eyes. He wears a black suit, with a white dress suit underneath, and black dress pants. Shiny black shoes complete the outfit. Reginald P. Lovecraft: Lovecraft however, well, isn't. As the oldest athlete in PTC at 49 years of age, definite wrinkles show in his face. His hair has turned grayish-white, and he wears a red suit, with red pants, looking for all the world like a thinner Brother Love. <b>Biography:</b> The tandem of Politically Incorrect has been just that: Politically Incorrect. However, few people know that Politically Incorrect has consisted of three men. The original PI consisted of William S. Powell, and Damian Beckett. Beckett was fired mere moments after being accepted into PCW, forcing Powell to find a replacement in Lovecraft. However, Powell still keeps in touch with Beckett…and Politically Incorrect could always use more stooges… Regardless, the tale of the current Politically Incorrect is a story of two very similar men. William S. Powell was born in an upper-class section of Des Moines, Iowa. The Powell family were well known in the hog industry, and were major tycoons. They died when Powell was 20 years old, however, this didn’t affect the young man much. Powell’s good looks and money have gotten him anything he wants. However, his craving for women is legendary. Widely rumored to have slept with thirty girls in 10th grade, including a future Miss Iowa, his sexual feats have yet to be topped in Des Moines. Despite all of this, he was seen as a clean cut young man, who inherited his father’s hog business. He was worth $100 million when this happened. At this point, he was noticed by the Republican party of Des Moines. Needing a candidate to run for city council, they spoke to Powell, who agreed to run. To the shock of the Republicans, and city political observers, Powell learned the game of corruption and bribery quickly, and was elected. His wealth and power only grew…giving him the time to workout and train to become a professional wrestling in between meetings… William V. Lovecraft was likewise born with a silver spoon in his mouth. Of course, he was born in the 1960’s. He was brought up in the south, educated with prejudice. He calls this his greatest regret, as he considers himself to be a fair-minded man, as he would later prove with his ideas and causes. His childhood is not well-known, but his political record is. A former representative on the city council of Atlanta, Georgia, and a former advisor to several Georgia governors, Lovecraft knows his way around the political arena. Currently, he runs C.O.N.D.O.M., a highly secretive fundraising group. He and Powell are both squirreling away money for a massive project, which is classified as unknown at this time. Lovecraft was hired by PCW to team with Powell after observing his work in a Georgia indy promotion. What has resulted is instant chemistry. Politically Incorrect stormed into PCW, attacking both Chris Chase and Corey Williams. In this attack, they executed Chris Chase with their soon to be world-famous Executioner's Chair. They opened their career with tag wins over Asylum and Misplaced, leading to a PCW Tag Team Title shot against Golden Game. They lost this match, with the interference of The Chef and his patented Tali-BANG finisher. After this occurrence, Politically Incorrect realized that they needed an ace in the hole. Roger Schultz, Politically Incorrect's campaign manager, contacted PCW's only Scientologist, Xavier Kannon. In a gesture of good will, the politicians razed a children's playground and an old folks home to build the First Church of Scientology in Des Moines, Iowa. Soon after this, the politicians entered a Gauntlet Match, outlasting Fear Factory, Metallyx, Misplaced, Cowabunga Connection, and Golden Game to win the PCW World Tag Team Titles in a dominating performance. Going on to Spring Fling, the politicians defended in a fourway dance against Golden Game, Cowabunga Connection, and Fear Factory. Just as it seemed the Cowabunga Connection would dethrone the tag champs, Xavier Kannon interjected himself, causing Politically Incorrect to retain. The only question remains is: in the corrupt world of wrestling, just how far CAN Politically Incorrect go? <i>PCW Match History</i> Match #001- Fri.
