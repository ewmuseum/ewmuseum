The Chef Biography Real Name Pierre Jacques Cousteau Alouette Nicknames The Culinary Conquistador Hometown Paris, France Birthday 5/20/65 Height 6'3' Weight 284 lbs Biography One day the Reverend finally succumbed to the temptation to each a nice fresh grilled sausage during a match, he was quickly nailed with an iron skillet and the PCW was never the same since. Pierre Jacques Cousteau Alouette, or “The Chef”, has competed in the PCW for almost two years obtaining many titles and sautéing the asses of countless foes. He has been accompanied by a cavalcade of characters, in the ring and in his promos. Probably the most significant character was his manager and wife, Bertha Stewart. Upon his arrival, The Chef found a tag team partner in Two Ton, and the two men formed the stable “Less Lean Cuisine” and the tag team “Supply and Demand”. The Chef and Two Ton later joined Troy Brown and Ed to form Team International. Team International feuded with The Think Tank in one of PCW’s all time great rivalries. The Chef slowly rose through the lower ranks of the PCW, defeating opponents like Cole Envy and Phoenix. Suddenly, in a surprise to all of PCW, the spirit of former French Emperor Napoleon Bonaparte possessed the Chef. Unsuccessful though in the Stable Wars match, Team International split up, and the Chef moved on to bigger and better things. Napoleon..err..The Chef declared war on the Roman Empire, and single handily destroyed the stable, toppling Romulus and taking his TV Title. The Chef held onto the title, defeating every foe that came in his way, including the deceased Judas Steele. Scott “The Hott One” Naket, who defeated the Chef in a cooking utensil match, finally brought down the Chef’s reign. The Chef was very distraught over losing on his home turf, but decided to carry on in the PCW. With Two Ton gone from the federation, the Chef chose to ally himself with Franky the Mook, and they formed a tag team called “Da Wizeguyz”. The Chef carried Franky on his shoulders, and they quickly won the Tag Team Titles, only to lose them back to Da Westsidaz a week later. The Chef and Franky split up soon afterwards, but there was a much more important split to come. The Chef and Martha Stewart met at a TCBY, and hooked up soon after. Martha brainwashed the Chef, and forced him to turn on Bertha, sending her off to the Center for Crazy Ass People. The Chef’s brief stint with Martha was full of mediocrity; Martha wasn’t a big draw like the foul-mouthed manager of the Chef’s past. Bertha managed to escape from the Center for Crazy Ass People, and attacked Martha at the following PPV. The Chef was torn between two women, but he went with his heart and reunited with Bertha. The two were engaged soon after, and were married at PCW Spring Fling. The Chef soon joined Tyler Nelson’s stable, MEDIA, and instantly became one of the top superstars in the group, winning the Rising Star Title, and making it the “European Title”. The European division never heated up though, and the Chef was forced to give up the title. The Chef and fellow MEDIA member Chet King soon formed a tag team called “C&C TV” and the two quickly won the Tag Team Titles. C&C TV soon lost their titles, and was promptly fired by Tyler Nelson. Two Ton had recently came back to the federation, and the two buds reestablished “Supply and Demand”. The team broke up soon after, as the Chef bought the Old Country Buffet restaurant chain. Two Ton’s heart was broken, and he lashed out at the Chef, but the Culinary Conquistador and Bertha were able to quell his rebellion and he left the PCW for a second time. The Chef and Bertha continued rising up the ranks of the PCW ever so slowly. Finally, the Chef found himself in a National Pride Match, but the Gallant Gastronomer was nearly killed by Ivan Stanislav and Tom Walczak in a display is sheer maniacal hatred for the French. The Chef woke up from his coma after a couple weeks, and against the doctor’s orders, he broke out of the hospital. The Chef soon found shelter with a bunch of hippies and smoked plenty of joints. The Chef also became a vegan, quickly shedding pounds, and after 5 trials on the Richard Hatch Home Liposuction Plan, the Chef had lost almost 80 pounds. The Chef came back to the PCW in a fury, hoping to get revenge on Ivan Stanislav. He soon formed a European Union, which consisted of Hans Kruger, Mr. UK, and finally Tom Walczak. Walczak’s turn to the European Union helped the Chef win the Continental Title, defeating Ivan Stanislav at “New Dawning”. Chef lost the Continental Title a few weeks later to Boda, but then Boda quickly turned on his communist brethren and joined the Chef’s Union. With the European Union going head to head with Stanislav’s Red Army, a collision going to occur, and it did at “Back to Old School”. Just before the pay per view, the wrestling world was shocked when Bertha Stewart suffered an untimely death during childbirth. The Chef held a funeral for her, but Ivan showed up, urinated on the coffin, and set it on fire. The Chef vowed to take care of Stanislav once and for all. At Back to Old School, the Chef did what he promised, defeating Stanislav and the World Champion Trashcan Man for the PCW World Title. The Chef, sobbing with tears of joy, dedicated the belt to his late wife. At the next PCW show, the Chef, appalled by the acts of September 11th 2001, made the announcement that he was going to retire from wrestling in order to join the Armed Forces as a mess hall cook. He gave up his title, and left the wrestling world, dedicating his career to Bertha Stewart. The Chef then surprised and shocked the world when he returned as a newly converted militant Muslim. He also used Osama Bin Laden's money and the help of Professor Herbert Dorkiman to create a clone of his wife Bertha Stewart. His reign of terror ended when Alexis Parshin, a member of the Chef's United Allah Enthusiasts stable, attacked him backstage. The Bertha Clone then successfully programmed the Chef back into being a 'normal' person through the use of sappy videos and wooden spoon beatings. After getting some revenge on the UAE through clever use of a fondue pot, the Chef looks to be back to his old self. The Chef is one of the PCW’s all time greats. Obtaining the PCW Title Grand Slam (World, Continental, Tag, and TV), winning the Tag Team Title with three different teams, the only European Champion, and the only man who can sauté ass in a remoulade of radicchio, rutabagas, and raspberries, the Chef is without a doubt the Culinary Conquistador.
