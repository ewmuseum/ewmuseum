Cyclonica Biography Federation Awards PCW Cruiserweight champion, High Priestess of the Neo Void God. Biography Wrestling Style: Divine Hybrid Style: Cross between Capoeria and Drunken Boxing, creating the ultimate Dance of Death! Littered within the style are traits of other martial arts, acrobatics, and technical, aerial, and brawler wrestling techniques. Real Name Cyclonica Fantasia Akiyama Nicknames The Divine Prophetess, the Omni-Oracle. Frost Hometown The Department of Divine Examination, Japan Birthday Unknown Height 6'3 Management Style Distracting, Divine Violence used when neccessary. Weight 163 Special Moves Set Up: Danse Macabre: A snapping High Angle Thrust Kick to the chin, knocking the opponent down to their back. Then she rolls behind him, flipping him over and driving him headfirst into the mat with a Reverse Rana variation. -Three Times the Prophetess: Cyclonica lifts her leg up with two repeating snapping hook kicks to the opponent’s face. Then she nails a stiff hooking side kick to the back of the head, knocking the opponent face down as she spins around to glare down at him. -Ice Breaker: Cyclonica performs a Karate slide in between the legs of the opponent and performs a low blow before nipping up behind the opponent.
