Bobby Stormbringer Biography Biography <b>Secondary Catch Phrases:</b> Shaddup. ..freggin'.. Whatevah. Sistah. No. Not really. <b>Main Catch Phrases:</b> No really. I mean it. And baby? That is no... b... s. <b>MATCH ENTRANCE:</b> PA system: I see a red door and I want to paint it black... Suddenly, all of the house lights go out into a deep shade of blue and silence prevails for a moment. The words of Mick Jagger fade into the opening techno riffs of 'Points Of Authority' by Linkin Park blaring out over the PA system. Only the Tron comes back on, showing Bobby Stormbringer standing on nothing and against a white background. The shot closes in on his face. B.S. reaches up and crushes his shades, the pieces falling away. Glowing blue eyes are shown and we zoom in at breakneck speed until it shatters right through the right eye. Inside are raging storm clouds that border the entire shot. In the middle is a name in futuristic silver letters: BOBBY STORMBRINGER CAMPBELL Two lightning bolts that are really only pyro 'strike' the stage. Two figures appear behind where each bolt just struck and all of the lights slowly fade back up to normal. Through the new mist we see the first figure standing with his head lowered and his hands on his hips. The smaller figure to his right has both arms crossed over her chest. Finally, they raise their heads and grin to most of the crowd, showing everyone that it is in fact Bobby Stormbringer and his future wife Kira to an even bigger pop. He strides confidently to the ring and stops at the end of the ramp with Kira holding his hand close in tow. B.S. takes his real shades off, puts them in his trench pocket, and slowly removes his silver trench to reveal his bare chest underneath to screams of delight from his target demographic of females between 18-34. After leaving his trenchcoat outside, The Showstopper rolls into the ring and leans back into the near corner as Kira stays right outside the ring. <b>Description:</b> In short, B.S. looks like a new age 'Macho Man' Randy Savage. He has long dark red hair that comes down a little past his shoulders. Sometimes it's slicked back into a pony tail. He has a dark red goatee as well. Except in wrestling matches, he always wears his custom Oakley sunglasses. They are black lenses with blue rims and handles. Three silver lightning bolts run down the lenses. He wears dark blue fingerless gloves and black leather boots (or T-Mac sneakers). To the ring he wears a dark silver trenchcoat with no shirt underneath, a Sting style of trench. He also wears deep silver tights with blue streaks of lightning running down the thighs. Out of the ring, he usually wears black jeans. In the face he resembles Johnny Rzeznik, lead singer of the Goo Goo Dolls. Rigid but boyish features. (Except he doesn't have that cleft in his chin like Johnny does. =P) <b>By Popular Demand! Background Story:</b> <u><i>IN THE BEGINNING....</i></u> Robert Maxwell Stormbringer Jr. was born to Rob Sr. and Althea Michelle Stormbringer (maiden name Archer) on October 30th, in the year 1976. A mere six minutes later, younger twin brother Randall Thomas Stormbringer was also born. Their father was Cauceasean while their mother was African-American. As has been explained before, the original family name was 'Sheridan.' However, Rob Sr.'s own father changed his last name in a drunken stupor to 'Stormbringer' because, in his own words, 'It just sounds cool!' Little Bobby was born within the confines of the mean streets of Bridgeport, Connecticut. How mean, you may ask? So mean that in the mid nineties the industrial city was declared bankrupt. No, no, not <i>part</i> of the city. <i>All</i> of the city! Despite these environmental shortcomings, through the first 15 years of Bobby's life, the standard of living steadily improved. After both redheads actually started Kidnergarden at 4 years old and going on 5 (unlike resident Florida kids who start at 6), they spent their first eight years of life in Connecticut before they all moved to Houston, Texas for two years. After moving to and then living in Las Vegas, Nevada for another 18 months, Bobby and Randy arrived with their parents in another national tourist capital, that being Orlando, Florida. For the most part, it would become their home to stay. It was early 1989 and the twins were only about 6 months away from being 13 years old. During all this time, both parental units were doing excitingly well in their own respective careers. 'Thea' was a nurse turned doctor, while Rob Sr. did well in several areas in the corporate sector. Real estate and the stock market are two prime and specific examples. Whether Rob in particular ever gained part of his success <i>illegally</i> is still up for debate to this very day. Just a few weeks before the twins' 14th birthday, he proclaimed that the family officially qualified as millionnaires. Life was good. Life was peaceful. Let's hope Bobby enjoyed it while it lasted. Both teens went to Liberty High School in Orlando at the right time. In middle school and high school both young bros exceled in different sports. Randy in Football and Bobby in Basketball. Early on in their freshman year of high school they did so well that <i>both</i> were expected to be high school All-Americans by their <i>junior</i> year, let alone their senior year. On a muggy Summer day in 1991, between their Freshman and Sophomore years, Randy and Bobby played an innocent pickup game of Tackle Football in the parking lot of a nearby apartment complex. Which would mean concrete and asphalt. Not a very bright idea when you think about it... It was then that Randy's life would be altered forever. Bobby played on opposite teams with his twin, who was playing out of position as a quarterback as opposed to a wide reciever, due to his overwelming talent in the sport. Bobby pass rushed hard and got lost in the moment, laying his bro right out with a massive sack. Bobby <i>drove</i> Randy hard into the ground. More to the point, right into the <i>concrete</i> ground. Randy suffered a grade A concussion, two cracked ribs, a fractured wrist, and a <i>SEVERELY</i> separated right shoulder. Randy would eventually overcome all of the injuries over the next several months... all the injuries except for <i>one</i>, that is. The former football star's shoulder injury was so hideous that it took <i>4</i> surgeries to piece it back together. As it was, a normal life could be achieved, but anything resembling a <i>life in athletics</i> was out of the question. Randy has never, will he <i>ever</i> fully forgive brother Bobby for this senseless accident. Bobby has apologized consistently over the years for his huge mistake, but to no avail. A 15-year-old Randy remained gradually more bitter and depressed at the slowness of the recovery. Randy returned to his Sophomore class in semi-decent shape just after the second semister began in January, 1992. Yes, the year of upheaval. The year of Nirvana. In several months time, Randy landed himself in critical trouble. Randy became constantly tormented and teased by the other jocks for 'letting the football team down.' After being threatened by one of these many school bullies one too many times, Randy stole a concealed hunting knife from the bully's own locker and quite literally stabbed him in the back with it, right in the middle of gym class! The misguided boy had to struggle for life and even briefly slipped into a coma, but eventually he pulled through. Both teenagers were expelled from the school, the bully for having a bad track record piled on top of the revelation of a knife in his locker. Assault with a deadly weapon and even <i>attempted murder</i> charges were leveled against Randy. His lawyer took a risk and didn't plea bargain. In reality, Randy couldn't count on much more luck to be on his side. First, at 16 years old, Randy was not charged as an adult, a <i>total rarity</i> in the State of Florida. When the decision came through, Randy was found 'Not Guilty' of attempted murder, but <I>'guilty'</i> of the assault charge. The judge, complete with a southern drawl, had no choice but to give Randy the harshest penalty he could muster under the law... Randy Stormbringer was sentenced to be retained in the custody of officials at a local juvenile detention center until the age of 21! Rob Sr. couldn't even bear to look at his son in the face for the entire trial and even left before the final verdict. Later he would <i>only</i> say about the subject of Randy: 'He is dead to me.' Randy rationalized things in his favor and claimed to everyone that Bobby put him up to it. The frightening thing without a shadow of a doubt was in Randy's eyes. He believed every single word and lie he said. The remaining members of the Stormbringer family did the best they could to piece their lives back together. Bobby was such a gifted student that thanks to extra summer school classes he actually graduated after his Junior year! The only reason to stay for the second half of his senior year was for his Liberty B-Ball team, which he would eventually do. Before that however, halfway through his Junior year, Bobby accomplished a first. For the first time in his entire life, Bobby shocked his well off parents. He announced his intention not to begin a College Basketball career on a full college scholarship... but to begin a professional career in- <i>Professional Wrestling!?!</i> Oddly enough? <i>They accepted this!</i> Bobby reasons to this day that his mom and pop must of simply been satisfied that Bobby had kept his nose out of trouble, unlike his slightly younger sibling. It is such a nice thing to have lots and lots of money. During the Summer and Autumn seasons of 1993, Bobby moved in with the Grandparents on his father's side to Calgary...... Alberta, Canada. After a month of flailing about (aboot?) with nowhere to turn, Bobby finally heard of a local guy who had actually made it out of Stu Hart's legendary dungeon... surviving to tell the tale! You know of this man now as 'Lance Storm.' For a healthy amount of money, 'Lance' taught Bobby all the basics. Pretty much everything you would see in a series of 'Tough Enough' these days. Now after several months of training and tryout matches in local gyms, Bobby knew what he was doing. He was still extremely <i>green</i> and a virtual rookie, but at least the knowledge had been gained. Just after Thanksgiving, 'Lance' and Bobby parted ways. Bobby with the tools he needed, and his teacher with a bit more cash in his pocket. Bobby stayed and chilled with his Grandparents, mostly resting his body until on the day of <i>December 13th, 1993</i> he heard the most horrible piece of news in his life, making it undoubtedly the worst day of his life with even September 11th, 2001 being only a close second. <i> His parents were dead.</i> In one of those infernal single engine airplanes, the couple went down just a mere thirty-five miles south of Douglasville, Georgia (Douglasville is just to the west of Atlanta). There were no survivors. Needless to say, Bobby and Randy were both wrecked by the ordeal, although Bobby doesn't believe Randy had any compassion for them. The detention center was changing Randy for the worse, no doubt about that. It wasn't even a <i>month and a half</i> past the 17th birthday for both twins when their parents passed on. Especially when Randy found out that his father, in a will, <i>left Bobby with their ENTIRE estate!!</i> Bobby lived with his redheaded sparkplug of a Grandmother (on his mom's side) until he could make it on he own. Bobby recieved full Basketball scholarship offers from University of Central Florida, Miami University, Central Connecticut State, Duke University, Illinois, UConn, Providence, UNLV, USC, Arizona, and most importantly of all, <i>Florida State University.</i> Had he chosen this route, as cool as it would of been to go full circle and return back to Connecticut to go to college... Bobby most likely would of stayed close to home and gone to FSU. He rejected them all, but still managed to graduate from Liberty High with honors. But B.S., as Bobby's initials happened to be, was hell bent and determined to go the harder, less traveled route. Money wasn't a concern... Not yet, at least... It was all about glory. As much as he loved B-Ball, B.S. knew down deep that this was the right choice to make. The pay was close to insulting. The now well-traveled eighteen year old couldn't even find a wrestling company sophisticated enough to even be on <i>ANY</i> sort of television. How could you get famous in this age of Sports Entertainment without getting your face out there? Simple answer. You can't. Everything was fantastically boring. No need for money, and loving <i>anyone</i> was the farthest thing from his mind, most especially his very own brother. The hunt continued, despite the fact that B.S. sometimes felt like he was running in circles.
