D-Kay Calyx Biography Real Name Darren Keith Calyx Nicknames D-Kay Hometown Chicago, Illinois Birthday November 23rd 1977 Height 6'2' Weight 235 lbs Biography Appearance: D-Kay is an African-American male, 24 years old, with a well built torso. His 6'2', 235 lbs frame is mostly muscle. His hair is black, but shaved very close to his head, with a Celtic pattern shaved into the back. (The pattern is a smaller version of his tattoo.) D-Kay has a tattoo of 'True - Born - Junglist' on his left bicep, and a Celtic design tattoo on his right forearm. His normal attire consists of a white basketball top with thick black bars on the sides (below the arms), a pair of blue jeans with D-Kay written in white writing down his right leg, and a pair of black boots. D-Kay also has a gold chain round his neck with a crucifix pendant on it. Biography November 23rd 1977, George and Sandra Calyx gave birth to a baby boy, named Darren Keith. For his early life, Darren was brought up in a 'posh' area of Chicago, Illinois. His parents both worked for respectful companies, and were able to provide Darren with an upbringing most children would love and enjoy. But Darren didn't. The rest of his friends resented Darren as he grew up, his school friends envious of the money in his family. He was shunned by people his own age, and in his early teenage years, was forced to the outside of the social circle, unless it was a gathering where the money he had would improve the time. He was used purely for the money in the family. At the age of 16, when most of his age group were starting summer jobs after school, and started experimenting with sex, drink and drugs; Darren was still living most of his free time indoors. His parents soon moved to the outskirts of Chicago, into a much bigger house with quicker access to their routes to work. The few friends Darren did have were suddenly left behind. Darren had to start again, not knowing anyone near him, and with bad experiences in his social life so far. At the age of 17, Darren managed to get a job at a local gym, not for the money, but for the chance to get out of the house, and a chance to try and inject a social standing to his persona. Although only 17 at the time, Darren was still made to feel a part of the family at the gym (Nixo's Gym in uptown Chicago). The regulars soon knew him, even though he was just helping clean the place and reset equipment. By the time he was 18, Darren was able to register at the gym as well as work there. He soon started weight training and general cardio vascular building routines, but a freak encounter with an Englishman changed his life for good. Darren had turned up for work on a Saturday afternoon, and walked into the boxing area of the gym, only to see the ring altered. In the ring were two men wrestling. Something Darren had only seen on TV a few times, and hadn't been interested due to the 'It's Fake' cynics in his family. Darren was enthralled at the action in the ring, and being this close, he knew that it wasn't as fake as it was made to be. He watched the match for the remaining 15 minutes, completely hooked, before the show was over. He quickly went back to work, not realising that one of the men he had to interrupt to get to a certain area was one of the wrestlers. His name was Joey Flash, a British wrestler who had just arrived in America to try and 'cut the big leagues'. Darren ended up talking to Joey for a long time, despite the six-year age gap between them. Joey, 24 then, was due to wrestle for an Indy company in Chicago, and was going through a last minute training match. Darren had never heard of a local wrestling show, let alone had the inclination to go to one, but had soon been given an invitation to be a VIP at the event. Darren accepted. Joey taught Darren the ropes in the wrestling business, taking him to the independent bookings he received all over America, and even flying him to England for a few shows. Darren was soon enthralled in the wrestling industry, and as his schooling finished, applied for the University of Chicago for a wrestling scholarship. Unfortunately for Darren, his grades didn't form a high enough level for him to sit the entry exam. Darren, undeterred that the traditional high school and college wrestling route had been blocked for him, decided to start training with Joey on a regular basis, quitting his job at the gym to become Joey's road help. It was a lower salary, but his expenses were paid in with Joey's for working a card, and the knowledge he was gaining looked to get him set for the future. Darren made his debut on a wrestling card just after his 20th birthday, appearing on a British show as Joey's manager. Darren got to take a few moves at the end of the match, which impressed the promoters so much, they booked him back the following week. Darren was in England for nearly a year, wrestling for British Independent promoters in singles matches, and in tag matches with Joey. Unfortunately for Darren, Joey sustained an injury to his left kneecap, and was forced to pull out of the opening show for NWA: UK Hammerlock. Darren, who was due to perform as Joey's tag partner, was cancelled from the show. Darren soon found the bookings coming in less frequently while Joey was on the shelf, and decided that to further his career, he had to come back to America. Darren, now taking on the name D-Kay due to his initials, soon started independent in America on a regular basis. He pulled off a good number of victories in his short time in the shows, before realising that to be able to make a living out of it, he would have to be working full time for one promotion. D-Kay saw PCW as that promotion. Word was soon passed around that Primetime Championship Wrestling where opening an Academy for younger stars to try and make it to the top. D-Kay applied, and was accepted in. Even wrestling just a few matches in PWA, Darren was accepted to PCW2, PCW's second company. D-Kay had a quick rise, winning the vacant Rising Star Title after Jason Snow was promoted to PCW at Gold and Glory. D-Kay then dropped the Rising Star belt as he won PCW2's Cruiserweight title, on what turned out to be the last show the Sibling Company would perform. D-Kay suddenly found himself with a contract to the Primetime family, but no federation, as he missed the cut to PCW. Scott Naket, one of the original teachers in the Academy, opened a 'Farm League' between PWA and PCW, but this folded before one show had been performed, and it was back to the PWA for Darren. Soon enough though, NTICW, a federation formerly run on as a small promoter, was accepted into the PTC family, and D-Kay had an employer again. Unfortunately, NTICW soon closed, and PWA for a third time was his destination. This time, however, things were different. He was given the role of headmaster, taking a class of students to help raise them up, but that soon fell as NTICW re-opened under new management, and D-Kay shifted back. Things felt like home for D-Kay, rivalries with Rich Cannon and Frankie Bones were getting him noticed, but he quickly became disillusioned, and disappeared. NTICW were worried, and then owner Blane Bishop assigned Joey Flash in to replace him on the short term. D-Kay did make a return, on what turned out to be NTICW's last show under Bishop. With the roster out of work again, PCW started applications to promote certain employees, and Darren was lucky enough to secure the place. D-Kay started off in Black Attack, a stable run purely for the African-American's in PCW, but he soon left, and fell in with former PCW2 comrades, Chris Sloboda, Tim Walden, White Mexican (aka Mexi) and Red Rock. The split within Wild Deuces gave D-Kay split loyalty, on one hand to his tag partner Red Rock, who was aligned with Mexi, and on the other hand, the friendship with Boda. Before D-Kay could establish what to do, however, an attack by Tim Walden on Pay Per View left him on the injured list. D-Kay had suffered a slight injury to his right shin, but it did require a two-month rest and recovery period. D-Kay did inform PCW, but, returned to full training to find his contract terminated. After a brief rest period, D-Kay entered RUSH, working a programme against Tyler Valentine that climaxed in a ladder match at the Crossroads Pay per View. Allison cited her family as the reason she took her own life. D-Kay, obviously angered by the news, decided to exact revenge on Tyler...but sustained numerous injuries, not only in the ladder match, but the roster wide main event. D-Kay was left on the shelf due to injuries, but was given release from his contract at the same time. His injuries not being as bad as first thought, have now healed, and the Chicago native is ready again to enter the ring. Staying away from bad memories, he hopes to make it in PCW to prove himself worthy of the shot he was given last time around. D-Kay is now healing quickly, and ready to jump into action in a PCW ring. Is he ready? Only time will tell. Other Feds PWA (x5), NTICW (x3), RUSH, PCW2 Other Fed Awards PWA Fourth Graduate, PCW Rising Star Championship, Last holder of PCW2 Cruiserweight Championship - retiring the belt as PCW2 closed.
