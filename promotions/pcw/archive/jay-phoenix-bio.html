Phoenix Biography Real Name Jay Phoenix Nicknames Eternal Flame, Dream Walker Hometown Flagstaff, Arizona Birthday 29 February 1976 Height 5'11 Weight 220lbs lbs Biography Jay Phoenix is 5’11 and 220lbs of pure lean muscle. His body is muscular, but not overly so, and his skin is healthily tanned. Long auburn hair flows freely down to his waist, letting piercing green eyes stare out from beneath. There is a tattoo of a flaming Phoenix on his left outside bicep, surrounded by an armband tattoo of fire, and when his chest is exposed another tattoo … a Dreamcatcher (a circle that is filled with criss-crossed strands) composed of flames. Phoenix wears a black leather look body suit, flame motifs running up the side of both legs, meeting at the thigh where they join together to form almost a belt of fire. The arms are cut off at the shoulders exposing muscular biceps, the left one covered with a glistening tattoo of a stylised Phoenix in flight surrounding by bands of gold and red flames. Black and red elbow pads are met by taping that covers his forearms and wrists. A silver necklace hangs around his neck, falling down to his chest, and when the light catches it at the right angle it can be seen to be a disc containing thin interlocking strands, a bright purple amethyst at it’s centre … a dreamcatcher, which is removed before wrestling. ~ Phoenix is both highly focused, and highly spiritual. Looks towards the Native American spirits to guide him ... very open and trusting, with an attitude of seeing the best in everyone until proved otherwise. Has a 'never give up' streak that means that he is very open to inury and pain, as he would prefer to face insurmountable odds and fail, rather than not try. ~ Jay Phoenix was born and raised on a Native American reservation, brought up to respect the land, and all its creatures, and to judge individuals on their merits alone, not who they are or what they are. While not massively built, or especially strong, Phoenix is naturally quick and powerful, and extremely resilient. He excelled at most sports, quickly mastering any he tried, especially the Martial Arts. Chosen to represent the United States of America as part of the Olympic Archery team, Phoenix’s mentor and guardian decided that wrestling was the career path that Phoenix should follow … so he did. Various stints in various federations followed, with the more important and career defining ones being a four-year contract with the PWW, an 14 month contract in the GIWF, and contracts in the GZW, PCW, the GDW and the IOWF. Phoenix has climbed the heights, and fell to the lows, so has seen the best and worst in the industry, but his desire and fire still burns bright, as evidenced by his participation in the Global Title Tournaments and the Universal Title Tournaments. In late 2001, Phoenix bought the fed that originally made him a star ... and closed it's doors forever ... so that he would not have to see it dwindle and fade ... which is something that haunts him now, as he wonders is that is something that will happen to his own career. After winning the World title in the UWF, Phoenix sustained a severe knee injury, and found that when he could not compete the federation closed it’s doors and none of the ‘big’ feds would take him on, claiming that he was an insurance risk. … until he heard of a small federation opening, the PWC, owned by someone from his past in Primetime Championship Wrestling. Ron Starr After weeks of haggling, Ron agreed to take Phoenix into the PWC, if he could prove that he was healthy … something that Starr never actually wanted, but instead colluded with another wrestler by the name of Romulus to first of all fake medical records that proved Phoenix’s 100% state of health, and then when that failed to re-injure him … this time in the hopes of permanently retiring him … by having Romulus and his colleagues (Collusus, a seven foot brute … the broker, a man of ideas, and the snake in the grass, a female masseuse) in the Empire focus their whole attention on the one goal. Ending Phoenix’s career. Their plans did not quite work, though before the doors closed on PWC Phoenix had already left. Returning to full health, and claiming the World title in a sister fed of the UWF … 4 Winds Wrestling … Phoenix seemed to have put the past behind him, but as the anniversary of the PWW closing drew nearer, his thoughts turned to his own career and the failures that he perceived in it … like the fact that there had been no closure in the PWC, and the dating back even further there had been no closure on his aborted stint in the PCW, something he would never be able to change as the PCW was closed, Ronn Starr finally retired from the business himself. Then came that fateful piece of information: PCW reopens … and the future is history … Other Feds PTC/WO GTT Finalist; UWF (GIWF) - [Grand Slam] World, North American, Intercontinental, Televsion; 4WW - North Wind (World); GZW - World; PWW - [Grand Slam] World (x2), Intercontinental, European, CWTV, Master of the Mat, CWMC Champion 2003; GDW - Western Quotes Feel the HEAT! Halos can break you see, pedestals can crumble, and no matter what sort of rose coloured glasses you look through, the world is still turning and people are still only human. We can only be what we give ourselves the power to be. Immortality is living a life worth living.
