Turmoil 38 3 Jul 2001 The PCW Pavilion, Atlantic City, NJ Out with the old, in with the new The show fades in from black to white, snowy static as the slow sounds of “Last Day of Acquaintance” from Sinead O’Connor slowly and eerily serves an audio backdrop. The snowy static slow gives way to a black and white screen as a masked man is show riding a zip line to a ring in ultra slow motion, as five other masked men await his arrival. Rapid fire, yet still slow motion shots of the six men pulling off their ski masks in succession is shown. The first man to unmask is none other than Professor Herbert D. Dorkiman. The second man to unmask is UFW Hardcore Legend Sy The third is recently signed PCW wrestler Ming. The fourth is UFW wrestler Bob “The Demolition Man” Ulrich. The fifth is none other than “The Scholar” Howard Porter. And last but not least, the final man to pull off his mask, is noneother than The Reverend Jon Hull, who walks around what appears to be an MWF ring, triumphantly, as the fans in attendance go wild. The camera focuses in on the face of The Reverend, then suddenly morphs into the sweat-covered face of Frisco, who uses his arm to wipe his the sweat from his forehead. The action is still in black and white and in slow motion. The scene shifts to Stewart Hix rushing towards Frisco with a clothesline, but Frisco agilely slips under the clothesline and behind Hix, grabs him by the waste, and fluidly crushes him with a German suplex in one fluid motion. The scene fades to a series of show motion shots of Frisco. First he is with Pgod and Doug Troy, in the trademark $3000 suits worn by members of DAMN. This is followed by five different shots of a battle worn triumphant holding up each different title he won throughout his PCW tenure. The screen splits into halves, as still shots of both Reverend and Frisco are shown triumphantly holding the PCW World Title belt above their heads Suddenly, the eerily soft and depressing tones of Sinead O’Connor abruptly give way to the opening guitar rift of “Young Man Blues” by the Who. The shots of The Reverend and Frisco are ripped from the screen unceremoniously, and onto the screen appear rapid fire, color, normal speed action highlights of The Storm Brothers, Shawn Prodigy, The White Mexican, Agent Orange, Vampir Nosferatu, Jack Valentine and Big Alan Steele laying waste to various opponents. The rotating pictures of PCW’s new talent continue rapidly gain speed and replacing one another on the screen in succession, then until they appear and change so quickly that it is impossible to make out who is who. The highlights abruptly stop, leaving nothing but an empty black screen with the stark white words. “Out with the old….” Highlights of Frisco, Bang Davenport, Brock Newbludd and the Reverend appear in succession on the screen, each fading away to give way to the next…. The highlights stop, giving way to an empty black screen with the cold white words. “….and in with the new.” Highlights of Tommy Christopher, The Educator and Ivan Stanislav roll across the screen in succession. Once again the screen is black except for the bone white words. “The new Primetime has arrived…” The shot fills with an explosion of flames…..then the screen fades to black. Doug stops at the ring, but doesn’t go in. Instead, he walks around it and makes for the announcer’s table. Jeff: Um… There’s no room here! Dave: Look’s like Doug wants to do some announcing for the match tonight. Jeff: He can do it somewhere else. TABLE’S FULL! Doug steps up to the announce table and pulls a seat up right next to Smith. With a quick pull, he yank’s Smith’s headset off of his head. Smith: Hey, that’s mine! Doug, in reply, slaps a piece of duct tape on Smith’s mouth. I’m out here because I felt like pulling a ‘Clyde’ and doing some commentating. Jeff: DA MAN you are not. ‘I’m not Trading’ by Sunna starts playing as Kid Wonder comes down to the ring. KW flips over the ropes and poses to the squeals of delighted teeny boppers. Clay: Introducing first, from Orlando, Florida, weighing in at 169 lbs, Kid Wonder! Dave: Oh, so is DAMN recruiting or something? Jeff (under his breath): Great, ANOTHER DAMN jobber. Doug: Excuse, Mr. Marx? Jeff: Oh, nothing. The Olympic theme plays through the arena as Big Allen Steele comes down to the ring without Sue Saknussum at his side. Once in the ring, he starts flexing for the crowd. Clay: Introducing next, from San Jose, California, weighing in at 264 pounds, he is the son of Zeus, Big Alan Steele! Dave: Where’s Sue Saknussum, BAS’s manager? She’s usually with him… The camera feed cuts to the back, where Sue is having a heated argument with VP-F. Dave: Looks like VP-F is too lazy to write her into the match. Once in the ring, BAS starts flexing for the crowd. Doug: Whoa, looks like someone got into Knox’s stash. Dave: Hey, Jeff. Hank! Jeff shoots Dave a “You son of a bitch” look and grits his teeth. Beads of sweat appear on Marx’s head as he tries to hold back his gut reaction. Doug: Huh? ‘The Rascal King’ by the Mighty Mighty Bosstones plays as THE Multiple Man, um, bikes his way down to the ring on a ten-speed. He circles the ring before stopping at the bottom of the ramp. He puts down the kickstand, pauses, then gives the ‘Power To The People’ signal with his arm. He accentuates his salute to the common man by ringing his little bicycle bell. Clay: Introducing next, from Parts Unknown, weighing in at 225 pounds, THE Multiple Man! Doug: Holy crap, that’s surreal. That has gotta be the whitest guy I’ve ever seen. Except for Marx, of course. Jeff continues to sweat with the effort of holding his gut reaction back, but it fails… Jeff: HANK! DOUG TROY JOBBED TO HANK! HANK I *WHAP!* Instead of his normal spot at the announce table, Jeff Marx is now laying on his back, staring at the ceiling. Dave bursts into laughter as Doug pulls his arm back to the desk. Jeff: You’re dead, Kern. Ow, my head… ‘Full Nelson’ by Limp Bizkit plays as the last combatant, Tyler Nelson, walks down to the ring. Upon entering the ring, he glares at the pole with the Duct Tape on it outside the ring. Clay: Introducing last, hailing from Dallas, Texas, weighing in at 251 pounds, he is the owner of PCW and the head of Greed Inc, Tyler Nelson! Jeff: MY HERO! CANCEL ALL OF THESE JOBBERS, BOSS! Dave: Ladies and gentlemen, our main event is about to start! Jeff pulls himself up off the floor while shooting a murderous glance at Dave. The bell rings as each competitor looks at each other from one of the four corners of the ring. Suddenly, Kid Wonder rushes at Big Allen Steele. Steele, ready for Wonder, leans over and back-body drops him over the ropes. Wonder holds on to the ropes, though, and lands on the apron with his feet. Steele poses, unaware of Wonder’s position right behind him. The posing is cut off when Wonder climbs to the top turnbuckle and gives Steele a Diamond Dust! Dave: And Steele learns his first big lesson in PCW: don’t turn your back on Kid Wonder. Doug checks his watch. Meanwhile, THE Multiple Man and Tyler Nelson are facing off on the other side of the ring. TMM, reaches behind and pulls out a sword wrapped in duct tape! Doug: PLUNDAH! TMM HAS PLUNDAH! TMM does a kata with the blade, twirling it around and doing elaborate poses with it. Tyler looks, mystified, at TMM while the kata continues. Suddenly, TMM stops the kata and charges at Tyler with a battle cry. Tyler dodges TMM, but TMM turns right back toward Tyler and charges again. Tyler dodges again, but TMM stops on his tracks and points to his own head in the classic “DAMN I’m smart” gesture before eating a dropkick from Nelson. Jeff: YES! GO TYLER! Doug checks his watch again. Dave: Why do you keep doing that? Doug: Huh? Oh, Vegas odds had it 15:1 that tonight’s main event would have a run-in about a minute and a half into the match. Meanwhile, Big Allen Steele and Kid Wonder are trading punches. Steele uses his weight advantage to win the exchange and starts staggering Kid Wonder with punches. He rears back for a mighty clothesline, but Wonder ducks and mule kicks Steele in the gut. Steele grabs the leg, rears back again and nearly takes Wonder’s head off with a clothesline. Dave: And Steele puts Kid Wonder down with a thunderous clothesline! Doug: 58 seconds… Jeff: You’re a regular Pete Rose, Doug… Doug: Huh? Oh, thanks. Steele picks Kid Wonder back up and puts him on his shoulders as TMM runs into him, a projectile launched by Tyler Nelson. All three fall to the mat in a train wreck while Tyler grabs the duct tape sword that TMM dropped. NELSON IS WEARING A CRIMSON MASK! Dave: Tyler’s got a small paper cut on his forehead from the duct tape, but it looks like it’s pissing him off more than it hurts. Jeff: It’ll take more than some blood to stop Nelson! GET HIM, BOSS! Tyler, pissed off, grabs TMM from behind as TMM brings his foot back into Nelson’s crotch. Jeff: DQ! DQ HIM REF! Dave: I think this is a no-DQ match, Jeff. Jeff: DIRTY POOL, I SAY! Doug: 30 seconds… Tyler goes down, hurting something fierce as TMM pulls a small bottle out of his tights. A bottle marked ‘Lemon Juice’. Dave: Uh-oh, this can’t be good for our CEO. TMM has the bane of all open wounds: lemon juice! TMM puts the lemon juice in his mouth and gets ready to unleash a painful yellow mist on Nelson. He’s stopped, however, when Big Allen Steele grabs him from behind and gives TMM a Full Nelson Bomb! TMM swallows the juice on impact and starts convulsing, his face contorted into a sour grimace. Doug: 15 seconds… Steele picks TMM back up as TMM continues to make gookie faces from the sour taste of the lemon juice. He quickly Irish Whips TMM into the ropes, but TMM slides out and starts to walk toward his bike on the ramp. BAS gets out of the ring to follow as Kid Wonder and Tyler Nelson start to battle. Doug helps KW to his feet, but KW seems a little confused after seeing who’s helping him up. Doug offers a handshake, but is suddenly cut off when Clyde blindsides him with a forearm to the back! Dave: Where the hell did he come here! KW goes to help his unlikely benefactor, but is cut off by Inphino Blitz, who crushes him with the Black Dragon Smasher! Dave: BLITZ IS HERE TOO!!! BLACK DRAGON SMASHER ON WONDER! Jeff: AND DA MAN IS BEATING UP DA JOBBER! Two of the biggest morons in PCW are being beat up and I LOVE IT!
