Ben J. Pailthorpe Biography Real Name Benjamin John Pailthorpe Biography Appearance: Ben J. has a very grunge like appearance, he favours baggy clothes and other such attire. He has no tatoos or piercings. His hair is shoulder length and is black with red streaks. He has green eyes. He is pretty well defined but not totally buff. His ring attire consists of simple black jogging bottoms with white lines going down each side. He tapes his wrists and hands when he wrestles. Ring Entrance: The opening riff of Aerial by System of a Down plays and the lights dim, white lights play around the arena as Ben J. Pailthorpe walks out onto the ramp. He looks around the arena before making his way down the ramp and climbing into the ring. Biography: Born Benjamin John Pailthorpe at 4am on the 26th July 1978 to parents Leonard and Vivienne Pailthorpe at Kings College Hospital in South London, Ben was two weeks premature and needed to stay in an incubator for a month. When he was born he weighed nothing more then a 1kg bag of sugar, no one expected him to go on to achieve great things. As a young child his family provided well for him, he recieved love and care and an upbringing which a child deserves. He attend Hollydale Primary school where his grades were consistantly good and he achieved high marks on his SATs. Moving on he attended Catholic school St Thomas The Apostle where his grades were not as high but he got through and went to college. When he was in college he developed a love for wrestling, so much so that by the time he was 17 he was training at the headquarters of NWA-UK Hammerlock in Sussex. By the age of eighteen he decided he wanted to go pro, packing up his belongings in a small suitcase and boarding on a plane bound for the USA. Almost as soon as his feet touched US soil Ben was approached by a man named J. Pape, James was the owner of a small feeder federation for the World CLass Wrestling Federation, Ben became the first signee to the newly Christened National Wrestling Federation. Ben spent many months preparing and it came down to new years eve, a huge tournament for the vacant NWF International title, the federations top prize. Ben pulled out all the stops and won, defeating all the odds and becoming the first NWF International Champion. He built strong friendships with members of the NWF and formed to NWF branch of the Youngbloods, a stable that spanned into the Wolrd CLass Wrestling Fderation. This was where Ben was bound after NWF was closed, he unified the NWf and WCWF International titles defeating The Judge in a steel gavel match. A month after this Ben was forced to quit the WCWF. he spent half a year out of the ring and at his age of eighteen he began drinking, at first it wasnt a problem. He then joined Brian Zane Wrestling, where he started out at the bottom of the ladder. He worked hard, he fought off the drink which was consuming him and he managed to capture the BZW Cruiserweight title in an epic Ladder Match with Shockwave. His title reign was short lived as he lost the title to the former champion a week later, but his win propelled him into the limelight and he captured his first Television title at a BZW Pay Per View. He was to have many more Television title reigns untill his career changed for the better. He began teaming with a young man named Kid Lightning, they formed a good friendship and a strong team and they captured the BZW Tag Team titles and became known as the greatest tag team in BZW history. After a 3 month reign Ben recieved his first chance at the World title, at the pay per view Badd Blood Ben was involved in a huge 32 man tournament for the vacant belt, he made it to the last three after the Commissioner made the final match a three way dance, he turned into an impromptue battle royale with all the tournament participants involved but once more Ben made it to the last three before he was eliminted. Down but not out Ben was on his way to the top. At the pay per view Halloween Hell, Ben brought home the gold and held it for a month before being defeated by his nemesis throughout his BZW career Death. He went on to have United States and Intercontinental reigns before he and Kid Lightning and a host of other stars quit the company following a screwjob by the boss. Ben and Kid lightning moved on to the Global Wrestling Federation, this was a meeting of Ben and his mentor as the man behind GWF was none ther then J. Pape. Ben started off steadily, gaining the Television title by being only one of two people in GWF to defeat Christopher Camero. He had a tag team title reign with former PCW and GWF star Hans Kruger and he was once again a member of the Youngbloods. At a GWF pay per view Ben turned his back on Hans Kruger and a week later on Overload Ben captured the GWF title. His reign was short lived as he was defeated by Kid Lightning after being screwed by Hans Kruger who was special ref. Following this ben was suspended from the GWF, his alcoholism hand increased to a dangerous level and he experimented with performance enhancing drug prolymethene. In his enforced hiatus Ben has a huge change of heart, he became a born again Christian denouncing all his past sins and looking to the future. He returned and won the huge Showdown Tournament and GWF's Pay Per View Showdown in Tokyo II, a month later on January 7th 2002 in london England Ben captured the GWF title for a second time, defeating TLM Jay Robson, Jean-Claude Dafoe and Jesse Briggs. He then lost the title a month later in a three way dance against Salaok and Dafoe. Then in March 2002 he invoked a contract clause which allowed him to leave the GWF, a week later he debuted on PCW Sunday Sabotage on St. Patricks Day brutally attacking Paddy O'Shea with a crowbar along with former GWF'ers John Crichton and Andy Murray.
