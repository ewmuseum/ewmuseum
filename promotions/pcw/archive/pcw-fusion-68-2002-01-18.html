Fusion 68 18 Jan 2002 The Bob Marley Arena, Kingston, Jamaica Kingston International Airport and a case of mistaken identity We open up at the Kingston International Airport in Jamaica, with a small logo in the bottom right corner that reads “Earlier Today”.
