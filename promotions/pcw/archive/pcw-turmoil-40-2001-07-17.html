Turmoil 40 17 Jul 2001 The Hershey Park Pavilion, Hershey, PA Vampir Nosferatu corners Barbara Bush-Kriegman The screen fades in to show the wife of former PCW World Heavyweight Champion Alan Kriegman, Barbara Bush-Kriegman, walking casually down a hallway in the backstage area of the Hershey Park Pavilion, in Hershey, PA. It is obvious that the crowd inside the arena is watching the same scene that the viewers at home are seeing, as a huge pop erupts from elsewhere in the building, can be heard in the background, at the sight of the voluptuous vixen. Suddenly, a black shadowy figure sweeps by behind for a brief instance. Barbara hears the noise and stops. She cautiously turns around, but sees nothing. She shrugs her shoulders and begins to casually turn back around. As she turns back around she is finds her self mere inches away from Vampir Nosferatu. Barabara’s jumps in a startled fashion, then her face goes stark white. The 6’7 Nosferatu towers over Barbara as she backs up against the wall in fear. Barbara suddenly turns to perhaps flee, but Vampir places a hand against the wall on each side of her trapping her up against the wall. Barbara looks as if she is trying to scream for help but nothing comes out of her mouth. From elsewhere in the building the sounds of a capacity crowd clamouring in concern can be heard. Vampir puts his face right next to hers as if he is taking in her scent. He smiles sadistically showing his fangs. Barbara’s face turns to true terror. Vampir continues to smile at this sight. Vampir: (licking his lips) Lovely…. Barbara closes her eyes and begins to break down to tears from the terror she is feeling. When she reopens her eyes, she sees that Vampir has disappeared. Still feeling the terror of this close encounter with the monster she breaks down hard into tears and just slides down the wall to a sitting position. She hides her face in her hands and begins to cry and tremble in fear….as the scene fades black.
