Pretty Cool Guys Biography Biography The Bio... of Rich Sherwin... Rich Sherwin was born under the full name of Rich Adam Sherwin in the small town of Watford, England on November 21 1976. His parents were Alison and Terry, a couple of average people who had pretty average jobs. They weren't special... or gifted... or anything else for that matter, I'd call them your typical parents. Sherwin spent the first five years of his life in a small appartment, having to make do with the sounds of obsenity around him. The parents of the future PCW Superstar were of no use either, they constantly bickered throughout his childhood and consequently forced their only son out of the picture. When Sherwin hit 13, he made a decision that would change his life... he decided to leave the pain behind him... he decided to get out of the burning picture and find himself another life. August 20th 1990 marked a significant day in the life of Sherwin, he'd been living with friends and other close family for a few months following his decision to leave home, but, by pure chance, he found himself being taken under the wing of a wealthy middle aged couple. Questionning his life-style, Sherwin informed these new people that he'd run away from home following domestic disputes... a reasoning that forced these people to pity the young Teenager and offer him space at their luxury Mansion. The years passed everyone by and Sherwin had become well accustomed to life in this new family. Refusing to even awknowledge the existance of his birthparents, Sherwin insisted that his 'second family' were his real parents, despite taking upon the other members of his 'Blood Family'. From there, Sherwin was quiet guy... until he decided to take up Wrestling at the age of 19. Since Sherwin was in a 'Rich Family'... he was able to do anything without worry of cost. Originally trained under the grooming eye of World Wrestling Assossciation Owner, 'Slick' Willy Weston, Sherwin took forth to his career... working many Independants... until he found his groove working for the mysterious 'Secret Suicidal Man' and 'Extremely Suicidal Wrestling'. His debut with the Boston based Company was nothing short of exciting. He forced his way into the Intercontinental Title scene, interupting a #1 Contendership match and attacking 'The Bionic Redneck', subsequently causing him to lose the contest and Title shot. Following that motion, Sherwin was quick to develop a reputation as a loud-mouth, shouting off at the stars who had worked hard to earn their spots! His first challenge was answered... but the shadowy figure known as 'Nightmare', and while the arena waited for the newcomer to be silenced... that... was far from the case. Sherwin dashed through the company to hold the Intercontinental and World Championship belts in record time, establishing himself as one of the companys Legends. From that point, Sherwin continued to work the independant scene... that was of course, until his path fell into 'Primetime Central'. Rich had been informed about this 'Community' by fellow former-ESW Superstar, Jordan Copeland, a man who had worked under the ring name of 'Jordan Crazy' during his ESW stint. Sherwin joined Wrestling School, 'Primetime Wrestling Acadamy', a place which he graced for a mere two weeks before being allocated a spot upon the roster of 'New True Internet Championship Wrestling'. Not one to break away from his previous career success, Sherwin was quick to establish himself as something within the walls of NTICW, immediatly forming the 'Pretty Cool Guys' with Ryan Moore and Sean Smith. Within four matches the Tag Team Titles were in their control... another ground-breaking moment for a team that had next-to-no reputation around this new land. After only a month of employment, Sherwin and his friends became disenchanted with NTICW and were grudgingly permitted to leave the company per their own request. Spending another few months on the sidelines, Sherwin wrestled few shows and worked as 'Vice President' of PTC Federation, 'International Championship Wrestling'. When 'The Phantom Booker' released all of his PCW Stars following a 'Pay-Per-View' show... Sherwin contacted Moore about joining the new-look 'Primetime Championship Wrestling'. Thinking that it was all a laugh, the two applied for roster positions... only to find themselves being offered PCW contracts... a week or so later. Will Sherwin be able to rise as fast here as he did in many of his other federations? Who knows... all will be revealed in the future though! Bio of Ryan Moore...
