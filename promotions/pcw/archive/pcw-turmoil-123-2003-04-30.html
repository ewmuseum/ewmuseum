Turmoil 123 30 Apr 2003 The Mobile Civic Center, Mobile, Alabama Matt Jackson held hostage by armed men Matt Jackson is sitting tied to a chair in his locker room. He’s unable to, however, and the trio tosses him over the top rope to meet the same fate as Hank~! and Lacuna Debris… Warrior has been eliminated! Dave: That was fast… Jeff: He’s a feckin’ jobber! Tomas: Look who’s in the ring… Gaz Green, The Pharaoh, Rich Sherwin, and Tyler Preston… and Miss Moonlight. While the three others approach the helpless Miss Moonlight, Gaz walks right up behind Preston, rears his buzzer-rigged hand back, and SLAMS it right into the spinal cord in Tyler’s neck!! An intimidating BBBBBBBBBBBZZZZZZZZZTTTTTTTTTTTTTTTTT sounds and Tyler drops to his knees, screaming in agony as Sherwin and Pharaoh looked on confused. Dave: APPARENTLY GAZ ISN’T GOING TO WORK WITH THE MEMBER OF E2 AT ALL! Jeff: BAH! Who needs him!! The Jester then takes Preston by the head and runs into the ropes, tossing Tyler right over the top one to the floor! VP-S shrugs and signals to the timekeeper. Tyler Preston has been eliminated! Jeff: That stupid bitch is in BIG trouble now! Titu Xansiv, Gaz Green, and Rich Sherwin now stand over Moonlight’s recovering person, grinning wildly. It looks as though all hope is lost… until… Dave: LOOK! HELP HAS ARRIVED!!!! Jeff: ARRRGG! MORE BITCHES!! Tomas: Guess that means you like men, right? Jeff: Wha…?! SHUT UP, CUNT! Tomas: Don’t make me jam my foot down your throat! Sure enough, Tessa Windsor and Brianna Washington are charging full force to the ring while the fans stand on their feet, nearly blowing the roof off with cheers. Brianna slides in first, followed by Tessa. Sherwin and Pharaoh break off their attack on Moonlight and run at the two women, looking to overpower them. Pharaoh throws his arm out for a clothesline, but Washington ducks, spins around, and blasts Titu in the back of the head with a kick, hanging him over the top rope. Sherwin doesn’t have much more luck, as before he can even try to hit Tessa, she drops to the ground and hooks his leg, causing him to drop neck first into the top rope! Jeff: CHEATING BITCHES!! ARRRGGGGGGGGGGG!! The PoPstar pulls himself up and leans back against the ropes, trying to get his bearing as Pharaoh joins him. Seeing a perfect opportunity to even the odds, Tessa storms right past Gaz on the other side of the ring, rebounds off the ropes, and comes running back straight at Pharaoh and Sherwin. Only about 4 feet from them, she leaps into the air with her arms outstretched, ready to take them both out. Unfortunately, Pharaoh ducks and Tessa only clotheslines Sherwin over! The PoPstar merely falls to the floor as Tessa comes crashing down like a burning airplane. The crowd is in an absolute frenzy! Dave: TESSA JUST SACRIFICED HERSELF TO EVEN THE ODDS, REDUCING IT TO THE FINAL FOUR!! WHAT A DISPLAY OF COURAGE!! Jeff: What a moronic bitch! She just gave up her chance at the CW Title! Tomas: That she did… Dave: There’s more to life than gold, Jeff! She just did Washington and Miss Moonlight a huge favor! Jeff: Bah… While the crowd applauds Tessa, Pharaoh finally recovers and stands up off the ropes, but is met by the elbow of Brianna Washington. Gadget lays in a couple of furious elbows right to his face and whips him against the far ropes. Gaz just watches on, prepping his electric buzzer as Miss Moonlight starts to climb to her feet. Titu rebounds off the ropes and comes back, running right into a perfect dropkick from Brianna Washington! Jeff: ARG! Tomas: There’s your fanatic. Gadget doesn’t lay off for a second, running towards the downed Pharaoh and leaping into the air, giving a 360 front flip and landing back first on Titu’s sternum for the Rolling Thunder! Titu curls up and holds his ribs, trying to regain his lost wind. Dave: Brianna has some sort of vendetta against the Pharaoh! Look at the fury! Tomas: Hell hath no fury like a woman scorned… Jeff: Or a PMSing woman. Washington quickly gets to her feet and picks the Egyptian Madman up, drilling him in his face with a few right punches. She looks to go for another, but Titu SLAMS her in the gut with his kneecap, taking every bit of wind right out of her. Her eyes nearly pop out of her head as she doubles over, clutching her struck gut. Pharaoh takes her by the neck forcefully with one hand and takes by the leg with the other, lifting her up above his head! Jeff: SHE’S GONNA MEET THE SAME FATE AS JONATHAN DRACHEN AND LACUNA DEBRIS! Dave: PUT HER DOWN, TITU! The member of the tri-religious power stable slowly stalks over to the ropes as Gaz looks on, not paying attention to the now recovered Miss Moonlight. Knowing her predicament, Brianna begins to kick wildly, trying to break free. As Pharaoh becomes adjacent with the ropes, he tries to steady Gadget, but her kicking and thrashing becomes too violent! While swinging her arm about, she clubs Pharaoh right in the back of the head and lurches him forward! Jeff: NOOOOOOOOOOOOOO!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! TITU!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! Tomas: DIE YOU SAND-SUCKING FUCK! In slow motion, Brianna falls forward from Titu’s arms towards the floor, but during her descent, she grabs the top rope and pulls it down with her. Having been clubbed in the head, Pharaoh staggers forward and right through the gap made by Gadget! Both bodies go tumbling to the floor as Jeff screams his disagreement in the background. Jeff: WHAT A CHEAP ASS MOVE!!!!!!!! ARRRRRRRGGGGGGGGG!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! Dave: BRIANNA HAS JUST ELIMINATED PHARAOH AND PHARAOH HAS ELIMINATED HER! IT’S DOWN TO GAZ AND MISS MOONLIGHT! Brianna Washington and The Pharaoh have been eliminated! Completely dumbfounded, The Jester just kind of stares as Washington and Pharaoh begin fighting on the outside… not a smart move on Gaz’s part. He fails to see Miss Moonlight throw herself into the ropes, rebound back, and leap into the air, driving her weight and knees right into his body! The impact is staggering and Gaz wobbles out of the corner, looking like he’s going to fall on his face! Miss Moonlight jumps onto the top rope. She gives the M symbol to her stomach and signals to the crowd, which pop massively! Dave: What’s she going to do???? Tomas: Wait and see! Jeff: I HOPE SHE FALLS OFF! The puro heroine leaps off the top rope and flips through the air, turning at least two and a half times. She lands perfectly on Gaz’s shoulders, but The Jester regains his wits right then, lifts her up, and drives her to the mat with a massive POWERBOMB! The crowd lets out a resounding boo of disgust as Gaz Green grins, staring down at his fallen opponent. Dave: This may be the end, folks… Gaz has her under control. Tomas: Don’t count that girl out yet! The Emerald Menace reaches down and takes her by the hair, yanking the hero to her feet! But before he can even do anything, Miss Moonlight sends a VERY stiff kick right into his precious gems!! Gaz doubles over in utter agony and can’t even scream from the pain. Crowd: OOOOOOOOO!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! Not wanting to waste any time, Miss Moonlight hooks her leg over the back of her neck and uses it as a springboard to flip herself over and to the back of Gaz. As soon as she lands, she wraps her muscular arms around his waist, lifts him into the air, and drills him neck first to the mat with a Back Drop Driver!! Dave: THE LUNA SEA!! THE ODDS MAY HAVE JUST CHANGED! Jeff: Arg… too… fast… Gaz’s spine gives a sickening crack and he instantly grips it, crying out in pain. Miss Moonlight gets up a look at him for a moment with a pseudo-concerned look in her eye… big mistake. BBBBBBBBBBZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZTTTTTTTTTTTTTT!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! The Jester reaches up suddenly and wraps the hand with the buzzer around her throat, giving her quite a shocking surprise!! Miss Moonlight gives a high-pitched scream and staggers back, gripping her electrocuted throat. Gaz doesn’t have time to move as a pair of shapely, muscular legs wrap around his head and pull him forward! The Jester puts as much weight as he can in his legs and thrashes about, desperately trying to keep his balance while breaking Moonlight’s grip! But he seems to be edging to the great beyond closer… . . . And closer… . . . And closer… until… Jeff: NOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!
