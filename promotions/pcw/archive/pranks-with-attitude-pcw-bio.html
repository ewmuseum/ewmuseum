Pranks With Attitude Biography Fed Titles 5 time Tag Team Champions (defeated Duzza + Makonie on Turmoil, 24th September 2002, lost to Playaz Inc, reclaimed them on the 29th October 2002 for a record breaking 53 day run, where Xavier Kannon stripped them. Gaz and Xavier won the titles on December Biography The duo of Gaz Green and Nerezza have been colleagues since their days in Primetime's academy. 'Such a great place, we stole the name!' cackles Gaz. The 'Emerald Menace' himself supplies the Pranks in this team, while the less jolly 'Living Nightmare' that calls himself Nerezza is his twisted backup. Their personalites almost contradict each other. Gaz is the forever-smiling, jovial man with a somewhat dark sense of humour and represents organised chaos while Nerezza is the permanently angry, solemn and melancholy Attitude half of the team that keeps the chaotic order. Despite their individual differences, never before has a more stable and teamwork based pair stepped forward. Their minds tick in synchronisation and their goal is pure mayhem. Nerezza is accused of feeding more on the negative emotion than the blood he biologically needs while Gaz is said to have never cracked a joke that didn't result in the downfall of another. With an underlying sense of sadism and the facade of entertainment, it would seem that Pranks With Attitude's commital to The Asylum will only cause more mental distress on PCW... If only. After being the hired guns for Miyagawa and Anarchy for so long with no backup, Gaz and Nerezza turned on Miyagawa and rammed his head into a wall, fracturing his skull and ending his career. Pranks With Attitude stands alone, devious and demented... Fed Awards Greatest overall Tag Team of 2002, Best team in PCW history (as voted by EW24/7 Magazine readers) Other Feds NTICW, PWA Wrestling Style All Rounder Finisher Name The Darker Side Of Comedy Finisher Description Nerezza hoists the victim up for a Crucifix Powerbomb. Gaz ascends the top turnbuckle, then leaps off and executes the Blockbuster Neckbreaker while Nerezza performs the Crucifix Powerbomb simultaneously Setup Move Instruments Of Destruction Setup Move Description Gaz and Nerezza whip the opponent to the ropes and hop diagonally back. As he returns, the two rush forward and catch him with a double shoulder block. Note that the impact points are the opponent's sides. Move Set The Committal: More of a post-match humiliation. One member restrains the victim while the other goes outside and fetches a straitjacket. Once the victim has been secured in it, Gaz and Nerezza will perform their 'Seen Green' and 'Violet Violence' moves respectively, then execute their setup move, Instruments Of Destruction. Sometimes they further the humiliation by following up with their tag finisher, The Darker Side Of Comedy. Head Shot: Simply a Double Brain-Buster. Nakobi Dance: Each wrestler faces the opponent and grabs one of their hands. They then kick the victim's legs apart with one foot, then both use their other foot to score a low blow. The Guilty Gear: Double Under-hook, holding the opponent horizontal both members simultaneously rotate forward creating a Face-Buster. Head In The Gutter: The victim lies on the mat, face up. Nerezza grips them for the classic catapult toss and as the opponent sails back, Gaz grabs them and executes a hair-grab facedriver (X-Factor) Entrance Music Basement Jaxx - Where's Your Head At?
