Kramer Wild Wing Biography Real Name Kramer Rinc Nicknames The Wild One Hometown Tampa Bay Florida Birthday March 21, 1969 Height 6'2 Weight 221 lbs Biography APPEARANCE: Facial Features: An oval shaped head, medium sized ears, a small nose, no facial hair, thick brown eyebrows, brown eyes, short brown hair (goes to the nape of the neck and is wavy), and a smile showing some teeth (if possible, if not don't worry about it). Body: Some muscles here and there but not bulging. He has semi hairy arms, but no hair on his chest. His skin is a tan like color. Clothing: Around his head is a white plastic band that has one multi-colored feather in the back. Kramer wears no shirt. His pants are made of black leather, and are baggy. Holding his pants up is a white belt with 'KFC' written all over it in red letters.----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- It was March 21, 1969 when Walter and Kateri Rinc conceived little Kramer Marshall Rinc. He weighed in at eight pounds and six ounces. They instantly got to take him home because he was a healthy bouncing baby boy. The Rinc family started off really nice. The little baby brought more meaning to the family, and both Kateri and Walter's lives were devoted towards him twenty four seven. It was really rare to see the three apart. As he grew up they noticed that nothing really was outstanding about him. He made average grades in school, played different sports such as baseball and basketball, and was just like any other normal American teenager. The one thing he always had going for him though was his humor. He could make just about anyone laugh just by looking at them (and no not because he was funny looking). One fateful day the once really happy family was torn apart from the inside. After going to the doctors for a yearly check up, Kateri found that she had brain cancer, and that it had progressed so much that they wouldn't be able to operate. This devestated the whole family. Kateri underwent chemotherapy, and day by day she just grew weeker. This caused lots of arguments between the family which caused Kramer to drift farther and farther away from the world. And you couldn't blame him, because thats a lot to take on as a fifteen year old boy. It was exactly one year later that things really got sour. One day after waking up Kramer had gone into the kitchen to get some breakfast. Normally his father would be sitting at the table ready to harp on him for no reason at all, but he wasn't there. Kramer, being a tad worried searched all around the house. The blow came when he checked his parents room. The closets were cleaned out and the car was gone. He come to find out that all of this pressure had gotten to his father so he fled. So there Kramer was, a sixteen year old boy, with no father, and a dying mother. His life started taking a downward spiral as he barely made passing grades in school. On April 16, 1985 Kateri Rinc had lost her battle. After Kramer went to her funeral he just gave up on life and looked for answers in the bad things of life. He had gone from being in a loving family to being a drop out and living on the streets, until one day when he met up with a guy by the name of Zachariah Doanz. Zachariah was Kramer's answer out of his problems. He got Kramer to finish his final two years of high school and on the side trained him as a wrestler, something Kramer had always dreamed of doing. Sometimes Kramer thought that Zachariah was his guardian angel, because he had come out of practically nowhere, and if it wasn't for him Kramer might not have been alive much longer. After training for several years Zachariah managed to get Kramer into Just Electrifying Wrestling where he was a lowcarder, but still got to live out his dream. After JEW flopped Kramer found a new home in the form of Primetime Championship Wrestling. There he had more oppertunity and since it was a bigger corporation he felt that he was worth more. Then one fateful day while visiting a hospital to check up on a friend Kramer had run into his father! At first he was confused on how to feel after all of these years without knowing how he was, and if he were still alive. And even though the thought of his dad leaving him when he needed him most was embedded into his memory, he welcomed him home with open arms and thus their family had been reunited. His father gave him extra strength to better in his wrestling skills. Kramer left PCW once to look elsewhere but soon found that PCW was his true home so he came back. A few months passed and he was released but soon enough he was back in again. During his off and on PCW career he had found success in being a promoter for KFC, Wild Wings. And ever since then his father and Zachariah have been at home pursuing other things, while Kramer's new manager, the infamous Jason Alexander took things over. Nowadays you can find Kramer with a smiling face. He may be a lowcarder but with all that he has been through he still manages to stay positive hoping one day that he makes a huge break for the top.
