Primetime Championship Wrestling — Rules The rules of this Primetime Championship Wrestling are very simply, and have gone unchanged since the fed's inception in September of 2000. Have Fun First and foremost, have fun. If you are not having fun here, then communicate this to a staff member to see if anything can be done to remedy the situation. Be Responsible If you take on the responsibility to write matches, segments, or whatever, then do your best to complete the task. "No showing" as we like to call it here in PCW-land is the bane of efeds, and frankly, will not be tolerated. This is also a hobby of where the grass is always greener "in the other fed." If you feel the need to quit, that's ok, but know that if you are involved in angles or feuds, that we reserve the right to use your character to finish the current cycle involving your character. Be Respectful to Others Flaming, bickering, whining, bitching and crying 'backstage' are not tolerated. The fun is sapped out of the game should people begin backstabbing and quibbling with each other. Character Exclusivity In order to keep PCW's unique, we like to make sure the personalities of PCW are only wrestling in PCW. Occasionally, we will make exceptions to this rule for storyline purposes, but not often. Have Fun Yes, this was already mentioned, but it bears repeating.
