March Madness 2001 4 Mar 2001 The Border Dome, San Diego, CA The CEO's setup at the limo The show opens by fading into the parking lot of the Border Dome in San Diego, California as a long, white limousine pulls into the parking lot.  The limo comes to a halt and bushy haired, mustached chauffeur steps of the driver's side door and quickly heads to the rear of the vehicle. Showtime begins clutching and clawing at his eyes, and in a sudden blur of activity, The PCW Tag Team Champion Media Assassins, PCW Television Champion Jon Kano, PCW President Chet King, PCW Continental Champion and PCW Commission Dan Hollywood enter the picture and begin laying waste to Mr. Showtime. The chauffeur and J-Lo step back while the five members of Media punch and kick at the blinded and defenseless Showtime.  Showtime valiantly tries to fight back, but is just a matter of seconds before the five Media members have him down on the ground and begin stomping away on him.
