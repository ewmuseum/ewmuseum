Meanstreak falls back against a door built into the wall. Reverend seizes the moment and pulls the monster back, smacking him with the golden object again. He holds it above his head, revealing it to be a golden bible. Meanstreak roars in pain as Reverend takes another spray with the perfume at him. The preacher man opens the door and shoves the monster into the small prayer room. His head smacks against the low hanging light bulb and smashes it into millions of pieces. He groans again as Reverend whips the bottle into the room. It smashes atop Streak's head, drenching him in the liquid. He seems to be out as Hull slams the door shut. Out of the back of the church runs a man similar to Reverend in height. He is clad in a white robe that completely covers his face. Reverend turns to him and watches as the druid falls forward, tripping over the material of his robe, and smacks his head into the floor. Reverend: "GET UP AND HELP ME WITH THESE!" The preacher man runs over to one of the pews and grabs it by end. The man in the robe climbs to his feet and runs over to help him. He trips once more and smashes his face into the seat of the pew. Reverend: "You moron!! GET UP!!" Hull helps the man to his feet and he takes a hold of the pew. The two push it over in front of the prayer room. They head to the next behind it and do the same. After about four or five pews are stacked atop each other, Reverend stands back, the man in the robe now gone. He catches his breath as a sudden slam comes to the door. Meanstreak roars from his wooden prison and drills his fist through the top of the room's door. He roars, unable to free himself. A moment passes and Reverend slowly approaches the prayer room. Reverend: "Look who's buried now..." (FADE TO BLACK)
