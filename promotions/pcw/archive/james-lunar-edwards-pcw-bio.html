James 'Lunar' Edwards Biography Real Name James Edwards Nicknames Spaceman Hometown Manchester England Birthday 21st August 1974 Height 6' 1 Weight 210 lbs Biography Appearance:Wrestlers Attire: Shoes: Black wrestling boots with a few stars on both of them Trousers: Silver MC Hammer style parachute pants with the word ‘STELLAR’ in gold down the left leg Torso: James wrestles topless showing his well-defined physique, which he acquired through rigorous training at NASA. Face: Always smiling showing his white teeth. His face is long and slim, his nose short and stubby. He has green eyes and light wispy brown eyebrows. He is also clean-shaven. Hair: Short brown hair, which is spiked up and contains blond tints. James’ very first memory is of sitting on a beach at night with his parents looking up at the stars. The sound of waves crashing on rocks in the background. It is his first and happiest memory and from that moment on James knew that he had to get there someday. Aged 22 Graduation Day: James comes one step closer to fulfilling his dream. Standing in his ceremonial gown outside Loughborough University clutching his astrophysics degree with pride. Once more it was an image of happy families with his family by his side, this time however there is another member a younger sister Emily who is looking up at her older brother with pride much as she always had done. Tucked away inside her back pocket is her acceptance letter to the same university and the very same course. Aged 26: Graduation Day Part II: Emily’s big day in the spotlight. Once more it is a picturesque family scene with a close-knit family unit. It was here that James’ opportunity happened to occur. A chance telephone call had given James the chance to live his dream he was going to N.A.S.A he was going into space. As family and friends gathered around James Emily was left alone, alone on her big day. It was at this point she realised she would always be living in her brothers shadow. Jan 5th 2003 That Fateful Day: The captain of the flight had been predetermined there was just one seat available on the launch and it was down to two candidates. James and another who had wished to remain anonymous. James could tell by the look on the captain’s face that the news was bad. He had not made it for the first time in his life he had not come first yet there was a pleasing result in store. The other person he knew very well because he loved her as a sister. They had favoured Emily over James that in itself made the hurt James felt subside, as it’s better a dream shared than a dream lost. April 2003: His training has taken him as far as he can go, getting ready for his first match at Tuesday Turmoil on the 29th of April against three other men...a baptism of fire for the Spaceman.
