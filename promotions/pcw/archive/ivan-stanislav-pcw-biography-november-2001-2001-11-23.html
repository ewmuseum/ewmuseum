Ivan Stanislav — PCW biography (November 2001)

23 November 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Praporschik Ivan Sergeiovich Stanislav Real Name: Ivan Stanislav Nick Name: The Russian Bear The Soviet Machine Catchphrase: I am unstoppable!! Character Handler: Brian H. MSN Account: AIM Address: Theme Song: The Soviet National Anthem Age: 39 From: Arkhangelsk, Russia Height: 7’1” Weight: 400 lbs. Wrestling Style: Powerhouse Signature Moves: One Handed Scoop Slam Two handed lifting choke Backbreaker Chokeslam Jumping Piledriver Powerbomb Super Powerbomb One Handed Military Press Standing Surfboard Shoulderbreaker Short Arm Clothesline Bear Hug Finisher Move: The Red Scare- Vertical Suplex released at vertical position. Usually throwing opponents into objects. Set-Up Move: The Iron Curtain- Lifts opponent with one hand by the head, and drops them, hitting them with a short arm clothesline and sending them flying across the ring. Manager: Alexei Ruslan Height: 5’9” Weight: 185 lbs. Current Team: The Red Army Past Teams & Groups: Title History: PCW World Champion Current (11-30-01 - ?) PCW Continental Champion (Eurasia) Awards History: 3 Time National Pride Winner (Russia) Description: Standing an imposing 7’1” feet tall and weighing a daunting 400 lbs, Ivan Stanislav is a force to be reckoned with. Ivan Stanislav has dark black hair that is cut in a military fashion high and tight on his head. Piercing brown eyes that do very little to hide the rage that always pumps through his veins. A square jaw that is set hard into his face. He possesses a huge barrel chest that sticks far from his body, a huge neck, and a solid stomach. His shoulders are extremely broad. Compared to his enormous frame, Ivan Stanislav’s legs are comparably smaller, but still capable of great power. While not necessarily “ripped” like most wrestlers of his size, Ivan is more “solid” than anything else. His weight is mainly muscle, which gives him a seemingly unchartable amount of strength. In the ring, Ivan wears a black t-shirt with a red hammer and sickle over the heart. A pair of black pants with two vertical stripes running up either leg and black leather boots. A trademark of Stanislav is his red suspenders which he always wears wherever he goes. Outside of the ring, Stanislav wears the same type of clothing, adhering to the type of rigid living that men living in the Soviet Union were subjected to. History: Born in December, 1961, Ivan Stanislav could very well be the oldest wrestler within the PCW roster today. But with age comes experience, and a knowledge of history that could be rivaled by any one of his opponents. Stanislav was born into a military family with his father being a high ranking officer within the Soviet Military. Early within his life, Stanislav was gifted with tremendous strength, size, height, and muscular weight. At an early age Ivan began to wrestle for the Soviet Union, fighting people roughly twice his age. By the age of eight, Stanislav was already becoming famous within the national circuit of Russian wrestling. When the war in Afghanistan broke out between the Soviet Union and Afghanistan, Ivan was one of the first men to invade the country. During the campaign, Ivan was able to use his considerable physical strength and tactical ability to help lead Red Army soldiers to several victories. During the campaign within Afghanistan, Stanislav met Alexei Ruslan, a then KGB Agent, and a powerful friendship between the two men was formed. Ruslan, who was quickly rising the ladder of Soviet Intelligence, had a plan for the massive Russian that he would not implement for many years later. . . . After Afghanistan, Stanislav became a national hero. Perfect for a propaganda spreader as being a “huge, unstoppable, and unbeatable hero of the Soviet Union.” He was also promoted to the position of “Praporschik,” which is the equivalent of the American Warrant Officer. During this time, Stanislav focused more on wrestling and promoting the Soviet Union and the communist ideal, something that he holds very dear to himself. Training in the wasteland of Siberia with virtually no protective clothing, Stanislav developed a way to withstand an almost insurmountable amount of punishment without feeling a thing. . . .which has made him only more unstoppable and powerful than before. With the collapse of the Soviet Union in 1991, Stanislav returned to his Motherland and focused on military matters in an attempt to rebuild The Russian Red Army. During this time, Alexei Ruslan, who now was a high ranking member of the child of the KGB, the Russian Federal Security Service, began to implement a plan to not only promote his country, the military, and Russia as a world power, but also secretly promote the communist ideal in an attempt to return to the old Soviet Regime. Ten years later, the plan was approved and Ruslan approached Stanislav with his plan. The orders were simple: Send a soldier over to the United States of America and fight publicly in wrestling matches. Promote the Russian Federation as well as the power of the Russian people. The money that is won is to be sent back to the military to fund the program and use propaganda to the greatest degree possible. The plan was set into motion, and Stanislav and Ruslan arrived to the PCW in early May of 2001. After quickly racking up wins and setting up a group of loyal Communists known as “The Red Army,” Stanislav and Ruslan have run roughshod over the ranks of the PCW in an attempt to collectivize and communize the Primetime Championship Wrestling Federation. Few thought the plan would be successful, but the wily Ruslan and The Russian Bear have continued to dominate and decimate all that have dared to stand in their way. As of late, Ivan Stanislav has cut back on his control of The Red Army after an implosion from within. Through this, The Red Army has changed from a pure physical group to a group of dedicated individuals who establish their beliefs not through force, but through guile instead.
