Brianna Washington Biography Real Name Brianna Washington Nicknames The Gadget Hometown Little Rock Arkansas Birthday 7/2/75 Height 5'8 Weight 138 lbs Biography Ever since she was a young child she had loved to build things. Whether it was as simple as a model airplane, or as complex as a car engine, she loved it. Realizing that her job must allow her to do what she loved, she signed up with Wonder Toys, joining the creative team designing toys for children of all ages. She created remote control cars that did flips and jumps and talking dolls which ultimately earned her a quick promotion. But the higher up she got, and the more work that was piled on, she started to slowly realize that this wasn't what she was supposed to be doing. Brianna had believed that this wasn't stretching her abilities, only bottling them. She needed to help make bigger and better things, and this just wasn't the place to get it done. So where could she go? After searching through tons of classifieds and asking around for several months, she found her answer. NASA was hiring for people to work on the rockets. Brianna was so excited, and went over there as soon as she had the chance. To her dismay, they wouldn't hire her because she didn't have the required background and training. She left the building and walked and walked, mad at the world. As she was walking she wasn't paying attention to where she was going and soon found herself trapped in the middle of the woods. At the time it may have seemed terrible, but this mistake guided her to a career that she would love for the rest of her life, wrestling! 'Hey you lost little lady?' A ragged man said as he approached Brianna from behind. Before he even gave her a chance to answer he pounced on her with much force. Brianna instantly started screaming, fearing for her life. The man grabbed her around the waist and turned her to face him while still staying on top. 'Me and you are gonna have some fun.' The man had leaned forward to plant a kiss on her face but something came over Brianna and she managed to push the guy off even though he was two times bigger than her. He tried to charge at her again and she moved out of the way, kicked him in the jewels, and then ran off. She had reported it to the police, but the guy was never found. That little incident opened up a part of Brianna she never knew existed. For a few brief seconds she was in charge and was loving it. After much research into the sport of wrestling, Brianna just couldn't get enough of it. She wanted to learn and perfect as many moves as possible, and make this wrestling thing work. For the next two years she spent hours upon hours at the gym with a personal trainer, while still creating things on her free time. That is Brianna's life thus far, but there is a long road still ahead. It may take a while to get people to notice, but they will, even if it damn near kills her trying.
