"We're The Blood Brothers and we're piss drunk and pissed off!" Character Handler: Gorman R. MSN Account: [detail withheld] AIM Address: [detail withheld] Theme Song: Single: "Revoultion is My Name" By Pantera Blood Brothers: "Blood Brothers" by Papa RoachAge: mid-20'sFrom:Milwaukee, Wi.Height: 6'3 Weight: 230 Wrestling Style: Technical/High Flyer Signature Moves: Superkick, Corkscrew Moonsualt, Frog Splask, Missle Dropkick, Inverted STF, Indian Deathlock, Mexican Surfboard, Bulldog, Flying Forearm, Hurracarrana,Piledriver, Stall Suplex, Side Russuian Legsweep, German SUplex, Liger Suplex 85' Finisher Move: Sky-Twister Press (Reverse Corkscrew Moonsault off the tope rope) Set-Up Move: The Old Style DDT (Brock's own variation of a reverse diving DDT) Current Team: Blood Brothers (w/Stephen Blood) Past Teams & Groups: Old School (w/Ed, Reverend, Miyagawa and Trashcan Man) Outcasts (w/Miyagawa, Stephen Blood, Jon Kano, Putter Mayhem and Jack McGriff)NUO (Death Dealer and some other guys he can't remember from MWF) Title History: 4-Time PCW Tag Team Champion(w/Stephen Blood) and PCW Extreme Champion Awards History: Top Tag Team of 2000 (The Blood Brothers w/Stephen Blood) Voted Favorite Wrestler by members of the Betty Ford Clinic Description: Brock has short spiked up brown hair. He has deep crystal blue eyes and a chin goatee. Sometimes his is cleanly shaven while other times he has a five o clock shadow. He has a fairly chiseled physique, and a atomic bomb type hair on his chest that runs to a line down to his belly button. A tattoo of a dragon is on his right bicep and a of the sword “Excalibur” on his left bicep. He ring garb consists of black wrestling tight/pants. With “Innovator” written in red on the back of his tights in a dripping blood design. He wears a pair of plain black wrestling boots. The same boots he’s had since he started wrestling. When outside of the ring, he can almost always be seen wearing his trademark black leather Harley jacket. History: Not much was known about the man they call “The Innovator” before he entered the Millenium Wrestling Federation in early September of 2000. He was a drifter who bounced around on the Japan and Mexico scene, never staying in one place for a extended period of time. In Mexico, he earned the nickname “The Innovator” by putting on great matches night after night for only the minimal of wage. The name was set in stone when he first preformed his self-taught finisher, The Skytwister Press in his final match before entering the MWF. He looked at the MWF as a chance to make some money in the sport he loves and quickly jumped on board with the federation. Brock’s first match in an American ring was against now PCW world champion Frisco. Though he put out a 110% effort he fell short in then end and lost. Then, MWF introduced the newly crafted Rising Star title, and Brock and Frisco would do battle for out. This time, Brock came out on top making his the first ever MWF Rising Star champion. He would later go on to become the MWF Rising Star champ one more time. A few weeks after Brock moved up to the main circuit of the MWF he scored a win over TV champ Thalokos, making him the new TV champ. The TV win, also caused the one of the biggest feuds in MWF/PCW history, one with the late Judas Steele. Steele wanted the title, and would get it by any means necessary. Then, Steele raised the level of the feud, by kidnapping Brock’s girlfriend and MWF reporter, Tiffany Jacobs. Brock debuted in PCW at its first PPV, Dawning. He debuted by punking out PCW’s newly crowned TV champ Revenant and challenging him to a match for his title. Brock never got the chance to face the cave dweller , for he was sent to jail the night of their match. His replacement…Brock’s arch nemesis, Judas Steele, who still had Tiffany. Judas beat Brock after interference by his bodyguard Bruno. A match was set between to end the war. A triple cage, Hell in a Cell match, with Tiffany locked in a cage on top of the other two. The stakes were high for the match, if Brock won, he got Tiffany’s freedom, if he lost, he would never wrestle in a PCW ring again. The match was a bloodbath filled with high spots and dangerous maneuvers. It could be one of the most emotional filled matches in PCW history. Both men fought with all of their hearts and then some. The match came to a end when Brock reversed a Steele powerbomb into a X-Factor and freed Jacobs. As Brock embraced his girlfriend, a most drastic change happened. As Brock urged Tiffany on to kick the downed Steele, Tiffany turned and kicked Brock in the junk, to the shock of fans and everybody else watching. Steele, Jacobs and Bruno preceded to beatdown the exhausted Newbludd as the fans threw garbage in the ring. The turn on Jacobs brought a new twist to the heated rivalry, no longer did Brock have to fight for someone else, he fought for revenge. Judas and his new partner Cain Manson Crow won the tag straps soon after, but the reign did not last long. Soon thereafter, to the shock of the wrestling world, Judas Steele died of a drug overdose in a unknown location. Brock was put into a deep depression by the loss of his arch-rival. But, the loss of Judas would bring about the rejuvenation of Newbludd’s career. For, the Tag Team titles were vacated and at the funeral of Steele, the tag titles were up for grabs. Somewhere in the graveyard, the two tag belts were waiting for their new owners. As Brock opened up Judas’s casket to place a beer in his casket, he did not find a dead corpse, but a PCW tag team belt. The other winner of the belt, was Brock’s best friend in PCW, newcomer Stephen Blood. So, on a day of mourning and sadness the Blood Brothers were born and the PCW tag scene has never been the same. Brock and Stephen managed to score the tag belts, a unprecedented four times. Beating such teams as Sex and Violence, Da Wiseguys and the Media Assassins. The Blood Brothers will go down into the annuls of PCW history as probably the greatest tag team to grace a PCW ring. “The Innovators” future on PCW looks very bright. He recently scored his first PCW singles gold by beating “The Mack” Eddie for the PCW Extreme title. But, then lost it to his best friend and tag team partner, “The New Legend” Stephen Blood. Brock then shortly thereafter retired and has comeback a couple of times, once with a stint in “Old School” to battle Ivan Stanislavs Red Army. But, he was quickly cut short by a kidney injury. Now, Brock is back with Stephen and they are ready to take back the throne that is rightly their’s, the PCW tag team titles. .
