Flaymin Afro Shaving — PCW in-card segment

March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Flaymin Afro Shaving Written: March 2002 [El Diablo is walking backstage, and comes across a door that appears to have no label on it and a buzzing coming from the inside.] Dave: What is he doing? Jeff: He's going to investigate. Dave: But most doors here aren't labelled. Duke: There is a buzzing sound too, hoss. [He opens the door and sees that it is the womens locker room. Only Sandy Wong is in there however, and she is standing looking into a mirror. The buzzing is coming from an electric razor that she is using on her arm pits.] Dave: Ewwwwwwwwwwwwwwwwwwwwww!!! Duke: Ewwwwwwwwwwwwwwwwwwwwww!!! Jeff: Ewwwwwwwwwwwwwwwwwwwwwwwwwwwwwwwwwwwww!!! Dave: That is vile. Duke: Why did El Diablo have to make us suffer this torture? [She turns around and sees El diablo.] Sandy: Get out, pervert. El Diablo: Sorry, um wrong room. [He closes the door behind him, with a look of disgust on his face as the scene fades out. -------------------------------------------------- [Flaymin is shown backstage slouching against a couch and watching TV. Surrounded by dozens of empty beer bottles, Flaymin scratches his head and lets out a loud burp. After a moment of silence and with his stomach heaving heavily, he manages to slur out a sentence] Flaymin: Jenna... where’s the damn beer? This Dr Quinn Medicine Woman 48 hour marathon is almost over and I’m not nearly intoxicated enough. [The door flings open and Jenna walks in with two cases of beer in her hand. Struggling to carry them, she begins to weave all over the room as she nears closer to Flaymin. Drunkenly, Flaymin raises his hand to grab a case of beer. However he misses the case completely and the beer bottles end up slamming into his cranium. Concussed, Flaymin just slumps to the floor in an unconscious state while Jenna looks on and shrugs her shoulders.] Jeff: He’s having more fun back there then we are out here! Duke: Dammit hoss, you’re missing the point- all that alcohol and no one sober to drink it! -------------------------------------------------- [Backstage, El Diablo is again walking down a corridor and sees Flaymin Aymin's locker room.] Jeff: Haha, Afro boy is passed out. Duke: But why are we following El Diablo then? Dave: There's got to be some reason. [Curiosity finally takes over El Diablo, who just has to take a peek in the locker room. He opens the door slowly and takes a look, he sees Flaymin passed out on the ground with beer cans surrounding him. He opens the door fully and walks in.] Dave: What's he doing? Duke: You got me stumped, hoss. Jeff: That's not difficult. SMACK!!!! [El Diablo walks up to the body of Flaymin Aymin and kicks him a few times to see if he'll awake. He smiles and heads back to the door.] El Diablo: This will be too good. Jeff: What's he talking about, Dave? Dave: I don't have a clue, but please no more Gary Coleman inpressions. [El Diablo opens the door and gets ready to leave.] El Diablo: This will get him back for last Turmoil. [He grins evilly and walks away, heading towards somewhere.] Dave: What's he got up his sleeve? -------------------------------------------------- [Backstage and El Diablo is seen leavin the womens locker room with something in in a bag.] Dave: Where is El Diablo heading? Duke: What has he got in the bag? Jeff: Womens locker room- NIPPAGE! Dave: Shut up, Jeff. [El Diablo rounds a corner and peeks in again to Flaymin's locker room. The coast is clear so he walks in.] Dave: So this is what he was talking about? Jeff: Duh! [El Diablo again kicks the downed Aymin to make sure he is completely passed out. He kneels down and pulls something out from the bag.] Jeff: OH MY GOD! He's got a razor! Dave: What’s El Diablo going to with the razor? Duke: I think he’s going to. Nah that can’t be. [El Diablo turns on the razor and sets it to it’s maximum speed. With the room now held victim to the razor’s intense buzzing sound, El Diablo picks up Flaymin’s head off the ground and grabs a piece of his hair] Dave: OH MY GOD!!!! Jeff: I DON’T BELIEVE THIS!!!!!! [El Diablo looks over his shoulder one more time to see if anyone is coming. With the coast clear, he turns back to Flaymin’s afro and takes one last look at PCW’s Premier Afro. Swiftly he buries the razor into Flaymin’s bushy hair and begins to mow down the afro.] Duke: Noooooooooooooooooooooo!!!!!!! Jeff: WAKE UP YOU IDIOT, WAKE UP!!!! Dave: Flaymin is out cold and there is no one to help!!! [El Diablo continues to shear off Flaymin’s hair. He shaves off the entire top and begins to work on the sides. Bit by bit, the hair begins to flutter down to the ground into an ever-growing pile of curly hair] Jeff: This is as shocking as when Sampson cut Delilah’s hair!! Duke: Isn’t it the other way round? Jeff: I don’t know, I only saw the XXX version. You should see it, they ended up shaving more then just her hair if you know what I mean! Dave: Dear God. [El Diablo finishes off shaving Flaymin’s head. He turns off the razor and throws it on the floor. Bald and still passed out, Flaymin lies on the ground surrounded by a pool of his own hair] Dave: This is surreal. Jeff: I haven’t seen anything this shocking since the ‘Evil JJ’ concept. [Duke stands up and bows his head. He places his right hand over his heart] Duke: Rest In Peace, Flaymin’s afro. [He looks at Dave and Jeff who are still seated] Duke: Stand up you heathens and show respect for this fallen hair!! I demand PCW undertake a One Minute Silence!! [Dave and Jeff reluctantly stand up and place their hands on their heart] -------------------------------------------------- Dave: We’ve received some shocking news! Flaymin Aymin is waking up, I repeat, Flaymin Aymin is waking up. Jeff: I never thought I’d say this but this I gotta see!! Duke: Enough talking! The hairless wonder is awakening!! [Flaymin is shown waking up backstage. Slowly he opens his eyes and lets out a yawn. Sluggishly he gets to his feet and looks at all the hair scattered on the floor] Dave: Here it comes!! Flaymin: Damn Jenna. Out of all the places to shave her legs. Jeff: Huh? What was that? [Flaymin raises his hand and rubs his eyes. He then uses the same hand to scratch his. Bald head...] Duke: Ah ha! This is it ! [Flaymin remains unfazed having just scratched his bald head.] Dave: What is wrong with this fool?? Duke: He’s drunk! He couldn’t tell shit from sugar. Jeff: That’s easy. One is black and the other is whi... Duke: Jeff, this ain’t the time to sing Michael Jackson songs! [Meanwhile, Flaymin walks over to the dressing table and picks up his ‘fro comb’. He stands in front of the mirror and begins to brush his shaved head] Flaymin: What the hell??? Jeff: Ha ha, it’s showtime! Flaymin: My skin looks silky smooth and I don’t remember putting any moisturiser on today. Duke: Pardon my French, but what the fuck? Hey where did Jeff go? Dave: Beats me. [Flaymin continues to brush his bald head in front of the mirror. Suddenly there is a knock on the door and Jeff Marx walks into the room] Dave: What is he doing?? Duke: I don’t know, looking for a free hair cut? [Flaymin stops brushing his hair and gives Jeff an odd stare] Flaymin: You got a problem? Jeff: YOU ARE BALD!!!!! YOU GOT NO ‘FRO!!!!!! [Flaymin looks into the mirror and instantly goes pale. His eyes pop wide open as he begins to breathe heavily. Next, his jaw drops wide open and he starts rubbing his hands all over his smooth head. Flaymin: AAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAARRRRRRRRRRGGGGGGGGGGGGGGGGGGGGGHHHHHHHHHHHHH!!!!! Jeff: It’s alright I know how you feel. [Flaymin takes one look at Jeff and then begins to yell some more as the scene fades to black] View El Diablo 's Biography
