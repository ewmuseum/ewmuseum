The Refugees Entrance Music: Freedom Fighter by Creed Status: Heel Manager(s): None as of Yet Tag Finisher: In the Name of Allah (Ali delievers three powerbombs to their opponent and Qaboos then does a five star frog splash for the pin.) Ali bin Ahmed bin Saleh Al-Fulani Hometown: Kabul,Afghanistan Height: 6'5 Weight: 190lbs. lbs. Qaboos bin Said bin Taimur Al-Busaid Hometown: Kabul,Afghanistan Height: 6'2 Weight: 180lbs. lbs. Signature Moves: Double Underhook Powerbomb,Complesxion(Double Diamond Cutter) Set Up Move: Nope (Nope ) PCW Title History: Nothing PCW Award History: Nothing Other Federations: PWA,XWA,GW,ProWC Other Federation Awards: PWA Top Student #3(1x),XWA Tag Champion(1x) Handler Information Handler Name: Zach H. AIM Name: [detail withheld] MSN Name: [detail withheld] Bio Ali Ali looks a bit older than most men of his age.His face is a bit worn out and he looks like a man of his late forties.He always has an emotionless yet thoughtful expression on his face.His arms are usually down at his side and he stands very straight.He has short black hair that is parted down the middle.He wears a tan turban over his hair.His eyes are light blue and his just above his right cheek is a small yet blatant scar.He wears a camoflague style tank top and pants.He also wears long black heavy boots. Qaboos Qaboos looks younger but still not like he is twenty five.He has a confident smile on his face.His arms are folded and he looks easy going and is leaned back.His hair is black and very short,it is almost never seen because of his blue turban which extends over his hair down to his neck.To tie his turban to his head Qaboos uses barbed wire which he occasionaly uses to interfere in matches.Often it will cut his face and leave scars.Many tiny scars are on his face,none though are as large as Ali's.Qaboos wears no shirt.Around his neck he always wears a gold chain with the letter 'N' on it.He wears dark jeans and a black belt along with black boots.
