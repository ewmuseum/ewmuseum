Meanstreak Biography Biography Meanstreak. The single most physically and mentally dominating force in PCW history. Since Ethan Giroux's arrival within the confines of PCW, the monster they call Meanstreak has made his mark numerous times with usual disastrous conclusions. It all began with the first card that Primetime Championship Wrestling held on a Friday Fusion show debuting September 22nd, 2000. The monster made his first appearance, rising from the crowd and giving his signature Reverse DVD (commonly know as 'At Death's Door' or ADD for short) to both the Third Degree and his soon-to-be rival, Kremmen Von Smackdown. This action surged into one of the biggest, although shortlived, feuds in PCW history. At the first Pay-Per-View 'PCW Dawning' falling on October 22nd of 2000, Meanstreak met with Doug Troy, The Reverend Jon Hull, and Kremmen in the finals of the first 64 man World Title Tourney. After losing the match to Kremmen immediately following a shock to the ribs by a ccattle prod, the beast met up with him only five days later in a return match with the title on the line. Riding a bull on his way to the ring, which gouged the already sore and battered Kremmen in the ribs, Meanstreak gave Kremmen his sick and twisted version of a cattle prod. The match went on to last for nearly twenty minutes, this time with Meanstreak holding the title over his head. Two weeks later, on yet another Fusion, both the World and Extreme Titles were put on the line in a four way match that went beyond the bounds of anything the PCW audience had ever witnessed. Facing the Druid, Ryan Knox, and Doug Troy, the beast was set to take them all out in one swift motion. What he didn't count on was the yellow humvee that would mow him down later that evening. As the Main Event progressed, Knox and Streak were the two last men standing. With the prone body of 'The Future' set across his shoulders, the monster was ready to deliver the ADD and make it all a successful defense as well as taking the Extreme Title around his waist. Roaring from backstage and obviously hellbent on taking the monster out, the driver of the yellow humvee floored it and hit the beast head on, knocking both Knox and Streak well out of it. Doug Troy took the moment and pinned them both, taking the World and Extreme titles to the land of DAMN. Little did he know, even being hit by such a large vehicle as a humvee, couldn't take out The Monster They Call Meanstreak. A week later, he made his return with a newfound rage burning brightly in his eyes. Causing Doug Troy a loss to Homicidal Anthony Cay for his Extreme Title, the monster was ready to find out just who the driver was. Ryan Knox was the main suspect for the driver, so the beast paid him a visit that same night, as well. Once extending his hand out for a generous shake, Meanstreak grabbed 'The Future' and ADDed him straight off the apron after his demands for an answer did not go through. All of this was soon to culminate at the upcoming PPV, 'Alliances'. Being the surprise last member of the Asylum, Meanstreak disregarded all his allies and set to work on doing his dirty work to everyone in the vicinity. Taking out a large number of men from the match, it was left to him, Scott Naket, and the yellow hummer. As Meanstreak was celebrating atop the cage, the hummer peeled out from the back and hit the cage head on, causing it to collapse and to spill the beast twenty feet to the canvas. As the door to the hummer opened, Meanstreak was already beginning to stand. The driver, clad in his yellow jumpsuit and duster mask, climbed the cage wall. Meanstreak watched as the driver executed two complete somersaults, landing atop the beast's head and pulling him across the ring in a hurricanrana. After the attack, the driver slipped off his mask to reveal himself to be Johnny Raindance. After basking in the crowd's shock, JRD began celebrating as Meanstreak again stood. He immediately lunged at the Son of Thunderbird, tearing him from the turnbuckle and burrying him with an ADD. As soon as he stood again, a cunning Scott Naket rolled him up quickly for the 1, 2, 3, securing the victory for his team. Naket turned heel and ran as the monster sat dumbfounded, staring a hole straight through JRD. This was the beginning of something huge. The heated rivalry went on for the following weeks, finding Meanstreak kidnapping the Sioux warrior's valet, trying to crush him with a car, and finally came to a close at the next PPV, 'Decimation'. JRD had fallen into the background at this time and wasn't even near prepared for the main event involving eight of PCW's finest in one of PCW's strangest matches. Kid Wonder, Trashcan Man, Alan Kriegman, and Johnny Raindance were set to take on Doug Troy, The Reverend, Kremmen, and Meanstreak in a match that would put the winning team against eachother in a match for the World Title that following Friday. After what turned out to be a backstage brawl, where Meanstreak was by far the dominant force and JRD was left in a heap the entire time, the monster went on to win with a pinfall over Alan Kriegman. That following Friday, the title was on the line and a brutal match ensued. It led backstage as well and ended with The Reverend and Meanstreak as the last two. In the parking lot of the arena, where the contenders had ventured to, the beast was busy being psychotic and trying to flip an ambulance. The Reverend came from behind, defibulators in hand, and shocked the beast. Streak immediately turned around, swatted the tools from his hand and ADDed him onto the pavement. Covering Hull and getting the 3 count, Meanstreak again was a World Champion. But, at the end of the broadcast, word was reported of a rematch between Hull and Streak set for only a week later. That day came, set in the Faith Baptist Church in Philadelphia where the Reverend pushed the monster out of the second story stained glass window and followed up with a pin. After the match, the disgruntled preacherman locked his opponent up in a small prayer room, barricading it and spraying the beast with a hallucinogenic drug designed to weaken and temporarily paralyze the monster. After a week or so of brainwashing, The Reverend let the beast out, only to have him come to the Fusion that evening and tear nearly every match apart. In the main event between Doug Troy and The Reverend, the monster showed himself again, this time under the name of 'War'. War was a different man than Meanstreak. Much more focused and much calmer, with that rage hiding just beneath the surface. Although this form was almost a step up from Meanstreak's usual demeanor, he sided with the Reverend for merely a week before disappearing into the shadows. Nearly a month later, the beast returned as Francis 'Anguish' Kent's bodyguard. Becoming a disease to Francis and his fiancee, the beast seperated himself from them. Later that week, during a Cruiserweight ladder match which Anguish was wrestling in, the beast showed his face again. The little lunatic was fueled with excitement, thinking the monster was there to help him. That was far from the truth. After a brutal attack, involving Anguish being thrown from the ladder, chokeslammed off the stage, and then dragged to the top of the tron, and chokeslammed off the top of that, the monster was back. After being back for a bit and offered a spot in the recently formed Media, the beast took it upon himself to push himself into the slot of the current and second 64 man World Title tournament. After responding to the offer to join Media, Meanstreak made himself known in the eyes of Tyler Nelson as he came out to give his final decision. Nelson, thinking he had the beast wrapped around his finger, was surprised when Meanstreak nothing short of annihilated him and took the man's spot in the tourney. Although Nelson made his way back into the tourney, it wasn't enough to bring him into the small tournament set at the end of the larger 64 man competition. The final four competitors were Kremmen, Frisco, Kid Wonder, and, of course, Meanstreak. The tourney was long and brutal and found Frisco and Meanstreak facing off in the end with the Reverend Jon Hull playing the role of the special referee. Of course, in typical Reverend fashion, the match ended in a still controversial finish with a quick count and a few interferences to boot. The Congregation formed and the beast slowly began to slip into the background, his drive lost after the match held with Frisco. At the next PPV, Meanstreak was set to face Frisco in a return bout for the PCW World Title. But, as a new flow of wrestlers came in, the match was thrown out, with Frisco remaining the champ. A short stint with those newer wrestlers and a few ADD's later, the monster was back in action and ready to try and capture the gold once more. With a new 'threat' in the form of Stewart Hix and a vacated title, a four way cage match sat firmly on the horizon. In this match, Frisco had returned after being previously stripped of his title. Tyler Nelson was trying to make a bigger impact on the company and make himself a bigger name at the same time. Stewart Hix was also hoping to try and capture some gold to whack off to in his times of need in the hours of the evening. Meanstreak made it almost certain that he was to hold that title once more. But, with a rare turn of events, Nelson left the match, newly crowned champion, and Streak was once again left disappointed. A week later, one more match was to take place for the World Title. A 24/7 rule was enstated, which led to the monster's downfall. Attempting to gain an edge and take the gold early, Meanstreak broke into the millionare's complex and attempted to get at him. But, unbeknownst to him, the local police had been phoned minutes after his arrival. It took nearly 20 policemen to cuff Meanstreak and send him off to the Pittsgrove Federal Penitentiary. Later that night, Stewart Hix won the title. Shortly after, the next PPV, 'Gold and Glory' went into full swing. The beast had been gone for a few weeks and found a new monster in his time off - Ivan Stanislav. At the PPV, the monster sent the Russian Bear the first of many messages to come through the tron, lacking of any physical appearance. Alexei Ruslan and Tom Walczak, two members of the Red Army, were both victims of the beast's return. The monster showed his face and took Ivan on, resulting with the Russian Bear flat on his back in the middle of the ring. Meanstreak was back for Ivan...but he was also back for one more reason - the third 64 mand World Title Tournament. Plowing through his first two opponents, The Arch and Boda, Meanstreak thought he had an easy path to victory. But, during his third round match with Big Alan Steele, the monster suffered from the medical problems he'd been experiencing over the past few weeks. The chronic migraines and constant hallucinations lead to the beast collapsing while attempting an ADD on BAS. He was immediately rushed to a nearby medical facility and pronounced in critical condition. The next day, it was reported that the beast had slipped into a coma. Since then, Meanstreak has remained in this coma and will likely never wrestle again. Although the PCW is without the monster, the memories he left behind will never be lost.
