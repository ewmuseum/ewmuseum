Fusion 122 16 May 2003 National Stadium, Kabul, Afghanistan Tyler Preston guards the backstage door The scene fades to the backstage area of the National Stadium. The camera settles on an empty doorway that leads in to the venue from the outside. The steel door is shut, and has the words ‘Staff Only’ written on it in black letters. The sound of rubber scraping against the cement echoes through the empty area. A man enters from the right, dragging his heavy boots to the floor. The camera raises and it is Tyler Preston with a goofy grin on his face. He is decked out in full PCW security attire. It is unknown how in the world he pulled the strings and scored one of these uniforms, but by the look on his face, he is preparing to have a little fun with it. He swings around the baton used for rowdy crowds and runs his fingers over its smoothness. Tyler drags a steel folding chair from the side where a cluttered mess is stationed. He sets it up right next to the entrance to the building. He sits down with a sigh and puts his hands behind his head. He relaxes with a grin on his face. John Gargano enters from the right, wearing his wrestling attire and looking at Tyler in awe.
