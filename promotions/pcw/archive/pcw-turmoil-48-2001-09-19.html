Turmoil 48 19 Sep 2001 The Big Foot Center, Twin Peaks, Washington In memoriam: Mick Palmer The show opens up to a screen of darkness. Suddenly, a black and white photo of top PWA superstar, Mick Palmer, who wreslted as Semore Smoke fades into the center of the screen as the chilling tones of “Heaven Beside You” from Alice and Chains act as a musical backdrop. After 1....2.....3 seconds, stark white letters appear beneath the still photo reading “Mick Palmer 1985-2001. Gone but not forgotten.” The scene then simply fades to black. The Chef's retirement announcement The usual opening for Tuesday Turmoil, depicting PCW superstars in action upon the backdrop of a ruined and ablaze city begins to roll. Wrestlers highlighted are Badge, Ed, Nikita Morozov, Clyde and The Chef. The scene fades into the Bigfoot Center in Twin Peaks, Washington State, as pyros shoot off from the ceiling, ringposts and entryway and “Nearly Lost You” from Screaming Trees serves as a musical backdrop. Cameras scan around the arena taking samples of the crowd. The first stop for our wandering camera of fame is upon an exceptionally hot and buxom blonde woman wearing nothing more than a fur-lined bikini. She licks her lips and looks into the camera, then holds up a sign which reads “I am Big Foot’s love child.” The camera then pans down, past her ample cleavage to her feet, to reveal her fur covered, size 16 ½ EEE bare feet. Quickly moving away from the image that would disturb even the horniest of all men, the scene shifts to where a group of flannel shirt wearing, unkept looking youths hold up a sign which reads “Drummer: Gone but not forgotten....dude” Shifting gears the camera moves to ringside, where popular TV cooking show hosts Julia Child’s, The Cookin’ Cajun Justin Thibideaux and Martin Yan and Yan Can Cook are shown gleefully holding up autographed 8” x 10” glossy photos of the new PCW World Heavyweight Champion, The Chef. Rapidly and wisely moving away from two alcoholics and a worst case picture of what can go wrong when an Chinese man receives a green card, the scene shifts to the ringside position of the three headed monstrosity that is the PCW announce team.
