Suburban Uprising Biography Tag Team Name Suburban Uprising Biography 'I think i dropped my brother on his head two many times when we were kids, that's why i have a massive IQ and he seems to only have a 3!' laughs Teddy Knight when talking about his dumbstruck but sucessful brother Christopher V Knight, from NTICW fame. Christopher doesn't really no what to say in these situations. 'My brother and me in the young days, i-i-i-i-i don't wanna talk about it!' cries Chris. He will get out that way or another way. 'OWWW MY HEAD!' This is what both of these guys are like, but we can talk about their lives. Both Chris and Teddy were born in 1980, on July 20th. Chris maybe the oldest of the boys, but the dumbest. Having an IQ of possibly 30 he looks up to his younger brother Teddy Graham Knight, who has an IQ of 130. They were good at a young age for some reason both intelligent, both at the top of the class and also great athletes, then the years went on they were involved in what parents called evil, they got into.... 'PUNK MUSIC' 'Punk Music is life' says Chris Knight when liking to talk about this time. 'I'd get my board, my bmx and i'd ride the waves' he says. Only for Teddy to interupt like usually. 'Chris, you are such an ass dude. You can't ride waves on BMX's you can do that on surf boards and surfers suck!'. Teddy and Chris knew they hated a few things. At around the age of 15/16 both boys grades started to slip, Teddy's by only a few marks. Christopher by a whole margin. Their Parents however were from the punk scene, they probably didn't notice much. 'Get off my lawn!' is all you ever here from Chris' and Teddy's Mom and Dad. But the boys don't care for much. 'Girls, Boarding, Biking, Girls, Beer, Boarding, Girls, Girls, Boarding, Beer, Biking' is really what all they are interested in. Good things have mostly taken up their lives, from being in bands to actually getting through collage. Well just Teddy anyway, but it was one night in 1999, both boys were drunk. They had an idea. 'You see this is why we need to write this biography for wherever we are, i don't remember any of this' says Teddy Knight, Christopher doesn't remember much either. 'Teddy hurt me that night, ah it's bringing back when i was born! AGHHHH!' screams Chris when that subject is picked up. Anyway that night around August, 1999. It was a peaceful summer night. With both guys playing in their garage with their friend Jack, it came to them. The boys don't know who started it first, but they went into the garden. Taking pillows and other soft things from their house, and started to play fight. Suddenly it got serious, as loving what he had seen on the TV. Chris of course climbed their house, and jumped off immitating the days of Rob Van Dam on ECW Television. 'At least i moved out of the way' remembers Teddy Knight. 'I only had to go the hostpital, and that fuckin' rocketh'd my socketh'ds' says Chris. After that, they took up training with some wrestlers in the area in San Diego area, they took around a year to complete their trainings, taking chances anyway they could Teddy decided to go to college, after finally getting a copy of the Decendants album. 'MILO GOES TO COLLEGE!'. 'MILO GOES TO COLLEGE, CHANGED MY LIFE MOTHERFUCKERS!' screams Teddy whenever he remembers some of the happy times. Chris was not so happy 'Man i had to get a job, and even McDonalds wouldn't even employee me! That sucked!' Christopher did get a job while Teddy went away. Still having time for the things he loved, he got accepted into a PCW Training Accademy called the PWA, after taking his time and getting beaten nearly every night. 'Once in his whole career' remembers Chris. 'He's lying' tells Teddy. He was then moved into NTICW, where after 3 months of action it closed. 'I won a title, can you belive it. It was lucky though, because i had been surfing all day and had one big cold. Through those 3 months, of doing stuff' said Chris, he won't tell us what the stuff was. 'I actually got somewhere. I was over the moon, then it closed. DICK SLAPS!' he says whenever he has to talk about it. Maybe Chris can't control what he says, but at least he gets somewhere doing he what he does. Now after this happend, he went nowhere, he left wrestling trying any place he could. Again McDonalds wouldn't employee. Around Feburary time, 2002 Teddy Knight called him up. 'Teddy called me up, he was in a tight call. I accidentally disconnected my brother, i had to wait two weeks for another call!'. And so did Chris, wether he did get hit by his brother for around a week needs to be solved, but now with his brother by his side both guys are gonna start trying again. 'I'M GONNA WHIP SOME CANDY ASS!' says Chris, only to be hit by his brother on the odd occasion. They have tried other places, namely OSW but OSW couldn't offer them enough so they couldn't by 10 acres of land for a skateboard park....They are pissed off about that. So instead they settle for PCW now, because you can get sexy bitches there, and not hores like other places. You can get money and Snapple~! to, everything seems to be going good. They also will be keeping it.... PUNK!
