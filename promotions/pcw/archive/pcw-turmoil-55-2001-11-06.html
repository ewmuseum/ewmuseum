Ivan smiles and points down at the table. Dave: NOOOOOOOOOOOO!!!! Jeff: DAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAA!!! Ivan grabs Ed and presses him up in the air. Ed comes to life and squirms free, coming down behind the Russian Bear. Ivan spins around and receives a kick in the gut from Ed, followed by a DDT ONTO THE STEEL RAMP! The crowd erupts. Dave: ED JUST PLANTED THE WORLD CHAMPION WITH A DDT ON THE RAMP! Jeff: WHAT A CHEATER!! Dave: THERE IS NO DISQUALIFICATIONS HERE!!! Jeff: SHUT UP!! Ed scrambles to his feet and plays to the crowd. Dave: LOOK OUT!! Ed’s expression changes from excitement to shock as Ivan Stanislav grabs him from behind and lifts him into the air. Dave: NOOOOOOOOOOOO!!! Jeff: BUUUUURRY HIM!!!!! He carries Ed over and CHOKESLAMS HIM OFF THE STAGE THROUGH THE PRODUCTION TABLE!! Dave: OH MY GOD!! Jeff: DAAAAAAAA!! The crowd erupts. Crowd: PCW!! PCW!! PCW!! PCW!! PCW!! Ivan roars as we look down off the stage at Ed who is motionless in the wreckage of the table below. Dave: ED IS DEAD!! Jeff: GOOD!! KILL THEM ALL!! Down at ringside Kremmen whips Howard into the steel barricade. Howard hits hard, then flips up and over into the crowd. Kremmen hops over the railing and continues his assault on Howard. He pulls Howard up and they start to trade right hands through the crowd. Dave: KREMMEN AND HOWARD ARE BRAWLING INTO THE CROWD!!! Jeff: Watch out for pick-pockets, Kremmen! Dave: I doubt he has any pockets in his wrestling gear. Jeff: Yeah, but they might try to steal his boots or something. Steven: Kremmen stole my heart. Dave: Both of you need to shut the hell up. Over in the ring, Nelson has Miyagawa locked in a figure Four leglock. Ivan climbs in the ring and starts to stomp on Miya while Nelson continues to apply the pressure on his leg. The referee is there to check on Miya but he won’t give up. Dave: Miyagawa is taking an enormous amount of punishment from Management! Jeff: You gotta love that. Nelson breaks the hold out of frustration and Ivan picks Miya up by his throat. Nelson throws his arms up in the air triumphantly as Ivan parades around the ring with Miya hoisted above his head. Dave: These men are unbelievably cocky. Jeff: You would be too if you had that kind of power. Miya manages to reach down and rake the eyes of Ivan, causing the Russian Bear to lose his grip. Miyagawa drops to the mat and nails Nelson from behind with a dropkick, sending him flying through the ropes to the outside. Miya then scrambles to the top turnbuckle as Ivan tries to get his faculties. Miya perches up top then flies off for a MOONSAULT! Dave: GET HIM MIYA!! Jeff: NYEEET!! Steven: BOOCAA!! Miya comes down and lands right in the arms of Ivan. The Russian Bear snarls and tosses Miya up in the air and nails him with THE IRON CURTAIN!! Ivan makes a cover.......1........2........CRACK! Dave: ED ROSE FROM THE DEAD AND CRACKED IVAN WITH BOB!! Jeff: WHAT THE HELL?! The crowd chants. Crowd: BOB!! BOB!! BOB!! BOB!! BOB!! A bloody Ed stands in the ring over Ivan, Bob in hand. Ivan clutches his head as Ed brings Bob down across the back of the Russian Bear. The crowd is in a frenzy as blood slowly starts to trickle down the forehead of Ivan. Dave: IVAN IS CUT!! THE RUSSIAN BEAR IS BLEEDING!! Jeff: IMPOSSIBLE!! Nelson reaches in the ring and drags Miyagawa out to the floor. Those two begin to fight up the ramp, trading right hands as they go. Nelson goes for a short clothesline, but Miya ducks underneath and cinches Tyler in a rear waist lock. He takes the President of PCW over with a GERMAN SUPLEX ON THE RAMP!! Miya gets to his feet and taunts Nelson, but gets nailed by a charging Edgecrusher with a spear. Dave: Edgecrusher finally managed to escape from Unstoppable Eugene. Jeff: What took him so long? Nelson isn’t going to be happy about that. In the crowd Howard and Kremmen are still going at it. Kremmen rams Howard’s face into the cement wall of the arena, then plants him with an INVERTED DDT TO THE ARENA FLOOR! Dave: A devastating move by the CEO! Back in the ring, Ivan runs his hand across his face and sees the blood on his hand. Fire fills his eyes as he rises to his feet and faces Ed, who stands ready with Bob in hand. Ivan roars and Ed swings Bob at the Russian Bear, clocking him on top of the head. The blow doesn’t phase Ivan as he returns fire with a punishing right hand. Ed lands with another shot from Bob to the head of Ivan, and the Russian Bear responds again with a right hand to Ed’s jaw. Dave: This is insane! Ivan is absorbing all those shots from Bob, and Ed is taking all of those lethal right hands from Ivan after he was chokeslammed off the stage! Jeff: KILL IVAN!!! KILL!!! The two men continue to exchange their blows, as the others carry on their battles on the outside of the ring. Suddenly the arena goes black. Dave: WHAT THE??? Jeff: PAY YOUR DAMN ELECTRIC BILLS, YOU WETBACK BASTARDS!!! Steven: Hold me! The sound of a meaty smack rings forth. Jeff: WHAT THE HELL IS GOING ON!!! I CAN’T SEE ANYTHING!!! Suddenly, the red and orange, fiery light illuminates the ring, from where can only be described as a “rift in the camvas.” Ed and Stanislav can be made out standing on both sides of the rift. Dave: What the... Jeff: ...hell? Suddenly, a surge of flames erupt out of the hole, shooting 20’ into the air. The flames lick both Ed and Stanislav, sending both men down to the canvas, as they try to roll themselves out! Dave: OH MY GOD!!! ITS AS IF A RIFT TO HELL HAS OPENED UP IN THE MIDDLE OF THE RING AND BOTH ED AND STANISLAV ARE ON FIRE!!! Jeff: STOP, DROP AND ROLL COMRADE!!! Steven: LOOK!!! Suddenly a hand reaches out of the rift and grabs Ed, and drags him down into the flames. Dave: WHAT THE HELL IS THAT?!?! Steven: ITS....ITS.... Jeff: DAAAAAA MAN!!! Dave: CLYDE’S ALIVE!!! BUT WHAT ABOUT ED!?!?! Sure enough, out of the firey pit comes The Burned Man they Call Clyde, to a chorus of boos. Meanwhile, Ivan Stanislav has gotten to his feet, but his red suspenders are still on fire. Unaware of Clyde’s presence, he turns, catches a boot in the gut, causing the World Champion to double over. Clyde then hooks up Ivan with a double armbar and edges Ivan towards the fiery hole. Steven: I have.... Jeff: DON’T EVEN THINK IT!!! Dave: WHAT IS CLYDE GONNA DO?!?! WE ARE ABOUT TO LOSE OUR FEED!!! Clyde then proceeds to show incredible strength, flipping the 400 lb champion, up, feet in the air... Dave: WHERE THE HELL DID CLYDE GET THIS KIND OF STRENGTH?!?! Jeff: I CAN’T WATCH!!! Clyde locks Ivan’s skull between his knees, and then showing his trademark lack of regard for his own safety and those around him, leaps through the fiery hole in the canvas. Dave: OH MY GOD!!!! The crowd erupts. The Crowd: PCW!!! PCW!!! PCW!!! PCW!!! PCW!!! Dave: CLYDE JUST PILEDROVE IVAN STANISLAV THROUGH THAT FLAME FILLED HOLE IN THE CANVAS!!! ALL THREE OF THE MEN WHO WILL MEET THIS FRIDAY NIGHT FOR WITH THE PCW WORLD HEAVYWEIGHT TITLE ON THE LINE HAVE DISAPPEARED INTO THAT PIT OF FLAME AND SMOKE!!! Jeff: SOMEONE CALL THE PARAMEDICS!!! Dave: WE ARE OUT OF TIME, WE WILL SEE YOU THIS FRIDAY NIGHT ON FUSION AND HOPEFULLY HAVE SOME ANSWERS HERE!!! The camera shifts to the ring, where Kremmen, Jeremy Howard, Miyagawa and Tyler Nelson have entered the ring, and try to get close to the pit to check on those who fell inside its fiery depth, but are driven back by the heat, smoke and flames....as the © 2001 Primetime Championship Wrestling logo hits the lower, central portion of the screen, and the screen fades to black.
