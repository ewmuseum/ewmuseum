Pete Biography Real Name Pete. Just...Pete Nicknames Pete; THE LEGEND OF PETE; Mix Master P; His Royal Chubbiness; Chubby Tubby. Hometown Cleveland, Ohio Birthday 02/29/59 Height 5'7' Weight 351 lbs Biography Pete is an enigma, wrapped in a riddle, hidden under the fridge...mmm...fridge... A former seamstress, Pete was signed to a GCW contract unwittingly when he angered GCW president and owner Steven Caldera. Forced to be beaten by other wrestlers, Pete endured many beatings at the hands of the staff. his comedic interludes are said to have been one of GCW's final saving graces in it's last months, and Pete's 'Legend of the Fridge' became the precursor to his superstardom. The Past few months are a blur, as Pete took time off the wrestling scene to work with Victoria's Secret on a line of Manzierres that were flattering, as well as manly. Description: Pete is a very large man, standing just 5'7 and 351 lbs. His eyes are always bloodshot, and are a deep green color. His hair is graying, and he's quite bald on top. He combs his greasy hair over in a God awful combover, and rarely shaves. His facial hair is like that of a 16 year old, coming in sporadically. He normally wears a flannel shirt, stained up with whatever meal he just ate, practically bursting out at the seams. His pants, are usually jeans, and never seem to fit him very well. More often than not he's smiling a gigantic smile showing his missing upper right front tooth. Other Feds GCW, cameo in FSW Other Fed Awards Former GCW hardcore champion Quotes NO! NO! NO! NO! NO! NO! You think you're going to walk down that aisle...get in this ring...and proceed to beat me down? Well I've got news for YOU...tough guy... You're going to have to catch me first....
