Avery Vance Biography Real Name Avery Vance Hometown Miami, Florida Birthday July 20, 1970 Height 6'0" Weight 241lbs lbs Biography Back on the streets of Miami after a long stretch in a maximum security prison for a drug bust in 1988, Avery Vance is sent to Miami, Florida, by his old boss, Vladmir Cortez. To be honest, Vladmir was never really a fan of Avery and saw him as a threat to his major corperation. Because of this, sending Avery to Miami, away from him, was a good idea. Upon arriving in Miami, things do not go as planned for Avery, who is out in the open. Avery is set up by the Cuban Mafia, and left with no money or merchandise. Vladmir wants his money back that Avery lost, but the Cuban Mafia have gone underground and haven't been seen. Most of Miami seems to know of Avery's style and want him dead. The only hope that Avery has is to take over the city himself, get the money, and earn back Vladmir Cortez's help. With great weather and beaches, and South America just across the ocean, Miami is filled with diverse people to become friends with. Miami is a place to see and meet athletes, pop stars, politicians, among other people. But, as Avery soon finds out, trust is hard to find and you can't survive if you aren't on your own. Still, Avery has the job of expanding Vladmir Cortez's empire by winning over land and making new friends and enemies. Avery also must win back Vladmir's money that was stolen away from him, a clear fifty thousand in green that was supposed to be used to go towards crime operations. Avery Vance soon meets his first friend in Miami, a man by the name of Luigi Leone. Luigi is an Italian lawyer who knows his way around Miami. Luigi advises Avery to get a side job, preferably one that moves around the country so that Avery can move from place to place doing side work for various people to gain some cash. Avery finds professional wrestling and all is well. Now, it is two years later and Avery Vance has the knowledge of a full professional wrestler. Doing small gigs, Avery collects enough money to pay rent in a run down motel that Luigi owns. Avery and Luigi have been undercover and have kept a low profile every since Avery arrived. With the name of Avery Vance almost gone from the minds of all of the gangsters, Avery is set to head back out into the real world and get his cash back. Still in touch with Vladmir, Avery vows to gain his trust back. Luigi tells Avery one night that he will need an actual high-profile wrestling federation to join, so he can move around better. Avery finds Primetime Wrestling Academy, a federation that is opening up again. Once Avery finds a wrestling federation, Luigi says that he will find work for Avery. That is only WHEN Avery finds a federation. How far will he have to look?
