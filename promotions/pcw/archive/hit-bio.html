The H.I.T. Biography Wrestler Name The H.I.T. Real Name Hardcore Isosceles Trapezoid (He had it legally changed) Nicknames The Sultan of Sides, The Archbishop of Angles, Baron of Bisection, The 10/18 Assailant, S.P.I.T., S.H.I.T. (Last two by his enemies) Hometown Salt Lake City, Utah Birthday 7/21/1978 Height 5'10' Weight 221 lbs Biography Appearance: The HIT has gone back to his giant red afro, shedding the mask and revealing his face. A long scar runs along the right side of his face along the cheekbone, along with puffy scar tissue on his forehead. His green eyes are bright, in contrast to the damaged face. If possible, have him bleeding profusely, his face a crimson mask. Trapezoid's ring attire now consists of a black t-shirt, with a bloody, dripping pattern on it done in a deep royal purple. His long pants/tights are black, with 'HIT' written down the front of his left leg in white, '10/18' written down the other leg in the same color. Biography: The Hardcore Isosceles Trapezoid has had a very interesting life, to say the least. He is a man of many talents, and almost as many quirks. When one lives in Mormon Salt Lake City, and is an atheist, one can expect many oddities. He was born to loving parents Jeffrey and Maria in a nice middle class neighborhood in Salt Lake City. Soon after his birth, the loving couple had family over at their house, to celebrate the HIT’s birth. To this day, his first and last name are unknown. Since he has legally changed his name to The HIT, (Mr. Trapezoid for teaching) and his parents will not speak to the media, it is likely this information will remain forever unknown. While passing young HIT to an uncle, Jeffrey unfortunately bobbled the child, dropping the infant on the side of his head. Maria immediately called 911, and the paramedics arrived, rushing the baby to the hospital. At first, it appeared no real damage was done. However, in his emergency room, young Trapezoid woke up next to a second grade student, who was desperately studying his shapes. One had been carelessly left on his bed. Trapezoid saw it…and the image on it, an isosceles trapezoid, would change the HIT’s life forever. In his early years, he believed that he WAS an isosceles trapezoid. He enjoyed counting games from the time he was six months old. He was well ahead of everyone else in his classes mathematically, studying such subjects as geometry and algebra when he was in 6th grade. He finished the most advanced calculus course his high school had to offer by 9th grade, leaving the school no choice but to bus him to Brigham Young University to take more advanced mathematics. Sadly, this would be the best college education he would get. His parents were too poor to send him to college, so he attended a local two-year community college, and graduated with honors, in math of course. His degree was in business, a subject he despised. However, he knew mathematics was his passion. So, he decided to use his degree to get something he always wanted…. A substitute teaching license. Going back to Brigham Young High School, The HIT stepped in as a substitute geometry teacher. His odd teaching techniques and bizarre behavior quickly made him a favorite substitute among students. While nearly being barred for life due to his substitute health teaching foray, after persuading the principal with what would come to be known as the SAS Postulate, The HIT was granted a permanent position as a 10th grade Geometry teacher, known to all as Mr. Trapezoid. Many of his students were wrestling fans, as was The HIT. For years, the HIT had been practicing on the side, joining various schools. He signed a contract with the IWO’s IWO Minor League, or the IML. He then worked out a deal with the school, in which he would only teach one period a day to allow for his schedule. This makes each of his students extra special. He debuted in the IML to rave reviews, capturing the IML United States Title in a three way match against Lance McNally and the champion Phyre with a 450 splash. Soon after though, he got the call up to the IWO. Dropping the belt to The Executioner, he was ready for the next stage of his career. In the IWO, he came out fast. Many expected him to be an utter joke, yet he rose quickly through the ranks. Facing Jax Stone for the IWO North American title, he dropped one fall to Jax’s Drop Like a Stone, yet took two straight falls for the title. He held this for about fifteen days, before dropping it to “The Real Heel” Evan Levine. From here, he was booked to take on Samuel Potright in a Trick or Treat match, the second of its kind. Trick or Treat II started out as being over the Extreme title, but quickly turned into a rivalry. The HIT emerged victorious in one of the bloodiest matches of all time, but his injury toll was staggering. Over 200 stitches, a broken leg, second degree burns over around 15 percent of his body..all of this forced him to vacate the Extreme Title a day later. While on the shelf for six months, the HIT’s warped mind went even further off the deep end, as he formulated a plan to go after Potright. Competing under a mask as Why, he assaulted Potright, and kidnapped his lovely wife Beth. After using a knife to draw her blood, and then drink it from a glass, he convinced the then World Champion to accept Trick or Treat III for the World Title. After the most emotional, violent, and sadistic match in the history of professional wrestling, Sam Potright drove Trapezoid headfirst into the mat in overtime with War Within a Breath to defeat him. However, an executive quirk left The HIT World Champion, and Samuel unable to see Beth for the rest of his life. At this point, The HIT was heartbroken. He had the World Title…but he couldn’t live with causing the emotional demise of his nemesis. He excersized a loophole in the match contract…sending him into retirement and surrendering the title, yet earning him Potright’s undying respect…for then. As he recovered from this, even more insane match, he received a call from an unlikely man: Kent Anathason. He needed someone to join himself and Nuke in Primetime Championship Wrestling, to form a group called Neurosis. Neurosis formed in the summer of 2001, fighting groups such as the Dark Ages. Quickly, things degenerated, as Neurosis was thrown headlong into the path of The Red Army. Anthason and Nuke quickly left, despite HIT and Nuke teaming to nearly overthrow Da Westsidaz reign as tag team champions of PCW. This left the HIT alone to battle The Red Army. Despite much opposition from groups like The European Union, The HIT received no help in his battles. He eliminated Alexander Popov and Nikita Morozov from PCW, while capturing the PCW Cruiserweight Title from White Mexican, and the Television Title from Kid Wonder. Finally though, his run came to an end. In a retirement match against Rent-A-Hero, Ivan Stanislav, leader of The Red Army, planted HIT with The Red Scare, allowing RAH to pick up a win, and evict HIT from PCW. He immediately swore vengeance on both men. It didn’t take him long to find a new federation, signing on with Epic Wrestling. In a Poisoned Pyramid Deathmatch, he won the promotions North American Title, holding it for two months before dropping it to nemesis Samuel Potright, and leaving. At this point, The HIT went looking back to PTC for work. He found PURE, a new Japanese promotion starting up. Seeing this as a perfect opportunity to test his skills, the HIT began a makeover. Masking himself, he entered both renditions of PURE, winning the PURE Jr. Heavyweight Title, and defeating Ivan Stanislav at Intervention. Now, the Trapezoid has come back, the Front Office the source of his ire. Can he take down the group as he did a portion of the Red Army? Only time shall tell.. Other Feds IWO, Epic Wrestling, PURE Other Fed Titles Other Fed Awards IWO North American Champion, IWO Extreme Champion, IWO World Heavyweight Champion (technically, reign was annuled at his own request), Epic North American Champion, PURE Jr. Heavyweight Champion
