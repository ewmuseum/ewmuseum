Turmoil 52 16 Oct 2001 The Ice Palace, St. Petersburg, Russia A mother's vigil at the clinic The show opens to reveal the queen of PCW, Brandi Wilson sitting in one of the overstuffed yet still uncomfortable chairs, usually associated with visiting a doctor’s office. The usually stunning Wilson is not as beautiful as normal these days, as the toll of constant worrying over the health of her newborn baby boy, Caleb, are beginning to take their toll in the forms of dark circles beneath the eyes, an unkept mane of hair and pale complexion. A look of worry sits frozen upon Brandi’s face, as she chews away on the cuticle of her index finger of her right hand. She looks up at a clock on the wall, then back down at her watch, an act that can only be explained by the obvious stress this young lady is under. Brandi stares intently at the a miscolored tile on the floor of the waiting room, but it is apparent to all who can see she is not examining the tile, but instead is looking within herself, for the strength to carry her and her prematurely born infant son through these crucial days.
