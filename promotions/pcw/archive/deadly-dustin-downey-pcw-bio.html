"Deadly" Dustin Downey Biography Biography Downey was born in the back of a '73 El Camino...the same one he was concieved in coincidentally. He was the son of two very poor trailer park patrons. They were the epitome of white trash. However when young Downey was born, he was considered a sign of luck to the couple. The night Dustin was born his father bought a lottery ticket, the latest issue of Hustler, and a six pack of beer....same as he always does. However this time...Mr. Downey won. The Downey's were now millionaires. Mr. Downey was convinced that Dustin was lucky, so lucky in fact that they never let him leave their 1,000,000 dollar trailer house. Upon reaching the age of 12, they let him venture out on his own. When he stole the car and ran over the local priest they decided maybe he should stay inside some more. Dustin spent his days watching televison, mostly wrestling. He considered the television to be his best friend. When Dustin turned 18, they let him out of the house where he immediately hooked up with some local mobsters. The mobsters took Dustin in as one of their own. One night over poker, Fat Tony bet Dustin he couldnt' eat a bag of mothballs in 5 minutes. Dustin ate the bag in 5 minutes...and forty three seconds. With that his fate was sealed...he would become a wrestler. He moved to Mexico to train and became Mexico's legendary Masked Mexican Avenger. However he was chased out of town for having nothing to avenge, not wearing a mask, and lastly, not being Mexican. Dustin then went on to PCW, PCW2, back to PCW, fired from PCW, back to PCW, fired from PCW again. Dustin is currently in the New Breed with his lifelong best friend Chris Chase.
