Fusion 44 3 Aug 2001 The Cinergy Center, Cincinnati, Ohio Barbara Bush-Kriegman's ritualistic awakening The show fades in from black to candle lit chamber with crimson curtains covering the walls. The camera pans around the dark, shadowy ceiling area, revealing the twisted images of gargoyles, who are watching over the room with their stony, unblinking gazes. The floor of the room is completely devoid of furnishings and its smooth, stone surface is almost completely featureless, save the pentagram carved into the dead center of the floor in the room. Laying upon the pentagram is the motionless form of Barbara Bush-Kriegman, clad in little more than a loose fitting, white cotton robe. The camera pans in upon the smooth skin on the face of The Evil Supergenius’ sultry wife showing not even a flutter on her eye lids. Panning down across her ample breasts, the camera poses for a moment. At first, it would seem to be a gratuitous shot of Barbara’s mam region for the male viewers at home, but after a second passes by, it becomes clear that the director has not asked for the cameraman to pause to satisfy some sort of perverted agenda, but to show that the chest cavity of Bush-Kriegman is not gently rising and falling to indicate she is breathing breathing. Suddenly, there is a sound of rushing liquid. The camera pans out to show that the grooves carved into the floor to accommodate the pentagram are now acting as canals for a thick red liquid. An eerie chant-like montra begins to echo throughout the chamber. The camera moves up from the rapidly filling satanic symbol to reveal several black robed figures moving to the edge of the outer circle of the pentagram, with candles in hand. The facial features of the figures, seven in all, are hidden beneath the shadowy folds of their cowls. The chanting of the group grows louder and faster. The cameras move to the smotionless form of Bush-Kriegman, then quickly to the final points of the pentagram which are not filled with the blood like liquid. Finally, from both sides of the shot, two separate flows of “blood” rush towards one another, as the chanting continues to rise in speed and volume. The shot switches to the face of Bush-Kriegman, which has gone from still and motionless, to a look of unconscious uncomfortableness. Switching back to the flowing blood, the scene arrives just in time to see the two flows of blood meet one another… Shifting back to the face of Bush-Kriegman, the sex-kitten’s eyes suddenly spring open, and a piercing shriek flies forth from her lips…which ends prematurely, as the scene is abruptly and unprofessionally suddenly goes black.
