Jonathan Drachen Biography Real Name Jonathan Drachen Nicknames Jonathan, The Arisen, JonDrach, JD Hometown Washington DC(birthplace) Dallas, Texas(home) Birthday 08-15-78 Height 6'2' Weight 195 lbs Biography Drachen grew up in and around Washington DC. Life was very difficult living even in the rich suburb of Alexandria, due to the urban influences felt by the nearby harsh ghetto, which also serves as the nation's capital. From it's pearly white centers of government to its sunken ghetto in the furthest depths of urban blight, DC seems to exude evil which seems to adversely affect all life for miles in every direction. Drachen grew up and learned Kung Fu from the age of 9 and became a black sash 9 years later. His master, Ting 'Frank' Cheng, was also his first employer at Domino's Pizza. This Pizza place was located in DC itself and offered Drachen a glance of what it is like to live in the depths of poverty-stricken hell. He was robbed, beaten and nearly murdered many times. Howevver, he survived with courage and stamina until he left DC and Virginia at the age of 19, when an essay he wrote about DC gained him a scholarship to Southern Methodist University. (He had previously planned not to attend college, because he didn't like the mentality of the place and his grades and purposeful low achievements(intentionally) kept him from receiving even acceptance to colleges.) Drachen went to SMU, at his parents' and Kung Fu master's urging, and graduated with a Bachelor's in business. Something that would leave his options open seemed ideal. He didn't go for his MBA, however, since he had already succeeded with many businesses he had set up during college in and around the Dallas area where SMU is located. Drachen opened up a pizza restaurant and named it after himself, Drachen's. Drachen served this function and prospered for a couple of years. At the age of 23, however, he decided to live amongst the people again, however, not in the direct manner as before. He decided to do it as a wrestler, as one the people could look up to and as one who could tell them what was right. He continued to deliver pizza, and used it as his gimmick. However, this was cut short after he graduated from the Primetime Wrestling Academy a year later. He realized that his goody-two-shoes gimmick would no longer work. He got a taste of real wrestling in the Xtreme WRestling Assosciation, and discovered the lies and deception and dishonor that can occur in a ring, as it was dealt by the hands of XWA's Syndicate. He had an 'epiphany' in essemce. He had a revelation, that he was not a mere delivery boy, he was a deliverer of the truth. He was a Prophet. He and his friend, Cege 'The Cynic' Hayabusa discovered their destiny, as bringers of the truth, to the wrestling, and the greater world. However, this dream was crushed when Drachen received a severe head wound and brain damage to his frontal lobe, in exactly the area that would destroy any powers of prophesy, or telepathy. Drachen's team was destroyed as well and he and Cege decided to end their assosciation. Drachen competed in only one match afterwards, as a singles competitor. He was defeated and had a concussion like injury to his head. Drachen went to a hospital and he asked for his release from PURE, because his of injuries to his head prevented him from being able to travel by airplane for two months, and missing a month of competition would be disastrous. He later found that he could fly with the condition, but did not retract his request for release, due to the fact that he was in RUSH. Jonathan fought a bitter feud with Cassius Deamont and his group the Solution in RUSH which left him burned, battered, and almost dead by it's conclusion. During this feud, Jonathan's personal life was ruined when he discovered that he had been implanted with false memories of his past, and that his life as he knew it was not true. He was never able to ascertain what his life truly was, or exactly how he became what he was at the time. The feud between himself and Deamont took all of his focus. The culmination of the feud was a bloody and brutal match for the gifting of the title of First RUSH World Champion. Jonathan was defeated and left for dead in the ring after severe beating with a blackjack. The doctors told RUSH officials that he would never be able to wrestle again, and he was released from his contract with RUSH. After these two severe head injuries, discovering he himself was not who he thought he was, and an overall lack of attention from the world: it was assumed that Jonathan was fairly gone and swept under the rug for all eternity. Drachen was not dead. Drachen lived, but was forced to spend months in a hospital and even more months in therapy with Neil Farr, a famed psychotherapist, because of severe paranoia and temporary insanity caused from his head injuries. Neil was able to work Drachen through his paranoia and quell his imagination that his life had been engineered by an unseen power. After going through rehabilitation for the physical and mental aspects of his injuries, Drachen has returned and is ready to battle for the purpose of glory yet again. He has arisen from the ashes, yet again. Other Feds XWA, PWA, RUSH, PURE Other Fed Awards XWA Tag Team Champion(w/ Cege Hayabusa), XWA TV Champion.
