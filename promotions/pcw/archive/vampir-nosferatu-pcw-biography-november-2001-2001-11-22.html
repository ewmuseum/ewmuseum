History: My name is Vampir Nosferatu, currently a wanderer, but fortunately you will all know my name. For almost two years I have stood in the shadows, out of the business, watching new names and new stars come to the forefront. For two years i have said that i will not re-enter the ring, a place where titles were won, and lost, a place where careers were ended, and mine, and a place so empassioned within. I once lost that passion, the times were a changing, and i simply just got burned out, i took too much on, and simply lost my edge. The Minion, and later on Draven's Cult accomplished so much, there wasn't much left to do. My old partner Maskimxul, my brother, seems like the underworld swallowed him up whole...havent heard from my old manager Tiamat in about a year and a half...at least that was the case until earlier this morning. You see Tiamat visited me this morning and we began to speak about our old conquests, victories and accomplishments. He reittereated to me the lessons he taught me when i was younger, the feelings of hatred that so constantly fueled me. All in all when it was all said and done, i realized that i was hungry again. Hungry for the souls of those i destroy in the ring. Although this time around its not solely a quest for the title, i am going to start at the bottom of the ladder, one by one i am going to cut the person above me until one day...there is no more ladder...and the shadows will once again vanquish their critics. Therefore, as of this date, i am coming out of retirement, with a whole new sense of determination, and ambition. Be forewarned the revolution of evolution is coming, most of you and your fifteen minutes of fame is coming to an end...the soul stalker has returned....so it is written so it shall be done.
