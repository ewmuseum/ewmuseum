Flaymin Aymin Biography Real Name Aymin Nicknames The Flaymin One Hometown Sydney, Australia Birthday Flaymin can't count backwards! Height 6'5 Weight 245 lbs Biography Flaymin hails from Sydney, Australia. Flaymin spent his time Bar Brawling and setting alight kangaroos and sleeping hobo's. But he knew there was more to life then seeing a homeless person put out the fire on his empty tin cans. Upon hearing about the money earnt wrestling professionally, Flaymin jumped aboard the first illegal, wooden raft heading for America and never looked back. The rest.... is history. Flaymin quickly rose to the ranks as a hardcore icon in PCW, aquiring the Extreme Title on two occasions. But as quickly as the dream began.... so to did it come to a crashing halt. At a PCW show in Portland back in November 2000, Flaymin commited the ultimate act of idiocy- accidentally running over 21 fans at the show with his tractor. What eventually resulted was tyranny. Tyler Nelson's strangehold on PCW changed PCW forever as a result of his purchase of the company... which of course all began when Flaymin flattened some fans. Flaymin's career began to dwindle in the eternal damnation of Jobber-land until by pure coincidence he was recruited to be a Pornstar after exposing his 'talents' during an Xavier Kannon television segment. With his trusty Pornstar assistant, Jenna Jameson, by his side Flaymin had resurrected his career... among other things. But that alliance came to a crushing end when Jenna turned her back on Flaymin, however not before repeatedly kicking Flaymin in the testicles. The brute force of the low blows caused Flaymin's left testicle to pop loose. With blood gushing out and one 'ball' short, Flaymin was sidelined from action for an extended period of time. Following the sudden death of his musical 'inspiration', Michelle Branch, Flaymin hit a new low. Unable to commit himself to another woman, a revolving door of valet's emerged with Flaymin unable to settle down with one valet in particular. To correct this sad situation, Flaymin recently launched 'Valet-rama', a worldwide search for a brand new valet. How this plays out is yet to be seen...
