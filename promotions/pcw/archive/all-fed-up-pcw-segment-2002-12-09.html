All Fed Up — PCW in-card segment

9 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
All Fed Up Written: 09.12.02 As the camera cuts away, the screen lights up and find ourselves in the general area of the back parking lot. Our sensory glands suddenly aroused; more specifically: our ears. The squealing sound of burning rubber can be heard as a fast-approaching vehicle speeds towards us. As it nears the entrance, fully coming into view, it’s noticeably moving at too great a speed, and the driver, whoever it may be, is forced to slam on the breaks. Raves: Looks like they can’t drive, probably a RUSH guy. As the truck spins around, pulling a quick one-eighty, the camera angle is shaken due to the dicing cameraman, fleeing for his life. Momentarily, the screen focuses back into view, and we find ourselves staring at the front end of a red Ford F-150. A big old “beater” wagon, this can only be the vehicle of one man: A junky pickup: the preferred transportation of Abner, PCW’s newest redneck. “Oh great, here we go again”, the cameraman mutters under his breath. Throwing the door open, Abner hops out and stomps past the cameraman. Obviously quite disturbed, we find ourselves gasping at a sudden realization: this hick, for once, is not under the influence of alcohol! Yes, his eyes are red……but not from Bud, but from anger rather. Amazing. So the enraged hick, pushes the cameraman aside and heads on down toward the locker room. With such a fierce look of fire and determination in his eyes, the cameraman is almost afraid to try and question the rampant hick. But finally, thinking of next week’s paycheck, the cameraman gets up the guts. “Um, excuse me…..sir?” Abner spins on his heel. His chest bulging out, his head hung hi, he appears to be not much different from an angered bull…..something in fact that he knows much about. “What!”, the hick snaps. “Sir, if I could just have a moment…..” “Make it quick!” Abner impatiently puts his hands on his hips and spits on the ground. “Well, I was just wondering if you might have a few words to tell us about…..” Abner doesn’t let him finish. “Ya know what, ya sheep-bangin’, momma-mountain’, hippie son-of-a-whore!?” The cameraman is in shock. “ …..I’m sick of you, and the goddamn WT media……you’re all a bunch of nosy bastards! All ya ever do is cause toruble……See, I got more ipmortant things on my mind right now…..more important things than tellin’ you pricks the name of the first gal I ever masturbated over, or ‘bout the first fart I ever took! Jesus Christ, I’m a pro wrestler, not a frigin……somehting’……” He scratches his head. “Anyway, I got things to worry ‘bout, hinks like, oh gee, I dunno’, mah career! Yeah, that’s right…..after getting whupped by that monkity-monkey-monk pussie, and losin’ unfairly to that back-stabbin’, Mexican soap-star, I ain’t got much more on my mind……” He sticks his face in the camera, his eyes bulging, “Except for kickin’ some ass!” The cameraman is silent. And so Abner, satisfied, lets out a grunt and adds an afterthought. “An if you don’t believe me, than that’s HORSESHIT!” Abner, seemingly from out of nowhere, pulls out a beer, shakes it up, opens the can and downs the Bud in a couple of seconds. With the remaining contents of the can, he splashes the camera. And so with alcohol sprinkling down the front of the screen, Abner tosses the can out of the way, shoves the cameraman aside, and the screen fades out.
