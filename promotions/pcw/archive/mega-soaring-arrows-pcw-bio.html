Mega Soaring Arrows Biography Biography Everyone in Japan knows of the puroresu legends Goemon Amuro and Naizen Matsuura. Goemon was a technical purist who had wrestled with greats all over the world in his time, including winning Olympic gold in his weight division at the 1964 Summer Olympics. Naizen excelled in acrobatics, and traveled the globe to learn Capoeria and both puro and lucha-libre styles to make a lethal weapon in the air. Everyone loved them, and the Prime Minister himself for their accolades for Japan decorated them. But as many great legends, their time had to come eventually. As they retired, the wrestling fans looked to their offspring for the next generation puroresu legends. Both Goemon and Naizen had sons, Basho and Ukyo. They were looked upon by the media as the next set of Goemon and Naizen, and everyone gave their support to the two young boys as they grew up. Basho and Ukyo trained in wrestling, their fathers teaching them much. While training Ukyo, Naizen was diagnosed with cancer, and was rushed off to the hospital weeks before his son?s first match. He struggled to survive, and after having seen his son?s victorious match on TV he died a proud father. Japan mourned the loss, and the funeral was televised? This is where Basho and Ukyo would meet. With Naizen gone, Goemon offered to help Ukyo finish his training. Basho and Ukyo trained together, and they emerged from the Amuro dojo as skilled as some experienced wrestlers are now. They went on to wrestle for a few months separate, and then for 6 months as a successful tag team. But as they wrestled, there was the constant comparison made between their fathers and themselves. It grew to the point where the boys would grow angry with press whenever asked about their fathers. Soon they realized that they could never excel in the Japanese wrestling world outside of being known as ?the sons of Goemon Amuro and Naizen Matsuura?. Basho and Ukyo in their short time developed a fan base in Japan, Ukyo given a few commercial spots, and he and Basho frequent guests on TV personality shows. They were rising stars, but they knew deep in their hearts they were only given special treatment because of their fathers. So one day a press conference was held, and in that conference they announced their departure from Japanese puroresu, and announced their pilgrimage to American-style wrestling, the SE style. Japan was shocked by their decision, but as a reporter questioned, quote ?What would your fathers say about this decision?? Ukyo and Basho snapped and wrecked the press conference podium and stage set-up. Upon their departure from Japan, they had both dishonored their family name, and dishonored their families, Basho his father and mother, and Ukyo his mother and his younger puroresu-style brother. They?re traitors to Japan now, and with no looking back Ukyo and Basho turn to the US, arriving in Seattle, Washington for a chance to join the upcoming Primetime Championship Wrestling and start their lives over? make a name for themselves, and not from their fathers.
