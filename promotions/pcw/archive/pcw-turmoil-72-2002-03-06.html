Turmoil 72 6 Mar 2002 The Baby Dome, Prairie View, Texas Tyler Nelson's federal court verdict We open up with a shot of PCW reporter Cal Seaver. He wears a brown sport coat with the PCW logo embroidered on the left breast pocket and a pair of tan slacks. Cal stands with a microphone raised to his mouth in front of a set of large, intricately hand carved oak doors with gold lettering across them reading “Federal Court”. Cal’s face is stone serious. Cal: Hello PCW fans, this is Cal Seaver and I am standing outside the Federal Courtroom in Kansas City, Missouri. Inside this courtroom the Judge is almost ready to render his verdict in the trial of PCW’s CEO, Tyler Nelson. His Honor Judge George Jefferson has given permission to this reporter and my camera crew to be present in the courtroom as the verdict is read. Let’s head in now! Cal motions to the cameraman to follow him as he spins around and pulls the huge door open. The camera keeps rolling as it follows Cal inside the courtroom, panning quickly around to reveal no other spectators present inside. Cal motions up to the left side of the room and the cameraman follows his arm, finding Tyler Nelson sitting at the defense table. The CEO of PCW looks like he has been through hell, with his hair extremely mussed, several days growth on his normally clean shaven face, and dark circles around his eyes undoubtedly from lack of sleep. Nelson sits at the table, still wearing the prison issue orange jumpsuit and matching rubber flip-flops. His hands are clasped together and resting on the desk in front of him, shackled together by handcuffs. The same can be said for his ankles as they cross underneath his chair. Tyler slowly turns around to see who has entered the courtroom and his eyes grow wide at what he sees. Tyler: What the hell are THEY doing here?! This is a mockery!! I demand they be removed from here IMMEDIATELY!! Nelson’s court appointed attorney simply rolls his eyes as he sits next to Tyler, twirling a pencil in his fingers. The young man, likely fresh out of law school, wears a cheap suit and has nothing in front of him but a “Trial Law” book. The prosecutor glances at Cal and the cameraman and can’t help but offer a slight grin. Prosecutor: What’s the matter, Mr. Nelson? Don’t want all your “fans” to see you get thrown in the slammer? Nelson turns his head toward the prosecutor and snarls at him. Tyler: As soon as I an exonerated of these charges I’m suing the entire justice system! You’ll be begging me for a chance to wipe the shit off of my ass you son of a bitch! The prosecutor shakes his head and looks down at some notes. Cal and the cameraman take seats in the middle of the courtroom and wait. After a few minutes the bailiff stands and comes forward. Bailiff: All rise for the honorable Judge George Jefferson! The defense and prosecuting attorney’s stand at the bailiff’s command, as do Cal and the cameraman. Nelson, on the other hand, scoffs at the request and remains seated. Judge Jefferson comes strolling out of chambers and looks toward the CEO, who smirks and nods in his direction. The middle aged Judge, with slightly graying hair and some wrinkles starting to set in on his face, snorts in disgust as he steps up to the bench and sits down. His voice is gruff and stern. Judge Jefferson: You may be seated. The attorney’s sit down as Tyler simply looks up at the Judge with a cocky grin on his face. Judge Jefferson: As you all can see I have asked that this PCW camera crew be present at the reading of this verdict because I believe that this verdict will have a profound effect on those individuals within the organization. The Judge pauses as Nelson looks back at the camera with a look of disdain. Jefferson clears his throat and Tyler slowly faces forward again. Judge Jefferson: Mr. Nelson, I have met a lot of evil and manipulative people in my 30 years on this bench. I’ve seen killers, rapists, sexual deviants, and all sorts of other people that make my stomach turn over. I’ve also seen plenty of white collar criminals....people who defraud, extort, embezzle, or otherwise steal from the innocent. I’ve seen it all, Mr. Nelson.... The Judge leans forward in his chair, leering down at the still smirking Tyler Nelson. Judge Jefferson: (continuing) ....and you are the sickest of them all. The smirk that had been emblazoned on Tyler’s face quickly is replaced by a look of shock. Judge Jefferson: You have absolutely no regard for anyone else but yourself, or anything else for that matter other than your money. You have used and abused the power that your money has given you to torture and torment innocent people and manipulate your way through life. Nelson’s eyes don’t blink as he listens to the Judge’s verbal barrage. The look of shock is still chiseled into his face. Judge Jefferson: You have ruined other people’s lives for the sake of your own personal gain, and you have made it a point to take joy in doing so. The CEO’s mouth tries to utter some words, but he cannot manage even a peep. The Judge leans back in his chair, but does not remove his cold glare from Tyler. Judge Jefferson: You have run roughshod over the world for long enough, Mr. Nelson, and now it’s time for you to pay back what you have so sadistically taken from others. Nelson looks away from the Judge and over to his attorney. The public defender is doodling on the inside cover of the “Trial Law” book with a pencil, seemingly oblivious to the Judge’s attack. A look of panic sets in now as Tyler looks back up at the Judge. Beads of sweat start to form on Tyler’s forehead as he clenches his hands together tighter and tighter, whitening his knuckles. The Judge is unphased and continues. Judge Jefferson: The hardest part in deciding this case was not the verdict, because that was really never in doubt. What I struggled with was determining a fitting sentence for the crimes you have committed. With that in mind, I would like for the defendant to rise. The public defender finishes off his cartoon and stands up from his chair. Nelson looks up at him and back at the judge, but does not stand. Judge Jefferson motions to the bailiff, who makes his way over to the defense table. The large officer grabs Nelson by the arm and jerks him up from the chair. Tyler is pale as a ghost as he faces the Judge. Judge Jefferson: Tyler Nelson, after reviewing all of the evidence in this case and hearing the testimony of the witnesses, it is my verdict that you are GUILTY of all charges pressed against you by the Federal Government of the United States of America. Tyler faints and falls to the ground. The bailiff is there once again to pull Nelson to his feet and slap him a few times to wake him up. Once Tyler is coherent the Judge continues. Judge Jefferson: I am also ready to pass sentence on you, which as I stated was the hardest part of this verdict. What do you do to a man that is harsh enough to balance out what he has done to others? Life imprisonment? Unfortunately that is not one of the options I have available for sentencing. Jail time and a fine? That doesn’t leave a lasting impression. Nelson looks puzzled as the Judge speaks in a sort of quizzical manner. The public defender yawns. The prosecutor smiles like the Cheshire Cat. Judge Jefferson: Then it came to me like a revelation from God himself. The perfect punishment for a man like you, Mr. Nelson. Something that would leave an indelible mark on you for the rest of your pathetic life. Tyler starts to waver again like he is going to faint, but the bailiff steadies him before he can do so. Tears start to well up in the eyes of the CEO as the verdict is handed down. Judge Jefferson: Tyler Nelson....by the power vested in me by the United States of America I hereby order you to forfeit all assets, both monetary and material, into the stewardship of the courts. This includes all businesses, property, possessions, and anything else that means anything to you in the slightest. Every red cent you own is now property of this court. Nelson burst into tears. Tyler: (sobbing) No....you can’t do that. Judge Jefferson: Oh yes I can and I just did. All of your assets will be distributed to various charities nationwide and your businesses sold off to the highest bidder. All money’s raised from the sale of said businesses will also go toward charity. As for Primetime Championship Wrestling, it will be sold to the highest bidder as well, but you will remain employed there as a wrestler. I spoke with a Mr. Duke Williams and had a pleasant conversation with him. He relayed the fact that there are plenty of people within that company that are owed compensation from you, and suggested the best way for them to receive that compensation is by getting you in the ring. Your contract will be for the rookie minimum, with half of your yearly salary donated to charity. Tyler: (sobbing) Nooooo..... Judge Jefferson: Yeeeesssss.....you see, I could have put you in prison for many, many years, but that wouldn’t have taught you a lesson. So I took the thing that means the most to you in the whole world, your wealth. You make me sick, Mr. Nelson, and I want you out of my sight. This court is adjourned. The Judge pounds his gavel on the bench several times and begins to collect his things. Tyler continues to sob almost uncontrollably, but he manages to spit out a few words. Tyler: C-Can I h-h-have my s-suit at l-least? (sniffle) The Judge looks up from his papers. Judge Jefferson: I think you look just fine like that. Your suit is considered an asset, as much as it costs. (looking toward bailiff) You may remove the restraints from Mr. Nelson and escort him from my courtroom. The bailiff complies with the Judge’s request and unlocks the handcuffs and leg restraints. He then takes Nelson by the arm and starts to lead him out of the courtroom. As he passes the public defender, Tyler shouts at him. Tyler: YOU”RE FIRED!! YOU HEAR ME?!?! YOU’RE FIRED!! The defense attorney simply smirks and looks away as the bailiff pulls Tyler by the arm down the aisle. Cal pops up from his seat and shoves the microphone in the face of Tyler as the cameraman gets a close up of his face. Nelson’s eyes are bloodshot and some veins are pulsing on his temples. Some snot is running down from his nose because of the sobbing. Cal: Mr. Nelson....Mr. Nelson.....do you have any words for the fans of PCW?! Tyler: GO TO HELL, SEAVER!! YOU’RE FIRED!! Cal: (chuckling) Hehe...you can’t do that anymore Mr. Nelson. Tyler: Screw you....SCREW YOU!!! Nelson’s voice trails off as he is dragged from the courtroom by the bailiff, still dressed in the orange jumpsuit and flip-flops. As they disappear out the doors one final scream can be heard from the former CEO of PCW. Tyler: I’LL GET YOU MEXI!!!! IIIII”LLLLL GGEEEEEETTTTT YYYOOOOOUUUUUU!!! The camera pans back around to Cal who stands with a joyful smile on his face. Cal: There you have it fans. Tyler Nelson is found guilty by Judge Jefferson and is sentenced to the loss of all his possessions. What a statement made by the Judge here today!! And probably the best news to come from this whole ordeal is that Tyler Nelson no longer owns PCW!! It will be sold off to the highest bidder with the proceeds going to charity!! Now the only question is....who will own PCW next? Reporting from Kansas City, this is Cal Seaver signing off. Cal smiles as the feed cuts to black.
