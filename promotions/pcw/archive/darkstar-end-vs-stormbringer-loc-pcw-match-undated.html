PCW match: DarkStar and The End vs. Bobby Stormbringer and Charlie Loc

Undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
DarkStar and The End vs Bobby Stormbringer and Charlie Loc Written: Tag Team Match The End and Darkstar vs Bobby Stormbringer and Charlie Loc 'Parade' by Lifer begins blasting the arena. It’s only time before Darkstar and the End grace the arena with their presence. Clay: First, coming down the ring aisle, weighing more than one person normally would provided that haven't hung out with Two Ton for too long...... DARKSTAR AND THE END...... THE DARK AGE!!!!! Jeff: Satanic jobbers unite! Duke: What's so satanic about the End? Dave: He has no eyes? Duke: Um, that'll work for some people.... I guess. Darkstar and the End step into the ring, awaiting the arrival of their opponents. Darkstar's personal theme cuts out, as 'Paint it Black' by the Rolling Stones begins pounding the PA system until Bobby Stormbringer and Charlie Loc begin making their way toward the ring. Clay: And their opponents, making their way toward the ring at this time, weighing just as much as the other two guys, give or take a few dead bodies from Loc's gangster ways...... BOBBY STORMBRINGER AND CHARLIE LOC..... THE SYNDICATE!!!!! Dave: Clay is trying to get fired. Its obvious. Duke: Clay's a funny hoss. Loc and Stormbringer hit the ring, engaging in a four man brawl with Darkstar and the End. Stormbringer pairs up with Darkstar, trading punches with the Dark Ager. Darkstar is reeling, backing up from punch after punch. Stormbringer goes for one final punch, but Darkstar ducks under it, then nails a mourning star suplex. Stormbringer lands face first on the mat. Jeff: Eat that jobber! Dave: Stormbringer just had canvas for supper. Jeff: He's lucky he doesn't get the same treatment as he got on Turmoil. Duke: Enough about that! Loc has End, sending him into the ropes. The End bounces off the ropes and into a spinning heel kick. The End stumbles backwards, trying to regain his composure. He's too late, as Loc hits a devastating Frankensteiner before the referee forces him out of the ring. Darkstar takes his place on the ring apron, leaving Stormbringer and the End to battle it out. Dave: We've got the combatants from one of Spring Fling's pay per view matches. Jeff: An alright one at that. Duke: These men fought to the very end with everything they had. I commend that. Jeff: OK, I still thought it was a jobber match. Dave: There is no hope for this man. Stormbringer slowly gets back to his feet, as does the End. Stormbringer charges in at the End. But, as he gets close enough, the End leaps into the air and hits Stormbringer with an ear clap kick. Stormbringer walks around, disoriented. The End leaps up and over Stormbringer's shoulders, hitting him with a head scissors takeover. The End makes the tag to Darkstar. Dave: Stormbringer needs to make the tag to Loc if this time is going anywhere in this match. Duke: The Syndicate isn't going anywhere, let alone in this match. Dave sighs. Dave: A bit quick to judge, aren't we? Duke: I do things Malice style, always. Darkstar steps into the ring and grabs Stormbringer in an armbar, then he leaps into the air and drops a leg across the back of Stormbringer's neck. Bobby hits the mat. Darkstar heads for the top rope. Darkstar sets up, then flies off with a shooting star press suicida. But, in mid air, Charlie Loc nails Darkstar with his bicycle kick set up manuever. Darkstar crashes to the mat. Loc drags Stormbringer to the corner, making the tag to him. Duke: Its safe to say, if Syndicate is going anywhere, in might be on the back of Charlie Loc. Jeff: LOC DAWG! Dave: Loc isn't playing games. He's here to take business into his own hands. Duke: It might be his best move. Loc gets back to his feet, then rushes for the ropes. Loc gets close enough to leap onto the second rope and nail Darkstar with an asai moonsault. Loc makes the pin. 1..... 2...... 3NO! END WITH THE BREAK! Dave: Darkstar has to be thankful for the blind man. Jeff: Had the referee not slapped the mat so hard, End wouldn't have known where to go. Dave: Thats uncalled for. Loc brings Darkstar to his feet. Loc lands a knee to Darkstar's stomach, then locks him in a headlock. Before Darkstar has any clue as to what is going on, Loc leaps toward the top turnbuckle, going for a tornado DDT. Before Loc can put Darkstar out of his misery though, Darkstar switches things up in mid air, nailing a diving reverse DDT. Loc slams into the mat hard, writhing in pain on the mat. Duke: That's how you get the upperhand. Make a man think he's in control then show him otherwise. Jeff: Is it time for the crucifixion yet? Dave: I don't think PCW needs anymore of those. Jeff: We don't? Dave and Duke: NOOOOOOO!!!!!!!!!!! Darkstar lifts Loc over his head, then drops him down onto his shoulders. With Loc on his shoulders, Darkstar pivots and drops Loc on his face. Loc grabs his face, trying to flush out the pain. Darkstar makes the tag to the End. Jeff: Ah yes. Bring in the blind man. Duke: For a blind man, he can still wrestle with the best of them. Jeff: Stormbringer? Pfft. Duke: Um, I'm just reading what my cue cards read. Jeff: That explains alot about you then. The End rushes in, lifting Loc into the air for a powerbomb. As the End is about to powerbomb Loc, he changes things and goes for a superkic. That is, until Bobby Stormbringer breaks that up with a spear. Loc hits the mat. End crashes into the mat. Stormbringer pulls Loc onto the End. 1...... 2...... 3NO! KICK OUT! Dave: That was close! Stormrbringer almost gave his team the win! Jeff: Close just isn't good enough! Duke: They both need to make a tag! Dave: End is reaching for Darkstar. Loc for Stormbringer. End, by use of Darkstar's constant screaming, moves toward his tag team partner. Loc reaches for Stormbringer. Both men get closer and closer to making the tag. Loc tags in Stormbringer. End lunges out, making the tag to Darkstar. Stormbringer leaps onto the ring. Darkstar steps into the ring. Dave: THEY'RE GOING TO COLLIDE! Jeff: DIE JOBBERS DIE! Darkstar leaps out at Stormbringer with a flying forearm. Stormbringer ducks under it, then grabs Darkstar and nails his Storm on the Horizon alternate finishing manuever. Darkstar's head slams into the mat viciously. Stormbringer goes to make the pin on Darkstar, but from the back saunters Mona down toward the ring Duke: This can't be good. Dave: MONA HAS HIT THE RING! Jeff: NIPPAGE~!. Duke: No shit Sherlock. The End grabs Loc from the arena floor, pulling him off the ring apron. Loc's head slams on the mat on his way down. End grabs Loc, beginning to brawl with him. Inside of the ring, Stormbringer neglects the pin, turning toward Mona to see what she wants. Mona reveals what she blowing him a kiss and he walks over only to receive the smack heard around the world. Dave: MONA JUST BLASTED STORMBRINGER! Jeff: The bitch has struck! Dave: I second that! Stormbringer falls limp to the mat and Mona waves to the crowd about 10 pounds of gold rings cover her fingers and the referee calls for the bell. Clay: The winners of this match, by decision of the referee...... THE SYNDICATE!!!! Winners: The Syndicate View DarkStar 's Biography Back
