Turmoil 131 25 Jun 2003 Quito, Ecuador A mysterious arrival at the airship Arrival A young woman with flashing sapphire eyes steps confidently through the parking lot outside of the Cloud Nine airship. Her gaze shifts somewhere around the surrounding area. Something about the way she walks seems to emante an aura of determination. Her sparkling eyes, copuple with a wry grin, seems entrancing enough to weaken the most powerful of wrestlers. She is clearly a beautiful woman, with dyed purple hair that dangles in neat strands beside her cheeks. A silver emblem, runic-looking, glitters in the dim light of the parking lot as the camera pans around to change its view. The symbol disappears as she turns the front of her sleeveless shirt away from the camera and makes her way toward the inviting airship bording entrance. One last sight of her faded blue jeans and black shirt can be caught as she disappears from view.
