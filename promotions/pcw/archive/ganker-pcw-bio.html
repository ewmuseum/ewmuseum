Ganker Biography Real Name Ganker Ransolver Nicknames Asshole, Prick Hometown Seattle, Washington Birthday September 5, 1980 Height 5'10' Weight 190 lbs Biography Ganker was born in Plymouth, Indiana and soon moved to Seattle, Washington where he met his bestfriend ever, Hank~! Ganker Ransolver really doesn't have much of a biography, not as much as Hank does, but he does have a good amount of moments. Like all the moving around him and his family did, his parents were divorced and he was living with his mother for thirteen years then moved to his fathers for the remainder of his teenage years. He pretty much lived in the anti-wrestling household and had to get his own thirteen inch television to even watch Raw Is War, it was a pity that he was one of the few/many who had to wait until Saturday to watch Smack Down, unless he was visiting his mom or his aunt who got UPN and SD on Thursday. Ganker is a diehard wrestling fan and would do anything. He got his first shot in in the UWF and then the NZW, where he captured the NZW Tag Team Titles with Drummer, now known as Hank. He hasn't had much early success other than the NZW Tag Title victory, but when he made his apperance in the RSE he captured the RSE Cruiserweight Championship, the UWF Cruiserweight Champion. He's well over proven his worth to any wrestling federation he's been in and now is his time to shine in PCW. He's been on the coat tails of Hank~! for years and now he figures is good a time as any to make his name heard and make everyone realize that it wasn't Hank carrying him, it was Ganker carrying Hank those Team Nirvana years. Could PCW Tag Title gold be in the future of this young rising superstar? Could Red Rock have to be faced again with the problem of Team Nirvana, it's a well-known fact that their NZW rivalry was only the start of things to come in the near future and now that Red Rock holds the RS singles title, Ganker could have his eyes set on Red Rock the target. Ganker Ransolver went out with a leg injury during his PCW-2 arrival and ever since coming back after several months of surgery and rehab, he has been on a roll and he is one hell of a force to be reckoned with. Will PCW be where he shines the most, will PCW be his time to win big gold? Only time will eventually tell. Appearance: Long blonde hair, brown eyes, muscular build, but not some skinny wimp, but not Mr. Six-Pack either. Usually Ganker likes to wear baggy blue jeans, black RSE< RingSide Entertainment t-shirt or a black with yellow text, Ganker Is God~! T-shirt. Ganker's hair is mainly for the most part all messed up and never looks like he brushes it at all or even bothers to do anything but part it in a various place each day, or he might just get lazy and pull it back in a ponytail. His favorite stance is what Rob Van Dam stole from him from day one, the thumb thing he does now. Usually a pretty serious face, but sometimes happy/smiley. Other Feds RSE Cruiserweight Champion, CMF Heavyweight Champion, PwF Intercontinental Champion, PwF Hardcore Champion
