International Terrorists Biography Tag Team Name International Terrorists Biography Appearence: Freak- Freak is tall and in good shape. He has a palish skin colour (veins in his skin are visible) with baby blue eyes and long blonde scruffy hair that goes down to his shoulders. He also has a scruffy brown goatee matched with his purple lips. His eyes are sagged underneath and are minorly bloodshot. His face is bold and skinny looking, matching his unique skin. Igor- Igor has brown eyes, palish purple lips and a shaven head with small black hairs visible. He has a small rugged black goatee, and small hairs sticking from the side of his face. He has thin eyebrows, black like his hair. He is in excelent build, being a cruiserweight, he is not too muscular, but highly flexable. He isnt very hairy, only on his chest and legs, just thin black hair. His head is more rounded then that of Freak's, so his cheeckbones are less visible. Attire: Freak- A loose muscle shirt (black) with blue jeans. Igor- Igor wears wrestling long-tight pants. They are purple and have cigars (thick and brown with smoke flowing out the end) on the side of each leg. Biography: Freak- Before the humans, before dinosaurs, before the plants, before the earth, there was nothing. The Universe as we know it, was once an endless void, with total emptyness. But, one day... something emerged from the emptyness. Two single unique thoughts dug itself out of nothing, giving a reason for something to live. The thoughts grew into more then thoughts, but emotions. These emotions grew, grew forms, the first two forms. One of the emotions developed as The Spirit Of Good, creating the emotions of love, happiness, and peace. The Spirit Of Good, came into a gigantic yellow glowing sphere. The other emotion was developed as The Spirit Of Evil, creating the emotions of hate, destruction, and suffering. The Spirit Of Evil came into a similar gigantic sphere, except it was filled with orange and red flames. They both were immortal, and just drifted in the emptyness for milleniums, focusing on increasing their powers. And they did increase their powers, strong enough for them too do incredible things. One day, The Spirit Of Good decided to use some of his abilities. He bound his energy together and created a planet, a planet a little bigger then all the planets in our solar system put together. The Spirit Of Good named the planet Helious, and then created inhabitants. He created Helious as the perfect planet, the beings, all caring and loving, no hatred, sorrow, or greed, and every being was lifeless, not immortal. The Spirit Of Evil came of notice to this new 'perfect' planet. He grew jealous... and furious. Summoning his powers, he shot at the planet Helious with a mighty fireball. It hit into the side of the planet and demolished it, leaving a smaller, but still large rock ablazed with flames. The debris --nine other planets--, scattered not too far from the blazing rock still holding. The Spirit Of Good noticed this immediately, and created an atmosphere for the planets, he created the stars, and gravity's pull, black holes etc. The planets gathered together around Helious, and began to orbit. All the planets now had unthinkable habitats for life, except for one. He named it earth, and began to muster what energy he had left and put together a near perfect planet for his new creations. Since it was not perfect, it needed things in order for it too support life. He moved earth over closer to Helious, what he renamed 'Sun' to give it warmth. He created oceans and streams, and then plants and food on this wonderful planet. Soon enough, he was exausted and had no energy to create inhabitants of his planet because of all the energy he used for Helious was still recharging. He returned to his rival, The Spirit Of Evil and begged for his help in making inhabitants of the new planet, 'Earth'. He agreed and they both combined their powers to make inhabitants. The Spirit Of Good created peaceful herbivores and plants and insects. He tried creating larger stroger animals similar to reptiles. He named them Dinosaurs. The Spirit Of Evil created them as well, except making blood thirsty animals destroying and murdering other animals mercilessly. It then added its own personel touches, setting flames all on the new planet making it more 'cozy', when really the planet was turning into another Helious. The Spirit Of Good discovered this and saved his planet, while still destroying his new creations with an ice age too cool the planet. The Spirit Of Good became furious, and went after the energy replenishing Spirit Of Evil. At the same energy level, they went into combat attacking with all they had. Blasting flames, and light beams, they tore at each other aiming for supremacy for over 4 milleniums. Their energy level lowered darastically, so they went down to the recently thawed Earth as a battlefeild. Taking the souls of the dead creatures, The Spirit Of Evil reincarnated them into new forms. He had a mass army of goblins, beasts, vampires, and a lot more different creatures. The fully repleneshed Spirit Of Evil ceased the weakened Spirit Of Good, but since he was immortal, all he did was restrain him with chains and guarded him with his most powerful soldiers, the vampires. A new creature natural evolved around the same time. These creatures were humans. A t that point, humans were nothing, a mere insect to the monsters and goblins. That is, until The Spirit Of Good blessed them with a gift, the gift of knowledge. With this knowledge, they gathered together and escaped The Spirit Of Evil by building a meraculous city underwater named Atlantis. The Atlantians remained hidden for years until there magic became intensly powerful, almost enough to fight the army of monsters in which out numbered them. Using there advanced technology and knowledge of magic, they attacked the vampires and slayed them, taking a serious blow to their army, but saving their master, in which they now called 'God'. With God replenishing and the Atlantians holding their ground on the Earth, Satin (The name of The Evil Spirit given by Atlantians) destroyed the newly descovered city, killing off the Atlantian soldier's home and families. The deplenished Atlantian army fought with all they had, their magic failed and there physical abilities were not as powerful as the monster army. All the Atlantians were killed... but two. God named them Adam and Eve and placed them in a magic garden protected by his power. Using the pure loving souls from the Atlantians, he reincarnated an army of his own. Except his army was made of angels, centaurs, and the most powerful, archangels, along with all the other creatures reincarnated. Again, the battle between Good and Evil continued. After all the feirce hardships, Good acctually won. God then had come up with an excellent solution for Satin. While creating Helious, he created a clone universe which he called, the Afterworld. Only God had free access to the Afterworld, except for the lost souls that travel there. God banished Satin and one of every different species he created into the Afterworld, while killing off the rest of his dark soldiers. God then moved back into the Afterworld, leaving the living world to the humans Adam and Eve. Using every soul that travelled to the Afterworld before Satin created his army, and created an invincible army of Light. Using the new army, he drew back Satin and left him the lower half of the clone planet Helious. Now, souls are judged in Limbo, and brought to the half that suits them, evil souls go too the Underworld, and the good souls go to the Heavens. After a century in either world, they begin to reincarnate into another form, like a vampire, or an angel depending on how good or evil they are. Years passed, and the Humans evolved more and more after God's son, Jesus reminded the Earth of its true religion. Satin decided to mimick this, to make a Messiah... a Messiah Of Evil. He took all the power he could, and broke the seal into the living world. He then sent a messenger, a vampire, into the world on a mission. He carried a bottle with a blue flame of of Satin. He was suppose to find the most holy good hearted woman and pregnate her with this flame. And he did, the woman was raped and using the flame, fertilized her womb. The woman had the child, but died after naming him Frank Ameson. He was now an orphan with demonic blood. The vampire which pregnated his mother with Satin's flame became a Canadian citizen and raised Frank into a monster. He tortured him, and only taught him about the ways of darkness. With his terrorized mind he unleashed his demonic powers uncontrolably attacking his stepfather, and started to kill everything in sight. He was caught, and contained by the military at the age of ten and was held for years until he reached the age of twenty-five. He was called a Freak more than Frank, so he came to be known by that name better. He began to get dreams, he could see worlds like no other, and he met his father, he found out about his true identity. Through his telepathic bond between him and the Afterworld, he began to understand his abilities, and use them to help himself escape. He did, mustering his vampire abilities, mach 2 speed, incredible strength, swift reflexes, increased healing rate. Once he escaped, he found something that interested him, wrestling. He joined the PWA and slowly made himself up too the NTICW and recently the PCW. Taking his outside wrestling career as seriously as his wrestling career, he balances both and tries to make his two ultimate goals, to be PCW Tag Team champions with his partner Igor Castro, and to get access to the Afterworld and reunite with his 'Kind'. Igor- In the midst of the night, twenty-five years ago... Sharline Castro gave birth too Pavel Castro's son, Igor. She had a very unsafe labour, due to lack of medical attention in Cuba, also from being banned from all facilities by Fidel Castro, Pavel's brother and nemisis. She gave birth in a old abandoned shed, and died shortly after because of the conditions. Igor managed to survive, and lived alongside with his father and older brother, Slanstav. They grew up together, traveling to Canada as Refugees when Igor was the age of three. The once honourable father became an abusive drunk and sold illegal norcoticts for money. 10 year old Slanstav became mentally shattered, and knew only what his father told him, his love for his father was unconditional, so whatever he did, Slanstav followed in his footsteps. He began an early start using illegal drugs, while young Igor took the blame of his mother's death, and was beaten badly. Stanslav followed his father's footsteps and began to beat Igor too, thinking that it was good for him. He would beat him right before or after his father would everyday, until he was six years old and knew how to talk somewhat, after being beaten by his brother, muttered out the words, 'You monster...' while coughing up blood and collapsed. Stanslav realized his missdoings for the first time, after all these years he had forgotten what a real family was like, back in Cuba. He appologized and set themselves off to run away. Stanslav knew much about the streets giving him an advantage. He also knew about drugs, how too plant and grow them, and also, how to use them. He stole Pavel's drugs and packed all the supplies he could, and took off at the age of thirteen along with his six year old brother. They moved in an alleyway with a bunch of local homeless people. He made his way by selling and planting drugs. He became addicted and became more violent, getting into fights and other things on the street. Igor too became addicted to drugs at his early age, except he was different. The drugs only gave him the high, nothing else. He smoked tabacoo for years after that, and did not cough once, nor grow tired of the affects of alcohol. His physical appearence did not suffer either. He became immune to the negative affects of drugs somehow. They became adults, and Stanslav began to work for an anonymous organization back in Cuba, which ended up being a rebelion against the Dictator Fidel Castro. His ranking grew so much, he became best friends with the leader of the organization. Meanwhile, Igor meraculously joined the American Navy, becoming a Navy Seal. Both were having great carreers even after the misfourtunate past, until a battle arose in the deserts of Cuba, Cuba's military against the rebelion. Even the leader of the organization, and Stanslav Castro fought in. Stanslav died, while the leader of the organization was fatally injured. Fidel won that battle, and the leader came close to death until he made his last request to his fellow terrorists... too seek Igor Castro, and make him the new leader. Igor at that time, was betrayed by the Americans. He and two others, Johnny Salinski, and Marvin Pianni survived a suicide mission that was not informed by the Americans. They became mercinaries until tracted down by the Terrorist organization. Igor became the new leader and repaired his army and created new hope for the men. He named his organization 'The International Terror' because they would back down to knowone and would spread terror over any enemy. He funded The International Terror with money from selling his drugs, becoming a drug lord, his organization grew. His base is in International waters, an old oil tanker and offshore oil rig that they captured from Americans. After a few years of his successful feared organization, he met a man in NTICW (a wrestling organization he recently was contracted with.), this man's name was Frank Ameson, also known as PCW's Freak. He was also wrestling for the sheer rush. They became allies, and joined as a tag team they named after the terrorist organization, International Terror. Igor had discovered many secrets from his tag partner, something more then drugs, wrestling and money, but life after death. Igor lived as a christian, but followed a different path after seeing the proof of Freak's religion. He beleived in the two souls of life, The Soul Of Good, and The Soul Of Evil. Freak taught him about all of it, after giving his proof. Freak is the son of Satin (Spirit Of Evil), and has the title of The Messiah Of Darkness. He revealed his true species, the vampire, with his supernatural abilities. Igor became obbsessed with this new religion and devoting his life too it, while still keeping his terrorist organization in tact.
