Fusion 30 13 Apr 2001 The Georgia Dome, in Atlanta, Georgia Hospital press conference on Frisco's condition The scene opens at what appears to be a breaking press conference in a hospital.  Several doctors in white coats crowd around a small podium, while several reporters sit in a makeshift audience area.  A small graphic in the lower right hand corner of the screen reads "Thursday Morning, Miami, Florida" After a moment, a short, heavyset, doctor of what appears to be Indian descent steps out of the crowd of medical personnel and up to the microphone at the podium.  Several photographers step forward and begin snapping pictures of the doctor, who shields his eyes from the blinding flashbulbs.
