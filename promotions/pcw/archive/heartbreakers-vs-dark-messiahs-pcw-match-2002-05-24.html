PCW match: The Heartbreakers vs. The Dark Messiahs

24 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The Heartbreakers vs. The Dark Messiahs Written: May 24, 2002 Dave: We’re ready for our last tag match of the evening. This is one of the more anticipated matches of the night since we get to see our most former tag team champions in action for the first time in the NEW PCW. Jeff: That’s not why it’s good. It’s good because those gay pink guys fight those weird dark guys. It’s the typical Light vs. Dark; Feminine Men vs. Satanists, these two are complete opposites. Dave: The Heartbreakers have never come out of the closet. Jeff: YET! Duke: Even I have to say that those Heartbreaker fellas do seem a bit on the gay side. Not ass-clown gay but “Let’s have a tea party?” gay. Dave: Thanks for that insight Duke, let’s get to the match. Clay: The following is scheduled for ONE fall and is part of the PCW Tag Team title tournament. Crowd: ONE FALL! ONE FALL! ONE FALL! Dave: I wonder why the fans get so excited over a one-fall match. It must be that energy they build up in the NEW PCW atmosphere. Jeff: No their cheering because this jobber fest will only last one fall. Clay: Making their way to the ring weighing in at 556 pounds from a land unknown are the deranged, the psychotic, the don’t want to bring home to your parents team called DAAAAAAAAAAARRRRRRRRRRRRKKKKKKK MESSSSIAAAAHHHHHHSSSS!!!!!!!! 'Tear Away' by Drowning Pool begins to play throughout the stadium as both men come slowly down to the ring. Both men with angry and ruthless looks on their faces don’t seem to be phased by the new larger fan base their not used to. Jeff: These guys are freaky. I don’t like these outcast type people. Duke: Ain’t nothing wrong with being different ya hear? Dave: These guys sure would fit in well with Darkstar and Clyde. Clay: Now making their way to the ring is the former PCW Tag Team Champions weighing in at a combined 561 pounds from Chicago Illinois are the Pink loving, flamboyant acting team named THE HEAAAAAAAAARRTTTTTT BBBBBBBBBBBBBBBRREEAAAKKKEEERRSSSSSSSSSSSSS!!!!!!!!! 'Pink' by Aerosmith hits the airwaves as the two men come strolling down to the ring posing together provocatively for the fans. Valentine even shows off a little bit of his bright pink boxers for a standing ovation from the fans. Jeff: I’ve just been blinded. The bell sounds and the match begins with Daniel Steele and Jack Valentine in the ring. Dave: This one is on its way. This should be a close one and I’m sure both teams are ready for the unexpected. Duke: What color you like better hoss, pink or black. Jeff: Black. Dave: Well I’d have to say pink. Jeff: You are officially homosexual. Back in the ring Steele and Valentine lock up. Steele headbutts Valentine in the shoulder, steps ack and clotheslines him down to the ground. Valentine shows his fitness by quickly jumping back up. Steele bounces off the ropes and charges at Valentine who leap frogs Steele. Steele turns around and is met with a spinning heelkick to the jaw. With Steele on the ground Valentine smacks his butt cheeks and drops them right on the head of Steele. Dave: Valentine is looking good early in this matchup. Jeff: That isn’t looking good that looks nasty, he just stuck his ass in a guys face. Duke: Heh….. You make it sound like you’ve never done it before. Don’t make me take out those fraternity tapes I have of you. Jeff: How did you get those? *Duke laughs sadistically * Valentine gets a good pop from the fans as he goes off the ropes and throws himself on Steele with a cross body block. Valentine then makes the pin. ………..1 ……………..2 NO! Dave: Valentine went for an early pin, but like always, early pins never get the win. Jeff: I think Valentine is getting excited in the ring. Duke: And I think your getting excited looking at him. Valentine then whips Steele in the corner and tags in Heart who grabs steel by the head and executes a running bulldog. Valentine quickly makes the pace of the match faster as he German suplexes Steele not once, not twice, but three times. The fans gives him a small pop as he slows the pace back down locking the Heart Cancer on Steele. Dave: Steele is in trouble and really needs to make a tag if he wants any chance of winning. Jeff: Didn’t you say that in the last match. Yes, you did, and it’s wrong, bad always triumphs over good. Good Bless America. Duke: Can you do us a favor? Jeff: What? Duke: Kill yourself! The referee goes over to Steele who is screaming in pain looking like he’s about to tap out. Before he does the huge Stone enters the ring and kicks Heart violently causing him to break the hold. The sound of the kick echoes throughout the whole stadium. Crowd: OOOOOOOOOOOO!!!!!!!!! Dave: That was a nasty kick by the big man. Jeff: These big guy small guy tag teams always do well. But the big guys are always the best ones. Duke: Are you stupid, Steele is still huge himself, in no way is he small. Dave: Yea, and if I recall the last real big guys little guy tag team was Golden Game. Are you saying Kevin Klaymore was better than Juan Borro? Jeff: YES I AM! YES ALLRIGHT! SHEESH! Back in the ring both men are down crawling to make a tag. Heart tags in Valentine first but less then a second later Steele tags Stone. Valentine doesn’t hold back as he goes head first into the big man trying to spear him. Stone grabs the charging Valentine by his head, and just starts to slowly crush it. Dave: Oh man, this barbarian of a man has a hand so big he can palm Valentine’s head. Jeff: You know what they say about guys with big hands. Dave: What? Jeff: They wear big gloves. Duke: Damn you’re lame! Stone lets go on the grasp and then gorilla suplexes Valentine right out of the ring. Next Stone goes over to his teammate in the ring and lifts him over his head. Stone then throws Steele to the outside of the ring, with him landing right on the fallen Valentine. Valentine is left gasping for air after losing his breath from the attack. Dave: They call that the Final Turn I believe. Jeff: Why is it the Final Turn? Dave: I don’t know do I look like part of the Dark Messiahs? Jeff: No, but you do look like you could be part of the Heartbreakers. Steel then throws Valentine in the ring, and Stone makes the cover cockily with just one boot on the chest of Valentine. Dave: Stone looks a bit too confident here, using just one foot for the pin. Jeff: He doesn’t have to be worried when he ways over three hundred and fifty pounds. I mean his foot weights as much as Valentine’s whole body. The referee starts the count. ……………….1 ……………………..2 ………………………..(NO!) Before the three count can be made Heart comes flying from the top rope dropkicking Stone to the ground. Valentine notices and rolls up the big man for the pin. Dave: Looks like Stone’s confidence didn’t pay off. Jeff: It’s because he was scared when he saw a Pink thing come flying at him. The referee gets right back down and makes the count again. …………………………………..1 ………………………………………………..2 ……………………………………………………………(NO!) Steele charges in to break up the count, as Valentine goes over and hassles the referee about the slow count. Meanwhile, Heart hits the ring and executes a flying double clothesline sending both Stone and Steele to the mat. Dave: We have four men in the ring, and this one should get interesting. Jeff: ORGY! Duke: Four people aren’t in an orgy you deuche. Jeff: Oh umm…. FORGY! *duke and Jeff nod their heads in shame * Meanwhile, Heart and Valentine grabs both Steele and Stone whipping them to opposite sides of the ring. While in the turnbuckle, both men get support from the fans and smile at one another. Jeff: What are these two planning? Dave: Who knows? Heart grabs Valentines hand. Jeff: They’re coming out of the closet, I KNEW IT! I KNEW IT ALL ALONG I TELL YOU! Heart then whips Valentine at Steel and Valentine twists around giving a butt splash right to the chest of Steel. Dave: They’re not coming out of the closet, they’re delivering none other than their move the BUTT BUDDIES. Jeff: Butt Buddies? Duke: What about their asses. Valentine then runs back and Heart whips him into Steel who he gives the same butt splash too with even more impact. Vaalentine then goes back and send Heart for the ride. The two continue this a few more times. Jeff: This is a sick display of butt use. Dave: Hey as you would say “Whatever gets the win.” right Jeff? Jeff: I take that back. When Heart goes in at Stone this time, Stone stick his boot out at Heart causing him to fall back. Valentine charges in his teammates defense but Stone flips him over his shoulder sending him to the outside. Heart gets up, but Steel comes from behind with a tackle to the knees of Valentine. Dave: Heartbreakers are in trouble it seems. Jeff: Yea, they might just have a heart attack. Duke: Not funny! Stone then lifts Heart by the neck as Steel climbs the top rope. Steel is about to execute his team finisher, but Valentine comes from behind exchanging punches with Steel in the top rope. Stone notices and drops Heart going for Valentine. Heart then pokes Stone from behind who turns around and is hit with a hard superkick to the chin. Valentine then throws Steel off the top rope and jumps his opponent connecting with the HEART ATTACK. Dave: Heart Attack on Stone! Duke: This one in over. Jeff: Damn Pink idiots. Referee goes over and makes the count for the pin. ………….1 ……………………2 …………………………….3 Winner: Heartbreakers (10:12) Dave: A good and clean tag team match up. Jeff: Looked like a bore fest to me. Duke: Your mom was a bore fest for me last night. Dave: Heartbreakers came out with the win, but Dark Messiahs had an excellent showing. Look for this team to do some great work in the tag division down the road. The fans give both teams a round of applaud as they make their way to the back. Dave: Let’s go to commercial break.
