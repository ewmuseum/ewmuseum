Stephan Mechowski Biography Real Name Stephan Mechowski Nicknames The Polish Powerhouse, The Polish Predator Hometown Krakow, Poland Birthday 24th of December, the year 1974 Height 6'8' Weight 327 lbs Biography Stephan Mechowski was born in the town of Krakow, Poland on the morning of December 24th, 1974. It was a cold, blistery day in Krakow, and that can basically describe Stephan. He was never much of what you’d call a “people person,” as he was never really good with his verbal skills. Not many people got close to Stephan during his childhood. One person Stephan did get along well with was his father. The thing that brought these two together, you ask? Combat. Both Stephan and his father Jasiu loved any sort of combat. Wrestling, Judo, Shootfighting, Boxing, Kickboxing, or just plain out street brawling. This was the only thing that brought these two together. The first time Jasiu took Stephan to a local bar to watch some barfights, which were extremely common in Krakow at the time, Stephan was hooked. He loved the intenseness of the competition. He loved the desire to win in these men. He wanted to do what they did. Well, little did Stephan know, soon enough he would be able to pursue his dream. Stephan soon would start studying the different types of combat that he could learn. Brazilian Jiu-Jitzu, Aikido, Judo, Shootfighting, Kickboxing, and Boxing were at the top of Stephan’s list. He loved all of their different styles of throws, kicks, and strikes, but one form of combat caught his eye. Judo. Stephan was uniquely built for it. He had the perfect body type. His muscular upper and lower body was excellent for all of the different throwing techniques and combinations of Judo, which made him extremely dangerous. Soon, he started practicing Judo at a local gym/martial arts studio on the other side of Krakow. He was inserted into a class of 11 students, all of which were about the same size as Stephan, and about the same age. The only difference between Stephan and his classmates was that Stephan excelled rapidly through the course, yet his fellow students struggled. Within five years, Stephan had graduated his course, at the head of the class. Now, with his learning complete, all he needed to do was find somewhere to compete. This was a major setback for Stephan. There were no MMA Associations out there in Poland that he would be able to join right away without requiring Stephan to leave Poland. Stephan would be out on his own in the world if he were to leave Poland and live in the U.S., which he definitely did not want at the time. Then, after multiple small independent fighting alliances through a span of about six years, he found Flatlined. The door of opportunity had opened for Stephan. It was perfect for him. He wouldn’t have to leave Poland, as Flatlined was an international company. After a few negotiations between Flatlined and himself, Stephan gave in and headed towards America in search of a MMA career with Flatlined… …but the door of opportunity that had opened for him, closed quicker than it opened, as Flatlined fighting closed down not even a month after Stephan had joined its ranks. Stephan wasn’t very pleased with the outcome of his venture to America, and was about ready to leave. …That is, Primetime Championship Wrestling saw him. What goes on from now is a completely new story. A story, that is to be written within the wrestling ring of PCW. Other Feds ACW, Flatlined Fighting Other Fed Awards n/a
