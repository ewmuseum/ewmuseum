Charlie Loc Biography Biography Little is known about Charlie’s history, other than the fact that his family sold everything they had to send him to America along with is cousin to escape the tragedy of the Vietnam War. Barely knowing his parents or siblings Charlie, formerly known as Khiem has hardly any recollection of his past other than his mother’s tears and his father waving good-bye to him as he was shipped on a boat with his mother and elder brother to immigrate to the United States to begin a new life. Khiem arrived in New York City when he was 7 years old. Having little knowledge of White people, other than the fact that they were murdering his family back in Vietnam he did not try nor want to fit in. Instead he kept to himself and somehow made his way into the underworld of China Town, attending schooling with the money that his family had sacrificed their belongings for. At the age of 11 Kheim had been dubbed Charlie as to sound more American and had picked up the gist of the English language. Maintaining his school till the age of 13 he was introduced to a life of Drugs and Violence. Learning to take out his anger on the White’s and slowly becoming involved with the Asian Crime Syndicate’s. Charlie’s mother had no idea of this but was aware of his older brother Minh’s involvement in this. As Charlie began to learn more and more about the way that the Asians ran things he began to form a close relationship with his brother before they were seperated due to Minh having a run-in with Juvenile Detention. Charlie was devestated and once again began to keep to himself, letting himself go and get pulled lower and lower into the world of crime. At the age of 15 Charlie had little knowledge of the World of education. His brother had strayed him off the path of good and his mother was beginning to suspect something. Using the tricks his brother had taught him, Charlie began to sneak out late at night and get into mischeif, hanging around with his brothers friends and learning how to commit crime. It wasn’t until Charlie was to prove himself worthy and enter the crew Y.A.D aka Young And Dangerous, when he was caught by his mother. The initiation process was to pull of a daring crime to which Charlie picked a convenience store robbery. Armed with only a black bandana and a machete Charlie robbed the local Corner Store sucessfully with his boys waiting outside to bare witness. Everything ran smooth until Charlie grabbed the cash and forgot to KO the clerk who pushed a silent alarm as he was exiting the store. As Charlie ran out the front, he bowled over none other than his mother who was out getting some Asprin. His mother not knowing who he was began to hit him until police came and unmasked him. He was put on trial before been given a severe warning and a fine, then put on home detention instead of being locked away like his brother. His mother wept many tears for fear of nearly losing her younger son and began to watch him around the clock 24/7. Charlie still began to sneak out and hang with his now 'crew' but kept his crime to a minimum as fear of breaking his mothers heart again. It wasn’t until the age of 18 that Charlie was let off of his home detention and was finally, legally allowed to leave his home again. By this time Charlie had picked up all the tricks of the trade and also worked his way up high in the ranks of his crew by defeating the leader after having a disagreement with the way things were run on the streets. He still bares the scar across his chest from the knife fight today! Charlie was classified as being 'hardcore' and began to make his name known on the streets once more but keeping his head out of sight for fear of harming his mother.
