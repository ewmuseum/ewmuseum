Boda Biography Real Name Chris Sloboda Nicknames Big Bad Boda Daddy Hometown Orlando, Florida Birthday May 27th, 1973 Height 6'11 Weight 335 lbs Biography Born May 27th, 1973 in a nice neighborhood in Kingston N.Y. By the time his brother was born 5 years later, Chris, his brother and mother were forced to move to a poor, mostly black neighborhood in Newburgh N.Y. See, Chris' father took off after David's birth. Chris' mother had to raise her boys, alone. Chris and Dave faced desperate situations every single day. Life was not well, being the only white kids in a school and neighborhood full of drugs, prostitution and racial tension. Boda protected his brother the best he could. As Chris got older, he started to grow steadily, and by the 9th grade, he was 6'8 inches tall. Chris started as the high school basketball teams center that year. And every year after. As he graduated high school in 1991, Chris was 6'11 and a high school all-american basketball star. After playing in the McDonalds All-Star game, Boda was contacted by Dean Smith.... head coach of the North Carolina Tarheels. Offered a full scholorship, Boda left the slums of poverty, and got a education at a fine university, and played basketball for them too. After a four year stint in the University system, Boda graduatd with a degree in business management. More importantly.... Boda got drafted by the upstart Orlando Magic basketball team. They were looking to re-coop from the loss of Shaquille O'Neal. Boda found success, got married, had a couple of kids. Well.... the American Dream came to an end, when in the season of 1998-1999 Boda proceeded to choke Shaq during a playoff game. Suspended, indefinetly by NBA officials, Boda laid around his Isleworth Mansion, for days on end, moping about like his life ended. One day, Boda was talked into going into a wrestling event in Ocala Fl. There, he met Dory Funk Jr, who taught the ways of wrestling. Boda signed up, figuring.... he's been a fan of wrestling since he could remember. While training, he and another student of the Funkin Dojo, were always trying to outdo one another. After graduating the Dojo, Boda went to a local Florida federation, and Mike Harper... aka Popo, aka Badge.... went back to his home state of California. Within a year, Boda finds himself in PCW. After a short stint, that amounted to only an Extreme title reign of 2 days, Boda got mad, changed names to Chris Allen. As Chris Allen, he found a little success, teaming with longtime enemy Homicidal Anthony Cay and becoming Tag Champions, then joining Media Inc. After the firing of HAC, Chris Allen... left. A day later, Boda returned to wrestling, only this time, in PCW2. And this time, helping HAC once again. There, Boda formed The Family with HAC, Lizzy, Rich Cannon and Bo Raider. Boda finally got to face Badge in PCW2, and still... has an undefeated record against Badge to this day. Well, HAC and Boda won the tag team titles within a week, and Boda started a personal fued with a new booker upon drawing first blood, with a steal chair to the noggin. God's Booker.... aka The Reverand John Hull, found himself in the way of a monster. Over the course of the next few month, Boda and God's Booker stopped at nothing to get to and at each others throats. Booker even brought in Prisioner 90210 [whatever] and Jeremy, who were no match for Boda and HAC. After paying his hard dues, Boda secured the PCW2 World Title, and holds it to this day. PCW2 closed, and the NEW PCW reforms, with only 64 well deserving wrestlers getting a roster spot. During a shocking turn of events, Boda discovered his roots. His father never left him and his mother and brother. For Father Sloboda was a Czechoslavakian spy. Sloboda is Czech for 'freedom'. Chris Sloboda is of Czech nationality. Well, Boda thought long and hard, and decided.... if Dad could do it, so can I. Against Meanstreak in Round 2 of the PCW World Title Tourney, Boda turned not only on Streak, but on the fans, and the U.S.A. He joined Ivan and The Red Army. This lead to the Red army becoming a even bigger force. Boda become a two time PCW Tag Team Champion with Nikita Morozov this time as his partner. Boda is the second person to hold the PCW Tag belts with two diferrent partners. Boda eventually became the PCW Continental Champion, winning it from The Chef, who had won it from Ivan. After loosing it to Duke, Boda had been paired as a tag partner with Duke, for the Lethal Lottery Tourney. Boda and Ivan have butted heads, and Boda was forcefully kicked from the Red Army. After Boda wrestled with a serious injury, Boda formed The Wild Deuces at Alliances and has seen the group grow into a success with the help of Mexi, D-Kay and Walden while Boda was out with an injury that required surgery. After recovering from shoulder surgery, Boda fueded with Jason Snow and the New Breed. Snow and Boda fought over the World Title held by Jason Snow. Another fued in that stable war was Boda vs Timo Bolamba, a score that was never settled as Timo left PCW abrubtly. After winning the title at March Madness, Boda lost it three days later to Miyagawa, and in effect, set off the motions that will lead Boda to winning it back one week later. First, destroying the Deuces, secondly, destroying Natalie Miyagawa and the unborn Miyagawa. In the month that came after rewinning the World Title, Boda discovered some more of his past, courtesy of his now enemy, Alexander Miyagawa. Seems Boda has a sister, and her name is Natalie. And it seems Natalie married a wrestler. And it seems that wrestler is Alexander Miyagawa. Boda destroyed his own flesh and blood. Coming around from this, Boda did the only thing he could.... sign a 'Loser Leaves PCW' match against Miyagawa at Supremecy. Well, Boda lost to Miya, after being deemed unconcious. After Boda came too, his parting words to Alexander Miyagawa.... 'Take Care Of My Sister'. And out of PCW, after two long years, went Chris Sloboda.
