Kenji Hirose — PCW biography (November 2001)

21 November 2001

Kenji Hirose Real Name: Belived to be 'Chun Ku-Jae' Nick Name: N/A Catchphase: 'You will see, soon...' Character Handler: Nick MSN Account: n/a AIM Address: n/a Theme Song: 'A Place for My Head' Linkin Park Age: 26 From: Atlantic City, NJ (formerly)Hong Hong, China Height: 5'8 Weight: 190 lbs Wrestling Style: Martial Arts Signature Moves: Dragon Fire (Spinning uppercut) The Serpent (Two footed twisting shotgun kick) Rapid Fire (Bicyle kick) Blast (Spinning side kick) Double Blast (Two footed spining side kick) Throat Chop Knife-Hand Chop Fire-Ball (Two handed thrust at Solar Plexus) Finisher Move: The Hong Kong Blitz - Side Kick/Spining Kick/Elbow Combo Set-Up Move: The Imobiliser - Stun blow to throat Current Team: Kon Xéuhuì w/ Itagaki Freeze Past Teams & Groups: n/a Title History: N/A Awards History: Former PWA Rising Star Description: Kenji looks like a history book picture of Genhis Kahn. He is Broad Faced with, an oriental goatee and his eye color is jet black. His dark brown hair is buzzed short. Usually, he dresses in a tailored black and red tracksuit, with a hood that covers alot of his face. He wears this to the ring, but strips down to his bagy black pants with a black tanktop. His arms are very well built, while his chest and abdomen is moderatly built. His shoulders are large and muscular. He has a large tatoo of a Chinese character that denotes 'power' on his left peck, and a small scorpion on his right shoulder blade. For footwear, he usually wears Army boots that are hidden by his bagy pants. History: Kenji Hirose was born as Chun Ku-Jae and into a poor family in the slums of Hong Kong. His mother died during birth, and his father did an extremely poor job at raising him. As a young boy, Kenji Hirose spent most of his time with his Grandfather, a former Grand Master of the Tiger style of Kung Fu. He rapidly begun to learn the martial arts, as his grand father could teach him little else. By the time he was 14, he would have passed his final black belt examination, but he decided to use his talents elsewhere. That year he was approached by a member of the local Triad. The man was brutally honest. He told Kenji of his fathers loansharking and grifting rackets. His father had apparently borrowed a large amount of money, and was refusing to pay it back. The gang wanted him dead. Kenji was coaxed under the wing of the gang, for the sole purpose of assassinating his father. A few years later, he planted a kilogram of C-4 DEMTEC Plastic explosive in the boot of his fathers car. Little did he know that one of Hong Kong's most influential politicians would be riding with his father that day. The Triad had used him for a political assassination, but he could hardly escape their grasp now. When he was 18, after a year of being a heavy for the growing Triad empire, he was selected to continue the expansion into the world richest market, the United States of America. He lead 6 successful illegal immigrant expeditions, always from Shanghai to California. Once he finally settled in Chinatown, San Francisco, he helped pioneer the import of Heroin into the country. Authorities have suspected him for 4 assassinations, but they have no clue to his name other then the whispers from the local Chinese community about a crazy assassin named only as "The Scorpion". He slowly became out of favour in the Triad community as his tactics where considered too dangerous. He changed his name to Kenji Hirose and moved to Atlantic City, where he made a name as a bare-knuckle fighter. He planed to join PCW as an extension to his fighting career, and to pay back current debts to the Triad, who are financially backing this expedition into the world of Professional Wrestling. He was offered a position in PWA, and he graduated, with his tag partner, Itagaki Freeze, in about a month. Whilst living in Atlantic City, has developed a significant Gambaling problem. Kenji is Currently 26.
