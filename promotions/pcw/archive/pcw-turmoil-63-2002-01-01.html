Turmoil 63 1 Jan 2002 The United Center, Chicago, Illinois Bongos in the dark and the voice of Axl Rose We get the normal opening to Turmoil. The burning city in ruins with the wrestlers running around. Wrestlers highlighted include Frankie Bones, Red Rock, Six Finger Cisco, Xavier Kannon, and Miyagawa. We cut into the United Center. The cameras are ready to do their usual pan around the crowd, but the arena lights suddenly go out, plunging us into complete darkness, and The rhythmatic, primitive sound of the tapping of bongos, shaking of rattles, and beating of drums break in over the sound system. Dave: What the...? That sounds like the beginning of "Sympathy for the Devil", Alan Kriegman's music, but he isn't even supposed to be here tonight. Jeff: Forget the evil super bastard, someone just touched my ass!! Who turned out the lights?? The voice of Axl Rose from Guns'n'Roses adds itself to the music as lighters are lit and thrust into the air, little dots scattered accross the black. "Please allow me to introduce myself. I'm a man of wealth and taste. I've been around for a long long year, stolen many man's soul, and faith. I was around when Jesus Christ had his moment of doubt and pain. Made damn sure that Pilot washed his hands, and sealed his fate. Hehehehehaha, Pleased to meet you, hope you guess my name!" A spotlight pierces through the darkness, shining down on the top of the far right hand side stage, where a band has set up their equipment and are playing on their respective instruments. The crowd immediately explodes into cheers. Dave: OH MY GOD! THAT'S GUNS'N'ROSES!!! THEY'RE PERFORMING ALAN KRIEGMAN'S THEME MUSIC LIVE HERE RIGHT NOW!! Jeff: Which version of G'n'R would that be? They've added and subtracted from themselves more times than a 1st grade math sheet. Dave: Sigh... Axl Rose, Slash, Dizzy Reed, Gilby Clarke and Duff McKagen. Duke: The real question is does anyone know who the hell any of the members of the band are besides Axl and Slash? Pyro of all colors set up on the stage explode into a brilliant flash of light, with the arena lights returning simultaneously. Blue spotlights swirl accross the arena as GNR continue their performance of "Sympathy for the Devil". Oddly, the Jumbotron actually begins to raise up higher from it's setting. Dave: The jumbotron is elevating! What the hell is going on?? The camera quickly cuts to the arena rafters, where an immense, burly man with a crewcut and a big nose is pulling on a chain, which appears to be tied to the top of the jumbotron. This man sweats. Profusely. Dave: That's Jockstrap, Alan Kriegman's evil robo-jock! The robotic brute continues to pull the jumbotron up, causing a hideous screeching sound to couple with Guns'n'Roses for a moment, until the massive screen has been pulled up about 15 feet off its base. There has been considerable damage done to the scaffolding that held it in place. Jeff: I bet that comes out of my paycheck... Dave: Jockstrap just lifted the Jumbotron maybe 15 feet up from it's setting!! The crowd's cheers, which are already enthusiastic due to Guns'n'Roses spontaneously appearing and performing live, and the jumbotron being lifted up by a bullybot, grow even louder when a marching band, all clad in black suits and sporting fake goatees, march their way out from under the elevated jumbotron in perfect unison, playing along with GNR. Duke: A marching band, now. Riiight. The marching band is followed by a team of baton girls in black cheerleader outfits, sporting sparkling silver "K"s on the chests, twirling their batons and throwing them into the air. One girl at the back of the two lines waves a black flag into the air with the image of Alan Kriegman's sinister face on it. Dave: What's next...? Jeff: Looks like a float to me. Indeed, a parade float that bears a striking resemblance to Alan Kriegman's Palmpilot...OF DEATH~!!! rolls it's way into the arena, only fitting under the jumbotron due to Jockstrap pulling it up. The massive Palmpilot...OF DEATH float actually appears to be fully functional, with several lights flashing and a screen on top. A team of scantily clad women in black leather dance atop the float. Jeff: If Barbara starts singing The Wind Beneath Our Ring I'm pulling out an AK and nobody's leaving this damn place alive.
