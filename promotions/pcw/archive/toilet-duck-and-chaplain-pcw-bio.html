Toilet Duck and Chaplain Biography Biography Toilet Duck is not new to Pro Wrestling, though he's not exactly old either. He got his start as a tag team with his half-brother, Cliff Thorpe, in a fed called IVW, Illinois Valley Wrestling, in 1998. The two were a tag team, 'The Metalheads,' in which Cliff was 'Smashin' Sam' and Jack was 'Rockin' Rick.' They sported punk-ish getups, including tattoos, fake piercings, and tattered Misfits memorabilia. That little stint didn't last too long, and the two eventually left. Cliff went on to become 'Mystic,' but Jack stayed out for about two years. Eventually, Cliff called him back in to be part of a new tag team, 'The Asylum,' in the North Atlantic Foundation of Wrestlers, or NAFW. They were a massive success, capturing Tag Gold and several noteworthy accolades. After about two years with the company, Jack quit once more, letting Cliff go on to blaze his own path in professional wrestling. He stayed out of the game for about five months, in which time he desperately tried to find a ways to make a living. He couldn't get any big acting deals, aside from the odd commercial appearance here and there. Chaplain, on the other hand, is very new to wrestling. He was formerly a Catholic priest, who moved to the United States with his family when he was fourteen. He was picked on in school for his accent, which made him angry, and eventually, violent. After trying several different things to vent his anger, such as being an explosives technician, and learning the ropes as a pro wrestler, eventually, he settled on being a Priest in the Catholic Church. Needless to say, he was a bit indecisive about the direction he wanted to take in his life. It was there that he met Toilet Duck. The two hit it off immediately, talking of Pro Wrestling. Chaplain intended to find out more, so he arranged to meet with Thorpe. That night, he caught wind of one of the priests in his church talking about their latest fundraising campaign, which was really a cover for their extortion. He went to the press with the news, and was later banished from the Priesthood. Ultimately, he joined with Toilet Duck as a brand new Tag Team, hoping to learn more and become a good wrestler in his own regard.
