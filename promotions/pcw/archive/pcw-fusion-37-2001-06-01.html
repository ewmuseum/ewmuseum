Fusion 37 1 Jun 2001 The Erie Center, Erie, Pennsylvania Wang, Ruslan and a stern walk down the hallway The scene fades into the backstage area of the Erie Center in Erie, Pennsylvania as Loyal Communist Party Member Wang and Alexei Ruslan are shown walking down a hallway flanked by Wang's two round-faced female cohorts.  All four appear stern faced and apprehensive as tonight their lieges, The Russian Bear Ivan Stanislav and The Polish Punisher Tom Walczak, will face their stiffest challenges to date, facing off against former PCW Extreme Champions Lord Crom Griffin Dagoth and Ryan Knox in tag team competition.
