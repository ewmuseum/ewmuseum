Leave it open so you can see the shirt. Black boots, nothing special. And that's it, height is 5'11. Lust. Passion. Emotion. Wishing I had them, wishing I could feel them. But I can’t, and there is nothing I can do about that. You see I’m not a stupid man. I know the facts of life, I know that sometimes everything isn’t what it seems to be, but I didn’t want to believe that. I was so young…and I was so stupid. I remember it like it was yesterday. The past is sometimes a shade of gray; Jesus Christ I don’t even know my mother’s maiden name. But there is one thing I know. I know how hard it was for me. If I could have done it all again…no I would have made the same mistakes. He’s such a freak. My mother says that he’s a bastard and that we should stay away from him. I peeked out and God I ran after that half eaten anchovies sandwich like it was the last meal I’d ever eat, but then again I never knew what would be my last meal those days. You see back in those days Barcelona was a shadow of what it is now. I would tell you the year, but I don’t even remember what it was. For me school was shelter. There were 5,000 children in the elementary school I attended and to them I was just a name. I went by the name Manuel and I barely ever attended classes due to the ridicule of classmates. I lived in the cellar. The janitor felt bad for me and gave me a spare set of keys in and out of the room. For me it was the cold nights that would stick out in my head. Miguel…. Miguel are you here? I’m in the back. Miguel I received my check from the school today and to celebrate I bought some salmon for us to share. Thank You. Migeul, tell me about your past. Why have you no home? I sometimes wonder if you’ll be able to make it in this world. As do I, as do I. At that time I was still at a young age, I don’t remember the exact age but it was young. All I remember was that three years later I was a reformation came across the school systems in Barcelona. The well to do children that were put in high class institutes, the smart in high learning schools, and the rest in public districts. Then there were the rest of us, the poor. Miguel De Saviour the minister of Barcelona set up a gracious housing system and school for us, or at least HE thought is was gracious. We called it the “El Infierno en la Tierra” because for us, hell was the only word that could describe such a place. SIT DOWN! Don’t make me say it again. I’m sorry, Signorina Morales. Sorry is not good enough, get up here. I’m sorry I will not do it again. GET UP HERE! Now get on your knees and extend your hands. Signorina Morales was what she made us call her, but we all thought she was a witch. Her punishments were beyond human. We’d stay out in the snow bare naked with no food or anything. Sometimes we’d be whipped, she’s throw us in burning hot showers. Anything you think of as incredibly inhumane she did to us. We tried to fight back, but it never worked. Even though this place was a hell, we needed it, because we had nowhere better to go. Then it happened, one day when I must have been around eleven or twelve years of age, my life changed. It’s almost like I woke up one morning and I was a completely new person. I looked different felt different and I held so much power. Only three months later I had completed my studies at El Infierno en la Tierra and I was back on my own. It was hard in the beginning to go back to that unsheltered world. But then I was given a signal. I frequently attended a poets café, I would make a few rhymes and it would pay my way day through day. I was the youngest one there, but they treated me like gold. I was a regular there and everyone knew me. It was one day when I was getting ready to leave that it all changed. Great Job Miguel your writing this week really touched me, I’ll see you same time next week. I’ll be here. I was walking home that cold winter night when I saw her outside. My little Angel! Huh? I turned around and saw a woman in rags with tears rolling from her eyes. Do you need any help? Don’t you know who I am? No I’m sorry but I’ve never seen you before. But you have, I’m you mother. What? And then it came to me, her eyes. They stuck out in my mind like a gunshot. For the first time I remembered the past. I’d have these long dreams of emerald green eyes with an orange tint and then I saw them in her. This was really my mother, and I didn’t know a thing to say. I had enough money now and I was renting this place from a woman with six children. It was small, but it was something. I brought my mother there and I tried to speak o her about my past but it came to no prevail. It was like something was wrong with her, she stuttered her words and didn’t remember anything. I finally had the chance to find out about my past and now it was just slipping away. I knew she was on some type of drugs, but I just wished she could have told me something. All she kept doing was sing this song to me. She repeated the words “My blue eyed boy, so strong, so grown.” I treated her well the next two days and even though she was hard to live with It meant a lot to me to know what she signified. I wish I could have had more feelings toward her, but to me she was just a woman. I did realize a mother was a big deal, but my heart just didn’t let it sink in. That morning I woke up to screams in the front of my small room. I ran out and just as I got there I saw a man pull a knife on my naked and abused mother. He slashed her right across the throat and did it with this sick smile on his face. The closest thing I ever felt to emotions came to me and I used it without thinking at all. I ran at the man and speared him to the ground. He tried to stab me, but I dodged and elbowed him in the chest. I then shoved my foot in his groin and punched him countless time until my fists were filled with blood. His friend then came charging from a car and hit me blindsided. He kicked me a few time before I threw him down and bent his knee back breaking it like a twig I then broke the other and had him on the ground screaming for mercy. I just wasn’t thinking as I planted both of their heads on the curb making them bite down on it. I slammed down on both of their heads busting their jaws so badly it hit their own brains. I felt satisfaction, a satisfaction I wanted to feel again. Revenge. Hate. Anger. They were three words I spoke of so much, but didn’t know a thing about them. I knew if I could have some type of emotion in me I could feel these words deep down in my heart as I spoke them. The night my mother lied there lifeless like some road kill I had the first real emotion I’ve ever felt. I had a moment where I was just pondering, “How could you do that”. I knew these men were animals, and people who do hateful crimes deserve to be punished, and by God I punished them. The lives of the two men didn’t mean much. They happened to be two pimps and my mother was a prostitute of there’s. The police recovered there bodies that night as I peeked over from a near a bush across the street. Right when we thought this city was being cleaned up we have more dirt bags and whores like these ones. Oh well at least there dead now. God I hate being called in the middle of the night just so I could discover some scum lying dead in the street. It should have no liked hearing those words they called my mother, but to me they were just words and these men actually spoke the truth. The next day I was off early and I arrived at the church to go to confession. Church was like a safe haven for me, whenever I felt lonely or like I needed someone the church would be there waiting for me. Bless me Father for I have Sinned it has been about one week since my last confession. Hello, my son what sins have you come here to tell me of? Well father I have done something awful. Something that even God himself couldn’t forgive me for. Tell me son, God is forgiving and hears your prayers. Father I have had the blood of men flow through my fingers in an act of rage. I have shown no remorse and feel but as if they had what they deserved. My poor child, are you telling me that you have murdered someone. Yes, my father but I know it is wrong. You know you must go to the police my child, for it is what God shall see as righteous. I can’t do that; I just can’t live a life of such restraints. I’m sorry but if you can’t, I’m obligated to do so. At that moment I ran out of the church in such a rush that I never saw the face of the priest. I knew it was a bad idea to tell a priest, but I had to so God would here them. Later that night rain began to fall and a storm was coming among us. Everyone was in their houses for shelter, so I used this time to return to the church. It was empty as I expected and I just sat there for hours pouring my soul out to the Lord. O good and ever so kind God, what shall you do with me. I am a fool among fools and I know that one day I will be punished. I then stood in silence and I stood in that silence for the next five years of my life. It was on a warm summer day where I was writing poetry in a journal of mine when I noticed an attractive young woman to the right of me. I looked at her and I smiled. She then nudged me on my shoulder and asked about what I was reading. I’m just writing some poetry for a café I read at. Would you mind if I read some. That’s how it all started. One thing went from another and then one day later I saw myself alone with her at her house. I never thought much of her, other than that she was a pretty girl and seemed to like my writing. Then she grabbed my leg and from there we began to make love. At that time I had so many things on my mind and so many problems that I was always rushing around and not enjoying life. For that small amount of time everything that was against me had disappeared and I felt completely cleansed. I didn’t so much love it for the perversion and lust, but more for the intimate and replenishing feeling. The next day came and I never saw her again. Within that year this happened a few more time, but I never turned it into something dirty or perverted. It was a way for me to release energy and I loved it. Plus, it was keeping me out of my other way of releasing energy, and that was fighting. Or at least it kept me out of fighting until Carlos Morales came along.
