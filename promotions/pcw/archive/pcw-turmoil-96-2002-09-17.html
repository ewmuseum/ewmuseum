Turmoil 96 17 Sep 2002 U.S. Cellular Arena in Milwaukee, Wisconsin Duke Williams and Nerezza's scythe We begin the feed with the immortal words "Earlier today" in the bottom left corner. Clyde's locker room trap Down a lonely hallway steps a massive figure. Raggedly dressed, but a grin splayed across his face....we pan up to the scorched face of the demon known as Clyde. Clyde: Hm. The killer stops before a door, his own name staring at him from an old and tarnished plate. Knowingness sets into his eyes....a familiar look. Clyde knows way too much for his own good, really. In this instance, he knows a trap awaits him. Nonetheless, driven by a morbid curiousity as to what could be awaiting him, he reaches for the knob....opens it, turns. The door creaks, and a small string attached to the inside snaps under the pressure. This in turn causes a trigger hidden inside to fire, igniting hidden gas canisters within Clyde's locker room. A massive fireball erupts from the small room.....it rushes towards the demon. But he does not shy....indeed, he smiles slightly. A chuckle escapes his lips as the conflageration engulfs him....growing to mad laughter as he vanishes into the blaze. Finally after a long moment the arena sprinkler systems come into play, dousing the flames....when they abate, only a single charred body lays still on the floor.
