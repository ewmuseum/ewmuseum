Clyde Clyde Real Name: Clyde Nick Name: The Burned Man, DA MAN Catchphase: Bah! Character Handler: Josh P. MSN Account: [detail withheld] AIM Address: [detail withheld] Theme Song: Seasons in the Abyss by Slayer Age: Unknown From: Texas Height: 6'1 Weight: 260 Wrestling Style: Brawler/Hardcore Signature Moves: Piledriver, low blow, fireball, brainbuster, mandible claw, eye bite, gorilla press Finisher Move: The Shattering (Elevated Tiger Driver 91) Set-Up Moves: Painbringer (Sets opponent's head in a chair, then jumps on it), Asphyxiator (Chairshot to the windpipe) Current Team: None Past Teams & Groups: The Congregation w/Reverend, Dark Age with Vampir Nosferatu, Seraph, Stryfe, Oracle, Darkstar Title History: PCW Rising Star PCW Cruiserweight (by theft) PCW Continental PCW Extreme (4 time) Awards History: Voted Greatest Continental Champion of All Time by a group of men held at gunpoint Voted Sickest Man In PCW by Everyone Manager Name: Bethany Height: 4'0 Weight: 70 Theme Song: Living Dead Girl by Rob Zombie Fighting Style: Hell Spawned 4'0 Testical Mangling Demonic Bitch Finisher Move: Die Die Die Die Die (Crippler crossface) Setup Move: Loss of your Manhood (Repeated puncturing of the crotchel area with whatever sharp objects are handy.....glass, knives, teeth) Description: Basically looks like a nightmare that just crawled out of the gutter.....old torn up clothes, burns over the majority of his face, long black hair.....think Flagg from the Stand if he got caught in a fire and lost his high priced devil gig causing him to be homeless for several years ;) History: Little has been revealed of the killer known only as Clyde......his past, his age, even his last name (which even he admits he cannot remember) remain shrouded in mystery. What is known is that since literally appearing out of nowhere he has gained a reputation as a man who will do anything to cause pain and suffering. Clyde first appeared in the Primetime Indy Circuit......rejected by PCW for his brutal tactics, he began a reign of terror through the fledgling group. One by one vicims fell.....finally the entire federation crumbled, leaving nothing but debris. The burned man survived, though......the burned man always survives. Entering into PCW Clyde started at the very bottom of the ladder.....feuding with the likes of Violator, a very large and powerful yet mildly retarded man and Scourge, a fellow refugee from the bowels of PIC. Eventually the burned man ended their careers.....they now follow him as his eternal lackeys. A tribute to those who would oppose him......a testament of their fate. Clyde made his name known by starting a killing spree that no one ever REALLY put a stop to. cameramen......the homeless.......JJ.......so many have died. And nothing seems to kill the burned man.....he has survived hand grenades, blows from Satan, God and having his testicles crushed by Trashcan Man. Still he moves on.....his eight year old lover Bethany constantly at his side, and the only one who could outdo him in pure evilness. In fact she'll be the one to kill him, or so she says. Until then the bloodshed continues......the body count increases. Bruce Campbell......Yuri Geller...... Funkmaster Johnny G. Diversified are the names of the dead......and only Clyde can say when the murder will end. When there is no one left to kill.
