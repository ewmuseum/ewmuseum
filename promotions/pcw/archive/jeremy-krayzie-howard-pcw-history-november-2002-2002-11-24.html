Jeremy "Krayzie" Howard — PCW history (November 2002)

24 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
History Jeremy is a man that has faced adversity all his life. His wrestling career started back at the age of sixteen, bringing mild success to the Krayzie One. But, it was a fateful day, during the summer of 1998, that Jeremy Howard's life took a turn for the worse. The IWA, a feeder federation to the big leagues, was hosting a championship match between Jeremy Howard and Matt Hinkle. But, it was what happened two days after Jeremy beat Matt Hinkle for the IWA-XXL championship title, that shattered Jeremy's life forever. For, just before the championship match, Jeremy was approached then assistant to the Governor, Richard O'Bannon. The Krayzie One was told, throw the match for Matt Hinkle, or face unrepairable consequences, because Matt Hinkle is Stan Hinkle's son. Therefore, Matt is the son of the Governor of Indiana. Fear ran through Jeremy before the match, but after being reassured by his brother, Jeremy made Matt tap out. It was then, two days later, that police officers stood on the doorstep of Jeremy Howard, ready to take his brother away for false charges of assault battery. Jeremy tried to fight them, but it was to no avail. He was outnumbered. Jeremy fought the police officers with everything he had. Yet, it wasn't enough. This was a major blow that Jeremy never would never recover from, as it launched him into the downward spiral into the Krayziness that he now lives everyday. Jeremy swore to himself that day, that he'd fight against any wayward souls that crossed his path. But, it'd take Jeremy two years before he'd even think of stepping back into the ring after what happened to his brother. One year later, Jeremy would resurface back in wrestling, fighting his way to the top of many federations. But, as the pain of losing his brother ate away at him, the Krazed Lunatic had to go through more turmoil in his life. His girlfriend, Jessica, gave birth to a son, Troy. Before Jeremy could ever have a chance to be a father to this baby though, his son was unceremoniously ripped away from Jeremy and Jessica by Stan Hinkle in a way to exert his power. The loss of her son shook Jessica up so badly that she strayed away from Jeremy and the rest of the world. This left the Krayzie One broken hearted and even further angry. Jeremy vowed then that he'd get back at Stan Hinkle. It didn't matter where Stan Hinkle was, if Jeremy could get to him, the Krayzie One was going to get to him. Jeremy set out to show Stan Hinkle that he'd messed with the wrong man. Two years later, at the age of twenty one, Jeremy's life took a turn for the better. For, as Jeremy rolled through the streets of Gary, Indiana, he spotted something in a dark alley. It was there, that Jeremy spotted the one person in Jeremy's life that could lift his spirits even when thinking about his brother. There, Jeremy found Jessica, a run away from home after a near sexual assault from her father. Jeremy slammed on his brakes, reuniting with the mother of his son. With Jessica now by his side, Jeremy would plot and scheme against Hinkle whenever he got the chance. This would cause Stan Hinkle to be pushed over the edge. Because of this, a non-sanctioned match was set up. Jeremy would take on Matt Hinkle for the rights to Jeremy's brother and his two year old son on Christmas Day 2001. The match itself was long, bloody and hellacious. And, in the end, Jeremy was able to overcome numerous amounts of cheating on the part of Stan Hinkle and Richard O'Bannon with the help of Jessica and a good friend of his to win! Three days later, Jeremy would go to the prison to pick his brother up from the hellhole that was his life for three years. But, when Jeremy got there, he'd get the worst news he possibly could have. His brother chose to turn his back on the Krazed Lunatic, when left with someone known only as a 'dark' figure. To add to that, Jeremy also found out that his son had been placed in a foster home that is apart of an occult, the now current Governor of Indiana, Richard O'Bannon. Once again, Jeremy had been kicked down. But, he refused to stay down. That beautiful blonde that has loved him through everything asked Jeremy to search there son..... And that's exactly what Jeremy plans to do. If his brother wants to go to the dark side, then so be it. The Krazed Lunatic has a new mission now. He freed his brother from the hell he caused him. Now, Jeremy plans to get his son back, before any hell can be brought unto him. Even if it means taking someone, anyone to a psychotic ending!
