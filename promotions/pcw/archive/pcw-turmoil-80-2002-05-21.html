Turmoil 80 21 May 2002 A studio in Atlantic City, New Jersey Boda's PCW legacy in memoriam There are no fireworks. No pyro. No fancy stuff. To greet us to the show, there's just a black background. Slowly, a message in white lettering fades onto the screen. Boda came to PCW in early October of 2000, just a few short weeks after the promotion began. He came to use with a goofy ex-basketball player gimmick, booted from the NBA for fighting too much. His promos were simple, even compared to some of the others in the developing organization, and his attitude was suspect at best. He lost his first few matches, complaining the whole way home about it, and finally caught a break as a replacement in the first ever PCW World Title Tourney, picking up the win, spawning a cry from the announce team that would stick with Boda throughout his PCW career "BODA WINS!!! BODA WINS!!! BODA WINS!!!" Though the glory of his first win was short lived (he jobbed to The Reverend two matches later) and the calls of BODA WINS!!! from the announce team were meant in a mocking, shooting manner (from the announcers and the card writers themselves,) the guy I never thought would amount to much did exactly what the announcers and I mockingly chastised him for. Boda won... ...and won... ...and won some more. Boda compiled more wins in his PCW career than any other wrestler, though the exact number is unknown. He went on to win various titles, ranging from the Extreme, to tag team gold and of course, his two World Heavyweight Title reigns. He grew from a whining loser, to a leader, and went from being an after thought to the center of the booking plans. He always and forever will not only be one of the greatest wrestlers ever to step into a PCW ring, but one of the greatest people ever to be involved in this promotion.
