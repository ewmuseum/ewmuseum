Turmoil 41 31 Jul 2001 The Igloo, Pittsburgh, PA Ivan and Alexei's midnight hospital visit The cameras fade up outside of Fenmore Medical Clinic, late in the night. A small graphic in the lower, right hand corner of the screen reads “Monday, July 30th, 2:47 am” The cameras pan in on the corner of the buildings, as two figures appear from behind the building, standing in front of the now wooden wall that was once a beautiful glass entryway, but was destroyed by a rampage by one Ivan Stanislav a few days earlier, in an attempt to get to his mortal enemy....The Monster they Call Meanstreak. The two figures slowly become more recognizable, one enormous man, one man much smaller, which means really only one thing: Ivan Stanislav and Alexei Ruslan have returned to the literal scene of the crime. Ruslan lights up a cigarette, the light from his lighter casting shadows over their faces. The two men wear black pants, black shirts, and black caps, but how do you hide a 7’1”, 400 lb Russian? Ruslan puffs on his cigarette and nods at Ivan, as the two walk to the front door, now replaced by a makeshift steel door. Alexei pulls out a small gadget from his pocket with what appears to be some kind of lock picking device. He jams a part of the gadget into the lock and hits a button, a short “breep” later, the door is open and the two men enter the building. The scene shifts to the inside of the lobby, as the the two men walk through the threshold silently. Alexei stops Ivan suddenly and pushes him away, pointing to a camera in the corner. The two run to the wall under the camera and look straight up at it, since they’re below the camera, it cannot see them nor can any hospital security personnel that may be watching from elsewhere in the facility. Alexei hands Ivan a pair of clippers, and the hulking Soviet merely reaches up and clips a wire on the camera, disabling it! Alexei grins as he takes back his clippers and slips them in his pocket, finally putting his cigarette out by smashing it intp the wall. The two men walk over to the makeshift desk in the lobby, which was put together hastily after Ivan smashed it on his previous visit to the facility. Alexei gets on the computer and puts some sort of disk into the floppy drive. The computer smokes and dies as Ruslan looks up at Ivan.. Ruslan- No more camera’s! Ivan- How? Ruslan- Just a trick I learned back in the KGB Praporschik...come! The two walk down a hallway and into a stairwell and make their way up to the third floor. Alexei pokes his head out of the doorway and motions for Ivan to follow. They run down the hallway until they are outside Meanstreak’s hospital room, the wall patched up beside the door where it was destroyed by Ivan during his previous visit to the hospital. Ivan smirks as he looks at the fruits of his handiwork. Alexei pulls out his little lock-picking device again and jams it in the lock, one “breep” later the door is open and the two men walk into the room. In the center of the room, on an enormous bed, lays the Monster known as Meanstreak. Oddly enough, he seems to be at peace for once, laying with an oxygen mask over his face, his eyes closed. The EKG equipment monitors his heartbeat, making the situation more-tense than ever as Ivan runs over to the side of Meanstreak, staring down at his prone body. Alexei grabs Meanstreak’s folder, which hangs at the foot of the Monster’s bed, and reads it. Alexei: (nodding) Da Comrade, it is a coma....perhaps I should just slip something into his IV.... Alexei fumbles with his pockets and pulls out a syringe, but Ivan stops him... Ivan- No, let me handle him.... Alexei puts away the syringe as Ivan suddenly clasps his hand over Meanstreak’s throat, obviously attempting to cut off his air supply. Meanstreak doesn’t react much, save for the EKG machine beeping slower. Ivan talks through clenched teeth... Ivan- I should kill him now....for what he did to you Alexei..... Alexei nods approvingly and smiles, while Ivan grits his teeth, squeezing harder as Meanstreak’s face turns red, the EKG dangerously slow, but Ivan lets go suddenly.... Ivan- That would be to easy, however.... Stanislav grabs the side of Meanstreak’s bed and suddenly flips it over, dumping the huge man to the floor, he lays there, the oxygen mask off now. Ivan rolls Streak over so he lays on his back and gets down on the floor, his head beside Meanstreak’s..... Ivan- I know you can hear me Meanstreak..I could kill you right now..but I would rather beat you to death in the ring! Ivan grins, slamming his huge fist into Meanstreak’s stomach, his body shakes lifelessly.... Ivan- You are a coward.....you know that? You should thank God that you are in here....and not on battlefield with me..... He laughs, hitting Streak again. Alexei fiddles with Streak’s report, writing things in on it. Ivan stands and kicks Meanstreak in the side, HARD, almost rolling him over..... Ivan- You pig you will curse the day you crossed me! Ivan starts to kick him violently, and then puts his booted foot over his throat. Alexei grins in finishes writing things on Streak’s report, placing it back where it’s supposed to be. Ivan takes his boot off finally and kicks Streak hard in the side of the face, cutting his cheek open.... Ivan- Got something to remember us by Meanstreak......heh.... Ivan glances over at Alexei and nods, as Alexei pulls out a small object, that looks like a car’s cigarette lighter. He pushes a cap on it for a second, and it pops a second later. When Alexei removes the cap, a red hot hammer and sickle is on the end of the lighter. Alexei hands it to Ivan, who jams the searing metal into Streak’s right bicep, burning the hammer and sickle into his arm. His flesh sizzles for a moment, and when Ivan pulls it away, the hammer and sickle is effectively branded into his arm. Ivan tosses it back to Alexei who puts the object away. The two stand and admire their handiwork..... Ivan- Bastard.... But an improvement to your ugly, flabby, capitalist body! Ivan roars and smashes the equipment around Meanstreak...and then kicks the beast one last time. He looks as Ruslan and the two share a smile and make their way through the doorway out of the Beast’s room....as the scene fades to black.
