PCW — History

Corey Williams — PCW history (November 2002)

24 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
History Corey Williams was born on the streets of New York. He grew up on the streets being called a young hooligan because what he did wasn't right. He learnt how to live on the streets, something which he later implemented into his wrestling. He decided to take wrestling lessons because he thought it would make him be able to hurt people. to be able to defend himself. It was there he met his teacher in wrestling and in life, Don Rolley. The reckless youth finally learnt respect, and used this to focus on professional wrestling. Corey was on the low card for awhile, but Corey was impatient to get up to the top. He finally got his big break in the most important match of his career, Corey managed to win by using a low blow. Bob thought corey had crossed the line and came into the ring after the match and abused him, these brought out Corey's old anger and he turned on Don, beating on the 75 year old man. The public were stunned and the spolight was shon upon the young man. In his eyes, any spotlight, even bad spotlight, was a good spotlight. So Corey learned that being controversial gets you noticed. After the attention died down, Corey craved to be in the spotlight again, and he would do anythign to get it. He worked his way up to a spot in the academy of the world's number 1 fed, PCW. Despite losing both of his matches at the academy, his controversial antics again got him noticed and he instantly graduated and was transferred to the PCW's sister fed, PCW2. He did well there, beating all competitors using his ring knowledge and skills in the art of 'dirty wrestling'. After single-handidly beating current PCWer's NAS and Agent Orange and also getting his name well known after his 'controversial stunts', Corey was considered surefire to get into the PCW after the PCW2 closed down. He seemed surefire that is, to everyone except the PCW selection commitee who demoted him back to the PWA. Corey blamed this on the fact that PCW were afraid of his controversy. He wasn't to be discouraged and left his short-term manager, Smokey J for a contract in Scott Naket's GAW. The GAW closed down without putting on a single show, and they claimed that Corey's contract was void due to the fact that he never wrestled there. This was the last straw for Corey, instead of returning to the PWA to earn his keep, Corey just simply gave up. He had started to doubt himself, started to doubt the philosophy on which his pro-wrestling career had been based on, that 'Controversy, is what the people wanna see!'. Corey soon became old news, people forgot who he was and camera crews stopped following him. The PWA just ignored him and sent him the minimum wage check in the mail every week, which he of course spent on booze until he was dry. One night someone who appeared to be a cameraman named George appeared at Corey's beat-up trailer van standing next to the GAW building. It was later revealed that this man was the nephew of Don Rolley, Corey's former manager and teacher. Don himself came to see Corey and convinced him that he should restart his career, not as the controversial man that he had begun as in PWA, but as the man he had started his career as, as a man that the fans truly wanted to see. Corey got back to basics, working in the gym, something he hadn't done since working with Don. Don showed him how to be a face, and how to work for his victories. The cycle was complete, but this time Corey was a much better wrestler and more acceptable to the PCW who no longer had to 'fear' his controversy. Then he came to PCW as the True Hero, and Don died. After seemingly joining Boda's PCW2 family stable, the Wild Deuces, Corey, along with Snow, Chris Chase and Timo Bolamba swerved~! everyone by revealing themselves as the 'New Breed' of superstars. They stood up against the heelish antics of Management (and to a lesser point coz they could never really get a heel gimmick) the Wild Deuces. Snow and Timo both went missing at this time, leaving only Chris and Corey to hold up the New Breed name (which they didn't do very well). Timo was later kicked out of New Breed after a short power struggle which resulted in Chris Chase and Corey Williams 'choosing' Snow as their leader and turning heel. Timo fled to OSW after a short feud with New Breed. Snow again went missing after being injured at the March Madness main event Battle Royal, which he came into as champion. Corey got thrown out 1st, Chris got thrown out 4th last. Chris and Corey are again left to hold up the New Breed legacy, which has changed a lot since last time. After being thrown out first in the March Madness battle royal Corey begins to become a little more agressive. He occaisionally snaps and beats up PCW employees (and angle which had to be toned down because Walden was doing a similar thing). Asylum and the Wild Deuces, the two strongest stables after Management falls, break up. Chris and Corey claim New Breed to be the most successful stable in the history of PCW. They then prove this by taking out the former leaders of the now-defunct Asylum and Wild Dueces, Miyagawa and Boda on numerous occaisions. Corey is set to face Miyagawa at the PPV, but Miya tries to get into the main event and escape Corey's wrath. He challenges Chris and Corey to a handicap match, if he wins he is upped into the main event. Unfortunately Corey finds out that his Father has died just moments before the match. He tries to leave but Chris stops him and brings him back. In no condition to wrestle, Corey makes an amateurish mistake whilst not focusing and injures his ankle. Boda comes out and costs Chris Chase, the man who won him his title, the match. The main event is Miya Vs Boda Vs Chase and after interference from Snow, Chase wins. The next night Chase is scheduled to face BAS and defend his title. But a non-promptu match is made by commissioner Kannon between Snow and Chase. Chase purposely jobs to Snow, who becomes Heavyweight champ. Chris Chase is beaten by BAS later that night, but the title has already changed hands. Snow comes out and interferes, along with Corey Williams and Dustin Downey. PCW World Champ Snow reestablishes himself as the leader of the New Breed.
