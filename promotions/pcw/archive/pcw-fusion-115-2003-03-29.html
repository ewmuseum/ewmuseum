Fusion 115 29 Mar 2003 The Long Beach Arena, Long Beach, California The orange suit and a defeated mind The scene shifts to the backstage area of the arena where a multitude of people and going to and fro, scurrying about and doing their business. The camera pans around and the crowd's cheers are audibly loud in the background as Jonathan Drachen comes strolling up the hallway with a grim look on his handsome face and a duffle bag slung around his shoulder. Giving a cold, irritated shake of his head, he approaches one of the many uniform doors in the corridor, paying no heed to those who give him a wondering stare, and flings the door open. The camera pans around to the back of him and catches sight of what has now revealed itself to the Pizza Man: A dirty orange jump suit, like you'd see in prison. Something draws Drachen near, but something also tells him to back away... Jonathan looks at the note a few times, then shakes his head defiantly. He then looks away from the camera and walks over to the wall, shaking his head more violently. The camera moves in from behind to the side of Jonathan's face as his head inclines downwards. His eyes seem to have a defeated and scared look in them, like a cat chased into a corner. He starts to tremble a bit, not a lot, more like a splash of a stone against a pond than a wave. Sneering again back at Chet, Pharaoh heads out of the ring as “Wherever I May Roam” hits and Chet is left in the ring, while the fans boo Pharaoh. Dave: Well, it looks as like things aren’t getting any better between those two. Jeff: If Chet would just stop hitting on him, we’d be alright. Dave: We’ll have to hear Teresa Tomas’s reasoning….but right now I’d say she’s the most hated woman in PCW. Jeff: I like her, actually. As the PCW logo comes up in the corner, the last shot we see is a shot of Chet leaning against the ropes, watching intently as Pharaoh leaves through the curtain. Dave: For Jeff Marx, I’m Dave Kern and perhaps we’ll find some more out on Turmoil. We see Chet, then a zoom-in shot to his face, and then, without further adieu, the screen fades to black.
