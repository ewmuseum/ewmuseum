The Masked Mullet Biography Real Name Clem Jankins Nicknames The Mullet, Mullet, Clem Hometown Lemmon, South Dakota Birthday unknown.. Height 6'1' Weight 157 lbs Biography Clem came from a small town, called Lemmon, South Dakota. He always dreamed of being a wrestler, though, he was always deemed too small, so he got a job close to the action. He joined the wrestling world as ring crew. He happily worked along side the wrestlers, constantly the butt of jokes, due to his looks, and redneck ways, but he still felt like he was living his dream. He joined CCW, as ring crew, and stayed there for quite a while. At least, until his dream came true. An injured Cianan, CCW champion at the time, and owner of CCW, as well. Became injured, and his increasing paranoia caused him to look for some 'muscle.' no one applied, exscept Clem. Cianan begrudgingly took him on, and gave Clem a shot at being a >wrestler, which Clem has never forgotten and to this day thanks, and is loyal towards his 'dream maker' Cianan. As The Masked Mullet, he struggled to over come adversity, beating opponents like Bojangles The Klown, and Devyl, and eventually winning #1 contendership to a hardcore title. The title shot never came. However, he did make quite a name for himself being as a 'hardcore icon.' (though, he never won the title) After struggling and adversity, he managed to beat several people, and win the newly re-instated Light Weight Champion, over the likes of Kyle 'Velocity' Lamen, Kensuke Suzahara, and Jesse Ramey. After a small, and rather painful experiance in the MET Tournament, (and losing to Lilith in a house show). He sank back into obscurity He came into RUSH with a bang, facing off against the confirmed murderer, Caleb McGlocklan. Mullet faced off against the accusations, and pulled off a critical win against Caleb at Rising From The Ashes. Everything was going fine, launched into a RUSH World Title Tournament, when Sylkk came to town.. and immediately.. well. You can see where this Bio is.
