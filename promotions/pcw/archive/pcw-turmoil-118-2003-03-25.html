Turmoil 118 25 Mar 2003 The ARCO Arena, Sacramento, California Agents Jackson and Hilton arrive at the arena The scene opens up to the parking lot where a dark blue Ford Explorer comes to a stop, outside the entrance to the arena. The driver side door swings open and out steps a rather small, but built man. His black suit clings to his body and his face is one of all smiles as he walks toward the passenger side door. He opens it and takes hold of the hand that sticks out. With the help of the man a young lady steps out and jumps toward the ground. She pushes her blonde hair out of her face and brushes the hem of her gray skirt. The couple begins to make their way towards the arena doors. Both are laughing and smiling as they talk. The couple reaches the doors and a massive security guard meets them. He is holding a clipboard and talking to another guard next too him, the four all go silent as they get closer to each other. Security Guard: I am sorry, folks, this entrance is only for wrestlers and authorized personal. Man: I do believe I am at the right place then. So, if you could just go a head and step to the side and let me go about my business, I think everything would work out. The man smiles and snickers a little at what he just said to the guard. Guard: Well, unless your name is on the list you aren’t getting in. Man: Look I have very pressing business to attend to with Mr. Kannon and I don’t want to be late. The man looks at his watch and lets out a sigh. Guard: Well if your name isn’t on the list I can’t let you get in. Man: Check the list for a Jackson and Hilton and then let us go in. The guard looks over the clipboard and then looks back up at the man. Guard: Sorry sir, the only Jackson on this list in Dante and I don’t think you are him. Man: Look, I am here under the order of Xavier Kannon. His smile is slowly fading as a look of anger replaces it. The guard gets on the radio and tells his boss everything that has happened and then stands there for a minute listening to the response. He looks back to the man and smiles. Guard: I am sorry sir, you were correct. Mr. Kannon is waiting for you. I am so sorry for the mix-up. Man: You should be moron, now get out of my way. I have pressing issues to attend deal with and then maybe when we are done with those. We can get to the firing of you. The man’s smile returns as he takes the women’s hand and pushes past the two guards. The new intro sequence for Turmoil hits the screens accompanied by the song “Plowed” by Sponge. The usual wrestler highlight reel appears on the side, showing Jeremy “Krayzie” Howard, Ellie~! and Xavier Kannon in the ring, plus The Overweighter, El Diablo, AJ Trinidad, Gynaeco Joe and Clyde. The screen then cuts into the ARCO Arena, Sacramento, California where Dave Kern and Jeff Marx sit at the announce table. The cameras around the arena pan to see various signs such as “Chris, Marry Me!!”, “Krayzie Forever” and “I miss Roadrunner!” Dave: GOOOOOOOOOD EVENING, WEST COAST CONSUMERS! Jeff: Boris Nakobi’s going to sue, ya know. Dave: Isn’t he Blaze Inferno now? Jeff: Does it matter? Hell, does HE matter? Dave: No, but here’s what does matter! We’re going to decide the new PCW World Champion tonight! It’s going to be 10 men in that ring, each one desperate to get that title! With the departure of Duke Williams, who’s going to win the most desired prize in PCW? Jeff: Plus the rumour of the 11th man, Dave. It could be the returning Avalanche~! Dave: Avalanche was blown up at Alliances 2K2, remember? Jeff: Then it could be Jobby Stalk-clinger! Dave: No, he’s too busy trying to sort reality out so he can apply to HBF. Jeff: Then… ANARCHY!? Dave: She’ll be back one day, we just don’t know when. Drachen evades rabid dogs and confronts Tracer in the parking garage The scene shifts to the side parking lot of the arena area, reserved specifically for employees of the venues and their temporary guests in PCW. Automobiles of all sorts are lined up in neat little rows, with the occasional vehicle out of line for whatever reason. Drachen: What the… And there, standing menacingly on four legs… actually, EIGHT… are a couple of rabid-looking German Shepards of astonishing size. Ok guys, stay back..come on…I’ll give you a treat… They don’t pay attention to Drachen, however, and begin to advance forward. When they get a little too close for comfort, Drachen bursts into a frightened sprint across the garage. The rabid canines are close behind, snapping at his heels and letting out frantic barks. People dive off in different directions, trying to dodge the mad stampede of man and beast. Drachen: SHIT!!! SOMEONE STOP THEM!!! But no one even tries. Beads of sweat begin to rain down from Drachen’s face as he tries to outrun the rabid beasts, which are closing in fast. Drachen sprints around a set of boxes and notices a long support beam next to the trailer of a semi truck. He makes a mad dash for the wooden beacon of hope and jumps onto it, clinging to it for dear life. Drachen quickly scoots himself up the beam and jumps on top of the trailer, sprawling out in exhaustion. Drachen, exhausted: God… damn… *Huff, huff* Voice: What’s going on here?? Drachen: …huh?
