Badge Biography Biography Mike Harper grew up in the poor side of Oakland California. Though his family was rich, they opted to disguise their fortune by living in the middle of the ghetto. This decision by Mike’s parents meant that he was the only white kid in his neighborhood. Growing up, he was often the number one target for most of the bullies, though he quickly learned how to defend himself. When Mike’s sophomore year in high school came around, he was 6’7 and was a standout on his local high school basketball team. By the time Mike graduated he was 7’0 and was heavily recruited by many of the top schools in the nation. Things would take a terrible turn in his life, when Badge’s parents were killed right in front of him. To this day it is unknown to anyone how they died, or what events led to their deaths but something snapped inside of Mike. He could no longer control himself. He became violent, stopped caring for anything but redemption, and revenge. As the year went by, the recruiters moved on and Mike was only left with an offer to go play on a full ride scholarship to the University of San Francisco. Badge chose to play for the Dons, though he was more concerned with revenge, and didn’t play up to potential. He finally graduated with a degree in Criminal Justice. There he immediately joined the police academy, and onto the Oakland police who just so happened to be desperate for help. Officer Mike Harper quickly became known in Oakland, as the cop most of the lawbreakers were afraid of. His violent nature kept him from being promoted, but he was still an indestructible force for the Oakland Police. After roughly three years with the force, and having tried to control the demons that were haunting him, Mike quit the police force. At the time he had become a fan of professional wrestling, and opted to become a professional wrestler instead. Mike heard that the best teacher of professional wrestling was Dory Funk Jr, so Mike got on a plane to Florida. While there Mike met up with another aspiring wrestler by the name of Chris Sloboda. Though Mike had more potential, he was too violent, and often injured the other trainees. Chris passed the school as the #1 student and moved on, while Dory sent Mike to Mexico to help improve his skills. While there, he drastically improved his speed and agility working with the luchadore wrestlers, and even picked up a few high risk moves before he left. When Mike returned to the states, he joined up with the independent wrestling circuit letting his abilities and charisma quickly boost him to the top. During a show, Mike was scouted by Primetime Championship Wrestling and was offered a contract. Mike was still not ready for the big time, so he was sent to two developmental leagues the Primetime Indy Circuit, and Neutral Zone Wrestling. While there, he began the gimmick that would carry him to the big time. “Popo” as he was known at the time, was one of the biggest heels, as everyone had a small hatred for the police. As Badge grew popularity, he was recruited to be one of the original members of a new secondary fed of Primetime Championship Wrestling called the PCW2. As Badge went through his first couple of matches, it was hard for him to adjust, and he was unsuccessful. Then came the man called “God’s Booker” a man that would guide Badge to help him become an indestructible force in the PCW2. Another familiar face was Chris Sloboda, who often butted heads with Mike as they were always trying to one up another. Mike still wasn’t till the level God’s Booker had hoped, until a psychotic Amish revolutionary calling himself Jacob Yoder became obsessed with him. Yoder kept pushing and kept pushing, until Mike couldn’t ignore him anymore. The two began what was arguably the longest and most heated feud in recent memory. Mike and his new enemy Yoder would always get in each other’s way causing a severe hatred for one another. After a long and exhausting battle, and with no one ever gaining any advantage, the feud would die. What wasn’t known to many was that Mike and Yoder were being recruited by PCW CEO Tyler Nelson to help him keep control of the PCW. Before Mike jumped ship, he had a meeting with several PCW executives. In one meeting with the Phantom Booker himself, TPB suggested that Mike change his name to a much more serious one, and suggested Badge. Though Mike was stubborn, he agreed and signed over to the PCW. In one last meeting with an unknown PCW executive, Badge was convinced that he needed to tone down his violent nature so that he didn’t hurt the big stars in the PCW. Badge agreed, and went to see a psychiatrist, who put a hypnotic spell on Badge, causing him to forget most of everything that made him angry. As Badge jumped to the PCW, he was joined by Ryan Knox, Jack McGriff and longtime enemy Jacob Yoder, to form GREED INC with the CEO in charge of the reigns. During Badge’s first match with his new federation, his new business associates would help Badge defeat Frisco to win the Continental and Television titles. Unfortunately, that would be the high point in Badge’s time with the PCW, as Badge lacked the same overpowering command that made him famous in the PCW2. Badge would gradually drop from the rankings, before leaving GREED INC to form his own company. Badge, now obsessed with money hired Tim Walden, Big Daveylicious, and Tom Walczak to form a business controlled by whomever would be willing to pay them, known as Thugs For Hire. After business went stale and T4H fell apart, Badge went on a heavy drinking binge. During that period, and in a wild weekend in Las Vegas, he met a stuggling psychiatrist student by the name of Dana Stevens. Dana was a big fan of Badge and the PCW, and took a special interest in him,sticking by his side, and helping him out of his alcohol problem. Badge returned the favor, keeping Dana by his side, and when PCW management saw the busty brunette, they offered her a contract to become Badge's valet. With Feletia by Badge's side he was approached by a few of his old PCW2 friends. Badge and his PCW2 comrades agreed to align themselves together at Alliances. The Wild Deuces dominance would be short lived, as two of the members would turn their backs on them at the very next show, thus sending the group into an immediate tailspin. Though most of the members left, Badge stayed loyal. It was then at a PCW show that a fellow stablemate would repay Badge for his loyalty, by pushing him off of the stage, causing several injuries to the corrupt cop. After a long and difficult recovery, Badge would be abducted by the most evil duo known to man, Clyde and Bethany. In a plan to use Badge in his evil plans, Clyde would erase the spell put on Badge by the psychologist, in hopes of awakening the sleeping demon inside of Badge. Again Badge would have the hatred and disregard for his own body that once made him feared by all who crossed his path.
