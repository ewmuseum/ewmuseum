Agent Matt Jackson — PCW move list, June 2003

12 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is The move list of Agent Matt Jackson’s page on PCW’s own site as the Wayback Machine captured it on 12 June 2003, the day before Fusion 126: his style, set-up move and finisher, the account of how he fights and his five commonly used moves. The page’s closing ‘Back’ link is not carried.
Wrestling Style: Technical/Highflyer/ Martial Arts Set Up Move: Nuclear Fission (A reverse backflip splash) Finisher: The Bourne Ultimatum (Matt picks them up like he is going to powerbomb them, but instead of holding them up he lets their upper body fall down and steps over the opponents arms sending them face first into the mat) Other Moves: Most of Matt's moves are based on quick pace matches, he tries to keep the match going, as fast as possible, letting his speed and endurence wear out his opponents. Being most people are bigger then him, he will keep to the high flying aspect and use moves that keep them on the mat. He knows a lot of martial arts from his CIA Training, most of the time, he will be defensive about it...blocking punches, kicks, and other moves that are thrown at him. He knows some attack martial arts, but not a ton. He mainly uses the attack martial arts, against men who are much bigger then him, 30lbs or more. Commonly Used Moves: Stairway To Heaven: Split-legged Moonsault Fatal Impact: Shooting Star Press Jackpot: Flying Headscissors Facebuster The Annihilator: A superkick, but instead of under the chin it is to the throat. Knocking On Death's Door: Using his arms as support, Matt using one leg to hook one of his opponent's ankle with a foot. Then with the other leg he kicks -into- the same leg causing the opponent to topple over on that one leg.(Note he only uses this on people who are taller then him, as it wouldn't work as well on a shorter person.)
