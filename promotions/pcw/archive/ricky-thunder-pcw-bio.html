Ricky Thunder Biography Wrestler Name Ricky Thunder Biography Ricky began watching wrestling when he was young, growing up with his older brother, Shane Thunder. They lived in Perth, Australia with their parents, whom were still happily married. Shane began working out throughout high school, and playing football and what not. Well his junior year he had a growth spurt that shot him up several inches in height. Needless to say his mobility was a bit awkward. Shane persisted though as Ricky watched him working out day in and day out. One day while at the gym, Ricky pointed out a sign to Shane. 'CCW Tryouts'. This was everything Shane had dreamed of. Shane worked long and hard until the day came when he got his tryout for CCW. He battled throughout suplexes, and ddt's, he took bump after bump only to jump right back to his feet. His resiliency impressed the judges. He made the CCW team. During his first year Shane Thunder spent most of his time behind the scenes only working dark matches. Shane one day got the call that he was the opening match for Friday Fury. He was over joyed upon hanging up the phone. Ricky who was now 15, was sitting in the front row watching his brother getting paid to do what he loved. That is when he decided for sure this is what he wanted to do. After the show had ended, Ricky met up with Shane who agreed to show him a few holds and moves. Shane demonstrated irish whips, and snapmares, all of the basic old school moves. Ricky growing bored decided to find a small time Indy circuit of his own. By the age of almost 16, Ricky had found a fed. It was small time, maybe enough people to fill a high school gymnasium came to the events. But it was doing what he loved. Ricky came out and put on the show of his life. The basic moves his brother taught him combined with his training, he had won the crowd. However, after his match was over, several people rushed from the back to assault him, in the natural 'heel sense', but a botched swinging neckbreaker took Ricky right back out of the game. A few days had passed when he got the call. It was his brother, Shane. He was calling to tell Ricky that he had won the CCW heavyweight title, but he had lost it back to the man he defeated for it. The Supreme Machine. Ricky congratulated his brother, but then picked up a quiver in his voice. Shane then explained to Ricky that he had been diagnosed with terminal cancer and he was dying. Ricky dropped the phone, as he tried to get out of bed. His dad rushed in and restrained him, then picked up the phone and received the news himself. 2 weeks later Shane Thunder died. On the way home from the funeral, they were blind sided by a dunk driver, causing even more injury to the neck of Ricky Thunder, but even worse, killing his parents in the process. 2 years had gone by, with extensive rehab and therapy on Ricky's neck, allowing him all the mobility he once had. In honor of his brother he vowed to be the best. He finally received a contract with a small time fed, that was known as the NSWA, where he quickly became a fan favorite and held every title there. While Heavyweight champion, a disagreement between management prompted a cancellation in his contract, and he was terminated. In his effort to sink the fed that he had created, the fan base was, basically shot to nil. They had supported Ricky and once they got wind of this, they decided for the last time, NSWA was finished. Soon after these events Ricky was contacted by his brother's former fed, the CCW. Ricky quickly accepted the offer and was thrown back into the spotlight again. He only spent about 6 months in the CCW, where he became the 2002 King of Extreme, and the Independent Champion. He then left CCW, dropping his title on one Ricky Coyote, after defeating him in his final CCW match. Upon leaving CCW, Ricky joined PCW. He outlasted many of the big names in the extreme title brawl only to lose to Miyagawa in his debut. Lovecraft also managed to outlast him, only because he hid throughout the match. In his second match he defeated Makonie via DQ, thanks to the interference by Crichton and Murray. The following week, Ricky suffered a knee injury at the hands of the same men.
