Fusion 74 15 Mar 2002 Omaha Civic Auditorium in Omaha, Nebraska The White Mexican unconscious in a hospital room The telecasts starts and we fade into a hospital room. The calm *beep beep* of the heart monitor serves as the rhythmic opening sound. As the camera flies around the room we can recognize that the unconscious man is none other then the White Mexican. A shadow appears lingering over Mexi when the feed cuts to black. Chris Chase's multimedia promo with static and cryptic words “On the first of January…1979…well, that was a glorious day…an empowering day for all of mankind” FZZT! A voice eminates onto a pitch black scene, the eccentric tones of the Canadian accent pick up well as the voice continues, to be segmented by occasional burst of Television Static noise… FZZT! “Heh…I suppose now’s the time that I claim to be the herald of PCW…being so freaking cliché that it rips the eyes out of the sockets of fair spectators…fair unknowing spectators…” FZZT! A wisp of hair, of brown hair can be seen floating on the screen, followed by the letters…C…and…C. The voice continues to talk…witticising cockily to himself… FZZT! “But oh no, clichés aren’t exactly my strongpoint, and as I’ve proven in the past, I’m no herald, no holy messenger sent from above to save you all…” FZZT! The enlarged facial features of the speaker fade onto the screen, not revealing exactly who this person is just yet, toying with the emotions of the arena spectators. His lips move, releasing the next battery of sentences… FZZT! “I mean, heck, you don’t really have to parade the fact that you just happen to be blessed, on the genetic and fortune level, with somewhat of a spectacle on your hands. It’s disappointing to be labelled as a some preacher, a prophet. Prophets and holy Heralds…they don’t exist…they don’t matter…” FZZT! The lips contort into a cocky smirk, as the camera gradually zooms away… FZZT! “So…enjoy the show…” Silence… “No really…enjoy it…” Silence… “Do I really have to draw you a picture?” The screen, finally fully zoomed out…reveals none-other than ‘The Messiah’ Chris Chase, who, after asking that last question…simply grins as an answer, grins arrogantly as his image fades away and the usual introduction for Fusion begins to air… The usual Fusion opening plays featuring members of PCW’s new tag teams, Samburu, Cowabunga Connection, Misplaced, Fear Factory and the yet unnamed team of Big D and Frampton. Chris Chase addresses the crowd about Boda and Miyagawa Dave: Welcome to the sold out Omaha Civic Center in Omaha, Nebraska… Suddenly, to a mixed chorus from the crowd… ‘One Vision’ by Queen hits the speakers, blaring loudly as the lights dim slightly and the opening chords begin to melodically play… Jeff: NEW BREED~! NEW BREED I SAY~! Dave: (Sighs) Well…Chase does have some explaining to do after Tuesday’s fiasco. Jeff: If you mean stripping Miya-jobber of the Title…then that’s all well and good. Handing it to Boda, though…well, that sucked… Duke: Boda does suck, doesn’t he? Jeff: Nah…Chase should have kept it for himself. He’s too fucking generous! Finally, after about a minute and six seconds…the culminating melodies of the One Vision intro cut out…and into a loud riff signalled by the setting off of a pair of white pyros on each side of the stage, as a young figure appears at the top of the ramp…to an ever increasing mixed reaction… Dave: Wow, Chase actually has a lengthy entrance for once… Jeff: Yeah, as soon as he smacked Boda around the back of the head, that two lines of his shot up to around forty. Not surprising really…the next thing we know, he’ll be replacing Duke at the announce table… SMACK!! Duke: Don’t even think about it Hoss… Jeff: No…I’m just imagining it… SMACK!! True to the theme music, Chris Chase begins to stroll down to the ring, slyly eyeing up the crowd as he walks by. Eventually, he slides into the ring and taunts the crowd before receiving a microphone and the music cuts. Chase, microphone in hand, leans against a turnbuckle…silently gazing around…before making an initial remark… Chris: And if Xavier Kannon could quit whining so these people could hear me…then that would be great. The crowd boo at the name of Xavier Kannon, enemy of Jeremy Howard, as Chris grins…pushing himself into the center of the ring… Chris: ~(Mimicking a Child)~ Why mom? Why, Why, WHY? Why did Chris Chase seal a deal with Boda? Boda sucks, so why would Chase fraternize with him? The smirk remains on Chris’ face as he removes the child mimic as the crowd boo at the mention of the current PCW World Champion… Chris: Two words, two words out of all of those used in that question happen to be the answer. Why ask me, when you know damn well ‘Why’? ‘Boda sucks’…simple fact really, the guy had to hire Downey to beat me, his “Family” blew more than sHs for MY sake and don’t even get me started on the whole “Comrade Sloboda” thing! Let’s face it, having Boda as the Champion is inane…yet wholly useful…don’t you think? Jeff: YES! Dave sighs Chris: I mean, had Miyagawa remained Champion, I’m one hundred percent sure that most of you people would be leaping around yelling “I’M GOING TO KILL YOU!” at the top of your voices, before doing ten to twenty in a state penitentiary after a C4 explosive related incident. Now, with Boda as your influence in life…then you’re placid, harmless…even perhaps, docile. Sure, you’re probably the human equivalent of an aging Labrador retriever, like Golden boy here…but then again, what price is there to pay? Chase pauses again… Chris: That suddenly every PCW fan turns into a former NBA player and immense waste of oxygen and hemoglobin? It’s just a great job that the effects won’t last for THAT long. At least, not when yours truly is running the show. Boda…I will become Number One Contender for that belt on Sunday… Jeff: Chef! Duke: Turncoat! Jeff: Fool! Smack!! Chris continues in a lighthearted, yet highly cocky tone… Chris: And then, I’ll do something that I’ve been waiting since PCW2 to do…to beat you for a World Title…the PCW World Heavyweight Title no less. A title far more prestigious than cretins like you could ever appreciate…now then…as far as that Goggled Gigalow goes… The crowd pops loudly at the mention, albeit insulting, of Miyagawa, former PCW World Heavyweight Champ… Chris: Well…he’s just another one of those has-beens that I’ve laid waste too…and will continue doing so. I didn’t enjoy what I did on Turmoil, I didn’t enjoy letting Boda take the glory. But what’s at stake between Boda and myself has much more of an implication when that title is thrown into the mix. It’s your loss Miyagawa…just don’t make sure it doesn’t drown the rest of your career… Chase pauses once more, wrapping up a short segment… Chris: And you can Justify…that! Dave: We’re going backstage, early action… Corey Williams finds his misspelled locker room sign and loses his mind Our camera cuts backstage where we see one "Controversial" Corey Williams walking down a corridor, wearing his usual attire of black leather pants and a bear fur coat, he is carrying a wrench that he recently bought from a department store in his right hand. Corey stops a male staff member who is passing by. Corey: 'Scuse me, do you happen to know where the New Breed locker room is? Staff Member: It's down the hall and on your third right. Corey walks on, without a word of thank you to the staff member. He walks past to doors on the right and reaches the third one. He turns and stops, staring at the door. He then begins to kick the sign on the ground, fury in his eyes and saliva flailing out of his mouth in all directions, like a rabid dog. He looks up to see the same staff member, looking on in disbelief. Corey: WHAT THE HELL ARE YOU LOOKING AT!? Corey: F*cking peasants…… (Corey turns to Dustin) Where's Chris? Dustin: I dunno... Corey: Damnit!
