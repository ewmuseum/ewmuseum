SOCIETY Biography Real Name Classified information Nicknames Experiment #67345, The Beacon of Normalcy, The Conformist Hometown Somewhere in the Falkland Islands, off the coast of Argentina Birthday Classified Information Height 6'10' Weight 357 lbs Biography Appearance: SOCIETY stands 6'10', weighing in at 357 lbs. He has short, closely cropped blond hair, and blue eyes, and is clean shaven. He goes bare-chested to the ring, to show off his massively muscled chest, with the word 'CONFORM' tattooed across it in huge letters. He wears a pair of black dress pants, with a black belt. Black, military style combat boots complete the outfit Biography: The Falkland Islands, off the coast of Argentina, is the home to a secretive think tank known as the Order of Society and Logic. The organization stands for monarchy, absolutism, and a society predicated on order, logic, and uniformity. Needless to say, the world does not share many of these ideals, and this is one of the reasons the group makes its home in obscurity. After existing for twenty years, however, the group could no longer wait. Under leader Michael L. Chang, they vowed to bring their new world order into existance. This was in roughly the early 80s. In order to do this, however, they needed a champion. They soon realized that they could never indoctrinate one fully, unless they started at a very young age. Working in secrecy with a sleeper agent in London, set up for this very purpose, an O.S.L. female was implanted in a London hospital with an embryo that had been created, but had been frozen away. Nine months later, she gave birth to what Chang dubbed 'Experiment #67345'. The child was brainwashed and indoctrinated with the group's theories. He turned out to be even better than expected, as he grew to the height of 6'10', and weighing 350 lbs., all muscle. He enjoyed a variety of sports in his free time, including rugby, baseball, and amateur wrestling. The group was ecstatic at their success. They finally had the champion they needed. But, they had to figure out a method to introduce him into the world. They composed a massive list of assassinations for him to carry out, of government and entertainment figures the world over. But, it would be too obvious and risky to send him on assassinations without a cover. They found that cover in professional wrestling. In a world of angles and gimmicks, they realized that their champion could not only spread their propaganda, but do so without reprisal. They sent in an application to join Primetime Championship Wrestling, a prominent, Atlantic City based, organization. Their application was accepted. They had decided to name him after their organization, referring to him as SOCIETY, in capital letters. Now, SOCIETY stands ready to conquer two worlds: professional wrestling, and the globe. But, what will happen when he realizes that his very birth runs contrary to the conformity he preaches?
