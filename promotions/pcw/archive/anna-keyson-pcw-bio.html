Anna Keyson Biography Biography <u><b>Appearance</u></b> Anna is slender yet muscular and is in appearance very long legged. She has a proportionally sized torso and a face that is slightly oval-like in shape. Anna has rather slender hips for a woman of her age and her skin appears clean and perfect. However it is rather hard to tell anything about her complexion beneath the make-up that smothers her suffocating her natural body. Anna wears one of two basic attires and make-ups randomly. Her first appearance is to wear a very light blue make-up all over her bare skin, she wears darker blue lipstick and eye liner, as well as blue contact lenses that are of an ice blue colour. Her hair is also dyed ice blue. When wearing this make-up Anna wears a blue custom designed top that is shiny and leather. The top covers her upper body except for her arms and is a slightly darker blue than her skin. Anna’s other appearance comprises of the same general layout however, her skin is a very soft purple and her eyes and lips are black. Anna’s contact lenses are a striking purple colour that reflects light well. Her top is the same except it is black and her hair is dark purple in colour. Despite her make-up Anna will always wear black women’s boots to the ring and she wears very tight leather trousers that are the same colour as her top and are attached to it. The complexion of Anna’s face comprises of the most menacing of eyes, with an almost cat-like pupil. She has very distinctive lips and small pointy ears. Her nose is small and almost indistinguishable beneath her make-up. Her eyes are not abnormally position and her forehead is rather large, as her hair curls inward with two tips leading toward her dark and vengeful eyes. Whilst walking to the ring the only addition to Anna’s costume is her lengthy black cloak that trails almost two meters behind her and shines due to its silky material. <u><b>History</u></b> Many moons ago a lady was rushed into hospital, this lady was Natalie Keyson. Pregnant with a young girl she was operated upon, although the foetus had become lodged in the uterus and was too large to pass through. A caesarian section was performed and as the warmth of the emergency room faded and the sunlight became a frosty and chilling tyrant, a child was born. However, this was no ordinary child for her mother had died during childbirth complications. The baby was an orphan. The father of the child vanished after the death of his wife in a state of manic depression he left his young carefree daughter Anna, to the nearest orphanage who would raise her. Days went by and Anna aged with those days, she grew both physically and mentally, at the age of 3 months, she uttered her first word: ‘Dark’ the word reflected her imagery and her mind, for within this poor helpless girl, was a darkness that nothing could fill a spot where here love and compassion for others was imminent only darkness lay there. Anna reached an age where she could go to school, the orphanage provided her with hardly enough food to live on and so she scavenged like a rabid, dark beast for food, anywhere she could, but found nothing. Her early school life was traumatic facing huge changes in her life reflected upon her subconscious. She would experience nightmares that even Satan himself would condemn. The soul of the young girl was gradually battered harder and harder, her very existence was threatened by this impending insanity that seemed to swell from within and cut her form the inside like blades dipped in the poisonous fountain of hate. Anna was a loner during her school days, her darkness was her exile and her rage was burning. Such a rage that if it were fire, an ocean could not quench it. She tried to contain this fiery war within herself, but was too weak. She finally revealed her first sign of dark power during her mid-school life. In her lesson, as usual she sat alone, she paid no attention but simply sat and pitied her peers. They were inferior to her, as she sat watching one of the pathetic beings pick their nose in silence, the teacher; Mrs. Atkinson came over and began to ask questions. Anna simply sat and stared at the woman and she became angry more angry than she ever had before. The whiny voice of this fat, sweaty thing incessantly chimed through her head to the point where she could take no more. Then for a moment time almost seemed to stop, as Anna’s eyes fluctuated and a test bookcase fell and crushed the teacher before her very eyes. As the other students screamed in anguish watching blood pour from the corpse of their teacher. Anna simply sat and sickly laughed to the extent of maniacal screeching. She finished her school days in a mental institution. Anna grew on and from that moment forward a sinister gift took the last slice of her sanity, she began to receive premonitions and visions of deaths. She would see somebody and then see their future flash before their eyes until their death. Bloody images of dismemberment and disgust echoed through her visual memory. Exposure to atrocity on such a large scale would plague this soul for the rest of its day’s. Anna realizing she could not defeat these curses, used them as gifts and to her advantage, she was able to increase and enhance her powers. Spending her life in the orphanage and the institution still enraged her, and she lamented her father and mother, whom abandoned her when she was so tender. Anna was now a lost soul reaped by the devil himself she was caught between realities and struggled to comprehend her own actions. She had lost control, her powers now overpowered her, she was possessed and she could do nothing. But sit back and wait. Wait for a time where the burdens would be lifted. The nightmares would cease to plague her every dream and the visions would desist. Anna would have to wait, but until the time came that she would be whole again. Wrestling seemed a suitable pastime to release the rage. Anna soon lost track of time, as she was simply lacking concern for when life ended or began, as she felt none of it's sweet and illustrious emotions. After several offences of a violent nature in her adulthood and serving prison sentences for an array of crimes. Anna was encouraged to enter wrestling to release her violent beast within free. So that it would not torment her soul and so that her unstable psyche would be at greater peace. The rage was still with her, but she was able to moderate the pain and the suffering from the war within her darkened soul by releasing this rage through inflicting pain upon others.
