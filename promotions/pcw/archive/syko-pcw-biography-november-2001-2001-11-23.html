Real Name: Umm... Syko? Nick Name: The Goblin Catchphase: "Enter the Goblin... Exit the Ratings!" Character Handler: Vernon S. MSN Account: [detail withheld] AIM Address: [detail withheld] Theme Song: "Crawling Eye" by the Misfits... Age: 19 1/2 From: Chicago Height: 5'6 1/2 Weight: 145 Wrestling Style: Sucidal-NO REGARD FOR HUMAN LIFE-Highflier... Alot of evasive techniques too. Signature Moves: 450 Splash, Shooting Star Press, Springboard corkscrew Sucide dive (Inside, and out side of ring), Twisting senton, Moonsault, Flying Head Scissors, Hurricane DDT, Dragon Ranna (Sometimes with a "Mocking bow.")And Basically and Any other HighFlying move you can think of. He thrives off them. Other moves: DDT, Cradle, Face buster, Bulldog, and his only submissive manuver, other than his finisher, the legendary CAMEL CLUTCH! Likes to evade alot, exp: Cartwheel, roll, slide out of ring, ect, ect, ect... SUPER DUPER TRADEMARK MOVES! 1) Slimer-Gamma, his manager, always brings to the ring a trademark bucket of goo. And, sometimes, he'll launch it into the ring, making it super slippery, and Syko with do a baseball slide through it to the standing opponent. (Rare.) 2)Booger Flingin'-Double Backflip moonsault. 3)Stench of the Goblin-He lays out the opponent, with their feet to the turnbuckle, and runs to the other side. He charges, and leaps over them, up to the top turnbuckle, moonsaults off, going into a twisting senton, to a double foot face stomp... ONLY TO PULL HIS FEET APART AT THE LAST SECOND! So, now he's squating over their head, and his butt's in their face. Suddenly, he farts a green mist into their face, with a special device of some sort... OR IS IT?! (PPV RARE!) Finisher Move: Parasitic Goblinoid-Leaps off turn buckle with their back to him, and locks them in a sleeper, clinging to their back. Set-Up Move: Haha! Fool!-Essentially... he whips them to the corner, and follows, sliding through their legs, quickly hopping to his feet, kicking them in the arse, and as they stumble foward he pivots the turn buckle, and goes for his finisher. Current Team: S&M (Syko&Miyagawa) Past Teams & Groups: CHAOS/Tag Team partner is Skitzo... Freaks By Choice Title History: ZERO! AND THATS THE WAY I LIKE IT!! Also, bring me my coffee with spit in it~! Awards History:(Spits in disgust.) Description: Syko- Skin Color: Pale. Eye Color: Brown. Hair: Syko has dark brown hair. It is pretty long, as it almost down to his shoulders, and is parted down the middle. His Hair is quite greasy, and should be matted down, with alot of uneven ends. Face: Syko has a pretty normal face, with nothing to special about it in shape. But, it does have large grotesque scars on it. But, it is pointless to make any scars, since he wears black makeup, in the shape of a bat, over his eyes. Also, Syko has a very rugged, unwell kept 5oclock shadow. And to top it all off, he has a devious smirk. Body: The only thing to note, that you can see of Syko's body, thats not covered by his ring attire, are his arms tattoos. Just completely cover his arms with tattoos! Also, you might say he's slightly cut for his size, but not totally. In-Ring attire: He wears a white skin-tight tank top-T. His lower body wears red tights, with a spikey "S" on the outside thigh of each pant(tight) leg. The "S" should go from a few inches below the waist, to a few inches above the kneecap. The "S" should be a spikey black "S", outlined in white. He has the black, PADDED style of boots, meant for the martial arts (Kickers...exapmles: Xpac's boots... only black) And Black Kneepads, so that his feet, knees, and shins are completely covered in black. And, the last bit would be his Arms. He has a both wrists taped up, and a very long (From mid forearm, to top of bicep) black elbow pad, for his left arm. Out-of-ring attire: A black shirt that says, "I'm with stupid" and arrows pointing everywhere, and black dirty jeans. Wears biker boots. History: Well... Let's just say PCW is Syko's first big thing in all his life. Basically, Syko grew up, mistreated, and beaten, and eventually ran away from his home... IN CHICAGO! So, at the age of 9, young little Syko was living in allyways, and feeding off trash. Eventually, the poor sap got some crazy ideas about wanting to be a wrestler, after seeing a few images of it all. So, when the first chance came, he bought (Stole) himself a ticket for and EHWF (Indy Fed), and waltzed (Snuck) right into the backstage area. There, he was found by EHWF CEO, and Owner, The Gamma King. Well, at first, Gamma was going to take Syko out back, and shoot him with a fire hose, being the evil man he was, but then he saw Syko was homeless... So he decided to take young little Syko under his wing. It was hard for Gamma to teach Syko things, and all that jazz, seeing as Syko had never been in school, and didn't have a name other than, "Boy." Then, it finally happened. His career in wrestling began. After being on the road with EHWF, Gamma finally hired Syko... As a hot dog vender. But, he was only 11, so Gamma thought that was only appropriate. But, Syko wasn't contempt with that! He soon became the "INTERFERING HOT DOG TERRORIST!!" About five years later he was nicknamed "The Goblin" for his small size which he obtained from stress on his bones at a young age... Go figure. And Gamma named him Syko. Well, anyway, Gamma has used Syko as his devious tool of destruction ever since! Syko's trail of (Snicker) destruction- PWA PCW2 PWA GAW PWA NTICW PWA EW NTICW And now... PCW... "Enter the Goblin."
