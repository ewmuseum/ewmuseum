A town of about 35,000 in East Texas. Stephens was a really smart kid, who excelled both in the classroom and playing various sports. He graduated 6th in his highschool class without working too hard. He was also a very solid highschool baseball player, signing to play with a junior college. Baseball was always his true love, and after two years at a junior college, he signed to play at Texas A&M. He was drafted, but the signing bonus wasn't that much, and the team refused to pay for the rest of his college if he didn't go pro, so he attented Texas A&M, living off campus in a little country home with a very close female friend and her older male cousin. He tore his rotator cuff beyond repair, his junior year, and got very frustrated. Finally, coming to grips with it all he managed to go ahead and obtain his master's in journalism. He aspired to be sports writer. He was a rare case, able to make the grades and party like he did. At a frat party he met his eventual first wife Melanie whom he married at the age of 24. She passed away 3 years later from a rare disease. After graduating he did some intern work for a small magazine, but longed to be famous. He was in superb physical condition and decided he would give wrestling a try, on account of he could act, could entertain, and was physcially sound. After a very short stint in a small indy federation he was signed by Golden State Wrestling of the National Wrestling Council, and joined the ranks of some of the best wrestlers in the world. While in GSW, he obtained the name 'Streak' because of his uncanny quickness, especially for a white man. Stephens was a determined youngster who would climb the ranks quickly, only to have a minor set back, and falter every time he was about to claim a major title. 'Streak' came to represent his inconsistency, and unpredictableness rather than his speed. Throughout his stay in the national wrestling council, he has wrestled in Golden State Wrestling who was bought out by the New England Wrestling Syndacite. After NEWS folded he disguised himself as Don Juan Leisure, and dumb, and goofy black guy in order to further entertain the fans. He had a short stay in Bluegrass State Wrestling as Leisure before losing his second J-crown shot, and giving up the gimmick, and wrestling all together. While on leave of absence from the NWC his first wife Melanie passed away, and after mourning her loss for quite some time he returned to the NWC, and Parliament City Wrestling, later bought out by Tri-State Wrestling where he won his 4th and 5th NWC titles, and was undefeated, before he decided he had, had done all he could do, and left for a new challenge. Before finding this new challenge, he found his old high school sweetheart, Dana, and the two were married. They get along very well. This new challenge would be to gain the respect of the wrestlers, staff, and fans of Prime Time Wrestling! Finisher Move The Downfall
