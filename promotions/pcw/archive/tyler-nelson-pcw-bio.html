Tyler Nelson Biography Real Name Tyler Nelson Nicknames 'The Dark Savior', 'The CEO' Hometown Dallas, Texas Birthday 12/27/74 Height 6'5' Weight 255 lbs Biography Tyler Nelson was born two days after Christmas in 1974. Having his birthday two days after Christmas meant that Tyler didn't get seperate Christmas and birthday presents. Family and friends always grouped the two together, screwing him out of gifts that most other kids received on their birthdays. By the time Tyler was 7 years old, he despised the fact that he was born on the day he was. He wanted to have all the presents that other kids got on their birthdays in March, April, September, and so on. Eventually Tyler would try to have his birthday moved to a different month, but his parents would not allow it. The hatred and jealously continued to build within young Tyler and created a insatiable greed within him that would drive him for the rest of his life. Tyler made his way through high school with good grades, as well as a good business sense. Tyler learned the arts of blackmail and extortion early on, and they became valuable tools in his high school years. Through various scams Tyler would amass a rather large sum of money and use it to start his own business, which he aptly named Greed, INC. The sole purpose of Greed, INC was acquisitions, mergers, and dispersion. He would buy businesses, rape and pillage them for any valuable assets, then sell off the unwanted portions. With little in the way of a conscious, the bottom line was the mighty dollar and the mighty dollar was king. He started off small with mom and pop stores, gas stations, small strip malls, and other various properties. Greed, INC grew into a multi-billion dollar corporation and soon Tyler Nelson was bargaining for islands and small countries. With a vast fortune at his disposal, and greed by his side, Tyler needed a new challenge. He started training to be a professional wrestler, hiring several top notch teachers to help him learn the sport. He broke into Primetime Championship Wrestling in December of 2000, and quickly proceeded to lose his first match as he was eliminated from the Santa's Workshop Brawl by a barefooted hillbilly named Bang Davenport. Unwavering in his resolve Tyler then was booked in his first legitimate match against one of PCW's top stars at the time, Ryan Knox. Although Tyler made a good showing for himself by kicking the hell out of Knox from ringpost to ringpost, the wiley veteran came out with the victory. When Tyler demanded a rematch Knox tucked tail and bailed, ducking the Greediest Player in the Game. Tyler would go on to win the Rising Star Championship on two occasions, totally dominating that division. Then would come the moment that would change history. In January of 2001 Tyler made perhaps the most aggressive and important acquisition of his business career. He bought PCW from The Phantom Booker after a slew of lawsuits threatened to force Booker to close it down. No sooner were the papers signed then the reign of terror began. MEDIA was introduced to the world at the Shanghei-ed PPV, spearheaded by the new CEO of PCW, Tyler Nelson. He, Dan Hollywood, Chet King, and some other guy would run roughshod over PCW and say and do what they pleased. During the Media era there were many highlights in Tyler's career, but none shine brighter than the historic Four Way Cage Match for the PCW World Title that Tyler won by beating Meanstreak, Frisco, and Stewart 'Fed Whore' Hix. The crown jewel of the wrestling world now belonged to Tyler....for a week.
