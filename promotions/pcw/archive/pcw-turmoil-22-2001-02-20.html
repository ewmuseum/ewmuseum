Turmoil 22 20 Feb 2001 The Black Hills Center, Sturgis, South Dakota Media arrives at the Black Hills Center The show opens as Media's black, hummer limousine slowly pulls into the parking lot of the Black Hills Center in Sturgis, South Dakota.  The modified military SUV comes to a slow halt at the back door of the arena and the back doors pop open, and out step Media members Chet King, PCW Continental Champion Dan Hollywood, PCW Telelvision Champion Mr. Showtime, Homicidal Anthony Cay, Chris Allen, The Chef and finally the CEO of PCW, Tyler Nelson.  It becomes obvious that those inside the building can see what is going on outside as a thundering chorus of boos can be heard from the inside of the arena. Tyler Nelson motions to the arena upon hearing the boos and a smile crosses his face. Nelson:  (sarcastic)  Hey guys, listen to the love oozing from this building. The other six members of Media smile and nod while bumping hands in solidarity. Nelson:  Well boys, that kind of reaction can only be attributed to JEALOUSY. The scene shifts just inside the back door of the arena, shooting out through the back door and into the parking lot, as the seven members of Media begin entering the arena.  Dan Hollywood is the last member of the PCW's ruling elite to pace through the threshhold, he fails to close the door behind him.
