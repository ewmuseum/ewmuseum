Fusion 45 10 Aug 2001 The Martin Luther King Center, Gary, Indiana A masked intruder at Fenmore Medical Center The scene fades into the hallways of what appears to be the somewhat infamous Fenmore Medical Center, which is the temporary, if not last home of the “Monster They Call Meanstreak.” The camera travels quickly down a long, corridor, which has patient rooms on both sides. The scene shifts up towards the end of the hallway, which is marked by a heavy iron door, which is approximately 30 meters away, with two intersecting hallways between the “intruder” and the iron door. Suddenly, approximately 20 meters away, a doctor followed by two nurses, emerge from a side corner, walking from left to right across the path of the intruder. The intruder, quickly bolts to the side down a nearby side corridor. After a moment, the view sent back to the fans at home is one of someone peeking back around the corner, where it is revealed the doctors have passed by across the hallway. Quickly, the intruder rushes the iron door, quickly covering the 30 meters of distance. Upon arriving at the door, the words “PROTECTIVE CUSTODY” can be clearly read upon the iron door. There is a jingling sound, very similar to the sound that is made when one grabs at a set of keys. The scene pans down to reveal the intruder looking down upon the folds of his black robe, as a gloved hand exits a pocket of the robe, with a set of keys. A loud metal on metal sound of the lock’s tumblers is heard, causing the intruder to look to the left and right, down the intersecting hallways where the doctor and two nurses had just walked from and to, making sure no one is coming. Then, in the blink of an eye, the intruder passes through the iron door. Closing the door ever so gently behind him, making a barely audible sound as he closes it. He rushes down the hallway towards its end, but then suddenly stops. He looks to the right, where a small mirror is affixed the wall, with the words “How do I look to my patients today?” written upon the mirror. The reflection in the mirror is not a shock at this point, as a black cowled, Scream Mask wearing visage of “Brandi’s #1 Fan” is finally revealed.
