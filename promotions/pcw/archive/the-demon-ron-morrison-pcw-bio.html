'The Demon' Ron Morrison Biography Real Name Ronald S. Taggart III Nicknames The Demon Hometown New York, New York by Way of Brazillia, Brazil Birthday 8/22/70 Height 6'4' Weight 285 lbs Biography Points Of Interest -Born in Brazillia, Brazil while parents were on a business trip. -Parents were wealthy due to owning large shares in various companies, and their own business, Taggart Corp. -Was trained in martial arts, fencing, and various other forms of self defense due to his parent's wealth. Ron took a liking to the martial arts, but wanted something edgier. He then began training in various styles of boxing and freestyle wrestling at age 16. -Ron is a rebel without a cause, so to speak. He listens to heavy death metal music, and believes in no religion. The only thing he believes in is himself. -Began competing in MMA and freestyle wrestling in 1987, and suffered only 2 losses before beginning the change to pro-wrestling in 2003. -Due to his career in MMA, Ron is extremely famous in Japan and Europe. Due to this, his ego is overly inflated. -The downfall to this is that some of the fame was gained when he broke a fighter's neck with a punch during a Pride tournament. The blow ended up killing the fighter a few days later. -It is said that when in competition, Ron Morrison is like a demon. From the glare in his eyes to his almost unstoppable body. It is said that if you are fighting Ron Morrison, it is like staring into the eyes of the devil. -Ron owns a large share of his parent's companies, and attends meetings from time to time, but he does not like being involved. He only cares about the money, not the business. Other Feds N/A Other Fed Awards N/A
