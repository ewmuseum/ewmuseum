Fusion 123 23 May 2003 Military Academy Stadium, Cairo, Egypt Miss Moonlight and Indigo Kasugano address the press The scene zooms in to show a podium in one of the dressing rooms with a microphone set up. Reporters sit waiting, murmuring to themselves. Then the door swings open, and they all come to life. Indigo Kasugano enters first, wearing a red blouse with a black jacket and black slacks and heels, her long hair pinned back in a bun. Following close behind, is the PCW Cruiserweight champion, Miss Moonlight. Wearing tight blue jeans, blue sneakers, and a black MISS MOONLIGHT Crescent Moon T-Shirt, she holds the CW title over her shoulder. The press stands up as Indigo stands behind the podium. Indigo: Thank you all for coming here tonight for this press conference. I hope that I’ll be able to answer all questions you have. Do you think that in her course of action here, is she ready for this match? Indigo: I think her progress thus far has been impeccable.
