History: When John was not event one year old, he was put up for adoption and lived in group homes for most of his life. All around him, people were picking up children who were skilled, smart and attractive. At the age of 10, John knew he was a good-looking kid, do he worked on his skills and education. It came easier than he would have thought, as he had a natural gift for sports, and everything his mind was told, he retained throughout the years. Luckily, one day, he was adopted by a young family with another son named Gavin. The family's name was the Coens' ... so John finally had a last name ... John Coens. Having a brother was great at first, for John that is. Gavin was always getting jealous that John could get all the girls, all the awards ... and all his parents attention. No matter what he did ... John excelled in, and Gavin was left behind. Finally, Gavin decided to try out wrestling, seeing as he always liked watching guys like Andre the Giant and Jesse the Body Ventura wrestle on tv. It worked. He was a star. He went on to wrestle pro in less than two years ... but that wasn't the end. John caught wind of how much fun it was, and decided to give it a shot, as he was always trying to do things to involve his brother, so they could do things together. John once again, stole the spotlight, and Gavin was left in the shadows, planning his revenge. It never happened though, because the bitter feud only led to a remarkable tag team. The two were such of kin in the ring, that they were unbeatable. Through that, they were able to forge a lasting friendship, and have always been able to separate career from life. Once John achieved that, he was happy, but he still had contrats to oblige, and he still found wrestling interesting ... so he stayed. Here he is today.
