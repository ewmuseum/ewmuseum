Fusion 119 25 Apr 2003 The Ericcson Center, Charlotte, South Carolina The Astronomer arrives early with questions about the Druid Earlier Today… The shining, metallic black-painted door of the sparkling Jeep Rubicon swings open and out steps ‘The Astronomer’ Dorn Tracer, with a smug look of annoyance on his clean-shaven, handsome face. The camera backtracks slowly, following everyone one of Tracer’s steps fluidly. The words ‘Earlier Today’ sit dormant in the corner of the feed screen, as well as the time of recording. It’s Friday, 3:30 PM. The PCW TV title shines beneath his coat as he strides forward. Tracer, to himself: I’m getting so damned sick of this Druid shit… someone has a ritualistic death wish… The camera pans around and reveals Dorn’s destination to be Ericcson Center Arena, the venue for Friday Fusion. The Astronomer has arrived early, for reasons best kept to himself. Dorn swiftly passes by and the camera follows him at good distance, trying not to invade his space. The new Television Champion swings one of the front doors open and stomps in, already setting his destination towards the back. Tracer, still to himself: Let’s just see if our hooded friend has dared arrive early as well… He cuts into a long, white corridor lined with wooden doors and looks at each one as he goes buy, looking for a particular one. His pace quickens as he continues on, but suddenly, he comes into a screeching halt and the camera jerks violently, trying to stop as well. Dorn turns and looks at a door to his right, a big grin on his face. Tracer: Ahh… this is it… Tracer: If the Druid wants to fuck with me, then I think it’s time I fucked with him, her, it… whatever. Tracer takes a piece of paper from his pocket and unfolds it, gazing at it for a moment before looking back up at the door and pocketing the piece of paper. He grips the silver knob and turns it slowly, as if expecting something. He scouts around for a minute before pushing the door open and slipping it, slamming it shut behind him so the camera cannot follow. When no noise emits from within the door, the camera backs away and cuts the feed into the usual opening.
