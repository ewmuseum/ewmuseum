Turmoil 64 8 Jan 2002 The PIT, Albequerque, New Mexico Silver writing on a black screen and a bladed pendulum The screen opens up with a black background. We know how it is to have your opponent teetering on the edge of destruction, held at bay by the pistol in your grasp. We also know how it is to have the very same pistol backfire and shoot right into your face. It makes you feel pretty foolish…like all your efforts have been in vain. The Greek demigod releases a refreshed sigh as he wipes his mouth. Tossing the empty bottle into the nearby trash bin, he reaches next for an apple. Rubbing it against his toga, he sinks his teeth into the fruit. Mistakes are made daily, and despite being human or inhuman, we will all have our fair share. There is one lesson we can learn, however, that will help us all as overcome our humiliating mistakes. Think...how can you reverse your bad situation into something that will benefit yourself? He shrugs as he puts the last finishing bites into his apple. Light…can we really keep our mistakes out of the light? If not for his utter mortification, we would not have PCW’s son of Zeus this day. He made good from his bad, though he actually had no intellectual intention on doing so.
