Suicidal Tendencies Biography Biography Ron Jacobs and Mike Andrews,aka Suicidal Tendencies, both grew up in Canada. Growing up their they realized when they got older that they wanted to become entertainers, and what better way to entertain the people than to become wrestlers. They started their careers off in Canada but then moved to the United States. When reaching the US, they found a home in Houston, Texas. It is there where they decided that this was going to be where they would like to call home and not have to go back to Canada, as they didn't find much success there. In Houston they meet up with a guy that turned out to become one of their best friends. James was also a wrestling promoter. He got ST work wherever it was needed, and ST didn't complain. They wanted to start off somwhere and they didn't really care where it was at. From making a statement from federation to federation, they came across the PCW. It is here where ST looks to prove that they are one of the best tag teams walking gods green earth.
