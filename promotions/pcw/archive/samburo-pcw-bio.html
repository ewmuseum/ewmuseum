Samburo Biography Biography <b>Jason Jameson Finisher:</b> Animalism <b>Finisher Description:</B> Reverse Death Valley Driver <b>Signature Moves:</B> <i>Primitive Takedown</i> - Running Spear <i>Raw Destruction</i> - Stalling Powerbomb <i>The Right Movement</i> - Hanging suplex into a dropping DDT <i>Critical Hit</i> - Chokeslam <i>WILDlife</i> - Release Belly-To-Belly Suplex <i>Natures Call</i> - Top Rope Cross Body (Rarely done) <i>Natural Existence</i> - Standing Tazzmission (Smaller Opponents would not touch the floor) <i>Final Cut</i> - Dragon sleeper into a chop neckbreaker <i>The Cause</i> - Baldo Bomb <i>The Cure</i> - Stalling Elevated Reverse DDT <i>The Third A.R.M.</i> - Knock-out uppercut <i>Becoming Extinct</i> - One leg Boston Crab <i>Gorilla Press Slam</i> - As the name says... <i>Lethal Blow</i> - Fall Forward Pulling Piledriver <i>Wild Fire</i> - Crucifix Powerbomb <i>Primal Rage</i> - Jason raises his opponent for an electric chair drop, however he jolts backwards causing his opponent to be knocked backwards and suspended in the air by his legs. This puts pressure on his opponents back. <b>Catchphrase:</b> “We are going to kick your ass, and no godly power will save you ‘cus I pray to every single one of them!” <b>Appearance:</b> Jason Jameson stands six foot ten and three hundred and ninety pounds. “The Star” is of a very muscular build. He has a very tanned complexion, almost like that of someone from the Mediterranean. Jason has blonde hair that goes to his shoulders. He has blue eyes and a narrow nose. Along his right hand cheek is a very long scar. It goes from the right of his eye to just above the edge of his mouth. Jason wrestlers with no top. He shows off his washboard stomach and very well developed muscles. He wears a pair of cream army trousers and “desert” coloured boots. <b>Nervil </b> <b>Finisher:</b> Fighting Finish <b>Finisher Description:</b> Springboard top rope spilt legged moonsault <b>Signature Moves:</b> <i>The Warrior Inside</i> - Running hurricanrana <i>Ewaso Ngiro (African river that the Samburu inhabit near)</i> - A sleeper hold, where Nervil holds his opponents head back, and holds his windpipe, cutting off air. <i>Uhuru (African for“Freedom” in English)</i> - Guillotine Leg Drop <i>Ushindani (African for “Resistance” in English)</i> - Reverse Diving Tornado DDT <i>Vernietiging (African for “Destruction” in English)</i> - Five Star Frog Splash <i>Krag (African for “Power” in English)</i> - Running Spear, followed by a nerve hold with his teeth <i>Sterf (African for “Die” in English)</i> - Running head scissors takedown <i>Weerlig (African for “Lightning” in English)</i> - 450 splash <b>Appearance:</b> Nervil is not as well built and is very small in comparison to his partner, Jason Jameson. The “Skinny Bastard” has a very dark complexion and no scars on tattoos. His head is shaven, and only a small amount of black hair can be seen at any time. He stands a small 5’ 7 and weighs in at a tiny 160lbs. He is not muscular but is very agile. Nervil can flex in numerous positions and has very, very good aerial skills. To the ring, he wears a red/orange dress, with brown sandals and no top. His ribs are visible through his skin. Around his neck, he wears a number of necklaces, destined for his bride. This gear will be wore at all times, never ever changing. <b>Background:</b> Jason Jameson was born on the January 3rd, 1978. He was born in Toronto, Canada, to Jim and Susan Jameson. Jason grew up in sub-urban Toronto, Canada as any normal kid. He played hockey, football and watched wrestling with a passion. He was a fine specimen of a young boy as well. When he was ten, Jason went to Australia with his parents for a family re-union. When there he was attacked by a Koala Bear and this resulted in a long scar along his cheek. Jason grew a hatred for animals. He feared and hated anything that wasn’t human. For that year, Jason began to hurt his numerous pets and make them suffer. Two years later, Jason returned to Australia for a family funeral with his parents. He was attacked again. However, he learned to respect the animals. He actually noticed this was a way of the animals calling to be respected by humans. Jason returned to Canada with a love of animals. He treated them as equals, no better or worse than himself. At eighteen, Jason now standing 6’ 8 left home to become a wrestler. He tried numerous schooled but got no-where. In his spare time, he worked with numerous animal charities, but felt none of these organisations treated animals in the correct manner. Years past and Jason went between schools and animals organisations, never finding a real home. At twenty five, Jason found himself in the PWA. After graduating and joining the DWF, Jason left to set-up the Animal Rights Movement. His own organisation that worked solely for the rights of animals. Four months later and Jason has returned to the square circle. Jameson returned to the ranks of NTICW, in the PTC. While there, Jameson went on a soul searching trip to Kenya. While on Mount Kenya, he became a “Honorary” member of the Samburu tribe, that lives on Mount Kenya’s side. As a gift he was given, Nervil as a son. Nervil grew up with the Samburu tribe. He knows no English and communicates with Jameson through actions. The pair are an unlikely match, but Jameson is using Nervil as a tag partner as a way of making two pay cheques, but also going after tag team gold.
