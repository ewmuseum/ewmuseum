PCW match: Mock Match: Snow (World Champion) vs. Corey Williams (L)

19 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Mock Match: Snow (World Champion) vs. Corey Williams (L) Written: April 19, 2002 The standard response from the crowd, booing and trash throwing. Dave: Arrogant bastard. Jeff: It’s confidence, Kern. Get some. Snow: You know, I want to be a fighting World Champion to set the standard for all the unworthy champions that will follow me after I decide to give up this title in about 30 years…. Dave: Meh. Snow: …so I had a little talk with out great Commissioner Xavier Kannon, and we worked out a match for me tonight. Dave: You already HAVE a match later tonight you idiot!! Jeff: Leave the champ alone. He knows what he is doing. Duke: The champ is full of shit. Snow: So tonight, not only will I kick Big Alan Steele’s ass, I will have an ADDITIONAL match! Dave: WHAT?! Jeff: DON’T DO IT!! The crowd hushes. Snow: So get that referee down here and let’s get this show on the road! On command a referee comes running down the ramp and climbs into the ring. Snow: Alrighty then…..being the great man that I am I will even introduce my opponent. He is… Snow is interrupted by the foot of Corey Williams as he nails the champion with a SUPERKICK!! Dave: HOLY SHIT!! Jeff: Wait a minute…. The bell sounds as Snow hits the mat. Williams falls on the champion and the referee makes the count…….1…….2…….3!! Dave: OH MY GOD!!! COREY WILLIAMS IS THE NEW PCW WORLD CHAMPION?!?! Jeff: …..this smells….. Duke: Something ain’t right here, hosses. Look at the replay. As the replay comes up on the screen we can plainly see that the Superkick from Williams misses the jaw of the champion by at least a foot. Jeff: ….like a SWERVE~! Dave: THESE SONS OF BITCHES HAVE DONE IT AGAIN!! THEY ARE TRASHING THE TRADITION OF THE PCW WORLD TITLE BY PASSING IT AROUND LIKE A HOOKER AT A BACHELOR PARTY!! Williams grabs the title and thrusts it in the air as Snow gets to his feet, feigning dizziness. The fans erupts into voracious boos as a smirk spreads across Snow’s face. He grabs his mic again. Snow: My Greatness!! That was certainly a devastating Superkick by my more than worthy opponent! Dave: THIS IS A TOTAL CROCK OF SHIT!! CCW lifts his foot up and fans it as if it was on fire. Snow: What this means to all you plebes is that as long as New Breed is in control of this belt, no one else will be. Not Nelson, not Boda, not Miyagawa, and certainly not that Hi-Keeba’ing moron Big Alan Steele! Dave: This is a disgrace!! View Snow 's Biography Back
