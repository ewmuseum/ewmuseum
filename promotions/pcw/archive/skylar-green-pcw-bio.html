Skylar Green Biography Real Name Skylar Green Nicknames Sky Hometown La Jolla, CA Birthday April 29th, 1977 Height 6'5' Weight 242 lbs Biography ****Entrance**** Sky walks to the ring, with his music playing over the speakers. With little in the way of acknowledgement of the fans, Sky calmly, very focused, walks out to the ring and jumps up onto the apron. Walking over to the corner, he jumps over the top rope, landing in the ring, then jumps back up to the second rope and raises his arms to cheers from the crowd. ****Bio**** Sky grew up without his parents, they died in a car accident when he was four. He doesn't dwell on it, per se, but he still remembers them fondly. Sky's life up to the age of eighteen is hard to follow, due to records being lost. It is believed that he was an orphan in Virginia, USA until that point, but the facility closed under undisclosed circumstances, and nothing but a burnt wreck stands in place of it. Once Sky got out of the orphange, he went to college and gained degrees in Biochemistry and Business Administration. He doesn't seem to care much for these fields, he probably just considered it something to do. We don't know how, but it is presumed that at some point he went to Japan. Sky says that he did, and saw a Puroresu event. He credits that as being his inspiration to get into the sport of wrestling. Sky came back to America, and apparently he gained employment in a garbage federation known as the HWF. We couldn't find many records from it as the federation has been closed for over a year, but we were able to verify his employment in the federation under the name 'Enigma.' This 'Enigma' would later surface in the UWWC, a bigger regional federation. Records from this federation show that Sky was its star performer, losing only 3 professional matches, none of which were clean defeats. He is famous for being the only Grand Slam (or as the UWWC referred to him, the 'OmniChampion') Champion in the federation's history. Sky changed his name to Seppuku during his tenure in UWWC and continued his rocket to regional stardom. When the UWWC closed last summer, Sky came to the PTC looking to make a name for himself. He joined the PWA and within a week was promoted to EW, a developmental organization for PCW. There he won his first PTC title, the Primetanium Title, against a man who had cheated to hold it for eight weeks with a crooked promoter working to keep it on the champion. During his time in EW, Sky's employer made the entire roster submit to house-arrest tagging. Sky willingly took the house arrest implant, but misinformation resulted in a faultly experimental chip to be implanted in the back of his neck. When Sky removed the chip himself, it caused him to go Sky. Sky's blindness, by all fabricated studies and medical reports, was irreversible and he must live with this condition for the rest of his life. Sky was faced with a hard choice, to give up wrestling, or to continue and work through his disability, turning it into an advantage. He worked hard, and quickly adjusted to his lack of sight, and within but a few short weeks, Sky was fully competent in Sky-fighting, and he returned to wrestling ready to regain his lost time in the ring. With his honor code, innovative offense, and disability overcome, Sky was one of EW's top stars until the federation was expelled from the PTC. At that point, Sky decided to stay with the PTC, and his loyalty and talent paid off as PCW finally accepted him into its roster. Skylar has wrestled for PCW and PURE, winning numerous championships, and thought both companies have closed, his achievements and trials in both federations will not be soon forgotten. After a short stint in eW, Inc (due to backstage issues concerning frequent changes to production from management), Skylar had gone to AJWF, where he immediately made an impact by going undefeated and in four matches, reached and defeated the world champion. He left it, unfulfilled, and devoted more time to his family. He took a job as the OSF President for a month, but that was as a favor more than anything. Now, Skylar has returned to PCW, where he plans to go with the flow and continue as he left off, wrestling and proving that you don't have to be larger than life to be a wrestler, just enjoy life.
