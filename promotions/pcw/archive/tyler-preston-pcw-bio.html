Tyler Preston Biography Real Name Tyler [name withheld] Nicknames The Golden Boy, Ty Hometown Knoxville, Tennessee Birthday August 7, 1977 Height 6'3' Weight 225 lbs Biography **QUICK OVERALL BACKGROUND INFORMATION** Tyler Preston was borned to Marie and Jonathan Preston in the year 1977 in Knoxville, Tennessee. Tyler has always been the class clown as he grew and progressed in a small private school. He got in occassional trouble, and was lacking in the academics. After his senior year, Tyler did not persue a college education, but instead became a free-lance comedian. He hit the small local clubs, and that is where he stayed for the majority of his career. This brought in little money, so he usually found himself skipping the occassional meal. Tyler took in money on the side by wrestling with various organizations such as the cWo. He was blessed with a large body, and he figured that even if he sucked at it, wrestling would still bring in his fair share of money. Finally, in the present time, Tyler Preston ditched the small city comedy clubs and moved out to New York City where he was discovered at the 'Laugh Factory'. Along his way he met the love of his life, Kalli Stearns, and is establising a relationship with her. Tyler now resides in the heart of New York City in a two room apartment complex. His roots stay grounded in Knoxville, yet his future lies somewhere else. **APPEARANCE** Tyler was lucky to be blessed with boyish good looks. It was his million dollar smile and pretty boy face that got him through the tough times when talent wasn't sufficient. Tyler has medium length blonde hair. His hair is worn in what is known as the 'bed head' fashion, tussled all around and even spiked in some places. He has bright blue eyes and a small straight nose. He has bright white teeth that are perfectly straight. His chin and mouth area generally stay cleanly shavened. He has a medium build. He isn't incredibly bulkly, but still keeps a toned look to him. In the ring he wears his comedian clothes to stay confortable in this somewhat foreign element. You can find Tyler Preston decked out in black dress pants with a white, button-down shirt. To add the special 'Preston Touch' he wears a scarelet red bow-tie and suspenders. When not wrestling or performing, Tyler generally wears jeans and a T-shirt so that he can stay comfortable. Tyler weighs in at 225 lbs. and stands at 6 feet and 3 inches. He wears a size 11 shoe. In pants he wears a waist 37 and length 35. His face generally is the home of a smile, as comedian who keeps an upbeat attitude for the most part. **PAST WRESTLING EXPERIENCE** Even though Tyler barely tolerates professional wrestling, he still uses it as an ample source of income therefore has been apart of quite a few wrestling organizations thus far. He was quite successful in the cWo where he earned the Kage, Television, and United States Championship titles during his stay with them. He is also considered the best upcoming prospect with the QIWC. Over the years, Tyler has gained a knack in the ring, and has polished his style. Heck, it might even look like he knows what he is doing. Whenever a conflict arrives between comedy and wrestling, he often chooses the comedy side unless the wrestling committment is monumental. He has, however, developed a liking towards the sport, ad it has mutated in to another one of his passions. He has never had the heart to drop either comedy or wrestling, but is cursed to forever juggle the two which seems to get a little stressful for him at times. The most recent edition to his wrestling resume` is the invitation to the PCW where he will debut and hopfully carry over the success that he has enjoy with other organizations to the PCW. **HIGH SCHOOL DAYS** [Coming Soon!] Other Feds cWo Other Fed Awards Former cWo U.S, Television, and Kage Champion
