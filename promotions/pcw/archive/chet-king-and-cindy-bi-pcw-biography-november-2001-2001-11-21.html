CHET KING 'THE CHAT KING' Real Name: Chet King Nick Name: 'The Chat King.' Character Handler: Ben MSN Account: [detail withheld] AIM Address: [detail withheld] Theme Song: "That's How People Get F*cked Up" by D12 Age: 26 From: Atlanta, GA Height: 6'1 Weight: 205lbs Status: Face Wrestling Style: A mix between a High Flyer, Brawler and Technical. Signature Moves: Frog Splash, Hurricanranna, Lion Tamer, Missile Drop Kick, Sharpshooter, Gullioteen Leg Drop. Finisher Move: Chat Back (Scorpion Death Drop) Current Team: Past Teams & Groups: MEDIA with Tyler Nelson, Dan Hollywood, Mr Showtime, Chris Allen, HAC, Jon Kano, and Bo Raider + Riptah and Short Circuit who were hired by Chet King and Dan Hollywood. Winner of 'Revelations' Main Event at FedWars. First person introduced into the PCW Edge's version of the Hall of Fame. Description: Take a look at the poser, a proper description will be coming soon! - Speaking of Posers, my full size poser has disappeared and all I've got left is my head shot! History: Coming Soon! ------------------------------------------------------- CHET KING'S VALET: CINDY BI. Cindy Bi stands at 5'10. She has long hair which is naturally blonde, but it's always streaked with multiple colors (Red, Orange, Green, Blue, Yellow). She usually wears short, tight, black leather pants and an equally tight white t-shirt. Cindy Bi, apart from being Chet King's manager, is also a former stripper and the cover girl of the October Issue of Playboy.
