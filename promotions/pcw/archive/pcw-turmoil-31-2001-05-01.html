Turmoil 31 1 May 2001 Freedom Hall, The Campus of Louisville U, Louisville, Kentucky New Radicals and twenty-four hours of hell The sounds of "Mother We Just Can't Get Enough" by New Radicals rock in the background as highlights of Tyler Nelson's 24 hours of hell are highlighted. Among the shots is Tyler escaping trouble on a few occasions. Then we see shots of Tyler in his referee shirt being attacked by the guys in the Texas Deathmatch before losing the title to, first, Trashcan Man, then winning it back only to drop it to Stewart Hix moments later. After the shots of Friday are shown, we see shots of the numerous title changes that occurred on Sabotage. From Jeremy Howard winning the Rising Star title to Kid Wonder winning the Crusierweight title to Doug Troy capturing the Extreme title. A shot of DT holding the Extreme title high in the air is shown, then slowly fades out to a shot of the red pickup truck that ran him down just a couple days earlier. The shot of the truck, which has a picture of a pot plant on the top, is the last thing we see before fading into the opening for Turmoil. The usual opening for Turmoil is shown. All the people running through the ruined city engulfed in flames. Among the wrestlers highlighted are JJ, Clyde, Tim Inman, Caged Explosion, Jeremy "Krayzie" Howard, and The Noble. We take a quick look outside of Freedom Hall on Louisville's campus. It's a beautiful evening outside with people walking about just outside the arena. After that quick shot, we shift inside the building to a packed crowd. The cameras do their usual pan around the crowd. Our first stop takes us to a couple of farm girls wearing "4:20" shirts and holding a sign that says, "Jake Keeton is 'The Future' of PCW!".
