After a night a pure wrestling greatness and a program that has topped itself over and over again in a single evening, add one more twist to the plot! The match between former two-time PCW Heavyweight Champion and impressive newcomer Big Alan Steele has been taken outside the ring and… and do we have cameras on this?” Kano: “Steele isn’t going after Meanstreak. He’s taking that ref out for some fun. You know those Greeks!” Kern: “Says in my notes he’s from San Jose… and yes we have a feed! They are outside in the plaza in front of the arena!” Outside, a large fountain gurgles as water splashes down around the sculpture inside. Colored lights dance amongst the tiny splashes, creating dazzling tricks of light, sending colored drops of water out in circular patterns. A small crowd stands a generous distance from the fountain, their eyes fixated, but not on the fountain. There, the man they call Meanstreak stands, his back to the arena and facing into the waters. The cameraman runs around the fountain to get a better shot of the Streak as he stares, eyes glazed and unmoving into the lights. Occasionally, the huge man winces, remembering the pain of memories long forgotten, either by choice or by design. Kern: “Meanstreak is just… is just… standing there! Is this related to his recent medical troubles? Could the migraines and sleepless nights have gotten the better of the most dominant man in PCW history?!” Kano: “I know a few people who might, just might take issue with that statement, but I have a better reason why he’s out of it. Drugs!” Kern: “Drugs? And which one’s have you been smoking?” Kano: “No! I mean Steele and his CCW jag-offs must have drugged Meanstreak! They knew that Fat and Old hero Slightly Above Average Sized Alan Steele wouldn’t be able to put a single dent in Meanstreak’s armor! I smell a fix! FIX!!!” Smith: “There’s something just so… I don’t know… SAVAGE about Meanstreak that just makes me want to get down on all fours, bite my pillow and let his little Monster have his way, if you know what I mean…” Kano: “Yech…” The people begin buzzing and pointing as Big Alan Steele, referee in tow, exits the MCI Center and stops at the top of the steps. Both stop taking in the sight before them as Meanstreak still stands unmoving, seemingly hypnotized by the waters in front of him. Steele looks at the ref with a questioning glance who responds with a shrug and a motion forward. The two slowly work their way down the steps, unsure of just what the Streak is up to. Meanstreak twitches, his face contorting in agony, for but a mere millisecond before continuing to take in the waters. Big Alan Steele comes up from behind, placing a hand on Meanstreak’s shoulder… who abruptly turns, bloodshot eyes focusing onto Steele! A remembered feeling – anger. NO ONE interrupts Meanstreak… Kano: “IT’S ON, BITCH!!” Kern: “Look at that Monster’s eyes!!” Meanstreak roars and grabs Steele by the throat, bodily picking him up for a moment and rushing back. Big Alan Steele gasps for air and manages to get his feet on the ground, but Meanstreak has the momentum and the two continue to pick up speed towards the steps. Steele looks back and, seeing the onrushing steps, swings his arm up and twists his body, shifting the momentum, but not enough. Meanstreak and Steele both slam sideways into the steps, Big Alan Steele seemingly bearing the brunt of the hit. Streak, oblivious to the pain, never loses his grip and climbs on top, intent on choking the life out of Alan. Kano: “Yeah!! Choke that bastard! KILL that joke!” Smith: “I saw up his toga! Whoo-Hoo!! He IS big!!” Kern: “No you didn’t, but Meanstreak is definitely trying to kill off PCW’s resident demi-god, Alan Steele! Steele is turning blue out there!” Smith: “Correction, BIG Alan Steele! AhhOoooGah!” Steele strains under the over 320 lbs. of the Streak, unable to un-wedge himself from underneath. Finally, Big Alan Steele places a foot in Streaks stomach and pushes. Sinew strains and muscles bulge, yet still Meanstreak refuses to let go… Steele rolls to the side and finally Meanstreak can’t hang on, the force incredible and sending the Monster flying, crashing over a stair railing and flipping wildly, crashing hard into the granite steps. Steele struggles to his feet, clutching at his raw throat. Kano: “Dammit! I want Steele dead, you hear me! DEAD! That would ROCK!” Kern: “Both men on their feet now, this ‘match’ getting started in earnest outside the arena!” The two men begin to fight for real. Each man hitting the other with their hardest shot, but both men remain on their feet. The two men exchange punches all the way back into the MCI Center. Dave: Big Alan Steele and Meanstreak going toe to toe! Winner will meet Howard Porter for a berth in the title match at New Dawning. Which man wants it more!?! Kano: KILL HIM, STREAK! KILL THE CCW REJECT! The crowd is eating all of this up. They are at the loudest they’ve been since the last appearance of Soy Boy. A few moments later, Meanstreak and BAS make their way onto the stage, still trading punches back and forth. The two big men brawl all the way down the ramp. Dave: neither man will give an inch. Kano: Ol’ Alan won’t be able to give any more inches after Meanstreak snaps his neck with At Death’s Door. Meanstreak starts to get the better of BAS. He goes to slam his head onto the apron, but BAS blocks, then slams The Monster’s head off the apron. He does it again and again. BAS slams Streak’s head about half a dozen times. Streak, in response to this, roars, then smashes BAS with a stiff punch to the face busting Steele’s nose open. Streak then throws BAS back in the ring. Kano: That’ll stop the CCW bastard. Streak grabs his head slightly and shakes it before climbing back in. Dave: it’s apparent that the migraines are starting to get to The Monster. Kano: Migraine or no migraine, Streak will kill him. BAS rises to his feet in the ring. He charges at Streak for a spear, but Meanstreak grabs BAS by the throat. He then lifts him high in the air and drops him with a powerful chokeslam. Streak pounds his chest and roars. Then follows that up by grabbing his head again. Kano: FINISH HIM! Streak shakes his head once more, then pulls BAS up. He lifts him up and sets him up for his patented signature move, At Death’s Door, Dave: This could be the end for the former CCW star! Kano: YES! YES! Suddenly, Meanstreak drops BAS. Kano: NO! Moments later, Streak collapses in the center of the ring. Kano: NOOOOOO! Dave: OH MY GOD! MEANSTREAK HAS JUST COLLAPSED IN THE MIDDLE OF THE RING! SOMEONE GET OUT HERE QUICK! Steven: I’ll be happy to give him mouth to mouth. Kano: SHUTUP! THIS AIN’T THE TIME! Big Alan Steele rises back to his feet. He shakes the cobwebs out, then looks down. He sees Streak passed out on the mat. A look comes across the face of BAS. It’s not really a look of concern. More like a look of disbelief. BAS scratches his head, then makes a cover on the unconciouss Meanstreak, 1……2……3 Winner: Big Alan Steele Dave: Big Alan Steele defeats Meanstreak! This is a huge upset! Kano: It woulda never happened had Streak not passed out! Dave: One can only believe that his migraines had something to do with it. Kano: Yep Dave: Medics have finally made their way out to attend to Meanstreak. We look in the ring as Big Alan Steele doesn’t know what to do. He looks down at the non-moving monster, then decides to just leave the ring. Medics come in and start to tend to Meanstreak. Dave: ladies and gentlemen, this is terrible. You hate to see this happen to anybody. Kano: Especially someone like Meanstreak. He’s the last man you expect this to happen to. If it can happen to him, it can happen to anybody. Dave: I hate to leave you on this somber note, but we’re out of time. We at PCW will update you on Meanstreak’s condition when it becomes available. For Steven Smith and Jon Kano, I’m Dave Kern. Good night. We see a good shot of Medics loading Meanstreak onto the stretcher as the PCW copyright information comes onto the screen and the show ends. ©2001 Primetime Championship Wrestling
