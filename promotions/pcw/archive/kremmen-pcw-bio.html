Kremmen Biography Real Name [name withheld] Nicknames None Birthday 14 August 1972 Height 6'3' Weight 275 lbs Biography Inducted by his PCW peers by virtue of a 17 vote margin over his nearest rival, Kremmen was the natural choice to be the first inductee into the PCW Hall of Fame. Kremmen, one of the founding members of PCW, defeated The Third Degree in the main event of the first ever PCW Card, after more than a week of wrangling at each other's throats before the event. This card also saw the debut of Meanstreak, Kremmen's arch-rival, as he interfered in the same match. Following that, Kremmen entered the 32 man tournament to crown the first ever PCW Heavyweight Champion at PCW's first ever Pay Per View event, PCW Dawning. Kremmen managed to come out on top over the cream of PCW's Roster in Reverend, Doug Troy and Meanstreak, all of whom have since held the prestigious Heavyweight Title. Kremmen's first stint in PCW was littered with shining moments. In the first all-in-brawl for the Extreme Title, Kremmen was able to eliminate several men from the match, and be the last man standing between Scott Naket and the Extreme Title before being run over by a Humvee driven by Ryan Knox. Along with the glory came pain, at the hands of none other than Meanstreak. Kremmen's feud with Meanstreak started the night of the first event, but began in earnest early on in the title tournament, as both men advanced through the early rounds. Recognising the potential threat early on, both men continually attacked each other, with Kremmen on the receiving end of some severe beat-downs and Meanstreak the target of Kremmen's more cerebral attacks, the most notable being sending Meanstreak on the Gerney Ride of Doom on one Friday Fusion, after repeatedly macing him and beating him down with a Kendo Stick. Adding Doug Troy and Reverend to the equation for the decider match to crown the first Heavyweight Champion only intensified the battle between the two men, but forced them to widen their scope to counter the two other accomplished superstar competitors. After winning the title, courtesy of a cattle prod to Streak's own rib cage ('the weapon of choice for ending Streaks' was Kremmen's first quote on winning the title, immediately before being attacked by 'The Scholar' Howard Porter and other members of the now defunct Think Tank stable), Kremmen wasted no time in offering the first title shot to Meanstreak stating that Doug Troy and Reverend would have their day soon enough. History shows that his prediction came true, with both having lengthy title reigns, and even today are still able to reclaim the gold should they seize an opportunity. History also shows that Meanstreak was victorious on the day of their first and only singles match to date, after riding a bull to ringside which gouged Kremmen's already injured ribs on the way. Kremmen, after already having competed under another identity in the now defunct UFW (Ultra Fantasy Wrestling) for almost 10 straight months previous, decided to retire at that point, coming back at the request of the Phantom Booker for two more matches the Main Event at PCW Decimation and the Main Event of the Friday Fusion immediately following. With the simultaneous announcement of a new 64 man tournament, and the return of Kremmen's arch foe Meanstreak, also since retired, the time seems right for a return by the great man. Time will tell if he can repeat the same feat and win the tournament, and lead PCW on and into 2001 as Heavyweight Champion. Footnote: After a retirment lasting several months, Kremmen came back to reach the final round of the 64 man Heavyweight Title tournament along with Meanstreak, Super Duper Masked Jobber King and Frisco, with Frisco walking away the PCW Heavyweight Champion. Kremmen formally announced his return to retirement, challenging Kid Wonder and 'The Scholar' Howard Porter to a match at Spring Fling for his last hurrah, in which he came out victorious, and later returned briefly to compete in and win the PCW Match of Champions, featuring all twelve former PCW Heavweight Champions (the Heavyweight Title being vacant at the time, so no current champ was there to compete). Footnote #2: After another retirement lasting several months, Kremmen returned in October 2001 as PCW's new CEO, to compete with former CEO Tyler Nelson as Team CEO in the Leathal Lottery tournament for the vacant PCW Heavyweight Title. Team CEO reached the finals of the tournament, taking out teams including Timo Bolamba/Kramer Wildwing, Boda/Duke Williams (then Continental Champ), Big Alan Steele and Stryfe (Meanstreak and Ivan Stanislav subbed for the missing Stryfe), Jeremy Howard/Rent-a-Hero, before losing in the final round of the tournament to Stanislav and Meanstreak, with Stanislav pinning Nelson. Kremmen went on to defeat Tyler Nelson in the playoff round for the right to be named #1 contender for the Heavyweight Title, right before Meanstreak won the title over Stansilav. For Kremmen's shot at the Heavyweight Title, he Nelson schemed to have Nelson included in the match, which was then turned into a fatal four way between Stanislav, Meanstreak, Nelson & Kremmen. The three men colluded, allowing Ivan Stanislav to defeat Meanstreak and the team of Management to be formed to compete at the Alliances PPV and rule PCW with an iron fist. Kremmen then feuded with Jeremy Howard, going 1W:1L:1D with the man who would later defeat Ivan Stanislav for the Heavyweight Title, and fought his last match in tag team with Tyler Nelson, losing to S&M (Miyagawa and Syko the Goblin) when Miyagawa pinned Nelson. Other Feds UFW, MWF, IWO Other Fed Awards 3 time UFW Heavyweight Champion, 1 x UFW US Champion, 2 x UFW Rising Star Champion, 1 x IWO Heavyweight Champion,
