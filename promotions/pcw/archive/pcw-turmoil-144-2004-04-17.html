A press conference is shown of Senator Dennis Sipowicz a senior Democrat from the state of Utah. His suit is well fitted as he approaches the microphone at the UTAH public building. For to long we have allowed our children to be brainwashed into thinking violence, profanity, and human flaw is ok. It is NOT ok. Wrestling is rubbish and these drug addict circus folk need to be taken off our public air waves. Unlike Boxing the charade that is wrestling doesn’t claim to be a sport but rather entertainment. A TV show designed to entertain an audience just like a sitcom. The senator clears his throat. Sipowicz: Since wrestling is not a sport it doesn’t have to follow certain rules such as drug screening or a gaming board. Well since wrestling got around our laws set up for fair play claiming to be entertainment I’ve decided to treat it as such. Unless these wrestling programs claim to be a ligament sport and follow the rules of the states it must be cleaned up as entertainment. The FCC has filed fines against the FOX television network for strong language used by many of the so called super stars. I urge the network to drop this filth and beg the advertisers to also pull out. If they don’t they will be fined and punished. This is a war for our children and I will not rest until wrestling is gone for good. Thank you.
