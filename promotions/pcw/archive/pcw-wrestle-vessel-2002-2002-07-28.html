Wrestle Vessel 2002 28 Jul 2002 Montego Bay, Jamaica Love Boat assault on the Wrestle Vessel A sweeping view of a crystal clear blue ocean fills the screen as the camera swoops over the water as if through the eyes of a bird. Speeding just above the waves we are quickly taken out to sea. As we speed along the view begins to elevate and off in the distance we can see the form of an enormous cruise ship, painted bright white with blue accents, knifing gracefully through the water. Suddenly a symphony begins to blare with horns sounding the beginning of the theme music to the hit TV show, 'The Love Boat'. As the intro begins we are taken above the luxury liner and around in a 360 degree circle over the top. 'Love.........exciting and new....' We quickly switch to a view on the boat, inside the control room. Standing there in his white captains outfit, looking somewhat older than we remember, is Gavin MacLeod aka Captain Merrill Stubing. He smiles big at the camera as the jingle continues. 'Come aboard........we're expecting youuuuuuuuuu....' Suddenly a steel chair swings into the scene and nearly takes off the good captain's head. His hat goes twirling and flipping into the air as Stubing falls like a rock to the floor. 'The Love Boat!' From off camera a hand reaches out and grabs the captain's hat. As we pan back we see the grinning face of the burned man himself, Clyde, as he happily places the captain's hat atop his head. Bethany is at his side as always, screaming down at Stubing. Bethany: DIE!! DIE!! DIE!! 'Soon we'll be making another run....' We move away from the sadistically grinning Clyde and the laid out Captain Stubing to the Prominade Deck where your cruise director Lauren Tewes, aka Julie McCoy, is standing by with the ships manifest of passengers. She greets several elderly couples that pass her by, smiling warmly to them and nodding respectfully. As she looks up and focuses her eyes off camera, her face becomes one of shock and horror. Panning over we see the source of her distress, a massive group of PCW wrestlers all heading toward her. Those we can make out include PCW Rising Star Champion Vennemon Pendragon, PCW Cruiserweight Champion The End, PCW Extreme Champion Rush Mason, PCW Tag Team Champion Akiyama, PCW Television Champion John Crichton, PCW Continental Champion Darkstar, and the PCW World Heavyweight Champion Big Alan Steele. The mob of humanity stampedes in Julies direction, causing her to scream and run off in fear. 'The Love Boat!' By the pool stands former US Representative and current President of Goodwill Industries Fred Gandy, aka Gopher. Next to him is Bernie Koppell, aka Dr. Bricker. The two men are dressed to the nines in their ship uniforms, consisting of tight white short-shorts and figure hugging officers shirts that they have no business wearing anymore. They gawk and drool over the bevy of bathing beauties laying out by the pool. Gopher nudges Dr. Bricker in the ribs and nods his head toward a lady lying on a chaise lounger across the deck. Both are smiles as Gopher strolls over toward her, grabbing a bottle of baby oil along the way. He stops next to the woman and sits down next to her, a suave smirk on his face. He begins to pour the baby oil on her back. Startled, the woman sits up on her elbows revealing her identity as Brandi. She frowns at Gopher and takes a swing at him, backhanding him off the chair and sending him tumbling to the deck. Dr. Bricker nearly falls out laughing, until a hand reaches up from the pool and drags him backward into it. After a second a head comes emerging from the pool we see the smiling Flaymin Aymin. 'Promises something for everyone.....' Over at the bar Ted Lange, aka Isaac Washington, wipes down the counter and notices the camera pointed at him. He does his trademark finger point with the crooked smile of his, trying to look hip at his advanced age. Soon enough two other black men step up to the bar and sit down. Jordan X. Shabazz and Thug roC motion for a round of drinks and Isaac is quick to oblige, setting a pair of tropical looking drinks in front of them complete with umbrellas and fruit on plastic swords. Isaac looks proudly at the concoctions as Jordan and Thug roC exchange bewildered looks. Suddenly the two Uprising members hop over the bar and begin to beat the living hell out of Isaac, stomping and punching him into unconsciousness. When they are through they dig underneath the bar and produce several bottles of Cristal, popping them open and beginning to drink straight from the bottle. 'Set a course for adventure, your mind on a new romance....' Moving to the aerobics room we see Jill Whelan, aka Vicki Stubing, trudging away on a stairmaster. Sweat rolls down her face as the daughter of the ships captain works out. From behind her walks Syko the Goblin, looking the homely woman up and down as if she was some sort of prize. She looks down at him with a disturbed face, Syko winks up at her. She scowls and turns her head quickly away, intentionally ignoring the Goblin. He cocks his head and reaches up, pressing the 'plus' button on the speed control. The stairs begin to move at an increased pace, causing Vicki to grasp a hold of the handles while almost sprinting up the stairs. Syko keeps his finger on the button until it reaches the maximum speed, then walks off as Vicki finally is unable to keep up and falls flat on her face, her head thumping sickly as the stairs continue to rotate around. 'It's loooooooooooooove.......welcome aboard it's looooooo....' We move to an aerial view of the ship, and we can see that it has become one giant melee with brawls happening on all parts of the ship. '..ooooooooooooooooove!!' Finally we get a view from the back of the ship. As the cruise ship heads off in the distance we can see Xavier Kannon and Ellie~! hanging off the back, spray painting over the words ‘Pacific Princess'. When they are finished the words they spray painted magically jump off the ship and plaster themselves across the screen.... WRESTLE VESSEL The scene fades to black as the music comes to a halt.
