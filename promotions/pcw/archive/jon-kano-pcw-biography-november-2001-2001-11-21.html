Jon Kano Real Name: Jonathan Kano Nick Name: "The Man Who Rocketh the Mosteth" Catchphase: Anything ending in "Kano ROCKS" Character Handler: Jon B. MSN Account: N/A AIM Address: [detail withheld] Theme Song: "That's Gangsta" by Shyne Age: 25 From: Las Vegas, Nevada Height: 6 feet, 10 and 1 half inches Weight: 304 pounds Wrestling Style: Technician/brawler Signature Moves: Suplexes, Martial Arts kicks and punches, Power Slam, Power Bomb, Top Rope Elbow Drop, Kanospear, Kanoplex, Gorilla Press Slam, Tombstone Piledriver, Piledriver Finisher Move: Kanoplex (overhead rock bottom, pin) Set-Up Move: Kanospear Current Team: None Past Teams & Groups: Media w/ Tyler Nelson, Chet King, Dan Hollywood, Chris Allen, Bo Raider, & The Chef Media Moguls w/ Chris Allen Outcasts Title History: PCW Continental Title PCW Television Title PCW Rising Star Title Awards History: None, unless you count having people across the globe chanting "Kano ROCKS." Description: Jon Kano is the image of a healthy American. He is dedicated to being a muscular man, boasting his 2.1 percent body fat percentage any time he can. He has long blond hair usually slicked back. History: Jonathan Kano was born on February 19, 1976 at Our Lady of the Sacred Heart hospital to Marc and Jennifer Kano. Kano showed a strong interest in sports throughout his childhood, mostly following his older brother, Ken,'s footsteps. Ken and Jon were very close and both enlisted in the Army following graduating from high school (Ken in '92, Jon in '94, seeing as they were two years apart in age). Both reached the rank of Specialist in demolition. Early in 1996, Ken was killed in a freak accident while defusing a so-called obsolete bomb. Jon was devastated following his brother's death, then his discharge from the Army. Kano bounced from job to job, mostly working as a bodyguard and bouncer until he found his calling as a professional wrestler. Kano has since been wrestling all across the world, capturing gold in 5 different continents. In May of 1998, Kano founded Kano Enterprises, Inc. This company funds different real estate projects and investments. This company has made Kano into a multi-millionaire along with his salary from wrestling. In late October 2000, Kano was signed by Primetime Championship Wrestling. Kano has ever since been a part of PCW history, and many believe that he is a future hall of famer.
