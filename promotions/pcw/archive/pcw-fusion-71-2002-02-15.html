Fusion 71 15 Feb 2002 The Staples Center, Los Angeles, California Tyler Nelson searches for White Mexican on a rural highway We open the show with a view of the California countryside as an “Earlier Today” logo appears in the top right corner of the screen. The sun bathes the sparsely populated area along a less traveled highway. On the side of the road sets a small produce stand, more akin to a large cart than anything else. The white paint is chipping off badly and a tattered tent serves to block the bright sun from the good underneath. A horse is tethered to a fence post down in the ditch, representing the only sign of life that we can see. After a few seconds the sound of a car approaching is heard, growing ever closer at a fairly high speed. Suddenly a black blur flashes across the screen. “STOP!” A familiar voice roars over the serenity of the scene and tires begin to screech to a halt. Several seconds pass before the squealing of the tires ceases. “Back up to that food stand back there, NOW!” The command brings the blur back into the picture, which turns out to be a black Hummer limousine. The limo backs up in front of the cart, off to the shoulder of the highway. The rear passenger door flies open and the CEO of PCW Tyler Nelson comes popping out of the back. He looks around, then digs a pair of sunglasses out of the inside pocket of his black Armani suit jacket. He slides the shades on his face and then proceeds to walk over to the cart. Tyler: MEXI?!? Nelson calls out the name, continuing to keep his head on a swivel as he scans left and right. He cups his hands around his mouth. Tyler: MEXI, ARE YOU OUT HERE?!? The CEO gets to the cart as a short, Hispanic old man comes hobbling out from the back side of the cart. He has on a worn out pair of bib overalls with a beat up straw hat. The white hair on his head stretches all around his face in a full beard. The old man’s bare feet shuffle along the gravel shoulder of the road. Old man: Hola, senor! Nelson scowls at the old man and points an accusing finger at him. Tyler: What the hell did you just say to me?! Have you seen a jobless piece of trash out this way? He probably tried to steal some of your food. The old man scratches his chin as he thinks.
