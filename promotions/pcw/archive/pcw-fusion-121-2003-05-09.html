Fusion 121 9 May 2003 The Nikos Goumas Stadium, Athens, Greece The Jester and Miss Moonlight's translator trouble EARLIER TODAY The feed opens backstage with PCW personnel bustling about completing their daily routines and competitors going over last minute preparation for what looks to be a massive Friday Night Fusion. The bare concrete floor is laden in electrical wires and large cargo boxes used for storage litter the floor-space and sitting atop of one of these containers is the current Cruiserweight Champion Miss Moonlight. The Japanese warrior stares fixated into her glistening plaque that sits nestled against her shoulder and completely oblivious to the Triple Threat that walks into view. Gaz Green: Everything is as planned I take it gentlemen? The Emerald Menace walks ahead in his custom green business suit with both members of Entertainment Extreme following in pursuit. Tyler Preston appears a carbon copy of his mentor while his partner fidgets about with a cringe of disgust in his face as he peers down at the long black trench coat he adorns. Tyler: Indeed squire… John: We topped the eighteen-wheelers’ petrol tanks with sugar so Primetime Championship Wrestling maybe a little late for Tuesday Turmoil… Tyler: (Interrupting) Further more we littered the car park with some sharp metallic objects, the type that can easily pierce rubber! John: And within this bag we have a few other surprises! Impressed the Jester patted his protégés on the shoulder and the Triple Threat ventured inward. Capturing the Cruiserweight Champion in the corner of his eye the Tag Champion sadistically smiled to himself and waltzed over with a cocky strut as Entertainment Extreme followed behind in attack formation. Tyler: (Ranting eagerly) I say we telephone the immigration office and get her deported back to Japan where she can make Manchester United shirts for living! Before we know Primetime Championship Wrestling will be over-ridden with Moonlight family members… John: It’s a fact that over in the Sushi shores of Japan, the head of the family single-handily picks one of their own to work over seas and when they earn enough money they come across and invade! Gaz Green: (Interrupting) Hence why they all own takeaways, it’s real meaning… We have not come here to sell you oriental food but to take your jobs, homes, money and more importantly gold. That’s exactly what Miss Moonlight took, my Cruiserweight Championship and in doing so my moment in the spotlight! With a finger gesture the comic parted company with his students and proceeded over to the lonesome Cruiserweight Champion who sits in silence, peering down at her prize as she waits for her interpreter…The Pimply Face Intern Kid. Gaz Green: DO YOU SPEAK ENGLISH? The green tailored Jester suddenly grins and reaches into the breast pocket of his business jacket and after rummaging momentarily he produces a small handheld gadget with air holes indented into the casing. Looking somewhat of a radio the Jester holds it aloft as though he was conducting an interview with a Dictaphone and begins the conversation again. Gaz Green: I know that thing is sparkly and intoxicating but it’s going to bring you nothing but misery so I’d be only to happy to remove this burden from you and we’ll call your victory a fluke, how’s that? Miss Moonlight: Anata wa kanbou desu ka? Radio: So you want to be a bus driver do you? The Jester’s angelic face cringes up in annoyance and turns red as his inner rage mounts but even his facial expressions don’t dishearten the Cruiserweight Champion as she happily watches on with a squint in her eyes and a twitch of a smile murmuring beneath her veil like mask. Gaz Green: Do you know who I am? I’m a five time PCW Tag Team Champion and I would be a damn singles star if I hadn’t of tripped and fell over the tope rope… You do know that rope was faulty right and I was plagued by misfortune? The Jester evilly grins as he concludes and extends his hand with the over-hanging lighting bringing a glisten to his infamous hand buzzer and Miss Moonlight is about to exchange when the device catches her eye. Unaware she slowly protrudes her arm further and in doing so she speaks, Miss Moonlight: Konbanwa!!!! Suddenly the radio translator bellows its response. Radio: I don’t take sweeties off strangers, Mister; I’m not that type of girl! The Lord of Laughter’s mouth drops ajar and hangs loosely by the insult before making his concluding retort. Gaz Green: That one word meant all that? I’ve said my peace and I’m now regarding you squire, sweet cheeks, whatever… As a loose end!
