Parking Lot Pounding — PCW in-card segment

2 August 2002

Parking Lot Pounding Written: August 2nd, 2002 The camera moves off Cal and zooms in once again on the Orange OSW t-shirt of Xavier Kannon, drawing more ravenous jeers as the shot fades out. Instead of going back to the announce table we move to another part of the arena’s parking lot where a black limo pulls up and then stops. The driver comes around to the door’s side and opens the back door to show…Rush Mason and Jerome “Blayze” Jordan, which brings more boos from the crowd. Dave: Oh great, these two again. We need to get this DAMN show on the road! Jeff: Oh shut up, Rush is one of the bravest men I know after receiving that cowardly attack from…*sniff* the artist formerly known as DA MAN. Duke: Too bad Clyde isn’t your butt buddy anymore, Marx. Neither Rush nor Blayze looks very happy, and we can see, through Rush’s open collared t-shirt that his ribs are very heavily taped, and he has bandages on his head and neck as well from the beatdown he received from Clyde on Turmoil. Rush: That son of a bitch Nelson… Blayze: It’s aite man, I’ll kick those two white boys asses lickety split so we can be gone before Clyde tries any of that funny shit. Even this doesn’t bring a smile to Rush’s face, but the mention of Clyde gets a pop from the crowd. Rush turns to the driver and speaks to him with boldness in his voice. Rush: Hey, you there! Second-class citizen! The driver gulps and uses a finger motion to say ‘Me?’ and Rush nods as the driver walks over slowly. Rush: Okay, here’s the deal. You and your sub-par self will stand in front of this limo. DO NOT move a muscle. Don’t even breathe my first-rate air, got it? The driver nods, looking timid as Rush glares at him. Rush: That flea-ridden goth loser is not putting his hands no me tonight. If he show’s his Kentucky Fried face around me I’m liable to just rip it off! With this statement, Rush and Blayze walk off towards their locker room. As soon as the two men turn their backs, the driver raises his hand and flips Rush off. The crowd pops huge. Dave: WHOA!! Jeff: Shhhh! No talking during segs like this. Dave: Sorry. Rush stops dead in his tracks, causing Blayze to stop alongside him and look over questioningly. Rush: I think that muthafucka just flipped me off! Blayze looks back at the driver who is standing next to the car, trying his best to look innocent. Blayze: How you know that? Rush: I can just feel it. It’s a Voodoo thang. With an exchange of looks and a nod between the two, both men spin on their heels and charge the driver. He freezes in fear as the two men run toward him. Soon they are on him and knock him to the ground. Rush puts the boots to him as Blayze kneels down and rocks the man with a series of right hands to the head. The arena erupts into a chorus of boos. Rush: YOU WANT SOME OF THIS, CLYDE? WHAT? HUH? I CAN’T HEAR YA MUTHAFUCKA!! The merciless attack continues until PCW security flows out from the arena and pulls Rush and Blayze off of the driver. The scene fades to black as Rush and Blayze struggle with security. Blayze: Awwww yeah….you see that shit, Clyde? You done pissed my boy Rush off something fierce!! Ya best ta watch ya back, suckaaaaaaa!!! View Jerome 's Biography Back
