In the ring, Tyler wears black wrestling trunks and boots with the word “Greed” across his backside and down his boots. Outside the ring he wears a vast array of Armani suits. History: Tyler grew up in a middle class family that always had what they needed, but he always wanted more. He used schemes, coercion, and blackmail to build an empire-Greed Incorporated.
