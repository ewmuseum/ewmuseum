Snow Biography Real Name Jason Adam Snow Nicknames The Hitter, The Lion Hometown Backwater, New Brunswick, Canada Birthday May 15, 1978 Height 6'1 Weight 248 lbs Biography **** COMING SOON!!!! Information on characters from the Tank and The Snow Show. **** Jason Adam Snow was born on the fifteenth of May, 1978, to David Snow and Mary Patterson (who were later married). Young Jason was born into a family notorious for their violent nature. His own father was likely the most feared man in Backwater New Brunswick, the small fishing village where Jason grew up. He was known for a fiery temper that seems to have been passed down to young Jason. Roots of Jason’s vicious temper can also be drawn to his maternal grandfather, who in his day, was noted for violent behaviour and had a reputation that stated he would “hit ya as soon as look at ya.” Later in life, before Jason Snow was born, Adam found religion and has been an upstanding, non-violent, Christian ever since. Christianity played a large part in Jason’s early life. Every Sunday, he would rise early and be driven to church by his maternal grandparents. He was raised with Christian beliefs, even though his own home was anything but Christian-like. By the time young Jason started school, he was smaller than all of the rest of the children. He soon found himself being picked on and bullied by the larger boys. Nothing changed when he entered high school. The future PCW World Champion was nearly a foot shorter than the other boys and endured their constant harassment. Deep inside, however, there was a tiny flame ignited in those years. Snow began working out vigorously, trying hard to put himself on par with the older boys, even though at the time he thought that he would never reach them in height. He got quick results, though none were visible. Snow’s physical strength and conditioning quickly became superior to that of the other boys his age, as was displayed in sports that young Jason took part in. Still, young Jason didn’t look physically impressive, and many bullies still felt that they could take advantage of the short, scrawny, awkward teenager. They soon would find that things had changed... Despite the fact that the boys laughed at, and ridiculed, young Jason, girls were starting to notice other things about the young man. By the time he was fourteen, the girls at Georgetown High School started to notice Jason’s bright blue eyes and golden blond hair and they started paying more attention to him. More attention than one particular bully would like... Story has it that one day while fourteen year old Jason Snow was standing at his locker, a particularly beautiful young lady started a conversation. Locals say that Snow was still shy and awkward at that age so it’s hard to imagine that he initiated any of it. The young lady’s boyfriend (a large boy, two years Snow’s senior and a good fifty pounds heavier than him), however, didn’t seem to care who initiated the conversation. He approached Snow fiercely, pushing him against his locker and pinning him there while shouting threats in the face of the future World Champion. It was at that very moment, witnesses say, that the now-common fire first appeared in Jason Snow’s eyes. The small teenager snapped. Years of being picked on had brought him to this point, and all of his fury was taken out on one unfortunate boy who thought he could push young Jason Snow around. Snow, who had been working out vigorously for years now, surprised the larger boy with his superior strength and quickness. Witnesses say that Snow beat the large boy viciously into unconsciousness and three male teachers were needed to pull him off the bloody body of his victim. A trial was held soon after, and young Jason Snow was sentenced to serve one year at a juvenile detention center. Little is known of what happened behind the walls of that facility, but when Jason Snow returned to Georgetown High School one year later, at the age of fifteen, jaws dropped. At fifteen, Jason Snow was no longer the short, scrawny, kid who would be lucky to way a hundred pounds soaking wet - he was now five feet, eleven inches tall, and weighed somewhere in the area of a hundred and seventy pounds. Needless to say, his days of being picked on long over. But physical changes weren’t the only ones in Jason Snow... “He was such a shy, sweet, kid before he went away,” says one anonymous woman who attended Georgetown High at the same time as Jason Snow, “But when he came back, everything was different. He started demanding that we call him ‘Snow’ and talked about ‘Superiority’ and ‘Greatness’ all the time. Everyone was afraid of him, and he made sure we stayed that way.” Physically, Snow was far superior to every other boy at Georgeville Highschool, and he knew it. That knowledge only fed his ever growing ego. He began making outrageous statements about his “Superiority.” He demanded things like straight A’s, his choice of seat in every room in the school, and even early graduation! Usually, Snow didn’t get his way, but people DID go out of their ways to make him more comfortable. Snow’s ego was growing at an alarming rate. He began referring to people as peasants, plebes, and simpletons. Girls at Georgetown High became afraid to refuse the cocky young student for a date. Most, however, were willing anyway. Snow was good looking and powerful, after all. But there was one girl who got deep inside Snow’s psyche. She paid little to no attention to him whatsoever, and drew him like a fly to a web... At sixteen, Snow melted. Soon after graduation, the two married and Snow began working at a cement factory. The marriage hit many bumps in the early going. By eighteen, Snow was already a heavy drinker and was dabbling in hard drugs as well. His wife knew little of the drinking and nothing of the drugs. The secret would later nearly ruin the marriage. Working at the cement factory, Snow soon realized that he was not making enough money to support himself and his wife who was studying to become an elementary school teacher. He found his niche, and an outlet to more money, when the IWA (International Wrestling Academy) came by looking for applicants. After a tryout, it was obvious that Snow had a natural gift and soon hit the road with the Academy, learning his new trade. While he was gone, however, his wife would make a shocking discovery. Hidden somewhere in the house, she found something unknown that would lead her to Snow’s drug habit. By the time Snow came home, she was gone, leaving nothing but a letter. Snow put his drug habit aside, but his abuse of alcohol became increasingly more severe. Snow was served with divorce papers on the same day as he received a contract with an up-and-coming Indie Federation known as SIW. It seemed that Snow was destined for stardom, but he soon would crash into a bump in the road... One night, while waiting for both the legal side of his divorce and his SIW contract to go through, Snow found himself a drunken wreck at a local bar in Georgetown. There, he caught wind that his wife was out that night with a med-student. Georgetown isn’t the largest place in the world, so the young future PCW superstar found his estranged wife and her date, and followed them throughout the night. Legend has it, the med-student dropped Snow’s estranged wife off at her apartment without even receiving a good night kiss. He would receive much more than he bargained for a little while later. Snow, who was still drinking, followed the young man home driving his 1988 GMC. After the young man retired into his house, the irrational soon-to-be Killin’ Villain drove his truck into the man’s living room! The shocked young man could do nothing as Snow’s violent temper took over and he beat the man into the hospital. Three months later, at the age of twenty, Jason Snow was sentenced to one year in prison. Most people who knew him thought that Jason Snow would be nothing but trouble in prison, since he was nothing but trouble in his every-day life. The opposite came to be true, however. Just like high school, word spread quickly about the young man’s violent temper and he wasn’t bothered while in prison. When he went into the Dorchestor Correctional Facility, Jason Snow was six feet, one inches tall, and weighed a little bit under two hundred pounds. When he was paroled, six months into his sentence, Snow weighed in at two hundred and thirty pounds. After surviving one of Canada’s harshest prisons without a scratch, Snow’s ego was at an all time high. One of the conditions of his parole, however, was Snow would have to attend one AA meeting per week. While in prison, his divorce went through... He walked out the front gates of the prison where Trevor Derrickson (owner of the now defunct SIW) was waiting in a limo. This was the beginning of twenty-one year old Jason Snow’s professional wrestling career, even if it was a little later than originally anticipated. Snow began his career with a bang. He came onto the SIW and turned every head, spiking the ratings for the small television station that it appeared on twice a week. As a bonus, Snow received a 1998 Dodge Viper which he showed off at every opportunity. He wore Snake Skin boots and black leather pants. He began referring to himself as “the Killin’ Villain”. And lastly, but not leastly, he made the outrageous claim that “the mere SIGHT of [him] has been KNOWN to cause ORGASMS!” Early in Snow’s career, he met the man who would soon become his greatest rival - a young man by the name of Doc Shock. Snow and Shock had a few memorable battles over the SIW European Championship. In those early days, Snow impressed a group of Indy fed legends known as DEATHROW. It’s said that behind the scenes, DEATHROW were showing Jason Snow the ropes of the wrestling business. Snow rose through the ranks of SIW, and when his parole was up, he turned back to the bottle. Soon, he found himself in trouble once more after assaulting a man at a bar. A judge gave Snow a slap on the wrist, sentencing him to spend time at a Betty Ford clinic to help him with his addiction. When Snow returned from the clinic, he put his life back in perspective. He returned to his wife and the two were soon remarried and life was well. SIW would go under and thanks to some wise investments, Snow was able to take some time off to spend with his wife (whose name he will not tell, and threatens a lawsuit on anyone who does). Soon however, he got a phonecall from the man who taught him most about the business, SIW legend, DEATH of DEATHROW. Snow learned that DEATH and the rest of his crew had joined a federation in Mexico. Snow thought it over, and soon headed to Mexico to join his old co-workers. His wife, soon after, learned that she was pregnant. In UWLM, Snow soon found himself wearing the Television Championship while his good friend, DEATH fought for the largest title in the federation, as usual. Despite their respect for each other, Snow and Doc Shock looked destined to clash again. That would never happen however, because in defence of DEATHROW, Jason Snow got banned from UWLM. Some say he quit. In protest of DEATHROW’s firing, Snow was last seen throwing his television championship into the crowd and walking out of Mexico (which he playfully renamed “Mexi-Snow”). Again, Snow took some time to spend with his expecting wife in their home, just outside Saint John, New Brunswick, Canada. Before long, however, he began getting the old itch to be inside a wrestling ring. In February of 2001, Jason Snow searched for a contract. His past problems with alcohol, combined with what happened in UWLM, made most major federations sceptical of the young talent. Soon, he found a home in a mediocre federation called AWA. Snow found that he didn’t fit in there, however, so when he received a call from PCW, telling him that they had a spot for him in the newly formed PWA, the young Killin’ Villain packed his bags and headed for the PCW Pavillion in New Jersey. PWA proved to be exactly what Snow needed. He skyrocketed and gained attention from everyone throughout PWA, PCW2, and PCW. Within a week, Snow signed a contract with PCW2 on the same day as Vampir Nosferatu, making them the first two PWA graduates. Snow stuck around PWA, despite having the contract, long enough to finish his final match where he received $20,000 for winning. Snow was on to PCW2... It was mid-May of 2002 and Jason Snow was getting all the recognition he could handle. PCW was taking notice too. Before long, Snow became the first PCW2 rising star champion by defeating his PWA rival, D-Kay Calyx. And then came Gold and Glory... PCW2 Champions met PCW Champions, with the titles on the line! Snow’s opponent was none other than fellow PWA graduate, the Polish Punisher, who had graduated from PWA straight into PCW. Snow was determined to prove himself, despite the fact that all PCW2 wrestlers were major underdogs. When the PPV was over, it was PCW2 who were laughing, including Jason Snow, who won the PCW rising star championship before officially being added to the roster. PCW proved much tougher than any federation Snow had ever been in. It was, after all, arguably the world’s best wrestling federation. But Snow was up to the challenge. In the PCW restart of 2001, the Killin’ Villain was stripped of his PCW Rising Star Championship. When the seeding for the Championship tournament, involving every member of the roster, was announced, PCW management shocked the world by seeding the young blooming star 3rd in his bracket. Snow would do well in the tournament, but was beaten by Tyler Nelson after a missed chairshot from Jeremy Howard. Later, we would learn that the chairshot might not have been accidental... Greed Inc formed, with Jeremy Howard and Tyler Nelson at the helm. Snow was the last addition and battled in the mid-card with the likes of Big Alan Steele and White Mexican while Jeremy Howard fought for the highest prize in wrestling - the PCW Championship title. In August of 2001, Snow became a father when Haley Sue Snow was born. Snow adapted well to father and was rumoured to be a model parent. By October, the Killin’ Villain and Kid Wonder (who hated each other with a passion) found themselves teaming together in a lethal lottery tournament for the PCW Championship. The two would make their exit from the tournament at Red October. After the second coming of Greed Inc went under, Snow soon found himself with another stable. New Breed, along with Timo Bolamba, Corey Williams, and Chris Chase, quickly became the most controversial stable in PCW. It wouldn’t be long before the Killin’ Villain would find himself at odds with none other than the man who took him under his wing in his earliest days of PCW, Jeremy Howard. Howard and Snow, who at the time were two of the most popular wrestlers in PCW, collided at Shanghai’d II over the PCW Heavyweight Championship of the World. After a memorable battle, Jason Snow won the highest prize in wrestling - but the night wasn’t over. Timo Bolamba was turned on by the other members of New Breed, once again making Jason Snow one of the most hated men in PCW. At that time, Snow’s marriage became rocky again. His wife informed him that she was leaving for the second time. “She simply could not handle the fact that Snow was flaunting himself around women every time she turned on the television,” said an anonymous friend of the family. By that time, January of 2002, the effects of PCW’s grueling schedule, combined with a series of small injuries, had taken their toll on the young Champion. Rumours of illness began rising when Snow had grown noticeably pale and seemed to be abandoning his vigorous physical routine. Months of PCW’s schedule, various injuries, as well as personal problems were finally catching up to Snow. At March Madness, Snow lost his PCW Heavyweight Championship to Boda in a 20 man battle royal. Upon being eliminated, Snow did further damage to his right knee, which was already injured. He went under the knife later that week and informed PCW that he would need time to recover. His knee wasn’t the only thing that recovered. In his time off, Snow once again made peace with his wife and the two moved back in together. It is said that Snow contemplated early retirement, since his time in Greed Inc had made him wealthy beyond belief and money was no longer a concern. A month passed, and Snow’s knee was near one hundred percent. After careful thought, he called PCW and informed them that he was ready to make his return. A month later, he learned that his wife was pregnant with their second child. Snow returned to PCW by helping his stablemate, Chris Chase, win the PCW Heavyweight Championship of the World. New Breed would later turn the wrestling world upside down when Chris Chase literally GAVE the Championship to Snow a few nights later, in an attempt to keep Big Alan Steele from getting his shot. Snow would then pass the Championship along to Corey Williams, and then get it back in the same fashion before heading into PCW Supremacy. There, Big Alan Steele would not be denied again. In a three way battle between Snow, Steele, and the CEO, Tyler Nelson, Jason Snow lost the PCW Championship when Tyler Nelson was pinned by Big Alan Steele. The night didn’t get any better from there. Next, the Phantom Booker shocked the world when he, along with Tyler Nelson, fired every member of PCW, including Snow! It was announced that PCW would return to action, with a new roster, at a later date. Snow took the time off to spend with his wife and daughter. He was able to spend his twenty-fourth birthday at home. Within the week, he learned that he was named to the new PCW roster. ~~~~ Jason Snow is the ultimate competitor. He gives his all in every match, regardless of which side the fans are on. He refuses to believe that there is another wrestler on the planet who can beat him, and will use any trick necessary to keep that belief strong. It’s also evident that his ego is out of control. Snow is constantly referring to his own “Greatness” and “Superiority.” He believes that he is the closest thing to perfection that God has ever created, and quickly is insulted if anyone even considers him otherwise.
