Reeco Suave Biography Biography Born in Rochester, New York in 1977 to a decent middle class family, he grew up with an average life. He was the kid next door. Just another average joe. His parents, however, treated him like more than that. To them, he was the greatest thing alive.. and they showed it. They showered him with everything and anything he wanted, even if it meant having to work extra jobs here and there to make up the money. To Reeco, this was normal. He didn't see anything wrong with this, as he had been getting this sort of treatment since the day he was born. And so, he entered school, thinking he was like everyone else. He was from a middle class, blue collar family. The rest of the kids, however, saw him as an asshole. In their eyes, he had a 'higher-than-thou' attitude. So for the majority of high school, Reeco was thought of as the 'outsider' in the school groups, 'cliques', etc. Reeco knew something was wrong. He knew he was being seperated from people all the time. However, after getting virtually everything he ever wanted his entire life, he began thinking like he was the normal one.. and everyone else had problems. He developed a bad attitude toward the rest of the schoolkids, which continued on until he graduated from High School. After graduation, he visited a touring independant wrestling federation that was in town. Once he saw some of the things these guys did in the ring, he immediately fell in love with it. The first thing he did going home that night, was tell his parents he wanted to become a professional wrestler. They did not agree. They wanted a better life for him.. better than they had. It was tough to hear, for the first time in his life.. that he couldn't have something. After years and years of getting anything he wanted, he finally stumbled upon the one thing he wanted more than life itself.. and he couldn't have it. So, now a legal adult, Reeco packed his bags and left. He went upstate to a wrestling school in northern New York. With the money he had saved for a few years, he was able to pay his tuition. And for the next four years, he trained.. and he learned more and more with each passing day. He was hailed as the best to ever come out of the school, and it did nothing to improve Reeco's way of thinking. He graduated from the wrestling school with top honors, and developed an ego. He toured the independant circuit for a while, always thinking highly of himself. Finally.. he came to rest in FedWars. Here, he found three new friends who had similar outlooks on life. Together with his three new friends; Syn, Token Weed, and Vinnie Catante.. he formed the Ideal 4. They epitomized everything that was great about the business. FedWars shut down. And like all good things, the Ideal 4 came to an end. Reeco went his own way, and returned to touring the indy circuit. In the indys, he gained a new appreciation for the sport, and he lost alot of his so-called ego. Now, almost a year later, he's back. However, with each passing moment, he struggles with his inner-self.. not wanting his ego to get the best of him like it has in the past. Description: Reeco Suave has short, dark brown hair that is parted at the front. His hair has a very slight spike to it. He has blue eyes, and slightly accentuated cheek bones. As far as facial hair goes, he has a sort of 'unshaven' look going. With a slight beard and goatee. He has a medium build, with well defined muscles.. but not to the point where he is 'muscle-bound'. He has his forearms, wrists, and hands taped with black wrestling tape. Reeco wears black thigh-length tights which say 'The Ego' on the back. He wears black wrestling boots which have padding covering his shins all the way up to his knees.
