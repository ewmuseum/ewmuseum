The Long Journey to Discover....pt2: Burning Issues

27 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 27th Oct It's only days away from a huge match that could decide who the next PCW Champion is, as the winner of this match will go on to face the current Champion Clyde at Alliances. The Mexican Devil El Diablo squares off against Duke Williams, Jason Snow and Tim Walden on Tuesday Turmoil to determine this. The Lord of Mexico knows very much about Williams and Snow as he has defeated both at one time or another, however Walden will be a new challenge to him. But if Walden can be taken control of by Teresa Tomas, surely there will be no trouble in eliminating Walden from the title picture. One thing is for certain; the match will be brutal, as all four men want that title.------------ 'Hey, lets go in that store,' Angela says to her husband. El Diablo, his wife Angela and his security Kurt and Leroy are in a shopping mall in Rome, Italy. The mall is nothing in comparison to American malls but is rather large nonetheless, and caters many stores- many known to American's too, such as Levi's. The reason they're in a mall in Italy is due to the PCW International tour, and even if unbooked El Diablo must go on the tour, but luckily for him he is booked on both Turmoil and Fusion, and he really loves to wrestle, no matter who it's against. But, why in a mall? Well, Angela like a typical woman just loves to shop, and has dragged El Diablo on as well as his 24/7 security. 'Not another perfume store, please?' pleads Diablo. Angela sulks a little, to try and get some attention from her husband. 'Fine, you go in there and we'll wait out here,' he compromises with her. Angela smiles and walks in to the store. 'What is it with women and toiletries?' El Diablo asks. Kurt and Leroy shrug as they all go to find a bench to sit on near the store as they wait for Angela to come out after spending more of her husband's money. 'You know, I'd have more money to spend if I was to be PCW Champion,' he comments with a smile, almost day dreaming at the possibility of becoming champion. Kurt looks across at him. 'It'll happen soon enough,' he states to reassure his friend and boss. The Lord of Mexico smirks. 'Oh I know Kurt, I know it,' Diablo states with pride. El Diablo is obsessed with becoming PCW Champion. It's been his dream ever since first appearing in the PWA. And just look at how far he has come since then, he now runs PWA and has a Rising Star Title reign behind him as well as being an upper card worker now. Not bad for someone who was once deemed too small to ever get into PCW, and that is his driving force, to prove everyone wrong, just like he has his whole career. 'Whoa, hold on right there,' yells Leroy to a passer by. The bald man who walks by, stops and looks confused at what is going on, and just points at himself as if to ask 'Who, me?' 'Yeah, you!' Leroy shouts as he stands up from the bench they are all seated on. He walks over to the confused and looks down at him. 'Listen, this man is a future PCW Champion, and people like you aren't allowed near him. Just imagine if you had a cold and sneezed right by him, he could fall sick and be unable to compete. JUST THINK!' just as he looks down at him, he talks down to him. The man remains confused, and mutters a short response to Leroy. 'Que?' Leroy shakes his head. 'Learn to speak English! Now get out of here,' he warns the Italian. The Italian man shrugs and walks away, just as Leroy sits back down. 'What is it with people not knowing how to speak English?' Leroy asks. The Director of Dictation, El Diablo shakes his head. 'I know, I mean I speak perfect English!' he comments. Of course it wasn't an easy task for him to learn English, but still he did it and now speaks it perfectly. Perhaps too perfectly, as his knowledge for the language is so deep, that he has become paranoid of all kind of governments, whether they are for a nation or just a company, much like he has become paranoid of the structure of PCW. 'Ah, here she comes,' Kurt, says quickly. All three of them stand up from the bench and walk over towards Angela, who is now carrying two new bags of bought items. 'OK, what did you get?' asks El Diablo to his shop hungry wife. She looks into the bags and smiles cheekily. 'You'll just have to wait and see,' she replies with a look of passion in her eyes. El Diablo doesn't pick up on her passion and just shakes his head quickly. 'OK, well lets find another store here,' Diablo, states whilst looking around. Angela and El Diablo go to look around, but when they look around they realise that Kurt and Leroy aren't there, but on second glance they see them both heading into a clothing store. They hurriedly run after Kurt and Leroy. 'Hey, wait up,' shouts Angela, attempting to run in her high-heeled boots. As Kurt and Leroy turn around, they see an angered Mexican Devil in front of them. 'You're MY security, never wander away from ME like that again,' Diablo puts emphasis on himself in the sentence. Kurt and Leroy, however just shrug off what he's said, and point at what looks like a row of t-shirts. El Diablo sees them and suddenly smiles at them. 'Good work,' he congratulates them. They all walk up to the t-shirts and then it becomes clear why El Diablo was smiling, as the first t-shirt in sight is one of himself. The t-shirt is black and has a red devil dressed up in the Mexican flag with big black lettering over it saying 'El Diablo' 'Now that is what I call a great shirt,' comments Diablo egotistically. Angela kisses him on the cheek. In a fast movement, Kurt picks up another t-shirt and holds it for El Diablo to see, making his smile change to that of anger, as on the shirt is Duke Williams. 'What shall I do with this?' Kurt asks as he holds, what El Diablo regards as a vile t-shirt in his hands. Diablo though snatches the shirt off of him quickly and stares at it almost in a trance. 'Duke Williams, a man who wants the title almost as much as me. ALMOST as much as me, but not quite,' he states adamantly. The Lord of Mexico must obviously hate the sight of Duke(Star) Williams(The Goblin) right now, as he is only days away from squaring off against him once again, but it is also in his memory of his recent victory over him. 'So, I must meet this 'hoss' again. Well nevermind, I don't care if he's a cowboy or whether he's a Goth, he WILL NOT, I repeat WILL NOT stand in my way of reaching my PCW Championship goal,' Diablo snarls before throwing the shirt back at Kurt. Kurt removes it from his face and smirks as he spots a Jason Snow t-shirt, and throws it to The Mexican Devil. All of them smile at this shirt and remember back to Red October at his victory over Snow. 'Do you think you can beat him?' Leroy asks sarcastically. El Diablo laughs sadistically as he holds the t-shirt. 'What do you think?' he asks rhetorically, 'I've already beaten this loser once, and it doesn't matter if he's a former champion,' Diablo finishes. Angela smiles and takes the shirt away from him. El Diablo must have a warped memory to forget how he 'beat' Snow, as it was all due to taking the referee out and then receiving a beat down from a chair and then the guards. But El Diablo truly he beats him all by himself without any outside help, it shows just how deluded his mind can be. 'HA! Here is another one!' laughs Leroy. Diablo looks across to the shirt he is holding, and what shirt is he holding, but that of Tim Walden. Diablo simply shakes his head in disbelief. 'It's obvious who bought their way into this match huh?' asks El Diablo to his wife. Angela smiles nicely and nods in agreement with her temperamental husband. 'It's shocking and horrific to know that he can win, all because he's part of that Front Office. That CORRUPT Front Office. We'll take him out though,' Diablo seethes at the thought of Tim Walden becoming PCW Champion. 'So what now?' Kurt asks El Diablo. 'We buy the shirts,' he replies quickly as he heads for the exit of the store. Kurt and Leroy look baffled. 'WHAT FOR?' they both shout after him. 'You'll see,' Diablo responds with an evil tone. El Diablo walks out of the store arm in arm with his wife Angela, as they wait for Kurt and Leroy to buy the shirts. What does he have up his sleeve though? What purpose can buying those shirts possible have? What can it achieve? Only The Lord of Mexico knows that. Diablo turns to his wife and looks at her with caring eyes, the only time El Diablo shows a caring side is with his wife. He kisses her softly. 'Don't be shocked by what I do next, just remember we are the King and Queen of Mexico and PCW!' he whispers slowly into his wife's ear. Angela nods, 'OK,' At that moment Kurt and Leroy walk out of the store with the shirts in a plastic bag, and hand it over to El Diablo. 'So what are you going to do?' Kurt asks curiously. Diablo smiles sadistically and removes the plastic bag so that he is just holding the shirts. He then walks over to a nearby waste bin in the mall, spits on the shirts then throws them into the bin. Kurt and Leroy look a little angry as they had to buy the shirts but just watch on anyway. 'It's time I burned their title hopes, just like I am these shirts,' Diablo says in an evil tone of voice. With that being said, he pulls a pack of matches out from his jacket pocket, strikes a match and throws it into the bin, and sure enough the shirts begin to light up. 'As I watch you burn, I watch Clyde's title reign burn away until that Title is in my hands. I WILL be PCW CHAMPION,' he says with demonic passion. He continues to watch the flames encompass the shirts, but soon is getting dragged away by Kurt and Leroy as they go to escape the mall to escape any retribution from the mall staff. Angela follows quickly behind them as El Diablo remains almost in a sick trance. 'It's my time to be Champion,' he states one last time.
