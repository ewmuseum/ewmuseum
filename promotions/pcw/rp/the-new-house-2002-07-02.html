The New House

2 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7-2-02 [Sunday Night Footage] Stephens: Yes, I know baby! It’s so great! I knew I would win! Makonie didn’t have enough ring smarts to beat me. [Stephens reaches up with his left hand, the one not holding the cordless phone he’s talking to his wife on, and gently rubs his stitched up head.] Stephens: Yeah, you’re right. He did have enough smarts to beat the hell out of me with a chair didn’t he? These stitches are really annoying. The itch like hell. [Stephens chuckles very lightly to himself. He has gotten used to not laughing very hard with the fractured ribs that seem to be healing quite well. Ribs usually take a little while to heal, but the fracture was small, and he should only have to wear the pad for another two weeks or so.] Stephens: I’ll be fine. I’m going to fly out in the morning. Did you find a good house? Stephens: Really! Where? [We hear the faint sound of his wife Dana talking on the other end of the phone. It’s a tiny murmur though, and we can only infer what she says from the words Stephens speaks.] Stephens: In Austin, really? Well I guess that’s not too far away from our families, and I’m sure it’s a good area, and a nice place. Stephens: That’s awesome. I glad you like it. Stephens: Yeah, I’m sure I’ll think it’s as beautiful as you do. Besides I trust my mother and your mother picking out a nice house for us. [Again, he chuckles quietly as she jokingly reprimands him, claiming that he should trust her.] Stephens: Well I’m going to go jump in the shower and get some rest, my flight leaves pretty early in the morning. I should see you around noon or so. Stephens: Okay, Okay, Okay, Good night, I love you! ******************************************* [Monday Footage] [The shot reveals us to be standing directly in front of a massive white stone house atop a cliff/hill, whatever you want to call it, over looking the Frio river way below. The house is rather large, but not ridiculous. It doesn’t feature massive white pillars or anything like that. It’s just a large, two-story stone house with nicely cut grass, and gorgeous flowerbeds. Ryan and his wife Dana are not accompanied by their mothers this time. They are merely standing their, Dana admiring her handy work, and Ryan in awe of the house.] Stephens: You couldn’t have picked better baby, this is beautiful. Come on, I want to see the inside. [He takes her by the hand, as she smiles. It’s obvious she’s incredibly happy. As they walk up the flower surrounded sidewalk, she reaches out and removes the for sale sign from it’s upright position and tosses it down on the lawn. Ryan looks at her and smiles as they enter the house. They haven’t bought the house yet; however, Dana was almost certain that they would and thus the real estate agent didn’t come along for the trip, hoping the couple would fall in love with the house themselves. When you’re a wrestler, despite not being very famous or anything, you get those kind of benefits.] [Upon entering the house Ryan looks around. They’re stationed in one of those fancy living room areas that you just are uncomfortable in and don’t want to touch. Dana gasps. Apparently the furniture wasn’t in here earlier, but the real estate agent took it upon herself to get an interior designer to put furniture in the house, hoping the couple would buy it all. They walk through the living room, featuring very nice furniture, vases, pictures, and of course a big piano. To their left and to there right are staircases that spiral up to the second floor. Ryan looks up, the middle of the second floor is hollowed out so you can kinda see up there from that living room.] [They walk through a doorway into a den area that seems much more comfortable. A big screen TV sits against he wall to their left near the entrance to their bedroom. There’s a leather couch straight in front of them, along with a glass coffee table. Both of which are in front of a large window overlooking a pool and hot tub. To the right are two leather chairs with a stand between them. Stephens gazes throughout the room, and then a saddened look comes across his face and he pooches his lip out as he speaks.] Stephens: Baby this is all really nice and all but it pales in comparison to The Multiple Man’s secret hidden lair. I mean it doesn’t have a select your own promo machine, or fun audio toys to play with or anything. All it has is a lot of luxurious furniture, a pool, a hot tub, a tennis court. [Dana looks very saddened, and acts as if she’s going to run off and cry, but Ryan grabs her by the shirt, and again starts laughing quietly. She hits him in the arm, and tears start welling up in her eyes.] Stephens: I am just messing with you baby. This is the most gorgeous home I’ve ever seen, and I’m really, really glad you found it. I was more or less just trying to garner a little heat in my upcoming match with The Multiple Man! [He hugs her tightly, and she wipes the tears from her eyes. She probably knew he was just playing, but with such an important decision as picking out a house on her shoulders, she didn’t feel like hearing any complaints from Ryan.] Dana: You already have another match? Baby, I heard about your ribs the other night on some obscure wrestling news channel, and those stitches look gross. You should take a couple of weeks off. Besides, you proved everyone wrong by beating Makonie. Stephens: I’m fine baby, really I am. And I can’t afford to take a week or two off. I’m starting to really prove myself and move up the ladder in PCW, and if I took a break now it would really set me back. Honestly, I should really be a Turmoil tomorrow night, but I think I’m going to stay down here and get everything situated with this house, and then you can fly back up with me Thursday and watch my match against The Multiple Man. [She nods in her approval of the idea; however, she still hasn’t fully gained her composure from getting upset only a few minutes ago. He takes her little hand in his and they go explore the kitchen. Don’t expect us to keep telling you everything about this house by the way!] Dana: The Multiple Man huh? He’s not that good is he? I figure coming off a big win like the PPV win over Makonie you’d get a title shot or something. Stephens: Well actually, the winner of this match between the Multiple Man and I will be the number on contender to John Crichton’s television title! You can’t underestimate TMM though baby, he’s really very good, he just doesn’t always bring his A game to the ring. I did a little research on him on my laptop on the flight down here. He has some pretty cool merchandise on his web site we could purchase and hang up in our new house. [This time she knows he’s kidding and she gently hits him in the stomach and laughs.] Dana: You think you’re so funny baby! Stephens: Would it be funny if I said, let’s take a peek at the bedroom? Dana: What are you trying to imply? We haven’t even officially bought the house yet! Stephens: Fine, the swimming pool will suffice. Dana: I can’t believe you! Stephens: Yeah, you’re right, I didn’t bring my swimsuit either. Well at least show me the rest of the house. Dana: Okay! [She’s rather excited that he seems to like her choice. She’s also excited because she really adores their soon to be new home. He is in awe of the house, and his wife, though as always when he has a match, his opponent rests in the back of his mind.] [The Multiple Man, and Mark Makonie. He thinks of them both as his wife leads him by the hand into various rooms. An office, a guest bedroom, even the laundry room. We fade out.]
