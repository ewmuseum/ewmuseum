Winter Wonderland

17 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/17/03 Winter Wonderland It just keeps coming harder and harder without pausing for a second or taking a break. You know I commend Mother Nature, for her strength is one stronger than anything in the fair world of ours. In the corner is El Romantico’s marketing manager Chico Romero. Chico has his feet planted next to the warms fire, with about ten blankets over him and a cup a hot cocoa in his left hand. If that’s the case I hate Mother Nature. Not a single car on the road, person in the street, soul in the atmosphere. Everyone and everything is at rest. Isn’t it funny how the world seems to stop when it snows heavily? It’s almost as if God wants some peace and wants to keep our problems and daily drama inside for just one day. I don’t know what you find interesting about this, I hate staying in an uncomfortable cabin all day long with none of the pleasures I live with everyday. I on the other hand love this type of weather. It lets me remember my youth and remember how clueless I was. It reminds me of how much I’ve changed in that small amount of time. Okay, well Romantico since we’re inside, we might as well get down to business. Now right here I have a poster with some marketing ideas I’ve had. PCW is starting to sell merchandise on a national level, and since El Romantico I probably not international than anyone else, we have to make you the most popular wrestler here in Europe. Chico takes out the poster as Romantico draws close to it and looks at some of the ideas Chico had drawn up. I don’t like any of them. WHAT? That’s right, all of them just aren’t what I stand for. It would ruin the image I work hard to obtain for myself. Romantico, image isn’t everything. And neither is money, I won’t support any of those ideas. Come on Romantico work with me here. What’s wrong with an El Romantico action figure? It even has your keen sense for woman and shows you as a villain. A real villain doesn’t have a doll. It’s an action figure. Same thing. Okay and what’s wrong with the Romantico rose card game. I can see children from everywhere becoming more obsessed with this than Pokemon or Digimon. How dare you even think about comparing an artifact of my dearest memories to games about fictional characters? My rose is a part of my life, a part of my life that cannot be copied and duplicated. Okay, I can see where you’re coming from there, but this next one is just genius. How can you say no to a line of Romantico cologne. I have no certain smell. I don’t need a certain smell; I’m irresistible without any fragrance or other aroma giver. Yes, but the people don’t know that. What I have can’t be duplicated. I was given this gift and I treasure it with all my heart. I don’t want people to think they can duplicate me. I’m on a higher level then all of society. If they think they’re the same as me, then what is the good of any of this? The good of it is about three or four more mansions five times bigger than the one you have now. Like I’ve said, I have little need for personal wealth. I work to obtain power not money. Money IS power. Money is a way to obtain power. I already have that first level of power, now I’m looking for a power of greater magnitude, one that brings fear into the eyes of humans, one that leaves the name El Romantico fresh in the minds of all, and one that leaves my name in the history books under “All-time human mysteries”. Maybe you should take it a little easy Romantico, right now all you are is a wrestler for one of many wrestling industries. Yes, for now, but the future is only a step away. Well then that’s one big step. Romantico suddenly goes to the door and opens it as a pile of snow plows in. Chico follows as Romantico walks out into the middle of the street with snow up to his waist. What are you doing? I’m taking that big step. You see I am now alone. I am doing what all fear to do. I am stepping up and taking over. All your doing is standing in snow. No, that’s what it seems to be to those with simple minds. What I’m really doing is showing I’m more than the normal man. Why’s that? I have my own Winter Wonderland. Chico shakes his head as Romantico just holds his arms high in the air in appraisal.
