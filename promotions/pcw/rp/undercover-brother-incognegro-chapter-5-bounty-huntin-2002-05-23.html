Undercover Brother, incogNEGRO [Chapter 5. Bounty Huntin']

23 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 05/23/02 The scene opens up in Dallas, Texas. The Texas suns beats down on the crowded sidewalks, as the cars zoom by with everybody having a place to go, and people to see. We are near a bunch of high-rise buildings in which many people stand in. There are several former workers of Enron looking for jobs in the Dallas area after leaving their home of Houston, Texas. Jordan X Shabazz was very unfamiliar to Dallas, though he had been working there for the better part of a month. The NAACP Texas offices were located in Dallas, and that's where Jordan spent most of his time away from the ring, and away from Japan. His schedule was very hard to juggle, between wrestling for PURE, and wrestling for the PCW, plus doing office work with the NAACP. But now, Jordan sits in his office, on the 34th floor of the 50-floor building. Typing away at his computer quickly, he is writing a report for the popular magazine 'Black Enterprise', about how hard it is working at the NAACP. Jordan shares a lot of different views from the NAACP. In Jordan's mind, most white people are inferior to him. Only his friends, Duke Williams, and Jon Kano, are the only two white people who are inferior to him. The NAACP, however, wants to work hand in hand with the white man, to stop the racial profiling by police officers, they want to make sure that all man, woman, and child is treated equally by the entire world. Jordan couldn't stand that about the NAACP. In his mind, black men were stronger than white men, which is evident in the sport of boxing. But as Jordan sat typing, he began to ponder his placing in the PCW. He hadn't been there more than a month, and yet he was getting some of the respect that he craved to earn, teaming with Duke Williams and Jon Kano is something that most all wrestlers dream of, but Jordan had already accomplished it. Wrestling on PCW Pay Per View is something most all wrestlers who kill for, and Jordan had already done it. But there's one thing Jordan had yet to do in the PCW, and that's prove to everybody that he could wrestle. He could talk a lot of trash, which is evident by the way he talks to the Phantom Booker every Sunday on PTC Malice, but he has yet to win a singles match in the PCW, which is why his match coming up on Turmoil was very important. He would grapple with the artist formerly known as Punisher 2K. He would battle with the man who is looking to gain respect... But it doesn't matter... He's white. So Jordan must murder him. Jordan thought. Suddenly, the door to his office opened. His boss, Debbie Barker walked in, she was wearing a jet-black business suit, and a look of pure business on her face. She also had a tint of anger in the way she walked; it was more of a swagger. She walked over to the desk where Jordan worked, which was scattered with paperwork and contracts of some sort. Magazines also littered the table, along with several Pepsi cans, and a few empty cups of coffee. She dropped a thick booklet on Jordan's desk; it had to be at least 30 pages. Jordan looked up and looked in her eyes, then he asked sarcastically 'What do I need the phone book for, Debs?' Needless to say, she was not amused. She had sent Jordan on a top-secret mission to Norma, Oklahoma. His mission was to sneak into a KKK funded church, and steals their funds, then brings them back to Dallas, where he would be rewarded handsomely. But instead, Jordan, and his Malice Crew mate, Rush Mason, ended up getting the KKK members riled up, and ending up in a car crash. Which didn't do them a bit of good. 'Jordan, that isn't the phone book, that's a report on the amount of money we wasted on you getting to Norma. Most of those charges would have been paid off if you and your friend accomplished your mission. But, the mission was aborted, and failed,' Debbie Barker said to Jordan. 'Also, in there's an outline of your next mission... don't mess this one up, you could get fired.' Debbie slowly walked out of Jordan's office, as Jordan quickly got ready to leave. He was going to get to work on this mission as soon as possible, and he was sure that he would complete his objective this time. **********************************It was cold in Dallas now. It was nearing ten PM, and Jordan X Shabazz was out here, walking the streets of Dallas, alone. His mission this time was far more straightforward for him. Alls he had to do was walk down the street, into the ghetto of Dallas, and head to some guy named 'Devin Polk's' house. Devin was one of the lucky men the NAACP decided to pay for his legal defense... At first. The deal was that Devin would pay half of the charges that it cost the NAACP. And the deal was, that if he didn't, he was liable to arrest. So, the NAACP decided to send a professional wrestler, into the highly dangerous if untrained business of Bounty Hunting. Jordan sighed heavily. NAACP my big ol' black ass. These are the type of people that want me to work with crackas and treat them as equals. After all these years of them treating me like I was inferior. Well payback's a bitch; I think we should have white slaves... Wouldn't that be incredible? You come home from work...You're mad as hell...And there's your white slave... You just go BAM! And hit them in the face. Oh man... I love being Black. As Jordan gets closer and closer to the home of Devin Polk, right in the heart of Ghetto Dallas, he continues his thoughts. Man, the PCW is run by an of over the hill, stupid cracka named Tyler Nelson. They [Nelson and The Phantom Booker] fire all the black guys and what do we get? NOTHING! Karl Leblanc, no doubt, the greatest Extreme Champion of all times, was fired... Mercilessly. Orpheus Grant, no doubt, the greatest thing to happen to Tyler Nelson, ever, was fired... Mercilessly. If that isn't racism, then I don't know what is, those two playas should be all up in the Main Event scene... But hell... I'll leave it be, nothing I say, do, or ask for, will happen, to the PCW, I'm just another regular N*****... Jordan pulls out his gun and he opens the gate of Devin Polk's house slowly. Two other NAACP workers emerge from the darkness. This was the original plan that Debbie had set up. The two men were dressed in all black, as was Jordan, he followed orders correctly this time. As Jordan neared the door, he nodded at his fellow men, as they knocked on the door hard. KNOCK! KNOCK! KNOCK! No answer. SHABLAM~! The door was down, and Jordan and his fellow amateur bounty hunters were in. The ran in the door and immediately began scouring the house for Devin Polk. The two other Bounty hunters, Kevin Jameson, and David Culpepper, both began screaming. Culpepper was about 5'11 and 195, so not too big. But Kevin was huge, 6'1, 200. So when they ran into the house it made a loud noise. Kevin and Dave both ran up the broken and torn down stairs. The place was a mess it smelled horribly. Their was a small 12 inch television in the living room, but not much else. There was two brown colored couches which had huge stains of juice, milk, and other things there. There was a stain of spaghetti sauce on the floor that Jordan barely avoided. There was also a lot of drug paraphernalia around. Jordan tried to look for Polk, but his heart wasn't into it, it was hard arresting another Negro, real hard. Besides, he wasn't trained, what if Polk jumped out of nowhere and knocked him da fuck out? Jordan waited at the front... Not seeing a thing... Then... It jumped out. Running towards the back door went Devin Polk. Dressed only in pajama pants, and with a cigarette in his mouth. Jordan began to run as fast as he could across the slippery and dirty floor. Jordan calls to Kevin and David as they both come lumbering down the stairs. Through the backyard Jordan goes, trying to catch up to Devin. Jordan dives, and he tackles Devin, both men roll down a small hill with some untrimmed grass. Jordan goes into his pockets and he pulls out the cuffs that he was given. He slaps them on Polk, and he frowns. 'Dog, I wish I didn't have to do this, but a n***a's gotta do, what a n***a's got to do.' Jordan walks an emotionless Devin Polk back to the house, where David and Kevin will put him in the backseat of their car, and take him downtown. The NAACP doesn't get their money back, but they teach another Negro, you are either with the NAACP, or against them. No in the middle, no 'Let me catch that free defense right quick.' It's all or nothing... just like in the Malice Crew. Jon Kano, Duke Williams, Rush Mason, they team for the first time on Fusion, and they're going to wrestle like a team... It's all for one. Not one for all... Erm... Is that how it goes? -The End... For now.
