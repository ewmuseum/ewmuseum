Blues Clues/Barney!

20 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: May 20th (The scene opens as Mike Frampton sits on the set of the children's television show 'Barney and Friends'. Mike watches the show clenching a piece of paper as the purple dinosaur hugs the kids and sings a rendition of his famous song 'I love you, you love me'. Mike's cell phone rings, he answers it.) Mike Frampton: Hello? Macarther Park: Hey Mike it's Macarther Park. Mike Frampton: Macarther Park? Macarther Park the agent? Macarther Park my agent? Macarther Park: That's right. It's been a while. Mike Frampton: We haven't spoke in a year and you haven't got me work in two. Macarther Park: Well I have got news for you. Mike Frampton: What? You hired that hitman for Barney? Macarther Park: *Fake laugh* Ahahaha, no, but I do have news. Mike Frampton: Well out with it. Macarther Park: I have found you work! Mike Frampton: What a porno with Nelson? Macarther Park: Close but better! Mike Frampton: A porno with Jenna Jamieson? Macarther Park: Nope! I found you work with, get this, 'BLUES CLUES'! Mike Frampton: The Blues Clues? Macarther Park: YES! Mike Frampton: WOO HOO! Macarther Park: Should I tell them you accept. *Beep* Mike Frampton: Sorry I got a call on the other line. *Click* Mike Frampton: Hello? Pimply Faced Intern Kid: Hello is this one Mike Frampton? Mike Frampton: Indeed. Pimply Faced Intern Kid: Well we here at the PCW have revamped our roster and you, YES YOU, have been selected. Mike Frampton: Me? Pimply Faced Intern Kid: Yes you! Mike Frampton: But I just got a job with Blues Clues! Pimply Faced Intern Kid: Excuse me? Mike Frampton: I just got a job on the Blues Clues show! Pimply Faced Intern Kid: You mean the show with the adult dog ramping man? Mike Frampton: That's the one! Pimply Faced Intern Kid: So what are you saying? *Beep* Mike Frampton: One second. *Click* Mike Frampton: Hello? Macarther Park: Hey Mike, the part they have for you to play is the what's the name, oh yes, they want you to be the wet t-shirt man. Mike Frampton: What? Macarther Park: You want it? Mike Frampton: You're fired! *Click* Mike Frampton: You still there? Pimply Faced Intern Kid: Yes. What is your decision? Mike Frampton: I'll be there Thursday! Pimply Faced Intern Kid: But we start Friday. Mike Frampton: I'll be there Saturday. Pimply Faced Intern Kid: ARG! (The scene fades out as Mike turns off the phone. He walks up to Barney and kicks him in the groin before running out the door.)
