The Sage of A Champion: Interlude Part 1

4 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10-4-02 BladeD: The Sage Of A Champion“The Return Of A Hero! Friend! And Amazing Wrestler!The Interlude Part 1” Chronicles!Chronicles!Chronicles! Chapter 1: Nuclear Weapons? :: The scene opens in February of 1994. Russia hasn’t been known for high technologies of any kind, but in this year a technology so powerful, so…. Nuclear…. Is about to hit the United States harder than it has ever been hit before. No one really knows exactly what is going on. Not even the CIA or NMA as any clue to what is going on… So no information has been given out to the press. The CIA is almost in fear because all they know is that a nuclear weapon is going to hit the United States, but they don’t know what kind. They are thinking like any person would by thinking it is just going to be some kind of bomb. The only problem is that the CIA and everyone else is so far off that it isn’t even funny. :: NMA of NYC General Powers: Yes Commander? Commander Fuller: We are in a sticky situation… General Powers: What is that sir? Commander Fuller: Well…. Russia is going to be hitting us with some kind of nuke, but we have no other information other than that. General Powers: It is probably just going to be some kind of weak bomb that we can take a part while it is in the sky about to come down on us. Why are you so worried? Commander Fuller: That is just the problem. It isn’t just any ordinary nuke. In fact, there is a rumor going around that it isn’t a bomb at all. General Powers: Well, if the Russians are so stupid they can’t even put together a bomb for their nuclear weapon, then why should we worry? Commander Fuller: Because they are saying that this nuclear weapon is strong enough to take out of the whole United States, in just a shot or two. General Powers: What the :: Fuller stops him :: Commander Fuller: Yes, I know what you want to say and that is exactly what I want to say, but if this is the truth, then we are going to have to find a way to stop it. General Powers: How? If we don’t know what it is we are after, then we won’t know how to get the information or the kind of people to send in to stop the thing. Commander Fuller: BladeD, General Powers. BladeD! General Powers: But he is just barely getting his wrestling career off the ground and on top of that the kid is only 14 years of age. How in the world are you going to let someone that young do something of this sort? Command Fuller: I know it is crazy talk, but we have sent in people 18 into battle before. General Powers: But a four-year’s difference is a lot. Commander Fuller: Trust me. He is got the skills far superior from any person we have right now. It isn’t a sad thing really. It is just this is a kid in a million. So we are going to have to get him into this mission or all could be lost. Basically our lives and the United States period is going to have to be held in the hands of someone that is only 14. :: With the year being 1994, this is before the time of PCW. This is when BladeD once stepped into wrestling into Indy feds and training facilities. Basically, just anything that would let him in. He wanted to become a famous wrestler we he got older and was doing anything he could to get there. He, like anyone else his age, didn’t have a clue to what is going on and you people are thinking a 14-year-old boy… better than anyone else in the United States that is in the NMA? Why that is insane! And to tell you the truth it is, but like Commander Fuller said, this kid is a one in a million. :: :: It is a scary thought knowing everyone’s lives are about to be put in the hands of a 14 year old, but there is no other choice. He is the only person with the kind of skills to this job and the sad matter is that right now there really isn’t much information to what is going on. All we know right now is that Russia is planning to destroy the world with something so powerful that we don’t even know what it is yet and it is rumored to be something other than a bomb of any sort. That is a scary thought, because there isn’t much of anything else it could be. :: Commander Fuller: You are just going to have to listen to me on this one general. General Powers: Yes, I understand sir. It is just going to be weird. Anyway, how are we going to get information needed for what all to do and the mission to plan out? Commander Fuller: Well, we have a guy right now going down to Russia to basically spy around and get all the information he can and hopefully the information we get back will be enough to get a mission plan and have BladeD do his thing. General Powers: Why are you having this person go out and do the rest of the job and when is he coming back? Commander Fuller: We sent out Corporal Fuller… and he is due back in two days. General Powers: You sent out that weirdo? OMG. Commander Fuller: I know, I know. The only problem is that he wants to do important things so bad and that was the only thing I could do without breaking the little guy’s heart. General Powers: That is why he doesn’t need to be in the NMA. You can’t be soft hearted and be in the NMA. Commander Fuller: But you know way back when, I had to make him something and give him things to do. :: Corporal Fuller is Commander Fuller’s brother. Corporal Fuller isn’t exactly the smartest tree on the branch, but basically when he became Commander the first thing he had to do (or he wouldn’t have been able to become Commander), was give his brother an ok spot in the ranking and give him “important” jobs to do, so basically Corporal Fuller didn’t earn the spot that he has and doesn’t have the skill to earn it either, so to make everything fair for all the other guys Corporal Fuller is going to be corporal for about…. Well a really long time, but this is all one big long story that doesn’t need to be gotten into at the moment. :: General Powers: Now I see why you didn’t give him this job. Commander Fuller: Ka-Boom General Powers! Ka-Boom! General Powers: Basically, yes that is what would happen, but that is sad to here you say something like that since he is your own flesh and blood. Commander Fuller: Well maybe if he would have done better in school, then I wouldn’t have to say bad things about him and he may have actually earned his spot as Corporal instead of myself being forced to give it to him. General Powers: True, true, but we are kind of getting off subject here. Commander Fuller: Yea, I know. We need to get back to what we are talking. General Powers: What kind of information do you hope your brother will get. Command Fuller: All I want to know is what the thing is that is going to hit. I don’t care about anything else other than that. As long as I know what it is that is coming after us, then we will be set. I am hoping though, that he can find out what time frame this explosion is set for because if we can find out when Russia plans to set this thing off, then the better off we are. I would hate for us to not know because then we won’t have time for delays or anything of that sort. Everything will be go, go, and go, and hurry, hurry, hurry! General Powers: Yea that is true. BladeD will probably come along problems while he is in Russia, so no matter what we are going to need room for mistakes, delays, and all those things. Commander Fuller: I understand completely general and that is why I am hoping that my brother finds out the time this thing is set to go off, but the main thing is that he finds out what kind bomb this actually is, then we can worry about what time it is set to go off afterwards. :: The scene is cutting here and this is a good start for an interlude to an amazing series that is taking place. How long will the interludes last? Who Knows? I guess you are going to have to stay in all and wonder for a while. ::
