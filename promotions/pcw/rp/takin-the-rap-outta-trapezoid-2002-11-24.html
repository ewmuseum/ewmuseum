Takin' the rap outta Trapezoid

24 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Title: Takin' the rap outta Trapezoid Featuring: Tim Walden Date: November 24, 2002 Tim walked down the street with a rather nervous expression on his face..This was going to be a new little experience indeed. He'd been through a lot of difficult, densely packed, and often dangerous matches, but this was something he'd never been involved in before... Not a tag title match, or a singles ladder match, or even the Alamo...This would probably be just as painful as all three combined, though not in quite the same way. Tim looked upward at the signs on the buildings, but not finding what he was looking for, tipped his gaze a little lower at the number on the door.. '134 5th Street East...' He was close, but yet he still couldn't see quite where he wanted to go...'What's this next one? Oh, 138..Going the wrong way then.' It was a rather hot day in San Antonio, and he was starting to tire of walking, the sweat beads gathering on his hair being scattered across the sidewalk with a sudden U-turn, the heat only repressed in his mind by the occasional sip of his A&W Root Beer..'126....122...118....114A 5th Street. Here we are!' He looked up at the sign across the top of the wall on the wide one-story building.. Bounceland Recording Studio: Where the hippest come to hop. Well, there went THAT statement's legitimacy...Tim pulled the door open, then walked through the left-bending initial corridor and into the reception area. 'Hi, Tim Walden, A&W guy said I should come here?' 'Oh, yes, they're ready for you upstairs!' The receptionist was pretty cheerful, and who could blame her? She probably just sat at the bloody computer all day, telling people when other people were ready to talk to them and typing whatever it was that she typed here... But anyway. Tim continued walking, up the steps and through the upstairs door to the recording studio's behind-the-scenes section, identifiable by its, well, rows upon rows of knobs and dials, obviously meant to be operated by the youthful man sitting in the second chair, next to the man who put this together..'Hey Rich.' The A&W guy, a middle-aged man with a rather sizable bald spot and camera in hand, nods...'Hi Tim. This is DJ Fresh Les, and he'll mix the track. We've got the copy of the lyrics you sent us already, so we think we can work with this..Now basically, we're working on a tight schedule, since we don't want to spend much on recording time, so what we need is for you to step through the door, and when you see the red light go on, you just do the rap, and that's all we'll need from you, we can piece the video portion from the footage I'm taking with the cameras, one of them's in the room so don't sit down on anything, and some wrestling shots. Okay?' Tim nods, and starts through the door, which shuts quickly, as he pulls the lines out of his pocket, obviously having practiced this a little...The red light turns on...And I'd say we all know that this is going to go horrendously for the better part of an hour, so let's skip to the big successful take...Tim nods, a little stressed-out from the failed takes, but attempting to look as determined as before to succeed here... 'P-C-Dub in da house! Wit' da chillin' A&Dub! Yeah, uh, uh, uh...Well it's the T-I-M, Wal-D-E-N, Bringin' it again, make ya ears ringin' again,Might wanna pretend you were on my level then, Ain't gonna bend for no Trapezoid has-been,'Cause I'll take ya to the mat, right off the bat,Heavy impact, you'll tap soon as I make contact,First through fourth acts, forget your contracts,Fallin' to my attacks, you be payin' the PAIN TAX! So when the matches get tough, wrestlin' at its worst,I'm not dryin' up, not gonna die of thirst,Come out a double champ, performance of the year,Thanks to the quenchin' powers of A&Dubya Root Beer! The competition's tightenin', opposition frightenin',But y'ain't gettin' nothin' on me, fast as lightnin',Technical superiority downright excitin', fightin',With the spite and the might to put the bite in,On any opponent thinkin' he can stop himself from sinkin',Doin' the pummelin' Playaz Inc. thing, don't be blinkin',Might miss 'em stinkin' up the ring, can of A&Dub I'm drinkin',P.W.A., can't find ya a way, to win so get outta my way,Or you get blown away, by the powers of my A&Dub, 'kay?HIT, with that witch Teresa Tomas, bringin' the nasty aromas,Might wanna run home, Front Office goin' malignant like melanoma,Tim Walden, Brandi Wilson, the start of a brand new lesson,Keep ya guessin', how many ways wit' ya heads we be messin',The next tag champs to be, straight to two thousand and three,Hangin' up the boots only when we want to show ya some mercy.. (okay, so this part would be a repeat of the chorus, why bother spamming up some more lines?) Wanna know, how it goes, Front Office goin' on a roll,Alliances main event, not even other feds can stop us any mo',Alamo, Schmalamo, take victory to ma' limo,A&W best soda, dope in floats a la mode,Take the gold, when I hold, the power of cold,Refreshment is old-school, bringin' out the bold,Beating down the other pop, why you want me to stop?I'm not gonna give up until everyone else has dropped. (one more chorus go) The light goes out, and the man from A&W looks happy..'All right, cut, that's a wrap!' Tim starts to leave, a smile on his face at the job well done finally, as the scene fades to black. ---- View Tim Walden's Biography
