Speak Your Mind

4 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/4/03 The sounds of children laughing fill the air. The little feet of boys and girls run back and forth to different objects on the playground, enjoying every moment of their youth while they still have it. Little Evan plays with Christa on the see-saw laughing all the while. Over by the swings Alex tries to go higher and higher to touch the sky. 'Tessa we really need to talk.' I turned toward the one I use to be able to call mine, Tessa Green. I shifted around on the park bench as I looked out at all the kids enjoying themselves on the local playground. Beside me Tessa sat silently. I hated to bring up problems between us but things with us were at an all time worst. My being silent and her being shy about it was not helping the situation any. My pleading eyes turned toward her in hopes that somewhere deep inside of her she'll have something to say or do that calms these rocky storms between us. For the first time in quite a few days I finally heard Chris actually say something to me. Shocked I turned my attention to him immediately wondering what was bothering his mind so much that he actually took the time to speak to me for the first time in nearly two weeks. “What about?” I asked curiously wondering what had finally convinced him to talk to me and what exactly he wanted to talk about. Perhaps he would apologize for his neglect for the past few days, or perhaps this would be the thing a feared the most. Perhaps this would be the last time we talked and he would call the relationship we shared over. I had to take a deep breath. This was by no means going to be easy. I turned my gaze away from Tessa and thought about the children that we were raising together. There was Alex, the oldest among them. Ever since his father, Tessa's brother, died he seemed to want to argue with everyone he saw as an authority figure. Now we got into our arguments before but for some reason Alex has looked up to me. I really cared for him and I truly do wish to be the father figure he needs in his life. Next was Christa, the little girl of the bunch. She was so sweet and adorable. She reminded me of Tessa. If I didn't know for a fact that she was Brandon's little girl I would swear she was Tessa's child. I just can't wait for the day when I'll be able to throw her boyfriends out of the house by the seat of their pants. Finally there was the youngest of them all, Evan. Evan was different from all the rest of them. He isn't Brandon's son. Rather we adopted him. I can actually call Evan my son, which sadly I haven't been acting very much like a father too. All that will change soon… I looked back at Tessa and grabbed her hand. I tried to smile but I couldn't force one. “Chris, what’s wrong?” I questioned still not having received a word from him about why he wanted to talk to me. I could tell he was thinking about something, but about what I would never know. I held his hand inside of the palm of one of mine and looked him in the eye, but still he couldn’t look me in the eye. Maybe he was ashamed of what he did, or just afraid of breaking my heart with what he would say next. No matter the consequences that would come with his words though, I wanted to know what was running through his mind and why he called over for me. This was it, time to bite the bullet I guess. I closed my eyes and gave her hand a little squeeze for support. When I opened my eyes I was staring directly at her, my face near hers. Every bit of me wanted to kiss her lips at that moment. I had to resist though. 'Tessa…I…' this was so hard for me to say. The words just didn't want to come out. I knew what to say and I knew I needed to say it. Yet there was something inside of me not allowing me to come out and say it. That was it. I couldn’t take the suspense anymore and an overwhelming feeling of bad luck came over me. He was going to say it. I just knew it, and before I knew it I would be succumbed by a flood of tears. I wasn’t in the mood to play guessing games even if it were with Chris so I shouted out, speaking before I could think.“Just tell me it’s over already, stop trying to delay the pain. If that’s what you’re going to say then get it out now so I can stop crying before the tournament comes around.” I said, hoping that this would get a word from him seeing as though it seemed nothing else would. I was stunned. I just stared at her with a blank look on my face. I needed to tell her. All this delaying was only making it worst. I took my hand and tried to place it on her cheek but Tessa moved and my hand brushed up against nothing but air. She clearly was not happy right now. I bite my bottom lip and decided now was the time that I should do it, no more stalling. 'Tessa, I know I have been a jerk as of late. Surely you have to understand why I haven't been asking like myself though. So look I really think that you deserve to hear this. Tessa I still love you.' I couldn’t believe my ears. Did he just tell me he still loved me? He sure did, but still I couldn’t find it believable. If he had still loved me even after what had happened then why did he refuse to talk to me for a week and have such trouble saying it, but at the same time I wanted to believe him because I loved him with all my heart and I wanted to be was happy knowing that Chris was happy as well so I gave in to my emotions and smiled at his response despite the fact that inside my mind I still had doubt floating around. “But Chris, why can’t you tell me what’s wrong? You haven’t been talking to me for nearly a week, there has to be something wrong.” I stated wanting answers that I felt I rightfully deserved. 'Well I…' I started to think about it. It's true that I have been avoiding Tessa for the past few weeks. I didn't know what to say so I just opened my mouth and hoped the words would come out. 'Tessa I won't lie to you. It kills me to hear the announcers call you Tessa Green. It kills me every time I have to hear someone talking about what happened. So I know I have been complaining that the PCW won't book me to wrestle but to be honest it's because I called and asked them not to. I can't even stand being in that arena and having to listen to all that. I'm sorry and I know it's childish of me.' I felt like I was about to break down and cry. Everything was just pouring from my soul now, bearing it to the world. 'I am really sorry Tessa, I am sure it was killing you instead that I was so cold but I just wanted the pain to go away. It was hurting me so much that I couldn't be with you. I love you yet it's forbidden my morals that I touch you in a loving manner because you belong to another man.' I had to stop, I couldn't go on anymore. It was getting to be too much for me to handle at once. All my emotions were rushing to the surface now. It wasn’t until now that I knew just where he was coming from. If I had to sit back each week and listen to the one person I know be announced as the husband of someone else I would do just he same if not worse, but at the same time I wondered why he hadn’t told me earlier. Did he not trust me? I wouldn’t blame him if he didn’t but at the same time I wish he would understand that I wanted nothing to do with Gaz. “But Chris, I don’t belong to….” I cut myself before I could finish that sentence. I couldn’t lie and say straight out that I didn’t belong to Gaz, that would be lying considering that I now bore the Green name because of an accident that should have never happened, therefore bonding me in some way to Gaz considering I couldn’t get away from unless he legally consented to it, which wouldn’t happen anytime soon knowing very well about the mind games P.W.A. would play because of this. Chris sighed and turned away from me knowing why I didn’t finish. He knew just as well as I that I was stuck in a situation that I didn’t want to be in, yet there was no way out of until Spring Fling rolled around. Until then it things would never go back to the way they used to be, no matter how hard I tried to put the Green name behind me.
