David Blaine can kiss my Malice Ass!!

29 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5/29/02 (A week ago Duke Williams watched David Blaine stand on a pole for a day and jumping off it. To say the least the Malice Man was not impressed. Why would ABC waste a whole hour of TV on a guy on a pole. Why were all those people cheering this boob on?? Duke does not understand any of this because he’s stood in bar restrooms waiting for an open pisser a hell of a lot longer then this tool stood on a poll.) -Not to be outdone Duke Williams is standing in Sea Side Park in New Jersey preparing for his stunt. A crowd starts to assemble around the eleven story pole. On top of the stool is a small stool. Duke Williams dressed in African garment waits at the bottom of the pole answering the press’s questions.- Reporter1: How is this different then David Blaine’s stunt? Williams: Its far more deadly. Instead of ten stories high, I’ll be eleven. Instead of twenty-two inches long, mine will be twenty-one. Reporter2: David Blaine stood on the platform I see a stool up there? Williams: Yes that is a standard bar stool. For any of my frat fans out there who spent their college years in bars they will understand. The barstool is meant for hour-long sittings. Anything else you risk anal damage, along with sever back pains, and numbness of the testicles. Much more dangerous then just standing. Reporter3: David Blaine fasted for three days prior to the stunt. Are you going to do anything similar. Williams: Fasting is for Muslims and hippies!! Which of I’m neither. I downed me a slab of ribs and I have a mini-bar up there. I will not have any water up there but every hour on the hour I will take a shot of whiskey to complicated the situation. I could get crazy drunk and die. Reporter: David jumped into boxes what are you going to land on? Williams: American ingenuity and my sponsors some good old fashion J-E-L-L-O!! Reporter4: You’re going to dive into Jello? Williams: Orange Jello to be accurate. -Duke reaches over to a tray of Jello shots.- Williams: Drink up boys!! To safety! -The men toast the Malice Man as he prepares to go up the platform. Before he enters the elevator he address the large crowd over the PA system.- Williams: Thank you all my hairy ass hillbillies for believing this morning!! One week ago David Blaine did a half ass trick standing on a poll in New York. -The Crowd Boo’s- Williams: Tonight I’ll make that trick really special by doing it here in New Jersey and doing it as drunk as ole Harry Carey calling baseball games! David Blaine can stick that in his pipe and smoke it!! When I do a trick it’s Malice Style!! -Crowd pops- Williams: Now if my Malice Muffins would remove my garment. -Two skimpy clad local Hoosier girls come behind Duke Williams and remove his African garment revealing his official PCW Malice Style boxers. The ladies then grab a brown bathrobe and place it on him. Duke cleans off his chrome shades and adjusts his Stetson hat and waves bye to the crowds. An elevator like object then takes him up the poll as the crowd cheers on.- Reporter2: This is Rock Rivers from action ten news where were covering the Duke on a Pole. Its vertigo Malice Style!! We have a two-way radio to talk with Duke if we need to and we will be covering this event as it unfolds. Duke has just arrived at the top of the platform where he is now taking his fist shot of whiskey and sitting down. -A chat of Duke is heard from the crowd as he gives them the thumbs up.- Rivers: Lets check in with Duke. Duke how is it up there? Williams: This is as easy as Ellie~! Rivers: Now Duke its only been three minutes!! Williams: God Damn I knew its been awhile!! Rivers: Hang in there Duke. Williams: Don’t worry Hoss I’m hung!! -One hour passes as Duke sits on the stool reading the Wall Street Journal. A buzzard goes off as Duke downs another shot of booze.- Rivers: Hour one is now over!! -The crowd goes wild as they chant Duke’s name. A young lady in the crowd flashes her breasts at Duke. Duke’s voice is heard though the two-way- Williams: Get that young ladies phone number for the old Malice Man!! Rivers: One hour Duke how do you feel? Williams: My ass is starting to feel like Kannon’s after an hour of hanging out with Tyler Nelson!! Rivers: I assume that’s not pleasant? Williams: About as pleasant as your prick in a blender set on liquefy. Rivers: not a pleasant picture there. -A half hour passes when the crowd starts to mutter as Duke pulls something out of his robe pocket.- Rivers: Duke are you standing by? Williams: Hey dick head I’m on atop of a pull where else would I be? Rivers: The crowd wants to know what you have in your hand. Williams: Its my mini-Tv! I’ll be damned if I’m to miss Judge Judy. Rivers: Is that in the rules? Williams: When has the Malice Man ever followed rules?? Just ask that pud Ty Nelson he’d tell you. Rivers: David Blaine didn’t use a TV. Williams: Yea but he was in Central Park he got to watch all the whack jobs in there element. It’s like the nature channel. Now shut up here comes Judge Judy! -The radio transmission ends with the theme of judge Judy- Rivers: Its now been four and half hours and six shots of whiskey. There is some movement up there as Duke is standing up. What’s going on? -Rock Rivers hops on the Two Way.- Rivers: What’s going on Duke are you ok? Williams: I have to take a piss like a Cajun horse. Rivers: …… Williams; Tell the men in the crowd to turn away while I pee. Rivers: You want me to have them turn there backs to you? Williams: You want me to speak in basic, simple, language, like a Cory Williams promo? Yes. -Rock Rivers gets on the P.A. system.- Rivers: Gentlemen, Duke needs to urinate he has requested that all the men in the crowd turn their backs while he relives himself. -A big wave of the crowd turns around. A few minutes later a golden stream starts to rain down from the top of the pole. A chant of “piss hoss piss” starts up. Duke finishes as the camera watches his ass shake as the final drop falls out. The crowd goes wild as Duke gives them the thumbs up.- Rivers: There you have it folk the Malice Man is on empty!! -Nine hours have passed, Duke just light up a cigar as he tuns on a little radio.- Rivers: Duke how are things?? Williams: I haven’t been this tired since I rode Ellie’s pussy from Amazing Grace to Floating opportunities!! Rivers: That’s pretty tired? Williams: Damn right it is!! Do you think Domino’s delivers up here? Rivers: I don’t know? Williams: Give them a call. -Thirty three minutes later a man with a pizza rides an elevator up the pole. The crowd chant’s domino’s as Duke pays the young man. Who hands him an extra package. Then the pizza man rides the pole down.- Rivers: What was that extra package Duke? Williams: If I’m high I might as well get higher if you dig me hoss? -Duke eats down the pizza then throws the box off the platform to the cheers of the crowd.- Rivers: It’s now been thirty-five hours, sixty shots of whiskey, four pizza’s, ten urine breaks, seven joints, eleven cigars, twelve hours of primetime TV, one crap filled box of pizza. The average man would be crazed and probably would die. Not Duke Williams he is now standing up ready to dive into the JELLO. He will fall eleven stories into the Jell-O. He is fatigued from sleep depravation and he is piss drunk. He very well may die!! -All of a sudden you see Duke’s body falling from the platform into the Jell-O. The rescue crews run to check on him. He is not moving.- Rivers: Oh my God lets cut to a break!! Is he dead?? -The rescue workers cut through the Jell-O till they come across Duke Williams who is not hurt but rather sleeping like a baby. – Rivers: He appears to be ok but sound asleep. Duke can you hear me? -Rock Rivers sticks the mic in Duke’s facce.- Rivers: Duke? Williams: ZZZ ZZZ give me ten more minutes mom. Rivers: Well the Malice Man’s ok and he has shattered David Blaine’s record….Malice Style!! This is Rock Rivers for action ten news signing off. -the camera fades yellow and black-
