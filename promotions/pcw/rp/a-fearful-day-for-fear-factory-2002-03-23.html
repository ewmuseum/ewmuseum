A Fearful Day for Fear Factory

23 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/23/02 thump. thump. thump. thump. thump. thump. thump. thump. thump. thump. thump. thump. thump. CREAK! (The scene slowly fades in to show Rudolph von Hackenmann opening a door on the right side of the main room inside the shoe factory. He slowly walks across the plank floor as it creaks lightly under his feet. He walks right in front of a conveyer belt, and as he goes to step around it, CRACK!, the planks under his feet give way and he falls almost eight feet, into a damp, dark room under the floor.) Rudolph: Damn it! (A door is heard slamming open, and from somewhere, obviously startled by the noise made by Rudolph's fall, comes Mason Creeper, with a small amount of green potion on his dirty, torn up hiking shorts. Mason looks down in the hole, and Rudolph looks up at him. The first thing Rudolph notices is the potion.) Rudolph: How did you get that potion on your pants? We're you in my lab? (Mason ignored Rudolph's inquiry. Angry, Rudolph tries to jump out of the hole, his hand missing the floor by about six inches. Mason looks down at him and laughs. Rudolph becomes even angrier.) Rudolph: I'LL KILL YOU! (Mason laughs a bit more, before walking back toward Rudolph's laboratory. Rudolph tries five times to jump out of the hole, but he finally gives up. The door to the lab slams shut once again, and the faint sound of shattering glass can be heard. Rudolph screams!) Rudolph: Damn it! (Rudolph continues to grumble things under his breath that cannot be made out, when all of a sudden, he steps on something. A loud pop echoes throughout the room, and dim lights suddenly pop on all throughout the dark cellar. The camera pans around the room, showing many abandoned experiments, including a human with bionic arms lying dead on the floor. Sitting in a puddle on the damp floor is an old, torn book, with dust all over it. Rudolph walks over to it and picks it out of the water. Blowing the dust off, he reads the cover.) Rudolph: Diary... (Rudolph's thin hand turns to the first page and he begins to read the first entry.) Rudolph: My father died today... It is a sad day, not only for our family, but for his company as well. I am not ready to inherit this company... I'm only seventeen! Yada yada... What the hell is this crap? (Rudolph flips through to the last page in the diary.) Rudolph: I lost everything I worked so hard to get... Levaring Shoes is doomed now. The authorities were tipped off about my secret activities by somebody inside the company, and they have siezed my building and all my wealth. The building is going to go up for auction next week... When I find out who did this to me, I swear I will kill them! (Rudolph gets a look of confusion on his face and he quickly closes the diary. He drops it back into the puddle and looks around the room. After scanning the room, Rudolph walks over to the bionic-armed man, lying stiff on the floor. Rudolph steps on something and it rolls over to the side of the man. He picks it up and looks at it. With a short click, an illuminating light comes out of the object, and Rudolph points it at the bionic-armed man. The man's eyes are settled into the back of his head, his skin is pale, and his body is visibly cold. Rudolph sudders at the sight, and uses the flashlight to look around the room some more. The light suddenly stops on an old pair of shoes. Rudolph walks up to the shoes and looks inside them, to see them filled to the top with blood. Rudolph's expression of confusion turns to an expression of complete fear as he speaks in a soft voice.) Rudolph: Oh my god... What the hell went on in this place? This guy was crazier than I am! I wish I could get out of here! I'm starting to get a little bit freaked out! (Rudolph quickly jumps from place to place with his flashlight, looking frantically for any sign of an exit. Suddenly, the light stops, to show a bloody knife. Rudolph starts to shake a little, and he speaks even softer than before.) Rudolph: Oh my god... (His soft speaking turns to a howling scream!) Rudolph: HELP! HELP!!!!! SOMEBODY GET ME THE HELL OUT OF THIS PLACE! (Rudolph moves his flashlight around the room some more, and sees a dark spot on the wall, which is his the closest lead he has had to an exit since he fell. He keeps his flashlight aimed on the spot and starts to run. Suddenly, out of the darkness, a pale hand grabs his foot and trips him to the ground! He screams horrifically loud!) Rudolph: A ZOMBIE! ZOMBIE! OH GOD PLEASE HELP ME! (From the darkness, a figure darts up, sitting up on the floor. It is Mason. Rudolph turns and looks at him, and gets a foul grin on his face.) Rudolph: Mason! You dumbass! You scared the living hell out of me! Mason: Hi Rudolph! Rudolph: Oh, by the way, how did you get down here? (Mason laughs.) Mason: Through that door you were running toward! I found a door behind your whiteboard! (Rudolph jitters around anxiously.) Rudolph: My lab! (Rudolph grabs Mason off the ground and they both run toward the door. Rudolph climbs a set of five stairs, turns, and climbs another set of five stairs. He comes in through the door behind where his whiteboard would be to find his entire laboratory wrecked!) Mason: Maybe I should tell you now... One of your gerbils is causing some trouble in town. (Rudolph laughs.) Rudolph: How much trouble could mygerbil cause? Mason: About fifteen times as much trouble as a normal one... (Rudolph gets an angry look on his face.) Rudolph: You dumped by enlarging potion on one of my gerbils! Damn it! And look at my laboratory! It is ruined! You... You have ruined me! (The camera, still focusing on the faces of Mason Creeper and Rudolph, show them turning to the left. All of a sudden, a look of utter horror comes across the face of both of them. The camera turns 180 degrees, to show a naked dead man, with the private parts blurred out, danging two feet above the floor, pale, pinned to the wall with a bloody axe through his throat. The camera turns back to Mason and Rudolph, still with a look of complete horror on their faces. The scene slowly fades out, ending with this fearful, and rather horrible event...)
