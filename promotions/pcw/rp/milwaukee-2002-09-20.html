Milwaukee

20 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 20th September 2002 Milwaukee, Wisconsin. A junkyard. A car crusher. A man in a black suit sitting in a truck with blond spiky hair. A man in wrestling attire sitting next to him, wearing goggles. Miyagawa: You know, are you sure that you’re old enough to drive? JJ: I’ve got my licence already. Miyagawa: Is it an Indian driving licence? You know the one where the test is that you have to drive forward fifty yards and not hit any livestock? JJ grins in his finely tailored black suit. JJ: You know you weren’t so grumpy when I unchained the coffin and let you out. I could have let you been crushed. Miyagawa laughs. Miyagawa: You would have, expect I don’t have a will at the moment so you would have got nothing. JJ: Although that changes tomorrow morning, right? Miyagawa: Meh… you get the TV, ok? JJ: Great! JJ sits there grinning and then a thought visibly creeps across his face. Miyagawa: What? JJ: Can I get the BMW too? Miyagawa shakes his head in annoyance. Miyagawa: Alright, but just the Beemer and the TV, right? JJ nods happily. Miyagawa: Come on, let’s get back to the arena, I want to pick up my stuff and call Natalia to let her know I’m alright. JJ: What if Duke sees you? Miyagawa thinks for a few seconds. Miyagawa: Alright, you can go in and fetch my stuff. I’ll wait in the car. JJ: This is Duke’s car, remember? Miyagawa: Alright, I’ll wait in my car. JJ: And I get to drive? Miyagawa: You’re not insured to drive my car. JJ coughs. Miyagawa: Oh bloody hell, you’re not insured to drive my car… YET… happy now? JJ nods happily. Miyagawa: I’ve got a funny feeling just dying might have been less taxing on my soul. **he pauses for a second** No, I am not paying your taxes, go to hell. JJ: I don’t pay taxes. You’re paying my wages, so why would I be taxed in order to pay my own? That would be incredibly stupid, don’t you think? Miyagawa: Aye, nearly as stupid as taxing a workforce to pay for other parts of the workforce. JJ: Whatever. JJ shifts the car into gear and the duo pull away from the scrap yard. That’s the short version. The long version includes the fact that JJ put it into reverse first and drove it backwards into a wall, causing the rear bumper to drop off before leaving. But I thought I’d spare Duke the pain of reading that. Damn, I just did it again didn’t I? Too much explanation. Bugger. *** Time Warp *** Images of the bloke from the Crystal Maze drift into your mind… Richard something I think… the bald guy from it… oh and Susan Sarandon in underwear. *** Return to Reality *** We arrive back at the arena for Turmoil where Miyagawa is now sitting in the backseat of his family saloon trying to keep a low profile. Suddenly a rapping is heard on the window and Miyagawa sits up. Standing outside is JJ with Miya’s kit bag. Miya reaches over and unlocks the door in the front; JJ opens it and throws the bag into the back for Miya to root through before sitting in the driver’s seat. Miyagawa: Now if you damage this car, you pay the full costs, ok? JJ: Deal. Miyagawa passes the keys to him before getting the cell phone from his bag and dialling his home number. He looks over at JJ once before dialling and simply says… Miyagawa: I know I’m going to regret this.
