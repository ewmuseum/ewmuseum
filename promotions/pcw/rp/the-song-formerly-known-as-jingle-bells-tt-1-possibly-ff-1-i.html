The Song Formerly Known As Jingle Bells (TT#1, Possibly FF#1 If Booked)

Date: not entered [The last time in which Ariz Ona was seen or heard from by anyone...] “Ariz, practice is at 10 am tomorrow morning. Don’t be late,” Mex Iko shouts. “I won’t. I promise,” The Rizmaster responds. Mex shuts the door. Ariz begins to walk back towards his little red car. That ended much better than I had expected. I just wanted to apologize to them for what had happened. Thanks to Mex I maintained my position in the band, which is quite a good thing for me. Ona gets into his car and pulls out of the driveway. Now I need to head back home. Misery should be awake by now. I can tell her the good news. He drives away from the green house, heading back towards his home in Skull Valley... [The next morning at around nine thirty am...] After waking up quite early I quickly got dressed, in my usual attire of a white t-shirt, jeans, and white tennis shoes of course, and headed for Mex’s house. I did not want to be late for practice. If I had shown up late, I might have gotten kicked out of the band. That would have been very bad. I would not like that to happen to me. Los Diablos is the only band I have ever been in. Being in another band would just be weird. However, the possibilities of me being late and fired by Mex no longer matter. Ariz is seen standing in front of the house that is shared by the other three members of Los Diablos, Mex Iko, Sid Nghe, and Cal E. Fornya. The garage door is currently open and can be seen by Riz. There are a couple of guitars and basses on stands, a drum set, a few microphones, and a few amps in the garage. Nothing else is in there other than that. Nobody is in the garage. They must all be in the house or something. I’ll just head into the garage rather than knock on the door. After drumming for a minute or two they will come out to discover that I am early for practice. The Rizmaster walks into the garage and sits down on the stool that sits next to the set of drums. He picks up the drumsticks and begins to pound them on the drums with a passion and fury that has not been seen in Ariz Ona in a long time. BA BA DA BUM CHING! BUM BUM CHING BA DA DA BUM CHING! Well, that really did not get them to come out from the house. Maybe playing the drums a little more and singing a little tune will do the trick. I know just the tune. It’s similar to Jingle Bells instead that it has different words to it. There’s two verses to my version of the song. A little bit of music to get into the spirit of Christmas. Here goes... BUM BUM BUM CHING BUM BUM BUM CHING BUM BUM BUM CHING BUM BUM BUM CHING He continues to go “BUM BUM BUM CHING” on the drums as he sings in a somewhat high pitched singing voice that he has. “It’s That Time Of YearThat’s Full Of Christmas CheerFamily And FriendsAnd Old Men Who Wear DependsHA! HA! HA! The Candy And The ToysThe Little Girls And BoysAnd That Weenie OsamaIs Crying For His Momma Oh... Dingoes Smell!Dingoes Smell!Dingoes All The Way!Oh What Fun It Is To Ride A Dingo For A Day! Hey! Dingoes Smell!Dingoes Smell!Dingoes All The Way!Oh What Fun It Is To Ride A Dingo For A Day!” This is funny, isn’t it? I thought so. Don’t worry. It gets funnier in my opinion. And my opinion should be accepted by all. If it is not, expect some dingoes to eat a few babies. BUM BUM BUM CHING BUM BUM BUM CHING BUM BUM BUM CHING BUM BUM BUM CHING Once again, The Rizmaster goes “BUM BUM BUM CHING” on the drums as he sings. “It’s Christmas Time AgainIt’s Coming Around The BendA Big Tree In The DenSaddam Looks Like A HenHA! HA! HA! Action Figures And A DollGeorge, John, And PaulGoing On Ricki LakeIs One Big Mistake Oh... Ringo Yells!Ringo Yells!Ringo Yells, ‘I’m Gay!’Oh What Fun It Is To Hear Ringo Yell That He Is Gay! Hey! Ringo Yells!Ringo Yells!Ringo Yells, ‘I’m Gay!’Oh What Fun It Is To Hear Ringo Yell That He Is Gay!“ I love that second verse, mostly because I like The Beatles. It’s better than the first verse in my view. After the song is over, all that can be heard is laughter. The Rizmaster looks over to the right to see Cal E. Fornya, Mex Iko, and Sid Nghe all busting up laughing. Good. They think it’s hilarious. I was hoping that someone would. I had this fear that nobody would think that this different version was funny enough to smile. A few minutes later, the laughter begins to die down. “Alright. Now that you all have each had your laugh of the day, can we play a few songs?” Ariz asks. “Yes. I believe that we can now,” Mex replies. As the scene fades Mex, Cal, and Sid all walk over to their respective instruments, pick them up, and begin to play them.
