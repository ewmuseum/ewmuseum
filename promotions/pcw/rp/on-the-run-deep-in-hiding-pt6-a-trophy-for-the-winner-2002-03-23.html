On The Run, Deep In Hiding pt6- A Trophy For The Winner

23 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 23rd March Recently El Diablo has been in Mexico in an attempt to clear the slate of his past and so he can look forward to the future. He neither succeeded nor failed, instead he found a happy medium where he can keep ahold of memories from the past and still look forward to a future where things will be different for him. Instead of losing in PCW and failing at many obstacles, he feels confident he can win frequently and finally succeed at the obstacles he has previously failed at. Whether this will ring true is another matter, but all that matters to El Diablo is that he finally has his confidence back and can concentrate on eliminating the cause of ALL his failures- Flaymin Aymin. Diablo truly believes that Flaymin has cost him everything. When Flaymin stole his mask, he stole a part of El Diablo, and that is the precise moment that Diablo pinpoints where all of his failures began. Hence the mad Australian is to blame. The Mexican Devil is in a local mall in Sacramento, the city hosting Tuesday Turmoil where El Diablo will get yet another shot at PCW's prestigious World Heavyweight Title. The shop Diablo is located in is a furniture shop, and why El Diablo would need to be in there is in fact, truly baffling. The shop is quite large and holds host to many kinds of different furnishings, for either the business place or the home, and as El Diablo lives in Chicago it can only mean that what he is purchasing is meant for business. Now that El Diablo is in a much cooler Sacramento than Puebla, Mexico, he is wearing slightly warmer clothing in a plain white t-shirt, black denim jeans and black sneakers. His short dyed red hair is styled upwards and his goatee is perfectly precisioned. El Diablo looks exceedingly calm today and upbeat. Hopefully the fact that he is shopping has nothing to do with the fact that he is shopping- after all that would make quite the girl. Diablo smiles as he approaches a section of trophy cabinets inside the dimly lit store. As he looks around, he sees the many different types of trophy cabinets, the large kinds with a thick glass casing for the man that wins a lot, and the small kinds with thin glass casings for the man that wins little yet likes to portray himself as a winner, also there are curved cabinets, square cabinets and oddly, triangular and hexagonal cabinets. El Diablo is intrigued by the variety and is sure to inspect them all. 'Wow, how I sure could use one of these, especially following Turmoil when I walk out finally as PCW Champion,' El Diablo speaks softly to himself. Diablo walks up to a curved trophy cabinet made of mahogany wood with a nice rounded glass casing. All the trophies are on shelves placed on the dark black carpeting. The store really does have a dark setting to it. 'What a man wouldn't do to have a trophy cabinet like this,' he whispers to himself, 'Hell, it's perfect for my inevitable title win on Turmoil, the PCW Title would fit in there perfectly,' The Mexican Devil smiles, almost like he is in awe of the trophy cabinets. Not that El Diablo has anything of significance to place inside a trophy cabinet; it just seems the thought of seeing himself, as a winner has really possessed him to buy one. How naive. He wanders around the cabinets, his right hand scratching his chin and his dark brown eyes narrowing to get a real perspective of which cabinet he'd like to purchase. 'Hmm, I could have won a PCW Title already,' Diablo comments to himself whilst concentrating, 'it's all down to that damned Flaymin Aymin, oh how I hate and despise him. He ruins everything of mine, the damned thorn in my side,' Diablo's reflection in one of the cabinet glass frames is that of anger. Diablo obviously hates Flaymin Aymin with a passion, and whether it is legitimate to blame him for all his World Title failures is undecided yet, as true El Diablo's career did take a bit of a downward slide upon the loss of his mask, but Flaymin never physically did anything to ruin his chances. So maybe El Diablo is a being a bit unfair towards Flaymin Aymin, but he has the right considering Flaymin is responsible for his brother's death and the loss of love he received from the rest of his family. The fact is, by that incident Flaymin Aymin created his own worst enemy in El Diablo and The Mexican Devil is only just beginning his quest to gain retribution on the Flaymin One. Diablo kneels down to look at another cabinet. 'You Flaymin are to blame for all my Title failures, whether it be the World, Continental or Cruiserweight Title, it has always been you that has cost me,' he continues to mutter to himself with that growing channel of anger within him becoming more evident by the second, 'You will not ruin or sabotage me again Aymin, I have been reborn and you ruining me is simply a part of my past,' Diablo seethes. The Mexican Devil then smiles as he gets to his feet to look at yet another cabinet, this time the hexagonal one. 'It's going to be different from now on though, it has to be. Aymin has ruined me for far too long and it's time to break that cycle,' the insane Mexican mutters under his breath to convince himself, 'No longer will he be my curse, I will eliminate from the equation of my future and that is why I'm buying a trophy cabinet, because I know he will no longer be playing a role in my downfall. I will move towards greatness once more, I will be PCW Champion some day and that day begins on Tuesday at the finale of Turmoil,' he carries on to try and talk himself into believing his own hype. If he does start to believe his own hype, maybe it'll be a good thing for him as for so long he has been carrying the burden of his brother's death on his back, the burden of blaming every one of his failures on Flaymin Aymin, and now that he is releasing some of those burdens, his own hype could help motivate him into a victory, into him being PCW Champion and into the PCW history books. Suddenly Diablo dives down onto the floor in a panic. 'SHIT!' he yells, 'What the hell is doing here?' Diablo asks himself. Walking by just outside of the store is none other than his mortal enemy, Flaymin Aymin. Diablo peers over a trophy cabinet to look outside, and luckily for him the Flaymin One has just left his vicinity. 'Damn, why is always near me?' The Mexican Devil asks himself while in a panic. El Diablo now gets up to his feet and straightens up his appearance, only to be grabbed from behind by a large man wearing a shirt, which reads 'Security' 'HEY! WHAT'S THE MEANING OF THIS?' Diablo shouts loudly. Diablo is obviously baffled as the Security Guard starts to push him towards the store's exit. 'How you dirty, rotten, homeless Mexican got in here is beyond me, but don't you come back in here ever again,' the Security Guard states his authority very sternly. El Diablo looks shocked as he pushed out of the door back into the main mall. 'HEY, I DO HAVE MONEY YOU KNOW! FUCKING MORON!' he shouts in anger back into the main store. The Security Guard turns around swiftly after that comment. El Diablo's look of anger goes again to that of panic, 'Uh oh,' he aptly quotes as the Security Guard storms towards the door.
