Totally Tubular Research: Music…

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Recently... [[Our scene opens up in southern California, just outside a small music store named ‘Surfside Sounds’. As we pan out a little, the bright yellow sands of a nearby beach come into view and somewhere I the far distance the sound of lapping waves grows gently audible. Los Angeles is a nice place, but to Titu Xansiv it means but one thing – Totally Tubular Tommy; one of two opponents he must defeat to capture the PCW World Heavyweight Title come this Sunday evening at PCW Supremacy. As the thankful Egyptian fans found on Friday, The Pharaoh didn’t attend the petite edition of Fusion before the huge PPV extravaganza, and for one simple reason – the fans themselves. After Clyde’s obliteration of Titu’s mind and the imminent destruction of half the population of Egypt, Titu Xansiv has become self-shunned from his home country, daring not to revisit the place that fills him with such fury and anguish. He keeps away from Egypt now, but visits every so often, only however when the God command it. As the cameras zoom in on the tiny music store, footsteps upon the gravel pathway fill the air and before long three familiar faces come into view. The Pharaoh Titu Xansiv looks up at the bright sign atop the entrance and frowns thoughtfully…]] The Pharaoh: “Surfside Sounds, ay? Hmm. This sounds JUST like the sort of place Tommy would purchase his ridiculous music from. Yes, indeed. Come, let us go inside, my love…and you too Azrateph.” [[Azrateph and Titu, both decked out in their usual Egyptian garb, go to open the doors of the small shop when Isiy’s voice stops them in mid-action. Titu turns to his wife to find a frown plaguing her brow.]] The Pharaoh: “Is there something wrong, my little jewel of the Nile?” Isiy, sighing: “Titu, EVERY song you’ve come out to the ring to in the past year or so has been rock or metal. So, if Tommy enjoys that sort of music, what the HELL are YOU talking about?” [[Titu chuckles dismissively.]] The Pharaoh: “PCW chooses my theme music, Isiy; and besides, it is Tommy’s strange brother Harry who enjoys this ‘metal’ music you speak of. His name, after all IS ‘Heavy Metal’ Harry. Anyway, let us venture forth! But be wary, my friends…for, who knows what sort of horrible cretins may reside within!” [[Carefully opening the doors, Azrateph and Titu vigilantly move inside the store. The tingling of a bell tolls, and for a brief moment it stuns and shocks the overly wary Pharaoh. Shaking off the abrupt fright, Xansiv, followed closely by the Egyptian priest Azrateph, slides into the store and quickly merges into a stack of LPs.]] Azrateph, frightfully: “Milord, what has happened to the light of day?! We have been plunged into eternal darkness, have we not?!” The Pharaoh, sickened: “Worry not, my friend, this is simply the atmosphere of this strange pace. Do not fear! The light is still out there…somewhere.” [[Isiy rolls her eyes as the two Egyptians crouch next to the LP stand, breathing heavily.]] Azrateph: “It’s hard to breathe in here! A strong taste of smoke lingers, pharaoh!” [[Titu nods with concurrence.]] The Pharaoh: “Strange, indeed.” [[Isiy folds her arms, shaking her head.]] Isiy: “Would you two get up? This is bloody ridiculous!” [[She lets out an embarrassed sigh.]] Isiy: “THIS is a M-U-S-I-C store, not an alien planet. Anyway, here comes a member of the staff.” [[Still crouching, Titu listens carefully as footsteps approach. A welcoming smile crosses the gorgeous face of Isiy Xansiv from above as the steps grow closer still. Needless to say, ‘Surfside Sounds’ is a huge place. Racks upon racks of CDs and LPs line the place, and only a few lights eliminate the shop. Indeed, a strong smell of smoke and quite possibly marijuana lingers in the air and lungs unused to the toxic odours find it difficult to take in the atmosphere. Titu looks on as a voice suddenly enters the air…]] Voice: “Hey, babe, what can I like totally do for you on this most bodacious day?” [[Upon these words, Titu pounces, grabbing the store clerk by the throat and forcing him up against one of the store’s walls. Isiy takes a few shocked steps back as Azrateph stands, taking the side of his master.]] The Pharaoh: “Tommy!” [[Quickly, Isiy grabs Titu’s arm and pulls it away from the startled man.]] Isiy: “Titu! That ISN’T Tommy! It’s just a staff member! Calm DOWN!” [[Titu inspects the clearly frightened man and takes a step back.]] The Pharaoh: “But…but he sounded so much like him!” [[The worker, a long blonde-haired man, dressed in a baggy, black Led Zeppelin T-Shirt, shakes off the surprise and grins widely.]] Worker: “Wow! That was like TOTALLY awesome, man!” [[Azrateph leans close to the puzzled Pharaoh.]] Azrateph, whispering: “He’s clearly shell-shocked, milord.” The Pharaoh, intrigued: “Yes, yes.” [[The worker looks Titu and Azrateph up and down and gins, shaking his head.]] Worker: “Dude, this SO rules! Look at what you guys’re wearing! Those clothes are far out, dudes!” [[The worker takes another look at the Egyptians and eventually meets Titu’s eyes.]] Worker: Where’d you buy ‘em?” The Pharaoh, angrily: “Peasant, these are the royal clothes of a P-H-A-R-A-O-H! How DARE you ask where I bought them! Why, I should have you set to work on the pyramids in The Palace gardens for such an atrocity!” [[The worker takes a step back, his dreamy blue eyes relaxing.]] Worker: “Look, take it easy, bro. Anyway, apart from this whole mistaken identity thing, what can I do you guys for? You’re here for music, right, dudes and dudette?” [[Pharaoh goes to speak when Isiy cuts him off.]] Isiy: “Right.” Worker: “Ok, so what sort of music are you looking for?” [[Isiy looks towards Titu who grins, looking the young staff member up and down with interest.]] The Pharaoh: “Well, what sort of music do YOU listen to?” [[The clerk sparks a smile.]] Worker: “Wow! People don’t usually ask that! …But to answer your MOST radical question, dude, I like most of the music in here! In fact, people usually think we’re gonna ACTUALLY sell like all that shitty pop music, but we sell mostly Beach Boys, Pennywise, AC/DC, Led Zep, and stuff like that.” [[Titu strokes his chin.]] The Pharaoh: “Interesting. Who are these AC/DCs?” Worker: “AC/DC, man! AND LIKE ONE OF THE GREATEST BANDS EVER!” [[Taken back by the sudden outburst, Xansiv steps backward into Azrateph.]] Azrateph: “Can you not play us some of this ‘AC/DC’?” [[The clerk nods, grins and walks away into the distance. Titu turns to the priest and frowns with confusion.]] The Pharaoh: “Why the Duat would we want to do that?” Azrateph: “You need to see what Tommy is LIKE, milord. Listen to his music, wear his clothes, get into his MIND! Do this and the PCW World Title is ALREADY yours! And after you and Skylar eliminate Tommy from the Main Event on Sunday, you will be granted the chance to defeat Skylar once and for all and take the title you have longed for so much!” [[Titu smiles at the proposal.]] The Pharaoh: “I like your way of thinking, high priest!” Azrateph, smirking: “Thank you, my liege.” Isiy, interrupting: “But remember, Titu, first you have to eliminate Tommy from the equation. Then it’s you and Skylar, FINALLY one on one.” [[Titu nods, knowingly.]] The Pharaoh: “Come Monday morning, PCW will see a new Champion rise from the smouldering ashes of Supremacy! THE PHARAOH TITU XANSIV SHALL FINALLY HAVE WHAT HE DESERVES!” [[Suddenly, the store’s loud speakers crackle to life and a blaring guitar riff begins to filter the air. The clerk returns to the trio with a grin to find Titu Xansiv scowling at the ceiling, a glint of agitation luminous in his dark brown eyes.]] The Pharaoh: “THIS is AC/DC?!” [[The clerk manages a laugh over the blasting noise.]] Worker: “TOTALLY!” [[Xansiv grimaces at the sound.]] The Pharaoh: “It’s awful! …This ‘music’ is AWFUL by Horus! I make no wonder ‘Heavy Metal’ Harry IS how he is!” Worker, surprised: “Wow! You know Har?” Isiy: “KNOW him! That bastard tried to kill me on Turmoil!” The Pharaoh: “I DID save you, my love.” Isiy: “That’s beside the point! He STILL tried to savage me!” [[Azrateph coughs, catching the attentions of the three arguing people.]] Azrateph: “Milady, if you don’t mind me saying, you DID beat him up quite badly. You shocked a fair few people—” The Pharaoh: “Including me.” Azrateph: “Including him.” [[Isiy shrugs.]] Isiy: “He STILL beat me.” The Pharaoh: “That doesn’t matter. What matters is, this coming Sunday, I’m going to beat Skylar AND Tommy to become the PCW WORLD HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPION!” [[Isiy sighs.]] Isiy: “You’re always thinking about yourself, Titu.” The Pharaoh: “Bah! That’s untrue and you KNOW it! Anyway, back to the point at hand here! This music is A-W-F-U-L! I shan’t listen to it anymore! Come, my friends, we’re leaving this wretched place!” [[Isiy shakes her head as ‘Give The Dog A Bone’ by AC/DC continues to heave over the PA system marking the exit of the Egyptian trio. Pharaoh scoffs angrily at the young clerk before he finally departs from ‘Surfside Sounds’, and in the following moments the worker stands and watches with puzzlement, silence enveloping him.]] Worker, eventually: “But The Tubular One likes The Beach Boys. And they’re like TOTALLY different from AC/DC!” [[Scene fades.]]
