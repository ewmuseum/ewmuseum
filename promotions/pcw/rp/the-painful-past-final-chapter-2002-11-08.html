The Painful Past (Final Chapter)

8 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/8/02 Perfection.Stardom.World Class. At the moment the world was treating as if I had all three of these qualities. Sure it sounds great, but until you’re in my shoes you could never understand why I was on the brink of a breakdown. My life was just a blur, a big blur on your cars windshield not allowing you to see the road in front of you. And unfortunately my review mirror had the same blur not allowing me to see the past. My thought on the world had changed though, all due to the night of December 25th, 1998, the night where I was meant to die. I was coming home from the arena one day, when the world was at it’s happiest, and I was at my lowest. (Choirs singing) We're walking in the air As I walked home that night I was at a point where it seemed like everything was at rock bottom. How can I much a star of a man in the eyes of my fans, be such a nothing in the eyes of myself. I didn’t want to continue. Everyone so happy and joyous made me sick. I envied them I wanted to BE them. That’s when it happened. (Choir Singing) We're floating in the moonlit sky The night was cold and I was so far from where I was staying that I needed to heat up my body. I couldn’t go in any house or building because people were too happy there. I couldn’t stand being near that. So I saw a fire between an alley and I thought that would be the perfect place to go. (Choir Singing) The people far below are sleeping as we fly When I arrived there I saw about five other people sitting in front of it. Dressed in rags, without a care in the world. It was a mother and her two children, middle-aged man, and an elder man. As I came over, they just looked at me and didn’t say a word. (Choir Singing) I'm holding very tight The mother’s baby began to cry and they all just stared at me. It was hard to look at. To think I could have been one of those babies, if my mother didn’t abandon me like she did. Maybe it was for the better that she did. (Choir Singing) I'm riding in the midnight blue Sit down! Words I heard from the oldest man. He seemed to have nothing and I know he must have had a hard life. To think he could still offer an ounce of generosity to someone was beyond me. (Choir Singing) I'm finding I can fly so high above with you I stood there for a while and even though nobody spoke a word, I was happier. It felt like I belonged with these people more than the outside world. That’s probably unusual for a supposed “STAR” as myself. I didn’t care about their foul smell, or there depressing attitude, I didn’t care about any of that at all. Any given day that person sitting there in rags and begging for food could have been me. (Choir Singing) Far across the world I left them at peace a few moments later and bumped into a man in his 20’s with a rugged beard and silly smirk. You look a little down my friend if you have this amount on you, I’ll put a smile on your face real quick. He handed me a piece of paper, and even though the figure on it seemed a lot, I was desperate and nothing could get much worse then how I was feeling now. (Choir Singing) The villages go by like trees I gave him the amount and in exchange he passed me a pill, I didn’t know what it was but I swallowed it. He told me it would get me away from this place, and by God I’d do anything to get away. (Choir Singing) the rivers and the hills A few moments later I became comfortably numb. I stumbled to the cold snow and my vision became blurry. Something was happening to my body, but oddly enough I liked it. I liked it a lot. (Choir Singing) The forest and the streams I was in a different world, everything was still so joyous and happy, but so was I. I had feelings. I felt things I’ve never felt before, and I cried. (Choir Singing) Children gaze open mouth As the tear rolled down my left cheek I was afraid. Afraid of what it was. It sent chills down my spine and I knew this couldn’t be real. How could a pill give me these feelings? I loved it, well until it gave me the reverse feeling. (Choir Singing) Taken by surprise Soon that happiness turned to anger. Why was this happening? I thought this was supposed to make me happy. I always thought I knew what anger was, but now I really knew what it was. My body couldn’t handle this and nor could my mind. This dark cloud was following me and I wanted to get away. (Choir Singing) Nobody down below believes their eyes My mood changed again. What was going on? Now I was just confused. I tried to call out for the people in the street, but no one could hear me. It’s like I didn’t exist. I closed my eyes, and when I opened them, I was suddenly flying over the clouds. (Choir singing) We're surfing in the airWe're swimming in the frozen skyWe're drifting over icymountains floating by The breeze was rubbing against my cheeks as I looked down at they beautiful array of lights. It was so majestic. God had created a beautiful work of art, and I was never able to enjoy it. (Choir Singing) Suddenly swooping low on an ocean deepArousing of a mighty monster from its sleep I flew all night, and a smile never left my face. Right when everything was at it’s high point, I fell. I fell deep into the ocean blue. Right as a hit, reality set in. And I was back in the world I had loved, but yet hated at the same time. I woke up in a hospital bed. My chest was being pumped and my eyes finally opened. The doctors congratulated each other in joy. I realized it was all a dream, but what did this dream symbolize. It was from that point on that I believed in myself. I knew that I could make my life a better place. I left the Spanish underground league. The glory was behind me. I was ready to start over. That’s why I’m here today, in the United States. My brother told me so much of this place, and that it was a wonderland. PCW is my new haven as I try and fulfill my brother’s dreams. Now I’m just an arm reach away from the title he loved with all of his heart. On Tuesday Turmoil the new legacy of my life begins. (Choir Singing) We're walking in the airWe're floating in the midnight skyAnd everyone who sees us greets us as we fly
