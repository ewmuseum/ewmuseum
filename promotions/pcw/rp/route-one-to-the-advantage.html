Route One to the Advantage?

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: not entered The day of the ‘Ultimate Judgement’ is closer than anyone around the world could ever imagine. The date for the PCW ‘Pay-Per-View’ is so close, its going to hit some of us in the face. The ‘Pretty Cool Guys’ have been on something of a streak recently… slowly fighting their way past some of the toughest competition that had been thrown at them. The hardest battle in their rather short PCW Careers being all of the encounters with ‘The Heartbreaker’… geez, not one of them have been pretty yet. One would wonder why they haven’t decided to team up and fight for one kind of ‘right’ to shine over the company… and the rest of the world… but alas, I guess that its only far that everyone fights for what they really believe in. Speaking of “Fighting for what you believe in”… The PoP Icons believe in the ways of ‘Cheating’ to win… doing anything to get something of an advantage going into a contest… going into a draw, hell, they even think of ways how to win when the competition doesn’t involve them. Now, for these two men to even stand a chance at winning the PCW Tag Team Titles at the ‘Wrestle Vessel’ PPV, the automatic assumption is that they’d have to be rather sneaky in-order to gain an advantage… hell, they’d have to be sneaky to gain any kind of advantage. Throughout the course of the next few days, both Rich Sherwin and Ryan Moore will be attempting to get something to swing the balance of favour in their side. Now, the easiest possible way would be to simply set traps that Team A, Team B and X would fall into… however, these days, its not so good to simply chase Teams… especially when there are those who have back-up. Lets take Akiyama for example… what good would it be to set a trap for him? He’d probably break free from whatever surprise and destroy the boys… all in the mere cost of about fifteen seconds! ‘Operation #1’ as we’d like it to be known, is pretty much the most basic and “Straight to the Point” Mission within the pages of ‘Devilishly Heel Tactics For Dummies: Volume III’, a book that not only lets you play the bad-guy… but allows you to devise some really cool plans too. Lets take ‘Operation #32’ for example, pretending to be pregnant so that your husband falls back in love with you… or how about ‘Operation #100’, form a radical group that take over both in backstage and on-camera direction. Really, I say a lot for my sense of reality when I recommend this great book. Now, as we come back to the matter at hand, ‘Operation #1’ really is as simply as it sounds… you merely, go to the most powerful individual in the contest and you try to swing their vote in your favour… you know, kinda like how Ibe Black is all “Buddy-Buddy” with the colored superstars of PCW. Now, the only difference from that situation, is all down on the fact that both Sherwin and Moore have yet to act on their plan… and in this case, the plan will be serenade the referee for the match, mainly down to the fact that their referee is a female… not a very convincing one, but one none-the-less. As you begin to descend on the journey that is ‘Operation #1’, I bring you due warning that some of the images produced could be rather irrational and impossible to understand… should you fail to understand the very minor concepts of ‘deception’ and ‘trickery’, then really, I would advise you to call your local “Momma” and ask her in a polite manner, to click onto something that doesn’t feature two weirdios. The scene slowly begins to open up outside what can only be assumed as a house. The shot that we are granted with, sees our very heroic PoP duo, standing alone outside the white door of the property. They look around the immediate area for a few moments, trying to figure out what to do… what to say… and what the next step within the book is. Sherwin and Moore are dressed in their usual PoP attire, something that is best to be seen rather than described. The pairing continued to remain standing in their single position, not looking anywhere for something to happen. Sherwin moved first, he brought down his left hand, the one that wasn’t in view of the camera and he opened up a little pocket book that documented everything one needed to know about being a “Heel” of Professional Wrestling. “What the hell are you doing waltzing around with that booklet?”, Ryan asked, not sounding too worried or bothered about it, more or less worried about the commitment that his Tag Team Partner has to going through with this deal. Sherwin stood unfazed as he turned through to page 4 of the booklet, running through the steps of how to pull of such a devious task. Ryan continued to look concerned, but refreshed himself when Sherwin put the little thing back into the rear pocket of his pants. Our hero turned to face the door and knocked once, making sure that the sound made was something short… something sharp… and something that would make a person come and answer their do. “OK… I’M COMING IN A FEW MOMENTS!”, boomed a rather gruff sounding female voice from within the home. Sherwin and Moore looked at one another for a few seconds, almost frozen in shock by the sheer density that was being conveyed along with the vocals of somebody who was supposed to be a member of the female race… and I’m not talking about one of those males who just happened to get the chop just to slide onto the other side of the picket line. Rich used the few moments that he had spare before the front door was answered to say a few words to his friend. “Hey Ryan… whatever happens with this meeting, regardless of how many times she happens to kill us and use our dead-bodies for punch-bags in her masculine themed gym, I just want you to know… that you’re the greatest PoP Partner in the history of the business…”. Ryan looked back at Rich and flashed him a respective smile, just to show him that the comments were appreciated… if they were actually needed, but its only safe to assume that Sherwin is one of those people who’d like to say their last requests before any of the action had actually passed through. The friends of the ‘Pretty Cool Guys’ continued to stand tight outside this rather large house, looking around their immediate area every so often, just to prevent themselves from getting bored by staring at nothing but the white paint coating of the home’s front door. “Do you think this chick could hurry up or something? Its like they automatically assume we have all day on our hands to deal with some little dirty tricks like this. Its so out of order for people to do something like that!”, Sherwin ranted, pulling something of a little minor temper-tantrum in the process. Ryan Moore looked down to the floor, unable to believe the way that his friend was showing such anger… but alas, he did have something of a point, even if the #1 rule from the book of becoming a “Heel” was to not lose your temper without being verbally insulted or what-not. “Rich… will you just do yourself a favour and calm down for a few moments?”, he asked, sounding quite worried about the status of his friend. Sherwin could only bring himself to nod his head a few times in a soft, gentle manner. Ryan continued to look on with some shock as Rich remained looking rather annoyed and generally pissed off with the slow-motion of things. As our hero started to shake on the step, worrying about what course of action to take next, the front door to the house sprung open and an amazingly buff woman stood there, looking out upon the Tag Team members like they were “Boy Scouts” running around to sell cookies to pay for a special day trip or something. Ryan couldn’t help himself and hid behind Sherwin… a character who had lost all of his rage, all of his anger… and all of that “Fatal Aggression” that was with him something like five minutes ago. “What in Gods name do you want?!”, she blared out, sounding quite the fierce women… something that we wouldn’t usually like to see upon our TV Screens, but something that is on our screens nonetheless. She places her eyes over Ryan Moore first, who promptly responds by ducking behind Sherwin completely. Following this such movement, she turns her attention to the so-called ‘Ringleader’ of the ‘Pretty Cool Movement’, looking him in the eye with all the hate and anger that would generally flow along with having to open your front-door to a couple of loser like individuals. She couldn’t take her attention off of PCG as they stood in her doorway, and so, once again, she decided to ask the duo what they wanted for a second time, “… erm, did you people happen to hear me? OR did you just decide to conveniently turn off your ears at that second in time, just lasting long enough for me to ask these questions… and then for me to kick your ass and send you back to whatever hell-hole you crawled from?!” Sherwin was amazed by the power in her voice and gluped once before even managing to think of something to say in response. The women looked amused… amused enough to break out with an evil filled smile. Sherwin tried to contain his ‘fear’ of this massive, mammoth-like frame of a woman… and it was all beginning to fall apart, until… Sherwin visualised himself being able to survive a match with Akiyama… something that soon made this huge chick seem like a tiny little duckling. She pouted somewhat upon realizing that Sherwin had overcome his fear of her… and asked him to state his business on her land one more time, “… WHAT… IN… THE… HELL… DO… YOU… WANT?!?!” Rich looked into her eyes with a dirty glare, one that would show disgust at the way that somebody else had just acted. It had become apparent that Sherwin didn’t even register the first two times that this female asked him to state his business, something that isn’t going to be too costly until he says the wrong thing and pays for it with a few well-timed right hooks, “First off all chico… I’d just like to know if you’re the PCW Official that they call… erm, Sally… Saddle?” The female blushed a little upon the mention of her name, assuming that the tone which Sherwin had selected to speak in was nothing more than the follow through to something a lot more exciting. In Sherwins mind, he was just doing what was right for the team… going out on a limb or so to see if the unfair advantage could be obtained! Sally on the otherhand, she wasn’t looking to give anything in the idealistic nature of leverage for a team… but she was looking to see if Sherwin was willing to come through with his ‘Dirty Tone’. “Dude… psst… Dude… that referee chick has SO got a crush on you!”, Ryan informed his friend, making sure that the message couldn’t be heard by Sally herself. Sherwin’s face dropped down to the floor upon hearing the “liking” that this referee had for him. Our main hero smiley nervously back at Sally… making her feel a little more wanted than the situation required. Dressed in nothing but a pair of baggy sweat-pants and a training top, she stepped out of her home and onto her front doorstep… a move which forced Sherwin to take a little number into the opposite direction… you know, just being the guy that is usually recommended to ‘make some space’ in a situation like the one in session right now. Sally looked to Rich again with a dirty look, her eyes flashing him as if to be sending some kind of telepathic message, ”… What’s wrong hot-stuff?”, she asked, making herself seem “Ravishing” in the almost non-existent way she could. The female referee of PCW’s roster took another step forward, making herself look a little caring as Sherwin and Ryan Moore could only bring themselves to take another step back. “Woah, Woah, Woah… Easy Thing… just hold your position there, I’m not here to get all randy and what-not… it’s a breach of my ‘Squeaky Clean’ imagine and a breach of the fact that you’re not named after, or look like my PoP Princess, Christina Aguillera… however, what I have come here for is a little business!”, Sherwin spoke with authority, making himself actually seem like somebody who had swing around the locker room area that is called “Primetime Championship Wrestling.” “What do you mean by all of that?! Are you saying… that you came to my house to talk a little business… just who the hell are you two idiots anyway?!”, she demanded, not even realizing that she was talking to a team who could very well go onwards to become the PCW Tag Team Champions of the World in just over four days on the ‘Wrestle Vessel’. Rich Sherwin looked a little surprised by the lack of knowledge being shown by this giant… but Ryan Moore tapped him on the shoulder, a sure-fire sign that he was already thinking of something worthwhile. “Psst… Hey Rich, this woman doesn’t have any clue about who shes going to be officiating this weekend on PPV. Why don’t you ask her if she wouldn’t mind giving ‘The Pretty Cool Guys’ something of a small advantage going into the battle… and suggest something that only counting fast when PCG make the cover?”, he whispered, hoping that Sherwin would be able to relay some of the ideas forth to this official and hopefully get the green light on a little bit of cheating. “Erm… excuse me dear woman, we’re both very big fans of PCW’s hit PoP Tag Team… ‘The Pretty Cool Guys’… and well, I was wondering if you would be willing to do us the ultimate favor of counting fast when PCG make a pinfall attempt on any man in the ring.”, Sherwin asked, once more, sounding confident, but not entirely one hundred percent on the acceptance of such a request. “Hmm… you know what, I think you two fellas look kinda familiar… I could have sworn I’ve seen your faces on PCW Television before… but I just can’t figure out who you are..”, she said, looking confused about such an offer… but at the same time, almost killing her own brain trying to find out what the deal was with both Sherwin and Moore, however… she didn’t know that the actual identity of these guys were Sherwin and Moore. “Well, if it helps… we…”, Ryan started to say before Sherwin covered his mouth and prevented the hapless Tag Team partner from anything which may very well blow their cover. “Oh… my friends just trying to say what wonderful… jugs you have… yes, what beautiful looking jugs you have indeed.” The female referee was all but ready to give the two PoP Icons a peak at her jugs, getting ready to pull her top up… when Rich and Ryan pointed dramatically to the vases that were placed on the windowsill of her “Dining Room”. Finally realizing what the talk was about, she lowered her top back into place, looking rather cautious… but fine overall. She looked at Sherwin and Moore for another few moments before looking down to the floor again. “Hmm… that’s it, I know exactly who the hell you are know… I should have seen it a long time ago, but I know who YOU REALLY ARE… and WHY YOU’VE COME TO SEE ME..”, she yelled, looking very angry about the whole situation. Sherwin and Moore could instantly sense the hatred within her voice and they ran away without due warning. The female referee looking totally confused as Sherwin and Moore ran off into the background of the picture, finally disappearing into the darkness of the day. She looked into the camera, cocking her eyebrow up in ‘mysterious fashion’ before stating, “… Sheesh, I couldn’t believe that both Matthew Roach and Jon Steele were here from my old school… damn, and to think they ran away too…. Oh well, hopefully I’ll never have to deal with those two freakos again..” The scene slowly but surely begins to fade out into the confinement of darkness as the referee, Sally Saddle walks back into her home and slams the door behind her. From this apparent viewing of attempted trickery, the actual completion of the task was not going to be something for the most talented or smartest of people… with the decision to go ‘Route #1’ thrown out of the window… one can only come to multiple conclusions about the next route that these fellas will take in order to get that all important advantage on Pay-Per-View! For now… all I can say is, TUNE IN AGAIN… same time, same place…. Same “Voyage to Cheat to Victory!”
