Nothin' Like Bit Of Revenge

23 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 23.07.02 The scene opens in a downtown area of New York. The sun is setting and the shadows from the sky scrapers make the street level very dark. The streets are full of people, walking up and down and the roads are full of traffic. A black BMW X5 can be seen driving up the road at a slow pace, the car pulls over, but the driver does not get out. The camera moves closer and it can be seen that the driver is Duzza. He is dressed in a pair of baggy white pants, a big baggy black hoody and he has his hair set back in long cornrows. His left eye is slightly bruised, the only visible mark left from his beating at the hands of The Uprising. Duzza is sitting in his car, nodding his head to “Get Away” by Mobb Deep, when someone taps in his window and jumps into his jeep. The guy is none other than former PCW and current RUSH star D-Kay Calyx. D-Kay: “Damn, Duzz what happened to your eye?!” Duzza: “Don’t act like you don’t know what happened” D-Kay: “Ha, sorry bro, I just wanted to hear your version of the beating the Uprising gave you” Duzza: “Ahh my man D-Kay, always there to make me feel worse when I’m thinking I can’t feel any worse” D-Kay: “Hey, what are friends for?” Duzza: “Err to have your back? To hang out with? I never heard anything about pushing them to suicide!” D-Kay: “Uh huh… anyway, what you call me up for bro? I’m a busy brother” Duzza: “I need ya help, to get back on the Uprising” D-Kay: “Me? Man, why me? I’m cool with Rush” Duzza: “Oh, so you turning on me as well?” D-Kay: “Nah, I never said that man, I’m just saying I really don’t need to catch a beat down like you did” Duzza: “Man, I ain’t gon’ get you to do anything, I just goin’ somewhere and I need you to come with me, so y’know I don’t get jacked again” D-Kay: “A’ight Duzz, where we going?” Duzza: “Haha, I knew you had my back, I won’t forget this playa, anytime you need me, I’ll be there” D-Kay: “When have I ever needed you Duzz! Nah, you know I’m playing.” Duzza: “A’ight let’s go” Duzza starts up the engine and as “Heaven, I Need A Hug” by R. Kelly starts playing, Duzza pulls away. D-Kay: “So, Duzz, where we going?” Duzza: “We’re going to… see a voodoo queen” D-Kay: “Sorry…what?” Duzza: “You ‘eard” D-Kay: “Why are we going to see a voodoo queen Duzz? Seriously?” Duzza: “You seen the shit I’ve had to put up with lately? I get spat on by my homies, I get white paint poured on me man, it took me three washes to get that white paint out of my cornrows dawg. I’m sick of it all, I’ma get my own back” D-Kay: “Duzz, why don’t you get them back in the ring? You’ve got a match against them on Turmoil, no?” Duzza: “D please! That ain’t a match, it’s nothin’ but another beatin’. I’m against Shabazz and Rush and who the fuck did they give me to team with? A strong black man? A strong white man? No! They gave me a fucking nazi! A fucking nazi” D-Kay: “Hey, it’s gonna be harder on Makonie than it will be on you. It’ll be hell for him, with three blacks” Duzza: “Man, he ain’t gonna show is he! He’ll wait for me to get in the ring and he’ll run, period. Which will leave me against Rush, Shabazz and that mother fucking Thug roC will probably show his black ass up too.” D-Kay: “Duzz, you really need to like, get an ally or something” Duzza: “Nigga, I had allies and they turned on me! You know, if Makonie sticks around he’ll have been a better ally than any of them nigga’s. Fuck them nigga’s. I made them nigga’s.” D-Kay: “Ok, whatever, listen what on earth can a voodoo queen do anyway? What she gonna do? Turn them into monkeys?” Duzza: “I wish. Nah, she’ll help me out, that’s for sho” D-Kay: “Duzz, you’ve changed. Back in the day you would of driven around till you saw them in the sheet and pull a drive by on them, but nowadays you go to a voodoo queen!” Duzza: “Yeah well…” The car parks in a dark, dingy predominately Jamaican area of NYC. The sounds of reggae can be heard coming out of the houses, the colours red, yellow and green can be seen all over the dark neighborhood. Duzza looks around before getting out then grabs a bag from the back seat and motions. He notices D-Kay hasn’t moved Duzza: “Oi, you comin’?” D-Kay: “Err…nah Duzz, I’ll keep look out from here, I got cha back” Duzza: “Aight, what ever nig, be back in 20” Duzza walks into a small doorway. On the other side of the door, is an exact replica of the voodoo women’s home from the Monkey Island games. There are trinkets hanging down from the roof, bones are attached to the walls, a Jamaican flag hangs down from the roof and pictures of a large black woman with Bob Marley are on a wall. The room is very dark, Duzza stands for a moment, trying to work out what to do. It’s then that a voice is heard Voice: “ahh my old friend Malek from Harlem, it’s been a long time, sit down” Suddenly a dim light comes on and a fat black women with long dredlocks can be seen sitting down and she motions for Duzza to sit down opposite her. He does so and she begins to speak Voodoo woman: “Ahh Malek, I hear you’re a big star now” Duzza: “Well, I like to think so” Voodoo woman: “Hmmm, I see nothing but pain in your future” Duzza: “Wha wha what! Well, damn” Voodoo woman: “I’m afraid so, but enough about your future Malek, how can I help you today?” Duzza: “Well, I kinda need a favor, see these nigga’s been hasslin’ me and I was wonderin’ if you could…you know, kill them” Voodoo woman: “Malek my friend, I cannot do that I’m afraid, but I’ll tell you what I will do, for you I will put them in pain, all I need is something that belongs to them and something, like a picture of them” Duzza smiles and goes into his bag, out of it he pulls a Rush Mason action figure and a big baggy black hoody Duzza: “Look, this action figure actually captures the dumb expression on Rush’s face that he always has! Ooh and this is a hoody of his that I err…found…laying around” The voodoo lady gives Duzza a strange look before taking the items and walking away. She comes back about 20 minutes later with a little black voodoo doll, she then hands it to Duzza Voodoo woman: “Now Malek, listen to me. You can only use this voodoo once and only once. So use it well Malek, and don’t tell anyone I did this, or I could get my voodoo woman liscence revoked ok?” Duzza: “Yeah for sho, yo I owe ya. Love, ya, peace” Duzza grabs his bag and voodoo doll and leaves with a large smile on his face. He looks at the doll and thinks of a way to cause pain to Rush. Suddenly it hits him, he’ll throw it in the path of a car. Duzza runs to the road and waits for a car to come past. Now, it is pretty late, so few cars are coming past, so Duzza stands waiting, and 10 minutes later he sits down by the curb. Then, finally a car comes past, Duzza throws the doll infront of the car front wheel and the car rolls right over it. Duzza lifts out a massive “yessssssss” as the scene changes. The scene now is of a warm, living room. Rush Mason and a female friend are both sitting watching tv, when suddenly Rush let’s out a massive scream of pain and rolls over holding his back. Rush: “FUCK! Ow ow ow ow, fuck” As soon as the pain came, it disappears, leaving Rush on his stomach, clutching his back, with his female friend looking down at him. Lady: “Err… are you ok boo?” Rush: “Oh, err yeah, I don’t know what came over me” The scene then flashes back to Duzza as he gets back in the jeep with D-Kay, a wide smile on his face. D-Kay: “What you so happy about?” Duzza: “Aww you know, just caused a large amount of pain to a back stabbing Negro, same ol’ same ol’. Now let’s get out of this area” The scene fades to black as the black jeep pulls away into the darkness.
