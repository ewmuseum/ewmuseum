The Crunkit Chapter 1: Setting Off On A Crunk Journey

26 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/26/02 Voice: Once upon a time in a place far far away… The scene fades in from total black and fades in to a shot on a small green hill, in a small valley. All around the valley there are people going up and down paths. These people are dressed in country style clothes: overalls and plaid shirts and that type of thing. However, as the camera goes in, we can see that the people are…little. Not midgets, the people just happen to be little, but their body parts are perfectly in accord with the size of their bodies, they just happen to be small scale versions of people. The camera pans out along the little village, inspecting all of the little people as they go along. They live happy peaceful lives in this small valley of the Shire, this region of it is called Crunkiton. The people there are the mysterious halflings known as Crunkits… The Crunkit A few names of main players in the movie roll across the screen marquee style Rush MasonJuan BorroSyko the GoblinHessianAkiyamaCivil DisobedienceJason Core Voice: Long ago, Middle Earth was a place where people could live in peace. When Isildoor struck down Sauron, that was supposedly the end. However, now people are trying to regain things lost during the War of Power. Dragons roam the world, and one must always be careful of the Shadow Madness that taints the earth… However, none of that has anything to do with our first shot, which opens on what looks like a house party, with a crunkit DJ at the helm, playing some awesome dance music, while Crunkits get down like white people when a gun is fired. Crunkits are making out on their little crunkit couches, there’s some ale being passed around. The crunkit at the DJ booth, with one of his DJ headphones on his ear, looks like the party host. He has blue baggy jeans on, with his left pant leg rolled up, a white do rag, a blue Enyce sweater, and tiny Timberland tan boots, Crunkit sized. The shot switches to outside, where a man, not tall by human standards, but huge in this small Crunkit town, is standing outside of Crunk End, the house where the very party we saw earlier was taking place. Knocking on the door irritably, the large man turns around and can be seen by the camera. He has a long brown cape on with a matching hat, while his long gray beard can be seen. Holding his staff in hand, he continues to knock on the door, which gyrates with the energy and sound from inside. Finally, muttering with a foul tone in his voice, the large man kicks the door down. Inside, he is utterly shocked to see the scene inside. Tall Man: Rushbo Baggins!!! All of a sudden, all the music stops and the Crunkits who were dancing before scatter all over the place, terrified of the large man with the gray beard. The only one left is the Crunkit DJ, who still has a look of surprise on his face. Rushbo: Who in crunk are you? Tall Man: You know of me, young Crunkit. Rushbo: Yeah, well I’m kinda on an ale buzz, so if you could help me out with a clue or something, that’d be great. Tall Man: We’ve met before, when you were younger. You may call me Juandalf. Rushbo: Juandalf the Wizard! Wait a second, are you an ale hallucination? Juandalf: I am not. I have come to your house to invite you on a quest with myself and some companions of mine. Rushbo: To where? Juandalf: I’m heading to the far north. Where the people are whiter than snow- Rushbo: No thanks that’s okay, no white people for me. Thanks a lot bye now. Rushbo tries to push the large man towards the door, but the attempt is futile as the man is almost three times as tall and as heavy as the small crunkit. Juandalf: Now, my good crunkit, I have some words of advice: Don’t be such a puss, little man. Rushbo: That doesn’t sound like advice to me, and where are these companions of yours. As if on cue, a knock can be heard at the door. Walking over, Juandalf welcomes some people in, though Rushbo cannot see who they are yet. As they make their way over towards Rush, he sees three bodies, a bit taller than him, but still nowhere near as tall as Juandalf. Juandalf: Rushbo, these are my friends Coren Oakenshield, Trevin, and Sethin. Rushbo: You’re trying to motivate me to come with you, and you’re going to protect me with three people as small as me? I know you like that pipe, but maybe you should cut back a little Walf. Juandalf: My name is Juandalf. The three men still haven’t said anything. Coren Oakenshield is wearing warrior’s mail with leather boots while the other two dwarves, Sethin and Trevin, are dressed in smaller versions of the same outerwear. Coren: Hello, what kind of creature are you? Sethin and Trevin: Dude, who the hell are you? Rushbo: You just got into my house and you’re asking me what the hell I am? I’m a crunkit, none better. I go by the name of Rushbo Baggins and personally, I’m not a big fan of adventures so Galdalf, you can just pack up. Juandalf: My name is Juandalf. Just remember Juandalf could kick your head straight off your head. Rushbo: Err… strange the name is sinking in now. Juandalf: You haven’t even heard of the journey, how can you reject it? Rushbo: Like this: I pity the foo’ who don’t get out of Crunk End in five minutes. Trevin: Dude, Mr. T hasn’t been created yet. Rushbo: Mr. T, is he a dwarvish god? Rush’s Handler Will: Dammit stop breaking kayfabe or I’ll break all your little legs and feed you to Two Ton! Juandalf: Like I was saying, Rushbo, why don’t you allow us to settle in and we will tell you of our journey and why we need you to come along. Rushbo, just wanting to get rid of the three as quick as possible, obliges and sits them down in his small living room. Juandalf makes even Rushbo’s largest chair seem like a child seat. Now that all four men have been seated, Coren begins to tell their story. Coren: We dwarves were long the most powerful forces on Middle Earth. Since the fall of Sauron, we have been trying to regain our treasure that was lost during the War with Sauron to his Orcs. Far in the north lies the Lonely Mountain and the Dragon, Smaugian Bloodlust by name but he is simply known as Smaug to the commonfolk. There lies our secret stash of gold and riches. Rushbo: And? Coren: And we are willing to make the voyage, but we are in need of a master thief, and we thought you could take the job so- Rushbo: Oh I see, just because I’m black I’m a master thief huh? Everyone find the crunkits, they’re black so they’re good at robbing stuff. Well I will not be oppressed like this!!! Sethin: Dude, Xavier Kannon’s bitchiness hasn’t come along either yet. I haven’t even beaten him in the GTT yet. Suddenly, out of the sky comes a falling pair of scissors, which land right in between Sethin’s legs, barely missing his dwarf nuts. Hey, stop getting aroused at the thought of dwarf nuts you sickos. Attached to the scissors is a little note that says “Stick to goddamn kayfabe.” Juandalf: Rushbo, I told them that you were the thief they were looking for. Rushbo: Well gee thanks, I’d love to break the color barrier in this little group, but I’ve got some Crunkit honeys who are coming over and to be honest, I’d rather chill with them than with some old white dudes. Really, it’s a great idea, but I’m not the Crunkit you want. Juandalf: You will become rich and famous, and you can even go on Oprin Winfreyin’s show. Rushbo: A Crunkit desires the smaller things in life, but hell yes I’ll go with you on one condition. The other three all look at him waiting for the condition to be named. Rushbo: We’re not going to the South, Alabama was bad enough. Suddenly a large rock hurdles down with kayfabe carved into it as the scene fades out. Finally, it comes back as all of the men have ponies and are on the road out of the shire. Rushbo has not yet got onto his pony. Rushbo: *sigh * Good-bye Crunk End. Rushbo slides up onto the pony and the four halflings, along with Juandalf set off down the road out of the shire, towards whatever awaits them on their new journey…
