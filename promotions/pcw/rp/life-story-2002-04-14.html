Life Story

14 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 14th April 2002 The rain begins to patter onto the grimy pavement of a familiar dead street as the storm clouds begin to darken overhead. It’s like a scene out of a movie, because all of a sudden the rain begins to ease and the clouds slowly begin to drift apart as the sun shines through the gap in the clouds of the 'Big Apple' also known as New York City. The smell of fresh rain begins to rise to the occasion as the dead street now slowly, but steadily begins to fill and life continues to go on. The street seems to also be something out of a movie, long and rather narrow with bunches of huge run-down apartments lining the sidewalk with a set of stairs leading to the ground floor from somewhere near the middle of them. They seem to stretch up forever, looking like they touch the clouds from which the rain just fell, they very might be, as the clouds do drop when it rains! But as for 'rising high' that’s as far as the Ghetto will ever go to being out of it’s poverty stricken life of hardship. As people begin to exit their apartments via the stairs at the front of them, a familiar looking man appears in the double door way of a rather large looking, red bricked apartment which very well could be about the best looking building on the street. Young children begin to fill the street with the life it needs as games of baseball begin to pick up in the middle of the road and the familiar 'Thugs' or 'Gangsters' as they so called also flock back to their hang-out spots in front of their apartment buildings, guarding their turf and just sitting back smoking some form of illicit drug or drinking. Noise also begins to pick up in the street as the children’s game of baseball begins to get heavily underway as the familiar looking man leans out and stretches, his black denim jacket shining into a puddle that has formed at the foot of his set of stairs on the pavement. Life goes back to normal as the familiar looking man finishes stretching and walks down a few steps, his black boots squelching in the rain that has collected on the steps. He doesn’t seem to care too much as he wanders down to the fourth to last step and takes a seat, viewing the street around him. The 'Thugs' don’t seem to care too much as they take a quick glance over before returning to their own business and the children don’t seem to notice at all, their whole concentration is focused on the rather large game of baseball that’s brewing on the road. As the familiar looking man sits down on the stairs, his head is hung low and his blonde tinged hair falls in front of his oriental looking face as he reaches into the pocket of his jacket and removes a deck of cigarettes and a silver lighter with a dragon engraved onto it, before placing a cigarette into his mouth and flipping the lid. He strikes the flint and sparks up his cigarette as a few storey’s above him a rather tired looking black mother wearing a green bandana leans out her window to hang out a loud of washing on the rack that hovers above it. The man raises his head as he takes a draw from his cigarette revealing himself to be none other than Charlie Loc as he exhales a thick cloud of smoke into the air, which hovers like the mist of the rain that had just fell before, as it soon begins to disappear blowing off into the direction that the wind is blowing. 'YOU’RE OUTA HERE!' One of the young children playing baseball in the road yells at the top of his little lungs and is followed by a rather loud cheer from his team mates on the side as he begins to collect high five’s from them as the team’s switch over. Charlie looks up after taking another draw from his cigarette as his usual ice cold emotions stay the same, he shakes his head and hangs it low. [i]It a pity that there chances of growing up and making it somewhere limited. They happy now but wait a year or so, till they teenagers and then you see what happen to them! It a shame that when they grow up, they probably won’t be playing together, instead they probably going to fight each other because they different color or they belong to different crew! It not right, these Children not deserve it….they not know any better![/i] As Charlie finishes thinking to himself, he looks up towards the game of baseball and he notices a rather built looking black man walking in the usual homeboy style, dressed in the usual homeboy way, walking up to meet his fellow 'G’s' further up the street, he seems to be wearing the predominant of color of red. Once again Charlie shakes his head as he squints his eyes to get a closer look……. [b]Name:[/b] Jamal Nixon[b]Age:[/b] 17[b]Family Chain:[/b] African-American, Born and bred into a life of the ghetto, family earning minimal wage and mother looking after three children including himself.[b]Gang:[/b] Blood[b]Gang Involvement:[/b] 4 Years. 'Hardcore' Member, heavily involved.[b]Crimes Committed:[/b] Assault, Weapons Possession, Drug Possession, Manslaughter Strangely, instead of a look of hatred crossing over his face due to the teenagers race, he shows no emotion, except for a glint of pain in his eyes. [i]Now he able to help himself, but he choose not to! He think he all good because he do all that shit and run with a crew…but he not know that it hurting himself and it hurting his family! He not want to know what it like to get a education, he not want to know what it like earning pay, he just want to know what it like fuckin’ bitches, gettin’ into fights and gettin’ into trouble! Just because he live in the Ghetto, he think he have to be all 'hardcore'. But he not realize that he nothing….he may have the balls to pull a gun, but he ain’t shit without the rest of his boys behind him tellin’ him to pull the trigger. He only 'hardcore' when he in the group, when he out he going to be on his own and he going to know what it like to be scared! He one of the reasons these children will grow up to be involved in crime…he deserve what he get![/i] He is bought back to reality as there is a loud 'CRACK!' followed by a loud roar as one of the children playing baseball sends the ball flying and scores himself and his team a home run. The glint of pain fades from Charlie’s eyes as he shakes his head and takes yet another draw from his cigarette, this time holding it all in. The white baseball makes a splashing sounds as it lands in a puddle in front of the stairs that Charlie is sitting on. Water flings up but it doesn’t manage to hit him, it only manages to spray a Caucasian teenager walking in front, staining his blue jeans with a murky dark water. Some feet can be heard slapping onto the bitumen of the road as one of the Children, a young looking black kid with an Afro runs to pick it up. 'NIGGA WHAT?!' The 'White Boy' throws his hands up in front of his face and stares down towards the child who has now stopped in his tracks due to the 'racial slur' that the 'White Boy' had just screamed. Slowly and carefully the black kid reaches down and grabs the ball from the puddle before running off with a look of fear in his eyes and returning to his game. The noise of the street picks back up as Charlie exhales his smoke and stares down at the 'White Boy'……. [b]Name:[/b] David Sommers [b]Age:[/b] 15[b]Family Chain:[/b] White-American, Born and bred in the United States, only child and family is fairly well off. Chooses to hang around the Ghetto.[b]Gang:[/b] None[b]Gang Involvement:[/b] 'Try-Hard', Member of no gang but is constantly made fun of and try’s to be like the people who are actually in gangs.[b]Crimes Committed:[/b] Shoplifting [i]Heh heh, this guy like the definition of 'Try-Hard'! He think he good because he dress and try to talk like a black, but really he nothing at all! I not get it though…why would somebody want to be black? From what he wearing he look like he have a lot of money, but he want to be all cool so he come down to the Ghetto and flaunt his stuff! He not realize that sooner of later he going to get mugged or jumped because people not like it when you try to be like them, he White trying to be Black! He think he all 'Down With It' because he steal shit, but I bet he got busted stealing a Chuppa Chup or something…. He not even in it, but he want to be! He need to learn the lesson the hard way because nothing else going to get through to him. He probably have no friends because of this, and when he grow up he probably going to look back and see how much of a 'Wigga' he was! He the definition of 'White Boy'! He nothing….he never will be![/i] Charlie can be heard chuckling to himself as he takes another draw from his cigarette as the 'White Boy' looks away from his pants, over towards the kids in the street before walking over to their game to strut his stuff and try and act 'hardcore'. The noise of the children’s baseball game cease’s as the 'White Boy' can be heard yelling at them. No sooner do the words leave his mouth when the 'G’s' sitting on the stairs all get up and advance on him. The 'White Boy' throws his hands in front of his face as one of them swings at him, before he turns and runs away. The 'G’s' can be heard laughing and slapping each other’s hand’s as they smile and strut back to their stairs to drink and rage up again, proud of just defending their turf. As Charlie is exhaling his smoke he looks to his left at the far end of the street and something catches his eye. He notices an Asian looking teenager dressed in the full denim gear looking around steadily as he hands something over to another Asian teenager standing in front of him! Charlie squints to see what they are doing, as it’s obvious that they are dealing some sort of drugs. As both teenagers reach into their pockets one produces a wad of cash, as the other produces a small money bag of white powder as they exchange goods, nod at each other and walk in opposite directions, the 'dealer' walking towards Charlie sitting on the stairs with a smile on his Asian face. Charlie shakes his head as a look of disgust crosses over his face….. [b]Name:[/b] Quang Le[b]Age:[/b] 13[b]Family Chain:[/b] Asian-American, Born in Asia and raised in America when his family fled from a war. One of three children, currently dealing with the loss of his older brother.[b]Gang:[/b] Young And Dangerous[b]Gang Involvement:[/b] 'Courier', proves his loyalty to the gang by delivering and trading goods.[b]Crimes Committed:[/b] Drug Dealing/Trafficking, Shoplifting, Grand Theft Auto [i]I not believe this…I tell him not to do this shit for me yet he still do?! I tell him he too young, that he not know what he gettin’ himself into, but he not listen and he not care! I not going to let anyone in my crew who not old enough to know what they doing! If he hang with us he going to find himself in trouble and he going to get himself into a lot of trouble dealing drugs! I knew his brother before he get robbed and killed for drugs….but he still choose to do it?!I promise his brother I not let him grow up to be like him! I promise….but everyone need to make a living I guess, right now his living by dealing drugs, he making money for the crew and for himself! I have a talk to him, see what going on, but if it not get through I tell his parents or I just keep close eye on him and teach him what to do….this probably my and his brothers fault! I too blame for this! If he want to play like a Gangster, then I teach him how to be Gangster….he not realize he more than that until it too late![/i] By the end of his thoughts Charlie’s look of disgust changes into a look of pride as he takes another draw from his cigarette and Quang Le walks past, nodding towards Charlie. Charlie nods back with the cigarette in his mouth as Quang Le smiles before focusing back on counting the cash that he had just scored in his hand. He disappears into the apartment a few stairs down from Charlie as the game of baseball grabs Charlie’s attention again. In the middle of the road there is a little black kid standing on a plate with the bat in his hand as there is a white kid standing in the center. The bases are loaded and the team behind the batter is revved up and the fielding team is in position. Just as the young pitcher gets the gleam of his pitch in his eye there is movement by the 'G’s' sitting on the stairs as Charlie turns to see what’s going on. Standing at the corner of the street is a Latin looking man, complete with muscles, white tank top and baggy jeans, holding something underneath a black Nike jacket which is slung over his arm. He begins to laugh maniacally at the 'G’s' by the stairs as he brandishes a gang tattoo on his arm. 'PUTO TO YOU ESSAY!' He can be heard yelling at the corner as the 'G’s' can be seen removing weapons. Charlie stands up on his stairs and exhales some smoke as the children don’t seem to notice what’s going on. For once in his life, a look of fear crosses over Charlie’s face…. [b]Name:[/b] Fernandez Herando[b]Age:[/b] 21[b]Family Chain:[/b] Hispanic (Mexican), Born in Mexico and illegally immigrated into America a few years back to escape crimes he had committed. Only member of his family in the States.[b]Gang:[/b] Latin Kings[b]Gang Involvement:[/b] 'Leader', heavily involved in everything illegal. Very violent.[b]Crimes Committed:[/b] Murder One, Grand Theft Auto, Assault and Battery, Injuring an Officer, Ram Raiding, Robbery, Breaking and Entering, Rape, Drug Use and Sale [i]This guy what it all about….he have no future! He have no life, he even exiled from his own country it just that the law not caught him here in America yet! This what you turn out like when you want to be 'hardcore' when you decide to be a Gangster. He so involved in crime and shit, he never going to get out even if he tried! He never going to learn how to live a normal life because he not have one! He brought this all on himself! He not able to use the excuse that his mother treat him bad or that his brother make him like this! Everyone there own person, he [u]choose[/u] to be like this! He the reason why America is so bad today! Not us, him! I think he nothing….but I wrong because he crazy, he have issues and he would do anything to kill somebody or to pay for his drugs…anything! I not want to see anything bad happen on my street, but I guess it going to play out like that now with this him at the end of the road! I not want to see somebody die today…[/i] Charlie takes another draw from his cigarette before throwing it off the balcony of the stairs and standing up, grasping something that’s concealed in his pants. The Hispanic can be heard yelling down the street as the 'G’s' can be heard yelling back. Just as the child pitches the ball everything seems to go into slow motion! The ball begins to travel through the air and the 'G’s' begin to run at the Hispanic at the corner as he laughs before yelling 'DIE ESSAY!' and throwing away the jacket on his arm to reveal a shiny silver 9mm revolver. The 'G’s' stop in their tracks and turn to run as the Hispanic raises the gun and fires….BOOM! Once….BOOM! Twice….BOOM! Three times! Screams begin to rise into the air as the 'G’s' run down the opposite end of the street the Hispanic laughs and tucks the revolver into his pocket before running around the corner as one of the 'G’s' falls to the ground clutching his calf as blood dribbles onto the floor. It doesn’t seem like much….until the look on Charlie’s face begins to change as he grits his teeth and lets go of the thing down his pants. He looks towards the baseball game to which the children are running all over the place and the young white…girl…with the bat is lying face first on the bitumen twisted with blood coming out of her pink dress. The children begin to cry as Charlie looks over at her twisted and still corpse…. [b]Name:[/b] Kimberly Jones[b]Age:[/b] 9[b]Family Chain:[/b] White-American, Lived with a single mother who did her best to provide and put food on the table, working two jobs to send her to school. Did not know her father.[b]Gang:[/b] None[b]Gang Involvement:[/b] None[b]Crimes Committed:[/b] None, Standing in the wrong place at the wrong Time [i] She not do nothing….This not her fault….This because of people who think it cool to belong to a crew! This because of people who think they cool because they do crime! This because of people who want to be proud to admit to doing the stupid muh-fuckin’ shit like this! Society is fucked up! Everything happen at the wrong time in the wrong places! You not even able to play in front of you’re own house without having to worry about something happening to you! The world is going crazy….and so am I! I can’t take no more of this shit…..[/i] The pitcher runs over to the dead girl and falls to his knees crying as the 'G' on this sidewalk picks himself up on one of the fences and slowly begins to limp away, possibly trying to take himself to the E.R. Sirens can be heard in the distance as Thunder begins to rumble overhead of the 'Big Apple'. The storm clouds seem to have re-gathered and the sky is once again dark, all traces of sunlight have been blocked! The rain begins to pour heavily as Charlie hangs his head low and walks up the stairs of the apartment before entering the doors and disappearing from where he came. As the rain begins to fall, the one child is left crying over his fallen’ friend and the rain begins to fall over the two of them. No one seems to notice, no one seems to care, it’s just another tragedy in the city of New York! The rain continues to fall at it’s pace as the blood that was coming out of the little girl’s back is slowly being washed away from the road, flowing in a stream down to the storm drain on the side of the pavement. The blood is gone, and so is the girl…but the scar which is left behind will never heal! The thunder cracks overhead once more and the rain can be heard pattering on the pavement as the sirens can be heard coming closer in the distance as the scene fades to black…….
