The Misinterpretation Of Talk And It's Disastrous Consequences

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Friday, the 13th of Decmeber. Completely bored with nothing to do as the weather lays lashes of rain and ice from the clouds above. My eyes crystallize, the glimmer of instant death in the emerald green bracelet of my eyes. It was a strange thing that happened to me the other day. As I walked outside in the freezing Southern climate, a person who I have never seen in my life cussed a curse over my shoulder after observing my mere presence. A few more strangers gathered around after the original ‘fuck you’ was thrown. Glinting anger upon his juvenile face, the only thing I could do was stare at his red cheeks, frozen from the spontaneous draft of pure, freezing wind, which swept through. He smiled distain. I ignored. The hatred rang through the swarm of people surrounding me. Few knew the real me, but all knew my face from the idiot box that they glue their worthless bodies to three nights a week, seeing my face. Sailing through the air was a blue Styrofoam cup filled to the brim with the sweat taste of street stall coffee. With a splash it exploded all of its contents within over my extravagantly priced jacket. But do I care? Fuck off. The opaque of ignorance thrashed all over my clothes. These people claim to be fans of the company I use to work for. Barely a word has been muttered from the crevasse in my lips, yet the wafting air of frozen wind seeps out directly into the navel passages of these ignorant followers of my every move. These people hate me for running away from them when they needed me most. Insults sling in every direction, cursing seeps from the minds of even the youngest of children standing before me. ‘You’re just a coward! Afraid to stand up to that asshole Kannon!’ one yells. Maybe these people I do not know are correct. They know more about me than I? Interesting logic. Losers. With my lips pursed I just stare at these people. Each one has an emerald green eye staring at it, wondering. My eye looks at a fat person, a skinny person, an old person, and a young person. They all mean the same to me. Each one is like a vexing visual, playing to my exact game. A small child steps through the pact surrounding my six foot, two inch frame. He could be no older than nine? Eh, who cares? He wears a bright red Tyler Valentine t-shirt over his winter attire. But I’m not interested in what he wears; it is what the little kid utters to me that finally concedes my attention. ‘I use to like you.’ Just five small, insignificant words etched a hole in my brain. My mind went to work, just thinking about what the little kid said to me. Drifting skyward, I looked at the forming grey mass of cold rain and icy sleet that would rain down any minute. Gazing down at the short stature in front of me, I still expect him and the hordes of people to still be there. Cursing me with every insult their ignorant Southern brains can think of. Something drastic happened just then. This... this child, voicing a sentence to me I thought I’d never hear in my wretched life. He was a fan. No, I doubt that could be. That was the Misinterpretation Of Talk And It's Disastrous Consequences.
