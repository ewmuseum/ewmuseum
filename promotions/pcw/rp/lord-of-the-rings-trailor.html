Lord of the Rings TRAILOR!

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Today... A dark screen is shown. Then, a delightful and calm kicks in to an unknown song. The black screen is now replaced by a picture of an amulet. The amulet is gold and blue. In the background of the amulet is a long forest. The picture changes to a small video of a guy with black hair and a sort of goatee/beard. The man is wearing a black mesh shirt and black fencing pants. The man is Mark Amulet. As images of Mark begin to flash across the screen, a man speaks. “One Amulet, stolen long ago...” A picture of a king is shown. The king is wearing a crown and is dressed in sparkly, gold gown like a priest would wear on Christmas or another important event. The king looks older, in his fifties as least and is starting to grow a small goatee around his chin that is silver or white. The announcer continues. “From the King who had great power.” A picture starts at a man’s boots. The camera pans up and up and up until you can see that the figure is the face of Mark Amulet once more. Mark wears a confident and warrior-like look. “Now, a young warrior...” The picture of Mark turns black and white suddenly as the music turns to a dark age music. Lightning is shown and then a picture of Jung “Sauron” Loc is shown. Loc is shown with an evil smile on his face as if something evil has been done by him. “Must fend off dark forces.” Various more pictures of Sauron is shown. The Asian Homie is seen doing dark, evil things. One picture shows Sauron in his castle, looking at something that is unseen by the camera. Another shot shows Sauron with a staff in his hand in the air that he is holding up. “Of course he has help though.” The music changes once again to a happy refreshing music of ‘Wild Child’ by Enya. Then, really quickly, three more pictures flash through. The three faces are those of Merry, Pippin, and Sam Gamgee, Mark’s team on his journey. Wild Child continues playing as a scene capture is shown. In the scene, it shows the four friends walking up a hill. Across each’s back is a pack full of clothes. Mark’s sword is sticking out of his backpack. The four friends stop on the top of a hill and look down at the scene. The view is a city. Not a big one but one that looks friendly. Past te city in the very distance, is an outline of something big that looks like a big house, mansion or a castle. The view of the town or city is replaced by Mark looking down at the city with a great smile. His goatee shines in the smooth breeze. Mark looks like he is scared but confident and knows what his mission is. Mark’s picture flashes to black momentarily and then comes back on to another scene as the narrator speaks again. “Now, take a great journey...” The scene is the four people walking down a path, a sort of castle is in front of them. All four of them look tired but sort of relived. “With some great friends...” Now, instead of showing the four friends, it shows various other characters. First, it shows the image of Tom Bombadil, the singing man who has become quite valuable to the journey. After Tom is a scene at nighttime of all the elves that certainly help out Mark and the group. A few more people are shown in various images and the friends end with Gandalf, the best friend of all. Gandalf looks wise as he has a smile over his face. “To find the amulet...” A picture of an amulet is shown. The amulet is attached to a chain and it is being held by an unknown person. The amulet sways back and forth. “And destroy it!” All of the images so far flash once more. The scenes go from Mark Amulet, to Sauron, to all four people, to the friends, to Tom Bombadil and every other person so far. The scene caps end with the amulet, hanging on a chain, being held by someone. After the amulet stays on the screen for a couple of seconds longer, an image of Mark Amulet is shown but the image of Mark is faded into the background. As seconds pass, the image of Mark Amulet fades more and more into the picture until the amulet isn’t seen any longer and the one picture of Mark Amulet is shown where he has a confident and happy look on his face. The scene fades to black as in gold writing, in classy font, sais: “The Lord of the Amulets: Fellowship of the Amulet” The words that say ‘Fellowship of the Amulet’ as in small writing under ‘The Lord of the Amulets’. Enya’s music is replaced to an ending toon that sounds like trumpets. For about five more seconds, the writing is on the screen as the scene fades to black.
