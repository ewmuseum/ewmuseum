The Rose Nightmare

3 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2/3/03 The Rose Nightmare The scene is shaking with different flashes appearing one after another. It appears as a dizzy trance with different colors blending together and taking different shapes. A face appears, a beautiful, gorgeous, and preciously soft face. Blood splatters. NO! Words try to come from the face that is now beaten and bruised, but she falls to the floor instead, with her eyes right open, staring straight in the air. Rushing…rushing…. rushing… I’m here! The two faces crowded around the body laugh as there bodies morph into huge demon type characters. They laugh over and over until blood drips from their open jaws. I appear from the dark shadow and beats over and over on the mysterious characters that keep laughing. The woman appears once again, without a single speck of blood upon her. She looks over smiling in a large beautiful white dress. She is glowing in joy and looks as if she was an angel from God. You…you’re still here. Sprinting hard…. sweat drips…I’m going to make it. A wall falls in my path. I knock and knock on it, dying for it to open. I keep going until I bleed more and even more. I finally break it down and feel overwhelming joy, as the beautiful woman is still there, happy as ever. Mo…. mother! A take one step closer but I feel a tug at my foot. I turn nervously, feeling my heart pound quicker and quicker. I see a body, a Portuguese torn jersey, with the name Borro in the back. The face explodes and the body turns to dust as I fall back with a strong chill running down my back. More nervous and tense then I had ever been, I turn around. Help…help! Only a little while away she is. The two men are near her, grabbing her, and pulling her to the ground. GET AWAY! I sprint over as the road to her seems to get longer and longer. I go until sweat begins to drip and my shirt becomes so drenched it falls from my body. My knees become weak and I fall into this huge hole. Where…. where am I. I find myself in this dark room where a light suddenly flicker on. I zoom my eyes down to the corner where I see her in a chair, with the two men still assaulting her. I see them grab her skin and laugh in joy. Don’t touch her! I try and try to get there, but my arms are locked back. When I turn I see these scorpions wrapped around my arms. The hiss and snarl at me. I fight and fight until the grasp is finally released and the scorpions disappear. I turn and see a photograph in front of me. It’s a snapshot of a man and a loving family. They are all wrapped together, as if they were the perfect family, with the perfect father. How could you? I charge through the picture breaking it into little pieces. I find myself back on the same cold long street as before. I still see her in the distance, but now I’m able to get closer. I’m coming mother; I’m going to save you. I reach within ten feet of her and throw the other two men away, beating them over and over until they no longer move. I put so much pain into them that their eyes cry for mercy and scream a horrid tune. I let their bodies be and turn to embrace this beauty. I’m here mother I’m here. I go to give her a huge hug, but as I do, I feel absolutely nothing. I look in front of me and she is gone. Then I feel something touching my leg. I look down and see her there bleeding and nearly dead. I fall to the floor and grab her in my arms. You’re bleeding…. I… You’re going to die you need to get help. Love… We have to get out of here and save you. You… I look over and see her eyes roll to the back of her head. I hear laughs in the background with the sky raining blood. Then everything goes clear and sunny as I look at her right hand. In it I see the most beautiful white rose. I grab it from her hand and hold it close to my chest, as a tear falls from my eye. FLASH…FLASH…FLASH! AHHH! I awaken in my cool room. I hop up from my bed, breathing heavily. When will this punishment end?
