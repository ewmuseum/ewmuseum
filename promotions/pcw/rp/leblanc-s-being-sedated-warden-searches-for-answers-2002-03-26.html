LeBlanc's being sedated; Warden searches for answers

26 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/26/02 [i]Scene opens inside Warden Riley's office. He is obviously perturbed over the recent state of affairs concerning his wrestler, Karl LeBlanc, and his status in PCW. LeBlanc's current record is disturbing to The Warden, who strongly believed LeBlanc would be invincible. Right now he is speaking with his top advisor and Chief of Security at Amaranth Penitentiary. Let's join the conversation in progress.[/i} Warden: GODDAMN IT!!! This is such bullshit! What the f*ck are we doing wrong? [i]Warden throws the stack of papers in his hand, littering his office floor. He picks up the glass on his desk and takes a long swig of his Whiskey Sour.[/i] Warden: I've NEVER been so gaddamn pissed off! I've tried training this guy... I've tried offering my expertise... I've allowed him out of his f*cking' prison cell for these wrestling matches... Security Chief: Sir, you should probably calm down a bit. That vein in your temple is bulging out again. It's not good for your heart to get all worked up like this. Warden Riley: Yeah, yeah, yeah... I know. But what the hell am I supposed to do. This is so frustrating. LeBlanc could dominate this f*cking fed if he wanted to. Security Chief: That's the problem... he doesn't want to. Warden, we can't connect to him. We come from a completely different background than LeBlanc. Think about it. You grew up in California, LeBlanc practically raised himself down in Louisiana... He's black, you're white. He's young, you're older. What do you all have in common? Warden: I guess you have a point. I dunno... I just don't understand. I thought as soon as we turned him loose in that ring... I thought, he'll go apeshit! He'll f*cking destroy whoever the opponent is that night. BUT NO! It's like he's a fucking zombie! What the hell's wrong with him? Security Chief: Well, maybe we'll have to back off a bit on his dosage. Do you really think he needs those sedatives? Warden: Yes, absolutely. Security Chief: Well, you know... if we quit giving him those sedatives before each match, he sure as hell wouldn't be so lethargic in the ring. Warden: I know that! BUT we cannot allow this man out in public without those drugs. If he was at full strength he would f*ckin' murder someone! Not even our guards could restrain him. This man's a ticking time bomb... I'm tellin' ya! Security Chief: Oh, come on, Warden... let's just see what happens. I know how much you hate Jason Core. Don't sedate LeBlanc this Friday... let him rip J-Core apart, let him get that Extreme Title belt back. Who knows... that might light a fire under his ass. That might be just what he needs to get going. Warden: No... it's very tempting, but we can't afford to try that. The legal ramifications could be disastrous, hell, they could shut down our entire prison if we didn't sedate LeBlanc in public. Besides, I have a better idea... [i]Warden Riley thumbs through his Roladex. He fkips to the 'T' section, picks up the telephone and dails the number. Scene fades to black.[/i] [b]To Be Continued...[/b]
