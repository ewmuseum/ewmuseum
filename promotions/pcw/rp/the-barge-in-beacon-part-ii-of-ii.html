Date: 29.11.-02 (#8 FF) And so here they are, an hour and a half later. And after eleven shots of hard liquor, nine beers, and two cigars between them, it’s safe to say: Abner and “Cowboy Jack” (as Abner so affectionately called him now) were cocked. Filled right to the brim with the vile liquid, their primordial, pre-civilized brains were now in control: just the way Abner liked it. And why was this particular PCWer, one who’s supposedly getting back on the “right track” trying to get wasted? Simple. Because he had a match to win. And indeed. What a strategy it is: if you don’t think you can out “wrestle” your opponent, why not out brawl him? If when your straight you don’t think you can lick ‘em, than fight ‘em, drunk. This, a virtual sacred Vermont tradition, was inherited by Abner from his father. And what a trait it was. All these hours, weeks, months of training, all for nothing? Certainly not. You see, as drunk as he was, and as much as he knew it, Abner still firmly believed that he could win. And hey, if worse can to worse, he could always vomit in the “monkity-monkey-monk’s” face if need be. And yes, it was a maneuver that the two had discussed…..in detail nonetheless! And so, with his conscience out the window, and all hope of sanity gone, Abner would enter his first match……. WONDERFULLY! Yes, that’s right, wonderfully! I mean, just stop and think for a moment about this guy’s geniuses: He’s a Vermonter: Vermonter’s can hold a lot of alcohol. He’s a big guy: it takes a lot of the liquid to fully knock him out. He’s a hick: hick’s naturally fight better drunk. And he’s Abner: Abnerr…..well, damnit, he’s just Abner! And so slowly taking his time in mathematically figure out the proper “chemical formula” of the best way to defeat his opponent, alcohol would become his fuel. Now was Abner a true drunk? Maybe. But in the future, I wouldn’t expect him to be so….so….so creative. You see, it’s a one time only thing. I mean, just think of it. On his professional wrestling debut……live nonetheless, and on national television too, this man would appear inebriated! What’s better for ratings? What’s better for entertainment? Nothing! This should be great. Ah yeah…..ugh…..that’s the stuff. Tipping back an emptying another shot-glass of whiskey. “Yeeeee-haaawww!” Jack stumbles across the floor and takes Abner by the arm. “Shall we?” ‘Hot damn, Jack! Let’s get ‘at party started!” A ring of cheers erupts out of the crowd of fellow drinkers, now numbering maybe thirty in all. The two men lock their arms together as a bluegrass version of “She’ll Be Comin’ ‘Round the Mountain” blares forth from the jukebox. And round and round they go. Abner swings Jack over the top of a table, and Jack attempts to do the same. The middle-aged cowboy, maybe two-thirds the weight of Aber, barely manages to get his partner’s feet off from the ground. Landing head first into the table, Abner knocks over a six-pac of Beck’s. The owner of the tasty green alcohol gets up from his chair. Drenched from the waste down in his own purchase, you’d expect the man to be upset. But no, quite the contrary. Grabbing Abner round the waste, this big-bellied, long-bearded fellow swings him to and fro. “By Jesus, I feel a party comin’ on! Yee-haw!” A random drunken cowboy chirps in for good measure. And before tool long, the whole barroom is erupt with an array of drunken, dancing and partying customers. Why even Jasper (who’s lost his coat by now) has joined in on the fun. A shiny, neon-pink jukebox lies in one corner, and a couple of tables on the other. The barroom, pretty small, perhaps thirty-by-thirty feet can’t take much more of this. A long bar extends opposite of the door across the room’s length, save a narrow nitch for the bathroom door though. Only three lights keep the place alive, three quarters of the alcohol is gone (the only thing rivaling the customers’ wallets), and yet, somehow, these people still manage to keep the party alive. “Hey Abner!” “Hey Jack!” The two buddies, as if they’ve known each other for years, embrace. Jack, stumbling over the feet of a sleeping drunkard, slips to the floor. As he slowly slides across the floor, in the general direction of Abner, laughter due to unknown joke erupts between the two. Git up! Abner, bending over, reaches down to pull up his fallen comrade. Big mistake. All hell is about to break loose…. Not knowing his own strength, he whirls the man up, and twisting him in the air, high above his head, suddenly loses control himself. Falling to the floor, Abner releases his catch….. Uh-oh….. …..Which doesn’t seem to leave the air for quite some time. Crash! Headfirst, Jack smacks into the back rack of the bar. And the, just as in the movies, all hell broke lose….. Abner, reaching out and grabbing the first man he can get, throws out a fist and lies the poor guy out flat. Smash. But justice is served. Ugh….. The last thing Abner remembers is the texture of the cold, hard glass as smashed against the back of his head, shattering into uncountable pieces. ---------------------------------------------------------- When he finally comes to, Jasper and a young uniformed officer are standing over him. The officer, dressed in an expensive blue suit, seems to be a pretty young cop. A nice guy….. And maybe, just maybe, Abner can get himself out of this one. “Officer…..” “Quiet!” Quickly raising a hand, the officer points Abner toward the door. “But officer I….. “We already took your breathalyzer….” Shit “And so you’re free to go.” Abner blinks at the officer in sheer astonishment. “But…..but…..but……” “Go!” Abner, not wanting to stick around, is more than happy to comply with this order. A smile coming to his face, he dodges past the other cops, headed for the door. But on the way out, brushing past Jasper, he stops and asks….”Hey?” “Yeah…..” “Why don’t they arrest me…..is it…..is it cause I’m a PCW wrestler?” “No you idiot, it’s ‘cause you ain’t drunk.” “Ain’t drunk? But….but….” “Stupid fool, all you had was non-alcoholic beverages…..” Right…. “Uh……right…..of yeah.…..that’s right….” And then, pretending to read a watch that doesn’t exist, “Oh, well guess I’d better get going…..I’ve got a match to win….”
