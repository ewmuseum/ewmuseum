Through The Eyes [7] Metaphysical

22 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 22.12.02 November 24th, 2002San Antonio, Texas Boom, then Alliances hit me. 'Dammit Dante, you need a doctor.' 'Fuck the medics, they don't know how to operate.' They were dragging me along, Tracy wearing a black halter along with leather pants and Desmond carrying me with all my weight. I'd gone through three matches in the night, and each one were more serious than the other - Alliances being the top one. My forehead happened to be a crimson mask, the sight of everything was blurry, almost had me walking around in a drunken state. 'Any doctors in the house?' 'Probably not, CAN WE PLEASE GET SOME SERVICE OVER HERE?!' Tracy yells for the help, but it doesn't seem to be coming, in the Alamo Dome, just about everybody happens to be caught up with a doctor or another, waiting on service for cuts, bruises, and blood spilled throughout the ring. I could hear Desmond in my head, talking. 'Son of a bitch, did you see all that blood out there? Looked like a morgue party, or something out of a video game.' 'Funny that Kannon won though, called him an underdog throughout the entire event.' Tracy told over my head. The ache and pain from trying to even see straight was painful, coming back here probably wasn't the best idea in the world, but as Tracy and Desmond said, I needed a doctor, quick. 'Can he even stand up right?' Desmond asked as I slouched down into a built in seat, stretching past my arms and letting me lay down holding my head from the head wound. Once the doctor would install the stitches, my head would become a walking puzzle, hard to figure things out and find new sounds to dig upon. 'No way, he's out and by the blood, he'll be in here for a good couple of weeks.' 'You're joking!' Tracy began to argue. 'He has a life to get back to, not be stuck with his ass hanging out at a hospital. He can smoke weed and be like that, or surf and own his own television program, but he can't be here right now. People'll start thinking why is he even here!' Desmond sighed, 'You must be out of your mind. There's not a single way in hell that we can get Dante to a hospital, or even get him back to walking on his own two feet. Its like trying to get the Eagles back up if Donovan McNabb broke his leg and couldn't play for the rest of the season.' 'I'm alight,' I struggled to even get up on my own two feet. My head wound had stopped bleeding, but my face was still this crimson mask that I couldn't get rid off. Tracy went over and snatched some paper towels from the Medical Center of the Alamo Dome. 'Lay down.' She whispered softly as I complied, slowly resting my head against cold terrain, the bench or chair just about as hard as a chair in York's Central Park. The burn of Tracy sticking the wound with cotton strung into paper caused me to scream, but Desmond held me down by his hands, completely swallowing me whole when his body came down on me, held me there for Tracy to play Nurse. Where was I this time last year? On the streets, selling tapes out of a Toyota sedan of some freestyles I'd just leaked onto Power 106.1, some radio located in the bustling part of Long Island. Yes, they listen to music there, its not all about drinks and having a social life. Here, I was supposed to get my break, have a nice single get played on the radio and then work from there. Maybe even making my own album and pushing it for seven or eight months. I was in a career jam, starting seeing psychiatrist for conditions of how was I fit in the world? I'd just graduated from Pennsylvania State Univeristy and was on the road, basically selling tapes to school kids and hip hop lovers who were nestled deep in the suburbs of Philly. Joyce McBride was her name, an odd passage to a Bronx tale she would spin for me every single time I met her. She was your Hillary Clinton 'power trip' type of woman, with the golden hair and business suit to prove it. A seesion with her was clearing everything I had on the plate - nothing basically. 'So, you're still fetching for this music gig?' she asked me, mid-December in New York while everybody else was off getting presents and gifts for their children. 'I mean, its a cat and dog like world isn't it?' 'My father was in the music business, I know the ropes. Plus, its nice to have a little side cash when your single hits airwaves.' She heartwarmingly smiled, 'Nice to know you've got a job.' 'Nice to know you're not up in my ass with questions.' She straightened up her look, her appearence for me by crossing her legs. 'And your marital status, is that good?' I smirked, 'Girlfriend. You?' 'Cheating husband with two kids.' I nodded, 'Life must suck.' She shook her head, because for her she felt liberated that her husband had done this, left her to be with a sillicone starlet. Why do I feel this, that sudden notion that I might care for her, her problems when she's analyzing mine for a paycheck. Its an odd flaw when you come to a place like this, you really don't appreciate things until people tell you about them, their lives. Their hopes, and dreams. Their futures. A tapping on my shoulder takes me away, brings me back to the realization or the point of thinking and knowing as Tracy hovers over my head. Looking around, I find myself stuck here in a hospital bed, stiches formed in my head and pain aching throughout my entire body. 'Morning, sleepy head.' 'Morning.' That's when I decided on the thought change, somehow I could remember just when I gave up on the party like a rock star and then kick a little ass style of thought. You just kick ass first, talk shit and then become the rock star. Just like my father.
