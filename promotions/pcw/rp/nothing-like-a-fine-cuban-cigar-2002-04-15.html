Nothing like a fine Cuban cigar

15 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/15/2002 ~A new era has begun in Kano's life. Jon Kano is no longer a swinging bachelor, he is now a happily married man (funny, I always thought that was an oxy-moron). He has always done his best to protect his family from the backlash of his career in professional wrestling. He refuses to allow his wife to be exposed to the life of professional wrestling. This Tuesday, it is one more time. One more time Jon Kano will walk down the aisle to a thunderous ovation from the crowd. One more time Tyler Nelson will walk down the aisle to a deafening chorus of boos from the crowd. These two men will enter the ring, and they will walk to the center of the ring. They will look into each other's eyes and they will speak their sh*t to one another. Then suddenly, one will strike the other, and the next chapter will begin to be written in the book of, 'Kano versus Nelson.' So the question posed now is what will be solved by this next battle? Well, Kano doesn't have to prove a damn thing against Nelson, because in their two previous two meetings in the ring, Kano has handed Nelson's ass to him. So can Nelson finally beat Kano or will Kano go 3-0 against Nelson?~ *The scene opens up inside of Jon Kano's private office on the top floor of Kano Towers, located in the beautiful city of Las Vegas, Nevada. As the camera zooms in, you can see PCW's very own one-man papparrazi, Cal Seaver, seated across from PCW's very own legend, Jon Kano, whom is sitting in his leather executive-style chair, smoking a fine Cuban cigar and has a fine brandy in a glass on his desk. Cal is dressed in his K-mart discontinued suit and Kano is dressed in head to toe golden Armani suit.* Kano: Jesus, it has been a long time since I have been in my office. My father sure kept it up to par, though. *Kano deeply inhales his cigar and blows out smoke rings.* Cal: Jon, how is married life so far? Kano: You know, people say that it is so much different when you marry than before you were married. It was like we were married for about the last 3 years. We lived together, we ate together, we slept together, it isn't a big change. Cal: What made you decide to marry her? Kano: Well, I had been thinking about it for a long time. I just didn't have the time, or ambition. Then when Tyler was ousted, I decided that I had all the time I needed to do this. She had made it very clear that she wanted to get married. Cal: How did you propose? Kano: I didn't. Cal: What? Kano: I made sure I kept her up all night one night, so she would sleep late into the day. Then I awoke her about 2 and told her to get in the shower and that we were going downtown and out all day and night long. So she got all ready, and I took her out to our backyard and everyone was out there waiting for the wedding. She looked at me and I smiled at her and told her it was time for her to have what she always wanted. It was a very nice ceremony. So now I got a wife and gold ring around my finger. Cal: Any plans for children? Kano: Right now, I don't want a child, but in a few years, maybe I will want a kid or two. *Kano again puffs on his cigar and now takes a sip of his fine brandy.* Cal: You know, Jon, usually a wrestler gets married when they are about to retire. Kano: Hold up. I am not retiring. I will not retire until I cannot physically still wrestle at my best night in and night out. Plus there are many things that I want to do in this industry. Cal: Like what? Kano: I want to end the corruption and the raping of tradition that exists here in PCW. Tyler Nelson was gone, everything was great, and now that son of a bitch is back. And I get to go one more time with that bastard on Tuesday. I love wrestling him. I get to beat him from post to post, from corner to corner, down the aisle, into the stands, through a table, and oh what the hell, Kanoplex him to the center of the ring, and pin him one more time. I am that thorn in Nelson's side. He can have me wrestle jobber after jobber. He can have me be a curtain jerker, but whenever he decides to wrestle me, he can't beat me. He tries his damnedest to beat me, but I counter and next thing he knows he is being pinned by the REAL LEGEND. Nelson can't stand the fact that no matter what he does, he will never get rid of me. If I go out today, the next day, a week from now, or twenty years from now, it will be on MY TERMS. I fight for every man who busted their ass for twenty years in this industry, only to be a working stiff at the end of their career. I fight for those men who laid the groundwork for this industry. I fight for those same men who died or are crippled for life because of wanting to be the best they could be in the ring. Tyler stands for the corruption and the men who continue to spit on tradition. Nelson will once again talk about how he is going to kick my ass all over the ring and everything. Well, when all is said and done, when all the dust has settled, he will be the one with his back on the mat looking up at the lights saying to himself, 'Self, I just got beaten for the third time by Kano and Jesus, my whole body hurts,' and it will be I, the man who rocketh the mosteth, who will have his hand raised in victory. Tyler may be the greediest, but I am the REAL LEGEND. And I truly am what REAL LEGENDS ARE MADE OF. And after Tuesday, I have a clear path straight to my newest objective. Cal: Which is? Kano: The one very presitigious title here in PCW I have not held. Cal: You aren't extreme enough to win the Extreme Title. Kano: That wasn't the title I was referring to, jackass. I have held all the title I have wanted to in PCW, except one and I feel now is the time to achieve my goal of winning that title. I hope Chris Chase keeps it nice and shiny for me. *The scene fades to black as Cal gasps in shock at Kano's latest revelation while Kano has a dreamy, almost euphoric look on his face.* ~A new era beginning both professionally and personally. Will Kano be able to capitalize? Was he serious with his last words or was he just blowing smoke up our asses?~
