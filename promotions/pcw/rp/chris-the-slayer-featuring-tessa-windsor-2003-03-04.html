Chris the Slayer(Featuring Tessa Windsor)

4 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3-4-03 A reanimated corpse said to suck blood of sleeping persons at night. They are known as vampires, ruthless hunters of the night that prey on the weak. To some they are only myths. To others they are a hard fact or reality. The deadly blood suckers have been the topic of countless books, movies, and stories across the globe. Their fangs dipped in blood of a fresh victim, peering out at yet another unsuspecting victim. They are the hunter of the night that hide in the shadows, only coming out with the sun has set on the southern sky. However with the darkness there is a small glimmer of light that shines through. They are the vampire hunters, mankinds only hope against the predators that strike without warning. They are few and far between but they appear when they are most needed. Like a white knight riding through the night they come. Their only mission in life is to seek out and rid the world of each and every last vampire. Their battle is a hard and everlasting one indeed. The two sides, one good and the other evil, battle every night. For centuries this has been going on. It seems never ending in reality. Yet things are about to change. Friday, March 7th, Friday Fusion, the Schmoopies vs. P.W.A. for the PCW Tag Team titles. How will this change anything? With the visions of their last encounter still fresh in Chris Storm's mind, he plans to tip the scales in the favor of the current champions and get the revenge his love so desires. Chris Storm will lace up not as a wrestle, but as a hunter of the night stalkers. He will be a vampire hunter. Nerreza's fangs sink in Tessa Windsor's neck, as he slowly begins to drain her of her blood. Tessa's skin becomes white and pale. Her eyes roll into the back of her head as she passes out from the lost of blood. Nerreza pulls his fangs out, letting everyone see the blood dripping from them. Tessa hits the mat with a sickening thud. As this thought leaves his mind perspiration forms on Chris Storm's brow as the events on that evening still remain in his memory. Chris sits on the floor of his bedroom Indian style; different little items are all around him. Chris looks at them, studying each one. 'Tessa; do we have any garlic?' he yells out. Past the doorway, across the hall, and in the kitchen Tessa is standing. She looks through the opening where she can see Chris sitting there. She looks around the kitchen for a second before yelling back to him. 'No, but we have some onions. Will that do?' she asks him. Chris rubs his chin thinking about it. A frown crosses his face. He sighs before saying, 'Yes that will do I guess.' Chris stands up and walks into the kitchen. He walks up right behind Tessa and gives her a hug. He puts his chin on her shoulder to see what she is doing. Tessa pulls the onions from the refrigerator and puts them on the counter for him. Chris gives her a kiss. 'Thank you' he tells her. 'Chris what are you doing to do with all of that stuff?' her curiosity has finally got the best of her. Chris scoops up the onions and starts to leave. Before he is out the kitchen he replies back to her. 'I'm getting ready for our match against P.W.A. I don't want a repeat of what happened last time we faced them. This time we are going to be ready for them.' Chris returns to his room and lays the onions on the ground. He twists his body around and looks at an open book resting on the bed. There is a picture of a bat on one of the open pages. Pictures of different charms line the pages. Chris' brow lowers as he studies the pictures. He turns his attention back to the objects sprawled across the floor. There were onions, a necklace made of pure silver, and two voodoo dolls that looks almost similar to Nerreza and Gaz. Chris picks up the silver necklace and examines it. 'Hmm, I think silver was for werewolves. I'm not sure. I can wear it to the ring and if it doesn't work maybe I can throw it at him and put out his eye.' Chris thinks about it almost like he was giving it serious thought. 'Nah, that's just stupid.' He tells himself as he hits himself in the head lightly. 'Come on now, think!' He puts down the necklace and looks at the dolls. It was actually just two paper dolls with Nerreza and Gaz drawn on them, although the art work is not the best. Chris grabs one in each hand and looks at them. String is used for the hair and little buttons do up their center to show buttons on clothes. Chris looks at them and nods. 'Someone wants to be a child again.' Tessa laughed as she peered over Chris' shoulder at the two paper doll replicas of the four time PCW Tag Champions. 'Hush Tessa I'm trying to concentrate.' Chris hissed as he turned around and placed his hand over Tessa's mouth to keep the silence alive. Instead of cooperating though Tessa responds to his actions by biting the hand of Chris that is placed over her mouth causing him to remove it and allowing to speak freely once again. 'HA, I WIN!' Tessa teased Chris as Chris looked down at his hand and then looked over at Tessa who looked ready to tumble over if her giggles went on any longer. 'Oh really?' Chris questioned as he walked in her direction with narrowed eyes. 'Really!' Tessa responded as she jumped into a fighting stance with a smile still widely across her face. 'Who do you think you are biting me like that? Nerezza or something?' Chris questioned as he stood face to face with her trying to keep a straight face which was nearly impossible for him to do around Tessa. 'NO I R TEREZZA, HEAR ME RAR!!' Tessa shouted in excitement as she jumped onto the bed and posed with her hands on her hips, looking down at Chris who looked ready to explode in laughter at any moment. Chris did his best to try and hide his laughter from Tessa, who clearly was in a very playful mood for some reason. He turns his attention back to his relics on the floor. Chris picks up the two paper dolls of Pranksters with Attitude and looks at them. Chris leans against the foot of the bed while he sat on the bed. Tessa moves around on the bed and laid down to where she was laying on top of his head, her arms crossing looking down at what he was doing. 'Yes?' Chris asks her as he tries to look up to see what she was doing. 'Oh nothing, I'm just waiting what you are doing. Very cute dolls though. Alex and Christa made them?' Tessa asked. Chris looked at the dolls and held them up. His voice was a little hurt. 'I made them.' He said. The weight of Tessa was suddenly lifted from his head as she fell back onto the bed laughing. Chris gets up and looks at her. 'What is so funny?' he asks her. Tessa answers him between laughs. 'That looks like something the kids would make.' Tessa fell back onto the bed laughing. She was rolling back and forth on the large king sized mattress. Chris walks over to the side of the bed. Suddenly and without warning Chris picks up Tessa and throws her over his shoulder. 'Terezza, I am the vampire hunter Chris Storm! Prepared to be slayed!' he shouts as he playfully drops her onto the bed, following close behind. 'Oh nooooooooo, you got meeeee.' Tessa shrieks with an abundance of laughter following behind these words. She lies on the bed defeated by Chris Storm the way she hoped that P.W.A. would be defeated by both herself and Chris but at the same time she realizes that Friday would not be all fun and games. P.W.A. would not be defeated as much as the mockery created by Tessa was. As a matter of fact Tessa realized that Friday could very end in the way the last match with P.W.A. had ended for both Chris and her and that was the very last thing that she wanted. To go through that against would be hell, a hell that caused Tessa to go silent as she lay there on the bed and realized that no matter how many jokes she made about it Friday would be not be fun and games.
