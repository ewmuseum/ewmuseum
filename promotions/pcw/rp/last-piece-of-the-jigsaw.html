Last Piece of the Jigsaw

Date: not entered And as the hours close in on March Madness, the hours close in on the last threads of sanity that El Diablo holds and the closer it is for him to show this new side of himself at March Madness. The deeper he gets involved with Babylonian history, the stranger and more enclosed he gets and the deeper he begins to change on the inside and outside, like the presence of his demons Asag and Gallu are controlling and changing him bit by bit, so that he can be the modern day demon. The most sickening part, however, is that El Diablo wants this. Page by page, the more the Mexican Devil begins to change, but will still disguise himself amongst it all to keep some integrity. It's like him and the demons are as one, where they may sacrifice things just like El Diablo may. Of course this is only mythical and superstition, so who really knows what is occurring within El Diablo. The fears of Angel are still well known, but she seems prepared to sacrifice those fears and follow the new El Diablo, who has piece of mind. Perhaps it is the demons inside of El Diablo that are causing his eyes to go a bloodshot red on occasions, and then go away just as quickly, but whatever it is, is very creepy and is enough to scare even the biggest of men, let alone a small woman, like Angel. There is obviously a power that El Diablo possesses, but where is this power drawn from and how can it become so visual, with only the blink of an eye? And then be taken away just on pure will. An oddness and eeriness it causes, but that may well be the new El Diablo on this world. Sure enough, El Diablo may be a man changing, but how much change can one man take without becoming withdrawn from the world and people around him? There is an aura around him already that whispers softly to keep away, or maybe that is just an imagination. The sacredness around him is so real it's freaky. The hours near towards March Madness, much like the hours near before the complete reformation of El Diablo occurs. As usual he remains seated on that same leather chair that he is read from before. His book is in hand and he sits riveted in the text before him. His wife, Angel, sits on the sofa opposite her husband, only now fully dressed in a short black skirt and tight black T-shirt that hugs her body well. 'This is interesting,' comments El Diablo with intrigue written all over his face. Angel looks across at him. 'What is?' asks Angel in partial curiosity. He keeps his head down reading the Babylonian book. 'Namtar! He would drag men by their hair to the underworld where they would live in an eternal damnation,' he speaks of Namtar gleefully. She looks on at him with a look of confusion. 'Oh, that's good,' she states cautiously as to not anger El Diablo or the demons within him. Still he keeps the book close to him, it seems that he cannot draw himself away from it. Babylonia is his life. 'He brought great plague to the world,' says El Diablo in a sinister tone, 'I like that. I will be able to bring great plagues to the ring with me and destroy those that oppose me,' he salivates with joy. His wife watches him, with a look of concern in her eyes for the sanity of husband. But chooses not to mention anything to him in an act of fear. El Diablo seems very serious about his quest for Babylonian history that anything Angel may say to change his mind on it may cause serious damage to them both. Not only is El Diablo so passionate on these beliefs, he is passionate on acting them out on unsuspecting victims. He grows stranger each day that his wife is finding him hard to follow, but like the loyal woman she is, will stick by what she thinks is a phase that he is going through. 'Discomfort is conforming to my soul, and I like it. It brings me piece of mind before my date with destiny on March 3rd, when the triad of demons within me become a reality and unleash themselves,' he comments in power, pride and passion. In fear of what may occur, Angel remains silent, whilst El Diablo keeps his burning red eyes locked on the book in front of him. If seen closer, the burning pits of hell are largely visible in the demonic eyes of this Mexican Devil. El Diablo in indeed focused on March Madness and Flaymin Aymin along with the Babylonian myths and demons he is so intrigued with. He stops looking at the book, and places it down again, a rare moment to be seen. Whilst apart from his book his book he stands up and looks down at Angel. 'The time is almost here for my affliction to occur,' he roars with passion, 'and soon the name El Diablo will be known, the name will forever be known as the true darkness,' he finishes with a look of pride in his eyes. After his minor speech he seats himself back down in that leather chair as Angel remains watching every move her husband makes. Deeper and deeper El Diablo involves himself in this Babylonian mythology and the deeper he wants to be drawn in.
