The Great Escape Part 3

21 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: March 21st, 2002 (The last time Mike Frampton was seen he was shaking hands with a man named Bones to signify the alliance that they have created. The goal; to destroy Doctor No. The plan; they have no idea. Mike Frampton is now wearing a white coat, white pants, a fake moustache and a fake beard. He walks beside Bones down the hall. They see Doctor No and Franky walking together, Franky now informing Doctor No of the attack by Mike Frampton. The two men walk up to Bones and Mike Frampton. Doctor No seems to be in an outrage.) Doctor No: Have either of you seen the son of a -(beep)- Mike Frampton? He is going to go under some of the worst experiments I have ever conceived. Nobody attacks Franky twice. Frampton is going to fry, and I mean that literally. Bones: I think I saw him heading towards one of the cellars. Franky: What about you? (Franky now pointing at Mike Frampton.) Franky: I have never seen you before. Who are you? (Frampton thinking quickly comes up with a false identity along with an accent.) Mike Frampton: My name is Yuri. I hail from mother Russia. I have not seen this Mr. Frampton that you speak of. I have had tremendous hangover after drinking lots of Vodka. Russians need to stay strong and powerful. I powerful. (His speech impediment seems so real that Doctor No and Franky nod.) Doctor No: Welcome aboard Yuri. Franky: If you see Mr. Frampton contact us. Yuri: Just who do I do that? Franky: Whistle. You do know how to whistle don't you? Yuri: No. Franky: You just put your lips together and blow. Yuri: Like Pamela? Franky: No, like this. (Franky whistles and walks off with Doctor No. Bones turns to Mike with a smile on his face.) Bones: Like Pamela? Mike Frampton: Hey I was thinking quick. Now you say they are looking for me but they are calling me Mr. Frampton. Who is Mr. Frampton? Bones: You are. Mike Frampton: Am I now? What does Mr. Frampton do? Bones: Mr. Frampton is actually Mike Frampton and he is a professional wrestler. Mike Frampton: Get out of town. They think I am a professional wrestler? Bones: Indeed, in fact Mike Frampton has only recently found work again. Mike Frampton: Well where does he work? Bones: I believe they call it Primetime Championship Wrestling. Mike Frampton: Owned by Ted Turner? Bones: I don't believe so. Mike Frampton: Tell me is Mr. Frampton any good? (The two men turn a corner and stop off at a water cooler.) Bones: Well, he is new to this PCW but he has been made out to be one vicious man. Mike Frampton: Tell me more. Bones: He made his debut with a friend named Big Daveylicious. He is here too I believe. Anyways, the two men terrible beat another man in the ring. I think they may have ended his career. Mike Frampton: So this Mike Frampton, he is a bad guy? Bones: Most definitely. The fans don't like him very much. He has only been in one match so far but he has one tomorrow. In fact he escaped from this place and wrestled a match. Later that night he was brought back as Doctor No saw him on the television. Mike Frampton: So you are saying this to me. If I escape and wrestle I will be brought back? Bones: Yes. That is why we must take down Doctor No. Mike Frampton: So we're gonna kill him? (Bones stares at Mike blandly.) Bones: No, blackmail. Mike Frampton: What do you purpose? Bones: We allow you to get caught and I film the experiments. Mike Frampton: You mean I am going to have to go through those experiments? Bones: It's the only way. Mike Frampton: What about Davey? Bones: He will be part of our demands. Mike Frampton: I don't know about this. Bones: You have to take one for the team. Mike Frampton: But what if I die? Bones: It's a risk I am willing to take. Mike Frampton: I see. Bones: Come on. (Mike drinks his water and crushes the paper cup.) Mike Frampton: After you tape me being experimented on for a minute, I want you to free me Bones. Bones: I will, I will make sure of it. Mike Frampton: Than I will do it. Bones: Thank you. Mike Frampton: But I better not come back to this place. Bones: Hmm, you wrestle tomorrow so I think you should leave. Then when you are brought back we can blackmail them. Mike Frampton: Agreed. Bones: Now get out of here. (Mike charges down the hall towards a window. Mike jumps and dives head first out the window as the scene fades to black.)
