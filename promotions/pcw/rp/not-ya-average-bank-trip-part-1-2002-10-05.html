Not Ya Average Bank Trip (Part 1)

5 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5.10.02 The scene opens up in a large bank in New York City. Outside you can hear the sound of NYC traffics, cars engines, car alarms, police sirens and the sound of thousands of people walking along the streets. Inside of the bank though, it is a different story. It is very quiet. A local radio station is playing quietly in the background. The bank is very large, with a aging, overweight security guard sitting at the entrance. There are a few desks around, where employees are talking to customers and there are five windows with bank clerks sitting behind them and queue’s behind them. The majority of people in the bank are white. Just then, two big African Americans come walking into the bank. One is dressed in a pair of baggy black jeans, Timablands, and he has a baggy white T-shirt on and a big platinum chain around his neck. His hair is set back in cornrows. He has a big black bag over his shoulders. This man is Duzza. Next to him is his faithful sidekick Pookie. Pookie, who just a few months ago was your average poor Negro, then won the lottery after borrowing some money from Duzza and then moved into the mansion next to Duzza. Pookie is dressed the same way as he used to be when he was still poor and in the ghetto, only now he has a ton of jewelry on him. Duzza and Pookie are in the back to pay in a couple of checks. All of the staff in the bank seem to be watching Duzza and Pookie carefully, which both men notice. Duzza: “Man, check these cracka’s lookin’ at us like we ‘bout to rob this place” Pookie: “I know… if only they knew we got more money that all of these fools” Duzza: “They’ll notice that when we pay in the checks, nawmean?” Pookie: “Yeah… and then they’ll accuse you of being a drug dealer and haul yo ass to the pen” Duzza: “Player please” Pookie: “Hey, I know. It happened to me” Duzza: “But you actually used to be a dealer” Pookie: “So?” Duzza: “So?… Man sometimes I wish buying that lottery ticket for you” Banker: “Hey gentlemen, how can I help?”Duzza and Pookie walk up to the gentle looking, middle aged white woman and hand over the checks. Duzza: “Hey, yeah I just come in to pay these checks” The lady looks at the checks and her eyes open wide when she looks at the amount of money the check is for. Banker: “Errr… excuse me, I will be right back, I just have to check something” Pookie: “See… I give it 2 minutes and the po-po will be here” Just then a tall black man, dressed all in black walks through the door. He has a black bandanna round his mouth like a cowboy and he has a black baseball cap on his head. Pookie: “Man, check out that dude. That’s a big ass mother fucker” Duzza: “Yeah… that’s nice… anyway” Just then the guy walks into the middle of the room and hollers Man: “AIGHT! THIS IS A STICK UP! EVERYONE ON THA FLO’ BEFO’ YOU GET SHOT UP” All the white people scream and dive to the floor, followed by Duzza and Pookie Duzza: “Aww shit” Pookie: “We gon’ die, we gonna die, I’m too young to die” Duzza: “You best shut up before someone fucks up and you get shot up” Man: “AIGHT! I WANT ALL THA MONEY IN THIS BANK PUT IN A BAG AND BOUGHT HERE TO ME. ANYBODY PRESS THAT LIL RED BUTTON Y’ALL GOT BEHIND THE DESK TO GET THE POLICE HERE AND YOU GON GET SHOT UP. BELIVE DAT” Duzza: “Pookie, this all yo fault” Pookie: “What?!” Duzza: “I was just gonna go to the bank near my house, but nooo, you said we had to come to NYC, if I die, I’ma be waiting at the crossroads for your ass” Man: “SHUT THE FUCK UP! NOW GET ME MY MONEY” Duzza: “This mother fucker think he all that just cause he got a gun he all that” Pookie: “Don’t anger the man, you’ll get shot more times than a Nigerian immigrant who reached for his wallet infront of the police” Man: “GET MA MONEY ‘FORE I HAVE TO CAP A MO FO!” A timid white women in a dress suit runs over to the man with a big bag of money as sirens start going of Man: “HAHAH I’M RICH! Shit… wheres the fire exit?” The lady points to the back of the bank and the bank robbers runs out of the bank, while everyone else stays on the ground not quite sure what to do, as sirens can be heard coming closer and closer Pookie: “Err… Duzz… what do we do? Duzza: “I’ma do what ever minority should do when they hear police sirens. Run” Pookie: “Naw dawg, naw, Chris Rock said not to run from the police, ‘cos when they catch up wit’ you they’ll beat on you like a white boy in the projects” Duzza: “I bet Chris Rock once, he ain’t that funny, now let’s get the fuck out of here” Duzza picks up his bag, dusts himself down and speeds out of the bank, followed by Pookie. They run to Duzza’s black low rider and Duzza chucks the bag onto the back seat and jumps into the front seat, not even bothering to open the door, Pookie soon follows him. He looks over at Duzza, a painful expression on his face Pookie: “Dawg, why we not going anywhere? What’s wrong?” Duzza: “I… I… landed on my keys” Pookie: “Oooh.. that’s gonna hurt in the morning” Duzza: “Man, it hurts enough now” After a few more moments of feeling sorry for himself, Duzza gets the keys out of his back pocket and starts up the low rider and speeds off just as the police arrive at the scene. End Of Part 1
