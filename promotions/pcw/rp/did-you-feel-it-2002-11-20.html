'Did You Feel It?'

20 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11-20-02 “You are the strength! You are the power!” screamed Dr. Jenkins. He was right when he said it too. G.O.D. was the power. In this match more than any other. He and Livewire would have to make up for most of the size in this match. It would be Livewire, the Genetic Oriented Drone on one team, Ivan Stanislav for Old School Wrestling, Akiyama being a strength force for The Celestials, and Nerezza for Team Bastardos. Those were the biggest of the biggest and G.O.D. was already pretty sure he could outmuscle them all. He was the biggest and the strongest in his mind and in the minds of many others. Power was his ally as much as Matt Elia, Tyler Valentine, and Livewire would be. RUSH was unimportant. It was something that needed to be put to the side until after Alliances 2002. Alliances 2002: featuring the Genetic Oriented Drone. How sweet a sound that was inside his head. Inside both their heads. And so G.O.D. continued to work on his strength as he pumped and pumped and pumped on the bench press while thoughts of the upcoming Sunday danced inside his head. Inside both their heads. Sunday would be a very good day, there is no doubt. Sunday, everything would come together. G.O.D. and Dr. Jenkins both thought that Team RUSH had a good chance. They both thought that RUSH Wrestling could finally put their name on the map and really prove to everyone out there. All the wrestlers… All the staff members… All the critics… All the fans… Everyone… … that RUSH was a force to be reckoned with. Rep after rep on the press strengthened his arms and his happiness and confidence helped fuel the reps. The reps would then fuel the win. Each rep brought his team closer and closer to glory. Closer and closer to victory. “C’mon Gene! You’ve finally done it! You’re going to the show!” the great doctor yelled out to his creation, “You’re going to win! You’re all going to win this thing Gene!” He was too. He could feel it coming. Can you feel it coming? “You’re going to win this motherfucker!” This caught both men off guard. Eugene! dropped the weight right on his chest. “Ooph!” he moaned. The swear. Dr. Jenkins never swears, especially in front of what he has created. The two were amazed. G.O.D. amazed at such foul language and Dr. Jenkins amazed that he actually said it. “Uhh…” the great doctor stuttered, “I’m… I’m sorry Gene.” G.O.D. just lay there with the weight on his chest and his mouth wide open. “Umm… let’s get back to work, Gene.” He just lay there. “Gene! You’re gonna do it!” He put his right hand on the bar and gripped. His four fingers wrapped around the cold metal he felt it. He felt the win in the palm of his hand. He lifted his other enormous arm and wrapped his fingers and thumb around that part of the bar. The cold. The win. He feels it. Can you feel it? You certainly will and then everyone, Sylkk, Matt Elia, G.O.D., Livewire, Valentine, McGlocklan, Joe Bananas, The Masked Mullet, Brock Newbludd, Marvin Harris, El Spiffy!, Stimpo, Mordred, Gidean Poe, hell even Matt Hunt, everyone will ask all around them the same question: ‘Did you feel the RUSH?!’
