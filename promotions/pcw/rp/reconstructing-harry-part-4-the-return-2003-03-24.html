Reconstructing Harry - Part 4 - The Return

24 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: March 24, 2003 [Tommy walks into the room expecting a gnarly layout but all that he seems to get is a half empty room with boxes stacked all over the place. He is sort of relieved about it because once that ventilation system kicks in Tommy can just send people over to grab the boxes instead of packing everything back up. Unfortunately, in a moment of weakness, Tommy is doubting that this plan of his is going to work out. Harry is extra intelligent for some unknown reason and it's just tough to get ANYTHING by him. Tommy knows that he's going to have to act as cool as possible around Harry no matter what is going on. He can't act any differently than he would if he didn't start burning all that weed into the ventilation system on the roof. Though the looks that Harry has been giving Tommy as they walk through the loft is a bit unsettling. It's as if the compadre KNOWS everything that goes on in Tommy's head no matter what it is. Even if it's a little white lie Harry sees right through it and if it's a HUGE LIE, like secretly getting him high he'll REALLY find out about it. That's if Tommy can play it cool...he has to] Harry: It's a really nice place. Right now it looks crappy with nothing really unpacked, but once everything is in place this is going to... Tommy: ...a poon palace. [Harry gives Tommy a stern look] Harry: Really nice is what I was going to say. This is my place, not yours Tommy. I'm not going to have statues of naked women out on display. Tommy: You know you want to though. [Tammy looks on with a smile on her face at the two brothers having an arguement. That little rat face, for some reason, is just so damn cute. Tommy can't quite place exactly WHY it's so cute but maybe it's good that he doesn't. That he just go with the flow and try to keep calm about the situation at hand] Tommy: How about me getting a World Title shot? Pretty bodacious, huh? Harry: Very 'bodacious', as you would say. It's going to be TOUGH going through those nine other guys...don't get too overconfident. Tommy: Your starting to sound like that totally heinous limo driver, Fat Tony. Harry: Well, maybe we're onto something. [Tommy stopped paying attention as he sort of scans the room over, going toward the vent. He takes a whiff in that area and doesn't even catch a hint of the weed he strategically placed into the ventilation system. He sighs knowing that defeat COULD be upon him] Harry: Tammy, you have to just KNOW my brother. He thinks that he's some kind of evil super villain that is going to conquer all in PCW. I have to give him credit for that really great Continental Title reign that he had, but to think that he could have the World Title around his waist is a little too premature if you ask me. Tommy: I heard that! Harry: I know, that's why I was saying it LOUDLY. Tammy: You guys are just two of a kind. It must be tough to be living far apart from each other being so close to one another all those years. Harry: It's going to be a battle, but it's for the best. [Tommy takes a seat on the leather chair that he let Harry take with him. It's like a piece of home right in New York City. Tommy just sits back and lets the depression begin to set in. He's already dealing with the fact that he's not going to be going back to California with his brother and that Harry will not be there for his big World Title match on Tuesday. Harry is going to be unpacking his things and making out with the lovely Tammy all night long. The compadre does deserve happiness, but who's to say that he wasn't happier with the life he had before he went sober. Honestly, who'd want to have a grasp onto reality in a totally bogus time like this? Harry must be crazy to have picked this time to go sober! He's better off in his haze of weed and spitting out hilarious fragments from time to time. Chicks dig a compadre who reeks of marijuana, especially when he shares it!!] Harry: You look so down, Tommy. What's wrong? Are you finally doubting yourself about your match on Tuesday? Tommy: No... Harry: Well, then what is it? Tommy: It's nothing that you'd understand. Harry: Yeah, man....err...I mean you can tell me. [Tammy and Tommy look up at Harry weird. Tammy is surprised to hear the change in Harry's voice as Tommy is having a different feeling. In fact, Tommy is having a feeling of elation] Tommy: Dude, it's nothing that you have to worry about now. [Tommy rises from his chair and begins making his way back over toward the vent. As he gets there the scent of marijuana is coming through strongly. His plan is working! It's actually working! Tommy looks over at the happy couple hugging each other, giving each other pecking kisses. Tommy just needs a reason to get Harry over in that corner of the room without making him suspicious. Once he catches the scent of the weed he's going to freak out so hopefully the huge odor will have to affect him strong enough to keep him off of his toes] Tommy: DUDE! A Cockroach! Tammy: EW!! Harry: Where?! Tommy: Get over here and check it out, dude. [Tommy has no idea how he's going to fake there being a cockroach, but just hope that Harry gets stoned enough to get out of that heinous intelligent state. Harry storms over ready to begin crushing the bug, but he stops halfway there. His brown eyes bug out of his head and he looks upward] Harry: Weed... Tommy: Where? Harry: It's coming from the vent. Tommy: No way, dude! That's impossible! Harry: I see the smoke coming from the vent now. It's strong. I might breathe in the fumes and be infected with it once again. I need to go to the roof and stop this. Tommy: You need to take care of this totally bogus cockroach. I think it's going to eat me! [Harry looks up at the vent and then over to his brother who is backing the corner, afraid that a cockroach is going to eat him. Harry then looks back to Tammy who is standing on the leather chair completely freaked out all the way across the room. It's a really crappy crossroads that Harry has come to because he is not sure if he's going to crush the roach, stop the weed, or console his lovely new girlfriend. She's safe so there is no need to worry about her for the time being, but the other two still remain. Tommy is a professional wrestler, damnit, he can fend for himself against one cockroach...] Tommy: HOLY CRAP!! THERE IS AN ARMY OF THEM NOW!!! [This has changed the situation for Harry now. The smoke is coming through the vent thick now and he can barely make out his brother who is shrieking in absolute terror. Well, it's a no brainer for Harry now...he HAS to save his brother! As he takes a few steps toward the vent he comes a realization, something that should have hit him immediately but it didn't. His mind is already a bit hazy, that could be what the problem is, but what are the chances a giant weed cloud appearing when Tommy has been wanting to get Harry high ALL DAY!! It's a set up. A very well planned set up by his usually stupid brother] Harry: I'm not falling for it, Tommy. I know that you set this up...man. [Harry shakes the cobwebs out feeling some effects from the weed but staying strong and holding the sensation down. He can no longer see his brother through the extremely thick smoke now. Harry begins taking steps back as the smoke is just going to slowly come billowing into the room and infect him anyway. What he needs to do is to get out of there and to the outside where he'll be immune to it's effects] Tommy: OH MY GOD!! Their teeth are totally sharp, dude!! My skin is burning off!! [It could be that he's somewhat stoned but he is believing his brother's cries for help now. He shouldn't, but for some reason he does. Maybe it's just that feeling people get when a family member is in trouble, but whatever it is he knows what he has to do now. With a leap through the large smoke he stomps, swings, and slams every part of his body hoping to take out the roaches. He opens his eyes to see Tommy standing in the corner with a bowl in reach. Harry looks at it with disgust and then looks back up at his brother with the same disgust. Just as he's about to let his brother have it this familiar feeling begins to sweep over him, like a long lost friend returning. Harry begins to feel like he's flying into the sky and his fingers begin to have a tingling sensation. The feeling...is just so damn good that he can do nothing but let out a smile now] Tommy: DUUUUUDE! You gotta take a hit! Come on, DUUUUUUUUUDE!! [Harry looks at the bowl and for some reason it just seems like a natrual reaction. He takes it and lights up with lightning quick reflexes...not even losing one single step. Tommy nods with approval as Harry's eyes begin to immediately turn bloodshot and his eyelids slope downward to make it look as if he's half asleep. Harry takes a few more hits and has totally killed the weed that was in the bowl. Smoke is EVERYWHERE in the room now and if there were actually cockroaches in the room they'd be baked just like everyone is right now] Tommy: It's good to see you again, compadre. Harry: YEAH, MAN!!!! [Tommy smiles brightly and gives his brother a thumbs up] Harry: Somebody crank on Twisted Sister or I'm going to put my head through this wall, man!! Fade to black
