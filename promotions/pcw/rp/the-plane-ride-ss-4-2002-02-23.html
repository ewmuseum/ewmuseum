The Plane Ride SS#4

23 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2-23-02 Segment One: Hank, Ganker, and Josh are all sitting on a plane in the same row. Josh is near the aisle, to make a quick get away. Hank is two seats in and Ganker in the middle. Hank is wearing baggy black JNCO jeans, black t-shirt with the words Team Nirvana in yellow print, and a long black leather trench coat. Ganker is wearing a black leather jacket, a yellow t-shirt with the words, 'Ganker is God' printed on the front in black text, and baggy generic blue jeans. Josh is wearing a black Metallica t-shirt and blue jeans. He sips on his ginger-ale as both Ganker and Hank drink their Sobes down the their respective hatches. Hank: Nothin' hits the spot more than a nice cold Sobe beverage. Ganker: I heard that bro. Josh: Ginger-Ale you numb sacks~! Hank: Traitor... Josh: (sighs) Just because I don't dirnk what you drink? Hank: Exactly~! Josh: Oi creepo the geeko. Hank: (confused) Huh? Josh: I don't know, I just said it cause it seemed to fit. Thinking back I don't know really why I said it to be honest with you. Hank: Cause yo fiz-nucked in the hiz-ead. Ganker: Stop talkin' black man. Hank: I will if yo will. Ganker: I don't, I talk Ganker style, people mimic me cause they be jealous, cause, (thumb thing) 'Ganker Is God'~! Hank: I'm so sure bout that. (laughs) Then I must be some ultimate being. Ganker: Are yo? Josh: Hopefully not. Hank: Smack him sh*tless Ganker. Ganker: Nah, that'd require effort me no want use at current state. (sips Sobe) Hank: Jus' wait til we get off the plane boyo. Josh: Damn, I'm a marked man. --- Josh dials a number on his cellphone. Josh: Hey Rosy, this is Josh Young... Yeah, you know the guy you met down the street? I might not live past today if Hank has his way with me. Hank: That doesn't sound right. Ganker: Please say no more, I might get an image in my head and there's no need for that. Hank: Maybe, maybe not. (grins deviously) Ganker: Do it and I take out the wrath on yo boy. Hank: Damnit, he's wise to my schemes. Ganker: Yo don't travel on the road wit' yo punk a** and not pick up on yo style. Hank: That poser over there never has. Ganker: Josh is stupid, a dumb a**, a dumb sh*t, need I go on. Hank: Yes, it's entertainin' to listen to a fellow Josh basher. (grins) Josh: Listen babe, I gotta go, they're talking about bashing me... No, not in a bloody way, just by name calling... Yes, unfortunately they are my closest friends... Yes I know that's sad... No, Sephiroth is even worse. --- Josh hangs up his cell and slides it back into his pocket as Ganker hands Hank a tissue, while trying to hold back on laughing repeatedly at Josh's stupidity. Hank: (blows nose and then reveals to be pretending to whimper) That was jus' so gosh darn... (pauses) PATHETIC~! Ganker: ANGH, ANGH~! Josh: GIMMICK INFRINGMENT~!!! Ganker: Who? Josh: Austin... Ganker: Damn that Bionic Thief... Steal my lines... Hank: Forgive him, but he makes bull up all the time... Now how come yo so whipped? Josh: I am not whipped~! Hank: Uh huh, yeah right. Josh: Shouldn't yo be takin' advantage and reading that information I gave you on Jason Core? Hank: What about Karl LeBlanc, we know he's not good enough for me to waste my time readin' facts bout him, but why didn't yo get me papers bout him? Josh: I figured yo already knew all yo could bout the loser chumpo. Hank: I do, but that's beside the point, I jus' would like to have all the facts and information I can bout my opponents, especially when MY PCW Extreme Championship is on the line. Yo know what that means don't yo Joshy? (Josh nods) That means I get to go Hank~! Gunyon~! on their a** and do whatever the f**k I want to them cause it's all legal. I'm goin' to show Jason Core his inner most core and then stomp on it and spit on it in front of his face, I haven't decided, the heart, the lungs, or the stomach. Josh: Why not the brain? Hank: He has one? I thought he was special like me and lacked that element? Josh: No, he does and so do you. You just fail to use it most of the time. Hank: Right, I forgot. Josh: Point proven. Hank: As for LeBlanc, I'll show him for the second time why I'm the better athlete, wrestler, I'll show that Swamp Freak who the real ex-con is. It would be me, if they could catch me. (laughs deviously) Josh: That's true and I haven't known yo for but maybe one and a half to two years. Ganker: I've known him most of my life and he's still a damn psycho, he kills for money and for pleasure. Pleasure most of the time... Rest in piece X-Con... Hank: Damn that loser was a chump. He talked too much, so I figured I'd shut his hole, like I'm goin' to do this Sunday to Jason Core and Swamp Freak-o Karl LeBlanc. Josh: That's a Spirit Buddy~! Hank: Never call me buddy again. It makes you sound gay/ Ganker: So? He looks gay too, what's yo point? --- Josh slumps down in his seat and starts to whimper. Ganker: Oh shut up yo crybaby~! Josh: It's my body and I'll cry if I wanna~! WHAAA~!!! --- Hank gets up and flips over Ganker and Josh and lands in the aisle on one knee and grins then turns toward Josh, slowly getting to his feet. He stands over Josh as Josh starts waving his hands in female slapping motion. Hank: Wussy Pussy~! Josh: WHAAA~! WHAAA~! WHAAA~! --- Hank then grabs Josh by the throat and lifts him to his feet, after undoing his seatbelt. Hank then plants him on his shoulders and nails him with, 'The Job' as you can hear cheers from the other passengers on the flight because he shut up that annoying crybaby. All but two are cheering for Hank, those two being Janelle Stout and Ana Lagunez. Hank puts Josh back in his seat before they see and gets back in his seat as they walkover to see what's all the commotion. Janelle: Hank, did you do this? Hank: I do not know what yo talkin' bout babe. I mean I've been a good b... Man and stayed right here, he's one seat over and Ganker's in my way and Ganker's obviously fine. Ganker: It was that old granny over there. Ana: He got beatup by an old granny again? Hank: Again? Yo haven't known him that long have yo? Ana: No, but Ganker's filled me in for the most part. Hank: How would Ganker know? Ana: No clue. Ganker: I have my sources. (hides papers that have Red Rock written on them) Hank: What were those boyo? Ganker: Nunya... Hank: Huh? Ganker: (saying it slow for Hank's stupidity to understand) N-o-n-e o-f y-o-u-r b-u-s-i-n-e-s-s~! Hank: Yo want yo a** kicked don't yo? Ganker: Nah, not really but if yo insist on tryin'. Hank: No tryin' bout it, but I need to conserve energy for LeBlanc and Core, not like I'll need it... Damn jobbers. Ganker: (sarcastically) Yeah, right, (cough) bullsh*t (cough)~! --- Ana and Janelle walk back to their seats as the scene fades out to absolute/complete/utter nothingness.
