Duplicates, South Park, Necrophilliacs, a Dead Sarah Michelle Gellar, and Triple H-Zilla; Part Two.

22 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: January 22nd, 2003. “And we’re back!” The scene returns to the original scene from “Duplicates, South Park, Necrophilliacs, and a Dead Sarah Michelle Gellar; Part One.”, the most recent promo from Jackson Rust and the other lot of people that were involved, unwillingly. Jackson Rust: Oh right…now, I do believe I was ignoring Al, right? Kimberly: That seemed to be the case. Al: Yeah. Jackson Rust: Where did that voice come from? Al: You can’t ignore me forever. Jackson Rust: Watch me. Al smirks. Al: You just responded to me! Ha! Ha! Jackson Rust: Dammit! Al: Ha! Ha! Ha! *SMACK* *KICK* *PUNT* *DIE YOU BASTARD* Al flies back, as the result of the brutal attack by the Narrator, and runs into the wall; well, not actually “runs”, but you know what I’m sayin’, right? Jackson Rust: So, Lemmiwinks…what should we do now? Narrator: Wait; you’re going to refer to Lemmiwinks for suggestions, opinions, ideas, etcetera, etcetera, instead of myself, or Kimberly? Jackson nods. Jackson Rust: All of you fail in comparison to the Gerbil F’N King, man. Steve: H-he’s g-got a p-poi-point, th-there. Kimberly: Albeit, I am a bit angry to be defined as lower than a gerbil, but if it’s the Gerbil F’N King, then I have no problem. Jackson takes a few steps back, quizzically. Jackson Rust: Really? Kimberly: NO YOU DOLT! IT’S AN INSULT TO ME, BITCH! Jackson Rust: Oh… Narrator: If you didn’t have the Gerbil F’N King in your possession, I would so asterisk SMACK asterisk you right here, right now. Jackson Rust: You mean… *SMACK* Steve: Ouch… Narrator: Yeah, that’s the one, but it’s more like this: *SMACK* Steve: OUCH! Kimberly: I didn’t see the difference. Jackson Rust: Me either. Narrator: *sighs* It’s the “slap of the Narrator”, and only I can perfect it…even if it is the exact same, mine’s better. Jackson Rust: Oh…right. Kimberly: I was thinking…you know how we said we’d be right back after a momentary break? Narrator: Yare, yare… Kimberly: Isn’t two days a bit much? Jackson Rust: Not for MY fans. Al, and Steve, and Kimberly, and the Narrator, and Editor #4 all break out into laughter. Even Editor #1 stops attempting to hump the corpse of Sarah Michelle Gellar to get a few chuckles in between breaths. Narrator: Fans? Yours? Please, Jacko, everyone KNOWS they watch these promos because of me! You just do the wrestling. Jackson Rust: Like you ever could. Besides, I’m not even sure you can beat down some of the opponent’s I’ve faced before; or going to face…like Chris Storm! Narrator: Oh ye— Kimberly: Wait a minute! Everyone shuts the hell up pretty damn quick, as Editor #1 goes back to humping. Kimberly pretends to ignore him, and his panting, as she sparks a thought. Kimberly: That sounded like the introduction to an old fashioned Jackson Rust verbal character assassination! Jackson scratches his chin because of this…he doesn’t normally scratch his chin. Jackson Rust: A…verbal beat down? Al: That’s what the lady said. Jackson Rust: Not now, Al…I have to be melodramatic here. Al: Oh, okay. Jackson Rust: So, let me rephrase that. Rust clears his throat. Jackson Rust: A…verbal beat down? Kimberly: Yeah… Narrator: But, but— Steve: T-THAT’S M-MY SK-SKIT B-B-BITCH! *SMACK* Jackson Rust: But, I haven’t given one of those…since my DWO days…as the ever-popular WORLD CHAMP, BEYOTCH~! Al: Well, maybe it’s time to do one… Jackson Rust: I…don’t know. I mean, I COULD insult Storm, and call him an overprotective Hitler to Tessa, but I don’t know. I COULD call him an emotional disaster, who needs to get some serious help to make him less of a douche bag, but…I don’t know. It wouldn’t be me. Narrator: Yes, it would. Jackson Rust: No, it would—okay, yes it would, but still…give me some credit; I’m trying to be a bit different than what I used to be… Kimberly: And getting nowhere doing it. Jackson Rust: Bah! I don’t take none of that shit, love and neither will you if ya know what’s good for you! Narrator: That was my MSN screen name thing for about two weeks. Jackson Rust: Err…no. Narrator: Yes… Jackson Rust: …no. *SMACK* *SMACK-AN-INFRINGER* Jackson Rust: *no sells*© Narrator: DAMMIT! Al: Man, that was a helluva move. Steve: I-I’ll s-say. Kimberly: Damn… Jackson all of a sudden starts moving about in an intimidating steroid-influenced fashion, before raising his arms above his head and spitting into his entourage! Jackson Rust: NO ONE’S ON MY LEAGUE-UH~! Rust snarls, like Triple H would in a situation like this. Jackson Rust: I AM GOD-UH~! His nose extends an addition eight inches outward, as he starts bulging with veins. Jackson Rust: AND I AM THE GAME-UH! AND I AM THAT...DAMN...GOOD-UH! The scene suddenly reverts to an old Godzilla monster movie, as a huge beast is destroying the city of Tokyo; but in this part, right n’yah, a picture of Triple H’s face is plastered where the head of Godzilla would be, and has the World title strapped to its green waist. The Japanese citizens flee for their lives, before two stupid foolish ones stop and point, speaking, but their mouths moving and not matching with the words. Japanese Guy #1: It’sa…TRIPLE H-AH! Japanese Guy #2: Then fuckin’ run! It’s only a matter of time before we job to the Pedigree! Triple H-Zilla: ROAR-UH! ROAR-UH! Japanese Guy #1: What do we do? Japanese Guy #2: Let’s make this a real feud, and have an arm wrestling match! Japanese Guy #2: How ‘bout we run! The Triple H-Zilla grabs a random Tokyo building, and tears it out of the ground, and holding it to his mouth. Triple H-Zilla: I AM THE GAME-UH! The Triple H-Zilla stops for a moment, before strutting around the deserted streets of Tokyo, looking around, and raising the building to his mouth again. Triple H-Zilla: Tokyo isn’t half a man that I am…because I AM THAT DAMN GOOD-UH! Japanese Guy #2: OH NO! He’s using his super promo powers! Cover your ears, Tokyo! Triple H-Zilla: NO ONE…in this industry has yet to escape from jobbing to the Pedigree…I mean, there isn’t anyone who I haven’t defeated…yeah. The scene abruptly reverts back into JacksonCO studios. Jackson has deflated back to normality, as the rest of the entourage blink blankly. Narrator: So that’s Triple H’s alternate career should he fail even –more- that he is. Jackson Rust: Seems it. Editor #1 walks up behind them, dragging the corpse of Sarah Michelle Gellar by a choker and chain, tuning in. Editor #1: What did I miss? Al: The fact that SMG is dead. Al nods as Jackson fumes at him. Jackson Rust: It’s SARAH MICHELLE GELLAR! NOT SMG! Al: Sor— *SMACK-A-DUMBASS* Editor #1: Sure…she’s a little cold to the touch, but that’s the way *uh huh, uh huh* I like it *uh huh, uh huh*! Kimberly: Never do that again, and I’ll forge Al’s signature onto his will when he dies; giving you his body. Editor #1: Deal. Al: HUH?! Editor #4: Al and Editor, sittin’ in a tree…F-U-C-K-I-N-G! Al: Stop, stop, and stop! Al runs off crying like a little schoolgirl, as the rest snicker behind his back. Jackson Rust: We’re gonna have to torment him ‘bout that. Narrator: Oh, hell yeah. Kimberly: Definitely, hehehe. Steve: *shrugs* The scene abruptly fades out to a picture of Triple H-Zilla, putting himself over King Kong as Kong takes the Pedigree into the Pacific Ocean. Triple H-Zilla: RAR~!
