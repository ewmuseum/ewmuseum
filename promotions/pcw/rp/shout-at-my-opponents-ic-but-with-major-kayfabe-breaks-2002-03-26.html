Shout at my opponents (IC, but with major kayfabe breaks)

26 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 03/26/02 Promos. They are what we do, we being wrestlers. We get booked to a card, we have certain opponents for that card, and in addition to training, we do promos. Why? Promos hype up the match, as well as ourselves. A person that doesn't cut a promo has a hard time getting over with crowds, just because they don't have much concrete (besides wrestling, which let's face it they don't pay as much attention to as we would like) information about us with which to establish connections with our characters and decide if they like us or hate us. Promos help define our characters, and the only great way to get over as faces or heels (few are truly neutral nowadays) is through promos to reiterate just how good or bad we can be. I do promos. My opponents do promos. Hell, even when Tyler Nelson was running the company he'd still put up the occasional promo. As part-time trainers in PCW, we help the students work on their promo skills, for we know and realize that those things are indeed important. Or, so they may seem to be important. Okay, look, I know that I've only started putting up RPs for Turmoil since yesterday, but it's become my habit to wait until the show prior to the one I am booked on is over before posting promos for it, though I do take my time to write them beforehand. That means that since my match with Clyde, Rush, and Chet King is on Turmoil, so I wait until RPing for Sabotage is finished so that there won't be any clutter at the top taking attention away from my promos. And then my opponents have to go screw everything up! You know how many promos I'll have submitted to PCW by the time you read this? Three. You know how many that my opponents have collectively produced? ZERO. Mason, Clyde, King: come on! I can't be that worthless in all of your opinions that you wouldn't even bother to do the normal thing when it comes to hyping a match with your opponent -- namely producing and submitting promos. Hell, the promos don't even have to be about anything, just so long as you send one in. But you f*ckers can't even do that! Rush, you're probably high off your ass from all the weed you smoke in your big lonely 'Church of Crunk.' You know it's only a matter of time before I destroy that place. You're not a god, you're just a very annoying muscular man, one that has yet to prove that if I kill you in the ring, that there will be great sorrow throughout the land. Clyde... heh, I've already beaten you, so you wouldn't dare try to get on my bad side. You know what it's like to step into the ring with the Son of Purity itself, and lose. You're the burned man? You're a demon that feeds on hate? You lost your edge in the ring, because I don't hate you. I just find you a waste of oxygen, but I don't hate you. Therefore you could not gain any strength from me, and you failed to win. It must eat you up inside to have lost to a mid-card 'jobber' like myself. As for Chet King, he's the 'Chat King' but damned if I've heard a word from him yet. Someone did inform him that he has a match with me, Rush, and Clyde, right? For the others, I can at least say for certain that they are afraid of me, but Chet King... is he even alive? Did he enter the witness protection program or something? This is very odd how one man who is supposed to be such a recognizable name in PCW is nowhere to be found... Since joining the Celestials with Akiyama, Jay Sylo, and to a lesser extent, Pharaoh, there seem to be many more people that are deathly afraid of me. Perhaps only now do they truly understand just how dangerous I can be? Or maybe they are just worried that Sylo and/or Akiyama would do a run-in? Don't worry about that... I'm more than capable of defeating anyone in PCW. Akiyama, Sylo... they know that I can defend myself. So, with this cleared up, there's only one thing that really needs to be asked of my opponents... 'Where are you?' I'm waiting... Don't disappoint me.... The End steps out of the pitch black recording room from which he just made his promo into the grey brick walled hallway. Jay Sylo meets him outside. 'You were certainly blunt,' Sylo says already dressed in his ring attire and his Celestial Star Title slung over his shoulder. 'And excellent kayfabe breaking, I might add.' The End shakes his head as he and Sylo walk past the crates of camera equipment, cable cords, lighting stands, and other various little things that all go into the larger production of PCW Tuesday Turmoil. It is already 4:40, and the show is going to start soon. The dark matches are already underway... Sylo and End get to the locker room where The End's opponents would be readying themselves... The End opens the door; nothing. They haven't shown yet. The End closes the door and turns to Sylo saying, 'I'm really getting pissed off how everyone keeps disregarding me. As if I'm not a threat to their very health.' The End and Sylo continue walking down the long hallway. After a while, Sylo says, 'Then again, after what we all did to Carmoon and Lance Bell on Sabotage, would anyone really want to face us?' The End shakes his head up and down in understanding. 'Good point,' says the blind one. 'So they're afraid of you... that's bad? That just proves how much of a monster you really are. PCW's first Cruiserweight Monster. And when you destroy your three oppponents tonight, and then you take Stormbringer out of action on Spring Fling, you'll be even more feared and respected. And the bookers will have to start giving you title shots again.' Sylo pats his Celestial Star Title with his free hand. 'And as for me, I've got matches against Drake Maxwell, John Crichton, and then the battle royale at Spring Fling. My Celestial Star record will be longer than Rent-A-Hero's, and no one is going to screw that up for me.' On that note, the camera filming Sylo and The End cuts off....
