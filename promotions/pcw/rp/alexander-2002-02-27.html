Alexander

27 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 27th Febuary 2002 We rejoin the illustrious duo on a short hop over flight from Toronto, Canada back to Anaheim, California. Well I say short... I mean comparatively short... The US is a big place you know, so just be glad you don’t have to pay a 10 to 25% airport tax like you do back over in the states. Anyway, sitting there in first class of this rather prestige jet is one Steve Jabowski dressed as always... like a frickin’ lumberjack and of course Miyagawa... who seems to be having this whole Angel complex going on of his own. That’s Angel from Buffy the Vampire Slayer, not Angel as in low flying aircraft debris on radar. He seems to be happy to be free of that white fibre prison that his face was trapped in for the last couple of weeks, but rather sad at the same time for other reasons discussed in the last promo cut by him and “young” Steven. Steve, being the aged old codger (what is it that once Wrestlers hit say... fifty, suddenly they don’t age until they become Freddie Blassey at 80? And how comes Freddie hasn’t aged in twenty years either?) is sitting there in first class, amongst people drinking champagne from tall glasses... with a bottle of Budweiser in his hand as he leans back watching an in flight movie with no great interest. For some reason “Chasing Amy” wasn’t exactly his cup of tea, and he was rather annoyed earlier to find that the airline had pulled “Jay and Silent Bob Strike Back” from the listings well... probably because someone had actually watched it and realised what it was about. He looks over towards Miyagawa who is scribbling in a notebook to himself. The pair themselves takes up both seats on one side of the plane, with there being an aisle and then another two seats. Of course with first class on such a small plane there isn’t exactly a heck of a lot of seats, hence why every seat in first class is taken. Steve: Hey Miya? Miyagawa: Hell, I guess at least I got that name right. How would you feel as if you feel like you’ve lived your entire life with one name, and then suddenly you get told you were wrong. I need some serious therapy after March Madness... hell, what a great name for the situation, I feel like I’m going completely crazy here. Which is dumb, because as you so graciously point out that I was never in an Asylum, so basically I never went crazy. Which doesn’t exactly pin the blame on anyone thing as to why I get so completely freaked out at points. Steve: Have you ever considered that you might just like to get freaked out like that occasionally? Miyagawa looks at Steve and ponders this for a few seconds, before finally retorting back at him a simple answer. Miyagawa: Good point. Steve: See, not every single thing can be explained rationally, some things you have to just sit on your boat in the shallow waters of life and let yourself be swept out to see with the hope that when you are swept back, you are still in the boat. Miyagawa: Dude, that was like a Chinese Sensei moment there, it totally was. I guess you and Buddha both have two things in common. Steve: Wise beyond our years and adored by millions? Miyagawa: I was aiming for fat and bald. Steve: I’m not bald... well... not ten minutes after I wake up each morning anyway until five minutes before I go to bed at night. Everything in this world is about faking it, so can you blame me for wanting hair back? Miyagawa: Yeah but you’ve gone from Patrick Stewart to Ric Flair, and you’ve got to ask yourself... is that a good thing? Because I don’t have a sensible answer to give you for that one, so you will have to decide for yourself. Steve looks at Miyagawa and realises exactly what he is trying to do here, which of course is shifting the centre of attention from himself and his small family crisis, to Steve and his large follicle crisis. Steve: Don’t try to turn the attention onto me... Alexander. Miyagawa: Don’t call me that, we have no proof, it’s not necessarily me yet, and you know it. Sure there is a huge percentage chance that it is defiantly me, even at this stage, but it’s not a hundred percent... maybe it’s ninety or ninety five percent, but it’s not a hundred, so don’t start acting like it is. Call me Miyagawa, at least we all know for certain that it is my name. It is my name, right? Steve: As far as anyone can tell, yes. Miyagawa: Good otherwise the promotion guys who do all the t-shirts and stuff at PCW would be majorly pissed. Can you imagine the same guy changing his main name halfway through his PCW tenor? Steve: JJ did it. Miyagawa: And look exactly how long JJ lasted as Jeremy Johnson in PCW before finally leaving. A sum total of about a month. It’s who the fans connect with that is the problem, and luckily they have connected with this mythic figure who wears goggles and does crazy stuff called “Miyagawa” and luckily I’ve not used “Isawa Miyagawa” a whole hell of a lot. Steve: Personally I think Alexander Miyagawa is a much stronger ring name. Isawa sound good, but the name was too short. Miyagawa: Alex Miyagawa. **listens to the sound of it** Nah that sounds like I’ve cut the important part off the first name, doesn’t it? Steve: That would be my opinion. Miyagawa: Gah, I guess this is where I make some sort of overused line about how at March Madness people will know my name... even if I don’t know what it is. Predictability sucks. Steve: Boda sucks. Miyagawa: Juan “Golden Rod” Borro sucks. Steve: This sucks. Miyagawa: What does? Steve: I think I only get one beer per flight. I wonder if that means I can have one of each type? Miyagawa: I doubt it. At least we know one thing for a hundred percent certain. Steve: Which is? Miyagawa: I'm still me. Changing the label on a bottle of wine doesn't change the contents of the bottle. I may not know my name, but people will know my actions at Madness. I give name to this Pay Per View with what I shall be doing. That was so cliched, don't you think? And we only again leave the intrepid duo on their voyage back to California, not exactly knowing much more than when we started to meet them. That sucks. Oh and why you ask? Because... I’m... Excellent. Sorry, it’s in the contract, it has to be said at least once per promo.
