Flaymin: If I can constantly break stuff by accident, then I’m pretty sure I can do it on purpose for large amounts of money. [The phone suddenly begins to ring. Flaymin: I’m trying to sell demolition… not dildos. [Jenna shrugs her shoulders.Meanwhile, Flaymin picks up a few peanuts and casually begins to eat them. [Flaymin and Jenna go their separate ways to collect their respective items. A few minutes later, Jenna returns to Flaymin with a whole bunch of deadly ammunition. She puts the ammo in a plastic container and hands it to Flaymin] Jenna: So do you know what it is we’re blowing up? Flaymin: Nope.We’ll have to see it when we get there. [Flaymin and Jenna exit the house and hop into their car. 45 minutes, 3 dead dogs, 18 wrong turns and 4 smashed cars later, Flaymin and Jenna arrive outside the building that they’re supposed to destroy. Flaymin gets out of the car and stares up at the 6-storey building] Flaymin: How do you think we should do this? Jenna: I don’t know.Just knock it down…and stuff. [A shady man with slicked back hair, wearing an Italian suit worth more than Flaymin’s entire PCW Contract walks over to the Demolition Duo] Maxamillion: I iz Maxamillion.Pleased to meet you Mr…err…fire guy. Do you still want me to do it anyway? [Flaymin slaps his forehead.He then raises Jenna up by the hair and puts her to one side] Flaymin: Stay! Jenna: But…. Flaymin: Staaaaaaaaaay!Staaaaaay! That’s a good slut! Now, just keep your mouth and legs closed. [Having silenced Jenna, Flaymin turns back to Maxamillion] Flaymin: So Maxamillion, how do you want us to do this? [Flaymin looks at the building and notices that there are still people entering and exiting the building] Flaymin: Hold on a sec, there are people still in there! Maxamillion: Only hobo’s and whores.I’m knocking down this homeless shelter to make way for a restaurant.
