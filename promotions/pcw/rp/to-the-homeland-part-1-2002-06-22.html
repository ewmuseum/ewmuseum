To The Homeland... (Part 1)

22 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 06/22/02 The Television Title. A Title that is usually used as a stepping-stone for wrestlers to a much bigger prize. Wrestlers who have gone on to become PCW World Heavyweight Champion have held Several times the title. Tyler Nelson, Miyagawa, Big Alan Steele, Jeremy Howard, Frisco, and The Chef. Six men who have held the Television Title and gone on to glory later on in the PCW. Will Jordan X Shabazz join them? Will Jordan X Shabazz become the next Television Champion of the PCW? Will he go on to further glory with that title? We'll have to wait and see... I told it to you before, and now I’ll tell you again, Jordan really wants the television Title. Internet Smarks around the world have been raving about Jordan’s in-ring ability, his workrate, and his talent on the microphone, but they said that Jordan hadn’t proved himself in the ring yet. Jordan would say that it was another example of ‘Whitey’ holding down a black man. But deep inside he knew the real reason. The real reason why he wasn’t getting the respect he deserved, the reason was because he couldn’t win the big match. He choked. Directly or indirectly, Jordan X Shabazz holds victories over Rush Mason, Duke Williams, Frankie Bones, Drew Hostyle, Jonathan Drachen, Dante Jackson, and a whole host of other people. But that was in a Clusterfuck. Jordan can’t win a big match, even against opponents that people feel are inferior to him. While in the NTICW, Jordan X Shabazz defeated current NTICW World Champion, Scott Taylor, for the NTICW Extreme Championship. His reign was short-lived, because he left for the PCW, but beating Scott Taylor was quite the accomplishment. The only problem was that the match happened on Showtime, a weekly NTICW card. Not a Pay Per View. And then at the PCW’s last Pay Per View, Supremacy, Jordan X Shabazz was pinned by Mark Makonie, a man many people believe that Jordan was more talented then. Why did this happen? It’s the lack of game time mentality. Jordan always has his mind elsewhere. N.I.G.G.A was going to hit the New York Stock Exchange in a few days, that was weighing heavily on his mind. He hadn’t gotten to be good buddies with Snow and Rent-A-Hero, that weighed heavily on his mind too. But this thing that was on his mind right now could be considered one of the best things any Civil Rights Activists can do… He’s going to Africa. But he isn’t going to Africa with no goal, he has a goal. His goal is to give as many televisions out to the poor African Children that he can. Exploited his own race for his own gain? Well… you could say that… if you were an Egotistical, non-caring, white person. (Jordan paid for that last line to be entered into the narration.) The scene opens up inside the airport. Jordan X Shabazz walks with two white men, you don’t know these men yet, but you will get to know them. One of them is Steve Armstrong. A young, poor, teenager who had moved out of his parents’ house when he was 16. He had been on his own growing up for the past five years, working odd jobs for older people. He didn’t have the skills to become a butler, but when Jordan X Shabazz saw him panhandling on the streets of Dallas, Texas, Jordan offered him the job of a butler. He couldn’t say no, he thought about stealing from Jordan, but the fact that Jordan carried a gun around with him at all times was a deterrent. But now they were at the airport, Jordan didn’t have his gun with him, but Steve wasn’t staying long. The other kid, Chris Bird. He grew up much like his friend, and fellow butler, Steve Armstrong. Almost identical lives. Neither has graduated high school, and both were panhandling when Jordan ’discovered’ them. They think that Jordan hired them to give them a chance of sorts in a life that has been nothing but one of pain and disappointment. But only Jordan knew the real reason. He loved having two white slaves. That’s right, slaves. That’s exactly what they were. Sure Jordan claimed they were butlers and well paid, but they weren’t. They lived in a small cramped pool house that Jordan owned. They had just moved in. They were paid $20 a day, and they worked every damned day. They usually worked at least three hours, cleaning up the house, running needless errands that Jordan had erected so he could have the power of ordering a white person around. Jordan had televisions in all of the bags, he had nothing but a few of the clothes that he would need for his wrestling road trip after getting back to America for Turmoil, other than that, he didn’t have anything but the small televisions that he was going to give to the unfortunate children in Africa. Getting through customs, for lack of a better term, was a bitch. In fact, Jordan wasn’t even allowed to get his televisions through customs. They had to break every single miniature television to check inside for weapons, and or drugs. This angered Jordan, our scene is opening where they are telling Jordan he cannot board the plane without getting rid of his televisions. The airport is buzzing. Steve Armstrong and Chris Bird are walking back to their pool house. They don’t have the key to Jordan’s house, nor do they know the security number for the alarm system, so their will be crazy party. Jordan X Shabazz is enraged that he couldn’t get through with his televisions. Jordan X Shabazz: “Oh give me a break, damn it! The children in Africa have earned this after creating the very world that we live in! LET ME BRING MY DAMNED TELEVISIONS WITH ME!” One of the security guards, apparently feeling in a humorous mood, replies to Jordan. Security Guard #1: “You’ll get your televisions back, Mr. Shabazz.” Security Guard #2: “…Yeah, back in pieces.” Both security guards begin laughing at Jordan as the anger is shown on Jordan’s face, he doesn’t say anything to them, knowing that God made ignorant white people to give him inspiration. **************************** 2 hours later. After being thoroughly searched, and having the televisions he brought with him to Africa, smashed open and checked, Jordan X Shabazz is on the plane now, moving to “The homeland.” His mind now was on Andy Murray and John Crichton for Tuesday Turmoil. It was a chance to get payback on Crichton for the attack on Fusion a few weeks back. And revenge on Andy Murray for ruining his four way match on the latest Fusion. He’s going to have to buy some televisions there, and he’s going to distribute them, so all the little African kids can see him on television, holding the TV Title…
