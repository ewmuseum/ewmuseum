Date: 3/14/02 The camera fades in… The Chef sits on a brown leather chair holding a book that looks to be at least 200 years old. He is dressed in his normal Islamic Militant garb, and his beard seems to growing by each passing day. The Chef: Greetings fellow Islamic Fundamentalists! I am the Chef, and I will be your guide as we examine Classical Myth… You see, the Greeks and Romans neglected the story of a hero who had the strength of Hercules, the survival instincts of Sisyphus, and the knowledge of The Fates. He was Cheficles! Suddenly the Bertha clone walks in front of the camera. Bertha: Hey f*cko! I don’t f*cking see any c*ck sucking reference to Cheficles in this mother f*cking mythology encyclo-f*cking-pedia, b*tch… The Chef: Uh, well that’s because he’s a special kind of hero that transcends the writings of Ovid, Homer, and Hesiod. He defeated countless monsters, restored order to the world, and ascended into the eternal realm as a god! Bertha: Wasn’t that essentially that f*ckface Hercules? The Chef: Maybe, but I’m betting that even with his impressive knowledge about Greek and Roman myth, Big Allan Steele has never heard of Cheficles. It’s this impressive knowledge that leads Steele to believe that he is in fact the great hero Hercules, but my hero is different. I know that in my heart Allah has given me godlike powers, even before I follow him in faith. Now I’m united with Allah, and the god within me shall be released! I am Cheficles! Don’t you see Bertha!?!? This whole story is just a metaphor for my life! Bertha: (Sarcastically) Well f*cking give away the secret already f*ckdaddy… The Chef sighs. The Chef: Let us begin our tale… Long before time, or about one year ago… There was a hero named Cheficles… He was the son of Pierre, the God of French people, and some whore whose name I don’t remember, but she did have webbed feet. Cheficles fought with bravery and honor, and never murdered his children in a crazy rage like some heroes… The Chef looks at the camera as if he was making a point about Steele’s character. Cheficles was brought into a world full of turmoil and sabotage. With his ultimate power, the power to smite anything with keen culinary intellect and rolling pin, he became a legend. Cheficles was forced to perform several great duties and tasks in order to become a god. His first task was to stimulate the fall of the Roman Empire. This empire not only featured talent with boring gimmicks, they also didn’t produce the required promos to stay within the world. Well, Cheficles took care of them, one by one. When the leader of the Empire was defeated in battle, Cheficles took his crown, and banished him from this world! His second task was to be in an angle with Naketicus, the personification of homosexuality. The two men fought each other over Cheficles’ crown. Naketicus, who used underhanded homosexual tactics, won the fight. While Cheficles did lose the huge battle against Naketicus, he managed to finish the angle, and remain in the world far longer than that crybaby Naketicus. Naketicus fell into a void of nothingness, and became another god’s bitch. His third task was to tame the wild stag in time for the Christmas celebration. Cheficles, with the help of his lover and partner Berthae, managed to domesticate the stag called Randolphus, and Berthae rode him triumphantly into the big fight that occurred a few days later. His fourth task was to conform to the infinite power of Ploutos, otherwise known as Nelsonis. Nelsonis tempted Cheficles with his endless stream of wealth, but Cheficles wasn’t swayed. Well, except for a couple occasions where he went to the Asian spa. Upset with Cheficles' lack of conformity, Nelsonis threw Cheficles into the farthest reaches of humankind, the midcard. While Cheficles did not complete his task of conformity, his opposition to such greed awed the world. His fifth task was to marry the hideous gorgon Medusa, or Berthae, as she is known to us laypersons. Berthae was once Cheficles trusted friend and lover, but she went insane, and Cheficles sent her to Vermont. Surprisingly, Berthae did not take the opportunity to find a same sex partner and get married there and returned in full health to Cheficles. While healthy in her mind, she had the body only Richard Simmons could love. Cheficles though took pity on her, and wed her, and he was never happy again, like most men after marriage. His sixth task was to topple the Titan Stanislaveus and his cohorts. Cheficles did so on two separate occasions, and Stanislaveus soon left Cheficles world, seeking a new crown and a way to lose his virginity. During one fight, Stanislaveus attempted to maim Cheficles, but Cheficles was helped by his trusty friends Walczakus, Mr. Ukeus, and some German guy named Hans. Yet another task Cheficles… The Chef is interrupted Bertha: This is soooooooooooo f*cking boring! The Chef: I think it’s quite amusing, I’m just adding “eus” to everyone’s name! Bertha: But what about the f*cking times you’ve been defeated? The Chef: Ah yes, I’ve…I mean Cheficles has had his fair share of difficulties in the world. Most recently… Cheficles had returned back to the mortal world after being granted immortality by the life force of the world, Ron. He made his return by beating a peon by the name of Theus Mutipleus Maneus. This miserable excuse for human, not any worthier of the crown than a tree nymph yanked the celestial trunks of Cheficles, showing his bare and immortal bottom to the world. Cheficles never forgave Theus Mutlipleus Maneus, and swore to sauté his @$$ on an Olympic scale for all to see. Cheficles would then reaffirm his allegiance to Allah and celebrate by scheduling some suicide bombings! Bertha: That’s f*cking great, but is this prophecy self-fulfilling? The Chef: You better believe your Tartarus-sized bottom on that my lemon tart! No priestess at Delphi can predict the level of @$$ sautéing that will take place within the next few days. That White Castle Chicken Ring the Multiple Man will wish he was in Ancient Greece. At least then he would have Hippocrates as a ring doctor…Ha Ha Ha! Bertha: That’s all f*cking well and b*tch @$$ mother f*cking good Cheffy baby, but how the f*ck are we gonna sauté Steele queer as f*ck @$$!?!? The Chef: That’s a good question B. The battle will be hero versus hero, with the addition of two culinary school dropouts…Miyagawa, and the Chase fellow. I really don’t have the time to talk about them, for they are only minor players. They are like the minor deities of the PCW Pantheon, but Steele and I are the heroes! We are the ageless wonders that endlessly clash in tremendous battles where the fate of the universe hangs in the balance. Unfortunately for Steele though, his “Hi-Keebas” and incredible strength will yield pitiful results to my keen culinary wit and vast assortment of utensils of mass destruction! Bertha: So is that your mother f*cking game plan? The Chef: Yes, ‘tis my Olympic @$$ sautéing plan! First it’s the Multiple Man, followed by a tough, but ultimately gratifying victory or Big Al, and finally I’ll recapture the World Title by sautéing Boda’s @$$ in a nice vinaigrette with black olives, feta cheese, peppers, and some baklava for desert! Bertha: You know what I like is those mother f*cking gyros! Can’t we make any of those f*ckdaddy? I’m so f*cking hungry, I’d eat the @$$hole out of a dead queer @$$ bitch who just had a f*cking d*ck sucking case of the mother f*cking piles! The Chef: Charming… The Chef accidentally flips through the pages of the old book and stumbles upon a story long forgotten, but universally known. The Chef: Ah ha! I have found it! Bertha: F*cking what d*ck @$$!?!? The Chef: The story of the chef, and the oaf… Bertha: Oh great, it’s the f*cking Learning Channel all f*cking over again! The Chef: SHHHHHH!!! The Chef begins… Long ago there was chef that made the finest baklava in all of Greece, and an oaf that aimlessly flaunted his strength. While the oaf tried to impress with his muscle, the chef impressed with his mastery of phylo dough. The chef continued his baking, and the oaf continued his loser ways. One day though, the oaf came to the bakery and demanded that he receive some baklava or he would twist the chef’s arm right out of his socket. Bertha: Oh f*cking my! The Chef: The chef said that him giving the baklava freely to the oaf would be unfair to the others in the town. The oaf again threatened the chef, this time saying he would make orphans of his illegitimate children. Faced with a life or death situation, the chef caved in, or did he? Bertha: Hmmm? The Chef: Ahem, as I was saying, did the chef really cave in? The pastry chef handed the oaf his baklava and the oaf like the glutton he was gobbled the sweet treat down. That wasn’t enough, he demanded. The Chef complied and gave him another, but that still wasn’t enough. Finally, fed up with the oaf's insistent badgering and annoying nature, the chef did what any person would do in that situation. Bertha: What did he f*cking do!?!? The Chef: He crumbled Chocolax on one piece and fed it to the oaf, who greedily ate the baklava. Seconds later, the oaf desired a trip to the bathroom. Unfortunately for the oaf, the chef’s bathroom was the nearest one, and all the others in the town were out of order. The oaf pleaded with the Chef as his stool began to “prairie dog”. The chef though requested some financial compensation for the gracious gesture. As the oaf began to slowly crap in his pants, he finally broke, and handed all his cash he got from flexing and bullying for that one trip to the bathroom. So what’s the moral of that story? Bertha: When you’ve gotta f*cking go, you’ve gotta f*cking go? The Chef: No… Intelligence is the primary weapon of choice for the elite my dear ignorant cow-woman… Steele can muster all the strength he wants, but I will prove the victor because I harness a higher, more effective power; the power of the mind! Let’s not forget about Allah’s power as well, praise be to Allah for his power! The Chef closes his book and looks directly into the camera. The Chef: The future of the PCW will be determined by one man, and one man only, and that is THE CHEF!!! Bertha: F*ck yeah! The camera fades out on the duo…
