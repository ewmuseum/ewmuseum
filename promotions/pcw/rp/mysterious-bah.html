Mysterious, bah!

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: not entered (And welcome to another typical day here in THE Multiple Man’s secret lair.) “Will somebody help me get him out of the cupboard? I can’t get to the pasta!” (Yup, just another normal day. Keira peers angrily inside the afore mentioned cupboard.) Keira: “Get out, or at least move some!” Tmm: “Close the door! I’m MYSTERIOUS!’ Keira: “Then go hide under a bed somewhere, it’s more traditional!” Tmm: “I’m not going anywhere. My cupboard, my lair, my pasta, so deal with it!” (Snarling in hungry anger, Keira retreats, her stomps rattling the dishware as she passes. Tmm slams the cupboard door closed. Enter Raul.) Raul: (opening cupboard) “[Something in Spanish.]!” Tmm: “I’m being mysterious.” Raul: “[Something in Spanish.]?” Tmm: “Because apparently that’s exactly what I am. Nobody knows anything about me. Nevermind the extensive archive, the photos, the thirty foot picture on the blimp, the billboards, the merchandise, the personal appearances at EVERY used car dealership within a hundred mile radius!” Raul: “[Something in Spanish]?” Tmm: “Yeah, and all the milk cartons too. Nope, none of it matters any; I’m nigh invisible. Wooooooooooo! Be afraid of me! I’m an gibbering eldritch thing from beyond the pale!” Raul: “[Something in Spanish.]?” Tmm: “Well…no, I’m not really like Vinnie Cantante. Skip that gibbering eldritch thing then. I’m like a… dark avenger of crime.” Raul: “[Something in Spanish.]?” Tmm: “I’m not really sure, since I’ve never actually avenged crime before, but it can’t be overly hard to do it. And the dark part’s easy enough. Click! Turn off the lights, what’s so hard about that?” Raul: “[Something in Spanish.].” Tmm: “Well... no, but I just said I was LIKE a dark avenger of crime, I didn’t say I was. It’s the mystery factor, like how many licks it takes to get to the center of a tootsie roll pop, even though that’s an irrelevant measure because nobody ever licks it, they suck on it. I am the unexplained crop circle! I am the bigfoot print and initials in the new concrete! I swear, it’s downright irritating. What the hell do I pay my publicity agent for, anyway?” Raul: “[Something in Spanish.].” Tmm: “Yes, I’m sure that both of his kids are doing just fine at Harvard, but in theory, I should get a little extra press because of his efforts. How’re my legion of fans…” Raul: “[Something in Spanish.].” Tmm: “… my three legions of fans…” Raul: “[Something in Spanish.].” Tmm: “Fine! My THREE fans supposed to keep up with all that is new when there’s nothing ever announced?” Raul: “[Something in Spanish.]?” (Tmm jumps in shock, bouncing his head off the top of the cupboard.) Tmm: (rubbing bump) “Oww! It didn’t escape, did it?! It’s not after me again!?” (Quick cut to Val’s laboratory where she carefully monitors a large tank filled with a viscous green luminescent goo. Floating inside, with sensors stuck all about its surface, is the PCW Lightweight title. Cut back.) Raul: ”[Something in Spanish.].” Tmm: “Good. The LAST thing I need is for it to get out that I’ve contracted another case of Lightweight title. Sales would plummet, parents would keep their kids at home, dogs would shy away…” Raul: “[Something in Spanish.].” Tmm: “That too. My only hope is that it jumps to some other, tastier target like the parasite it is. That fool Anton may be just the bait I need.” (Tmm laughs mysteriously as the camera fades out.)
