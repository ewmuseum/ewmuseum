Defeat? Nay I Say...In Reality...Victory?

4 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6 - 04 - 03 To Bruce Shanahan: By now you’ve probably received wind of my ‘defeat’ to not only Gaz Green, but his conniving bitch Tessa Windsor as well. Yes, a double loss. A HUMILIATING one at that. It still boggles my mind how it all came to pass. I was teamed with Pharaoh and Dante Jackson against a blind, sorry soul, a clown, and his whore. Should have been CAKE, Bruce. But instead, I found myself staring at the blinding lights above not once, but TWICE! I don’t think that’s quite the extent of it, though. That’s not what truly bothers me the most, Bruce. You want to know what it is? It’s not the loss of the Television Title, either. Any guesses?...It’s the total insubordinate nature of our so-called ‘friend’. You know exactly whom I mean. He didn’t seem very concerned that the Triumvirate had been dealt a SERIOUS blow. This doesn’t exactly do wonders for our image, Bruce. It doesn’t display our strong case of unity and defiance. How could we POSSIBLY be on top when we’re constantly lying on the bottom? The Triumvirate went from holding two of the most major titles in the federation to holding ABSOLUTELY nothing! The titles are now basking under the sickening light of GREEN! Skylar Green, Gaz Green, and Tessa Windsor-Green. All were flukes. You know that, I know that, and I ASSUME Pharaoh realizes that. I can only hope he does. He’s begun to worry me, Bruce. His mind is focused in on the World Title, but it seems to ALWAYS be out of his reach. It was evident in our match. He didn’t make any attempts to help me whatsoever, to help US! Should he have intervened, I’m positive I’d still be wearing the gold. I’m not making excuses, I lost and I know that. I will admit Tessa put up quite a fight, but I was not in peak condition. Carrying the match wore me down and I couldn’t pick myself up. Perhaps it’s my own fault entirely, but I still cannot fathom WHY Pharaoh would not help. We need to look into this, Bruce. Our team will fall apart without all three members on the same page. You cannot POSSIBLY read a book without reading a page at a time. We’re reading different pages and ending up on different subjects. I battle Brianna to defend the Triumvirate. You deal with that fool Jackson, who was also a weak link in the chain during our match, to defend US! Pharaoh tries to win the World Title for his own personal glory. This is unacceptable. Our golden assets are gone, we must battle with more fury than ever before. We cannot the infidels walk all over us. We cannot let petty, temporary glory that sits around our waists distract us. Now is the time to STRIKE! In fact, Fusion is the beginning of the end for them. I have a game plan, but I cannot discuss it here. We must speak in person about this. I know you must feel like shit, but I know you’ll understand the importance. Brianna Washington, Tessa Windsor, Skylar Green, Gaz Green, Dante Jackson… all of them… They have nothing to fear now. NOW… but just wait… For soon, it all comes crashing down. Until later, my friend… keep it real. - Dorn
