The Road Trip

2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3-8-02 -We open our scene driving down an open freeway in a black Camaro on a cloudy Saturday afternoon. Amidst the sound of the engine, and the screeching of the tires as they speed down the open road.. we can hear the faint sounds of 'Zero' by Smashing Pumpkins on the car radio. ::A flash of light blinds us:: We now see a picture of a man in black cargo pants and a blue shirt with long black hair that has purple streaks, standing proudly amidst a group of four other wrestlers... ::Another flash of light..:: The words 'Ideal 4' pop into view.. the words themselves, as if alive.. are throbbing, symbolic of some sort of tension. Finally, as the letters are throbbing, we begin to see a crack run down the middle of the word.. ::Yet another flash of light fills our eyes with nothing but white:: We are now back inside the black Camaro. As we now take a look at the man behind the driver seat, we see that it is the same man from the flashback. However, the cargo pants, the blue shirt, the long black hair with purple straks in it.. all gone. Replaced by khaki shorts, a black tanktop, and short wavy dark brown hair. The similarities between the man, and the flashback are obvious.. yet, he does not seem to be the same person. ::The light flashes into our eyes, yet again:: We see a joyful grin on the face of the long, black haired individual. ::The light flashes one more:: And reality is brought back to us. We look, and look.. but cannot find any signs of a joyful grin on the face of the driver. Instead, we see a look of determination. A look of seriousness. With all the changes this man has gone through since the last time we saw him, its amazing that we are still looking at the same person.. the same Reeco Suave. As he drives, seemingly on a pre-determined path, we see him notice a sign on the side of the road. As the mile markers pass by.. second by second, we see him finally pull to the side of the freeway, and up a ramp. He pulls into a gas station on the side of the road at the top. An attendant quickly makes his way to the Camaro, with a grease-stained rag sticking out of the pocket of his grease-stained jumpsuit. He looks like a stereotypical overweight southern bald guy with buck teeth. Attendant: Haddy doo, sir! Mah names Hoss, we have some recently cleaned beautiful facilities near the back of the station, and we have a fully stocked store inside..! Reeco Suave: Just fill up the tank. 'Hoss' takes the pump and sticks it into the hole near the back of the spotless black car. After five minutes, Hoss is still trying to get the pump to work. We see him shake the gas tank..- nothing. With the nossle still inside the Camaro, he kicks the tank and we see a large lump of gasoline start to make its way down the hose. Hoss, not noticing it is still kicking the gas tank until the lump of gasoline finally reaches the nossle and we see nearly all of it spray out of the nossle, which has been left unattended. Back inside the car, Reeco just shakes his head in dismay. He gets out of the car and walks to the back of the gas station. As he opens the door to the restroom, we see a green mist.. an almost nasty stench-like green mist exit the restroom. Inside, we see that the facility has not seemingly been cleaned in years. Excrement on the ground.. the putrid stench of piss lingering in the air.. and cobwebs forming around the ceilings. Reeco Suave: You have got to be kidding me. THIS is the recently cleaned 'beautiful' restroom facility?! If I stay here any longer I may just puke.. Reeco walks out of the restroom, making sure to close the door properly.. locking the putrid stench of piss inside. He walks over to some bushes behind the gas station - and we now focus our attention back on Hoss, who is still trying to get control of the nossle. As we look at the ground, we see a small puddle of gasoline forming. As Hoss is trying to turn the nossle off, we hear a few curses and obsenities coming out of his mouth.. out of the corner of our eye, we see Reeco making his way to the car. Reeco Suave: Nice, uh.. facilities you have there - Hoss: Ya think so? Well ah'll be darn tooten! You da first person to ever actually say somethin nice about this here gas station! Reeco Suave: Er.. yeah. I bet.. I can't imagine why that could be. Anyway.. how much is the gas? Hoss: That'll be $54.99 sir..! Reeco Suave: '$54.. 99? No.. there must be some mistake. You spilled half the god damn gas on the ground, and you're charging me $54.99?! Hoss: Ah'm a tryin to run a business here sir.. Reeco Suave: You.. don't know who I am, do you? Hoss: .... Reeco Suave: You're telling me, you've never heard of Reeco Sua- Hoss: Wait.. you.. him.. nah, it couldn't be. Reeco Suave: No, no.. me.. him.. its true! Hoss: Well ah'll be darned! You that there 'rassler!! Reeco Suave: Uh, well.. yea- Hoss: WHOOO-WEEE! Holy sweet Aunt Jemihma.. I can't believe it! I love that there 'rasslin! Mah son.. he.. he idolizes it! He wants to be a 'rassler someday.. but.. he's fraid he'll knock his only two teeth out. Reeco Suave: Well.. I'm sorry to hear that. Hoss: Say... you woulnd't mind signing an autograph for mah poor ol' boy Billy Joe Bob John Steve Jefforiah Frederick Michael Smith would ya?! Reeco Suave: I'm kind of in a hurry here, and - Hoss: Ah knew it!! Reeco just stands there with an annoyed look on his face. This gas station attendant, Hoss, reaches into his pockets and pulls out a small notepad, as he is fidgeting with it.. the grease on his hands gets all over the paper. He gives it to an annoyed Reeco Reeco Suave: *sigh* Who will this be made out to again? Hoss: Billy Joe Bob John Steve Jefforiah Frederick Michael Smith. Reeco Suave: .. And, this is one person? Hoss: Pshaw.. what ya think? Of course its one person.. thats mah son! Reeco just shakes his head, trying to get over with this whole ordeal. He signs the notepad, and hands it back to the greasy Hoss. With his greasy fingers now fidgeting with the notepad, Hoss places it back into his pocket. Reeco Suave: So, eh, I really have to be going.. how much will the gas be again? Hoss: Aw ah can't charge you! Shucks.. Ah'd feel bad chargin mah sons favorite 'rassler for some gas - Reeco Suave: Thanks, now.. I gotta go. Reeco quickly opens the door to the Camaro and gets in, in one quick move. He turns the motor on, and closes the door.. within seconds, he has pulled out of the gas station, going back to the freeway. As he gets onto the open road of the freeway, we see him wipe his forehead in shame. Reeco Suave: My god.. people like that give wrestling a bad game. Overweight, buck-toothed hicks. Back at the gas station, we see Hoss walk up to the store. He leans against the door and reaches into his greasy pocket, pulling out the notepad. We see that Reeco's signature has been smeared by all the grease, both on Hoss' hands and inside Hoss' pocket. Hoss: Aw.. GOSH DARNIT! Billy Joe Bob John Steve Jefforiah Frederick Michael Smith is gonna kill me! That boy'll have mah head.. STUPID STUPID STUPID! As we see Hoss bang his head against the side of the store next to the gas station, we are left with one last shot of Reeco's black Camaro speeding past a speed limit sign. ::The all too familiar blinding white light flashes into our eyes once more.:: This time, we see a shot of the old Reeco Suave.. with long locks and all, standing atop a turnbuckle with the smile on his face, and a seemingly joyful outlook on life. We end our little look into Reeco's return with the faint sounds of his radio being heard on a blank screen.. as an old Reeco 'tune' is playing. 'My Hero' by the Foo Fighters.
