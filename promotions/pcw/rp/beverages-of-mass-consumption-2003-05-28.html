Beverages of Mass Consumption

28 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 28.05.03 A hand shoots up in the air followed by the tall, burly body of Abner. The young rednecks gets to his feet, and immediately, everyone is drawn to attention. “Yes?” The mayor asks. Abner, stumbling through the words, tries to sound as polite as possible. “Hell no! I got a better idea. How about ‘stead a taxin’ folks by their money, you tax them their stupidity!” A chuckle echoes throughout the meeting house. “Yeah, that’s right…a ‘stupid tax’! I mean, that’s all these damn frigin taxes are anyway…stupid!” By this time, the audience is roaring in laughter. The embarrassed mayor turns his crimson red face to his assistant who then gives a young man to her left the “throat slicing” signal. Abner’s mic goes dead. And this is the way it always is at the town meeting. Ever since Abner can remember, even as a little boy, this was the routine. The mayor gets started, rambles on about some nonsense, and puts him to sleep. But then, after this, the major takes questions, and after the first few serious ones the audience get a collective smile, Abner gets up, he gets called on, and the rest is history. He’s a fan favorite and the town always turns to him for a good “ hootin’ and hollerin” as they call it. “Okay, Abner, that was quite an interesting question, but I think I’ll p…” “Pass, my ass!” Abner interrupts. “I ain’t finished yet!” The crowd roars in approval. “Go get ‘em, Ab!” An old timer in the back hollers out. “Oh, hush up!” his snappy wife nabs, smacking him with a pocketbook. “Ow!” The old man cries before sitting back in his seat. “Yes sir, that’s right…I’m sick of you and yer goddamn hippie n’ horseshit policies!” Quickly turning to see that the audience’s still behind him, Abner rants on. “And the hardworking people of this town…” he turns to face the audience, “well, anyway, the people of this town…” he gets another laugh, “don’t need you and yer half-assed policies enforced by a half assed-cop force, doin’ a half-asses job!” The major, who’s jacket is all ruffled up, struts out his chest and takes a step forward. “Now, that will be enough of you…Mr…Mr Abner! I’ll shall not have any more of it!” At this time, a few old men in the back are rolling on the ground in laughter, remembering the typical old scenario, being played out now, that Abner’s father forced on the mayor in the old days. “Any more of what, Mr. Mayor!?!” Abner asks in a demanding tone, with a snobbish stress on “mayor”, “Any more of the truth?” “But…” “Ah-ah-ah!” Abner puts a hand in the air and silences the mayor. “Now there’s no use for you to use such language at a public meeting, mayor.” “But…” Abner cuts him short again. “Now, now mayor, that’ll be enough…” By this time, the younger children are in tears. One cries out into between sobs, “Hahaha…the mayor…the mayor…he…he just said ‘butt’!” More people collapse in their chairs and tip their heads back in laughter as Abner keeps the pressure on. “But…” “Hole?” “But…” “Head?” The mayor, clenching his fists, “Why-why…” “…Are you a butthead?” “Why-why….why, this is absurd!” He slams his fist down on the podium and leaps off the stage. “Mr. Abner, if you don’t keep your mouth shut, I’ll have security move you this instance!” “Move! Move! Speaking of “move”, how bout I move you with some words? Huh, would you like that?” An ovation from the audience, and Abner nods his head, continuing on his war-word against the mayor. “Yeah, here it goes: if you threaten to have me reMOVED again, I’ll MOVE out of my chair, MOVE right up that aisle, MOVE my cow-shit stained boots…and by the way, cows say MOO!…and gently MOVE it straight up your dainty little ass, through your slim anus, pick up my boot, and MOVE you to other side of town at the docks whereas I’ll MOVE…” Abner blanks, scratching his hea, “..I’LL KICK YOUR ASS!” At this, the mayor jumps forward, arms flailing, and momentarily regaining control of himself, points an accusing finger at Abner, yelling, “Get him! Get him! Get him outta here! NOW! NOW! NOW!” He stomps up and down like a baby as two men in tuxes step forward. A grinning Abner also steps forward, his advantage being quite obvious. He’s easily got three or four inches and well over 40 pounds on both these guys, and for the moment, doesn’t seem to be going anywhere. The man to the mayor’s left, the smaller of the two, folds his expensive shades and places them in his coat pocket. “FIGHT! FIGHT! FIGHT!” The townspeople cry in unison. As the taller of the two steps forward, folding his arms over his chest, there’s a small rupture in the crowd as some of the women of the town scoop up their young children and sprint across the green in a hurry. Some stay, covering their children’s eyes, they wanting themselves to see, as others simply draw attention to the fight by grabbing their children by the arms and pointing (these actions, of course, are mostly perpetuated by the redneck families). “Danny!” With an outstretched hand, Abner shakes the hand of the smaller of the two. “Abner.” “Sean.” Abner nods his head toward the taller one. “Wh…what the….ooohhhh!” The frustrated mayor swings wildly and pushes his security team away. “What the hell are you doing!?!” The smaller one shrugs. “Havin’ a drink with my cuz…” “You’re…you’re related!?!” With a smile on his face, the one named Danny reaches out and accepts a Budweiser offered by Abner. The redneck tosses the taller man one too, and opening the cans, together, they chug down their respective beers, and then toss the cans aside. “This IS an outrage!” the mayor cries out, “…and this town certainly won’t stand for it!” And if on Q, the entire population of the town quickly take their seats, smiling up at their mayor. “Well, not without beer!” Abner retorts. And then, rolling out from behind the stage area, comes a tall, dark man dressed in a red and white stripped shirt. He’s pushing a long black cart which is filled to the brim with fresh, icy cans of beer. “BEER BRAWL!” The old man in the back cries out. And all of a sudden, with no time to react, the mayor is shoved aside by a mob of beer-lusting townsfolk, rushing to the side of the cart man. Unfortunately, another whack of his wife’s pocketbook silences the man in the back, and he’s forced to stay in his seat pouting as the rest get their fill. “Stop it, stop it, stop it!” The distressful mayor cries out to no avail. Reaching up with a shaky, wrangled fist he begins to rip at his flaky, graying hair as the mob continues to drink away. “Full bellies, and poisoned bloodstreams equal closed ears, Mr. Mayor, sir.” “Abner, I’ll have you jailed for this!” “What? For lettin’ people have a little fun?” “No, for starting a mob!” “I didn’t start it…” Abner puts his hands to his eyes and begins to peer around the audience”…that man did!” He points an accusing finger at a young ruffian, running by, Budweiser in hand. “Abner, he’s ELEVEN years old!” “Heh…startin’ younger these days!” “Ooohhh!” Shaking his fists he whirls around and attempts to grab the redneck by the shirt. But simply side-stepping it, the redneck backs off and allows the mayor to trip over his own feet, falling into the dirt. “Whoops! Better watch your step…” The mayor, mud covered and enraged, hops back to his feet, and with fire in his eyes, begins to stare the redneck down. “You’ll pay for this!” “Pay for this? What? I didn’t pay for this…the town’s tax-dollars did” The mayor, in shock, simply looks up at Abner, blinking, and doesn’t say a word. After several moments and a long, deep breath, he finally ushers out “w…wh…wh…what?” “Yeah, that’s right…the people paid for it. See, earlier this mornin’, ‘fore you folks showed up, we had ourselves a little, perfectly legal, referendum…now the people have spoken….soo….DEAL WITH IT!” The mayor has finally lost it. Swinging wildly, he connects with Abner’s jaw, sending the surprised hick reeling. Taking a few steps back, he begins to absorb the blows with ease. And in finally regaining his balance, Abner, simply sidesteps and dusks the blows, not even bothering to block or counter. The major sees that it’s useless, but the blows keep on coming. “Aww!” Abner yawns, sick of the attack and not feeling motivated to duck the next couple of blows. But they simply bounce harmlessly off his beer-belly, and the crowd is loving it. “Why you…” Abner suddenly doubles over, and rolling to the side, a hard punch by the mayor misses its mark, and again, he finds himself sprawled out on the ground lying in a puddle of mud. “You…you damned people!”, he cries out, hopping to his feet. “You miserable, wretched, disgusting, redneck PUKES!” But somewhere between “disgusting” and “redneck” Abner found his fist and rolled it into a ball…whipping back and letting it go directly into the chest of the mayor. “DING! DING! That’s number three mayor…you lose!” The man in the back cries out again. A few chuckles go out here and there as his wife, fed up and embarrassed, grabs a nearby brick, drops it into her bag, and clocks him in the head. The man goes unconscious and drops his head. “You all think it’s some sorta joke! Huh, you don’t take my job seriously? Well I’m an elected official goddamn it, and I’m not gonna tolerate this kind of behavior anymore…no more!” Grabbing the nearest man, the mayor gives him a thrashing, kicking him in the knee and letting him drop. With a sick, twisted smile of satisfaction on his face, he grabs another. But before he can choke him out, Abner is on top of him. He picks him up by the shoulders and tosses him aside. 'Alright boy, that's enough...' By this time, the drunken crowd has formed a circle around the two, waiting for the 'brawl' to begin. But as can be expected, a small town mayor wouldn't have much a chance to defeat, or even hang with, a PCWer...and nonetheless, a to-be Hardcore Champion! And so it goes, Abner has his way with him. A couple ducks, blocks and hip-tosses here and there, and the mayor lies huffing and puffing on the ground, while Abner, who's barely broken a sweat, stands above him, looking him in the eye. 'Damn it, boy, you made me hungry!' The crowd breaks out into another round of laughter.'Hey...Mayor MacCheese, got a burger?' More laughter. They've seen a lot in their years in Hicksville, but nothing like this. Sure, there's a few brawls here and there, and yeah, as you can tell, the town meetings are...interesting, but nothing on this magnitude. Up until now, no one has physically assaulted the mayor, and of course, the mayor have never physically assaulted a CITIZEN before either! But hey, that's Abner for you. Hardcore as hell, at both the physical, and psychological level. But suddeny, out of the depths of the mud and waste left from the intoxicated rednecks, the mayor somehow manages to get to his feet, and bolts across the circle of hicks. He manages ot make it to the stage, and darts to the podium. A loud screech echoes across the green, and momentarily, the rednecks cover their eyes as a piece of mud drops off from his chin, and onto the mic. Some rednecks fall to their knees and puke, while others bend over...and puke. The mayor angrily rips the mic out of the stand and raises it to his lips. His lips quiver and his knees tremble as he tightly gribs the mic. Before speaking, a deep weezing sound comes out of his throat, ecoing across the town. He regains his breath, anb the verbal war on the people of Hicksville is on. 'You miserable, filthy people. You've betrayed me...you've all betrayed me...wasting my precious...er...I mean, your precious...dollars on that vile, wretched liquid!' For this, he recieves a hailstorm of boos, and in come cases, death threats...'I'm sick and tired of you wasting your lives on such petty entertainment, spending your time doing useless activites, and occupying yourselves in such filth.. You all smell...you barely shower, and help me God, not a one of you speaks proper English!' Surprsingly, he gets a minor scatter of cheers for this last statement. 'Grr...see!?! ...You're just so damn ignorant, you're all ignorant! You and your shutguns, and fishing poles, and tobacco, and NASCAR...and, and Budweisers!' A resounding 'BUDWEISER KICKS ASS' chant, started by yours truly, inspires other to do the same, and soon, he's overwhelmed. Throwing in his head down in defeat 'I...I quit!!!' He tosses the mic down and falls to the floor and begins to bawl like a baby. By now, Abner makes his presence known on stage, and himself takes over the vacant spot at the podium. 'Citizens of Hicksville, on thsi historic occasion, the day that will be remembered the first time we firced and humiliated a perfectly good, fit mayor into leaving our town, but certainly not the last time...May, the tweny-eighth, 2003...I officially commemorate, and declare, forever more..as...HICKSVILLE MOTHER-FUCKIN' HOOTIN' N' HAPPENIN' BEER GUZZLIN GLORY JAMBOREE DAY!!!' But Abner, looking across the green, realizes that not a one of his fellow Hickvillians have heard this...for their, spread acorss the beautiful expensive of the green Vermont field, is a thousand drunk, hungover, and sleeping rednecks... ----You see, Abner's always been hardcore, and growing up in a town like this, you have to be. Bitches, beers and and blue-eyed queer hunting...the three B's of Hicksville...'----
