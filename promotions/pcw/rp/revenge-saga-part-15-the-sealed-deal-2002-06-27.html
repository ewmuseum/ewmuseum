Revenge Saga Part 15: The Sealed Deal...

27 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 27/6/02 Revenge Saga Part 15: The Sealed Deal… Fleming readied himself as the seven, fully armed, Russian soldiers, hesitantly made their way over to the designated battle area. Fleming just hoped that nothing went wrong with battle suits machinery, for the suit was made out of only the necessary materials to make it work, no materials for safety or anything like that. However, Fleming had to cut the costs, for the this deal to become vaguely profitable, so this was crunch time, precisely why he had decided to be the one controlling the battle suit. No mistakes could be made. Colonel Kolof: Fleming couldn’t understand Russian, but the way the seven Russian soldiers were now charging at him, he figured that it must have been the order to attack. The soldiers began to fire at the battle suit, however the bullets simply bounced off him like flies against a window. Fleming then pressed the red button, just left on the control sticks, and a revolving machine gun formed upon the right shoulder of the battle suit. Fleming pressed the fire button and simply kept his finger upon the button. All seven soldiers were ‘nullified’ instantly, and not for the first time that day, he began to wonder what went through the minds of the Russian officers. Earlier in the day, Colonel Kolof had informed him of the nature of the weapons testing. Fleming had never expected that the test would involve killing real people, but he could live with it. After all, it wouldn’t be the first time he had to kill and sure as hell wasn’t going to be the last. Fleming didn’t know whether to respect or hate, Kolof’s decision to send seven of his men to certain death. Colonel Kolof stood immediately after the machine disappeared from the battle suit’s shoulder, clapping at the battle suits performance. He walks from behind the bullet-proof protective glass, surrounding the porch area of the Zemof Mansion and onto the grassy battle grounds. Kolof circles the Fleming, who is deep within the bowels of the suit, and nods in approval. Colonel Kolof: Impressive performance Mr. Fleming! Fleming: Not really. No offence to your officers, but they were no match for this machine. Colonel Kolof: I realise that now, to be honest I didn’t think that the suit would be up to much, but I am now pleasantly surprised. Fleming: I see. This suit would easily quell attacks from tanks never mind ground troops, and is equipped with fast-tracking, anti-aircraft guns, which could easily destroy any aerial opposition that you may encounter. Colonel Kolof: [Smiling] Excellent! Well, Mr. Fleming it appears that we have ourselves a deal. Fleming: [Smiling] Great! This calls for a little celebration [ejecting himself from the battle suit.] ************ It had taken him the majority of the day, but Crichton had finally found the perfect gift for Jaden. The perfect gift was in the right price range, i.e. $50 or below, and he found it in the fifth jewellery shop he visited. The gift was a solid gold bracelet, and he envisioned it to look lovely upon Jaden’s wrist. He had the woman in the shop neatly wrap it and put a small pink bow around it for no extra cost whatsoever. He was confident that she would like the present, but whether they made up after it was another matter in itself. He didn’t even want to think about that though. He had also lasted more than an hour without being attacked by three crazed Chinese Triads, even though he realised that it was only a matter of time before they resurfaced for another attack. They told him that they had to keep coming for him, he suspected that if they went back to China without him, they would be killed. It was kind of a catch 22 situation for them because if they pissed him off much more, he would have to kill them. What was that feeling? I remember that feeling from somewhere, but where? He deliberated silently. It dawned on him, he sensed something was going to happen something bad. As if on cue, he then heard a screech of tires. He turns and sees a car coming right for him. Before he had a chance to move, the car had ploughed into him. As he hit the windscreen and toppled over the car’s roof he couldn’t help thinking, not for the first time that day, I do not need this shit! . Crichton hit the cold concrete floor hard, and scrapped all the side of his face upon the curb. He also had a pain coming from his ribs, he suspected that he had broken them, but he wasn’t sure. He is slowly trying to push himself up from the pavement, as the Chinese Triads step out of the car and head his way. All three of them stand there surrounding Crichton, as he fights to stay conscious and seems to be losing…badly. Triad 1: Well that seemed to have slowed him down! Triad 2: What now? Triad 3: What do you mean, ‘what now?’ Triad 2: Well what do we do, beat on him some more to make sure he’s unconscious or simply take him to consulate now? Triad 3: [Stroking his chin a little] we should make sure he remains unconscious, we don’t want any ‘nasty surprises’ while we’re driving. You two get the baseball bats. As the other Triads run back to the car, the third Triad kneels down and nudges Crichton a little. He then looks over at the progress of the two Triads, as Crichton flips onto his back, lightening fast. The Triad is taken of guard and in all probability shat himself by an equally lightening quick, directly on his jaw, sending him up at least a foot in the air. Crichton then executes a perfect kip-up and faces the other two Triads. One of the Triads, quickly grabs a baseball bat from the car and charges at Crichton, in a very suicidal manner. Crichton: [Sighing] These bastards never learn! Crichton swiftly side steps to the left and gets out of the way of the baseball bat blow, immediately replying with a stiff punch to the ribs, followed by a forearm across the face. Just as the Triad falls to the ground, Crichton receives a crack across the back of the neck by something hard. The blow makes him stumble forward, only stopping himself from falling over, by propping himself up on the wall. Crichton turns to see the baseball bat heading for right between the eyes. He manages to move his whole body to the left, narrowly avoiding the blow. Crichton responds with, what his father called, a ‘Glasgow Kiss’, or to everyone else a perfectly placed headbutt. The final Triad stumbles backwards, holding desperately to his now bloodied nose. Crichton strides confidently out from the wall and finishes the Triad off with a roundhouse spin kick. The Triad falls to the floor, almost in slow motion, unconscious before he even hit the ground. Crichton composes himself, stands up straight and brushes the dust from his knee long leather jacket. For the millionth time that day he sighed, before checking his coat pocket, to check that gift was still there. He felt small comfort upon finding that the gift was still there, but it was slightly better than not finding. Then he remembered the old saying, Hell have no fury like a woman scorned and smiled to himself, instantly thinking of Jaden. He was no all set to face the woman, but he was more scared to face her than an army of men. He knew one thing, he sure as hell wasn’t going to face her sober. So that left only one thing to do, find a bar! Crichton bent down and checked the Triads pockets. He pulls out a brown leather wallet and checks the insides. He extracts the twenty dollars and then heads off in search of a bar. ************ Fleming sat behind his temporary mahogany desk at the very luxurious Zemoff mansion, looking directly into the eyes of his current business partner, Colonel Kolof, who was sat directly opposite him, awkwardly positioned upon the stern wooden chair. Fleming noted the two sturdy looking soldiers stood at either side of Kolof, and suspected that they had been strategically placed in an attempt to intimidate him, but it wasn’t working that much. That was one of the main drawbacks of dealing with Russians; they always used intimidation during negotiations. Fleming: So, Colonel Kolof, shall we conclude the deal? Colonel Kolof: [Nodding] Agreed! There is only so longer I can stay in America without becoming severly ill. Fleming: [Getting a little angry, but trying to keep calm] I’m sorry to here that! Colonel Kolof turns to the Russian soldier to his left and whispers something into his ear. Totally unnecessary in Fleming’s mind, because he couldn’t understand a word of Russian anyhow. The soldier to his left, then produces a silver suitcase and slams it down in front of Fleming, before opening it up and revealing the contents before him. American Dollars. A LOT of American Dollars. Colonel Kolof: We will pay you half of the first instalment now and the rest when the battle suit arrives and from then on we will pay you monthly instalments, leading up to the agreed price. When one year is up, Russia will assess the battle suits and decide whether to continue our business. Is this agreeable to you? Fleming: [Nodding] That is agreeable with me Colonel. Colonel Kolof: [Rising from his seat and extending his hand to Fleming] A pleasure doing business with you, Mr Fleming!] Fleming: [Also rising and then shaking Kolof’s hand] And you colonel. Now Fleming had the capital and the means to strike back at John Crichton, and he intended to do so as fast and as efficiently as possible. As soon as Colonel Kolof, and his men, leave the room Fleming presses the intercom button. A faint crackle is heard on the other side. Assistant: Yes Sir. Fleming: Could you please arrange me a meeting with The Thunderbolts, Carla? Carla: Yes Sir. The first step of the plan was getting the Thunderbolts on board, and he knew that would happen, in a matter of time.
