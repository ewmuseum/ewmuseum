The Material Saga: Manual.

26 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7-26-02 DarkStar decided he needed some time to think, after he said goodbye to Nelson and Clyde he wandered towards his room, it was on the upper deck with the rest of the bigger rooms. He had to stop and ask for directions for Room #201, he eventually found it. Gods. He opened the door and walked in, Talena was already sitting on the couch unpacking her things. DarkStar looked over at his luggage and decided to leave it for now, he walked around the room in stunned amazement. The entire room was beautiful. It seemed that they threw black furniture in there to make him feel more at home (although the carpet was still a dark red), the TV was huge and it had a entertainment system. Talena was right about the bathroom, it had a hot tub, there was a pool table on the other side of the room, and to save room there was only one bed, big enough for at least four people. This was how to live. DarkStar smiled and flopped down next to Talena on the couch in the living part of the room. 'You should really unpack, apparently alot of us have to do really pointless promotion for the PPV, I think you and I have a interview with MTV of all people in a few hours.' DarkStars jaw dropped. Why was Talena concerned with this stuff? She seemed almost normal, like a damned publiscist. 'What?' DarkStar was hoping he heard things wrong, 'Hunny, it's a good oppurtunity, although we hate the medium in itself.' DarkStar agreed, of course, but it was just different. 'Talena... are you on your medication again??' She smirked but didn't answer the question, she leaned over and kissed DarkStars forhead, leaving a faint purple lipmark. She continued unpacking as DarkStar stood up, he grabbed the bag that had most of his clothing and opened it up. He unpacked the vulgar t-shirts and the fishnets, the big pants and the leather, the backup metallic piercings, and he put it all in its respective drawers. He looked in the mirror, his make up needed a touch up. 'I need to change, my clothes are starting to have a mind of their own. He grabbed the other suitcase and walked into the bathroom. He stripped down to his, disturbingly also gothic, boxers and opened the luggage. Great. He flinched as he saw there were only two choices in this case, he obviously grabbed the wrong one. He made a face when he went from the 'Fuck' Shirt that he has made so popular over the last year and the black dress shirt, gray tie, and dress trench coat. What was he turning into. This fashion thing, it was never about the fashion. The clothing made him happy. Now he was in the public eye though... Would wearing a suit and sucking up to some people really be that bad. He glanced back to the suitcase and grabbed his fuck shirt, sliding it on over his head and pulling his baggy black light pants on. Fuck fitting in. He wasn't their mainstream whore. He went to open the door, but something in his mind stopped him. 'Come on, it wouldn't hurt to play up to them a little, think of the exposure, think of the chances at becoming more famous then you already are that are floating on this boat.' DarkStar now realized why split personalities were such a bitch. He tried not to listen, but he could feel himself putting on the dress trench coat, zipping it up and straigtening it, he could feel himself putting better make up on, touching it up instead of leaving it with the Robert Smith look. He could feel himself abandoning what he felt before. He looked in the mirror and saw himself from the eyes of the population that made him what he is, that made him famous, that made him as big as a rock star, he saw himself as a handsome, successful, gothic role model. Someone that was marketable both in and out of the ring. Someone with millions of dollars in the bank and smart investments, someone who knew when to be himself and when to play it up for the camera. He was Corporate. It was about time he acted like it. Tyler was slowly becoming more and more like him and Clyde, and without Tylers business savvy, who did they have to control that aspect of the battle? The whole light vs darkness thing sure is fun, but what will they do in a year when its all over, how will they succeed, DarkStar was hell bent on making sure that no matter what, he would still be a rock star. Then he saw himself in the mirror again, and he realized this wasn't him. This, this person he had become, worried about investments and social standing, fame and fortune, forgetting that it was all about Evil vs Good, this was exactly what he had preached against for years. He reared his hand back and shattered the mirror with one blow. Talena hopped up and ran into the bathroom, DarkStar growled. 'Lover? You nearly woke up Emily, whats a matter?' He looked at the shattered pieces of glass around the room. 'This isn't us.' She was confused and he hugged her, the music she was listning to became apparent, and it made DarkStar shudder just a little. So you're at war with yourself, with space and time you'll find a breakthrough, if you don't lose what's real, you have our blessing, clear your conscience and ride away. He let go of Talena and kissed her passionately, a long, smoldering kiss. The entire time his mind was racing, the view pulled out to look at them from outside of the bathroom door. Truthfully though, he straightened the collar of the trench coat and smoothed the creases in the pants. Maybe being like this wasn't so bad. Talena pulled DarkStar down to the floor, and the black door swung shut, closing the chapter for now. fin
