Timmy boy will learn.

13 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/13/2003 ~Friends. Friends in a wrestler's life are few and far between. You have a million acquaintances, but few really good friends. Jon Kano has been lucky, he has made some of his best friends in the wrestling industry. He has had and still has friendships with some of PCW's legends including The Phantom Booker, Chris Sloboda, and Duke Williams. While The Phantom Booker and Chris Sloboda have left PCW, Duke Williams and Jon Kano are still gainfully employed by PCW, at least for the moment. Duke and Jon have many things in common. They live life to the fullest. They take chances. They are both, by definition, alcoholics. What a fine existence these two live.~ *The scene opens up in Jon Kano's living room in his vast, 32 room private villa that he shares with his lovely wife, Kristin, which overlooks Las Vegas, Nevada, Kano's hometown and the site of this week's Friday Fusion. Jon Kano, dressed in a 'I MADE PCW' t-shirt and baggy blue jeans, is seated in front of his 60 inch flat screen plasma television, watching CNN. Suddenly, Kano's doorbell is rung. Kano begins to stand up, but his wife, dressed in tight jeans and an oversized 'Kano ROCKS~!' t-shirt beats him to the door. She opens the door and hugs the visitor, the one, the only, 'Malice Man' Duke Williams. Duke walks into Kano's living room and Kano stands for his long time friend. The two living legends shake hands and sit down, Kano onto his black leather couch with Kristin and Duke in a matching chair perpendicular to the couch.* Kano: Duke, you shoulda swung by earlier, Chris was here. He was in town on a little business today. He swung by and we had a good conversation. Duke: Damn, I woulda loved that, boss man. Kristin: What are you two going to have to drink today? *Duke looks a little surprised.* Duke: Jon, no wonder you married this woman. *All three smile a little.* Kano: Duke, what are you gonna have, my man? Duke: Same as you, hoss. Kano: Honey, get us, uhhh, a bottle of tangueray, two glases, and tonic water. Kristin: Alright, honey. *Kristin leaves to get the booze.* Kano: Big week this week, Duke. Duke: For both of us. Kano: Bigger than you can imagine. You are going to get the strap at March Madness, I can feel it. Duke: And you are back here in Sin City, must feel good. Kano: Feels great. In the last month, I have been here 24 days. Do you realize all of last year, I was only here 60 days? Jesus, it feels pretty good to be pissed off at the company. Duke: I hear that, hoss. Kano: Nice way of disposing of that little rat bastard Walden on Tuesday. Duke: What the hell was up with you losing to Young Tiger? Kano: I just wanted to put him over. I don't care anymore. I gave him a few lumps and I just wanted him to get a little bump up from me. He is a good kid. He trains hard, he's got heart, and he's got character. Duke: But you know he will never get anywhere here. Kano: Yeah because of the bullshit that goes on in PCW. *Kristin now returns with a tray, complete with all the aforementioned alcoholic materials. Kano pours Duke a drink, then himself. The two men toasts each other.* Kano: Here's looking up your old address. *The two men take a drink.* Kristin: Jon, aren't you fighting Timmy on Fusion? Duke: Who the hell is Timmy? Kano: Tim Walden. She always called him Timmy, that was when he wanted to learn from one of the best teachers in this game, me. You know Duke, we are part of a business that often mixes personal business with professional business. Duke: Yeah. How well I know. Kano: I just can't get over him. He came to me when he first got into PCW and asked me to teach him how to become truly great. He might think that I didn't teach him a lot, but I did. I took him out in the middle of nowhere, and made him go to his limits of his mind, body, and soul. Timmy became a man that weekend. He hated me from 6 am Friday morning to 9 pm Sunday night. He had no sleep, he was on the move most of the time. He called me everything those three days. But on that final night, I lit a fire and I taught him the greatest lesson he will ever learn. I taught him about that fire that burns within every truly great competitor in professional wrestling. After all that, he lost his first match back. That was what this training was designed to do, because after that, it taught you how to deal with defeat. He became better and better with each match, regardless of the outcome. But Tim got a big head. Duke: And he aligned himself with a bunch of assholes. Kano: He also decided that his teacher wasn't shit. I let him run his mouth all he wanted, then we got in the ring. I beat him. A little while later, he made excuses. He wanted another shot to kick his teacher's ass and well, hell, lets just watch the final couple minutes of that. *Kano fumbles around with the remote control and up pops up a scene from Kano/Walden's match at Supremacy 2002, Kano hits play and away we go: In the ring it is just Kano and Walden. Kano is still down, so Walden quickly rushes over and goes for the quick pin. 1………2……………………………………………….(NO!) Right in the nick of time Kano kicks out which start up a huge cheer from the fans. Thhe KANO ROCKS~! Chant begins again and Kano hops up pounding his chest getting ready to fight. Walden tries to run out of the ring, but Kano grabs him right by the head, keeping him in their. Duke: No place to run Walden, you have to stay their and take it like a man. Jeff: I can’t watch. Dave: Kano has fought off numerous men and is still on top. This is incredible, I don’t know how he’s doing it, but he is. Duke: What the heck KANO ROCKS~! KANO ROCKS~! KANO ROCKS~! Kano is suplexing Walden all over the ring. He then tries out some martial art kicks leaving Walden senseless not even knowing where he is. Kano is getting so pumped that his strength multiples. He picks Walden up over him head and starts spinning until he lets Waldan g and fly into the stands. Dave: What a throw by Kano, Walden has to be finished. Duke: This one shouldn’t be much longer. Jeff: No way, Walden will make the comeback, you’ll see just wait. Walden isn’t move on the outside and the fans are crowding around him. Kano goes to get him, but when he reaches the area, Walden has disappeared. Dave: Where did he go? Jeff: He must have done the smart thing and gotten out of there. Duke: I wouldn’t be surprised if the pansy did. Suddenly, Walden appears behind Kano. He taps Kano on the shoulder, and punches him straight in the face wearing brass knuckles. Dave: OMG! Walden must have just broken Kano’s nose Jeff: I didn’t think it was possible for him to get uglier but he did. Walden continues to punch Kano with the brass knuckles until Kano is practically knocked unconscious. Walden starts celebrating and puts his foot on the chest of Kano for the pin. The referee tries to get through the crowd, but it seems like the crowd isn’t letting him through. Crowd: KANO ROCKS~! Crowd: DOWN WITH UA. The crowd exchanges chants, as the members of the crowd start getting violent and not letting the referee get through. Jeff: Where is security? Dave: The fans have had enough Walden notices and gets upset, so he pushes through the crowd and drags the referee to where Kano is lying down. Walden then puts his foot on Kano for the pin. Jeff: This one is over. 1………….2……………………..……………………..NO! Right as the referee is about to make the three count a fan comes out of nowhere and takes out Walden. Walden throws the fan off and the rest start to get rowdy. Inn just a few short moments about twenty fans are all mobbing Walden in the defense of Kano. Dave: THE FANS HAVE HAD ENOUGH OF UA. Duke: REVOLUTION! Walden runs from the crowd in a circle but ends up right in the arms of Jon Kano, who unleashes the force of the KANOPLEX. Crowd: KANO ROCKS~! KANO ROCKS~! KANO ROCKS~! Jeff: NOOOOOOOOOOOOO!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! Duke: YEEEEEEEEEEESSSSSSSSS!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! Kano goes for the pin. 1……………………………2………………………………………………………………………………3 Clay: The winner of the match and #1 contender to the Continental Title…….JON KANO. Kano grabs the remote and turns the whole system off.* Kano: Damn, that is still something that Tim hates to see. He brought out all of his guns, his gay little buddies from West Hollywood, and every move in his arsenal. I took a beating and I arose victorious. Tim will always hate me for that one match, because it showed one thing, that I am exactly what I say I am, I am WHAT REAL LEGENDS ARE MADE OF!!!!!! *The scene fades to black as Kano and Duke finish their drinks. Kano whispers something in Duke's ear and Duke has a surprised look on his face.* Duke: You are going to do what at March Madness? *The scene fades completely fades to black.*
