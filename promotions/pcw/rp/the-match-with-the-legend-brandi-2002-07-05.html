The match with the legend, BRANDI

5 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7/5/02 “I am ready! I can do this! I will win! I am unstoppable!” Jaden repeated this in her mind before her match with Brandi, the PCW legend. “The following match is scheduled for one fall! Introducing first, hailing from Baltimore, Maryland and weighing in at 137 pounds, the lovely JADEN!” As Jaden enters the ring standing at the top of the ramp she is pleased to her the cheers from the crowd calling her name. “Jaden.” It is no more than a whisper at first, however it is loud enough for her to hear it. As she draws closer to the ring the chant of her name begins to get louder, and faster. “JADEN! JADEN! JADEN!” She walks out with a smile on her face, this is very unusually for her especially when she begins her matches. She usually has her game face on but today is a different day, unlike none other. She will be facing a legend, better known, as Brandi. Jaden believes that by facing Brandi this will be her big break. The word famous flashes before Jaden’s eyes because that’s what she will be and if not she will make Brandi more famous then she ever was. How? Jaden has her ways of getting what she wants. If she cannot get it, she will do what it takes to make you suffer with her. “JADEN! JADEN! JADEN!” The voice cries out again but even louder but it is interrupted by a kiss on her forehead. “Did I wake you?” John asks Jaden, glad to see his girl in bed beside him. “No!” Jaden begins to yawn from her pleasant dream. She had a feeling the dream was not what it was set out to be. The crowd was cheering her own. Why would they do that? She has not won a match since she has been in PCW, however, she has helped other win matches like her boyfriend John Crichton. “I am up. I need to talk to you about my match tonight.” “What about?” “I want to win tonight. This will be a big break for me. Beating Brandi pinning her to the mat is all I ask for. I know you can pull some strings somehow and help me win.” “I guess I could but don’t…” “Shit John! All I ask of you is this one simple task just help me out if I am falling short. I did that for you. How do you think you got that belt around you waist? You would have never gotten it with out help! Just do the damn thing so we can go out and celebrate on me this time.” “Ok, if that’s what you want. I will try. When you win well will go to a bar and drink to we pass out so bring you purse this time I don’t want to hear any excuse from you this time.” “Err-umm…no let’s not do the bar scene tonight maybe something else. I have to go wash up I have a match in a few hours!” Later on that night… Friday Fusion… “I am ready! I can do this! I will win! I am unstoppable!” (CROWD begins to call out Jaden’s name repeatedly) “Maybe my dream was real. I might have a real shot of winning this match,” Jaden says aloud, unaware that Brandi is behind her. “You, a jobber, beating me, I doubt that! I do not know what they were thinking when they gave you a PCW contract. How about I do you a favor. I wont beat you that bad just enough to make the crowd jump. See you in the ring!” With that said, Brandi brushes passed Jaden laughing and making a mockery of Jaden as if she is worthless. Jaden face lights up with hatred. She is ready for her match with Brandi. She plans to make her bleed and suffer. Whatever it takes to win, she will do it. “OH THIS WILL BE HELL FOR YOU AND JUST CALL ME THE DEVIL BECAUSE THIS IS ABOUT TO GET UGLY!”
