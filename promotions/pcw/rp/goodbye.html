Goodbye

Date: 29/05/03 - Nerezza&#039;s 1st birthday A mass of spectators gather in their seats, sitting patiently for their performer to arrive. Although this is not the PCW arena – Instead of the famous 20x20 feet of canvas and rope, a massive red velvet curtain hangs in front of them, and a microphone stand waits alone on a shiny black stage. The lights of this building dim as a spotlight searches for something, someone… the curtains part and now fully in the spotlight, although being blinded by it, Nascosto Nerezza appears, bowing his head from the light. A roar of applause greets the dapper-dressed vampire, clad in a dark red tuxedo with a lacy, ruffled shirt and pristine white gloves and matching trousers. His purple hair is tied back into a ponytail and for once, his red circular sunglasses are nowhere to be seen. He approaches the microphone and the spotlight dims a little as he reaches out and plucks it from the stand. In front of the stage, a musical conductor, illuminated slightly by some light spilling off the stage, taps his baton a few times, then a classic tune begins to play. Nerezza places his hands in front of his chest, fingers interlocked and a rather heavy look weighs his face down, like a man facing his death sentence. He parts his lips as the music reaches the correct point, and begins… “And now… The End is near…” A small mass of grayish hair appears from the parting in the curtains and Skylar Green pokes his head through the curtains behind Nerezza, then quickly ducks behind. Nerezza turns around with a scowl on his face and keeps looking at the curtains to make sure Skylar doesn’t appear again. “And so I face… my final curtain…My friend… I’ll say it clear, I’ll state my case of which I’m certain…I’ve lived… un-life that’s full…” Nerezza notices the curtains twitching again and stops for a moment, diving inside his tuxedo pocket and pulling out a road map, flings it towards the moving waves of velvet. A small “OW!” is heard, then the motions stop. “I’ve traveled each and every highway… but more… much more than this… I did it myyyyyyy waaaaaaaay.” At this point, NTICW’s former boss Evan Levine steps out onto the stage, waving a home-made banner that reads “NWCTI SI IXTREEM!” Nerezza buries his face in his palms in embarrassment of his former company, then continues the song. “Regrets… I’ve had a few… but then again… too few to mention.” The vampire pulls a sign of his own out of his tuxedo jacket and shows it to the audience and Evan, who breaks down in tears when he sees the writing, which happens to be “I QUIT!” “I did… what I had to do… and saw it through… without exemption.” Nerezza turns the sign around, which now reads “For PCW!” “I planned each charted course… each careful step along the byway…” The Living Nightmare runs across the stage and stomps on Evan’s fingers, then calmly walks back to the middle of the stage with an apologetic smile as Evan winces and limps off, stage-right. “But more… much more than this… I did it myyyyyy waaaaaaaaaaaaaaay.” From the direction Evan disappeared, the Neo Void God, Akira Akiyama strides onto the stage and walks behind Nerezza, who is completely unaware of the vinyl-clad warrior’s presence. He looks towards the vampire with a foul grin and advances towards him, raising his hands in a predatory manner. “Yes, there were tiiiimes, I’m sure you knew. When I bit off… more than I could chew.” Akiyama scoops Nerezza up in a Reverse Death Valley Driver position, then swings him around and floors him with a one-handed Powerbomb, getting a giant gasp from the audience in shock. “But through it all, when there was doubt… I ate it up…” Nerezza quickly spits a blob of sticky purple fluid into Akiyama’s eyes, who staggers through the curtains, temporarily blinded. Nerezza remains on the floor, still singing but looking rather messed up with his hair out of place and jacket pulled up around his upper body. “And spit it out.I faced it all… and I stood tall…” Nerezza struggles to his feet and stands at his full 6’7” height, puffing out his chest. “And did it myyyyyyyyyyyyyy waaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaay.” Out of nowhere and in the biggest shock of the year, the blue-haired Anarchy who has been missing since God knows when peeks out of the side of the stage, causing a sea of camera flashes to go off. She then runs to the curtains to help her blinded father, Akiyama. Nerezza catches sight of her, then adjusts his clothing and continues, staring at her with an enchanted look in his face. “I’ve loved…” From the same side she appeared, Byron Calver chases after her, followed by Ellie~!, who has half a handcuff on her wrist and a hacksaw in her other hand. She catches up to Byron and begins to slap him, then the foursome of Akiyama, Anarchy, Byron and Ellie~! vanish stage left. “And laughed…” Save for Ellie~!, who points at Nerezza accusingly and delivers the Prep Slap O’ Doom. Nerezza collapses to the floor and Ellie~! puts one foot on him, pinning him and counting… rather slowly… to three. Nerezza looks up, with Ellie~! completely unaware that he can see right up her skirt. “And cried… I’ve had my fill… my share of losing.” The Prep joins the others off-stage and Nerezza once again struggles to his feet. “And now… as the tears subside… I found it all…” Nerezza reaches inside his jacket pocket again and pulls out a photo of Ellie~! in lingerie from the covers of Now That’s What I Call Bad Entrance Themes Volume 8. “So amusing.To think… I did all thatAnd may I say - not in a shy way, Nerezza tosses the photo into the audience, causing a bunch of young men in the front row to dive and compete for the picture. 'No, oh no not me…I did it myyyyyyy waaaaaaaaaaaay'. Skitzo then appears on stage right, dragging a trash bag behind him with the remains of Gynaeco Joe seen through the clear plastic, as well as his dresses and wigs from the time he was stuck in a lesbian mind-frame. “For what is a man? What has he got? If not himself…” Skitzo slings the bag behind the curtains and dusts his hands off, then shudders as his muscles bulge and another Skitzo appears to “pop” from his back, which then gives him a Hi-5. The pair put each other’s arms around the other’s shoulders and look towards the audience, giving the thumbs up. “Then he has naught.To say the things… he truly feels…” Gaz Green appears from behind the curtain, holding up a sign, which reads “STEPHEN HAWKING IS A CABBAGE!” “And not the words… of one who kneels.” Gaz then drops the sign and disappears backstage, running back with a man in a wheelchair, then tipping it forwards and forcing him onto his knees. An electronic voice from behind Nerezza squeals out – OH CRAP, THE GREEN GUY AGAIN. THE POLICE WILL HEAR ABOUT THIS! Nerezza turns around, then nods to Gaz, who unplugs Stephen Hawking’s voice box and grins sadistically. “The record shows” Nerezza tears off his tuxedo jacket and shirt to show a pile of scars on his chest, presumably where people have tried to ram wooden stakes into him. “… I took the blows” - The music builds up with a drum roll as all the previous people interrupting the performance line up on the stage, including Anarchy, who once again causes a huge synchronized flash of cameras. Nerezza, standing in the middle, transforms into his full vampire shape, then spreads his wings wide and he finishes off the classic song… “AAAAAAAND DID IT…. MYYYYYYYYYYYYYYYYYYYYYYYYYYYYYYYYYYYY WAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAY!” ~ Thanks to everyone who made Nerezza who he is. Hessian for making him the “man” he wasn’t (exactly one year ago to this day), Angelo Deville for being Nerezza’s first enemy, Gaz Green for being his only friend, Redemption Song for our first tag match, Xavier Kannon for the first big shot, The Schmoopies for our most enjoyable feud and PCW for giving Nerezza something to hold his jeans up with five times. Matt Jay
