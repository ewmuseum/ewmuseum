Playin dominoes and talkin shit. Core and Rush is gonna get they wigs split.

19 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/19/02 {Damn....things can change in a hurry, can’t they? One minute things are running smoothly with big Ty at the helm of PCW, the next minute things are going to hell with the redneck in charge. What’s a nigga to do? The only thing he knows how to do...and that’s kick some muthafuckin ass. Since ol Ty helped tha OG out a bit, it’s only fair that he returns the favor. It’s made all that much easier since the targets are a couple of punk ass muthafucka’s named Rush and Core. Simple enough.....and tha OG even has a badass fuckin partner in tha Swamp Dawg. He’s been on lockdown, same as tha OG, so they think the same way. Kick ass first, don’t even worry about taking names.} Omaha, Nebraska....hometown to Orpheus Grant, tha OG. Wintertime is usually a cold son of a bitch in Omaha, but this year has been pretty mild. Only snowed like two or three times, with temps generally in the tolerable range for the time of year. Today the sky is overcast, looking like it wants to do something but can’t figure out what. We soar around the city of Omaha, the aerial view somewhat majestic as it passes over the Missouri River, then by the Woodmen of the World building which is currently the tallest in the city. Nearby that is the building that plans on taking it’s place for that honor, the still under construction Federal Building. Amazing what the government can find to spend money on instead of giving it back to the muthafuckas that’s giving it to them. Anyway, we continue through the drab gray sky and start to enter North Omaha. North O is the home of the majority of the African American population in the city. It’s also one of the most financially deprived areas in the city as well. As we travel farther north we move into rougher and more run down neighborhoods, typical of any city’s ghetto. Right around the 24th and Lake area we swoop down from the sky, running along the street up to a beat up white house that looks like it could fall any minute. There is plywood over the spots where the front windows used to be, and the front screen door barely hangs on by one hinge. The front porch is covered by an overhang, but roughly half of the porch is rotted away revealing the bare dirt underneath. Getting closer to the front door we can hear music coming from inside, as well as several voices talking over the tunes. Moving inside the house we find that it is in much better shape than the outside would suggest. The home is lavishly furnished with soft leather furniture in the living room, featuring a couch and chair with ottoman. The finely polished hardwood floors glint from the track lighting that runs along the ceiling. A large tiger print area rug covers most of the living room, with the head resting and facing toward the huge 60 inch big screen television. The walls are painted in a flat white with various paintings and photos hanging carefully throughout. The music that we hear is provided by a sophisticated tower stereo system situated in the corner of the living room. “Me Against the World” by 2-Pac is the current selection being played. As the view floats through the living room we come to the dining room, which is decorated equally as fancy as the living room. A plush tan carpet covers this room, with a glass top table in the center of the room underneath an expensive looking crystal chandelier. Sitting at the table, playing dominoes, is tha man himself Orpheus Grant. He has a 40 oz of Olde English “8 Ball” sitting in front of him next to his bones. Also seated at the table, starting from the left of OG, are Greedy, Mookie, and Big L. Each man is dressed in some variation of designer clothing whether it be Timberland, Sean John, Hilfiger, Polo, or whatever. OG simply wears a pair of black leather pants with tan Timberland boots and a white wife beater tank top undershirt. There is a pattern of dominoes already laid out on the table as OG looks over the ones in front of him. “I ain’t got shit, man” says OG disgustedly. He reaches over and pulls a domino from the extra pile and looks it over, a slight grin crossing his lips. “Hell yeah.” Orpheus puts the domino at one end of the pattern on the table and picks up his 40 oz, taking a long pull. “Damn!” exclaims Greedy as he throws his head down in disappointment. “I was gonna point big time off that spot!” Orpheus just smiles and shrugs his shoulders. Greedy goes back to studying his bones. Across the table from OG sits Mookie, his afro picked out to it’s fullest. The slightly built man sits casually in his chair, sliding about halfway down to where he is almost eye level with the table. Mookie’s eye lids are heavy as he lazily stares into space. Over his right ear is a home rolled cigarette, it’s contents will be left to the imagination. “Boooyyyyy,” starts Mookie slowly. “I dunno ‘bout you OG, but you bet make sure that Ty handles his business in that PCW, man. I like this high livin shit.” “You high in more than one way, nigga” quips Orpheus. Greedy and Big L share a laugh as Mookie continues to stare off in a chronic induced daze. “Naw, fo real do....he paid fo all this sweet ass shit. We need ta keep that bankroll comin,” says Mookie. “I can buy as much weed as I want and not have to go to the pawn shop to hock my TV.” Orpheus frowns at his stoned friend, then dismisses the comment. “Look nigga, I don’t need ta help Ty Nelson do shit. OG don’t owe nobody nothin, and I sho nuff don’t need that cracka ta make some ends.” A couple of surprised looks from the men around the table as Orpheus speaks confidently. “I choose ta help out ol Ty ‘cause it benefits tha OG. When it ceases ta benefit my black ass, then he may just get his muthafuckin wig split,” says Orpheus rather defiantly. “Ty ain’t a bad guy an all, but tha OG can’t be played like no bitch ya know what I’m sayin nigga?” Mookie lazily responds. “Heeeell yeahhhh, niggaaa.” Finally Greedy throws down a domino and claps his hands together. “That’s what I’m talkin’ bout, nigga. Count that shit up!” he says excitedly. Orpheus, Big L, and to a lesser extent Mookie all look down at the table. Each man gets a rather peculiar look on his face. “Five?” questions Big L. “Five?” asks OG. “Damn.....I can’t see straight,” says Mookie. “What’s that nigga got?” Orpheus looks over at Greedy who is staring down with a confused look on his face. “You got all Goddamn happy about five muthafuckin points? Is you trippin on some of Mookie’s shit?” “I ain’t shared my shit with that sorry ass, no countin nigga!” blurts Mookie. Greedy swipes at Mookie with his left hand, coming nowhere near his friend. “Shut tha fuck up, nigga.” Several seconds later Mookie dodges the swing. Greedy pounds on the table in frustration. “Damnit! I fucked that all up!” “You sho nuff did!” exclaims Mookie as he slams his last domino down on the glass table, almost shattering it. “DOMINO MUTHAFUCKAS!” “Fuck!” “Shit!” “Goddamn Greg!” Mookie sinks a little lower in the chair, smiling big at his victory. “I told y’all punks that ya couldn’t beat me in no dominos. Pay this nigga his muthafuckin money!” Bills start to fly in from three different directions as Mookie does his best to collect them all. He finally scoops them all up in a handful and stuffs them in his pocket. “That’s dinner at the Golden Corral, baby.” As Mookie revels in his win, Big L looks over to OG. “So what’s this shit ya got on Tuesday? I saw in the TV guide that you got a match with some punks in the Malice Crew.” “Yeah,” says OG. “Me and that crazy muthafucka LeBlanc kicked they asses last week and so they cried to that redneck Duke Williams fo a match wit us. Dumb ass bitches.” “What’s up wit that LeBlanc fool anyway?” says Greedy. “Seems like he’s just a flat out psycho muthafucka.” “Heh,” chuckles Orpheus. “He pretty much is. Kicked the shit outta some fan a few weeks back and got himself suspended. With him being a con like me I figured that we should hook up and do some real ass kicking, prison style.” “What....you gonna have a soap on a rope match?” mocks Mookie. Greedy and Big L laugh at the joke, as does OG to a certain extent. “Naw muthafucka, that’ll be a special three way between me, LeBlanc, and yo scrawny ass.” OG and Big L exchange high fives as Mookie laughs for a second, then realizes what Orpheus just said. His face sobers and he sits up a bit in the chair. “That’s wrong, nigga,” says Mookie. “That’s just wrong.” OG gets up from his chair and walks into the living room, plopping down on the comfortable black leather sofa. “I’m just playin, nigga,: concedes OG. Mookie calms and a smile comes back across his face. “I knew that, playa.” “I’d let those homo’s Rush and Core get a piece of you,” ribs OG. “I would kick they sorry asses, nigga,” says Mookie soberly. “And you know that.” “Who couldn’t?” questions OG rhetorically. “They is just two little bumps in the road fo the convict express to run over. If it helps Ty out in the process, then fine. But I gots my own agenda these days. I’m lookin out fo tha OG. As long as Nelson continues to be a nice meal ticket then he’s cool wit me. After that, he bet watch his muthafuckin back.” “Amen ta that, nigga,” responds Big L. OG leans over and grabs the remote for the big screen from the end table, switching on the set. Currently showing is ‘Scarface’ starring Al Pacino. Orpheus watches the movie intently. “That’s a bad ass cracka right there. But his momma wouldn’t recognize him when I was done wit him, ya heard?” The scene begins to fade out with the sounds of automatic gunfire being heard from the television. Right before we fade totally out we hear Tony Montana shout one of the most famous movie lines ever. “I kill you fuckin cockaroaches!”
