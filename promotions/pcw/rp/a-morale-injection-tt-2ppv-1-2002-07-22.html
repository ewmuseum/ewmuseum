A Morale Injection (TT#2PPV#1)

22 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7-22-02 The top of a large, oval-shaped, oak desk is shown cluttered with stacks of documents, maps, and diagrams. Many sets of hands handle the papers and make gestures as their owners chatter amongst themselves. The many voices join to make an unintelligible murmur which raises and lowers in tone every other second. After approximately 30 seconds of chatter, a door squeaks open and everyone at the table stands up quickly. We rise from the top of the table to see a dozen Argentinean men--half dressed in business suits and the other half dressed in military uniforms. Each man bows his head and salutes in a distinctive cross-armed finger pointing gesture, which signifies El Partido de la Pantera de Argentina's policy of offense as its defense. As all men are saluting, El Presidente walks through the door with a scowl on his face and a half-smoked Cuban cigar in his mouth. Behind him are Asklepios and two advisors. Frisco stands to the side to allow his associates to take their seats. Asklepios adjusts his toga to sit, but is quickly nudged by the advisors to join in the salute. The mental patient eventually obliges and Frisco finally puffs and throws his hand in a dismissal motion as he barks. Frisco: 'Alright already, for fuck's sake! We have important matters to attend to. Stop this stalling and let's get to work!' (The politicians and generals cease their saluting and begin to sit down. Frisco snaps and opens his mouth to yell, which causes his cigar to fall on the floor.) Frisco: 'I did not say sit down, you inconsiderate pig felchers!' (The room apologizes and stays standing. Frisco looks at his cigar burning a hole in the floor and sighs deeply. After a few seconds of stewing in his own anger, Frisco erupts.) Frisco: 'I--Is anyone going to pick that up? Dios fucking mio! This is exactly why I'm the underdog going into the matches on Turmoil and WrestleVessel! None of you assholes are loyal and all you're doing is thinking of yourselves! This puts all the pressure on me to pick up all of my own trash and not to mention all the idiotic crap you people do on a regular basis. All the responsibility that is thrust upon our party and country is not being assumed by the well-paid, and supposedly highly-skilled men I have in my cabinet. What in the name of God do you shitbrained morons do all day? Because it sure as hell isn't helping me! If it were, I wouldn't be so goddamned stressed today! And I damn sure would be back to my prime and ready to whip the living shit out of my opponents on a regular basis!' (Frisco breathes heavily and looks around at his subjects, who hang their heads.) Frisco: 'Things are going to change around here, pendejos. No more of this lax attitude that has been getting us...me!...nowhere. My campaign has the momentum of a paralyzed slug in a vise and polls have me losing by a substantial margin! Yes, I said losing! I DON'T LOSE, YOU ASSHOLES! Did you not get the fucking memo!?' (Frisco begins to rub his temples.) Frisco: 'Oh, and this whole wrestling business. That jackass Big Alan Steele is still walking around with my title. Did you hear that? My motherfucking cocksucking asslicking--....!!!!!' (Frisco twitches slightly and tries to crack a sincere smile.) Frisco: '....precious Primetime Championship Wrestling World Heavyweight...' (Frisco grits his teeth and begins to shake just before he explodes again.) Frisco: 'CHAMPION-MOTHERFUCKING-SHIP ASS MOTHERFUCKING BELT!!!' (Frisco picks up his burning Cuban cigar and flings it at his Secretary of Strategic Strategies and Banana Sandwiches.) Frisco: 'If I don't win this next match, I'm firing half of the people in this room! And if I don't win the World Title on Sunday, I'm firing every single one of you!' (Asklepios frowns.) Asklepios: 'There is a hole in your logic, my dear angry God. I am not paid by you, therefore not employed by you. I cannot be fir--...' Frisco: 'Shut up! Shut up! Shut up!' (Frisco grabs a coffee mug from the table and throws it straight at Asklepios' head, who ducks out of the way and watches it shatter against a Mao-esque banner of Frisco on the South wall.) Frisco: 'I can fire you by sending you back to the nuthouse, so don't think you're not exempt!' (Asklepios lowers his head. Frisco paces around and tries to calm himself down.) Frisco: 'Gentlemen, this is tough love and you will understand in time. I am not blaming any single person in this room and I'm sure many of you have done your jobs well. However, this party is not proving to be an effective, productive group. This is evident by my campaign and current career sagging heavily.' (Frisco quickly frowns and points at his men.) Frisco: 'And don't even think for a second that this is remotely my fault. My skills are not deteriorating! I am better than I ever was. My charisma, intellect, and overall strategies in my work are still set permanently in place. You all are just loading so much stress onto me that I can no longer function the way I am meant to.' (Frisco heads to the end of the table and pulls his own chair out forcefully. He sits in his chair, puts his handsome face in his hands and peers at his standing party members.) Frisco: 'You may be seated.' (The men finally take their seats after receiving their verbal abuse. Frisco reaches for the bottle of STOLICHNAYA VODKA~! that lies in front of him and hastily begins to gulp it down like an alcoholic. After finishing a fourth of the bottle, Frisco puts it down and sighs.) Frisco: 'So, any suggestions?' (An apprehensive silence fills the room. Frisco shakes his head and puts his bottle up to his mouth.) 'Sir...' (Frisco flings his bottle straight across the room and begins to scream as the bottle shatters against a bust of Julius Caesar.) Frisco: 'Did you not see I was trying to drink!? Please! If you braindead fucktards are not going to help me, at least let me drink myself to death in peace!' (Frisco plops himself back down in his intimidating bejeweled chair and looks around.) Frisco: 'Someone get me another bottle of vodka.' (Almost half the room volunteers by standing up. Frisco slams his fist on the table.) Frisco: 'WHAT THE HELL IS THIS!? Yo--You're all trying to walk out on me aren't you!?' 'No, Presidente!' 'Defeeneetly no, Jefe!' 'We would never!' (Frisco darts his eyes around.) Frisco: 'Goddamn ingrates! You're trying to organize a coup! That's it! You were all going to use the opportunity to get me a bottle of vodka to have your little conspiracy to depose me! I knew it all along!' 'No, senor! We all juanted to serve ju!' 'Si! Si!' 'Jes, thas eet! (Frisco carefully examines the intentions of the room.) Frisco: 'Very well. Prove your loyalty....' (Frisco points to his Supreme Commander of the Imperial Militia of El Partido de la Pantera de Argentina's 9MM handgun.) Frisco: 'You there! Shoot yourself in the arm!' (The commander blinks and stammers.) Commander: 'Shoot...myself, sir?' Frisco: 'Yes, yes! In the leg...but first pistol whip Secretary Rodriguez.' (The secretary jerks his head back at the commander and pleads.) Frisco: 'Hey! Do not try to nullify my order, assdouche!...General Garichas...carry out your order!' Commander: 'Si, senor Presidente!' (The general quickly removes his handgun from his holster and whacks the secretary over the head with it. The secretary grunts and falls onto the floor, barely conscious. The general then closes his eyes as he presses the gun against his left arm and pulls the trigger.) Commander: 'Ay!' (Frisco looks the commander in the eye. The tough military leader straightens up and holds back his painful winces as his wound bleeds profusely.) Frisco: 'Thank you.' (Frisco darts his eyes at Asklepios.) Frisco: 'Tend to that man's wound!' Asklepios: 'Very well.' (The toga-wearing mental patient approaches the wounded military officer with his hands outstretched and his eyes closed. The general looks down at the crazy middle-aged man with a raised eyebrow. Meanwhile, Frisco points at his advisors.) Frisco: 'You two...' Advisor #1: 'Yes, sir?' Advisor #2: 'What can we do for ju, sir?' (Frisco grins.) Frisco: 'Kick eachother's asses.' Advisor #1: 'Fight, sir?' Frisco: 'Yes.' Advisor #2: 'With punches?' Frisco: 'Yes, goddamnit! Just don't knock anything over and keep going until both of you get knocked out. I want you gentlemen to show me that you actually have a desire to win. You have yet to show me that you care about anything. So care about not wanting to get beaten senseless. Fight!' (The advisors look at eachother and begin to timidly shove and smack eachother.) Frisco: 'If it helps, I heard something about a certain advisor sleeping around with a certain advisor's wife.' Advisor #1: 'Mi esposa!? Tu pinche cabron! Te matare!' (The offending advisor tries to downplay the rumor, but receives a punch to the jaw, knocking him down. The enraged husband drops and begins to strangle the accused.) Frisco: 'There! That's the spirit.' (Frisco finally begins to crack a smile. He then looks around and points to his Secretary of Female Affairs and Secretary of Presidential Groupie Selection.) Frisco: 'With all the bad morale around here, I want you two to show that we all want to get along.' (The fairly pretty Secretary Meraquez of Female Affairs leans in and questions the dictator.) Secretary Meraquez: 'May I ask how, senor? We are already getting along well.' Frisco: 'You two engage in sexual intercourse.' (Secretary Cordoba of Presidential Groupie Selection grins and widely opens his eyes.) Secretary Cordoba: 'I get to pork her, sir?' Frisco: 'Ehh, yes, if you must be so crude.' (The female employee stammers and begins to pull at her hair.) Secretary Meraquez: 'Are ju serious!? I'm engaged!' Frisco: 'Hey! Don't you dare give me such a ridiculous excuse. This is why morale is so poor around here. Everyone is so goddamn uptight!' (Frisco steps over his two advisors who are still on the ground punching and strangling eachother and opens the bathroom door while motioning to come inside.) Frisco: 'The two of you go in there and bump uglies! Now!' (Secretary Cordoba giggles and begins to lead the shocked female employee to the bathroom.) Secretary Meraquez: 'Dios mio, what am I doing....My fiance is going to kill me.' Secretary Cordoba: 'Reeelaaaaax. El Presidente knows what he's doing.' Frisco: 'Indeed I do. This will benefit all of us. Make the best out of this sacrifice!' (The lady secretary sighs and shakes her head.) Secretary Meraquez: 'This better be worth it.' Secretary Cordoba: 'It'll be great, baby!' (Frisco gives a thumbs up as his secretaries enter the bathroom to oil the party morale.) Frisco: 'Excellent.' (Secretary Ordono of Public Relations raises his hand as he looks around.) Secretary Ordono: 'I don't have to have sex with anyone, do I? There are no more women left in the room' Frisco: 'Eh! You would like that wouldn't you!?...But no, you're not going to engage in intercourse with any remaining member.' (The secretary gives a sigh of relief.) Frisco: 'But you will--...' (The two advisors have gotten to their feet and have eachother in headlocks as they bump against the walls close to Frisco.) Advisor #2: 'DIIIIIIIEEEEEEEEE!!!!!!!!!!' (Frisco steps to the side and ignores his advisors.) Frisco: 'But you will have t--...' Commander: 'Ow! Goddamnit! Stop that!' (Frisco looks over to his injured general who receives his medical attention from the son of Apollo. The mental patient jabs his gummy worm-entwined cane into the wound as he chants.) Asklepios: 'Arepolas valikenomanos catomalis venisora!' (Frisco frowns.) Frisco: 'Keep it the fuck down over there!' (Frisco looks back to his secretary as he squints.) Frisco: 'What was I saying again?' (Frisco ponders for a second and throws his hands to his side.) Frisco: 'Just get me another bottle of vodka.' Secretary Ordono: 'Jes, sir.' (The secretary leaves the room as Frisco looks at his remaining secretaries and military officers.) Frisco: 'As for the rest of you...make a human pyramid out of yourselves so I may knock you over. That may give me some added amusement before I kill the Egyptian anal plug on Turmoil.' (The party members stand and confusedly begin to plan their pyramid. Meanwhile, Frisco stands next to the bathroom door and smiles at the moans emitting from the inside.) Frisco: 'Yes, yes! This is the sound of success. Soon we'll be back on track....all of us working as a team to defeat these heathen oppressors of our people!' (The two advisors weakly swings punches at eachother's heads near Asklepios who swings his cane at the balding fighters.) Frisco: 'I am a genius.'
