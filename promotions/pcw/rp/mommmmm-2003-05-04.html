MOMMMMM!

4 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5/4/03 BOOOOOOM Thunder echoed in the light blue sky above which was quickly facing into gray as the sun slowly went into hiding. The crappy weather was not the only thing I would have to deal with on the ride home though, in the passenger seat sat my mother strapped into the seat, waiting for me to make the wrong turn or say the wrong thing so that she could prove her supremacy over me once more. Meanwhile in the back seat Evan had fallen fast asleep and blocked the conflict of my mother and me being in the same care together out of his head. “Tessa put the petal to the metal. I don’t think I can stand another minute in this car.” My mother complained, which was nothing quite unusual being her. She was even worse than a spoiled child in the sense that it was her way or the highway, and there was no way around it. “Mom I can’t go any faster, I’ll skid off the road.” I argued my case back to her, as I kept my eyes focused on the road rather than her and her cold intimidating stare. I didn’t care if it was a sign of disrespect in her eyes; the last thing I wanted was to suffer the same fate as Brandon that September evening, so she could deal without the attention of my eyes. “Tessa if you don’t speed up so help me god…” Her motherly instincts kicked in as the tone of her voice became louder and much sterner as if it were supposed to make me listen. “MOM SHUT UP!” I screamed back, tired of her whining although in the back of my mind I knew I would have to get used to it with her living in the same house as I once more, only this time I owned the house so I could make my own rules that she would have to abide by. How great would it be to finally be the head of the household knowing that everyone would have to listen and me, the way everyone would have to respect me in PCW if I beat someone as respected as El Diablo, a former Rising Star champion. “Excuse me? Is that any way to talk to your mother?” She retorted in her usual bitchy attitude as if she were queen of the world and everyone else’s purpose on this earth was to bow down before her and kiss her feet. The way she acted had annoyed me all my life and it wasn’t about to get any less annoying, in fact it would probably become worse if anything since I had brought fame to the Windsor name, something she wished she could accomplish. “Mom I’m twenty one now, that’s not going to work.” I grinned slightly, trying my best to hold back the egotistical laughter that was erupting in my mind with the comment that had just come out of my mouth and in my opinion put her in her place. “You are such a spoiled brat.” Was the only response my mother could manage to cough out, knowing very well that it was the other way and if anyone should be accused of being a brat it should be her herself. “If you say so.” I sighed, knowing she would hound me relentlessly until she got her way and I admitted she was right. After that I let a moment of silence fall between us, but it wasn’t a long pause seeing as though she had much more on her mind to say. I pushed slightly harder against the metal allowing the speedometer to climb up to seventy. I could feel the tires sliding over the slick road, covered with water from the sky and all I could do was pray that this trip wouldn’t get any worse than it had already been, but as usual I jinxed myself by doing so. SQUEAKKKKKK The sound of the brakes locking as the car turned the opposite direction of where I wanted echoed across the empty highway as Evan shot forward slightly, being caught by his seatbelt while my mother looked over at me like I had a death wish that she was willing to grant. “What the hell are you doing? Let me drive!” She screamed at me, as she unbuckled her seat belt and slowly edged over to the drivers seat, pushing me up against the door before sneaking her door over to the handle and pulling it so that the door opened, causing me to tumble out of the car. “MOMMMM!” I squealed, as I stood up, dusting myself off looking over at her wanting to return the favor to her, but then being told by my conscious that it would solve nothing so instead I climbed into the passenger seat and fastened my seat belt, hoping that this trip would not last much longer, until then I would just have to block my mother out of my mind and focus in on my match for Friday where I had the chance to prove to everyone that I wasn’t just some pushover and that I could make it just fine on my own without Chris there to help me, after all if I could survive the wrath of my mother on my own, then I could tackle just about any task handed to me.
