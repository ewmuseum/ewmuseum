Date: 01/09/02 “What?!?!? We actually PAY ring crew guys?!?!” Our shot opens up on the surprised face of PCW CEO Xavier Kannon. He’s sitting at his desk with a large stack of paperwork in front of him, most consisting of numbers. Xavier : “When did we start doing that!” The bemused PCW Champion pulls a sheet from towards the bottom of the stack and compares it to the one he’s holding, a startled expression wiping it’s way down from his brow to chin. Xavier : “Sweet Hubbard... PCW has ALWAYS paid them!” “Always... Paid.... Them?” As the camera pulls away from the CEO’s desk we see Eleanor sprawled out on the deep Red leather sofa in the far corner of the room, filing her nails as she looks to the CEO. Xavier : “Yes... FOR WHAT?!??! It’s not like they do much, they just take the ring apart and put it back together again.” He exclaims by throwing his hands up into the air then returning his gaze to the blanket of White paper that covers his desk. Xavier : “If they just left the fucking thing in one piece they’d save themselves the effort... Not like it’s hard work anyway.” As a mildly stressed Kannon pushes a pile of documents to the left of the desk, Ellie~! returns her gaze to her nails which she is filing with a lot more care and patience than Xavier is taking with PCW’s accounts. Eleanor : “I dunno.. Remember when I found that video laying around called “The Ring Crew”?” Kannon’s eye shoot up, widened. Eleanor : “That looked hard work, I mean she was, like, SO taking it up the as-” Xavier : “I TOLD YOU ALREADY THAT WAS THE PHANTOM BOOKER’S!!!“ The CEO’s face is Red as Ellie~! just rolls her eyes to the ceiling and fidgets to get comfy on her back while continuing her nail filing. Eleanor : “What... EVER!!!“ Growling in annoyance briefly, Kannon looks down at the next set of accounts, his eyes showing bewilderment at all the high numbers in the “Expenditure” column.
