Lord of the Amulets: Shadows of the Past (double repost)

Date: Fucking rp thing. Something fucked up. Inside this rp is just me and Mark “Gandalf” Makonie. Hessian, Jung “Sauron” Loc and Jesse “Sam” Ramey are mentioned in the rp. Read on! I sat there, perched up on my bed, watching a show on the TV set. I wasn’t really interested in the TV show, nor did I ever watch it anywhere else but I was trying to pass the time. Was I worried about my PCW matches coming up ahead? Definitely not. I had better things to do. I still had no leads on the ancient amulet and where it might be. I had no friends from the old castle who had called, visited, or made any attempt to contact me and that, along with the fact that I wasn’t exactly Mr. Popular around the PCW dressing room, my evenings were pretty boring. The show was just ending up. I guess I should goto bed. It was only 8:30 but I had an important match and had nothing better to do. I got ready to pull the covers to the bed down when I heard a knock at the door. Who could that be? I hadn’t ordered room service and was just thinking to myself how I had no real friends yet. I wasn’t being loud, was I? I stood up and walked to the door. I had an old shirt and some checkered pants on, nothing fancy at all but it was presentable. First, I looked through the pee-hole at who it was and was amazed. “Gandalf, come in!” The man proceeded into my hotel room. The man was Mark ‘Gandalf’ Makonie. He was a very wise person, though being fairly young. He had light skin, with a hint of a tan. He had a white mustache around his lips that was thin. He was just wearing some everyday clothes. Mark was a servant at the castle where I trained for four years. Him and the king were great friends and the king often called on him for duties and important information but what did Mark want now? Was the king not happy with my progress so far? “Long time, no see, eh Mark?” Gandalf spoke to me in that friendship, pleasant tone. “Yeah, what are you doing here?” “Have a seat.” He motioned for me to sit down on the bed while he took his seat in a chair near a desk, beside the TV where I was watching. Night had passed, Gandalf still sat in the same chair, smoking a pipe, me still laying on the bed. All night, we had talked about the good times at the castle, four years ago. I knew that’s not what he wanted to talk to me about and I’m sure that he knew I was onto him but still, he hadn’t mentioned the amulet one bit, except for a little part. He told me that the amulet was best left to talk about in the morning, so now, as the sun started shining on the bright day, I asked him. “Last night, you told me that the amulet was best to be talked about in the morning and now, it’s the morning. I’m interested in hearing what you have to say about it.” Gandalf looked up as he put his pipe to the side. As he looked at me, his white beard glimmered in the light. “Ok, I have found out a lot in my research of the amulet. The amulet that you are looking for is far more powerful than I ever would have thought. The amulet is very dangerous,” started Gandalf. I saw the look in his eye that told me that there was a lot of information I needed to know. Gandalf continued, “Long ago, in the ancient land, seven amulet were made. The amulet’s were said to have been given a special spell to be made by swords and bows and arrows and to die by swords and bows and arrows, which is why you were told to destroy the amulet with ‘old-school’ methods. Six amulets that were made weren’t that powerful, could destroy a five foot diameter with a skilled owner, nothing more. But the seven amulet that was made was the most powerful one. The amulet was made with a special, unknown material that is powerful, no doubt. In the centre of the amulet is a blue crest. The blue crest is the power of the whole amulet.” Gandalf stopped momentarily and looked at me. Some of the information he was telling me I already knew but some of it was new information that fascinated me. “Now, I know you know that the seven amulets were owned by the king’s father and then passed down to the king in which you know very well,” nodded Makonie, “On a birthday party that the king was having, a strong, unknown commotion was cause and one single amulet was stolen. That amulet was the strongest amulet, the most important.” I had knew that part of the story too but the look in Mark’s eye told me that the story wasn’t over and a crucial part was still left. I listened intently as the sun from outside peaked into the room more than before, making the room bright. “For many years, the king has been trying to find out who has been the thief of the amulet. Secretly, I have been trying to find the same information out. The only clue that was left from the party was black cloth. Surprisingly, no fingerprints were found at all on the scene and the fabric had no other hints or anything.” “So how did you find out who has the ring?” Gandalf looked at me for a second, continuing on. “Just wait my eager friend. I will get to that.” I blushed lightly. I wanted to know everything all at once and was a little anxious. I shifted my position on the bed and waited for Makonie to continue. “About a month or two ago, I found a small article from the middle east about an un-related topic. The article was about Sauron who lives in the Middle East, a place called Mordor. Sauron was talking about his superior power and him inheriting tons of money. Sauron has always been a very fond fan of black magic and evilness. Sources said that Sauron had used the money to buy a small holder. The holder was so protective, the small box couldn’t be burn or anything. Around the box was said to be almost like a protective shield. What caught my attention was that a picture of the inside of the box was shown. And the box had an outline of an amulet. I scaled the box to it’s actually size and realized that the amulet would fit into the box.” explained Gandalf who knew his information well. I was amazed at this information and this Sauron. I had so many small questions to ask that would take too long so I asked the one question that was on my mind the most. I was about to ask my question by Makonie continued instead so I stopped to listen more. “This is the only lead I have but since there has been no information before this, this small hint and mistake on Sauron’s part has given you a journey. If you up for it of course.” “Of course!” This was great! I could finally get the amulet! “Now, I have told the king this information, more or less and I have decided myself that Jesse “Sam” Ramey will go with you on your journey and the king has excepted.” “Sam Ramey, he was the gardener at the king’s castle, not?” “Indeed he was.” “That’s great.” I almost wanted to cry, I was so happy that I could finally do my job. I knew it wouldn’t be easy, the terrain on the earth wasn’t easy. “But I must warn you,” said Gandalf, “Sauron has a servant, Hessian who will rip you limb from limb if he catches you. Please be careful and try to not make any commotion of your leaving. Leave soon!” “I will.” Gandalf nodded to me and got up out of his chair. He scratched his small beard and walked to the door, saying. “I’m off. Ramey will be here tomorrow or the next day. Good Luck.” With that, Makonie walked out of the door. I stood there for a long time. Time to catch that amulet!
