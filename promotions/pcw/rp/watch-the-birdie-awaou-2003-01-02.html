Watch the birdie Awaou...

2 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 01/02/03 Even the strongest of barriers break eventually Hate Lasts but not forever... And when it ends... Even the hardest heart... Feels remorse... When We Last Left Shin Yagyuu.... Soldier: You.... YOU BASTARD! I'm a bastard? Shin slowly stands up... You willingly help a company that destroys families.. Kills men.. Uses people... and breaks souls... and I AM A BASTARD! The unholy metal scrape of a raZed scream fills the air as Shin crosses the gap between the men and himself.......... Fade But The Story Continues A sharp hard kick slams into the side of the captains skull. Shin lands on the concrete and sweeps out the nearest soldiers feet. In the time it would take any normal man to gasp for air Shin leaps back to feet and slams the energy blade into the fallen captains chest. The other soldiers back away slowly as Shin reaches over and snaps the fallen soldiers neck... Now.... Who is a bastard... Who is a murderer? Shin slowly stands up and takes a step towards the men... Fear covers the remaining three soldiers faces as they each turn pale and begin to back up... Lambs to the slaughter.. Led like toys... Shin shakes his hands as excess blood drips off them.. Hurry out the entrance before I change my mind... The men's eyes widen as they all run for the warehouse door.. Shin slowly slumps down to a sitting position next to the vehicle that brought him to his unknown location... Life..... Death..... Suddenly Shin shakes his head as memories shock into his mind.. -=- Bright lights blind view as trees sway with the wind in a beautiful park in Kobe, Japan. A young boy runs up to a japanese man and hugs him... Boy: Daddy, today I caught a frog... Man: You let it go right? Boy: Yes daddy. Keeping it would be wrong... Man: And why? Boy: Captivity is not life... Captivity is sentenced.. Man: Good boy.. Across the park a woman stands up from her reading and begins to walk across the clearing.. Woman: Hello Shinosuke... Man: Hello my love... The memory spins and shakes as it fades to black... -=- Shin's eyes slide open to a customized Magnum .50 Desert Eagle... At the end of the barrel a man stands in a black leather trench coat and a black leather cap... Man: Hello Shinosuke... Hi ugly... The man raises the gun and walks away.. A spin on his heel locks the gun back on the spot between Shin's eyes..... Man: Names Shigatsu Awaou... Shigatsu huh.... Shigatsu: Yup.. They call me the Steel Angel... Another raZed? How quaint... And what exactly are you here for... Shigatsu slumps in defeat.. Shigatsu: Ya ejit... I'm here to have a good old fashion gun fight with ya.. Shin shakes his head Silly bastard... What's the point in that? Shigatsu: Well I aint much for fightin with my fists or with swords... Guns are my specialty... Shin simply shakes his head and leaps to his feet... Got an extra gun 'hoss'.... Shigatsu tosses a gun to Shin as the two bolt off to different areas of the warehouse. As Shin ducks behind a box he fires off two blind shots... Shit! Did I hit you already Shigatsu? Shigatsu: Yeah.... In the ankle... You giving up? BANG! I'll take that as a no... Shin fires off another shot. Shigatsu: you got 3 bullets I got 5 Yagyuu.... Two more shots fire from Yagyuu's gun into the ceiling... Shigatsu: CRAZY ASSHOLE I like your style... I only need one bullet to kill you Awaou... Shin bursts with speed from behind his boxes. Shin rears back and throws his gun at Shigatsu as he pulls out from behind his hiding spot. Two bullet zing from Shigatu's gun both hitting Shin's throwing the weapon into the air.. As it comes down Shin leaps into the air and round house kicks the gun... Watch the birdie Awaou... The handle hits Awaou hard between the eyes as Yagyuu lands behind him catching his own gun and holding it to Awaou's head... Shigatsu: Damn man.... You're as good as they say you are... Thank you. Shin's finger tightens on the trigger.. Shigatsu: WHOA I Aint a Perfect raZed I will die! Stop! Click.... Shin drops the empty gun on the ground. Mr. Awaou... I would suggest you stay away from this kind of life... Shin takes Awaou's gun and bends it at the barrel... Good luck in life... Shin walks over to the Vehicle that brought him to the warehouse and leaps into its Driver seat... Fade
