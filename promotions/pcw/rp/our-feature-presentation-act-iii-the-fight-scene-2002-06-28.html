Our Feature Presentation: Act III: THE FIGHT SCENE!

28 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Title: Our Feature Presentation: Act III: THE FIGHT SCENE! Featuring: Politically Incorrect Date: June 28 CORRUPTION MANACT III: The Fight Scene!No new people, just lots of fighting! :The infamous narrator begins to speak, to introduce our new development.:: A shadow looms large over the meeting of the United League of Do-Gooders. A second shadow quickly joins it. Corruption Man and Filibuster Boy are on the scene. It is time for the legendary showdown between good and evil. It is time for words like 'BAM!' 'WHAP!' and 'KER-POW!' to be thrown around like candy in Willy Wonka and the Chocolate Factory. It's time to see who's a little fruity in the booty, who's getting kicked in the belly-welly, and, most importantly, WHO'S GOIN' TO THE PAY WINNNNNNDDAAA!! THERE BE CLUBBERIN', LADIES AND GENTLEMEN! So sit tight! The fight is on its way!! ::The two shadowy figures leap down from the roof, crashing down onto the tables. Corruption Man and Filibuster Boy stand atop the table, amid scared looks from the League.:: Corruption Man: HALT, DO-GOODERS! Rent-A-Hero: No! We will not halt! I am SICK AND TIRED OF HALTING! I WILL NOT KOWTOW TO THE FORCES OF EVIL! Filibuster Boy: Damn, what got in his pants? Corruption Man: Ah, Sexual Innuendo Girl left her. Something about a proposal and her fucking someone else... :The Hero's eyes dim......his perpetual smile fades. He indeed appears to be but a shell of what he used to be, but he is yet dangerous....perhaps more so now than ever. He launches forthwith his dramatic monologue..: Rent-A-Hero: You know, that's lower than I ever thought you capable. I am a broken man now......and it's all thanks to you. You, and all of those people I have helped for years. This is my last battle. I could care less whether I am good, righteous, evil, horrible, whatever. All I want right now is to destroy you and your little boy... :The Wonder Hearts smile, and lick their lips.:: Wonder Hearts: LITTLE BOY?! WHERE?! ::Hero shoots them a withering glance.:: Rent-A-Hero: DON'T INTERRUPT MY DRAMATIC MONOLOGUE, DAMNIT! ::The Wonder Hearts quiet down, as RAH continues.:: Rent-A-Hero: To destroy you.....Corruption Man......to destroy your little boy....and to destroy the people that both of you are. Whoever you are behind those masks and costumes......my only goal now is to make you as wretched as I am now. There is no other way, no other time. Prepare for your fate. LEAGUE......ATTACK!!! :Fried Chicken quickly springs into action, taking out a giant drumstick at his side. He swings this weapon at Filibuster Boy, but it only hits the ground with a mighty.....: *CLUNK!* ::Seizing the advantage, Corruption Man fires off a quick series of right hands, ending with a roundhouse right!:: *WHAP!* ::Fried Chicken slumps back, but the God of Goodness charges, nailing both men with a vicious lariat.:: *CRASH!* God of Goodness: We are the God of Goodness.......Good-frickin-ness..... Corruption Man: ZOUNDS! It's worse than we thought! Filibuster Boy: What do you mean? Corruption Man: He's reliving his past gimmicks! ::The God charges, nailing Corruption Man with a fierce right!:: *KAYFABE!* ::Filibuster Boy charges, and ducks under a Boot of Goodness, and kicks the God in the kneecap, sending him down with a....:: *SMASH!* Trojan Man: Hey! I KNOW HOW TO END THIS!! Wonder Hearts, get out the Sailor Cannons! Rent-A-Hero: Sailor Cannons? ::The Wonder Hearts 'drop trou', as in trousers, and ready their 'sailor cannons', which are basically manmande giant penises.:: Trojan Man: Yeah, Sailor......seaman. Semen cannons. Rent-A-Hero: What's semen? ::Trojan Man shakes his head.:: Trojan Man: Now, remember, the mere SIGHT of me has been known to CAUSE orgasms, so......ORGASMIC POWER, LEVEL FIVE! :He turns to look at the two Wonder Hearts, and just stares. Eventually, the Hearts gasp, and an odd-looking white liquid spurts out of the Sailor Cannons. It hits Corruption Man and Filibuster Boy dead on, making it hard for them to move.:: Rent-a-Hero: Egads! That's glue! Trojan Man: Exactly! They can't fight if they can't move! Now, I have an idea on how to bring them down, once and for all.. THIRTY MINUTES LATER.... ::Corruption Man and Filibuster Boy lie strapped to a giant wooden table. Rent-A-Hero's booming voice can be heard.:: Rent-A-Hero: IT IS OVER FOR YOU TWO NOW! LOOK ABOVE YOU! ::A giant gavel looms above them, moving down ever so slowly towards our villians.:: Rent-A-Hero: Now, we shall leave, and let you wallow in your own impending death, that you will likely escape from! HAHHAHAHA!!! :The Wonder Hearts, Rent-A-Hero, and Trojan Man all leave, leaving Corruption Man and the Boy of Fillibustering to figure a way out of their predicament.:: Filibuster Boy: Corruption Man! How do we escape?! Corruption Man: I'm THINKING! I'M THINK-- ::Suddenly, a new figure bursts through a window into the room. She is clad in a very secretaryish outfit. Both Corruption Man and Filibuster Boy seem happy to see her.:: Corruption Man: INTERN GIRL! Can you help us? Intern Girl: I have an idea! :Working quickly, Intern Girl unties our two villains, pulling them off the wooden table just before the gavel smashes into it. At the sound of the gavel, the remenants of the United League of Do-Gooders enter.:: Rent-A-Hero: WHAT?! DAMN YOU INTERN GIRL! MY PLAN WAS FOOLPROOF!TROJAN MAN! WONDER HEARTS! OBLITERATE!! ::The Wonder Hearts get into battle position, but, as they see Intern Girl ready to battle them, they run off for the hills.:: Wonder Hearts: AHHHHHHHHHHHH!!!!! ICCCCCKKKKYYYYYYYYYY!!!! Corruption Man: Well, that leaves just you and Trojan Man, Rent-A-Dweeb! ::The incensed Hero and Man charge, but are soundly caught by a pair of spinning back kicks, striking with a mighty.:: *KER-POWIE!* ::The supervillains stand, proud as ever.:: Corruption Man: We have made Des Moines safe for villainy! CORRUPTION MAN, AWAY!!! ::They both leave, as the credits fade onto the screen. Back in the theater, the audience applauds, and begins to file out of the theater. Powell, Lovecraft, and Schultz sit front row, in awe at their accomplishment.:: William S. Powell: This has been fun, but now.....it's business time. Election Day. Reginald V. Lovecraft: Where Politically Incorrect ALWAYS triumphs... ::Fade to...:: Paid for by the Friends of Politically Incorrect, 2002.The Next PCW Tag Team Champions. ---- View Politically Incorrect's Biography
