PCW Wars: New Hope Part 8

26 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 26 - 10 - 02 The scene emerges on the conversation between the goliath Syth Lord, Darth Pharaoh and his commanding officer Grand Moff Tarkannon as they discuss a possible disturbance within the force. Darth Pharaoh: 'He is here....' Tarkannon: 'Who? Charlie Loc Kenobi?!?' Darth Pharaoh: 'No... Elvis! Of course I'm referring to my old master, I can feel a tremor within the force, something I haven't felt since I was last in the company of my mentor!!!' Tarkannon: 'Surely the old fool is dead. You my friend are the last of your kind, the Jedi are extinct....' Suddenly the inter-com flickered into life, relieving the room of its buzz of silence. Inter-Com: 'Governor Tarkannon - We have a small, well it’s more of a medium sort of problem involving detention block A twenty-three-... Err-... Nothing we can't deal with but I thought you had better know, since it’s your signature signing my cheques!' Tarkannon: 'Fuc-... All sections to be put on alert. And peon, sort that damn Gareth Gates stutter out, you've given the Rebels a head start with your stalling!' Darth Pharaoh: 'Charlie Loc is here. The force is with him' The scene reverts back to the detention cell where the Stormbingers have been barricaded from the premises by the large metallic elevator door whilst the Rebel force of our hero's try desperately to secure a escape route. A series of explosions startles the Millennium Eagle crew of Gaz Solo and Cherezza as they place their weight in front of the blockade. Anarchy: 'Captain Calamity it looks like you've managed to cut off our only escape route!' Gaz Solo: 'Listen up bitc-... Listen up your highness; maybe you would prefer the comfort of your cell? No butler to order about, no company and most importantly no hair drier?!?' Anarchy: 'Are you implying my hair looks that of a common street rat like yourself or you can see my split ends, please tell me, this is a royal nightmar-...' Gaz interrupted the Princess during her moment of winging as Luke mutters over a transmitter to the droid collective of Rush-3PO and R2 D2. Gaz Solo: 'And your a royal pain in the ass~!' As the explosions near and the giggles of Gaz Solo increasing, the blue-haired Princess raced forward snatching the blaster of the rugged smuggler. Taking aim, her target being that of Gaz Solo, she fires a wayward shot that sails into a grate, penetrating the wall, which leads to the garbage chute. Anarchy: 'This is some rescue. Plan less, mindless, I'm surprised you've made it this far!' A. Skywalker: 'Err-... Arr-... Well-...' Gaz Solo: 'He's the brains, sweetheart!' Anarchy: 'Yeah Right - Girl Power every time....' Princess Anarchy began playfully swaying the weapon around her finger in a flirtatious manner, when suddenly the trigger sounds followed by laser blast that ricochets off the wall. In an act of courage Cherezza threw his comrade Gaz from the blast and down into the darkness of the garbage, both beings hurtling together in a heap. As Alexander Skywalker peered down, Gaz reacts in the blackness with a stereotypical Byron Calver response. Gaz Solo: 'EEEEEEEEEEEWWWWWWWWWWWWWWW!!!!!!!!!' A. Skywalker: 'Well we had better-....' Anarchy: 'I'm like not going down there! I mean rats, germs, filth, decay, dirt, grime, sludge, diseases, garbage people, like... HELLO!' A. Skywalker: 'If you've quite finished!!!' Alex spears the royal through the grate in an act of rage; with seconds to spare as a hoard of Stormbringers storm the detention block. Emerging through the smoke they slowly creep around the holding area, unaware that the Rebel force sit idly by in the Death Stars compactor. Gaz Solo: 'Oh! The garbage chute was a real wonderful idea. What an incredible smell you have discovered, let's get out of here' Gaz taking the blaster from the clenched hands of Cherezza and fires a bolt, which bounces about randomly almost beheading the captive crew. Anarchy: 'Magnetically sealed, I mean this escape has been sooooo pointless. From being the higher power of Alderaan to a filth swimming commoner, all in a matter of hours....' A. Skywalker: 'Well at-least you’re still alive! Always look on the bright side of life....' As though a musical awe had crept in the team continue the lyrics of the song, adding a little moral to the fallen camp. Everybody: 'ALWAYS LOOK ON THE BRIGHT SIDE OF LIFE DA-DA DA-....' Gaz Solo: 'This is ludicrous, I rather be captured than sit in this skank hell-hole singing nursery rhymes with runaway circus freaks!' A loud, horrible, inhuman moan works its way up from the murky depths. Cherezza let out a terrified howl and slowly edged away as the remainder of the team drew their laser pistols. The Wookie himself cowering nearer to the wall. A. Skywalker: 'IT'S ALIVE, IT'S ALIVE!!!' Anarchy: 'Yeah but what?' A. Skywalker: 'How should I know, but one things sure, I ain't staying here to find out... Women and farmers first!' With a quick shudder the walls began to edge forward. If the claustrophobia doesn't kill them, then the caving in of their craniums will most certainly will as Alexander Skywalker reached for his com-link. A. Skywalker: 'Rush-3PO do you copy, Rush-3PO do you copy... For Christ sake you silly bronze coloured, gibberish talking piece of jun-...' As those final words filtered over the com-link, the device responded with the voice of Rush-3PO, a rage fuelled droid who has had enough of the insults from his lowly owner. Rush-3PO: 'Oh, white boy I'm gonna come up there and personally remove your spinal cord biatch - I'm gonna get me main man R2 here to throw his cylinder frame into you legs crippling you like some drooling piece of shit Steven bleeding Hawk-...' A. Skywalker: 'Look you worthless-....' Gaz losing his patience with the child like squabble and the fact that the walls are closing in on their fragile bodies, gripped the com-link and pleaded for the droids assistance. Gaz Solo: 'Look dude, I hear you... The kid is a jerk and after this mission we could do a little double team on his scrawny ass but that means we got to bail him out of this mess first so be a champ and work your magic!' Rush-3PO: 'I mean, nobody takes us droids emotions into consideration-....' As the walls gain on the Rebel force, too the point of being only a few feet away Gaz screamed down the com-link for the droid to quickly pull his weight. Gaz Solo: 'HURRY THE FUCK UP!!!' The com went silent and the team squared up to death by looking it in the face when suddenly the device blurted some random whistles, most likely from the R2 unit as he tampers with the locking mechanism. Again the com-link goes silent, but to the cheers of the Rebel team lead by the Jedi wannabe Alexander Skywalker the walls cease. Rush-3PO: 'Are you there my soon-to-be BITCH?' A. Skywalker: 'WE MADE IT! WE MADE IT! WE MADE IT! WE MADE IT!' Gaz Solo: 'We get the message!' As the team cheers their survival the scene switches back to the wise Jedi Knight of Charlie Loc Kenobi who vanishes out of sight from a group of patrolling Stormbringers. Slipping into the shadows the white troopers pass their target who lurks only feet away. Officer: 'Secure this area until the alert has been cancelled!' First Trooper: 'Give me regular reports' The group seperate, leaving but the initial two pairing of the officer and trooper who converse over the whole episode. First Trooper: 'So what the hell is going on, man?' Officer: 'Probably a drill... Either that or we've been attacked by a bunch of comical Rebels?!?' The Imperial guards laugh at the suggestion and Charlie Loc quickly races off after gesturing with an open palm. The pair think they hear something in the other hallway, a demonstration of the force, which allows the Jedi to venture on unnoticed. First Trooper: 'Err-... What was that?' Officer: 'Oh, I thought you wouldn't hear. Silent but violent, eeeeewwwwww!' First Trooper: 'Damn, if I maybe permitted to comment....' Officer: 'Continue!' First Trooper: 'Well 'DAMN' has a Jawa crawled up your anus and died, that be a foul smell, sir!' The scene again flips, this time back to comfort of our hero's who head hotfooted to the hangar, where Gaz Solo's ship lays siege to hundreds of patrolling Stormbringers. Peering through a glass panel, they witness the siege and huddle together in order for them to muster together a full-proof plan. Anarchy: 'You came in that thing? Your braver than I thought!' Gaz Solo: 'Nice! Come on - Oh, and not in the literal sense....' Cherezza blurting a howl of hysterics, high fives his smuggler colleague of Gaz Solo as they bask in their moment of glory. The commoner getting one up over the royal so to speak. Anarchy: 'My life never ceases to amaze me. Filth-ridden scumbag and his walking carpet... are mocking me, THIS MUST BE DISNEY AND WE ARE IN ALADIN!!!' As the Princess and the smuggler Gaz exchange a cold-hearted stare, the scene slowly closes as we enter the home straight of this series. - To be continued -
