Becoming an Angel

25 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 25th Febuary 2002 We start off this mini-adventure in a field of green grass. The sun beats down on the grass and you can smell the fresh morning air on the breeze. All is calm and peaceful, that is until you look overhead. Mainly because a plane flies overhead. It’s not exactly a big plane... but one all the same. The camera cuts to the interior of the plane, and well, it’s not what you would expect. It’s kitted out for parachute jumping, and on board is not only one Isawa Miyagawa, but also for the first time in several promos, Natalia Miyagawa as well. The duo are dressed in suitably attired clothing, jumpsuits and parachutes (with the little clips hooking up to the ceiling already as they sit down. Miyagawa is also wearing his Television Title around his waist. Natalia: I can’t believe that on the worst booked week of your life, you suggested going skydiving. Miyagawa: So? You’re a trained skydiver aren’t you? You used to tell me all those stories about doing it. So I just figured it would take my mind off the over booking. Besides on Turmoil it’ll be revealed that Tyler Nelson really does have it in for me and has booked me to be first out at March Madness. You know life sucks when you’re this damned good. No wait... almost hit copyright infringement there; I’d better retract that... set me up... Natalia: Do I have to? Miyagawa: Yes? Why did the chicken cross the road? Natalia: Why? Miyagawa: Because... I’m... Excellent. That’s much better, it’s a better rounded ending to a sentence, and don’t you agree? Natalia is about to say something when you hear a little “ding”...well a fairly loud “ding” come from somewhere. The duo look up and the five minute warning light has come on. Natalia: Not too late to back out now, you know. Miyagawa: Gah, I have never backed away from anything. Apart from the first GTT Tournament, but that was because I was away on honeymoon with you, and I figured it was probably the best reason for dropping out that I will ever have in the history of my life. Natalia: And if you hadn’t dropped out, I would never have married you. Miyagawa looks slightly shocked at that news. Miyagawa: Really? Natalia: No of course not, I would have waited a week. And it’s a good job that this is another pre-recorded interview, as you of course know that when this is being screened I will in fact be in Anaheim General Hospital with Doctor Mark Sloan performing another baby scan on me. Miyagawa: That’s just before he operates on my burned face... which ironically is yet to be burned. And they say that wrestling is predetermined. Bah I say, Bah! Suddenly Jeff Marx’s voice is heard over the intercom system to the surprised passengers of Natalia and Miyagawa. Marx: Good afternoon passengers, this is your captain speaking. We are currently flying at an altitude of some ten feet... no damn, that’s the wrong gauge... we’re flying at a moderate cruising level are coming up to the jump point in approximately three minutes. And by the way, good job on breaking kayfabe there Miya-jobber. Miyagawa and Natalia turn to each other and try to hug over the equipment they have strapped to their bodies... they partially succeed at that task. Miyagawa: Still, at least I wasn’t booked on Sabotage. At least it meant I get some time to myself, although I was backstage... but only because they give away free Sobe. Natalia: You do realise we’re in an aeroplane, there is no need to shill branded drinks. You don’t see Xavier trying to sell Snapple on board of a seven-four-seven, now do you? Although admittedly, I didn’t see him aboard one recently. Miyagawa: Well here’s the plan for this week. Turmoil... beat off a rather weak assed challenge from Drake Maxwell... because let’s face it, if I was Maxwell, I’d be conserving my energy to face Krayzie at Madness. In fact Drake might nearly as badly booked as I am, have to face three Asylum members in one on one matches three times in a week. That sucks big hairy donkey balls. Gah, now I’m picking up Walden style sayings. Although speaking of Walden, finally the match I have requested for weeks is on Fusion... the one show that I could have really had done with having off to prepare for the PPV. Life’s a bitch, and then she kicks you in the nuts. Natalia: Anyone in particular? Miyagawa: Yeah, Kid Wonder’s kid sister, Amanda. INCEST~! Natalia: I don’t think it’s fair to say that. There is no proof the pair are related in anyway, shape or form. Miyagawa: But there is no proof that they are NOT related in anyway shape, or form. And they do look kinda similar... in the way that they both look like they should be in a Britney Spears video or something. Although I lost ten pounds after betting that Kid Wonder was going to be the replacement member of Hear’Say over in the UK. Damn that annoying gimboid, he must have heard of my plot to win twenty quid. Natalia: I’d say about a minute to go; we’d better get to our feet. Natalia and Miyagawa stand up and check the clips from their parachutes to the bar going along the interior side of the aircraft. Natalia: Now remember, jump out, and the parachute will open automatically, there is nothing to fear but fear itself. Stalin said that. Or it might have been Lenin, I’m not sure. Miyagawa: I’m certain it wasn’t either of them... and it certainly wasn’t the leaders of Russia prior to the Communist revolution... unless they said “There is nothing to fear, da, unless you could three thousand peasants with torches and hand weapons in that, da.” Natalia crosses her arms in annoyance. Miyagawa: Sorry, I’m just nervous. After this, the week’s work will seem like nothing at all. After throwing myself down to earth, defeating a Fallen Angel and a Fallen Wrestler will be easy as pie. And then it just leaves the World Title, and that isn’t anyone’s hands apart from Lady Luck’s and Master Destiny’s. The launch like blinks and Natalia slides the side door open. Miyagawa looks out into the abyss. Miyagawa: And we’ll land near Saint Louis, right? And why couldn’t we take the bus again? Natalia shoves him from behind, throwing him out the aircraft. She waits five seconds before jumping herself. The camera however stays inside the aircraft, because have you ever met a PCW camera man who was dumb enough to put himself at risk?
