Nerezzident Evil: The End

28 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 28th May 2003 “Check me out – I got more ammunition than NATO here!” triumphantly wailed the Prince of Pranks placing a huge rocket launcher onto his shoulder. Taking aim, Gaz Green locked in the target of one Nascosto Ireul Nerezza. Concern flooded Primetime Championship Wrestling’s resident Jester as he looked across the spectrum; the Vampire had been in many battles as an ally but during their tenure in Raccoon City Alabama the Living Nightmare was more foe than friend. The question played on the mind of the green tailored comic, his eye’s sunk with solemn but as niggling as it were an answer was needed and that answer would be generated courtesy of a parable, “Do unto those that they would do unto you!” A simplistic sentence but nonetheless those nine words held the answer he craved. The seizure had subsided and the Vampire knelt in a knighthood position but what had came to light was that the Angel of Fear had returned to normal, that if you could ever couplet the words Vampire and normality in the same sentence. His size had reduced and the scaly green facial mask had vanished without trace, this metamorphosis was more of a reversal than an actual change. With the Living Nightmare seemingly back to normal, would there be any need to squeeze the trigger? “Rezz?” murmured the King Midas of Tag. His finger still rubbed against the trigger but the pressure he had applied had been vanquished and in a state of confusion he gazed over to the Vampire statuette. The suit-clad comedian edged closer with a puzzling expression etched along his angelic face. Shuffling his feet forward he drew closer and closer to his motionless partner who seemingly was locked in his own state of transfixion; possibly a side effect to the trauma he endured. Concerned Gaz Green pressed on when suddenly the beast turned its head, snapping its jaws, the razor sharp teeth had oppressed but his two piercing canines were equally threatening. Interestingly the change came about as a by-product of the chase, putting the beast through his paces had burnt up the tyrant’s blood in his system like a fossil fuel and now the Jester wouldn’t be facing the Super Vampire but one he was more accustomed to, Nascosto Ireul Nerezza. The Vampire mindlessly barked and it would be this rage-fuelled retort that would finally answer the niggling question of, ‘Will he or won’t he shoot?’ Snapping his index finger backwards the trigger clicked and the weapon kicked back. The Lord of Laughter was knocked cleanly off his feet and the rocket launcher fell from his grasp as its barrel smoked, squinting his eyes as he adjusted to the poor visibility the Jester peered over to the weapons intended. His puzzling expression shifted to that of amazement, his eyes glassy and wide for what he observed was nothing short of weird, a word that could litter this episode tenfold. “Huh?” muttered the Jester as he squinted his eyes for a second opinion. Sitting before the Pranks With Attitude behemoth was a small cube like object, it’s six sides littered with an assortment of different colours. No missile, no lethal projectile, just a Rubix cube, which has about as much use as a prostitute spread across Tim Walden’s bed covers… It had become apparent that running was no longer an option, the end indeed looked nigh. In his shredded attire Nerezza stood erect. Casting a looming shadow over his partner a duel looked to erupt. The helicopter continued to parade its light source atop of the mansion and the diameter served as the ring so to speak. Nerezza still in his vegetated state of mind spluttered a sentence together, “Die Jester… The Jester will die…” The Living Nightmare from left field picked up the coloured cube and inspected it. Finding the squares to move the colours began to align, then suddenly the plastic object fell to the ground as the beasts hands came up clutching the sides of his head in pain. Shaking the cobwebs the Living Nightmare murmured, “Where, where am I?” The Jester retorted with a dumbfounded look. In his groggy condition the Vampire snapped his neck back relieving himself of all the kinks and waved a talon through his netted black hair to showcase his fiery red eyes. Retracting his jaws shut he put away his two saliva soaked canines much to the relief of one Gaz “The Jester” Green. “What the hell has been going on?” hissed the Vampire. “I remember agreeing to settle down for the night in some pitiful placed called Raccoon City and finding a habitable hotel and then, well nothing, I must’ve passed out?” continued the behemoth in a reminisce fashion. “So you don’t remember anything squire, how strange!” replied the Emerald Menace. With his arm wrapped across the broad shoulders of the trench coat clad Vampire, the duo walk from the crime scene, retracing their previous steps one would assume. Cube still smothered in the clenched fist of the bloodsucker the Conniving Collective vanished behind the metallic door marked ‘Exit’ and the helicopter rose into the night sky, the darkness blanketing its retreat. The scene fades to black in typical fashion, accompanied by a monotone voice, “WE SHALL MEET AGAIN MR. GREEN!!!!” The villainous comment conclusion only serves as a point in which to reopen the scene and the caper recommences from the reception area. Nothing appeared out of place with the decorative ash table still standing proud, it’s polished work surface glistening from the neighbouring candlelight and the shards of glass of the emergency box showers the crimson carpet. “…FOR I AM THE AMAZING…” “It cant be!” interrupts the distinctive voice of the jovial prankster, his goliath in tow. The mastermind behind this entire charade sits with his back turned from the advancing Vampire and Jester collective. The shadows camouflage the culprit’s identity as he sits in his confinement, a wheelchair laden with electrical motors and a network of plastic tubes, “I AM THE AMAZING STEVEN HAWKINGS!!!!” The Jester gulps hard and his associates face ignites with blind rage. The Vampire cringes his face and snarls knowing his shenanigans were part of the evil super genius Steven Hawkings puppeteer masquerade. The motors of the wheelchair murmured into life and the frame pivoted full-circle with all parties locked in a Western style duel pose. “I AM THE AMAZING STEVEN HAWKINGS!!!!” The monotonous computer aided voice bellows again. With saliva washing the underside of his chin the mastermind sits limp in his motorised wheelchair whilst the devious duo close him down. Twitching his control stick the chair rotated around and the mastermind unclips a cap atop of the control stick to unveil a small red button contained beneath. Fleeing the crime scene the Jester’s nemesis makes his concluding comments, “HE WHO FIGHTS AND RUNS AWAY, LIVES TO FIGHT ANOTHER DAY!!!! MUAH HA HA HA HA!!!!” The vehicle jerked forward and sped off, its tuned motors hitting its top speed of five miles per hour almost immediately. “He’s getting away squire…” hastily injected the Prince of Pranks. A swirling sound of gas filtered and the Pranks With Attitude pair hunched over to see two chrome canisters marked ‘Nitrous Oxide’ strapped beneath the metallic getaway vehicle. Suddenly spewing from the backend of the arm rests came a shower of red flame and the wheelchair propelled forward on a collision course with the mansion entrance, “I wouldn’t bank on that!” snarled the un-dead goliath wrenching his arm back and quickly catapulting the coloured cube towards the want away evil super genius Steven Hawkings. The plastic cube sailed through the air before gravity stepped in and the child’s puzzle hurtled back to Earth, catching the world renowned professor on the back of the head. Seemingly unconscious from the blow, Steven Hawkings sat slumped in his wheelchair, his limp body keeled over catching the control stick and the Nitrous fuelled wheelchair pummelled into the wall offset of the mansion door. The scene strangely fades and reopens in the distinctive backdrop of the Jester’s apartment, more precisely the bedroom. Opening within the Las Vegas dwelling its occupier emerges from his slumber, sitting erect, “WHERE AM… OH IT WAS A DREAM!!!!”
