Cheering Up Teresa

19 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Title: Cheering Up Teresa Featuring: Rent-a-Hero Date: 3-19-2003 ..::/::..Our story begins as “Last Action Hero” blasts out of the speakers of a 67’ Chevy, candy red paint coating it as the engine roars with power down the highway, the ride smooth and without incident. At the wheel is the beautiful Teresa Tomas, her hair tied up by a red and white bandanna as she leans one arm out of the open truck window, the her free hand gripping the steering wheel gently as she mouths to the music. In the passenger seat sit PCW’s one and only superhero, Rent-A-Hero, adorned in a black and white Venom replica costume with the PCW Crusierweight title about his waist as he looks about at the outside world whizzing by him. He smiles at the sight of the warming sun, it’s ray’s showering his spandex enwrapped face as he looks over to the straight-faced Teresa, speaking with exuberance..::/::.. Rent-A-Hero:”You look troubled my raging redneck sidekick of mine? What’s the problem?” ..::/::..Teresa lets out a small sigh, not looking our hero in his aqua blue eyes as she keeps her focus on the road. She switches lanes effortlessly as she replies to our hero in a no-nonsense tone, not looking to be quite open at the moment..::/::..Teresa:”I’m just PEACHY thank you. And I AIN’T your sidekick….pardner’s, yes. Let’s get that straight.”Rent-A-Hero:”Righty-O sidekick of mine…”..::/::..Teresa grumbles under her breath, gripping the steering wheel tighter in slight annoyance as our hero continues..::/::.. Rent-A-Hero:”I KNOW!! I know why your so mad Teresa….I mean…I know you did get eliminated by that decisively evil weasel, Tim Walden. I know you also lost your coveted TV title to him in a GRUELING 2 out of 3 falls submission match, where he made you tap to the “No Evasion”. I know he beat you in front of THOUSANDS of screaming PCW fans. I kn…” Teresa:”SHUT UP ALREADY! If ya got a point, get to it Rent-Boy….and I mean FAST.”..::/::..Our hero sinks into his seat a little deeper, choosing not to bring up the night that was March Madness any longer as he continues his talk with Teresa..::/::.. Rent-A-Hero:”Look Teresa….you can’t let the night that was March Madness phase you. Yes it was a bad night overall for you, but you have GOT to bounce back from it, just like Flash Gordon did when Mongo tried to banish him to the reaches of Fridgia. You can’t just say “it’s over”, Teresa. We’re a team you and me, and I’ll use ALL my RENT-A-POWERS to help you get your self-esteem up milady! So come on…let’s see that smile….plllllllllllllllllease?”..::/::..Teresa turns to our hero, striking a sarcastically wide grin towards our hero before putting her focus back on the road, obviously still peeved about her night at the pay-per-view. The truck is silent, our hero not knowing what to say to Teresa to cheer her up. On the other spectrum, Teresa chooses not to relate her feelings to our spandex enwrapped superhero as she continues driving. Rent-A-Hero presses the “seek” button on Teresa’s radio, the radio stopping on a station playing “Sunshine Lollipops” as our hero nods his head to the music giddily, Teresa in enraged shock as our hero sings along with the sugary sweet song..::/::..Rent-A-Hero:”SUNSHINE LOLLIPOPS AND RAINBOWS EVERYDAY!! SUNSHINE LOLLI…”!!~POP~!! ..::/::..Our hero holds the back of his head in sharp pain, Teresa changing the radio to a country music station as she angrily scolds Rent-A-Hero..::/::.. Rent-A-Hero:”WHAT’D I DO?! I WAS JUST TRYING TO CHEER YOU UP!!Teresa:”NEVER TOUCH A GOOD OL’ GIRLS RADIO!! Now ya do ONE more thang in this truck that annoys me and I’ll slap the spandex off yer hide!”..::/::..The truck grows silent yet again, our hero rubbing the back of his head as Teresa simply mouths to the music as she did before. Our hero however, cannot resist trying to cheer her up in her downed state, exclaiming jubilantly in hopes of getting her to respond positively…::/::..Rent-A-Hero:”Teresa…look, how about you and I hit the city and hope it doesn’t hit back?! Whaddya say, the dynamic duo of PCW hitting the town for a little fun? How does that sound to your Teresa-Senses?”..::/::…The offer catches her attention, Teresa’s facial expression slowly transforming from straight to a little grin as she looks to our hero. She then asks..::/::.. Teresa:”Sure…why not, but I pick tha’ place. Can ya deal with that?”Rent-A-Hero:”Don’t you forget Teresa…I am RENT-A-HERO…CAHMPION OF THE CRUSIERWEIGHT NATION! There’s not a place I haven’t been…don’t believe me, check my RP archives.”..::/::>>Rent-A-Hero grins smugly at the camera with a thumbs up, showing his mastery of the cheap plug as Teresa replies with a huge smile on her face..::/::..Teresa:”Good…I got just tha’ place. Hehe…” Rent-A-Hero: -gulp- “Last time I heard “hehe”…I was tied to a flagpole with my boxers over my head….that’s never a good sign.”..::/::..Teresa puts the pedal to the metal, the truck letting out a roar that could rival a lion’s as it speeds down the highway…the fate of their evening resting in Teresa’s hands. The scene fades slowly, our hero developing a smile, knowing that he may have just brightened the day of his new sidekick, Teresa..::/::..WHAT DOES TERESA HAVE IN MIND FOR THE EVENING?! WILL RENT-A-HERO LIVE TO TELL ABOUT IT?! WHAT’S UP WITH RENT-A-HERO’S MUSICAL TASTES?! FIND OUT…SAME RENT-A-TIME…SAME RENT-A-CHANNEL!!! ---- View Rent-a-Hero's Biography
