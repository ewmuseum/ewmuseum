There's...no...more...beer

9 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2-9-03 Date: 2-9-03Location: Australian OutbackTitle: There's...no...more...beer! Adam and AJ are now sitting in the front as thegirls have moved to the back. Adam looks back to bothof them sleeping much as they were some hours ago. AJpunches him in the shoulder. Stryker: OW! What the f*ck? Adam rubs his shoulder as AJ laughs. Trinidad: What? You weren’t paying attention. Ihad to get your attention somehow. Stryker: Gee AJ saying, ‘Hey Adam!’ didn’tenter your mind did it? Trinidad: Of course not. Stryker: That’s what I thought. What were yousaying anyway? Trinidad: What was that message Jaslene gaveyou earlier? Stryker: Jesus Christ! Did you have to punch mefor that? She told me that I have a match on Tuesdayagainst Skylar Green. Trinidad: Oh…and yeah I did have to punch youfor that. I will not be ignored Goddamit! So thismeans we are going to have to get you back at areasonable time huh? Stryker: Seems that way, yes. Figures too, Ifinally get away and what happens? I get roped into amatch. I thought I was going to get some time off toheal myself but oh well… Trinidad: Things could always be worse. Stryker: Yeah that’s most certainly true. Trinidad: Home sweet home! The Range Rover comes to a sudden stop as the girlsin the back jerk forward. Jaslene: GODDAMIT AJ! I could’vebroken my neck! Trinidad: But you didn’t and that’s theimportant thing. AJ smirks as he opens the door and gets out. Hewalks to the back and opens the door. Jaslene stepsout and smacks him in the shoulder. She storms aroundthe car and to the back. Amira gets out of the carslowly. She’s holding her head. Trinidad: Are you ok bebe? Amira: Yeah…I just got WOKEN UP BY ANINCONSIDERATE JERK! She gets into his face. Adam laughs as he pulls asmall suitcase from the back of the vehicle. He slamsthe door back down as he picks up Jaslene’s bag aswell as his own. Amira walks past AJ. Adam waits forAmira before continuing on. She leads them into thehome. Adam steps into the home and is instantlyknocked back outside by an unseen force. AJ comesrunning up and helps pick Adam up off of theground. Stryker(Dusts himself off): What thehell was that? A kangaroo comes out of the doorway. Jaslene standsoff to the side, mouth agape just staring at thekangaroo. Trinidad: HANK! Come on buddy! The kangaroo looks at AJ and then at Adam and cockshis head to the side. Hank lowers his body a bitrealizing he’s done something wrong. AJ walks over toHank and places his hand on the kangaroo’s head. Stryker: So this is…ugh…Hank? The son of abitch sure packs a punch. Trinidad: Yeah sorry about that. I guess hethought I was the one coming inside. He was probablypissed because I didn’t come home with Amira. Amira: Your damn right he was pissed! He wasabout to fight with me but lucky for him he knewbetter than that. Amira turns and heads into the house, Jaslenefollows her with the bags in her hand. Adam walks overand stands in front of Hank and just looks him up anddown. Trinidad: What’s the matter? Never seen akangaroo before or something? Stryker: Well not up close anyway. I’ve seenthem in zoos and whatnot but never like this. Does hebite? AJ laughs as Adam rubs Hank’s head between hisears. Trinidad: Well he must like you since he hasn’tattacked you yet. Stryker: Attacked me? Trinidad: Oh yeah, Hank here is a fighter. Ifhe doesn’t like someone he usually lets the personknow pretty quickly. But I guess he likes you, that oris upset that he hit you and not me. Either way worksout. Stryker: Oh… Trinidad: Ok now that we are here are you readyto go out and have some fun? Adam looks perplexed as AJ walks into the house.Adam stands outside still petting Hank. AJ reemergesfrom the house with a couple of coolers under hisarms. He walks right past Adam. Stryker: Were are you going? Trinidad: WE my friend are going outthere. AJ points in some random direction out past theRange Rover. He drops the coolers down to the groundand heads around the back of the home. Adam stilllooks confused as AJ disappears around the house. Stryker: What the hell is going on around here? A roar can be heard from the other side of the house. Stryker: I wonder if I should be gettingworried or not? A Hummer comes flying around the house. AJ brakes andturns the wheel coming to a stop right in front of astunned Adam Stryker. Trinidad: Well? Are you going to get in or areyou just going to stand there looking like an idiot? Adam shakes his head and reaches out for thehandle. He opens the door but before he can get in,Hank jumps up and into the front seat. Adampauses. Stryker: There is no way I’m going to let afriggin’ kangaroo ride shotgun over me. Trinidad: Come on Hank, back to the back seatwith you now. AJ motions to the back seat. Hank looks down atAdam before moving back. Adam reaches down and picksup the coolers before he steps up and into the car.Just as he pulls the door close AJ hits the gas andtakes off. Adam gets slammed back into the seat. Stryker: JESUS AJ! Slow down! Trinidad: Come on, what’s life if you don’tlive a little? AJ puts the peddle to the floor as they head up ahill. Trinidad: I love this hill! Hang on! Adam screams out as the Hummer ramps off the top ofthe hill and hangs in mid air for a moment beforecrashing back down. Stryker: AHHHHHHHHH~! That was awesome! Can wedo it again? AJ laughs as he reaches back and opens one of thecoolers. He pulls out a beer and tosses it to Adambefore reaching back again and grabbing one forhimself. He opens it up as they continue to fly in theoutback. Stryker: So where exactly are we going? Trinidad: Don’t worry about it. Just sit backand enjoy the ride. Adam takes a drink from his beer as a third can isheard opening. Adam looks to the back where Hank isholding a beer. He does a double take as Hank takes adrink from the can. Stryker: Huh? Wha? What in the hell? Hankdrinks? Trinidad: Yeah I told you that before remember?At the bar that night? Adam gives him a blank stare. Trinidad: No? Stryker: Come on do you not remember how shitfaced I was that night? I hardly remember anythingafter that first drink. I barely remember you eventelling me about Hank in the first place. Trinidad: Oh, well yeah he drinks and drinkslike a pro. We usually go out to this place, get pissass drunk and rumble for a bit. There is nothing likea kangaroo fight to get you in great shape before areal fight. Damn things are fast. Adam nods as he finishes the beer and drops it intothe back seat. He looks out across the outback andjust admires the scenery. Trinidad: How are you feeling about this matchwith Skylar? Stryker: I don’t really know to be honest withyou. I’m a bit nervous I haven’t been in the ring in aweek or so, not since the last DWO PPV. And even thenI took a beating. Trinidad: Yeah but your first PCW match, I’msure you’ll make an impression on them one way oranother. Where’s your match on the card? Adam smirks as he reaches back and pulls outanother couple of beers. He hands one to AJ beforeopening his. Stryker: Last match before the main event noless. Trinidad: Wow, starting high up are we? Stryker: Yeah but I can’t somehow thinksomething’s not right. I don’t feel comfortablestarting so high up on the card for my debut match. Trinidad: Well you are Adam Stryker, a DWOLegend, of course they are going to start you high onthe card. I would take it as an insult if they didn’t. Stryker: I guess you’re right… Trinidad: That’s the spirit! The scene fades out as Adam pounds his beer. A few hours later. It’s night out now. The moon hangs in the sky asAdam, AJ and Hank all sit around a fire. Adam rubs theback his neck as he leans back up against a rock. Stryker: Damn…you weren’t getting Hank hitslike a freaking tank… Trinidad: Hey man I warned you earlier. Atleast you had some beer in you to numb the pain a bit. Stryker: Yeah that’s true. But damn he’s quick. Adam looks over to Hank who has a beer in his hand.Hank looks back and kind of smiles at Adam. AJ gets upand takes another beer from the cooler. He looks intothe cooler and comes back out with a worried look onhis face. Stryker: Something wrong AJ? Trinidad: There’s…no…more…beer! Hank looks up quickly as though he knows exactlywhat AJ is talking about. The kangaroo gets up andreaches down into it’s pouch. Stryker: What the hell is Hank doing? Trinidad: Oh Hank, you have some left in yourstash? The kangaroos retracts it’s arm as the moonlightgleams off of a bottle. Hank tosses the bottle to AJwho in turn opens it up and takes a big swig. Trinidad: Ahhhhhh…Old Creek whiskey, Hankalways keeps a secret stash on him just for times likethese. Adam laughs as AJ tosses him the bottle. Adam opensit up and takes a huge swig himself. Stryker: YEWOUCH! That’s some strong stuffthere…but damn it’s good stuff. Adam hands the bottle to Hank before leaning backand staring up at the night sky. Stryker: Wow… Trinidad: I think it’s time to turn in. Hank takes one last drink from the bottle beforereturning it to the pouch it came from. The scenefades out as AJ lays down in front of Hank and Adamstares up at the stars.
