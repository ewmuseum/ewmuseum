Sliding to the end... of Level 2

Date: not entered The third installment of 'Rich Payne' had seen our pathetic heros attempts to save his best friend continue with the small interference of insignificant staff characters. Throughout the first three installments of the dramatic times, we had seen Ryan Moore abducted... the first battle between 'Rich Payne' and the legions of the Dark Army of Doctorhood had taken place, two of the smaller guards being the characters to feel the first real painful shots of Rich Sherwin. As that scene, we jumped up to the second floor of the building... where Rich Sherwin was indeed, attacked by a former college of 'Community General' Hospital... the man known as Jack Stewart, came back to the grounds looking for revenge... looking for revenge from the days of 'New True Internet Championship Wrestling'... when the 'Pretty Cool Guys' trio made their first real appearence on Television. The fight was something that shouldn't be desired... or watched on the box again for that matter, but it was something that made Sherwin look at himself and understand just how pathetic he has been... running around a hospital trying to save somebody who for all we know, could be plotting the very demise of 'Rich Payne' with them. What if this whole thing was nothing more than a set-up? Nothing more than a ploy to get Sherwin to come in... and attack him while he is weak? Maybe.. they'd attack him to the point where he'd submit and give all the rights of 'PCG' to Ryan Moore and Mark Sloan... making our group of teeny boppin' heros turn into a tandem of 'Youth' and 'Ancient' Age, flooding the world with music that should have been released in the era of Queen Victoria... and not the era of Rich Sherwin! The big question for everyone at the moment... was the indentity of the character that had seemingly come from the darkness and laid forth their claim to saving the life of 'Rich Payne'. Who could this one special character be as the conquest continues? Would we be stunned with the return of 'PCG's' third leg? Would we be amazed at the daring return of Nick Carter? Or hell, would Dante Jackson have come into the picture... solely to sue the crew of 'Pretty Cool' for their comments about him and Crack in one of the previous episodes? The answers for who these people could be was really rather great... and the list of suspects could go on forever and ever... but really, who would this guy turn out to be? The answers to that question... and the answer to two-hundred thousand times three coming up... in the following episode of 'Pretty Cool G'... The scene had been set...The battlelines had been drawn...Dr Jack Stewart stood over 'Rich Payne' like a bad nightmare, glancing around the immediate area, never losing track of the character that stood down the corridor opposite him. The intensity around the immediate area was beginning to amass, electricity was beginning to form between the parties as the level of their staredown began to increase. 'Payne' stopped on the floor and began to slowly move back, creating some space between both himself and Jack Stewart. The silence was devestating... you could cut through it with a knife... something that one person would like to do to somebody else at this second in time, but for the benefit of everyone seated... or standing in their homes, I won't reveal any names because Jack Stewart informed me that I would die if I told anyone any kind of information. He stepped forward a few paces, increasing the distance between both himself and 'Payne', decreasing the distance that would follow between himself and the cloaked figure. Stewart stopped in his tracks and clenched his fists, resetting the bones, getting ready for whatever confrontation would follow us within the next few moments of scenic action. The cloaked figure stood high and mighty, looking upon Jack Stewart like he was nothing more than common street trash. 'Payne' looked over in suprised, watching the eager grin forming on the face of the cloaked character slide to its greatest width. Jack was about to take a step forward, when the cloaked one raised a hand and forced the Doctor to stop on the spot. We slowly began to zoom in on the cloaked character as his right hand dropped down to his side again. 'Do... not... attempt... to defeat... what you... do... no know..', is everything that the cloaked figure managed to say before looking off into the direction of another corridor next to him. Jack Stewart was slowly beginning to lose his patience with it all, but he was not about to let go of the only thing in his possession at this moment in time. The figure returned his gaze to Dr Jack, making him feel a little uneasy as the piercing began. 'Rich Payne' pushed himself up against the back wall and pulled himself back to his feet. The 'hero' of the story continued to look over past Jack Stewart at the character that was still standing in the middle of the corridor... the magical fans from one of the rooms, blowing past him and allowing his cloak to float in the air... breezing past, making him look a little tougher than he should really be. Our hero jumped into Jack Stewart and took him to the ground with a primitive looking tackle, the character turned away from the following brawl and made his way back into the confinements of the elevator from where he came. 'Payne' looked up from the grounded Jack and was able to catch a glimpse of the cloaked character wave to him before vanishing behind the doors of steel. Jack began to writh around beneath 'Payne' and it wasn't long before Rich was taking the long flight off the back of the Doctor. Almost immediatly, the two men got back to their feet and looked one another in the eye... the level of intensity began to rise again, the emotion began to flow like nothing had ever done in the history of the world. Stewart stood tall, looking at 'Payne' and laughing to himself a little bit before getting into that all familiar fighting stance. 'Payne' realized the events that were going to follow within the next few minutes or so... and he too stood ready for a fight that could very well determine the next stage of the rescue mission. Jack started the movement by faking a kick, raising his leg up off the ground somewhat to confuse and discomfort 'Payne'... who foolishly took the bait and allowed himself to cower back a few paces, expecting the kick to pass and fall fiendishly too soon. Jack laughed again, realizing that 'Payne' had allowed himself to be suckered by the fake. 'Payne... you are still nothing when placed in comparision to me... I have spent the best part of my working life, protecting the very innocence of scum like you... well, no more... nothing of that sort will ever happen again... and when I take all the jewelry from your prying cold fingers... you will remember the name of.... ', Jack shouts before taking in a large amount of air, inhailing very deeply before slowly allowing all of the air to escape with, 'Jackdust...'. 'Payne' looked in amusement at the way that Jack Stewart was getting into the swing of things, pretending to be one of the more lacklustre members of the real talent roster of the World Wrestling Entertainment. He decided to take a few paces back, sliding toward the elevator that he was now on the side of. Stewart, being the bright spark that he was.. quickly saw the plan before it began to unfold, and charged at 'Payne' with his arms out, looking to nail something of a 'tackle takedown'. 'Rich Payne' was able to use some of his technical offense now, and the future PCW Tag Team Champion took down Stewart with a well timed 'Drop Toehold.' 'Now THAT... foo... was not only, the most devestating move in the history of 'Primetime Championship Wrestling'... it was not only, the damnest move to exist in the history of Professional Wrestling as we view it upon todays modern culture... and By God... you should be thankful that I had the common courtsey, to not accidently place one of those steel contraptions in the way...', 'Rich Payne' declared before standing up tall and beginning to walk away from Jack Stewart. The 'battle' was apparently over... sooner that it had 'apparently' begun... Jack Stewart, remaining on the floor, looking dazed and confused about the events that had just taken place. The distraction from the cloaked warrior... the untimely move again 'Rich Payne' whilst in pure battle... and the failure within his proposed mission to eliminate 'Payne' and prevent him from moving to the next floor of the building. 'Rich' took three paces away, when Stewarts voice was heard... it was faint... but detectable nonetheless. 'Your... momma... is... so... fat... that her body mass will give her a heart attack soon.', Stewart cried, laughing with hysterial as he expected the effects of the rather lame 'Your Momma' joke to settle in and prevent 'Payne' into making a mistake that he may look upon and curse in the future of his existance within this planet. 'Payne' stopped in his tracks, the first signs of the reaction beginning to show its ugly head to both Dr Stewart and the watching world. Everyone could sense the ironic tension build again (... and they say its only going to be worthwhile placing one element of tension in... pfft, people these days... they know about as much as Madonna did when she was flashing her tits for a pound-a-time.), it was moving through the roof at a rate faster than Superman and the Speeding Bullet that really did manage to pass him at one time. 'Payne' walked over to Dr Jack, kneeling down infront of his face and looking deep into his eyes, firstly attempting to phase him out with quick game of 'Stares'... but soon decided to resort to the verbal comeback when he realized that the game wasn't going to be as easy as previously assumed. 'Jack Stewart... my dear boy, you just happen to remind me of Akiyamahama at this moment in time... you prance around, thinking that you're a God.. thinking that you're something that nobody else is going to be able to defeat... you consider yourself to be something that has no equal... something that doesn't understand the meaning of being lonely. I watch you... walk around like a fancy assed bad man... but you know, one of the simplest moves in the history of Professional Wrestling cut down your era of madness... that very same move, it made you look like the most pathetic individual on the face of this Earth... and you know what else? The very fact, that Akiayamahama is going to fall to a move like this at 'Gold and Glory'... I'm damned sure that just goes to show us, the winners are the ones that dupe everyone else.', 'Payne' informed him before knocking him around the face and getting back to a vertical basis. 'Payne' looked down at Stewart for a few more moments, feeling a little bad about the course of action that had to be taken. He turns away from the downed Doctor and continues his walk to the elevator, pressing the green button on the left hand side before standing still and awaiting the arrival of the mystical device. Within the moments, the doors for the elevator begin to open... making that very interesting swooshing sound as they do. We see 'Rich Payne' take a few paces into the elevator before turning around and looking into the direction of the device thats recording all of this top class data. 'Ryan Moore... you seem to have really been taken into the Hell Mouth of this location... you really seem to be somewhere thats going to take me longer to find... for the sake of all things PoPtastic in this world... may you hold onto however many strands of life you have left...', Sherwin calmly said before pressing the '3' button from the Floor Selection and looking on blankly as the doors closing began to close... moving together before shutting completely and allowing the movement of the contraption to take place. As the scene began to fade off into the department of darkness, we were left to wonder, who IS that God Damn character wearing the cloak? Why didn't they do anything exciting in this chapter of the story? Does God know that Jack Stewart used a 'Your Momma...' joke thats so lame it still wasn't popular back in 19BC? To find out the answers to these questions and a whole lot more... I strongly suggest that you tune into the next episode of Pretty Cool G...
