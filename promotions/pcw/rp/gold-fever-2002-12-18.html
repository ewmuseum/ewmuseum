Gold Fever

18 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12-18-02 RP 2 PPV 22nd It first started with the Taxman, Tim Walden with the Front Office and the chokehold they had on the PCW Roster. Teresa was a time bomb ready to explode. Tim’s brown nose and stupid clipboard brought our Top Rope Temptress to her peak so to speak. With each blow she threw, he went down only to bounce back up and throw it back in her face. In time this bommerange effect wore Teresa to a forced leave of absence and Walden winning the CW Title. Never again. So many times she’s came close to winning a title belt. She would make it to the big match only to fall at her opponent’s mercy and suffer defeat. The belt would be so close she could almost grab it. To her dismay, Teresa would always slip. Oh, she can do it. She knows she can. All of us knows she can. On Decimation she will grasp the victory by the buckles and around her waist it will go. That night my friends, will be the night Teresa Tomas blows the roff off PCW. Her and her fellow rednecks will make Steve Austin look like a preschooler. It continues with Brandi. Also a member of Front Office, she is like the fruit that went bad. Once she had a heart of gold and was PCW’s sweetheart. With time the dark and vile force of Kannon and his followers drew her in their web of deceit. Now she is no less rotten than the rest of the Front Office worms. Brandi took it upon herself to butt in and help Walden when Teresa knew she brougth him down for good. To Teresa, Brandi isn’t but a pretty face with a large book of moves. The same book which Teresa overcame and defeated. That wasn’t the first and more than likely won’t be the last. Now Teresa has returned. Quiet as a mouse she comes and as a lioness to her prey she will attack Kano. Ahh yes, Kano, was one man she had no beef with. Why did she even bother helping him on Fusion? Let’s just say she got into the Christmas spirit of giving, giving her aid. Or maybe it’s the fact Teresa can’t stand to sit back while Brandi and the rest of the Front Office holds all the gold. In return, Kano attacked Tomas and left with the belt. On Decimation, there is no stopping. Teresa will not give in or give up until she defeats Kano and for the first time in her life, SHE will become champion. All fear the furry of a woman, especially a woman with gold fever. Of course I’m ready. Mom, I’m getting another chance to be a champion. Teresa rests her right arm over the steering wheel while her left hand holds a cell phone to her hear. She is talking to her mother in Nashville. The midnight purple stepside coasts down a highway on this fine sunny cool day. Yes mother, I am mentally ready too. Don’t worry. I’ll be just fine. Once I finish Kano with a touch of Southern Comfort, it’s all over but the crying and you’re baby girl will be Television Champion. Teresa rolls her eyes listening to her mother’s motherly chatter and worries about her only daughter fighting in the square circle with large men. What about Kannon? He ain’t shit. Didn’t you see what happened last Sunday when he had the nerve to set me up for his Halmark? That’s right Duke stoped it in a heartbeat. Like I said before, I dealt with him in NTICW. I know how the sleezbag works. Besides, he’s too absorbed in his rage for Duke. Yes Mom, I’ll bring the boys by next time we’re in the neighborhood. They can’t wait to try your famous chili. Yeah, I love you too. I’ll call you after Sunday ok? Bye. The cell phone is hung up and placed on the seat beside Teresa. She turns on the radio and speeds down the open highway to an old Guns and Roses tune.
