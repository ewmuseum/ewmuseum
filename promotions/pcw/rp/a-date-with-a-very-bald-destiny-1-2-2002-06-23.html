A Date With A Very Bald Destiny (1/2)

23 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 23/06/02 Although most of the limelight is given to those Scientologists in the media, be it actors such as John Travolta and Juliet Lewis, TV stars like Kirstey Alley and Nancy Cartwright or wrestlers like Xavier Kannon.... Most Scientologists are normal people, going about their jobs and providing for their family.... However, a lot on the world don’t treat them as “Normal”... Thus, Hubbardville was born. The highest earning Scientologists made it possible for a purpose built City to be constructed in a sleepy corner of Wisconsin where Scientologists could work and live beside each other free from prejudice and fear..... A place where they can set off for work knowing that the enemies of their faith cannot touch them. As for Scientologists with enemies, they don’t come any more plentiful than Xavier Kannon’s.... Over the last few months they have featured Jeremy Howard, Duke Williams and now Miyagawa.... But Kannon has topped that, he also has an entire Religion on his back.... The Hari Krishnas. They have worked in the shadows plotting their revenge for the acts he has perpetrated upon them, and now, as he returns to his home, bruised and battered after Fusion... He will have to face their payback... While Howard only took his sense of fearlessness, while Duke only took his blood and while Miya has only sabotaged his wedding... The Hari Krishnas will want to take something more precious than all of them... .... HIS CITY. “Haha..... Time and again they have thwarted us.... But this time we have them.... AND THEIR CITY!!!” “HAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHA!!!” Our shot opens up on one of the lush Green Wisconsin hills overlooking the City of Hubbardville, the place built for the sole purpose of housing Scientologists who have fled from the oppression of the outside world.... Even from this distance the tall shimmering towers of the Financial district, and the 427 foot tall statue of L Ron Hubbard himself can all be made out. “They have no Army... Their citizens are not trained for War.... And they will not be expecting our little visit!” The camera pans away from the view of Hubbardville, slightly blurry due to looking through all the moisture in the air between camera and City.... We then see a small man, maybe 5’8 at the most with a shaven head, round glasses and an Orange robe... A Hari Krishna. Hari : “For months I have wanted to destroy Scientology, but until now they have been spread out, infesting the country.... But those Hubbard-loving idiots have just given us what we wanted, all of them in the same place!” He chuckles evilly to himself as off camera some agreeing laughs can also be heard..... The aggressive manner of this man can be explained as we zoom in on his bare right bicep where the initials “XK” are present with a big Black cross through them... Hari : “I still remember the time when Scientologist and Hari Krishna lived at peace beside one another.... Getting along amicably in the pigeon hole that was “Minor Religion”.... But on November the 12th 2001 a line was crossed....” The camera zooms out and sweeping, dramatic orchestral music plays..... Behind the man we see a line of 10 more.... Then behind them rank upon rank upon rank of Orange robed bald men stretching as far as the eye can see..... We cut back to the man who appears to be the commander as he turns and looks upon the Hari Krishna army he has assembled. Hari : “The one they call...” His face screws up in disgust. Hari : “KANNON.... Said he would watch our stall at the Los Angeles convention of Major and Minor Religions.... BUT HE LIED... HE DUPED US.... He told them that we had given up and all converted to Dianetics in order to sign up OUR VISITORS!!! Breaking all codes, as well as the one about being rude to those who’s stalls are next to yours!” His eyes narrow, thinking back to the great betrayal that led to the bitter, if so far extremely non-violent rivalry between Hari Krishnas and Scientologists... Hari : “But within the minute, we shall descend upon their little City.... Hubbardville.... And claim it as our own. Our tailors will make a huge Orange robe to put over their grotesque image of Hubbard, and our metallurgists will shave his metal head of all it’s ha-” “HA! YOU THINK WE DIDN’T KNOW YOU LITTLE BALD MIDGETS WOULD TRY SOMETHING LIKE THIS!?!?!?” From the row of officers behind him leaps the tallest Hari Krishna, removing a piece of skin coloured latex from his head to reveal Brown hair and as he takes of his glasses REVEALS HIMSELF AS FAMOUS SCIENTOLOGIST GIOVANNI RIBISI!!! Ribisi : “You will never take over Hubbardville, and now I know your plan you are doomed!” All of the Hari Krishnas around him just stop and stare. Hari : “So you now know we are going to invade Hubbardville... Which you must have known already to infiltrate us.... Only then you were safe in the City.... And now you are surrounded by us...” The smile slowly fades from Giovanni’s face as he realises his predicament and his bottom lip starts to quiver, his eyes shooting in all directions and a bead of sweat makes it’s way down his brow... Ribisi : “Er... I think we went a little wrong.... They said this was an important mission and that I ha-” Before he even comes to the realisation that Scientology sold him out as punishment for making such bad movies as the Mod Squad and bringing a bad name upon their faith he is besieged by Orange robes, his form being lost in the mass of attacking bodies. “AGGGGHHHHHHHHH!!!!!” The Hari Krishna, who are free of their “No harm to others” vow after making another to bring down Kannon and Scientology at all costs pile on and on with Ribisi’s cries muffled out through the sheer weight of humanity that is upon him... .... Cutting back to the lead Hari Krishna we see his smiling face turn to his Generals and Armies... Hari : “THEY MAY HAVE TAKEN OUR POTENTIAL MEMBERS..... BUT THEY’LL NEVER TAKE OUR... Um... What do they want to take.... Er... ATTACK!!!” The sound of drums rhythmically beating starts up followed by horns and slowly, marching, the Army makes it’s way down the hill towards Hubbardville, rank upon rank of soldier walking in time to the music in a truly awesome scene. “Um.... Like... Did someone roll a HUGE bag of Oranges down that hill over there?” Our shot opens up on the fiancé of Xavier Kannon, Miss Eleanor Hall... She has a hand held over her pale Blue eyes to keep the sun out as she stares into the distance... ... We zoom out further to see that she is standing beside her man on Wehatenicolekidman Boulevard in Hubbardville, where the east end of the street has a clear view right into the hills surrounding the city, which have turned from brilliant Green to menacing Orange... “In Hubb-.... Wait, greater than that.... IN XENU’S NAME WHAT IS THAT!?!??!?!” Wrestling’s only Scientologist, wearing a plaster over his forehead after he sent himself and Miyagawa crashing down through the announce table stares transfixed on the scene... His long, Brown leather coat blowing in the breeze along with his long Red hair. “It’s them!” “Let’s get out of here!” “We can’t win!” “Whoa... It’s like Zulu.... Except they aren’t Zulus!” All around them the citizens go into mass panic, cars skid and crash and the pedestrians run for their cars, their homes, or cover.... All around the City a public announce system is bellowing out a repeated message... “DO NOT BE ALARMED..... DO NOT BE ALARMED....“ Amidst the storm of panic the 1st couple of Scientology stand calmly, Kannon just slapping his forehead with a palm as Ellie~! watches the hill, thinking how cool it is that they are now Orange despite it’s implications. Xavier : “Great, this is the LAST thing I need! As if Miyagawa being my best man, having to fight Duke’s fucking Jobber Crew AGAIN on Tuesday with that Japo-American FREAK thrown in.... I KNOW he will have some sort of Buck’s party lied up before he finally shafts me on OUR big day.... Then I’ll get locked in a Cage with 3 guys who, oh, ONLY HAPPEN TO HATE MY GUTS.... AND NOW THIS SHIT HAS TO HAPPEN?!?!?” Still, all around him is in disarray, people fleeing in opposite directions... One panicked man grabs Xavier by the shoulders, shaking him, his eyes wide open and blood shot. Man : “WE HAVE TO GET OUT OF HERE!!! THEY’LL KILL US ALL! ALL I TELL YOU!” The pre-occupied Gold Patron Meritorious unceremoniously shoves the man down to the floor. Xavier : “Although your presence in this fine City is enough for me NOT to call you a PEON, still, do not put your hands on me.... I have too many things to think about than to be worried about some little bald headed homo colour wearing weirdos!” The man climbs up to his feet and quickly dusts himself off before running off to an alleyway, nearly getting hit by two cars as he does so. Beside the annoyed Scientologist, Ellie~! just surveys all that is going on around her, a look of pure joy on her face. Eleanor : “Like... YAY THIS!!! It’s like, Independence Day, only the sky isn’t on fire and those big round thins aren’t...” She pauses to take a look up at the clear Blue sky... Eleanor : “....Aren’t above us.... Although if they were, they’d like BLOW UP all the buildings and they’d all explode and stuff.... That’d RULE!“ Kannon ignores his valet’s excited words and sneers, his face going Red. Xavier : “I BET MIYA TOLD THEM TO DO THIS!!!! I’m sure one of those little weirdos he hangs around with... The spotty one or the Goblin one is a Hari Krishna or something and he told them I’d be here... WELL IT ISN’T GOING TO WORK!!! Blowing up my City with me in it is NOT going to stop me stop you being at MY WEDDING!!!” His words confuse Eleanor, which isn’t unusual, but this time she is correct. Eleanor : “Um... If you get blown up won’t it kinda, stop the Wedding happening? Oh, and by the way, try not to get blown up until after next Sunday.... If you can...” He looks to her, not even knowing what he is going on about in his anger. Xavier : “What are you talking about?!?!?” Just as the Prep goes to reply the voice of the Hari Krishna leader booms out, echoing through every street in the City causing the widespread panic to stop, everyone freezing on the stop at the sound of his ear-splitting voice. “WE HAVE YOUR CITY SURROUNDED.... THE HARI KRISHNAS HAVE FINALLY GOT THE SCIENTOLOGISTS ON THEIR KNEES..... ONLY ONE THING WILL SAVE THIS PLACE NOW....“ Everyone turns to each other and they all inquire as to what it might be before they are again silenced by the voice. “SEND US THE ONE YOU CALL...... KANNON!!!“ Everyone gasps and looks at Xavier who is still blinded with anger at the timing of their revenge... Beside him Eleanor stands with her hands on the hips of her Blue flared jeans.... Looking more insulted than fearful. Eleanor : “Hey Mr Baldy Men! I’m like his fiancé!” She waves her huge Diamond encrusted ring around. Eleanor : “So if he is, like, going somewhere then I WANNA GO THERE TOO!” The leader of the Hari Krishna Army is heard sighing. “SO BE IT....... SEND US THE YAPPY LITTLE ONE WITH HIM AS WELL!!!“ She turns to all the scared faces around her, pointing to herself proudly... Eleanor : “Me...... As....... Well!” “Xavi, don’t go!” “We will stand and fight Gold Patron Meritorious Kannon!” “Lets give them Ribisi instead... Wait, where is he?” All around the Scientologist rally around Kannon in a moment of tear jerking solidarity.... Deciding to stand and fight against insurmountable odds and far superior numbers in order to protect the one they hold in such high regard. Xavier : “Where the fuck are those bald little shit heads.... Someone drive me there!” The men and women around him stand agasp, not believing what he is saying. “You can’t go... They’ll kill you!” “Don’t do it Xavier.... Don’t do it!” His pissed off facial expression says it all as he answers back to their pleas. Xavier : “Just last night I put myself through a fucking TABLE! I’ve had Greek God throw me down 15 feet, I’ve had my head spit open with a Blackjack, my Scientological blood has been spilled by a redneck hick, I’ve been powerbombed through solid wood AND I AM NOT GOING TO LET A BUNCH OF BUDDHA WANNABES PISS ME OFF ANY MORE THAN I ALREADY AM!!!” Our shot begins to fade to Black as we watch the defiant Scientologist start his trek down Wehatenicolekidman Boulevard to the edge of the City where he will meet his destiny... Or death at the hands of the Hari Krishna Army unless Ellie~!, who is in tow can annoy them out of the state. Eleanor : “Can we stop for a Milkshake on the way?” The first couple of Scientology disappear from view as we finally fade to Black on the tearful faces of Hubbardville’s residents... Not knowing what will become of one of their heroes.....
