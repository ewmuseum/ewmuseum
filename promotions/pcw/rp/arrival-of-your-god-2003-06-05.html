Arrival of your GOD!

5 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6-5-03 As Ganker sits in the local strip joint watching the strippers dance around their little poles, he eyes around to see if he recognizes anybody. Ganker then lifts his beer up and finishes it off in one gulp. Ganker then gets up and exits the strip joint. The scene shifts to Ganker walking in the night along the streets of Seattle, Washington with his hands stuffed into the pockets of his black leather jacket. Ganker walks along a couple blocks before he gets to his black Corvette. Ganker unlocks the door and just sits there in the driver’s seat staring out the windshield. Ganker eventually puts the key in the ignition and starts his Corvette and drives off. The scene shifts to Ganker pulling into a nice house, probably the nicest on the block. Ganker pulls into the garage and then walks into the house. Ganker gets a soda from the kitchen then walks into the living room and takes a seat on his black leather couch. Ganker lifts up the cushion on the arm to reveal some buttons. Ganker pushes one, and then the wall in front of him turns and reveals a wrestling wall. Ganker admires every aspect of the wall; by the way the wrestling wall is past accomplishments that Ganker Ransolver has completed, like past titles, and past contracts that are now void. Ganker hits the button again as the wall turns back around. Ganker hits another button and a remote control pops out of a slot in the front of the arm of the couch Ganker’s sitting by. Ganker flips on the television and begins to flip through the channels. Ganker gets bored and eventually just leaves the television on MTV 2, watch a few music videos for awhile. As Ganker sits, he reaches his hand into his back pocket and pulls out a slip of paper. Ganker reads it over then snickers as he reads his opponent for Tuesday Turmoil. Ganker throws the piece of paper in a pile on the desk to his right as his girlfriend Sheilla Hisser walks downstairs and sits beside Ganker, her head on his shoulder and her legs curled up on the couch cushion beside her.”Hey baby, how’s your day been?””Pretty good Sheilla, pretty damn good. I mean damn I find out I’m contracted by the PCW because Hank doesn’t wanted to finish out his long-term PCW contract!””So you’re only signed to PCW because your friend only wanted to go out with one last win to his belt?” (Ganker nods) “So you know the only reason you have this PCW contract is because of your friend whose already been signed?””Yeah and I really don’t care. Sheilla, it doesn’t matter how I got my PCW contract, it’s what I plan to do wit’ it. I want this more than any other PCW superstar, even more than Hank wanted it!””I hear you have a match already, good job.” (smiles)”Yeah I guess they really want to test me before they start putting me against the main-eventers and giving me title shots left and right.””You know you truly are full of yourself.”(smiling) “Hell it’s hard not to be when you’re as perfect as Ganker Ransolver.”“Perfect?” (smiles) “I was thinking God like.””Well I’d love to stay here and cuddle wit’ you even longer Sheilla, but I have an airship to board. I have to ready myself physically and mentally to take part my opponent piece by piece.””Who is your opponent by the way?””Menolly Xenos, some chic they’re matching me up against. Not to be sexist, because I’d say this even if she wasn’t a woman, but to me she sounds like a walk over kind of opponent.””Just remember not to take any opponent lightly, ever. That’s how you end up losing your matches! That’s why I think Hank was such a failure here and only won the extreme championship.”“Yeah well I’m not Hank, I’m freaking better!””Yeah, I’ll agree with that.”~ Ganker gets up after kissing his girlfriend and heads upstairs to pack to leave for an airship. The scene cuts off to black. OOC: Sorry so short, this is just to break him in.
