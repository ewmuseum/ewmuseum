Twas the night before Fusion

13 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 13/06/03 'Twas the night before Fusion, when all through the airshipNot a wrestler was stirring, not even a bit.Their belts were hung by the lockers with care,In hopes to defend them to lose they could not bare. The lower card were nestled all snug in their beds,While visions of main-events danced in their heads;And Emily in space and me in the air,Could not sleep too well I was struggling with fear. When outside my room there arose such a clatter,I shot out of the door to see what was the matter.Away down the corridor I flew like a flash,Turned around the corner maintaining my dash The moon on the clouds which looked just like snowSo thick I could not see the surface below,When, what to my wondering eyes should appear,But little Gaz Green pranking with cheer. With a 240volt buzzer attached to his hand,He turned to look at me when I turned and ran,My legs ran fast as hard as they can.Not wanting to be shocked by the green jester man. Now Skylar and Gaz and Tessa, Labor,Miss Moonlight E2 and myself all stood tall.The champions of PCW we were called, Everyone was against us, no friends at all. As I turned and returned to my room on cloud nine,I wondered how long this belt would be mine. To wear across my waist to carry with pride. I’d seen Jackson lose it, hell he had cried. Now once again I would have to face him.Always wondering what would happen with SyN.Knowing that it could be my last.I returned to my room and closed the lock fast. I was dressed in pyjamas from head down to toes. But you don’t need a belt with them everyone knows.I picked up my belt it looked at it deep. Knowing that this belt was not mine to keep. I had done it before but this time I thought.Things wouldn’t go right for the wanna be astronaut.To fight with Matt Jackson for the fifth dance,Was to fight with a lion who was caught in a trance Jackson knew Edwards and Edwards knew he,The question of the matter is what will come to be. Of the rising star title which I’ll have to defend A belt that I thought I would defend to the end. I sighed a deep sigh and hung the belt up,Returned to my bed and sipped from my cup,I then closed my eyes and drifted to sleep,I Had no need for counting sheep. Laughter outside followed by a scream.Showed Gaz had got someone like the cat gets the cream.Somewhere on the ship Jackson would be.Thinking of my title…thinking of me. Twas the night before fusion, where all over the airship. Not a wrestler was stirring, not even a bit.Now James was lying sound asleep in his bed.His title belt hanging over his head. To dream of Turmoil and to dream of what’s right.Cos listen up kids. James is keeping his belt that night
