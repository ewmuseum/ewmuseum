The Future Looks Bright For Me

21 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: June 21st Time passes you by, not knowing where it has gone. Does it just disappear? Does it store itself away? Can time just evaporate into thin air. The questions I ask have no point, but I still ask. Is time like air, you can not see it but it is there? Or is time non-existant, just a man made legend to make our lives easier? Time is playing with us. Time is in control and we are just a simple game for time. When you are on the run to work what are you racing? Time. When you are on your death bed and cancer is trying to take your life, what are you trying to gain? Time. Time is not your friend, time is your enemy, time is what we must defeat. To defeat time it takes time, how do you use it and beat? Time is invincible. Time always wins and time has always defeated myself. I see myself in the future. Smiling and laughing, finally reaching out to the fans and being loved for it. I am receiving all the love that my father could not give me. The love that was taken from me when I was abandoned in Toronto. This love, this love from total strangers is making life worth while. How can it be? How can these Americans who do not know me, love me? Strangly enough I seem to accept this love and give it back during my matches. Somehow I have found 'It'. The 'It' which has eluded me for the last three years. These people have helped me find 'It'. But this future, is it far or is it near? Is this a week ahead or two years? I can not handle this. I need this to be now. My future is now. I will do this. I will kick my enemy till I love my foot, I will punch him to I lose my arm, I will headbutt him till I lose my head, I will bite him till I have no teeth, then I will send him back to hell. I know that I found 'It'. 'It' was here all along, the fans had it and now I know. I stand here back in the present excited, excited for what the future will bring. But now I falter, I think of what I will have to do to reach my goal. Will I have to be cut-throat like Tyler? Or will I have to be like Miyagawa? How will I reach the top? Will I crush the little ones below me or will I fly them with me on wins of gold? I know one day I will do it. I will make it to the top and I will not allow myself to be like Tyler. I will not be the evil sadistic man who will do anything for what he wants. I will be me, I will be Mike Frampton. I will not take any BS from anyone. I will listen, learn and prosper. My future is in studying the others. I know one day with my vigor I can become the old me. The me who had made it in the Five Star corporation. I can do it again, without them. I will do it again. The light from my candle dims and I am being brought back into the dark of night. The future fades as time again has taken control, forming an alliance with night. How time and night work together to put me out. I want to know, why? Why must I be put to sleep now? Why can I not keep watching my future mezmorized. I loved it, I want to be there now. The only way to get there is the way that I do not know. I need to see more so I can learn what I had to do to get to where I am. I need to see how I won all my matches. Did I cheat? Or did I use my honest to God skills? The future is frightening but I must go on and see what I will make of it with my new found 'It. I lay awake, the stars above my head and the clouds swirling to block them out. The moon casts shadows around me as I let my eye lids shut. As sleep begins to crawl on me, I think back to the wonderful sites that I have seen this day. I know that my future for now is right where I am, but that can only last for about two more months. The future is bright, brighter than gold!
