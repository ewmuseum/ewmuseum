You did WHAT!!!!!

5 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5-5-03 “Goal!!!!!!!!” A.J. Trinidad shouts as he takes off in a dead sprint around the couch knocking over the end table, spilling all of it’s contents onto the floor, before sliding head first onto the tiled kitchen floor. The clatter in the living room brings Amira shooting out from inside the bedroom. “Soccer?” Amira questions. A.J. simply shakes his head signaling a wrong answer. “Hockey” She asks? Dusting himself off as he stands up from the kitchen floor, “No, not hockey either.” “Well then, what they hell are you yelling about?” Amira finally asks here question as opposed to guessing the answer. “Foosball!” A.J. responds, “and Crespo has been kicking my ass around the apartment all day.” Amira shoots a gaze over to the bounding Kangaroo who still has a hold of the sticks on the foosball table, then back to A.J. “You know, it certainly was a lot more quiet around here when Seymore Smoke was babysitting Crespo.” “I hardly doubt kidnapping, babysitting,” A.J. retorts as he walks back over to the foosball table. Amira cuts A.J. off in mid stride putting her hand up to his chest, her soft coral colored eyes connect with his as a slight look of concern grows on her face. “You never told me you were leaving last week,” begins Amira. “I had no idea where you went, no idea why you went, no idea if you were coming back. Now I wake up yesterday and you had returned sometime during the night, but you’ve failed to even give me a hint as to where you were. I’m sorry if I’m a bit confused here A.J. but when you left you were sick, now you seem to be healed? A.J. simply smiles down to her, “that’s because I am.” A.J. tries to step passed Amira but she shuffles her small frame back in front of him. As adorable as she looks in her short cut-off gray jogging pants, shorts, and one of his over sized white NIKE t-shirts, A.J. can sense something amiss and succumbs to her beckon for answers. “Ecuador,” A.J. simply states, “I was in Ecuador.” “Why A.J.?” Amira says as the confusion grows more definite on her face. “To get better, to relieve my pains in my head, and to confront my demons.” He answers. “The medicine here, in the United States, you never even gave it a chance to work A.J.. How do you know that you couldn’t have gotten better here?” Amira continues her interrogation. “I don’t expect you to understand Amira, but I’ll do my best to explain.” A.J. guides her over to the couch and sits her down. “In the Ecuadorian Highlands there is a ceremony where one cleanses their being completely, only then was I allowed to focus on what was really ailing me. The high shaman boils a cactus plant there bears with it the ability to focus on our demons. After the cleansing, he determines the amount of the stew each of us is allowed to drink, then the healing begins.” “What happens after you drink this stew?” Amira asks with much apprehension in her voice. “The reactions to the stew happens almost immediately after ingesting it. Reality seems skewed, in a manner not dissimilar to the affects of Lysergic Acid Diethylamide. The affects are more commonly called hallucinations, but in this controlled environment they are visions that we need to learn from. You begin to hear colors and see sounds, kind of.” A.J. trails off as he stares out the window. “YOU TOOK ACID!” Amira yells at A.J. tearing him out of his trance. “No, it wasn’t acid, it was a cactus, it was a religious ceremony,” A.J. tries to explain further. “Since that night by the lake, I’ve been able to sleep again freely of any nightmares or struggle. Since that night I have no more headaches or pain, and since that night, I finally feel at piece in my ability to compete. The only thing I’m uneasy about is what the Shaman said to me after I had taken by dose of the stew, which I had not understood, but a native man explained to me the day after on the hike back down the mountain” “You must fight with in yourself for the freedom of your soul.” “Well, what is that supposed to mean?” questions Amira. “I don’t know.” Answers A.J. “But I do know that I have a refocused outlook on life now that I am returned, despite this overwhelming feel of a natural struggle inside of me which 99% of the time my body seems to welcome. I guess I attribute it to the natural competition inside myself to be the best I can.” Amira pauses and looks up into A.J.’s eyes again, trying to peer through his eyes into his soul almost to gather for herself the information that she can tell A.J. is holding back. She places her right hand on the left side of his face which is unshaven and still slightly sullied. “If you say that you have been healed, then I am happy for you A.J., but next time please be honest with me as to where you are so I don’t worry about you, ok?” “OK, I promise,” responds A.J. “I think we should get going now, I really don’t know my way around Sana, Yemen and we should get to the arena. I hear they finally booked me in a match tonight and I can’t wait to get back in the ring.” Amira stands up and returns to her room in the hotel suite, turning back, “I’ll be out in a moment, just let me get my stuff together.” A.J. smiles to her as she disappears behind the wooden oak door, then shoots a gaze over to Crespo who is still standing idly by the foosball table. “You can’t say a word about what really happened in Ecuador, you know what would happen.” A.J. sternly demands. Crespo simply looks down at his huge over-sized Kangaroo feet, then up to A.J. giving some fashion of reassurance. A.J. picks his gym back up off the floor and moves towards the door as Crespo bounds over the couch and joins him as the two leave for the hotel lobby.
