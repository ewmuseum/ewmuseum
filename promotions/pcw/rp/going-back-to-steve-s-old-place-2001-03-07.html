Going Back to Steve's Old Place

7 March 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7th March The scene opens in a grim dusty yard. It’s the yard of a large trailer park, somewhere in the mid west. Miyagawa stands there in a pair of black stonewashed jeans, nike trainers and a long coat woollen black trench-coat, buttoned right to the top. Instead of his normal goggles, he wears a pair of sand goggles that in this weather seem like a damned good idea. The wind picks up and the sand gets gusted around the yard at quite a pace. He stops and stares at the door of the nearby trailer for a second, before carrying on walking. He stops at the next one and knocks on the door three times before waiting for it’s occupant to get his ass off the couch and open the door. Minutes pass and still nothing. Miyagawa bangs his fist on the door. Miyagawa: STEVE! OPEN THE FRICKING DOOR! Finally Steve Jabowski himself gets to the door. He’s dressed in a plain black t-shirt and a pair of brown shorts. And a pair of pink bunny slippers. Miyagawa: Nice slippers geezer. Somehow the goggle wearing man manages to stifle a laugh. Steve looks down to his feet for a second and realises he still has them on, he turns into the room and kicks them off quickly. Miyagawa: Aren't you going to let me in then? Steve: Only if you don’t say a thing about the damned slippers. Steve backs up giving Miyagawa enough room to get into the trailer. Steve: So what do you want then? Steve closes the door as he says that. Miyagawa: Oh just the usual. You know. Steve: What? Miyagawa: To rid the world of stupid Champion jobbers of course. Jesus, it was bad enough when either the Stryfe or Alan Kriegman had the title, and now that bastard son of a bitch Boda has it. And a think everyone remembers how I feel about Boda. Steve: You mean you want to stand round and scream I want to kill you at the top of your voice? Because you havn’t done that for ages. It used to be quite the money spinner. Miyagawa: Yeah something like that. Don't you think he has been a bit of a headless chicken since Stanislav went over to OSW? Steve: Well you know how I feel about that. Want a beer? Miyagawa nods. Miyagawa: Yeah sure. Steve walks over to his fridge and retrieves a beer from it and tosses it to Miyagawa, before getting one for himself. A double hiss hits the room as the cans are opened. Miyagawa: Of course tonight I get to try and fix that problem. Right the wrong that happened at the PPV and get that belt off Boda and around my own waist where it’s much safer, and frankly... better looking anyway. Steve: Boda’s defiantly come up in the ranks from a year ago when you guys last faced off several times. Miyagawa: It was only in tag team matches when he was a part of Media and I was in the Outcasts. You see he was called “Hardknocks” Chris Allen back then and he was tag teaming with Jon Kano as the Media Moguls Steve: Kano. Now that's funny. Didn't he lose it to them twice? Miyagawa: Yeah... unfortuantly. Steve: So I'm guessing that you are planning to tidy this up then? Miyagawa: Well someone's got to do it. Let's look at the facts, Boda has tried more times than anyone in history to win the PCW Championship... in fact I’d guess that he’s tried more times than Howard “Not Again” Porter has. Although I’ve got to give it to Boda because people expected success from Porter, whereas they always expect failure from Chris Sloboda. Steve: And I thought you two were friends now. Miyagawa: I wouldn’t say friends. I wouldn’t say that we even know each other very well. I’d like to think he respected me, but from what I heard he probably doesn’t. I respect him... to a degree if you count calling him a bone idle stupid useless buttfinch as a type of respect, which I’m pretty sure means that I in fact don’t respect the balding overweight retard at all. Steve pauses for a second. Steve: A simple “no” would have sufficed. Miyagawa takes a slug of beer out of the can. Steve: Of course, you know what PCW is like... there’s going to be a tonne and a half of run ins during this match. Theres the New Breed left over even though Snow is out injured, and of course Asylum and the Wild Dueces. Miyagawa: Ah the New Bitches and the Wild Dunces. Well I’ve told the guys not to come out unless Boda drags his boys out. I at least want a nice clean match, even if other people don’t seem to like sticking to the rules. At least this is a Turmoil, I’m not that good at PPVs... I don’t win very often. Steve: But the last time that a title changed on a non PPV was Ivan vs Streak on a Tuesday Turmoil wasn’t it or something... and before then you probably have to go back a year or so to find it. Miyagawa: Yeah, but at least it isn’t unheard of. And because I’m such a sport I’ll even give the over the hill looser a rematch. I mean Boda by that of course, not Kid Wonder and his sister. Steve: One of these days Wonder is going to notice you saying those things about him and he’s going to do something about it. Miyagawa: Bah. What’s the worst that he could do? Give me the Cruiserweight Title again? That would just have me be a triple champion in PCW which is completely unheard of at all in the entire history. In fact if I win the World Title tonight I go down as only the second person to hold the World Title while holding another belt. Steve: Who else? Miyagawa: Doug Troy. But if I beat Akiyama on Fusion then I’ll do everyone a huge favour. I’m going to give up the Television Title. Amazingly Steve pauses drinking his beer to here this little announcement. Steve: But still… Miyagawa gets a little irate. Miyagawa: Look, it isn’t fair me hogging belts when other people out there could be making new names for themselves. Look at the young people... Anton the Kid for instance. I’m sure he has a mother and father to support, he could sell the gold and buy his parents a house or something. Steve: Why are you bitching about Anton the Kid? Miyagawa: Kramer Wild Wing... imagine the endorsement from KFC if he won the Television Title. That’s another year’s contract at least. Steve: Kid Wonder? Miyagawa: Bah, even with the Television Title Kid Wonder isn’t credible. He wouldn’t be a credible Extreme Champion. **looks at his watch** Sorry geez, I’ve got to dash. I’m meeting Natalia in town to pick out some baby gear. Steve: For a boy or girl? Miyagawa: I’m aiming for unisex seeing as she’s convinced it’s a girl and I’m convinced it’s a boy. Steve: Can’t you find out then? Miyagawa: It would spoil the surprise. You’ve got to play the game properly if you expect to get the experience of winning it properly at the end, right? Steve: Yep. Have fun then. Miyagawa quickly downloads the dregs at the bottom of the can and hands it to Steve who throws it underarm into a wastepaper bin at the other end of the trailer as Miyagawa departs the place back into the dust.
