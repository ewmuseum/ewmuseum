The War: PINK! vs. Purple [Part VII]

23 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: July 23, 2002 The directions that Johnny Heart had given Jack Valentine were clear in the extreme, even in the gathering darkness Valentine had not found it difficult to find the entrance to the nightclub, though ordinarily he would have walked past it without knowing it was there lodged between two shop windows (Contrary to popular belief about The Heartbreakers...they don't know all the gay clubs around). Strangers looked out above his head. The two bouncers on the door wore PURPLE~! shirts with gathering damp sweat stains under the arms, although the night air was cold. They frisked him, in which he seemed to enjoy it rather much, but particularly to make sure that he was not bringing his own drink, or PINK! for that matter into the place. After a cursory check he was sent up the stairs, the three men that had arrived behind him were wearing tights, skin tight leotards and skin tight tops, and each had a purse. The bouncers accordingly started a five minute long frisk to ensure that they were not smuggling in any bottles of spirits. A very disgusting sight for a homophobic person. The stairs were steep and twisted back on themselves a couple of times before finally Valentine reached a small booth and a DJ. The music was by now deafening and the slumped figure in the booth shouted noiselessly until Valentine gave her ten dollars and the DJ left him alone. He pushed at a swing door, and the noise boomed out, he walked through. The room was large and in semi darkness, to the right was a bright oasis of a bar, on the left was a stage. PURPLE~! rectangular speakers were in each corner, a few empty pint glasses were on one speaker, there was the usual paraphernalia lying around the stage. A mixing deck had been set up close to the door and the engineer was chatting to some adoring girls. The girls had dyed black hair, white faces and black leather jackets, the usual type for this kind of setting. One was quite attractive while the other two looked absolutely horrendous. It was nice to see there were still some Lesbian Goths about. As soon as the initial impact of the scene faded, a sort of contemporary version of an old PURPLE~! speakeasy, Valentine began to realize what a mistake he had made. He was the only person in the entire room the size of an aircraft hanger to be wearing something other than PURPLE~!. He had thought that he looked quite smart when he had gone out, nice tie, fresh shirt and the new black overcoat. Here he was in a hanger full of extras from Night of the Living PURPLE~!. The general mood of the place seemed ugly to say the least. The mystery man would have felt right at home, but then he was eight foot tall and trained to kill. Valentine disliked breaking sweat when he wasn't in the ring. He headed over to the bar, the pool of people seemed to part magically, he stayed at the edge of the space, and realized that an exceptionally drunk and aggressive man was staggering round the dance floor. A space surrounded him wherever he went. As long as you kept out of the way he would remain drunkenly staggering. Valentine let the loose cannon pass, and then the dance floor returned to its former density and he had to struggle through the heaving bodies. He got to the bar, the quicker he got served the quicker he got out of here. He placed an elbow on the bar and tried to catch the barman's eye. After what seemed an age, or about two dance mixes over the PA, he got a pint of PURPLE~! punch. Picking it up he felt his elbow still sticking to the bar. The elbow of his good overcoat was sticky with what looked like soup, but probably wasn’t. It had diced carrots. The bar was apparently reserved for people with a freehold lease on the barstool and a need for continuous alcohol. One group had gone through a couple of rounds of pints in the time that it had taken him to get served. He tried to find somewhere to avoid physical violence and drink his PURPLE~! punch. The place had a lower floor which included the bar and dance floor, there was also an upper floor which seem marginally quieter, he headed up the spiral staircase. The band had started by now, when the roadies came on they got an enormous round of applause, now the band had actually arrived the noise was of a painful volume. From the raised viewpoint Valentine could see that there were bouncers on each corner of the upper parapet pointing to trouble makers. Troublemakers were gobbing at the stage, the aggressive drunk was still circling unhindered in his own personal patch of clear dance floor. The two huge bouncers on the dance floor grabbed at a student in a V-neck, and looked up at the pointing bouncers, one shook his head, the student was dropped to the floor again, and they grabbed someone else, this one got a nod, and he was dragged protesting from the dance floor. The crowd parted and down the stairs he went. Valentine was not sure whether the student had actually gobbed at the stage. It did not seem to make any difference. The singer was helping a girl in a black leather bikers cap onto the stage, and then starting on another number, no more junky business. It was like sensory deprivation, Valentine could hear nothing apart from the PA, he could smell nothing but the mix of cigarette smoke and PURPLE~! Punch, reality itself seemed to be getting simplified and blurred round the edges. He had finished his drink, he was still alive, time to go. The bar was still as busy, but the entire crowd on the dance floor had crowded up to the stage end, so it was relatively easy to make it to the exit. There were a few gay men arguing at the main door, but they evidently were not going to get in. He made it down the stairs, some PINK! was splattered over one of the walls, where the stair jackknifed back on itself. And finally he was out. A figure slumped in the gutter, a cut across his forehead was bleeding profusely. A rank of taxis buzzed beside the slumped figure. A long queue of people in t-shirts and other summer attire stood patiently shivering. Valentine gulped at the cold fresh breeze, he could feel the stab of cold as the sweat patches under his arms were discovered by the cold. He could hear a huge roaring in his ears, it seemed to be retreating slowly. He realized with a start that he could hear again after being at the overly played 'Village People' club. A group of belligerent drunks had approached and were trying to start a fight because he had come out of 'that' nightclub. He tried to get away, but a particularly large and stupid gang member was blocking his way. Only just reacquainted with the realm of sound again it was difficult to make out what the jabbing and pointing group wanted. One caught on his coat sleeve and twisted him round, he kept his feet and they backed off slightly, he could feel the assailant tensing to twist him round again. Valentine punched clenched fingers into the throat of the man, a singularly unpleasant groan broke through the ocean of rumbles and hiss that were the residue of the noise in the club. Maintaining momentum Valentine ran, a superkick of steel toed brogues created a gap in the circling group. The street seemed empty, there was no chance of doubling back, he ran till his lungs felt like bursting. He was on some wasteland, some derelict buildings poked dirty stubby fingers in the air. He had not actually run far, but he had sprinted while the pursuing gang had jogged, they were now closing as he bent double gasping for breath. The dirt was cinder and clinker, if he made it beyond the stubby walls he might lose them. The ground changed to concrete, some huge factory or warehouse must have stood here, now only some walls and the floor remained. He managed a quick jog, then lost his footing slipping and falling. He picked himself up, his hands were raw and skinned. He brushed the dirt off his coat cursing the whole evening, mainly for the damage it had done to his good coat. He started running again, his every footstep rang out loud and clear, he had stepped on some broken glass and it was lodged hard into the leather sole of his shoe now. The gang were close behind him, he could hear their voices. He turned round, he could see their faces, there was nothing at hand that could be turned into a weapon. The closest he had to a weapon was his wrestling background, this was it, it was the end, he could not hope to out-run them or escape them. They slowed and spread out. He slipped off the overcoat with regret revealing his PINK! shirt, up until that night it had been his smartest overcoat. If he had had any sense he would never have got involved in all this, he could have just not worried about Johnny's apprehensions and stayed with the good forces of the PINK!, or prepare for his match with Akiyama, Politically Incorrect and the Pretty Cool Guys, but now his good coat was ruined, and his pants were not much better, he picked fluff off them, and now he would get his head kicked in and probably not be able to wrestle... An animal roar tore through the cold night air, the gang parted quickly and a motorbike the size of a small car appeared at his side. It was the mystery man from the side of the PURPLE~! Mystery Man: “Come on Valentine let’s get the fuck out of here, the natives are restless tonight.” The mystery man had no helmet on and was not hanging around. The gang seemed to consist of about ten people by now. Valentine looked at the motorbike, he had never been on a motorbike before, and without a helmet it would be very dangerous. Jack Valentine: “You want me to get on that thing?” He stated in a very gay tone. Mystery Man: “Suit yourself Valentine, I’m off” With a twist the bike revved and roared. The gang members were closing fast, without a word Valentine jumped onto the back of the bike and it exploded into the night. Again the PURPLE~! mystery man was there for Valentine, and Johnny Heart was nowhere to be seen. Johnny Heart set Jack Valentine up on this mission, and it almost got Valentine killed. What kind of thoughts would Valentine have after the PURPLE~! saved his ass once again and the PINK! was out in the blue? What effect would this have on The Heartbreakers' friendship going into Sunday? Stay tuned for Part VIII to have more secrets revealed about the War: PINK! vs. PURPLE~!
