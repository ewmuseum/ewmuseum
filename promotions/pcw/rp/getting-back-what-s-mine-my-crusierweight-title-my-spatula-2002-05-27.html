Title: Getting Back What's Mine...My Crusierweight Title & My Spatula Featuring: Rent-a-Hero Date: 5-27-2002 ..::/::..Our story begins within the humble confines of the apartment we all know so well...the only apartment littered with various Star Wars memorabillia and Power Rangers merchandise....the only apartment where EGM and Gamepro magazines litter the floor as if it were carpet...we speak fo the home of PCW's very own Rent-A-Hero. Everything looks to be peaceful and in a calm state, the suns rays sneaking in through the closed blinds of our heroes apartment window as we find him laying comfortably on his leather couch, flipping through the channels on his widescreen Sony TV with a depressed wimper on his face, scratching his nude rock hard chest as he aimlessly continues his channel surfing. Looking at his yin-yang glass coffee table, our hero sighs with displeasure at the sight of that coffee table without the one item that used to make it's home there when not around the shoulder or waist of our spandex enwrapped hero...the PCW Crusierweight Championship. Having vanquished his foe Juan Borro to hold onto the title at PCW Supremacy, it came as a shock to him that after the earth-shattering announcement by the returning Phantom Booker that he would lose the gold he worked so hard to attain...the belt he climbed more then 20 feet for on a ladder at the Spring Fling pay-per-view....ripped from his grasp by an internal struggle within the company. He runs his fingers over the spot where it would normally rest and now choosing to sit up, with his Underdog imprint boxers now visible, as he takes his other hand and rests his masked face upon it. He fixes his red and yellow Flash replica mask correctly so every strand of his golden blond hair hangs out free then looking to the ceiling and saying to himself..::/::..Rent-A-Hero:'It's just not the same...I want my baby back. I took numerous plummets from the heavens and fended off all challengers just to make claim to being the PCW Crusierweight Champion...and it's ironic that I'd lose it not by pinfall, submission, forefit, or knockout...but by being STRIPPED...because that was the ONLY way anyone would ever get that belt from around my waist. Well I must say, I'm not pleased with that decision but I have a chance to restore balance to the Crusierweight universe once again...by reclaiming what I NEVER lost on Tuesday Turmoil. To think, I shall be put into a tournament with five of the PCW's preimer Crusierweights...including El Diablo, Juan Borro, The End, Kramer Wild Wing, and TMM. Well I'd like to make something apparent...I proved EVERY single earthling day I had that belt that I was the best Crusierweight Champion the PCW could ever offer and that shall be repeated yet again on Tuesday when I best these five great...I'm not preparing for a certain wrestler, or predicting I shall meet one...I'm just going in with one plan...TO RECLAIM WHAT WAS RIGHTFULLY RIPPED FORM THE GRASP OF MY HANDS!!' *Standing up, getting deeper and deeper into his speech* 'All these men may have talent...but whatever they bring, I shall bring to this tourney to the tenth power, for I WILL not let anyone get away with taking my belt without me rightfully losing it the way it was supposed to be! It's time I dipped into my sacred THUNDERCAT ARTS, because I'm as serious as a Kragglian asteroid plummeting towards a populated planet about getting back that ten pounds of gold that proved to EVERYONE that I was the strongest Crusierweight force in any universe! Turmoil shall be the proving night for everyone's favorite hero, and when the Crusierweight title is back around my waist, the world for all 230 pounders and under shall eb safe yet again I say!! He shortly returns, limping and muttering 'owwie' under his breath but still eager to dig into the frozen pizza delight he enjoys so much. He grabs the handle on the electric oven door and pull it down, the heat hurridly escaping from the oven as our hero turns his head so as not to take the full brunt of it. That moment of discomfort is well worth it as he looks back to the oven, his mouth watering and saliva flowing like a Nile waterfall as he feasts his eyes upon a circular thing of beauty...the crust golden in crispy just as he likes it, looking further to find the cheese melted evenly and bubbling to his delight, the finely diced pepperoni's, onions, peppers, and sausage dancing about on the cheese as our hero can't help but be overcome with a smile..::/::..
