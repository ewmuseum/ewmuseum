Vengeance: The Diversion...Part 3 of ?

23 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8-23-2002 Deep in the heart of the Goblin Temple of the Night is where we last left our hero, the Crusader of Truth, Venneman Pendragon. Hopelessly outnumbered, he has enlisted the help of a female Goblin, Yiko. Why is it every Goblin's name ends with that vowel sound? Eluding the guards for the moment, Pendragon prepares to let Yiko in on his plan for a diversion.... Venneman: I must say that the plan may be a bit dangerous, but it may just work.... Yiko: I'll do what you ask, good Sir Knight. Venneman: Good.... 'It was not my way to repay a kindness with the flash of cold steel, so Yiko would live to see my departure. Even if her fellow goblins were to turn upon her, she would survive. Kindness in a world of cowardly monsters is a thing that should indeed be preserved...' Venneman pulls his shirt of armor up to his chest revealing a glimmering belt, engraved with PCW TV CHAMPION... Venneman: Though I wear a replica, my real quest concerns me getting my genuine gold back. Since goblins are greedy by nature, and since I am sure you are all proud of Syko 'taking' the TV Title from me, I plan on using this belt, and you as a diversion. You shall take this replica of the title to the alcove that sits above the great hall. Yell to your fellows that an intruder dropped this...Syko's TV Title. Say that you have him penned down and that they need to come and help you with him, as he is rather big. As they rush the stairs, I will jump from the balcony, take the Jewel of the Night, and dash out the back door behind the statue....Any questions? Yiko's eyes have not moved from the glimmering belt about Venneman's waist. He snaps his fingers in front of her eyes and she comes out of her trance... Yiko: I am sorry, yes? But you know how we Goblin's get around gold, no? Venneman: Did you hear a word that I was saying? Yiko shakes her head and so Venneman gives her the spiel all over again. She listens intently as she struggles to keep her gaze from the gold... Venneman: Any questions? Yiko: How do you know there is a back door behind the statue? You have never been here before, yes? Venneman: There is ALWAYS a back door behind the statue. All the great adventurers know this. Now, let us be off. They get up and leave the room. In a second, Venneman reappears and snatches up the cloth and the hat and stuffs both down his shirt. He looks at the camera and shrugs before taking off after Yiko. 'So I was grabbing the disguise. So what? It is not like I wanted to dress up like a female goblin again, but who knew when the disguise would come in handy once more? Better to be prepared than to be dead...That's what I always say; but the material does feel pretty good against my skin...Err...Never mind...Let's get to the slaughter!' Venneman chases after Yiko down the corridor. It would appear that she knows exactly where she is going. At last, they are in the alcove where Venneman downed the two guards. The bodies have been moved, so the beasts must know something is afoot. Venneman reaches beneath his shirt and pulls out the belt. He hands it to Yiko and she almost purrs as she strokes it's golden surface... Yiko: Yes...You have come home my pretty...Yes... Venneman: What the hell are you doing? You know the plan...Stick to it! Yiko: My my! I do try... But this gold.... Feels so cold.... Just to hold.... I feel like I might fly! Venneman: What? Give the signal, straight away! I need to finish this charade today! Yiko: Why so cold? Gaze on the gold! Venneman shakes his head in frustration.... Venneman: Only fools should speak in rhyme... 'tis not for us, I haven't time! He points to the edge and Yiko reluctantly steps to the railing with the belt. Venneman crouches beside her, ready to leap from the ledge and race to the statue. She holds the belt high and yells down to her compatriots! Venneman: I have the intruder! He has Syko's belt! He is here, come quickly! All eyes turn to the ledge and glaze over as they gaze upon the TV Title. In a mad rush, the throng makes its way to the stairs with shouts of 'Death to the infidel!' and other Goblinesh phrases. As they hit the stairs, Venneman leaps the railing and steals over to the statue. He lifts the stone from its lap and smiles, placing it inside his shirt. He pats it and takes a look at the stairs where the goblins are pushing at one another to get to the belt at the alcove upstairs. He shakes his head and heads behind the statue... 'Stupid Goblins. So easily fooled, so easily distracted. Their being centers upon the acquisition of the property of others. My being, right now, centered upon vengeance. Vengeance upon Syko for stealing my precious gold. The question I would pose is this: how can a man, err, Goblin, who has not wrestled a match in nearly a month be the number one contender for the TV Title? The next question would be how can a creature who I have destroyed on more than one occasion think he can stand toe to toe with a Knight in the ring?' Venneman reaches the area behind the statue only to find that there is in fact, No door! He looks around but keeps his cool. From the room behind a shout goes up... THE JEWEL!! THE JEWEL IS GONE!! DEATH TO THE CRUSADER!! DAMN THE KNIGHT!! Venneman: Hmm. I had almost hoped this would not be easy. Excalibur is thirsty for Goblin blood and I do hate to disappoint... With those words, the statue in front of him gives a shudder and a groan. It shakes a little and moves its arms overhead... Venneman: Ah shit! YES! THE GOBLIN GOD OF NIGHT IS AWAKENED!! NO MERE MAN CAN STAND THE WRATH OF HE WHO IS NAMELESS! THE KNIGHT SHALL FALL BY THE HAND OF OUR LORD AND MASTER!!! 'Well. This was unexpected. How could I deal with a God of all things? I mean, Akiyama wrestles all the time, and he is a god, but this was different. This...This Thing was an animated statue. I really must say, in all my years of Knighting I have never had to do battle with a statue. Maybe taking this damned jewel was not the good idea I had originally thought...' Venneman pulls the scroll from inside his shirt, rips it in two and lets the pieces fall to the ground... 'And that damned prophecy failed to mention a statue god that would contest my cleanly planned getaway. I guess it goes to show that you shouldn't believe everything you read! Right now, there are only two things I know for sure: first, on Sunday, I would have a much easier time with Syko than I was about to have with this beast...Second? This God was one big ugly bastard!' >>>>TO BE CONTINUED
