Deus Ex Machina.

Date: not entered “What are you going to do? Shoot me in cold blood?” We rejoin our ruthlessly efficient heroine, trusted manager Sue Saksnussum, as she levels the barrel of a .45 at the man’s chest. She cracks a thin smile as her thumb cocks the hammer back. “If I have to.” Okay, I suppose you, the reader, is entitled to a little bit of backstory as to how we have reached this apex of suspense. With the recent capture of our Big and Bold hero, Big Alan Steele, and subsequent pillaging of San Jose, Sue took desperate measures to locate his whereabouts. Namely, the sharply-dressed PI now staring at the business end of her gun, Cain. Unfortunately, others would like to keep Big Alan Steele’s current locale as secretive as possible. Thusly, a dangerous game of cat and mouse ensued between Sue and her hired man against Frisco’s undercover operatives, as well as various notorious criminal masterminds that would enjoy seeing Steele out of San Jose’s landscape… permanently. Sue had made friends. She had encountered foes. She eluded various death traps, hired goons and sniper attempts on her life. Her meticulously cared for Jetta now lies in ruins, the innocent victim of a car bomb plot to erase her from the picture. And now, new evidence has surfaced pointing directly at Cain as a co-conspirator in rubbing her out of the way. Looking back on it, although they were together most of the time, he never really seemed threatened by the obstacles set before her… “Now… drop your gun. I don’t think you want a lead-injection to your brain,” Sue growls, her eyes locked in a glare that meets Cain’s head-on. Cain gives a kind of half smile, slowly moving his hand to his belt, loosening the weapon and letting it thunk into the soft earth of Stately Steele Manor’s backyard. Just as slowly, he raises his hands to show his defenselessness. “I’m not the guy you’re looking for.” “I think I’ll let some proper authorities take a good, hard look into that statement, as I personally have had enough of you today. Consider this termination of our contract.” “What makes you think its me? What makes you think I’m the Mole?” “Showler went into your briefcase after the whole Radisson affair in downtown San Jose,” says Sue, her words even and measured, “We saw the dossier on me, and the Swiss bank account transaction.” “I won’t lie,” Cain smoothes, taking a small step forward, “Frisco’s people contacted me immediately after you did, but I’m a professional… one customer at a time.” “Stay where you are, or you’ll find your cranium surprisingly breezy. It doesn’t explain where you were when I was attacked at the Station.” “Look, I already told you I had my OWN problems to deal with there… I don’t know what else to tell you.” Sue makes a quick motion to the house with the nozzle of the gun, “Well don’t worry about it too much. I’ll just let the police sort all this out…” The smile is quickly erased from Cain’s face, a rise of scarlet in his cheeks. “You know I can’t go with them, Sue. You know they’ll send me back…” “Sure you can,” counters Sue, “All you have to do is learn to enjoy total violation like the other inmates do. Now, turn slowly and move towards the house. Hands behind you head now, please.” Cain begins is slow turn, his teeth grinding against each other. “I can’t let you take me back…” It happens in a flash. A throwing dagger comes into Cain’s hand with a snick, and he whirls around, his arm already in a throwing motion… BLAM!! The knife flies a mere foot to the side of Sue, as Cain staggers backwards, his chest exploding in a geyser of red. He falls back, dead before he hits the ground. Sue stares wide eyed at her gun… which hasn’t gone off. “Lucky for you I showed up in the nick of time.” Sue turns around, the horror of the event just witnessed still fresh on her face. There, standing smugly with his revolver flipping around his finger is Showler, the CIA operative assigned to the Big Alan Steele case. His mirrored glasses glint in the sunlight, as he smiles grandly. “Always pleased to help a damsel in distress.” Sue, eyes wide in fear, levels the gun now at Showler, who doesn’t appreciate the gesture. “What are you doing?!” Showler hollers, his gun now pointing in the air, “I just saved your life!!” Sue’s eyes narrow as she steadies her nerves, “I don’t know WHAT is going on anymore… so I’m eliminating trust from my diet. Now drop it.” Showler’s face sets, and he slowly begins to crouch, keeping his firearm low… suddenly, he springs to the side, rolling and coming up, gun aimed for Sue! BLAM!! The first shot catches Showler in the side, forcing him into a spin and dropping the gun. Another shot rings out, caching him square in the back, and he falls face first into Big Al’s coveted pepper garden. Sue again looks aghast, staring at her gun, still cold. Following the shot trajectories, she looks right. “Capt. Pierce!” The officer with the kindly face and the white hair nods to Sue as he keeps his weapon trained on Showler, slowly moving forward and nudging him with his toe. Satisfied, he holsters his weapon. “There’s your traitor, Ms. Sue. We found out too late unfortunately for Cain, though. Shame, he was a damn good PI. Not a good person, but a great private dick.” BLAM!! BLAM!! The two shots rip through the Captain, throwing great sprays of blood into the air. He collapses in a heap, dead. Sue throws up her hands in disgust and turns. “Dr. Carlson, I see you have joined the party, hmmm?” The doctor nods stiffly, putting away his gun. “Yes, I checked the San Jose PD records for Capt. Pierce, but couldn’t find any,” he nods, assured in victory, “He was an imposter.” BLAM!! The shot slams into the Doc’s back forcing him onto Sue, who gives an exasperated sigh and steps aside, letting the man fall lifeless to the ground. “Okay, now who are you again?” “I was the bellhop at the hotel. We found a cummunicae in Doc’s hotel room he apparently missed. He was one of Dr. Doom’s men…” BLAM!! The bellhop’s head explodes like a ripe watermelon, his headless corpse falling to the blood-soaked ground. A handsome man pops up behind the bushes and walks forward to a very pissed off Sue. “I’m surprised at you, Sue. A bellhop in San Jose that has been identified in recognizance photos as Frisco’s cabana boy? Tsk tsk tsk.. I thought you were more careful than that.” “Could you please stand over a couple of feet?” Sue grumbles, waving him to the side. “Why is that?” BLAM!! BLAM!! BLAM!! BLAM!! The man falls under the hail of lead, as Sue silently chuckles under her breath. “That’s why.” Another man rushes into the yard to view his handiwork. “Let me guess,” Sue proposes, “You are an operative who just found out that guy was an undercover assassin sent by… Lex Luthor, maybe?” “Uh, no… Apocalypse. But close… how did you know? You don’t have that intel…” BLAM!! BLAM!! “Call it a hunch.” Another man in a dark suit rushes in as Sue sighs, tossing her gun into the air in resignation. “That guy was a Frisco agent, right?” she queries. “Yeah…” BLAM!! The lifeless form topples, ready for the big dirt nap, as another guy rushes into the yard brandishing his gun. “Thank God I arrived in time… hey! Where are you going? Don’t you care?” Sue turns to the house, moving to go inside. “I just need to make a snack or something… but please, keep me informed because I AM interested,” Sue tosses out over her shoulder. BLAM!! That man falls and another rushes in, as Sue slides the porch screen door closed. BLAM!! Old dead, new in… BLAM!! The kitchen of Stately Steele Manor, as a harried Sue tries to relax at the table with a cigarette, a cup of coffee, and a few Pepperidge Farm Chessmen. Enter one of the blonde and beautiful Graces, Suzi as she notices the commotion in the backyard and goes to a window. BLAM!! “Uh, Sue?” she asks nervously, “What’s going on out there?” BLAM!! “You know, Suzi, I’m just not sure anymore,” replies Sue in between drags on her cig, “It started out as an attempt to find the Big Guy, but it ended up being… something else.” BLAM!! “What are we going to do with all those bodies?! There’s like fourteen…” BLAM!! “Fifteen bodies out there!! They’re stacked like cordwood, and I don’t think the recycling guys will pick them up on Monday!” Sue just takes a nibble of a Chessman, washing it down with coffee and a smile. More Graces have come into the room, nosing the windows to view the carnage. BLAM!! “Hey, that guy was hot!” “You mean before his face got blown off?” “Of course…” BLAM!! BLAM!! BLAM!! BLAM!! “Ooooh! Look at the cute little monkey!!” BLAM!! “Oh… bye-bye little monkey,” Cindi pouts. BLAM!! “Is all that blood good for the lawn? I know if we leave the corpses that’ll kill the grass…” BLAM!! “I suppose we could compost them…” BLAM!! “Oh, that’s your solution to everything! Compost this… compost that!” BLAM!! “Hey, I’m the gardening type! And its good for the environment!” They all wait expectantly… but no more shots ring out. A lone gunman stands amidst the piles of bodies, scratching his head with the heated muzzle of his weapon, in obvious confusion. “Sounds like it’s over…” Sue slams the last Chessmen into her mouth, rinsing it down with a large gulp of coffee. Running her hands through her hair and smoothing down her navy blue business suit, she saunters past the Graces, pushing open the sliding door with a wink. “Showtime, girlies!” The Graces ‘oooooooh’ and yell out ‘you go, girl’ as she strides into the yard, facing down the lone man left. He turns to her, shaking, his gun pointed directly at her. “What’s going on here?!?!” he screams out, his voice cracking, “Why is your yard a bloodbath?! ANSWER ME!!!” Sue brings a hand to her chin, looking about the grisly scene, seemingly completely oblivious to the shaking man. He tries again to elicit a response. “Where did all these bodies come from?!” Sue bites her upper lip, deep in thought, before bending over, making her choice. “I’ll shoot!!” BLAM!! The man spins around, the bullet piercing directly at his heart and spinning him on his center of gravity. He spirals to the ground, landing on his back. Desperately, he reaches for his gun, but Sue slams her shoe’s high heel into his forearm, forcing another screech of pain from the man. Blood bubbles from his mouth as he spits his contempt at Sue. “How… how did you know?” Sue looms overhead, a smarmy smile etched into her beautiful, when angry, face. She raises the smoking gun to her mouth, blowing away the fumes, “Simple. In the end, it’s always the traitor that lives. Except this time. Toodles.” “You’ll never get Big Al! Frisco has all the angles covered…” “Meh. I’ll just grab him at the upcoming show then. All good plot-points end up there.” And her heel comes down with ruthless efficiency.
