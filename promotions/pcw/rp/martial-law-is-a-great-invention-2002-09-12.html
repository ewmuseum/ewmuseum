Martial Law Is A Great Invention

12 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12/09/02 “WHERE ARE THE SURFACE TO BURNED FREAK MISSILES!?!?!?!“ With the thunderous bellowing of the PCW CEO we open up on the streets of Wisconsin’s most secretive city, the new home of Scientology, Hubbardville. “We’re transporting them from their underground bunkers now, Gold Patron Meritorious...” The PCW Champion looks frustrated and tired as he barks orders in every direction where all around him Scientologists rush to carry out his every command. Xavier : “Good... We have a pissed off killer who wants to, well... KILL ME, coming here on Friday and I want to make sure this city is ready for him...” Kannon stands on the corner of Wehatenicolekidman Avenue in downtown Hubbardville, which is mysteriously deserted apart from the Gold Patron Meritorious and his followers. Roads are blocked off... Shutters are down... And men in very, very dark Grey fatigues patrol the streets. It is for all intents and purposes.. Locked down. “Gold Patron Meritorious, units 4 through 9 have checked in.. No evidence of this “CLYDE” has been seen. We’re expecting 10 through 15 to check in within the next 5 minutes from the South side of the city...” This news seems to please the Scientologist as he breaths a sigh of relief and returns his usual smug grin to his lips. The bright Green eyes that sparkle in their sockets survey the city around him, although looking dead.. Also looking secure. Xavier : “Good, good... I will have no PCW wrestlers, especially Clyde, running riot in MY city....” Behind him stands an intimidating sand stone coloured building marked “HUBBARDVILLE POLICE PRECINCT” and to another smirk from Kannon the doors fly open, out walking rank after rank of Hubbardville Police in full riot gear... Masks, Shields, Armour, Weapons and Gas. Xavier : “Martial Law really is a wonderful invention...” As the ranks of officers walk past the Gold Patron Meritorious he nods his head in approval, just as they break off into groups and disperse themselves around the city. “Mr Kannon, I’m Jenny Reid from the Hubbardville Gazette...” His attention is then removed from the Police and is placed onto an attractive reporter in a smart Pink skirt suit... Microphone ready in hand. “Can I ask you, as Mayor, what precautions you are taking to protect Hubbardville from the oppression of the outside world this Friday?” The CEO seems all to happy to answer... And boast. Xavier : “Well, as you can see all of Hubbardville’s security details are on alert until Saturday morning. Downtown has been closed off, Police roam every street in the city and our secret services are on full alert to locate any trouble makers.... And anyone desecrating our fine city will find themselves in the end of ZERO TOLERANCE.“ “Thank you, Gold Patron Meritorious Kannon.” The reporter nods to her camera man to stop filming then heads off with him to edit the footage while Kannon heads over to the last detail of Riot Police. Xavier : “Remember... If you see a burned man with a little girl, test out your weapons and combat equipment to the MAXIMUM.. He won’t be missed.” With their riot masks flipped up, the Police smile and not to Kannon’s command. “Consider it done.” The officers then continue their trek to the West side of the city as Kannon’s radio, attached to his suit’s breast pocket sounds out. “Units 10 through 15 have reported in.... No sign of the Clyde you are talking about, and the roadblocks have been ordered to search all vehicles for him.” The frustrated look we saw previously on Kannon’s face has not been all but wiped off, his plan to keep Clyde out of his home city so far succeeding. As he camera pulls out further we can see the full extent of the “Lockdown”... Fences have been erected to shut off streets, the main routes in and out of the city have been blocked off by checkpoints... All to keep out one man and a little girl who are baying for Kannon’s blood. Xavier : “Ahhhh, the ace in the hold...” Around the corner from Wearenotacult Avenue, the tip head of a warhead is seen, before a very, very dark Grey truck crawls around the corner, it’s load consisting of 5 of the warheads each attached to a ballistic missile. Xavier : “Aren’t they beautiful...” The driver continue to carefully guide his cargo around the inner city streets, stopping just by the Gold Patron Meritorious who hops up onto the outside footrests and talks to the driver through the window. Xavier : “Those the anti-Burned Freak warheads?” The man nods proudly, checking his inventory. “Indeed they are, where do you want the delivered?” Xavier : “Better take 3 to tactical command, other 2 can go the at SAT Battery we had modified... As soon as the satellites lock in on Clyde, these babies will send the freak a welcome package!” Both Scientologists chuckle at their defence capabilities as the Gold Patron Meritorious leaps back down onto the Hubbardville asphalt as he watches the truck and it’s load continue it’s journey. “Can’t you, like, just ask him SO not to come?” As Kannon looks behind him we see his preppy wife, Eleanor walking over to him, not exactly dressed like those around her who carry out Kannon’s orders, deciding to go with a short denim skirt and Pink vest top instead. Xavier : “Afraid not my little Poon Puppy, the man will listen to reason... And as well as that, where’s the fun in just asking him?” He gestures to all the commotion that is still going on around him with the erecting of barriers and construction of fortifications... Seemingly all built to thwart the intentions of one demon. Eleanor : “Yeah, but did you have to close the SHOPS?!?! I mean, HELLO?!?!?... He’s hardly going to bring the city to it’s knees by, like, buying all it’s stuff!” The annoyed little prep’s voice takes on a whiny tone as she points to the metal shutters and barricades that used to the socked full windows of her favourite boutiques and stores. Xavier : “Yeah... But clothes shops just don’t have that “Martial Law” feel to them, my little Shag Squirrel.... T-” *BOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOM!!!!!!!!!* The camera suddenly shakes as an explosion rocks the centre of Hubbardville.. Sending Kannon leaping on top of his wife to protect her, their bodies landing hard on the concrete as the explosion echoes. As the sound slowly dissipates, Kannon removes his hands from Ellie~!’s ears, the prep not looking to comfortable laying front first on the concrete with the Scientologist on top of her. Eleanor : “Well, if explosions do it for you...” His wife reaches around and goes to unzip the Gold Patron Meritorious’ fly but Xavier darts up to his feet and looks around him, wondering what the explosion was... Ellie~! looking a little frustrated. Eleanor : “Fine.. I wasn’t to happy with the whole “In Public” thing either...” The prep crawls up to her feet and pulls her skirt back down and starts to fix her hair while her husband bellows into his radio. Xavier : “WHAT IN THE NAME OF L RON HUBBARD’S CHRYOGENICALLY FROZEN HEAD WAS THAT??!?!?” A huge cloud of dust rises into the air a few blocks away and Kannon’s Green eyes are wide open, not blinking as he thinks of the destruction. Eleanor : “Wow, Clyde and Bethany must be bombing you or something... Bet you, like, SO didn’t think of that, did ya?” The prep nudges her despairing husband. Eleanor : “Did ya?” His jaw slowly drops as Ellie~! keeps nudging him and only the crackle of a voice over his radio snaps him out of it. “Er.... A problem has developed, Gol-” Kannon grabs his radio so tightly his finger nails turn creamy White and he roars down the radio. Xavier : “NO FUCKING SHIT!!!!! HOW DID THE DAMN BURNED FREAK GE-” “Erm.. It wasn’t him.. It was us...” The CEO growls as Ellie~! leans her head in to get a listen to what his being said, her Blond highlighted hair getting in Kannon’s face much to his annoyance before he gently pushes her out of the way, much to her annoyance. Eleanor : “Oooh, touchEY!!! Just because you’re city got blown up!” She flashes a sarcastic wide grin at him before he again growls down the radio as sirens are heard from the direction of the explosion. Xavier : “What did YOU do?!?!?” “Um.. We prepped the Surface to Burned Freak Missiles but someone on Giovanni Ribisi Drive must have burnt themselves or something.... Then the missile launched and he got blown up... Along with his building... And block...” Xavier : “Listen carefully... I want you to kill yourself, you follow?” The man gulps. “Hanging or bullet to the head?” Xavier : “I DON’T CARE!!!!” The pissed off Gold Patron Meritorious throws the radio down on the floor and stamps on it as Ellie~! tries to resist giggling. Xavier : “And what is so funny?!?!?” The Scientologist’s head snaps around as Ellie~! slaps her hand across her mouth to stop the laughing, mumbling from underneath her palm. Eleanor : “Nhmmm-Hmmmm...” Kannon takes her by the arm and storms off towards the site of the explosion, Ellie~! trying hard to keep up, being dragged along by her man’s grip as the shot fades out to Black on the dust cloud slowly descending back to earth. “If he hasn’t killed himself by the time I get their he’s DEAD!” “Mmmmm, aren’t we wanting it rough today, Tiger!” And with the sound of the preppy Commissioner purring we hit Black.
