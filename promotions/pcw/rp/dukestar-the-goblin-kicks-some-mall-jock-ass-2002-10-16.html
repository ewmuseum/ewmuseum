Dukestar The Goblin kicks some mall jock ass.

Title: Dukestar The Goblin kicks some mall jock ass. Featuring: Duke Williams Date: 10/16/02 Date: October 16, 2002Time: 5:45 PM MST Place: The Village Mall in Fargo, North DakotaMission: Learn the enviroment and people. -Duke Williams walk through a mall in Fargo, North Dakota as he preps for the mall brawl in Japan. Duke is wearing doc Martin boots, tight black leather pants, a rockstar T-shirt, a spike chain around his neck, and his jet black hair is put back in pig tails. Dukestar the Goblin gets some strange looks from some ornery locals.- Dukestar: Since I got burnt I get nothing but stares. They look at me and see freak ,and they think freak. Little do they know the inner me. The inner GOTH GOD that is me. Unlike Akiyama I still choose to speak with you mortals. I don’t need no mouthpiece regardless of what the “Idiot’s Guide To Goth God Behavior” says. I’m my own free thinking Goth God. Why do these people just stare at me? If only I could be accepted for what I am. -I little kid runs up to Duke with a pen and paper.- Kid: Your Duke Williams!!! Can I have your autograph? Dukestar: Don’t I scare you? Kid: No you’re on TV and your funny. -Dukestar tries to scare the kid with evil faces….evil GOTH GOD faces.- Dukestar: Nothing? -The kid just stares at Duke and holds out the pen.- Dukestar: BOO!!! -Duke lets out an evil Goth God laugh but the kid just stares back.- Dukestar: Maybe you’re a little friend?? A tad? No? Nothing huh? -The kid just shakes his head “no”. Duke signs the kids autograph as he runs off.- Dukestar: You see the young are so innocent and untampered. If that were an adult he probably would have spit on me since I’m a freaky Goth GOD. But for all the looks and glares comes an unfathomed amount of power and strength. Take on the whole roster to defend my Continental title? For a mortal that may see extreme but for a Goth God that is just like a walk in the park with your pecker hanging out. Free swinging comfort my Evil Hoss Friends. -Some college jocks give Duke a nasty glare in front of the Radio Shack.- Jock: Hey buddy Halloween isn’t till the END of the month. -Duke smiles at the kid then lunges for him places his forearm in the kids throat.- Dukestar: What was that? Jock2: GET OFF HIM. -Duke shoots a glance at him that would make Bruce Willis wet his pants. In the process of lunging Duke’s shades fell off and that exposes his red eyes.- Dukestar: Come again?? Jock2: Never mind. Dukestar: Here’s for all the freaks you’ve fucked with. -Duke punches the kid in the stomach and walks away.- Dukestar: My people and myself will not be suppressed any longer. I am the leader of the GOTH revolution. What Darkstar, Clyde, Akiyama in their different degrees of difference could not accomplish I shall. In the words of Anton Szandor LaVey “To fight with the dark is to fight with the right. Misconception is goods only ally. The future is ours.” -Some goth kids are hanging out side of the fashion barn. Dukestar walks over to them and raises up his arms like delivering a sermon.- Dukestar: My faithless servants I am your Evil Goth God…..I am DUKESTAR THE GOBLIN!!!!! Goth: So? Dukestar: I’m like your hero dude. -The Goths look at each other through their make up like who is this goof.- Dukestar: This Friday I on our turf the EVIL malls of Japan will beat out all the norms, twits, and nitwits, on the PCW roster. Goth2:PCW? Dukestar: You know the wrestling federation…the number one fed in the world..P…C…W !!! -The kids just look at each other again with confusion.- Goth1: Are you in a band or something cus your kind of old to be dressed that way. Dukestar: A band? No I’m the God of all Goths. Goth2: We hate sports….wrestling blows. -Duke looks disgruntled as he stumbles for the right words to say.- Dukestar: You don’t understand I AM THE SOUL GOD OF GOTH!!!! Goth1: Does that mean you could like buy us beer and stuff? Goth2: Dude if your looking for sex or something you better back off we don’t play that. -Duke is at a loss for words.- Goth1: SO can you buy us beer? Dukestar: I am your defender just a few minutes ago I beat up some jock bully. He made a wise comment about the way I looked so I beat him down for you guys!! -Duke stands their with his arms crossed full of pride.- Goth3: Was he a blond guy about 6’4. -Duke just smiles and nods.- Goth3: Did he have a Ball State baseball cap on? -Duke nods in agreement.- Goth3: You asshole that’s my brother!!!! -The other two “Goths” hold back the disgruntled brother of the beat up jock.- Dukestar: Ok you know what your three are banned and exhaled from the Goth community. Good bye sirs. -Duke walks away with a look of rage in his eyes which are covered again by his EVIL Goth God shades.- Dukestar: Regardless of what these boobs thing I have a sense of the Mall community and I’m ready to take out the roster. One mortal at a time will fall to the hands of Malice. You see the bottem line is simple really I’m a Goth God and your not. You can’t stop a God and you can’t stop Dukestar The Goblin That my norms is the Front Office Honky Tonk Malice Goth God Truth, hoss. -Duke exits the mall quickly as he sees security heading his way.- =w= Goth Malice Style =w= ---- View Duke Williams's Biography
