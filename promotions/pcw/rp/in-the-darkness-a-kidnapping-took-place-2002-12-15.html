In The Darkness A Kidnapping Took Place

15 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12-15-02 AloneDarknessA ChildMurder Alone in the dark is how we will forever Spend our days.. My love for another cost me too much to let it happen again..... Alone on the steeple of a church Mani Shigeru perched alone his Eyes glowing bright in the darkness of the night. A few buildings away he saw the red flash that meant his partner had arrived. What made us who we are? What made us so violent? What made us killers? The answers come to me too easily... Politics, Men, and Weakness... The lies and hatred of Men made them want a strong weapon... The Politics of Men made them want their own bodies as these weapons... And their WEAKNESS made them choose men like me... A cold wind rustles by as the green eyed assassin slowly moves on his perch watching a window very closely.. Across the street on a rooftop sits Shin Yagyuu. Yagyuu's eyes burn a fiery red as he stares down at the same window... Mani Shigeru, Twice you have killed me yet still I respect you... What am I to do with myself if I begin to feel pity for the weak pathetic humans like you do.. Will I become the hero in the dark as you have or will I lose myself in madness as every other Angel has... Shin, The lights are out pay attention... Shin's eyes snapped to attention as a harmonic Chinese accented voice filtered into his ears.. So sad that due to this... 'sport' My companion can never speak... A great man struck down by two men and burned alive... His facial features have returned but why... No, When will his voice return? Shin slowly lifted his wrist and whispered into a comm device. Mani, You tell me when you think they are out... On the other side of the street Mani's eyes glowed emerald green. In the darkness of his mind swirled endless hate and filtered weak love.. The love of Kiana his long deceased wife, The Betrayal of Jude... His only friend and murderer of his only love... The Bastard played friendly hoping I wouldn't remember..... The hatred swirled in Mani's eyes as he leapt off the steeple and landed in the street.. Shin's steel heel boots landing not far from his as the two entered the house. Shin, There are three people in this home, A woman and two boys. One is five the other is nineteen... The five year old is on the top floor second room on the left... Shin instinctively scaled the stairs as Mani crept into the first floor bedroom to find the woman sprawled on her bed half asleep. The light of Mani's emerald eyes was now non existent as he snuck to the woman's side and raised his wrist. A pull at his wrist revealed a long thin blue string of wire, In one fatal motion the woman's head was separated at her neck. Her eyes were plainly opened in eternal shock as Mani watched the wire dissipate into nothing. On the second floor Shin slowly stepped into the five year olds room. The boy was plainly awake and immediately called out 'Santa?' Stupid Child.... Shin smiled under his mask and stepped into the light. The boys voice came clear as day but quiet as night 'Whoa a Ninja! Can I see GAAAAAAAAK' The shuriken pierced the boys throat before he could finish his sentence.. Shin smiled under the mask watching in delight as the child squirmed for air. Seconds went by slowly as the boy struggled for each breath, blood most likely filling the young child's lung and drowning him as he attempted to breath. Shin's enjoyment was cut short as Mani blurred by him at a speed the young child wouldn't have even seen. A quick snap rang as Mani broke the child's neck Awww.. You're no fun.. Mani glared at Shin as he laid the boy down and closed his eyes Innocent... Shin stared in shock as the metallic grain of Mani's voice actually came from his lips. Mani stood up and glared at Shin walking out and slamming into him on the way by. The eldest boy died without a struggle. Too tired from football practice he slept soundly until his death a simple slit of his throat.. The regular actions would now be taken as Shin poured out a can of gasoline on the first floor. Mani stood alone outside awaiting his apprentice to set the home ablaze Alone in The Dark a single mistake cost me My voice... The Half dragon and his holy knight companion burned the vocal chords from my throat... The house began to burn as Shin stepped out giggling to himself.. The thoughts in his head razing of the flesh of his victims burning to ash. The two slowly walked away from the home and leapt to the roofs of the city as they made their way back to the 'base camp' A small deserted apartment served as Base for this week. The two stepped inside as Mani looked over to the corner... Shin walked over to the small figure huddle alone and tied up. Hello Christa... Isn't it sad how Aunty Tessa is putting you through this? Yagyuu enough.... Shin turned slowly and went into another room as Mani grabbed a small amount of food and handed it to the small girl.. A day before they had been inside the home of Tessa Windsor. The small child named Christa was all a part of Mani's grand scheme and sadly it meant kidnapping her.. Tessa slept soundly as Mani crept in alone and gagged the young Christa taking her from Tessa.. Taking her from Storm... Now in the shadow filled apartment the young girl sat curled in the corner bound at the legs as she ate the food Mani had given her. In the darkest depths of his heart Shin wants to skin her alive... I will not allow that.. If she must die for our plan then she will die quickly.... Mani sits alone as the darkness grows ever more and the scene FADES TO BLACK
