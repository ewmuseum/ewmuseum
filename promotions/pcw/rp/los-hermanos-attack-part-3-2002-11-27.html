Los Hermanos Attack (Part 3)

27 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/27/02 Defenseless That’s the one word that could sum up all of last night. Never in my life have I been so powerless. I just watched as everything went down, because I was afraid, afraid to die. It was never like me to be that way, usually I wouldn’t even think about dying and I’d just do whatever it takes to resolve the solution, but not this time, this time I was scared. That’s right I was just as scared as a little girl. I let someone close to me get harmed and then I let THEM get the last laugh. Just thinking about everything that happened last night sends chills up my spine. I had to be saved. Usually I’m the one doing the saving, but not this time. The only thing between a funeral and me last night was a police siren. I sometimes thing “What am I getting involved in?” And then I think, why can’t I handle it. I’ve NEVER had a problem handling something. I’m probably stronger than every one of those bastards put together, but what’s the difference. I stand there and ponder for a moment and then it comes to me. Those men weren’t anything but scared little boy if they didn’t have any weapons. A weapon, it was there line of defense, the cowards’ toy. I won’t take this sitting down though. I’ll avenge the harm they’ve caused me physically and mentally, and they’ll realize they invaded the wrong Upper Eastside Household. What they don’t know is how far I’ll go to make sure they pay. I grip my hands tightly and clutch my knuckles till I see the red of my blood through them. THEY WILL PAY! I had to do something, and something today. I couldn’t go off into a ring knowing I ran away from controversy and let them get the best of me. It just isn’t happening I tell you. Today is when it all goes down. That day I was ready for anything. I went all through the town looking for the proper ways to defend myself. {Flashback} Watching a knife plunge through his mother’s stomach and the blood pouring out onto the floor in front of him. MISERY! Watching friends of his year-by-year die of diseases they’ve picked up on the streets, and without a single person caring. MISERY! Seeing my own brother be pulled away gasping for air after a brutal and cowardly attack by a man who couldn’t successfully fight for himself. MISERY! Seeing a woman that mean a lot to him be violated so rudely last night, by what can be considered the biggest scum in the earth. MISERY! {Flashback ends} NO MORE MISERY! It was on. I worked hours on end for a plan that would put the greatest masterminds of this time to shame, and then I executed. I’ve lost too many battles in my lifetime; I will not lose this one. That afternoon I went back to the hood I once lived in on the West Side. I found a few angry looking men and made a proposition to them. How would you guys like to make an easy one grand? The men would all just stare at one another and smile. It was too easy; I almost had to pinch myself to believe it was reality. A gang was not hard to put together, by God I could put together one by myself. I met with those men and I went through all the details. Then it was down to contact. I packed every one of there cars with radio systems so we could get a hold of one another. Just as I was putting on in to one of the cars, a man approached me. It’ll never work. Excuse me? I said it’d never work. You won’t defeat the Los Hermanos, there just too powerful. It may not seem that way, but they are. They are dangerous people to get involved with. And so am I, but unfortunately they got involved with me already, so now there’s no turning back. Do you think sending these young men to their deaths will make you feel better? No one will die if it turns out the way I plan it to. And what if it doesn’t turn out the way you plan to. That’s not an option, and hey why are you trying to get involved in this so much anyway, this has nothing to do with you. Oh but it does. You see the Los Hermanos had killed my wife and my son. I tried to do what you did, but it didn’t prevail, all I got from it was this. The man turns his face and shows Romantico the completely burnt side that looks mutilated. They stuck my face on a grill and all chanted along as I roasted. Those are the type of men you’re dealing with. They’re ruthless. I then went to the police for some help, but when it has to do with gang activity they never pay attention to it. I’m sorry about what they’ve don to you, but you just have to trust me, that they won’t do the same to me. How can you be so confident? You have about five men and a measly radio station. You think that’s going to stop a gang of about thirty to forty men. That’s all it takes, that and God by my side. God won’t help you here. God doesn’t want to see people die for no reason. God believes in justification, and by God justification is what will be served. I left there pondering what the man said to me, but it didn’t phase me much. The night has arrived and everything was set up. The plan was we’d have three of us sent to one of the gang member houses, and three sent to the other house. We’ve kept close tabs on them and found out that they were going to a gang gathering. They’d most likely come back drunk and a little gullible, so we were ready to take advantage of that. We broke into their houses where about three of them lived together in and just waited. The time had come for arrival. The gang members neared toward the door and when they opened it they saw a gun drawn right to their faces. What the fuck! Right as they were about to pull out there weapons, two of the other men Romantico brought had appeared and drew their guns at them. NOW GET IN HERE! To Be Continued………..
