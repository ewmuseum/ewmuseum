The Bellzier Chronicles: Of Dreams and Nightmares [Part Six]

27 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Feb. 27, 2002 Dream LandTime Unknown The dramatic pause was over, and it was time for Nutcracker to finally admit the truth. The sad truth was that he would have to join us, or risk the ridicule of the entire group. Peer pressure can be a great thing. Nutcracker finally gave in. “I guess I’ve worked with Maples for too long. Maybe I’ve seen too much corruption…maybe I’ve been exposed to too many lame executions…maybe, just maybe, something inside of this furry interior wants to be a clean squirrel again. I can’t believe I’m saying this…but I’m in as well. [I was taken aback by the announcement. The animal that trapped me here was now on my side. I looked back at my teammates now…1) The Jello Warrior, who was strength, integrity, and morality all wrapped into a jello shell. He was also extremely vindictive, which was always a good thing. 2) Sherri, the Smartest Girl in the Land, who was the brains and strategic center of our team. And she was young and innocent – wait, that’s not a good thing. 3) And finally Nutcracker, the willy turncoat and could be the greatest addition to my team. Could I trust him? Could he actually be the factor that gets me back home? 4) Tom Lexian, himself. He was lame, untalented, and an idiot. Gosh, this was looking better and better with each passing moment.” Scene fades out…. …aha, you thought it would, didn’t you. Nope. Okay, it’s time to switch things off to…THE THIRD PERSON, OMNICIENT NARRATOR! Whoo! I’m back, and ready to give you a little extra bonus for continuing to watch Tom Lexian promos. The scene changes to a dark, and haunted castle, somewhere on the Westside of Dream Land. We now zoom in to the inside of the castle, where there is a boiling pot in the middle of a room, and a cloaked figure throwing ingredients into it. Cloaked Figure: Boil, boil….toil, toil….VOILA! The pot begins to bubble insanely fast, and then it blows out a puff of smoke, knocking the figure on his buttocks. The cloak flies off, to reveal a young man on the ground, with the letter “K” tattooed on his head. He looks very sad. King Coverdale: Gosh darnit! I can’t believe it didn’t work! The box said that recipe would be good enough to get out all sorts of pie stains out of clothing…[picks up his cloak, which has a jam stain on it.] How in the great maker’s name am I going to get this Fozzleberry jam stain out of my best cloak now?! A Loud Voice: Coverdale! What are you doing down there!? King Coverdale: Oh no! It’s Marine Levine! Oh gosh, he’s going to have my buttocks for making this mess! I have to clean it up…[points his finger at a mop on the other side of the room]…HOCUS POCUS! [The mop flies into his hand, and he begins to mop up the mess] The door to the room flies open, and now we’re greeted by a large man wearing a traditional knight’s armor. It glistens in the light, and the man has a large axe on his backside. There is blood dripping down the axe. Marine Levine: Coverdale! What in the blazes is going on down here? I heard an….IMPLOSION! [Narrator’s Note: Ahem. I think he means “explosion.”] King Coverdale: I don’t know what you mean. I was just down here doing some cleaning. See? Clean, clean, clean… Marine Levine: [bends down to pick up his cloak] What’s this? [smells it] Is that fozzleberry jam I smell? King Coverdale: Uhm, no? Marine Levine: DON’T LIE TO ME! This is fozzleberry jam…don’t you know how hard it is to get out of clothing? Those fozzleberry atoms like to get right into the fibers, making little houses and taking up resident there. You can’t get them out. They’re probably reproducing already. [throws the cloak down] You were trying to clean it, weren’t you? You bought one of those damn cleaners that those solicitors try to shove down our throats. King Coverdale: So what if I was? I am the king, and I can do whatever the hell I want to…so, NA NA NA NA NA! I AM THE KING,AND YOU’RE NOT! NA NA NA NA NA! Marine Levine: Fucking hell, what is this, the third grade? Act your age, Coverdale. Let’s get down to business, which is you trying to take over Dream Land again… King Coverdale: It’s all well in hand. Once my Jello warriors get a hold of that outsider, Lexian, we’ll have the Maple conclave by the end of the week, and I will be using Maples at a coffee table. Marine Levine: Coverdale, you know that Lexian doesn’t want to help us. And the Jello people don’t like you very much… King Coverdale: [shocked] What do you mean they don’t like me? They keep sending me these wonderful jello desserts! Marine Levine: They were all poisoned, sir. And it’s my belief that they were the ones that sent the fozzleberry jam to your door, just because they knew you would stain your clothes with it. King Coverdale: [angry] DARN THEM! They will have no mercy when I come back into power. We’ll just have to get our ace in the hole now. Get me…THE ULTIMATE WARRIOR! Marine Levine: You mean Jim Helwig, the guy with face paint and streamers from the WWF? King Coverdale: No, I mean, THE OTHER ULTIMATE WARRIOR! Levine leaves the room for a moment, and returns with a large box with a question mark on it. It has a sign which reads: THE ULTIMATE WARRIER! Unfortunately, what Levine doesn’t know, is that “warrior” is spelled with an “O,” not an “E.” The box explodes, and a huge anteater sits there by his lonesome. The scene fades out, as you wonder what sounds an anteater really does make. Has Coverdale really unveiled the ultimate plan that will takeover Dream Land? Has he? Has he? C’mon, e-mail Tom back with your opinions! You know you want to. Now fade out! Scene fades out. Good!
