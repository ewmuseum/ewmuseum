The New Saga: Home

6 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7-6-02 DarkStar had a grin the size of the moon on his face as he pulled that bronzed key out of his pocket. He hummed to himself as he reached down to the large padlock holding the rusty chain to the gates of the estate. He turned the key, the creaking seemed to sing to him like it was going for a Grammy. The the gates opened, the noise was made, and the path was cleared. DarkStar and Talena were finally home again. 'Baby! Its so great to be back!' Talena squealed happily as she jumped into DarkStars arms, he swung her around, kissing her until he gently placed her back onto the ground. 'Its about time. I couldnt stand living in the dream realm any longer. Its a nice place to work... but ugh. If I had to put up with one more fantasy about Avril Lavigne I was going to have to kill someone.' The raven haired goddess purred at him at the mention of killing. 'I love when you say that.' DarkStar put his finger on his lovers lips, silencing her. 'Save it for later, we have work we need to do,' he stopped and looked upward at the building they called home, 'It's still so beautiful.' The years changed, it was much earlier, and the leaves on the trees were that one perfect shade of orange that always dominates your memories of trees in October. The exact shade. Everything sounds distant as it is just a memory, but it is still there. 'Is this...' Talena was a little younger then, less scars, less insanity, her doll was located in her purse. The realitor gave the couple a strange look. Yes, the other, it's definately DarkStar, the hair is greener and the body is thinner and less muscular, but it is him. 'This is... er... was the oldest church in Philadelphia, its pretty much abandoned now, the city was looking for a contractor to pay to knock it down and build a parking lot or something... why...?' DarkStar looked down at Talena and hugged her tight. 'It's perfect... the spires, the room, the area. Its us.' The realitor looked up again, the place was falling apart, there were bats in the upper portion of the bell tower, water marks where the main area was, and various other annoying problems that would have to be addressed. The couple seemed so happy. Freaks. The pair walked up to the door, arm in arm. DarkStar surveyed the front of the building, everything still seemed in really good shape, especially considering how old the restored church really was. He put his key into the door and swung it open, looking around the main part of the cathedral. And seeing someone laying on a blanket in the middle of the floor. 'What the hell..?' he was already walking towards the figure, Talena slightly behind him. He leaned down and took the blanket off of his head... 'Clyde?!?!' he kept sleeping. 'CLYDE!!!!' 'Oh he won't wake up by you just doing that', DarkStar turned around startled, Talena clapped happily and hugged Bethany as best as she could. 'Ugh, get off me you loon.' Bethany shrugged Talena off and walked over to DarkStar. 'Haven't seen you for a while Darky.' 'Yeah, it's been a while since we could actually afford living here, we just speant most of our time in the other realms. You would be surprised how low rent is in heaven.' Bethany gave him a look. 'Well I really wouldn't think they had many residents up there anymore... nowadays and all.' DarkStar nodded, the chain around his neck jingled. 'Anyway Dark, you gotta kick him in his one ball, thats how you wake the loser' she reeled her little foot back and kicked with all her might, almost sending Clyde sliding across the floor and into the wall. He reels in pain with his hands cupping his well kicked manhood. 'Quit your whining ya big baby.' Bethany shows no mercy. Clyde begins to stand, putting his arms on his legs and bending over to catch his breath. A few moments go by and he stands up, perfectly normal and nods at DarkStar. 'Hey Darky, what took you so long. I haven't seen you around forever.' 'Clyde, I was unemployed. What are you doing here,' he had a confused look on his face, his blue hair a little damp from the small misting of rain outside. Clyde looked perplexed, his brow furrowed and showing the network of scars and burns on his face. 'You said I could crash here anytime I needed a place,' he looked around, taking in the massive building, 'well I needed a place, I was wondering where you were.' DarkStar sighed and shook his head. 'I thought the assumption was that I would actually be living here at the time, eh, no harm no foul,' DarkStar hesitated and glanced to the open door to the wine cellar and the basement, 'You didn't hide any dead bodies in the game room again, did you?' Clyde laughed like he was just told the funniest joke in the world, 'Of course not, I just dice them up now. Good for stock.' A agreence from DarkStar. Then a raised eyebrow. Then agreence again. 'Well it sure is nice to see you again Mr. Clyde, the family is almost all together now. Its beautiful really. Miss Emily hasn't been this happy since that awful man fired us.' Talena sways back and forth, as if she is the only one who hears the music. 'Well most of us at least. Vamp is in OSW right now, and Stryfe... well... I guess he is the bastard son of us.' 'I heard he was working for McDonalds.' Clyde said with genuine knowledge, DarkStar shot him a simple 'who cares' look. 'I also heard we have a match Tuesday' Silence. Creaking from the belltower. 'What?' 'Er... we have a match on Tuesday' DarkStar looked up and sighed greatly. 'We suck.' Clyde nods and grabs a beer from the freezer. God are we gonna need this. fin
