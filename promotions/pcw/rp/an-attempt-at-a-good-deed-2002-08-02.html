An attempt at a good deed

2 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8-2-02 Brandi stands at the beginning of a long, paved driveway. Her eyes follow the pavement up the small slope, past the huge iron rod fence. They come to a rest on an older looking, three story house. Brandi reaches in her person and her fingers run across the outline of a man’s wallet. She had found it a few days earlier, just laying on the sidewalk down her street. When she opened it, it appeared that everything was in its place. All the credit cards were still there, there was quite a bit of money inside as well. Luckily there had been an ID inside, and she had used it to find this address. Now she takes her hand out of the purse, and starts the long walk towards the gate. She comes to a stop in front of the gate. She now sees that her earlier assumption of the house had been a mistake. The place is huge. A large, perfectly trimmed lawn stretches out across the property, for as far as can be seen. Over on one side is an Olympic sized swimming pool. It’s water looks cool, clean, and inviting. Brandi looks to the other side of the property. A large tree blocks the view of the tennis court. She tries to slide by the tree to get a better view. Just then, a small camera pops out of one of nearby bushes. Brandi stumbles backwards as a voice booms out of nowhere, ordering her off the grass. The camera zooms in on Brandi’s face. She leans over and looks into it. Voice- Who are you? Brandi looks around the bush curiously. She hadn’t seen the camera before and was wondering how it could just pop up out of nowhere. The camera swivels on its base, following her every moment around the bush. Finally she stops again in front of the bush again. The camera jerks to a stop. Voice- I said, who are you? Brandi is suddenly aware that the voice is talking to her. She smiles and gives the camera a little wave. Brandi- I’m Brandi! The camera moves up and down Brandi’s body, before coming to a rest on her face. Voice- Are you expected? Brandi shakes her head and reaches into her purse. She rummages around inside for a bit, then pulls out a new leather wallet. She holds it up in front of the camera. Brandi- I think that Mr. Mathers might be wanting this back. I found it on my street the other day, and managed to find the address with no trouble. I just wanted to stop by and drop this off real fast. Muffled voices come through the speakers hidden throughout the yard. They seem to be arguing with each other. Brandi stands in front of the gate, looking impatient. Brandi- Look, I really should get going. I can just put it right here by the fence and be on my way. Voice- No, Miss. Mr. Mathers would prefer if you would bring him the wallet personally. Before Brandi has a chance to protest, the gates slide open. Brandi walks inside the property cautiously. Voice- Come on in, Miss. Mr. Mathers will be expecting you. Brandi walks up the driveway quickly, anxious to get rid of the wallet and catch Wrestle Vessel before it leaves. Just as she gets to the steps leading to the door, it swings open. Brandi walks up the steps, and goes inside. She turns around to close the door behind her, but it somehow manages to close by itself. A solid looking man appears at the top of the staircase. He looks down at Brandi and smiles. Man- Welcome to Mr. Mathers’ home! If you would like to wait right here, he’ll be with you in a few moments. The man goes back into a room on the second floor. Brandi stands there, waiting. After a few minutes, curiosity gets the best of Brandi and she decides that she has look at a few of the many rooms in the house. She looks up the stairs one last time, and when there’s no sign of the large man, she hurries down one of the halls. She quickly goes into the first room to her left. The room is empty except for a large piano in the middle of the room. She doesn’t know how to play, so she quietly leaves and goes into the next room. Inside are many glass cases with various medals and trophies. Brandi peers through the glass. Brandi- He was some sort of athlete while he was in high school and in college. Hmmm…. Brandi walks around the room, looking at the many pictures of a young man which are hanging along the walls. Brandi turns around quickly. She thought she heard something come from the hall behind her. Brandi- Oh, great. I’m going to get in trouble for being in here. Brandi hurries out of the room and runs down the hall. She stops when she gets back to the room she was originally in. Brandi walks to one corner where a large suit of armor stands. She looks over it closely and laughs. Brandi- Now this is what Snow is going to need if he thinks that he’s going to walk out of the handicap match the same way he walked in. I don’t see where he thinks he has the right to walk around and beat up on Rent-A-Villain like that. I mean he’s so…so…I can’t find the word I’m looking for. Brandi lifts up the arm of the statue to get a better look. A man walks up behind Brandi, but she doesn’t notice him. He clears his throat loudly, scaring Brandi. She drops the arm and it clangs against the floor. She turns around quickly, and the man cringes. Brandi- I’m so sorry! I didn’t mean to do that. I’ll pay for it if you’d like. The man laughs and shakes his head. Man- No, that’s alright. I can have it replaced. My name is Mathers. The man holds out his hand, which Brandi nervously shakes. Man- Did I hear you correctly? Brandi- Excuse me? Mathers- I heard you talking about Jason Snow, did I not? Brandi- Oh, yeah. You did. Brandi fishes around in her purse and pulls out the wallet. Mathers- You know, I’m a big Snow fan. Brandi steps forward and hands the man his wallet. She realizes that this whole time Mr. Mathers has been staring a little south of her face. Brandi feels uncomfortable and starts to back away towards the door. Mathers- In fact…if you’d like to come with me… Brandi- No thanks. I need to get going. I have a match to be at. Brandi turns around and hurries out the front door with Mr. Mathers following. Mr. Mathers- Well, if you change your mind… Brandi runs down the walkway and slams the heavy metal gate behind her.
