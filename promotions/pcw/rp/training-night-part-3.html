Date: not entered The night was set to come… Turmoil was afoot, and so was the big Cruiserweight Battle Royal. Miss Moonlight has been training night and da… well, mostly night for the time when she will debut. This is a big deal to her… and if she could make a STELLAR impact by capturing the Cruiserweight title upon her first match in PCW, then it would only prove that she is both a worthy hero for Primetime Championship Wrestling, as well as a daunting female task for the men in the company. Miss Moonlight showed a taste of her heroic attributes on Fusion when she rescued the fair damsel in distress Cindy Bi from the HE-MAN WOMAN HATING newfound clutches of Chet King, her longtime lover and friend. He preached his evil ways of misogyny and paid the price for it from the Mistress of the Moon herself! One would think he would have learned his lesson from that…but no. Now he faces Miss Moonlight twice this week, once in the Cruiserweight battle royal, and another on Fusion, where she takes on El Diablo, Flaymin Aymin, and Chet King once more. Fatal Four Way match… scheduled for one fall… well this woman wouldn’t be falling on Fusion… and you wouldn’t see her falling out of the ring on Turmoil. Chet was mad for losing to Tessa Windsor? Well… Chet King will have a Galactic number of men joining him in his misogynous following… after Miss Moonlight vanquishes them all. By the Light of the Mooon… it shall be so. ----------=---------- “Hey, is anyone here?” Miss Moonlight stepped into the ring, the spotlight shining down from lights above. She stood in the Tokyo-famous TWL Dojo, donning her entire Miss Moonlight gear. She stood there, awaiting a response for her question before she stopped dead center in the middle of the ring. A smile came on her face. Then she tan up the turnbuckle and flashed the MOONLIGHT sign, cupping her hands around her mouth and yelling “MOOOOOOOONLLIIIIIIIIIGHT!!!” “AHEM!” M/M turned around with an embarrassed look, finding a stocky older man wearing a black tank top and black combat pants. Miss Moonlight jumped off the turnbuckle and bowed to him deeply. “Instructor Daiametro!” she said respectfully. “It’s good to see you again!” Suddenly she got a rap on the back of her head, causing her to lean down forward upon impact. She looked up robbing the back of her head as Dai gazed sternly at her. “Never take your eyes off your opponent… even when you bow.” He ordered. Miss Moonlight grinned shamefacedly. Instructor Daiametro was a former combat instructor for the Japanese Defense Force, but left when he felt his place was needed elsewhere. And it has been needed… training the future puroresu stars of the world in the Tokyo Wrestling League Dojo. He was as tough as he sounded… famous for his “Dying” uppercut clothesline. “Hai, sensei!” she nodded, bowing slightly (this time keeping her eyes on him). “How have you been?” Dai sighed, shaking his head slowly. “They don’t make kids like they used to. One guy I was training yesterday complained that his back was hurting… so I gave him a backbreaker, and he was crying about “NOW IT HURTS MORE!!” I said, “Kid, if you wanna be a wrestler, backbreakers are the best chiropractic method for back therapy!” Miss Moonlight stared at him for a moment… Maybe it should’ve been mentioned that Dai was a bit stiff with his wrestling skills… if you survive his class at the Dojo, you were labeled afterwards to be the “Invincible Alumni”… because you had to be invincible to walk out of his class, let alone pass. Miss Moonlight’s final exam in that class was to go a gauntlet with the entire class… only method of elimination as to reverse their finishers into her own. Going through 10 guys in a row…that’ll tire anyone out. But lucky for her… because of her final exam she would be set to blaze through the numbers on Turmoil. “Anyways… I hear you need some Alumni training for this big match you got coming up in PCW?” Miss Moonlight nodded. “Yeah! My debut match is for the Cruiserweight title! I’ll show everyone that I deserve to be the HERO OF PCW!” “Yeah, that’s good stuff.” Dai never got on with that superhero business. He was one of the old school style badasses. “Anyway, what’ kinda fight is this?” “Battle Royal.” Dai nodded, thinking hard. “Battle Royal training… yeah I think I can come up with some good ideas for it.” Miss Moonlight’s eyes widened as the trainer began to laugh heartily. That was never a good sign… it always meant trouble… and that someone was getting hurt. But hey, pain came with the job of being a hero. Can’t beat up bad guys without taking a few punches here and there. ----------=---------- “What… is this?” “This is called the boiler room training process. Like it?” “Um… I don’t know. This is gonna help me train for the over the top battle royal right?” “Hey… you’ll learn our lesson if you fall out…never to go over the top again.” Miss Moonlight stared down from the ring at the three people standing there…. PURE’s Boogiepop Dynasty. Rampage and Kyoshiro stood prepped, wearing insulated gloves while holding a steaming tub of hot water. The female member of the group, Cell, stood nearby with a fresh steaming bucket of water in case they ran out. Miss Moonlight turned back to Dai with a confused look. “What’s the water for again?” Dai grinned maliciously. “You’ll see. Now are you ready?” Rampage gave a thumbs up to Dai. “Let’s ROCK!!” “HOLD THE VAT!” Kyo growled, having to support himself as the scalding water almost tipped over. Rampage slid his hand back underneath and grinned, looking over at Cell who sighed at his action. Miss Moonlight shook her head and turned back to Dai, finding him holding his hand out to her in good sportsmanship. “Good luck.” He smiled. Miss Moonlight smiled back and took his hand… and one would think that the uppercutting “Dying” lariat from Dai would have been seen coming a mile away… but it seems like Miss Moonlight doesn’t have that super sight most superheroes do… She flew over the top rope… and Rampage and Kyo stood ready to catch her. SPLOOOSH!!! “ARRRRGH!!! IT BURNS!!!” Miss Moonlight scrambled immediately out of the vat, falling onto the floor and shaking off immediately. Cell shook her head as she walked over, picking her up by her arm and waist, and threw her back into the ring. Miss Moonlight looked up with wincing eyes, finding Diametro with his arm stretched out to her. “Hot, isn’t it?” he said with a cocky smirk. “The SUN would be cooler than that watery HELL!!” He laughed out, Rampage and Kyo joining him on the outside. Miss Moonlight glared back at them through the ropes, malice on her face. “Come on, then. I’m sure if you can’t stand the heat, you won’t get knocked over the top. Got it?” Moonlight nodded. “Yeah, I’m on guard now!” She took his hand, and as he pulled her up, Moonlight found herself in a prone predicament once more… She saw the lariat coming from below… but by then it was too late. SPLOOOSH!! “BY THE SEARING GATES OF HADES!!!” Moonlight jumped out of the vat, landing in a crouched position with her hands around her knees. Cell approached her in this withdrawn state, but Miss Moonlight swung at her to back off. Then she stood up, the steam rising off of her body as she stared up at Daiametro standing at the ropes. She pointed at him, a grin on her face filled with determination… a determination that would make defeating her almost near impossible. “All right, Dai… it’s time to…die…” she looked away, the smile fading as she listened to the redundancy of that quote. “Hell, let’s train!” Just then, a bell rang out in the distance of the training hall. Dai looked away, sliding out of the ring. “Phone call. I’ll be right back.” He snapped his fingers at Cell and thumbed her towards the ring. “You take over Cell. Don’t let her play anything on you, okay?” Cell nodded, climbing into the ring and waving Miss Moonlight in. “Come on now, Moony… I’ve got a recording session later tonight and I don’t want to stress myself out yet.” Did she just taunt the Mistress of the Moon? A foolhardy move indeed Before any of them saw it coming, Miss Moonlight had Cell from behind and threw her over the top rope with the Luna Sea!! Rampage and Kyo tried to move out of the way, but Cell landed back first into the scorching waters and forced them to drop it. As soon as the vat hit the ground, the water splashed up on Rampage and Kyo sending both of them jumping back and yelling out in pain. Cell climbed out of the hot vat and ran to the side, her friends joining her as they vigorously toweled themselves off. Miss Moonlight smiled at the Boogiepop Dynasty. “Hot stuff, huh?” They glared hard at the hero… no body liked to be taunted…especially if you just got dunked in a tub of roasting hot water hard on the back of your head. Daiametro came walking back, finding three guys all wet on the outside, and one drying Mistress of the Moon inside. “What happened?” he said disappointedly. Miss Moonlight shrugged. “They’re just like my competition I guess… ALL WET.” ----------=---------- “This next exercise is the angry lumberjacks method.” “And why is that?” Daiametro winked. “You’ll see why. You ready guys?” Cell, Rampage, and Kyo grinned hard, a suspicious look on their faces. Miss Moonlight was a bit wary of this exercise, but she ignored it. If this exercise was to prepare her for the brawl to come, then she would be ready no matter what. Dai and Moonlight circled in the ring slowly. They had eyes locked on each other… but suddenly Kyo jumped on the apron and swung at her with a lariat! Moonlight ducked and cracked him in the face with a Crescent Moon Kick, sending him falling back off the apron. But as she turned around… DYING LARIATOOOOO!!! Miss Moonlight flipped over the top rope, and landed hard on her rear. As soon as she hit the ground the Boogiepop Dynasty pounced on her, putting the boots to her as hard as they could. Daiametro leaned over onto the ropes and smiled, nodding approvingly. “Don’t beat her down too bad guys… she’s gotta get back in the ring and finish her training.” Just then Miss Moonlight started to fight back, sweeping out legs from under the trio. She stomped on each of them before backing away and striking her fight stance. The Boogiepop Dynasty got back up and advanced on her, a three on one brawl erupting. “Okay…that’s enough…let’s get her back in the ring!” They ignored Daiametro…well, more like Miss Moonlight ignored him. She began to back away the trio, throwing punches and kicks at each of them, spinning in a furious cyclone of assaults. Then she cartwheeled past them all and kicked them with a spanning roundhouse kick to the back of their skulls, stunning them momentarily. ---------=--------- Dai leaned against the ropes, his head slouched over wearily. He looked up, watching as Miss Moonlight nailed Rampage with a Luna Sea, making it an even triple KO on the entire Boogiepop Dynasty. She turned around, breathing heavily as she looked up at her former trainer. “I did good, huh teach?” Dai nodded reluctantly. “Yeah…I guess. You showed you’d be able to handle yourself if attacked three on one at least.” Suddenly she burst out into laughter. “HOHOHOHOHO!! Then I shall be successful when I face Chet, Diablo, and Aymin for the Cruiwerweight title I’m destined to take ownership of after Turmoil! BY THE LIGHT OF THE MOON, THEY WILL LEARN HOW BRIGHT THE MOONLIGHT CAN SHINE!!” “Yeah, okay then…” he sighed again. “Jackass hero crap…”
