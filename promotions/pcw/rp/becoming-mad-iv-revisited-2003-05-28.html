Becoming Mad IV: Revisited

28 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5-28-03 INSANITY!!!!!!!!!!! Water drops in an old run down room producing the only audible sound available. Drip, drop, drip, drop, drip, drop. The redundant accuracy of each drop into the empty metal sink is enough to drive any sane person over the edge, if the painfully drab interior hasn’t already. Pale brown, wooden walls match precisely against the same color floor. The windows have all virtually been shaded over with years of dust and other masses of particles that have collected and clung to them, letting in a very small shimmer of light. Cobwebs hang gingerly around the corners of the squared abode as the spiders and other vermin seem to mock from afar by saying nothing, but of course we know they are present. A wooden picnic style table and benches are set up in the middle of the room which also seem to match the bland grayish, brown tint that seems to be painted onto the walls and floor. “What an ugly color,” A.J. Trinidad thinks to himself as he turns his attention back to the small flicker of a fire that dances before his eyes in what can only be considered a rut of a fireplace. Dressed in black cargo pants and a plain white T-shirt, Trinidad sits alone somewhere here, lost in Oregon. No real reason to be here save for the letter he received from Mr. White regarding some further knowledge A.J. must acquire before his match against Tessa Windsor on Friday’s Fusion. Suddenly, above the droplets of water echoing from inside the sink, the door creaks open and the wooden floor carries the melodic creaking along in stride as footsteps trace the floor moving closer to where A.J. is seated. “I see you’ve chosen to take the next step young Trinidad”, a voice softly starts from behind A.J. “You indeed are as dedicated as I remember.” Trinidad immediately swings around causing his long raven hair to wrap around his face almost completely covering it for only a brief moment before falling back to the side as his face is cemented with astonishment. “St. Jon?” A.J. managed to mutter out in disbelief. “But I saw you, I saw you and you were dead? How can this even be possible?” Trinidad’s hands run up across his eyes trying to wipe them clear as he continues to stare on in incredulity. Tears begin to well up in his eyes as he drops his gaze to the floor unable to face his old mentor at this point. “Dead is a many splendor thing my son,” St. Jon begins. One truly is unaware of all that leaving this world has to offer until it is taken from you and you soon realize that it is the greatest event that could have ever occurred in your life. Dying in reality is simply being reborn into something bigger and better, but you will find this out all in due time.” St. Jon walks around the table and seats himself directly to the right of A.J. who is still staring down at the floor. Placing his left hand under Trinidad’s chin, St. Jon softly pulls A.J.’s head up to face him, eye to eye, once again, a moment in which Trinidad never thought he would find himself again. Tears stream down A.J.’s face as the silence between the two men grows deafening until St. Jon begins to speak again. “Why is it you weep at the sight of me my student?” asks St. Jon in a very sincere tone. “Honestly, I don’t know why I cry before you” responds A.J. “ I guess I am just completely overwhelmed with what has happened since your dying all coming back to me in a flood, all at once. I guess I just don’t know how else to react.” “Understandable.” States St. Jon in his very calming intonation. I’m not here to scare you or make you feel as if though you have been unworthy in your trials through the wrestling world. I am here to simply aid you in your future endeavor, which is to be the reason you’ve been chosen, by me, all those years ago. You ultimate goal has nothing to do with your match against Ms. Tessa Windsor this Friday, however, every ounce of ability you can take away from any match will prove to be a great asset down the line. I have chosen to come to you to prepare you for what you will need to become in order to fulfill the reason you’ve been created as such.” “I’m not sure I fully understand St. Jon, I mean, you’re speaking in circles and I don’t seem capable yet of following.” A.J. admits. “Do not worry Angel, you don’t need to understand, not yet, “states St. Jon. “You’ve proven to be as tough minded as I trained you to be, I truly believe you are ready to begin now, starting this Friday, it’s a whole new training regiment. Each match you must look at through the eyes of your opponent, become their weakness. Only then, will you understand how to take advantage of your opportunities.” “I’m beginning to understand St. Jon,” replies A.J. still staring at his former mentor. “I’ll work hard to do you proud, and in the end…..” “In the end,” St. Jon takes over, cutting Trinidad off. “In the end, you will fully understand and hopefully grasp why we have done what he have done, and why you have been chosen. Unfortunately, the choice was taken out of your hands a long time ago, however, you were very receptive to the training, it’s now only your mind that has to be convinced.” A.J. pauses for a moment before offering a quirky look of misunderstanding, “what do you mean when you say ‘we’, St. Jon? I had always thought that it was you and I, but now you speak as if there are more people involved than I ever knew about.” “Indeed you prove your wisdom once again.” St. Jon covers up as he breaks his eye-lock with Trinidad. “What it is we are doing too big for you to understand right now, just feel comfort in knowing that you’ve already begun, and are on the correct path to where we need you to be.” A silent pause breaks in the conversation as A.J. turns to look to St. Jon once again, but he is gone leaving A.J. searching around the room. His head swivels back and forth as he calls out, “St. Jon! St. Jon! Don’t leave me again!” His cries for St. Jon go unanswered as a hush once again envelopes the room. For minutes A.J. sits noiselessly in the room, staring now back at the red and black coals in the fireplace that have since burnt out. Until another voice perks his ears up. “I trust you found what you were looking for?” Trinidad stands up and turns on his heel to face the man behind the voice and finds himself staring back at Mr. White, who is adorned in the same wardrobe as usual. “I saw, him, I spoke with him, St. Jon was here, with me, only but a few minutes ago.” A.J. spurts out quickly, almost in an indeterminate manor. “Then you know what you needed to know,” Mr. White begins. “St. Jon has been watching you for a long time waiting for this moment to confront you again with your next steps, I just hope you are truly ready for what he has planned for you.” “Trinidad walks up to Mr. White placing his hand on the shoulder of the pristine white jacket of the man standing across from him. “I understand now what it is I have to do, I am ready to proceed.” With that, the camera fades back and out of of the cabin disappearing into the dense foliage that surrounds it.
