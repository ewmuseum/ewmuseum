Rebirth

27 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 27/04/03 “I’ve given up…” An exhausted Nascosto Nerezza, drained of almost all his life… if he could ever be considered alive… scraped the welded trunk door of Gaz’s Shelby GT 500. The car had been his casket since exactly seven days ago when he was bundled into the back of it and sealed in, then plummeted inside it into a river. For a week, he had been confined to the smell of his own decaying body and one uncomfortable position, denied light and blood. And now, at the end of his hope and faith, the vampire lies still, barely scratching a blunt and bloody claw at the metal surrounding him. “I wonder… will I go to hell and meet all the people I’ve killed? Will I see the faces of them all and have them torture me for revenge? Or shall I ascend to heaven for my deeds of ridding the world one by one of all these corrupted souls, helping to return the planet to it’s rightful state? Or will I simply lose consciousness and fade into nothing? Is there really an afterlife? Or… I’m just going to sleep… I’m … tired…” Nerezza’s eyes closed. His hand floated slowly down in the water, laying still. Only his purple hair moved in the slight current as he remained motionless. A few bubbles floated from out of his ear and… no more. … … … … BANG. BANG. The car stuck out from the surface, front end first, lodged in place by rocks. Nerezza still didn’t move. until he heard voices. Slowly, his eyes fluttered open, still yellowed and not focusing. His mouth parted slightly, showing a pair of fangs that crept over his lips more as though they were desperate to leave his mouth and pierce a blood vessel. Then something was being jammed into the one tiny gap in the welded-shut trunk door, ramming this way and that, trying to loosen the molten grip. “…?” Slowly, more light crept through the hole… the banging noises grew louder as someone… or something… attempted to break the metal casket open. As the final piece of metal broke, the bonds were cut and the trunk opened… “Food…” Nerezza sprung out of the trunk onto the first thing he saw, clamping his jaws down and pinching skin and flesh with his teeth, tearing away the chunk of muscle and swallowing it whole. With no vocal cords to scream with, the victim simply twitched in horror as Nerezza’s mouth formed a loop and planted itself into the gaping crimson hole, loudly slurping up every drop of blood that he could. Behind him, a sailor slowly paced backwards, trying to tell if he was hallucinating, when a sudden electrical shock to his head dropped him to the floor. Nerezza continued to slash away with his broken claws, opening new cuts and drinking in an absolute frenzy. The victim’s skin was as white as his now, and no more blood would come out. “Hungry…..HUNGRY….” Nerezza slobbered as saliva rolled between his bloodstained teeth and dripped down the front of his jaw. The monster’s eyes were once again alive, glassy and shiny, now searching for the next meal. “In the mood for some Captain Birds-Eye, Squire?” came a familiar voice. “…Gaz?” Nerezza drooled as he turned to see The Jester standing over the unconscious captain. “We finally managed to track you down! Of course, I made the cover about wanting my car back, conveniently forgetting to tell the sea dogs what was inside the trunk.” Gaz proudly smirked. Nerezza’s eyes locked dead with Gaz Green’s, then sank down to the stirring captain. Still weak, the vampire fell onto his hands and knees, crawling forwards with his soaked hair masking his face. The captain propped himself up, rubbing his head. “Arrr… what happened…?” He groaned, his face wrinkling as his eyes opened wide to the horror in front of him. He gasped as he saw the torn and ripped fingernails on the skeletal white hands that advanced towards him. He tried to crawl away, but found himself unable to turn away from Nerezza’s hair-covered face. He found himself pinned up against a rock on the riverbank, unable to go left, right, or backwards. Nerezza began to stand up, still covering his face with the dripping wet hair. The Captain’s mouth opened wide but no words came out as Nerezza loomed closer. A sinister chuckle escaped his mouth as blood and river water spurted out, joining the stink of his already rotting body. The Captain gave one final gasp as he saw Nerezza’s eye – red and missing most of the lower eyelid, gazing down onto him… then… “Ugh…” Gaz Green turned away in disgust as Nerezza inserted his hands into the Captain’s mouth and pulled them away from each other. A rubbery SNAP signified Nerezza had found a suitable place to feed and the slurping and sucking noises indicated it was best not to say anything to disturb the vampire. The noises continued for a few minutes until a greedy burp ended the feeding frenzy. “Ahhh… “ Nerezza gasped in delight. As Gaz turned back to face him, his appearance had changed. His mouth was now more of a horizontal slit that showed a legion of sharpened teeth, all stained with a dark red. He stood at his full height of 6’7”, with ivory skin that broke in patches. His ribcage looked at though nothing was inside it – Gaz was reminded of the appeals to send money to Ethiopia when he saw it. “New look, Squire?” Gaz quirked, staring at his partner. Nerezza looked down with a hideous smile through his hair onto his body. He gazed at it with increasing pleasure, giggling to himself sickly. Most of his body had been repaired, but still a few guts and gashes remained opened up. it was as if his vampire and human sides had collided in the darkness of the river. Nerezza stood reborn, changed, and hungering. “New… new body…” Nerezza laughed, looking down at his fingertips. “But… so hungry… people made me hungry. Who made me hungry, Gaz?” the vampire stared accusingly at his partner, awaiting for the answer. Gaz gulped as Nerezza once again dropped onto his hands and knees and began crawling over to Gaz. “The culprits stepped forwards, Squire… E2 claim responsibility!” Gaz shrieked as Nerezza drew threateningly closer. The beast stopped, gazing out of his hair, twitched his fingers, then drew himself up to full height again. “...Gaz…” Nerezza hissed, staring down at his partner. “…do you know how to drive a boat?” “Shouldn’t be too hard, Squire, but let me get that car loaded on first. After that, I’ll see how we do.” Gaz nodded. For a moment, there was almost a silence, save for the trickling of the cold water of the river and a few birds in the surrounding area. Then a rustling sound as Nerezza grasped the ankles of the two corpses and began to drag them towards the gangplank of the boat, smacking their still skulls on the bumps of the wood. Gaz, already on board, found himself at the controls of the winch. A mechanical whirring started as Gaz’s battered Shelby GT 500 was pulled up onto the boat’s deck, dripping water everywhere. “My poor baby!” Gaz cried, racing over to his car and soaking himself as he spread himself over the left door. “Don’t worry… E2 will pay for this…” he whimpered, caressing the mirror with closed eyes. “E2 will suffer… everyone will suffer.” Nerezza quietly snarled as he tossed the two bodies over the starboard of the boat. The pair marched to the railings situated at the rear, looking at the water as it was thrashed about by the propeller. “First, we’d better get you back to get you cleaned up a bit, Squire. I need to find a garage for my baby over there. Then we prepare to give a piece of our minds to E2.” Gaz instructed. His knuckles turned white as he wrapped his fingers tighter around the rail, probably imagining it was the throat of either John Gargano or Tyler Preston. “Our minds…” Nerezza grinned, revealing his teeth again through the parting in his hair to the Jester on his left. The vampire continued to stare down at the propeller, the got down on his hands and knees as something began to float in the wake of the boat. Pushing his head over the edge to look more closely, he watched on with malicious glee as shredded pieces of a Captain’s and First Mate’s uniforms… and shredded pieces of a Captain and First Mate… floated for a brief instance before sinking into the depths of the river. Satisfied, Nerezza stood back up and looked at the cruel, hooked claws on his hands. “And our bodies…”
