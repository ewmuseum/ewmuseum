A Change of Heart: Part I

30 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: July 30th, 2002 Dear Brother Yo brotha, what has been going down lately? You know I have been scanning those Internet rumor sites and a few said the last CCW Champion signed with PCW. I know you never liked PCW, you always had negative comments about them and suddenly you jumped ships to the enemy? I tell ya brotha, I almost choked on my Jack Daniels when I read that bullshit. Have you lost your mind? Yo I’d like to hear what you have to say for yourself now, and man, I am currently unemployed and sittin’ at my crib doing nothing. I hope you have no plans of forgetting anything about CCW and Menace II Society but yo, you need to contact a brotha and give him the 411 on what the hell is going on inside that head of yours. Holla Back Your Brother, Darren “D-Boy” Jordan The letter was quite intriguing at first, why my brother, who always supported me at all times, has a change of heart when I suddenly make an important move. I was expecting the usual, “sup kiddo” or “met any new hoochies lately” but instead I was hit with a small wind of possibly…jealousy? [Blayze moves the letter under his small lampshade in the comfort of his hotel room at the ‘Le Plaza’ Hotel in Port-Au-Prince Haiti. The bright sun shiny day of the Haitien atmosphere casting a fluorescent shadow threw the curtains of the window. The window is slightly open and a miniature amount of breeze enters the room, a fresh summer day feeling. Blayze decides to shut off the lamp on his night-table, the letter which he held is his hand is now folded in two, slipped into the back pocket of his Karl Kani Jeans. Still a bit confused on his brother’s response towards his PCW signing, Blayze rest his head on the bed pillow and tries to get some shut-eye.] Blayze: * Grumbles * Dayum, my own brother jealous about me finally getting some decent exposure? I mean, I knew I was good, I just did not know I was so great that my own blood would give me the weird look as they say. We gonna have to speak about this, but I have to relax 5 Minutes Pass Blayze: Forget this, I have to give Darren a call. [Incapable of getting some afternoon rest, Blayze picks up the phone and dials Darren (1-800-COLLECT of course). Eagerly waiting for Darren to pick up the phone down in Compton, the 3rd ring has now passed and still no one has picked up the receiver. Blayze drops his head after the 4th ring then as he goes to hang up, he hears a faint ‘hello?’ on the phone.] Blayze: Darren! Darren: Jerome? [Pausing for a moment, Blayze regains his posture and replies] Blayze: Yea brotha Darren: What up? Ya need something? [Blayze, gripping the phone to his hear with his shoulder, scuffles into his back pocket of his jeans and pulls out the letter.] Blayze: Indeed I do. Darren: Well? Blayze: You know I received your letter this morning, I was quite appalled but I managed to just accept your comments. I think we should have a nice talk, you and me. Darren: Listen brotha, I just had to speak my mind but I do not think the phone is the right place for us to discuss this. We need to speak face to face, why don’t you come down to….. Blayze: No No, I am currently in Port-Au-Prince, Haiti for Tuesday Turmoil, I will be glad to pay your trip down here. [A short pause, then Darren speaks] Darren: You want me to come all the way down to Haiti? Blayze: You want me to miss my premier show and come down to Compton? Darren: * Sigh * Fine.. Blayze: Good, I will call up American Airlines and get you on a rush, one way flight right to Haiti. I will meet you at the airport and we shall take the cab to the Cathedral of Port-Au-Prince. Darren: The fuck? You want me to repent some sins? Hahah [While Darren chucks over the phone, Blayze slowly pulls out a .45 caliber from under the pillow of the hotel bed. As the laughing fades, Blayze strokes the gun with his right hand] Blayze: Possibly….. Darren: You nuts brotha, but alright, I can consider this a “paid-vacation” of sorts. Blayze: If you want to call it that.. Darren: Aight, hook me up with more info later..Peace Blayze: Peace. [Blayze hangs up then focuses on the shine of his caliber pistol] Blayze: You know, maybe I should repent my sins, because besides making an impact on the poor souls of PCW, I might have to send another “impact” of sorts to a few non-believers. [A devilish smirk rises on Blayze faces, he puts on his Johnny Blaze buttoned shirt, slips the .45 in his pants, turns his cell phone on, then just walks out of the hotel room.]
