Getting serious about ending the losing streak in the cleanest gym in all of Mexico!

30 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: March 30, 2002 “Come on, lift those weights!” An enthusiastic voice yells out support at what appears to be a second rate gym, with a dirt floor and no windows in the wooden building. “Come on, you can do it, lift those weights!” The enthusiastic voice continues to yell out motivational comments while a putrid mixture of sweat and dirt looms in the hot gym. “Lift those weight Chet, you can do it!” The enthusiastic voice is yelling at Chet King who’s lifting weights in a dark, misty corner of the gym. “Cindy, can you do me a favor?” asks Chet King. “Sure, what do you want?” The enthusiastic voice, which is that of Cindy Bi, wonders. Chet King looks at Cindy who’s standing a couple of feet away from him, “I want you to shut up!” Cindy Bi looks shocked at Chet’s comment. “Uh, what?” asks Cindy. “You heard me, I want you to shut up” Chet tells Cindy. “Why? I’m trying to motivate you.” “You may be trying to motivate me, but you’re actually annoying me.” “What am I doing wrong?” “You’re talking” Cindy Bi doesn’t know what to think as Chet King tells her that she’s annoying him. “Look Cindy, I know I’ve got to train for my match on Sabotage against Clyde, I don’t need you to give me support when I’m lifting weights.” Chet King explains to Cindy “I do know how to lift weights after all.” Cindy Bi’s cheeks go red in a show of slight embarrassment as Chet tells her the obvious, that he knows how to lift weights. “I know you know how to lift weights, I just want to make sure you train to your fullest for this match on Sabotage” says Cindy “Like we said yesterday, this match on Sabotage is a must win match for you, it will determine whether or not you can put an end to this losing streak that’s been plaguing you.” “I know, I know.” “You’ve got to get serious about this match.” “I am serious about this match, I wouldn’t come to this piece of crap of a building, with the most horrible, stinking atmosphere possible while it’s boiling hot!” states Chet “I wonder where the good gyms are in Mexico.” “Yes, alright, I realize that you’re serious about this match. But I just want to make sure as this match is just so important for you.” “Look, I’m not going to allow myself to be lose, I refuse to let this losing streak continue.” “Good, good. You do know that this victory can’t just be a one time thing, you can’t let you losing streak continue after you win on Sunday.” “Of course I know that!” “Good, good. You’re going to have to defeat Darkstar at Spring Fling to prove to the world that you’re over your losing streak.” “Don’t worry, I’m going to defeat Clyde at Sabotage to end my losing streak, and then I’m going to defeat Clyde’s sex slave at Spring Fling to start a winning streak, hopefully a winning streak that will take me to the very top.” “You mean to the world title?” Cindy questions. “Obviously.” “Don’t get too ahead of yourself.” “What do you mean get too ahead of myself? If guys like Boda and Chris Chase can fight for the title, what says I can’t fight for and win the thing?” “For one they’re not in the middle of losing streaks.” “I’m not in the middle, I’m at the end as this streak will come to an end on Sabotage” Chet corrects Cindy. “What I meant to say was end this losing streak first my defeating Clyde, then go on to Spring Fling and get revenge on Darkstar for some of the attacks he’s done to you over the past few weeks.” “And for contributing to my losing streak?” Chet rhetorically questions. “That too.” Says Cindy “You’ve just got to punk his bitch ass out and defeat him to make up for what he’s done to you.” “Punking his bitch ass out isn’t going to be a problem!” Cindy smiles at the thought of Chet taking down Darkstar at Spring Fling, but suddenly her smile disappears and her face turns white. “What’s wrong, Cindy?” asks Chet. Cindy turns around and runs a few yards before stopping and spewing all over the dirt floor. Chet drops the weights and goes to check on Cindy. “Sh*t, Cindy, are you alright?” a worried Chet King asks. “I’m okay, it’s just the smell in this place is revolting.” “Indeed it is” “And the heat plus having no windows doesn’t help” complains Cindy “And the amount of dust from the dirt in here is enough to make the healthiest person sick.” “Are you okay now?” “Yes, I’m okay now that I’ve gotten that out of my system.” Cindy glances at the mess below her feet. “Do you think anyone will notice that?” Cindy wonders. Chet looks down at the mess, which might not have been such a great idea as it causes him to turn around and vomit over the dirt floor as well. “You alright Chet?” Cindy asks. “Yeah I’m fine, just the revolting atmosphere in here on top of seeing what you produced caused me to lose my lunch!” “I’m surprised the health inspectors haven’t shut this place down.” “This is Mexico, I’m not even sure if they have health inspectors” Chet tells Cindy “And if they do, they certainly don’t close anything down, as long as there’s a bribe involved.” “How did Mexi and Mona cope living down here?” “I can’t say I care how Mona coped, but Mexi coped by drinking Tequila~! instead of the dirty water down here.” “I suppose that was good for him.” Chet King looks a bit shocked. “Good? What do you mean? He lost all feeling in his body!” Chet King tells Cindy. “Which is good when he wrestles, it means he can never get hurt.” “Ah Cindy, have you forgotten Shanghi-ed 2 already? Despite the fact that Mexi has no feeling in his body, which is bad enough, he felt pain for a moment when I forced him to tap out.” Says Chet “It’s got to be horrible not knowing how your body is going to react.” “Yeah, you’re right.” Cindy agrees with Chet “Speaking of Mexi, any word his chances in buying PCW?” “Nothing more as of yet, but I’d say that he’s in with a very good chance.” “That’s good to know. I’ve got some more Mexi related questions to ask you later, but you’d better get back to training now.” “Mexi related questions? What’s that supposed to mean?” “I was just going to ask you about what’s happening with yours and Mexi’s show, but I’ll ask you about that later. For now you’ve got to get back to training.” Chet looks around at the mess he and Cindy have made on the floor, and takes in a breath of the horrible atmosphere, before scrunching his face up. “To be honest Cindy, I don’t think I should train here any more.” “You’ve got to train though! You’ve got to train so that you can defeat Clyde on Sunday Sabotage and end your losing streak.” “I think training here might be doing me more harm then good though.” Chet says as he looks about ready to throw up again. “If we stay here much longer, we’re both going to be sick for the next five weeks.” Reluctantly, Cindy seems to agree with Chet. “I see your point, I guess we should leave now then.” “Sure thing, but I’ve just got to do one thing first.” “What’s that?” asks Cindy. Chet turns around and once again vomits all over the dirt floor. “That, now let’s leave,” states Chet. “Do you think anyone will notice the mess we’ve left?” “Just kick some dirt on to it and no one will notice.” “Good plan” Chet and Cindy kick some dirt on to their messes as the temperature in the building rises. “That’s it, we’re done” says Chet “Let’s get out of this dump.” “Sounds like a very good idea to me.” Chet King and Cindy Bi walk out of the gym with the putrid smell still drifting in the air and the heat striking their bodies. They pass a few local Mexican’s, who are used to this kind of atmosphere in the gym, on the way out. As the two walk through the exit, they pass a sign which reads; ‘The Mexican Government Rates This Facility Five Stars Out Of Five For Health And Safety – The Cleanest Gym In All Of Mexico!’
