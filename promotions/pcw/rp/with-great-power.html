With Great Power...

Date: not entered The big top has arrived in town again. Streams of people flock to the gaily colored spectacle, lured by the call of the ringmaster, a man with a bright red coat with tails off-setting his white tights. His knee-high boots clack against the hastily made platform as he gestures grandly with his cane, even occasionally taking off his top hat to emphasize his declarations that the greatest show on Earth has arrived! Ringmaster: (shouting) “Come one come all! See the Amazing Spiroughilis perform their death-defying feats of aerial acrobatics, without the benefit of a safety net below! Look on in fascination, as the Human Cannonball is launched a full 300 feet, the entire length of a football field! The most fierce animals on Earth, direct from the unforgiving African plains, placed in a cage with world renowned lion and tiger taming master Andrea Fuller, armed with only a whip against their razor-sharp claws! All under the big top for one low, low price! Children get in for only four dollars! Watch in wonder, as strongman Alan Steele performs feats of strength so incredible, no human could perform! Come San Jose! Come watch, as around the center stage, the fabulous hippodrome, where our riders shall delight and entertain you with equestrian feats of…” The crowds surge past the ringmaster, who continues his quickened cries of urging, past murals on the tents showing the performers in various stages of their acts. High-wire. Snake-boy. Clowns. A magician. One depicts a huge, muscular man with a finely trimmed beard, lifting a monstrous weight bar, 500 written on the balled ends. Alan Steele. His wide grin and manly features are unable to mistaken. The throngs filter into the three-ring big top, taking their seats, waiting of the show to begin. In a short amount of time, the opening parade spills out into the big tent, much to the delight of the crowd. The clowns tumble and cartwheel around; the show animals are prancing with pride. All the performers walk out, waving to the crowd. Steele makes his entrance, his muscles bulging under a brilliant red, white and blue singlet, bedecked with stars and sparkles. He waved grandly, occasionally flexing his pectorals and other sundry musculature. He’s an impressive sight to behold, and he is easily noticed within the dazzling sights offered by the circus. The night moves on, each act more impressive than the last. The lady who spins on a rope while hanging on by merely her teeth has just whipped the stands into tumultuous applause, bringing her out for standing ovation for her suspension superiority. She blows kisses to the crowd and makes her way to the backstage and away from the spotlight. The ringmaster takes up his spot and addresses the crowd. Ringmaster: “And now, ladies and gentlemen. Prepare yourselves for feats of strength and bravado that is… legendary! He has a combined power no mere mortal could possibly posses! Ladies and gentlemen… please welcome to the center ring… the most powerful man in the world… ALAN STEELE!!” Alan walks out to an appropriate trumpet fanfare gathering a nice reaction from the crowd. He struts his way out under the spotlight, to an already prepared center circle, where he begins to perform his stunts… Snapping metal bands off his biceps merely by flexing… Rending two stacked telephone books in twain… Holding two women over his head in each hand, and switching them over to the other hand in succession… A Volkswagen Beetle is rolled up, a midget honking its puny horn to the crowd’s delight. Steele looks down at the diminutive vehicle and frowns. Playing it up, he makes a motion for MORE midgets to get inside. Pint-sized clowns stuff themselves into the Beetle, and Al grunts and groans, straining at the ‘car’ and lifting it up over his head, to many oohs and ahhs. He caps off a night of amazing feats by tossing an elephant. The crowd roars its approval, on its feet cheering. Two curtain calls for Alan Steele. In the backstage area, after-hours, a haze hangs oppressively in the air. Steele leans back against a large wooden post, riffling through a thick wad of cash. Smiling contentedly, he counts it again and again, enjoying his newfound success and powers. “Freeze! Stop that guy!” Steele casts a disinterested eye over his fat stack, watching the scene unfold. A man in snakeskin boots and shiny black leather pants hustles by, mere inches from the haughty Steele. His ponytail billows out behind him, as he crashes through the backstage area in a desperate attempt to escape. Two police officers immediately speed by as well, giving chase to the villain. They follow as best they can, but at the exit of the tents, the culprit was lost in the catacomb-like maze created by the traveling circus shanty town. Steele shrugs and goes back to counting his money. The flap of the tent is angrily ripped aside, and the two police officers storm back inside, growling as they get up in Alan’s face. Steele looks impassively at each cop in succession. Alan Steele: (smarmy) “Can I help you gentlemen?” The first cop jabs a finger into the meaty chest of Steele, his face crimson with rage. Cop: “What’s wrong with you? You coulda stopped him!! Why didn’t you help?!” Alan Steele: “Help? I hardly see that as my job…” Second Cop: “But look at the size of you! And besides, you could have just tripped him! He was close enough…” Alan Steele: (getting angry) “What?! And risk my OWN life to do YOUR jobs?! You must be on copious amounts of crack.” Cop: (fuming) “You jackass!! You’re nothing but… but a PUNK!” Alan Steele: (growling) “I suggest you leave me now, gentlemen. As I have recently seen, you have a job to do and you aren’t quite holding up YOUR end of the taxpayer’s bargain. I have better things to do than have you explain that it was somehow MY fault you are inept.” The first cop turns an ugly shade of purple, and his hand strays to his truncheon. The second officer puts a hand on his arm, stopping him. Second Cop: “No man… he ain’t worth it. Come on. We gotta put an APB out on Snow.” The first cop snarls at Steele, but allows himself to be led away. Alan looks on with distain, clucking at their insinuations that he should have helped. Yawning, Steele stretches out and suddenly realizes that he was supposed to visit his parents at his boyhood San Jose home! Without even changing out of his glittering singlet, he rushes off to make his way home… The lights. As he drives towards to the humble home of his parents, the swirling red and blue lights can be seen from a couple blocks away. A sense of dread falls over Steele as the lights grow stronger as he nears the home. The emergency vehicles have the street blocked off, so Steele just stops his car in the center of the road and sprints by the police barking at him. Blood pounds in his ears as he screeches to a stop at the sidewalk outside his parent’s home. Police tape has marked the house off as a crime scene. Roaring in frustration and fear, Steele tears through the tape and sprints for the house. Police run over to try and stop him, but he just stiff arms them and crashes through the front door. Wild-eyed and panicked, he quickly scans the area. He notices people in the living room and runs inside… … to be greeted by a grisly scene… … Steele stumbles backwards, leaning against a wall, unable to assimilate the tragedy… … his mother… … his father… … lying in pools of blood… A coroner stands over their prostrate forms, chomping at a cigar, and talking to another officer. Coroner: “So, they think it was that Jason Snow, huh?” Snow. The guy trying to escape at the circus. The scene replays itself in his mind, the Killin’ Villain running by a greedy Alan Steele… And laughing at him. Tears stain Steele’s face and he rears back and cries out in bitter anguish. We now rejoin our Big and Bold hero, Big Alan Steele, as he trembles with emotion, hands balled into fists, as he shakes with fury. We are in Stately Steele Manor and our hero is surrounded by his normal compliment of supplemental characters: namely trusted manager Sue Saksnussum and a few Graces. Big Al’s voice strains with the bitter memories. Big Alan Steele: (heavy with sorrow) “Twas that very day that I learned that with my great power comes also great responsibility. Never again should I allow one to suffer so at the hands of villainous scum like Lord Snow! And I have dedicated my life to eradicating his demonic brood! Snow took my parents… and for that he shall pay… DEARLY!! Hi-Keeba!!” The Graces eyes are wet with tears. Sue’s eyes merely roll… Sue: “Big Al… your parents are still alive. In fact, they just visited yesterday…” BAS: (low, sinister tones) “And for that Snow and New Breed shall pay…” Cindi: “OH! Is THAT the guy who kept saying he had some candy in his pocket for me, and all I had to do was pull it out?” Sue: (throwing hands up in the air) “Next you’ll be telling me you were bit by some radioactive wrestler or something.” BAS: (raising a finger) “I have no Earthly parents, as I was torn from the thigh of Zeus! But twas the vile hand of the Killin’ Villain which robbed me of my Earthly guardians! Oh, how I remember the long passed days of my youth within the loving love of my lovely and dear mortal guardians. Twas my rebellious time, when I had forsaken my true father Zeus and clung to the bosom of the mother Hera…” Cut to a childhood home, created in the blurry mind’s eye. Big Al’s two parents are arguing mightily. Father: (yelling) “It’s just not right, I tell ya!! First, he wears those dresses around all day…” Mother: (patiently) “They are togas dear. And you wore bell-bottoms just a little while ago, and they look no less silly.” Father: “But now this?!?! It’s too much! I won’t have it! No son of mine!” Out steps a little, pudgy Little Alan Steele, a golden tiara on his head and red, knee-high boots on his feet. Dressed like Wonder Woman, he swings around a golden lasso and brandishes wristbands. LAS: “For Hera! Hi-Keeba!!” Mother: (still patient) “It’s just a phase, dear…” Cut back to Stately Steele Manor, where Sue and the Graces look positively horrified as Big Al gets a far-away look in his eyes. BAS: (reflective) “Those undergarments chafed me so…” Sue: (shaking her head, trying to clear the image) “Argh! I know it’s not true, but it doesn’t make it any less disturbing!” Terri: (about to cry) “Sue? I feel dirty.” Candi: “Me too!” Sue: “Yes, we ALL deserve nice, long, hot showers with lots of antibacterial lather. Al… you just stay here and remember one thing – make New Breed pay! If not for YOUR false memories, then for subjecting US to your false memories!” BAS: “And that they shall! Hi-Keeba!”
