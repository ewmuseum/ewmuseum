A nice little cruise

25 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7/25/2002 ~'God must have had an off day when he created him.' - Andrew Dice Clay, 1989. Whom can this phrase be applied to as it relates to Jon Kano? Well it could be applied to two people, those two being Semore Smoke and Corey Williams. These three men have been at each other's throats for a little over a month now, with Corey and Jon Kano at each other's throats for the last two months. This Sunday, at Wrestle Vessel, all hell will break loose. It will be time to put up or shut up for these three. Each man will be tied at the wrist to each other by a leather strap. There will be no escape, and no time to talk trash to one another. Three men ready to kill each other, all three tied to each other, what does this make for? QUALITY TELEVISION. Semore Smoke, a pot smoking burnout, can't win. A man who does that much narcotics never amounted to much. Corey Williams, a little rat bastard who's greatest ambition in life is to be as great as Kano, can't win.~ *The scene opens up a huge cruise ship heading towards Jacksonville, the location of this Friday's Fusion. You see various PCW superstars living it up on the various decks of the ship, but at the front of the ship, looking over the waters as the ship barrels at about 6 knots through the water is Jon Kano. Kano is dressed in tan khakis shorts, brown leather sandles, and a Hawaiian shirt, unbuttoned to expose his inpressive body. The beautiful reflection of the sunset on the Caribbean Sea is on the lenses of his Armani sunglasses as he sips a martini.* Kano: Well it is about time that Tyler Nelson did something right, it only took him a year and a half. He finally got some sense and acquired this luxurious cruise ship. I love watching the sunset on the ocean from this deck. But don't let this beautiful scene get you all mushy inside, I am still one very pissed off legend. I was sitting in my room, watching the tape from Turmoil and I saw not one, but two sumbitches in my locker room, talking about me. I knew when I went in there it smelt like sh*t, but I just thought that Cal Seaver went to sniff my wrestling tights, sick bastard. You know I don't mind friends of mine dropping by my locker room and shooting the sh*t with me, but my friends don't include two men that I have spent the last month beating the piss out of. Corey Williams sitting in MY chair, and then Semore Smoke said that he was going to smoke my ass. Now I don't know if that is some prison term or whatever, but I ain't down for any smoking of my ass. *Kano takes a sip of his martini.* Kano: But even more than those two being in MY locker room is the fact that they were there TOGETHER. That should be written down in history because it was the first time two out of three of us got together and not a punch was thrown. That just doesn't sit right with this REAL LEGEND. This Sunday, hell will pause in horror of what goes down in that ring. Children will cry for their mothers, grown men will wince, and even Clyde will be amazed at how brutal this match will be. Not one, not two, but three men tied to each other like wild animals ready to kill. I don't plan on walking out of this match underneath my own power, and you know what? I want it that way. I want this to be the final chapter, the closing door on this sick, twisted violence triangle we got going on. The three of us will walk into my ring. Wait, do you want to know why I say it is my ring? I came here in October of 2000, soon after PCW had come into existence. I came in and wasn't given a snowball's chance in hell of lasting. I was told, 'You are big over in Japan, but you ain't shit here.' I said, 'I am big over in Japan, and give me six months.' I set PCW on fire. Never before had PCW seen a man my size use such a variety of moves executed with such agility. I was tagged with the infamous, 'Kano ROCKS~!' by Argyle. I soon became known as the 'Man who Rocketh the Mosteth,' and soon I realized that I was a legend, a REAL legend. I poured my life into that ring. I bled in that ring, I sweated in that ring, and the fans appreciated it. I give everything I have night in and night out. I sacrifice my own well being to win. I'll do nothing less at Wrestle Vessel. I am bringing my A game to Montego Beach. *Kano reaches into his pocket and pulls out a pack of his trademark Marlboros. He lights one and puts the pack away.* Kano (exhaling smoke as he speaks): Corey Williams is a piece of work. I am twenty six, and if you listen to him you would think that I was in my fifties, nearing the end of my career. Well as Judas Preist put it, YOU GOT ANOTHER THING COMIN'. I am twenty six years old, I have done everything I have ever aspired to in life, and I still got another twenty years left in my body for this industry. Corey Williams is jealous of what I have accomplished. He and I are so close in age, but I have accomplished so much more than he has in this industry. Ask someone who is a PCW fan who the top five big men are in PCW history. Do you know what they will say? Boda, Ivan, Meanstreak, Akiyama, and Kano. Am I the best who has ever walked down the aisle? Probably not, but I am in pretty good company there. I look at Corey and all I see is jealousy raging from his body, and jealousy will ultimately destroy whatever is left of Corey Williams after Sunday. *Kano takes a sip of his martini, then takes a long drag off of his cigarette, savoring the cancerous smoke.* Kano: Semore Smoke is a man who made a lot of waves over in my buddy, Chris Sloboda, 's fed, NTICW, but NTICW is down here while PCW is up here. We are completely different ballgame and whatever greatness Smoke had in NTICW doesn't amount to shit here. I have done more in a week here than he has in the month he has been here. I, quite frankly, don't give a damn who Semore Smoke is, nor what he has accomplished other places. I am here, he is here, and we gonna rumble. We didn't used to have a problem, but now we do. I am not an easy person to get on the bad side of, but Smoke managed to do it. Ask Chuck Manson how it is being on my bad side, and if you can understand him through his three teeth he has remaining, you'll hear, 'Don't get on his bad side, look where I am now.' Smoke needs to lay off the drugs for the next couple days, because unless he comes into Wrestle Vessel completely numb, like a piece of f*cking meat, he won't win. I intend on inflicting a great amount of pain in this match, but I know that I will have a great amount inflicted upon me. But when all the smoke has cleared, and all the dust has settled, it will be I, the Man who Rocketh the Mosteth, who has proven once again why he is what REAL LEGENDS ARE MADE OF. *The scene fades to black as Kano continues to sip his martini and smoke his cigarette.*
