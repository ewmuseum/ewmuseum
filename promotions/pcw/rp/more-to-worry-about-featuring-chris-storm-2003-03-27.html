More to Worry About (Featuring Chris Storm)

27 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/27/03 A beautiful pink sun set lights up the sky as the moisture in the air increases. It seemed that at any moment the sky would crack open and drops of precipitation would fall from the sky, but that wouldn’t put a damper on anything except the ground for the mood in the Windsor-Storm household was already as dampened as it could get. Lingering freshly on the minds of both Chris and Tessa was the uniting of Tessa and Gaz that had taken place this past Monday and been brought up once more on Tuesday. There was no consent from either party, yet in just a second lives were changed with the simple words from a vicar, “You may now kiss the bride.” Though the bride this man was speaking about was Tessa Windsor these words have seemed to have affected Chris in a much larger deal than Tessa. It could be seen in even his most simple actions or words, and as much as Tessa wanted to help there was simply nothing she could do about it. Boxes were stacked in the Windsor-Storm home. This time it was not from boxes of moving in however. After the recent events Chris had decided to move out of the room with Tessa and into the guest room were he claimed 'he belonged'. His stuff was already moved out of the master bedroom and into the guest room. All that remained now was unpacking it. Although after three days Chris still hadn't did that. In fact Chris hadn't did much of anything in the past three days except for come home and go to sleep after watching a little television in his room. It was almost as if he didn't even live with them anymore. He merely kept to himself and never bothered anyone. 'Chris' Tessa's voice called out. There was no reply as usual. Tessa let out a sigh as she frowned. In the guest room Chris just sat on the side of the bed looking at the small TV he had set up. There was nothing but static playing but Chris' blue eyes were focused in on it as he kept his head hanging low. He heard Tessa call for him and turned his head toward the sound of her voice. Rather then call back or go see what she wanted he just turned back and looked at the statically screen and let out a soft whimper. “Chris please listen, I know you hear me.” Tessa pleaded, hoping that he would respond in anyway, even if it were telling her to shut up, but just as before her pleas fell on deaf ears. Instead of answering, Chris stood up from his chair and walked away to prevent Tessa’s voice from getting to his mind. Tessa takes a seat in the seat Chris stood up from and lets out a sigh of her own. It seems that in the last three days it has been hopeless getting Chris to communicate with her, despite the fact that what had happened was not her fault. Looking back she sees Chris in the doorway with his head resting up against up against the doorframe. Just seeing Chris in this state made Tessa wish that she were the one feeling this way and not Chris simply because she would give the world to make him happy, even if it meant suffering every inch of pain for him. Looking at Tessa in her sad state Chris had to turn away from her. He closed the door behind him blocking her sight from his mind. He knew he was hurting her with the way he was acting as of late. He didn't like it really but he was broken hearted and needed time to himself to get over what had happened. Right now everything was turned upside down for Chris. He was one half of the tag team champions so he should be happy. Yet he was never more miserable then now. Chris just flopped down on the bed and decided to go to sleep to avoid it all. Instead of following Chris, Tessa simply sunk back into the seat and let her emotions overcome her. It was not easy knowing that once these tears started there was no telling when they would stop, but if she held them back anymore she felt like her crystalline blue eyes would explode. So she sat there and wept, soaking herself in her own tears. Even if she was one half of the tag team champions with Chris she felt like things couldn’t get worse even if the belt happened to fall out of their possession. “Tessa?” A voice questioned from the doorway. The voice was too high pitched to belong to Chris. Tessa turned around once more in her seat to see young Evan looking over at Tessa with concern shinning in his four year old eyes. “Come on in Evan.” She said, wiping away some of the tears from her eyes in an attempt to look the slightest bit better than she actually was, but no matter how many tears she wiped away the sorrow would still show on her face. She knew that what had occurred would perhaps permanently damage her and Chris’ relationship and there was nothing she could do to fix that. “Why are you crying Tessa?” Little Evan questioned as he affectionately wrapped his arms as far around her body as he could, embracing her in the best possible hug he could give. “Just for grown up reason’s Evan.” She answered, not wanting to let him in on what really happened. How could she possibly let him know that because of what happened there might also be a chance that he might lose another person who was a father figure to him. No matter how hard she tried she just couldn’t break herself to do it. “Are you sad for the same reason Chris is sad?” He came back with yet another question. He knew that something was up the minute Chris no longer wanted to be around me. “Yes, why don’t you go see Chris and try to cheer him up. I need some time alone.” Tessa commented before burying her face deep into the palms of her hands letting out yet another depressing sigh. The little one makes his way to the guest room and knocks on the door. Chris lifted his head up and looked at the door yet he made no reply to it. Chris just laid his head back down against the pillow. The door slowly crept open. Chris lifts his head again but sees no one at the door. Thinking it's just someone looking in he lays his head back down and closes his eyes. Suddenly he fills a weight on his stomach. 'Oomph!' he lets out as the impact nearly knocks the air out of him. His eyes shoot open to see Evan sitting on his stomach. Evan lies down onto Chris' chest and examines his face. Chris tries to ignore him in hopes that Evan will get bore and move on. Instead the little one processed to poke Chris in the face. 'Chris….' Evan says as he continues to poke away at Chris' face in an attempt to wake him up. '…are you asleep?' 'Yes.' came the simple reply. “Evannnn” A voice called from down the hall. The young boy’s ears perked up knowing that it was Tessa’s and he immediately crawled off of Chris stomach and waited to see what command would follow it like an obedient dog. “I’m in here!” The boy responded as he walked towards the doorway to lead Tessa closer with his voice figuring that if he could get Tessa and Chris in the same room perhaps he could patch up the tension between the two. “Evan, can you…” Before Tessa could finish her sentence as she entered in through the doorway and noticed Chris spread across the bed, still looking rather upset. Instead of finishing her sentence, Tessa’s head drops as she walks away from the door trying to best to keep the tears forming in her eyes from the sight of Chris and his obvious pain. It is obvious that come next Tuesday she will have a lot more to worry about than P.W.A. and Teresa Tomas; she will have to worry about Chris as well.
