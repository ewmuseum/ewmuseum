The Legend of the Fridge Vol. 8: Pete's Lament

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: FF #1 [Inside the cold, dark, clandestine plastic and wire room, several bottles lay half empty. Deli meat is strewn about. The baking soda, tipped over. Soda leaks off a shelf, and falls onto the ground, seemingly miles down. Groans are heard thorought the room.] Voice #1: Ugh...my head... Voice #2: Shh...he might come back... Voice #1: But...my head hurts... Voice #2: Come here, let me take a look at that. [Clinking sounds are heard as something starts moving in the dark. With every clink, there's a groan. The sound of plastic rustling and crinkling breaks the other silence.] Voice #2: Can I get some light here? A light turns on, and a bottle of Tabasco™ Sause is right next to a head of lettuce: Lettuce: Ohhh...my head. Hot Sauce: Damn. That foo took a big ol' chunk outta ya dome, bro. Lettuce: Tell me about it. Hot Sauce: Can I get a Casualty Report? [Instantly the other food products spring to life, checking each other out.] Ketchup: Hot! Hot! Oh my God! Come here!!! [The Hot Sauce slides down the shelf and looks onto the platter containing nothing but a few rib bones, and grisle.] Hot: Poor, poor Ribs... Ketchup: The Watermelon's gone too! Hot: Damnit! Damnit. I hates days like this. Milk: What can we do? That's what we're here for. Hot: I know that, foo'! Don't be thinkin I ain't be knowin what I be here fo'.[Suddenly, the door opens again. The food inside gasps as Pete looks in. The older looking man does not wear his years of abuse well. His large body doesn't hide his girth well. His combed over grey greasy hair does nothing to hide the bald spot on his head. He looks around and reaches for a Soda with his chubby fingers, but stops short. He looks at the mess inside, and sighs.]Hot: Eh? Something wrong boss? Pete: Huh? Oh. It's nothing. Hot: Come on, you know yo punk ass can tell us. We yo homies. Pete: Yeah. I guess. It's just...well. I put in an application for a job, but they got my resume mixed up with another pile, and hired me as a wrestler. Ketchup: You're going back into wrestling? Pete: I don't have a choice. Hot: Damn. I feel sorry for them crazy as mofos that be buyin tickets. Who you wrestling? Pete: Duzza and Jamie Celt. Hot: Damn. Yous gunna get yo ass handed to you dawg. Duzza be my homie. What you gunna do? Pete: I...I don't know. I guess...I'll go, get a beating, and go home. Not much I can do.[There's a knock at the door. Pete closes the fridge door, and walks to his front door in his small apartment. He stumbles and falls over a pile of cans in the middle of the floor, as another knock comes at the door, this one a bit more urgent.]Pete: Damnit! I'm Coming! Jesus. Give a fat man a chance will you. Damn.[He opens the door and looks around seeing nothing. Suddenly, Pete feels a tug on his pants leg. Standing infront of him is a 3'6' Chinese Midget. He looks at Pete with narrowing eyes. he wears a Pokemon T-Shirt, with a pair of jeans and a Batman hat. He looks just as grubby as Pete does.]Pete: Hey! You're late. I ordered chinese an hour ago! Midget: ...! Pete: What are you talking about? Midget: ...? Pete: Yes, I ordered General Tso Platter. Why? Midget: [Pulling out an ID] ... Pete: General...Tso? You're General Tso? Midget: ... Pete: [Staring at the midget] How the hell am I going to cook you? Tso: ...?! Pete: What? I can't eat you? Damnit. Go back and get me my chinese food. Tso: [Pulling out a little card, hands it to Pete] Pete: What? No Refunds? I'm stuck with you? Tso: ...! Pete: Damnit. Fine. Come on in. [Pete closes the door as the midget walks into his pigsty. Tso looks around and shakes his head back and forth. He starts to clean up a little so he can actually walk around with his little legs. Pete picks up the phone, and dials.] Pete: Hello Mom? Hey, I'm glad you're home. Guess what I got today? No...No, i got a job too, but I got something else. Huh? No. A Midget. Yeah. A midget. How the hell do you take care of a midget anyways? [General Tso looks at Pete and shakes his head. This could be the beginning of a beautiful friendship...either that or Pete will eat him in his sleep. Regardless, A fat guy and a midget is pure gold, folks...]
