The Becoming Saga Part 1: The Letter

9 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9th April Entry Date: 9th April 2002 Today was a pretty strange day for me … After turning my back on the dark side I think that I may have finally found out where my future truly lies. I am not going to go into much detail on the matter, but instead I will let you view for yourself, for a change. I still do not know if I am going to follow the occurrences that happened today, as I am not entirely sure what it all meant. But hey, that’s life. - Andy Start Tape The scene slowly opens up, inside the darkened bedroom of Andy Murray’s Milwaukee apartment. The room is a very dull, dreary and bleak sort of place, and is not visually pleasing in the slightest. It’s more of an eyesore than anything else. The walls of the square shaped room are painted in a light shade of grey, in some places the paint has started to peel away from the wall. The roof is also painted in this same shade of grey, whereas the floor is made of strong and sturdy, hard-wearing wooden floor boards, which look like they are varnished. However, most of this varnish has worn or peeled away, leaving the grin bare floorboards below exposed. There is a large, rectangular window at the far end of the room, which has a white blind pulled down across it. Light barely pours in from the gaps of the blind, clearly it is sunny outside. The floor of the room is also pretty messy; ruffled up grey rugs, clothes, documents and a whole host of other things are scattered randomly around the room. Along the right hand side wall of this room, there is a weathered chest of drawers, painted white. 2 of the 4 drawers that occupy the chest are pulled slightly open, one is closed, and the bottom drawer hangs out onto the floor below, empty. Directly across from this chest of drawers along the left hand side wall, is a large cupboard, which stands tall above the rest of the room’s furniture. Beside the aforementioned window, running along the far wall of the room, is a large, single bed. Within the confides of it’s black sheets, lies the sleeping for of Andy Murray, who seems to be tossing and turning in his sleep. At the foot of the bed is a small table, on which sits a small, old, colour television, which frankly looks Stone Age. Past that table & television, on the right hand wall is a door, it’s frame painted black, standing out from the walls. The door is slightly a-jar, but not enough so that we can see through it. We slowly move forwards towards the bed, homing in on Andy as he moves during his sleep. We can see through the tin white sheets that Andy is wearing a black vest as well as a pair of black shorts as he sleeps. He continues his restless slumber, it’s as if he was having some kind of nightmare. Andy having a nightmare? That idea may be quite absurd considering the type of person Andy was just a few weeks ago, and considering all he has went through in life, but with his sudden change of heart as of late, it is a distinct possibility. Last Sunday at Spring Fling, Andy took part in a battle royal to determine the Rising Star champion, Andy managed to avoid looking like a total bonce, but he did put on a slightly disappointing performance, but hey, he DID eliminate Jay Sylo after all. However, this coming Sunday at Sabotage, Andy will get the chance to win the RS title once again, as he takes on the battle royal winner Shawn Mosley. Mosley already holds a victory over Murray, perhaps this time could be different? Also this week, Murray will face off against Kramer Wild Wing, Jack Valentine, and Violence Jack in a 4 way dance. Andy already holds a count out victory over Valentine, will he be able to repeat his victory? Or will Valentine, Kramer or Violence Jack pick up the victory? Suddenly, Andy sits bolt upright in his bed, now wide awake and breathing heavily. Andy’s eyes are wide open, with a state of near shock across his face. “The New Nightmare” raises his hand to his brow, and wipes away the beads of sweat, which were running down his forehead. Andy then pauses for a second, before brushing the sheets aside, turning round, and sitting with his legs over the side of the bed. Andy holds his head of his hands, and sits motionless for a few seconds, contemplating the nightmare he probably just had. Andy then slowly rises to his feet, and takes a long, deep breath in, followed by an equally long deep breath out, closing his eyes as he does this. Andy opens his eyes again, and looks around the mess that is his bedroom, like a vulture watching it’s prey dying right before it goes in for the kill. Andy then slowly begins to trundle through the mess on the floor, and walks across to the chest of drawers. When he reaches the drawers, Andy pulls the top drawer open, and pulls out a pair of black jeans. Andy wipes the fine layer of dust from the jeans, before unfolding them, and sliding them on, fastening them with a large duty black belt. Andy then walks across towards the cupboard, and pulls out a pair of heavy black boots from the bottom of the cupboard. Murray slowly walks towards the bed, and takes a seat as he Slides the boots on, and ties the laces hard. Andy pauses for another few seconds, as we begin to notice the blank look across his rough face. Andy then slowly turns around, facing the dawn blind and the window. Reaching across towards one side of the blind, Andy pulls the cord and suddenly the blind flips up fast. Sunlight begins to floor the room, Andy squints, he is more or less staring directly at the sun unintentionally. Using his hand as a shield to the harsh Sun, Andy pulls the window open vertically, and takes another deep breath, taking in the cool, crisp morning air. Through the newly opened window, we can see that Andy’s apartment must be near the top floor, judging by the height we are above the ground. Down below we see a busy city during the morning rush hour. People run back and forth along the sidewalks, and there is near gridlock down on the congested roads. Andy then jumps down from the bed, and walks across the room again, this time towards the door. Murray gently prods the door with the palm of his hand; it gently slides open. Andy steps into a corridor, which is much, like his bedroom; dull and bleak. It is decorated in the exact same way, just shows how bad a Satanist’s decorating tastes can be. There is another window along the left hand wall of this corridor, which is slightly smaller than the one in the bedroom, and does not have a blind drawn down on it. There are a further 3 doors on the other wall, all of which are closed. There is also a door at the end of this small corridor, with a letterbox and a peep-hole, several letters lie on the floor in front of it. On the large windowsill of this window, sits an old looking white telephone and answering machine, a small red light flashes on the answering machine; Andy has a message. Wiping the sleep from his eyes, Andy reaches down, and presses the flashing red button; a loud “BEEP” follows, followed by the voice of an annoying salesperson. ”Hi there, my name is Ken from Auto Insurance Direct! Did you know that you can save up to 20% on your automobile insurance from Auto Insurance Direct? We-” Before “Ken” can finish, Andy quickly taps the button on more time, as the messages comes to an end. Another message, accompanied by the “BEEP” follows. ”Hey Andy, Gregg from the book store here … Listen, we’ve got those orders you requested in, just pop by anytime to pick them up … And try not to hurt anyone this time please … Thanks!” Another “BEEP” signifies the end of all the messages … Andy starts to walk towards the door at the end of the corridor, which quite obviously leads out of the apartment. When he reaches the door, Andy kneels down, and picks up the letters from the floor. ”Rubbish … Rubbish …Nonsense … Hmm … Worth a look” Andy mumbles to himself as he looks through the letters. Andy drops the “Rubbish, Rubbish & Nonsense” letters to the ground, and rises to his feet, holding the other letter in his hands. He then turns one of the handles of the doors along the wall, and enters into his kitchen, which, surprisingly, is not as dull as the other rooms of his apartment. Although it is not exactly expertly decorated, it still looks quite nice in comparison to everything else. The walls are painted in white, and much of the wooden panelling & interior is painted in a chestnut colour. A large table with 6 chairs set out around sits in the middle of the brown tiled floor, and a tall, thin window at the other end of the room lets the light in. Andy’s kitchen is well decked out as well, with everything you’d expect to see in a kitchen these days; kettle, fridge, freezer, oven, microwave, toaster, stove … etc. A really old picture of Andy in his youth, alongside his late parents and lost brother, enjoying a family picnic, hangs on one of the walls. Andy does not take his eyes off of the letter as he walks around the table, until he comes to the picture. Andy stops in his tracks, and stares at the picture, his eyes full of emotion. He recalls days past when his family was whole, and when life was a breeze, before switching his view away from the picture, and taking a seat at the table. He holds the letter out in front of him, his address is handwritten on the front. Staring at it for a few further seconds, Andy shrugs, before slowly opening the letter. Once the envelope has been opened, Andy pulls the letter out, unfolds it, and sits it down on the table, before proceeding to read it; Mr. Murray, The following letter has been sent to you because our representatives have been scouting you for a number of months now, and we see you as a perfect additions to our organisation. My identity shall remain secret to you at the moment, but if you choose to join us all will become clear. The Digital Reality Corporation are, like you, a group of individuals who have searched fro the truth all of our lives. But, where others have failed in the quest of knowledge, we have succeeded. We know the truth behind more things than even you yourself can imagine. Of course our constant uncovering of top secret information has made us natural enemies of the government, and we are constantly evading their presence, but all we are doing is fighting the peoples battle. We feel that a man of your intelligence and attitude would make a great addition to our corporation, and that is why you are receiving this letter. To discuss further, I wish to meet with you, tomorrow, 11am at Fabrizio’s Coffee Shop, you know the one just around the corner? Meet me there, and we shall discuss things further. You must not, I repeat, MUST NOT inform anyone of you receiving this letter, if you do then the consequences will be huge. Thank you. - A Representative, The Digital Reality Corporation After reading this strange letter, a look of confusion spreads across Murray’s face, which quickly changes to a look that says “What harm can it do?” ”Ah, what the hell … It’s worth a shot …” Andy says to himself, as he carefully folds the letter up again, and slides it back into the envelope. The scene slowly fades out, into blackness. Will Andy meet the representative tomorrow morning? Guess we’ll just have to wait and see.
