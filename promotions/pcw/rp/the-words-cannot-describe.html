Date: 4/24/04 - translated onto film 4/25/04 You know, it's pretty funny when somebody drops you out of your chance to attain something big. You feel sluggish, vapid even. Somehow, somewhat you tend to be a bit weepish.At first, I looked at my huge beating by Kano and his clique and took it for what it was, an average beating on a new guy who looked to solidify himself in the place.Then I took a second glance at it.And it pissed me off so much that I could barely even breathe correctly.'Fucking posers,' I could hear myself muttering at the sight of it once more, replaying over and over again in my head that I had been snatched away from the main event at an upcoming Pay Per View because of one medical breakdown.The Board wasn't going to deal with Donovan Emerson, his 'Father' was going to come down on them with a serious force that it would shut PCW down yet again and keep it down this time.I'm a damn lackey to every single thing that I do.My father has to have a hand in each of my moves, and each of my various positions. I wouldn't be crazed or incesed enough by his ever known presence over me - it scares me even.He scares me.Nothing else in this world scares me more than my father, Samuel Emerson.And any Emerson would never back down from a challenge.Especially if they had been knocked down before.NEVER!
