Unnusual Occurence

4 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5/04/03 The black punching bag sagged in the middle and swung violently on it’s chain with every blow delivered by Brianna’s raw fists. She had been at it for hours not letting the constant flow of sweat down her face, or her sore muscles stop her. Having come off a huge loss against Jackson she needed something to take out her frustrations on and the bag was just sitting there, beckoning her to pound it to pieces, or so she thought. “I….can’t….believe…..I….lost…my…title!” Brianna said with clenched teeth, looking at the punching bag with anger. Brianna bites her bottom lip as the pain surges through her right fist having busted it open. Ignoring the pain, she decides to continue on, sending harder punches then ever in the air. “How could I lose on my first title defense? That’s just pathetic!” she screams at the top of her lungs as she continues to lay into the bag, leaning forward, and finally stopping as she rests her head on it with tears streaming down her face as though a flood gate had just been blown up. Being in an open gym, Brianna had caught the attention of almost everyone there, who was watching her break down right in front of their eyes. She hadn’t even realized it, and still hadn’t, as she still leaned across the bag mumbling stuff which couldn’t be made out. Too enthralled with her actions to do anything, Brianna is left by herself getting no help from anyone in the room. That is until a man walks in through the front door looking shocked to see everyone’s eyes staring at someone at the far end of the room by a punching bag. Not just any man however, Thomas, a curious person himself, he walks around a maze of machines. “What’s going on here?” he asks a man sitting at the rowing machine with a nice chiseled body showing for the world to see. “Some chick is freaking out, and is going out of control on that punching bag.” Thomas turns around a corner and spots the reddish brown curls growing a look of concern on his face as he steps towards her. “Brianna?” he asks timidly while touching her shoulder. Brianna turns to face Thomas and her face is red and tear ridden. Wanting some support she jumps into his arms and buries her head in his chest sobbing away carelessly. Thomas grabs her and escorts her out of the gym, absolutely appalled that no one offered her any help. His face is lit up with joy because this is the first time in a while he has held Brianna this close. He thinks back to the old days and his smile brightens remembering when they were once a couple. “What’s wrong with you?” he asks as they make it outside and she pulls away to wipe her eyes. “I don’t want to talk about it.” She continues to swipe away the tears with her hands, and Thomas catches her bloodied knuckles, grabbing her hands and looking down at them. “You’re bleeding Bri!” “It’s nothing at all.” She pulls her hands away and turns to walk towards her car. “I can’t let you drive in this state.” Thomas says, catching up to her, grabbing onto her shoulder to stop her. “Please just let me give you a ride home.” He looks at her and she knows his intentions are good. “Fine. Thank you.” “No problem Miss.” He flashes her a smile as he guides her to her car, opening the door for her, and stepping over to the driver’s side. As soon as he sits inside, she hands him her keys somewhat reluctantly, realizing just who is sitting next to her. Her ill feelings towards him awkwardly disappear as he starts the car and takes off to the streets. “So do you mind telling me why you were having a nervous breakdown in the middle of Gold’s Gym back there?” In her mind she debates on whether or not to tell him, fearing that he will just use it against her in the future anyway. But the way she feels in the car let’s her know that he won’t dare do it. For some reason she doesn’t feel uncanny telling him anything ready to let her feelings spill. “My intentions weren’t to go there and bawl my eyes out. I had gone there just to have a good old work to get my cardiovascular system pumping. Things were going great for about half an hour until I overheard someone mention my loss to Jackson on Turmoil and it just got me to thinking. After all of the crap I had to deal with Skitzo, and to earn the damn title, I just threw it away! I could have won, but I didn’t, showing me that all of my hard work and effort was wasted. I began to compare that little tidbit to my life and realized that that’s not the first time I’ve given something up so easily after having worked so hard to get it.” She said as Thomas listened intently. Hearing her mention her last sentence made Thomas wonder if she was referring to their relationship long ago. But the more he thought about it he realized she probably pushed that far out of her mind. “You can’t beat yourself up over it.” Thomas offers up as he makes a sharp turn to beat a red light. “I just feel like a failure.” “Hunny you’re no failure! You’ve done more in your life than people can dream. And so what if you lost the title? There will always be other chances. I mean don’t you face Miss Moonlight for a belt on Friday?” “Yes. But there is no way I can beat her. She’s the new wave at PCW, and she’s a great wrestler to boot.” “No wonder you are feeling crappy. Stop being such a pessimist. Think happy thoughts. Ever since I’ve known you you’ve always been able to root the good from the bad. Like that time I fell out of that tree trying to get your cat…” They both look at each other for a split second reminiscing about the past. Thomas then realizes that it was a bad idea to bring up that time in the past however as he sees a sad look overcome Brianna. “I’m sorry…” “No problem. Well there’s my house.” She says hurriedly ready to leave. Sure enough they had already reached Brianna’s house. Thomas parks and gets out of the car helping Brianna out. She gives him a big hug and kiss on the cheek. “Thank you Thomas. I like you a lot better when you're not a jerk.” She laughs a bit as she takes her keys from his outstretched arms and walks up the steps leading to her porch. Thomas looks on pleased with his good deed, and starts walking away feeling no grudge towards Brianna for once. “You’re no failure Bri, and you’ll never be one in my eyes.” With that the scene slowly fades to black.
