Ahhhhh, the pub

3 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/3/2002 *The scene opens up inside of a London pub. Several of Great Britain's finest citizens are sloshed off their asses and sitting/laying/leaning on the bar. The bartender is washing the now empty bar glasses in a sink as the pub door swings open. The sunlight comes in, causing the semi-conscience drunkards to cover their eyes and wince at the natural light. Through the doorway, enters Jon Kano. Kano begins to walk to the bar, dressed in a tight, black t-shirt with 'REAL LEGEND' in white lettering across the chest, and a pair of baggy blue jeans. Kano grabs a stool and sits at the bar with a drunk Englishman to each of his sides.* Bartender: What are ye havin, son? Kano: Jack and Coke on the rocks, tall glass. Bartender: Aye. *The bartender begins to make Kano's drink.* Kano: What is this guy's story? *Kano gestures toward the man who is passed out to his right. The bartender gives Kano his drink.* Bartender: His wife left him. Kano: I see. I would probably get shitfaced too. Bartender: She left him six years ago. Kano: Damn. Bartender: He won the lottery about four months after she left and has spent about every dime in here. He is the first one in and the last one out everyday. Kano: Jesus, and people say I drink too much. Bartender: What are you doing in London? I hear your American accent in your voice. Kano: Very good. I am a professional wrestler. Bartender: Ahhh, I heard one of those wrestling companies was in town doing a show. Kano: This isn't just a company. Primetime Championship Wrestling is the industry leader in sports entertainment. *The man to Kano's wife comes out of his stooper and stands up straight.* Man: I think pro wrestlers are pussies. *Kano stands up and looks down at the drunken man, still sitting on his stool.* Kano: Care to prove you hypothesis? Bartender: Hey, he's drunk. Kano: Well then he won't feel the pain until tommorrow. *The man stumbles/falls off of his stool. He throws an off balance right at Kano. Kano swerves to avoid the punch. The drunk throws a left. Kano avoids it again, but throws a knee into the drunk's midsection. The drunk doubles over in pain. Kano begins to dance around like Ali until he stops and just pushes the drunk over onto the bar room floor.* Bartender: Well at least you went easy on him. Kano: You're damn right I went easy. Come to see us on Tuesday, and I'll show you what I can do. I am wrestling this punk motherf*cker named Ali Khadhfi. He made himself a name in PURE but has slowly made himself a joke here in PCW. No one takes him seriously, but I got something to prove and I am going to use him to prove it. Bartender: What do you have to prove? Kano: I get to slowly prove that I still got what it takes despite what any so-called celestial says. If this guy named the End thinks I don't hear what he says, then he will know in the upcoming week why I am WHAT REAL LEGENDS ARE MADE OF!!!!!! *The scene fades to black as Kano bursts out the door of the London pub.*
