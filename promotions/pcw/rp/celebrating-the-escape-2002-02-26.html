Celebrating the Escape

26 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2/26/02 Time: 6: 38 P.MLocation: Lisbon, PortugalScene: Restaurant Part 4 “Cause the whole world loves it when you’re in the news.” The scene opens with Juan Borro waiting in the center of the city in Lisbon, to meet Juliet, so they can all go out and celebrate. Juan has been waiting for a while, but he knows Juliet will be coming soon enough. Juan doesn’t know what he’s going to say to Juliet, but he knows he has so many great things he wants to tell her. Juan wonders if Juliet will still show him the same love and compassion she always has. He thinks maybe she didn’t believe him when he called earlier. She did seem surprised and in doubt. Maybe she thought she was going to be ambushed, in that case it is better off that she doesn’t come. Juan glances at his watch once again, as fifteen minutes have passed. Juan then goes off and starts walking the other way. A few seconds later Juliet’s cry is heard from behind Juan. Juliet- Juan! Juan slowly turns around and sees Juliet just standing there out of breath. No one is in Juan’s way, so he just goes charging at her. Juan- Juliet! I can’t believe you made it. The two run straight up to one another, and share a big hug and kiss. The two are so excited they are trembling. Juliet tries to speak, but Juan smothers her with kisses on every part of her lips. Juliet- It’s great to see you to. I didn’t have time to ask you before, but how did you escape the prison? Juan- It was actually pretty easy, other than the fact that I lost a great friend in the process, but you shouldn’t have to be burdened with the tragedies of the story. All that matters now is that I’m back. All I want to do tonight is go out, and the two of us along with the others are going to have a great time. Juliet- Sure, I’m just happy to see you back. Are they going to find out, and make you go back their though? Juan- I’m never going back their again. I may have to go for the trial, but they’re working on that, so I might not have to do anything. Over here China can no longer touch me, so there is nothing they can do. Juliet- Great. The two rejoice once again. They then hold hands and begin to walk off to the restaurant where they will be having their meal tonight. After a few minutes of walking, Juan and Juliet arrive at the restaurant. When they make their way to the restaurant, they go up to the front, and ask if reservations have been made earlier. The waiter tells them how their has, and leads them over to their table. After being led to their table, they see Cesar and his mother already there, sitting down, and eating some bread. Juan- Cesar! You were able to make it this is great. Cesar- Yea, well I did just get out of prison, and un like some of the other guys I didn’t have a to do list. Juan- I’m guess that’s your mother. Cesar- Yes. Mom, this is Juan Borro, the man who is responsible for me being here right now. Juliet listens on as she sees the man speak of Juan in such a highly manner. She then looks at Juan who is shaking hands with the mother of Cesar. She sees the happiness in his eyes, and her smile gets even bigger. Juan- Oh, I almost forgot. This right here is Juliet. Yes, the Juliet I talked about like every single day in prison. Juliet smiles as she shakes hands with Cesar, who gives he a kiss on the right check. After everyone is finished being introduced they sit down and wait for the others. While waiting, they order a small appetizer. Juliet- So how bad really was this prison. From the impression I’m getting it was an actual hell on earth. Juan- It was bad. It’s so bad you don’t even want to talk of it too much; you get chills up your back. I could never imagine spending my whole life there, or even a year like the other guys did. Cesar- Yea, it’s hard. You have to know the right things to do, and the right people to meet in order to survive. Anyone is in complete danger during his or her first three months. Those times were probably the hardest. I’m just happy all of that is finally over now. Suddenly, Paolo and Jose come walking in, wearing black tuxedos, and looking as sharp as knives. Paolo- I see you guys have been starting things without us, what is up with that? So how is everyone doing? Everyone begins speaking at once, as Jose and Paolo introduce themselves to the rest of the table. Raul, the last of the men then comes hopping in on a pair of crutches. He notices the table and shouts over to them. Raul- Don’t worry, I’m here. Jose- Ha! It should be the other way around. Were worried when you are here. So Raul, we see you got the leg looked at. Raul- Yea, I have to use crutches, but the isn’t bad at all. Reminds me of when I was a kid and would always get in sport accidents. Paolo- Raul, who are you fooling, you were a fat little kid. The only sport you played was who could finish their dinner first. Everyone shares a laugh, as they go on eating. Juan’s attention is drawn to Juliet, as the to share some intimate moments. Meanwhile, the other guys become a little hungry and order their food. Cesar- I know the rest of you like sitting around, but I’m dying for something to eat. Were actually going to be eating good food tonight boys. When was the last time you can say we did that? Paolo- Oh man your right let’s eat. The men all order their food, as the waiter comes by and takes everybody’s individual orders. As they all wait for their food, Juan grabs a whine glass and makes a little toast to the whole table. Everyone notices and starts chanting the same words over and over. “TOAST…TOAST……TOAST” Juan smiles and then begins. Juan- Okay, well I’m not going to make this difficulty long bore you all to death, so here it goes. You people here at this table, mean more to me then you will ever know. A man’s life can be fully complete with a lady by his side, and a group of great friends. I went through a stage of depression back in jail, and I looked to you guys for guidance, and you were definitely there for guidance. The role you guys played was so significant. You were there for me to show me the routine when I first got there. You got me out of numerous fights, and probably saved my life on a few occasions. You were all like my big brothers, and I give you the most gratitude ever for it. As for you Juliet, well you were a different kind of inspiration. You were spiritual guidance, in a time where you couldn’t be there physically. You are probably the reason I am free right now. I wanted to be with you so badly that I would do anything to do it, and you were the reason I made the plans and escape to begin with. So, you see you guys are all my friends and inspiration. I hope we will stay friends for a long time, and hope we get in as little fights and arguments as humanly possible. I wish we stay friends, and I know everything will turn out fine. And I toast to that……………… Cesar- TOAST!!!!!! Paolo- Toast! Everyone puts their glasses high in the air, and touch one another. Everyone says toast, and with that the food is on it’s way. The scene fades, with the food dishes being put on the table, and everyone laughing and celebrating. Everything is just the way it should be.
