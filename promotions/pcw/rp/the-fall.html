The Fall

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/13 - 4/14 ******The Fall***** (The scene opens with Kid Krazie in his motel room sitting in the leather chair with his feet propped up on a stool. He is reading a book called: The Idiot’s Guide to Wrasslin. Krazie just got past the part on how to create an original captivating persona. He folds the corner of the page he just read and closes the book and sets it on the floor. He then gets up and walks over to the mirror and stands there looking at his reflection. He begins to make certain faces everywhere from super hero to super villain. He even tries one that makes his look like a crack fiend slash mentally retarded seven year old.) Tiffany: “What are you doing?” (Krazie turns around sharply and sees his girlfriend standing there laughing at his expense. He turns a confused look her way as she just stands there laughing.) Tiffany: “Do that face again.” (Krazie looks at her and gives her an angry glare causing her to stop laughing and shrug her shoulders.) Tiffany: “You reminded me of my little brother.” (Tiffany shrugs her shoulders again before she leaves Krazie to his face making. Krazie looks back at his reflection and does a couple of more faces before he throws his hands up in frustration. He turns and walks out of the room and across the hall into his second story bedroom. As he enters the room he sees Tiffany sitting on the ground looking through some old photo albums. Krazie smiles as he walks over to her and takes a seat on the edge of the bed looking over her shoulder at the pictures.) Tiffany: “These were some good times weren’t they babe?” Krazie: “Sure were. But then again it doesn’t mean that we won’t have some more good times.” Tiffany: “I saw your debut match last night.” Krazie: “What can I say?” (Tiffany shrugs and looks up and Krazie who just shakes his head in frustration. He looks into his girlfriend’s eyes and sees the beauty in those big brown eyes.) Krazie: “Looked like people had some business to take care of with The Multiple Man.” Tiffany: “…” Krazie: “And they had to take care of their business during my debut match.” Tiffany: “…” Krazie: “So be it. I have a match with Syko on Sunday and maybe then I’ll show the fans what I am really capable of.” Tiffany: “If you say so…” (Krazie puts his hand on her shoulder and she puts her hand over his. He pats her on the shoulder and stands. As he makes his way to the door he stops and turns back around towards her.) Krazie: “I’m going to the store. Do you need anything?” Tiffany: “Yeah, I need some pads…” (Krazie looks at her like she is crazy and then begins shaking his head side to side furiously. She begins to laugh as he puts his hand up and tells her to stop.) Krazie: “No way! You know how I feel when it comes to this stuff! It makes me feel weird. Walking to the checkout counter carrying a bag of pads with people just staring at me freaks me out.” Tiffany: “Fine! If you don’t want to that’s fine!” (Krazie turns and walks out the room but is stopped in the hall as he is suddenly sent on a guilt trip as he sees a mental image of Tiffany’s beautiful face taunted with sadness. He shakes his head and smiles as he walks down the stairs and out to the driveway where his supped 32’ Ford Coupe is sitting and waiting.) ********Twenty Minutes Later********* (Krazie is walking to the checkout counter with two baskets. He is pushing one basket in front of him with one hand and pulling another basket with his second hand behind him. He walks up to aisle nine in the Randall’s Flagship grocery store with a smile on his face. The checkout girl looks at the two baskets and then looks up at Krazie who is standing there smiling. She then looks from Krazie to the sacker standing ready to bag with a confused look on his face. The two, sacker and checkout girl, both look up at Krazie who takes out his Remarkable Card and hands it out the checkout girl.) Checkout Girl: “Is this… all… for you today?” (Krazie nods his head as the sacker takes his Remarkable Card and hands it back to him. She then begins to slide the products across the scanner as Krazie stands there watching the price rise.) Sacker: “Paper or plastic!?” (Krazie looks at the sacker and laughs.) Krazie: “Surprise me…” (The sacker nods his head with a confused look on his face as Krazie turns his attention back to the price screen. Many other shoppers nearby stop and look over at Krazie who is oblivious to the unwanted attention.) Checkout Girl: “Ok, that’d be three hundred and twenty-one dollars and forty-four cents please.” (Krazie pulls out his wallet and hands her one of his Platinum Credit Cards. She swipes the card and charges the money to Krazie as he now turns his attention to the young sacker who is switching between plastic and paper and even a combination of both!) Krazie: “Thank you very much.” (Krazie takes his card back from the checkout girl and then walks over and takes one of the grocery carts by the bar and begins pushing towards the sliding doors. The young checker right behind him with the second basket.) Checker: “Sir!?” (Krazie stops as he waits for the door to slide open giving the checker enough time to catch up to him.) Krazie: “Yeah?” Checker: “What do you need all these for?” (Krazie begins walking out towards the parking lot laughing at the question that was just asked. But more importantly he is laughing at the answer he is about to give. As the two walk towards the flaming 32’ Ford Coupe Krazie answers the question.) Krazie: “When you get a girlfriend or wife you’ll understand why I bought all these…” (Krazie looks down to the bags upon bags of Playtex Tampons and Pandora Pads. The young checker shrugs his shoulders and helps Krazie put the bags of woman accessories in the trunk and back seat of the ’32 Ford Coupe. As they finish Krazie hands him a five dollar bill and helps take one of the empty baskets over to the basket drop off.) Checker: “Thank you sir and I hope you enjoy your supply of woman accessories.” (Krazie smiles and chuckles as he waves to the departing kid. He turns around and walks back to his ride. But as he nears the 32’ Coupe he steps in a rather large puddle causing him to slip. He flies into the air and as the theory says ‘what goes up must come down’ he comes down hard!) *****KABLAM~!***** (Krazie hits the back of his head on the concrete parking lot followed by the rest of his body sandwiching him flat. He lays there unconscious as the young checker hears the sickening thud and turns around to see Krazie laying on his back unmoving.) Clerk: “Oh shit!” (The fourteen-year-old checker comes running with a look of shock and surprise on his face. As he comes to a halt near the motionless fallen Krazie he notices a small puddle of blood forming around his head vicinity.) Checker: “Oh shit!” (The checker kneels down shaking Krazie in an attempt of waking the fallen superstar.) Checker: “Oh shit! Come on mister, wake up!” (He begins to slap Krazie still in a vain attempt of waking the fallen Kid Krazie. As the checker continues to slap Krazie a crowd of on looking pedestrians begins to flock around the scene.) Checker: “Oh shit!” (The checker looks up and sees people watching him beat an unconscious man. He stops beating Krazie and stands up dusting off his apron and looking around as people begin to whisper and point.) Checker: “I was trying to wake this guy up…” (The people begin to whisper even more along with more fingers pointing his way. Out of nowhere the manager of the grocery store comes running along with some Paramedics. The Paramedics run to the side of Krazie as the manager runs over to the young abusive checker.) Manager: “What happened here Ronald?” Ronald: “I was walking from helping this guy with his groceries when I heard this sickening thud. When I turned back I see this guy laying on the ground not moving. So I run over and try to wake him up. I tried everything… Oh shit! I’m fired aren’t I?” (The Paramedics put an oxygen mask on him and wait a few minutes for a response. After a few seconds of the oxygen Krazie begins to stir. He looks up at the curious looking crowd.) Paramedic: “Sir? Do you know where you are?” Krazie: “Of course!” Paramedic: “Do you know your name?” Krazie: “Of course!” Paramedic: “Are you ok?” Krazie: “Of course!” (The Paramedics put their tools back in their tool kits and get to their feet as the crowd looks on anxiously. The Paramedics walk away but are stopped by the manager of the grocery store.) Manager: “Are you sure he’s ok? He had a pretty rough fall!” Paramedic: “Of course!” (The Paramedics leave as Krazie slowly climbs to his feet. The crowd begins to dissemble leaving Krazie, Ronald and the store manager in the parking lot. Krazie looks around scratching his head. He notices a red fluid type substance on his fingers. He puts it to his nose as he smells it. He smiles and then licks his fingers causing Ronald to freak out.) Ronald: “Oh shit! That was blood dude! Oh shit!” (Krazie smiles and begins to walk off away from his ’32 Ford Coupe humming a little tune.) Manager: (To Ronald) “I didn’t see anything if you didn’t ok?” Ronald: “See what?” (The manager pats Ronald on the back as the two turn and head back to the store. They stop a few times and see Krazie still walking further and further away from his car.) Manager & Ronald: “Oh shit!” (The scene fades as the two walk into the store.) ******To Be Continued*******
