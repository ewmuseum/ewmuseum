The 80's [2]

11 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11.01.03 Shivers and ripples upon the timeline have been known to occur thanks to the movies of Michael J. Fox and Christopher Lloyd, specifically Back To The Future; one through three. Oddly however, is that when you actually get the pair of men and women you call superstars, their hearing or thinking goes completely out of the window. Same goes for Dante Jackson whenever he happens to be in a tough situation. Dante: HELP ME! As the group continues on in the space time continuim, they appear to be a bit normal, their appearences haven't changed, nor have their minds. But their line of questioning towards where they're going happens to be that way. Dante stands, whimpering witha few tears dropping from his round face. Still tuck in a leather Kid Rock ensamble, he turns around as the group appears locked in a circular type of thing as you would be accustomed to see if you played Final Fantasy a lot. Dante: So, anybody got any ideas as into where we are? From the group of Jackson Rust, Jesse Ramey, Mani, Shin, Vanilla Skillz, Chet King, The Masked Mullet, Rent - A - Hero (with Shiznittlebam powers), Statuz Quo (with Ebonics powers), and a host of other people whose names I've forgotten stand inside of the moving circle as Shin speaks up. Shin: Jackson.... Dante: Yes, you with the headgear and the Motral Kombat psuedoms? Shin sighs. Shin: Urgh, I hate being reminded of that. Dante: Well you choose to be a ninja, TOUGH COOKIE! Can you please with all of your video game experience tell us where we are? Shin clears his throat, before Mani grabs him by the arm joint. Shin turns his head and nods towards the mute wrestler before Dante stamps his foot, urgently wanting the wrestler to come abide by his side at once. Makes you think Dante isn't like Waylon Smithers to Mr. Burns eh? Shin: It seems we are in some vortex of the space time continuium and we are riding this wave towards a certain point in time. Whether it be past or present, we will soon find out as we take our path through the light. In other words for mortals and imbeciles like yourself Jackson, we're travelling through time. Dante: Time? Like reliving the time I beat - Chet nudges Dante on the shoulder. Dante: Sorry, but I did beat. Chet nudges Dante on the shoulder a bit harder, causing him to step forward, before reseting his foot back to where it was. Dante: Or HOW TOTALLY TUBULAR TOMMY OR SKYLAR GREEN SCREWED US OUT OF THE CONTINENTAL TITLE?! Chet: Thank you, now I can go back to Georgia saying I didn't lose to a nig - Soon a pistol is placed at the head of Chet King as his eyes focus upon the weapon, its handler none other than the prince of Ebonics next to Bill Clinton, Statuz Quo. Quo, decked out in Blood colors, with a red bandana tied around his head in a Tupac fashion, tightens his grip on the gun, before Vanilla Skillz steps in-between the two of them and produces heat of his own. Vanilla Skillz: Guess we all gon be som' dead muthas up in dis piece. Quo tightens his grip on the weapon as Dante steps in, looking around at the triangle of meele and plethora of soon to be violence re-enacted, casuing a definite rift in the time continuium. He hops in, waving his arms around like a mad man before point towards a non-existent plane. Dante: LOOK, VANILLA ICE! Skillz & Quo: WHERE DAT CRACKA AT?! Jesse Ramey: Dude, chill ouuuuuuuuuuut! Vanilla Skillz: Yo, you be on dat rabbit weed huh? Jesse looks around dumbfounded. Jesse Ramey: Me? Vanilla Skillz: Naw, that biatch behind you. Jesse turns around, finding former RUSH World Heavyweight Champion, Chris Matthews leaning back upon the invisible wall with a Joe Cool smile on his face. The Canadian born superstar is rushed by Marvin Harris and Vanilla Skillz as the pounce upon him! Dante: GUYS! Vanilla Skillz (while punching Matthews in the face): Yea? Dante: If you kill him, you doom us all! Suddenly, Marvin and Skillz stop attacking Matthews, causing him to get up and then charge at the Resistance pair before the massive Mani Shigeru stands in front of him, waving his finger without much hesistation. Chris tries to attack once more, but is knocked back down to the ground. Shin smirks at this before beginning to talk under his breath. Soon, the portal ahead opens, each of the men starring into the obscure blackness of the warp as Dante begins to string his finger inside of the hole, trying to feel anything. Wafting aroun done time too many, his finger get SUCKED IN! Dante: JESUS, JEBUS, CONTOR! Chet King: Oh God, he's gone mad... Chet trails off as the others laugh at Dante, Rent A Hero smiling himself while holding his stomach. Rent A Hero: Looks like YOU couldn't wait to get lost. What are you, MINI ME?! Everyone looks At Rent A Hero and stares for a second. Rent A Hero: The character, not me you silly goozles, shiznittlebam on all of you. I mean seriou - HOLY SHIZNITTLEBAM! As the superhero blurts out one of his mere snappy catchphrases, the hole begins sucking in the travellers one by one as rent A Hero tries to utter a few words before being sucked up himself. The various screams and outlandish statements from Dante can still be heard as the scene opens up towards another point in our heroes destination. Swift trees sway in the wind as men and women skae down the boulevard in roller skates and various bikini wear. They continue their treatment, sitting back upon the sun, glazing and tanning to perfect brown. Looking upward at the sky, the large blackness of the previously seen hole cuts through the eerie blue sky as our group falls through, each one of them changed somewhat. Chet King: MY HAIR! Letting his palms tell the scene going on upstairs, a long Mullet spawns from what used to be the short black hair commonly seen with Chet as everyone seems to change, including Skylar Green with the amazing - METALLICA T-shirt! Skylar Green: Aw shit! I have a cool name, AND A COOL T-SHIRT! I ROCK! He runs off, away from the group as everyone tends to stare into where they are. The only person who hasn't changed somewhat happens to be Dante Jackson, still stuck in the clothes he was wearing earlier as he stares into a billboard, the picture of then First Lady Nancy Reagan plastered near a canceled out sign of cigarettes. 'JUST SAY NO!' Dante shivers in complete fear as he turns back to the group, each one of them accustomed to the style of dress as a wide smile is not even present on the hero of all heros beside Big Alan Steele. Rent A Hero: YEAH BOYYEEEEEEEEE! Renty breaks off into a tic motion, waving his arms around with a lengthy gold chain dangling from his neck. Dante turns back to what he was looking at before muttering to himself. Dante: Dear God, I'm now the FOURTH man to travel back in time!
