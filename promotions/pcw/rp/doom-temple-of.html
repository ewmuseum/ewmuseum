Doom, Temple of

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Straight Parody... An animal scream startles the group and causes nested birds to explode from their treetop perches. The guides, all stout men from the jungle, swallow hard and trade nervous glances. Futilely trying to wipe the grime and sweat from their faces, they turn towards the man in front, who hasn’t stopped hacking his way though the dense foliage, singular in purpose. The man is Big. Very Big. The tangling vines and thick underbrush don’t even seem to phase him in the least, as his machete swings left and right, cutting a large swath for the party to tread. A well-worn and wide-rimmed hat fits snuggly onto his head, matching nicely the leather the leather jacket stretched across his expansive back. A revolver on his right and a coiled whip slung on his left, the man looks the part. Only one thing seems out of place, causing the guides to occasionally chuckle in derision. The toga under the jacket, filthy with grime and plant juices. But his methods cannot be questioned as the seasoned local guides are shuddering in fear as the intrepid explorer hacks his way ever deeper into the sacred jungle temple of the Hevitos. The jungles seems endless, the progress seemingly fruitless, until suddenly, with a final machete hack, a shroud of foliage falls from a stone marker – a twisted, screaming face carved into an oblong rock. The mouth is stretched wide in its eternal lament, and at the intrusion of the sun, a flood of bats pour out of the exit, frantically flapping to find better refuge. The explorer ducks the leather-winged horde, but the men fall back, shrieking in superstitious horror upon seeing the carving. The ‘leader’ of the guides, a tribal member of the New Breed, Snow, steps forward, his teeth chattering. “S-S-S-senor! That is a marker of bad luck! An evil fate awaits all who cross this post! It means death for all!!” The adventurer straightens himself from his stoop, brushing leaves off his shoulders. Without even acknowledging his companion, he presses on, past the grim ward. Snow gives a befuddled shrug to the other men, who hastily make tracks in the opposite direction. Caught between fear and the possible rewards ahead, Snow grits his teeth and hustles down the path blazed forth by the man in the toga. It does not take long for the lost temple of the Hevitos to be revealed to the two remaining men. Overgrown with brush and jungle vegetation, the gaping maw of the entrance beckons in front of them, promising fortune and death all at once. The ornate carvings of the temple have been sloughed away by time, creating a malevolent feeling to the darkened temple. We rejoin our Big and Bold hero, Big Alan Steele, as his eyes narrow at the impressive structure before him. He thoughtfully rubs at his finely-trimmed beard, guessing that the top side structure of the temple is really just the tip of the iceberg, as underneath may lie a great labyrinth of tunnels and underground passages that could entrap a soul forever. Steele pauses to take a long slug from his canteen, Snow doing the same. Snow wipes his mouth and tugs at the sleeve of our Bold and Adventurous hero, who has stooped down and stuffs sand into a small satchel… “Senor… there is no turning back now. We have angered the Hevitos gods by our presence. All that is left is for us to gain…” Dramatic pause. “THE TITLE!” Steele warily looks sideways at his guide and nods in curt agreement. Tying off his bag of sand, he stands and strides forth powerfully into the darkness, Snow hurrying to keep pace. Inside, the light grows dimmer and dimmer. Big Al scans the walls and finds a torch embedded in the walls. Grabbing it and striking a flint, the torch flares to life, giving the damp walls an eerie, flickering glow. “S-S-S-Senor!!” Snow stammers out in a heavy whisper, “Your back!” Steele grunts questioningly, and cranes his head back to see. Making themselves at home on his leather are a couple of large, hairy spiders, poison dripping from glistening fangs. Big Al shrugs and un-straps his coiled whip, brushing the arachnid intruders off without incident. Steele then turns to his companion,. Frozen to the spot. With a gesture, Steele bids him turn around, and shakily Snow complies. An enormous amount of tarantulas crawl on his own person, and Steele briskly whisks them away with his whip. With Snow safe from the crawling predators, Steele leans in close. “Lord Snow, from here on out lie incomparable dangers! Should you wish to survive, it would be advisable to remain close by my side, as I have been favored by OTHER gods – the gods of Olympia! Any misgivings about your continuation should be heeded now, lest the chance not reveal itself later.” Snow wipes the cold sweat from his forehead, “Let’s go.” A smile tugs at the corner of Steele’s mouth, before he turns and plunges deeper into the depths of the temple. With occasional instructions from an ancient map, weathered by hundreds of years, the two make their way through the cavernous hallways. Up ahead, a shaft of light beams down from the broken ceiling. It angles across the passage as the two make their ways forward. Big Al stops, as Snow continues forward… until Steele stops him by grabbing his collar and jerking him backwards. Angry, Snow turns to confront Steele, who is already examining the walls, running his hands along the dripping rock. “What are we waiting for?! The title lies just…” “Do not enter into the light,” say Steele, stopping Snow abruptly. The guide begins to protest as his treatment as Big Al bends over and grabs a piece of wood and slowly extends it into the shaft of light. “Of all the ridiculous notions, we must hurry, for… AAAAHHHHHHHH!!!” The guide’s words are cut off by his own scream, as a row of jagged spears, with unlucky skeletons still impaled, rips from the walls and slices into the stream of light. Steele withdraws the stick and the javelins retract, leaving the men unharmed, but Snow noticeably shaken. He breathes deep and heavy as Steele has already started to edge his way around the almost unnatural shaft of light. The local New Breed guide follows suit, and catches up with our hero, Big Alan Steele. The walls begin to become more smooth, carved to a finer degree. Archways begin to appear and a definite architecture becomes apparent. Steele and guide continue on, until a deep pit blocks their path. Big Al frowns at the interruption, and tosses a nearby rock down the well to test for depth. He listens intently for a sound. And gets none. Shrugging, Steele scans the room. Huge roots of monstrous jungle trees have broken through the temple stones and now criss-cross the ceiling. Tugging his whip from his toga, he snaps the whip upwards. Whup-CRACK!! The end of the whips snags a roots and wraps itself around at the end. Steele pulls on it, testing its strength, and nods in satisfaction. Snow makes a grand gesture, waving Steele forward. “After you, Senor.” Big Al smiles grimly and leans back. “Hi-Keeba!” With more grace than a man his size should have, he swings across the pit and touches down on the other side. With a smug look, he pushes the whip back and it swings over to his guide, who doesn’t look pleased at the situation. But, to his credit, the man grabs the whips, holds on tight, and swings across. Looking quite impressed with himself, the guide scurries ahead to catch up with Steele, who stands just ahead. Snow comes up behind Steele, and can’t believe his eyes. The passageway opens up to a huge room, the temple proper. Huge, ornate carvings encircle the room. Pillars holding up an impressive ceiling have been quarried with single rocks. The floor is tiled with irregular and decorative stones, each with a differing face upon it. And in the center of the room, a beautiful dais, with steps leading up to a pedestal. Snow’s eyes grow wide and he licks his lips. Upon the pedestal, and glowing seemingly of its own accord, is their prize… the PCW World Title Belt! The gold beckons hauntingly, glinting and fully visible amidst the darkened altar. The guide begins to rush ahead, but Steele stops him, blocking his path with a powerful arm. Big Al’s companions scowls as Steele goes to a knee, grabbing a large wooden tree branch. He hesitates for a moment, looking across the room, before jabbing it into a stone. FFFFTT!! An innumerable amount of tiny darts explode from the walls, some sticking into the wood. Steele pulls one out, and examines the green, sticky fluid running down the dart. Snow chuckle nervously and steps back, “I shall wait for you here, senor.” Big Al allows a wan smile to cross his features before steeling himself before the task at hand. He takes a beep breath and begins to tip-toe across the tiled floor, making sure to avoid each and every stone imbedded in the floor. Snow watches in strained silence as Big Al hops and sidesteps his way through the room, finally leaping onto the dais. Light pours down from the ceiling, illuminating the golden belt and glints at Big Alan Steele. Steele’s eyes go wide, the object of his travels so close in his grasp. He does a quick check of the pedestal and examines the placement of the belt. He reaches into the folds of his toga and withdraws the small bag of sand. Eyes never leaving the prize, he holds the bag in an upturned palm, trying to judge the weight of the belt at hand in comparison with the sand. Sweat begins to bead on his forehead as he reaches over and slowly dips his hand into the bag, letting rivulets of sand trickle out in between his fingers. The guide gnaws nervously on his fingertips, watching Steele intently as he again judges the weight of the satchel. Steele poises his hands over the title, one with the sand and one waiting to grasp at the belt. Several tense moments pass as he readies himself… until finally snorting in derision and tossing the sack away. Standing tall, he swipes at the belt and quickly wraps it around his waist. Grinning widely, he turns around and poses, flexing for his New Breed companion, who half-smiles back. A grating stone-on-stone sound, as the center of the pedestal slowly descends into the dais. Steele eyes widen in terror as he slowly turns back around, watching the scene unfold. A deep rumbling shakes the temple, and large stones begin to drop from the ceiling. Shaken back to his senses Steele, is spurred to finally move, shrugging the leather jacket up over his head and dashing across the tiles. Poison-tipped needles sweep across the room as Steele sprints as fast as he can, hoping to avoid the falling rocks. Snow dances in place, panicking as the room begins to cave in on them. Steele roars and shoves him forward, down the hall as large stone slabs begin descending from the archways, threatening to entrap the two to grisly doom. Steele runs on, cognizant enough to dodge falling rubble. He overtakes Snow and empties his lungs… before screeching to a halt. He skids on his sandals, waving his arms frantically to gain his balance as he skirts the edge of the pit. Steele falls backwards and breathes a heavy sigh of relief before he looks skyward. The ceiling looks like it’s breathing, bulging downward and warping itself as boulders rain from the above, only the tree roots keeping part of the roof intact. Big Al rears back the whip and cracks it at the overhanging roots breaking through the ceiling. The tip reliably snakes around a root and passes the tug test. Snow emerges from the tunnel, and Steele graciously hands over the whip. Snow grasps the whip and swings across the chasm… and the root snaps! Fortunately, Snow’s forward momentum carries him across and he crashes into a heap at the other end of the pit. The slabs continue their slow, methodical trek down through the archways, and Steele frantically waves over to Snow. “Throw me the whip!” Snow sneers, looking down casually at the whip, “Throw me the title.” “What? By the gods, are you mad?! Have you gone bereft of your senses?! Throw me the whip!!” “Throw me the title!!” Big Al’s face contorts into a scowl, and he hastily unhooks the belt. Taking one last look at its golden surface, he slings the title across the chasm, the belt slamming into Snow’s chest. The New Breed guide bares his stained teeth in a greedy smile. Steele shakes a fist angrily at his companion. “Now!! Throw me the whip!!” “Adios, senor,” Snow chuckles, dropping the whip to the floor. Steele’s face turns a deep crimson as he watches Snow duck under the sliding stone barrier and dash off into the temple hallways. Big Al steps back, pushing up against the descending stone and pushing off, trying to get as much speed up as he can. He leaps from the edge of the pit and flies across, landing harshly with his upper body on the floor and lower body dangling into the abyss, the jarring impact loosening his hat and sliding it near the wall. The stones are still lowering as he starts to slide backwards, the dusty floor making him scrabble to maintain his hold. Grasping at a small plat root on the floor, he gets handhold and smiles briefly… before the root partially gives way and he falls backwards! Big Al manages to grasp at a larger root just before plummeting below and uses it to pull himself up onto floor. Running ahead, he goes into a baseball slide, tearing up his toga and just barely managing to get underneath the lowering slab. Steele feels the top of his bare head and reaches back under the slab, just barely managing to grab his hat and swipe it under the door before its contact against the stone floor rings through the passageway. He replaces his hat and turns to hustle… and turns right into face of Snow, frozen forever in a scream, barbed tipped spears piercing through his form, illuminated by the shaft of light. Steele blanches, but plucks the PCW Heavyweight Title from his rigored hands. Scooting around the dangerous light stream, he again goes into a full-fledged scamper, busting through cobwebs and dancing around the falling debris… until a huge unlatching sound ricochets through the temple. Steele slowly turns and looks overhead, unable to comprehend what he sees. A huge rolling stone of immense proportions shatters through it’s moorings and careens down tracks, heading straight for our hero, who thinks to himself ‘How did I miss those tracks before?’ Big Alan Steele pivots and explodes forward, pumping his arms furiously, the title slung across his left shoulder. The stone rips across the floors, smashing through various sculptures and intending to crush our intrepid explorer. Big Al’s heart threatens to leap out of his chest as he runs for dear life, heading forward as fast as his legs will carry him and diving out of the way of falling ceiling blocks. Finally, a light appears at the end of the tunnel and Steele kicks into another gear he didn’t have. Looking over his shoulder, he sees the stone gaining on him, a mere few feet away. Steele roars and dives forward. “HI-KEEBA~!” Outside, Big Al looks like he was shot through a cannon, propelled through the exit, covered in dusty cobwebs as the stone ball crashes into the more narrow exit, the boom of the impact shaking the ground and crumbling the temple to the earth. Steele tumbles upside-down into a tree, falling into a heap of foliage at the trunks base. Coughing up dust, he tries to clear his face of the grime and cobwebs, plucking debris from his eyes so he can see… hundreds upon hundreds of New Breed warriors, all decked out in traditional jungle garb and all aiming blow guns and poisoned spears at him. “Ah! Big Alan Steele! We meet again!” A dapper man in an Armani with a matching pith hat strolls up amidst the New Breed, an evil grin plastered on his smug face. “Nelson!” Big Al snarls, the word with the same emphasis one would put on ‘Paulie Shore Marathon.’ Tyler Nelson grins even more maliciously, stooping down to get into the face of the upended Steele. “And once again we see that there is nothing you posses that I cannot take away. The New Breeders could have warned you… if only you spoke to New Breeders!!” Nelson plucks the title from the begrudging Big Al, and turns, dramatically holding it up in front of the New Breed tribe, defenders of valuing a title. The tribesmen bow their heads and prostrate themselves before the glittering, golden title. “Yoinks!” Big Al leaps up and snatches the title, before bursting into the underbrush. The warriors look up at the seething Nelson expectantly. “Hakanda-Matusu! Hu-what-thi!” Nelson punctuates the line with quick draws across his throat from his thumb. The New Breed rise up in force, yelling out tribal war cries and flowing past the flustered Nelson, jumping into the jungle after Steele. Spears whip past Steele’s head as he screams through the foliage, trying to plow through the jungle and keep as much cover about him as possible as blow darts stick into his leather. Trusted manager, Sue Saksnussum, lies on the top wing of her bi-plane designed for water landings, a towel underneath her as she holds a mirrored plate underneath her face for maximal sun bathing. The plane bobs merrily in the river, anchored securely in place by two ropes on land. A strange sound piques her interest, as she looks across the river to the jungle. Big Alan Steele crests a small hill, dust flying off his body, the hint of gold on his shoulder. He bounds down the hill, just as the tribal warriors crest it, slinging all manner of deadly implements at our hero, who is screaming at the top of his lungs. “Start the engines! Start the engines!! By the gods, start the engines!!” Sue frowns at the interruption of her bathing routine and slides off the wing into the plane cockpit. The engine roars to life, the propellers sputtering into a motion, after a few well-placed smacks from Sue. Steele then emerges from the jungle, grabbing onto a hanging vine and swinging as far across the river to the creaking bi-plane. New Breeders line the river banks, firing their blowguns and chucking their spears as A weary Big Al manages to pull himself onto the wing, slicing at the restraints with his machete. The plane taxis down the river, the angry tribesmen shouting epithets at Sue and Big Al. Steele hauls himself into his seat… and leaps up! Staring up at him is a pint-sized clown, complete with a honking horn. He angrily squeezes the horn at Steele, who stares down at the midget in terror. “Great Caesar’s ghost!!! What is this Hades-spawned demon doing in my seat?!” The clown again squawks the horn in protest. Sue grins as she pulls a pair of flying goggles down over her head. “That there is Jo-Jums the clown. He asked if I could give him a ride. Funny, I never expected a clown out here…” “I loathe clowns!!” Steele screeches in horror, “Allow me to dispatch him at once!!” “Show a little backbone, ya wuss!” Sue laughs, as the plane skims the river and lifts off into the air. Big Al curses in disgust, “Hi-Keeba!!” We re-rejoin our Big and Bold hero, Big Alan Steele as he twitches and convulses, grunting the word ‘Hi-Keeba’ as he flops about on the sofa. The warm warming glow of the TV fills the room with its incandescence. Trusted manager Sue Saksnussum walks into the room and smiles warmly towards her nestling charge. Producing a blanket, she tucks our hero in, making him as comfortable as can be. She leans in close and brushes his hair to the side, bringing her lips close to his ears. Softly, she whispers gently into his ear. “If you don’t win tomorrow, I’ll kill you myself.” Walking back across the room, she snaps off the TV and briskly walks away… “I can’t understand why ‘Raiders of the Lost Ark’ would be on the SciFi channel, the idiots.” Steele hugs the covers tighter, clutching at his imaginary title and smacking his lips. “Hi-Keeba.”
