Bringing the bad guys down: A chat with AquaMan

2 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: June 2, 2002 My back was aching from the pain, but that was the last thing on my mind. After all, who is a hero without a bit of pain along the way? Just keep telling yourself that, Tom. That way when you wake up from this dream, maybe you won’t feel so bad. And then I saw the Continental title sitting on the kitchen counter top, and I knew that it was no dream. I just told myself that it was a dream because I wouldn’t believe that I had finally reached a goal. I had failed so many times recently that I thought I might have to hang it all up. Perhaps there was more to me than meet’s the eye. Look at that RPG3 trophy. You are damn near a God now. How can anyone stop you? How could anyone beat you? You’ve proven yourself time and time again. You are wrestling’s most prized possession… Tom Lexian: No! Stop it! I grabbed my head to make the pain stop and I tumbled backwards, tripping over my coffee table and landing on my back. There I lay, quiet as ever, yet my mind raced with each passing thought. How could I ever live up to myself? When you reach a goal, you have to make another one – a loftier one. Perhaps now you realize the dilemma. Ring! Ring! The doorbell was ringing. Shit, I’m not answering it. I’ll be damned if I answer it. No one’s going to see me in this condition. Not when everything seems like it’s going completely down the shitter. Perhaps I wouldn’t. I could hear the oozing sound of something coming from underneath the door, and as I craned my neck to see who was coming, I saw a man standing in front of me. He was dripping wet and had his arms crossed, apparently not very happy with me. Tom Lexian: Aquaman? That’s right, you heard me. Aquaman, the guy that could swim and talk with the fishes, yet could also turn himself into liquid and begin oozing around underneath cracks and crevices. Sort of like the one underneath my door. Aquaman: Hello there, Tom. It’s been awhile, hasn’t it? I got back to my feet, slowly at first, and sat on my couch slowly. I had to rub my head from the fall I took. The bump on my head felt sort of serious, but I was ready to just let that go. After all, I took three hundred and twenty bumps in a single night in the ring – what was one coffee table bruise? Tom Lexian: I think it’s been a few months. Last time I saw you, we were fighting that giant squid that was terrorizing San Francisco, were we not? Aquaman smiled and nodded his head. Aquaman: I remember that guy. He was a nasty one, especially. He had all of those arms and he could talk too. Sort of an odd fellow. Something about the squids in the world not getting any good publicity, but he was big enough to get it for them. Tom Lexian: So he decided to eat people. Aquaman: [shrugs] Well, he didn’t say good publicity. Touche. Yet as I sat there, remembering the battles we had with that squid, I had to wonder what he was doing here. No, not the squid…Aquaman. But I couldn’t ask him that. No, no way. Not when he was standing here in my living room. One hero to another, he had about four hundred more powers than me. What did I have? My amulet that gave me stronger than average strength and the ability to feel vampires? Aquaman: So, aren’t you going to ask me why I’m here? Tom Lexian: What? Did you all of a sudden get telepathic powers or what? Aquaman: No, I’m not Robert…or what’s his other name, Sun Shard? Tom Lexian: No, not yet. It’s Night Shard, and he can’t read minds…he’s telekinetic. A confused comes over Aquaman’s face. I guess all that water in the brain messes things up from time to time. Tom Lexian: That means he can move things with his mind. Aquaman: Gotcha. Well, with powers like that, how could he not be a superhero? Tom Lexian: I don’t always think he has his head on straight sometimes. He always wonders why he’s a hero, thinking about all of the money and fame he could have being a villain, and it makes me angry sometimes. Aquaman: Angry that he could be so selfish? Tom Lexian: I know where Thinh is coming from. He understands, like I do, that while being a hero is hard, it’s the unsaid rewards that we receive that is enough. Robert is still thinking like he needs to get something in order to be successful. Aquaman: You know, I used to be just like him. Tom Lexian: Psssh. Get the hell out of here. You? You’re Mr. Good Guy all the way around. What do you have to hide? He circled around for a moment and then sat at my kitchen table. For awhile, I wondered why he didn’t just sit down at the table, but then I realized that he was soaking wet. Well, thanks buddy. Just get the whole floor wet and my kitchen for that matter. Aquaman: There was a time when I would have joined that giant squid. All I wanted was some crusade to fight for. But it wasn’t right, you know? I learned a long time ago that being a hero is more special than being a villain. I think you know that. Tom Lexian: Of course. It’s in the Crusader Code. Aquaman: Look, I came over here because you have a problem. I pointed over at my sink. Tom Lexian: I know. I’ve got eight hundred dishes to wash and no dish washing soap. Aquaman: No, not that. I’m talking about the two heroes you’ve got to save. Rent-A-Hero and Duke Williams. I got up from the couch and spit on the floor in disgust. Aquaman: [looks at the spit] I’m not going to clean that up. I don’t care if I am made of water, I’m not cleaning that. Tom Lexian: [points his finger at Aquaman] I don’t have anything to say about those two losers. They turned their back on the fans. Aquaman: I know you must be upset. I would too. But every good guy has that temptation to turn the other way. We both know that. Tom Lexian: We have a choice. We can either take that temptation or we can resist and remember what we’re fighting for. I spoke defiantly, as if I was some messiah for all heroes in the world. I felt vindicated, like I was telling Aquaman how it was. Aquaman: Look at the best heroes in the world and tell me how they fared. Darth Vader? Need I remind you that he could have been one of the greatest heroes of all time? Tom Lexian: [shrugged] But he turned his back on the Jedi and became an evil Sithlord. He killed the other Jedi off. Aquaman: But he came back to the other side. And you have to show Duke and RAH that they need to come back as well. I shrugged off the suggestion as if he was asking me to cut off my arm because it was “the right thing to do.” Tom Lexian: You don’t seem to understand. Over at PCW, I fight alongside two other men. All we do is try to make things right over there, but all we get in return is one criticism after another. I can take it, but I’ll be damned if I’m going to reason to one of “them.” Aquaman: By “them,” do you mean the villains? Tom Lexian: [snorts] Yeah, them. They think they’re so above everyone else. Like they can step on your average, everyday joe and get away with it. It’s that arrogance that makes them the scum of the earth. Aquaman: But it’s even more arrogant to believe that you can’t help them. That’s what heroes do right? Help people. Tom Lexian: They can’t be helped. RAH was one of the most beloved figures in PCW, and in one fell swoop, he becomes someone else. All over a girl? Get out of town. Where are his priorities? Aquaman stepped forward towards me and put a hand on my shoulder. Aquaman: Then make him understand. Get in his head and bring back the hero that we all know is there. He can’t be too far gone. Your match on Turmoil could be what he needs to snap out of this trance that he’s in. It’s like he’s in a nightmare that he can’t get out of. Tom Lexian: A nightmare? Maybe it was his fantasy all along to join the bad guys. Aquaman: You have to try. Tom Lexian: [shakes his head] I don’t know. I just don’t know. Duke’s just another story altogether. I’m going to try to make amends with him on Turmoil, but we’ll see if he accepts it. I just hope he does. I hate to call him a villain, but… Aquaman: He might not be. Maybe he’s just being misunderstood. Tom Lexian: Yeah, but then again, maybe he is. And then what do we do? Aquaman: You do whatever you have to do. Remember the code… I took his hand and shook it. May Peace and Justice Prevail!
