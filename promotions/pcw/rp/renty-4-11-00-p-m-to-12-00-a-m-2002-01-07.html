Renty-4-11:00 p.m. to 12:00 a.m.

Title: Renty-4-11:00 p.m. to 12:00 a.m. Featuring: Rent-a-Hero Date: 1-7-2002 The Following Is Taking Place Between The Hours Of 11:00 p.m. to 12:00 a.m. ....This Is.... Renty-4 4... 3... 2...1... 11:01 ..::/::..Our story begins where we last left off....in a sad and depressing manner, where we find our hero...the spandex protector of all that is good within the world, Rent-A-Hero, beaten to a pulp and laying in a battered condition under heaps of fallen metal scarps of his Rent-Cave. The base of operations which he took so much pride in was destroyed....torn apart viciously in a battle with his evil twin, Rent-A-Villain, without mercy. Meanwhile, his assistant Rosaria found herself in serious trouble, having to deal with a mysterious strike force led by Lady Kintara who was sent to capture her and the children of Rent-A-Dad's orphanage...which they have successfully completed. The sounds around our hero begin to grow increasingly louder...starting with no sound at all, to annoyingly blaring as he tries to lift his head up. His face is covered in crimson lifeforce from his battle with Rent-A-Villain, the lower half of his body pinned down by the aforementioned scrap metal...::/::.. Rent-A-Hero:'Ros....Rosaria....the...the...the kids....my cave...wha...'Evil Voice:'It's all happening so fast isn't it? It feels like you've gotten EVERYTHING just snatched from your grasp without warning huh?' ..::/::..Our hero still can't find the strength in his body to lift his neck up...but he is shortly aided by a hand covered in a spandex black and green glove, our hero opening his blood covered eyes to find his own sinister image staring back at him...the image of Rent-A-Villain. His diabolical counterpart simply smiles, running his finger down the face of our beaten hero and examining the blood which now stains his glove with glee..::/::..Rent-A-Villain:'I told you I'd destroy all you cared about and loved didn't I? Why did you think our battle could end in the only way it couldn't....with you victorious. Look at yourself...beaten, bloodied, without aid. Your beloved children and Rosaria are in the hands of Lady Kintara...and your power is draining ever slowly the longer you are without your eigth blue dot...hero. Oh how the mighty have fallen. I could leave you hear to die...or...I can end your suffering right now and save you the pain of what we have planned next, brother. What shall it be?' ..::/::..With a long and quiet stare, the wreckage surrounding the both of them as random screams of the children can be heard from the upstairs area of the building, our hero looks into the eyes of virtually his worst enemy...himself. With the stare lasting for what seems to be forever, our hero answers Rent-A-Villain the best way he can...with a blood and saliva cocktail of spit right in his face. Our hero's drippage slowly rawls down the mask of Rent-A-Villain, making him sneer angrily before crashing hsi fist across the jaw of our hero..::/::..Rent-A-Villain:'HAVE IT YOUR WAY! At least my death for you would have been PAINLESS!' *Standing up in preperation to head upstairs* 'Goodbye my inferior counterpart....you were quite the challenge. Also, I've seen you defy the odds before....which is why I REALLY hope you somehow survive this....then maybe I'll get to finish you myself like I've always wanted. Here's hoping...'..::/::..The vile villai turns his back to the fallen Rent-A-Hero, digging into a black sack he has about his shoulder before making his exit. In due time, he unearths two silver orbs with sky blue 'eyes' upon them, both about the size of a basketball. With a smile, Rent-A-Villain flips a switch on them both as the two orbs start floating on their own with their lights illuminating as they rotate in the direction of our hero..::/::..Rent-A-Villain:'The beauty of mechanized murderers....they don't take any pity on you when you beg. Hahaha!!' ..::/::..Rent-A-Villain leaves, the two silver orbs floating slowly towards their target as their eyes glow even more brilliantly.....our hero face down on the ground and unaware of their subtle and sadistic approach..::/::.. Meanwhile.... 11:27 p.m. Rosaria:'He'll save us....Rent-A-Hero never lets us down.'..::/::..Those are the words of Rent-A-Hero's beautiful blonde childcare assistant, Rosaria, as she sits captively with her hands bound behind her back with handcuffs. Sitting across from her is the lovely, yet menacing Lady Kintara as the two appear to be riding in the back of van, moving at top speed as the world outside the doors seems to whiz by as they both occasionally glace through the windows. Kintara just stares at Rosaria....what for, unknown, but it creates an uneasy feeling for her as she asks..::/::.. Rosaria:'Where are the children? Why are you doing this? Answer me...ANSWER ME DAMN IT!!' ..::/::..Kintara silences Rosaria's sudden outburst by lunging forward, a sharp knife unsheathed and at the ready against Rosaria's dainty throat as she slows her breathing, Kintara in full control over her life at the moment as she indeffierently just stares at her hostage, finally opening her mouth to answer Rosaria..::/::.. Kintara:'The kids are in another van. They are safe, for now. If you wish to stay safe, you'd better learn some manners in a hurry little girl. As for why I am doing this? Well....it's not as if I personally know this Rent-A-Hero or you....I'm just following orders. So if we both just do what we're told, we'll both come out ahead...understand?'Rosaria:'......' Kintara:'Understand?' *Kintara further pushes the knife against Rosaria's throat.* Rosaria:'Yes...'..::/::..Kintara sits back down across from Rosaria, continuing her morbid stare at her hostage as she sheaths the knife. However, Rosaria patiently tampers with the lock of the handcuffs using a small hairclip, trying to be as inconspicuous as possible..::/::..Rosaria:'Why do I get the feeling your not as bad as I want to make you out to be?'Kintara:'Evil and good are just perceptions...keep that in mind. I'm only doing what I'm told.'Driver:'KINTARA, SOMETHING'S WRONG, WE'RE BEI...' !!!!~BOOM~!!!!..::/::..In what seems like a mere instant, the van flips over violently, rolling repeatedly on either side as the driver, Kintara, and Rosaria get tossed about within wildly. Rosaria is screaming for her very life as she slams into each wall of the van, unexpectedly however, Kintara takes ahold of Rosaria and holds her tight to her as she takes impact against each of the metal walls of the van, protecting Rosaria as it seems..::/::.. Meanwhile...11:58 p.m. Rent-A-Hero:'Ugh.....muuu...must stop Rent-A-Villan...mus....' ..::/::..Our hero looks up, spent for strength as he views the two silver orbs hovering above his head, their eyes fully illuminated as they both stare down upon him as our hero tries to pull himself from under the wreckage, to no avail as the blue lights of both orbs begin to glow ever more rapidly, magnifying in size as our hero continues to struggle...::/::... Orb:'PREPARE FOR TOTAL VIOLATION....I MEAN, EXTERMINATION!!! Damn Violator...' ..::/::..The huge blue light of both orbs is directed right towards our hero, unable to escape from his prison as his very death could soon be very near...::/::.. 5... 4...3... 2...1... 12:00... ---- View Rent-a-Hero's Biography
