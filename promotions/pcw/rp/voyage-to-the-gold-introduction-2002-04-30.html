Voyage to the Gold: Introduction

30 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: April 30, 2002 OOC Note: This is the beginning of an RP series that I have planned to last beyond the PPV. I hope to enthrall you all with this series and finally prove that I can be a serious force in PCW. The ideas are a little far-fetched and you have to understand that I use a lot of metaphors in this series. This first one is just an introduction to the rest, so it's not one of my typical promos. Anyways, I hope you enjoy the promos that I cut during this series and if you have any comments/questions/concerns, drop me an e-mail or post something on the OOC Board. Thanks! : ) “The power to become habituated to his surroundings is a marked characteristic of mankind.” -John Maynard Keynes Very few of us realize with conviction the intensely unusual, unstable, complicated, unreliable, temporary nature of the team. We assume some of the most peculiar and temporary of our late advantages as natural, permanent, and to be depended on, and we lay our plans accordingly to our opposite. On this sandy and false foundation we scheme for improvement in standing and dress the part, pursue our animosities and particular ambitions, and feel ourselves with enough margin in hand to foster, not assuage, civil conflict in the tag team division. Moved by insane delusion and reckless self-regard, the Heartbreakers overturned the foundations on which we all have come to know within the tag team division. A division once dominated by Politically Incorrect is no more. Politically Incorrect, although the champions, have yet to make their presence felt ever since capturing the precious items that The Heartbreakers have set out to steal from them. Hell, the tag team division is almost no more. Only four teams remain in a field that was once represented by about twelve. S & M, Cowabunga Connection, Politically Incorrect and The Heartbreakers. These teams are all that’s left of the broken division, mainly thanks to the current champions making a mockery of the title belts. Jack Valentine & Johnny Heart are trying their best to bring the tag team division back up to where it should be. Maybe with the help of new teams like S & M and the Controversial Messiahs, the tag team division will regain its stature amongst the fans, but until then, Jack and Johnny will continue giving a hundred and ten percent. The promos which follow this have been extracted from a the minds of Jack Valentine and Johnny Heart, and are intended for only a few to hear. Apparently, the promos to come in the future will entail the team’s quest for gold. Gold, championship, winning; these all seem to be things that each member of The Heartbreakers has had in their youth. However, they had parted since, losing that sense of winning, losing the sense of championship, and losing that sense of gold. Years passed. Then something recalled to the team and they rekindled their desire to have what was once in their youth. It was like the championship gold was calling their names. Like it was talking to them… “I have been hearing of you lately and all of your success within the federation. I know where life has brought you. You certainly selected your own road. But to me, left behind, it always looked as if you had struck out into a pathless desert. I have always regarded you as people that must be given up for lost. But you have turned up again; and though I may never see you, my memory welcomes you and I confess to you I should like to know the incidents on the road which has led you to where you are now, a top team in PCW,” the gold would say. And it was like The Heartbreakers would speak back with the gold, but only at heart: “I believe you are the only one now alive who remembers us as a child. We have heard of you from time to time, and we have almost reunited on several occasions in PCW, but I wonder what sort of person you are now. If we once start to tell you we would want you to feel that you have been there yourself. We may overtax your patience with the story of our life so different from yours, not only in all the facts but altogether in spirit. You may not understand. You may even be shocked. I say all this to myself; but I know I shall succumb! We have a distinct recollection that in the old days, and we want to go back. The only thing that is stopping us from going back to those days are the other three teams in PCW, mainly Politically Incorrect.'' What kind of thoughts were going through the minds of Jack and Johnny? Did they really believe that gold was actually speaking to them?!? And did they really think that they could speak back to the gold like it was some kind of lost childhood memory?!? This new obsession with gold, championship and winning may be a good or bad thing. The good would be that they would be able to focus purely on their match at the pay-per-view. The bad would be that they would lose motivation in all of their other endeavors in PCW. They would lose thought of one of them becoming an extreme champion on Turmoil and they would lose thought of all other tag team matches that they may compete in, such as a big match against Syko and Miyagawa on Sunday Sabotage. Still, they succumbed to the thought of becoming champions once again. They begin their first serious quest for gold; a quest that took almost twelve months to develop in this federation. In the form in which gold is presented here it has been pruned of all allusions to their common past, of all asides, disquisitions, and explanations addressed directly to the friend of theirchildhood. And even as it is the whole quest is of considerable length. It seems that they had not only a memory but that they also knew how to remember. Jack was a former bodybuilding and modeling champion and Johnny was a leader in training other superstars. They were both champions and held “gold” in their previous fields, and the thought of them becoming champions again enlightens their PINK! Minds. Will they ever succeed in their quest? Opinions differ, but one thing is for sure…all of their antics aside, they will be trying their best. This, their first great adventure, one would call this quest for the gold, begins in the ring. It ends there, too. Yet it might have happened anywhere. This does not mean that the people concerned could have come together in pure space, it had to take place in PCW, the one organization that brought the two together. The locality had a definite importance. It is perhaps the last instance of team's adventure for a crown that history will have to record with the usual grave moral disapproval tinged by a shamefaced regret for the team. Future’s history may not look at The Heartbreakers as of now as a great team, but in due time they will realize the vast domination that they control over the others. Although they wear pink tights and carry themselves in a very flamboyant manner, their wrestling ability backs up everything that they say and for that they will go down in history as greatness. However, history has nothing to do with this quest. Neither is the moral justification or condemnation of conduct aimed at here. If anything it is perhaps a little sympathy that the wrestlers expect for their buried youth, as he lives it over again at the end of their insignificant course in this federation. Strange people---yet perhaps not so very different from everyone else Their quest begins Tuesday night, as there first test as a dominate tag team is working together in a match that pits them against each other. If they can survive that grueling task, they will go on Sunday to face a tag team that is very new to them, very new to their style…Syko and Miyagawa. The Heartbreakers have yet to be pinned, and are slowly proving just why they deserve a title shot at Supremacy. It’s just this very quest that they are about to embark on that will be the true test of if they can endure through this grueling place known as P…C…W.
