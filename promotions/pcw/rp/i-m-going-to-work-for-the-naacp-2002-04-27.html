I'm Going To Work For The NAACP!

27 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 04/27/02 OOC-I suggest you download 'Cop Killer' by Ice T after reading this. It allows you to see where some people are coming from. It was literally just a few minutes after my debut match in which I teamed with Duke Williams to take on Mark Makonie and Xavier Kannon that I got the phone call. I had just gotten out of the shower and I was getting dressed. I was supposed to head back to my hotel then immediately head towards Cincinnati, for Tuesday Turmoil. My head was hurting like a bitch but I was trying to fight the pain. My entire neck burned with pain from the X Bomb that Xavier Kannon gave me. I think I landed wrongly. It was my debut match, and I lost… But it’s nothing to be ashamed of. I think I proved myself enough to the fans, and more importantly, to the Malice Crew. I was looking forward to getting another chance to wrestle members of the Ultimate Authority, even though I felt that Duke and I would be totally outnumbered, it didn’t matter much to me. Any time I can hurt a Neo-Nazi, or just a white person in general, I giggle with glee. Anyways, there I was, I had put on some baggy khaki shorts that came down to my shins, and I had put on my large khaki colored Phat Farm t-shirt. I sprayed Adidas cologne on me just in case I met a fine honey on my way to Ohio. I was pulling on my socks when my cellular phone rang. I immediately checked the small Nokia caller ID placement, it stated “No Caller ID.” I chuckled to myself as I answered the phone. “Hello?” “Yo, Jordan, it’s me, Andrew,” the voice boomed back at me. I couldn’t help but smile at the fact that even though he had a huge match coming up tonight, my older brother and current OSW superstar Drew Hostyle would call me. “Hey man, what’s up?” I asked Drew while putting on my socks. “Well, some crackas just called me asking for you.” “Really? What did they want?” I asked incredulously. “They claim to be from the NAACP, they left a number for you to call them at.” “Alrighty, what’s the number?” I said while going into my large FUBU jacket pocket. “The number is [detail withheld].” Drew replied. “Okay, got it, this area code, isn’t it from a Texas city?” “Yeah foo’, it’s in Houston. Just chillax and give them a shout.” “Okay Drew, thanks for calling me man.” “No probs nigga, I’ll talk to you after my Pay-Per-View G.” “Okay.” “Holla.” And that was that. He hung up. I looked around the small and grimy locker room and I realized that I should call the NAACP offices in Houston immediately. The locker room literally made me sick to my stomach, so I was trying to get out of it as quickly as I could. Water still dripped from my head as I got all my bags together. The shower was refreshing and made my stay in Indiana just a bit more enjoyable. I really didn’t like the city; it wasn’t my type of city… Too many white people. It was like everywhere I turned some guy was asking me if I was lost or something. Like they couldn’t believe the fact that a black man was in the city of Indiana. I opened the locker room door and peered out, the coast was clear. I was afraid of being pestered by that annoying little Pimply Faced Intern Kid. The smile on my face widened as I thought about what had happened earlier in the night, I had been defeated by perhaps one of the best wrestlers in the PCW, Xavier Kannon, sure it hurt to lose my first match, but I think it’d hurt even more if I hadn’t put in all the effort I did into the match. It was closer than I thought it would be. Honestly though, I don’t know how much longer I stand being around so many white people. I turned the corner and begin to walk to my car. I turned on my cell phone and dialed the number Drew gave me from memory. RING, RING! The phone rang loudly in my ear. I pulled the phone back a bit as it rang twice. Somebody picked up the phone. “Hello, NAACP headquarters, how may I help you?” Came the voice of what sounded like a beautiful woman. I smiled. “Hello, I’m Jordan X Shabazz, current PCW wrestler, my brother called me saying that…” I was trying to say before she cut me off. “Yes! Mr. Copeland, we want to give you a job here,” came the reply. I was almost giddy, I could get a nice and easy job where I could work through my computer, sending out potential lawsuits, and accusing people of racism! I LOVE getting white people in trouble for no reason. In fact, I would say it turns me on… “Really? That’s great. What kind of position is it?” I asked with excitement in my voice. “Well, we’re going to hold a press conference Monday morning in Dallas, Texas. We want you to make a speech there, you are going to become our official spokesperson.” I didn’t know what to say. They wanted me to not go directly to Cincinnati; instead they wanted me to come straight to Dallas? I couldn’t believe this, it was… Totally out of the ordinary. I wonder how much a job like this would pay anyways… “Okay,” I began while entering my rented Jeep and fumbling around my pockets for the keys. “How much does a job like this pay?” “Well, we’re thinking of $40,000 a year for starters. But for every lawsuit won, there’s a bonus of $25, 000.” She replied. I started the car and began driving to a freeway that would somehow get me to Texas. Shoot, I know the job wasn’t exactly mine yet, but I was willing to do everything in my power to get it. “That sounds excellent,” I said while almost killing myself by swerving the car uncontrollably. “The press conference will be held at the Dallas NAACP headquarters. We’ll wire the address to you through your two-way pager,” the woman said to me. “Okay. Have a good night.” I said. CLICK! End of phone call. I drove off the interstate and entered the small town of Nile, Michigan. I would probably have to take a greyhound bus or something, there’s no way I could get from Nile to Dallas by car. I turned on the radio as I entered a residential area. It was nice and quiet, the perfect neighborhood for… The Klan. That’s right, the Klu Klux Klan. I was driving rather slowly now, checking out the houses just in case my new one in Albany didn’t work out. Then, I saw it. It stood about twenty feet tall, and it was on fire… A burning cross. As “What If I Was White” appropriately played on my radio, I was shocked. I swear I thought the KKK was long gone by now, I thought all the had were confused young bald headed bastards who lived at home with their parents. For some reason, I stopped. I went into my back seat and pulled out the .9 that Drew had given me for my birthday. I didn’t know what I was going to do, and I didn’t know why I got the gun. I walked out as the what seemed like… 50 men in their white and black suits, complete with masks, looked at me. “Look, it’s a n****** here to still our cars and rob our houses!” A particularly fat man in a completely black suit said with his arms still in the form of a cross. The all were staring at me, and I didn’t know what to do. I decided I wanted to scare them. Make them KNOW who runs the F^cking country known as America. I fired two warning shots into the air. Nothing happened. They just looked, and smiled. Some of them bolted into the two-story house whose lawn they were using for this sick and twisted ritual. The remaining KKKites looked at me. I didn’t know what to do. I fired three more shots into the air. “Y’all white f*gs can suck my big, black, coc-…” I tried to scream before I realized that cops had surrounded the area. I turned around to see three cops behind two different patrol vehicles. “DROP THE WEAPON!” One of the officers shouted at me. I had no choice; I slowly placed my gun on the ground as the officers approached me slowly. “PUT YOUR HANDS BEHIND YOUR HEAD!” the second officer screamed at me. I obliged and put my hands behind my head. I smiled at the KKK members and mouthed the words “I’ll get your bitch asses.” The few Klansmen who went inside to get their shotguns reappeared. They ran towards me with their shotguns and pointed them right in my face. The two officers at the car looked at them, and to my surprise…did nothing. “YO, HOLD UP DOG, OFFICER, GET THIS CAT OUTTA MY FACE!” I screamed with both of my hands now cuffed. The police officers literally turned the other way as the Klansmen both hit me in my face with the butt of their shotguns. I fell to the ground. The cops still didn’t do anything as the three Klansmen stomped on my body. I tried to fight back, but it was kind of hard with both hands shackled behind my back. Finally, after what seemed like an eternity, the officers called my attackers off. “Okay boys, that’s enough of that. I think this dirty little perpetrator learned his lesson,” one of the officers said jovially to the Klansmen. The cross-had stopped burning. The other Klansmen watched on, drinking beers and cracking jokes about me. “How do you get a n***** to commit suicide?” I heard one of them scream. “HOW?” The rest of the Klansmen screamed back. “YA THROW A BUCKET OF CHICKEN INTO THE HIGHWAY!” the man screamed again. The Klansmen roared with laughter as the cops lifted me to my feet. There was three of them. All white, and all dressed in identical “Michigan State Police” clothing. Two of them were of medium build. I couldn’t see their hair, but I saw their stone cold blue eyes staring me down. There was a fat ass officer who had brown eyes, he seemed to be enjoying treating me like this the most. They looked at me up and down, and they checked my pockets. They took my wallet out, and they checked the insides of it. They found the money from the payoff I had gotten earlier in the night for my match at Fusion. “What’s this here?” the fat officer asked me. “It’s my payoff from…” I tried to spit out before being interrupted. “Ya hear that Bill? He’s a drug dealer! He just got a payoff for selling four pounds of cocaine to another drug dealer,” the fat ass screamed to his partners standing by their cars. “I’m afraid I’ll have to confiscate this,” the officer took all the money out of my wallet, $300, and he put it in his pocket. “Now, I won’t press charges, but you make sure to stay out of trouble kid, we’re watching.” The officer un cuffed me and he let me drop to the floor. I was physically spent, I had just wrestled a match with the Continental Champion of the PCW and they had just watched me be assaulted by some Klansmen. ***************************************************** When I awoke it was almost morning, I was still on the side of the road. My head throbbed as if I had been drinking all night long. Blood caked my shirt and my pants. The cut had stopped thankfully, or else I probably would have died. A band-aid was on my forehead, probably placed there by the Police to avoid suspicion of a murder… Or at least an attempted one. I stood up slowly and fumbled in my pocket for my keys. I found them, and I got into my car. I didn’t call the police to report the assault because I knew they wouldn’t believe me. They’d accuse me of disturbing the Peace and mischief; they’d put my in jail for ten years probably. I put the keys in the ignition and immediately drove myself to a small hotel on the outskirts of Detroit. It’s where I’d spend the night before heading to Dallas. I quickly used my credit card to pay for the room and I fell asleep. This was the longest night of my life…And even though I was Rodney Kinged…. It was the best; fore I had debuted in the PCW, and fulfilled my dream…
