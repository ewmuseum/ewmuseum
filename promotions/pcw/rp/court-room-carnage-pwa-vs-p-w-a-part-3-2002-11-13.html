Court Room Carnage (PWA vs. P.W.A part 3)

13 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 13 - 11 - 02 Prologue: Leaking like a canister of ooze is the information regarding the legal struggle between Primetime Wrestling Academy (PWA) and recent graduates Pranks With Attitude (P.W.A) over the use of these three initials. Formally the Academy have filed a law suit on the grounds of copyright infringement and wish for the current Tag Team Champions to abolish any use of this abbreviation but luckily the collective of Gaz Green and Nerezza have found a flaw in this ultimatum. According to the documentation received, the Academy have actually stated that the name 'Pranks With Attitude' should be changed and not the abbreviation, obviously a mistake by the prosecution. However, the devious duo has planned an offensive of their own as they counter-sue the Academy on the grounds of free publicity and advertising. Providing they can conjure up the necessary information and prove that the Academy have indeed benefited from the use of the initials P.W.A by attracting an influx of applications then the team maybe able to play a swerve ball of their own and reverse this ultimatum in their favour. The dim backdrop that opens the next caper involving the dynamic duo Gaz Green and Nascosta Ireul Nerezza, collectively known as Pranks With Attitude appears as the Court House confined in the heart of Las Vegas Nevada. In attendance is the behemoth Nerezza, clad in a crimson red funeral suit and looking rather formal he is accompanied by the recently acquired Natalia Miyagawa who paces back and forth in tight fitting trousers and a matching black jacket that partially hides her blouse, hidden beneath. 'Where the hell is he?' hissed the satanic goliath. Natalia merely shrugged her shoulders, rolling her eyes in stereotypical lady like fashion. 'He'll be here-....' retorted the picture of Russian beauty Natalia Miyagawa. Suddenly the sound of somebody clicking their fingers rhythmically filtered over the buzzing murmur of activity. Growing in volume, the former Asylum members turned in unison to bare witness to the jiving Jester as he made his entrance with a few fancy steps and his interpretation of Dead Or Alive's 'You Spin Me Round (Like A Record)' looking every bit the personification of confidence. '.... Watch out here I come. You spin me right round baby, right round like a record baby-....' sung the jovial Jester to the onward glares of the public, when suddenly his performance stopped with a firm thud as Nerezza's open palm crashed against the back of his partners head. 'ARGH~!' 'Pay attention - This court case is important!' injected the Angel Of Fear, gazing upon Gaz Green sternly. 'Important? Me becoming Cruiserweight Champion is important but you don't hear me boasting about how I'm going to offer Eddie Mandrake the same fate as his employer Xavier Kannon-....' replied the Emerald Menace before an untimely interruption on the part of Natalia Miyagawa. 'Which is?!?' 'Me delivering the 'Last Laugh' and if his manager Spider wishes to become a factor then rest assured I shall clip his legs, come hell or high water I shall walk away from Fusion as the new Cruiserweight Champion!!!' said Gaz 'The Jester' Green triumphantly, holding his victory pose aloft, which spurned a few negative reactions in due process. 'WEIRDO!!!' 'WHAT A LOSER!!!' Soaking in the response without any appropriate counter-strike, the triple threat of Gaz Green, Nerezza and Natalia Miyagawa were called forth by the man chosen to represent them on this issue, Mr. Johnny Cockran. The renowned lawyer, attired for such an occasion guided the team to the specified Court Room, passing two large oak doors they stood in awe at the congregation gathered for such an event. Pressing on they found the vacant seating arrangement and remained erect for the arrival of the Judge. Stepping from the shadows an officer's presence alone put an end to the constant whisper and murmur. Approaching the raised pedestal, the officer in question announced the arrival of the Judge, a similar entrance accustomed to the PCW collective, something seen by both Gaz and Nerezza countless times. 'Please stand for the honourable Mills Lane!' Immediately lifting their body masses the congregation stood allowing the cape clad individual to make his imminent entrance. Entering like the Angel Of Death, the Judge swept to his platform and set the ground rules for this hearing. 'Right - I want a nice clean hearing with as minimum objections as possible so, LET'S GET IT ON!!!' First to step up to the plate was a scrawny gentleman, with round Harry Potter-esque glasses and a clothes designer’s nightmare in his chosen attire. Clad in a brown jumper with leather elbow pads, the Academy's representative took centre stage and put across the prosecution. 'Your honour - My clients would like the defendants, in this case the collective known as Pranks With Attitude to abolish the name on the grounds of copyright infringement. We would like this to take immediate effect and any further proceeds gathered from the remaining merchandise baring this name will be put into a trust for the development of further talents!' 'OBJECTION!' roared the Lord Of Laughter. 'Yes, Mr. Green-....' acknowledged the Judge, allowing the Jester his opportunity to retort the previous statement. 'Your Judge'ness! I mean how can you listen to this, this, this man with his mere Harvard degree. Quite frankly I have doubts over his degree, would anyone with a mirror at home and certainly an education wear such articles, it's simply inhuman-....' said Gaz Green completely side tracked, his team clawing at him, trying desperately to end this charade. 'Objection your honour, my clothes aren't the issue here!' bellowed the tacky looking Las Vegas native, tormented by the snickers of the congregation. 'Sustained - Please Mr. Green continue!' injected the Judge, Mr. Mills Lane. 'In other words Junior-... 'SIT DOWN'-... Anyway, if you could cast your eyes over this document you will see that student applications have risen since the graduation of Pranks With Attitude and that the Academy didn't drag this topic up for a number of months due to this influx. Basically we were used as free publicity and advertising, now that this business so to speak has dried up the Academy have decide to cash in!' The Judge browsed over the document, making a mental note of the graphical information, which clearly stated that there had indeed been a surge in applications just after the departure of the Trickster Team, P.W.A. 'Hmm-... It appears as though we are at a stalemate. Granted you did break a legitimate copyright yet according to these figures Primetime Wrestling Academy did benefit from your exit hence we are at log aheads!' said the Judge, scratching his head dumbfounded. The Courtroom silenced as the Judge pondered, Johnny Cockran's hand swaying eagerly, obviously the lawyer had a trump card still to play. Openly the African-American waved but Mills Lane simply brushed off his efforts as he tried to find the best resolve in this issue, a resolve to suit both parties. 'According to this you have asked for Pranks With Attitude to be scampered and not the initials P.W.A, which are the copyright infringement so I propose we place a temporary restraining order on the name and the initials will remain accessible!' 'WHAT?!?' cried the Harvard graduate. 'Mr. Bigtime, it's quite simple, the initials shall remain due to your incompetence but the team P.W.A will now have to rename themselves temporarily until they can finalise a copyright on the name 'Pranks With Attitude'. This will take immediate effect from the dated paperwork and then once everything has been concluded over the copyright with regards to Pranks With Attitude then they may resume-...' informed the cloaked Judge, his decision approved by that of Gaz Green and Nerezza, however the Academy representative was far from happy with the verdict. 'THIS IS LUDICROUS~!' wailed the lawyer, Mr. Bigtime. With a subtle nod from the Judge as he ushered into the back, his officer at hand leapt forward spearing the scrawny carcass of the Academy representative who put up a mediocre resistance. With a howling flock, all hysterical at the events unfolding, the PCW employee's of Gaz Green, Nerezza and Natalia Miyagawa made their escape under a torrent of flashing camera's as the media flooded the scene. 'Arr-... Man, I never got to do the damn Chewbacca Defence!' groaned Johnny Cockran, the unsung hero who unearthed the material used by that of Gaz 'The Jester' Green. As the scene erupted into chaos and our party of three vanished into the density of the crowd, the screen faded to black, drawing a close from yet another segment involving the Conniving Collective, Pran-... P.W.A (To be announced).
