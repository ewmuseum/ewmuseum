The Wizard...(Wizard of Gauze part 4 of 4...Next Up, The Crusader Chronicles...)

3 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8-3-2002 (When last we left our hero, Venneman had ridden across the land of Gauze, following the yellow brick road under the unusual guidance of Lyco the Joblin. Lyco waits for revenge as Venneman ate the creature's only true love, Yarkstar, a duck. The shot follows Pendragon as he boots Xerxes through the door of the huge tower. Once inside, the shot pans to the door which suddenly disappears. Pendragon shudders a little as the door is now an opaque glass wall. He reaches into his saddle bag and pulls out a torch that he lights with his flint and steel. Perhaps we oughtta call that a Swiss Army Saddle, as much useful stuff as it holds. Anyhow...Pendragon dismounts and shines the torch along the walls of a cavernous room that he is now in. He is surprised to find the walls covered with different illustrations of him...In some, he slays what can only be imaginary beasts...In others, he does battle in the ring with other men. He smiles at the scenes depicting his greatness, and he returns to Xerxes...) Venneman: Now boy...I gotta leave you here. Cannot risk you on the stairs I am certain to find. If Lyco comes back, you know how to take care of yourself...Stay calm, for I shall return shortly. (Xerxes nickers a tad uneasily, but he looks as if he understands. Venneman pats him on the nose and takes off after Lyco who has disappeared into the dimness of the vast room. He walks cautiously, Excalibur in his right hand, the torch in his left, and his chain mail shirt glitters in the firelight, as does his championship belt. At last he reaches a set of stairs where waits Lyco, with a stern look set upon his face...) Lyco: I warn you once more. When you finish here, you are mine. Nobody gets away with what you did, and you shall pay. Lucky for you I fear the Master, else you would already have been dinner for me. (Pendragon's eyes flame with the fires of what can only be insanity, and he replies harshly...) Venneman: You should worry about me. Evidently the 'Master' admires me, maybe even fears me. Why else would he wish my presence upon himself? Perhaps he is afraid that I am here to assert my authority over this realm as well...And that is not a bad idea. This place has some definite appeal...And the food is pretty good too. (Lyco starts to reply, but turns abruptly and races up the stairs. Venneman, more cautious than ever, follows, muscles tensed, as he waits for creatures to attack from the darkness. He climbs up for what seems like an eternity before he mounts the top stair and is met with a sight he has longed for, yet never thought he would see. Across the room sits a dragon. Smoke billows from the beast's nostrils as Pendragon looks on in wonder. Lyco stands behind the dragon, on another set of stairs, but Pendragon stands mesmerized...) Lyco: Ok, hero...Let's see if you have what it takes to get to the master. Porthos, KILLLLLLLLLLLLL!!!!! (Porthos, the dragon, climbs to his feet and lumbers over to Pendragon. He rears back to blow a jet of fire, but Pendragon stands his ground and holds his sword before him to ward of the flames. Fire bursts from Porthos' mouth but is absorbed by the ancient blade. The dragon looks confused as he puts his face closer to the sword. He looks at the inscription, 'Toward Eternity' and sees the Pendragon sigil. A startled look comes across the dragon's visage, if dragons can become startled, and the dragon places its head at the feet of Pendragon. Pendragon looks down at the creature and then at his sword. He shakes his head as he sheathes the weapon, and he kneels down to stroke the immense beast's head. Soon, the dragon is snoring and Pendragon looks up at Lycos and smiles. Lycos stomps his foot and runs up the stairs. Pendragon bellows a short laugh, draws his sword once more, and follows. As he reaches the top of the stairs, an axe is swung at him. He ducks and rolls to the center of the room. He looks around and finds himself surrounded by six figures in full suits of armor. Three carry immense battle axes, three carry heavy broadswords. Pendragon smiles at the figures, and where eyes should be a red light can be seen. Pendragon stands the torch on the floor and assumes the ready position, knees bent and the sword held overhead with both hands. An axe wielder rushes him and swings madly. Venneman spins to the left and slashes backward with his blade. The figure stops and looks down as his body splits in two. He disintegrates when he hits the floor. Pendragon spins the mystical blade in the air before coming to rest in the same position. This time, an axe wielder rushes him from one side, and a swordsman rushes him from the other. Both swing at the same time, one high and one low. Pendragon dives between the blades and instantly rolls to his feet. He lops off the axe wielder's legs and the figure turns to dust. He then charges toward the swordsman who catches his thrust with a block from his broadsword. Pendragon slices and hacks, and soon pieces are dropping from the dark figure. With a big swing, he lops off the head and once more a figure 'bites the dust'. Three down, three to go. They look at one another, but before they can move, Pendragon charges them, his blade a circling blur. Once in their midst, he ends it quickly as all three figures turn to dust from his 'helicopter' blade. Venneman looks around, and when he is satisfied he has diffused the threat, he picks up the torch and walks up yet another flight of stairs. At the top of this he pauses and stares, dumbfounded at the site. The room is full of ducks, and they are pissed. The rush Pendragon who begins slashing in every direction. Soon, the room is a mess of blood and feathers. Venneman chuckles at this latest trap, if you want to call it that. He smiles at Lyco who stands on the next set of stairs, fuming.) Venneman: I have come here prepared for anything. After the way you treated Psycho, I could not trust you. I knew that you were bringing me to what you believed my doom, but I will prove, as I always prove, that strength and honor shall overcome even the gravest of odds. Keep going, Lyco...I am not stopping. 'I didn't know what to think as Lyco ran up another flight of stairs. Who was the master? Would I recognize him? Would he be merely another incarnation of Syko from my realm? I had to follow. Curiosity had the better of me...' (Pendragon charges up the stairs behind Lyco, and once at the top, he looks around. Nothing. This room is empty. Except for one thing. He walks over to the right hand wall and holds the torch next to it. On the wall is a large switch labeled 'Lights'. Pendragon scratches his head as he throws the switch. Instantly, overhead lights come on and Pendragon smiles. He sees a set of stairs in front at the end of the room, and he strides towards them, tossing his torch onto the smooth floor. He walks confidently up, Excalibur in his right hand, and a smirk upon his face. At the top of the stairs waits Lyco. He sits on the ground with his arms in front of his chest.) Lyco: Behind this door resides the master. He has been waiting on you, and this is as far as I go. But forget not the fate that awaits you when he finishes... Venneman: Don't you worry. You'll get your shot. If you are anything like Syko, you'll get endless shots. (Pendragon pushes the door open and disappears. The next shot shows him standing in a vast room with enormously high ceilings. A light pulses in the center of the room, and from this light comes a disembodied voice. The voice is strong and commanding, and Pendragon looks struck as he listens...) Voice: I have been waiting. The Land has needed a true hero for some time. You fit the mold nicely, Crusader... 'I had not worn that moniker in some time. Why was this being calling me out on my past? Sure, I used to be a Crusader. I fought for what needed fighting, and lived the life of honor. Too much disrespect on that route, though. The commoners need a ruler with a bit of an iron hand, and thus I have changed with the times. What was this about?' Voice: Think what you will...Truth be told, you are a Crusader. A hero set in the archaic mold. And I have been waiting. Do you think this is a dream, Crusader? Think it if you will, but know this: this realm is a mirror of your own. Actions there affect actions here, and vice versa. (Pendragon scratches his head and looks past the light and sees a curtain that appears to be concealing something. He walks over and pushes the curtain aside revealing a man. The fellow looks startled and Pendragon takes a couple of steps back and covers his mouth...) Pendragon: I know you...You bear a striking resemblance to someone I know.... (The man stands up and straightens out his robe. People don't dress the same here evidently. He clears his throat as he speaks...) Voice: My name is Skyler Welson, and I am the ruler of Gauze. The Land is infested, and you are the only one who can rid the realm of the taint. I have been waiting, and at last my wants are answered. You will carry the torch of light. You will be my chosen champion... Venneman: But in my realm, you are Tyler Nelson...He would do nothing to help me. Hell, he is on the same side as that Goth, Syko. Why do you think the little bastard has been given so many shots at my title? Skyler: Some things are mirrored, while some things are opposite in the Land of Gauze and its counterpart, PCW. It is true. Gauze is merely an extension of that place, not your entire dimension. And here, I am a just and fair ruler. Already you wear championship gold, and now, I send you on a mission to reclaim more gold from the infidel who holds it.... (Skyler motions to the light and it changes into the shape of a man's face. The image sharpens and we are met with the image of John Crichton...Pendragon shakes his head...) Venneman: Great. A mortal Crusader against a superhero test subject? Why don't you animate a comic book and get Superman to do the work? I am just a man... Skyler: You are the CHOSEN man! It is you who must lead the Land from its days of darkness. You start with this champion. He brings no glory to his station. Now go. ******FLASH******* (The scene changes and we find Venneman Pendragon lying on his couch. He sits up with a start...) Venneman: A dream...I KNEW it! (But was it? He looks down at himself and he notices that he holds Excalibur tightly and that his armor is splattered with blood and feathers. He shakes his head in wonder....) Venneman: Chosen to defeat Crichton. And this fellow was certainly no 'Joblin.' So be it. Destiny awaits us all, and who wanted to live forever anyway? FINE~ Next up, The Crusader Chronicles
