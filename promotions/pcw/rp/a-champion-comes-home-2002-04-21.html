A Champion Comes Home

21 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/21/02 *Monologue* Betrayal. Something you’d never expect. Something Big Daveylicious didn’t expect from Mike Frampton. Esspecially, what Big D has done for Mike. Did Frampton honestly overcome the odds and make it to the PCW all by himself? NO! Are you kidding me? Our Extreme Champion helped him get here. Frampton was only brought to the PCW so they can tag team. But Big D didn’t think he was gonna be carrying dead weight. But he went along with it but in Big D’s first title defense what does Mike do? He tries to screw Big D over. For what? Jealousy. Just for the Extreme title. He was sick of seeing Big D gloat over this championship. Just for the gold Mike has ruined a friendship and his career in the process. But expect Big D to stop gloating after Sunday. He fights a porno star, Flaymin Aymin. Who’s part of International Incident. The most dominate stable since the Boy Scouts. I mean what is with them? Why should we fear them? Because there leader is a guy who bent over and let Osama Bin Laden take his Taliban dick rape him? Rape him of his pride. Made him betray America just like Frampton betrayed Big D. Is it because they hate the USA because Uncle Bin hates it? Or is it because of there God awful promos of preaching? I’ll let you decide. (The scene fades to well known white stucco, three-story beach house’s driveway. A black hummer pulls up on the driveway. We hear the engine be turned off as the driver’s door of the hummer opens and out comes the occupant of the hummer. He jumps on to his knees on the driveway; tilts his bleached blond head complete with the ever-present Oakleys back and hold’s the Extreme title in the air with his arms outstretched in joy. Yes, it’s Big Daveylicious. You know that when you clicked onto this rp.) Big Daveylicious: Whoooooooooo! Your Extreme Champion has come home, baby! Whooooooooooooooo! (Suddenly, from behind Big D we see his car door shut. It is his lackey, Michael Graves holding Big D's suit case. Shaking his head. We notice our Extreme champion is clad in Nike running pants, with a tucked in Big Daveylicious shirt.) Michael Graves: Give it a rest. Already I mean you act more excited than a 13 year old beating it at a fake nude of Britney Spears. Big Daveylicious: Well, at least I don't beat with it. I’m the Extreme Champion! (Graves rolls his eyes and walks up to Big D's front door carrying the suitcase along. He takes out the key to the beach house and opens the door. He goes in with the suitcase leaving Big D kneeling on the pavement still with his arms stretched in joy with his head tilted towards that blaring Florida sun. Mike comes back from Big D’s beach house.) Michael Graves:...uh...you coming in? Big Daveylicious: Leave me be. I want this gold to shine and shine! (Graves shrugs as the Extreme champion lays on his knees with the Extreme title raised up with his hands. We notice a shadow of a seagull. The bird’s shadow swiftly moves down close to Big D’s title and a white substance lands right on to the title as the bird moves off. Big D is startled to an odd smell.) Big Daveylicious: What’s that smell? It smell’s like sh*t. (He takes a look at the title and see’s the bird droppings.) Big Daveylicious: It’s bird sh*t! Hasn’t this title been sh*ted on enough?! GET BACK HERE BIRD! (He drops his title and continues to yell at the bird flying away.) Big Daveylicious: YOU WIMP! YOUR AFRAID OF THE EXTREME CHAMPION! (He suddenly he turns back towards his title. He looks up to see dozens of seagulls flying towards his title with their droppings about to fall on it. He leaps onto the title as the birds droppings fall onto his back.) Big Daveylicious: Have they no remorse? Have they no feelings? (He stands himself up back covered with bird crud. He dusts himself off, grabs the title, and runs off into the front door of his house. Graves holding a towel throws it to the champion. Big D takes off his shirt and throws it on Graves head.) Michael Graves: MMMMMMMMM...Poppy seeds and some bread crumbs. Big Daveylicious: Ugh. (They both enter the house entering his living room. Big D looks to the left of the living room were he sees his Answer Machine. The red light is blinking. He smiles knowing he usually never gets a message. He walks himself towards the message machine proudly even though he was just pooped on by birds.) Big Daveylicious: Ah, the message machine. Somebody called me! I wonder who? Maybe Time magazine asking me for an interview or even better Playboy! Michael Graves: Yeah. Maybe even Playgirl! Big Daveylicious: Huh? What are you talking about? I don’t know any Play girls. I play with women. What an idiot! (He pushes his message button. The machine begins to play to a recorded woman’s voice.) Answering Machine: You have 4 new messages. Big Daveylicious: 4 messages! Yes, I’m definitely getting an interview by Playboy! Answering Machine: Message 1. (We suddenly hear a old womanly voice arouse on the answering machine.) Answering Machine: Hello? David, Are you there? I guess not. You never pick up the phone anymore. What is it your too good for your own mother? By the way congratulations on winning the PCW Extreme thingy. Oh, and stop being so mean to Graves and that Frampton boy. There nice boys. You should give them a hug. (Big D rolls his eyes as Michael smirks as he opens his arms up.) Michael Graves: Ah, give me a hug? Big Daveylicious: What momma don’t know won’t hurt her. So I suggest you keep those hands to yourself, idiot. Answering Machine: Now, did you have safe protective sex with those three women you said on the pay-per-view? Big Daveylicious: Of course. I did. Answering machine: I don’t have to tell you about STD’s again do I? I better not. You be a good boy. I love you Daday. (Big D shakes his head in anger. Michael chuckles.) Michael Graves: I love you too, Daday. Big Daveylicious: Shut up! Must a Extreme Champion be pestered by his mother? I think not. I’m 23 years old, damn it! (The answering machine goes to it’s regular robotic voice.) Answering Machine: End of message. Message 2. (Suddenly, a voice of an elderly man appears on the phone kinda tired.) Answering Machine: Dave? ...it’s Dad. You must wear a glove to make love, son. Oh, yeah. Call your mom so she doesn’t think your ignoring her. Later.{beep.} Big Daveylicious: I’m not ignoring her. I’ve just been busy. Michael Graves: Don’t forget your not gonna make love with me without your glove. Big Daveylicious: I’d never have sex with you if my life depended on it. Michael Graves: What if you were a corpse? Big Daveylicious: I still wouldn’t. Michael Graves: Ha ha! You’d have no choice! (Big D sighs.) Big Daveylicious: Necrophiliac. Note to self: Get cremated. Answering Machine: End of message. Message 3. (The answering machine voice changes to message three. City streets can be heard in the backround.) Answering Machine: Hey, Big D. It’s your agent, Tommy Stillinger. I looked around in Hollywood and I got ya a big... big part! I got you in a porno movie! Now, remember shave the pubs.{beep.} Big Daveylicious: Whooooooooooooooo! A porno movie! Finally, chicks will see there Extreme champion get extreme in more than one ways. Michael Graves: Yay! I’m hoping it’ll be entitled “Davey Does Dallas”. Big Daveylicious: Isn’t Dallas a man? Michael Graves: Yeah! Big Daveylicious: Ugh. It better not be. Answering Machine: End of message. Message 4. Big Daveylicious: Playboy magazine interview. This has to be it! (The voice changes to a womanly voice.) Answering Machine: Mr. Daveylicious, this is Debbie from Playgirl magazine. You charged your credit card for a special edition of Cowboys and Indians Sexfest issue and haven’t paid for it. We’d suggest you do please. Before your subscription is over. Big Daveylicious: Indians? Cowboys? Sexfest? Ugh! They must have the wrong guy. (Michael Graves starts to tip-toe out of the room as Big D’s eyes look over to Graves.) Big Daveylicious: GRAVES! Michael Graves: I’m sorry! (With that the scene fades to the red light of the answering machine.)
