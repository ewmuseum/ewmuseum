Harry's Decision - Prelude - The Announcement

10 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: March 10, 2003 [It's just so amazing how just changing one certain thing about a man can just change EVERYTHING he does with his life. Lets take 'Heavy Metal' Harry for instance. He was the pothead. He was the stoned loser. He was the high headbanger. Well, by an accident Harry just didn't have any weed. He just stopped, cold turkey. Ever since that bright, sunny day things have been really changing in his life. For starters he no longer has that weird body odor, he can see straight, he can remember things like to go to the bathroom, and he no longer has a stunted vocabulary. He's finding himself doing different things like no longer listening to Twisted Sister and taking a liking to the softer jams like John Tesh and Kenny G. It's like their music is really speaking to him now in a way he never thought music could. It's not that he HATES Twisted Sister now, it's just that all he hears is the horrible distorted guitars and it's just HELL on his ears. For a few days he actually thought that he was going deaf and then he washed his ears out of the brutal wax build up. Suddenly he finds that women are actually talking to him. They approach him, instead of him approaching them. If he does happen to use one of those phone numbers he got over the past few weeks he'll have to redecorate the awful room he's in. The cannibus posters and heavy metal posters are just going to have to go. Some new wallpaper, get the hardwood rewaxed...in fact...maybe he should just have the entire room disinfected if he's going to do that. Who KNOWS what the hell happened in the room when he was on his 13 year high. One moment has totally changed Harry's life and it's been for good. Some nights he's gotten depressed because he feels that he's wasted thirteen really good years of his life, but it's never too late to start over. So, that's what he's doing. He's going to really change things] ['Totally Tubular' Tommy and Harry walk out of the back door together, walking into the afternoon sunlight. Tommy is dressed in his yellow g-string swimsuit due to Harry selling the blue one to some babe at the laundramat. Tommy's well defined muscles bulge out after his most grueling weight lifting session half an hour ago. Tommy smiles at the sun and looks down to the pool ready to do his laps and heighten his stamina. Harry is dressed in a short sleeve Polo shirt and khaki dress shorts. You know...Harry sort of resembles a yuppie, but maybe it's just the way the sun is hitting him. The two men stand next to the black golf cart with 'Twisted Sister' painted on the side. Harry begins walking down the path toward the pool as Tommy stands by the cart still, shaking out his arms to loosen them up some] 'Heavy Metal' Harry: Are you going to do your laps or just shake like a retarded, white James Brown? 'Totally Tubular' Tommy: Are you going to take those heinous looking shorts or try to celebrate Halloween seven months early? [Harry sighs and just shakes his head. It could phase him in the least bit as he knows who he is and what he's all about] Harry: Well, I'll meet you down there. Tommy: DUDE! Harry: What?! Tommy: Aren't you going to drive me down? [Harry just looks confused, honestly not knowing what Tommy is talking about] Harry: Tommy, where am I going to drive you? We're in the backyard. [Tommy points the the golf cart with his eyes opened wide, motioning to Harry as if he should know what to do with the golf cart. It only takes Harry a milisecond to realize what Tommy wants him to do and he is really finding it RIDICULOUS that Tommy wants him to drive him to the pool in a golf cart. Where did that thing COME FROM anyway?] Tommy: The golf cart, duh! You only drive me down there everyday! Are you totally losing your most bogus mind in your young age? [Harry stays exactly where he is at and points behind him] Harry: Tommy, you do realize that the pool is just fifteen feet away. If you take REALLY big steps you'll get down there in only a few seconds. Even if you walk you'll make it down there in less than thirty seconds! You just did a two hour workout in the weight room, I'm sure fifteen feet to the pool ISN'T going to kill you. [Tommy just stares at his brother with an emotionless look for a few moments and then he points back to the golf cart] Tommy: Dude...get in the golf cart. [Harry rolls his eyes and walks back up to the golf cart, getting into the driver seat. A few moments later Tommy sits in the passenger seat with a smile gracing his tanned face once again. Harry sighs as he starts the golf cart up and lets it puts down the small grade of hill leading toward the pool. As Harry is doing this he's getting the strange sense of deja vu, like he has done this before. Well, he realizes that he has but he's never really felt deja vu like this before. He's getting visions of himself driving INTO the pool which is something that he hasn't done yet...or has he?] Harry: Listen, Tommy, we're going to have to talk. [Harry stops the cart right in front of the pool. Tommy just sits there as if he's completely stunned] Harry: What is it? Tommy: You didn't drive into the pool... [Harry gives Tommy a strange look and turns off the golf cart] Harry: Well, I'm sorry to disappoint but those days are over. Which is part of what I want to talk to you about. [Tommy shakes his head and dips his foot into the water, bringing it back from the chilly temperature that the water is at. He grits his teeth and does a canonball into the pool] Tommy: ARGH! That's cold! Oh man, that feeling is TOTALLY BODACIOUS! [Harry looks at the pool of water that landed in front of him from the brutal canonball. He then looks back down to Tommy who paddles his way to the edge of the pool] Harry: If you haven't noticed, which I really hope you have, that I've been going through a lot of changes the past few weeks. Over the past few days I really have been thinking about my future and everything. I know that I did dabble in professional wrestling with you as part of The Cowabunga Connection but a back injury put me out, which would explain the unbelievable pain in my spine sometimes when I wake up in the morning. There are A LOT of things I've been wanting to experience in life but have been too HIGH to even think about them. Now that I'm not high it's time for me to go out there and just get things done. [Tommy just keeps paddling from one side of the pool to the other with his arms and legs moving quickly. His pace is very quick and up there with some Olympic swimmers. The words from Harry slowly sink into Tommy's head as he slows down a little bit keeping his head above the water to respond] Tommy: Your right, compadre. I have noticed a lot of changes in you. I have pinpointed it and I know exactly how to fix everything. You just need to smoke the BODACIOUS BUD again! That's all you have to do and then all of these totally heinous decisions will just go away. Then you can just drive the golf cart in the pool again. [Tommy then begins to pick up his pace again, the water splashing up all over the place. Harry just looks up to the blue sky shaking his head. Tommy just isn't getting it. Harry is trying to tell him that this is the person that he REALLY is and Tommy is thinking the exact opposite. Has he been high so long that Tommy has gotten used to him being high that that's all he views him as? That is REALLY HORRIBLE!] Harry: Tommy, I know that things are really busy for you now. You've got The Pharaoh coming up this Tuesday, he's a HUGE threat to the World Champion and you've got March Madness...but it's time that I spend a little time on myself for once. You know that I'll always be there for you... [Tommy just stops swimming in the middle of the pool and looks up toward his brother with a confused look] Tommy: Are you saying that your like leaving me? Harry: Well...yeah. [There is an odd silence between the two brothers as Tommy looks down at the water, the chlorine fumes going into his nose, something that he actually likes to smell too] Tommy: DUDE!! This is just out of NOWHERE! I mean...YOUR MY HENCHMAN! Harry: Tommy, your not a supervillain. Tommy: I am so. I stole a candy bar from Derek's Corner Store yesterday. Harry: That's just being an asshole, bro. Tommy: Well, super villains can be assholes too. Dude, you just can't leave me. This week is most important to me, Har. I'm going to need you there for me THE MOST!! Yeah I've got The Pharaoh on Tuesday but I'll totally mess that compadre up without even breaking a sweat. Harry: He's a man on a rampage, Tommy. I wouldn't take that lightly. Tommy: SEE! That's what I'm talking about. How am I going to possibly make it through the week without gnarly guidance like that? I WON'T!! I mean...I'll still win...but I'll get the poo beaten out of me! Plus, Rent-A-Jerkface is always lurking around and everything. Your always those bodacious extra eyes that help me out with him... Harry: I've been thinking about Rent-A-Hero. He seems like he's a really good guy and maybe your really blowing this...no, YOU ARE blowing this supervillain/superhero thing out of proportion. Tommy: The guy is HEINOUS! You saw what he did to my naked lady statue. [Harry rolls his eyes] Harry: Yeah, how could I forget? Tommy: Har, you need to be with me this week, dude. Be there when I successfully defend my Continental Title and be there with me when I FINALLY capture the World Title in that totally gnarly battle royal. I want you to be there when I finally hold the belt in the air and we can both relish in the victory! Then it's PARTY TIME at Hotel California and I KNOW that you'll never miss that, dude! Harry: I don't have to be here all week, Tommy. No matter what I'll always be there for your big matches. It's not like I'm going to be leaving you forever, I'm just going to be off for a while here and there to put things into perspective. Tommy: BUT I NEED YOU THIS WEEK!! Harry: Tommy... [Harry looks down at his brother and looks into his saddened blue eyes. He may see things a lot differently now that he actually isn't stoned, but there is one thing that has never really changed and that's the fact that he loves his brother. He may be dumb sometimes and really follow the wrong path, but it's his brother. It's the guy that he grew up with since his birth and the guy who taught him a lot of things about life. Even in his horrid mental state Tommy still knew where his loyalty lied with his brother and never turned his back on him. Even when Harry's back was hurt and the Cowabunga Connection was finished, he tried to get Harry back on his feet so that they could be a tag team again. He rejected his start as a singles star in PCW!] Harry: ...I'll stay this week. Tommy: BODACIOUS!! Harry: I'm going to be doing a lot of thinking about what I want to do. About where I'm going to go when you complete your goals at March Madness. After the party at Hotel California I'm going to pursue my future...do you got that? [Tommy looks up at Harry as if he already knew what he was going to say] Tommy: Dude, do I look like some kind of an idiot? [Harry just finds so much irony in that statement] Fade to black
