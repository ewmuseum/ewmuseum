Damn Headaches

18 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: January 18th, 2003 They say life is an emotional roller coaster, I believe them. The only things I’ve encountered as of late were ups and downs. Evans friend almost drowns, my emotions sink down, then his friend survives because of me, another up, but what goes up must come down as I find out Evans mother had been shot by none other than his father. With Evan around my life took a turn that changed the direction of everything. It added another member to my average sized family of four and increased it to five. It may have taken awhile but finally Chris had finally accepted Evan as a part of the family and to top it all off I had defeated Teresa Tomas this past Tuesday. Things seemed like they couldn’t get any better, and I was right because as always with life you can’t always stay happy and with next Sunday approaching closer and closer I knew it would be a long ride and whether I liked it or not a choice would unintentionally be made next Sunday, when I made either Chris Storm or Jackson Rust a loser. It left me wondering why they just couldn’t settle their differences. Jackson Rust seemed willing, but Chris wanted no one cutting in on what was his and would do anything to keep Jackson from even speaking to me. I understood where Chris was coming from, but he was taking it much too far and he needed to understand that no matter what and I would make it clear to him no matter what, even if it meant costing him the match. But for now I had other things to worry about. Before the upcoming pay per view I had to defeat both John Kano and Bob Vurdo with the help of Brianna Washington. But even before that I had to kill this lingering headache. “Ahhh.” I screamed while the blood rushed through the vessels in my head, throbbing more and more with each beat of my heart. “”Tessa?” A small voice asked, as I walked past the slightly opened door towards the bathroom, ignoring the voice. “We better have some aspirin or I swear…” “Tessa?” The small voice cut in once again. “What?” I snapped, not wanting to have to listen to anyone for fear that the pain may just intensify if I wait any longer to find some relief, and having a conversation with someone would just take up more of that time that I needed for the pain to go away. “Are you okay?” I felt a tug on my arm and looked down to see little Christa at my side, rubbing her eyes. I must have woken her up with all my agonizing screams. “Why are you up? Get back in bed.” I asked, avoiding the question. I would hate to have her find out something is wrong and go wake up Chris. The way she would dramatize it to him would make him believe something much worse was going on, and I’d hate to wake him up over something as small as a headache. “But Tessa…” She argued back, obviously wanting to find out what was causing all this commotion. “No buts, get to sleep.” I responded, walking into the bathroom starting to search through the cabinets for anything that would relive me from all this pain. “Okay.” She pouted, squeezing through the small opening of the bedroom door and heading back off to bed. “Where is the aspirin, damn it!” I mumbled to myself while fumbling through the medicine cabinet in search of any type of relief. “Tessa Bear?” I heard Chris’ voice call followed by a yawn. “I’m in the bathroom!” I responded, closing the door behind me and locking it shut. Chris was stressed as it was about having to team with Jackson on Tuesday, but he wasn’t the only one with a tag team match. I too would have a match this Sunday with Brianna Washington in which we would be going up again two members of the opposite sex, John Kano and Bob Vurdo. “Are you okay in there? I heard an awful lot of screaming.” He said as I could hear his feet treading across the carpet, closer and closer to the bathroom. “I’m fine!” I said not wanting to be babied. As far as I was concerned it was the girl that was supposed to be worried about the guy, not the guy about the girl. Then again it was not right to make generalizations like that about a certain sex. I was sure that as far as everyone else was concerned Brianna and I would get our asses kicked just because we were women, we were the inferior sex. But how wrong they would turn out to be once Sunday rolled around. I was going to make sure we won, even if I had to do it all by myself. Chris would not have to worry about me, Brianna and I would be fine. We would do just what no one would expect us to do and we would prove them all wrong. “Okay.” He said, yawning once more and then turning in the opposite direction walking back off to his bed, closing the door behind him. “Damn it, where is the aspirin! How can you not have any aspirin?” I screamed in frustration, slamming the glass medicine cabinet shut. “Tessa?” Chris’ voice called once more. “What is going on in there?” “Nothing.” I signed, opening the bathroom door, walking out with my hands clasping onto my head. “Are you sure?” He asked, shutting the door to his room once more as he walked towards me, extending his arms to me. “I’m positive.” I said, letting my head fall forward onto his shoulder resting it there. “But when you’re out tomorrow make sure to get some aspirin, you never know when we’ll need it.” “Anything else?” He asked, running his hands up and down the back of my shirt as I closed my eyes feeling slightly comforted just by having him by my side. “No, just aspirin and an ass kicking for Clyde and Darkstar.” I teased with him, while wrapping my arms around his chest and pulling him closer to me. “Anything for you.” He smiled, running his head through my hair slightly easing the pain running through my head as I thought to myself. Damn Headaches.
