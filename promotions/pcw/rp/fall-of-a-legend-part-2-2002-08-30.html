Fall of a Legend...Part 2....

30 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8-30-2002 Inside the elevator, Venneman nods his head in time to the beat of a watered down version of what appears to be The Thong Song....Hmm...Odd choice for an elevator. I guess what is even more strange is that Pendragon actually seems to know the song as we can see him mouthing some of the words. Abruptly he stops, as if he realizes someone is watching him once more. With a 'BING' the elevator stops and opens. Pendragon looks at the floor number and realizes it is not the one he was seeking. Into the elevator walks a portly woman who has a secretarial look about her. She has a pencil tucked behind one ear and carries an armload of folders. She pauses when she sees the disheveled knight but quickly regains her composure... Woman: You know that office hours are long since past, do you not? Venneman: ..... Woman: Perhaps there is something I can help you with? She shakes her rather large hips at Pendragon and his eyes widen. He shakes his head in disbelief at the audacious woman.... Venneman: Have times become so poor that a...umm...maiden would toss herself at the first gentlemen she sees? Though the offer is flattering, I must decline. Knight's code, you see.... Woman: I see....(Mumbles)Homo.... Pendragon's face again perks up as he hears the mumbled insult. He stares wide eyed at the large woman and sneers.... Venneman: I assure you I enjoy women very much...I merely prefer them to be smaller than I... Woman: Why! You Sonofabitch! How dare you! Men would kill to have me! Venneman begins laughing. Slowly at first, but soon he is rolling. The woman shrinks away from him but has nowhere to go as the elevator door has slid shut once more. A stricken look passes her face as he becomes calm instantly... Venneman: Look here, Wench...The only men willing to kill to have you would be the whalers who would like to have you on their deck as they strip the blubber from your overdeveloped carcass. Now. Silence lest ye incite my anger to even higher proportions. By Arthur's sword; I am honorable, not DESPERATE! The woman backs to the wall nearest the buttons and presses the open button. The elevator stops and the woman runs from the elevator, dropping her files in the process... Venneman: Lady! You dropped your files....Hmm...Hello! Venneman bends down and picks up a file marked KANO. He smiles as he thumbs through it. He keeps flipping pages and finally his smile turns into a frown... 'Hmm...I had hoped something important had fallen into my lap. All the file said is that Kano is a washed up wrestler with an ego his body can no longer back up. I knew that of the heathen dog already. I guess I needed to continue to Kannon's office. I only hoped that he would be in.' Pendragon ducks his head back into the elevator and presses a button. The elevator starts moving again and Pendragon settles back against the wall.... 'It had been a long one so far, but I guess I was closer to my goal. In a way, I hoped that Kannon would be elsewhere. If he is here I might be leaving with my pink slip. BAH! They could not fire their largest new draw! Regardless of the reasons...' The elevator comes to a halt once more. Pendragon looks annoyed as the doors slide open again, STILL on the wrong floor. Nobody gets in this time...Pendragon steps from the elevator and sees what would appear to be a small goblinesh figure riding on an elephant... Venneman: What in the hell? The Goblin looks over at him, points and starts laughing. Pendragon draws his sword and rushes to meet the vile creature... Venneman: Die, ye vile vermin! Taste cold steel! He runs full throttle towards the elephant and takes a mighty swing. The sound of steel on stone rings out and sparks fly. Pendragon's hand vibrate harshly from the blow, but he stays with the attack, his blade a blinding blur in his hands. He strikes and rolls, defends and parries with his foe that is obscured by a growing cloud of dust that seems to be rising from the enemy itself. Finally, Venneman staves off his offensive and sees that instead of a Goblin on an elephant, there is now a very cracked statue of Xavier Kannon. Pendragon rubs his hands and surveys the immense damage to the repulsive form.... Venneman: Damn. I am seeing Goblins in hallucinations now! I must get my gold back... He staggers over to the elevator once more, one hand on his forehead. He looks back at the statue before boarding the contraption and turns to face the camera once more. He presses a button and the doors slide shut... 'This was getting old. I needed my title. NEEDED IT! And they give me Kano. A man on the outs, thrown to a man on the way up. Was there a limit to how far I would go? The only limitations on me would be placed there by the poor booking I was getting. Can I blame the powers that be for keeping the gold from their hottest rookie? Not really. Everyone should have to work at attaining glory a little. It has always been just so easy for me though. Unstoppable, insatiable and unbeatable. Three words of a champion...Three words to describe me. I just hoped the damned elevator would make it to the top of this building, and soon. I needed to get back to reality. I needed to have my head checked. I guess I am just lucky that imaginary Goblin's elephant was not pink...Then I would REALLY be in some trouble....' ***BING***
