The Haunting part II

11 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11 march 2003 Lacuna had just finished banging on the door after her drunken antics the night before. She had managed to get herself into a new room, one with a door thankfully. Jade staggered into a sense of soberness. If that is at all humanly possible. Now Jade ended in a new ‘her room.’ She felt a little crazy but with the sun up and the light of day shining in through the pricey 1900’s stained glass windows she felt a whole lot more comfortable. It still was a long shot from Jade’s actual home. In fact this room itself was approximately as big as Jade’s apartment and club all shuffled into one. Jade sat on a small lavish stool as she looked into the mirror belonging to the pine dressing table. Her eyes stare fixed on the reflection from the shiny surface. Her hands grasp drunkenly on the glass. From out of nowhere her head drops down with a loud bang onto the top of the dressing table. Several of the objects which are there to make Jade look younger, dare it be said prettier hover an inch for a brief second as she does so. Jade had slept not a single week for the past two days and found it hard to even relax in this big house. The way the police were going on with her club it was a mystery they had anything done. The forensics report was inconclusive and even with all this money free, waving in the air waiting to be spent, there was not a single thing in the whole of the bed room for her to do but mope in her own ‘little’ room like a grounded child waiting for their punishment. The only thing she could do was drink, and that was exactly what she was doing. The power to the room it self had been cut off and there was certainly no master light switch in there. After the last ignominy Brook was not prepared to even try and go through that again. Jade’s hand caresses the soft golden handle to the drawer and gently wraps her hands around it. If she did not do something to keep her mind away from trying to sleep tonight, then they may as well pack her off to the funny farm right now because the thought of another night alone with the nothing would no doubt create a travesty in the Devries household. The drawer slides open and Jade’s eyes seem to light up at its contents. Her long gaunt fingers trace the insides of the drawer. Jade giggled to herself and pulled the draw from its home. She emptied its contents on to the dressing table and flung the drawer onto the white sheet loaded bed. Now spread all over the counter was some –really- old make up. Jade begins to pull everything close to her as she sets off on her own little, attention grabbing mission. “This will keep the nothings away from me tonight. I’ll scare the hell out of them!” Jade bellowed insane laughter at the top of her voice and began to smudge the various paint effect items, which were previously untouched, right across her face. Jade’s head bobs up and down with satisfied giggles as she relaxes back, nearly falling over with the lack of back rest there is for her there. Jade laughs again. It is false laughter, of course. Soon the fear would be hear for her. -[Fade]- There is no longer light in the room, instead there is a wide spread darkness. After a few seconds needed to adjust to the change you see the hall again. The hall which holds those priceless, yet dirty portraits of memories long forgotten scattered along each wall. Splinters of wood rest on the pine door, the remnants of the fierce, however quiet, kicking down of the door yesterday. Jade’s head sticks out of the bed room and into the hall. Looking around she sees nothing. Jade tip toes onto the hall once again, allowing the moonlight to reflect against the scary make up which is tarnished across her face like war paint. Like a warrior she is going to chase the nothings out for this house. Once and for all, with the help of her mighty baseball bat there should not be much of a challenge. Jade flinched when she noticed Lacuna was still slumped against the wall, sat fast asleep. Jade decided she had to have a little fun with the remaining black lip stick she had left. After all, significant others had to have a similar look didn’t they? Jade kneeled down and pulled out the lipstick a smirk spread across her multicoloured face. Jade played for a few seconds and then began her process of walking down the long corridor again, whistling happily. The creaking of floor boards beneath Jade was what set her of on her slightly insane streak again. “Gotcha!” Jade slid down the banister with her baseball bat held high she swung into the moving shadows, which just happened to belong to the trees, unlucky for Jade there just happened to be an expensive looking vase on the other end of that swipe and it smashed against the wall. Jade laughed out loud. “Ooops, sorry Mr. Vase!” Jade runs into the living room. Candle’s dance in the darkness above the fireplace. Jade gasps in awe of the beautiful little dance as she looks up in response to the creaking above her. “Those are made by the nothings!” Jade’s lips tighten as she looks to the ceiling. In all reality it was Lacuna hearing the bangs from blow. She had noticed Jade’s door was now ajar. Knowing there would no doubt be an argument to follow she had become that same old misty eyed person looking for someone to blame for all of her troubles. That was Jade; I guess it was just the easy thing for her to do. Now Lacuna was storming down the stairs and down into the hall. Lacuna dives into the front room and lunges into nothingness. She walks past a mirror and looks at the reflection. The black lipstick has black dots on her eyes. Lacuna looks on in amazement, totally dumbfounded at the state of her beautiful face. Jade pounces out of the darkness onto the front of Lacuna forcing her to the floor with the edge of her baseball bat. Jade looks down at Lacuna slightly confused. “You’re the nothing?” Lacuna struggles under Jade but Jade begins giggling. Jade presses her face against Lacunas and rubs the black lipstick across her face. Lacuna lets out a sigh and then tosses Jade over herself and back down to the floor. Lacuna dives on top of Jade, Lacuna blows a raspberry into Jade’s face. Both ladies laugh to their hearts desire. End]-
