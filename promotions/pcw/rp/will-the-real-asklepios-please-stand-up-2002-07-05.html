Will The Real Asklepios Please Stand Up

5 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 07-05-02 (We are presented with the skyline of San Diego at approximately 9:21AM. The haze of the populous city dims the morning sun and obscures the blue of the sky. We come closer into the urban area of the city, where we pass along the highway that was terrorized by the future dictator of Argentina's convoy last weekend. Traveling quickly through the streets of the city, we arrive at the convoy of heavily armed, black vehicles with Argentinean flags and once again see the presidential humvee with the trigger happy gunner at his post. The convoy moves through the city streets and towards a hospital that is approximately a quarter mile away and a police escort clears the path for the convoy to enter the parking lot of the hospital. Coming closer to the hospital, we see the silver humvee parking in front of the entrance, and the driver stepping out to open the backdoor. Frisco and a couple of his secretaries are let out of the vehicle and immediately head into the structure known as the Cielo Vista Mental Hospital. We follow Frisco and his yes-men into the building as the presidential hummer's gunner and the rest of the convoy stay behind...with the exception of the hunchback Honduran midget Pablo, who exits one of the vehicles and waddles quickly toward the hospital screaming at Frisco.) Pablo: '¡Espera! ¡Espera! ¡Deseo venir también, señor!' (Frisco looks back at his associate and sighs.) Frisco: 'No! You go back to the car, you stupid douche!' Pablo: 'Estoy viniendo. ¡Por favor!' (Frisco slaps his forehead.) Frisco: 'Ugh.' (The clumsy midget trips on a speed bump and lands flat on his face. The gunner guffaws loudly and berates the little person.) Gunner: 'Estúpido cabronsito!' (Pablo quickly gets to his feet, flips off the gunner, and runs inside to safety before he gets shot. Upon entering the mental hospital, Frisco's slaps the midget upside the head and shoves him into his stooges.) Frisco: 'Pablo, didn't I tell you to wait in the car?' Pablo: 'Eh, no?' (Frisco grits his teeth.) Frisco: 'Just don't get in any trouble. I'm still pissed at you for screwing up that hit on Big Alan Steele last week. You were supposed to hit Big Al in the leg, not our own man. Is it really that hard to swing a metal object at another object that is only a few feet away?' Pablo: 'Si.' Frisco: 'Wrong! You're a trained killer! You've learned under my guidance and our party's finest soldiers and assassins. When given a job to do, you're supposed to do it, and do it right! I place my loss at Gold and Glory entirely on your failure. If you had done your job correctly, I would've been the World Champion right now!' (Pablo hangs his head.) Pablo: 'Yo se.' Frisco: 'Bah. At least Steele kicked you into the wall like the ugly ball of braindead midget that you are. Now, just keep your mouth shut, your hands to yourself, and try not to scare the living fuck out of the patients.' Pablo: 'Bueno, jefe.' (Frisco motions for his associates to follow him to the front desk of the mental hospital. Frisco taps on the table to get the attention of a young girl with headphones, painting her nails and humming to the music she listens to. The girl looks up briefly and continues to paint her nails. Frisco frowns deeply.) Frisco: 'Ahem!' (The young girl sighs and points to her headphones.) Receptionist: 'Sir, if you want me to help you, you're gonna have to take these off. My nails are wet.' Frisco: 'Your nails are we--..You're supposed to be doing your job, you crab-infested fuckslut! How dare you make such a request to me. Don't you know who I am!? I'm the next president of Argentina! You're supposed to have this floor lined with rose petals and you and your nurses should be naked and ready to give me a sponge bath in honey! All I request is that you receive my queries and you ask me to take of your headphones!?' (The receptionist blinks.) Receptionist: 'Argentina? Is that in France?' (Frisco huffs and thinks about yelling at the girl, but decides to look down at Pablo to give him an order.) Frisco: 'Pablo, take care of this.' (The 3' homely, hunchback midget is hoisted on the table and proceeds to make lewd gestures and rubs himself on the receptionist's face. The young girl begins to panic and screams.) Receptionist: 'Ahhh! Get off me you little pervert!' (Pablo reaches for her goodies, all the while making pelvic thrusts and saying disgusting things in his native tongue. The receptionist finally punches Pablo square in the nuts and runs out of the hospital cringing and gagging. Pablo squeals and falls off the table, holding onto his injured package. Frisco looks down to the fallen midget and gives a thumbs up.) Frisco: 'Nice going, Pablo.' Pablo: 'Erg!' (Frisco points Secretary Enriquez to behind the desk.) Frisco: 'Find the room number for Asklepios.' Secretary Enriquez: 'Yes, señor Presidente.' (The secretary goes around the desk to sift through the documents. Meanwhile, Pablo tries to recuperate and Frisco waves at a mouth-opened, drooling mentally handicapped man who is being escorted through the lobby by two employees.) Frisco: 'Excuse me, sir!' (The two male nurses who escort the patient stop and look over at Frisco, who approaches them.) Frisco: 'May I have a word with your patient?' Nurse #1: 'Sir, we have to get him to his room. Are you family?' Frisco: 'I'm not sure. It's a possibility that I might've had sex with a cousin of mine...but that was a long time and many drinks ago. If there was a kid, it'd probably be a little younger.' (The patient stares at Frisco and tries to point behind him. He yells out with slurred speech.) Patient: 'Der afer meee! MEEHHHHHHH!!!!!!!!!!' Frisco: 'Who are they?' Patient: 'Der a (unintelligible)...and a hamurger dat talks! Wans a eat meee!' (Frisco sarcastically puts his hand over his mouth and gasps.) Frisco: 'Holy shit! I think it's here!' (The patient begins struggling and screaming at the top of his lungs.) Patient: 'EHHHHHHHHHHH!!!!!! EHHHHHHHHH!!!! HEEEPPPP MEHHH! HEP MEHHH!....(unintelligible)!!!' Nurse #2: 'No, Louis, stop! It's okay! It's okay!' (The patient overpowers both nurses and shoves them to the ground. He then waddles out of the building with haste, drooling on himself and flailing his arms. The nurses yell out to the escaping patient and try to chase after him.) Nurse #2: 'Come back! The hamburger won't hurt you, Louis!' (In the typical Friscosity Production fashion, the Olympic theme song plays as the retard runs away from the imaginary hamburger in slow motion. Frisco encourages the patient as he pushes down an employee and exits the hospital.) Frisco: 'Go! Go! Go! Run towards freedom!' (Frisco clips and smiles as various employees frown at him on their way out to catch the escaped patient.) Frisco: 'Well, that certainly brightened up my day...Enriquez, have you found the files yet?' Secretary Enriquez: 'No, señor. There is no Asklepios listed here.' (Frisco smacks his forehead.) Frisco: 'No, fucktard, Asklepios is a Greek demigod--son of Apollo. Obviously a real demigod would not be hanging around a mental hospital. The guy were looking for is just a mortal loon--like Big Alan Steele. He has an American name.' Secretary Enriquez: 'What is his real name?' Frisco: 'How the hell should I know!? I'm not his friend.' Secretary Enriquez: 'Neither am I, sir.' (Frisco frowns.) Frisco: 'Don't you dare speak to me in such an insubordinate tone! You just find his real name and which room number he's in so we can go up there and milk him for information.' (Enriquez nods and continues to thumb through documents. Pablo finally recovers from his shot to the nuts and leans himself up against the wall.) Pablo: '¡Duelen mis huevos!' (Frisco smirks.) Frisco: 'Good. That's what you get for making such rude and vulgar advances to that young lady.' Pablo: 'Pero, me dijo a--..' Frisco: 'Nevermind what I told you. That was downright disrespectful. Next time learn to handle your differences through words...or blunt force.' Pablo: 'Me disculpo.' Secretary Enriquez: 'Found him!' (Frisco approaches the desk and looks over at the document.) Secretary Enriquez: 'A señor...Jonathan Farmington?' (Frisco taps his temples as he reads.) Frisco: 'Yes, this look about right. Vamanos.' (Frisco brings the document and waves his secretaries and midget to come with him. Meanwhile, outside the escaped retard has stripped naked and jumps on the convoy vehicles as the presidential humvee gunner tries to take aim.) Gunner: 'Hasta la vista...restraso!' (The gunner grunts as he pulls the trigger, but frowns as the gun only clicks instead of firing off lethal rounds.) Gunner: 'Ay, mierda!' (The gunner hastily tries to find some ammo, as the mental ill patient jumps down from one of the humvees and starts throwing haymakers toward the hospital employees.) Patient: 'Isa hamurger! Hamurgers gonna eame! Ehhhhhhhhhh!' (Just as the patient knocks over 4 employees, he wonders right in front of a bus which carries a platoon of Frisco's militiamen. The doors of the bus swing open and a dozen armed men in fatigues attack and subdue the raging tard. One of the officers picks up his radio and calls in the status.) Soldier: 'Hemos sometido la amenaza. Cambio.' (A voice emits from the radio as the rest of the soldiers tie up the screaming patient.) Soldier: El presidente está adentro. Retransmita el mensaje a él. (The employees rush over to the soldiers--completely out of breath and covered in feces.) Nurse #3: 'Thank you! We thought we'd never catch him.' (The decorated officer pushes down his sunglasses and smirks slightly. He then speaks up in a thick Argentinean accent.) Soldier: 'Ju cannot have heem. We goeeng do dake heem een for eentarogasion!' (The nurses all look at eachother and scratch their heads.) Nurse #1: 'What the hell did he say?' (The other employees shrug and whisper. The soldier turns around and whistles to his troops as the naked, mentally ill patient lies on the ground drooling.) Soldier: 'Puesto le en el autobús!' (The soldiers pick up the patient and load him into the bus to the objections of the staff.) Nurse #4: 'Hey, you can't do that!' Nurse #2: 'He's our patient!' (A few of the remaining soldiers next to the commanding officer draw their assault rifles and point them at the staff.) Soldier: 'He ees a threat to nasheenol sacurety!' Nurse #1: 'What nation!?' Soldier: 'El Imperio de Argentina! Presidente Frisco dakes dees preesonar alife!' (The soldiers keep their weapons drawn as the officer heads into the bus. They then follow as the bus backs up and speeds away from the hospital. The hospital staff members stand in complete shock at what just happened. A large female nurse shakes her head and yells out.) Nurse #3: 'What the fuck!? The--they...' (A younger male nurse nods and responds.) Nurse #2: 'Yes, a bus of armed South American militants just stole one of our patients for interrogation.' (The other male nurse from earlier brushes a piece of feces from the corner of his eye and shrugs helplessly.) Nurse #1: 'Well, I can see an upside to it.' (The nurses all concur and head back inside.) (Meanwhile, Frisco and his secretaries have arrived at the patient Jonathan Farmington's room. Frisco looks over to his Secretary of Prison and Slavery Affairs--Secretary Juan Icayez.) Frisco: 'Well, what are you waiting for? Knock on the goddamn door!' Secretary Icayez: 'Si, señor' (The secretary's knuckles barely come in contact with the door before it swings open revealing a wild-haired, long-bearded man in his 40's. The insane, disheveled-looking man wears a plain white toga that used to be his bedsheets and holds a cane which is entwined with gummy worms. The demigod wannabe yells out at the men standing in front of his door.) Asklepios: 'What is it!?' Secretary Icayez: 'Mr. Farmington?' (The mental patient frowns.) Asklepios: 'No, gentlemen. I am Asklepios! Son of Apollo and helpful healer of mortal wounds. I dare say you've been misinformed! Good day to you!' (The patient slams the door to the puzzlement of Frisco and his assistants. Frisco smacks his Secretary of Prison and Slavery Affairs upside the head.) Frisco: 'You moron. Look what you've done!' Secretary Icayez: 'I'm sorry, señor!' (Frisco shakes his head in disgust and knocks on the door. The doors swings open immediately once again with the demigod's eyes wide open and peering at Frisco.) Asklepios: 'I have already informed you that the mortal you are looking for does not reside here. However, if you are in need of assistance with illness, Asklepios may allow you to enter.' Frisco: 'Yes...Asklepios. We would like to speak to you about someone.' (The patient raises a bushy eyebrow.) Frisco: 'His name is Big Alan Steele.' (The patient shakes his head.) Asklepios: 'I have not heard of such a man.' Frisco: 'Are you sure? He claims to be a son of Zeus.' (Asklepios gasps and stumbles back.) Asklepios: 'Zeus!? Why did you not inform me of this earlier!? Enter my domicile at once!' (Frisco motions his assistants inside as Asklepios quickly bolts to the door and slams it behind the group. He then begins to pace around nervously.) Asklepios: 'Do you not know that Zeus is in pursuit of Asklepios!? He fears what he does not understand. He is trying to inflict a great quantity of harm upon my person and send me to Hades! I trust you all are not messengers of that cruel, merciless God!?' (Frisco shakes his finger and speaks.) Frisco: 'No, no, definitely not....But I thought he already struck you with lightning?' (Asklepios looks around and shakes his head.) Asklepios: 'Lies, lies, lies. Zeus has not been able to track me down, try as he might. He made this fabrication of defeating me to appease his critics who claim I have too much power for a half mortal! He is a spineless toad, I say! I wish for my father to take his lyre and violate the orifices of that vile, power-abusing demon!' (Frisco nods while his secretaries hold back their laughter.) Frisco: 'Well, sir. It's good to know that you're well.' Asklepios: 'I am not well! I have been fearing for my life for the past two thousand years! If Zeus sees that I am roaming the earth, he will strike me down for sure!' (Frisco tries to put his hand on the shoulder of the crazed man, but gets it smacked with the gummy-worm entwined cane.) Asklepios: 'Back!' (Frisco backs up and puts his hands in the air.) Frisco: 'Whoa, whoa. Easy there, buddy.' Asklepios: 'Now, tell me of this son of Zeus.' (Pablo comes closer to Asklepios and tries to touch his cane, but is knocked over the head with it.) Pablo: 'Ay!' Asklepios: 'I said back with thee...miserable, deformed dwarf!' (Pablo flips off the son of Apollo.) Pablo: 'Chinga tu madre!' (The secretaries drag Pablo off and Frisco begins to speak.) Frisco: 'Big Alan Steele claims to be a son of Zeus. He is a massive man and does hold powers considerable to those of Herakles. Lately, I've been trying to defeat him, as he has something of mine in his possession. He's proven to be exceedingly difficult to disable, but I blame this primarily on the failures of my people.' (Asklepios shakes his head.) Asklepios: 'No, it is the work of Zeus. He has dispatched his son to track me down and end my life! I must leave this place. It is no longer safe.' Frisco: 'Well, that's good. Because we were planning on abducting you anyway.' (Asklepios frowns.) Asklepios: 'Do you have a force that will protect me?' Frisco: 'I have 300 highly trained warriors.' Asklepios: 'Magnificent! Let us exit and find a suitable base. Upon arrival we will talk strategy of defeating this Big Alan Steele.' (Frisco grins and opens the door. Asklepios looks around quickly before slowly creeping out of his room. Frisco's secretaries and Pablo follow, as Frisco's armed militiamen have already occupied the entire hospital and allow their problem-free escape.)
