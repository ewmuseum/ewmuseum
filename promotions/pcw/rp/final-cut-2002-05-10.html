Final Cut

10 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5/10/02 The camera fades in… The Chef is shown, holding a bottle of Perrier in the front of the camera. Behind him is a giant Perrier poster with the logo and a giant bottle. The Chef stands and watches as make-up people prep him up and as his dialogue is read to him by some queer writer with orange hair. Writer: Now your motivation for this commercial is that you like Perrier, and you want to tell the world about it! The Chef: (Sarcastically) Oh my Allah, I thought I was supposed to despise Perrier! What ever should I do? I think I have to read my lines all over again…moron… Writer: HMMMPH! And I was going to ask you about escorting me to the Liza concert, but you just struck out mister! The Chef: Newsflash “Enrique” I don’t swing that way, praise Allah… The cameraman sets the camera in the proper position. The director comes out onto the sets and welcomes all of the staff. Director: Hello everyone, and thank you for participating in my last…(He begins to whimper)…my last Perrier commercial. You see, I’m retiring after filming… The Chef interrupts. The Chef: BORING! Allah would smite you if he wasn’t already busy smiting Jerry Falwell! The director takes the Chef’s comment very personally. Director: You sir, are a fiend of the highest caliber! The Chef: Well I’m sorry Mr. Director, sir, but you’ve got to be one of the most pathetic guys I’ve ever meant. I mean, you come out here and expect sympathy from these people because you’ve been paid endless amounts of money to direct people who already know how to act in soft drink commercials! Give these people and me a freaking break! Director: (Smugly) I not only direct these commercials, I directed Bio-Dome!!! The Chef: (Cringes) Oh, my apologies sir, for that fright fest was worse that watching Bertha belly dance… Director: Well, we did get two stars from the Washington Post… The Chef: Listen pops, you’re old, you’re worthless, and you’re comb over is quite obvious… The director, seething with anger, points at the Chef. Director: You are an ungrateful bastard! The Chef: You are a culinary school dropout and an infidel in the highest degree! Director: Clod! The Chef: Hack! Director: Idiot! The Chef: Drama Queen! Director: Jerk! The Chef: Pansy! Director: ARRRGGGHHHHH!!! The director, distraught with rage, grabs the camera. Director: Let’s shoot this damn commercial, and gets this living hell over with, NOW!!! The camera fades out on the scene… The camera then fades in to show the Chef, Bertha, and Manuel all relaxing on the sofa, awaiting the debut of the Chef’s new Perrier commercial. Bertha: This will be soooooo f*cking sweet! The Chef: I know… Manuel: You’re a star, Chef! The Chef: (Smiling) I know… Bertha: You’re so f*cking hot! The Chef: don’t say that again around Manuel, but I know. Suddenly, streaming on the Alouette family RCA twenty-one inch Television is the ad they’ve all been waiting for.The same scene of the Chef holding the bottle of Perrier and the logo behind him is shown. The Chef: (In the Commercial) Hello, I’m the Chef, a PCW veteran, and a heck of a DUMBASS… The Chef: What’s I didn’t say that!?!? I said, “Nice Guy” not “Dumbass”!!!! The Chef: (In the Commercial) I’m a French MORON WHO LIKES SNIFFING OLD CHEESE AND EATING SNAILS! The Chef: This is rude, crude, and unacceptable!!! That’s someone else’s voice!!! Bertha: It’s f*cking you, f*cko…and you couldn’t be f*cking funnier! Manuel: You sniff old cheese daddy? The Chef: Yes, but, it’s not a bad thing! The Chef: (In the Commercial) Even though I’m a MORON I still HAVE ENOUGH INTELLIGENCE to enjoy the crisp, bubbly, citrus taste of Perrier! Drink up EVERY BOTTLE IS ON ME!!! Bertha: F*cking HA! F*cking HA! F*cking HA! F*cking HA! F*cking HA! The Chef: This is horrible, now everyone thinks I’ll pay for their Perrier! I’m going to sue that Director!!! Bertha: Wait, there’s a f*cking message, f*cko!!! The screen of the Chef fades to black, and a message is shown. “In the memory of William H. Laslow: Director extraordinaire, 1931-2002” The Chef: Wha? Another message appears. “He dedicated his life to Perrier Ads and Pauly Shore movies; there is no doubt in our minds he’s making good friends in heaven.” The Chef: NOOOOOOOOOOO! That old fart screwed with me, and now he’s dead!!! Bertha: Maybe Allah f*cking smote him b*tch @$$! The Chef: Nah, Allah wouldn’t do that to me. He’d at least let me get some revenge…wouldn’t he? On earth the Chef questions his own religion, but the camera quickly rises up into the skies overlooking the planet, and streams upward into the upper heavens of the earth. Between the white clouds and the cherubs, two men chat. Director: I got that sucker good! Napoleon: Ah, eet wus werth eet! Thaut peez of trash has been on mah sheet leest evur seence he turned to ze Taleeban!!! Director: Well, hopefully he gets his shit straight… Napoleon: Ah theenk he weel, eet weel just take sum tiame…VIVA LA DIRECTOR!!! Director: Yes, viva indeed…haha! The two men share a chuckle as the camera slowly fades to black.
