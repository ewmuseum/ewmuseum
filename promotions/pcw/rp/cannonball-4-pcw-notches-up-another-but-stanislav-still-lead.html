Cannonball 4, PCW notches up another... but Stanislav still leads...

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Sometime last week {{{{Scene}}}}The scene opens up into a sunshine filled day. The scene is of a highway with several vehicles driving back and forth on it. The scene is focusing on a single blue Dodge Viper though, which is obviously speeding down the highway, swerving left and right to dodge the other cars. You hear the sounds that you would normally hear on a highway, beeping horns, speeding cars, tires squealing as people forget to turn off on their exit, and the distant sound of police sirens as they chase the average speeder and pull them over. The scene widens a bit so you can see other cars ahead of Semore's car. Up ahead of Semore you can see a single familiar car. It's the small blue electric Mini Cooper S that is driven by Old School Wrestling diva, Tempest, and RUSH superstar, Stimpo. The scene now switches to the inside of Semore's blue Dodge, which he's still driving. The End is passed out in the passenger seat, no longer dressed in his Hamburgular costume but in his regular street clothes. Semore nudges him and calls out to him as he realizes who is directly in front of him now… Semore Smoke: Yo, The End, wake up! Wake up now! {{{{End stirs in his seat and then wakes up. He turns towards Smoke and wipes the sleep out of his eyes as he looks where Smoke is sitting. He puts on his sunglasses so his eyes don't get agitated by the sunlight and asks…}}}} End: What Semore, what's going on? {{{{Smoke points ahead through the windshield, and then he remembers that End can't see and he just tells him…}}}} Semore Smoke: Up ahead of us is that blue Mini Cooper S. End: I'm not sure whose car that is. Semore Smoke: It's OSW's Tempest and RUSH's Stimpo. End: Oh! Yeah! I think I remember someone telling me that before. Semore Smoke: Yeah… I guess you just forgot from the incredible amount of weed you smoked the other night. Look…. We need to stop them before they reach the Nevada and California state line. End: Well I can't help you really… Semore Smoke: Yes you can, heh. {{{{Semore gets a sadistic smile across his face as the scene leaves the inside of the car and enters the inside of the blue Mini Cooper S. Tempest is sitting in the driver seat and Stimpo is in the passenger seat, eating a Twinkie…}}}} Tempest: Well, nice to see you finally woke up. Stimpo: Yeah, I needed something to eat. You talk to Ivan lately? Tempest: Yeah, talked to him on the phone about thirty minutes ago, he's less then thirty minutes away from the state line by now. We've got about an hour to go until we get there. {{{{Stimpo smiles and turns towards Tempest…}}}} Stimpo: What on Semore Smoke or any of the other PCW wrestlers? Tempest: I haven't seen them for the past few days guess they're just too slow. {{{{Tempest looks out the window and gasp. Sitting there in front of her is an ass sticking out the window of a blue Dodge Viper, she is mesmerized by it and just stares at it and the car starts to barrel off the road and onto the shoulder…}}}} Stimpo: Tempest! Look out! {{{{Tempest looks forward and they both scream as she slams on the brakes, too late, they run right into a billboard and come out through the other end. As the car barrels through, it goes through a wall and enters what seems to be a racetrack. Tempest and Stimpo both breathe a sigh of relief and then scream again as they look forward and see a barrage of monster trucks heading their way. They swerve off the track and into the pits, parking the car in a line of parked cars and they continue to breath a sigh of relief then. You hear the muffled sound of an announcer and then the roar of a truck. Tempest and Stimpo both look towards their right and then up in the air. A monster truck just hit a ramp and is now jumping over the line of cars, which they parked at the end of. Tempest and Stimpo both barrel towards the driver side door to open it and get out. They look back as they scramble to open the door and finally do get the door open. They look back one more time to see the truck almost on top of them and then run out of the car and out of the way within nanoseconds of being crushed. The truck lands on the small Mini Cooper S and smashes it into pieces easily. They both look back in horror and then stare at each other. They then look back at the car with horror expressions and then pass out, falling backwards to their backs. The scene changes to Semore Smoke who is laughing, as well as The End, End pulls up his pants and sits back down in the seat and puts his seatbelt back on…}}}} Semore Smoke: Haha! That was the best thing I had ever seen! We got them good! End: What happened to them? Semore Smoke: Heh, they barreled through a billboard sign and into a monster truck race. They then parked and almost got squashed by a truck, but their ok and out of the race. End: Good, as long as no one was hurt. {{{{The scene changes to the UPS DHL van that is being driven by D-Kay Calyx and Joey Flash. In front and behind them are two Nevada state troopers whom are following the orders from the North Carolina state trooper who stated that he would help escort them all the way to the UCLA hospital, where they're supposed to drop off a box of medicine that may cure cancer. Well, of course they made up the story and the box is empty, but the escort is helpful on getting them there by speeding and not being pulled over for it. But they do have one problem, when they get to California, they'll need to find a way to ditch the cops, and they have less then two hours until they reach the state line. The scene enters the cab of the van where you see Joey Flash driving and D-Kay sitting back thinking…}}}} Joey: Are you still thinking about what we're going to do when we reach California? D-Kay: Yeah… I can't believe it… we're probably ahead of everyone in the time, but we still have one problem that could slow us down by the time we get there… I need to think of something… Ah ha! {{{{D-Kay sits up and has a huge grin on his face. He pulls out an atlas of the USA and looks at the map he then looks at the highway that their on and then back at the map. He pinpoints where they are and smiles an even bigger smile…}}}} D-Kay: After four or five states, I've finally come up with the plan! I've got it! According to the map, we'll run into Las Vegas in about thirty minutes, when we get there we pull over to some convenient store. Right now I'm going to call ahead, I've got family that lives in Las Vegas, can't believe I didn't think about it before! Those fuckin' cops won't see it comin'! Joey: Cool! But what's the damn plan? D-Kay: We'll pull over and pick up another vehicle and drive in that the rest of the way, we won't lose much time by it, plus we'll ditch the cops and have about an hour to go until the state line is reached, and then we have about another hour or two until we reach LA! Joey: Coo'! Make the call! {{{{D-Kay whips out his cell phone from his pocket and flips it open. He dials a number on the phone and then puts it to his ear after hitting 'send'. He waits a few seconds as the phone is ringing and then speaks up with a happy voice…}}}} D-Kay: Auntie? That you? It's me D-Kay, yeah, yo lil' nephew hear. Look, we're headin' towards Las Vegas right now, and I was wondering if we could borrow one of your cars… Uh huh…. Ok… all right! Great! Hey, park it at the nearest Circle K, I think I remember one right there in front of Caesar's Palace, we'll pick it up there… Nah…. I've got a key. Ok… I'll talk to ya lata then… see ya… bye… {{{{D-Kay flips the phone closed and then smiles at Joey, they both grin at each other and Joey turns back to drive, and they drive away silently… PCW and miscellaneous drivers are on the rise once again… but the threat of Ivan Stanislav up front still beckons towards them, will they be able to attempt to take out Stanislav and win the race for PCW, if not themselves? The answer will come next time!…}}}} TO BE CONTINUED…
