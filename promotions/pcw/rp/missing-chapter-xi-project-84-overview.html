Date: Chapter 11 PROJECT 84 Introduction In 1998, Sony’s Playstation 2 was set to begin releasing in countries likes China and Japan, where the game systems were being made. The only problem with the initial release of the system was that the first hundred or so Playstation 2’s that were pre-ordered by anxious fans had a horrible glitch to the game. Though this glitch was in the game, the console’s still worked like a charm to the kids who bragged to their friends about the treasure they had. The kids didn’t know or find any problem in the system but Sony did. Sony even pulled back the real release date to the console’s back a few months to change the glitch. So what was the glitch, exactly? Well, in the Playstation 2, a new chip was formed that was stronger than any other chip in a console. This chip was so powerful that con-artists and smart terrorists that knew what they were doing could use this chip to create a very powerful explosive bomb. In fact, only two of these chips hooked up to a small battery pack and an ordinary b-b gun bullet could create an explosion the size to burn your own house down. Sony, realizing this problem, needed something to do. They pulled the release date back on the system by about a few months to stop the problem and ‘lighten’ the chip inside the PS2. The only problem with stalling on the release date was that one hundred Playstation 2’s had already been released to un-suspecting people who found no problem. Sony quickly put out a search to find and bring all the Playstation’s back and it did almost work except for one small problem. Five of the Playstation’s could not be retrieved back to Sony Inc. Now I know what you’re saying. Five PS2’s are no big deal and the owner’s of the consoles are probably just kids anyway. What are the chances of one of these chips being brought into the hands of a terrorist? Very good, actually. In fact, this is where our whole story of SyN and his mission comes into play. A very smart mastermind terrorist who helped in the creation of many highjacks and explosions, Henry Claval, knew about these chips. You see, a few years before that in, let’s say 1995, Henry Claval had read about this new chip in the Playstation 2 that would blow all the other video game consoles away. Claval, being the smart person he is, found something odd about the chip, which was being called Project 84. Claval had been searching on what exactly would connect to the chip to create the game engine. The problem was that two wires that were being connected to the system wouldn’t agree with each other. To save any confusion, lets just say that a red wire and a blue wire can’t go together. Well in the console, there was to be a red wire and a blue wire connected into the same chip, meaning that a smart person could use these chips to his advantage in creating a huge bomb. Henry Claval, knowing this through years of research, eagerly waited until he got his hand on a PS 2 to plan his next vicious plan. So, acting like an excited kid, he quickly pre-ordered FIVE of the one hundred consoles that were being pre-released from Sony. Henry patiently waited for the Playstation’s to get within his reach and when they did, Henry was like a kid in a candy store. A few weeks later, hearing that Sony was looking to contact the one hundred owners of the system’s to call back the consoles they began phoning people. Henry Claval, hearing about this scheme, could not give back his prize possessions. As fast as he could, Henry changed addresses and phone numbers and anything he could to hide. A few weeks later, when the news was released to the public that five of the PS2’s had not been returned, there was buzz surrounding the situation. After a few weeks though, the buzz died down and Sony Inc. had concluded that nobody need be alarmed because the Playstation 2’s were in good hands. And they were in good hands, to a very powerful terrorist. For a couple years, Henry Claval stayed low in his place, planning and plotting a huge nuclear bomb. Why was he planning this scheme? What purpose did he have? The only purpose Claval had was that of pure evil to spread his knowledge into pain for the world. With his two sidekicks, Ivan Chistov and Clair Rivence, Claval hid himself from everyday life and lost the police and FBI from his tale. Claval stayed in Germany, his home country in the basement of a priest’s house. The priest? Ivan Chistov. The housemaid? Clair Rivence. Together, the three that went into business together planned a mission called Project 84. The Destruction One chip when put together right has the power to blow up a house easily. So how much damage can five do? About TEN times the size. You see, with one chip, you have two wires that don’t go together which causes the explosion. If you add a second chip and put all four wires into one socket, it not only doubles in strength but because the wires are opposites, it adds even more power to an already deadly bomb. So how much damage can five chips together in one socket, with a substantial amount of gunpowder do? Well, picture this: The Hiroshima bomb that dropped down in Japan could be felt from fifty miles away. This bomb can be felt over one hundred miles away, killing anybody instantly within twenty feet of the explosion. And Henry Claval owned this bomb. What could he do with it? How will SyN stop him? What will SyN do to help the situation? What is Ivan Chistov’s last alias? Tune in NEXT time to find out all those questions and more!
