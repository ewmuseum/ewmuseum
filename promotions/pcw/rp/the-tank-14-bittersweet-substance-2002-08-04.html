The Tank 14 - Bittersweet Substance

The Tank __14— ********* Bittersweet Substance Crawl inside my skin ...like a lover... ~Leigh Donovan Thursday, August 1 - Day 28 As soon as he got back to Unit B after Turmoil, Snow headed straight for Doug Hattoni’s cell. And now, as he rounds the corner, he finds himself staring at a man who must be at least twentyyears younger than Doug, and close to two hundred pounds lighter. “Who the hell are you?”Snow asks with a voice full of suspicion, taking a hand off his back which still aches from lastnight’s match with the Chef. “Funny, that’s exactly what I was about to ask you,” says the young man that Snow believes tobe an intruder in Doug Hattoni’s cell - Snow has learned to be suspicious of all non-Italianslately, even though he’s Irish himself. The man in Hattoni’s cell is actually Hattoni’s newcellmate, Tim McDonald. “I asked first,” says Snow, stepping up to the much smaller McDonald, breathing heavily downon him. “Who the fuck are you?” Snow’s time in the Tank has taken everything that wasgentle out of him. He’s as rough cut as every other inmate here now - he does what he needs todo to survive. “Who am I?” asks Tim McDonald, with a cocky expression on his face - he refuses to allowSnow to intimidate him, despite his physical inferiority. He notices the wedding ring on Snow’sleft hand, “I’m the guy that fucked your wife right before I came here.” He sticks his tongue outand flicks it a few times at Snow. Snow flashes a smile of his own at Tim McDonald, though his eyes are consumed by blue rage. “I gave you a chance,” he says a mere moment before grabbing McDonald by his wiry arms andpushing him hard against the far wall of the cell. “Jesus Christ!” swears Tim McDonald as his back slams into the concrete wall, realizing theseriousness of the situation he’s in. Since coming to the Tank, Snow has transformed into atruly dangerous man. “Come on, ma -” Tim is interrupted by Snow’s open right palm which comes slapping across his clean-shavenface. The stinging pain sends the terrified Tim McDonald to the floor, holding his right hand upin a gesture that begs Snow to stop - Snow’s never been one to listen to begging. The young lionresponds by giving McDonald a stiff kick in the ribs that leaves the young man gasping for anyair he can squeeze into his lungs. Suddenly, a new voice booms - “What the fuck is going on here!?” Snow turns around, Timpeers up, and they both find themselves under the disapproving gaze of the fat mobster himself,Doug Hattoni. “Jesus Christ!” Hattoni takes the Lord’s name in vein as he tosses a damp wettowel onto his lower bunk, “I go out for a fucking shower, and when I come back, all hell’sbreaking loose!” “Fuck, Doug,” gasps McDonald from the ground, clutching his stomach as if it were trying toburst from his body, “what the fuck kind of animal is this supposed to be?” “This is Jason Snow,” Doug says to his floored cellmate without a drop of sympathy. “He’s themain muscle around here. Get off the fucking floor.” “My legs are trying,” says Tim McDonald, finally catching his breath, “but everything else is alittle bruised right now.” “Wait a damn minute, here!” Snow says, turning to Doug while pointing down at his victim,“You know this little prick?” “He’s my cellmate,” says Doug. He then turns his attention to Tim, “Didn’t I just tell you to getoff the fucking floor? I’m an old man - my aim’s not what it used to be. I’ve pissed on thatfloor, you know.” Obediently, Tim McDonald pulls himself up off the floor, sure to keep his eye on Snow. “SoDoug,” he begins, still clutching his ribs, “don’t you think some more formal introductions are inorder? Or should I just keep thinking this guy is some fucking retarded ape on steroids?” Timknows that the insult is a little out of line no more than it comes from his mouth, and he’simmediately punished by a second hard slap in the face from Snow. This time, he keels over,takes a step toward the toilet and spits a little blood into it. “For fuck’s sakes!” he exclaims atthe shot. “I think your cellmate should learn to keep his mouth shut, Doug,” Snow says, turning a glaretoward Hattoni. “And I think your slap-happy friend should learn to take a fucking joke,” Tim says, still leaningover the toilet. “Shut up, the both of you,” Doug says, waving his hand at them. “I don’t have time to put upwith a bitchfest between my main headbuster and my main drug man. Despite the fact thatwe’re in a prison, I do have a business to run here and you’re both a key part of it.” “I think I missed something,” says Snow, holding up a finger. “How the hell is thisbastard a key part of your business!?” “He’s got talents,” says Hattoni, glancing up at the young blond lion who he depends on forprotection. “Why don’t we just leave it at that?” Snow understands perfectly. Hattoni referred to Tim as the ‘drugman,’ but Hattoni also knowsthat Snow wants no part of that side of the business. He’ll protect Doug Hattoni as a means ofsurvival, but he refuses to take part in Doug’s narcotic monopoly. Within the first year ofSnow’s marriage, his own drug abuse nearly ended it, and he’s been stubbornly appalled by thesubstances ever since. “Alright,” Snow says, trying to calm himself, “Whatever. I’m going to go take a fucking shower. I’ll let your boys know that I’m out, so they’ll keep watch. But if this little fucker getsout of line,” Snow says as he gestures to Tim McDonald, “I’ll let them know they’re to save himfor me.” With that, Snow stalks out of the cell, trying to calm himself about his first meetingwith Tim McDonald. “Fuck, Doug,” says Tim McDonald as he climbs up to his top bunk, “you told me the muscle hada temper problem - but I had no fucking idea he was a nutcase.” “He’s a good kid,” says Doug, pulling his deck of cards out from under his pillow. Tim leans down over his bunk and stares at Hattoni. “Yeah,” he begins, “but don’t you think it’sa little, you know, dangerous to keep him around you? He’s got a fucking hair trigger. I mean,maybe you should send him packing. He might take you out at any minute.” Doug chuckles a bit at the suggestion. “Listen kid,” he says, looking up at Tim. “Between youand me, keeping that fucking lion around is the only thing keeping Harvey Blake and his fuckingNazis from getting brave enough to stick a blade in my back.” Tim reluctantly concedes before rolling over onto his back. “Seems a little dangerous to me,” hesighs. “I mean, I’m only thinking of your best interest, Doug.” “So is he,” Doug shrugs. “He kicked the shit out of you because he thought he was protectingme. There’s nothing wrong with that.” On the top bunk, Tim frowns. “I wouldn’t say he kicked the shit out of me,” he says in anattempt to protect his masculinity. “Whatever you want to call it,” Doug chuckles, remembering how helpless McDonald lookedwhen he was sprawled out on the floor gasping for breath. “And don’t pretend you didn’t saysomething to set him off, either.” “I don’t know what you’re talking about,” McDonald tries to sound sincere. “The hell you don’t,” Doug fires upward as he begins to deal himself into a game of solitaire. “You don’t fool me, Timmy. You’re a little fucking weasel - I know that. It just so happens,you remind me a hell of a lot of myself at your age. And you’re exactly the kind of man I need.” *** From the Office of the Warden “Two thousand years from now, there’s going to be history class looking back at our era.” beginsthe Warden from behind his desk, “And they’re not going to talk about George Dubya. They’renot going to talk about JFK. They’re not even going to talk about Martin Luther King. Why? Because the most important objects of our era are drugs. I’m not talking about issues, either. I’m talking about objects. Little fucking pills, little lines of powder, maybe a big field full ofplants.” “Drugs are without a doubt the highest grossing products in our century. And why? Becauseonce you get them, you want more... and more... and more. And what happens when you takethe drugs away? Well, maybe that’s the real reason why they’re so important in oursociety.” *** Friday, August 2 - Day 29 Leigh Donovan lies uncomfortable in his flat infirmary bed remembering the days when he waslead singer of the most famous rock band in the world, playing to packed arenas every night. Itall went so fast, he thinks. It was only a few weeks ago, yet it seems like an eternity already. As he lies in the sweat-soaked bed, his broken ribs, broken wrist, busted kneecap, and majorconcussion are giving him the least of his discomfort. His craving for the sweet realms ofheroine have him trembling violently. “I’m a fucking rockstar!” he screams, drawing the attention of the night staff, one doctor andtwo nurses. He also manages to wake every inmate in the infirmary. “Yeah, and now you’ll shut the fuck up so I can get some sleep, motherfucker!” snaps the inmateto his left. “I need it!” says Leigh through clenched teeth. “I fucking need it!” He begins coughingviolently, sits up in his bed, and vomits all over himself. “Donovan!” shouts the male doctor as he stomps out from his office, carrying a flashlight. “Ifyou’re not quiet, we’ll be forced to sedate you. You don’t want that, do you?” “Sedate me!” screams Leigh before the doctor arrives in front of him. “Fucking sedate me,motherfucker! PLEASE!” Leigh begins coughing once more, each time his broken ribs causehim enormous pain. “For Christ’s sake, Donovan!” the doctor says a moment before turning his flashlight to thebroken Australian rockstar. When he finally sees Leigh and the state that he’s in, the doctor isso shocked that he drops the flashlight. “Jesus!” he breaks a commandment. Leigh is sitting upin bed, his chest, stomach, and legs soaked in a mixture of blood and vomit that’s still runningfrom both his mouth and nose. It’s already made it’s way to his shoulder-length bleached blondhair and is sticking it to the sides of his face. What’s more disturbing to the doctor is the factthat Leigh has managed to claw half of his cast away from his wrist, and he holds it up as if hewere showing off the disgusting way the joint bends backwards. The soft bandages around hisblack and blue ribs have also been discarded and are now lying on the floor next to his bed. “Come on man,” Leigh pleads with the doctor, his eyes wild and bloodshot. “I just need afucking fix. Just one. You can get something for me, can’t you? Can’t you!?” The doctor reaches down for his radio to call for help. “I need a couple of C.O’s to get downhere and transport a disorderly patient.” “Fucking please, man!” Leigh pleads as he begins to cry. “Do you know who I am? I’mfucking Leigh Donovan. I can make you a rich man! A fucking millionaire! Just give me afucking fix. Heroine, man - fucking heroine! Please, man! I’m going to die! You don’tunderstand! I’m going to fucking die!” Leigh leans forward while speaking. He soon loses hisbalance and falls out of the bed onto his stomach. The doctor takes a few steps back as Leigh screams from the pain the fall shoots through hisbroken bones. “Just wait here,” says the doctor, trying not to be as terrified as he is by themonstrous young heroine-addict. “People are coming to help you.” “They’re bringing the heroine?” Leigh asks as he begins to lose his voice. Pathetically, hecrawls along the toward the doctor, who continues to back up. Soon, the doctor finds that he’sback up as far as he can, and is leaning against another patient’s bed. Leigh closes in and hugs the doctor’s left ankle. “You... have... to... help... me...” he saysweakly, looking up at the doctor before vomiting all over his shoe and lower leg. “Get off me!” the doctor desperately screams as he kicks at Leigh to no avail. “Help me!” Leigh says again. “Get the fuck off me!” the doctor shouts. The patients are all sitting up now, trying to see what’sgoing on despite the darkness. “Help me!” Leigh shrieks in an animal-like voice. Suddenly, a team of night-staff C.O’s burst into the infirmary, charging the scene. The doctorimmediately takes charge of the situation - “Strap him in the bed!” he commands. “No!” Leigh screams in protest as the C.O’s roughly pull him off the floor and hurl him into thebed - they know nothing of his broken bones and wouldn’t care if they did. While this is goingon, the doctor rushes back to his office. “I just need a fucking fix!” Leigh screams. “Yeah,” says one of the C.O’s as he tighens the strap around Leigh’s half-casted broken wrist. “We’ll fix ya real good.” A moment later, the doctor returns with a syringe in hand. Without hesitation, he jabs the longneedle into Leigh’s strapped-down arm and begins pressing the liquid from its glass body intoLeigh’s broken one. “This’ll quiet him down,” he says as the last drop of the liquid makes itsway from the long needle to Leigh’s blood stream. Within moments, Leigh is silent and motionless - under heavy sedation. The doctor wipes sweat off his forehead and says sternly, “Take him to the hole.” *** From the Office of the Warden “No matter how much we tell people ‘drugs are bad’, what they’re getting out of everything theysee is ‘drugs are cool’. And in the society we live in, ‘cool’ is what counts.” The Warden standsup from his desk and steps over to the window. “Why else do people pay hundreds of dollars forclothing? Because it’s cool. The same reason why a lot of people, at least in the beginning, trydrugs. What most of these cool people don’t realize is one day they’re going to wake up and‘cool’ simply won’t matter to them anymore.” *** Saturday, August 3 - Day 30 “A visitor!?” Snow exclaims, glaring at the C.O through a haze of sweat. “For fuck’s sake!” heshouts, letting everyone in the gym know his displeasure. Unlike the last time he had a visitor, Snow knows exactly who he’ll be ‘visited’ by - and he’s nottoo happy about it. He specifically told Krista to go home and he’s in no mood for repeatinghimself, especially after what went down last night on Fusion. The evidence of the damage hesuffered last night is in the form of a dark blue bruise on the right side of his back. And ofcourse, adding insult to injury, Darkstar had to stick his nose where it didn’t - and doesn’t -belong. “Come on, Snow,” says the inpatient C.O. “If you want to go, follow me. Personally, I don’tgive two fucks what you do.” Snow shakes his head, angrily. Part of him wants to tell the C.O to go tell Krista to go backhome - he doesn’t want to see her. However, another part of him wants to tell Krista thathimself, so he obliges and follows the officer to the glass-divided visitation room. Just as he thought, he finds Krista sitting on the other side of the glass, days after he told her togo home. California’s no place for a naive young girl, Snow had always told her when she spokeof coming out here. She always resented the fact that he thought her naive. “The fuck are you still doing here?” Snow says sharply after snapping up his phone receiver. “Is that any way to say hello?” Krista asks, her eyes wide and somewhat distant. “Answer the fucking question!” Snow glares at her and slams his fist down on the table in frontof him. “Just thought I’d come say hello to my favorite brother in law before I go back to Canada,”Krista says, though the words are so emotionless that Snow knows something is wrong. Instantly, his voice transforms from an angry one to one filled with concern, “Krista, what’swrong?” He eyes ere suspiciously, wondering if something horrible has happened to her. At thismoment, he feels terrible for being so sharp with her when she came in. She’s his sister-in-law,but so much more than that at the same time. She’s like a sister - maybe a daughter. “Krista?” The young girl is silent. “Krista?” Snow repeats, his voice beginning to shake. “Oh. What?” she says, snapping out of her days. Snow leans back in his chair, trying to put the pieces together. “What the hell...” his voice trailsoff. Suddenly, he bursts out of his chair and screams into the phone, “YOU’RE FUCKINGSTONED!” She doesn’t even flinch. “Where the fuck did you get it!?” he screams at her, “Who the fuck gave it to you!? I’ll walkright through these fucking walls and rip his God damn heart out! Krista! KRISTA!” The young woman’s eyes snap up at the sound of her repeated name, but her delayed reactiononly enrages her brother-in-law further. “How could you fucking do this, Krista!?” he screams as the C.O’s behind him step forward - allthree of them have their right hands on their billy clubs. “You know how your sister feels aboutthis! How could you do it!? Who the fuck gave it to you!? God damn it, Krista!” he screams,throwing the phone receiver at the pane of glass. It strikes the glass with a loud thud, but leavesit undamaged. Without the phone, Krista can’t hear Snow’s screams, but in her haze she still manages tounderstand most of his words by reading his lips. “WHY!?” he screams as on of the C.O’s grabshim by the arm. Snow still thinks of Krista as a child - a child that he must protect. And now, atthis very moment, he finally sees that while he’s confined within these walls, he’s useless to her. He can’t protect her from anything in here. His desperate sorrow surfaces through utter rage. “Snow!” shouts one of the C.O’s as the trio of them all grab one of his limbs. It’s useless - he’stoo powerful for any of them. He pulls away from them, sloppily, and screams at the glass in vein. “How could you, youfucking bitch!” he screams at her, though he knows she won’t truly understand until whateverdrug she’s on wears off. “Fuck you, Krista!” he screams, knowing nothing else to say, “Fuckyou!”
