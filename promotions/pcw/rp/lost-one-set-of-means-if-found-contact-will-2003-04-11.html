Title: Lost: One Set Of Means. If Found, Contact Will. Featuring: Tim Walden Date: April 11, 2003 Where there is a will.. The match was made for Spring Fling. I didn't really honestly know whether it was the work of Jeremy or Ellie, but the important thing was that either way, I had to fight Akiyama there, an opportunity to back up everything I had talked up previously, to win the match on my own accord and rub it in the faces of the ignorant crowd... ...But first, I was going to have to deal with the devil. El Diablo, the man whom I'd had to sit by and watch take the shot I deserved to have won for Alliances, waste his opportunity and still get another, waste once more. The resentment of the failure to get what would have been my big break still resounded... Much as it was only outside circumstance that had cost me the bout, the Trapezoid making the worst decision of his wretched life in costing himself the opportunity to say he had battled with the World Champion himself, it was still the last we'd truly met in the ring, the crossroads between a career that got all the opportunities to succeed and one saddled from the start by the interjection of those who didn't deserve to wrestle with me and those who didn't hold the credibility to do my talent justice, who would only hold me down in the people's eyes.. ...As deservedly as they liked to think, since I would obviously never be able to compete with the men I never had the opportunity to fight... ...The rare time one that they gave the opportunity to battle did hold candle to me, it never truly mattered. Win or lose, there always seemed to be an illicitness in the result. When I fought Teresa for the TV Title, I was cheated not once in the calling of premature submission, not twice from the ignorance of submission, but a full three times where an error was made regarding it, and all I got from it was further disrespect, in spite of my own personal satisfaction with my performance. When I fought against the Mourning Star over the title neither man cared so much for, the result was a foregone conclusion in spite of the quality of both of our wrestling skills.. Why should I have made any effort to break away from the headlock? It was little more than a symbol of the predestined finish, a reason not to care..Unlike El Diablo. He was talented to a point, and beating a man of the past World Title contention could build my hope, if not for that he was wasting his time with men of Flaymin Aymin's lack of talent...But at least here, the match would be my opportunity to earn respect, a little deserved dignity... ...Yeah, right. There was little to gain, where the comments that were relayed, the opinion to be taken as testament was concerned. Any victory I got would be dismissed as cheating, an upset of great proportions by Kern. There was little to do about that but speak to the affirmation of my own hard-fought victory, but that would never do... ...As if they would believe the man they had been taught to hate. I had asked enough by declaring my need for a true challenge; Now, Howard had seen fit to accept my demands, willingly throwing the skilled opposition at me like water coming toward the child given an abrupt first test in swimming...There was nothing to be gained from the involvement of the Office here, and besides they had seemed to take over separate destinies now with Kannon's disinterest in remaining, dragging in 'Special agents' who he felt more capable of the destruction of PCW than P.W.A., too busy trying to get hold of their record, and myself... ...Why did he hold no confidence in me, while becoming so positive in the hope that his new couple could swing the distance? I would get to see what they brought to the ring come Tuesday, whether they were any more capable of defeating the monstrous man who had been sticking in our side for months than he felt I was... It was Xavier's efforts that got them this far, got them their shot at the monster that he hoped would remove him from the threatening status he held..It was their turn to sink or swim, and I was supposed to act as the flotation device that would keep them from failure in any case. But what would it make me? Just another man, a man holding the key to a fated ending, the paver of a road with only one destination.... A destination that could only hold true to one finish, no matter how hard he tried. Akiyama was a man of great will, but even his will would not be enough to deal with three people whose success would be dependent upon his failure... There is a way. ---- View Tim Walden's Biography
