Good Meal

17 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1/17/2003 *The scene opens up in a high class restaurant somewhere in Midwest America. Seated by himself in the corner with his back to the wall is the one true legend of PCW, Jon Kano. Kano is resting after finishing off a massive meal and several before, during, and after dinner drinks. Kano is dressed (from what you can see of his body) in a bright yellow suit with a jet black tie. A waiter dressed in a tuxedo shirt with vest and black pants approaches Kano.* Waiter: Mr. Kano, how was your meal this evening? Kano: The lobster thermodor was excellent. My compliments to the chef. In fact, I would like to speak with him out here at my table if at all possible. Waiter: Yes, sir. *The waiter takes the empty plate from Kano's table and scurries back to the kitchen. Kano, meanwhile lights a fine cigar and orders another Bacardi and Coke from the bar. Finally, a man of about 6' tall and about 250 pounds dressed in traditional chef's wear. He approaches Kano's table. Kano stands up and shakes his hand.* Kano: Sit down. Chef: I shouldn't, sir. Kano: I insist. Chef: Well if you insist, sir. *The chef takes a seat across from Kano.* Kano: What is your name? Chef: My name is Ron Ringsdorf. Kano: My name is Jon Kano. Ron: I know that, Mr. Kano, I try to watch you and PCW as much as possible. Kano: That is good to hear. It is always nice to meet someone who appreciates what I do. Ron: I hear that. It is not very often I get a chance to come out here. Kano: I just wanted to tell you that the lobster thermador was excellent. Ron: Well thank you, Mr. Kano. May I ask you something? Kano: Shoot at will. Ron: You have a match on Sunday where you tag with Bob Vurdo. You two face Brianna Washington and Tessa Windsor. All three of those seem toi be classified as newbies and we both know how you feel about newbies. Kano: True. You see, Bob Vurdo is still young blood, but he has talent. He is coming along nicely to the fact that he has come up to me backstage and actually asked about PCW's history and heritage. He is beginning to have respect for PCW and for the people who built PCW that long time ago like myself, Doug Troy, Meanstreak, PHD, and others. These two women are part of Xavier's little plan to build up a women's division in PCW. Well, I am not big on wrestling women, but if they want to play in a man's world, then they can get treated like a man. Ron: Testify. Kano: I look at those two and I see weakness because women are not meant to be put in the ring against men the caliber of me. Its just not good policy to put a 5'10' woman who weighs 135 pounds soaking wet against someone like me who is a foot taller and twice the weight. Simple physics would tell you this. So on Sunday, I will walk down the aisle, step into the ring, and rock that bitch like only I can because I am WHAT REAL LEGENDS ARE MADE OF!!!! *Kano and Chef Ron stand up, shake hands, and both leave their separate ways as the scene fades to black.*
