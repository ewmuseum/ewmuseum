Radio Show Interview?!

Date: not entered It has been a mere weekend since Rich Sherwin and Ryan Moore were able to pull off the upset of the century… it has only been a mere two full days since Sherwin and Moore were able to defeat ‘The Heartbreakers’ and take home the very item that so many people would have automatically assumed was theirs. Everyone within their existence had pitted ‘The Heartbreakers’ to finally put ‘1’ back on their record against the PoP-Icon duo… everyone had put their money down on the ‘Wrestlers’ coming through and defeating a couple of punks who seem to be more interested with their careers in the world of ‘PoP’ than anything else. It would have seemed as though the story’s end should have come after this high moment in the careers of the ‘Pretty Cool Guys’… and one could easily assume that since their peak has been reached, nothing else could really top it! However, the ones that think such a thing are the foolish people that will not look toward the week ahead. This coming Sunday 28th July 2002, the ‘PCW Tag Team Championship’ Titles will be on the line… in a Four Corners Elimination contest that will pit, the Tag Team Champion, Akiyama… against Politically Incorrect, The Heartbreakers… and none other than the Pretty Cool Guys themselves! With this titanic contest in mind, one would have to remember that there are still two shows beforehand! Anything could happen within those two shows… Akiyama could get food poisoning… Mr Valentine could be confused with Skeeter from Nickelodeon Show, “Doug”… hell, Reggie Loveheart could accidentally be accused of being a “Legit” member of the Political Family! With the match in mind, one would have to think about which team is favourite to win those ‘Tag Team Championship’ Belts! The first bet could go on Akiyama because he is one of the most dominating characters within the business! He is one of the damnest individuals that has ever come to exist within this period of the Earths existence! However, on the second hand… we have a team within Politically Correct, a team of Masterminds who have not only been able to give Akiyama a bit more than he can chew at certain times, but they’ve become accustomed to the taste of a fine Championship belt… just ask Lovecraft for Christs sake… he was the holder of the ‘PCW Extreme Championship’! Now, within the third hand… we have another awesome force within PCW, the team that happen to prance around the place in “Pink” and call themselves ‘The Heartbreakers’! This duo have been able to topple ‘Politically Incorrect’ once before… and that was when those damn Tag Team Titles were on the line! The HB’ers however… may not be the strongest link statistically… namely down to the one remaining team who are yet to receive their shout-out.. Yes, those “Pretty Cool Guys” have actually got quite the winning run against their opposition in the ‘PoP Wars’. Sherwin and Moore, two guys who always get hassled for their apparent lack of talent, have defeated ‘The Heartbreakers’ in some shape or form… somewhere within the region of four times maybe! They eliminated ‘The Heartbreakers’ from the Tag Team Battle Royal last Month… Sherwin defeated Valentine in ‘Fatal Fourway’ contest a week before the show… Sherwin and Moore took that recording contract away from the ‘Heartbreakers’ last week on Fusion… and they even brushed the “Pink” duo aside when asked about positions within the “Pretty Cool Guys” band! So… at the moment, it seems to be working, Politically Correct defeat Akiyama… The Heartbreakers defeat Politically Incorrect… and then the Pretty Cool Guys pick up the final few pieces and eliminate The Heartbreakers to stand TALL… as the ‘PCW Tag Team Champions of the WORLD!!!’. Will this tainted dream have the chance to become the one reality that everyone would hate to happen? Nobody… not even the boys themselves know what kind of things will be happening with their match… but one thing is for sure, you won’t know either unless you bother to watch PCW’s “Wrestle Vessel”, L!ve on Pay-Per-View… Sunday 28th July… Cable Customers Contact Your Local Operat… heh, almost had me doing the complete advertisement plug there… sometimes I just love to ‘Thank’ the fact that I understand what I’m saying! We join our hero’s as they sit ready for an exclusive interview on one of the many unknown and ignored ‘Radio Stations’ across the entire country. With the fact that ‘Fusion’ has passed us by… and the campaign to quash the attempts of ‘The Heartbreakers’ debut single behind us, this Radio Show interview can only be about one thing and one thing only… their MASSIVE “Tag Team Title” contest that will be coming to us in a matter of six mere days! The scene completely unfolds with the view of a door. ‘WOW!!!’, we don’t think… looking at this door as if it were actually a religious symbol or something that meant a lot to the people of the world. Upon this plain white door were the words, “Radio Studio – Guests Entrance”. Nothing could be heard coming from this establishment… although it was pretty obvious to assume that something was taking place behind the closed door. We looked up once to see a bright red bulb, something that wasn’t switched on (maybe the first thing that was naturally turned off >_>; ). Due to the fact that the “Radio Show” wasn’t in session anymore for the time being, the light was off… that actually meaning that people with permission were allowed into the studio to do whatever business they really needed to do… except those two kinds of business… because they really are nothing short of being totally sick. The door was pulled open and we were greeted with the view of a relatively slim character, dressed in a pair of flared jeans and dark blue, “Dragonball Z” T~Shirt. His hair was pretty damn short and a pair of glasses were the only differences between an establishment of ‘eye contact’. “Hey… so, you two must be the really cool ‘Boy Band’ that my executives asked to come onto the show?”, he said, sounding unsecure about the identity of the characters that stood infront of him. For once, not to be offended by the lack of knowledge that this little warrior was exerting, Rich Sherwin looked him through his pale glasses and nodded his head somewhat. “We’re the group that were supposed to be on your show..”, he said, sounding confident and happy with the way things were currently going. After all, both Sherwin and Moore were only minutes away from making their official radio debuts – A FIRST for the world industry! The ‘Radio Guy’ took a few steps back and allowed Sherwin and Moore to enter the studio itself. We followed the duo into the location and looked around the room briefly while they found themselves being asked to sit down on two chairs about four feet away from the ‘Jockey’ behind another mic. “Alright…”, the ‘Radio Guy’ began, “… this show isn’t going to be anything too over the top… I know that we don’t get a lot of listeners tuning into this show every week and so forth, but if you wouldn’t mind keeping the language down… I mean, the majority of our listeners are old folk anyway…” “What do you mean? I was told that quite a few kids listens to this show and what not? Sheesh… I’m looking for fans that are going to be able to jump around in the air… you know, celebrate when the “Pretty Cool Guys” walk out of the ‘Wrestle Vessel’ Pay-Per-View with the “PCW World Tag Team Championship” Belts strapped around their waists! One thing… that neither I or my Tag Team partner want… is to watch a couple of eighty-year old scarecrows jump up and break a hip or something…”, Sherwin slammed back at the ‘Radio Guy’, hoping that this verbal attack would do something to change the situation around here. Moments after this verbal attack, Ryan Moore began to laugh to himself in his seat. At first, Sherwin found himself totally ignoring the laughter of his best-friend… but soon decided to see what in Gods name the problem was, “Whats up with you man? Is it impossible for you to stay serious or something?”, he asked with a great deal of intensity now lingering upon his voice. “Like… totally imagine this…”, Ryan began, looking off into the distant space along with Sherwin, using his hands to demonstrate what he was laughing at. Rich allowed himself to watch whatever Ryan was being said and Moore continued, “… we have some eighty-five year old dude in the audience jumping as we win… he just happens to jump into a ninty-year old who’s standing next to him… and CRUNCH!… look at that guys hip… and he can’t get up… wouldn’t it be like… so funny to see that thing happen?… and at one of OUR matches too… that would SO rule..” “Come to think of it… that would SO rule man… how comes you come up with all the cool ideas?”, Sherwin asked, actually looking impressed with the idea. The two ‘PoP Icons’ laugh to themselves and start to mess around, pretending to be a couple of old people who’d colliding celebrating a ‘PCG’ win. There was all sorts of laughter coming from people staring through the window in the next room as well… although the only character not to really give two shits about this… was the ‘Radio Guy’ himself… guess he has a soft spot for the old, ancient relics of Earth or something. “You know… you two are only here for a small interview… something that really doesn’t need to broadcast to the rest of the Country… I can easily make you leave the building, with or without assistance too.”, the ‘Jockey’ Boasted, sounding proud of himself as both members of ‘PCG’ came to a standstill from their little round of “Old People Mocking”. There was a loud groan from all the other spectators in the other room as Sherwin and Moore stopped… but they guessed that it was better to have ‘PCG’ do the interview as well… instead of just having them act like goofs for the better part of thirty seconds or so. Rich and Ryan are asked to remain totally quiet as the ‘Jockey’ starts up the show and gets things running. “HELLO… and welcome back to the Daytime Show, as usual, I’m your host for today and right now, we’re at that same old part of the show, where I have asked two very special athletes to come into the station and record something of an interview… you know, muck around a little here and there… take some calls, maybe even do a little bit of their side-talents just to let Corporate America take a peak of what they’re missing… or in some cases, what they’re not. Now… since I’ve stated it was that time of the show, I think it’ll be good for us to introduce to you… two of the fastest rising stars with the ‘Hit’ Professional Wrestling Company, “Primetime Championship Wrestling”… The Pretty Cool Guys!”, shouts the ‘Jockey’, allowing his voice to grow extremely loud as he calls out the names of the special guests for todays show. We see the ‘Jockey’ reach over to one of the buttons on his control panel and he presses it once. A loud cheer follows, obviously being one of those “Pre-Recorded” Cheers… but without any doubt, it still did well as a real crowd giving the welcome to PCG. “So Guys…”, he continued, “… I’d just like to take the opportunity to welcome you both to the show.” “Thanks”, both Ryan and Rich managed to spit out at the same time. They looked over to the show host and showed a look of appreciation, something that couldn’t be seen by the listeners around the world… but something that could be picked up by the camera hanging around inside the room. “Well, it seems as though things have been going pretty well for the both of you inside and out of the Wrestling ring. Would you care to explain developments of that level to us?”, the show host asked, looking interested to hear exactly how Sherwin and Moore were able to do this… or that… or the other thing around the corner. Rich and Ryan started to debate who should go first with the answer… and after a short interval, they were able to select Ryan as being the first speaker. “Well… like, I’d say that things have been going up for both Rich and myself since our month within ‘New True Internet Championship Wrestling’… both Rich and myself, alongside a former member of the band, we won the NTICW Tag Team Titles after just four matches… and we totally released a #1 Single too.”, he proclaimed, placing a lot of emphasis on the details of the record that had been released back in their N-Dub days. Ryan continued, “… after we finished the month with their company, Rich and I felt that we needed to focus on nothing but the music for a few months… that was until PCW announced their decision to fire their entire roster and hold interviews for new talent.” “PCW… they fired their entire roster just to make way… for the next wave of talent? Is that what you’re telling myself and the rest of the World right now?”, the ‘Jockey’ asked, assuming that he was already getting to something very deep… although this minor fact was something that all of the wrestling community knew of anyway. “Erm… dude, everyone around the world already knows about the things that happened around that time… its not a scandal… and its nothing that you’re going to be able to expose, before attempting to sue the company for whatever reason comes to mind…”, Sherwin reminded him, making sure that the point of ‘everyone’ knowing was something that would stay with him forever. “Well… moving on along this topic, I’ve been told that you were able to defeat “The Heartbreakers” this past week on ‘Friday Fusion’… and with that fantastic win, you were also able to get your hands on a ‘Recording Contract’ with Virgin… would you care to discuss any of the details that are deep within that piece of paper?”, asked the ‘Jockey’, just trying his damn hardest to get some worthwhile information mentioned on HIS OWN show. “You know… dude, while we would like to be able to talk about the details of our contract on your really… lame… show, its against the rules to talk about that kind of business on air… you never know what kinds of people are going to be listening. Maybe even that big dude over in the biggest media company will say “Damn It to Hell” when he finds out about this..” The scene quickly changes to that of another office somewhere else around the world. We look at a relatively “Big Man” as he sits in the comfortable confinements of his spinny chair. It would appear as though he is just one of the people that actually bothers to listen to the Radio Shows. Another individual walks into the room seconds later and says something regarding something to his “Boss”. “Really… Really… Big Boss… of the Biggest Media Company… have you just heard what Ryan Moore had to say about his contract details on that Mid-Western station?”, he asked, sounding a little concerned about the ideas that his boss was currently in the middle of having. “Damn It to Hell… Damn It to Hell”, he says, repeating the same phrase over and over until the scene slowly begins to watch back to the studio where Ryan, Rich and this annoying little Radio Show host are. “Well guys… at the moment, you haven’t been able to say a lot about yourselves or your careers for that matter… how about we talk about something a little closer to home? Lets take your match this coming Sunday against The Heartbreakers, Politically Incorrect and Akiyama for the Tag Team Titles… there has to be a lot of stuff flying around your head at the moment right?”, the ‘Jockey’ asked, hoping that this question was going to be worth something relatively good. “This contest… its going to be something that’s really easy for both Ryan and myself to win. I think this is going to be one of the easiest matches that we’ve ever had to be in… especially since last month, it was the Connection that spelt the end of our challenge… and this time, those fellas aren’t anywhere… nowhere at all.”, Sherwin explained, suggesting that he and Ryan Moore have those Tag Team Championship Straps in the bag. “Yeah… Akiyama is only one guy, he’s not going to be able to take on all two of us and still win… Politically Incorrect, they used to be something really big around here… but if you’ve ever had the chance to see the offence that both Rich and myself are able to utilize… you’ll understand our judgement here…and finally, we have those other guys down at ‘The Heartbreakers’… and to make something of a long story seem short, we’ve beaten them down too many times for their own good these days.”, Ryan informed the ‘Jockey’, seconding Sherwins decision to suggestion ‘PCG’ will be the only team with an easy ride on the ‘PCW Pay-Per-View’. “Well Guys, I’m sorry to cut the interview off at this point but that’s about all the time we have for you… I’d like to thank you both for taking the time to come out here and talk about a few things, even if we only did get the low-down on one of your future career decisions… Ladies and Gentlemen, that was PCG!”, the ‘Jockey cries out as the scene fades out deep into black. Following their match against The Heartbreakers, confidence seems to be at something of an all-time high in the camp. Will Sherwin and Moore be able to do another one of the small impossibles and take the ‘Tag Team’ crown away from Akiyama on Sunday Night?… or will they become nothing more than another statistic? There is only one way to find out… and if you want to see what that is… the rewind to the beginning of this installlment!
