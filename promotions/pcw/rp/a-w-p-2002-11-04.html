A.W.P

4 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 04/11/02 The first thing audible is the tick, tock of footsteps marching in synchronization. The camera starts off at the feet as they traverse the corridor. A pair of smart black shoes, highly polished contrasts the heavy, thick soles of industrial boots. As the camera lifts up slowly, it takes in the eggshell walls and heating pipes behind a pair of green casual trousers and slightly torn stonewash jeans. The jeans are whipped every now and then by shiny scarlet. The camera follows still, moving past the open door to the cafeteria. No time for lunch now, but those burgers are making my tongue wet before I've even got to them. The pair don't seem distracted by the laughter coming from inside that suspiciously increases as they walk past. The lens escalates once more, now showing a classy business jacket and tie, both the same deep forest green as lower down. It focuses on a black T-shirt with some sort of cartoon lizard on the front. Some strange plastic adorns the chest, covered mostly by the red coat on the shoulders. Finally, it raises for the last time to show the familiar faces we are accustomed to. Emerging from the top of the white, spotless shirt and sporting the trademark sunglasses, a cascade of purple flows down the back, while on the other head, short green spikes pierce... WHAT!? The camera pulls back then, showing Nerezza in the green custom suit and Gaz Green wearing the rocker outfit. Both have unusually large smiles on their faces. They look each other in the eye. 'Now remember what to do if a human touches you. If they ask for money or help...' Nerezza growls, straightening his collar. 'No worries, Squire. I know...' Gaz says. In an instant, he throws a backfist that stops mere inches from Nerezza's face. The wind brushes some strands of violet backwards. 'And at the same time, if a girl calls me?' 'Let me get this straight...' Nerezza clears his throat and tilts his head to the side. ' Ello Sweet cheeks, Gaz Green here. I don't need to tickle to make you laugh but I'll do it anyway!' 'Perfect... and if someone gets a little out of hand?' The Emerald Mena... err, Living Nightmare squawks. 'Umm... Easy, tiger!' Says Nerezza. 'Right then.' The pair take a deep breath, then turn in opposite directions. 'OFF WE GO!' * * * The purple hearse pulls up outside a fancy looking club. Actually, it's the one across the road from Primetime Wrestling Academy's main building, right next door to the rap 'n' metal entrance music store. Which now has 'Now that's what I call Bad Entrance Themes Vol 8 now in stock!' written on a piece of card in the window. Reporters and ring-rats frequent the outside, cameras, notebooks and underwear in hand. A valet in a bright red hat and matching outfit steps up and the door opens to reveal the ladies man that all had been expecting. Cameras flash and questions fly as Nerezza steps out, clad in Gaz's attire. As he opens his mouth to speak, a black padded bra swoops towards his teeth. The moment is immediately caught by the sea of magnesium burning. Nerezza stands for a second with the strap impaled on a sharp canine tooth. He plucks the item from his own jaws and uses it to mop his brow before proceeding slowly down towards the doors, guarded by men with 'Identical Twin Security' badges on their classy tuxedos. 'How did you all know I'd be here tonight' he asks as the crowd of press employees gather. Looking around, he sees a small Daschund with a camera attached to the collar scampering past the guards and into the club with no apparent difficulty. With a sigh of amusement, Nerezza shakes his head. 'Bloody PCW Hound...' * * * The bell above the door chimes as it swings open stiffly. Gaz walks inside the Barber on the outskirts of town that Nerezza had given him the address to. The rotating candy-cane pole outside indicated that the place must be right. Shuffling in, Gaz sees that the clientele are mainly well-groomed people with more money than sense. Clad in designer clothes and looking like they were attending a high class movie premiere, they sat on comfortable looking leather chairs, either listening to the radio playing Frank Sinatra's 'My Way' or nosing through a magazine. Gaz sits himself down into one of the chairs and nearly sinks like the leather was covering quicksand. He hums quietly to the tune and the relaxing atmosphere nearly lulls him to sleep, except for the occasional sharp SNIP or the buzz of the electric razor... * * * Inside the club, Nerezza stands by the bar, leaning over and looking at the myriad of coloured glass. He skims his eyes over each of the labels while he waits his turn. The crowd conversation makes up most of the noise level until feedback from a microphone whines and snatches the attention of the occupants. Holding the mic up on stage is a man dressed in a black leather jacket and jeans cut off at the knee. His hair dangles to his chin and silver chains run around his attire randomly. 'Okay, people! Are we READY!?' he calls out. The congregation give a hearty cheer back. Nerezza smiles, trying to keep in character and raises a fist. 'Alright, please give a warm welcome to a band that play here regularly, we know you love them - give it up for ECHO SYSTEM!!!' The females of the crowd emit screams and the males brandish bottles and devil-horn hand signs as the lights on the stage bathe it in white, illuminating it above the rest of the club. The lead steps forward and pulls another round of cheers from the audience. He stands around 6'2 with blood red hair to his chin and a trimmed goatee. His lanky frame approaches the mic and the guitar in his hand begins to strum... 'When I look into your eyes I see a lot of hatePushing me awayYou're haunted by a pastA past that brings you downSo you throw it all awayAgain...' Nerezza appears to be swaying to the music slightly when a hand taps him on the shoulder. Surprised, he turns to see a female bartender, wearing a black leather top and tape around the wrists. Obviously, her themed costume is meant to be that of a wrestler. Her auburn, curly hair sweeps to her shoulders and just below. Nerezza, not being someone usually acquainted with females in a friendly sense, simply gapes at the amount of flesh in front of his face. The trance is luckily broken up by 'Can I help you?' Nerezza blinks his eyes rapidly as he snaps back into the real world. 'Oh, yeah. Rezz Bul... eerr.. Aftershock, please.' The lady dips underneath the bar and the sound of glass barely covers the band, which is drawing a big reaction from the audience. 'Damn, almost forgot Gaz drunk alcohol... worthless, human invention.' he thinks to himself as a glass is set in front of him. Handing over the required money, Nerezza is forced to keep the ploy up and sips it slowly, almost coughing it back up as it runs rampantly into his throat. Deciding to do as he'd seen Gaz do and against his better knowledge, he tilts his head back and quickly drains the liquid. Shuddering and shaking his head about in recoil from it, Nerezza slammed the glass down, screwing his eyes shut. 'GUH.... disgusting... but...' the vampire said inside his head. 'I'll have another, please. Actually, make it another TWO, sweet-cheeks...' * * * 'Zzzzzz....' 'Mr Nerezza?' 'ZZZzzz....' Mr Nerezza?' 'ZZZZZ!' A tap across the cheek woke Gaz from his slumber. He opened his eyes and found himself submerged in the chair. His main body had sunk inside, leaving the edges of his coat and four limbs being the only things sticking out. 'Huh? Wha? Eh?' The sound of tape peeling from a roll tears across the room as Gaz frees himself from the chair's jaws, slowly dripping out of it. Suddenly, his mission came back to him as Beethoven's 5th symphony reminded him of his partner's musical taste. Noticing that he and the barber 'Aye, that's right... what the bloody hell do you call this?' he exclaims, pointing to his hair. The barber, looking the epitome of grooming itself with his brown hair expertly combed and set into a middle parting, carefully inspected the lime spikes jutting from the crown of Gaz's head. 'Why, it looks like green dye, sir. And may I say that you've lost a good deal of weight and gained some colour in your cheeks. Not to mention you smell a great deal better than...' 'I KNOW IT'S GREEN DYE! WHAT'S IT DOING IN MY HEAD?!' Gaz shrieked. The shouting was uncommon for him, usually with the outbursts of anger coming from his partner. His throat was feeling the effects of it too, understanding the advantages of only needing to breathe when you wanted to talk. '...it appears to be making your hair green, sir.' the barber replied. 'LOOK! I appear every single week on a wrestling show, you know that, right?' he yelled. 'I can't say that I ever enjoy watching such a profession but you've mentioned it once or twice, sir.' turning his nose up, the barber acknowledge it. 'Well I've gone out there every single week with long, purple hair. I came in here for a trim, woke up only to find my lifelong pride cut THIS short and dyed green! Is this professional conduct?!' The Jester leaned over and gazed through the red lenses on his noise, enjoying the intimidation act that his partner was known for. 'I can assure you, sir, that your hair was that way when you walked in her-' 'THAT'S THE OLDEST EXCUSE IN THE BOOK!' he interrupted with rage, jabbing a finger into the now cowering hairstylist's chest. 'But .. but the other customers saw you walk in...' he whimpered 'And are any of them here right now to prove it? You are on the verge of ruining my career!' Gaz bellowed. 'Gulp... I'm s-s-sure we can come to some agreement, sir... perhaps a wig? I have an extremely large range of highly realistic wigs for men, starting at a hundred and' 'I WANT A REFUND! IF YOU EXPECT ME TO PAY FOR THE MISTAKES YOU MADE, I'LL TAKE THOSE CLIPPERS AND MAKE SURE THE NEXT TIME YOU SHAVE A CUSTOMER'S HEAD, YOU'LL HAVE TO BEND OVER AND TURN AROUND TO DO SO! AM I UNDERSTOOD!?!' The Ker-Ching! of the cash register replaced the bell above the door as Gaz left, making full use of the six pockets in Nerezza's coat to harvest the contents of a now trembling beautician. 'Well... that's alright for now. Time to see how Gaz is doing...' The Jester grinned. * * * 'Nerezza' entered the club without too much of a reaction, with the main audience distracted by the band. Gaz walks past a group of girls towards the direction of the bar, when one chirps: 'My GOD! I have NEVER seen Gaz Green looking so rough!' He turns to her, thinking that she's seen through his disguise. To his shock, her eyes are looking towards the dancefloor. His gaze follows to see a scene from a horror movie. 'Gaz' is on the dancefloor with a stereotypical busty blonde wearing less cotton that found in a bottle of aspirin, with spilled vodka on the now wrecked green suit, dancing like the one nerd at the school disco. His tie is loosened and he looks an absolute mess. Immediately, the real Gaz marches onto the dancefloor and grips Nerezza by the wrist with the force he'd normally apply for a submission hold. 'COME ON, GAZ, WE HAVE A MATCH TO PREPARE FOR...' he growls, more into the character than ever. As he attempts to wrench his partner out of the club, 'Gaz' yells back to the girl: 'I SHALL TICKLE YOOOOOOOOOOOOOU!' * * * The Hotel room stinks of booze. Gaz has changed into a spare suit while Nerezza lays halfway on a bed, wearing only his wrestling gear while his clothes were sent to laundry. His wings sneak out of the edges, revealing the spines on the tips. He flaps around on his back like a walrus, attempting to gain some kind of vertical posture and giggles to himself. 'Rezz... have you been drinking?' Gaz asks accusingly. Nerezza suddenly jerks himself up, looking with scandalized eyes at Gaz. 'How.... DAAAAAAAAAAARE YOU... GAH! How DARE you accuse me of drinkinginginging... ME!? Your oldest pal, partner and mate, ooooooooold Rezz, old vampy-slashy-pranky thing.... why, I SHOULD TEAR YOU LIMB FROM LIMB!' he groggily stammers, managing to raise himself off his bed. He steps forward to deliver a clothesline but falls flat on his face, smacking his nose into the ground. Gaz moves not an inch during the display. 'No. But you have, though, haven't you?' The Jester says, looking down at the fallen behemoth. Nerezza picks his face up and shows his nose to be bleeding. 'Yeah.'
