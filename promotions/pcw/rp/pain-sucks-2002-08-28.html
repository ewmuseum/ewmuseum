Pain SUCKS!!!!!!!!!

28 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8/28/2002 ~The son of a bitch actually did it. He did exactly what he said he would do. He ended his rival's career in the culmination of a multi-month feud that went back and forth between two fierce competitors. One would attack the other, and on the next show the other would return the favor. At Third Dawning, both men gave it all they had. One man fought for the Old School while another fought for the New School. On that day, the Old School prevailed triumphant over the New School. But now what? The Old School man has spent the better part of three months fighting the Old School man, but what is next? This Friday, the Old School collides with a man who ever since entering PCW about two or three months ago, has been raising all kinds of hell. Who will prevail on that day? Will the Old School reign supreme once again? Or will the new man on a roll continue his winning ways? I guess we will just have to wait and see on this week's Friday Fusion.~ *The scene opens up inside of Kano's locker room following Third Dawning. The illustrious PPV from PCW's home in the PCW Pavillion has been over for about thirty five minutes or so. Jon Kano, still dressed in his wrestling attire is sitting on a steel chair in the middle of his room with his elbows on his knees and his head hanging, looking down at the floor, with a towel draping his head. There is a knock at his locker room door, and in walks Cal Seaver, microphone in hand.* Kano (looking up and looking exhausted): Oh Christ. Cal: Hey Jon, do you think I could get a few words with you? Kano: Doesn't matter what I say, you are still going to ask me your stupid little questions, so shit, pull up a chair. *Cal Seaver does as Kano says, and sits about three feet in front of the tired Man who Rocketh the Mosteth.* Cal: Well Jon, you did what you said you were going to do here tonight, you ended Corey Williams' career. Kano: Yeah, I did, but I don't feel like the 'Man who Rocketh the Mosteth' right now. I feel old. I shouldn't feel like this, I am only 26. I got another twenty years left in me, but right now I feel like I am fifty and about eighty pounds overweight. Corey gave it everything he had in that ring, and I can feel it. My whole damn body hurts. It feels like I got hit in the face with a brass fist. Oh wait I did. I forgot about that. Cal: Does it still hurt? Kano: No, it feels good. I wish every match I would get punched in the jaw with a pair of brass knuckles. Nothing is broken, I don't think. He just knocked about three of my teeth loose. Then I did something really stupid. I speared a chair. Can you believe that? I speared a steel chair deep into Corey Williams, but still it hurts like a son of a bitch. Cal: Well, I don't know if you are going to like my next bit of news. Kano: Don't tell me Ivan is back in PCW, I don't feel like having to be the only one to have the balls to stand nose to nose with him again. Cal: No, but you have a match for Fusion, against Pendragon. Kano: Great. Wonderful. I feel like I just got ran over with an eighteen wheeler and I get the luck of facing the hottest wrestler in PCW today of Friday. Cal: I sense you are not pleased. Kano: You sense right. I gave everything I had in that ring tonight. I didn't think I was in such bad shape until the adrenaline wore off about an hour after my match. Then I realized that Corey took a piece of me with him. I give him props for that, he brought it to me, but I am the one who won. I still am gainfully employed here and he is not. But as far as Fusion goes, f*ck me. If I feel half as bad as I do now, then Pendragon could knock me over with a feather. I give this young buck his props, too. He has come into PCW and rocked the boat. Reminds of me of someone I once knew, me. Cal: How so? What do you mean by that? Kano: When I first came to PCW, I was a man well known in the realm of professional wrestling in the Orient. I made my career there. I came home and wrestled in a few indy feds until I came across PCW. I interviewed with PCW, and I was signed to a pay-per-appearance contract for a month. Within my first month, I had the Rising Star Title. Within my first three months, I had the Continental Title. Within my first six months, I had the Television Title. Ever since then, I have been focused on one thing and one thing only. It isn't personal glory, I have had enough of that in my career. I see PCW, and I see the best wrestling organization in the world, period. There are no feds in our league right now. We were down for awhile. OSW was going apeshit all over us in the ratings, but we are back. Why are we at the top of the perverbial mountain again? Because of people like me, who have shunned personal glory and are out for the better of PCW. I don't give a damn about titles, I have been there and I have done that. I don't give a damn if I am the most popular wrestler in PCW. I only care to help PCW. If that means taking a man like Pendragon and giving him a reality check, so be it. I see him and I can only think of what happened to me. I was here for personal glory for the first few months of my career. I even joined MEDIA. I was part of the most dominant stable in PCW history. MEDIA can never be topped. The Red Army was dominant, but not like MEDIA. MEDIA had no weak links. We had enforcers, we had business men, we had some of the most sound technical wrestlers in PCW at the time, and we had everything in between. I became an egomaniac. I wanted to do things my way, regardless of the reprocussions on PCW. That is what scares me in this New Era and when I see a man like Pendragon, so alike to me. He has unlimited potential, but I feel it is my duty and my honor to just give him a little heads up on what PCW is all about. I am doing it for his own good, and for PCW's own good. Cal: Do you have concussion? Kano: Hell no. I feel fine. But I am tired as hell, so I will leave you with my trademark catchphrase, on Friday, I will show Vennemann Pendragon what REAL LEGENDS ARE MADE OF. Now get the hell out, so I can get changed and go lick my wounds. *The scene fades to black as Cal Seaver leaves Jon Kano's locker room.* ~Well, I guess that is what.~
