And the Trigger was Executed |2|

18 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6.18.02 A beautiful sunset after a beautiful day has come and gone in the nation’s capital. There’s nothing like a pleasant night to fall upon after a hard days work for many. The stars seem to be suspended in the night sky, hanging up only by hope. This city has had its problems and faults, as many would know. The Symbol of Military Might, The Pentagon, looks up-to-the-minute, although merely a few months ago, half of it was spearheaded into. Recently sprouted tree limbs etch the temperate, grey late June night sky. The branches attain across the atmosphere interrupting the nuance. The barren, brown extensions of the tree seem to be reaching out across the sky trying to grab something that is out of their reach and the clouds move ever so slightly, drifting aimlessly. The stars make up a perfect sky of leading lights and the moonlight shines downward on the large metropolitan below. The gorgeous reflection of the night sky blends in with that of the trees swaying in the wind. The Ronald Reagan airport’s jetliners do their daily routine, lighting up the sky with the blinking of lights. “Tonight…we teach that bastard a lesson,” The voice of a male teen, aged between 16 or 17 old, rings out. “He fucked up when he decided to mess with us.” “Yeah, but he went to prison for it. Doing a pessimistic act of crime to the man will never bring your sister back, Cliff?” Another boy’s voice comes out. “Don’t you say anything like that ever again! He deserves what he gets, Jeff. It’s as simple as that,” The teenage boy states with a firm tone in his voice. The lead teenage boy, Cliff Torstar, sits back on a twin sized bed with a brown woolly blanked draping over the sides. They are in a bedroom, easily noticed, with posters of every heavy metal band in sight. His long black bangs rest at the sides of his face, and his black tank top goes with his blue jeans. The other, Jeff Donaldson, sits at a brown chair with it turned around. His blonde hair is a lot shorter than Cliff’s, with it spiked up in the front. His legs are propped close to the bed as he watches Cliff move from the bed to a nearby dresser. “What are we gonna do to him?” Jeff asks Cliff, leaning back on the chair. He tosses a small baseball into the air repeatedly, with the same motion consistently. “Just scare him a bit…ya know?” Cliff utters kneeling down to his bottom dresser drawer. He pulls on the golden latch and watches it open with a smile. “Scare him…and how do you expect that we scare a criminal?” Jeff asks, again tossing the ball back into the air, almost nailing the ceiling fan. “With this…,” Cliff states, turning around to show a chrome Beretta handgun, with a black grip. As he does so, the baseball pegging the ceiling fan, making a loud racking noise, before rolling to the ground with scores along the sides. “Where did you get that?” Jeff says with dismay, unaware of the actions of his best friend. “From under my dad’s mattress…where else? It’s pretty cool, huh?” Cliff says with a grin, showcasing the firearm in the air. “Don’t worry; my dad never keeps it loaded. It’s just to scare the motherfucker, that’s all.” A few hours pass, and the same boys are outside on the sidewalk, walking along without much conversation. Jeff really hasn’t said much to Cliff since he brought out the revolver, not really sure about what was going to go down. “Here we are…,” Finally, Cliff broke the melancholy with a whisper after about what seemed like an hour of silence. “You positive that this is the right place?” “Of course I am…This is where it all happened. This is where Britney told us she was going the night of her………you know,” It seemed hard for Jeff not to feel bad for Cliff. -- 2 years ago in early October, Jeff’s sister, Britney went out to what was to be a party. It was all to be an enjoyable thing, but things were turned upside down. Britney never came home that night. After a three day search, Britney’s decapitated head was found embedded into a jack-o-lantern as a decoration. Jeff still hasn’t recovered from the tragic event. His sister was his best friend, and he blames himself for what had happened. Believing that he should have been there for her, he could have changed it all…but he wasn’t. Britney’s body was found in the bathtub of the accused slayer’s house. She was stripped and was assumed beaten, tortured, and finally before her last breath…decapitated. Her parents went nuts, blaming the whole world for what had happened. After an autopsy was given, they had found out even worse news. All assumptions were confirmed truthful with the right evidence, but Britney Torstar was not only tortured to death…she was sexually assaulted against her own will. The accused, Kevin Whundy, was trialled and plead guilty first degree murder. The trial didn’t last but one day. He was sentenced to the West Virginia State Prison for a period of 4 years, but was released a year and a half later, due to a good reputation. A murderer was charged with only 4 years of imprisonment. That’s basically saying that after four years, a dangerous man is going to be let back out on the streets to do the same thing over again. Four years then revolved into not even two years. Cliff and his family were crushed. Was the law upheld? Hell no it wasn’t…. *To Be Continued*
