Mission Improbable

24 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/24/02 The silence of the night is a tangible thing. No sound at all enters our ears, as stealthily, barely there at really, a form drifts through the shadows. If one didn't know better, they might think it WAS a shadow. Our mystery figure darts to a nearby wall, looking all around. Taking in the situation, it begins scaling....we pan out to see a large factory, stark against the black of the evening. Climbing, climbing, the figure makes its way to the top at last....carefully it works its way over the barbed wire netting at the top. Then fate steps in, as his foot becomes entangled in the sharp wire. Figure: Argh! The being tries to free itself, but only succeeds in further catching his ankle in the security netting. What was once deadly quiet now grows rather noisy as it grunts and groans. Figure: Son of a....argh! Argh! Dogs begin barking in the distance, awakened by the ruckus. Our stealthy one pulls and pulls, blood seeping from his pant leg but still the hellish wire holds him steady. Figure: OW! The steady hum of noise grows louder, the animals guarding growing more restless. Finally with a mighy effort our erstwhile ninja yanks himself free, toppling headfirst and backwards down the interior of the wall. Figure: AAAAAAAAAAAH! At last he crashes, splattering hard to the cold and unforgiving ground. Figure: Oooooooooh. A light then shines in his eyes. Figure: Huh? Blinking sleepily, the intruder tries to shoo away the blinding illumination....a kick in the ribs quickly brings him back to the land of the living though. 'Will you wake the hell up!?'A dainty hand reaches down, pulling our hero roughly to his feet. It then yanks his mask off, revealing none other than the demon known as Clyde. Clyde: (Rubbing his ankle) That frigging hurt. Bethany looks to her lover for a moment, her face paralyzed with rage. Then she shrieks, fuming. Bethany: DIDN'T YOU EVEN REALIZE THE FRONT GATE WAS UNLOCKED!? Clyde: Well yes, but what kind of dramatic entrance would that have been? Bethany: BAH~! The little girl swings her flashlight, cracking it straight over Clyde's skull. Once more he slinks to the ground, as she stomps off. Bethany: Come on, we've got a job to do! Crawling, the burned man follows Bethany further into the compound. They reach a window....Clyde opens it, then boosts Bethany inside. Bethany: Hurry up! Shaking his head, the demon backs up. He then takes a flying leap straight through the window, but gets stuck halfway in. Clyde: Urgh! Clyde struggles, but finds himself unable to move as Bethany holds her head. Bethany: Told you not to eat those homeless people before we came here, didn't I? Clyde: Shut up! Bethany: WILL YOU HURRY UP FATASS, WE HAVE TO GET OUT OF HERE!!!!! Clyde: I'm trying! Bethany: URGH!!!!! Bethany throws her flashlight down furiously, then stomps over and grabs Clyde by the hair. Clyde: Hey, NO!!!!Bethany: (Pulling) SHUT THE HELL UP!!!! Clyde: ARGH!!!! Yanking and tearing with all her might, the child tries to wrench the demon free of the window. At last she does so, both of them crash and skid across the floor painfully. Bethany: You.....IDIOT!!!!! Clyde: Ugh. This was your bright idea! Bethany: No it wasn't! Clyde: Was. But dammit let's go already, I want to go home and die. Again our demonic friend walks off, leaving Bethany to turn beet red and punch the air insanely. Finally after a long while the two reach seemingly their destination, a large garage. In the middle of it, a truck sits with several wooden crates lining the rear. Bethany: Well there it is, the last truck full of Poison Sobe. Clyde: Amazing how it's still here after all this time. Clyde strolls over to the driver's seat, running a hand along the outside of the vehicle. Clyde: Well, come on. The burned man opens the door, hopping into the truck. Bethany enters the passenger side, as the ignition is hit. Clyde: All in all this was easier than I thou..... Our killer's voice is cut off as suddenly every light in the building erupts to life. Blinding, intense light shines from every direction, as heavily armored SWAT personnel appear on seemingly every inch of the Sobe factory. Voice: CLYDE! THIS IS A STING! PUT YOUR HANDS IN THE AIR, RIGHT NOW! Clyde: .......well that was unexpected. Bethany: DRIVE YOU FOOL!!!!! Clyde: It's a stick, I don't know how to drive a stick.Bethany: YOU'VE BEEN ALIVE 2000 SOME ODD YEARS AND YOU DON'T KNOW HOW TO DRIVE A STICK!?!!? Clyde: I've gotten used to just being wherever I need to be, dear, like all good psycho murderers. Bethany: BAH~!!!!!! Bethany throws herself to the left, stomping the gas pedal with all her might just as a hail of gunfire rips through the building. Clyde: AGH! G-force causes the demon to be thrown back in his seat, as the truck jets forward straight into the door of the parking garage. With a terrible wrench of steel the vehicle crashes straight through it, breaking it into a million pieces. Bethany: STEER YOU BABOON!!!!! Clyde: DON'T TELL ME HOW TO DRIVE!!!! Bethany: URGH!!!!!! The truck swerves drunkenly to the right and to the left, gaining speed and making its shaky way into the distance as a platoon of officers give chase into the night and we mercifully fade to black.......
