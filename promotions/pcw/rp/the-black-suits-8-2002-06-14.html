The Black Suits [8]

14 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: June 14, 2002 The elevator doors were opened wide enough for me to squeeze through, although I felt a bit uncomfortable doing so. Squeezing through made me vulnerable to an attack, but there really wasn't another way. I made my way through the doors as sleekly as I could, and quickly had the gun pointed forward once more. That's when I heard gunfire and the familiar shriek of Sporty....they were nearby. I didn't want to rush, but I also thought that being hasty would help the situation a bit. A flash of light caught my eye, drawing me towards a door on my left. I looked at a sign on the wall, which read, 'Containment Facility.' I ran inside, rifle pointed outwards, and tried to assess the situation. Chopsticks was on the ground, firing his pistol at Sporty, who was flying above the center storage lock. Sourpuss was leaning against tthe wall, holding his arm whcih was bleeding, and he looked very hurt. He nor Chopsticks had noticed that I had come in yet. I thought that I saw the crystal in Sporty's hand, but I realized that he hadn't even gotten into the storage chamber yet. Chopsticks and Sourpuss had kept him busy. I wasn't one to be left out of this kind of fun, so I aimed and fired my rifle. Most of the shots went wild, just catching his attention, although one bullet caught him int he arm. He looked at me surprised, and shrieked, forcing me to wince at the high pitched scream. He swooped in towards Chopsticks, knocking the gun out of his hand and firing rights and lefts at him. He looked helpless, and he would be beaten to death if I didn't intervene. I fired the rifle again, this time risking Chopsticks life if I missed by an inch. The shots caught Sporty straight in the back, and he fell backwards, flying again in the air. I fired aimlessly, trying to desperately to take out his wings, but he moved out of my sight before I could do so. The gun jammed, and I threw the gun to the ground in frustration. I ran over to Sourpuss, who was still dazed. When I reached him, he was still thinking that I was Chopsticks. I suppose all asians look alike, especially when you were near unconscious. 'Chopsticks, I'm hurt bad...' He said, trying to put his arm around me. I wasn't the touchy feely type with men, so I brushed his arm away. 'You're going to be alright,' I replied, using my most reassuring tone. I touched the wound where he was hurt, and saw bone pointing outwards. The shoulder had been popped out of place, and bone was sticking it. Not a good sign. I suppose the sound of my voice had been enough to tip Sourpuss off, because he soon realized that I was not Chopsticks. 'Who are you? You aren't Chopsticks...aww hell, Lexian? What in god's name are you doing here?' 'Shut up and relax,' I snapped. 'Take this gun. If he comes near you, just fire at him.' He shook his head and laughed, as if I had said something humorous. 'Kid, bullets don't hurt him. They hardly even slow him down. If he comes near me, I'm dead. You're dead. We're all dead. The only consolation is, the second the crystal leaves the storage compartment, this entire facility is going up in smoke.' 'I'm not ready to die,' I said firmly. I had meant it, although I wasn't sure if I would be able to back it up. 'You can try, but sometimes life is a bitch. And bitches don't care if you deserve it or not, kid. That's why they're bitches.' I ignored the last comment, still clinging on to the thought that I still had control over my own destiny. Not him, not Chopsticks, and certainly not some over grown butterfly was going to change that for me. As I turned around, I noticed that Chopsticks had regained control of his gun, and was firing it at Sporty, who taken another round around the storage compartment. He came in fast and quick, avoiding all of the bullets that he had fired. He knocked the gun out of his hand and pushed him into the wall. I was almost certain that Chopsticks had a concussion now, and he just layed, slumped against the wall. Sporty looked like he was going in for the kill...I wasn't about to let him do that. I pulled the uzi out of my pants and took of the safety. I sized up my target and pulled the trigger, and watched as a wave of bullets struck Sporty. They were like mini icepicks being embedded into his back, and he reeled back as they caught him in the back and upper chest region. I never let go of the trigger, continuing to pour as much lead as I could possibly pump in to him. He roared, taking to the air once more. I tried to keep the gun on him, but the guy was avoiding ten bullets a second rushing past his face. It was almost a slow motion action film, and he knew exactly where each bullet was going to be placed next. Once he was wirhin ten feet of me, I knew I was in trouble. That's when the uzi ran out of ammo, and he slapped the gun of my hand, taking me into the air with him. I wanted to scream, but I was too shocked to open my mouth. He was all around me...his scent, his breath, his arrogance...I looked down, realizing that I was well over twenty feet in the air. If he dropped me from here, I wasn't sure if I would be able to catch myself. But if I was going to go, I sure as hell was going to take him with me. I owed him that much. I pulled the Raid canister from my pocket, trying to keep a grip on it as I felt him loosening his hold on me. He was going to drop me soon. That's when I sprayed him in the face with the Raid. He screamed, as if his face were on fire, and wound up dropping me anyways. I hit the floor with incredible impact, feeling my back jump into my throat and my legs collapse beside me. I wasn't sure if anything was broken, but I was hurt bad. I looked up to see Sporty flying erratically....suddenly, he just began to drop in midair. While dropping, a creature ripped from the buddy, fluttering away as the body hit the ground with a loud splat. It was a colorful creature, which looked at me for a moment, and then flew out of the door. I then closed my eyes and prepared to meet whomever was on the other side.
