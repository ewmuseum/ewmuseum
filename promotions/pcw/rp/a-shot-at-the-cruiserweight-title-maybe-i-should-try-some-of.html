A shot at the Cruiserweight Title? Maybe I should try some of that 'anti-training.' It worked for TMM. ;-)

Date: Can&#039;t remember The End sits alone inside his super-secret-mansion that everybody knows about. With him are his Hispanic stunt-double, Julio, who is played by a naked (save for some black Hanes boxers), and Ving Rhames in black skin-tight shirt and pants. His boots are of military cut. Julio can only speak Spanish, while Ving Rhames wiill be playing the silent black sidekick, Henry. Hear that, Jordan X Shabazz? Black people should be seen and not heard. Also here are The End's white cameraman Will, played by Johnny Knoxville, his bodyguard Anasha played by Jessica Alba in her usual Dark Angel attire (yum), and scientist Melody (we got Elizabeth Shue in her lab outfit from Hollow Man). The End is punching a red punching bag suspended from the ceiling by a chain, held steady by Henry. The bag is Julio. Julio: *In pain, broken speech*'{Something in Spanish?}' End:'You're the stunt-double, so you're paid to take this kind of abuse!' Julio:'{Something in Spanish!}' Will:'He's got a point there. You don't pay him to take abuse at your hands.' The End ceases his punches, though to be honest, he didn't do any damage. Julio must pretend to be hurt. The End, however, is exhausted. End:'Very well, let's take a break.' Henry unwraps Julio, who falls to the floor clutching his ribs. Before End can walk off, Henry taps him on the shoulder, and then stares at him without saying anything. Henry:'....' End:'Well, if you're bored, go watch TV.' Henry goes over to the red 'Friends' sofa that sits in front of the 27-inch Daewoo TV. Picking up the remote, he turns it on and goes channel surfing. End goes into the kitchen and fixes himself a marshmallow and chocolate chip sandwich, with honey buns substituting for bread. He also gets a gallon of GMC's Weight-Gainer 5 Billion. Anasha comments on his choice of food. Anasha:'Given that your career doesn't ever get any further than the Light-Heavyweight Championship, you might want to take a little more pride in getting a shot at it.' End:'I pay you to protect and support me, and to beat the living hell out of me in sparring, not offer those negative opinions. If you're disagreeable one more time, I'll dock your pay.' Anasha:'You haven't paid us in months.' The End takes a bite of his sandwich and drinks the Weight-Gainer. End:'Good point.' Henry comes running in the room, screaming at the top of his lungs. Henry:'AAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAARRRRRGGGGHHH!' Henry collapses onto the ground and curls into the fetal position, sucking his thumb. Anasha kicks Henry while he's down. Anasha:'Melody, how many times do I have to tell you -- If you want to offer a man sex, use the vaginal soap I gave you first!' Melody looks up from the machine she's tinkering with. Melody:'Screw you! I'm getting ready for Hollow Man 2, I don't need this!' Will breaks in. Will:'Hey, how come Hollow Man was supposed to be an invisible man-movie, but there was always water or smoke or heat-signatures or something to let you see him? That's pretty pointless.' Anasha returns to making fun of Melody. Anasha:'Your next movie is going to bomb, I guarantee it!' Melody drums up the courage to stand up and yell at Anasha. Melody:'Oh yeah? I'll personally blow you if my movie doesn't succeed!' Will:'You did, remember?' Melody:'Oh, yeah. Last New Year's Eve. I'm still wanted in 48 states.' Then, she withdraws and talks quietly to herself. End sits down to watch the TV that Henry was watching. End:'So, Will, you're sure that my last promo won't upset the blind people?' Will:'Maybe. But you're blind, and you're not offended.' End:'Perhaps. And when I was asked about Hiro Saotome, and I said that we should have nuked all the dirty Japs when we had the chance?' Will:'Well, ignoring the fact that you're half-Japanese, that might've offended some people.' The End jumps up from the couch. End:'Why didn't you tell me?' Will:'You got really into it and started acting like you were a soldier in the Army in 1944. Didn't want to spoil your fun.' End:'But I could have all five important Japanese people in America angry at me!' Will:'Well, at least you'll be safe from your derogatory comments against the Amish.' End:'Yeah, there's no way in Hell they'll know.' A sigh comes from End. His body language suggests a deep breath, and only his mouth shown from a hole in his mask shows it. End:'But if I walk down the street and get pelted with Sony CD players or Bibles, I'm blaming you.' The End eats more of his food. Melody comments on his nutritional habits. Melody:'Really, you should eat better. This stuff'll get you all fat. End:'That's the point! I've only got to gain 6 pounds before I'm over the Light-Heavyweight limit of 230 lb. This sandwich is fine. There's got to be nearly a million calories in this one snack.' Anasha:'Especially when you added the lard to it.' End:'It's for flavor!' The Fattest Man in the World walks into the picture. FMW:'You should eat healthier.' Everyone stops and looks at The Fattest Man in the World. He feels very awkward. After a few moments, everyone, even Julio, bears down on him with punches and kicks. With The Fattest Man in the World knocked out, The End turns his attention to Julio, who returns to his spot on the floor. End:'Julio, I keep telling you to lock the door!' Julio: *Weakly*'{Something... in... Spanish.}' End:'He's got a point there; I did tie him up and bring him down here before he could do his check of the doors and windows. But I needed to anti-train. I've got a big match coming up against Hiro Saotome and two other people for the #1 contendership to the Cruiserweight Title. Who are my other opponents, anyway?' No one seems to know, just shrugging and going back to whatever they were doing. Will knows, and tells The End. Will:'Other than Hiro Saotome--' The End interrupts his cameraman. End:'Dirty Jap.' Anasha walks over and smacks The End upside the head. Anasha:'You're Japanese!' The End pretends as though he didn't feel the smack. End:'Oh, right! I keep forgetting since it's not important to me, like all Japanese people.' A barrage of Sony CD players hits The End. He covers up to protect from the blows. End:'Alright, alright! I'm sorry!' The pelting stops. End:'Geez.' Will continues his briefing. Will:'You've also got Kramer WildWing and Kid Krazie.' The End scarfs down the rest of his sandwich and chugs his Weight-Gainer 5 Billion. Wiping his mouth, he adds in his speech. End:'Kramer WildWing? Isn't he in with the KFC people? Maybe I can use him to get access to greasy, fatty fried chicken. What can Kid Krazie offer me?' Will:'A dented shopping cart named Lulu.' The End cocks his head to the side, curious about Kid Krazie. End:'Is Lulu a Winn-Dixie or Publix shopping cart?' Will:'I believe it's a Food Lion cart.' The End shoots up from his chair. End:'Food Lion? The punk b*tch of the grocery industry? He must die!' The End sits back down. End:'Anyway, what was Henry watching?' End turns the channel. It's a new commercial featuring Bob Dole in a speedo, talking about his increased sexual energy thanks to Viagra. Everyone's face goes pale, and fear comes across it. 'FEAR' is written on End's mask, to show it in addition to his quivering mouth. End:'AAAAARRRRGGGHH!' Will:'AAAAARRRRGGGHH!' Anasha:'AAAAARRRRGGGHH!' Melody:'AAAAARRRRGGGHH!' Julio:'AAAAARRRRGGGHH!' Everyone runs into the kitchen with Henry and falls into the fetal position, rocking side-to-side and sucking on their thumbs.
