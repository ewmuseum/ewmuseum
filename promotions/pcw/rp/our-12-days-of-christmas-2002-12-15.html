Our 12 days of Christmas

15 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12-15-02 RP 1 for PPV 22nd Say, Abner, what you want for Christmas? Our beloved trio lounge comfortably in Teresa’s private cabin in the hills of Tennessee. There is show in Knoxville on Sunday and she couldn’t pass up the opportunity to go home for a few days. Teresa, Abner and Duke are enjoying the warmth of a fire and the refreshing taste of Jack Daniel’s and a Papa John’s Pizza. Abner has the couch, Duke is comfortable on the loveseat and Teresa has tackled the Lazy Boy recliner. On the TV is a Titans football game. Abner spits some dip in an empty Mt. Dew bottle then answers Teresa. Aww shit Tomas, ya know what I want fer Christmas. Both Duke and Teresa give Abner an anticipated stare. Teresa takes a swig of her bottle and belches causing Duke to avert his attention to her in surprise that a female has the balls to belch so loud. Teresa merely smirks proudly and licks her lips before reaching for another slice of pizza. Looking back to Abner, Duke finally speaks. Well Hoss, spill the beans. Tell the lady what ya want for Christmas. Abner sits up from his slouch and rests his arms on the back of the couch. He then clears his throat and begins to sing quite drunkenly. Parts to my Mustang GT! A moment of silence passes between the three rednecks. On the television, the Titans score a touchdown. Teresa shakes her head at Abner’s response and Duke slides his black Stetson down over his eyes. Teresa finally speaks. Abner man, you’re drunk. You ain’t got a Mustang. Abner shrugs and the Malice Man’s voice rises continuing in the song. On the second day of Christmas, my true love gave to me TWO HUNTIN’ DAWGS… Abner continues then Teresa ends up joining in. …AND PARTS TO MY MUSTANG GT! On the third day of Christmas, my true love gave to me THREE SHOTGUN SHELLS.. TWO HUNTIN’ DAWGS.. AND PARTS TO MY MUSTANG GT! On the fourth day of Christmas, my true love gave to me FOUR MUDGRIP TIRES.. THREE SHOTGUN SHELLS TWO HUNTIN’ DAWGS AND PARTS TO MY MUSTANG GT! On the fifth day of Christmas my true love gave to me The three sing in unison FIVE FLANNEL SHIRTS! FOUR MUDGRIP TIRES.. THREE SHOTGUN SHELLS TWO HUNTIN’ DAWGS AND PARTS TO MY MUSTANG GT! Whoa! Hold on a sec. This is getting kinda long and I’m nearly out of Jack. Teresa brings the black labeled bottle to her lips and throws her head back to finish the bottle. Not wanting a female to out drink them, Duke and Abner does the same with their own bottles then Teresa continues with the usual drunken slur. TWELVE PACKS OF BUD ELEVEN WRASSLIN’ TICKETS WOOOO~! TIN OF COPENHAGEN NINE YEARS PROBATION EIGHT TABLE DANCERS WOOO WHOOO! I’m getting the good ones! SEVEN PACKS OF REDMAN Hey Hoss, I want the table dancers! SIX CANS OF SPAM The three sing in drunken unison FIVE FLANNEL SHIRTS! FOUR MUDGRIP TIRES THREE MUDGRIP TIRES TWO HUNTIN’ DAWGS AND PARTS TO MY MUSTANG GEEEEEEE…TEEEEE! Our favorite rednecks take in a deep breath having finished the song and stare at the last piece of pizza. All three each can taste the greasy cheese and sauce coating the inside of their mouth. Taking their eyes off the single slice of pizza, they look to each other in silent communication. This time Duke breaks the silence. Take it Teresa. You’re the lady in the room. Teresa shakes her head. Nah, I can’t take it. You take it. You bought the damn thing. Teresa and Duke continue this debate on who will eat the last slice of pizza. In the meantime, Abner as taken this advantage to his favor. Our Director of Redneck Affairs quietly picks up the slice and savors every bite as he watches Teresa and Duke continue arguing over the now eaten slice.
