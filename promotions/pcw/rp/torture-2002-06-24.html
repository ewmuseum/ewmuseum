Torture = $$$~!

24 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 06-24-02 (A black screen is seen as the following white text appears.) A week ago PCW World Champion Big Alan Steele was cleverly deceived, kidnapped, and taken off to a secret compound where he would find himself in a physically and emotionally draining ordeal for 3 days. The tortures and interrogations that took place were documented and filmed. With the recent and unfortunate escape of Big Alan Steele from his captors, the eye-opening footage has been de-classified for your enjoyment. ...And will soon be released on DVD--uncut and uncensored with special guest commentary by the actual genius behind the plan Frisco! Friscosity Productions now presents to you a sneak peak of this spectacular DVD with the bonus commentary included! The following segment was taped on the 2nd day of captivity at approximately 2:49PM. The full 3 day agony fest will be available on July 1st and will contain bonus footage of the kidnapping itself as well as Big Alan Steele being severely injured at the hands of Frisco at the Gold and Glory Pay-Per-View on June 30th. Enjoy. (Without any fanfare or well-produced introductions, we are taken directly to Big Alan Steele sitting on a small wooden chair and bound with heavy chains. The demi-god hangs his head and stares at the ground with a distressed, but far from intimidated look on his face. Sweat drips off his face and hair and onto the floor with consistency. Only eerie silence can be heard at this moment in the compact, sterile, bright white room that looks more like a doctor's office than a torture chamber.) (Frisco's commentary over the scene can now be heard and suggests something has happened moments earlier.) Frisco: *Chuckling* 'Now, that originally wasn't my idea, I'll admit. My dear Secretary of Central American Affairs and Tacos came up with that after he had a little too much to drink. Needless to say, I was all up for it when I heard how immensely cruel it would be. Unfortunately, I didn't care much for Big Al's reaction. It left too much to the imagination and I'm not totally sure it worked. I would've preferred to see a scream or a whimper. As always, Big Al ruins yet another potentially good piece of entertainment.' (Some voices and squeaking is heard in the interrogation room. BAS raises his head slightly to see what the commotion is all about. He quickly shakes his drenched hair to get it out of his eyes and stares unfazed at whatever is about to take place.) Frisco: 'This upcoming scene was thought up prior to the the kidnapping. We weren't entirely sure if we were going to go through with it, but since we had the materials and staff, we decided to try it out and see what would happen. I was pleasantly surprised.' (A man in a lion suit walks into the interrogation suit and in front of the chained hero. BAS glares at the vision with a confused look.) Big Alan Steele: 'Eh?' Nemean Lion: 'I am not dead, Steele. You must fight me!' Big Alan Steele: 'What kind of preposterous offal is this?' Nemean Lion: 'I am.....' (The man in the costume scratches his head.) Nemean Lion: 'Line!' (A voice off screen shouts something. The character nods.) Nemean Lion: 'I am the Nemean Lion!' (The lion growls. Big Alan Steele cracks a barely visible smile.' Big Alan Steele: 'Dimwitted mortal, you are a mere thespian in an elaborate costume. Besides, I am not Hercules. You're looking for Hercules, and I believe he finished his labors long, long ago, so you, my friend, are superfluous.' Nemean Lion: 'What!?' (Frisco's commentary comes out once again.) Frisco: 'I was beginning to worry about this, but luckily our Nemean Lion came up with a brilliant plan. Listen in.' (The lion sighs and looks around.) Nemean Lion: 'Fuck! This is not working.' Big Alan Steele: 'Watch your tongue, aspiring young thespian. You shan't get anywhere with such an offensive demeanor.' Nemean Lion: 'We need some more drugs here! They've worn off!' Crewmember: 'Where are the druggers!? Get 'em in here!' (The man in the lion costume walks out of the room shaking his head and mumbling. Frisco's commentary picks up once again.) Frisco: 'It's all smooth sailing from here....for the most part. We figured that Big Al was much to smart to fall for a man in a costume, so my scientists came up with a precise calculation of the type and quality of drugs needed to make Steele believe that our actor was, in fact, the legendary Nemean Lion himself. Unfortunately, Mr. Steele is exceedingly healthy and the drugs tended to wear off easier than we had hoped. Thus, our scene didn't go as perfect as it could've.' (Back in the room, a few persons in white cloaks and a brown briefcase surround Big Alan Steele and inject him with drugs of some kind. After a few quick checks of pulse rate and pupils, the doctors leave the room and the costumed man walks back in. Big Alan Steele looks up with his half-opened glazed over eyes and jerks his head back.) BAS: 'By the Gods! You....' (The Nemean Lion snarls.) BAS: 'This cannot be! You were slain long ago! What evil spirit from Hades hast come alive in your monstrous body!?' (Frisco's voice pops up.) Frisco: 'See? Now, that's more like it. Drugs make a much more exciting, entertaining Big Al. Perhaps we have given him an addiction thereby making him someone worth paying attention to. If so, you all may thank me for saving his career.' (The Nemean Lion's voice comes out normally and unintimidating.) Nemean Lion: 'Steele!..erm..goddamnit!' (The lion smacks his costume and a speaker pops loudly. The lion resumes speaking, this time in a loud, booming voice. Steele frowns slightly.) Nemean Lion: 'Steele! I am indeed the Nemean Lion. Hercules failed his labor and I have defeated him!' (Big Al shakes his head with his eyes watering from the drugs and unbelievable revelation.) BAS: 'Lies! Lies! Lies!' Nemean Lion: 'Behold my triumph!' (The man in the costume looks around and waits.) Nemean Lion: 'Ahem!' (Big Al looks up at the strange lion confusedly. The lion taps his foot on the ground impatiently and looks around.) Nemean Lion: 'I said...BEHOLD MY TRIUMPH!' (A panel from the ceiling opens and a latex skin and model skull of Hercules falls to the ground. The lion snorts and walks over to it. Big Alan Steele looks on in horror.) (Frisco's commentary butts in briefly.) Frisco: 'Again, another problem here. The sound in the room was not piped in to the crew upstairs for some reason and the cue was not heard. I was not happy about this at all, but fortunately the scene did not go to waste. Seems to me as if the planning into the actual kidnapping was set up much better than the subsequent interrogation. Practice makes perfect. of course. We intend to plan things out much better for our next abduction.' (The Nemean Lion hoists the fake skin and severed head onto his body. The jaw of the drugged hero drops and his lips tremble. His voice is forced out almost painfully.) Big Alan Steele: 'You shall pay for this...vile beast! Hi......Kee....ba!' (Big Al tries to fight out of the heavy chains, but finds himself depleted of his strength and energy. The restrained champion breathes heavily and hangs his head low, sweating even more profusely.) Nemean Lion: 'Hahaha! Pathetic weakling....you will now...umm...' (The absent minded actor pauses and grumbles.) Nemean Lion: 'Line!' (The script supervisor off stage yells out the line.) Script Supervisor: 'Pay for all of your dead brother's failures! Zeus commands it!' Nemean Lion: 'Yeah...Suess commands it!' Script Supervisor: 'No, Zeus! Zeus! You idiot!' Nemean Lion: 'No, Zeus! Zeus! You idiot.' Script Supervisor: 'Ugh.' (Big Alan Steele shakes his head and groans at a headache that seems to be coming from the actions of this strange talking lion and the mutilated corpse of Hercules he wears atop his back.) (Frisco pipes in again.) Frisco: 'This actor sucks a fat one. Granted he had 4 hours to learn the script, but this guy was horrible. The only reason he got the part is because he owned the costume--which you can clearly tell is the Cowardly Lion's costume from the Wizard of Oz. A fucking travesty, I know, but I'm a busy man and I don't have time to check accuracies. As long as a drugged up Big Alan Steele would fall for it, I would consider it a success.' Big Alan Steele: 'Unchain me, Zeus!...so...I...may...slay...this creature!' (Meanwhile, the Nemean Lion circles Big Alan Steele and growls at him. BAS tries to stay alert and fend off the beast with his wet locks of hair.) Nemean Lion: 'I shall make you suffer and finally cast you into the arms of Hades!' Big Alan Steele: 'Never! Hi-Kee..ahhhhgggghhhh!!!' (The Nemean Lion wraps his paws around BAS' thick neck and chokes him with all his might. Steele groans and gurgles, but cannot free himself from the chains so he may defeat the haunting image which throttles him.) Frisco: 'Originally, we thought of strangling BAS with his own toga, but we decided to let the man do all the strangling. As you can see, he's fairly big. Not too bright, but he has enough force to make for an impressive strangling beast.' (Gasping and gargling can still be heard as Frisco comments.) Frisco: 'I love the sound effects here. I thought Big Al did a good job of choking and gasping for air. I had figured he had practice with having his air cut off by various members entering his throat, but he really surprised me here. For such a piss poor prisoner who had an overall awful performance, he really shined here. It made the bumbling actor and the production mishaps all worth while.' (Frisco claps as BAS has seemingly passed out from the lack of oxygen to his brain. The Nemean Lion stops and looks around. He then rolls up his sleeve to check his watch.) Nemean Lion: 'Alright, he's out of it. May I get the hell out of this place now?' Crewmember: 'Yes, you are free to go.' (The actor walks out of the room while BAS' red face hangs low with a thin stream of drool dripping onto his chest from his pale lips. Some commotion is heard as an oxygen tank is brought into the room. Big Al is generously given oxygen and checked on by a few nurses.) Frisco: 'To anyone who says I'm a vicious fiend who doesn't care about human life, I can't tell you how wrong you are. As you can see, I care even about my opponents...as deluded and braindead as they may be. I was worried when I received word that Big Al was unconscious. I was scared and I wanted to make sure he was alright. After all, god forbid he dies in my hands and the World Title goes vacant. That would be horrible! It would mean a tournament for the title instead of me slaughtering Steele for it. We certainly cannot have that.' (With a sniff of smelling salts, the enduring hero picks his head up and groans. Just as quick as he has passed out, Big Al regains consciousness and is seemingly alert and healthy, but he is subsequently injected with more drugs by the staff who are standing by. BAS groans and shakes his head at the room that must be spinning in his view. He seems to choke back some vomit and twitches violently. With a gulp and a huge expulsion of breath, BAS jerks his head up towards the heavens and screams in a slightly slurred and scratchy voice.) Big Alan Steele: 'Zeus, my..God...my father! What have I done!?' (Frisco's off screen voice cackles.) Frisco: 'Oh, man. I loved this part right here!' (A speaker attached to the ceiling pops slightly and a booming voice comes from above.) Zeus: 'STEELE!' (Big Al's eyelids almost peel off his face as he gasps at the voice coming seemingly from nowhere.) BAS: 'Father!?' (Steele tries to look around the confined room to see the voice of the King of the Gods.) Zeus: 'You know why you are here.' Big Alan Steele: 'I do not, father! Why am I confronted with such threatening, harmful visions in this abhorrent prison? What have I done to fail the Gods?' Zeus: 'You didn't take out the trash when your mother told you to.' Big Alan Steele: 'She is not my mother!' Zeus: 'Boy! Don't make me hurl a thunderbolt at your ass!' Big Alan Steele: 'Bu-bu-but father!' Zeus: 'I am not your father, Steele. You are adopted and I don't love you. You are a disgrace to every demigod and hero alike. This is because you are a mortal. You always have been and you are destined to die a mortal's death....most likely at the hands of Frisco when he takes your title next Sunday.' (BAS' bloodshot, vacant eyes stare at the ceiling as he tries to hold himself back from believing this twisted experience that has presented itself to the drugged mind he can't control.) (Frisco's commentary picks up again.) Frisco: 'Is that not a great piece of drama right there? I could tell I was really fucking his mind in the ass when he heard he was 100% mortal. I'm surprised he didn't just snap right that instant. At any rate, the almighty Zeus speaks the truth. By the time this DVD is released, I will be the PCW World Champ again, and Big Al will most likely be at his home, morbidly obese, and vigorously stroking his Hydra to my magnificent title reign.' (Just as Frisco's comment ends, BAS is taken away from the room by 2 of Frisco's armed Argentinean militiamen. The sneak preview of the DVD feature cuts off and is once again followed by a black screen with white text.)
