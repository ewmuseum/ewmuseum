Golden Heart Chronicles: Mind Over Matter (Chapter 3 Part 3)

10 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/10/02 Golden Heart Chronicles Chapter 3 Part 3: The first two chapters of the Golden Heart chronicles were the insight to the life of Juan Borro. Through these chronicles we were able to experience what Juan went through to lead to how he turned out now. The first two chapters in the Golden Heart Chronicles are where something sparks “flashbacks” during Juan’s present day life to take us back to his childhood. In chapter three and it’s following parts will focus on Juan and his relationship with being a fighter. He puts other things behind him and works on becoming a determined fighter. Everyone Read and enjoy the final part to chapter three. Time: 11:47 A.MLocation: HollandScene: Warriors Gym (Mind over Strength always Prevail) THE FINALE The mind is a powerful thing. You may not know it, but you use it more than anything. Everything you do is controlled by your mind. You can thank your whole existence on that one little tool. Sure there are other things that can try and defeat it, but it doesn’t really help. Nothing else can possibly help you as much as your mind. The same type of thing accounts for wrestling. People try to defeat you with strength and power. You become intimidated and scared, knowing you’re the smaller man and not having many things in your advantage. Do not worry though, because if you can use your mind to your advantage, no size or amount of strength can ever stop you. You see, that my friend is a fact. The scene opens with Juan once again entering Warriors gym this is the second time Juan has been to the gym, with the first time not being the greatest type of experience. Juan is hoping this one is better, and is only coming back so he doesn’t seem rude. I know what you’re thinking. You’re all probably wondering why Juan is feeling bad about being rude. Well you see Juan has been going through attitude changes. Juan has always tried to act like a wise and mature man lately, but deep inside he was really the same little Boy. Only a little boy feels he has to hate everyone different from him and be angry toward outsiders. Juan has grown out of his ways and detached himself from his fellow partners and stable mates. All of that is beside the point though. The point is Juan has grown out of his old naïve immature ways. With this Juan hopes to become a better person and receive more of his goals in life through it. Juan has been having a great deal of luck lately though, even though some of it has sparked through bad events. I mean he’s won the cruiserweight title, tag titles twice, and has had an incredible record throughout his whole stay in PCW, but he still feels it was never really earned. All of this thinking seemed to have sparked after Juan’s first match with Rent-A-Hero. That match made Juan Borro feel like he finally deserved something. Juan is used to fighting through all odds to win. He loved that feeling, and that’s the feeling he felt when he first faced RAH. Juan’s (GOAL~!) now is to remain successful in his future endeavors against the worthy and maybe challenging Deadly Dustin Downy, which would lead to a shot at RAH. Juan wants the Cruiserweight title. He wants the feeling of independence. Not only does he wants to be known as the champion, but he wants to be known as a fighter, or in this case a warrior. Warrior sounds so triumphant and there is nothing he would love more than that. Juan needs this title. He needs it to lift his career and his self of steam, and believe me Juan will get it. Not through run-ins or foul play, but right down the line. Because when you cheat life like Juan has in the past, you cheat yourself. Back at Warriors gym, Juan is entering the room of Hans Daigen the man who has offered his assistance to train Juan. Juan is quite spectacle, but hopes that everything will turn out for the best. One thing Juan did notice was that when he was at the front desk, Hans made sure to leave a note that this visit was completely free. Not like Juan cares about money, but it shows Hans wants to help him rather than just take his money. Juan enters the gym and sees Hans sitting there mumbling some words to himself. Juan notices and doesn’t want to be rude so he starts to walk back into the waiting area of the room. Hans hears Juan’s footsteps, and quickly calls over to stop him. “Stop! Come over here and sit right over by me, Juan.” Hans says with his eyes still closed, in a meditating type position. Juan doesn’t know how he knew it was him, but decides to not ask any questions and just do what Hans says. “Sorry, I didn’t know if you wanted to be alone or something.” Juan says while bending over and taking a seat on the mat. Hans opens on eye to make sure if Juan is securely seated. He then looks over to Juan and give him a serious stare dead in the eyes. “What’s wrong is their something on my face, or do you just want to kill me?” Juan says while feeling his forehead making sure he has nothing on it. He then stares back at Hans a little confused. “You see, I’m looking into you’re eyes in search of maybe a real warriors.” Hans says concentrating heavily. “Well what do you see?” Juan responds. “I see a scared young man not wanting to come out of his shell. Deep down inside of you I do say a warriors, but that warrior has to come out.” Hans says while getting out of his position and standing up. “So how am I supposed to do that?” Juan replies being a little confused. “Just stand up and follow me. I’ll show you exactly how you’re going to do it.” Hans says as he escort Juan over to another room. When they enter the room, Hans bring Juan toward an ordinary punching bag and stands by it. He points at it and tells Juan: “That’s where you start my friend. Take a few punches at it.” Juan does like commanded for a few minutes, but doesn’t really see how it matter to his (GOAL~!) at all. “I don’t get what this is doing for me.” Juan says bored of punching the bag. “Just keep going” Hans replies. After another few minutes Hans take a hard ball right out of his pocket. When Juan isn’t looking at him, he chucks it right at Juan’s head. Juan notices it and reacts quickly by lifting his leg and kicking the ball away. Juan then puts his hands over his head and looks over at Hans, with his heart beating quickly not knowing what’s going on. “What was that for? You could have killed me.” Juan says enraged. “You see I could have killed you, but you’re inner warrior wouldn’t let me. Right when you think you’re screwed your warrior stops it. Now just imagine how powerful you would be if you could always be that warrior. I mean if you could always have the warrior come out, no one would be able to stop you.” Hans says while directing Juan to a new room and area. “But I can’t seem to get it out. The only time its there is when I’m really frightened or something like that.” Juan replies unsure of him self. “That’s what I’m here for.” Hans says as he gives a big smile. Hans leads Juan into the next room with a big ring in it. Juan looks at the ring and is impressed. “You see that’s my little ring over there. I’m going to get some padding on and were going to fight.” Hans says as he enters the ring. “Wait, I’m going to fight you. Not to be mean, but you’re kind of overweight and older than me.” Juan says not wanting to be insulting. “Well I’m two hundred and eighty pounds, so I am rather large. What I want from you is to lift me over your head and drop me.” Hans says completely serious. Juan just laughs. “ You’re way too big and I don’t want to hurt you,” Juan says still laughing. Instead of replying Hans just hits Juan in the head. Juan doesn’t feel he needs to fight Hans so he stands there. Hans keep doing it and it start to get Juan mad. Right before Juan breaks, Hans kicks him as hard as he can right in the leg. Juan finally becomes full Juan finally becomes full enraged as he grabs and throws him overhead arms like a sack of potatoes. He then lifts him high in the air and drops him right on the mat. “That’s what you get.” Juan screams after being pushed to the edge. To Juan’s surprise Hans begins to applaud Juan’s actions. “Great job. I told you could pick me up over your head. Now I want you to do it again, even harder.” Hans says with a straight face. “Wow! I guess I did, but I’m not mad anymore.” Juan replies. “Make yourself mad. I don’t care how you do it, but just do it.” Hans says as he gets back to his feet. Juan builds up the rage and does the same exact move even more perfectly. Juan Is stoked that he actually did it and feels a real satisfaction. “THAT WAS GREAT!” Juan replies ecstatically. “Great, that means you’ll be even more ready for our next step.” Hans says as he walks up to the next floor.” Juan follows still happy about this past accomplishment. Then reach the upstairs level, where there is a balcony about fifteen feet over the ring. “What’s this all about?” Juan ask a bit skeptically. “This is the big step, where you get to trust in yourself.” Hans says answering Juan’s question. “How do I do that?” Juan replies. “What’s you’re best top rope move?” Hans asks. “Probably a 540 splash I guess, why?” Juan says still confused. “Well you see I out a fake doll about the size of another wrestler down by the ring. I want you to get on this balcony and execute that move on him.” Hans says as he begins waiting for Juan to go. “Are you crazy, I’ll kill myself!” Juan scream thinking Hans is out of his mind. “Just do it. Trust and me and most importantly trust in yourself.” Hans replies still waiting for Juan to make the jump. Juan shakes his head, but still steps up to the balcony to set up to the insane move. Juan takes a big gulf and breathes out. He then takes the leap, which feels like it was an eternity long. When Juan reached the bottom he opened his eyes and noticed he was on top of the body, and nothing was even damaged on his body. “Holy Sh*t! Did I just do that?” Juan screams. “You sure did.” Hans says as he makes his way back downstairs. “You’re inner warrior did that all. You don’t have to thank me for what you just did. I just got the real you out in the open.” Hans says as he gives Juan a pat in the back. “That was crazy!” Juan replies still fully enjoyed. “Well I have to get going to meet up with my next customer. It’s been great.” Hans says as he shakes Juan’s hand. “But this is my last day here then I have to leave. What will I do without you here? You’re my best trainer I’ve ever had.” Juan says not wanting to go. “Oh Juan you don’t need me or any trainer. You did everything on your own. Now that you know what to do, nothing is left for me to teach. Good luck and live up to the name of WARRIORS GYM.” Hans says as he exits. The scene fades with Juan just standing there looking up and smiling, before he finally exits. THE END The Following promo was paid for and publicized by the Golden Heart Chronicles.
