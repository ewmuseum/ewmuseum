The Tank 5 - Enter Lion

10 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: July 10, 2002 Tuesday, July 9 - Day 4 Miggy lies motionless, but conscious, in his white bed in the infirmary. The bed is more comfortable than any he’s slept on in years - since the last time he had to visit the infirmary. Until this moment, he never knew how tangible cleanliness is. He stares straight up at the ceiling, enjoying every moment that he doesn’t have to live the horror of Unit B. Snow’s words from earlier in the day hurt him more than the welts that can be found all over his body from the beating he took at the hands of the Aryans. His left eye is swollen completely shut - he thinks this must be how Harvey Blake sees the world - but he regrets trying to persuade Snow to enter the Nazi regime more than walking into the gym earlier. It’s nearly midnight and the lights are out. Jason Snow is probably on his way back from Turmoil by now, thinks Miggy. Will Snow forgive him? He hopes so. He was quite fond of his new friend. It’s not often one finds someone so physically impressive as Snow and friendly too - not in the Tank, anyway. “You’re not sleeping yet, Miggy?” asks the night doctor as he stops his nightly roam, holding a clipboard tightly in his right hand. “No, not yet,” Miggy answers. “I don’t suppose you’re thinking about telling me who did this to you?” the doctor says sweetly to the old man as he approaches and takes a seat on the bed by Miggy’s legs. Miggy shakes his head. “Miguel,” the doctor sighs, “we all know who did it. Why don’t you just come clean.” “I don’t make the rules,” Miggy says in a quiet voice, clutching his blankets to his chest. “What does that mean?” “The rules say that if I tell you who did this to me, I don’t live to see the first of the week,” Miggy says, looking into the doctor’s empathetic eyes. He’s such a sweet man, the doctor thinks - he’s not quite capable of imagining how Miggy could have snapped on that day over forty years ago and murdered his wife, her lover, and even his child - though that was an accident. “Miggy,” the doctor sighs, brushing his hand through his thinning brown hair, “don’t you think you’re being a little overly dramatic?” “You don’t have to sleep in a cell,” Miggy says, “You don’t understand. To them, I’m nothing. I have no one to protect me. My life is quite literally in their hands.” The doctor sighs. He knows Miggy is right, and now comes the impossible task of responding to that information. In the end, he decides to simply tap Miggy on the leg with his clipboard, stand up, and move on. *** From the Office of the Warden “The strong must eat the weak to survive,” Gavin Miller, Warden of the Tank, begins from behind his desk. “It’s the way of nature. Why we humans deny the fact that we’re just super-smart animals is beyond me - or anyone who’s ever spent time in a prison for that matter.” The Warden stands up and peers out the window over the yard of his prison where a few prisoners lounge around and go about their business. “Darwin said it decades ago, but we still can’t seem to grasp the fact that the strong prey on the weak. You see, the strong simply know that they are, indeed, strong. When do they find out? Well, some know from birth... others... well...” *** Jason Snow, returning from Turmoil, strolls into Unit B at a few minutes after 2 am and is soon uncuffed by his pair of armed guards. From there, a night duty C.O approaches to take Snow to his cell, unlocks it, and sends the young lion inside. “Been waiting up for you,” says his cellmate, Darryl Jarvis. Jarvis, a black inmate and lower ranking member of the Thugs, lies in his lower bunk with his right hand on his stomach - inching closer to the waistline of his pants. Snow says nothing. He simply stares out through the bars at the rest of Unit B - it’s so quiet and peaceful at night... kind of like a hibernation. All of the monsters are resting their destructive juices until morning. Well, all but the one in the lower bunk who’s beginning to masturbate. “I was thinking, yo,” says Darryl Jarvis with his eyes closed. Snow doesn’t have to look at him to know what he’s doing. “I was thinking that this would be the night.” “The night?” Snow says emotionlessly. “Yeah,” Jarvis sighs, still with his eyes closed and his right hand buried beneath his sweatpants, “Don’t pretend you don’t know what I’m talking about, prag.” “Prag?” Snow smirks, repeating the exact tone that he used the first night that he stayed in the Tank. He knows what the word means now. Servant - worse... slave - worse... Snow slowly turns around at the same moment that Darryl Jarvis is opening his eyes. Terror. Terror envelopes Darryl Jarvis as he gets a good look at his cellmate. Jason Snow is the real monster in this room, and Darryl Jarvis sees that now. In the dim lights that remain illuminated in Unit B, Jason Snow not only looks like a lion - he is a lion; In both mind and body. His long blond hair and matching beard - a mane. His two hands, closing into fists - his fangs. His cold, yet fiery, blue eyes that are set on destruction - an undying craving for blood. ...Or maybe that’s the adrenalin left over from Turmoil. “Prag?” Snow repeats, stepping toward Jarvis - neither of his hands are near his pants now, “I’m a... Prag?” Snow leans down and glares straight into Jarvis’ dark brown eyes - he can smell the fear. Jarvis considers striking first, but in the end, he decides that might only make Snow angrier - maybe he can avoid the situation by staying quiet. Wrong. Snow grabs Jarvis by his short dread locks and picks drags him up from the bed. “Fuck man!” Jarvis screams in protest. Snow doesn’t care about protests. He tosses Jarvis hard against the dirty white sink of the cell, splitting his head wide open. “Who’s the fucking prag, now!?” Snow bellows, drawing the attention of the night Correctional Officers. It’s a long trip from their post up to Snow’s cell, however, and meanwhile, the lion is devouring his prey. Snow sends a pair of hard right hands into the left side of Jarvis’ face, stealing a little more of his consciousness. “Snow!” screams one of the Officers as he sprints up the stairs, drawing his nightstick. “Put your hands on the bars, Snow!” The bars - good idea, thinks the hunting lion. He lifts Jarvis to his feet and proceeds to send him crashing into the bars. Blood splashes on the wall and drips down toward the floor of the cell. Jarvis lies on the floor, barely conscious, the only instinct left working telling him to cover his bloody head. “Get on the floor!” screams a guard over the cracking sound that his nightsick makes as it slams into the left side of Snow’s back. Two smashes later and the young lion is forced to his knees and cuffed. Those cold blue eyes burn more than ever now and his teeth are clenched tightly - the hunt is over. Of course, he claims to be a hetero, and supposedly was on the outside. But since coming to the Tank, he’s been nothing but a sexual predator.” The Warden picks up Darryl Jarvis’ file from his desk and begins to browse through. “Jarvis was a small timer for years,” he sighs, “He never got caught because he never did anything too serious. And then, in 2000, he decided to take a step up - shot an off-duty police officer in the back... and then five times in the head.” “Now he’s my problem,” says the Warden, tossing the file back onto the desk. “Darryl Jarvis has been preying on new prisoners since he got to the Tank. He has a thing about power - he needs it. Physically, he’s nothing impressive compared to the rest of these guys, so he tries to get power in other ways... and sometimes... it backfires in his face.” *** Wednesday, July 10 - Day 5 “I know he probably deserved it,” says Geoffery Rankin, Unit B manager. He sits behind his desk, staring across at Jason Snow. “But you have to learn that there are other ways of dealing with your problems than punching people out. That’s what got you here in the first place.” He tries to sound empathetic. This is his Unit, and Snow is sitting in his office - empathy is the only thing he can offer that might seem sincere. “What the hell was I supposed to do?” Snow asks, his voice nearing a bellow, “stand there and watch him stroke his fucking...” his voice trails off, but his eyes are still burning blue rage. “Couldn’t you have just ignored him?” Rankin asks, extending his hands in a plea of reason, “Couldn’t you have just climbed up to your bunk and gone to sleep?” Snow stands up from his flimsey blue chair and glares at Rankin, still bound in handcuffs. “Ignored him?” he sneers, “And when he climbs up and cums in my fucking face, do I ignore that too? What the fuck would you know about it?” Rankin slinks in his chair - he knows Snow’s right. He brushes his right hand though his thick blond hair and wonders why he ever took this job in the first place. He always wanted to help people, but maybe he’s doing more harm than help by sitting behind this desk at the tender age of twenty-seven - the youngest Unit Manager in the Tank’s history. “Ok,” Rankin says in an attempt to calm Snow, “You know what I’m going to do?” “The hole?” Snow says, raising an eyebrow - refusing to show fear. He’s always heard about the notorious hole and the things that it can do to a man over an extended period of time. “No,” says Rankin, pulling a piece of paper out of his desk. “That’s probably what I should do, but that’s not what I’m going to do...” “Then what are you going to do?” Snow asks, his curiosity rising. “I’m going to sign you in to one of Jennifer Vegas’ anger management classes.” says Rankin as he writes on the paper. “Jennifer Vegas?” Snow vaguely remembers Miggy saying something about that name when he first entered the Tank. Miggy, Snow thinks, where is he anyway? *** From the Office of the Warden The Warden rubs his clean-shaven head while looking down at a piece of paper. “Anger,” he begins, “Is inside all of us. Each of us conveys it and stores it in a different way - it’s true - but the key is to make our angle work for us.” “Police Officers do it every day,” the Warden says, looking up from his paper, “and believe me, there are police officers in the Tank - and I’m not talking about the C.O’s.” *** “You wanted to see me?” Snow asks as he peeks around the corner into Doug Hattoni’s cell, located on the opposite side of the main floor from Snow’s. “Jason Snow?” Doug’s eyes perk up, though they never make contact with the young lion standing in the entry way. Doug sits on his bed, as usual, playing solitaire with a wrinkled deck of cards. His thick graying hair gleams under the lights of his cell. “What did you want?” Snow asks, suspicious of the situation. “Are you Italian, by any chance?” Doug asks, hoping the bright blond hair might not be Snow’s natural color - it is. “No,” Snow answers plainly. “Not at all?” “Irish,” Snow says, stepping into the cell, “A hundred percent.” “Fucking Mick,” Doug says jokingly, still without so much as glancing at Jason Snow. When he’s finished his raspy laugh, he says: “It doesn’t matter anyway.” “What am I here for?” Snow asks again, quickly becoming familiar with the way the man they call ‘The Doug’ likes to beat around the bush. “I’m a very powerful man in here, Jason,” Doug says, flipping an ace of spades into its proper place, “I can make this place as comfortable as home for you - well, almost. I can give you everything available to us in here, and in excess. I can give you power, drugs, money - whatever you like.” “Money?” Snow’s eyes perk up. He didn’t realize money was an issue in prison. For the first time, Doug looks directly at the young lion, “What the fuck did you think we used?” he asks, “The barter system?” Snow shrugs before asking, “What are you offering me?” “My grip over this prison has weakened of late,” Doug says quietly as if his mind isn’t completely on the conversation. “In my line of work, Jason, a man is only as powerful as his muscle. And as you can tell by looking at me, my muscle has always been hired. Lately, I’ve noticed that my top headbreaker is finally showing signs of his age.” “And...” Snow tries to lead Doug into his next point. “And I think you might be a good replacement,” Doug says bluntly - finally. “Of course, in order to receive the honor of sitting to my right, you will have to prove yourself, and your loyalty, to me.” “How do I do that?” Snow raises an eyebrow. “In due time, Jason,” Doug smiles, “In due time.” Still beating around the bush, thinks Snow. *** From the Office of the Warden “In the Tank,” says the Warden as he paces his office, “Gangs make the rules. I can tell the C.O’s to enforce whatever the fuck I please, but in the end, the Gangs make the rules that the prisoners play by.” “Sure,” he continues, “I have the power to throw these motherfuckers in the whole - but unlike the Gangs, I don’t have the power to kill them. Their comfort rests in my hands, but their lives rest in the hands of the prevailing Gangs of the Tank. And no Gang is more dominate right now... than the Thugs.” The Warden stops at his filing cabinet and pulls out a thick file on the respective members of the Thugs. “If you’re black, know a good way to murder someone, and are currently incarcerated in the Tank, there’s a good chance you’ll be recruited by the Thugs. If not for the Nazis and the Stallions - the Italians - the Thugs would squeeze this place dry.” “I remember the late seventies,” the Warden sighs, “when the Thugs rule was at an all-time high. Of course, Doug Hattoni was quietly running things and keeping his Italians protected. But at the time, non-Italian whites were being killed on a daily basis. Even the Italians, who were controlling the drug import, were constantly watching their backs. Without the brains of Doug Hattoni, they never would have survived.” “But the Thugs prove that mixed with brains, brawn will go a long way.” *** Darryl Jarvis nervously strolls past the Nazi’s during their six-thirty workout toward his own fellow gang members - the Thugs - who are working out at the opposite end of the gym. As he passes, he can feel the burning gaze of Harvey Blake’s hateful one eye. “Can I talk to you?” Jarvis asks as he steps up to a massive black man. “Talk,” the gigantic black man answers in his low, gruff, voice. This is Jeremy Doris, more commonly known as J-Thug, leader of the largest organized black Gang in the Tank. Everything about his exterior is intimidating, from the pure thickness of him to the way his stocking hat covers his entire head right down to his eyeballs. “Last night,” Jarvis says, pointing to the thick swollen scab on the side of his head. J-Thug holds up a powerful left hand to quiet him. “I know all about last night, nigger,” he says as he leans against the wall with his bulging arms folded across his massive chest. “Are you coming up in here to ask me to stick up for your punk ass, nigger? Before you do, you’d better understand that I ain’t been satisfied with the effort you been putting in around here lately. All you do is come by asking for favours! But when there’s a job to be done - what the fuck do you do? You guy try to see what motherfucker you can fuck in the ass!” “J-Thug!” Jarvis pleads, “This is about more than me!” “Oh?” The Thug leader asks, stepping away from the wall and glaring down at Darryl Jarvis, “Why don’t you tell me then, motherfucker, what more is this then Jason Snow whipping your faggot ass?” “I hear the motherfucking Nazi’s are trying to recruit him!” spits Darryl Jarvis. J-Thug is silent for a moment, staring over at the Aryans who are working out across the gym. “Well then,” he says quietly, “I suppose that is different.”
