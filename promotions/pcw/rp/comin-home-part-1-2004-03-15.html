Comin' Home Part 1

15 March 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/15/04 It was an early March morning in Detroit, Michigan. Cal Roberts had decided to fly home after the most recent Turmoil to do some thinking. He lay on his bed as the cold air blew, and the early morning sunshine intruded through the windows of his spacious apartment. The sound of cars speeding by his window kept him awake as he thought about his career...his life. He was exhausted from work and life in general. He'd been World Champion, wrestled great matches, jailed for assault of a transvestite, stopped a robbery, and most recently been involved in a war with Skitzo. He couldn't sleep even if he wanted to after all that had occured recently. It all happened so fast that it seemed he couldn't take it all in. 'I guess that's what it takes to be on top', he thought. He knew that he had to suck it up if he wanted to keep the spot he had pretty much gotten for being in the right place at the right time. He had the talent, and everyone knew that. But it was almost as if he was becoming burnt out after seven years of hard work. He turned over in his bed and stared at the pale white wall next to his bed. The only other object that could be seen was a framed photo of Roberts and a woman. It looked to be an old picture from years ago, and it made him turn back over as soon as he laid his eyes on it. 'Twenty nine years old next month', he thought as he laid. He may have been a PCW newcomer, but he was a veteran to the sport of wrestling. Grizzled? Not yet but he was close. Reaching over for a can of coke he bumped his hand on an alarm clock that read 5:30 a.m. He sat up in bed, after grabbing hold of the can of pop. As he gulped the drink he looked at his 'commemorative' PCW title belt that he received the night he won the championship. He looked on the floor of the room, and spotted a wrestling magazine. On the cover a picture showed him holding the belt high over his head with the headline 'The Future of PCW?'. He laughed at the sight, until he saw a small picture of Boda on the cover with the heading 'Triumphant Return' hovering over it. 'It's not my belt. That's Boda and Eric Sagar's belt', he mumbled. He may have wrestled hard that night, but they had helped him win that night. It had become his goal to win the belt on his own, and prove his claims of being the best, true. He had a long road ahead of him in order to do so. He had already lost his seed in the tournament to Skitzo. He was surprised at how easily he let himself become defeated. Mindgames.....Skitzo had gotten into the head of Cal Roberts. If he didn't stop letting the games get to him, 'The Buzz' would quickly become 'The Bust'. Skitzo didn't worry him, however. He knew he could beat Skitzo. He knew he could beat almost anyone in PCW if motivated. Then what was it that worried 'The Buzz' so much to not get any sleep? What would cause a man, who took much pride in his abilities, to worry himself sleepless? One word....... FAILURE.... He wouldn't fail though because Robert's was always thinking one step ahead. The whole reason he came home was to do what he would do later in the day. Remind himself why he must succeed. He turned and looked up at the picture of himself and the woman once again. And why giving her up was worth it.
