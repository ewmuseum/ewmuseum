The Transformation: The Beginning

21 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1/21/03 A New Perspective Footsteps outside of the once peaceful place I called home. My eyes run deep and wide, showing the little sleep I’ve received in days. My face is pale and I squint as the sun brushes off my cheeks. A new day, a new beginning, a new perspective Twenty four hours ago I was a man with little hope, but I still had hope. Now hope is gone. Hope abandoned me back in that alley six years ago when I felt the blood of my own mother poor through my fingers. I asked so much, but now words have no usage. It’s up to me to do what I’ve wanted to do for so long. The gates are open and I am a free man. As I take my first footstep into the same streets of Barcelona I’ve taken a footstep over thousands of times, I smile a sick calm smile of relief. Everything is the same, but it couldn’t be any more different. I’m a changed man. I’ve changed by outlook, my priorities, and everything I’ve believed in. The wind gushes by me blowing my dark blue pea coat into the air. A few children on bikes pass me as I snarl at them, wanting to avoid the faces of happiness. Don’t take this the wrong way. I will be happy. The way I go about may just be a little different. January 21, 2003 The night is setting in as I reach the footsteps to the “La Casa del Pecado”. It’s a rather popular place for people to go. You see all types of people. The good, the bad, the depressed, and the high all show up. It doesn’t matter to me who was there; I just considered this a place for my new initiation. I didn’t know many people, but that will change. My emotions won’t be bottled; everything is going to come out. A Spaniard and his people, that’s what I want. I don’t starve for love, I did, but that was yesterday, today is today. Love is a joke power is the new key. I skip through the line and watch the faces miserably wait on the long line. Excuse m… El Romantico of PCW, pardon me. Nobody was getting in my way. I was through the door of a new beginning, and this door wouldn’t be closed, for a long long time. May I offer a glass of wine for the most becoming sir? I looked at her body of uttermost temptations and smiled. I took a breathe through the cracks of my teeth and felt my blood rumble. Darling, it will take more than a glass for me tonight. May I put you down for two then? Do what you please; wine will calm down this determination of mine. Right away sir! I sat down looking. It seemed like the world had become ten times more beautiful than I had ever realized. So many women starving so have the touch of the Romantic one. Seven months of the lust going unattended to. Tonight it would be different. Tonight is when everything changes, and the master finds himself a maiden. I see this one tempting signorita pass by the right side of my eye. I lick my lips as I see the back of such beauty. I wonder if she could be the one. That’s the one. As the words come from my mouth, I see another pass from the opposite side. This one was ten times more beautiful than the last, innocent but yet dangerous. It was a combination that will leave my body content. My mind was made up and I would go for it. Your two drinks sir, have a lovely night. Oh I will! More than you will ever know. I make my way toward this beauty and I just stand behind her huffing in the sweet aroma of her neck. So tasty, I just had to have a part of it. Words were about to come out of the suave mouth of mine as I see a man walk toward her about to move his own lips. “Hey…” I grabbed him by the neck as his glass fell from his hand. I catch it in midair so my maiden does not turn around and worry. He looks in my eye and backs away. He looked close enough to know that those were not the eyes of a normal man, those were the eyes of something different. Isn’t that what I am, different. I introduced myself. She says hello. I find out her name, and well the rest was just a stroke of genius by me. Less than an hour later I find myself on a silk covered bed. The red of the pillow brushes against my leg. She lays back in her tempting lingerie, moving her lips to the tone of my name. Romantico, the first thing she calls out as I breeze my lips on her neck. It becomes so comfortable and so serene. My hand fades down her body and I kiss her lips. THE REAL EL ROMANTICO HAS RETURNED!
