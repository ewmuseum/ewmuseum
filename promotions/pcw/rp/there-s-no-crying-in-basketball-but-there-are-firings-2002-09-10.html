There's No Crying In Basketball!...But There Are Firings!

10 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 09-10-02 '....Second place?' 'Segundo...' 'HOW THE FUCK COULD YOU LOSE TO A BUNCH OF CHAIN-SMOKING...COCK-SMOKING YUGO-FRIGGIN'-SLAVIANS!?!?' (We fade into the fierce image of fascist rage in the face of Francisco Valerius. El Presidente stands proudly in his decorated military fatigues berating Argentina's basketball team. The dejected team hangs their heads as Frisco paces around shaking his head in disgust.) Frisco: 'Yugoslavia...a country so weak that it broke up into 5 different countries in 2 years! How could you embarrass our great nation like that? We've never been bitch slapped by NATO like they have, yet you slack off and let those slavist fucks take it all? I don't think so. You sons of bitches are going to practice for the next 2 years so we will make sure that the next championship comes to Argentina.' (One of Argentina's players--forward Emanuel Ginobili lifts up his hand and picks his eyes up slightly. Frisco frowns and points.) Frisco: 'You've got something to say? Are you about to make some stupid excuse of why you lost?' Ginobili: 'Senor...we tried!' (The dictator scoffs.) Frisco: 'Tried!? How can you try and lose at the same time? Not once in my career have I actually tried to win a match and lost it. When I try, I win. What is so complicated about that? I will try to take the presidency of Argentina, so that means I will take it. You have to be a real fuck-up to try and still lose. If that's your excuse, it makes you even more pathetic than I had estimated.' (The player looks down in shame.) Ginobili: 'Okay, we did no try.' (Frisco nods.) Frisco: 'That's what I thought. You thought just because you handed the United States their first loss since they started using NBA players, that you could just gaily skip to the gold, but let me tell you something. Beating the best doesn't mean that you are ready to beat everyone else. Hell, look at that Pharaoh douche that I was involved with awhile back. He squeaked out a couple wins over me, yet he's on route to Nowheresville...' (Another player, Juan Sanchez, raises his hand.) Frisco: 'What?' Sanchez: 'Erm, donde esta el Nowheresville?' (El Mero Mero frowns.) Frisco: 'I would think you would already know where that is. The way you just lost a couple days ago, it seems like you're already well on your way towards its location. But at any rate, what I'm saying is that you can't just beat the best, you have to beat the worst, the decent, and everyone in-between...' (The team nods in agreement.) Frisco: 'Perfect example. Someone of my great stature...a Hall of Famer who has won every single title in PCW is fighting over the piece of shit Continental Title. I have a match tonight against Rush Mason, Snow, and El Diablo. A bunch of guys who couldn't achieve legend status if they doused themselves in my prestigious semen. Not to mention Rent-A-Fuckfaced Anal Wart on Friday. I won't even begin to demonstrate how immensely idiotic that fruit is.' Ginobili: 'Shu go beat them?' (Frisco whips out a Cuban cigar and lights it up.) Frisco: 'Claro que si.' (The Hall of Famer Dictator puffs his Cuban.) Frisco: 'Sure, I've won the Continental Title twice before and I honestly couldn't care less if I were fighting over a gum wrapper in place of it, but the important thing is that I go into this match and wipe the floor with these goat felching rimjobbers so I may keep my name in high standing. What you gentlemen did with our country's name after you defeated the Great Satan was threw it into the dumpster after losing to Yugo's labia.' Palladino: 'Shugo's..labia?' (Frisco frowns.) Frisco: 'Asshole, I paid for this team's English lessons so you could taunt the Americans after you defeated them, so please make sure you understand it from now on.' Palladino: 'Shes, sir Presidente.' (Frisco shakes his head and blows out another puff of smoke as he looks at the large, empty gym that he has his national players cooped up in.) Frisco: 'I hope you all understand why I am so disappointed. You let a good thing turn to shit. What you did would be like me winning the World Title and then getting my ass kicked by Lord Crom Griffin Dagoth a minute afterwards!' (Frisco stops and thinks for a second.) Frisco: 'Wait, forget I said that. I think that might've been some awful dream I had once. A better analogy would be when I win the presidency of Argentina, if I would just sit back and let my country continue without conquering the United Kingdom. I would let everyone down. Defeating and enslaving the British is part of my platform and shall be enforced upon my election. Just as winning it all was your promise that you unrightfully broke. Got it?' (The team nods again.) Frisco: 'Good, now everyone get up and start practicing!' (El Presidente replaces the cigar with a whistle and blows it loudly sending the Argentine team onto the floor.) Frisco: 'Remember! The object of this game is to put the ball into the basket!' (The Argentineans look back at Frisco in question.) Frisco: 'Yes, right in the basket! It'll all come back to you, my boys.' (Frisco makes a corny shooting motion as he blows his whistle once again. The team spreads out as ballboys bring bags of basketballs onto the court.) Frisco: 'You there! Number 25!' (The player in question points to himself.) Frisco: 'Yes, you! Make a basket!' (The player frowns and picks up a ball.) Frisco: 'Go on! You dickweeds don't interfere with the man's shot because if he misses, he gets fired and deported.' (Leonardo Guiterrez begins to shake as he stares at the goal. Frisco looks on patiently with his eyebrow raised as the rest of the players stand around.) Frisco: 'Hey!...' (The player jumps as the rest of the team looks back.) Frisco: 'I didn't say stop practicing! Go, go, go! We have a lot of work to do, you lazy sacks of anal pus. Go practice on the other side!' (Frisco franticly blows on his whistle as the team rushes over to the other side of the court with their balls. Shoes squeak and nets swish as the Argentine team begins to practice energetically.) Frisco: 'Hm?' (Guiterrez looks around and wipes the beads of nervous sweat off of his brow. Frisco lets the whistle drop around his neck and pops his cigar back into his mouth. The center, whose career and home is on the line, pivots his feet into the hardwood and faints with his arms a few times. Frisco looks down impatiently and groans.) Frisco: 'I don't have all fucking day. Either make it and show me that you're not entirely worthless, or miss it and allow me to purge my country and country's team of one less braindead peasant.' (Guiterrez finally takes a deep breath and takes a step forward as he thrusts the ball up into the air from the three point line. The ball soars through the air as the rest of the team looks back and Frisco stares straightforward, only listening to the result...) SWISH! (Frisco cracks a slight smile as the team cheers and the player opens his eyes to see the favorable result.) Frisco: 'Good!' (El Presidente claps a few times.) Frisco: 'You lucked out there. If I had heard the ball hitting rim or backboard, you would've been assigned to some local fishery's team in Uruguay.' (Guiterrez gives a nervous chuckle as Frisco flashes a large grin.) Frisco: 'Ahem!' (Frisco's grin turns into a frown as he spots his team standing around again.) Frisco: 'BACK TO WORK, GODDAMNIT!' (El Presidente brings his whistle to his face, accidentally knocking his cigar out of his mouth. He blows the whistle loudly and screams.) Frisco: 'WORK! WORK! WORK!...FUCK!' (The angry dictator looks down at his cigar, smoking on the floor.) Frisco: 'You!' (Frisco points at #36 who stares straight at him.) Frisco: 'Pick up my cigar!' (The player continues to stare at Frisco, but doesn't move.) Frisco: 'Hey, fucktard! I'm talking to you.' (The player finally looks around and points at himself.) Frisco: 'Yes, pibe, get over here and put that cigar back in my mouth...then pick up a ball and make a lay-up!' (The player--Luis Scola salutes the dictator and sprints towards El Presidente, almost tripping in the process.) Frisco: 'Ugh.' (Scola finally arrives and picks up the lit end of the cigar.) Scola: 'AY!' (Frisco shakes his head as he watches the player jump up and down, sucking his finger.) Frisco: 'My cigar, assplug. You dropped it.' (The player quickly goes back down to pick it up carefully. He then slides it into Frisco's lips--the dictator frowns and shivers uncomfortably.) Frisco: 'WHAT WAS THAT!?' (The cigar falls back out of Frisco's mouth.) Scola: 'Como!?' Frisco: 'You slid that cigar into my mouth like it was a phallus! What are you insinuating here with that homoerotic gesture!? Are you trying to say I'm gay? Is that what you're trying to say!?' Scola: 'No, no, no! I th--...' (Frisco shakes his head.) Frisco: 'I give you failures another chance and you repay me by pissing on my polite gesture and replacing it with one of homosexual overtones. You disgust me, boy. You're off the team!' (The player looks on in shock and jumps as Frisco blares on his whistle.) Frisco: 'QUICK! SOMEONE THROW A BALL AT THIS JOKER AND I'LL GIVE YOU A LONG-TERM CONTRACT!' (The cruel dictator shoves his fired player back onto the court and watches him be pelted with basketballs by his once fellow teammates. The player is hit in the face, head, and abdomen with full force and finally falls to the floor in a fetal position. Frisco shakes his head.) Frisco: 'Oh, now, look at this disgraceful scene! You all are so quick to betray your teammate to make a buck. Greedy, pitiful bastards. Now you won't get squat!' (The players hang their heads and offer quiet apologies to the downed Scola.) Frisco: 'Ahem, I didn't say stop practicing.' (El Presidente rolls his eyes and blows his whistle to resume practice. He then picks up a ball and spins it on his finger as the ball is zoomed into and the scene fades out.)
