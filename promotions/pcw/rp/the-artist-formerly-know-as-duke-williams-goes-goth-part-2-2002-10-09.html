The Artist Formerly Know as Duke Williams Goes Goth!! Part 2

9 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Title: The Artist Formerly Know as Duke Williams Goes Goth!! Part 2 Featuring: Duke Williams Date: 10/9/02 -Loud unfamiliar music blasts from the speakers as a strobe light flashes. We see glimpses of what appears to be a party put on in an empty warehouse. It is called a rave and the artist formally know as Duke Williams now called Dukestar the Goblin is new to the scene. Duke is wear all black from his leather pants to his torn up “rock star” shirt. His age makes him look out of place but the black mascara oddly makes him look like a part of the party. Duke is hanging out with his new friend Spike and his girlfriend Malice. They are standing in the corner looking on to the party.- Spike: This party is whack yo. Dukestar: Whack? -Duke makes a gun with his finger as Malice and Spike just laugh.- Spike: You so old school you mind whore. Whack ain’t like I’m going to whack that guy. Whack is like cool. Dukestar: Ok hoss I jive!! -Duke smiles as he shakes his head in agreement.- Malice: Hoss? Spike: Jive? Dukestar: Yea right on man, right on. Spike: This may be hopeless. -Malice and Spike just shake their heads in pity. Duke eyes some guy who’s giving him a dirty look.- Dukestar: I’m going to go over there and bust that son of a bitches nose hoss. Spike: Don’t be no pit fiend man. Malice: Just chill out Dukestar. Have one some of these. -Malice hands Duke some ecstasy tablets, Duke downs them not knowing what they are.- Malice: Stop being so sheeple. Spike: Yea your being a smore. Dukestar: Sorry guys. Lets get out there and dance!! -Spike and Malice hold Duke back before he can shake it.- Spike: One step at a time man. Malice: I’ll dance with you later baby. Dukestar: Ok I can dig that. -Some orange haired fellow walks over to the three Goth’s.- Orange: What are you doing here with the suit in Goth cloths? We all know he’s a cop man. Dukestar: Would a cop stand up and break you fucking nose for the hell of it hoss? -Duke and orange go head to head as Spike and Malice try to break it up.- Orange: Come on baby killer what do you have? Huh you pig. Dukestar: I have enough to break you before you can blink. Spike: Dude he’s not a cop he’s a pro-wrestler. Orange: That could be worse a jock. Dukestar: All pro Defensive Tackle from Penn State university and former New England Patriot. Orange: I rest my case. Spike: Dukestar the Goblin your not helping. Down boy. Malice: Come on Duke lets dance. -Malice grabs Duke and pulls him on the dance floor while Spike and Orange straighten things out.- Spike: What the hell man? Orange: He doesn’t belong he’s a buddha, man. Spike: Just chill out let it go. Orange: He better watch his back we don’t accept plastic goths. Spike: OK, ok, ok, ok just chill. -The camera moves to Duke and Malice on the dance floor who are grinding up against each other.- Dukestar: Damn your thicker then a snicker! Malice: Thanks baby. Dukestar: I’m feeling exceptionally horney. Malice: That’s the X kicking in. Dukestar: Damn that feels good. -The song ends and Duke and Malice rejoin Spike in their standing space.- Spike: Its all cool. Dukestar: You should have let me pop him but now I feel like fucking something. Spike: The X? Malice: Yea. Spike: Now its safe for you to go out there and dance Duke just no fights. Dukestar: I wouldn’t think about it. -Duke goes out to the floor and starts dancing with the young goth girls.- Spike: I wonder how long this is going to last? Malice: Who knows? =w= ---- View Duke Williams's Biography
