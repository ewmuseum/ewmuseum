Get your Ass up!

24 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3-24-03 The normally spotless bedroom of A.J. Trinidad is seen in chaotic dysfunction. Clothes thrown about, wadded up Kleenex scatter across the floor, half empty glasses of water litter the end tables, and A.J himself lay lifeless in his bed. The curtains are pulled shut allowing no real light into the room, but the glow from the morning sun outside offers enough light to make the room slightly visible. There is almost a must or fog floating about the room, dancing around the airspace with precision fluidity. ~Craaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaashhh!~ The door to the room comes crashing open, getting no reaction from Trinidad, but nonetheless Amira comes tearing through. “Damn it A.J.!” She begins to yell. “You’ve been sulking in here for 3 days now! I haven’t even seen you come out to use the bathroom!” “Who cares.”? A.J. responds slightly muddled. “Who cares? Who cares!?” Amira replies as her intensity grows. “I fucking care A.J.! Some asshole has kidnapped our beloved Crespo, and believe it or not, I love that kangaroo too! All you can do is sit her on your ass and fucking sulk about it?! Well fuck that! I’m going out to find that son of a bitch and tear his god damn head off!” “But what can we do about it Amira?” A.J. pathetically asks. “Seymore Smoke could be anywhere. We can’t just go walking around the streets expecting to find him on some corner.” Amira walks over to the bed and rips the blankets off of Trinidad exposing him in the same clothes he wore home from the airport on Saturday. “What the hell happened to you?” Amira scolds, “The old A.J. would have been out tracking this bastard down! The A.J. I know would go find his best friend in the whole world and make sure that the asshole responsible paid ten-fold for his actions! I’m going to get his ass, either you’re with me, or you lay here sulking like a little bitch!” “Amira, calm down,” A.J. begs. “Smoke is probably far underground plotting out his next step to torture Crespo by now.” Slapping A.J. across the face, Amira interrupts him, “Christ A.J.! Its Tuesday ya know! That stoner is more than stupid enough to turn up at Turmoil tonight! Now if you’d just get dressed, we can go get his ass and make him pay!” “You’re right” A.J. responds with a hint of hope in his voice. “Seymore Smoke will show up at Turmoil tonight, and just maybe, he’ll have Crespo with him.” A.J. shoots up out of bed, takes two steps and falls to the floor in pain. “Ahhhhhhh!” A.J. screams out. “Pins and needles! Pins and needles!” A.J. lay, rolling around on the bedroom floor violently striking his legs that are obviously suffering from lack of movement these past few days. “I’ll get the shower going!” Amira yells out as he hustles out of the room. “Get your ass up and get ready to go! We got some ass to kick baby!” The scene begins to fade out with A.J. attempting to stand, then falling back to the floor. A very painful expression crosses Trinidad’s face as the scene fades to black.
