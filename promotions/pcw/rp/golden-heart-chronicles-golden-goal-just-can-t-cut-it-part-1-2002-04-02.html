Golden Heart Chronicles: Golden Goal Just Can't Cut It Part 1 Chapter 3

2 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/2/02 Golden Heart Chronicles Chapter 3: The first two chapters of the Golden Heart chronicles were the insight to the life of Juan Borro. Through these chronicles we were able to experience what Juan went through to lead to how he turned out now. The first two chapters in the Golden Heart Chronicles are where something sparks “flashbacks” during Juan’s present day life to take us back to his childhood. This chapter and the following will focus on Juan and his relationship with being a fighter. He puts other things behind him and works on becoming a determined fighter. Read and Enjoy, hopefully this will fulfill you’re thoughts on how Juan Borro really is. Time: 4:15 P.M Location: NorwayScene: Warriors Gym “ The Golden Goal Just Can’t cut it” As know if you tuned into Sunday Sabotage, Juan fought harm and did all he could in his win against Rent-A-Hero. Juan did get the win, but wasn’t pleased in how things turned out. One of his favorite moves and the move that he uses most to finish off his opponent is the “Golden Goal”. On Sabotage it took Juan three shots to finally get the win of Rent-A-Hero. A move Juan once saw as unstoppable and a definite win, was nothing more than a slight damage to the strong RAH. Juan has been in PCW awhile now, but has never felt as much competition in the Cruiserweight division as he has against Rent-A-Hero. Juan sees him as a worthy competitor, and awaits future confrontations. Juan knows if he is to do this though, he’s going to need a better finishing move. Juan has a lot of things he can do well, but needs to be able to put them together, so he can be more dominant. To be successful in this feat Juan has traveled to Norway where he is being expected at Warriors Gym, one of the best gym’s for wrestlers all around the world. They have expert trainers, and have seen some great fighters in their time. The scene opens with Juan entering the big doors of the gym. The place is very quite and peaceful. It looks more like a monument then a gym, but Juan is still attracted to it. When Juan steps in he sees a big billboard with all of the individual trainers names. He checks out the card he has in his pocket with the name of the trainer. The trainers name is Hans Daigen. Juan looks up the floor and room he is in, then goes over to the elevator to make it to the floor. While in the elevator, Juan sees a man carrying some packages. The man keeps saying different words in Dutch to Juan, but Juan shrugs not knowing what he’s saying. Juan: Excuse me, but I can’t understand what you’re saying? (The man continues, until Juan finally leaves the elevator in the right floor. The man stays on the elevator continuing to speak, but Juan just nods and goes the other way. Juan finds the room where the trainer is it.) (Juan reaches the room and opens the door. When Juan opens the door, he sees a small area with a desk and a bell to ring. Juan looks around, and notices no one is there, so he rings the bell.) (A few moments later, a woman comes up to the desk, and starts speaking to Juan in Dutch.) Juan: I only speak Portuguese, Espanol, and Inglese. (The Dutch woman understands, and puts her hand up as she goes in the back to get a different secretary.) (A new English-speaking assistant comes up and greets Juan. Juan goes up to her and shows he the card, as she responds.) Secretary: Well Mr. Daigen has someone with him now, but should be with you shortly. It seems like you’ve already paid all the bills, and gone through all of our forms, so you’re ready to go. If you’d like to go change, go down the hall and you’ll see a few rooms over to the left. (Secretary guides Juan to the room with her hand.) (Juan goes into the changing room and gets into some sweat clothing so he can perform well and get ready to perform. Juan then goes into the waiting room where he waits for about five minutes until Hans comes out and greets him.) Hans: Ahh so you are ze PCW athlete Von Borro, nice meeting you. (Juan shakes hands with Hans.) Juan: Yea, it’s Juan Borro. Hans: Ahh Vatever. Ze see bevore we get into ze training area vy don’t you tell me your problem? Juan: Well it’s not really a problem. I’m pretty well fit and good at executing my moves, but I need a new finishing move, and other type of arsenal of moves. Hans: Ahh I zee. Vell let’s go into ze training area and get started. (Juan follows Hans into the training area as the two men get ready for action.) Hans: Vet’s start off vy stretching. (Hans shows Juan some old and new stretches until they are finally finished with that segment. Juan feels good and loose as they move on.) Hans: Next, ve should clear our minds and become one vith our inner fighting lions. Zu you understand? Juan: I understand. (The two meditate with certain herbs and candles so they could completely get everything out of their systems. Juan has a lot of things on his mind, but manages to do what’s told and get it all out of him.) Hans: So before we go on, why zon’t you tell me vhat happened there after Vusion? Juan: You say Fusion? Hans: Zure I did, I wondered what happened zit you teammates after that match you had with ze superhero, dah. Juan: Nothing really, my teammates tended to avoid me a little. I don’t think they like the fact that I wasn’t the mean and slimy dirt bag they think I should be. Hans: Hmmm…. Juan: Yea, and you don’t have to be mean to every American, just because you want to help your own. Some Americans are rather stupid, but some aren’t. I odn’t feel I should have to hate others. And Rent-A-Hero did do well. Hans: Ahh I see. Juan: Yea, that’s also partially one of the reasons I’m here you see. Hans: Vat do you mean? Juan: Well I’m here to learn moves against future confrontations, but especially Rent-A-Hero in a type of rematch or something. Hans: Zat’s great, I know exactly zat you can do, but before that can you explain to me zy you are in International Incident and Golden Game? Juan: What do you mean? Hans: You don’t zeem to be like the others. You zeem like a wondering soul not knowing where you belong. I know you don’t like the fans booing you and not being loved by all. Juan: I don’t, but it has to be done. Hans: I’ll leave you with this, why does it have to be done? (Juan thinks about that to himself for a while, but doesn’t respond. Hans then got his stuff and gets ready to leave.) Juan: What are you doing? Hans: Well I’m leaving Von. Juan: What about teaching me the moves. Hans: I have to go now, but don’t worry I have everything planned, stop by tomorrow at the same time. Juan: Okay. The scene then fades to black with Juan leaving the gym.
