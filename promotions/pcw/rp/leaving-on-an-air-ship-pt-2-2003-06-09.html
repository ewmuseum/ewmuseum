Leaving, On An Air Ship (Pt. 2)

9 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: June 9, 2003 TAKES PLACE DURING THE BOARDING OF THE CLOUD NINE... Just as Tyler Preston was about to join his partner, John Gargano, aboard the luxurious Cloud Nine air ship, things got complicated. Tyler’s former girlfriend, who he thought he would never see again, shows up at the boarding docks, and she has something to say. Tyler’s love life hangs in the balance as John Gargano starts drinking already. The scene fades back to the boarding docks where Tyler is holding Kalli tightly in a hug. Her brown hair blows all around her face and covers up her million dollar smile. Her thin, white sun dress blows around as well. Tyler smiles at her face to face, and is truly happy. “Kalli, I am so glad that you decided to come see me off. You have no idea what I was going through with out you,” Tyler says with much appreciation in his voice. He squeezes her tightly in his arms for much longer than she anticipated. She is all right with it as she lets the large man take control. “Actually, I do know what you were going through because I was going through much of the same thing. I really need you Tyler, no matter if you have time for me or not,” Kalli admits as she struggles from out of his arms to look at him face to face. Tyler looks down, ashamed at the fact he can not treat his girl friend with the kindness that she deserves. He is afraid to look at her eye-to-eye until her index finger forces Tyler’s head up. “Listen, I’ll call up the executives right now and tell them that Tyler Preston won’t be present on the air ship. I think I need to start making some time for you,” Tyler says as he looks around for someone important looking, wielding a clipboard favorably. He doesn’t find anyone in the moments that he glances, but Kalli who slaps him on the chest slows his attempts. “No Tyler, you aren’t canceling this great opportunity just because of me. I’ll miss you a lot, but its time that I start making some sacrifices for you,” Kalli says in a thoughtful voice as she gives him another version of hug with one of her small, skinny arms. “Kalli, you said it yourself before you let me go, I have to start making some time for you. This is a great way to get some time. I’ll be off from the comedy club because they think I’m on the air ship,” Tyler pleads with her. Now that she has come back to him, he no longer wants to be apart of the Cloud Nine festivities. “I did some thinking while I was away from you. I came to the conclusion that I am a very lucky person to be going out with a guy like you,” Kalli says, adjusting the collar to his white dress shirt. He smiles are her attempts, but then displays a baffled look at her comment. He never thought of her to be the lucky one. “I wouldn’t call you the lucky one. I mean, you deserve some one that can treat you a little better than I can,” admits Tyler, hoping that she won’t actually listen to him and have a sudden change of heart. Tyler looks around the area to find PCW wrestlers flooding on to the Cloud Nine air ship, but he doesn’t get nervous. “I am lucky Tyler, and it is not fair that I hold you back from doing things that you want to do. You never did that to me. Remember when I went on that five-day trip to England for work? You were right there by my side and didn’t get angered in the least bit. I need to start modeling my reactions over that instance,” Kalli pours out from her heart. Tyler listens with a soft heart, and gives her a sympathetic look, as if she has been the victim all this time. “But right now, the thing that I want to do is be with you. I want this more than what any air ship could offer me,” Tyler admits as he runs his callused hands through Kalli’s soft brown hair. He pulls it out of her eyes so that he can get lost in them for yet another moment. “I can’t let you miss an opportunity like this though. It’s a chance of a lifetime. I’ve never been on an air ship, so you are going to have to tell me what it is like,” Kalli says as she begins to pick up some various articles of luggage that he has brought with him. She nudges him towards the entrance to the air ship so that he will not miss his boarding time. “Let’s go Ty. Kick it in the pants. If you don’t get on this ship in a matter of seconds, we are going to get stuck with a room next to Matt Jackson. You know you don’t want that. He’ll be dusting all out stuff for fingerprints all night,” John screams from the doorway of the mammoth ship. He has already shed his large black suitcases and is walking around with a drink in his hand. He motions a stocky right arm into the ship. Tyler looks at him, but then glances back at the refreshingly beautiful Kalli. He is torn between the two. “So this is it huh? I’m going to be forced to decide between my business and my friend, or else my girlfriend. I know that my last decision like this got me in some real trouble,” Tyler says as he recalls losing Kalli that cold and rainy May night. Kalli puts her soft hand on his giant shoulder to comfort him. “It’s not a decision Tyler, your mind is already made up,” Kalli helps him out as she continue to push him towards the ship. She struggles with the larger, cream suitcase, so Tyler, thoughtfully, picks it up out of her grasp. She smiles at him, marveling at his strength. “I’m going to miss you Kalli. More than you could ever know. It’s hard, finally snatching the one you love back in to your arms, only to lose them the very next second,” Tyler admits, hugging Kalli for the third time in a very short period of minutes. She accepts the hug graciously. “I know its tough, because I’ll miss you just the same. Please make sure to call, write hell, even visit if you can find the time. But remember, above all else, I’m thinking about you this entire time,” Kalli says sincerely. Tyler nods his head in acceptance, but can’t muster up a smile at this particular time. “Come on kissy face, let’s get a move on,” shouts John once again from the doorway of the air ship. He is having a very good time serving as the complimentary boarding greeter. He attempts to shake a few wrestlers’ hands, but they simply stare back at him with angry looks. “You know, we do get off the ship to wrestle every Tuesday and Friday. Look us up, and give me a visit. That would be the best,” Tyler says as he finally picks up the luggage under his own free will. Tears start to well up at the bottom skin line of Kalli’s dark brown eyes. She smiles to show that they are not tears of pure sadness, but also happiness. She wipes them quickly before they can fall down her soft, pink cheek. Tyler straps at black duffel bag around his shoulder and wields the large cream suitcase with his left arm. His arm bulges under the confines of the white dress shirt. Tyler looks Kalli over one last time and heads on to the ship. He doesn’t want to look back, knowing that if he did, his mind might change once again. He hands the man at the door his pass. “It’s about time you love bird. How much could there seriously be to talk about. We have to hit the entertainment deck; there could definitely be some possibilities to gain exposure on there,” John frantically yelps at Tyler who is not even fully on the ship yet. Tyler just sighs, irritated at the fact that John wants to do everything right at that very minute while Tyler is still struggling emotionally. “Just hold on a second man, I’m not even in the door,” Tyler barks with no hint of joking on his face, even though the comment was intended to be one. “Welcome to the Cloud Nine air ship Mr. Preston. There are maps to your right, and you can reach a service technician on any of the floors for directions and advice. Enjoy your stay,” the man at the door methodically says like a machine. This is obvious evidence that he has stated this fact one to many times that day. Tyler nods at him with thanks. Tyler walks through the huge iron door way to be greeted by a bell hop with a large crate on wheels. Tyler relieves the strain on his body by plopping the two pieces of luggage down on the crate. The bellhop takes out a small piece of paper from his scarlet red, polyester jump suit. His square shaped hat falls over his eyes, but he pushes it back on to his bushy blonde head. “Name please?” The bellhop asks in a squeaky, pre-pubescent voice. By the sounds of it, he seems to be a teenager. “Tyler Preston,” Tyler simply states with no emotion in his voice. The bellhop scribbles writing on to the piece of paper and quickly takes wheels away the loaded crate. The wheels squeal loudly. John runs up from behind Tyler and jumps on his back. Tyler sighs, and tries to act perky so that he doesn’t ruin things for his friend to. “Welcome to paradise Mr. Tyler Preston. We are going to wreak absolute havoc on this place. You do know that, don’t you?” John asks with laughter in his voice. It is obvious that this duo could do a lot of trouble with their mischievous behavior in a place like this. Tyler musters out a smile, and nods his head. “You know it,” Tyler states as his quick answer. He quickly makes a beeline for the rest room to get at least a few moment of quiet time so he can process his thoughts. John follows him, up until he reaches the door. When Tyler swings open the door, John stops in his tracks and smiles. John takes a seat outside the door to way for his counterpart. As he does so, the air ship continues to load, as Cloud Nine prepares to makes its majestic departure on what promises to be a very ‘eventful’ month of sailing.
