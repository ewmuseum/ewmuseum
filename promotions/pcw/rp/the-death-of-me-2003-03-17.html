The Death Of Me

17 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Title: The Death Of Me Featuring: Xavier Kannon Date: 17/03/03 I hate PCW. I really do. Once I lived for this place. When I woke up in the morning, PCW was the first thing I thought about. That realisation I was in Primetime marked the start of a new day for me, made me remember who I was. Only when I looked across the bed to see Eleanor did I remember her. It was PCW that I lived for. And it is the place that will kill me. It is the entity that has scarred my body, nearly put me in a wheel chair and shortened by life by who knows how many years. But at least I endured most of that for a reason. Every night when I went to sleep, my body aching and my wife entwined around me, I knew I was one step closer to my goal. I wanted to own PCW, yet now I see it really owns me. I’m sincere, a least give me that much. If you saw through the blood that masked my face at Third Dawning you’d see that look on my face. Pride. Jubilation. An expression that says everything I did was worth it. I didn’t expect you people to cheer, I’d accepted that, but you could have been fucking grateful. I rid PCW of Tyler Nelson. Of Greed. Hate me, fine. Detest me, your right. Loathe me, I may have deserved it. Respect me? Not a fucking chance from you peons. I respect people I hate, then again, I’m far more of a man than the great unwashed who watch this product. I can look at someone with a seething stare and want to see their dead eyes roll back into their head as I wrap my hands around their throat... Trust me, I feel like that a lot. But I can have a measure of respect for them. I have to respect a man I hate to that degree of fury. PCW fans hate me, that I can shrug off. But I want my respect. I want them to say “I REALLY want Kannon to die a long and painful death, I want to piss on his grave as he rots, I want to dig up his carcass, bend it over his gravestone and fu-”, well, you get the picture. But I want them to at least acknowledge why they hate me that much. And I want my dues. You know what makes it all the worse? Standing in the back at March Madness, waiting to watch the Main Event. Many men would go out there and give their all, they knew they could be beaten up by 10, 15, 20 guys but they ran out there with such vigour. I can’t stand most of them, but they fought hard. But who did you peons cheer for the most? Duke Williams & Jon Kano. “The Malice Man” and “The REAL Legend”. 29 men in that Main Event knew they’d walk into Turmoil ready to wrestle, knew any injury they picked up in that match they’d have to wrestle through this week. They’ll be here next week, and the week after. They haven’t left you. That’s the one thing I’ll never understand about PCW fans. Most of your peculiarities I can put down to ignorance and a lack of decent education, but I just can’t grasp your fondness of the past. Did your Jesus hang there up on the cross, struggling for breath and think ‘Hey, I sure do miss that Judas, things were great when he was around’? Kano is a Judas. He left you. He betrayed you. You love him and he walked out. Still going to cheer for him when he walks into another Federation? Your PCW “KANO ROCKS~!” t-shirt is going to be a little out of date then. When I finally free myself of this bond to PCW that bemuses even me, when I finally find out how I can come full circle and find my release from here... Will you speak well of me? Will I be one of those figures from the past that you cherish? Somehow I doubt it. I’ll always be too good for you, a reminder of what you could of been, a constant taunt that you aren’t happy with your lives. I’m the Scientologist with the gorgeous young wife who has money and power at his fingertips while you’re the bozo who sits in Church every Sunday wishing a work of fiction would make your lives better. Occasionally jackhammering away at the wife to fulfil your ‘manly duties’ as she counts the cracks in the ceiling tiles and wonders what she’ll make her illegitimate kids for dinner tomorrow. So many times people have told me “The fans make PCW”, or “It’s history make PCW”. And people wonder why I loathe this Federation so much. It amazes me every show that you people have the cranial capacity to even drive to shows, let alone exchange dialogue to order the ticket. Twice every week, the PCW wrestlers give you there all, they’ll hurt themselves, they’ll hurt others, they’ll bleed... For you... And what do you think when you see it? Is there even any gratitude? No, you’re thinking how much better it was “In the old days”. Fucking ingrates. If they cared half as much about PCW as you do, don’t you think they’d be back? Don’t you think they’d have a PCW contract with their name on? Your attachment to them and the past is pathetic. They flew the nest, went to what they saw as greener pastures. They abandoned you. And rather than jeering them, rather than being angry and wondering why Primetime and your adulation wasn’t enough for them, you just made yourself a little comfort zone. Your own little imaginary existence to justify it. They must have left because of Nelson, or Kannon. They left because they grew the same disgust for this putrid cesspool that I have. I envy them, they walked away. For some reason I stay. PCW hates me. I hate it back with equal passion. It was once my life, and it will be the death of me... Unless I kill it first. ---- View Xavier Kannon's Biography
