Finally Xavier Heads To St Louis....Thanks To The Preps...

4 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/3/02 The seconds are ticking. The time is closing in. Never has the time been better for Xavier to get back to St Louis. He was stranded at the South Pole, on a Desert Island and lost at sea....But now he is on dry land, in a city.....In fact, he’s in charge of the city. After being the one, albeit accidentally, who killed the previous Governor, the unhappy citizens on San Lorenzo Argentina have placed Xavier and Ellie~! in control of their city, which could just give Xavier the chance he needs to get back for March Madness, while also being the 1st Governor of San Lorenzo to quit rather then be killed! We open up on the steps of the Governor’s building in San Lorenzo where Xavier and Eleanor are standing ready to address the population of the city with their mandate. Eleanor : “So......Er.....Hi!” She gives them a nervous wave, biting her bottom lip. She is still wearing the clothes given to her aboard the HMS Halesworth, a plain White t-shirt that looks a size to big and doesn’t show off her figure as well as most of her outfits. With it she has a pair of plain Blue jeans and generic Black shoes, looking far more frumpy than she does even in her worst nightmares. Her long, wavy Brown hair with subtle Blond highlights is loosely tied back, with most of it falling down the sides of her face, helping highlight her pale Blue eyes. Eleanor : “God to see you all out here........Nice day for a revolution isn’t it?” The crowd stand deadly quiet before the 1st couple of Scientology, folding arms and glaring impatiently. Beside Ellie~! stands Xavier who is wearing the plain Black shirt and sunglasses h was issued on the British Navy Destroyer that accompany his pair of Black boot-cut jeans with Green stitching and Purple embroidery down the left leg. His long Auburn hair is tightly tied back with the sunglasses resting atop his head as his piercing Green eyes look out into the crowd, scanning the faces. Xavier : “Hello fello-.....Um, hello Argentines! This is quite hard for me, you see.....I didn’t mean to throw the brick.....I tr-” He is stopped by Ellie~! whacking him on the arm and whispering out of the side of her mouth, trying to look inconspicuous. Eleanor : “Don’t tell them that....That’s probably why they wanted to overthrow the last guy because he killed the person by accident....Tell them you are proud or something!” Kannon clears his throat and loosens his short collar as he faces back to the crowd. Xavier : “It’s an honour to be the new Governor of San Lor-....” He turns to Ellie~! Xavier : “.....It’s San Lorenzo.....Right?” His preppy valet then shrugs, tilting her head to the side. Eleanor : “Sounds about right...” Xavier : “....To be Governor of San Lorenzo, which, when all the debris from your little rioting spree is cleared up it will look a bit better to! Now many of you will be wondering what the first thing I will do in charge of San Lorenzo is?” The assembled crowd remain dead to his words, shuffling around and acting disinterested. Xavier : “Well...I can see you are eager to know, I will be making this fine city better by going to St Louis! Where they have....Um, an arch thing, which we shall build and become the envy of all South America!” He holds his hands out, preaching to the crowd but most don’t understand what he is saying and those that do show a fair amount of apathy towards his proposal. Xavier : “Peru is famous for having that capital people always ask for, Colombia has Cocaine and Shakira....Though I’m not saying your Cocaine is bad, they are just more famous for it....Brazil has soccer and what does Argentina have?.....Some movie with Madonna in it! Hah! A fine Scientologist like Jenna Elfman would have played Eva Peron so much better!” Even the mention of a national icon and her decimation on screen fails to rouse a response. Xavier : “So.....If one of you would be kind enough to point me in the direction of my private jet and I’ll try and be back by Wednesday......Hello?......Is my larynx on? Eleanor taps him on the shoulder and whispers to him. Eleanor : “You are really dying up here......Want me to take over?” Throwing all sense and wisdom aside, Kannon nods, opening the crowd up to the preppyness of his valet. Eleanor : “Okay....Like, hello? I little appreciation wouldn’t go amiss! he did SO kill the last guy for you to save you the effort, it would have taken you at least 10 minutes to either lynch him or rip his flash from the bone and Xavier just took him right out of the game for you!” Kannon slaps his forehead as he realises what he has done. Eleanor : “....And please will you clear the mess up please? If you are going to be so uncivilised as to wreck all the good things left in your cesspit of a city you ca at least clean it up, I’m sure most of you do menial little tasks like that for a living anyway! Well.....What are you waiting for?” As Ellie~! speaks a whisper goes around the crowd, which annoys the prep further more. Eleanor : “HELLO? Speaking here.....Could you possibly SO insult me anymore? I come for a CIVILISED country, you should be listening to me and taking notes, not trying to whisper over me! You...Juan, be quite......Pablo, shut up.....Panc-” Suddenly she is cut off as a huge roar breaks out from the crowd standing a the foot of the steps. Crowd : “REVOLUTION! REVOLUTION! REVOLUTION!” She stands at the top of the steps with her hands on her hips, not looking too happy with their reaction. Beside her Xavier starts to shuffle back towards he main doors. Eleanor : “What is WITH this rudeness? Is this any way to treat the valet of your leader? He MADE this little dirt city! Without him you would be urinating in the streets! Wit-” With Ellie~!’s further insults another roar goes up. Crowd : “REVOLUTION! REVOLUTION! REVOLUTION!” They then begin to charge the steps as the Police run in to block their path and form a protective pocket around the 1st couple of Scientology as bricks and rubble start to rain in on them. Xavier : “Great Ellie~! You’ve really won them over this time!” The couple run up the steps but the prep stops and sees something out of the corner of her eye and makes a detour. Xavier : “Where are you going?” Eleanor : “Someone has to get us out of here.....I’m summoning the PREPPIECOPTOR!” She has noticed a large spotlight that points up at the sky and hunts around for a way to turn it on, after a few seconds of dodging concrete she finds it and a column of light is launched up into the San Lorenzo sky, which by pure coincidence has turned a lot more overcast. Xavier : “What are you DOING!” Xavier stands at the top of the steps, calling for her to follow him into the safety of the palace. Eleanor : “Hello? Getting us OUT of here!” She then holds her palm out, staring at it before thrusting it into the light. Eleanor : “Like.........What-EVER!” With her hand in the light it casts an eerie image of the Prep Palm onto the clouds above. She keeps the signal there for a few seconds then joins Xavier in running up the steps and through the front door as the Police struggle to keep their barrier. Xavier : “What WAS that?” Eleanor : “Us Preps look after our own, if ever a Prep is in peril all she need do is give the sign and she will be helped by International Prep Rescue.....To the roof!” The 1st couple of Scientology run through the Hall and up the steps, scrambling up them before opening a window to climb out onto the garage roof so they can make their way higher. Crowd : “REVOLUTION! REVOLUTION! REVOLUTION!” They dodge bits of debris being thrown as the chants of the crowd are drowned out by the sound of a helicopter rotor as one is seen approaching, homing in on the beam of light. Xavier : “You know those people?” Eleanor : “Yeah.....It’s the Preppiecopter!” The trees surrounding the palace blow wildly as the Black helicopter touches down on the roof and the door open for the 1st couple of Scientology to clamber into to be met with a very modernly decorated interior. Xavier : “You lot decorate your Helicopter?” Eleanor : “There is no excuse for bad decor Xavi!” The doors close and it pulls up just as the house becomes overrun by the irate locals and turns North before setting off on it’s journey to St Louis to deliver it’s passengers from harm and into a World Title shot. THE END.
