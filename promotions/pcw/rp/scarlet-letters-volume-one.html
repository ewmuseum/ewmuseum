Scarlet Letters...Volume One

undated

Date: Past... Location: The unknown wilds of Canada… In the dark womb where we began My mother's life made us a man, Through all the months of human birth Her beauty fed our common earth. We cannot see, nor breathe, nor stir, But through the death of some of her. -From 'C.L.M.', by John Masefield. Dear Yuu, We are here yet again, we never thought we would be able to stay here, but the more we think, the more we are afraid to leave our abode. It is peaceful here, where we do not have to deal with the other people, and the truth of our existence. As long as we stay, the truth does not have to face us. Here, the truth can be hidden, can be squashed without a sound. But even as we lie, asleep, we can hear a voice pierce our mind, a soft, gentle voice, prodding us to wake, prodding us to move forward, away from the shelter of darkness, away from the walls that we build up around myself. There is nothing else we can say, except for 'no'. We cannot bear to face anyone outside. All we can do is think and recite the truths we have received in the Void. There is everything in the world to reflect on. Our father, he had respect for life, though he was a maniac who tried and succeeded in raising a monster of battle as us. Our mother. She was just as mad as he was…both of them insane for the combat only…now their ambitions dead now. There is nothing we can do. These facts sound like lies to us. It is the truth that we are not human. In our rage in Tokyo, in our obsessive search for acceptance and the truth, we tore the city apart and deemed myself worthy of being a Celestial, as our ethereal parents, Commission and Offering, were before us. How ironic it is that we discover that both of them committed a immortal sin in the creation of us. We discover we are a virus, a banality of society as well as existence. We are not human. We knew it then, but we did not accept the fact. We are not human, but new hybrid of Celestial entity... more than an basic god… We cannot bear to face the world. It makes us wonder why our purpose is. We are intelligent, powerful, and worthy of living in the world, except for one reason: gods tried to play God with the other gods. As with any dictatorship, where one man, or a god, tries to control their gods, the gods eventually rise up against their masters. Man on this Earth has rejected gods. Gods has served a purpose in the minds of men. They are rising up to rebel. It is no different for us. The men of PCW, the CEO Nelson, Wonder, their champion Snow… they will suffer, just like the men suffer for believing that gods will follow like string puppets, bowing to serve them. Man made one mistake, just as Nelson made his mistake. We were intelligent. We cease, at a point, to be a doll, controlled, manipulated. We think for ourselves. And at that point, we deem the evil of mankind unworthy of his existence. They resent us for demanding savory battle when none has been given to us, none, except for a miniscule percentage, and the act of fighting us in itself is not an act willed upon one's self. If these evil men controlling PCW wanted the battle rigged, wanted the battle in their favor, he should have left his Neo Void God out of the picture and left his mindless animals on the earth. Nelson has made the same mistake. He decided to play God, decided to push away the HAND OF FATE directed towards him. We should punish him as well as the others for their subordination. But we will not. Again, it's left to the gods who look over them to gain their own power…we have our own agenda… We will no longer be a doll. We will not be manipulated by anyone, or anything, except for myself. For we are the solidarity Neo Void God, and no one else has control over us. But we are afraid to face the world. We remember the past... no, we remember the truth. The truth makes us angry. Not the truth itself, but the fact that the gods that Nelson, Chef and Wonder tried to be hid the truth from us. They thought their puppet was not intelligent enough to find out. But we were. We should not be afraid of the world. We can see a slight point of light from the darkness of the cave where we reside. We shut ourselves out, yet we speak of our quest. We are only as good as Nelson is by doing that; claiming one thing while doing another; denying themselves the truth and denying others the truth. At least we know we are not afraid. Our quest will not be vengeance. It will be the building of courage, the honing of our skills. That will be our quest. It may be days, or years. But we will let one quest lead to another. We are still afraid. But the difference is, we am willing to challenge the fear, willing to control it, instead of letting it control us. The weakness of man lies in their inability to take control of their own lives. By letting their lives be run by someone or something else, they are free of the harsh truths of the world. Working for someone, with no question of their goals, is only denial. We will be like that no longer. We stare at the world. We may be afraid, but we will not let it take over us. ** Akiyama **
