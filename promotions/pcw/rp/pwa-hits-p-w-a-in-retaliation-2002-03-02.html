PWA hits P.W.A. In retaliation.

2 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 02/03/02 Day 5. We’re almost out of here. Just have to refill the glitter hoppers and hold Tempest’s punch bag for one more session. Gaz woke up about 2 hours ago, says he’s going to let off some steam on Turmoil with someone. Gah, the Mistress approaches. I better stop writing. Nerezza folded up the black bound notebook and tucked it inside his coat. His eyes wandered from behind the frames of his red sunglasses to the approaching Tempest, headmistress of the Primetime Wrestling Academy, formerly run by greats such as Hessian and Scott Taylor. It was also the womb of P.W.A themselves, as well as such names as Angelo Deville, Porky Jack Bunson and PCW’s latest recruit, Cozette Dú Pres. And now, the place of punishment for Pranks With Attitude. “REZZY!” Tempest cried and flung herself at him. Despite being 6’0” and 260lbs, Nerezza’s feet flew from under him as the girl who looked as light as a father pounced on him with the force of a Professional American Footballer. “GUH!” the vampire gasped as the little air in his lungs was driven out of him. While Nerezza had never taken on Tempest in a match, he knew that her spears and bear-hugs were not just friendly greetings, but also powerful moves. Perhaps her cheerful appearance was a deception to allow her to practice them. Either way, his strength was decreasing as the girl with a purple aura eliminated all forms of negativity around him. “Tempie-Poohs!” Nerezza squeaked in a fake smile, trying his best not to show his fangs. “I’ve been looking for you, where did you go after refilling the glitter hoppers?” she said, finally releasing him from the hug. “Er, well, after the fall, I thought I’d better watch Gaz until he woke up.” Nerezza laughed, nervously scratching the back of his head. His thoughts wandered back to earlier…. earlier…. earlier… and the PWA’s walls began to wave as Nerezza did a flashback scene. How this was accomplished, I don’t know, but the brick turned all rubbery and waved about. A bit. Or something. “GAZZYKINS!” Tempest screamed as she launched herself at the Emerald Menace, taking him to the floor. The Jester had been training some of the new recruits in the correct way to run away from a horse if it ever looks at you in the wrong way – or is that the right way… - and just come out from the showers. No sooner had he stepped out into the main arena, 133lbs of purple pounce had grounded him. “WAAAAAAAAAH!” he yelled as his back hit the floor, which was lucky to have purple crash-mats lying around. “Ooh! Ooh! Could you do me a favour?” Tempest clapped as she rolled off him like a playful kitten. Gaz sat up as though he’d just been hit by the world’s most cheerful cruise missile and shook the stars from his head. “Uhh… what do you need me to do, Squiress?” Gaz said with a wavy lack of coherence. “Well…” began the glitter queen. “I need someone to hold the punch bag for me. You see, we had a support for it but I, um, pounced it and… well, anyway, could you hold it for me?” she asked, raising her eyebrows and opening her eyes until they were almost big enough to swallow Gaz alive. “She’s not big enough to do much… this’ll be easy compared to that game of dodge-ball she ordered everyone into earlier” The Jester thought to himself. “Sure I will!” He replied and gave his trademark grin. “WHEEEE! Thankyou!” She said, promptly turning herself into the usual Tempest Neck Accessory™. Gaz literally became green until he managed to convince Tempest to release him. The girl stood up and bounced on the spot, placing her hands behind her back. She looked up to the higher seats and gave a call out. “HOW’S THE PAINTING COMING ALONG, REZZY?” Nerezza clung to the ceiling by his claws. His red coat hung towards the floor, as did his purple hair. His belt had the handle of a tin of purple paint attached to it and in his free right hand, a paintbrush carefully stroked up and down. From the ground, Gaz looked up to see that Nerezza had already painted three giant purple letters: “R”, “A” and “V” on the ceiling. The vampire shuffled around, trying to finish off the final letter. “R, A, V… Ravioli?” The Jester enquired. “No, silly!” Tempest chirped. “Rave!” As the words left Tempest’s lips, the lights went dim for a moment before suddenly, a techno tune blasted from nowhere, shaking Nerezza around. He became dazzled as lights of all colours penetrated the lenses of his sunglasses, blinding him. “What the hell?” Gaz said, startled by the sudden incident. “Oh, you don’t know? It’s voice-activated, when I say that word, the lights and music start up!” bounced Tempest, jumping up and down. Nerezza tried his best to maintain his concentration and not drop anything while his brain was being assaulted on all sides by the music and lights. His eyes screwed up and his teeth pressed together tightly, trying not to lose the tin or brush. “Awwww, phooey. The glitter-hoppers are empty!” Tempest whined and held her hands out as though expecting rain. “Glitter-hoppers?” Gaz scratched his head. “Yeah, Glitter-hoppers! Those things that pour glitter down on us. Except they aren’t pouring anything because they’re empty. Would you fill ‘em back up for me, pweeeeease?” Tempest said, giving her little puppy dog eyes again. Except for the little part. “I’m down here. I’ll ask his Rezz-ness do do it.” Gaz said, turning his head upwards. “OH, SQUIRE! COULD YOU - ” SPLAT! A huge puddle of paint formed on the floor, complete with a vampire in the middle. If Nerezza’s blood was purple, it could have been mistaken for his demise. Picking his head up, Nerezza looked over to Gaz and spat a paintbrush out. “Yeth?” “Err… never mind…” Gaz shrugged. 5 minutes later… The rave-effects had been switched off, leaving Nerezza to recover himself. Gaz was holding a purple leather punch bag for Tempest, who aimed a few jabs at it. She was dressed in a sparkling violet halter top and black shorts, barefoot. Gaz held the punch bag in place, absorbing the blows well. The rhythmic pattern stopped after a few seconds. “Out of stamina already? Heh.” Gaz thought. Peeking out form one side, Gaz’s expression soon turned to panic as the little glittery rocket sped towards him. Dropping the bag as soon as possible, The Jester turned tail and began to run in the opposite direction as though his life depended on it. Tempest leaped forward and pounced the bag, which skidded along for a few feet from her momentum. “WHEEEEE!” she squealed in delight as Gaz looked on in disbelief and shock. The sound woke Nerezza up. The beast sat up, his clothes and hair covered in purple paint. His eyes looked even more bloodshot than normal and there was an annoying ringing sounds echoing and whining inside his head. “REZZ!” Gaz exclaimed. “Squire, we need to confer…” “Huh?” The vampire groaned. “I don’t know if you noticed, but the last time we were here, Scott Taylor ran the place with fluid efficiency. When we were being schooled, Hessian had us going through things that a medieval torturer would object to like a little Christian schoolgirl. We’re here and the place is a disco! And we have to refill those things that pour glitter out now. Is it just me or are we the handymen for this place?” Gaz muttered in disappointment. “We’re here as an obvious punishment, you mook. Besides, we’re doing this for Tempest more than anyone else.” Nerezza replied, picking the half-dried paint off himself. “She might appear to be Ellie 2.0, but she’s got hair like mine. I feel like it’s some sort of duty.” Gaz frowned and raised an eyebrow. “Hey Rezz… what’s 6 feet tall and wears red, then 6 inches and is all red?” “Dunno.” “A vampire who has a crush on Ivan Stanislav’s girl.” “I don’t like her like that, mook. Besides, I know better than that. Look, let’s just fill up those glitter balls or whatever they are, DON’T say the “R” word and we’ll probably get a rest.” The pair looked up at the ceiling. The glitter-hoppers were four small transparent spheres that hung over the ring. It looked as though they spewed out Tempest’s “blessing” when they spun around. “Well, how do you plan to fill the glitter into those things? They’re about 30 feet up, there’s no way you can fly up there without showing your wings and your arms can’t reach that long.” Gaz pondered, locking his bright green eyes onto the four orbs in the sky. His facial expression changed from one of wonder into one of terror as Nerezza shoved a bottle of glitter into his hand and then gripped him by the collar. Gaz turned around to see Nerezza fully transformed, but looking into the air. “Rezz… Squire… You wouldn’t…” Gaz panicked and Nerezza’s claws tightened around the fabric of his suit as the behemoth growled and locked his legs into place. “The end justifies the means.” Nerezza growled in conclusion, swinging his arm up towards the roof and taking Gaz with it. “REEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEZZ!” screamed the Jester as he flew into the air like a weightless astronaut. As he flew past one of the transparent orbs, he quickly grabbed onto it, praying that the cable that suspended it would support his weight. The Prince Of Pranks swung on it for a moment and to his euphoric surprise, it held fast. Wasting no time, the Jester unscrewed the bottle cap with his teeth and emptied the millions of tiny sparkles into the ball. “Now how do I get down?!?” the Emerald Menace yelled, looking towards the ring. “Drop and I’ll catch you!” the vampire ordered. “It’s 30 feet! Do I look like I use a Sharpshooter?” Gaz retorted. Nerezza shrugged his shoulders and closed his eyes. “Well…” he sighed. “You can drop down with me here, or I can go take a rest.” Without any choice in the matter, Gaz released his grip on the cable and plummeted towards the ring. Nerezza’s long, snake-like arm shot out as though it were a grappling hook and quickly locked a hold onto the back of Gaz’s jacket. The Jester jerked to an instant halt, dangling above the ring like a bright green bauble on a Christmas Tree. “Told ya.” Nerezza gloated. “NOOOOOOOOO!” Gaz screamed out loud. “What? I caught you…” the Living Nightmare protested. “Are you ever satisfied with anything…” His words were cut short as he felt something running across the back of his jacket. It felt like some kind of spider with all the legs flicking and scampering around. As Nerezza turned his grey, fanged face around to see what it was, his expression turned to the same kind of fear as Gaz’s and the feeling scurried to under his arms. “COOCHY-COOCHY-COO!” Tempest giggled, tickling Nerezza’s arms. Affected by Tempest’s cheerfulness, he almost instantly lost his ability to transform, shrinking back to human form while laughing loudly. His cackling echoed throughout the arena, much to Gaz’s dismay as he felt the grip weakening on his jacket while Nerezza’s arm began to shrink. “Come on, submit!” Tempest demanded, unaware of Gaz’s situation. Tears of laughter streamed down Nerezza’s face until he finally managed to shout “I give!” through his laughter. At that point, Gaz resumed his descent towards the ring mat with a loud “WAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAH!”, landing face first on the canvas. The last thing he heard before he passed out was the combined giggling of Tempest and Nerezza… The walls began to do the strange warping thing again as Nerezza’s flashback ended and he snapped back to the present. “So we only managed to do one of the hoppers and Gaz is messed up for the day.” The vampire concluded. “Oooh… sowwy! Tempest said, her eyes looking over to the unconscious Gaz Green. “But I have good news! Tim Walden called, apparently you’re needed back over at PCW. He should be here any minute now!” As if on cue, the doors of the main hallway swung open and PCW’s Financial Reclamations Editor entered. And a glitter hopper dropped onto his head. And he joined Gaz on the floor. “Eek…” Tempest squeaked, pressing her hands to her face as Nerezza scooped up his partner and stablemate over each shoulder. Nerezza took one last look over his shoulder with an incredibly faked smile. With his teeth gritted firmly together, he growled at Tempest. “I’ll see myself out.”
