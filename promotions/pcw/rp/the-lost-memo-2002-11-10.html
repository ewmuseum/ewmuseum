The Lost Memo

10 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Title: The Lost Memo Featuring: Xavier Kannon Date: 10/11/02 “THAT’S IT... No more fucking with the Front Office... No more being conned by Welfare cheating fucking Niggers with their fat Coon bitches... No more spandex wearing fucking faggot masked fucktards ruining my night....” Our shot opens up on the Red, enraged face of PCW CEO Xavier Kannon. The camera tracks his upper body while he paces back and forth, a vein on the side of his head throbbing under his skin as his bright Green eyes burn with anger. Xavier : “First off, I have a pair of fucking Nelly wannabes telling me what matches to put them in, then that Rentboy FUCK shows up AND STOPS ME GETTING MY BELT BACK!” His lips are slightly parted in a scowl, showing his teeth grinding together in frustration after a BAD night at Fusion. “Your...... Belt..... Back!” The camera pans to show his desk where in the expanse of his leather chair, his wife Ellie~! is curled up, sneering herself. Xavier : “Heh, those peons may have had their moments of glory at Fusion but they don’t make the rules of this game...” In the chair, Ellie~! looks confused as she hugs her knees to her chest, trying to work out who does. Xavier : “... I do.” The CEO sneers and Ellie~! raises her head and eyebrows, ‘Ahhhhhhh’ing now she knows who makes the rules, Xavier looking at her with a peculiar expression and the little prep looking innocent as if she knew it all the time. Eleanor : “I, like, SO knew that.... And stuff.” A determined look crossing his previously angry face, Kannon strides over to his office’s door and pulls it open, looking outside for any sign of a PCW employee in their Docklands offices but all the lights are out. Xavier : “And what the fuck is up with these AngloPeons? Have they actually heard of working, I dunno... A FULL DAY?!?!?“ Still curled up in Xavier’s desk chair, Ellie looks at her chunky watch and mouths words to herself... Eleanor : “Big hand is, like hours... Little hand is, SO minutes... Hmmm, it’s like WAY digital thought...” After a little bit more studying she looks over to her husband who slams the door behind him, making her huddle tighter as it crashes into the frame. Eleanor : “It might, like, kinda be because it’s SO 2am?” Letting out a loud and gravely sigh, the CEO strides back towards the desk, his Navy Blue suit looking creased after a long day, his top button open and his tie loose around his neck... He looks both stressed and worn out. Xavier : “Fine, you’ll just have to be secretary and type what I say...” Her pale Blue eyes give away the fact she thinks it may be a little bit beyond her ‘Skills’. Eleanor : “Can’t I, like, just sleep or something? Typing sounds hard....” Xavi doesn’t seem to concerned about her reservations, his tunnel vision setting in as he paces back and forth in front of the desk. Eleanor : “I mean, I have, like...” Her vacuous eyes hop across each of her fingers. Eleanor : “10 fingers, and the keyboard SO has, like....” She tries to look at each key to count it but gives up when she gets to F4. Eleanor : “Seventeen hundred billion keys!” Before her, Kannon keeps pacing across his office, muttering under his breath what he has planned before snapping out of it to look to his wife with a distinct lack of patience at her reluctance to do anything other than sleep, shop or groom. She rolls her eyes. Eleanor : “Fine, I’ll, like, type and stuff!” Taking the keyboard off the desk, she places it on her lap, still sat cross legged in his chair, ready to type. Xavier : “Memo to all PCW staff from the office of Xavier Kannon...” The little prep looks up. Eleanor : “And Ellie~! Don’t forget Ellie~!” He turns to her, confused. Xavier : “Huh?” She points to herself while he looks annoyed his rhythm has been broken so early. Eleanor : “Like, the office of Xavier Kannon.... And Ellie~!... You know? Me?” Sighing, Kannon just gestures for her to type what she wants. Eleanor : “YAY ME!“ As she hurriedly types her name after his, Xavier gets back into his speech, the pacing starting up once more and eyes focused high onto the ceiling as he talks. Xavier : “From Turmoil, only White wrestlers will be able to enter Arenas fee of charge, all minorities will have to pay an “Entrance Tax” of $400 before they can set a foot inside... And they’re banned from bringing any fat women in with them... And get in touch with the Klan, having them work security should show them.” Feverishly, Ellie~! types away, her fingers becoming a blur. Eleanor : “Should.... Show.... Them!” Xavier : “Also, Tax ebonics... For each incomplete word or bad example of English, make them pay $500 on the spot...” Breaking from his rant, Kannon casts an eye over his wife. Xavier : “...Er, the Front Office are excluded from this rule.” Ellie~! grins. Eleanor : “Like, SO excluded and stuff!” Xavier : “Next... Anyone setting foot in a PCW ring MAY ONLY WEAR ONE MASK!!! Have security, Road Agents, whoever, enforce that... Anyone double masking you can hand over to the Klan guys we got to keep the Niggers in check to practice the new lynching techniques on or something.” Still, the little prep types at an unexpectedly fast pace. Eleanor : “Or....... Some....... Thing!” Xavier : “Hmmmmm... Anything I’ve forgotten?” Ellie~! thinks for a bit, then looks back blankly. Xavier : “Okay.... Send it out and then we can go.... Just hit the button.” Suddenly he realises this is Ellie~! he’s talking to. Xavier : And NOT the delete one!” She looks back at him almost annoyed that he thinks she’s ditzy enough to delete the memo. Eleanor : “Like... HELLO?!?!?! I’m SO not THAT dumb!” She sighs and reaches to the PC where after pressing the round button on the front of the tower, the whirring of the machine dies down as Kannon turns to her, the shot fading to Black as Ellie~! looks back with an innocent grin on her face. “Er.... I think I, like, pressed the wrong button and stuff!” ---- View Xavier Kannon's Biography
