Anger, Hate, and Misery - Part 1 - Bogus Can Be Good

28 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: May 28 [The blue 2002 Mustang convertible rides at a high speed through the California interstate. The angered young man behind the wheel is named 'Totally Tubular' Tommy. His usually cheerful blue eyes are slanted in anger. The happy go lucky grin that always lines his face has been wiped clean for the first time in a decade. His tan hands are turned pale as his grip on the steering wheel is vice-like. His bright blonde hair waves in the wind, hoping maybe to cool himself off, but it isn't helping one bit. His mind has been wandering for the past several days but it all comes back to the recent loss to Suicidal Tendencies on Friday Fusion] *We were totally robbed! Those bogus, stupid dudes never would have a chance against a most bodacious team like the Cowabunga Connection. They got lucky and now they are heinously marching through the tournament toward the tag team titles, THOSE ARE OUR TITLES!* [Tommy now begins pounding on the steering wheel, imagining that it's those bogus, stupid dudes, Suicidal Tendencies] *I'm sick and tired of me and Harry being the dudes that take all the bogus crap around here. I'm tired of being put in those stupid 'Your Fired If You Don't Suck Tyler Nelson's Small, Miniscule Coc---WHOA!* [In his blind rage Tommy doesn't notice the hitchhiker on the side of the highway and almost takes out the medium build man with his Mustang. Tommy recovers and shakes off the shock of almost killing a man. Suddenly a grin crosses his face, as a new feeling begins to come over his body] *That felt kind of bodacious. It would have been bogus to see that dude die, but it would have felt so GNARLY to have watched all that blood hit my windshield and to see his body parts decorate the road in a radical gore fest. Wait...that's pretty sick, dude. I shouldn't be having these thoughts. I shouldn't be thinking about other people dying, especially being hit by my Mustang. That's just not tubular, dude....but it's not like it happened or anything* [Tommy's grin grows a little larger as he jumps on the Santa Monica exit. The Mustang speeds through the late night, now with a goal] *I'm signed up for that Extreme Title match. That's pretty gnarly, but isn't this the second one of these I've been in? It is. Talk about PCW being TOTALLY heinous. I mean I guess I'd waste the entire tag team division and put them in an Extreme Title match while these new bogus teams battle for tag team gold. They have yet to pay their dues, they have yet to even taste the big time and they are getting a shot at PCW gold?!?! BOGUS!!!* [Tommy's Mustang begins slowing down as he hits Rodeo Drive. His eyes begin to scan the expensive stores that only a select few can enter. Gucci, Armani, and so on...if it's expensive then it's somewhere on Rodeo Drive. The Tubular One stops in front of Gucci shoes and stares at the front window display of fancy leather dress shoes] *I remember when I was in there buying a pair of black dress shoes to go with my bodacious tuxedo for the Surfing Convention. Those dudes treated me like I was some sort of bogus, poor dude. HOW DARE THEY!! I am 'Totally Tubular' Tommy!! I am the greatest professional surfer in the entire world, there are none more tubular than me! I should have a red carpet layed down everywhere I walk! I am the greatest technician in the wrestling world today! Nobody can compare to my true execution of moves, NOBODY!! IT's BOGUS!! ALL BOGUS!!* [Tommy jumps out of the Mustang with his tire iron in hand. He begins bashing through the window which immediately sets off an alarm, but he seems to not hear it at all as he keeps bashing through the window display. He knocks the shoes all over the display and even knocks some in the streets. He grabs the shoe holders and bashes them into the ground making them break into hundreds of pieces. As he now leaps out of the display and leans against the passenger side of his car sirens sound and two police cars pull up to the Gucci shoe store. The uniformed police officers get out with their guns pulled at the store, and not at Tommy. The dark haired, middle aged officer takes charge] Officer: Sir, what happened here? [Tommy seems absolutely stunned at how fast the cops showed up. He only beat the crap out of the window display for a minute or so. He then begins to notice that the officiers aren't pointing the guns at him] 'Totally Tubular' Tommy: Dude, it was the most bogus thing I ever saw. These black dudes jumped through the window and just started taking stuff. It was TOTALLY heinous. Officer: BLACK GUYS!! DAMN THOSE BLACK PEOPLE!! TTT: I tried to fight the dudes off but there was just too many of them. [The officer seems concerned about this brave young man] Officer: How many were there? TTT: Ummm, Uhhh, Ten of them. Officer: TEN?!?! Good Lord, boy! That's a lot of blackness you tried to fend off. TTT: I know, dude, it was like being in the Million Man March. Officer: Are you all right? TTT: Of course! I'm a most bodacious compadre and can certainly take a beating. Mostly I just broke a sweat but they got away with only a few pairs of shoes. Officer: I'm sure the owner will be very happy to hear that. You better head home, but I'll need your information to contact you about a reward. TTT: Ok... [The officer takes down Tommy's info and thanks him for all the help. The rest of the officers are going through the broken glass and fallen shoes hoping to find clues. Tommy drives off in his car with a very sly smile across his face] *Those dudes totally bought my bogus lie! Everybody always put me down for being stupid, even my own mother, but look who's stupid now!! I had that cop eating out of the palm of my hand! Stupid...PFT...NOT EVEN CLOSE, COMPADRE!* [Tommy jumps back on the interstate to head back home. Images of the broken glass and feeling it break in his hands begins to run through his head. An orgasmic-like smile crosses his face, something that he only reserves for playing Yahtzee with the bodacious babes. This is a different kind of pleasure, something that he's never felt before] *I like this feeling. I feel so bodacious and bogus at the same time. It's TUBULAR! I need more, I need to cause more havoc!* [Through the entire night he randomly breaks windows, flattens tires, and spray paints racial slurs on park walls. Driving the entire night away Tommy smiles the whole time, never showing the fear of getting caught. The feeling of that horrible loss to Sucicidal Tendencies has passed] *It's doesn't even matter anymore, dude. Those guys will never live up to our name, and certainly will never be on our level...EVER! The only thing I need to focus on is causing this chaos and havoc on this coming Friday. Why worry about tag team titles when I can be a singles champion? Especially the Extreme Champion! Every competitor will bogusly fear me and I will reign bodaciously. This is going to be so radical, dude* [The blue mustang peels out, leaving smoke behind in it's path. The Tubular One no longer feeling happy go lucky and extremely upbeat, he's feeling a new anger and a new pain. Whoever stands in his path may they be either fearful or run like hell because this bogus dude is not taking s**t from anyone]
