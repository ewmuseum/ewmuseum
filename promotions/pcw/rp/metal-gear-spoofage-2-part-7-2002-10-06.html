Metal Gear Spoofage 2 (part 7)

6 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/6/02 In a solitary chamber, a commando in a sailor suit stands paralyzed in fear. Having infiltrated a room full of hostages to locate agent Aimz, he now finds himself in a sticky situation. Hosselot: (Holding his ass) I hate my life. Aimz: Hold still, Hosselot's coming! Raiden: Buh....buh..... Aimz: I said hold still! Raiden: What do I doooooooo..... Leah: Jack do you know what day it is tomorrow? Raiden: Argh! Hosselot: You there! Trotting over with the posture of one who has just had a hand forcibly shoved up his ass, Hosselot inspects Raiden. Hosselot: Don't I know you from somewhere? Raiden: (In a squeaky voice) I am maid Helga, come to leave fluffy pillows and make bed. Aimz: (Slaps forehead) Hosselot: (Looking down) Uh.... Aimz: Oh, uh....I asked him to remove my tape. I'm ill you see. Hosselot: (Fanning his hand in front of his nose) I can tell that. Aimz: (Hangs head) Hosselot: But I know where you're really coming from, 'Colonel' Aimz. Aimz: What? Who told you? Hosselot: No need for denials. I know you were sent here to watch the president. Nearby Raiden dozes on his feet. He then snaps awake groggily as Hosselot draws his gun and points it at the captive. Hosselot: Sorry, stinky. You failed to carry out your duties. Aimz: You'll never escape the Lalelulelo! Hosselot: Oh is that so. For a few seconds Hosselot merely watches Aimz. In return, the agent watches him back....an awkward silence falls. Hosselot: .......you should be dropping dead of a heart attack any second. Aimz: What? A few more seconds pass.....Revolver Hosselot begins kicking the hostage lightly in the chest. Hosselot: Stupid buggy virus. Aimz: I don't know what you.....ARGH!!!!! Aimz begins spasming violently. The agent stands, stumbling weakly after Hosselot. Aimz: Now I understand....you're a mod too, you.... Finally the poor stinky person falls to the ground dead. Raiden kneels.....Revolver regards him. Hosselot: Who the hell ARE you? Raiden: I'm you. Hosselot: Pardon me? Raiden: Yeah, you heard me, I'm you! Hosselot: What do you mean? Raiden: I mean BYE! Jack suddenly darts past a confused Hosselot. Unfortunately he then trips over his own untied shoelaces, falling on his own face. Hosselot: (Shaking his head) My but you're pathetic. Raiden: (Groans) Suddenly a form leaps from the ceiling, swinging a blade. Hosselot moves his arm just in time to avoid having it amputated.....before him stands the ninja. Hosselot: You! You're the one who made me take this psychotic hand! Almost as if it hears him, Hosselot's hand reaches up and punches him in the nose. Hosselot: (Holding his face) You'll pay. Get him! A swarm of guards rush the room....the ninja begins fighting them off. Raiden stumbles to his feet and runs as fast as he can out of the room, the terrorists right on his tail. Raiden: Argh! At last Raiden rounds a corner, darting straight out of the building and back to the outside of the Shell. He slumps painfully, as his ear buzzes. Leah: Jack do you know what day it is tomorrow? Raiden: ...... Leah: You look so cute in that sailor suit, Jack! Raiden: (Ripping at the suit) Die. Leah: You say the sweetest things! Do you remember what day it is tomorrow? Raiden: NO! I DON'T! Leah: Tee hee! Raiden: ARGH! Burying his head in his arms, Raiden attempts to make it all go away. Raiden: (Whining) This suit's too tight on me! And it itches! And it smells! Colonel: Yes, it'd probably look better on a young boy, preferably one with olive skin. Leah: That was inappropr.... Leah stares at the Colonel blankly on the monitor. Leah: ....wait a minute, what did you say? Colonel: Foxdie in aisle two. Leah: WhaYEAAAARGH!!!!! Leah falls over, as Raiden stares at the Colonel. Colonel: ....what? Stop looking at me. Raiden shakes his head, then his ear then buzzes again though. Leah: (Groaning) Don't worry Jack, I'm still alive! Raiden: Why? Leah: Hehe! Oh yeah, do you remember what day it is tomorrow? Raiden: ARGH! Jack hangs up the connection, but again it rings as soon as he does. Raiden: (Clutching his ear) Listen you stupid bitch, I'm tired of your crap! Pliskin: I know I didn't hear what I think I just heard. Raiden: Uh, er.... Pliskin: ..... Raiden: Sorry....I..... Pliskin: I found a ride. Helicopter. Raiden: Can you fly one of those things? Pliskin: No, but I brought a gearhead with me. Pliskin's face fades on the monitor.....Raiden gasps in shock as it's replace by none other than Bobby Stormbringer. Raiden: YOU! Bobby: Do I know you? Pliskin: Do you know him? Raiden: I just killed you! Bobby: That was probably my brother Randy. Pliskin and Raiden: ...... Bobby: What? Pliskin: I really miss Byron. Anyway kid, we need to think about how to get those hostages out. Raiden: Aren't there any more helicopters? Pliskin: Well, there were....but I sabotaged the other. Raiden: You did? Pliskin: Well....take out the word 'sabotage' and insert the word 'forgot to put gas in the tank and wrecked horribly into the ocean'. Raiden: I see. Pliskin: So yeah. I.... Raiden: Huh? Raiden suddenly looks up to see a figure in a black cloak standing at the far end of the bridge. His terrible (and admittedly evil) voice booms out. Kriegidus: I've been waiting for you. A messenger from the mods. The person looks slightly over his heavy cape, regarding Raiden. Kriegidus: I know you from somewhere. Have we met? Raiden: (Shakes his head) Kriegidus: Did we used to work together? Raiden: (Shakes his head) Kriegidus: Go to the same school? Burn the same orphanages? Raiden: (Shakes his head) Kriegidus: Damnation, now this will bother me for weeks to come. Raiden: So you're the boss around here, eh? Kriegidus: Not just the boss....I'm the boss to surpass Dai Lo himself. The man raises his head, showing his very evil face. Kriegidus: Solid Snake! Abruptly a voice yells from above, a helicopter zooming into view. Pliskin: No! You're not Solid Snake! Kriegidus: And why wouldn't I be? Pliskin: Er....uh....you just aren't, that's all. Kriegidus: Why? Pliskin: Because! Kriegidus: Why why why? Pliskin: Because because because! Kriegidus: I'll tell you why. Because YOU are Solid Snake, brother! Raiden: (Gasps) Snake: Damn you! You had to blow my disguise didn't you? Kriegidus: Well I am evil after all. Kriegidus suddenly charges forward, a burst of flame rising around him. His cape goes up like flash paper as he looks up. Kriegidus: I'm a whole different game from Liquid! Snake: On the evil scale you're around a Tiddlywinks to Liquid's Monopoly. Kriegidus: Shut up. The evil one reaches out and grips both sides of the bridge railing. Muscles seem to pop from his body, he grows to twice his original size. Kriegidus: Now, let'sAGH!!!!! A grenade from the helecopter suddenly cuts off Kriegidus, he flies through the air. Landing on a nearby pipe, he looks up. Kriegidus: Is that the best you canAGH!!!!! Another explosion nails Kriegidus, causing him to launch straight off the bridge. High above, Snake looks over. Snake: Did I get him? For a moment all is silence.....then a roar erupts. Raiden: Oh crap! A harrier jet rises from below the bridge. On top of it stands Kriegidus, fighting against the wind and trying to maintain his balance. Snake: Oh crap! Argy: (At the helm) Oh crap! Kriegidus: (Looks down) Argy: Sorry boss. Kriegidus flexes, his muscles once again fading. Reducing to a normal size, he slowly walks towards the cockpit along the top of the plane. Kriegidus: (Walking) Argh.....argh..... Snake: Will you hurry it up? Kriegidus: Shut up! This is rather tricky. Snake: Is not! Kriegidus: I'd like to see you do it! Snake: Bring the plane up here and I'll do it with no problem. Kriegidus: Talk talk talk. Snake: You should talk! Raiden: Uh.... Kriegidus and Snake: What!? Raiden: Nothing. Finally Kriegidus reaches the hatch, which opens. He dives in, it seals back over him as the plane rises. Kriegidus: The world only needs one Dai Lo! Snake: Fine, I'll just kill you then. Kriegidus: DIE!!!!! The harrier's rockets shift into action as Kriegidus locks on. Raiden: CRAP!!!!!! To Be Continued
