Something is Wrong

10 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4-10-02 ::It’s been about 24 hours since the triumph victory at Spring Fling, the biggest match in Shawn Mosely’s PCW young career. It was a night of champions, celebrities, and pure craziness. Shawn lived up to the hype as he has done so many other times in his career. He walked into the PPV unknown and without a belt. He walked out a champion on the rise. Many questions have been put in the air after the match, though. Can Shawn continue his rise?, hold the belt?, or just move on to the next challenge. And on top of all of this, no one has seen the Golden One since Spring Fling. Now we finally get some answers. We are in Atlanta, Georgia. . home of James Creon’s big time gym. There is where Shawn trained for Spring Fling, and will continue to train until he retires, or when his body drops dead since wrestling is his life. We are in the training room where Shawn Mosely is sitting on the bench press, bench. He’s wearing a black beeder with black shorts with his dirty blonde hair tied back. James Creon is sitting right next to him as he is wearing a pair of brown dockers and a regular white button down shirt with the sleeves rolled up. They both start to converse:: “How are you doing ?” James says as he sits down about 5 feet away from him. “What? Great. . of course. I’m a champion now” Shawn replies as he starts his second set of reps. James just nods his head. “1. . . 2. . . 3 . . .4. . .” Shawn says after each rep. James looks on patiently as he waits for Shawn to finish off the set. “5. . 6. . 7. . .8 . .9 . .Te. . “ Shawn struggles to get out. “You can do it. Push!” James says to get Shawn past rep 10. “10! *Pauses* Damn . . I love this.” Shawn says as he struggles to catch his breathe. James looks on with a glare, knowing that Shawn is at his normal self, the slef in which James’s goal was to get him at the whole time. “You shocked a lot of people, Shawn. I’m proud of you.” James replies with a delightful smile. Shawn relax’s his hands from the bar and looks up at James. “James. . .I’m proud of me too. Never thought I’d make it back here. To the promise land as Martin Luther King called it.” Shawn says. “I’m glad to see everything is going great Shawn.” James replies as he gets up and pats Shawn on his shoulders and gets ready to walk to his office. “Wait. James” Shawn says with every square inch of oxygen he had left. James turns around immediately. “What?” James replies with an urgent look. “I need your help and approval.” Shawn says with an innocent look. James just sighs and responds. “And what would that be Shawn?” James asks. “I’m worried.” Shawn says. James cuts in quickly with words. “Worried about what? Your match with Andy Murray? Don’t worry. Shawn. The guy didn’t pose a problem Sunday, and won’t next Sunday.” James says strongly. Shawn just shakes his head “no”. “No. Something else.” Shawn replies. “What? What is it?” James says. “Well. *Takes a deep breathe* I can’t handle it anymore. I need to find Stacy” Shawn replies. “Stacy?! What are you talking about” James says with a confused face. “Stacy. My old girlfriend. I went to visit her the other day and she wasn’t there.” Shawn replies. “Why? That’s all in the past. Don’t mess things up now Shawn.” James says. Shawn shrugs his shoulders. “I’m going after to her James. . I’m going after her” Shawn replies. :James just gives Shawn a nasty look as he walks out of the room. Shawn continues to weight lift in preparation for his next match Vs Andy Murray. All this is on the eve of a very important match. Shawn is now on his search for possibly the only woman besides his mom that he has ever loved. Can this be the last piece of his life to complete his confused life, and make it enjoyable once again with a woman and a championship belt::
