Date: 3-29-02 **OOC NOTE: This takes place after the promo done by Sebastian 'Switchblade' Toomes, the OSW World Champion. For more info, check out the OSW site.** **Theme Music: 'Bouncing Off The Walls' by Sugarcult.** ----------------------------------------Dear Jenna, What on EARTH is the media doing here? Love,Jason---------------------------------------- [The scene comes up outside the CWS, where J-Core is cleaning up house after a LONG day of work. The warehouse has been swept, the ring's been taken care of...and now it's time to close up shop for the night, and take the rest of the night to relax and get ready for a grueling Extreme Title defense. Right?] Voice: Mr. Core! Mr. Core! [Nope. It's never quite that easy.] Voice: Mr. Core!! [A nattily dressed man busts into the room, yelling at the top of his well-dressed lungs.] Man: Jason Core? Extreme Champion Jason Core? [The always reserved J-Core lifts his head at this odd interruption, which has become an almost regulaar occurrence with the cast of characters that inhabit his life.] Jason: Don't tell me you want to join the school… Man: Actually, no. My name is Van Tibalt. Pleased to meet you. [The man rushes up to J-Core, and throws a tape recorder in his face.] Van: Mr. Core, as a discrening reporter of intelligence, I must ask you…are you going to Old School Wrestling? Jason: I beg your pardon? [Van adjusts his lapels and grins broadly.] Van: I work for [detail withheld], the HIGHEST rated insider wrestling website in America. [Jason groans. With good reason.] Van: I know for a FACT that your contract with PCW is an unguaranteed contract. Due to your prior injuries, when PCW signed you, they were afraid you might be a liability. Jason: Yep, yep. So what? Van: You've been seen training with the OSW World Champion. Jason: Yep. Van: There've been rumors you've been begging for an OSW job. Jason: Uh-huh. Van: And you're continually booked in brutal matches against a BRUTAL opponent. Jason: Sure sure. Van: All this leads to an unhealthy working environment, and you want to leave PCW, right? Jason: Nope. [Van blinks, and does a double-take.] Van: What?! Jason: J-Core isn't going ANYWHERE… [The poor reporter looking for his scoop looks dejected.] Jason: …until the new ownership's decided. [His ears perk back up.] Jason: Apparently, I work for the hardest working, best man in the business today, Duke Williams. So apparently, my situation is squeaky clean, and I wouldn't have it any other way. But if that scumbag Nelson gets back in control of PCW, I have a few options. Van: And those are? Jason: I can either get the hell outta Dodge, before the fed collapses…or I can stand toe-to-toe against a man that holds my career in the palm of his hand, and fight for what's right. Van: Wow… Jason: And it appears that the Swamp Dawg is working for 'the man' Tyler Nelson, and you know what that means? Van: What? Jason: Right now…I'm leaning towards staying and ripping Ty a new anus. And you can quote that. Van: Wow…got anything left to say to LeBlanc? Jason: I think I said everything to him I need to. See you on Friday, Karl. Now get the hell out of here, I've got a shower with my name ALL over it. [Van, a bit initmidated, rushes back out of the building in his double-espresso way. In the meantime, Jason takes a moment to chuckle to himself about the OSW situation…and go back to cleaning his one real home.]
