A Change of Heart: Part II

1 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: August 1st, 2002 [The Cathedral of Port-au-Prince is quite a magnificent attraction, it might not be the Sixteenth Chapel but it has its own variety of artistic elegance. Its attractive modern structure, mesmerizing hazel color and artistic religious window make this Cathedral, the city’s most populated church. It is early morning on Wednesday, the heat-wave is over powering the city, not humid, just warm. A few people walking the streets in their shorts and sandals, sporting their shades and a few women dressed in bikinis and the built men strutting topless on the sidewalks. From far, the arrogant thug model, Jerome “Blayze” Jordan is seeing taking with a hooded figure near the entrance of the Cathedral, he nods his head and the person enters while Blayze now leans against the railing of the steps. We notice in the palm of his hands, a Verizon wireless cell phone, putting it to his ear, it seems a call has been forwarded] Blayze: Who is this? Darren: Where in God’s name are you? [Blayze holds back his laughter] Blayze: There has been a change of plans my brother. Darren: Dammit! You better not have… Blayze: Silence, just walk outside where all the cabs are usually parked Darren: Fine.. [We can vividly hear the on and off mumbling anger of Darren as he walks through the airport with his cell in hand] A minute or so later Darren: Yeah Im outside and? Blayze: Took you long enough. Now what you are going to do is look for a specific cab. Darren: What the fuck???? There is like a dozen cabs, they are all the same! [Blayze notices how Darren is getting very restless, but keeps his cool and his smile] Blayze: Yes I know that but you do you see a yellow one? [The tension in Darren’s system rises] Darren: Are you trying to be a smart-ass little bro? Blayze: It was a question and I am still expecting my answer Darren: Yes I see your fuckin cabs, but one thing, where the hell are you? Blayze: Where I told you we were going to meet, at the Port-au-Prince Cathedral, I was not lying my friend. Darren: Yo I do not hear no priest preaching proverbs or any choir chants, how do I know you aint on some whore filled street, sitting at a resto-bar as I make an ass of myself here at this airport. [Blayze shakes his head with a short chuckle] Blayze: Darren, you can do one of two things: Believe me, come down to the Cathedral and all things can go as planned or you can think I am bullshitting you, and search all over the town for me with no money, no guide and no plane ticket back home. Yes Darren, without me, you aint getting back to Compton, so unless you follow my specific directions, you will be wondering the streets of Port-au-Prince like a dumbfounded tourist. Darren: Jesus…. Blayze: Now, do you, by any chance, seeing a slender, tall Haitian male with a black buttoned shirt, black dress pants, black shades and corn rows Darren: Hold Up…Oh yeah, I see that punk Blayze: Go over to him, state who you are and who sent you, he will nod and open the backseat door for you. You will get in and just say “ Port-au-Prince Cathedral”. He knows the way here. Darren: Alright, but I swear, if I end up in some transvestite voodoo club, oh you gonna die if I catch you [Another laugh from Blayze as this time he cannot hold it in] Blayze: You just have to have trust in me Darren: That is what I am afraid of Blayze: Fine then, I will be sitting on the steps of the Cathedral, see you in about, oh I should say, 20 minutes? Darren: I guess so, I aint no my fuckin way around. This driver, he better be an actual cab driver Blayze: Yes Yes, don’t worry, just go in the cab and allow him to do his job Darren: Aight, holla at you when I arrive, Peace Blayze: Peace [And with that, Blayze hangs up and hooks his cell back onto his belt. The time passes by quite a bit slowly, it seems like an eternity as Blayze focuses on his watch, only 2 minutes have gone by. No smoke to light, no Bacardi to drink and no ice to water his sweaty forehead, talk about a bitch and a half. As Blayze sits on those concrete steps, he begins to wonder about what might go down once his brother arrives] Why am I even going through with this? * Sigh *, I guess when the time comes to face the music, everyone has to pay their price. Will my own flesh and blood accept the on going procedures? What I am doing, is it just plain right? Oh well, might as well play it by ear [Blayze looks like he is about to fall asleep on the side of the railing when finally the ordered cab makes a stop in front of the Cathedral. As the backseat door opens, a huge figure, about 6’4 steps out, built a la Brock Lesnar with his immense black body, pumped up biceps and massive six-pack busting through his white wife-beater, steps out of the cab. Darren Jordan, the monster of a man, thanks the cab driver and begins to walk up towards the steps. His brother Jerome, glancing at him with a devious smile, gets up and as his Darren walks up to him, he taps him on the shoulder then hugs Darren] Blayze: Haha! Long time to see brother, how’s the life? Darren: You betta not have dragged my ass down here just to ask me how I am doing. Blayze: Oh no, not at all, please, come into the Cathedral with me [Darren moves back with a confused look on his face] Darren: You want me to pray to the Lord and ask for forgiveness or some bullshit like that? Blayze: Just follow me [Darren shakes his head in disagreement but follows anyways] Darren: Why do I put myself in these situations [They enter the astonishing cathedral and right away, Darren begins to admire the artistic work done on the foundation and walls. Walking down the aisle as they pass the newly polished wood benches, each equipped with their own pocket bibles. It is silent, no priests and no choirs, not even a single soul kneeling down in the benches or lighting a candle for a loved one. A few candles have already been pre-lit and the choir’s piano seems totally brand new. They arrive at the carpet-covered steps and walk up onto the next stage, facing the tabernacle.] Darren: So now I would like to know the point of this whole meeting. Blayze: Allow me to do one thing first. [Blayze turns his back and dumps his two fingers into the bowl of holy water. Taking them out, he makes the sign of the cross on his forehead and murmurs an “Amen”.] Blayze: I feel much better now Darren: Spill the beans brother Blayze: When I received your letter the other day, I was quite concerned about your emotional state when writing it. I sensed a tiny little bit of jealousy in your behavior. Darren: Hold the fuck up.. Blayze: Sshhh, no vulgarity in the church my brother Darren: Jealousy?? Why in G…Why would I be jealous of you? Infact I am glad you finally achieved something but it was the fact that you joined a federation that you have trashed on airwaves and interviews all around. I do not want to say this but don’t you consider yourself a hypocrite? Blayze: I consider myself as a human being with choices and goals. My personal goal is to prove that black athletes are the real stars in the sports entertainment industry, not a bunch of white nazi seekers or red hair scientology freaks. I came to earn myself some exposure, to “return to my roots” as they say. I am Jerome Jordan, one of the most infamous thugs to ever step foot on the streets of Compton ever since I was young. I went through hell and survived it, now I am going to take a bunch of pathetic morons on the same journey I went through, except this time, it will be more brutal [Darren puts his palm on Blayze’s forehead as if he was checking his temperature] Darren: You see a bit warm, are you fine? No brain wires have disconnected or anything? Blayze: Brother, you saw what we were in CCW, the most dominant force to ever hit that fed. You knew that I had a behavior that would soon be unleashed from my system, setting off a brand new string of sadistic satisfaction. That’s why I am feel I am ready, ready to go further then I did in CCW, proclaim my spot in PCW and force them to comprehend that the future of professional wrestling lies in the hands of Black Power. I raise the fist, not to look arrogant, but to make a statement. I take no prisoners and that is a fact. Darren: So that’s it, you are just going to throw all the good you had in you and return to the lifestyle you were so eager to get out of? Now you walk around with that baby blue bandana, acting as if still being a member of the Crips is just something to accept. You have to understand brother that it is not an easy road to revisit, I do not want to see you jump back into that circle of gang warfare Blayze: You are NOT my guardian anymore!! [Blayze steps up and shoves his face right in Darren’s and hisses] Darren: I’d step back if I were you [Blayze takes a step back and begins to settle down] Blayze: You are right, I need to just relax, I cannot go buckwild when I have a fierce competitive match to worry about. Darren: Do not make the same mistake I did, do not try and go back into any gang related affairs. Jerome, as your brother… Blayze: Silence!! [Blayze’s voice echoes around the church, sending an eerie vibe into the atmosphere] Blayze: I do not need anyone, especially my own flesh and blood to tell me how to run my life. Understand? Darren: I actually think it is you who does not understand. [As Darren and Blayze get into a heated argument, a hooded figure is shown stepping out of one of the confessional booths. Slowly creeping up the aisle as Darren sees nothing, Blayze notices the figure over the shoulder of Darren and begins to light up with a devilish smirk.] Blayze: You know Darren, maybe you are right, I do need to understand more. Am I really smart by getting myself into this mess? I do not think so, what I will do now, to wash away my sins, I will step into one of those confessionals and speak to the father. Darren: So you come to your senses I see Blayze: Yes I have, but I think you just lost yours… Darren: What? [Blayze points to behind Darren and as he turns his head, he receives a deafening blow to the head with what seems to be a lead pipe. As Darren hits the wooden floor like a ton of bricks, a loud “thump” echoes enormously around the cathedral. ] Blayze: You are still right my brother, I do not understand….do not understand how I could still consider you my brother! [The hooded figure sticks the pipe back into his shirt and pulls off his head. Sporting a baby blue bandana, it seems Blayze hired a local crip member to get the job done on his brother] Blayze: You have done well [The hooded figure nods in agreement. Blayze pulls out his wallet and hands him a 100$ bill. The crip member nods again and makes his way towards the exit.] Blayze: I am sorry Darren but now as I leave for Texas and get ready for my match on Fusion, I am afraid you will just have to find your own way back to Compton. [Blayze pulls out Darren’s return flight ticket and rips it to shreds, having the pieces slowly glide through the air and land on the floor, scattered around Darren. Blayze gives another sign of the cross and murmurs another “Amen”. As Darren lays there on his stomach, in a state of unconsciousness, Blayze turns away and walks towards the exit of the Cathedral.]
