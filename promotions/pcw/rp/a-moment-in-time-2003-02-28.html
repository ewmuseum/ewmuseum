A Moment In Time

28 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2/28/03 Laying in the bed beside her, I watch the darkness flow before my eyes. Diving through the air towards the ground below, I see it speed towards me as a bullet fired at my heart. As I fall, as the wind sweeps my hair up and casts my face into an even more horrid and twisted mockery of what it was, my mind flashes to earlier times. To future times. To each time in between. A church. I enter it, paying no mind to the gawkers around me. I looked a bit differently then....that was a lifetime ago, after all. That was before my death. That was before my rebirth, into something that cannot die. Ah, but I was cursed then as well, wasn't I? Cursed with ignorance....I did not know what I was then. Or at the very least, my memories were hazy. Much as they are now. For anything I attempt to remember before that point, fades into the mist like a ship eternally lost at sea. She's laying beside me, barely stirring as the black rain falls. I walk down the aisle, a tattered hood covering my slightly scarred face. Oh, how things changed. How my appearance changed! It is funny what being engulfed in a blaze will do to you. Both physically and mentally. I take my seat, and wonder vaguely as I continue to fall and continue to lie and watch the one I adore. Am I in charge of my destiny here, as I watch the events of my recent past? Or was I ever in charge? Was I falling from this building as I sat in that church? Was I laying beside Bethany, even as my first glimpse of her approached? The perils of knowing too much for your own good. I laugh as I fall, sit and gaze. 'Why are you holding the fire in your hand like that?' I seem to have missed a few seconds, for here she is in front of me. The words escape my lips in past, present and future. 'Because fire is beautiful, child.' It would be enough to move me to tears, if I still had tears to shed. The past, which can never be revisted. At least humans have the benefit of being able to die. They can die, and can escape the grand torture. The torture of knowing that each moment that passes, can never be revisited. Every mistake made, can never be rectified. Both human and demon, monster and hero must know this. This great, cosmic joke.....that every one of us are thrown into this world merely to suffer. I however, also shall never leave it. There is no escape for me. If there is a God, he should die a thousand deaths for creating such a nightmare. If there is no God, then there is no justification and no rhyme nor reason for any of this. In either case, we are all cursed. In a few moments I will escape the church with this little girl. In a few moments I will hit the ground and claim yet another faceless life. In a few moments I will lay my head down and let the darkness claim me. I wonder now which it could be. The former, or the latter? God, or no God? If the first, I am doomed to never be able to face him down. For I shall never die. I know this now. I shall never be allowed to slap his holy face, and curse him. I shall never know the grand joy of what would be to come afterwards. If the second, then perhaps an even worse fate for us all. Perhaps I truly am the only God. A God who falls to the earth, who sits in a church toying with fire like a child, and who lays on a bed and loses himself. A God who knows not which reality is the current, or if any is current at all. A God who is as much of a victim as those he kills. A God who is little more than a vampire, save for the simple fact his bloodlust stems from a never ending hatred, one that can never be sated. What, what is lesser of the two evils? It is a question that I cannot answer. The answer itself might be the only thing to destroy me, if ever I knew it. However, not knowing might destroy me as well, in time. I have an eternity to ponder it. An eternity. That word echoes through my blackened soul, mocking me, tearing me into blood soaked shreds. Eternity. No escape for me. Eternity. Never escaping. Eternity. The door breaks in the old church, revealing my meaningless pursuers. My feet hit, kicking up the dust of the pavement erected to cover and choke the earth. My mouth opens as I lie in bed. I rise. I lift my knife into the night before my victim. My eyes widen in the darkness. I scream as I see her face again for the first time. I scream as my blade sinks into flesh again. I scream as I stare into the abyss, and know I shall never know anything else. I scream, and my nightmare engulfs me, holding me in her arms, never to let me go.
