Hunted Saga Part Eight: Close Encounters

3 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/4/02 The camera opens in downtown Chicago, Illinois, in one of the main roads in a birds-eye kind of camera position. We are placed specifically above the walkways of the city, as hundreds upon hundreds of people walk in both directions, attempting to get to their various destinations. The camera zooms in focusing upon the now mentally restored John Crichton and Aeryn Sun walking along the street, side by side. Crichton moves his lips closer to Aeryn’s ear, so no unwanted listeners have the chance of over hearing the conversation. “Ya know, we really shouldn’t be walkin’ around in the public like this. They could still be looking for us, Aeryn.” Crichton stated with a hint of concern etched into his voice. Aeryn simply shuts her eyes and shakes her head, seemingly in disagreement. “Crichton, I’ve told you, they’re definitely still looking for you, but I need to draw my savings out of the bank before we can go on this road trip.” Aeryn replies in a cool calm manner. “Yeah all I’m saying is, it’s too dangerous for us to go out and draw the money out of the account ourselves.” Crichton argues equally calm. Aeryn turns to face him in an aggressive manner. “So you want to get even more people involved in this thing!” Aeryn shouts, losing all coolness. Crichton takes a step back in shock. “No I don’t mean that.” he replies. “Well John, shut up and lets get through this.” Aeryn commands with a stern tone in her voice and an equally stern look upon her face. Crichton simply leant forward and kissed her tenderly upon the lips. That was the trouble with Crichton. Aeryn mused silently You just can’t keep a straight face with him around. “Come on lets just get our asses in the bank and then out again before we do get seen by somebody.” Aeryn simply states pushing open the big glass bank doors and entering the massive, marble tiled bank reception area. Crichton and Aeryn continue to the receptionist’s massive desk, the central point of the room. Crichton noticed that the receptionist was a little too cheery for his liking, but then again he mused, it was her job. “We have an appointment to see the bank manager at 12.30pm.” Aeryn stated. The receptionist nodded then looked down, into her logbook and after a couple of seconds looks back up, with a wide smile upon her face. Crichton could feel his stomach churn, for he didn’t like people who were that happy all of the time. “I assume you’re a Mr. John Crichton and Ms. Aeryn Sun, yes?” The woman questions. Aeryn nods “Yes. That’s us.” Aeryn simply replies. “In that case you may go directly into the bank managers office. It is located at the very back of the bank.” The receptionist informs, erecting another massive smile upon her face. Aeryn nods and erects a smile of her own, which Crichton suspecting was to patronise the receptionist. “Thank you very much” Aeryn replies. Crichton and Aeryn then begin to move away from the reception area and make their way to the back of the building in the direction, of the clearly marked Bank Manager room, as Crichton and Aeryn pass, the camera stops and focuses on a suspicious looking man reading a local news paper. When Crichton and Aeryn are out of sight, he slowly lowers the opened newspaper and looks in the direction in which they went. We can clearly mke out the man to be none other than Crichton’s unknown pursuer, Mr. Fleming. The camera cuts to inside a luxurious looking office, with mahogany tables and chairs potted plans and filing cabinets. An old man, with a bald head and small spectacles is sat behind the desk, looking through some papers, occasionally signing a few of the documents casually, with his expensive looking ball point pen. Then a knock can be heard at his door and the bank manager places the documents back upon his desk. “Enter!” The bank manager beckons. The wooden door opens and Crichton and Aeryn enter the room, shutting the door behind them carefully. The bank manager gets to his feet in their presence and holds his hand out to them both. Crichton steps forward first and shakes the man’s hand. Crichton lets go of the man’s hand and he instantly pulls his hand back in a flash and begins to shake it painfully. Crichton shrugs. “Sorry Mr. Lebowitz, I guess I don’t know my own strength.” Crichton simply expresses. Mr. Lebowitz simply shakes his head. “Don’t worry about it Mr. Crichton.” Mr. Lebowitz replies with a homely smile etched upon his face. “Oh you can call me John.” Crichton states happily. “Okay John please, both of you sit down.” Mr. Lebowitz Crichton and Aeryn take their allotted mahogany chairs opposite the bank manager known as Mr. Lebowitz. Lebowitz leans forward a little. “Well what can I help you two with? You were a little hazy on the phone earlier.” Mr. Lebowitz declared. “Well Mr. Lebowitz, I simply want to extract all of my money from my account in cash.” Aeryn replied. “Why? Are you unhappy with this bank or something?” Mr. Lebowitz questions, almost sounding a little bit hurt. Aeryn reassuringly shakes her head. “No it isn’t anything to do with…” Aeryn began. “Are you moving to another bank, I assure you we have the best interest rates in the whole city.” Mr. Lebowitz almost pleads. “No, like I was saying, we’re not moving to any other bank, we just want to extract the money out of our account. To tell you the truth, we’re going on a cross country excursion across the United States.” Aeryn explains. “Ah…I see. I think that I can help you out with this. Let us discuss the terms and consitions.” Mr. Lebowitz responds as the camera cuts back to the main banking area. The camera focuses in on Fleming as he is still looking in the direction of the bank manager’s office. As soon as the office door shuts, Fleming swiftly extracts himself from his current seat and makes his way stealthily over to the office. Fleming put his ear up against the wooden door and attempting to listen in of the conversation. Then the noise of the hefty bank doors being swung open distracts his hearing ability and instinctively looks in the direction of the source of the noise. The camera follows his gaze as two hefty looking guys, wearing long leather coats and tights over their heads, compressing their features in an almost comic way. Then the man on the left of our view raises his shotgun in the air and fires at the roof, presumably to get everyone’s attention. The resultant of the blast, unleashing a part of the plaster board ceiling, which falls and hits one of the bank tellers on the head, knocking him unconscious. “Everyone, you guessed it, this is a bank robbery.” The bank robber on the right declares to the entire bank. “Right now, everybody get on the floor NOW!” The other bank robber commands With that everybody in the bank immediately jumps to the floor, some that don’t get down quick enough, are shoved to the floor violently. Fleming felt his stomach churn as One of the heads over to his position. The robber rounds the corner and immediately pulls his shotgun up to eye level and aims it at Fleming. “Come on fool, get your ass back there with rest of them!” The robber demands in a very raised voice. Fleming simply pulls a disappointed expression on his face and casually walks over to the rest of the bank’s customers. This seemingly infuriates the robber and he pushes Fleming a little, Fleming instinctively turns with speed and lands a punch to the robbers jaw. The punch itself held no specific power, but the robber was caught off guard and falls to the ground letting go of his gun. “Boy, you just don’t know who your dealing with!” Fleming shouts with rage clearly apparent within his voice. “No, you don’t know who you’re dealing with!” The voice of the other robber boomed from behind Fleming. Fleming began to turn to view the man, but felt a hard blow between his shoulder blade, before completing the action. The robber pulls his shot gun back up to eye level as he retracts the shotgun from striking Fleming to the ground. The other robber gets to his feet and plants the butt end of his shotgun to Fleming’s ribs. The first robber then turns to the rest of the civilians within the bank. “See this is what you will receive if you defy us! Simply co-operate and we’ll be outta here before you know it.” The first robber reasons, before throwing Fleming into the mass of the other captives. The robbers then seemingly huddle up for a private conversation. The camera zooms in, in order to capture the conversation. “Did you check all of the rooms back there?” the robber who hit Fleming in the back with butt end of his shotgun, whispers. “No, I found him outside. I don’t think he is the bank manager.” The other robber declares. “Right then, go back and find him.” The other bank robber begins “And no more screw ups!”
