In Search of the Joblin...Part 3.

4 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10-4-2002 The shot opens to show a magnificent sunrise. As the sun starts to slide above the horizon to reveal a cloudless sky, the shot pans down to a small campsite. Venneman Pendragon rolls his bedroll and takes in the morning air wearing only his pants and boots. Kona, his companion, lies sprawled on the ground, still in all his clothes. Pendragon sighs as he walks his bedroll over to Xerxes and ties it behind the saddle. He pulls a canteen from a saddle bag and strides over to where Kona lies, and dumps the contents onto the sleeping man’s face. Instantly, Kona is ripped from his slumber and is on his feet with one hand around Venneman’s throat. After a quick look around, he drops his hand and looks quizzically at the larger Pendragon… Kona: Where have ye brought me? Damned if my head is not fuzzy as hell though… Venneman: Thou agreed to ride with me to Domfilla. Lyco the Cruel awaits us, my friend. Kona: Yes…Lyco the Cruel. He and I have a small..Debt that we need to settle. Venneman: As do he and I. The score is far too much in the vermin’s favor for my tastes as it stands. But he ne’er expected me to return. To return and vanquish him as I should have done the first time I had the chance… Kona: Well, then…What do we wait for? The day is fresh and new, and we have many miles to cover before Domfilla. Venneman stares at the suddenly eager to be off Kona. He smiles and shakes his head before walking over to his horse to pull a chain mail shirt and cloak from a saddlebag… Kona: What? What was that look for? Venneman: Thou hast been passed out for nearly eight hours and when thou awaketh, thou wouldst preach to me about time being wasted. I made good time this past night leading thy horse with thou strapped to the saddle. I cannot believe thou has no stiffness of neck or of back. Kona throws back his head and roars with laughter. After a good laugh, he looks at Pendragon, who has finished dressing… Kona: Once we barbarians are up, we are ready to take on the world…Hmm…You speak strangely, outlander. Why is that? Venneman: I know not what thou meanest, barbarian. I know of no other way to speak… Kona shakes his head and claps Venneman on the back. He Strides over to his horse and leaps into the saddle… Kona: Come, Venneman. Time is short, and the way is long…Remember. In the lands between here and Domfilla, there may be danger…Bandits, monsters, anything you can imagine may be waiting upon us. We must be always on our guard. Venneman: I am always on my guard regardless, barbar; ‘tis how I have stayed alive this long. Kona laughs as Pendragon climbs into his saddle and the two start off across the untamed wilderness, which unbeknownst to Pendragon, is known by the name of “The Blackened Lands” by the people of The Land. The two top a rise and Pendragon sees nothing out of the ordinary. The view is phenomenal. Lush green trees and wild grasses cover a vast valley that stretches out for miles ahead. Human hands had yet to defile this wonder of nature, as had not been the case in Pendragon’s world. Here though, Pendragon reflected the health of the land. He seemed to be even stronger in this wilderness than in the black top covered land that he was most used to. As the two men ride, they pass plants and creatures that are definitely foreign. A bird flies by, but it is like no bird Venneman has seen. It lacks a beak and seems to be covered in fur rather than feathers. Pendragon stares open mouthed at the bird before shaking his head and concentrating on the way ahead… Kona: This is the land before we come to a place called the Blackened Lands by the locals. Here, the land is as it should be; healthy, vibrant and full of life. In the Blackened Lands, the natural beauty of the land has been twisted by an ancient evil that is said to have taken abode there in times past. Supposedly, the darkness moved on, yet the lands never fully recovered from the taint of the evil that roosted there. Though it seems as though it tries to heal, the land lacks the recuperative powers that it needs. Venneman: If the place is so bad, why do we not take another way, you and I? Kona shakes his head… Kona: The fastest route to Domfilla is straight through those blighted lands. I know that your mission is important to the land, so I will take the chance. You and I will be able to survive the hazards we may come in contact with…It merely pains my heart to see the land wounded as it is when I know that it should be beautiful as this land is…….. Pendragon holds a hand up and motions for Kona to follow him into a dense thicket. Momentarily, five mounted men pass by, armor clanking. They speak a tad loud for what it appears they are about… >Boss said it’d be worth it to kill the blonde fellow… >Aye. Said it would line our pockets with gold… >Did you ask about the other one? >If he gets in the way, kill him. >Easy enough. >That will probably be the way to go anyhow.. >Aye. Lyco will want to see both their heads… >DAMNIT! Speak not his name. He wants no one to know of this action… >Yes…An unfortunate accident in the Blackened Lands, that’s what he said. >Accident. Indeed… The men laugh as they ride off, and the shot comes back to show Pendragon’s face, his eyes tittering on the brink of insanity… Venneman: An accident. Lyco would send these brigands to plan an accident for the Lord King. They will pay…They will pay dearly. Kona: Why don’t we just ride up behind them and kill them now? Would be a simple thing to do. Venneman: Nay, Kona. I want these fools to feel true fear. I want yonder bastards to experience a great series of…Accidents. Nobody plans my assassination. Nobody. Kona, did you recognize anything about those men? Kona: Aye. They wear the sigil of the House of Dannon. Venneman: Crazier’s men. Should I have expected less? We follow, but keep our distance. The hunters just became the hunted….And they are hunted by a for more fearsome adversary than we were… The two men draw out of the thicket and ride in pursuit of the five men who would hunt them… “Someone knows I am here. It matters not. I will still do what I am here to do. Rid the land of the vermin named Lyco. Just as I plan to rescue the wench and rid the world of PCW’s Syko at Red October. Everyone was in for a rather large surprise from the Lord King. I was going to enjoy inflicting this pain….HAHAHA…HAHAHAHA!!!” In his minds eye, Pendragon laughs uncontrollably, yet he maintains a calm face and body. His craziness is a deep rooted insanity, intertwined with his very soul. It is what makes him great…
