Revenge Saga Part 18: Examining the Opposition...

29 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 29/6/02 OOC Note – The section about the dog isn’t supposed to be too racist, that actually happened to my Grandad, obviously not in Harlem… Revenge Saga Part 18: Examining the Opposition… Fleming entered his officer, closely followed by Mach 1, and headed directly over to the silver suitcase. He flicks the two catches upon the front and opens it for Mach 1 to see, he then sits upon his black leather chair, his fingers interlocked resting upon the table before him. A smile develops upon his face as Mach 1’s eyes widen at the sight of the money. Fleming: I bet you’ve never seen that much money before! Mach 1: [Looking up from the money] Of course I have! [Muttering] I used to rob banks for a living you know! Fleming: [Fleming nods, his smile remaining firmly placed upon his face] So do we have a deal? Mach 1: I think so. Fleming: Excellent! Mach 1: But I can’t make the decision for the entire ground. Fleming: No? I thought you assumed control of The Thunderbolts? Mach 1: No we have a silent leader, who doesn’t want the world to know he’s still alive. Fleming: [Raising his eyebrow in suspicion] Really? Mach 1: Yeah. I’m gonna have to run the idea by him first. Fleming: I see. So when will I get to meet this mysterious leader of yours? Mach 1: [Smiling] You don’t. Fleming: May I ask why? Mach 1: Because our leader sees nobody…but us! Fleming: [Nodding] I see. That’s not much of an arrangement you have there! Mach 1: Maybe not, but it’s the only one there is. Mach 1 turns and walks out of Fleming’s luxurious office and makes his way back to his fellow teammates, leaving Fleming with a puzzled look upon his face. He didn’t like this. He didn’t like this one little bit. The Thunderbolts already had a leader and Fleming saw this as a threat to the entire mission. This leader of theirs is a known element to the equation. Granted, it could end up in his favour, but he very much doubted it. Anyhow, there was nothing he could do about it now anyway. The Thunderbolts were the only group of people who could pull off the job for him, they were perfect for this mission. In the battle between good and evil in the world, good had taken had taken the dominance, and there were very few super-villains left for hire. Plus, he knew that when it came down to the crunch The Thunderbolts could get the job done. But this new element to the equation meant that this whole situation was precarious, very precarious indeed. ************ Crichton had been at the same bar now for the last two hours, and was already through his fifth J.D. and Coke. A combination of things had prevented him from leaving the establishment, The perfect credentials for any successful public house! He mused silently, taking another sip of his drink. At first it was the reluctance of another confrontation with Jaden, then it was the very attractive bar woman who he was still trying to chat up, and the final thing was Shabazz. His ugly face was all over the TV, almost haunting Crichton. He would face the Black Civil Rights man at the up-coming Pay Per View entitled Gold and Glory, very fitting indeed. PCW’s Television Title had been vacant for more than a month now, ever since the roster cut way back in May. “The Chief Anarchist” John Crichton had won the title from the very impressive TV Champion of the time, The Pharaoh and the man known as Jason Core, in a three way dance. Crichton won the title fair and square, no outside interference, no cheating, just the simple one…two…three in the middle of the squared circle. Unfortunately, during the match The Pharaoh was injured and put on the shelf for a couple of weeks, leaving no real challengers for ‘his’ title. Then it happened. The roster cut. Mere hours after he beat The Pharaoh and Jason Core, the entire PCW roster was fired, and all but one title was vacated. Now, months ahead of the roster cut and reformation, all but two champions had been decided. The Continental and The Television Champion. Both matches to decide the champions would happen at the Pay Per View, aptly titled Gold and Glory. From the PPV, Crichton hoped to gain both of these attributes from the event, he wanted the Television Title and all of the glory that went with that. He felt he was ready, he was in peak physical condition and he wanted it. He wanted it more than Shabazz at any rate. He once tasted the glory that came with becoming a champion, even if it was mere hours. The crowd reacted to him differently, the other workers in the back looked at him differently in the locker room. Then the title was literally snatched away from his grasp. Then came Shabazz. He decided to fuck with John Crichton, he wanted to get higher up the card…he wanted to get recognised by the wrestling fans…he wanted to get pushed higher and higher up the card. Well over the last couple of weeks, Crichton had warned him and proven to him that he was the one with whom not to fuck. Crichton had dished out savage beating’s continuously to the man over the past couple of weeks. So much as happened between the two of them, Crichton could barely recall how and why the whole thing had started. The only thing he did know, was that he hated Shabazz. He hated everything the man stood for…the small minded racial issues he stood and often contradicted himself with…everything about the man he despised. This was obvious when Crichton kicked his ass for the first time, backstage at a house show two weeks ago. Crichton bust Shabazz’s ass all over the place, drawing blood with the greatest of ease. Then there was the four way dance, occurring not two weeks ago, in which he faced Shabazz, End and El Diablo. Crichton almost ended Shabazz’s career with one of his patented moves – “The Punishment of the Guilty”. He had failed to do so, but it wasn’t through the want of trying. Deep within his thoughts he heard something, something from the outside. The more he concentrated upon the noise, the more he left his thoughts, until finally he was literally ripped out of his day dream and back into the harshness of reality. He realises the noise was somebody speaking…speaking to him. He looks to his left and sees Reginald’s mouth flapping. Crichton: Uh…I’m sorry Reginald could you repeat that? Reginald: Oh I was just saying that I had a dog named nigger. Crichton: [Incredibly not interested] Really? Reginald: Yeah, one day I was walking the dog through Harlem… Crichton: [Still not interested] Yeah? Reginald: Then the dog ran away from me. You know what I did then? Crichton: No. Reginald: I went through Harlem calling after my dog. Crichton: [Crichton spits out a mouthful of Jack Daniels] You did what? Reginald: Yeah, I went through Harlem shout Nigger! Nigger! Nigger! Luckily a police car was driving otherwise I woulda been killed. Crichton: Yeah I can imagine! So is there any morale to this story? Reginald: No…not really, just that Shabazz reminds me the guy who tried to kill me. Crichton: Well that was useful [Sarcastically] Reginald: Well everything I generally say is always useful. [Standing from the bar stood] Well I gotta go now see ya buddy. Crichton: Awwww…come on stay for a while, I’ll get you another drink. Reginald: Naw…I got be back in the home by 8.
