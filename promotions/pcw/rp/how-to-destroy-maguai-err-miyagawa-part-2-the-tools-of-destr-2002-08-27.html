How to destroy Maguai---err...Miyagawa - Part 2 - The Tools Of Destruction

27 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: August 27 [It's truly ashame just how underestimated the Gremlins movies have gone. It was totally the early 80's when the first gnarly film made it's debut. The most radical animatronics involved to make all those totally cool creatures come to life is something that still boggles the noggin' to this day. How can you not forget the absolutely gnarly scene in which the resident Maguai, Gizmo, is kickin' it in his pink convertible, chasing down that bogus Gremlin dude. You know that bogus one, with the spiked hair and the yellow eyes. It was most creepy at first, but it passed. The second one was the most underrated of all, dudes. The Gremlins took over the entire building that Billy worked in and it was a fight to take out all the bogus Gremlins. It's still most disturbing when those things die, they just turn into a puddle of crud. Talk about TOTALLY HEINOUS, DUDES!] [Atop Hotel California, The Tubular One was debating on whether or not to end his wrestling career, but realized that he was just being bogus. The whole reason he got into the business was to carry on the name, to make his father proud of him. The legacy of Radical needs to be carried on. Harry and Tommy are just the dudes for the job. With The Tubular One facing former PCW Heavyweight Champion, Miyagawa, things are about to get very bodacious and fast. The main event. The top bill of Turmoil. Tommy is in the spotlight in PCW for once, but he has to watch his most excellent back. Those totally bogus dudes, P.A.W. are right around the corner with their leader, Miyagawa. Could there be a most excellent upset in the wings for our Tubular hero? We'll have to find out at Turmoil] [The cardboard box is torn about torn to shreds and really doesn't look like it can be much use, but somehow it is coming into use. The box holds a jub of water, a couple giant flashlights, and a couple radio clocks with 12:00 A.M. ingrained on them with red crayon. Tommy's tanned, strong jawed face is dropped in confusion. How in the name of the Beach Boys are these things going to help him defeat Miyagawa at Turmoil?] Harry: I've got the tools for each job, man. [Tommy looks at each item carefully and puts it through his mind. The water. He knows what this is all about now. You put water on a Maguai and it multiples into a bunch of other Maguai, which if that was the case with Miyagawa, Tommy doesn't need a bunch of Miyagawa's running around the ring with him. Just the one is bogus enough] TTT: Dude.... Harry: Don't say a word, man. These are the tools that are going to have you beat a former PCW Heavyweight Champion, man! Your going to get the biggest upset of the year, MAN!! [Tommy then looks at the flashlights and just shrugs. Light will kill a Maguai if it is held underneath it. If Miyagawa were hurt by the light the flashlights would certainly come in most handy for the match] TTT: Dude... Harry: NOT A WORD, MAN!! Just take your tools, put them in your suitcase, and we'll go to Turmoil, man. [Tommy sighs and rolls his eyes looking at the radio clocks with the crayon written on them. This is completely bogus. Would any fool believe that it's actually midnight? Not even a child coming out of the womb would believe that nonsense] TTT: Harry, you have to... Harry: Shhhhhh, man. Lets go. [Tommy picks up the flimsy box and then tosses it over the side of the building. Harry's eye's dart out not believing what Tommy has done. He's thrown away the tools of destruction! Not far out!] Harry: WHAT ARE YOU DOING, MAN!! TTT: Dude!! I don't want to throw water on him because then he'd just multiply. I don't want him to multiply. I certainly don't want to feed the dude after midnight because then he'd turn into a most ferocious creature and be twice as dangerous!! The flashlights would work.... [Harry smiles with pride circulating from his body] TTT: BUT IT WON'T WORK ANYWAY, DUDE!! Because his name is Miyagawa!! Harry: Maguai, man. TTT: MEEEEEE....YA....GA...WA!!!!! [Harry thinks about it for a few seconds, looking to the night sky for some help, and obviously it doesn't really help his stoned mind out much] Harry: Miyagawa, man? [Tommy smiles] TTT: YES!! Harry: I saw Maguai, man. TTT: I see that, but it's Miyagawa, dude. Harry: It's a cover up, man. It's Maguai. TTT: No it's not! Harry: Yeah it is, man. TTT: He's not a Maguai!! He's a human being who won't melt when light hits his face. He won't multiply when water is splashed on him, and he won't turn into a most bogus Gremlin when he eats after midnight!! [Harry shakes his head, disagreeing, of course] TTT: What is it, compadre?? Harry: Then why does he wear those goggles, man? TTT: Because their stylish, dude!! It really doesn't matter. Harry: He wears them because the light will kill him, man. There are lights about the ring and he knows that they will kill him so he wears the goggles, man. TTT: Dude, he stopped wearing the goggles for a while and now he's wearing them again. When he wasn't wearing them he didn't melt, dude. Harry: That's because he had on contact lenses, man. [Tommy grunts loudly like some sort of angry caveman and makes his way toward the hatch, passing the generators] Harry: Your going down to get the stuff aren't you, man? That's a good thing. I'm sure the water is wasted by now, but we can always get some of that easily, man. TTT: I'm not getting the stuff, because it's useless, dude. Harry: It's like a treasure, man! TTT: You are stoned beyond all belief, compadre. You've also been watching too many movies. I'm hiding the DVD player from you. Harry: He's a Maguai, man. TTT: His name is Miyagawa and I'm going to wrestle him on Tuesday. I'm going to go in that ring with my most gnarly game face on and I'm going to take it to him, dude. I'm going to get revenge on the leader of The Asylum for what his bogus followers did to us at the Pay Per View, dude. It's really the last time that we get screwed out of the tag titles, dude. So, I'm going to beat down Miyagawa, give him the Wipeout, and then come back home to play Yahtzee with some TOTALLY bodacious babes! [Harry nods, pleased with the attitude of Tommy] Harry: And use the flashlights, man. [Tommy jumps in the air and slams his feet down on the tarred up rooftop] TTT: NO!! They will be useless. I'll look like a most bogus compadre shining a flashlight in Miyagawa's face. Harry: Maguai, man. [Tommy grabs Harry by his shoulders and looks into his brown, bloodshot eyes] TTT: Dude, Miyagawa is a human being. He isn't a 12 inch furry compadre that hums gnarly notes. Miyagawa is a totally gnarly wrestler who has proven himself like the radical dude that he is, and I have a chance to make a statement for the Cowabunga Connection. He's not a Maguai, he's Miyagawa. Got it, compadre?? [Harry stands there for a few moments, looking down at the rooftop, noticing that the tarred shingles seem to be coming loose. They could fall and die! But wait, it's just a bunch of ants carrying food to the nest. THat's far out. Everybody has to eat somehow, man. Miyagawa? Harry doesn't get it. Me Ya Ga Wa...how do you get that out of Miyagawa. Harry sees 'Maguai'. A total mindblow, man] Harry: Maguai. TTT: Miyagawa!! Harry: Maguai, man. TTT; Miyagawa!! Harry: Maguai, man! TTT: MIYAGAWA!! Harry: Miyagawa, man. TTT: MAGUAI!!!!!!!!!!!! [Harry smiles and gives Tommy a thumbs up] Harry: I knew that you'd finally open your eyes and see, man. [Tommy just lowers his head in shame. This is going to be a most bogus night on Tuesday] Fade to Black
