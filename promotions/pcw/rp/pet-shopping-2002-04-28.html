Pet Shopping!

28 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/28/02 The camera fades in… The Chef and Bertha along with Manuel Hung stroll through the door of the “Khandahar Public Humane Society” and walk into the office. A woman, dressed in a black shawl from head to toe greets the trio. “Hello, and welcome to the Humane Society, are you interested in purchasing any of our Afghani Islamic Militant Certified Animals?” The Chef: Why yes we are… Bertha: Why the f*ck did we have to travel 36 f*cking @$$ c*ck hours on the queer as f*ck jet to get here, f*cko! The Chef: Because, my double-chinned dandelion, we are looking for a “Certified” Islamic Militant Pet for Manuel. The Chef points at the tyke and Bertha stares at the boy angrily. Bertha: Did you f*cking agree to this!?!? Manuel: I dis many years old! He holds up two fingers. The Chef: Don’t stress out B, this was a surprise, and we’ll be back in the States in time for our tag team match against Rent A Queero and Juan “Golden Shmoe” Borro. Bertha: We f*cking better be, I’ve been hankering to get my mother f*ck dirty @$$ f*ck @$$ b*tch hands on someone! The Chef: Yes, and then we move on to Turmoil, where I will sauté the @$$es of dozens of men in order to grab the Extreme Title and finally bring some honor back to the “Jobber Championship”. Manuel tugs at his father’s camouflage pants. The Chef: Ok sport, now that we’ve talked about our future matches, thus fulfilling the requirements of the promo; let’s search for a pet! Manuel screams with joy and runs through the door to the Pet Center. The Chef and Bertha follow him into the wonderful world of Islamic Militant Family Friends. Manuel: Me like him! The child points to a creature lying on the floor. The Chef: No, that’s a refugee… Manuel: (Pointing in another direction) Him! The Chef: No, that’s another refugee, and clearly a “Her”. Manuel: (Pointing in yet another direction) That! The Chef: That’s Bertha… Big B perks up. Bertha: OOOOH, you f*cking noticed my new @$$ f*cking in the @$$ b*tch ho mascara!?!? The Chef then voluntarily begins a verbal path to a Bertha beating. Let’s face it folks, he’s just not good with women. The Chef: I’d hate to burst your bubble B, but he thought you were an animal… SMACKACHEF!!! The Chef: I deserved that… The lady from the office walks into the hall and spots the Culinary Couple with Manuel peeking at the many exotic species of animals in the facility. The Chef: All I see are captured British Soldiers and rats… Office Lady: Yes sir! Our site is the best PWO Pet Lease facility in the Khandahar region! The Chef: The best you say? Bertha sighs, quietly saying, “F*cking c*cksuckers…” Manuel steps up near the cage of one British Soldier and stares at the man. Manuel: That’s a nice flesh wound you got there mister… The solider just growls at Manuel and smacks the plastic barrier, causing Manuel to jump back and run crying back to Bertha. Bertha picks up the sobbing son of a gun and reassures him that everything will be ok. Bertha: (Singing) Stop that f*cking crying b*tch @$$…Or I’ll f*cking kick your scrawny queer as f*ck @$$…Don’t you f*cking say a word, or I’ll f*cking crush you like a piece of sh*t b*tch @$$ f*cking mother @$$ humping d*ck c*ck, ho @$$ skank fairy! Manuel lets out a comforting smile and hugs Bertha. The Chef walks up to the undoubtedly aggravated solider and knocks on the glass. The Chef: Would you like a new home? The solider just grits his teeth and glares angrily at the Culinary Conquistador. The Chef: (Looking up) Allah, if this soldier is going to be our new pet, give me a sign… Bertha interrupts. Bertha: That f*cking pansy @$$ mother f*cker f*cking scared the f*ck out of f*cking Manuel, b*tch ho! The Chef tries to plead his case. The Chef: Which would mean that we’ve finally found an individual that can tame this defiant, shin kicking, booger picking, pant wetting, Allah forsaken piece of rancid pork!!! The light bulb finally appears over Bertha’s head, not literally folks, and she drops Manuel, running to the plastic barrier and waving at the new Alouette family pet. Bertha: What’s you f*cking name c*cksucker!?!? The man does not reply. Bertha starts slams her hands on the barrier. Bertha: F*cking tell me ho @$$!!! The office woman has to restrain Bertha with a tranquilizer shot. She takes two syringes and stabs Bertha in the back of the head, injecting the sleeping fluid into her body. She then drops to the floor like a sack of potatoes. The Chef: She needed a nap… The Chef looks at the solider again, and notices the grizzled blonde haired man stand up and give him the New York State Bird. While giving the gesture, his middle finger, poorly attached with Scotch Tape by Afghani doctors, detached from the hand and falls straight to the floor. The Chef: Hmmm, he’s definitely a fixer upper… The Chef watches as Manuel Hung walks up to the plastic barrier again and tries to reason with the soldier. Manuel: Hello, I guess you are coming with us since Cheffy wants for you to be our pet, but I want to tell you…I THINK YOU ARE POOPY!!! The solider smiles at Manuel and replies… Solider: The feeling is mutual, mate… The Chef: I don’t care is Allah gives me a sign; this guy is coming with us!!! The office woman nods and takes her pet shop skeleton key, opening the door to the soldier’s cell; the Chef and Manuel anxiously wait. The Chef: (Putting his arm around Manuel) Good times are afoot son…Praise Allah!!! The camera fades to black…
