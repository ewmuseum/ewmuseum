Date: Last chapter was XII not VII Signing onto my AIM for the fourth or so time since I got my mission, there was nobody online, yet again. Sure, I only had two people on my AIM, but I always hoped they would sign on. Actually, there was only hope for one person signing on, the missionary back at the Hare Krishna. The other name I had on my screen was that of Clair Rivence, and I was positive she had blocked me the last time we had talked after I lied and told her I was a friend of Claval. That was probably a mistake. After I had taken the mission guide for Project 84 a few days ago, Clair Rivence probably thought me the first suspect to take them. I hadn’t known of anyone else who was trying to stop the mission and Rivence besides maybe the police and they couldn’t steal anything without a warrant of some kind. So, with that, Rivence probably knew I was the one who had stolen those precious documents and found out exactly what the group was trying to do. For all I knew, Clair was getting Claval and Sven to help her out in trying to hack my account, get where I was located, and come shoot me down. Sven, writing that name felt so odd. Sven had gone to the evil, terrorist side while I was the loveable person. Yeah, right. Ever since I got back in PCW, harming people for the hell of it has been a turn on for me and I have been anything but a nice guy. Just look at the Overweighter and how I manipulated him without any problem and he is five hundred pounds! But of course, Sven didn’t know that. He thought I was still a good person, going to church. Yeah right, if you call the Krishna a church. They are merely a place for me to let my feelings and anger out when I’m not getting rid of it in the ring. The past few weeks, PCW has been on an international tour and I have been away from America and the Krishna. Because of this, my anger has only refueled itself and without the missionary, I have wanted even more to harm and cause pain to people. Which is probably a good reason in why I have a chance at fighting for the Rising Star title. When I first started out in PCW, I started off good with two good wins. I found the Krishna and started giving my energy to them and I started losing matches that I should not have lost. Now, I have been overseas with PCW and no Krishna to talk to. I won a match in the gauntlet on Fusion. People say that I won that match because of cheating and having my feet on the ropes for leverage. I would’ve won that match anyway so why should it matter? *DING* The sound that signified a person coming online on my AIM flashed and I woke from my daydream. Wait a minute, nobody EVER comes online to talk. Who could it be? I as shocked as I read the name of my only contact online. “ClairRivence” She had unblocked me! I sat staring at the screen for a few seconds without realizing what I was doing. Suddenly, a message box popped up, awakening me one more time. Clair was talking to me! I fell into another trance as I talked with Clair. Clair: Hello? SyN: Yes? Clair: I know it was you. SyN: What are you talking about? Clair: Don’t play games with me. I know what you have taken. SyN: I don’t know what you mean. Clair: It was you who stole a copy of the sheets for Project 84. You’re the only person who knows anything. SyN: What if it was I? Clair: What if it was you? I know it was you now you better just admit it. SyN: I admit to nothing. Clair: Oh really? If it wasn’t you, then why was the car that drove away yours? SyN: How do you know that? Clair: Honey, don’t play with me. I called up a few people and the place where you rented the vehicle told me it was your, Stephen Neirlinger. SyN: How do you know that is my name? Clair: Its called brains, maybe you should find yours. Your AIM screen name is under as ‘SyN’. I just took Stephen and Neirlinger, the ‘S’ and ‘N’ of you name. SyN: I’ll give it to you that your smart but I will not hand over any sheets to you. Clair: I’m not asking you to hand over anything. I’m just asking you not to get involved with anything. You see, only the master copy of those sheets says the actual date when Project 84 will go through. You don’t have the master sheets and don’t know when we will strike. SyN: Not get involved? I am involved already. Clair: Stay out of this you punk. For nearly five years, nobody found out about our scheme. Nobody knew about the game systems or even where Claval was hidden. Now someone is going to stop us? I don’t think so. If you get involved, you will get killed. SyN: But I do know when you will strike. Clair: You think you can outsmart me? I’ve read over the sheets you have in your possession a thousand times. You don’t know anything about the date and it will stay like that, you got it? SyN: I can’t promise anything. Clair: Then you will be interested in knowing that I have also obtained in my possession the exact hotel you are staring at while on your ‘wrestling’ tour. SyN: Your bluffing. Clair: Oh, am I? Room 871 at the hotel of Iraq. Do you need anymore evidence? SyN: No, I won’t get involved. I quickly went offline before anything else could be said. She was on my tail and I knew it. I would have to find some way to act like I am staying out of the way, but find out what date has been picked. All this detective work was making it very hard to concentrate on the PCW Rising Star title. I decided that no more detective work would be good until after the PPV. I needed to focus on what was important, the title that is mine.
