G.I Ellie~! D

28 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 28/08/02 “Eugh.. I look like an Austrian lesbian!” With those quite bizarre words our shot opens up on PCW’s preppy Commissioner, Eleanor Hall in the rather bizarre dress of Army fatigues. Eleanor : “Maybe I could switch to some Cargo Pants, maybe a Khaki crop top and just put on some extra lip gloss instead of Camouflage?” Yes. Just a few days away from facing new PCW signing “The Infantry Man” BladeD, Ellie~! has decided that she will train for the match with none other than the people who trained her opponent.. The US Army. Her questions is met with a stern blank face from the giant Drill Sergeant who towers above her 5’7 frame and she gulps. Eleanor : “Fine... I’ll carry on looking like Goldie Hawn in “Private Benjamin”.. At least I’ll never have her face... Or her body... Or her hair...” Despite his years of extreme training, front line combat and back breaking fitness regimes... The Drill Sergeant has never, ever, seen anything quite like Ellie~! All around them, young men and women dressed in the same fatigues as the little prep run around a seemingly harsh obstacle course, Bergens on their backs that help weigh them down while they climb nets and straddle walls. Eleanor : “I’m not going to be blown up am I? I mean, you guys SO don’t exactly have the best record at, like, not exploding your own!” Her whining has finally gotten to him and he leans his thickly moustached face just an inch away from her nervous one and looks into the vacuous wells that are her pale Blue eyes. “GET TO IT CADET!!!!“ Eleanor’s face screws up as if she is going to cry then as the Drill Sergeant lunges she screams and runs towards the course. “CADET... PICK UP YOUR BERGEN!!!“ As the Sergeant marches off to look around the course, the little prep looks down at the huge Camouflaged back and shakes her head. Eleanor : “They can spend, like, Trillions on bomb but they can’t, like, spare some money for Prada backpacks?” With a sigh she goes to pick the backpack up, grabbing hold of one of the straps and leaning back with all her weight.... Not moving the bag at all. Eleanor : “Um.... Hello! Anyone? Someone, like, stuck my bag to the floor!” As she stands on the muddy ground trying to pull the Bergen along the floor, the muddy, sweaty and exhausted Cadets come around to complete another lap. The little prep waves her hands to try and get their attention and then leaps in front of one of them. “HEY! OUT OF MY WAY!!!” As he tries to run around her she side-steps, widening her eyes as wide as possible and fluttering her eyelids and after a few seconds he stops trying to get past her. Eleanor : “Could you, like, carry my bag? Pleeeeeeeeeeeeease? It’s, like, SO too heavy and that water bottle isn’t even filled with Evian.” After a few more seconds of flirty body language the man caves in and pulls the heavy Bergen up, somehow throwing it around onto his back and he staggers off like Quasimodo around the course, hunched over by the bag. Eleanor : “That’s it.. GO!.... RUN!!!” Unbeknownst to her the Drill Sergeant is now standing right behind her again and after the prep has finished bouncing on the spot and clapping he bellows. “CADETTTTTTTTT!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!“ The Commissioner lets out a shriek and bolts... Scurrying through the muddy ground to start the course before she gets to a 10 foot wooden barrier. Eleanor : “HELLO?!?!? There’s, like, a WALL here!!!” The instructor by the obstacle shakes his head and points to the top of it. “You go over it.” Ellie~! stands right by the contraption made of wooden planks... Poking it gently with her finger just to check that it is indeed there. Eleanor : “Can I get a leg up?” She tilts her head to the perfect “Give me sympathy” angle and the tough man melts, walking around from the side and cupping his hand ready for the prep’s little foot but she is pointing to the floor. Eleanor : “If you, like, kneel I can just climb on you to get over... Pleeeeeeeeeeeeease?” He looks at her lightly freckled face, a picture of innocence then drops onto his knees and lowers his head. Ellie~! then quickly looks left then right and runs around the side of the obstacle and back onto the muddy track. “HEY!!!” She continues her scurrying until she reaches a large pool of muddy water where the Cadet’s that lap her just charge through, the prep jumping back and waving her arms as she avoid the splashes. Eleanor : “Okay..... SOMEONE needs to call their Pool Guy!” She continues to look at the puddle, getting up close to it and slowly dipping the toe of her boot into it before pulling it back.. An evil little grin then breaks across her Pink lips and she waits until she can hear running behind her and sticks her little leg out, tripping the Cadet and sending him flying into the middle of the puddle. “ARGH!!!!!!!!” Then bravely, well, for her... She takes a step back and leaps half the puddle to land on the cadet’s ass. “Umph!” Then in one last bound she leaps and lands on the other side, looking back to see she has cleared it and giving herself a round of applause. Eleanor : “YAY ME!” After another little clapping session she continues up the track, watching her every step in the mud until she gets to a row on 4 plastic tunnels in a row that stretch for about 10 metres ahead of her. She crouches down to look inside. Eleanor : “Talk about insufficient lighting... You can’t even see where you are going... I could get lost!” She takes another look inside it when the familiar yell is heard once more. “CADET!!!!!!!!!“ With the booming voice of the Drill Sergeant sounding off near her she jumps up into the air then drops to scurry into the tunnel, curling herself up just inside the entrance and looking out to see if he is gone. Her head pops our like a Merecat’s as she looks all around and judging it is safe, crawls back out again. Eleanor : “Hey, I can, like, SO see the end from here!” True.. The end is about 25 metres away from her.... This of course ignoring the 17 other obstacles that the muddy and exhausted Cadets continue to make their way through or over... But of course, this is Ellie~! so she skips off towards the end but there is one obstacle she didn’t consider. A small puddle blocks her path.. That and the 6’7 Drill Sergeant. “CADET!“ Ellie~! gulps at the obstacle in her path, wondering how she’ll get around it before she runs at the Sergeant and to his utter confusion grabs his hat to throw onto the puddle before carefully placing one of her feet on it and stepping over. Eleanor : “Wow, they didn’t say there would be any brain taxing problems to solve...” As the Sergeant strokes his moustache in pure disbelief, Ellie~! jogs to he end of the course and jumps on the spot, waving her arms around. Eleanor : “ELLIE~! WINS!!!” She then takes a moment to absorb all she has done.. Well, all that her mind is telling her she has done while she sees all the others climbing over nets and swinging across ponds. Eleanor : “Wow, looks like I SO found the shortest route too.. I’d rule in the Army...” “EHEM!” The smile fades off her face and her eyes open wide as she turns around to see a Cadet who’s entire front and face is covered in mud and the camera shows a muddy boot print on his ass. *GULP* Eleanor : “I think it’s time to go, like, SO A.W.O.L!!!” As the muddy Cadet growls, Ellie~! turns on her heels and takes flight as the shot fades out on the Cadet’s scowling mud caked face.
