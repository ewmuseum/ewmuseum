Failure

26 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 26/11/02 The scene opens inside the giant metal roofless container that Gaz Green and Nerezza now inhabit. Gaz is tightly tucked into one corner, kept there by a steady rhythmic clanging of hollow thumps against the metal. 'WHY!?' A scream rips across the room. CLANG! 'IS!?' CLANG! 'HE!?' CLANG!' 'WALKING!?' ... Nerezza collapses onto his rear, tucking his fists underneath his armpits and pressing his teeth together in anger. His face is screwed up in both physical and mental agony. Gaz inches out of the corner slowly as the monster in front of him breathes heavily out of instinct. His normally pale skin is reddened with anger and his fangs can be heard grinding together like an ivory mill stone. As the sounds from the vampire's mouth decrease, Gaz peeks around to look at Nerezza's face. The beast's eyes are locked onto the dents he made and don't budge until Gaz speaks. 'Easy, tiger...' The Jester says timidly. 'EASY!? I CRUSHED HIS FUCKING SKULL MYSELF! WHY IS HE STILL ABLE TO WALK, LET ALONE WRESTLE!?' The Living Nightmare roars, causing his voice to resonate the container itself. Gaz leaps back as Nerezza stands up with speed and slams the bottom of his fists into the container one more time. 'Miyagawa... was incapacitated... by me. YOU rubbed salt in the wounds by charming his wife. Now he's had his skull fortified and his spine infused as well. He's like something out of the X-Men but the point is he is ALIVE, WELL, AND WRESTLING.... and it doesn't sit well with me at all, Gaz!' he snarls. 'Rezz, that's the point, you injured him so badly that he's walking around and being more of a metal head than the combined forces of Ozzfest '99! Surely that's a credit on your resume, Squire.' The Emerald Menace whimpers as Nerezza's eyes turn to the side. They look as though they're about to spring from their sockets. Slowly, Nerezza is pushed away from the wall... by a pair of increasingly long arms. His skin begins to darken to grey and his head twists in form. Gaz steps backwards as Nerezza's transformation takes place, only to be distracted by the door of the container opening... by the worst person in the world to show up at that moment. 'Hello boys... something wrong?' Natalia Miyagawa pouts as she catches sight of the hideous animal only a few feet away. In an instant, Nerezza's arm shoots past Natalia's head, forcing the door shut behind her. The vampire follows over, shrinking his arm enough to keep the door forced closed but barring the Russian beauty's escape. 'YOU....' Nerezza slobbers, purple saliva falling from his mouth. 'you KNEW he'd be back... and you kept it a secret from us... kept it a secret from ME...' 'Nyet!' Natalia defends herself. 'I keep away from him, I don't even let him see his own son! He cheated on me... he cheated on his own wife!' 'IRRELEVANT! YOU MUST HAVE.... you... yooooouuu.....' Nerezza begins to tremble and lowers his eyelids over his crimson glowing eyes. His lower lip shakes and his arm suddenly shoots back to it's normal length, as does his other. His transformation reverses and he ends up in his human guise again, staggering around. He shakes his head around violently to try and clear it, still advancing towards Natalia until THUMP Gaz smacks one of the container's folding chairs over his head. Nerezza doesn't know what hit him and falls down, impacting the floor. Both The Jester and Natalia breathe a collective sigh of relief, then pick up one wrist each and attempt to drag the heavy man over to a corner. They prop him with his back to the wall, leaving him slumped over with purple liquid dangling like a string from his mouth. * * * 26th November, 2002. After Alliances, we managed to retain the belts but I lost the Alamo Match and Team Bastardos was eliminated completely, even after Skitzo's collective personalities should have given us the advantage. Gaz and I had our fun with Avalanche and Revenant though. I don't think they'll be back for another Alliances event. Speaking of Revenant, I have no idea who or what he was but his blood was extremely weak. Gaz says I tried to transform earlier but collapsed and hit my head on the wall. I can't remember why I transformed but Natalia seems to be scared of me for some reason. I must have looked bad when it all went backwards and I reversed. Dante Jackson and I are going to fight on Turmoil. He's probably bitter after losing the tag titles, seeing Quo's head was so badly messed up after he felt the Darker Side Of Comedy. I don't know why, but I have this nagging feeling in the back of my head. Even after keeping the belts, it feels that something went wrong at Alliances. The soda endorsement doesn't matter too much and even the main event doesn't matter. I can't remember, but every time I try to think, this bump on the back of my head begins to hurt me. Every time I ask Gaz, he says something in Revenant's blood must have something to do with it. He's acting strangely, keeps asking me to calm down, relax, chill out and so on. I don't understand, it's like I've done something. Perhaps he's scared that if I get angry again, my transformation might backfire on me. I don't know, now isn't the time to have a conscience of any kind. I'll wrap this entry up here. Maybe one day, this diary will become the new vampire records. In the meantime, it's the only thing on Earth that understands me. Rezz
