Don't Play With The Monkey (part 2)

3 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/03/02 The scene opens up where we last left off. Kramer is sitting on the couch with Binky right next to him. Kramer is kind of scared of what the monkey might do so he tries not to make any sudden movements. Kramer: So how are you doing little guy? ((short pause)) Who am I kidding, you're a monkey, and monkeys can't talk. And if you did I would be running and screaming at the top of my lungs. So for my sake please don't say anything except for in monkey talk. As soon as he finishes Binky stands up on his hind legs and reaches for Kramer's cap and taps it while making little screeching noises. Kramer: You want the hat? Binky shakes his head as he backs away waiting for Kramer to give it up. Kramer pulls it off of his head and puts it on Binky's head which nearly engulfs him. Kramer: That hat might be a bit to big for you Binky. But have no fear I have a smaller one near. Stay here. Kramer leaps to his feet and rushes off into a seperate room. When he returns he has a smaller version of the same hat, from what we just don't know. He places it on Binky's head who jumps with joy. Binky reaches his hand out and Kramer slowly does the same not sure what to expect. As he reaches his hand out Binky gives him a high five! Then he turns his cap to the side. Kramer: So what, are you a ghetto monkey? Binky nods his head up and down making more monkey noises. Kramer: You don't seem like such a bad monkey. I wonder why you ran away. As soon as he says that the doorbell rings. Kramer gets to his feet to answer it. When he opens it up he is shocked by the amount of people trying to pile up to his porch. There are camermen and reporters, photographers, police, and of course the ring master of the Rumpledink Circus, Alfred Winnipeg who is wearing a black tailcoat over a white button down shirt and black pants. Alfred and a police officer are toe to toe with Kramer. Kramer: I take it you are here for the monkey? Alfred: Of course, we aren't here for you. Now where is my monkey? Feeling now is not the time for a crude joke as a reply, Kramer invites Alfred inside. Kramer: Let me get him, he is just sitting right.... He is about to say on the couch but Binky isn't there. Kramer searches in some other spots but still doesn't see him. Alfred and Kramer start to yell Binky's name out in hopes he will show himself, but he doesn't. After a few minutes of waiting and no Binky, Alfred looks a tad bit upset. Alfred: Is this your idea of a joke? That monkey isn't in here. Kramer: I swear he was sir. He was sitting right on the couch and then when I got up I guess he scampered off some where. Alfred: What ever. Alfred walks towards the door and goes out. The flashing of the cameras, and eager reporters wanting to talk with Kramer die down. As the crowd leaves Kramer stands at his door as he closes it shaking his head. Kramer: Hey Binky get out here now! I know you're still here. Binky comes out from underneath the couch with a sad look on his face like he was caught stealing something. Kramer walks over to him and bends down, and as he does Binky jumps into his arms surpisingly. Kramer: Don't be sad. It wasn't your fault. I just can't understand why you don't want to go back to the circus. Do you want to go back Binky? Binky lets out a scream like he is saying in monkey tongue, 'Heck no!!'. Kramer: Why are you so scared to go back buddy? Is something wrong? Binky shakes his head in agreeance. The way he does it Kramer realizes what has been going on and why Binky doesn't want to go back. Kramer: Hey buddy don't worry, if you don't want to go back you don't have to. Hey listen we'll figure out what we are going to do some other time. Right now is fun time ok? So what do you want to do? Binky walks away and Kramer follows behind him into his game room which contains a pool table, PCW pinball machine, and video game station with PS, PS2, N64, Nintendo, and even an Atari! Binky goes over to this area and taps on the Playstation 2. Kramer: Nice choice. Kramer walks over and and hooks the system up to the big screen tv and picks out the first ever PCW video game. But before they can start playing Binky jumps over towards the pool table and then out of the room. Kramer rushes after him and Binky is back on the couch sitting. Kramer: Ok, we don't have to play games. Kramer walks over and sits right next to Binky yet again. Kramer sits down and looks at the clock on the wall. Kramer: Not too long from now Binky. Not too long before I go into March Madness and compete for the Cruiserweight number one contendership. You know who I want to see win if I don't Binky? Binky sends him an 'I want to know' glance. Kramer: That Anton guy. I mean he sees my success that I have had, which isn't a lot and he calls me a veteran! I just love the way this kid sees me. I just wish more people would recognize my accomplishments instead of my faults. Besides it would be good for him to win and get a chance at the title because I feel he deserves it. Red Rock is a jerk off, and so is Ganker so I hope to god neither of those two win. Besides Ganker hangs around Hank for crying out loud! That means he has to suck, and Red Rock...I don't know about him. All I know is that Anton is the youngest guy in this match, I have held the most titles out of all of us, and I am the oldest. Besides that I am pretty much at a loss. Besides with people like the great Duke Williams rooting for me I don't really want to let them down. So I just hope the power of the chicken is with me at the PPV. Yet again the scene turns to black.
