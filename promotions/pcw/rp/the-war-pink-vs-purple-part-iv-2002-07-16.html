The War: PINK! vs. Purple [Part IV]

16 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: July 16, 2002 Andrew Slater was short but broad. His thumbs in his waistband he pulled back to his full height, Heartbreakers towered over him. But this was his operation, his shout. He’d spent an obscene amount of time on setting up this record deal for The Heartbreakers. Music was a funny business. Unlikely bedfellows, dropouts, villains and one hell of a lot of hippies. For years The Heartbreakers had avoided it, knowing they had the talent, but never putting it to good use. If someone wants to give them shit, well there is not a lot that anyone can do about it...except for the Pretty Cool Guys. Stop one PINK! singer and another fills the market. Music was like water they would always find their way to the level. Hell, you couldn’t even keep them off a stage now with their new hit 'P.I.N.K', how could you try to take this record deal away from them. Politics excluded it was a dirty unrewarding business you could never get the PoP fans to like the P.I.N.K. and when they did, someone else would shut them up. Slater had been happy with The Heartbreakers and the like. But, he has heard some of the Pretty Cool Guys stuff, and does like it. Both groups have a shot at making it big, but he only has one record contract to give out. He was not one to do things by half, he had prepared this one, he had passed up a lot of opportunities, when the targets had been acquired it was squeaky clean. This ladder match on Friday was the big one...it could make or break the careers of both The Heartbreakers and Pretty Cool Guys, both as musicians and as wrestlers. That was aside the fact though. Although the Pretty Cool Guys/Heartbreakers ladder match could possibly be the greatest match of 2002 in PCW, there was a bigger war going on. A war between the PINK! and the Purple. There was a personal aspect to it, an old 'partner' of Valentine's had been nailed to the floor in a purple squat by this YMCA gang. Cold hatred flowed in his veins, he had calculated odds and waited for the best chance. This was it, the resources were marshalled and the time was right for the PINK! to finally strike back. The pointer stuttered as Valentine jabbed the map, the PINK! car moved this way, the Purple that, this was the observation point, this was the command center. The blind points were here, here and here. The danger times were that three minutes and those two there. The PINK! were bored with the details, but they knew them inside out. Every week for a month they had observed this particular transaction, the Village People and their men arrived ten minutes early with the money, a suitcase full of used low denomination bills. Then another man arrived in his car with his men, they had the goods, the purple goods. The transfer took place in the disused pub on a street corner. The Village People left with the money and the mystery man left ten minutes later with the suitcase of purple. It was a bit amateurish really, using the same location every week. The pub was on a junction which did make it more difficult to seal exits, but it was all a bit obvious. They had also taken the trouble to demolish the fencing at the back of the pub, so that the back could also be used as an exit, but apart from that there was nothing professional about the transfer. There were probably a few people on the ground working for the Purple looking for unwanted surveillance, but with the resources that The Heartbreakers and their Rainbow Squad had been able to muster, they did not need to worry about a couple of leg men. Johnny Heart was in the command center, it had a clear view of the pub and the junction in front of it, although it was actually a fair distance from it. Purple clearance had opened up a lot of space around here. He drank PINK! lemonade from a plastic cup, it was cold and refreshing. The room was the child of well-decorated wallpaper, the person who had chosen the wallpaper was obviously a decorator and a member of the Rainbow Squad. The window was covered by a large, extravagant draperies and two sets of powerful binoculars were set up there. No one was using the binoculars, with the radio desk and the close circuit televisions that they had patched in there was nothing to see out the window, that could not be established more easily from other sources. Nevertheless a command center traditionally was within sight of the target, and these sorts of habits die hard for PINK! professionals. A young, skinny, gay male was operating the radio desk. The Village People had arrived on time, the suitcase of purple in hand. The Cowboy and his second in command, the Indian, sat facing each other across a table. Waxed paper had been attached to it using drawing pins. They were both purple professionals, Johnny Heart just wanted to go in and douse The Cowboy with PINK!, that would settle that old score, and then he could settle back to wrestling. He only had a matter of months to go before Liberace and Elton John would end this whole PINK! fiasco anyways. The radio called in the sightings as the second purple car came into the area. Valentine and Heart were motionless, their minds focused on what was happening. Valentine's eyes looked at the map on the wall, he traced out the route the car was taking, the radio crackled and the route was confirmed. He stood up instinctively and walked over to the radio desk, he motioned Heart to go to the binoculars. The car with The Cowboy, the Village People and the Purple was approaching. It turned the final corner. The seconds stretched, the PINK! lemonade had left a foul taste in Heart’s mouth, he resisted the urge to spit (Don't think that way sicko). The last PINK! surveillance car was calling out the last few moves, the target car was slowing, it stopped. They were moving, one car door opened, then another, something was wrong already Valentine could sense it, there was shouting on the radio, the final surveillance car was suddenly in distress they were all shouting, gunshots were heard, a chatter of automatic weapons, the final surveillance car fell silent, a moments silence and then every radio was trying to make contact. A large purple explosion had taken out one of the Rainbow Squad cars. Jack Valentine: “What do you see Johnny?” Johnny Heart: “I can’t quite see, there’s people running about Jack Valentine: “Where’s Gay Man #1?” Johnny Heart: “He’s in the car, someone's coming out of the pub, they’ve got the purple, getting into Village People’s car, VP’s car is leaving now. Dickley Street, heading this way.” Jack Valentine: “All units close on target location, secure perimeter and stick it hard to those purple bastards. Targets armed and dangerous with purple weapons. Enter slowly but then pump them hard when you get close.' A small pause comes across the scene as Jack looks with a smirk at the camera. Valentine radioed in the situation, PINK! backup was being made available and should be in the area within five minutes. They waited, and waited. The target car was a purple VW Bug. It passed them at a perfectly normal speed, the Village People were sitting in it, along with the mystery man. That must have been the one that ran out of the pub with the purple at the time the shooting started. The Heartbreakers and the remaining Rainbow Squad let the car pass and pull over the slight ridge on the road ahead of them before they started up after them. Valentine drummed his fingers all the faster, Heart chewed at a finger nail. They caught up with the car quarter of a mile later, it was empty, doors open, keys in the ignition outside the giant purple warehouse door. The shuttered door was open, inside was dark and smelt of heavy plum. Jack led the way, Johnny followed with a banana in hand. Once inside they could see a port cabin office on scaffolding at the far end of the warehouse. Solid metal stairs led up to it and the door was ajar. Valentine beckoned for the others to follow and they picked their way past the huge purple warehouse to the stairs. Heart paused to grab a heavy monkey wrench, Valentine stared at him, and he put it back down, and pulled out the far shorter regulation truncheon (Even though he is not shorter than regulation.) The light was on in the cabin, they crept up the stairs step by step. Once at the top they positioned themselves on each side of the door. Valentine gave the nod and Heart ran into the port cabin. It was all one room, at one end was a large desk and 6 purple leather executive chairs. The walls were covered in purple with anti-PINK! signs all over. The desk had the two cases on it, The Cowboy was sitting. All of the sudden the rest of the Village People, along with the mystery man surrounded the Heartbreakers and the Rainbow Squad as a TO BE CONTINUED logo flashed at the bottom of the screen. An amusing site, yet a scary site for the PINK! nonetheless....you'll just have to stay tuned to see what happens to our PINK! friends.
