Blitzkrieg

3 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/03/02 Last we saw 'Amazing' Mike Storm he was by the famous Arch. He had a lot to say, telling people why Brock Newbludd is jealous of him, since then we ahve heard Newbludd speak, not much in response to the claims except stating that he is not jealous. That is okay though. Now we have moved closer to the beginning of March Madness, we are closer to the match between the aforementioned two men. We are closer to what is probably one of the matches with the most hostile feelings between the competitors. These two men have exchanged words and blows over the last few weeks, thet have faced off in the ring, and been involved in three matches with each other, only one with someone coming out victorious, that was the first of the battle, when Storm beat Brock in the return of Brock Newbludd. Now we join Storm once again. Scene: Camera opens to the gym area of the hotel where 'Amazing' Mike Storm is staying. We find him running on the treadmill. He is there in his sweat pants, he no longers wears the hooded sweat shirt, but now has on just a white wife beater shirt. We can see the sweat on his chest as he runs. He is in incredible shape, very surprising for a Cruiserweight, and unlikr Brock, he has no gut from drinking to much, and the fact that he actually works out. He runs a little while longer. Then get gets off the treadmill and grabs a towel that is on a bench next to his hooded sweat shirt, and a bottle of water. He wipes sweat from his neck and chest area before sitting down. He grabs the bottle of water, and drinks about half of it. The buts it down. He looks to the camera. 'Amazing' Mike Storm: Well there is nothing like a little bit of exercise to keep that blood going. To get the adrenaline going. I am pumped for tonight. I have so much energy, I simply could not sit around, it made me uneasy. I am up and moving, and it shows no sign of stopping. I feel more alive then ever. I got a good sleep in, I ate right today and the last few days, and I just feeling the energy flowing through me, It is in blood, I feel it in my muscles. I feel it all over me. Unlike Brock I did not wake up with a ahng over and drink my breakfast. Unlike him I am in the greatest shape of mylife, I feel like I can get in that ring and going on for hours without end. I feel like I can go, and then by the time I am at the pojnt of collapse, by the time I think I am going to not be able to move anymore. Then I will remember what an indignity it is to the Television title if you were the number one contender. I will remember how much I simply hate scum like you, and I will get the adrenaline going again. I am not worried about that happening though. You see, because Brock, you are definitely out of shape. You are no where near my standards as far as physical shape. You are my inferior as far as endurance. You can not last like me. You will not be able to keep up with me Brock. So even if you could hold a candle to me as far as talent goes, which you can not, but if by some chance you can pull out an actual wrestling move or two. If you some how keep close as far as actual wrestling goes, then I will just pick up the pace on you. You will tire out. You will not be able to sustain my pace, and will be lucky if you do not collapse. You will be lucky if you do just drop. You will be lucky if you cna ever phase me, as I continue to rain down upon you with moves you can not even see comnig any more. I will be like a blitzkrieg, which for those who do not know, it is a German word for lightning war. Which basically means I will be moving so fast, you will not even know what hit you. Scene: Storm takes another sip from his bottle. Mike Storm: Brock you can try to hide your jealousy. You can attempt to make yourself look better than you are. Your sad attempts and making a mockery of me do nothing. Try as you may, your jokes and lies cover nothing to anyone of decent intelligence. That also explains why all of your followers are a bunch of drunks who have very few brain cells left. People with an IQ that actually makes you look like you know what your doing. They are fitting to be your followers. They are just versions of you. Sad, out of shape, and alcoholic. Scene: Storm gets up and grabs his towel, sweat shirt, and his bottle of water. He motions for the camera to fool him as he leaves the hotel gym. The camera follows him through the lobby, to the stairs and up to his room. He unlocks the door, when he walks in he finishes off the bottle of water, and he throws it into the garbage. He throws the towel and hooded sweat shirt on to the bed. He talks as he is gathering his stuff to head over to the arena. Mike Storm: The time rapidly approaches Brock. We are close to stepping into the ring. We are close to getting near that ladder. Soon your head will meet that metal. Soon you will hear the sickening thud of your skull and the metal of the ladder. You will feel the pain as well. When I am done you will wish you got put behind bars long ago. You will wish that you never ever came to PCW and messed with me. Brock you have no idea how pissed off I am. I have never been as angry with someone as I am with you right now. Even I do not know what Iam capable of in this state, if I were you i would be afraid. No this is not to intimidate you, but to warn you. To let it be known that anything can happen to you once you get into that ring with me. You see this way, this way I do not feel as bad when I see you taken away on a stretcher. This way it is not as much as a weight on my conscience, I warned you already, now I do not take responsibility for what may come. Mike Storm: You know. Brock, you have pissed me off over the last few weeks, as I have already said. You know what pisses me off even more though? You just out right underestimate me. You think you have already won. You spend your time at strip clubs and planning parties, and you almost ignore the match we have. You seem to have forgotten that you have to beat me. You seem to ignore the fact that I am in this match. Well those ideas will come back to haunt you my friend. Your foolish ways will come back to bite you in the ass, I warn you of that now. Scene: Storms looks at the clock, seeing the time. Mike Storm: Okay wel come with me down to my car, I got a little more to say, but I am running late. Scene: Storm throws his hooded sweater on, and grabs his bag. He heads out the door as rhe camera follows him back down the stairs and out to the parking lot. He gets into his hummer. He talks as he leaves the lot. Mike Storm: I believe they made of the fact that I may not drive a cool car, like it has any effect on anything to do with wrestling me. They simply can not think of anything intelligent to say that is wrestling related so they have to come up with something dumb. Look, this match is going to be brutal, and Brock, you are going to realize that I tuly am simply AMAZING! To be Continued.
