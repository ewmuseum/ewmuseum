Date: (Alliances #6 Celestials) PCW Desire… “Elite” By Deftones, the Celestial Anthem. Voiceover by Cyclonica Akiyama. ---===---===---===---===---===---===---===---===---===---===---===---===--- Black and white…static… Then a flash of a silver barcode snaps onto the screen before shifting to the roaring face of the Neo Void God, Akiyama. When you’re ripe…you’ll bleed out of controlyou’ll bleed out of control… When you’re ripe…you’llBleed out of controlYou’ll bleed out of control… The raging onslaught of screeching lyrics and impacting violence shots of Akiyama, The End, and Cyclonica in the ring pauses momentarily upon a black and white image of Akiyama, End, and Cyclonica standing tall in the ring together. “Elite…it’s something that describes the Celestials perfectly. The song begins just as we make our mark on these mortals…it comes out of the blue and knocks you on your back without warning. Every time that music hits, the people know that they’re about to be enlightened. It’s a spiritual experience just to see us walk out before them…because they know the actions we perform in the ring… They’re a GODsend in comparison to the low-level trained minions of this company… Akiyama is shown in slow motion leaping out from under the stage as explosions shoot out all around him. He lands in a crouched position, his angered face glaring down at the fist hammered into the stage. Cyclonica is shown next, a slow motion pan around her sitting on the top turnbuckle looking evilly out at the fans. Her hand slowly raises before her face as she blows out a kiss to the unworthy mortals. The End walks slowly down to the ring while lights flash above him, his listless eyes staring blankly ahead. Out of all the mortals in this organization, the three of us provide the most innovative and extreme style of combat. The End with his illogical techniques…me with my dance of death martial arts…and Akiyama with his punishing impacts… No one can stand against our style… The god flies through the air… An Aurora Akiyama is performed by the nVg off the top of the cell down into the ring. The god banishes his foes… With a faceless name in hand, Akiyama stands in ring and chokeslams a hapless victim into the dreaded VOID. The Neo Void God…destroys… Impact…Impact…Impact…body after body after body is driven into the mat by the might of Akiyama and his Absolute Destiny Apocalypse. The End…his skill and technique are fearsome enough, if not for his silent appearance. A collage of End footage showing him leaping into the air with flips and kicks is shown…closing with rapid shots of Never Again being hit on multiple people from multiple angles…followed by a ending fadeout shot of an man yelling in pain from End’s patented Dragon Sleeper maneuver. And me…Cyclonica…the Divine Prophetess… The daughter of the Neo Void God snaps kicks into various wrestler’s face…Teresa Tomas, Eddie Mandrake, Anarchy, William H Powell… the highlight reel ceasing with the Divine Cyclone…Cyclonica rolling out of the capoeria spin kick and glaring into the downed opponent. Best legs in the business. You like attention…It’s proof to you you’re alive… Everyone likes to talk…everyone likes to stand in the center of the ring and make themselves the center of attention. Everyone’s conceited…only thinking about their own greedy little gains. But Akiyama doesn’t need to reassure himself of his superiority. He doesn’t need the spotlight. He doesn’t need to address the people, and make sure that he’s got the eye and ear of the entire audience. He is silent…because actions speak louder than words. Akiyama gores into the corner, blasting Darkstar and Cyclonica. Another clip of Duke Williams getting popped with the Divine Sword rolls through from Red October. Then a barrage of HAND OF FATE shots roll through, each victim being different, but all of them dropping like flies… Like the competition was bound to do against Akiyama. When we sit backstage and listen to some Bobby Blabbermouth out here in the ring, we do nothing but laugh. It’s amusing to sit there and watch them promote themselves…because if they don’t know that people are thinking about them, worrying about them, hearing about them… …they wont even know if they’re even alive anymore… Stop…parading your angles… Kannon in the ring, glaring out at Rent A Hero and Darkstar as they stand on the stage. He climbs out and begins to walk towards them, a smile forming. It’s all about who can swerve who, isn’t it flat-scans? Well while you’re all out there trying to make yourselves look like master strategists, like you can script out everyone’s lives…trying to prove just how smart you people think you really are… All it takes is Divine Intervention to silence your stupid words. Akiyama guns out from backstage with the Celestial Spear on Darkstar from behind. Another shot shows Darkstar preparing to speak, but Cyclonica smacks him right in his face! Avalanche is shown receiving the Neurotic Shutdown before a final ADA, Cyclonica putting a foot on his chest. Run your angles…pull out surprises…spin your swerves… It makes N O Difference! Because despite all your politricks… everything that makes you look like a mastermind… …in the end….it still takes skill and power to get the job done… …It’s funny… The one thing you need…you don’t have… But…we DO. Confused? You’ll know when you’re ripe… Darkstar walks into his locker room to find Cyclonica and Talena in a passionate encounter? Following that, he sits dumbfounded as TWO Talenas are in his midst…one of them turning out to be a black mannequin. Your confusion leads you down the path of destruction. Talena stares up as Cyclonica and Akiyama tower over her and her bloodied Darkstar. Her frightened face is frozen as Cyclonica paints Talena’s lips with her man’s blood. Don’t worry…we’ll lead the way for you all. Another shaky series of images roll through, showing Akiyama roaring as he, End, and Cyclonica mow down numerous PCW superstars. When you’re ripe…you’llbleed out of control… Akiyama…smacking the ring bell into Darkstar’s face, busting him wide open. you’ll bleed out of control… Darkstar’s head bounces off the steel steps from the Halcyon Hammer delivered from Akiyama. He falls back, his face beginning to flood with red. When you’re ripe…you’llbleed out of control… Cyclonica holds Teresa Tomas in the Silence, blood flowing from her mouth. you’ll bleed out of control… Isiy Xansiv is face up on the mat holding her throat as blood trickles down…Cyclonica standing nearby with crimson fingertips. You’re pregnant…with all this…space thick with honey… …but I lost my taste… Akiyama’s bored face stares at something off camera, the image slowly zooming in. They just keep coming and coming… The wave of challengers never stop… Cyclonica’s cold emotionless face is focused as she stands in the ring, the image frozen, and zooming in on her crystal eyes. …But the pain never stops…the agony never stops… The taste of weakness is strong within them all… We never lose the taste… for violence… We never lose…the taste of blood… Darkstar…standing tall with Talena by his side…a lovely couple. You’re into depression…causeit matches your eyes… Your lifestyles are your owns only because it suits your outermost character… It’s all shallow… Because if you all really look inside yourselves…and knew who you really were… You’d find out you’re nothing like the person you dream to be. An assault of images, going from Kannon to Duke to Skitzo to PWA to Playaz Inc. While others…you’ll find nothing at all. Ellie and Cyclonica sit glaring at each other at the commentary table…a second camera shot appearing zoomed in on … …Ellie… Stop…this faux to be famous… Talena kneels over a knocked out Darkstar…both of them branded for destruction. A bloody 999 on Darkstar’s back, while a matching one is drawn on Talena’s forehead. It’s all a lie you know… Everything you think you’re doing right to make yourself a everyday name in the household of wrestling… The things you’re doing to make yourself look dominant… All the ruse you concoct to make yourself look like the A L P H A MALE… You think this is your pack…and you’re at the head of the pack… Well we’re outside your PCW pack…in a pack all our own… And our Alpha Male…he makes your PCW Alpha Male look like an ALPHA BITCH!! You children want to be wolves among sheep… The hunters are here…and we have you dead in our aim. Confused? You’ll know when you’re ripe… We’ll shepherd you disoriented sheep down to where you belong… There’s nothing to fear… Shepherds… with bloody aprons and meat cleavers for guidance... When you’re ripe…you’llbleed out of control… you’ll bleed out of control… Akiyama goes through victim after victim…Duke, Darkstar, El Diablo, Pharaoh…one after one they come face to face with their ABSOLUTE DESTINY APOCALYPSE… When you’re ripe…you’llbleed out of control… you’ll bleed out of c o n t r o l … The songs screeches out on a guitar, showing the entire Celestial clan standing on stage glaring out at the people. End with Sandy Wong by his side…Anarchy with Cyclonica near Akiyama… The god raises his palm up…and the scene cuts slowly to black and white hissing static again as the camera zooms in on the silver barcode… -=-=-“Elite” by Deftones…-=-=-
