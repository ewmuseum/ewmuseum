Portugal= WORLD CUP WINNINERS!

23 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5/23/02 Time: 1:30 P.MDate: 5/23/02Location: Lisbon, PortugalScene: FIFA Press Meeting “Portugal WILL Win!” *Beginning Thought* Three days ago Juan was notified about an extraordinary happening. Juan was placed on the coaching and training staff of the Portuguese National Team to train them during the World Cup. Juan is expected to be receiving twelve million dollars through the training and endorsements. With this happening, Juan is placed as one of the Top ten richest men in Portugal coming in at number nine. Juan was astonished after hearing this all, but now has to stick to his responsibilities. This might not mean so great for Juan’s PCW career, since he won’t be having as much time, but he is still a hundred percent concentrated on winning the PCW Cruiserweight title. Now Juan is at a FIFA council meeting where he will be announcing his new position to the public. *Beginning Thoughts Ended* The scene opens with Juan Borro about to enter a mob of newpaper and television reporters from all around the world. He’s feeling a little bit nervous about the whole experience, but knows it’s something he has to do. It’s always scary to be right in front of the public eye and it’s been ages since Juan has sat in that interview seat. As Juan looks at it he is reminded of that time about ten months ago when he was at a meeting about the United States National team. It was at that point where Juan found PCW, and from then on his life has never been the same. Juan sits down in his seat, just thinking, until he in interrupted by one of the directors of the meeting. “Mr. Borro, you’r set to go on. Give us the signal when your ready and we’ll announce you to the public.” The female director sates and Juan agrees. Juan knows at this point its show time. He takes one more deep breath and then heads out like a soldier on a white horse bringing a message to the opposing army. Juan gives the director the signal and she signals the man on stage that introduces Juan. “I’d like to introduce to all of you, a former Portuguese soccer icon by the name of Juan Borro.” The announcer says. At this point all of the media is having frenzy and waiting in anticipation for Juan to come out and make his statement. Juan takes that once big leap that seems to be an eternity long. The second he enters the scene he is blinded by photos taken of him. He can’t see anything but black suits and lights. He then sees a seat with a glass of water and microphone near it. He approaches that area and takes his seat. The crowd is still rowdy and the staff workers for the FIFA conference tame down the crowd until thye become completely silent. The pure feeling of no sound and no pictures refreshes Juan. He feels like he’s in harmony for that small moment. Then it ends when he notices all of the people with their eyes glued on him. It’s one of those times where you wish you weren’t as important as you are. Juan stares over to the side of the stage and the female director sticks her thumbs up and for Juan to begin his speech. “Hello, my name as most of you know is Juan Borro. You have probably realized that I have not been around as much this year with my new residence in the United States and employment in the American organization Primetime Championship Wrestling. Well this year I am stating my comeback to professional soccer.” Juan states as he is suddenly interrupted. The crowd burst in talking and chatting among one another with a huge amount of gasps and people typing notes on their laptop or writing them on pieces of paper. Juan nods his head and tries to rephrase that comment. “I’m back, but not as you would expect. I am not coming back as a player.” Juan states before being interrupted by the emotions of the crowd once again. The reporters and columnists seem disappointed and show it by acting in a rude manner, one even screams out a comment at Juan. “If you’re not coming back to soccer, what the hell are you at a FIFA conference.” A reporter rudely asks without Juan calling on him. “I usually wouldn’t answer such a rude question, but since it’s essential I’ll have to. You see I have been placed as part of Portugal’s new coaching and training staff for the World Cup in Korea and Japan.” Juan says pausing for the media to make comments. Mixed reactions are shown between the crowd, some extremely happy and other very disappointed in the turn event. More snapshots of Juan are taken and more screaming and confrontation takes place in the crowd. “ I am receiving compensation for my action so they aren’t volunteer. I will be their throughout the whole event, leading my team closer and closer to the much desired World Cup we are favored by some to win. I will not disappoint and I will work to the capacity of my abilities for this to work.” Juan says confident in his actions and what he’ll be doing with the team. “I just hope the next time I come to hold a meeting like this, it will be about winning the 2002 World Cup. I will not settle for less and Portugal who will be hosting Eurocup 2004 will be bringing the World Cup trophy with them. This isn’t just about winning, this is about putting an underrated country on the map.” Juan then finishes steeping back for the reporters to shoot with questions. Hands go flying in the air, with people shouting all types of questions. A staff member in the front is there to choose people with questions. He points at a man in a blue tuxedo who quickly asks his question. “Juan, how do you think you’re qualified for this job at hand? Why wouldn’t Portugal get a real coach?” “I’m afraid I can’t fully answer that questions. I think I’m qualified because I’ve been around the international soccer scene since a very young age. I know all about it and I know what these men need to do in order for proper conditioning and working to their full capacity. As for the part about why I was chosen I’m not the one to answer that question.” Juan answers staying calm under pressure. The next person to ask a question is selected and begins. “What will you do to get your team ready for United States on June 5th?” “I will give them a rather intensive schedule where they will have to earn their spot on the team. I’m not afraid to go and pick a complte new team from the Portuguese leagues to replace the men now, I just hope I don’t have to. These men will be in such good physical and mental shape come the fifth they won’t even be able to comprehend the word “mistakes”. The United States is not a strong team, and they lack in skill. We’ve beaten Holland, who took complete control over the United States a few days ago so were not worrying tremendously about them, but we will still not take the match lightly. The first match is what sets the pace for the rest of the tournament. My motto is it ain’t worth winning if you can’t win big. So we will not only defeat the United States, but we’ll make an example out of them.” Juan says fully confident in his team’s abilities. Juan signals for a last question, because of tie restraints. “All right, Juan we know your wrestling now also, so how will that be affected? I heard you have a title match coming up next week. How will that conflict with your training schedule?” “I’m just as confident in my team as I am with myself. I will be training myself for my match, while training my team for their game. I will be present at PCW’s Tuesday Turmoil and it shouldn’t really interfere with any training times. My mind is fully drawn to this, and right now PCW is put a close second. I will use all my power to balance out both things.” Juan says as he exits the stage to a chorus of picture taking and shouting of more questions. The announcer comes out and makes a final announcement. “Juan will be available for more comments later in the week.” Juan walks out of the room calmed down and happy the moment is over, and with this the scene fades to black.
