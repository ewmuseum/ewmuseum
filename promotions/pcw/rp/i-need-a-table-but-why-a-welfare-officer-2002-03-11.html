I need a table, but why a welfare officer?

11 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3-11-02 Krayzie done playing games. Forget reasoning with Xavier Kannon. That got the Krazed Lunatic a tazer to his back. The Krayzie One tried to resolve his differences with the Gold Patron Meritorious. But now, its become quite clear that these two men are out to injure the other. And, with Turmoil's tables match for the Continental title, both men will have every chance to take the other man out. Who will.... survive? If Xavier Kannon is expecting the same guy that he was able to push around at the beginning of Fusion, he'd better be prepared for the worst. For, as the scene fades into a furniture store somewhere outside of Norman, Oklahoma, it appears the Krayzie One is taking this match seriously. Because, in his hands, Jeremy Howard is holding up an oak table for his girlfriend, Jessica, to see. Jeremy: This the size of this table is perfect for his head to go through? Jessica kind of sizes up the table with her eyes a bit before replying. Jessica: Hmmm. Too small for the size of Kannon's fat head. Maybe I could use that one his b!tch. Jeremy: She does seem to have this fantasy about you. Jessica: I need to change that. She's..... Like..... A FRIGGIN' AIRHEAD! A number of other customers turn there heads in the direction of Jeremy and Jessica, looking at them with odd looks in there face. Jessica jumps back, covering her mouth after the outburst. Jeremy shakes his head, trying to understand what came over his lovely beauty. Jessica: I'm sorry. Jeremy: Its okay. If I had some preppy b!tch dreaming about me, I'd have an out burst too. Jeremy sets the table back down, looking over the way toward a few others. Jessica: Well, Kannon can't seem to keep you out of this thoughts. A shiver runs through Jeremy. Jeremy: God, you'd think those two were stalking us or something. Jessica: You trying to make me vomit? The last thing I need is some two bit bimbo stalking me. Jeremy: If Kannon ever hopes for her to mean something, then he needs to model her after you a little better. Everytime the Krazed Lunatic opens his mouth, one has to be leery of what he's going to say. For, with this man, there is no censoring what he says. He says what he wants to say and that's that. And, this time is no different. In the words of Jeremy Howard, he just said that Xavier Kannon's valet, Eleanor Hall, is nothing. That, if she ever wants to amount to anything, then he needs have her act like Jessica more. In a sense, the Krazed Lunatic just said that Ellie is nothing compared to what Jessica is. Jessica: Thats even worse. A copycat. Jeremy grabs Jessica by the hand, leading her over to another table. A black table that has a few more inches added to it than the previous table had. Jeremy: This this'll cover it? Jessica: Have you seen the SIZE of Kannon's head? I mean, he had the nerve to blame you for his loss at March Madness. His head is like..... HUGE! Jeremy stops just before the table, taking a step back. He pauses a second to ponder Jessica's question before answering. Jeremy: I think you have a point. He couldn't face a loss like a man. This table is big enough.... Jeremy reaches into the back pocket of his name brand blue jeans, pulling out a can of white spray paint. Jeremy: .... for you to put Ellie's head through! Jessica: Whoo! Good call! Jeremy holds the can of spray paint out for Jessica to grab. She takes it, then points it directly at the table. In the proceeding seconds, the lovely Jessica writes out Xavier Kannon's valet's name on the table. Jeremy: Good. The next time that funky looking whore mentions my son's name in a demeaning manner, I'll find a worse piece of furniture to use on her. Jessica: A chainsaw? Jeremy sighs. Jeremy: That's not a piece of furniture. Jessica: Who cares. She bashed on our son. Jeremy: For fear of you going to jail, why don't we just stick with the table for now? Jessica shrugs, not really wanting to just stick with the table. But, she caves in. Jessica: Oh, alright. It's one thing to bash on another wrestling superstar. The Krayzie One is used to it. He's had to continually take the verbal onslaught of Kid Wonder for a year. Jeremy can handle it. Jessica's even had her name mentioned in bashes on the Krazed Lunatic. Alexi Ruslan, known sidekick of Ivan Stanislav, couldn't keep Jessica out of his thoughts when the Krazed Lunatic was fueding with the Russian Bear. But, this time, Kannon and his valet brought Troy into this...... Bad idea! Jeremy: Now, I just need to find a table for Kannon's big fat arrogant head. Jessica starts to giggle for no apparent reason. Jeremy: What? Jessica: I was just thinking. Jessica's giggle kicks up a notch, becoming an uncontrollable laughter. Jeremy: What? Jessica is somehow able to maintain herself long enough for.... Jessica: We can't find any tables big enough for Kannon's fat head. So, why don't we just stick his head in a car compactor and crush it down? Jeremy: Damn, if it weren't for the fact that I've known you for the last ten years, I'd think you were a Clyde-ish like freak. Jessica: Thats a real nice thing to say about me. Jeremy smiles. Jeremy: At least your not copying every word I say like an annoying whore. Jessica: Isn't that what Ellie does? Jeremy: My point exactly. All of the sudden, Jeremy's cell phone begins to ring on the hip of his jeans. Jeremy reaches for his hip and pulls the cell phone off its hook. Jeremy: Yes? Jeremy raises the cell phone all the way to his ear. Voice on the Other End: Can I speak with Jeremy Howard. Jeremy: This is. Voice on the Other End: This is the Welfare Officer of Indiana. I've recieved a complaint an Xavier Kannon. An enraged look crosses Jeremy's face, leaving Jessica puzzled. Jeremy: And? Welfare Officer: I'm going to need to have a meeting with you sir, sometime this week. A silence covers the phone call. Welfare Officer: Jeremy? When can I have a meeting with you? Jeremy: I'll be there. Welfare Officer: What day. Jeremy grips the phone tight, his anger evident. Jeremy: real stern Don't press me. I'll be there this week. Jeremy hangs up the phone, in his own way. That way being throwing the cell phone against a wall of the furniture store. Jessica shakes her head from side to side. Jessica: How am I supposed to call Justin and check up on Troy now? Jeremy: Thats not a priority right now. We need to think of how we're going to approach this welfare officer that just called me. A look of horror falls over Jessica's beautiful face. She takes a step back, trying to collect herself, showing off the lovely white dress that she's chosen to wear today. Jeremy reaches out and grabs her, trying to quell his own anger by holding her. The scene fades away to the two embracing in the furniture store, still without the table that the Krazed Lunatic plans to put Xavier Kannon's head through..... to black!
