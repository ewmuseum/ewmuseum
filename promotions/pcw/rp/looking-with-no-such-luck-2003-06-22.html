Looking, With No Such Luck

22 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: June 22, 2003 The scene opens up to a highly distraught looking Brianna Washington who is out roaming the halls in a casual black butterfly shirt and black hip hugger pants. She has her hands planted along her sides as she continues her trek down the hall apparently looking for something. Her eyes rest upon one unmarked door at the very end of the hall. Brianna bites her lower lip as she looks around to see if anyone is near. Seeing that the ghost is clear, she walks to the door and tries to pull it open, but it won’t budge. “Damnit!” she shouts irritably jiggling the doorknob to no effect. Just as it seems aren’t going her way, she reaches into her pulled back hair and retrieves a bobby pin. She flattens it out and then proceeds to the small hole on the doorknob. “I know this is bad but I have to find it!” she says to herself while wiggling the bobby pin in the hole. Brianna grabs onto the knob again and this time it fully turns to her delight. The wooden door is pulled open to reveal nothing more than your ordinary janitor’s closet. Feeling that things could be hidden, she steps inside and starts moving stuff around, finding no hidden spaces or trapped doors. “Arggg! This is so uncool!” With that she backs out of the closet and slams the door shut, which grabs the attention of a janitor who comes walking around the corner. “Hey what are you doing in there?” he asks in a scruffy voice trying to be intimidating. “Nothing…it’s not like anything is in there but cleaning crap anyway.” “That’s because it’s a janitor’s closet.” the man replies in a condescending manner. “I know that Sherlock!” Brianna yells as the walks around the corner, leaving the janitor be, not wanting another confrontation. “Where could they have hidden it!” She pauses by a door and leans against the wall clutching at her forehead with defeat stamped across her pale face. “This is just ridiculous! The gadgets I brought onboard were tested successfully! No sparks, fires, everything was in perfect working condition! But no, does anyone care to ask? They demanded that I turn them over, so I did compliantly. I can’t live with this! My gadgets are like air…..I need them to survive! They’re my fricken life and they were taken away from me!!” Brianna stops to take a much-needed breath of air as she removes herself from the wall and continues her walk down yet another corridor. “Fearing that I would go over the deep end? What in the hell kind of reasoning is that? I am a very sane person and I wouldn’t let a person like Dorn Tracer get the best of me, I have more sense than that. So not only do the PCW and airship personnel refuse to give me a chance to explain the safeness of my gadgets, but they also doubt my control. Wow that’s great to know that I can’t be trusted.” She says sarcastically coming to the end of yet another hall. Instead of continuing she turns back virtually retracing her steps. She cuts a few corner and stands in front of her room. She pulls the key out and is ready to place it in the hole until she grabs her stomach grimacing in pain. “Ahh….” she bellows. “All of this stress is getting to me, I really need to just take a break and lay down.” The key finds it’s way to the hole and the door unlocks. Brianna charges in and slams it shut, heading straight to her bed, which she plops down onto. “I’ll just continue my search when I wake up. They may have hidden them, but I know I’ll find them. After all I can’t afford someone else getting a hold of them, or worse, breaking them! God knows what another months work down the tube will do to me. I will be one highly ticked of chick!” Reaching an end to her rambling on, Brianna closes her eyes and the scene fades to black….
