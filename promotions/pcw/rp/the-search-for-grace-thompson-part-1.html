The Search for Grace Thompson Part 1

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Uhh? Every superstar in PCW gets fan mail. Everyone from the World Champ, Boda, to the newest man on the roster, Duzza. They all get appreciation from the fans, and they all deserve it. The men in the locker room at the PCW arenas work hard, putting their bodies on the line, day in and day out, for none other than the fans of PCW. Sure, most people may deny the support of the fans, like Chris Chase and Tim Walden, but deep in their hearts they are very greatful for the support and cheers fromt he crowd. They may never admit it til the day they die, but they do. Xavier Kannon was an NTICW World Champion, and he ran on the fuel provided by the applause and screams from the crowd. But when he went up against the mega face that is Drake Maxwell, the crowd changed sides and Xavier lost the title. Another man, Mark Estep, has always thrived off the support of the crowd, and he looks to do so again this Friday against newcomer, Shawn Mosely. The PCW superstars love to hear the screams from the crowd in the arenas, but even better is when they get a letter of approval from an individual fan. When this happens, it puts them in a zone before their match. Before every match, Mark Estep can be found in his room reading through twenty or thirty different letters from his fans, to get him pumped up for the challenge that is ahead of him. Well, its Wednesday night, and Mark is at his house in Chicago, Illinois going through a fresh new shipment of PCW fan mail, sent to him. 'Wow, three letters this time! Thats a new record for me,' I said, suprised as ever. The cameraman, trying not to laugh, leans over to me and whispers, 'You know, the camera's running...' 'Oh, i mean, three letters? What's wrong with kids today?' I said, trying to cover my tracks. 'Hmm, lets see what this letter says...' Dear Mark, In the PCW, you are one of my favorite superstars of all time, but im afraid that lately you have been dragging. You aren't the same 'kick ass, drink beer, and sleep with women' type of person. I guess what im trying to get at is, if you don't go back to your old NTICW ways, then you are going to lose fans, one by one. -Amanda Tampa, FL. 'Well that was kind of harsh.' i said, with my head dropped in shame. The cameraman, trying not to bruise a lung laughing, looks over at me and yells, 'HAHA! SHE SAID YOU SUCK!! IT'S TRUE TOO!! HAHA!!!' 'Ya know, i dont care what she said, I'll just read this next one. I'm sure this kid has something nice to say about me.' i said, with a stern sound in my voice. Dear Mark, I have been following your career since day one. When you started back in the old CWF, now XCWF, and weren't well known, to the days now, when you are going for a shot at the Rising Star Title at the next PCW PPV, Spring Fling. Well, as of late, im sorry to say, i have lost interest in being your number one fan. There are many reasons, your lack of consistancy, your non-ability to develop a 'work rate', and your down right suckyness that you have been displaying the last couple months, and i would just like to say that if you don't go back to your old self, like in NTICW, you are going to lose a fan. I think all of this stardom that the PCW has brought to you is going to your head, and it all needs to change now... Your Mother, Chicago, Ill. 'Dude, your own mom hates you!' says the cameraman, giggling like a little school girl. I stare at him as my face begins to turn red in anger, 'I CAN READ, YA KNOW!!' i exclaimed, very angrily. 'There's gotta be atleast one good letter in here,' I said, convinced, 'here... this one!' I picked up the last letter and opened the envelope and unfolded the paper. I held it up to the camera as i read. It was a rather long letter this time... Dear Mr. Estep, I am Grace Thompson, your ex-fiance. First, i would like to tell you that you are scum, and the way you broke it off with me was just dreadful! I set the letter down for a second and begin to think of how i broke it off with Grace, so many months ago. Thoughts- The scene opens with myself and Grace sitting on the couch at Grace's house. Grace is sitting with her feet underneigth her, with a pair of grey sweat pants on and a grey t-shirt. I'm sitting next to her with the remote control to the television in my right hand, and im wearing a pair of red NTICW shorts with the words 'New, True' on the bottom of the right leg. Im also wearing a red Chicago Bulls t-shirt to top the look off. It was the night that The Phantom Booker announced the 13 men that made the PCW cut, and i was flipping through the channels anxiously to try to find the draft. I find it, and it is just starting. It mentions the names Drake Maxwell, then Xavier Kannon, then Frankie Bones... I run over to the telephone and pick it up and call my tag team partner at the time, Epic, on the telephone. He picks up after a few rings and i walk back over and sit down on the couch. 'Hey man' i said into the telephone. 'Hey Mark, how's it goin? You watchin' the draft?' asked Epic, with an excited tone of voice. 'Yeah' i replied. Some more chit chat goes on between Epic and myself as they finally name the last couple people in the draft. 'Young Tiger, Six Finger Sisco...' i heard, as my dreams shattered.... 'Oh, and Mark Estep,' were the last things that came out of the box. I jumped up and down, screaming and yelling in excitement as i have just been drafted into the PCW. After four different attempts at the PCW, i had finally made it. But, on the other side of the phone was silence. Nothing came out of my good friend Epic, except for the sound of his heart breaking. 'Dude, i made it to PCW!! Ain't that great!!?' i yelled, 'Yeah...great...uh huh' he replied. I sat down on the couch as i realized what had happened. I had planned on going to PCW as a tag team, but it didnt turn out that way. When i went in for the interview just days before, i was asked if i would like to come in as a singles competitor or a tag team, and i replied 'tag team'. At the time, the PCW had a terrible tag team and i wanted to be one of the two men to make it better than ever. Just months before, i saw great teams like The Blood Brothers, The Storm Brothers, Da Westsidaz, and Goovil, and i had hoped to be as great as some, maybe even all of those teams. I had high hopes for my partner and I, and i was going to make sure that it was my partner and I, not just I. So i got off the phone with Epic and i drove down to the PCW building that was conveniently in Chicago Ill. I left Grace behind, never to see her again. Now, i don't want to end the story there, so im going to go ahead and tell you what happened at the PCW building. Well, with the NTICW main building being in Chicago at the time, the Pimply Faced Intern Kid was there, and all the NTICW members that had been drafted should go there before anything else, and they would get security passes and such. Well, i went there and i pulled the PFIK asside, and asked him why Epic wasnt drafted and he replied with 'He just didnt make the cut.' I couldn't believe it, Epic, my own tag team partner, wasn't good enough for PCW? But now i am? Something doesn't seem right, because after all those months of teaming up, Epic had always boasted and bragged about how he carried the team, but he wasn't good enough for PCW? A PCW that asked me to join? Hmm, come to think of it, he never beat me in a singles match either, no matter how much he tried, he couldn't beat the Estepper in any type of match. Not many people could, with me only losing to Drake Maxwell and Totally Tubular Tommy. But, thats all in the past, now you know what happened between me and Grace all those months ago, but i've moved on. I'm now engaged to Amber, whom i love dearly, and she is going to have my kid. I think.... I would just like to say that i miss you, and i do still want to marry you. Oh, before i go, i just want you to know that I'm pregnant. Thanks... Grace Thompson Chicago, Ill.'Oh, that sucks... whats your fiance gonna say about it?' says the camera man. I ignore his question and get up quickly and rush to the phone. I pick it up and take a second to remember a number, and then dial. The phone rings a few times and then a man picks up. 'Is Grace there?' 'Grace who?' 'Thompson, is Grace Thompson there?!' 'Nah, im sorry, she moved out almost a month ago.' 'Did she leave a number or address?' 'Can't say that she did...' And then the man hangs up on me. I hang the phone up and then walk over and sit back down. The camera fades out as i hang my head....
