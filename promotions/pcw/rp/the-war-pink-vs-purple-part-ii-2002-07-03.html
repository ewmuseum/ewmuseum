The War: PINK! vs. Purple [Part II]

3 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: July 3, 2002 Johnny Heart put down the half bottle of his PINK! rum. He sat back in his chair, stretching backwards. Johnny Heart: 'This is f*cking ridiculous. How could we let those Village People Idiots take control of the PINK!? How could we not have seen the plan that the stupid little Village People Cop had in mind? F*ck this shit.' Now normally Johnny Heart was very well-mannered, but after a half of bottle of rum, and someone messing with the PINK!, anything could happen with Johnny Heart. One of the rainbow wearing gay men was sympathetic... Gay Man #1: 'It had to happen sooner or later, the rest of us have been brought under the special task PINK! training force and the anti-Purple act of 2002. You only got away with it for this long because they were scared of you, because you held the tag team titles. Now it's their turn to play the bad guys.' Johnny just looked down, visibly upset at what happened to his anti-Purple crew yesterday, but more visibly upset at what happened to his tag team partner, Jack Valentine. Jack was doused in purple ooze, worse than any of the purple that Heart or his gay brigade encountered. Jack locked himself in his room ever since the day that purple 'overcame' him. Who knows if he will be ready in time for their match on Friday with the Pretty Cool Guys and Cowabunga Connection? A match that would decide the fate of their second reign as tag team champions here in PCW. Johnny Heart: 'I don't know, Gay Man, I feel like I've been in this PINK! supporting job forever, doing all the f*cking shitty jobs that no-one else wanted to touch. It's the joke of PCW, I'm the PINK! loving sissy, because I'll do all the shit that no one else would touch, and that no one else could do. I'm the best thing that PINK! ever had, and what do those idiot Village People do, they get some f*cking idea that they will join the side of purple, and come down on me. Maybe they're right, maybe I should just join the purple after all. Jack may never come out of that room.' Gay Man #1 was ten years older than Johnny Heart, and the relationship between the two men was like a father and son. Basically the PINK! destroyed purple, maimed purple, followed purple, destructed purple's every path. All this at the bidding of their PINK! And for this they received all kinds of money from the head guys supporting the PINK!, Elton John and Liberace. After years you became closer than family, life seemed more real, it was better, and they were supporting something that they loved anyways, the PINK! Gay Man #1 had heard all this before, everyone in the various PINK! organizations had complained, their old freedoms were vanishing, they were being called to account. Being asked to justify their every move, agree to be silent about the PINK! operations. For people that had spent a lifetime fighting for the PINK! and destroying purple, behest with scant thanks, this was a bitter pill to swallow. However it was the night before another mission. The rainbow colored gay man was one of the best, but every mission was different, you had to give every minute of the mission one hundred percent, just like in a PCW ring, Jack and Johnny give one hundred percent. Their PINK! missions are probably where they get their will and desire to be the best in PCW. Johnny Heart: 'What is the mission tomorrow then Gay Man?' Heart was trying to get Gay Man #1 focused on the job in hand. It was difficult, Gay Man #1 had a studied nonchalance about what he did. But underneath there was the cold heart of a shark beating. Gay Man #1: 'Standard grab and spray job. Some purple Gucci that they want. He's too sharp for anything obvious so we're playing the percentages, I block one way, he has a choice of two and with a fifty percent chance he comes our way.' Johnny Heart: 'What happens then?' Heart knew that the Gay Man #1 did not know and did not care, but by asking the question he could keep Gay Man on the matter in hand. He didn‘t want the gay man to focus on the severe loss the PINK! took in Jack Valentine being doused in purple the other day. Hell, he was more purple than Anna Keyson’s fired ass, and no one knows if he’s even cleaned himself up yet, he was so distraught. Gay Man #1: 'Don't know, don't care. I do my bit and go home, end of story.' Gay Man #1 head was pulling back, his eyes were dead with hate. All he wanted to do was get this job over with, and go back to his nice cozy home, watch Birdcage and put on his PINK! slippers. Gay Man #1 had the walkie talkie on as he drove along Rodeo Drive, he'd received the signal and he was on the planned route. It was a job that even a fresh PINK! trainee could do. You just kept on with the traffic and did a right turn at the next set of traffic lights. The radio was playing “YMCA” by those idiot Village People. Johnny, in the front seat, smashed the radio in with his fist. Johnny Heart: “Damn purple yuppies!” He watched the Summer crowds on the streets, they were buying lots of purple items. They thought that the purple color could be trusted and that there was goodness in the hearts of those other men. They did not know purple was the root of all evil, they did not know that the men who supported purple were evil as well, they did not know the dangerous little game that was being played out before them. And they never would know. That's why the PINK! men did this, they was inside, the others were outside, and this has nothing to do with the backside. The lights were green, they approached the junction, the man in a purple raincoat on the corner opposite lifted his left arm to look at a watch. They probably thought he was wearing a walkman. Gay Man #1 felt an overwhelming need to urinate, his heart beat faster, he flicked the walkie talkie on and started to turn to the right. They couldn’t do this without Jack, Jack was always there on their missions against purple...this was something new, but they had to retaliate for him. They spotted the clothing store, of course next to a gay bar, at the end of the street. They pulled out their PINK! weapons and made themselves ready to destroy the purple in the name of Jack. Johnny and Gay Man #1 were walking down the street, something didn’t feel right, something was off, and it wasn’t the fact that they didn’t have Jack. They made it past an alleyway and were right in front of the door. They could hear something, they was not sure what. It sounded like a Cowboy and Indian movie, there was an odd smell, painty fresh. Johnny and Gay Man #1 blinked for one second and before they new it, they were covered in the purple ooze again. They wiped their eyes and spotted the Village People Cowboy and Indian skipping down the street with their purple clothes. Johnny Heart: (In a drunken rage) “F*ck, not again!” -TO BE CONTINUED- Would Jack be ok for Fusion? What would come of the war between The Heartbreakers and the gay men against the Village People, but more importantly, what would come of the war between PINK! and Purple. Purple has won the past few days, but PINK! is bound to return to their top form...after all, they have the most heart...
