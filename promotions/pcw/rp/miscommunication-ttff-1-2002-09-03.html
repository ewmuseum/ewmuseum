Miscommunication (TTFF#1)

3 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 09-03-02 (We are presented with the beautiful landscape of the providence of Saskatchewan, Canada on this a mostly sunny September day. Hovering high overhead a forested area spattered with bodies of water, we come upon a huge yacht slowly cruising along the sparkling waters of Lake Athabasca. This yacht, which is strangely armored and fitted with large machine guns and Argentine flags intimidates all fishing boats and ski-doos that dare linger around its vicinity. Coming closer to El Presidente's yacht, we would like to remind you that Frisco does not give a good goddamn if you lack the frame of reference or a good enough imagination to know the abstract ideas or moods of this scene and what Frisco's current situation is--so we will go directly down to the yacht where sufficient dialogue is already in progress.) Frisco: 'What? Saska--what? Tell me what the name of this place was before the idiot Canadians made it unintelligible.' (El Mero Mero emerges from his cabin, sucking on a Cuban and brushing lint off the decorated shoulders of his fascist uniform.) Frisco: 'Well?' (A small South American man appears with a pad with the letters S-A-S-K-A-T-C-H-E-W-A-N scribbled on it. Frisco nods and takes his cellphone from behind his back, speaking into it.) Frisco: 'Saskatchewan. I'm in Saskatchewan.....Yes, the one in Canada. And where, may I ask, the fuck are you?' (The dictator rolls his eyes and blows out a cloud of smoke.) Frisco: 'Rwanda? You're in Africa? Why would you be in Africa when Saskatchewan is in Canada? See, there's something very wrong with that...well, many actually. One, Rwanda is a country and Saskatchewan is a providence. Two, Saskatchewan is in Canada and Rwanda is in Africa. Thirdly, there is that minor disparity of completely different continents and pronunciations....No! So what if they both have W's in their names, they're different! Extremely different.' (The voice on the other end says something that cannot be heard.) Frisco: 'No, you need to listen better. Little things like this can hurt us and our cause. When I order a unit of my militia to a certain country...that's exactly where you're supposed to take them...not somewhere else. Do you see the problem here?' (The disappointed leader sighs at the chatter on the other end.) Frisco: 'You're not understanding this, Colonel...YOU'RE IN THE WRONG FUCKING PLACE! Comprendes? I wanted some men here to protect me, yet you brought my protection to a place where I am not currently at and have no desire to be at.' (Francisco walks towards the end of the boat's railing and leans over as the voice of the lost officer explains his situation.) Frisco: 'No, it was not an honest mistake...it was a stupid mistake! Did you see what happened to me on Friday? I was attacked by some....female! Granted, I should've had my groupie patrol on alert, but you guys are supposed to be aware...and in your correct location at all times. That bitch could've done some permanent damage if I wasn't so strong and overflowing in the area in which she targeted.' (Frisco slumps at the frantic voice bursting through the phone.) Frisco: 'The kids are stealing your MRIs? Well, that's a given, shithead! You're in a 3rd world country when you're supposed to be in...whatever slightly improved world this country is in. Go ahead and pick off the skinnies and report back to La Plata...that's in Argentina, by the way.' (El Presidente shakes his head and shuts off the phone as he flings his cigar out into the lake.) Frisco: 'That moron will lead his unit to Azerbaijan if someone doesn't give him a map that he can understand.' (A whistle from the dictator draws his Secretary of Maritime Operations and Fishing Poles, Juan de la Reza.) Frisco: 'Juan...' Juan: 'Que necesitas, Presidente?' (The dictator winces as he removes a melted ice pack from his crotch.) Frisco: 'Otro paquete del hielo para mis huevos, por favor.' Juan: 'Si, Presidente!' (The secretary rushes off to fetch the ice pack, but bumps into El Magnifico Busmuchacho de Mysterio on the deck. The Guatemalan luchador shoves the secretary and exclaims:) El Magnifico: '¿Qué estás haciendo? ¡Ahora déjeme!' (The colorful wrestler makes a dismissal motion to the Argentine politician, but is kicked in the shin for his troubles.) El Magnifico: 'Ay! Cabron!' (The politician quickly disappears below deck and the luchador begins to follow, but is pulled back by the strong grip of the Hall of Famer Dictator.) Frisco: 'Leave him alone, Busmuchacho!' (Magnifico frowns and stomps his feet.) El Magnifico: 'Me llamo Magnifico!' (El Presidente smirks and rolls his eyes.) Frisco: 'No, no. There is nothing magnificent about you whatsoever. However there is something very busboy about you...' El Magnifico: 'Busboy!? Que es eso!?' (Frisco puts his arm around Magnifico and limps slightly, adjusting his swollen testicles.) Frisco: 'See, Busmuchacho, your career as a wrestler is long gone. Now you are my personal assassin, ambassador of Guatemala, and fall guy. You have a very important role that exceeds magnificent and falls somewhere along the lines of...busboy. That is why I cannot belittle you with such a meaningless part of your name. Comprendes?' (The Spanish speaking Central American shakes his head.) El Magnifico: 'Ehhh...no comprendo, Jefe.' (Frisco smiles.) Frisco: 'Yo se, yo se.' (The politician rushes back to Frisco with the icepack. He and Busmuchacho exchange glances and then blows as Busmuchacho reaches for the ice pack.) El Magnifico: 'Damelo! Damelo!' Juan: 'No! Es para El Presidente!' (Frisco slumps and watches the disgraceful scene.) El Magnifico: 'Quiero a darlo al Mero Mero!' Juan: 'No! Es mi trabajo!' (After pulling on both sides of the pack, the two feuding Latinos rip it open, spilling ice all over the deck. Frisco scowls and barks at his servants.) Frisco: 'Now look what you've done!' (El Magnifico looks at Juan and mimics.) El Magnifico: 'Mire que lo hiciste!' (The Guatemalan receives a slap to the back of the head courtesy of his boss.) Frisco: 'Vos tambien!' (Magnifico looks down in shame.) Frisco: 'Listen, my balls are not feeling well at all. It is imperative that they are healed as I have a match tonight against Rent-a-Cock Smoking Shit for Brains Asslicking Peasant and that unrefined wetback Diablo. More importantly, I have another match this Friday against Chris Chase that will allow me to gain the #1 contendership for the Continental Title so I may take it from Rent-a-Fucktard's polluting grasp.' (Frisco darts his eyes at both men.) Frisco: 'Is this getting through?' (Juan nods his head while Busmuchacho looks on in confusion, but nods his head after seeing his enemy do so.) Frisco: 'Magnifico, te gusta El Diablo?' El Magnifico: 'No! Es un mexicano sucio!' Frisco: 'Y Alquiler-un-Joto?' El Magnifico: 'Ehh, no le conozco, pero jotos...' (The Guatemalan looks over to Secretary Juan.) El Magnifico: 'No.' (Juan frowns and begins to approach the masked luchador, only to be stopped by Frisco.) Frisco: 'Gentlemen, we must work together. My success leads to your advancements. When I win, nosotros ganemos. Si?' (Both men shake their heads.) Frisco: 'Beautiful. Now, both of you go get another icepack and make sure all the ice is still in there when it is pressed against my sack.' (El Presidente shoos his men away as he leans up against his rail, staring out at the peaceful Canadian lakes.)
