Manfred Mania - Part 1 - Introducing...MANFED!

27 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: April 27 [The scene opens on a pile of crumbled up tissues. These crumbled masses of Kleenex are covered in a greenish yellow liquid, which looks unbelievably gross. A man coughing can be heard in the background, it sounds so horrible. It's almost like a mix between a car that can't start up and a frog] [The scene slowly moves upward to a fairly large figure lying on a leather couch. The bright blonde haired man, who usually looks happy and upbeat, lies in misery. His bright blue eyes don't look so bright anymore as they are bloodshot and watering to no end. He wears a blue and yellow bathrobe and a box of Kleenex sits on top of his chest for quick tissue grabbing. 'Totally Tubular' Tommy, one half of PCW's bodacious tag team Cowabunga Connection, moans to himself, wanting this sickness to leave his body. He wonders to himself why it had to happen at this moment, just when they were on their way to chase down those bogus dudes, Politically Incorrect. Maybe those dudes were the ones that gave him this sickness. That is the only explanation!] [In the background you can now hear clanking and cupboards closing. Tommy looks to his right, where the kitchen is, to see exactly what is the matter] 'Totally Tubular' Tommy: Harry, what's going on in there, dude? [More clanking and banging is heard in the kitchen. A loud beep, obviously from a microwave, follows the clanking and then there is a pause of silence as the stoner voice of 'Heavy Metal' Harry, the other half of the Cowabunga Connection, comes from the distance] Voice of 'Heavy Metal' Harry: I'm making you soup, man! [Tommy's face shows complete confusion as you can hear Harry open the microwave, then a scream soon follows] Scream of Harry: ARGH! It's hot, man. [Tommy's face brightens for the first time in weeks as he enjoys a cheap laugh out of his best friends pain. The confusion soon returns to Tommy's face though. Harry doesn't really cook, and he sure doesn't know how to use the microwave, it's too complicated for him. Maybe he's turned over a new leaf in life, considering Tommy has been deathly ill for the past week] Voice of Harry: Here comes the soup, man. [Harry walks into the scene with his long, brown hair tied in a pony tail and he wears his black leather pants, accompanied by his black cowboy boots. The odd thing about his wardrobe now is that he's wearing a black apron that reads 'Twisted Sister Rules, Tool Drools'. He holds a large bowl in his hands, filled to the top with soup. He holds it over Tommy wearing a large cheesy smile, obviously proud of his accomplishment of making soup in a microwave] Harry: Get ready for the most far out soup, man. [Harry goes to feed Tommy some Campbell's Chicken Soup but unfortunately Harry forgot that you have to still hold the bowl when feeding someone soup. The bowl drops right on Tommy's crotch, leaving the steaming hot soup to fry his tubular testicles] TTT: YARGH!!!! AHHHH!!! NOOO!! [Tommy, even in his sickly state, is able to leap off the leather couch and hop around as if his crotch is on fire, which in a way it is. He falls to the hardwood floor moaning in pain from his crotch and in complete exhaustion from this virus that has plagued his body for seven plus days. Harry stands over Tommy in a frozen state of shock] Harry: I'm sorry, man. TTT: ARGH!! MY GNARLY NUTS!!! [Harry lets out a small giggle. Then quickly thinks about how he can solve this situation. Harry leans over Tommy and begins to stick his hand down his bathrobe. Tommy quickly rejects his best friend's hand] TTT: ACK!! What are you doing, compadre?! Harry: I was going to massage your gnarly nuts, man. [Tommy moves the pain and exhaustion aside and looks at his best friend, since birth, with total disgust] TTT: NO WAY, DUDE!! As bodacious as you can be I don't want your hands anywhere near my gnarly nuts! I'll save that for that most bodacious babe, Jessica Alba. [Harry then smiles at the mentioning of Jessica Alba, the Dark Angel to the FOX viewers, but the perfect angel to the Cowabuna Connection. Harry quickly changes the subject from the awkward situation of Tommy's gnarly nuts to Jessica Alba] Harry: Do you think that she is into threesomes, man? [Tommy's face now brightens for a second time in the day. His mind wonders on just who the other bodacious babe could be to enter the threesome. There is plenty of Yahtzee playing action to go around from the Tubular One, so a foursome might be in order] TTT: I don't know, but next time I talk to her I'll be sure to ask, compadre. [Harry smiles, happy with Tommy's decision] Harry: That would be far out with you getting her in the back door and I'll be fuc-- [Tommy quickly cuts Harry off] TTT: DUDE!!! What is the name of bogusness are you talking about?! [Harry pauses for a moment, wondering where Tommy's hostility is coming from] Harry: A threesome, man. We'll give her a Cowabunga sandwich. [Tommy now has the look of disgust on his face once again. He can't believe what he's hearing from Harry right now. FIrst he tries to massage his gnarly nuts and now Harry wants to join him in sandwich action with Jessica?! Harry must be in a bad way] TTT: We're best friends and tag team partners, dude. As gnarly as it would sound for Jessica to be with two bodacious dudes in the sack there is absolutely no way that I'm doing that. We've known each other since we were born and I don't want to see you naked. [Harry pauses once again, using his mind for once, and he only uses it when it comes to sexs, weed, and wrestling] Harry: But I saw you naked when we were born, man. TTT: That doesn't count, dude! We were little wrinkly, gnarly dudes then. I can't even remember it. Harry: I do, man. TTT: There is no way you could have. Harry: Yeah, man. I was hung like a horse, man. [Tommy looks at him with even more disgust as he raises himself back on the leather couch. Tommy assumes his position recovering from his illness, wondering if Harry is ill too, mentally that is] TTT: It's impossible for you to be hung like a horse when your born. Your already all little a squirmy and to have a gigantic coc--- Harry: It is possible, man!! TTT: NO WAY! Harry: WAY, MAN!! I was the most hung baby in that hospital and all the far out babes could keep their eyes off my schlong, man! TTT: They were all babies, dude. The little bodacious baby babes didn't think about penis yet. That comes later on down the road when they grow radical breasts. [Harry shakes his head, upset that his best friend and tag team partner won't believe that he was a hung baby and that all the chick babies were digging his horse like coc--] Harry: Your wrong, man. They wanted my weiner because it was gigantic and your just jealous because I got all the babes. You just CHOOSE not to remember, man!! TTT: Nobody remembers right when they are born, dude! It's a proven fact. Harry: Who proved it then, man? TTT: A bunch of scientist dudes. Harry: Your LYING, man!! TTT: I am not lying, dude. Harry: It's just this sickness that is messing with you, man. It's frying your brain from seeing what truly is the truth, man. TTT: No, it's not, compadre. I know that you can't remember when you were born and you can't be hung like a horse when your just a little gnarly baby. It's just not possible. Harry: NO! No, man! Are you possessed by one of those little creatures like in that movie Alien, man? I'LL SAVE YOU!! [Harry leaps on the couch and begins pounding Tommy's chest to try and release a little alien from him. He needs to free Tommy's mind and show him that you can remember when you were born and you can have a giant horse-like penis AND the little baby babes can check you out!!] TTT: STOP IT, DUDE!!! When I get better I'm going to make sure I nail you with THE WIPEOUT!! Harry: GRAAH, man!! [Just then a figure appears in the kitchen. The man looks extremely tall and very bulky. His forehead extends out very far and his black hair is slicked back perfect. He has a very black, bushy mustache that wiggles back and forth from time to time. He wears a tuxedo that is in perfect condition and nothing is out of place] TTT: Who is that, dude? [Harry smiles brightly and walks over to this stranger that resembles Lurch from the Addams Family] Harry: This is Manfred, man. TTT: Who's Manfred? Harry: He's the dude that is going to help you get better so that we can go and kick Dustin Downey's and Karl LeBlanc's ass, man!! [Tommy's eyes grow very large as the man named Manfred just stands there and nods. The scene then fades to black]
