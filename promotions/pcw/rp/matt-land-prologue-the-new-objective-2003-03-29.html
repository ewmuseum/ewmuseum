Matt Land, Prologue: The New Objective

29 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/29/03 Matt Jackson swaggers with confidence into the conference room, of the Central Intelligence Agency in Washington, DC. He stands facing the famed round table where the Joint Chiefs of Staff commonly meet. Big enough to seat 25 people, the round oak table only sits four people today. At the head was Peter Benson the DIC and to his right and left where his two deputy directors. The other is his handler Denis Manellza; he is the only outsider in this group. The four men focus on the young Irish man that has just taken a seat in front of them. He is their best agent and one day would probably be the man taking their jobs. None of them knew much about Matt, but hardly anyone did. He was nothing more then a ghost and they wanted to keep it like that. He runs his hands through his short brown hair and smiles. “Good morning gentlemen, now that everyone is here I believe we can start the meeting,” the DCI starts off, pressing his wooden pipe to his lips. “I hope you guys looked over my briefing about the current situation with Mr. Kannon. I outline the entire plan of action in there and now I just need the green light,” Matt bellows out, his brown eyes softly scanning over the four middle-aged men in front of him. “Yes, I do believe we have all looked over it,” Denis responds, with a sense of eagerness in his voice. “You have the green light to do whatever you need. Phone taps, pulling of files, and anything else you want. Edward has been informed to give you anything you need.” “That isn’t our main concern though Matt,” Benson cuts in. “We are a little worried about this new girl you have working with you. She doesn’t seem to have any experience and this mission is a little out of the ordinary.” “I understand and I assure you that she is fully capable of handling anything that comes our way. You all have to remember that Christian MacManus wasn’t just born the best. It took me years to get to this point and I am sure you know that better then anyone else Peter.” “Matt, I was there with the SEALs in Hong Kong and I know what you are capable of doing. I trust your opinion more then anyone’s in this building. I just hope you are thinking a little more with your head, then you are your dick,” the DCI laughs. He knows that Matt knows what he is doing. This is a rather simple mission for him, much easier then the things he did in Paris and Hong Kong. None the less it was still important because this was the first time in history that a mission would be on National television week after week and thousands of viewers would see what was happening, every step of the way. “My head is fully on the mission, I can assure you of that. I plan on doing what I was sent to do and that is destroying the PCW. Nothing is going to hold us back and that means even if I have to remove people from the picture.” An evil smile comes over his face, knowing that what he is doing. This mission had been right up his alley and he knew it would be easy. There were so many favors that he would still be able to call in and so many ways to go about this. It was just about finding the proper and quickest way to strike and get out. Everyone knew what he had been signed for and this was going to cause some major hostility, but Matt was ready for it. Xavier had practically told him that he was going to be allowed to fire anyone who stepped in his way. The main corner stone of this plan was the demise of Jeremy Howard. He had to be removed from power and then Xavier would have free reign over the PCW. Kannon had handled him once before and Matt trusted that he could do it again, but if anything stepped in his way. Matt would do the job himself, this mission might have been Xavier’s idea, but Matt wouldn’t let him stand in his way. He was going to destroy PCW and nothing was in his way. “The other reason we called for you today is there is something going on that we need help with Matt,” the DCI starts up. “Some bastard over at British intelligence is giving us some problems and we can’t get anything on him. A lot of high security clearance and we can’t get past it.” The DCI takes a vanilla envelope from the briefcase in front of him and slides it across the oak table to Matt. He scans over it and gently rubs his chin with his forefinger and thumb, memorizing the information inside. Pictures of the target, information about their schedule, and the mission as a whole, everything he needs to know to obtain his goal. “This can easily be done. Just give me a few days and I should have everything you need after Turmoil,” Matt says, closing the folder and smoothing out his black suit jacket. His appearance had become key as of late and the suit was becoming a common ware. He wanted to make sure that everyone thought very highly of him. “The other thing before you leave that we need to discuss is exactly what you are going to do in the ring,” the deputy to his right starts up. “Once again, all eyes are on you Agent Jackson and we want to know that you are going to get everything done. You see when you are wrestling you make us look good. You need to be cold inside that ring. You have to show that the CIA is ruthless and I really don’t care how you do it.” “I completely understand. I will give you guys a small preview on Turmoil,” Matt hisses back, trying not to hide the joy in his voice. He has had the two on his mind all day today, but he still knew nothing about them. Jade and Lacuna Debris were just a pair of names. They had done nothing to stand out or make themselves noticed to him. Each had great careers outside of the PCW from the rumors. Nothing that caught Matt’s eye and nothing that brought any worry to him. Just two people that were standing in his way from getting his work done and he is going to remove the problem with or without Haley’s help. He knows that she isn’t going to be the dominant force that he has to be. He has to strike fear into both of them, just like he has to strike fear into the British intelligence officer’s daughter. She was his objective and he would get the information the agency wanted. Looking over the profile he hadn’t seen anything out of the ordinary. She was the daughter of a high-ranking agent in British intelligence and seemed to be nothing more then a spoiled little girl. From her habits she didn’t do much except lounge around. Which was going to make it difficult to get to her without someone noticing and he didn’t need that. British intelligence would want his ass on a platter if they found out what he was doing and it was going to be added problems he didn’t need. His match on Turmoil and Xavier’s demanding workload was going to be more then enough. He was slowly sorting out everything in his head and it was just a matter of time when he should strike. “I will have her by sometime tonight,” Matt starts, pulling himself out of the pit that is his mind. “She will be in that room and I will have her broken by first light and then after that the little British bitch will give us whatever we want.” “I knew you would be the perfect choice for this job. No one quite does it like Matt Jackson,” the DCI bellows back. “Just leave Hilton out of this one, I don’t need her getting her feet wet on a mission that is this important. I am sure you understand.” “You think I am going to let some chick stand in the way of my objective? I think you guys are stating to take this a little to far. By now I think you guys would know exactly what I am capable of and that I will do whatever it takes.” “We know exactly what is going on Matt. We aren’t blind, you rescued the damisile in distress and now you are looking out for her. She is attractive; I would do the same thing in your situation. You just want to get your dick wet,” Denis jokingly says. “Look just drop it and leave it up too me. You will get your information and I will handle the PCW situation my way.” Matt stands up and pushes the flimsy wooded chair away from him and walked out of the conference room. He would have his objective reached by the end of the night and he would make sure he got everything, he needed from her, by any means necessary and he knew that was a promise. I am Matt Jackson, This is my mission, This is my organization, No one can stop me.
