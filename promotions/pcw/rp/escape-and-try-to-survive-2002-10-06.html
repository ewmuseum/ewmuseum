Escape and try to survive

6 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 06/10/02 “As powerful as he is, his power cannot compare to the power I feel in my heart.” – Titu Xansiv (06/10/02) -==-***-==- The past. As told by The Pharaoh… Before Cyclonia came along and muted the Neo Void God, Akiyama had been quite the speaker. I can clearly remember many a time when Akira spoke wise and mystical words to me…ones which, and I do not deny this fact, have helped me through my life and aided me in difficult situations. The God and I were once close friends. Perhaps it denies it now, but in a day long ago Akira Akiyama and Titu Xansiv were equals – we were both on the same page. As Tag Team Champions we dominated every team that opposed us and sent them to a personal void where they linger today. We were unstoppable… Together, we would dine in fine restaurants, we would find new and dominant ways of defeating opposition…I, in fact, was there when Akira received one of its most devastating occurrences – it changed the beast’s life forever. The day that Japan banished Akiyama from its own home was a day that the God will forever wish had never happened. In its own way, it dealt with the exile, but as much as it wants to forget the incident it never actually will. For the Celestial it was a grief-stricken day and it all happened as it was opening fan mail… Never did Akiyama expect that last letter to be from the emperor of Japan, and suffice to say, it certainly never expected the shard of white paper to address the fact that, as of that day, the God was never allowed to return to any part of Japan in its immortal life. From then on much changed in Akira’s personality. The monster began to speak as though it were two different people. It changed its name simply to Akiyama. And it began a rage-flushed destruction upon PCW’s roster. I was stupid enough to challenge the God. And yet for my stupidly I gained a massive amount of respect from the other wrestlers in PCW and more importantly the TV title. It was because of Japan’s neglect for the nVg that it is how it is today. Deep down it can’t take in the fact that its home country would toss it the curb as they did. The God is full of fiery fury. And thanks to Japan I now have to face that inner-rage this Sunday… The God now hates me. Cyclonia herself has said so. She described me as the Neo-Inphino Blitz. A nice title for a mortal such as I, huh? Inphino Blitz was the man Akiyama drove from PCW; the man he hates the most out of every other mortal upon this planet, and I get described as second to him on Aki’s ‘hate scale’. Now, I don’t like being hated by anyone, but then again Akira Akiyama isn’t just anyone, is it? The God of the Void wants a piece of me and frankly I’m now willingly to give it to him. I just hope that evil son of a bitch knows what it’s getting itself into… For its sake, I truly do… -==-***-==- At this time of the year it is said that atop Mount Fuji temperatures can drop to -18°. Alas, it is not somewhere one would wish to be trapped alone, never mind even contemplate venturing. And yet some insane people do attempt to battle the wintry climate in hopes of worldwide recognition. Those stupid few seldom make it back down to the bottom of their own accord, and just as many actually make it back down alive. But on a place such as Mount Fuji of Japan, how do you know that the climate is all that could kill you? The answer, my friends, is that you don’t… -==-***-==- Silence. It enveloped the tree-flushed valley and deprived it of any sound whatsoever. Deep within the dark confines of a cluster of tall and green-leaved trees, a shivering group of three companions huddled around a log fire, warmth from the fiery stove acting as their only defence against the bitter cold of winter. The men, all of whom looked to be battle-worn and weary, wore thick furs of unknown beasts; yet from the outlook these didn’t seem to protect them from the snow that was beginning to settle about their feet whatsoever. One of them looked up at the hazy, white sky and mumbled a few clearly startled words. “How… the hell did we… manage to escape… it?” His companion, a small, shivering man looked about them, his face twisting and grimacing as he frantically peered into the unforgiving darkness beyond. “Who the fuck knows?! I don’t even know what it was—” “Be quiet, man!” hissed the third person, a man shrouded in darkness. “We do not wish to attract attention to ourselves!” Far off in the wilderness a roar of pure hatred erupted over the skyline of trees. “If we silence ourselves we’ll be fine. Just keep quiet,” he continued, “I don’t know what the hell it was either…but the look in its eye was more than enough to tell me it wanted our blood upon its hands!” “And what hands of a monster they were! Did you see them?!” began the second of the trio, ignoring the words of his friend, “Almost as big as hams, and I was sure there was the slightest glimmer of silver from within the closed fist.” “I too saw that,” concurred his comrade, “horrible they were, truly bloody horrible.” “Will you listen to yourselves?! You’re going to get us all killed dammit!” bellowed the third of the men, a wild mix of frustration, fear and determination to actually stay alive present in his voice. The other two shared looks and nodded solemnly. “Yes…we must hush ourselves before the creature unearths us.” One murmured. “Please, forgive me, Mark-Anthony, but it struck such fear into my heart that I have been ushered into swearing!” the other persisted, “It is far from my usual character to use foul language…” “Indeed, but if you’d just keep quiet, Joseph—“ Another roar rendered the mountaineer to fall silent. The echo of the monster’s bellow flooded the snowy trees and forced the ice itself to change direction in the wind. The men cowered and watched vigilantly for any sign of the beast that was tracking them through the harsh wilderness. Deep down they knew they couldn’t escape the creature’s wrath, but they clung to hope and prayed silently to God that they would come out of this situation alive. Suddenly the crunch of snow under a heavy foot rendered the men to turn. Their eyes filled with terror as the creature and an unknown accompaniment stood behind them, their clothes blowing almost poetically in the wind. “Praying to God won’t help you.” Said the unknown person. The voice was that of a woman. “There is only one God now.” She patted the monster on the shoulder. -==-***-==- A fountain of harsh black smoke billowed from the now rocky area. The destruction was more than obvious. The clove of formerly sheltering trees had been blown away with such force that a crater of ominous size had been left in its place. And high above the scene of terror and rage, a lone helicopter circled the unholy hole… The frantic voice of the news reporter from within the steel contraption was spoken in a native Japanese tongue, and as the fire from some of the remaining trees ripped through the atmosphere the monster and its master walked methodically away from their doing. -==-***-==- “We interrupt this in-flight movie to bring you news of a startling discovery.” Dammit! Why did this always happen? As if the flight to Japan wasn’t boring enough, I now had to put up with some dumbass news report. “An explosion has wrecked a large portion of Mount Fuji, and early reports indicate that three men could have been killed.” They had my attention now. And for some reason I got the picture that the images they were showing us were a little more than natural. Whatever had blown half that mountain away had been a—a Godsdamned monster… A monster? Questions arose… What in this world was more powerful than anything else? And what on this earth was actually capable of blowing up half of Mount Fuji? And what in this world hated Japan so much that it would blow up a portion of the great mountain? I knew that it was responsible now. That fuckin’ beast… That frickin’ creature… That Neo Void T H I N G! Did it hate Japan so much that it would actually kill people who decided they wished to set foot in the country? Was that its take on things these days? Was that what it wished to do? Well no more…it wasn’t going to get away with this… The police wouldn’t know what did it. The authorities wouldn’t either… But I sure as Duat knew… I knew what had killed those men… And its name was Akiyama. “Wait! We’ve just got word that a survivor has been found! We’re taking you straight to the scene now!” screamed the news reporter. The image on the wide-screen quickly changed to show a small, aged man, covered in ash and bleeding from small gashes upon his face. Next to his shaking form stood a Japanese news reported holding a microphone in his hand. “Wha’ actually happened here today?” said the reporter in a typical Japanese-English accent. Akiyama’s victim narrowed his eyes. “I don’t know… We were just—just hiking up the mountain and then it came…its terrible black eyes…its icy stare, and - the man shuddered – its…friend…” “Friend?” the reporter repeated. The man nodded solemnly. “Who ever she was, what ever IT was, I’ll never get over that thin-lipped smile…eguuhhh…. I don’t wish to talk about it any more…Please? Can I go?” The Japanese man turned his eyes back to the cameras. “OK, we take you back to the news center in Tokyo, now!” I turned my eyes away from the screen. No longer did I want to watch the actions of the God. For its evil it would pay. As I’d said to myself many times in the recent past: “It was no longer about the World Title…it was about banishing that beast to its own personal void.” No longer could I stand by and let the two self-proclaimed ‘celestials’ go about as they pleased. We have rules upon this earth for the love of Horus! And killing people for no reason isn’t one of them. At Red October I’d put up a fight…. And even if I was beaten at least I’d tried to stop it in its unstoppable path of destruction. But if I did win, then fuck you, Akira. “I just beat your fucking ass, nVg!”
