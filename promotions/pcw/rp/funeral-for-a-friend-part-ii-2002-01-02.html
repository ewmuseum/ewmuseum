Funeral For A Friend (Part II)

2 January 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1/2/02 Funeral for a Friend Part 2 We all knew Mr. Valendez oh so well. He will be sourly missed in all of our lives. The compassion, love, and goodwill this man generated was one that cannot be forgotten by the hands of the clock. It was his attitude that touched us, it was his smile that greeted us, and it was through his strength that he spoke to us. He was a trooper till the end, and he never had a bad thing to say about anyone. Life was a puzzle for him, but he embraced the many different people he knew to help him solve that puzzle. I wish to be half the man that Salvador was. He guided, he led, he followed, and he listened. What more could you possibly ask from a person? My words echoed through the church as tears looked right back up at me. I meant every word I said, and I meant it with more than just my heart, but rather my entire body. A heart is an instrument that you use to express a message; I could have expressed this message without a heart to make it easier. As I sat back in my pew and folded my hands across my chest I though about how our dynasty started. I may have been the one doing all the wrestling, but we were a team and not anybody could take that away from us. “Step inside.” Those were the first words he said to me as he stood inside a round ring with a steel cage around it. “What is this?” I looked on at this contraption and didn’t have the slightest clue to what it was. “This my friend is the ring that you will shed blood, sweat, and tears in.” His words were as loud as a bullet, but I didn’t know how true they were until years later. “Get in we have no time to waste.” I followed him not knowing what I was getting into, but as hours passed and my mind expanded on this gift of fighting, I became a veteran. It finally felt like I was doing something right with the body God gave me and I was thankful for that. “You know Salvador, I’m happy I came here today.” I spoke those words with truth. I knew something new and great was coming, and I just had to let it all sink in. “Here’s something that will make you a little more thankful.” He handed me an envelope and I quickly opened it. I found a check inside, and I couldn’t believe what I was actually holding. “What is this for, I did nothing to deserve pay.” I couldn’t understand for the life of me why someone would give something I didn’t deserve to me. My whole life I had to do three time the amount of work a normal person would do to get somewhere, and now I was getting somewhere without doing any work at all. “Think of it as an early present. You deserve this pay because you came here today and gave me the honor of working with such brute force and technique as that of which you possess.” He made sure I kept that check that day and then he invited me into his home to stay. That night, I slept under a roof. And it wasn’t just a shelter where they stick people. It was a place where I felt at home. A home I never had. So here I am sitting and listening to everyone pour their souls out about this spark of greatness they call Mr. Valendez. I sometimes wonder why God must take something that hurts so many of us. God grants life to help put smiles in our face, but then takes it away to keep us from getting too greedy. I guess I just don’t understand the world. I may be a complex man, but I’ll never be complex enough to understand the realms and plot of this world. I walked home that night and I did so alone. I was alone again and realized I had no one to go to anymore with my problems. This man wasn’t only a business partner he was a friend, the type of friend that can’t be replaced. *Flashback* I had just come out of the ring and made my tenth successful title defense surpassing all Spanish Underground fighters before me. I was at a state of self-satisfaction. The words Salvador said to me that day will stick in my head forever. Hey Romantico I got something for you over here. I followed him not knowing what to expect. Look at this. I grabbed this framed photo he handed me and looked at it with strong emotions. It’s you and I when we first met. Now take out the photo from the frame and look at what it says in the back. I did as instructed and what I saw was a small amount of words that delivered a large amount of impact to me. In large written black handwriting it said “Me and the soon to be Champion. The son I never had.” I looked at him and wanted to cry, but my body didn’t allow it. He smiled at me and I just smiled back. I held in the words I wanted to say and just stood their saying not a word. *Flashback Ends* If only he were here today I would simply say to him, “And you were the father I never had”.
