Scorpion: I Remember

24 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8-24-02 I remember the first day that I heard his name… Scorpion. Such an eager young fellow. Dying to break into the business. He thought he would win everything… and there was much hype. Defeat didn't cross his mind. He was booked against me in part of the tremendous battle royal to crown a champion of WWCF. But he ignored me. He thought he was too much higher, too much better, to ever sink to the level of Trigger. Did he even notice me? Did he even comprehend the possibility that I could rise above him, snatching the meager glory he so longed for? Scorpion. The name sounded interesting at first. Quick and fragile with a deadly surprise? Powerful yet agile? An unstoppable beast in the body of an insect? All of these things could have been true. All could have been useful. All could have been deadly. But in the end I realized that Scorpion was nothing more than a coward, reaching desperately towards the spotlight, but never coming close. I wouldn't learn for months what this creature was truly like. It didn't matter. After countless promos with childish insults and cheap boasts, I realized that Scorpion was out of his league. Scorpion didn't know what he was in for. Much time later, I heard his name again. This time in a much different light. With WWCF closing before our match could take place, I always wondered what become of the opponent… always longed for the clash that never took place. His name in my mind was identified with hatred. What I wouldn't have given for a few minutes in the ring with him… for a chance to watch the blood roll gently don't his face. His face which was still a mystery to me. I remember the first day that I saw his face… So arrogant. So cocky. So self-assured. So fearless. Didn't he understand? Didn't he see that he was stepping into a whole different world when he entered the realm of Primetime Championship Wrestling federation? It was only when he came to PCW that I first got a glimpse of this foe in person. It was only then that my hatred could become physical. Of course we fought each other almost immediately. Fate isn't so mysterious after all. Of course he had become everything that I hated so much. A gimmick: so cheesy. When it comes down to it, who is truly afraid of a hustler? Isn't that just a word made to make small men feel big? I didn't even take notice of him long enough to hear his catchphrases. I can only guess. 'I'm gonna deal you some pain!'? Ridiculous… such a catastrophe, such a fool he must be. A fool that had eyes bigger than his talent. Aiming right for a division he should have stayed out of. The Rising Star division… my division. His face is one I'll not soon forget. So arrogant. So cocky. So self-assured. So fearless. He shouldn't have been. I remember the first day that I smelled his fear… In his eyes the first time we fought. On the outside, he was a stone. But the scent of fear is not a light one, and it drifted from the innermost cavities of his soul. He wasn't nervous. He wasn't afraid to loose. But he was afraid of me. He can deny it, and certainly will if asked. But in the end, there is no denying the truth. There is only one person that I have met that is not afraid of me. And I'm terrified of him. Lance Minion, The Insane One, the sole possessor of my vulnerability. Nothing like Scorpion. I will never fear Scorpion. Scorpion will always fear me. I remember the first day that I tasted victory… His fear immediately led to his demise. He was scared--and I took advantage of it. I don't quite remember our first encounter… that was a time when many things were unclear to me. I do remember my loss in the match… but in another sense victory over Scorpion. Not in a bragging-rights sort of way… more as a plain and simple fact. Victory. I was distracted that night. Determined to get a piece of the one opponent that had slipped away. I lost the battle… but I won the war. I lost my Rising Star title, but I gained victory. Not true victory, but moral victory. My will was stronger than his. I remember the first day that I touched defeat… All morals aside, a loss is a loss, and that is what I suffered. The agony was intense… I felt as though my life was over--I am not accustomed to such things as defeat. Nevertheless, I took the defeat, and I didn't complain. I triumphed over Scorpion in my own twisted way. I didn't pin him, nor he I. Ever. And perhaps deep down, he truly does feel the same was about me, as if he gained something from out encounters. I only believe what I think I know… I only think I know why my soul tells me to. My soul never lies. I remember the first day that his spirit died… I remember the past. I remember the present. I remember the future. Friday night on PCW Fusion, Scorpion and I will face again. This time, things in my mind are clearer. There will be no draws, no miscommunications. There will be a victor, and I rest assured that it will be me. After this Friday, I will remember how I triumphed once again, and the arrogant Scorpion crumbled to nothing… …At the hands of a warrior. I remember you, Scorpion, and I'll never forget.
