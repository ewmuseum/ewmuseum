Shabazz Undergoes Sensitivity Counselling...(And Becomes Something He Never Wanted To Become)

4 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 06/04/02 Jordan X Shabazz is Crazy. He’s freakin’ crazy. He just can’t be mentally stable. Look at some of the arguments he makes, he thinks everything has something to do with race. He thinks there is going to be a race war. He has animated conversations with himself over racism. He’s crazy. But now, PCW officials have forced him to under go some psychiatric counseling to learn how to work with, and respect white people. The only white men that Jordan respects, publicly at least, are Jon Kano and Duke Williams, and that’s probably because Jordan believes that they treat him with respect, as he should be treated. He was going to have somebody, a friend, come with him, but Jordan thought better of it, there’s no reason to have another black brotha with you and be embarrassed by being forced to be interviewed, by the white man! The scene opens up in a plush office that is very roomy. Leather chairs adorn the room; a nice Afghan is covering the majority of the floor. A good breeze is coming from an open window. There’s a bookshelf with lots of books on physiology. There’s a clean desk with the words ‘Doctor Steven Chambers’ on it, that is has a neatly stacked amount of books on it. The doctor sits in a leather chair, as Jordan lies in a larger leather chair. His eyes are closed; the session has just begun because the doctor has an empty notepad in front of him. Doctor Steven Chambers: “Okay Mr. Shabazz, I want you to envision yourself in a room full of white people. They are not saying anything mean, they are not arguing with you, they are treating you with respect, like they should.” Jordan begins to fidget around uncomfortably. He looks nervous. His hair is very nappy and uncombed, he wears baggy khaki shorts, some untied brown Lugz boots, and a large orange Platinum Fubu t-shirt. Sweat drenches his forehead and t-shirt. Doctor Steven Chambers: “Okay Jordan, tell me what you see?” Jordan takes a deep breath, he appears to be extremely strung out, as if he hadn’t gotten any sleep in the past few days. His eyes are closed still. Jordan X Shabazz: “I see Duke Williams… Jon Kano… The Phantom Booker, Ryan Stephens, BloodAngel, Ricky Thunder, And the Supreme Machine.” The Doctor quickly jots something down on his notepad. He nods and ruffles his dark brown suit and wipes sweat from his graying hair. Doctor Steven Chambers: “And these people are wrestling friends, I assume?” Jordan nods slightly, eyes still closed. Doctor Steven Chambers: “Care to explain to me why you do not like white people? Or at least the majority of them?” A scowl comes across Jordan’s face, he can’t stand the thought of thinking of white people while being interviewed by one, but he begins to speak. Jordan X Shabazz: “Everything about them makes me sick. They think they are better than everybody else, they think they’re so damned cool because they do their little ‘Extreme sports’ bullshit, come on, skateboarding, skydiving, white people do extreme sports, but black people live extreme lives.” Jordan finally opens his eyes for the first time in the session; he looks at the doctor with a lot of anger visible on his face. Jordan X Shabazz: “Damn it, this session isn’t helping me, I hate white people because they have the unfortunate luck of being white, and this is not going to change me.” Steven Chambers nods slowly. Doctor Steven Chambers: “Well, if you don’t want to be helped, than I can’t help you. But since you are a Civil Rights Activist, I thought that you should learn to work with white people through this therapy.” Jordan doesn’t reply, he has gives Doctor Steven Chambers stony faced glare. Doctor Steven Chambers: “Now, there’s this one exercise I want you to do, I’m sure that it will help you…” Jordan X Shabazz: “God damn it, I don’t need to be helped! I’ve grown up with white people, and I know how they are, they aren’t ever going to change. They are nothing but a bunch of jealous bastards who always have a knife ready to stab you in your back.” Doctor Steven Chambers quickly scribbles something on a notepad and nods at Jordan. Doctor Steven Chambers: “Well please Mr. Shabazz, just give this a chance.” Jordan stares and nods slightly at Steven Chambers, almost as if giving him permission to go on. Doctor Steven Chambers: “I want you to dress up like a white person, and live the life of a white person for the day.” Jordan X Shabazz: “Aw hell no!” Jordan is now standing up and right in the face of the Doctor. The doctor wipes sweat from his forehead and quickly tousles his grayish balding hair. Jordan is breathing heavily in his face. Jordan X Shabazz: “You’ve got to be fucked up. There is no way in hell I’ll dress like a white person. All the other Civil Rights Activists will laugh and point at me.” Doctor Steven Chambers: “Mr. Shabazz, I assure you that we can get the best make up artists on you, nobody but you and I will know about this.” Jordan X Shabazz backs up for a few minutes and he begins doing some serious thinking. Duzza is a white guy now… maybe I can see what it’s like for him… Jordan thinks to himself. Jordan begins fidgeting around his pocket for his wallet. He goes into his wallet and pulls out a fifty-dollar bill. Jordan X Shabazz: “Here.” He thrusts the bill in the face of the surprised Doctor Steven Chambers. Doctor Steven Chambers: “What is this for?” Jordan X Shabazz: “This is so that you don’t go around telling no tabloids about this shit.” The Doctor refuses Jordan’s money and shakes his head. Doctor Steven Chambers: “Don’t worry about it. If you don’t want to be recognized, all you have to do is change your style of speaking, the way you walk, and the clothing you wear. You’ve got to act white, to not be noticed. I’ll call the make up artists right now.” The Doctor goes into the coat pocket of his brown suit, and he pulls out a cellular phone. *********************************************** Four hours later. It was over. The torture of putting on the make up, the torture of trying on numerous t-shirts and numerous pairs of pants. Jordan was now completely white in every way shape or form… Well… Except in his pants, if you know what I mean [Insert winking smiley here]. Jordan stands up. He was alone now. In the elevator on his way down to the streets. He wears tight leather pants, a white ‘Megadeth’ t-shirt, and some torn up white Nikes. He is obviously uncomfortable in his new ‘white’ duds. The elevator door opens as Jordan walks from the doctor’s office the crowded mall that houses it. He sees people zooming by, everybody in a hurry. He begins walking towards a small convenience store. He was hungry and he was going to buy a few things, and perhaps a wrestling magazine, just so he is caught up on everything that is going on in the wrestling world. As he enters the store, the man working the counter, immediately nods at Jordan, and Jordan nods back. That’s weird, when he was regular and black storeowners weren’t just smiling and nodding at him for no apparent reason, he must have something on his face, Jordan thought to himself. Jordan immediately headed towards the junk food section, and he grabs a few bags of Doritos and he grabs a large Pepsi from the refrigerator thingy that houses the drinks. Jordan walks by the magazine rack and pulls off the latest issue of Pro Wrestling Illustrated Jordan takes the magazine to the front and he drops all of the things he got onto the table. The man working the desk quickly rings everything up. “That’ll be $14.56,” says the store clerk, who appears to be rather old. Around fifty. Jordan reaches into his pocket and places a ten dollar bill on the table. He begins fidgeting in his pocket for the change that is needed to pay for his purchases. The store clerk leans over the desk, and stops Jordan. “It’s okay my son, take your food and your money and go,” he whispers. HOLD UP! What the fuck?! This never happened when he was black and normal, is it because he’s white looking now that he is getting his purchases free from an old, white man? Is this something that happens regularly, just out of the eyesight of the black man? White people giving other white people free things? Jordan, needless to say, is shocked. He quickly grabs his money and his leaves. He’s a black man who hardly gets anything free, he’s grateful that he has free stuff, that stuff doesn’t usually happen to a Negro like him. As Jordan walks out of the restaurant, he ponders the events that just happened. He was so white that he couldn’t be recognized by the average Joe. Jordan looks for a place to sit, and he finds one. It’s a small brown bench, nothing too important about the bench. Jordan takes a seat next to an old lady, with her purse wide open and some money hanging out. That’s weird, Jordan thought. How come she isn’t taking her bag away. What’s going on? Had this been a regular day in the life of a black man, she would have taken the purse and ran. But she’s just acting as if Jordan’s nothing, just a normal guy who poses no harm to her. Jordan pretends to flip through the pages of the magazine while eating some of the chips that he just bought. Suddenly, out of the nowhere… “How is your day, honey?” WHAT THE FUCK?! Now an old lady was making small talk with him, all of this because he was a white man? What the hell? “Uh… Good,” Jordan replied politely, he had to take all the southern Ebony slang out of his voice when he replied too. This was some serious stuff, he had to somehow get this on videotape. He could do a documentary exposé on the white world! Think of the press it would get. ‘Civil Rights Activist exposes crackas to the world. The visions danced in Jordan’s mind, not only would he be announced as ‘World Famous Professional Wrestler, he would become World Famous Civil Rights Activist.’ A man who fought for black rights, and still had time to get in the ring and whoop ass! This, was the best thing that could have happened to the Shabazzonator X. The God of Crunk must have been looking upon him today, because he has just gotten an idea, and some free food. He had to do this again sometime…
