Date: Dates are not made, they are grown on trees. (I think.) (We see a scratchy, grainy, black and white film playing. The title appears: Empty Road, Empty Hearts The camera cuts to a lonely stretch of highway. Naught but dust and the occasional crow on the telephone wire breaks the endless monotony of the asphalt ribbon as it winds its way across the countryside. A grubby little Volkswagen Jackrabbit chugs along, seemingly slow but for the lack of reference points with which to gauge its actual speed. Seated inside the primer gray, diesel smog-maker, THE Multiple Man Entourage members Raul and HV fight against the inevitable malaise that comes from long association with the open road.) Raul: “[Something dubbed in French.].” Subtitle: “We travel long, down ze dark pathways of ze heart, my companion. Where we have been, it matters not. Where we are going is equally unimportant. What matters now is zat we keep going.” (HV shrugs, his eyelids drooping uncontrollably in fatigue.) Subtitle: “Yes, my friend, I agree. But do not despair in your infinite loneliness. Grab your dreams with both hands and let them take you past ze misery and ze pain of living.” Raul: “[Something dubbed in French.].” Subtitle: “Ah, but the bleakness surrounds me inside and out, it’s load unbearable and torturous to my feeble flesh. How can I resist ze night when she is a part of me? Ah, I can see her face even now, calling me, beckoning always! Bah, speak to me not of zese dreams, for zey are ze cause of my emptiness.” (The back of HV’s head hits the headrest with a soft thunk and snaps upright again as he breaks free from the seductive embrace of microsleep.) Subtitle: “My poor friend, I too, hear ze sirens call of ze void… but what is zere? An endless sea of nothing upon which we are bits of nothing bobbing along ze tide. No, let ze dead remain in zere graves. T’would be better for both us and zem to make a clean break from all our old lives.” Raul: “[Something dubbed in French.].” Subtitle: “Perhaps you are right. Perhaps I will see past ze beauty of ze ebon twilight to ze bitter cold light of ze coming dawn… and be thankful.” (‘THE END’ scrolls up and the ending credits begin to play as the camera pulls back to the inside of THE Multiple Man’s secret creative lair and mosh pit… okay, not mosh pit, but I had you going there for a second… Tmm and Keira are flopped on the battered old couch staring at the TV in utter incomprehension.) Keira: “What in the fuck was THAT all about?!” Subtitle: “Notre liaison amoureuse de torrid m'ennuie. N'y a-t-il rien plus?” Tmm: “You think I know? I don’t know which is worse! That these bonehead French Independent film directors are making bootlegs of company footage, or that they seem obsessed with taking the most drab and boring bits of it!” Subtitle: “Votre hantise avec la chair me rend malade. Elle m'a toujours rendu malade.” (Keira rips the baseball cap she is currently wearing off of her head and tosses it squarely into the TV picture frame.) Keira: “If it wasn’t for the booze in the fridge, I’d be strangling myself with my own intestines right now! It’s like they very carefully and deliberately avoided anything that might have made the flick the least bit INTERESTING!” Subtitle: “Vous ne savez rien du mépris loathsome auquel je vous tiens. que je pourrais me libérer des feux de mon hantise.” Tmm: “Still I thought the cinematography had some merit…” Subtitle: “Les chaînes qui vous lient à moi sont plus légères que ceux qui me lient à vous. Vous êtes le poison qui m'accomplit.” Keira: “IT WAS OUR CINEMATOGRAPHY!!! All thirty seconds of the film loop that they KEPT REPEATING!!!” Subtitle: “Maudissez-moi pour un imbécile! Ma faim pour vous écrase le mon embittered l'esprit ainsi et me rend délaissé pour résister!” Tmm: “That could be why I liked it sorta. Tell ya what, lets all of us go out and see Spiderman or something.” Subtitle: “Je vois votre désir et il ne signifie rien à moi.” Keira: “Again? You buying?” Subtitle: “Votre mockery brise mon essence comme rien autrement que j'ai jamais éprouvé! Comment peux je supporter?” Tmm: “Depends on how many of us are going, I’d think.” Subtitle: “Je ne crois pas vos mots, mensonges viens aussi facilement que la vérité à vos lèvres.” Keira: (ticking off names on her fingers) “Raul and HV aren’t back yet from getting Steve from the clinic, but Val’s still here.” Subtitle: “Il n'y a aucune vérité mais besoin, et mon a besoin de truie les graines de ma destruction.” Tmm: “Wasn’t she gonna go out with that jerk face of hers?” Subtitle: “Un fruit amer pour ceux tel que nous. L'oui amer, mais améliorent avec qu'en dehors!” Keira: “Was, but you hired him to scope out that Suma guy and he needed the money.” Subtitle: “Oui! Prenez-moi maintenant et laissez ma douleur se développer de sorte qu'elle puisse me consommer tout à fait!” Tmm: “Not my fault! I hired him a WEEK ago! I was doing the ass a favor tossing work his way!” Subtitle: “Je volonté, mais pas pour vous je fais ceci. Non, je seul fais ceci pour I et moi!” (Keira shrugs indifferently.) Keira: “I think she forgot about it anyway. We ran out of sticky notes a couple of days ago and there haven’t been quite enough reminders up for it to register with her. Worked out for the best this time.” Subtitle: “Je ne m'inquiète pas! La vie est douleur et la douleur est la vie! Laissez-moi être vivant, si seulement quelques moments précieux et passagers!” Tmm: “Such is life.” Subtitle: “Telle est la vie.” (‘THE END’ scrolls up and the ending credits begin to play as the camera pulls back to the inside of a trendy little film café located deep in the heart of Paris. Instantly, chairs are knocked to the floor as dozens of people spring to their feet and applaud wildly! The camera fades out.)
