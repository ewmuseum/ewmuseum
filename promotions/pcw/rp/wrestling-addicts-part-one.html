Wrestling Addicts (Part one)

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 28 Dec &#039;02 The scene opens inside of the FUCKS of America building (See previous promos). We are focused in on a room on the second floor. On the door reads ‘Wrestling Addicts’. Once inside the room we see a bunch of people of all different ages, sizes and sexes sitting around in a circle. To the left corner of the room we see Chet King and Dr Roundstone (See previous promos) talking to each other. Chet King: These people are freaks! You sure it’s safe to talk to them? Dr Roundstone: Don’t worry, they’re harmless, except that one in the brown shirt over there. Chet looks over to the circle of wrestling addicts, and see’s a skinny 10 year old darting his eyes side to side and shaking. Chet King: Ah, he looks a bit crazy. Dr Roundstone: Don’t worry, he won’t hurt you as long as you don’t say the word ‘cramacudealer’. Chet King: Is that even a word? Dr Roundstone: No, but it still makes this kid want to hurt people, he enjoys giving people the old kick to the groin trick. Chet King: I’m pretty sure I shouldn’t have any trouble not saying that word. Dr Roundstone: Good, good. Chet King: What am I going to be doing today? Dr Roundstone: I’ll go over there and introduce you to the group, then you can just give them a brief talk about why they shouldn’t be addicted to wrestling, etcetera. Chet King: Am I meant to be working individually with them? Dr Roundstone: Yeah, but we’ll get to that later. Chet King: I’m set then, I’m ready to meet these freaks. Dr Roundstone: I don’t think they appreciate being called freaks. Chet King: Do they appreciate being called weird? Dr Roundstone: They don’t mind that. Chet King: Good, let’s go meet these weird people then. Dr Roundstone and Chet King walk over to the circle of wrestling addicts. They all sit there staring at Chet King, as after all he is a wrestler. Dr Roundstone: Hello group, I trust that you’ve all been able to stay off watching wrestling for the last week. I know it must have been hard with such quality programming like the Decimation pay per view, and the Carnival of Carnage. Tell me, honestly, who survived this week? Out of the fifteen or so in the circle, only one puts their hand up. It’s a 20 year old girl. Dr Roundstone: Very good Veronica, you’ve done very well. I’m disappointed in the rest of you though. Veronica beams as the other addicts glare at her. Dr Roundstone: I thought that you would have been able to last, especially you Scotty. Dr Roundstone looks in the direction of a muscular man 20 something year old man, who looks normal except for his eyes which appear to be lost in a world of his own. Dr Roundstone: You were doing so well last week Scotty, I thought that you had been cure. The man starts to sulk as Dr Roundstone consoles him. Dr Roundstone: Don’t worry about it Scotty, you’ll get there one day. As for the rest of you, three words come to mind – shame, shame, shame. As you can see I’ve got a special guest here today to talk to you about your addiction, he is none other then PCW’s own Chet King ‘The Chat King’ The group stare at Chet King with their eyes wide open as Chet seems a bit nervous. Chet King: Hello everybody. In a monotonous voice, the group all replies ‘Hello Chet King’ Chet King: I’ve been told to come here to talk to you guys today about your problem, about your addiction to wrestling. I hope to get to know each one of you better a little later and find out why you watch wrestling so much, but for now I’ll just introduce myself. As you probably know since you’re all fr… Dr Roundstone whispers to Chet. Dr Roundstone: Don’t call them freaks, they won’t appreciate it. Chet nods his head. Chet King: As you probably know since you’re all weird people who just can’t stop watching wrestling, I’m Chet King. I’ve been involved in wrestling ever since I got a job as the host of a wrestling based talk show, which subsequently caused PCW to pick me up in hopes of me turning my show into one fully devoted to PCW. Chet King: I can assure you that before I got involved in wrestling I was never addicted to it, I may of watched a show once a month or so, certainly not on a regular basis or an insane basis, such as watching videos, replays and shows twenty hours a day like you freaks do! This mention of calling them freaks upsets the group as some of them start to cry. Dr Roundstone shakes his head at Chet. Dr Roundstone: Come on Chet, I warned you. Chet King: Sorry, I didn’t mean to call you freaks, I just meant to call you weird. This makes the group smile. Chet King: Anyway, I don’t see why you’d watch wrestling so much. I mean some of the stuff that gets shown makes me sick. Especially stuff with guys like The End, a guy who just gets on and contradicts himself. He tells me I can’t let things lie and I had to come back at him, but then he just comes back at me. Who would want to watch wrestling when you’ve got guys contradicting themselves and insulting everyone’s intelligence? Chet King: But then again, The End is retiring so that means that wrestling won’t be so bad next week. Heck, it probably won’t even insult your intelligence next week, you’ll be free to watch it! Dr Roundstone: Ah, Chet, you don’t want to encourage these guys to watch wrestling. Chet King: Oops, that’s right. I’m not meant to be encouraging you to watch wrestling, and I’m not meant to be saying cramacudealer. Don’t worry, there won’t be any mention of either from now on. Just as Chet is about to continue to talk, the 10 year old kid gets up and kicks Chet straight in his groin causing Chet to fall over in pain. Dr Roundstone: Uh, guys, why don’t you take a break and come back in ten minutes, Chet’s going to need some time to recover by the looks of things. Chet rolls around in pain as the scene fades out. The scene then fades back in on the wrestling addicts who are now back in their chairs while Chet has continued his talk….oh wait, the scene isn’t meant to have faded back in yet, that’ll happen next promo.
