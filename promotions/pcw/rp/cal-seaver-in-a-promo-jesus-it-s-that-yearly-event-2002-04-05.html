Cal Seaver in a promo? Jesus it's that Yearly Event!

5 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5th April 2002 Miyagawa waited patiently outside the Asylum gym here in Anaheim, California. Well it’s called the Anaheim Dungeon... and like most things in Anaheim will more than likely appear in a low budget Disney film soon, possibly starring Emilio Estevez, or maybe Charlie Sheen. Miyagawa always fancied Robert De Niro to play himself... although probably Robert from about twenty years ago. Miyagawa: Hmmm... Maybe that bloke who plays Casey or Lacey from Dawson’s Creek... god I can’t stand that show but the blonde chick is... He notices the camera and smiles happily towards before carrying on speaking almost as if he did before... just watching his words a little more now than he did before. Miyagawa: Is so not as much good looking as my dear wife Natalia, Hi Natalia, I know you’re watching this! And yes I have taped Dawson’s Creek... just JJ keeps watching it in the house probably because he fancies Dawson or something. The mild manner suited man known as Cal “Hey, this is an Armani suit jackass” Seaver appears on screen, probably from behind the camera. Miyagawa: Sex shops closed today, eh Cal? Cal smirks and grunts at Miyagawa before turning up the collars of his shirt. Then the realisation that it would reveal that his black tie on white shirt (on nicely tailored black suit) would be revealed as a clip-on and promptly turns the collar back down again. Cal: That’s the good thing about Primetime Championship Wrestling as a company I mean. We get through so many wrestlers and so much talent that no one will even remember what happened in that sex shop with the Blood Brothers. Miyagawa: You mean you having a quick case of bashing the bishop to the pornographic sections featuring a video starring another one of our amazingly underused interviewing staff. Do people even know we have them anymore? Cal: DAMMIT! Now people will try to look that up... luckily I burned all video evidence... you can’t get me that easily. Miyagawa: Apart from the security tape from the sex shop that Brock sold to the National Enquirer. Unfortunately a well known man wanking in a sex shop wasn’t radical enough for them... so they ran a story on Chris Chase being straight instead. Cal: Chris Chase being straight? Bah, I’m closer to being Timo Bolambo with heavy amounts of foundation. So what are we doing here anyway? Do you just like cutting quick and simple promos in your driveway? Miyagawa: Nah, I’m out here waiting for JJ and Natalia’s sister to arrive. JJ said he’d pick her up from the airport. He’s got a part time job there. Cal looks astonished. Cal: Natalia has a sister? Miyagawa grins at him. Miyagawa: I thought for a second you were going to ask about JJ working at an airport. Cal shrugs at the formerly goggle wearing man (although he does wear shades just like Bono from U2 still which do look kinda like goggles from the front). Cal: Nah, he’s a sixteen year old idiot. He works in either Burger King or McDonalds there, right? Miyagawa: Nope. He works in a comic book store. It’s actually pretty neat, plus he gets me discount air travel... hence why I can fly everywhere to places even though I’m on one of the lowest salaries in PCW. Cal: You are? Wait; stop trying to change the subject. Natalia has a sister... how long? Miyagawa: I figure that she’s been around since she was born. Pretty hard to be around prior to conception you know. I would have thought you would have known about conception with that entire pornographic library of yours. **Miya’s eyes go beady like a member of the Spanish inquisition** You’re not one of those people are you? Cal steps backwards away from Miyagawa (but still on the pavement, obviously... he doesn’t want to be run over by Chitty Chitty Bang Bang or something... you never know with Disney next door.) Cal: I’m not gay; I’m not like Chris Chase. Miyagawa laughs at Cal. Miyagawa: I didn’t mean Cal. I meant Germans. They have this really weird concept of pornography... hair... shit... dogs... and that’s just the women. You’re not into that sort of thing, are you cal? Cal (lying out of his ass): No of course not, don’t be silly. Me... nah... never. Not going to happen. I’m over selling aren’t I? Miyagawa just raises an eyebrow at Seaver. Cal (hastily changing the subject quicker than a subject has ever been changed before): So when are they due? Miyagawa: They? We’re not expecting twins or anything. We’ve got about two months to go. Cal looks frustrated... probably because this isn’t going well as an advertisement to get other wrestlers to actually ask him to be used. In fact people are more likely to use Tiffany Jennings because she has large breasts than he because he has a wit like no other... probably means no one else is as witless as he is. Cal: God I’m depressed... I haven’t had sex in three years. Miyagawa (not catching what Cal said): What? Cal: Um.... I said you must be getting harassed... by the fans... you’ve been in PCW almost two years now. Miyagawa: Oh right... nah I haven’t been getting harassed, they know where I live, if they want to drop by then I simply go fetch the shot gun and chase them from my property. Cal: Really? Miyagawa: No, we’ve actually got a six meter high steel fence that extends all around the complex; just it’s being blotted out by very good digital imaging artists. They’ve even managed to remove that ugly zip on your face. Cal: That’s my nose. Miyagawa: Ah. Get some cream on it, it looks horrible. Cal: Predictions? Miyagawa: A boy. Just don’t tell Natalia, she wants a girl. If her sister is staying for a couple of weeks I think that might just drive me insane... three girls and me in a house. Cal: But the baby isn’t due for another months... **realisation strikes** oh, you mean JJ. Miyagawa: Of course I mean JJ. He’s more a girl than Shania Twain. Just without the rather large ti... mammary glands... **turns to the camera** Hi honey, gonna be at the hospital as soon as your little sister arrives... and Lara too... **grins at the JJ put down** Suddenly a yellow airport taxi cab pulls up. A scream is heard inside the cab and one of the passenger windows rolls down quickly and a young lady pokes her head out of the door... its Natalia’s sister Lara. Lara (thick Russian accent): JJ only brought ten bucks with him for the cab, and it came to thirty seven fifty, do you perchance have that sort of dollars on you? Miyagawa looks at Cal. Miyagawa: Armani suit wearer, pay the cab. Cal: Hey! This is finest George Armani, Disney Boulevard Sunday Market! Miyagawa: Gah... I’ll pay for the damn cab then... at least then we get make it to the hospital before it visiting hours end. And maybe then I might be able to get JJ off my hands so I can actually get some training in for Sunday. Cal: Don’t be a joker; you’ll never be able to loose JJ. Miyagawa looks at Cal. Cal looks back at Miyagawa. Miyagawa looks at Cal some more. Cal gets worried. Miyagawa: Joker. Cal, you are a genius.
