Tubular Tan

29 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: August 29 [The scene opens on a blonde and brunette woman, sitting on a beach. Both women spreading tanning lotion all over their tan, tight bodies. Their sunglasses hiding their eyes, but yet making them look even more sexy. The blonde in a pink two piece, and the brunette in a blue two piece, the women continue spreading lotion] Blonde: This lotion just isn't doing the trick. Its just missing something. Brunette: Like, for sure. [The women shrug their shoulders in search for a better tanning lotion. Then from the right side a man on a surfboard comes flying in front of the ladies. They jump in fright, wondering just where this man came from. The wavy, bright blonde hair blows in the wind, the perfect brown tan, muscular body, the bright blue swim trunks, and the Oakley sunglasses. This man is none other than 'Totally Tubular' Tommy! He shines a bright smile to the camera, showing his perly white teeth. He looks at the ladies] 'Totally Tubluar' Tommy: Dudettes, there is no need to be bummered out. I've got just the perfect solution to your most BOGUS problems! [He reaches into his right swim trunk pocket and goes to pull an object out, but his urge looks like he's trying too hard to just simply pull something out of his pocket. He adds just a bit too much force to his motion and a bottle of tanning lotion that reads 'Tubular Tan' flies through the air and nails the blonde beauty in her head] Voice from far away: CUT!!! Damnit, Damnit, Damnit!! [The scene zooms out, showing a television set. The sand fake, the sun made by studio lighting, and the breeze by a wind machine. A very tall, thin, and balding man sits in a folding chair throwing his arms in the air. The frustration is begining to make his balding problem worse than ever before] Director: Tommy, this is becoming ridiculous! We've been here for five hours trying to shoot this thirty second commercial. You either jump in and crack Missy with that damn surfboard, or your hitting her with the lotion bottle!! [Before the director can finish his rant, the blonde woman, Missy, stomps her way from the beach set over to the defending director. He puts his arm around her and she leans into his scrawny, hairy chest, looking for comfort] Missy, the blonde actress: Michael, I can't take this. I have lumps all over my head. I may have brain damage from this guy. Can't we just get rid of him and get another person. Director: Missy, we can't do that. Tubular Tan is his product, he designed it, and we need him to be in this commercial, if not then we lose this and some other director will score me out. This is my chance to go Hollywood! [Meanwhile, Tommy still stands on the beach set, motioning with his hand to his pocket and then out again. He can't understand why the bottle keeps flying out of his hand. The brunette woman stands and walks over to Tommy, she removes the sunglasses, and her eyes show much sympathy] Brunette: Its all right, Tommy. In show business things like this always happen. There is always someone that messes up, but most of us are professionals and know how to handle it. TTT: Your really righteous to be supporting me. I just feel completely bogus about this. I don't mean to knock that chilla in her head, it just happens. Its hard to just hop onto a set with my stick and not have control. [The brunette woman smiles and hugs Tommy. She then lets go and looks at him with an angelic smile, forming dimples. Tommy suddenly gets that feeling deep inside him, this woman has unlocked his feelings. He has the strong urge to do what it takes....to play Yahtzee with her] Brunette: I'm going to get something to drink, do you want anything? TTT: Thats all right, babe. There is no need for righteous liquid at this time. [The brunette walks off the set and toward a table filled with bottled water and sandwiches. Tommy holds his head down in shame, feeling so low. He sits down on the fake sand and begins running it through his hands. In his mid thought about whether he should stop this commercial a figure begins rising from the fake sand. Tommy shoots up, thinking about his nightmares about evil bogus sand beasts, but this figure looks extremely familiar] TTT: Totally untubular, dude!! [The man stands and begins shaking sand off of his body, giggling madly to himself. His brown hair reaches down to the small of his back and his goatee is tied in a braid reaching to his chest. The sand clings to his black T-shirt and to his black leather pants. His eyes are extremely red and halfway closed. He stumbles over the pile of sand he created over to Tommy. 'Heavy Metal' Harry, brother and tag team partner to Tommy, puts his hand on Tommy's shoulder] 'Heavy Metal' Harry: Listen, man, its all right. I'm sure it took that Robert Deniro guy to do that Rain Man stuff in 120 takes, man. TTT: That was a movie, compadre, and this is a commercial. I'm no where near Deniro status, dude. [Harry stumbles to his butt to sit down next to Tommy. He realizes that he is stoned beyond all belief, Tommy might catch on. Thank god for that technician kid making that secret smoke out room below the beach set, he needed a good fix to perk up the spirits through this trying commercial shoot. Harry puts his arm around Tommy, looking to console his buddy] Harry: Man, your just being way too hard on yourself and your trying too hard to make Deniro status. Or is it that stupid crap that happened on Turmoil, man? When you got sprayed, man? TTT: Yeah, my mind is going over that, dude. It was TOTALLY BOGUS what those dudes did to us! I'm still sore from head to toe from that totally heinous beating that I took. I can't believe that dude sprayed the Anarchy symbol on me, compadre. It's like they marked me for some kind of totally bogus death. They cost us our tag titles, and now they humilate me in front of all our most bodacious fans! These dudes are TOAST! Harry: Yeah, man. How do you think I feel being locked in a closet with the Olsen Twins, man? [Tommy looks at his brother/tag team partner with his eyes wide open, taking in every single word he has said. Besides the odd smoke smell he takes in, the words have really gotten to him] TTT: The Olsen Twins?! Dude! What in the name of Kelly Slater are you talking about?! Harry: I woke up in a closet with the Olsen twins, man. [Tommy looks at Harry with is bright blue eyes widened in horror] TTT: Dude.... Harry: What, man? TTT: Did you DO SOMETHING WITH THEM?!? Harry: No, man. [Tommy breathes a sigh of relief. He then stands up and takes off his sunglasses, staring up to the sky with his baby blue eyes] TTT: We're going into Fusion with our most bodacious eyes set on taking out Playaz Inc., dude. Then once we prove to those totally bogus newbies who the best is around this place we are TOTALLY going to take care of those heinous dudes P.W.A. Harry: Yeah, man. [Harry then walks off of the beach set. Tommy shrugs his shoulders and replaces his sunglass back on his tan, strong face. He smiles at his realization and feels that good things are only ahead for him. The brunette woman walks back over sucking down some water from her bottled water. She stands beside him looking at in curiosity] Brunette: You look so much more upbeat now, Tommy. Think about that siutation more? TTT: Babe, I realized many bodacious things and its time to move on to bigger, better things. I'm a professional wrestler now, and nothing bogus and untubular will stop me. Things are going to be outrageously radically for me in the near future. [The brunette smiles widely, showing those dimples. Tommy can see why she is an actress and looks like she'll be a face in everyones heart. Hopefully his first] Brunette: That is so great! I wish you luck. TTT: Hey, I've been thinking about this since the moment we first talked, dudette. I can't hold this back any longer, it would be bogus to hold it back. Brunette: What is it, Tommy? TTT: I would love for you to accompany me to a game of Yahtzee! [The brunette seems confused at first, but her face then brightens up. She smiles and takes Tommy's hand. She puts her lips right up to his ear and begins to whisper] Brunette: I'd love nothing more than a long, hard game of Yahtzee with you, Tommy. [Tommy smiles, showing the perfect white teeth. Nothing is better than a righteous game of Yahtzee with a bodacious babe] TTT: Things are going to be bodaciously bad ass, babe. [The scene then changes over to Harry gobbling down sandwiches left and right. The after effects are always what get him the most, the munchies really get him in the end. He over heard Tommy with the brown haired woman and wonders how he does it everytime. Women find him irresistable, it makes him sick sometimes. Then the blonde girl walks up grabbing a bottle of water. Harry decides this is his time, its now or never] Harry: Hey there, hot stuff. My name is Harry, man. Missy the Blonde actress: Um, Hi. Harry: Yeah, baby. So you want to partake in a game of Yahtzee with me? [The woman looks offended and then smacks Harry across the face with much force. She puts her hands on her hips with anger in her eyes] Missy the Blonde actress: I am not that kind of woman!! How dare you! I'm going to tell Michael about this and make sure that you get fired, you'll never work in this town again! [Harry seems confused about her words. He doesn't remember being hired and working in any town for that matter. If she isn't into board games, he might as well try a more direct approach at getting this fine, hot honey. The woman is storming off to the director as Harry begins to take the approach] Harry: If your not into Yahtzee, man. Maybe you'll like my one eyed monster, and we can do it ALL NIGHT LONG, man. [Harry laughs to himself and gives a thumbs up, knowing that this is his chance. Suddenly a high heel shoe flies past him, almost stabbing him in the head. He sits on the table of food, showing signs of relief that he didn't get hit. He stands and looks at the back of his leather pants. A sandwich sticks to his leather pants. He looks mad and upset at the discovery, but then shrugs and takes a bit of the sandwich] Harry: Its still good, man. [The scene then fades to black]
