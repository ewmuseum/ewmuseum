Title: Mastering: The Kendo Stick! Featuring: Politically Incorrect Date: 26th Extreme. It is a term that means different things different places. To the Hardcore Isosceles Trapezoid, extreme means Trick or Treat, arguably the most dangerous match in wrestling. To Miyagawa, it means the Japanese Deathmatch war he waged with Kensuke Suzahara. To Big Daveylicious, it means fighting Brandi, while trying to pick her up. The hardcore mindset is a very interesting thing. To different people, 'hardcore' or 'extreme' are different things. It is because of this truism that anyone can be hardcore. Whether attacking an opponent with a flaming barbed wire wrapped nail bat, or bashing an opponent in the skull with the much more inocuous cookie sheet...either activity can classify you as 'extreme'. It all depends on your surroundings, your environment, and who you are. To a southern aristocrat, hardcore is not something that would appear to be second nature. You would be wrong. Reginald V. Lovecraft is a very hardcore man. His childhood was hardcore. His political career was hardcore. Hardcore does not necesarily denote a style of wrestling. It can denote a mindset, a way of life. Despite being a politician, Reginald V. Lovecraft fights against politics he feels to be harmful. He refuses to accept the status quo at face value. To some people, this makes him extreme, or hardcore. After all, it does not take that much skill to bash a man in the skull with a weapon. It takes much more skill to face him with no weapon and explain why he is wrong. Yes, Mr. Lovecraft is hardcore. But his weapon skills need work. Enter Katherine Willis, a black belt in various martial arts disciplines, and a pair of kendo sticks. Let the games begin! **THWACK!** ::The sound of bamboo kendo stick hitting flesh rings throughout the hotel hall. Reginald V. Lovecraft cautiously raises his sword again, only to have his opponent strike him again, this time in the shoulder. **THWACK!** Katherine Willis: Come on, Reginald, is that all you have? ::Katherine cockily smiles at her boss. She is a picture of loveliness, even in her workout attire. Lovecraft, though, looks out of place in his, as it is the first time we have seen Reginald without his trademark red suit. He shakes his shoulder out, in an effort to alleviate the pain, as he shakes his head and attacks again, swinging the weapon overhead, but Katherine manages to side step, and smack him in the stomach.:: Reginald V. Lovecraft: Ouch! Katherine Willis: Come on! The other guys in that match aren't going to take it easy on you! :She swings what she thinks to be a knockout blow to the head, as the weapon cracks off Reginald's head, but The Governor does not go down. He straightens, and manages to parry her second attack, and swings a huge blow, which shatters a nearby flower pot.:: Katherine Willis: That's it! Channel the pain! ::Lovecraft doesn't seem to have much technical knowledge of the weapon's use, but he uses it quite ably, albeit wildly, to beat down his intern's guard. She smiles, and quickly calls out.:: Katherine Willis: Enough! Excellent, Reginald! :She smiles, embracing and placing a gentle kiss on Lovecraft's cheek.:: Reginald V. Lovecraft: Thank you....I fear it is only a beginning, but a good one nonetheless! I am now master of the kendo stick! :Lovecraft smiles, somewhat cheesily, as he raises the kendo stick over his head, and swings it in a circle three times: Katherine Willis: Soo, what do we do, now that we have fought in a hotel lobby? Reginald V. Lovecraft: I'll need to learn more weapons, but that can come another day... Katherine Willis: So, what did you have in mind? Reginald V. Lovecraft: Well...I kind of wanted to know if you'd.....well, like to go out tommorow? ::Katherine smiles...despite the intern innuendo that has ensued during Lovecraft's PCW tenure, there has been no romantic relationship between the two. This has been no fault of Katherine's, as she has wanted this moment to come for some time now.:: Katherine Willis: Yes! Yes, of course I will! :She smiles:: Reginald V. Lovecraft: We'll have to find somewhere private to go...you know, I'm really quite worried about William S. Powell. I wonder where the man is.... ::He looks down at his cellular phone, and notices a message on it. He raises it to his ear, and hears the voice of his tag team partner: William S. Powell's Voice: Hello Reginald, this is William. I've been trying to contact you for some time now, but there has been no answer. I'm slowly getting over this Price Brothers thing, although, I'M STILL PISSED AS ALL HELL! I heard you're entering the Extreme Title match...good for you. I'm not. I've got some things that need to be taken care of back in Des Moines. I'll be flying back out there after Turmoil to take care of political business. If I do not see you before then, farewell for now. **BEEP** Katherine Willis: What is it? Reginald V. Lovecraft: Powell is off to his home after Turmoil. He has business he must take care of in Des Moines, he IS a city councilman after all... Katherine Willis: Then, bid him well, we have things to take care of now. Reginald V. Lovecraft: We do? Katherine Willis: We must decide where we are going on our date tommorow! Reginald V. Lovecraft: Indeed! The Governor is in office once again. PCW...mark my words...you are looking at your next Extreme Champion! ::We fade to the usual advertisement:: Paid for by the friends of Reginald V.
