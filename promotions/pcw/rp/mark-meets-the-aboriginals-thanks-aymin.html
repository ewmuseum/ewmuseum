Mark meets the aboriginals......(Thanks Aymin)

2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7-9-02 ::Introduction:: Following the loss on Fusion against The End, Mark has realized he has slacked even more in PCW. Losing about four matches in a row now he has Turmoil off like every week. Now he has Ricky Thunder on Friday Fusion, the man whos parents really didn't die in a car crash or whatever. They died because of Mark Makonie and his death camp that he has. No one in PCW knows that as it was too harsh to show with the video footage a month ago. Mark has just realized the tough times in his life and now he has another one in his way. He needs to win a match to prove himself worthy in the PCW and so he doesn't lose all faith in himself. That might keep him from quiting. ::End Introduction:: The scene opens up to the wilderness somewhere in the world. It is covered with the trees, berries, and wild flowers around the place. Mark is sitting down in a little spot that has berries bushes around him. He has a simple slingshot in his hand and he has some ammo sitting right down to the right of him. He has one eye shut with the other eye trying to get his sight focused on what he is aiming at. Something makes a noise that sounds like someone stepped on a brach and it cracked. 'Shhh...we are hunting rabbits.' Mark says in a cartoon character voice. Mark has a round ball shell as his ammo and he goes back to aiming in the direction of his animal he wants to get. All of a sudden a brown streak runs out from behind the tree. Mark goes back with his right hand and then he lets go of the rubber band on the sling shot. The round ball goes flying and it misses right behind the brown streak. 'Just missed.' Mark says as he is loading up another ball. 'I was taught to hunt by the greatest Indian that lived. His name was Tecumseh and he was one of the best hunters that ever lived. He left me a book on how to hunt and I can relate that to hunting right now.' Now before I go on, people might think that Mark is going crazy. Why is he hunting? Well it is something to get his mind off of wrestling, but wrestling is always with him. So this is something to relieve his stress. 'I am known to be one of the greats to hunt out here and people know that I get the biggest game every year.' Mark says as he is still trying to load up another round ball in the sling shot. Mark loads up another ball in the slingshot and he now gets to lay down on his stomach. He peeks out of the bushes limbs to see the animals that run across the stretch of grass and wild flowers. Mark sits there for a little bit humming 'Ich Will' by Rammstein. 'For this sport you have to be very patient with the animals.' Mark says as he is still looking out of the bush's little hole that he made. 'You will never know when a rabbit or another small animal is going to pop out from behind a tree or a bush.' Mark sees another brown blur go across the stretch and he holds the sling shot back. He then releases it as the rabbit get to the little hole. The bunny goes down as the round ball hits the rabbit in the side and into the ribs. Mark gets out of his little spot and he goes over to the rabbit. He sees that it is bleeding where the bullet went in and where it dropped there is a little puddle of blood. 'I got him straight in the sweet spot.' Mark says as he kneels down to the rabbit and points out where he hit the rabbit. 'This is the best spot to hit them because if you hit them in this spot then they just drop right like it did.' Mark picks up the dead rabbit and he starts to carry it even farther into the forest. He is carrying it by the fur and some if it starts to rip out of his hand and falling onto the ground. Making kind of a trail back to where Mark was hunting at. Some footsteps can be heard throughout the forest with the breaking of limbs echoing though the forest and Mark just turns around to look. 'Hello, is anyone there?' Mark asks as he is looking around in the forest, behind trees and in bushes. Of course no one answers and Mark just shakes his head as he carries the rabbit deeper into the forest. The noises come again and Mark just ignores them as he thinks they are in his mind. 'AAHHHHH!' Screams a guy who comes out from behind a tree with a spear. Mark jumps way back as he is scared out of his pants. He was scared so bad I think that he will have to change his pants when he gets back into camp.Mark is getting up as a group of people start to surround Mark and they all have spears. 'What do you all want from me?' Mark asks as he is getting up from the ground. 'GRAH!' One of the people say as Mark thinks they look like Indians. 'Well it looks as if you need a dentist visit.' Mark says as he is looking at the man's teeth as he makes that funny noise. The people do not say anything else as Mark is just standing there with his rabbit in his hand with his slingshot in his backpocket. Mark stands there for even longer looking into each of the mens' eyes. There has to be at least ten people that are surrounding Mark now and he is not feeling once bit worried. 'Well if you just wanted to scare me then you did.' Mark says as he starts to head to the outside of the circle. 'If that is all I think I am going to be leaving now.' Mark goes towards three men to get outside of the circle. Mark thinks that he can get out easy, then the people hold up their spears in an X so that Mark cannot pass though the X. Mark goes over to two more men and they do the same as the first people. 'Well it looks as if you don't want me to leave.' Mark says as he is going back to the middle of the circle. 'What do you want from me?' One of them points at the rabbit with his spear and he makes a weird call. Mark then looks over at the man and then at the rabbit. 'Oh you want my rabbit so you can eat it.' Mark says as he is looking at the men once again. 'Well you guys look as if you are familiar with the lifestyle so why don't you go out and get your own rabbit.' The men just look at Mark once again and Mark just look at them again. Mark then takes out his slingshot from his backpocket and he loads up one of the iron round balls he has. 'See you take this and you load this ball into it.' Mark starts to say as he is loading the ball in the slingshot. 'Then you pull back and you select your target. After you get your target selected then you just let go.' Mark lets go of the rubber band on the slingshot and he shoots one of the man in the testicles. The man goes down on both of his knees and then he falls straight into the ground with his face dug in the dirt. 'ARGH!' One of the men says as he goes over to see the slingshot. 'You guys act like you have been shut away from the world for about 30,000 years.' Mark says as he is letting the man examine the slingshot. 'That is what is wrong with civilization today, there are still people who don't have one. They just walk around like they are stumps and they fish and hunt their lives away.' 'What did you say?' The man says really fast as Mark can barely understand what he said. 'Excuse me man, slow down.' Mark says as he is looking at the man's look. 'If you all don't know what a slingshot is then you must not know what television is.' 'Television?' The whole group says as they all look at each other than at Mark. 'Wow where have you all been your lives?' Mark asks them. 'We have been hunting and fishing.' One of them says as he is looking at Mark. 'We do things the old way over here as people say.' 'What are you guys, some kind of Indians?' Mark asks as he is looking at the group of men who have went away from the circle and bunched together. 'Indians?' One of them asks. 'We were here way before the Indians were and we are a lot better than them.' 'If you are not Indians, then you are just a bunch of Indian imposters.' Mark says as he looks at the group who are all standing at the moment. The group look at each other with a mean look as they know they are not imposters. Then one of the men whistles at the back of the men and they make a path. 'I knew you imposters would finally let me go.' Mark says as he is heading to go though the path of aboriginals. Then all of a sudden a pack of dingos start to come through the path and Mark drops what he has in his hands and starts to run. He heads through the forest with the aboriginals throwing their spears at Mark and with the dingos on Mark's heels in the middle of the forest.
