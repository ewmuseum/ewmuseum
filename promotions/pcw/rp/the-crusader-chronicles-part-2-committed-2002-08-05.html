The Crusader Chronicles, Part 2...Committed.

5 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Aug. 5, 2002 (The four men in black suits usher our hero, Venneman Pendragon, down one corridor after another. Pendragon looks around, interested in the different goings on in the Pentagon. He smiles and acknowledges everyone he passes...) 'About damned time. These guys seemed to know what they were doing and exactly what it was that I needed...Information. I was not going to be denied this time...' (Down winding hallways they go, until they come to an area populated by men in white jackets and pants. Their shoes are soft sneakers and each one appears to be off in their own little world. Pendragon looks at them and seems a little surprised...) Venneman: Who are those fellows? Guys? Hello! You gonna ignore me the whole time we are here? You guys are supposed to take me to where I can get the information I need. Come on. The silent treatment is getting old... (The men lead our hero to a door and motion for him to go inside. He walks through and looks around. The room is fairly empty, housing a strange couch, a desk, and a grandfatherly looking figure behind the desk. He wears a doctor's coat and smiles warmly as Pendragon enters the room. The men in black shut the door behind him and stand guard outside/. Pendragon loosens his sword in its scabbard. This is not a comfortable looking situation...) Doctor: Have a seat, Mr. Pendragon. I have been expecting you. Here, you will find all of the answers that you need. Just have seat on the lovely couch and tell me everything that is on your mind... (Pendragon looks at the couch and then back at the doctor. He shrugs and sits down on the couch. He finds the couch to be most relaxing and he goes ahead and stretches out upon it. The doctor moves his chair over and sits next to the hero, and he presses record on a hand held tape recorder. He sits back and listens, but Pendragon says nothing...) Doctor: Well? Venneman: Well what? Doc: What do you want to know? Venneman: Oh...I thought maybe you had some questions...It is not everyday you get to meet the King. (The Doctor just scares at Venneman....) Venneman: Ok, OK...Here goes. I have come here seeking important information. Evidently, rogue superheroes and Super Soldiers, genetically altered men, are going to play a part in the next battle that I must face. I am a warrior in PCW, and I have been chosen by the Wizard of Gauze to strip the King's Gold from the waist of John Crichton... (With the mention of Crichton, the Doctor straightens up a little...) Venneman:...Everything that goes on in PCW affects an entire other world. I am that land's chosen hero, and I must protect all of humanity from the Super Human Crichton.... Doc: And how long have you known yourself to be a saviour? (Venneman cranes his neck around and peers questioningly at the Doctor...) Venneman: I never said I was a saviour. People with saviour complexes are delusional and crazy. I am a hero, chosen by the ruler of a large and beautiful land to defend his domain from the actions of evil men like John Crichton. I am not crazy...I am chosen. Doc: I see. So you believe that this John Crichton has been injected with a Super Soldier serum, and that we can help you? Venneman: Exactly. I need to find the original Super Soldier...Captain America. I figured that you people would be the ones who could tell me where he is. (The Doctor raises an eyebrow at this admission and holds back a chuckle...) Doc: Ahem....Captain America...Unfortunately, I am unsure of his exact whereabouts, him being a superhero and all...Why do you need him so desperately? (Venneman sits up and looks at the Doctor. His eyes smolder insanely, and he begins to speak, harshly...) Venneman: Now look here. How many times do I need to explain myself? I am a hero....H-E-R-O. That spells hero. When you think hero, you think Venneman Pendragon, Crusader of Truth. I have been anointed by Skyler Welson, ruler of Gauze, to do all that I can in PCW to keep his people safe. Gauze is a kind of mirror of PCW, and what happens there has either an adverse or a positive effect on the land. What don't you people understand? (The Doctor gets up and presses stop on the tape recorder. He walks over to the desk and takes a seat.) Doc: So you believe that this Crichton was altered and then rescued by a band of superheroes? DO you believe everything you hear, young man? Venneman: I don't recall saying that he was rescued by superheroes...How do you know that? (The Doctor gets a surprised look upon his face. He smiles and shrugs. He then nods towards the door. Venneman jumps up and draws his weapon, but he is too late. One of the black suited men shoots a dart into his neck. Pendragon drops the sword and staggers back. He pulls the dart out and looks at, surprise writ large upon his face. He holds the dart towards the doctor and a 'What the Fuck?' look passes over his face. He then drops face first onto the floor...) Doc: I hate it when people get too close. Get him out of here. Put him in B-Wing....Delusional ward. And make sure restraints are in place. (The rest of the men enter the room and pick the motionless body of our hero up and carry him into the hallway....) 'After the dart hit, everything went black. I was off to dreamland, and I did not want to be there. I found myself in the vast hall of Skyler Welson, but something seemed different. When last I was there, it seemed a happy place. But now, it had a doomed feel about it. I tried to walk around to investigate, but I felt as though I was moving in slow motion. It was as if something was holding me back. I looked down at myself and I saw that I no longer wore the armor and sword of Pendragon the hero, but a pure white jumpsuit and a straight jacket. No wonder I had a tough time moving. I looked around, and everywhere was the visage of john Crichton's face. He laughed. It seemed as though he were laughing at me...But how could he be? This was a dream. This was not real. I had to get back to reality, and I had to get there fast....' .........wake up............ ..........Wake up......... ........WAKE UP......... .......WAKE UP!!!! ********Flash********* (The scene changes to the inside of a white room with soft walls. Pendragon lies, bound by a straight jacket, on the padded floor. A man who looks out of place in the orderly's uniform is waking him from his sleep. Finally Pendragon's eyes open and he stares into the face of Benjamin, his trusted shouter. How did he get here?) Venneman: Glad to see you got my message... Benjamin: I would have been here quicker if you would use a phone instead of a pigeon. Venneman: You know me... Benjamin: I don't know how you knew this was trouble? I mean, why would you think there was a cover up? (Venneman shakes his head, trying to clear the drugged grogginess, and sits up; quite a feat in a straight jacket...) Venneman: Everyone is so secretive. Whenever I have mentioned something about Crichton, they get nervous, scared. Something is going on, and people seem afraid that I am too close. These government people know something, and I need to find out what they know. The world may well rest on the outcome of my battle with the super soldier. Benjamin: Did you ever read comic books as a child? Venneman: What books? Benjamin: Captain America is a comic book hero. And I heard you have been asking where he is. That is why you are in here. People think you are off your gourd. Do you really believe that you have stumbled onto a government conspiracy? (Pendragon calmly looks into the eyes of his questioning retainer...) Venneman: Did you like the stories of the famed Captain America? Were you entertained? Benjamin: Sure. Cap'n kicked some serious ass.... Venneman: Then realize this...The best fiction is based on fact. Perhaps he is another cover up. (The handle of the door begins to turn, and the Doctor walks in. Benjamin has stood and lays a kick to the ribs of the prone Pendragon....) Benjamin: Sorry Doc...Bastard tried to bite me when I gave him his medicine... Doc: It is alright....Sometimes you must control the violent patients with violence in turn. Head down to room six seventy...Having some problems with a fellow named Freeze.... Benjamin: No problem.... (Benjamin shoots a glance at Pendragon before he takes off. The Doctor takes a seat on the floor opposite Pendragon and stares at him sympathetically....) Doc: I trust you slept well? Venneman: You'll pay for this transgression.... Doc: I am sure that you, the chosen one, will punish me...Now. Tell me all about this John Crichton. What you know and what you think you know as well.... (Pendragon looks at the doctor in surprise...) Venneman: My sword? Doc: Crichton first... 'I knew I was close, and I was right where they wanted me. I guess I would talk. Maybe, just maybe I would get out of here with what I needed. These guys wanted to mess with Lord King Pendragon....I'd show them things they never dreamed...And DAMN that Benjamin for kicking me!!' (Pendragon looks at the Doctor with the fires of hate and insanity mixing in his eyes. He grins a little as he starts....) Venneman: Crichton.... >>>>TO BE CONTINUED
