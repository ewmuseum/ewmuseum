Date: Dont worry bout it ;) “Otto!” Mina de Margo slid into the front hallway and smiled at the person standing in the doorway. Standing there in a pair of tight light blue jeans, black heeled boots, and a thick black coat and scarf was Ayumi Hamasaki. The light brown haired woman smiled back, waving her hand at the manager of Inphino Blitz. “Konnichiwa.” She greeted, stepping into the genkan of the traditional Japanese house and taking off her boots. Mina walked over to her and helped her peel off her winter exterior, revealing a white sweater underneath. Mina was dressed in a pair of dark grey cargo pants with a black tank top and black and white striped arm warmers. “Yo Ayu,” Mina responded, taking lead as Ayu followed her into the hallway. “We’ve been waiting for you. It’s almost time.” Ayu took on a look of worry. “Oh, am I late? Sorry, I didn’t know!” Mina shook her head, her black and red ponytail shaking behind her. “Nah, you’re pretty much right on time. I just have to get Inphino from out back and we’re all set.” Ayu walked into the living room to find a honey haired girl sitting on the floor in front of the big screen TV. She plopped down on the sofa and nudged the girl in the back with her toe, bringing the girl to pause the game she was playing. The pretty girl turned around, releasing a smile at Ayu upon realizing who she was. “Hey there, Ayu-chan.” Hana Maxis said, jabbing the pop idol in the leg playfully. Hana was the daughter of Japan business mogul Alexander James Maxis, as well as the world renown genetics scientist Ryoko Godai. Recently Hana has fancied the company of Inphino Blitz and his friends despite her parents’ disapproval, though sometimes they do permit knowing full well she’ll do it anyway. Just a little backstory for those of you who don’t know… “What’re you playing there Hana?” Ayu asked, peering up at the screen. Two wrestlers were in the ring, while two others were on the apron; a tag team match. Hana turned back to the game and started playing again as she responded. “PCW Turmoil, for PS2. It’s got the latest roster, even the RUSH brand wrestlers, and secret wrestlers including Akiyama, Big Alan Steele, Miyagawa, and more.” Ayu nodded, her eyes still on screen. “Inphino’s not in PCW, so why’s he in the game?” “I made him, so he and Aki could team up against Front Office.” There was admiration in her voice, not just for Blitz, but for Akiyama as well. She had her reasons for both… “Heh, unlikely dear child.” Mina stood in the doorway with her arms folded across her chest. Hana whipped her head back quick enough to stick her tongue out at Mina as Ayu giggled. “A girl can dream, can’t she?” Hana swooned, looking up to see Inphino throw punches at a digital Xavier Kannon. Even in pixel form, XK wasn’t much to look at…which went double for the digital Ellie standing by ringside coaching her husband and Gaz Green on. Mina nodded, agreeing with the young Maxis heir. “Yeah, I guess if dreams came true, I’d have Inphino working in PURE.” “How’s things going there for you anyway?” Ayu asked Mina. De Margo shrugged her shoulders before giving Ayu a thumbs up. “It’s cool, I have a few snags here and there with some of the delinquent wrestlers, but it’s nothing that I can’t handle.” “Enmity is something you can handle?” Hana asked doubtingly. It was Mina’s turn to stick her tongue out now. “Enmity is being dealt with! He’s suspended, and there will be precautions made as for his reckless behavior.” Hana nodded with a laugh. “Then you still have to deal with Raizo Tenzan and the Black Dragons. You think your makeshift security stooges can stop them? Shaun Styles? I think not!” Hana started laughing at Mina’s expense…until a hard slap rang out down the back of her head. The game paused as Hana leaned forward and groaned, her hand rubbing the back of her head. Mina stood behind her with her eyes closed, an annoyed look on her face. “Stop relating to the heels just because your daddy’s a Yakuza boss!” Mina said through gritted teeth. Ayu’s hand went to her mouth in realization. “Oh dear, he in the Yakuza, isn’t he?” Hana turned around and stuck her tongue out at Mina again with the intent to annoy. “You don’t want me to tell my father, DO YOU MINA?” “Chikkusho!” Mina cursed at the 18 year old. “Keep your daddy out of this!” “Make me!” Words soon turned to wrestling as Mina and Hana went at it in the middle of the floor. Ayu sat there on the black sofa watching with a smile of her face. “You two are so playful!” she said before laughing. Sometimes Ayu had a knack for missing the obvious…it wasn’t that she was dim or naïve…she was just innocent and trusting. Mina had Hana pinned to the ground, sitting on top of her chest with her arms pinned to the floor. Hana’s legs were kicking out behind Mina, trying to push the older girl off of her. “Yamero! Yamero!” she snapped at her. “You better give up!” “Ow! Ow! Okay, yamete! Yamete!” just as she submitted, a throat cleared at the doorway. The three women turned around to find Inphino Blitz standing there, dressed in his trademark all black with a blank look on his face. “Having fun?” he asked calmly. Mina and Hana grinned as the both stood to their feet embarrassed. Ayu stood up too, holding back her laughs. “They’re just like sisters, they are!” she chuckled, missing the look of disgust from Mina and Hana. Inphino didn’t miss it, and sighed with a shake of his head. “Come on then…it’s time for dinner.” -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- “So…Ayu…” Dinner over, and the four of them were sitting around the table having green tea. Hana, still dressed in her school outfit of black pleated skirt and white blouse, looked up at Ayumi with curious eyes. The singer returned the look. “Yes?” Hana’s eyes lowered back to her tea. “I was just wondering…how your cousin was doing…that’s all.” The room grew silent, as they all knew the situation between Inphino and Ayumi’s cousin… “Akiyama…” she said, as if she was trying to remember who he was. “Akira is…doing good, after the whole mess that happened with Cyclonica and his friends.” Inphino stared hard down at the table, his jaw squared off as Ayu talked about his rival. Mina looked up at the ceiling, muttering silently about merchandising figures for PURE. Inphino spoke next, to the surprise of everyone. “What is he doing now?” Ayu shrugged her shoulders at that question. “I don’t really know now. I know just as much as you do. After the big fight over at Jahana Castle with those guys in the suits---“ “Executives,” he interrupted. “…they called themselves Executives.” “After the fight with the Executives, he just locked himself up in the castle. He’s not answering phone calls, and not coming to the front gate or anything.” “Where’s his daughters?” Hana asked. She was out of the loop, and needed clarification. “Anarchy’s moved back to the states with Jenna to seek out better recovery for her ankle. Cyclonica…she disappeared after everything started blowing up over there with her friend Matrix. Akiyama threw Sonata out after a big fight they had about her betraying him, and from what I saw, Cyclonica’s mother, Glacia, is either dead or in a wheelchair somewhere.” “How’d that happen?” Inphino shook his head. “Akiyama snapped her spine.” “Snapped her spine??” Hana couldn’t believe that. Ayu nodded slowly with Inphino. “Why’d he do that?” Ayu was the one to answer this one. “I think it had to do with something about his parents’ death. I think she knew who did it and Akiyama exploded on her. I was so freaked out that I couldn’t piece everything together…but Inphino ran in and stopped Akiyama before he could do any more damage.” Hana took it all in with a grain of salt. “So after all this drama, Akiyama’s just sitting there alone in his castle?” The three of them nodded. Hana sipped her tea slowly before sighing. “Wow…poor guy. Wish I could do something for him.” -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- There’s nothing anyone can do for us… We’re past feeling remorse…or regret…or anything else. Our family was set on destruction the day Cyclonica betrayed us with that hammer. It all spiraled down… Our daughters are gone…our parents are gone… We thought about ending it all…but just how does a god commit suicide? Then the answer came to us… …we don’t… As we sit here in the dark depths of this ancient castle we learned the true reason for our existence. It’s not about getting your greedy little hands on as much money as you can. It’s not about how much ass you can smash. It’s not about being the best in what you do. It’s not about any of that… What we live for…is destruction. Men, women, children…material items…buildings…cities…worlds… Even a company like PCW… Even a man like Xavier Kannon… Lotta kuso happens in a week or two…or four or five… Each day since January first came about, we’ve been in our training room…preparing ourselves for the resurrection. The god’s resurrection… Lot of people have seeped into the cracks of the place that still employs us…PCW. New faces…some old faces as well…the joint’s become crowded… We’re just the being to clear out the numbers…if you belong in PCW, you gotta take your beating and ask for more, show us you’ve got what it takes to remain in the hell we’re about to unleash. If you can’t take the beating that’s in store for you when we return…get the f-ck out now. Hell, even if you can take the beating, if you value your health get the f-ck out now. We’re making a comeback on all cylinders…and if you don’t want your pistons broken you’ll get outta the way before we gun over you. Because in the end…everything is destroyed, be it by time, or by action. Everything reverts back…to nothingness. The Void…? Heh…nah, we’re not talking about that. We’re through with that kuso. Nothing says banishment better than straight up death. Shine… that means “god wills that you drop dead.” Nice little credo for the new year…something for all of you to remember. Next time one of you goons thinks you have what it takes to stand up against the nVg, remember that my intentions are already clear. “god wills that you drop dead” Kore wa… nanji no Zettai Unmei Mokushiroku desu.
