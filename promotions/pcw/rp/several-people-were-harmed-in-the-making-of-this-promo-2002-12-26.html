Several People Were Harmed in the Making of This Promo.

26 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: December 26, 2002. As the scene struggles to open to the first Jackson Rust promo that has been aired in a long while, we cut to, well, unsurprisingly, a rather silly location, and situation that can only lead to another humourous situation. Jackson, and his entourage – Al, his faithful cameraman; Steve, well…just Steve; and the damn fine looking in a bikini personal assistant, Kimberly walk down the long corridor, looking at random doors, attempting to locate what you would think is a number. Steve holds onto a stereo, the extension plug dragging behind, as Rust simply carries a compact disk as they walk through a local hospital. Jackson Rust: You know, that narrator has some issues, calling, and saying that my promos are in some way “humourous”, and making me seem like a lazy prick. The only advantage of having him introduce my damned promos is to inform me of you guys’ errors. Steve, pick up the extension cord. Steve picks the extension cord from the ground, wrapping it in circles around his arm. Narrator: Stick with the script, all right? I have enough crap to worry about without my own character ridiculing me, okay? Jackson Rust: Fine… Kimberly: Okay with me. Al: Jackson started it. Jackson Rust: I did not, you jackass! Steve: Heh-heh-heh…a-a-ass. Narrator: I shun you! Considering my narrator duties, I have absolute control over each of you. And Steve, for your comment, you will be lashed unless you moon Jackson’s grandfather! Jackson Rust: Ewww…man, that’s nasty! Don’t make him do— Narrator: Quiet you. Jackson silences himself. Steve: Sh-sh-shit. Narrator: It happens. Steve: Sh-sh-shit? Narrator: Sometimes. Steve: O-oh. Jackson Rust: Enough with the craptacular Forrest Gump rip-offs, eh? All of a sudden, a large glove slaps Jackson in the center of the hallway. Jackson Rust: Ouch! Narrator: Wanna knock Forrest some more? Jackson Rust: But the movie droned for hours! Again, the glove slaps Jackson, this time on the opposing side of his face. Narrator: Now, hang your head in shame, and say “No, sir,” after I ask you once more. Jackson Rust: Okay… Narrator: Ahem. Wanna knock Forrest some more? Steve, Al, and Kimberly all look to Jackson, hinting him to do as he’s told so the promo can continue. Jackson rolls his eyes, uncaringly, and shrugs, hanging his head down in “shame”. Jackson Rust: No, sir. Narrator: Now…get on with the promo before I tell the readers your first name. Jackson Rust: Oh crap, no! Okay! Everyone in position, and go! Al, start us off! Jackson gets rather bossy, pushing Al, hinting him to go. Al: Hey! Jackson Rust: Hurry the hell up before the narrator leaks my first name into media! Rust looks around suspiciously, narrowing his eyes. Al: For pushing me, I’m purposely not going to do it. Jackson Rust: I control your salary; I see it best as you do it. Al: Oh, right. Jackson and the rest of the crew nod, as Al starts. Al: Remind me why we’re here again? Jackson sighs in relief, as the promo is on a roll, and his name isn’t announced. Jackson Rust: Because, dude. My grandfather is dying, and I need to quickly repay all the years I ignored him to get his inheritance, man! Jackson snaps his fingers impatiently, getting Kimberly to read her lines. Kimberly: Oh, sorry. Yeah, err…don’t you think that’s a bit rude? Jackson Rust: Of course not. They way I figure it, every minute that I’m either thinking of him, or spending with him, it’s a year repaid. And so far…I’ve paid off… Jackson glances down to his wristwatch, and begins counting on his fingers; superb acting, eh? Jackson Rust: ONLY FOURTEEN YEARS?! Al: But the drive here alone, was two hours, plus the conflict with that crappy narrator! The same huge glove slaps Al. Al: OUCH! Jackson shrugs, keeping up with the promo, as if nothing had happened to Al, although he whimpers in pain. Jackson Rust: I know…but it doesn’t mean I thought about him, man. I had better things to do. Steve: L-l-li-like w-w-hat? Jackson Rust: Cut the stutter, dude, it equals hard times understanding, and the ratings to drop. Steve: I-I c-ca-can’t h-h-hel-help I-it, m-man. Jackson Rust: Very well, then we’re just going to have to make it work, now won’t we? Narrator: Stop stalling and do the damned promo. Jackson again nods, continuing from where he left off. Jackson Rust: Well, my stuttering friend…remember that Corvette I trailed along side for a good twenty minutes? Steve: Y-yeah? Jackson Rust: Well, the driver just so happened to have the biggest rack I’ve laid eyes upon…although I must admit; eyes weren’t what I wanted to lay on them. Rust smirks, as he pats down his Jackson Rust t-shirt; the only t-shirt that will impress the nearly dead – in stores now. Jackson Rust: Excellent cheap plug, man. Narrator: Thanks. Al doesn’t bother criticizing, continuing. Al: That’s a bit shallow. Jackson Rust: And I suppose filming Steve as he takes a leak, and then selling it in the parking lot to make quick cash isn’t? Al tugs on his collar, blushing, as Steve glares at him, possibly contemplating invasion of privacy charges. Steve: Y-y-you w-w-wh-what?! Al: I’d rather not talk about it; it was when I was desperate, and living with my parents. The posse suddenly hooks a left down another hallway, almost simultaneously; seems a bit scripted, eh? Jackson Rust: Oh great job ya jackass! Now they might think the promo is written on cue cards, and scripted! Jackson quickly signals for the unseen cards to be tossed, as the infamous glove slaps the taste out of his mouth again. Narrator: Why did you have to go and say that, huh? The glove slaps Jackson again, leaving a hand mark across his cheek. Narrator: Continue, Kimberly. Kimberly: Okay, will do. Don’t you still live with your parents, though? Again, Al tugs at his collar. Al: But who says I’m desperate? Jackson Rust: Well, considering that you couldn’t get a girl, even in a properly lit room, and enough alcohol, I say pretty desperate. Al: I have feelings, too, y’know. The quartet, well, by quartet I mean everyone but Al, chuckles under their breath, as they stop at a door. The door’s label reads “ER”. Jackson steps to the front of the pack, and turns about, facing them. Jackson Rust: ER, eh? My grandpa’s initials are ER…maybe this is his room? Kimberly: I don’t think— Narrator: Let him go, Kimberly. Making a jackass out of himself was bound to happen sometime today. Kimberly: Oh, alright. Jackson stops her talking, swiftly opening the door, and stepping in. Without Jackson seeing, the beginning of an operation is taking place, as ER really stands for emergency room. One of the doctors’ has a scalpel in hand, and is about to make an incision. Jackson Rust: GRANDPA! I’m here! The abrupt scream causes the doctor to impale the patient’s chest with the scalpel, blood spewing out, emulating a large scream from the female, and the queasy nurses. Narrator: What an imbecile. Doctor: Damn it! Jackson walks the rest of the way in, the entourage following closely behind. Jackson Rust: Is my grandpa in here, masked dude? Narrator: He’s called a surgeon, Jack. The doctor turns to Jackson, leaving the other nurses and other high tech mumbo jumbo named medical staff attending to the new wound. Doctor: What the hell are you doing in here? Jackson points to the door behind him, as the doctor races over to confront Rust. Jackson Rust: ER…my grandfather’s initials…I thought he might be in here. But… Jackson peers over the doctor’s shoulder, looking in, and at the blood spewing into the air. Jackson Rust: I can see he isn’t. But what’s with the “ER” thing, anyway? The doctor sighs. Doctor: It means “Emergency Room”, you imbecile! Narrator: You tell him. Jackson Rust: Shut up, man. The doctor looks at him quizzically, but shrugs it off, and continues with his spiel. Doctor: We just have inadequate funding to pay from actual full-length names for the doors. I mean, look down the hallway! Bathrooms are labeled as “The Pits” for God’s sake! The doctor takes a deep breath. Doctor: I’m the only fucking minimum waged doctor in the state! Jackson Rust: Whoa, dude…take it easy. I was just looking for my grandfather; I don’t need your life story added to my list of worries. Jackson reaches into his pants pocket, and emerges with a notepad, labeled “LIST OF WORRIES” in bold letters…rather similar to the HTML I just inserted to make it bold, eh? The notepad itself seems empty. What a jackass Jackson is. Jackson Rust: Shut the hell up, dude! Doctor: Whom are you talking to? Jackson Rust: My lousy narrator! Narrator: Lousy?! I have the power to get Kannon to make you job! Al: He has a point there, Jacko. Jackson Rust: Shut up, dude. Narrator: Don’t tell him to shut up! The glove again appears, and goes for the slap, but Jackson ducks, causing it to hit the doctor in the mouth! Doctor: Ouch! Jackson stands up, and smirks, but the glove comes back off of the rebound, and whacks him in the back of the head. Jackson Rust: …die. Narrator: No…you. Doctor: STOP YOUR GOD DAMNED FUCKING BICKERING! Narrator: Do you kiss your mother with that mouth? Doctor: One more word from you and I’ll castrate you with a bloody, rusty fishing knife! Narrator: Eeep. The doctor, obviously frustrated, looks Jackson up and down, as if trying to reach something within his mind. Doctor: Wait a minute! You’re Jackson Lauriet, aren’t you? Jackson nods, acting all proud of himself. Jackson Rust: The one and only. I prefer Jackson Rust, though. Doctor: The same Jackson LAURIET that enjoyed egging houses, and putting racing stripes on neighbour’s cars? Jackson Rust: Hell yeah. The doctor immediately pushes Jackson against the wall, and brings the scalpel to his neck. Doctor: YOU DESTROYED MY LIFE~! Jackson Rust: Huh? Whoa, watch it with that sharp thingy, man. That could take an eye out. Doctor: I’m going to kill you! All of a sudden, the infamous glove strikes again, this time loaded with lead, striking the back of the doctor’s head, sending him to the ground as limp as can be. Narrator: No one threatens my manly manhood! Jackson Rust: I can’t believe you just said that, man. That just killed ratings. Narrator: Yeah, but what can ya do, right? Jackson Rust: I suppose. Anyway, I need to offer my services real quick. Rust, as corny as it seems, walks into the emergency room, and goes right to the bleeding body. He looks to the medical staff around the body. Jackson Rust: Stitch him up, and if he pulls through – and then asks, tell him it’s been there is whole life. Rust winks, before turning around and leaving, as the doctor’s ponder about taking Rust’s advice, which would be a real bad idea. Al: Yeah it would. Steve: Y-y-yep. Kimberly: Definitely. He doesn’t have all of six brain cells. Narrator: Shhhh! Here he comes. Jackson walks back to his posse, and begins to converse. Jackson Rust: Okay…time to find grandpa, and get my inheritance that I will earn, or forge his signature for. Hey, narrator, do you know where his room is. Narrator: Down the hall, take a right, then a left, and the third door on the right side of the hallway is his. Al: How do you know? Narrator: Because I’m a booming, heavenly voice of reason, and truth. I think I know what I’m talking about. Steve: Y-y-yeah! Al: I thought you were just a narra— Narrator: Shu’p. Al: A narra— Narrator: SHUT UP ALREADY! Jackson Rust: Yeah…ass. Kimberly: Come on…come on; let’s go find your grandfather… Jackson Rust: Good idea. Narrator: That’s why she’s here, because God knows you couldn’t create a thought in your fragile mind. Jackson’s eyes get a bit misty, as he turns his head. Jackson Rust: Come on, man…I have feelings, too. They all shrug, along with moi, the narrator, as they go down the long hallways, attempting to remember the directions given to them by the narrator. They pass nurses pushing carts with medical equipment, and miscellaneous items through the hallways, until they finally reach the destined room. Jackson knocks, and opens the door, not waiting for a response. Jackson Rust: Dude, it smells like death in here! Kimberly: Don’t say that! Kimberly slaps Jackson in the shoulder. Across the room, in a bed lies an elderly man, decked out in a fancy looking robe. Jackson Rust: Gramps! What’s up with that robe? Did Michael Jackson’s chimp have a garage sale?! The elderly man tries to push himself up, to look at the intruders. Al and Steve rush over to help him up, after Steve puts the radio on the floor, but the man that is Jackson’s grandfather slaps them in the heads. Gramps: I don’t need help from two lack-wits! Al: Ouch, man. Very ouch. Gramps: C’m’here Jacko. Narrator: Better listen to the old man, Jack. Gramps: Don’t call me ‘old man’, you fucktard. Narrator: Sorry. Jackson walks to the bed, and sits down, looking at his grandfather. Jackson Rust: Heya, man. Gramps: What the hell is the radio for, Jacko? Jackson turns around to see the radio, and then finds himself holding the CD he originally brought in the building. Jackson Rust: Oh, that radio. I found this awesome song that I think would express my emotions toward you. Gramps: Oh…well, plug it in this here socket. The old man leans over, and pulls a cord from the wall. Kimberly: I think that’s your life support, Mr. Jackson’s Grandfather, sir. The old man looks at the cord, then the socket, and then the machine that is conveniently hooked up behind him. Gramps: Is that what that’s for? Oh well, I can last long enough to here a song. Jackson shrugs, and grabs the radio, plugging the cord into the wall, and swiftly taking the CD from it’s case, and placing it into the CD player of the stereo, all the while being a constant professional in this extremely long run on sentence. Jackson Rust: Okies…and…play! “Oh why won’t you die, Grandpa, why must you fight? You old motherfucker, just walk toward the God damned light!” Jackson abruptly presses the ‘stop’ button, and looks to his grandfather. Jackson Rust: I am so sorry… Gramps: No worries. Jackson Rust: I forgot to change it to the next track. This one never works. Jackson skips ahead one track on the stereo, and proceeds to turn it up a notch, as “When Grandfather Dies” by Stephen Lynch starts up Download it now! “When grandfather dies…life will be strange. When grandfather dies, my whole world will change. When grandfather dies, I’ll scream and I’ll yell; ‘cause I’ll be fucking rich as hell!” Jackson starts to nod his head to the awesome acoustic melody as the rest of the posse slap their foreheads in shock. “So grandfather die! Don’t keep me in suspense! So grandfather cough – up that inheritance! So grandfather don’t – hold on another day! I love you to death; but I got bills to pay!” Before the song can go on any further, Al dives forward, and stops the track, as Jackson’s grandfather looks at him in shock. Gramps: You bastard! You ain’t getting jack shit! Jackson Rust: But I deserve it! I spent the most time with you out of any relative! Gramps: How the hell do ya figure that, you goofy, half educated jackass! Jackson Rust: You have to use my system to understand this. One minute spent with you, or thinking about you is one year caught up, alright? So…the time it took writing this promo was two hours, and it was spent thinking about you. The time I thought about you through my whole life is approximately forty-five minutes, so add that together. Kimberly: That’s one hundred and sixty five years. Gramps: I’m only eighty-two, though. Jackson Rust: That means that’s an extra eighty-three years with you. So in my book, I have spent the most time with you, and I at least deserve three-fourths of your fortune of, what is it? Six million? Seven million? Gramps: Yeah… Jackson Rust: I take cash or check. Jackson’s grandfather’s expression goes from one of confusion, to anger, as he sits up and starts yelling! Gramps: OUT! OUT OF HERE YOU SCAVENGER! Narrator: But Jackson spent time thinking…actually THINKING about this. Gramps: One more word out of you and I’ll stab you in the face with a soldering iron! Narrator: There’s a lot of misdirected hostility, here. Gramps: Die~! The elderly man reaches under his bed, and indeed pulls out a soldering iron, standing up to impale any and all, as Jackson quickly takes his stereo, and runs out the door after his entourage, as the scene finally closes!
