Meeting with the family, preparations for Wednesday night...

17 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: March 17, 2003 {{{{Scene}}}}The scene opens up onto a sunny day in Atlantic City, New Jersey. A long black limo pulls up to an office building and stops in front of it. The street is scarce of activity other then the limo driving up, so the street must be known for some criminal activity. Semore Smoke steps out of the limo with several of his family members along with him. He enters the door of the business building and the camera scene changes to the inside of the building as well. It’s a narrow corridor with an elevator down at the end of it. Smoke walks down the hallway with his family members beside him. He enters the elevator and turns around allowing the other two men to enter with him. He hits a button on the elevator control panel and the elevator doors close, instead of going up though, the elevator goes down it goes down for a few seconds and then stops at an underground stop. The elevator doors open and you see a huge room filled with several men dressed in expensive suits. They’re seated around a table and near the head of the table three seats are empty, two at each side of the table, and one at the head of the table. Smoke sits down at the head of the table and the other two men sit opposite each other at the same end as Smoke. Smoke looks around at the quiet room and then speaks up… Semore Smoke: So… what’s the news today? {{{{The man in the far right corner of the table, stands up and speaks to Smoke…}}}} Man: Well, we’ve conducted more business around the area. My group has also done what you wanted us to do. By branching out the Cocaine industry from the Atlantic City to state wide. Also, we collected a grand total of twenty million dollars from our collection spots. Pretty good haul for the week. {{{{Smoke nods his head in understanding as the man sits down…}}}} Semore Smoke: Sounds good actually it sounds great to be honest, way up from last weeks take in. Did you take care of business down at the local 48? {{{{The other man on the other corner across from the first man stands up to answer the question…}}}} Man #2: Yes sir. All it took was a little bit of a beating and some personal persuasion with a picture of his family. {{{{The second man then sits down again and Smoke is nodding again in understanding…}}}} Semore Smoke: I see. Well, as long as you got the money, I don’t care what force you take, as long as you see it necessary. So… is everything set up between you two on how smooth everything will run tomorrow when I leave for San Jose, California to wrestle again? {{{{Both men nod their heads towards Smoke as to say yes…}}}} Semore Smoke: Good. I’ll take whatever spheres of influence I can get in PCW and turn that into yet another company of my own in the future you can bet that PCW will become mine. You did set up the meeting with the bounty hunter for Thursday correct? {{{{This time Smoke is speaking to the henchmen who came in the room with him. Both nod and the one on Smoke’s right speaks up…}}}} Man #3: The hunter has agreed to bring you the man you want alive for the price of five hundred thousand. {{{{Smoke smiles a devilish smile…}}}} Semore Smoke: Excellent. When I get rid of AJ Trinidad this Wednesday night I want to know at the same time, I could be getting rid of the man who ordered the murder of my father. Has the prisoner of ours begin talking on the situation yet? {{{{The other man speaks up for this question, on Smoke’s right…}}}} Man #4: Yes he has. But he won’t get past the single name of James, even though there is no boss here in this town or even in New York that is named James. So it’s either someone from a family out of Chicago who feels threatened on this family’s ambition to outreach from New Jersey State to the nation. {{{{Smoke stands up after he stops his report and then asks for him to stand as well…}}}} Semore Smoke: Well you show me where we’re holding him, I’ll like to take the liberty of asking him a few questions myself, personally. {{{{The man stands and nods his head…}}}} Man #4: Yes sir. {{{{The two walk out of the room to a side door of the room that leads to another corridor. As soon as Smoke walks out of the room, the room loosens up and everyone begins to chat a bit about the meeting. The scene changes to the inside of the corridor as Smoke is led all the way down the hallway to the last door on the left. The man opens the door and Smoke enters the room to see another man tied to a chair and a blindfold on his eyes. He’s battered and bruised and is bloodied from head to toe. He’s missing two or three fingers, and beside the chair is a set of several surgery type instruments. Smoke walks up to the man and removes the blindfold, the man stirs in his seat and looks up at Smoke. He then looks away, but Smoke makes him look back at him again…}}}} Semore Smoke: Look bastard, the least you could do is look at me! Now, I’m not going to be so nice as these guys have been to you. First things first, who killed my pop, or who ordered it!? {{{{The man hocks up mucus and then spits it in the eye of Smoke… Smoke lets go of the man’s jaw and wipes the sit from his eyes and face. He then looks at the man as if to say “I know you just didn’t.” and he rears back and slaps him across the face. Smoke looks down at the instruments and then back at his henchmen standing behind him at the door. Smoke puts the blindfold back on the man in the chair and then turns around to see his henchmen. Smoke looks at him with a serious look on his face…}}}} Semore Smoke: If he doesn’t talk by the time I get back on Thursday, then kill him. {{{{The man nods his head as Smoke exits the door…}}}} Semore Smoke: Now if you excuse me, I’ve got to get ready to get back into the ring after six months, a lot of work needs to be done…
