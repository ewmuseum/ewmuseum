Facing the Phantom, and facing a legend.

4 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Title: Facing the Phantom, and facing a legend. Featuring: Politically Incorrect Date: June 4 Phantom. A phantom is a spirit, a ghost. It is also used to describe something that seemingly exists, but does not. Despite not being haunted, PCW has a Phantom. A Phantom Booker. Like a Phantom....The Phantom Booker has returned to PCW, quietly, but then making a definite impact. Like a spirit misplacing objects in a room, he has turned Primetime Championship Wrestling on its ear with its injection of new blood, also known as the Phantom Phive. But, it is the manager and leader of the Phantom Phive, in the Phantom Booker, that will step into the squared circle on Friday to defend his group. A man who has competed in rather few PCW contests. Needless to say, The Master Debator is chomping at the bit to step into the ring with TPB. Similarly, Reginald Lovecraft engages in business on Fusion. Alexander Miyagawa has what is rightfully his: The PCW Extreme Title. Again, Lovecraft does not hate the soon to be father. He just must do what must be done. For, Fusion will be like most things are for Politically Incorrect. Strictly business. Staying in a luxury suite at a hotel in Philadelphia, Politically Incorrect are on their way to the Mellon Arena for Friday. The room has accomodations for seven, as William S. Powell, Reginald V. Lovecraft, Roger Schultz, Katherine Willis, and Louis Brown are sitting around. There are two queen sized beds in the room, and three regular ones. Which, of course, begs the question...:: Roger Schultz: Why are there accomodations for seven people in this room? ::William S. Powell smiles...about to reveal his plans. Are they important plans? To him, yes. To PCW, absolutely not.:: William S. Powell: Well, Reginald and Katherine will occupy one queen sized bed, Roger, you and Louis will have the single beds, along with Mr. John Crichton in the third. Roger Schultz: Crichton's coming to stay with us? William S. Powell: Yes, we're doing a 'Meet Your Fellow Stablemates' type deal soon....to get acquainted with each other. Reginald V. Lovecraft: Yeah, from what I know Mr. Crichton and Miss Jaden have already gotten 'acquainted'. ::Chuckles all around the room at Lovecraft's cheap remark.:: William S. Powell: We'll have to ask Crichton about that.. anyway, the other queen bed will be accompanied by myself and my new intern. Louis Brown: You are getting a new intern? William S. Powell: I'm holding the 'William S. Powell Intern Search' tommorow. I'm sick of going to sleep alone. I WILL HAVE AN INTERN CAPABLE OF HANDLING MY PERSONAL AFFAIRS, IN BED AND OTHERWISE, BY THIS TIME TOMMOROW! ::Everyone in the room claps.:: Reginald V. Lovecraft: Good for you! No more banging your own gavel! William S. Powell: BASTARD! *sniff* I've only had to do that thrice a day the past few months! ::More laughter from everyone at Powell's expense.:: William S. Powell: Regardless, I believe that it is time to not focus on getting laid. I know, I know, it's a radical idea for me, but I have a tough contest coming up. Three Primetime Championship Wrestling legends will have the honor of facing William S. Powell on Fusion. For the first time, I face The Phantom Booker down....in any sense. The plans to leave The Ultimate Authority were in the cards even before the Phantom Phive came into town...so, I had no problem with Booker then. ::Powell's trademark cocky grin comes across his face, as his finger raises, making a straight point out ahead of him.:: William S. Powell: It does not matter that I have no personal problem with Booker. I now have a proffessional issue with him. He wants to make the Phantom Phive the most dominant group in wrestling...I wish to make The Political Machine the same. Bottom line....Booker is going down. STAT. ::Lovecraft smiles, and goes into his suit coat pocket. Out of it, he pulls a small 3' x 5' press photo of Alexander Miyagawa, holding the PCW Extreme Championship.:: Reginald V. Lovecraft: Congratulations, Miyagawa. You finally captured that Extreme title you have longed after so long. It is thus rather unfortunate that your time with it shall be exactly one week. On Friday Fusion, we go at it once more, man to man, throat to throat, for the title of Extreme....with no one else. No Political Machine, no Ultimate Authority, no Phantom Phive, no Malice Crew, no Media, no Old School...hell, for the first time in probably a year, PCW is going to have a match with a clean ending! ::He laughs, a laugh devoid of genuine mirth. Lovecraft's ire is evident...although more directed at others, not Miyagawa.:: Reginald V. Lovecraft: Think about that Miyagawa. Only you, I, and a referee in the ring to help us settle things. I have been wrestling now approximately three months...you are competing in something like your one hundred and seventh contest. How embarassing it will be for you when you lose to a man in his first one on one contest! I may not have any wrestling expertise, but I can brawl, I can use weapons, I know some high impact offense...and no amount of high flying or goggle wearing on your part will prevent you from going down with one of my southpaw punches. William S. Powell: You MUST win, Reginald! Our first piece of gold in The Political Machine! My only question is, why were we not slated for Turmoil in the tag tournament? Roger Schultz: Dark Messiahs quit...so there are seven teams. Awfully convienient way for Nelson to phase you out of the tag hunt... Reginald V. Lovecraft: NO MATTER! If Tyler Nelson wishes to play partisan politics with us, that is fine! For, he can keep Politically Incorrect out of the tournament, but there is NOTHING he can do when I step into the Mellon Arena with Alexander Miyagawa this Friday! Miyagawa's legend in PCW will come to the end at the hands of myself...it is as the stars work. A star grows big, and bright, and then it explodes, destroyed from within...and a new star is formed from the gases. From the bloated legend of Miyagawa shall emerge Reginald V. Lovecraft, PCW Extreme Champion! I'd love to see Jack Valentine or Totally Tubular Tom try to match THIS feat! William S. Powell: Indeed...Politically Incorrect are more than a mere tag team! We are the greatest two man unit in Primetime Championship Wrestling! And, we will prove it on Fusion as The Political Machine wins....IN A LANDSLIDE!! ::Fade to:: Paid for by the Friends of The Political Machine!Vote Lovecraft for Extreme Champion! ---- View Politically Incorrect's Biography
