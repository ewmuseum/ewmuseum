Religion: A Game Of Chess

1 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4 - 1 - 03 “Why do people turn to religion? What sparks the seemingly intelligent masses to go beyond what science can prove and put their faith in a being that is only RUMORED to exist? They’ve never seen the entity, nor have they felt his presence. Some will have claimed to, but then again, you will probably find marijuana stuffed in their underpants. Is it merely a state of mind? Or is it the desperate plea for help by the human race for guidance through their terrible, death-stricken lives? The answer: None of the above. Religion is actually more a game, a game of chess to be specific. Humans operate under the ‘guidance’ of an upper being, sometimes multiple beings, believing them to be their leaders, fathers, mothers, or rulers. If they say jump, humans say how high. If they say jump off the cliff to the rocks below, the humans say right away. It works like this: King – God(s)Queen – Goddess(s)Knight – High Disciple(s)Rook – ClergyBishop – ClergyPawns – The rest of the human race A proverbial hierarchy of servitude. The lesser beings ALL answer to the King and Queen, giving them infinite praise and ultimately dying for them. But why? Why would anyone just go and die for something they’re not even sure exists? It’s the paranoia of human mentality. The entire world is against them. They have nothing else to rely on, so they turn to a ‘supreme being’. They can’t rely on each other, that would just lead to chaos and backstabbing. Humans are fickle, nasty creatures that will be the end of each other. They think through servitude to a god or being that really doesn’t exist, they’ll be eternally be protected from the evils of this world. Foolish nonsense. Humans are scared. They’re stupid. If I told them that, they’d be like “I can believe whatever I want! What business is it of yours?” Well obviously, if you push your shit on me, it becomes my business. The fucking fanatics that kill themselves and others over this so-called ‘religion’ deal make it my business. When thousands of people die just because someone doesn’t believe what the other does, it becomes my business. Am I going to die too? Just because I don’t think God really exists? Possibly, and if most humans had their way, I’d be hanged for blasphemy. But who are THEY to judge me? What gives them the power to take my life? To take ANYONE’S? They claim to do it in the name of their God… but why? Did their deity tell them to do it? In their eyes: definitely. Take Bin Laden. Fanatical bastard that kills in the name of Allah. Has he met this so-called Allah? Talked to him? Had fucking coffee with me? Discussed the finer points of the Mona Lisa? NO! Religion is a fucking sad excuse for humans to fulfill their bloodlust. They can’t do it without horrible repercussions, so they claim to do it in the name of the deity. In some countries, namely America, they won’t get prosecuted as badly because that would be religious discrimination. Fucking bullshit, I tell ya. But no, THEY don’t think so. My dad died at the North Tower on September 11th. My mother was a doctor in Libya; she got killed in the fanatical bombing of a Catholic Church by fascist fucks that refuse to accept Christianity in their area. Does peace come with religion? Of course not. Look at the fucking Inquisitions, the Crusades, the entire conflict in the Middle East, and the Protestant Reformation. Millions upon millions of people have died for believing something different other than what their fellow man believes. Like I said, religion is just an excuse for bloodlust. The King ‘commands’ that a piece on the opposite side is taken. Bishops and Knights move forward to secure the pawns. Pawns blindly move forward into battle, against another set of pawns doing the same thing. Pawns kill each other, Bishops and Knights kill each other, Rooks kill each other… All that’s left are the Kings and Queens. What do you have my friends? Stalemate. All that shit for NOTHING. A draw. No victory. Just a big fat fucking nothing that will probably remain that way forever. That’s all religion is about. A stalemate. If Humans took control, made their own decisions and didn’t depend on a fucking deity that doesn’t exist to make excuses and decisions for them, we’d be better off. Killers would be in jail and secured away, despite claims that God told them to kill someone. People wouldn’t waste their lives dressed in silly clothing acting like rejecting the outside world will bring them protection. Things would be a lot better in general. The strategy would change. The pawns would make their own minds. The Bishops and Knights would make up THEIR own minds. What would you have then my friends, if no religion was around? Checkmate.” - Dorn Alexander Tracer II
