The Clone Wars Part 2: The Emeril Strikes Back

3 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6/3/02 ***Note: This Lucas-like saga was filmed in the Australian desert over one month ago. The Chef is perfectly fine and is eligible for all upcoming PCW matches, just in case his handler gets a case of the “lazy rping” syndrome so rampant in ewrestling today and decides not to include the rest of the saga before Turmoil… The camera fades in… We see a still image of the Chef’s office, with Bertha pointing her finger at the Chef. Narrator: Welcome back to another rump-tingling Chef promo! When we last left the Chef he had gone to answer the door and greet a visitor. Unfortunately, this narrator believes he was ambushed! Hence the “thump” and “clink” sounds we heard earlier. Who is behind this chicanery? We’ll find out…NOW!!! The still image begins to move, showing a scene from the previous promo. Bertha: F*cko! You got a f*cking visitor at the ho @$$ f*cking door, b*tch skank f*ck! The Chef: Thank you B, it’s probably some upper class entertainer or media mogul who’s welcoming me to the neighborhood… Bertha: I don’t f*cking think so, f*ck face… The Chef: Then who is it my plump princess? Bertha: Who do I f*cking look like f*cko? A c*ck @$$ f*cking secretary!?!? The Chef: Actually you’re physical stature is probably most comparable to the field of computer programmer or bus driver… Bertha: Yeah! So I f*cking ain’t so secre-f*cking-tary b*otch!!! The Chef: Touché, I guess… The Chef gets up and walks out of the office… The camera does not follow the Chef as it is stuck on a tripod in the room. All we here is a strong sounding whack from outside. CLINK!!! THUMP!!! “F*cking Sweet!” “Thank you my dear, now I will finally get my revenge on the Chef, BAM!!!” Narrator: And now, on to the new sh*t!!! The camera fades out and then fades in… The Chef wakes up in a dark room, possibly a storage room beneath the surface floor of some gigantic building. Or in Emeril’s basement…whatever… The Chef: Ugh…Ack…Engh…Huh…Argh…Gugh…Meh…Ha…Hey! Where am I!?!? The Chef realizes that he has been bound and hog-tied on the floor. Through the darkness of the room he can vaguely sees rows of wine bottles, aging hard cheese, salted and smoked meat. The Chef: Sniff…Sniff… Ah the sweet smell of jerky… The Chef tries to roll over and loosen the tightly wound rope, but he is unsuccessful. The Chef: Prostrating Pete! This is hopeless… Suddenly a small sliver of light appears off in the distance. A creaking sound is heard. The Chef: Someone is opening a door… SLAM!!! The light completely shines in the face of the Chef, distorting the image of being coming forward towards the Culinary Conquistador… The Chef: (Covering his eyes) Umm, Bertha? Manuel? Yakov? “SIIIILLLLLEEENNNNNCCCEEEE!!!” The power of the shout causes a wind to whiz right through the Chef, all huddled on the floor. The Chef: Who’s there? “Hah, you’d think a righteous soul like you would recognize your maker when you met him!” The Chef: Allah?!?! “Duh…” The Chef: Allah! Free me now! I must find out who did this to me, and them smite them in your name! “I cannot free you…” The Chef: You won’t!?!? How about if I praise you nonstop for the next five years? “No…no…I’ve had enough of the praising” The Chef: Then what are you here for, oh gracious and powerful Allah? “I have come to give you some advice…” The Chef: Well could you step away from the light so I can see you? “NOOOOOOOOOO!!!” The Chef: Eep! Sorry…. “If a mortal like you saw my true form your head would explode because your futile brain could not process the true nature of Allah!” The Chef: Wow, you’re quite special! “As the great prophet Funaki once said…”Indeed!!!”” The Chef: Well, how about some advice? “Ah yes, I have come to help you on your mission?” The Chef: What mission? “Well first of all, you need to free yourself from those ropes…” The Chef: OK… “And second, you need to get revenge on that vile foul mouthed whore that put you here!!!” The Chef: Vile? Foul Mouthed? Horrible Odor? Whore? That’s Bertha!!! “I never said “Horrible Odor”…” The Chef: Bertha put me here!?!? And it wasn’t for our S&M night!?!? “My me! That truly is disgusting…” The Chef: Why did she imprison me here in this jerky filled heck hole? “As supreme being of the universe, I see a bunch of sh*t…and after you where beat over the head with a metal frying pan, the very same Bertha kissed that rat fink Emeril on the cheek!” The Chef: Emeril!?!? That culinary school graduate! I will sauté his @$$ permanently…Engh…as soon as I free myself!!! “Well, now that your quest is revealed to you, I must offer you some advice…” The Chef: Yes Allah, fill me with some of that oh so good divine wisdom! The same you shared to Mohammed! “Umm, shake it off, and hang in there….Tootles!!!” In a flash of light, the being is gone…total darkness covers the room. The Chef: Great, for a wise supreme being you suck at motivational speaking!!! A lightning bolt suddenly travels down through the heavens, crashes through the ceiling of the room and zaps the Chef right on the ass! The Chef: YEEEEEEOOOOOOWWWWWZZZAAAA!!! The Chef jumps up and runs around the basement, trying to waft cool air onto his bottom. The Chef: You’re not very nice Allah! Suddenly a voice crashes down from the sky. “You idiot! I just freed you! Now get your booty in gear and get some revenge, Militant Islamic Style!!!” The Chef looks and notices that the extreme temperatures of the lightning bolt cut the rope that once confined him. The Chef salutes the sky. The Chef: Will do, Allah!!! The Chef runs up the stairs of the room, opens the door and travels up another flight of stairs. He opens another door… “Hello f*cko!” POW!!! Big B socks a surprised Chef right in the kisser!!! He tumbles down the steps all the way back down to the basement and lands there with a sickening thud… “F*cking sweet as f*ck!” As the whale-like woman retrieves the Chef from the ground, a familiar villain in the Chef promo series casually walks down the stairs to meet his foe. Emeril: Well well well Mr. Chef, it seems we’ve met again, and once again I’ve stolen your woman! BAM!!! Bertha holds the Chef back up. The Culinary Conquistador, bruised and barely conscious, replies… The Chef: Kiss my grits you piece of rancid pork! SMACK!!! Bertha wallops the Chef in the back of the head with a thunderous slap! Emeril: I think you’d better shut that yap of yours or my new LOVER is going to make a pulled meat sandwich out of you…BAM!!! The Chef: MMMMM…pulled pork…does it come with a poppy seed bun? SMACK!!! Emeril: Take him to the kitchen…OF DEATH!!! BAM!!! Bertha drags the Chef away as Emeril laughs maniacally… Narrator: What will become of our Culinary Conquistador!?!? Will he become a pulled meat sandwich!?!? We’ll find out…same Chef time…same Chef channel!!! NANANANANANANANANANANANANANANANA…THE CHEF!!! The image becomes still and the camera fades to black…
