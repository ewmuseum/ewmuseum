Video Game Junkie

6 January 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: January 6, 2002 [Dudes are competitive. 'Totally Tubular' Tommy is TOTALLY competitive. When it comes right down to it he must be the best at anything and everything that he comes across. Take for instance, Playstation 2. Our Tubular hero has played many games on this system and has mastered them all, but a challenge set by the ever so bogus Rent A Hero set something in motion that has truly created a heinous monster. A Playstation 2 junkie ever since the events on Friday Fusion. Playing his video games like the bogus, mindless zombie that the video game industry wants people to be. Instead of hitting the weights and going for his morning jog, Tommy was playing Grand Theft Auto: Vice City. Instead of studying his opponents, Rent-A-Hero and Nezzera, he toasted Final Fantasy 10. Harry then let him know that Final Fantasy isn't a two player game. Tommy smashed the coffee table, cried about it, and then started playing Kelly Slater's Pro Surfer. That's when The Tubular One realized that his mug should be on this video game and not the heinous Slater. Talk about bogus. That's when Tommy kicked the milk crate, which was taking the place of the coffee table, and he broke the new stereo system. He would have cried, but his tears were extremely tapped out from the bogus coffee table incident. The weekend has plagued him into a madness which he cannot seem to control, all because of Rent-A-Hero and his bogus game] ['Heavy Metal' Harry browses the bargain bin at the Los Angeles area Best Buy. The place is packed with people trying to still get those most excellent after Christmas deals. Harry, equipped with gift card, hopes to buy some far out rare Twisted Sister merchandise. People ask themselves, 'Rare Twisted Sister merchandise at Best Buy? IN THE BARGAIN BIN?!' Well, that's what hair metal has come to...the bargain bin. An entire era of music stuck into an overflowing bin, just hoping some fan searching for some gnarly nostalgia will purchase them. That's where Harry comes in. The far out compadre who can't shake the 1980's. He can't shake the style, the smile, or the bad ass music. To him, it's the greatest ever made and nothing can come close to it. No matter how many far out bands try they will never top Twisted Sister, Quiet Riot, Bon Jovi, Def Leppard, and Ratt. As his pale, pudgy hands flip through the CD's like a child searching for that rare He-Man toy. His blood shot, brown eyes move like lightning to catch any glimpse of Twisted Sister, anything that even resembles their band logo. He's beginning to think that his gift card will go unused, that Twisted Sister will never be found. He will go home empty handed and let down, and what a bogus feeling THAT can be. That's when his eyes beam like the lights on a Christmas tree. That's when his stoned face contorts into pure shock that he could, without a doubt, wet his tight leather pants. Twisted Sister: Live in Morocco. FAR 'FREAKIN'' OUT, MAN! He grabs it up and hugs it for a few moments, letting the plastic casing dig into his heart. The other customers walk past with looks of fright and weirdness. They just don't understand the AWESOME magnitude of a find like this. You don't find stuff like this anywhere else. Only in the bargain bin] 'Heavy Metal' Harry: I FOUND IT, MAN!!! IT'S COMPLETE!! [His deep, stoned voice carries through the store like a deadly virus. The other customers and the employees stare as if Harry has giant aliens sticking out of his head. He holds the Twisted Sister CD in the air as if HE just won the PCW Continental Title. He pushes his way through the cell phones, then the vacuum cleaners, and finally into the video games section. This is where we find 'Totally Tubular' Tommy with his eyes glued upward to the television screen. His tanned, manicured hands pushing the controller buttons with such force that it seems he could break the controller at any moment. Harry does a little dance around Tommy flashing the CD out to his brother, but Tommy doesn't notice. In fact, the only thing he notices are the football players on the screen. As Tommy's quarterback drops back and passes, and gets intercepted] 'Totally Tubular' Tommy: BOGUS!! CHEATER, CHEATER, CHEATER!!! [Tommy mashes the buttons, thinking that will somehow bring the ball back to his possession, but it won't. Tommy's gray PCW T-shirt is stained with sweat, as if he had been working out, but he hasn't. He's only been sitting around, eating nachos, and playing video games. He knows he's the best at the PS2, X-Box, and Gamecube. He's the best at everything that he does. Why is his name 'Totally Tubular' Tommy? Because he's the best. Not a tough question, dudes. Tommy has bags underneath his eyes, which shows he needs his daily face cream, but he'll forget to put it on due to his game playing. His tanned skin is flaking up due to the cooler weather they are facing. The Los Angeles winter is taking it's toll on Tommy, pissing him off even further as he tackles the opposing teams running back. He laughs wildly as the player does not get back up, he is injured. Tommy cackles as if he's some kind of bogus demon, taking a soul for himself. Harry is frightened, but not sad. He has his rare CD and is beaming because of it] Harry: Maybe you should be working out, man. TTT: No way, dude. Harry: You've got an important tag team match coming up, man. TTT: It's not THAT important... Harry: Yeah it is, man. TTT: NO! Now leave me be before something bogus happens. [Harry stands there for a few moments staring off into space. Tommy is really bothered by this whole video game thing, man. It was just a game, that's all. Rent-A-Hero can't get it done where it counts, in the ring. That's all that really matters in this whole situation, not about stupid little games. Harry notices Tommy's obsessed blue eyes radiating that horrible aura that only the bogus can radiate] Harry: I got Twisted Sister: Live In Morocco, man. [Tommy is obsessed with his game] Harry: We can play it while your workout, man. [Tommy can only tackle the bogus Ravens] Harry: We can go get far out chicks at the strip club down the street, man. My treat. [Tommy can only see destroying Rent A Bogus Super Hero Dude in EVERY Playstation 2 game] Harry: Naked boobs, man. [Tommy is deaf to reality] Harry: Beach Boys, man. [Tommy begins a new offensive drive, his strategy must be key] Harry: Kelly Slater is here, man. [Running the ball must be done, for this Ravens secondary has been on his receivers all day] Harry: The Swedish Bikini Team is here, man. And they're in heat. [First down with a brutal run by Jerome Bettis. The giant halfback can come in handy for something, and that's going to be winning Tommy this football game] Man: Excuse me? [Tommy turns slightly to see a balding middle age fat man standing behind him, his little boy at his side. It's obvious that they want to play the game. Tommy then concentrates on his screen play. Good blocking. Offensive line looks bodacious, and Jerome nails another first down. Tommy is pleased, but not ready to celebrate just yet] Man: If you wouldn't mind, me and my son would like to play a quarter against each other. TTT: Buy the game, dude. Man: Well, we don't want to buy it without seeing how the game play is. TTT: Game? Do you think that this is a game, dude? Your bogus. Man: Well...it is. TTT: It is not-- Harry: It is, man. TTT: Shut up. Harry: Ok, man. [Tommy drives a few more downs and is able to, yet again, score another first down. The Steelers are driving like true warriors now, nothing can stop them now] Man: Please, sir, my son REALLY wants to play. Just one quarter and you can have it back. TTT: No. Man: Well, we'll see what an employee has to say about this... [As the man goes to turn he bumps Tommy, making his sweaty hands slip on the controller, and then Tommy Maddox tosses a pass into the hands of a Ravens defender] TTT: NOOOOOOOOO!!! Harry: That sucks, man. Lets go. TTT: YOU! [Tommy points his finger at the man with his child. His blue eyes have pure hate in them and it's safe to say that this man is in serious trouble] TTT: You cost me the game, dude!! YOU BOGUS SON OF A BITCH!! Harry: That's mean, man. TTT: It's mean to cost me the game! Harry: See, you just said it's a game, man. TTT: It's not! Harry: But you just said it was, man. TTT: IT'S A SERIOUS GAME!! Harry: But it's still a game, man. TTT: SHUT UP!! I'm going to TOTALLY mess this compadre up now! [The man looks frightened beyond belief. Stupid or not, Tommy is a large and intimidating force. Plus he does have some skill in the ring, and there is no denying that he could rip the poor man limb from limb] Man: Look, just let my son go and you can do whatever the hell you want, sir. TTT: I plan on it, compadre. [Tommy rips the Playstation controller out of the outlet it was plugged into and begins spinning above his head like some sort of gnarly lasso] TTT: It's round up time, dude! [Tommy slings the controller cord, but misses. The plastic end smashes an approaching security guard right in the face. His nose begins spilling out blood as Tommy looks left and right for a place to run] Harry: Your screwed now, man! [Fade to black]
