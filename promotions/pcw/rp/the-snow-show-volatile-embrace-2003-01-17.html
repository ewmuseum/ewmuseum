THE SNOW SHOW: Volatile Embrace

17 January 2003

Date: January 17, 2003 ﻿************************************The Snow ShowSeason I, Episode 10“Volatile Embrace”************************************ Location: Hattoni Mansion - New York CityDate: January 6, 2003Time: 9:30 pm While deep in thought, Justin Hattoni, the young Don of the Hattoni family, set his jaw andhardened his brown eyes. Quietly, almost silently, he tapped an index finger on the massive oakdesk in front of him. This room, giant and eloquent, while dark and mysterious, was designed byhis father, the infamous Doug Hattoni. Even though the old Don had only been dead for a fewmonths, Justin had occupied the room, and the family, since the Don’s incarceration nearly adecade ago. They all called him the wonder child. Now, one would be hard pressed to know that Justin was in his early thirties. He looked like aman at least ten years older, and on the right day, one might even be persuaded to believe theyoung Don was actually twice his age. At thirty three, Justin was already used to his grey hairand the many lines on his face. And he didn’t only look old - he felt old. “Look,” Petey said from across the room, smoking a cigarette by an open window, “I’m notsaying that the hit isn’t necessary - that’s not it at all. And of course, I’ll be happy with whateverdecision you make on this. But Justin - this kid isn’t ready for a job this fucking big. He’sbarely got his feet wet.” “Well,” Justin shrugged from his reclined chair, “I guess we’re going to see if he can swim.” “He’s going to fucking drown,” Pete said quietly. Justin sighed but his eyes remained hard. “I have faith in him,” Justin finally said. “We’re talking about the man who guarded my father’slife. He’s got ice in his veins, this one. And you know it, Petey!” Justin pointed at his trustedcompanion. “You know as well as anyone. How long did you spend with this kid on pettytasks?” Petey nodded in submission. “And how many times did he fuck up?” Justin asked. “How many times did he give anysign that he doesn’t have what it takes for this business?” “Justin,” Petey finally got back into the conversation, “I know what you’re saying. But all thesame, he’s been in for about two fucking months - maybe three. There are guys who’ve bustedtheir ass in this family for a decade who wouldn’t want anything to do with this job. We’re nottalking about whacking some citizen here, Justin. It would be bad e-fucking-nough if we weregoing to whack a made guy. But Justin, you’re talking about whacking a boss! A Don!” Justin’s eyes narrowed in thought. Petey was right, and there was no getting around it. Not onlywas John Mossilano the Don of the Mossilano family, but the Mossilano family was one of themost powerful underworld families in the United States, only a notch or two below the Hattonifamily, which was in the very top bracket. Mossilano had constantly disrespected Justin,however, and now it seemed necessary to remove him from the picture - especially after Justinreceived a subtle push in that direction from a high ranking member of the Mossilano family. “I know all of this,” Justin said finally. “Why the hell do you think I’m going to give the job tothe kid, anyway? If there was someone else who I trusted, and who was willing,and who...” his voice trailed off, but Petey knew what was coming next. As high as their hopes were for Jason Snow, at this point, he was expendable. “Alright, alright,” Petey sighed. “But even if you do have to send this kid in, what makesyou think he’s going to want the job more than anyone else? He ain’t like these other youngthugs we’ve got on the payroll, Justin. He’s got a wife. He’s got a kid.” Justin took a deep breath and turned his head to his smoking companion. “I plan to make the reward worth the risk,” the young Don said. “Should Jason Snow completethis task, he’ll be Made - on the spot.” “Jesus Christ!” exclaimed Petey. “You’re going to make this kid a Made guy!? He’s twenty-four fucking years, old, Justin! This isn’t going to sit! We’ve got guys who’ve worked for thisfamily for ten, twenty, some even twenty-five fucking years, who ain’t been Made yet!” “And I wouldn’t trust any of them with this job,” the young Don said. Petey took a deep breathand accepted his boss’ logic. “It’s not just the time thing though, chief,” Petey sighed as he tossed his cigarette out thewindow and approached the desk. “It’s a blood thing. If you hadn’t noticed, Justin, the kid ain’texactly a pure bread.” Justin nodded slowly. “I’ve considered it,” he answered. “It’s never been done before,” Petey said quietly. “You’re talking about bringing Irish into anItalian family. Maybe it wouldn’t be so fucking bad if he didn’t have bright yellow hair and Goddamn eyes that Hitler would have been proud of.” Justin snapped his eyes toward his companion. He knew that Petey was right, but the young Donremained firm in his position. “If there are complaints,” he began, “or if there are whispers of complaints, send them to me. Ifanyone in this family or any other family have the balls to speak up, send them to me! This ismy family, God damn it, and I’ll do whatever the hell I want with it. If that meansbreaking tradition, then all of the Sicily-preachers had better bite their motherfucking tonguesand hop on for the ride, because in this fucking family, my word is final!” *** Location: Marty Verrelli’s Apartment - New York City Date: January 6, 2003Time: 9:40 pm “What’s wrong?” Marty quickly asked when he opened the door of his apartment and foundKrista McVicker, the young sister-in-law of Jason Snow, standing in the hallway. She wascrying, or had been crying. He couldn’t tell. “He’s a fucking asshole!” she said loudly as he barged passed him and into the living room ofthe apartment. “They both are! Can’t they see that I’m a grown woman!? That I can make myown decisions!?” “Slow down!” Marty said as he followed her in. He took the jacket from her shoulders anddraped it over the back of his green sofa. She whirled around to stare at him. “Whathappened?” Over the next fifteen minutes, she complained at length about her sister and brother-in-law. Shecomplained about the way they controlled her, and had controlled her throughout her teen years. She cried hard and soon found herself wrapped in Marty’s strong Italian arms, with her faceburied in his chest. He wasn’t sure what to think when she told him that Snow had stronglysuggested that she not see him anymore. “Don’t worry about it,” Marty said to her. “I can handle Snow.” Krista looked at him as if he had one of the lowest IQ’s she had ever known. “You can handle him?” she snickered. “You barely know him! You have no idea howprotective he can get! If it wasn’t for Raven, he’d be here now trying to break your legs... orwhatever he does.” Marty showed no intimidation, even though the pit of his stomach felt more hollow than amoment before. “Don’t worry,” he soothed her. “Your brother-in-law’s a big nasty guy, but I’m telling you, I’veseen guys like him before. If you want to see me, Krista, he can’t stop us.” She closed her eyes and fell backward into him, wishing what he was saying was true. She knewit wasn’t. She knew that Snow could burst into the room at any second, destroy it, leave Martylying bloody on the floor, and then drag her back home like a dominant slaving ogre. “But now I’ve got to ask you, Krista,” he whispered in through her long brown hair and into herear, “do you want to see me?” With a little hesitation, she turned and kissed him. It was a gentle kiss at first, but before long, itbecame hard and passionate. Somewhere in the kiss, she forgot everything. She lost herself, and allowed his will to take over. *** Location: Snow Residence - New York SuburbsDate: January 6, 2003Time: 11:25 pm Snow paced the kitchen glaring across the room at his little wife, who met his hard gaze. Hisjacket was already on, and so were his sneakers. He couldn’t explain what it was in her gazethat kept him from marching out the door and heading over to Marty Verrelli’s apartment. Heheld his keys in his right hand. “If you go over there,” Raven said firmly, “you’re only going to push her further away.” “I can pull her back,” Snow said, “by force if I have to. Either way, she’s not going to keepseeing that little bastard!” Raven hated Snow’s slips into fowl language. Just like his father, she thought. “Why don’t we just go to bed,” she said, finally softening. “You’re tired - I can see it. Andyou’ve got a match coming up.” “I’ve always got matches coming up,” he rolled his eyes at her. He sighed: “Raven, we can’t letKrista see this guy. He’s not good for her.” “Whether you like it or not, Jason,” sighed his wife, “she’s going to have to be the one to makethat decision.”
