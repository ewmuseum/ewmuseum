The Death Saga: It Begins...

24 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2-24-02 His eyes opened as he was smacked in the face by a burst of cold water. Noone was around him but Harle as he looked into the sky, this was hell, noone was gonna be concerned. She looked down at him smacking him in the cheek a little. Harle Quinn: Gavyn? Ahre 'ou awake? Surprise to see moi, No? He shook the cobwebs from his brain. Noting the fact it felt like he had spiders in his head, and sat up, this was the third time in as short as he was dead that he fell unconcious. This wasnt going well. He rested his arms on his knees and had next to nothing to say. DarkStar: What the hell are you doing in... um.. .hell? She smiled. That coy, jokerish smile he knew well. Harle Quinn: Well... when oi doied, oi came 'ere, it makes poifect sense. He nodded in agreence. DarkStar: It's nice to see you again, I guess. He made a mental note of her new appearence, she was still the harlequin insane jester, he could tell, but the cape was kind of odd, it made her seem like a superhero. What the hell, does she think its halloween, freak... Wait... shes normally a Harlequin, the capes the only addition, and what the... DarkStar: Your looking good. Harle: Merci, as do 'ou, 'ow is death treating 'ou? He shrugged. DarkStar: It's going to take a little to get used to. She nodded, but seemed distant, DarkStar finally stood and she put her hand on his shoulder. Harle: 'ou may not 'ave to... Lucifer wants to see 'ou. DarkStar: For what? Um.. Those people died under natural causes! She furrowed her brow. Harle: What? DarkStar: ... Nothing. She shrugged. Putting her small hands on her hips. Harle: Come on, Oi need to tell 'ou something. He walked next to her, making sure that they didnt get within a stride away from eachother. He cracked his neck with both hands. Harle: 'ou see, Lucifer es ready to see 'ou now, and there ez more to theeze then 'ou think. He shook his head. DarkStar: What is it? Im already in hell, does he want to put me in a even lower version of hell... like Queens or something. Harle: No... Et iz about something else... someting ovarly imporant. Just follow me. They continued walking. Further into the hellish world (that really isnt much different then the world of normal). They regailed eachother with stories, far into the time of the late day (for it never became darker around here). The buildings began to thin out, time taking a slower pace as they reached the outskirts of hell. DarkStar didnt even realize that the buildings turned into trees, and the trees grew thicker into this dark forest, he looked around at the beauty, at the splendor, that something this amazing could come out of hell warped his small mind. Some of the trees seem like they have been growing for a entire eternity (and they probably have been). Harle finally stops, looking out into the distance, DarkStar is too busy talking to turn around, but her gaze leads him on, he turns slowly to the left. Harle: Your Destiny 'as been written long before toime existed, before 'ou existed as a Dream King and before oi existed az 'our loveur. 'ou can look out into the distance and see ze Palace of Le Morningstar, and az anybody with common knowledge knows, 'our destiny runs with 'im... His eyes went wide as the black and red caverns sprawl outward for miles and miles, the long, winding, almost translucent road goes over the entire length. For the first time since he has been down here, he noticed the moon fall, the light was illuminating Harle's face, she smiled, looking towards the winding road. She pointed up. Harle: Welcome to Spirit Road... Your Journey begins 'ere. His gaze continued, the view was breathtaking in its endlessness... He smiled.
