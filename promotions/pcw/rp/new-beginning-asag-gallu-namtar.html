New Beginning: Asag, Gallu, Namtar

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: not entered Turmoil, Tuesday 26th February 2002, a day that will be forever etched into the mind of El Diablo. For that day, a jealous and callus Flaymin Aymin attacked him in the ring, leaving him grounded, knocked out and bloody after his win. That day was a cold day for El Diablo and a day that he will never forget as long as he may live. Since that headshot with the fire extinguisher, El Diablo has seemed a different man; he seems a lot quieter and a lot less like the El Diablo of old. No one close to him has any idea what has happened to him or how he has changed and that's what is worrying the close people around him. El Diablo is different, but how different could this Mexican Devil be? A chair, El Diablo is seated in. A black leather padded chair, it looks particularly comfortable and El Diablo looks satisfied being seated in it. The mask he wears is getting a sharp reflection from the lighting in the room, but El Diablo seems too involved in the book that he is reading to even notice. The book title seems unclear, until the word 'Baby' is seen. It is a possibility that Angel could be pregnant and it seems a viable reason as to why El Diablo attitude has changed as of late. But, what a disturbing thought of what child might spawn from the freak known as El Diablo and his gothic wife Angel. The book is raised a little and there is more than just the word 'Baby' there, the word is now extended and the rooms lighting hits the silver font of the book perfectly to see this whole word 'Babylonia' Babylonia? What possible reason could El Diablo have to be reading up on Babylonia? A confusing sight to see, until El Diablo places this huge book down on the brown coffee table right next to him. His wife of only a few months walks into the room, wearing only a white bathrobe and her dyed black hair glistens in the light like that of a spiders web after a rainy night, and funny how both are so similar and sinister to one another. Wet footsteps are left on the pale blue carpet as Angel walks further into the room and sits down on the opposite leather couch to El Diablo, she crosses her legs as El Diablo just stares into a blank space, not even acknowledging that Angel is in the room, that is of course if he even noticed in the first place. Angel coughs quietly at first, but when that failed to grab El Diablo's attention she coughs louder than a troop of elephants, which finally alerts El Diablo from his oblivious state. 'Is something wrong?' queries El Diablo with little sincerity. Angel looks across at El Diablo with her stern eyes. 'Nothing is wrong with me,' replies Angel with a little anger in her voice. El Diablo is too caught up in himself even to notice Angel's anger. 'I will never forget Turmoil,' states El Diablo, moving on from the last topic of conversation. The Mexican Devil seems quite inconsiderate moving on from what Angel was trying to bring up earlier, but what occurred on Turmoil could be taken into account for his strange behaviour. 'Obviously, you got floored by a walking afro,' Angel reminds El Diablo in a very raw way. El Diablo simply nods as Angel crosses her legs over again. 'I won't forget it,' says the calm and thorough Mexican, 'because while I was out and bleeding I came upon a realisation,' he comments with a look of joy in his eyes, 'and that realisation is ancient Babylonia,' he continues with great pride in his voice. Angel sighs loudly so that El Diablo can easily hear her. 'And how will this help you?' asks Angel with obvious doubts of El Diablo's realisation. El Diablo turns and looks across at the book and places his hand over it with great pride. 'This will be my bible,' states Diablo in a sinister fashion, 'while I was unconscious these three demons hailed me, and now I must follow my beliefs,' he states while removing his hand from his so called 'bible' Angel gives a cold look before she speaks again. 'And they were called?' queries Angel while feigning an interest. A large smile comes across El Diablo's face, a smile as wide as a rainbow, and just as joyful a sight. 'Asag, Gallu and Namtar,' Diablo speaks coherently of them, 'they are the greatest of all gods, myths and demons. They summoned me and now I know how I will live my life,' he speaks with a look of enthusiasm in his eyes. The wet foot marks on the carpet that Angel left are now slowly drying and fading from site just as Angel stands up once more and begins bending down to touch her toes in one of her routine exercises. 'And,' says Angel before bending down again, 'how,' again she bends down, 'is that?' she finishes while stretching her arms. A look of amazement comes over El Diablo, like expected Angel to understand how he should live his life according to his new beliefs. 'That's easy,' he states with a look of power roaring in his eyes, 'the seven clay tablets,' El Diablo says followed by a smile. 'The seven tablets?' she curiously asks as she sits down from her exercises. He stares again at her in a look of surprise. But it must be his visions for Babylonia that make all of this seem so crystal clear for him that it blocks out that Angel will not know, most like the rest of the world won't know. 'They were what was written in those times and I will find inner divinity while living this way,' answers El Diablo with an astounding passion. Angel nods to show her husband that she is listening, but not understanding. Although, Diablo being so wrapped up in his own world he fails to notice her uninterest. 'In a way, I should thank Aymin for this realisation, and on Fusion I will,' he states with an evil smile, 'Shadows of abyss, heathens of my soul. This is a new beginning for me, and you,' Diablo comments with a look of sheer evil on his face. Angel gulps like she was swallowing a whole melon and El Diablo picks up his book and reads on with a passion never seen in him before.
