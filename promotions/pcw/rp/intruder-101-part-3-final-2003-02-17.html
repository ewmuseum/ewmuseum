Intruder 101 (part 3 final)

17 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2-17-03 Two times Brianna has managed to elude her intruders. But with each little trick she displays, the more red they see, meaning less chance of Brianna making it out in one piece. After testing the several phones throughout her house, she has come to the sad conclusion that the phone lines have been cut, and the odds of her calling for help are slim. If she plans on making it out, and seeing the light of day again, she has to rely on her wits and wrestling abilities. Brianna can make a mistake in the ring and recover, but one mistake in this situation can ultimately cost her her life. “Lady you do realize that if you would just give yourself up and hand over the mouse, we can ensure your safety. There is no need to be scared of us. We’re sensitive guys and would never lay a finger on such a magnificent example of beauty such as yourself.” the bigger guy exclaims trying to get Brianna to come out with his sweet talk. “Uh…we already tied her up and attacked her. Do you really think she is dumb enough to believe what you just said Tony?” “Crap!” “And they called me the dumb one.” “I believe you both have an equivalent intelligence.” Brianna jumps out in front of them and pulls out a thick rope from behind her back. She throws it out and manages to lasso the smaller guy whose efforts of fighting result in the tightening of the rope. “Ah let me go!” the guy pleads as he falls to the carpeting with the ropes cutting off the circulation around his midsection and arms. “Nice move honey, but you forgot about me. And I’m not trying to brag, but I think I am a much bigger problem.” Tony exclaims as he runs and spears Brianna to the ground. Being much heavier, he manages to pin Brianna once again and feels pretty confident in himself. “And you forgot about one thing Bozo!” Brianna oddly yells out seemingly beaten. “What would that be?” he replies curiously. “Grimer!” As soon as that name is said a small white airplane comes zooming in the room with Grimer at the helm. He has his little white paws on the controls and drives the small craft over Tony and drops a small pellet shaped object onto his head. “Oh yeah that was something alright.” Tony chuckles as he goes to swat the object off of his head. But as he does Brianna gives a smirk as she pulls a controller from her pocket and presses a small white button. As she does this the object on Tony’s head spans out and latches on like an octopus clinging for dear life. As strong as he is, Tony can’t manage to pull the object off of his head which causes him to go into hysterics not sure what it will do. “Get it off! Get it off me!” he screams repeatedly. “We’ll just wait for the cops. Until then, you are just going to be hanging around. Grimer parks the airplane and Brianna throws a metal disk in the air which clings to the ceiling. The camera shows Tony and he starts to float off of the ground and towards the metal disk on the ceiling. He flails around wildly but is stuck midair with no where to go. “Sure enough you can understand the magic of magnets? You actually believed that you could come in my house and try to take my new little buddy? Use this experience and tell whoever else is involved with you, that in order to get to Grimer you are going to have to go through me. And if I can manage to beat up guys three times bigger than me on a daily basis, and deal with PMS once a month, I think I can handle big goobers like you.” “Believe me you haven’t heard the last of us lady.” Tony yells out trying to keep his tough guy image up. “Well in that case learn my name! It’s Brianna Washington, and I can promise I can bribe a few guys at work, who are much bigger mind you, to take care of you for me. So your pathetic threats don’t bother me in the least bit.” As the time goes by the police show up and take the two men into custody. Again peace and quiet it restored in Brianna’s house, and her mind and thoughts are shifted to her match up on Turmoil against Brandi. As Brianna is preparing for bed, a note slips under her front door which she quickly scoops up. I understand you got into a bit of a problem, but you handled it nicely. It just makes me all that much happier that you are watching over Grimer. I couldn’t have asked for a better person and I promise once things are settled, I will pay you back with however much you want. And as for why these guys are after me and Grimer, I still can’t say. Until another time… Kramer After reading that Brianna slides it into her pants pocket before the scene slowly fades to black.
