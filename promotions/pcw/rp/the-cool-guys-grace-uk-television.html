The Cool Guys grace UK Television?!

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: not entered Following their very close victory over ‘Misplaced’ last Friday Night, the three members of top Boy Band, ‘The Pretty Cool Guys’, have yet to be able to do anything worthwhile with regards to their unification. Despite finding themselves split between two companies within the expanding arm of the ‘Wrestling Tattler’ community, Sherwin, Moore and Stimpo, have still been able to get together to discuss various ideas for songs… and to determine the next Target on their newly establish “PoP-list” – Something that basically details every band which needs to be beaten down into pulps. However, with all of that in mind… the plan for both Sherwin, Moore and Stimpo… is to look forward to the Tag Team contest that they’ll be appearing in on Pay-Per-View. Unfortunately, Stimpo will not be one of the men fighting for the Tag Team gold… but he should however, be one of the people that is allowed to stand around the immediate ringside area… maybe even sway the contest into the favor of his newly found band-mates. We open up this interesting scene… upon the set that is more commonly used for MTV’s very own ‘Hitlist UK’ show. Everything seems to start as normal, running through the opening credits before being given the standard shot of both Cat Deeley and Edith Bowman. The two women sit down on their Super-White sofa and look into the camera, waiting for the all clear before eventually saying something to get the show on the road. “Hello… and welcome to yet another amazing edition of “Hitlist UK”… as you should all know by now, I’m Cat…”, she says, flashing an amazingly large smile at the camera while her co-presenter follows through with her own name, just to give the introduction a sense of having both individual personalities present. Edith looks around the screen for a few seconds before staring it dead in the middle and going over what the viewers at home are going to be able to expect during the course of todays amazingly cool show. “Well everyone, today we’re going to have something of a rather different start to the show… you see, I’ve just been told, that the first segment is going to be dedicated to a band who have made something of a mark across the Atlantic in the U-S-of-A… but have yet to really do something in the United Kingdom… the trio of Rich Sherwin, Ryan Moore and Brendon Stimson will be making their debut over here… coming onto the show to talk about their careers… and their plans in the PoP Industry.”, Edith informed everyone, looking rather out-of-breath for having to cover so much information in such a short period of time. Cat looked at her with a worried kind of stare, just trying to see if her ‘friend’ was able to continue the show or not. “Yes… these three men are called the ‘Pretty Cool Guys’… and unlike the majority of people in their shoes, the time is passed for these… by working as wrestlers for Americas very own, “Primetime Championship Wrestling” organisation.”, Cat said, finishing off what was probably going to be spoken by Edith. Bowman looks to Deeley and nods as a sign of thanks, just to get the subtle hint that shes grateful for what she just did. Cat seems ready to continue the show when the remix of “Bye, Bye, Bye” by the ‘Pretty Cool Guys’ starts to hit on a couple of mystery speakers in the studio. “Whats happening?!”, Cat screams, unable to understand what in the hell is supposed to be going on at the moment in the show. Strangely enough, it doesn’t even look like anyone was ready for the arrival of PCG… well, actually, looking at everything right now… I think its just normal for nobody to be expecting PCG to appear on Television screens of any sort. As the music continues to play over this pair of speakers, all three members of the exciting ‘Boy Band’ appear from what seems to be nowhere, jumping onto the scene… and generally making fools out of themselves in the process. Sherwin attempts to leap onto the sofa… but overshoots it and falls on the floor, Ryan and Stimpo taking their respective seats next to Cat and Edith, placing their arms around the ladies and winking to the cameras. Their music soon follows into the depths of nothingness, just as Sherwin is able to spring up from the floor… brushing himself off and making it look as though nothing had actually happened. “What are you doing here? The show script doesn’t even document your appearance on the show being this early. I mean… no offence to you guys or anything… but the fact that you have yet to make it over here, really doesn’t give you permission to do anything like you just did.”, Cat informs them, sounding stern with every single word that found its way out of her mouth. Edith nods her head in agreement, preferring to stand back and allow her co-presenter to deal with things… instead of bothering to get her own hands dirty in something that would really only take one person to sort out. Sherwin, Moore and Stimpo looked rather shocked at the comments that they had just heard… and their faces almost went into a state of ‘tears’… but alas, they just decided to continue with their antics, hoping this kind of behaviour would make them… ‘rebel’ like, and friendly with all their previous haters. “Well Cat… the whole reason why PCG have decided to come along… and crash your show, is because we want to have, something that a lot of these artists have had… but groups like myself, have been unable to gain. You see cutie, Rich Sherwin and the ‘Pretty Cool Guys’ have been in existence for something in the region of five… six, hell, even seven months now… and we’ve got Cult status with young teenage girls across the world… form America, to Argentina, to Western Africa… to the United Kingdom.”, Rich informed Cat, looking rather intelligence in doing so if it isn’t said so myself. He winks to her upon finishing his line, knowing that he’s finally been able to score a point against the unforgiving hand of fate. Ryan and Stimpo begin to laugh at the Presenter, stopping every few seconds to see if it was time for the amazingly cool interview to take place. Seeing her ‘friend’ appear to be in a little bit of discomfort, Edith decides that it would be better to conduct such an interview now… instead of having these amateur artists dance and parade their way around the show for another twenty five minutes or so. “You know what guys? How about you all sit down somewhere close by… and we have this interview that you’re apparently supposed to be having on the show? It looks as though its been written in ‘Black Marker’ on the boards… and in a hurry too, but hey… I’m not about to throw caution to the wind and allow my job to be placed into question.”, Edith said in something of a nervous tone. She smiled to the camera, making the last few moments of dialogue seem to be nothing more than a light hearted joke… when everyone watching knew that this was as far from a joke as humanly possible. Still smiling, she looked at all three band members and started the little segment, praying that this little thing wouldn’t kill the ratings of the usually popular show. “So… it seems as though you’re not just your average Boy-Band. Apparently, you also take part in a variety of wrestling shows, working for ‘Primetime Championship Wrestling’. Exactly what made you choose Wrestling as your primary job?”, she asked, sounding genuinely interested in the reason as to why three guys chose a hard hitting job like Wrestling, over the money filled, musical job that is being a full-time musician/artist. “Well, for the case of myself…” Sherwin began, getting ready to start talking about his last several years in the business, “… I’ve been around in Wrestling for quite a while longer than both Stimpo and Ryan here. You see… my career started on a warm Summer Night, something in the region of several years ago now. I broke into this business with a guy who was my best friend.” Sherwin attempted to continue but was cut off by an uprising Edith Bowman hand, one that shot right into the air and cut out hero off from saying anything else. Deciding that this was nor the time… or the place to be cut off in his story, Sherwin pushed her hand away and continued with, “… yeah, this guy who was my best friend, we broke into the business together… I worked various different shows for various companies for something in the region of six years before coming to the ‘PTC Community’ and getting work there. Something which seemed to happen rather quickly…” “Yes… its all good and well to say that you wondered around these different companies, but I cut you off a moment ago because I felt that fans should be rushing to place their pre-orders for your autobiography, entitled, “The Sherwin Era”… but no, you wanted to go on… and tell these people everything there is to know about your career… but hey, don’t let me stop you… I’m just trying to prevent you from losing any kind of money.”, Edith informed Rich, sounding very harsh and very unfriendly with her selected tone. Annoyed at his treatment, Sherwin turns from Bowman and looks to the back of the room, refusing to look into the camera again for the rest of the interview. Edith looked to both Ryan and Stimpo, both of whom were pretty much looking away from the camera, attempting to put across the fact that they were not interested in answering that question… not if they were going to get cut off two seconds into it. She turned to Ryan and decided to try and put forth another question… something that wouldn’t exactly kill anyone or anything. “Hmm… well, Ryan, why don’t you tell everyone about your debut single for the UK Charts? I’m sure its something a lot of these “Young Girls” will be wanting to know about.” Moore’s face lit up like a light, he looked into the camera and smiled his sheepish smile before relaying all the relevant information. “Well…”, he began, sounding a little worried about his tone and picture, “… Rich, Stimpo and myself are looking to release our debut UK Single, “Pray, It Goes OK?” sometime near the end of the year. Its pretty much a cover of one of the songs that was found with the ‘Grand Theft Auto III’ video game, playing on the LiPs 106 station. Well anyway, I’m pretty sure the release date will be November 21st… which also happens to be the birthday of Rich Sherwin!” Moore shouted out the last few pieces of information, getting everyone watching to know and understand that the birthday of Sherwin is in November… and that their debut UK single will also be released on the same day. Edith started to look a little shocked at the way that Moore had pushed out the details of Sherwin’s Birthday and the release of their song. She looked into the camera again… and smiled briefly, trying to get through everything that had already happened on the show. Stimpo yawned, trying to establish himself within the show and answer a question. Bowman looked to Stimpo with a look of concern, obviously being unable to read the actual plan that went with such a yawn. Instead of asking the newest member of PCG a question, she turned back to Sherwin, who was still sitting with his back facing the camera. “Rich… I’ve just been informed that this weekend, on PCW PPV, you have something of a huge Tag Team Championship match… with ‘The Heartbreakers’, ‘Cowabunga Connection’… and a very odd pairing in both Duzza and Mark Makonie. What do you have to say about that?”, Edith asked, trying to sound normal… and trying to get an answer out of our main hero. Sherwin turned around from his position and started to give Edith… ‘The Demon Eye’ glare, before making an obscene hand-gesture and turning back to face the wall. Seeing this as the perfect opportunity to say something, Stimpo jumps into action, taking over about two-thirds of the screen! “Well… you see, this Championship match on Pay-Per-View… its going to be everything that all of our fans around the World have been waiting for. Its going to be the match… or matches! Its going to be the night… that Rich Sherwin and Ryan Moore, are allowed to leave a PCW Show… with the Tag Team Championships hung over their shoulders! Heartbreakers, they’ve been crushed by the ‘Pretty Cool Guys’… Cowabunga Connection, they know what happens when you mess… with the “Pretty Cool Guys”… and lastly, Buzza and Macaroni…”, screams Stimpo before Edith is able to cut him off with another uprising of her common hand. Stimpo looks irate… although this is merely emotion that is being carried with his uncontested rant for his boys PPV contest this Sunday Night. “Erm… Stimpo?… you just went out and called your teams opponents for the night… Buzza and Macaroni. I think their names were Duzza and Makonie. Just thought I’d go outta my way and correct you before you make a big… goof of yourself or something.”, she says, laughing at the same time… obviously trying to add some humor to the dying show. Stimpo doesn’t look too impressed and gives her a poor impression of a raised eyebrow. He gets up from his seat and walks over to Sherwin, lifting him up from the floor and turning him to face the camera, within moments, he walks to Moore and does the same thing… all three members of PCG standing tall and mighty within the screenshot. “What are we doing on our feet?”, Sherwin asked, wondering why the hell he even bothered to come on such a show. Moore shrugged and walked off the set, Sherwin following him away from things. Stimpo remained on set, looking into the camera for a few moments… realizing that he and his band members had just come to screw everything up. Moments later.. he walks away from the set, allowing the camera to look down to both Edith and Cat, both of whom… are crying about the fact that this could be… the very end of their show… FOREVER!!! Will PCG spell the end of “Hitlist UK”? … or will it merely just allow the MTV Executives to think about who should replace these two women? Better yet… whats the real story behind PCG’s thoughts on their match this Sunday Night?! All of this information will be revealed at a later day… or even in the next Promo… of the Pretty Cool Guys!
