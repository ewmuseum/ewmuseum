Introducing Grimer The Mouse

2 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6/2/02 A timid white baby mouse, nestled in the corner, tries to hide itself from the eager hand reaching in to pull him away from home. Three fingers grasp him from the waste and pull him up. He kicks around the bedding but stops, figuring as small as he is, all attempts to fight would be useless. Voice: Oh, he's so cute I'll take him. I wonder why nobody wanted him before. The camera pans up and focuses on the face of Kramer Wild Wing, who is wearing a huge smile. Kramer pulls the baby mouse up to eye level and gives him a good long stare. The mouse, being as scared as he is, urinates all over Kramer's yellow baggy shirt. Voice: That's the reason no one wants the little bugger. He has a slight bladder problem. Kramer peers over to the four eyed old man, with his Australian accent, and white lab coat. Then he looks back over to the little mouse who looks helpless. Lab Guy: Listen mate I can understand if you don't want the little guy. Kramer: No...I'll take him. Lab Guy: Well alright then. Little Grimer is yours. Kramer: That's his name? Lab Guy: To make a long story short, we were watching Ghostbusters when he was brought here, and since he eats a lot we named him Grimer. Kramer: Interesting. Kramer places Grimer back into the cage and puts the top down. He then lifts it up and follows the lab guy out of the squeaky clean laboratory. They make it to the front door and Kramer exits as the lab guy thanks him yet again. Kramer proceeds to his black truck. He places Grimer's cage on the top as he opens up the door. He picks the cage back up again, and places it in the front passenger seat, and straps the seatbelt across it to keep the cage from shifting around during the ride home. Kramer then gets into the car, and straps himself in. He shoves the key in and revs up the engine. The truck takes off. After about twenty minutes Kramer makes it home, to his two story dark blue house. He exits the truck with cage in hand, and walks up the steps and into his house. Kramer: Anyone here? He gets no response so he shuts the door and walks into his living room, setting the cage onto his sofa. He opens the cage and pulls out Grimer who looks a bit calmer now. Kramer: Hey little buddy, this is your new home. I assure that you will love it here. You can eat all the grub you want, and over there we can set up a tunnel for you to play around in. Either Grimer is excited or scared because yet again he urinates on Kramer's shirt. Kramer sets him down onto the couch, and Grimer looks up with bright blue eyes looking sorry. Kramer: Don't worry.....it matches the shirt. I'll be back in two crackles of a pop, I just have to change. And when I come back I will get you something to eat. Kramer turns and bolts up the stairs and into his room. Grimer on the other hand looks around checking his new surroundings. He walks to the edge of the couch to stare at the floor and loses his balance. He falls to the floor but luckily doesn't hurt himself. Grimer is startled as the front door bursts open and in walks Jason wearing a blue pinstriped shirt and black pants. Grimer slides under the couch scared to death of Jason. Jason on the other hand doesn't notice the mouse and walks over to the kitchen which is to the right of the living room. He grabs a can of Dr. Pepper from the refrigerator and walks over towards the couch. By this time Grimer is trying hard to pull himself back to his original spot on the couch, but with has luck. Jason sets the can of pop onto the coffee table in front of the couch and turns his head. Jason: AHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHH!!! Jason wails in a screechy voice as he jumps on top of the coffee table. Jason: A mouse!! It's a mouse!!! With all of his shrieking, Grimer is scared to death as well. Grimer finally manages to pull himself onto the couch and huddles into the corner shaking violently. Jason on the other hand has taken off the black belt from around his waist and is ready to slam Grimer with it. Down the steps with a new shirt on is Kramer. Kramer: Whats all of the commotion for? Kramer asks while flipping thorugh the tv guide. Jason: Die mouse die! Kramer looks up in a questioning glance and sees Jason with the belt whipped back ready to fly. Kramer then looks over towards Grimer is trying desperately to get into the couch to excape harm. Jason brings the belt forward, and Kramer charges after him knocking him off of the coffee table with a spear taking the hit from the belt. Jason pushes Kramer off of him and they both get up at the same time. Jason: What in the world is your problem? You speared me! Kramer: Well you hit me with that belt! Jason: Well I wouldn't have hit you if you wouldn't have prevented me from killing that rodent on your couch. Kramer: How dare you try to kill Grimer! Jason: Grimer? Kramer walks over to the scared mouse with Jason right behind. Kramer picks Grimer up and cradles him in his arms which seem to work in calming him down. Kramer: Jason this is Grimer, my new pet. Jason: Why on God's green earth would you want a mouse as a pet? Kramer: I was looking through the classifieds this morning and Warped Laboratory had posted a classified about a mouse who needed a home. I thought about it and decided that I could use a pet around the house so I went and picked up Grimer. Jason: Kramer how do you expect to keep him when your wrestling schedule has you everywhere but home? Kramer: Oh that's simple. I'm taking him with me. Jason looks at Kramer as though he has grown two heads. Kramer: Hey what brings you here anyway? I thought you were on vacation or something. Jason: I was, but it was cancelled due to the weather. I just came here to tell you that I got the phone call from the Roleplay God 3 officials, and unfortunately you didn't make the cut. Kramer: Now why doesn't that surprise me. Jason: Maybe next time kid. Kramer: Maybe. Kramer sets Grimer back onto the couch and instantly he is roaming around fearless. Kramer: The cool thing is that I get to spend the whole week with you little buddy. Jason: That's not a good thing Kramer. No work equals no money. Kramer: Stop trying to spoil the fun. Jason: No I'm serious. You could have had a chance for the extreme title had you not been a lazy ass and signed up. Kramer: I wasn't going to win so why should I bust my hump. Jason: I don't see why I even bother sometimes. Jason grabs the pop from the coffee table and starts gulping it down while Kramer is busy playing with his new pet. The scene slowly fades to black as Jason looks over to Kramer one more time and shakes his head in disapproval.
