Work Out

29 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Title: Work Out Featuring: Duke Williams Date: 10/29/02 To say El Diablo is a complex being is an understatement? Who writes this crap! El D is as simple as it gets. He’s a punk kid who sticks his nose where it doesn’t belong. You don’t need to be a Mexican devil to figure out who’s corrupt and who isn’t. I can tell by looking in ones eyes. That’s all it takes. No inquisition, no harassment, no paper trail. Just a glance. I guess that’s what elevates me to the level of God while simpletons like El Diablo investigate absolutely nothing. Dateline: October 28, 2002Time: Noon CST Place: Gold’s Gym Purpose: Work out -Duke is dressed in an all black gym suit dripping in sweat as he sits down and talks to his trainer Joe P.- Joe: Your not done yet are you old man? Dukestar: I’m here busting my ass while closeted homosexuals like El Diablo are reenacting old poker scripts from John Wayne movies. He El D I have a table for you to tip right here. -Dukestar grabs his Evil Goth Crotch.- Dukestar: Hoss you going to throw a little temper tantrum when I cheat to win our match? You going to act like a little El Dibabyo Huh? You fucking pussy. That’s why its great to be a God I don’t get red in the face cus I control my surroundings. Just like I will control the arena come Turmoil. Joe: But you know he is the lord of Mexico. Dukestar: That’s like owning a fisher price Ferrari. Who gives a shit. Mexico is the bowls of the USA. Why do you think their skin is brown?? That’s right nothing but the fecal matter of the Great US. I’m sorry he’s only a lord one day he can aspire to be a God like me! -Duke smiles as he points to himself.- Joe P: You know the way El D barks out orders he reminds me of a dictator like Hitler. Dukestar: The closet El D will ever get to being a leader is maybe a chairmen on the board of residents in his retirement village in fifty years. When ever he talks I think Badges…we don’t need know stinking badges. He’s comical in delivery. This guy is not the guy you want representing PCW as world champion. Hell if I owned a McDonalds I wouldn’t even want him representing my drive thru. I’d stick him in back on the fry machine. Joe: That’s harsh. Dukestar: This guy is the male version of Anna Nichole Smith. He has that clueless aura too him. He thinks the world revolves around him but in really he has zero baring on it. I’ve been trying to get it though his head for the last week. But no he lives in fantasy. Look boss da plane da plane! -Duke laughs in an EVIL GOTH GOD laugh.- Dukestar: What really irks me is the fact he has his focus set on Clyde. Hey dip shit you have to get though me first. What a fucking pansy. This guy is a disease to PCW and the world in general. Joe: Just don’t take his lightly D-man he’s beaten you before. Dukestar: Fuck him that was before I was a goth god!!! -Duke flexes his muscles which you can see the evilness in.- Joe: What about your buddy Tim Walden. -Duke lays down on the bench and puts up three hundred pounds three times.- Joe: Don’t kill yourself there. Dukestar: Fuck you I’ll add more later. Joe: So back to Timmy. Dukestar: If I understand what Tim is saying then he obviously doesn’t understand me. He wants us to work together as members of the front office to take out the other two pricks before going for the pin. I’m not to break up his pins and he’s not to break up mine. Hey Timmy I don’t know if I can make this any clearer. You work for me. -Duke points at his burnt body.- Dukestar: I’m the boss here Mr. Walden. I’m the president. If you want to keep your job you’ll help me win this match. Cost me and lose your job. Point blank hoss….point blank. Lets keep harmony in the Front Office. Your job is simple, help me win. Don’t do the job get fired. -Duke has a stern look in his mascara covered face.- Dukestar: You see kiddies the truth is I’m sick of waiting for my turn. I’m sick of waiting for a chance at the gold. So how do I end that drought? I take action. I water my crops till they rise out of the ground. I don’t care whom I have to beat, kill, or maim to get the gold. I will do it. Truth behold I’m an evil Goth God and I should just be handed the belt but I’m willing to go through the mortal process cus I’m that nice of a guy. Joe: A nice guy would add some more weight to that bar. Dukestar: What are you the weight police. Joe: I’m your trainer oh great Goth God. Dukestar: Sarcastic bastard. Joe: What about Snow. Dukestar: He talks about me beating him cus he has a lot on his mind. So what’s change? He’s still in the same exact place and mind frame as a week ago. He’s not a factor. I bet if you ask half of the young guys on PCW’s roster half of them would not know that Snow had a great legacy at one point in his career. Sad but true. Just an example of how hard he has fallen. Joe: Give me a set of 600. Dukestar: give me a chili Dog. -The two men bicker as the camera fades.- =w= ---- View Duke Williams's Biography
