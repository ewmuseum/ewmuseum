Psycho Prankster (Part 2)

6 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 06 December Prologue: Shady Acres Asylum for the Mentally Disabled was the scene for Pranks With Attitudes next masterminded caper. Missing the muscle of the team in Noscosto Ireul Nerezza, it had been left in the capable hands of the PCW Jester to come up trumps in one of the grimmest settings featuring the colourful comic. Masquerading as a doctor, Gaz Green entered the complex with one thing in mind 'CHAOS' a personal message to this week’s opponent, Mr Psycho... Skitzo. Long gone is the bond between Gaz 'The Jester' Green and Skitzo, a bond established for the PCW spectacle known as Alliances2K2 where the uncanny duo joined forces under the banner of Team Bastardos. From friends to foes. Now going into this contest blind, Gaz Green aided by Natalia Miyagawa wish to send out a personal message, a message that the tie is now severed and that Skitzo is in for a long night come Fusion. Anyway... Last time we saw the two-third collective of Pranks With Attitude they had entered into the heart of the Asylum. Standing before a corridor of howling inmates, all secluded behind Perspex plastic we were about to witness the self-proclaimed 'Prince Of Pranks' in action when suddenly a hand crashed down on the shoulder of our host Gaz Green who retaliated with a thunderous bellow that brought forth the resulting silence. 'WHAT THE FUCK-......' With the camera swinging violently we firstly see the assailant’s feet, followed by a slow upward movement that finally came to rest on the strangers face. A Professor Herbert Dorkiman look-alike stepped back, cowering from the aftermath of the Jester's initial rage, probably expecting to be clipped he subtly smiled, his first real welcoming gesture. 'Arr-... Err-... Who might you people be?' murmured the middle-aged man, twitching the side of his glasses and oozing with every known nerd quality, if you could deem such attributes a wanted quality. Taking another step back, shying away from the Conniving Collective of Natalia Miyagawa and her devious client Gaz Green, the Asylum worker again broke the silence, the silence of the conversation since the inmates had broken theirs minutes earlier with rage-fuelled screams and the exchanging of verbal bickering. 'Hello I'm Doctor Kim Howells and who might you be?' asked the 'NOW' known doctor Kim Howells, interrupted upon by the amused comic Gaz Green who takes a cheap pop at his name knowing full-well that sarcasm is the lowest form of wit. 'KIM? WHA-... YOUR PARENTS WERE HOPING FOR A GIRL, HUH?!?' 'WELL CLOSE ENOUGH KIMBERLY!!!' 'GGGGGGAAAAAAZZZZZZ~!' hissed the Russian beauty, slowly unravelling their false identities accidentally, to late however as the doctor quickly intervenes warranting an explanation. 'Gaz? Hold on-... The guard called down earlier saying that we had a Doctor Stewart Tony Davies doing some field work for another branch in the Mentally Disabled field?' Looking puzzled and the caper looking failed, a real first for the Velvet Prankster, Natalia Miyagawa showed her client exactly why she isn't just the sweet and innocent bystander but an integral part of the now established Pranks With Attitude (P.W.A). The Jester's frowns were soon turned upside down as Natalia became his very own guardian angel with an intervention that would make for front-page news... Providing it were true of course. 'Mr. Howells - I had better come clean. Mr. Davies and myself have been having an affair that was kinder low profile until thirty seconds ago and Gaz is my pet name for him. Relationships in the workplace are not so much forbidden but aren't encouraged neither so-....' The smile alone of the good doctor secured their stay as he firstly nodded his approval, maybe not towards the actions of the duo but the fact that this matter had now been resolved sati factionary and that he wouldn't pursue things further, his response surely authenticated his smile. 'I see, point taken-... So Mr. Davies I hear you have a few new techniques you wish to put forward, theory into practise so to speak!' 'That's right squire!' retorted the jovial Jester breathing a sigh of relief. With all pleasantries passing by and the fact that the charades secrecy is now cemented, Gaz grasped his briefcase firmly lifting it into the air and with a skip in his step the three-man party observed some of the celebrities on show. Like a freak show the inmates went about their above-normal business, watched by Gaz who merely smirked with a sinister grin, a grin that emitted the question, 'I know something you people don't!' Watching one peon puzzlingly playing with a metallic spring, Gaz saw his first victim. The inmate himself looked rather normal, a middle-aged gentleman with cropped hair clad in overalls and desperately trying to salvage his sanity as boredom toyed with him. With an out-stretched arm Gaz Green reached for the door, his progress stopped by his tour guide Kim Howells who looked amazed at both his bravery and stupidity. 'What are you doing? This prisoner was caught drenched in blood, bathing in the blood of his butchered family, you do know he's a convicted murderer, you are aware that by going into this enclosure it may provoke him!' informed the doctor acting on instinct, a plead ignored by the PCW Tag Team Champion. 'He's just another Skitzo!' 'Arr-... Err-... Skitzo is politically incorrect don't you think. We refer to them as Liabilities Of Society, Mentally Disabled-...' injected the doctor before a Gaz Green interruption, both a verbal interruption and physical as he entered the belly of the beast, staring into the face of a real-life serial killer. 'He's just another Skitzo-....' Suddenly the door swung ajar, Gaz took one step forward and his associates took one step back, not discouraged with events behind him, the Jester entered into the company of a killer. With the door slammed shut, it was a contest of another magnitude as the lady-killer faced a real life brutal killer where a loss could mean the loss of life. Unscathed by the uneasy feel of being in a room with evil incarnate, Gaz put his masterminded plan into action, beginning with some verbal insults. 'So Skitzo-... Mr. Psycho-... It's just you and me! Nobody here to save you, no Meanstreak, no Syko The Goblin and as you can see Nerezza is engaged in an affair of his own!' 'URGH?!?' murmured the inmate, still hooked on the spring that he expands and contracts, rattling the metal that brings a smile to his grotesque face. Another clang and another smile, the prisoner ignores the other entity in his chamber as Gaz attempts to get under his skin for a second time. 'Come Friday, Skitzo you wont be able to ignore me-... Rest assured I'm going to, well, FUCK YOU UP!!! To cut a long story short, why wait till Friday Night, huh?!?' Witnesses to a potential blood bath, Natalia shrieked fearing for her client’s life. Doctor Kim Howells watched with interest trying to fathom these techniques of the false organisation of Patient Specialist Youth Care Offices (PSYCHO) expecting Gaz to literally enter the mind of a killer through a strange course of action, namely the insults yet truth be told, the Jester merely wanted to experience brutality from someone mentally retarded. 'GGGGGGAAAAAAZZZZZZ!!!!!!' cried Natalia as the inmate glared over at Gaz who responded with a grin and the lowering of his bag. Dropping his spring he stood erect, nothing to boast about but certainly a challenge. 'That's it-... You know I'm going to FUCK YOU right up, best you get in first-...' roared the Emerald Menace, the case springing open, unveiling an arsenal of gadgets, baring the question of, 'Who's the sicker?' Looking into the case, the inmate took a boot to Gaz as the on lookers in Natalia and doctor Kim Howells screamed with terror, fearing the life of a client and associate. Another boot, then another, a verbal exchange from the inmate was followed by yet another pleads from the hearted Natalia, her eyes tearful. 'I'M GOING TO KILL YOU LIKE THAT WHORE!!!' 'Is that right BITCH!?!' retaliated Gaz Green reaching into his bag of tricks and removing a stun gun. With a flash, a blinding white light the inmate was taken off his feet, crashing to the bare concrete where the Jester repeatedly beat him with one blow after another, sending his victim into a spasm. Squirming over the floor in a fit, Gaz regained his composure and saw the aftermath of his handiwork. With a thud the door sprung open with Kim Howells fighting to save the life of his patient who continued to jerk about like a wounded animal, Natalia a little sceptic, taking a long hard stare at the monster Gaz had become. 'Now who's the real Mr. Psycho?' murmured the Lord Of Laughter Gaz 'The Jester' Green to himself, gazing over a successful caper, successful a word that could easily be argued. With the cries of Kim Howells and the howling of the other inmates, the scene slowly closed fixated on the tainted red eyes of its host Gaz 'The Jester' Green who minutes earlier showed the world another side, camouflaged by his gleaming white smile, oppressed by his fun loving nature, he himself could easily be deemed a monster. - The End -
