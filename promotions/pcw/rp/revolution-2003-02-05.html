Revolution

5 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2/5/03 Revolution We need help sir. Our men are wounded and are dying more and more day after day. Their blood has splattered and the enemy is rejoicing more and more with every body we bury. Our bodies are bruised, many of us have gone days without eating and cannot even lift our weapons. King we beg to you for mercy and for the proper resources needed in order to win this battle. The king, who the man was speaking to, smiles back with the beautifully plated gold on his body shining through the air. The man who is on his knees for this small deed looks around and sees the painted and gold plated walls, with ancient and valuable china the king posses. The man just looks at the emotionless king clinching his hands together in utter anger. I’m sorry young sir, but I believe the resources just aren’t here to aid your men. When your men’s families can raise the proper money needed, then you may spend more frequently. I ask these men to give me their lives, and now I am expected to steal from the hands of their wives and children to fight a war in which none of us had nay say in. Excuse my mouth but that gesture is purely ludicrous my sir. You are to never speak to a man of my caliber in that manner. Now go off, I have nothing to give to you or your peasants. You will fight as told, and fight until you cannot any longer. That is all I ask of you. That is all… The man grabs a dagger from his pocket as he stares in the eyes of the King who looks ever so confident surrounded by his gold and riches. He looks behind himself at the two guards realizing any sudden move will cost him his life in a split second. Doubt comes into the man’s mind, but in the end he follows what his heart told him to do. The man charges at the King with a dagger. The King looks at him in horror right before the initial stab, throwing his hands in the air with his gold pottery crashing to the floor. The man stabs the kind right through the heart and holds it in there applying as much pressure as he can. Within ten seconds both guards club the man in the back of the head and spear him with their weapons, leaving his blood splattered on the floor of this King’s mansion. The scene fades out and shows El Romantico standing and applauding the end of the film. Behind him is a row of seats that are in an average movie theatre. The crowd shows their sadness, while Romantico just laughs and applauds the ending. Romantico then goes over to a family next to him and speaks loudly. A fine work of cinema, I must say. There is nothing like a good man lying in his own cold blood for an ending. The family quickly walks off with the father holding the ears of the children, defending them from the thoughtless remarks of El Romantico. Romantico then goes into a brief soliloquy as he leaves the movie theatre. So as you may have seen, the film was quite entertaining. Of course the facts and happenings may have been somewhat realistic, but it’s nice to see something that isn’t such a fantasy. Yes a fantasy. You see lately the world has been living in this huge fantasy bubble, not realizing the happening around us. I mean we have terrorism sweeping through our lands, nuclear weapons being searched for, countries at civil war, places where people literally starve in the street, and utter chaos. But the odd part is people still manage to believe in this fantasy world. Everything will turn out fine… That’s what they tell themselves day in and day out. Believe me, I know for I’ve spoken those words myself. Unfortunately they are a lie. Nothing turns out the way we want. Everything ends up being tainted along the way. It’s up to us how much those things become tainted. Tainted… When I say this word I speak of something that is no longer pure. Tainted Love, yes even love is something so tainted. And instead of living with this tainted life of mine, I’d rather pass it off to others. I do not live to please all, but to please myself. I know you all do, and if you say you don’t then that is an utter lie. We all think about ourselves and only ourselves. Let’s take the production we have just watched as an example. The man asking for money was in the most part doing things for himself. You see he planned on using his money for others, but he sure as hell wouldn’t have minded having it for himself as well. That’s where that anger came from. When you can’t get something you desire, you tend to feel this anger. That reminds me of a certain Tessa Windsor. Poor dear finds the need to ruin others, because she herself cannot be satisfied. May we just pray that this heavenly demon be blessed. Now the man was not the only one who thought about himself, for the King was obviously selfish. He is a man of such fame and fortune keeping everything for himself and not giving it to the others. But then again he did earn everything that was his. And I believe in the Bible it says to keep that which belongs to thou. Now I’m not saying the King was an angel, but he had a right to his possessions. The king did have one problem though. And that was his confidence. You must believe the unexpected and be ready. That would be the difference between myself in the king. I would never have let it gone to the point where I laid there lifeless. I guess everything is just a movie. A revolution doesn’t have to have a whole army. A revolution is over something you want yourself. Greed is everywhere, do not try and make as if you don’t possess it.
