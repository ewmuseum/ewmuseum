Meeting Manger's Crew...The Hunt For Makonie Is On.

14 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5/14/03 Turmoil is my biggest match to date in the PCW…the one where all eyes will be on me. People wanting to see if I am everything I have been hyped up to be. This isn’t a normal match though; it’s a damn gauntlet, where I have to prove I am smarter then everyone else. I know it doesn’t seem like it’s going to be much of a challenge because I am the last person in that ring, with quite possible a worn down guy, but that’s when people are at there best. People never seem to falter when their backs are against the wall. That is why I joined the skinheads, I didn’t have a choice, no one sees Mark Makonie outside of PCW, which makes it almost impossible to get my princess back. So I entered his world…his domain. Befriending Tom Manger so that when the time is right, I will be able to pull the trigger on the gun…leave Makonie laying at my feet, just like I should have done on Turmoil. Something stopped me though…something changed me from the once stone cold killer that I was…Haley? Probably, she is the one who made me believe that I could survive through the kidnapping and the beatings, she is the reason I kept going on and she is the reason I am so close to the Raising Star title. All the hardships and pain are going to be worth it when I am finally able to hold my baby in my arms…saving her from the jaws of evil. That’s why I became the Chameleon once again…joining these raciest bastards to hunt down the one man that I call enemy. He has done more to me then anyone has ever dared too…playing the game like a master, but in the end it’s just not good enough. His moves aren’t all there anymore and slowly like the seasoned player I am…he’s going to be in checkmate. Makonie is to blind to see it. I used his own queen to get him into the position and now it’s time to use my queen, my princess…Haley, to make the final moves and end him like he deserves. His down fall like so many others was based on greed and now he is paying the price of that greed…being hunted. *** Pushing his hands into the pockets of his brown trench coat, Matt Jackson silently walks the Norticfest grounds with Tom Manger. The nazi leader is dressed in a pair of black jeans and a sleeveless white tee shirt, proudly flaunting his swastika tattoo on his right bicep. The two skinheads’ continue to walk side-by-side, taking in the sights. Matt doing his best not to be sick at the sight of so many racists in one area, all of them spread out over the grassy field, each one a different age…men and women, but all wearing close to the same thing and all sharing the same beliefs. Taking a deep breath, Matt smells the muddy dead grass that is slowly turning brown from the mass amount of stomping that is attacking it. The operative just shakes his head, letting his black dress pants swoosh in the hot summer air. His long coat is beginning to make him sweat, but he wipes it off with his sleeves and keeps going, not letting the humid climate of LA get to him. On the operatives left side is an enormous stage, with huge speakers strapped to the rafters…this thing would even make Society seem small. A top of the stage, there is a skinhead worker setting up for the arrival of Shane Weathers, the young nazi rock star…Mangers skinhead in training. All around them are different circus type of tents, some housing venders selling nazi flags and tee shirts, others selling refreshments…all having the same white tee shirt and jeans on that their famed leader Manger does. Straight in front of them, is a smaller yellow tent, which isn’t as open as the vender’s, but still rather easy to see inside…where there are three tattoo chairs sitting, each occupied with a faithful nazi that wants a swastika on some part of their body. Matt’s angular face is one of distain, as his brown irises scan the area, seeing the young children running around, dressed just like their parents…the future nazi’s of America. I can’t believe parents would put there kids though this…they don’t even get a chance to make their own moral choices, instead they are forced to believe in the same bullshit their parents do. I wonder if Makonie was brought up like this? Pushing past all different sizes of skinheads, Manger keeps up his pace doing to his destination…following behind him like a lost egret is the Chameleon, who is still wearing his flesh coloured swimming cap, to hide the fact his head really isn’t shaved…the master of disguise knows that no one is going to question it, as it perfectly hides his brown hair. Running his petite hands over his form fitting white shirt, which hugs his barrel of a chest so perfectly, Matt slows down, not wanting to get on the heels of the foul smelling leader…who’s shirt is drenched in sweat and is clinging to his over weight body. The men don’t stop, even at the sound of some raciest music blaring over the speakers. It’s nothing more then a pre-recorded tape of some of Weather’s work, Jackson guesses. It’s nothing more then filler…just like the gauntlet match on Turmoil. They want to give the fans something to look forward to before Supremacy and in all reality they are just giving me another chance to get closer to Kid Wonder’s undefeated record. What an honour that would be…surpassing a man as great as K-Dub, probably the greatest cruiserweight to ever live. Thinking about K-Dub puts a smile on the Chameleon’s face, giving him a moment to forget all the nazi propaganda that he is flooding himself with. Snapping out of it, as quickly as he fell into it, Matt stops, almost running into the back of his nazi recruiter. Standing in front of the pair, is another skinhead…really makes no difference to Jackson, as they have just become a massive sea to him now. This one isn’t any different from the others; wearing the same clothes…the only difference is the fact that attached to his belt is some sort of blade…long in it’s design and complemented by it’s sleek ivory handle. “Simon, this is the man I was telling you about before…he is one of the leading members for the cause. That blade on his belt, as sliced the necks of many young niggers,” Manger begins. “I hold him as my right hand man…you could learn a lot from him Simon.” “It would be a great honour sir…learning from a man who has given so much to the movement. I one day hope I can say I gave the same amount of effort you did sir,” Jackson lies, nodding to the man, giving him a big smile, showing his white teeth. The murder smiles at him and reaches out his hand to shake the Chameleon’s. Matt sticks his hand out too, trying to be as genuine as possible, but wanting to pull it back, as soon as he feels the blood trickling from the man’s hand. The two of them lock eyes, the man’s face like stone, not flinching a bit as the operative stares into his black eyes. Realizing Matt’s hand the man teases his blade a bit with his monster hands and Jackson lets his arm fall into place, making sure to brush his holster, just to double check the Beretta is there…just in case. He doesn’t plan on getting to cocky like he did with the captors. Rubbing the blade the man offers: “I have made so many minorities conform to my blade…you know, I don’t even remember my kill count. When I was in prison it shot up so much, it’s not even imaginable what it’s like. Maybe ten or eleven.” That’s it? You think Christian MacManus is going to be impressed by that. I didn’t get the title killer-for-hire because I had a kill count of ten or eleven. Hell, I did things unimaginable…killing people without even pulling the trigger myself, that is until Haley came along…she changed everything. “Interesting, that’s quite an achievement…it’s to bad I don’t conform to anything, including Society. I do things the way I see fit, of course…seeing things a certain way is the best way I think I can help the movement,” Matt says, trying to set the blade wielding man at ease. The man takes his hand away from the blade, but rests it on his leather belt next to it. “Yes, I have gone to leaps and bounds to help the movement myself…killing a lot of black women, to make sure those dumb niggers knew their place. It was worth it in the long run though, because when we take over, Manger is going to remember my sacrifices.” You sick bastard, you might not be Stone Burns, but you sure as hell remind me of him. Killing innocent women because of your twisted beliefs…after I hunt down Makonie and get Haley back, I have half a mind to come back and take you out for your horrible crimes. Just like I am going to do to him on Fusion and then again on Turmoil, just like I did the two times before. Holding back his disgust, the Chameleon just nods at the nazi and tries to move away…not before Manger grabs his shoulder and spins him towards him. “Where are you going Simon?” He probes, worried that Simon might not be as into the movement as he first believed. “I wanted to go pick up one of those tee shirts, this jacket and dress shirt are starting to get hotter then a nigger in the African sun,” He quickly jokes, hoping that Manger doesn’t suspect something is up. Manger releases his shoulder and the three men laugh, with Manger trying to spit out some comment about a minority, but it’s unnoticeable as they continue to bellow. Matt turns away and starts to make his way towards one of the vending tents…trying to ignore the groups of people that he passes, wishing that he had never gotten himself into this situation…pretending that Haley is standing next to him holding his hand on his voyage to get her back. I will have you back soon Haley and then I can put all this skinhead bullshit behind me. Forgetting that they ever existed and forgetting that I ever became a part of them…it’s all going to be worth it when I am holding you in my arms again at Turmoil, after I win the gauntlet match.
