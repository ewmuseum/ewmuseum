Back to the memory

23 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4-23-02 That house. That simple structure. Bring back dreams and memories… nightmares coming true I tried to scream. No one heard me. This hell is still very real. I lived it everyday there. The nightmare was so peaceful, so serene. Never again awaken me from it. *Nine years…. Nine long painstaking years had fallen since he left that horrible place. He left everything behind then. His foster parents, his faith in humanity, his own life as he knew it…. Everything. As he looked out the window from his hotel room, all he could think about is what has become of him since that fateful day. All he could ask himself was if he made the right decision when he left just a few weeks after he turned fifteen. What would have happened if he had stayed there? So many questions that no one could ever answer. He tried so very hard not to let it cross his mind, but it still haunted him. Maybe he could have been saved. Maybe he could have been a true success in life. But what did it matter? What’s done was done and couldn’t have be changed anyway. Then it happened. A newsflash appeared on the television, which he was watching that broke to the masses a revelation to him that shook the very foundation of the questions he had been pondering. One of KMSP’s reporters, a young woman to be precise stood outside the old foster home. It was in ruins. Almost nothing was left of it, but when anyone went through what Jeremy did you quite easily can recognize it. The lemon yellow coat of paint, which was peeling off, the large hill it sat on, and most of all the marble steps that led up that hill to the doorway.* “I’m standing out in front of the old Guiding Hand Foster Home, where today marks the nine year anniversary of the tragic murder of everyone inside had taken place. No one knows how it was possible, but a confirmed count of forty-six people were found dead inside two days later. They had all been killed in their sleep by what was apparently a fatal gas leak of some sort which police believed was set intentionally. Even all this time later, people continue to mourn the losses.” *It seemed the answer finally came to him after all this time. Surely he would have joined those who perished from the epidemic. And if he somehow survived, he surely would have gone insane from the constant torment that plagued his everyday life. His life was a complete catch-22 then, and he sure as hell still believes the same thing even as of now. Perhaps if he faced his old fears, he could finally gain some piece of mind. His mind was set. It was time to go back where he feared most, where the demons dwelled. It was time to go back… home.* 3857 Fillmore Avenue. *There it was in all its fallen glory. Just like the camera suggested it should have looked like the day earlier. Not much had scared Stryfe anymore, but this was quite an exception. A chill embraced his body as he ascended those stone steps. He took a deep breath as the door creaked open to show his eyes the darkness that lied within the house. He could barely even see a thing as he became encircled by it, but he groped his way through the screens of black. His eyes eventually adjusted to a horrific sight of old belongings, broken and scattered everywhere with cobwebs and the like forming. Many of these sights were quite familiar to him. The first of these is caught out of the left corner of his eye.* “Ah yes, the old TV.” He said aloud to himself, “I remember when we would all huddle around it to watch our shows like The Price Is Right.” *He had for some strange reason a profound fascination with that show that was rather odd, almost obsessive.* Tell him what she can win, Roddy. A brand new car! He continued. “And there is the old play area, where the young ones would frolick around. They had so much fun.” *A large vacant space with a large mat spread out was right behind him. He took note of that as well with his eyes.* “Ring around the rosies. A pocket full of posies. Ashes, ashes, we all fall down.” *And then he saw it. Right in front of him lied the old wooden staircase. Just up these stairs lied the nightmares and realities that still haunt him even now. The faded red carpeting covering the steps was quite appropriate. To him it resembled his blood… his dignity that spilled on the floor above. Even the one who had seemingly seen it all in his brief life was reluctant to open the door.* Now just between you and me, this is gonna be our little secret. *With one creak of the door, his frightening past was revealed to him. He saw not a vacant room with old cots and rusty springs sticking out, but a place of unspeakable torment. A scene from his memory then flashed before Stryfe’s eyes.* June 17, 1990: 4:47 p.m. *Claudia Peterson was a middle-aged woman, perhaps around forty-five years of age. Her hair already going gray only complimented the downright scary and sadistic gleam in her eye, which complimented that “old witch” look. She was always known for wearing a hideous dress that would make someone like Edith Bunker jealous if she weren’t a fictional character. She was spooky alright. A lot of people would say she was out of her mind just by taking a look at her. Of course, no one truly knew what thought floated around that supposedly sickly mind of hers. Young Jeremy was twelve then. He just moved into the home about a week ago. He was still grieving over the loss of his family. Peterson was just coming back from a brief vacation and had not seen the boy yet. She walked in the doorway and immediately squealed with delight at the sight of the child.* “This must be Jeremy. I’ve heard so much about you!” Claudia cackled. One of the volunteer workers who Stryfe couldn’t even remember the name of walked up to her. She confirmed to her, “Yes, here he is. He just lost his entire family in a car accident.” “Oooohhh. I’m so sorry to hear that.” Claudia told him. *He had nothing to say. Bringing up his family to him was no different than a Christian burning down his own church. It was simply was a bad idea. He tried to let it slide though. She didn’t know any better anyway. Time passed as the man soon to be Stryfe popped in a tape of his favorite show again. He never got sick of it no matter how many times he watched it. He sat down in his favorite beanbag chair with a Pepsi in hand. From the force of him plopping heavily down on the cushion, the liquid spilled all over. Claudia wasn’t pleased. Or perhaps she was now that an opportunity had arisen.* “Jeremy! What have you done?!” She shouted to him. “B-but it was an accide-“ “No buts mister. I’m taking you upstairs to teach you a lesson!” *Her hand dragged Connors upstairs into the bedroom. She literally slammed him onto the bed. After this he could only have wondered what would happen next. What he didn’t expect was her taking out a long leather whip, handcuffs and duct tape. He fought with all his might, but Claudia was no weakling by a long shot. Shackled and strapped to the bed, he was stripped naked for her pleasure. She whipped away at much more than his skin. She struck a nerve in his very soul.* Ow! Stop it! You’re hurting me! Ouch! Stop it! Ouch! Don’t! Quit it! Don’t… Please… Stop… Please… *It may have taken place twelve years, about half of his earthly existence ago, but it still plays like a bad dream over and over in his head. On the bed sheet he found the most horrific thing of all: A drop of blood, a small crimson spot on the sheets. It was his blood. He could smell it.* “This blood. It will be spill once more. This time it will not be my own. I have regained my piece of mind and my head is clearing. My focus is regained on my enemies. Syko… Miyagawa…. They must feel these twelve years of pain. These twelve years of suffering… These twelve years of, well… ……… Strife.” *With his mantra spoken, he walked out of the horror house with his head held high. His outlet for his disturbed mind and anger had now been recharged.* The answers have arrived. And now arise the questions. Could his opposition handle these truths. Only the ring can answer such a thing. The time is now… … Two more will fall off the ledge into the great unconsciousness where no one ever awakes.
