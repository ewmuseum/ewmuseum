A nice 'lil home

2 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11-2-02 Brandi hurries up to the large stone building. Her face is fixed in a look of determination mixed with a hint of anger. The black sparkly dress she’s wearing flows out behind her and her high heels click against the blacktop of the parking lot. Nathan runs after her, wearing an expensive looking suit with the jacket unbuttoned. As Brandi turns around to see where he’s at, she steps in a puddle of water, which instantly fills her shoe. Brandi- Damnit! She leans against the side of the building and pulls the shoe off, holding it upside down so that the water runs out. Nathan catches up finally and looks at her apologetically. Brandi- Nathan, get inside…now! Nathan scrambles inside the building, holding the door open for Brandi as she slips the wet shoe back on. Once she gets inside, he lets the door close…right on her trailing dress. Neither one of them notices, and the two continue inside a few steps, not stopping until Brandi hears a distinct ripping sound. She stops, and Nathan gallops back to the door to retrieve the end of the dress. Brandi- Nathan…go away. Brandi walks up to a desk, her telephone conversation still fresh on her mind. The woman had sounded angry. No, not angry. Frustrated. Brandi turns around to see Nathan smoothing out the suit before casually walking towards her. Yes, frustrated. Much like she was feeling right now. Brandi- Hello, I received a phone call last night asking me to come here this afternoon but I have dinner reservations in an hour, so can we make this quick? The woman on the other side of the desk ruffles through some paperwork and nods. She pulls out a folder and places it in front of Brandi. Woman- This is the folder. It has the room number on it. I’ll have a doctor meet you in there within the next couple of minutes. He needs to talk to you about a few things. Brandi takes the folder and hands it to Nathan, who leads the way down the hall. The two go inside the door marked according to the file without knocking. Inside the room a middle-aged couple sits on the edge of the bed holding hands. They both look up when they hear the door open and are obviously surprised by who they see standing in their doorway. The woman jumps up and starts to walk towards Brandi, but the man stands between the two women. Brandi grins. Brandi- Why Mother, you seem upset over something. Everything is alright, I hope? Mother- You know very well that everything is not alright. Brandi looks shocked. She looks around the room and helps herself to a set in the corner, next to the only window. Brandi- Are you trying to accuse me something, Mother dearest? Mother- Yes, I am! I know that you put us in here. You know there’s nothing wrong with either one of us, yet you had them lock us up. This…this is horrible! Brandi- Horrible? You ungrateful bitch! What exactly is so horrible about it? The fact that someone is making payments so that you can live here? Or is it the fact that you also get free food? Wait just a second. I forgot that you have someone that will wait on you hand and foot. You tell me what’s so horrible about that you wench! The woman pushes past her husband and stands in front of Brandi’s chair. As she stands there, Brandi glares up at her. Nathan moves into a corner. He looks grateful to have gone unnoticed. Mother- Oh, don’t try to tell me you don’t know about the meds they have us on. The constant supervision. Not a moment’s worth of privacy. They think we’re crazy! Doctors come in and out of here constantly. Brandi grins and stands up. As she walks towards her mother the woman backs up until she runs into the bed and sits down. Brandi- You did it to yourself. You were slowly driving me insane. Always asking for things like a child. A damn child! Brandi’s mother stands up and moves close to her daughter’s face. Their noses are only inches apart. Each can feel the temper of the other rising. Mother- That’s why you lied to that child, isn’t it? Why you made her believe that you’re her friend. Then you turned on her. Threw her out like she was trash. Brandi raises her hand back and hits the woman right in the mouth. She falls backwards and lands on the soft bed. Brandi’s father starts to lunge across the room, but a quick motion from Brandi causes Nathan to grab him and hold him still. Brandi- You bitch! Don’t you ever mention that again. She was trash, just like you are. She deserved what happened to her. Brandi’s father frees himself from Nathan’s grasp and runs over to his wife’s side. Brandi walks back over to Nathan and looks at him pleadingly. Brandi- Hit me. Nathan- What?! Brandi- Hit me. Nathan- I’m not going to hit you! Brandi shrugs and hits her head against the wall. Blood slowly moves from a cut on her forehead. The door opens and a doctor rushes in. He sees the cut on Brandi’s forehead and immediately escorts her and Nathan out of the room. He barks some kind of orders into a walkie-talkie clipped onto his belt and a team of nurses rushes into the room. Doctor- I’m sorry about that. I knew I shouldn’t have you speak to them without someone else present. I didn’t expect that either one of them would attempt to hurt you. I mean, they were doing so well. Anyway, are you alright? Brandi- Yeah. I’m fine. Brandi turns her attention back to the door in time to see a group of the male nurses dragging her father out of the room and down the hall. He looks at her with a blank expression and she gives him a little wave as he goes around a corner. Brandi- I think that maybe you should keep the two of them separated for awhile. Put her on some sedatives or something. You know. I wouldn’t want to see my father getting hurt or anything. Doctor- Yes, Miss Wilson. That’s actually being arranged this very moment. Everything will be taken care of. Brandi- Thank you Doctor. Doctor- I’m just sorry if I caused you an inconvenience in any way. I know that you must be very busy. Brandi- Oh, it was no problem Doctor. I just had a dinner meeting to be at. Doctor- Surely you won’t be late? Brandi- It’s fine. Thanks again. Doctor- I have some paperwork at the front desk that I need you to fill out before you leave, if you could. Brandi nods and starts walking back to the front desk with Nathan at her side. He is unsure of what to think about what he just witnessed, and just seems to put one foot in front of the other as he thinks. Brandi pulls a cell phone out of her purse and hands it to him as they near the desk. Brandi- Why don’t you call Xavier and Tim and tell them that we’ll be a little late while I take care of this real quickly? Then we can run back to the hotel so I can change real quick. Nathan looks embarrassed at Brandi’s last sentence, but he takes the phone and goes off to do as he is told. Brandi walks up to the desk, sighs, and starts signing the many forms.
