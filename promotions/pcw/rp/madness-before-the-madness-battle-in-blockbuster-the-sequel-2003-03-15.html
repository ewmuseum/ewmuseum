MADNESS BEFORE THE MADNESS!-Battle in Blockbuster, THE SEQUEL!

15 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Title: MADNESS BEFORE THE MADNESS!-Battle in Blockbuster, THE SEQUEL! Featuring: Rent-a-Hero Date: 3-15-2003 !!!~WHAM~!!! !!!~POW~!!! !!!~OWW THAT HURT~!!!..::/::..When we last left our spandex enwrapped superhero of PCW, he was confronted within the local Blockbuster Video by a group of three deadly ninja's while looking for 'Unchained Heart', his video request for the evening. This triad of ninjas seem to want to impede Rent-A-Hero's progress to competing in the March Madness battle royal, where he will attempt to not only settle his score with 'Totally Tubular' Tommy, but also win the PCW World Heavyweight title. But before he can even BEGIN to think of his pay-per-view obligations, he must first combat and defeat these three deadly ninja's without fail....sadly, he is failing at the moment as the overexerted action graphics would make clear as he is being decimated from all sides by the deadly ninja force coming to end him, our story fading in as we find out hero being hurtled across the store and into a shelf of 'Action' videos ranging from 'Rambo' to 'Cleopatra 2525', the tapes toppling over on our hero and creating a makeshift burial ground as store employees and patrons hide in fear, the three ninjas, one in blue, the other red, and the leader black; closing in on Rent-A-Hero's fallen ground..::/::..Black Ninja:'I'd say his journey to March Madness will end quite shortly. Let's just check him to make sure he's taken care of.'Red Ninja:'Y..'..::/::..Before the red ninja can even push the first syllable out of his mouth for what he's saying, the black ninja's fist rises up with the utmost speed and the deadliest of power towards the red ninja's jaw.... !!!~Back-!!!~SMACK~!!! ...The red ninja holds his jaw in the utmost pain, falling to one knee and using the video shelf to pull himself up after that intense blow. The blue ninja dares not to even look at the damage it has done to his protege, too scared to witness the pain just dealt out as the black ninja addresses the red ninja forcefully, fixing the dark silk sash tied around his waist..::/::..Black Ninja:'Your just a henchman...villain rule #7 says henchmen don't speak. Geez....you lackies are so amateur. Now let us check on the body.'..::/::..The red ninja sucks up the pain, nodding submissively to his leader as the three slowly stalk towards the pile of tapes which happen to house Rent-A-Hero beneath them, there steps slow and calculated and without haste. As the blue ninja reaches to the tapes to unclear them, a fist breaks through the pile and connects with his jaw, sending the ninja back abruptly as he topples into a plastic stand-up sign with various Blockbuster specials, cracking it into shards upon impact. With that, none other then our hero thrusts himself out of the pile, tapes scattering about as he throws up his best fighting stance, the bright fluorescent ceiling lights of the store showering Rent-A-Hero's Spiderman 2099 replica suit in light, a Japanese anime light spark emitting from his costume as he heroically exclaims..::/::..Rent-A-Hero:'BY THE FISTS OF THE NORTH STAR, THIS BATTLE IS YET TO BE OVER YOU NASTY NINJA NEMESIS'S YOU!! YOU CAN'T EXPECT TO DEFEAT RENT-A-HERO WITHOUT A FIGHT!! One man who needs to take that into consideration is that felonious “Totally Tuubular” Tommy....and it will be no surprise when I knock him out of orbit and send him on a collision course with defeat once I lay my SUPER SAIEN STRENGTH AND STUFF UPON HIM!! Never could a mere mortal like that ever expect to defeat the PCW's ONLY superhero...RENT-A-HERO!! BOW DOWN EVILDOERS!!'..::/::..So enthralled in his speech, he is yet to notice that the ninja's, hiding store patrons, and hiding employees have fallen into a deep slumber induced by his long spiel. He drops his fighting stance, letting childish whining take over as he throws his hands about angrily and with a pout says..::/::..Rent-A-Hero:'They NEVER stay awake for the long heroic monologue....' ..::/::..Before he can receive more time to continue his pouting, the blue ninja he smited a few moments ago flies in from nowhere and sidekicks Rent-A-Hero in his incredibly ripped chest, sending the hero toppling to the floor and falling on his butt. The blue ninja lands upon his feet, fury in his dark eyes which can barely be seen through the slit in his silky almost stalking cap-like mask as he unleashes a set of nun-chaku(nun or num-chuks) from behind his back, twirling the instrument of destruction about his back and sides with precision as he rushes in to attack our hero. He swings with deadly momentum each shot barely missing Rent-A-Hero as he quickly backs up every few swings, but the blue ninja still manages to violently swipe at videotapes or store props in an effort to cause damage to Rent-A-Hero. With his back against the wall and the blue ninja ready to close in for the crushing blow, our hero holds out a 'halt' gesture to the blue ninja, who does so with numchuks ready for use as Rent-A-Hero exclaims..::/::..Rent-A-Hero:'BE WARNED NINJA OF BLUENESS, I shall recreate the feats as seen on Crouching Tiger Hidden Dragon and run up this wall and upon reaching the ceiling, I shall Shooting Star Press upon you and subdue you! EAT SPANKERING SAUCE EVILDOER!! ...::/::..Although his plans ALWAYS sound good in theory to him, Rent-A-Hero always seems to forget that thing called REALITY, proven so as he tries running up the battle-damaged white wall of the store, but upon raising his other foot up....the laws of gravity prove our hero right as he falls smack dab on his back, a loud scream of pain filling the store as his face construes into many shows of pain..::/::..Rent-A-Hero:'TV....WHY DO YOU LIE TO ME!! I THOUGHT YOU WERE MY FRIEND!!' ..::/::..The blue ninja cannot wait for our hero's end any longer, crashing down the numchuks violently, but missing by only a few seconds as Rent-A-Hero rolls away and against a shelf of films. He turns his head up and looking to the film title above his shoulder it reads 'Use This Video To Smack Over A Ninja's Head In A Fight-Volume 2'. Our hero looks to the camera, shrugging to the audience as he grasps the tape in hand. As the blue ninja tries yet again for a violent numchuk swing, our hero ducks and brings his hand up, SMASHING the tape across the blue ninja's jaw as the scene slows down, the ninja spiraling downward until finally falling to the blue carpet of the store. Our hero once again looks to the camera, flashing a smile and a thumbs up..::/::..Rent-A-Hero:'HAHAH, never mess with a hero and his VIDEOTAPE O' VEGETA-LIKE VIOLENCE EVILDOER!!'..::/::..The gloating is cut short as a katana RIPS through the store shelf behind our hero, the blade barely missing his back as he turns about to find the red ninja, twirling the bladed weapon in hand with intention to kill. Without pause, the red ninja rushes at Rent-A-Hero with blade forward, slashing and thrusting at our hero as he swivels and bends in ways that would make Cash Money's backup dancers jealous. Our hero swings for a right hook, but is met by only open space as the red ninja limberly spin about and roundhouse kicks our hero far back as he crashes into the wall and slides off, falling behind the counter of the store where the manager is quivering in fear, on his knees and sweat beading down his thinning hair, pudgy cheeks, and Coke bottle thick frames...::/::.. Rent-A-Hero:'Fear not pudgy manager type person, Rent-A-Hero is here to save the day without fail! It's a pity Hair Club for men couldn't save you though.'..::/::..Our hero, breathing heavily and with very little time notices a set of items laying behind the counter at his disposal...one being a steel police baton with a trick handle like the Big Bossman and the other being a sharp Bowie knife, gleaming and sharpened for the kill! In mere moments, Rent-A-Hero leaps on the orange counter with white ceiling debris strewn all over it, the red ninja standing just a few feet away as he eyes Rent-A-Hero...our hero raising up a deadly...destructive....PRICING GUN TO COMBAT THE KATANA?! Excuse me for saying this, but who comes up with this stuff?!..::/::..Rent-A-Hero: *looks up* 'Excuse me, BUT DO I TELL YOU HOW TO DO YOUR JOB?!' ..::/::..*sigh* Anyways, our hero points the pricing gun at the red ninja, who almost wants to bust out in laughter over Rent-A-Hero's silly choice of weaponry, exclaiming with the utmost confidence..::/::..Red Ninja:'And what do you think your going to do with that?!' Rent-A-Hero:'Ring you up...the same way I'm gonna ring up 29 other men in the March Madness Battle Royal mind you.' *Looks to screen* 'I haven't forgotten this is an RP after all guys. What you think I forgot? Heavens no!'..::/::..With that said, Rent-A-Hero pulls the trigger in the pricing gun, the crimson infared beam emitting from the gun and showering the red ninja's eyes in radiation. Shortly, the constant showering causes the red ninja to drop his katana, shaking violently as he falls to his side, convulsing rapidly on the floor until he finally just goes unconscious. With that villain defeated, our hero blows the top of the pricing gun and drops it, delivering a one-liner as if he were in a classic 80's action flick..::/::.. Rent-A-Hero:'Price check on convulsing villain...aisle 4. Hehe, beat that Rambo. Wait, darn...it was supposed to be a one-liner! GREAT GASKETS OF GOBBLEDY GOOKER!!'..::/::..Leave it to Rent-A-Hero to mess up a one-liner. Anyways, as Rent-A-Hero makes his departure from the counter, he shortly feels the forceful jab of a cold metal barrel against the side of his yellow and red mask. He turns his eyes to find the final ninja, the black one, as he holds a Colt 45 pistol to his head and locking his dark dagger-like eyes upon the sea blue eyes of the superhero wonder..::/::.. Black Ninja:' I must admit...I didn't expect to have to use such crude and volatile means to defeat you...but I have a mission Rent-A-Hero. Good bye friend, you truly were a valiant warrior...' !!!~THWAP~!!! ..::/::..Before the trigger can even be pulled, Rent-A-Hero's eyes closed to accept his fate, a shuriken pierces the back of the black ninja...the gun and then shortly the ninja plummeting to the ground. Our hero opens his eyes and looks across from him to find what appears to be…THE STORE MANAGER! Our hero is amazed, but not as much as the meek manager who can’t believe he just subdued the evil ninja..::/::. Rent-A-Hero:'WHOA…NICE THROW! ' Manager:'Did…did…did I do that?!' –looks about- “OH MY GOD, MY STORE?!” ..::/::..Our hero begins looking about at all the damage caused in the store as the customers and employees rise up from their hiding places. Our hero quickly locks eyes upon a copy of 'Unchained Hearts', snatching it up form off the ground and dashing to the door. He quickly tosses a crisp one dollar bill with 99 cents upon the counter before exclaiming..::/::..Rent-A-Hero:'AS MUCH SUPERPOWER AS I HAVE....CLEANING THIS MESS UP WILL TAKE ME TILL NEXT RENT-A-SEASON!! I'M DISAPPEARING FASTER THEN VANILLA ICE'S CAREER!! GOOD DAY CITIZENS!!' ..::/::..With that said, Rent-A-Hero dashes out of the Blockbuster store...leaving the store looking like a warzone and now knowing that is more imperative then ever to make haste to get to March Madness….before the Madness engulfs him..::/::.. WILL RENT-A-HERO MAKE IT TO MARCH MADNESS INTACT?! WHY ARE ALL THESE EVIL FORCES AGAINST OUR HERO NOW?! IS THAT MOVIE ANY GOOD?! FIND OUT SAME RENT-A-TIME….SAME RENT-A-CHANNEL!!! ---- View Rent-a-Hero's Biography
