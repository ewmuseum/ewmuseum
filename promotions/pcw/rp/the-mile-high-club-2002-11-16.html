Title: The Mile High Club Featuring: Duke Williams Date: 11/16/02 Me and a friend named Street Life Spike. We got a bottle of red and we walked up town after I won my title fight. One hand on the jug, one on the gold, and another on time least that’s what I was told. Three times continental champion would make some jump for joy but it just left me felling like an old used toy. Freaks of the world unite as I may just sleep well tonight. The dark cowboy from Missoula now seems to know ya and the ways that you act. Making me one dangerous cat. You can’t hold me back forever cus I’m too fucking clever. I’ll find a way to have my day soon I’m sure it will be. When I stand on top of the world and piss down from that giant oak tree. Jesus was persecuted for being to jive my problem is I like to hang out in late night dives. Bloody Mary’s in the morning take my senses from me. Whiskey at night makes my body feel right. Detox is for losers and those who get caught. Like a mouse stealing cheese from that snap back trap. Steal through your mind is all your left from that crap. The life of an addict is rough and tough. The life of a Goth Champion is a walk in the park. Most wish they could be me and I wish I was them. We always want what we can’t have its part of being man. So I take this Gold with a sigh of relief and contemplate the alternatives as I just sit her and drink my red with street life spike and the rest of my friends. -Duke Williams sits on a plane headed back to the most Goth state in the continental United States, Montana. It’s United Airlines flight 222 New York to Missoula none stop. Duke sits back listing to Cole Porter while his old friend the continental title sits on his shoulder. You jive junkie? Hey look I’m a straight shooter with a straight shoot so lets duel this out. Nobody on the roster is more gangsta then me. I’m the blackest brother on the roster and that’s the 4-1-1 GQ. I’m also the evilest, darkest, Goth God the PCW or the worlds has ever seen.
