The Day Dreaming Lord

22 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2/22/02 (The scene fades to an open shot of the a smile on a man’s face as we back away it is reveled that the smile belongs to Big Daveylicious. He is clad in khaki pants, a unbuttoned white see through poly shirt, and Oakley sunglasses His arms are to the back of his head with no worries. Just lounging back enjoying the quietness.) Big Daveylicious: This is the life. Voice: What the hell are you doing? You got an important match on Friday and a TV Title shot Sunday. (Big D turns his chair to revel it is the voice of his friend, Nick.) Big Daveylicious: I know. Nick: Shouldn’t you be training for the match? You’re against DarkStar. Big Daveylicious: No, I’m fighting a corpse. Nick: He’s not a corpse anymore. (Big D’s smile fades as he becomes confused.) Big Daveylicious: What are you talking about? They had a funeral and a party for his death. Nick: Sorry, Big D but he is alive. (Big D doesn’t seem to be so calm and relaxed. He puts his hands through his hair.) Big Daveylicious: Can’t Clyde kill a man longer than two weeks?! How am I supposed to prepare for a match against somebody who has been dead for two weeks? Nick: Like usual, train. Big Daveylicious: I got to get inside the head of DarkStar. I got to have fun in what is another man’s hell. I got to meet a King. I got to bring him back for my help. But isn’t this going a little far, Nick? (One can only think of what Big D is actually thinking this momment as the scene fades to black than to a shot of a small town American street. The houses are arranged nicely with white picket fences and scorched front lawns from the searing heat. A black figure can be seen moving in our direction. Halfway we can make out that he is riding a small tiny Razor scooter. He moves past us and lands into the bushes in front of a house. He flips over the bushes onto the ground into the grass with his face in it. He stands himself up and dust himself off. His face and bleached blond hair looks reconizable oddly enough. His attire of choice on this hot day is a 100% leather pants, 100% leather trench coat, black boots, and he also is wearing an a fishnet t-shirt with the “A” logo on the front with the words beneath it saying “A Meany.”He wipes the sweat his forehead.One most wonder what this man is doing. He pulls a pillow sack out of his leather trench coat pocket, walks up to the door of the house, and rings the doorbell. The door opens to a redneck in a wife beater. ) Redneck: Wu’t da hell ya want? Redneck: Trick or treat? Redneck: Uh? Man: I said trick or treat, dick! Now, give me some candy! Redneck: Halloween’s ain’t now, son. Man: This is your King speaking to you, mortal! Now, fetch me a Snickers before I tear your wretched body into a thousand teeny weeny pieces and feed them to the pigeons in the park! Redneck: I knows you. You’s that Big Dawveylicious guy offa the TV. You’s a cool guy. Man: Baah! If it weren’t for me Big Daveylicious would be competing in Mud Wrestling match’s in Dan’s Strip Palace. I am the The Day Dreaming Lord, I am…DaveStar! Redneck: I don’t’s cares about yous. Big Daa is better than yous. DaveStar: How dare you insult the greatness of DaveStar! I am gonna get my cats from hell and they will poop on your carpet and not even Mr. Clean will get it out! Redneck: Here's yer damn Snickers you son of a b*tch. Now, GET OFF MY PROPERTY! (The redneck throws the Snickers into the hands of DaveStar and the redneck slams the doors. DarkStar puts the Snickers into the pillow sack as he jumps up and down excitedly.) DaveStar: Mwhahaha! (He dashes over to the next covered in sweat he wipes his face with his sleeve and rings the door bell. This time as the door opens a 5 year old kid opens the door with a lollipop in his mouth. ) Kid: Hewwo. DaveStar: Trick or treat? Kid: Gimme something good to eat. DaveStar: You foolish adolescent mortal! How dare you demand the Snoring Star, DaveStar to give you something to eat. Now, gimmee some candy before I have my girlfriend who’s a witch cast a spell upon you! (With that the little kid hands him his lollipop that he had already had in his mouth to DaveStar. He than throws it on the ground.) Kid: I gave you candy. Please go away. DaveStar: This is your, King! I’m not some mere mortal. I’m the King Of Flushing...DaveStar. Now, slave go fetch me sun block, a towel, and a water bottle. Kid: Drop dead. DaveStar: I might. I did for two weeks. Kid: Mommy, says I shouldn’t talk to strangers. DaveStar: You dare defy the command of DaveStar?! On your knees and beg forgiveness, slave! Before I snatch your head, tie it with a bow, put it in a box, with black wrapping paper, and... (The Kid gets tired of this and hits DaveStar in the nuts. He than slams the door as DaveStar falls to the ground in pain.) DaveStar: How long can a boy go without sleeping? When he hits his nightmare in the nuts. (With that the scene fades to black.)
