Psycho Prankster (Part 1)

5 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 05 December With a gentle breeze rustling the bushes of a forest tree line, branches swaying calmly and the skyline overlooking all of the above dim yet cloudless this opening scene emits a moment of de ja vu. The street looking over this setting appeared quiet and unoccupied, a ghost town, so peaceful it makes the hairs on the back of your neck stand erect. As the camera basked on this tranquil yet spooky scene, two silhouettes waltz into view like something from a Spaghetti Western, minus the whole cowboy masquerade. Wearing virgin white, pristine overalls similar to those worn by people in the healthcare profession, namely doctors, nurses and those further up this corporate ladder our duo head towards the forest. From viewing the unknown pair of individuals from the back of their heads, not a usual place to look for things of distinction we can identify these soles as two members of Pranks With Attitude. 'This uniform is-... is-... so not me!' murmured the sweet and innocent voice belonging to Natalia Miyagawa, her head swinging freely like a pendulum as she continually glanced over her attire. 'Stop complaining! You look perfect-... I have one helluva itch in the groin area!' said Gaz 'The Jester' Green with a look of discomfort that was somehow missed by his partner as the camera swung around revealing the parties faces. 'TWITCH, REALLY?!?' replied the Russian beauty, her eyes wide with interest as she misinterpreted the words of her co-worker. Mistaking the Jester's aggravation for a cheesy chat-up line her complaints fizzle out until her colleague intervenes with an untimely interruption. 'Twitch? Twitch? Oh-... No, I said ITCH, these bloody overalls are aggravating my skin, must be the fabric!' Sulking like a spoilt child, Natalia distant herself from the remainder of conversation, her first real indication of taking somewhat of a shine to her client. Briefcase in hand, Gaz Green pushed past the overhanging greenery of the trees as he walked along the dank, dusty road that led to a forgotten place, forgotten and unspoken by the natives of this town. Accompanying the duo, a whistling wind bellowed down the dirt-ridden path. The howling of the wind, the rustling of leaves, the stirring of things in the darkness were almost warning signs that this place was away from humanity for a purpose yet with fear comes curiosity and the latter usually takes the bigger strangle hold, certainly in this case. 'I'm going to die in a nurses uniform!' whispered the P.W.A manager under her breath, unbeknownst that the comment had lingered further and was overheard by her guide. Setting another foot down, the moist ground almost absorbed her petite feet, the event indicated by the look of disgust plastered over her face, another Eleanor Kannon-Hall moment. 'You and Alex must've played doctors and nurses, huh?!?' asked the inquisitive comic, raising his eyebrows, literally toying Natalia into an answer. 'NONE OF YOUR BUSINESS!!!' The thunderous roar of this female dragon took her Champion by surprise. With a gulp, Gaz bit his tongue and continued further into the unknown tracked solemnly by the Pranks With Attitude valet who kept her associate well in view. The Jester's tempo upped and he savagely fought his way though the dense plant life vanishing from sight, causing Natalia to panic as she desperately tried to track the missing superstar. 'Gaz-... Gaz-... Gaz-... Hello-... Anyone here!?!' 'BOO~!' blurted the Conniving Comic from behind, frightening the lowly valet almost to the brink of shock, when suddenly the prank had been countered by another. 'Can I help you?' hissed a harsh voice from behind. A towering figure in uniform shadowed the dynamic duo as he guarded the entrance to a complex, a setting seen before in similar circumstances, the nametag bold with the words, 'Shady Acres Asylum for the Mentally Disabled...' Dark, dank, depressing, these were a few words that could best describe the compound lurking in the background. With wire-mesh fencing and security furnishings well in place, this institute for those mentally disabled was shroud in mystery, a mystery first unravelled by that of Mr. Psycho himself... Skitzo. 'Can I-....' said the guard again, the latter half of his sentence cut short by the intrusion of the PCW comedian, Gareth Green. 'Yes you can be of assistance my good man-... My name is err-... arr-... Stewart Tony Davies and I'm with Patient Specialist Youth Care Offices (PSYCHO)' 'Stewart Tony Davies? You do know that abbreviates as S.T.D, as in Sexually Transmitted Diseases, and we now work for PSYCHO, hello, this full-proof plan is full of cracks and please forgive the pun-...' whispered the unofficial third member of Pranks With Attitude, well at-least the good looking member. With a subtle grunt, the guard pitifully fell for the Jester's tall tale. Lowering his torch, turning his back, the guard led both Gaz Green and Natalia Miyagawa into the concrete structure, the belly of the beast so to speak. Opening a large metallic door, the guard pulled his left arm to one side as an indication for them to enter and like blind sheep they did so strolling along an arc corridor with bad electrics as the lights flickered giving the whole segment a horror movie awe. 'Skitzo must have testicles like grapefruits to hang-out here!' injected the Lord Of Laughter creeping further down the corridor that looked similar to an air-raid bunker, still however continuing the whole feel of claustrophobia. 'And a brain like putty-... It's filthy down here, no wonder he's left talking to himself-... I mean who would want to be friendly with a guy who gets his kicks out of Mental Asylums!' yelped the ditzy diva Natalia Miyagawa. 'He's a schizophrenic, the last thing crossing his liquefied brain is the dirt and grime!' retorted her client, speaking the obvious. Now almost at the end of this tunnel, the duo stopped before a door covered in various warning signs, splashes of red and various shapes that would stimulate the mind of The H.I.T make for their next obstacle. Another large metallic door stood stern, fortifying the monsters of this institute inside, away from the light of day and more importantly the oppressed minds of the public. Grasping the handle tightly, Gaz wrenched the locking mechanism clockwise causing a thud to echo from within the inches of steel that make up the door. Swinging open ajar with a creak, Pranks With Attitude's Gaz 'The Jester' Green and Natalia Miyagawa were firstly greeted with a blinding white light and then the disturbing howls of the inmates. 'Gaz maybe this isn't such a good idea?!?' choked the separated wife of former PCW performer Alexander Miyagawa. Tugging back at the Jester's overalls, Gaz surely entered the belly of the beast, tackling the very bowels of the complex where he plans to put theory into practise with a series of masterminded pranks. 'Nonsense - All these monsters are locked away. There's no way they could-....' said the former NTICW superstar, his conversation cut short through an unforeseen surprise. As the duo stood before the holding chambers in the midst of conversation the scene closed as a hand came crashing down on the broad shoulder of the Jester, leaving many questions to be answered in the next instalment of this P.W.A mini-series. The closure of this scene concluding with howls, bellows, frightening cries belonging to the captives of this institute and a blood curdling scream from one Gaz Green before the image turned to a snowy mist of silence. 'WHAT THE FUCK-......' - To be continued -
