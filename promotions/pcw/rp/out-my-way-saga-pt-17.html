Out my Way (Saga pt.17)

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 27th December Deuce 00 Deuce Cami liu paced around the room restlessly. Eliza watched her with a sombre gaze from the chair she was sitting in. I should be out there looking for Kai, not waiting around here like a frightened child. She thought to herself. The truth be known, she was scared. Scared of again losing someone she cared deeply about. Cami liu clenched her fists tightly and closed her eyes. She was not going to give into despair. 'Cami?' Eliza asked. 'Hmmm?' Cami liu responded. 'Why don’t you and I go out and check around. I know Ben wants us to stay here, but we could do more good out there looking. Besides, you look like you could use some fresh air.' Eliza said. Meanwhile... A crowd had formed around the two combatants in the street. Both fighters watched the other waiting on who would throw the first punch. Harrison came in with a low swing towards Ben’s stomach that was easily knocked away. However, as Ben parried the first strike he was taken by surprise to a follow up punch to the face. Damn! He is a fast one! Ben thought to himself as he recovered from the blow. I have no time to mess around with him, lets see how he likes this one?' Ben dashed in to deliver a series of quick punches aimed towards the boxer’s chest. Harrison parried each with ease. As Ben was about to throw the last punch, he instead intentionally missed, and whirled around with lightning speed and threw a roundhouse kick aimed towards the back of Harrison’s head. The boxer tried to bring up his guard fast enough, but Ben’s powerful kick connected staggering him shortly. That was all the time Ben needed to finish him off. Leaping into the air drawing upon the Chi burning inside of him, he lashed out with a vicious kick to Harrison’s0 face, followed by a vast barrage of kicks. 'TATSUMAKISEMPUKAKU!!!!' Ben yelled, as he became a whirling vortex of power. The Shotokan master’s foot repeatedly hit the boxer. When Ben finally landed back on the ground, Harrison had been knocked back into a luggage rack and collapsed in a heap. Ben retrieved his shirt from the ground and walked over to the doorman who was watching the fight. Ben pulled out a couple of hundred dollar bills and handed them to the man. 'Sorry about the mess. But you saw that he would not accept my apology, right?' 'Of course monsieur. He was clearly in the wrong.' Replied the doorman eyeing the bills. 'Did you happen to see which way the gentleman that checked in with my party went today?' Ben asked. 'I’m sorry monsieur, I can not recall that I did.' He apologized. 'Thanks any ways.' Ben replied as he headed towards the street to hail down a taxi. Kai had made it a good distance away from the city. The sun was now beaming down brightly and the wind had picked up somewhat making for a cool breeze. It felt good to be on the road walking again. Not confined to a vehicle, though it made travel faster. He did not mind that it would take longer to reach his destination; it would give him time to focus his thoughts. He regretted having left his friends without saying goodbye. It was better this way. He knew that they would insist on coming. Kai could not allow anything to happen to them, especially Cami liu. Shoki had threatened to kill them, if Kai did not accept his challenge. The Demon did not have to threaten them; I would have come even if he had challenged me otherwise he thought to himself. This was another reason to extinguish his evil from the world permanently. As Kai continued down the country road heading east, a black Rolls slowly came up beside him as he was walking along side the road. At first Kai thought, his friends had found him, but this was not the case. The car windows were tinted dark, so no one could see inside them. The rear window of the car slowly came down and a woman with purple hair and a strikingly beautiful face of peered out. 'Can we offer you a lift? She asked politely. 'Thank you, but I must decline.' Kai said giving a short bow. He readjusted the weight of his duffel bag and started to continue on his way. 'But you are ill prepared to face your Demon.” She called out. Kai stopped dead in his tracks. He turned around to face her with one eyebrow raised high. 'I am sorry, what did you say?' he asked. 'You will never conquer your Demon on your present course. Let me help you.' She replied. Kai walked closer to the car window. 'What do you know of my Demon? I have never met you before, have I?' 'No, come with me. I will tell you.' She said smiling warmly at him. 'Just who are you?' Kai asked suspiciously. 'My name is Holly, and I am a friend. You are walking down a dangerous path right now, let me help. Come with me and I will explain. You will just have to trust me, wanderer.' She said as she opened the door for him. Kai thought for a second. It was against his better judgement, but he was curious about what she knew. 'Alright, I will listen to what you have to say.' Kai said as he climbed into the back of the car with her. The car pulled back onto the road and continued heading east.
