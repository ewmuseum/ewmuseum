Date: Oct.30/02 A number of hungry rookies make their singles debuts . . . One more hungry than the others. That hungry young superstar was me . . . Mark Michigan, and I have a feeling no one will be forgetting about me for a long time. I set out to make a statement . . . to make an impact on the PCW . . . and well . . . MISSION ACCOMPLISHED! It was a sunny, yet chilly day and I was just arriving in Zürich, Switzerland. With each breath I took, the air stabbed at my throat with knives of icy, cold torment. As I waited for my baggage to be unloaded from the plane, I thought back to last weeks Fusion and the words of Rich Rollins . . . “It’s a no-brainer. He’s been spending so much time trying to find this sandblasted country, he hasn’t had the chance to prepare for the reason he’s here in the first place.” Although I hated to admit it . . . he was right. Instead of worrying about what people thought about me, and what kind of gimmick I needed . . . I should have been focussing on more important things like . . . What kind of strategy I needed? How I would expose Rollins’ weaknesses? Maybe the debut victory that is sought after by so many rookies lays within the answers to those questions. However . . . what Rollins didn’t realize until it was too late, was that I did know why I was there. I knew exactly what I was doing . . . and as a matter of fact, winning the match wasn’t even first and foremost on my mind. I knew that I’d have plenty of victories in the future, but for right now, it’s all about making a name for myself. And with a chair-shot to Rollins’ face, I did just that. The PCW Front Office couldn’t help but take notice of Mark Michigan . . . the only rookie to make a debut impact like that. I’ve only been in PCW for two weeks now, and already my name is on everyone’s mind. ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ It was during the last few moments of my match with Rollins, when I came to an important realization. It was as if Rollins ‘Anarchist Elbow’ squeezed every drop of decency and respect out of my body, like he was wringing water from a cloth. When that elbow slammed into my sternum, it must have sent a shock wave up my spine that rattled my brain like beans in a maraca. And as JJ’s hand slap the mat three times, all I could hear in the background was the crowd cheering . . . for Rollins. It was at that moment that I realized the fans didn’t matter . . . no one back home mattered . . . all that mattered was ME. I realized that I had to make all the right decisions . . . I had to make all the right moves . . . regardless of how my fans and friends reacted. And that’s why I have turned my back on them all . . . ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ All that was a lot to think about in the fifteen minutes it took to unload the plane . . . and I found myself just standing there lost in thought as my baggage passed me by several times on the conveyer belt. As I snapped out of my daze, I felt a strange sensation emanating from my nether regions. Suddenly it occurred to me that my flight was several hours long, and I hadn’t had a pit stop since I left Israel. I looked around for a washroom, but all the signs were in Swiss . . . but the unmistakable picture of a toilet eventually led me in the right direction. I managed to waddle myself over to the washroom without pissin’ my pants, and I took a seat in on of the greatly undersized bathroom stalls. As the warm, excess fluids flowed from my body and the pleasant feeling of relief coursed through urinal track, I looked on the bathroom wall noticed something peculiar. There on the wall, written in black pen, was some sort of poem . . . in perfect English. I thought it was quite odd to find English in a place like this, so I read the passage . . . “One must notget caught ina quagmire of indulgenceon the path to thePromised Land Maintain a balancebetween vice and virtue The wise will break‘the cycle’ The rest are just suckersand homosexual dog-f***ers” - The Shithouse Prophet Now, most people wouldn’t take to heart the words of some ‘so-called prophet’ who writes such cryptic and foolish things on a bathroom stall, but there was just something about the words. “Maintain a balance between vice and virtue” . . . that sounded like me just a week ago. I was all caught up in trying to figure out who I was . . . trying to find a balance between intellect and aggression . . . oh, what a fool I was. “The wise will break the cycle” . . . this was so true. As I sat on the toilet, I could only think of how the PCW has been stuck in a vicious-cycle of lame, below average talent. Like the ‘Prophet’ said . . . the rest are just suckers and homosexual dog-f***ers. And just as the prophecy predicts . . . the wise WILL break the cycle . . . I WILL break the PCW’s cycle . . . and I WILL find the Promised Land. Maybe not today . . . maybe not tomorrow . . . but someday. I still had plans to make, and a future to map out. ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ After I had finished my business in the washroom, I made my way to airport’s main doors and waved down a taxi. I tossed my bags in the trunk, then took my seat in the back of the taxi . . . all the while repeating the words I had read on the bathroom wall. And once again, I found myself asking the wrong questions in my mind . . . Who is The Shithouse Prophet? Was that passage a sign from God? Was I right to turn my back on my fans and friends? ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ I had finally made it to the hotel and I was just settling into my room, when a large sign from down the street caught my attention. I walked around the queen-sized bed, and peered out the window . . . the light of the moon and stars reflected off the gothic-style buildings of Zürich, giving the gargoyles a lifelike demeanor. As I scanned the skyline, the sign that had caught my attention in the first place once again captured my focus. At the top in flashing, blue neon, the letters P-C-W blazed in the night sky. Below the huge humming lights read a list of matches to take place during that week’s Friday Fusion. Of course, headlining the show was ‘Mark Michigan Vs. BladeD’. Ah yes, BladeD . . . it would soon be time to worry about that young fellow . . . but not quite yet . . . I still had plans to make . . . and a future to map out.
