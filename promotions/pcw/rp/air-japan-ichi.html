Pharaoh is the reason we’re in this mess to begin with!” Sonata turned back around, posing in her yellow stretch jeans, matching short yellow jacket cutting off at her hips, a short black halter top revealing the yellow thong at her waist, a yellow scarf around her neck, and black sunglasses on her face, with her black and yellow streaked hair tied up in two pigtails coming down her shoulders. “I’m ready!” she beamed towards the other three people in the room. Cyclonica released Matrix from the psychic chokehold and shook her head sadly. Matrix, grateful for air, began to chuckle to himself as his fingers typed across the keyboard in his lap. “The tickets are waiting for us at the terminal. They’re under the names of Masato Kibagame and family.” He tossed a bundle to the Divine Prophetess, who caught it with one hand and opened it. “There are your IDs… you, Divine Prophetess, are Ai Kibagame, daughter of Masato Kibagame. Miss Sonata here is Maru Kibagame, wife of the god here…and me, I’m Tepei Kibagame, your loving brother!” Cyclonica rolled her eyes as she threw an ID card to Sonata. “F*ck me with a hammer…” the card landed at the feet of Sonata, who bended over and retrieved it. Matrix yelped as the palm of the god wrapped around his throat, swinging him into the air with one upward motion of his thick arm. Matrix looked up in fear as he stared eye to eye with the demon god Akiyama. He knew he just sinned…and it was time for repentance. “SORRY, MY LORD! I WILL NOT LET IMPURE THOUGHT CROSS MY MIND AGAIN!” Sonata stood back up straight and turned around, looking at Akiyama hold the youth in the air. “Akira…what are you doing with Matrix?” Akiyama looked at her before dropping Matrix back onto the sofa. Coughing from the second throat attack on him in the past five minutes, he looked over and watched as the god walked silently over to Sonata. She grinned in his face, reaching up and scratching him under his chin. “You stop playin’ around now you hear?” she said sweetly…surprisingly, a tone that the god didn’t tolerate from anyone normally. “We’re gonna go home and have lots of fun like we used to when we were kids! Okay?” Cyclonica cursed under her breath as Akiyama nodded slowly. Then she stood up and walked over to the two of them, stepping in front of Sonata and handing the god his ID card. “Enough time wasted, I say. Cars were speeding along the highway on their way to their destinations. The airport, like any other airport, was busy with people trying to get to their flights or be picked up from their flights. At the front gate, a blue airport van pulled to a stop. From inside, muffled voices could be heard. They stopped for a second before the door slid open. Sonata stepped out, with a yellow parasol raised over her head to keep out the sun. Matrix came out next, his hair dyed a sandy brown aside from his usual jet black. He was dressed in a pair of light blue carpenter jeans with black split toe sandals and a green tee shirt and a gray hoodie peeking out from underneath it. He chuckled, adjusting the tinted green shades on his face as he looked back inside the car. The next sight…you will never see again in your life. Cyclonica stepped out of the van…her black and ice blue hair tinted a solid dark violet color and completely braided in one solid braid behind her back. Her Divine Prophetess garb gone, she wore a purple baby tee with the word GODDESS written across in white glitter, and tight black hip-huggers with a silver belly chain, black open toed heels, and a purple thong exposed at her waist. She stood there in front of the door and grimaced, wiggling her exposed purple-nailed toes uncomfortably. “I feel a fool.” She muttered. “A mother f*cking Neo Void God-damn fool.” Sonata shook her head as she smiled at the Divine Prophetess. “Nonsense! You look pretty! You look like the average teen your age!” she leaned over towards Matrix and whispered to him “How old is she?” “You look like the average 19 year old!” he chuckled, his eyes scanning over her body. “And you’re pretty hot when you’re not in all that creepy crap you wear!” Cyclonica glared up through her black sunglasses, holding up her thumb and forefinger apart. “Don’t think I wont choke you out right here in front of all these people. The god and I have no remorse in making this a violent and bloody mission straight through, instead of starting the fun in Japan.” She heard a grunt come out from behind her, causing her to move out of the way. And the next scene was even more unbelievable than looking at normal Cyclonica. Dressed in a pair of black faux fur jeans, black silver buckled boots, a black silver buckled belt, a long sleeved black shirt unbuttoned three buttons down exposing his chest, and black sunglasses…was the Neo Void God. Looking like he stepped straight out of a scientologist-produced fashion show, the god’s hair was colored a dark brown, and tied back behind him in a ponytail tied by a yellow ribbon. His face was covered in stubble, as if he had yet to shave for months. He stood there with his fists balled up at his sides as he towered over the van. Sonata smiled as she looked adoringly at him. “Now…who’s a good looking god??” she stepped forward and grabbed his wrists, looking up into his eyes with that sweet smile of hers. “YOU ARE! YES YOU ARE!” Cyclonica and Matrix sighed at the same time, their heads lowering as the god stared down at Sonata treating him like that. “Even I’m tired of it after a whole hour of it in the car.” Matrix muttered to Cyclonica. “Come on!” Cyclonica bellowed, waving her hand towards the trunk of the car. “Get the bags and let’s be on our way! Our place is scheduled to leave in about thirty minutes!” Minutes later, Cyclonica and Matrix are walking along the crowded halls of the airport, each of them with a black satchel slung across their backs. Matrix had his briefcase in his hand, and a cell phone in his other hand. Cyclonica growled as she looked back, watching as the god lugged four suitcases in his hand, while Sonata followed closely behind with her parasol still open above her. Matrix watched her mutter something under her breath harshly. “What’s the deal between those two anyway?” he asked Cyclonica, who looked back at him with a blank expression. “That mortal and the god grew up together. They were inseparable, until they took their separate ways after graduate school. She became a fashion consultant…and Akiyama went into the Tokyo Wrestling League. After that…it was Maxis Inc Yakuza hit man…to freelance bodyguard…to Neo Void God.” “So were they an item or something?” Cyclonica shrugged her shoulders as they turned the corner. “The god never told me what happened between them. And I can’t bear to talk with that woman for less than three minutes without wanting to crush her voice box and shut her up. But the god is protective of her for some reason.” Matrix gave her a curious look. “Why don’t you just rape ‘em and get the info yourself?” “Mind-f*cking isn’t that simple, Matrix. It’s a complicated process that you have to prepare for.” She then looked away, back at the god and the mortal behind them. “Plus I can’t rape either one of them…” “What was that?” he asked, grinning. “You can’t do either of them?” She sighed. “No…I tried Akiyama, and before I could even begin something happened between us that sent me flying through a door into the hallway. Cyclonica shook her head at her. “No, they’re with the Pathetisad, of Egypt. Jackass low budget dummies who think they can keep god out of their lives. Holy Wars they say…dumbasses…” The receptionist nodded, putting that weak smile back on her face. “I’ll be sure to advise my superiors.” The Celestials, plus two walked away, throwing their bags onto the baggage carousel and walking away. Matrix patted Cyclonica on her shoulder as they headed for the terminal to board. “Good work back there!” he snickered. Cyclonica gave him a cold glare, which was enough to tell him to remove his hand from her shoulder. Then he looked ahead, and noticed the checkpoint between the terminal and them. What seemed like a SWAT team of Japanese government soldiers stood guard at the terminal. Every passenger going through the metal detector was being given a retina scan with a small hand-held scanner. Cyclonica turned to Matrix and nodded. “To work you go, boy.” She ordered to him. Then she looked back at Akiyama and Sonata, who weren’t far behind, and waved them in closer. Matrix cracked his knuckles as he attached his cell phone to his clip, the four of them standing in like the rest of the passengers. “It’s my time to shine, people.” He grinned. Ahead, two of the Japanese SWAT team looked on ahead, seeing the huge Japanese man towering over the rest of the people. The right one leaned over to the left one and whispered. “Would’ja look at that tall son of a b*tch in line there?” he muttered through his helmet. The left one looked on and nodded slowly. “That’s one tall mothe-.” “Shut your mouth!” the right one hissed suddenly. The left one looked at him baffled. “I’m just talking about that guy!” The right one shook his head. “No! Here comes the Lieutenant!” The two of them stood to attention as a taller man wearing a bulletproof vest and black combat pants and boots walked past them. He turned to them cocking an eyebrow at them and stroking his black beard before moving on. Cyclonica growled as they stepped forward slowly in the line. “What in the f*cking Void is going on?” she burst out suddenly. “The god will not wait for you mortals! The god has a mission of the utmost importance to complete!!” Everyone stared at her…including Matrix and Sonata. Cyclonica folded her arms and sulked back into the cover of the god’s frame. “Well…that is, if the god was here…which he isn’t…cause he’s not…so there.” After a few minutes of standing there, the four of them made it to the metal detector. Cyclonica had it arranged so that Sonata and Matrix went first, followed by herself and the god. It was important that Matrix be in front of them for this to work. Sonata stepped through…no beep. The soldier scanned her eyes….nothing. Matrix stepped through the metal detector…no beep. The soldier scanned his eyes…nothing. Cyclonica stepped through the metal detector…it beeped. The soldier scanned a hand held metal detector over her…and found it to be her silver belly chain. Cyclonica shook her head as they prepared to scan her eyes. Matrix took the time to press a few buttons on his cell phone just as they scanned her eyes. …no beep… The nVg came through next. There was no beep from the metal detector. And just as before, Matrix pressed a few buttons on his cell phone before they scanned his eyes. There was no beep. Everyone was in the clear. But as they walked through…someone stopped them. “Excuse me.” Akiyama and Cyclonica turned around to see the Lieutenant standing behind them stroking his beard. He looked down to Akiyama’s right hand, taped in gauze. “May I see your hand please?” Cyclonica glared at the man with brown contacts as the brown eyed Akiyama held his taped hand out. His other hand was slowly curling into a fist as the lieutenant reached for it. The Divine Prophetess reached back and snatched the cell phone from Matrix. “Let me borrow this.” Then she shoved Matrix into the metal detector and jammed the keypad down on the phone. Suddenly the metal detector goes insane with beeps and alarms. “What the HELL??” Matrix exclaimed as the soldiers rush over to the area as the lieutenant turned to Matrix. “HIM!” the soldiers swarmed upon him and dragged him off. The lieutenant spoke to one of the soldiers, who nodded before reaching into their pocket and pulling out two latex gloves. He snapped them onto his hands and followed, gripping his hands and clenching them tightly. By the time the lieutenant turned back to the god and the Divine Prophetess… They were gone… He cursed as he looked over to the security monitor. A soldier looked at the screen silently, as his superior noticed it as well. The word MATCH appeared on the screen. The Lieutenant’s face lowered as he stared out the window at the Japan Air plane, now boarding with passengers…
