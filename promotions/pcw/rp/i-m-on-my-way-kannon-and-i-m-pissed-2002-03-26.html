I'm on my way Kannon.... And I'm pissed!

26 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3-26-02 One night ago, Jeremy Howard had something powerful to say. If Xavier Kannon was listening, like he had better be, he would have heard the Krazed Lunatic say that he'd break Kannon's f^cking face. When the Krayzie One walks into Turmoil for the first time since Kannon wreaked his havoc on the Howard-Walden Continental title match, will Scientology survive the onslaught of a lunatic on the warpath? If Eleanor Hall hopes to have someone to use her catchy phrases on come Wednesday, Kannon had better pray something detours the psychotic train known as Jeremy Howard. Because, as the scene fades into the inside of Krayzie's 2002 Cadillac Escalade EXT, one can already notice the Continental Champion's less than spectacular attitude. Rather, the Krazed Lunatic is still pissed. Jessica: Honey, its not healthy to always be pissed off. Jeremy, already wearing the black 'Krayzie Till I Die' fans are used to seeing him in during PCW shows, barely glances in Jessica's direction as thoughts come to mind. Jeremy: After I get done with Kannon, I'll quit being pissed. But if he's going to mock my friend and his wife, then I'll be damned if I just stand by. Jessica: I understand that. But, what about Troy, Justin and I? Jeremy whips his brand new Escalade onto the shoulder, slamming on the gas pedal. Jeremy: What do you mean, what about Troy, Justin and you? Jessica turns her entire body toward Jeremy, allowing everyone watching this particular Krayzie promo to see her revealing leather full body suit that lacks certain parts around the breast and navel areas. Jessica: I don't want to argue with you. I'm just saying. Jeremy: Say what you have to say. Jessica sighs. Jessica: You asked for it..... I think you should leave the events of PCW at PCW and not bring them home. I don't want Troy seeing the hostility of your professional life. Jeremy sits in the driver's seat, almost stunned. Jeremy: You trying to say that I'm being too wild around Troy? Jessica: No! I'm saying that you need to keep your hatred for Kannon at PCW and not at home. I don't want Troy seeing it. Its hard to tell what Jeremy is thinking. A silence falls over the Cadillac Escalade EXT, while passing cars honk away at the unmoving vehicle. His face, expressionless. How could the Krayzie One have let such an annoying prick like Xavier Kannon get into his personal life so much, that he's beginning to alienate the one's he loves? Is he really that hostile of a person to be around? Jessica: I'm sorry. I just thought it had to be said. Still, Jeremy's silent. Jessica: I wasn't going to say anything, but Justin thought I should. Jeremy: Justin thought you should say something, and you also? Jessica knods her head, 'yes'. Jeremy: It's nice to see you two have bonded. Jessica: We talk from time to time. Jeremy starts his Escalade back up, pushing the gas pedal in to send the Cadillac back onto the road and on its course toward Tuesday Turmoil. Jeremy: Next time, talk to me first. Jessica: I'm sorry. I didn't know how to approach you about this. I know Kannon has been getting to you lately. Jeremy: He isn't getting to me. His bitch is. Jessica: Her?!?!?! Normally, Jessica is nothing more than eye candy at ringside, while Jeremy is laying his ass beating down on some unfortunate soul. As of late though, she's had to play the role of sideshow fued with Eleanor Hall. Come this Friday at Fusion, Jessica and Eleanor will meet for the first time in the ring when they take part in the mixed tag match with Kannon and Howard. Last night, Krayzie said he had a plan. For Turmoil or Fusion? Jeremy: Every single day I turn on the PCW promo show, I see her making comments about you being a whore or making fun of Natalia. Its about time to put a stop to this bullshit. Jessica: Whose that bitch calling a whore? I'm not the one claiming a religion I know nothing about or providing gang bangs for the entire chapel, just so I have a home to stay in. Things have gotten downright nasty now. In most Xavier Kannon promos, one is sure to see Ellie taking a shot at Jessica. Not anymore. If Ellie is going to play that game, she can expect it right back. Jessica: I mean, its quite obvious she has no clue what Scientology is. To her, its probably something to do with the stars. Two bit crack whore. Jeremy sighs. Jeremy: And you say I'm hostile. Jessica: I keep it away from Troy and home. Jeremy: Alright, I'll keep my hatred for Kannon confined to my office while we're at home. A smile graces Jessica's face. Jessica: Thank you.... Now, lets go and take care of this problem once and for all. Jeremy: Thank f^cking God you said that.... It's about time. In the next four days, Xavier Kannon is going to have to meet up with the Krayzie One twice in the ring. The question is, can he handle the heat he's going to get when this rabid dog is let loose? Jeremy: Time for me to squash this little piss ant like I should have when he stepped to me right after March Madness. I don't have time for ungrateful son of a bitches anymore. With his eyes watching the road with the full intent on getting to Turmoil, Krayzie's mind begins slipping back into his hatred for Kannon. With his personal life set on how he needs to act in front of his two year old son, his professional life is now open for all the hostility in the world. And, when he steps into the ring tonight, thats everything Corey Williams and Xavier Kannon should expect. Knowing that, the scene fades away to Krayzie driving his Cadillac Escalade toward Turmoil... to black!
