RAH in High School-Part 2 of Many-The Perfect Boy Meets Girl Morning

18 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Title: RAH in High School-Part 2 of Many-The Perfect Boy Meets Girl Morning Featuring: Rent-a-Hero Date: 8-18-2002 'A long time ago....during the ancient year of 1997....when R.Kelly was 'bumpin and grindin'' older women, Steve Austin was a fresh new face in the WWF, Beanie Babies ran rampant throughout the world....and Power Rangers were JUST starting to go Aqua....there was a normal town in The City, USA....Normalville, USA. Those were the days....the days before I even fathomed becoming a PCW superstar. Back then, my dreams were WAAAAAAAAAAY more feasible...marry Carmen Elektra, go back in time to save Krypton from exploding...and have my own private Rent-Cave. But first...I had to conquer my greatest challenge ever. More challenging then the Laser Lesbians of Licktoria....and even the dreaded MUSIC MASTER....I had to survive...THE 12TH GRADE!! Yes yes, I know...between the battles with acne, popularity, and fashion....even the greatest of superpowers never seemed like enough in those days. When I think of my high school days, I always think of that last week...that Saturday was prom...and there I was....just a normal young city boy....on a typical Wednesday....' ..::/::..The camera pans slowly to the right as the scene opens up, the suns rays beaming down on the cast-iron metal sign driven into the ground next to the concrete road, labeling the neighborhood up ahead as 'Normalville' in fancy cursive writing as the camera continues to delve further into the neighborhood. It looks to be the picture of normal, 'Beaver Cleaver' type innocence, the houses all nice and beautiful and coming in either sky blue, cream white, or light shades of golden yellow that beldn in perfectly with the slivers of the suns ray at this time of day. The lawns are all cut, lush and green with the soft feel of a carpet, ALL the residents taking pride in their picture perfect, Norman Rockwell neighborhood as the sun begins to rise higher and higher in the sky, ready to take its rightful place in the heavens above all men and women below. One house in particular seems to interest the camera more then anything....the house clearly labeled '1234' via the black numbers on the roof. As the camera comes closer and closer to the house, a loud male voice can be heard from within sternly yelling..::/::..Loud Male Voice: JAYSON...JAYSON!!! GET DOWN HERE BOY!!! ..::/::..The voice seems to grow louder and louder as the we now find ourselves within the house, in one room in particular actually....the walls littered with Image, Marvel, and DC comic superheroes while the floor is cluttered with Wizard magazines and other graphical literature. Panning up to the king sized bed holed up in the right corner of the meager bedroom, the window right above it blocking out any sembelance of the sun via the Superman emblem curtains. Looking at the bed, the sheets have been tossed and turned all over, a set of muscular legs poking out from the 'wreckage' and a well developed arm hanging over the side of the bed as their seems to be someone lost in Slumberland under the covers, wishing to be undisturbed....but that wish is shortly broken as the door is kicked wide open, standing within the arch being a husky man with a sruffy brown and grey beard and wearing a white dress shirt and slacks, his eyes locked upon the bed as he takes slow steps towards it, his feet SWAMPED in comci. He clenches his hands upon the sheets, tearing them away from the bed as he unveils a young teenager with a lightly tanned, ripped body, long blonde locks of hair flowing down to the mid point of his shoulder, and a pair of Batman emblem boxers; his face remains buried within the bed comforter. Annoyed quite enough, the husky man exlcaims..::/::..'Ah yes...my father. He worked hard at 'City Cracker Factory' for over 20 years...at least until the Black Panthers came and burned it down, they thought the factory name was code for a KKK meeting house, silly Panthers. Even though he never seemed to understand my cause to fight for truth and justice, he loved me with all his heart. I've only seen my father smile once in life...all the rest of the times he's been yelling, screaming, sighing, or simply quiet out of sheer annoyance or anger and people always took that as a fault on him....BUT HE WAS MY ANGRY, QUIET, NEVER SMILING DAD DAG-NAB IT!! I could never forget how he used to wake me up either....it made any son proud to have a father like him....'Man: JAYSON, GET UP!! YOUR GOING TO MISS SCHOOL!!' ..::/::..The loud exclamation shocks the young teen from his bed, leaping high into the air before falling and hitting his face on the floor, the husky man before him sighing and nodding his head in psuedo-shame. Jayson seems desperate to cover his face, leaping up to his feet with one hand covering the width of his face, while the other swings wildly in front of him as if to subdue an unknown attacker as he exclaims..::/::.. Jayson:'I CAN'T BELIEVE IT...GIGANTOR HAS BREACHED THE DEFENSES TO MY SECRET LAIR SO HE CAN FIND OUT MY SECRET IDENTITY!! WELL STAND BACK BEFORE YOU'RE KARATICIZED YOU EVIL FIEND OF GIGANTICNESS!! NO ONE SHALL FIND OUT MY SCER....Oh wait...hehe...hey dad.' ..::/::..Jayson glows red with embarassment, quickly whipping his head around to the bed and picking up a thin, black mask in the style of Robin. He puts it on, turning back to his father to reveal a handsome young man with the deepest of blue eyes and a smile that could rival the purest of pearls, standing with his hands to his hips as he looks to his father, who seems much more in touch with reality then his offspring..::/::.. Jayson:'Don't scare me like that father...do you realize how many villains have tried to break into my secret residence?! Come on, what happened to the passcode? 'The Red-Kool Aid gets drank by the ghetto man'?'Jayson's Dad:'Oh will you cut it out with that 'Duperman' stuff and for the last time...WHAT IS WITH THE SILLY MASK?!' ..::/:>.Jayson jumps back, startled at his father's bashing of his beloved mask as he cltches one hand to his heart and the other has now rocketed high in the air, Jayson replying..::/::.. Jayson:'Father! I am appaled! How could you say such things about my mask? The same mask I fight innane amounts of crime in. Need I remind you that I keep the streets of Normalville SAFE for old ladies, little children, and the average Joe Schmoe here! You should be proud to have a son endowed with powers like mine...' Jayson's Dad:'You mean the power to sleep in late on school days, spend more than half his allowance on comic books, and make up confusing words that NOBODY can understand!?!' Jayson:'Father, enough with your contabulating and flatulatiming of my powers! Now I must wash up and prepare to recieve my free education under the guise of my mild-mannered alter-ego....JAYSON --' Jayson's Father: *cuts Jayson off( 'Look...just get dressed and check the mail before you leave...' *under his breath* 'God, I work hard, live good...why couldn't I have normal kids?'..::/::..Jayson's father leaves, the youthful teen yet to be known as 'Rent-A-Hero' throwing his arm in air and smiling wide before exclaming within the confies of his 'lair'..::/::..Jayson:'NO DAY OF SCHOOL IS IMPOSSIBLE FOR ME TO SURVIVE...FOR I AM....SUPER SPANDEX TEEN!! Wait no...too long....ummm.....JAYSON-MAN....okay, that's just lame....JASON JUPITER....do I look like I wrestled in CCW? We'll work on that name thing later I suppose. TO THE BATHROOM!!'..::/::..Making all kinds of silly 'whir' sounds, Jayson races out of his room...preparing to wash up in preperation for his trek to school..::/::..Shortly......::/::..We now go outside to the front lawn of Jayson as he walks out to the neighborhood standard black mailbox, his stride tall and proud as he now dons a pair of wide-legged JnCO jeans and a Green Lantern emblem tanktop, his hair tied up in a nice ponytail as he rounds to his mailbox and opens it, finding nothing within as he says to himself..::/::.. Jayson:'Must be a slow day...no fan mail, letter bombs, or electronic sonic devices from Doctor Servo.....go figure.' ..::/::..Jayson closes the mailbox door, turning around.....and that one turns freezes him in place as emerging from her house right across from our young hero...is none other then a beautiful brunette with satin soft brown eyes, her lips full and lucious and her body the perfect mix of angelic sculpture and seductive curvature. Her books are held tight to her chest as the split of her jean skirt flies within the wind...giving Jayson a slight hint of her gorgeous legs as she steps out upon her grass, the wind playfully breezing through her hair as the world seems to slow down for him..::/::..'You know how they always talk about 'the girl next door'? Well I had the 'girl across the street'...Erica Angel. To this day, she is the most beautiful and perfect girl to me. Smart, funny, and her looks rivaled that of Elektra. Every time I looked at her, I felt I was cheating on my graphical literature goddess. She was so perfect. Everyone loved Erica and I can't remember a single person having any kind of strife with her. Myself...I always envisioned sweeping her off her feet and soaring high in the sky with her like on the first Superman movie...but unfortuantely, Superman got ALL the good Kryptonian powers and all I got were the cool costumes, figures, they always liked Kal-El more. I swear...everytime I saw her, music played.' ..::/:..And just like those classic movie moments of 'boy meets girl', music plays in the background....'I Want You' by Savage Garden to be exact, fitting perfectly to the feelings being held for her by young Jayson. As the music plays...he actually turns her head from her for only a moment, looking to the side to actually find SAVAGE GARDEN on his front lawn! The lead singer, Darren, sings with painstakingly real feeling as he continues the song, Jayson looking at him and the full band behind him with an inqusitive look..::/::..Darren: *singing* 'Ooooooooo I WANT YOU, I DON'T KNOW IF I NEED YOU BUT, Ooooooooo I'D DIE TO FIIIND OUT...' Jayson:'WHOA WHOA WHOA WHOA!! What are you guys doing on my lawn!' Darren:'Oh oh...we're sorry mate, just thought it'd add a little extra effect. Besides...our CD is bombing right now anyways, we're playing anywhere we can get.'Jayson:'Ahh...well if that's the case....continue.'..::/::..As instructed, Darren picks the song back up from where he left off, Jayson doing a silly swagger to the song for a little until the unexpected happens...THE SIREN BY THE NAME OF ERICA BEGINS TO WALK HIS WAY!! Jayson is frozen in his tracks, staring at the Normalville goddess as she slowly strides towards him, his mind a jumble of feelings and emotions...unable to think of what to say as the scene freezes right there..::/::..WHAT WILL HAPPEN TO OUR HORMONE DRIVEN TEENAGE HERO?! WHY IS ERICA APPROACHING HIM?! HOW MUCH FOR SAVAGE GARDEN TO PLAY MY SISTER'S BIRTHDAY PARTY?! FIND OUT....SAME JAYSON TIME...SAME JAYSON CHANNEL!!! ---- View Rent-a-Hero's Biography
