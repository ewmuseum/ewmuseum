Reconstruction: Part II

22 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: May 22, 2002 OOC Note: Short, because Part III is coming soon. I don’t want to wear you out with having so much to read. : ) No man knew when The Heartbreakers began their slow and heavy weaving from shore to shore, that it was held and guided by the great hand of Fate. Fate alone saw the meaning of the web it wove, the might of it, and its place in the making of a tag team’s and the world's history. Men thought but little of either web or weaving, calling them by other names and lighter ones, for the time unconscious of the strength of the thread thrown across thousands of miles of leaping, heaving, red or blue blood. Fate and Life planned the weaving, and it seemed mere circumstance which guided the Heartbreakers to and fro between two worlds divided by a couple of championship belts -- the belts of a bitter quarrel deepened by hatred and the shedding of friend’s blood. Between the two worlds of Winners and Losers there was no will to draw nearer. Each held apart. Those who had rebelled against that which their skills called tyranny, having struggled madly and shed blood in tearing themselves free from the “other” federations, turned stern backs upon their unconquered enemies, broke all cords that bound them to the past, flinging off ties of name, kinship and rank, beginning with fierce disdain a new life. Those who, being rebelled against, found the rebels too passionate in their determination and too desperate in their defense of their wills to become champions, to be no less than unconquerable, weny back haughtily to the world which seemed so far the greater power. Plunging into new battles, they added new conquests and splendor to their reign of PINK!, looking back with something of contempt to the half-savage title reign left to build its own civilization without other aid in the history books. It has been almost 10 days since the Heartbreakers were STRIPPED of their titles, for lack of a better word. They were mugged of the championship. On a better note however, they went out undefeated, unconquered...they went out as true champions should. Handing the belts downward because there was no greater than than them. The scene opens up on a golf course, the same golf course where Jack Valentine cut his first promo back in PWA, under the guidance of Johnny Heart. They are out on the first tee and the Starter tells them that it is okay to go ahead and swing. Jack has on a PINK! vest with a white shirt on underneath to go with his hot PINK! pants and PINK! shoes. Johnny on the other hand is wearing a PINK! shirt underneath his heavenly glow of white of everything else. Jack steps up and takes a swing...he slices it way right and it plops in the water making an ever so gentle splash. “Damnit,” Jack exclaimed, “Every damn time I come to this course I get more and more pissed off!” “Why do you think I brought you here,” Johnny started. “All this pent up aggression that you’ll carry into our match in a few days...it’ll be great, and keep in mind Jack...we HAVE to win this match if we even want to be considered as contenders for those titles.” “Contenders? Ha. We are the top contenders from where I stand...hell, no one ever beat us for those titles, and I’ll be damned if anyone says we weren’t the greatest champions EVER!” Jack proclaimed. “Excuse me sirs, but I’ve been known to watch a few PCW events in my day and I can tell you that you are NO...” the starter started to say before being interrupted by Johnny Heart. “You’re not excused, so shut your mouth old man...no one said you could talk,” Johnny yelled. “Now if you don’t mind, I’m trying to hit this shot, so please be quiet.” Johnny sets up a PINK! ball on his PINK! tee and takes his PINK! headcover off of his...well, normal colored club. He lines up and laces one right down the middle of the fairway. “Great shot partner,” Jack says as he pats Johnny on the behind. The starter looks on in disgust at the public display of well, whatever it is that Jack and Johnny are. The two hop in their personal PINK! golf cart and drive off down the fairway. Jack and Johnny detested the starter that worked at this golf course. Hell, they disliked pretty much everyone who they came into contact with...especially this tag team that they were facing on Friday, the Dark Messiahs. But these two wrestlers awakened in them more than mere dislike. These two brought out pure hatred. Maybe it was because Jack and Johnny were in a little bit of fear. How bad would it look for the most recent tag team champions to lose in the first round to a couple of rookies? It would turn the color PINK! into a black day for The Heartbreakers...that’s what it would do. As Jack and Johnny approach their balls, Johnny smiles and mocks Jack. “Where’s your ball hot stuff,” Johnny said. “Very funny, shut your mouth flamer,” Jack responded. The two carried on in their very feminine fashion while looking for Jack’s ball. There it sat in the middle of the water near a sign that said “reconstruction: do not enter.” Jack tried to reach for his PINK! ball but failed in his efforts. Jack got pissed and continued on a cussing rampage for several minutes before the ranger came over to Jack and told him that he needs to keep moving because there are people waiting to tee off. “Oh really, well ladi-frickin da!” Jack screamed, making an ever so flamboyant scene on the first fairway. “Are those people more important than me?” The ranger looked down at his feet and responded... “Well, no sir...” “Do you like those people better than me,” Jack asked. No response from the ranger. “Well, tell them they can either wait until I get my PINK! ball back, or they can have my PINK! fist shoved straight up their asses!” Jack shouted. A very peculiar pause came over the screen, in what was a very awkward moment for everyone. The ranger just drove off, with little comment at all and rode back to the first tee as Jack continued his search. The people began shouting and screaming at Valentine who through even more of a temper tantrum to the delight of Johnny Heart. “There you go Jack, build up that anger. Think about the Dark Messiahs shouting at you!” Johnny told him. “Shut up Heart!” Jack said. Jack began waiving his arms and shouting at the people back on the first tee. All of the sudden a ball came flying by and came inches away from Jack’s foot. Jack went and picked up the ball and tossed it into the lake. “How do you like that, faggots?!?” Jack ironically said. Two very big black men came driving down in their cart and climbed out. They stood nose to nose with Jack and Johnny, and Johnny standing at a whopping 6’5”, only reached the shorter one’s chin. Just as they started screaming, a TO BE CONTINUED... sign flashed on the screen as it faded to PINK!
