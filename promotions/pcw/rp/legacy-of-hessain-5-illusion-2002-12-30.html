Legacy Of Hessain 5: Illusion

30 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 30/12/02 Recap: Hessain is taking a slight detour from his path to Skitzrapto's keep. A lunatic beggar has told him of a disguise that would aid him, despite the fact that the ones who made it were the gypsies. In an effort to move around more easily, Hessain travels to Vasserbunde, just south of Skitzrapto's retreat in search of this spell. * * * Legacy of Hessain Part 5: Illusion Night had fallen once again, with the sky mildly illustrated in the flames of the gypsy campfires. I stood carefully behind a rock, scraping my exposed skull against the crumbling stone. A few of the gypsies, dressed in their charming clothes, danced and drank happily. Intoxicated humans have already been an easy target and my appetite was yet to be sated, but I would need to slip by unnoticed rather than disturbing them. For the gypsies are harbourers of many half-truths, they are also carriers of potent magic. I slid among the shadows around them, gazing at their bright, gaudy tents. The colours of orange and green were no doubt used to attract the eyes of the weary traveler in need of shelter... for a price. To me, they served only as distraction. I knelt slowly as best I could without disturbing the festivities, peering underneath the flapping cloth of one of their tents. Against the shadows prancing outside, I looked around the interior. Laden with mainly odds and ends from the gullible souls that surrendered their belongings for the false hope and misinformation that the gypsies gave, I knew that no such magic would come from these pathetic items. Lamps that explorers would light their paths with, weapons, bottles of unknown... and perhaps best left that way... liquid. I was about to leave, when I heard a clatter behind me. In front of me, barely able to stand and white as my own skin was a young gypsy girl, barely the age for ale and gazing at me in confusion. She fainted inside the tent. At that moment, the instincts that Morgazius had implanted inside me took over. I took slow strides over to the slumped body, ensuring that nobody was about to follow the girl. The rest of the tricksters were too busy exercising their collective libido in front of a raging fire. I dragged the girl further inside and set her up against a chest containing what looked like a pile of old books. As her elbow smacked onto the edge, she shook loose a packet of what appeared to be tarot cards. I picked them up, hoping to leave things as I had found them... for the most part. I noticed a curious thing... all the cards bore a peasant with his face turned away. Turning the card over in my hand, I noticed a wax seal... it was in red wax and had a rune stamped into it. This... this must have been what I was looking for! 'Unnh...' the girl stirred. I had little time left... wiping the seal off with my thumb, a blue glow floated from the card and wisped around my arms. In my mind, I felt the threat of being discovered from the girl's screams and wished for a way to avoid the situation... and in an instant, I was surrounded in this pale cerulean ether. I could feel myself changing, as though a sack of silk were being pulled tight over my entire body. When the aura faded, I looked at myself and saw that my clothes had changed from the hell-born armour that the Necroprankster had given me, to the attire of the peasant on the card. With the seal broken, I hid the card inside my boot as the youngster's eyes fluttered open in a disoriented fashion. 'Am... I... Where... am I?' she whispered. 'Rest thine eyes, little one. You are sleepy and to recline would do you well.' I said in the most soothing tone I could muster. Her dilated pupils were covered by my hand, enforcing my suggestion. She simply groaned and gently brushed my palm away, trying to make herself comfortable. With as much silence as I could gather, I lifted up the flap of the tent and snuck back out, leaving the sleeping nymph there. As quickly as I could, I scuttled from the camp and up to the north of the camps. Once again, I would have to travel this path of woodland and gravel with an empty stomach. My strength was fading and I would need to find a method of sustenance soon, lest my death become permanent. Luckily, from across the wooden bridge ahead of me, I heard chatter and laughter. I looked ahead, seeing a bright light pouring from all the windows of the building ahead. I could not distinguish the name on the side of the building, but the sounds that reached my ears from the top window made it all too clear that this was an establishment for Vasserbunde's immoral liaisons. It was also a perfect place to scream while somebody ate you... perfect! As I rounded the corner, I noticed a man held guard over the entrance. His impatient look led me to believe he would be looking for trouble. This would be a true test of the gypsy's magic. I sauntered along, putting on the most lecherous face I could. The guard looked down at me along his nose, clearly looking for any excuse he could find to draw the dagger that his palm lay on. With a surly smile, I crossed the threshold. 'Evening, sir...' I hissed. He gave no other acknowledgement of my presence other than spitting on the floor. A far less offensive gesture than what he could have done. As I entered the brothel, a lady... if that really was a suitable term... in a garment that showed far more than it covered, latched onto my arm. Her hand snaked around my belt... but it was the pouch of coins that my disguise gave me she fondled. In reality, she grasped the Serioli sword and if not for my hand diverting hers, my disguise may have been broken. I hastily pushed her hand to my chest and led her towards the staircase. The guard outside was still fixing his eyes on me. His instincts were keen, but thankfully, privacy was an abundant right, here. As we staggered into a vacant room, the whore wasted no time in disrobing the flimsy dress and pushing me into the four poster bed. If she tried to remove my clothes, the spell would break and I would be revealed. I had no choice but to make my move. As she leaned onto me, I quickly sprang up to her neck and sunk my fangs into her neck. 'Ooooooh, sweet sanguine relief....' I moaned. The girl moaned too, except the penetration she was looking for was not the one I gave. As I took more of her life to prolong my own twisted version of it, I was not aware that slowly, my ragged tunic was melting away to the floor like liquid and my armour below was baring itself. The wretch opened her eyes to a startling sight of cold, white clammy flesh... and as I took the last of her blood, she let out a scream that resonated in my ears like a banshee's shattering cry... And one that the guard outside as well as the ones in the hut across the bridge heard and were heeding to...
