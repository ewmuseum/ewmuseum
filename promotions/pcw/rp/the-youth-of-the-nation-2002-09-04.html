The Youth Of The Nation

4 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 04/09/02 “Whoa... I’m like, SO getting some “VILLAGE OF THE DAMNED” vibes here!” Our shot opens up on the shocked face of PCW Commissioner Eleanor Hall who’s wide, Doe-esque eye’s pupils are reflecting a series of children walking in perfect formation. “I know... Amazing isn’t it.” Zooming out we see the 1st couple of PCW looking at a small village high up in the Hubbardville mountains. Series of stone paths connect wooden huts, and along them walk groups of 10 children spilt into 2 columns. Xavier : “The camp still needs a bit of the make-over but with the extra revenue from the Front Office Tax, we should be able to spruce it up.” Each of the children is wearing an “I’M A HAPPY SCIENTOLOGIST” t-shirt and have matching expressions of contentment on their faces, not even fazed by seeing the great Gold Patron Meritorious watching over them. Eleanor : “So how come this place is, like, secret?” More and more children emerge from the huts and walk in eerie time towards another hut, under no supervision. Xavier : “Well after all the fuss about the Catholic Priests holding young children in Mountain retreats away from people.. And Police Paedophilia units... We thought we’d better keep this place under-wraps. Trust the MAJORITY religions and their child-beefing ways to try and screw things up for us.” Ellie~! tuts and shakes her head. Eleanor : “Up... For... You!” The couple walk further into the village and stop outside a hut where a lesson seems to be in progress, the adult voice addressing the group of young Hubbard followers and they all reply as one to her. “What religion are you?” “I’M A SCIENTOLOGIST.“ “What if I say you can’t be?” “DON’T OPPRESS MY RIGHT TO FREE EXPRESSION OF FAITH.“ “What if I say that your parents are trying to brainwash you?” “IT IS THE MAJORITY RELIGIONS THAT BRAINWASH.“ “Good, very good. I see this “Dealing With Social Services” lesson is going to be easy for you.” The CEO smiles warmly and nods his head, please at the activity and it’s success going on in the classroom while Ellie~! looks a cross between worried and bemused. Eleanor : “You aren’t, like, building an Army of Genetically Engineered Super Children to take over the World are you?” He looks to her oddly as they walk away from the classroom’s window. Xavier : “Er... No. This place is a retreat where children of Scientologist’s from outside Hubbardville can come to escape the interfering of the outside World.” The prep breathes a sigh of relief. Eleanor : “Ah.. Okay, because us preps, like, SO wanted to take over the world by ourselves... It’s fashion sense needs some serious Genocide.” While Ellie~! imagines the whole planet decked our in Burberry, they get to the outside of another hut where the lesson in progress inside can be heard. “Who’s works do you read?” “I READ AND FOLLOW THE WORKS OF L. RON HUBBARD.“ “What if I tell you that you have to read C.S. Lewis instead?” “I HAVE THE RIGHT TO READ WHATEVER LITERATURE I FREELY CHOOSE TOO.“ “What if I say that Hubbard’s work is all a scam?” “IT IS FAR MORE BELIEVABLE THAN THE FICTIONAL WORLD OF NARNIA AND A TALKING LION NAMED ASLAN.“ “Do your parents force you to read Hubbard?” “MY CHOICE IS AS FREE AS MY RIGHT TO FOLLOW WHICH EVER RELIGION I CHOOSE.“ “Good, good... You’re starting well. You already look like you can handle yourselves if you ever have to go to an oppressive “Non-Scientological” school.” Ellie~! seems quite taken with the whole thing. Eleanor : “Cool..... They’re, like, Brainwashed Furbies!!!” She makes her hands into a beak and starts to talk in a monotone voice while wobbling from side to side. Eleanor : “I... Am.... A.... Sci.... En... Tol... O... Gist!” Her giggly, pale Blue eyes look up to Kannon’s stern face and she stops, trying not to laugh. Eleanor : “Sorry...” They walk onto another path and follow it between the huts, spaced out between lush Green lawns. Xavier : “These children are Scientology’s future.... Their wages will go on to buy the tapes and self-help lectures in years to come... Then their children shall follow and the Scientological race shall rise up from the squalor that is mankind.” Ellie~! grins. Eleanor : “Not if the preps get their first!” The path they are taking passes another hut where the teacher is in the middle of another lesson. “Scientology ruins the family unit.” “SCIENTOLOGY SAVED MY FATHER FROM SUICIDE AND MADE OUR LIVES BETTER.“ “It’s broken up families with it’s desire for follower’s money.” “BECAUSE OF SCIENTOLOGY I STILL HAVE TWO PARENTS.“ “It forces it’s children to follow the faith with repercussions for the parents if they don’t.” “JUST BECAUSE TWO GENERATIONS HAVE SEEN THE RIGHT PATH DOESN’T MEAN IT WASN’T MY FREE CHOICE.“ “Scientology is one of society’s evils.” “SOCIETY IS SOCIETY’S BIGGEST EVIL.“ “Great job children! You’re already up to level 4 on the “How to deal with Anti-Scientology” propaganda!” The Gold Patron Meritorious glows with pride and has to wipe the formation of a tear from his right eye. Xavier : “They are... They are.. The youth of the nation...” The proud Scientologist puts his arm around his wife and they walk across the stretch of lush Wisconsin grass towards the Hubbardville Mountain pass as the shot fades out to Black. “That song, like, sucked... Link them to an O-Town song or something....” “I’m afraid “Liquid Dreams” is already used for Catholic Priests’ thought about their young followers.” “Ewwwwwwwwwzies!”
