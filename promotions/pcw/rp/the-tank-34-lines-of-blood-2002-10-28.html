The Tank 34 - Lines of Blood

28 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: October 28, 2002 ﻿*********The Tank —34—********* Lines of Blood Streaky or in capsulesTransportation is kingThe only question is the destination ~Leigh Donovan Thursday, October 24 - Day 113 “It’s not like I would even be able to recognize them if I saw them now,” Harvey Blake quietlysobs in the confines of his cell. Jake Hooper, his young skin head companion, nods while sittingon the bunk next to the weeping one-eyed Nazi leader. “That doesn’t make it any fucking easierthough. It’s just so hard to believe...” “I can’t even fucking imagine, Harv,” Jake sighs, shaking his head. “Jesus,” whispers Harvey while wiping his lone tearful eye. “My kids... my parents... even myfucking ex-wife... fucking gone. One gunshot each - right in the forehead. Sick fuckingbastards...” Harvey puts his head down to his knees and weeps openly, leaving Jakeuncomfortably wondering what the best gesture would be in this situation. In fact, the only sure thing to either of them was that seven people were dead - shot downefficiently and within hours of each other, despite the fact that they were spread in separatelocations. First, his parents were found by a family friend who grew suspicious when Harvey’sfather hadn’t shown up for a meeting with his rifle club. The body of his ex-wife and her new boyfriend were found next at a hotel room that they hadfailed to check out of on time. No reports of gun fire had been turned in at the time of theshooting. Harvey’s daughter was discovered in her car outside the diner where she worked, the seatbeltover one shoulder. Reports were made indicating that the blood splattered clear to the backwindshield. By this time, the police had connected the dots and realized that this was not an attack on any ofthese individuals, but on Harvey Blake himself. That’s when they went in search of his twosons, each of whom were found dead. The eldest found in the bathtub of his apartment and theyoungest in the alley behind a building that he was known to buy crack at. “One thing’s for certain,” Jake says, putting a consoling hand on Harvey’s shoulder. “Someonein here set this shit up. How the fuck else can it be explained? It wasn’t just a kid. It wasn’tjust your fucking parents. It was your whole fucking family, Harv. All of them!” “Don’t you think I see that!?” Harvey explodes, standing up from his bunk and pulling Jake offit. Even in his middle age, and seemingly physically inferior to Jake, Harvey is able to hurl theyounger and larger man to the floor. Jake sits there for a moment, staring up in horror at thepain-filled face of the one-eyed monster. “Don’t you think I put that shit together as soon asthey told me!? You smug little prick!” “Harvey, I didn’t mean nothing by it...” “Fuck you!” Harvey exclaims. “I’ve been in this game a hell of a lot longer than you have son,and I don’t need you doing my math for me!” Cautiously, Jake slowly rises to his feet. “Who do you think set it up?” “Are you kidding me?” Harvey says, his lone eye welling with tears once more as it looks acrossthe gape in the circular cell balcony to J-Thug’s cell. “Who the fuck kills six people in coldblood!? I’ll tell you who - animals! Jesus Christ! This has nigger written all over it!” Thursday, October 24 - Day 113 Jason Snow sits quietly in his cell with his face buried in his hand. Every breath is a sigh. He’s long since heard the news that Harvey Blake’s family was murdered, in its entirety, thismorning. Snow knows immediately who’s responsible - the Italian mafia. Shane Hood, theyoung man who attempted to make Vincent Giovanni’s phone call yesterday was only able tospit out one guilty party in Doug Hattoni’s murder yesterday before he was apprehended by theon-duty C.O. That name was Harvey Blake. Tim McDonald’s plan worked brilliantly yesterday. He caused the commotion needed for animmediate lock down, and Hood’s phone call ended briefly. Still, he was able to give the dangerous murderers on the other end of that call one name. Theowner of that name saw his entire family exterminated. Snow can’t help but be glad that the list of names Vincent Giovanni gave young Shane Hoodwas in alphabetical order. Had he said the name Jason Snow instead of Harvey Blake, Snowknows that he would be in solitaire for the rest of his life, his family would be completely wipedout, and Vincent Giovanni would be lying limp in the middle of Unit B with his heart rippedfrom his chest. Still, despite being thankful for his luck, Snow feels guilt. As much as he hates Harvey Blakeand everything the balding Nazi stands for, he can’t imagine the pain the man must be goingthrough at this instant. He remembers the way it felt when he received the phone call informing him that his wifemiscarried their second child - it would have been a son. The pain was unbearable. How muchmore would it have hurt had the voice on the other end informed him that not only had theirbeen a miscarried child, but his wife was dead. His sister-in-law was dead. Even his infantdaughter, Haley. He knows that it would have sent him into an unimaginable state of oblivion,one that he would never have the strength to pull himself out of. “Concentrating on your big match, are you mate?” Leigh Donovan snickers as he enters the cellthey share. Snow hasn’t actually thought of the match all morning. Since losing the PCW Championshipnearly half a year ago, his thoughts have usually been constantly consumed with the prospect ofregaining it. But now, forced to realize that a stroke of luck is the only reason why his family isstill breathing, the title seems to have lost a little of it’s gleam. Besides, once Snow gets in the ring, professionalism and prizes are forgotten. When he sees theman or men in the ring with him, he sees nothing more than opponents in a death fight. Withevery ounce of his strength and will, he wants nothing, in that moment, but to destroy everythingwithin reach. When the matches are over, his mind turns to titles and the prospect of glory. Now, however, in the cold confines of his cell, waiting for the lights to turn out before he’stransported to Fusion in the morning, he can thinking of nothing but be thankful that his familyis still alive. “Not exactly,” Snow answers coldly, not bothering to take his hands from his face. “You ain’t getting soft on me, are you mate?” Leigh asks with a smirk, his head cocked to theside. Snow would like nothing more than to slap the Australian rockstar from time to time. Henotices Leigh is sporting a second Aryan tattoo. This one, an iron cross, is large and bold, lyingon his forearm for everyone to see. “Just shut the fuck up and go to bed, you Nazi fuck,” Snow responds, finally taking his face fromhis hands and reaching down to pull his Snake Skin boots off. He sighs and collapses on hisupper bunk, silently wondering what must be going through Harvey Blake’s sick mind. Hedidn’t even know what was going on, Snow thinks. Both Snow and Tim McDonald were wellaware of the deadly games being played. He had no chance, whatsoever, to defend himself. “Whatever you say, boss,” Leigh smirks, kicking his loafers into the corner of the cell. “I’ve gotan early meeting with Harv. One of the boys says he’s got a job for me.” “Fucking Nazi,” Snow spits, not bothering to gauge Leigh’s reaction. “Hey,” Leigh snaps, his Australian accent lacing his serious tones. “Harvey Blake’s amotherfucking God in this place. And he could make things a hell of a lot easier for youif you just let him.” “If I painted motherfucking swastikas on my body and burned crosses outside the cell?” Snowsnickers, though his voice is harshly serious. “Fuck that. Fuck that, fuck you, and fuck all thatbull shit. If you want to salute the fucking fuhrer, or whatever you bastards do, go right the fuckahead. I’m not going to look out for your ass anymore. But keep that Nazi bullshit out of myface. I don’t want you to so much as mention that one-eyed fucker’s name around me.” By theend of the speech, Snow realizes that his empathy for Harvey Blake does nothing to soften hishatred for the man. Snow’s not empathetic for the man, but the situation. “It’s sad,” Leigh says, regurgitating a line heard from Harvey Blake earlier, “when blue eyes goblind.” “Fuck you, Donovan.” Friday, October 25 - Day 114 Despite Snow’s wishes, Harvey Blake arrives at the cell no sooner than roll call ends. The one-eyed leader of the Aryan gang has regained some of his color, though for the most part, hisshocked state is still written all over his face and woven into his body language. He hasn’t eatensince hearing the news and was unable to sleep last night. This morning, however, he vowedthat the time for crying was over - now is the time for revenge. “What the hell do you want?” Snow asks the Nazi no sooner than he steps into the open cell. “What kind of a welcome is that?” Harvey asks, feigning a smile. Behind the balding Nazi,Snow spots his shadow approaching in the form of Jake Hooper, the large young companion ofthe Aryan leader. “Who the fuck said you were welcome?” Snow sneers. “Not me - so get the fuck out.” “I’m not here for you, you disrespectful prick,” Harvey fires. “But I will some day, and I’ll becarrying a nigger-lover’s noose - you can mark my words on that.” “Anytime, Nazi,” Snow counters. “Anytime.” Finally, Leigh Donovan enters the conversation, his toothbrush handle still sticking awkwardlyout of his mouth. “I think Mr. Blake and I would like to speak alone, Jay.” He smilessarcastically at Snow - very few people have lived through calling him ‘Jason.’ ‘Jay’ is groundsfor prolonging death through torture. “You want to talk alone?” Snow spits back. “Get the fuck out then. Talk all you fucking want.” “I believe the young man is asking you to leave, Snow,” Harvey says, his lone cold eye fixed onSnow’s face. Jake Hooper has entered the cell now, and is standing tall with his arms crossedover his massive chest - he’s been growing at the same rate as Snow since entering the Tank. “Snow!” a new voice enters the bellows. Snow instinctively glares at Jake Hooper. When hefinds Hooper unresponsive, he realizes the voice came from yet another party. Standing justoutside the cell is a C.O. holding a pair of cuffs in his left hand and a baton in his right. “Timeto go to the airport. Snow blows a hot puff of air through his nostrils at Harvey. “Looks like you got off easy,” hesays in a quiet hateful voice. No matter how much he feels for this man after his lost, he can’thelp but be filled with contempt by the very sight of him. The Aryan symbols covering hisbody... the bright red swastika that’s sewn onto his eye patch... the man’s very soul is somethingworth hating. Without another word, he steps by Harvey Blake, and allows himself to be handcuffed by theguard. Another guard will be waiting at the truck, he knows. “Now that your waste of good skin cellmate is gone,” Harvey sighs, taking a seat on Leigh’slower bunk, “I’ve got a job for you.” “Great,” Leigh says with a smile as he pulls a white wife-beater tank top over his head andworks out the creases around his abdomen in back. He’s been waiting for a chance to provehimself to Harvey Blake - to prove to the entire Unit that he’s not the same pretty boy Australianprag who entered the Tank nearly four months ago. “As you may or may not have heard,” Blake sighs while his lone blue eye roams the floor, “myentire family was wiped out early yesterday morning. Kids, parents - everyone.” “Jesus,” Leigh is stunned. “I caught wind of it, but I thought it was just a fucking rumor. Whothe fuck do you think is responsible.” “Seems clear enough,” shrugs Blake. “Ever since my boys beat the black of some nigger ass inthe Unit E riot about two months back, that jungle bunny has been itching to get back at me. Ithought he’d try to do it in here. Take me out. Take my boys out. I should have known better. Niggers are the lowest type of animals - they’ll go after whatever cheap revenge they can get.” “So what do we do?” Leigh asks, taking a seat by Harvey on the lower bunk. “We cut their fucking hearts out,” Blake says in serious tones as he turns to face his youngAustralian companion. “The last war between white and black happened twenty years ago. Ithink it’s time we put the motherfuckers back in their places. From now on, no nigger in thiswhole fucking prison is safe. Every man who kills a nigger in my army will be rewarded greatly. But to kick things off, we’re going to go right for the throat. That’s where you come in, son.” Harvey, blinded by his hatred and prejudices, can’t see that the real killers were members of theItalian mafia, in revenge for Doug Hattoni’s murder. “Just tell me what you want and it’s done,” Leigh says quietly, pointing at his right knee toemphasise his seriousness. A long moment of silence passes before Harvey leans close to theyoung Australian.
