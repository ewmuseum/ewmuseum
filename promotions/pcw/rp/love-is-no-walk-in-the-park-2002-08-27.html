Love Is No Walk In the Park

27 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8-27-02 “I don’t understand you... and I’m not sure I want to...” An average looking woman sits on a park bench, watching the birds hopping along the sidewalk in front of her. As she runs a hand through her auburn hair, the camera pans back, revealing another woman on the bench. This woman doesn’t seem to be paying attention to anything. “I mean, for one thing, look at how you’re dressed....” Right after these words escape the pink lips of this woman, the other quickly turns to face her in shock. “What do you mean the way I dress? My style is....” The woman stops and looks herself over. A black gothic chocker with spikes and a black midriff tank top, which is cut and stringy at the bottom. She also wears a pair of bright blue leather pants, with a chain belt that hangs down over one hip. Upon her wrists are various bracelets with odd designs and spikes. Realizing that maybe she does have an odd sense of fashion, the woman looks back up and faces the other. “...is....” “...unique.” The auburn haired woman finishes the other’s sentence. “Honestly, Cecilia, I don’t understand why or how you wrestle... especially with that jewelry....” Now identified as Cecilia, the unique woman smiles at the auburn haired woman. “The jewelry can be quite an advantage... if an advantage is needed...” Cecilia flashes a quick smile and then pushes some of her dirty blond hair behind her ear. “Besides, Audrey, your attire isn’t exactly normal....” Audrey, the girl with the auburn hair, looks her attire over. She doesn’t seem to see anything wrong with it... and neither would the common person. A black tee-shirt with a pair of dark blue jeans. A very normal outfit.... “You’re crazy...” Audrey says, shaking her head. “I don’t see how you find my outfit abnormal.... It’s so simple. At least it doesn’t take me three years to put my jewelry on...” Audrey smiles at her witty remark, but Cecilia only rolls her eyes. “I was kidding, Cecilia.” Both women stop talking and look away from each other. Far off in the horizon, the sun sinks behind the calm waters of the river. A few boats slowly pass by and a duck skids across the water. Suddenly, Audrey looks at her watch and her jaw drops. “Sh*t, Cecilia, I was supposed to be home an hour ago. Jake is waiting for me...” Cecilia looks up with the same shocked expression. “We better get going then...” She jumps up from her seat on the bench and begins to head towards the parking lot. Then, something stops her and she turns around. “Wait... why didn’t he call you? You’ve had the cell phone on all day... and he never bothered to call you? Something’s up there...” Looking a bit apprehensive, Audrey shakes her head. “Oh no, Cecilia, don’t you even start with that bull sh*t. How many times do I have to tell you and prove to you that Jake is faithful before it finally goes through that thick skull of yours?” Quickly, Cecilia’s stance becomes more defensive. Noticing this and deciding that she doesn’t want to bother with an agreement, Audrey shakes her head. “Just joking...” The Latin Beauty rolls her eyes, but then a huge smile plasters itself onto her face. “Come on, girl, let’s do some investigating...” As Cecilia begins to run back to the car, her combat boots hit the cement with loud thuds. “Cecilia, I swear to god you’ll never hear the end of this!!!” Audrey screams at Cecilia as she chases her up the walk, tripping over stones, twigs, and cracks. ~*~*~*~*~*~*~ The night sky is filled with stars, but few are visible due to the fog and clouds in the air. There are many houses lining the street that this scene takes place at, but very few have the lights on. Most of the houses look the same, with similar coloring and similar yards. Just when it seems that this shot may be just a bit too dull, a pair of headlights become visible rounding the bend on the street. This vehicle inches slowly along the road, as if it were trying to be sneaky. Then, the car pulls into a gravel side lot, pieces of stone crunching and flying in all directions. The motor shuts off and the headlights die down. Cecilia Verucana quietly opens the driver’s door and steps out of it, shutting it as gently as possible. Looking up at the house in front of her, she notices that none of the lights are on .... except for one in the far back. Arcing one of her slender brown eyebrows, she signals for Audrey to get out of the passenger side. Audrey shakes her head, unwilling to get out of the car. Shrugging, Cecilia tip-toes across the lush green lawn and to the side of the house. Once she reaches her destination, Cecilia begins to search for a place to enter. Most of the windows are locked and there aren’t any basement windows to break. When she turns to go around to the other side, she runs right into another person. Stopping short of a scream, Cecilia squints her eyes to try and figure out who she rammed into. Seeing the auburn hair, Cecilia chuckles lightly. “I can’t believe you’re actually going through with this....” Audrey shakes her head in disapproval. “It’ll do nothing but piss Jake off.” “Good,” Cecilia says, looking around for another place of entry... or even a tree to climb so that she can peer into that window. “The guy needs some f*cking thrill in his life... other than the sex.” Cecilia brings her gaze back onto Audrey’s face. “You honestly believe he doesn’t f*ck around with other women?” Staring Cecilia down, Audrey shakes her head. “I’m positive. Jake is very faithful.” After hearing this, Cecilia can’t help but laugh at how naive Audrey is. “Just because we’ve never done anything...” “...oh please,” Cecilia says, her joyful tone suddenly changed to a more cynical one. “The man is like a pimp, I swear to god. What makes you think he’s gonna put up with a virgin? He’s only holding on to you and playing with you because he wants to pop your cherry...” After Cecilia says this, a look of disgust falls over Audrey’s face. “That’s disgusting... and definitely not true.” Audrey looks at Cecilia with satisfaction. “There’s no way Jake’s like that.” “Eh... you are so f*cking naive,” Cecilia says, rolling her eyes. “Explain why there is only one light on in the whole house.. and it’s his bedroom light.” A smug smile hits Cecilia’s face, as she looks at Audrey, thinking she made a comment there is no way around. “He’s getting ready for bed... or he’s reading.” Audrey scrambles for an excuse, knowing that Cecilia’s argument is valid. Normally Jake leaves other lights on. “Well if you really believe that,” Cecilia says with a laugh, “...then give me the key.” Audrey’s jaw drops and she looks at Cecilia in shock. “What!?! I can’t do that! He’d kill you... and me!!!.....” Audrey stares at Cecilia in bewilderment, wondering where Cecilia got the nerve to ask such a question. “You asshole,” Cecilia says. “I’m not going in by myself. You’re coming with me.” Cecilia gives Audrey an annoyed stare, as if Audrey should have known this. Sighing, Audrey shoves a hand in one of her pockets and fumbles around a little bit, before finally producing a key. “Jake’s gonna kill me....” Audrey says with a sigh, as Cecilia grabs the key. “Will you stop being so paranoid?” Cecilia looks at the key and then at the front door. “Is this the key for the front or the back?” Turning her attention back to Audrey, who is looking quite appalled. “Me, paranoid? Ha! You’re the one trying to catch my boyfriend cheating on me!” Audrey looks up at the bedroom light and then back at Cecilia. “And to answer your question, the front door, why?” “Damnit... do you have the key for the back door?” Cecilia looks behind her. For some reason, she had the feeling that someone is looking at her... but there is nobody around but her and Audrey. “No I don’t.....” Audrey looks Cecilia over curiously. “Why does that matter, anyway?” “Because he’d expect you to come through the front door, so he’s probably listening for it. Oh well, let’s just go in that way. There’s no escape for the bimbo he’s got up there. Make sure you’re quiet...” Cecilia beings to creep towards the front door, careful not to step on any branches. The two women climb the front steps and then approach the front door cautiously. Cecilia slowly slips the key into the keyhole and then turns the knob. It works and the door opens, without making a sound. The Punk Princess turns to Audrey and smiles, before slowly pushing the door forward. Both women enter the house and Cecilia removes the key from the keyhole and returns it to Audrey. Then, she closes the door silently and looks around. “Where are the stairs,” she whispers. “Follow me,” Audrey says and then tip toes to a corridor on the left. As she follows Audrey, Cecilia notices that there aren’t any doors in this corridor and finds it strange. Making a left, Cecilia finds that they are on the stairs. “Be extra quiet, Audrey. Are the stairs creaky?” Cecilia applies pressure to one and the nervous look on her face changes to joy when no sound erupts. “Nope, you’re in luck,” Audrey says with a shake of her head. “I can’t believe I’m doing this....” “You’d think they’d be extra creaky.... so he’d hear you coming up the steps when he’s f*cking the brains out of some broad,” Cecilia murmurs as they start up the steps, very silently. “You’re disgusting,” Audrey says back when they reach to top of the stairs. “His room is on the left. I’ll let you do the honors, Lara Croft.” Cecilia nods and smiles at Audrey as she walks up to the door and presses her ear against it. At first there isn’t a sound and Cecilia looks disappointed. Audrey, on the other hand, looks pleased and has an ‘I-Told-You-So’ look on her face. Then, it happens. A muffled moan can be heard from the other side. Her jaw drops and Audrey seems confused and angry. A sadistic smile on her face, Cecilia raises her foot a bit and looks at Audrey. Frightened, Audrey begins to shake her head and waves her arms, trying to stop Cecilia from doing anything. But her frantic fighting is in vain, because before Audrey can even try to stop her, Cecilia has brought her foot stomping to the ground. Audrey slumps, looking defeated. From the other side of the door, there is the sound of sheets ruffling and someone whispering in an alarmed voice. Looking even more malicious, Cecilia opens the door to the bedroom and walks in, looking around. When her eyes fall upon Jake, her eyes light up and she smiles. “Hey there, Jake, we’ve been looking everywhere for you!” Jake’s face is red and flushed. He’s taken quite a liberty in covering his private area with a blanket. However, he did a poor job of hiding the other person in the bed, since there is a huge lump of something under the other part of the sheets. “Wow, Jake,” Cecilia says with a laugh. “What’s that there?” The out of breath and naked man scowls at her. “It’s the dog,” he says with an angry and defensive voice. “What were you doing to that poor animal!??!” Cecilia fakes her shock and curiosity. “And that’s one big dog....” Audrey enters the room and puts a hand to her mouth, seeing the scene in front of her. Seeing Audrey, Jake springs up in bed, making sure the sheet is still covering him. Cecilia pulls the blanket off of the bed to reveal a nude woman.... and Jake‘s genitals. “Wow, Jake, your dog seems to shed a lot... and has a nice rack. Wow, where’d you find it, on the corner of 5th and 6th?” Jake looks at Cecilia with disgust and then down at the nude woman on the bed, who has now jumped up and is scrambling to find her clothes. “Look, Audrey, I can explain,” he says, suddenly realizing that he is sitting completely nude on the bed. He desperately tries to cover himself up with his hands. “Oh, don’t worry about that,” Cecilia says with a smile. “There isn’t much to cover up anyway. I’m surprised... how do you manage to bed so many women with a tool like that?” Cecilia questions Jake with a tone of victory. “Let me ask your bimbo....” Cecilia turns to the now clothed woman. “I mean, how did you even find the damn thing?” “Shut up!” Jake screams, as he jumps up and walks over to the closet to find some clothes. “What is wrong with you, you stupid bitch. I mean, couldn’t a butch like you have found something better to do with your time... like training for your match on Wednesday or something?” “What is there to train for? I’m fighting some 85 pound girl. It’s overkill,” Cecilia says, with a laugh. “And besides, I find it more fun to bust an asshole like you in the act.” “And besides,” Audrey exclaims with fury. “Who are you to question us? You’re the one doing the wrong doing here.... I caught YOU cheating on me with another woman!” “Well what did you want me to do? Sit around and wait for you? I have some needs that need taken care of....” Jake looks at Audrey with anger. Nobody notices that the woman he was with has snuck out of the room. “You’re a stereotypical man,” Cecilia says with an aggressive tone. “And you’ll never be anything but a pathetic little boy who fantasizes about those big blond bimbos in the porno mags, because you know you’re never getting any of them... not an ugly ass sleaze ball like you. I hope you’re content with this life style, Jake, because in thirty years, you’re going to wonder where your life went... and why you never settled down with anyone and raised a family....” Cecilia marches over to Audrey and grabs her by the arm. “Come on, lets go.” The two women disappear through the door, leaving Jake completely stunned., alone and.... naked... in the middle of his own bedroom. Just when it seems like things couldn’t get any worse, Audrey shows back up in the doorway and throws the house key at him. The key smacks him right in the middle of the forehead. “Serves you right, you asshole,” Audrey says, as she marches from the room. ~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~ “Just calm down, he’s an asshole. Get over it, everything will be okay.” Cecilia desperately tries to calm Audrey down as they drive down the deserted highway. “You can spend the night at my place, if you like.” Cecilia stops and thinks about this for a minute. “Actually, I’d be delighted if you would come to the show on Wednesday, it’s my debut match...” Audrey looks up at her best friend and manages to laugh through her tears. “You know I hate wrestling.... I don’t even like the idea of you in the ring...” “I’d really like to have you there to support me,” Cecilia says with a smile. “Please, just this once....” Audrey looks at Cecilia with regret and finally gives in. “Fine, but just this once. I’ll be there to support you.” As the scene fades out, Cecilia Verucana smiles and puts her attention back on the road.
