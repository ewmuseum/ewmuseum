Heinrich's Spa - Part 1 - Mein Freund

5 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: May 5, 2003 [Heinrich's Spa is one of the most bodacious places to go in California, that's if your looking for a most relaxing time. It's a 300,000 square foot structure of nothing but gnarly equipment, medicine, and bodacious babes that are there to make you feel great. Many famous movie stars and musicians come to the spa after a lot of hard work and just let loose for an entire weekend at the spa. Heinrich Kroeger, owner and founder, has made a lot of powerful friends over the years and it's really helped to make him a very gnarly dude with celebrities. One of the best guys that he knows is 'Totally Tubular' Tommy. When Heinrich first met Tommy he was just a speck on the radar in the Primetime Wrestling Academy and Tommy was still trying to find his wrestling style. His first visit to the spa helped Tommy finally relax and get all those bogus things off of his mind. He was able to find a good style and totally radical game plan that sent him to NTICW for a most excellent run. Tommy owes a small portion of his wrestling success to Heinrich, even though he wasn't directly involved with Tommy's relaxation, and he always tries to go to the spa as much as possible] Heinrich Kroeger: TOMMY! Das ist sehr gut to see you! [As Tommy comes to the main lobby, filled with the most powerful and beautiful people in the world, he smiles at the prescence of Heinrich. Heinrich has black hair that is slicked back completely and always looks wet. His trimmed goatee looks like it's been drawn onto his face but Tommy knows that Heinrich is just that bodacious with a razor blade. Heinrich is very fit looking. He may not be very large, only standing at five foot seven inches, but his extremely cut muscles only make up for his height disadvantage] Heinrich: Mein Freund!! [Tommy does find it annoying that Heinrich always mixes his native language, German, with English. It's most bogus when your trying to have a conversation and he starts saying things like, 'Das Wetter ist das blod!' out of absolutely nowhere. At first Tommy figured that the dude was insulting him in another language, but it just turns out the dude has some head problems from a fight he was in when he was a teenager. Some punk kid smashed him in the side of the head with a mailbox. Where this kid got a mailbox is beyond Tommy at this point, but it would still be most heinous to have a metal mailbox upside your head. Every since that day Heinrich will sometimes spurt out random statements that have nothing to do with what your talking to him about] 'Totally Tubular' Tommy: HEINRICH! Your looking totally righteous today! [Tommy is decked out in a blue satin dress shirt with blue dress pants that have yellow stripes down the side. Many of the women turn their heads immediately and begin to whisper to each other as Tommy makes his way through the lobby. Some of the patrons of the spa are a bit jealous of Tommy since he gets the ultimate VIP treatment. He doesn't have to wait due to his gnarly status as PCW Champion. Unfortunately, many of the patrons do not know Tommy got this treatment long before he was The Champ. It's just that now he's the best of the best it makes sense to people why he gets the treatment] Heinrich: My good friend! I've been watching recently and I have to say I'm a bit disappointed that your not on the shows very often. Tommy: Well, you can't have too much exposure of The Champion or else people will get tired of it. I have to be able to keep myself fresh and most bodacious! It's just like when your going for the babes, Heinrich. You age but you have to be able to keep yourself fresh, evolve your approaches to all bodacious babes. If you keep it the same you'll NEVER be able to get anywhere. As PCW Champion, I have to keep evolving my styles and my attitude as there are a lot of compadres coming after the belt! Heinrich: Das ist sehr gut, mein freund!! [They walk through a series of double doors and through a hallway painted in a bright white. The lights above reflect on the white paint making it a lot more bright then it should be. The light from the ceiling and the relfection on the walls make Tommy have to squint through the hallway as they come to the pool area of the spa. The entire pool area is filled with some of the most bodacious babes that Tommy has ever set his eyes on. All dressed in very revealing swimsuits and toweling each other off from a hard days swimming] Tommy: Heinrich...your totally gnarly! If I were gay I would totally jump your bones right now. [Heinrich is silent for a few moments, not really understanding that comment. One can be hit in the head with a metal mailbox and still be confused by statements like that] Tommy: Well, I'm not gay and this sight is one that I don't want to erase from my memory! Heinrich: Only the best for my best customer. [Tommy pulls out a wad of one hundred dollar bills and hands them all over to Heinrich. Tommy nods to him quickly and then turns his head back toward the pool] Tommy: That should cover my tab from the past few months, dude. I've been most busy with the Continental Title and World Title to really get onto paying you back. Heinrich: I know you have been, mein freund. I know that your good for it... [Heinrich holds up the wad of money] Heinrich: ...as I see right here. [Tommy just stares at all of the beautiful women down at the pool. Most in their pool chair enjoying the bright California sun beating down onto their milky, tanned skin. They are into conversation and they are smiling, showing off their perfect white teeth. Some have their tops off lying stomach down to get sun onto their backs. Others are in the pool doing laps or just enjoying the wonderful feeling of being wet. That's one thing that has always drove The Tubular One crazy the most, a wet woman. He doesn't really understand how they can be so sexy when they have their hair wet and the streaks of water run down their bodies. It's just absolutely erotic to witness] Tommy: How'd you get all these totally bodacious babes out here without other dudes getting in the way? Heinrich: I have connections, Tommy. Tommy: That's awesome. Heinrich: There are some things that I am worried about, mein freund. [Tommy keeps his blue eyes focused on the beautiful women around the pool. He's actually feeling a bit anxious about getting out there to greet these ladies] Tommy: What is that, dude? Heinrich: I'm a little worried about former opponents of yours stepping up and maybe hurting you badly. I am a long time fan of PCW and I'm just a little worried that something very bad could happen to you. Tommy: Don't worry, Heinrich, I've got it all covered. Heinrich: Well, I am sure that you have thought about it, but you can think about it all you want because you still don't know who or what will happen. You've made enemies even when you were teamed with your brother as The Cowabunga Connection. Even if the fans still loved you then you still made some very strong enemies then and lately you've been making them left and right. I'm just worried, at this conjuncture in your career, that your going to end up in a hospital bed just like that poor Venneman man. [The words pierce Tommy like a hot knife into an already opened wound. He never thought about the heinous enemies that he made when he was part of the Cowabunga Connection. All of the tag teams that they had beaten and pushed out of the company with their great skills. Tommy and Harry were hellbent on just embarrassing and humiliating their opponents to the point where they had to leave the company. So many of them and so little of him now. He's the PCW Champion, a marked man already, and there is no telling who or what can come out of the shadows. Even with the sight of beautiful women in front of him it's hard to not think about what could happen to him in the near future] Tommy: Your absolutely right, dude. I have been thinking about this over the past few days and it has been haunting me a little bit, making me feel a little heinous. I feel it's just a minor set back in being a most excellent villain, that's all. I have to start watching my steps a little bit and become a bit more devious. Just gotta stay a step ahead of everyone else and that belt will stay secured around my totally bodacious waist! Heinrich: Of course. [Tommy takes his eyes away from the pool for a moment, even though it pains him to do so. He looks into the dark eyes of Heinrich and sees the doubt displayed. Tommy can't believe that one of his good friends, Heinrich, is actually doubting that Tommy can handle the burden that will soon be onto his shoulders. Tommy is actually a bit hurt that Heinrich would go and question Tommy's most bodacious skills. This is the dude who always supported Tommy and told him that he had a lot more going for him than he thought. Heinrich was like Mickey to Rocky, dudes. It's most bodacious to see a dude like that doubt a dude like Tommy] Tommy: Dude, you need to give it to me straight. Heinrich: I just think that maybe your in a little over your head. Maybe things have been happening far too fast and finally the rest of time is going to catch up with you. All that I mean is that I think everyone in PCW is going to catch up to you and it's going to make it a dangerous situation for you. Tommy: There is nothing dangerous about all those totally heinous compadres in PCW! They are all amateurs compared to a dude like me. Heinrich: What about Dorn Tracer and Violence Jack? They don't seem like amateurs. Tommy: Dude, those guys are the definition of it! Tracer is just totally lucky and Violence Jack is trying to live off of the greatness I put into that Continental Title. Heinrich: Dorn is one of the fastest rising stars in PCW, already going from Rising Star Champ to TV Champion in a matter of weeks. Violence Jack has dominated opponents for years and has earned his spot as Continental Champion. I think that maybe your REALLY not giving any of these guys credit, mein freund. Das Wetter ist blod!! Tommy: I don't need to worry about dudes like that, Heinrich. I've got it taken care of this Tuesday... [Tommy looks back out to the pool and the smile appears back onto his face. Babes really help him escape from his true feelings and right now he needs all the babes that he can get his hands on. With the amount of babes down there he can really take is 'training' to an entirely different level] Tommy: ...just don't worry about it. Fade to black
