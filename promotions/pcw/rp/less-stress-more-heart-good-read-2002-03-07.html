Less Stress, More Heart (Good Read)

7 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/7/02 Time: 3: 26 A.MLocation: Coimbra, PortugalScene: Beach House The scene opens with Juan Borro sleeping in bed with his girlfriend Juliet. Juan is in a deep sleep at his new beach house, where the secret officers relocated him to for safety purposes. Juan doesn’t mind the identity change too much, as everything has been pretty nice and peaceful for him. Juan begins to think about a few things that occurred over the course of the last month, in his dreams. Like all dreams everything occurring is a little blurry. With all the silence and peace going around, Juan has had a lot of time to sleep and think about things. So many things Juan thought he could do, but doesn’t have a passion for. Juan knows he’s through with soccer. It brought him far, but he knows that is a life he will never return to. Then run tried to world icon thing, which seemed to not work, No matter what you do, you can’t have the whole world respect you. Juan will still work toward international equality, but knows they won’t look at him like their hero. Juan even tried the city council position, which he quickly knew wasn’t for him. Juan has been looking for a bunch of things, without ever concentrating on the thing he should be concerned with and that’s wrestling. Juan thought about the whole subject for a while and thought about all the goals he is yet to succeed. He then thinks about his team International Incident. The same team that failed, because he couldn’t pull things together. The ego and rotten souls within the group destroyed it right under Juan’s eyes. International Incident is coming back, and Juan knows he will be more active into it then ever. The new International Incident is still small now with himself, Klaymore, Aymin, Chef, and Bethany, but the five will pull through and prove that they are a force to be reckoned with. As for Akiyama and Pharaoh Juan looks at them as pure trash. They are two men who are out for nothing but them selves. Juan looks at them and knows they are nowhere near any type of force as Golden Game. They will soon use they’re greed to fight amongst each other leading to all chaos. Juan knows that those will be the first two men on the International Incident hit list. They think they can get away with what they did, but they won’t, they will pay for their actions, and the payment is coming soon. Another man Juan is concerned with is El Diablo. A man Juan once called his friend, somebody he could confide in. El Diablo proved to be just another little rat that had to take the easy way out. Juan has no respect for the man, and will make sure he pays for his actions as well. Juan thinks within his dreams about all different PCW issues and how to deal with them. What Juan doesn’t realize, is the controversy right in front of him. After all he is hiding from people who want to kill him. He is also getting ready for a trial, and so much more to follow. He has a tournament to be in, and he has to make sure to spend time with his girlfriend Juliet. The anxiety seems to be getting to Juan and he doesn’t know what to do with all. The stress and tension is ruining him more and more every day. Juan is trying to be strong about the whole thing, but feels as if he just wants to break down and cry sometimes. What Juan doesn’t know is in just a few moments, his life will take a huge leap toward becoming much better…….. (Phone rings) Juan is quickly woken up to the sound of the phone. Juan leans over to grab the phone as it continues ringing. Juliet gets up tired and yawns. She then leans over to Juan, who puts the phone to his ear and begins listening. Juan- Hello, whom am I speaking to. On the other line, you see Juan’s attorney, with a big smile on his face, as he gets ready to give Juan the good news. Juan’s attorney- I have great news that you are going to love hearing. Are you ready to hear it? Juan- Yes, of course I am, tell me already. Juliet listens as she hears Juan talking. She gains some concern and gets Juan attention to see who is on the phone. Juliet- Who’s calling at this hour Juan? Is it important? Juan- It’s my attorney Juliet. Don’t worry everything is fine, just go back to sleep and get your rest. Juliet gets back into bed, as she closes her precious eyes and falls back into the deep sleep she was in. Juan- This better be good. Juan’s Attorney- You are a completely free man of all charges. Just thirty minutes ago, the real killer was found, and taken to a local prison. The man even pleaded guilty, so there is no doubt that he did it. Juan is in such a state of shock that he drops the phone and stands completely still not even blinking. Juan’s world flashes right past his eyes, as in a huge gasp of excitement he passes out. Meanwhile, the phone is on the ground and his defense attorney is still rambling on in the phone. Defense Attorney- Yea Juan, it’s great. You can even go back to your normal life. I’ve talked it over with the Chinese government and they actually apologized and everything, it’s great isn’t it? (No Answer) Defense Attorney- Juan are you there? (No Answer) Defense Attorney- Hello are you there? (No answer) Defense Attorney- JUAN! (For the final time there in no answer. The defense attorney shows concern, but just hangs the phone up.) In the meantime Juliet is woken up from the loud noise of Juan’s fall. She rushes over and notices Juan on the ground. She lets out a huge scream, and runs over toward the phone. Juliet dials 911 a she stares at Juan motionless on the floor. She talks frantically on the phone. Juliet- My boyfriend has just passed out and is just lying down on the floor and not moving at all. 911 Operator- Okay calm down, we’ll…… The rest of the events just fade out, as the scene goes into Juan’s mind. Everything seems blurry and is hard to make out, but everything just needs to be put together for it to make sense. Juan is lying there with a smile on his face. A smile of satisfaction and a smile of peace, something Juan has been yet to have in a long time. Inside Juan’s head, flashes keep going through his minds. The different flashes start off slow and keep becoming quicker and quicker. Juan first sees a picture of Juliet, him, and Kevin, all smiling in a picture. The three look so peaceful and happy together. Then a picture of Juan’s father crying comes up and the mood becomes a bit sorrowful. Juan thinks about his father and how he must have felt about the whole situation and how things turned out. Next, he sees a picture of the murder scene where the Chinese executive was murdered. It brings back bad thoughts in Juan’s head. He thinks about all of the hell he was put through when the story first went out. The next group of images comes in a huge collage of photos of men in the prison. The men are screaming, crying, bleeding, and staring. A bunch of pictures come across Juan’s mind as the more traumatizing they are the more Juan thinks about them. All of the hate Juan is thinking about makes him become violent and angry inside. Juan feels like he just wants to break something or hurt somebody. Juan then sees nothing but flashes of blood and screaming. ************************************************************************ Juan then woke up a bit, but his eyes are not yet fully open. He sees a man beating his chest, and people pushing him in a stretcher, all nervous. He them sees Juliet standing above him, as words are slowly coming out of her mouth. Juliet- I…… Juan then flashes and shakes again, but Juliet continues. Juliet- Love…….. Juan quickly goes back into a state of euphoria for a moment, as he shakes as his eyes roll back. He takes one last glance as Juliet’s lips as they sound out: Juliet- You…… Juan doesn’t even hear the end of the word before he completely goes unconscious again. This time Juan doesn’t come up again for a few moments. ************************************************************************ When back in his unconscious state the flashes continue. The start off slow and get quicker just like last time. The prison flashes keep coming really fast, but then slow down as they focus on the man who fell to the first floor of the prison when Juan was there. Juan stared at him and saw his tongue hanging out and the blood pouring from his head. Juan stared as they mopped up the blood, and it’s an image Juan will never forget. Another image that Juan will never forget slowly comes up next. The image is of his cellmate, who was reaching out to him, with blood dripping from his hand. He put his life on the line for Juan, and that moment might have affected Juan’s attitude for the rest of his life. From there the images go to the secret officers trying to hide him. The people giving him dirty looking on the street, and much more. Suddenly, everything turns to light. For a second, Juan thinks he’s dead, but he really isn’t. Juan is running in a windy plain with his mother and father, ad they all old hands and run together. Juan smiles and hugs his mother for the first time since she died. Juan then goes to kiss her, but he regains consciousness ************************************************************************ When Juan wakes up he sees a bunch of people working on him. They notice Juan is awake and they stop what they are doing and help him up. Juan goes over to a room in the hospital to rest for a while. When put in the room he sees Juliet sleeping and waiting for him. He thinks about waking her up, but notices how beautiful and peaceful she looks so he decides not to. Juan grabs the remote and turns on the television. What he sees on the television is a news show. In the news it has the real criminal to the crime walking out of the courthouse in handcuffs. The man stares at the screen and smiles. After Juan sees the smile, he gets goose bumps all up his spine. Juan seems a bit scared, but shakes it off and turns off the television. Juan thinks about all the problems and hell that man on the screen caused for him. Juan knows it all could have been avoided, but is just happy it’s now over. Juan puts his hand in his heart just to make sure it’s still beating. He then looks around, and sees a hospital room. He sees a hospital room that is actually opportunity to Juan. What Juan really sees is a stress less and anxiety future ahead of him. One that should actually bring enjoyment into his life. Now that Juan has gotten all of these things out of his way, it seems life everything is going to be all right. Watch out BAS, you may be the bigger and stronger mad, but my heart will prevail. FOR I AM THE GOLDEN HEART……………… Scene Fades to Black.
