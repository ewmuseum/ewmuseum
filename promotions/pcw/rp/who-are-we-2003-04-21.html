Who are we?

21 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 21st April Pretty Cool ChroniclesChapter #1: Who are we? ‘Who are we?’It’s a question that a lot of people have asked me throughout my time in the businesses that are ‘Professional Wrestling’ and ‘Musical Entertainment’. It’s a question that a lot of people tend to ask, because we’ve been away from the screens of people at home, for something in the range of a whole year now. I know, it sounds somewhat odd to have people of this accord even try to come back into the flow of things after so long away. I know what you’re thinking, how you’re feeling… I know the excitement that boils through the veins of sixteen year old girls across the great country that is, the United States of America. Believe me, I know the kinds of things that these young, barely legal girls will do for the smallest glimpse of my plush American bum-bum. Yeah, I know that it all sounds weird and I know that, it’s nothing that you people will really come to understand without a proper video explanation. That’s why, we’re going to show you something that could very well go on to explain everything that you don’t need to know about the dodgy ‘Pop’ tandem that are known around the interplanetary world as the ‘Pretty Cool Guys’. Defenders of Peace, Justice and Love… Protectors of fourteen, fifteen, sixteen and seventeen year old girls. We’re a team of people that could, truly make a difference to the way that everyone looks upon the industry that is Pop. Our first case as the interplanetary defenders of Pop justice, is to change the abnormal trash that happened to be the ‘Everybody’ video by the “Backstreet Boys”. At the time of doing this, we weren’t sure about anything, so you’ll have to excuse our lack of knowledge on the matter… HOWEVER, everything, does come to a happy ending… -- Production Start Production Date: 20/04/03Current Location: The Outskirts of Raccoon City… “Rich, do you have any idea about why two, normal guys, like us… are standing outside a city that was destroyed in Resident Evil 3… and supposed to be filled, with nasty things that you and I would like to call Zombies.” “Ryan… I don’t know why you’re trippin’ or anything at the moment, you know? I mean, its not like this place is going to just happen to be, exactly the same as that unfortunate town.” The scene slowly began to open in what appeared to be an empty road. The two individuals that were both myself and Ryan Moore, stood coolly and calmly at what just happened to be the ‘Welcoming Sign’ to the city most renowned for its count of Zombies and other deadly creatures. Surrounding the two heroes didn’t seem to be a lot… just a long stretch of grass and trees to the left and right, the city standing an impressive ten minute walk away. “Rich, what’s going to happen to us if, this whole entire city just happens to be infested with skin eating freaks?” Ryan asked in something of a calm tone, his body shaking slightly, the fear of death slowly beginning to run rampant through his body. As the leader of the team, I felt that it was my duty to do something that’d calm down both himself… and his nerves. “… Well, should the worse come to the worst, we’re just going to have do everything those guys did in the ‘Backstreet Boys’ video, “Everybody”. You know, that song where Nick Carter and company just happened to dance with a lot of dead people.” I responded, my arms slowly beginning to tremble, the fear of being wrong slowly creeping into the back of my mind. “Dance like the ‘Backstreet Boys’?” Ryan said to me in response, his tone not holding the kind of friendliness that I would have been only too happy to accept. In fact, his voice seemed to carry a lot of sarcasm with it, an obvious amount of rejection that would only prove to be a possible blockade should the worst case scenario happen to come along. He crossed his arms over his chest and looked into my eye, cocking up his right eyebrow somewhat. I could only look back for that moment in time, I could only look back and return his questioning glare with something that was… different. “… Well, hey…” I began, continuing to sound extremely nervous about everything that could happen in this notorious city, “… What else are we going to do is this bad situation does happen to occur? I mean, didn’t we have to operate around that ‘Dark Gimmick’ character last year in Primetime Championship Wrestling?” Ryan’s face slowly began to lose its expression of ‘uncoolness’, it seemed like my example of ‘Dark Gimmick’ had been quite a good one to use. I breathed heavily, thankful that my brain had actually operated beyond PoP music and Pantyville. It’d been a long since I had to think like a rational adult… and for my sake, I hoped I wouldn’t have to experience this kind of responsibility ever again. “I suppose that you’re right on this one, Rich…” he began, his voice slowly beginning to trail off into the night sky. I looked around my current surrounding and sighed somewhat, time was passing pretty quickly and I didn’t want to stay out here. A few more moments passed us by before Ryan began to speak up again, he said, “… I think we’re just going to have to take a chance and walk into that City. I don’t think I’m going to feel at all safe out here.” Whether or not he’d gotten those vibes of insecurity from myself, was something that I didn’t know… and, it wasn’t something that I really wanted to find out in the immediate future. We looked behind us to make sure that nobody was around, that feeling of paranoia… that feeling of being watched, it had crept up on us whilst we were talking. A cold chill seemed to tickle my back and I fidgeted on the spot, Ryan turned to look at me, seemingly surprised at my reaction. “Rich… are you feeling OK?” he asked, concerned with my own personal welfare. I nodded somewhat and continued to walk, edging past him with any consideration for what could be lurking around us. He shrugged the moment off and thought nothing of it, catching up with me as I continued to walk the road to the city that had been labelled, “The City of the undead.” “… Ryan, how do you feel about all of this?” I asked, a slight tingle of fear acting as the sugar coating to every word that carefully flew out of my mouth. Another cold chill ran up my back, tickling me as it went along its way. I tried to ignore the developing feeling of insecurity, but I couldn’t help myself and fidgeted once more. Ryan stared at me, knowing that something was wrong… he could feel the presence of paranoia surrounding us, he could feel the fear that I was experiencing… for it was running alongside every limb of his body too. He tried to open his mouth and give something of an answer for my question, but that very feeling of fear and stopped him from doing anything that we took for granted. He remained still, the grip of that demonic emotion taking control of everything. “Urrrrgh…” came an emotionless voice, soon followed by the exact same phrase, this time, coming from what could only be assumed as a larger number of people. Ryan Moore had lost control of himself, he was frozen to the floor with fear… absolute, undying fear. I looked from left to right of myself, shadows slowly beginning emerge from the darkness. I’d been something of a wreck until this point, but I knew what I had to do whenever we were to come under fire by a mass of, sexually deprived housewives, or overly eager seventeen-year-old girls. The plodding and dragging of limbs against the highway surface continued to get louder with each motion. Both Ryan Moore and myself knew that there was a possibility that the end would come soon… we both knew that these, things weren’t your average humanoid lifeform… and thanks to ‘Dark Gimmick’, we knew something of the pain that was going to follow if we didn’t do anything that… would save our lives. Slowly, and extremely steadily, the figures began to come into view. Their clothing was in rags… fabric hanging and dragging against the floor. I couldn’t believe my eyes… the state of these things was beyond understandable. Adidas, Nike, Reebok, Ben Sherman… known brands of clothing, destroyed beyond their very comprehension. For a few moments, I felt truly sorry about these people and their crimes against looking good for the uncanny pairing that just happened to be both myself and Ryan Moore.As I glared around myself, the feeling of fear started to subside… and, an everlasting feeling of sorrow began to overcome everything. How could these beings, as stupid as they were, simply allow their precious clothing and items to deteriorate into nothingness… how could they have allowed it? “… Urrrgh… Argggh… Urrrrgaaahhh…” the mob continued to moan, in their, dark, horrific voices. My face slowly began to cringe and twist into unspeakable emotions as the zombie-like masses surrounded both Ryan and myself. We now understood, that if we didn’t have any kind of plan ready to operate within the next three minutes… our lives and careers were going to be nothing more than a jaded memory… an unfortunate nightmare for girls Nationwide. A thousand thoughts seemed to run rampant through my head as I tried to think of a way to save both Ryan and myself. I thought back to the start of this whole episode, where I was suggesting to Ryan about using the Backstreet Boys song of, ‘Everybody’ to bring these undead creatures under our control. I chuckled to myself somewhat upon reliving that moment, the sheer stupidity of the assumption making me want to forget it completely. “Ev.. Every… body… yeaah-eah..” ‘Who was saying those magical words?’ I thought to myself as they slapped me across the face in a violent manner. I turned around to look at my partner, Ryan Moore and saw that he was the one using these lyrics as a last gasp attempt to save us from the mob of deadly zombies. His feet began to move from side to side in sequence, his arms slowly begin to follow in the quest to drive away these horrible fiends. I could see by the blank expression on his face, that this wasn’t the last thing he was wanting to do before he’d lose his life… and everything that he’d work to get. The zombie mob continued to move toward him, regardless of the magical lyrics that were flying in their direction. “R.. Rock your bo… body… yeaah-eah…” The words seemed to spring into existence from my body without consent. It felt like Ryan Moore was spurring me on. The whole period of desperation, the whole fight against the undead masses… I felt my body beginning to move in the same way as Nick Carters did in that Backstreet Boys video. The steps to the left and right, the arm swaying like I was one of these undead creatures. “Everybody, yeah-eah… Rock your body right, Backstreet’s Back… ALRIGHT!” We both sounded almost too powerful at the end of that novel line, screaming out, “Alright” like it was the most important thing in the world. Our bodies continued to move around in tune to an invisible beat, the zombies… somehow managing to capture the techniques that we were using, joining in with the dance at the same time. I can’t explain what happened to the two of us next, it was almost as if we’d become part of the ‘Zombie Nation’ without losing any of our blood or skin. My body temperature began to stabilize at its normal level, my hands started to become less clammy and the feeling of a cold chill slowly began to vanish from my back… running back to the location where it’d come from. Ryan Moore seemed to feel the same as I did, his face beginning to show a distinct enjoyment of the song and dance session with these beings.. even if it was to just save ourselves. Ryan and I looked to one another and stared at all of these creatures as they continued to dance in the way that those infamous Backstreet Boys had done before them. We sighed loudly and slowly began to dance through the gaps that were created by these demonic people. Once we’d come to a point where there was nobody infront of us, both Ryan and myself ran away into the blanket of the night, promising to never ever wander into the valley that was ‘Raccoon City’ again. -- Production End Well, maybe this didn’t entail the kind of happy ending that you may have been looking for, but at least both myself and Ryan… we managed to dance our way our of being lunch for a few thousand zombie people. At the same time, like I said we did… we totally improved the “Everybody” music video that was done by the ‘Backstreet Boys’. Ladies and Gentlemen, all I do for now is warn you to stay tuned… because, one day, when you least expect it… we’ll strike again. It may not be in the deadly confinements of a zombie-infested village that has us singing and dancing to the tune of the ‘Backstreet Boys’… but it may very well have us perfecting the works of N’Sync… O-Town, or yet another one of our traumatic enemies. For now… Good Night
