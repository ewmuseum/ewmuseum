9 Ball Bet

11 January 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1-11-02 RP 1 Turmoil Jan. 14th Neon lights flash brightly in a tinted window. A thick blanket of cigarette smoke carries a stale scent of beer and cheep cologne. Teresa rests her back against the wall of this Nashville bar. She wears her usual favorite Levi’s and Tennessee Titans sweatshirt. With a Bud Ice bottle in one hand and a pool stick in the other, she watches as her comrades Duke Williams decked out in his Stetson and snakeskin boots, and Abner in a simple pair of worn out jeans and a Winston tee-shirt with the sleeves cut out, involve themselves in an innocent game of nine ball. In the background a live ZZ Top tribute bland plays ‘Sharp Dressed Man’. “I got bids on Abner with this game. Then I’m gonna show him how a nine ball is really played.” Both men move their attention off the pool table to look at Teresa. Duke has had the table the whole game and there is only one ball left. Duke adjusts his cowboy had and grabs his beer from a nearby table. “How you figure that Hossette? I got this ole boy beat. Abner ain’t got a chance against the Malice Man.” Abner growls low under his breath and grabs an empty Mt. Dew bottle, which he uses to spit. Through the entire game, he has been standing beside the pool table, stick in hand watching pretty women walk by while Duke helps himself to 8 of the 9 balls. Teresa gives Abner a quick glance and returns to Duke already aiming for his last shot. “You’ll miss and Abner will take over. He’ll win. Bet ya a bottle of Jack.” Abner still not paying much mind to the game has his eyes fixed on a slender woman’s rear as she bends over taking a shot at the table next to theirs. Duke sees this and flashes Teresa a cocky smile. “It’s a deal.” Teresa pokes Abner in the rib with the end of her pool stick to get his attention back to the game. The redneck jumps causing the woman he was talking to giggle. His face turns red and after he throws Teresa a sharp glare, he watches as Duke takes aim for the 9 ball in left corner pocket. The move appears to be a simple direct shot into the hole. The Malice Man carefully angles the cue tip so that it would hit the cue ball perfectly. Teresa takes a sip of her Bud Ice and Abner spits more dip into his Mt. Dew bottle. The pool stick is drawn back and BANG! The cue ball brushes the side of the 9 ball and falls in the corner pocket. “Dammit Tomas!” Duke blasts out. Abner takes the cue ball out of the corner pocket and places it on the table for the move that could win the game. Teresa shrugs innocently. “What? I only told you what would happen. Looks like you’ll owe me a bottle of Jack Daniel’s. “Umph! The game ain’t over yet.” Duke replies and stands next to Teresa. Abner takes a minute to find the perfect angle, aims, and BANG! . Game over. “Well, Malice Man ole buddy, you owe me some booze.” Teresa reaches her arm around Duke and pats him on the back. Abner is too busy flexing for a group of ladies to notice that there was even a bet made. “Those pretty Hossettes haven’t seen a real man yet.” Duke gives Teresa a wink and joins Abner. He unbuttons his shirt and drops it on the floor. Teresa rolls her eyes watching even more females gather around the two wrestlers. “You’d think they never seen a man with muscles.” Teresa mumbles to herself then decides to entertain her ownself. She takes out the 9 balls from the pool table pockets and makes a diamond shape with the balls. She lines the white cue ball to a particular ball, aims, pulls back with the stick and BANG! Clears the table with one move. “Damn, I’m good.
