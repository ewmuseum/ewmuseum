Getting the Call

10 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: October 10 Getting the Call “Your in next, sir.” “Ok.” I still didn’t know what I was doing here. One day, I’m sitting at home in my small suburban apartment and the next thing I know, I get a call from the British Emperor’s men, saying that I’m 67th in line to the throne, through my dad’s small sized family. The man that phoned me said that him and many Royal Advisors had been watching me, along with the rest of the distant relations to the throne but I had what it takes. I was smart and had dreams and goals and had recently graduated from Oxford with a scientist degree. I was twenty-two and was settling into life. Now, I was walking down a fairly big hallway. I passed dozens of doors on my way to the king thrones, the most elegant room in the whole castle that I was in. The room was at the end of the hallway, behind two, wooden doors. The man that was leading me, opened the door and stepped aside for me to walk in. As I entered, I looked up, starry eyed at the room before me. The roof was a good thirty feet up and hanging from the roof were lights and fans, hanging and spinning around and around. There was a red carpet, more elegant and perfect than the one in the hallway that led all the way up five steps and to the kings throne. Beside the carpets, were white, pillars that led beside the carpet up to the throne. I walked up to the throne on the red carpet and before I had to take the five steps up to the throne, two guys, fairly big stopped me shortly. “You may go up ahead, sir.” With that, they bowed slightly which made me feel a bit special. I walked up to the throne to the king, who was in his forties but still in very good shape. The king was weary a gold attire, like a priest would wear at Christmas, only more shiny and expensive. It was awkward because I didn’t know what to say but thankfully, the king spoke first. “Mark, your just like the pictures I have seen of you. A smart, average, British boy.” I was taken at what this king must’ve knew. He had obviously done his math on me and that I was born and raised in Windsor, England. My parents, Lily and William had raised me well in a fairly big house. I had honours in College and all the way through school for that matter, which must’ve helped in picking me, 67th in line to the throne to see the king, but why? I hadn’t really been told why I was here. “Your hair is a little longer than normal, right?” Wow! I hadn’t really expected someone with such high authority to really have said something so normal. I expected him to talk with authority and elegant speech. But, nevertheless, I had grown my hair a bit long than normal. I had black hair, down to just my shoulder, no longer. People had said that I was a focussed man with heart and will, which helped how I looked and my outlook on life. I had a small, black goatee around the top of my lips and a small one underneath. I usually just liked wearing normal clothes. Dress pants and a flannel for my professional life and a regular t-shirt and pants for everyday. “I’ll take that as a compliment.” God, how stupid was I when I said that! I wanted to make a good impression here but I didn’t think I was and I think I was starting to sweat! “Down to business,” said the king, looking at me, “I have talked to your parents already and they have said it is okay but I need you to do something great. Your profile has said that you are a very smart and eager man with hopes and dreams but would you be willing to go away from your original plans to do a special task?” I had tried to understand everything that he had said but words were very hard to understand, with his English accent. I had managed to understand enough, though. “Yes. If my parents have said that this plan is okay, than I will do the task. May I ask what the task is about?” The king looked at me with great wisdom. Creases were shown in his forehead and his blue eyes glowed mysteriously. “Not yet my child. First, you must make your way through vigorous training which will require tons of work but soon, you will be ready.” “Ok.” I wanted to know what the task at hand was as much as anybody else but I knew that I had to be true to the king and respect his wishes to not tell me yet. “Your personal trainer will be James, he will show you your room and give you more of an outline of the task that is at hand that I have picked you to do.” From the door behind me, it opened and in stepped a man. The man was wearing a black mesh shirt and black, fencing pants, very thing fabric. The attire was something special, I could tell. His face was also very strong-like. He had short, black hair that was parted to two sides like a wedge. He himself had a small goatee under his lips. As he walked up the stairs, he said. “I am James. I will show you your room. Please follow me.” It was all he said before he walked down the stairs. Before following him, I turned back to the king and bowed like the two men had before when I entered the room. A small smile spread across his face as he nodded at me, looking proud that I was the right choice for the job. I turned back to James who was still walking out the room. I speed-walked down to him, joining up with him as we walked out of the room, closing the doors behind him. I didn’t know or understand the task at hand. All I knew was that I was distant family relations to a great king and that he had chosen me to do a special task. What the task was, I only knew that it was something special, nothing more but I was determined to find out more on the task and perform the task, whatever it may be. fin
