Six Flags [Part 2]

27 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 06/27/02 When we last left our Civil Rights Hero, he was with his good friend, Duzza, and they were in an amusement park. Jordan and Duzza were trying to get their minds off of the huge Pay Per View matches in which both of these young African Americans could become titleholders. Jordan knocked a kid out, literally. It was an accident of course; Jordan thought the kid was trying to cut in front of Jordan in line for KFC. So Jordan ran up, and knocked the kid in the back of the head with a forearm. Needless to say his parents weren’t happy. Then, Jordan and Duzza entered the KFC and tried to order their food, they were shocked to find that this KFC was fresh out of chicken, due to the rush of people in the park that day. Jordan began screaming of some sort of Government conspiracy in which the Government would try to deprive Jordan X Shabazz and Duzza of their KFC, so they could lose their matches at the Pay Per View. John Crichton was a part of a military, meaning that he worked for the Government, and knowing Jordan’s paranoia, he put two and two together. As Jordan and the DUZZER left the KFC, we cut away. That was part 1, now for part two! The scene opens up in the same amusement park we left at last times, Six Flags in Memphis, Tennessee. The sun was shining brilliantly, and the heat was heavy. Jordan X Shabazz and Duzza were standing by a huge map of the park. Duzza was decked out in baggy khakis, and a R. Kelly t-shirt. On his feet is a pair of Lugz boots. Covering the nappiness of Duzza’s hair is a wave cap. Jordan X Shabazz is dressed in black Nike sweat pants, a black Malcolm X t-shirt. On Jordan’s feet are some white Nike cross-trainers. Jordan’s hair is picked out into a small afro, it’s nice and brushed, unlike Rush Mason’s nappy head, but let’s not get into the nappiness of that poor man’s head. Duzza points to a place on the map, and Jordan screams. Jordan X Shabazz: “ARGH, DUZZER!! YOU’RE A GENIUS!! LET’S GO!!” Jordan X Shabazz and Duzza begin walking quickly to the place where Duzza pointed to on the map. We see that they are headed towards a large ride called the “Thunder dome.” Jordan and Duzza see the large line. Duzza looks at Jordan, dejected. Jordan X Shabazz: “Don’t worry Duzza, just follow me.” Jordan begins walking through the line cutting through everybody saying, “Excuse, Civil Rights coming through,” and Shabazz was getting through the line. He wasn’t being stopped by anybody, until he got near the front. Instead of waiting for the few people in front of them, Jordan and Duzza continued to try and weasel their way into the front of the line. Just as they are about to get to the VERY front of the line, a security guard grabs Jordan’s arm. Security Guard: “Excuse me sir, you’re going to have to go to the back of the line.” The security guard is rather large, and he looks really serious, and is not amused by Jordan’s maneuvering into the front of the line. Jordan X Shabazz: “Excuse me, Mr. Cracka sir, get your hand off of my arm please.” The security guard obliges Jordan’s wishes and he lets go of Jordan’s arm. Jordan X Shabazz: “Now, I’m Civil Rights, and so is my friend here…” Duzza nods. Jordan X Shabazz: “And if you force me to the back of the line, I WILL FILE A LAWSUIT AGAINST THIS ENTIRE PARK!! THIS IS RACISM!” Jordan screams loudly. The security guard knows the effects that a potential lawsuit could cause, so he allows Jordan onto the ride. ************ 10 minutes later… Jordan and Duzza come off the ride. Duzza is showing no effects of being excited, and/or sickness. Jordan X Shabazz on the other hand, has a sparkle in his eyes. He’s excited that the ride came out the way it did, it was better than he assumed it would be. Jordan also appears to be sick. He’s walking drowsily. Jordan X Shabazz: “Duzzer…. I…. Should… sue…. These…. Crackas….” Jordan says while running towards a public restroom where he will go to vomit. Duzza stands outside of the restroom, waiting for Jordan X Shabazz to come out, so they can continue on their day of madness and fun. While Duzza is waiting, Duzza spots something. It’s a little girl, in a baby’s carriage, holding two unopened bags of Carmel popcorn, and cotton candy. As Jordan comes out of the restroom, he looks at Duzza. Duzza grins, and he looks at the girl in the baby carriage, which is unattended. Duzza: “Jordan, you hungry right?” Jordan nods. Duzza: “Hold up, just follow me right quick, I’ll get us somethin’ to eat, it ain’t no KFC, but it’s somethin’ to snack on ‘til we find somewhere we can get hooked up with some chicken.” The Duzzer walks near the baby carriage, he looks around, repeatedly, making sure that he isn’t being watched. When he’s sure that nobody is watching him, Duzza reaches into the baby’s carriage and he snatches the candy! He turns to Jordan and he tosses Jordan the bags, as they are making their get away somebody screams. “HEY! YOU TWO! COME BACK HERE!!” Needless to say, Duzza and Jordan start running. It’s been said before, and I’ll say it again, when you hear on the news that a bunch of white people get shot at work, or at school, or whatever, it’s not too shocking. But notice you never hear about a bunch of black people getting shot or killed in groups like that, it’s because black people run. Cedric The Entertainer said it on the Kings of Comedy, and this is one of those cases. Black people run when they see other black people run. After they’re done running, they’ll find out why they were running. Soon ONE white man, who is angry over being violated while he was using the restroom, is chasing enough, as Jordan and Duzza. His child was holding his food, and now, Jordan X Shabazz is holding his food. Behind Jordan and Duzza is a group of black people. One of them catches up to Jordan. Person: “Why are you running?” Jordan is obviously winded, and about to come to a stop as he realizes that the man is no longer chasing him. Jordan X Shabazz (Panting): “This… white… man… was…. Chasing… me…” The all look at Jordan, and sigh heavily, they start walking back to where they were before starting to run with Duzza and Jordan. Jordan and Duzza begin eating the food they stole as they being walking to another one of those “You are here” maps that white people make up in their spare time. As Jordan and Duzza are finishing up their food and they near a ride that they want to ride, a woman steps in front of Jordan and smiles. She’s beautiful. Shoulder length blonde hair, blue eyes, she is dressed in a white half t-shirt, and some tight blue stylishly faded jeans. She’s the opposite of what Jordan likes to support. She looks in Jordan’s eyes. Woman: “Are you Jordan Copeland?” Jordan is unfazed, she could have easily gone onto the Internet, and pulled up the PCW’s Website, and read his bio, which would have included his real name. Woman: “I’m Mary Retina, you impregnated me back in high school. Birch mount Collegiate.” Jordan looks stunned, almost as stunned as his friend Duzza. Duzza: “Jordan, you know this white girl? YOU IMPREGNATED A WHITE GIRL? HAHAHAHAHHAHA! WAIT ‘TIL I CALL ‘THE NEWS’ ABOUT THIS! HA, I’LL CALL THE TORCH TOO! 1WRESTLING.COM! ALL OF THE NEWS SITES!!” Jordan is dumbfounded, he doesn’t know what to say, and when he finally does speak all that he can say is… Jordan X Shabazz: “You’re crazy… I never touched a white girl in high school…” Jordan thinks about what he said. Jordan X Shabazz: “Well, I never touched YOU in high school. As Jordan tries to walk away from the woman, Jordan is grabbed on his right arm, again. This time, by a masculine hand… To…Be…CONTINUED!!
