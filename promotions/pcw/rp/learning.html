Date: Filmed May 20th, 2003 'So, what you're trying to tell me is that God came down while you were crushed in that car and just...healed you with a gift?' We join our newly ordained Messiah as he takes a brisk walk along the steps of Mount Olympus, site of the first Olympic Games. The historical statue has brought upon such lineage as any man whom has ever taken part in an Olympiad. As Dante Jackson takes his walk up these marble steps, the resounding voice of his wife Tracy reverberates throughout his ears. Dante: 'Dear God!' He turns around, finding a small piece of folded trash lying alongside the monument, as Tracy looks on, holding onto her purse and watching the small piece get tossed into a trash recpticle. Dante rises back from the bended position, smiling a bit before the trees been rustling behind him. Tracy: 'Oh babe, stop thinking somebody's out here to kill you.' Dante: 'But - you need to know! There are people out to get you when you become a newly ordained Messiah. They come back, chasing you through out the woods and such before you defend yourself. Its one of those...' Tracy: 'Male bonding things?' He nods towards her, sending her in upheavel as she folds her arms along her breastplace. Dante grimces towards himself as he continues his pace, this time focused away from the small bridge connecting the otherside of the park with its entrance. HEEEEEEOOOOO! From out of a basic nowhere srpout three men, dressed in black karate gear with their faces shrouded in black mask. Waving karate star in the air, the men inch closer and closer towards Dante, continuing ever close as the Messiah shrouds his wife by standing in front of her, wrapping his arms around her and looking at each one of the ninjas, one on the left, one on the right, and one dead ahead in his center sight. Ninja #1: 'Sir, you shall walk away from these premices as our kind is treated here with respect and dignity. Yours is not.' Ninja #2: 'Leave!' Dante looks gestured at Tracy who only shrugs her shoulders in wonder as Dante can only smile towards the men, leaving them wanting to wonder themselves. The third ninja with a cocky smile on his face tosses a punch along the base of Dante's jaw, but Dante ducks under in a Matrix esque swim move before elbowing the ninja in the stomach. The others look on in surreal horror as Dante stands over their fallen comrade, flinging his fingers away in waiting as another begins to step up towards him, trying to test the new skill of the Lyrical Messiah. He tests him, throwing a four fingered sandwich to the white T-shirt covered pec muscle, but hits nothing but rocky muscle tissue. The man stomps back, clutching his wrist in the process, before moving towards Dante's head. Dante leans back, catching the top of the man's lobe and clutching it in a tight grasp. Dante: 'I haven't done this one since middle school.' Tracy: 'Its a bit trivial don't ya think?' The Messiah releases the small ninja as he falls to the grass, ass first. Tracy: 'You're beating up upon 5'8', one hundered and forty pound men who are not only smaller than you, but not even carrying the same mental capacity as you!' Dante holds his chin for a quick second, running over his wife's words inside of his head. As if a lightbulb pops and breaks over the dome of the Supreme Shit Talker, he shrugs out of it as Tracy stands, waiting for an answer. Finally, he gives her an answer. Dante: 'So? They're good sparring partners!' Tracy sighs at the six foot, four inches of Dante as he flicks the small ninja off of him, leaving him to be eating dust as well. In close quarters combat, the style of fighting is merely punches and kicks, but in open area combat, there is no style. Just street brawling. A third ninja rises out from behind Dante, giving Tracy short chills up her spine as the blade of the ninja star comes desperately close to the eye of the Messiah. He struggles for a bit as the ninja has clenched his neck tightly, pulling farther and farther back as he can before motioning over for his comrades to join in with him in the mele`. Dante looks towards both sides of him, one ninja crawling back to his feet, huffing and puffing while the other cracks his neck in a show of his readiness. One reveals to have nunchucks, waving them around in old school bravado that Dante could clap if there were need to do so. The other swiftly turns his torso sideways as he swipes a small piece of paper out of Tracy's handbag, sending her into a detailed search of what she's missing. The third ninja springs into action, trying to sweep our hero down from behind, but Dante will have none of it. He blackflips his way out of the situation, looking over as the second Ninja tosses a star aimed right for his head! Dante: 'HI-KEEBA!!' In a complete roar, he ducks under the ninja blade as it swims through the air with balance and precision, finally landing upon the ground a few inches away from Tracy. Dante sweeps the aisleway in front of him, knocking the second ninja down with a front kick to the abdomen. The third shakes out the cobwebs, slamming a fist into the back of Jackson. He leans forward in a pivot move, but backhands the silent warrior, crushing his jaw line in the process. He swings in the air, somersaults forward before landing on his back, hard. Ninja #3: 'Owww...' With the second and third ninjas disposed of, Jackson makes his way towards the first one who imeediately sends a big roundhouse kick in his direction. Dante weaves under it, popping the man in the mouth with a straight left hand. Picking up the nunchucks one of the ninjas left over, the first ninja swings them around, almost clocking Dante and knocking him out cold. The Messiah graps one of the nunchucks, palms it into his clutches before swinging it back, nailing the small ninja and sending him sailing through the air. Dante: 'That should do it.' With an excited roar, Dante poses in a Ken/Ryu stance, tossing up the peace sign before Tracy taps him on the shoulder, alerting him of her presence. Tracy: 'You forgot the grocery list!' Dante: 'Well I'll be DAMNED!'
