Two very good questions... that would usually be followed by a remark like... 'Oh... so close, better luck next time or...' .... 'Yes, you were going along the right lines with that answer... but I'm afraid its wrong'... instead of that generall bull that comes from all of these narrators our brother figures, Gameshow voiceovers... Meh, regardless of that ever so kind intermission from the 'Whipsnade Wild Animal Park', we are joined in the large room... by a rather skinny individual who's decided to sit down in a nice white sofa. He looks around the immediate area with what could appear to be a hint of confusion spreading upon his face... or, maybe like us, we can all see that the room is white... with paintjobs that would make even the most unable artist seem like Picasso... or somebody famous in the world or drawing. The character seated... in this small chair... in the center.. of the room, looked into the source of screen viewing. He smartens himself up... taking a few seconds to press out any visable creases and cover any stains before motioning for the 'piece' to move in.
