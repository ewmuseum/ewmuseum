The Call

16 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: June 16, 2002 Monday, July 15, 2002(11:44 pm) Hello…(says with a yawn)…who is this… …why r u calling so early in the morning? What if John answered the phone, you would have gotten me in trouble with him. I am hiding something from him not you! I know he didn’t but what if he did. I will call you tomorrow! Jaden, who are you talking to? John turns over and looks at the clock, with his eyes barely open while as yawn flutters out of his mouth. Its 11:46, who is it, give me the phone… As Jaden hands John the phone, he gets a dial tone… …It was the wrong number! Yea, ok, sure…go back to sleep. Jaden slides back down underneath the covers wrapping her around them while she gazes at the ceiling fan rotating above her slowly. The fan is slowly putting her asleep, not before long she falls asleep, with head on John chest. Tuesday, July 16, 2002(7:03 am) Jaden is awake by rattling dishes. She becomes nervous because John might be good in the ring, great in the (well I think you know where I am getting at!) but he is no chef. Jaden scoots her feet off the bed sliding them into her slips as she heads to the kitchen to check up on John. She see him fumbling around mixing eggs, frying bacon, while he also tries to flip pancakes. She leans against the wall hand over mouth. She doesn’t know what to say or do. What are you doing? She says as she begins to laugh looking at John in little apron. That apron looks good on you to! Why are you in the kitchen you can’t cook…? Well thank you MISS JADEN! I was just trying to fix you a little anniversary breakfast, since I was away last week. …umm John the food. The FOOD! FOOD! You are burning the food and grease is popping all over the floor. That’s sweet that you want to make me breakfast but how about you go get some from the deli down the street. It should be open right about now. Ok; Are you sure you don’t want me to fix you any breakfast. As her eyes light up with fear and torment, she pushes John out of the kitchen. I am positive. John chuckles, Ok if you say so! As John leaves out the door Jaden runs to the phone in the bedroom bypassing the cordless phone next to the door on the wooden stand. She reaches inside the desk drawer and retrieves a small folded piece of paper with a number on it with the letters D. on it. …Hello, ok I can talk now I think John probably will be back soon. She says peeping through the window shades. …I sent him to the store. Don’t worry about him; let’s talk about us. Ok! Ok, I am fine with that. Are you going to let me know, when? You sure, let me know ASAP, I will be thinking about all day, today (laughs)! “…I think John might be on his way. If he found out about us, I don’t know what I would do. I would probably have to move in with you. …I know you would like that! … found out about us, I don’t know what I would do. I would probably have to move in with you. …I know you would like that!” “…HELLO! …HELLO!” (Click)…John slowly lowers the phone and leaves the motel room. …Oh I heard the door got to go. I will talk to you later bye!
