1st rule of payback... intimidate...

12 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: April 12, 2003 {{{{Scene}}}}The scene opens up within a sinister looking room. The lights in the room are that of a dimmed red and are all focused on a single section of the room, where you see a huge carved log it seems standing, and tied to it, is none other then AJ Trinidad’s kangaroo, Crespo. The door that is behind the camera opens and the camera turns as the room fills with the white light of the corridor. Smoke walks through the door and is obviously not in a good mood from the week he’s had. He walks through the room and hits the light switch, changing the dim red light to a bright white light that illuminates the entire room. Across from Crespo you now see a huge mirror, or what is obviously a one-way mirror, so Smoke and his guards can keep their eyes peeled on their prisoner from a distance. Smoke walks up to the kangaroo, which seems extremely depleted from its original form. It looks as if it’s been to hell and back and it’s on yet another trip there. Smoke taps the kangaroo on the stomach, causing it to stir a bit in it’s chains, and it looks down towards Smoke and sneers a mean type of sneer that would make a wild animal look tame. Smoke chuckles a bit and just leans against the near wall that the log is adjacent to…. Semore Smoke: Heh. I know what you’re thinking Crespo, how could anyone be so mean? Well, I was being nice to you to begin with, that’s because Trinidad wasn’t as much of a thorn. But lately he’s even fooled me once or twice, and I don’t enjoy being fooled by anyone. Adam Stryker was supposed to send him on a wild goose chase, but instead, he’s teamed up with Trinidad and has decided to help fight against me. I’m glad I never told Stryker where I was hiding, but he has enough clues to figure it out and that’s not good. So, you’re still my insurance policy, with Trinidad and Stryker out there looking for me, I’ll be sure to keep you here and use you as a defense. They better not think about coming after me now. {{{{Smoke is interrupted as one of his henchmen enters the room with an urgent sound to his voice…}}}} Henchman: Boss! Boss! We’ve got a small problem. {{{{Smoke turns and sneers at the henchman then asks…}}}} Semore Smoke: What is this so-called “problem”? Henchman: A report has come in that AJ Trinidad and Adam Stryker have been spotted here in New Jersey. {{{{Smoke’s eyes light up in surprise…}}}} Semore Smoke: What!? Now!? They couldn’t have figured out where I was that quickly! {{{{The henchman shrugs…}}}} Henchman: We don’t think they know where you are though, so you’re still safe. They’re just in New Jersey searching for you, they’re headed towards Atlantic City now. Do you want me to make sure our Casino workers are posted on them if they happen to show up? {{{{Smoke nods his head…}}}} Semore Smoke: Yes. Make sure that all reports about them come in directly to me so I can keep posted on where they are. In the ring I’m safe because they can’t do much to hurt me other then fight, but out in the real world, I’ve got to worry about Trinidad trying to kill me, or whatever. Oh, speaking of wrestling, is my private helicopter going to be ready tomorrow to lift off and head towards Indiana, where Turmoil is being held? {{{{The henchman nods his head towards Smoke and cracks a grin…}}}} Henchman: It’s being filled up with fuel now and it’ll be on top of the roof by tonight. What time do you plan on leaving tomorrow? Semore Smoke: As early as I can, I don’t want to chance Trinidad and Stryker finding me around here. They’ll have to wait until Tuesday if they want to have any remote chance in hell in getting their hands on me. Now if you’ll excuse me, I’ve got some things to discuss with the kangaroo. {{{{The henchman nods his head and exits the room without a word. Smoke turns to face the kangaroo again and speaks in a angry tone…}}}} Semore Smoke: You see Crespo, you’ll have to keep me safe for tonight. And you’re coming with me tomorrow for Indiana, I think it’s time to show that I will do whatever I want with you and that’ll be sure to keep Trinidad on guard of what he does. He in anyway comes near this location, then you’ll be harmed. I hope like hell he’s watching this promo, because if he is, he’ll be sure to stay away. Tomorrow we leave, so I’ll be sure to feed you your weekly fill of food. Good night Crespo, for this week could be your final week of life, and tomorrow might be the first day of a real hell. {{{{Smoke turns and hits the light switch again, dimming the lights and cutting on the dimmed red light. He exits the room and closes the door behind him, darkening the room. Smoke then walks into the next room where you find the other end of the window that is facing into the room where Crespo is. There are lots of monitors that are positioned on Crespo in several different spots in the room. Also, there is a life monitor that is detecting the heart beat of the kangaroo, Crespo must stay alive if Smoke wants to use him for his plans, so Smoke wants to make sure that all ways of his life is monitored just right so he doesn’t have any chance of losing his insurance policy with AJ Trinidad. There are three henchmen sitting in the room, a different one monitoring a different part of the monitors. Smoke taps the closest one on the shoulder and the henchman turns around to face Smoke…}}}} Semore Smoke: Be sure to tell me when its’ time to feed Crespo, I want to be here personally to feed him. {{{{The henchman nods his head and looks back through the window towards the kangaroo…}}}} Henchman #2: Yes sir, I’ll be sure to call you. You’ll be in your office, correct boss? {{{{Smoke nods his head as he exits the room…}}}} Semore Smoke: Yes, I’ll be in there, this week will be the week of hell… {{{{The scene fades as Smoke exits the room and walks down the corridor towards his office…}}}}
