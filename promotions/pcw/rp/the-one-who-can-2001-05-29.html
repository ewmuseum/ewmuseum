The One Who Can

29 May 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5-29-01 [Fade In] [PCW Rising Star, Ryan “Streak” Stephens is sitting in a rather small room consisting of about eight chairs, a coffee table covered with magazines, and some small shrubbery lining the walls. Stephens sits at one of the chairs very near the coffee table and opposite the window in the wall directly in front of him. We can see only the lady’s head through the window, and she seems focussed on something we can’t see in the window, most likely a computer. She looks up momentarily and peeks over the counter jutting out from the wall beneath the window.] Secretary: The office is a little busy today Mr. Stephens. It will probably be ten to fifteen minutes before we can get you back. Streak: It’s no problem mam, thank you. [Ten or fifteen minutes! To most this would seem a long and annoying time to wait for a dentist; however, throughout Stephens’ career he has learned that patience is a virtue. Patience is what allows Stephens to realize that Prime Time Championship Wrestling is one of the best federations in the world, and that it will take me a great amount of hard work, discipline, sweat and even blood, to establish himself as a true competitor within the PCW. Patience is what allows Stephens to understand that even though he’s been wrestling for almost four years now, he still has a lot to learn. Patience is what will allow Stephens to learn from his mistakes, and to eventually win the Rising Star Championship. A championship that in retrospect may seem small, but is a HUGE step in establishing yourself in PCW. He may not have a gimmick, but at least he has patience.] [Stephens stares at the pictures on the wall, various paintings of nothing in particular hung to add a little flare to the boring and dull waiting room. While he stares he envisions himself competing in the upcoming School’s out brawl on Friday Fusion. He imagines what it would be like keep a star like Xavier Kannon, or Miyagawa from being the first out. Though, by fighting this match he is merely trying to get more exposure, more heat, Stephens still dreams of what it would be like to withstand the brutal beating that he will most likely receive and to hold the PCW extreme title above his head.] [Heart. Stephens has always had the heart, and determination to prevail in such matches. Letting his mind take him to a place where he doesn’t feel the pain, Stephens is often able to persevere and prevail under the most difficult adversity. And this brawl, it will be a situation in which there will be much adversity for everyone involved. It’s been about three minutes now and we anticipate Stephens to pick up a magazine or something and flip through it until he is called to the back; however, he doesn’t, his thoughts keep wondering about.] [Guys like Miyagawa, Xavier Kannon, and The Rush are out to snag this belt, and whose to stop guys of that caliber. Lovecraft seems to want the belt, but that old man surely can’t compete with the likes of the other three when it comes to such a brutal battle as the one that will ensue come Friday in that schoolyard. What about fellow rising stars Mark Makonie and Jordan Shabazz you say? Well chances are they’ll be to occupied by each other to worry about getting out of the school yard, and besides that Makonie might claim the rising star title earlier in the evening and careless about the extreme title.] [Stephens is staring at the ground now, in one of those dazes where you just want to snap your fingers in front of their face and tell them to snap out of it. His buttocks is on the end of the chair and he’s leaning back so that his shoulders rather than the middle of his back rest on the back of the chair. He continues debating with himself who can step up and challenge the three men he considers to be favorites for the extreme title. Tubular Tommy is hungry for a win after he and his partner lost to Suicidal Tendencies, a pair of “generic eric’s” if you will, using the term seemingly coined by Xavier Kannon. But he’s not used to singles brawling, how can he withstand a match like this with limited singles experience.] [It’s becoming increasingly clear to Stephens that perhaps he is the only one who can truly step up and challenge the likes of Xavier Kannon, Miyagawa, and The Rush. He and his fellow stable mate Ricky Thunder of course. Anyhow, back to Stephens, he’s yet to really prove himself in PCW yet, and this could be his opportunity. He doesn’t have to win this match, he’s not expected to win this match, but what if he did? What is Ryan “Streak” Stephens out dueled, out smarted some of the best wrestlers in PCW and became the Extreme Title Championship. Bah, it could never happen, he’s not ready. Oh but it could, and he is. A good showing in a clusterf*ck like this could propel Stephens’ career. Patience Ryan, Patience, don’t get ahead of yourself.] Secretary: Mr. Stephens, Mr. Stephens! [He snaps out of the daze he’s in and looks up towards the secretary.] Secretary: The dentist will see you now. Streak: Thank you. [Stephens rises to his feet and makes his way to a white door in the corner of the room on the same wall of the window. As he reaches for the knob the door swings open, and the secretary, not really an attractive woman, but seemingly nice stands there, ushering him to the back. She’s wearing normal clothes, not a robe or anything that you might see an assistant wear. She seems to be about forty five or so with shoulder length black hair featuring some thin slivers of gray, but not that any of this matters.] Secretary: First door on your left. Mr. Murphy (the dentist) will be with you shortly. [Stephens rolls his eyes, but she doesn’t see. The hall is very short, and he can hear the dentists voice a door or so down telling someone about the growth of their jaw and the urgency in which that person needed to get their orthodontist to fix it. The first thought that crossed his mind was why the hell was he still waiting, but he was patient he told himself, no big deal. He then told himself that he still thinks the orthodontist industry is crooked, and merely finds things wrong to make more money.] [His mind then wanders back to Fusion. A chance to shine, a chance to obtain some respect. He sighs, he thinks about the recent polling of most talented wrestlers. He was near the bottom, but he yearns to move up, and very well may with what would be an unprecedented victory of some of the best talents in PCW. You’d think his mind would venture off to something else. Like if he has a cavity or running home to that gorgeous wife of his, but no, it remains on Friday’s match. Why can’t he get his mind off the match? Is it that important to him?] [As he thinks, the dentist, Mr. Murphy appears in the doorway and asks…] Dentist: How are you doing today Mr. Stephens? Streak: I’m doing fine thank you. Dentist: I don’t recall you ever coming here before, are you new in town? Streak: I’m just passing through. I was hoping to get my teeth checked out, and make sure nothing is remotely loose or anything. Dentist: I’m sure I can handle that. Have you been having trouble with your teeth? Streak: No not really, I just want to make sure they’re all in tact before this really hectic brawl goes down that I’m going to be involved in. I don’t want to come out looking like a hockey player you know? [The dentist seems baffled because Stephens doesn’t appear to be a gangster or a trouble maker or anything like that, but he doesn’t ask any questions. He just nods with a funny look on his face and takes a little pick and a mirror off of a nearby tray.] Dentist: Open wide! [Stephens opens his mouth, and the shot zooms in to the infinite darkness within his mouth. We see the darkness, and then the shot fades from that darkness to complete and utter darkness and we’re gone.]
