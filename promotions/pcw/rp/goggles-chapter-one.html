Goggles...........Chapter One

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 13/14th March 2002 Goggles: A Speculative Showdown… RP#2 for Fusion and #1 for Sabotage… Location: Calgary, Alberta, Canada – Home of our Intrepid, yet highly arrogant hero – ‘The Messiah’ Chris Chase. Date: 13/03/2002. Time: Noon. Weather: More irritating than Dustin Downey with a weasel on his face. Chapter One: Unlucky is not in my vocabulary…nor is fail, or any other random negative adjective… “Ah…the Piece de Resistance! My tenth scale model of the Eiffel Tower, made entirely from milk cartons, is nearly finished! One more section, and at last I’ll be triumphant! And those insolent fools backstage thought I would fail…Dyahahahahahahahahahahahahahaha!” “Dustin, you freakishly moronic and downright stupid pal of mine…would you mind rambling insanely outside? For crying out loud, didn’t I tell you that I needed ABSOLUTE peace for the grandiose spectacle that I’m destined to become? And besides…save the laughter until after I humiliate Goggle-Boy.” “*fumble* *crash* *BANG*” “ARGH! My masterpiece! It’s ruined! And to think it was caused by this stinking weasel! That’s the last time that I order from Weasels Down Your Pants Ltd…and the last time that I use cheap milk cartons in the making of the two-hundredth wonder of the world…” ::.~(The Voice of Chris Chase)~ You know, I honestly don’t know how I’ve come to be THIS lucky. I mean, come on…look at me, look at my talents. To call me blessed is a severe understatement, and one that should never be applied in this context ever again. And do you folk know why, or do I need to draw you a picture? To call me blessed is like submitting to the powers that be, those cretins that censor out some of the truths behind society. You know, like the REAL reason why Dustin is a bungling buffoon, and why they even let Boda-Boy eke a living in this world. By calling me blessed, it’s a depreciation of the entire value of Chase-dom, which in itself is more precious than any amount of Platinum that these Fat cats can muster. I hate understatements…as much as I hate my own luck, at least the bad luck anyway. While I’ve been rotting away in the recesses of Midcard-dom, proven failures like Boda have even gone on to win the PCW World Title…and that of course, is nothing to do with my lack of effort…I’m just…unlucky, in a circumstance respect…and of course, I get myself into these things… It’s almost as if I’m a magnet to these undesirable and quite DAMN annoying elements, attracting tomfoolery in a mile radius… It’s been what, two days since Turmoil, right? Two days since I made one of the hardest bargains in my life, by forging a deal with long time enemy Boda-Boy, to actually help him to win the PCW World Title. And ever since then, it’s not been surprising that folk of all shapes and sizes have been posing the very same old tired question to yours truly. Why? (Sigh) Is it really THAT hard to understand, that you bunch of self-conscious retards can’t tell head nor tail of the implications of handing Boda the World Title? Time and time again, I’ve been the thorn in Boda’s side, defeating him isn’t exactly rocket science…and neither is the reasoning behind the superficially “shocking” happenings on Turmoil. Let’s face it, Boda sucks more ass that a Ream Team/sHs reunion and to fight for a title against a person that I’ve beaten a fair few times is little more than an easily won contest. However, I’ve yet to prove myself against the likes of a certain Goggle-wearing Japo-American... My eyes twitch and flicker from side to side on the television screen before which I sit, staring into a digital camera (Which is wired to the television incidentally) from little more than a tenth of a meter away. In nearly a whole year in this business, I would say that I’d be used to being followed by a camera in every hijinks that I’ve managed to get myself into over the last year…but the fact stands that I’ve hated the camera. The constant feeling of being watched, like some 17 year old Computer Whizkid is treating this like some sort of game, controlling my every action, throwing weird distractions into the mix such as a former Tag Team partner of mine. I convince myself that this is not the case, and with an arrogant smirk and a grunt, I turn my back to the camera, if only to find myself faced with the prospect of consoling a Dustin with low self-esteem after single-handedly destroying his own creation. A milk carton sails across the room, and whacks the door with a slight thud and about much force as Boda-Boy could ever apply to the likes of yours truly, as another television blares in the background….:: Advertisement Voiceover: Ever experienced the true power of watching PCW Professional Wrestling? Have you ever watched the PCW superstars work their stuff in the squared circle, LIVE? Well, you’re not going TO for a VERY long time. The PCW Live events for the next 20 weeks are Sold out. Now please, stop bothering us and go watch the second rate crap of one Vincent K. McMahon… Dustin: ~(Punches the Air)~ Curses, I lost the most vital part to my model…without it, I can’t erect the thing whatsoever. Say, I wonder if my hamster would be able to take the weight as one of the four struts. ::. One can only wonder. Almost giving up entirely, my goofy looking manager stumbles over the heavily strewn floor in my direction, as I swing coolly on my chair. A fluorescent light above me flickers, barely adding to the bright sunlight that pierces through the windows of the apartment building. Yes, that apartment building that has played so many vital roles in the history of ‘The Messiah’ Chris Chase…vital, of course, in the sense of senseless entertainment. The age old Dustin Downey entrance music of Tool’s Aenema blares out of the television on the far side of the room….:: Advertisement Voiceover: WHOO~! I AM JESUS!: Hardback edition. Catch the latest addition to the Dustin Downey book franchise at participating book stores now. WHOO~! I AM JESUS!: The Dustin Downey Autobiography, detailing every movement of Dustin’s since that fateful day in Texas. ::.At the very sound of this, Dustin halts in his footsteps, and thumps the air triumphantly, almost slicing his own hand off in the ceiling fan…quipping to himself in a self-gratifying way….:: Dustin: Well, would ya look at that! Finally, these television boffins had the nerve to air my advertisements…and on primetime television too. That’s astounding, eh Chris? ::.I’ve seen more impressive Boda matches. So thus, I’m entitled to retort with concise reasoning and cocky witticisms about these lard ass folk that sit in front of television screens for three quarters of their home lives….:: Chris: Dustin, must I have to remind you that this is daytime television? Hence why they air some of the lesser advertisements to save them being mistakenly placed later when more consumers are lazing around on their fat asses, watching the tube. Dustin: ~(Laughs moronically)~ Haha Chris! “Day time television”, that’s a good one! Are you implying that Primetime Championship Wrestling promotional advertisements are inferior to the likes of truly brilliant compositions from Downey productions? ::.Leaping up from my seat as if a thousand volts of electricity had just been pumped through my ass, I slap Dustin around the back of his head in a smooth motion from that leap. There is silence for but two seconds.:: Chris: Good Chris Chase, Dustin! I’d never imply such a downright stupid thing. However, I would say that your advertisements are about as much use as talent to Boda-cakes. Heh, you didn’t happen to catch the moment when I literally handing that dolt the PCW World Heavyweight Championship, did you perchance? In the history of the most brilliant things that I’ve managed to pull off…that there, has to be the prototypical example of a Chris Chase caper gone right, you think Dustin? ::.Tossing a milk carton out of his shaking hands, Dustin frowns at the mere mention of the words “You think Dustin?”. The Texan rich boy looks at me with a half-assed irritated expression, tipping up the bulbous purple hat that covers his short brown hair….:: Dustin: How many times have you asked me that one, Chris? Of course I don’t think! Why the hell then did I decide to make a tenth scale model of the Eiffel Tower out of chunky Milk Cartons? Any half-brained person could see that that wasn’t exactly the best material to make a model of a girdered tower out of, eh? EH? Tell me that one, Mister MESSIAH! Chris: Without trying to be as cliché as Diminutive Japo-American maniacs…you truly are a pathetic moron, Dustin! Dustin: Those are the words that I live for Chris…thank you. I mean, being called a moron isn’t an every day occurrence, is it really? ::. To the above question, I shake my head, to Dustin’s typical surprise. He is quite taken aback by my disagreement with him, although he really should be used to it by now….:: Chris: Dustin, I call you a moron at nearly every single calling moment, as if I were constantly answering the call of those cretins that need my assistance once more, the entertaining and wholesome assistance that can only be drawn from the depths of my genetic tract. So I helped Boda win the oh-so lucrative PCW World Heavyweight Title, that’s about as big a deal as Miyagawa is to me, it’s not like Boda’s going to be holding onto it for any longer than a month… ::. It’s a classic feature in the entertainment, comedy world…passed over by many as a lesser act of humour. And I’m not surprised as to why it’s passed over. Cutting in in the very middle of an eccentric rant is possibly more frustrating than having to watch yourself put over a talentless buffoon like Boda, and then realising that beating a five foot ten Japo-American is just as likely an achievement as beating a former NBA Basketball player. And it’s Dustin’s nature to commit such a vile comedic act, to spoil any hope of a clean promotion, far far from the likes of tomfoolery and moronic conversations as what usually tends to be the norm in these parts….:: Dustin: ~(Cutting in)~ Look Chris, I may have been a professional wrestler…but I’m a retard too. So, I’ve deduced one clear fact…that Boda is going to be a very shortlived champion… ::.Slapping my forehead with the hand that isn’t eternally attached to my hip, I sigh exasperatedly at Dustin’s usual bluntness….:: Chris: ~(sighs)~ Don’t you know anything? Meh…forget that question, but Boda already was one of the shortest lived PCW World Heavyweight Champions there ever have been in the history of the federation…lasting for three days…THREE DAYS! ::.Dustin nods in agreement, yet in a vague way that makes it seem as if he doesn’t quite understand what I’m actually getting at. I sigh once more, directing my glance out of the 36th floor window of my apartment block, looking at the tall buildings that box the apartment in. It’s an effect similar to that of claustrophobia, yet, there’s no time to be feeling somewhat queasy when Dustin taxes even the sensible and most rational of people, which for me probably is an impossible task…knowing my track record of course….:: Dustin: Yes…and? Chris: And handing him the PCW World Title is a good idea. It promises that when I achieve Number One contender status, I’ll swiftly pick up the title, and ascend with it into the uncharted regions of PCW Stardom. Miyagawa’s nothing more than a deflated ballbag waiting for his golden era to return to him, it’s obvious that he’s going to subside to let a fresher hero take precedence among the hallowed halls of legendary status, to fade away if only to be pestered by the likes of hasbeens like Jon Kano for crying out loud. That, Dustin, that is a disease that will never grab hold of Chris Chase, wrenching the entertaining life from him like Xavier Kannon sucking the lifeforce out of innocent spectators. I’ll keep one step ahead of fading away…I’m not a quitter, I don’t whine…I ensure that my survival as a hero to the masses is preserved forever more… ::. Yet again, Dustin cuts in…serving as an ideal resbite between random rantings, random rantings that could prove key in a testimonial stage of the whole issue with repeated assaults on both that Goggled Gigalow and Boda-boy…especially when the justification needed for beating Boda around the head with a steel chair is slightly less than eccentric….:: Dustin: So…why fight it now? Could it be that in the past, you were…unlucky, perhaps? Chris: Unlucky will never be in my vocabulary, Dustin…at least in the future anyhow. You see, I’ve failed one too many times to keep failing, and there is a point, especially when Boda is Champion for crying out loud…that I’ve got to pull myself out from under the likes of Jeremy Howard and Miyagawa…and into the limelight, my spotlight…My scene. Jeez…did I really use to say that, Dustin? ::.Dustin nods….:: Dustin: Uh huh…and you kept on rambling about targets and other such random things Chris… Chris: Darn…remind me never to ever think about being nostalgic again Dustin… ::. Dustin nods again….:: Dustin: Never ever think about being Nostalgic again, Chris… Chris: ~(Sighs)~ And you can Justify…that! ::.Fade to White….::
