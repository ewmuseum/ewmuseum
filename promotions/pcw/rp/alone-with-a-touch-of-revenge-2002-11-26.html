Alone with a touch of Revenge

26 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: November 26th, 2002 November 25th 2002Vilas homestead - Bangkok, Thailand12:34 a.m. It was late, probably around twelve thirty if time really mattered. The moonlight glared down, breaking through the open cracks that spread out between the clouds that whisk by like the rolling tumbleweed. The gentle winds blew what few leaves were still a flutter on the ground, flushing them up into the night sky and gently laying them back down on the floor below. Alone he stood; still shocked over the thoughts of last night’s events and the pressure that was rested on his shoulders. The rooftop provided a nesting ground or more of a nest for a one-man flock. All curled up, Vilas sat still, watching his breathe come out and react with the cool air. He didn’t move and he barley flinched a muscle out of place, considering he wasn’t the big to begin with. But alone he sat, like a wounded bird unable to rejoin the flock and hunt for the prey. One by one images played over in his mind, watching and taunting him with every past. Thoughts of nightsticks striking his body, pushing the pain up his spin added more insult to injury, as the image of getting beaten by an actor haunted his imagination, as millions laughed around the world. It was not beautiful or glamorous but sheer horror. How is that possible? I gave everything I had for nothing. I was defeated by a no-body in the business and yet I still survived to receive more punishment. Where was my team? Where was my Alliance in the first place? They didn’t care; they only wanted what was best for them. Typical humans, thinking the weaker have something less to prove. It just shows how I am judged not for my abilities, but rather as a joke that is in progress. I don’t do work in progresses. I only do what is here in front of me and what is reality. Alliances was a calling and more importantly a sign that should bring new light. My elimination was the greatest thing to ever happen in my short primetime career. I have nothing to feel sorry about. Nothing to lose and the world to gain. Revenge will be my key and my ability will be their torture. Vilas stood up and looked out on the city with a New Hope and view. His down fall at Sunday’s Alliances was not in anyway the worst cause scenario, but rather the Christmas present of a lifetime. He was given the gift of revenge, handed down and able to place on anyway at any time. But there was only one person alive who should receive this UN-fortunate twist of fate. The one man who caused more trouble during the week’s prior then at the actual event. He would soon feel the wrath. Vilas walked over to the edge of the roof and looked down in disgust. The people, small from where he was made him sick and sore to the eyes to look at. He spit down on them, disrespecting their life as a whole. He walked up and down the rooftop and looked over the city of his origin He soon would have to leave but only in disgust. Simple minded beings and their primitive ways of life. They have no goals besides money and greed. It shows every where in this world, considering everyone is out for them self and money. I spit on their reason and bit my thumb at their reality. I couldn’t see any of them even fulfilling any goals or promises that isn’t benifical to them. They are all the same, every last one of them. “Look at your selves,” Vilas shouted out, drawing no attention from the crowd below as he got even closer to the edge of the roof. “Your all simple just like Romantico, and you disgust me just as much. It’s wonder he hasn’t killed himself yet for all his foolish games he plays with women and men in general. He isn’t a ladies man; he is just another lost cause, looking for cheap thrills and easy handouts. Such a pity even he can’t see his own fate,” Vilas whispered to himself as he picked out people from his perch on the rooftop. His face turned red with anger as he turned around and headed back up the roof. He took one last look out at the city and spit again on the soil of his homeland, marking his reactions in his own establishment. He smiled before reentering his home and carefully looked up at the moonlight. “Such beautiful light the moon emulates. It’s to bad it’s wasted on these pitiful excuses for human beings. November 26th, 2002City Market - Bangkok Thailand7: 43 a.m. The fast pace action surrounded the market square, as piles on piles of people filled every gapping whole trying to get a bargain on rice and corn. People shoving and pushing their way to the top of trading posts, robbing people of not only money but also the little ounce of dignity they have left. Elbows flew each direction and words of hate of a scarf were thrown out like a breath of air. Vilas’s stood in the middle of the madness, watching and waiting. Simple minded, one goal, one life. Just like Romantico and the whole Primetime family in it’s self. Look at these people. People stealing money from the UN-experience just like the head office. Veterans stealing from the rookies all over a loaf of bread. Where is the justice in any of this as a way of life? Have we gone so low in civilization that we now act like animals? Pathetic… Vilas made his way through the pushing and abusive words to reach the boarder of the market. He looked on in as thousands seemed to flock in like seagulls looking for a scrap of food. Vilas shook his head is disbelief as even young children stole from their elders. It was a punishment rather then a journey to the market. Pain has more fury when simple-minded beings see a way to keep them self-one penny penny above the rest. Can’t they see what is happening? Just like normal, people deprive them self on stealing to win. Playing games that throws off everything else. This is not an act of an intelligent being but rather a coward in it’s self. I am affiliated with cowards every day, have them push me forward when I want to stand still. Trying what ever is necessary to survive. Life isn’t full of teams. TEAMS ARE FOR THE WEAK AT HEART… “Give me that…I had it first…” Same torture every day. It makes your stomach crawl. It makes me wonder why someone such as myself, who is a lot smaller then anyone is more enlightened with life? Is it because I am not over taken by big arms and large muscles, or is it because I don’t care for how much coin I actually bring in? The fact remains my so-called lightweight body is a home to an enormous amount of respect and self control. I’m surprised even these people and those in my own company can even stand someone such as me, without having to take a swing. I dare anyone of them to, you know, just see how far they can push the button. Weight is only a number and when it comes down to it, it’s all about respect and honor. Something I know these people lack, Primetime lacks and of course Romantico lacks. Quote me when I say, “NO MORE”
