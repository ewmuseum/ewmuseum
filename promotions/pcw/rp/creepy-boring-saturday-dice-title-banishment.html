Date: ... They picked the wrong moment to mess with the Neo Void God...street punks...and for their worth, they were sacrificed. Much like D-Kay and Brock...they were no way near decent enough to grace this TV title and don the title of god of television. Akiyama would show them the truth...that they were not worthy of being in his presence... Banishment wasn’t even an option... Present… Cyclonica, we killed them, life spilled in your name. Enough of blood, red sweet offering, held up hot and trembling rich before you. Salt and bitter stinging sweet, in return for ruthless truth. For you. You are witness before the sky, our hand and our skills are yours. We lift our talons, and hundreds fear the arc of our HAND OF FATE. Helpless before our very name. {~Sweat, and shrieking nerves. A drenched field, a tattered battle standard. With your back to your subordinates, your forehead pressed useless to the wall, eyes on the missive in your still taped hands. The Neo Void God, the Celestial seal. Adversaries on their knees.~} They shall not profane the holy, they shall not invade the sacrosanct. It is ours alone, this end, this glorious-- Cyclonica, may we come? Too long dormant, they were busy while we watched, those architects of death. Too much they thought they knew. How could they seek to understand the sweep, the breadth, the very breathing of their existence? Walk across the wounded world, speak a word of un-creation. Souls are raw and earth is blanketed in their own version of darkness, we alone may touch the heavens. {~The one with the fall-dark light-streaked hair, as lush as autumn's ending, the one with the easy laugh. Who never broke a promise. Keep the faith, brother-trust. His eyes were brown, sweet as crushed cinnamon, in the twilight, and it didn't matter who had died or who had shouted victory. Comrades understand. His hands were steady as the sun went down, and he never spoke a word.~} Cyclonica, will you give us back the heartbeat, the shivering water drop of self that we poured into you? Shimmer with strength and open your eyes, and break them all-- shatter them, scatter them, shards of brilliance cast like diamond dust. Nothing so beautiful, Cyclonica, as their destined disaster, the delicious cadence of their dying pulse. You will unmake the world, Cyclonica, and we your only ally. These razor-fine wings tear at the clouds, will rend the bleeding sky. Greater still than battle, or the heat and reach of war, this catastrophe will blind them, bring them screaming to their feet. From this slip of golden universe, along the lines of solar grace, we shall fall into the beginning and banish them all... {~The one with the brown-toned skin, too rich for muddy waters, the one with the awe-colored voice. Who never forgot a thing you told him. Keep the image, idol-worship. His eyes were brown, sharp as fresh dirt, in the afternoon, and it didn't matter if you were wrong or if you were right. Fellow warriors respect. His hands were shaking as the battle began, and he never spoke a word.~} --Here they come, Cyclonica. ~Oh, tumble down our burrowed hole, come step closer into my web. The silk won't hold you, little ones, the strands of my mind cannot keep, cannot catch you tight. Flail your tiny fists at us, strength stuttering through your veins, it will not help you. Ours to dissolve, and watch the ebbing tide that is your legacy... Ours to enfold-- the benison of our blade-edged wings shall bless you, all.~ And then our defenses are split open, all my secrets spinning free. How--? What self flutters there, butterfly arc of his attack so familiar? Too frail for angel's wings, his aura is wrought delicate as spider dreams, as new as raw potential. And we know him. Pharaoh…? …D-kay… {~You taught him all he knows.~} Cyclonica, where are we? Who stands here, how dare he? {~Who are you?~} Head so heavy, memory dimming, we dare not look away. Our eyes are yet my own, my vision un-healing. Eliminate the threat, demolish the unholy. Cherish the pain, it alone will save the world. We wield the vanguard of our hope for you-- {~You were a shining warrior-lord, your face would bow their heads. Now you do not even remember the name of your fist, only a form of your palm.~} Cyclonica we breathe-- Cyclonica we aid-- Cyclonica we come-- {~You must-- beyond the encroaching battle-lines of sanity, beyond the front of doom and clamor of bells-- Remember your name!~} 'Akiyama.' Oh, yes. And we smile, and cool, cool wind blows over us. We lift our head silently, and the green smooth stream wells up from nowhere, like fresh lifeblood struck near the heart, like answers to questions unasked. There. Something there. A seeking glance, an outstretched hand, a distance un-eclipsed. Succor-- the universe never ceases to astonish. Comrades understand. Fellow warriors respect. And we are not alone. {~You died, and died again, every time I touched you. Enlightenment, come again, trip across your soul and fall with hands outstretched into the Celestial Heavens.~} Everything never forgotten is a rush of singing through my veins, and we are weightless in the cloudless sea, letting it carry us home. Past… He walked along the street zombie-like. His eyes stared ahead of him, his feet following their gaze. The pedestrians around him avoided the beeline that he marked out…no one would dare cross his path… But two would do so later on…thinking that they were decent enough to take up arms and do battle against him D-Kay Calyx… Brock Newbludd… Foolishness was never so alive as it was inside these two. Akiyama had nothing left to do at this point. He knew that because of the swerve in morals Pharaoh has taken, the Divine titles were lost for the moment. It’s not like he couldn’t get another person to conquer the division yet again. The Celestial Knight or Blind…Jay Sylo and The End…they both seemed worthy of taking the belts back yet again. But the Akivision belt…that was going to be cemented into his possession for a long time…there was no-frickin-way defeat was possible. The Neo Void God hated these lazy Saturday mornings… Hot, humid, hazy, disgusting weather. The usual attire of the citizens was tossed aside for more breathable clothing…belly shirts, tank tops, short shorts, sandals…but the god ignored all temperature. It was the same black vinyl and PVC for him. Heat? Just another mortal qualm… The god stopped suddenly in the middle of the sidewalk. His gaze tilted vertically towards the sun. The ultraviolet rays pounded into his optical lenses. The light began to swallow up his vision bit by bit… {~Staring into the sun isn’t such a wise maneuver, Akiyama.~} “Meh…” {~I understand your despair, but you think that impairing your optical nerves will make you feel any better?~} “It is nothing but a ball of gas…blazing from reactions. But gases can be neutralized…from heat to chill…and then everything would be frozen solid. Perfect…frozen…unified…harmony.” {~Listen to yourself. You sound like Itagaki Freeze.~} The NVG shook his head, breaking his gaze away from the sun. Cyclonica was right…that’s the last thing he wanted to sound like…just another one time flavor of the month like D-Kay. Brock was another story…he seemed more like the cheese of the month…just kept coming back no matter how many times you tried to return it. Either way…the flavors were soon to pass…and blood would be the only thing left for them to taste in their mouths. These people…they glanced on with wary eyes. The god had been talking out loud to the unknown voice in his head. If you needed explanation of voices in the Neo Void God’s head, then you need know that there is just one. One collective…one unified voice. Anything else that speaks out of the collective voice inside his mind…was foreign. Then there was a tapping at the glass next to him. Akiyama looked over towards the large glass pane to find some teenager staring at him. He waved his hand, motioning for the NVG to walk in. Akiyama sighed before lazily entering the building. Inside, he found a plethora of computers lined about the room, along with people utilizing them. The teen came up to him and smiled. “Hey! Akiyama!! Wow, it’s a pleasure to see you!” he bowed deeply and looked back up at him with admiration in his wide green eyes. Akiyama stared down at this kid in blue jeans and white tee shirt, bewilderment following. “Do we know you?” he replied. “No. I’m a fan. My name’s Max.” Akiyama looked down at the teen’s outstretched hand. Then he looked back down at his face. “Rule number one, child: no touching of the NVG.” He took his hand back and nodded, staring up at him. Akiyama sighed yet again before folding his arms. “Rule number two: No looking the NVG directly in the eye.” Max complied quickly, lowering his gaze towards the boots of the god. “You’re going to have to earn that right just like everyone else that claimed to be our associate. Now…what do you want that you have to interrupt our boring day of sulking?” “Um…” he began, his eyes still down at the floor. “I thought you might wanted to play a game with me online. That is…if you’re not too busy.” Akiyama rubbed his chin. “Well we had a full schedule of walking around talking about how much D-Kay and Brock were losers, but that grows old so fast…it’s like talking about how hot the sun is. Everyone knows it…so why bother talking about it any more? Okay, child…you have yourself a gamer.” “Really?!?!” he looked up at the god with happy eyes. “EYES BACK DOWN.” The entire room looked over in his direction. He gave them all a look that would make someone misdial 911 because of the fear. “WELL???” All of their eyes turned back to their monitors. Akiyama shook his head. “Disrespectful flat-scans. All right, you filthy stink child. Direct us towards the computer so we may thrash you soundly in gaming events.” Max led the god towards a computer in front of the display window. Sitting the NVG next to him, Akiyama opened the Internet Explorer. “First we check our email…so we can see what matches we’re booked in.” going through a few sites, he arrives at his destination…[detail withheld]. “Son of a b!tch.” “What?” Max asked, keeping his eyes away from the NVG’s and ignoring the profanity. “We’re not booked for all next week. Alas…woe and despair has fallen upon us, and we sink yet deeper into the abyss of sorrow and~” “BLING!!” “What the freak was that?” the NVG asked. A box appears on his screen with some dialogue within it. ByronCalver has invited you to Chat Room: PTC. “That’s a chat invite. Those are my friends.” Max explains. “Go ahead in.” Akiyama clicked on Accept, and the rectangular box opened before him, with a list of names appearing on the side box. The text begin to flow through as follows: ByronCalver: Ok, who wants to challenge Rojl1341 to his PTC Continental dice belt? “What?” Max shook his head. “Go on…I bet you could challenge him.” Akiyama sighed before typing slowly.
