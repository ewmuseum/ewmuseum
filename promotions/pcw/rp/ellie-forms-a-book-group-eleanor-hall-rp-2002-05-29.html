Ellie~! Forms A Book Group (Eleanor Hall RP)

29 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 29/5/02 Eleanor Hall has been in Primetime Championship Wrestling since mid-December when her now Fiancé Xavier Kannon signed his contract, ensuring that Wrestling’s #1 Prep was signed as his valet along side him. Throughout her tenure in PCW, she has been the most involved valet in the promotion, intervening in the matters of Xavier and the Ultimate Authority.... ....However, each of those times she has had the comfort of being shielded by not only Xavier, but the entire U.A as well.... But this could all change on Tuesday when she takes part in her first ever singles match against new-comer Jaden... Although Xavier is referee, if he was to do any kind of competent job his valet could be on the end of something not too pleasant from Jaden, possibly the toughest woman in PCW.... But then again, Xavier being fair is hardly likely.... Still, has Ellie~! bitten off more than her preppy little mouth can chew? “Oh... By the way.... I decided to form a Book Group...” Our shot opens up on the Diamond ring we saw Xavier give to Ellie~! on Turmoil, the finger it adorns belonging to a petite hand that holds a White phone handset. “..So they are coming around in a few minutes... I thought it would help pass the time while you are off doing that wrestling thing.... Wait, aren’t I doing that too? Whatever... Anyway... Bye!” The phone is hung up and we see the full figure of Xavier Kannon’s valet, Ellie~! as she turns away from the phone and looks around the living room of Xavier’s Greenwich Village apartment. Eleanor : “Hmmm.... Maybe I should cancel this Book Group thing and train for the match instead?” She looks down past one of the chairs to a foot stall and walks over to it, doing 4 quick step ups on it before stopping and looking rather bored with it already. Eleanor : “Exercise is overrated.... I can get by on my Preppyness alone...” The Prep adjusts her long, wavy Brown hair with Blond highlights after her second or two of activity and heads through into the kitchen which contains such delights as expensive Balsamic vinigers, infused extra virgin olive oils on display.... None of which seem to be open and only acting as props. She looks around at all the items on the work surfaces. Eleanor : “Maybe I should give them something to eat....” As she ponders this she opens up the fridge and has a good look through it, then goes to the cupboard above it and rummages through before letting the door fall shut and seemingly losing all interest in that idea. Eleanor : “Or maybe they will bring the food.... yeah, I like that idea even more....” Zzzzzzzzzzzzzzz!!! Her attention is grabbed as the intercom goes off and she scurries excitedly towards the door where she picks up the receiver mounted on the wall. Eleanor : “Er..... Like, hello?” A rather nervous, stuttering voice then replies, sounding slightly crackley through the intercom. “H-H-H-H-Hello..... I’m here for the B-B-B-B-B-Book Group...” As the man tries to get the words out, Ellie~! raises her eyebrows a his voice, screwing up her face as he speaks and really not looking thrilled at the prospect of letting him into her flat.... ....After he finishes, Ellie~! just stands there, receiver still held up to her head as the man below wonders what the wait is for. “H-H-H-H-H-H-Hello?” Again, the prep look disgusted at the thought of letting someone she considers “lower” than her into her life and then, reluctantly, tries to form an answer. Eleanor : “Er.... Are you, like, mentally ill or something?” The appalled voice then replies, sounding shocked. “I-I-I-I-I-I-I-I BEG YOU P-P-P-PARDON!?!?!?” Eleanor : “Like, no offence or anything, you just, like, SO sound like you are..... Hello?” Before she even finishes her sentence, delivered in her usual innocent way yet still managing to offend, there is a click as the man at the door hands up and leaves. Ellie~! stands there for about 20 more seconds, waiting for a reply before hanging up and looking puzzled. Eleanor : “Hmmm... He must have been called away somewhere.... looks like the Book Group is down to 5.... YAY BOOK GROUP... Wait, I organised it... I’m hosting it.... YAY ME!” She bounces on the spot and gives herself a little round of applause before checking her watch and going to walk back into the living room when... Zzzzzzzzzzzzzzz!!! .....The buzzer is heard again and she hurries excitedly back to the intercom. Eleanor : “Hi... You here for Ellie~!’s Preppy Book Group of Excellence?” A woman replies, wondering if she has the right place. “Er.... Is this the Book Group?” Eleanor : “Yes... Come on up... Goody!” She excitedly presses the button to unlock the door downstairs and she waits impatiently for the woman to make her way up to the apartment. Eleanor : “Like.... Come... On!” The Prep checks her watch as footsteps are heard outside the door and knocking is heard. *Knock* *Knock* *Knock* The impatient Prep then hurriedly opens the door, her pale Blue eyes sparkling with anticipation. Eleanor : “Hello! Welcome to Ellie~!’s Book Grou-... Wait, can I help you?” At the door stands an obese woman in a baggy sweatshirt and cringingly tight leggings... Eleanor looks her up and down, almost looking like she wants to vomit. Woman : “Erm... This is the Book Group isn’t it?” Ellie~! is narrowing her eyes, trying to look away from the woman without it looking to obvious.... And failing in the latter. Eleanor : “Er... Sorry, the Book Group is cancelled...” The obese woman looks at the Prep, confused and wondering what is wrong. Woman : “But just a minute ago you said to come up here!” The Preppiest of Preps looks at the fat woman’s forehead which is glistening with moisture then looks to the stairs, 4 flights from the front door to Xavier’s apartment. Eleanor : “Are you sweating? My Hubbard you are... You’re sweating!” She takes a step back disgusted while the obese woman, still catching her breath wipes the perspiration from her brow. Eleanor : “Sorry.... There wasn’t enough room in the advertisement to put “No Fat People”... My mistake, just a misunderstanding.... Bye bye!” The obese woman’s jaw drops in horror at the Prep’s words. Woman : “What did you say?” Ellie~!, not realising in her Preppiest of minds that she is saying anything wrong continues to try and explain her prejudice. Eleanor : “Nothing personal, I just don’t like fat people near me... I mean, I’m feeling 5 pounds heavier just talking to you..... I fear it is contagious, I talk to fat people.... I start accepting it... I get fat.... Then we have icky Ellie~!, and Ellie~! doesn’t like icky Ellie~!” The fat woman shakes her head and sneers at Ellie~! Woman : “....Bitch...” She turns around and goes to walk away when Ellie~! decides the time is wise to make a comment of her own... As usual the Prep is wrong. Eleanor : “Like..... FAT Bitch!” The woman turns around, having had enough of the Prep and her put downs. Eleanor : “Eeeeeeeeeeeeeek!” The Woman turns into a wobbling blur of obesity as she charges at Ellie~!’s door and the Prep just manages to slam it shut in time and bolts it shut before putting the chain on as well just to be sure. There is pounding on the door as the woman’s big, fat hands drum on it, angry at the Prep’s judgement of her. “I AM SICK AND TIRED OF SKINNY LITTLE STICKS LIKE YOU LOOKING OVER YOUR COPIES OF VOGUE AND COSMO TO LOOK DOWN AT US LARGER WOMEN...” The Prep cowers with her back against the door as the woman outside continues to shout at her. “....STUFF YOUR BOOK GROUP, I WOULDN’T WANT TO BE STUCK IN A ROOM WITH YOU FOR MORE THAN 2 MINUTES.... I’D PROBABLY VOMIT!” The loud sound of her hefty stomps going down the hallway towards the stairs are heard and Ellie~! mutters to herself, getting up off the floor where she was sitting, leaning against the inside of the door. Eleanor : “Well maybe if you started vomiting 10 years ago when you started eating for 7 you’d be more like me!” The Prep dusts herself off and fixes her hair when the familiar.... Zzzzzzzzzzzzzzz!!! ....Is heard again, causing her to jump in shock and residual fear..... She takes a deep breath and holds her chest before answering it. Eleanor : “Er.... Like..... Hello?” “Hi... I’m here for the Book Group.... I like Books.... Some of my best friends are Books.... Well, my only friends are Books...” Her face contorts as a squeaky, geeky voice speaks through the receiver to her.... She rolls her eyes and sighs. Eleanor : “Er.... I think I’m like, going to cancel it...” There is no reply from downstairs and only the sound of heavy breathing is heard.... Eleanor : “Like.... Hello?” An excited, breathless voice replies... “Don’t stop talking....I’m nearly there...” At this the Prep immediately slams the receiver down and screws her face up, shaking her head violently as if to try and get the thought out. Eleanor : “Like...... EWWWWWWWWWZZIES!!!!” The shot begins to fade to Black on her sickened face... Eleanor : “Okay.... This Book Group was, like, SO not a good idea!” Black. “I, like, HATE other people....”
