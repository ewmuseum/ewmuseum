Final Escape?

2 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/2/02 Over the last three days, a very strange and confusing sequence of events has occurred. Starting with the strange kidnapping of the Rush by a group of Satanists trying to deny his godliness, everyone just got stranger from there. Then, Rush was in a car accident, which claimed a life, but luckily not his. Another man tried to throw him from the car but ended up saving his life and the man died while Rush lived, those his injuries are quite severe. Now, the man who originally kidnapped him is helping him out escape from his son’s men who are trying to kidnap Rush once again to bring back to Damien. Now, Rush and Master, Lucifer by name, are on the run, though Rush was passed out last time we saw him, from his exhaustion after being passed out for two days before. We will see whether they can make it to a place where they will be safe from their captors or not. This particular scene opens up with a shot of Lucifer, clad of course in his black cloak, hood down, carrying the large body of the Rush. For a small looking man, he has incredible strength, as much as that of a professional wrestler, because he is able to carry Rush who is around 275 pounds. They are in a strange looking part of some place, but the heavy fog is still around and Lucifer seems to have been turned around because he looks lost and finds a large rock to lay Rush’s large form down on. He too sits down; sweat dripping from the inside of the darkness inside the cloak. Lucifer: Well, my friend, it seems I have kidnapped you once again. Strange, I thought I was saving you when I brought you out the first time but now I really have to save you from my own flesh and blood, Damien. Lucifer looks around at the area around him, and realizes that there is no way that they will be able to get out of their current predicament tonight. Lucifer: We’re not going anywhere tonight. Sit tight, my friend, though you won’t be doing much else anyway. We will get you to safety somehow. And then I will settle my own score with Damien. Then, and only then… The man known as Master lays his head against the large rock. Slowly, his body slides down until the hood falls over onto his face, and he drifts off into the same sleep that Rush is now in, exhausted from the past day’s work. Speaking of Rush’s sleep, the man is tossing and turning on the rock, obviously upset in his realm of sleep, but we can’t do a dream sequence right now because it really doesn’t fit in the RP. With this, the shot fades to black for a momentary pause in the action… When the camera shows the next shot, time has progressed to the next day and Lucifer is already up. The day is a gloomy one, much like the day before it. A fog is still heavy in the air, and the ground is sprinkled with the past day’s precipitation and the morning dew. Rush is still in his coma-like state, so Lucifer decides to rest until his comrade can do at least a little walking on his own. He slips back into the quiet, peacefulness of sleep and waits for Rush to finally awaken to meet the man who has saved his life and kidnapped all within the same week. When Rush finally awakens from his long, long slumber, he sees his kidnapper lying next to him. His first instinct is to think that all of what he just encountered, the car crash and everything, was a dream, but then he looks around and sees that he is in some wooded area, but for some reason he is with his kidnapper from before, the same black cloaked figure he wouldn’t forget from the punishment that he received on the night of his kidnapping. As the black figure stirs, Rush readies himself for a fight, looking his enemy right into the black void that is the inside of his cloak. The figure sees Rush staring right into his face, though we still have not seen his face, and gets up slowly brushing off his cloak, a bit dirtied and wet from lying on the rock. Lucifer: So I see, you have awoken my friend. Perhaps you misunderstand why I am here with you in this strange place. Rush can’t respond, his lips dry and cracked. Lucifer hands him a canteen full of water, which Rush drinks all of, smacking his lips together, now wetted and refreshed. Rush: Thanks for the water, man. But why are you here with me? You tried to kidnap me from civilization about three days ago, and now you’re giving me water when I’m thirsty and you moved me from the site of the car crash. What’s going on? Lucifer: I can’t explain it all here, as we don’t have much time. All I can tell you right now is that you can trust me; I’m on your side. We must hurry though; my son’s men are pursuing us. Rush: Your son? Lucifer: He was the man you talked to on the way back from your kidnapping. I apologize for that, my vision was somewhat clouded at the time by another event. You are now my ally, whether you wish so or not. Rush: Fine, I don’t trust you, but if you’ve told me the truth so far, then I might as well head wherever you’re going. Any place is better than these disgusting woods. The two men begin to walk off through the woods, quiet as they can but still with speed. Lucifer leads the way as they walk through the thick fog. For a long while, all the two men do is walk straight in the same direction, silently traveling to who knows where. Suddenly, there’s a noise close by. Crashing through the brush can be heard, and human voices can be made out. Both men stop in their tracks and whisper to each other as the noises come closer. Rush: You think that’s your son and his boys? Lucifer: I have no idea. Damien isn’t a genius, but he’s too smart to be caught out here where he might face me one on one. I think it may be my former servants, who will try to drive us to the end of the forest, where Damien is presumably waiting to kill me. If we can’t find a way around this, then we’re good as dead. Rush: Dammit. So, we’re basically in a trap that has no escape from it. Either you and I take on five guys, who probably have guns, or we walk right into your son. Is there anything we can do? You have any evil super plans that might save us now? Lucifer: I’m afraid not. We can hide, but we may be found anyway. However, with our chances right now, that seems like a better shot than most other things. We head due north, then we find a place to hide or… Rush: Or we die. Right. Let’s get going, because they’re getting closer. The two start to hurry off as Rush mutters to himself, something about his terrible luck with March Madness only a few days away. The two men hurry as fast as they can, maintaining their soft sounds through the forest, until they arrive at where the dirt meets the grass. Looking around, Lucifer sees no immediate ideas for hiding places and the edge of the forest is only about a half mile away. Rush plops down onto a small stone, leaning back and sighing. Rush: Well, looks like this is it. This is the end of line for us. We’ve got nowhere to run, and nowhere to hide, literally. I won’t get to go to March Madness, I’ll never get to proclaim my godliness at a PPV stage. Neither man talks in the silent recognition that they may very well die, though Rush is still technically dead in PCW. No emotion is ever seen, because Lucifer’s face is never seen, but the very silence shows emotion of its own. The crashing through the woods can now be heard again, though it’s very faint at the moment. Rush: I guess we just wait here. Go down fighting. At least they won’t take the small amount of honor that I have. What about you, Lucifer? Lucifer: I will stand by you until the end. You are what my son came for; I am just a small part of that. If you say you will fight, then I will lend you my life, so that you may have a chance to escape. The shot switches to the group of five men, quickly gaining towards the area where Lucifer and Rush are awaiting their meeting with fate. All five are wearing dress clothes, and look rather out of place walking clumsily along in the forest. The typical big, dumb guys arrive at the very spot, as dramatic music (I can’t think of any) plays in the background as they search around the spot. The camera shows Rush and Lucifer, behind the large rock, ready to ambush. They will strike when they can make the most damage, when their enemy will be the least ready, but they will both most likely die. Lucifer’s thoughts go to his son, and these men who were formerly his henchmen. He sees Rush, distraught but not showing it, but Lucifer is a master in emotion interpretation, being cold and calculated has paid off in paying attention to those around him. Lucifer suddenly has a change of heart. He whispers to Rush in a low-muddle tone. Lucifer: Go. Go ahead. Rush: What?! Are you crazy, we can’t go ahead now; they’d catch us quicker than if we were wearing bright orange jumpsuits? Lucifer: We would be caught. That’s why I’m not going with you. I will draw their fire while you escape. I can bargain with them for long enough to buy you some time, try to change their mind, but they will kill me. Rush: No way man. I’m not gonna have your life on my head. Gods don’t roll like that. I’m the Crunk as Hell God, and these punks will just have to find out, like those guys in the March Madness match would’ve… Lucifer: You desire to wrestle again. I can see it within you. You will go, because I am going out there whether you care or not. So, you can make use of the time that I give you, or my life can be squandered. The choice is yours. May you too escape the horrors of this life one day, my friend, Rush. Rush can’t reply, can’t speak to this man anymore because of his emotions. He’s too afraid that he will try to find a reason for the man to stop, but he knows that he will not find one. Lucifer sees his chance and pats Rush on the back before hopping out into the open area. Rush hears the voices, but they sound become just a blur as he hurries off the way that he walked before with Lucifer. Five minutes later, the sound of automatic gunfire can be heard, but Rush doesn’t hear it; he’s too far away to know what happens to his friend specifically, but he can guess well enough that Lucifer met his end on his own terms. He finally reaches the edge of the forest, sweating and chilled at the same time. There is no one there, as Damien has probably joined his henchmen at the sight of his father’s corpse. Rush sees the paved highway, and has no other choice but to start walking down the lonely road, his only chance at freedom. Rush walks and walks, his legs aching and expelling bile because he is sick to his stomach but has nothing inside of his body, no nutrients. Every once in a while, Rush will stop by a small pond or stream on the side of the road to sip a small amount, but he is still very dehydrated. Somehow, he continues well into the night, walking along the road, finally seeing a vision that is more beautiful than any he has ever seen before in his life. He sees a sight that gives him a reason to continue walking, if he could walk another inch. He sees the beautiful green and white of a roadside sign that reads: Lancaster County, PA2 miles In his joy over seeing this Rush starts to talk to himself, the first thing that he has heard in his many many hours of walking. Rush: Oh Jesus Christ, I actually made it. I don’t know what the hell Lancaster County is, but it sure as hell is the greatest city on the earth right now. Rush now lies down next to the large sign, and with his next destination firmly in mind, and new hope, Rush slides off into sleep, with dreams of March Madness. Somehow he will make it there, by Crunk he will…
