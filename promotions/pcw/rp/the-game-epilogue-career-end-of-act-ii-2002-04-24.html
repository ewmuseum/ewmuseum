THE GAME - Epilogue (Career: End Of Act II)

24 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4-24-02 Necessary OOC Note: GO READ PART TEN FIRST! =P Song Listening Guide: 'Glycerine' by Bush. In need of something more mushy? 'Girlfriend (Remix)' by N'Sync and Nelly. BOBBY S. CAMPBELL THE GAME - Epilogue Game n. A contest governed by specific rules; a way of entertaining oneself; a calculated way to do something; animals, birds, or fish that are hunted for sport or food. gamer, gamest adj., gamely adv., gameness n. (Definition Credit: Webster's Dictionary, Paperback Edition. Text Copyright (c) 1990, K. Nichols.) * * * Then.... The crowd hushes down and Duzza takes a few deep breaths. The now PCW star drives from the middle of the court from the half court line and does the most spectacular thing ever seen on a basketball court. In fact, now his plain mortality has to be called into question. Eyes closed. From just inside the free throw line. One handed. Three-sixty front flip dunk. 'Nuff said. The crowd, by the way, explodes. Metaphorically. No, no one was stupid enough to bring a bomb, much to B.S.'s surprise. Darrell Armstrong. Ten. Grant Hill. Ten. Triple H. Ten. Tracy McGrady. Ten. Carrot Top. Oh God, here we go.......... TEN! Immediately, Duzza's friends and even a couple family members rush the court in stupifying celebration in the winning score of ninety-six, lifting him onto their shoulders like he just won the PCW World Title. Blayze meanwhile, just chases Carrot Top right out of the building with murderous intent in his eyes. The scene fades out while leaning against the side wall behind Fountain and Decker and watching from his vantage point- Bobby Stormbringer just smiles and applauds an old friend. And now.... * * * --4-20-02, Saturday night, 11:20 pm, the end of Stormbringer's first day as a free agent! Key word: Freedom. WOO!!-- There is little still left to tell.... The last scene fades in on a simple view. That of Bobby Stormbringer Campbell and his girlfriend Kira York lounging on their porch at Bobby's home in Orlando, Florida. At least it will be for another week. On the twenty-seventh of this month they plan to finally move back into the Stormbringer Family Home. Kira does have an apartment of her own, but.. . she just hasn't gone back there for a long time. Well, not too long. A month. She has already moved her stuff out, little by little over time. Of course, Bobby didn't really mind at all. When he saw Kira's toothbrush appear from seemingly thin air, he knew that was a clear sign of things to come. 'Heya. If they want the house keys so bad than they can come get 'em. Hehe!' is what she said about the situation before. Both sit in a sort of 'lawn couch' instead of lawn chairs, silk pajamas of different colors the attire for the night. Dark green for Bobby, a soft violet for Kira. The adorable vixen has her right leg draped over Bobby's left, and occassionally Bobby rubs her thigh. More out of habit than anything else. 'Are you sure you don't wanna go see a late movie, hon? There's one open 'til two in the morn.' Bobby gives his love the infamous 'blinking puppy dog eyes' like he's been known to do when he is too tired or lazy to dish out all of his natural charisma to get his own way. At this moment he is THIS close to pouting. 'Ehhhh nah. Besides, if we go tomorrow there'll be less peoples,' Kira yawns and grins at her boy toy. 'True, true.' Bobby nods at the pure logic. 'Heya.' 'Yeah..?' 'You ever gonna tell everybody what happened at the B-Ball Game?? You know.. how it ended?' Kira's voice is strained at the end of her question, but only because she is stretching. The doctors say that her voice is fully healed, thank goodness. Bobby turns his head and looks down at the ground as if he's pondering the possibilities. Then he looks straight at the camera and whoever is holding it before making statements. 'Team Heel made a raging comeback, led by Borro and Makonie. We brought Boda back in and things were scoreless for a while, Loc and Boda playin' real good defense. Aimz and Boda chipped away at the lead until we caught up, but Badge scored a three-point-ranger and a medium range dunk shot. It was twenty to eighteen, game point for them. I hit a three, then Aimz hit a three, and it was tied at twenty. Then that cowardly f*cker Badge clipped Boda's knee! He wasn't badly hurt, but I think he may of strained a muscle. Point is he couldn't play b-ball for the next several minutes, when we needed him most! Hell, that's why Badge did it. Almost started a damn riot when he did that. So anyway, K-Dub came back in, even though he would of been happy just eating freggin' grapes with Amanda-' B.S. is cut off by a click of the tongue from Kira at the mere mention of Amanda's name. Bobby is shortly reminded of his sister's distaste for almost all other women. The Showstopper shakes the thought from his mind and presses on with the end of THE GAME. 'Long story slightly shorter, Aimz got point twenty-one on a lay up and a foul! A foul means we got the ball back! After a couple hectic minutes of near misses by both sides.. including that damn dunk I missed over Badge.. Kid Wonder spot up seventeen feet away from the rim in the far corner and Aimz found 'im somehow, bless 'er heart. Wonder nailed it. His only damn point of THE GAME! But it was the winning one. But come to find out... Amanda left Team Heel's fifteenth point off during their big run after halftime! Amanda claims it was an accident but... c'mon. Let's get real.' 'Kinda sucks... so you being the great man you are, you called it off.. called it a draw.' Kira beams up to B.S. for his virtues and principles while he looks back at her modestly. 'Give credit to The End. What is he... Mr. Fairness, all of a sudden, I think. He's the one who pointed out to me that they had been jipped. But hey... at least our team had a few moments to celebrate. If we can't be proud of anything else, at least we won that last battle of hustle at the end. Somethin' else I'm very proud of...' Bobby grins a mischievious grin. 'Yes..?' Kira knows with that particular look that Bobby is usually looking for *ahem* love and affection. Bobby slowly wraps his arms around the waist of his sexy mate. 'You were the greatest cheerleader a guy could hope for out there. No doubt about it....' Bobby fades off as he tilts his head and looks serious for a few seconds. This confuses Kira mightily. Of all the looks her boyfriend has given her in the past... she's never seen this one before tonight. 'Um. Bobby? Something wrong?' 'No. Nothing... I was just thinking... thinking that this is the right moment. This is the right day. Not just thinking.. I know- yeah. Yeah, I know it is. I know yer a bit young, but... 'Hee hee, babe? You aren't makin' much sense, I gotta tel-' Kira's words turns to a gasp as right before her very eyes, Bobby Campbell slips out from under her leg and drops to one knee on the wooden paneled ground. He fishes around in one of his pajama pockets and fishes out a small and plush black box, letting it rest in his open right palm. He holds it out to Kira and smiles very pleasantly, not in the smartarse way that Bobby would usually smile. Kira holds both her hands over her mouth in total shock. 'Kira. I've done this once before. With someone pretty famous, actually, but... you're the only natural redhead for me. No really. I mean it! A couple of teary chuckles peek their way through Kira's defenses as Bobby winks up to his dream girl. They are tears of complete joy. For what could be the final time in PCW history, the camera shot zooms in on the calm and adoring face of Mr. Prime Time. His face with rigid features that still maintain a boyish quality. All of his red hair falling back and away from his eyes since he is looking upward. No shades. Just a goatee and dark blue eyes that speak of only two things every second of every single day. The Perfect Storm lurking inside and a burning love that is so very much easier to see. He opens the case to reveal the diamond and emerald ring inside. There is only one thing left to ask: 'Kira. Simply put, I really do love the hell out of you. Will you do me the honor... of becoming my wife?' ~END OF ACT II THIS HAS BEEN A B.O.T.S. PRODUCTION. (c) 2002. ALL RIGHTS RESERVED. SEE YOU SOON.
