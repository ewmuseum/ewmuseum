Dante Jackson Tries To Get Jordan Back To Being 'Black'...

7 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 06.07.02 Shabazz thoughts pre-party. WHOO! Bookage~! I’m going to be wrestling on Tuesday Turmoil, this is my first match in a while and I’m very excited. Sadly, I’ve got to go in the ring with that pervert-loving freak, Duzza. He WAS a good guy, but his love for R. Kelly has been getting to me. I tried to convince him once that R. Kelly was a fraud, and a freak, but he didn’t go with it, in fact, he threw me out of the car… on the freeway! Well, it’s all-good, because I have my chance to make that man pay for his sins as a fake black man. I’ve got to show them that intelligence is the key to success, not supporting perverts and acting like a ‘gangsta.’ Duzza’s one of the people that have caused the moral decay of African Americans in this world, and I am the one who will return us to our rightful place, at the top of the United States… My other two opponents are pretty much teammates Andy Murray and John Crichton, two former members of the Forgotten Ones and now current members of The Political Machine. Crichton is trying to put his Mack on Jaden, who looks kinda black… So I guess it is my duty to make him realize that he should stick to loving his own, nasty race, and I will. Andy Murray, bah! He looks like a Gorilla, end of sentence. When we last left the Civil Rights Extremist, Jordan X Shabazz, he was taken away by budding talent and rising superstar, in both professional wrestling, and the music world, Dante Jackson. Jordan was kicked out of Def Jam headquarters by Russell Simmons are Jordan snapped at Russell and began calling him several names. Dante promised to make Jordan get back to normal, how so? That’s what part 2 is for! The scene opens up on the outside of a large house, obviously in the Hollywood hills. Signs advertising certain things are everywhere. This house isn’t a regular run of the mill large house; it’s the type of thing that you would expect from MTV cribs. The front is gated; meaning that only the richest and prestigious of people would live in this area. We now enter the home and we see that we have entered in a full-blown party. People are dancing with girls in the living room, soft, romantic music is playing, some guys are chilling out with a girl at their side, some guys are chilling out with a drink in front of them, and some guys are chilling out with a blunt or bong in front of them. We now head to the front door of the party, the doorbell rings; somebody runs and gets the door. The door peers open and we see Jordan X Shabazz and Dante Jackson. Jordan X Shabazz has his head covered by a Toronto Maple Leafs baseball cap, he wears a dark blue ‘Malice Style, Hoss‘ t-shirt with a picture of Duke Williams on it, and some blue Fubu sweat pants. Not exactly what he would wear if he were in his right mind. Dante Jackson on the other hand, is dressed much more for the urban nightlife; he wears baggy khaki shorts, a gray ‘Platinum Fubu’ t-shirt with Phat Albert on the front. Brown Lugz boots adorn the budding music stars feet, and a smile on his face. Dante Jackson hugs the man that answers the door. “Hey playa, long times no see.” The man waves Jordan into his home; Jordan walks in, and is immediately overtaken by the smell of marijuana and alcohol. Dante and Jordan are now walking alone, as the man that hugged Dante when they first entered the place has now left to greet some other guests. This is obviously one of those urban parties, mainly black people, a few drunken white women. Dante and Jordan take a seat on the couch. “How is this supposed to help me get that ‘blackness’ back? The ‘blackness’ that you claim will help me with my organization, and in the PCW.” Jordan asks tiredly. His eyelids are getting heavier every minute and he appears to be worn out from a long day of travel. “Stop complaining Shabazz. I’ll get you in tip-top shape mentally; you can do whatever you want physically. I need to get you back in the mindset you were in while you were in the NTICW,” Dante replies while combing through the crowd of people for a certain person, or so it seems. Dante has now gotten up and is finds what he was looking for. The table full of food and drinks. Dante passes on the food and heads straight to the drink section. There’s a variety of alcoholic drinks there, Bacardi, rum, Jack Daniels, plain ol’ beer, and some fruit punch. Dante Jackson fixes himself a drink of straight rum. Also, before Dante leaves, Dante pours some fruit punch, and he tops it off with a good portion of Jack Daniels. Dante carries both drinks carefully and he makes his way back to Jordan X Shabazz, who is on the verge of passing out. Dante sits down beside Jordan and he places both drinks on the small brown coffee table that is in front of them. “Here, drink this,” Dante says to Jordan while handing him the fruit punch topped off with Jack Daniels. Jordan X Shabazz is now sweating more so than before, and almost knocked out. “No. I’m not in the mood for any alcohol.” “Jordan, dog, there is no alcohol in this drink, it’s plain old fruit punch Kool Aid. Just like you used to drink back in the day.” Jordan smiles as he fondly remembers his days growing up in Queens, New York. Dante Jackson was smart, he knew that Jordan wouldn’t be in the mood to drink alcohol, so Dante made it appear as if he was giving him Fruit punch Kool Aid. “What is the point of me drinking this anyways?” Jordan asks skeptically. “It’ll get you back in the ‘black’ state of mind. You gotta keep it real or the black people will so through your phoniness and realize that the real Jordan X Shabazz isn’t nothing but an Uncle Tom playin’ fool.” Dante replies convincingly. Jordan looks at Dante, and then takes a sip of the fruit punch. A smile comes across Jordan’s face. “This is damn good,” says Jordan. “That’s just the beginning Shabazz, there’s much more where that came from.” After a few minutes, Jordan X Shabazz has consumed more alcohol than he could have imagined. He’s drunk out of his mind, and he doesn’t know it. He thinks that the Kool Aid is giving him a sugar rush like he did when he was in his youth. His thoughts are a blur of Duzza, John Crichton, and Andy Murray. His match on Tuesday Turmoil was weighing heavily on his mind, his first match in a week. He liked getting booked because it paid the bills, and he is thinking that if he can win his match in the four way, that perhaps he could be in line for one of those vacant titles, or even a number one contenders match. Dante Jackson sees that Jordan is getting sidetracked, Dante really believes that by getting Jordan to act like he did before, a baggy pants wearing, Ebonics speaking, trash talking, disrespecting asshole, that it would be good not only for himself, but for the entire group of people that followed him. “Okay Jordan, it’s time,” says the unemployed grappler. “T-time for w-w-what?” asks a drunken Jordan X Shabazz. “Time to get you some black booty, that’ll bring you back to your senses.” “N-N-no way Dante. You h-h-have got to be kidding me.” “Oh God…” says Dante as he realizes that not only is Jordan speaking properly and portraying himself as an intelligent being, but he is now using white boy phrases like ‘You have got to be kidding me.’ Dante realizes that sitting down he’ll never get Jordan in the mood to try and get his Mack on. So he stands up. He grabs the 228 pounder by his shirt and he jerks him forward. Shabazz falls onto the coffee table and vomits on the red carpet. Dante looks around and makes sure that nobody is seeing what’s going on. “Good, good. Now that you’ve gotten all of that out of your system when some of the fine honeys see you here tonight, you’ll get some booty. And by tomorrow, you’ll be back to normal.” Dante says. “B-b-but I am normal,” slurs Jordan. “Shut the hell up and let’s go.” Dante grabbed Jordan by the back of his Duke Williams t-shirt and got him to his feet. Jordan stumbled his first few steps by found his footing and began walking side by side with Dante Jackson, albeit really slowly. It sort of looked like what some street thugs call a ‘Pimp walk’; Jordan had a goofy smile on his face as they approached a group of Caucasian females who were engaging in some talk over some movie stars that they had met. They all were dressed as if they were sluts, so they probably were call girls. Jordan was just about to try and get his Mack on when Dante grabbed him by his shoulder. “Ah no you don’t.” “W-Why *Hic* not?” “Jordan, those are white girls. We’re trying to get you back to your ‘blackness’, don’t you think it would be counterproductive if you went after the white girls while trying to regain your blackness?” Dante asks half-sarcastically and half-seriously. Jordan, confused by the words that Dante just used, decides not to argue. The men continue through the maze of televisions, coffee tables, drugs and alcohol. The house is pure mayhem. They passed what appeared to be a broom closet in which two females were making out in, and they entered the very large and vast ‘Chillin’ room.’ The room was full of black women shaking what God gave them on a small dance floor. The pimps and playas just watched the booties jiggle while sippin’ on some alcoholic beverages. In the corner of the room there is two fine looking light-skinned black women, they’ve got their eyes on Jordan and Dante. Dante makes a beeline for the lady’s; he sits right beside one of the them, and immediately begins whispering some words into her ear. Jordan sits beside the other girl, who is now eyeballing him and Jordan does nothing. Dante is now kissing the girl that he is sitting beside passionately, he notices that the girl that Jordan is sitting beside is becoming increasingly agitated by the fact that her friend is getting some sexy chocolate and Jordan X Shabazz is sitting beside her, drooling, and asleep, also Jordan smells of alcohol. Dante stops making out with the girl and he looks at Jordan. “Jordan. Jordan. JORDAN!” Dante screams loud enough to wake up Jordan, but not loud enough to disturb the booty dancers on the makeshift dance floor. Jordan is stunned, but he opens his eyes. Dante helps him to his feet as the two men head to a secluded corner of the dance floor. “Jordan, this isn’t working, but I’ve got an idea.” Jordan doesn’t reply he’s too drunk. Dante leans Jordan against the wall and he calmly walks towards one of the tables that the big time playas are sitting it. The host of the party, the man that owns the house, Mark Davidson, is sitting down having a glass of Bacardi Silver. Dante speaks with him briefly. The man shakes Dante’s hand and let’s Dante go. Dante comes back to Jordan X Shabazz with a smile on his face. “Okay, if this doesn’t work, then nothing will,” Dante says while leading Jordan to a room on the side of the dance floor. Dante goes in first as Jordan waits by the door. Jordan hears some moving about through the door and over the music. Jordan thinks nothing of it because he is still drunk. Suddenyl, he is pulled into the room and sat on a chair. His arms are tied on the arms of the chair. The lights are out. The Television goes on and ‘We Ain’t Goin’ Nowhere’ by P. Diddy and the Bad boy family is playing on the television screen. The lights come on and Dante smiles. “Now, you’ll watch all of these rap videos until tomorrow morning, and if you fall asleep, it’ll be embedded into your mind because it’s the last thing that you see. This is bound to return the blackness to the hero of so many… Or so few… Same difference.” Dante Jackson gets out of the room as Jordan is left watching the video. He is still drunk, but beginning to sober up. The scene fades to black with a drooling and tied up Jordan watching the rap videos on the television.
