Just what does Krayzie need to overcome these odds?

24 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2-24-02 It's only hours away. The first of three of the most significant matches in the career of Jeremy Howard against the sick and sadistic man they call Clyde. With each and every second that passes by, questions swirl around the mind of the Krazed Lunatic. Most prominently of them, when and where is Kid Wonder going to strike at the Krayzie One and is Drake Maxwell going to attempt to get into the mind of Jeremy Howard before the epic battle next Sunday at March Madness? Just how is the Krazed Lunatic going to handle this situation that has come before him? That's a question that's going to get answered, all the Krayzie fans hope, at Sabotage. Firstly though, the scene fades into the inside of the black BMW X5 of the Krayzie One. Sitting in the driver's seat, wearing the same blue jeans, white t-shirt and flannel shirt from before is the former World Champion, Jeremy Howard. Jeremy: No way am I going to let Kid Wonder get in the way this time. No f^cking way. The camera widens a bit, revealing the rest of the sport utility, showing off the lovely Jessica in the passenger's seat still in the same white jeans and matching shirt she was last seen in. Jessica: Then you'd better hope that your plan goes through, or he's going to be running around in the back waiting to spring on his opportunity to get at you. Jeremy: Tell me something I don't know. A pause fills the air, creating a disturbing silence. Jessica: I know that it took every single police officer in Elkhart General Hospital to hold you down the day they took Troy from us. Jessica takes a breathe, then.... Jessica: I know that if Kid Wonder steps in the way of you getting your brother back, that same kind of burning hatred will fill your body.... I just hope that Amanda has enough sense to have him hold off just this one time, so he doesn't risk getting himself put through another car window. Jeremy: I remember that day, too. In a little over a month, the Krayzie One will turn twenty two years of age. That means, for five years he's been without the friendship of his brother. That means, for three years he's been without the emotional bond of his son. Three years ago, Jeremy Howard's whole life took an extreme turn for the worse when the second member of his immediate family was stripped away from the day he was born. With every single shred of strength he had, the Krazed Lunatic tried to fight back against the numerous police officers there that day, only to come up short. While his failure of the day might be a significant piece of history, the courage and strength he displayed will go down in Jessica's mind as the day the man of her dreams came full circle. It took a total of seventeen police officers to fully keep this man down and unable to save his child from being taken away. So, if Kid Wonder were to cost Jeremy teh freedom of his brother, just how far would the Krayzie One go to get back at the Quintessential Teen Idol? A safe bet would be mutilation of some sort. But, one might not want to put past something far greater, should it come to it. Jessica: I was laying there in that hospital bed, just having given birth to Troy the natural way, being stitched up when I saw them come in. Jessica stops for a moment, trying to collect herself from the tears that begin to well up in her eyes. Then..... Jessica: You were by the area in which they were cleaning up the baby. Jeremy: They were almost done with him. I can still feel the anticipation of wanting to hold him. Jessica: They burst in the door. As plain as day, I remember your facial expression when you saw O'Bannon enter the room. You went right at him, hoping to somehow stop him but the amount of police he had with him that day was just too much for you. Even Kid Wonder couldn't hire the amount of police officers it took that day to keep Jeremy away from saving his son. Richard O'Bannon led them all in. Jeremy saw O'Bannon and charged right at him. The Krayzie One knew that day that something was up. He just didn't want to let it escalate before it was too late. Sadly, too late came and seventeen police officers rushed the Krazed Lunatic, pinning him to the ground. Jeremy fought back with everything he had, but it still wasn't enough. Richard O'Bannon took the newborn baby right out of his crib and off into the night without a single word having been spoken. Jessica: I saw our baby lifted out of his crib and taken away by that son of a b!tch, with you doing all that you could to break free. I can remember screaming as they laid nightclubs to your head, trying to subdue you more, but something inside of you just wouldn't quit. Jeremy: O'Bannon is lucky I didn't remember that day when I saw him the day before Shanghaied. Tears continue to roll down the eyes of Jessica as she recounts what happened that fateful night. Jessica: Suddenly, our baby was gone. Those d#mn nurses didn't do a thing, nor did that doctor. I got stitched up and sent along my way the next morning. Jeremy: You think that doctor had anything to do with it? Jessica: Well, how else would O'Bannon and his men know about Troy? Jeremy faces forward, staring out of the black BMW. His mind becomes consumed with thoughts. Jeremy: I know.... I just don't see what you're getting at. Jessica turns toward Jeremy, tears flowing down her cheeks. But, those aren't going to stop her from saying what she has to say. And, by the looks of the expression that graces her face, it's important. Jeremy: Jessica? Jessica: What I'm getting at, is that you need something that'll help you get past these obstacles. Something that'll drive you so much that you could take on seventeen police officers and almost win. Jeremy: I'm still not following. Jessica: It's all quite simple. You need the love of your son with you at ringside when you step into the ring on Friday against Chet King and at March Madness against Drake Maxwell. She stops for a moment, collecting herself while letting that thought sink into Jeremy's head. Then.... Jessica: If can fuel the fire in you that gave you the strength to take on and almost beat seventeen police officers at the age of nineteen, then he can surely give you the strength to take on Chet King, Drake Maxwell and Kid Wonder if it comes down to it at the age of twenty two. Jeremy knods his head toward her, showing that he's paying attention to her and the road ahead of him. Jeremy: You saying that we need to get Troy back and now? Jessica knods her head, 'yes'. Jeremy: Then by God, it's time to quit talking about it and just do it. A smile breaks through the dreery looking face of Jessica. Jeremy: Keith Lipowitz of Terre Haute, Indiana. I'm sure I'll be able to find something out... starting tomorrow! Jessica: We're starting tomorrow? Jeremy: Yes. Right after disposing of that sick f^ck, Clyde, we're going to get Troy. I will have my sone by my side when I beat Chet King for the Continental title. Jessica's arms fling out around the neck of Jeremy. Jessica: Yay! Jeremy: And, our son will be there when I beat Drake Maxwell for the freedom of my brother. Jeremy pauses, letting that thought sink in as Jessica pulls slowly away from the hug. Then.... Jeremy: Come March fifth, the Howard household is going to have a family reunion! Whether Chet King or Drake Maxwell like it, they should just begin etching in the next mark in there loss column. For, coming there way is a man possessed on winning. But, its not winning just to win. It's winning for the Continental title and the freedom of Justin Howard. The latter being the bigger of the two. So, if Kid Wonder wants to get in the way of this, he can just plan for a homicidal maniac to be chasing him the next day. This is no laughing matter as the scene fades out to Jeremy heading for Sabotage.... to black!
