The thrilling conclusion to The Spice Girls Saga!

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: not entered [When we last left off, Flaymin had at long last rallied the troops: All five Spice Girls communicating with each other via a conference call about possibly reforming the most powerful girl group ever known to mankind. Scene picks up where we last left off in Ginger Spice’s Conference room with Flaymin trying to persuade the Spice Girls to re-unite via satellite] Flaymin: Now ladies, I’ll cut straight to the point….One fateful afternoon not too long ago, I was sitting in my lovely lounge, furnished with Ikea products from their gorgeous 2002 Summer Range.I mean their Winter range was awful and I wasn’t about to plunder $85 for a wooden coffee table from their Winter Collection because of all the bad press it received in the Annual Edition of ‘Furniture Freaks’….. do you know what I mean? [A long, awkward silence resounds around the room and through the conference call satellite. Geri, leans over and whispers into Flaymin’s ear] Geri: Just get to the point. Flaymin: Sorry Ginger.Right, so anyways, there I was at the front counter at Ikea about to purchase this lovely lamp whe- Geri: I SAID GET TO THE F*CKING POINT ABOUT THE SPICE GIRLS!! Flaymin: Oh… that. Ahem, good afternoon ladies. [Sporty, Baby, Posh and Scary all respond with a brief hello] Flaymin: Now the reason I have called you all in today is because there is a very important issue destabilising the world right now. Posh: Imminent war? Scary: Poverty and Famine? Sporty: The Aids Epidemic? [Flaymin shakes his head in disappointment] Flaymin: I said IMPORTANT.Don’t you bimbo’s pay attention to a word I say? Baby: Do we have to take notes? Flaymin: Sure, why not. Now as I was saying- the world has a void that must be filled…. Sporty: You mean the Ozone layer? Flaymin: No dammit!GIRL POWER! The world needs Girl Power to rise from the ashes! Posh: Are you saying you want us to reform the Spice Girls? Flaymin: Babe, do you think I came here to sell you Ikea furniture?Of course, I want the band to reform! Geri: When you say ‘band’, what exactly do you mean? Flaymin: Miming…Bad Dance routines….Airhead lyrics….And oh yeah most importantly- tight spandex. Baby Spice: I’m up for that. Sporty: So am I! Flaymin: Hey, I’m getting it up as we speak! [Geri looks at Flaymin in disgust] Flaymin: What??I was referring to my ruler! Geri: Oh.I’m sorry. [Geri apologises to Flaymin and pats him on the shoulder warmly] Geri: So where is the ruler? Flaymin: What ruler?I was referring to my donger! [Without notice, Geri swings a swift right hand slap right across Flaymin’s face] Geri: Asshole! [Realising that this conference is plunging into chaos and anarchy, Flaymin decides to quickly restore order before things get too out of hand] Scary: What the f*ck is going on over there? Flaymin: Uh… nothing really.Geri’s right hand is working overtime… I guess. [Flaymin touches his throbbing face while Geri looks at her red right hand] Posh: She’s doing what??? Flaymin: I know, I know- its usually me that has the right hand working overtime! [Once again an awkward silence resonates around the room. Thinking this is a technical problem, Flaymin picks up the conference call telephone and begins to bang it against the table violently] Flaymin: Hmmph!Technology these days! [Flaymin flips the phone over and looks at the bottom of it] Flaymin: Just as I thought…. Made in Nokia. [Before Flaymin can resume the meeting, a large, burly Security guard marches into the room, and is led to Flaymin by Ginger Spice] Geri: Get this hobo out of here now! Flaymin: What?I thought we were making progress…. I could feel the vibe! Geri: Vibe this! [Geri extends her middle finger as Flaymin is dragged out of the room kicking and screaming] Geri: Thanks for all your help but I think it is best that you leave now! Flaymin: Wait! Wait!I have one more request…. Please! [Geri nods at the Security Guard who then stops dragging Flaymin along the floor momentarily] Geri: Yes? Flaymin: Having worked my magic here, I’m going to go out now and reform The Jackson Five….. Say, you wouldn’t happen to know where Tito lives, do you? [Scene fades out as The Guard picks Flaymin up by his afro and drags him out of the room shrieking like a woman]
