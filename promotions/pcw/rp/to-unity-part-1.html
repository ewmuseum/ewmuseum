Date: *shrugs* Eddy had been off from wrestling just for a couple of weeks while the March Madness was going on, visiting people and places. He decided to go down to San Antonio, Texas earlier and visit some historical landmarks and maybe have a good time checking out the river walk. Eddy didn't really know how to get away from the world today. All the problems on the news and all the problems around him. He didn't know what, or rephrased, how to feel in this time. Eddy was driving around in San Antonio, fingers gripped tightly around the wheel as it sped down the highway and through and exit sign. Eddy couldn't find any great radio stations out here, either. He wasn't in San Antonio, but awhile outside of it. Eddy flipped through, and all he could find was country music and a political talk show, and he would no listen to the talk show simply because he disagreed with everything the hosts said. He decided to listen to country music, just to help the time, even though Eddy didn't 'love' country music, he no true problem with it. He strummed his fingers along the dashboard to a tune on the radio, thinking about his upcoming match with the racist known as Mark Makonie. Eddy leaned back into his chair and saw a rest stop with cola's and a restroom. He had been needing to use the restroom for awhile now and decided to go now. Eddy stopped his truck on the side of the rest area and opened his door with a pop sound as Eddy felt fresh air now. Eddy slammed the door of his car and began up to go to the restrooms. Nobody was actually even around at this time of morning. It was crystal clear and very early: The sun rose only maybe 10 minutes ago. Eddy began into the restrooms, looking at the smooth brown tiles lining the walls and floors. Eddy had a black shirt, solid black, no words or advertisements, and his pants were blue jeans. Eddy usually wore a solid colid and blue jeans, sometimes the khakis or the black jeans he had. Eddy began to unzip his fly and lean into the stall when a white guy, balding and ugly looked over at him. 'Are you Young Tiger?', he asked. 'Yes', said Eddy, expecting a hearty kind word or wanting an autograph. But he got exact opposite. 'You fucking suck! That Mark Makonie is going to kick your Hispanic ass!' 'Why is reference to my race a deal?' 'He's my man: I want these fucking coons, these fucking Hisp's like you, those damn Asians and anybody else not white!' 'Why? Why do you hate other races? Don't you know good lies within each race of humans?' 'Good? Pfft. How are niggers good?' Eddy was pissed at the man for the 'nigger' reference. 'Stop referring to them as that word! Don't you know they have feelings too?' 'Don't know and don't care', said the man zipping his pants up. 'That's you...I believe every race is beautiful and wonderful in many ways and they are all good. I don't se why you have to hate them without really knowing them.' He turned and stared at Eddy coldly. 'Do I need to describe why? I don't need to! That's why I won't. You want to know why? Because I know more then you. Much more then you...Much more then you...', said him. Eddy shook his head. 'I don't have to argue anymore. I think I made my point. I don't care if you want Makonie to win, that's fine. We all have our opinions on things like that. I know my philosophy isn't loved by every man and my goals aren't praised by everyone else, yet I know that if I truly think I do the right think there is no need to feel bad about it. Sure, I base some of my ideas and opinions on others, but I'm just here wrestling for inspiration for the ones who have trouble believing in themselves. I'll give inspiration to kids, inspiration to adults, inspiration to those about to die and go off. It's in my heart to do so. I'm also he to make a statement of what I may think. I disagree with you, but that's my opinion. I don't press my opinions on other people...' The man shook his head and started out of the bath room. 'I hope Makonie kills you.' 'If that's what you hope, hope that. I'm not stopping your mind.' The man snickered and left as Eddy zipped his fly and went over to wash his hands. The soap was thick and a gooey green color with an odd lime scent. It was a real odd mixture, and Eddy press the little button to get soap to come out and looked at himself in the mirror. What exactly am I wrestling for? Inspiration? Do kids even care about me anymore? Am I of any importance anymore? Eddy rubbed his hands under the water and looked deeper. Yes they do..I'm just being pessimistic...I'm just confused over what that man said...It's OK... Eddy was having a fight between the devil/angel on his shoulder kind of way. Except one was pessimism or optimism. What about realism? Nothing. What is real these days? Nothing shocks me anymore...I'm always confused at why things are decided and why they happen...Politically, emotionally...God, I'm just confused right now...' Eddy washed his hand off and grabbed the paper towels. They were brown and thin as the paper soaked through when he touched it, but it didn't matter because he wasn't paying attention to that. I'm going to fight my match versus Makonie for every race...Every person...I'm Hispanic, but I won't be just that. I'm going to be everything. I'm fighting for every race, every religion, every gender, every everything to prove that everybody is equal among us. Nobody is any better then anybody else. We are all here to learn from each other. Not one person is of no use. Not one person was put on Earth for no reason. Not one person is worthless. We are all important. All united. Eddy began out the door into the air again, about to walk over to the vending machines. He pulled out his money and inserted 2 quarters into the slot and pressed 'Dr. Pepper'. There was a clanking sound as it felt down and Eddy lifted it from the slot and popped it open, heaidng back to his vehicle.
