Becoming... A Schmoopie (Gaz Green)

6 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6th Feb 2003 A vacation is exactly what the doctor ordered. I had never been away with someone I loved; I had spent a number of weekends in the comfort of a conquest but never had I ventured into my own pocket to take one of these thrill seekers away. Nonetheless Natalia deserved everything I could put on a platter. When she joined forces with Pranks With Attitude we were a well-oiled unit, inseparable then the adhesive slowly eroded away and now the plot thickens. Every relationship, be it business or personal encounters problems but how many women can say that they have been abducted by a man they once trusted. Maybe that was a little vague, how about this analogy, a woman is kidnapped in an attempt to get to her partner in a malicious manner only for the two to reunite their dead friendship? I had to make an impression, more of an impact, though be it one away from the squared circle. I had time to spare and I vainly peered into the mirror looking over my trim physique before walking over to the bed where my green tailored suit lay in pristine condition. I got dressed but as I did so, one question tormented my mind… “Why is Nerezza being so easily led?” After all, the new owner may wish to retain his services; Primetime Championship Wrestling wouldn’t survive without a merchandise salesman. Maybe the latter was a little white lie; after all, salesmen are available in their truckload but the vampire seems to have fallen to easy for my taste. Anyway, his intentions would eventually come to light. Where as before I had to rely on my own sixth sense, Nerezza is now apart of, be it reluctantly The Front Office and his backstabbing will not lay undetected. Enough of talking about somebody who doesn’t even have the luxury of a pulse, I should concentrate on the picture of beauty I am about to share dinner with. I warped my emerald jacket over my broad shoulders and made a second opinion courtesy of the mirror once more. Looking every bit as good as a fight night, my dyed hair was spiked to perfection, my clothes crisp and I was ready to make my partners acquaintance. “Wait!” the word drummed into my head, I was missing something. Everything appeared in order so what was I missing, oh, remembering last minute I strolled over to the bedside cabinet and yanked back the top draw, revealing a collection of fragrances. First I quickly added the usual body spray before concluding with a particular favourite of mine, a small red bottle with the nametag ‘Joop’ and I simply bathed myself in the bottles content of liquid. And who said that ‘The Jester’ didn’t leave impressions? These last three months have been nothing but impressions. A roller coaster of events, firstly I put myself first for once and joined the Front Office, literally becoming a hated man over night. Even this transition didn’t have the greatest of starts, a loss to Nerezza at Decimation, actually a loss is putting it politely, I was beaten to a near inch of my life by the fiend yet the vampire wasn’t contempt to just leave the score settled in December he wanted to further punish me. Physically I couldn’t match up to my old friend so I approached the scenario differently and refused his proposition for a return bout but then in terms of Nerezza, desperate times call for desperate measures and this analogy will probably prove just how desperate he was. He was desperate enough to kidnap an innocent person, but luckily no harm came to Natalia, his plot doomed by my intervention, however he offered me something that even Gaz Green couldn’t refuse, The ownership of Pranks With Attitude (P.W.A) And so we fought, Shanghaied, First Blood Match, Winner Takes All… How very true. My victory meant the Front Office would acquire the greatest Tag Team in PCW’s history and at the Pay-Per-View I defeated the behemoth to become the sole owner of P.W.A, a name without purpose though. Bitterly the Living Nightmare threw away our Championship reign to Chris Storm and Tessa Windsor but with my job safe and his hanging by a thread I coaxed the vampire into joining the Front Office and resurrecting Pranks With Attitude under the watchful eye of Xavier Kannon. Something unappreciated by Natalia and for obvious reasons, maybe this is why I plan on going through with tonight’s proceedings. I twitched nervously, never had I thought that at twenty-four would I be in this very predicament but Natalia has been a tower of strength, what a year for the Soviet Scarlet? A separation from her husband, a vague relationship with her husbands former colleague and a kidnapping, it’s a soap opera on its own but the surprises don’t stop there. Maybe Natalia’s silence is because I have Nerezza on a leash or perhaps she believes I have some secretive swerve I plan to unveil, personally I would prefer for her to voice her disgust and give me something to retort but no. Maybe it is because of this silence, her reluctance to speak up that has spurned me into making this decision, however doubts and second thoughts clouded my mind, and I found myself asking questions about my forthcoming actions. “Am I doing the right thing?” was the biggest question. I closed my eyes and blanked the further questions that were advancing to the front of my mind and focused on the task at hand. I breathed a sigh of relief and re-composed myself before slyly dropping my hand into my green business jacket. Rummaging, a little reluctant to showcase the article I raised my hand from within the pocket and in toe came a small black box. To anybody it was apparent that this was a jewellery case and making last minute checks I opened the container to showcase a dazzling gold ring, lined with glistening diamonds that twinkled from the piercing sun that radiates my apartment’s bedroom. “It is now or never…” I thought, closing the box and shielding it from prying eyes by returning it back into jacket pocket. I was all set. There was no turning back. I ventured over to the door with my cocky strut and closing it behind me I patiently waited for the elevator, which was prompt as ever. Descending to the lobby where my chariot awaits, those niggling questions resurfaced and I became like a alcoholic fighting his inner demons but Gaz Green has never backed down from a challenge I thought, so why start now. Actually I backed down from Nerezza’s proposal of a match at Shanghaied but that was so that I could bargain for a contest that would be beneficial, the point is I will go through with this come hell or high water, actually their presence would stop the proceedings and stop me from making what many would deem as a mistake. But as far as I was aware there hadn’t been a forecast for an Apocalypse, maybe a shower of rain but certainly not a tsunami or a volcanic inferno. Giggling at my own silly thoughts I waltzed through the lobby unfazed by the stares of the onlookers and straight into a virgin white limousine that sat against the sidewalk coughing smoke, before vanishing into the distance to greet my maiden. - To be continued -
