Date: 2-24 {The scene opens up with a rather large man much larger than what Kevin was. Standing at about six feet five inches and looks about 277 pounds of pure muscle. From the little light emitting this person is wearing a tight red shirt with a black dragon on the right sleeve, and a pair of baggy dark blue jeans and a pair of the new Reebok’s. While calmly stepping through the old hallway of this rather antique like but beautiful building. Keeping his normal pace, continuing to walk, he runs his fingers through his thin shoulder length blonde hair. After bringing his hand to his jaw, he begins to straighten out his short faded goatee.} {The sound of his pants rubbing back and forth, back and forth, back and forth, are combined with the sound of lonely eerie footsteps down the hall. Keeping his posture in perfect placement he continues walking. Nothing is changing in his pace, it’s just the same slow steady pace that is enough to emit fear into the people around him. Such a huge man, walking slowly without change, and keeping his eyes on you can bring most men to their knees in quicksand and fear into their soul. But in all reality does it even matter what we fear? Because fear is a way of life that coexists with hate. This man lived in peace and love and was forced to hate and denounce everything that he was. He turned his back on everything, and in the end no one was there for him. Even the people who he thought was his friends were against him, tearing away at his existence.} {Keeping his faith and love at hand he opens his mouth and begins to speak in a soft and quiet tone.} Man: Oh woe are the souls that are trapped within the timely portals of life. What is the extremity tearing away at the barrier of right and wrong? Life is nothing but a short time here; we should all sit back, relax and enjoy what we can. But dude… now that I’m back man, it’s time to get this party started. {The figure steps through a door and walks outside and towards a black Dodge with red stripes down the front. He steps inside and sits down on the tan leather interior. He starts up the car and pops a c.d. in his Jenson stereo system. He quickly forwards quite a bit in order to play one song. As the beginning riffs play the theme of “Only God Knows Why” is heard through the blaring speakers.} {He rolls the windows down letting the cold crisp winter night air fill up his truck. As the huge specimen for a man shifts into gears he quickly leaves the parking area.} Man: Dude things have changed since I was gone. Like my best bud Juan is now in jail; but Juan don’t worry I know a member of the Imperial Family in Japan that owe me. This dude is the Foreign Trade Ambassador to China and Phuc Yui owes me one. {If you weren’t able to tell this huge specimen for a man is none other than Kevin Klaymore himself. During his time off he’s been hitting the gym, and perfecting other moves. Some that may look harmless but can definitely bring you down to your knees. Even perfecting moves such as the Dim Mach Pinky Poke of Death, and the deadly Kustom Fitted, the Kolosal Krush, the Scottish Shooting Star, and Explosive Behavior. Now enough about Kevin, let’s get back to Kevin driving to a secret location in order to help a friend.} Kevin: I’ve been sittin’ here trying to find myself, I get behind myself I need to rewind myself. Dude I was livin’ large and had everything. The fans loved me and I was getting married but all in all fans only matter. The old ways are gone and the tickin’ time bomb Kevin Klaymore is here to stay. See… {Kevin begins to take a series of turns to what seems to be the expressway.} Kevin: I got caught up in the glory and the money, but now everything has changed. The reason I’m back is based on three reasons. Number one: in wrestling it’s about how much money you have; number two: it’s about whom you know; and number three: it’s about whom owes you. {Kevin does another series of turns which seems like he’s lost but why? Is he throwing someone off the trail? Well we’ll see you soon when Kevin makes his return to the ring on Sunday. Will he help Juan out with his slight problem? Only he knows and this surfer is back to his roots.} {The scene pans in on the newly huge Kevin Karlos Klaymore as he continues on his way. The scene then picks up outside as his truck passes the traffic light. Kevin has returned so be ready.} {End Transmission}
