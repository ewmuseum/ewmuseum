It's all about the GREEN!

Title: It's all about the GREEN! Featuring: Politically Incorrect Date: Today ::At approximately the same time around the nation, a radio advertisement hits numerous stations. The voice is familiar: That of the Politically Incorrect narrator! What does he have to say? Stay tuned! It's CAMPAIGN MEDIA!!: In Primetime Championship Wrestling, there appears to be a youth movement going on. Why do I say this? Because, Primetime Championship Wrestling's tag teams, like many other youngsters in the heat of summer, are yearning for summer camp. Yes, summer camp! Everyone's favorite place to engage in tennis, swimming, and other fun activities. But, the highlight for every camper is at the end of camp, when the camp is split in two, and everyone engages in Color War! The Reds against the Blues, the Yellows against the Oranges, whatever, everyone gives it their all in an effort to rise to the top. In PCW, however, there is a four way color war, between several odd colors. First is PINK!, professed by the Heartbreakers, the unambiguously gay former Tag Team Champions of the World. Then come the Pretty Cool Guys, who profess PURPLE~!, at least, that's what I got from watching Valentine and Heart priss around like ninnies. Maybe PURPLE~! is some evil entity, I don't know, I don't care, and I'm not speaking of it again. Then comes the more stylish Akiyama, with hues of black and silver. Well, black technically is the ABSENCE of color, but I'm not an art major so I don't give a damn... These three colors are at war. But, get ready! For a fourth color is about to enter the war on behalf of Politically Incorrect! For your hint as to what it is, listen to the beginning of the theme song.. 'Halls of justice, painted GREEN, money talking..' Yes, Politically Incorrect are full supporters of the newest hued force in PCW. For, indeed, anyone in business knows its all about the GREEN! THE DINERO! THE GREENBACKS! THE BENJAMINS! THE GRANTS, WASHINGTONS, HAMILTONS, LINCOLNS, AND JEFFERSONS TOO, FOR THAT MATTER! Although, PI usually carry a lot of old $10,000 Samuel P. Chase bills...BUT THAT'S NOT THE POINT! Green has many more applications than pink, purple, black, or silver. I mean, green is the color of grass! Do you realize how much grass there is in the world? Therefore, it stands to reason that there is more GREEN! covering the world than pink, purple, black, or silver Also, green is the hue of leaves! Everyone loves nice shiny leaves! Leaves can be red, yellow, orange, or GREEN! But, they can never be black, silver, or pink. Now, some may argue that leaves can be purple, but indeed, these so-called purple leaves are merely shades of red, so indeed, those that argue do so fruitlessly. Now that I have expounded the virtues of green to you all, I shall now explain why the other colors are bad, mmkay? Now, pink is a nice color, for several things. It is a nice color for girls to wear, for example. It is a nice color for hearts, lipstick, and other lovey dovey things. It is NOT, however, a good color for guys. On guys, pink is VERY VERY BAD. Although, I must admit, Pink on me would be nice, but that is a different VARIETY of Pink, so remember, pink (the color) on guys, IS ICKY! And probably homosexual too, not that Politically Incorrect is AGAINST homosexuals or anything.. And then we come to purple. Purple in its day was a noble color...used to denote royalty in Roman culture. At banquets, the amount of purple in one's toga was an indicator of social status. Now, purple is nothing, other than the favorite color of children. Come on! You all know that when you were five, purple was your favorite color, until you realized that there were BETTER colors out there! Then black...I must admit, black is cool. Black is style. But, black is also gothic people. *Shudder* Gothic people scare the crap out of me! I mean, just take one look at DarkStar or Clyde, and you know why black sucks right there. I mean, just.......ick. Anyway, black is also representative of the Void. You all like the Void...WELL, THINK OF THE POOR PEOPLE THAT BASTARD AKIYAMA TRAPS IN THERE! Think of poor Ripper Ryckman, a former Rising Star Champion! Think of Carmoon, the FIRST Pharoah bodyguard to beat Xavier Kannon. Do you think THEY like the Void much? Hell no! Because of you INSENSITIVE PRICKS who cheer whenever Akiyama Void Banishes someone, it leads to MORE POOR PEOPLE TRAPPED IN THE BLACKNESS! DAMNIT ALL, HOW DO YOU SLEEP WITH YOURSELVES AT NIGHT?! Sorry about that...little conscience problem. Now, onto the last color. Silver. I have to admit, Politically Incorrect LIKE silver. Silver bullion is very nice. But, unlike nice green bills.....silver tarnishes. It loses its luster. Green money is always green. But silver does not always stay pure. And impurity is a sign of weakness. The lesson you have all learned today? GREEN, and therefore Politically Incorrect rule. Thank you for your time. Paid for by the Friends of Politically Incorrect. We now return you to your regularly scheduled programming. ---- View Politically Incorrect's Biography
