Date: 15-5-03 The two captors look up at Mark with a wicked scary expression as they both look at each other. Mark smiles at the two as the driver looks up at the road and he sees the paved driveway where he is suppose to go. He transfers his foot over from the gas to the brake and the mph along with the rpm’s start to slow down very fast. He clicks up the turn signal on his left hand side and the green light comes up on the screen where all the other tools are at. “Where in the hell are we going?” Mark asks as he looks over at the captor over in the passenger’s seat. “We are going to a place so that we can all talk and get along at.” The captor in the passenger’s seat says. “Well you all might want to look back at your old friend here because I don’t think he is living any longer.” Mark says as he smiles and looks back at the dead body laying there on the seat of the van. The captor in the passenger’s seat gets up and Mark steps off to the side so that he can go and look at the dead captor. Mark goes and he sits down in the chair where the captor was and he looks out at all the trees. This reminds him of Mexico when he had his meeting with Jax, but yet its not the same place. They pull up and the trees keep on going back, and in the distance is a trailer that has been there for quite some time now it looks like. Mark squints his eyes to make out the trailer and he can see that the metal hand rail has been broken off, and pieces of paper are drifting in the wind, weightless. The rusted van starts to stroll up to the side of the trailer. Mark looks back in the back to see what the reaction of the other captor was and the guy looks like he is going to lose his lunch. He has his hand over his mouth looking like he needs a puke bag in front of him. “Whats a matter you fucking dick licker?” Mark asks trying to piss off the captors some more with his language. “You can’t take the heat that I bring in here, or is it that your just a pussy and can’t hold your afternoon lunch? I think that you are disgusted with what I did and I do appreciate to see the look upon your face as it thrills me that a mick is just about to puke. But on the other hand you disgust me as you are on your knees looking like you are praying to the Gods of the coon race.” “How did you know I was Irish?” The captor asks as he still has his hand over his mouth. “Well first of all your working for Haley’s dad.” Mark starts to say as he is thinking about something to say next. “Haley is in love with a damn mick, so in coincidence I would only think that the father would only hire international relations to cover this job.” The guy still doesn’t look good as his face is getting pale white, almost looking like a ghost. Sweat starts to come out across the forehead of the captor and the sweat shimmers in the light of the sun upon his eyebrows. The driver pulls up next to the side of the trailer as he puts the van in park with his right hand going up on the shifter. He takes the keys and he turns them back to turn off the ignition to let the engine cool down while they are here. The temperature gage was getting up there in heat. Mark finds the rusted out door handle as well to his right side as he pulls it out to release the door to be opened. The door opens with a creak, that sounds like it needs some WD 40 on the hinges of the door. Mark heads over to the back of the van and he takes the door handle and he pulls it to him so that the sliding door is released. The captor who is still living in the back is still on his knees and as the door opens the tail of his coat floats in the air from the wind from the nearby lake. The captor looks turns his head around to see the evil look of Mark right in front of him. ”I still want to live man!” The captor screams as Mark comes closer. “What are you thinking that you are going to do. The secret agents are going to get you if you kill us all.” Mark grabs onto the collar of the captor as the captor raises his shoulders in fear of what is going to happen next. Mark then with his other arm goes around his mid section area and he picks him up off of the metal floor from the van. The captor’s dress pants at the knees are wrinkled like he just picked them up from a pile of dirty laundry and he has stains on them that he just knelt in a pile full of shit. Up in the air the captor’s mind is going crazy as the grip feels like it is a deathlock. ”Please God in your name I only wish that you would help me get through this.” The captor starts to say in his mind as the world seems to stop for a second and the wind feels like a death storm upon him. “Why does this have to happen to me? Why couldn’t I just stay in Ireland and enjoy the service work that I was doing there. The father of Haley made me come over here for this nonsense.” The thoughts are shut off as the body of the captor his the green grass of what is covering the earth’s surface. Mark lets go of the hold that he has on the captor as his head nails the earth and his whole mind goes blank. Mark looks over at the other captor and he gives him an evil expression on his face as well. Mark starts to come near the captor as he looks to be a hungry wolf. “I just want to talk to you about the Haley situation.” The captor says as he starts to take a couple of steps back. “Why do we need to talk about it?” Mark asks as he gets closer and closer to the captor. “She is in my fucking hands right now and what good does it do to talk to you international fuckers about it?” “We don’t want her in Jackson’s hands.” The captor says as his back hits the metal of the van’s passenger door. “Who the fuck cares man?” Mark says as he gets in the captor’s face now. “I have control of her and from the looks of it now, she is not going to be leaving me soon.” “We are the people who had the idea for you to take her Mark.” The captor says as he puts his hands up in a sign of not wanting to fight. “Your not paying me damn penny you fuckin dirty ass immigrant, so why don’t you stay out of my life and go back to being a damn agent and let me do my job.” Mark says in a loud tone of voice as it echoes through the forest’s trees. “The boss told us that we need to have her back so she can not be in the hands of Jackson easier.” The captor says as he tries to work this out. “Fuck your international boss as well or whatever minority he is.” Mark says as his fist moves back in a punching motion. “And you all think that I am going to give Haley back to Jackson then your all fucking stupid cause don’t you all see that I’m the one hating Jackson? Fucking posers.” Mark’s fist comes forward fast as his fist that is griped as hard and tight as a rock comes into the face of the captor. The fist goes right up on the captor’s nose. His bones in his nose collide up with his skull and break through there having several pieces of bone chip down into his eye sockets and other holes that are not filled. Bones go up into the brain of the captor as it goes into the juicy part of the brain sticking in there. The captor’s eyes roll back into the back of his head as his legs weaken and they go out from under him. His upper body comes down across and drops down to the ground as well as blood starts to run out of his mouth a little bit. Mark smiles at the man as he looks at the other guy who he slammed on the ground and his eyes aren’t even showing. “And they said that I can’t keep a hold of Haley and they can.” Mark says as he starts to smile as he goes over to the driver’s side of the van. “What are they thinking?”
