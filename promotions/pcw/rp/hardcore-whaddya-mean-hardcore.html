Hardcore?? Whaddya mean Hardcore?!?!

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: not entered [The scene opens in the living room of Alexander Pryce’s Californian Beach House. The room itself is in immaculate condition- complete with glossy coloured walls and funky IKEA furniture. In the center of the room is an ‘L’ Shaped Blue Sofa. Sitting against the sofa is Alexander, strumming on his guitar. He carefully listens to a ‘How to Play Guitar’ CD that’s currently playing on his radio. Voice on the radio: Now place the plectrum between your thumb and index fingers. [Alexander looks at his hand, unsure which fingers to use] Voice: Make sure you grip the plectrum tightly. Alex: Hey, slow down, I’m still on the first ste- Voice: Now strum the first and second strings. Alex: What was that first thing again? Voice: The sound should resemble the following. [A simple guitar tune is then played on the radio. Alexander tries to copy the tune. So he slips the plectrum between his index and middle fingers and strums the guitar roughly only to hear that the sound is terrible and nowhere near the version on the radio. However, Alexander is more concerned with the fact that he’s dropped his plectrum into the gap on the front of the guitar. Voice: We will now progress to playing a different arrangement. Alex: What do I do if I drop the plastic thing in the guitar?? Voice: The Plectrum…. Alex: That’s the one! Voice: Make sure it is tightly secured in your hands…. Alex: NO!! It slipped!! Voice: Place your forefingers over the- Alex: Bah, this blows. [Alex reaches for his remote control and switches off the radio. He then begins to shake the guitar up and down, hoping the plectrum will fall out. Meanwhile, the phone rings in the background] Alex: Ma! Phone! [Alex’s mother, Marie, is busy moving the bed in Alex’s room to a more ‘sunny’ location] Marie: Can you get that? Alex: No- that’s your job. Marie: But I’m pushing your bed! Alex: That’s alright, I think you’re talented enough to do two things at once. [Disgruntled and mumbling profanities under her breath, Marie walks into the living room and heads over to the phone. She takes one look at Alex banging the guitar against his forehead trying to get the plectrum out and rolls her eyes. She picks up the phone and begins to talk into it. As the person on the other end of the line talks, she nods her head and listens carefully] Marie: HARDCORE? WHAT DO YOU MEAN HARDCORE?!?!? [Alexander stops banging the guitar against his forehead and looks up at his mother who is now yelling down the phone] Marie: That’s ludicrous! He doesn’t have the experience to be doing anything like that. Phone: ………. Marie: I don’t care if it’s in his contract- my boy is too damn pretty to be getting his gorgeous face busted up by some brute with a trashcan! [By this point, Alexander begins to look worried. Marie suddenly slams down the phone in anger and turns to her son] Alex: What was all that about? Marie: That was the PCW Administration saying that you have a match on Turmoil. Alex: And? Marie: And it’s a hardcore match… [Alexander’s eyes boggle open and he stumbles backwards only to topple over his guitar] Alex: But I’m an entertainer…. Not some uneducated, smelly in-bred who blows donkeys in his spare time. [Marie looks at Alex and raises an eyebrow] Marie: Didn’t I tell you to stay away from that Beastiality Channel? Alex: Uhh… yeah…. My finger slipped on the remote… [Marie walks away with a worried look on her face and starts stroking her forehead in a stressful manner] Alex: What are we going to do? The most hardcore I’ve ever gotten is listening to DMX! And even then I had no freakin’ idea what he was yelling about. Marie: Stop babbling, I’m sure we’ll come up with something. Alex: What if I dressed like DMX….. And came down to the ring with some serious bang-bang… [Marie gives her son an odd look] Marie: Don’t you mean bling-bling? Alex: No, I was talking about a shotgun. Marie: *sigh* Don’t worry, we’ll figure something out…. [Scene fades out].
