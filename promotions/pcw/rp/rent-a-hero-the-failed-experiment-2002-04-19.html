Rent-A-Hero...The Failed Experiment

19 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4-19-2002 ..::/::..When we last left our hero in his silly misadventures, we found him trying to end the harassment of Aimz by the religious group of C.L.I.T., Christian Ladies International Tribunal, a feminist church group angered by the 'Cool Book' recently released by OSW's fiery redhead and co-partner in an interpromtional love affair with PCW's current Crusierweight champion and superhero, Rent-A-Hero. He walked into the confines of C.L.I.T. to find himself confronted by the lovely and virtuous Clarise Honypanties, a new follower of the ways of C.L.I.T. When confronted about the recent happenings and violent harassements by C.L.I.T., Clarise seemed void of any information, but during the middle of their bonding, Rent-A-Hero was called to the office of a woman...a woman high up within the C.L.I.T. web and our hero was soon to meet her. We now find him following the lovely Miss Honeypanties as he follows her strictly, only taking his eyes off her direction to look at the lovely religious artwork covering the long hallway he is now walking within. Our hero can't help but feel a little nervous about meeting this strange woman, fixing his red and black Spiderman 2099 mesh replica mask and hoping for the best within his wandering mind as he keeps close steps behind Clarise, the blonde Bible thumper saying to our hero with her backed turned to him..::/::..Clarise:'Mr.Hero...you seem like a very good man, I hope you find the answers that your looking for.' Rent-A-Hero:'As do I Clarise...as do I. Tell me, who is this strange female who called me to the confines of her work area on that intercom caller thing-a-ma-bobber?' Clarise:'Oh, that's Miss Hennessy, the leader of this chapter of C.L.I.T. and one of the senior members of the group, she is very influential and she is the very EPITOME of what a C.L.I.T. girl should be....she gave us the motto that 'Every C.L.I.T. girl should always be ready to get down on her knees and get them dirty....' *long pause* '....And pray.'..::/::..Our heor looks to the camera, giving the audience a blank stare as he continues walking and says..::/::..Rent-A-Hero:'Something tells me if I had a better understanding of the whole 'sex' deal that that would be funny to me...but I'm Rent-A-Hero so as usual I'm clueless. Carry on citizens.' ..::/::..FINALLY, our hero reaches the office of Miss Hennessey, marked with a articulate and elegant nameplate with 'Miss Hennessey' engraved in lovely cursive writing upon it. Clarise stands aside, smiling at our hero and says..::/::..Clarise:'Well, I'll leave you to it Mr.Hero, I wish you the best and hope everything is cleared up. I'm sure you'll come to find that Miss Hennessey is a VERY open person and she'll let ANYONE in who's willing to give a little effort.' Rent-A-Hero:'I shall remember this Clarise, your assitance has been helpful.' ..::/::..Our hero opens the door and steps within, Clarise looking a bit concerned once he goes within as she begins to walk away from the door and back down the long hallway, saying to herself with only her echo speaking back to her..::/::.. Clarise:'I knew something strange was happening here...but what is it? Lord help me...'In Miss Hennessey's Office.....::/::..We now find ourselves within the office of Miss Hennessey, lavishly furnished with 19th century style cherry oak furniture, ranging from the shrunk against the wall to the very desk just a few feet in front of him. The office is neatly arranged, everything having some sort of place and order and nothing out of it's way. All these items obviously took time to amass, ranging from French crystal birds to Oriental ceramic eggs. Our hero stands before her arranged desk, his hands clasped at his front as he awaits Miss Hennessy...and his wait is not a long one. Emerging our from a side door in the office is Miss Hennessy...and her looks can be summed up much like the drink that is her last namesake...seductive, intoxicating, and smooth. She emerges from that side door dressed within a tight spahghetti strap dress of the purest white, her long dark hair tied in a neat French roll and her walk one of a regal princess...slow and calculated steps that can't help but get notice. Her eye are like two shimmering diamonds, looking over our hero as she flashes him a slight grin, taking a seat at her desk and crossing her legs, the split in her dress revealing the smooth and well built wonders as our hero can't help but feel a little uncomfortable in her prescence. She makes a slight cage with her fingers as she looks to Rent-A-Hero and she speaks to him, articulate with her wording..::/::..Miss Hennessey:'Hello Mr.Hero...welcome to C.L.I.T. Headquarters. I've heard much about your well-documented wrestling career and you seem to be quite popular amongst many. Might I ask, what is your business within the walls of C.L.I.T.?'Rent-A-Hero:'Well ma'am...I don't appreciate you members harassing my Lois Lane and I have come to demand that it stops before severe reprecussions are taken to ensure that it won't happen anymore. I just want the off-beat C.L.I.T. shenanigans ended before things get out of hand...not that they aren't all ready Miss Hennessey lady type person.'Miss Hennessey: *Gives him a 'weird' look in the eyes* 'Is it hard?' Rent-A-Hero:'What in the Hell-ios star system?' Miss Hennessey:'Is it hard being with that trollop you dare call a girlfriend. Aren't you aware of how unclean, impure, and damaged that vixen is? She is nothing but a heathen of society and it's a shame a man of your purity and stature has sunk so low on the ladder Mr.Hero and that you choose to defend her.' Rent-A-Hero:'NOW SEE HERE MISS HENNESSEY....SHE IS NOT UNCLEAN, SHE TAKES SHOWERS EVERY DAY!!! Now the rest of that stuff, it tis true....but that's what I love about my Amy and that's why she's in my life. Now unless you wanna get beat like The End on Fusion, where I'm gonna smack him about until he cries like a little girl at an N'Sync concert and retain my precious Crusierweight title and keep it safe from unentertaining champions, you better stop harassing my Amy or there's gonna be some thrashing in this biznitch!!' ..::/::..Miss Hennessey arises from her seat, walking around her desk slowly, keeping eye contact with our hero as he begisn to sweat beneath his spandex, the charms of Miss Hennessy starting to take some effect on Rent-A-Hero, who tries not to pay her much attention so he can keep control. She makes her way behind Rent-A-Hero, placing her soft hands upon his shoulders and lightly forcing the standing Rent-A-Hero into a seat, lightly massaging his broad shoulders once forcing him into that. Our hero tries his hardest to fight this, even scrunching up his shoulder as Miss Hennessey begins speaking to our hero..:/::..Miss Hennessey:'Tell me something Rent-A-Hero...haven't you wanted to be apart of something huge? Something INCREDIBLE? Have you ever felt you haven't had a home?'Rent-A-Hero:'On the contrary Miss Hennessey, I am apart of something big...I am apart of something worthwhile and special. One of them being wrestling, entertaining masses of fans and giving them something to believe in every week, and that pattern will continue like always this week when I take End in the ring and I soubdly defeat him. that ring will be filled with talent when he and I step between those ropes, but I will leave the standing and solid victor after showing him WHY I battled my way up a ladder to attain that precious Crusierweight title. I went to the deepest pits of Hades and back to make claim to that belt as my own, and I'll be darned if he is the one that takes it from me Miss Hennessey....NO one will take this belt from me unless it's ONE person...Juan Borro, and when I face him....he will only leave sore and beaten, NOT with my belt. That second thing I am apart of is a relationship Miss Hennessey...I've found something within Aimz that I could not discover on my own. I want to give her everything, take it all away, and give some more. She completes me in the full circle of heroism and I could never walk away from her...for she is my love. Those are the two voids I fill every day and I love them with a passion only a hero could have.' ..::/::..Miss Hennessey takes her hands from off the shoulders of Rent-A-Hero, and moves about to his ear approaching it, a smile tattooed on her face. She licks her lips, nearing them to hsi ear as our hero holds his ground and keeps his feelings in check, having no urge to give into this woman as she says to him quietly..::/::..Miss Hennessey:'That's just what we wanted to hear.'Rent-A-Hero:'HUH? HOLY MEDICINAL HERBS, YOUR WHACKED OUT OF YOUR MIND LADY?! Who is 'we' and what did you want to 'hear'?! I want answers woman!' ..::/::..Rent-A-Hero raises up from out of the chair, Miss Hennessey releasing a hard laugh as she round her desk once again, still looking to Rent-A-Hero with a snicker. Our hero looks incredibly peeved at this display, folding his arms angrily. Miss Hennessey finally calms down and after taking a breath in, she says..::/::.. Miss Hennessey:'Don't you see Rent-A-Hero? Your just a pawn...a toy...a prototype. Don't you understand what has happened? We have been watching you for quite a long time and you suceeded in one thing...having feelings and desire to want to do something or love something....so in that same aspect, you have the desire and want to HATE and to lust to DESTROY. Rent-A-Hero....you have no real memories of your past...because you were CREATED!!!'Rent-A-Hero:'What in the flying monkey poo are you speaking of lady? Your starting to scare me!' *backs away* Miss Hennessey:'You are an ARTIFICIAL PRODUCT Rent-A-Hero...the first in a series...and you became lost shortly after we released you. You came out wrong...you weren't supposed to be so...God....so....nice and caring. We needed a way to find you and test you emotions Rent-A-Hero, so this whole C.L.I.T. thing was a slow plan to get to YOU. We mess with your lover...we knew you'd come to us in due time. You are home now Rent-A-Hero...and we're gonna make you what you SHOULD have been now.'Rent-A-Hero: *Speakinf disarrayed, trying to convince himself* 'YOU LIE!! YOUR A LIAR!!! YOU SPEAK NOTHING BUT LIES!!! I AM RENT-A-HERO, CHAMPION OF PCW, KRYPTON'S OTHER LAST SON, LOVER OF AMY CAMPBELL, HERO TO MILLI..'Miss Hennessey:'ALL A BUNCH OF BULL HERO!! YOU ARE A SCREW UP PROJECT!! YOU WERE GLITCHED FROM THE MOMENT YOU WERE RELEASED YOU FU**-UP!! But we're going to fix you, I swear to that! But first...you need to take a rest.' Rent-A-Hero:'I DON'T NEED A REST, I NEED THE TRUTH!! YOU LIE, THESE AREALL HORRIBLE HORRIBLE LIES EVIL WOMAN LADY!!'Miss Hennessey:'I can see we have work to do...I'm sorry you can't see the truth Hero.'..::/::..Miss Hennessey pulls a drawer open in her desk and presses a tiny green button within it, activating a strange red gas that begins to seep through the air conditioning alarming Rent-A-Hero. Miss Hennessey just stands and laugh as the gas engulfs the room, our hero trying to get to Miss Hennessey, but the effects of the gas begin to overwhelm him...his body slowly breaking down before him as he drops to one knee...then the other....before lying flat on his face, beaten by the gas and by his TRUE past...all that's left being his world going dark..::/::..Somewhere, Hidden In The C.L.I.T. Headquarters.... ..::/::..Darkness....darkness is the only thing we can 'see' as the sounds of beeping and automated equipment repeat over and over in an electronic symphony...the surroundings not able to be made out. The only thing that stands out from the excessive beeping and other automated sounds, is a voice....a stern and evil voice speaking to what appears to be Miss Hennessey, her voice unmistakeable in it's tone as the two converse..::/::..Miss Hennessey:'He fell for it just as you said...'Evil Voice:'Was there ever a doubt. I'm amazed though. Our observations show he displayed drive, motivation, even ended up growing to love and dislike things. I believe when we were originally feeding him the genetic information, there was a screw-up and he ended up like this yapping superhero...but all will soon change, it's not too late to make him what he truly should have been. He even made his soon to be NEW transformation easier...our stats on him shows that he has made love recently, making the original chemical in his body that much easier to activate now. He was never meant to be this yapping...loving...caring...hand-slapping...game playing piece of crap he is now, he was meant to fufill a destiny...to DESTROY all that stood before him with question. I will fix my grave mistake and when I find out what it takes to make him a machine of death...I will soon begin on an army, which will be sold to the HIGHEST bidder.' Miss Hennessey:'But he has a life now...he feels love...he feels need sir....are you sure you wanna rip him away from all of that?' Evil Voice:'When I'm done with him....those feelings will be mere afterthoughts in his life. All I need is time and he will soon be what he should have been long ago...the bringer of death. Prepare everything Miss Hennessey and make sure the ladies have no idea what is going on. By the way, keep EVERYONE out of the building....I want no distractions from completing my perfect super soilder.'Miss Hennessey:'Yes sir...'Evil Voice:'Excellent...'..::/::..The camera pans, light beginning to make it's prescence felt before shortly pushing back the darkness to reveal...Rent-A-Hero...floating lifelessly in a tube filled with chemicals and with many lines hooked up to his limp body....whatever has been planned by this mysterious man looking to take place quite soon...and the effects of it could be life-shattering. WILL THIS EVIL FORCE RETURN RENT-A-HERO TO WHAT HE ORIGINALLY SHOULD HAVE BEEN....A KILLER?! WHO CREATED RENT-A-HERO IN THE FIRST PLACE?! WHO IS GOING TO SAVE RENT-A-HERO?! FIND OUT HOPEFULLY SAME RENT-A-TIME....SAME RENT-A-CHANNEL!!!
