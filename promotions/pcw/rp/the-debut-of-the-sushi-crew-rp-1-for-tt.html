The Debut of the Sushi Crew. RP #1 for TT

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: not entered ** Death, Pain, blood, tears, sweat these are all the things the sushi Crew have endured during their short but successful Wrestling Careers only when they joined forces or were brainwashed into joining forces with the still Mysterious Mr Morozo and joining his Cult “the followers of the hidden star” Mr Morozo prophesied about the end and the higher power rising, Only after Joining “the Followers of the hidden star” did the sushi crew’s luck turn around and they once held the NTICW tag team titles for quite some time but in exchange for gold and glory the sushi crew traded in their souls, their identities becoming mindless zombies. They were one of the biggest tag teams in NTICW, a force to be reckoned with but like all things, that changed for the better being recruited by PCW, now they have changed their target, destruction for all teams who stand in their way for the titles. ** Mr Morozo: “Primetime Championship Wrestling, the so called pinnacle of Wrestling the top place, the best of the best, well there’s no doubt why The Sushi crew have finally made it to PCW then. And on Tuesday, the Sushi crew will debut against the dark messiahs, the debuting of hell, fire, punishment NTICW closed because of the terror The Sushi Crew brought.” ** Ryu Appears from a place and walks next to Mr Morozo, and grunts** Mr Morozo: “ahh Ryu, I see you want to say something to the Dark Messiahs?” ** Ryu takes off his hood revealing, a shaven head, dark rings around his eyes. He resembles Uncle Fester from the hit TV show The Addams Family as he cracks a smile first time in quite a while he has done that. He just simply nods in reply to his master ** Mr Morozo: “then by all means go right ahead” ** Mr Morozo steps back as Ryu looks up fiercely growling** Ryu: “grrr..Dark Messiahs” ** Ryu’s voice booms evilly, his voice has changed since joining this mysterious Cult** Ryu: “ Tuesday Turmoil is your funeral, I am your executioner, have your last meals, say goodbye to your love ones and prepare to move on to the next life, for you shall be the first to fall and be sacrificed to the all mighty himself to show him that me and Otoshi are worthy of being in his presence” ** Mr Morozo merely stands back and watches, smirking almost as if he knows what Ryu is going to say** Ryu: “ Pray for your souls, not that it will help! That is all” ** Ryu walks away back into the darkness vanishing away as Mr Morozo just claps and walks back forward**Mr Morozo: “ He finally speaks, and he has spoken the truth! PCW dark days have begun, the 7 months of devastation are upon us, convert to the Followers of the Hidden Star and save your poor pathetic souls from ultimate Damnation. Its so easy but I know most Humans are stubborn and are hard headed and will think this is just some little ploy but believe me it will not seem like a ploy when your screaming for mercy upon the wrath of the Anti Christ himself while he laughs at your pitiful attempts for salvation when you wasted your chance! Be smart make the right decision and join us, I am not forcing you, I am merely warning you. That is all for now” ** He Walk’s away to the hidden depths of the cave that is the “ The Followers of the hidden star”, meeting place. Are his prophecies true or just shock tatics for people to join his evil cult, does he really want to help people or simply brainwash them. Does the Sushi Crew actually stand a chance against one of the top teams in PCW today? Too many Questions, not enough Answers…yet**
