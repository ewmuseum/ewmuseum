An Unhinged Scene...

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Sunday morning. ‘He’s not laughing now, is he? IS HE? …no… He’s not anymore. The Demon…by Ra! Ha! Laugh not, foul creature, for I hath just struck thy head! MUHAHAAH! His skull’s gotta be split, right? RIGHT?! DAMMIT! You’re no help…’ (The scene begins to fade in from perpetual darkness to show the illuminated chamber of Titu Xansiv. By the Egyptian’s side sits a long, thin sledge hammer, blood stained upon its shimmering head and along its wooden handle. Pharaoh looks around frantically, chuckling every now and again as he grasps at his face, grinning strangely.) The Pharaoh: “No…you’re no good to TALK to! Never were, never WILL be! Fuck off, you silly little shit! Oh no…I can’t be angry with you, though, can I? Ra and the others would surely punish me for shunning my favoured weapon! …Indeed?” (He sprawls out across the sheets of the bed and faces the bloodied anvil, his eyes flickering madly.) The Pharaoh: “No…But you can’t be with me forever! Why, if the police find me here, they’ll take you away! Away I say! Anyway, you did it…not I! NOT I! But he MADE me…all the torment, all the plagues…oh, those dreadful plagues…T’was HIS OWN fault!” (Titu moves away from the weapon and sits up, his face blank and expressionless as he stares aghast into space.) The Pharaoh: “You’re letting it get to you, Titu. NO I’M NOT! Yes…yes you are. Look, you’re talking to yourself! BAH! SHUT UP! Never! You will…will what? Will, you? Neigh!” (Gaining his footing, Titu moves across the elegantly decorated bedroom and looks at himself in the vast, gold-framed mirror just to the left of the four-poster bed.) The Pharaoh: “Totally Tubular Tommy, eh? Yes! …Kill him? No…he hasn’t done anything wrong to us, yet. Us?! There is only I. Yes…only I. But we must defeat him! He’s the Continental Champion. You always wanted that title, didn’t you? ALWAYS! Yes.” (A moment of silence ensues as the Egyptian wrestler continues to gaze upon his reflection. His face twists this way and that, as though fighting against his own skin, something wishing to reveal itself to the world! …And then his face remains colourless, his eyes fixed and cold.) The Pharaoh: “Why?” (He hangs his head, his eyes growing sad as they meet the carpeted floor below.) The Pharaoh: “Why? Why has he done this to me? Look at me. LOOK AT ME! ARGHHH!” (With a sudden burst of anger, Titu runs his arms over a nearby chest of draws, scattering the ornaments sat upon it all over the floor - none break however. Titu falls to one knee, his eyes welling up with tears as he sluggishly looks around the room at the mess he’s created.) The Pharaoh: “You FUCKING son of a bitch, Clyde! You…son…of a bitch…LOOK WHAT YOU’VE DONE!” (As though drunk, Pharaoh stumbles to a vertical base and rushes towards the sledgehammer resting on the sheets. The blood from Clyde’s skull has long since dried and so the virgin-white sheets are unaffected by The Demon’s venomous fluids. Raising an accusing finger at the weapon, Titu scorns the thing, his voice growing ever louder…) The Pharaoh: “Why did YOU HAVE to come along and make things so DIFFICULT?! Who gave you the divine right to take a chunk from that beast’s skull?! ME?! HOW THE DUAT CAN YOU SAY THAT?! I did NOT!” (Titu picks up the hammer by the anvil and throws it across the room. It hits the chamber door and dents it harshly before tumbling to the ground. Fuming, Xansiv watches for a moment with intensity…that moment passes before long and he speaks again…) The Pharaoh: “Speak no more, eh? Good. I need you not anyway!” (He folds his arms across his muscular chest and raises his nose to the ceiling, his eyes arrogant and a tight-lipped expression painted over his maw.) The Pharaoh: “Not so big now, are we? Not so AMAZING! Not so brutal… Brutal… …brutal.” (His eyes grow sad once more.) The Pharaoh: “What have I become? Eh? What have I become? I have turned dark, almost like the demon I fight. I have LOST myself in this moment of anger, and now look... LOOK AT ME NOW!” (He scowls towards the world in general and throws a fist at the air, stumbling a little as he struggles to regain his composure.) The Pharaoh: “And what’s that? You say I have a match against him at March Madness? WHAT IS THIS? Some kind of conspiracy?! I cannot fight him! His face is utterly crushed, his skull ultimately destroyed and his visage full of many more scars! It would be unfair to take him on…one-on-one.” (Clearly deranged by whatever he believes he’s just heard, Titu’s eyes grow wide, his neck twitching and his hands clenching and unclenching time and time again. He sighs desperately and looks around him as though requesting some kind of support.) The Pharaoh: “An unnatural being, you murmur? Able to fight on, you say? Still going to be waiting for me at every single show up until March Madness, you utter?! How can THIS possibly BE? Though I doubt the fact that he’s dead, I can’t say I believe you when you make such ridiculous allegations. The Gods themselves would not believe such idiocy! …But what if you speak the truth? What if you DO indeed speak the truth?” (He considers his own insane words for a second and then begins to nibble at his fingernails, his eyes just as frantic and ever wary as they were when we first faded in on this unhinged scene.) The Pharaoh: “Yes. Yes, I must do that! Make haste, good sire, for you MUST defeat the fiend! Yes…words so true have NEVER been spoken before. A very, very valid point! World Title? WHAT ABOUT IT?! …But my FRIEND Snow HOLDS IT CLOSE TO HIS HEART?! Rip it from his chest? Make the Gods proud?” (He pauses, deliberated over the proposal.) The Pharaoh: “Wicked thoughts…very wicked thoughts. But you DESERVE it! You’ve worked for it! You NEED it, and after you get rid of that demonic Set you can CLAIM it! Good idea. Yeah. Nice…” (A sick grin float over his mouth as the scene begins to move out of the room to the hallways beyond the chamber. Pharaoh’s voice is still audible nevertheless.) “March Madness. MY night. MY night to SHINE! And the plagues? Well, Clyde won’t manage to bring them ALL about, trust me! Trust you? TRUST ME! How can anyone trust a reject such as thy?!” (The groaning frames of the majority of The Palace guard lie bloodied outside the door, littering the broad hallway leading to the fancily decorated room. Obviously some sort of commotion took place here earlier on, but surely it wasn’t The Pharaoh who brutalised these unfortunate men?) “Just trust me. Have SOME respect for my wise words, will you? Wise? HA! Neigh, you shouldn’t distrust the Gods. Indeed. Indeed…” (The scene fades.)
