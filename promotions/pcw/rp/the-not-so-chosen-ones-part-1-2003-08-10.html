The (Not So) Chosen Ones Part 1

10 August 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8 - 10 - 03 Every year MTV holds its annual Movie Awards Show. Usually the show’s hosts consist of 1 popular guy and 1 gorgeous and popular girl. Some of its recent hosts have been Jack Black and Sarah Michelle Gellar, Jimmy Fallon and Kirsten Dunst and even Jon Lovitz and Courtney Cox. Some years MTV mixes it up a little bit and either has one person host the show or as seen this year two men host the show. When MTV went looking for the two best men for the job who do you think wanted it more than anything? They might have wanted it but you don’t get what you always want in life. MTV made the choice and picked pop singer Justin Timberlake and actor Sean William Scott to host the awards much to the dismay of the Tag Team Champions. Thursday June 5th, 2003 (8:50 P.M.) Preston: John hurry up! The MTV Movie awards are about to start! A voice is heard as a mirror like image of “The Golden Boy” is shown from the TV. A little flash is seen as the words…MTV Special Production gleams from the set. A various array of different celebrities and superhero statues are shown widely spread out along a long shiny red carpet known to many award shows and banquets. Gargano: (Sarcastically) Oh! God forbid I miss the Oh so spectacular MTV Movie awards hosted by that pissed on dude from American Pie and that little no talent fruit Justin Timbergay! Preston: Still upset about Sean William Scott and Justin Timberlake getting to host the awards show instead of us eh? John snaps abruptly and begins to stare at Tyler with somewhat of a dumb founded look upon his slender and stubbly face. Tyler ignoring the look just turns away and turns his attention back to the movie awards that are just beginning. Tyler sitting in John’s comfy lazy-boy recliner catches a glimpse out of the side of his eye. He notices that John is now sitting directly in line with the TV reading a newspaper. Tyler also notices that John’s full attention is not sonly on the contents of the paper as he sees John glance up every second to catch the happenings of the opening monologue. The usual opening monologue consists of a hilarious parody of a movie that is currently a hit in the box office. This year Justin Timberlake and Sean William Scott did a parody of the hit sequel “The Matrix: Reloaded”. The parody continues as Tyler chuckles lightly at the numerous jokes planted throughout it. John just sits back and acts as if he is not paying attention but we all know….he is. As the parody finishes up…John begins to smirk but as soon as he catches a hint of Tyler’s attention he puts his head back down to his paper putting on a front for him. Gargano: (Angrily) Why do we have to watch this lame show! I mean they obviously made a bad choice…..Looks like they do not want there show to bring in some big ratings because if they did they would have clearly brought in E2! I mean look at some of the guests they brought in…..Hilary Duff….Amanda Bynes? What target audience are they trying to get? Little boys going thru puberty? Oh I know! They’re trying to get R Kelly to watch the show oh that’s it right? The night passes on………. Thursday June 5th, 2003 (11:30 P.M.) Preston: (Yawning) Well…..John you have to admit Justin and Sean did one hell of a job hosting the awards. I was rolling on the floor and I think I saw your stone face smirk a little bit there during the beat box segment. Gargano: (Surprised) What?! I did not smirk…I uh…I…had a kernel of popcorn stuck in-between my gums and I was trying to get it out with my tongue. That’s all! Preston: John you didn’t even eat popcorn…You just sat there and read the paper. What were you reading that was so interesting that you didn’t even turn the page? Gargano: I……..I……I was reading about this! John looks down abruptly and snatches the paper and without looking hands it to Preston who suddenly gets a surprised look upon his face as he begins to chuckle softly to himself showing the ad back to John. Preston: (Chuckling) Breast Implants huh? Yeah I always wanted some of those. Gargano: Breast Imp...What?! Oh! That Justin Timberlake and Sean William Scott have me so frustrated I can’t even think straight. I’m telling you if I ever have a chance to meet face to face with one of those two…I’m going to…I’m going to. Suddenly John stops mid sentence and stares down at the coffee table directly next to the recliner. He notices a little slip of paper with two tickets posted inside for the upcoming PCW Cloud Nine Airship Tour. Next to the tickets there is a little slip of paper with a listing of all the celebrities that are going to be on the tour. John begins to read the list out loud to himself. Gargano: Dave Matthews Band….. System of a Down….. Britney Spears……..Justin Timberlake... Seann William Scott! This is a sign…from God! Tyler get ready...We’re going to fly on Cloud Nine! End Part 1
