A Space Ship Crash?

6 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6/6/03 After hearing about the rooms filling up quickly on the PCW airship, Brianna phoned in to reserve herself a spot. What drove her to want to spend close to two weeks in the air, possibly running across Dorn Tracer on a day-to-day basis? The first thing that got her was the monstrosity itself, the airship. Inventions so great enthralled her every being, and she would make it her mission to find out all there was to know about the mechanics behind it. The second was the fact that it served as a vacation from life. It’s kind of weird how you can be on vacation, and work at the same time, but PCW somehow managed it all to become a reality. The scene opens up to a cloud of smoke penetrating through the air. The smoke slowly fades away into the sky and gives us a better view of the surroundings, which for the most part is an unoccupied area of land with nothing but tall grass for miles. The reason for the smoke comes into view as the camera pans around. It lands on a pile of burnt up metal which still has small slivers of smoke escaping it. “I thought I had it!” a very irate Brianna Washington belts out coming into view with a soiled face. She eyes the pile of junk and kicks it to relieve some anger. Brianna walks away from the wreckage and back towards her newly bought red Saturn, which sits about eight feet from the ball of smoke. Brianna opens up the passenger side door and pulls out a black duffel bag. She unzips it and opens it up immediately going for a sketchpad. “Hmm…” Brianna let’s out while flipping through the pages, stopping on a picture that can be best described as a blue print for a small rocket. She looks at it for a few more seconds before tearing it out and crumpling it up into a tight ball, which she launches into the pile of charred metal. “Another wasted afternoon, with another junk invention. I guess in the end it never hurts to try, even if things have the tendency to blow up in your face.” She laughs while throwing the duffel bag back in the passengers seat. Instead of going straight home she decides to sit atop the cars hood staring up at the sky, which is partly cloudy at this time of day. “Just a little relaxation time for myself and I’ll go back home. I think a solid half hour out here will help clear my mind.” She says somewhat groggily as her eyes shut. Instead of doing the thinking she thought she would, Brianna ultimately falls into the dark hole known as sleep. The hours go by and yet she is still pretty much isolated until a raggedy old truck comes barreling through the grass stopping right next to the car. Two big men (length and width wise) emerge from the car wearing dirty overalls and baseball caps. They come over and stare at Brianna as though she were some oddity. “What you reckon’ she doin out here pop?” the younger looking man says while extending a finger to touch Brianna on the arm. Before he does, his dad grabs his arm and smacks him in the gut. “Don’t touch her! That’s how they get you with the harassment.” “Smart thinkin pops. But don’t you think we should wake her up? I mean it is getting pretty dark and for all we know she be scared of the dark.” “Just hold your tongue boy!” he steps away from his son and looks around. His son joins in as they look around the open land, stopping every once and a while to spit globs of saliva to the ground. “Pop come look at this!” The son is standing over the pile of burnt metal which is somehow still letting out spurts of smoke. “What in the world?” the dad bends down and reaches his hand out to touch it getting a singed finger in the end. “OUUUCH!” he bellows stepping away. “What happened?” being the ignorant human being he is, the son shoves his hand on the heated metal and comes back screaming. He races around in circles holding his hand in pain. “Where’s the water pop? I need water! It hurts!” “Spit on it.” The son makes a hocking noise and spits on his hand which does nothing. Realizing (which is hard for him to do) that he has nothing to ease the pain, he just holds his hand out shaking it feverishly walking back to his dad. Somehow all of the screaming doesn’t bother Brianna as she is still fast asleep on top of the car looking quite contempt. “You want to know what I think this is? I think it’s a spaceship.” The dad says stroking his matted graying beard. “You don’t think none of those green alien things are running around waiting to probe us do ya?” the son looks around nervously cowering at the thought. “Man if only it was legal to put people to sleep.” “What was that pop?” “Nothing son….nothing.” Several minutes go by with out much noise as the two men still stand above the heap of junk trying to figure it out. Being the brighter of the two, the father beams with pride as an idea comes to his beer ridden mind. “Let’s call the news! They take stories like this all the time, and we could even get money!” The two rush back to their truck and drive off, Brianna still fast asleep. About an hour later the two men return with a Ch. 8 news crew following close behind. A lady wearing a gray business suit comes out from the van, with her cameraman following close behind. “So where’s this so called spaceship crash?” the lady asks somewhat ecstatic. The men go back to the heap of smoking metal and the newslady stops by Brianna staring with a raised eyebrow. “Who’s this?” “She was here when we got here. I speculate that she was returned to earth after being probed by rogue aliens from outer space.” The dad explains getting a few weird looks from the news crew. They walk over to the used to be rocket and the lady looks at it suspiciously trying to determine if this story has some merit at all. That’s when Brianna sits up from the hood of her car letting out a big yawn which startles everyone. Brianna looks a bit confused. “Is something wrong?” she asks, sliding off the hood of her car and walking over to the hovering group. “We’re reporting a space ship crash.” The son says happily trying to get close to Brianna who steps away, repulsed by his grunginess. “Space ship? It’s a rocket!” she throws out trying to bring some sense to things. “How would you know?” the father says matter of factly. “Because I built it. It’s a rocket.” No one seems to believe her so she reaches down and grabs up the crumbled piece of paper lying beside it. “See.” She unravels it and reveals the blueprints for the rocket which gets frowns. “I knew it was too good to be true. Come on Roy. Let’s go cover that orphan fire.” The news crew get back into their van and drive off. The father and son walk away after a few minutes having nothing to say. As they drive off Brianna laughs to herself shaking her head in total disbelief. “A space ship? Nice to know that my inventions have accomplished hysteria. Now if only I could get them to work properly.” Brianna laughs again and slides into the driver’s seat of her Saturn. As she turns on the car she takes a look at the time and is shocked to see that she had been out here for about five hours. Instead of fretting, she starts the car and drives off. Never a dull moment in this life of mine…never a bit of normalcy either. I guess all I have to look forward to now is Turmoil, and the long vacation away from all of this craziness. The scene fades to black.
