Ingrates and Usurpers. Far More Dangerous Than Any Tag Team.

15 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3-15-02 (The scene opens up to the great city of Omaha, Nebraska, home of....steaks.....and Doug Troy. We are immediately introduced to the urban area which is set before the blue midwestern sky and thin cirrus clouds. The sun shines brightly on this afternoon in March, and glints off the tinted window of a vehicle that is gliding through the streets. We are brought closer to the car, which is identified as the silver 'presidential' limousine that was parked outside the Houston office building last time we saw our future dictator. The baby blue and white flags of Argentina wave and flap with the passing winds as the vehicle cruises at a relatively slow pace and is surrounded by local Omaha police motorcycles and black luxury cars--which are also adorned with mini Argentinean flags. A huge backlog of cars stretches out for hundreds of feet as the aspiring political leader's precession goes on. The impatient scream of dozens honking horns rises up in the air and echoes through the tall buildings of downtown Omaha as we are now transferred into the limo itself--viewing our Frisco in a gray FRISCACEMANI~! suit and a cell phone in his hand. The South American playboy puffs on a fine Cuban cigar and rolls his eyes at whatever voice is coming out of the phone. Surrounding the legend are professional-looking Hispanic men in suits. There are 4 of them--2 next to Frisco and 2 across from him. They all look fairly busy, a few talking on their phones and the rest, sifting through their briefcases. Frisco's voice bursts out loudly through the commotion of the vehicle.) Frisco: 'Cierren sus bocas, cabrones!' (A brief silence is heard as Frisco frowns and covers his phone with his hand.) Frisco: 'Gracias.' (Frisco shakes his head as the other occupants lower their tones and slow their actions.) Frisco: 'Now, as I was saying, I want a red carpet leading up to the platform...not a goddamn brown one!' (Frisco bobs his head impatiently.) Frisco: 'No, you see--red is for important people, brown is for peasants and nobodies....No, I don't care if it's a $10,000 carpet, it's fucking brown! How am I supposed to make a clear, forceful statement with a brown fucking carpet!?' (Frisco blows out a cloud of smoke.) Frisco: 'Uh huh...with my overflowing charisma? Yes, I appreciate your noble attempt at asskissing, but that's not going to work. Now that you've disrespected me with an ugly brown carpet, I want a red carpet with gold fringes and Argentinean flag logos...' (Frisco smirks and glares a hole at one of his associates when his briefcase shuts. The associate holds his hand up in apology.) Frisco: 'You don't know where to get one? Well, that's just too bad. You better have one in there by...' (Frisco glances at his watch.) Frisco: 'The end of the hour...or your little convention will have to settle for one of my dumbshit local executive committee members instead of me.' (One of the associates jerks up in shock and pouts. Frisco speaks into his phone again.) Frisco: '...I--I don't care. If you have to dig up your rotting whore of a grandmother from her grave and make her weave my rug on an 18th century loom, that's what you'll do.' (Frisco chuckles.) Frisco: 'Oh, your grandmother was cremated? Well, son, you're shit out of luck then, aren't ya!?' (Frisco slams the phone shut and sets it down. He sucks on his cigar and blows out the smoke with a slight cough. One of the party members speaks up in a thick Spanish accent.) Party Member: 'Senor Presidente, aren't ju being a little too harsh on these guys?' (Frisco's eyes bulge with this disrespectful act of questioning.) Frisco: 'Excuse me? Me, harsh? I am the benevolent fucking saint of Argentina. Harshness is not one of my virtues. Try merciful.' Party Member: 'But...' (Frisco leans toward the party member and grabs him by the tie.) Frisco: 'Call me merciful now, or I'll fire you and throw you out of the fucking car!' (The party member shakes nervously.) Party Member: 'Jou're merciful! Benevolent Saint of Argentina...jeh, jeh jou're right!' (Frisco's lips twitch as he nods.) Frisco: 'Thank you, my dear man, you're now promoted to uhh...Secretary of uhh..' (Frisco looks at his cigar.) Frisco: 'Cigars. Secretary of Cigars.' (The party member's eyebrows go up.) Party Member: 'Umm, thank you?' Frisco: 'You're welcome.' (The new Secretary of Cigars frowns and scratches his head.) Party Member: 'Soo..uhh...what does this Secretary of Cigars entail esatly?' (Frisco shrugs helplessly.) Frisco: 'Hell if I know, I'll have my Secretary of Definitions come up with that, but it'll have more perks and increased salary than what you were...' (The party member smiles. Another party member speaks up in a similar accent of the last man.) Party Member #2: 'Excuse me...President Valerius, but ju fired jur Secretary of Definitions last week.' (Frisco sighs.) Frisco: 'I did?' Party Member #2: 'Si, presidente, ju were mad becouse his pen ran out of ink and he asked ju for a new one.' (Frisco snaps his fingers.) Frisco: 'Oh, that fucking ingrate! He had it coming. Asking me for a new pen...what does he think, my campaign has an unlimited budget? Pfff! He should've been more prepared.' Party Member #2: 'Claro que si, jefe.' (Frisco looks down for a second and looks back up at the previously speaking party member.) Frisco: 'Who are you again?' Party Member #2: 'Ehh...Victor Bargonez. I'm in charge of the Puerto Santa Cruz chapter.' (Frisco thinks for a second as he blows out a cloud of smoke.) Frisco: 'Yes, of course, Puerto Santa Cruz...How would you like to be the new Secretary of Definitions?' (The party members nods.) Party Member #2: 'Oh...okay. Sure.' Frisco: 'Congratulations.' (Frisco puts out his cigar in a platinum ash tray with a large replica sestertius of the Roman Emperor Nero.) Party Member #2: 'But..Senor Presidente..?' (Frisco raises an eyebrow.) Frisco: 'Yes?' Party Member #2: 'Who will be in charge of Puerto Santa Cruz?' (Frisco scoffs.) Frisco: 'What are you trying to say?' Party Member #2: 'I'm..uhhh...just sayin-...' Frisco: 'Are you saying that I don't know how to do my job?' Party Member #2: 'No, no, no!' Frisco: 'You're saying that I'm making a mistake? You don't think that was a good idea to leave the Puerto Santa Cruz chapter without a head?' Party Member #2: 'I'm n--...' (Frisco grabs the new Secretary of Definitions by the tie.) Frisco: 'Oh, I get it, you greedy piece of shit! You want to be the head of the Puerto Santa Cruz chapter AND the Secretary of Definitions? You're trying to gain more power so you can usurp my presidency! That's what you're doing! I should've known all along, you devious power-hungry pendejo!' (Frisco looks over at the door.) Frisco: 'Abre la puerta!' Party Member #2: 'No, por favor!' (One of the nearby party members panics and opens the door. Frisco looks back at the petrified man.) Frisco: 'You fucking ingrate! I promote you to a nice position and you try to rebel against me and take over my future office! You are no longer my Secretary of Definitions! You're fired from El Partido de la Pantera de Argentina and you will be deported from Argentina upon my ascension!' (Frisco shoves the ex-party member to the member by the door.) Frisco: 'Tiralo!' Party Member #2: 'Noooooooooooooo! Por favor! Ayudame! Ayudame!' (The man by the door shoves the ex-party member out of the moving car as he screams and begs. The man drops out of the limo and rolls onto the street as the limo continues on its 10 mile per hour path. Frisco looks back as he sees the man get up safely and stumble away, crying his eyes out.) Frisco: 'Ingrates! A party full of ingrates!' (Frisco sighs loudly.) Frisco: 'I need a new Secretary of Definitions and a chapter head for Puerto Santa Cruz. Any takers?' (The 3 remaining party members don't budge.) Frisco: 'Come on! Don't be so fucking modest! You all are in need of a promotion.' (One of the members raises his hand.) Frisco: 'Yes, you there!?' Party Member #3: 'Can I be the new head of Puerto Santa Cruz?' Frisco: 'Are you from there?' Party Member #3: 'No, but one of my cousins lives there.' (Frisco nods.) Frisco: 'Good enough, you got it.' (Frisco looks over to the remaining member.) Frisco: 'How about you? You wanna be the new Secretary of Definitions?' (The party member looks up with passive eyes.) Party Member #4: 'I'm already the Secretary of Liquor, senor presidente.' (Frisco nods.) Frisco: 'You can be both, there's no harm in that.' (The party member nods.) Frisco: 'Good man, you accepted both positions! I admire your ambition! You are definitely going places!' Party Member #4: 'Gracias, presidente Frisco.' (Frisco pats the man on the shoulder.) Frisco: 'Now, poor me a glass of STOLICHNAYA~! please.' Party Member #4: 'Yes, sir.' (The party member snatches a bottle of fine STOLICHNAYA VODKA~! out of the mini fridge and grabs a chilled martini glass. He pours it in as Frisco looks through his tinted windows and farther up into the section where the limo's gunners stand by their turrets.) Frisco: 'Hmm...' (Frisco peers through the window a little harder.) Party Member #4: 'Your drink, jefe...' Frisco: 'Shhh!' (Frisco grabs the drink out of his secretary's hand and takes a sip.) Frisco: 'Do you see that?' (Frisco squints his eyes and points his glass at something outside.) Frisco: 'A kid with an Akiyama t-shirt...' Party Member #1: 'So....?' (Frisco frowns.) Frisco: 'So!? Do you know how rare those kinds of people are?' (Frisco takes a sip of his martini.) Party Member #3: 'No...' Frisco: 'Extremely rare! I'm gonna bag it!' (Frisco taps on the separating glass window where his two gunners sit. The window rolls down. Frisco points out the window to a burly Mexican man in sunglasses and an old flak jacket.) Frisco: 'Guerra, tírele!' (The man grins and nods. He then pulls the trigger on the huge protruding machine gun and sprays the ground with bullets. The kid in the Akiyama t-shirt jumps and runs away with his arms flailing in the air.) Frisco: 'Ha ha!' (The kid disappears from view with the only victim being a fire hydrant, which was pierced by a few bullets and sprays water all over the street and the passing cars. The gunner stops shooting.) Guerra: 'Ay, mierda! Lo siento, presidente!' (Frisco smiles and sips his vodka.) Frisco: 'Esta bien. Buen esfuerzo.' (Frisco yawns and slinks down a little in his seat.) Frisco: 'You guys get back to work and handle any calls I may get...' (Frisco turns his cell phone back on, which immediately rings. Frisco throws it over to his Secretary of Definitions and Liquor.) Frisco: 'I'm going to take a nap. Wake me when we get to the convention.' (The party members nod as the Secretary of Definitions and Liquor answers the phone and the rest of the members open their briefcases up to do paperwork. Frisco takes another sip of his vodka as he closes his eyes and exhales deeply. The scene switches to outside of the limo, which still cruises at a leisurely pace through downtown Omaha.
