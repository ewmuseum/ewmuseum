Title: Back In Black: Don't Be Drooling On My Food Man. Featuring: Duke Williams Date: 12/17/02 If you want to get to heaven you got to D-I-E put on your coat and T-I-E. If you want to beat Kannon at his sick game you got get just as sick and dirty. You become nothing higher then a weasel or any rodent in the hierarchy of life. Hotel, motel, Tuesday night the malice man gets hungry and goes out for a bite. In a town as jive as Jesus Christ, Chester the cat, and Kenny G, its hard to find a dive that suits Duke just right. But the man in black pounds on and strolls the clean streets of Hubbardville. Williams: I hate Wisconsin. The people in this state are so backwards and drunk they might as call it Canada. I mean it almost is in Canada so lets just give it to then for all the pollution we send them. It would be an even trade. Duke lights up a punch cigar as he strolls with a hip Midwestern pimp limp down the white bread streets of masked perfection. Williams: Of all the cities I get stuck in it has to be the only dry one. This city is capital L 7 hoss. It’s about as interesting as an Ellie/Xavier sex video leaked out on the Internet. Have you seen this tape?? It affectionately known as the steer and the queer tape. You can find it on Kazza I’m sure. Duke shakes his head in disgust when a traffic cop approaches him. Cop: Sir where do you think your going?? Williams: The Hubbard burger. Cop: Its 8:30pm we have curfews in this town. Williams: I have a curfew in my pants but you don’t see me bragging. Cop: Sir have you been drinking. Duke looks at the cop like he’s nuts. Williams: Hey hoss don’t you know this town is as dry as Martha Stewart’s twat. Cop: Sir!! Williams: Why do you know where I can score some booze? Cop: I’ve had about enough of you. Hey aren’t you the guy who is fighting our great sports icon Xavier Kannon this Sunday at Decimation? Williams: What that Duke Williams guy? Cop: Yea that’s the unsavory character. Williams: No, no, no, you may have seen me on TV I’m one of Mr. Kannon’s bodyguards. Cop: You work for Kannon??? Williams: Yes sir. He is a great man isn’t he? Cop: Oh yes!! Well I’m sorry Mr? Williams: Walden….Tim “dick smack” Walden. Cop: I’m sorry I didn’t recognize you Mr. Walden. Williams: I get that a lot. Cop: Well good luck to Mr. Kannon and have a great dinner. The cop strolls along as Duke heads to the tasty Hubbard burger. Williams: Jesus I wish the rest of America’s cops were that gullible. Its like pissing on a tree in Disney land. This is a wonderful town, I bet I can nail the bank, church, grocery, store and every broad in the ass and nobody would catch me. Duke smiles at the thought of free sex, money, and an outlaw image. Williams: Naw I got bigger fish to fry. Kicking Kannon’s ass would be a lot more enjoyable. And if that chubby chipmunk Ellie Kannon lays a greasy paw on big daddy Duke she’s going to get it. I’m not playing games this round with Poncho Villa and lard ass. Duke jives into the local hamburger joint. Remember when a joint was still just a bad place to be?? Before Nixon and Clinton lied to us all on TV. Coke was still cola?? Anyways there are no lines so Duke strolls right up to the pimply faced Hubbard Burger server. Williams: Christ even Hubbard couldn’t cure your acne. That’s fucking gross man. That’s the last thing I want to see walking into a restaurant to order greasy food. You’re like an anti-greasy food promotion. Instead of truth.com it needs to be zits.org anywho I need some food young man. Jimmy: Ah…Ah..Ah….ok…welcome to Hubbard Burger would you be interested in trying out new E-meter chilly fries? Williams: Jimmy the menu is right in front of me. You silly bastards put your fucking chilly fries in bold print with a picture of them above your puss filled head. Let me tell you what I want Jimmy. I want four hamburgers and an order of fries. On those hamburgers I want ketchup and nothing else. NOTHING ELSE means no fucking mustard, onions, pickles, NOTHING. You follow cus if I find anything other then bun, meat, and ketchup on my hamburgers I will personally ram them down your piss hole and fill you up with Gatorade and watch you wiz Jimmy. You don’t know pain till you fuck with me. Just like Kannon is going to learn come Sunday. Jimmy goes back to make the order. Williams: And Jimmy don’t be drooling on my food man I’m watching. Duke pulls out his money clip with a picture of Brandi naked on it from his hidden camera in the ladies room. Jimmy: That will be six dollars sir. Williams: Jimmy you’ve done well I’m proud of you. Keep the change. Duke tosses the young man a Hamilton then sits down in a booth. He checks to make sure his burgers are the way he ordered them. Duke smiles with satisfaction as he starts to eat. Williams: Kannon you know the story of the tortoise and the hare? Everybody thought that the ugly, scientologist loving, fat assed wife, bunny would win the race. Everybody counted the big, bad, sexy, turtle out. How can an old, slow, turtle beat a young rabbit? Well in the end the Rabbit let his confidence, ego, and power get the best of him and the old turtle won. Duke checks the time on his gold watch. Williams: You see in this match I’m the turtle and you’re the hare. You think your jive jones but the fact is you ain’t. I’m the real McCoy while your just a fart in the wind. So root and toot and try to blow me down baby but once the stink clears you ain’t nothing. This Sunday your ass is grass and hoss I’m John Deere. See you Sunday and get ready to be sent Whiskey Bent and Hell Bound! Duke finishes up his food as the camera fades. =w= ---- View Duke Williams's Biography
