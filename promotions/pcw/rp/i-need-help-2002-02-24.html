I Need Help

24 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2/24/02 (The scene fades inside an office. The early morning sun shines through the blinds on the window, onto the leather chair in the middle of the room, and Big Daveylicious lays back on. Big D’s choice of attire this morning is Oakley sunglasses, black boots, Tommy Hilfinger jeans, and a black 100% cotton Thugs For Hire t-shirt. Big D’s old stable. On front of the shirt it is “The Sopranos” styled. On the front of the shirt in black in white are Big D, Tom Walzack, Badge, and Big D’s opponent for Sabotage, Tim Walden. Walden and Big D were good friends in the Thugs For Hire. We all know that Badge and Walden betrayed Walzack and Big D at Alliances. Thus splitting the Thugs For Hire. The question is do they remain friends? We look around the office and notice several degrees in picture frames on the walls of the office. On the opposite direction of Big D stands a desk. Sitting behind the desk in another leather chair consists of a bald middle aged man in a suit. He flips through a certain folder. He introduces himself while staring at inside the folder. ) Man: Hello, I’m Dr. John Inston. Big Daveylicious: Nice to meet you. Dr. Inston: This is a treat! It’s not every day you get PCW Wrestler in waltz into your office. Big Daveylicious: No, it’s not... Dr. Inston: Now, think of the celebrity status I’ll get. I’ll be the worlds favorite psychotherapist! Big Daveylicious: Doc... Dr. Inston: I’ll even have celebrities coming to me with their problems! I’ll be on the covers of magazines! I’ll have books and television spots! I’ll... Big Daveylicious: Hello, Doc? Dr. Inston: I’ll retire in class and spending my days in Bermuda with the beach! Big Daveylicious: Doctor, you don’t watch a lot of PCW wrestling do you? Dr. Inston: Well...uh...no. Big Daveylicious: I haven’t even won a title. The only damn good thing that has happened to me was Sassy’s crotch! (Inston eyes fill in disappointment about his dream fading away.)Big Daveylicious: Plus, what about that “Doctor-Patient” relationship? Dr. Inston: I’m sorry, Mr. Daveylicious but I’m not a homosexual. Big Daveylicious: I didn’t mean that. I meant the oath promise deal where the only information that goes in this room stays in it between me and you. Dr. Inston: Oh, that old thing. (Inston laughs.) Dr. Inston: You actually believe in that thing do you? Big Daveylicious: Yes, actually. Dr. Inston: It’s just away to make our patients feel good to see us again and pay extraordinary amounts of money! Big Daveylicious: Doc, you gotta keep this meeting a secret! Nobody is to know what goes on here. Some people shouldn’t just know! Just don’t say anything about me being here today! Dr. Inston: Sure, no need to get worried. I’m not allowed to tell anybody. The doctor-patient relationship prevents it. Big Daveylicious: You said that... Dr. Inston: Put the jokes aside and everything will be great. Big Daveylicious: You better not be scamming me. Dr. Inston: Maybe your problem starts with paranoia. Do you usually look over your shoulder or be in places where nobody is and still look around? Big Daveylicious: Sometimes... Dr. Inston: Are you aggravated in your situation at work? Big Daveylicious: Yeah, tell me about it. They got rid of the women! Dr. Inston: Does this urge make you strive to be the best? Does this cause you to ignore others and pay attention to only yourself? Big Daveylicious: Correct! Dr. Inston: You want to marry your grandmother and you hate your grandfather. Your a very sick disturbing man. I recommend Advil. Now, my fee... Big Daveylicious: That’s not my problem! Dr. Inston: It isn’t? Big Daveylicious: No, it isn’t my problem. Do I look like some inbred ingrate?! My problem is that I have barely seen a naked women this month! Dr. Inston: You should try give them the pill. It works for... Big Daveylicious: I’m still wondering if the Feminist groups are done battling me and to top it off yesterday after a autograph leaving an autograph session. A hot looking girl... *Flash Back* (The scene fades to the end of a PCW autograph session in a mall. Several people are leaving. Big D decides to leave but a great looking girl in a red mini skirt and black raven like hair catches his attention. He walks over to the girl and calls her out) Big Daveylicious: Hey, baby wanna see why I am the Main Attraction of Every Women's Satisfaction? (She turns to him with a smirk.) Girl: I got a boyfriend, ya big handsome sexist bully. (Big D stares off like somebody just killed his best friend. Now, we return to the Dr. Instons office with a view of Big D.) Big Daveylicious: I mean she called me a sexist bully. It hurt. It really hurt. People are so quick to judge, and they shun my point of view. If they got to know Big Daveylicious they'd know I have feelings too. It hurts me when I have to destroy a man or Walden into next week with the Daveylicious Bomb. I mean Tim was a good friend and... (Big D stops for a moment turns around to see Dr. Inston asleep on his desk.) Big Daveylicious: Doc... (No reaction.) Big Daveylicious: DOC! (The doctor wakes up in shock. Wipes his eyes and looks at his wrist watch.)Dr. Inston: Okay, times up and my fee is $300 dollars. Thank you and come again. Big Daveylicious: Did you even listen to me? (With the evil greedy smile of the doctor and the angry Big D the scene fades to black.)
