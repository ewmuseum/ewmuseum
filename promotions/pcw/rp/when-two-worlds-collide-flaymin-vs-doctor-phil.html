When Two Worlds Collide- Flaymin Vs Doctor Phil

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: not entered When we last left off, Flaymin was a psychological wreck.With his life in tatters, his wrestling career in jeopardy…. And his fridge out of beer- the world was obviously crashing down around him. In steps Dr Phil, the world’s most nauseating television psychologist- can he turn around Flaymin’s fortunes, especially with Alliances closing in fast? 2 people from the different side of the tracks….2 people with a clashing view on issues….2 people, one with no hair and the other with a whole lotta afro-tastic hair…. 2 people….. the grand total of people who will read this rp! [Scene opens in the TV Studio where ‘Doctor Phil’ is filmed. Inside his dressing room, Flaymin rifles through the mini-bar unimpressed by all the bottled water and vegetables placed within the fridge] Flaymin: What kind of a mini-bar is this? [Flaymin swiftly kicks the side of the fridge, knocking it over and causing all the water bottles and vegetables to roll all over the room. Suddenly, the door opens and in steps the show’s producer- A woman in her mid-30’s, wearing a headset and clutching a clipboard in one hand and a walkie-talkie in the other. She looks at the scattered bottles of water and vegetables and frowns] Producer: Is there something wrong with the mini-bar? Flaymin: Where do I start….. [Flaymin takes a deep breath] Flaymin: No beerNo wineNo chipsNo meatNo candyNo chocolateNO NOTHING! Is this a freaking health farm or a TV show? Producer: Dr. Phil recommends only the healthiest foods. [Flaymin mumbles something underneath his breath] Producer: Look you can go and personally complain to him if you want. Flaymin: Really? Producer: Yep, you’re on the air in five minutes. [The producer and Flaymin walk out of the dressing room and down the corridor. They walk through a main walkway and onto the actual TV set. Producer: Remember, No profanity, no aggressive behaviour. Flaymin: I come in peace…. Producer: Good. [A few minutes pass as the set assistants carefully re-arrange the furniture while the cameramen put their cameras into position. Dr Phil then walks out from an adjacent door, he takes the microphone and walks onto the set to talk to the studio audience] Dr Phil: Welcome to the show, I’m Doctor Phil.Today we have got a special guest- an athlete.Over the past few months he has plummeted into depression.Partly due to his alcohol addition. And partly because he was consumed by pornstars. Lets give Flaymin Aymin a warm welcome! [The studio audience, most of whom don’t even watch wrestling just shrug their shoulders before deciding to clap politely as Flaymin walks onto the set and takes a seat opposite Dr Phil. Phil stares at Flaymin….. and Flaymin uncomfortably stares right back. Flaymin: Soooo…. Dr Phil: I look at you and see a scared little boy who is running away from something. [Flaymin stamps his foot down and speaks in a stern voice] Flaymin: Look, lets set the record straight!I didn’t touch no scared little boys at ‘Never Never Land’, you know…. That was all Michael.I was busy trying to get my car keys off Bubbles the monkey the whole time…. I swear. [Dr Phil gives Flaymin and strange look before raising an eyebrow and shaking his head] Dr Phil: How about we start again. Flaymin: Sure, I’m getting paid by the minute, anyways. Dr Phil: Ahem, now how did your alcohol addiction begin? Flaymin: Well back in Australia there was a cow shortage.The cow shortage led to a milk shortage.The milk shortage led to a- [Frustrated, Phil interrupts Flaymin mid-sentence] Dr Phil: Just, get to the point. Flaymin: Well without any milk, we had to find an alternative to milk to put on our cereal in the morning. Dr Phil: And you used whisky? Flaymin: What?No!What kind of fool do you take me for? Whisky cost a fortune back then so we just had to settle with beer, instead. Dr Phil: I see.Now looking back on your child hood, do you think this excessive beer consumption has led to any rash decisions or misfortune? Flaymin: Nope, I feed my seven children beer n’ cereal all the time and I’m sure they’ll turn out fine too. [Dr Phil puts his hand on arms on Flaymin’s shoulder] Dr Phil: Repeat after me- ‘I am an alcoholic’. [Flaymin looks at Phil in confusion.Phil nods his head, anticipating Flaymin to repeat the statement] Dr Phil: Come on, ‘I am an alcoholic’. [Flaymin reaches over and pats Dr Phil on the head] Flaymin: There, there, I’m sure we can find a clinic to help you kick the habit.Tell me, do you think this excessive beer consumption has led to any rash decisions or misfortune? [Dr Phil’s face begins to turn red out of irritation] Dr Phil: WHAT THE HELL ARE YOU TALKING ABOUT???YOU JUST REPEATED EVERYTHING I JUST SAID!! Flaymin: Correct me if I’m wrong, but didn’t you tell me just 30 seconds ago to repeat everything you said? Dr Phil: BUT YOU SAID THE WRONG STATEMENT!! [Flaymin, places his right leg over his left one and strokes his chin] Flaymin: Tell me, does this problem of memory loss and confusion affect you very often? Dr Phil: YOU are the one with the problem! Flaymin: This coming from the guy yelling and screaming, with a head as red as a tomato…. Somehow I believe this issue is related to your poor standard of mini-bars backstage. The fact that you fill them up with nothing but water and vegetables must really frustrate you.Its eating away at your soul because just once you’d like to have a chocolate bar instead of a carrot stick. [With sweat now running down his face, Phil nervously shakes his head] Dr Phil: Not true! [Flaymin soothingly shakes Dr Phil’s hand] Flaymin: There is no need to suppress this anger.I am envisioning your spiritual awakening. Dr Phil: No, no…. it can’t be! Flaymin: I look into your eyes and I see a scared little boy who just wants to scream out ‘FUCK YOU OPRAH, IF IT WEREN’T FOR ME YOUR SHOW WOULD HAVE BEEN CANNED BY NOW!!’ Dr Phil: No, I won’t say it! Flaymin: Come on, Phil, unleash the anger!!Tell Oprah what’s been on your mind this whole time! [Unable to take anymore of this interrogation, Phil clutches his head, digs his fingernails into his scalp and begins to yell] Dr Phil: AAAAAAARGGGGGHHHHHH!!!!!! OPRAH YOU LITTLE WHORE, I…… I…… NEVER LIKED YOUR BOOK CLUB AND…. AND YOUR MAGAZINE SUCKS MONKEY BALLS!! [Phil gets up and rips off his tie and throws it on the ground.He overturns the coffee table in front of the sofa and then turns back around and knocks over the sofa too. He then looks up to the heavens and begins to shriek] Dr Phil: MY SOUL IS FREE!!!BEAM ME UP, GODDESS OF DARKNESS!!!WE SHALL DESTROY THE OPRAH CHRONICLES TOGETHER!! [Yelling and screaming, Phil runs off the set to fetch his shotgun and take out Oprah. Meanwhile, Flaymin remains on the trashed set looking around at the ruins with a smirk on his face] Flaymin: Some people have serious issues…. [Scene fades to black]
