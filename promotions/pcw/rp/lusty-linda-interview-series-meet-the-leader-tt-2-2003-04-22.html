Lusty Linda Interview Series: Meet the Leader (TT#2)

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 22/4/03 A faint crackle, then the picture renders, showing a nice-looking, blonde woman, with a light blue skirt and a matching light blue blouse. She is stood, leaning, seductively against the side of the wall, with a decidedly mischievous smile etched upon her face. She slowly raises her arm, then sexily beckons the camera over with her hand. The camera slowly zooms in, first to her ample breasts and then to her beautiful, tanned visage. “I know what your thinking…” She seductively, suggests. “You’re thinking; who the hell is this woman?; and where are the superhero team you’re all just…dying to meet.” The camera slowly zooms out again, after another detour of her breasts, as she points to the door next to her. Upon the door there is a name, etched in a child like fashion. The camera zooms in and we can plainly see that it says ‘Blind Rage’. The camera zooms out again. “My name’s Lusty Linda, Justice Incorporated’s brand new agent and I have arranged a series of interviews with the team, which firmly establish them as likely contenders for the PCW Tag Team Titles.” With that she opens the door and beckons the camera into the room, again with seductive intentions in mind. The scene cuts to the massive, African-American frame of Primary Justice Inc member Blind Rage, as he sits, facing he left of the screen in a typical interview type position. Linda: [From behind the camera] So, Blind Rage, how and why did you become a superhero? Blind Rage: Well, there is a little confusion at that point. I’m not quite sure how my powers developed, however, I have been examined many times in the past and therefore have an explanation of how I am so strong. Linda: Really? Blind Rage: Indeed. My body contains an abnormal amount of adrenaline, over twenty times the amount of a normal human being and as a resultant of this my muscles developed, bigger and stronger. Over night I grew to this size, and have remained so for many years. Linda: We all know that you established the Justice Incorporated organisation with fellow superhero friend, Warrior. How exactly did you meet, and why did you decide to form a profit business with him? Blind Rage: Well, I met Warrior for the first time on a job in the Bronx. It had been a week or so since my powers emerged and throughout the previous week I had been getting used to my skills. Anyhow, on the way home I saw an old woman being mugged in a dark alley. Obviously, with my new powers my confidence grew and I went over to help. Linda: Then what happened? Blind Rage: Warrior happened. Before I could get there Warrior made it on the scene, swing his laser axes, left, right and centre. At first the mugger was scared, but then Warrior tripped over himself, trying to hit a complex karate move. [laughing to himself] it was the funniest damn thing I ever did see! Linda: [Joining in with thee laughter] Sounds like him!…then what happened. Blind Rage: Well of course I went over there and sorted the situation out. I walked over; the mugger tried to stab me; I dodged, then threw him against the wall; then I picked Warrior up from the floor. We hit it off right away and he insisted that I buy him a beer. In a drunken conversation he came up with the idea of a superhero company based on profit. Linda: What were your initial thoughts on this subject? Blind Rage: Well comin’ straight from the streets with no job and definitely no real future to speak of I went along with the idea. Linda: This seems to be a touchy subject for you. Care to elaborate? Blind Rage: Well, at first it was okay, but then when we were making the mega bucks after adequately publicising ourselves by wrestling in LOW, we had to turn down pleas of help, because we simply didn’t have the resources to help everyone. That kinda hurt and went against everything, Justice Inc stood for. Linda: I see. How do you see your immediate future, in both your wrestling career and your superhero career? Blind Rage: Well to tell you the truth, Linda, I’m quite glad to be back in the wrestling industry. I really missed the business when I was away, but I’m glad the hiatus didn’t last too long. Linda: And your superhero career? Blind Rage: Well Linda, we accept call outs the same as ever, but until we get our first callout we will be investigating the information leak. Linda: Information leak? Blind Rage: Yes, somehow the press managed to obtain information from an anonymous source, which led to the teams initial downfall. Linda: How so? Blind Rage: The press found out about Warrior’s addiction. Linda: Addiction? Blind Rage: Yes, Warrior exchanged the adrenaline rush that you can only get from being a professional wrestler, with an addiction to extra-strong mints. The press blew things out of all proportions and as a resultant the public never trusted us again. Linda: I see. Thank you for time Mr. Rage. The camera cuts to a side view of them both, as they stand from their seated position. Linda seems to spot the camera, and consequently begins to unbutton her light blue top. Blind Rage quickly catches on to her actions and firmly grabs hold of her wrists. Blind Rage: Just because the camera is on you, it doesn’t mean you have to get nekkid! Linda: But it’s what the viewers want…right? The camera nods a yes. Blind Rage: I don’t care! We are a reputable company! We don’t need ex-porn stars using their ‘assets’ to sell our services. Linda: That’s exactly what you need! Blind Rage: But… Linda: No butts, till later! The scene fades to black as Lusty Linda takes her top off and suggestively drops it onto the floor.
