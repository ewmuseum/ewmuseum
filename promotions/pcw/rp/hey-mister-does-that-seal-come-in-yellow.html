Hey mister.... does that seal come in yellow??

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: not entered [The Scene opens at a local zoo, complete with all the wildest and most dangerous animals on earth. Flaymin Aymin walks into the zoo and picks up a map detailing the locations of all the different animals. He begins wandering around the zoo, hoping that somewhere he’ll be able to find himself a pet. Flaymin strolls down to the Seal pool, he stops and decides to watch the seals and otters merrily splash around in the water.He smiles at their playful ways and wonders to himself if maybe a seal is the perfect pet for him. Suddenly, a large, African zoo keeper approaches Flaymin.He winks at Flaymin and then looks at the seals] Zoo Keeper: Lovely, aren’t they? Flaymin: Yeah, they seem to enjoy swimming around. Zoo Keeper: Yes, seals enjoy the water.They’re very easy going animals that everyone loves. [Flaymin strokes his chin and begins to ponder…. He then turns back to the zoo keeper] Flaymin: You know what?I think I’ll take that one over there! [Flaymin points at the black seal in the distance. Meanwhile the puzzled zoo keeper stares at Flaymin] Zoo Keeper: Excuse me? Flaymin: I want to buy that seal over there.Does he come in yellow, because I don’t want a black seal? [The black, African zoo keeper raises an eyebrow and sternly stares at Flaymin] Zoo Keeper: What did you say? Flaymin: No, no!I didn’t mean it like that.It’s just that I think a yellow one would more suit my lifestyle. And a black one wouldn’t like me very much because we don’t have much in common. [The annoyed Zoo Keeper looks Flaymin up and down] Zoo Keeper: Do you have some sort of problem with my race?? Flaymin: No, of course not.I’m sure you could beat me in a race. But how about we just stick to the topic? Zoo Keeper: Are you cracking jokes?Boy, you better hope you ain’t cracking jokes! [Flaymin looks at the Zoo Keeper in confusion and then points at the seal in the pool] Flaymin: Can I just…. please have one of those? Preferably in yellow? Zoo Keeper: You don’t get it, do you?!? Flaymin: I don’t get the seal? Zoo Keeper: No, idiot! Flaymin: Why not?How much do you want for him? Zoo Keeper: That seal ain’t for sale. [Flaymin shakes his head in frustration and then mumbles under his breath] Flaymin: It’s like talking to an idiot…. Zoo Keeper: What did you say? Flaymin: I said I don’t want THAT seal.I… WANT…. A…. YELLOW…. ONE! [Having heard enough, the zoo keeper reaches for his walkie talkie.He pushes the button on it and begins to speak] Zoo Keeper: Joe, it’s me.I think we’ve got a wild one here.Send the guards….. [The zoo keeper glares at Flaymin] Zoo Keeper: And few tranquillisers might come in handy. [The static voice of ‘Joe’ on the other end of the walkie-talkie begins to speak] Joe: Affirmative.They’ll be there soon. Flaymin: So are those guys coming to hunt down my yellow seal? Zoo Keeper: No, they’re coming down to hunt your yellow ass. [Flaymin looks around in bewilderment] Flaymin: I don’t get it. Zoo Keeper: Good, because you’re not getting anything. [Outraged, Flaymin begins to flail his arms in the air] Flaymin: What do you mean I’m not getting anything?!?!?I thought we had an agreement! Zoo Keeper: For the last time, we ain’t got no yellow seals.NONE.AT ALL.Matter of fact, you won’t find any yellow seals ANYWHERE!!Kapisch? [Flaymin nods his head] Flaymin: See, now would it have been so hard to say that in the first place? [The Zoo Keeper sighs and begins to walk away from Flaymin] Flaymin: Hey, I’ve got one more question. [The zoo keeper groans and then turns back to Flaymin] Flaymin: How about…. Yellow otters? Zoo Keeper: GET THE HELL OUT OF MY SIGHT, BOY!! [The zoo keeper shoves Flaymin away. Flaymin stumbles back and in the corner of his eye he notices the two security guards, armed with tranquilliser guns, quickly approaching. Thinking fast, Flaymin quickly turns around and dashes out of sight, before the guards can catch him as the scene fades to black] The search for a pet continues….. But will Flaymin ever find his perfect animal?
