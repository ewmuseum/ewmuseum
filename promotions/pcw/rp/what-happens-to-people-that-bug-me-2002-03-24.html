What happens to people that bug me

24 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3.24.02 OOC Note:Something i decided to do cuz of writers block... Snoooore..... Breath Out... Snoooore..... Breath Out... This sound goes on for minutes before Mark Estep sits up from his bed and looks around. The snoring still continues even though Mark is sitting up in his bed. He rubs his eyes and kicks his legs over the side of the bed and walks a few feet on his had wood floor. He walks into the bathroom and opens the cabinet and grabs his toothbrush and toothpaste. Snoooore..... Breath Out... Snoooore..... Breath Out... The snoring continues as he scrubs his teeth to get rid of any morning breathe, or bad taste that is left in his mouth from the night before. After about three minutes of scrubbing, he spits in the sink and then sticks his tongue out and scrubs it. He spits in the sink again and washes his toothbrush out under the running water. He opens the cabinet back up and places his toothbrush and toothpaste back where he got it. Before he closes the cabinet door, he grabs a bottle of Scope Mouthwash and pours some in his mouth. Snoooore..... Breath Out... Snoooore..... Breath Out... He gargles the mouthwash trying to drowned out the sound of the snoring. He leans over and spits back into his white, porcelain sink. He puts the bottle back into the cabinet and shuts the door and reaches down with his right hand and grabs the brass nob, and turns on the water. He leans down and drinks some of the cold water coming from the faucet. He then takes both of his hands in a cup shape, and reaches under the water and gets a scoop full and runs it down his face. Water drips off of his chin as he stands up straight and looks at himself in the mirror, as the snoring continues. Snoooore..... Breath Out... Snoooore..... Breath Out... 'Thats it', he says as he slams his hand down on the sink. He turns to his left and walks back through the door and walks out of the bedroom. He walks another couple feet before walking down the stairs and into the family room of the two story house. He leans over the couch where myself, Mark's handler, is sound asleep snoring like a chainsaw. 'Hey! Wake up!' he said, as he nudged on my shoulder. Snoooore..... Breath Out... Snoooore..... Breath Out... Snoring is all he got out of me, as i turned over and faced the other way. Mark gets angry and walks around to the other side and he nudges on me again. The snoring stops as my eyes open and i look up at my own character, living and breathing. 'Dude, you gotta get up, its time to role-play.' he says urging me to wake up. I stare at him blankly for quite some time, and with my eyes still open, the snoring starts again. 'Are...are you....are you sleeping?' says Mark, trying to figure it out. Snoooore..... Breath Out... Snoooore..... Breath Out... Sure enough, im laying there, sleeping with my eyes open. Mark scratches the back of his head, thinking of what to do next. The light above his head turns on as he points his finger into the air, meaning he has an idea. He notices the light and he turns around, and Amber is standing in the door way. 'What are you doing' she says, 'It's pretty early in the morning.' Mark replies with 'Not really, it's actually around ten-thirty.' Amber raises her face up and nods. She asks, 'So what are you doing? And who's that man on our couch?' Mark looks at her, as if she was crazy, 'That man on the couch is the guy thats making you say all of these things, thats your's and my handler, and I'm trying to wake him up. 'Oh, well i have an idea' says Amber, as she turns her back and walks towards the kitchen. She walks across about 25 black and white tiles on the floor before reaching the sink. She reaches up over top of the sink, and opens the brown wooden cabinet, with 4 pieces of glass in it. He grabs a tall glass and shuts the cabinet. She turns on the hot water, full blast, and fills the glass up with it. Steam rolls off of the water as the cold air hits the hot water. She walks back into the living room and over to the couch, where she pours the water on my face. 'Ahhhh!! My Eyes!!' i yell, as i sit up quickly from the couch. I fall over onto the floor and roll around on the ground. 'Dude, calm down', says Amber as i roll around. She kneels down and takes my hands from my eyes. My eyes are pressed closed very tightly, and after a few seconds i open my right eye. I realize that it doesn't hurt any more and i open both of them. I sit up from my laid back position and Mark helps me to my feet. 'Who's stupid idea was that?' i said. 'Mine' Mark replies, 'You want to make something of it?' I looked at him as if he was crazy. He doesn't know who he's talking to. I'm the man behind the hardcore icon that is Mark Estep. Everything he knows, everything he does, is all because i make him. His intellegence only goes as far as i let it, and he doesn't realize that. 'Shat aap!!' i said, 'Now, what do you want!?' 'All I ask is that you role-play.' 'For what?' i asked. He replied, 'I have a match against Shawn Mosely and you only have one role-play out for the match so far.' 'Well, he hasn't role-played yet, why do i have to be out there with two role-plays to his none?' i asked, and he answers with, 'Because i do actually want to win a match for once. I mean, i've had like ten matches and haven't won one yet.' I looked at him and answered, 'I know, but thats not my fault. After six years of doing this, you kind of start to run out of ideas.' 'Yeah, well we are lucky that PCW hasn't fired me yet because of your piss poor workrate.' he argued. I walked over to my computer desk and sat down in my seat and open up my lap top. 'Alright, you want me to role-play? I'll role-play!!' I place my hands on the keyboard and start typing in letters to the computer. Off in the background, Mark starts punching himself in the stomach. 'Haha, you have to do whatever i type in this here little box.' i said while chuckling at the now battered and bruised Mark Estep. 'Wait, i have a better idea.' i said. I lean over my laptop and start to type something else. While im typing, Amber walks over to me and starts to dance right in front of me. She takes her shirt off to show a black bra and she continues to dance. She starts to unbutton her pants when Mark runs over to interveen. 'Whoa whoa whoa, hold on there killer', i said as Mark was running towards me. I turn back to the computer and keep hitting backspace as time seems to go in reverse. Amber is now standing back over by Mark who doesn't seem to know what just happened. A slight smirk comes across my face as i turn back to the computer for the last time and Mark begins to walk outside. He opens the door and stands on the front steps, as he shuts the door behind him. 'What's he doin' out there?' Amber asks, as i point to the window. She walks over to the window to see a naked Mark Estep running across the neighbors yards as the scene begins to fade out. Before it goes to complete black, i mumble 'I bet he will never bug me to do another role-play in HIS lifetime...'
