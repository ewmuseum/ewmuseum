The Chat King Wrestling Show (Special Guest: Tim Walden)

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8 Feb &#039;03 Onto the screen comes a shot of a studio audience cheering before we pan over to a stage where there are two comfortable looking chairs, sitting on one of the chairs is Chet King ‘The Chat King’. The sound of a voice over mans voice comes onto the screen as the shot once again pans over the studio audience. Voice Over Man: Welcome to another episode of The Chat King Wrestling Show with the host of the show, Chet King ‘The Chat King’! Chet stands up and starts to talk as the audience cheers. Chet King: Hello all, and thanks for tuning into another episode of The Chat King Wrestling Show. Our guest for tonight’s show is a veteran in PCW, and currently has high ranking position with the Front Office. Whether you like him or not, and personally I don’t, you’ve got to admit that he is a talented wrestler. And although I’m not a fan of his, I’m still excited to have him on the show. He is a former TV and Cruiserweight Champion. He is Tim Walden! ‘Right Now’ by SR-71 plays as the studio audience starts booing. Tim Walden then walk out from the back into the studio with a grin on his face. He walks over to Chet King, who offers to shake Walden’s hand, but Walden just ignores Chet and takes a seat on the second chair. Chet then sits down as the audience continue to boo Tim Walden. Chet King: Welcome to the show Tim. I take it you didn’t want to shake my hand? Tim Walden: (Sarcastically) You’re a smart guy Chet, how’d you figure that one out? Chet rolls his eyes. Tim Walden: You’re not worthy enough to have your hand shaken by mine, Chet. Maybe if you had managed to put up a fight against me in our match a couple weeks back I might think more highly of you, but you proved that if you ever were skilled at wrestling, all that skill has disappeared now. Chet King: (Sighing) I knew from the moment you agreed to appear on this show that you were going to bring up your victory over me, but you’re forgetting how you won. Tim Walden: Oh no I’m not. I remember what happened. I pinned you 1-2-3! Chet King: After interference from the Front Office. Tim Walden: Quit your crying Chet, take your loss like a man. Chet King: Don’t worry, I’ve taken my loss like a man. I just hope you’re taking your loss to Tessa like a man. Walden doesn’t seem to be too happy about Chet bringing up his loss in the March Madness qualifying tournament. Tim Walden: Tessa only won because Teresa interfered. I should of won! I should be going to the world title battle royal at March Madness! Chet King: Take it like a man, Tim. Tim Walden: I’ll take it, but you know and so do all these people, that I got screwed. If it wasn’t for that…bitch…Teresa, I would be going on to March Madness to win the world title. Chet King: Speaking of Teresa, do you have any plans on facing her in a match in the near future? Tim Walden: I’m ready to face her any time, any place. She may be able to attack me from behind while I’m in a bathroom or while I’m in a match, but she won’t be able to beat me when we’re fighting one on one. Chet King: I’ve heard rumors that there will be a conference between the two of you at March Madness, and truth behind that? Because that’s a match I’d like to see, specifically the part when Teresa beats you. Tim Walden: We’ll just have to wait and see, but if there is then she will suffer the same fate you did Chet. Chet King: Like I said before, you beat me only due to the Front Office. But I’m not mad, because I get my chance for revenge on the Front Office on Tuesday in my match with Gaz. Tim Walden: You think you have a chance? Gaz will slaughter you. Chet King: I don’t think so Tim. I’m going to be at the very top of my game. Tim Walden: That’s not saying much. Chet King: It’s saying more then someone who loses to referee Lou Micelli when they’re at the top of their game. Walden’s face goes red with embarrassment after the mentioning of his infamous loss to the referee. Tim Walden: You want to bring up embarrassing losses, what about our ten second loss to some guy who’s name I can’t even remember now because he was so poor. Now it’s Chet’s turn to get embarrassed. Chet King: What about the times when you used to be Mexi’s lackey? Tim Walden: How about when your studio audience used to chant that horribly cheesy chant ‘Chat King, Yeah, Yeah, Yeah!’ and you used it for your theme music as well! This causes the studio audience to start to chant the classic lines ‘Chat King, Yeah, Yeah, Yeah!’ Chet then covers his ears up and shudders at the cheesiness of the chant. Tim Walden: Haha, that’s the stupidest chant I’ve ever heard. You should be ashamed for having something so bad chanted in support of you. Chet King: I am. But how about we update the chant a little bit, why don’t we make it ‘Tim Walden, Sucks, Sucks, Sucks! Tim Walden, Sucks, Sucks, Sucks! Tim Walden, Sucks, Sucks, Sucks!’ The studio audience then launches into the new, but still as cheesy as ever, rendition of the chant. Tim Walden: You can try to get these sheep you call a studio audience to chant what you want, but everyone still remembers the original. Just like everyone remembers how I beat you. Chet ignores Tim’s last comment. Chet King: How about we discuss how I took money from you and the Front Office while I was out on my extended injury leave. That was pretty funny, wasn’t it? Tim Walden: That wasn’t funny at all, but what was funny was when I taught you a lesson about being out on extended injury leave when I beat you. Chet rolls his eyes again. Chet King: You can bring it up as much as you want, but I’m going to get the last laugh over the Front Office when I defeat Gaz this Tuesday. Tim Walden: You don’t have a chance Chet. Chet King: Just watch on Turmoil, you’ll see me moving onto the world title battle royal. You can even try to interfere if you want, but I’ll be ready for you this time. Tim Walden: I won’t have a need to interfere Chet, Gaz won’t have any problem with you. Chet King: I think what you meant to say was that you won’t be able to interfere as you’ll be getting smacked around by Teresa, while I’m defeating Gaz. Tim Walden: Heh, if Teresa tries to start me again, she’ll be on the receiving end of a beating this time around. Chet King: Well that’s all the time we have on tonight’s show. Tim Walden: But remember to tune in next time, and watch PCW programming this week. Chet King: Hey, that’s my line! You’re reading my line off the auto queue! Tim Walden: I can read your lines if I want, after all I did beat you. Goodnight everyone! The credits roll as Tim Walden and Chet King have a few insulting words between each other while the crowd chants the new rendition of the cheesy chant; ‘Tim Walden, Sucks, Sucks, Sucks!’
