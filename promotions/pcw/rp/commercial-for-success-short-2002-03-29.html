Commercial for success (short)

29 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Title: Commercial for success (short) Featuring: Politically Incorrect Date: March 29th, 2002 Gametime. This saying means many different things, in many different languages. But in the end, it means one thing to all. Get ready to GO! In each of their respective rooms, Fear Factory, the Cowabunga Connection, Metallyx, Misplaced, and Golden Game, the tag champions sit, getting geared up and psyched up for running the gauntlet. The champions have the definite advantage of being the last men out, but the others, mostly NTICW graduates....are prepared to go all out to become champions. Can they do it? We shall see......we soon shall see.. ::Politically Incorrect sit in their dressing room, two bottles of champagne sitting on a table, in a bucket of ice, with some glasses nearby, presumeably to celebrate a later victory. The room is decked out in red, white, and blue, like a campaign celebration. The words 'POLITICALLY INCORRECT' appear above these, as Politically Incorrect relax, watching the television.:: William S. Powell: An hour or two more....that long until our destiny. Reginald V. Lovecraft: That is correct..we've been here for a few months. It is time to start a new era in tag team wrestling here in Primetime Championship Wrestling. A new team will now step to the forefront. And that team just happens to be.....Politically Incorrect. ::Suddenly, Powell's cell phone rings madly. Grabbing the phone from his belt, Powell answers:: William S. Powell: Councilman Powell. ::It is Roger Schultz on the other end of the phone:: Roger Schultz: Hello, Mr. Powell. Your commercial is ready. It is set to be aired on Channel 7 in Sacramento. It will be on, oh.....now. William S. Powell: YAY!! ::He hangs up:: William S. Powell: Our commercial is on! Lovecraft, turn to channel 7! ::Lovecraft turns to channel 7.....just in time to see the letters 'PCW' appear on the screen:: PCW. Tonight...in all liklihood, PCW will crown new tag team champions. Do you want nice tag team champions? ::A picture of politicians shaking hands scrolls on screen.:: Do you want tag team champions you can look up to? If not, Politically Incorrect is the team for you! ::'..And Justice For All', the Politically Incorrect theme, strikes, as a montage of clips from the careers of Lovecraft and Powell begin playing.:: They don't show favortism to members of the opposite sex... ::A clip plays of Politically Incorrect assaulting current OSW superstar Amy 'Aimz' Campbell in the NTICW Playboy Mansion match:: They aren't the most uncorruptable people in the world... ::The fateful clip establishing a Church of Scientology at the expense of a children's playground and old folks home is shown.:: But they can fly really high... ::A clip from the Politically Incorrect debut match plays, showing Powell striking Bobby Stormbringer with his trademark 450 moonsault, Election Day.:: And they can beat people up fast! ::The entire Misplaced-PI match plays, showing Politically Incorrect systematically destroying all opposition.:: And so, support Politically Incorrect!
