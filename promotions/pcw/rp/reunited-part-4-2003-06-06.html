Reunited Part 4

6 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6/6/03 As raindrops poured from the gray painted clouds up above it only caused more worries to stir up inside my mind. I had to get to Evan and save him but surely it was too late. Keeping my foot glued to the gas petal I continued my way down the empty country road as fast as I could hoping that out of nowhere another car would come and push me off course and into a land of death which I had just escaped from. “Fuck!” I screamed out as I pulled into the parking lot of the store where I had left Evan with Allison. They were nowhere in sight. But where could they have gone? After asking that question to myself I found the answer in the corner of my eyes off to the side. “Oh my god…” I looked over towards the woods as I stepped out of the car and realized that if Alison had the same plans for Evan that Ein had for me then his chances of survival were slim. But what about her own kids? Were they destined for the same fate? I wasn’t going to hang around much longer to find out, I had to find them. “Evannnnn, Alexxxxx, Christaaaaa.” I cried out as the rain continued to pour from the sky, drenching me and the matching pink tank top and blue denim shorts I was wearing. As I trudged through the branches and dead leaves covering the ground I tried m best to block out the sound of the impacting rain falling all around me and the sticks and leaves crumbling underneath my weight. Suddenly the slight squeal of a little girl caught my ears. It had to have been Christa but the only problem was that I could not distinguish which direction the squeal was coming from. “Christa, where are you guys?” I cried out, hoping to get a response. Instead all I got was yet another squeal from an unknown direction so I decided the best thing to do would be to continue even further into the woods and hope that I would come across them somewhere on my journey. “Please let them be okay.” I whispered to myself and hoped that someone was listening and would grant my wish. Fortunately enough it must have been a lucky day because almost right on cue I spotted something moving around in the bushes ahead of me. Behind it I could see a darker hair girl and realized that I had found just who I was looking for. Instead of shouting out to them to let them know of my presence, I crept silently across the ground waiting to make my move. “Mom, stop!” Cried little Christa into the cold night air which stirred alarm inside of me. Was she bringing harm towards Evan? She better not have been or I’d hurt her more than I had been planning to hurt Gaz on Tuesday once I finally had the chance to right all the wrongs he had put in my life. “Hush Christa or you’ll be next.” Allison hissed at the girl. Immediately I began to sprint towards the scene of the crime and gulped hoping I wasn’t too late. “Tessa!” Screamed out the voice of four year old Evan, assuring me that he was still alive as I emerged onto the scene. But soon I realized that it was not his life in danger, it was another child’s, my nephew, ten year old Alex. There he stood with Alison’s arms around his neck so that he could not breathe and a gun pressed up against his blonde head. “You fucking bitch, don’t you dare!” I screamed out towards Alison so wrapped up in anger that I had forgotten that there were children’s ears all around me picking up on these cusses. “Oh what’s that Tessa, you want to play Truth or Dare? In that case I pick dare. I dare you to stay quiet or my son here will breathe his last breath right here in front of you and both of these kids!” She hissed as her eyes glowed with hatred in my direction. But why kill her own child because of her hatred towards me? I didn’t know and I wasn’t about to ask and find out since it was a child’s life on the line at the expense of my words. “Mom please don’t!” Pleaded Christa as tears ran down her rosy red cheeks, quite obviously frightened with the events unfolding before her young blue eyes. “SHUT UP NOW!” Screamed her mother like a mad woman as she moved her finger towards the trigger and closer to the death of her only son. “In fact I can’t take this anymore, you had the chance to save him Tessa but you were trying to take Ein away from me just like you trying to steal my kids away from me. Well it’s over now Tessa and I win!” “No don’t!” I screamed not realizing that I was only about to make matters worse by disobeying the command given to me. BANG! “Nooooooo!!!!” I screamed out in tears as if it were my own life being taken but before I could even begin to think of what to do there was yet another gun shot heard and I turned around to see that the assailant had taken her own life now. “Please god no!” I screamed as I rushed over to the bloodied body of the ten year old child I had watched grown up before my very eyes. This couldn’t be happening. First my only brother Brandon, then Evan’s mother, and now an innocent child. Why must death follow me like an infant follows its mother’s voice? I fell down beside the child and cradled him in my arms. “Somebody help please!” I screamed into the nighttime sky hoping that someone would be there to help, which there was. The gunshot must have triggered an alarm inside someone besides me because within a moment I found myself surrounded by a flock of cops who eyes rested on me. “Get some help, please!” I pleaded with them but they knew that even the best of help would do little good. The child was going to die and there was nothing I could do about it. “Mam I am sorry but there is nothing we can do.” One of the cops, a rather chubby man with no hair answered in a sorrowful tone knowing very well that even if an ambulance was right here at this very moment. “The only thing I can suggest for you to do right now is for you to take these kids and get the hell out of here before her partner in crime gets a hold of you. I suggest that you get out of this state if possible.” The man gave his best advice possible, obviously wanted to avoid a repeat of the situation which just occurred right there inside the woods. “Partner in crime? You mean these two children were placed in harms way while you all knew these two were involved in crime?” I snapped, enraged with their words. I couldn’t believe that a child’s life could have been sparred provided the right research was done. But this was no time to argue and I wanted to keep Christa and Evan out of harms way before something happened to them as well so I grabbed them by the arm and began to rush towards my car away from the crime scene and then out of the state. I hated to listen to the advice of the cops who put the children in this position in the first place by not doing their research when handing custody over to Allison, but it for the better of the two children. But where would I go I had no clue. All I had to do is hope that somewhere out in this messed up world there was a place where I could finally be happy and get away from all the troubles of the world.
