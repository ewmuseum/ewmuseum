Through The Eyes [4] Passing The Torch

19 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 19.12.02 Date: September 1st, 1977Place: New York, New York (Madison Square Garden)Time: 11:00 PM (EST) With the heat inside of the garden already brewing, wrestling fans had been waiting for this moment since the end of the Ali/Fraizer fight when Ali avenged his earlier lost to Fraizer in 1971. Paul Hanson came out towards the referee, checking his equipment to receive instructions over the ear piece as usual custom and he sat next to the timekepper, alongside the usual commentators Mike Jones and Dennis Branch. Here it was, a semi packed house at the World's Most Famous Arena, Madison Square Garden and Clash of The Titans, the main show in NEW History, and Clive Owens was still in the back, stretching out for what promises to be the defining moment of his career. 'You think this plan is going to work?' Owens shot up from inside his locker, busy gearing up. Over on the side of him, close to doing the exact same is Eric Jackson. The two men are set to do battle tonight over the coveted NEW World Heavyweight championship, currently held by Owens. A smug look on his face as he turns to face Jackson. 'Obviously, Hanson is out of his mind thinking that you with the belt going to make sure the company blossoms.' 'Clive, I've been listening to you talk about the 'plan' for weeks.' Jackson cooly emphasizes on the 'plan'. 'Listen, its not really much of a choice now is it? Its either do it, or find another job.' Ownes nodded, 'Exactly. But, I don't want my heat to get crafted away by helping some lowbrow newcomer get his boots in. I'm too proud for that. And besides, I like the title - not the man behind it.' 'You don't like yourself?' Jackson asked grimly. 'Hanson,' Owens cut back to the topic at hand. 'Paul fucking Hanson is the man behind the NEW World Heavyweight title. Its never me, just him pulling the strings controlling things his way, his mold. 'C'mon! Hanson? That's believeable.' 'Don't believe it then, don't think Hanson's only got you picked for champion because he made a mistake by giving Chambers the US title. He wants you for the title, you've been picked since the moment you came here. He's wanted you and only you to carry him into a national success.' Jackson sat back down, 'You're shitting me, he's not going to let a black man get higher billing than the rest of them.' 'You'd be surprised.' Owens smirked. 'No race barrier, not a real one anyway.' 'Great, so you're falling after, or before the chairshot?' 'After.' 'Okay, you swing, I duck and hit the 'Flashback' for the three. Still, feels uneasy doing it.' Owens gave Jackson a nod. 'There ya go.' Jackson nodded to the champion, a man he wanted to eclipse by personal gain, but admire in longevity and skill. When you've been in NEW as long as Clive Owens has, you tend to get a bit cranky, albeit agitated from your friends. They are your closest advisors, or your Judas', the bleeding heart stuck in the fold. Luckily, Eric didn't have to think of Clive Owens as that, or did he? 'Clive,' Eric whispered in a baratone voice. Owens shook his head in Jackson's direction, showing that he indeed heeded the call before. 'showtime.' Clive laughed, before 'Perfect Strangers' by Deep Purple cued up on the sound system. Owens walked over past Jackson, who eyed the NEW World Heavyweight title with a sparkle in his eye, before Owens snatched it out of his sight, picking it up and latching it around his waist. 'Don't worry, it'll be yours in a little while.' Clive snickered at Jackson, who nodded back. 'Mine in a little while.' Owens walked out of the lockerroom, the NEW World Heavyweight champion. Eric cracked his neck, a precaution if he had landed wrong on it during a blown spot, or stiff shot from Owens. Tonight, he was in the Garden - wrestling the for the most coveted prize in all of Buffalo. This was better than a Bills/Giants Superbowl, even more astounding that the Yankees going back to the Fall Classic. This was Eric Jackson's night. One he'd watch go by in a blaze of glory. Date: September 1st, 1977Place: New York, New York (Madison Square Garden)Time: 11:25 PM (EST) Back and forth they went, eying each other after a grueling contest had led them this far, nearly twenty minutes after their respective entrances, Jackson and Owens leaned back against the ropes, each one struggling to get a clear distance of where their opponent happened to be. 'Jackson's out of it, Owens clearly has this in the bag!' Branch shouted through his microphone, easily making himself as the heel or bad guy commentator. In all of the moments at MSG, not a single fan could wait for what was going to happen next. Owens charges, blasting Jackson back down to the mat with a thunderous clothsline. Owens hissed at Eric, 'Get your ass up!' before doing it himself, picking the former United States champion by the head. Hard irish whip into the ropes, before Owens locked in his deadly 'Cobra Clutch.' The fans were booing, almost at a crossroad between whether to cheer Owens or not. Suddenly, Jackson humbles over, latching onto Owens in a White Russian Leg Sweep, bringing him down with all his might. GRRRRAAH! The ropes snap back and forth like a cut powerline, the ring trembled with every blow as Owens rolled on the mat, clutching his head in pain. 'He scouted it!' Jones smiled at the Leg Sweep. 'Cover him Eric, cover him!' Eric dragged his body over, throwing a shoulder onto Owens as the referee jumped into the fray. 'One, two, shoulder up!' cried the ref as Clive threw a shoulder up in protest. Eric released his body weight off of Clive, before booting him in the gut. Underhooking the arms, he butterfiles out of it before slamming Owens back on the mat with a Powerbomb. 'One!' the fans were in joyous ovation. 'Two!' 'Thr - NO KICKOUT!' The crowd groaned, obviously their wait for a Jackson victory would have to come later. The eyes of the champion glazed over, as he shook his head from the cobwebs and got up. Jackson turns around, only to get caught with a neckbreaker for his troubles. Owens snorts spit, as he goes towards the outside, pushing the owner out of his seat. 'Owens, don't do something stupid.' Jones pleaded with him, but Owens couldn't hear him. All he could hear was the elaborate breathing of Eric Jackson as he sat in the ring, waiting for Owens to clobber him with a steel chair. Owens dove under the ropes, quietly mosing past the referee, before launching the chair in the air. Jackson ducks, spins Owens around and drops him for maybe the loudest and most effective Flashback of his career. 'There it is, hook of the leg! One!' Jones screamed. 'NOOOO!' Branch cried. 'TWO!' the fans got up. 'THREE!' New champ. A smile came upon Jackson's face, as 'Ironman' by Black Sabbath struck the chords of the Garden, tens upon thousands of fans jumping up and down, screaming their heads off as if O.J. Simpson had rushed his way for a Bills Superbowl. The NEW World Heavyweight title came unbuckled from the hands of Paul Hanson and were handed to its new owner. Eric Jackson. Yet, nearly twenty five years later, he'd never think his son would be in the exact same position.
