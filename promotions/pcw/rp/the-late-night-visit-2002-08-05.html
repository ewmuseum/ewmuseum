The Late Night Visit

5 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Aug. 5, 2002 (The scene opens at Arlene Johnson’s (Jaden’s Grandmother) home in Baltimore, Maryland. The front of her house is all dark and gloomy. It appears to be, from the way the full moon hits her house just right. It is a brick house with weeds embracing its sides. An uncut bush in her front yard and gassing growing all around that hasn’t been cut for months. To Arlene, it is home but to Jaden, it is a reincarnation of hell.) Jaden slowly creeps up on the house waiting for any surprise it might have, although her facial expression does not show she is afraid but she expresses it unknowingly. “You need to get you grass cut. This is ridiculous!” She says as she takes another step. “Why do I have to live in this hell hole? A pretty girl like me does not need to be seen living in this garbage.” *Jaden’s Thoughts* ~If I see something jump, I am out of this bitch!~All these bugs and shit.~Hell where am I? The damn jungle and shit! “Come on girl. Get in this here house before the bugs start to fly in!” She says closing the screen door behind herself as she enters her home, for 25 years. *Jaden’s Thoughts* ~No she did not!~The old hag does not even have a screen in the screen door, talking bout “Get in this here house before the bugs start to fly in!”~She better go head with that “faggotedy-bullshit!” Jaden walks on the porch, dropping her bags, taking a breath and begins throwing her bags threw the opening in the door, one by one. *Jaden’s Thoughts* ~Oh she better not say anything to me tonight!~She knows I don’t like her stank ass!~All she asking for is trouble!~I do not want to have to beat this bitch down again! Jaden finally has all her bags inside. She leaves them situated next to the steps because she knows she will not be staying to long. She can anticipate her grandmother kicking her out the house once more. She hops on the couch grabs the remote and turns on the television. She lies back, letting her headrest upon the arm of the chair and legs dangling at the other end. “Ah Shit, no cable!” She says frustrated throwing the remote on the ground watching the batteries pop out. “I got to get the fuck out of here! I am missing the Real World!” “You will if you keep cussing in my house! And you ass will sho’ as hell be in the real world!” Just then, the telephone rings. “PHONE!” Jaden screams at the top of her lungs not making an effort of getting up to get it. She really does not have to get up to get it. The telephone is at arms distance from her. ~*RING!*~*RING!*~RING!*~ The telephone on the little table flutters as it continues to ring! “Shit Jaden get the damn phone!” “Oh, I didn’t know you wanted me to. This is not my house. I live with John remember.” Jaden reaches for the telephone chuckling. “Hello, who this be? Hold on!” She drops the receiver on the ground and walks in the kitchen. “Hey lady, pour me something to drink since your washing the dishes. Be a good sport! Never mind, I don’t trust you. You might try to poison me.” Her grandmother snares at her. “Jaden who was on the phone?” “They still are on the phone. I told them to hold on,” she says snapping back, reaching into the refrigerator pouring her some Orange juice. “Its some white lady!” “How come you didn’t tell me the phone?” “The Phone,” Jaden says, being a smart-ass. “And who the hell do I look like Florence from off the Jefferson’s. You got the wrong one baby!” “Jaden, don’t start with me,” She says turning the faucet off and drying her hands on her apron. “I am not the one to be messed with, not to night. I am to tired to be messing with somebody’s bad-ass child!” Jaden looks around, “I don’t see your mother nowhere!” She then leans back and gulps her orange juice down again. “Hello, I am sorry. My granddaughter didn’t tell me the phone.” “Hello Ms. Johnson, yes this is Mrs. Verman-beletolantic! You called my office earlier to report that you have your granddaughter staying with you again and that you would like …like …to to…to to …take…take…” What the women did not know was that Jaden was listening in on their conversation. “…full custody of your granddaughter, Rica Ebony Jaden Lopez.” Is that right?” “Why yes it is.” “God forbid,” she mumbles. “What was that?” “Nothing!” “Oh, ok. Well all I need you to do is come down to our area office and fill out the paper work we have for you. Is that ok with you ma’am. Or would you like me to bring the paper work to you?” “Yea that would be great if you would bring me the work!” ~*CLICK*~ (Jaden hangs the phone up! Her heart sinks and dies. “Me living with my grandmother. Hell no, it ain’t gonna happen. I got to leave this hell filled place of demons!”) “Hello,” Mrs. Verman-beletolantic says. “I thought you hung up!” She says. “Yes I am here!” “Ok, well I will drop them off to you tomorrow afternoon. Is that ok?” “Yes its perfect!” By this time, Jaden has slipped her way out the backdoor, after grabbing her grabbing her grandmother’s keys off the hook. “The hell if I stay here. I am moving back in with my baby. He still loves me!” Jaden has been gone for a good thirty minutes now but her grandmother does not pay it any mind. She is glad that she can sit and enjoy the quiet. {{{{{{{~*In the car as she thinks to herself*~}}}}}}} “Lipstick, check!” She says applying her finally blood red coat.“Ok, now what will I say when he sees him. What will he say,” she begins to laugh. “I will just wing it like I always do,” she says pulling into the motels Parking lot. Jaden looks around nervously for John’s car. “Oh good, he isn’t here. I have time to fix myself up! He is probably at a bar somewhere drowning his sorrows thinking of me over a cold glass of beer. I know he misses me.” She fills her head with untold lies and she uses her key to the motel room and opens the door. “What the hell! He must have had some kind of party last night! Clothes everywhere, oh yes, he still needs me to look after her.” Jaden begins to shout, “I am back baby, I am back!,” at the top out her lungs. She gathers the clothes in one big pile and takes them into the bedroom laying them on the bed neatly. She sits down on the bed lays back and relaxes, “Ah, this feels great, except for whatever this is that is poking me in my ass,” she frowns moving her hand to find out what it is. She begins to feel it before looking at it his. She draws a mental picture of it. (Round! Ruff! Pointy at one end! Loop shape!) “OH HELL! NO HE DIDN’T! NO THE FUCK HE DIDN’T!” Jaden takes her right hand and checks to see if she is missing an earring. “No, mine are still there. Next, she holds it up into the light. “Oh, I would not wear anything this cheap or disgusting. No wonder why he was rushing me out. He wanted to have a freak party. It’s a fool moon to! I might not be a freak but I am sure is hell going to have a party! Jaden walks over to the double door closet in the bedroom and scavenges around for something, a wooden bat! “Let’s play ball, HARD BALL!” Jaden is out raged. She begins to twirl the bat in her right hand, letting it fly down and cracking John’s glass table. “I will teach this bitch to mess with me!” Jaden gets into a batters position and turn on the television. The baseball game happens to be on. “And he looks and the pitch is made,” the announcer says. SMASH!!!!! Sparks begin to fly from the television. The bat is stuck inside the television. Jaden retrieves the bat and gets back to her demolition work. “Next stop living room!” Jaden looks around the living room for a certain prized possession. “Where is it. Where is it,” she calls out. Jaden pick up this vase like container with beautiful flowers painted on it. “Oh here she is! Hi Aeryn! I have come to pay my respects.” The vase is not a vase; it’s an urn’ Aeryn’s urn. Aeryn was cremated when she died. Jaden lifts the bat once more. She swings the bat downward in a fast motion with no hesitation; however, she come to a stop. “I can not do this. That would be real rude of me. The girl is already dead.” Jaden then looks over to John’s stash of assorted coffee blends. Jaden grabs the urn and begins to pour the ashes into the coffee. She stirs it around so he will not notice the different coloration. She sits the urn back down and goes at it CRASH!!!!!! SMASH!!!!! BANG!!!!!! Jaden drops the bat, dusts herself clean, and heads for the door. “Wait, I got to do one last thing for the team.” Jaden closes the door and retrieves her red lipstick out of her purse. She pops the cap and applies some on her lips before writing on the door. “John, I was here today and I happened to find another woman’s earring. I thought you loved me. You do love me right. I do not want to do anything crazy and you know, I will be back. XOXOXO Love Always,Jaden.” She ends her little note to John with kisses all on his door with the red lipstick dripping on his fluffy white carpet, closing the door behind her once more.
