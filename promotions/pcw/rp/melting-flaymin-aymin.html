Melting Flaymin Aymin

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Sometime before you read it. “So Mr. Aymin, nothing to say before your execution? No half-witty joke? No tired barb as dull as your wits?” (Aymin, strapped into a chair with what seems to be miles upon miles of duct tape all over his body and mouth, refrains from responding.) “Very well. Then I will see you in hell.” (A dull boom as the massive door of the airlock slams closed. We see THE Multiple Man chuckling to himself as he dogs the hatch securely.) Tmm: “Mwahahahaha!” (Turning his attentions to an insanely over-complicated bank of controls he begins pushing buttons, spinning knobs, pulling levers and all that other stuff you do to activate a death trap. Resident Entourage techie Val Dietrich wanders up, munching on an apple.) Tmm: “You’re just in time to witness the demise of PCW loser, Flaymin Aymin within the confines of my diabolical melting machine!” Val: “Melting machine?” Tmm: “For which to melt with!” Val: “So it melts things?” Tmm: “That’s the general idea, yeah.” Val: “So what’s to keep the chamber itself from melting as well?” Tmm: “Judicious applications of SCIENCE.” Val: “Science, eh? I suppose that might work.” Tmm: “Assume it does. What do you think of it?” (Val peers through the thickly shielded glass into the chamber. Inside the perfectly spherical room, the walls gleam an almost blindingly bright whitish-chrome all along its fifty-foot diameter. Here and there embedded in the curved surface are mysterious tubes and bumps of unknown but decidedly malevolent design.) Val: “Can’t see how I never noticed this before, one would think it was too big for the watertower.” Tmm: “It is. That’s why I’ve constructed it just off of the subterranean parking lot, near the underground moose pen.” (Val thoughtfully chews on the apple.) Val: “Where’d we get the money for it? Looks expensive and Healey’s been slashing salaries like he’s the Freddy Krueger of Accountants.” Tmm: “Correct. It is expensive, incredibly so. But I have my ways…” (Cut to the front door of PCW offices. Tmm is walking out wearing a longcoat that bulges like a small carnival tent, a trail of staplers, paperclips and pens all emblazoned with the PCW logo marks his path.) Security Guard: “G’night.” Tmm: (Smiling broadly) “See ya tomorrow!” (Cut back to the exterior of THE Melting Machine. [Huh, that’s a happy coincidence of naming, isn’t it?]) Tmm: “… and they suffice in their own small way.” Val: “Okay… explain to me why I’m in a tunnel connecting to an underground parking lot eating an apple.” Tmm: “Hmm… since it’s near the Rare Albino Underground Moose pen, you’re here to see Pookie.” Val: “I’ll buy that. What about the apple?” Tmm: “It’s just a prop used to break up blocks of text.” (Crunch, Val takes another bite.) Val: (chewing) “Um-hmm.” Tmm: “Now make an observation about Flaymin Aymin’s grisly demise currently happening.” (Val swallows the apple chunk.) Val: (dutifully) “So… melting Flaymin Aymin, eh?” (Tmm nods approvingly.) Tmm: “I sure am!” Val: “Doesn’t look like him. Looks like an action figure.” Tmm: “We do what we can in these troubled times.” Val: “Melting action figures?” Tmm: “What else would you use a melting chamber for?” Val: “I should ask you.” Tmm: “That, and melting butter for popcorn. Mostly it’s for the butter, though.” Val: “Indeed.” Tmm: “You wouldn’t believe how hard it was finding a Flaymin Aymin action figure…” Val: “You check Ebay?” Tmm: “The best I could manage was a wad of used chewing gum. Turns out that only one of the little suckers is still around.” Val: “Yeah?” Tmm: “Yah, Aymin has it.” Val: (scornfully) “He’s not good enough to be able to afford one!” Tmm: “True, his Grandmother gave it to him on the condition that he never again come to visit, or so the rumor goes.” Val: “So what did you do?” Tmm: “I offered to buy it. Can you believe he wanted five whole dollars for that thing?!” Val: “So…?” Tmm: “So I did the next best thing, I sanded the face off an old G.I. Joe and made do.” Val: “Nice crayon drawing of the face, regardless.” Tmm: “I’m very proud of it.” (The interior of the melting chamber begins to flash and pulse as the camera fades out.)
