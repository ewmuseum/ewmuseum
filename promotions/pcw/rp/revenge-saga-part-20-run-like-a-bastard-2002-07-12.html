Revenge Saga Part 20: Run Like a Bastard...

12 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12/7/02 Revenge Saga Part 20: Run like a Bastard… From Last Time… He never got the chance. Just as the thought entered his head the door of his motel burst open, in spectacular fashion. Crichton stood and backed away from the door the best he could, expecting some really big guy, or yet another bastard robot, but it was none of those things. There before him, stood a man wearing a bubble-gum pink spandex suit, with a Hand Bag in his left hand, and the letters HBW eloquently sewn into the front of his suit. Crichton ran his hand over his face. Crichton: I really don’t believe this! Guy: Believe it villain, for on this day, you will surely fall at the hands of THE HAND BAG WARRIOR! Crichton: The what now? HBW: The Handbag Warrior! Crichton: Your gay, aren’t you? HBW: I am, but I can kick your ass nonetheless! Crichton: With what? Your Handbag o’ Doom? HBW: I see your familiar with it’s power, villain! Crchton: [Holding his forehead in his hands] This is going to be one of those days! TO BE CONTINUED… Crichton couldn’t believe both his bad luck and the person standing before him. He looked like an extra from the village people…an extra ass to be precise. Crichton wouldn’t have laughed at his face, but he feared it would be politically incorrect for him to do so (no pun intended). After all he did manage to break the door down…not many of the other wannabe superhero’s could do that. Crichton: So tell me, Mr. Warrior…how the hell did you find me? HBW: I didn’t find you… Crichton: No? HBW: No…the power of the Handbag o’ Doom brought me to your location. Crichton: Really? [In an incredibly sarcastic tone] HBW: Indeed. It will also be the instrument with which I will kick your ass. Crichton: Really? [With an even more sarcastic tone] HBW: You sound sceptical mon frere! Don’t be! For the powers of my Handbag O’ Doom are very real indeed, as I will no doubt show you! Crichton: No doubt. HBW: I have no desire to hurt you… Crichton: [Backing up against the wall] …it what way do you mean ‘hurt me’? HBW: [Sighing and tutting almost simultaneously] Not that way! Jesus H. Christ I knew you were some kind of Villain, but I didn’t expect you to be a homophobic! Crichton: I’m not homophobic, but God created Adam and Eve…not Adam and Steve! Besides people can do whatever they like in their own homes [counting the things off upon his fingers]…be homosexual…fuck ship…watch the incredibly bad film called ‘Soldier’ with Kurt Russell in it…It doesn’t matter to me, just as long as people don’t jam it into my face and expect me to like it! HBW: [Putting one hand on his waist, and looking extremely camp] I’m not gonna jam anything into YOUR face mister! Crichton: [With disgusted look etched upon his face] The sexual innuendoes never end do they? HBW: Just my witty superhero banter coming into play…nothing to be afraid of. Crichton: [Even though he looks a little sceptical, Crichton breathes a sigh of relief] Thank god for that! HBW: NOW! Prepare for combat villain! Crichton: [sighing] Listen guy, I really don’t want to fight you! HBW: That’s what all the superheroes say…nobody wants to fight THE HANDBAG WARRIOR! Crichton grins as HBW charges at him, the handbag leading the way. Crichton: I really don’t want to hurt … Crichton breaks off as the Handbag o’ Doom impacts against his jaw sending him back, slamming into the wall with such velocity, a huge Crichton-shaped dint is left behind, as he slumps to the floor. He couldn’t believe it! The Handbag o’ Doom had more power than he ever could have imagined! He had severely underestimated both HBW and the Handbag o’ Doom, he would have never suspected that either of them would posses the power to knock him down. He now saw HBW in a new light, perhaps even respected him a little, but he sure as hell wasn’t going to underestimate him or the handbag again. Crichton pushes himself up off the ground, shaking the plaster board dust from his hair and from his shoulders. He turns to see HBW confidently ‘mincing’ out of the room, one hand on his hip, the other twirling the Handbag o’ Doom around almost cockily. Crichton: [Taking a deep breath and cracking his knuckles] Is that all you got? The Handbag Warrior turns slowly to face him, with a surprised look upon his face. He looks at the Handbag o’ Doom, then back at Crichton. HBW: Nobody has have stood back up from the almighty blow, of the Handbag o’ Doom before! Crichton: Well I’m not just anybody now am I? Otherwise you wouldn’t be here right now, would ya? HBW: I guess not! Crichton: What made you come after me anyhow? HBW: Umm…huh…the government wants you and they hired me. Crichton: Little dangerous don’t you think? HBW: Why? Crichton: Well, the government has the support of the Avengers, so why did they hire you? HBW:I…uh…I don’t …know? Crichton: Do you follow the news at all? HBW: W..w..why? Crichton: Because then you’d know that I fought the Avengers to a standstill. HBW: Oh…I…uh…didn’t know that? Crichton: Really? Tell me, what kind of superhero goes into a fight blind, not knowing all of the details about your opponent, huh? HBW: Good…um… Crichton: Question? HBW: Yeah. Crichton: You’re not an experienced superhero are you? HBW: umm… The Handbag Warrior looks blankly at Crichton for a second, before quickly turning and running like a bastard. It worked. Crichton managed to intimidate the ‘man’ into leaving him alone, but he sure as hell knew that if that guy had managed to find him, others would soon follow. He had to get out of this place. However, he did learn something from The Handbag Warrior, something he needed to know if he was going to keep the Television Title of his. HBW taught him that surprises were abundant in life and a champion must be prepared for anything that his opponent(s) could throw at him. For his match against Shabazz both on Friday Fusion and on Tuesday Turmoil he would be prepared. He would be prepared for any eventually that would occur during the match – he hoped at least. There was an old saying, ‘You can never be too prepared’, he decided that it would be his new catch phrase to coin…and to live by.
