The Tank 26 - Manipulation of Many Colors

25 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: September 25, 2002 ﻿*********The Tank __26—********* Manipulation of Many Colors The way you make me feelsickis like the way you make me feelwonderful.Where time won’t growis where I’ll hollow my ground. ~Leigh Donovan Friday, September 13 - Day 71 “Thankfully,” Geoffery Rankin begins as he rounds to the front of his desk and takes a seat onthe surface, “it looks like they’re going to be able to save Jeremy Doris’ penis.” “Halle-fucking-lujuah,” Leigh Donovan responds with a roll of the eyes. He adds, sarcastically,“For a minute there, I was worried the big black bastard wouldn’t be able to rape me anymore. Iguess I won’t have to worry about that anymore, will I, mate?” His pale blue eyes pierceRankin’s youthful skin as he sits in a chair with his hands cuffed to his ankles - for Rankin’ssafety. Geoffery, the young manager of the entire volatile unit, runs his fingers through his thick brownhair. “Ok, Leigh,” he sighs. “I know Doris made things difficult for you... but biting his penishalf off? Was that really the solution?” “Seems to be working fine so far, mate,” chuckles Leigh. “Alright,” Rankin says, tilting his head back to look up at the ceiling in defeat. “I’m not going tosend you to the hole. I’m not even going to put you in solitary... considering the circumstances. But before Doris gets out of the infirmary, you’ll be getting a new cell.” Rankin grabs a file offthe desk and flips through it for a brief moment. He then peeks up over the edge, “Jason Snowand Darryl Jarvis have had plenty of trouble over the last couple of months. I’m going to switchyou and Jarvis up.” “So you’re taking me away from one oaf, and giving me to another?” Leigh asks with a sarcasticsmile. “However you want to think of it, Donovan,” shrugs Rankin. “With Snow, you can be sure thathe won’t be trying to ram his dick down your throat. But if you step out of line with him...” Leigh’s smile slowly fades - he knows Rankin’s right. Jason Snow’s reputation for violentoutbursts has spread throughout the Tank by now, and Leigh Rankin has seen the man known as‘the Lion’ wandering through Unit B at the late Doug Hatton’s side. “What about J-Thug?” Leigh asks, suddenly. “What about him?” “Won’t he be put into solitaire?” asks Leigh. “Quite frankly, Donovan,” says Geoffery, “I think he’s been punished enough.” September 19 - Day 77 “What the fuck is this?” Timothy McDonald fires sharply as he enters his cell and finds VincentGiovanni stuffing clothes into the drawer beneath the bottom bunk. “Oh,” Giovanni stammers, looking up to see his new cell mate, “I was just unpacking. I’m yournew cell mate.” He leaves his drawer to extend a hand to the Italian mob leader of Scottishdescent. McDonald ignores the gesture. “Who said I wanted a new fucking cellmate?” he asks. “Well,” chuckles Giovanni, “I don’t think you have a choice in the matter.” “You’d be surprised.” Something in Giovanni’s eyes ignites, and for a brief moment, Tim McDonald realizes that thisman is not so jolly as he presents himself. At the same moment, Tim can’t help but notice thesheer mass of the husky Italian. He stands three inches taller than six feet, though he gives theillusion that he’s a much shorter man because he’s built like a bull - a thick layer of fat overwhat is probably a muscular body. But there’s something else as well... “Hey,” Tim says, lifting his chin. “Don’t I know you from somewhere?” Giovanni goes back to slipping folding his two sets of clothing and putting them neatly under hisbunk. “Naw,” he finally answers. “I’m good with faces. If we knew each other, I’d rememberyou.” “What the fuck ever,” shrugs McDonald as he climbs up onto his top bunk. “Just making sure Ididn’t knock off one of your fucking grocery stores or something on the outside.” With that, helays flat on his bunk, puts his hands behind his head, and closes his eyes. Below, Vincent Giovanni keeps his dark eyes locked on the young man, curiously. This man isno grocery store owner, however, and deep down, Tim McDonald knows that. “So,” Vincent says in his gruff friendly voice, trying to keep the lines of communication open, “Ihear you run the Italians in here.” “Fucking right,” Tim responds as he takes a deep breath, preparing for a mid day nap. “Can’t say I picture you as a leader of the mob boys in here,” chuckles the husky Vincent as herolls onto his bottom bunk. “Oh yeah?” Tim says, finally opening his eyes. “How the fuck did you picture the leader aroundhere?” “I don’t know,” admits Vincent. “Just thought he’d have a little more - you know - Sicilian inhim. You like your grandparents came from the land of the fucking albinos, kid.” With this,Vincent begins chuckling hard. “Whatever you want to think,” Tim shrugs, planting his hands back behind his head. “But everymotherfucker in this joint knows that I get shit done. I may not take the pretty road, but I makesure shit gets done.” “Oh yeah?” Vincent’s pitch raises with curiosity. “What kind of shit do you do?” “You looking to sign up with the bad ass guineas?” Tim chuckles. “I wouldn’t go that far,” answers Vincent. “But you know, I guess that kind of shit’s alwaysinterested me. I’ve just never been in the position to get involved.” “Well, you’re in the position now,” says Tim McDonald as he rolls over to get a good look at thebroad body of Vincent below. “It just so happens I’ve got a little business to take care of with acertain biker motherfucker who got here last week.” “What kind of business?” “Just business,” responds Tim, in a tone that suggests he doesn’t want to dive into the subject. “Anyway, it just so happens that the motherfucker did what all large white assholes do - hejumped on board with Harvey Blake and those God damn Nazis.” “So you’re going to send some of your boys to take this biker out?” “Fuck no,” Tim scowls, “aren’t you listening? I know you’ve only been here for amotherfucking week or two, but you should know by now that the Italians and the fucking Nazisare just coming out of a full blown fucking war!” “Jesus,” says Vincent with a gaping mouth. “Is that what happened to Doug Hattoni?” “What?” Tim asks with an annoyed tone. “What the fuck’s it matter what happened to DougHattoni? The point is, we don’t feel like going back to war with the God damn pricks because itcuts into business.” “So what are you going to do to this guy?” “That’s what you’re going to learn about me Vincent,” smiles Tim McDonald. “Just like Ididn’t have to be an Italian to run the fucking gang, I don’t have to send an Italian to get thefucking job done.” September 21 - Day 79 “How’s your new cellmate been treating you, Jason?” asks the young red-haired cellmate knownas the Rat, while he slings a card in Snow’s direction. “He make a move to bite your schlong offyet?” “I’ve been sure to keep it out of his mouth,” Snow says without a smile. He slips his completelydealt hand off the table and takes a peak at it - a losing hand, as usual. Life’s been dealingnothing but losers to Snow of late. “Seems like a strange cat to me,” says the Rat, glancing up at Snow’s cell where Leigh Donovanis pacing just outside, smoking a cigarette carelessly. “I mean, look at the difference in him nowfrom when he first came here.” “You going to bet or what?” The Rat’s blue eyes lock on Snow’s for a moment. “Sure, man,” he says with a smile. “Justmaking some small chat before I win your fucking pants.” He then pulls a dollar out of hispocket and lays it on top of the table. “Start it off at a buck,” he says with a look to the nearbyguard who has decided to say nothing about the gambling going on. “Fine,” Snow says, tossing a dollar of his own on the table. “Jesus,” the Rat exclaims, looking over Snow’s shoulder. “You going to lose your fucking dollar or something?” “Forget the dollar!” spits the Rat, nodding to an enormous man walking behind Snow. “Youknow who that mean looking motherfucker is?” Snow turns around to get a good look at the mammoth man. To Snow, he looks like anincarcerated member of the Hell’s Angels. Every exposed part of his body, including his faceand shaved head, has at least one tattoo on it. His girth is made up more of fat than muscle, butany eye can see that the man is a natural storage house of power. “Should I care?” Snow asks, turning back to the game. “I don’t fucking know,” says the Rat sarcastically. “But I care. That big motherfucker’s name isJim Geraldson. Big bad ass on the outside, as you can probably tell by his appearance. Butthat’s not the fucking interesting part. The interesting part’s what he’s in here for.” “He knock off a tattoo shop?” Snow smirks. “Why don’t you shut the fuck up and listen to me?” the Rat says seriously, finally gainingSnow’s full attention. “Looking at that big bastard you’d think he was in for fucking murder,assault - you know, some shit like that.” “But he’s not?” Snow says to lead the Rat in. “Right,” the Rat says. “Seems that’s not his crime of choice.” “And what would his crime of choice be?” “That big chunk of bacon got his thrills by fucking young girls,” the Rat says, leaning close toSnow and whispering. Snow’s eyes grow cold and for a long moment, he’s silent. “He what?” the young wrestlerfinally asks. “He fucks kids,” the Rat says, tapping a finger on the table. “Like, little girls. You know,fucking fourteen year olds and shit. They got him on three counts of it, but the DA said there’sno fucking telling how many l’il bitches he got to.” “Are you sure about this?” Snow asks, looking over his shoulder at the bald white monster. “Damn sure,” the Rat nods. “It’s all over the fucking papers - don’t you read, man? This guy’sas big a fucking celebrity as you and your cock-biting cellmate are.” Slowly, Snow stands up from his seat and turns toward Jim Geraldson, some ten yards away. Snow’s not thinking any more. In place of his thoughts, raw emotions are rushing through themost technical parts of his brain - memories of when he learned that his nineteen year old sister-in-law, Krista, had been raped. This Jim Geraldson obviously was not the man who rapedKrista, since her rapist committed suicide, but he was a rapist. And at the moment, Snowdoesn’t care much for splitting hairs. The young denim-clad incarcerated wrestler marches up to the massive biker, who’s lighting acigarette. “What’s your name?” Snow asks him, glaring a hole through the man with his laserblue eyes. Jim Geraldson is a full five inches taller than Snow and at least a hundred poundsheavier. “That’s me,” the enormous man says, blowing a mouthful of smoke out to the side. “You one ofHarvey’s boys?” “No,” Snow answers. “You?” “I know the winning side when I see it,” Geraldson smiles. “I hear you also know how to rape teenage girls,” Snow says sharply. His words, however, don’t phase the enormous man in front of him. “Yeah,” he chuckles,letting his slight southern accent show through. “a few people say that I guess. But you know,to each his own, brother. Right?” Snow is silent. “Right?” the enormous biker’s eyes narrow on the bearded young man in front of him, his tonebecoming more insistent. Without warning, Snow strikes the enormous man like an angry lion hunting large prey. Aquick, yet strong, right hand to the side of the face is enough to stagger the whale, and a followup left elbow is enough to drop him like a wounded beast. “I’ll kill you!” Snow screams, pouncing on top of the fallen mammoth, grabbing the fat throatwith his left hand and striking the tattoo’ed face with his right. Blood comes out of the man’sbulging face in spurts, soaking his black goatee and running into his eyes. Snow doesn’t care. He continues to strike away at the defenseless whale’s face - again and againwith each blow coming with an increased amount of force behind it to offset the numbingsensation that’s coming at the same rate. Another blow. More blood. Another. More. A second later, Snow finds that he’s not alone with the large rapist. Three brave C.O’s rush intothe scene, swinging their billy clubs at full force - the sickening crack of the wood on skull ringsthrough the Unit, but Snow refuses to cease the beating. “Get the fuck up!” screams on guard desperately before swinging his club like a bat andconnecting with the back of Snow’s head a second time. Again, Snow seems not to feel it. Another blow lands on the left side of Geraldson’s battered face. The second guard attempts to grab Snow’s arm, but soon realizes the approach is useless - Snowis too strong, and too far into his world of rage. “Get him off me!” shrieks Geraldson as tears begin to mix with the blood that’s stinging hiseyes. Snow doesn’t hear him - another blow, harder than the rest, lands in the side ofGeraldson’s broken face. A third crack rings through the Unit as another billy club strikes Snow - this one on the side ofthe head. Momentarily, Snow wavers from the task at hand - beating his victim to death - and hetips backward long enough to receive another billy club in the back of the head. This one seemsto snap him back into his rage, and he lands another right hand on Geraldson, who’s now driftingfrom consciousness. “God damn it!” screams one of the guards, delivering a crushing blow that makes everyobserving prisoner wince. Snow doesn’t hesitate from his beating for a moment, though bloodhas now soaked the back of his yellow locks of hair. Realizing that they must now put Geraldson’s life ahead of Snow’s, all three guards hold theirbreath and begin sending repeated billy club strikes into the back of Snow’s bloody head. Andfinally, the lion crumbles. His consciousness doesn’t fade out like Geraldson’s. Instead, it was snapped away like atelevision that has just had it’s power cut. He collapses onto his side and lies motionless,completely unconscious, with blood forming a puddle beneath him from several wounds on hishead and the side of his face. Jim Geraldson isn’t in much better shape. Various bones in his face lie broken beneath hisblood soaked skin and his left eye has already swollen to the size of a baseball and is rapidlyturning to a deep color of purple. His nose is bend in three different places and there’s bloodspurting into the air from two open wounds in it. “Jesus Christ!” exclaims one of the guards, dropping his billy club and looking down at thecarnage. “Some get some fucking stretchers!” He then looks down at the sheer girth ofGeraldson. “Maybe three of the fucking things! Now, God damn it!” Some twenty yards away, Tim McDonald walks by the Rat and slips him a handful of cash.
