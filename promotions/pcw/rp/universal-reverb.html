...Universal Reverb...

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: ... Getting our just deserts? Some would call it karma...turning back to strike against he who would cause the negative type. Us...we call it universal reverb... ...Like the HAND OF FATE turning to slap us in the face. We had kept it all quiet so far, but as embarrassing as it is, we should speak of it. We're not to bottle something up inside like that Pharaoh and let it fester until it explodes at the wrong moment. Cyclonica says we have nothing to hide. Remember Portalis? Of course you do... Well...we fought him...and the outcome wasn't to our liking. The air was thick. If you didn't know any better, you'd say it was fog. But no...it was tension...two men were set to square off right then and there in that street, and there wasn't anything that any of them could do to stop it. It was a battle that would much have the same atmosphere as Golden Game, Legend Inc, Frisco, and us. The tension would be thick...and there would be nothing to stop any of them from obtaining victory. Of course we were the one who would step out of that match with his hand raised in victory. Screw Pharaoh... We stood atop of the limo with our fists balled up, ready to strike. Across from us in the street, the one called Portalis stared him down hard with those glowing yellow eyes of his. 'You're just like all the others, Portalis.' we called out to the man. 'You're just another no-talent flat-scan with a little something under his belt that makes him special. You're a loser...Juan's a golden loser, Legend Inc's a legendary loser, that banished spoiled boy Frisco's a hall of fame loser...and that other guy’s a loser as well. Why don't you just go home and stroke your little spear there?' Portalis reached behind him and pulled out his weapon. It was a light brown spear with a silver spear head shaped in an odd design. He twirled it around in the air a few times before taking his fighting stance. 'You speak like Darkcyde...only he and his Void Elements says flat-scan.' Portalis muttered. 'You must be of the Void.' We chuckled a bit. 'Score one for Mr. Clueless here. So you know a bit about the Void Elements, eh? It's no matter...you're definitely not going to collect on the nine billion dollar bounty.' 'This is for no bounty.' Portalis dug the soles of his feet into the street. 'This is for the means of battle, Akira Akiyama. Defeating you will bring accolades to me as well as my allies of battle.' 'It's no matter, man. We hope you like being just another number on the list of those banished.' much like Frisco was now... Of course we had to snatch him out of the Void right after that match...we have no need for PCW rest home fodder inside our haven. We balled up our hands and prepared for the battle. We crashed into the ground...hard. We could feel that nerve sensation pulsing through our body. You mortals would call it pain. We called it limitation. Wiping the crimson trickle from the corner of our lips, we stood back up, taking our fighting stance. Behind us, the spectators watched. Seraphina Jameson, along with the celebrities watched from inside the limo. But Yuu...she stood outside watching on with concerned eyes. We stood before her in the heat of battle, and she was still by our side. That's determination... ...The type that we would show throughout the oncoming battles. Portalis held his spear tightly, waving it back and forth in front of his face. His face was as blank as ours; it took skill to mask the distracting emotions during battle. Pharaoh...he's one that never learned that skill. If he had a problem with us...he should have mentioned it later on afterwards...once the job was done and all those Divine champions posers were taken care of. Portalis stared at us hard...sizing up our moves and our stance. We lunged at his face with the HAND OF FATE, but he side stepped it. We should really stop using that move so freely... it lowers it's value. Portalis was quick about countering, a series of rapid shots from the spear followed by a slash sent us back down to the street. Our eyes turned to Yuu...and she stood there watching still. We couldn't give up in front of her... We don't know why, but the little scamp's grown onto us lately. She's totally opposite from us, but she makes up for it by determination and loyalty to us. We still wonder who she really is and why she's even with us to begin with...but we thank her for what she's done so far. Climbing back to our feet, we growled hard at the shogun. 'Wouldn't be so tough without your lousy weapon there, mister.' Fusion was just as similar. With Pharaoh suddenly growing a set of testicles as well as a code of honor, we would be working from a one on five disadvantage. And even though Golden Game is as impotent as old man Frisco in the head, but they're still considered worthy opponents since they won that number one contender's spot. But Akiyama's a god, they'll remark...he should be more than capable of taking a few mortals and another god if he's so mighty. How their sarcasm thinks so highly of us when the odds are to their advantage. Since Pharaoh was not being cooperative with us...each and every one of those flat-scans might as well have a weapon so they can brutalize the Neo Void God. That little remark rewarded our salty tongue with another jarring blow. The dear girl came up to us, trying to help us to our feet. We pushed her off...we didn't need any help. But it was then that we realized something about ourselves...we're always pushing others away, while only drawing ourselves nearer. There's so much of a reason for that though. We would rather not surround ourselves with imperfection to taint our demeanor with their flaws. Interacting with the flawed individuals in PCW was a different story. Through there, we at least wished to show them the guiding perfection so that they can know how they should change themselves for the better of society and eventually humanity. Turns out all this time we were associating with imperfection in the guise of Titu Xansiv...who you people would know as the Pharaoh. 'Back off, Yuu...' we bellowed. 'You're only going to get in the way.' the feeling was mutual about Pharaoh...only in the way of our progress. Portalis shook his head slowly, driving the head of his spear down into the pavement. 'I grow tired of fighting with you now, Neo Void God. It is time that you were delivered to he who wishes for your presence. This is our Wicked Gate.' Heh... 'You're just another money-grubbing prick...Frisco-like, eh?' our voice was going gravelly, that metallic taste of blood seeping across our taste buds. 'Well come on, child. We'll toss you aside in our quest for unity!!' The yellow-eyed man dressed in silver spun his spear in a helicopter fashion in front of him. We watched as the circular pattern he made began to glow...before a huge yellow circle appeared before him. With the tip of his spear, he launched the sphere towards us. We narrowly dodged the attack, the force of our evasion causing us to crash into the limo, skidding it several feet to the curb. Screams came muffled from the inside, but we shook it off and got back to our feet. Our eyes felt glassy... As Portalis formed yet another Wicked Gate, the darkness began to creep around us, lurking around our feet and clutching our palms. The shadow behind us began to serpent around our ankles, spiraling around like a cyclone of black. Our eyes stared on at our adversary, his attack building up before his face. About this point, there was nothing more important than taking this guy out...it would be the same way later on at Fusion...taking care of business the only priority. For you Anime gurus out there that need a more accurate description, the darkness swirling around him could be compared to going Super Saiyan of Dragon Ball Z fame. “Pass through this Wicked Gate!!” that was his call to launch his glowing yellow portal at us once more. We stood there watching his attack barrel at us at full force...then we had our counter strike. “Darkness Revolution!!” whatever that was, we shot it straight through his Wicked Gate, shattering the yellow energy particles in all directions as a tri-beam of darkness bolted for him. The shogun had no idea what just happened, but just as it is nature...he ran from what was unknown to him. Portalis stepped to the left...and all seemed safe. We had missed our target... ...Until he swerved it...that is... “What?” Portalis began running down the street as the black beams turned and aimed for his chest. We held our hands out, acting as a guide for the beam, directing it towards its target. It flowed after him, making each accurate turn and twist as Portalis tried to evade his demise. The shogun then leaped vertically into the air. He twirled his spear in the air and launched another Wicked Gate towards the following darkness, but yet again it broke through and pursued further. Just as it looked as if Portalis was through, he back flipped in mid air and landed on the street. The Darkness Revolution beam looped in the air and headed for him straight like a bullet. “You’re through now!!” we jeered. Hey...what use is it to win and not taunt the man as well? Portalis leaned against his spear, a look of exasperation, yet loftiness, laying in his eyes. That moment there was like time slowed for tension as well as dramatic response. Both the Darkness Revolution and Portalis stared each other down until the last split second... ...When Portalis dived out of the way. But that wasn’t the climax...that wasn’t the shocker of the event... The Darkness Revolution stormed on and made impact... ...With Yuu... She had been standing behind Portalis...he must have strategically positioned himself there. We had no way of withdrawing the attack... And it was at that point where the Plexiglas wall around our emotions...shattered. We dropped to our knees and watched on helplessly...helpless because once you begin, you can never interrupt. Her shadow swirled around her pain-stricken body, her face struck with grief. Then the darkness began to take her...from her toes spreading up through her...materializing like deleted data. Seraphina and the others watched as well, terror upon their faces. Even Portalis had a surprised look on his face. But through all that pain...all that suffering...Yuu still looked on at us...and she smiled that same warming smile at us one last time. We heard her voice...up close, as if we were listening to her final thoughts before she was taken away. “Don’t you forget about me, Akiyama...never forget that I’ll be waiting for you...waiting for you to find me...Aki!!” And the darkness took her whole. It was over...and after that...silence. Nothing more, nothing less...we knelt there staring at the place she once stood with watered eyes...in disbelief at the scene. A small gust began blowing...how dramatic... Then we stood up... Portalis stepped back cautiously. “This is not good.” “What??” Seraphina asked nervously. Portalis said nothing as he stretched out his hand. A glowing yellow portal opened before him. He grabbed her, along with the celebrities currently with her, and threw them into the portal. Then with one last glance as us...he dived in himself. Our arms were held out at our sides... our palms clawed upwards...back hunched forward slightly. Then with a thrust backwards, our head looked upwards, a sonic-pitched roar echoing from our lungs through the city, shattering glass and rumbling the ground. Our eyes closed...and those tears we welled up in our eyes streamed down our cheeks and hit the pavement... ...In a splatter of darkness... Seraphina Jameson found herself atop the balcony of a high rise across town. The black woman looked up shaken at the sudden teleportation that she was forced through. Her eyes gazed up slowly to find Portalis staring out at something. She stood up as well and followed his gaze towards his target...and gasped. “What in the world is happening???” she whispered in awe. Portalis bowed his head. “That...is the sight of that whole 999 meter radius...receiving the despair of the Neo Void God.” “ENTIRE SECTOR OF CITY VANISHES!!” Hordes of media, public, and officials gathered behind the yellow police tape as police inspected a blank area covered by nothing but dirt. For the entire nine hundred and ninety nine mile radius...there was nothing but dirt. Everything else...gone. A black nailed hand scooped up one of the freshly printed newspapers. The man unfurled it and gazed at the headline slowly. The paperboy standing there with the stack turned towards the man with his hand stretched out. “Hey, Mack. This ain’t no library. That’ll be half a buck.” near seven feet in height...didn’t intimidate him...rippling with muscles and veins...not frightened in the least. But when that man glared back at him from under that tangled mess of black and silver hair...the boy only saw darkness... “Keep it...” he squeaked. Little maggot...if we wanted a paper, we were taking it. Stepping away from the scene...we walked forward towards battle... With who? At this point...the world seemed like a pretty good adversary. Yuu was gone...banished in our own realm somewhere... And we were going to get her back...even if the universe had to be bathed in darkness to see her again... We may have lost her...but there’s no way we’re going to fail our task... And the Reverb that the universe tends to toss back at us? We may lose the Divine Tag Titles...but there’s no way we’re losing the Akivision Title...
