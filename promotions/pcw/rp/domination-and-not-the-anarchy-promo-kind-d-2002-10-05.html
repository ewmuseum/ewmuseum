Title: Domination (And not the Anarchy promo kind):-D Featuring: Tim Walden Date: Oct. 5, 2002 The point? Simple..You choose a goal, you make progress toward that goal, and one way or another, you remove the obstacles in your way so that you can reach your goal...Hasn't been too difficult so far. The goal? To strike fear into those who wish to defy the Front Office's fair and honest taxation methods. The progress? Each and every week, each and every match I wrestled or planned-out action I made, it was not about the glory of one little victory so much now, nor was it of any true value to my ego when I had not faced any true challengers yet..No, this was of greater significance..Every foe that I have triumphed over to this point has been of too little merit to truly establish myself, but enough regard so that my performance against them can send fear into the hearts of those of us who aren't business-savvy enough to attain the greatness that is tax exemption.. It's a simple plan, but one that hasn't failed me yet: Crush the opposition and the tax dollars will pour in. Kramer Wild Wing's attempts to evade our grasp with his KFC deal were clever at first, but once he fell under my grip, there was no evasion for him...Total Violation had been a thorn in my side for almost a year now, but with the CEO backing me, how could I not show them the true meaning of tax and finally get a measure of revenge, even with that inferno freak's pyrotechnic antics? It was ridiculous to put people in such grave danger during their match as to ignite them! Someone could seriously have been hurt because of Clyde's sloppy and unsafe fire-play...But that's irrelevant, Xavi can take good enough care of him himself, no means to worry about him there, but if he wants some help from his friends, I don't mind aiding the corporate cause of maintaining the balance of power, it's all for the sake of keeping PCW on top where we deserve to be.. Hell, I'm not averse to a little help now and then when it's strictly business, but I fight my own battles generally, if someone tries to take their tax frustrations out on me then I can take 'em right back at 'em..Case in point, Teresa Tomas. I could've been nice about it, and to be honest, I was, I gave her the chance to take the easy way out, don't get any bother just as long as she paid up...And she looked to take it, but then she just HAD to go and make the mistake of trying to get one over on me...Hurts a lot less to take a swift kick to the cup than a short trip through the catering display, but that's just the penalty for reneging on one's side of the deal..I could've been happy there with my foe unconscious, but then something sparked in my mind...'Give 'em something to remember you by.'...And I took it to heart..In fact, I'd have to say that I think I'll be rememberin' it a little more than the fans. Well, I can't say that I didn't probably tick off the viewers to see a guy takin' advantage of their favourite li'l Southern Belle, but it just goes to show that we in the Front Office truly will not rest until the rest of the roster is paralyzed in fear at our domination of the federation...Taxation is my tool of the power, which I back up with a total technical superiority, and if you stand against my power, and more importantly the power of PCW you'd best be watching out, because it just means I'll have to put the muscle behind my authority again, and this time...Cianan, long time legend of the sport, but never had to face a true challenge above the levels of indies like CCW and the Japanese federations...Enmity, upstart, his overconfidence will be his downfall...Ali Khadafi, misogynist pig, I think I may step back and let Brandi beat the everloving hell out of him for her pleasure...Suicidal Stevenson, he must live up to his name to go into this match so positively....And most of all, Jamar Wilson, the man whose arrogance took a once-respectable federation and dragged it down from great wrestling action to attempting to stage bra-and-panties matches and over-the-top battle royals for title contendership...He could have had power in Japan much like that we hold here, but he blew it. He now makes one last gasp for glory, and it is going to fail miserably, for when he finally reaches PCW programming with his lack of wrestling experience, ultimately he will be most likely to fail, to discover that he, like the rest...Has no evasion. And like this impending victory, a thought comes back from my own little 2 minutes of pleasure last Friday.. ...Southern Comfort never tasted so good. ---- View Tim Walden's Biography
