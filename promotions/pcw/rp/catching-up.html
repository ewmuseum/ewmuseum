Catching up

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 23-10-87 Bob Vurdo is back from his trip to Trappani in Sicily and he has brought back a lot with him. He found the girl of his dreams over there and they fell in love, subsequently she made the trip back to America with Bob Vurdo to live with him in the country she always dreamed of living in. He also managed to find his mother and bring her home as he planned. She thought Bob was dead after he disappeared and when she saw him she was overwhelmed by joy. After his arrival he has caught up with his sleep and is ready to get back into 'working' and wrestling. He doesn't know if the family will accept Rosanna the girl he brought home with him. It is a very important part of the relationship, if she isn't accepted then he might have to leave the business in order to save her life. If she is accepted it will mean they can continue the relationship happily without making any sacrifices. Mrs Vurdo and Rosanna seem to get along well which make Bob extremely happy. At the money his life couldn't be better he made it to PCW, he met the love of his life and he has his mother with him again. In recent weeks the Vurdo family has been at war with the Monchuca family and when Bob left things were starting to heat up. Bob hasn't heard any news about the happenings since he left Bob Vurdo is standing against the pole of a street light with the rain pelting down on his trenchant. The night is dark and the street light only lights up a small portion of the back street in Amber Hill. The streetlight highlights the rain falling at a slight angle as there is a small cool breeze. Bob is smoking a cigar as he watches a rare car drive past splashing water from under its wheels onto Bobs already drenched shoes. Bob shakes the water from his hair when he is interrupted by the familiar voice of Tony his best friend and business advisor. 'Only Bob Vurdo would call a meeting at this time in the night in the rain.' Bob turns around to see Tony walking up to him with a smile on his face. Tony greets Bob with a short hug before looking up and down Bob. Tony is tall and thin but you can tell he is strong and viscous just by looking at him. He looks of Italian decent with thick curly dark brown hair and natural olive skin. His face bears two scars each telling a long story from fights when he was just a goon for the Vurdo family. He also wears a trench coat which is covering his body clothes. His thick curly hair absorbs a lot of the rain pressing it further and further down against the top of his head. 'Something's happened,' Tony smiles at Bob and looks at him in an inquisitive yet happy way. 'What are you so happy about?' Tony continues smiling but you have to wonder how he can tell this Bob isn't smiling or moving a bit he never really smiles and it is his usual pose. 'I can tell there's something about you?' Bob smiles at Tony before replying 'I got a girl I want ya to meet.' Tony smiles back at Bob and gently hits him on the shoulder. 'You brought back a Sicilian woman did you?' Bob nods before shaking the water out of his hair. 'We have a lot to talk about. I want to get away from this place and out of the rain.' 'Why the fuck did we meet here anyway?' 'I didn't know it was ganna rain and besides Sammy aint here yet.' Tony puts his hand on Bob's shoulder and begins walking with Bob toward the carpark. The carpark is lit by a single light which stands above the black Mercedes lighting it up, making it stand out in the empty carpark. 'A lot has happened while you where gone,' Tony says as he continues leading Bob toward the car. --------------------------------***---------------------------------------- Tony and Bob sit at a table in the Broad Street Social club. They are the only two men in the club other than the bartender. They both smoke a cigar as they catch up with each other after the last week. The room is dark as each light hangs low from the ceiling only lighting up the table it hangs over. 'Why did you have us meet out in the rain?' 'I felt like a walk and I didn't know that it would start raining that hard.' Tony nods before deciding to begin talking business. 'Monchuca is dead,' announces Tony in a tone of glorification. Tony doesn't let Bob reply he continues talking but this time he talks slower to make sure each word sinks in. 'A lot of our men have died and we are very week at the moment. We need to go recruiting quickly cause if another war breaks out we are fucked.' Tony is very serious with what he is saying. Bob has a concerned and serious expression on his face as he looks at Tony. 'Keep your eyes out for new talent.' Bobs and Tony's eyes don't part this is a very serious conversation. Talking business is very important for these two as they are the two main men in the Vurdo family. 'Sammy is in hospital, he got shot in the leg four times.' Tony shakes his head as he says this in a sign of disappointment. Bob sighs and nods at Tony 'I'll go and see him tomorrow sometime.' 'Also a lot of our business's where attacked and we are going to need to work all that out.' Again Bob nods in approval 'It will take a lot of time to get things back up again.' Tony sighs before changing the topic for the time being 'So who's this new girl?' There is a lot of rebuilding to do for Bob Vurdo and who knows what will come up with that? Also what will the rest of the family think about Rosanna? All that and more in the coming days.
