The Painting

23 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1/23/03 A man squirms and writhes under hot lights. For as long as he can remember he has been here (or all of five minutes, either way), in the uncomfortable place.....he does not know when he may leave it. 'HOLD STEEL!!!!!' 'Bah.' We pan in on the grumbling face of Clyde, as he sits in a small wooden chair. Across from him, an odd French man in a beret sticks out a thumb in his direction and squints. Artist: Za, we cannot paint ze peecture wit ze freakshow MOVING! Clyde: What? 'He says he can't do your crappy ass picture with you moving!' Clyde turns his head, but the voice of the Frenchman screeches forth. Artist: HOLD STEEL!!!!! Clyde: Urgh! An evil cackle rings out from behind the demon....around him prances Bethany, weilding a rather sharp knife. Clyde: I can't hold still with you being an idiot either, dearie. Bethany: Oh that's not very NICE! The little girl accentuates this last statement with a prod from her blade, poking Clyde in the knee. Clyde: OW!!!!! Artist: YOU ARE WORST PERZON EVAR!!!!! Clyde: (Clutching his knee) Shut your hole! With a grumble Clyde sits still again, staring straight ahead at the frenchie as he mutters and goes back to painting. Bethany: (Juggling her knife) We wouldn't need to be doing this if you didn't break every camera we tried to use on you, ugmo. Clyde: Every camera you've tried to use on me's annoyed me. Bethany: Oh, so you just break everything that annoys you? Clyde: (Smiling) Let's test that theory. The burned man reaches out, wrapping one hand around the top of Bethany's head and lifting her off the ground. The mad frenchman screeches again. Artist: HOLD STEEL!!!!! HOLD STEEL!!!!! CAN YOU NOT HOLD STEEL!!!!! Bethany kicks her feet insanely, managing to boot her demonic lover right in the nose. Clyde: (Holding his face) OW!!!!!! OW!!!!! OW!!!!!! A small trickle of blood seeps down Clyde's face through his hands, as his hired artist seethes. Artist: Now look, ve must use ze red!!!!! I HAVE NO RED TO GEEV, STOOPID AMERICAN!!!!!! Clyde: (Nasally) Oh shut up. Very gingerly, the demon removes his hands from his face revealing a nose now comically bent in an almost Marsha Bradyesqe manner. Bethany: ......bwaha! Clyde: What, what!? Bethany: Oh nothing. Clyde: Tell me, wench! Bethany: What are you, a viking now? Clyde: Oh shut up. Clyde carefully pats his nose, wincing as he taps the broken part. He then grabs it and shakes his head extremely fast, his features becoming a blur.....then just as fast he stops, his nose back to its original state. Clyde: (Grinning) There. Across from him, the Frenchman is not impressed. Artist: Zat was not holding steel. DO YOU KNOW WHAT HOLDING STEEL MEANS!?!?!? Clyde: (Grin fading) Hurm. Blinking slowly, Clyde resumes his original position. Bethany remains helpful by prancing in a circle around him, jabbing him in various locations with her knife every few seconds. Clyde: Ow. Ow. Ow. Ow. Ow. Ow. Ow. Artist: HOLD..... Clyde: Steel. Yes, I'm holding steel. I'm holding all sorts of metallic alloys. Shut up. Bethany trots around to the front again, where Clyde nonchalantely sticks out a foot and trips her, causing her to fly clear across the room and hit the far wall with a crash. Clyde: (Smiling) Go ahead. Sticking his tongue out of the side of his mouth, the artist strokes his brush rapidly over the canvas. Taking an occassional glance at the demon, he hums to himself....then stands straight and puts a thumb up. Artist: Iz ready! Clyde: That was fast. Artist: You vould be shocked vat I can do when stoopid American peegs HOLD STEEL!!!!!! Clyde: You're worse than my barber. Bethany: (Groggily standing) You haven't even brushed your hair in a millenia, what barber? Clyde: Shut up. And let me see it. Clyde finally stands, walking over to the easel. He tilts his head and examines the portrait, as Bethany runs up behind him and jabs him in the side. Clyde: Ow. Ignoring her, he inspects the picture....scratches his head. Clyde: It's....nice. The burned man looks upon the interpretation of himself.....a picture looking much younger than he actually is. A single scar runs along the side of his chest, as his haunted red eyes look on. Artist: I used ze red, in spite of your Amereecan peegness. Clyde: It's actually very good. Clyde looks upon his visage on the canvas....slowly, he nods his head. Clyde: Extremely good. I....you're actually not bad at this. And.... A thud, followed by a high pitched shriek is the demon's only answer. He turns to find Bethany standing over the french artist, a hammer in her hand as he groans on the floor. Bethany: Urgh, if you get a painting I'm getting a stuffed frog! No ifs ands or buts! The little girl grabs the man by the foot and begins dragging him out the door.....Clyde shrugs, scoops up his painting and follows as we mercifully fade to black......
