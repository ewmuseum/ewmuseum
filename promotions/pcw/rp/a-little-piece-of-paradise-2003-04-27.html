A little piece of paradise

27 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 27/4/03 It could be said that the history of space flight began almost from the dawn of mankind. From the moment that first person looked up upon the heavens, we have dreamed of exploring. From mythology such as Daedalus and Icarus, who flew too close to the sun on wings made of wax, to Archimides, Newton, Galileo, Copernicus, DaVinci, Jules Verne, and H.G. Wells, all have served, through science or fiction, to inspire modern day scientists and pioneers such as the Wright brothers, Robert Goddard, Kanstantin Tsiolkovsky, Hermann Oberth, Wernher Von Braun, Sergey Korolev (the famed 'Chief Designer' of the Soviet space program), Yuri Gagarin, and Neil Armstrong to reach for the stars. Goals, which may not seem achievable, are never out of reach if you have the heart, if you have the belief in what you do, belief in your own ability then nothing will ever be totally out of reach. You may have to work that little bit harder but everything is possible if you have belief. Picture if you will a small garden, a picturesque flower bed positioned to the side of a cobbled path with a various assortment of flowers filling the garden with a pleasant smell which attacks the sinus’ and reminds you of summer. Look past the small pond with a fountain trickling gently the sound of it pleasant to your eardrums and you will see a man kneeling in the middle of this little piece of paradise. The man who we all know by now as James Edwards. There is a slight breeze blowing through the garden almost playing with James’ hair like a man plays with his lover’s hair, stroking it feeling the soft textures and the smoothness of it underneath his fingertips. The sunlight highlighting the gold tints at the tips of it, his soft green eyes focused on something in front of him a relatively large yellow rose bush with the flowers just coming into bloom. Beneath the yellow sun kissed flowers is a small wooden plaque no bigger than the size of an A4 piece of card, the dark mahogany showing age but still full of strength and beauty. As James slowly moves his head down towards the plaque the sun hits the golden panel on the front of it, clearly well polished and well loved the gold plating glistens as though alive under the beams of light being projected towards it by the sun. James slowly bends down further and clasps the plaque in his hands staring at the engraved writing on it a small tear begins to well up in his right eye which drops down and cascades off the golden plaque, tracing his fingers slowly along the writing James sighs deeply and sadly as his eyes read the engraving like he had done many times before. ‘In loving memory of Sarah and Robert Edwards taken away in their prime’A few months had passed since the death of James’ parents he had come to terms with their death knowing that everything happens for a reason, even if we do not want to see it, or know it. Nobody asks for their lives to change James knew that. He also knew that it’s what you did afterwards that counted, it’s the actions and steps that you took which showed the true spirit and character. It showed who you really were. A bird chirped in the distant singing it’s song as though nature was trying to communicate with James for him to try and cheer up. Placing the plaque back under the rose bush James lightly pushed two fingers against his lips and placed them on the gold plate screwed onto the plaque. “I’m keeping the garden like you said mum, the irises’ and lily’s are coming into bloom about now, we lost one of the fish the other day but the others seem to be reasonably ok about it. The roses are doing great…well they would do they were your pride and joy. I remember as a child you telling me that you didn’t want a big fuss made when you died no big mourning…I remember you taking me to one side and simply asking me to do two things, plant a yellow rose bush and maintain the garden. I hope I am doing you proud mum, I really hope I am.” James’ words coming directly from the heart full of emotion and meaning. “I haven’t heard back from Emily just yet she’s probably busy having the time of her life. I remember back on her graduation day when we were all there stood around her, dad was so proud of her he never said a lot that wasn’t his style but it was in his eyes they were burning with pride he was so happy for her. She would have wanted you to be at her launch.” James’ head hung slightly as he said this realising that he was all alone in this world for the time being. “Do you remember when we were at the beach looking at the stars, before Emily was born. I couldn’t take my eyes off them I was just staring at the mass quantity of stars, watching them shimmer and flicker in the night sky and the moon… the crescent moon that seemed to be in the sky just for us. Emily is up there now…and I’m not, I’ve got a new profession” James laughs slightly to himself still unable to see him doing anything successful as a wrestler. At best James was hoping for a few years at jobber salary to fund him through NASA’s next training regime. “I have to go mum my flight leaves soon and I need to finish packing. I will see you again as soon as I get back…if I get back and don’t worry about the garden I’ve got the Johnson’s checking in on it while I’m away, they know where everything is so everything should be sorted.” James slowly rose to his feet and looked down at the rose bush once more, a small be was rooting around in one of the budding flowers, presumably looking for pollen to take back to it’s queen who would proceed to make the honey. James couldn’t help but smile at this as he pivoted slowly on one foot and proceeded to head towards the exit of the garden and his little piece of paradise. Life would always continue long after James was gone, life would always flourish new generations thrown into this life head first not knowing where to turn, but in essence that’s what life is. If we knew what to do with it, if we knew where we were headed in our existence there would be no fun, life would be dull and monotonous. James knew that one day he would visit the stars for it was his destiny but fate had decided to play one of his cards, PCW was just another obstacle that James would over come. James paused momentarily before turning back towards the rose bush, his lips parted as he projected the last few words out with deep emotional breath. “I miss you”
