The Chickens Come Home to Roost

20 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 03/20/02 We pan in on a quaint park...........the grass green and ever expanding, as far as the eye can see. The clear blue skies stretch overhead............birds sing, sing their sweet singing song. It is a beautiful day here.............a gentle wind stirring the air. In the distance a dog barks, yipping happily as his master no doubt prepares to launch a frisbee for him to fetch..........all in all, it is the kind of day people dream of. We journey through it all, onward, onward up to a swingset. It's metal link chains have held countless children aloft, their faint cries of joy still audible if you are so attuned to such things. It is no child which rests upon the seat now. No indeed, no playful kiddie frolics on this day............instead we zoom over to witness a plain white bucket resting upon the swing. An elderly man's face adorns the side, smiling eternally even from beyond his grave..........steam rises from it, the edges of it's contents poking from the top. It is a bucket of chicken. Original recipe to be exact. We focus on this bucket of chicken for quite some time..........admiring it's stillness, it's sheer chickeness. Finally we zoom back to see none other than Clyde standing before us, with Bethany at his side. Clyde: Ah yes. The demon continues to glance down at the bucket............appraising, looking into it's very soul. Bethany: Clyde, why are you staring at a bucket of chicken in a park? The little girl's words register, but they are far off..............Clyde is too far into his own musings to really hear. Nonetheless he responds.......... Clyde: What are your dreams, little bucket of chicken. Let me look into your being............ Bethany: Dammit! Bethany stomps up to the 10 piece bucket, takes it in her arms. Clyde blinks suddenly, snapping out of the trance he was in. Clyde: Wha? Bethany: I said, why the hell are we in a park with a bucket of chicken? Clyde: Oh yes. It's really quite simple............you see my opponent the End cut a promo recently, claiming it was for our match upcoming at Tuesday Turmoil. However the contents of said promo oddly enough weren't about me at all, they were about one Jeremy Howard. So I've decided the hell with it, if he wants to cut a promo about Jeremy Howard I'm going to ignore him entirely and devote this promo to Kramer Wild Wing. The evil child stands with the 10 piece bucket in the crook of her arm...........scratches her head. Bethany: I.............suppose that makes sense. Kind of. The burned man sits upon the ground...........his eyes roll in his head as he chants. Clyde: Oh dark god Baal............grant me the power to cut a promo about a jobber who gains strength from a box of original recipe............ We zoom in on Clyde's eye..........into his brain where a raging maelstrom blares. Thunder claps, lighting flashing as his spirit leaves his body, zooming at impossible speeds through endless terrain. Finally it reaches it's destination.............the burned man looks up as a giant black demon turns to face him. Baal: I'm sorry Clyde but this is a stupid idea even for you, I'll have no part of it. Begone. With a snap of his finger Baal sends the ghost of Clyde careening back through time and space again.............into his body which hits the ground with a thud. Clyde: Ow. Bethany: Lemme guess, Baal thought this was a dumb idea too. Clyde: Bah! Screw Baal, we'll come up with some other way of cutting a promo against Kramer Wild Wing and ignoring the End totally like he ignored us. Clyde motions for Bethany to follow him...........munching on a piece of chicken she does so. The pair walks..........when out of nowhere steps a man in a white suit. Bethany: I know you, you're Randy Quaid right? Randy Quaid: You look like a pair in need of gainful employment. What say you and me talk? Clyde: Do I LOOK like a man who was fired from his job as a serial killer by a quality control team and is now desperately trying to cut a promo against a chicken worshipper he knows nothing about really? Randy Quaid: Yes. Clyde: Damn. Randy Quaid: Come on kids............we'll set you up real nice. A short time later we zoom in on the burned man again............a smile of twisted pain on his face. His mind clearly broken, he grins..........we pan out to see he is dressed in a white suit, with a chef's hat adorning his head. Next to him Bethany wears the same...........however she is not grinning in the slightest. Bethany: You asshole! Look what you've gotten us into! Clyde: Shut up and kill chickens. I'm going to pretend both Kramer Wild Wing and the End never happened, you do the same. Clyde kneels, grabs a chicken with one hand and beheads it with the butcher knife he holds in the other............suddenly the chickens turn to him. Glaring at him with their narrow eyes, they circle.......... Clyde: Ah crap. The poultry rush, engulfing the demon. In a swarm of feathery death they begin to peck at him, taking revenge for the death of their comrade............Bethany looks on and shakes her head.......... Bethany: Mercifully fade to black already! .............and we mercifully fade to black................
