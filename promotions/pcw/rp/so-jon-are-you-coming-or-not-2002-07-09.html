So, Jon, are you coming or not?

9 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7/9/2002 ~What happens when circumstances make a man question what he does? All hell breaks loose within that man. He tends to go through an emotional roller coaster of hell. He tends to make people's lives a living hell in the process. Kano is going through this right now. So what? If you think Kano was making others' lives a living hell before, well then you haven't seen anything yet.~ *The scene opens up on Jon Kano's vast estate in Las Vegas, Nevada. It sits on a cliff overlooking Sin City, and its impressive contemporary design of the house makes quite an impression. The scene fades out and then back to the back of Kano's house. Jon Kano is standing on this deck overlooking his property. He is just standing there, looking into the distance, not saying a word. Kano's lovely wife, Kristin, comes out of the house and puts her arms around her loving husband.* Kristin: Honey, Duke is here. Kano: Why the hell is he here? Kristin: I don't know. Do you want me to tell him to go away. Kano: No. Send him back here. *Kristin slips back into the house and a few moments later, Duke Williams comes out on the porch, in all of his cowboy attired glory. He stands next to Kano.* Kano: Hey Duke. Duke: Jon, can I ask you why you are here and not on your way to Louisiana for Turmoil? Kano: Duke, I have been married for six months to the most amazing woman in the world. I love her with all of my heart. Do you know how much time I have spent with her? Three weeks. I am taking some time to spend with her. I flew home about 1 am Saturday morning and I plan on leaving in a few hours from here. Duke: Alright, I just got in town to make sure you were coming. I am a little concerned. Kano: About what? Duke: You haven't been yourself lately. You and I aren't going out drinking together. You sit in the corner of some hotel bar by yourself until like two or three in the morning then you go to your hotel room until we have to leave the next day to go wherever. Kano: Shoot bullets through me. Duke, do you see those buildings being constructed over there about three clicks to the right and about 6 and a half miles out? Duke: Yeah. Kano: That is a casino out in the middle of nowhere being built on a brand new road being financed by my company. Five years from now, all of that road will be filled with shops and affordable housing. And all of it will be owned by me. Duke: What the hell does that have to do with anything? Kano: So why do I put my body though hell, night in and night out? Duke: That is something I never got about you, hoss. You are independently wealthy and yet you put your body through hell. Kano: I think I might know. Duke: And that reason would be what, hoss? Kano: I love wrestling. I love everything about it. I love the history, I love the tradition. I love seeing young bucks trying to make a name for themselves. I love to perform in front of thousands. I do what I do because I love doing what I do. Duke: That is some pretty deep sh*t, hoss. Kano: Well I guess I should be getting to Louisiana here pretty quick. Duke: And what about Fusion? Kano: What about it? Duke: You have a match with Corey Williams and Semore Smoke. Kano: Cool, I know. Well, I can only say one thing. I am on the card, so I'll wrestle, and I'll wrestle to the best of my God-given ability. And why is that? Because I am WHAT REAL LEGENDS ARE MADE OF. Duke: Well I need to get back to the airport to catch my flight back. You coming? *Kano pauses for a moment.* Duke: If you don't, Jon, Nelson will fire your ass. Kano: Wouldn't be the first time. *The two friends share a laugh as the scene fades to black.* ~Yep. It's about time for all hell to break loose.~
