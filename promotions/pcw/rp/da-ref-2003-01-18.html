Title: Da Ref!! Featuring: Duke Williams Date: 1/18/03 It’s the Dew Drop Inn bar and grill in Missoula Montana. Sitting around the old bar on their hard strolls are five rugged outdoor men and Duke Williams. This is his local watering hole, and in ring pictures of Duke grace the wall. Bartender: So these there fellows all thing they have what it takes to out talk you? Williams: Yup their talking all about who’s got the better show and rating this and ratings that. Billy: Yea but Sunday morning all of them all listening to Duke Williams. Bubba: Malice is rated through the roof. Them boys ain’t got a virgin’s ass on Duke. Frank: Them hosses are like a Saturday morning cartoon and Duke’s like Saturday night live. Sam: Frank your fucking drunk shut up. Frank: You shut up. Bartender: How about a round for everybody. The bar erupts in cheer. Williams: Everybody knows I have the highest rated show in the world and those guys are just wannabe’s. But none of that matters all that matters is I get to take my frustrations out on Kannon this Tuesday. Bartender: You got a match. Williams: Better hoss, better. Sam: What’s better then that? Are you getting a blowjob during the match or something? Williams: Well maybe not that good killer. Frank: I bet your gonna shot that bastard. Sam: Have another one Frank. Frank: Fuck you. Williams: No, I get to be the ref. Bartender: The official? Bubba: No dip shit he’s going to be the reference man. Bartender: Fuck you bubba. Williams: I’m going to dawn the zebra stripes. Charlie: You ain’t gonna turn into one of them dang old rule book guys are ya? Williams: Actually I’m going to bring some rakes, trashcans, and chairs into the ring then put on a blind fold. Sam: Let them kill each other then sort out the bodies. Frank: Like Nam’ man only in a square circle. Why do they call it a square circle that don’t make any sense. Sam: Your drunk Frank. Frank: Fuck you and pass me another can of eight-ounce nutrition. Williams: I’ll take a little more ammunition too hoss. The bartender passes out cold cans of Colorado Cool-Aid. Williams: Shit hoss things are going to get whiskey bent and hell bound. BAR: HOSS!!!!! =w= ---- View Duke Williams's Biography
