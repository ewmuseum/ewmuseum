On a rainy day...

26 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 26 June 2002 Change... Now I’m not talking about the sort of change you get for a dollar. No the sort of changes I am talking about is the changing of the world. Some people love change, they believe it brings variety to their lives. Others hate change; these people are called conservatives. They are content on what they have at the moment and don't want any changes to be made to their lifestyle. However if the majority of people were conservatives then nothing in this world would progress, in fact we would still be in the caveman era, and so in steps the Radical. The Radical is the one who makes the change on an impulse, who doesn't think about the consequences. They just keep pushing the envelope of progress until they change the thing they set out to change. Indeed there is always change happening somewhere in the world, no matter how small, it is still a change... So what side of change are The Dark Messiahs on? Are they Radicals or Conservative? Well as we have seen time and time again, the twisted duo keep switching between 2 personas. It is a conflict of change within themselves, who will win? Well last time we saw The Dark Messiahs it looked like their evil side seemed to be winning the war. Now with a possible charge of grand theft auto on their hands after stealing the taxi, it looks like The Dark Messiahs are on their way to the Fusion arena. We join them now in a restaurant just across the road from the Thompson-Boling Assembly Center... The scene opens up at the entrance of the restaurant. It is pouring with rain outside, so much so that some water has started to leak in through the entrance door. People run for cover from the rain outside and cars drive past splashing the pedestrians as they go. Indeed it is very dull day outside but inside the restaurant it looks very cosy and warm. It is not extremely busy, but it is busy enough to keep the restaurant staff working. The lighting in the restaurant is just right, it's not too bright and it's not too dull. There are a few booths at the back of the restaurant but mainly wooden tables, which have most of the couples, sat round. As we look around a beautiful blonde bombshell waitress walks past us carrying a tray of drinks. We quickly follow her as we dodge through the crowd of couples sat at the wooden tables. We soon come to the booths at the back with the majority of them being filled by families. As we walk past each we see all of them trying to struggle with their kid who is fidgeting in the seat and moaning about how they want their dessert. We soon stop as the waitress places the drinks on the table. We look over the waitress' shoulder and see none other than Steel and Stone sat on the table... Waitress: (Smiling and placing the drinks on the table) Okay so that is 1 Carling and 1 Becks, are you ready to order? The waitress takes out her order pad and a pen as Steel looks up at Stone from his menu. Stone shakes his head... Daniel Steel: (Smiling) We are still deciding, thanks. Waitress: (Smiling back) Okay, I’ll be back in about 10 minutes, enjoy your drinks. Steel and Stone nods, and the waitress turns and walks over to another table. Steel looks over to Stone again, he puts his menu down and his face becomes serious... Daniel Steel: (Serious) So what did you do with that taxi? Stone as usual scribbles something down on the notepad he has by his side... Daniel Steel: (Reading out note) Don't worry about it, I hid it somewhere it's fine. (Still not satisfied) But what about fingerprints and such like? Another note from Stone... Daniel Steel: (Reads out note) We aren't on the police records, we don't exist on any records. Remember we ran away before we could be put onto any government records. (Sighs) Thank god for that, one thing we don't want now is trouble with the law. I mean how the hell did we end up in that taxi? One minute we were outside that sleazy motel the next we are on the motorway towards (name of town here). Stone thinks for a while then something comes to him. He quickly scribbles something down and then hands it to Steel... Daniel Steel: (Reads out note) You think it could have something to do with that dream 2 days ago? (Screws up note and shakes his head) Will you stop talking about the dream already, it was just a dream, it meant nothing. Now let's forget about the damn dream and the taxi for now, we have much bigger fish to fry. Stone looks confused and scribbles a word down and passes to Steel with a confused grin on his face... Daniel Steel: (Reads out note) Big? (Laughs) Okay, we have other fish to fry. They may not be big but they are still significant, now you sure you are ready for the match at Fusion? I mean your throat hasn't got any better, and to be honest with you I am getting damn tired of reading all these notes. Stone looks at Steel and grins as he scribbles down on the notepad and hands it to Steel... Daniel Steel: (Sighs as he reads out note) The medical staff said it would be the day after Gold and Glory until I got my voice back. And anyway I know how much you like the sound of your own voice. (Laughs) But in all seriousness, are you sure you are comfortable to fight at Fusion? I mean I don't want to jeopardise our chances at Gold and Glory if your not feeling too good. Stone sighs as scribbles again and hands it to Steel... Daniel Steel: (Reads note) How many times do I have to tell you I am fine. I am in a lot better state then the rest of the other guys in the match, I mean you see Turmoil. Akiyama slaughtered them all, he is definitely a favourite going into Gold and Glory no doubt about it. (Nods) I agree, he is very dangerous even though he is 1 man, but remember even though powerful he is still 1 man, and like everyone he must have a weakness. Sure he can say he is invincible and all that, but the truth of the matter is they are all just playing in a world they don't understand. Looking at all the promos each team has been doing, it looks like all they seem to care about is themselves and how hard the battle royal is going to be. Yes the battle royal is going to be tough but one thing you shouldn't be concentrating on is yourself. This may sound strange but if you think about it, it makes perfect sense. I mean if you start concentrating on yourself, you realise how hurt you are and this affects you mentally and before you know it you are flying over that top rope. Going into that match all that will fill our minds is the thought of holding about those tag team titles. We don't care about ourselves, we don't care how much blood we spill, because we are willing to sacrifice anything just to hold those tag team belts! Stone smiles and scribbles something down on the notepad and hands it to Steel... Daniel Steel: (Reads note) Fusion and Gold and Glory is where we start our legacy in the PCW. We may not have had much success so far, but what better way to turn it all around by winning those PCW tag team titles. (Agrees) You took the words right out of my m... Before Steel can finish the familiar scream of pain comes from his throat. The entire restaurant completely stops as every one turns to face Steel and Stone who are now on the restaurant floor holding their heads. The waitress from earlier along with the manager comes running over. As they do Steel and Stone rise to their feet. The waitress and the manager stop in their tracks, as Steel and Stone stare them straight in the eye... Daniel Steel: (Picking up beer glass behind him) This beer is too damn warm, fix it! The waitress steps forward to take the glass of Steel but before she does Steel throws the beer all over her. The waitress screams and runs off to the kitchen area. The manager looks outraged... Manager: (Angry) That was uncalled for! Steel looks shocked with this manager's out burst. He looks at the manager who is quite tubby guy with glasses and smiles evilly... Daniel Steel: (Sarcasm) Oh I am sorry, here (pulls out a 20 dollar bill and hands it to manager) a deposit for your plastic surgeon. Manager: (Confused) Plastic surgeon? What do I... Before the manager can finish, Stone has grabbed him by the throat and lifted him in the air and chokeslammed him straight through the closet wooden table. Steel quickly grabs one of the seats and smashes it straight on the manger's face, which is now oozing with blood. Steel looks down at the manager then spits on his face. Steel looks around the restaurant with an enraged face... Daniel Steel: (Shouting) You see that! That is why we are going to be the ones holding those PCW tag team titles at the end of Gold and Glory! We don't care who we hurt, all we car about is those tag team titles! The rest of the WrestlingTattler community has written us off as possible winners! Not one of those freaks even mentioned our names! Well just you wait because after Gold and Glory The Dark Messiahs legacy begins, as the PCW Tag Team Champions! Don't believe us, just watch us, watch our legacy begin! With that Steel and Stone walk out of the restaurant and into the pouring rain. The rest of the restaurant watch them go with pure terror in their eyes. The scene fades to black...
