Voyage to the Gold: Part III

9 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: May 9, 2002 The scene opens as Johnny Heart lowers his hands holding an extinct and even cold pipe before his big face. 'Hmm...Turmoil and Fusion are cancelled...quite intriguing,' Heart said. 'A terra-cotta bust I believe. It's just another ploy for Tyler Nelson and his Ultimate Authority rejects to try and steal the PCW World title though...but hey, if it gets us more time off before our match with the Politically Inept...more power to him,' Jack Valentine responded. Valentine and Heart are both wearing bright pink robes and trying to sound more sophisticated than their little PINK minds are capable of. 'A lot of bad thoughts have been imposed on us from the fans, other wrestlers, commentators and pretty much everyone in the entire world Jack...why do you think people think that we are homosexuals?' Johnny questioned. 'I don't think they actually believe that...I think that they are just jealous of our devilishly handsome looks, great sense of style and our ability to be thought of whenever someone mentions the word PINK...or even sees something that is PINK!' Jack said. 'Can you imagine what it will be like if we win that contract at Supremacy for PINK Lemonade to be the official drink of PCW? It will be complete chaos!' 'Indeed Jack, indeed,' Johnny added as he slapped Jack on the ass in a very feminine fashion. The two continued prancing around their house in a very girly manner. They acted as if they were royalty in Great Britain...with their noses held high and talking in a refined approach. However, when a commercial appeared on the TV screen for Supremacy, highlights of Politically Incorrect and other Ultimate Authority members were shown... Johnny growled as much as a man of his accomplished manner and voice could growl. 'Look at that...a waste of a commercial, and a waste of my perfect vision,' Johnny said. 'If they just would've shown us posing in the dressing room, or pictures of our perfect asses, more people would buy this pay-per-view...now I doubt anyone will buy it because WE weren't in the promo,' Jack replied in a very serious tone. Jack's face remained grave. Very grave. Johnny was amused at those little venomous outbreaks of the fatal Jack Valentine. Again they felt themselves being utterly forgotten. But they didn't feel dull and I didn't even feel like losers. That last strikes them as strange at this distance of time, in regard of their tender years and of the depressing hour which will follow if they do not capture their dream of becoming PCW champions. Yes, the ambiguously gay duo did have a serious side. It may have even been that serious side that made the fans cheer for them Sunday on Sabotage, or it just may have been the fact that they hated Politically Incorrect so much. It was that old glory that opened the series of companions of those morning rides; a series which when the fans cheered for them, their heads got even bigger. They were losing focus...their heads were getting too big and they weren't focusing on the match at hand. This was very similar to the demise of Jack Valentine in late 2001, and Jack seems to be at fault for the slow development of The Heartbreakers. His head is getting too big and he needs to get his head back in the game and focus on winning that first PCW title. But they really were geniuses...they had made it seem to their opponents that they were a little on the “femme” side. Their opponents have become homophobic around them and that will be to their definite advantage at Supremacy. Maybe they were actually like that in “real” life, or maybe they just portray themselves to be that way to get their image over. Regardless, it was working...and despite what everyone thinks, they are the favorites going into this match...at least in their heads. They paused in their flamboyant pacing and flashed their white teeth at us most agreeably, but the upper part of his face, the shadowed setting of his eyes, and the slight drawing in of his eyebrows gave a fatal suggestion. As the narration suggested that they weren’t really like that outside of the ring, and they only portray that, they seemed to hear it and agree, however no one could be sure with the way these two act all the time. They insisted to us that the first and only time one of them had seen a title up close was back at Sea World in Orlando, Florida. Jack Valentine and Ed were the remaining two...Bob and Shamu would have nothing of Jack walking out with a title that night though, as the whale assisted Ed in capturing his record setting 3rd Extreme Championship. Since, Jack has not come close to even seeing a title belt, and now Jack and Johnny will be up close and personal with Politically Incorrect this Sunday. Were they nervous? Maybe. Were they excited? Of Course. Would they walk out as champions? Only time will tell. “Lovecraft and Powell? What kind of names are those?” Johnny humorously asked. “Gay ones at that,” Jack said with a laugh. “If those Ultimate Assholes try to come even close to the ring this Sunday, I will be sure to unleash a threshold of PINK! at all of them,” Johnny said as a unique and awkward silence came over the room...seconds later Jack laughed. Jack and Johnny exchanged but a few more words as they walked down towards the center of the town. In the warming tempestuous dawn of spring, they strolled along musingly, disregarding the discomfort and nervousness of their upcoming match, the depressing influence of the situation, the desolation of the empty streets in which the dry dust rose in whirls in front of us, behind us, flew upon them from the side streets. The gay men they encountered the previous night had all cleared the street as they walked into the rising sun a new day began...leaving the old behind. Perhaps symbolically representing the leaving of the old which had no titles and walking into the new era of domination in the tag team division. Nonetheless, the TO BE CONTINUED sign came up that has become a familiarity in this series as the scene faded to black. Stay tuned for Part IV...
