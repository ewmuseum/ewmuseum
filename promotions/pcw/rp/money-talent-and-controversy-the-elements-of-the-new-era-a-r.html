Money, Talent and Controversy: The elements of the New Era. (A reintroduction of sorts.)

undated

Date: Live in the Now! ::Our scene does not open in the head office of CCW Enterprises, but rather in a modern looking mansion in the outskirts of New York City. The camera cuts to an overhead shot and slowly moves past the mansion, even from this far away distance we can see that the mansion is huge. The camera moves past the house and fixes on a large swimming pool to the back of it. There, next to the pool, we can just make out an object from out satellite’s-eye view. The camera begins to zoom in, slowly at first but growing in speed by the second, eventually the camera is zooming in so fast that we can see nothing but black. Our sense of hearing comes into play instead.:: BRRINNG BRRIIING! BRRINNG BRINNG! ::There is silence for a moment, all we can hear is the sound of somebody pursing their lips together in thought. That pattern of thought last for only a few seconds before it becomes a sudden outburst.:: 'That's it! You're FIRED!' ::Those are the first words we will ever hear from Corey in the new PCW. Somewhat ironic, considering just a week earlier Corey himself was fired. Our vision is finally restored, this time at a normal height and distance. Straight away we see the unforgettable figure of Corey Williams. Slender but muscular body, young looking face with just the hint of facial hair, the same can be said for his chest. And that smile, greeting at first but with an underlying tone that says 'if you mess with me I'll show you who I am.' He lays back casually on a banana lounge to the right of the pool, but with a stature that makes him seem a little superior, the sun gleaming off of his long blonde hair. He is not dressed in his controversial bear fur coat and tight leather pants that we had often seen during his days as the most controversial athlete in PCW, the newcomer to PCW who was turning all the heads. Neither is he dressed in the sharp business suit that we saw during the last month or so of the old PCW as the dominating controversial billionaire, the man who used his power to force people's heads in his direction. Rather Corey is dressed in somewhat of a merge of the two, leopard skin underpants/trunks showing his controversial side and a strictly-business looking; jet-black cell phone that constantly reminds us of the power this young man wields. And to top it all off are the dark black glasses that completely hide Corey's eyes. A trick he often uses to manipulate people or make them feel uncomfortable. Corey has often claimed that nobody can control the fans like he can and by hiding his eyes he can select what emotions and parts of himself he wants to reveal to the fans. Likewise he can give the impression of one emotion and then suddenly let down the barrier and show you what he was feeling all along, seemingly snapping in an instant. Corey snaps the lid of the phone shut and stares at it for a few moments. His head slowly turns and his eyes slowly shift towards the camera. He brandishes the phone, holding it up for the camera to see, his hand slightly shaking as if the phone possesses some kind of power itself.:: Corey: This phone is power. ::Corey looks back down at the phone, cradling it like a new-born baby.:: Corey: A billion dollars concentrated into one 10-by-5-by-3 rectangular prism that acts as a gateway to the World. I can do anything with this phone..... ::Corey removes the dark sunglasses from his head and looks up to the camera with wide eyes.:: Corey: .....anything. ::His eyes gleam and that semi-evil grin comes onto his face.:: Corey: I can fire someone or get someone fired, I can hire a lawyer or bribe a judge, I can make it legal to hunt whales or illegal to cook pasta. I can decide what I'm gonna have for dinner.... ::Corey places the glasses back on his head.:: Corey: ...Or have someone killed. ::Corey smirks and rubs his chin in thought at the same time, and with the glasses back on we can't tell if he's serious or not. His thoughts detain him for a few moments before he is suddenly back in action, his eyes snapping up to the camera and his mouth going into storytelling mode.:: Corey: And so the 'new' PCW begins, a brand 'new' roster of 35 or so men. There's this strange atmosphere in the locker room, a huge feeling of excitement, everywhere I go I hear people promising to the fans that they 'feel like a new man'. But for God's sake, I don't care about your love life! These idiots keep going on about adapting and having success in the new era, everyone's feeling really proud about themselves for moving onto the new era, but I've been LIVING in the new era for months now! All these 'so-called' legends are so damn obsessed with the past that they don't even see me standing in front of them. You can go out onto the streets and ask anyone who is the one that gives them what they wanna see, go ask someone who is the future of this company, go ask someone who started and brought on the new era. They're not gonna say Duke Williams, they're not gonna say Jon Kano, they're not even gonna say Big Alan Steele. Because whilst all these people at the front office are applauding Big Alan Steele for moving PCW forward, he's really just playing catch-up when compared to me. Whilst all these suits in management are patting Big Alan Steele on the back for winning the PCW World Title and creating a turning point in PCW, they don't seem to realize that that turning point was actually created by me just a few weeks earlier when I won the PCW World Title in a most CONTROVERSIAL fashion. ::Corey turns over on his banana lounge so that his back is facing the sky.:: Corey: Those management losers are just as dumb as the fucking fans, the only difference is that I can't manipulate them, corrupt them or make them do or feel whatever I want like I can with the fans through my acts of controversy...I finally learned that. Although controversy will get you so far, sometimes a little force, a little talent and a little power are needed to take charge. They WILL realize that is I, not Big Alan Steele, that will lead PCW into its prosperous era of controversy. I mean, what can BAS do anyway apart from scratch his ass, beat his chest and look good in a toga? Big Alan Steele doesn't have one new school bone in his body, he has no attitude, no style and no class. When I look at Steele, I don't see the future of PCW, I see a guy who might as well be one of those 'so-called' legends like Duke Williams or Jon Kano. Corey: You know, those guys who couldn't achieve their dreams in their prime coz they weren’t good enough so they cling onto their spot and hope that someone will just GIVE them a title..... ::Corey shakes his head disgustedly, obviously ignoring the fact that only a few weeks back Jason Snow GAVE him the World Championship:: Corey: I hate guys like that. ::Corey gets up and begins to walk towards his mansion.:: Corey: You know, those guys who take up valuable roster spots and do whatever they can to stop moving PCW forward so that no one will realize just how much behind everyone else they are? ::Corey opens a glass-sliding door and steps into a room of his huge mansion. He keeps walking without stopping, but we have just a fraction of a second to stop and look around, to admire its incredible size, before the camera must catch up with Corey.:: Corey: You know, those guys who expect us to call them legends for sticking around because they haven't done enough in PCW to retire? ::Corey continues walking through his huge mansion until finally he reaches the end and walks out the front door at the opposite end of the house from which he entered. The camera cuts to an overhead shot. Corey looks up at the camera and spreads his arms out wide, he yells something which we can only just hear because of our distance from him.:: Corey: PCW! I present to you.......... YOUR LEGENDS! ::And with that the camera begins to zoom out even further than it already is, slowly at first but becoming faster by the second. We zoom right out until Corey's huge house is a dot. The camera keeps on zooming out, now we can see the entire city of New York. Pretty soon we can see the entire US, and eventually the entire world as it is seen from space. By now the camera is zooming out so fast that everything is a blur, it continues speeding up until finally all we can see is nothingness, complete black. Again, all we can hear is a voice, one voice, Corey's voice.:: Corey: Thank god we've got some superstars as well. !The End!
