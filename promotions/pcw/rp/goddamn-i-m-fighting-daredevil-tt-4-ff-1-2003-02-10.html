Goddamn I'm fighting Daredevil! (TT#4/FF#1)

10 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2-10-03 [The scene opens to a Range Rover cruising through theAustralian Outback. The shot zooms in closer andcloser until it finally cuts to the inside. AJ isdriving with Adam riding shotgun. Jaslene and Amiraare sitting in the back chit chatting. Then there’sHank. Sitting in the cargo area, wedged between Adamand Jaslene’s luggage, he rests his head on theheadrest between Jaslene and Amira.] Jaslene: Damn, I don’t want to leave just yet. Itfeels like we only just got here. Amira: That’s because you only just got here but dutycalls. Adam’s booked solid through the wee- [Jaslene quickly slaps her hand across Amira’s mouth.Hank is taken aback as he looks cockeyed at Jaslene.Adam raises an eyebrow before dropping the sun visor.He flips open a covering to reveal a mirror that heuses to look at Jaslene.] Stryker: Jas…honey…what did she mean by, ‘bookedsolid’? [Jaslene shifts her eyes around trying to come up withsomething in response to Adam.] Stryker: Well? [Jaslene, finally realizing defeat, lets go of Amira’smouth before sinking back into her seat. Adamcontinues to stare at her via the mirror. A nervouschuckle escapes Jaslene.] Jaslene: Well…uh…you see…um…how to put this? Amira: Just come on out with it Jaslene. Stryker: Yeah Jas, just come out with it. [Jaslene takes in one last deep breath.] Jaslene: You got a couple of calls while you and AJwere out. One of the calls said was to inform youabout a six-man tag match involving you, Erik Erraticand El Romantico against Rust, Storm and Windsor. Trinidad: Well that doesn’t sound too bad. Stryker: Damnit, I hate tag matches. I don’t likebeing in them. I’ve spent most of my career trying toavoid tag team wrestling. Trinidad: Why? Stryker: Because I could always hold my own but my tagteam partners always seemed to fall short, fromLatrisha Lamonte in the DWO to J.L. Knight in the AWR.Not too many people can maintain my high standards fora partner. I’ve only had three real partners, people Iwas proud to team with. People I respected… [Adam quickly flips the visor up and looks out thewindow. An uncomfortable silence falls over the caruntil Amira finally breaks it.] Amira: Well? Stryker: Well what? Amira: Who were the three people? Stryker: Oh, Kensai in the DWO…we were champions. GinaMuniz in the DWO…we were champions. And Chris Kinsleyin the PTC…we should have been champions but we gotscrewed. [And once again the silence sets in. Jaslene sits backin her seat and breathes a sign of relief. Hankreaches over and licks Jaslene’s cheek.] Jaslene: EWWWWWWWW! [Everyone starts laughing as Jaslene playfully pushesHank’s head away. She wipes the saliva from her cheek.AJ seems to be contemplating something.] Trinidad: Hey Jaslene? Jaslene: Y-yeah? Trinidad: Amira said Adam was booked solid through theweek but Tuesday and Friday aren’t really solid forthe week. Is there something more? Jaslene: Um… [Adam swiftly reopens the visor and again stares atJaslene.] Stryker: Don’t tell me there’s more. Is there Jas? Jaslene: Well kind of… Stryker: Kind of? What do you mean kind of? Jaslene: Well aside from your match with Skylar Greenon Tuesday and your six man tag match on Friday…youhave a couple of personal appearances on Wednesday andThursday. Stryker: Wednesday AND Thursday?!? [AJ bursts out laughing as Jaslene slowly nods herhead. Adam snaps the visor shut in frustration.] Stryker: I hate PR appearances!! Trinidad: I know, remember that time in the DWO I hadto cancel on due to the GTT and you had to take myplace? Stryker: Don’t remind me… Trinidad: That was hilarious! I remember you wereextremely pissed after that. Stryker: Yeah real f**king funny a-hole! Trinidad: Yeah that was great! [Adam scowls as he slinks back deeps into the seat.] Stryker: Well where is this going to happen Jas? Jaslene: Uh, Wednesday is at a local wrestling schoolin the Los Angeles metro area… Stryker: That doesn’t sound so bad, and Thursday? Jaslene: Um…we…it’s going to be at a…[Jaslenecoughs]Chucky-E-Cheese’s… Stryker: Where? Jas…come on what did you say? [Amira starts to chuckle a bit but throws her handover her mouth to try and control herself. Jaslenesighs.] Jaslene: Chucky-E-Cheese… [Adam does a double take as AJ starts to laugh again.Even Amira is having a hard time controlling herselfnow. She bursts out into laughter falling forward overher knees.] Amira: Can you imagine…haha…Adam ata…hehe…Chucky-E-Cheese’s? HAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHA!! Trinidad: Oh man that really sucks…but it’s really,REALLY, funny! HAHAHAHAHAHA!! Stryker: Yeah, yeah…laugh it up all you can. One ofthese days I’ll have my comeuppance. Do you hear me?MY COMEPPANCE! [Adam shakes his fist in AJ’s face. Everyone in thecar, even Hank, is laughing at the thought of Adam ina Chucky-E-Cheese’s. AJ wipes a tear from his face.] Trinidad: But in all seriousness, how do you feelabout this match with Syklar? Stryker: Well I’d be lying if I said I wasn’t theleast bit nervous but of course I’m confident. I meanhell I’m Adam-f**cking-Stryker. No one can stop.Skylar doesn’t stand a chance. Amira: Skylar is a former PCW Champion though Adam,confidence can’t hide facts. He’s faced some toughopponents. He did beat Jackson Rust. Stryker: So what? So did I? Jaslene: But Jackson also beat you once. Stryker: So what? Just a minor set back. What titledid Green hold Amira? Amira: The Cruiserweight Champion…twice I do believeoh and he was an Extreme Champion as well. Stryker: Cruiserweight Champion? Isn’t he my size? Amira: Uh yeah I think so, six foot eight I do believeand I think two hundred and eighty pounds. Stryker: He’s an inch taller than me and about tenpounds lighter than me? Great I’m fighting the PCWequivalent of Chris Kinsley. Cruiserweight my ass… Trinidad: Just because he’s some giant who likes topush around kids smaller than him doesn’t make him anyless of a threat Adam. Don’t let that fool you. Stryker: But come on AJ! A blind man? A man who flipsaround and whatnot? Goddamn I’m fighting Daredevil! Iwonder if he’d sign an autograph of two for me… Jaslene: Adam! Focus! Amira: I don’t know about that Jaslene, Adam is right.It’s like he’s fighting Daredevil. Maybe this is thebest mindset he can take into the ring. Trinidad: How so bebe? Amira; Well think about it, what does Daredevil do? Heuses lighting quick strikes. Maybe Skylar is going touse a lot of moves like that. Stryker: Doesn’t matter what he does, as soon as hefeels the pain that the Restraining Order causes he’llget grounded quickly. I’ve faced people like Skylarbefore, granted none of them happened to be blind butoh well, minor details. Jaslene: How does it work? Stryker: How does what work? Jaslene: How does a blind man wrestle? Especially insuch a competitive fed such as the PCW? Stryker: I don’t know Jas but I’ll be sure to ask himtomorrow night when I take a break from beating hisass into the mat. Trinidad: Don’t get too confident Adam. You never knowwhat could happen especially since you are new andreally didn’t make any friends Friday when youattacked Tessa Windsor and Chris Storm. Stryker: Storm… Amira; AJ! Show some confidence in your friends. Jaslene: Yeah seriously AJ, Adam did beat you did henot? Trinidad: No he didn’t! And he’ll be the first to tellyou that he didn’t win the match…right Adam? Stryker: You are correct sir, I may have beat theliving hell out of you but I didn’t win the match perse. Trinidad: Yeah well you’re still oh and one againstme. Stryker: Step back into a ring some day and that willchange. Amira: Ohhhhhhhh! [Adam looks at AJ with anger but suddenly cracks asmile as his laid back demeanor comes shiningthrough.] Stryker: Nah I’m just kidding though Stryker vs.Trinidad two would be a massive event. Trinidad: No doubt. Stryker: Which reminds, are you going to be in thestates anytime soon? [AJ looks back to Amira. She nods.] Trinidad: Yeah I could be. Stryker: Just remember that whenever you are in thestates, you have a home in Los Angeles. Trinidad: Thanks man that means a lot to me. Heh, everget the urge to come back to Australia just look meup. We can have another night like the one we had withHank. Stryker: Nice! [Adam and AJ shakes hands in the front. Jaslene rubsHank’s head as the scene fades out.]
