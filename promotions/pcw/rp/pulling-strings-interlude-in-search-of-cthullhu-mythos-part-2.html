Pulling Strings - Interlude - In Search Of Cthullhu Mythos - Part Two; Subsection A

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: April 4th, 2003 (I&#039;m 15 BABY!) Dante Jackson is sort of a man on a mission, he doesn't technically understands why people down play him as “one dimensional” nor does he get the fact that no matter how good he is in the ring, his exposure, his talents on the microphone has made him just a piece of skin waiting to be dug into. For now however, he tends to sit back as the scene opens up towards a comic book store in the lower portion of Houston. The eye gauzes back as he leans up against the building's right corner, eying as many pass through with smiles, grumbles, or plain out asshole-ness. A quiet spring day oddly, as sometime around this time last year, he was just on the come up, waiting and watching as Xavier Kannon started to make it big, started to become the “Man” he is today. The shot fades inward towards a Target superstore, the exact same building Dante was leaning up against earlier as he walks into the ladies section, holding his hand towards his chin, obviously in thought of some kind. Dante Jackson: “...wonder if Tray will want something.” He stands there as a store clerk bumps into him on accident, the burnt red colored shirt looks a bit worn out as stretch marks are apparent from the trained eye as Dante grunts at the clerk who basically responds back with a good old' Texas sized... Target Clerk: “SCREW YOU!” The Target clerk whips his back facing towards Dante as he walks away from him, leaving Dante with his mouth partly open. Not only shocked, but feeling like a baby just beat him in Def Jam Vendetta. Dante Jackson: “AMERICANS!” He stands proudly with his hands on his hips, before he turns his face to see almost everybody currently inside of the store looking at him crazy. Dante Jackson: “...will defeat the Iraqi's! Ya'll didn't let me finish my statement! Yeah, that's right! America will defeat Sadaam Hussein just like I'll beat The Pharaoh and Gaz Green for the Cruiserweight title, and dub myself...EMPEROR OF THE CRUISERWEIGHTS!” Everybody just shakes their heads and go back to doing whatever they were previously doing. Dante shrugs his shoulders as he walks over towards the game department of the store, heralded right under electronics as to the left of the PS2 set up happens to the Bookcase and the CD Rack, compiled with artist ranging from Foo Fighters, Killa Mike, and *NSYNC. Looking over towards the PS2 set up, right next to it happens to be an XBox of the same set up, just a lethargic controller which the Overweighter would mistake for a giant black M&M. He smiles as one, about the age of seven or eight begins to play NBA Live 2003 while the other on the XBox plays a demo version of Tom Clancy's Splinter Cell. Dante Jackson: “Jeez, I still can’t remember the last time I played one of these things…” He walks behind the kid, starring deeply into the computer animation before looking back behind, to see if anybody had even come near him, or recognized him. Towards the left, nothing but an empty aisle, checked with various shelves and items atop of one another. To the right, the exact same thing, although the boxes and items arrange in variety, they are of little importance, just mere interior decor. And in the middle, a completely tiled white floor, usually moped daily and always taken care of. Remember, this is Target where your compact disc are never always the edited version (thank you Wal-Mart!) So, if he was watching his back for anything, Dante Jackson was safe. Not that safe, but still safe in the least part of the sense of being. He taps the kid’s shoulder, his eyes focus and rather glued to the television set stationed inside of the large PS2 poser with Sam Fisher plastered on the right side. Dante taps once more, but the kid is still unresponsive towards the Lyrical Messiah. Citing desperate measures, Dante yells out, causing the kid to shake back and looking both ways. Dante Jackson: “Look everybody…IT’S RUSH MASON!” Child: “RUSH MASON?!” The small kid flashes through the aisles with the wink of an eye as he is in search of the ever forgettable, and humiliating Continental champion back in November - December. Dante snickers to himself, before busting out in laughter, howling even. Dante Jackson: “Rush Mason! If there was a Rush Mason of the Cruiserweight division, then it’d have to go to…” The Lyrical Messiah looks around, checking various game consoles, before his eye catches the latest PCW video game entitled, Decimation. He picks up a copy, checking to see if he was even on the cover, but then sets it back down. Dante Jackson: “Why was the background so black? You could barely even see me! I mean to take the same poser from the Pay-Per-View last year just exhibits your laziness towards things!” He smugly places the copy back, before coming back towards the Playstation Console, finding the same kid from before, a New York Rangers cap twisted backwards around his head with a red Yankees shirt and a pair of black shorts. The former Continental champion stares a hole into the child, before spinning him around, snatching the cap off of his head. Dante Jackson: “Listen kid, there’s a lot of other machine….a-a-around…here…” The thing that has Dante baffled at the most happens to be the kids choice of hair color. Dante Jackson: “GREEN!” Child: “What, just because I’m from Las Vegas, play pranks on people and like wearing the color green makes me look like a St. Patrick’s Day Bart Simpson don’t it?” Dante Jackson: “I wasn’t going to say…” Child: “No bub, you’ve said your share. And now, in the words of Gaz Green, ‘WHERE’S YOUR HEAD AT?”Dante looks around dumbfounded. Dante Jackson: “On my neck and body maybe?” Suddenly, the kid in a knee jerk reaction picks up something out of his pocket before hurling it at Dante, splashing in directly in the face. He groans, before falling back into the pottery section, knocking over a shelf and causing a plant to topples over and falls directly onto his head. He slumps back down, almost in a euphoric state before getting back up, the water raining down from his forehead as he looks for the child who busted him in the face with a giant water balloon, but stops his tracks. He turns towards the game, looking as the demo screen flashes quickly, holding its position as Dante flicks the controller, searching to see if PCW has a demo on the disk. Passing through Def Jam Vendetta, Tony Hawk Pro Skater 4, and NBA Live 2003, Jackson looks at the PCW Decimation logo, before checking to see if it’s playable. He taps X on the controller as a small montage plays instead of the intro as Dante stares at it for a second. Dante Jackson: “So, um…where’s the game?” He taps X once more, but this time the screen fades into the small little opening. Store Clerk: “Hey, it’s a video man. Didn’t you see that?” Dante shakes his head. Dante Jackson: “No actually I was paying attention to the demo part, since this is a DEMO after all.” The Store Clerk nods for a second, before looking directly at Dante, eying him up before shaking his head. Store Clerk: “Hey…aren’t you that Dante Jackson fella?” Dante Jackson: “YEAH! Why yes I am! How nice of you to figure that one after FOURTEEN SECONDS of contact between one another. You should have just come upon the spot as soon as you saw my face, you could have gone like, “Hey! That’s Dante Jackson, the next Cruiserweight Champion! He’s a legend to the black community!” The Store Clerk only shrugs his shoulders before walking off as Dante stares back into the game, looking at the video of PCW Decimation once more, finding three people on main screen. Totally Tubular Tommy with the PCW Continental Championship slung over his shoulder. Xavier Kannon with an evil smirk. Clyde with the PCW World Title around his waist. Dante slams his fist along the console as he tosses the controller away, once again pissed off that he isn’t atop one of the best in PCW…even for a video game. Dante Jackson: “When the black man has oppression, its been used before. When a white guy uses it, it’s called a damn GENIUS IDEA! But I can’t be mad can I? I mean, look at me, a man going for a third title in PCW, and the second one in two weeks. I could be a double champion by Sunday! Sunday of all things!” He chuckles to himself, before exposing himself to full blown out laughter as he begins walking away from the PS2 and XBox game consoles as he turns towards the book section of the Target. Raising an eyebrow, he picks up a comic book, written in black and white as he flips through the book entitled: “The Emerald Menace”. Dante Jackson: (Reading) “Paradise City.” A cloaked figure stands with a rose in his hand. Man: “I’m sorry, Natalia.” He tosses the rose upon the tombstone as the rain begins to pour. He walks and walks and walks more before being stopped. A young red headed boy comes from behind him, Boy: “I’m sorry to hear about it, David.” David: “It means nothing Clark, nothing.” He walks off as the scene changes towards the high rise of the city, as a black man stands in a suit, pinstriped, looking over the cloudy sky as rain continues to fall. He smirks. Black Man: “She dead?” Right Handman: “Ye-yeah boss, she is dead!” Black Man: “Good. See to it that our handy man gets the job done on him tonight.” Right Hand Man: “T-t-t-tonight?! But boss, Menace ain’t comin’ out tonight!” Black Man: “He may not, but I am…for like a promo from PCW…I am…the OMEGA!” Dante quickly closes the book, smugly looking at it before just shaking his head. Dante Jackson: “Bah, like a Pharaoh promo, Time but no substance. Or is that a Gaz Green?” The Lyrical Messiah shakes his head once more as he begins on his path towards Fusion, with the scene fading towards a blank white, almost making it look like the entire thing was a charade. And thank God it wasn’t.
