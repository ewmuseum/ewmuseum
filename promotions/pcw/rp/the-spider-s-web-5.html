Date: Febuary &#039;04 The Spider's Web.5. Today, ‘home’ lacks a certain familiarity. From where I’m sitting, on the floor of the bathroom I can hear nothing. No dogs barking, no singing, nothing. I know they’re all dead, I know Mikey’s in the building, so I go to the left hand side of the door and wait for death to come knocking on my door. Raw knuckles smack the door causing the floor to vibrate for seconds. I’ve forgotten everything. I don’t know whether I’m supposed to answer or not. Or whether I was supposed to leave the door open. Either way I hold the door knob with my left hand and pull it open. Standing on the left hand side, counting 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, anything to keep my mind off the fact that I’m about to die. 6, 7, 8, 9, 10. No, not dead yet. 11 12 13 14 Still alive, I take a peak out the door. This wasn’t planned so I don’t know how this will go down. 15 16 17 18 And a heel creeps back and I fall back on the sofa. This is what you’d call ‘bad acting’. 19 20 21 The leg’s inside the door, taking 1, 2, 3 steps in my direction. A face peeks in through the crack in the door. 25 And I’ve lost count. I stand up again, left hand side of the door, and pretend to be surprised. This is just another planned death. This is Jesus Christ all over again. Come on, as if that whole death thing wasn’t rehearsed. Two more steps and a familiar pair of legs stand in front of me while I beg for mercy. I yank her dress and pull myself to my feet. Autumn? Her brown eyes narrow and she takes a seat on the sofa. I mean.... I thought you were.... “Going to kill you?” .... “Terlayne, we’re all a part of it, number 43, number 89, I’m just another pawn.... A pawn who’s been paid to kill you....” Autumn digs her nails into a handful of ginger locks, then looks down at nothing in particular. “There’s a way out, but that involves you dying too.” She takes a few steps in my direction. “Then again, there’s another path that ends with me dying.” OK, which way ends in you dying again? And she makes an arrow between two ramshackle buildings to a bus. A bus for the mentally insane. Destination: Las Vegas. “You take that bus, you take it to Las Vegas, and you kill yourself, you kill those old people.” And you die.... How? “You’ll see....” And she makes a beeline trail to the door. “One of our paths will end, it’s up to you to decide which one.” And she’s gone.
