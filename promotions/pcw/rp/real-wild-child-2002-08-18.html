Real Wild Child

18 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8/18/02 -The Malice Man stands in front of a black backdrop with a Tuesday Turmoil logo on it in white. Duke is dressed to kill wearing black wranglers; ostrich skin boots; a Tyler Nelson T-shirt; and his logo black Stetson hat. Standing next to his is the legendary Jeff Marx broadcast partner of Duke Williams.- Jeff: Standing here is the Malice Man Duke Williams. -Duke grabs Jeff’s hand and raises it up to Duke mouth.- Williams: Hold the damn mic up hoss or I’ll slap you like this is Fusion. Jeff: Damn it I haven’t done this in years just chill out. -The two veterans start over.- Jeff: I’m standing here with “The Man Of MALICE” Duke Williams. Duke any thoughts on your up coming Turmoil match? Williams: Oh my god is it really 1980?? I wrestler is standing back stage conducting an interview. IS this the Goddamn twilight zone? Am I having an acid flashback??? Jeff: No this is really happing…are you on drugs now? Williams: Shut up Marx. Its Malice Time. Oh I used a catch phrase there’s a novel concept. Put it in the history books baby. Time my in fact change everything….everything but the Malice Man. It’s 2002 and its still the same old me. Jeff: Minus a lot of gold and plus about twenty pounds what are you up to three hundred now? -Jeff pats Duke’s gut.- Williams: Marx I swear to God I’ll slap you like an Xavier Kannon whore. Jeff: Ellie? Williams: Bingo. Pharaoh, Chase, Akiyama, you kids…yea that’s right I called you kids. You kids have no idea what this business is all about. You have no idea of the history I have paved so you three piss holes can come on TV each week and ramble about f*cking mummies, Gods, and Christ if I know what. To me you three drag queens are an insult to all I have done. Jeff: Yea but you realizes your going to lose to at least one of them right? Williams: Marx don’t make me get all Jerry Springer white trash up on your ass. Don’t think I haven’t seen the fans board. Don’t think I haven’t heard the whispers back stage saying I’m done. Jeff: Did you see the first round results for the GTT? You lost there too. Williams: Jeff your not helping. Jeff: Sorry. Williams: Cus kiddies I hear it all of it. These people are supposed to be my fans and who do they pick? Fucking Akiyama? Oh yea he’s a god. A god of cum swappers in the local adult bookstore. The god of toilet skid marks when you flush a giant god like crap. Here’s a provirbe for you. Akiyama I hope, pray and dream that thou shalt have your partner in a kissing marathon be afflicted with severe halitosis, you festering boil on a whore's bottom! HUH F*ck you. You know Marx I think fighting these inbred, mongoloid, from Poland I may need a translator. Jeff: Why’s that? Williams: Because they don’t seem to get it. Here’s a novel idea talk into a camera and communicate. Comm-un-ic-ate look into the camera and say something. Enough of this symbolism and other none direct bullshit. -The camera starts shaking as Duke grabs it.- Williams: You see this I’m looking right at your and communicating. Huh Fuck You!! Pharaoh you wan to prove your more then a one trick pony you need to go through me. Being the senior wrestler of this company let my tell you something nobody else has the balls to tell you. -Marx looks at Duke who just smiles.- Williams: You smell like urine. I’m not teasing you either. You smell like a glass of piss. A dirty diper. Now I understand you’re a mummy or some shit but the rest of us would really like if you took a shower. A nice long one. Use some lye soap or some shit. Pharaoh you are an indescribably witless subhuman and a primitive, urine-reeking depravity of genetics. A fucking joke and a stinky one at that. Jeff: What about Chris Chase didn’t he just beat you like a week ago. Williams: Oh yea he beat me. Who hasn’t it? I get no respect and hoss I’m tired of it. Who’s the hot flavor of the week I’m going to put over this time? Who’s the hero of the month?? Who’s management’s pony this time??? Fact is fact and I am going back to tradition. If I’m booked to lose this man I’m going to take the booking into my own hands and finish it my way. This is the real deal and these three juvenals ain’t got shit on me. Jeff: So Chris Chase won’t beat you. Williams: Give me that mic your done talking. Chris Chase is the most boring wrestler ever. This kid has the personality of a used tampon. This kid’s only bad habit is breathing. You dig my jive hoss. If you gave this kid a penny for his thoughts he’d give you change. Jeff: Are you afraid of the God Akiyama? Williams: This guy is as much of a God as Paul Rubens has making a career comeback. What is this a f*cking story book or pro-wrestling?? Why doesn’t this guy do us all a favor and take a piggyback ride on a buzz saw hoss? Akiyama you still don’t impress me. I’m not one of these guys who think you’re the next big star. As a matter of fact I don’t understand why I need to be in another match with you. Akiyama hasn’t anybody told you God wants spiritual fruit, not religious nuts. Jeff: Are we going to meet Kern for dinner now? Williams: Hey I’m in the middle of something here. Jeff: Please end it. Your going to start repeating yourself again. Williams: Fuck you and fuck this little cunts come Tuesday I will show the world I am retired from losing when I send these children Whiskey Bent and Hell Bound. Jeff: That’s the long winded truth.
