Pinky Swear

15 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Title: Pinky Swear Featuring: Rent-a-Hero Date: 11-15-2002 ..:/::..Our story begins within the confines of City hospital, the largest and most state of the art medical facility in all of The City, where hundreds of thousands of people are saved everyday from whatever affliction there may be. The building is tall and regal, beautifully illuminated under the blanket of the night sky as each floor burns bright with light emitting from each and every room. Within these rooms, we find different patients all housed and healing...recovering or awaiting care...although each and everyone of their stories might be quite interesting to hear, there is but one room in particular that is our focus...room 390...the room of Amy Lois Elektra A-Hero...the eight-year old only daughter of the PCW's only superhero...Rent-A-Hero. She lays limply in her bed, her head turned to the right as the heavy covers bury her, the tiny beauty gazing outside at the twinkling jewels in the sky called 'stars' as she plays with but a tiny strand of her soft blonde hair. Obviously with no care to look at anything to her left, which would reveal a modest hospital room with two wodden chairs, a tall emerald green potted plant, and a ceiling mounted TV; Amy cares nothing for any of these items as she keeps her eyes fixated on the outside world....he mind racing with thoughts of the man who put her there...Xavier Kannon..::/::..Amy:'I want my daddy...'Rent-A-Hero:'You called...' ..::/::..With a sudden jerk, Amy turns her head to the left...her hospital room door opened wide enough for her to see her father...Rent-A-Hero. A smile that seemed non-existant on her face now forms without conflict, her blue eyes now fixated upon her protector, who is enwrapped in a spandex replica of the Green Lantern costume, complete with mask. He holds his composure, trying his best not to just break down in estatic joy of seeing his daughter as slowly makes his way into the room, smiling at the sight of his little girl as he approaches her side near the bed. Our hero's face is quite different from previous times before....when he started...his face was clean cut and smooth like a babies bottom....but as time can weather a mountain, so it has our hero. His face now brandishes various war scars, bruises, and Band-Aids from his brutal battles as of late....yet all that can be looked past just by looking into that aqua blue eyes of his and catching a glimpse of his pearly white smile, the sight of his daughter enough to make him forget the pain outside of the room...and Amy's sight of her father, enough for her to break free of her pain, even if only for a moment. Our hero, uncharacteristically normal at this time, opts for a soft, comforting tone then his usual extroverted and heroic voice, saying to Amy..::/::.. Rent-A-Hero:'Hi honey...I came off the road as soon as I heard you came off critical condition. How are ypu? How's your neck?' Amy:'It's still owwie Rent-A-Papa....I can't lift my head up. Doctor said it wasn't strong enough yet....so I just look at the starries....' ..::/::..Rent-A-Hero opens his hand, caressing his fingers through the blonde silk strands of little Amy's hair as she noticeably looks much happier, her heart filled with protection now that our hero is there by her side. Rent-A-Hero just gazes at her...happy she's okay, but furious deep inside at how she got that way...that image of the Hallmark through the announce table replaying over and over like a skipping CD through his head. That one move destroyed her neck...and Xavier Kannon showed no remorse, no pity...using her all in an effort to get to our hero. He moves his hand to her tiny hand, taking it in his as he kneels over a little bit as they continue to converse, our hero taking a deep breath before saying to her, Amy hanging on his every word as she lays their, gripping his hand tightly...::/::.. Rent-A-Hero:'Amy....do you remember the bad man who did this to you?'Amy:'Yes....he hurt me so bad Rent-A-Papa....it hurt so bad.' Rent-A-Hero:'Sweety...I know you think you hurt right now, I know you do....but daddy's in so much more pain. He used you...he used you to hurt daddy, to try to drive daddy crazy baby.'Amy:'Your not crazy daddy...' Rent-A-Hero:'No no no Amy...I am. I am sweety...I'm crazy for you. My whole life....I've always had someone or something to cling to...and then they leave me, hurt me, or I lose it. Amy, the day I adopted you was the day a very large piece of me came back...I felt like the Green Lantern with his ring again....Superman with his cape....The Lone Ranger with his mask, honey. I feel so much stronger when I look in your eyes, and I see you happy...and I see you growing. You are my greatest treasure Amy...and Xavier knew that tryng to destroy you would drive me over the edge. It has. And he'll be sorry he ever did it..'Amy:'Are you...are you...going to kill him daddy?'..::/::..Our hero squats, now looking eye to eye with his daughter with their hands still clutched tight together, Rent-A-Hero's following words filled with overwhelming meaning to him, growing ever more passionate as he thinks and recollects on how this all came to be...::/::..Rent-A-Hero:'No honey...I'm not going to kill him, nooo. See, you may not understand what I'm saying to you now, but you will when you grow up to be a big girl, and your the Lois Lane all the guys are after. See...it's easy to kill someone sweety, but it's another thing to make someone suffer, punish them. Amy, NEVER grow so angry that all you think about is killing someone...I never want to hear that word from your mouth again. BUT....if someone like Xavier, a bad man like him, EVER tries to destroy what you care about sweety...you make sure he pays...you make sure he gets what's coming to him. You stand your ground, keep your chin up, and show whoever it is that hurt you what that last name 'A-Hero' means. You be brave and you stand up for yourself, make sure they NEVER hurt you again. That's what I'm doing Amy....I'm going to punish him for hurting the only thing real to me...you. I have to stand up for myself, or else he'll think he can do this ANYTIME he wants. I know you hate being here, I know you want to go home...but I have to get the bad man Amy, I have to make him pay. It won't be much longer....but when it's done, we'll both be happy.' Amy:'I understand. But can you please do something for me?' ..::/::..That sentence breaks Rent-A-Hero down, our hero willing to do anything to see a smile across the face of his little daughter, his pride and joy..::/::..Rent-A-Hero:'Anything.' Amy:'That you hurt that bad man so bad....he'll never even think about hurting anyone else again, ever. Promise?' ..::/::..Our hero is silent....words not even needed for this pact as he holds out his hand, all his fingers curled up except for one...the pinky. She smiles, reaching her pinky out for his as they interwine and lock, the promise forever sealed between the two as smiles overtake their faces. To Amy...our hero is her guardian, her champion...unstoppable in her heart and mind. To Rent-A-Hero...Amy is his treasure, his connection to anything 'normal' about him. With that promise....our hero must now eliminate what it is that has disrupted the balance between the two...no matter the cost. With that, Amy sweetly asks her guardian, clutching his hand extra tight to persuade him..::/::.. Amy:'Stay here tonight Rent-A-Papa...you'll fight the monsters under my bed won't you?' Rent-A-Hero:'Always Amy...always.'..::/::..With that, our hero kneels, Amy laying her head back and closing her eyes as she keeps a firm grasp on the hand of Rent-A-Hero...not letting him go for anything as he just continues to gaze at her as she prepare to drift into dreamland...knowing full well that when the sun comes up tomorrow...his quest to end Xavier Kannon begins anew...::/::..
