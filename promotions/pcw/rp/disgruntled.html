Disgruntled.

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Late! Click. “… and it’s a beautiful Thursday morning here in WARM radio land, a pleasant 72 degrees outside and not a cloud in the sky. But later in the day keep indoors as the temperature should soar in excess of 90. It’s 40 minutes past the hour, and…” The rumpled figure bolts out of bed, a look of shock registering on his sleepy features. “Oh NO!! I must’ve hit snooze too many times!!” The rather normal looking man bolts out of bed and hurriedly begins to go into the rushed motions of getting ready for work. The sleepy figure of his wife, still hugging the pillows, groans at the intrusion of noise. “Isn’t today that big meeting, too?” she manages to say, smacking her lips. The man stops, true terror registering on his face. “OMIGOD!! You’re right!! Oh jeezus… and the boss said if anything goes wrong… Oh crap! OH CRAP!!” The woman rolls onto her back, stuffing a pillow onto her face, yet her single word response is clear. “Loser.” “You’ll see! You’ll see!! Someday… I’ll be a big success in the wingding business world!! And then the world will be our oyster!! I promise you!” “I thought you made woppets?” No… that was last month, honey. Please keep up.” “Loser.” “A little support would be nice, Melinda… I mean, I work hard to keep us supported…” The woman shoots up, her eyes flaring. “SUPPORTED?! When we got married, you promised me the moon! And all I’ve seen is the filthy lower-east side of San Jose. You promised me diamonds!! Yet, when I had my engagement ring appraised, it was only cubic zirconium.” “Hey! You aren’t supposed to do that!” “Shut up, Pete! You are a total loser!! And you’ll always be a loser!!” “You’ll see... after today’s big meeting…” Melinda rolls her eyes and plops back into bed, pulling the covers up over her head. Pete, hurriedly begins his panic-induced dance to get himself ready for work. A few minutes later and he is a bit disheveled, but passable. Grabbing his briefcase he runs to the door, opening it up, but pausing before leaving. “You’ll see, Melinda,” he says breathlessly, “After today, when I pull down that big Healey deal, you and me will be living the high life!” A muffled response, “Whatever.” Pete races down the stairs, past a muscular man in dark blue working clothes, the patch ‘ACE Cable’ sewn above his heart. Pete turns around, curious. “Hey… what’s up?” “Oh, not much,” replies the grinning, blonde man with the nice tan, “just have to lay some cable in apartment 206. Apparently Ms. Griglock’s cable is out …again.” The man winks and smiles lasciviously. Pete blinks. “Huh, third time this week… Well, Melinda is home… just knock and she’ll let you in.” “Oh, I know!” Pete cocks his head, but decides to make haste, sprinting for his car. Gears shift, tires peel, and smoke pours out the back of Pete’s 1993 Geo Prism, as his white-knuckled hands grip the wheel. Weaving in and out of the downtown traffic, he actually manages to make good time, only a couple of minutes away from his destination. Suddenly, he is forced to slam on his brakes. The tiny compact fishtails as he stands on the brake, gritting his teeth and closing his eyes, bracing for impact, as a modern SUV bares his path. The little car shudders to a stop, less than a foot from the back end of the truck in front. Pete lets out a sigh and wipes eh sweat from his forehead. That was close!! He chuckles as he thinks of the consequences that would have ensued should he have hit the SUV. But now he grows impatient, as his time is precious. Honking his horn, he rolls down the side window and sticks his head out to see down the street to see exactly what the hold-up is. His eyes open wide in surprise as he tries to make sense of the sight before him. An object not unlike the classic UFO design hovers above the stoplights, metallic tentacles dangling down, each end crackling with exposed energy. A cackling man with wild, white hair slaps at the buttons in his acrylic bubble, laughing with each push of a button. In the street below, is our own Big and Bold hero, Big Alan Steele, dripping with perspiration as he tries desperately to fend himself from a horde of mechanical menaces, robots built by the hovering man, terrible in their design and deadly in intent. Each brightly colored robot seems to have a different weapon, which they fire in unison at Big Al, the Greek demi-god forced to dive, leap, and slide out of the pathways of the horrible projectiles. This all creates a huge traffic jam, as it takes place in an intersection. Pete clamps down on the steering wheel and withdraws his head, finally remembering to breath. He looks down at his watch, the time causing him to choke again. He slams his car into reverse, but can’t move anywhere, as traffic has already backed up behind him, boxing him in. Pete begins to rant in anger, slamming his fists into the wheel. “BATTER UP!!” Outside, there is a great rending of metal, then the famous catchphrase, “Hi-Keeba!!” A mere nanosecond later is a cacophony of explosions, gaily colored robot parts streaming and arcing into the air past Pete’s car. When it looks like the projectiles have ceased, Pete again sticks his head out the window, daring a look. There, standing in the intersection with a big grin on his Mighty visage is Big Alan Steele, wielding a traffic light post as a baseball bat, obviously just sweeping through the man in the UFOs mechanical minions. The man screeches horribly, his seething anger mixed with surprise and fear. Steele steps forward, grinning, as he picks up the arm of one of the dismembered robots. “And now, Lord of Mechanical Puppetry, I shall defeat you with the ultimate irony… your own Hades-spawned creation’s weaponry!!” “Fool!!” the man cries out, “Though you have won this battle, I have yet to lose the war!! But for now, I must digress, as I WILL live on to fight another day!! So long, Big Alan STUPID!! BWAHAHAHAHA!!” The rockets underneath the UFO-ish vehicle roar to life, spinning around and zooming away. Steele raises a cannon and presses the trigger on the forearm… “What is this madness… BUBBLES?! Bah!! I shall defeat you with yon BARE hands!! Hi-Keeba!!” The UFO races down the right side of the street, various people screaming and diving for cover. It dips down for a moment then crosses to the left side of the street… directly over the ’93 Geo Prism. Pete feels the heat of the rockets scorch his car as it quickly flies overhead. Pete instinctively ducks, looking across his car and into the rearview, watching the maniacal man hover away, still laughing crazily. Pete sighs and checks his watch, smiling. Surely, now that that was over, traffic will start up again… WHA-THUMP!!! The hood of the Prism sinks low, tipping the car momentarily forward. Pete pitches ahead, slapping his head off the steering wheel, just barely getting a glimpse of a sandaled foot and a meaty calf, square in the middle of his car’s hood. Big Al is gone just as quickly, leaping off the small compact and continuing his chase after the UFO-like object. Pete spits out a curse and looks through his windshield. A large foot indentation has caved in his hood, viscous steam pouring out along the front edges of the car. “Oh no…” He twists the key in the ignition and guns the pedal. An audible clunk can be heard inside the hood, even more steam escaping. “Oh no!!” Traffic starts to move in front of him. Traffic starts to move to the side of him. The still cars behind him begin to honk in annoyance. Pete desperately tries the key again, a horrible grinding sound the reward for his efforts. Now the steam has turned to smoke, a black ichor spewing out of the front of his car, filling the street with a wretched smell. “OH NO!!!” Pete leaps out of the car and leaves it behind, sprinting down the street, furiously pumping his arms and legs in his desperate attempt to make it to work on time. Four blocks… three… two… one… he dashes into the lobby of the high rise and slides to a stop in front of the elevators. ‘Out of Order.’ He checks his watch and notes a mere minute or two to go. With the strength and endurance of a man fighting for his proverbial life, he takes the stairwell three steps at a time, huffing and puffing. He explodes through the seventh floor door, tripping on the plush carpeting and sliding across the floor to a set of shined Italian shoes. “Ah… I see your men take the initiative when it comes to boot-licking. Absolutely exemplary!” Pete rolls onto his face, into the grinning face of Doctor Rupert Healey as he sips a bourbon… and the scowling face of his boss. “Griglock!! What is wrong with you?! Are you high on goofballs or something?!?! You’re a mess!!” Gasping for breath, Pete, internally had to admit that was probably true. His face was red and puffy. His suit was soaked with perspiration and torn in a few places. He rolls over and tries to straighten himself as his boss frowns. Healey chuckles after tossing back the last few drops of his bourbon. “Relax. My mind is made up in this matter. All I really need form you people is the contracts my lawyers sent you, then we can put International Wingdings out of business forever… as you fly the Healey Corporate Empire flag!” I have to apologize for Griglock sir… normally he is a very reliable…” “Tut tut, Mr. Thompson. I understand the need for mind-altering substances more than anyone, as I see your man here does. Now, the documents, as I have a busy day of drinking and whoring to get to…” The boss frowns helping Pete to his feet. “Okay Pete. Get the papers…” Pete pats down his suit, all the color draining from his cheeks. “NOOOOOOOOOOOOOO!!!” His briefcase sits in the smoking Prism… which bursts into flames. “GRIGLOCK!! YOU’RE FIRED!!!” Pete drops to his knees sobbing, as his boss grovels to Doc Healey, gulping at his refreshed drink. “I’m so terribly sorry, Dr. Healey!! I promise you this is rather unusual…” “Oh, think nothing of it. In fact, I rather enjoy a good firing or two. However, the loss of the contracts is quite a concern to me…” “I’ll have my lawyers draft a new one up immediately…” “No,” sighs Healey, though the glee shines through the façade, “I think I’ll just go through with my other offer… to International.” “You.. you can’t!!” “Done! Tootles!” A distraught Pete slinks back home on foot, carrying a load of personal items in cardboard box, muttering to himself. If only that Big Alan Steele hadn’t stepped on my car, I would have made it to work on time, with the papers, and been the hero. Now, because of Big Alan Steele, I have no job and no car. He creeps up the steps to his apartment building, making the long journey to his front door. He jingles his keys trying to unlock the door… hmmm. That’s funny. My key doesn’t work… His haunted eyes level off, finally seeing the note pinned to the front door. “Dear Pete. It’s over. Since I’ve been signing your meager checks, it is MY signature of yours that is legal. So I went and put the apartment in my name, plus had you already sign the papers filing for divorce. And no, you won’t be invited to Ace and my wedding. Loser. Good riddance, Melinda.” It starts to rain as he shuffles outside. Fire burns in his eyes, heat rising in his cheeks. If only Big Alan Steele hadn’t ruined my car, then I would have a job, a wife… a home… a life… Big Alan Steele ruined my life!! That bastard! It’s all his fault!! SNAP!! That was the sound of Pete’s sanity breaking like Louie Anderson’s suspenders. Jingle-jingle-jingle! The fat, greasy man behind the counter of the Stone Bone Pawn Shop looks up behind his cage. In walks Pete, wild-eyed and muttering about Big Alan Steele. “Yeah…” begins Pete, twitching violently, “Are you the pawn shop hat specializes in… recycled supervillain gear?” The greasy guy straightens up, taking a long and deliberate look around his shop at the various rocket sleds, armors, back packs, doomsday devices, spandex and death trap paraphernalia. “No, I only sell the Queen Mum’s jewels. What does it look like I carry?!” “Excellent… I’ll take that… a couple of these… one of those… oooooh! I like the look of that… do you take credit?” The man behind the cage grins ferally, baring yellow teeth. “Indeed I do.” Knock, knock, knock!! We rejoin our Big and Bold hero, Big Alan Steele, as he slowly opens the door of Stately Steele Manor, chewing on the huge bite he has taken out of a Dagwood-sized sandwich. He cocks his head in confusion, at the seething sight before him. Pete heaves with heavy breathing anger, decked head to toe in a fiery red-orange spandex vulcanized rubber suit, complete with Kevlar abdominal augmentation and muscular definition. A dazzling array of high-tech gimmickry extend from his person, deadly weapons aimed at the head of our hero. Pete throws his head back and screams out loud in insane laughter, that echoes all the way across the street. Steele raises his sandwich and chews slowly, puzzled. “HAHAHAHAHA!!!” roars Pete, pointing an accusing finger into the chest of Big Al, “You took everything from me!! You stole my livelihood, my life, my love… and for THAT YOU SHALL PAY!! Oh yes, I made promises… promises I intend to keep!! Soon I shall defeat you and soon I shall rule the WORLD!!” “Listen,” Big Al starts out, hastily swallowing his bite, “If this intrusion is about yon newspaper payments, twas merely forgotten. I swear to you, that come the next pay period…” “What?! No, you fool!! I’m not here to collect paper money!! I’m here to collect your life!! A fair trade… your life for mine!!” “While certainly the bargain doth sound even, as one life equals another, I have to admit a certain fondness for my current conditions…” “What?!?! WHAT?! No, you IDIOT!! I’m a supervillain!! Here to kill you! I am… DISGRUNTLED PETE!! BWAHAHAHAHA” Steele sniggers, barely containing his laughter. “Nuh-uh!!” “Foolish moron!! Then I shall teach you my intentions, soon the world praising the name of Disgruntled Pete for ridding us of the curse that is Big Alan Steele! En guard!” The steel doors of the prison slide open for Disgruntled Pete, who shuffles forward to the yellow line, severely restricted by the heavy chains. He looks around menacingly at the platoon of guards, all leveling their shotguns at him. The head security guard looks at Pete nervously, gulping hard before speaking. “Time for your daily exercise, Disgruntled Pete. Let’s go…” Pete snarls at the men, who all jump in alarm, taking steps backwards. He smiles, satisfied with their reactions and shuffles down the thick yellow line, through the many heavily guarded doors to the sunshine of the outside. The Prison courtyard of Arkham is abuzz with excitement as Pete makes his appearance. The guards all continue their diligent marking of Pete through their sights, as the whispers begin… “That’s him! That’s the guy!” “He beat Big Alan Steele!!” “No way!! He must be powerful!!” “Watch out, boyo. He’s one baaaaaaad mutha fugga…” Disgruntled Pete looks back at the guards with disdain, using his most menacing voice. “Think we can do this WITHOUT the chains today, kids?” “Sorry… warden’s orders. We are forced to give you an exercise period each day, but that’s it.” Pete snarls and clanks over to a picnic table, already a slew of gawking supervillains coming over to get a glimpse, a few daring for autographs which he signs begrudgingly. The Joker comes close, gulping heavily and nervously addressing Pete. “Uh, excuse me Mr. Disgruntled… but I just HAVE to ask! How did you do it? How did you beat… HIM?!?!” “You wouldn’t understand the complexity and strategies involved,” Pete dismisses with a clinking wave of his hand, “It was a superbly sublime strategies that I doubt your simpleton mind could comprehend.” “Oh, uh, yeah. Silly, silly me. But how did he get away?!” Pete stands, roaring out in anger, making the entire courtyard freeze in panic for a moment’s breath. “A simple miscalculation in my formulas for my death trap!! Must I be reminded of my failure by a whelp such as you?!?!” Joker almost looks like he’s going to cry and he scampers off. “Psst!! Hey, Disgruntled Pete! Can I… you know… see it?” Pete looks at Dr. doom warily. He taps the wood of the table imperiously, a wry smile on his lips. “What’s it to ya?” “Seven cigarettes and a coke!” Pete nods in satisfaction and slowly reaches inside his orange jumpsuit. The guards all jump to attention, watching his movements intently… “Here it is.” “Wow!! I didn’t believe it was true until now!!” A Polaroid photo of Disgruntled Pete, standing proudly with his foot resting on the chest of a prone Big Alan Steele, his tongue lolled out to the side and his eyes closed in unconsciousness. Steele looks pretty beat up and Pete is radiant in his smile. We now re-rejoin our Big and Bold hero, Big Alan Steele, as he sits back in his favorite chair in Stately Steele Manor, along with the usual suspects. “You know,” says Sue, deep in thought, “normally I’m against your ideas, but what you did for Disgruntled Pete was really… um… what’s that word? Opposite of ‘awful’…?” “Nice?” Grace Steffi offers. “Yeah, that’s it. Nice. That was nice of you, Al.” Big Al crosses his arms gruffly, “Well, what other choice did I have? After he threw a punch, failed miserably and began sobbing upon my toga, I was informed of his entire terrible tale, and admittedly I felt the bitter pangs of regret for him, as well as a sliver of partial responsibility. Twas the only honorable way to defeat the embittered supervillain, as well as lend a helping hand to the troubled soul trapped under the weight of his wretched life.” “Yeah, but you took a hit in the credibility for that guy!! I bet all the other supervillains are sooooo jealous of him right now…” “Well,” Big Al finally smiling, “I did enjoy his elation at yon solution. I hope that small token of mine serves him well as he serves out his sentence… Hi-Keeba!!” Cut back to the prison, where Disgruntled Pete looms over a cowering Juggernaught, who is polishing a pair of shoes. “… and I want to see my face this time, you hear me, punk?!” “Y-y-y-es sir!! Sorry sir!!” Pete allows himself a small, self-satisfied grin.
