Burning Car, Man That Stings

12 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: May 12th, 2003 How does it feel to be trapped inside of a burning car? Yes, this is the answer most are not probably wondering since not a single person decided to give me a thank you card, a simple apology or thoughtful “Get Better”. Its all in the culmination of things to come as the body begins to wear down and thus, you end up like a man named Shanahan. “NURSE!” Catcalls would follow the Nurse every time her little uniform just happened to catch my eye. Even though I was almost mummified in bandages still did not make me feel things, “down below”. By the reading of the doctor’s chart, I had sustained a severe gash upon my cheek which would need stitches, a few broken ribs and a crushed ankle which would be completely swollen by the time they took the cast off. Not to mention the abundant cuts upon my right arm, the number of gauze pads holding down the bleeding and a piece of glass that got stuck inside my earlobe which had to be removed. To say I went through hell is one thing. To say I’ve been through hell, had toast with the Devil and then flew my ass right back up here would be a more correct way of putting it. The bleeding stopped a few days after Thursday, about Sunday or so as I continued to watch the Kings get thoroughly dominated by the Mavericks in overtime, and double overtime before the game was over. Meanwhile, I watched a few more bits and pieces from old Violence Jack film tapes, game time as if you were a mathematician and studied. I broke them down, move by move and almost nearly broke a finger in writing it all down. The blood would rush every time a Turmoil replay was found and while watching Fusion from this little room in Yemen, I kind of figured for myself that this was more than a feud between me and Jack. It had become personal. Over the same damn thing we wanted. Twenty five pounds of gold, written in red markings with the stains of Alan Kriegman, Jeremy Howard and Xavier Kannon upon it, basked in a gold hum. The PCW Continental title, or for lack of a better word: stardom. They say when you bleed, you continue to make use of your time, walking, stopping, moving and halting before the body catches up with your actions. Blood loss is apparent; the smell of death creeping upon you and once more everything your priest told you would happen when your time is up. It felt like that after the roof of the car collided with my body. Pain shot up in joints, muscles, in rows of teeth, in painful doses as nobody could hear my screams. Just Bruce Shanahan, just Dorn Christopher Tracer the Third, and myself. “Mr. Jackson? Are you okay?” The Nurse was already speaking plain English, watered down from simple words of mixed in Afghan and other dialects. By the time she saw me standing up trying to hop out of the window, her eyes were fixed upon me as if I were some chrome magnum specimen just thawed out of a freezer. “Mr. Jackson!” Is it me or did that just sound as if she saw me naked? “You must put some clothes on now!” “Why?” the fortuned voice of mine could not even describe what I was really going through right now. To be booked, hurt as hell and yet still wanting to defend my Extreme title is something called dedication. I bet if I were Osama she’d understand my feelings! “You’re healed up and just about naked!” Oh shit. I just remembered the Hospital issued diaper was still on me and I was naked from the waist up. But then the first part she said struck me even more. I’m healed up. Healed up. Healed.
