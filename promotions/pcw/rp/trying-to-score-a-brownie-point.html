Trying to score a Brownie Point?!

undated

Date: not entered Pink… and The Heartbreakers… If there were two things in the world that I’d have to select as being the most annoying things… they would the color pink… and the Heartbreakers themselves. Why? Why o’ Why have I decided to go along with these two things as being the most annoying? Well, the very fact that they just happen to get on our nerves all the time… that really spells out the reason by itself. THEN… there’s the fact that they tried to steal all of our PoP Limelight… and the fact they tried to record a very dismal record… and THEN, even after that… there is the very fact that they’re the two boys who just happen to be holding the PCW Tag Team Championship at this very moment in time. Today is going to be a little different from every other day within the lives of the ‘Pretty Cool Guys’… its going to be something that resembles all the Spoof-ness that people have come to hate… and yet, it’ll retain all the seriousness that people have come to love… erm, maybe that’s the other way around? Well, either way you’d like to look at this, myself… Ryan Moore and Stimpo, will be trying to construct the very thing… that the Heartbreakers spent so long to defeat… and the chances are, we’ll be able to sort out everything within this very episode! So… it begins, on a brisk Summer Afternoon… kinda warm, kinda calm, a fresh breeze pushing past the citizens of the city. Where are we at the moment? Isn’t it going to be kinda obvious where a team like myself is going to be… something like four days before their companies Pay-Per-View? Regardless, I found myself standing outside a ‘Coffee Shop’ on a busy street… busy in the way that people and cars were filling up about seventy-five to ninety-five percent of my view. I sighed… longly and loudly, as I remained standing outside this establishment with a fresh cup of Coffee in one hand and a Cell Phone in the other. “Geez…”, I muttered to myself, being unable to understand why it was taking my fellow stars so long to get their cups of Coffee from inside the established Coffee House. I looked from left to right, taking a few seconds to appreciate whatever emptiness I could see. “… I wish I were back home… back where the streets weren’t too packed… back where I could do whatever I wanted… without the hassle of coming out, without the hassle of all these people, running up and down the streets at the very same time.” I continued, sounding more like a moaner than anything I’d ever been able to experience before. Impatiently, I looked down at the Rolex on my right wrist, the sun hit the glass panel and made it impossible to see the position of the watch-hands. Again, I sighed and looked up to the sky, my face beginning to show the unhappiness that had been buried inside of me for the better part of the last few days. Unable to show any signs of ‘weakness’ to my friends, I sucked it all in and walked back into the Coffee House, finally able to establish the time at being “2:44pm” and… looking on as my apparent “Friends” sat down at two individual tables signing autographs for various teenage girls. I began to feel… angry… maybe even jealous, my right eye began to twitch… I was beginning to enter the zone… something of a zonal rage. I could feel it all rising up inside of me… and I was about to strike… when… “OH MY GOD!!! ITS RICH SHERWIN!!! THAT SEXY BITCH RICH SHERWIN… HE’S AT THE DOOR!!!” It was the scream that I’d been dreading… and one that made me realize it would have been a lot safer to just wait on the outside of the place, even if there was a good chance I’d be waiting for something like two to three hours. I really would have preferred to have just stayed out of it all… to have just been able to sit down on the outside… even in its congestion, but… that would have only been the case if I knew this was happening inside the Coffee House. I tried to turn away from the advancing mob… but my body didn’t want to respond… running away was the wrong thing… and while I didn’t give two diddy-dilts about that, my conscience forced me to stay and slave away with the same things that my friends were doing. Today was supposed to be the day… that we would be able to dance in the “Purple Flame”… and show those Pink Lovin’ Fools… exactly how you take care of problems. After being led from the door to an empty single table about three feet from Ryan, I was offered a seat and then pushed down to a meal that involved five courses of 8x10 Glossies and a starter of Permanent Marker. I picked up the pen… and awaited the first fan. “La-Le-La-Le-La… Lo-Le-Lo-Le-Lo…”, I said to myself as my table remained vacant for a world-record time of about five seconds. The first fans that came to me appeared to be two young female adults. One of them looked quite hot… very tasty indeed, the other… had their back toward me, so I wasn’t really in the kind of position to be commenting about the way that she looked. The attractive girl stood infront of the table and looked me in the eye… with her large provocative eyes… in something, of a provoking stare. “Um… w-w-w-wassup? Erm, what can I do for you?”, I asked, sounding nervous and worried at the exact same time. The girl laughed a little at my nervousness… I guessed that she was just being polite about something that really shouldn’t have happened. “Hey Rich… I was wondering, as well as having one of those pictures… would you be willing to go on a single date with my friend here?” she asked, sounding quite sincere about it all… and probably just trying to make the day of somebody a little less unfortunate than everyone else. Now, if this girl was cute… but just lacked the fortitude to go out there and ask somebody on a date, then I’d understand and would go out with them just to give their confidence a boost. This “woman” was something that I’m never going to be able to forget in a hurry at all. Peered over the table at the legs of this other girl-like character that was with this fan… I silently cringed as I saw the masses of leg hair that protruded from every inch of them. I tried to understand what would have allowed something that bad to happen… whether she was just having a bad time with her hormones… or whether the invention called a “Razor” never quite made it into her household. “Uhm… well, I’m really not too sure about anything at the moment… you see, I’ve got a Girlfriend who’s living with my mom back in Atlanta… and I wouldn’t want anything bad to happen between the two of us. I’m all for giving you both autographed glossies… but, I’m not going to be able to give you anything within the region of a date… regardless of how grote… erm, I mean, regardless of how much you need it!” Sherwin stammered out, stopping midway through “Grotesque” so that he didn’t say anything that would result in another woman running out of the story crying. The attractive girl didn’t say anything at first and slowly began to walk away… the “Girl” with her back toward me, remained still as I finished signing the single glossy. “I’m… uhm, sorry that I’m not going to be able to help you with… anything… especially the Razors…” Some people experience those moments of madness where they do or say something quite insane… all because they can’t hold it in for any longer. Well, unfortunately for me… this was one of those moments… and upon finishing that line, I burst out into a fit of laughter, pointing down at the legs of this thing and balling my eyes out. It was seriously mean for me to do something like this… but I just couldn’t resist the idea of leaving a shot that so was blatant ‘unused’. Hell… that would be like walking down a street, passing a lost $100 bill and then leaving it on the floor for some other sap to pick it up. I assumed that was going to be the end of the confrontation for today… but I didn’t know what the end would be until this sicko turned around and looked me in the eye. “IT” span around quickly… coming about three inches from my face before pulling back. Shocked by the sheer disgust of character… I fell back from my chair, landing on the floor in a heap. I looked over to both Ryan and Stimpo, who were both now looking at me. I mouthed out, ‘Fall Down’ and they nodded their heads in response before taking a few seconds to fall down due to their own reasons. Fearing for THEIR lives and not ours, everyone within the building began to make their way out, rushing past as the “Good Guys” of the shack managed to crawl past everyone… deep into the back of the Coffee House. “Phew… I never ever thought that would have ended at a sooner time!” I exclaimed, wiping the back my hand across my brow. Both Ryan and Stimpo looked at me before laughing, I assumed they were laughing at the fact we were able to pull off something so vain… and for that, I also assumed that it was a silent mark of respect more than anything else. Despite clashing at the very beginning, Ryan and Stimpo had been able to get on with everything… actually doing what a lot of the guys in bands are unable to do... connect… and become friends. “Well…” I started to say when everything had died down a little, “… I think its about time we deal with the little problem that we originally came out to sort.” Both Ryan and Stimpo looked at on another before nodding and glancing over to me again. This was the moment that we’d all been waiting for… this was the moment, that our minutes of arson would be for the benefit of somebody. Of course… neither Ryan nor myself had been involved in anything of the sort before… so we were silently hoping that Stimpo would be able to take charge and show us exactly what to do. “OK!!!!” I yelled in the same fashion that ‘Commander’ would do when leading his platoon to war, “… The plan is… to break into the “House of Purple” across the road… and burn everything to the ground… and the reason that we’re going to go out on a limb and do all of this… is because ‘The Heartbreakers’ will let us win the Tag Titles… if we’re able to show them, that we silently care about Pink… really, we couldn’t give a rats-ass about Pink… but today, its “Showtime”.. yep?” Ryan and Stimpo agree in the way that they’re going to do this for the hell of doing so. I would have preferred them to be doing this because they supported my plan… but hey, everyone’s gotta have their own opinions… and as long as they don’t start to back out, I guess everything’s gonna be alright? Well, we managed to make our way out of a back-door… avoiding everyone that was patiently waiting on the other side of the complex, waiting for PCG to come out from their little accident and tell everyone that they were OK. I laughed to myself as I thought about the people still waiting outside when night came… and then into the morning. I guess it was a little sad to be laughing at something like this… but my mood needed something to cheer it up, and this was the first thing that came to mind. We all ran through various different alleys… charging through past loose trash bags and loose bags… pushing through anything and everything that happened to come in our way. Ironically, this was the first time that I’d never given a damn about the rules of the law… or rules and general… and yet, I understood that if we were ‘caught’… it would pretty much be the end of our record deal… maybe even our contract too. “Hmmm… I see the outlet you mentioned Rich, its only about five minutes away from here. Shall we proceed across the road?”, Stimpo asked… sounding like he was trying to push his way into control. Usually, I would freak if somebody was trying to discretely push me from the top of the podium… but for a plan like this, I was willing to take a backseat and allow somebody else to drive. Stimpo didn’t take much time to look around… instead, he crouched and ran across the road, keeping low so that nobody would really be able to see him… even in this crowded street. I thought that we should have come back later at night… especially when the amount of witnesses could have been a lot less… but hey, I guess I’m just going to have to listen to the dude in charge. Once he was on the other side, Stimpo backed up against the wall and signalled for both of us to come along too. I looked to Ryan and shrugged a little before taking off, him following behind. “Rich… do… you… think… this… is… the… best… thing… to… be… doing?” he asked, the whole entire line broken up because he was taking breathes between word… and step. I didn’t bother to stop and answer his question at the time because it was more important to get across than stop midway, answer his little question and then get caught by the mob of fans again. However, that didn’t stop me from answering the question when we crossed the road and successfully made it to the other side. Ryan literally jumped up and down anyway… so I couldn’t simply ignore it… figures really, him being the desperation that he is. “So… do you?” he asked, not even giving me as much choice as I’d given you the impression of having just a few seconds ago. I looked around the immediate area, making sure that there was nobody about to report use to the Cops once everything had been done. As Stimpo began to break into the “House of Purple”, I looked to Ryan and answered his question in the only way that I was able to do so. “Ryan… this, is going to be the best course of action that we could ever bring ourselves to have at the moment… it is the one thing that’s going to keep us going… and its one of the things that could make that Tag Team Championship match swing in our favour. You see, unlike us… they won’t reject people who do something good for their cause. They’d allow us to do whatever… and then, when it comes to us and them… we attack, kick their asses… WHAM!!! BAMMM!!! KAPOW!! Its all over… and Sherwin, Moore… PCG too, will be the Primetime Championship Wrestling… Tag Team Champions… of… the… WWWWWWOOOOOOOOOORRRRRLLLLLLLDDDDDDD!” I cried out every single word with immeasurable amounts of enthusiasm. Ryan looked happy at the way I was happy about the plan… and about the outcome of the Tag Team contest come Sunday Night. We looked behind us and saw that Stimpo had forced entry to the building… and then, smoke began to pour out of it all. Stimpo charged out next, laughing his head off… holding a lighter in the air. He took one look behind him before screaming, “GAHH… FIRE… LETS RUN!!!!!” … And as we all managed to escape the scene of the crime, the door on another chapter within the book of PCG is closed. Will this act of arson help us in the books with the ‘Heartbreakers’… or will it do nothing to it? Will Ryan and myself be able to lift the Tag Team Championship with this influence only? Nobody knows… Nobody knows… but I do know, that I’ll do my damnest… to make sure this dream comes true. Until the next time…
