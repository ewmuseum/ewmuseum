With victory, comes defeat!

3 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/3/02 An event: A time of participation; a thing that happens or takes place; each of several contests, making up a sport occasion. There are many names for which it can be called. But when an event is so big, that the adrenaline runs through your body like a waterfall, and then there is nothing that can beat it. Events can be big, small, boring, fun, but at the end of the day, winning is sweet, everyone wants to win, that is what makes events so great. The small crowd roared as the match continued. Anton took his opponent down against with a simple backwards trip. Anton McGonnell is at the National Judo Under-17 Tournament for honorary black belt. Already he has defeated three men, and will soon have the chance to claim his black belt, and maybe even win the whole tournament, things are looking up for him so far, but he must take it further. He knows what is at stake. If he could manage to secure enough points to claim a black belt then he could leave it and start the next Martial Art with his teacher Tsunami Chow-Lee, the greatest Martial Arts teacher of all time. The arena was quite small, more like a large Gym, there was a crowd of about 200 people, and managers were beside the mats cheering their students on. Anton was on the centre mat, his opponent was a sixteen year old, they were about the same size, but Anton was the stronger on the two due to his wrestling career. So far, Anton had dominated the mach and had a lot of hit points. The two began to grapple, Anton’s opponent, who was the same size as him, ducked under Anton and tried to get him in an arm-lock. Though Anton quickly doubled up the turn and put his opponent is an arm-lock of his own. He pulled tightly at first making his opponent shout in pain. He then loosed in slightly and hit his opponent with a back Suplex manoeuvre. His opponent was down as the crowd cheered again for Anton. They had taken a liking to Anton, as they knew him from Television, and he came from around the area. The room was quite large; there were stands over the room, were a crowd of about 200 sat. There were basketball nets at either end showing it was indeed a large gym. The floor was wooden, but was covered by thick blue mats. At the sides there were competitors and trainers watching the matches. There were also tables with drinks and food on them, reserved for the competitors. That was it, the match was nearly over, one more take down and Anton would win. They circled the ring like hungry lions, staring at each other with w passion in their eyes. Anton wore a black Martial Arts suit with an Asian symbol in it. He also wore a red belt, though that did not symbolize his current level; it was only there for decoration purposes. His opponent was wearing a red one with a brown belt that was recognizing his current level. He had black hair and a big nose. He looked quite lanky and pale. “He is leaving his right shin unguarded.” Anton thought to himself as he circled the mat. “If he can get a quick kick in there he will take a second or two to recover, then I should be able to take him down with the Miboatso Take-Down, which he had learned from the great Tsunami Chow-Lee. Anton made sure not to look at his opponents right-shin for too long. Then he would notice it and keep it protected. Anton went back to his green eyes and stared at them. He then made his way into the centre of the mat. His opponent stayed were he was, it was obvious now that Anton’s opponent would be the one making the first move, though Anton would be ready for him. Eventually the opponent, whose name up on the board where the scores were kept was Jeremy Fridley, from Toronto, Canada, made his move. He lunged at Anton rather unprofessionally; though it was obvious he was going for effectiveness rather than looks. Anton quickly jumped as high as he could, which seemed incredibly high. Jeremy went right through Anton’s legs, and when gravity pulled Anton back down, he put his foot out which connected with Jeremy’s shin. He then ran for Jeremy’s stomach and hit him with the Miboatso Take-Down. “WINNER, ANTON MCGONNELL!” Shouts the head judge who was an old man with a rather old and wary looking, brown suit. Anton raises his hands up in the air as the crowd cheer for him at the top their voices. Anton looks over to see Tsunami Chow-Lee, who was wearing a blue training jacket, and white, baggy Karate pants, clapping and smiling in a mellow way. Anton ran over to him. “I did it Mater Tsunami!” Anton shouts as he reaches him. “Yes, you did Anton-san, but do not let it go to your head, for you still have many fights before you can win, and earn a black belt.” He said in a melodious voice. “Yeah, I know. I have to win a black belt, or I will never know the basics of Martial Arts!” Anton said solemnly. “Indeed, and you shall also need to use your black belt honour to your advantage at March Madness.” Tsunami adds. “I realize that, though Red Rock, Ganker and Kramer Wild Wing are all amazing opponents, I shall try my hardest of course, but victory is doubtful.” Anton said unconfidently. “You must not speak like that Anton-san, for if you believe you are good enough, you are indeed good enough. You must release all tension within, ands go into the match with a sense of revitalization and confidence.” Tsunami said enlighteningly. “Yes Master Tsunami.” Anton answers. “I better go into the locker room, I need to go to the toilet and then prepare for my next match.” Anton tells Tsunami. “Very well.” They bow to each other and Anton then leaves the Gym and enters a small corridor where he goes through a door on the right. He enters a small locker room, in which there is only one wardrobe and a small bench. Anton sits down and puts his hands in his head. He had four rounds left, and if he won all four he would be Tournament champion, if he won one more, he would receive a Black Belt. It would be a great honour. Though the tournament wasn’t his main focus, he was heavily thinking about his opponents at March Madness; Red Rock, Ganker and Kramer Wild Wing, is he could somehow grasp a win, he would then have the chance to face the mighty Chris Chase for the Cruiserweight title. Chris Chase was a member of the stable; New Breed. The usually travelled in packs, and if Anton could win on Sunday, he would have trouble against Chase. Then there were so many other talented Cruiserweights, like Xavier Kannon and Juan Borro, who would be threatening for the title. Though, he would need to get past March Madness before he thought about that. He could well have been in the Main event at March Madness, he had beaten the mighty Vampir Nosferatu in the first round of the March Madness Tournament, but lost to El Diablo due to disqualification in the next round. He was devastated, but when he saw the alternate match he had at March Madness the brightness arrived back in his eyes. The match he had would be tough, but the reward would be great, a shot at the PCW Cruiserweight title. First there was the mighty Red Rock, the former PCW Rising Star Champion. He was said to have balls of steal, so low-blowing him would be very effective, despite that not being Anton’s style at all. Though he did have many weaknesses, and also many strengths. He was 223lbs, probably the heaviest in the match. He didn’t have a massive selection of moves, but still the moves he did have were very effective. He was a traditional High-Flyer, much like Anton, though he had no Martial Arts skills. He was 27 years old, though Anton had thought he was younger though when he checked his records, which were what appeared. He had plenty of experience despite coming straight from PWA, as he had been in many top feds such as RSE. He would be a massive threat and was probably favourite to win it. Next came Ganker, he had had a long feud with Red Rock is past feds, he was a good friend of the infamous Hank~! He too was an experienced wrestler, despite only being about 19. He was the second youngest in the match. He weighed about the same as Anton, which would be good, and then Anton could use some of his Judo flips, etc. in the match. He was 5’7 about and inch taller than Anton. This was good for Anton. Though Ganker would still be an extremely tough opponent. He had a great finisher, the RSE-Vengeance. He was a high-flyer as expected. He also would be a big threat; Anton hoped he could keep him under wraps though. Last, but most certainly not least, wads the most experienced of them all. Former PCW Television and Cruiserweight Champion, Kramer Wild Wing. He was 33 years of age, quite old in current wrestling times. Though he would play his experience to his advantage, he was not a former Cruiserweight and Television Champion for nothing. His skills were magnificent. He was an all-rounder, he could use any style to his advantage, Anton hoped that he would not use his multiple skills in the match, and just stick to one, that way Anton could scout him more efficiently. He was 221lbs, which was quite heavy for a Cruiserweight; Anton would have a tough time with him. His experience, strength, all-round abilities would play to his advantage so much in the match. Anton didn’t know how he would cope with the three talent superstars. Though he would do his best, he would use as much of his Martial Art knowledge as he could. It would surely be an extremely tough match. But no match could be better than a Pay-Per-View match, the crowd was massive, the buzz was amazing, and the adrenaline was exhilarating. “Time for you next match Anton!” Shouted a man from outside the door. This brought Anton back to reality, he got up and stretched a bit. He then made his way out as the crowd cheered for him. His next opponent was taller older and stronger than him, but Anton used his speed well and beat his opponent easily. He had done it! He has gotten his black belt, and he still had a chance of winning the tournament, it was a great honour for him. He had finally completed his first Martial Art, and he couldn’t wait to begin the next one. He still had three matches left. His next opponent was the same age has him, scrawny, but very fast and efficient, Anton put him away, though with a bit of trouble. Then came the semi-final. This opponent was sixteen and was extremely strong. Anton used his speed, but the strength of his man was incredible. After 11 rounds Anton finally won. The crowd cheered in anticipation for the Grand-Final. His final opponent was seventeen, the reigning champion, already a black belt; he was the favourite, quick strong, agile. Anton did his best, he fought and fought, and the man’s judo skills were exceptional. Anton tried and tried and tried. He hit all his best moves but the man hit them harder and better. His skills were phenomenon; he would be a great wrestler, Anton thought. After 14 rounds, Anton was tired, he tried one last move, and exceptional flip taught to him by Master Tsunami, but it was reversed, Anton fell to the ground and didn’t get up. He lost, he was devastated, would the same thing happen at March Madness, would his opponents out class him in everyway, he didn’t know, he had put his best into the match and lost. Though he would keep trying, he now had a black belt in judo, and he would reach the top no matter what. Red Roc, Kramer and Ganker, were the opponents, March Madness was the venue, and victory was the essentialness.
