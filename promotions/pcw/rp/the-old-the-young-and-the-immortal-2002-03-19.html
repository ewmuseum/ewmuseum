The Old, The Young, And The Immortal

19 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 19/03/02 The Old, The Young, and The Immortal… The scene opens up in what can only be described as a school hall. The floor is well polished and it shines brightly in the light pouring in through a huge open window. A small podium seems to have been set up for some kind of talk; whether it is to be an assembly for the children of this school or a teachers meeting in unknown at present, but hopefully things will be revealed soon enough. Many chairs are lined up in long, strenuous rows and as we grow closer to a small selection of them three dark, shadowy figures are already found seated… Figure 1: Brings back fond memories, does it not? Figure 2: No, I’m afraid it doesn’t, I never went to a school like this… Figure 1: Come to think of it, neither did I! The first figure chuckles to himself. Figure 3: Why are we even here? Figure 2: This is the perfect place to compare Bobby Stormbringer to… Figure 3: Why is it? Figure 2 sighs. Figure 2: Bobby Stormbringer is practically a child, so therefore this is where he should be! In a damn school! Come to think of it, so should PCW’s current cruiserweight champion, Anton the Kid! What do you think, Master Horus? We close in to reveal the three figures as The Pharaoh, Isiy Xansiv and Horus, the great Egyptian God of the sky! Horus leans forward in his seat resting on his black cane, Titu on the other hand sits back, slouching in his chair and as for his wife, well she simply sits with her legs crossed and her hands on her lap. Horus: I can see your point, Titu. This Bobby Stormbringer, he sounds full of himself…is this true? The Pharaoh: Very much so, my old friend. Horus: Ah! His arrogance will be his downfall… The Pharaoh: Correction, Horus…I will be his downfall! Horus laughs. Horus: You also seem quite full of yourself these days Titu, why is that? The Pharaoh: It’s mainly down to the fact that I am, and know that I am better than most of the PCW roster. But working with the inferior has its downside, Horus, its not all wins and the occasional and I repeat occasional, defeat. You see some of them are quite stupid, in fact most are! There are a select few who are quite intellectual though, but most are far from it! Horus: I see…I see… Pharaoh looks down at his wrist where we find a silver watch, finely crafted and expensive looking. The Pharaoh: Where are those children?! I requested for a few to come in and get autographs signed, but they are highly late! Isiy: Maybe they just don’t like you? Pharaoh turns and scowls at his wife. The Pharaoh: I’ll forget I heard that! Suddenly to the left of the three immortals two huge double doors fly open and in pours a dozen children all smiling and chattering amongst themselves. Pharaoh grins as the children are all lined up on the podium and told to be quiet by an old, frail looking man; obviously a teacher of some sort. The Pharaoh: Now you Mortals may be wondering why you have been assembled here today… The children, all around the age of 10 and 11 look at each other in confusion. Finally one of them, a fat lad wearing a pair of blue Nike shorts and a white Nike T-Shirt asks Pharaoh a question that most of the others are currently wondering. Fat Kid: Hey, Mister Xansiv, what dopes Assembled mean? Pharaoh sighs. The Pharaoh: It means brought together little one, now shut up while I ask you all some exiting questions…after that- Pharaoh Shudders. The Pharaoh: I’ll be signing autographs… The children all look at each other in awe and wonder and abruptly begin to talk amongst themselves again. The old teacher who led them in quietens them down at length and then Pharaoh continues his speech. The Pharaoh: Now as many of you know, you have been selected because all of you are loyal Primetime Championship Wrestling fans and know the current affairs of the worlds leading federation in the professional wrestling circuit. The children, who are obvious not truly literate yet all look at each other in confusion again. Pharaoh raises his hand to his brow and places his hand on it. He sighs again and then continues. The Pharaoh: Look I am immortal, and you youthful children are beginning to wear away my patience! Now look, I’m going to put this as simply as I can! You have been brought here to answer some questions that I have! That too difficult for you to understand? The children nod and smile. The Pharaoh: Ok…lets begin…as you know, this Friday on PCW Fusion a number one contenders match for the PCW TV title has been set. I, along with the previously named Mystery man, Ben J. Pailthorpe, Big Davelicious, and Bobby Stormbringer are to be involved…what I would like to know is… who are your picks to win this match? In coalition the children begin to shout out their picks causing a large din to which Pharaoh, as he seems to have grown accustomed to doing throughout this promo, sighs. The Pharaoh: Can’t you keep these children at bay? The teacher standing next to the children begins to sweat quite violently; he runs around after them and attempts to make them speak at once in singular fashion, but to no avail… The Pharaoh: Kids, if you don’t all take your time and tell me individually who your picks are then I’m afraid there won’t be any autographs at the end of all this! On saying these words the children stop their whining and the din ends suddenly. The Pharaoh: Thank you…now lets start with you! Titu smiles and points towards a smallish boy standing out of line with the other children in a poor attempt not to be seen; he pears around looking quite coy and at length speaks… Coy looking Boy: Erm, w-well…I like your chances, Mr Pharaoh, b-but Bobby Stormbringer is pretty good, and, and, well I just think Bobby will win… Pharaoh looks over to Horus in dismay. The Pharaoh: You see Horus I am the underdog in this match! Pharaoh sighs. Horus: I see! Yet I believe that the other eleven children may think otherwise, my pupil! Voice: Yeah, I think you can win, Mr Xansiv! Pharaoh looks up. The Pharaoh: Who said that? A tall boy, taller than the rest of the children that is, raises his hand. Tall lad: It was me… An arrogant expression begins to fill Xansiv’s face. The Pharaoh: And you think I can win? Tall lad: Yeah, yeah I do! Pharaoh smiles arrogantly. The Pharaoh: Then that settles it! With at least one person here who thinks I can defeat Bobby, Ben, and Big D that gives me strength! I shall defeat these three men and then go on to defeat that rat, Akiyama! Horus: That’s the spirit, Titu! Pharaoh smiles. The Pharaoh: Come, our time here draws to an end…and to be honest I cannot be pestered with signing twelve autographs! Isiy: But that would be letting these kids down, Titu! You can’t do that! You’re the good guy remember! The Pharaoh: Yes, yes, that is all good, Isiy, my dear, but I really can’t be bothered… Isiy: If you don’t sign these autographs then you’re losing twelve fans that could be cheering and chanting for you while your kicking Akiyama’s ass! Pharaoh nods approvingly. The Pharaoh: Yes, that is true…perhaps its time to get into the swing of this new “Good guy” thing… Pharaoh stands up so that all twelve children can see him clearly. The Pharaoh: Come, my Mortal youths, I have taken much of your precious time already, come and get your autographs, friends! The children, including the old teacher rush down off the podium to greet Pharaoh, pictures and magazine and all sorts are handed to him and quickly Titu goes about signing all the merchandise. As the last of the twelve kids has their picture signed, the old teacher approaches Pharaoh. Old teacher: Thank you, Mr Xansiv. You have made the day of these pupils…but… The old man pauses. The Pharaoh: But what? Old teacher: Well I’m a big fan of yours too! Could you sign this for me? The old teacher begins to produce a picture of the Pharaoh holding his tag team title belt in the air from his inside jacket pocket. Pharaoh chuckles as he does and so does Horus. Eventually even the old teacher does. The Pharaoh: No! The old man looks stunned. The Pharaoh: My generosity has gone far enough for one day! Now if you’ll excuse us, we have places to go and people to see! Pharaoh begins walking towards the double doors of the school hall as the old man stands in shock of Pharaoh’s unlikely answer. Horus and Isiy follow our resident Egyptian God as the scene begins to close. The Pharaoh: I should have had them pay for those autographs! Pharaoh sighs for possibly the nineteenth time in this promo as the scene finally fades to black.
