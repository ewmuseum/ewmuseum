Chapter XIV: The Meeting With Jax...

12 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12-3-03 ::Introduction:: Now is the time that Mark has been waiting for. The man who has taught him everything is right in front of him. They lost connection, but now they are about to be reunited. Its like a reunion of some sort or something like that. So why do I keep you all waiting with this introduction? I don’t know lets go back into Mark’s past some more. ::End Introduction:: Chapter XIV: The Meeting With Jax… The image that Mark can see in front of him is the one he has been waiting for awhile now. Jax can now give him some answers to his questions. Mark stands there as Jax can see his silhouette from some of the light outside. It is just like Jax remembers him. He is standing tall as can be, but he can’t see the colors or anything that he is wearing. All he can see is some of Mark’s new wife beater torn off and is waving around while the wind blows it. Jax reaches out to the middle of the table and he takes out a book of matches. He tears a match off and closes the flap, then he takes the match and strikes it across the flint and the light reflects Jax’s image. Mark can see the same ol face that he has seen many times before. He looks up at his hair to make sure that it is still all off and indeed it is. He has not changed his beliefs by his image. “Come chosen one and sit down.” Jax says as he lights the lantern that is in the middle of the table. Mark walks across the dirt floor as dust is being left behind from him kicking in the dirt. He gets closer to the table and a knot forms in his stomach from wanting to know where Jax has been lately. He is wondering if he is still a Neo and all of these questions are starting to come to him as he walks. He gets to the table and he pulls out the wooden chair. He goes ahead and he sits down. The lantern shows Jax and his face and half way down his upper body. “Where in the hell have you been?” Mark asks as he puts his elbows upon the table. “I should ask you the same.” Jax says as he calmly looks through his dark shaded glasses. “I have been busy Jax, you know that.” Mark says as he is now starting to look at Jax straight into the eyes. “Well I have been where I have been all of my life.” Jax starts to say as his hands are calmly resting on his quads. “I have been in the town I met you in. When you were in college and doing all that natural shit. Why haven’t you been back? Gangra has asked about you.” “I have been wrestling, but now I was fired just not too long ago.” Mark says as he is starting to shake a little bit. “I wasn’t doing anything and I needed to get out, but instead they fired me for no obvious reason. I was on the shelf for a little bit as well from a damn coon.” “Wait a minute my boy.” Jax says as he puts his hands in the air to stop Mark. “Your telling me that you let a coon injure you?” “It was a cheap shot Jax.” Mark says as he is trying to remember the story. “I was blindsided from a distraction and a weapon was involved and no one was out there to help me. It was all just me and me alone.” “Well boy we have missed you.” Jax says as his hands are now resting on the table as he looks into the lantern. “We have missed your presence and Gangra wants to see how far along you have came with the Neos.” “Yes I have missed the group and Gangra myself.” Mark says as he is leaning over into the lantern so that he can get a better look at Jax. “I have come a long way, but now I want more than just the coons.” “Oh yea?” Jax questions as he seems to sound interested. “What else do you want?” “Well I went around towns and spread the word about the coon belief.” Mark starts out as he is resting now in the back of his chair. “No one would seem to listen so I had to do something else to get the message through. So I went around and tried some curbings and tried to just torture all of them. Nothing was working, some people would follow me, but no real damage has been done.” “Ah I understand.” Jax says as he interrupts Mark. “I had this problem; everyone has this problem when they start out. That is why everyone sticks together when you first get into all of these beliefs. So that you actually have some people to back you up and get the message through. But tell me your new idea.” “Well the new idea is this.” Mark starts to answer as he is trying to find the words to say. “I jumped into the plane as the police was chasing me and I find that the pilot is a spic. No big deal at first, but then I realized that they could be a target. Just look at everyone who is around here in Mexico. They are usually related to one another and when the come to America they can’t speak the language. Not to mention they smell like shit dude.” “Ah yes you have learned to go and try some new minorities.” Jax says as he gets up from his chair and starts to walk around the old hut. “You just have to keep on trying and trying until you find the right minority that fits you. Its like a shirt. You have to have the right size to fit you, because if not you will not fit in. So where do I come in at?” “Well I need some ideas to get this through to the people.” Mark says as he is looking up at Jax as he strolls around the hut. “I need help and I need it pretty quickly.” “Well with spics you can’t try to get the belief through down here.” Jax says as he stops strutting around and he leans up against one of the walls. “See if you look around then you have spics all over the place. But if you goto America where spics are as well, then you have the white people around to.” “Ah yes you make a very good point.” Mark says as he gets up and is going over to Jax. “Another thing is that spics are a very easy target in my opinion.” Jax starts to say as he takes a toothpick from his jacket pocket and he sticks it into his mouth. “They are the people who are taking all of our jobs away and why? Just because they can speak an international language. They don’t deserve to even have a job since they can’t even speak English. Plus you have already mentioned a couple of more reasons to target at.” “I have got you now.” Mark says as he is now going towards the door. “So this lesson is over for now.” Jax says as he is starting to now pick his teeth. “I have got you a gift though.” Mark looks over at Jax as he goes towards the lantern so that he can see. He reaches into his inside jacket pocket and pulls out a cell phone. He looks up at Mark and then he tosses the phone over in his direction. Mark catches with ease, but is timid with it at first. “Why do I need this?” Mark asks as he is looking at the phone he has just received. “So that no matter where you are you can reach me and now I can reach you and we can stay in contact with one another.” Jax says as he leans up from the table. “Its like a reunion gift and plus this way I can check up on you.” Mark nods his head like Gangra always did when he saw him. He reaches for the door handle and he feels for it and reaches it. He turns it in the opposite direction then before as he pushes on the door as well. The door opens and Mark looks back at his new teacher, but also a very close friend that he can goto. He steps outside into the sand as he puts the phone in his front pocket. He walks out of the hut with his head high and a new belief set even better into his mind.
