Date: 4-22-03 Scene: The moon is out, the night is young. A man in a long black leather trench coat, hands stuffed into the side pockets, is the only one walking along the sidewalk. The man’s face is covered with a pair of black sunglasses and a brown hat, his long blonde hair tied back into a ponytail for once in his longtime wrestling career. The man in the trench coat stops outside of a small café and leans up against a light post, the light shadowing the man’s face even more because of his hat. A few moments of standing alone outside of a café a young black haired girl comes running out and wraps her arms around him, the two kiss and then start to walk down the sidewalk together. Man:”Hello Jenn, it’s good to see you.” Jenn: “So babe, you got back in after all these years?” Man: “Yeah, looks like that to be the case now doesn’t it?” Jenn: “Maybe now you’ll shut up about not having a stable income and not being able to support me.” (she says with a subtle hint of laughter in her voice) “Well all just the same I’m glad you’re happy hun.” Man: “I’m glad I’m happy too. I even get to go after a title I am no stranger to, being a man of the extreme arts and having the heart to keep going on when normal men fall to the challenge set before them. Sure is jus’ a number one contendership, but it’s a good place to start for a man like me.” Jenn: “Whose the match against?” Man: “Some guy named Adam Stryker.” Jenn: “Do you know anything about him?” Man: “I know the man’s a master submissionist, or so he claims to be. I only know what I could dig up on him from PCW records, the ones I’m not banned from accessing.” (sighs) “His finisher is called the restraining order, a mere cross face, nothing really to sweat if you ask me, It’s nothing to half of my arsenal.” Jenn: “I can vouch for that hun, but are you sure you’re ready for PCW talent and to hang where you once did?” Man: “Where did I hang Jenn? I wasn’t really much of anything in PCW! If you ask me, I don’t think I can do any worse then the last time I was wit’ PCW, I can only do better, starting wit’ winning the PCW Extreme Championship number one contendership.” The scene one cuts off to black. Scene II: The same man in the trench coat is shown a few hours later standing out side of Adam Stryker’s hotel room, just pacing the hallway in front back and forth for several minutes. PCW reporter approaches him. The man in the trench coat stops and begins to speak, back to the reporter. Man: “May I help you, Ms. Jennings?” Tiffany: “Yeah by telling me how you knew it was me without seeing me first?” Man: “I know a lot just by touch.” (snickers) “That and the fact your touch was that only of a woman, and not I know you were not my wonderful girlfriend Jenn either. Jus’ trust me when I say I know a lot wit’ little actual sight.” Tiffany: “Well you don’t have to turn around, but why are you standing outside of this hotel room?” Man: “He’s my opponent Ms. Jennings. This Wednesday Night Turmoil I face that man in the ring for the number one contendership.” Tiffany: “So you’re Mr. Surprise?” Man: “That would be correct, and if you don’t mind I have to be going, I only came by to see where my opponent is staying jus’ incase I’m bored and wish to play a foolish kid game wit’ the poor, pathetic, utterly useless pile of cow dung known as Adam Stryker.” Tiffany: “Well it was nice to meet you, sort of.” Man: “You too, see you around after I win my match.” The man in the trench coat walks out of the hall as Tiffany walks the other direction. The scene follows with the man in the trench coat. The man in the trench coat walks straight into the parking lot once exiting the hotel and heads straight for his black Corvette parked pretty close to the entrance. The man in the trench coat gets in the driver’s side and just sits there after placing the key in the ignition. The car remains off as the man in the trench coat just sits there staring at the plain grassy field. Eventually Jenn comes walking out of the hotel and gets into the same black Vette. Jenn: “You going to go or what?” Man: (laughs) “I was thinking about jus’ sitting here for hours and boring you to death.” Jenn: (sarcastically) “Ha, ha, really funny!” The man in the trench coat and starts his black Vette and drives out of the hotel parking lot.
