Exclusive Interview with Warden Riley

28 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2/28/02 Scene opens inside Amaranth Penitentiary. It shows “Swamp Dawg” Karl LeBlanc inside his jail cell. He is doing pull-ups on a bar bolted to the concrete wall of his cell. He is dripping with sweat and smells like a damn goat. He has been training all morning in preparation for his upcoming Extreme Title defenses. Scene cuts to inside “The Warden” Jack Riley’s office. The Pimple Faced Intern Kid has flown to New Mexico for an exclusive interview. PFIK (knocks on Warden Riley’s office door): Mr. Riley, sir… Warden: Yeah, come on in son. PFIK: Mr. Riley, do you have time for a few questions? Warden: Sure, I’ll give you a minute. Did PCW pay to have this camera crew flown out here with you? PFIK: Yes, sir. Warden: Wow, I feel important! PFIK: Well, sir… that’s what happens when you have the Extreme Champ in your stable. Warden signs a paper on his desk and looks up. Warden Riley: So, what do want to talk about son? PFIK: First of all, since LeBlanc still isn’t speaking, can you just tell us what his thoughts are? Does he speak to you, Warden Riley? Warden: Yes, of course he speaks to me. And I can tell you this… I’d hate to be Jason Core. LeBlanc has made it very clear that he intends to end Jason Core’s career at the Pay-Per-View. AND… he might get a head start if Core shows his ugly mug at Fusion this Friday. PFIK: Do YOU have a bone to pick with Jason Core, Warden. The guy knocked you out at Turmoil. Warden: Oh yes, he’s gotta “Warden Knuckle-Sandwich” coming his way. PFIK: Mr. Riley, you were an NCAA National wrestling champ, correct? Warden: Correct, although that’s been quite a few years ago. PFIK: Would you ever consider stepping in the ring… maybe a Tag match with you and LeBlanc vs. Jason Core and his nephew Mark Estep? Warden: Well, if that ever happened I know I could hold my own, but hell I’m getting too old for that stuff. PFIK: You’ve taught LeBlanc some killer moves, though. This new arsenal of raw power moves laced with submission holds has made a huge difference. Warden: Well, I think the “Gator Clutch” and the “Louisiana Lockdown” have worked out quite well. PFIK: The Louisiana Lockdown is my favorite. A double-underhook, sit-down powerbomb! Warden: Have you ever seen a more devastating powerbomb! PFIK: No sir, I sure haven’t. Warden: Well, mark my words… that move will take LeBlanc all the way to the top of PCW. I’ve already made the prediction… LeBlanc made his debut at Shanghaied 2002, by the time Shanghaied 2003 rolls around, the SWAMP DAWG will be World Title material. PFIK: Any predictions for the up-coming week? Warden Riley: Mark Estep will be destroyed tomorrow night at Fusion. If Jason Core interferes, he too will get his ass handed to him. PFIK: What about the PPV March 4th? Warden: Too early to think about that. Friday comes first. LeBlanc is totally focused on Estep right now. BUT, when the time comes, Jason Core will become the primary focus. And when he does, he better understand what he’s gotten himself into. PFIK: In a recent interview, Jason Core voiced his disgust over Estep interfering. Core said, “I’m all about fair matches.” What do you think about that comment? Warden: Well, if he’s looking for fair matches, then perhaps the PCW Extreme division isn’t for him. You see, LeBlanc will revolutionize the Extreme Division and he sure as hell doesn’t fight fair. I hope Core is ready to strap it on and I hope he retirement plans… because LeBlanc has spoken… he WILL end his career. PFIK (turns to the camera): It all begins this Friday. The road to March Madness runs through Fusion. You think things are heating up in the Extreme Division? With the Swamp Dawg at the top of the food chain… YOU BETTER BELIEVE IT!!! I’m the Pimply Faced Intern Kid signing off.
