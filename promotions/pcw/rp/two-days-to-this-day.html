Two days to this day...

Date: Who really knows... One day later: after Skylar’s visit: The sun burnt in through the windows. Pharaoh looked out onto the street below and smiled. His body was bruised and beaten, but that wasn’t going to stop him from enjoying himself over the next few days. After the Last Man Standing match at Shang’heid, Pharaoh had insisted that the company give him a short three-day break. He’d miss Turmoil this week but would gain some composure in Egypt, his home. He looked out over the suburbia below and frowned slowly. His match with DarkStar had been much more than he’d expected. The Gothic monster has pushed the boundaries on Sunday and Titu was feeling the pain greatly now. He wondered how Snow felt; apparently he’d been coughing up glass fragmentation. By Horus…he thought, sighing and shaking his head. Isiy entered the room, a suitcase under her arm and a spear in the other. She glared. ‘Titu, would you please tell the Guard to control their throwing practice? I found this protruding from the balcony this morning. It took me forever to get it out.’ Pharaoh was a little distant this morn. ‘Yeah, yeah, sure.’ Isiy cocked her head to the side. ‘Are you listening to me?’ The tire, the aching and the stinging were working their magic today - he really needed to sleep. Hopefully the trip would help him through this rough patch. ‘Yeah…yeah, sure.’ Isiy sighed, dropping the case on the bed and launching the spear into the floor with a thud. It twanged like a spring as it tried to find its bearings. Pharaoh spun around, startled by the sudden burst of noise. ‘What in the name of Ra are you doing, woman?’ he said, his eyes wide with astonishment. ‘I’m trying my damned hardest to get some sort of reaction out of you!’ she roared. ‘What’s with the spear?’ he said earnestly, his eyes fixed on the bobbling weapon. ‘The guard have been doing target practice again, and judging from the place I found this I can’t say they’re very good at it.’ Pharaoh stared for a while. ‘Why, where did you find it?’ Isiy grinned, adding an edge of cynicism to her voice. ‘Driven deep into the side of the balcony.’ Pharaoh shook his head, sighing deeply. ‘I’ll try having a word with them. Some of them…well, they’re new to the job, you know? They’re not really that experienced in throwing a spear. Or fielding a halberd, or well…fighting in any way at all.’ Isiy chuckled, ‘Yes, I noticed. Especially when they had to fight those slaves the other week.’ She paused, ‘Anyway, I’m done and packed, how are you doing?’ Pharaoh shrugged. ‘Haven’t started yet, my love. I’m thinking.’ Isiy sat down next to him on the spare bed. ‘What about? Is something bothering you?’ Titu dropped his head, looking down at his feet. ‘Skylar’s visit made me think, Isiy. It made me realize that this Friday I have to face off against one of my friends—’ ‘Snow?’ said Isiy, rolling her eyes, ‘I thought you didn’t like him.’ ‘When I first met him, I admit that I wasn’t too impressed, but now…after the past few weeks, I’ve realized he isn’t so bad. He’s saved my skin, and I’ve saved his.’ ‘Ah,’ she said, ‘I see.’ Isiy stopped for a moment, looking out over the street ahead with blank eyes. The spare room was well furbished, and the low window gave a perfect view over the Californian street far below. It was nice, even if Titu had insisted on the same-old ancient Egyptian decor as the other rooms. ‘But, you worked so hard to win that match at Shang’heid, you can’t give up hope of winning the title now…even if your opponent is Snow.’ Pharaoh breathed in heavily. ‘I know, I know. And it’s not that, it’s going to be a brilliant match; the fans’ll love it! But, as I was saying yesterday, I know damned well that Clyde and DarkStar are going to get involved and ruin the whole damned thing. That’s what’s pissing me off.’ Pharaoh gets up off the bed and walks across the room towards the door. ‘Cummon then, you can help me pack…then we’ll be off.’ He sighed, gloomily. Isiy walks up towards him, smiling. ‘Ok, let’s go.’ She placed a loving hand on his shoulder and walked him out of the room… *** Two days after the incident at the Nile’s banks: ‘What did I tell you! I told you Clyde’d get involved in my business, didn’t I? As if attacking me after my match with DarkStar wasn’t enough, he has to turn the goddamn Nile red with blood!’ Pharaoh slumped down on the bed in the Cairo hotel and looked up towards the ceiling. Isiy stared at him from across the room, biting her nails. ‘Look, I thought you told me Clyde was in America.’ She queried after a while. ‘I thought he was. But I should have known something like this would happen! It’s one of the plagues, Isiy, isn’t it? And the Nile ran red with blood!’ He quoted, shivering. ‘Good Gods! How am I expected to go out there on Fusion with this on my mind, huh?’ Silence ensued the bedroom. ‘How the Duat am I meant to win the title with the ever-growing knowledge that Clyde’s stalking me over the world trailing the plagues behind his rotten ass?!’ Titu got up from the bed and walked across to the balcony overlooking the richer side of Cairo. ‘Gods sakes,’ he sighed, wiping beads sweat from his brow. ‘Just try not to worry about it too much—’ ‘Just try not to worry about it too much?! Don’t you remember what the tenth plague is?! People could die, and considering this is Clyde we’re talking about, they probably will!’ Well, you will taunt him, won’t you, Titu. Isiy thought moodily. If you hadn’t of cut that in-ring promo a few weeks ago where you told Clyde you wanted him to take you to the edge of insanity then he probably wouldn’t have done this, would he? ‘Well what am I suppose to say?’ she mumbled, ‘Cummon, the flight leaves soon. Get your stuff together.’ Pharaoh stood looking over the ancient city for a while and then turned, an expression a little more positive filling his face. ‘I’m going to ask him, Isiy.’ He said. Isiy’s brow furrowed, ‘Ask who what?’ Pharaoh began to walk towards the room’s entrance. ‘I’m going to ask Clyde whether that was him we saw. I can’t stand it any longer, I need to know.’ Pharaoh left the room, leaving Isiy alone. ‘Titu, what about your suitcases?’ Xansiv rushed back into the room. ‘Whoops, almost forgot about those.’ Isiy sighed, it was going to be a long month, and she knew it…
