No More Shadows

22 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 07/22/02 Ricky: Get off me man! What the fuck!? *The strange voice he has been hearing now seems to echo all around him. The words before, had been methodical. Concentrated, and well thought out, now this voice. This odd sounding monotone voice. Has...laughter? The arms that had grabbed Ricky and drug him into the alley now releasing the hold as he spins around, only to see that there is nobody there* Voice: Ricky, this is the time. Ricky: What time? Voice: The time for us to make our move. the time for us to begin to climb the appropriate ranks in PCW. I have stood around and watched as you did nothing for the past 2 weeks. You got pissed off over not being booked. You got pissed off over a double DQ, no thanks to that scandalous coward. Now you will go get back into that ring and do your thing. Ricky: What thing is that? Get DQ'd again? I mean damn my singles record in PCW is 1-0-3 ALL numbers coming by DQ. This is getting ridiculous. 4 matches, 4 DQ's. What is the point in even trying? Voice: SILENCE RICKY! You WILL go back to that PCW ring, and you WILL defeat Makonie. Ricky: I am done with Makonie. It has become obvious that I will not receive a fair shot at him. In the mean time I will however, head back to the gym and resume my training. Voice: NO! You go to the PCW offices, and you demand you want Makonie in a no interference match! You then take Makonie, you completely dismantle him and you prove to the world what you are capable of. Ricky: The WORLD knows what I am capable of, it is PCW that fails to recognize. Voice: Do as I say Ricky. The time is now. *The voice trails off in a whisper as Ricky is standing in the dark alley alone. He glances around at the graffiti ridden walls and slowly decides to take his leave of this shithole. He steps back out into the high yellow sidewalks of New York as the sun continues to throb overhead. Ricky makes his way down the sidewalk disappearing in to the sea of people, where somehow through the masses, it is still quite desolate. Ricky raises his head to the captivating sun and slowly begins to speak out.* Ricky: What the hell is with this voice I keep hearing? Will it ever stop? Will it ever just go away? What kind of driving force does it expect to have? I mean there is nothing it can do to control me. It is an imaginary voice in my head. That is all. I must find a way to get past this. Past this voice, past this aggression in my career. Past this goddamn knee injury. Past everything. I must, push through. *The clouds in the New York sky suddenly swirl, as storm clouds fill the area. A few people opening umbrellas continue to walk down the street, but for some reason Ricky steps inside of the antiques shop off to his left. As soon as he enters the building, balls of ice rain from the sky. Ricky watches out the window as people begin to scatter to and fro. He scratches his head when an older voice speaks up from his left shoulder.* Old Man: Odd thing, this weather is. Ricky: Indeed. One moment it's nice and bright, the next, you get shit on. Old Man: Young man, that isn't just the weather, that is everyday life. *Ricky spins around and the old man is shuffling back towards the counter. He takes a few steps towards him when the man presents a tarnished award, that he lies on the counter. Ricky approaches it and looks down at it.* ~~~Shane Thunder: Collegiate Athlete of the Year~~~ Ricky: Wha!? How did you.... *Ricky looks up only to notice that the old man had disappeared again. He looks around the shop and notices the small curtain swinging to and fro in the back corner of the shop. Ricky calls out to the old man but nobody responds. Ricky makes his way around the counter and cautiously towards the curtain. He looks back to the door, and notices that the hail has stopped. He takes a step back from the counter, and grabs the medallion. Looking down at it he begins to speak* Ricky: Well I guess he wanted me to have this. Maybe I should just go. *Ricky makes his way to the door and suddenly the hail begins to fall again. He steps back a few steps and notices that it stops. He again moves towards the door, and once again, the hail rains down. He can hear a chuckling coming from the room in the back so he decides to make his way back there. He heads behind the counter and notices the back of the glass is shattered in a awkward star shaped pattern. He dips his head through the curtain and sees the old man kneeling in the center of an empty room with a peaceful smile on his face. His eyes closed, not speaking a word, he motions for Ricky to join him. Ricky makes his way to the center of the room, and the man grabs his arm, pulling him down to a kneeling position as well.* Old Man: Think of your brother Ricky. Ricky: How do you know.... Old Man: FOCUS! *Ricky takes the kneeling position in silence's solitude, as visions of his brother training and working out that day at the gym when he found the CCW flyer. The visions go to Shane defeating The Supreme Machine for the Heavyweight title. Then tragically the final visions are that of Shane lying withered and battered in his casket, where days of endless suffering, had finally ended. A small tear runs downs the face of Ricky as the mysterious voice from earlier returns* Voice: Finally Ricky, you have returned. You now understand. You have seen what can be done. I did that. I was the one who created my path in this ill fated destiny. IT was me, your brother, your own flesh and blood. Ricky: Shane..... Voice: The times are near Ricky. All that bullshit I fed you earlier, you know how we were as kids, I was just messing with you. All the while guiding you here. Ricky: You have been the voice in my head..... Shane: Yes Ricky. I have been trying to reach out to you. I needed to tell you of a few things. In PCW, you are a loner. In life, you will always have SuMa there to watch over you. But as for now, you are the lone survivor of the Thunder name. Mom, Dad, Me....all gone. It is up to you Ricky. This bullshit about living in a shadow is over. This is your time to breakout and show the world what the Thunder family name is all about. Ricky: How...I mean..why... Shane: My time here is over. I will no longer be your guiding voice. I will no longer be your guardian angel so to speak. This is your time Ricky. Make it the best you can. Ricky: Thank you Shane. *Ricky bows his head slightly Thank You. *Ricky opens his eyes and reaches to wipe them but the man stops him* Old Man: Tears cleanse the soul Ricky Ricky: How did you know? How did you...I mean everything...It was like... Old Man: It isn't about knowing Ricky. It is about feeling. *The old man grabs Ricky's hand and places it across Ricky's own heart.* Old Man: Sometimes you can just feel the way it is. Much like you could feel that Shane was in this room. Was Shane here? Ricky: Yes...he spoke to me. Didn't you hear it? Old Man: No Ricky. Shane is in your heart. Not mine. All I can do Ricky is open that passage for you. Once opened all you need to do is choose how you would like to travel it. That is all life is my son. A maze of winding paths and locked doors. Once things become clear, and you begin to learn to unlock these doors. You will then understand the true powers of Zen. Ricky: Zen? You? Old Man: Yes. It is now my time to bid you farewell. I must take my leave of you as it is time for me to pray. Unless of course you would prefer to join me. The legendary Zen always accepts new followers, and it has made my life so much better. If you do decide to join me. You may understand more of what Shane has always tried to tell you. Ricky: I will think on it. Thanks for the offer however. Old Man: My shop is always open. *Ricky gets to his feet and makes his way back towards the curtain. But before he exits he looks to the old man, and sure enough the man is motioning for him to wait.* Old Man: Before you go, I would like to tell you a short story if you don't mind. Ricky: For everything you have just done for me, How could I mind? Old Man: During a momentous battle, a Japanese general decided to attack even though his army was greatly outnumbered. He was confident they would win, but his men were filled with doubt. On the way to the battle, they stopped at a religious shrine. After praying with the men, the general took out a coin and said, 'I shall now toss this coin. If it is heads, we shall win. If tails, we shall lose. Destiny will now reveal itself.' He threw the coin into the air and all watched intently as it landed. It was heads. The soldiers were so overjoyed and filled with confidence that they vigorously attacked the enemy and were victorious. After the battle, a lieutenant remarked to the general, 'No one can change destiny.' 'Quite right,' the general replied as he showed the lieutenant the coin, which had heads on both sides. Ricky: Huh? Old Man: The true meaning here Ricky...You can change your destiny. If you aren't responsible for yourself, who will be? *With this said the man silently walks away as Ricky disappears through the curtain. A small smile appears on the old man's face as a jingling of the bell above the door can be heard as Ricky steps out onto the once again heat baked sidewalks of sunny New York.*
