South Dakota sucks, and why is Kano in S. D.?

26 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2/26/2002 ~Destiny. One of the most fundamental internal arguments in the human phsyche. Are we just floating around in the universe, all masters of our own futures? Or are all of our futures predetermined, all part of a grand master plan made by the Supreme Being in the universe at the very beginning of time? Many people have asked themselves these very questions. Jon Kano is one of these such people. What do you believe? Jon Kano believes in predetermined destiny. He believes that at the beginning of time, the Supreme Being in the universe determined what Kano would do in each and every day. Is that crazy? Well probably, after all Kano is not the most mentally stable man in the world. Jon Kano believes that it is his destiny to be the PCW Heavyweight Champion of the World. He dreams about it at night, he thinks about it non stop during the day. He is obsessed with the world title, but even with his obsession, he keeps the thought of his match before March Madness. His four way match with Brock Newbludd, Lance Bell, and Mike Storm.~ *The scene opens up in the middle of nowhere in South Dakota. For those have ever been in South Dakota, you know that there is nothing but nothing in South Dakota. It is quite an amazing place. You can go for fifty miles and not see another human being. The badlands of South Dakota are truly bad. If you were to be stranded off the interstate in the badlands, you are dead, f*cking dead. Yet Kano is out there, in the middle of nowhere, training for his upcoming battles. Kano is seen sitting in the middle of a desolate field in the chilling cold with a light snow on the ground, but Kano has built a small fire in front of him. He is wearing a black t-shirt, a black leather jacket, blue jeans, and brown Doc Martin boots.* Kano: Chilling, isn't it? Not the weather, but my demeanor. It is getting to be game time again. Time for me to take it to the next level. Time for me to realize my destiny. But most importantly, it is time for me to represent the old school. All of the fallen greats, the one who helped, alongside me, to build PCW into the juggernaut it is today. Frisco, Doug Troy, Reverend, Meanstreak, and myself built this company. What is the difference between those four and myself? They all held the World Title, while I was content to build the Television and Continental divisions. I set the precedent for the Continental Title. I never asked for a shot at the World Title, and I have gotten one shot in my whole PCW career. And what happens? The whole damn Red Army beats the piss out of me and then Ivan pins me. I am so glad that Big Bad Russian Son of a bitch is out of here and in that third rate indy fed known as OSW. But again that is all in the past. Sunday is the future. I am more than comfortable with my ability to win the battle royal. I am one of the biggest men in it. I have all the tools to do it, and the only question is if I can completely concentrate on the match. After tonight, I can. I first must take care of business in Wisconsin tonight against Brock Newbludd, Lance Bell, and 'Amazing' Mike Storm. Sweet Jesus, I love four way matches. I am the undisputed king of the four way match. I hardly ever lose these sort of matches. I love it because I can just sit back and watch three sons of bitches wear themselves out and I can just come in and win with ease. I am the thinking man's wrestler. Out of these three men, Brock is the only one who has any amount of respect coming from me. He and I go back along time. All the way back to the Judas Steele funeral about a year and a half ago. I delivered a little speech at the funeral. And at the end of the funeral we saw the formation of the Blood Brothers and they won the tag team titles at the funeral. A few months later, Brock, Stephen, and myself formed the Outcasts. I was the Continental Champion and the leader of the Outcasts. I had a vision, a vision that no one else could truly appreciate at that time. The Outcasts and Media forming a superstable. My God Almighty, it would have been beautiful. But Brock couldn't allow that to happen. He refused to open his mind, and I left the Outcasts in disgust and I KILLED THE OUTCASTS, THE STABLE I PUT ON THE MAP. I don't regret creating the Outcasts, and I damn sure don't regret destroying the Outcasts. Brock is a phenomenal tag team wrestler, but his career has been in a downward spiral ever since Stephen left. And I would love to know where the hell his head was last week when he DDTed me. But I guess the envy got to him. I am the only one out of Outcasts who made something of himself in a singles career. But all of Brock's envy can't amount to the talent in this REAL LEGEND's body. Then we have Lance Bell, Rising Star Champ. Goodie, he won the Rising Star Title. A titlethat has had no credibility to it ever since Tyler Nelson held it. Lance Bell came into PCW claiming he was going to revolutionize this company, he was going to raise the bar. Then in his first match he met me and he found out the definition of revolutionize and what it meant to raise the bar. He found out what REAL LEGENDS ARE MADE OF. He realized why they call me 'The Man Who Rocketh the Mosteth.' And finally, 'Amazing' Mike Storm. Mike Storm, the soul survivor of the Storm family. Well that is an accomplishment. But there is an old saying that applies to Mike Storm, 'You can't shine sh*t.' Mike is cursed with the Storm family name and can never get rid of the stigma that goes with it. He might be a decent cruiserweight, but he can't hang with the big guys, I proved that last week. So lets summarize, I have beaten two of the three men before, and the other one I have never wrestled, but he has never been able to accomplish much in his singles career whereas I am a three time PCW title holder. This match is like an ant hill compared to March Madness which is Mount Everest. I am about half way up Mount Everest right now, and at March Madness, I will stand on the peak and yell down to the world below that I am truly WHAT REAL LEGENDS ARE MADE OF. Now to catch my flight to Wisconsin. *The scene fades to black as Kano puts out his fire and leaves.* ~Destiny. Kano's destiny may very well be the PCW World Title, or maybe it is not. Either way the next few days are going to be ones for the ages.~
