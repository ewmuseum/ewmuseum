The Saves the Day Saga: The Vast Spoils of America...

9 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: May 9th, 2002 Tap, Tap, Tap DarkStar's black painted fingernails echoed through the nearly empty room. He sat in a black throne, cast iron and ornate. The throne raised high into the air. Tap, Tap, Tap See, he sat here contemplating where to go next. What to do. Who to speak to. Who to see, and who to talk too. It was a hard choice. Tap, Tap, Tap His tapping was a nervous twitch he picked up years ago, when he was little. In grade school he used to be ragged on viciously by his peers. He would just sit there and take it. Tap, Tap, Tap And he would tap his fingers on his desk. Tap, Tap, Tap This is for a different reason however. Primetime Championship Wrestling. A place where DarkStar has lived and breathed and bled for a year now. He never got respect from his peers, was ragged on constantly, and was never given respect from those that he deserved respect from. Tap, Tap, Tap And now he was tired of it. He decided that with the world collapsing below him, now was a time to take out his brutal frustrations on any living thing he could find, animal or human. Tap, Tap, Tap The gods have all abandoned him, all because of Deaths little chess game with Fate. That bitch. It still bothers him, the world does nothing but flame out and die, people are killing, things are exploding, sodomazation and the end of the world imminent. Tap, Tap, Tap Yet, all he can care about is his problems. Is that such a bad thing? That he thinks about himself now and then. He is forced to help everyone with their fucking innocent little dreams, no matter what, not people he cares about, not every so often, but forever. He is forced to eat breathe and sleep pitiful dreams, boring dreams, dreams that didn't matter. Tap, Tap, Tap Sure, occasionally he used it as a passtime, he would pay more attention to things that mattered. He would gain revenge through his powers, he would help those that truely deserved it. Like that girl and her violin. He saved her, it made him feel great. It brought out this 'dream lord' side of him, a side that seemed to be loved. Something he rarely ever felt. Tap, Tap, Tap Except now it was jeapordized. He decided that this wasn't his thing. He was angry. Tap, Tap, Tap His eyes changed color, almost whitening, he snarled to himself on unearthly growl. He stood up from his chair and it slid back. All of its ornate dressings and cast iron weight nearly toppled with the force. He walked down the long, marble hallway. His feet the only noise. Tap, Tap, Tap He reached up to his wall and grabbed his favorite blade. A custom katana, thinner and longer then the traditional japanese sword, almost more of a saber. He loved it, it cut the air. He reached the front door, a black wooden door done in middle ages style design. He pushed it open slightly before he heard a voice behind him. He turned and saw his ravyn haired goddess standing in front of him. Oh how he loved her. Her face, her body, everything was perfect. She wore a funeral dress, she always did. Her doll was nestled in her right arm. Talena: ...DarkStar? A sick grin slid across his face, his teeth bared. She went to ask a question. But she knew. Metaphors were out the door right now. Things like compassion towards the human race didn't exist. She ran to him, diving into his arms and kissing him deeply. The smoldering kiss pushed him almost out the door as she got down off of him, fixing her dress. He reached up and rubbed the back of his hand over her pale cheek. She laughed loudly. Talena: Violence hunny? He nodded. DarkStar: I am forced to sit alone, watching as other gods sit around and do nothing about the current state of the world. They abandoned it, they abandoned it all like it was nothing. Like it was just a game. It was their fault this is happening, not mine. But I am the scape goat, the traditional animal of blame. It hurts. It even hurts me, who has no heart except for you, my love. All the God's have left, dissapeared, without even a word. He opened the door. Revealing where we were. This is the dream realm. Down below him is the world in flames, its a confusing sight... you can see every conflict, every bit of pain that this has caused. The world is truely collapsing. DarkStar: And I notice that this is a commentary, a precursor to what has happened. I now believe in Destiny. All the Gods have left the earth because of one move. Much like Primtime Championship Wrestling. Months ago... the names, the Gods of PCW left because of one move. The creation of Old School Wrestling. They ran off like the ship was sinking. I stayed behind, and what did I get for it? He smirked and looked to the side. DarkStar: I got dirt rubbed in my face. I had the weight of the world, nay... the GALAXY, thrust onto my smallish shoulders. I am the scape goat. He swung the blade in the air, the reflections of pain, heartbreak, and misery seemed to reflect off of the steel. DarkStar: ...I will fight back. Talena clapped and giggled, and closed the door behind her.
