On the Job (Part I of......Some)

Date: 28.12.02 (#1 FF) Abner’s hungry. His stomach growls. His heart throbs for blood. His brain aches for flesh. His soul screams out for carnage. The one-time content redneck, is no longer in his stage of calmness. The mindset of this hick rides on the back of a leaky old raft, tossed around in a roaring sea of ambition. After nearly a week of not participating in any PCW events, and after almost two of no scheduled matches, Abner can no longer take it. Vengeful of Walden’s cheap-shot at Carnival of Carnage, and weary of Kannon’s RUSH backers at Decimation, this redneck is in the mood to step back into the ring. “No match for me, eh? Horseshit!” Abner grumbles to himself as he takes his seat at his desk. Sitting comfortably in the office of PCW’s Redneck Affairs, Abner tries to sooth himself. “Maybe doin’ some more Goddamn paperwork might just calm me down.” And working along this line, Abner reaches down and pulls out a pad of paper from the drawer in front of him. “Nope, guess not!” Tossing it in the trash, he leans back. With his feet now reclined up on the shiny wooden surface of the desk, Abner lets out a yawn and shuffles around in his chair. “Too Goddamn soft……Horseshit!” Abner, not usually a complainer, goes out of character momentarily due to the circumstances of his present situation. “An expensive, nice black, lather Goddamn piece of shit! Taint worth the milk outta a nanny’s tit, and taint worth the money that ol’ Duke spent on it!” Spitting down into a small trash barrel, Abner throws back his head and covers his eyes in dismay. “What the hell’s a fightin’ man to do when there just ain’t any fightin’ to be done?” He then thinks better of it. “I’ll tell you what to do……you go out and start one yerself Goddamnit!” Rising from his chair, Abner throws on his checkered hunting vest and heads toward the door. RING. He stops dead in his tracks; the phone juts rang. So bolting over to the desk, Abner looks down in wonder. “Now I wonder who in the Hell that is?” Not expecting any calls, and Duke and Tomas getting in some exercise, the bewildered redneck scratches his head in puzzlement. Shrugging, Abner reaches down and prepares to press a small red button on the side of the phone (speaker function). But turning about three inches to the left, he spots a nice, cold Budweiser. Popping open the beer, he takes a few swigs before setting it down and finally answering the phone. In the rudest possible voice in he can make, “What do you want!?” The other line is soft for a moment. And then, filling the DRA’s (Director of Redneck Affair’s) ears, is the gentle voice of what appears to belong to a telemarketer. “Gooooood evening!” Taking the phone back away from his ear for a moment, Abner looks at for a few moments. “Gooooood evening!” And this time Abner responds. “Uh…….evenin’…..” “Hi, I’m Jay Jay Jay of J & J & J’s Redneck Jukebox Junkie magazine, but you can just call me J.J.J……” “Uh…..okay…..” “And I’m here to make your dreams come true!” “Sorry…..End already did that, bye.” But before he can hang up, the salesmen continues. “Oh sir, I’m here to make you the offer of your lifetime!” Sighing, Abner replies, “Sorry…..yer freakin MOTHER already did!” Ignoring this, JJJ continues, “And it’s one you can’t refuse!” “Your right…..I didn’t refuse her…..” Sighing and taking a moment to recover, the salesmen, well trained and trying to stay professional, continues onward. “Okay, buddy I can see the ol’ telemarketer, “kiss the customers ass” approach ain’t gonna work here…..” Amused by this, “Go on….” “So cuttin’ the shit, what this call really is about, is rednecks.” Abner scratches his chin. “Hmm…..” “So let me put it to you plain and simple……now just a few hours ago, from right out of the blue, a certain individual……a friend of yours if you will, got in contact with me…….” Abner’s ears perk up. “You see, in honor of the Rednecks great victories at Decimation, and in honor of this……friend……he would like to donate money to a certain fund of yours…..” “Yes?” “This generous young man, proud to have watched the Rednecks flower and bloom in such a short amount of time, is willing to give you money……if you are to willing to put it to good use? “Good use?” “Yes. Toward an award…..” “An award!?” Abner scratches his head….. “Yes, an award……” Setting down the phone, Abner picks up the Bud, downs it, and throws it in the trash. After a nice, long belch, “Okay, let’s talk……”
