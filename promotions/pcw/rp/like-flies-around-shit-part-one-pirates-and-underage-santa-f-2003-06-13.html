Like Flies Around Shit Part One: Pirates and Underage Santa Fucking...

13 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 13/6/03 “No. You can’t see Pierce Brosnan.” said the Security guard. “Why not?” Warrior moaned. “Because, he doesn’t want to see you!” the Security Guard said. Blind Rage, Warrior and Linda stood, arced around the door of Pierce Brosnan’s VIP suite, where the inept guard stood, poised. He wasn’t going to budge on this one, but Blind Rage had the tendency to be able move the unmovable objects, usually with his brawn. This situation would be no different. “How do you know he doesn’t want to see us?” Blind Rage asked. “Because he told me specifically; ‘if those two psycho’s and that slut come around here bothering me, then don’t let them in.’” the Security Guard explained. Linda is taken back by the comments. “Who’s he callin’ a slut?” “You!” Warrior whispered into her ear, causing Linda to shoot him a dirty look. “I know why he doesn’t want to see us…” Blind Rage stated, smiling. “Yes, because he saw your act with Ben Affleck and Tobey McGuire yesterday!” the Security guard informed. “Exactly!” Blind Rage exclaimed. Both Warrior and Linda looked at him confused. Blind Rage felt them stare at him and so continued with what he was saying. “Brosnan saw us with Affleck and McGuire and realised what was going on. Now he refuses to see us – that’s a guilty conscience if I ever saw one!” Warrior nodded in agreement. “What are you hiding from us?” The Security Guard sighed. “Look, go away or I’ll call the law!” “I AM THE LAW!” Blind Rage bellowed. “And I’m going in whether…” Blind Rage’s rant was cut short by the brash noise of the Airship alarms and the red, shinning danger lights at the end of each corridor. The four of them glance at each other in confusion. “This ain’t good.” Warrior nervously exclaimed. “What the hell is going on?” the Security Guard asked. “How…the…hell…should…I…know!” Blind Rage replied, trying perilously to control his temper. “How could you not know?” the Security Guard replied. “I though that you were the law!” Blind Rage sighed. “We’d better go check it out!” Warrior shook his head. “Hell no! I ain’t goin’ out there, it’s cold!” “Come on!” Blind Rage said, dragging the hapless Warrior out by the base of his neck. Linda looked at the Security Guard woefully. “Sorry about that!” “It’s okay, I guess.” The Guard replied, smirking a little. “Tell Pierce I’ll be along later, for our appointment.” “Do you think you can fit me in sometime tomorrow morning? Pierce has given me the mornin’ off and I could use a little R and R.” Linda extracts he diary and quickly flicks to June 14th. “That costs a little extra though.” She said with a smile. “Yeah, thought so, how does 10.30am suit ya? I can fit you in between Bruce Willis and the Airship Captain.” The Security Guard seemed to think about the prospect a little. “Yeah. That’ll work. Is it the usual price?” “For straight sex, yeah, but if you want R and R that’ll cost extra.” “Okay. I can go for that, see you at 10.30am.” With that Linda wrote the appointment down in her diary. “My feet aren’t gonna touch the FLOOR this weekend!” She then followed the path that Blind Rage and Warrior had set, along the narrow VIP corridor of rooms and up a number of sets of stairs, to the now deserted sun deck. She spotted Blind Rage and Warrior across the way, staring aimlessly up into the sky. She ran over to them and looked at the spectacle herself. It some kind of giant ship, almost the same size as the PCW one, except this ship had guns. Lots of guns. “Oh my god…” Linda exclaimed. “Looks like pirates.” Blind Rage informed, eying up the style of the opposing air ship. Warrior turned to him, slightly, with a disturbed expression upon his face. “Why is it always pirates?” Blind Rage smiled. “Because God hates us Warrior, God hates us.” “He doesn’t hate me!” Linda exclaimed, cheerfully. “Did you fuck him, too?” Warrior asked. “No. God has a white beard, the only guy I fucked with a white beard was that guy dressed up as Santa in the mall, when I was twelve.” She replied, with a grin. “TWELVE?” Blind Rage and Warrior exclaimed in unison. Linda shrugged. “Well, I wanted a pony!”
