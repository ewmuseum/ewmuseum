Back In Black: Mexicans kinda smell like piss

10 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Title: Back In Black: Mexicans kinda smell like piss Featuring: Duke Williams Date: 12/10/02 Mexicans always have a slight funky smell of urine or some shit. Some times one story can fuck up a whole perception of a race. You may call it stereotyping or profiling I call it life lessons that add up. Stereotypes are produced on an element of fact, right or wrong. Most blacks do like fried chicken and watermelon, whites can’t jive, Mexicans smell like urine. It’s all relative. It was back when I was in sixth grade and my school was going to White Pines to spend a week in the wilderness. Most schools take similar trips, in seventh grade it was our state capital, and eighth grade was our nations capital. A progression in travel that marvels only that of maybe John Madden and his cruiser. Anyways about half way into the four-hour bus ride Ray Bentavetaz (a Mexican) had to take a piss. The bus wouldn’t stop and Ray bitched and moaned till finally a small steam came rolling down the bus floor. Ray wet himself. As the years often do we lose touch with those we knew as kids. I was probably a freshman in college when I heard the local news reporter call out the name of Ray Bentavetaz on my TV. I instantly froze and stared at the screen at the kid I knew who was now in man trouble. A kid I even hung out with after school was staring back at me. Him and his father hit up a pawnshop and things got nasty. Ray ended up beating a man to death with a hammer. It also became the first story I covered as a journalist. I’ll never forget going to the cook county jailhouse to interview this murderer I once called a friend. Time had hardened him and I guess me to a lesser extent. It was cold and eerie but I did my job and he played the role of hard gangsta. In the back of my mind the whole time was the whole pee incident. I wonder if that was the one event that triggered him into a life a crime. Some may say environment, others victim of circumstance or society, but me I stick with piss. (“Hog Wild” by Hank Jr. blasts from the speakers as the Malice Man drives down the highway in search for a convenient store to stock up on some booze and beef jerky. He is decked out in sweat pants and t-shirt both of which are stark black. He turns to the presidential camera to cut a promo against that stinky Diablo.) Williams: Ahhh buckaroo’s back for another dose of the Malice Man in the Malice express ready to cut a Malicious sting of sentences tearing into the ass of El Diablo. Woo this is a lot of work don’t mind me if I take a sip of some booze. -Duke has a flask full of whiskey between his legs that he sips on as he drives down the rode.- Williams: Now kids remember not to drink and drive I’m a paid professional. Anywho lets get down to brass tacks shall we? -Duke scrapes some crap out of his teeth looking into the rearview mirror.- Williams: The wetback and the wet liver mix it up yet again. A man from the manhole of thee United States versus a flag waving patriotic nephew of my uncle Sam a rough rowdy fighting Yankee man. How many times are we going to fight this here battle? Everybody knows President Fox makes President Bush look like Steven Hawking. You jive my jam hoss? -Duke smiles- Williams: Mexico, Mexicans, and especially devilish Mexicans are not a factor in the life of any American. Including this glory lover. I bet you have no problem getting a job on Halloween. The haunted house owners love the fact they can hire you at a nickel an hour and you do the bushes around the house. American Devils charge up to five bucks an hour and don’t do yard work. To be honest the American economy strives on you the Mexican Devil. You guys do the jobs no American is willing to do and you do it at half the price. -Duke laughs.- Williams: Why change international procedure this Friday on Fusion? It could spark a whole revolution and open up a bag of shit we don’t want to smell. So lets stick to custom and have you do the job this Friday at half the price!!! -Duke gives a big thumbs up.- Williams: Since I’ve reconverted to Scientology basically I repaid my dues. I too just like Kannon can walk on water. Hey Kannon if your listening its true we can walk on water go give it a try in a real deep shark ridden pool of H2O. -Duke gives the double thumbs up.- Williams: You know what really bites my balls hoss? Why am I not in the main event. Hey spic and span you should be pissed too. El Diablo jobbing to Duke Williams should headline the show not some Rush title match. Who gives two shits from the dumpy ass of Ellie Kannon-Hall. I mean really what is this happy horseshit. -Duke takes a swig from the traveling cantina.- Williams: I mean if you want to ruin a show just end the show with Kannon and Ellie yapping but at least your ruining the show on PCW terms. Now you’re putting the show into the hands of a bunch of talent less hacks. BOO. If you want to ruin a federation give Clyde the title and let him hold it longer then a week. But at least a guy who paid his dues is ruing our fed. Not some Rush rashes here to make our balls itch. This is complete shit. Way to go Kannon. -Duke shakes his head in disgust. Williams: This federation is going to hell in a hand basket and I’m not going to allow it. That kids is the Honky Tonk Truth. HOSS -Duke speeds away.- =w= ---- View Duke Williams's Biography
