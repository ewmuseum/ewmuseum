Ok, I’ll let a classic go.

30 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/30/02 -Duke is sitting in his presidential office at the PCW headquarters. His boots are kicked up on the desk as he’s laid back in his chair talking to the speaker phone.- Speaker: Frisco. Williams: Frisco?? Speaker: Yes on Fusion you have to fight Frisco for a shot at Kannon. Williams: I thought he was retired and running a landscaping business in Fargo?? Speaker: Your thinking Francisco your ex-wife’s landscaper who she cheated on you with. Williams: OOO OOOO you mean the former world champion Frisco? Speaker: That would be the one. Williams: I didn’t know he was still around I thought he went to OSW or some sh*t. Speaker: No he’s running for president. Williams: I’m THE PRESIDENT DAMN IT!!! HAS EVERYBODY FORGOTTEN THAT??? Speaker: More or less yes!! Williams: ASSH*LE!! Speaker: Calm down Duke, he’s running for president of Argentina. Williams: What the hell is an Argentina? Sounds like a yuppie style adult beverage. Speaker: It’s a country. Williams: Well I’ve never heard of it!?! Speaker: Trust me its real. Williams: If you say so hoss. Look if Francisco wants to run for president of some third world country that’s fine with me. I wonder if his platform consists of bringing indoor plumbing and rubbers to the Argeneans of Argentina. Maybe it’s the promise that every foot will have a shoe to wear and every belly will have some slop to eat. Speaker: They’re a little more advanced then that Duke. Williams: Right, and next thing your going to tell is Elvis is alive, or Hulkamaina lives, or the Cosby’s are having a reunion special, or maybe that I have no chance against the UA this Tuesday. Speaker: Ahhhhh yes…. Williams: Come on Jimmy I’m the Malice Man!!! I’m the President of the number one entertainment Mecca in the world. I get I could fly to ArVirginia and find thousands of my hairy ass hillbillies!! Speaker: It’s Argentina. Williams: What ever the point is Frisco and I are both cigar smokers. Speaker: True. Williams: We both enjoy a vocabulary of four letters. Speaker: Um-hum. Williams: His grammar is even almost as good as mine!!! Speaker: Sure thing boss what ever you say. Williams: Jimmy your being sarcastic. Speaker: So? Williams: We’re both men who have been on top of the game. Speaker: OK? Williams: But that’s where the comparisons end. Speaker: They do? Williams: Yes they do. Frisco is a wannabe president, I’m the real thing. Frisco comes out too crappy old man music, I enter to explosive kid friendly music. Frisco knows Lucha moves, I know like three moves and that works just fine for me. Speaker: I mean who doesn’t love the bell clap? Williams: That’s what I’m saying!! It’s a classic that leaves a ringing in your ears. A RINGING IN YOUR EARS!! You don’t find moves like that with that Leach Lube stuff. I represent the hard working men and women of North America he represents the people of some country God knows where. Speaker: Don’t forget the Malice Crew. Williams: That’s right I head up the must deadliest group of wrestlers EVER assembled. Speaker: Right I mean look at all the gold you guys are carrying. Williams: You lucky this is over the phone or I’d staple your tongue to my boot so you can taste me kicking your ass. Speaker: Thanks for the pleasant mental picture. Williams: F*ck you. Speaker: Your place or mine? Williams: Hoss your making get red faced. Look Frisco maybe the nicest guy in the world but fact is fact and the fact is I want Kannon in the ring. Frisco is a mare roadblock that I’m going to have to run over. Speaker: Are you going to go into your run a way Malice train rant now? Williams: Yes. Speaker: Good cus I have to go to the bathroom rant away. Williams: Frisco needs to understand he’s nothing more then a washed up star standing on the railroad tracks at the bottom of a steep hill. I’m a train of Malice with no breaks heading down that hill. Chugging along at full thrust aimed right for Kannon. You stand in the way hoss, but you won’t stand for very long. My freight is the people and all their hopes and dreams. My destination is Hubbardville. ETA: MALICE TIME. Speaker: Ok I’m back. I see you added a new like. Williams: Yea what did you think? Speaker: ETA?? Williams: Estimated Time of Arrival. Speaker: I know that, but ETA: Malice Time is a tad lame. Williams: Thanks for you opinion Jimmy. Speaker: Your not going to change it are you? Williams: No. Speaker: You’ve been using that train line since the late eighty’s. Its was cute in the ICWA but it don’t cut it in the PCW. Williams: So I should drop it? Speaker: I would. Williams: Ok, I’ll let a classic go. Speaker: Good. Williams: I’m keeping Malice Time though. Speaker: That works. Williams: So anyway the bottom line is this….. Speaker: I don’t think you can say that. Williams: Why not? Speaker: Another wrestler uses it. Williams: Really? Ok Speaker: …. Williams: How about so the point is… Speaker: That works. Williams: So the point is this, Frisco I dig ya hoss but its not your time. I want Kannons little neck and I’ll send anybody in the way WHISKEY BENT…. Speaker: AND HELL BOUND. Williams: You knew that was coming didn’t you? Speaker: If you were going for the element of surprise you failed. Williams: Why do I have a promo coach anyway? Speaker: They thought you needed me to modernize your style. Williams: This sucks!! Modernize this. -Duke slams the phone down, then looks into the camera.- Williams: Bottom line is this Frisco, your only chance against me is if the UA put me in a body cast on Turmoil. But hoss, that ain’t gonna happen so I’m sorry to inform you but your going on a little trip. Whiskey Bent and Hell Bound HOOOOOSSSSSSSSSSSSSSSSSSSSSSSSSSSSSS!! -The camera fades.-
