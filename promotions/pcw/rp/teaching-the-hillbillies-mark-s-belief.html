Teaching the hillbillies Mark's belief...

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6-31-02 ::Introduction:: Well it looks as if Mark Makonie is perhaps the stupidest wrestler ever to walk to face of the Earth. How can he miss a question that easy on a IQ test? Well I am not going to bullshit you, it was just one of those mental mistakes on his mind. Mark was not I repeat was not looking past Akiyama on Fusion. I was manhandled by the Neo Void God himself because someone came out from the back and made me lose. That person was Ryan “Streak” Stephens and he made me lose concentration in my match on Fusion. Now he said that Akiyama gave me a real bad beating. Well that could have been because when Akiyama and Mark were pretty much even on the outside you come out and just gave Mark a smirk that just wanted me to go up and beat your ass down. Mark didn’t have time to because I was met with a big boot to the face by Akiyama himself. So if you want to play the mind games with Mark, then that is fine because tonight is the night that Mark Makonie is going to get his wish with you in the ring. ::End Introduction:: The scene opens up to a local Kentucky town with the normal hillbillies surrounding the local pub. Mark is making a trip around the town looking for something to do. Mark is looking around and he sees some local hillbillies making fun of some out of towners. These out of towners are not any out of towners, they are African Americans who have never ever seen hillbillies in their life before. Mark tells the bus driver to stop at the curb in which the hillbillies are at. They all have very pale skin and the teeth that are crooked and some that are missing. They are wearing overalls and the have on some farm boots that have manure on the bottom of them. Mark gets off of the bus and he starts to walk on the gray cemented sidewalk walking near the hillbillies. “You going coon hunting?” asks one of the hillbillies who has on a straw hat and is chewing on a long piece of hay. “What in the hell do I look like?” Mark asks as he turns around to look at the hillbillies. “Do I actually look like a hillbilly to you?” “Well are you going to go coon hunting, because if you are we know this real good spot to go.” The same hillbilly says as he smiles with the piece of straw in his mouth. “Well my coon hunting is probably different from where I come from then what your is.” Says Mark as he is now walking towards the three hillbillies who are still looking at Mark. ‘How is that so?” A different hillbilly says as he has a bitter beer in his hand, sipping it every two seconds. “Well where I come from we call them over there coons.” Mark says as he points over to the African Americans who are lost in the little town in Kentucky. “Oh yeah?” The hillbilly with the bitter beer in his hand asks. “Yes that is true, by a matter of fact I think that it is time that people like you guys should learn a little lesson in where I come from.” Mark says as he eyes all three of the hillbillies as they still are paying attention to Mark. “See where I come from I teach people to believe in a certain belief.” “Fishing?” asks the third hillbilly who is leaning up on the building. “No it is not fishing as a lot of hillbillies like you do.” Mark says as the sun is glaring down on Mark’s baldhead. “It is something else that no one around here has probably been introduced to. It is a certain and special belief that only I know and that I am going to take down to the grave with me. People call it a bad belief, but I just look at it that they have a different opinion than what I do.' 'So tell us all what this belief that you have is.' The hillbilly in the straw hat says as he sounds interested. 'Well as you can see I do not like the African American race as it is.' Mark says as he is thinking about what to say to his future followers. 'I think that they should not even be in America with the white race, that would be us, and I think that I could use a few followers down here in Kentucky.' 'Well this concept sounds pretty interesting, but we all don't see that many African Americans down here in Kentucky.' the hillbilly with the bitter beer in his hand says as they all look intently interested in this concept. 'It is a very basic concept.' Mark starts to explain as he wants these hillbilles to represent Kentucky. 'All you have to do is believe that the African American race should not be in America and that they are not equal to what we are. This is accomplished by going around making fun of African Americans and pointing out what they do wrong. This is very easy and hoping that all of you are interested in doing this.' 'Do we have to dress up in those white gowns and wear those pointed hats?' The hillbillies all ask as they don't want to wear that. 'Well you can wear whatever you want because it is not what you look like, it is what kind of personality that you have.' Mark says as he is still looking at the hillbillies, getting sweat built up on his brow. 'Come inside with us.' The hillbillies say as they look at Mark still and stare. Mark nods as the hillbillies start to head inside of the local pub. They open up the glass door as they head inside and a gust of cold air comes out of the building as Mark goes in last following the hillbillies. Inside of the pub the place is filled up with hillbillies sitting around talking and drinking their beer that they brew themselves. The three hillbillies go up to the bar counter to see the old man dressed in a flannel shirt and a straw hat. He has a gray beard hanging down to his chest with tobacoo dried up in it. 'What can I get you'll?' asks the man with the gray beard as he opens his mouth to show the tobacoo stuck inbetween his yellow stained teeth. 'Well we have a friend here who hasn't been in this part of Kentucky.' The guy with the bitter beer starts to say. 'He taught us the right belief and now we are going to treat him to anything he would like in here.' 'So what will it be?' asks the bartender as he is still smiling to show off his teeth and tobacoo teeth. 'Let me try some of your finest brewed beer that you have here in Kentucky because I have heard that this is the best.' Mark says as he is about to get a shocker in the beer. The bartender goes over to the tap as Mark takes a seat in the barstool. He watches the bartender pull down the lever to have the gold liquid go down into the chilled glass and then he lets up on it as the foam reaches the top of the glass. The bartender goes back over to where Mark is sitting and he slams down the glass making some of the beer go out of the glass. 'There you go son.' The bartender says as he goes back to the cash register. Mark takes the glass and he raises it up to his mouth. He tilts the glass to have the gold liquid run down into his throat only to have it come back up and being spit on all three of the hillbillies. The hillbillies get drilled with the mist of beer as they all start to laugh as they are standing there smelling like beer. 'This is the worst beer I have ever tasted and this is the worst place I have ever been in my life.' Mark says as he stands up from his seat. 'I can't believe people actually come here and visit. Oh yeah you three I don't want you to represent my beliefs because you will probably tell people that African Americans are good.' With that said Mark heads out of the local pub and he waits by the bus stop. All of the people in the pub start to talk again as Mark is just sitting all by himself and thinking about the upcoming match tonight with Streak. 'Who cares that I missed a question?' Mark starts to say to himself as he has his head down. 'I don't like I said it is not the math or english that has to deal with wrestling, it is how you perform every night. So I am just going to say Streak, that this match tonight is up for grabs, but now that I think about it it is already in the bag for me.' With that said the bus pulls up to the stop and it opens up its doors. Mark gets into the bus and he finds out that the whole bus is filled with nuns that are singing. ::See you at Gold and Glory tonight!::
