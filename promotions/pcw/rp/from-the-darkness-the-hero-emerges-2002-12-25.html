From The Darkness... The Hero Emerges

25 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12/25/02 In this darkness... I have murdered many... Many have felt the last caress of the Lost Angel... But this time things were different... The dim lights click on as Mani enters the abandoned hotel. Scuffling sounds overhead as he looks up and bolts for the stairs. The children... Alex and Christa... Two kids whom I had kidnapped... Two innocents whom I had brought fear to... The door in front of Mani folded and splintered under the strength of his legs as a figure bent over the children spun on its heel and stared at Shigeru... The one man I thought was like me... The one man I thought would understand my cause... YAGYUUUUUUUUUUUU! Shin Yagyuu slowly stood up a knife in his hand... Shin: Mani... You are here just in time for the fun... Alex and Christa looked up at the man they called capture as if he were their savior.. Alex's face a pale white as a thin line of blood formed from a hairline cut. The cut was seemingly just deep enough to draw blood as the boy stared into the golden eyes of Mani. Shin your sick games leave me with no choice but to kill you again... Shin: Mani what is the point of killing me over and over? Will you watch these two every moment of every day? No Shin... I won't... But you and I both know the one thing even you fear... The one dark shadow that looms over your blackened heart... The shadow is me... I can end you at anytime and you know it... I have been attempting to open your eyes to what you could be... But if I find you have hurt these children then I will give you a reason for your fears.... The white hell Akiyama imprisoned you in is nothing compared to what I can do to you.... Shin's eyes slowly turned away from Mani. Within a blink the gap between the men was closed as Mani lunged at Shin. A swift hard kick connected with Shin's midsection as a stiff knee to his sternum followed it. When Shin... A hard blow to the skull slammed Shin Yagyuu's body to the wooden floor as Mani raised his heel When will you learn that Life is PRECIOUS! The heel came down hard driving Yagyuu through the floor into the room below.. Alex and Christa hugged each other closely as Mani dropped down into the blackness of the hole. What will it take before you open those eyes and understand what Life is... As Mani fell he readied his knee for impact.. seconds later his knee drove hard into Shin's stomach sending the two to the ground floor. Shin black mask suddenly stained red as he coughed up blood. Shin: Mani! Stop! Mani however refused to stop. Grasping Shin's head he raised his knee slamming it into the Japanese assassin's temple. Teacher vs Pupil Yagyuu... How long can you last against my perfection.... You know you are flawed... As flawed as any regular human being is.. Shin's eyes widened in fear as Mani ripped the blue razor wire from his wrist.. Shin: NOOOOOOOOOOO! A desperation kick landed on Mani's chest throwing him off as Shin jumped to his feet and disappeared out into the night.... --==-- Two stories up Christa and Alex sat huddled their legs bound. Fear crept through their hearts as they heard the noises below... Two men fighting for their lives apparently... Then came the sudden metallic grinding scream that both men brandished like a hacksaw on bone... NOOOOOOOOOOO! Both children would have screamed out had their mouths not been gagged... Utter silence filled the room for a few moments... --==-- Mani slowly stood and dusted himself off. The razor wire at his wrist dissolved as he fixed his mask. Shin Yagyuu... What ever will I do with you... Maybe I'll just slit your throat and rid the world of your menace... The Silent Assassin slowly walked over to the steps and began to scale them --==-- The children sat pressed against each other in dread as footsteps slowly began up the stairs.. The same thud that had accompanied Yagyuu when he scaled them the first time... However when the masked figure reached the top of the stairs... Golden glow accompanied him as his eyes shone bright... A sigh of relief breathed through the gags as the two children watched the young oriental man leap across the hole in the floor and land next to them... As he landed though the floor gave way and all three fell... --==-- The fall caught Mani by shock as his hands quickly grabbed Christa and Alex... Air rushed past his face as he awaited the certain shock of death that followed each fatal moment he crossed paths with...... A sudden spike of pain shot through his midsection as he slowly set the children down... The bright white of death consumed him as he lay in the vast garden... Kiana.... The smell of his only love... It was always strong in this flash of the after life... Voice: Mani sweetheart... You are fighting so hard.. I'm so proud of you... 2nd Voice: I'm proud too dad! Suddenly the white light vanished as Mani awoke impaled on a steel girder... Alex and Christa both laid next to him still bound with tears in their eyes... I wonder... How much it will shock them when I pull myself up off this deathbed... Mani's hands suddenly shot up grasping the thin metal and forcing himself up off it.. Both children stared in utter shock as he stood up and picked them both up.. Alex, the voice you hear in your head is the best way I have to speak.. I know you are scared but I promise you will get home... The young boy nodded his head as Mani removed the gag. A moment later he removed Christa's and carried the children into the blackness of the night... The morning came quickly as the sun rose... The home seemed deafeningly empty without the children who had graced its halls... The children who now slept silently on the couch... Storm was no where to be found as Mani leaned up against the wall awaiting someone who could take care of the two children to awaken... A slight rustling in another room snapped Mani's eyes open as he turned his head towards the door.. Tessa Windsor stumbled out of the room as though she had just woken up... The young woman walked right by Mani in her daze.. But as she saw the sleeping bodies of Alex and Christa her energy shot through the roof.. Tessa bolted across the room and woke both children.. Tessa: Oh my god... How... Alex slowly sat up sleep still in his eyes and pointed behind Tessa.. The woman turned to see Mani Shigeru... He slowly stood up from leaning against the wall and locked eyes with her... I'm sorry is the best I can say... I never meant for it to go this far... Oh and I got a little blood on your wall and carpet... Mani slowly backed up and disappeared through a window.. On the floor a puddle of black blood sat.. Large enough to have been from hours of bleeding... In darkness I must live... Light is for the happy... Fade
