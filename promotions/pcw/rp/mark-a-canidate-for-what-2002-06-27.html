Mark a canidate? For what?

27 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6-27-02 ::Introduction:: With getting bailed on Friday morning by one of his good buddies and then he had to fly to the Fusion card in hopes to beat Streak. With a result of a double count out Mark was kind of happy that no one won because we will find that out at Gold and Glory where there will be no countout. But he has a warmup match with Akiyama on Friday Fusion, but he knows that match will be the momentum swinger for him for Gold and Glory. Mark has no problem with Akiyama it is just they want to give Mark a challenge before he goes against Streak. Now it is up to Mark to see if he really wants this pride of his or not. If he does then he will not get upsetted at Gold and Glory because if that happens then he is going to torture himself in a way that is not thinkable, only to Mark. ::End Introduction:: The scene opens up on the small streets of a small town in California. The sun is beating down on the groud. A kid without any shoes on is running across the street barefoot and he looks as the bottom of his feet get burned just by going across the black top twice of a local street. Mark is inside a white one story building with a black shingled rook. It has a beautiful white door on it and a neatly cut lawn. Inside the house Mark is sitting down on the couch and is talking to a local friend of his. 'I just want to thank you for making me see life in a different way.' Mark says as he is talking to his friend who is sitting on a chair to the left of Mark. 'Well you just looked as if you needed a way out of your life and it seems that you have found that light at the end of the tunnel you needed.' Jax, Mark's friend, says as he is looking at Mark and the swastika found on Mark's shirt. 'You definatly did that for me because you see now I am a pretty recognized star in the PCW.' Mark says as he is looking at the Chinese symbols on Jax's shirt. 'When you came up to me that one day my life has changed and I will prove it to you now.' 'How is that Mark?' asks Jax as he sits back in the reclining chair more. 'I have seen your preformances on tv with wrestling and how you have been against African Americans since I got you into it. How are you going to prove it to me?' 'Just come downtown with me to the square and I will show you what I mean.' Mark says as he sits back in the couch like Jax did in the recliner. Mark just gets up out of the couch and he heads over to the white door. Jax follows him to the front door and Mark puts his hand on the silver door handle as he turns it clockwise. He heads out of the door with Jax locking the door on his way out behind Mark. They both go in Jax's looking mafia car across the street from his house. They both open up their doors with Jax going to the driver's seat. Mark sits in on the passenger side and then Jax starts up the black colored mafia looking car with the cd player blasting Rammstein over the speakers. 'Just goto the square like I told you.' Mark says as he turns down the radio. Jax just nods and he turns the radio back up as the pulls out from the curb and onto the hot black top of the road. In a spot on the road where tar is sitting there are bubbles coming up from it as the sun is making it bubble like that because it is so hot. As they get up to the square, the main part of the small town where there are shops and main attractions, and they find a parking spot. There are a whole bunch of cars parked and lined up for a special occasions. 'What in the hell is going on up here?' Jax asks as he is shutting the car's engine off. 'Well they were advertising an election for the National Socialist Party Of America and I was going to run for it.' Mark says as he gets out of the car and as Jax does too. 'So you are telling me that you are in this election for the National Scoialist Party Of America, the one that was started by George Lincoln Rockwell?' Jax asks as he is getting excited about the mention of the National Socialist Party. 'Yes the one that was started by my good ol friend and one of my true rolemodles George Rockwell.' says Mark as he is talking to Jax as they walk one part of the square. 'Well yes he is our spritual leader as they say for the Neo-Nazis and the regular Nazis.' Jax says as they cross a street going from one part of the square to another. 'He is the guy alongside from Adloph Hitler who made us see what we need to accomplish though life with the right races and whatnot.' That ends the conversation as Mark and Jax get to the sight of where everyone is lined up waiting to hear the speeches of the canidates of the runners. There is a guy with a long beard hanging down to his chest who is giving a speech about how he is the best for this job. Mark is the last person to give his speech, right after this guy, as he was the last person to be nominated for the spot. 'That is why I should be elected for the National Socialist Party Of America because I care about the white race and I can really show what race is the main race.' says the man up on the podium as he finishes up his speech. Mark walks over to the podium as Jax goes back to the rows of seats that they have. As people look on Mark steps up on the podium and he takes out a piece of paper from his pocket and he unfolds it. He places it up on the slanted part of the podium as the sun beats down on top of his bald head. He puts the microphone down to his height and then he takes a couple of breaths. 'What is a right canidate for the National Socialist Party Of America?' Mark starts out saying for his speech. 'Well I think a person who believes in what out leader did, George Rockwell, and I think that I do believe in what George has taught us.' Mark is still looking down at his little sheet of paper and then flips on the light to help him see better. Although it is all sunny out and at midday time, but Mark is beyond nervous at this point right now. People in the audience are wearing sunglasses and that makes it better for Mark as he doesn't have to stare in fifty some or more eyes. 'I do not mind the Jews, but it is the African American race in which I do not believe should be here in American living.' Mark starts his speech again after he pauses for a little bit. 'Now I think that the African American race is not as good as us white race and that they should be put in death camps or sent back to where they came from.' Mark again looks into the crowd and he sees that Jax is sitting back into his seat, relaxing like always. He looks out and he sees the other canidates just sitting down looking up at Mark while they are dressed in their nice suits. 'Like people have said: This is not a joke and should not be taken lightly. People are believing in what I believe in right now and I know some people who are like that. I have been looking for something like this ever since I have been believing in what I do and now that I have found one I think that I can make it better.' Mark had the shortest speech of everyone as he gets down from the podium with his little piece of paper. He puts the paper back into his pocket and he goes over to where Jax is sitting. 'Now since everyone has heard the canidates it is time to vote.' says a lady who got up on the podium after Mark. People get up out of their chairs and they all start to head over to the voting booths on the otherside of the square. Mark and Jax just reamain seated looking up at the podium. 'Well you did your best.' says Jax as he looks at Mark as he looks like he didn't do good. 'Well I thought that I could've gave more information on the history of the party, but I wasn't here to listen to everyone elses speech.' Mark says as he is looking down at the ground now. Mark is thinking of Stephens right now and about the Pay Per View. Akiyama is not on his mind as that match is not that big, only a mood setter for Gold and Glory. Mark is hoping that nothing bad happens from now to then.
