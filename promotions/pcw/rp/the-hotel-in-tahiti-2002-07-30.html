The hotel in Tahiti....

30 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7-30-02 ::Introduction:: Mark’s life has changed a lot since the last pay per view there was. There was first the resigning of Stephens and then Mark wasn’t booked for a little while after that. Then Mark finally gets booked, but in a tag team match with the one person he doesn’t want as his partner, Duzza. Then after Duzza screws the team over by losing the match for the team he isn’t booked for Wrestle Vessel. But then the next two cards Mark has to comprehend with tagging with Duzza and trying to pick up some tag team wins. Now who are these turbans wearing Refugees who Mark and Duzza have to fight later tonight? Well they are a tag team who don’t even cut promos in PCW and who don’t even deserve a shot to fight the team of Mark and Duzza. Why you ask? Well as Duzza’s promo shows, the two can’t live one minute without complaining about one another, but Mark thinks that this is the way that he will go to win gold. Why is Mark teaming with an African American then? Because the management probably thinks it is funny to see two men who are on the same team fight with each other. Now to Mark this is not funny at all, he is only doing this to win some gold, not for friendship. But will this turn Mark into liking African Americans now? ::End Introduction:: The scene opens up to outside of Tahiti. The bright sun shines into the hotel room as Mark starts to wiggle around in the bed no wanting to wake up. Mark gets up out of his bed and he goes over to the window. He looks out of the window as he sees the clear blue water that is on the ocean. Mark seems to have peace with the ocean, unlike Duzza. The sun shines right in Mark’s eyes as they are still sensitive to light because he has been sleeping. He rubs the tiredness out of his eyes as he looks out of the window some more thinking of where he is in life right now. “Tonight is the night that we will find out where the team of me and Duzza is.” Mark starts to say as he goes over to the table and he pulls out a chair and places it right in front of the window. “Tonight we will see how my team tags together and how they will be able to cope with each other. Now these turban people are probably not going to show up for a promo shot or anything like that. So if that happens then we have the match in the bag. Or at least I do since I am the team right now. I have a crappy partner that probably wants to lose instead of winning. Mark thinks some more about the match and then he gets up out of the chair. He goes over to the drawers underneath the television. Mark pulls out one of the drawers and he picks out a t-shirt that has a picture of Mark on it. It says: The only real African American hater in the world, on the back of the t-shirt. Mark slips that over his head as he doesn’t have to worry about messing up his hair. Then he puts on some white socks as he goes over to where he put his boots the night before. Mark slips them on as he laces them up and ties them. “Man this is way too early for me to be up.” Mark says to himself as he sits on the bed. Mark doesn’t even look at the time yet as he goes out of the door. He takes his key as he sticks it in his pocket as he closes the door behind him. He starts to head down the hallway as Mark is going to the elevator. Mark goes over to the elevator as no one is around. But before he goes on the elevator, he goes over to one of the candy vending machines. “I need some food before I go out today.” Mark says as he takes a dollar bill out of his pocket. “Hmm….what should I pick? Baby Ruth? No that has too much chocolate to it. Hmm…Reese’s? Nah that has peanut butter in it and plus it is covered with black chocolate. Ahh, here we go, a Chewy Oatmeal Bar. That is what I will have as since it has oatmeal in it and plus it don’t have anything black in it.” Mark puts the dollar bill inside of the vending machine as he pushes to button to get the Chewy Oatmeal Bar. He puts his hand down to the slot as he pushes in the plastic to get the bar. He takes it out of the slot and he unwraps the bar. He puts the wrapper under the vending machine since he didn’t see a trash can around any where. He heads back over to the elevator and he pushes the down button. “Looks like a nice day to take a scenic route of the Tahiti countryside.” Mark says as the elevator doors open up to see that no one is on the elevator with him. “That is how I like it also, peace and quiet.” Mark hits the ground level button on the elevator as it goes down to the second floor. The doors open and who is standing there waiting to get on the elevator? Duzza with his headphones on his ears listening to the child molester. “Oh no not you again!” Mark screams as Duzza can hear him through the headphones and as he looks up. “Oh no you again.” Duzza says as he takes the headphones off of his ears. “You better not step in this elevator or I swear that I will beat you down to a bloody pulp.” Mark says as he backs up to the rear of the elevator. “Well if I do step on this elevator and if you beat my ass down then you will not have a partner for tonight.” Duzza says as he shuts off his walkman and he takes a step into the elevator. “Well that is the same ol same ol since you lose the matches for our team.” Mark says as he is building up some anger. “Well should I just take the stairs then?” Duzza asks to himself as he looks into Mark’s baby blue eyes. “Yes!” Mark says as he is looking angrier than ever. “Well no I will more likely save my energy for tonight’s match.” Duzza says as he steps inside of the elevator. Inside the elevator Duzza stands right in front of Mark and Mark steps right in front of Duzza. The two get right up in each others face as they have some words to exchange with each other. “I swear if you fuck up again one more time than our team is gone.” Mark says as he is nose to nose with Duzza. “I wasn’t the one who was fucking up and plus you don’t have control on how the team stays or how it leaves, the management does.” Duzza says as he is nose to nose with Mark still. “Well you better have my back for the next two matches since we are teaming in those two.” Mark says as he is backing up from Duzza. “Plus on Fusion, do you know how bad I want to demolish Rush Mason?” “I will have your back Mark, but only if you have mine.” Duzza says as the two men are backed away. “Plus, I want to destruct the rest of the so called “Uprising” with you since there is only one man to go. And that man will go down when we get a hold of him on Fusion.” The two men stare each other down as the elevator door opens up to the ground floor. There is a crowd of people standing there waiting to get on the elevator. Mark and Duzza are still staring each other down as they look around to see the people staring at them. The crowd of people go in a laughter as Mark and Duzza leave the elevator. “Now you probably have that whole crowd of people thinking we are gay Duzza.” Mark says as the two of them go past the crowd. “You were the one who was staring at me so don’t blame me for it.” Duzza says as they start to go their own ways. “I don’t want to see you until tonight in the ring because you will just ruin the whole day for me and you already have.” Mark shouts as Duzza exits the hotel and Mark goes into the bathroom. With that, Mark heads into the bathroom with even more on his mind than just Duzza. He is thinking about how they are both going to cope with each other at the match tonight. Will Duzza screw him over tonight and on Fusion? Who knows, all Mark is thinking that if he does get screwed than the team will not be able to function well. Mark only lives his life some at the time. He doesn’t plan out what he is doing in advanced, he just goes full force and will pick up anyone on the way. But if you are not going to go, than get out of his way because he has a purpose in life.
