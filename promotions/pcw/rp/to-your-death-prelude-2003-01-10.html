To Your Death: Prelude

10 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1/10/03 A man creeps through darkened catacombs. With a torch gripped in his hand, he brushes ancient cobwebs aside as he continues onward....his destination unknown. For a short while has he hunted for relics, those lost trinkets of times past in the old lands of Egypt....however little does he suspect that now, something hunts him as well.... Man: Hm. With his companions behind him, the slightly round faced adventurer reaches his goal. An ancient sarcophagus, the face of a long dead ruler enblazened on the front. Man: Alright, here we go. Putting the handle of the torch between his teeth, he reaches out for the lid of the old coffin....wraps his hands around it, and pulls with all his might. Man: (Straining) Ruuuuuuuurgh! In spite of his best efforts, the lid refuses to budge. Nevertheless he tries again.....slowly, ever so slowly, it creaks. Man: Ruuuuuuurgh......ruuuuuuurgh...... More, more, the sarcophagas moves, then at last falls to the dusty ground with a loud crash. Man: Okay, now let's.... Our friend hunches over the box, picking up his torch again and holding it close. Not seeing much, he squints.....then yelps as a hand shoots out of the black, clutching him by the windpipe. Man: (Choking) Hackack! From the darkness of the coffin, a form rises. It sneers as it squeezes even tighter, its eyes full of cruel humor. Clyde: Beware of curses, Mr. Fraser. You of all people should know that. A chuckle arises from the demon's lips, as he pulls and yanks the poor man's throat clean out.....fading however as he looks closer. Clyde: Hey you're not Brendan Fraser..... 'CUT!!!!!' The voice of a director booms forth, as the lights snap back to life in the tomb. Clyde: This is an outrage. That man was not Brendan Fraser! I demand Brendan Fraser! The poor facsimile of Mr. Fraser slumps to the ground dead, as another man bearing an odd resemblance to a more overweight and lower budget Steven Spielberg stomps forth. Not Steven Spielberg: You moron, that was our replacement Brendan! Clyde: .....then where's the real Brendan? Not Steven Spielberg: He's dead! Clyde: He can't be dead, I haven't killed him yet! Not Steven Spielberg: Well he is. Clyde: Is not. Not Steven Spielberg: Is! Clyde: Not! Not Steven Spielberg: Is! Clyde: Not! Not Steven Spielberg: Is! Clyde: Bah! Still sitting in the empty prop coffin, the burned man fumes. Clyde: How the hell did he die? Not Steven Spielberg: Death by removal of all his organs, then eating of the organs, then vomiting up of the organs and stepping on them until they were squishy. Clyde: Bet the coroners had fun with that one. Not Steven Spielberg: And how! I'm already in the bidding for the movie rights....just need to throw in the giant shark. It'll be MONEY IN THE BANK! Clyde: Bah! How am I supposed to pointlessly kill Brendan Fraser in a veiled yet slightly witty jab at Pharaoh if Brendan Fraser's already been pointlessly killed? The demon rages, his features tightening in hatred. Clyde: That's MY shtick! MINE! I kill the celebrities around here! Me! His eyes blaze, bleeding fire. Clyde: Who would DARE such an affront? I will kill them! I swear on the souls of all that suffer in the torments of Hell, whoever it is shall DIE!!!!! Not Steven Spielberg: A skeleton from the witness accounts. Skeleton in a cloak. Clyde: ...... The burned man pauses, looking on. Clyde: Hm. Interesting. Not Steven Spielberg: Still gonna kill him? This could be GOLD! Clyde: Yes, yes I suppose I shall. Clyde turns, his face darkening. Clyde: Dark Gimmick.....you shall DIE!
