Back to Bainbridge - Rosemary Boyd

14 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 05/14/03 (OOC: Back to regular style of writing.. as in 'unknown narrator' speaker.. or something of that matter.) The moon hung over the sky like a carcass on rope. Emitting a deep white glow over the Bainbridge Necropolis, scant of light. Wind barreled through in to the vault of graves, and sepulchers. Browsing through the cemetery full of posies and tombs, finds the blade wielding magnifico, Stone Burns standing over a burial ground, next to his immured chamber. His eyes were unusually bleared, but he was still vestured in his dark black tux, and firmly holding his now newly reinstated blade to his side. But to the possession of his other arm lay three roses, all in equal size. 'Anna Blasch -- a dainty soul, yet still an evil lurking carelessly.. death was the only answer.' he began, placing the first out of three roses to the on the soiled ground. 'Emily Burns -- my only love, no evil, no death needed.. but she was the resolution to my campaign.' he continued digging in to one of his two roses, and placing it adjacent to the other rose before it. It came to the last rose where he stared at the meager stem, and then up.. “Stephanie Burns -- my true love, but the cuckold that made me carry on all of this.. she was the fuel to my campaign.. and to her lays all the evil in women.. That’s why they should be dwindled by a driven male.' he ended, placing the last rose to the side of the others, standing up, but kneeling down and spitting upon each rose in utter disgust, allaying the water and redness in his eyes and cheeks. Gazing up in to the ever SO radiant stars, and in to the moon now changed in moiety. The wroth wind continued, as he blurted several words, yet not audible from the pounding audio of the wind. After moments of the wind swinging back and forth, up and down like a seesaw, it stopped as Stone looked down, and dug in to his pocket.. Exposing a sheet of paper in a riffle ball.. flattening it, a name was unveiled, with the far from quaint scripter Stone was the name was at bare sight, adding to that was the sand-blind view of Stone.. But impertinent to the subject.. the name dryly read.. 'Rosemary Boyd' With the devilish grin caked across his face, he littered the paper over the cemetery showing no respect of his scenery.. Stone strolled forward, leaving a slight clue of what the name meant but still slubber of vagueness.
