*Secret Chat Transcript*

8 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/8/02 To all you knowledge-hungry bastards: The following is a chat transcript from a private meeting between two parties. The parties will not be named because their screen names pretty much give away their identity. You better enjoy this; we lost three men retrieving this from the hard drive of a prominent Islamic Militant’s computer. Um, did I say Islamic Militant? Damn, I just said it again! Well, I’m about to pop a suicide pill here any second, so take care, and pray for my soul (I was never baptized). Sincerely, Stanley WatsonChief Director of Chef Promos Inc. and the FBI or something like that ***Chat Transcript*** Date: 11/5/02 Time: 2200 hrs. Chat Room: STAYTHEHELLOUT!!! *Cheffy4u has entered the chat room* *Osama69xxx has entered the chat room* Cheffy4u: Sup Osama69xxx: Howdy! Cheffy4u: How are things? Osama69xxx: Good. The cave reeked of dead Special Forces agents, but one of the corpses had a Bath and Body Works bottle. “Woodland” was the scent I believe, anyways, I sprayed it around and everything’s cool now. Cheffy4u: Sweet Osama69xxx: Have you been reading the Holy Koran? Cheffy4u: Nah, Bertha locked it up in some cabinet. Osama69xxx: That’s a shame, how will you make it to paradise without God’s infallible word as your guide? Cheffy4u: I think I’ll wait until the resurrection of Christ, and then cook him some muffins or something. That’s a guaranteed free ticket to heaven! Osama69xxx: *Sighs* Cheffy4u: Are you mad at me? Osama69xxx: I’m mad at myself. I’ve gained twenty pounds in these caves, there’s nothing to do besides play X-Box. Cheffy4u: I understand completely. Even with games like Circus Maximus, days and days of constant playing will eventually led to boredom, and disappointment… Osama69xxx: You said it. So… why did you want to talk to me? Cheffy4u: I’ve got a problem. Osama69xxx: I figured that… Cheffy4u: Well, considering the problem I do have, I think you, above all other people, would know… Osama69xxx: Shoot Cheffy4u: I think someone is trying to kill me! Cheffy4u: O-Dawg? You there? Osama69xxx: Isn’t it obvious? My guards, assassins, fanatics, peons, and lawyers have been scouting and searching around the world looking for you! You blasphemed against Allah and single-handedly destroyed the first Muslim stable in PCW!!! You must pay for your transgressions against the one true faith!!! Cheffy4u: I didn’t mean you, I meant someone else Osama69xxx: Oh, well um… why do you think someone is trying to kill you, besides me of course? Cheffy4u: I found sticks of dynamite in my brioche… Osama69xxx: So? Not a day goes by when I find some in mine… Cheffy4u: There was a grenade hidden in my turtle soup! Osama69xxx: Meh Cheffy4u: Meh!?!? Osama69xxx: Yeah, Meh… Cheffy4u: Well, I found parasitic ringworms in my angel hair pasta! Osama69xxx: Angel Hair!?!? With the lemon and balsamic vinaigrette? With or without capers? Cheffy4u: With capers… Osama69xxx: DAMN!!! I miss your cooking Cheffy4u: Thanks for the compliment, but that still doesn’t solve my predicament!!! Osama69xxx: Listen Chef, I’m being hunted by the most powerful nation in the world, and I still haven’t been found! (Note: People that haven’t played the game will not get the following joke) Cheffy4u: Yeah, but only because of the Invisible technique you learned from Sly Cooper… Osama69xxx: What can I say, not everyone has a Thievius Raccoonus in their possession… (Note: Even if you played the game and understand the joke, it probably wasn’t very funny) Cheffy4u: I’m scared… frightened… terrified! I know someone is trying to hand me a one-way ticket to slab city… I know it!!! Osama69xxx: Unless it’s one of my henchmen, you probably have nothing to worry about. Cheffy4u: You really think so!?!? Osama69xxx: Seriously, how many times has someone tried to kill you in the past? Cheffy4u: Let’s see there’s at least ten separate occasions of life or death struggles with Emeril, then there’s Martha Stewart, Tom Cruise or “King of the Homos”, George W. Bush, the Dalai Lama, Moses Malone, The Green Arrow, Steve Martin, Bertha Stewart Clone #2, Jim Bob the Retarded Hillbilly, you, Chester Bin Laden, and that evil guy from Color Tile who exploits my allergy to linoleum… Osama69xxx: Wow, you are a marked man… Cheffy4u: I know! Osama69xxx: But I stand by my advice. Please go back to your normal life and resume all normal daily functions, and be sure to stand by the window a lot so my snipers can pick you off…wait, did I just type that!?!? I’m such a goose… Cheffy4u: Ok, thanks buddy… Osama69xxx: Anytime, infidel… *Cheffy4u has left the chat room* *ItsRenoTime666 has entered the chat room* ItsRenoTime666: Hey Osama… Osama69xxx: Janet!!! Long time no chat… ***End of Chat Transcript***
