Life Without Love & Many Other Rent-A-Thoughts In A Test Tube...

19 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4-19-2002 ..::/::..I suppose this is where you expect the narrator to jump in and say something to the effect of 'when we last left our hero'...is that your final answer citizen? If it is, your wrong citizen. For the first and possibly last time, I....Rent-A-Hero will narrate my own story. It is true...no matter how much I don't want to believe it...I am the product of a test tube. Hehe, isn't it ironic, people have been saying I've been for the past few years and gosh darn it if they aren't right. What have I come to find now...I'm a failure. To think, the very hero millions cheer was supposed to be a genetic weapon of pure and utter destruction of chaos. It may have not been a big deal to me had I just been created and moved on....but I've experienced so many things since that time. So many WONDERFUL things that make death and destrruction so base and vile in my heart of hearts. One thing that REALLY changed me....was love..::/::..Amy:'I love you...'..::/::..I could remember Amy saying that to me over and over again in my ear, and everytime she said it I always believed it and I still do. In that Rent-A-Car was where I released a part of me I never knew I had within....a part of me that wanted her just as badly as I began to tell she wanted me. I don't know to this day how I knew what to do, where to touch, how to please her....but everything turned out right for some strange reason and when all was said and done....our love was stronger then ever before. The experience we shared in the car changed EVERYTHING...and I'm so glad it happened. Amy will never truly realize how much I care about her and how much I want to keep her in my life...I never was given the chance to tell her this. Which relates me back to my current dilemma...being caught inside this big glass test tube...I feel like a goldfish waiting to get fed a pellet hereor something..::/::.. Miss Hennessey:'You are an ARTIFICIAL PRODUCT Rent-A-Hero...the first in a series...and you became lost shortly after we released you. You came out wrong...you weren't supposed to be so...God....so....nice and caring. We needed a way to find you and test you emotions Rent-A-Hero, so this whole C.L.I.T. thing was a slow plan to get to YOU. We mess with your lover...we knew you'd come to us in due time. You are home now Rent-A-Hero...and we're gonna make you what you SHOULD have been now.' ..::/::..Make me what I should have been? First in a series? That's a lot to get hit with all at one time ya know. I always thought I was one a of a kind and I strive to hold that stigma...I want to be remembered and never forgotten in the hearts and minds of the many whocome across me. I don't want to be 'what I should have been'. Why can't they see that I've grown attached to my cushy, whacked out life. I don't want them snatching that away so I can be some kinda psycho Terminator killer. On a side note....how does hot sauce stay hot when you put it in the refridgerator? Why do they call animal crackers 'crackers'...you can dunk them in milk and they're sweet....that's the characteristics of a cookie! If...okay okay, hey I gotta stay sane somehow in this tube, not like it's a luxurious stay or anything..::/::..Ring Announcer:'THE WINNER OF THIS MATCH, AND NEEEEEEW PCW CRUSIERWEIGHT CHAMPION, RENT-A-HEEEEEEEERO!!!!'Fans:'RENT-A-HERO!!! RENT-A-HERO!!!' ..::/::..That PCW Crusierweight title...oh how I love it so...too bad it's back at the apartment. I may never see it again after this 'change' if it goes through. The End was always lingering in the back of my mind for Fusion....he seemes so ready to take me and I'm ready to oblige him. A part of me thirsts for competition and The End would provide it, I KNOW IT. He's incredible, very good in fact....but I am better, that title proves it. When I came crashing down from that ladder with that belt in my hand, the pain was a sidenote. HOLDING THAT BELT IN MY HAND MADE ME INVINCIBLE, just like the superheros I idolize. NO ONE will take that feeling from me, especially not The End if you know what I mean. I would beat him towards the end....then I'd await Juan, my TRUE competiton. Juan has taken me to a limit I've never experienced many times before and I crave facing him, because he brings out a better part of me, much like I do him. I simply see the End as a tune-up for the real deal, Juan is who I want and it kills me he had beaten me twice, yet I have no win over him. This will soon change, and the Crusierweight title will be the bait for that rematch..::/::..Rent-A-Hero: *to Aimz* 'HOLD IT DOWN IN THERE AIMZ...CAN'T YOU SEE I'M IN THE MIDDLE OF A HEROIC MONOLUGE...JEEBUS WOMAN!!!' *Ahem* 'As I was saying...the infamous End. He seems to be next in line to try to take none other then my very own...' *reaches on the yin-yang coffee table and upsurps the PCW Crusierweight title into his hands* '...PCW Crusierweight Championship. I don't know if he realizes this, but NOBODY is gonna rip this title from my grasp for QUITE a long time and I'm sure even he is not blind enough to realize that. He will a be a formidable foe to face this upcoming Fusion, but he is just a roadblock of all things and I will KAMEHAMEHA BOMB my way through him so I can reach my true goal...battling Juan Borro and FINALLY beating him like my destiny calls upon me to do. The End will be a great challenge, he is no paper wrestler and I'm sure the match we will put on will be a memorable one...but the FINAL memory will be of me with my hand raised high in the air, title in hand, and him laid out on the mat after I defied his gravity! I just ask him to bring all his Energon and give it all he's got...cause when I beat him, he will have no excuse as to why he lost...he can only simply say he lost to the better man of the match and he must step aside so I can bring down Juan Borro once and for all...my most bitter rival. It won't be THE END for everyone's favorite son of Krypton, and that other guy with the 'S' on his chest too, when I beat THE END...but it will be THE END of his thoughts of EVER gaining my title. But I have no more time to ponder my battle with him....I must race to make end of this evil church group before they push my Amy off the cliff of sanity...although sometimes you gotta wonder if she hasn't been thrown off of it at times already. NO MORE TIME FOR GAMES...IT'S TIME TO LICK THAT C.L.I.T.!!' ........'TO THE RENT-CA....*sigh*....My room. WHEN AM I GONNA GET A RENT-CAVE...' ..::/::..That shows just how little we think of the next day. I'd never imagine I'd end up in a test tube just trying to help my love Aimz. They were smart, I'd say too smart....they knew they could get to me through Aimz, they know she is my weakness. To think they went through all this intricate planning...all the lies...all the deceit....just to claim a failure. I hate them for this. Some things your just better off not knowing. I was willing to accept any past other than the truth....now I know why I was doing that for so many years...because the truth hurts so badly. The truth is painstaking...the truth makes you want to come to tears. The truth is...I may not make it out of here and live life like I wanted. In thenext few hours, I could become a machine that destroys lives...that destroys the good in people's lives. After knowing the joy love, and friends, and all these other things can bring....why....WHY would I dare accept killing as a viable alternative....because I have NO choice in the matter. If it was up to me, me and and Amy would be laying down watching Sailor Moon and eating strawberry ice cream with Gummi Bears in it...but I'm afraid that the saying may hold true....'All good things come to an end.'..::/::.. Rent-A-Hero:'NOW SEE HERE MISS HENNESSEY....SHE IS NOT UNCLEAN, SHE TAKES SHOWERS EVERY DAY!!! Now the rest of that stuff, it tis true....but that's what I love about my Amy and that's why she's in my life. Now unless you wanna get beat like The End on Fusion, where I'm gonna smack him about until he cries like a little girl at an N'Sync concert and retain my precious Crusierweight title and keep it safe from unentertaining champions, you better stop harassing my Amy or there's gonna be some thrashing in this biznitch!!' For saying I'm a hero, that situation above one of my many moments of bravery, I'm so scared right now....scared of change. Scared to lose the things I've fallen in love with...love is like a drug....once you've felt it, you want more and more...and now I have to give that up to satisfy some mad scientists wet dream....what a BIZNITCH!! So now I can only sit, wait, and hope. Either one of two things can happen. A miracle will come and I will somehow find freedom and beat this evil force...then get back to the things I love....or two....I could lead a fate worse then death...and that is life without no love in it. WILL FATE BE KIND TO ME?! WILL I LOSE ALL THAT I HAVE WORKED FOR?! I will find out soon....SAME RENT-A-TIME, SAME RENT-A-CHANNEL....if I don't get canceled first....
