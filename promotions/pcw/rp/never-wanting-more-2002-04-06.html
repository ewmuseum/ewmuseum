Never Wanting More

6 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: April 6, 2002 Who do you think you are anyways? Always trying to get me to say what you want me to say and do what you want me to do... I will do what I want. I am an individual. If you want some one to smile at the camera and continue to shine some happiness on the people, why don't you hire a clown? My thoughts raced as I stood quietly underneath the under hang of a building. It was pouring rain, the winds were whistling as if a fury they had pent up for so long had finally been unleashed, and I hated it all. The weather was about as reliable as my mood nowadays. Always changing, never predictable, and basically a pain in the ass. Thats fine though. Let it continue the way it is. It just makes me feel like I'm not the only one going down the shitter, I suppose. Downtown San Francisco. Such a busy place, although at three a.m. in the morning, there weren't too many passer bys. That, and the fact that it was like a monsoon out here. I was waiting for my ride to come pick me up. You probably remember him. His name is Sean 'The Impaler' O'Reilly, a young fellow that has been by my side for the majority of my career. I always wonder what he would have been able to do in his life if he hadn't gotten hurt. Would there have been a time when we would have gone up against each other? Bah. I always worry about the dumbest things. People never think of the present. They dwell in the past, they allow demons from yesterday dictate what occurs in the future, and I promised myself that I would let that go. What happened has happened, and this is no time to be sentimental. Besides, there's too much going on right now. Too much mud being slung in my direction, and it was too soon for answer to be given. Finally, a car pulls up to the curb, splashing water near my feet as it comes to a stop. I made a face and cursed under my breath. 'Fuckin' A, Sean,' I mumured under my breath. 'How about you slow down before splashing half the pacific ocean on my shoes?' I stepped out into the rain, feeling under fire from the constant stream of water drops on my face. I huddled into my coat and opened the passenger side door. I rushed in, closing the door behind me and brushing off some of the water that had come along for the ride on my jacket. 'That took awhile,' I said. As I looked up, Sean was not there. My eyes widened in surprise. 'Who the hell are you?' The woman smiled as she pulled away from the curb. 'Your new friend. Calm down, Tom. You were about to say how upset you were about how PCW fired you.' I clenched my fist in a ball, not knowing exactly what I wanted to do. Lord knows, I couldn't hit a woman. But this was like kidnapping, only not quite. 'Unclench your fist, Tom,' She replied, still staring ahead at the road. 'It's not going to do you any good. You can't hurt me.' 'Is that right?' She smirked. 'If you want, you can try to hit me, but then you'd have to ride in this car unconscious for the entire trip. That wouldn't be any fun, now would it? I was so hoping we could discuss your future and where your destiny is headed.' 'My destiny? What the hell are you talking about?' 'Hell...' She said laughing. 'Oh, we'll talk about that too. So much to say and so little time. What a world this is.' I blinked. Dammit, I didn't wake up.
