Smokie Tang in trouble!?(Part 4)

19 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: July 19, 2002 {{{{Scene}}}}The scene opens up into the D-Kay Calyx’s office at the top of Calyx Incorporated business towers. Still sitting in front of D-Kay and his desk are Sloppy Makonie and Clean P Corg. D-Kay is wearing his best business suit Sloppy Makonie is wearing a thug’s style suit, while Clean P Corg is wearing a bleached white fur coat and a white suit and tie underneath. A buzz is heard in the room and D-Kay smiles as he hits a red button on the desk near the computer. The door to his office opens up and the Virgin, Charlene Loc, and walks into the room waving the contract that she got Smokie Tang to sign… Charlene Loc: Lookie Lookie, I’ve got a cookie. {{{{D-Kay gets a huge grin on his face as Clean P Corg and Sloppy Makonie look around confused….}}}} D-Kay Calyx: Well, nice to see that my little plan worked. You see boys, this is the big plan, that piece of paper that Charlene holds in her hand is a contract my friends. A contract that Smokie Tang himself has signed to endorse my products. {{{{Clean P Corg just looks at Charlene with an amazed look on his face. He stands up and grabs the piece of paper from Charlene’s hand. He looks at it for a few seconds and then throws it on the desk and smiles…}}}} Clean P Corg: We got that sucker now. Something so simple, yet so hard, I’ve got him going legit now. But, what happens if he skips town on you? D-Kay Calyx: Remember, I’m a corporate executive, I’ve got eyes all over the world. There isn’t a place he can go without me finding out. {{{{They all just smile and hug each other as they have the Smokie Tang problem all figured out and solved…}}}} Narrator: Damn it! Smokie how could you sign that contract, virgin or no virgin, you have your own free will damn it! But anyway, you’re Smokie Tang, maybe you can find a place to hide. {{{{The scene fades and the words “The Very Next Day” appear at the bottom of the screen and the scene opens up into a sunny day with Smokie Tang being escorted up into the Calyx Incorporated business building in downtown Wilson, North Carolina. Smokie tries to fight the goons taking him up there, but he’s up against five people, two of them work for Clean P Corg and are the Dark Messiahs, and the other three are security guards that work for D-Kay Calyx himself. He gets pulled into the elevator kicking and screaming and one of the guards’ hits for the top floor of the building. The elevator goes up at a slow pace and one of the guards just looks at Smokie and tells him…}}}} Guard: You got yourself into this when you signed that contract… actually you got yourself into this when you started to influence the kids with drugs and such. You might as well just give up, because you’re not going to get away from the five of us, and Mr. Calyx is bound determined to have a word with you about your job with him for now on. {{{{Smokie then stops fighting and just stands up. He yanks his arms free from the Dark Messiahs, and then says with a frown on his face…}}}} Smokie Tang: I guess I’ve got to do what I’ve got to do then. I did sign a legal contract. Narrator: No Smokie! Don’t do it! You’ve got your whole life ahead of you with the whore business and the drugs and alcohol! Don’t throw that all away just because of a contract that you were forced to sign by a virgin slut! Oh man, how will you get out of this one. {{{{The elevator continues to rise without a word or movement from any of the men on it. The elevator stops finally at the top floor and the doors open. The corridor of the top floor is bigger then that of the bottom near the lobby. They all walk out escorting Smokie Tang towards the left as they walk. They step through huge double doors into the secretary’s office for D-Kay Calyx. The secretary notices that they’re escorting Smokie Tang to D-Kay Calyx and hits the buzzer on her desk. The buzzing sound, which was heard yesterday is heard again and the door opens at the far end of the secretary’s office. All six men walk through the door keeping Smokie Tang up front and they enter two by two into the room. D-Kay stands up and looks at Smokie Tang with a huge smile on his face…}}}} D-Kay Calyx: Well, if it isn’t the famous Smokie Tang. At seventeen years of age you’re already a huge icon around here to the children. Well, I think you know what I’ve got planned for you, don’t you Mr. Tang? {{{{Smokie sits down in one of the two chairs in front of D-Kay’s desk, and the guards and Dark Messiahs leave the room. D-Kay sits down as Smokie answers D-Kay…}}}} Smokie Tang: That’s Smokie to you. I don’t like formalities, even if it is with someone I don’t want to work with. I’ve got to give it to you, you go me where it hurts. But who’s really behind all this, you possibly couldn’t have thought this all up by yourself. {{{{D-Kay just leans back in his chair and then answers Smokie with grin on his face…}}}} D-Kay Calyx: I can’t put anything past you now can I? Actually I thought up most of the scheme, but I did have some help from an enemy of yours. Maybe you know Clean P Corg and Sloppy Makonie. {{{{As if that were their cue to come in, both Clean P Corg and Sloppy Makonie walk into the office through a secret side door. Smokie Tang gets up in the face of Clean P Corg and before anything can happen, D-Kay says…}}}} D-Kay Calyx: Nope, I don’t think so. You two work together now, and if you do anything to hurt Clean P Corg or any of the other employees then you’ll be carted off to jail for a while. {{{{Hearing this Smokie let’s go of Clean P Corg’s collar and sits down in one of the chairs. Clean P Corg sits in the other and Sloppy Makonie just stands up beside his boss, Clean P Corg…}}}} Smokie Tang: Well, I guess you actually got me going legit for once Clean P Corg, congratulations, took you how long? {{{{Clean P Corg bites his lip as he answers with a growl towards Smokie Tang…}}}} Clean P Corg: Three years… Smokie Tang: Ah, three years. Well you’ve finally got me, that’s all that matters I guess. Yo, Calyx… {{{{D-Kay interrupts him with a frown on his face…}}}} D-Kay Calyx: That’s Mr. Calyx to you Smokie. Smokie Tang: Right, sorry. Mr. Calyx then, what do you want me to do here and where do I do it? {{{{D-Kay’s frown literally turns upside down as he then starts to laugh, Smokie joins in, but he doesn’t know what they’re laughing about…}}}} D-Kay Calyx: You’re already working for me. You see, your picture is on the front of every fruit juice, every grocery department, it’s on everything that is good and right with the world. So you see, your image is now shattered. {{{{Smokie’s smile that he was maintaining through out the time falls and he slumps in his chair. He kind of looks down at the floor as Clean P Corg and D-Kay start to laugh…}}}} D-Kay Calyx: Oh and I almost forgot to tell you Smokie. Any insubordination from you, and you’re thrown in jail! Ha! So you’ll need to follow my instructions or else. {{{{Smokie just continues to look at the floor as if it were open space as far as the eye could see. He makes a move to get up out of the chair, but Sloppy slams him back down…}}}} Narrator: Oh no Smokie! Man you have got yourself into a problem here, but how will you get out of it? I hope you can man… no Smokie Tang hoes? No Smokie tang weed? This world is turning upside down! But, even Smokie can come up with a plan on such short notice hopefully he’ll be able to think of something to get out of this. Anything can be better then this. {{{{The scene fades to black on Smokie's frowning face...}}}} TO BE CONTINED...
