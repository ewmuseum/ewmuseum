Not Just Ya Average Day At The Beach

13 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 13.08.02 The scene opens on a large, crowded beach. The sun is shining high up in the sky and there is not a cloud to be seen in the light blue sky. It is a perfect day at the beach. There is a slight breeze coming from the sea, but otherwise it is quite hot. The beach is very busy. There are lots of families, sitting on their deckchairs and lots of kids and teenagers and the occasional old guy trying to prove he can still go, in the sea on surfboards, throwing balls to each other or just swimming and splashing about. Duzza and his family can be seen walking down onto the crowded beach. Duzza is dressed in nothing but a pair of baggy white swimming shorts and some black trainers, he has a pair of sunglasses on and the sun is reflecting of his muscular chest. Next to him is his beautiful, black queen of a wife, Brittany. She has curves in all the right places, a perfect figure, with nice sized bosom and a ghetto booty. She is dressed in tight white bikini, showing off all her curves. Her hair is in long braids which reach her shoulders. In between Duzza and his wife, is there 6 year old son Jimmy. Who, looks like a minute Duzza, just minus the tattoo, platinum and muscles. Further behind them are four of Duzza’s brothers, Jamal, Riston, Marcus and Sean, each of them with either girlfriends, baby mothers or wifes, and their kids. They find a nice spot on the beach and all relax. Jamal: “Yo Malek dawg, nice idea for this beach picnic thang” Duzza: “Thank my wife, it was her idea player” Jamal: “Well… thanks Brit” Brittany: “Don’t mention it” Jamal: “So Malek dawg, here you be teamin’ wit’ a nazi” Duzza: “What chu mean ‘you hear?’ don’t you watch the show?” Jamal: “Err, not really” Duzza: “Damn dawg, your own brother and you ain’t even watching him on the tv? ‘sup with that bro?” Jamal: “Hey, we black, we all family” Duzza: “Wha?? Where you come up with that?” Jamal: “Man, I think Tupac said it once?” Duzza: “Word?” Jamal: “Word” It is then, that Duzza see’s his son and wife walking into the water, sick of talking to his brother, he decides to join them. He runs over to them just as they are getting into the water. Duzza: “What y’all getting up to?” Jimmy: “We’re swimming daddy” Duzza: “Where you learn to swim?” Brittany: “I’ve been taking him to lessons for over a year now Malek, for Gods’ sake” Duzza: “Oh… err… cool” Duzza looks around and noticed a few rocks and suddenly has a great idea. He picks up Jimmy and walks over to the rocks and climbs up. Brittany: “Malek, get down from there NOW!” Duzza: “Hush up, hon, I’m jus’ gonna show the boy about diving, black style, nawmean?” Brittany: “Nigga please, last time you tried showing Jimmy how to dive you ended up in hospital, your wrestling career is actually going well now, you don’t wanna go to jail Malek” Duzza: “Woman please, I know what I’m doing. A’ight Jimmy, check this out, I’ma dive in yeah and then you copy exactly what I do, just I’ll be waiting to catch you in the sea ok?” Jimmy: “Err.. pops?” Duzza: “Yeahmon” Jimmy: “I think there is p-p-poisonous jelly fish down there”Duzza looks down over the edge of the rocks at a load of pink things, floating in the seaDuzza: “Hmmm… nah, that’s just some exotic seaweed..from like Jamaica or somethin’” Jimmy: “Are you sure?” Duzza: “Of course I’m sure, what do I look like to you? Meh, don’t even answer that bro” Duzza then, either without fear or without brains dives straight into the sea, right where all the pink “seaweed” is at. About 10 seconds after Duzza reemerges from the sea, he lets out a big scream of pain, followed by swearing Duzza: “ARGHHHHHHH…FUCK FUCK FUCK” Jimmy: “Daddy? What is it?” Duzza’s wife and his brothers all come rushing over, pulling Duzza out of the sea Marcus: “What is it?” Jimmy: “He dived into a swarm of jelly fish” Brittany: “Duzza, you really do get dumber by the day don’t chu” Duzza: “errr….help me…” Then, Duzza loses unconsciousness as medics run over to him. Will Duzza make it to Turmoil ok? Will he even make it off the beach? All will be revealed sooner or later.
