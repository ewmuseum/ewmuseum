Family Therapy

17 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 17/09/02 OOC: This promo was written after watching an episode of Mad About You. If you’ve seen the episode that I saw, you will no doubt recognise it, so I’m admitting inspiration here so as to prevent calls of ripping them off later. Possible Alternative Titles:Mad About YouBaby on BoardRE: Tale Therapy Actual Title: Having Your Cake and Eating It The camera opens on a red couch. On the read couch seated are none other than the leader of Asylum, Alexander Miyagawa and his wife, Natalia Miyagawa. Miyagawa isn’t wearing his goggles today, as he is in a much more formal dress. Am I boring some of you with clothing description? What a shame. As I said he’s not wearing the goggles today as he’s out an about with his wife, he wears a plain light blue shirt over a pair of dark blue jeans. Natalia herself wears a cream light coloured dressed with fine straps going over her shoulders holding it up. The camera pans around to show another person sitting in a plush red leather chair across the room from the duo in the plush red couch. Between the couch and the chair lay a glass topped table with a few magazines seated on it and also a Dictaphone unit as well. The woman on the chair is wearing a grey business suit. The suit jacket is open and she wears a black blouse underneath. In her hands are a small notepad and pen, and she writes quickly and quietly as Alexander and Natalia talk along with her. Miyagawa: Now what it all boils down to is this… it’s the sex. The therapist looks up at him, as does Natalia. Miyagawa: Now I know we’ve talked about this, and I fully appreciate that we can’t yet. After all, there’s the medical problems with having sex before six months… Natalia and the female Therapist look at each other and then look back. Miyagawa picks up on this and appears anxious. Miyagawa: What was that? Therapist: What was what? Miyagawa: That look, I saw it… you two had a look. Natalia shrugs her shoulders. Natalia: We didn’t have a look. Miyagawa: About the six months thing, you said we couldn’t have sex before a six month deadline after giving birth. Natalia and the Therapist share another “look”. Miyagawa: There you go again! See! Natalia bites her bottom lip. Natalia: I may have made a slight exaggeration. Miyagawa raises his eyebrows at what she says. Miyagawa: Exaggeration? How much of an exaggeration? A lie? Natalia shakes her head from side to side in a sort of iffy manner. Miyagawa: How long exactly is the right number then? Natalia: Not exactly six months… six weeks. Miyagawa: Weeks? Weeks? **turns to the Therapist** Did you know about this? The Therapist just shrugs her shoulders at him. Miyagawa: So in week seven you didn’t tell me… in week eight you didn’t tell me… when were you going to tell me? When Chris was going to college? Natalia frowns at her own non action. Natalia: I just wanted to give myself some time… I wasn’t sure if I was ready to have… it again. Not yet. It’s a rather traumatic experience **she gestures towards her loins** and I wasn’t sure exactly when I wanted to have… cake again. Miyagawa: Cake… well I’ve plenty of cake here, I’m a raving patisserie here. Miyagawa edges towards her on the couch. Miyagawa: But I understand the whole no pressure thing, I appreciate that really. She budges herself away from him, only realising that she’s already right next to the arm of the couch as Miyagawa edges himself a few more inches closer to her, almost leaning over the top of her. Miyagawa: I’m a man of no pressure, you won’t see any pressure from me, I’m a big type of no pressure centre. Miyagawa realises that he’s leaning over her and slides back to the other side, his own side, of the couch. Therapist: You need to stop exerting so much pressure on Natalia, Alexander. Miyagawa: Me? Exert pressure? Never… tell her honey, I’ve never exerted any pressure sexually ever have I? Natalia frowns trying to think of a tactful response, but luckily is cut off before she can. Therapist: You do realise that is a type of pressure, don’t you? Miyagawa: frowns at the therapist. Miyagawa: You always have to take her side don’t you? The female Therapist writes something down in her little notebook before talking again. Therapist: But the question is this… do you both want… well… cake? Natalia: I would like… maybe… some cake. Miyagawa turns back to Natalia. Miyagawa: Really? It’s not just an “oh, you want cake, so I’ll say that I want cake” moment? Natalia: No, I think that I would actually like to try some cake. Miyagawa: Ok… well… good. Natalia: But no pressure, at my own speed of course? Miyagawa holds his hands up in a defensive posture. Miyagawa: Of course, at your own speed, I wouldn’t have it any other way at all. Always your own speed. Therapist: You’re going for that other type of pressure again, Alexander. Miyagawa turns back to the Therapist. Miyagawa: You and me are going to fall out lady, you hear what I’m saying? We’re just sitting around talking about cake and you have to bring pressure into this. I’m not pressuing anything right now. The Therapist glances at her watch. Therapist: And that’s our time up… same time tomorrow? Natalia nods. Therapist: We’ve made some excellent progress, and you are defiantly becoming my best clients. Miyagawa: We should be… four sessions a week at three hundred dollars per session. Just be glad I’m a good boy and I pay my taxes on time. As the two get up to leave the credits start rolling… they include such notables as “Helen Hunt” and “The Nerdy One from My Two Dads” in it. Ok I think his name’s Paul or something. Or that might just be the character. Whatever. Ok this thing’s over. So why am I still typing? Maybe I want to do an OOC rant. Probably not. FADES TO BLACK ALREADY.
