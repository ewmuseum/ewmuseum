Captain MARVEL...the Motion Picture: Part 2 (Mike Marvelous Alliances Promo #2)

20 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/20/02 The camera opens to darkness, then pans around until it catches a glimpse of light. This light is coming from the center of the cavernous room deep inside the underground lair of The Swindler. The Swindler...knows to most as Tyler Nelson...stood in the center of the room with an evil grin smattered across his face. A diabolical scheme was cooking inside his evil mind. In FSW...the fans hated him. Secretly, he loved it. But they hated a pretend character. A figment of their imagination. He wanted to be hated in real life. Sure, he made quite a lot of money from wrestling, but he wanted MORE! He wanted to be Tyler Nelson on a greater scale...he wanted to truly rule the earth. As he paced back and force, his muscles rippled through his green, spandex suit marked with black dollar signs. His minions stood before him. Angelo Deville...in FSW, a shady, mean-spirited mobster. Now standing before The Swindler as Dare Deville...dressed in red spandex with small horns on the top of his cowl...his eyes covered by his mask. Beside him is Ming...The Mingster...The Sexiest Chinese Man Alive...but gone are those good looks. Now he looks like a monster. He is Ming...The Thing, a large, brown creature...made of rock. The last man on the left is Chris Chase. A bitter, hate-filled individual. Now he was showing his true self as Gambrit...dressed in a brown trench coat with a band wrapped around his head and a large metal staff in his hand. The Swindler turned to his minions and spoke. 'Gentlemen...we have already taken over FSW...but that is not enough. We will rule the world. We will suck the very marrow from the bones of every man, woman and child and reap the benefits. We will be Gods. Kings. And who can stop us? That blundering idiot Captain MARVEL, The Tiki Kid and the rest of those Super Zeroes?' Dare Deville, Ming the Thing and Gambrit all shake their heads and voice their protest. 'Hell no, Swindler.' 'Not a chance.' 'We can take care of those schmucks!' The Swindler grinned. He was proud of his minions. They were ready to do his bidding. 'Then very well, gentlemen. Today...we will take WT City. Tomorrow...we will take the WORLD! MUAHAHAHAHA!!!--' The Swindler's evil laughter was interrupted by the Dare Deville. 'Uh...one question, Ty.' 'What?!' 'I noticed you guys all have cool powers...Ming has super strength and can turn into rock...Chasey can transform potential energy in any object into kinetic energy by touch....and I get to be blind. Sort of a shitty deal, Ty...don'tcha think?' The Swindler grimaced at this dissent. 'I get to be cryptic! Not much of a power if you ask me...but you don't see ME complaining!' The Dare Deville let out a sigh, still not satisfied. 'But Ty...people are gonna start thinkin' I'm that shmuck from PCW, The End.' 'Unlike THAT jobber, you have super radar sensory powers and a cool grappling hook/baton!' Suddenly, PCW's very own The End, rounds a corner. He frowns at each of the men, having overheard everything. 'Hey! I'm not a jobber! I was PCW Cruiserweight Champion!' The Swindler squints his eyes and shrugs. 'Who hasn't been!?' 'Well...I was the TALLEST PCW Cruiserweight Champion of all time!' '....Whoop dee doo....' Suddenly, The Void opens up out of nowhere and swallows up The End. 'Nooooo...not agaaaaiin--' His words fade off as The Void closes up. Gambrit sneers with delight, speaking in his thick British accent that he usually tries to hide on national television. 'Hah...how many times has that happened to that bloke?' Ming the Thing scratches his head with wonderment. 'Hey...I thought a duck ate that thing...' The Swindler smacks himself in the forehead. 'Shut up, Thing! You're such a rock head!'' A look of sadness appears on Ming the Thing's rocky complexion. 'Hey guys...just cause I'm made of rock doesn't mean I'm dumb. I have a college education!' 'Quiet you!...Now where was I...?' Dare Deville clears his throat. 'Muahahaha?' 'Oh...right....MUAHAHAHAHAHAHA! MUHAHAHAHAHAHA! MUAAAAHAHAHAHAHAHAAAA!!!' 'So Ty...how bout you give me a better power? Like the ability to see through women's clothing?' 'Shut up! We have a city to take over!' 'Ahem...right.' Scene fades out as The Swindler goes back to laughing maniacally as his minions watch. ---------------------------------------- Meanwhile at X-Academy...a large, steel, building structure...Front Office are doing a little plotting of their own. Except they're not Front Office anymore...they're the X-People(Hey...we're living in a sexually equal world!). Xavier Kannon, or Dr. X, as he is known as within the X-Academy, sits upon his hovering wheel chair with a twisted frown on his face. He looks weak and old...but underneath that strawberry blonde hair is telekenetic ability and the power to conrol the thoughts of any man. His wife and manager, Ellie~!, stands by his side as always. She is wearing tight yellow and green spandex with a brown, leather jacket. She is no longer Ellie~!...she is now Vogue. Just a touch from her can drain the energy from any being. To keep her power from harming her husband or any other of the X-People, she wears mahogany-colored, suade gloves with lambswool lining that she found in the Winter Sears catologue. Standing off to the side wearing his cowboy hat is Duke Williams...or Duke-Neto. He has the ability to control metallic object and create magnetic fields. And last but not least, Tim Walden and Brandi. But they don't look like they normally do...Tim Walden has 8 inch razor-sharp adamantium claws pretruding from his knuckles and Brandi has...Grey Jeans. They are known respectfully as Walderine and Jeans Grey. Dr. X drums his fingers impatiently on the arm of his wheel chair then speaks. 'Today is the day, children...' Vogue looks up from filing her nails and bats her eyes rapidly. 'Like...what are you talking about, baby?' 'I'm talking about World domination...mind control...bending the will of every feeble minded peon in WT City to meet my expectations!' 'Uhh...you lost me at the whole, ya know, World Domination thing...' Dr. X shakes his head in disdain. 'I'm talking about the forced evolution of mankind! Today, I will brainwash ALL of WT City so they will follow me...follow Hubbard...and walk in the light of Scientology!!!' 'Ooooh, that's what you meant.' Duke-neto suddenly steps out of the shadows with a curious look upon his face. 'And how are you planning to do all this, Hoss?' 'I have built a device that will interupt all radio and television frequencies around the world and play a special subliminal message that will brainwash all of them, leaving them as mindless zombies that will do my bidding!' Walderine goes to scratch his head then yelps as one of his claws knicks him. 'Ouchzzies....umm...Dr. X...What about the people who don't watch t.v. or listen to the radio?' Dr. X stares blankly at Walderine, who starts to grow uneasy. Sweat appears on his brow and his knees shake. 'Umm...w-what...what are you gonna do?' Suddenly, Dr. X uses his telekinetic powers and forces Walderine to stick his claws up his ass. 'OOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOUUUUUUUUUCCCCCCCCHHHHHHZZZZZZIIIIIIIEEEEEESSSSS!!!!' 'Question me not, peon. Now...any more questions?' Vogue steps forward and flips open a magazine. 'Umm, like, can you buy me this coat? It would like...SO go with my beige mini-skirt!' Dr. X let's out a sigh. 'Fine...' 'Oh...my...God. Thank you soooo much, baby!' She leans down and gives Dr. X a peck on the cheek. Dr. X smiles then turns back to Walderine. 'Hey Waldo...there's a question I've always wanted to ask you....does it hurt to wipe you ass with those 8 inch adamantium claws and all...?' 'Ewwzzies...' Scene fades to black as the X-People dispatch out of the X-Academy to carry forth with their evil scheme. ---------------------------------------- Meanwhile, back at Mike's mansion, a red phone inside glass covering rings. Mike glances up from his comic book and frantically grabs the phone. His friends take notice as well and stop their game of volleyball. 'Hello? Annonymous tipster?....yeah.....yeah....yeah....you don't say!.....YOU DON'T SAY!.....EGADS!.....HORRORS OF HORRORS.....Right!' The MARVELous One slams the phone back on it's hook and springs to his feet. 'Gentlemen...I have just caught word from the all-knowing annonymous tipster that two groups of evil villains are planning to unleash schemes of world domination on WT City today!' Timo's cocks his eye and answers sollemnly. 'Well, you know what we have to do then....' 'Indeed, Timo....gentlemen...let's SUIT UP!!! TO THE MARVEL CAVE!' The five men spring from the pool and dive into a secret hatch hidden behind a green hedge in Mike's lawn. They travel down a tunnel and land in the MARVEL Cave, then quickly hop into 5 seperate phone booths. 5 Seconds Later... The five friends spring forth from the telephone booths, but they are no longer Mike Marvelous, Timo Bolamba, Azala Zameer, Six Finger Cisco and Casey Deltura...now they are Captain MARVEL, The Tiki Kid, The Human Spear, Triclops and Cider Man! Captain MARVEL is the first to step forward. 'Gentlemen...today the fate of WT City depends on us. We face evil schemery the likes of which no hero has seen before...but NEVER THE LESS...we will use our fantabulous super powers, mix it with blind, bull-headed bravery...add a pinch of testicular fortitude...and we will KICK EVIL SQUARE IN THE NUGGETS!' The Tiki Kid steps forward. 'I'm ready, Captain MARVEL. With my super speed, agility and exploding pineapples, evil had better run and hide!' The Human Spear steps forward. 'I'm with you. Azala have no super powers. He have no death rays or spandex suit...but he big...he strong...and he like to run at people at high speeds! FEAR THE SPEAR...OF ZAMEER~!!!' Triclops steps forward. 'Do you guys have any idea how hard it was to find ruby quartz goggles, let alone a pair with 3 lenses? Anyway...with my optic blast, evil fiends will be no match! Freaks rule!' Cider Man steps forward. 'Sure...I may not be able to fly or shoot rays out of my face...but dispense everybody favorite tasy, brown, tangy beverage, made from natures favorite fruit...the apple! And with these Cider Slingers, I'm ready to dispense cider for the good of all mankind!' Captain MARVEL pulls out a video phone. 'Let me call re-enforcements' Mike dials a number and Ivan Stanislav's face appears on the screen, along with Tempest, Kid Wonder and White Mexican. Ivan is wearing a brown, iron suit, Kid Wonder is wearing the stars and stripes and carrying a golden lasso, Tempest is wearing a black and white spandex suit with a white wig, and White Mexi has a cop uniform and his skin has turned green and scaly, not to mention his has a fin growing out of his head. 'Da?' 'Hello...Juggerbear. There's work to be done, my friend. The Swindler's Gang and the X-People are threatening all of WT City. We must stop them!' 'Loud and clear, Captain MARVEL. You need now say more. Juggerbear, Wonder Guy, Storm and El Savage Dragano will crush evil fiends! DYAAHAAHAA!!!' 'Alright my fellow defender of justice! See you later...in the field of battle!' Captain MARVEL hangs up. 'Ok, gentlemen. Today we fight a war. A war against evil. Some of us may die fighting. Some of us will live on to fight another day. But either way....justice WILL prevail! UP...UP...AND AWAAAAAYYY!!!!' Captain MARVEL takes off in flight then suddenly notices he's alone. He flies back to the ground where everyone is waiting for him. 'Heh heh...forgot that you guys can't fly...' 'Ahem...' 'Heh...I'll call a cab.' Scene fades to black. Tune in for the NEXT installment of Captain MARVEL...the Motion Picture!
