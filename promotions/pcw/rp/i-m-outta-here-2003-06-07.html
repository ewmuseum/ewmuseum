I'm Outta Here!

7 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6/7/03 As the sun rose above the clouds, evaporating the puddles left over from the rain storm the night before Christa, Evan, and I sat huddled together on the couch inside m living room trying to understand the events the events that unfolded before out eyes the night before. I wished the sun could evaporate all the tears that had been shed; we were practically drowning in a river that we had cried. Why did it have to happen? Why did such a young child have to lose his life because of a woman who shouldn’t have gotten custody of him in the first place? It made no sense and it had seemed like a nightmare turned reality. Yet somewhere among all the pain stored inside her little heart Christa was able to point out something to us which would put some light into the situation though it wouldn’t make the action anymore just. “At least Alex will be up in heaven with daddy now.” She said in between sobs as she nestled her head on my shoulder for comfort. I wished I could be of more support to her but there the events had left me speechless myself and I had no clue what to say or how to feel, all I could do is continually cry. “Tessa you got this in the mail while you were gone yesterday.” My mom said as she handed me a large manila envelope addressed to me from the PCW offices. “Thanks.” I answered surprised that my mom had not given me her usual attitude. Perhaps she had finally grown up and realized that her attitude only made matters worse in rough times. As Evan and Christa continued to weep with their heads buried in my shoulder I looked down at the envelope and tour it open, wondering what could possibly be inside. Once I finally got the envelope successfully open I tipped it upside down as a small white card came pouring out of the envelope along with a white piece of paper with the words “Cloud Nine” heading the paper. “Oh my god, I forgot all about this.” I exclaimed to myself as my eyes widened as I realized inside my hand laid a piece of paper with an invitation for me to board the luxurious PCW airship on Saturday, June 18th at 8:00 right outside Dallas, Texas. Here was the invitation I needed to get the away from all my problems and at the same time bring Evan and Christa on a vacation they would enjoy. At the same tie I’d also be taking the advice of the police and getting the hell out of here before something else happened to me or either of the children. It was perfect timing, the only problem was time was running thin to get there on time so I would have to have to pull them away from their mourning time for the child that was a brother to both of them, and get ready to be introduced a world that was new to both them and I, the world of professional wrestling aboard a luxurious airship. “Christa and Alex I have a job for both of you.” I said as I stood up from the couch and looked over at both of them who looked up at me with teary eyes that had been crying all morning and waited for their assignment. “I want you to go upstairs right now and get all your things packed into the suitcases inside your closet.” “How come?” Questioned Christa, confused as to why we were leaving already when we had just gotten home only a few hours back. “I’ll tell you later, just go get ready.” I said, as both of them obeyed their command and ran up the carpeted stairs which muffled the scampering of their feet as my mom looked over at me totally confused as to what was going on as well. “What are you doing? You are going to get them all excited for nothing!” My mother proclaimed in a strong voice, obviously making sure that her opinion was heard as usual. Little did she know that what I was planning on doing would give both her and I a vacation, from each other. “We are going away. I’m leaving the house to you mom. It’s all yours for a month; we’ll be back on July 14th.” I said as I too started to head towards the stairs to pack my own stuff. “July 14th? You are taking children on a vacation for that long? There is no way you’re going to survive even a week.” My mom said with her usual lack of confidence in me. I simply shrugged it off and continued my way up the stairs before shouting down to her. “Don’t worry mom, look at it this way. At least they’ll get to find out a lot about professional wrestling.” I said before reaching the top of the stairs. There was a brief moment of silence and I wondered if my mom had actually began to accept the position I had in PCW as the current Television Champion who was set to face two other champion on Turmoil, Gaz Green and Manuel Labor. Surely enough though after a short pause of silence which allowed my words to sink into my mother’s mind, my other realized what I was saying and quickly marched up the stairs behind me, breaking the silence with her screaming. “You are NOT bringing those kids to a wrestling event.” My mom shouted as she jumped in front of me which only caused me to walk around her barricade towards the closet where my suitcase laid. “Oh but I am and there is nothing you can do about it.” I harassed her like a schoolyard bully knowing that it would only further agitate her and cause her to become even more infuriated with me, too bad there was nothing she could do about it now that I was all grown up and had guardianship over two children of my own who I refused to leave behind with a woman like her who would corrupt them into believing I was the worst mother ever. My mother simply sighed and for the first time in her life gave into me knowing that I was absolutely right, there was nothing she could do about it. Besides who could complain about time alone which would allow a little bit of rest and relaxation? Certainly not her since it seemed she had wanted me out of her life since day one. I could only hope that the trip aboard the PCW airship would provide the same amount of rest and relaxation for me along with a win against both Manuel Labor and Gaz Green.
