Empire Saga Part Three: Keeping Control....

5 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5/8/02 The Empire Saga Part Three: Keeping Control… R…E…S…P…E…C…T The word meant a lot of things to a lot of people… R…E…S…P…E…C…T Did he have respect? It was debatable… R…E…S…P…E…C…T Did he need respect? The answer was undoubtedly…Yes. R…E…S…P…E…C…T Had he shown respect to his ‘employees’? He really didn’t know…he talked to them with respect, even though deep down, he would never respect these people. R…E…S…P…E…C…T What would respect do for? What function did it posses? In the wrestling business he had little or no respect…after all…what had he ever accomplished to deserve respect? He could list all of his title conquests…all of his tournament wins…but what would that do? Nothing. Respect was earned by the way one carried one’s self. People didn’t like the way John Crichton carried himself. He didn’t care what other people thought. This was the way he was…the way he was always going to be. If he changed the way he lived for anyone who disapproved, what would he be then? R…E…S…P…E…C…T An important word...almost impossible to gain…but sometimes respect wasn’t the only a person needed to get on the world… ************ Crichton stood at the massive, bullet proof, windowpane, with a cigar in one had and a glass of cognac in the other. Some say he was neurotically in search of safety. Largely, the people who said this were right…but then again they hadn’t gone through half of the things he had…they hadn’t been in half of the near-death experiences he had…they hadn’t been hunted, like he had. They would never know of his pain…and that was the way it was going to be. Initially he seized control of this criminal empire as a safety net…something to fall back on when things got rough…and in his life things were constantly rough. Now he was thinking about the seizure of power in a different light…the way the crowd of petty thugs and criminals were listening to him during his speech last night got him thinking about a few things.What if he COULD make this ‘business’ of his work? That was the question…the answer was simple…he would earn the amounts of money he talked about in his speech…he would be the billionaire…if he was as rich as he was powerful, he could buy his way out of any dangerous encounter…it might keep the government off his back at least…although he hadn’t had any troubles with the government for a while…this new ‘business venture’ could soon turn that around…but he could buy his way out of any situation, regardless! Just like the Kennedy’s! The business would work…it had to…it was the only thing keeping his power in check…the promise of riches beyond their imagination…the only trouble with this was…he may have over done it a little. A number of his sources have informed him of some of his workers activities since making the popular speech the previous night…his men are getting greedy…they listened to HIS speech and decided that they could be billionaires…they could be powerful…and that they could do all this WITHOUT his help…they worked outside the business’ parameters and it attracted police attention without reason. This had to be dealt with…and fast. What if they all felt this way? He never had sources in all sections of the crowd that were listening to his speech, so what if more of them were doing the exact same thing? No…this could continue, he had to do something…he had to do something now. True…he had established control quite successfully…now he had to keep it, he could think of only one way to keep his control over them…without resorting to force all of the time…otherwise he would be back to square one…fearing for his own life…the organisation was a safety net for him now, but if someone within the organisation WAS going to try to kill over leadership and control…he might as well of not bothered. Basically it came down to that seven-letter word again he needed… R…E…S…P…E…C…T Nothing less that respect from his ‘employees’ would do…it would ensure their loyalty…and ultimate devotion to the cause…whatever that was…the strange thing was he knew HOW to get the respect he needed…it involved a little bit of violence…but violence his employees would appreciate…and perhaps admire him for later. He needed the crowd to be assembled again…he would show them who is in charge and gain their respect at the very same speech…two birds, with one stone as the old saying went. Crichton: George… One of the henchmen steps away from the door… Henchman George: Yes sir? Crichton: I want to hold another mass meeting for tomorrow night…arrange it for me would you? Henchman George: Yes sir! Crichton watched as George exited the room and went to follow Crichton’s command…at least he had a FEW loyal subjects…or so he hoped. ************ So there he stood…again…behind the professional looking wooden stand…waiting for the last of his men to enter the building. The room was almost full, just waiting for a few people here and there…but was pleasantly surprised by the turnout with such short notice…for he had people and subsidiary organisations based all over the country…it was remarkable they shown up at all. Again the henchman at the door nodded over to Crichton again as the last man entered the establishment and hastily took a seat on the back row… Crichton: Right, I believe it’s time to begin… Man: [Standing up from the front row] What the hell is this all about? Crichton: Hold on…I’m coming to that…[clearing his throat slightly] First off I have heard from various sources that some of you are getting greedy…working outside of the guidelines that we have already established…i.e. hitting high security banks and drawing even more unnecessary attention to this little organisation of ours… …This HAS got to STOP…and has to stop NOW… Man: [The same man stands up on the front row] What do you mean ‘our organisation’…you’re running this place with an iron fist…you seized control and you think we should just follow you like mindless lambs? Crichton: [Breaking a smile] And that brings me to my next point…my leadership…I DON’T want to rule this place with an iron fist…hell no. Yeah, okay I seized power, but that don’t mean diddly-shit unless I get respect from my employees… Man: How the hell are going to get respect? Crichton: I seized power in a fair fight against Mr. Freeze…so therefore I’m putting to the floor…to this crowd right here…right now…ANYTIME somebody doesn’t like the way things are run around here come and see me. We can work through the hardship and develop this organisation into something respectable… However…if you have a problem with my leadership min general…you can challenge it…[the crowd gasp at this revelation] Anybody can challenge my leadership…all you have to do is knock me out, much in the same fashion I knocked Freeze out. In a fair fight one on one…if the challenger wins…he gets to call the shots and I’ll take my ball and go play somewhere else…but if I win I stay in charge… Anyone can challenge me…anywhere…anytime…but it WILL be one on one and it WILL be fair…otherwise certain repercussions will follow… Man: As if anyone here believes that you will honour these statements! If one of us beats your ass, you will get one of your thugs to kill our asses! Crichton: [laughing slightly] Really? Is that what you think? Then step up to plate and we’ll show everyone whether I’m lying or not! George, Reginald if I lose this, you’re to do nothing to aid me! [The two henchmen nod in understanding] Crichton: Come on then…put your money where your mouth is! Man: [obviously thinking it through slightly] Okay… The man steps up, from the front row, onto the stage with great confidence. Crichton simply smiles, patting down his knee-long leather trench coat slightly. The man takes off his jacket… and takes a step forward. Crichton: When one of us in unconscious the match stops! Man: I’ll make sure I stop, don’t worry! Crichton: Confident, I see! Man: Yep… With that the man steps forward to deliver a blow…Crichton moves his head to one side and the blow hits nothing but air. Crichton then casually grabs the man by the throat and hoists him high into the air… Crichton: [Speaking to everyone] Oh sorry, I didn’t tell you did I? I have super human strength and height reflexes…[The crowd gasp in awe] I really should tell people these things before hand! With that Crichton throws the man across the stage without even breaking a sweat. Crichton: I know who the greedy people are in this organisation…so if I hear of it again, whoever’s responsible will be ‘dealt’ with accordingly…do I make myself clear? The whole room begins to murmur…whilst many of them nod their heads in agreement. He seemed to have established his dominance over the group…the only kind of respect he would get from this encounter…but halfway through the speech he suspected as much…after all Respect was almost impossible to get…
