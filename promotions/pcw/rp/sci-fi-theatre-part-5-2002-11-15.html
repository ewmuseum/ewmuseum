Sci-Fi Theatre(Part 5)

15 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: November 15, 2002 [We return from the commercial to see Spider back on the set of the Enterprise, he’s in the chair, now with garbage strewn everywhere. Most of it from the local donut joint.] Spider: **BUUUURP!** Director: Good Lord! Spider: Ugh, I ate too much, but we are back, let’s end this shizznit! [Spider clears the garbage off of his chair, and sending Donut boxes flying, hits that dreaded button. The scene returns to the entrance of the Ordos palace. We see Eddie, and the two members of the command squad, Lieutenant Citadell, and Sergeant Snowball. They storm into the entrance of the palace, Eddie has a fever’s pace.] Citadell: Overseer, you may have condemned us to a fate under Fremen rule, and personally, I do not want to be a slave to those desert freaks. Eddie: You won’t have to be if the Executrix agrees to the plan. Citadell: What do you mean? The Executrix will recall you to Draconis 4, and have you killed! Eddie: Perhaps. But at the very least, I would think he’ll listen to the plan. Snowball: I don’t think so. The Mentat will shoot your idea down before you even get to talk to the Executrix. Eddie: I am sure she’ll be receiving to the idea. Ordos and Spice are a rare combination indeed. So, if you’ll excuse me gentlemen. [Eddie storms off to return to his office. Eddie opens the door to the office, and walks into. He throws his armour onto a nearby couch, he then heads to his desk and has a seat. He lets loose a very contented sigh as he sits down in his chair. He then hits a few buttons.] Eddie: Computer, send a sub-space message to Draconis 4, Ordos Palace, Message to the Executrix. [The computer beeps in acknowledgement, and the Ordos logo appears on the screen, then the Mentat appears on the screen.] Mentat: Ah, Overseer, you haven’t contacted us in three months, is there something wrong? Eddie: On the contrary…I have spoken with the desert people, and we may have an alliance… Mentat: Do the Fremen know our reputation when it comes to alliances? Eddie: They do as a matter of fact. So they rejected the idea… Mentat: I am not pleased. Eddie: Hear me out Mentat. They have agreed however, when they get into power on Arrakis, that they will pay us an annual Spice tribute… Mentat: Getting better Eddie: …at fifteen percent of total spice profits… [The mentat’s eyes glow with excitement, and she begins to drool.] Mentat: Oh yeah, we would be unstoppable! Eddie: All we would have to do, is pull out of Arrakis. [The screen fizzles to the revolting image of The Speaker] Speaker: WHAT?! THE DESERT PEOPLE WILL HAVE US OFF OF ARRAKIS WHEN I AM DEAD! You are hereby ordered to now not only purge the Harkonnen, but purge the Fremen as well! Eddie: My Lord…I cannot. Speaker: May I ask why? Eddie: I am a regional governor. I am nowhere near someone qualified to do something like this. To not only purge a house from Arrakeen, but to kill the entire population! That is something around a moral order, and I refuse to follow it. Speaker: MORALS?! THE ORDOS TO DO NOT CARE FOR MORALS! Effective Immediately, you are being re-called to Draconis 4 for immediate termination, you have failed. A death squad will be there shortly. Eddie: But my Lord… [The transmission is cut off before Eddie could provide the final plea for his life. Eddie relaxes in his chair. He then hits a lone button on his desk.] Eddie: Lieutenant, Round up three quarters of our force, infantry and artillery, we attack Arrakeen…Now. [Within fifteen minutes the Ordos force is mobilized headed for Arrakeen.] ***A Few Hours Later*** [We see the Throne room of Arrakeen, and in result of that…the overall capitol of Arrakis. In there, there are many guards all wearing Red and Black suits of powered armour. Except for the person sitting in the throne of Arrakis itself, the Beast Rabban. The tyrant who has been killing every single Fremen terrorist that has cropped up since his rise to power, and the fall of the Atreides almost two years earlier. Rabban is just sitting there, reading reports of how his troops fail, and of course, getting angered by it.] Rabban: DAMMIT! We have lost almost 15,000 troops over the last two years, can someone explain to me, WHY?! Voice: Lord Rabban, we have a surprise for you. [In walk two guards dragging Eddie by the arms into the throne chamber. He is extremely dazed and half-conscious. Rabban, looking inquisitive, stands out of his throne, and walks up to him.] Rabban: Who is this? Guard: The Ordos Governor, sir. He led a failed attack, however we pacified it before it even reached the city. Rabban: You’re telling me that you idiots actually accomplished something?! Guard: Yes SIR! Rabban: Nice…Execute him! [The guard drags Eddie away from Rabban, as Rabban, for the first time…smiles at his guards.] Narrator: The Overseer was executed that evening at Sundown. With the defeat of the Ordos attack force, it sealed their fate on Arrakis. The Harkonnen destroyed the Ordos base once and for all. However, no less than three months later, the great revolt, made by a combined force of Fremen, and Atreides Expatriates, would begin, destroying the Harkonnen, and changing the lines of succession forever. As for the Ordos…Due to the lack of Spice, they quickly dissolved from a House Major, to a House Minor. The failure of the Overseer of Arrakis would doom the Ordos for centuries to come. [We return to the bridge of the Enterprise. Spider has fallen asleep, and is being poked with a stick. He gets nailed with a nasty shot to the stomach.] Spider: OW! YOU SON OF A BITCH! Oh, well, that’s all for Sci-Fi Masterpiece Theatre, I hope you enjoyed it. We’ll see you next week, and I have to go kick the Key Grip’s ass. [Spider runs off the set as the credits begin to roll.]
