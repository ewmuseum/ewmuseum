AWOL and outta control!

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: not entered [Scene opens in a typical hotel room- Complete with Flame-Resistant curtains that are blotched with multiple odd stains.In the middle of the room is a coffee table that has been chained to a nearby couch to ensure no one steals it. Meanwhile, slumped over the dirty carpet is none other than Flaymin Aymin.Surrounded by a dozen empty beer bottles, he just lies there in a motionless state. Suddenly the front door opens and in walks Michelle Branch.She notices Flaymin in the corner of the room and walks over to him] Michelle: Alright, time to get up. [Flaymin ignores the request and continues to sleep….] [Michelle gets on her knees and…. (don’t get ideas you dirty bastards) begins to prod Flaymin with her fingers. A moment later she stops poking him and looks at her fingers which are now covered in some strange liquid.Hesitantly she lifts her finger up to her nose to sniff the mysterious fluid.Upon taking a whiff she instantly jerks her head back and begins to wipe the urine onto the floor] Michelle: UGH!That asshole! [Michelle gets back up of the floor and picks up a few empty beer bottles.She then walks back over to Flaymin and begins to gently nudge him with her foot in an effort to wake him up] Michelle: Lets go!Its time to get up! [Still asleep, Flaymin just waves his hands in the air and mutters something under his breath….] Flaymin: Two all beef patties…. *Snore* ….. special sauce…. *Snore*…. Pickles…. onio-AAAAAGGGGGHHHH!!!! [Flaymin suddenly reacts to the bombardment of empty beer bottles being launched at his head by Michelle. Slowly he begins to awake.He rubs his bruised head and wipes the remnants of all the smashed beer bottles from off of his body. He looks up at Michelle and winces] Flaymin: You’re late. Michelle: Excuse me? Flaymin: You’re late dammit- I ordered room service 2 hours ago. Michelle: Do you know who I am? [Once again, Flaymin gingerly looks up at Michelle and runs his fingers through his afro] Flaymin: The Fonz? [Frustrated, Michelle picks up another empty beer bottle and hurls it at Flaymin.Flaymin tries to duck out of the way and in the process bumps his head against the wall violently. He strokes his head and then rubs his eyes. He looks around the trashed hotel room in confusion] Flaymin: What on earth happened here? [Flaymin looks up at Michelle and frowns] Flaymin: Dammit woman, I doze off for an hour and when I wake up you’ve already trashed the room!Have you no decency?No respect?No…. crowbar? I mean I would have done a way better job then this. Michelle: You did do it.You’ve been holed up in this room for the last 2 weeks. [Flaymin groans] Flaymin: Am I on America’s Most Wanted again?I swear I didn’t steal that case of whisky- I just simply got in my car and it was just…. Mysteriously waiting for me in the passengers seat. Michelle: No, idiot.You haven’t been really active for the last 2 months.And you’ve reverted back to your alcoholic ways.Once again your life is going down the shitter. Flaymin: You’re right.I need to get my life back on track. Michelle: Exactly. Flaymin: Can I borrow $29.95 so I can….. uh get my life back on track.[Michelle curiously ponders over Flaymin’s request] Michelle: Why do you need exactly $29.95? Flaymin: Because that’s how much a case of bee…err…. Bees costs. Michelle: You were going to buy more beers, weren’t you? Flaymin: I said bees!I’m getting into the honey making business! [Bemused by the poor effort to cover-up his alcohol addiction, Michelle slaps Flaymin across the face] Michelle: You know, as of late I’ve released a Platinum Album, won an MTV Music Award AND I’ve just done a duet with Carlos Santana.What have you done? Flaymin: Santana?Is he somehow related to my good friend Sambuca? Michelle: WHAT HAVE YOU DONE?? [Flaymin shrugs his shoulders] Flaymin: I don’t know, the last 2 months have been a blur.Heck, I barely remember anything from the last 20 years. Michelle: And if you don’t change your ways- you won’t live to see another 20. Flaymin: That’s a shame. Michelle: And people will remember you as nothing more than an alcoholic. Flaymin: That’s a shame. Michelle: And more importantly, you’re currently on the verge of losing your PCW contract. Flaymin: That’s a shame. Michelle: Are you listening to a word I’m saying? Flaymin: That’s a shame. [Michelle picks up another empty beer bottle and heaves it right into Flaymin’s gut. Flaymin reacts to the pain by falling back to the floor and breathing heavily. Meanwhile, Michelle walks over to a nearby suitcase and unzips it.She takes out some clean clothes and walks back to Flaymin.She puts the clothes on the couch and then looks down at the hurting Flaymin. Michelle: Put these clothes on- I’m taking you to see a friend. Flaymin: What? Michelle: Since I can’t turn your life around, I’m going to let a true professional handle this matter…… Doctor Phil. [Flaymin’s eyes boggle open in astonishment as the scene fades to black] In the next instalment:‘When two worlds collide’- Flaymin Meets Doctor Phil.
