One Last Time

22 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2-22-03 RP 1 Turmoil 24th One last bar scene, one last honky tonk, and one last game of nine-ball with her newly found drinking buddy and good friend, Duke Williams. Their friendship has been short thus far, but will never be forgotten. From the short weeks spent with Abner to the recent day preparing for the pay per view. Each moment and in between will always have its place in both her heart and mind. The place is the White Horse Saloon in Nashville, Tennessee. Televised line dancing is taking place on the wooden dance floor. Over to the left of the crowded dance floor is a designated area especially for pool tables. There are a small handful gathered in this area of the saloon that chooses a relaxing game of pool over being seen on national television making a fool of themselves. Among this small group I s Teresa. The nine-ball game has already started. Duke dressed in his usual black aims his pool stick toward the cue ball while Teresa, dressed in her usual Levi’s and Tennessee Titans sweatshirt, chalks the tip of her stick with a blue chalk cube. “This is in honor of Paycheck. May he now rest in peace.” Duke says this before taking his shot. The three-ball makes a side pocket. Johnny Paycheck was a Country Music Legend who died recently in his sleep. Paycheck was famous for the song ‘Take This Job and Shove It.’ “A-men to that my friend. A-men to that. Good shot too.” “Thank ya Hossette. I’m determined to win my forty bucks back before I retire” While Duke takes another shot, Teresa picks up her bottle of Bud Ice and downs a long swig. “You sure you want to hang up your boots? I mean, it ain’t gonna be the same backstage without you around giving all the rookies a hard time.” Duke misses his shot and approaches Teresa. With his pool stick in one hand, he wraps his arm around her neck with the other. The smell of whiskey and cigars fill Teresa’s nostrils. “Aww c’mon Teresa. You know if ever you wanted me to give you a hard time, all you had to do was ask.” He winks jokingly trying to cheer her up. “I’ll still be around. You just have to know where to find me.” Teresa smiles and gives Duke a kiss on the cheek then makes her way to the pool table for her turn. They have been close friends these past few months, that’s no secret, nothing more, nothing less. “I just don’t like good-byes. Ya know that.” Duke replies with a slow nod. There are only a select few he will actually miss in this wrestling industry. One is being his hossette, Teresa. Within minutes, Teresa clears the table like usual, winning the game. The two rednecks spend the rest of the night doing what they usually do, drink, get drunk, play pool, and act like fools.
