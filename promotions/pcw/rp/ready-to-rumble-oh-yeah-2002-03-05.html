Ready to rumble? Oh yeah!

5 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3-5-02 Read To Rumble: HELL YEAH HE WAS PISSED! Jay Sylo, a man who excelled at everything he did had lost. He lost a match which should have been his. A disqualification? Who ever made that rule should have been shot then hung, then shot again. Sylo decided to forgive Akiyama though seeing as he was always good to help him and he knew that's all he was trying to do. So what now? Would Sylo quit? HELL NO! It was time to train again, it was time to get ready to destroy Karl Leblanc, it was time to get ready so he could stroll in on Fusion and take the Rising Star title. He also took on a new personality in ways, he didn't really care who he hurt, who he destroyed, because in the end they were only looking out for themselves, not the fans. It was time now, time to train. Sylo had Akiyama open a special void just for his training purposes. What he didn't know was the fact he was about to step into the bowels of hell. We join the training now, Sylo's ready to go face the demons ahead so that he may get HIS title. (A loud snarl moved through the darkness cutting it like a hot knife through butter. Ice blue puddles could be seen floating around in the darkness but upon further inspection it was a set of eyes. A large void which swirled colors of blue and silver opened up from the nothingness ahead. Through this void laid something worse than heaven or hell, something worse than good or evil, something which would push Sylo to the breaking point.) 'Are we ready yet?' Sylo snarled into the darkness. 'Yes, it's ready you may enter,' The voice of Akiyama boomed. (The void seemed to pull Sylo in like a magnet. He snarled and charged in quickly surrounded by the silver and blue colors. A loud pop went off and soon everything was dark once more. A dim light faded in as explosives going off could be heard. In front of him was a city, left in ruins. The sky was dark red, stained with blood which matched the ground. Dead bodies laid scattered in various spots as fires burned all around. Sylo snarled and walked forward stepping over the bodies, one by one. His ice blue hair and goatee seemed to be the only light thing in this place, everything was crumbling around him. This was truly hell.) 'Hm, Akiyama where will I find these.....obstacles,' Sylo snarled out. 'Search for them, you will know when you find them,' The voice of Akiyama boomed out through nothingness. (Letting out another Snarl Sylo moved forward. His mind wondered because he knew he would finally get the chance to rip Ryckman apart limb by limb until he was left in a pool of blood. This time there would be no interference, this time Sylo would destroy Ryckman on his own. Fear was something Sylo over looked and never had, he never feared any man and Ryckman was no different. Except this time he would become the victim just like Karl Leblanc. So now here he was walking through the ruins of what once appeared to be a city. His eyes were glowing their ice blue color as he snarled once more. Slowly scanning the surroundings, trying to pick up signs of life, until a set of yellow, cat like, eyes caught his attention from the darkness. Sylo smirked and let out another snarl.) 'Let me guess, you represent the piece of shit Leblanc,' Sylo yelled into the darkness. (The eyes slowly moved up and down as if they were saying 'yes'. Sylo's smirk widened as the yellow, cat like eyes narrowed. From the darkness stepped a creature Sylo had never seen before. It was covered with muscles and looked like a leopard. It's eyes glowed like a cats as it hissed. It's teeth were sharp as razor blades and it wore torn pieces of cloth.) 'I am Lameth but you may call me the one which will kill you!' The beast snarled. (Sylo snarled once more and narrowed his glazed eyes on the creature. He pictured the creature as Karl Leblanc, the man he wanted to destroy on Wednesday. Sylo pulled his fighting position and motioned for the beast to come on. It charged and started clawing at Sylo. Sylo leaped backwards away from it's grasp finally delivering a kick to the gut. He charged forward grabbing the beast and throwing him over his head through a near by wall. The wall crumbled like sand around the beast as Sylo walked forward snarling once more. Quickly the animal type creature leaped from the debris onto Sylo clawing at his face. Fighting to get his feet up, Sylo finally got his feet on the stomach of the beast and flipped him making it sprawl onto it's back. Sylo snarled once more as he began to lace his boot into the side of the beast's head. Blood flew across the already stained ground.) 'You see, I cannot be stopped. I do not need Akiyama to win, I do not need anyone to win!' Sylo yelled out as he continued slamming his foot into the head of the beast creature. (The animal creature, who by now had lost a good bit of blood, tried to stand up. Sylo picked him up and smacked him around a little bit until he noticed a pole sticking up from the ground. He lifted the creature into a type of spine buster and with one might roar drove the creature through the pole, impaling it. The beast screamed in pain as it began to die. Sylo only snarled.) 'See, this is what happens when you push me....Since you 'represent' Leblanc I will talk to you like I would him. See Leblanc this is your fate, laying on your back staring up at me!' Sylo snarled. (He drove the creature farther down on the poll making sure it was dead as he let out another snarl. In front of the road he was standing on there appeared to be a large building, could have at one time been an office building. It was tall and scraped the sky's surface. Pieces crumbled down and nailed the ground as Sylo thought the one representing Ryckman could be here. He began to walk over the dead bodies as blood began to rain from the sky. This didn't phase him, he wanted the blood of Ryckman, but he was ready to settle for the blood of the one representing him....for now. So he walked to the building, making his way inside and once in he found a few demons which worked for the one who always tried to bring him down....Satan.) 'Will you ever die?!' Sylo yelled as the little red beast whipped their tails around. 'Get em!!!' the little demons yelled charging at Sylo. (They grabbed his arms and legs but Sylo began to flail them around sending the demons flying. He through one into the wall crushing it's skull. He grabbed another and bashed it into another demon killing them both. One bit him and Sylo roared as he reached down grabbing the small beast squeezing it's skull and crushing it.) 'DIE!' yelled Sylo as he kicked one demon through a near by wall. (He picked up the last two and slung them through the front door leading to the outside. Both demons crashed into a sharp, metal object which impaled them both. Now he could make his way to the top where he would face the one which represented Ryckman. Quickly he leaped up the stairs, he had determination running through his veins as he reached the second floor. He headed for the door but just then a demon, large and red, dropped in front of him.) 'And who in the hell are you?' Sylo asked as he snarled. 'I am the second demon which represents Leblanc,' The demons replied. (He had large muscles and was a tad bit smaller than Sylo. In his hand he held an axe. On his body he had armor which covered every inch. His eyes glowed black which matched his heart and on his forehead he had a large, sharp and deadly, horn.) 'Fine,' Jay says sighing. 'Time for you to die!' (Sylo charged sending a few stiff kicks to the large knee of the demon. The demon retaliated by backhanding Sylo through a wall. He quickly jumped back up to his feet and charged drop kicking the beast in the face. His face squeezed in a little bit as he let out a roar. Sylo snarled and charged back spearing the Demon through the reverse wall. They laid in the rubble for a second but both soon stood up. They began to exchange blows back and forth until the demon cold clocked Sylo with the axe. Sylo flew back landing on his back as the breath escaped his lungs. Slowly he fought back to his feet as the Demon charged. With all the strength he had he lifted the charging demon up and slammed him through the floor making him fall all the way down to the bottom. The demon landed on his neck breaking it which killed him instantly.) 'Bitch,' Sylo said snarling once more. (He snarled as he headed for the door leading to the top. It was now time to take care of Ryckman. Like a flash of lighting Sylo headed up the stairs which lead into an old run down office building. He scanned the room which had scattered papers, burning desk, and various pieces of glass. He continued to scan until a figure floated down staring him in the eyes. Sylo stepped back staring at the man which wore a black cloak, had long black hair, and the teeth of a monster. He was tall but not as tall as Jay and he seemed to be a tad bit puny. Sylo snarled once more.) 'Let me guess you represent that Trash Ryckman,' Sylo snarled again. 'No I represent the voice in his head,' (Quickly the figure through his fingers out sending a spike through the shoulder of Sylo. He roared in pain as he reached up feeling the warm blood run from the wound. Acting fast he ripped the spike from his arm letting out another roar of pain. The figure smirked as Jay's breathing increased. He let out a roar which filled the air as a blue aura began to circle around him a small bit. Sylo charged throwing punches left and right but the figure blocked them with no problem. The figure lifted Sylo with no problem and threw him toward a burning desk. Sylo turned though landing on his feet as he charged forward. The figure smirked but soon it faded as he looked down shocked. Sticking through his stomach was a shard of glass which Sylo had shoved through. The figure yelled in pain as Sylo grabbed him by the legs and slammed him face first into one of the burning desks. He could hear his yells of pain but Sylo held him there until there was nothing more than a skull left. Sylo slinked against a near by wall breathing heavy as he attended to his wound on his shoulder. It was sensitive to the touch and he still had one more to destroy, the one which represented Ryckman himself.) 'So this is the way it must be? You will be destroyed, oh yes. That rising star title is mine, Karl Leblanc is mine, and both of you will feel my wrath once I'm done. You will have wished Akiyama had came to stop the match once I'm done,' Sylo snarled once again. (He stood up once more looking at the door which lead to the top of the building. He walked to the door and opened it walking up the cold dark stairs toward another door. The stairs seemed to go on forever and just as he though he could walk no more he reached the door. Slowly, he opened it walking out on top of the building. On top he could see the blood red sky which hovered close over head. The building began to shake a little as thunder boomed through the perils of the sky. He snarled once more as a demon stepped from behind a fan duct. He was large, very large with green eyes but looked human. He resembled Ryckman in a way due to the arrogant smile on his face. Sylo clinched his fist together and stepped forward toward the figure.) 'So, your the poor soul representing Ryckman,' Sylo lashed out. 'Ah, yes and you see...Ryckman will not have the chance to destroy you because once I'm done this world will become your home!' The beast roared. (Sylo charged as well as the figure. Both slammed fist into one another as they both threw kicks and punches. The figure got the best of Sylo though and slammed his face into it's knee a few times. Sylo stumbled back and roared as he charged nailing the figure with a few more punches, it barley fazed it. Quickly the figure slammed a foot into the chest of Sylo making him fly into one of the various air ducts. The figure smirked as Sylo reached in ripping off an old rusty fan blade. He threw it making it slice one of the figures arms off. The figure roared out in pain as Sylo charged knocking him to the ground and pounding on his face.) 'HOW IT FEEL NOW?!' Sylo yelled out driving his fist into the face of the creature. (He continued to drive his fist into the face of the creature until it grabbed him with it's good arm and tossed him head first into another fan duct. Sylo slumped into a heap of bones as The creature snarled and walked forward. Slowly Sylo came too looking up.) 'Now, see this is why I am better than you, this is why Ryckman is better than you,' The creature snarled. 'No,' Sylo whispered out. (And with that he leaped to his feet and began to nail the creature with various lefts and rights. He then reached around it's neck and lifted it high into the air choking off the air supply. Sylo snarled once more.) 'You fear me, you don't know me so you fear me. You hate me because you fear me, you will die because you fear me. The same goes for Ryckman and Leblanc, hate leads to fear and fear leads to death; And now it's your time to die!' (With one mighty thrust Sylo sent the creature flying from the building, down, down, down until he landed on the ground bouncing a small bit. Dark blood began to run from the creatures mouth as Sylo snarled once more. Lighting began to crackle and pop through the blood red sky as a void opened up in front of Sylo. He stepped through and once again found himself where he started. The darkness surrounded him and all he could see was the figure of Akiyama.) 'Once again you have ruled our void, congratulations,' Akiyama said looking to Sylo. 'Just do me a favor, when I'm out their destroying Karl Leblanc and then out there making Ryckman choke on his own words DO NOT come to the ring no matter what, that goes for you and pharaoh both. This is my time and no one will ruin it!' Sylo snarled out. 'We understand,' Akiyama replied. (Sylo nodded and began to walk toward the exit. He pushed open the old wooden door but soon was stopped by the voice of Akiyama.) 'Good luck,' Akiyama boomed out. 'Luck? Thanks but I don't need luck....right now I need the blood of Leblanc and Ryckman....and that IS what I will get!' Sylo yelled out. (He pushed his way through the door once more. This time the light of a diminishing day shot down on his face half way blinding him until his eyes became adjusted. He snarled one more time as he walked away from the old warehouse in which he had entered the void. Was he ready for Leblanc and was he ready for Ryckman? You better believe it.)
