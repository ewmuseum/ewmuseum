PCW Wars: New Hope Part 9

27 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 27 - 10 - 02 As we saw in the last New Hope outing, moral has reached an all time low as the green-haired smuggler Gaz Solo squabbles with the snotty nosed royal Princess Anarchy Organa. We last saw the duo bickering over the half-baked attempts of a rescue attempt, as they see their chances of survival diminish. Hoards of Stormbringers flood the hallways of the Rebel's metallic prison of a Death Star battle station and time has become nothing short of a luxury. Gaz Solo: 'Alex take Tara Palmer-Tompkinson here and get to the ship!' A. Skywalker: 'Where are you going? And who the hell is Tara Palmer-Tompkinson....' Gaz doesn't wait around to hear the pleads of his shipmates, high-tailing it around a blind corner and into an ambush of Stormbringers, ten Imperial troopers flooding a long sub hallway. Yelling and brandishing his blaster the troops retaliate, backed into a dead end they turn and face the lone threat of Gaz Solo. Taking up a defensive position only feet away Gaz squeezes back on the trigger of his weapon, his face filled with blind rage. Gaz Solo: 'AAAAARRRRRGGGGGGHHHHHHH~!' With his eyes fastened shut and the silence broken up by that of snickers, Gaz looks down at his blaster just as his courageous shipmate Cherezza joins his side. Cherezza unleashed a thunderous howl as the Stormbringers took up their attack position. Gaz Solo: 'Err-... I left the safety on. RUNAWAY!!!' As the Wookie and smuggler connection run for their lives, the scene reverts back to Alexander Skywalker escorting the once captive Princess Anarchy towards the hangar that holds their getaway vehicle, The Millennium Eagle. Pursued by another batch of trigger-happy Stormbringers, Alex peers back and fires wildly as the duo embark through a series of chambers. Finally stumbling through a hatch, their route has been cut off by the retracting of the bridge that allows passage over a deep shaft that appears to go into infinity. Almost rushing over and into the abyss, Alex loses his balance only to be saved by the awareness of Anarchy who catches hold of the young farmers arm, pulling him to safety. A. Skywalker: 'JESUS CHRIST!!!!' Anarchy: 'Who? Never mind, I think we've gone the wrong way!' Blasts from the Stormbringers' laser guns explode nearby reminding the evasive pair of the oncoming danger. Alex again fires back at the advancing threat just as Anarchy takes the initiative to hit the hatch switch, sealing the door shut with a resounding boom. Perched on a piece of over-hanging bridge, the Imperial troops continue to fire at the steel door. A. Skywalker: 'If I can speak freely - I'd say we've been screwed worse than your average Jenna Jameson flick!!!' Anarchy: 'Don't you mean Ellie?!?' A. Skywalker: 'Both are experts in their respected fields of screwing!' Anarchy: 'Less banter and a little more participation... How are we going to get out of this predicament?' Alex gripped his handgun and fired a shot into a terminal located on the wall, containing both parties either side of the hatch. A. Skywalker: 'THAT'LL HOLD THEM!' Anarchy: 'Quick, we've got to get across. Find the computer that extends the bridge!' Looking rather sheepishly the farmer retorts the statement of his companion. A. Skywalker: 'Oh, wait I think I just blasted it. WAIT, we could use an umbrella to pull off a Mary Poppin’s re-enactment and literally glide across this gorge!' A bellow of laser fire startles the dumbfounded Princess Anarchy. Almost looking death in the face yet again the door partially slides open unveiling the footwear of a Stormbringer; obviously time is vastly becoming scarce and with no planning the duo look doomed. A. Skywalker: 'I KNOW!!! We'll tie our laces together and make a lasso-...' Anarchy: 'THAT'S IT!!!' Alex glaring with confusion watched on as Anarchy took a chord from his armoured suit. Fastened with a grappling hook, Anarchy tossed the chord around an outcropping pipe and instantly threw her and Alex's body mass across the abyss in desperation. Holding on to the nylon cable the duo swing across the treacherous pit and into a corresponding hatchway on the opposite side. Reaching the other side of the canyon, Anarchy grabs Alex with relief and kisses him full on the lips, taking the sheltered farmers-boy by surprise. A. Skywalker: 'Eeeewwww - What will Natalie think?' Anarchy: 'And there's me thinking your objection had something to do with incest, after all we're like brother and sister, common knowledge, I mean hello...' A. Skywalker: 'Err-... Arr-... That isn't suppose to be revealed until Return Of The Jedi, but seriously was I any good?' Anarchy's mouth remained sealed, reframing from any sort of answer the collective are fired upon by the Imperial force of Stormbringers who have defeated the door, causing our hero's to duck into a connecting hallway and out of sight. Returning back to Charlie Loc, we find the Jedi Knight lurking in the shadows, allowing a select group of Stormbringers to pass. Ushering around a corner Charlie Loc remains out of sight, only visible to the towering figure of Darth Pharaoh who stalks his incoherent prey. The scene fades out and re-opens on the frantic Rush-3PO who paces back and forth. Rush-3PO: 'Where be those white dudes? Well give'em five more minutes, then its every droid for themselves... 'Cause I'd be getting the fuck outta here!!!' As the droids wait in the wings, Gaz Solo and Cherezza emerge, still under close watch by several trailing Stormbringers. Trooper: 'CLOSE THE BLAST DOORS!' At the end of the hallway, the blast doors begin to close in front of the pilot and his assistant. The young star pilot and his furry companion race past the doors as they slowly close, even managing a couple of laser blasts, stalling the Stormbringers long enough for the doors to slam shut on them. Trooper: 'Open the blast doors! Open the blast doors!' Another Trooper: 'Do it yourself-... I'm sick of being messed about!' Checking in on Charlie Loc, we find the wise old man heading along a tunnel that leads to the hangar when suddenly Darth Pharaoh steps into view. The Syth Lord reaches under his black cloak and holds up a walking cane, high above his head. Darth Pharaoh: 'I've been waiting for you Charlie Loc. We meet again, at last. Now the circle is complete-... When I left you I was merely the student; now I am the master!' C. Loc Kenobi: 'Looks like you haven't learnt much in my absence!!!' Charlie Loc hints towards the raised cane, which makes the Syth Lord look up. Throwing the walking cane aside, the Dark Knight reaches under his cloak once more, this time brandishing the traditional lightsabre as he took up a defensive but fearsome stance. Darth Pharaoh: 'Emperor Hessious's things always seem to find their way into this cloak-... It's like a Neo Void or something!' Kenobi moving with elegant ease took up a classical offensive position and the galactic warriors stood perfectly still, sizing one another up. Charlie Loc seemed to be under increasing pressure and strain; he shook his head and blinked in a futile attempt of re-composing himself. Charlie Loc lunged forward, but was checked by a masterful stroke of the behemoth, Darth Pharaoh. Another of the Jedi's blows are blocked and countered, setting off an eye-candy duel of swordsmanship. Darth Pharaoh: 'Your powers are weak old man!' C. Loc Kenobi: 'Who you calling old circus freak? Even if you do strike me down I shall become more powerful than you could possibly imagine!' The battle continues as the camera captures the reunion of the Millennium Eagle crew and its cargo of Alexander Skywalker, Princess Anarchy and the droid collective. Flooding into the vast vessel, they converse in the confines of the cock-pit as the Stormbringers cease fire, slowly edging towards the contest of good versus evil, displayed by that of Darth Pharaoh and former mentor Charlie Loc Kenobi. Gaz Solo: 'What kept you?' A. Skywalker: 'Tell you later - Is the ship all right?' Gaz Solo: 'Seems fine! Just hope the old man has taken care of that bleeding tractor beam....' Yelling and pointing with an out-stretched arm, Cherezza's howls indicate the contest. Watching on we see the lightsabres slashing through the air, colliding with great force as neither warrior wishes to give the other any sort of advantage. Momentarily looking towards the giant pirate ship, Darth Pharaoh uses the distraction to wave his hand. As Kenobi looked at his weapon his mouth dropped ajar, his lightsabre had been replaced with the vision of a rubber chicken, leaving the Jedi dumbfounded and cock-eyed. Unable to react, the mind trick begins to wear off but it appears to late for the wise old man as Darth Pharaoh waved his lightsabre through his fickle body. The cloak dropped to the floor, no body to gloat over, leaving the Syth Lord puzzled at Charlie Loc's disappearance, the whole episode watched by the young adventurers. A. Skywalker: 'NOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO~!' The apprentice's voice swamped the hangar, drawing the attention of the Imperial force that was still at-large. As the scene became bathed in a firefight the Millennium Eagle rose from its stationary position and fled the hangar with a blast of its engines. Powering from the Death Stars docking bay, the craft made a spectacular turn before vanishing into the vastness of space, allowing this particular segment to draw to a close. - To be continued -
