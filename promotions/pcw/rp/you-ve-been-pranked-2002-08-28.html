You've been Pranked!

28 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 28/08/02 A small series of letters and numbers appears in the bottom left of the screen. 'South London, England, United Kingdom. 05:47 am, 28th August 2002'. The full stop blinks momentarily on the screen and gradually fades away with the rest of the text. The screen fades in from black and for a moment, we see an image of a brand new, shiny looking vehicle. A gorgeous Nissan Micra, to be precise. The paintwork on it shines brightly against the dawn and it looks to be owned by a very proud person. The license plate has been pixelated to keep the identity a secret, but the next thing to appear is now secret whatsoever. Jamming his nose into the camera lens is the vampire known throughout the world as Nascosto Nerezza. His gigantic looking nostrils engulf the entire field of vision for an instant before he places one of his deep red eyes into the lens. 'This thing on?' he says, tapping the sides and shaking the camera. He withdraws his head and thankfully, his features return to normal size. He ducks out of the way and slowly, the camera shuffles about, now mounted on his shoulder. As it shakes about, we catch a glimpse of the street. The other cars pale in comparison to the beauty of the reflective wonder. The picture is further spoiled as the cocky strut of the figure walking in identifies him as Gaz 'The Jester' Green. As he nears the lens, the signature green spiked hair and classy bottle-green suit become apparent to the eye. In his hand, he clutches a microphone and on his face, he wears his green sunglasses and the omnipresent confident grin. 'Hello, and welcome to You've Been Pranked!' he begins. 'On this once-in-an-occasional-series edition, we'll be playing a joke on the owner of this lovely car. Of course, it's not actually HIS car, but a hollow replica designed to look like his. The real thing has been towed to safety, but he doesn't know that. Now, we sent letters to everyone in this street except this man. That means his family too. Everyone else has had a notice warning them to have their cars out of here by 6. It's now coming close to that time and everyone's moved their vehicles away to the other side of the street. So, without further ado...' The Emerald Menace strolls off and places the microphone into his breast pocket. The chirping of the morning chorus is all that fills the empty air for a moment, before a squeaking in the distance causes the swallows to disperse rapidly. The camera shifts suddenly as Nerezza's claws shove the lens and focus into the street corner where squeaking originates from. After a second or two of no movement, Gaz parades around the corner pushing some sort of device which resembles a lawnmower! As Nerezza zooms in, Gaz's image becomes blurred momentarily before it clears to reveal him pouring something into the device. As he places the drum of whatever it was down, he removes the mic once more and resumes his narration. 'Ladies and gentlemen... I give you - The Power Of The Paint!' Replacing the mic again, Gaz begins his waddle forward, keeping his legs apart to avoid the trail coming from the back of the machine... a trail of yellow paint in two neat, straight lines. The squeaking continues and it seems only luck has kept the car's owner from awakening. The Jester marches on, leaving his trail of yellow behind him until he reaches the Micra. He shuffles back towards the drum, which is revealed as a tin full of yellow paint. Retrieving it, he invites his partner over and removes a pair of thick paintbrushes from the inside of his jacket's pocket. The camera shakes awkwardly and settles still once more as Nerezza once again shoves his face in front of it, then stomps over to the paint, rolling up the sleeves of his coat in preparation. The pair dip their brushes in and set to work on the car, leaving several coats of bright paint in two perfect lines spreading over the front, roof and finally rear of the car, destroying the perfect design. A smile appears on the face of Nerezza for once as his arm extends across to smear another lick over the sunroof. Once the pair are satisfied, Gaz Green rolls the sleeves of his jacket back down and points towards the camera. Nerezza obliges and hoists it up to his mountainous shoulder. Gaz takes the mic again and starts the analysis. 'As you can see, this man has clearly double yellow lines parked on him. We're now going to execute phase two... don't go away!' The scene fades to black as the image of Gaz pushing away the painter becomes the final vision, accompanied by the partner of Squeak... Squeak... Squeak... ____ Almost an hour later... ____ The scene fades in again with the display in the corner reading 07:04 am. The viewpoint has moved to a place across the street and diagonal to where the original camera was placed. This time, it is the face of Gaz that fills up the screen and his nose slopes downward as two huge eyes peer from behind green lenses into the camera. The picture shakes around as the camera is this time placed onto the smaller shoulders of the Emerald Menace, who opens the second segment with his usual cheerful voice. 'Now initiating the second phase. Nerezza, step forward!' From behind the camera, Nerezza sounds a little reluctant. 'Do I HAVE to wear this ridiculous outfit?' he groans. 'If you ever seen a member of Royal Mail walking about dressed like the one who got kicked out of Metallica before they became famous, your plan shows no flaws! However, they don't, you aren't and it does, so GO~!' Nerezza plods out in front of the camera dressed in the full Postman's uniform of blue and red with a bag slung over one shoulder. His hair has been carefully hidden beneath the disguise of the hat and his usual unique red circular glasses are replaced by a common pair of black, cheap shades. 'I feel like a goof.' Nerezza sighs. 'You look like one too, so it works. Now go do your stuff!' Gaz commands. With a disappointed moan, Nerezza steps over to cross the street and steps up to the house of the Micra owner. A sharp series of knocks on the door calls the man out. He looks to be in his late 20's, with messy blonde hair and has just woken up, judging by the dressing gown and slippers he wears. A thick layer of stubble coats his chin and his eyes strain under the light of the new day. Gaz keeps the camera trained on the incident from his hiding place in a secluded bush on the other side of the road, making sure the unsuspecting victim is oblivious... 'Yawright?' Nerezza greets the citizen in a faked accent. 'Look, I hate to be the one to tell you this, Guv, but someone seems to have put double yella loines on ya car'. In disbelief, the man steps out and onto the porch, whereby he sees a brand new set of lines across the street... and all over the prized Nissan Micra. As Gaz notices the man's face turning a deep red, the quickly slides a finger towards the MUTE button of the camera as a torrent of foul language spills forward that would make the Devil himself cover his ears. 'If I was you I'd get on the dog and bone to sort this out, mate, that's a nice car there...' Nerezza says again in his assumed voice as he rounds the corner and quickly sneaks off. A few moments later, he rejoins Gaz in the hiding place and throws his hat down, allowing his dark purple and black hair to spill out. Gaz is still busy capturing the agony and rage of the victim as he gazes in horror at his beloved possession. 'Right... time for Gaz to make his appearance... the AA give quality service, let me tell you!' Gaz then steps out and leaves the camera on a tripod. As he emerges from the bushes, he's shown to be dressed in a protective set of overalls and a baseball cap, a far cry from his usual smart attire. At his side is a toolbox. As he approaches, he catches the victim's eye, who is still clueless to the situation. ''Scuse me squire, are you the owner of this here vandalised piece? Gaz asks to the fuming prankee. 'You BEEP! right I am! Some BEEP!'s put double yellow BEEP!ing lines on me BEEP!ing car!' the seething soul replies. 'Awww, right. Well, one of the neighbours called and asked me to fix up the thing. Gimme an hour or two and she'll be back to her gorgeous self like brand new.' Looking slightly suspicious, the man has no choice but to disappear indoors and ride out the storm. Gaz unpacks the tools by his side and starts assembling an array of painting and spraying devices. Again, the camera fades to black as Gaz begins to get to work... ___ Sometime later... ___ The scene opens up now with 10:22 in the corner. Gaz is in the same position with a flask of spilled coffee by his side and paint of various colours on the ground. None of which match the original shade of the Micra. A huge drape of some kind covers the car with exception of the immediate area that The Jester is working on. Gaz is then surprised by the owner opening the front door. He quickly stands up and walks to one side of the car and places one hand on the drape. 'How's it going?' he asks nervously. 'Mr Stormwolf, allow me to show you the bang-up job!' the prince of pranks exclaims. With one swift movement, he whips the covering off, revealing to both the owner and camera the psychedelic paint job that looks like a 70's hippie painting. The covering drags the wet paint with it, smearing and splashing the design about. Gaz then moves in close and begins to point with a thin paintbrush. 'Y'see, I went for this Back To Nature look with the flowers and there's a little hint of oriental in there. What do you think?!' The man responds by dropping to his knees as tears flow from his eyes in thin streams. Loud sobbing is the only thing he seems to be able to say at this point. 'Tears of joy, Squire? Yeah, I love my work too. You'll be the envy of the town!' Gaz continues and slaps a gloved hand onto the door, which promptly falls off and into the kerb... followed by a wheel.... followed by the front part springing open... followed by a dead fish popping out... followed by the owner hitting the floor. HARD. 'Ooh... crap...' Gaz muttered, dragging his finger across his throat to Nerezza, as the tape faded to black for the final time...
