Growing Up Sucks; Part Three. Babysitting.

4 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: February 4th, Responsibility. It’s yet another adulthood trait. Jackson figures that if he is being more mature, then he’ll have to have this on his side. And that’s why he took on the duty of babysitting his dearest friends, Tessa Windsor and Chris Storm’s little hoodlum children; Evan, Christa, and Alex. They were a nice group of kids, but from what anyone who has handled children before, they were a handful to keep track of…or so said Tessa and Chris. There’s nothing stopping anyone from accomplishing anything if they use self-confidence, mature behavior, and has a strong grip of reality; obviously traits that Jackson has yet to pick up. Anything could happen…and anything will happen while tending to three other lives. It’s time to be an adult, Jackson Rust…can you handle it? Al: Isn’t this were you swear at the voiceover? The scene opens into Jackson’s Louisiana home once more, as he shrugs, standing up in front of his sofa while looking down upon Chris and Tessa’s kids, as Al and Steve sit on a separate couch across the room, and Kimberly sat between Alex and Christa, with the youngest, Evan sitting next to his big sister. Jackson walks to Al, and whispers into his ear. Jackson Rust: *whispering* I would, but I don’t want to influence the kids wrongly. Al: That’s very mature of you— Jackson Rust: I just don’t want them to call Tessa or Chris a “fucking bitch”. Al: Never mind. Jackson turns around and slowly approaches the children, looking in a serious manner. Jackson Rust: Now; you know the basics at your house, right? Alex: Yes…Mister Jackson, sir. Kimberly: Aww, how cute! Jackson Rust: Y’all can call me Jackson… Rust leans over between the children and looks to all of them. Jackson Rust: Calling me “Mister” makes me sound old…y’know? Christa: *giggles* Kimberly: Aww, how cute! Jackson Rust: Anyway…I just gotta lay down the basic rules for my house…after all, I don’t want anything broken, not that you would do such a thing…you darling little rugrats…but, yah…my bedroom equals off limits, and stay away from anything that looks like it has been in the fridge for over seven days. That’s all. Have fun! Alex: Cool! Jackson Rust: You bet, buddy. That was the basics. That was the first hour of having the children over. Let’s fast-forward three hours ahead. The house is in ruins, almost, anyway. The children and the adults are scattered about the living room: Jackson and Alex are sitting in front of the television, playing random video games on the Playstation Two. Kimberly and Evan are in the middle of a serious game of concentration (featuring Disney channel cards), and Al and Christa are walking about the house, the little girl whining. Jackson Rust: *smashing the buttons of the controller* Ha! You got me the first thirteen times, but not number fourteen, baby! Alex: No fair! You can’t smash the buttons to win! Jackson Rust: Watch me! Alex: Okay then. *smashes buttons of the controller* Jackson Rust: Hey no fair! You can’t win! This is my time! MY TIME I SAY~! Alex: Loser. Jackson Rust: *pouts* The scene cuts to Al standing with Christa watching Rust and Alex play video games from behind, Jackson trying to snatch the little boy’s controller away from him. Christa: Do you have any cake? Al: No. Christa: Can we get some cake? Al: No. Christa: Please? Al: No. Christa: *whines* Pleeeeeeeease? Pretty, pretty please with sugar on top? Al: No. She puts on an angered face before kicking Al in the shin. Al yelps in pain before lifting his leg up to hold the injured limb. Al: You little motherfu—rascal. Christa: Meanie. Steve walks up to Al, and places his hand on Christa’s slight shoulder. Steve: Y-yeah…m-me-mean-meanie. Al: You can’t hit on her. Steve: H-hey! I w-was-wasn’t! Al: But you were going to. Steve: N-no! Christa tugs on Steve’s sleeve (haha, it rhymes. I’m a poet and I didn’t even know it!). Steve looks down instinctively. Steve: Y-yes? Christa: Do you have any cake? Steve: N-no. Christa: Can we get some cake? Steve: I d-don-don’t s-see w-why n-not. Christa: Yay! You’re better than he is! Al: I have a name, y’know. Christa: It doesn’t matter. Al: Bit— *SMACK* Al stumbles back as Christa points and laughs. Narrator: I AM BACK, BABY! Jackson presses the pause button on the game, and looks to Al, where the Narrator would be. Jackson Rust: Where did you go? As Rust waits for an answer, Alex slyly presses the un-pause button, and continues with the game. Narrator: I tried making a TV shadow, like you did…but I remembered I was just a voice. Jackson Rust: Oh. On the television screen, a big “PLAYER TWO WINS” logo flashes upon the game, and Alex cheers. Alex: Yes! Jackson Rust: What is it? Alex: I beat you…again! Jackson Rust: What?! Rust turns his view to the television set to see the blinking “PLAYER TWO RULES; PLAYER ONE STINKS” logo now. Jackson Rust: Cheater! Cheater! Alex: I’m the dirtiest player in this game. Jackson shrugs, knowing there isn’t a think he can do. Jackson Rust: You’d school Ric Flair. The scene cuts to where Kimberly is playing against Evan in a grueling match on concentration, which is right behind where everything else is. Kimberly: Mickey Mouse…Minnie Mouse…*grins* I win. Evan: No! You need to Mickey’s! Kimberly: Oh…I…didn’t know that. She angrily puts the two cards down as Evan looks at the face down Disney cards, before carefully choosing one. Evan: Donald Duck… He smiles as he strategically picks another card up. Evan: And Donald Duck! Yay! Another match! Kimberly: You’re good. Evan: I know…and it’s my turn again! Yay! He puts down his matching Donald Ducks and looks at the scattered cards, most of which are in his possession (bar the one match of two Goofy’s that Kimberly managed to pick up while Evan wasn’t looking). He picks another card up. Evan: Mickey Mouse and… He looks at the cards once more before picking another up. Evan: And Mickey Mouse! Yay me! Kimberly: That’s Minnie! Evan: It’s Mickey Mouse… Kimberly: See the dress? Kimberly points to the card, but blocks the lower body of the drawn mouse. Evan: No… Kimberly: Well, it’s there. Now put it back and play fairly. Evan: *pouts and sighs* Okay… The saddened little boy puts down his two cards, and lets them fall from his small hands. Kimberly: My turn! She quickly picks up the two discarded cards that Evan was manipulated into putting down and taunts the four-year-old boy as the rest of the entourage, and the newly acquired children circle around the game. Kimberly: Mickey and Mickey! Woo! Evan: That’s Minnie. Kimberly: No. Evan: See the dress? He points his small finger at the dress on the mouse. Kimberly: Pooh. She regretfully places the cards down and points to Evan, signaling for his turn. Jackson Rust: That was cheap, Kim…trying to scam a little boy out of his cards. Al: You should talk…button masher! Alex: Yeah! Cheater! Jackson Rust: I wouldn’t talk…no-cake-buyer-for-the-precious-little-girl! Christa: Meanie! Al: *rolls eyes* I’m a brute…I know. Evan quickly picks up two cards, scooping them up in one fluent motion. Evan: Mickey and Mickey! No dresses! Kimberly: Aww, sh— Narrator: Shitake mushrooms. Jackson Rust: What? Narrator: Censoring Kim. Jackson Rust: Oh…good one. Christa: Who are you talking to? Jackson Rust: That would be my lousy, abusive narrator, doll. Christa: Oh. Narrator: Bas— Al: Bass fishing! Narrator: Nice save. Al: You owe me… Narrator: Consider that one asterisk SMACK asterisk deducted. Al: Woohoo! Jackson kneels down aside Evan, as he counts his matches. Jackson looks at the cards for a moment, before pointing to two of them, hinting for Evan to pick them up. Alex: You can’t help him win! No fair! Jackson Rust: Hold on buddy, I’ll play with you in a minute. Evan picks up the two cards. Evan: Minnie and Minnie! I win! Kimberly: How?! Jackson Rust: He has too many matches for you to catch up and win. Kimberly: Clever little rascal. She quickly gets to her feet, as Rust gently shakes Evan by the shoulders, in a congratulatory way. Jackson Rust: Good job, dude. You got the game, but you need the poker face, still. Evan: Poker face? Jackson Rust: I’ll tell you later. Evan: Okay…wanna face me in Mickey-Minnie matches? Jackson Rust: Just let me job to your brother. Evan: Job? Jackson Rust: Lose. Evan: Otay. Christa tugs on Steve’s shirt again. Christa: Can we get cake, now? Steve: J-jack… Jackson Rust: Yeah? Steve: C-can I b-br-bring h-her w-with m-me t-to b-buy c-cake? Jackson Rust: *thinks* Christa: Pweeeeease? Jackson Rust: With sugar on top? Christa: Yeah! Sugar! Jackson Rust: Buckle up…it would be what the “responsible adult-type” would say, anyway. Christa: So we can? Jackson Rust: Of course. Christa: Yay! She runs to Rust and hugs him, reaching only to his ribs, the short little girl. Jackson Rust: Have fun. She runs back with Steve, and grabs his hand, tugging him along. Al: He was a bi--*beep* to us, and he’s now her bi--*beep*. Narrator: I love my powers of censoring! Kimberly: Don’t look now, Jacko, but you may be turning responsible. Jackson Rust: I doubt it. Kimberly: Yeah, I was just blushing, too. Jackson Rust: That was hint to say “No, you’re doing great”. Kimberly: I’d be lying. Jackson Rust: I’m doing pretty well! Narrator: Yep. The whining voice of Alex interrupts. Alex: C’mon, Jacko! I need to kick your butt again! Jackson Rust: Yare, yare…what’s new. Alex: *chuckles* Rust pounces to his seat in front of the screen, as he promptly picks up the controller again, and tries to press the buttons, but Alex played a trick on him; switching Jackson to second player….the biggest insult to a video gamer…luckily, Rust isn’t a frequent gamer. Jackson Rust: You dirty thief! Alex: I’m the best! I should be first player! Jackson Rust: *sighs* I suppose. The scene slowly fades out. Stay tuned for the next portion of the babysitting promo series. If you liked this, you’ll hate the other parts, as Jackson will partake in a Ku Klux Klan meeting, dip Christa in boiling water, and serve Evan to starved dingoes. All this and much more in the next JacksonCO Jackson Rust promo. The above cruel actions will not be occurring in the next promo…end disclaimer. Damn.
