Pete vs. Iron Chef Morimoto

15 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12/15/02 “If memory serves me correctly, I first saw this weeks challenger in the back of a restaurant, many years ago. He was upset that his fish was not prepared the way it should have been. In a matter of moments, he whipped up a design, with red fish, garlic, and squid that I thought I had died and gone to heaven. It was magical, as the fish seemed to melt in my mouth. I asked him to come to my stadium to challenge my Iron Chefs, but he resisted many times. Finally he relented, and he has now come to Japan, to challenge my Iron Chefs!” Pete- I’ll win. Trust me. I’ll win. You never ever bet against a fat man when it comes to cooking up a good meal, in America. Then again, these Japs over here probably got those new fangled Rice cookers, and…like…food makers…and…knives…and…stuff…yeah.” “As large as he is stupid, Pete is an anomaly of humanity. His one track mind is legendary, and once he is hungry, do not get in his way, as his first Japanese assistant found out this morning.” Pete- I thought he said his name was Sushi Sashimi. I was hungry, and well…I’m sorry. “I followed him to the corners of the globe and now, he is in my kitchen, ready to take on one of my Iron Chefs!” [The Man walks up to a podium, pictures of the Iron Chefs behind him. Chef Hiroyuki Sakai, Iron Chef French I, often called the Delacroix of French Cuisine, the man who first infused Japanese cooking techniques into French cooking. Iron Chef Japanese, Masahiru Morimoto. His unique fusion cuisine takes advantage of Japanese color combinations and aromas and uses Chinese spices and simple Italian ingredients, while maintaining a refined French style of presentation. Iron Chef Chinese I, Chen Kenichi, part of the dynasty of “Japanese Szechwan” inheriting cooking techniques from his father. And Iron Chef Italitan, Masahiko Kobe, who has taken the nickname “The Prince of Pasta”, for his imaginative techniques in presenting pasta dishes. The Man then looks up at the pictures of all 4 men, and smiles, a devilish smile. His eyes, return to face forward as He smiles still. 100 Chefs appear in the kitchen that he calls home. They stand, steadfast, ready to move on a moments whim. The Man reaches into a basket of peppers, and pulls out a golden, yellow colored pepper, He looks at it, then bites. Slowly he turns his head, with an even more devilish smile. This, is the master of the Iron Chefs. Takeshi Kaga. He lives in his large castle, with a small army of chefs. His supreme pleasure is seeing them battle each other, just to find out who is the best. Tonight, An American, tries to take a victory from an Iron Chef. Who will win? Who’s cuisine will reign supreme!?] Kaga: Today, in Kitchen Stadium, we have been invaded through our American friends. Our popularity, is booming, there as well as here, in Japan. The sport of Wrestling has always been linked with fine cuisine. And today’s competitor, has shown that more than any other. He is coming onto Japanese soil, as a yokozuka, per se. He is like, a Godzilla to our Iron Chefs. And is an American in a decidedly Japanese game. Iron Chef Challenger…Pete! ~Pete walks out dressed in white. He walks out slowly, and smiles to the many fans in attendance in Kitchen Stadium. Pete bows to Chairman Takeshi Kaga, and turns to look at the men to challenge.~ Kaga: Pete-san it is good to see you in my kitchen. Rumors of your propensity for cooking are definitely world reknowned, as well as your enormous appetite. Pete: Thank you Kaga-san. Kaga: Pete-san, which of my Iron Chefs do you challenge? Iron Chef Japanese? Iron Chef Chinese? Iron Chef French? Or Iron Chef Italian? Which will it be? Pete: IRON CHEF MORIMOTO!!! Kaga: Yomigaru Iron Chef (Rise, Iron Chef!) Morimoto stares at Pete with an Iron Stare. Pete stares back, and makes a slashing motion across his throat at Morimoto. Morimoto nods, and smiles at Pete, and begins pounding a fist into his hand. Pete begins to turn white, and sneaks behind Chairman Kaga. Fukui: Pete has chosen Iron Chef Japanese to be his challenge! Masaharu Morimoto, His unique fusion cuisine takes advantage of Japanese color combinations and aromas and uses Chinese spices and simple Italian ingredients, while maintaining a refined French style of presentation. Will Morimoto be victorious against “The Black Hole of Cuisine?” Kaga: Today, we have special challenge match so, todays theme ingredient should be special as well. I have scoured the globe for this, and now I present, the finest in the world. It has many many wanna be’s to it’s salty goodness but there is none quite like it…Kyoo no tema wa kore desu – (Today’s ingredient is…) [A hush falls over the crowd as Chairman Kaga lifts the blanket covering the main dishes. He points his hand out, palm up, and looks at the crowd. Suddenly, the dry ice fog lifts and sitting in the center of the moving platform is 30 cans of a Quality American Meat Substitute.] Kaga: SPAM! [Pete immediately runs up to the Spam and takes six cans. He hurries down to the prep area and orders his assistants around. Iron Chef Morimoto stands staring at the Spam, not knowing what the hell to do.] Fukui- Spam? What the hell? Has Chairman Kaga lost his mind? Hattori- Spam is an American Delicacy, and I think would adapt well to many many dishes. Fukui – I…what the…I thought this was a gourmet show…Spam? Ohta- Fukui-san!? Fukui- Kitchen reporter Ohta-san go! Ohta- I asked Iron Chef Morimoto what he thought of today’s ingredient, and he’s baffled. he was under the impression that we would be working with pork, thanks to Pete's enormous bulk. Back to you. Fukui- We’re still trying to figure that out here, but one thing we do know, is that you can not use the can itself to cook with, isn’t that right? [Morimoto finally walks up and grabs a couple of cans. Pete's Kitchen Assistant Ariz Ona starts a pot of water boiling. Pete opens another can of the meat treat.] Hattori- That’s right, yes. The can is just for decoration, and the meat inside, is compared a lot with the saltiness of bacon, so I can imagine that it can be used to wrap sushi, or even with octopus. Fukui- Spam-topus? Could that be one of the dishes? Could it be that Pete could beat the iron chef who’s still sitting flabbergasted at Today’s Theme ingredient? Hattori- I think it's entirely possible, yes. [Pete opens yet another can. Morimoto opens one of his cans, and smells it, dropping it to the ground and nearly throwing up. He's clearly disgusted and shoots Chairman Kaga a look of anger.] Fukui- Pete now, has one of the cans open, and he's trying to get the Spam out in one peice. What's the strategy here? Hattori- Well, Spam makes a marvelous meatloaf, and paired with ketchup, it's truely an american delicacy. Fukui- I don't know about all that, but right now lets introduce our guest commentators, and the men who will be helping taste these dishes this evening. First at my right, a Member of RUSH Wrestling and probably the first person we've ever had here wearing a gas mask. Mr. Harris, let me say that it's an honor- Marvin- Of course it is. Im blessing your idiotic existence with my presence. Fukui- Have you ever had Spam? Harris- Hell no! Do you think I'm some sort of degenerate? Spam's disgusting. Do you know what kind of stuff they put in that? It's poison! Fukui- Fair enough. And the man sitting to his right, Former RUSH Wrestling World Champion, Chris Matthews, Mr. Matthews, i understand you and Pete share a weakness for the same foods. Matthews- Yeah, but I can metabolize it better than that fat bastard. Fukui- I heard you once beat him in an eating contest. What food did you eat more than him? Matthews- We were at a steakhouse with the whole crew, and he tagged along. I bet him twenty dollars I cold eat more salad than he could. Fat bastard couldn't even finish a bowl. [Ariz Ona looks into the pot of water, now at a steady boil. He turns around and drops into the water, 4 boxes of Kraft Macaroni and Cheese Dinner. He adds a little bit of salt to the pot and gives it a quick stir. Morimoto has pulled out some Fenil, and some Artichokes.] Ohta- Fukui-san! Fukui- Floor Reporter Shinichiro Ohta, GO! Ohta- I asked Pete, what he thought of his chances, and he said, and I quote so no one thinks this came from me: 'That slanty eyed bastard's going to taste defeat at my hands.' When I told Chef Morimoto of Pete's comments, Morimoto said 'Next week, I'm going to make fried whale if he decides to stay here one moment after this battle.' [Pete looks over at Morimoto who's shaking his head looking at the fat bastard. Pete smiles and shrugs, and puts his fingers to his eyes, making them look slanted. Morimoto starts to charge Pete, but is held back by his kitchen staff. Morimoto tosses a knife at Pete that misses his girth by a few inches before sticking in Pete's cutting board. Pete ducks behind an oven, and stands there til Morimoto stops yelling at him in Japanese] Fukui- Racism running rampant on the floor here. Matthews- Pete isn't known much for his tact, Fuck-yooie. Fukui- That's Foo-Quee. Matthews- Yeah. Whatever. Hattori- Lots of tension here on the floor, Fukui. Seem's Pete's trying to put on a show for everyone! [Pete runs around the kitchen, sauteeing onions, and garlic together in a pan. Morimoto is slicing the Spam wafer thin, and setting each slice on a plate. Quickly, Pete throws a can of Spam, chopping it up with his spatula. he turns and browns it lightly as it crumbles in the pan. The Iron Chef logo pops up, as the show takes it's first break.]
