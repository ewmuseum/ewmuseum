Death to Cheffy: Part 3

18 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/18/02 The camera fades in… We see a still image of the previous Chef promo. The Chef and the Director are looking around, trying to find where the sound was coming from. Narrator: Welcome back to another mindless Chef promo! In the previous promo’s heart-stopping climax, the sound of a jingling bell got the attention of both the Chef and the Director. Could it be the notorious Michael “Jingle” Jackson? Of course!!! If you actually read the rest of the promo, you would have known… Roll it! The still image begins to play… Director: Did you hear anything? The Chef: (Singing to himself) We’re tiny, we’re toony, we’re all a little loony…huh? Director: I thought I hear the sound of bells…jingling… The Chef: Jingling bells eh…hmmm reminds me of a song… Director: Yes, a song about jingling bells… Director: What could that song possibly be called? The Chef: … Director: … “IT’S JINGLE BELLS YOU MORONS!!! HE HE HE!!!” Director: What was that!?!? “Um… nobody… HE HE HE!!!” The Chef and the director both shrug and walk off the set, probably going to round up the rest of the crew and the kids to finish shooting. “Soon this will be all mine again…all mi-uh-AAAAHHHHHHHHH!!!” Suddenly down from the ceiling falls Michael Jackson! He lads on the tiled floor with a sickening thud! Michael: Ouch, that feels worse than a rejection from Elizabeth Taylor, HE HE HE!!! He grabs his crotch and moonwalks. Jackson then pops his face (Being that it’s mostly plastic) back into the correct position and tip toes his way off the set as the camera mercifully fades to black… Narrator: Now on with the next part in our ridiculous saga! The camera fades back in… We see the Chef and the Director both standing outside in a very dark and dimly lit alley. Smoke rises up from the ground, most likely meaning that the homeless are barbecuing rats again. A puff of smoke flies out of the chimney on the roof of Mao’s Moo Goo Gai Pagoda, most likely meaning that the cooks are barbecuing rats again. A crack addict, a crack whore, Jimmy Crack Corn, and Uncle Kracker all sit next to a dumpster sharing some crack and the whore. The parrot on Uncle Kracker’s shoulder spouts out some nonsensical parrot talk. Parrot: WRAAAAA! Polly want a crack whore! Polly want a crack whore! The homeless obliges, passes the whore down, and zips up his pants. The Chef and the Director, holding hands, pass the four people with “Crack” or “Krack” somewhere in their name. They walk further down into the alley, passing the sign with an arrow pointing in the other direction that reads, “Right Side of Town”. Director: Is this the “21 Jump Street” set? The Chef: That would explain why Richard Grieco is behind that fence eating a live cat… The Director nods, and tightly holds onto the Chef’s hand. Director: I’m scared, let’s make out… The Chef: Pickled Piper Perabo! Is everyone in Hollywood Gay!?!? Director: Either that or they play gay people… The Chef nods. Director: So??? The Chef: No… Director: Even a little? The Chef: Sorry sir, I’m a one man…er…woman man! Director: I won’t use my tongue… The Chef: DEAL!!! The Chef is just about to lock lips when suddenly!!! “YOU SICK PERVERTS!!! HE HE HE!!!” The Chef: Who is that!?!? “If there’s one thing I can’t stand, it’s a non-pedophilic relationship! DISGUSTING!!! HE HE HE!!!” Director: You’re a sick freak! “Yeah, well at least I don’t bone Bertha Stewart…” Director: HA HA!!! You’re right, she is really fucking fat!!! The Chef hits the Director in the back of the head for that remark. “And you want to bone the guy who bones Bertha!!!” The Chef cracks up laughing, and subsequently gets a slap in the face for that. “Enough accusations! It’s time for me to reclaim my throne as rightful heir to the Kid Net empire!!! HE HE HE!!!”…”I’m grabbing my croctch, HE HE HE!!!” The Chef: Ewww, why did you tell us that? “Well I really did, and since technically you can’t see me, you wouldn’t be able to put that down in the promo…” Narrator: He’s got us there ol’ boy! “See! HE HE HE!!!” The Chef: There’s still no excuse for it Jackson! You are providing a negative image of society and also contributing to the discrimination towards more moderate crotch-grabbers! Jackson: Oh, and your record is sparkling Mr. Alouette!?!? The Chef: Sparkly as a fresh glass of Perrier… I may be a traitor, a liar, a racist, a fanatic, and a moderate crotch-grabber, but I am no a PEDOPHILE!!! Jackson: Just because children like to play with my Jingle Bells, it doesn’t make me a pedophile, HE HE HEEEEEE!!! Parrot: WRAAAAA! CRAPPY JOKE! WRAAAAA! The Chef nods in approval. Director: Hiding from us is going to you no good Jackson; confront us like a man! Jackson: Nuts to that, I’d rather hide in the body of the dead cat Richard Grieco was eating…ooops, did I give my hiding place away!?!? The Chef: TO THE CAT!!! The Chef and the Director push Richard Grieco aside and huddle around the cat. The Chef: Scalpel!!! 2 minutes later… The Chef steps back from a bloodied cat corpse with red stains all over his once pearly-white chef suit. The Chef: Wait a second…in relative proportion to the cat’s body; your body could not fit…logically… Director: Does that mean I can stop pulling stool out of his large intestine? The Chef: I believe so… “HA HA HA!!! HE HE HEEEE!!!” The Chef: You tricked us, Jackson! You aren’t in that dead cat’s body!!! Jackson: You are fools for thinking that!?!? Haven’t you taken elementary math!?!? The Chef stands forward, growling angrily at Jackson. The Chef: In France we don’t learn Math…the only things important in France are the periodic tables of Wine and Cheese, and Puff Pastry 101… Jackson: What about Descartes!?!? The Chef: Not de carte…it’s pronounced “a la carte”… Jackson: Quiet you!!! I’d have enough of you insolent behavior!!! It is now time to face your destiny and receive your just desserts for negating my right to sexually abuse children on live television! HE HE HEEEE!!! The Chef: Oh yeah, and what are you going to do about it!?!? “CHEF, HELP!!!” The Chef: Wha!?!? That voice…sounds familiar… “I DIS MANY YEARS OLD!!!” Jackson: He’s holding up two fingers! The Chef: …MANUEL!!! YOU WILL NOT VIOLATE MY ADOPTED SON, JACKSON!!! Jackson: HA!!! Then meet me at Never-Neverland! And Bring your TV Contract!!! The Chef: Or what!?!? Jackson: (Quietly) Jingle…bells…Jingle…bells…Jingle…all…the…way…TO NEVER-NEVERLAND OR MANUEL WILL BE MY LOVE SLAVE!!! HE HE HEEEEEEE!!! Director: He really needs a woman…that’s over the age of 13… The Chef nods, and then lets out a Vince McMahon-esque Adam’s Apple gulp. The camera begins to fade to black… Narrator: Will the Chef give up his dream in order to save his son from Jingle Jackson!?!? Will he be able to defeat Jackson and keep his spot on the Kid Net TV schedule!?!? How will Bertha celebrate Kwanzaa this year!?!? We’ll find out…same Chef time…same Chef channel… NANANANANANANANANANANANANANANA…THE CHEF!!! The camera fades to black.
