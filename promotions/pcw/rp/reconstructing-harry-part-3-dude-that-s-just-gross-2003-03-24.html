Reconstructing Harry - Part 3 - Dude, That's Just GROSS!

24 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: March 24, 2003 [He smells the perfume already as he ascends the staircase. His tuned nostrils are picking up the sweet scent of Chanel Number Five which was a passing fad years ago. This babe definetly isn't down with the times but at least it shows she's trying. He predicts the woman to have light brown hair, almost blonde, but more toward brown. She'll be medium height, around five foot, five inches or so. She won't be in high heels because they probably hurt her feet to the point where she's almost in tears. She'll definetly be in a flat show that looks plain as not to attract attention to her feet which she is very concious about. Could they smell? He doubts it. Could she have bogus toenail juice build up? That is usually the case in all accounts of babes hiding their feet, but options ARE open. He pauses at the second floor entrance to ponder on the woman's body size. The way the perfume scent is scattered through the stairwell isn't giving him the correct read out, or maybe he's losing it in his old age. Meh, she probably won't be a dog or anything. If there is one thing Harry learned, stoned or not, is that he is to pick the best of the best. Though that husky babe from years ago was an exception she DID slim down big time! So, that's something bodacious about the situation. He walks to the third floor with a smile crossing his tanned face, the pearly white teeth sparkling even under the dim lights of the stairwell. He approaches the stained glass on the left side of the metallic door and decides to take a gander inside before he lets his totally bodacious arrival be known. He finally finds a white piece of the glass where he can see inside....] [Not a bad looking place for only have moved into it four days ago. A couch, love seat, and leather chair fill the right corner of the room which is surrounded by a bunch of padding. Where in the name of the Dick Van Dyke Show is the television at? Maybe he didn't have time to purchase one with all the money Tommy gave him to start out with his new place. The Tubular One, crouched in front of the stained glass, moves his eyes to the right noticing the bed in the opposite corner of the room. He can see two pairs of feet dangling from the end of the bed but cannot see any further up. The smile on Tommy's face grows wider as he finds a warm place in his heart that says, 'Good for Harry, dude.' Tommy decides that maybe barging into the place now could spoil the relationship between Harry and his babe...but with his rad bro in town Harry won't have ANY problem bagging another bodacious babe! Tommy backs up slowly, trying to hold back his giggling. He breathes deeply sustaining his laughter and charges into the door as it swings open with a loud clang] 'Totally Tubular' Tommy: OOGA BOOGA!!! [Tommy's face is of pure comedy as he leaps onto the hardwood floor. He looks at the bed with the smile still across his face and it slowly begins to drop further and further by the millisecond. Two heads rise up from underneath the covers and smiles cross their faces. Tommy's face has completely contorted into absolute terror as if the people in the bed were brain eating, decaying zombies] Man: Look, Billy, it's a threesome. Billy: You know it's not my birthday, Gregory! Gregory: I thought this was for me?! Billy: I didn't know about it! [The two gay lovers look to one another with sly smiles on their faces, then they turn those smiles over to the former PCW Continental Champion. Tommy is frozen in fear, but his bright blue eyes are moving back and forth as if he's trying to think. The men then extend their index fingers waving them toward themselves, motioning the world's greatest surfer to join them] Gregory: We don't bite... Billy: ...HARD! [Tommy turns and runs out of the room like PURE LIGHTNING! He books up the stairs not really knowing exactly where he's running to. He just knows that he needs to get away from that room] Tommy: OH MY GOD!! BOGUS!! BOGUS!! BOGUS!! [The 'Super Villain' of PCW charges up the stairs repeating the word 'Bogus' over and over until he reaches a wooden door that reads, 'Roof'. He jumps in the air nailing a pick perfect leaping front kick and it opens up with no problem. He jogs toward the edge of the building still repeating 'Bogus' and he hangs his head over the ledge as if he's going to vomit. He breathes deeply not from the running but from the overwhelming fear that consumes him. He looks down to the ground seeing all the people coming home from work, filling the sidewalks like really tall ants. His breathing begins to become normal again as he focuses his mind onto such things as Heather Locklear, Kirsten Dunst, and that babe in the Doritos commericials. Slowly his heart rate returns to normal and he wipes the sweat from his brow] Tommy: Whew...that is something I DO NOT want to do again! Voice: Hey, baby! Tommy: OH POOPSICLES!!! [Tommy falls backwards onto the hard roof and crawls against a ventilation fan, curling up into a tight ball] Voice: What the hell is wrong with you?! [Tommy slowly looks up and notices the long brown hair wrapped into a tight ponytail, the brown eyes glimmering with gnarly intelligence, and that Polo button up shirt. That can be only one compadre, Tommy's brother...'Heavy Metal' Harry!! Tommy quickly climbs to his feet and throws a tight hug around his brother, whom he hasn't seen in only four days. It feels like four years to Tommy and he squeezes Harry tight...then Tommy realizes what's going on. He releases quickly knowing he has had enough gayness for his lifetime a few moments ago. Harry just sort of laughs at the situation not knowing what his brother's problem is, but he comes to the conclusion that he'll NEVER know how the guy's mind works] Tommy: You have no idea how bodacious it is to see you, dude! I was on my way to your room I... Harry: You accidentally went into the room with the gay guys? Tommy: DUDE!! THAT'S GROSS!! Harry: Yeah, I suppose but they are just doing what nature intended them to do. Tommy: Well, nature is bogus. Harry: Well, it's over now. Just so you know their room is 2 and a half, not three. Don't ask me why there is a loft 2 and a half, but there just is. I was almost guessing you wouldn't be coming out here with that huge World Title shot that your getting on Turmoil. [Tommy just shrugs and comes to the quick conclusion not to tell his brother that he just found out about it only hours ago. Yes, he realizes it was a mistake to not answer the phone when PCW was calling, but what do you expect when a compadre loses HIS prestigious Continental Championship. Well, at least he didn't found out THAT DAY or there would be one of those totally HEINOUS disasters. It will just give him the gnarly amount of motivation he needs to get Harry back to his old self] Harry: You just found out about it, didn't you? Tommy: What? Me? Find out about the match just a few hours ago? Pft. How would I just IGNORE a totally bodacious message like that?! Harry: Well, for starters I didn't say anything about a few hours ago and how you rub your foot sideways on the ground when I mentioned it tells me something too. Tommy: FINE! I just found out about it a few hours ago, Colombo!! Are you happy?! Does that make you feel more bodacious about yourself? Harry: Actually...yes. But what makes me feel even better is someone I met the other day. Tammy, come on and meet my brother. [Before she walks out Tommy leans into Harry's ear...] Tommy: I've got something that will make you feel REALLY good! Harry: What? Tommy: Weed. Freshly grown weed! Harry: Tommy, quit kidding around. [The woman comes around the corner and the odor of Chanel Number Five hits Tommy's nostrils once again. He was DEAD ON about everything about the woman. Her body is very skinny, but looks to be a bit athletic with a bit of tone in your arms. Her tight black dress could help enhance that, but Tommy is sure that the dress isn't THAT tight. She's not heavy on the make up which is a RADICAL sign that she can look most bodacious without blue sparkles all over her face. She smiles and extends her hand out to Tommy] Tammy: Hi, Tommy. Tommy: Hey, babe. Our names nearly sound the same. We're just a few vowels away from each other. [She giggles a little bit] Tammy: Your right...we are! [Her face scrunges up into a sort of rat faced look as she giggles which sort of turns Tommy off, but as he keeps looking at it...it's kind of cute] Harry: We met at the art museum and I found out that she is attending the same art school that I signed up for. Tommy: DUDE! You didn't tell me about art school! Are you going to learn how to draw stick figures better? [Tommy then turns his attention to Tammy] Tommy: This dude can draw a BODACIOUS stick figure! [Tammy giggles louder now as her face contorts into the rat face again. Harry just laughs and nods, really wishing Tommy hadn't brought that up. Of the things Harry remembers about his high haze is drawing 'Stick Figure Theater' for Tommy] Harry: I'm not going to be drawing, bro. I'm going to be going for video production and stuff like that. It's going to be a real challenge but I'm ready for one. Tommy: Right. So, what do you say we go down to the apartment and get RIPPED!! Tammy: I'm up for that, how about you, Harry Berry! [Tommy laughs] Harry: Tam, I don't think we should. Maybe just sort of hang out...drug free. Tommy: Come on, Harry Berry!! Lets get high! Harry: Tommy, you know what will happen if I get high again. Tommy: Right... Harry: Lets go check out the place... [Harry and Tammy begin going toward the roof entrance. Tommy hangs back feeling extremely defeated and he turns to the ventilation system. He gives it a small kick to let out his moment of frustration, and that's when the light bulb goes off...even if it is low wattage. He looks at the ventilation system....then at the bag of marijuana in his pocket. A sly smile crosses his face] Fade to black
