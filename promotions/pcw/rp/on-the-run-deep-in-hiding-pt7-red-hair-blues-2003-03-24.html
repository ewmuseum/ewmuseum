On The Run, Deep In Hiding pt7- Red Hair Blues

24 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 24th March When The Mexican Devil returned at March Madness, he returned with a new look but the pretty much the same old attitude he has always had. Diablo's new look is obviously heavily noticeable considering how he looked before his absence. El Diablo no longer wears any face paint, he's had his hair cut and dyed and has grown a goatee. Apparently Diablo's red hair is supposed to symbolise his rebirth, and the loss of face paint is supposed to symbolise himself moving on from certain factors in his past. But for now, it is time for El Diablo to get a haircut. El Diablo is waiting inside a Barber Shop inside the same mall in Sacramento that he was in when attempting to purchase a trophy cabinet before being thrown out for very idiotic reasons. The inside of the Barber Shop is very nice, it's very clean and on the walls are pictures of pretty young women, which'll always make El Diablo smile. Why they'd have pictures of women is baffling, unless they're up there to direct the men's attention away from the horrible hair cuts they're receiving....nah it can't be...can it? Not for El Diablo anyway, his barber for the last five years works there. He goes by the name of Rodrigo, but looks more like a John or a Luke, especially considering that he doesn't even look like he hails from a tropical place. He's as white as a pimento loaf, and the tanned customers are always sure to remind him of that fact. Tease the man about what he can never have. There aren't many more people to go ahead of El Diablo actually, just a teenage child with ginger hair and ridiculously large spectacles and a middle aged man with thinning gray hair that El Diablo could have sworn was a comb over. And even though there are still two people to go before him, there are two barbers working, and only one of them will get seen to by Rodrigo unless they demand to see him much like Raul is planning to see him. So whilst he waits, Diablo just sits down and makes sure to look around the outside of the Barber Shop to see what is going on there, and of more note to keep watch to make sure his mortal enemy Flaymin Aymin won't appear, like he has done at the bar he went to and at that furniture store where Diablo was going to buy a trophy cabinet. With how close Flaymin Aymin has been at tracking the Mexican Devil it has become a big worry for El Diablo, especially because it seems somebody is tipping Flaymin off and the worst part is Diablo doesn't have a clue who. At last Rodrigo is ready to take another customer, and up steps the middle-aged man. Rodrigo has just walked out from the staff room where he was having a coffee break, after all it's lunchtime and he has been at work since 7am. A very long day. Diablo smirks in knowing that the man with the comb over will not take much time to cut and the ginger kid by then will be getting his hair cut by the very large woman dressed in black. To put it bluntly, El Diablo would rather die than to ever interact with that woman, and quite rightfully so because she is a very vile woman. 'And the back,' the woman asks. She receives a nod from the pale and thin female customer, so she lowers the chair and leads her to the money till. 'Seven dollars please,' the large woman screeches in her horrible Texan accent. The thin customer hands her the exact amount and proceeds to leave a very satisfied woman- probably because her husband can no longer satisfy her needs. The large woman closes the money till and waddles back over to her area of the shop and shouts, 'NEXT PLEASE,' as she prepares to cut another person's hair. The ginger haired teenager reluctantly gets up and walks over to the seat, by this point El Diablo didn't even notice that the middle-aged man had finished and paid and that it was his turn to get his hair done. 'Raul, you're next,' Rodrigo says in his typical gay sounding voice. Diablo looks up and quite fitting the moment, remarks, 'Huh?' Before he realises and walks up to the chair and takes a seat. 'OK, so what would you like doing this time?' Rodrigo asks as he places the mat around El Diablo's shoulders and raises the chair so that he is at a good level in facing the mirror. The Mexican Devil looks at himself in the mirror and smirks, obviously approving at the site of himself. 'Just the same as last time please, Rodrigo,' he quickly responds. 'Super,' Rodrigo exclaims. He picks up a spray canister full of water and sprays it all over El Diablo's hair to soften it up. He then quickly picks up a pair of scissors, ready to start on El Diablo's hair. But what kind of an rp would this be if that was it? Rodrigo isn't just a barber, he's also El Diablo's confidant- the person who will listen to his complaints and troubles with life, without passing judgement but just giving advice. 'It's a crummy business at times you know,' Diablo says in his heavy Mexican accent. El Diablo can pretty much raise and lower the bar of his accent whenever he wants to, but sometimes he just gives up trying to sound American. 'How so?' Rodrigo asks with only little curiosity, as he needs to concentrate on his job of cutting Diablo's hair. Diablo takes a deep long sigh. 'Well this Tuesday I am Main Eventing a show for the PCW World Title and then on Friday I'm facing some guy called Semore Smoke,' he whines. Rodrigo smiles, showing his perfect white teeth. 'Isn't he the Principal on the Simpsons?' Rodrigo asks back. El Diablo smiles. 'That's what I thought, but apparently he works for PCW and I have to wrestle him early in the card,' Diablo continues to complain, 'I mean, I appreciate the fact that I'm getting a World Title shot but to be put against an unknown so early in the card is an insult. Not only to me, but to my heritage,' Rodrigo just nods, carrying on with his job. 'I hate being insulted, hell I hate my life Rodrigo. What did I do to deserve this kind of disrespectful treatment?' a light bulb goes off within El Diablo, 'Oh I know, it's that damned corruption which runs through PCW's veins, not to mention Flaymin Aymin probably had something to do with it, that bastard,' Diablo moans to Rodrigo. Rodrigo continues snipping, 'I see, I see,' 'The worst part is though, if I lose Semore Smoke it could kill my career. Imagine the shame of losing to someone I've never even heard of, I'd be a laughing stock.... you know what? Flaymin Aymin probably did set up this match, and for that I'll kill him. And if he interferes, I'll kill him. DAMN HIM! DAMN HIM!' El Diablo rages at the thought of Flaymin Aymin being the conspirer to ruin his career. His face has actually gone red, as he can see by staring into the mirror to look at his face. 'I really hate my tortured li....' Diablo stops in mid sentence. He goes as white as a ghost, as through the mirror's reflection he can see none other than Flaymin Aymin looking around outside the Barbers Shop. El Diablo panics and leaps his head down so he can't be visible to Aymin. 'OWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWW!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!' El Diablo screams in pain. As when he ducked down, he wasn't looking at what Rodrigo was doing, and ended up with getting the scissors stabbed into his outer ear. His ear is gushing with blood. 'OH MY! OH MY! OH MY!' Rodrigo exclaims, 'I'M SOOOOOOO SORRY,' Diablo instantly covers his ear up and peers over to see if Aymin is gone, and indeed he is. El Diablo suddenly regains his composure and sits back up before leaping off the chair. He looks around nervously, and then quickly flees the store, muttering some expletives about Aymin under his breath. 'HEY YOU STILL GOT TO PAY!' Rodrigo shouts after him.
