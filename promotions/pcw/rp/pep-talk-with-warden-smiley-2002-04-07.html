Pep talk with Warden Smiley

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/7/02 *Monologue* Maybe I’m too nice or maybe I’ve been a rough on ol’ Karl LeBlanc. I think “Swamp Dawg” should get the same amount of respect Miyawaga gets. Okay, not that much. I think we’ve all be rough on LeBlanc. We all doubted he’d ever win a title but through screwjobs and cheating courtesy of Big Daveylicious he did. This addition of a Big Daveylicious promo is dedicated to the hard working soul of Karl LeBlanc. Despite how much he sucks...that didn’t come out right. Let’s try this again. Despite how much he takes...Ugh. That doesn’t sound good either. Despite how a person of his skill should be fighting in Uncle Bob’s Bumpkin Wrasslin’ or Little Timmy’s Backyard Wrasslin’ he still is a PCW star. Congratulations, Karl LeBlanc and good luck getting money for your prison’s new high tech security system or toilet. Sorry, to have to make you lose the title so quick. But the fact is we’ll always remember you for your attributes to the Extreme title. Like making it even look worse. But don’t worry it’ll be a title of glory. At least you got a Big D promo dedicated to you. (The scene fades to evil cold blue eyes. As we move back it is reveled that these eyes belong to none other than Big Daveylicious. He’s clad in Armani suit and Gucci shoes. Now, were able to see what our surroundings are as we see that were in his living room. He sits comfortably on his lazy boy. His lackey, Michael Graves and his tag partner, Mike Frampton sit on the leather couch. Big D has a Television remote in his hand.) Big Daveylicious: Guys, I’m glad your here. Mike Frampton: I’m glad too. Listen, I still got your thong and here it is. I tried to wipe off the poop marks but couldn’t get’em all. (Mike takes Big D’s tuxedo thong out of his pocket to Big D’s pocket. Mike tries to hand it to a disgusted Big D.) Big Daveylicious: Mike gets that thing away from me! Mike Frampton: Well, I don’t want it... Michael Graves: I’ll keep it! (Graves grabs the tuxedo thong from Frampton and starts smelling it as a disgusted PCW Extreme title contender shakes his head.) Big Daveylicious: Damn it, Graves stop smelling the thong! Michael Graves: It’s a thong, thong, thong, thong, thong where Big D‘s shlong, shlong, shlong, shlong has been and it smells so freshly clean, clean! See smell it! (Graves throws the thong onto Big D’s head. Big D cringes. ) Big Daveylicious: Ughhhhhhhhhhh! (He throws the thong on the floor.) Big Daveylicious: That smelled like sh*t. Michael Graves: But it smells good. Mike Frampton: Sh*t usually smells sh*ty. (Big D stomps his foot to get there attention.) Big Daveylicious: Guys, can we ever have a serious conversation really? Mike Frampton: But I am SERIOUS sh*t does smell sh*ty! Big Daveylicious:...idiots. Why do I hang out with you guys?Michael Graves: Because were the only men in the world that thinks your cool. Mike Frampton: Because I can make the best damn muffins in the wrestling business. Big Daveylicious: ... Mike Frampton: Or maybe it’s my sex appeal. (Big D rolls his eyes at Big Daveylicious: Ugh. Why do I even bother you guys with a lifetime of a chance? Michael Graves: I love that channel. Those Golden Girls kick a$$! Big Daveylicious: I mean guys. What I’m about to do is this going too far? I mean how far is too far? (Graves jumps up and down joyfully.) Michael Graves:Big D’s gonna strip! I’m ready to lose my virginity, Big D! Big Daveylicious:I’m not stripping or gonna have sex with you. Michael Graves: Damn it, you know after cleaning your toilet everyday you’d think you would finally give in and have sex with me. (Big D rolls his eyes.) Big Daveylicious: Off to the prison! Mike Frampton: Isn’t that the place where guys named Tito get you up the butt? Big Daveylicious: Yeah. Michael Graves: LETS GO! (Michael Graves jumps up and down joyfully as the scene fades inside a local police station jailhouse. Prisoners in their cells equipped with a toilet, a sink, and a bunk bed with some of their own personal items. The only thing you can see in the jail is the dim lights hanging on the roof of the jailhouse. A guard with a man in a suit comes walking past the hallway of cells. ’Till they come at a halt to a cell with an inmate with his face looking down at the pavement sitting on the bottom of a bunk bed. He wears an orange jail uniform. On his arms are various fake Micky Mouse tattoos. For some odd reason the bleached blond hair and black goatee throw us off. ) Guard: He’s all yours, Warden Smiley. (The guard opens the cell with his keys, slides the cell door open, and the Guard and the Warden enter.) Warden Smiley: What’s a matter Dave LeCrakylicious? (No reaction.) Warden Smiley: Did you get raped again? (Big...I mean LeCrankylicious punches the mattress.) Guard: Sorry for interrupting, Warden. But why on earth are you doing this? Warden Smiley: I’m warming him up for his match. Guard: It looks like your pissing him off, sir. Warden Smiley: Well, I figured giving him a warm bath would relax him. Nothing but cold showers with men raping my boy, LeCrankylicious! Ain’t that right, LeCrank? (LeCranky punches the bed again and than two more times.) Guard: Stop him! He’s beating up the mattress! Warden Smiley: Why I taught LeCrankylicious how to beat a mattress. He can beat a mattress faster than a teenager after seeing a fake nude of Britney Spears on the Internet. Guard: Sir, how can he wrestle if he is convicted for 4 murders? Shouldn’t he of had the chair now? Warden Smiley: He was raped by all four of them. He bit their penises off and they died by blood loss. (Davey...I mean LeCrankylicious starts to multiply punching the mattress.) Warden Smiley: That it boy! Don’t let that mattress rape you! Guard: Whys he wrestling? Why would the state allow this? Warden Smiley: He’s gonna get money from wrestling so we can get the new high tech super duper prison security system. Guard: What’s that? Warden Smiley: I don’t know. To tell you the truth LeCrankylicious is just some made up wrestling jobber character. Guard: Than why in the hell is he doing this... Warden Smiley: Shhhhhhh. He don’t know that. (Big D...LeCrankylicious stands up we get a good view of his orange prison uniform. He turns his backs to us and the Guard and Warden Smiley. We notice on the back of the prison uniform in big bold black letters “Wardens B*tch”. The guard looks at Warden who shrugs and lets out a little laugh.) Warden Smiley: Now, son bend over and kick that mattress! (LeCrankylicious bends over and starts to pull his prison uniform pants off. The Warden interrupts. He stands up) Warden Smiley: “Swamp Dawgystyle”, I’m afraid you misunderstood me. I said “ Bend over and kick the mattress“. (Swamp Dawgystyle pulls his pants up and stands they’re scratching his head. Than bends over again.) Warden Smiley: Not now! Later! Ah, just beat the crap out of the mattress until you pass out. (He stands there confused. Pulls his pants up and gives his first eye contact with the Warden.) Dave LeCrankylicious: Why must I beat up things? Why can’t we all just get along? I mean make love. Not war! Sometimes I just want a hug not a shot up the butt. Why can’t we be like cuddly wuddly teddy bears and little tiny fairies all living in harmony with a magic rainbow? Is that too much to ask? Warden Smiley: Just beat up the mattress and shut up. (Smiley yawns.) Dave LeCrankylicious: Okay. (He starts beating up more on the mattress as we fade into the big yellow spot of the mattress.)
