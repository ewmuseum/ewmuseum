Was PoP really our destiny?

undated

Date: Today The Road to the Championship…Episode I: Was PoP really our destiny?Part I of II It’s a somewhat, cold and chilly night in the confinements of a little city called Watford… in the country that we call, England. We’re here today for no real reason… well, no real reason that we’d like to disclose to the masses of people around the World, instead… for the folks seated at home, doing nothing but wonder what will hit the Television Screen next, we’re here in Watford, with PCG… also known, as the Pretty Cool Guys.Following their most recent defeat at the hands of ‘Evangelion Times Two’ on Turmoil of a week or so ago, both Rich Sherwin and Ryan Moore had done nothing but drift to a little place where nobody would be able to disturb them. Being PoP Stars, unfortunate losses in both the ring, and the charts, would bring along a strop that’d drive the boys into their own darkness. This darkness would allow the two to think about their failure and look at a way for this kind of scenario to never ever happen again. We joined the two as they sat down for breakfast in a small Motel Room. It’d been partially decorated but never actually completed, something that would annoy our boys usually… but due to their depressive moods, they just couldn’t care. “… How is it, that we were able to jump over a couple of mindless bodies known as Warrior and Blind Rage… yet unable to jump over an obstacle, as small as the pairing of Gargantuanko and Tyre Preston?” The two remained seated at their breakfast table, Sherwin looking over to the dejected Moore, the question of their failure still running through his mind… gnawing at the little object that was his ‘Brain’. Ryan didn’t know how to answer the question posed to him and continued to stare down at the table, his eyes fixed on nothing more than a complete slice of white toast. “… Is it truly impossible for us to do anything whilst we are believed to be nothing more than a couple of PoP Stars? Are we going to just simply drift through PCW for a second time… getting fluke victory, upon fluke victory?” Rich looked up to the ceiling and raised his right hand into the sky, as if to be reaching out for some kind of opportunity that would allow the duo to overcome all and begin a run as a dominant force within the walls of the PCW Tag Team Division. Ryan continued to stare down at his toast, seemingly oblivious to everything that was happening around him. Rich’s eyes lowered from the ceiling to catch Ryan in his ‘zombie-like’ trance. After waiting for a few moments, Sherwin dropped him arm and sighed loudly. Perhaps this is everything that the ‘Pretty Cool Guys’ were destined to be? Perhaps this was everything that was going find its way into the hands of our budding heroes? “Is your ignoring of my words, a sign that the Tag Team Division, just isn’t the place for me to showcase all of my talents? Is your ignoring of my words, a sign that says we’re not made to be singing… and dancing people? That, we’re not supposed to be entertaining the crowd with our songs and lyrics of ‘Love and Romance’?” Sherwin sighed again, heavier this time and brings his face down to the table, staring at what was his empty plate and the table cloth that it lay upon. What kind of message was being sent to these two athletes with their current run? What message, was being delivered from those that are above, to two men who obviously needed some form of change… some form of new element to their entire outlook?Rich Sherwin had been a man of many things before he’d come back as a dancing individual. He’d been through independent federations… he’d been through, gruelling matches that tested his physical strength and determination to survive. Rich Sherwin… he was not a PoP icon. Rich Sherwin… he was not meant to be, a boy who danced around on a stage, singing and shouting various different words to those in the crowd… to those who mocked and ridiculed his decisions throughout life. Ryan Moore, he had the ability to sing and dance his way throughout life… to sing and dance his way to the top of the Billboard Charts… but Sherwin, deep down beneath the sugar coating that’d been sprinkled on his falseness, he knew that he was supposed to be a Champion, he was supposed to be a real… Superstar. Those days, where he would show the World, intensity… those days, where he would show the World, determination… and those days, where he show the World, victory… “Ryan… Do you think this is the right way for me go? Do you think, that my entire career will now revolve around singing and dancing for people? Do you think that, I won’t be able to repeat my successes and victories of the past?” Rich Sherwin sounded, somewhat puzzled and confused, like a little child lost in a large supermarket. He’d been there before, but he didn’t know which way to turn… which way to run to get to the familiar land. Ryan Moore finally looked up from his toast, a lack of expression running across his face. It had looked like he too was having doubts about the whole point of the ‘Pretty Cool Guys’… doubts about, the whole reason for their existence in this business. “… I often ask myself a question. A question… with an answer that I am fully aware of, but a method that I fail to understand. Rich, what are we honestly doing here? Why are trying to fight against the tide of failure and misery? Should we have not realized from the last time, that victory for something as precious as a Championship, just won’t occur? Haven’t we come to understand that, this just isn’t what we were born to do?” Ryan Moore pushed himself back from the table and stood up. He was dressed in a pair of black shorts, black sandals without a T-shirt. He walked away from the table, shaking his head from side to side in a gentle manner. Both members of the ‘Pretty Cool Guys’ were having doubts about everything that they should have done. Both individuals were questioning the roads that they had taken throughout life… questioning that decision they made when first employed into NTICW, the birthplace of this combination. “Well, if we really weren’t made to do this? Then what were we supposed to do?” Sherwin’s question drove both men to think about the possibilities of their lives without the whole influence of PoP. Where would they have gone? Would they have even been in employment with NTICW if the phenomenon hadn’t actually started? There was a silence as both individuals thought… and then the screen began to sway from left to right, and eventually… fade out… “… W-where am I?” Rich Sherwin’s voice brought illumination to the screen as it slowly began to open up again. We were standing outside one of the many arenas across America, Sherwin dressed in relatively smart clothes with the PCW World Championship slung over his shoulder. He looked around, confused about the reason why he was standing here… instead of being at the breakfast table with Ryan Moore, talking about the routes that could have been taken if PoP didn’t come along into life. “… Hello…. HELLO… is anyone there?” He called out in desperation, trying to get somebody to hear his cries, somebody to hear his pleas for recognition… but the silence continued to build around the athlete, it continued to settle like a rabid disease, eating away at everything around him. “… Hmm, my right shoulder feels somewhat heavier than usual for some reason. Perhaps I put on a few pounds or something?” Sherwin asks himself the question in a very sarcastic tone, turning his attentions to his right shoulder to see the PCW World Championship laying there. Would this have been the road right now if Rich Sherwin didn’t allow himself to party on with his ‘Pretty Cool’ buddies over in the walls of NTICW? Would this have been the end result for Rich Sherwin if he’d just done what he wanted to do, and continued up the merry path of success alone?He knew that he was good enough to strike the Ultimate Championship in federations in time… his history was decorated and adored, his character respected and loved. Those other stomping grounds had been forced into handing out respect… forced into recognizing the talent that was burrowed within. “… Is this the level that I should be competing at right now? Is this the item that I should have had in my sights if I simply dropped the whole ‘PoP’ scenario from its place on the wall?” Rich Sherwin removed the World Championship from his shoulder and held it infront of him. He stared at the Gold plating and held the belt close to his chest. He looked up to the sky and wondered, but when he tried to look down at that Championship again… it had vanished into the coldness of the scenery. The opportunity could have been there if he’d broken away from the ‘Pretty Cool Guys’ last year and worked all the way through to now… … the opportunity to become the best could have been within his grasp, if his attentions hadn’t been spent on ‘singing and dancing’, ‘entertaining teenage girls’ and ‘signing autographs’ for people that would come along to sessions just for the Hell of it. Had fate been playing a cruel trick on Sherwin from the moment he chose to become a ‘Professional’? Had fate allowed Sherwin to waltz up the wrong path… knowing that this reflection would come at some point? “… This message… this whole message. The signs of being a great Champion… the glory of being a winner. Is this really… truthfully and honestly, the pathway that was better than the one that I’d gone for?” Still surrounded by nothing but silence and the dimly lit streets of his surroundings, Sherwin took a few steps forward, walking down a small flight of steps and stepping out onto the open road. More of his picture slowly began to come into view… the ghostly outlines of a city during the day. Children and Adults moving from left to right, cars and other vehicles slowly moving up the roads. Rich looked over to a building on the corner of one of the streets to see a huge banner hanging over the entrance. “… PCW… World Champion?” The ‘PCG’ front-man walked across the street, in the direction of the building, passing through the ghostly images of cars and people like they were nothing… everything around him continuing to play along as though there was no interference. Sherwin stepped into this building, strangely enough… a Book Store. He glanced over to where a large grouping of people were amassed, his interest levels peaking with regards to the identity of the individual who was there. “… Ladies and Gentlemen, it has been a long time waiting for this moment to finally come along. It has been so long, since we’d started to think about this moment ever coming to light. If you will, please put your hands together and show appreciation for the PCW World Heavyweight Champion… Rich Sherwin!” Sherwin stood where he was, frozen in shock at the announcement of himself as the World’s Heavyweight Champion. He looked over to the back of the room a little harder to see a ghostly ‘Sherwin’ figure step out from behind a closed door. The figure stepped over to the PCW Official and shook his hand, looking to the flashing bulbs of spectator’s cameras. After a few moments of holding that same pose, he released the officials hand and addressed all of the people in the room. “Everyone, as you are well aware… it’s been something in the space of a month since I won the PCW World Heavyweight Championship. I’ve been through some of the toughest matches in the history of the organization… and now, I’ve felt its time for me… to celebrate this month with the release of a book… An autobiographical piece, that documents my time in my first federation… all the way to my World Championship win, just last month. Now, as all of you are well aware… this victory would have never come to me, if I didn’t turn away from the Pretty Cool Guys and walk my own path… all the way back in March of 2002. If I’d stayed with them… who knows what would have happened, perhaps I’d still be trying to win my first PCW Championship today?” The ghostly figure of Rich Sherwin let out a hearty laugh, actually mocking the possibility of not winning anything… a reality that was all but too real for the actual Rich Sherwin. He turned around, his head looking down to the floor, the sorrows of not having what could have been, filling his heart, mind and soul. “… Why must the alternate future be so cruel?” The view slowly began to fade out into the confinements of darkness, the alternate lifeline of Rich Sherwin being viewed before all of our eyes. Could he have really become a World Champion if he didn’t walk along the same line as the ‘Pretty Cool Guys’ today? Would he really be able to do anything of that kind? Nobody knows for now… although next time, we’ll be seeing if Ryan Moore had those same aspirations.
