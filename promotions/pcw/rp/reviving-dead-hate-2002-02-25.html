Reviving dead hate

25 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 25/02/02 The scene opens up in a cheapo motel room just where Sunday Sabotage was held. The opening shot is outside the motel. There are rows of doors with numbers on and many random cars parked. The time is about 2:06am and all the lights are off in all the room except one of the rooms where there is still a light on. The camera slowly tracks in on it and two shadow figures of two men. The noises coming from the room is mainly stamping and crashing. Then a chair comes flying out the window and the camera zooms in to see it is Red Rock and Boolie. Red Rock is still dressed in his wrestling attire and Boolie is dressed in some jeans and a black plain shirt.Red Rock: GODDAMN LANCE FREAKING BELL! Red Rock kicks a little table and it flies across the room hitting the wall making the picture fall down.Boolie: Red you have to calm down everyone looses some time! Red Rock: you know that was the first time I was pinned one on one! My record is out the window along with my belt and my cool looking win loss records! I’m pissed off Boolie I’m very pissed off. I feel naked without that damn belt I worked years to get PCW gold and I lost it to some second rate loser in 10 minutes. I’m pathetic ARGH!Boolie: hey it’s not that bad…Red Rock: what do you know? You never held a title in your live you can’t possibly know what it’s like for me Red Rock punches the wall making a loud thud before kicking the wall.Boolie: HEY! The wall didn’t do anything to you walls have feelings too!Red Rock: that is the stupidest thing you have ever said now get out of my face I’m releasing tension! Boolie shrugs and exits the room probably to take a crap whilst Red Rock back fists a plant pot shattering it, but little did he realise how much it would hurt.Red Rock: EEEEEEEEYAAAAAAAAAGHOOOOOOOOW! Red Rock begins jumping up and down shaking his hand around in pain blowing it.Red Rock: AH BOLLOCKS! Why can’t I have HANDS OF STEEL! I just have BALLS OF STEEL! Red Rock resumes waving his hand around insanely. Boolie walks back in to the room to get a view of Red Rock shaking his hand near his groin whilst moaning in pain, but from Boolie’s view it looking different.Boolie: are you beating the meat?Red Rock: what?! NO! I hurt my hand! Boolie: that’s because you’re being a moody little brat who lost his titleRed Rock: I know I’m being a moody brat who lost his title, that’s why I’m hitting things, you oaf! Red Rock holds his hand in pain and sits down on the sofa and begins to sulk.Boolie: hey it’s not that bad you’ll get over itRed Rock: get over it? This week sucked I lost to Chef because of his queer curry powder then I loose my world title shot then I loose my Rising Star title, how can it get worse? Oh yeah and I just crippled my hand.Boolie: ok that’s pretty bad but look on the bright side at least you scored a Cruiserweight number 1 contender ship match at March Madness Red Rock looks up from his world of misery and begins to show some sunshine.Red Rock: really? I do kick ass! Who do I have to beat?Boolie: Krammer Wild WingRed Rock: KFC freak? Heh that guy should be no problem Boolie: Anton the KidRed Rock: is he still around here? Damn I would of thought he’d try to learn to read before facing me!Boolie: AND…. Ganker Suddenly the dark clouds circle and Red Rock’s face turns to anger, His former NZW nemesis Ganker in the same ring as him once again, almost one year ago Ganker and Red Rock battled in an NZW ring trying to get one up on each other but Red Rock had never been able to fight since Ganker’s PCW2 injury. Red Rock never forgot what he always wanted to do, this makes Red Rock grin from ear to ear and rub his hands together.Red Rock: Ganker Ransolver that little freak who stole my hair style and defaces the name of RSE my NZW nemesis I get to have a shot at facing him? This is going to be sweet!Boolie: you what else? He hangs around with Hank~!Red Rock: Oh great…that guy man he pisses me off am I going to have to shove my foot in his poo’er too? Well who cares I’m getting a shot at Ganker and the #1 contender ship to the Cruiserweight title! Boolie: see things are beginning to look up alreadyRed Rock: hey it’s a new week with new a possibility, who knows what could happen. Heh, heh, heh Boolie: are you planning on killing Ganker before the PPV? Red Rock’ eyes widen and he shakes his head.Red Rock: de…err…no…no I’m not err…heh, heh, heh… Boolie glares at Red Rock in a truth-extracting manor then Red Rock sighs and hangs his head in shame.Red Rock: I was only going to cut his break cablesBoolie: the kid isn’t even old enough to drive he’s like 11Red Rock: oh great I have Anton the Kid who is 13 and Ganker who is 11, what am I the PCW nanny? Jesus H Christ the things I do for gold sickens me.Boolie: so do you think you can beat Wanker? He gave you some trouble in NZW Red Rock’s eyes shoot open and he puts his hand over Boolie’s mouth.Red Rock: Don’t say that out loud I don’t want people knowing that jobber gave me a fight once! But it’s different now I’m going to f*ck him up good and proper! Wanker is going down! Boolie: yeah he’s going down on Anton the Kid!Red Rock: BOOLIE! That is child porn and very illegal do not say that! Boolie bows his head in shame and silences.Red Rock: you…you sicken me I mean urgh!Boolie: well…Red Rock: no that’s rank you were talking about Ganker an 11 year old and Anton the 13-year-old going down on each otherBoolie: well Ganker is actually 19 you know?Red Rock: but he looks like he is 11, and he stole my hairstyle the fool! I’m going to shave his head when I’m done kick his 11 year old ass back to hell with his hero Kurt Cobaine! He is a Nirvana boy! Metallica rule over Nirvana everyone knows that! At least Metallica’s lead singer could handle the pressure without taking a bullet to himself! Oh yeah and Ganker has an ugly big beak of a nose…and he is a skinny sissyBoolie: now those insults were just childishRed Rock: No one is too cool for childishness!Boolie: I’m going to bed, actually I think we should run away before they realise you totalled this roomRed Rock: good plan! The scene fades out to black with Red Rock and Boolie sneaking around to escape from the motel.
