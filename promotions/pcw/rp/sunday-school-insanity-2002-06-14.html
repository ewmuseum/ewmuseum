Sunday School Insanity

14 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6/14/02 The camera fades in… The Chef sits on an ottoman in front of a group of small children. The room is brightly lit with cartoon-like pictures of Mohammed, Jesus, Abraham, and other heroes of the great three monotheistic religions. The kids, dressed formally for Sunday morning services, seem uncomfortable in their mosque garb. Many of the children’s eyes wander aimlessly around the room, staring blankly at the great images of the past with uncertainty. One of the children asks the Chef a no-brainer in the Culinary Conquistador’s mind… “Is Mohammed real or pretend Mr. Chef sir?” The Chef rubs his beard, and twists the long strands of facial hair into curly little knots. The Chef: Without a doubt son, he’s as real as you, me, Allah, or George Lucas’ double chin… A girl, covered in a black silk garment, with a shawl covered the majority of her face continues the discussion. “Does Allah like me?” The Chef pauses and soaks in the stinking basement air, no doubt made worse by the amorous flatulence from the children. The Chef: He loves everyone my dear, even the infidels. “Then why do people die?” Faced with such a hard question, the Chef reverts to his militant ideals. The Chef: Some people deserve to my sweet little lemon tart. Many die with faith in Allah, many die for Allah, but the ones that don’t die with Allah on their minds are lost forever in a realm without Allah. “What is that place called?” The Chef answers in a direct and easy-going manner. The Chef: That is the realm of Iblis, the devil, the one who wouldn’t bow to man…but you’ll learn more about him later… The children frown and whimper at the Chef, wondering if they truly are with Allah. The Chef: Don’t look so forlorn, you are Allah’s children…Let’s finger paint!!! The children’s frowns turn to ecstatic smiles. They all jump up and sprint over to the art supplies in the corner of the room. The Chef: Don’t be shy; take as much as you want. It all goes on Osama Bin Laden’s Discover card! Praise be to Allah!!! The kids begin to grab at the various colors of finger paint, and start to fight over the more popular colors like green, blue, and pink. The Chef: Kids! Kids! Quit bickering like that! It’s rude, crude, and unacceptable!!! Don’t you remember the Golden Rule!?!? The kids turn around and look at the Chef. The Chef: In Allah’s Word, the Golden Rule is: “Do unto all men as you would wish to have done unto you; and reject for other what you would reject for yourselves”… One kids launches an inquiry into what the Chef just said. “What’s that mean teacher?” “It’s means shut your f*cking traps b*tch @$$!!!” A maddened Bertha tramples over a few small children as she stampedes into the Sunday school classroom. Bertha: It’s f*cking lunch time, we better head over to the c*ck @$$ KFC, motha f*cka!!! The Chef: Bertha… I told you I had duties here after mosque time… Bertha: F*ck duties, I want f*cking food! One kids tries to calm Bertha. “Remember the Golden Rule of Allah ma’am…” Bertha turns and addresses her concerns to the small child. Bertha: Shut the f*ck up f*cker! I’ll f*cking f*ck with who I f*cking wanna, b*tch!!! The Chef: Bertha! That’s no language for the children to be hearing. The Chef points to his adopted son, Manuel Hung, who’s crying in the corner. Bertha frowns, realizing she’s scarred the kid emotionally for the rest of his life.Bertha: Awww, the little mother f*cker… I f*cking made him ho @$$ skank crazy again… Little f*ck face… Bertha grabs the tyke out from the corner of the room and hoists him up in the air by the collar of his shirt. Bertha: Are you f*cking ok kiddo? Manuel: Mommy *sniff-sniff*…Omar took my green paint… The Chef realizes that Manuel wasn’t crying over her bad language and also realizes that Omar is in some deep trouble. The Chef: Awww crap… Bertha turns around and addresses the children. Bertha: Which one of you mother f*ckers is Omar!?!? One kids stands up from the bunch and holds out in his hand a jar of green finger paint. Bertha: F*cking guilty as charged! Prepare to face the wrath of f*cking Big B mother f*cking c*ck sucking fairy @$$ed b*tch ho!!! Skank F*ck!!! But before Bertha can charge at the innocent and terrified young man, the Chef restrains her. The Chef: You don’t want to do this my little sugarplum! Remember what Allah says to us in the Koran!!! “Forgive thy servants seventy times a day!!!” Bertha holds back the stampede, quietly thinking to herself. Bertha: Well f*cko, if you forgave that f*cker Stanislav for attacking you, you wouldn’t have been the mother f*cking champ…f*cking right ho? The Chef: Touché… The Chef lets go of Bertha and she charges at the little boy. Bertha: F*cking mother f*cker!!! Little does Bertha know that a small wooden caboose from a train set is placed right in front of the little boy. Omar prostrates towards Mecca for some divine help from Allah. Bertha charges at the boy, and unknowingly slips on the wooden caboose, flying over the boy and straight through the window of the Sunday School classroom, landing on her caboose in a pile of trash in a disposal container outside. Bertha: Awww f*ck… The Chef: And let that be another lesson to you all… Another Golden rule of Allah… “Whatever good you do for others, you send it before your own soul and shall find it with Allah, who sees all you do”… The Chef then pauses, and begins to prayer to Allah… “Praise be to Allah, the Lord of all creatures; the most merciful, the king of the Day of Judgment. Thee do we worship, and of thee do we beg assistance. Direct us in the right way, in the way of those to whom thou hast been gracious; not of those against who thou art incensed, nor of those who go astray… The Chef continues… “And Allah, you’re so fine, you blow our minds… Please watch over us, the suicide bombers, the Ayatollahs, the clerics, and even the infidels to an extent…” “Class Dismissed!” All the kids shout an enthusiastic “Yay!” and many “Praise Allah!” shouts can be heard as well. The kids rush out of the classroom, ready to greet their parents, who have been busy praying and receiving Allah’s various divine messages. The Chef takes Manuel by the hand and wanders outside to check on his beloved clone. Manuel: There’s mommy! Manuel runs towards a shadowy figure lurking behind the trash dispenser, licking the wrapper of an Egg McMuffin. Bertha: MMMM…f*cking cholesterol!!! The Chef: Bertha, now you know you’re not supposed to be eating trash… Bertha: But there’s a f*cking piece of ham still f*cking stuck to the wrapper!!! The Chef sighs. The Chef: I’ll be in the BMW waiting and reading Allah’s word… Come on Manuel, let’s leave mommy to be alone in her sanctuary for right now… Manuel takes his fathers hand and they walk away from Bertha and the garbage as the camera fades to black…
