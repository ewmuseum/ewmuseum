Rehearsal: Time For Home

2 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 04/02/2002 A life can take so many changes. Change for the good and change for the bad. But what happens when a lifetime of change occurs in only a matter of weeks? For that answer you may want to ask El Diablo, as all the change for the good and for the bad has happened to him over the last six weeks all due to one man, Flaymin Aymin. With less than a week to go to Spring Fling, things have really heated up between Flaymin and Diablo. From sick photos to beer keg explosions and still both men have survived, and it seems unlikely that their meeting at Spring Fling will end this rivalry between the two. But for one night that rivalry will have to take a backseat, as The Mexican Devil will square off against the 6'10' intimidating figure of Jon Kano. El Diablo may have bigger plans to take care of but Jon Kano will have to learn a lesson from the Mexican Devil, providing that a certain Flaymin Aymin doesn't interfere in Turmoil's happenings. Of course it will be impossible to stop Flaymin Aymin from attempting to get in the way, but it won't be impossible for El Diablo to take care of Flaymin, much like he did a few weeks ago when he defeated him on Sabotage. One win over him though does not guarantee he will get that win on Sunday at Spring Fling. The stakes are high, it's Flaymin's afro against Diablo's mask and the loser must never have that special attire ever again. Imagine, Flaymin Aymin with no more afro ever again, that would be heaven to the Mexican Devil, but the one thing he has to keep in mind is that his face could finally be revealed to the world, and losing that mask would ruin all the integrity he has in Mexico and his reputation would forever be tarnished. With a lot on his mind, El Diablo stands outside somewhere in Mexico on a lonely and hot night. He sports the mask that is on the line at Spring Fling and has his singed black hair running out the top of his mask and down his back. Diablo wears a dark black jacket and black denim pants with his black sneakers. He seems to be away from all civilisation on this dark night, he is leant against a broken wall in a desert area with some fields around that must have contained some animals such as cattle not so long ago, but now just a baron area of land waiting for someone to transform it into some kind of usage. The sky is clear and the many stars so visible to the naked eye, a warm breeze blows softly. El Diablo taps his foot on the ground like he is attempting to gain some sort of attention from somewhere when there is no one around. 'All you see behind is mine, something my grandfather left me many years ago. Only until now, I haven't had any chance to utilize it,' he says like he is talking to someone he knows. 'All these years I've wasted in not making a use of this,' he says with a sound of remorse, 'My grandfather left me this land and until now I've failed to use it. Funny isn't it? How I turned my back on my own grandfather like this, it should never happen to family,' he states with deep regret. He remains leant against the wall with his head looking up to sky staring into the night. 'Family?' he questions in the night air, 'Aymin would know all about that,' comments Diablo bizarrely. Diablo sighs softly, strangely, mitigating away all that he is thinking. 'Shadows of abyss, heathens of my soul. The only thing preventing me from revealing the truth now is this,' he says in an evil tone while placing his hand over his heart, 'I have some emotions after all and that is why I won't reveal a thing,' he finishes while looking around the area for at least some sign of life. The gentle breeze continues blowing; it blows the blades of grass and El Diablo's dark hair. 'It feels good to be home, it feels like I've been away a lifetime and that is Aymin's fault. My issues with him have prevented my initial plans. He shaved off my wife's hair, that is why she is not here,' he states coldly and angrily whilst thinking of his nemesis, 'I feel so lonely,' he finishes in a pathetic sounding voice. He slides down the wall until his knees are opposite his chin, but still his head remains staring upwards into the sky. 'It's sick isn't it, how all that mattered is gone all because of that one human being. I haven't forgotten my heritage although he wanted to, but Flaymin will never succeed,' he nearly shrieks in a crossed kind of voice. Before Spring Fling he faces Jon Kano and so far the one mistake he is making is disregarding the challenge of Kano on Turmoil, albeit that he does have bigger things on his mind than a one off match against someone he cares very little for especially in his build up for his big match, his destiny will occur that night. 'Somehow I will prevail on Turmoil and Spring Fling and somehow I shall prevail in this personal battle I'm going through. The world will know everything,' he shouts into the night without a soul around to hear it. He remains on the ground pondering what could happen to his life in the next week. All the change for the good and the bad rests on El Diablo's shoulders, so if you need to know about change whom do you go to ask? El Diablo.
