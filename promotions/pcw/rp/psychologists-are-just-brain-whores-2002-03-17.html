Psychologists are just brain whores

17 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/17/02 “You know, I must say it’s great to be back. It’s been a long time since I’ve been this free. Too f*cking long if you ask me.” The scene opens in a small office in downtown Oakland. As we get a brief look at the room we see the perverse cop, lying on a long leather couch, he continues his discussion... “I don’t know, but I think I’ve finally seen the error of my ways. I haven’t felt this free since my days back in the PCW2.“ Badge continues on, while we still try to figure out exactly where we are. “Well, all of the alcohol has sh*tted on my brain, but the last time I’ve felt this way, was right before I went to some headshrinkers office. Kinda like this one actually.” Did he say a headshrinkers office? If so, it begins to makes sense where he is. After getting a glimpse of the doctor’s credentials on the wall it all but confirms it. “Yeah, they used to think I was too violent, and out of control, so they had this stupid bastard put some sort of hocus pocus spell on me to keep me from destroying everyone.” As we remember, Badge was too destructive in the PCW2, so management forced him to calm himself down. “You know, those suits thought that I was too crazy for their tastes, so they ordered me to get help... Help, that’s an ironic word as that’s the last word that mumbled from that quacks mouth.” What exactly does he mean by that? And who the heck is he talking to anyways? “No please! I don’t know what I did to you, but I apologize! Somebody Help! He cried. The stupid bastard didn’t even remember what he had done to me. He was probably preoccupied with F*cking up everyone’s lives to ever realize what he was really doing to those people.” Of course not, what doctor actually cares about anything else but the money his patient will pay him? “I couldn’t blame him for not remembering me though. It would be kind of hard for anyone to try and think straight when they had a clamp squeezing their head in.” Did he say a clamp? He didn’t...did he? “It serves me right though. I should have killed the doc the first time I saw him. I shouldn’t of let him anywhere near my head. It’s his fault I was injured.“ How is it the doctor’s fault? If I remember correctly, Badge was pushed off the stage by Red Rock. “Of course you‘d say that Doc, you don’t know what it’s like to have to go through that much rehab. I spent a good two months trying to recover from the torn ligaments in my shoulder, and the two broken ribs.” True rehab is a b*tch, but it wasn’t the doctor who injured Badge. It was... Ah forget it. “I had to sit and watch these losers ruin the PCW, and could do nothing about it. You don’t know how many times I wanted to go to the shows and snap a few necks.” Hey now. The PCW is doing just fine. Badge is just bitter about being gone so long. “He was the one who made me forget everything. He made me forget all those little sh*ts who beat me up in school. He made me forget about those stupid crackheads, whores, and gang bangers that always tried to f*ck with the law. And he made me forget about that stupid mexican bastard that tortured my family. For that, the son of a b*tch needed to be put through the pain that I felt.” What the Heck is he talking about? And what’s that awful smell? “You know, the more I tightened the clamp on his head, the more his eyes popped out of their sockets. I let out a laugh at the time because he reminded me of the crazy b*tch from the talk show circuits who used to pop her eyes out like that.” Yes, we’ve all seen the strange black woman that used to pop her eyes out of their sockets. What does the have to do with anything? “It was a shame though, after a few more turns with the press, the bastards skull caved in, and he croaked. The sad thing was that I hadn’t paid him for the last session.“ That’s disturbing. Good thing it’s just talk...Right? “You know, that doc was just like those f*ckers from the PCW. None of them ever took me seriously. I was a big joke to them. Red Rock sure as h*ll didn’t take me serious, and now he’s in serious condition.” Yes, Red Rock was hurt badly when Badge had unleashed those evil children of his on Red Rock. “I still don’t know why he needed medical attention anyways. He just had a few children beat him. F*cking p*ssy.” That’s true, there were children beating on Red Rock, but what Badge didn’t mention was that the children were also biting, scratching, and kicking things, that shouldn’t be kicked. And he also left out the beating he had given Red Rock prior to the attack from the children. “You know, that reminds me. I’ve got to get those kids away from Clyde. Knowing that silly B@stard, he’d probably try and screw a couple of ‘em, Heh!” Knowing Clyde, that’s probably true. Unless Bethany has something to say about that. “I gotta give that crazy b@stard credit. He finally got rid of that spell that doc put me in.” So this is why Badge was seen associating with that tortured soul Clyde. “Why you changin’ the subject on me Doc? I don’t wanna talk about sex.” When the hell is Badge not willing to talk about sex? What did you do to him Clyde?! “No Doc, I don’t think Feletia cares what the h*ll I do, just as long as I bring my f*ckrod home for her to sit on.” Knowing Feletia, that’s probably true, though I think Feletia is looking to be more than friends. “You know, you’re right Doc! That’s probably the best advise you’ve given me! I’ll have to remember that.” Ok we already know that Badge is at a psycologists office, but why haven’t we heard anything from the doctor? Is the doctor even in the room? “Well, doc, I really needed this talk. Thanks again.!” As Badge gets up from the couch, he tosses a few dollar bills at the chair resting in front of him. As the camera pans around. We get a horrific glimpse at the doctor. There he rests, dead as a doornob, slumped over in his chair, his skull obviously caved in. Badge makes sure to give the doc a child-like noogie as he exits the doctors office. (The scene fades)
