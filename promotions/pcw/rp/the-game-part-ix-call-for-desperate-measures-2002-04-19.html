THE GAME - Part IX - ....Call For Desperate Measures

19 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4-19-02 OOC: To me, this is one of the three best RPs I've ever done. I hope you agree. =) Song Listening Guide: 'Alive' by P.O.D. (First half in basement) and 'Re-arranged' by Limp Bizkit. (Second half upstairs, lyrics: from B.S. to Mona.) There ARE NOT any better songs out there to go with this promo. Period. BOBBY S. CAMPBELL THE GAME - Part IX - ....Call For Desperate Measures. Then.... -It's safe to say that something that pisses The Showstopper off to no end has happened. Innocence has been lost. Beautiful, precious innocence... gone for all time and there isn't a freggin' thing to be done about any of it. Kira walks off to the right, to the smallish hallway between the dining room and the living room. Instead of going to the bathroom or the couple's bedroom, she takes a short right and decends a couple of steps down the stairwell heading down, as opposed to the other staircase leading up towards the modified attic. Kira looks behind her at a confuzzled Aimz and waves her on with a weak smile. Aimz nervously smiles back and follows her most likely future sister-in-law on down to the house's depths.... Where Amy Campbell will be only the third person on Earth to learn of a wild and unimaginable secret. Where she will learn of a secret that has around a fifty fifty chance of completely changing not only her life, but also the future of her entire family line... Forever. The truth of 'The Perfect Storm' will be revealed once and for all because desperate times... call for desperate measures. And now.... * * * --4-18-02, Thursday, 1:14 pm EST-- The scene fades in on the dank basement of a Florida home and the sounds of something being hit. Over and over again. Mountains of moving boxes and videotapes litter the immediate area in front of the camera, blocking the view. The camera sweeps around one side of the boxes, showing clearly B.S.'s newfangled TV monitor and VCR set up. Just like upstairs, there is also a PS2 system down on the floor next to the TV's stand as well. Controllers, memory cards, and a few gory video games all make up the assorted mess. Still, the sounds of something getting beaten at a persistent, steady level continue. The camera pans off to the right and towards one of the corners. Within the inky black shadows stands a large man. Large, but well-proportioned. You wouldn't be all together surprised if he was an Olymic swimmer as opposed to an Olympic weightlifter, for example. The long, redhaired male wears a pair of old and flexible Reebok sneakers and dark blue jeans. His upper front, shoulders, and back are bare, showing off his stressed and flexing muscles. Fresh sweat glistens off of him, creating the illusion of a shine thanks to the sunlight streaming in from a nearby window off to the side of him. The not-such-a-mystery figure is chopping, kicking, punching, kneeing, and elbowing a blood red punching bag into submission. All with textbook technique and focus. Not that an inantimate object can actually submit to... eh, never mind. A spinning roundhouse kick. A side elbow smash. A vertical elbow strike. A knee lift. A body blow with the right. A body blow with the left. A fake knee lift, right into an axe kick. Suddenly the guy growls and starts to punch the bag very quickly with hooks and straight right hands. The familiar voice slows down a little and starts to speak a name everytime he makes contact with the exercise equipment. Surprisingly enough, he says the names calmly while his actions are anything but. 'Kannon... Howard... Miya... End... Nelson... Nelson... NELSON!!' The figure pauses long enough to suck a deep breath into his lungs before starting over with the left hand. He yells louder yet... the demeanor in his words is still steadfastly calm and stoic. 'Badge! Clyde! Darkstar! Talena!' What..? The valet of one of Primetime Championship Wrestling's most accomplished veterans?? 'Talena! TALENA! RANDY!! ..Randy.. Randy..' As the man says these names there is a strange sort of crackling sound. The loud flesh-smacking pops that can be heard against the punching bag get louder... And louder. 'Brother! Brother! BROTHER..!? DON'T F*CKIN' THINK SO!!' Suddenly bright blue flashes start to light up the dingy corner and erase the surrounding shadows for just split seconds at a time. The strong figure stops talking and starts breaking the very structure of the bag down with smooth but deadly haymaker right hands. The impact produce hollow BANG! noises now. Tendrils of silverish blue energy rake up and down his tense back. Eventually, both bare and bruised hands shake slightly in a strange sort of navy blue glow. The bright blue flashes start to happen more rapidly and even brighter than before. The man can faintly hear a concerned voice in the background. Yet he pays it no mind since he is too far gone. Once you even start down this treacherous road there is quite simply no going back. The point of no return is the starting blocks. The athlete stops punching for a moment, his heavy breathing all that can be heard for a moment. Then... without warning: 'BBRRROOOTTTTHHHAAAHHHH!!!' A deep northern accent rings through the well-known voice as he opens his hands and spreads his arms out wide in a Chris Jericho entrance-like motion... while the entire area explodes! Don't worry yourself. It only explodes in light and noise. Blinding light. Sheer blinding light that is the same color of the words you are now reading. After a while, when the light seems to simply wash itself away down a drain that no one could possibly see, anyone watching this now and anyone watching him then can plainly see that this 'human' has changed. Rather dramatically, I might add. He stands there in the view of a side profile... simple and almost unfeeling. The anger passed as quickly as it had arrived. Unlike the prior times this has happened, the hair is normal and unspiked. All of it sloped back from his sweating forehead. All of the muscles are relaxed as if he just woke up from a deep sleep. The most strikingly odd appearance is a clear and transparent blue 'aura' that surrounds him now from head to toe. Ringed sparks of silver energy ripple through the 'shield' from up to down at random intervals. He looks entirely blue because of it, but that is only because of the light that has to filter through the 'aura'. In reality, the person hasn't changed color at all. 'Rrrrrrrahh!' The freak bellows out one more time while bringing his fist back behind him in what may end up being an uppercut. In a thunderous blow so fast that it is possible to not even be able to see the flight of it, this person brings his fist, arm, and almost the rest of his body right through the whole punching bag. As a matter of fact, the overhand right makes a rather gigantic spider web type of dent in the back basement wall as well! After three distinct gasps for much needed air after the ordeal of a sudden summoning of a higher power, The warrior slowly pulls his arm back through the clean hole. As a result, the sand and sawdust inside spills out all over the concrete floor. Just then a soft and nearly intimidated voice peeks through the chaos. Nearly intimidated. But not quite. 'Bobby..?' Even though it all began quickly... it ends even faster. The person's shoulders jump slightly. The guy has been noticeably startled. At the torso the person turns, red hair swaying off to the side. Smoky gray orbs replace where the person's eyes used to be. Much like a mood ring, the color of gray represents his undying neutrality at the moment. Unlike Switzerland, who stays out of everything to stay neutral... this person will do the opposite, in fact wading into the think of battle with anyone. At anytime. That viewpoint is beginning to change. The freak is focusing not on groups of people now. But just like he used to... he is now starting to focus on individuals. The individual that he is focusing on at this moment is OSW's Amy Campbell. The abuser of a certain punching bag would be PCW's Bobby Campbell. B.S. closes his eyes and in a quick flash of the same blue light, he is completely back to normal! Unfortunately the more important something is, the higher the price that must be paid for it. The Showstopper opens his eyes and staggers before dropping to his hands and knees in a huff. 'Bobby!?' 'Aimz' runs right over to her newfound older brother and kneels at the side of him. She puts a hand on his back to check his breathing. The only other thing she can do is wait for him to respond, which he does quickly. 'I... I...' 'Bobby,' Amy says quickly, 'what the f*ck was that?? What happened!?' 'I didn't know.. you'd be here.. so soon.' Bobby gingerly rises back up until he is on his knees and sitting back on his the heels of his feet. Kira York rushes over from the other side of the basement where she had been searching for a shirt or a towel. His lover hands him both. Bobby looks up to Kira with a warm smile afterwards. He rushes to talk to Kira before Amy can overreact or get the wrong idea. Already his breathing has become virtually normal again. 'Thank you, baby. I appreciate it.' Kira nods pleasantly to her boyfriend while Bobby wipes of his face and shoulders with the white towel. At the same time, Amy just glares at him. B.S. must sense the sudden tension to his left because he talks through the towel, the words coming out muffled. 'Hang on, sis, hang on.' 'Don't you 'sis' me, tell me what's going on! Why aren't you burned!?' 'Eh? Why you askin'? You want me to be?' 'No- Oh just shut up. You know what I mean!' Bobby has the nerve to snicker a little while he throws the towel aside, letting it land near the TV stand. While he puts what just so happens to be a very old and red fWo-era Tempest t-shirt on, he talks further. 'Yeah yeah. I know what you mean. I'll tell you everything... but I suggest we go upstairs first. Sitting on concrete doesn't agree with me. Unless I have to. Before you ask... I knew you were coming because I have sources.' 'Sources!?' 'Uh-huh. When you get money? The first thing you do is get yerself some sources.' B.S. finishes putting his shirt on. When he looks over at the kneeling Amy Campbell she just has her arms folded. She's shaking her head and fuming. Apparently, at any moment she just might blow a gasket. Bobby's eyes open wide because he's seen that look plenty of times before. In the mirror. 'Okay! Geez, don't go off on me. Don't worry about the punchin' bag, I'll clean it up later. C'mon, let's go.' B.S. springs to his feet and climbs the stairs two at a time, forgetting his mildly used towel. Kira and Aimz walk to the bottom of the stairs before looking at each other. Kira breaks out into a chuckling grin while Amy just rolls her eyes and mutters, but in a pleasant, non-nasty way. 'Down the stairs.. up the stairs. Down the stairs.. up the stairs! Oh joy.' Kira giggles a bit and they ascend to follow the... 'unorthodox' fellow. Bobby refused to explain anything until Amy agreed to stay long enough for a long lunch. That way he could make it up to her for not being able to keep the 'dinner date' they had planned. Timo Bolamba, Kira, Bobby, and Amy being the participants of that planned supper back in March when the OSW crew came to Bobby's hometown of Orlando. Completely curious about what she saw in the laser show display and what she did NOT see in the way of burned skin, Amy agreed. Rent-A-Hero's squeeze claimed her plane to the city of the next OSW Slam! event didn't leave until six thirty. Which meant she'd hav eto leave by six o'clock to be safe. Almost five hours alone with his two favorite women in all of existence?? Mr. Prime Time couldn't possibly be more pleased. * * * --4-18-02, Thursday, 1:56 pm EST-- It didn't take much time for B.S. to return from the closest Subway(tm) with hearty goodness. Amy had asked for McDonald's(tm) or as she put it, 'any burger joint', but Bobby (in full big bro mode) refused to give her something as totally unhealthy as that. When Kira pointed out the amount of sodium in those subs she was immediately cut off with a snickering 'Shaddup!' Mmm-hmmm. Two females in the house with no Carl Everstrong around to help our anti-hero out. Guaranteed verbal double teaming. *narrator smirks* Bobby and Kira sit at the heads of the dining room table pressed against the corresponding white wall. Amy sits on the opposite side of the table from the wall. A WWF Calender is hanging from it. Yes, that jobber fed. This month, Y2J provides for the top picture. A ceiling fan with a lamp hangs over them. The fan is switched on and the lamp is not. Behind Amy on the other side of the room sits two file cabinets, a tall CD stand, and an ancient, 1995 computer. The comp sits on a desk that has been built right into the wall. Cabinets are also built into the wall, above the monitor and the computer itself. Most of the cabinets are empty, as is the CD stand since the sexy couple are preparing to move back into 'Bobby Stormbringer's' old family mansion in just a few weeks. Everything that resided in those containers is already packed. The trio eats slowly while they talk. Kira obviously has to eat a bit slower because of her throat problems. To put it simply, Bobby hasn't felt this much at peace since he first signed with PCW. The last time he felt this kind of serenity was the first few times he met Kira, the week before last Christmas. 'B.S.' was out of the wrestling limelight and he could tell that he was falling madly in love with the chick. Things were simple then... he was looking forward to things being that simple again. At least for a month or two. Perhaps longer. 'So you're telling me that... that was Randy who visited me before that Slam! and not you??' Amy was staring at Bobby, incredulous. (Unbelieving. Stay in school, kiddos, and pass yer finals!) 'You got it. I think he jumped me like... a day or two before that? Right in our own freggin' home, right in front of the TV.' Bobby pointed over to the entertainment center in the living room and spoke with his mouth half-full. It annoyed Kira a little bit, but she usually lets the small things like that pass. Amy barely even notices right now. 'I can't believe this... how could I-' '-Not know? Don't worry about it. He and I used to pull switch-ups in school all the time. Heh... especially in Junior High. By ninth grade he got pretty damn good at it, come to think of it.' 'Umm. Why..? I mean, why do that?' 'It was usually for good reasons. I'd switch classes with him one day to help him on a test. I love history and math... even English. Just not science. Gawd, I just could not stand that stuff.' Kira smirks at her boy toy while Aimz rolls her eyes, not for the first time. 'I can't stand school, period.' B.S. finishes one half of his sandwich and wipes his chin with a napkin while he nods vigorously. 'I hear ya. So now you know what's happened... tomorrow night on Fusion... I'll clear everything up. It's my last show.' Kira finally speaks up. The voice is strained but still fairly easy to understand. 'Why.. go out with anything else.. but a bang..?' 'Right,' Bobby quickly responds, 'if I don't make some sort of an impact I'll prolly get ill. At least one on Randy's freggin' skull.. now Amy. About what you saw down there-' This time it was Amy's turn to interrupt Bobby. 'Lemme guess. You're some sort of a.. um.' 'Of a what..?' Bobby leans forward a bit to hear his sister's mumblings. 'Some kinda god..?' B.S. leans back and lets out a hearty laugh. Aimz looks over to Kira but all she gets is a confused shrug in return. 'Nah. At least I hope not... I'm agnostic. Which means I don't know what to believe, religion-wise. I know it may look awesome but... I think it's a curse. Not a blessing.' 'Wha!? Look what you did! You could win the World Belt tomorrow with that kinda power! So you keep it hidden..? I just don't think I get you sometimes, bro.' Bobby's lips twist in a sneer and then a movie star quality grin while Aimz looks to him in more befuddlement. 'What I say?' 'First time you ever called me your brother. Congrats.' B.S. raises his glass of soda to Amy and she just clicks her tongue. 'Shaddup.' 'I don't know much about it, I wish I did. Whenever I get angry... I mean REALLY angry, like the kind of rage that makes you shake all over? Sometimes it just... comes out. I once almost killed a man because of that. I will never use it in the ring. Especially not with thousands of innocent people all around it!' 'Okay,' Aimz nods and speaks quietly. 'I think I understand. We can talk about it later if we need to.' 'Speaking of congrats... kick*ss win on that b!tch Mona. Oh... and those other two chickitys, too.' Bobby sucks his teeth clean of some food particle or another. 'What- oh you mean Intervention. Aw no.' Aimz buries her face in her hands to cover the blushing and talks through them. 'You didn't see all of the Pay-Per-View... did you?' 'Yyyyeepp.' Kira clears her throat and starts to mess with Amy a bit herself. 'Amy... Rentie... sittin' in tree. F. U. C- 'Hey!' Amy's head pops back up in a look of shock for the usually demure Kira. Kira just giggles at the look on Amy's face while Bobby can't help but snicker himself. 'Now you know why I love this girl. Only New York-ahs have no shame and brutal honesty like that.' Bobby winks playfully to his love while Kira giggles a little more and blushes right away for The Showstopper complimenting her in front of someone else. 'Fantastic freggin' day. RAH finished second to Sylo, who I like too. I jus' like his style. You won your match and just pretty much stole the show. RAH n' you totally equal ratings. Heheh!' 'Stttoooppp yer embarrassing me!' Amy points at the camera, and therefore right at you. 'Plus you're taping this? Jerk.' B.S. laughs at the sudden insult and continues. 'Xavier freggin' Kannon lost. Although I'll admit it was all Duke's fault, just like most other things...' Aimz sighs at Bobby for his vendetta against the PCW Prez while he presses on. '...and Loc murder-ized Status Quo! Beautiful Day, no doubt. Oh, and HIT beating that big red commie's arse, too. I feel a little bad for.. Temp.. woops! Never mind.' Bobby suddenly remembers he's wearing a Tempest shirt and he does a pretty piss poor job at covering it up. Kira smiles a big smile at him and shakes her head. Bobby cracks a grin but recovers again into a straight face. Kira speaks up again with her pretty voice that can truthfully melt her love's soul if she so desired. 'You.. such-' 'I'll take care of it,' Aimz says dryly. 'You are SUCH a bad lier!' 'Am not!' B.S. gets defensive. 'I'm a very good lier. I mean uh-' Kira giggles at Bobby's freudian slip while Aimz and B.S. go at it in TOTAL breaks of kayfabe. 'Are to.' 'Am not.' 'Are to.' 'Am not, I say.' 'Are to!' 'Am not!' 'Bullsh*t.' 'Pfft! Glen call lately..? I hear there's a new strap on model that you might want to-' 'Jobby! You lost to lazy ol' Red Rock, nyeh nyeh!' 'At least I can kiss girls for less than f-' 'DON'T GO THERE!' Aimz' eyes are as wide as ever. 'Ha hah, hehehe. Aiight. No prob.' 'Enough, enough, enough. I'm just worried about one more thing, Bob.' 'Don't... call me... Bob. Ever again. I beg of you.' Bobby looks totally disgusted while Amy looks rather pleased with herself for getting the last shot in. 'Tell me what you're going to do about Mona. I can't believe you did that. After all the crap that slut pulled on me??' 'I know, I'm sorry. I was desperate. If I didn't get SOMEONE in Asylum back for their backstabbing then I would of lost it, I swear it to both of you. Then again, at least Randy's been takin' the fall for it. Not me. F*ckin' PCW. I am beyond sick of their nonsense. Only word for it. Nonsense. No matter who is in charge, things aren't about to change.' 'Psst... OSW!' Aimz grins at Bobby while he chuckles at the little vixen. She always means well, which is all that really matters to Mr. Prime Time. 'We'll see, babe. We'll see. I'll tell you exactly what I'm gonna do, though. About Mona..?' 'Yeah!' 'I'm gonna go to her hotel room tomorrow. I know she'll be in town, for Fusion. Either ordering someone to f*ck with 'me' or maybe even another slap. Don't know, don't care. I'm gonna knock the door down. She'll be in some nightgown.. she'll get scared and try to seduce me, since that's all she knows how to do.' Kira frowns and punches her open palm in a suggestion that Kira might just pummel Mona to near death herself but Bobby just smiles and waves down towards the table. 'It's okay sweetie, I know... I know. So I'm gonna grab her by the throat n' press her against the wall. One more freggin' warning. One. If anyone touches me OR Randy that night... you know, besides for me kickin' that snot-nosed punk around? If anything negative and physical happens then I'll hold her personally responsible. 'Ooo. I like the sound of that! I'm gonna have ta tape it since I'll be workin'.' Aimz rubs her hands together in anticipation. 'Besides. Our original agreement involved Mona and Loc leaving you alone for good. Mona broke that agreement when she trash talked you a few weeks back, and again when she used those brass knuckles at Intervention. I think Mona agreed to the Syndicate expansion thing just to get her claws into me. Literally. Gawd knows why.' A look of concern grows over Aimz' face in the realization that Bobby just may be heading into another trap. One that wouldn't find Randy this time, either. 'I don't want you getting hurt. That's all... don't forget all those names you called 'er, by the way. I'm sure that didn't help.' 'What? Like... oh, I remember. Young Tiger'll change the same day that Mona quits cold turkey at being a sick f-f-f-f-fffffffreak!' 'Heheh. That's the one!' 'Don't sweat her, sis. She ain' nothin' but a piece of peasant gutter trash. No really.' 'You... mean it?' Kira runs her fingers through her hair and winks to Bobby before holding her neck in some slight discomfort. 'Sure do.' Bobby sits back while the three share a short laugh. The rest of the afternoon... they talked about everything. Movies, games, sports, politics, Boy Hits Car, Old Metallica versus new Metallica, Linkin Park, how dominant Boda was at the PTC basketball game, Disney World, how dirty Daytona Beach is lately, Timo's strange habits, how much Amy can't stand being around Eugene, shopping (Bobby went to the bathroom), Byron Calver, Rent-A-Hero, Bre, Boy Hits Car for the fourty-second time, and anything else under the sun. Anything but wrestling. Everyone needs a break. Everyone needs peace and quiet. Everyone needs to feel loved. Everyone needs someone to talk to... Everyone just needs someone to listen to what they have to say. Just listen. (Everyone also needs to find out who won the Slam Dunk Contest and the big GAME last March fifteenth. So next time... everyone will.) TO BE CONCLUDED. THIS HAS BEEN A B.O.T.S. PRODUCTION. (c) 2002. ALL RIGHTS RESERVED.
