The Undiscovered Truth (Part 4)

19 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12/19/02 The Undiscovered Truth Part 4 *Hands twitch and eyes shutter open* Where? Where? *Deep breaths, strong need for rest as the dizziness that’s indulging my brain fades in over and over again. * I didn’t know where I was. My body was cold and the conditions were terrible. I don’t know why I’m here, and I don’t know what these people want with me, but I already sense something bad to come. I look over and in the corner is a small bucket with some clean water and a toothbrush. The last thing I’m thinking about is brushing my teeth. I look around and all I see are these padded walls and cement floor. There is a door in the corner, but it’s locked shut. This bump on my head is still moist with blood. I try to remember what happened to me, but it’s all a blur. I don’t even know what the purpose of this is, I’m being treated like some type of psycho under these conditions. HELLO! Nobody answers and I realize that I’m all-alone and that very few probably know where I am. The first thing that popped into my mind was escaping. I look around and the only opening anywhere is in the crack of the door. I continue with my human instincts and pound the door trying to open it, and that’s when I hear voices. This guy is gold, whether it’s the last thing you do; you make sure he stays exactly where he is now. You have my word on that. I better, because this guy can have us all retire as early as next week if we get him cooperating. I can’t see him being too happy after that bat to the head yesterday afternoon. Hopefully, he won’t be too upset. But that’s all up to you. I’m depending on you to keep him happy. I hope you realize you’re a vital part of this operation. I’ll do my best. Romantico then removes his ear from the door and holds his hand tight to his moist forehead. He then clutches his knuckles and pounds at the door. He hears footsteps approaching, and then jumps back in the corner acting as if he’s still knocked out. The man opens the door slowly and cautiously walks toward Romantico. Scared the tentative, the man graces his foot against the back of Romantico to make sure he’s still unconscious. Romantico with a sick smirk on his face opens his eyes as the man is standing behind him. He waits for the perfect time when suddenly… AHHHHHH!!!!!! Romantico sweeps his quickly the ground. The man tries to pull out his stun gun but Romantico jumps on him and head butts him in the face. AHHH!! You broke my nose. And I’ll do more than that if you don’t tell me where the hell I am. The man tries to hold his silence as he throbs in pain but Romantico grabs his hand and slowly breaks his knuckles until he speaks. Okay okay…. Your at the Star research Center of Madrid. What in God’s name am I doing here? It’s…it’s…. Romantico tightens the grip on his hand. It’s where we brought you so we can do testing on your DNA, because we believe you have a substance used in your bone structure that could change life as we see it today. Now please let go. Romantico let’s go and sits back thinking as the man throbs in pain huffing loudly after getting all of his words out so quickly. Romantico still dazed and confused demands to know how he got there. You tell me and you tell me quick, how I got to this place to begin with? The man takes a deep breath and begins… Well yesterday you probably remember us coming to your house and asking you to come with us. Well we were bringing you outside when we wanted you to come in our vehicle and you refused to go in. We made it clear that you had no option and that you had to come with us no matter what, but you responded physically hitting one of our men in the chest. The other man then took out a small bat and hit you over the head with it. Bastard… Yea well they when stuffed you in the back of the car and brought you here. What else happened? Any tests? Did they try and take my blood. No, they had to wait until you regained consciousness before they could get proper results from your body. They better not have, because If I find out they did, you’ll be the first one to be killed without a second thought. I promise I’m telling the truth. Romantico then sits back and doesn’t say anything for a few moments. The man finally breaks the silence. So what are you going to do? I’m going to get out of here, and YOU’RE going to help me. Oh no!
