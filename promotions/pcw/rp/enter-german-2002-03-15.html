Enter German

15 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: March 15th, 2002 (The scene fades to Mike Frampton is seen sitting at a table in a grimy coffee shop. The lights are dim with a yellow hue and the ceiling is clouded by smoke. The seats are orange, many of the seats have tears and rips exposing the internal stuffing of the seat. Mike has a glass of coca-cola in front of him. The glass is dirty on the outside and finger prints can be made out. As Mike takes a sip of his drink an old friend enters the shop. He is a large man with a superior build and seems to stand over everything inside of this coffee shop. He has a black goatee, blonde hair and is wearing semi-transparent sunglasses with an orange tint. He is wearing what seems to be a very expensive Armani suit with excellent Gucci shoes. This man is PCW superstar Big Daveylicious. He spots Mike and heads over to his table. He removes his sunglasses and places them in his front pocket of his suit coat. Davey looks down at the seats in which he is about to sit. He pulls out several napkins from the dispenser and places them carefully on the seat. He then pauses, thinks about sitting. Finally he decides to sit down.) Big Daveylicious: Hey Mike. Mike Frampton: Hallo. Big Daveylicious: Ah, don't you mean hello? Mike Frampton: Was holt Sie hier? Big Daveylicious: Have you been smoking crack, Mike? Sipping back on grampa's old cough medicine? Double downing a few haymakers? (Mike stares at Davey in utter confusion. Mike then smiles and chuckles.) Mike Frampton: Was Nachrichten holen Sie? (This time it is Davey's turn to stare in utter confusion. Why on earth is Mike talking this way? He is a born and bread Canadian.) Big Daveylicious: Sorry, Mike I can't seem to understand a word you are saying. Damn, your multiple personalities! Damn, them to... Mike Frampton: Beantworten Sie mich! Big Daveylicious: Ummm. Is that German you are speaking? By any chance are you extremely drunk, high, constipated? Mike. Hello in there? Anyone home? Mike Frampton: Stoppen Sie, mich zu beleidiggen. Was auf Masse wünschen Sie? (This time Big D is totally lost. He stands up, brushes off his suit and pulls out his sunglasses. He stares down at Mike.) Big Daveylicious: I will be right back. Listen, you order yourself a Bratwurst and I will be right back. Mike Frampton: Wohin gehen Sie? (Big Daveylicious bolts out of the coffee shop. The scene fades out to white. A moment later the scene fades back in. Big Daveylicious has just returned to the dingy, grimy coffee shop. He has brought with him his lackey, Michael Graves. Who happens to be carrying a German book.) Big Daveylicious: Mike, this is Michael Graves. He claims to be a Master of the German language. Michael Graves: Du Arshloch. (Mike Frampton stands up out of his seat and slaps Michael Graves across the face.) Big Daveylicious: What did you say to him? Michael Graves: I just said salutations. Big Daveylicious: Well be more careful you careless fool. Mike Frampton: Jetzt lassen Sie uns Davey an Geschäft unten gelangen. Big Daveylicious: What did he say? Michael Graves: He said he wishes to give you a friendly kiss. (Mike Frampton hears what Graves says and slaps his head. All he said was now Davey let's get down to business.) Big Daveylicious: Tell him I am not into that sorta thing. Michael Graves: Er ist nicht homosexuell. Mike Frampton: Verfluchen Sie Sie Michael-Graves. Alles, das ich tun möchte, soll meine Markenteamübereinstimmung behandeln Sie bafoon. Big Daveylicious: This time Michael, without screwing up, tell me what he said. Michael Graves: He said he is the father of your child and he once had sex with a bafoon. (Mike Frampton slams the table and his coca-cola splashes onto the table as he listens to the sub par translations given by Michael Graves to his new tag team partner. All he wanted to tell Davey is that he wanted to discuss his tag team match and that Michael Graves was a bafoon.) Mike Frampton: Jackass! Big Daveylicious: Hey, that’s English! Michael Graves: Don't worry, in german Jackass means I love you. Big Daveylicious: I am losing faith in you Michael. Michael Graves: Hey! Who here is the master german translator? (Davey looks reluctantly at Michael Graves and shakes his head.) Michael Graves: That's right and you better not forget it! Big Daveylicious: Tell him I want to discuss our tag team match. Michael Graves: Wir möchten unsere Markenteamübereinstimmung behandeln. Mike Frampton: Sicher! Michael Graves: He said sure he would love too and those shoes are great. Big Daveylicious: I sure know they are Gucci and... Stay on task! Tell Mike we have a match against Mettalyx. Michael Graves: Ihre Übereinstimmung ist gegen das Team von Mettalyx. Mike Frampton: Ja. Michael Graves: He says yes and by the way I think I am pregnant. Big Daveylicious: Get off it Graves, Ja means yes. Everyone knows that. Michael Graves: It is open to interpretation. In the German language expression can mean the difference between words. Big Daveylicious: Shut up and ask him if he is ready. Michael Graves: Sind Sie betriebsbereit? Mike Frampton: Ja! Michael Graves: He said no. Big Daveylicious: But a minute ago Ja meant yes. You are a jack@ss. Mike Frampton: Ja ist er. Big Daveylicious: What did he just say? Michael Graves: He made a derogatory comment about me. Big Daveylicious: I really doubt that Michael. Michael Graves: He did. He called me a snot nosed pug! Big Daveylicious: But pugs are cute. Michael Graves: Are you coming on to me? Mike Frampton: Michael-Graves, sind Sie so das Bumsen homosexuell! Michael Graves: Davey? Big Daveylicious: Yeah? Michael Graves: He just called you a homosexual. Big Daveylicious: Then why did he say your name? Michael Graves: He was talking to me! You see I have this soft little warm spot for you... Big Daveylicious: Graves, I have a warm spot for you too. Michael Graves: Really? Big Daveylicious: Yes, my foot in your a$$... (Michael jumps up and down excitedly as Big D shakes his head as his temper flares up from this on going shenanigans.) Michael Graves: Can you do it now? Big Daveylicious: Get the hell out of here! (Graves looks on the ground disappointed on fulfilling his mentor’s request as he walks out of our view to the exit of the coffee shop. Big D shakes his head. ) Big Daveylicious: Idiot. Me trusting him to translate the German language correctly is beyond me. Mike Frampton: Erklären Sie mir über es, das er dumber als ein Stapel Kartoffeln ist. Verdammen Sie gotischen Scheißestreifen. Big Daveylicious: Well said. Mike Frampton: Damn right! (Big D gives a surprised stares back at Frampton as the scene fades to black. )
