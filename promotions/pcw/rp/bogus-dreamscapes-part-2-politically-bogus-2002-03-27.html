Bogus Dreamscapes - Part 2 - Politically Bogus

27 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: March 27 *CONTINUED from Bogus Dreamscapes Part 1* [The scene opens in a brilliant white light. It is just like the first time Tommy fell asleep. He was in his white jumpsuit, which he looks most bodacius in, and the white light was surrounding him. His voice echoed and vibrated off of everything which just made his voice even more bodacious. The only bogus thing he remembers from this brilliant, white light is that damn Echo. It started out to be a most gnarly Echo but once Cowabunga Connection was mentioned the Echo turned on him. How dare that bogus Echo do that to the Tubular One! The Cowabunga Connection is the most radical tag team in PCW to date and that Echo could do nothing but talk about how Golden Game ruled. Tommy supposes that everyone can't have good taste, especially that heinous Echo] [Tommy is floating throught the light, happy to be back in the gnarly white jumpsuit. He treads his arms in front of him to make his floating more rapid and steady. He smiles and the light reflects off of his perfect teeth, just like it did the first time. It seems so blinding, all that whiteness. Then it seems that there is another figure joining him in the whiteness. Tommy thinks about the Echo, maybe its taken to human form and he can give it a Bodacious Bomb for talking about Cowabunga Connection the way it did. The figure has long hair, you can tell that much through the blinding light. A very stocky looking man, but isn't fat, just right. Who can this be? The figure gets close enough to be revealed as...'HEAVY METAL' HARRY!] 'Totally Tubular' Tommy: Harry, what are you doing in my dream? [The voices have an odd echo to them] 'Heavy Metal' Harry: I should be asking you the same thing, man. [The two men float together in the brilliant white light, enjoying it. Tommy then remembers the Echo and decides that he needs to confront this evil bogus thing. He now has his tag team partner with him, and just like in that soccer field, the Cowabunga Connection came out on top] TTT: TUBULAR! Echo: TUBULAR...tubular... TTT: There is that most bogus echo. [Harry looks at Tommy upset with the comment] Harry: That Echo is far out, man. TTT: Trust me, its TOTALLY heinous. Harry: FAR OUT, MAN! Echo: FAR OUT, MAN...far out, man... [Harry giggles to himself like a little boy discovering something for the first time. Tommy just shakes his head, knowing that the Echo just builds up confidence and then smashes you into little pieces once it has you in it's clutches. Okay, maybe Tommy is going a bit too far, but the Echo isn't bodacious at all] Harry: TWISTED SISTER RULES, MAN! Echo: DIO RULES, MAN...dio rules, man... Harry: Dio, man? TTT: I told you, compadre. It's an Evil Echo. Harry: QUIET RIOT RULES, MAN! Evil Echo: POLITICALLY INCORRECT RULES, MAN...politically incorrect rules, man... Harry: Those guys suck, man. TTT: TOTALLY! Evil Echo: TOTALLY SHUT UP...totally shut up... TTT: Listen, Evil Echo. I played your game before and I ended up on a soccer field. I must say it was totally bogus to do that to us. I'm sure you did see how tubular we were taking out all of those bogus Juan wanna be's. Golden Game won't be able to hold us down, and neither will Politically Incorrect! We are the COWABUNGA CONNECTION and we are BODACIOUS!! Harry: YEAH, MAN!! Evil Echo: NO, MAN...no, man... Harry: EVIL ECHO SUCKS, MAN!! Evil Echo: 'HEAVY METAL' HARRY SUCKS, MAN...'heavy metal' harry sucks, man... [Harry's begins getting angry. His face is turning just as red as his bloodshot eyes are. He begins swinging his fist at the air, hoping to catch a piece of that damn Echo. Tommy holds Harry back, helping him get composure over himself] TTT: Take a chill pill, dude. We have to find a way to outsmart this echo. Harry: Yeah, man. TTT: When it doesn't like something it will reverse what we said. So, thanks to that bodacious psychology course I took I'm going to use reverse psychology. It's a most gnarly method. Harry: Far out, man. TTT: POLITICALLY INCORRECT RULES! Evil Echo: I AGREE...i agree... TTT: BOGUS!! [Then the white light fades and darkness insues on the Cowabunga Connection] TTT: There goes that darkness thing again. Harry: I'm scared, man. [A few dim lights begin to shine. They are evenly spread apart from each other and get brighter with each passing second. It looks like it is a long stretch of hallway. The Tubular One and Harry stand together, out of the white jump suits. They are still in jump suits but they are very gray and dull looking. Tommy seems sad again] TTT: Why can't we keep those gnarly white jump suits? Harry: I don't know, man. [The lights come to full illumination, showing a very upper class like hallway. The carpet is red with golden stitching of various designs throughout the rug. The hallway, which stretches about fifty feet, is lined with fancy lamps on each wall. There are many paintings and pictures as the hallway progresses and there are a few doors] [Tommy is impressed with this place. There must be some kind of important, bodacious dude that lives here. It's much better than that bogus soccer field that they were on, thats for damn sure] [Harry feels bored and completely out of place. He wants to go back to that Echo and kick it's ass. Who did that Echo think he was, saying that Dio ruled? Dio isn't even in the league of Quiet Riot and Twisted Sister. That Echo just had no taste in music...AT ALL. He then discovers a very odd picture on the wall. It's of a very fat man wearing cowboy like attire. Underneath his picture it reads 'Billy Bob Green - Governor'] Harry: Check out this picture, man. [Tommy looks the picture over and his eyes light up with recognition] TTT: That one compadre from Politically Incorrect is a Governor! That..uhh..his name is...uhh...LOVECRAFT! That dude is totally bogus. [Harry giggles a little bit, dwelling on the name Lovecraft] Harry: Do you think he loves Craft, man? TTT: What are you talking about, dude? Harry: LOVE...CRAFT, man. I bet he loves Craft. TTT: The macaroni and cheese? Harry: YEAH, MAN! TTT: GNARLY! [The two men then walk to the first door on their right. Harry inspects it and opens it a bit, looking in. It seems very quiet in there and no one is around. Maybe they can hang out in there and take a couple bong hits. As Harry is about to walk in a lamp flies by and almost takes off his head] Harry: Whoa, man! TTT: What happened? [Then there is crashing and thumping all through out the room. The two men lean over and look through the tiny crack in the door. A very tall, dark and handsome man in a very expensive looking suit is breaking things all around him with a baseball bat. He's taking out mirrors, lamps, vases, and everything that lies in his path] TTT: This dude has totally lost it. Harry: Yeah, man. [The man looks to a large wall map of the entire world. He stops his violence and stands there breathing heavily. His strong featured face is contorted into an evil sneer. He walks very slowly toward the map, now wearing a very devilish smile] TTT: I think he likes geography, compadre. Harry: Yeah, man. [The man begins pummeling the bat into various parts of the map, screaming over his insane violence] Man with Bat: I am GOD! The rest of you will die! EUROPE!! ASIA!! DIE!! I am your God! [He lays hole after hole into the land of Europe and Asia, as if being some kind of God with a baseball bat] Man With Bat: AMERICA WILL RULE YOU ALL!! AMERICA RULES!! I am the president and you will all bow BEFORE ME!! [The Cowabunga Connection closes the door completely, letting the president have his way with the map. The two men look at each other with very fearful faces] TTT: Politicians are messed up, dude. Harry: Yeah, man. [Just then two very large hands come down on their shoulders. They turn around to see two very large police officers, with clubs in hand] TTT: Hello, officers. Harry: Hey, man. [The cops look at each other, then back at the Connection. Tommy wonders what is going on here. Why do these officers look so bogus. Harry steps forward and he sees what the problem could be. On the back of the dull, gray jumpsuits are the words 'BOGUS PRISONERS'. Tommy's face begins going pale. They are prisoners, and bogus ones at that. He's not bogus, he's Totally Tubular! Harry isn't bogus either! This is a trick. A trick by Politically Incorrect] [The officers smack the two men with their billy clubs, and they fall, in a heap, to the expensive carpet. The officers then smile and look to one another] Officer # 1: Think we should take advantage of the situation? Officer # 2: No, it's wrong to do that to someone who is unconscious. Officer # 1: But I'm HORNY! Officer # 2: I'll use the billy club tonight. Officer # 1: YAY! [The scene then changes to the full moon. It hangs in the clear night sky, giving it much illumination. It's on nights like these that you can see clearly on dark stretches of road, as if the moon is guiding you to a certain death. This is true for the PCW tag team, Cowabunga Connection. A large mob of townsfolk have gathered in town square to witness a monumental hanging. Two foreigners will be hung for their sins against the United States, for their betrayal. The local politicians rally on to the townsfolk, getting them to hate and despise everything that isn't American. They've convinced the crowd so heavily that they forgot what they were cheering for] [Harry and Tommy stand on the old, wooden platform with the noose already around their necks. The president, the guy who was bashing the map, stands proudly beside the so called evil doers. Tommy looks at him and is hoping that this dude knows the mistake that he is making. The Cowabunga Connection can't die, they have a title match on Friday. This dude will be haunted for the rest of his most bogus life if he does this] President: Ladies and gentlemen! Welcome to this magnificent event. These foreigners came into our country, our United States and tried to push their foreign beliefs on us. Since they are from another country, and not the United States...THEY WILL PERISH! TTT: Dude, we're from California! Harry: Yeah, man. President: Silence! TTT: Seriously, we are from Los Angeles. Born and raised there, compadre. I live on the beach. I walk out of my door, I've got sand blowing in my face. It's gnarly! President: BLASPHEMY!! These men are trying to destroy America. They are going to ruin the American Dream for us all. For all of you. For that...YOU WILL DIE! [The crowd cheers, somewhat partial though because these Cowabunga guys seem to be making sense] TTT: What did we do anyway? President: You...uhhh...HAD SEX WITH MY WIFE! TTT: Really? Is she hot? Harry: Yeah, man. Did I have sex too, man? [Then a fat, old woman with warts all over her face walks up to the platform. She seems to be broken up] Fat, Ugly Woman: They raped me! It was horrible. [Harry and Tommy begin to throw up on the platform. The crowd gasps in disgust] President: They are demons as well!! TTT: Demons? Your wife is the demon, dude. Do you think that a tubular dude like me would even touch a thing like that? TOTALLY BOGUS! I wouldn't touch your wife with a ten foot pole. Since your being bogus and going to hang us, I think the worst torture would be to make us be with that heinous thing you call a wife! Ugh... [The crowd begins murmuring to each other. Harry spits out the excess throw up and shakes his head in disgust] Harry: Not far out, man. Not far out at all. Crowd member: They are right! WE REVOLT!! [The crowd begins to charge the platform, taking down the President. The Cowabunga Connection stands a top the platform with smiles on their faces, watching the carnage unleashed] President: No...you don't understand...ARGH!! Watch my ball----YEEEARGH!! TTT: Ouch. Harry: Yeah, man. [A few of the townsfolk free Harry and Tommy, so that the men can take to the podium and speak] TTT: Dudes and Dudettes! We aren't politicians...we are BODACIOUS! [The crowd cheers, not really knowing what the hell a bodacious is] TTT: This President dude preached nothing but false promises so that he could look better to you. It feels bogus to be tricked but politicians like him will do that, just to have that most heinous power. Your free now, free to do as you wish, and can bodaciously PARTY! [The crowd cheers and begins dancing with each other. Harry and Tommy both exchange high fives, once again overcoming the odds to defeat evil. The Cowabunga Connection is two for two in the ways of evil, but what will come at them next? Whatever it is...they can handle it] Fat, Ugly Woman: RAHHH!! [They turn around to see the president's huge wife running at them, full speed] TTT: NOOOOOO!! [The scene quickly changes to Tommy absolutely drenched in sweat. He lies spread out on the bed, with a small puddle forming next to him. It feels like he has just taken a shower. Harry quickly follows behind Tommy, shooting forward in his bed. The two men then look at each other with fright and then at the poster of Jessica Alba] TTT: She's really giving me a workout, dude. Harry: Me too, man. No, Fat, Ugly, Wart filled women at all...only Jessica, man. TTT: Gnarly... [The two men then lay back down, looking up at the ceiling, and afraid to close their eyes. It's too hard to fight the urge though. The road has been long and hard the past few weeks and sleep is much needed. The two men fall into the darkness and then thats when the bright, white light engulfs them...] TO BE CONTINUED!!
