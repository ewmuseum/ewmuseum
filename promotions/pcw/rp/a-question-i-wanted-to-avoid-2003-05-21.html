A Question I Wanted To Avoid

21 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5/21/03 The television roared with the high pitched laughter of the cartoon characters prancing across its screen as I sat there watching my adopted son Evan absorb himself in the television. I thought to myself how peaceful the day was and how thankful I was that it seemed the day would stay like this with my mother seemingly isolating herself from the both of us up in her bedroom. Not only did it give me peace and quiet but it also gave me time to think ahead about my match which would place me against A.J. Trinidad on Fusion with the number one contendership on the line. “Tessa are you really married?” Four year Evan questioned, popping a question which had shocked me and left me speechless. I had kept the marriage a secret from him for obvious reasons, especially considering that kid had been through enough trauma in his life with his blood mother being murdered by her husband and then being adopted by Chris and I only to have Chris take off and disappear, the last thing this kid needed was yet another curve thrown into his life. “Where did you hear this?” I threw back a question of my own pondering where he got this source of information. Had he actually been up one night watching Turmoil or Fusion and heard me being announced as the name I regretted having, Tessa Windsor-Green, or had he been in contact with someone who spilled this information to him I wasn’t too sure but I was hopefully about to find out. “Your mom told me.” He answered with some caution as if he had been told not to tell me which would not surprise me any if the information had truly my mom. But my mom, how did she know? I sat there and pondered from my seat when I suddenly remembered that she had known for quite awhile now. As a matter of fact only a week had passed before she had shown up at the door demanding to meet my new husband and ranted about how much better of a husband he would be than Chris, too bad she knew nothing about him other than the fact that he was the man I had been married to, quite unfortunately. “Well…yeah…I am.” I stuttered not too sure about how to approach this topic considering I never planned on telling Evan considering it wouldn’t impact him much if he didn’t know. It wasn’t like Gaz was moving in or anything, especially considering how much I loathed every second he was around. “To who?” He questioned with the average curiosity of a child his age before popping yet another question. “Is it Chris?” “…no…” I answered regretfully as I remembered back to when Gaz and I were bonded in a marriage that was intended for Chris and I. Every time I thought about that moment it had brought tears to my eyes. How could something that was supposed to be so happy go so wrong? I’ll tell you why. Because of a jester named Gaz Green and his partner in crime Nerezza who couldn’t resist making our lives a living hell even if their original intentions were not to bond Gaz and me. “Then to who?” He questioned once more obviously not ready to give up till he gets the answer he is searching for. “Wow look at that!” I pointed towards the television screen hoping that he would once again become absorbed in the program and forget what we were talking about so that we could drop the subject and forget it was even brought up, but my plan did not work as well as I hoped it would considering after taking a peak at the screen he once again turned his attention back to me and went back to asking the same question. “Who are you married to?” He asked once more as he looked up with me with large blue eyes that were telling me that there was no way to avoid answering this question. “I’m married to a guy named Gaz Green.” I answered him, hoping that his mind couldn’t be anymore curious but surely enough after one question came another. “But I thought you loved Chris, why aren’t you married to Chris?” The child questioned confused about this whole situation and why I was married to a man named Gaz who he had never met. “I do love him, but things just happen.” I said before walking away leaving the child clueless as to what he should be feeling. Should he be happy that he got his answer? Or should he be feeling guilty that he asked a question that really wasn’t any of his business. I felt sorry for the kid but I just couldn’t bring myself to talking about Gaz around him. I didn’t want him to start thinking that just because I was married to Gaz his new father was, as a matter of fact that was the last thing I wanted him to think considering that last person I wanted to be a father figure to him was Gaz. I just wanted things to go back to normal with no Gaz no problems, but that seemed highly doubtful especially with someone like the prince of pranks as your husband.
