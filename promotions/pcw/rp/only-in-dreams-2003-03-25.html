Only in Dreams?

25 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 25-03-03 Jonathan went home after delivering his contract to the bank. The only steps he took into his one bedroom luxury unit were the ones that led to his queen-sized bed, and he fell upon it like a samurai upon his seppuku blade. Jonathan falls asleep within a few seconds. His eyes are gummed with dearth of sleep, and they find the closure they’ve been desperately seeking… ------------------ The dark immerses Jonathan so quickly, that he doesn’t even notice he’s in it until he is. The dream comes and it is beautiful. And then the light…it streams into his eyes, then it cascades, then it flows as an ocean. Finally, it takes shape and varies its hues to show a picture, rather than pure light. A sun moves through a beautiful cloudless sky and lights an emerald plain with a golden light only imagined. Jonathan stands, dressed in simple white garb and looks upon the sky whose hue matches his eyes. He stares at the immaculate and yet amorphous tall grass of the plain and sees not a clearing in it. He smiles, and his teeth shine with the brightness of a sun. He doesn’t need to walk, he can float, but he chooses to do so. He chooses to enjoy the leisurely stroll through the plain. Jonathan walks for a while, and passes over a few hills, not knowing where he is going. He seems to be just venturing on the wind that does not exist in this world. Jonathan walks for what seems like days in this ecstasy, in this world that cannot exist, until he comes upon what he wants to find, a circle of boulders, not four feet tall at the highest one but boulders nonetheless, in a valley between two hills in the plain. The circle of boulders is filled within its circle with soft white sand. Jonathan smiles, and quickly, yet joyfully moves towards this landmark. In the center of the circle of boulders and sand, is a shining object half-buried within the sand. Jonathan smiles, he has an inkling of what this object might be, and the heavenly plain seems to do nothing but push him towards it. All resistance to his motion stops, and it seems that every single force seems to now aid him in his pursuit of this new waypoint. Music of triumph and ecstasy never matched by any romantic symphony plays in the air. Jonathan is beyond the definition of the word enthralled. He’s never felt so compelled, and it gives him more life than he’s ever known. Jonathan comes within meters, and perhaps it is the lowering of the ground, but for some reason, the boulders are larger. The boulders are perhaps more than the 4 feet he had previously estimated. However, what had been a glint in the white sand now becomes a beacon to Jonathan. It seems even then to be growing brighter. Jonathan stops for a moment, and that which had pushed him on, now comforts him in his cessation of movement. His eyes look upon the light object he seeks, and it just grows brighter every second. No longer a beacon, now a lustrous idol of bright. Jonathan begins his movement yet again, giving up walking, and now just moving on the forces that seem to be precipitating him towards his light, towards what will be his own illumination. No longer a lustrous idol of bright, now lightning flashing over and over again. The circular wall of boulders, seems yet again to have grown. Jonathan does not stop to examine it though, he is pulled more towards the object in the sand than before. He looks upon the object, and only sees it growing brighter. Somehow though, it does not burn his eyes as it becomes… Stellar… He is now at the wall of boulders, and they have all of a sudden grown very high, higher than he can even see over. Yet the light shines above the apex of the wall, and draws Jonathan to it stronger than ever. A Shooting Star Jonathan looks at what is now an invincible retaining wall about his quarry, and then looks within himself, as one can only imagine doing in a dream. He summons the courage, and looks defiantly towards what he can see of the otherwise blinding light. He gives a cry, and leaps with all of the only-imaginary power he can summon. He soars through the air like the greatest of birds could only imagine. His look of determination fades into a smile as he realizes that he will soon be with what he has sought. He soars higher, higher, until he can no longer see the ground and then… Crashes into the wall which has risen with him… Jonathan shows a look of fear as he sees the distance he now has to fall. He begins to fall, and all of a sudden rather than pushing him on, the forces of this world begin to push against him, to hurtle him downward. Jonathan’s eyes go wide as they see what awaits the rest of him, and all of a sudden, the emerald ground is gone and he is falling towards a gravel and black street, as if he jumped from a building… And… He wakes up. -------------- Jonathan rises from his position upon which he fell on the bed. His eyes are in the same wide state as in when he beheld the ground awaiting him. He looks all around his apartment in fear, in ecstasy to not be falling at the same time though. He turns over on the bed, into a sitting position, and brings his knees to his chest. His eyes narrow just a bit, and he seems to calm down as his breathing slows. He tries to speak, to no in particular, but still only stammers out a few disconnected sounds. He takes a few minutes to collect himself and slow down the thoughts still racing through his head. Jonathan is no expert in dream interpretation, but it’s fairly obvious what this dream symbolized. Thoughts of meaning run through his head, but one question seems to remain constant… Will it evade me again?
