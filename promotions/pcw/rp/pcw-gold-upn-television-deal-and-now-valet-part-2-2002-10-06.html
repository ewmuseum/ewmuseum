PCW Gold, UPN Television Deal and now Valet? (Part 2)

6 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 06 - 10 - 02 Prologue: Returning from the set of UPN's flagship show 'P.W.A Tonight' the dynamic duo celebrated backstage in the confines of their private locker-room, when Gaz Green informed his fellow tag partner that he had devised a plan to hire a valet for PCW's Pay-Per-View, entitled Red October. After a disappointing first applicant in the form of 80's television star Mr. T who played B A Baracus in the hit show The A' Team their second has been regarded as a thorn in the side of Gaz 'The Jester' Green, super genius Steven Hawking. And it is from here that we pick up the events in the Pranks With Attitude saga.... 'I AM THE AMAZING STEVEN HAWKING~!' roared the monotone voice of Steven's on board computer. Nerezza stood motionless, his mouth twitching as though he were about to explode with laughter, his pale complexion tainted with a slight shade of red that occupied his cheeks as he looks on at the dumbfounded expression of Gaz Green's face. The unimpressed Jester dropped to his knees, hands held in the air and his mouth stepping up a gear. 'WHY GOD, WHY?!?' 'I've left you to bake in the sun during our short stint in NTICW, I've stranded you in the confines of a mine field... Earlier tonight I strapped C4 explosives to you and still you keep coming!' groaned the Conniving Comic, reaching the end of his tether. The tilted head of Gaz Green rose, almost begging for an explanation, his once jovial face now adorned with a frown, a shell of his former self. 'I AM THE AMAZING STEVEN HAWKING~!' said the robotic voice once more, toying with the Jester. 'Gaz, I think you should bury the hatchet....' insisted the behemoth Nerezza before an untimely Gaz Green interruption. 'What-... In his back! But as always your right, if you can't beat them, join them' The locker room became swamped in silence, an atmosphere that could be cut with a knife as the eyes of the super genius Steven Hawking locked on those of the present PCW Tag Team Champion Gaz 'The Jester' Green, almost a Western style showdown. Gaz leaning forward extended his arm and said, 'Steve, please... Don't get up!!!' Suddenly the relationship was consummated with a handshake and everything appeared swell when a miracle occurred as Steven Hawking stood erect, adjusting his weight for balance he tore away the excessive pipes and leads to the amazed glances of Pranks With Attitude. 'I-... I'M-... A HEA-... I'M A HEALER, PRAISE THE LORD, I HAVE BEEN TOUCHED BY GOD AND NOT THE EVAN LEVINE BETA VERSION!!!' cried the Prince Of Pranks in glee. Bouncing about like a small child, distracting the attention of Noscosta Ireul Nerezza both parties don't notice Steven Hawking removing fragments of his face. In all the commotion they don't hear Steven addressing them with a rather distinctive voice, the voice is almost infamous but yet neither does P.W.A notice nor home audiences distinguish the vocal prowess of the imposter. 'Err-... Arr-... Hmm, excuse me fellar's!' murmured the stranger, his face seeping through the disguise, but not enough to make a final judgment. The Jester regained his composure and turned with a star struck gaze, a similar expression to that of Nerezza as they lay witness to the masquerading Steven Hawking. 'Is that some skin condition there squire?' questioned the inquisitive Lord Of Laughter. 'I think I have some explaining to do....' replied the unknown gentleman as Nerezza intruded on the conversation. 'EXPLAING HUH?!? I THINK YOU SHOULD PUT YOUR TONGUE IN GEAR, DON'T YOU?' hissed the Living Nightmare with a snarl, the stranger acknowledging with a nod of his head. The imposter savagely removed his facial mask, which appeared as latex, his face finally unveiled as that of leading Hollywood actor Tom Cruise. The Top Gun actor stood with a gleaming smile before addressing the Trickster Team.... 'Well guys I'm here about the valet opening and by the looks of your faces I think I've left an impression, huh?!?' 'Hmm, you could say that Tommy boy!' retorted the Emerald Menace. 'This number was courtesy of the special effects team with Mission Impossible, thought I'd make an entrance. It'll be nice to work for Primetime now Xavier Kannon is in control, devil come down from Hubbardville trip....' continued the former husband of Nicole Kidman as Nerezza cut in with an intrusion. 'So you think you can benefit Pranks With Attitude?' asked the Angel Of Fear. 'So how can a vertically challenged scientologist assist us, you're not intimidating in the slightest!' 'Vertically challenged, huh?!? Why don't I whisper into the ear of my old buddy Xavier Kannon....' muttered the scientologist Tom Cruise. 'What-... And get him to screw us?' questioned the PCW comedian, watching on as Tom Cruise rolled his eyes. 'Talking of screwing, did you hand in your free Ellie coupon, entitling every scientologist to the ride of their lives?' joked Nerezza, brandishing his teeth. 'Err-... Arr-... Well-... Hmm-... You-... I'll tell him that you have tarnished Ellie's fine name' injected the irate actor pacing back and forth. 'Ride of your life? Always thought that was about driving a 206, but hell it won't be the first time we've mocked Kannon's 'F*ck Ferret' so please don't let the door hit you in the ass on the way out!' instructed the King Of Komedy, his arm out stretched, pointing towards the ajar door. 'NO, I'M A MOVIE STAR, I'M THE KING OF THE-....' yelped the leading star of blockbuster smash hit Minority Report. 'AND I'M YOUR WORST NIGHTMARE~!' yelled the Spawn Of Evil Nerezza. The vampire features of Nerezza came to light, his teeth fully displayed, his canines brandished for the world to see, dripping with oozing saliva. Tom looked on unimpressed, even taking the time to subtly smirk, and then re-addressed the towering collective of Pranks With Attitude. 'Nice make-up, personally mine in 'Interview With A Vampire' looked a more realistic but us stars have nothing but the best in support!!!' 'FORGET THE DOOR HITTING YOU, YOU'LL BE LUCKY TO MAKE IT TO THE DOOR....' proclaimed the goliath as he gripped the collar of Mr. Cruise and literally threw him from the premises to a thunderous thud. As the duo prepare to leave for lunch, well most likely to go home they descend upon the door when suddenly another familiar face cockily strutted up full of smiles, the celebrity being Chris Tucker. The black actor makes it as far as the door and has his progress stopped from the departing P.W.A who inform him that their valet search is in jeopardy and that the Jester, vampire combination would re-evaluate. 'Sorry Chris - It's been a long day. From washed up 80's celebrities to Halloween loving scientologists, it's kinder taken its toll, so maybe we'll phone you if we continue to pursue this further!' informed the Velvet Prankster Gareth Green with a look of fatigue. 'DAMN! NO SHIT - WE'LL ME BROTHERS, IT LOOKS LIKE I GOT TO FILM RUSH HOUR 3 WITH ME MAIN MAN BRUCE LEE AND ADIOS COMPADRES!!!' said Chris Tucker as the three-man party waltzed from view. The scene eventually coming to an abrupt closure with nothing but a black screen remaining.
