Doing A Last Minute Promo Against Rush Mason to beat him, just like last time.

Date: Meh So here I am... On the crossroads of life. I could've come from the path of evil. Could've continued being a dark gimmick. Or tried to be a dark gimmick. Meh, I was always too honorable to be good at that. Back to the Dark Ages, when I refused to personally get involved in their issues. Or how I wouldn't let them interfere in my business. Look at my career. Started in PWA, was in it for a week before getting promoted. Sure, it was to EW, but it was a good business decision. Anyone who looks good there is doubly impressive. So, in the cesspool of suck, wrestlers like myself, Six Finger Cisco, and Young Tiger stood out and as soon as the split from PTC, we all jumped at PCW's offer. I hung around the bottom of the barrel, true, but I was working with people in the upper-tier! If there was someone in the Continental Division, I was somehow invovled with what they were doing. I myself was in the Rising Star Division shortly before being placed in the ailing Cruiserweight Division. I propped up the Cruiserweight Division and then held the belt twice. And look who I've defeated along the way. Clyde. Darkstar. Stormbringer (not that this is worth anything, but it's just fun to mention) Drake Maxwell (throwing him off a cage. Heh) Look who Rush Mason has defeated? Kramer Wild Wing. ...... that's it. Shame. I've spent the past 18 months working in this industry to become the best in the business. And I'm not going anywhere any time soon. PCW is the top of the wrestling business, being anywhere else just wouldn't be right. I've been a regional success before. But here, I'm a worldwide success. And whether or not Rush Mason likes that, it is not going to change anytime soon. This is short, I'm not spending much time dealing with Mason. He will be out of PCW by Sunday night, and since he wouldn't want to work anywhere else, I know he'll just retire. I just hope that he and CCW don't challenge Kano and I to a tag match in a month or so. Beating up unemployed people sucks.
