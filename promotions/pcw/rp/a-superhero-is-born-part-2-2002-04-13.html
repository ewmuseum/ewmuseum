A Superhero Is Born Part 2

13 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: April 13th, 2002 (Mike Frampton walks a lonely street at the late hours of night. A woman screams in the background. Mike drops his bag. He turns towards the direction of the scream.) Mike Frampton: My spider sense is tingling. Someone needs my help. (Mike opens his bag. He pulls out a spray can. The label reads 'Actual Real, Not Fake, Not Imitation, Real, Real, Real Spiderman Webbing'.) Mike Frampton: I must go help! (Mike runs into the nearest telephone booth. The scene is spun with a white circle. The camera becomes normal. Mike then jumps out the telephone booth in a child sized costume of Spiderman. It is extremely tight and is ripping in some areas.) Mike Frampton: Now I must go to help! (Mike runs off as the scene fades to black. A moment later the scene fades back in. Mike stands outside a burning house with his Spiderman costume on. He sees a woman in the window begging for help. The fire department can not quite reach her. Mike locks in a cannister of his webbing.) Mike Frampton: Let's rock! (Mike sprays the webbing at a high point just under the window. The webbing sticks and Mike flies up just under the window. The 'Actual Real, Not Fake, Not Imitation, Real, Real, Real Spiderman Webbing' worked! Mike then jumps off the wall and grabs onto woman. He shoots another line of webbing to a fire truck ladder. He and the woman fly off and land on the ladder. The woman climbs to down as does Mike.) Mary Mo: Thank you ever so much you kind stranger. But my cat is inside the building. Mike Frampton: Never fear I will save your cat! (Mike shoots another line of webbing to the woman’s window. Mike climbs into her burning apartment. A crowd of people surround the inferno. A line of webbing then shoots out onto the ladder. Mike flies out of the apartment with a White Tiger. Mike climbs down with the tiger on his shoulders. He reaches the ground and puts the tiger down. Enraged by the fire the tiger runs into the crowd of people. The tiger grabs the little boy Tod who was thrown into the streets earlier in the day. The Tiger whips Tod into the wall. The woman runs up to Mike.) Mary Mo: Thank you oh thank you. (The sound of a piano rings inside the building.) Mary Mo: Oh no! My grand player piano! Mike Frampton: Never fear I will save your piano. (Mike shoots a line of webbing. It reaches the window and Mike begins to fly. Mike flies into the window. The crowd gasps. As Mike is inside the Tiger grabs Tod and begins to chew on Tod's leg. Suddenly a line of webbing shoots out from the window. Mike and the piano crash through the wall. The wall crumbles. Mike flies out but loses his grip on the piano. The tiger lets Tod be.) Tod: WOO! (Tod looks up.) Tod: Uh oh.(The piano lands on top of Tod. The crowd sees this and turns to Mike. They begin to cheer for his deeds.) Mike Frampton: Thank you, thank you. Mary Mo: Oh thank you! My piano is perfect and not even burnt! Mike Frampton: Now I must leave you. (Mike shoots a line of webbing down the street. Instead of locking onto a building it hits a truck. Mike flies into the side of the truck. He is dragged alongside the road.) Mike Frampton: Ouch! Ouch, ooo, OUCH! (Mike frees himself, as he does this he hits his head. The crowd runs over. Mary Mo helps Mike up.) Mary Mo: Are you ok? Mike Frampton: I think so. Mary Mo: By the way you never said who you were. Mike Frampton: That's my secret. (Mike gets up and brushes off. He walks into the street. Suddenly he is hit with a car and flung into the air as the scene fades out.)
