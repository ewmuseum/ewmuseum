The Tank 28 - Shedding Skins

1 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: October 1, 2002 ﻿*********THE TANK __28—********* Shedding Skins High light of eye lightthere’s no light in twilightwhere wind blowsand hate growsI’ll see you tomorrow ~Leigh Donovan Thursday, September 26 - Day 84 Most people hate the sound their alarm clock makes in the morning - they’ve never heard themorning buzzer inside the Tank. Loud, intrusive, and a constant reminder to each prisoner thathe has yet another day of misery to live through, the buzzer blares through Unit B, and theprisoners quickly sit up in their bunks. Jason Snow comes breaking out of a dream about the way things used to be - his family, his job,and the life he took for granted. He sits up quickly, his blond hair darkened slightly be sweat,and looks below where his cellmate, Leigh Donovan, is rolling out of his lower bunk. “Morning,” Donovan says sarcastically in his thick Australian accent. “I wonder what fun todayhas in store for us.” He then turns his head toward Snow and remarks, “You were late gettingback in from your show yesterday. Usually you get back the same night, don’t you?” “Fucking storms,” Snow says coldly. “Pilot wouldn’t take off until the weather cleared.” “I wouldn’t complain,” says Leigh as he leans toward the mirror to inspect his face. “Everyminute out there is a minute you don’t have to spend in this fucking hell hole.” “Yeah, it’s great,” Snow says sarcastically. “Sitting in a chair, shackled at my wrists and ankles,between two fucking pricks who I’m paying to wait for the opportunity to clip me. Compared to that, this is fucking paradise.” Leigh never considered this. He’d always assumed Snow’s time out of the Tank was spent like anormal civilian. Until now, he wasn’t aware that even when beyond the confines of these walls,Snow is still a prisoner - just like him. “What the fuck is that?” Snow asks suddenly, and coldly, pointing to the mirror where he canclearly see Leigh’s naked upper body. “What?” “Who the fuck put that on you?” Snow roars, sliding down off the bed and taking the two stepsto Leigh. He grabs the young Australian by his wiry arm and whirls him around. There, on theleft side of Leigh’s exposed chest, is a thick black swastika. “Why the hell should you care?” Leigh asks, his face contorting with short-fused anger. Impressively, he’s able to push Snow away. “You’re going to walk out there with that shit on your fucking body?” Snow says, pushing Leighback with such force that the smaller man’s back connects painfully with the metal sink. “Whatthe fuck do you think the Thugs are going to do to you when they see that?” “What the fuck do you care!?” Leigh snaps back, refusing to give in to the pain long enough toput a soothing hand on his aching back. “I don’t,” Snow responds coldly. “But you should. J-Thug’s already going to put your ass on aplatter for every sex-deprived maniac in this hell hole. Now, when he gets out, he’s going to pullyour heart out right through the fucking ink on your chest.” “You think I’m scared of that big n!gger?” Leigh fires without remorse. “Let him come for me! I’ve got people watching my back now, mate. The Leigh Donovan that came into this place isn’tthe one that’s walking out! J-Thug already killed him. But he can’t control me any more! Iwon’t fucking let him!” “Fine!” Snow barks as the bars of their cell electronically slide open. He extends his arm towardthe gaping exit. “After you.” Leigh leads Snow out of the cell for morning roll call, and stands next to ‘Nam, from the nextcell over, whose recently been released from solitaire confinement. Standing shirtless, with asymbol of his new beliefs scarred over his heart, the young Australian rockstar can feel the heatfrom every eye in the unit. “Go see Harvey Blake,” Snow whispers under his breath. “I suppose it’s better for him to stick itin your ass figuratively than the Thugs to do it literally.” “Jason Snow!” bellows one of the C.O’s below. “Did I say you could speak!? Shut your fuckingface!” Friday, September 27 - Day 85 “Harvey’s a fucking genius, man,” says Jake Hooper with a smile as he tosses a pillow toVincent Giovanni. The two men have been assigned to work with each other, washing pillows,by Geoffery Rankin. “I hear much to that affect,” Giovanni nods as he tosses the pillow over a large red curtain,where it’ll be picked up later by a cart and shipped off to the laundry room. “They say he’s aleader to every white man in here.” “A God is more like it,” Jake says while scratching his big bald head before grabbing anotherpillow from his pile. “In here, Harvey controls white men’s lives. They do what he says, orelse.” “Or else what?” “Bad things happen.” “So,” Giovanni begins with curiosity lacing his voice, “Blake could conceivably request amurder? And someone would do it for him without him even having to raise a finger?” “Conceivably?” chuckles Jake. “Fucking guinea. That kind of shit goes down everymotherfucking day in here. A n!gger goes missing for a couple of days and ends up in a garbagebag, you can usually bet Harv gave the order.” “But don’t they have a gang too? Call themselves the Thugs?” Giovanni asks. “Sure,” shrugs Jake. “But what can you expect of a gang run by a bunch of fucking monkeys. They have no organization. The n!ggers in here ain’t like the Aryans, or even like the way youfucking guineas run things. They don’t give a fuck about order in their gang - they just follow J-Thug because they’re so fucking afraid of him.” Giovanni knows that most of Harvey Blake’s followers fear the one eyed Nazi too. He thinksJake is a typical young skinhead - blind and confused, despite the fact that he has such obviouswit. “How do you guys run things then?” asks Vincent Goivanni. “I mean, you and the ‘guinneas’?” “Harvey’s a fucking leader,” says Jake with pride. “People follow him because they know whata great man he is.” “And the Italians?” Jake shrugs, “They follow Tim McDonald because they know he can bring in the most money. Fucking drug dealer. Smart as fuck though - I’ll give him that.” “But didn’t Doug Hattoni run things before?” asks Vincent as he catches another pillow androutinely tosses it over the curtain. “I mean, why’d they follow him?” “He was so big on the outside, I guess,” shrugs Jake. “In here, all you fucking guineas justcrowded him and ordained the fat motherfucker. I’ll give that stupid prick one thing though - heran a tight ship.” “Damn,” Vincent sighs while shaking his head. “It would have been such a thriller to meet theguy.” Jake chuckles, “You fucking guineas. Hattoni ran a tight ship, like I said, but the motherfuckerwas still nothing more than a fat heroin pushing cocksucker.” “Come on,” objects Giovanni. “You’ve got to give the guy something. He was a great fuckingleader. I mean, of all the people in here who must have wanted to have him clipped? He musthave been great to stay alive as long as he did.” A cocky grin comes over Jake’s face as he tosses another pillow. “Well,” he begins, “he’s stillno fucking Harvey Blake.” Suddenly, Jake’s tone turns sharp. “Because don’t you forget, youfucking guinea, who won the war between you and the Aryans.” Vincent raises a curious, and suspicious, eyebrow. Friday, September 27 - Day 85 Only moments after the ‘Lights Out’ buzzer sounds, Tim McDonald struts back toward his cellunder the heavy taunting of an anonymous C.O. “Fucking cock,” McDonald sneers as he turnsto the corner of his cell to find Vincent Giovanni, once again, writing, with a soft-tipped pen, ona small note pad. “What is it you and that fucking poetry? Your ass is going to be soil for a big black man’s seedwith all this pansy shit,” McDonald says with an insulting chuckle as he enters. Surprised by hiscellmate’s appearance, Vincent Giovanni immediately fumbles with the notepad until he’sfinally able to stuff it under his bunk. “Timmy,” Vincent stutters, trying to retain his cool exterior and not appear shaken byMcDonald’s sudden presence. He should have known, he knows - the buzzer did justsound for all the prisoners to make their way back to their cells. But usually, Tim McDonaldtakes his time returning. “I wasn’t expecting you back for a few minutes.” “What the fuck?” Tim narrows his eyes, thinking not of Giovanni’s comments, but his quickdiscard of his notebook. “Huh?” Vincent Giovanni acts confused. “Why’d you hide that God damn notebook?” snaps Tim, pointing down at Giovanni’s bunk. “Hide it?” Vincent chuckles, poorly disguising his nervousness. “I’m not hiding anything fromyou Timmy.” “Great,” smiles Tim, stepping toward the bulky Italian. “Then lets take a peek.” Still smiling, Tim reaches down for the Giovanni’s drawer. When he slides it open, however,he’s surprised to find Vincent’s deceptively strong (even for his bullish stature) hand clutchingTim’s much weaker wrist. A moment of silence passes as Tim looks coldly over at Vincent. “Take... your... fucking... hand... off me,” he says slowly, adding new depth to his stare witheach syllable. Hesitantly, Vincent obliges and removes his grip on Tim’s wrist. However, when Tim reachesback for the drawer, the Italian bull snaps! Vincent Giovanni reaches once more for TimMcDonald, but this time, he goes straight for the throat. “Fuck!” McDonald curses as he’s effortlessly whipped from the side of the bunks to the oppositewall, where he finds himself pinned by the enormous strength the greying stallion. “What thefuck are you doing!?” “Listen you little cocksucker!” snaps Giovanni as he effortlessly holds McDonald against hiswill. “I don’t give a fuck who you are, what’s mine is mine! You don’t touch it! Haven’t you ever heard of privacy!?” After one final long stare, Giovanni releases the bewildered Tim McDonald and turns back to hisbunk. He wipes the sweat nervously off his face, wondering what the consequences of hisactions will be. “I’m sorry,” he finally says while looking over his shoulder at the stunned youngprison-mob boss. “I just can’t control that shit sometimes.” “Yeah,” McDonald responds, finally moving away from the wall, rubbing his sore throat. “Justremember who you’re shoving around next time,” he says with a voice full of malice and staringcoldly at his attacker. Without another word, he climbs up onto his bunk, pretending to haveforgotten about the mysterious notebook beneath Giovanni’s bunk. A Warden’s voice destroys any commotion left in the Unit: “Lights out!”
