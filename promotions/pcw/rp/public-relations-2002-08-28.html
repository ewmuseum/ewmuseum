Public Relations

28 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 28/08/02 “Um.. Like, WHY are we doing this again?” Our shot opens up on the Commissioner of PCW, Eleanor Hall, peering through a curtain, a look of displeasure on her face. “Because, my little Fuck Ferret, it’s called Public Relations.” The camera pulls back to show that the little prep is beside her husband, the PCW CEO, Owner and Champion Xavier Kannon who’s dressed smartly in a classic cut Navy Blue suit and is currently straightening his tie and checking his cuff-links. Eleanor : “But... But... They’re all, like... THE PUBLIC!” She lets the curtain go and it falls back into position as Xavier reassures her. Xavier : “I know they are, and they are smelly, dirty and they all believe in this God. But the more we raise our public image, the more money PCW will make.” The mentioning of currency and revenue bring Ellie~! back around in a second, her pale Blue eyes shimmering once more. Eleanor : “Enough to buy me that Dinosaur you keep saying we can’t afford?” As she looks at him with such hope in her eyes, even a man as cold as Kannon can’t give the reply most would. Xavier : “We’ll see, we’ll see... It’s time to do this now.” The Scientologist walks around his wife and holds the curtain out for her to enter the conference room before he follows her in. Both cast their eyes over the “Focus Group” of assembled wrestling journalists and PCW fans... The CEO sneering. Xavier : “Yee Hubbards... They’re feeding the Scientologists to the Peons!” The couple take their seats on the lightly raised stage under the gaze of all those assembled in the room. 5 members of PCW security are dotted around in high profile positions but it does nothing to ease Xavier’s displeasure at being so close to society. Xavier : “Okay, let’s get this over with....” It appears that the questions have already been vetted and ordered as rather than a flurry of questions, most of the room look to where they know it will come from.. A large lady towards the back who stands. “Mr Kannon, is it true that already you have halved the Charitable Donations that PCW regularly gives?” She remains standing as Kannon rolls his eyes, seemingly bored with the exercise already. Xavier : “It’s just like a mere Peon like you to get the facts wrong.... I can categorically state that I have NOT halved them...... They have been reduced by 64% to be exact. Next question?” The woman doesn’t sit back down and seems keen on pressing the point. “Can you tell me why that is?” The Scientologist sighs as Ellie~!, after looking at the woman with disgust, weighs in with her opinion. Eleanor : “Because you’re fat.” The woman’s jaw drops and her eyes widen in a mix of shock and embarrassment.. Her chubby face turning Red. “I BEG YOUR PARDON!” The little prep tilts her head and looks with sympathy at the large woman. Eleanor : “Oh.. Didn’t you know you were overweight? I guess you took all the mirrors away to stop you crying.” Strangely, the prep actually seems to be winning something as the woman who asked the Charity question appears lost for words. “I-..... I-....” The Commissioner’s voice takes on an uncharacteristic soft tone, a far cry from her usual preppy, sarcastabitch voice. Eleanor : “I feel your inner pain.... I was once 8 ounces overweight, but you can beat it. I did.” The humbled and embarrassed women mumbles to herself, her eyes looking with hope at Ellie~! who gives a comforting nod before she sits down, her questioning over and now reflecting on her life as a whole. “Mr Kannon.... What if I were to say that your position of CEO is in direct conflict to that of your position as a wrestler, and more importantly the PCW Champion.” The CEO’s Green eyes skip across the room to where a tall, lanky man with thick Black rimmed glasses is standing, looking up at the couple. A sneer forms on Kannon’s face as he looks the man up and down. Xavier : “I’d say you are an idiotic Peon and that I need not answer your question...” The man tries to get a retort in but Kannon points to his chair. Xavier : “SIDOWN!” As the lanky man drops to his chair the next question come flying at the stage. “Do you believe that religion should play no part in wrestling... Especially after the Islamic Fundamentalism that was rife in PCW not even 6 months after September 11th?” In contrast to how he dealt with the last question, Xavier takes a solemn approach this time. His head drops and shakes in almost a disappointed manner before he runs his hands through his mane of long, Strawberry Blonde hair. Xavier : “You’d like that wouldn’t you?” The woman who asked the question gestures to herself, confused by his response. Xavier : “WHAT GIVES YOU THE RIGHT TO SAY I SHOULDN’T BE ABLE TO BE A FOLLOWER OF SCIENTOLOGY JUST BECAUSE I AM ON NATIONAL TELEVISION!!!“ She looks rather taken aback by the temperamental PCW owner’s outburst and tries to explain her question and defend herself. “I wasn’t sa-” But the Scientologist is giving her no ground to manoeuvre. Xavier : “I suppose you’d still allow CHRISTIANITY...” Kannon cringes and shudders. Eleanor : “Still..... Allow..... Christianity!” Now his wife sneers at the woman. Xavier : “Because that’s a MAJORITY religion isn’t it.... It’s ACCEPTED! IT’S THE NORM! You know what is wrong with America? PEOPLE LIKE YOU!!!” All eyes in the conference room are now on the smartly dressed woman who asked he question and her face is the stereotypical “Rabbit in the Headlights”, not looking comfortable with all the gazes that converge on her. “Bu-” Xavier : “I BET YOU’D BURN ALL THE MOSQUES AND TURN SYNAGOGUES INTO GAS CHAMBERS!!!” Desperately the woman tries to defend herself but she simply cannot get a word in as the CEO berates her, and Ellie~! backs him up with “Yeah”s. Xavier : “Not even HITLER tried to ban Scientology!” As Xavier takes a second to exclaim his point the woman manages to get a word in. “Scientology wasn’t invented until after Hitler di-” Xavier : “INVENTED?!?!?!?“ The CEO springs to his feet, his bright Green eyes wild and his hair flowing behind him. Xavier : “SO WHILE CHRISTIANITY WAS FOUNDED ON THE OH SO BELIEVABLE STORY OF A HIPPIE BEING NAILED TO A CROSS YOU BUY IT... BUT A FAITH BASED AROUND DIANETICS HAS TO BE INVENTED?!?!?!“ The woman now desperately tries to deflect his accusations but the words are simply not there, and now even Eleanor is weighing in. Eleanor : “You should... Like, have your own country called, like, NAZILAND or something.. Wait, isn’t that what Germany used to be called?” All the eyes on the room are now on the woman. Xavier : “I’m going to leave now, woman.. THAT MAKE YOU HAPPY? Driving the Scientologist from the room LIKE YOU WANT TO DRIVE THEM FROM THE PLANET!!! I’ll leave you to explain to everyone here by you’re bigoted views cost them this chance to ask their questions... Good day.” The temperamental CEO turns and walks out, Ellie~! looking to the woman and smirking. Eleanor : “Like, YEAH!” As she follows her husband out we watch them emerge the other side as the crowd in the room tut and aim their disapproving comments at the woman. Out of the room, Xavier suddenly look a lot less offended. Xavier : “Well my little Poon Puppy.... Mark that down as a successful way to get out of any future Peon Conferences!” He puts his arm around his wife and they stroll down the corridor, happy with their efforts. Eleanor : “Yup.. Like, YAY US!”
