A Scientologist's Dream Of The Future (Part Two)

20 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 20/03/02 Beep Beep Beep Beep Beep Xavier : “Display Sender....” The blind then flashes up 7 words which seem perfectly natural to the pair of Scientologists who are now both staring at the projection of the sender’s identity.... PRESIDENT OF THE FREE WORLD ELEANOR HALL..... He saw the words flashing up on the screen, and of course in this dream state, fully immersed in the environment he believed it. His valet, Ellie~! was now the ruler of the World that he and Scientology created. Ellie~!, who may have won the Homecoming Queen and Prom Queen elections....Winning the biggest election in the history of the World? Wonder how that could have happened.... Xavier : “Okay, open transmission....” The mid-40’s Kannon and the elderly John Travolta pull their seats around to look at the image projection on the screen that opens up to show Eleanor Hall, not looking a day different from when she was 23. Eleanor : “Um, Hello? This thing uses a laser-diamond delivery system it SO shouldn’t take that long!” We see Ellie~! sitting on a throne in a regal look court, just as one would expect her to have if she did, in any reality, reach her current station in life. She is wearing a skinny fit Pink t-shirt with “Heaven Sent“ written across the chest in White. With this she wears a pair of Blue flared jeans with Silver stars running down the legs. Her hair is still long and wavy, Brown with Blond highlights. Eleanor : “Well....What-EVER!.....Aren’t you two forgetting something?” Xavier and John look to each other and cringe before dropping to their knees, John a lot more slowly than Xavier to avoid any damage to his knees and they bow down to the screen 5 times in We are not Worthy! style. Xavier and John : “Oh great exalted one we are not worthy of your preppy bordering on godly presence....” As they reluctantly carry out the act, punishable by death due to treason laws if they don’t, Ellie~! grins on the screen, as she does every time her populous carry out the act in front of her. Eleanor : “I have like, NO, idea how I managed to pass that law but I am like, SO glad I did!” John and Xavier rise back up and drop back into their seats as Eleanor continues. Eleanor : “Any way, I’m bored so I want you to come over here....These Politicians are like, SO boring. All they do is talk about the country and how to make it better, taxes, services, budgets....YA-HAWN!!! Anyway, come over...” Xavier : “Well we were a bit busy, I just found out that the time I beat Big Alan Steele, over 20 years ago was in fact the pivotal moment I the history of the human species and we-” Suddenly a Purple light shines all around Xavier and John, flowing like raw electricity that encapsulates them and they gradually fade away. On the screen Eleanor waves their departure. Eleanor : “Glad you could make the time, see you in a few seconds!” She grins broadly and then ends the transmission that causes the blinds to shoot back up to the ceiling and the World outside is seen once again shining in through the window. “I REALLY wish you would stop doing that!!!” We cut to the room where we saw Ellie~! before and now Xavier and John are standing in front of her, Kannon not looking too happy at being dragged through space and time at Ellie~!’s beck and call. She just points to herself. Eleanor : “Um...Hello? Like, ruler of the Free World here!” A grumpy Xavier walks up to the Throne as Ellie~! descends from it, the Scientologists still seeming annoyed. Xavier : “Yeah, and don’t forget who rigged the election to get you voted in!” He greets her with a kiss and the Eleanor holds her arms out, gesturing to the vast expanse of the room that houses her throne. Eleanor : “So how do you like the room? I just had it redecorated....” Every wall houses a giant portrait if Eleanor in different clothes and striking different posed. The spaces of wall not covered with these giant paintings are smaller ones, all depicting Xavier’s Preppy ex-valet, done having the same clothes or pose, or even hairstyle for that matter. Eleanor : “Oh, and you HAVE to check out the ceiling!” All three of them look up to the ceiling to show a perfect replica of the ceiling is the Cistine Chapel, but something looks a little different somehow.... Xavier : “Did you replace all the Angels with pictures of you?” Taking a closer look at the masterpiece it is seen that all the Angels have indeed been replaces with images of Eleanor, a fitting tribute to her own self-loving. Xavier : “.....Nice...” As he gives a look of distaste to John, Ellie~! is already walking towards one of the adjacent rooms, gesturing for Kannon and Travolta to come and follow her. Eleanor : “Come on, I need your opinions on something....” Xavier and John follow her into a perfectly square room, the walls, floor and ceiling coloured in brilliant White and not a single piece of furniture to be seen. Eleanor : “Computer....Er...Do that Globe thing...” At her rather vague command the room comes to life and that are now imprisoned within a depiction of the World, all the countries and their new borders and names being shown. Their view is as if they are standing at the Earth’s core, so that they are at the centre of a bog sphere. Eleanor : “I just don’t think it looks right....I was thinking of maybe moving Norway to the Bahamas, I went there the other day and it was like, REALLY cold so then if I have to go there again it will be warm!” John and Xavier exchange another glance, their eyes saying everything for them... “Did we actually PUT her in charge of the World?” Xavier : “Um, Ellie~! my little Poon Puppy, I don’t think the people on Norway will be too happy about you moving their country, the people of Slovenia weren’t too happy when you moved to them to the moon!” Ellie~! doesn’t look to pleased at Xavier bringing up and obviously touchy subject. Eleanor : “Hey! I said that no way on Earth was their a country called Slovenia, I thought they were trying to trick me by making up places...So I moved them to the Moon I was right...yay me! Anyway, what is there for these Norwayinians or whatever they are called not to like?” Xavier : “Maybe the fact that their count- “WOULD YOU.....LIKE......STOP DOING THAT!!!!!!!” We are suddenly pulled from the Utopian dreamworld and back to Xavier’s bedroom where we see Ellie~! catch him with an elbow into the Scientologist’s ribs. Xavier : “OWWWWWWWWWWW!” He jolts up, clutching his ribs, grimacing through the pain. Xavier : “What was THAT for?” Ellie~! flicks the bedside lamp on, flooding the room in Orange light to compliment the early morning sun that is filtering through the blinds. Eleanor : “Okay, little Pop Quiz....When I g to bead is it for A....Sleep, or B....Do hear you muttering on all night about some girl named Utopia....You have a LOT of questions to answer! So who is she? Some little slut who’ll spread them quic-” The still groggy Xavier, while clutching his ribs and being forced further towards the edge of the bed by Ellie~! aggressive questioning drops off the side and down onto the floor with a thud. Xavier : “Ow....Ribs.....” Eleanor lies across the bed, her head hanging over the edge and staring right down at Xavier’s wincing Green eyes. Eleanor : “Well, who is she? Who is this Utopia bitch? Does she have better hair than me because I know it has been a bit dry as of late but it’s this new shampoo, I am going to stop usi-” Xavier : “UTOPIA IS NOT A WOMAN!!!” Kannon shouts up from the floor in answer to Ellie~!’s questioning and despite the angry tone in which he replies she seems pleased with the answer. Eleanor : “Oh...Good...And about my hair, it isn’t really dry, it’s just the water content in the air surrounding it that makes it seem that way...For the record...” She rolls back onto the bed and pulls the covers back over her, leaving Xavier flat on his back on the bedroom floor. Xavier : “Actually I was having this amazing dream of a future society where Scientology’s beliefs had been translated into everyday life and everyone was living in perfect harmony....” BOR-RIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIING! Ellie~!’s voice is heard back up on the bed followed by the rustling of the sheets as she tries to get back to sleep. Xavier : “.....And in this world you were ruler of all the free world and had your own throne and a big room with pictures of you everywhere, it turned out me beating Big Alan Steele this week kicked off this whole new reality...” Another rustling is then heard as Ellie~!’s head pokes over the edge of the bed eagerly. Eleanor : “What? Then why are you sleeping, you should be TRAINING! GO TRAIN!.........Don’t just lay there!!!” Kannon tries to pull himself up, favouring his left side where Eleanor’s elbow dug in. Xavier : “It was just a dream, it won’t come true....Well, me beating Steele will but I’m not sure about the rest....” Ellie~! keeps staring down off the side of the bed at him, a bemused look in her eyes. Eleanor : “Why are you still there? GO TRAIN! TRAIN!!!” As Kannon gets to his feet Ellie~! slides out of bed and ushers him towards the bedroom door. Eleanor : “Go......Train....Make me President....Go...” The Scientologist sighs as he is forced out of the door and Ellie~! shuts it behind him and laps onto the bed as the shot fades to black. Eleanor : “Wow....I am like, SO going to rule the World!”
