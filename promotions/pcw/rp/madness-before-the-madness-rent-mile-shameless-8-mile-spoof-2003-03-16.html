MADNESS BEFORE THE MADNESS-Rent Mile.....shameless 8 Mile spoof...lose yourself...in the RP that is

16 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Title: MADNESS BEFORE THE MADNESS-Rent Mile.....shameless 8 Mile spoof...lose yourself...in the RP that is Featuring: Rent-a-Hero Date: 3-16-2003 ...::/:::..Our story begins in a dark and dingy bathroom in an unknown building, the sound of water dripping from a rusted faucet into a blackened sink only being overshadowed by the rhythmic tap of feet against the linoleum floor. The mirror above the sink is smeared with finger prints and streaks, disappearing and reappearing in the light as a face can be seen staring directly into it’s reflective surface…that face belonging to Rent-A-Hero. His aqua blue eyes pierce his own reflection, a straight and serious demeanor overcoming him as he continues to bounce about in rhythm, the type of bounce that would make a boxer jealous. Our hero fixes his Flash mask to his face, his hands once again gripping the edges of the sink as his bounce continues in step, unaltered by anything. As he continues to stare at himself in the mirror, the bathroom door opens as a young man with a scruffy red beard pokes his head through. He looks apprehensive at the thought of disturbing our hero, but works up the nerve to as he says..::/::.. Man:”They’re calling your name…” ..::/::..Our hero continues to stare at his own reflection, his fight dance finally derailed as he asks in a simple tone..::/::.. Rent-A-Hero:”Really?” Man:”Well…they’re saying “KILL THE WHITE BOY” and “BRING HIS BITCH ASS OUT”…but your name fell in there somewhere too.” Rent-A-Hero:”Groovy…” ..::/::..Our hero continues to bounce in place, left right, left right. He closes his eyes as he continues the steps repeatedly, finding solace in them as the bearded youth continues to keep an eye on him. He then asks..::/::.. Man:”Yo dude…why are you bouncing around like that?” ..::/::..Our hero stops, turning about face as he applies a skull cap over his honey blonde hair, looking to the bearded youth and replying..::/::.. Rent-A-Hero:”Come on man, Eminem did it in “8 Mile”…I mean this IS a parody, duh!” –looking to the audience- “RP extra’s…no sense at all.” ..::/::..The man moves out of the way while opening the door wide for our hero, who walks through it and down a dark hallway. The only light visible is a small crack of it far down the hall, which our hero is headed towards while punching at the air….preparing for what he has to face when he leaves the hallway. The sound of rabid cheering can be heard behind the door as he continues his journey, which finally ends as he feels a cold metal door. Taking in a deep, our hero pushes the door wide open…the hallway FLOODING with light as our hero now finds himself upon a stage, hot lights shining down upon him and a sea of young, black hip-hoppers hoping and hollering non-stop. On stage is a man with thick dreadlocks, hyping up the crowd via the house mic as he exclaims..::/::..Dreadhead:”YO YO YO, YA’LL READY TO SEE SOME CRUCIAL FREESTYLE BATTLES TONIGHT?!” Fans:”HELL YEAH!!! ..::/::..The dreadhead turns to our hero, smiling now that he has made his presence on stage as he motions him to the center. Our hero receives weird and confused looks from the crowd as he takes small steps to the center, our hero taking notice of the four men who jumped him in the last installment sitting comfortably on a house speaker as they shoot cocky smiles to the spandex enwrapped superhero..::/::.. Dreadhead:”YO YO YO, it’s time for tonight’s MAIN EVENT…the Freestyle Handicap match where Da City Hustlers….comprised of my man, Lysol, and the Freestyle World Champio, FRONTS!”..::/::..The predominately black crowd roars for the intro of the four men who assaulted our hero, the bald headed thug stepping forward along with the champion, Fronts, his teeth gleaming as the house lights strike them. The dreadhead then looks to our hero, putting an arm around him as he exclaims..::/::.. Dreadhead:”AND THEIR OPPONENT….FROM WAY OUT HERE IN CRACKA’ COUNTRY….MY BOY… -covers the mic as he whispers in our hero’s ear- …What’s ya name man?” Rent-A-Hero:”How can you NOT know my name? I’m RENT-A-HERO…PCW Crusierweight CHAMPION! The ONLY PCW SUPERHERO! I come on NBC ALL the time on Tuesday’s and Friday’s guy!”Dreadhead:”Oh for reals…aight man… -uncovers mic- ….THEIR OPPONENT….MY DAWG…SPANDEX!! ..::/::..The fans boo, our hero shaking his head in shame at the name bestowed upon him as the bald headed thug known as Lysol steps up, grinning as he looks to our hero, seeing no challenge whatsoever…::/::.. Dreadhead:”AIGHT AIGHT…LYSOL IS GONNA START THIS FREESTYLE BATTLE OFF, THEN MY MAN SPANDEX IS GONA COME BACK…THEN YOU GUYS’LL BE DA’ JUDGES! If Spandex wins…he’ll face the champ, Fronts! NOW LET’S GET IT ON!!”..::/::..The dreadhead steps back, a fast rap beat overtaking the speakers as Lysol tucks one hand into his FUBU jeans while holding the mic with the other, nodding his head to the beat as Rent-A-Hero stands in expectance of his verbal assault. Finally, Lysol opens his mouth, angrily rapping at our hero..::/::.. Lysol:”I can’t believe this….who pulled this used condom out da’ trash, I’m bout to smash ya, roll ya, and toke ya like some Jamaican hash, Where you get off thinking you some kinda comic book superhero, Take the “E” and “R” away and your just that…..a SUPER-HO,And me being a pimp, I keep my ho’s in line with a smack, I’ll rip open ya stomach and hang ya’ with your intestines askin’ “How’d ya do that?” Lysol’ll disinfect ya, beat ya down and have ya crying for momma like your sick, If your looking for her though, check my house…SHE’S ON MY DICK!!”Crowd:”OOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO!!!” ..::/::..The crowd is solidly behind Lysol’s verbal destruction of Rent-A-Hero, Lysol bowing for the crowd as if the battle has already been won as the dreadhead attempts to calm the crowd down. As Lysol slaps hands with Fronts, the dreadhead exclaims..::/::.. Draedhead:”AIGHT AIGHT, THAT WAS TIGHT….now let’s see what we got from my man…SPANDEX!! KICK A BEAT!”..::/::..The crowd grows quiet, Lysol folding his arms awaiting our hero to make a rebuttal. Our hero looks lost for words with the mic in hand, sweat beading down his forehead as he looks out to the rabid crowd, already booing him before he can get a word out. The dreadhead moves to our hero, whispering in his ear..::/::.. Dreadhead:”Dawg…just say what you gotta say. When you spit, it won’t matter WHAT color you are.” ..::/::..The sound of a record stopping overtakes the arena, our hero shooting angry eyes at the dreadhead as he exclaims..::/::.. Rent-A-Hero:”DON’T YOU EVER CALL ME WHITE AGAIN…I’M KRYPTONIAN FOOL!! Thanks a lot for ruining my dramatic effect wise-guy!” ..::/::..The dreadhead backs up with his head hanging low, the rap beat starting up again as our hero nods his head to the beat, putting the mic to his lips as he flows back on Lysol..::/::..Rent-A-Hero:”HOLY FLOWMASTERS YA’LL, I can’t believe I’m fighting a villain called “LYSOL”?!, What kind of name is that for a rapper, do you have any creativity at all,You best to go back home, or get the VERBAL BIZNITCH SLAP O’ DOOM~! , When I hit you, graphics appear like “WHAM”, “ZAP”, and “BOOM”!, Your rhymes are ditzy and dumb like a promo from Ellie~!,You make me wanna say “LOL” more times then a Statuz Quo RP, I’m Violent like Jack, I sparkle like Tempest, you can’t oppress me like Kannon, and I’m more Tubular then Tomm-ay, I don’t need all my Rent-A-Powers to beat you…I’ll let you sit and collect dust like a RP in the PWA, I’m meaner then ‘Streak, more shady then Phantom Booker, about as big as Alan Steele, and I’m more classic then Vendetta,Don’t even TRY to switch this Rent-A-Channel….there’s no superhero who can flow betta,So be gone, your banned from flowing like a spammer on EW 24-7 forums, You’re a Lysol-dactyl…you just got ATE by a Rent-A-Sauraus!! ..::/::..The crowd is in utter shock…amazed by the flow of our hero as they roar loudly for his comeback, Lysol looking pissed off as he folds his arms tight..::/::.. Dreadhead:”WHO YA’LL FEELIN IN THIS BATTLE?!”Fans:”SPANDEX!!! SPANDEX!!! SPANDEX!!!”Dreadhead:”SPANDEX MOVES TO THE NEXT ROUND…TO FACE FRONTS!!”..::/::..The fans roar, Lysol being scolded heavily by his peers as Fronts locks eyes with out hero…the two set to do battle for the Freestyle championship as the fans stand solidly behind our hero, continuing their “spandex” chant as the scene freeze….”to be continued” shown at the bottom of the screen..::/::..WILL RENT-A-HERO BEAT FRONTS AND BECOME A FREESTYLE CHAMPION?! WILL OUR HERO QUIT GETTING IN DUMB SITUATIONS AND REACH MARCH MADNESS?! DO YOU THINK HE CAN EGT A RECORD DEAL?! HOW SHAMELESS IS THIS OF AN 8 MILE PARODY?!
