Club Preston (Pt. 1)

10 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6.10.03 The Cloud Nine air ship is the home of careless attitudes as its passengers hover thousands of feet from the ground in the confines of the most luxurious craft, possibly, on the face of the earth. This feeling of little worries can finally be adopted by Tyler Preston now that things are squared away with his girlfriend. She was the only issue that nagged in the back of his mind, not allowing him to have the stress free, fun time that he had earned after winning and defending the PCW Tag Team Titles with his friend John Gargano. Sure, anyone can get caught up in the sport of wrestling, but Tyler exhibits more of a human attitude by caring about things away from the ring. He knows that true happiness does not lie within the gold of the title belts, but in his own home with his family and friends. Fortunately for him, that issue was put to rest after Kalli came by to see him off as he boarded the Cloud Nine air ship. With little weight holding him down, Tyler is now free to do as he pleases and concentrate a little bit on his wrestling career. Tyler walks down the narrow corridor of the hall ways aboard the Cloud Nine air ship. He is on the third floor, attempting to locate his room that he was no very informed upon. As he passes each set of cream colored doors, he glances at each side. The golden numbers on the door mean nothing to him. Fortunately, there are name tags on each of the doors. He uses these white, laminated pieces of paper as his guide. He slowly paces down the brightly lit hall. His jeans rub together to create the only sound at that time. He passes another set of doors. To his right the name tag reads ‘System of the Down’. Tyler cringes, not being a very big fan of their political nonsense they call music. To the other side the white name tag boasts ‘Justice Inc: Warrior’. Tyler rolls his eyes and continues on his way. He shoves his hands in his pockets and attempts to look casual during his bout of confusion. Finally, after about six set of cream doors, he arrives at the one that reads Tyler Preston. He smiles, finally gaining conformation that he does belong on the ship. ‘Do you think they have me far enough down the hall? Goodness, I’m going to have to run a marathon every time I want to do something on this damn ship,’ his mind complains although his face still harbors a smile over his success of locating his room. He shoves the firm, plastic key card in to the golden slot. The door clicks and a green light illuminates on the handle. He tugs the long brass handle downward and shoves the door open. He walks through the door frame as his brown eyes try to adjust to the extreme darkness of the room. Nothing can be made out; just simple shapes of the essential elements such as his bed and couch. He rubs the left wall in attempts to find a light switch, but the only causes a soothing sound of his hand rubbing against the dry wall. ‘This place has the greatest technologies and they don’t even know where to put a light switch. Where the hell is the thing?’ His mind growls, gaining more agitation as his misfortunes continue. He continues to rub the wall as he walks further and further in to the room. He finally reaches the center of the room and feels a steel string dangle against his sweaty forehead. He grabs it and gives it a tug. A lamp that is attached to the ceiling lights up the entire room. Tyler smiles, but only for a moment. He notices he has been stuck with the ‘bare essentials’ type of room. A bed, bathroom, and mirror are the center pieces of these dull quarters. Tyler rolls his big brown eyes up to the ceiling in dismay. The only bright spot in this moment is that he locates his luggage, piled in the corner. At least he will have some means of entertainment with the things that he has brought. ‘I’m stuck with this jail cell while Matt Jackson is probably chilling in his room with all the extras. Hell, I wouldn’t even be surprised if he had a sauna in his room. I don’t really understand why this jerk gets all the breaks. Wow, one of the rising stars of the PCW, what does that even mean? Everyone is a rising star at once, it’s nothing special,’ his mind wanders off. He shakes the agitating thoughts from his head and approaches his large stack of luggage. ‘Don’t worry about it here Tyler; you have worked through adversity before. This could be a good thing. It looks like I’ll just have to do some redecorating of my own. John will definitely want in on this. Once I get done with this room, it is going to be the greatest guest room on this ship,’ he thinks for more of an optimistic view. His eyes move back and forth around the room to visualize the modifications that he will do to it. A smile spreads across his face as a great idea pops in to his head. An evil laugh even escapes from his semi-opened lips. ‘If they’re going to stick me in a room with barely anything in it, then I’m going to fill this place up. Once I get one designing this place, it will be ‘Club Preston’. That’s just what this air ship needs, a comedy, slash, dance club. Sometime my ideas are so good they even scare me,’ Tyler’s mind rambles on as he giggles like a small school child. He unbuttons his white dress shirt to reveal another, plain white T-shirt. He does this to cool off as he begins planning the basic blue prints for ‘Club Preston’. Tyler walks over to the mahogany night stand that holds up a small porcelain lamp. To the right of the lamp sits a small telephone unit. Tyler dashes to the phone and presses just one of the buttons. He holds the ear piece to his ear, and listens through the tufts of blonde hair that have drooped over his ear. Silence takes over, but only for a minute until Tyler barks out his orders through the phone. He lies on the bed as he sorts through everything he needs. “This is room service correct? Good! This is Tyler Preston, PCW’s very own. I just got settled in my room and it appears that there are a few things missing. I know I didn’t have any formal orders printed out for you guys, but now I’m going to need a few things,” Tyler chirps in to the phone with an enthusiastic tone of voice. The person on the other end may not be taking him seriously. Tyler sits in silence, listening to an explanation from the other end. “I know it’s only the first day here but I just won’t be able to get settled in unless I get a few things to my room immediately. Please have the bell hop bring up fifty folding chairs, a large red curtain, a fog machine, twenty twelve watt light bulbs, and of course two more bath robes,” Tyler says, as if those were fairly standard things needed by everyone staying aboard the ship. Tyler almost laughs as the person on the other end voices their disapproval at how impossible of a task this is. He puts his hand behind his head and stares up at the ceiling, tuning out the voice of the person. “I’m sure this wouldn’t be a problem for Matt Jackson or Silvio Rochelli. I know that all that stuff is available. Listen, if you just get the guy to bring it up here, there will be a little something extra for you both on the side. I’m really counting on you, so please don’t let me down. I’ll be waiting here,” Tyler barks out. Just as he finishes, he slams the phone back on to the unit. The unit rings because of the bell inside. Tyler shakes his head, hoping that he got his message through to the Cloud Nine staffing. He digs his fingers into his black dress shoes and pries them off his sweaty feet. ‘I bet they would bring all that stuff to Silvio in a second. I can’t believe this. What ever happened to the days of seniority? Those have obviously gone out the window. Now this new punk runs in to the PCW and gets treated than I ever will in my career. Oh well, this is the life I chose. I am more than used to the fickle ways of people. I’ve seen it one to many times in my comedy career,’ his mind whines, never seeming to say anything in the least bit optimistic. ‘I never thought this could be so, but oddly enough, I feel closeness with the one that they call Warrior. After all, we share the common bond of the ‘elite’ athletes counting us out of the match. We’re in the match to! We pose a threat! Ah…forget it. There are a lot more important things to think about then that stupid match that will mean nothing. So Matt and Silvio can have fun dueling it out against each other to win a match that means jack shit,’ his mind finally concludes so that the issue will not press at his mind the entire night. Nothing stresses Tyler out more than lingering thoughts of something that bugs him. After shedding his stuffy footwear, he makes a beeline for his large duffel bag. He unzips the zipper in a hurry and pulls out a medium sized silver boom box. He holds it up to his mouth and blows firmly on the front panel of it to rid the buttons and gauges of any dust that may have clung to it. The front panel is absolutely filled with buttons, levers, and gadgets. This is obviously the state of the art technology. It’s silver coating gives it somewhat of a ‘space age’ feel to it. Tyler grabs the loose black cord that dangles from the end of it, and crams it home inside the electrical outlet to the left of the nightstand. ‘There is nothing like a little music to get me in the re-decorating mood. It gives it kind of that ‘Trading Spaces’ feel to it,’ his minds jokes to himself as he recalls one of his favorite interior decorating shows on television. He flips one of the levers up and hits the play button. The sound of a CD spinning on the inside takes over as the machine starts up. The scratching sound is soon replaced with the heavy rock tunes that are arranged on his custom compact disc. Tyler adjusts the volume a little bit louder, and does some form of a dance. He nods his head with the heavy bass drum beat as he unpacks the rest of his stuff. 'It's about time to start making this ride enjoyable. I'm not going to let a couple of arrogant punks ruin my time. It's time rock out,' his mind speaks to him in his energetic frenzy. He goes about his unpacking with a relaxed, 'rocking out' look on his face. The scene fades out of the room that will soon be transformed into the greatest creation on the Cloud Nine air ship. 'Club Preston' is beginning to form.
