Makonie/Green Syndrome: Symptoms include low intelligence, being naive and being all round bitches

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 13 March &#039;03 Chet King and his manager Cindy Bi are standing around drinking coffee out of white plastic cups in what looks to be some sort of filming studio with cameras set up filming what looks to be some sort of dodgy B-grade science fiction, 70’s flashback, porno. Cindy Bi: It’s worked perfectly Chet. You’ve had a poor performance on the wrestling side of things as of late, and it looked like you were going to have little or no attention at all going for you going in to March Madness. Chet King: Hey! I haven’t been wrestling that badly. Cindy Bi: Yes you have. You lost to Violence Jack on Fusion. That’s pathetic. But I’m sure March Madness you’ll change it all by taking the world title. But nonetheless if it wasn’t for those two promos you did you wouldn’t have any heat going into Madness. It’s just lucky that you’ve always been talented on the mic. Heh, I guess that’s why you’re a chat show host after all. Chet King: Well I have to admit that Status Quo taught me a few things. I just followed his format of spitting out a few insults and the heat came straight to me. It’s just too bad that the reply’s that came were uneducated, naïve and as much of a joke as Skylar’s career. Cindy Bi: Are you going to go in to the room, and do a promo replying to them? Chet King: I’m going to go in there and reply to them this promo. Cindy Bi: What are you talking about Chet? We’re not doing a promo at the moment. We’re just chatting. There’s been no mention of a camera filming us yet. Chet King: What the fuck are you talking about? People are sitting at home watching us talk right now. Cindy Bi: They are? I’ve been under the impression that we have adventures that change issues dealing with us and others around us, but no one at home sees these little episodes, yet they still accept our character development. I’ve also been under the impression that we tell stories about our past when we went to Mexico just for the sake of telling the story to no one in particular. I didn’t realize that people at home heard us telling the stories, I thought we were just telling the stories to ourselves for fun. Chet King: I see you’ve been suffering from the syndrome more commonly known as Green/Makonie Naïveness. Cindy Bi: Indeed I have. How can I ever be cured? Chet King: Just take yourself out of this promo, and trust me we are currently in a promo because people at home can see us. Just take yourself out of this promo and go to a TV, turn the TV on and watch while I go to studio B to address a hardcore coffee spiling Neo Nazi and a guy who has a delusion that his career means something. Cindy Bi: Thanks Chet! Thanks for making me wake up to the fact that the reasons we have our sometimes wacky adventures and tell stories about our past when we went to Mexico to no one in particular isn’t just for no reason. It’s because we’re doing promos! Cindy, obviously using complete sarcasm this entire time exits the studio and goes to look for a TV while Chet also leaves the studio and heads to a door marked ‘Studio B’. Once inside we see a chair and a camera set up on a tripod in the middle of the room. Chet takes a seat on the chair while brushing against the grey wall. The view then switches from the camera which we haven’t seen (and which we don’t need to see to realize that a promo has been going on) to the camera on the tripod. Chet King: Mark, Skylar, I’m sure you already have seen what I had to say about your naïve opinions of promos. Or maybe you didn’t, because you thought that since I, or Cindy, or anyone else never personally addressed the viewers we weren’t doing a promo. You just thought that you some how magically saw what we said. I guess that’s what happens when you suffer from Skylar/Makonie syndrome. Skylar, I listened to your promo of what Mark must be thinking. I must commend you on the good job whoever played Mark’s part did. I also would like to take a quote from them: And I'm not on-camera. Never have been. But that doesn't matter, Chet King will probably hear about this and quote me word for word, despite the fact that this is not a promo, I'm merely sitting on my porch talking to you, Jax. Well whether you believe it or not, I did hear every word that was said. And I’m not the only one, because if I can quote our good coffee spilling friend Mark Makonie: I am going to say the same thing as Skylar said…stay the FUCK out of my life CHET KING! You see Skylar? Even Mark saw your promo! Now that it’s clear that you do do promos, and I see them on TV, let me talk about these accusations that I didn’t realize that Makonie telling his little, and may I say enthralling, coffee spilling story and Skylar’s telling of his history all happened in the past. Of course I fucking knew you, I’m not the idiot here who doesn’t realize that some of the things they do are shown as promos on TV. As you said yourself, how would I know what happened in your past? I wasn’t there. I only knew about it once I saw your promos come on to the TV, with the words ‘5 Weeks Ago’ or whatever it is used to make sure the viewer knows that it’s in the past. I know that since that time Sandy Wong has died, the FBI is off Skylar’s case and other events have taken place. I’m not a fucking idiot. However, the footage shown on your promo was filmed at a time when Sandy was alive and the FBI was after Skylar’s ass because they wanted to put him away for having such a joke of a career that it’s a crime. Therefore discussing what went down in your promo, no matter when it was filmed, is still relevant. I know that I’ve repeated the fact that what you do are promos, and that I realize that some things shown happened in the past, over and over, so much so that it’s probably overwhelmed a lot of people, but I’m sure the Skylar and Mark still don’t understand. I guess there’s just not much you can do educated a man who thinks being the tallest cruiserweight is an achievements, and a man who thinks spilling coffee is hardcore. So I think I’ll just mention a couple thinks that they might be able to follow. Was I forced to watch Skylar’s promo by Skylar? Uhh, of course not. What I meant is that I force myself to watch every promo by everyone in PCW to see if there’s anything important going on. I was merely complaining about having to sit through Skylar’s career history, or ‘joke’ as it shall be now officially known as, to wait for something important to come on. Do I wank off to every mention of me as the actor playing Mark in Skylar’s promo suggested? I’ll answer that by saying that if I did, my cum supply’s would be far beyond depleted, as it’s so easy to get a rise out of you guys. But let me assure the women out there, I’m still loaded! Mark, you’re trying to build up suspense for your attack on the Mexicans? What the fuck? Do you think you’re in a James Bond movie? If you do, then you should know your place. The only role you could play in a Bond movie is the Bond girl. Bitches, I guess I’ll see you at Madness. And by the way, I hope this promo has helped cure at least a few people of Green/Makonie syndrome.
