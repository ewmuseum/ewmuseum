The Hell that is Boda-Land - Part Three - Radio Bombs, The Sound of Freedom, the Army of Immigrants and the Final Laugh? (High Impact nostalgia)

7 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7th April OOC: This is the final Roleplay that I’m writing for the Pay-Per-View. Over this past week, I’ve noticed a large change in the Pay-Per-View Rping trend…where most people seem to be writing quality Roleplays yet only writing 5-6 of them. For that, it’s hats off to those people, the last few days I’ve been continually amazed by the stuff being written by the likes of Jeremy Howard, Xavier Kannon, DarkStar, Boda, Miya etc…plus a load more. Well done guys. Now then, as for what MGB song to theme this last RP on…I pick ‘Radio Bomb’ off their Indie release “Last of the Ghetto Astronauts” and ‘Deep Six’ off the Underdogs Album, both being particularly catchy songs. Enjoy this, my longest RP for quite a while...7 pages Size 10 font, 10 pages size 12 font, 3468 words, 20K, 430 lines. The Chris Chase Continued Chronicles – Book One: In the Council of the Immortals…… RP#6 for Spring Fling… Location: The Central Core of Boda-Land, in the deep depths of Chris Chase’s sleeping mind, in a secret Pre-Pay-Per-View resting area, (Insert PPV location here). Time: Approximately 13:00 Sunday 7th April. Chapter Nine: The Hell that is Boda-Land – Part Three (Of Whatever :-P) – Radio Bombs, The Sound of Freedom, the Army of Immigrants and the Final Laugh? (High Impact nostalgia) Boda…Miyagawa… Do you two really remember what I was like in PCW2? What I was capable of back then? So I’m not as decorated as either of you, I’ve been lying on my back for the majority of my first year, and neither of you have bothered to pick up on that. Sheesh…and I thought veterans were wiser than rookies like yours truly could ever be… Back in PCW2, I was the real future…ahead of my time, WAY ahead of my time...a YEAR ahead of my time. Let’s face it, I was predestined to become PCW2 World Champion…but I screwed up…I made a rookie mistake. Everyone makes those kinds of mistakes…even you. Now, I’m living out what I could have been…I’m back, I’ve returned from the dead a million times…I’ve been crucified, I’ve been mocked…I’ve even been accused of batting for the wrong team… But, what has that proven? That I can come back from the dead quicker than you can think of another lame, half-witted insult? Today guys, Today is my day…the day in which I finally top that ladder…Today --- DAMNIT Dustin! I’m trying to talk here! Yes, of course we can start the promo now…have you been on the Nyquil again? THIS IS THE PROMO! Look, will you stop leaping around like a freaking idiot…and at least wait until I’ve finished? Thank you… *ahem* Today…Boda…Miyagawa…I’ll kill all your hopes of another World Championship reign…I’ll bring back the originality, the creative flair needed for this federation to hurtle skyward at great velocity… Now then…let’s finish off what I started… RADIO FIZZ… “There they are! STOP! STOP IN THE NAME OF BODA! Jeez…why don’t they ever stop? … Calling in all reinforcements…calling in all reinforcements. We’ve got a code 010179(#) on our hands…” ::. ~(Voice of Chris Chase)~ Good Morning, Good Evening and Goodnight. Everyone is made of Polyurethane…all these expendable jobbers in the quest for the ultimate prize, the PCW World Title. This whole week, I’ve been exploring the idea of if I didn’t exist…what’d the World would be like and so forth. And I’ve come to this conclusion: It’d suck more than Boda and a roomful of suckling children. A world without Chris Chase simply wouldn’t be a world at all…it’d be a vile, desolate place ruled by Boda and his little enemy of a lackey by the name of Miyagawa. Boda wouldn’t have me to rip off…and he’d simply instead feed off those helpless citizens of the world, satisfying his own greed. In the name of all that is good and awesome, Chris Chase isn’t going to let that happen ever. I’m not going to rest until these cretins are nothing but memories…Miya less so…but anyone who gets their facts incredibly screwed up is worth a decent punking out in any case. Now, Boda’s dream regime is crumbling…under pressure from a new, fresh and exciting figure. A sleeper hit…the underdog. I mean, who other than myself, Dustin and other Chase fans would predict me to win this match tonight? Exactly… The youthful new competitor, against the tired old cretins of old. The resourceful brash hero against a father and a soon-to-be father. The rational thinking, brave saviour against odds that he alone can’t exactly comprehend. Boy, is it good to be the Underdog or what? I mean, come on…it’s easy for all these people to buy what I’m saying…since I’m the guy they’d least expect to make the win…to become the champion for the first time. I wouldn’t be expected to pop my PCW World Title cherry on my first full-on PCW World Title match would I? Of course not…that’s my whole point in this matter. Come on guys, look at me, I’m young…can’t you follow your nature as dirty old men and exploit the advantage – In a non-sexual way you freaking perverts. Sheesh, all I’ve heard from you two all this week is how much of a “Homosexual” I am. And all I’ve done is take little notice of such false accusations, and responded with the venom expected of a future great like me… Boda-Land and its owners never knew what hit it. Within a week, I had penetrated the central core, which is a good few days walk from the outside border. One of three operatives, I slipped in unnoticed…rather like Boda into his wife if you know what I mean. Heck, I managed it, even with the most loud of morons, ‘Deadly’ Dustin Downey. ‘Deadly’? Well, he did manage to disable two waves of security by accident…and ‘Deadly’ is a tiny bit less of a derogatory term than ‘Dipshit’, don’t you think? It’s past high noon…the time for great showdowns is past…or is it? The outer section of the central core, which is essentially a castle by any means, is coated in a thick residue of neo-modern style. Sliding doors, android agents controlled by the central hub of the BAMIA##. So far, so good…Chris Andrews, Dustin Downey and myself have managed to evade the attentions of any of the security protocols…but knowing my luck…my opponent is expecting me. Somewhere deep in the center of Boda-Land…a large shadowy figure sits at a console, with a small little minion beside him….:: Large Shadowy Figure: Ah, I was expecting this…isn’t that right Miyanion? Didn’t I tell you that the real Chase would eventually show up to destroy all my devious plans to rule the world…damn that Chase and whatever it is that makes him talented… Miyanion: Meh…says you, grand overlord of Boda-Land. In fact, the prophecies were correct…not you, you golddigging retard! If they’re telling the truth…and they’re poor liars at that…then Chris Chase will overthrow you come the end of the day. And besides, if you hit my wife again, I’ll…I’ll… ::. The large shadowy figure snorts and lays out Miyanion, sending the small man flying onto his back with a swift smack, before chuckling maniacally in the face of his surly employee. He points up at the Close Circuit television monitor with his right hand, as Miyanion pulls himself to his feet….:: Large Shadowy figure: You’ll do what exactly? Kill me? HA! Not even Chris Chase can defeat me…he’s nothing more than living fiction, he’s a spot on the shirt of evil. And besides Miyanion, you’ve in fact got a spot on your shirt… ::.Miyanion scrambles around, rubbing down is shirt, looking keenly for the spot on his shirt, as the figure winds up again….:: Miyanion: Where? SMACK!! Large Shadowy figure: That’s for looking at me like that. Now then, I’ll send for the Immigrant army to “Chase” down and fuck over this trio…therefore eradicating any chance of them dethroning me. I mean, it’s what the real Boda would do, isn’t it? Attack people before they have a decent chance of beating you…huh? ::.The Large shadowy figure turns away from his console, so that only his hands can be seen in the pale light. He rubs them together, laughing maniacally again as Miyanion frowns up at his overlord. In the background, on one of the monitors, a familiar face can be seen…not that of mine, or my likeness in this infernal world. But that of Downey. Hands wave and contort in derogatory ways as the overlord rubs his fans…delivering a typical plot to his cynical minion….:: Dustin: ~(In the distance)~ YOO HOO! Hello? Is any evil dude there? PSST…this is your captain speaking…warp speed, factor 18 on my mark! WHOOOOOOOO! HYPER SPEED! Miyanion: Sir…I think I hear morons calling… Large Shadowy Figure: Meh…shut up then Miyanion! Sheesh, you always have to ruin my days of great tyranny. Muahahahahahaha! Chris Chase thinks he can thwart me, just like he thinks he can thwart the real Boda. Ha! Well…that’s simply not going to happen…after all, I am the gimmickless being, an evil…massive beast capable of destroying any Cruiser-Heavyweight. After all…I’ve got a particularly devilish weapon in my corner…the BODA-BOMB!! MUAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHA--- ::. Of course, this can be noted as a moment when annoying sidekicks cut in with the most moronic of interjections. Instead, Miyanion whips out a clipboard and shakes his head reaching out to console his rather delusional boss….:: Miyanion: I’m sorry sir, we can’t call our weapon that. I’m very sorry, I tried to purchase the rights to it over the internet, but some bastard called Healy stole it from us. So now, it’s copyrighted and every time that you say it, we have to pay $500,000 Canadian in royalty fees. So thus, we had to come up with some alternatives. Our R&D specialists shortlisted a dozen names for the move, such as the “Boca-Bomb”, the “Yoda-Bomb”, the “Boda-Bastard-Bomb”…most of which were derogatory due to your tyranny sir… Large Shadowy Figure: Well…what do we have then that isn’t derogatory? Something particularly Boda-y, yes? Miyanion: Radio Bomb, sir… Large Shadowy Figure: ~(sighs)~ Radio-Bomb?!? Well…it’ll have to do, after all, we can’t have a weapon that can be mocked by our most feared of enemies, can we? Meh, do we have a report from our field officer? ::.Miyanion nods….:: Miyanion: Yep…and it’s not good sir…apparently Chase and his company managed to slip past the BAMIA agents, and he’s now headed this way. But, just in case, I sent the ~(Crash of Lightning)~ Army of Immigrant midgets to intercept them…they’ll never get the chance to overthrow us sir… Large Shadowy Figure: At last, I’ll crush all remnants of the Chris Chase that prophecies have warned me about. This day might actually turn out to be good after all, Miyanion. Care to laugh maniacally with me? After all, the day is young…and this can really clean the vocal cords… Miyanion: Why not? It’s an appropriately evil thing to do at times like this, don’t you think, you frickin’ idiotic excuse for a tyrant? Both: DYAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHA…HAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHA… DYAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHA! Meh…that’s enough… ::.Meanwhile… Another faceless, featureless hallway…the hundreth hallway that I’ve passed in the past seven hours. It’s like beating another faceless jobber on a weekly show, it gets tiresome after all. It’s no longer a challenge, it’s boring and tedious to boot. I smirk as we walk down the corridor, continually plagued by the concern that we might be jumped by yet another ineffectual attack…as can be seen by the long trail of robot parts that extends most of the way back along the hallway. To my right, is the usual right hand man of Dustin Downey, my former PCW2 and early PCW Tag Team partner in the stable High Impact. In fact, back then in PCW2, we were less of a tag team and more of a pair of singles wrestlers…trying to fight against whatever nimrods decided that they’d be unentertaining and boring. Back then, it was Boda and his family. It’s ironic that Dustin and I have to team up in the dream world to take on Boda and Miyagawa… To my left is my dream world equivalent, Chris Andrews. Introduced in the first encounter of Boda-Land back a week and a half ago, Chris Andrews is what would have resulted had my birth parents not died in a car crash. I would have grown up English, and not Canadian…and I wouldn’t have developed my passion for Professional wrestling…or my undoubted talent in that field. See Boda I said talent…in that field…hence that I’m talented at professional wrestling…MORON! There is a low rumbling sound as we come to a generic sliding door, like the thousands that we’ve passed on our way into the center of this mile-wide maze. As the door slides open, we’re greeted to a room filled with a host of soldiers…most of which being immigrant midgets, standing in front of a screen as if they were extras in Metal Gear Solid two….:: Dustin: FUCK! Look at all these freaking immigrant midgets…this is like Dustin-Hell! I tell you Chris and Chris, we should turn back right now…look, there’s the door…I’m going. GOODBYE! ::. Dustin starts to walk off…mumbling to himself, but is caught by both arms by both Andrews and myself…and we drag him behind a crate. He struggles for a minute, but eventually gives up as soon as he finds that he’s safe from the horde of a hundred immigrants. He looks at us with a puzzled look, sure that he could have escaped from this room….:: Dustin: Why’d you have to go and do that for, huh? What have I done to deserve being decapitated by a bunch of frickin’ midgets. You both should know my hatred for midgets…those freaking gopher like creatures. The only thing is that they’re nothing like gophers… ::. Andrews and I slap our foreheads at this, simultaneously…sighing with much exasperation as Dustin continues to whine. Andrews pulls himself up, frowns and says something that he might live to regret – which seems to be the trend with clones of yours truly….:: Chris (Andrews): I’ll take this army on…you two go on ahead. I’m pretty sure that I can take care of a bunch of midgets…after all, we’ve heard all about your anti-midget exploits in the PCW Chase Numero Uno. We’ve heard all about your mission to eradicate midgets from promos, particularly the three same midgets that terrorized the real Dustin back in Kentucky. Isn’t that correct Chris? ::. It sure is correct…but not quite in the way he thought. It was by no means, my mission. My mission since day one was to make a name for myself…and today, I’m closer than I’ve ever been. I’m closer to achieving my primary objective than ever before, and I’m ready to become a champion, recognised throughout the world for my achievements and such…so, I nod in response to Andrews…who silently winks before throwing off his jacket…over the horde of midgets….:: Chris (Andrews): Hey, Hey…you bunch of cretinous dwarves…let’s see if you can keep up with ‘The Messiah’ Chris Andrews…the saving grace of this poor world… ::. The horde of midgets sweep around at once, reaching behind them for their miniature pistol weaponry…as Dustin and I can be seen sneakily picking a way around them. One of the midgets, who is obviously the platoon leader…barks out orders as Andrews runs out of the opposite door, dodging the bullet fire from their guns….:: Midget Leader: THERE HE IS! GET HIM!! MIDGETS POWER UP…GET CHRIS CHASE DAMNIT! Midget: But we’ve yet to watch the latest Barney the Dinosaur episode, this one’s featuring Mark Estep. Come on boss, can’t we just watch it for a little while, then we could hunt down Chris Chase? Surely it wouldn’t take long to spot such a cocky and over-confident individual…trust me boss. Half an hour later… Dustin: So Chris…I hear that you’ve got some Pay-Per-View match tonight, maineventing apparently, yeah? ::. Half an hour later…and we seem to be getting closer to our target that is the very center of Boda-Land, as is indicated by the large neon signs all around us…indicating us to turn back or face certain doom. Obviously Boda’s been expecting me. As we walk, Dustin strangely becomes interested in the real life exploits of Chris Chase, which of course…being Chris Chase, I’m obliged to acknowledge and explain of course. Grinning, I begin to elaborate on tonight’s match, where I face Miyagawa and Boda for real…for the PCW World Title. Great, there I go…shamelessly plugging PCW events and matches. At least I can claim with little repute that I’m going to be walking back…unbloodied, and PCW World Champion….:: Chris: Of course I’m maineventing Dustin, isn’t that a law given these days…that the most celebrated athletes headline events and such. Given the fact that I could never truly overshadow the new owner of the PCW no matter what I do…a victory tonight would ensure that I’ll be immortalised by holding the PCW World Title…the latest in a long line of champions. To think, ‘The Messiah’ Chris Chase – PCW World Champion…it’s got a ring to it, don’t you think. And to think that I can now brag to Toomes and Kannon…and whoever may be close as a rival to yours truly that I’m holding the most prestigious title in the business, in the largest federation on the planet…it’s kinda humbling don’t you think? Dustin: Of course I don’t think…and besides…I think…we’re there now… Chris: Huh? What? We’re there already? Why didn’t anyone tell me? Dustin: I don’t think I have the mental capacity to answer so many questions at once Chris… ::. I slap my forehead as we enter a room, a large cavernous room with a large shadowy figure and his minion in the very center, in the middle of a wrestling ring. They’d been expecting me all along…and I know that deep within my heart…I know that I have to beat them. Whether I’ll beat them or not…it’s anyones guess…but with taking two enemies on at once…isn’t that a little too much for ‘The Messiah’ Chris Chase? Well…we’ll find out…tonight. But first…isn’t there a little mockery to do before I head into battle with these two men?.:: Chris: So…you must be the boss around here, right? After all, only a large guy such as yourself would have the nerve to alert people to your presence with neon signs. How frickin’ subtle… Large Shadowy Figure: You dare mock me, Chris Chase? Come up here, and step into the ring…don’t be shy, it won’t hurt a bit… ::.I nod….:: Chris: Of course it won’t hurt a bit…after all, you are Boda…right? And is that Miya-Jobber I see there too? Miyanion: Gah…feck off you damn homosexual…we don’t need your kind here… Chris: Homosexual? Ha! Is that the best you can come up with? Boda-Replica: Hehe…is that really all you can come up with? ::. Miyanion slaps the Boda-Replica…hardly making a mark on the big man….:: Miyanion: Hey, you came up with it…you big ugly retard! Dustin: HOW DARE YOU COMPARE HIM TO ME…MIYA-JOBBER! Boda-Replica: Do you want a piece of me too, eh short stuff? You’ve been after my company after day one…right come here you… ::. There is a bit of a scramble between the two…as they fight amongst themselves. I tap my foot on the floor…growing ever impatient….:: Chris: I hate to interrupt a domestic…but isn’t there something that we’ve got to get out of the way first? ::. And with that I slowly stride towards my battle…my battle of all battles. The battle of a battle which can never be a bigger battle. Gah…enough about battles. And, in the interest of fairness…we’ll leave this on a cliffhanger…where Chris Chase goes to battle with Miyagawa and Boda…with the World at stake. Who will win? Well…find out tonight, on PCW Spring Fling…live and only on Pay-Per-View. Who will be the World Champion come tomorrow morning? Boda? Miya? Chase? Five was always a bigger number than four. And you can Justify…that!.:: TO BE CONTINUED…ON PCW TELEVISION!!! TUNE IN!! ‘The Messiah’ Chris Chase – Canadian Wrestler, Born 1st January 1979. A cocky and over-confident individual at times, his PCW career has been a tiny bit disappointing up until the year 2002. Former PCW2 Continental Champion, Former PCW Cruiserweight Champion. Soon-to-be PCW World Champion? 2002 – Not the Year of Kannon…Not the Year of Boda…Not the Year of Toomes…The Year of ‘The Messiah’… And you can Justify…that! # - An interesting reference…and use of rearranging. 01/01/79 is Chris Chase’s birthday…## - The Boda and Miya (Un)Intelligence Agency
