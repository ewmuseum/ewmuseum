A date with destiny...and midgets Part 2 FF#3

8 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8/5/03 Justice Inc RP#10 The shone down brightly upon the park. People seemed to be going about their business in a relatively normal fashion. The sight of the calm and tranquillity had the four superheroes a little bemused. There was no chaos in the park. Well no more than normal, this was the Bronx after all. Despite their scepticism about the whole situation they decided to stay a while, make their presence known, at the same time busting a few small time crooks up to no good. They had been there for about half an hour before they decided that the phone call was indeed a prank one. Linda led the way back to the Justice ‘mobile’ (it was debateable), when it happened. In the far distance there was a faint scream coming from some unfortunate woman somewhere. The team instantly turn at the sound of the blood curdling scream and consequently rush in the vague direction of the noise. Lusty Linda briefly filed a complaint, something about wasting time and money, but Justice Inc were nowhere in the vicinity and consequently missed the out burst. The team was led by Blind Rage, as they made their way along the recognised mud pathway. They saw the true meaning of chaos as soon as they turned the corner, looking past the small patch of tree’s. There were small patches of fire on the ground and on a few branches here and there, engulfed and drowned out somewhat by the mass of people all over the place fleeing for their lives. At first it was difficult to see what was causing the chaos, in fact when the team didn’t see anyone on the ground they looked into the air looking for some sign. It wasn’t until the fairly butch man staggered across their path that they noticed the cause of this mayhem. “What in the hell’s goin’ on here?” Warrior exclaimed, even more bemused than before. It was worse than any of them expected. They didn’t think they would have to go through the same ordeal again, taking into account what happened in the day care centre the other week. It was midgets. A plague of them in fact. Blind Rage stepped forward and pulled the midget from the poor man’s back and held it out in front of him. Upon closer examination the midget seemed to be of metallic substance, which meant only one thing. These things weren’t just midgets…they were robotic midgets. Blind Rage held the little bugger out in front of the rest of them, as it helplessly squirmed, trying to gain it’s freedom. “Midget Robots.” Blind Rage simply said in explanation. “Well damn, sugah!” The Handbag Warrior exclaimed. “A plague of them.” Badger Man wore a thoughtful look. “Do you think we were set up here Rage?” he asked. Warrior began to nod. “Yeah. That day care incident. That guy was a midget.” “What makes you think it was him?” Badger Man asked. Warrior sighed. “How many evil midgets can there be? He’s obviously out for revenge.” “Keep that thought on the back burner.” Blind Rage said. “We need to take the rest of these guys out, keep a look out for the ring leader. The last time he was wearing a white mouse suit, so a doubt he will be less conspicuous in this encounter.” The rest of them simply nodded in reply. “How hard are these bastards to kill?” Warrior asked, cautiously feeling the laser axe attached to his left leg. Blind Rage simply shrugs. “Lets see shall we?” He said, holding the robot midget out in front of his further. Effortlessly he tossed the midget up in the air and punting the little monstrosity in the air. The robot was launched into the air and flew just about as far as the air can see. Blind Rage smiled and nodded. “It seems they can be defeated.” Quickly the courageous four rushed towards the chaotic crowd. Upon seeing the superheroes heading in their direction instantly brought a look of calmness over the faces of the crowd, which could only be brought upon them by the view of safety - until Warrior fell over a bush and fell on his ass. That didn’t fill the crowd with much confidence. Blind Rage charged through the crowd of robots shrugging them off here, kicking them away there. Some of the little beggars surrounded The Handbag Warrior, whom simply swung his enchanted handbag around him, destroying the robots upon the slightest of touches. Badger Man scurried through the battle, keeping his ever so keen eyes on the look out for the non-metallic midget, the rind leader behind the whole incident. However, the mass of robotic midgets were too big in number and thus he was forced to join in with the battle, especially when a number of them jumped upon him forcing him to the ground. Whilst the battle was commencing upon the field before him, Warrior had only just picked himself up from the grounded. He was quite bitter about the fact that none of the civilians that they had come here to help had even offered to help one of their saviours to his feet. He dusted himself down and that’s when he spotted him. The midget was now wearing a purple suit, seemingly abandoning the new one, but Warrior recognised his face. He was the bastard that wrecked his Lego city back in the day care centre. Warrior eyed the little evil menace up closely. He was relieving himself on a tree! Warrior filled with rage. It took him hours to build that Lego city. All of that work…for nothing. Warrior charged over toward the midget and let out a primal cream. Little Willie turned around his match stick in his hand to catch Warrior’s size 10 and half in the face. The little guy’s head rebounded off the tree and he feel motionlessly to the floor. Piss covering his pants. Whilst this little victory could not bring back the Lego village the little bastard destroyed, it made him feel a little better at least. Warrior emerged from the bushes at about the same time as the rest of the team had finished up with robots. There was a large metal pile of motionless tiny bodies in the middle of the park. His team mates met him with a smile, as soon as they saw the little bastard dragging motionlessly behind him. “I think it’s time we questioned the little git…don’t you?” Blind Rage asked, with a mischievous smile upon his face. Then Lusty Linda ran over, almost breathless she started to violently shake Blind Rage by the arm, whilst pointing at the crowd at the same time. “Quick…” she panted. “Let’s hold a whip round!”
