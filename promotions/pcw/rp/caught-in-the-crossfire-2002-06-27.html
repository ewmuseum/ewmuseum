Caught In The Crossfire

27 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 27th June 2002 As the world spins around, time passes….as time passes things change! People change….there is nothing that can be said or done to change that fact! But in two warring worlds OSW and PCW things can change at anytime and any place….some may say that history itself can be re-written!These are two places of great, power and might as anything can and will happen at any given time! But then again the argument of changing time still stands!However in these two Worlds things can change as quick as the seconds on a clock or as long as years on a calendar! Either way the point still stays that time does change things….Some people argue that the future is predictable, others say it’s nonsense and that everything is fate! Maybe it was the OSW Superstar, Corg’s fate to become OSW Cruiser Weight Champion…maybe that’s what was written for him! Maybe it could be PCW Superstar, Charlie Loc’s fate to become the next PCW Cruiser Weight Champion….maybe that’s what is written! Then again, people argue that everyone writes their own story and chooses their own fate, whatever it may be, something more than fate has managed to draw these two superstars from Rival Companies….and that thing is none other than, The Oprah Winfrey Show~! 7:30pm EST, LIVE! New York City, New York….The Oprah Winfrey Show'Role-Models And The Examples They Set' Someone cut the lights….or maybe it’s just another one of the routine power black outs in the fine or not so fine city of New York. Whatever it was the darkness didn’t seem to effect much of life at all as instead it just gave more atmosphere to wherever the hell it was! Darkness, as Darkness does eases up after a while and as it was doing now things began to become apparent as a hundred and one soft murmuring voices sliced through the darkness like a knife and shed new light on the situation…but not literally! It was as though the dark room was filled with life that didn’t seem to care if they could see or not because through all the male and female voices and conversations that could be heard going on in the background, not once did anyone refer to the lighting! Perhaps it was a cinema….or maybe even a wrestling stadium as both of them are usually delved into darkness before something is scheduled to start! Or maybe something wasn’t even scheduled to start! Maybe it was a homeless center to care for the hundreds or even thousands of homeless people living in New York! Whatever it may have been was suddenly forgotten as a blinding flash of bright white light lit up the rather large room and showed a sea of people of every race, color, size and shape sitting down on comfortable looking plush seats and talking or looking down towards a stage. Sitting on a comfortable, red, plump arm chair with a glass coffee table next to another two chairs was none other than the familiar looking black woman…Oprah Winfrey! Coming complete with a huge white smile, dark skin, big boofy hair and the professional look that she always tried to attain. As the scene became apparent flashing purple and white signs became seen hanging from above the stage with flashing words such as 'The Oprah Winfrey Show LIVE From New York City' and a backdrop of a rather well looked after wall, complete with plants and glass doors could also be seen behind it all. Sometimes things don’t become to clear to the mind until the mind is exposed to them, or as the old saying goes 'If it had been a snake, it would’ve bitten you!' but it was no snake so there was no bite and everything evened itself out as Oprah could be seen talking to herself…probably rehearsing script before the show was scheduled to start! After all this was seen and absorbed it became apparent that everything had a feel to it….that feel was somewhat 'Fake' and it probably came along when the unlit signs that said 'Applause' and 'Laugh' and 'Silent' revealed themselves hanging next to the signs promoting the show. The murmuring continued to go on as Oprah continued to rehearse her lines and the 'Fake' feeling slowly sank in. It seemed typical American….. 'And were going to cut LIVE in 1 minute and counting!' A voice of a camera man could be heard loud and clear over the P.A System as 5 cameras complete with cameramen wearing headsets and 'Oprah Winfrey Staff' shirts moved in front of the stage ready to film. Suddenly everything went from being relaxed straight into being focused as Oprah bolted upright in her armchair and the faces in the sea of people could be seen shuffling around in their seats, getting comfortable, training their eyes on Oprah and getting ready for the show. The murmuring finally died down to silence as Oprah cleared her throat and took a deep breath before positioning herself comfortably in the arm chair and looking towards a camera in front of her with a big, fake, white smile. 'And were LIVE in 5…4…3…2…' The voice trailed off of the P.A System as a brighter light was shone down on Oprah and the theme music for her show began to fill around as the 'Applause' lights lit up and everyone began to clap. As the music slowly died down so did the clapping as the camera crew got a decent shot of the crowd before cutting back to a shot of Oprah as she introduced the show. 'Hi and welcome to the Oprah Winfrey Show! I’m Oprah Winfrey….' Her polite, yet fake black American accent came complete with a fake white smile as she trailed off. 'As you would know todays topic is 'Role-Models And The Example They Set' so let me introduce to you our first guest who happens to stand 6’0 and is literally looked up to by a lot of America’s youth! He is currently working in a wrestling promotion titled 'Old School Wrestling' and is their very first and own Cruiser Weight Champion….Ladies and Gentlemen, Corg!' Oprah’s voice trails off as the smile follows and she puts her hands together as the 'Applause' sign lights up yet again. Out of the darkness a door opens and a spotlight is shone onto it as Corg with his OSW Cruiser Weight Championship slung over his shoulder makes his way down the stairs and waves to the fans before scratching the makeup on his face, adjusting his dog collar and sitting down on the seat near Oprah. As the 'Applause' dies down Oprah leans over and gives him a soft kiss on the cheek as Corg cringes and leans back in his seat as Oprah puts on another gleaming white smile. 'Hi Corg, welcome to the show!' Oprah’s sweet American voice ends nicely. Corg glares at Oprah, creating a long pauses and wasting valuable show time. 'Ahem…' Oprah clears her throat. 'Okay, Corg, so you’re an OSW superstar and the Cruiserweight champion, how do you feel about all of this?' 'Well, um, to tell you the truth Oprah, I feel like shit.' Corg says rather uneasily. Silence fills the room. Oprah’s face turns red, as if she doesn’t know what to do in a situation like this. 'Excuse me, Corg, but could you please tone down the language a bit?' Oprah asks politely, still trying to maintain her wholesome image. Corg coughs, as a sign of disrespect. 'Yeah, sure, whatever,' he says 'Back to the question. I don’t feel very ‘happy’ about any of this. My whole goal wasn’t to win the- erm… why do you even care?' Oprah looks shocked, as Corg once again ruins valuable TV time. 'Well, uh…' 'You people piss me off! This fucking sucks, I don’t want to be on this lame-ass talk show!' Corg exerts some of his anger at the crowd, as he begins to rise from his chair. 'Fucking OSW made me come here today, I don’t give a shit about any of you!' 'Corg, please, calm yourself! We can help you, Dr. Phil is here today! He’s a psychiatrist!' Oprah begins to panic. 'Shut the fuck up!' Corg begins to get even angrier. One of the TV producer signals at Oprah, giving her an ‘OK’ sign. 'Please, Corg…' Oprah tries a pleading voice and puts on the big puppy brown eyes as the crowd begins to look a little shocked and uneasy. 'Everyone’s the fucking-same!' Corg throws his Cruiserweight title down on the ground. 'You people are lucky that I don’t come out there and fucking rape you, too!' A hush falls over the crowd as Oprah covers her mouth with one of her hands as she looks as though she could nearly faint. Corg nods his head and flips off one of the camera’s before sitting down low in his seat and staring sadistically at Oprah. As the camera pans over the crowd, they look truly shocked as Corg seems to have no remorse for the actions he just displayed. A young child in the front row has tears welling in his blue eyes as his mother pulls his head close into her breasts trying to comfort him. Oprah finally regains her courage and removes her hand away from her mouth as she mouths some words before sound slowly and softly comes out. 'I think I’ll just…introduce our next guest…..which was supposed to be the main….topic of the show' Oprah finally finishes her sentence as her theme music cuts in again and the spotlight shines onto the same dark door that Corg entered from. Corg doesn’t even seem to want to know who’s coming out of the door as he sits down low in his chair and begins to pick his nose as Oprah can be seen fanning herself with a sheet of paper before she leans down and grabs a glass of water from the coffee table beside her. The 'Applause' sign lights up one more time as strolling out through the door is none other than the PCW Superstar himself, the Vietnamese Viper…Charlie Loc! A few claps rise at first as everyone is still shocked from Corg’s rudeness but people soon get the picture as the 'Applause' sign begins to flash trying to give any epileptic person a fit. The clapping continues as Charlie walks over near Oprah and goes to shake her hand. Just as he reaches out for it, he looks past his baggy jeans and sees Corg slumped in the chair opposite him picking his nose with nothing other than his middle finger as he manages a fake smile to Charlie as the young Loc shakes his head and takes his seat gritting his teeth. 'Wel…*AhEm* Welcome to the show Charlie! Ladies and Gentlemen, another round of Applause for the PCW Superstar…Charlie Loc' Oprah finally gets all her confidence back and continues with the show as the crowd claps. Charlie doesn’t seem to hear or notice what Oprah is saying, instead he is looking at Corg with pure hatred in his eyes as Corg continues to pay no attention and just keeps on 'Digging For Gold' as Charlie finally focuses back on Oprah. 'How old are you Charlie?' Oprah asks her first question matter of factly. 'Why you need to know that?! Why you even have to bring me here?! Go onto the Internet and you get all the information you want on me!…..I 19!' After a huge spill Charlie finally gives up the answer as a few people in the crowd can be hear sniggering. 'And who do you look up to as a 19 Year Old Charlie?' Oprah asks as her eyes sparkle as she clearly gets over the incident that happened before. 'No one….' Charlie’s Asian accent slowly trails off as he goes back to eyeing out Corg. 'No really Charlie, you must look up to someone as a 19 Year Old! Don’t you have any goals? I know you’re wrestling for the PCW Cruiser Weight Title this weekend! Who pushes you to do that?' Oprah tries to coax an answer out of Charlie like a mother. Whilst staring at Corg, Charlie manages a reply. 'I serious….I not look up to no one, and after this weekend I going to be the PCW Cruiser Weight Champion! No one push me to do that, I just want to be better than all the rest…..well more than I already am!' Charlie smirks at the end of his speech as Corg’s ears prick up and he looks at Charlie then at Oprah. 'Wait, wait, wait! You brought me on here because you thought this little….this little….this little Vietnamese Fucker looked up to me?! ARE YOU FUCKING INSULTING ME?!' Corg begins to scream as he eyes down Charlie Loc and once again scared the crowd. Oprah seems to be scared as she pushes herself deep into her red and comfortable seat as she mouths the words 'Help Me' to the camera man as Corg and Charlie Loc begin to exchange words. 'I going to be the Greatest Cruiser Weight Champion in the World! That right, not just PCW going to say 'Wow Charlie Damn Good', the whole World going to say it after Gold and Glory!' The trademark sinister grin of Charlie’s crosses over his face as the red blood begins to boil behind Corg’s white face paint and Oprah cowers. Corg laughs out loud and shakes his head before looking angrily at Charlie. 'Well Chuck…Chuck, Schmuck, Fuck, whatever you want to call yourself! I’m the greatest Cruiser Weight in the world and their ain’t shit you can fucking do about it!' 'You Nothing…..' Charlie shakes his head and looks away not being able to argue any further as he doesn’t hold the PCW Cruiser Weight Title. 'Really?! I must be something to have won this!' Corg reaches down and picks up OSW Cruiser Weight Belt at his feet and taps the gold. 'Your the one who’s fucking nothing….nothing but a damn pussy!' Charlie turns to the side and spits onto the cream carpet of the stage as the crowd can all be heard gasping as Oprah near faints in her chair. 'You want trouble HUH?! YOU WANT TROUBLE?!' Charlie begins to yell at Corg as Corg clearly get’s pissed off as he clenches his fist around the Title. 'You’re so lucky you don’t hold the Cruiser Weight Title over in Pussy Championship Wrestling, Otherwise I’d kick your ass now! But all I can do is tell you your days are fucking numbered! And….YEAH I WANT TROUBLE BITCH!' Corg stands up and lets go of his belt as it thuds when it hits the carpet. 'Alright……I GIVE YOU TROUBLE~!' Charlie Loc gets up out of his seat and steps up to Corg who is the full 2inches taller than him as Charlie shows no fear and doesn’t back down. Just as Corg opens his mouth censors finally hit the live screening of Oprah as every word that comes out of either of their mouths is beeped out, with the occasional 'the' and 'you' being heard every now and again.As the two men continue to dispute over their rights to claim their thrones the whole show topic gets thrown out of proportion as Corg pushes Charlie sending him flying near the edge of the stage as members of the crowd can be seen leaving their seats. Charlie recovers and shakes his head as he runs at Corg sending him crashing through the Coffee table as screams can be heard in the sea of people in the crowd as the two begin to fight it out on the set of Oprah Winfrey. Chairs can be heard being shifted back as everyone rushes for the doors as Security hesitates to come onto the stage as the two begin a bitter brawl. 'If you were 'Sunshine' I’d kill you right now!' Charlie’s muffled voice could be heard screaming at Corg. 'If you were Aimz I’d fuck you up even more than I’m about to now~!' Corg screams back before laughing psychotically. Suddenly Corg releases his hold of Charlie reaches up and sends a huge fist crashing into the 'fake' yet perfect face of Oprah Winfrey as she drops into a crumpled heap on her chair as Corg begins to laugh. 'That’s for inviting me onto this stupid fucking show!' Corg pounces back on Loc as the two continue to brawl over bragging rights as they knock a cable and the show cuts to static and then quickly to a commercial break….. (Are you tired of sleeping while you drive?! Is Coffee becoming to addictive for you?! Then why not try….Sleep No More! The drug…err, pill that is endorsed by none other than PCW Superstar, Charlie Loc. Only $19.95 per pack!) Is this a taste of what’s to come at Gold & Glory? Why the hell is Corg bothering to do anything with Charlie Loc and the one question that still remains unanswered…..are they going to get paid for this?!Only time will tell…….
