Coming Out of the Closet? No. Harassing Candians? Yes.

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: With An Intent To Kill The scene opens up with the three newest PCW soda conossieurs: Rush Mason, Jason Core, and Jordan X Shabazz. They’re all sitting in a large room that appears to be a living room. There is a black leather couch and a matching recliner. On the wall are shelves full of DVD’s and the 72-inch TV is muted. Rush is sitting on the recliner right next to a table with a phone, and Jordan and J-Core are both seated on the couch. Rush: Okay, so we’re goin to call information in France and ask for the number of that Perrier stuff. J-Core: Rush, you do know that they sell Perrier here in America right? Rush, in his normal fashion of late, ignores J-Core’s comments and all logic. Rush: Jordan, you dial, you’re the one who knows French. J-Core looks surprised to hear about another one of Jordan’s talents. J-Core: You speak French? Jordan: I had to learn to shake them French haters off. Jordan picks up the phone and dials in some numbers, then hears a voice on the other end and translates for Rush. Jordan: Okay, it’s some French lady. She said that Perrier doesn’t have any factories in France. Rush: Well then where are the factories? Jordan: Okay.(To the phone) Ou est-ce que Perrier? He listens for a moment, and then translates back to Rush. Jordan: She said that they’ve got some big factories in Canada. Rush: Well, we’re in Canada. Ask her for the number. This time Jordan’s perfect French goes fast and fluent, which means this writer doesn’t feel like translating any more French. He then hangs up the phone and Rush looks at him, waiting for an answer. Rush: Well? Jordan: Well what? Rush: The number?Jordan: No, but she did say I was cute. Rush and J-Core both sigh loudly. J-Core: You’ve got a face that’s beautiful over the phone Jordan. Jordan: Thanks Jason, I knew that you could be mature about this. Wait a second! Meanwhile, Rush and J-Core are already walking down the stairs to the lobby, which they reach very quickly and then walk outside a huge glass sliding door. They step outside and Rush smells the air. Rush: Eww…it smells Canadian out here. Jordan: Shut up, I grew up in Canada. Rush: No wonder you’re a jegro. J-Core: Excuse me? Rush: A jegro. Oh sorry, I forgot your skin color prohibits you from being cool, J-Core. J-Core just rolls his eyes as Rush explains. Rush: A jegro is a black guy who acts like and wants to be white. Jordan: Like Rush, par example. Rush: Canadienegro boy, that’s enough. I need to find one of these Canadian French people who can tell me where I can get some Perrier. Just look for one of those ugly little beret hats. J-Core: We’re looking for French-Canadians, not Monica Lewinsky. The three walk for a few blocks, when they come upon a man who has a certain French-Canadian look, if there is such a thing. Rush: Say there, Jean-Pierre, can you tell me how I’d get some of that Perrier stuff you French people are always drinking? Jean-Pierre: How did you know my name, monsieur. J-Core: Because we’ve got a lazy handler writing who doesn’t feel like giving you a real name? Jean-Pierre: Well, I do not know where you could get some Perrier water. You could most likely find at the nearest Walmart or something. Jordan: Walmart?! Those hick, oppressive white people are out to oppress the black man and take away the power from those like the Revered Al Sharpton and- J-Core: We don’t need the entire history of the black people, Jordan. Jean-Pierre: Well, Perrier, is rather popular. You can find it almost anywhere. Rush: What exactly is Perrier? Is it water with French blood in it or something? Jean-Pierre: No Rush: Is it freshly brewed coffee water? Jean-Pierre: No Rush: Is it even fruit2o? Jean-Pierre:Nope. Rush looks around, confused. Rush: So, basically, you French people are peddling money by selling water with some made-up name? Jean-Pierre: Well I suppose if you want to get specific- Rush: That’s enough. Jean-Pierre just shrugs and walks off down the street, past the three Malice Crew members. Rush: I don’t need to drink any bin Laden brew to know that it tastes disgusting then. That’ll do. That means Perrier’s off the list. Now where we will find some Krylorian Cola, Poison Sobe, and others? Jordan: We’ll have to go to the place that produces the most digusting, most revolting substance know to mankind. J-Core: We have to go to a nuclear powerplant? Jordan: Even worse. Rush: A Michael Bolton concert?Jordan: Stay in the bounds of reality here. We have to go to… a wine specialty store. Both J-Core and Rush look around, puzzle at that last comment. Jordan: You know how snippy those old white bitches can be! Rush: Umm…ok Jordan. Remember to take those meds. For the second time, Rush and J-Core start walking off while Jordan curses under his breath. J-Core: What the hell did we accomplish today? Rush: No idea. Ugh…white people. Jordan: Seriously, I need a catchphrase like that. Rush: If you’re not black, you’re wack, kids, don’t smoke crack. Jordan looks at Rush, wondering what the hell he just said, and somewhere off in dream world, Mr. Batee starts to yell about crack, but before that can happen, our scene fades to black…
