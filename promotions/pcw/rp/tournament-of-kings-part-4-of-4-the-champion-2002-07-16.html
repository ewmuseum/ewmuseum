Tournament of Kings part 4 of 4...The Champion.

16 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7-16-2002 (The scene opens outside of a golden and blue tent that bears the Pendragon sigil, the crowned golden dragon. The camera moves slowly into the tent, and we are met with Venneman Pendragon, clad only in black breeches and boots, kneeling before a small altar bearing the mighty blade Excalibur. His head is bowed, and his whispers are barely audible as he seeks a blessing from the past...) Venneman: Oh past Pendragon's...With your guidance, there will be no doubt about my superiority. I only ask that you protect the fools who have the gall to face me today. It is always messy when someone is killed or is seriously injured during the joust...Glory and honor... (Not exactly the kind of blessing most of us would seek, but Pendragon is a strange fellow, so he will do what he will. Finished, he stands and sheathes his weapon on his left hip. He then moves to the bed and picks up his golden shirt of armor and pulls it over his bare chest. He takes one look around his Spartan tent before he nods and takes off at a brisk pace out the back flap. The camera obediently follows as he strides to the tent where Xerxes, his steed, is kept. He pushes into the tent and is met with the visage of Peter the Hostler giving the golden armor that now adorns the massive war horse a final shine. Xerxes looks even more fearsome with his golden face plating, his chest armor and his leg wraps. A magnificent saddle sits astride his back atop a dark blue riding blanket. Venneman smiles at the love that Peter shows for the great horse...) 'Peter would never own such a magnificent animal...Not because he could not afford it, I pay him well. No, he will never have an animal such as Xerxes because Xerxes chose his master...Me. You cannot own a spirit...You can only control it. Because of my mastery, Xerxes is mine. Not merely because I bought him. I could not sell him if I tried. The animal would not make that choice.' (Venneman moves over and pats the fierce animal's muzzle. He rubs the animal before taking the reigns and leading him from the tent. He nods to Peter as he leaves....) Peter: Give em hell, Lord Pendragon. Venneman: All that they can handle and more, my friend. (Peter grins sheepishly at his outburst, but Venneman just smiles. His eyes seem to be getting crazier looking by the second. Perhaps it is because he is so close to a competition that matters to him. Hell, maybe it's cause he is merely insane. You make the call. The camera follows obediently as Venneman climbs into the saddle. He looks like a born king upon his white war horse, and he looks like a dangerous warrior even though his armor is but a chain mail shirt and he wears no helmet. He pauses for a second, framed against the rising sun in a shot that oozes nobility. After his camera moment, he rides off towards the main arena. >>>>>>>Flash
