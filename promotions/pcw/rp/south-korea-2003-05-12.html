SOUTH KOREA!!

12 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5-12-03 (OOC- sorry but the person that usually posts for me so it doesnt get all f'ed up couldnt post for me last night.) The scene opens to Adam sitting in a black leather recliner. He’s dressed in a pair of black mesh shorts and white Hanes shirt. He is holding a beer in one hand and a remote in the other. Adam moves his head from side to side in an attempt to crack his neck. He takes a drink from his beer. “Ahhhhh…” Adam says placing the bottle on a small table off to his right, “Having this past week off has been nothing but great.” He says with a smile as he leans back in the chair putting his feet up. He starts to flip through the channels. “I wonder when I’m going to have to go back though, I haven’t heard anything from PCW.” He says to himself, “I hope this isn’t how they terminate people because that would surely suck.” Suddenly his jaw drops to the ground as he sits up in the seat. He stammers for a bit just staring at the TV. It’s a commercial but a commercial with Jaslene in it. He watches it through and when it finishes sits back in his seat. “JAS!” he screams out, “Could you come in here please?” Adam shakes his head from side to side as Jaslene comes into the room. She’s dressed in a pair of midnight blue shorts with a white tank top on. She crosses the room quickly and quietly before pouncing onto Adam’s lap. “Oof!” Adam groans as Jaslene comes crashing down into his lap unexpectedly. “Hello there.” She kisses him on the forehead, “What’s up?” “I just saw a commercial on TV,” Adam starts to say but is quickly cut off by Jaslene. “Was it mine?!?” she asks excitedly. “Uh…yeah it was actually, that’s what I wanted to talk to you about.” He says in response to her question. “Oh yeah? What do you want to know?” Jaslene says in a slight sarcastic tone. Adam is quick to pick up on it. “Is that a hint of sarcasm in your voice?” Adam asks looking at her cockeyed. “Gee, how did you ever manage to figure that out without your trusty sarcasm detector?” Jaslene says with a smirk. “Luckily I just happened to have one ingrained into my brain dear,” Adam says returning her smirk, “But what’s with the sarcasm all of the sudden?” “You can’t be serious Adam.” Jaslene says looking into his eyes. Adam just sits there and shakes his head. “What?” he asks, “Why can’t I be serious? What can’t I be serious about? Jas I have no idea what you are talking about.” Jaslene drops her head in frustration. “Adam! You were there when I signed the contract!” she exclaims. “Contact? What contract?” Adam says in a quizzical tone. “My contract with T.M.R., I signed on to be a spokeswomen for some of their feminine products.” Jaslene says but still notices that Adam has no idea what she is talking about, “You don’t remember?” “T.M.R.? What in the hell is T.M.R.?” Adam asks shaking his head. “T.M. Revolutionary, they are a large worldwide conglomerate company that makes everything you can think. They range from weapons for the military to the shit that I’m peddling in those commercials.” Jaslene explains. “They even offered to give you some endorsements.” “They did?” Adam says in disbelief, “Why? When? For what?” “Earlier this week Adam, we had a meeting with the company to discuss my potential as a spokeswoman,” Jaslene says, “And then they turned their attention to you, they were very interested in getting you to sign on.” “How interested?” Adam asks. “Let’s just say that they were very willing to make in worth your while,” Jaslene says, “They showed you a check with no decimal point on it and told you that you could put it wherever you wanted if you would sign right then and there.” Adam shakes his head again in disbelief. “Well?” Adam asks. “Well what?” Jaslene responds with a question of her own. “What did I do?” Adam asks, “Did I sign the contract or what?” “Of course you did Adam, don’t be silly. You stand to make in the millions if you play your cards right with the PCW.” Jaslene says. “What do you mean?” Adam asks, his questions continuing as though he were a small child. “Christ Adam, did you pay attention at all?” Jaslene says in a somewhat condescending tone. “I’m sorry Jas but I just don’t remember any of this.” Adam says rubbing his head. “O…k,” Jaslene says getting up off of Adam and standing in front of him, “They put a lot of clauses in your contract that would make it worth more money depending on your PCW career. You do well, you get more money, you suck it up you get less. That sort of thing.” “Like what though? Just winning matches?” Adam asks sitting up in the chair. “Matches, titles, major PPV events things of that nature.” Jaslene responds. Adam reaches out for his beer but Jaslene swipes it just before he can grab it. She finishes it and tosses it back to Adam. “Shit!” Jaslene exclaims. “What?” Adam says looking into the empty beer bottle. “I just remembered,” Jaslene starts walking away from Adam. He gets up and follows her through the hallway to the front part of the house. She begins to head up the stairs. “You remembered what Jas?” Adam asks. “That you have a match tomorrow.” Jaslene says. “Is that it? Just a match?” Adam asks, “That’s no reason to be running off like that.” “Yeah it is when the match is in South Korea.” Jaslene answers as she heads up the stairs once again. Adam’s eyes nearly bug out of his head. “SOUTH KOREA!!” he screams out running up after her. He walks into the main bedroom and is nearly hit with a suitcase. Jaslene is in the closet just tossing clothes, both hers and his, out into the bedroom. Another suitcase comes flying out of nowhere and then another. “Jas, what do you mean South Korea?” Adam says still not quite believing her. “It’s the PCW USO tour, it’s making stops all over the world.” Jaslene answers, “It started last week but I guess since you had the week off they didn’t call for you to be on the show until you were needed which would be this week in South Korea for your match against Dante Jackson for the Extreme Title.” “What?!?!” Adam says, “The Extreme Title…in South Korea…tomorrow? SHIT! Jas we’ll never make it there on time. Not with SARS hitting that part of the world as it is.” “Actually babe, SARS isn’t as big of a threat as you might think, besides they are developing treatment for it as we speak but just in case would you be willing to sign everything over to me in case you do get SARS and die?” Jaslene says with a laugh. “Not a chance Jas.” Adam calls back picking up some of the clothes and placing them into the suitcases. “Why not?” Jaslene asks coming out of the closet with an armful of clothes. “Because if I get SARS I’ll make sure that you also get it and die to, that’s why!” Adam says sticking his tongue out her. “That’s not nice.” She responds throwing some clothes into another suitcase. “Well I never claimed to be nice, now did I?” Adam says with a smirk. “Shut up and pack Adam, we have to be quick if we are going to make it to South Korea on time.” Jaslene says throwing a pair of socks at him. “I don’t have a good feeling about this Jas, I haven’t had any time to prepare for this match plus I have spent the last week just sitting on my ass doing practically nothing!” Adam says as Jaslene heads back into the closet.” “Don’t worry about it Adam, you’ll be just fine.” Jaslene calls out from the closet. “Extreme matches seem to be your best, remember?” “Yeah I suppose you are right about that but if I don’t make it there on time I won’t even get the chance.” Adam says. “Exactly so shut up and pack.” Jaslene says coming out of the closet with another armful of clothes. The scene fades out on the two as they continue to pack.
