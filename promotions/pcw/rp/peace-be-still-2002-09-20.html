Peace be Still

20 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9-20-02 RP 1 Turmoil 24th Time is constantly changing. In order to survive, one must find a way to adapt to the changes, or be forever lost in the past. When once chooses to adapt, he or she usually learns to take what is given and lead a perfectly normal happy life. On the other hand, when one chooses to turn his or her back on the forever changing world, he or she tends to spend the rest of their lives searching, longing and desperate to get back to times before. In result, the person finds himself or herself digging a deeper rut and is alone. Many may offer assistance or give a hand, but the person is too blinded whit the hopeless dream of going back and reliving the past. In order to be saved, the person must face reality as is. Take the hand that is offered and pull him or her out of their own rut. It has been said, life is what you make it. Do not let the opinions of others determine your self worth. If you cannot make your own self happy, how can you expect others to do so as well? What shall it profit a man to gain the whole world and loose his own soul? These may be thoughts of the narrator, but today as Teresa sits alone on a bluff in the middle of nowhere somewhere in her home state of Tennessee. She has heard it been said that this state is God’s country. Deep down, she always felt the whole world was His country. The mountains and acres beyond acres of woods and wilderness untouched by men have always been her favorite places to explore. This is her sacred place she goes when she wants to get away. It is mid morning and Teresa has been watching the sun rise ever since it peeked over the horizon. Parked to the side of her is a Honda Rancher ATV, or locally referred to as simply a four wheeler. The Rancher is a dark orange. She has a large Igloo cooler bungie strapped to the front. Inside are bottles of water and a bottle of Wild Turkey Bourbon. Teresa sits against a maple tree feeling the cool fall breeze blowing onto her face pulling strands of her black and blonde hair off her cheeks. So much change has happened the past few months. She wonders what will be waiting for her in the future. Will she win a championship? How far will she go? Tuesday she will face perhaps the biggest challengers in her career thus far. There is no title at stake, no contendership to win, and no job at risk. To her opponents, this may be a drop in the bucket. They do not need to win. They have nothing to prove and nothing to lose. Would it hurt their higher-ranking status to loose to a lower rank competitor such as Teresa? Either way, Teresa feels she has something to prove, not to the fans, not to the Front Office, but to herself. If she doesn’t win, she is determined to give the two women a good fight and prove to herself that she can make it in the big leagues. This is a dog eat dog business and in her heart, she knows she is giving her all. Perhaps it’s not her turn to shine. Soon the ugly duckling will grow into a beautiful swan. That’s when Teresa will rise tall and say, “How do you like me now?” Patience is a virtue. The three most important men in her life are now gone. Her brother, Eric Tomas backstabbed her while in NTICW. When he left with her opponent’s girlfriend, he proved what blood meant to him. Nothing. She was at her best then, even though the fed had a bad rep or even jinxed with a curse. Jack Slayter might as well have done the same as Eric. She made a mistake by giving him a second chance and actually trusting the self-centered Texan. He used her fame and money in means to regain his own fame. She put him in his place and he attacked and humiliated her. May his crotch hairs be infested with fleas and die an itching death. Scott Taylor is the one man her heart burns the most for. The night NTICW closed and Boda decided to ship most of the roster to RUSH, Scott had went behind everyone’s back and signed himself to OSW. She never knew. The whole UO stable never knew. The same stable Scott created and led. The best friends she ever known was in that stable. That night Teresa stood alongside her stable mates, Andi the Magnificent with is valet, Fluffy and Syn. They watched as Scott signed the papers and left with that Reagan chick. Teresa felt the stake pierce a whole in her hart. That’s when the rage within her began. Fear leads to anger. Anger leads to hate. Hate leads to the dark side. May the force be with you. Too much deep thinking gives Teresa a headache. That’s when the Wild Turkey comes in. She rises to her feet and steps to her four wheeler. She unstraps the cooler and opens the lid. She takes the neck of the Turkey and with her teeth, pops open the cork. Teresa holds the cork with her free hand and takes a long swig of the bourbon. She feels the Turkey’s strong bite burn down to her stomach as she closes her eyes. After taking a deep breath, she feels somewhat better and replaces the cork. She puts the bottle back in the cooler and replaces the lid. It is now near noon and our lone Diva decides to return to her mother’s farmhouse about 10 miles away. She turns the key and moments later; Teresa disappears back into the woods.
