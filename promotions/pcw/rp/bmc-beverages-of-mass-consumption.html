BMC: Beverages of Mass Consumption

2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 03.06.03 Our story takes place at a small town meeting located on the green of Hicksville, Vermont. As the rest of the nation carried out their political scheming and misgivings months ago, backwards, redneck Hicksville gets down to business under a (surprisingly) hot May sun. It is Saturday, the day before Abner leaves for Cloud Nine, cruising the world high above the clouds and touring with PCW, and several days after his defeat at the hands of Labor for the Extreme Title. Tomorrow, he takes to the skies...but today, today he takes care of some unfinished business. Ever since Mayor O’Neil first took office as a town selectmen, several years before Abner served his sentence, he’s been a thorn in the side of every redneck, hick and hillbilly West of the Connecticut river. Righteous, subversive, and egotistical...but worst of all, snooty...Abner’s least favorite quality in men, those of whom look down on the 'redneck race'. And so sitting there in his brand new, jet black tuxedo (compliments of PCW-PR), and considering the losses and gains of his last (failed) venture verses the Mexican janitor, Abner looks up at the stage to notice a weakness...a penetrable point in the mayor’s shield, and acts on this venerability. From some seat in the crowd, toward the front, a hand shoots up. And as the mayor calls 'yes', the hand is followed by the tall, burly structure of Abner. The young rednecks gets to his feet, and immediately, everyone is drawn to attention. He looks around at the fellow townsfolk of his youth, trying to anticipate their agendas before acting on their behalf. 'Yes?' The mayor finally asks. Abner stumbles through a random patter of words. Though he tries to sound as polite as possible, the satellite on O’Neil’s only picks up bits and pieces of his 'low' frequency redneck vibe, and is thoroughly offended by what he hears. 'Hell no! I got a better idea...' Abner spouts, in reference to the mayor’s purposed tax cut. 'How about ‘stead a taxin’ folks by their money, you tax them by their stupidity!' A a cast of chuckles plays across the green. 'Yeah, that’s right…a 'stupid tax'! I mean, that’s all these damn frigin taxes are anyway…stupid!' The mayor steps back at this, holding his hand up defensively. By this time, the audience is roaring with laughter. The embarrassed mayor turns his crimson red face to the side, and then whispers something to his assistant who in-turn gives a young man to her left the 'throat slicing' signal. All of a sudden, Abner’s mic goes dead. And this is the way of the Hicksville town meeting. Ever since Abner can remember, even as a little boy, this was the routine: The mayor gets started, rambles on about some nonsense...and it puts Abner to sleep. But then, after the bullshit, the major takes questions, and after the first few (serious...and pre-planned) ones, the audience get a collective smile...because they know what comes next. Abner gets up, gets called on, and gets down to business. And the rest is history... He’s a fan favorite and the town always turns to him for a good ol’ ' hootin’ and hollerin'. The mayor starts in on Abner. 'Okay, Abner, that was quite an interesting question, but I think I’ll p…' 'Pass my ass!' Abner interrupts. 'I ain’t finished yet!' The crowd roars in approval. 'Go get ‘em, Ab!' Cries an old timer from somewhere in the back row. 'Oh, hush up!' his snappy wife hastily replies, smacking him with a pocketbook. 'Ow!' The old man takes his seat again. 'Yes sir, that’s right…I’m sick of you and yer goddamn hippie n’ horseshit policies!' Quickly turning to see that the audience’s still behind him, Abner rants on. 'And the hardworking people of this town…' he turns to face the audience, 'well, annnnyyywwwaaayyy, the people of this town…' he gets another laugh, 'don’t need you and yer half-assed policies, enforced by a half assed-cop crew, doin’ a half-asses job, rammed down our throats! See, the only ones who want it rammed down their throats are the ladies...and by 'it' I mean...'(his mic goes dead, momentarily). The major, who’s jacket is all ruffled up, struts out his chest and takes a step forward. 'Now, that]s enough of you…Mr…Mr Abner! I shall not have any more of it!' At this time, a few old men from the back row are on the ground, rolling in laughter, remembering the typical old scenario, being played out now, that Abner’s father forced back on the mayors back in the old days. 'Any more of what, Mr. Mayor!?!' Abner asks in a demanding tone, with a snobbish stress on 'mayor', 'Any more of the truth?' 'But…' (the mayor stops, but is quickly cut off). 'Ah-ah-ah!' Abner puts a hand in the air and shushes the mayor. 'Now there’s no use for you to use such language at a public meeting, sir...' 'But…' Abner cuts him short again. 'Now, now mayor, that’ll be enough…' By this time, the younger children are in tears, laughing. One cries out, in-between sobs, 'Hahaha…the mayor…the mayor…he…he just said ‘butt’!' More people collapse in their chairs and tip their heads back in laughter while Abner keeps up the pressure on O’Neil. 'But…' 'Hole?' 'But…' 'Head?' The mayor, clenching his fists, 'Why-why…' '…Why...Are you a butthead?' Even the assistant is laughing now, unable to suppress a smile as the rampaging, raging redneck ruthlessly continues his relentless roast. (Sorry, couldn’t help myself...) 'Why-why….why, this is absurd!' The mayor, half-recovered, slams his fist down on the podium, and aptly leaps off the stage. 'Mr. Abner' threateningly 'if you don’t shut your mouth, I’ll have security move you this instance!' 'Move! Move! Speaking of 'move', how bout I move you with some words of my own, eh? Heh, how’d you like that?' An ovation from the audience, and Abner nods his head, continuing on his word war against the mayor. 'Yeah, here it goes: if you threaten to have me reMOVED again, I’ll MOVE out of my chair, MOVE right up that aisle, MOVE my cow-shit stained boots…and by the way, cows say MOO!…and gently MOVE it straight up your dainty little ass, through your slim anus, pick up my boot, and MOVE you to other side of town at the docks whereas I’ll MOVE…' Abner blanks, scratching his hea, '..I’LL KICK YOUR ASS!' Again, the audience dies in laughter, amazed the innovational lack of the rednecks innovation. At this, the mayor jumps forward, arms flailing, only momentarily able to regain control of himself. Then, he points an accusing finger at Abner, yelling, 'Get him! Get him! Get him outta here! NOW! NOW! NOW!' He stomps up and down throwing a childlike fit as two men in tuxes come running forward. A grinning Abner also steps forward, his advantage being quite obvious. He’s easily got three or four inches and well over 40 pounds on both these guys, and for the moment, doesn’t seem to be going anywhere. The man to the mayor’s left, the smaller of the two, folds his expensive shades and places them in his coat pocket. 'FIGHT! FIGHT! FIGHT!' The townspeople cry in unison. As the taller of the two steps forward, folding his arms over his chest, there’s a small rupture in the crowd as some of the women of the town scoop up their young children and sprint across the green with their loved ones in their arms. Some stay, covering their children’s eyes, still wanting to see it themselves though, as others simply draw their children’s attention to the fight by pointing, purposely wanting them to see the fight. (These immoral actions of course taken mostly by redneck families.)Nut ten, all of a sudden, 'Danny!' Abner’s voice cuts across the crowd, and with an outstretched paw, Abner shakes the hand of the smaller of the two. 'Abner.' Nodding back. 'Sean.' Abner nods his head toward the taller one. 'Abner.' 'Wh…what the….ooohhhh!' The frustrated mayor swings wildly with his fists and pushes the security team away. 'What the hell are you doing!?!' he asks. The smaller one shrugs. 'Havin’ a drink with my cuz…' 'You’re…you’re related!?!' With a smile on his face, the one named Danny reaches out and accepts a Budweiser offered by Abner. The redneck tosses the taller man one too, and opening the cans together, they chug down their respective beers, and then toss the cans aside. 'This IS an outrage!' the mayor cries out, '…and this town certainly won’t stand for it!' And if on Q, the entire population of the town quickly take their seats, smiling up at their mayor. 'Well, not without beer!' Abner retorts. And then, rolling out from behind the stage area, along black cart emerges pushed by a tall, dark man dressed in a red and white stripped shirt. The cart is long and black and is filled to the brim with fresh, ice-cold cans of beer. 'BEER BRAWL!' The old man in the back cries out. And all of a sudden, with no time to react, the mayor is shoved aside by a mob of beer-lusting townsfolk, rushing to the side of the cart man. Unfortunately, another whack of his wife’s pocketbook silences the man in the back, and he’s forced to stay in his seat pouting as the rest get their fill... 'Aw...' 'Stop it, stop it, stop it!' The distressed and notably shaken mayor cries out to no avail. Reaching up with a shaky, wrangled fist he begins to rip at his flaky, graying hair as the mob continues to drink away. He tugs harder and harder until shreds of hair stick to the sweat of his palm. 'Full bellies, and poisoned bloodstreams equal closed ears, Mr. Mayor, sir.' Abner replies. I’ll have you jailed for this!' 'What? For lettin’ people have a little fun?' 'No, for starting a mob!' 'I didn’t start it…' Abner puts his hands to his eyes and begins to peer around the audience '…that man did!' He points an accusing finger at a young ruffian, running by, Budweiser in hand. 'Abner' the mayor starts, 'he’s ELEVEN years old!' 'Heh…startin’ younger these days!' 'Ooohhh!' Shaking his fists he whirls around and attempts to grab the redneck by the shirt. But simply side-stepping it, the redneck backs off and allows the mayor to trip over his own feet, falling to the dirt. 'Whoops! Better watch your step…' The mayor, mud covered and enraged, hops back to his feet, and with fire in his eyes, begins to stare the redneck down. 'You’ll pay for this!' 'Pay for this? What? I didn’t pay for this…the town’s tax-dollars did...' The mayor’s fear is realized as Abner points over at the now empty cart of beer. The mayor, in shock, simply looks up at Abner, blinking, and doesn’t say a word. After several moments and a long, deep breath, he finally ushers out 'w…wh…wh…what?' 'Yeah, that’s right…the people paid for it. See, earlier this mornin’, ‘fore you folks showed up, we had ourselves a little, perfectly legal, referendum…now the people have spoken….soo….DEAL WITH IT!' The mayor has finally lost it. Swinging wildly, he connects with Abner’s jaw, sending the surprised hick reeling. Taking a few steps back, he begins to absorb the blows with ease. And in finally regaining his balance, Abner, simply sidesteps and ducks the blows, not even bothering to block or counter. The major sees that it’s useless, but the blows keep on coming. 'Aww!' Abner yawns, sick of the attack and not feeling motivated to duck the next couple of blows. But they simply bounce harmlessly off his beer-belly, and the crowd is loving it. 'Why you…' Abner suddenly doubles over, and rolling to the side, a hard punch by the mayor misses its mark, and again, he finds himself sprawled out on the ground lying in a puddle of mud. 'You…you damned people!', he cries out, hopping to his feet. 'You miserable, wretched, disgusting, redneck PUKES!' But somewhere between 'disgusting' and 'redneck' Abner found his fist and rolled it into a ball…whipping back and letting it go directly into the chest of the mayor. 'DING! DING! That’s number three mayor…you lose!' The man in the back cries out again. A few chuckles go out here and there as his wife, fed up and embarrassed, grabs a nearby brick, drops it into her bag, and clocks him in the head. The man goes unconscious and drops his head. In the mud, O’Neil continues to protest. 'You all think it’s some sorta joke! Huh, you don’t take my job seriously? Well I’m an elected official goddamn it, and I’m not gonna tolerate this kind of behavior anymore…no more!' Grabbing the nearest man, the mayor gives him a thrashing, kicking him in the knee and letting him drop. With a sick, twisted smile of satisfaction, he grabs another. But before he can choke him out, Abner is on top of him. He picks him up by the shoulders and tosses him aside. 'All right, that’ll be enough of you, hippie.' Abner reaches down to help the mayor up. O’Neil pulls back his hand. 'You...you...you’ll pay for this! No! No! Nooooo!' Flailing wildly in a circle, the mayor rolls over on his back and begins to beat the ground with his fists. His head snaps back and forth in his temper tantrum and he’s lucky that he hasn’t bruised or broken his neck. Suddenly, he gets to his feet and makes a beeline to the stage. He hops up on the platform, jumps over the podium and grabs the mic. His heavy breathing causes a minor amount of feedback and momentarily the audience covers their ears as the screech rackets their brain. 'Sheriff! Sheriff! Sheriff Brady...' even louder 'SHEEEERRRRIIIIF BRADY!!!' The sheriff stumbles forward, making his way out oft he depths of the mob. His large, round beer-belly is exposed, shiny, with beer running down his chest, hairy and flabby as he waddles toward the stage. He whips his head in drunken fashion and loses his balance, falling to his knees. A bottle of booze falls from his right hand as he balances himself on his paws. 'Yeah...?' He asks, looking up at O’Neil, shrugging. The mayor leans his head forward, eyes bulging in shock at the anarchy. 'What the HELL is going on here!?! Have you all gone insane!?!' 'Naw...' Abner waves it off '...they’re just drunk.' The mayor can’t begin to speak as he stutters his merry way into confusion. 'B...bbb...b...bbb...bbbbuuuhh...uh....heh...' His shoulders go limp and his head drops down as the life seems to be draining out of his body. His fingers go numb, then his hands, he drops the mic, and his legs go numb too. 'Uhhhh....' the sheriff scratches his head, 'here ya go.' Brady tosses his cuffs to Abner as the redneck whisks by. 'All right mayor, I reckon it’s time for you to go...' 'B...bbbb..bbbaaahhhh...ehh...grrr...' Looney Tune style he reaches up with his pointer and runs it up and down his lips. By the time Abner has reaches him, he’s curled up like a baby on the stage, sucking his thumb. There, there, O’Neil.' Abner gently reaches down and cuffs one hand of the mayor, and locks the other to himself. 'Eh heh heh!' the old man in the back laughs out ' ‘at a boy Ab, lock ‘em up.' His wife simply rolls her eyes knowing that it will never stop. Abner nods his head, and gives the chain a good link, pulling the mayor to his feet. 'C’mon, hippie, your days of horseshitin’ and baby-tit’n are over!' 'Heh eh heh heh!' this time, the old man in the back is standing on his chair, 'yes sir, Abner, that’s number four!' The whole crowd laughs out, 'Silly Abner!', as the hick drags the prick away. And yes, this is also a tradition of Hicksville. For generations now the Abner’s of the town have driven the mayors and other elected officials insane, forcing them to resign, give up their seats. This is only Abner’s fourth, but as a twenty-three year-old, he’s got the record. You see my friends, in the backward, old-fashioned, dog-eat-dog township of Abner’s youth, the rules of the game aren’t the same. These folks live in their real world, idealism and values given up for the crude form of every day life. A community of 'crazed' rednecks is in fact more intune to the real world than society itself. Their hostile, drunkards, and overwhelmingly xenophobic, and best of all (if not worst), they have a sense of humor. A humor that’s been lost in the mumbo-jumbo of mainstream American life. It’s not that materialism is gone, on the contrary, the bigger guns (literally) always win out, however, they know the value that goes along with it. So why do they, every few years, drive their mayor out of town? Well mainly for fun, and also, as forementioned, it comes from the fact that they don’t take kindly to outsiders....and well, as nobody living IN town would possibly want to be mayor (as they know what comes with the job, the unwanted territory and such...), the mayors usually end up coming from a different town, a different country, or sometimes even worse...from Massachusetts. So year after year, Abner and his friends dispose of their unwanted guests, and another takes his place. The never ending cycle of redneckism in the Vermont. What a lovely, lovely story. And of course, Abner never breaks with tradition. Even in his prison years, many a nights were spent in his cell at D29 dentition under candlelight, writing letters of disapproval to the mayor... So before heading off on his month plus tour with PCW, Abner, the down to earth kind of guy he is, just couldn’t resist making such a scene. And he did. And it was... But soon, he’ll be boarding Cloud Nine, and soon Hicksville, Vermont will be little more than a nameless, unimportant speck seen out of the corner of his eye as he flies around the world. After his loss to Manuel Labor, his xenophobic attitude toward foreigners have grown stronger, and he still hasn’t been able to shake off the message he received after his match last Tuesday. This Friday he’ll be facing Matt Jackson in a singles match for the what could be a major setp up the ranks, and it’s hard to get psyched up. He’s got too much on his find...too much on his plate, and not enough room in his stomach...which probably has something to do with the daily twenty-four pack of Budweiser. But what can you do? Abner’s never really 'prepared' for a match before in his life, and yeah, he does his two-hour a day workout...but what’s more effective? That, or carrying around a case of beer? And what wins him matches? His 'technical abilities', or his low-down, in the dirt redneck brawling? He says it, and he sticks to it, that people in this industry take themselves too seriously. Will he be ready to fight this Friday? No. Will he fight this Friday though? Probably. And will he fight well? I wouldn’t’ bet on it, but hey, he might surprise you. You see, the thing with Abner is...well....honestly, there is no 'thing' with Abner, he just...is. They’ll be beer aboard the ship, and he’ll have a great time. Maybe a surprise here and there, and maybe a few of his own. But Abner doesn’t worry about the future, cause quite frankly, it’s not here yet. So praise the lord and pass the...beer...
