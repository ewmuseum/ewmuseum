Title: Acquiring legal counsel Featuring: Politically Incorrect Date: 31th Alone. Solitude can be a wonderful thing. However, in the cutthroat world of Primetime Championship Wrestling, solitude can lead to destruction. Big Alan Steele had to fight long and hard to overcome the numbers advantage perpetuated by The New Breed. Politically Incorrect are indeed a great tandem. But, they also realize that the resources of one Tyler Nelson are vast. At any time, Nelson could assemble a new Ultimate Authority, and send them after Politically Incorrect. Reginald V. Lovecraft already has Katherine Willis, his faithful intern. But, more than interns will be needed. Politically Incorrect will likely be the victims of questionable tactics at the hands of Nelson and Kannon. And, unlike other oppressed stars, they WILL pursue legal remedies if things do not go to their liking. Which is why Reginald Lovecraft has made a slight detour to a town called East Brunswick. Through friends, he has heard of a brilliant attorney by the name of Mr. Louis Brown. In need of legal representation, Reginald is off to Mr. Brown's home, in the upscale part of East Brunswick.. Lovecraft has a way of convincing people to join him. If you're guessing that Reginald has a rather large amount of semolians to offer to Louis Brown in exchange for his services, you would be absolutely correct. A couple million dollars has a way of doing that to you... ::Reginald V. Lovecraft smiles to himself as his driver pilots the Politically Incorrect limousine down an affluent East Brunswick street. His eyes scan the street numbers as he looks for house number 30. For, it is in this house that Mr. Louis Brown resides.:: Reginald V. Lovecraft: Ah, here we are. ::Reginald makes a quick gesture, which is spotted in the mirror by his driver. The driver smoothly turns the limousine into a long driveway. Parking it, driver signals to Lovecraft that it is safe to exit, which Reginald does.:: Reginald V. Lovecraft: Ah....I'm finally here. You see, this is but another step in Politically Incorrect's post-Ultimate Authority plans. It doesn't take a genius to see that CEO Nelson and Ass-Kisser Extrordinaire Xavier Kannon will likely find a way to take vengeance upon us. Our plan to prevent this is something that no one seems to have used against Media, Management, or The Ultimate Authority..the Almighty Weapon of Law. I find it amazing how easily mighty men in charge of things kowtow to one's will once one whispers the magical word: lawsuit. ::Lovecraft looks around him, taking in the lush landscaping of the attorney's front yard.:: Reginald V. Lovecraft: If the Ultimate Authority wants to get revenge, they will have to come inside a Primetime Championship Wrestling ring, and sign a contest in which they face us, under fair conditions. If they even remotely TRY to fuck us over, I am about to go hire the man that will help us sue for COMPLETE ownership of Primetime Championship Wrestling. ::Stepping up to the door, Reginald takes hold of the solid brass door knocker, knocking three times. Footsteps begin moving towards the door. As it opens, Reginald immediately greets his attorney friend.:: Reginald V. Lovecraft: Louis! Long time no see! ::He extends a hand, which Louis shakes vigorously. Mr. Louis Brown is a relatively short man, with gray hair around the sides of his head framing a considerable bald spot. He wears a sharp blake suit, with a navy blue tie.:: Mr. Louis Brown: Welcome Reginald! You had something you wanted to discuss? ::Mr. Brown invites Lovecraft inside. Both men take a seat at a large table. Louis goes into his suit pocket, and emerges with two cigars. Lighting each, he places one in his own mouth, and then hands one to Reginald.:: Reginald V. Lovecraft: Yes indeed, business, Mr. Brown...business. Mr. Louis Brown: What do you need? :Lovecraft takes a puff of his cigar: Reginald V. Lovecraft: Since you last saw me, I entered professional wrestling with a Republican from Iowa. We call ourselves Politically Incorrect, and reigned for 45 days as our federation's tag team champions. We just broke off a business deal with some former associates...and we fear that they will attempt to perpetuate harm upon us. We need protection...will you help us? ::Brown looks at Lovecraft, chomping down hard upon his cigar:: Mr. Louis Brown: You know I would love to help you, but you know how it is. No help without the green....the green...the.... ::Going into his own pocket, Reginald V. Lovecraft takes out roughly $20,000, all in $100 bills. A shocked Louis Brown counts the money.:: Reginald V. Lovecraft: That's your payment for the first job. More will come as it is needed. Mr. Louis Brown: In that case, I am officially Politically Incorrect's legal counsel. Reginald V. Lovecraft: Excellent. Then, let us go aside...I have a limo waiting. You'll be coming on the road with us.. ::The two men step outside the home, as Mr. Brown locks it up. Louis immediately rushes to enter the limo, but Reginald stops on the steps.:: Reginald V. Lovecraft: Today, Politically Incorrect expands by one. We now have the legal muscle to back up our physical muscle. Woe be to any who cross William S. Powell in Teaneck, or to The Ultimate Authority anywhere. Your next Extreme Champion has his protection in order.... ::Reginald smiles, walking over to the infamous limousine as we fade to..:: Paid for by the Friends of Politically IncorrectNow Four People Strong! ---- View Politically Incorrect's Biography
