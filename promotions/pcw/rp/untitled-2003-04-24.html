Untitled

24 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/24/03 Glee shone brightly on the face of a once very sullen Brianna Washington. Her big ear-to-ear smile had been consistent ever since winning the Extreme Title at Spring Fling. Though the pain was a bit unbearable from the blows she took, she was still very grateful on making out the winner, and not suffering as much pain as Skitzo who was probably still in a hospital healing. But most of all she was just happy to have accomplished something that few people had faith in her doing. “Brianna you have a visitor.” Milo chimed in, interrupting Brianna’s deep thought, which didn’t really bother her much as she was curious to whom the visitor was. She shot up out of the lawn chair and pulled on a baggy black t-shirt over her two-piece baby blue bikini. She then slid on a pair of shorts buttoning them up as she walked inside, and straight into the living room which offered up a cool breeze as opposed to the heat of the sun she was just facing moments ago. “Who’s here?” she asked with much eagerness in her voice. “He didn’t want to come inside, and I’m not sure why. Instead he decided to stay seated on the porch.” Milo replies opening the door for her. “He?” Brianna quickly walks to the door and looks out where the chairs are situated, and sees nothing but a few fallen leaves. “BOOO!” Brianna nearly jumps out of her skin as the voice comes from the opposite side of her. She turns to look at the perpetrator, and her frown instantly turns around as she sees the familiar face of Kramer Wild Wing (or as he would rather be called, Kramer Rinc). “Kramer?” Brianna asks unsure if this is all a dream. She reaches out and then lunges into his arms as he gives her a toothy smile. They embrace for several seconds before Brianna pulls away to regroup herself. “So it’s really you?” “Of course.” “It’s been a while. Way too long! You know you had me worried sick.” Brianna says as the both of them walk over and sit in the two lonely creaky wooden chairs. “Yeah about that, I’m sorry. Like I said in the letter I’m just sorry.” He says looking into her eyes, which triggers a question in her brain. “What is it that you’re hiding from me?” she asks demanding for an answer with her eyes. Uneasiness has obviously arisen as Kramer stands up from the chair taking a couple of steps away from Brianna, leaning against the porch railing looking out across the sky. “I figured you would ask that, and that’s why I was uncomfortable coming over here. As much as I want to tell you, I just can’t. I just wish you could understand that.” Kramer says peering back at Brianna for a split second to catch her reaction to his last statement. “Ok then…so why did you come here in the first place?” Brianna asks while joining him leaning on the railing. He laughs a bit at her persistence and then looks away in the opposite direction from her. “I just wanted to congratulate you on winning the title. You really deserved it.” “Well thank you.” There is a bit of silence between the two as they just stare off in different directions. A cell phone begins to ring which brings the both back to reality. Kramer digs into his pocket and pulls out a black Nokia, flipping up the top and sticking it to his ear. “Hello….yeah…..so they didn’t find him? Do they know where he’s at?…..you need me to come back……but I’m kind of in the middle of something……you can’t find someone else to fill it?….Fine…alright, bye.” Kramer slams the cell phone shut and looks at it. “This is just ridiculous.” Kramer spits out throwing the cell phone into his pocket. “What is?” “Just when I finally get time off, I have to go back into work. Unfortunately I have to do it right away.” “That’s ok. Maybe we could talk some other time.” “Oh before I forget…” Kramer digs into his pocket yet again and this time pulls out a small wad of cash and extends his hand out to Brianna. “What’s that for?” “For all your trouble.” “I can’t except that.” Brianna replies pushing his outstretched hand away. “Are you sure? It’s the least I can do?” “No I’m fine. Just be sure to visit more often. That should be payment enough.” There is a moment of silence in the air before Kramer reluctantly puts the wad of bills back in his pocket. “Well I really should be going. See you later.” He walks off of the porch and out to his little silver Omni. He rolls down the window and gives Brianna a big wave as he drives off. Brianna watches him until his car disappears down the street. Before she gets to the door someone calls her name. “Brianna!” She turns and sees Thomas, which doesn’t rally up any emotion with her. “Hey.” She says halfheartedly, walking back towards the door giving Thomas the good ol’ brush off. “Oh so you get all googily eyed after seeing that big joke, and you can’t even give me five seconds of your time? That’s really dirty Bri.” “Googily eyed? He’s my friend Thomas. Why do you always come over here and start stuff anyway? Can’t you ever get the hint that I don’t want to talk?” “Your friend? Please! I’ve seen the way you two were up there. Admit it, you have the hots for each other. I just came to tell you that Al Sirmol was thinking about giving you that grant but I guess I’ll have to find someone else, since you hate me so much.” Thomas turns to walk away, with his emotions shattered inside. Brianna looks very upset as she has been waiting for a grant for her inventions for a while now. “Please wait Thomas!” “You missed your chance toots.” With that Thomas walks away kicking a few rocks along the way. The scene cuts back to Brianna who watches him walk away. “Your never going to change Thomas. You’re always going to be a jerk.” She says before turning around and going back inside. The scene slowly fades to black as it catches Thomas’s facial expression, filled with sadness.
