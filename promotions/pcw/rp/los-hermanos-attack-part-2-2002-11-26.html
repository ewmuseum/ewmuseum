Los Hermanos Attack (Part 2)

26 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/26/02 A Night of Love Turns Into a Night of Hate It’s a cold night and Spain is seeing it’s first taste of winter in awhile. Romantico is cuddled alone at home, but not for long, as a female friend of his will be joining him later in the night. Romantico is decked out in an Armani suit with gold cufflinks and a diamond watch. He puts on some soothing music and begins preparation for his date. Usually Romantico doesn’t get this worked up for when he has a woman come over, but this time it’s different. The woman he’s having over was the same girl that he knew back when he was living on the streets. To Romantico she was the sweetest girl in the world. She would always let Romantico come into her house at nights and sleep in the basement. Even though it was strictly on a friend basis, he loved every moment of it. She took care of him, and he owes a lot to her and her generosity. Of course Romantico is also trying to make this a more pleasurable event for him self, testing the waters between the two in a sexual manner. Romantico’s confidant that she’ll have feelings for him, he just hopes she’s still the same sweet angel he always knew. She had called Romantico a week ago after seeing him on television she looked him up in the pages and found his number. From that point on they spoke and reflected on a lot of great and sad moments. Overall it was a real great time. Just hearing her warm voice was comforting to his soul. The second he heard it he felt safe. Romantico knows it’s weird for him to have to feel safe in the arms of another, but sometimes it’s just human instinct to want someone else there with you, so you don’t have to handle things all by yourself. Romantico prepares and entire meal and sets the table perfectly from the napkin alignment to the two lit candles. He then makes his way upstairs and puts fresheners, new towels, and new soap in the bathroom. He tightens up the master bedroom and lights candles around the bed. Right at the door he has a bouquet of roses to present to her when she walks in. Everything is set for the perfect night of lust and remembrance. Romantico then sits back and waits for the clock to hit eight, when her destination will be anticipated. So caught up in the date, Romantico doesn’t even both to think about the call me received from the Los Hermanos the other night. It was almost like he totally forgot about the issue. One thing is for sure though, the Los Hermanos didn’t forget. Outside behind a of trees are three red and black Hermanos Mustangs filled with gang members, just waiting for the right moment to hit Romantico when he leaves the house. They’ve been there for two hours, and are anticipating something to happen so they could get their rightful revenge. This is crazy, esse. We’ve been here like two fuckin hours and this bitch ain’t moved a hair. I say we jus go the hell in there and cap that bitch. Yea smart-ass, we go inside and kill him. That’ll work. Then the next day we’ll be on trial for our lives after his alarms go off and the police storm the place. Are you retarded? You know the police protect these rich people like one of their own. Stop talking out of you ass. Hey all I want is for this motha fucka to get what his ass deserves. Don’t worry he will. The gang member’s wait a few minutes longer, when they notice a car pull up in El Romantico’s driveway. Who the fuck is that? Shut up, and let me concentrate. Yo, Paulo give me those damn binoculars, lemme check this out. Paulo throws the Gang leader some binoculars, so he can check out what’s going down. What he sees is a beautifully seductive woman about to get out of her car. Guys this two hours of waiting is gonna be more than damn worth it. They each look at each other and smile. Back in the house Romantico is staring at his watch, wondering where she could be. He paces around the house a few times and even thinks she might have stood him up for a moment. He lies down for the few moments to help clear his mind, when everything goes down. A knock arrives at the doorstep of Romantico, and he quickly races to the door thinking it is Veronica, the girl he is anticipating. When he answers the door he sees a man quickly running back. Romantico wonders what’s going on, but then he looks up and it all becomes clear. There are three gang cars parked in front of his driveway, and in the middle is Veronica, with another mans hands all over her, covering her mouth from screaming. Her eyes shoot up in shock as she sees Romantico. She bites the man’s hand and screams out to Romantico. HELP ME! The gang member then slaps her for screaming. Romantico doesn’t even think for a second and just charges at the cars senselessly. He takes about eight steps forward before all the gang members get out of there cars and draw guns. Romantico then stops in his path as the leader of Los Hermanos comes out of the car with Veronica wrapped around him. What’s wrong tough guy, aren’t you going to save your princess? What does she have to do with this? It doesn’t what she has to do with this, because now she’s a part of it. Don’t worry though; we don’t have anything we want to do with her, just you. Well now that I think about it, there would be a few things I would like to do with her. The gang leader then breezes his hands through her and slowly kisses her cheek. Don’t be afraid precious; you’re in good hands. The gang leader then leads his right hand down her shirt and smiles as she screams for help. Shut up, you dumb bitch. LET HER GO, NOW! Romantico shouts out and charges again, but the other gang members hold him back. Oh you think you’re a big guy because you can beat up on woman. Well we’ll settle it right now one on one. One on one, oh man that’s hilarious. You see that’s not the way we work it here. If you mess with one of us, you mess with all of us. You know it was not a smart thing do that to our companions the other night, not smart at all. They asked for it. Well you know, you asked for this. The gang leader goes up to Romantico and pistol-whips him the floor. He then kicks him in the face. Romantico pops up, but six gang members hold him back as the leader laughs. Get him out of my sight boys. The six men throw him down to the ground and then kick away at his head and ribs. One of the men draws a gun and is about to shoot, but before he does a siren is heard. POLICE! POLICE! They all run away and jump into their cars, leaving Romantico bloodied with Veronica left behind. The leader makes sure to get in a last few words before he leaves. This isn’t over pretty boy. Romantico lays there after being completely defenseless and hate runs through his entire body as the police come over to check on him.
