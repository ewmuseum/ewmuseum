The Calling (The Black Dragons)

22 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 22 - 12 - 02 Feng Shui, the idea that furniture placement creates personal space, a tranquil and harmonic setting; all could be incorporated for this opening scene. White washed walls, minimum furnishing, and gaping windows that welcome in the sunlight that magnifies from the glass and the tangible smell of polished pine that defines the laminate flooring. Standing monstrously against the northern most walls was a golden statuette of Tibetan origin adding to the oriental awe of this building, suddenly from the connecting room came thud after thud echoing off the solid foundations. - THUD - Again the volume grows louder as the camera swings by the wall to investigate. Clad in tight fitting white overalls and in the midst of some intense training was the collective known as 'The Black Dragons' a unit based upon the Japanese concept of Puro. The product of developmental federation PURE, the duo now reside in Primetime Championship Wrestling (PCW) where they are on a collision course for the Tag Team Championships. However the team saw a set back in their quest as they were beaten by the present Champions only weeks earlier, yet we capture the unit as they hit their strict training regime. 'AAAAAAAAAARRRRRRRRRGGGGGGGGGGHHHHHHHHHH~!' The two combatants were absolute eye candy with their array of martial arts. Flip after flip, different variations of kicks and punches, a contest between the two members of The Black Dragons, Chen Lei-Chia and Chi'ing, no where in sight though is their unofficial third member Chiang Ho Ham. With this martial art display, one can only wonder why they haven't adorned gold, maybe it is because they simply can't win the big one, and maybe it’s the elevation of performing on a larger magnitude or something a little simpler. The elusive answer maybe buried in their bio, 'What bio?' The team are more secretive than anything buried at Area 51 or the NTICW Tag Division with its Great Latinos, Team Discovery Channel and Nu Era, all of which have fallen at its first obstacle, Pranks With Attitude. As though on queue the session ceased and with a subtle bow the duo like a pair of robots heading to the edge of the safety mats and over to a simplistic wooden bench. Panting and Wheezing heavily, a torrent of sweat washing their respected faces and their faces a burning red colour the duo look on the brink of collapse, maybe it's their stamina that could be deemed the teams flaw because from a spectators point, The Black Dragons look impressive enough to rival the best of Tag Divisions including PCW's. 'We must do better, failure is no longer an option?' said Chen Lei-Chia, wiping the flow of bodily fluids with a nearby towel. His tunic sticking tightly from his perspiring, his hair washed to the roots and the cringing of his face defines that his body has been subjected to pain. 'Agreed - We shall find ourselves further down the pecking order if we don't shine soon. With Chris Storm and Tessa Windsor joining alongside the recently acquired Angel Society then The Black Dragons may become nothing more than a memory!' retorted his partner Chi'ing angered by the teams failure at cashing in on the missed opportunities, one disappointment after another, the loss to Gaz Green and Nerezza over-shadowing all others. It appears as though the team is on borrowed time. With recent additions and firings, PCW higher powers may deem The Black Dragons a liability and with CEO Xavier Kannon's 'No Tolerance' looming, it’s a case of fighting for their PCW survival or face being thrown to the lions as a consequence. 'I AM THE VIOLATOR~!' roared Chi'ing for no apparent reason. 'PREPARE FOR TOTAL VIOLATION~!' continued The Black Dragon's member standing aloft, holding a triumphant pose sarcastically whilst Chen Lei-Chia looked with disgust. Trying to hold back his bombardment of insults, his face cringing, angered, frustrated by their lack of ambition and the list of disgraces above them, Chen Lei-Chia was almost at the end of his tether, looking to explode. It appeared a case of watching steam seep from his ears, his face intensify to the point of dis-colouring him when suddenly a voice filtered. 'YOUR A DISGRACE!!! You only have yourselves to blame. A unit who work in tandem, perfection personified I told the family, yet you have nothing to show your peers, nothing but excuses and now I hear that the parody of Total Violation are more worthy than my Black Dragons?!?' Standing before the collective was a small sage, an Asian man with a poisonous tongue. The Mountain Lord held a bamboo cane, wielding the weapon, toying with his team of misfits who flinch as it cuts the air with every swing, a series of near misses. 'You have had countless opportunities to please the family, two tenures in the pitiful promotion of PURE, a time consuming stint with OSW and through this degradation Xavier Kannon still kept faith, offering you YET another chance-.... What have you to say for yourselves?' asked the infuriated master. The room remained a desolate silence, no excuses or at-least plausible answers, prompting the Black Dragon master-... Not Inphino Blitz-... Pai Po to intervene praising the Scientologist for yet another opportunity to silence the critics. 'PRAISE HUBBARD!!!' 'PRAISE HUBBARD!!!' said the lowly Tag Team in unison as they bowed before their personal peer of the Mountain Lord Pai Po. As the duo bowed Pai Po unleashed his fury and brought down the Singapore cane crashing over his protégés back, causing him to yelp in pain, red markings of abuse and the trickle of blood. Chen Lei-Chia watched on in horror as his partners suffered at the ruthless and merciless hands of his mentor only to wait patiently for his own fate. Stopping momentarily as Chi'ing crashed to the ground in a heap, Pai Po positioned the bamboo weapon over the head of his other student; ready to deliver a crimson mask when suddenly the entrance crashed open. - CRASH - Standing in the doorway like a Guardian Angel was Chiang Ho Ham. The third member of this triad collective held a document aloft, waving the paper frantically causing his comrades to look with a glimmer of hope. Racing over, the document found itself in the hands of Pai Po who inspected the print closely, his head swaying from side to side, his eyes locked on like a pair of cross hairs. 'Well Master-....' murmured Chiang Ho Ham. A subtle grunt and the cain was discarded to the ground offering the Black Dragons a moment of relief, their master seemingly pleased with the article he held so dearly. With his mouth ajar, Pai Po told his students of the nature of this meaningless piece of paper, which brought forth a pair of gaping smiles, YET another chance to redeem themselves, one that they must conquer or face another frightening showdown with the Mountain Lord. 'It is addressed to JEFFREY and SNOOPY, bah-....' 'It says here that the collective known as The Black Dragons have a chance at capturing the number one contenders spot for the Tag Team Championship, simply by attending a meeting by the idiotic duo of Pranks With Attitude. The legit is that you must travel to the address marked on the back of this letter and you will be given further instructions-.... You shall attend and make the effort in bringing the family some wealth, lime light, this is YOUR LAST CHANCE and I suggest you don’t blow it, huh?!?' concluded the Asian master with an evil glint in his eye, undetected by his students who bow in homage, a mark of respect. 'YES MASTER!!!' added Chi'ing and Chen Lei-Chia in unison. With a thunderous grunt the duo stood erect and the earlier contest was ushered on by the spectating master Pai Po who instructed his combatants work out regime, preparing them for the journey they will embark on. An array of kicks, punches, flips and reversals that would have any onlooker foaming at the mouth took place under the watchful eye of the Mountain Lord, allowing the scene to slowly draw to a close, ending the final part of 'The Calling'. - To be continued -
