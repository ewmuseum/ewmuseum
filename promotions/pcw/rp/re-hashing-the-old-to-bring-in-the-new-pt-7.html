Date: I will not be denied Know a man through his past… Learn a man through his present… Break a man’s future, for that is the command of Khadafi… Know these are the laws of the Dem.on.M.aw. ‘See this?’ A thin, almost feminine finger jabbed down at the burnished wood. ‘C/O ‘99’ was carved in a lewd scrawl across the bench. The senior bench. The junior class had defaced it, but not a lot of the seniors gave a fuck. It was less than a month until graduation that made them short timers. Short timers could care less about what the juniors said about them. Had it been a month, even two weeks before, reprisals would have been swift and brutal. Now, it was only the junior’s vendetta. The seniors could care less. ‘See this?’ That finger again. That irritating, raspy voice. Those dull brown eyes and the fucking big hair. The skinny body, and the deep tan of the black skin. The high schools baseball jacket hung across the lank frame. The baggy shorts that reached to the shins, inches above the white tube socks and dirty vans. Ali Khadafi was sick of this kid. He was tired of having this kid in his face; knowing that his peers were talking shit behind his back didn’t help much either. ‘Yeah. Think I give a fuck?’ His voice was a low neutral, and eyes downcast. Ali hated conflict, even though most people would have considered him the hardest guy in the school. He noted the slight predatory gleam in the juniors eyes, and felt a coil of hatred tighten in his gut. He knew what the kid was thinking. ‘Chicken-Shit’ Ali felt his arms tense and release. The junior’s eyes flicked down to the arms, which were big for anyone Ali’s age. Ali knew the kid was out to get him, and Ali excepted that knowledge with a grain of salt. He never wanted to be at the top of the pecking order in the first place. Shit, he was just a poor black kid who liked to lift weights. He didn’t drive a fancy car and he didn’t fuck any incredibly beautiful or popular girls. In fact, Ali stuck to fucking the local sluts, knowing that the was probably the safest route. The white kids feared him because he was big, bald, and black. The black kids didn’t care because he kept his mouth shut. It was just this fucking junior. This junior who was in his face, finger still jabbing at the scarred bench. ‘This is on your fucking bench, Ali…’ ‘Fuck off.’ Cutting off the junior in mid-spiel and pushing past him. Ali was down the hall in seconds, but he saw what the look in the junior’s eyes before he left. Ali knew there was going to be trouble. The junior wasn’t going to let this one go. No sir, this time Ali had made it personal. A dry, humorless grin split his dark visage. He wasn’t surprised that he didn’t care. The no-color of cigarette smoke filtered through the pale light of the afternoon. It was sixth period. Shop-class, where the know-nothing jocks went to try and learn a manual trade. A lot of black and Hispanic kids, quite a few whites if you really looked. Almost a quarter of the football team was in the saw-dusty room periods three through seven, learning how to eke out a living by manipulating wrenches, screwdrivers and saws. Ali liked the period because the shop teacher, Mr. Crocker, didn’t care if they smoked or not. The light from his smoke glowed ember-like in his cranny of the shop class, ashes falling onto the dirty floor as Khadafi selected a socket wrench from the large red Craftsman toolbox. A real stickler for Craftsman was James Crocker. Ali jetted smoke from his nostrils and turned to the carb on the workbench in front of him, hitching up his baggy blue jeans. He sighed when he looked at the carb, which was no more than so much black, twisted metal. He didn’t know what Crocker wanted him to do with this piece of shit. It was obviously a fuckarow if there ever was one, but since maintaining his GPA was on the to do list, Ali fell to it with slow methodical way that was so thorough that it hurt to watch. Thirty minutes later Khadafi removed himself from the charred guts of the carb and reached for his pack of Kools. Sha-Thunk A screwdriver grew out of the middle of his smokes. Ali flicked his eyes upward and wasn’t really surprised to see the junior from earlier today. He wore a cocky, self-assured smile that spoke volumes. It told Ali that there was no way he was going to get out of this classroom in one piece. It told Ali that he wasn’t anything but a stupid jock, all talk and no action. It told Ali that the class of 99 was going to own, and fuck what the seniors thought. It told Ali that he was about to receive the beating of his life. ‘You know those are bad for your health Ali.’ That smile. Good Lord Almighty! Ali felt that coil of anger turn into a glowing brick of rage in the pit of his belly, and tried desperately to squash it. It was no-good; feeling the old hatred, the old rage, the old fear washed over him made his head spin. He gripped the table and looked into the eyes of the junior…. Fuck you baby… This is a dance that you can’t win… This is like the night of prom, and you’re my virgin date all prettied up in a nice red dress… Baby, you’re gonna get the fucking of your life… You ready baby? You ready to dance the dance of the demons? The junior took a shaky step back, stunned grin plastered on his face. He didn’t know where that shit came from, but it was freaky. He looked at Ali for a moment, and knew that he was indeed fucked. Air whistled around the wrench as Khadafi guided it towards the face of the junior. Blood splattered… Bones shattered… Screams echoed through the airy rafters of the shop room…. It took three students and Mr. Crocker to pull Khadafi off of the junior. Seventy-five stitches donned his face for a month. Surgery took five hours and in the end he had a steel plate for a cheekbone and twelve screws to keep his face from falling in out itself. It was horrible. It was tragic. That junior realized something about Ali Khadafi when he looked into his face that fateful afternoon. Ali had: Dem.on.Ey.e.s.
