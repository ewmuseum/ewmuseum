By Reading This, You Run the Risk of Decreasing Your Intelligence.

2 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: January 02, 2003. “What a bastard…” The scene opens up to see Jackson sitting solemnly on his recliner. The recliner itself isn’t in position to give maximum comfort, but instead just sits still, like a normal chair. Jackson leans over, with his hand holding up his jaw, his elbow on his knee. Narrator: What’s wrong? Looking up from his state of concentration, Rust sees the usually entourage of friends: Al, Steve, Kimberly, and the Narrator, who can’t be seen. Jackson Rust: *sighs* Al: Awww...is wittle Jacky upset? As Al curls his bottom lip, for more sarcastic expression, Rust stands up almost immediately, and raises his fist, as if he was going to deck Al, which wouldn’t be too bad of an idea. Al: Hey! Narrator: Sorry. Rather than punching, Rust extends his hand out, chuckling to Al. Whelp; there goes the ‘serious’ portion of this promo. Al: *looks quizzically at the hand* Jackson Rust: Dude…stupid. Al: Huh? Jackson Rust: You finally got me, man! It took you a few years, but you got me! Al: Huh? Jackson puts his hand down to his side, as Kimberly, and Steve looks on. Jackson Rust: Remember waaaaaay back when, when I said you’d never catch me with an insult, joke, limerick, funny saying, expression, sarcastic remark, curt remark, and etcetera, etcetera? Al: Umm…no? Jackson Rust: Well then…it looks like it’s time for an OLD FASHIONED FLASHBACK! Steve: Sh-shit. Kimberly: You said that right. As the scene begins to get all wobbly like, thanks to the newest form of special effects, the Jacksonrustflashbackoramamachineofdoom, the posse look at each other, before deriving into a flashback. Al: CRAAAAAAAAAP! As we enter a new world, one of black and white coloring (just for the “waaaaaaay back when” feeling), we come to see Jackson Rust, and Al in what looks to be around age fifteen or so. Jackson Rust: I bet that you’ll never catch me with an insult, joke, limerick, funny saying, expression, sarcastic remark, curt remark, and etcetera, etcetera. Al: You’re on. Jackson Rust: Dumbass. Al: Why are we doing this? Jackson Rust: In case of the future. After all, it’s not everyone who will eventually grow up to go into the futuristic wrestling company by the name of Primetime Championship Wrestling, and succeed…and by succeeding, cutting promos that involve coming back to the past, in this scene, to relive this exact moment. Al: Oh. Jackson Rust: Yeah~! Al: Are there such things as tildes, or tilde abuse in the future? Jackson Rust: I~! don’t~! know~! Al: Tilde abuser~! Jackson Rust: Ha! You just abused a tilde of your own~! Al: Dammit~! I’m a tilde abuser~! Al begins to pout. Jackson Rust: Suck it up you crybaby tilde abuser~! Al: You’re violating futuristic tilde abuse, too~! Jackson Rust: Yeah, but I don’t care~! Al: Crap~! Jackson Rust: Jackass~! Al: Let’s cut the tilde abuse, and go back to the present scene, eh~! Jackson Rust: Let’s~! As the younger versions of Jackson Rust and Al nod, the scene cuts from black and white, to color picture (surround sound where available)! Now, back the present, the promo continues. Jackson Rust: See…I did say that. Al: Meh… Narrator: You should be fired for abusing the Almighty tilde. Kimberly: Tilde abusers. Steve: Y-y-yeah. Jackson Rust: Now, Steve…we don’t want to see the flashback of you getting your head pounded in by a certain Mr. Ali, do you? Before you had the legendary stutter? Steve: N-no. Kimberly: When DID you get your stutter? Narrator: Yeah…you speak like you play air hockey…sort of jittery. Al: Hey! It’s not cool to mock the disabled. Narrator: Bite me. Al: *sighs* Kimberly: If there aren’t any more interruptions! Kimberly is seen holding a leather whip in hand, smacking her hand intimidating like. Jackson Rust: Bondage time~! *CRACK-A-WHIP* *SMACK-A-TILDE-ABUSE* *SMACK-A-PERV* Jackson Rust: That’s enough! Sorry! As Jackson holds his face and miscellaneous body parts, Kimberly urges Steve to continue, or…start the story. Steve: I-it w-was a d-da-dark a-and s-st-stor-stormy n-ni-night— Kimberly: Narrator, please tell us the story, so we don’t have to wait for Steve to spit out the words, and the promo can be done with. Al: It’s not cool to mock the disab— *SMACK-AN-INTERRUPTER* Al: You’ve been reading too many PCW results. *SMACK-A-NO!* Jackson grabs the whip from Kimberly’s hand, and chucks it backwards, promptly followed by the shattering of what could be a priceless glass artifact given to Jackson from his ancestors. Jackson Rust: *cringes* The rest of the crew looks to what broke, as Jackson just sits down… Jackson Rust: Shit. Narrator: Anyway…on with the story. As the glass shards continue to litter the floor, the posse turns around to listen to the story of how Steve got his stutter. Narrator: Like I was saying… Kimberly: Continue. Al: Yeah, go ahead. Steve: Y-yeah. Jackson Rust: *continues to cringe* Narrator: Like Steve spit out before…it was a dark and stormy night…just like tonight… Al: But it’s daylight…and partially cloudy. Narrator: Would you shut up! *POOF* Just like that, Al has major layers of ultra strong duct tape crisscrossing around his mouth, preventing him from any more ridiculing, belittling comments. Narrator: That’s what you get. Al: Mmmpphhhh…mmmphh… Kimberly: What was that? Al’s Thoughts: Fuck you. Al: Mmmmpph…mmmou. Steve: Huh? Al’s Thoughts: …die, bitch. Al: …mmmphi…mmmph. Narrator: This is hilarious. Hey, Jackson…want to take a shot at amusing yourself at the expense of Al? Jackson Rust: Sure~! Kimberly: Tilde abuse~! Steve: Y-yeah~! Jackson Rust: Steve, if you have a stutter, wouldn’t the tilde have to stutter itself out as well? Kimberly: It’s just an act, isn’t it! Steve: N-no! Narrator: How ‘bout this…I threaten you with the glove, and will hit you if you lie. The glove appears, and menacingly waves across Steve’s line of vision. Steve: …o-oh-okay. Narrator: Good. Kimberly: Yes…very much so. Al: Mmmphh, mmmphh. Jackson Rust: How about that we all don’t put in our “yes’s”, and stuff, eh? Kimberly: Ruin all the fun, why don’t you. Narrator: Moving along to business…Steve, is your whole stutter— *SMACK* Narrator: He’s lying! He’s lying! As the glove viciously slaps at Steve with brutal bitch slaps, the rest of the crew watch on, as Al sits down, pissed off, and looking around for something to tear the tape off with. Jackson Rust: He hasn’t given an answer yet. Narrator: Oh…sorry about that. The glove helps Steve to his feet, as now Steve looks pissed off, and searching around for something to kill the narrator with. Narrator: Now…Steve, is your stutter just an act? Steve: N-no, m-man. Narrator: I don’t know. Steve: I-I h-have a s-s-st-st-stut-stutter. The glove smacks Steve upside his head, and Steve goes down in pain. Steve: Ow-ouch. The glove comes back and slaps him again, but this time…Steve no-sells. Narrator: Eeep! No-sell ‘o’ doom~! The glove smacks Steve in the head again, but again…Steve no-sells. Jackson Rust: He’s better than Triple H and Hulk Hogan put together! As Steve menacingly waves his finger at the glove, he begins to intimidating-like flex his muscles, or lack thereof, before leaping onto the glove, and beginning a brawl. Steve hits a closed fist to the glove’s index finger, but the glove comes back with a thumb to the head. Steve: My stutter! It’s fucking gone! The glove comes back, and hits him with another thumb. Steve: B-ba-bastard! All of a sudden, the glove disappears. Kimberly: Where’d it go? Narrator: It’s effecting my typing skills. Al: Oh. Jackson Rust: How’d you get out of the tape? Al points to the vacuum cleaner that is sitting in the center of the room. Al: I made the thing suck the tape off of my face. Jackson Rust: Right. Narrator: You know that this promo had to goals to accomplish…and not one of them was done? Kimberly: Being which? Narrator: The whole Chris Storm/Tessa Windsor/Steel Chair segment, and the story of how Steve got his stutter. Jackson Rust: Oh. Al: See what happens when I can’t talk? Bad things happen. Jackson Rust: Shut up. Kimberly slaps Al about the face. Al: D-d-damn-it. Steve: T-th-that’s m-my s-sk-skit, b-b-bit-bitch! The scene again, and as usual…fades out.
