I Got You Under My Skin

13 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Title: I Got You Under My Skin Featuring: Xavier Kannon Date: 13/04/03 Xavier Kannon sits alone in the dark, contemplating his life as it is now. With his leather chair pulled towards the ceiling to floor windows in his Hubbardville apartment, the lights of the Scientological city highlight his chiselled features with pale light. Not often does Xavier sit like this, at least not nowadays. But at Fusion, something happened that got to him more than he’ll ever be willing to admit. They cheered him. It might not seem a lot, but put yourself in Kannon’s shoes, if anyone would really want to. For 14 months all they’ve given you is jeering and abuse. Some insults they’d throw at you would make you blush. Right now the saying “You don’t know what you’ve got ‘til it’s gone” is rather fitting. It’s 2:19 in the morning, he can’t sleep. And even if he did drift into slumber, he knows what he would be dreaming. Or maybe Nightmare would be a more appropriate description. Hurling insults at him, the PCW fans never got to him before, but all it took was one burst of applause to rattle him. So now, he’s left thinking about that moment, the city he helped build below him. “Er... Hello? You do, like, realise that it’s SO not awake time and stuff?” The peace that was surrounding him is shattered as a tired looking Eleanor walks into view behind him, her bare feet making a unique sound on the recently varnished floorboards. Scarce light reflecting off her pink silk robe, Ellie~! approaches him, Kannon not moving nor reacting to her presence. All he can do is look onto the sleeping city. Eleanor : “Don’t you, like, think it’s a bit odd just sitting there and being all brooding and stuff?” She’s now standing right behind his chair, her hands resting on the back. Leaning into the slightly brighter light we can make her out a bit more clearly. Her stylised face sitting between flowing blonde and pink streaked hair. Eleanor : “Wait.... You aren’t Batman are you?” Not even he can resist Eleanor’s close proximity for long, her speculative yet totally off the wall question making him break into a smile, almost chuckling. Xavier : “No, I’m not Batman, my little Poon Puppy.” His right hand reaches out onto the masterfully crafted table beside the chair where a glass sits atop a coaster, protecting the surface of the beautiful furniture. It’s been there for some time, the ice cubes having nearly melted entirely into the burned amber liquor. Knocking it back in one, Kannon’s face flinches for a second as it warms it’s way down his throat. Eleanor : “So what are you doing? Plotting World Domination, because that would be WAY cool! I can be President of Ellieland.” At least his wife is taking his mind of the events of Fusion, she’s generally good for things like that. She grounds him, nothing can seem to overbearing or serious when Eleanor is around. Xavier : “I’m just thinking.” Well, he was trying to. Eleanor sets off, walking around the chair as Kannon’s eyes migrate to the stars. To many, Kannon may seem big. He’s famous, fairly wealthy, has exposure. But as arrogant as some say he is, Xavier knows he’s a mere speck on something microscopic in the Universe. Eleanor : “Thinking is, like, SO boring. I tried it once and YAWWWN!” As she acts out the long drawn out yawn, she drops down onto his lap. Her robe just having enough to it to reserve her modesty. Yawning again, this time naturally, she wraps her arms around him. Smiling, eyes closed and letting out a breathless sigh, she finds a comfortable place to rest her head on his shoulder. Having found it, she fidgets to get fully comfortable, Kannon just playing with her hair with his right hand. After kissing the top of her head tenderly, he goes back to his contemplation. He’ll never tell a soul, and some probably wouldn’t believe him if he did, but Fusion had him thinking back to when they cheered for him every show. When “Rock is Dead” was met with a pop. When he wrestled and took risks for their adulation. He whispers to himself, not wanting to wake Ellie who’s drifted right off. Xavier : “Why did you have to do that, J...” The thing that really gets him is he doesn’t know how to feel about it. One burst of cheering wasn’t enough to make him suddenly decide he wants to repent, but on the other hand he didn’t loath it... No matter how he acted. It was just.... Happening. Not often, if ever, do we see the Scientologist dither, or sit on the fence, or be undecided. But in this case he honestly doesn’t know what to think. Xavier : “Couldn’t you of just hit me with a chair or something?” It wasn’t like this last year. Spring Fling then was a lot simpler, 2 men who had been tearing one another to shreds for weeks finally settling it inside a steel cage. They bled, they bruised, they ached. Now it’s complicated. Xavier : “At least that’s just instinct.” Maybe, just maybe, he wants to say sorry. Somewhere deep down he could have feelings of regret, couldn’t he? Questions, that’s the problem. Too many questions. His head drops back, staring up a the ceiling and the jolting waking Eleanor from her fragile sleep. Moaning at being awoken, Ellie~! recoils her arms from round her man and stretched them above her head, yawning. Eleanor : “Are you, like, still thinking?” Shaking it off, Kannon decides that it’s effected him enough, time to move on. Xavier : “Not anymore, my little Shag Squirrel... Coming back to bed?” She nods tiredly, Kannon scooping her up in his arms as he stands. Walking away from the view over his sleeping city, Xavier carries his sleepy wife back to bed, hoping to quench the thoughts running through his head. Will he dream of the cheering? That all depends on his tired Eleanor is and how much sleep he’ll end up getting tonight. ---- View Xavier Kannon's Biography
