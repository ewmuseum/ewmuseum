Family Matter pt3- It Continues

11 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11th April [OOC Note: It's short because I'm at a loss for time and it's simply for some character building.] 'What the hell do you mean I'm in Utah?' says the crazed voice of The Mexican Devil, El Diablo. Diablo steps out of the limo he hired and shakes his head. 'What did I do to deserve to be in this useless state?' he comments. Diablo runs both his hands through his dyed red hair and walks up to the limo driver's door, taps the window and instantly the driver rolls the window down. 'Yes, sir?' the driver responds politely. Diablo leans into the window so the driver will definitely hear him. 'Leave my bags in the car and circle around Salt Lake City if you so wish, just be back here by 10pm Jeeves,' El Diablo says The driver looks mad but simply nods. Diablo slowly starts to walk away from the limo, and in the distance a voice is heard. 'My name's not Jeeves, you dimwit,' the limo driver aptly responds before driving away. Diablo though, just laughs and speaks to himself. 'I'm the dimwit? Ha, I'm not the one who won't be getting paid,' he continues to laugh. El Diablo is walking around the streets of Salt Lake City looking for a decent place to eat, but is coming to no avail. 'Does this vermin ridden town not know about good food?' he asks himself out loud. Nonetheless he keeps on looking and as he does he gets interrupted by a homeless man wearing dirty, smelly, and ripped clothing, begging for money. 'Got any spare change?' the young and skinny homeless black man asks. Diablo looks down at him, not just physically but hierachially, he then looks at his own clothes of a black jacket, black denim jeans and sneakers and then at the clothes of the homeless man. 'Sure I do buddy,' Diablo comments, 'But you ain't getting a dime, go get a job you pathetic waste of life,' he finishes harshly. The Mexican Devil slowly walks away, carrying on looking for a decent restaurant. 'Hey man, a simple, 'no' would have sufficed,' the homeless man shouts back at him, before going into a fit of coughing. El Diablo though really doesn't care and just carries on walking, until he gets interrupted once more, much to his annoyance. Standing in front of him and halting his path is a middle aged woman, with long grey hair and many, many wrinkles. She is wearing a large fur coat that covers her from top to bottom in this cold night in Utah. 'I saw and heard that,' the woman exclaims, 'It was wrong, don't you know that man could have been Jesus testing you?' she continues to say. Diablo smirks. 'Why would a superstar like me care if it was Jesus?' he replies cynically, 'I don't believe in Jesus, I don't believe in the afterlife and better yet I don't really care about homeless people who are too lazy to get off their asses and get a job,' Diablo rants. The woman, however doesn't look angry and just places her right hand on Diablo's left shoulder. 'I forgive you,' the woman says politely before walking past him. El Diablo remains standing there dumbfounded by what just happened for a few moments, before shaking his head and carrying on his journey. 'I hate this city and state already, the only solice in this is that Flaymin Aymin is nowhere near me,' Diablo says under his breath. Further along the street in which he is walking he sees a man with an afro. 'What the hell is this street? Get on Diablo's nerves street?' he states. Diablo shakes his head as he walks past the man with the afro and deliberately places his foot out so the man trips over it and falls harshly onto the ground. Raul slowly turns around and stops walking. 'NEVER PICK AN AFRO FOR A HAIRCUT!' the crazed Mexican yells loudly. He then spits on the man and carries on walking away, as the fallen man is bleeding heavily from the nose and his forehead. 'Great, what more can happen?' he carries on talking to himself. Suddenly Diablo hears a ringing. El Diablo stands still and pulls out his cell phone from his pocket and proceeds to answer it. 'Yeah?' 'No...no we aren't gonna meet up again,' 'No...ok fine, have it your way. Meet me in Indiana, it's where I am next,' 'If you can't make it then forget it,' 'So, you'll be there then?' 'Right, bye and don't call me brother.' Diablo then cancels the call and puts his cell phone away after speaking to his half-sister Patty, briefly. 'I hate my life,' Diablo exclaims.
