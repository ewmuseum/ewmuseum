Good Karma left behind

1 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/1/03 He had arrived at the home of this week’s Turmoil, Chapel Hill in North Carolina yesterday. Something in his stomach told him that he and the rest of the so called Player Haters had forgotten something back in Raleigh where last week’s PCW installment was held. It wasn’t quite that all too familiar sinking feeling but instead a known uneasiness that had to be resolved. So after arriving at Chapel Hill Knox had declined lunch with the rest of the Player Haters, he instead went to the nearest Enterprise rent a car and rented a comfy, modest, American made Lincoln Town Car. He had wanted a Cadillac but the town car was the best they could do for him. So Knox paid the fee to have the car for 48 hours, grabbed his carry bag full of CD’s and now found himself speeding back to the home of the NC State Wolfpack. This was a goodwill mission. Knox shuffled through a pile of compact discs scattered on the passenger seat of the car. He grabbed a one of his newer CD’s “Killswitch Engage: The End of Heartache”. It was a new CD by friends from his hometown of Boston. Knox had quickly fallen for the CD. It was the perfect blend of near relentless brutality with short melodic breakdowns was a great soundtrack for his life and a near perfect analogy for his wrestling style. He slid the CD into the cars compact disc player and hit the random play button. The LCD screen blinked like eyes focusing on daylight after a long nights sleep before reading the number “7-The End of Heartache”. In synch with music’s melodic, powerful opening guitar riff, Knox tossed his Boston Red Sox cap onto the passenger seat atop the pile of CDs. He rubbed his unkempt hair almost letting it breath in a way. Knox released a deep breath and continued rubbing his head trying to relieve his own tension while half heartedly keeping his focus on the task ahead…driving.“This distance, this disillusion, like links to memories while falling.Sleep brings relief and the hope of a new day, waking the misery of being without you.” The smooth call of the voice on the CD rang out. As always, any other time he had heard a song referring to the past and memories. He immediately felt his heart tear into two and the thought of his wife Amy came to him like a blot of lighting. She was the most important thing in his life, but for now, he had to keep her locked away in a deep, dark part of his mind and heart. Where only he had the key to unlock. His love for her was the greatest feeling he ever had, but became his greatest pain and most obvious weakness. Knox took a deep breath pushing back his heart from his throat. He wanted to reach for his cell phone and call her. Not now, now was not the time.“For comfort, for solace, for completion, I’ll be waiting for the end of my broken heart”Knox found the words transcending from the speakers directly to his mind. The words formed questions in his head that he already had the answer to. His comfort? His solace? His sense of completion? That process began at Turmoil this week in Chapel Hill. Sure he came back to help Boda but make no mistake about it. He came back to win the world title. The one thing that was stolen from him by a crazy Indian in a Hum V and a Surge soda addict, then kept from him by the powers that be in the PCW. The World title sat in front of him from day 1 in PCW but it always sat just inches from his grasp. The future of Ryan Knox would no longer be held hostage. He had more motivation than he ever needed, The woman he loved had left him, The company he made his mark in was in the control of Jon Kano, a modern day tyrant, and now like never before he had an army of fans behind him.It was somewhat ironic to him that he once left the PCW filled with hatred for the company and what they had done to him, Now in his head, if for nothing more than his own piece of mind. He was back to rescue the PCW from stagnant ratings and similar management tyrant that nearly killed the PCW years ago, that being Tyler Nelson. Two years ago Knox had the chance to put a cold hard end to Media Inc. he defeated Nelson in a brutal match, gained control of PCW, only to sell his soul for another shot at PCW glory. He would not make the same mistake with the Primetime Players. He will not stop until they were through. Nearly one week ago with his friend Boda by his side, They took a huge chunk out of the Primetime Players armor when they defeated Kris Kanton and Kano in a tag match. It felt wonderful to be in the ring again. Knox was high from the adrenaline that night. Planting Kanton with the FutureShock was the only thing that topped walking out the curtain initially to the ocean of cheering fans. Going into Turmoil Knox knew he was in the head of Kris Kanton. Kanton hadn’t been seen or heard from the week leading up to Turmoil. In his heart, Knox had taken out one member of the Primetime Players that night. That left three more Primetime Players to be eliminated on his journey to the World Title. “Surrender! Give In!”The screaming chant filled the car. Thoughts of Jesse Ramey flew to mind. Knox slightly gritted his teeth and clenched his free hand for a moment. The mere thought of someone standing in his way filled him with intense fury. The metal stylings of Killswitch Engage weren’t going to help calm him down. Why couldn’t Jesse Ramey see what was ahead of him and if he could see what was ahead of him, Why hadn’t he run yet. At 5’11 205 pounds, Jesse Ramey stacked up to him like Geicko car insurance Lizzard standing next to a Raptor. Knox didn’t have any personal ill will towards Ramey, Well....Only if you don’t consider trying to steal someone’s well being from them ill will.“I don’t respect him, I don’t care about him, he’s done nothing in this business” Knox pounded into his head for the sole reason of mentally preparing himself for his match in a few days.Ramey had recently talked about boot camp at a young age. Knox felt some sadness for his counterpart; A rough childhood was no stranger to him. Sadly for ole Jesse Ramey he was about to be introduced to a new version of boot camp. “My boot camping laces high up his ass” Knox commented to himself of the beating he had in preparation for Jesse Ramey.Knox remembered when he was 17, he briefly worked for someone with the last name Ramey. He wondered if their were any relation from his former boss Doug Ramey and Jesse Ramey. If so, things were getting bleaker by the instant for Jesse. Knox hated that job. Bussing tables for a terrible wage. For a guy who had a gazillion dollars but refused to pay more than minimum wage. Mean? When it came to mean he gave a hungry pit-bull a run for it’s money. Things were getting worse and worse for Jesse Ramey indeed.“You know me, You know me all too well.” Howard Jones of Killswitch Engage called out full of emotional angst. The fidelity filled his soul and drove his rage toward Ramey closer to his boiling point. He had studied the biography of Jesse Ramey when the match was first signed. Ramey was 28 years old and had already been in 13 or so documented wrestling organizations. Jesse Ramey was clearly nothing more than pro wrestling’s version of the NBA’s Oliver Miller. Good enough to get hired, not good enough to keep the job. Knox reached over and shutdown the CD player. His music of choice on this day was in danger of inspiring road rage. Knox withdrew his arm to his face and began stroking the 4 day shadow that had grown in on his face.“I’m going to break this guy.” Knox shook his head in disbelief, he did not respect Ramey, and in fact Knox didn’t respect 90% of the PCW. This new age of wrestler didn’t know what it meant to earn your keep. Pausing for a moment to replay, his last passing thoughts, Knox came to a conclusion.“I’m officially the oldest 29 year old on the planet, talking about these youngsters don’t know how to earn their keep. God what’s gotten into me?”It did seem somewhat ludicrous considering Knox himself debuted by running a world title contender over with a fifty thousand dollar truck. Shaking himself from his dazing thoughts, Knox looked up and took notice of the “Rex Hospital” sign up ahead. He quickly spun the hard right into the hospital lot, signaling a chorus of horns and vulgarity passing behind him.“Guess I should try the directional blinker next time” Knox giggled to himself.The lot seems filled to capacity. Knox wondered why a hospital lot would be filled like it was black Friday at the mall of America.“What’s goin on in NC man?” he wondered to himself“Healthcare be boomin and shit”Up ahead Knox spotted a car pulling out of a space. He waited for the car to pull out and as the space cleared, he began to turn into the spot. Suddenly a sky blue Buick slowly pulled in front of him into the space.“Oh hell no…”His jaw gaped open in disbelief that someone nonchalantly pulled into his space like that. It was clear to him and anyone with eyes he was there first and waiting for the other car to pull out.“I’m going to piledrive this person through their trunk.”The door to the blue Buick slowly kicked open. Two tiny legs followed the door. Brown walking shows and navy blue slacks came out in toe. Slowly the culprit exited the car. A woman approximately 4’11, 77 years old slowly closed the car door behind her. She looked over at the furious pro wrestler and gave a quaint little smile.“c*nt, bitch, f**khead, whore, motherbitch” The curse words flooded his head. They quickly rushed to his mouth and just as they were about to exit his mouth he realized.“Damn it…. I’m a “face” , I’m over…. I can’t break kayfabe now”With that, He signaled a friendly wave to the old women.As she slowly shuffled by Knox, the women reached for something on her key chain::bleep, bleep::“Better not do anything but look, you blond bitch.” The old woman muttered under her breath as she shuffled away.“Awww..Awwww” Choking on his own words as he overheard the woman.What was going on in North Carolina, Hospitals were packed and old women stole parking spots, had car alarms and talked shit under their breath. Knox circled around the front past the fire lane where a large Van sat. A middle aged man sat smoking a cigarette reading a magazine. Already running hot because of Grandma larceny, Knox could’ve done without the van blocking half the right lane, making it a tight squeeze for him to drive by. Knox pulled up aside the van, rolling down his passenger window. The man in the car looked over, noticing Knox signaling him to roll down his window.“Can I help you?” The man with the cigarette asked.“I was wondering, do you own this lot?” Knox asked…“Huh?! No” The man replied“THEN WHY ARE YOU PARKING LIKE YOU DO!!” Knox shoutedHe then calmly drove away leaving the inconsiderate person in his wake.“How long am I going to have to look for a damn spot?”His business at the hospital was important, But was it this important? He figured chances to really build his good karma didn’t come along often so he better take advantage of having already made the drive back when his good spirits were intact, But good lord was invoking the good karma going to be tough, if he didn’t eventually find somewhere to park……To Be Continued
