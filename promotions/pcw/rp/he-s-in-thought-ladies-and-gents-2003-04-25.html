He's in thought ladies and gents...

25 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 25 April A man can only go so far in life, every sat there, staring into a mirror, into YOUR eyes, thinking, ‘who am I?’ and getting this dull, lifeless feeling throbbing through your body. Sivasian had to do something, had to make an impact on the federation. He wanted more to prove to himself that the sniper wasn’t a mere one off job for himself. There wasn’t much money left in the world to pay a sniper for legal reasons. He’d tried shooting rabbits In the cornfields of Yugoslavia, only to find he was paid less than a Chinese factory worker. He felt the need to prove to the world that there was life in the Ukraine, in a country where nothing goes the way of the rest of the world. Sure, Japan still bitches that the states dropped the atomic bomb on it. Hell, you even know what happened in the Ukraine, an incident so horrific that people are still suffering the effects. A nuclear power station exploded, had a meltdown, the equivalent of 7 atomic bombs hitting the one place in the world it appears no one cares about, Ukraine. What’s more, Sivasian is wrestling for himself, and for the money. Not a pathetic $10,000,000 a year, let me live in a luxury neighborhood with security gates type money, but wanting to give money to his wife, family and kids. Sivasian was left with nothing when he left the Ukrainian army. Nothing at all. He had savings, but that’s about it. These went towards a newly made house in some cheap suburb of New York. He has nothing no more, and yet, he still has no visa. The thought, however, of Americans paying for him to stay in their country horrifies him. He sat, lonely in the midday sun, staring into the bright yellow and golden sun lying above the tree tops. Stillness rode through his body. He was wearing the usual leather coat, the sniping gloves, and as per usual, Americans were staring at him, having a go, laughing, sniggering. He felt like snapping, like shouting ‘You want some?’ and almost definitely breaking their necks, but this would prove them right, and break his whole cool, calm persona. Most of all, it would lead to an arrest, and money would be paid for bail, where would he get the money? He posed the question to himself as to how he could, with so many skills, the ability to fight, the ability to shoot, an excellent array of 6 or 7 language skills. Someone so intelligent, who lives in a country of such ignorance, and downright unintelligent. How a trailer park hick, with racism at his core, can earn thousands in benefits AND still have a job, and yet he has nothing, at least till his visa clears. The sweat slowly drips down him. He hates the heat like a brooding dog hates getting muted, and yet he doesn’t move. He doesn’t move for the bottle of water on his side, he sits, staring vaguely in the direction of a flag pole, and onto the beauty of the son and trees against it. His wife walks over, and sits down, she’s seen him in this thoughtful mood before. He wouldn’t harm a butterfly in this mood, and yet he still would demolish anything for his family. Yet he hated this place, he hated touching American soil, and yet, in an ironic way, touching American soil, of the people he hated, was the only way he could possibly let the world know what happened, and truly get down to the bottom of what was happening. But he couldn’t just use the wrestling federation as a place to lay down his plans to try and discover the murderers of his parents. He twitches a little and has a cold stern stare on his face as he thinks of his parents. He still fully blames the states, but has never truly revealed why. ‘Hey honey, ‘ starts his wife, ‘let’s go, we’ve got a 3 hour drive to get to the arena, we should go early, I thought we could stop on the way for something to eat.’ He nods and smiles, ‘you’ve always been there for me, haven’t you?’ he replies. ‘Only because I love you…’ she leaves it hanging in the air. ‘But do you, or are you with me for something else, money, fame, there has got to be something, or am I being that paranoid?’ he says, still staring up ahead, no where near his wife. ‘Paranoia,’ his wife says, cuddling upto his arm, ‘I love you for being you, not for being anyone else.’ ‘Of course,’ he says, ‘what am I thinking about. You’re you, and I’m me. You think I have anything to worry about with this SOCIETY and this Egyptian man? Ones quick, ones a lummox.’ His wife laughs a little, but Sivasian still doesn’t budge, ‘You’ve got nothing to worry about, my darling. After all, you’ve got the lot, you’ve got the package o completo!’ she says in a shrieking voice. For once Sivasian turns towards her, ‘Please, don’t do that again.’ She nods back, wanting, willing to say something, but waiting for her husband to make the first move. ‘You know something, I can’t believe the Americans are going to be paying me, and that money’s going to be going towards a house to live in America.’ He says at last, clenching his fist, still in deep thought. ‘Honey, are you not hot in that ridiculous coat and gloves, in this beautiful 80 degree weather?’ she asks back, rather randomly. He doesn’t reply, and still clenches his fist, his wife bites her bottom lip, ‘Honey I said…’ ‘I HEARD WHAT YOU SAID.’ He shouts back, rather shocking his wife. She recoils a bit. ‘Siv! You shouted at me!’ she replies in a high voice. ‘I know honey, I’m sorry, but I’ve got some stuff I need to get off my chest. Honey, go start the car, I’ll meet you in 10 minutes at this bench.’ Still moderately scared, his wife takes off, as he sits, staring ahead. He cocks his head slightly and stares at the American flag, proudly blowing in the sunny, hot, breeze day. ‘Damn Americans, ‘ he mutters, ‘You’ll pay, you’ll pay.’
