The killing starts up again.....

14 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 14-5-03 Mark looks down at the red rag with the faded black oil spills in it. He tosses the rag between both of his hands, catching it and the transferring it over to the other hand. The captors are just sitting up in the front having a good time and laughing. “There is this girl who is not getting pleased that much by her man. So her man goes out of town for a week and she hears the doorbell ring.” The driver captor starts to say. “She goes to the door to find this handsome man standing out there. She lets him in and then you know they start to have sex and then they go to sleep that night. The next morning she goes and finds out that the man next to her is not there anymore.” “Is that all?” The passenger captor asks. “No you moron.” The driver captor says as the black captor in the back is laughing at the two. “Well then the day after the guy ran away the doorbell rings again. She comes down the stairs to look out the peephole to see another guy who is standing there smiling. She lets him in and you know they go up to her room and they have sex. After that he starts to beat her and call her a whore and everything you know. So she kicks him out of the house. Now both of these guys were good lovers so she is happy with their love life, just not their attitudes. So she wakes up the next morning to the sound of the doorbell once again. She goes down the stairs and she looks out the peephole again. She doesn’t see anyone so she opens up the door. To look out and find a guy in a wheel chair.” “The guy says, well I have no legs so I can’t run away, and I have no arms so I can’t beat you.” The lady says “then what good can you do for me?” The guy responds “I rang the doorbell didn’t I?” All three of the captors crack up laughing as the guy in the back seat can hardly sit still. The guy in the passenger’s seat has to press his hand up against the padded part over the airbag to keep his body up right. The driver can hardly drive as his hands are turning the wheel this way and that way. Mark is sitting in the back thinking the best time to strike is. ”I can feel this is the moment. My hands are starting to sweat, but the adrenaline inside of me is running high that nothing is going to stop me. Size doesn’t matter, all it is is brain power and that’s what I have over this fucking coon. Coons are not smart people and yet will never be as good as us.” Mark thinks in his mind. Mark peeps his head over the back seat once again as he looks to see what everyone is doing and they are all still laughing. The coon is rocking back and forth to try and catch his breath. Mark gets up on the balls of his feet to balance himself for leverage so that he can get the power. His kneecaps start to shake as his feet are getting tired and are unsteady. Mark takes the rag in his right hand as the oil smell from the rag is getting on the white skin of Mark. He grips it into a cup and the coon has his knees in his stomach right now, bent over. Mark’s head starts to drip sweat down along his forehead and are going down right for his eyes. The coon captor comes back and Mark sees his shot so he takes the rag and he puts it over the coon’s mouth, while with his other hand he pinches the nose close so that the captor can’t get any oxygen. The other captors in the front seat are laughing too loud to hear the coon scream and waste his last seconds to live on that. ”What is this feeling?” The coon captor asks himself as he can’t think right now. “My eyes are popping out of my head; my chest is caving in and NO ONE CAN HEAR ME!” “This is why white power rules.” Mark starts to whisper in the captor’s ear. “You should feel honored to be in this position right now. I mean if I was you I would be honored to have a white guy killing me. Ah yes it would be an honor, and this is just for what your coon fucking community has done to me. I’ve had to get surgery on my jaw and then people would hide in dark alley ways and jump me. Just coons’ piss me right the fuck off and your paying for it right now. I have killed lots of your people and I will continue to do so until you all realize that the white power is the best around. I just can’t help myself; I have this great feeling inside of me that won’t go away when I kill one of you. I get this adrenaline rush that just goes through my body and I just love the feeling. It’s like cigarettes, once you have one it just keeps on getting worse and worse and you keep on smoking more and more. That’s the way with me killing off all the minorities. White power rules.” The coon drops down to the padded seat like a rock. Mark hops over the seat as he gets to the coon to check his neck for a pulse. Mark feels nothing in his neck as the signs are death right now. Mark takes his right arm as he checks a pulse in the wrist, but finds again there is nothing there and there is a death in the van. Mark feels his forehead as its already getting down in temperature, and his facial expression is gone to a straight face. Mark smiles as he looks at the damage he has done to the coon. Mark heads up to the two captors up in the front of the van as he goes up there to see what they are doing. They are just getting over from their little laughing episode. “Hey fucks.” Mark says as both of the men look back with a scare expression on both of their faces.
