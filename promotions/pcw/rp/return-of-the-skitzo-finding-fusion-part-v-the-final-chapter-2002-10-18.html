Return of the Skitzo: Finding Fusion Part V - The Final Chapter: Saving Scruffy!

18 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 18-10-02 Skitzo sat twiddling his thumbs. Generally fidgeting about with himself and waiting impatiently for this train was getting on his nerves, even watching all the interesting people passing him by wasn’t sufficing as a way to pass the time. His backpack was his only form of companionship on this foreign venture, and even that only contained some clothes, deodorant, The Big Book of Wrestling Moves and-… Doo-doo-doo-doo Da-da-da-da-da Dee-dee-dee-dee-dee-…DOOOO!!! -…and a cellular phone. Skitzo cocked his head, not remembering having packed a cell phone, but he didn’t own one anyway? Scrounging around in the backpack, Skitzo pulled out the sleek little phone, opening the phone and holding it to his ear. Skitzo: Hello? The voice on the other end of the phone belonged to a rather irate sounding woman of about 40. Skitzo listened, his eyes suddenly widened as the news was delivered to him. Skitzo: Turmoil? What the-…but I haven’t even been on Fusion ye-…WHAT?! Skitzo: Are you kidding me? Skitzo: Where the FUCK is Brunei Darussalam?!?!?! Well I don’t give a shit if I’m booked, what the-…ok yes I know I signed the contract-…well I-…look if you just-…ok calm down now…well…ok, look-… Skitzo: -…WOULD YOU JUST FUCK THE SHUT UP AND-..erm…SHUT THE FUCK UP AND LISTEN TO ME FOR ONE GODDAMN MINUTE!!!!!!!!! The entire train station fell silent as Skitzo cursed to the heavens over the cell phone. Skitzo: Ok I understand you’re busy…so I’m booked for Turmoil in Brunei Darussalam?……………how do I get there then?……………take a left where? Oh look I know I signed the contract and my first match is Friday, yes…I know…Syko and Chef and Kramer Wild Wing? Awwww ok look I’ll tell you what…Kannon told you to phone me huh…………new guy I see……planted a phone because you knew I’d get lost-…LOOK YOU OLD BIDDY! IF BEING EMPLOYED BY SOME BRITISH GUY WHO TAKES ORDERS FROM A HIPTASTIC MONARCH GETS YOU THIS FRUSTRATED IN ONE PHONE CALL I SUGGEST YOU STICK THAT RECEIVER UP YOUR ASS AND LET IT RING ‘TIL YOU GET A GODDAMN TUMOR IN YOUR GODDAMN RECTUM………………………ok I’ll be there on Tuesday, bye sweetie! BEEP! Skitzo: Nice move smart ass…hang up on the bitch! Skitzo: I didn’t hang up I said goodbye… Skitzo: She was three-quarters of the way through a motherfucker before you hung up on her! Skitzo: Serves the bitch right… Skitzo: Mind don’t diss Kannon or he be all up in yo’ face like flies on a mookie stank! Skitzo: 10-4 on that one… Skitzo sighs and planks the phone inside his jacket pocket. Hooking the backpack over his shoulder, Skitzo sits up; readying himself to get up and leave and wander around a little before the train arrives. As he stands up however, Skitzo feels something pawing against his leg. Upon looking down, Skitzo finds some kind of creature wagging its tail by his foot. Skitzo: What the fuck is that… Skitzo: A foul tongue… Skitzo: No that! Skitzo: A mop? Skitzo nudges the thing with his foot, and surely enough it pips out, barking pathetically at Skitzo, who finally gives in to temptation and kneels down to further examine the creature. Petting its head and pulling the hair to the side, Skitzo gasps as he realises what it is… Skitzo: A dog! Skitzo: What kind? Skitzo: Hairyus Bastardus Skitzo: I know what you are! You’re a-… SHITZO!!! OH MA GOSCH!!! SHITZO!!! EET IZ SCHOO!!! Skitzo’s eyes widen in horror as he catches out of the corner of his eye a rather plump fellow waddling across the station towards him. Plump fellow: SHITZO!!! I AM BEEG PEE CEE DUBBLE-YOO FAN!!! Skitzo recoils in horror as the large boy comes waddling furiously across the station, and skids to a halt before him. Removing a pad of paper and a pen from inside his rather tightly fitting jacket, the boy holds the pen and paper before Skitzo, with a bright smile across his face. Plump fellow: I AM TO BE WANTING YOU AUTOGLAF SHITZO!!! GIMME-A YOO AUTOGLAF!!! Skitzo sits the dog thing that ran up to him on the seat, and takes the pen and paper. Looking up at the kid with a rather frustrated glare, Skitzo begins jotting down the usual scribbles on the paper to suit the boy’s needs. Skitzo: ………Ok what’s yer name kid? Portly boy: MY NAME IS KYOSHO MARAKYTO………but my friends call me Ky. Skitzo: Ok Ky, well as you’ll see here I have not written out an AUTOGLAF for you as you would have liked, but rather some instructions from me to you… Skitzo hands the piece of paper and the pen back to Ky and sits back with a smug look on his face as Ky looks closely at the handwriting and gasps. Ky: Ohhh thanking you please Mr. Skitzo sir…………Take this…piece of paper…and…and…cr-cr-cr-cr-…cr-am…cram it up your cellul-…celluli-…cellulicious ARSE-AHOLE!!!!!!!!! OHH THANKING YOU SO MUCH MR. SKITZO! IT IS BEST THING I HAVE EVER RECEIVED!!!!!!!!! Ky puts the piece of paper in his pocket and continues smiling at Skitzo, who is now petting the dog he found. Ky decides to take a chance and proceeds to sit down next to the dog, and pets it a couple of times. Skitzo: Ok I gave you an autograph, what else do you want? Skitzo: A cookie? Skitzo: A Dollar? Ky: I just want to sit with you for a while, while I wait for my train, sir! Skitzo: Ok so… Skitzo and Ky sit petting the dog, who seems oblivious to anything going on around it, simply sitting there wagging its tail and blinking through its thick mane of hair. As Skitzo grabs the dog to inspect what breed it could be, he notices a door swinging open on the other side of the station, and what appear to be four chefs running out and towards him. Skitzo: What do they want? Skitzo: A cookie? Skitzo: A Dollar? Ky: They want you Doggie, he very delicious in this part of country… Skitzo: WHAT?! Fuck that they ain’t taking my…erm…Scruffy from me!!! Ky: He no you Scruffy…he Hoo Waa’s Scruffy! At that particular moment the chefs walk up on Skitzo, surrounding him and brandishing kitchen knives and utensils in a public train station…as you do. Chef #1: Give us our doggy back! Skitzo: Dog? Chef #3: Yes!!! Give dog back now! Skitzo: This isn’t a dog… Chef #2: If he no dog, what that wagging at its buttocks! Skitzo: That’s my ponytail, I wear a wig you see… Chef #4: YOU A’READY HAVE HAIR! Skitzo: No I don’t… Chef #1: Then what is that… Skitzo: I don’t know why don’t you-…WHAT THE HELL IS THAT?!!?!?!?! Skitzo points behind the chefs in horror, and as they all turn he takes off, grabbing the dog and his backpack, heading for the locker rooms as the portly fellow watches the chefs turn back around. Chef #1: Where he go? Ky: He weeeent-ahhh…THATTAWAY! Ky points in the direction Skitzo ran, not understanding the American concept of run-the-fuck-away-from-the-knife-wielding-chefs! All four take off in the direction of the locker rooms, and upon pushing past the few people still in there locking their possessions away, begin stalking along the corridor, brandishing their weapons. Passing a skinny little man with an obtuse haircut and an old woman with a large bust, the chefs growl in search of their ingredient and its captor. As they come to the end of the locker room, they notice someone standing with their back to them, and arms in front of them out of their sight. Chef #1 walks up to the person and grabs them by the shoulder, spinning them around to reveal a young French woman with her hands tucked in a hand scarf thingy, a white fuzzy one akin to the dog. Chef #3: SHE HAS-A THE DOGGIE!!! CHOPPY CHOPPY!!! All chefs bring their utensils down at once over the woman’s hands expecting to clean-cut the dog, but instead watch in horror as a fine pair of hands drop to the ground. The French woman looks horrified, and falls unconscious over her own hands from the horror of it all. Chef #4: IT’S A GHASLTY NIGHTMARE! GHASTLY! GHASTLY! GHASTLY! Chef #2: Shuttupi! Where our doggie? Chef #1: Fat boy must have lie…LET’S GO! Chefs together: GO GO POWER CHEFS! As the chefs run out of the locker room, one of the end lockers swings open to reveal a rather cramped Skitzo, with the dog hidden inside his jacket. Skitzo: Motherfucking… Skitzo: Cocksucking… Skitzo: BASTARDS!!! Skitzo: Let’s bizounce up outta dis lockafest! Skitzo pops out of the locker with surprising ease, and with his backpack and the dog, runs off through the station, passing Ky who is being quizzed by the chefs. Chef #1: Where he go? Chef #4: AAAAAAAAAAAAAAH HE WENT THATTAWAY!!! The chefs turns to find Skitzo running away into a large crowd, and follow him, growling and wheezing as the people all around them at the Liyama train station watch on in amusement. They stop in the middle of the crowd and look around. Chef #1 sees a man with white hair walking past and grabs him, but alas it is not Skitzo. Chef #1: DAMN YOU WHITE BOY! White hair man: WOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO!!! Chef #2 and #3 run off in different directions. Chef #2 notices a white lump on the ground and grabs at it, but pulls away nothing but sawdust. Chef #2: Ohhhhhhhh stupid vomit boy! Chef #3 meanwhile sees a man running away from him at high speed with a backpack not unlike Skitzo’s. Taking chase, the chef throws a three-pronged fork at the man, who trips up and falls flat on his face. Chef #3 stops before the man and turns him over, to find only a stranger wearing a black ski mask. As he slaps the man out of disgust, a man in a security guard’s outfit stops beside him and pats him on the shoulder. Security guard: Well done mate, thought we’d never catch the blighter! Chef #3: Brighter? AH GIVE YOU BRIGHTER!!! Unfortunately, hitting a security guard with a frying pan results in 2-4 for assault…leaving only Chef’s #1, #2 and #4 to find Skitzo, who by this time is waiting in queue for the early arrival of his train. Skitzo: I won’t let them hurt you Scruffy! Scruffy: Baaa… Skitzo: Ah you speak different languages too I see! Skitzo continues to wait as off in the distance Chefs #1 and #4 run towards the queue, brandishing their egg whisks. As they jump into the queue however, the angry people before them start throwing them back, until both chefs throw their whisks into the crowd, who part…and watch in horror as the whisks stick into the back of a rather large sumo, who turns around, rather peeved off… Sumo: Ohhhhhhhhhhhhhh that was a beeg a-mistake you make! The two chefs find themselves standing in a puddle of their own delectable fluids as the Sumo’s shadow befalls them, and the next thing they know, they’re crushed underneath the full 856lbs of an angry sumo with egg whisks in his back. Skitzo: That’s three down, one to go… Skitzo: When’s the train arriving… Skitzo: Boards say five more minutes. 4.5 minutes later Skitzo: Almost there… Skitzo: Where’s the other chef… Skitzo: I don’t-… Chef #2: HIYYYYYYYYAAAAAAAA!!!!!!!!! The last chef flies from the heavens, kicking out towards Skitzo below him. A horrified look graces Skitzo’s face, and as the chef reaches within a couple of feet of Skitzo, spinning his knives in his hands wildly…Skitzo steps back and smirks as the chef crashes down onto the rail tracks. Brushing himself off as he gets back up, the chef looks into Skitzo’s eyes with a determined fury about his gaze. Chef #2: Now is time for you to give doggie back! I told you before and I tell you again, you can’t beat Chef! We are immortal! We are good cooks! WE ARE-… HOOOOOOOOOOOONK HOOOOOOOOOOOONK!!!!!!!!!!!!!! Chef #2: OHHHHHMYGOD NO NO NO THE FR’UCKIN TRAIN IS A-ERRLY!!! Skitzo: Ye Damn Skippy… BOOM-SPLAT! ………And with that the last of the Chefs was crushed and buckled under the full 500 tonne of a heavy passenger train. No one really seemed to notice though, and as Skitzo boarded the train and took his seat, he sighed a breath of relief. Skitzo: Well that’s the end of that chapter… Skitzo: Now to get to Fusion! Skitzo: Kick me some arse! Rrrrrrumble! Skitzo looks down at his stomach, and then at Scruffy. A sad look of woe comes over his face as the train pulls off for Tokyo, and for Friday Fusion. By the time the train had pulled into the Tokyo station, Skitzo was looking rather guilty, but nonetheless he was happy with his little adventure, even after he burped up a dog hair or too… THE END!!! SHAAAOOOOOWMMMM MUTHAFUCKA!
