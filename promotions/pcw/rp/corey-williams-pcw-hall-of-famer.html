Corey Williams: PCW Hall of Famer?

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: now CCW and Kano have never exactly seen eye to eye. They have met numerous times in the past and to this date Kano has yet to hold a win over Corey. However although he couldn't win over Corey, he always managed to win over the crowd; he commands great respect and has always gotten it. To Kano, Corey was just another new school punk who had more ego than he did ethic. CCW was nothing but a disrespectful wannabe. To Corey, Kano was just another old school fogy who was better at sucking up to the fans than he was at wrestling. Kano was nothing but an unjustly respected has-been. And though he always constantly bitched about the fans lack of better judgment and respect for the 'truth', Corey liked it that way. I mean, despite all his complaints Corey was happy to be the disrespectful talent rather than the unjustly respected hack. But as these men slowly and surely provoked each other they began to move towards each other; destined to meet in the middle. And with two huge superstars like these two, everyone thought that there would be a collision. What they didn't expect was for these two to walk right past each other. Perhaps, these two had gotten so used to their comfort zones that they didn't feel it necessary to collide, perhaps they were distracted, Corey with Snow and Kano with.... I dunno, changing his adult diapers. U turn! Things barely calmed down before these two were back at their throats once again; Kano came out and outwrestled Corey, Corey responded with a brutal beat-down. It seems these two were back on track to collide once again. .....But something was different. Kano, one of the most respect-receiving and respect-giving wrestlers in the PCW locker room, was suddenly disrespecting CCW, a man who despite his dislike for had always given a fair go. Corey, the disrespectful and most certainly controversial billionaire, was using plain and simple beat downs to get the message through to not only Kano but the fans as well. Corey was sick of being the guy no one took seriously and Kano was just plain sick of Corey's attitude; it was time to teach him the only way he'd learn. A sort of role reversal had taken place. This new school VS old school feud had just gotten a whole lot more interesting as far as CCW and Kano were concerned. Just when it seemed that this feud was a whole lot bigger than the both of them; things changed and both men grew in size. Both men have been pushed to new heights, fueled on by some of the most powerful allies, rage, pride, ego and above all a burning hatred for the other and what they stand for. These two personalities, the walk in each other's shoes seemingly not helping them to empathize, will finally collide on Sunday June 30th at PCW Gold and Glory. It is a battle that will suit both men, for in its simplicity can be found the greatest of meanings. It is a battle dating back to the days of the gladiators; two men walk into the arena, one man leaves standing, the other does not. There will be no more discrepancies as to the manner of the victory, this is a match seen in black and white: Corey being (ironically) black and Kano being white. After all the complexities of Corey and Kano's disagreements in both the philosophical and existential senses, it will all come down to good Vs bad in a battle to the end. Both men put their reputations on the line, both have respect to gain or lose. Both have done great things in PCW. But this Sunday the question will be posed.... What is a REAL legend made of? And two men will attempt to tear each other apart to prove their point, and by the end will most certainly learn: WHO THE TRUE LEGEND IS! ::Our scene opens up at the PCW headquarters in Atlantic City. It has been half a year since Corey last attended this building, but so much has changed since then. Last time Corey was the poor, flamboyant and talented kid who was a member of the newly formed New Breed, Nelson's enemies number 1. He came in with bright eyes hoping to not only make a name for himself but earn the opportunity to work with the men he had idolized going through the ranks of PWA and PCW2. Now Corey is a billionaire, a cocky superstar with a World Title under his belt, one of the biggest names in PCW today. He is considered disrespectful by the general public (and a bloody genius by the privileged few), right now he and Tyler Nelson are somewhat buddies. Although not exactly chummy and although both men are completely and utterly aware of the self-centered natures of the other, Nelson and CCW work together for both a mutual cause as well as helping each other out for their own causes. Tyler Nelson is a business man, this is a business deal. Our first view of the building is of a receptionist's desk. From the corner of our eyes we notice the large marble pillars, the distinctly hard marble floor as well as the almost ridiculous amount of space in this room. Sure, we do wonder why a room that is built more for employees and special visitors than for the fans houses such an elaborate design as well as containing so many different vases and statues, but for right now our attention is upon the reception desk and the receptionist, quite obviously female (would Tyler Nelson have it any other way?), who stands at it. Our camera swings around 180 degrees and we see CCW, Corey Williams, cruising up to the desk, eyeing the receptionist with a sleazy look usually used by old men, or in Corey's case, a sleazy look used for old men.:: Corey: What's new good looking? ::Corey spreads that disgusting grin of his as he cockily chews his gum, a look the likes of Kano would never get away with, however he gets no response from the receptionist, who is face down and shuffling through various papers. Corey's grin subsides as he realizes that he is not the centre of attention, he chews his gum loudly in an attempt to get the receptionist's attention without looking like he's trying to get it. Finally the young, blonde, receptionist looks up innocently, blue eyes wide.:: Receptionist: Yes? Corey: Hi, I'm Corey Williams. ::Corey Williams looks around expectantly, the receptionist gives a small laugh.:: Receptionist: And? ::Corey is taken aback.:: Corey: Corey Williams..... PCW's Corey Williams? Former PCW World Heavyweight Champion Corey Williams? The controversial one Corey Williams? The controversial billionaire Corey Williams? The creator of the new era Corey Williams? Receptionist: Yes? Corey: Well..... Receptionist: Yes? Corey: Well.... DO YOU FUCKING RECOGNIZE ME OR NOT!! ::The sound echoes all over the huge room, causing a few stares from the people spread out amongst it. Corey Williams, particularly a Corey Williams in a foul mood or even worse, a respect-hungry Corey Williams, can be quite an intimidating force. This receptionist however refuses the intimidation, or rather ignores it. Her warm blue eyes stare calmly at CCW and she forms a kind smile.:: Receptionist: I'm afraid not. ::CCW turns away with a scowl. There are many factors that make him more recognizable than the average bear and as such ensure that he is treated with the respect that he deserves and does actually get from the mainstream public, despite the severe lack of it from both wrestling fans and wrestlers. His money and power, his look, the people he associates with and his status as PCW's most controversial superstar all make him stand out as a super-human. The first and last have seemingly not worked on this receptionist and so Corey must rethink his strategy. After a moments thought he turns back.:: Corey: Well.... I'm pretty good looking, right. ::Not a question, a statement.:: Receptionist: Yeh.....so? ::Again CCW turns in a scowl, his strategy foiled. He managed to get this receptionist to accept that he was indeed good-looking, but didn't even change her attitude towards him.... perhaps she is a lesbian. Time for one last shot.:: Corey: Look, even though you've never heard of me and even though you're sexual preference and taste, or lack thereof, blinds your judgment, and for your information I'm a frikken hotty! But despite all your lack of attention to the World around you, possibly caused by drugs, I assure you that I'm an incredibly important person. Receptionist: Is that so? Corey: Yes. In fact, I happen to be a close, personal friend of Tyler Nelson. Receptionist: Oh... ::Instantly the receptionist’s expression changes, just like Corey expected it to, however the expression changes from bad to worse. In fact, it's barely even an expression at all. The receptionist looks down, back to her papers. Not only is she treating Corey like some normal human, now she is treating him like a sub-human! She simply refuses to look Corey in eye, man-to-brutish lesbian.:: Receptionist: Well you're in luck, Mr. Nelson isn't busy for once, I assume you know where his office is.... ::Her voice trails off and she assumes that's the end of it, after all, the majority of her job is sending Mr. Nelson's 'friends' in. As she attends to her papers she suddenly hears a strange sound over and over again. She looks up to see the ever consistent Corey Williams chewing his gum furiously. He grins and her and follows it up with a wink.:: Receptionist: Yes? Corey: Well I'm not here to see Ty. Receptionist: Well then why did you tell me you were a close personal friend of him. Corey: Coz I wanted respect, of course! Receptionist: .....Ok. ::The receptionist shrugs her shoulders.:: Receptionist: So tell me then, why exactly are you here. Corey: Well..........I wanna join the hall of fame. Receptionist: Excuse me? Corey: I said I wanna join the hall of fame! Alright!? ::The receptionist struggles to refrain from laughing.:: Receptionist: You mean the PCW hall of fame? Corey: Yes! I want to be in the PCW hall of fame like my buddy Chef and all those other losers. Is there something a matter with that? Receptionist: Well for a start you have to be retired... Corey: Retired? That's bullshit? Is Chef retired? Hell no I saw him just a few days ago on Turmoil as we kicked the ass of the Jobber Crew! Receptionist: Well you see... Corey: Meanstreak is in there and it was just a few months ago that that dirty whore sold us out for another dirty whore Jane Reagan, birds of a feather, y'know what I mean. But the point is that he was more active than Kano in the sack. Receptionist: Wait, you mean J--... Corey: Besides, what's the point in being in the hall of fame if you're not even around to receive the respect you, at least in my case, rightfully deserve? That's just idiotic. ::Clearly unable to win this debate, or even participate in it, the receptionist tries a different approach.:: Receptionist: Well, you have to have made a major contribution to PCW... Corey: And I haven't? I'm only the greatest PCW World Champion of all time, Jon is a 'real' legend and he hasn't even one the damn belt... Receptionist: You mean K--..... Corey: I also happen to have been a member of both the most successful stable in PCW history and the most dominating stable in PCW history. And that's still not including the biggest one of them all, that being the most controversial man, now not only in PCW, but as sole owner of controversy, the World! Receptionist: Well, that's great. But even if you do manage to convince Mr. Nelson that you don't need to be retired... Corey: Or I could even copy one of those cheap bastards in there and retire for a week just to get in... Receptionist: Or that. And even if you do that, as well as convince Mr. Nelson, who isn't in the hall of fame himself, that your contribution to PCW has been outstanding. Even if you do ALL that, you're still forgetting one minor point... Corey: What? That the PCW Hall of Fame is an OOC thang and by mentioning it I'm kinda breaking kayfabe? Receptionist: No, you're forgetting that to get into the PCW Hall of Fame you have to be nominated by yourself and then voted on a ballot along with other RETIRED superstars and get the most votes! ::CCW thinks for a moment, as if just remembering something.:: Corey: ...........Oh yeh...... ::The reality sets in on CCW and he comes down from whatever drug it was that he was on. He tries to keep his cool, after all, he doesn't wanna look bad, does he?:: Corey: Never mind then. ::Corey begins to turn and walk off before he decides that he needs one last piece of information.:: Corey: Wait a second, what's your name? Receptionist: Patricia Deliaheart, why? ::Corey's response is so casual it's frightening.:: Corey: Oh nothing, I just wanted to know the name of the person I'm gonna get Nelson to fire for being a total bitch.... ::CCW turns and begins to walk in the direction we must assume is Nelson's office.:: Patricia (Receptionist): Wait! ::The receptionist Patricia lunges over her desk and grabs Corey by the arm of his zebra fur coat. Not so casual now, she has suddenly gained some respect for the power CCW wields, even if it is power that comes through Nelson. CCW turns towards Patricia, a delightful grin on his face; now the tables have turned.:: Corey: Yes? Patricia: Let me ask you a question....Um.... Why is it that you want to get into the hall of fame.... ::The receptionist sees a good opportunity to turn on the charm and save her ass.:: Patricia: I mean, you don't strike me as the type who'd want to be associated with those losers.... Corey: Well said. But the respect gained from entering the hall of fame is well worth the bad company. Not to mention the look on Kano's face when he realizes that it is I who have achieved official legend status before him, the supposed 'real' legend. Patricia: Kano? As in Jon Kano? As in the man who rocketh the mosteth? ::Corey scowls at the name of his arch-nemesis.:: Corey: Yes. Patricia: Wait a second, I know who you are! You're....um.... Corey: 'Controversial' Corey Williams? Patricia: No, that's not it. You're...... I know! You're the guy with the small package! ::Corey hides his scowl this time, trying to keep a straight face.:: Corey: I think you must've gotten me mistaken with one of the many people in pro-wrestling that stole my gimmick... I have no clue what you're a talking about. Patricia: No, no, no! You're that C...C...W guy, right? Corey: Well yes but I don't think.... ::It's too late, again the tables have changed.:: Patricia: You're that guy who keeps getting his ass Kanoplexed, right? Corey: Well I guess so, but that's de-- Patricia: Then you ARE the guy with the small d*ck! Corey: No... I think.... This can't.... STOP MAKING FUN OF ME! ::Corey suddenly places both hands on either side of his head over his ears and frantically runs for the door like a little sissy girl, forgetting about his promise to get the young girl fired. On CCW's way to the exit we can hear various disrespectful giggles coming either side of him, causing him to run faster. Eventually he is through the door and gone without a trace. There is about a second of awkward silence...... and then the employees return to their daily schedule and the job of generally making PCW the great fed it is today. Scene fades to plaid.:: !The End!
