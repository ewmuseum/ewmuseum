The Start Of Something New?

28 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 28th April OOC: Ok, I know I asked for some time off, but I figured that I can find time between revision to write some roleplays. So, without further ado ... Entry Date: 28th April 2002 The last time you saw me, I was headed for the Digital Reality Headquarters, where I met with one Clark Simons, with the possibility of becoming a part of the DRC. Well, things have changed since then. For those of you who know little about me, let me give a run down on the past few weeks events. A few weeks ago, after I had turned my back on Satanism for good, I received a letter through the mail, from The Digital Reality Corporation. I met up with a representative of the DRC, Clark Simons, in a coffee shop the next day. He tried to convince me into believing some of the most absurd stuff I have ever heard in my life. He tried to convince me, that a United Nations agency, known as the World Peacekeeping Organisation, had killed my parents when I was young. He tried to tell me that they had organised the disappearance of my brother, and back then, I didn’t quite know what to believe. So I agreed to meet him at the DRC headquarters, where you saw me last. He showed me around the building, and I must say, I was very impressed. He introduced me to some of the other agents working there, they seemed like decent enough people, and he even took me too see the leader of the DRC, Steven Thomas. Again, he seemed like a pretty genuine guy, and for a moment I was considering joining the DRC, but then, he told me what they were all about. Basically, they are this group, who claim to seek out “The truth behind the government’s lies.” Apparently, the FBI, or the government wants every DRC agent dead, and frankly, I was not willing to become an enemy of the state. Now I have done some pretty stupid things in my life, and I was not willing to do another. After that, I left. They held no grudges against me, just bid my a kind farewell. Or at least, that’s what I thought. Over the past few days, I swear that I have seen Clark Simons hanging around suspiciously on several occasions. He was there when I went to grab a bite to eat, he was there when I headed for the bookshop, and he was there when I caught a bus downtown. Now it may just be some sort of weird coincidence, but somehow, I doubt it. I think this guy is following me, I think that the DRC are bitter that I refused to join then, and that Simons has something up his sleeve. I don’t know what it is, but I just hope he disappears real soon. I am beginning to regret the fact that I agreed to meet him in the first place, I mean, this could escalate into something more than I ever could have expected. Why can’t people just leave me alone for once? All my life people have been bullying me, following me, trying to be around me … Can’t they understand that all I want is to be alone? Another interesting development over the past few days, has been my recievance of yet another initiation, this time from the US Government of all people. I don’t want to go into much detail, instead, I’ll let you read it for yourself: Dear Mr. Murray. Receiving this letter may come has a surprise to you, or it may not, as I am perfectly aware of recent events concerning you and the Digital Reality Corporation. But I ask you, please read this letter fully. Please do not discard it, no matter what you feel. Basically, I want to make this whole situation a lot clearer for you. The DRC are enemies of the US government, they are everything that they say they are, and they are out to try and “make a difference,” however, this whole World Peacekeeping Organisation is nothing but mere fabrications. Believe me, The US Government DID NOT kill your parents. That was all a ploy of theirs to try and convince you into joining their organisation. We are also aware that one Clark Simons has been following you over the past few weeks, for one simple reason. You see, the DRC doesn’t take kindly to people declining their offers, they like to have complete control over their own way, and those who decline usually end up … Dead. Mr. Murray, believe me, we do not want to see you dead. You have something special, a gift, and that gift needs to be saved. Now I understand that this may seem a little unacceptable to you at the moment, but we see this as perfect opportunity for you to join a division of the US Government, our division, the United International Justice Organisation. Myself and my associates will be around to meet with you tomorrow, your apartment, early afternoon. If you are interested, all that you need to do is draw the curtains on your lobby and bedroom windows at approximately 7pm tonight. One of our agents will be on the lookout, and will inform us whether or not you wish to meet with us. This is seem by us as a perfect opportunity for you to get the DRC off your back once and for all, as it is feared that otherwise, they could hunt you down, and end your days. I know this is a lot of information for you to comprehend at the moment, but I urge you to take some time to look over what I have said, and consider it deeply. Thank you,Walton Daniels- Agent, United International Justice Organisation After all that, I am still not sure what to believe. I closed my curtains, like they requested, and I am going to meet with this guy tomorrow. I am not quite sure what to expect, I don’t know if this is just a ploy from Simons and the DRC, but if it gets nasty, I think that I can take care of things myself. I’ll listen to what these guys have to say, and then I’ll make my decision. I’ll either john with the UIJO, and find my next life, or I shall decline them, and continue to live a life of wondering. All I know right now, is that I have made a definite enemy in the DRC, and that I may have made one of the biggest mistakes of my life. Goodbye. - Andy
