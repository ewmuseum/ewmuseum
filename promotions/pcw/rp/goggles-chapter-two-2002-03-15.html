Goggles..........Chapter Two

15 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 15th March 2002 Goggles: A Speculative Showdown… RP#3 for Fusion and #2 for Sabotage… Location: Meh, some street in Calgary, Alberta, Canada – Place of Chris Chase residence. Note: Mind the steep curb. Date: 15th March 2002. Approximate Time: 1:00 PM. Weather: A typically cold yet clear skied day in the city. Chapter Two: Revolving around a Lamp post? “Nostalgia, eh Chris? Doesn’t that make you just the little bit queasy…the putrifying food poisoning from undercooked and raw portions of meat and dairy items? …oh, that’s Salmonella. Sorry…remind me not to do that again…” “(sighs) Look, no matter what I friggin’ say, you’ll always end up going off in a complete tangent as per usual in the whole run of promotions of this extremely high calibur. Now then, what in Chris Chase’s name are you doing to that lamp post?” “You mean this right?” “Yes…that Lamp post…the one that you’ve chained yourself to…” “Well Chris, there’s a very simple explanation for that, a very simple explanation that even an idiot such as myself could understand. But, I seem to have left it in my other pants…and being in the state that I am…I can’t really leave to go fetch them. Now then, care to run a little errand for me?” ::.~(Voice of Chris Chase)~ The twinkle in my eye, the sharp, alert reactions, the general awareness. I know, in the general sense, that these signs mean only one thing. It’s nigh on two years since I entered this business professionally…grappling my way through drove after drove of unsubstantial Independent wrestlers for a year or so. It was this time last year, that I was beginning to become the staple diet of PCW2 fans across the world, those fans that found the spectacular likes of myself readily digested in their search for their own competitor to wage a war against the likes of the PCW federation’s finest, the top dogs so to speak. Back then, Kid Wonder was by no means, a quitter…the Chef was making his culinary impact upon the PCW federation, Miyagawa was nothing more than a goggle wearing weirdo and Tyler Nelson had more power and influence over the actions of others than he does now. It was a daunting thought, nonetheless…that as hero to the ever frivolous PCW2 masses, I’d have to swagger out onto that stage eventually as their hero and saviour…within a year. A year on, the PCW2 is long gone…Boda’s some how managed to win the PCW World Championship (No names mentioned). And Miyagawa STILL is that goggle wearing Japo-American weirdo! Miyagawa and I have rarely crossed each other’s paths in the eight months that I’ve been under the PCW label, but all too recently, I’ve been forced into scraps with the guy married to a Russian wife, the man who lost to OSW wrestler and Communist dolt Ivan Stanislav not so long ago. Now, in the space of a week starting from tonight, I will be thrust into combat with the Goggled Gigalow, for three DAMN times…THREE! All that time without so much as an exchange of slaps between the two of us, it’s only now, in a hugely clustered space of time…that we battle…two former Cruiserweight Champions. And thanks to my actions on Tuesday, I’ve made an already insane five foot ten Japo-American ever more focused on pummeling my pretty boy skull into oblivion. Ha! How pedestrian…how typical of the idle threats from these self-righteous losers. Gah, I hate clichés… It’s a biting, yet clear skied day in Calgary, Canada…the rays of bright early afternoon sunlight piercing the gaps between the high rise buildings that tower above a shadowed street…enveloped by those buildings that surround it. An office block, thousands of tons of concrete and glass, stands proudly in the view of the camera…glimmering brilliantly in the bold light of the sun. A shadow is cast against the building, the shadow of yours truly imposes the defined shape of the Celestial Cowboy, ‘The Messiah’ Chris Chase. My hometown is something of great importance to myself, if anything for the familiarity. I know every nook and crannie of Calgary, as opposed to the limited knowledge of the other towns and cities that I tour through with the PCW contingent, traipsing all over the country without so much as a care… Sure, call me stubborn…but I’d rather remain in my home…the place from which I was raised. I studied here…I played soccer here…I deported Snapple from here. A great deal of memories are attached to this place…so it’s silly for me to leave, just yet….:: Chris: ~(Snapping back)~ That’s your problem and your responsibility Dustin…it’s not my fault that you’ve chained yourself to a lamp post without so much as a decent justification to back it up. Why, had you have become an attorney…you’d fail miserably! Old Lady: Yup sonny…why I can remember it just as if it was yesterday. You…you’d managed to get yourself into a spot of bother by finding a fifty dollar bill, and your little maniac friend here was on hand to help you with your defense… Dustin: See…she said “HELP” Chris, which is something you aren’t doing too much of around these parts…ah, I remember that day… ::. I stammer, rather taken aback by the ramblings of a crippled old lady, who stands before Dustin and I…as we stand there, doing nothing but scorning each other for the sake of doing so. It had been roughly twenty-four hours since the last mention of the name Chris Chase, accompanied by his former Tag Team partner and current manager… ‘Deadly’ Dustin Downey…and in that short space of time, Dustin had managed to chain himself to a lamp post a couple of blocks from my home and base of operations…New Breed and otherwise. Of course, this had led largely to a long wait for someone with some kind of heavy machinery to break the heavy-duty chains, and release Dustin from his temporary prisonment. It’s been six hours now, and with my hands firmly attached to my hips, I sigh…shaking my head in exasperation of this whole tomfoolery… The elderly woman brings up a good point, however, as wavy lines begin to propagate on the screen…almost causing my excitable former Tag Team partner to barf up on the pavement for the fiftieth time in the space of two hours….:: Dustin: Ugh…alright…I know flashbacks are cliché, Chris…but that doesn’t mean that you have to make me puke up for the fiftieth time in the space of two hours. ::. Confused, I cock my head in a curious manner, questionning Dustin with the look in my deep brown, focused and confident eyes. And then, realizing the implications and use of that last sentence from a steadily puking Dustin Downey, I look up to the sky…seemingly watching for the image of some 17 year old handler who abuses Dustin for his own sick, selfish gratification….:: Handler Chris: Muhahahahahahaha! That’ll teach you for tempting me to write a flashback sequence, Dustin Downey! ~(coughs)~ Excuse me…but I’m trying to poke fun at some moron that was handled by a good friend of mine. Oh, alright then…have it your way… ::.Whoa…talk about an out of character experience. Dustin’s queasiness subsides as if by magic, and although treading in his own pile of undigested corndogs…Dustin glances at me with that same blissfully ignorant expression as always…as I sigh, knowing that I could have left hours ago, to prepare for the first of the three encounters with Miyagawa in the next week or so. Pondering this, I gaze at my watch, which blinks on the one o’clock mark. Obviously this poses a minor threat to the time constraints of this promotion. The convertible 1970’s Corvette sits invitingly beside my position, beckoning me with that chrome finish…tempting yours truly to leave a distracting and spluttering Texan to the proverbial wolves of this city…those man-eating Executive whores from the business district….:: Dustin: ~(Splutters)~ Flashbacks being the eternal pain in the ass or not, Chris…that old woman standing next to you with her finger in her nose was right…I do remember acting as your attorney in that case. Why, I could remember it as clear as this beautiful blue sky above me… ::. I cynically shake my head. You see, I can no longer recall such an event, I mean…when did that ever happen? It’s no use asking half of you…since that’d be quite foolish of me, and we’ve wasted enough precious time as it is….:: Chris: That’s funny, I can barely recall such an event occuring…let alone actually happening for real. Dustin, as much as I hate to tell you this and let you down once more, that never happened…you hear me? ::.Funnily enough, Dustin shakes his head…looking keen to correct me in an almost serious way. It genuinely looks as if he’s somehow managing to conjure something that’s actually believable…unless the next blurb is anything to go by….:: Dustin: Sure it did…remember, Jim Carrey was on the prosecution and that measly Anne Robinson was the judge for the hearing. Al Snow was there, along with a whole other host of weird and colourful characters. Surely you remember at least that. Meh…it’s no use trying to stimulate you into remembering that…I mean, it was a traumatic experience for you…and I’m sure that you’ve blocked it out of your memory entirely. So, how about Miyagawa huh? Isn’t he great? Chris: What brings on the sudden interest in that has been Dustin? I mean, heck, sure, he was on a par with you as far as the Cruiserweight regime goes…but up until now you’ve always considered him to be an annoying ass spelunker who wears goggles for the hell of it. ~(Sighs)~ Of course he isn’t GREAT! If Miyagawa was great, then the apocalypse would dawn upon us, comets will rain down on us from the heavens and the destruction of the world would be little more than a few seconds away. Are you seeing where I’m going with this Dustin? Miyagawa being great, or even good is detrimental to the entire well being of this planet that I’m probably destined to save from the evils of mediocre-dom. And only God knows what I’ve brought upon the world by handing BODA the PCW World Heavyweight Title… ::.Squinting up at my silhouette, created by the glare of the bright sunlight…Dustin glares at me in a confused, befuddled manner…his general facial expression revealing the extent of this as he fiddles around with the chain that binds him to the lamp post. Meanwhile, I steady myself after going off on more tangents than you can shake a Streak sized stick at….:: Dustin: Mediocre…dom, perhaps? Heck, I don’t know…why don’t you tell me? Old Lady: Yes…tell him sonny…tell him the implications of letting Boda-Boy have all the glory for himself. Damnit Mister Chase, tell Dustin exactly why you let that below-average jobber take your rightful position as PCW World Heavyweight Champion… ::. Once again, before even managing to swiftly clear up any misgivings about the whole Boda incident, I am swept almost entirely off my feet by the insane ramblings of a crippled old woman that serves to be even more of a distracting annoyance than Dustin Downey himself, or even Rich Cannon for that matter. Blinded by my own ego, I find myself subjected by these colourful characters at every beckoning moment, which does nothing more than justify the fact that I really do get in with the wrong crowd…with the exception of Corey Williams and the injured Jason Snow. They’re the kind of roughed up, arrogant folk that are the very kindred spirits of the likes of myself…a juxtaposition to Dustin Downey who is as similar to me as Boda is talented… It’s now five minutes since I first looked at my semi-digital Rolex, a piece of equipment that had been bought with the leftovers from my deportation of that foul tasting Snapple last month. Whoever said that Xavier Kannon was nothing more than a cheap whiner was about as right as Boda having the degree of talent to lose to the likes of Tim Walden week in, week out. That in itself, is probably the most likely thing to happen once I dethrone Boda-Boy….:: Chris: Boda-Boy? Do you know how much painstaking effort that it took me to come up with that phrase, if only for some crippled old woman to rip it off and use it for her own purposes? For crying out loud, it’s SO obvious as to why I “foolishly” let Boda manage to win the title for the second time, that even an idiot such as the likes of my friend here could see… Dustin: Actually Chris, I meant to point that out to you…I’m still lost as far as that’s concerned, and you didn’t really address the question beforehand… Chris: Reluctantly thrown into the depths of having to answer stupid questions again, eh? ::. Dustin shakes his head…looking at me as I gradually slip away from him…creeping closer to the car….:: Dustin: Actually…they were your own stupid questions. Say, what are you up to now Chris? Chris: Dustin, do you know how far away Nebraska is? Well…let me tell you something. I have to make it to a PCW event in 7 hours, or else this precious little match with Miya-jobber is going to be void…now then, would you at least slip your hand from out of the chain, and get halfway decent to leave? Dustin: What’s brought this on all of a sudden? Chris: Meh…Mood swings… ::. Dustin manages to slip his hand from out of the chains that bind him…making his way over to the Corvette…grinning moronically with a hum in his tune. Stepping into the car, I hit the gas…and leave the old woman behind as the scene fades to a close. Oh, and you can Justify…that..::
