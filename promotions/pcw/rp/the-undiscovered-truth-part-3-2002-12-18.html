The Undiscovered Truth (Part 3)

18 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12/18/02 The Undiscovered Truth Part 3 Why won’t this pain go away? It follows me wherever I go. I want to be accepted as normal, but they just can’t let it be. They need to prove things to help society in the long run, but that’s just ludicrous. People used to be happy; their minds weren’t so tainted. Now were all the same, we think we know it all and that leads to crime, murder, and fighting. God stands above all of us watching and just shaking his head, as we had to ruin such an untainted paradise. And now, well now not much has changed. They ruin peoples lives through wanting to expand the human mind, but do they realize what their doing to that person. The pain man puts man through is a pain that should stay undiscovered. May the world learn that not everything needs to be discovered? *Alarm clock sounds* Oh no, I’m late… Rushing through the house she’s putting on her pants at the same time as she brushes her crystal teeth. She grabs something to eat for breakfast and combs her hair as she makes her way out the door. She then realizes that she had forgotten some lab reports. She goes back and discovers nothing but AIR! Oh my God! Where is it? Her hands begin to shake as she rushes around the house looking for her folder with various x-rays, x-rays that included that of El Romantico’s. *At the same exact time 2,000 miles away scientists are already conversing over this spectacular happening. * I’ve never seen anything like this before. And to think this research doctor gave this too us, for the small amount we gave him. This might just change the way people live if we can get a sample of this fluid. But who’s the man? Not sure, I was told he goes by the name of Romantic or something of that magnitude. He won’t be easy to trace down. I just can’t believe that these x-rays are valid. Are you sure their weren’t any glitches in the system. I’m not sure, but I’ll find out sooner or later. *Back in the hospital* *Ring, ring, ring* Ms. Santes, and how are you this morning? Angelica I need to know some important information, it’s very critical. Calm down sweetie, take one breath at a time and tell me your problem. I need to know what other doctors were here in the research lab two days ago after 10 PM. Let me just plug some of this into my computer, and here we go, got it. Mr. Rijas didn’t sign out until 10:23 that night, and he was the only person other than you in the research lab. Thank you so much Angelica, I’m sorry I barged in like this, but I’ve made a big mistake that needs to be fixed. *Mr. Rijas office* Marcos! Hello who’s th….. Marcos I know you took my x-rays and I need to know where you put them, this is a matter of life and death. X-rays? Don’t play stupid. She then charges at the doctor and wraps her hands around his neck tightly. Get off me this instance. First of all you shouldn’t be leaving your things around in the lab and then falling asleep. Secondly, what are you doing with those types of x-rays? Things like that should go straight to the state science association. Do you realize what those x-rays were? Yes, I more than realize what they are. Do you realize what is going to happen to the poor man those x-rays were taken of. No sweat off my back. Oh, I am not in a good mood, now you give me those x-rays now or I will make your life a living hell. Too late babe, I already sent it off and got quite the profit from it. How dare you do that with my property! Oh don’t you give me that, I approved in with the research lab, and that x-ray in fact belongs to them. You’re just jealous you didn’t get the 5,000 Euros for it. How unacknowledged are you? Do you realize that you could have gotten at least a hundred times that price if you diagnosed the x-ray? *Mr. Rijas sits their speechless. * I never want to see you again. *El Romantico’s apartment* *Knock…. knock* Romantico quickly answers the door and sees two men on his doorsteps. Mr. Romantico you’re going to have to come with us? Romantico looks on clueless as the scene fades to black.
