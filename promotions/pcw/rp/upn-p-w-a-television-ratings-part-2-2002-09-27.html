UPN + P.W.A = Television Ratings Part 2

27 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 27 - 09 - 02 Prologue: Our last episode involving the Asylum collective known as Pranks With Attitude saw them offered a television contract by a mysterious female supposedly a representative of the UPN network with regards to the duo's own chat show. Eager to tie up the deal, UPN have proposed a contract signing for later the following day, unveiling both Nerezza and Gaz Green as their newest acquisitions providing everything runs according to plan. 'WHO'S A TAG TEAM CHAMPION - WHO'S A TAG TEAM CHAMPION...' sang the distinctive voice of the man who brought in the colour green from the cold and made it the new red for this year, Gaz 'The Jester' Green. The scene opened on the reflection of the Jester as he gazed into the sparkling gold plaque, better known as the PCW Tag Team Championship. He stood in awe, his eyes firmly fixated on the prize held between his clenched hands as he performed a catalogue pose accompanied with a smug facial expression that cried arrogance. The camera panned backwards capturing the conniving comic with nothing more than a towel as trickles of water ran along his toned body and his hair had draped along his face forcing the rookie to brush his hand through for styling purposes but on doing so he positioned every strand courtesy of the gold before him. Towering the vain comedian was the behemoth Nerezza, his reflection stranded in limbo and like an assassin his prey had no clue to his presence, no need to breath and his hand movements came across as nothing more than a gentle breeze. 'If you spent the same amount of time training as you do with each strand of hair that adorns your head, Pranks With Attitude would be unstoppable....' hissed the goliath, startling the life out of his incoherent partner. Gaz Green immediately dropped his newfound wealth to the ground as he held his chest area, slowly the colour returned to his cheeks but that maybe deemed the least of his problems as the slack towel around his waist followed in the footsteps of his title and joined it's side along the floor causing the Jester to shield his manhood. 'Err-... Well-... Hmm-... I'd be getting dressed now!' murmured the former NTICW competitor, his face now crimson red whilst the remainder of his body appeared as virgin white. 'That is probably the most sensible thing you have said since our unity?!?' proclaimed the Angel Of Fear, his expressionless face drawing a smile grudgingly. With haste in his stride, Gaz set hotfooted across the room still surveyed by the camera as he raced to his living quarters. With a firm thud of his wooden door the camera acknowledged his disappearance and focused on the wardrobe of Noscosta Ireul Nerezza who stood erect but motionless clad in a claret red suit commonly associated with funerals, but it was the case of the vampire looking his best and the era of the trench coat had long expired. 'What's taking so long...' whispered Nerezza under his breath, his head peering down at his watch less wrist and his foot crashing down on the surface of the floor a second time with impatience. Suddenly the jovial Jester cockily strutted into frame, his emerald business attire, present and correct accompanied as always by his exaggerated smile that showcases his teeth, and the kinder smile you would expect to twinkle on viewing whilst his dyed green hair stands tall. As the Jester waltzes over to his partners side the dynamic duo look to have put a great deal of effort into their appearances, the only call for alarm would be punctuality as 1 o'clock looms, a thousand questions running through their active minds simultaneously. 'What if it were a prank telephone call?' 'What if Ms. Martin has had second thoughts after Gaz Green's initial behaviour?' 'Could it be the doing of Playaz Inc. to gain an unfair advantage over the current Champions?' 'What if I have blood smears over my teeth, that won't look good?' thought the vampire washing his teeth in saliva. 'How much wood could a wood chuck, chuck if a wood chuck could chuck wood?' thought Gaz 'The Jester' Green, his mouth miming the catchphrase as he said it over in his head. As Gaz continued his rendition of the phrase over in his head, Nerezza could only offer a dumbfounded gaze as he shadowed his smaller comrade who jerked about as though a personal stereo were playing but as the moment lost it's appeal the monotone 'Ring - Ring' of the telephone brought both party's back down to reality with a crash. As though a moment of de ja vu, the evil incarnate (Nerezza) swept his talons over the dials and the speakerphone flickered into life with the educated voice of the receptionist. 'Hmm, Mr. Green and guests, your carriage awaits....' 'Guests? Err-... Mr. Green and myself will be eating in today, that'll be all' retorted the Living Nightmare with a snobby tone, almost a sarcastic portrayal of the receptionist. 'Very well, sir!!!' responded the voice over the speakerphone and the line fizzled out, capturing nothing but interference. 'Nez... What do you mean we're eating in? There's nothing you'll like on the menu, I mean Hannibal Lector was once a guest here and they found accommodating him difficult, you'd be the Holy Grail of problems!' injected the Lord Of Laughter with a slight giggle. 'Well - Lets just say your butler has offered.... 'HIMSELF' posh nosh would be the common name for it!' bellowed the Spawn Of Evil almost foaming at the mouth as he slipped into a dreamlike state. 'SNAP OUT OF IT SQUIRE, WE CANT AFFORD TO BE LATE~!' cried the Emerald Menace in bated breath as they left the apartment in unison, single file through the entrance as they targeted the elevator. As though on queue the elevator sat perched to the pent house floor allowing the Asylum members to enter and make their way down to the ground floor where the transportation awaits their arrival. Entering the pimple faced bellboy, shuffled his scrawny carcase to one side and asked for P.W.A's designation with a croaky voice, as though it hadn't broke yet. 'Floor?!?' 'Ground floor if you wouldn't mind squire...' replied the Prince Of Pranks. The elevator descended rapidly and the heat of the enclosure began to show as Gaz waved his hand over his forehead to wipe away the sweat when suddenly the pimple- faced teen began coughing violently. 'You alright Guv 'nor?' asked the concerned Jester. 'Y-Y-Y-YEAH THANKSSS!!!' said the hired help, not realising Gaz had placed a sign on his back with the message, 'Kick Me - You Stuck Up B*stards!' in bold lettering. The journey finally came to an abrupt end as the trickster team left the confines of claustrophobia and into the spacious reception area that was swamped in activity as people of all statures went about their daily lives. The collective however were against time and couldn't bask in the rush hour so with titanic strides they flee the scene via huge glass panel doors that lead onto the sidewalk where they were greeted by a driver dressed, head to toe in a black tailored suit. 'Mr. Green and Mr. Nerezza I take it?' said the driver as he held the door ajar to the stretching limousine, a real gentleman. The Tag Team Champions minus their covenant gold entered the vehicle in single file tilting their bodies and slouched into the reclined seating arrangement before the doors firmly shut with a thunderous 'THUD' and with the use of tinted windows they remained concealed from the prying eyes of the Las Vegas natives. - To be continued -
