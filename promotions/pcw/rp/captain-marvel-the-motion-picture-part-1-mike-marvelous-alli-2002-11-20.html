Captain MARVEL...the Motion: Picture Part 1(Mike Marvelous Alliances Promo #1)

20 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/20/02 Scene opens outside an Empire Movie Theatre in some generic American city. There is a large line up at the doors. Above all the people is a white billboard with many film titles printed upon it in bold, black letters. The camera focuses on two young boys in the line, both around the age of 13. One is a dumb looking kid called Jay who is wearing a baseball cap and the other is a smart-alecky kid named Spencer with a head full of scruffy hair. The kid with the hat speaks up first. 'I hate line ups...' 'Well, once we get in, it'll be all worth the wait.' 'I still say we should go see Half Past Dead or 8 Mile...' 'Why!? One of them has a fat, aging action hero and the other has a scrawny white actor trying to play James Dean!' 'What about Harry Potter and the Chamber of Secrets?' 'Dude...are you fucking gay or something?' 'Hey! I read the books! They were good!' The line-up moves up, bringing the boys ever so closer to the elusive ticket booth. Spencer leans over and talks to Jay again. 'We're almost there...' 'Yay...' 'Ok! It's our turn! Let's go get the tickets!' The two boys walk up to the counter and peer up at the pimply faced ticket boy. 'Two adults for Captain MARVEL, please.' The pimply faced ticket boy looks back and forth at the two boys then glowers at them, revealing a row of yellow, crooked teeth with metal braces over them. 'Uhh...Captain MARVEL is rated PG for language and comic book violence...you two kids are gonna hafta get a parent or guardian to come in with ya..' 'I have you know that we're both 40 year old college professors!' 'Oh...sorry prof, enjoy the show.' The pimply face ticket boy rips two small, white pieces of paper off and gives them to the two boys and they're on their way. 'Hey Spence, let's go grab popcorn and stuff.' 'Not enough time! The curtain open in 1 minute and 3 seconds!' 'But I'm starvi--' 'Shut up!' Spencer grabs Jay by the arm and drags him over to the ticket ripper, who rips their tickets and gives the stubs back to them. Then both run down the long hallway until they reach cinema 13, which has a Captain MARVEL poster outside of it. 'Here it is! Captain MARVEL! Dammit, I hear music! It must be starting!' Both boys dart through the doors and run in just in time for the sound test, which nearly knocks them off their feet. 'Let's grab a front row seat!' 'Naw man, my neck gets too sore. If we sit in the middle, we'll get better sound quality.' 'But it's better to be closer to the screen!' 'Sound quality!' 'Screen!' 'Sound quality!' 'Scr--' Suddenly, an old man interrupts them both in a loud, angry voice. 'Why don't both you kids shut yer pie holes and sit down!' Both kids frown and sit down in the front. A couple previews play and then music starts...and the movie begins... Captain MARVEL...the Motion Picture Twas a day like any other in WT City... Thousands of people...going about their business...unknowing...non-caring...blind to the truth... And the truth is...WT City is a city like no other. And within it, a secret war is being fought. Good vs. Evil. This war has been going on for years right under the noses of the WT City people...and yet they have no idea. To them, everything was perfect. Sure, there was a heat wave moving through the city, and the streets were plagued by a high crime rate...but that's the truth about all big cities. To them, it was an urban paraside... ....Until the truth came out...and the war began. --------------------------------------------------------------------- Scene opens outside the mansion of mild-mannered professional wrestler, Michael M. Marvelous. He sat by his beautiful, maple-leaf shaped in-ground pool made of marble tile. His friends, Timo, Azala, Cisco and Casey all splashed around in the water, playing a game of volleyball. They were his friends. He worked with them in FSW...the wrestling federation he wrestled for...but away from the screen, he lived a comfortable, relaxed life with his friends and family. This was just an ordinary day in the life of the Canadian Hero. It was a hot day, too...98 degrees...sweltering hot. Almost unbearable for a Canadian. But he was no ordinary Canadian. He was no ordinary man. Neither were his friends. 'Hey Mike, you comin' in?' Mr. FSW looked up to see Timo beckoning him into the pool with a friendly grin. The pool did look inviting in this heat. But he was quite contempt enjoying his comic book and MARVELade Sports Beverage. 'No thanks, Timo. You guys keep playing. Tell me when you're taking the last shot and I'll call it.' 'Alright man, suit yourself...' The splashing continued...and Mike went back to his book and his drink, enjoying the shade of his umbrella...while elsewhere in WT City....Evil was running amuck. Stay tuned for the next part of the movie...go grab some popcorn or some over-priced, watered down pop...ah hell, just get the hell outta here.
