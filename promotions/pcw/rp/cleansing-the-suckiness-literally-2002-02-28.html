Cleansing the Suckiness...LITERALLY!

28 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 28th February 2002 The State of Defense… RP#4 for March Madness and #2.5 for Fusion… Location: Lucky Star Bed and Breakfast, on the banks of the River Missouri…Time: 11:00, Weather: Sunny with a smattering of clouds. Chapter Four: Cleansing the Suckiness…and anything else that might be catching… “Rise and Shine, Mister Chase…you had some celebration last night. It’s Eleven o’ clock now, don’t you think that you should be at least preparing yourself for the rest of the day?” “Eleven o’ clock? Oh jeez…I can still smell the Kannon on me, EUGH! Move out of the way cretin, I need to get to the washroom, and FAST! Oh, and whilst you’re packing away the bedding, make sure my ride’s ready for me when I get out of the washroom…” “Sure Mister Chase…oh, and I forgot to tell you, Mister Downey’s already paid for yours and his bill. Bearing in mind…most of his is based on his gross consumption of funnel cakes and the cleaning bill…” ::. ~(Voice of ‘The Messiah’ Chris Chase)~ One Hundred Percent, One Hundred Percent. It was little more than two days ago now that I defeated Xavier Kannon for the third time, to retain my PCW Cruiserweight Championship. It was a display of epic proportions…however much the ending may have been spoiled by internet spoilers…those accursed idiots. Anyhow, now the focus shifts, literally, to that of March Madness, that ever looming Pay-Per-View on the proverbial horizon…to my first PCW World Heavyweight Title shot. I mean, I can’t say that I didn’t deserve it…I mean, heck, I’m on a hot streak that no-one seems to be paying particular attention to, and when I wrap it up with a surprise victory at this most recent Super-Card…then it’ll be little time before those self-satisfied morons come knocking around my door for a piece of me… And so, the constant soul-sucking cycle continues…much like the water cycle incidentally…that ever constant stream of H20 circulating in this world of ours, this world made up of two parts water to one of land...except in the wrestling business, it’s sixty-odd parts of sheer suckitude to three parts of, well, talent. Have you ever heard the saying that Cleanliness is close to Godliness, adopted by the ancient Egyptians in their quest to be as close to their deities as possible? Being who I am, I live by exactly the same code as these Egyptians…sure, as long as I wash myself after I beat the living snot into Xavier Kannon, then I’ll probably never end up as truly sucky as he is…damn Scientologists. He along with 17 other random jobbers, make up the non-New Breed majority of the competitors in Sunday’s match. Great, so I suppose that he’ll be looking for vengeance after the events of the past week or so…how pedestrian… Nestling on the banks of the great Missouri River, the Lucky Star Bed and Breakfast served as the perfect place to rest after a long night’s celebration after the success of the entire of New Breed during Tuesday night. I pull myself out of bed, rubbing my eyes in a tired manner, before being faced by the sight of a bulky maid pulling the covers of the bed that I’d just got out of. Querying her, she points to a door in the far corner of the room, obviously leading to the washroom that I had so obviously needed much after figuring out that I’d need to cleanse all the Xavier Kannon off of me. Flicking my messy brown hair over my shoulders, I open the door, and step into the washroom, cutting off the intrusive camera that follows almost all my escapades as if this was some twisted re-take of Ed-TV. An hour later, and after much scrubbing and soaping up, I emerge from the claustrophobic environment of a B&B Washroom, sparkling uncannily in the light piercing the net curtains that cover the windows… And without so much as a care, I strut downstairs, bidding adieu to the proprietors of this calm little place…before joining the likes of Dustin Downey in the motel’s forecourt, as he runs a hand along the side of the coach brought as my ticket to Kansas City and the Friday Fusion match against Afwa Kilimanjaro. In my presence, Dustin looks up at me with that same old goofy look…before saying something that he might live to regret….:: Dustin: Ah, why it’s the PCW Cruiserweight Champion…say, didn’t you cheat to beat Xavier, by getting your New Breed buddies to help you out in your time of great need? ::. I groan audibly, not really having the patience to explain my actions of late….:: Chris: Weren’t you paying attention Dustin? I’m sure that those fools at Asylum were going to run in at any moment, so basically I had Corey and Snow eradicate the possibilities of such an occurrence… ::. One second in, and I’m already having to cover up…or give reasons as to why the New Breed helped me to my victory on Tuesday night. I sigh, slapping Dustin around the back of his head…before eventually leaning on the side of the coach, crossing my arms back behind my head as the bold sun burns my skin, slowly, as I bask in its rays. Dustin, on the other hand, rubs the back of his head swiftly…trying to ease the minor pain. After managing to do so, he reaches down, and pulls open the luggage compartment in the lower recesses of the coach…releasing a small horde of pet hamsters as he does so….:: Dustin: GAH! How’d this lot get in here? Please tell me that I didn’t forget to close the cage again! ::. Swinging his arms around in protest, futilely attempting to herd the hamsters back into the luggage compartment, Dustin mutters angrily at himself obviously. I can’t help but wonder why he’d mention that they were kept in a cage, when he actually knows that they haven’t and never will be kept in a cage – especially if they manage to make it out of the moron’s clumsy grasp. Looking around for something other than to watch Dustin to keep my time largely out of a point where it could be wasted…unfortunately…curiosity and the attraction of ridiculing my former Tag Partner bids me to stay, drawing me in like a mouse to cheese….:: Chris: Dustin, you didn’t close the cage…I mean, what cage? Don’t you just let these tiny, little, irritating pests run amok whilst you look down upon them in an adoring, moronic way? I mean, what if the PCW Management mistakenly allowed Scientologists run amok in the PCW? We’d have a global epidemic of supreme suckiness, that’s WHAT, Dustin! And thanks to my efforts, none of them will ever be bringing their merchandise into Canada again, and none of them will ever hold the PCW Cruiserweight Title… ::. Confused and somewhat baffled in most senses, Dustin looks up in my direction whilst kneeling on the ground. Groaning with dread, I begin to ponder exactly what this idiot could want from me now….:: Dustin: You know Chris, I didn’t think that hamsters had merchandise or wrestled in the PCW…of course, I could always be mistaken, after all…it happens more often than those homely raft riding folk realise… ::.My left eye twitches at this bizarre comment, for which Dustin is usually famed. But this quote puzzles me more than the usual, obviously Dustin’s talking about viewers…lard-assed folk who can’t concentrate on anything for hours except their television sets. Scratching my chin, I begin to ponder this, before throwing my baggage, comprising of a couple of suitcases, into the expansive luggage compartment. Rising from the dusty forecourt surface and dusting off the knees of his torn jeans, Dustin points over to the Missouri river. The Missouri, a striking river by any means, also whereupon which a mid-aged couple pass by slowly atop a reed-built raft…waving to Dustin as they pass. Of course, this causes yours truly to make an audible gagging sound, which is usually heard when Dustin actually makes sense for once….:: Chris: I can see what you mean Dustin. They were completely oblivious to the FACT that you’re a blithering idiot who stashes hamsters in probably the most inane and downright foolish places. Now then, did you receive word on what I’m supposed to be promoting, or did you somehow lose THAT too? ::. Dustin shakes his head, to which question, I don’t actually know just yet. Gone are the days when Chris Chase decided where Chris Chase is going, and to be DAMN honest, it’s not that fun any more. Being herded by Dustin to various places, traipsing gallantly all over the country…is a classic sign of High Impact-it is, a rare strain that only affects the most successful member of High Impact. And considering my record…and all the matches that have proven this, it’s no wonder why I’m suffering at the moment, like a caged animal waiting to burst out and onto the scene as a serious performer. It’s just that certain elements keep holding me back… With a slow stride, I follow Dustin towards the coach entrance, staring up at the New Breed stylized behemoth that commands the respect of the road like a Shepherd over his Dog, or like Chris Chase over Xavier Kannon. With a wipe of my brow, I step inside the chilled interior of our chosen method of transport…brushing my hand against the leather upholstered furniture. It’s moments like this that force me to remember the side of me that doesn’t like to reflect so much on the past, and more on the future…particularly my own future. Chuckling to himself, Dustin picks up a snow globe from the worktop surface, passing it to me without so much as a care… Glancing down upon it, I notice the lettering along the rim of the globe: “The Mystery Vortex”. Ah, the place that Dustin visited often in his youth…a crazy mixed up place where up was down and down was up. Dustin grins in a blissfully unapprised manner, staring me directly in the eye with earnest….:: Dustin: Well…there you go Chris…that’s what we’re finding time to visit today, the Mystery Vortex…the most maniacally twisted place on Good Mother Earth. Doesn’t it just drive you delirious with delight at the prospect of going there? Chris: Delirious? Yes…Delight? No. You know Dustin, with every DAMN stupid notion that you get…the more you drive me up the freakin’ wall! I don’t know whether you’ve noticed or not, but I can pick destinations without a moments notice…but yours seem downright CRAZY! Dustin: Ok then, Chris…why don’t you decide where to go?
