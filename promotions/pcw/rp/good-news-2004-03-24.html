Good news

24 March 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/24/04 ‘Hello?’ I answered the phone. Now, it was rather late, and I usually don’t answer calls past 11 pm, but tonight I was still awake. I had gotten some really good news today, and I couldn’t sleep. So instinctively I picked up the cell phone on the end table. ‘Is Ofeelya there?’ The strange voice on the other end asked. I really didn’t pay it no mind, as it was nearing midnight. I was really tired, but still so excited from the news. ‘Ofeelya who?’ I was to busy reading the letter I had received earlier from Fed-Ex. I simply asked whom the caller was looking for, instead of hanging up. ‘Ofeelya Hiney’ ‘Ofeelya Hiney?’ I questioned, again, paying only half attention to the call. ‘What are you, some sort of faggot? You’ll feel my hiney? You queer.’ Now the caller had my attention. I dropped my letter and looked at the caller I.D box. ‘Fuck you Ryan, you stupid son of a bitch.’ I barked at Knox on the other end. ‘You think you’re real funny don’t ya?’ The only answer I got was laughter. And then silence. ‘I got you, didn’t I?’ Ryan asked, half laughing, half snorting. I knew that when me and Ryan reunited, that’d he’d do this prank calling shit again. After all, him and Derrick Diamond were notorious for their ribs and pranks back in the day. So why would things be any different now? It’s good that things don’t change. ‘Whatcha doing Big Daddy?’ Ryan asked next. ‘Just reading man, I got a letter today from my old college.’ I decided to let Ryan be the first to know about the news. After all, I needed to tell someone. And I wasn’t about to call my ex-wife and wake the girls up. That could wait till the morning. ‘Get this man, The University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill wants to hold a special ceremony next Saturday night at our Turmoil show.’ ‘Cool, what’s it about?’ Knox tries to get more out of me. And I’m more than willing to share it with him. ‘Get this man, next week, The Tarheels are going to retire my college jersey and number, and they want to do it at Turmoil. I’m fucking stoked now man. My jersey and my number, is going to hang with the likes of Jordan and Worthy.’ I explain to Ryan, as happy as a kid in a candy store. I knew I had a good career there, before my time and self destruction in the NBA, but I never thought they’d retire my jersey and number. ‘Dude, that’s sweet. Man, I’m kinda sorry for pranking your ass now.’ Ryan was half hearted in the apology. He’s never been one to say he’s sorry. ‘Nah man, that’s cool. I was so excited from this news, or else I’d be asleep. But I’m going to go, I got some things to take care off. We’ll talk tomorrow, all right?’ Me and Ryan said our good nights. I couldn’t help but wonder what Mike and Luke were doing at this same time. But for some reason, I found myself wondering about Kano too. I don’t know why, but I wondered what he was doing right now. More than likely, they all were sleeping, but dammit…. Today was a good day, and I couldn’t fall asleep to save my life. And to think, only 3 weeks ago, I was stabbed in the back at Shanghaied. And now, I’ve worked my way into the world title tournament, beating Kano and proving who the REAL legend is. I formulated and put together a group of wrestlers, that are 100 times better than Propaganda, Kris Kanton, Brian Arson and Jon Kano all rolled into one. And now, to top it all off, I’m going to be honored at UNC Chapel Hill. GO ME. Things are looking up. Now to top it off, to put that cherry ontop of the sundae of life…. Me and Ryan are going to show Kano and Kanton why we hate them. Oh, and we’re going to beat them in the match too. Because that’s what we do, we win matches, we beat people and we look good doing it. Wow, 12:30 am. I better not call mom, she’s probably finished a bottle of vino, and is passed out by the pool. And Dave, well, his doctor ass has early appointments, or was that early tee times…. I don’t remember. Fuck it, they can wait till tomorrow. After all, I’m going to invite them to come to the school and the show, so they can attend the ceremony live. Yeah, I’m happier than a fat kid with a dozen cupcakes covered with gummy worms right about now.
