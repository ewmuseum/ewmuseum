Hunted Saga Part Ten: Trouble is always around the corner...

5 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5/4/02 Prologue… Hey, thanks for the help back there!” Aeryn shouts over to Fleming. Fleming walks over to Aeryn and Crichton and stands before them. “Hey that’s okay, let me guess your John Crichton.” Fleming declares. Crichton instantly felt his blood rush to his head, he didn’t like this, he didn’t like this at all. But then he figured that nobody from the army had found his identity and if they had he would probably in there custody. “Yeah, why?” Crichton asks hesitantly. “Well, because your coming with me!” Fleming declares. Pointing a pistol at Crichton. Crichton takes a step back in shock. “What the hell are ya doin’?” Crichton questions. “I’m from a special government agency, hired to track you down John. Now come quietly and no blood will be spilt.” Fleming replies. Crichton nods, “Ah right!” Crichton simply says as he knocks the direction of the barrel of the gun away from him, then whacks Fleming across the face. Fleming stumbles backwards momentarily in shock at the speed of Crichton’s attack. Crichton follows it up by booting Fleming’s wrist, forcing Fleming’s gun out of his hands. Crichton then plants a knee into Fleming’s gut before, ramming his face into the metal boot of his white car. Fleming doesn’t get up from this, but Crichton doesn’t wait to find out and gets into the driving seat of the car and starts the engine. Fleming pushes himself up off the ground and slumps over the boot of the car. The car then begins to move and in a second the car moves away at speed. Fleming simply slumps to the floor on his back. A smile grows upon his face and it slowly develops to a maniacal laugh, as the camera fades to black. The camera opens in streets of Chicago, Illinois once more as Fleming stops his manaical laugh and gets further to his feet, hold his injured head. Fleming barely noted the cut on the side of his forehead and decided that it wasn’t important. “You’ve won this round John Crichton, but there will be other rounds you can guarantee it!” Fleming declares more to himself, than anybody else. Fleming then walks away out of the scene as the camera cuts to the open road of the Nevada desert. The camera zooms in a little upon this Nevada desert road and focuses in on a white car. The camera cuts to inside the car, as Crichton is driving the car and Aeryn is looking at a road map of the area. Crichton looks over a little and then puts his eyes back onto the road with a smile on his face. “We’re lost aren’t we?” Crichton inquired to the struggling Aeryn. Aeryn struggles to get the map under control. “No.” She replies rather guiltily for Crichton’s liking. “Okay then, when’s the next turning?” Crichton questions further. “A couple of miles down the road.” Aeryn simply states. “That’s it we’re pulling over at the next service station and we’re asking for directions.” Crichton commented. “Okay, but I am right, ya know.” Aeryn replies almost hurt. “We could use some gas and something to eat.” Crichton explains. “Yeah I suppose so.” Aeryn idly comments It had been a couple of days since the incident at the bank in central Chicago, Illinois and Crichton had tried not to think about the situation, for it had almost deadly implications for his and Aeryn’s situation. However, he realised that one of these he must face his fears and his suspicions, and he figured now was a good time as any. The man known as Fleming, assisted Aeryn at the robbery and didn’t appear to be after them in any kind of way. Judging by Crichton’s dealings on the battle field and his too many meetings with such government types will still in service of his country, in the US military, Fleming was attempting to get to know his enemies to understand them better and plot their next moves better. The question was, had Fleming learnt enough about them to plot their next moves? Crichton very much doubted it, but there was one leak, which Crichton instantly thought of. The bank manager, Mr. Lebowitz. If he had survived his serious heart attack, if Spider-Man had got him to the hospital in time, could Fleming have questioned him and found that they were in fact touring the Nevada desert? This was a possibility and was also one of the things that Crichton was trying not to think about. Crichton realised that he was incredibly lucky to be able to escape his last encounter with the well-trained Fleming, he also realised if they ever met again, Crichton had doubts to whether he could elude him or not. Crichton suddenly felt incredibly depressed and decided it was time that he took his mind off proceedings. “Aeryn, did you manage to find out who I facing in my match this Sunday at Spring Fling?” Crichton asked trying to both take his mind off the current situation at hand, and of course to better his mood. “Yeah, you have a chance to win a title!” Aeryn replied. Crichton looked around at her in amazement. “Really? I do? Which title?” Crichton questions.Aeryn sighs. “Calm down, one question at a time. You will have a chance to capture Jay Sylo’s Rising Star title at the PPV.” Aeryn answers. Crichton looks at her once more, then puts his eyes back on the road, almost as if he’s trying to figure something out. “Your not telling me everything are you?” Crichton asks. Aeryn looks at him for a second then looks down. “No. You do have a CHANCE at capturing Sylo’s title, but your not the only one.” Aeryn states. “Eh? Aeryn you’d better just tell me everything, I’m gonna find out anyhow.” Crichton replies with a hint of anger etched into his voice. “Alright. At Spring Fling there will be an Over-the-top-rope Battle Royal to determine the Rising Star champion, nine or ten guys will be involved in total, and Jay Sylo will obviously be taking part in the proceedings.” Aeryn explained calmly. “Oh shit! I hate Battle Royals! They’re so unpredictable and overall a poor way of determining any kind title.” Crichton states profusely. “What do you mean?” Aeryn questions. “Well for example, the toughest guy, the most deserving guy of that damn title, could be double teamed and eliminated and then a guy who hasn’t earned the title, who isn’t the toughest guy or the most deserving guy has the potential to win.” Crichton declares. “That’s not the only thing John.” Aeryn confesses. “What? What else is there?” Crichton questions. “All of your stable mates are involved in the Battle Royal also, John.” Aeryn replies. “Oh great, so I may have to fight both Andy Murray and Ben Pailthorpe as well!” Crichton declares in a too loud of a voice. “Look on the bright side. You three could work together and kick everybody else’s ass!” Aeryn states, hoping to lighten Crichton’s mood. Yeah I suppose your right.” Crichton replies, visibly calming down.The camera pans to the left, to see the open road through the front windshield of the car as a road sign begins to, quickly, get closer and closer. The camera pans back to the right as it captures Crichton’s expression upon his face after seeing the road sign. Crichton nudges Aeryn, almost in excitement. “Hey look, services 40 miles. At last I’m so hungry I could eat several horses. With fries obviously.” Crichton comments, anything to keep his mind off of the Fleming situation. The camera cuts to a gas pump and a diner, what some people constitute as ‘Services’, as Crichton’s white car pulls into the Diner’s car park and comes to a shuddering alt. After a few seconds the engine goes dead and both Crichton and Aeryn step out of the car. They compose themselves a little before entering the diner door. The camera then cuts to the inside of a ‘Zemoff Industries’ helicopter, as we see Fleming looking down at the terrain before him. “I can’t see him yet, but I know he’s around here somewhere.” Fleming comments. The camera pans to the right as we see four or five guys, in full combat gear, bullet proof vests and army issue machine guns. The camera then takes the viewpoint of Fleming, using binoculars as he scours the landscape beneath him. Then our view comes across a service station, with a gas station and a diner. The camera cuts to a close up of Fleming. “Down there! Land near there, but out of all possible viewpoints!” Fleming commanded. The camera cuts to inside the diner, where we see Crichton and Aeryn sat across from each other in one of the diner’s booths. “So, Aeryn have you got me tapes of the guys, so I can study their moves and the such.” Crichton questions intently. “Yeah, they said that they would leave in the locker room for us.” Aeryn answered. Crichton nodded looking down at his ‘Mega-Max Burger’, breaking a piece off and placing it into his mouth. “Excellent. That’ll come in handy.” Crichton commented. Aeryn smiled. “Your worried, aren’t you?” Aeryn asked. “Well it is my first title chance, that ain’t gonna end in a brawl.” Crichton replied. “No, it’s gonna start a brawl.” Aeryn commented. Crichton simply nodded in agreement. Crichton picked his burger up and took a bite, as the window, a few booths down, smashed and something hurtled through hitting the back wall. Smoke began emanating from the object, as the camera fades to black.
