The Experiment

2 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12/02/02 The wind sweeps, ever cold and unmerciful over a lonely bridge. Cars race by, spewing the heat of their pollution into the December air, as the roar of their engines break the silence and then drift away like so many memories. Assorted trash and debris waft on the breeze, flitting and fluttering without rhyme or reason. Pieces of plastic, paper, refuse long discarded blows on and on, it's destination unknown but yet known all the same. Oblivion. Down, down the bridge we travel to the gaps beneath where dank and dreary, it awaits the fall of the sun for one more day. Here the biting chill of the winds are somewhat blunted.....though the pain in the heart of one who sits now in the midst of it may never be. Hunched over a pathetic excuse for a fire, this man rubs his hands furiously as he tries to build friction, to build any measure of heat that he can. Around him discarded bottles of cheap liquor rest, evidence of the other measures he has taken to survive in this existence. As he looks around, he longs in some bitter and secret place in his heart for when he could see needles as well in the dirt and rubble around him. But circumstances have long since robbed him of the money necessary to purchase drugs. Drugs are too good for him. Life is too good for him. A terrible shudder rocks the body of this man, as the wind changes direction almost as if to spite him. Whimpering, he backpedals into the far recesses of his pathetic home to try and escape. But one cannot escape the wind when it decides to pursue you. And one cannot escape memories either. They will follow you until your dying day. Shivering, the man's teeth chatter inside his skull. The sun will be setting soon for him, another day passing, the cold of night descending and taking it's horrible toll. Will he survive? Does he WANT to survive? This questions race through him, equaled only by the visions of the past. Horrors, nightmares that never ever end. The homeless man curls into a ball, trying to survive not only against the elements but against himself. But he finds this is a losing battle, and is rapidly becoming one not worth fighting over. 'I know.' A voice? Understanding, though deep as thunder and very rough. What is it, the broken man wonders? A new trick of his mind, or merely some bored police officer looking to..... 'I'm here to help.' Again the voice. Speaking to him, but why? Why would it..... Realization, shock dawns on him. His heart skips a beat in his chest, causing a wave of pain to rock through his body. Realization that he had not spoken a word aloud, yet this voice responded to him all the same. 'Yes, I know.' Slowly, ever so slowly the homeless man opens his eyes. What stands before him is a tall gentleman, wearing a long black trenchcoat and looking on with an expression of calmness. 'Who.....are.....', the transient rasps before breaking into a coughing fit. He rolls about in the muck, the filth, the mire of his own life led so horribly astray as he struggles for breath. And they say smoking kills you. He would laugh, if the pain allowed such. 'I can allow such, sir.' Once more he looks up to this strange fellow, who gazes down upon him not blinking an eye. 'You gonna get me more hooch?', he chuckles lowly before coughing yet again. The man above him however simply shakes his head. 'As cliche as it may sound, 'hooch' will not solve your problems. Nor will it add warmth to your body, or sanity to your mind.' Groggily, the waves of pain still pulsing through his form, the homeless man pulls himself to a sitting position. With his arms wrapped around his knees, he takes in his guest seriously for the first time. 'And what will solve my problems?', the poor man asks. 'What will solve my problems? I've been under this bridge for the better part of.....' He stops short, his eyes blank. He tries to remember, but even this fails him. 'I.....can't remember how long.' Slowly the man blinks, shaking his head. Tears then stream down his eyes, as he weeps into the cold air. 'I can't remember. Can't remember.....can't.....can't.' 'We all have trouble remembering our pasts,' the stranger replies with an air of nostalgia in his voice. 'But that isn't a bad thing sometimes....some things are better left forgotten.' 'No kidding.' Wiping his face of the already freezing tears, the lost one reaches out for the bottle at his side. He raises it to his lips, before remembering he has no liquor left to consume. Anger flashes through him. Anger, rage, and utter and complete loss. 'Yeaaaaaaaaaaaaaaargh!' With a wail of despair the man throws the bottle towards the pylon of the bridge. It clinks harmlessly to the ground, not even breaking.....both his memory and his strength seem to have left him. 'What have I become.....it didn't used to be like this,' he sobs quietly. 'It didn't used to be like this at all.' 'I know.' 'You know,' the homeless man spits weakly. 'You know a lot, but what can you do about it? Nothing. The same as me.' 'You do nothing because you WANT to do nothing.' 'Huh?' 'Yes. You know I am right.....this is your choice, isn't it? To be here, under this stinking bridge in the refuse and the rubble and the trash.....if you don't like it, why don't you leave?' 'BECAUSE IT'S TOO LATE FOR ME!', the lost man shrieks. 'Ahhh....' Once more the tears stream as the man broken lies in the dirt. Footsteps fall, as his strange guest walks to him and places a hand on his head. 'What are you doing?' 'The real reason I'm here,' he replies somewhat distantly. 'We're going to do a little experiment. To find out if happiness is truly what you're seeking.' 'Experiment?' 'Yes,' the stranger answers. 'I'll need to ask you to stand up.' Shakily, the transient does as asked. He wobbles, but the tall fellow holds him steady. 'Now, close your eyes.' 'I don't know what you're up to.....', says the man sardonically but he does as asked. 'Count to three.' 'What is this?' 'Sir, count to three. And then open your eyes.' 'Ugh.....fine,' he snorts. 'One....two.....' three 'HAPPY BIRTHDAY!' Once more the man's heart flutters, as his eyes snap open. He looks around in bewilderment to find his envirnment altered, his company changed from trash and debris to actual warm human beings. So warm in here. 'What is.....', he asks as a beautiful young lady embraces him. All around, smiling faces chuckle and beam. 'It's your birthday, silly.' She smiles at him, kissing his now somehow clean shaven cheek. He rubs the spot where her lips met, feeling both the warmth and the strange lack of mangy beard. 'How am I.....' 'Come on,' she says while ignoring her silly husband. 'Open your gifts.' Taking him by the hand, the lass begins leading the fellow through the room. The assorted guests break into a round of song, dancing and making merry as the pair reaches their destination. 'I....don't know what to say,' the man stutters garning more laughter from the partygoers. 'You don't have to say anything,' the girl answers with a smile. 'The fact you're here is enough.' A smile crosses the man's lips as well, the first smile to cross them in forever. He hugs her tight, holding her in his arms. A short time later, and the party has ended. In a darkened bedroom the man sits, looking into the eyes of his beloved. 'I.....' 'What's wrong,' the lady asks. 'You haven't seemed yourself.' Thoughts pass through the man's mind, fleeting and brief. Confusion sets in, as he shakes his head. 'But....I went to Vietnam,' he remembers with much effort. 'I got.....I got.....' Slowly he blinks, the fog refusing to leave him. His love merely looks on again, smiling. 'I have something else to tell you,' she says softly. The man looks up at her, into her blue eyes. He begins to say something, but she silences him with a single finger to his lips. 'I'm pregnant.' Silence falls over the chamber. The man looks on.....then breaks into a massive smile. 'Oh my god.' He throws his arms around her, hugging and kissing her cheek and holding her close. 'Hehe, stop that!, she giggles. 'I thought though you couldn't.....' 'I thought so too, but....well, I am!', she answers happily. 'Congratulations, daddy.' 'Daddy.....I didn't think I'd ever hear myself called that.' Laughing to each other, the pair hugs again and dances about. The man laughs, honestly laughs....happiness in his mind. Such happiness. Happiness. Happiness. Happinessssssssss Under a lonely bridge, a burned man stands over the body of a lost soul. He looks down at his eyes, wide and staring, his final expression one of utter joy.....slowly, he kneels and shuts them with one hand. 'So.....', he intones to himself, 'this is how it turns out. 'Happiness, peace.....all these things you craved. Once you got them, your heart couldn't even take it.' Standing again, the demon shakes his head. 'Happiness caused your death. Had you been miserable, had I allowed you to continue your path you may have survived for years to come.....' Sighing, he looks on. 'But I wonder what would have been the worse fate?' Removing his trenchcoat, the stranger throws it upon the dead body of the transient. Paying no mind at all to the winds blowing against his bare skin, he turns and makes his way back into the darkness whence he came......
