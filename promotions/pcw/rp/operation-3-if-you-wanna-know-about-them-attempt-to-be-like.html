Operation 3... if you wanna know about them... attempt to be like them?!

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: not entered Three days until showtime! That’s all there is left until the biggest Pay-Per-View event of the Summer is allowed to get underway. Many athletes from the ranks of PCW will be performing on the night, some of them ending personal battles with opponents… others starting what could be a tale for decades to come. Some stars are already on that ‘Superstar’ platform… others will use the night to give reason for an inclusion in that possible Elite. One team looking to make it into that band of elite people, is the ‘Musical and Lyrical’ duo that parade around the many arenas of America calling themselves… ‘The Pretty Cool Guys’. Over the last few days, we’ve seen Rich Sherwin and Ryan Moore attempt to do things that would seem out of the ordinary… at least when viewed upon by any normal member of the general public… although, for this very interesting setup of life, the two guys have been trying to figure out a way to get the ‘unfair advantage’ so-to-speak for their ‘Tag Team Championship’ Elimination match on Sunday Night at the Wrestle Vessel Pay-Per-View. The first operation attempt saw both Sherwin and Moore attempt to get the favour of the female official that would be in charge of their match… this would have been the greatest target to obtain, especially since this woman is the character that’ll be making the pinfall… or calling the submission on whoever does what. In an unfortunate turn of events, and the lingering ‘feelings’ that this monstrosity of an official was holding for Sherwin, the plan was yet another project in the book of the boys that fell through… something that wasn’t expected you wonder? Lets just say that short-cuts don’t quite guarantee victory like they do in the video-games. Our second attempt then saw Sherwin and Moore… try once to get to the place known as the “Neo Void” and attempt to live through the experience. The scene saw the boys throw themselves back onto mats… attempting to recreate the kind of experience that will probably be in-store for them at the hands of Akiyama on the show. However, in their typical fashion… the desired effect was far from obtained and the characters did more damage to themselves than gain any reasonable answer for surviving the “Neo Void Banishment”. Today is going to be the last day for jokes and games… its going to be the last day of devious ideas before the show itself, why would this happen to be the last day? Well, when a team comes through with two failures over the course of two very short days… you do come to conclusion that playing to fail just isn’t the way you do things in this day and age. The rest of you, who may or may not care, will probably be wondering what course of action could be taken next. They’ve tried to influence a specific character… they’ve tried to withstand the most feared move in the contest… so, what in Gods name could be left to try? Well… there is supposed to be a saying that goes along the lines of “The Way to defeating the enemy… is to truly know the enemy.” I’m not sure if this is real… I’m not even sure if its something I just made up, but what I do know, is the basis of that very line, is something that you’re all going to have to experience today in this very… subliminally cool promo, brought to you… by the ‘One and Only PoP-tastic duo’, “The Pretty Cool Guys”… The scene slowly but surely begins to open again within the confinements of an office… its not the kind of office that would belong to a typical man, its not the type of office that would belong to any general working man for that matter. Instead, the location that we’re currently viewing in all of its glory… just happens to be one that has been set-out in the kind of fashion that would suggest its owner was the member of a ‘Political Party’ or what-not. The camera moved around the inside of this office… getting a good look at fake achievement awards for various different political movements that had never seen the light of day. “The Radialist Movement of PoP”… “Prime Minister of PoP Activities 2002”… “6th Grade Spelling Competition Winner”… things of that sort were hung up. After looking around at the décor, the view turned to a large pine desk, an object that was covered with loose papers… and paperweights, strangely enough, the two most important items on a desk-space. The view shifted up a little and a black, leather chair was seen, its back toward the camera. We waited patiently for a few moments before the chair slowly began to turn in a clockwise direction… it came round to completing a 180 degree turn… but passed it without warning and completed the 360. We assumed that the next turn would have seen the chair stop after 180 degrees… but once again, it span around and completed a second full turn. Instead of making the third run of things, there was a knock at the door of the office and the camera turned around almost instantly. “Come in…”, the voice from the chair said, speaking in something of a familiar manner. There was a loud creaking sound as the door slowly opened to reveal a character standing in the way. It didn’t take too long to see that the character himself was Ryan Moore… but the reason as to why he was dressed up in clothes of the sort… was quite confusing indeed. Our paperweight hero was dressed in a typical business suit, generic down to the very word. Accompanying this look, Moore had placed on a pair of specs, something that apparently made people seem a lot more intellectual than they are… at least, that’s what its like in some cases around the world. Ryan continued to make his way into the office, looking rather good and taking each step in a careful manner, making sure that he didn’t do anything to scuff up the shoes being worn. “Hmm… Rich, do you think think its seriously necessary to be dressing up like a couple of Political Fools… prancing around in an office of the sort while we attempt to find out what life is like this way… I mean, we’re going to be trying to be “Politically Incorrect” on their Wrestling ability… not their ability to fix an election to become ‘President of the World’ or something.”, Ryan said, continuing to walk into the office at the same time. The camera looks back around at the place where the chair is and notices that this time it has been turned around in the 180 degree sense. Sherwin looked around at the interior of the office and nodded approvingly, giving Ryan the time to walk up to his desk before saying anything about his comment. “Well Ryan… you wanted to try and get into the “Neo Void” yesterday… try to survive the vicious attack that is Akiyama… and now, I’ve decided that we should try to be like the smartest fighters in the contest. Everyone says how Akiyama is going to win… but he’s a guy who likes to base all of his attacks on Raw-Power and nothing else… The Heartbreakers are a tandem that are just… so… far behind us in the worlds of both Musical and Wrestling skills… but, then, Politically Incorrect… are not only a team of pretty average Wrestlers… but they’re a duo of above average tactics.”, Sherwin informed his friend, trying to get across the point of being like the enemy… to understand their plan and gaming tactics. Ryan caught on almost immediately and understood the course of action that required for preparations sake. Sherwin decided to carry on in any case with, “… you see, this contest on Sunday Night, while we may have failed the other plans… these two are the ones that we need to eliminate at all costs. Everyone else… who cares about them? Who really gives two little jelly-beans about the other three people? Eliminate “P.I” and the Tag Team Titles… will… be… ours!” Ryan jumped up and down on the spot like an excited schoolgirl getting asked on her first date. It was like he was almost sure this single plan was going to be the single most important factor that will determine whether the win… or the loss will be in their column by the night after. He stopped his little parade after a few seconds and looked to Rich with a big grin spread across his face, “… I can’t believe that we could be THIS close to actually winning those damn Tag Team Titles… and to take out the smartest players of the game first, Richie… you are without a shadow of a doubt, the most influential and smartest man within the PoP/Wrestling industry.” “I know… I know… but you know what? This company would have been about several thousand times better if I really was at the helm of it all… just picture the kinds of events and the kinds of matches that we would see week in… and week out! Rich Sherwin v Big Alan Steele in a ‘Singing Contest’… Ryan Moore v Darkstar in a ‘Dancing Contest’… oh my, and how about, “The Pretty Cool Guys” against that “Akiyama” in a “Akiyama can’t touch us or he’s fired from PCW” match… that would so totally be the coolest thing ever.”, Sherwin claimed… dreaming up matches that would never be allowed to happen within the entire history of this business. He relaxed in his big leather chair and span around in a full turn once, letting the wind hit his face and the dreams of his time as PCW Owner pass without question. Ryan Moore thought about the match that had been posed by Sherwin… and began to like what he was ‘day dreaming’ about… but soon became a little upset at the fact that he wasn’t in the fantasy booking against Big Alan Steele. “Hold on a sec… why aren’t I able to go against Alan… and why can’t you go up against Darkstar?!”, he asked, sounding rather annoyed… but losing the plot on the fact that this was nothing more than a dream. Sherwin looked up to his friend and sighed a long sigh, he placed his hands behind his head and sat back… forcing Moore to understand the wrong of his ways. “Well…”, he began, “… since I’ve just made a complete fool out of myself, asking about being in a match that doesn’t exist and what-not… what exactly are we going to do in this office? What are we going to do… that’ll make us anywhere near as… clever as them?” Ryan didn’t sound too confident now… he was beginning to lose faith in the idea that was playing out as we spoke. There was always the small chance that something like this could help them to win their respective match on Sunday Night… but the chances of that happening, slim to none in the book of odds. “Ryan… while I can understand the problems that you may have… I mean, for a while I wasn’t sure if this plan was going to work, but then… I found a book in the local library that explained how to know your enemy better than yourself… and it suggested, that the first step to doing this… was to spend a little bit of time in their shoes… and in our cases, that little bit of time will be spent as fake Politicians!”, Sherwin beamed in an almost triumphant sounding shout. Ryan tried to look for a positive side in everything that Sherwin had just informed him… but for some reason, he just couldn’t do that small task. Maybe it was the fact that Sherwin said “Step One” which killed his ability to find positives… or maybe its just the fact that while he knows its going to be wrestling that wins the Tag Titles… Sherwin isn’t too sure of this important fact. “Erm… Rich, I know that you just told me the whole deal about this plan and everything… and how you got this idea from a book and all… but did you say “Step One” because you felt like adding a bit more personality to the thing… or, are my fears going to be confirmed when you inform me that its like a twelve step program that lasts for a good… I don’t know, three… maybe four weeks or so?”, Ryan asked, his voice really beginning to show blatant signs of fear and disappointment. One could claim that Sherwin was a little stupid… maybe even a little hapless for thinking that a whole book on how to be like your enemy could be breezed through in the matter of a single day… maybe if they’d had a week to go until the “Pay-Per-View”… a thing like this would have worked… but with, three days left until showtime… heh, its not likely… infact, to be as frank as humanly possible… this little plan being totally completely before… is not possible. Well, the only way I see this being possible… is if all of time stopped for the world, allowing Sherwin and Moore to carry out this thing without anyone else losing a second of their day. “Well Ryan… to be perfectly honest with you, I didn’t say that I had time to read the whole book… the thing just looked so boring, so I did the thing that any smart individual would do… I looked through the contents page for something that was remotely important, and figured I could base the whole plan from that.”, Richie informed Ryan, sounding rather confident in his abilities to have achieved something remotely worthwhile before the end of the day. Ryan began to pace around in the confinements of the small office… shaking his head from side to side, not even beginning to risk having faith in his Tag partner. With his worst fears confirmed, Ryan decided that the only thing to do… would be to simply sit through this plan and hope something will actually come to them both when it was all said and done…. “Oh why… why did I have to get myself involved in these… almost pointless schemes!”, he muttered as the scene slowly began to fade out into the confinements of the color black. With Sherwin and Moore coming to the end of their campaign of ‘Advantage Attempts’, we find everyone else doing whatever they need to do to prepare for the tough contest ahead. Can the boys from PoP City actually find it within themselves to make it to the very end of the yellow brick road… with Gold Belts around their waists? Like stated in the first promo… followed through in the second… and repeated in the third, there is only one way you’ll find the answer to this question… and that’s by tuning into the “Wrestle Vessel” Pay-Per-View… Sunday Night… L!VE!!!
