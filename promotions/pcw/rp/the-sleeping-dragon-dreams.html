The Sleeping Dragon Dreams...

Date: Who cares, just not no-showing Turmoil, which I&#039;ve no intention of winning. Bodies everywhere; the ruins of a fallen city around me. Fires all around, no buildings left standing. Even the trees are knocked over. Bodies everywhere; overturned cars and massive traffic accidents. Hydrants spewing foul water upon the ground as hail beats into the earth from above. Bodies everywhere; the skies red from fire and smoke, the rivers and lakes red with blood. Bodies everywhere; and all of it because of me. All of it because of me. They rise. The bodies, the victims of my uncontrolled power. Some of them smashed flat in areas, others missing limbs. Bloodsoaked throwaway husks where life essence once resided, all of them rising from their dormancy. They can't speak, they have no readable expressions upon their obscured and sometimes missing faces. I know that they are angry; they're angry at me. I never meant to hurt them. Didn't want it to happen like this. Didn't want to destroy. But I did. They all come for me, wanting revenge. I killed them, and their vessels, controlled by their restless souls, had risen to claim me. Anyone else would run. I didn't. They wanted my life and I owed it to them. They take me, bear down upon me in an immeasurable rioting mob. They rip at my flesh and pull me apart limb by limb. I don't try to fight them; this is what I deserve. Their teeth sink into me; I thank them for every bite and scratch, bless them as they pull my head open and feast on my worthless innards. I don't try to fight them; this is what I deserve. Down, down they pull me into the earth. Down, passing me arm by arm, the screams of the eternally scarred souls I have so unjustly taken. They carry me, they drag me, piece by piece, into the underworld. Mephistopheles floats above, greeting me with a tip of a burning red top hat and taking mannerisms similar to the Cheshire Cat in 'Alice in Wonderland.' He looks odd wearing a green pinstriped business suit and sporting cloven hooves and horns. He greets me, welcomes me to my destiny. The victims, they don't deserve this. They don't deserve to be here. They wanted to be, though. Not to be punished, but to watch and partake in mine. Fire and brimstone, the bodies of the damned cooking and festering, strung out on barbed wire and hacked to bits by axe-wielding demons, having their organs eaten by vultures to grow back and go through the process all over again. This was my destiny, my eternity. I knew not what I did, if I did anything, to be fated to a forever-time of being meat, a chopping block... Charles Manson and Ted Bundy were jaywalkers compared to me, and yet I don't know why I was chosen to be what I was. I didn't want to hurt anyone. But I did. There was no hope for me. No chance for a happy life. No chance for a peaceful death. I wonder why I was created. What purpose could I have but to destroy? How could I be of any good? How could I ever live in a world full of people when they were the cause of my destructive nature? 'I must be cursed,' I repeated to myself as my brains were bashed into jelly with large sledgehammers while still in my skull. Original sin. Adam and Eve ate those freaking apples, and so they opened the Pandora's Box of human evolution. I'm the Sleeping Dragon, fated to judge the universe and decide whether it deserves to continue or should be wiped out. Did I ask for this responsibility? No. Original sin. They ate the apples of knowledge, now I'm forced to use that knowledge. Thank you, frickin' Adam and Eve. I see them waving to me from a pool of maggots that digest their skin. 'You are welcome,' they say calmly, accepting of their fate and taking it not as punishment, but as a cleansing. The maggots are some pool for ablution. How I wanted to jump in with them, but God himself reached out his hand and erected a great wall that I could not breach, could not climb. Of all the times where there needed to be some sort of safe structure that I could not destroy, this had to be the time when they were erected all around me. I wish it didn't have to be like this. I didn't mean to hurt anyone. I'm truly sorry; I couldn't feel any worse than when I wake up in the morning and realize that I'm still alive. But that can't ever be enough. I will live and die with this, and I will never be clean. Never clean...
