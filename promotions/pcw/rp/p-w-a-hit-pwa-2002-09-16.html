P.W.A hit PWA

16 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 16/09/02 A figure lurked in the shadows. It had the instantly recognizable clothing - a crimson sleeveless trenchcoat that swooped to the ankle. A black T-shirt and a small piece of armor in the chest to protect the vulnerable heart. Dark blue jeans and heavy industrial boots, polished black that reflected just a smidgeon of the light. Purple fingerless gloves with a gaudy orange wristband and the red belt with a silver buckle that had the letter N engraved into it. Purple strands fell from the head and wrapped around the shoulders. Finally, the unmistakable circular red lenses sat on his nose. With a heavy thud, it took a step forward into the light, camouflaging his face still in shadow. 'Are you going to make it act like you all day long?' Gaz rammed his shiny shoe into the back of the coat, forcing the EvaNerezzion to shuffle forward into the glare of the bulb above. 'Look, it needed a dramatic entrance. Besides, last time we tried to make it act independently, the bloody thing ran around in circles and beat his chest laughing.' Came the bitter reply. The mechanical whirring of the robot stopped dead as Nerezza deactivated the remote control. On all sides, the duo were surrounded by oily rags, nuts, bolts, sheets of metal and tools. The garage they had rented was illuminated by a single bulb hanging overhead that provided just enough light for the two to work on their machine. 'So what are attempting this time around?' Gaz tapped the back of the robot's head, causing it to turn 180 in a scene familiar to The Exorcist. The grey metallic face was devoid of any form of feeling, despite the mouth being shaped to resemble Nerezza's own depraved smirk. 'We're going to send this to boldly go where no robot has gone before - to Primetime Wrestling Academy!' Gaz blinked at Nerezza's claim, then stared back into the lenses of the android, then back again. 'And why are we sending him to school already? He's too young, only 3 months! He's not ready yet!' The Jester cried in a mock panic. Nerezza saw the joke approaching and put a hand on Gaz's shoulder. 'No, Gaz. He's our little creation, he can stand on his own two feet now. He's got to go back out into that wide open world now.' As if on cue, EvaNerezzion falls forward onto his face, sending an array of springs and screws into the air. Gaz shot his tag partner a stern look. 'Why couldn't we have just been more careful...?' * * * The purple Hearse pulls up outside the PWA Training Centre. In a perfect line, a series of Dodge Vipers are parked, all with the doors unlocked and still playing music. The sound is an awful mixture of various hip-hop, rap and R & B. Nelly, Eminem and Cypress Hill clash in a dreadful compound of drumming. 'Must be a bunch of wigga gimmicks in the building.' Gaz says, unenthusiastically. As the trio step out of the Hearse, another Dodge Viper pulls up into the seemingly infinite parking space, blasting 'Without Me' by Eminem. The driver steps out, smoking presumably some illegal substances. Slamming the door behind him, he also fails to lock it and carelessly leaves his music blaring. The man stands around 6 feet tall and has an extremely muscular build. A goatee beard covers his chin and he has jewelry all over his body, be it rings, piercings or chains. Tattoos also litter his skin as well as a few scars. He immediately begins talking to himself. 'So this is PWA. This place isn't much. I'll be the champion and graduate and be promoted in no time.' Gaz looks around to see who he could possibly be conversing with and draws a blank, shrugging his shoulders at his partner and the robot. After a few seconds more of boasting to himself, a police car screeches next to him and two officers hop out, beating him to the ground. The man somehow dodges the punches that clearly couldn't miss and one punch to one of the officers lays him out cold, despite hitting his shoulder. The other produced a tazer from his belt and drives it into the man's back, forcing him once more to the concrete. Once he is neutralized, the cop walks over to his face and begins to shout at him. 'You ignored simple rules, son! You left your car unattended, created noise pollution, you dress like the other 30 we arrested earlier for possession of Marijuana and you no-sold our punches! Now change gimmick and TRY AGAIN!' he yells to the stunned victim. 'Damn. Reminds me of the good old days' Nerezza said in nostalgia. 'It's true when they say Grading is getting tougher, eh?' Gaz remarked in awe. The three stroll inside the building, where a receptionist looks up from her computer to see them enter. Instantly, she leans backwards, showing off her fabulous figure and ample chest. Her blonde hair is tied in a bun and a pair of spectacles enhance her pretty face with a touch of elegance. As she shifts her position on her chair, her skirt 'accidentally' rides higher, exposing more thigh. 'Good afternoon, Miss. We'd like an enrolment form for the Academy, please.' Nerezza says, gazing at her neck. 'And be day, we haven't got all quick' mumbles Gaz in an absent minded stammer. 'Coming right UP, sir...' she replies in a husky voice. Nerezza breaks his trance and notices a waste paper bin overflowing with screwed up pieces of paper. On the bin is a label - 'PWA applicant phone numbers'. Nerezza smirks and raises a hand. 'It's okay, the robot is the one enrolling, you don't have to flirt with us and we're not going to give you the numbers.' he says in a calming tone. 'THANK GOD!' yells the receptionist. 'You're the first men to walk in here and not expect to screw me later!' Gaz turns and utters a few expletives as the woman tugs her skirt down and buttons up her blouse, no longer sitting in a provocative manner. Before the woman can fetch a form, a familiar figure appears from the men's room... 'Rezz?' 'Mr Taylor, so glad you could take some time out' smiles the vampire. Standing before the trio in his in-ring attire of black full length wrestling tights with bright lightning-strike flames down the sides and hair messily slung over one shoulder in a ponytail was OSW's Scott Taylor. His bright green tattoo covers his upper arm and he stands with his confident grin. The two march forward and shake hands as though they had known each other for years. 'So you landed a place as a teacher after all... ' 'That's right.' Scott replies and gazes over at The Emerald Menace and the metallic replica of Nerezza. 'Is this the one?' he asks and points towards EvaNerezzion. 'Yessir, that's the most advanced robotic competitor in wrestling to date!' Gaz proclaims. The robot doesn't respond at all. 'So can we get him a little training?' Nerezza turns to Scott and lowers his sunglasses. 'Well, who DOESN'T want to train with The Legend In The Making?' Scott grins with a cocky tone to his voice. 'Now then, let's get to the ring where I can add another success story to my repertoire. Come along now!' Taylor marches off down a hallway, being followed by Pranks With Attitude and EvaNerezzion. * * * 'Let me give you guys a tour of my class.' Scott ushers the three into an elevator and joins them, pressing the B button to take them down to basement level. With a PING! the doors open to a dimly lit basement area. Candles line the room and barbed wire coats some of the corners. Tables, chairs and chains decorate the place and lined up in a single row are several men all dressed in clothing that advises any others to keep away, from hooded robes to black leather trench coats. Some sit with a bare torso, covered in scars and skull tattoos. Most of them have long black, unwashed and messy hair. The focal point for them is a TV and Video combo, showing Hellraiser on the screen. As Pinhead laughs, the gathering attempts to copy his evil laughter. Scott frowns and reaches across to a light switch, turning it on. The assembly covers their eyes and moans as though in pain. 'Do it with more feeling or you people walk out of here less pale or even worse... WITH NORMAL COLOURED SKIN!' Scott shouts in anger at them. A more sinister wave of laughter escapes that makes Gaz shudder as he sees Nerezza grinning. Taylor turns the light off and stands proudly with his hands on his hips. 'Good. Tomorrow I expect you all to laugh like that all the time. Make it good and I'll show you the secrets of how to ignite synchronized pyros.' A happy cry sounds from the crowd and the host turns to his guests. 'I have to hand it to you, those are some promising looking Dark-Gimmicked wrestlers you have there.' Nerezza says with a smile. 'And take it from me, I know a good one when I see them.' 'Thanks, Rezz. Not all PWAers were lucky enough to study under Hessian, but of course, every student knows that Scott Taylor is the best teacher out there. For example, I was originally going to teach them all power moves but I've learned that even people under 240lbs can make excellent spooksters. I even have one that goes to the top rope on occasion!' '....a dark gimmick that goes Aerial? Men must not defy nature's order, Scott!' Nerezza cried out. 'Don't worry, if it doesn't work out, we can say he's from Mexico and somehow remove those scars. Anyway, shall we press on?' The group retreats to the elevator where they leave behind a wave of sinister cackling. The door spring open once more inside a futuristic looking corridor. 'Where are we now?' Gaz says, gazing around in awe of the fantastic looking equipment. 'This is where we train men with the Army Gimmicks. We find a Commando setting makes them feel more comfortable. Before we go further, I must ask you to wear these...' Scott hands the three a set of dome helmets much like World War 2's. The trio strap them under the chin and get used to the feel before Scott enters the first room. Inside, two men seem to be going through vigorous chin-ups, weightlifting and cardio exercises. 'TEEEEEEEEEEEEEN-HUT!' Sergeant Scott yells. The army men snap from their exercises almost instantly and salute. 'Men, we have guests today. Please welcome General Gaz, Major Nerezza and Unit 01-N3R3ZZA.' 'SIR!' they shout in synchronicity. Gaz steps forward, looking at the recruits with a gleam in his eye. 'So what can you do, soldier?' Gaz says to one of the men. 'Sir, I am trained in several army fighting techniques, martial arts and I am qualified in over 50 types of firearms, Sir!' he responds in the strictest disciplined manner. 'Private, how many wrestling holds do you know?' The Jester asks, rolling up his sleeves. 'Sir, I am.... uhhh.... OOOWWW!' he screams as Gaz applies the simplest of arm bars to the man. The Emerald Menace releases the hold, disappointed. '57 varieties of bloody kempo and they can't tell a padlock from a headlock.' Nerezza exclaims, slowly wiping his gloved had down his face. 'Men, I told you to study what you see on the television and these videotapes!' screams Taylor, pointing towards the monitor in the corner. 'Sir, we were, but a Chuck Norris movie was on and well, we couldn't refuse Sir.' pipes the second soldier. Taylor turns an angry shade of red and takes a quick sidestep before his leg shoots out, connecting the flat of his foot with the chin of the now-silenced soldier. 'SHUT UP AND LEARN TO ACTUALLY WRESTLE! CONSIDER YOURSELF COURT-MARSHALED!!' With that, he turns to the still conscious soldier and nods at him. 'Carry on, Private.' The four leave the room and head back to the elevator. * * * 'So, gentlemen, how do you like my teaching skills so far?' Taylor asks, showing a set of spotless white teeth. 'Not bad at all, Scott. I think the EvaNerezzion has absorbed enough data here for a class.' Nerezza responds. 'Oh, not yet, I still have more to show you!' Taylor proclaims as the doors once more open. The hallway before them resembles a Nursery. Pale pastel shades of pink and blue stretch across the walls with happy-faced stars and clouds accompanying them. 'You have a place for Baby gimmicks? I don't think that's been done before...' Gaz muses. 'Oh, this is the area for our larger competitors' Taylor points to one of the rooms. Inside, kneeling down is an enormous man, measuring perhaps seven feet tall and weighing far above 300lbs. He is trying to push a star-shaped block inside a square hole. To his sadness, it simply doesn't fit, causing him to throw it against the wall in temper. Scott opens the door as a wave of sobbing breaks out from the giant. 'Now now, Gargantuoso' he says, patting the man on the back. 'All you have to do is match the right shape to the right hole and it'll go in.' The giant lumbers forward on his hands and knees, picking up the cube once more and this time, inserting the star shaped piece into the correct hole. As it slots inside, he giggles and gives himself a round of applause. Taylor slips out of the room, hopefully un-noticed and quietly closes the door behind him. 'Isn't this facility.... you know... just a bit of an insult to this man's intelligence?' Gaz says, scratching his head in confusion. 'WHAT intelligence?!' Scott explodes. 'Lesson one about anyone measuring that size - it can barely pull off anything that isn't a Gorilla Press Slam. Expecting a DDT from them is like expecting them not to hit their head on the way out. Their arsenal of moves is nothing close to mine. For example, I have the Super Kick. Standing Side Kick. Spinning Heel Kick. Inside Crescent Kick. Missile Dropkick. Standing Dropkick. Springboard Dropkick. Snap Suplex. Belly-To-Back Suplex...' 'Understood!' Gaz says, breaking the move list up. 'Shall we see what's next?' 'Ahem... right this way, gentlemen. We thought that grouping the super geniuses next to the less gifted might rub off on them. Allow me to show you the technical perfectionists.' At the end of the corridor, past the beaming sun, Taylor pushes against the wall and slides it across. The four stand in a room full of anatomical diagrams, muscle charts, plastic skeletons and wrestlers decked out in white lab coats. 'Ah, Mr Taylor! That was an excellent turning wrist entry twisting grip.' comes the happy cry from a spectacled man. '...what the bloody hell did he say?' Nerezza whispers in Gaz's ear. 'He liked the way Scott opened the door.' the Jester whispers back. 'How's the training coming along, boys?' Taylor grins. 'Well, Mr. Taylor...' the man says. 'This morning I pulled off an absolutely stunning Multiple Inverted Reverse Sweeping Oral Assisted Friction Swipe.' Taylor looks unamused. 'And that was...?' Nerezza whispered. 'He brushed his teeth.' Gaz said in anguish with lowered eyelids. Scott turned to his guests and ushered them out of the room, leaving the people to work on their technical move list. 'Guys, it's been tough so far... would you accompany me to the bar across the street? It's right next to the tattoo parlour and Dave's Heavy Metal And Rap Entrance Music.' 'As long as they serve Rezz Bull there...' the vampire nods. 'I'm kinda parched myself, I could use a drink. But let's visit the music store first, we could use a change from the usual opera Nerezza forces on me in that car.' * * * The four are greeted by the sound of Godsmack's 'I Stand Alone'. For some inexplicable reason, makeshift pyrotechnics go off on either side of the door. Posters of various contemporary music stars such as Fred Durst and Marilyn Manson line the walls and behind the counter is a man with long greasy brown hair, wearing a Metallica T-Shirt. Lining the shelves seem to be a limited choice of music... Linkin Park, Limp Bizkit, Korn and several one-hit wonders such as The Drowning Pool's 'Bodies'. On the other wall, Eminem, Nelly and Usher line the CD racks. Browsing over them is a man almost identical to the one seen beaten by the police earlier, down to the dress code and the drugs being smoked in his mouth. He picks up 'Hot In Herre' and walks over to the counter, pulling a massive bundle of money out that from the first look must have been in excess of a few thousand. 'Kizeep the chizange' he says, shoving the CD inside the pockets of his Fubu jeans. The store owner's eye grow wide as he picks up the cash and greedily shoves it inside the till. Gaz searches continually throughout the shelves and eventually strides to the counter. 'Excuse me, Squire... would you happen to have Now That's What I Call Bad Entrance Themes Volume 8?' The owner looks at him is disgust. 'WIZAT!? You can't be serious, ain't nuthin for no white boy who actually acts white in here! Only music for pizosers in this store!' he yells in an undecipherable accent. Gaz shrugs his shoulders and indicates with his head for the other three to leave. Out on the street, the quartet find themselves next to a nightclub with a giant neon flashing sign that reads 'Nu B's'. A long line of people stand in a queue, looking as though they have other things to do by their dress codes. Guarding the door are a pair of black men with dreadlocks in their hair and smart suits. They are built impressively and wear expensive looking Ray-Bans. One of them spots the wrestlers and begins to call out for them. 'Yo! Boys, c'mere!' he shouts to the surprised group. 'I didn't know you hung out with the bouncer...' Taylor says, puzzled. 'I don't...' Gaz replies, looking confused. 'I know one, but that ain't him.' Nerezza shrugs. All three turn towards the EvaNerezzion and glare at him, before letting out a synchronized 'Naaaaaaah.' They make their way around the barricades and to the front door but before they can reach him, a Dodge Viper pulls up in front of the club and another man with a goatee, tattoos, wifebeater shirt and Fubu jeans smoking weed hops out, playing Nelly's '#1'. He charges down the middle and steps between Gaz and Nerezza, shoving them aside. The Jester begins to charge up his 240 volt hand buzzer as the arrogant man steps up to the bouncers. 'YO! Lemme in, I wanna Freestyle with mah posse and crew, yo.' he says, making odd gestures with his hands. The bouncers look at each other in confusion. 'WHAT?!' 'Ah said, you gonna let me in or do I have to Bizeat yo asses up?' Before any action can take place, Gaz unleashed an electrical charge to the man. As he drops to the ground, Gaz walks to his head, leans over and starts yelling in his face. 'YOU DO NOT EVER QUESTION MY AUTHORITAH!' he bellows, as two policemen arrive and drag the fried student off into the distance. 'Wow, this grading thing is easier than I thought...' he giggles and blows the smoke from his hand buzzer. Scott Taylor turns to the bouncer that called him and motions towards the door. 'So, can we come in?' he asks, once again showing his winning smile. 'I know that no club is complete without The Next Big Icon, The Living Nightmare and The King Of Komedy!' 'No chance.' The bouncer says, standing his ground with a stern look on his face. 'And why the bloody hell not?' Nerezza says, preparing to show his fangs. 'Because you ain't bribed me or beat me up, man. Nobody passes through these doors without that happening! Don't you ever watch PWA television?' 'PWA doesn't have a television show...' Scott replies, crossing his arms over his chest. 'AW, SHIZNIT!' the bouncer shouts, taking an enrolment form from his pocket and ripping it into pieces. 'And the receptionist was gonna screw me, I could tell she wanted me!' Three simultaneous slaps to three foreheads followed, accompanied by a groan of disappointment. * * * The club was everything it promised. Multicoloured lights streamed throughout and all varieties of women from goth girls in see-through mesh and lacy dresses, blonde temptresses in tight snakeskin catsuits and casually yet provocatively dresses redheads littered the dancefloor. A small man in a black robe ran across the podiums, screaming 'C'MERE FIRESNATCH!' in a squeaky voice as behind him trailed a mountain of a man clad in medieval armour and a skull mask. 'So this is where the collective like to go when not stealing or getting high.' Nerezza deduced out loud. 'Aw, shit, this is the third time today...' Gaz said, banging is head into a wall. 'What's wrong now?' Scott asked. The Jester groaned and pointed at one of the stages, where a dozen men stood, all wearing wifebeater shirts, Fubu jeans, K-Swiss shoes and sporting eyebrow rings, goatee beards and collectively rapping to Eminem's 'Cleaning Out My Closet' badly. 'There's a thousand of them... they must keep them in cold storage and thaw one out everytime a grader gets sadistic!' the vampire cried in horror. 'Don't worry boys, I have the situation well in hand' says the Louisiana native, calling for a microphone to the PA system. 'AHEM. WOULD THE OWNER OF THE DODGE VIPER IN THE PARKING LOT PLEASE COME UP FRONT, THE POLICE WISH TO SPEAK TO YOU.' In just a moment, the entire stage empties as the back door of the club opens under the pressure of a fleeing wave of the former patrons. The only thing left on the stage is a collection of microphones. 'Scott, I have to say, allowing the EvaNerezzion to train under you may be the best decision we've made in a long time.' Nerezza says, mixing a can of Red Bull with some Vodka. 'I'll bet my Rezz Square that he'll emerge as the most dangerous thing in wrestling since that big Russian man... what's his name... Boris something...' 'Absolutely, Squire.' Gaz piped up. ' It's known that you're considered The Greatest Man Of All Time in some circles. Hopefully, you can make him the greatest robot of all time.' 'Well, just let me take care of him, boys. EvaNerezzion, take your future into your own hands. Now is the time to make the decision that will give you the best possible future.' Scott said to the robot. Scanning over the three wrestlers, the android took a step backwards, pointed one metallic arm into the air... ... and self destructed in a violent explosion, sending pieces of itself flying across the club and hitting various customers and damaging property. Gaz and Nerezza looked at each other, then to Scott, who gave an embarrassed glance to them both. The pair then took one more look at the few remaining chunks of the robot, then uttered together the same line in frustration. 'BOLLOCKS!'
