Prep Defense

30 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 30/09/02 “WHY do I have to do this again?” With the familiar, annoying and whiny tone of PCW’s Commissioner Eleanor Hall, we fade in on the little prep dressed a little differently than usual. Eleanor : “And then explain why I’m dressed like the guy who delivered our Chinese take-out...” Ellie~! has dispersed of her own brand of hooker-wear, seemingly under duress, and instead is decked out in full White Karate suit, even sporting a Black headband that holds her long and wavy light Brown hair with it’s Blond highlights. “Because, my little Poon Puppy, I want you to learn self defence.” Walking across a large Blue mat towards the prep is PCW Champion Xavier Kannon wearing his Black Karate pants with Gold Dragons that he wrestles in, this time paired with the matching remains of the suit. Eleanor : “SELF defence? Does that mean I have to do it?” The Scientologist tries to ignore her protests for her own good, trying to build up the enthusiasm that her apathetic body language is seriously lacking. Xavier : “Yes, the “SELF part kind of suggests that.” His wife repeats his words under her breath in her best “Tard” tone, tucking her tongue under her bottom lip then quickly trying to act normal as Kannon shoots his attention towards her. Xavier : “With Clyde and Akiyama wanting to, well, kill me... I need to make sure you are safe.” Even though his intentions are noble, Ellie~! just feigns a yawn and goes to check her watch before she realises she isn’t wearing it. Eleanor : “Again... WHY?!?!?! I already have Pepper Spray....” Kannon goes to interrupt her when she continues. Eleanor : “.... And a Gun.” Her husband’s eyes widen and stare at her in disbelief as she delivers the line with perfect calm. Xavier : “YOU HAVE A GUN.... YOU?.... Dear Hubbard...” Annoyed at his tone, the little prep puts her hands on her hips and stares right at him, her eyes boaring holes through his. Eleanor : “Well, no... BUT, you wouldn’t have attacked me if you thought I had. And WHAT to you mean by YOU?“ The Scientologist takes a pause and tries to think of the best words. Xavier : “Ummm.... Er.... Well, you did throw a knife at the Mail Man this morning.” Not caring that much, the prep just shrugs. Eleanor : “Well, then he, like, SO shouldn’t have been delivering Junk Mail!” Despite her protests, the Scientologist continues to press the point, wanting his wife protected above all things from his foes who are quite rightfully coming after him. Xavier : “Look, you’re learning self defence and that’s all there is to it...” The prep purrs and winks back at him, Xavier trying to stay strong as his wife licks her lips. Eleanor : “Mmmmmmmmm, forceful...” Shaking his head, Kannon keeps his focus. Xavier : “I’m going to charge at you, and you have to block my advance and throw me.... Ready?” Ellie~! just stands there playing with a loose strand of Blonde hair and not really looking as if she is expecting an attack. Eleanor : “You know, these people, like, want to kill YOU... Who would want to kill an Ellie~!?” As the prep ponders her own question, Kannon tries to take her by surprise and charges, only for Ellie~! to reach quickly into her suit’s pocket and pulls out a small spray can. Eleanor : “Like, SO take that!” Seeing the can, the Scientologist tries to stop in his tracks but his momentum thwarts him... Then, as Ellie~!’s Pink and Silver painted nailed thumb presses down a fine mist engulfs Xavier’s face. Xavier : “ARGHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHH!!!!!!“ His ahnds reach right for his eyes and encase his face as his yells continue. Eleanor : “I, like, SO lulled you into a TOTAL false sense of security!” In front of her, Kannon cusses enough to make Hubbard blush as he blinks as fast as Scientologicaly possible, trying to wash the Pepper Spray from his eyes while his wife just looks on.. Confused. Eleanor : “Um.....” Still looking at him oddly, she then kicks him as hard as possible in the shin... This being deceptively painful as the camera shows she has forgone the normal bare feet in favour of her usual heels. Xavier : “ARGH.... LAY OFF!!!“ She giggles as Kannon hops on his good leg, still blinded from the burning felt in his eye sockets that follows the optic nerve right to his brain. Eleanor : “Wow, this is.. Like... Realistic!” The hands move away from his eyes to show they are Red and puffy, staring at his wife like a test Bunny. Xavier : “Can’t.....See....” As her man groans, Ellie~! looks on in awe.... Seemingly enjoying her first self defence class. Eleanor : “Self-Defence rules..... Come for me again!” Hopping around on his good leg, Kannon stumbles and staggers towards his wife... The little prep reading this as the attack she called for. Xavier : “Eyes..... Burning....” Reaching out in front of him, groping around to see where he is a hand falls on Ellie~!’s left breast. Eleanor : “Ah... A Molester... Good Roleplay!” With his eyes exposed, the prep pokes him square in his eyeball with one of her intricately decorated finger nails. Xavier : “GHAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAR!!!!“ As Ellie~! grins broadly, Kannon whips his hand away from her chest and grabs his face again, his now burning eyes searing with fresh pain. Eleanor : “Ellie~! fights off the groper.... YAY ME!!!“ As the prep bounces up on the mat applauding herself, her husband crawls around on the floor, trying to find his water bottle before pulling it to him like it his life’s energy itself. Xavier : “Water... Water... Eyes.... Burn....” His quickly screws the top off and rolls onto his back, pouring the bottle’s contents down into his eyes, rinsing the Pepper Spray out helping relieve the pain from the eye poke. Eleanor : “I, like, SO kicked your ass!” The Scientologist breathes a relaxing sigh, the burning sensation being purged from his eyes as Ellie~! stands over him, looking down at her man curiously as the shot fades out to Black. “Er... Maybe you should, like, get me to teach you self defense!”
