Meet The Windsor's

10 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10 April 2003 Where am I? This was the initial question running through my head as my eyes roamed my confinement. The assortment of teddy bears and ghastly pink furnishings almost made me vomit for the dwelling could be nothing less than a female’s bedroom. The obvious giveaways were all in place and my surroundings are totally alien as I inspected more thoroughly, there had to be something I could relate to and that something soon materialized in the form of a picture. You’re joking? I indeed needed to question myself for ‘You’re Joking?’ was a line a recipient of the Jester would commonly use but now I find myself muttering that very same sentence with my heart racing and my adrenaline pumped to capacity. A cold sweat seeped along the brow of my forehead, I pinched myself for a second opinion but to no avail and the creases running faintly through my emerald business suit indicated that I must’ve been asleep for what seemed like an eternity. In all truth this wouldn’t be the first time the Jester had woken up in alien surroundings but in those cases my conquest was always asleep on the other side allowing for a stealth like exit but not today and certainly not when there’s a picture of the rooms proprietor looming down on me. Tessa? Maybe this was our delayed honeymoon. The only bond Tessa and I share is one of disgust for our marriage is nothing more than a sham. An accident that benefited Pranks With Attitude but not the Jester from a personal stand-point, indeed we now had the collateral to stage a contest at Spring Fling and the door was now open for a fifth Tag Team Championship reign but how long was it for that very same door to be shut back in my face? The picture continued to gaze down at me with its sweet and innocent smile but her real motivation behind that facial expression was camouflage for her motives towards me. I inspected the picture as its beauty engrossed me. Her brunette coloured hair was brushed to perfection and her angelic skin was glistening with that ‘Sadistic’ smile the icing on the cake, these feelings I had felt before, then suddenly like being struck by lightening Natalia’s face flooded my mind and the happiness turned to guilt. A sound filtered from the corridor, the pleasant murmuring of a young girl singing and like a fugitive I knew my time was elapsing so I rummaged through the bedroom for somewhere to hide. I was desperate, the wardrobe was the most obvious of choices but like a fool I opted for under the bed and lay motionless like a sniper in cold conditions, my teeth chattering subtly and that cold sweat made an unwelcome return. The singing was now above me and flooded the room like an infestation but my body had seized up and the only active part of my anatomy was my brain for it was trying desperately to muster a full-proof plan that will allow me to make that beeline for freedom. I was a prisoner in the harshest of surroundings and I soon found myself relating to those who suffered on The Rock, the impregnable fortress they call Alcatraz and like it’s inmates they endeavored a green mile of patience for nobody beats the system. I had to try! The singing subsided and as I lay waiting for my queue a pair of pink panties fluttered to the ground almost enticing me to make my presence known but the word freedom looped within my mind and no amount of cheap seduction would derail me from my goal. The feet began to lap the room and I could even hear the wardrobes wooden doors striking against one another as they closed and then my prayers were answered for silence swamped the room once more. I scurried from beneath the bed like a rodent and strife towards the door before making that executive decision of making my escape, the corridor was clear, the stairs appeared unoccupied and everything had ‘go’ written all over it. Something is wrong? I had those niggles of doubt poking me like tribesmen but I was desperate and we all know the cliché that surrounds that word, so I ventured into the unknown and with swift like feet I raced along the second floor corridor and down the winding staircase with the exit beckoning. The adrenaline rush was amazing for the years just seeped from me and I coolly waltzed over to the front door, my hand touching the cold brass of the lock and I began to turn the latch with the door slowly winding ajar and the daylight oozed into the premises. Where you going lad? A rugged voice bellowed from behind me and I had no alternative but to quench my curiosity for standing with his arms out to embrace was a middle-aged fellow in a cricket jumper, a neat looking chap with a trimmed beard and an apparent lack of head hair. I froze on the spot and as though I were being pushed by unforeseen entity I ventured towards him, not knowing who he was or whether he had any clue of my being there? Gareth I presume. I do apologise that we couldn’t make your acquaintance last night but the wife was adamant that you would be half-mast from your journey. Before I introduce you to the family I would like to say that your marriage to my daughter was hazy at best and around these parts we like things to be traditional but all that aside my Tessa Bear is the happiest I have seen her in awhile and that’s all thanks to you! I needed to gulp hard for the gentlemen was none other than Tessa’s father, the head of the Windsor household and I knew from that moment on I was either in the Twilight Zone or my worst nightmare had materialised. With his hand rooted to my back he led me into an exquisite room, which had been furnished by an artisan for there was pricey furnishings that would’ve rivalled my own taste. Sight seeing was over mind you for sitting sipping sherry in small goblet like glasses was the rest of the family, a smug arrogant looking young man I deduced was Tessa’s brother and the rest aged accordingly. HELP~! Help looped again and again, for I became the source of the murmurs. Let me introduce you to the family… Let’s not I imagined saying as her father swept his hand around anti-clockwise, introducing the congregation of Windsor’s who were made up by Doctors and persons of a higher stature. A real collection of snobs and I to them was the oddity, the social outcast but I continued to adorn my brave smile and remain pleasant and tight-lipped. I sat down on the sofa clutching my own glass tightly and narcolepsy was soon setting in as my host’s converse about medical and political topics, which were never my strong points. I could hear them taking cheap swiped towards my profession of Professional Wrestling but I remained a pillar of strength and soon envisioned them not being there until a brain wave struck me once more. I am the Jester! I’m happy when all others are miserable and like the parable ‘If you can’t beat them join them!’ or in this case, the role will be vice versa for they will join me. I have never been a sheep but more of a Shepard and this would surely inject that statement. Would you all like topping up? I asked. Soon I was weighed down by the overwhelming amount of discarded glasses and like a circus act I stumbled over to the drinks cabinet and poured my newfound associates another dose of sherry, only this time with a twist. Somehow I knew there was a drug within my jacket pocket, nothing lethal and certainly nothing I would participate in but its use was something I wanted to experiment with and the timing was perfect. I emptied the white powered content equally into each of the glasses and etched a smile of sarcasm across my face as I cockily strutted over. Cheers! Why, thank you! Ta… The gestures meant nothing to me for I sat and waited for the drug to engulf their chemical free bodies and as I waited I was once again overwhelmed with conversations of boredom and so I concentrated on the grandfather clock that ticked for a whole hour before this party began to evolve. - One hour later – The party made a dramatic u-turn and for the better one may add. Like a savage Tessa’s father was soon intoxicated enough to seduce his own wife playfully. Burying his head in her cleavage she merely giggled whilst the others spurned them on, the brother had reduced himself to chain smoking and profanity as he adapted every swear word into his private school education. The others rolled out the ‘Twister’ mat and the whole scene looked like a fraternity party of students as the drinks cabinet was emptied and the levels of IQ were quenched. Clothes were soon discarded and chaos seeped in, which truly put a smile back on my face, well until the calling of my name was heard over the decibels of the party. GAZ!!!! GAZ!!!! GAZ!!!! GAZ!!!! The sound belonged to my beloved or so I thought when suddenly I was struck across the face harshly. What? I rose only to find that the whole charade was nothing more than a dream, a nightmare that had suddenly u-turned and my surroundings were that of my very own bedroom. I sat up squinting my eyes as they adjusted to the dim lighting and standing above me in an intimidating manner was the behemoth Nascosto Ireul Nerezza who wore a tight fitting leather trench coat. What brings you here at this hour was the question I was bout to ask when suddenly the beast spoke, We have work to do! I peeled back the bed sheets and my feet touched down on the cold laminate flooring. My dream plagued my mind and I must admit I would’ve liked to have seen the outcome but as my partner stated, we had work to do and my life needed prioritising. I was having an awesome dream squire…
