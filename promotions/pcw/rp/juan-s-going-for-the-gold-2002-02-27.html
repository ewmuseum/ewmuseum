Juan's Going for the GOLD

27 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2/27/02 Time: 9: 46 P.MLocation: Lisbon, PortugalScene: Apartment Room “I’m gonna rock the party all night long.” The scene opens with Juan Borro in his apartment room along with Jose, Paolo, Raul Cesar, and PCW wrestlers Kevin Klaymore, Flaymin Aymin, and Chef. All of the men are together on the couches, joking around, drinking, and watching some television. The guys are all talking about the upcoming PCW pay-per view, which they will all be attending, either as wrestler or spectator. The guys are first shown with Juan going up and getting a pack of beers for the guys. Jose, Kevin, and Paolo are lying down in one couch as they are watching a football game which their betting over. Raul, Cesar, Flaymin Aymin, and Chef are playing some poker, but it looks as if Chef is cleaning house. Raul- How are you always winning, are you cheating or something, because this doesn’t seem on the level? Chef- Ha! I am no cheater. Yes, I may have relations to Bin Laden, but in no ways does that mean I am cheating. Three cards then come falling out of Chef’s pocket as everyone just stares at him, and he gives one of those looks like “How did that get there?” Chef- Oh, so I guess you all think I’m cheating now. Geesh, just because a man is seen with some evidence against him does not mean he’s guilty. Flaymin Aymin- Yea, but in your case, your never innocent. I mean who is actually innocent. Half of the people convicted for crimes are guilty anyway, so they should just throw them all in their. Raul- Ha! Now you sound like the Chinese government. I rotted in a cell for a whole year, because a bunch of idiots made a decision based on absolutely no facts. If I had my way I would kill that guys. Chef- I can have it done you know. Chef responds completely serious, as Raul begins laughing, but it fades away as he sees the way Chef is reacting. Raul- You know what, forget I even said anything. Juan then comes walking into the room and handing out beers, as everyone accept Juan and Kevin drink the beer. Chef- Eww!!!! This tastes like piss, Juan. Are you trying to kill something, or us because that’s what’s going to happen? Juan- Hey don’t blame me, the beer is courtesy of Aymin on there. He’s the one who got the beer supply to me. Flaymin Aymin nods, as Chef slaps him in the back of the head and causes the beer to spill on him. Meanwhile, the other guys take tastes of the beer, and their eyes begin to water over it. Flaymin Aymin- What’s wrong with you guys, this is some of the finest stuff in the world? I made sure I even made these specific drinks myself. And thanks for the credit Juan; if it wasn’t for me we’d have no drink. Chef- Heh…maybe that would have been better, you think? And no wonder their so bad, you said it yourself, that you specifically made the drinks yourself. Everyone shares a little laugh, as Juan goes over to see what Kevin, Jose, and Paolo are up to. The guys are watching a tape of a college football game, and making some bets over it. Juan notices it’s a tape, and comments. Juan- You do know you guys are betting on Kevin’s ta….. Kevin throws a pillow at Juan, with such impact that he falls down. He then winks over to Juan, who laughs in return. Kevin- So like I was saying. The real big money betters put one thousand dollars on each bet. By the way, I think I’ll put five thousand out on this one. Jose- All right I’ll match that bet. Paolo- so will I. Kevin- Yes! I sure do love the country of Portugal. This place has the best people in the whole world. Paolo and Jose then begin cheering excitedly, as the team they picked, just made the winning field goal in the game. Paolo- Woohoo, we win the money. I love these Spanish, they’re so easy to beat in bets, aren’t they. Jose- they sure are. Kevin looks on in awe, as he can’t realize how he lost. He had the game on tape and everything. After analyzing it, Kevin notices it was a different tape. Kevin- Why do all of the bad things happen to me? Juan- Ah…. Don’t worry man, not all bad things happen to you. We do have a Tag Team number one contenders match on next Turmoil. Kevin- I guess you’re right. Knowing my luck though, I’ll end up jobbing the HANK~! HANK~! I SAY. Juan- Did I mention how important it is for us to win that match. It’s a matter of just saving face if you know what I mean. Kevin- Yea, well you don’t have to worry. I mean after all, you are Mr. Bigtime PCW Main Eventer now? Juan- I can’t argue with that one. The road there was pretty easy. I only had to face one guy and everything. It was the easiest tournament I was in, in my entire life, if you’re really asking me about it. Kevin- Yea, it seems that way. Juan- If you want a tough tournament then the Global Title Tournament is the thing. I’m in the second round and got it pretty easy, but down the road it looks really difficult, with some big names. Chef then interferes in the conversation. Chef- I see you two are talking about my Main Event at the Pay-Per View. I hope you don’t car, that I’ll be taking home the gold for International Incident once all of you have viciously fallen. Juan-Yea sure. Flaymin Aymin- Hey, I think everyone could make that tournament. Even that dead beat El Diablo made it. Juan- Yea, that traitor better watch his back. I mean if he made it we all know this is no big deal, but buddies I plan to take this one home. Flaymin Aymin- Ha! You and your little self are going to take home the PCW World Championship. I strongly doubt that. Juan- Oh yea, I bet I could get a lot of people who would believe to the contrary of that belief. Flaymin Aymin- I bet you wouldn’t get anyone. Juan- Want to put your money where your mouth is. I bet there are a lot of guys who will seriously support Juan as World Champ. I’d put good cash on that. Flaymin Aymin- Yea, but you would have to get ready to lose that cash, because that’s what’s going to happen. Juan- No way, this pay-per view, I’m going to do the things, I wasn’t able to do last time. I’m first going to go after Kannon and Chase. I don’t care what I have to do, but I am going to eliminate the two of those men if I have to give it all. Kevin- Yea, what’s up with you fading out of the Cruiserweight scene? Juan- Don’t worry, I’m still their, I’ve just had a few other things on my mind lately. Any day I would take a rematch with either of those men. You don’t know how badly I just want to beat the hell out of those two. Kevin- I think you’re by far the top cruiserweight, and we are probably the best big guys and small guy combination in PCW history. Juan- We sure are. Chef- Ha! You two can keep dreaming, but I’m up for a game of pool, anybody want to take that challenge. Juan- Sure, as long as you don’t hide the balls in your sleeve. Chef- Funny. The guys go off to play some pool, and discuss the Pay-Per View some more. Juan is confident that this pay-per view will treat him better than the ones in his past. March Madness will be the event where Juan proves himself. Scene Fades to Black.
