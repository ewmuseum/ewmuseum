White Men Can't Jump

3 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 03 - 11 - 02 From the lighting spectacle that is Las Vegas Nevada to a simple suburban home arrest in the outskirts of the dank City of London. Las Vegas is renowned for a number of things; it's casinos and high profile life-styles, sex and prostitution, Gaz 'The Jester' Green and his cunning grasp of the PCW Tag Team Championships. In saying that we ask ourselves what has London ever been renowned for-.... Err-.... Arr-.... Well-.... Nothing really yet CEO Xavier Kannon wishes to showcase the greatest successful tag title defence in a City that couldn't tell the difference between a wristlock and wristwatch. Opening on a pleasant cul de sac that homed a number of residential properties, we bask from an exterior view allowing the camera to pin point the next escapade in the Pranks With Attitude saga. An open garage door draws the camera nearer. With a musty smell filtering over the fresh air and lighting creeping along the dirt ridden walls like swaying branches of a tree in a thunder storm we hear the roar of a television set. Sitting vegetated to his chair, eyes fixated on the televised action, notably a past ladder match from the archives of Primetime Championship Wrestling is Gaz Green. Glued and incoherent, the Jester sits in his velvet business attire watching closely as the footage continues to unfold when suddenly a ray of light shone across his angelic face. Capturing the comics attention we hear the sweet and innocent, yet distinctive voice of Natalie Miyagawa. 'You've been here all night-....' 'Twelve hours and thirty-six minutes, but who's counting?' retorted the Emerald Menace rubbing his flickering eyes as they fight to stay open, his head tilting enough to look upon Natalie Miyagawa. 'Your going to do yourself an injury!' informed P.W.A's recent acquisition, rolling her eyes in a motherly fashion. 'Injury? I'm the Instigator Of Injury mind you, but that nickname would much rather suit Nerezza after ending your husbands PCW tenure and almost his insignificant life-...' 'Hold on - His life only became insignificant when he decided on his adulterous asshole gimmick, we were happy together once, happy enough to have children!' Standing in a fit of rage, grinding her teeth, eyes glazed over, Natalie Miyagawa personified the parable, 'Hell knows no fury like a woman scorned'. As though this moment were foreseen Gaz Green tampered with the remote control and played the fateful footage of Alexander Miyagawa's demise at the hands of Noscosta Ireul Nerezza. Natalie peering over watched intriguingly as her husbands head crashed not once but seven times against a concrete wall, eventually falling to a heap in a crimson red mask. 'Again-... Again-... Again-... Again-... Again-...' roared the petite Russian. The Jester displaying a sinister grin rose from his seated position and walked out into the driveway; a gentle breeze toying with his spiked green hair seemed his only concern. Natalie however slouched into the unoccupied seat, grasping the remote in the process and playing the heinous attack over repeatedly. If Alexander’s head had hit once, it must've made contact a hundred times as the former PCW superstar's wife and valet gloated in her husbands demise. 'So you've found the rewind button then?!?' sarcastically said the cocky comedian, racing about with a basketball, trying desperately to dunk the ball in a basket suspended fifteen feet from the ground. 'Is this basket regulation size?' groaned the Jester as every attempted dunk falls short. His 5' 11' frame unable to reach the unobtainable basket, not enough spring in his jump nor height as he continues to fight the odds that have been stacked against him. Meanwhile Natalie appears quite contempt to watch the night her husbands career finished. The film couldn't draw a smile or disgust as her face remained expressionless, her only motion, the tapping of the remote to simply rewind and play. 'I'm sure this sport is prejudice, kinder like our match scheduled for Friday Night Fusion-... All the competitors involved have a height advantage, well, except Syko The Hobbit who isn't going to be a factor!' murmured the Lord Of Laughter, again falling short of the basket. Grasping his hair tightly in a psycho pose, face angered by his futile attempts to dunk the basketball, Gaz walked out from the confines of the driveway and into the street. Feet away stood a huge metallic trashcan on castor, which the Jester rolled up and under the basketball hoop allowing him enough leverage to dunk the ball. 'I AM THE SMARTEST MAN ALIVE~!' cried the Velvet Prankster in an accomplished tone, only to be interrupted upon by the zombie-esque Natalie Miyagawa. 'Not quite-... You have forgotten that Steven Hawking who is much smarter than you!' Cringing at the very mention of Steven Hawking, Gaz Green climbed the box shaped trash holder with a struggle, ball tucked under his arm he eventually reaches the summit. 'YYYYYYEEEEEESSSSSSSSSSSS!!!' Bouncing atop of the trashcan Gaz adjusts for that all-important dunk that has now eluded him for a good hour. With a pivot of the foot Gaz leapt into the air and smashed the ball through the basket, envisioning some of basketballs greats like Magic Johnson, Michael Jordan and Shaquille O'Neal. As the ball falls to the ground via gravity, Gaz 'The Jester' Green remains left hanging as his hands remain clenched to the rim of the basket, his body freezing in an erect position. 'YEAH!!! Watch-out Fusion because the Jester has a few tricks still to play!' said the former NTICW employee enthusiastically. As his energetic voice bellowed over the television set that still has the fateful Turmoil still playing, Natalie retorted with a few encouraging words of her own, a little something to spurn on the Prince Of Pranks. 'Oh! That's nice....' With a subtle smile Gaz looked down from his fifteen-foot pedestal, his cell if you would. Long gone has the accomplished showboating and cocky grin as a look of fear surfaces over in their place, realising a little late that Gareth Green suffers vertigo, a thought locked away in the far reaches of his warped mind. 'Err-... Arr-... Hmm-... Natalie-... Oh, Natalie could you offer a little assistance!' winced the current PCW Tag Champion. The small Russian Natalie Miyagawa emerged with a giggle as Gaz Green locked down with puppy dog eyes. Putting in a halfhearted attempt at rolling the trashcan under the basket, Natalie shrugged her shoulders, looking back with a classic Eleanor Kannon-Hall glare. 'So I can't move this thing, how about I catch you?' 'You can't move a 150 lbs garbage can, so how do you suppose your going to catch a falling 226 lbs person, do the maths?' yelped Gaz as his grip slowly loosened. 'How do you suppose I'm going to catch you? With my hands silly-....' informed the new P.W.A valet, her out-stretched arms waiting in preparation. With a huge gulp Gaz closed his eyes and dropped towards the earth and hopefully into the stable arms of Natalie Miyagawa. Drawing closer and obviously picking up pace Natalie appeared to have the catch sealed when suddenly her composure was disturbed upon by the tinkling sound of an approaching ice-cream van who's melody filled the air. THUD!!!! As Natalie peered back Gaz Green was already lifting himself from his heap. Rubbing down his velvet business jacket, creaking his neck and offering nothing but a harsh stare Gaz waltzed into the garage soaking himself in the pile of Rezz-Bull left by his partner days earlier. 'I'm soooooooooo sorry!' murmured the PCW diva, trying to contain her giggles. The event however still fresh in her mind, she locked her jaw shut before breaking into a mass of hysterics, which wasn't sitting well with the falls recipient. 'Not now-....' hissed the once jovial Jester nursing his back. Returning to his seated position of earlier he begins to drown his sorrows in the energy drink of Rezz-Bull, one by one the cans become metallic shells as their content is consumed. 'Excuse me - What do you think your doing?' questioned the inquisitive valet, her face centre of attention as the tip of her nose touches that of Gaz Green's in an up close and personal moment. 'I call this 'Plan B' drink loads of this Rezz-Bull and according to the advert it gives you wings, which aren't top of my wanted list but I'm willing to do anything to retain my tag team championship!!!' Shaking her head Natalie raced off into the temporary home of Pranks With Attitude, allowing the scene to close on that of the thirst driven Gaz 'The Jester' Green, emptying the content of yet another can down his narrow throat.
