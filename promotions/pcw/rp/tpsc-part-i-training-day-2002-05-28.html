TPSC - Part I - Training Day

28 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5-28-02 OOC Note: This one is dedicated to all my critics. I love them. They sometimes make me angry... When I get angry? I get motivated. .Song Listening Guide: 'All You Wanted' by Michelle Branch. (From Kira's perspective) BOBBY S. CAMPBELL The Perfect Storm Chronicles - Part I - Training Day Then.... Now legally Bobby Campbell, to share his real father's last name, there was still much to do, even in quiet and peaceful times. Bobby, by the way, kept his last name as his middle name. Stormbringer. After all, if it was anything different than that then how would Bobby sell all those 'B.S.' t-shirts!? Exactly. So for the past three weeks the final student of Triple H has, in a sense, graduated himself to a new level. Now Bobby Campbell is the mentor. And Kira York is the student. And now.... * * * --5-21-02, 1:07 pm, last Tuesday afternoon-- Our scene slowly fades up on the face of Bobby Stormbringer Campbell's personal assistant Carl Everstrong, slightly obscured by three ring ropes. One blue, one red, and one white. The balding man wears a pair of gold rimmed spectacles and a white t-shirt. A gray towel hangs from his neck and shoulders. He apparently sits on a bench of some sort behind the ring. We quickly zoom out and away from him as his stoic eyes dart back and forth to something in the ring. Eventually we come to a wide angle of the squared circle itself, nothing but concrete, stone walls, and paned windows around it. Recessed lighting overhead allows everything to be visible. The apron and the sides of the ring are black. The rest of the room stretches off to the left. The turnbuckles situated on every rope in every corner show that this is a wrestling ring and not a boxing ring. Inside the ring itself simply stands two people. A young brunette woman named Kira York in one, and a man known as 'Bobby Stormbringer' in public, 'Bobby Campbell' in private in the other. B.S. wears his usual ring attire of black boots and dark silver tights with royal blue lightning streaks down the thighs. His dark red hair is tucked back in a pony tail and he wears nothing over his defined chest. The only other things are his blue fingerless gloves. Kira wears black boots small enough to fit her and a white one piece bodysuit. Her wrists are taped as well. Bobby outweighs Kira by over a hundred pounds, most of it muscle. B.S.'s face shows an odd mixture of determination and concern. Kira's? Just determination. Abruptly, Carl's voice echoes throughout the basement of the couple's family mansion. 'Begin.' B.S. takes a couple of uneasy steps forward while Kira rushes towards him! B.S. can almost be heard sighing while he simply drop toe hold's her right into the mat. Kira lands on both arms to protect her face. B.S. quickly rises up and shakes his head slightly. Kira gets a little red in the face and grits her teeth. She bounces back up quickly and glares at B.S. before running at him again. B.S. turns from Kira's right until he is on Kira's left and hip tosses her hard onto the mat. The Showstopper clicks his tongue audibly and leans down over his new student. 'C'mon, damnit! I taught you better than that! Fight smart, not hard.' Bobby yells down to Kira and backs away a few paces. Kira holds her lower back and leans her head down for a moment. Bobby's face softens as he wonders to himself if he was too rough on her just now. But as he walks over to help her up, Kira gets back to her knees and leg sweeps her fiansce' right off his feet! 'Oof!' B.S. lands with a thud of his own. Kira smirks for playing possum so effectively and gets back up, but not for long. She tries to drive a Bret Hart/Stone Cold elbow into the sternum of her boy toy, but B.S. simply rolls away. In one fluid motion, Mr. Prime Time gets up in mid roll and runs against the near ropes. Before Kira can do much more than sit up, B.S. springs back with a low dropkick right to the side of her head! Carl can be heard groaning at the impact, and B.S. notes through the disorientation of which way is up or down that Kira takes a standard back bump from it like she is supposed to. Kira rolls over onto her stomach and breathes heavily. By this time Bobby has taught his future wife everything that he learned back in Calgary all those years ago. Now is the time for the advanced lessons. 'Well,' Bobby peers over Kira, not even breaking a sweat yet. 'Ready to give up?' A back elbow to the forehead is a simple enough answer. Bobby's eyes comically bug out in shock as Kira rises with the speed of lightning and knees him right in the temple! 'Hurgh!' B.S. exclaims. B.S. rolls over himself in the opposite direction. Male or female, bone is bone... and a knee bone to your skull hurts. Before B.S. can react, Kira floats over his back and snatches him into a front facelock. Just like The Showstopper taught her, she comes up to her knees and leans down into the move, applying even more pressure. Bobby hoarsely laughs and squeezes out a word. 'Good!' But B.S. spins out and suddenly stands above her in a hammerlock and a sneer. 'Just not good enough.' Kira giggles herself and turns while standing up, forcing B.S. into a top wristlock. Just before B.S. easily overpowers her, Kira spins herself into an armbar. B.S. reverses into his own, but Kira expected it. A quick knee lift separates them, and Kira dropkicks B.S. down! Kira smiles wide at her fallen lover before responding to his taunt. 'I beg to differ.' B.S. tries to get up but Kira dropkicks him in the back of the head! Kira gets overzealous, however, and tries for a third. This time The Showstopper steps aside and catches one of the legs. Kira falls hard and squeezes her eyes shut from the pain of not quite being used enough to the unforgiving canvas. B.S. quickly turns Kira over into a single leg crab, but doesn't wrench it in as much as he is capable of. But still a little. Kira stuggles underneath and she isn't very close to the ropes at all. B.S. is in the way of the closest side. 'Let's go, baby, we went through this one just yesterday,' B.S. says without even looking back at her. 'Don't think! React.' 'Easy for you to say!' Kira nearly yells through gritted teeth. Regardless, Kira tucks herself closer to Bobby and kicks up with her free leg, right into Bobby's nose! Kira can hear the impact and the resulting deep growl. Kira gasps when she realizes she just may of broken Bobby's nose but she doesn't have the chance for much else. Her air leaves her when B.S. lets go of the move and stomps down hard on the small of her back, compressing her lungs against the mat. Bobby angrily drags Kira up by her long hair to a short squeal. Then he roughly knocks her back down again with a sharp forearm shiver to the back of the head. Kira groans while B.S. is obviously extremely pissed right the hell off. 'That's the third Gawddamn time this week, Kira,' B.S. snarls. 'Now you tell me what the first two freggin' rules are!' The Showstopper drops to a knee and slips Kira's left arm in between his legs. Then he locks her into a painful Kneeling Crippler Crossface than he used to call 'Edge Of The Hood.' B.S. nearly brings Kira up to his chest before Kira cries out in pain. Carl stands up at ringside to protest but a simple glare from his employer sits him right back down. Before any of the training started Bobby warned Carl that there would be tense, sometimes even harsh moments in the ring. When Kira and Bobby square off against eachother, even in sparring, it's nothing short of pure combat. But all of it is necessary to build a professional wrestler. In Mr. Prime Time's old school train of thought, all new trainees have to be thrown into the fire. Either they will be consumed by the flames... Or they will be tempered by them. This is how greatness can be polished. How frauds are uncovered. 'FIRST TWO RULES!' The Showstopper coarsely booms down to the pretty but tough-minded nineteen-year-old. 'Rule... number one...' Kira's voice somehow remains steady, betraying none of the anguish she must feel. 'Always... listen to your... trainers and manager!' 'The second!?' B.S. visably relaxes the submission hold into something less extreme, but even a so-called normal amount of pain in a move like this can be terribly dehabilitating. 'Rule number two... always protect... the other wrestler... in the ring..?' Kira squeezes an eye shut and looks over at Campbell, but can't quite see his face. B.S. slowly lets go and stands up. Kira rubs her cheek bone, the side of her face already sore from Bobby's wrist being buried into it. She weakly rises up on shaky knees and risks a peek to her angry teacher. By the time she looks though, Bobby is already quietly leaning against a corner and all she can see is his mane of red hair coming down just past his shoulders. Kira walks gingerly over to the massive man and gasps again when he finally turns his head to look at her. Bobby's nose is bleeding profusely from both nostrils. Despite this, it seems that all of his anger has disappeared and the rage has been replaced with a stoic and maybe even saddened attitude. 'Christ! Babe, I'm so sorry...' Kira trails off, embarrassed as Carl throws B.S. his gray towel. 'Forget it.' Bobby says sharply while vigorously not looking at Kira and wiping his face clean. The kind of talking that a disappointed parent might use with a child. Then his voice softens but the dissatisfaction in his voice and face remains. 'Just... forget it. No really. I mean it. Hit the showers, please. When you're finished, come outside because I need to speak with you about a few things, if you would...' B.S. checks his damaged but seemingly unbroken nose while he climbs through the ropes and walks back up the basement stairs to the ground floor. Carl steps through the ropes to get into the ring himself while Kira leans against the ropes, looking after her mentor and husband-to-be. Carl looks to Kira and sees the tears streaming down her cheeks. She stands silent, however. 'I think that he is still distressed about the news of his father. Of the kidney cancer. I implore you not to take him too seriously in these times, Miss Kira. He's never truly dealt with the death of his mother, nor his stepfather. Now this... I fear this all may be just too much to bear.' 'Don't say that!' Kira snaps her head around to meet him, her sudden tears forgotten. 'He's pulled through everything else before, all the heartaches, all the adversity. He'll get through this, too. Have faith in him. Besides... Amy said their dad'll be just fine in the long run.' 'I don't doubt that, but...' Carl sighs softly. 'I'm not worried about him in a professional wrestling sense. He takes the duty he has given himself in protecting his family very seriously. It's no doubt rooted in how he hasn't yet dealt with his mother being taken from him. To this day he-' 'It's because he loves them, damnit,' Kira cuts off Carl. 'Even that jerk Randy. Don't overrationalize it.' 'What we both say is true, Miss Kira.' Carl continues doggedly. 'You should also realize that he loves you too, you know. As family.' Kira looks down and nods a bit. 'Yeah... you're right. But it doesn't excuse the fact that I screwed up out here. I keep going like this I'll break some loser's neck with a suplex, or something. This was my idea, I don't blame him for being upset with me. I.. I dunno.' Kira lets out a deep breath while Carl speaks a little lower. 'Bobby didn't want me to tell you this and make you overconfident. But he told me just a few days ago how impressed he was with everything. He said that you are actually twice as far ahead as he was a month into his original wrestling training.' Kira looks up and her expression changes to an odd mixture of relief and surprise. 'He.. he really said that? You're not just saying that to make me feel better, are you?' 'No, no, certainly not.' Carl steps back and waves his hands in front of his face briefly. 'His only criticism would be that your timing is slightly off. But... in private... he has beamed about you. I think he's very proud of you, Miss Kira. I only think that he is nitpicking because he is concerned about your decision to enter the wrestling business someday. Neither of us want to see you get hurt. The more you practice, the more capable you will be on your own.' 'Carl? How do you know all this?' 'Oh, erm, besides what he has told me? He seems to be implementing many of the same techniques that he says 'Trip' used with him. So...' 'Why not the same mindset all the way around, then.' 'Exactly, Miss Kira. My point precisely. I think he was rough on you at the very end because that's what was done with him. It must tough, training your wife.' 'Soon to be, you mean.' 'Yes. Except in his heart, you already are. Surely you must know this.' 'Carl, Carl, Carl...' Kira shakes her head at the older man. 'What would we do without you?' 'Uhhh... have much more money, I would imagine?' Kira laughs at the confused expression on Carl Everstrong's face. 'I better get going. Don't worry about him though. If any of us has a master plan, it's definitely Mr. Anal. Don't tell 'im I said that, either.' Carl smiles and nods while Kira giggles and rolls out of the ring, avoiding the ropes all together. Carl begins to clean up Bobby Campbell's blood in the corner while Kira gently makes her way up the stairs. She finds herself craving the comforts of a nice warm bath. A shower would have to do... Carl was right, she thought. As bad as it may appear, she had all the confidence in the world that her Bobby only wanted the best for her. She knew that B.S. didn't approve of her wanting to wrestle. That he 'wanted better for her,' whatever that means. But he respected her enough to let her live her life... compromise is part of any good relationship. Yet... Kira is left wondering exactly what Bobby has to talk about that could be so important. It seemed to be a lot more serious than just a botched wrestling move. He also wasn't the type to lecture. Not for long periods of time, anyway. She was worried about him. He has grown increasingly distant over these past few weeks, even stretching back into the end of their ten day vacation together. She was determined to help him fix anything and everything that was bothering him. After this was all over, she just wanted them both to be at peace. A couple of kids, a good relationship with his new sister and father, not to mention her own parents. If it meant walking right through hell itself instead of tip-toeing around it? So be it.
