Tim Walden, you're not missing some of life's essentials

21 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3-21-02 Tonight, Tim Walden finds out just how badly he was wrong about Jeremy Howard. For, the happenings nine days before Decimation were a fluke. Had Ivan Stanislav and Frisco kept their noses out of the Krayzie One's business, Invincible Tim Walden would never have walked out the Television Champion that night. That night, against Jeremy Howard, he never was invincible. He was lucky. Now, he has his chance to prove the Krazed Lunatic otherwise. But, that is a task in itself. It was only a week ago that the Krayzie One made the challenge to Tim Walden. It wasn't the normal challenger making the challenge. It was the champion making the challenge. And, while it might seem strange to some, it just goes to show what kind of fighting champion Jeremy Howard really is. But, if Fusion is scheduled in Las Vegas, Nevada, then why have cameras spotting the Krayzie One in Saskatoon, Saskatchewan? To find out, the scene fades into a the tourist bureau of Saskatoon. There, a camera picks up PCW Continental Champion, Jeremy Howard, holding his title belt over his shoulder, while holding up a telephone book with his one free hand. Standing next to him, wearing a knee length white dress with no sleeves, is the lovely and beautiful Jessica. Jessica: There it is. Jessica extends her hand, pointing to a telephone number in the book. Jeremy's eyes follow her finger, spotting the telephone number of the Saskatoon electrical company, SaskPower. Jeremy: Read that number off to me. Jessica: Okay. Jeremy lifts the telephone to his ear, awaiting Jessica to read the numbers off. Jessica: Nine... Seven... Five..... Two... Four... Zero... Zero. Jeremy: Thanks. Jeremy waits a few seconds, until he gets an answer from the other end of the phone. Its takes a few moments, but he finally gets an answer. A Female's Voice: Hello, SaskPower. Jeremy: Yes, this is Tim Walden. I'm calling to have my electricity disconnected. Female's Voice: Alright. Let me take down your address and phone number. Jessica pulls out a piece of paper from her purse, showing Jeremy the address and phone number they've obviously taken from information gathered on Tim Walden. The Krayzie One has to answer a few questions more to finish the process, including this last one.... Female's Voice: One last question sir. I need your reason for shutting your electricity off? Jeremy: I disgraced myself, my family and my country when I jobbed to Jeremy Howard in two seconds. I should have never talked shit about him behind his back, so I'm running away like a scared bitch before I meet a psychotic ending. Female's Voice: Okay..... I think. Jeremy: My name is Tim Walden and I'm a first class loser. The lady on the other line gives 'Tim' his cancellation number, before Jeremy hangs up the phone. A sly grin crosses Jeremy's face. If Tim Walden thinks about going home tonight to his warm house after he recieves the ass kicking he asked for when he ran his mouth, he'll have another thing coming. Tim Walden now doesn't have any electricity on his home. Jessica: Um, do you know if he has any kids? Jeremy looks toward Jessica with the 'are you f^cking nuts?' look. Jeremy: Think of it this way, Rent A Hero and Aimz with no Aimz in sight. Jessica: He's scared of woman or doesn't have one? Jeremy: I don't even think a female dog would give him the time of day. Jessica's face turns white, kind of like she's about to throw up at the thought. Jeremy smiles. Jeremy: I'm only kidding.... I hope. Read off the number to the gas company. Jessica flips a few pages, searching out the number for, SaskEnergy, the gas company in Saskatoon, Saskatchewan. Which, is the hometown of the Krazed Lunatic's opponent for Fusion, Tim Walden. She finds it, reading off the number one by one just like she did the last time. Seconds after Jeremy types on the last number, one of those annoying custom service representatives picks up. A Male CSR: Hi, you've reached the SaskEnergy. How may I help you today? That maniacal smile overtakes Jeremy's face once he's got his connection to the gas company. Jeremy: Hi, my name is Tim Walden and I'd like to disconnect my service. Male CSR: I'm sorry to hear that, may I ask why sir? Jeremy: Because I ran my mouth too much like a bitch would. So I have to run away in fear that I may never get to have sex with my hand before I die. Jessica nearly loses herself, laughing so hard. Male CSR: Is this a prank call? Jeremy: No sir. I'm really scared. Can we speed this up. I have to leave. I just want that one chance at feeling like a man before I meet my imminent ending. Male CSR: Sounds like did something really bad. Jeremy: I did. I'm a Canadien jobber who thought he was more than he really is. Over the phone, Jeremy can hear the customer service representative typing away on his computer. And, as always with these type of people, it takes them forever to do one simple task. Male CSR: Oh, I'm sorry to hear that. Jeremy: It's alright. Had I just kept my mouth shut, I would've been alright. Now, I may never know what its like to experience what orgasms are like with female horses. Male CSR: Uh, yeah. Okay. Well, here is your cancellation number sir. Have a nice day. Click. It might not have been one of the more ordinary calls Jeremy's ever had, but it got the job done. Tim Walden can now add gas to the list of utilities he no longer has running within his home. Thing is, the man that calls himself 'Invincible' doesn't even know it. While Tim Walden is getting his ass handed to him, the cold Canadien winds are freezing his home over. Jeremy: Damn, Canadiens sure are sorry for alot of things. Jessica: You're going to cause an international nightmare. Jeremy: What? I'm just having some Krayzie fun. Again, Jeremy smiles. Had Tim Walden not opening his mouth and bragged to everyone who'd listen, about beating Jeremy Howard for the Television title because of Frisco and Ivan's interference, he would have never had to deal with this. But now, he's going against the man that can raise more hell than the devil himself. Of course, the devil doesn't have anything on the Krazed Lunatic. The scene fades out, as Jeremy and Jessica walk out of the tourist bureau, laughing at there accomplishment..... to black!
