The tape

29 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 29/04/03 In practically every town, every city, there’ll be an abandoned factory or warehouse. They may as well open up a chain of Abandoned Warehouse INC or Unused Factory LTD places. But one of these factories is very much abandoned, but not uninhabited… the gratings of gears and whirring of cogs fills the air inside this noisy environment, accompanied by two insane men. One was driven insane through a method of torture that would have killed a normal man. The other is simply up at 4 in the morning, assisting his friend. One stands tall, but hunched over. The other stands next to him, hands in his pockets, with a worried look on his shadowed face. “Squire, I know you have a slight dislike for the human race, but is this really the solution?” the smaller one chirped with concern. Gazing through some wandering strands of hair, the other turned around slowly and menacingly. “Media is the thing which has damned this planet. It controls the thoughts and alliances of the world, thrusting its biased lies onto everyone within reach of a screen. It inserts ideas into the heads of the weak and gullible. It’s almost a form of forceful telepathy. But it is true of all things in existence that once you understand the nature of something, you may use it to your own advantage. And the media shall be my new weapon against the masses.” he wheezed with a vengeful tone. A conveyor belt in front of him, set low to the ground, carried a series of unidentified items along, each whizzing past the observer’s face. Kneeling down and then getting onto all fours, the man’s eye followed each one as it went past. “But this is just a little too much, Squire. Don’t you think this is going a bit too far?” the smaller one protested. The large one kept his eye on the conveyor belt, not even turning his head. “The guilty shall be judged. There is a crime that people are committing and are completely unaware of it. But a crime is a crime, nonetheless. I’m simply delivering the justice that will punish these offensive actions. And unless someone has a good reason to claim innocence…” A cruel smile peeked out from behind the mask of his hair, gleaming in the darkness. “Everyone will suffer.” ~ “Ship to…” taptaptap… “Pay by…” taptaptap…. “Send. Haha! Done!” A girl sat in front of a computer, wearing a black PCW T-Shirt and jeans. She was small in build, wore glasses and had dark brown hair tied into a high ponytail. On her wrists were several cheap plastic bracelets. The screen displayed PCW’s shopzone, in particular, a VHS of the new Pranks With Attitude video, entitled “Die Laughing”. A new page loaded in, with the message “Congratulations! Your order has been received and will be shipped within 14 working days. Thankyou for shopping with us.” “Yesss!” the girl squealed in delight and clapped once. “You’re such a nerd, you know that, Donna?” a heavy, deep male voice from behind called her. “Get some sunlight or something, I swear you’re as pale as that vampire guy.” “He’s called Nerezza, Darren. And I get plenty of sunlight. It all comes in through the window, see?” the girl replied, poking her tongue out. She indicated to the window, which indeed was allowing a stream of sunshine in. The rest of the room was a shrine to PCW, comprising of PCW action figures, posters, a huge, untidy stack of magazines and a video case with nothing but PCW Pay-per-view events and “Best of” compilations. Even her bed had a PCW cover on it and a pillowcase with Jason Snow’s image on it. “Whatever…” Darren unenthusiastically groaned and pulled on the neck of his jersey. His brown hair was wet with perspiration and he clutched a basketball under one arm. “I’m out to shoot a few. Smell ya later.” The door behind him closed with a little slam, shaking down a figure from one of Donna’s shelves. It crashed to the floor with a loud thump, causing Donna to turn around with concern. As she looked towards her carpet, she saw her favourite, a Clyde figure laying down. “Oh, Clyde…” she said as though he were real, placing him back on the shelf and re-posing him next to a miniature Bethany. “Silly demon.” smiled Donna. ~Two days later…~ “I’m coming, hold on!” Donna yelled as she almost fell down the stairs to answer the front door. Making a quick check in the mirror and pushing a few waves of hair behind her ears, Donna opened the door to meet a delivery boy. “Donna Ray?” he said, scratching the top of his head with a pen. “Yeah, that’s me…” she replied, curiously and with a little grin. “Package for you. If you’d just like to sign here and print your name here…” he indicated with the pen. Donna’s face lit up as she wrote down a squiggle on the paper and almost printed her name just as badly. Handing the clipboard back to the delivery boy, she bounced up and down on the spot as the boy handed her a brown, rectangular package. She snatched it out of his hands with glee, beaming all over her face. The delivery boy shrugged and turned to walk away. Before he could even manage to turn a quarter of the way around, the door had already shut and the sound of feet pounding the staircase could be heard outside. “EEEEEEEEEEH!” she squeaked as she ran into her bedroom, ripping the package open. Greeting her was a photograph of Gaz Green and Nascosto Nerezza on the cover with a shot of a wrestling ring behind them and an embossed “Die Laughing” graphic at the top. “Whoohoo! Special embossed cover edition!” Stuffing the video in, she scrambled for her remote control and tapped play repeatedly. Finally, the picture came up with the usual copyright warnings. Donna tapped the fast forward button until the PCW logo showed up on the screen. Quickly hitting the play button, she watched as the animated logo shone brightly, then faded to black in preparation for the video. “Huh…?” she peeped. The screen was snowy and blurred. Her tracking buttons failed to correct the picture too, leaving her disappointed with her purchase. As she reached over to press the eject button, the picture suddenly snapped into crystal clear vision, except the images were not as expected. First, a shot of a school of fish swimming as though the camera were held below their bellies. Then an image of a jagged, broken seashell or something that looked like it. Next, a shot of one of the PCW Tag Team titles inside some moist, muscular structure, like those educational videos that show you the inside of the body. The counter on the video begins to flash for no reason and remains at 00:00 despite the tape obviously playing. Next in the sequence is a rocky riverbank with a coffin half-submerged in the water. Finally, two circles, a red one inside a white one surrounded by all black shows. The video then automatically ejected itself, almost spitting it out in disgust. “What the….” Donna, shaken up, crawled backwards slightly. A hand came down onto her shoulder, causing her to scream out loud… “Whoawhoawhoawhoa, cool down sis, it’s me!” Darren pleaded, raising his hands into the air. Breathing rapidly, Donna clutched her hand to her chest, feeling her heart pounding. “God Darren, don’t you EVER knock?” She gasped with a wide-open mouth. “I’m a little brother, it’s my job to be nosy and snoop around. Besides, I saw the delivery guy leaving. I knew exactly where to find you.” he sneered. “So what’s the tape like?” “It won’t play in my video… can I try it out in yours?” she begged. Her shocked expression and breathing made him think twice before replying, but out of that little teenager’s curiosity… “…Sure.” “I’ll rewind it and bring it in as soon as it’s done.” Donna smiled, weakly. ~ “Okay, don’t fast-forward or anything, just play it.” Donna instructed. “Maybe it screwed up because of that?” Darren guessed out loud, placing the remote control down. He sat directly in front of his TV while Donna pulled her knees up to her chest, sitting on his bed with the packaging in her left hand.. The room belonged to the usual stereotyped Kid Brother’s bedroom. Magazine pull-outs of sports stars and female musical celebrities lined the walls, with an air gun propped up in one corner. A few band T-shirts slung messily over the floor gave the finishing touch. The video began to play as normal with the trailers and logos of any PCW video. “So what happened?” Darren inquisitively asked. “Well…” Donna began. “First of all, the picture went all weird and the tracking controls didn’t work, and then…” “Mine’s a newer model, it’ll probably work better.” Darren interrupted as the animated PCW logo faded. The same white noise effect came up on the screen, followed by the same abstract sequence of images. Again, the video cassette was spat out of the player and onto the floor, leaving the siblings confused and looking at it. “I think we’d better return it…” Darren nodded to himself. Donna simply stared at it, bewildered and spooked by the video. Tightening her hands around the packing, she noticed a small piece of paper sitting inside. Pushing her glasses further up the bridge of her nose and unfolding the slip of paper, she began to read the information. The order number, address and credit information were all correct. Turning it over, the return information was faded and in feint ink. “If unsatisfied, return in original condition within seven days of date of purchase. Or else.” “Or… else?” Donna gasped. Five days left.
