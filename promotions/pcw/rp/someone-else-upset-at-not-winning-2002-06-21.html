Someone Else Upset At Not Winning.

21 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 21/06/02 Xavier Kannon doesn’t take losing very well.... Most PCW fans know that from what happened after March Madness. However, when he loses something without actually LOSING it, then he takes it even worse... Example of case, the Continental Title. The belt he has made it his goal to win back at Gold and Glory when he is shut in a cage with Miyagawa, who hours before will be the Best Man at his wedding... Legend Inc. and Duke Williams, the man he beat to retain the Title before being stripped of it. But some people seem to take loss even worse than Xavier.... While most of the losses mourned in the World are trivial or material possessions, they still mean a lot to those who have the feeling off loss.... We are about to meet such a man... “COME ON THEN!!! COME ON!!! ENGURLUND! ENGURLUND! ENGURLUUUUUND!!!” Our shot opens up on the teary, sad, rather pathetic face of a rampaging England soccer fan, the loss against Brazil just a few hours ago being too much for his Lager based existence to take... So there is only one thing he can do.... Break stuff. *SMASH* In rather Molsen match fashion he lobs an empty bottle into the wall behind him. the momentum of his follow through spinning his drunken well-build body around so he has to stagger to stay on his feet. English Soccer Fan : “ENGERLUND! ENGURLUND! ENGURLUND!” He grabs the 3 Lions badge on his White England football shirt and kisses it before wiping the tears from his eyes with the front of the shirt. As his face is finally revealed again the anger still remains, only exclaimed by his shaven head and ear piercing. English Soccer Fan : “The.... Three...” He again wipes a tear from his eye. English Soccer Fan : “....Lions!” His British Bulldog-esque podgy face looks down at his pint of lager, a tear dripping into the glass in almost poetic fashion.... A moment kind of ruined as he kicks out and sends a Plastic chair flying. English Soccer Fan : “COS I RE-MEM-BER 3 LIONS ON A SHIRT.... JULES RIMET STILL GLEAMING... NO MORE YEARS OF HURT.... NEVER STOP ME DREAMING.... 3 LIONS ON A SHIRT!!!” He breaks out of song and with a grunt turns over one of the outside tables then throws the parasol at the wall, nearly putting the window through. English Soccer Fan : “COME ON YOU BRAZILIANS!!! COME ON... YOU CAN BEAT US IN FOOTBALL BUT I’LL GIYA A SCHLAP!!!” He clenches his fists and shouts at the passers by under the delusion that they either care or are Brazilian..... He pushes another table over, this one still with empty glasses and bottles on top that smash on the concrete upon impact. English Soccer Fan : “WHERE ARE YA NOW?!?!?!?! YOU MUPPETS! YOU SCHLAGS!!! I’LL GIYA A SCHLAP!!!” He holds one of his big meaty fists up into the air and gestures to it with his head at the people still walking by who are crossing the road to avoid him. “Excuse me Sir, but ve must ask yo to leave...” Behind him we see a French waiter, a tray of drinks in hand who politely asks our dejected Englishman, proud of Queen, Country and soccer team to quieten down and vacate. English Soccer Fan : “You French.... YOU A DAMN FROG YOU MUPPET!?!? I’LL GIYA NUT!” He lunges his shaven head forward and connects with the bridge of the waiters nose sending him stumbling back. *SMASH* The drunken lout then smashes his pint glass against the edge of a table and thrusts it into the Frenchman’s face, dropping the man who screams as a trickle of watered down blood starts to flow from his face. Waiter : “ARGHHHHHHHHHHH!!! MY EYES!!!” English Soccer Fan : “HAHA! JUST LIKE AT WATERLOO YOU FRENCH SCHLAG! WATERLOO... VINDALOO... VINDALOO! VINDALOO! VINDALOO! VINDALOO NAH NAH!!!” He then starts to do something only he and his kind can do and master, a phenomenon that nobody else quite understands.... The Drunken English Soccer Thug Dance.... An alcohol fuelled, one man Conga like dance with incorporated Jazz hands. English Soccer Fan : “WE’RE ON THE BALL! WE’RE ON THE BALL! WE’RE ON THE BALL! WE’RE ON THE BALL! NEVILLE TO CAMPBELL, CAMPBELL TO RIO, RIO TO SCHOLESY... PINT TO BIG DAVE... PINT GLASS TO FROG’S FACE! WE’RE ON THE BALL! WE’RE ON THE BALL! WE’RE ON THE BALL!” All the people look on in disgust, wondering how a man such as this could infect their little World... Except one who seems to be finding the whole affair rather entertaining. “Like... WAY COOL! That, like, SO ruled.... Is that his blood?” Onto the scene walks the last person you’d think to see approaching a drunken Soccer hooligan.... The fiancé of Xavier Kannon, Miss Eleanor Hall... Her pale Blue eyes look down in glee at the bleeding Frenchman, nodding almost hypnotically. English Soccer Fan : “OWIGHT DARLIN’!” She looks to him blankly, wondering what he said but kind of nodding to it anyway and answering. Eleanor : “Er.... Yeah?.... Are you from that English place?” The huge England shirt clad man’s eyes well up again at the thought of his homeland... Bangers and Mash, Fish and Chips, Special Brew.... All the things that makes him so proud of his heritage. English Soccer Fan : “That I am darlin’... That I am.... Cockney through and through... BORN WITHIN THE RING OF THE BOW BELLS....” He them puts his right hand over the badge on his shirt and stands up straight, shoulders back and teary eyes looking right ahead much to Eleanor’s bemusement. English Soccer Fan : “...GOD SAVE OUR GRACIOUS QUEEN....” As he belts out a slurred, drunken version of the English national anthem he valet looks him over, still confused as usual. Eleanor : “So.... Did you like, lose, or something?” He breaks out of song and looks at her with angry eyes, his Red face still masked in rage... If Ellie were anyone else other than.. Well, Ellie she may have taken this as a hint but instead she just tilts her head, gesturing for an answer. English Soccer Fan : “WE WUZ ROBBED!!! THOSE DAMN BLEEDIN’ DIVING FOREIGNERS WITH THEIR BIG FRONT TEETH!!!” He picks up another table and hurls it through the window, sending fragments of glass flying everywhere to the transfixed stare of the Prep. Eleanor : “Like, COOL!!!” She snaps out of it and looks back to him, her eyes starting to show sympathy. Eleanor : “Anyway, I know how you feel... My rich, handsome, good-looking, famous, powerful and did I mention rich fiancé lost the other day... Well, not lost, more didn’t win...” The soccer thug seems to be mellowing under the influence of Ellie~! as he picks up one of the chairs and puts it upright, taking a seat as the valet perches on the side of a table. English Soccer Fan : “Was it on penalties... BECAUSE WE’D HAVE KILLED THEM ON PENALTIES! THIS ISN’T 1990, 1996 OR 1998... WE’D HAVE ROCKETED ‘EM IN! NO CHRISSY WADDLE, NO GARRETH SOUTHGATE! WE’D HAVE WON IT!!!” In the interests of her own safety the Prep slides off the table and takes a few steps back. Eleanor : “Um... Kinda like that, anyway, he didn’t win so now we have a Japo-American man who wears goggles at our wedding!” He sneers. English Soccer Fan : “Don’t get me started on the Japs love... My Granddad was a Pris-” Even though cutting off a violent soccer thug while he’s talking about the suffering of his grandparent isn’t exactly a wise thing to do... This is Ellie~! so it happens. Eleanor : “So now he only has like 9 days to cripple or kill this guy so he can’t be at the wedding.... But I thought that the Groom and Best Man were meant to get on.... Anyway, so now he has to fight Legend In-” This time she tastes her own medicine as the emotional brute cuts her off, a fact she doesn’t seem to appreciate but is in no position to stop his rant. English Soccer Fan : “LEGENDS.... I’LL TELL YOU ABOUT LEGENDS... 1966, ENGLAND GERMANY, 4-2.... BOOBY MOORE, JEFF HURST, MARTIN PETERS, BOBBY CHARLTON.. LEGENDS... ALL OF ‘EM LEGENDS!” Her eyes narrow while she tries to understand a single word just said. Eleanor : “Okay.... So there’s the Jap, the legend and a drunk smelly guy who says all these words I don’t even understand.... No offence by the way...” She breathes a sigh of relief as none is taken... Eleanor : “Anyway, so they are all fighting inside a Cage... You know, like in that football ad with the Frenchman?” The thug springs up to his feet. English Soccer Fan : “FROGS!!! WHERE?!?!?” The Prep takes a little leap backwards in shock then points to the waiter. Eleanor : “Isn’t he French?” The thug then runs over to the fallen French waiter and kicks him in the ribs a few times while he is down, singing away gleefully as he does so to the entertainment of the Prep. English Soccer Fan : “WE’RE SINGING FOR ENGLAND... ENGURLUND! WE’RE SINGING HIS SONG... WE’RE SINGING FOR ENGLAND... ENGURLUND!” He ceases his vicious attack on the Frenchman and walks back over to Ellie~!, brushing his hands off with a smile on his face until he looks down at his shoes and snarls. English Soccer Fan : “NOW I GOT DAMN FROG’S BLOOD ON MY SHOES!!!” He grabs a glass and throws it at the Frenchman’s head, smashing it on the back of his skull. Eleanor : “Anyway, where was I? Oh... So now he has to fight this Greek guy again on Friday....” English Soccer Fan : “Greek?” The man’s face lights up, finally joy returning to his eyes as he thinks back to fond memories. English Soccer Fan : “I REMEMBER IT NOW!!! ENGLAND AGAINST GREECE.... NINETIETH MINUTE... DAMN CHEATING GREEK BASTARDS ATE 2-1 UP.... FREE KICK ON THE EDGE OF THE BOX... BECKHAM RUNS UP.... GOAL! STUFF THAT IN A VINE LEAF AND EAT IT YOU GREEK SCHLAGS!” Mush to Ellie~!’s disgust he breaks out the Drunken English Soccer Thug Dance again, the drunken staggering turning Ellie~! off him and she slinks away as he is distracted, fuelled by strong Lager and the memory of that goal flying in. English Soccer Fan : “WE’RE ON THE BALL! WE’RE ON THE BALL! WE’RE ON THE BALL! WE’RE ON THE BALL! WE’RE ON THE BALL! WE’RE ON THE BALL! WE’RE ON THE BALL! WE’RE ON THE BALL!” With Ellie~! now gone, our shot fades out on the embarrassing, jigging figure of Big Dave the English Soccer Fan as he belts out the terrace hits to all that will listen. “ COS I RE-MEM-BER 3 LIONS ON A SHIRT.... JULES RIMET STILL GLEAMING... NO MORE YEARS OF HURT.... NEVER STOP ME DREAMING.... 3 LIONS ON A SHIRT!!!”
