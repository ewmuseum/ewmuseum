The Death Of Rent-A-Hero Part 5....The Burial...And The Beginning Of Something New

28 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6-28-2002 OOC:Believe it or not, things are going back to normal after this RP, so no fear. I won't be using novel style or being depressive after this, it's only being doen to prove a point :) Don't go thinking I'm gonna go goth or anything cause it just ain't me. Enjoy the finale of the Death of RAH and be ready for his next evolution because change is ALWAYS good and trust me....you'll liek this change, and I wouldn't do it if I didn't think it'd entertain the fans. Anyways, enjoy, holla. It took a loooong walk...but you made it home... You finally felt the feeling in your legs after a total beating from the two thug friends that 'murdered' you at the bar. But you can't help but feel like a World War 2 survivor or something......you know, the ones that get mercilessly battered on the field and are sent home, missing a leg or some other organ. In cases like that....YOU PUT HIM OUT OF HIS MISERY. Because everyone knows that most would prefer a quick death over a slow, never-ending, and unknown one. You were bathed in your blood in that alley...crusted crimson blood is all over you as you enter your usually humble apartment with slight steps...still feelignt he sting of meaty punches and brutal boot heels. You can still taste the blood in your mouth...having no other choice but to guzzle down the crimson juice of life. You ignore you luxuries like the wide screen TV... The leather couch... The horde of expensive gaming systems... The racks of comcis... And go straight into the bathroom, flicking the light switch on lacksidasilly, leaving a smudge of blood on the milky white switch panel as a reminder you were there. You reach over to the Duck Tales towel with Scrooge McDuck imprints upon it and snatch it from the iron towel rack, wiping your blood flooded eyes before discarding it to the ground in it's blood-soaked form on the tiled floor. All was well with you... Until you looked in the friggin mirror... With your eyes cleared and your vision back to you....you actually get a chance to LOOK at what has been done to you... You look yourself in the same soft blue eyes that you use to see everyone else...The answer came to you with that long cold stare, too shocked to even scream at what you've let yourself become. You stare at the swollen lip... Grow angry at the rivers of blood you've let flow from the alley to back here...Take notice of the rips and tears in what used to be your pride, your superhero replica suits... And you say to yourself...'Rent-A-Hero must die...' Dead set on that decision, you tear away at the already ripped spandex top of your costume...ripping it into shreds until your chest is free, soft red bruises littering your ripped chest . You discard the spandex to the side as you sling the bathroom medicine cabinet open, your hand knocking over bottles of pills and other toiletries until you grasp a pair of sewing sheers sitting idly upon the top rack. You gaze at them, knowing they will be the beginning of the end of you..... And after a short pause... You take the sheers and raise them to the blonde locks of slicked hair you proudly wore for all these years...being one of the trademarks you were known for as yourself...And without fail, you cut them mercilessly. You hack and slash away at those blonde locks as they fall into the sink...remembering everytime Amy would run her fingers through them and make you feel tingly all over...that thought just pissing you off more as you sheer more and more...until your hair is as short as you need, the sink full of shards of the blond hair you used to treasure so much as you stalk outof the bathroom with your hair uneven and jagged. You end up in your room, bursting into the 'Closet O' Costumes' and looking at them with disdian...disgust....hatred... Reminders of how weak and gulliable you were...It took you years to collect them and fill this closet with these 'treasures'... Now you are consumed with an all absorbing hatred to destroy them... And who are you to deny your hatred? So you snatch suit after suit off the racks in your closet....each costume a reminder of bad memories....EACH COSTUME A REMINDER OF HER!! THEY MUST BE DESTROYED!! You pile them high within the bath tub of your bathroom until the entire closet is purged...not a single God-forsaken costume left. Now there's only one thign left to do.... Destroy them...And that you did as you came back into the bathroom with a match in hand, striking it against the bathroom sink as the crimson flame danced atop the wooden stick, waitingto be set free and burn whatever you desired...You desired those costumes burned....And with a flick of that match into the tub.... Your prayers were answered.... You couldn't help but sit in the bathroom and just watch each and every last one of those costumes torch and smoke into burning flames...a demented smile overcoming your face as you can hear the sizzle of melting spandex...
