The Land Of Default

18 March 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/18/04 “Get the fuck outta my h—house, bitch!” The drunken shout was followed by a flying shot glass that exploded as it hit the back of the bar door, inches away from a terrified man running for his life. The man threw open the door and took off at a sprint down the moonlit street in some random city. “HaHAHA! YES!” The booming laugh came from none other than the pride of PCW, Brian Arson, who was slumped in one of the barstools with beer bottles and shot glasses scattered along the counter and floor around him. With the exception of the petrified bartender, Arson was the only remaining occupant in the small bar. At this point in time he had nearly drunk himself retarded and had no idea what city he was in, let alone why he was there. “Excuse me, good sir!” Arson yelled to the bartender who was no more than three feet away. “Could you tell me...” His head drifted backward in a daze, but he jolted it back into position. “Excuse me! You!” He pointed at the bartender, who shrugged his shoulders in confusion. “Yes?” The bartender asked half-amused. “No, not you... you!” Arson pointed at the bartender again and stayed in a pointing position for what seemed like a few minutes or so, staring at the man’s name tag in a trance. “Uhm, sir, what do you—” “Shhhh!” Arson put his index finger to his mouth. “Can you not... I’m speaking! Jesus, what? Okay...” “Sir, I’m going to have to ask you—” “Hey! Time! Watch... hmm?” Arson shouted, slapping his own wrist. “What?” The bartender was obviously getting a little perturbed. “Oh... What time is it? Uhm, it’s 1:50. I have to be closing, sir, I’m going to need you to—” “1:50! Jesus Christ... what city are... city we in?” Arson interrupted the man once again, and by looking at the bartender it was obvious that he just wanted to get the drunk out of here as quickly as possible. “You’re in the city of Default... it’s where people go when they haven’t been told where they’re going.” “...” “Just think of the Sims...” “Great game...” Arson smiled and thought about the entertaining computer game for a few minutes, chuckling out loud at the thought of the characters catching on fire. “But seriously, sir, you need to go... I’m sorry.” The bartender’s voice grew stern as he finished cleaning out a margarita glass. He slammed his fist down on to the bar, snapping Arson out of his daydream. Brian rubbed out his eyes and stepped down from the barstool only to slip on a shot glass he’d dropped on the floor. Flying forward, Arson busted his head on the edge of the bar and reversed direction, landing on the hard floor with a large SMACK! “Ah my fuck...” Arson let out a muffled whimper as he held his nose with both his hands. Blood trickled through the slits of his fingers and all over his shirt. Steadying himself with one hand, he managed to get to his feet and then took off his ruined shirt, using it to stop the uncontrollable bleeding. “Jesus Christ, man, are you all ri—” “Fuck, shut up, I’m fine!” Arson lashed out at the bartender and grabbed his fallen coat, proceeding to stumble out of the empty bar. Out on the street, the sidewalks were empty and only a few cars could be seen off in the distance. The moon was partially hidden by some drifting clouds. Arson decided to go left and dragged himself down the street, resembling Jack Nicholson sauntering down the hallway in The Shining, only Brian was leaving the bar and he was much hotter than Nicholson. Arson’s shirt had turned a light crimson, but the bleeding seemed to have stopped, so he threw the piece of clothing into a trash can where some homeless person would probably find it and contract AIDS. Brian knew he was here for some reason, but couldn’t exactly remember what it was at the moment. He just knew the word ‘buzz’ sounded funny and snickered happily down the street repeating the word over and over. Somewhere off in the distance Arson saw a light flashing brightly, like the gates of Heaven opening and urging him toward them. Brian’s jaw dropped in awe and his eyes filled with glee, reflecting the shimmering light... “Live Girls...” Brian whispered to himself. Arson’s pace quickened, steadily turning into an awkward drunken jog. He ran across the street toward the bright sign, tripping on the curb, nearly cracking his head times two on a parking meter. Brian hurried toward the door only to be met with a locked door handle. Pounding on the glass, he tried to get someone to let him in, but there was no one in sight, and he hobbled over to a bench in defeat. Pulling out a cell phone, Brian dialed a random number, hoping he’d hear a familiar voice. After a few rings someone picked up the phone on the other line. Brian sat there dazed, a sad and pathetic look on his face. “Help meee...” He muttered into the phone. He was completely lost, his driver had taken off hours ago, and now he was so fucked up that he didn’t even know what city he was in. Dropping the phone to his side, Brian curled up into a ball and lay down on the cold street bench, blood dripping down his nose once again. There was nothing for him to do until he was sober, and at this rate, he might have to take the entire weekend off. Hopefully someone would answer his plea, because until then, there was nothing for him to do but wait in pain and misery... Will someone answer Brian’s call for help? Will he be able to regain his composure before his crucial tournament match? And will someone please tell me what fucking city we’re in? Stay tuned...
