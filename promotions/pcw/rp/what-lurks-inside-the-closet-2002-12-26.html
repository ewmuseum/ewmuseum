What Lurks Inside The Closet

26 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12/26/02 Only one word could describe the actions of the man who had booked my match this week… Unjustified What had I ever done to him, whoever he was? Absolutely nothing as far as I was concerned. And yet somehow they find it justified to place me against two of my close friends, Chris Storm and Jackson Rust. I couldn’t fight the man of my dreams along with the man who had helped me out this past Tuesday, it just wasn’t right. I had to find someway out of this. Maybe they would take it into consideration. But until then how was I supposed to look Chris in the eye and tell him I love him, yet also let him know that I wouldn’t back down for anyone. It was impossible. You can’t just tell someone you love them but then inform them you would have to beat them up. And how would I explain this to the children? I couldn’t just tell them that Chris and I would have to fight. That would make it seem like they had to pick a side, and that’s the last thing I wanted them to feel especially after the great amount trauma they had gone through in the past few weeks. I looked over at the kitchen table spotting the Santa suit that Chris had worn this past Tuesday. I smiled remembering how cute he had looked in it and then decided that perhaps I should make up for what would take place this coming Tuesday by cleaning up his apartment for him and hanging up the suit. It might not be much, but surely it was better than nothing. I grabbed the suit and sneaked as quietly as I could into his room, being careful not to make too much noise because the last thing I wanted to do is wake up the kids who would just make a bigger mess even if it wasn’t their intentions. With the suit tossed over my shoulder I walked into the room looking for a place to put it. Even though Christmas was over perhaps he would want to use it again soon. The question was where should I put it? I couldn’t leave it on the bed the kids might stumble across it and for Christa who still believed in Santa Clause that would just be a horrible experience. I could leave it in the closet, but there was not much room left in there. But finally it struck me and I felt rather dense. All I had to do is move some things around in the closet and perhaps put them under Chris’ bed for safe keeping. Surely Chris wouldn’t mind, it wasn’t like I was ransacking his closet and taking everything in sight. I was simply moving things around. I placed the Santa suit on the bed as I looked around the closet for something that would fit under Chris’ bed, and then I spotted it. A large black shoebox. The Santa suit would fit perfect in that spot. All I had to do is get the shoebox and place it under Chris’ bed. Surely moving a shoebox wouldn’t be that big of a deal. All I had to do is let Chris know where it was once he finally got back home. I reached up towards to box grabbing it with my arms while standing on the tip of my toes. Finally swatting the box at just the right angle it slipped right into my arms. I caught it placing in on the bed beside the Santa suit. I wrapped the suit up into a ball and tossed it like a basketball fitting it in perfectly. Who cared if it was a little wrinkled? Irons were created for a reason. After placing the suit in the closet I looked over at the box lying on the bed as a sudden temptation crept over me. I looked at the box as I pondered to myself what was inside the box. I reached for the lid but then a feeling of guilt struck me. You know Tessa you wouldn’t like it if Chris went through your stuff without permission so why are you going to do the same thing to him? I pulled my hand back towards my body but then a contradicting thought stuck me. You do have a right to know though what is inside those boxes, after all he is your boyfriend and you don’t keep secrets from him. My hand reached back out and I pulled the lid off. Looking down inside the box the first thing I spotted was a little black book. I picked up the book and began to scroll through it. The first thing I spotted inside the book was a number written down next to woman’s name. The name read Kim Simpson. I had remembered that name from WIW. That was the snot nosed ring announcer that I couldn’t stand. Tossing the book to the side I noticed something else lying underneath it. I looked down and spotted a Playgirl magazine with Chris on the cover. My eyes stung with tears but inside my mind filled with fury. From what I understood Chris had given up this lifestyle and yet right before my eyes laid a cover that I could have ripped in half. Without showing any care I tossed the magazine to the side and dug deeper into the box. I found just what I was expecting. A collection of adult videos that he had made before he had gotten with me. I knew I shouldn’t be upset considering this all happened before I came along, but at the same time I couldn’t help but wonder why he would still have these tapes especially when I remembered that Chris had told me he was a changed man. Without bothering to clean up I walked out of Chris room back to the couch where I could think. I began to wonder if I had made the right choice in life. First putting my brother’s children in danger and then finding out that the man I loved might not even care about me after all. Maybe I was just another one of his toys, after all how did I know he had changed? There was surely no proof to tell me that. Sure he kissed me and cuddled me close, but he had done the same type of things with many other women as well. I couldn’t take it anymore. Sitting down on the couch I reached out for the phone calling the one man I knew would listen to me. The man who had helped me when Chris wasn’t there on Tuesday. The man by the name of Jackson Rust.
