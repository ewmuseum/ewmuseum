Last Stand In Open Country #2

2 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Title: Last Stand In Open Country #2 Featuring: Duke Williams Date: 02/02/03 -Getting back to the basics of wrestling has been fun. No more detailed bullshit just pure trash talk. One month till I’m gone and I’m going out my way. This time Duke Williams is sitting at a bar waiting to go to his first OSW Slam show. He is drinking whiskey by the gallon and it’s a good thing cus a PCW camera is there to catch his rant.- Williams: You fuckers where here just a few days ago and what did I tell you about this Jack fellow. Not very direct is he?? Afraid he’ll say the wrong thing to the wrong man so he “entertains” us with some dribble. This new generation of wrestler is so fucking pathetic it’s a waste. -Duke takes a shot of whiskey.- Williams: I remember when a guy with a name like Violence Jack would mean he was a bad ass who told it like it is. Now he’s dicking around with words like m’lord. What the fuck is that? -Duke laughs.- Williams: I got me one of them computer things at home with some games on it. I play this one called Warcraft where you build up an army. They got these little servant guys that when you click on them and tell them to go chop wood they say. “Yes m’lord.” -Duke shakes his head.- Williams: I guess what I’m getting at in my drunken stupor is you’re about as scary as a video game. I think in your long, drown out, promo you mentioned me once. Thanks for the common courtesy hoss. You said something about annihilating me or some shit. I don’t know I really wasn’t paying attention. As a matter of fact I was falling asleep watching your promo, then I heard my name, perked up, and it was over. -Duke shrugs his shoulders.- Williams: So kid your on your little mission to purify PCW or some crap like that. I’m on a mission to exit PCW in style. Where at a crossroad my friend. I’m sure you’ll understand when my big rig runs over your horse and buggy to get where I have to go. -Duke smiles.- Williams: If you don’t understand then why don’t you go film another one of your movie clips. I’m sure it will be entertaining. You go entertain I’ll go wrestle. It’s kind of like the old joke you know why there’s no dogs in Poland? -Duke cracks another smile.- Williams: Its because the dogs like to piss on Poles. You see kid you are who you are. I’m a trained, professional, wrestler. You a storyteller. Well I got a story for you. Man returns to PCW, man gets his ass kicked by a veteran, veteran smiles. -Duke drinks down some whiskey.- Williams: Get ready to be sent WHISKEY BENT AND HELL BOUND!!! -Duke toasts the camera.- =w= ---- View Duke Williams's Biography
