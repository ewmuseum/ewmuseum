Fellowship of the Amulets: Conspiracy Unmasked

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: ..... The ferry ride across the lake was peaceful. It was now me, Jesse ‘Sam’ Ramey, AJ ‘Pippin’ Palmer, and Meridoc Kaldorn or just Merry. The ferry was seated for six people so it fit alright. Moments ago, we had all thought that a black rider had approached us in the dark but it turned out to be just Merry. Merry was house-keeping my new ‘house’ in Brickethollow and had said that she had given it a homely look. I could tell Merry wanted to know more about these black riders that we had mentioned about. She didn’t know anything about the journey that we have had so far and wanted to know everything but I had stopped her and told her that we would explain all at a great supper time to celebrate making it to Brickethollow. The lake that we were passing was pretty big. A couple of miles it was but the ferry wasn’t controlled by a captain but instead, some kind of remote control motor thing that wasn’t very fast, but it moved steadily without too much shifting to one side. The ferry hit the dock and all five of us got out of the boat. The sun was just starting to rise and the shadows of us all stretched far out to the side. We all walked nonstop without talking for about two hours. Nobody complained that there feet were sore for we all knew that we were almost there. When we could see the small town of Brickethollow ahead of us, the thought that was in the back of my head the whole time came back to me. Everybody still thought that I was staying in Brickethollow for a while when really, I was planning on leaving alone as soon as possible to go find the ring. I had talked about this with the elves during the night we met them but nobody from my party knew about it and I was particularly pleased with myself for keeping the secret. We walked into the village of Brickethollow. The town was made of about 12 streets. There were no skyscrapers or really big buildings and the town looked friendly. Our house was along the main road, towards the edge of the town. As we walked down main street, we had some on-lookers staring at us. We hadn’t attracted many attention but I guess the fact of someone knew entering the town was amazing enough for the towns people. Getting to our house, it was just like I had expected. The outside was pretty old and the house looked small but on the inside, it was a lot more cozy. The kitchen in the house had brand new cupboards and tiles on the floor. It was humungous but big enough. There was a small dining room with a table for 6 or 8 if you squished in. The living room was cozy and off to the sides were three bedrooms. The bedrooms weren’t big but the master bedroom had a bathroom off of it. Inside the house were paintings on the walls. Some of the paintings I had recognized from the castle. Some of the other stuff I noticed were from the small bedroom that I had stayed in. The rest of the stuff was thrown in to add more comfort. For a couple of hours, we all sat on the couch and talked. We talked about things like the king and how he was doing and other various stuff from the outside world, not really mentioning the ring at all. Then, Merry, who was dying to know everything had finally asked to hear the whole story from the beginning. I was the one who told Merry the whole story and any parts that I left out, Sam filled in with little bits. We told him everything from the black riders, to the elves and how they helped us. We told him about the mushroom field and Farmer Maggot. I went up to the part where Merry showed up and Merry filled in the rest. “Very interesting.” was the only thing that Merry said. “I shall start preparing a huge feat for us!” suddenly said AJ ‘Pippin’ Palmer, off topic. He got up out of his chair and walked to the kitchen, taking out some pans. We all ignored him and Merry turned her attention back to me. “Amulet, I have some news that may be good, or bad, depending on how you look at it.” suddenly said Merry. “What is it?” replied I. What could Merry know? “Well, I have received word from the king’s castle and have found out that Mark ‘Gandalf’ Makonie has gone out on a trip. He hasn’t told anybody where he is going but with the news of these black riders, I can only fear the worst.” Gandalf had gone on a trip? He was very adventurous but I didn’t like the thought of the black riders suddenly catching up to him. Gandalf wouldn’t say anything about where I was, it was the type of person he was and that worried me. What if the riders killed him if he didn’t tell them where I was or what I was heading for? “This isn’t good.” was all I said. For the next half hour of forty five minutes, we talked about other things, mostly anonymous things that weren’t really irrelevant. We were almost running out of things to talk about when Pippin called from the kitchen. “Supper is served!” The supper that Pippin had prepared was huge! There were potatoes and carrots and ham and cheese and pickles and french fries, all layed out neatly for us all to pig out. When we were almost done, I decided to break the news to the bunch about me leaving so soon. “Hey gang, I have to tell you something.” “What is it?” For a moment, Sam didn’t look too happy but then it looked like he decided to let me speak before saying anything or worrying anymore. “Well, I don’t plan on staying on Brickethollow very long. I want to leave as soon as possible to find the amulet.” AJ, Jesse, and Kaldorn all looked at each other for a moment and then Sam Ramey turned to me and replied. “We know your plan, Mark.” I looked at them for a moment, shocked. How could they know my plan? I was so secret about it. The satisfied thought in my head suddenly turned to disappointed. “What? You think you were so secret about it?” asked Pippin. “Well, YEAH!” Merry, Pippin, and Sam all laughed at me for a moment. I still couldn’t believe that they knew. “I found out when you were talking to the elves.” said Sam, continuing, “I then told Pippin and we informed Merry not so long ago.” “Wow.” Merry first looked at Sam and Pippin as for asking to say something. The two nodded and Merry said. “Mark, we want to come with you!”
