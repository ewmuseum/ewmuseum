The After Effects of Boda's Retirement

21 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 21st May 2002 The world keeps spinning around... around... still damned spinning. Makes you want to reach out and grab something, shake it violently and scream at it to stop moving. Jesus, who invented head injuries? It certainly wasn’t a man with a head, because then he would have suffered one and felt exactly how bad the damned things are. Miyagawa stumbles into the bathroom, the colour drained from his face. He wears a pair of Calvin Klein black boxer shorts only, and there is sweat lightly pouring down his face. He lifts his hand up and wipes it off his brow. Miyagawa: Goddammit. I’m going to kill Boda. **pauses for a few seconds and he realises what he just said** Oh wait, I already retired his ass. Oh well, I guess I can’t go killing an unemployed man... that’s far to Clyde-esque. He turns about and goes toward a medicine cabinet on the wall. He opens one of the mirror doors and pulls out a bottle of pills. He opens the bottle and tips out two pills into the left hand. Placing the bottle back on the shelf, he closes the cabinet and turns towards the white enamel sink. Miyagawa: I hate concussions. I just really hate stinking concussions. Miyagawa tips his head into the sink and drinks straight from the cold water tap, before standing back up and slapping the pills into his mouth... and then drinking more water to get them down. Miyagawa: And I hate taking pills. I hate taking pills for stinking concussions. I’m gonna kill Boda. Damn, why’d I have to retire his basketball playing fat ass... who can I blame now? Miyagawa ponders on the idea for a second as he wipes the loose water from his mouth. Miyagawa: Screw it. I’ll find someone. Miyagawa wipes his brow once again with a towel and wanders back toward the bathroom door when it swings open inwards. Standing there in her dressing gown is his heavily pregnant wife and the half sister of the aforementioned Boda, Natalia Miyagawa. Miyagawa: Did I wake you? Natalia: Yes. Miyagawa: I think I have a concussion, so I wanted to take something for the nausea. Natalia points at her inflated belly. Natalia: Snap. Hand them over... what’s the word... da, buster, yes that’s it. Hand them over buster. Miyagawa opens the cabinet again and hands the bottle of pills over to his wife who takes two into her left hand and hands the bottle back. Miyagawa: And to think that ten months ago we were out on the tiles, drinking the night away and then throwing up in the morning. Natalia: Well we still do the throwing up in the morning, but I think if I drank anything alcoholic right now, then I’d just throw up some more. Miyagawa: Amen to that. Natalia: Pass me the drinking beaker, will you? Miyagawa grabs a plastic beaker off a shelf above the bath and hands it to Natalia who fills it with water from the tap and uses it to take the pills in the similar fashion to how her husband, Alexander Miyagawa did. Natalia: Well, I did warn you that my brother would probably beat the crap out of you... and well, he did. Miyagawa: I won, and that’s what matters. I may be concussed and had my left shoulder dislocated, but I won. As he mentions that, you do notice that Miyagawa’s left shoulder is pretty red and puffy. Miyagawa: The good won out in the end and I ended Boda’s career. Unfortunately I think I might have gained that columnist douche as a friend but I can live with that. Either that or I’ll send Syko to hang out with him until he stops talking about me being so great. Natalia: Can we talk about this in the morning? I have to get up early to throw up. Miyagawa: Give me a shout; I think I’ll throw up early as well. And together the odd couple of PCW head back to bed, turning off the bathroom light on the way.
