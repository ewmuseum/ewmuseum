Reclaiming Misty

11 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11th September 2002 Misty looks around the hotel room after Redemption Song’s big debut victory over Misplaced on Turmoil. For some strange reason, the valet that always accompanies PCW’s newest tag-team superstars didn’t accompany them down to the ring for their match. Instead, she was just used as a distraction for the fierce beating that Jim Surge and Ben Nighthorse Campbell laid down on Stephen Cairns and Carey Nowski, a beating so fierce that Misplaced themselves were completely dominated and humiliated in what could turn out to be their last ever appearance on PCW television, at least for a while. They should be back any second now. I wonder what could be… Ding! A thin smile creeps across Misty’s lips as she seemingly floats her way over to the kitchen area of the Portland Marriott Residence Inn and opens the stove. A creaking sound is heard from her brand new black leather pants as she bends down and Misty pulls her face back from the heat. I’m such an idiot. Why did I just stick my head in there? I don’t even have oven mitts! Misty stands back up and straightens her tight white t-shirt with “Redemption Song” written across the front and back in scratchy black lettering as she opens a kitchen drawer and rummages through looking for an oven mitt. She finds a couple of old dishtowels, which will do, and she returns to the still-open oven and retries a fresh-baked apple pie. Misty turns carefully and slowly opens the refrigerator door with her left hand and sets the pie down inside to cool down. As she does, a fax machine on the counter starts to wizz and buzz with activity. Misty looks down and smiles at what she sees, it must mean a favorable result for Redemption Song at Turmoil and she leaves the fax in the machine as she grabs three small plates and sets the table for dessert. Those two boys are in for a BIG surprise! Just then, Misty hears an elevator door open out in the hallway and the excited and pumped up voices of Jim Surge and Ben Nighthorse Campbell are also heard echoing through the hotel’s hallway. After a few seconds of fumbling around with the room’s keycard, the door bursts open and the cowboy and Indian team of Redemption Song stride confidently into the room with big smiles on their faces! “Did you see it Mist?” Jim says excitedly as he makes his way over to his longtime valet. Surge, looking fresh and clean in a black Nike jogging suit after his post-match shower puts his arms around Misty’s neck but the feisty former wrestler shrugs him off and slides out of his grasp. Jim looks a little surprised that Misty would pull away so quickly, especially after the positive result. “No, I didn’t see it,” Misty says as she uses all of her will power to hold back the contempt in her voice. “You guys didn’t ask me to come down to ringside for your debut match. That’s cool though, I made you both a pie.” Sure, I’m good enough to be used as bait but these jerks don’t even have the decency to bring me down to ringside when it really counts. Well they’ll soon learn that good old Misty isn’t their lap dog who just goes along with whatever they say. “Something smells GOOD!” Ben says, closing the door behind him as he finally makes his way into the room. Ben’s smile looks especially silly as his red skin offsets his white teeth making them look extremely bright. “A PIE?! Awesome!” Ben exclaims! The Apache, also dressed in a black track suit and with his hair tied back uncharacteristically in a pony tail, runs in and plops himself on the couch, turning on the TV to catch a late-night rebroadcast of Turmoil. “It’s my mother’s recipe,” Misty says as she opens the refrigerator and pulls the still steaming pie back out onto the top of the stove. “I though either way I should do something for your debut match! It’s chock full of fresh apples.” That ain’t all it’s chocked with boys. “Excellent!” Jim says as he moves over to the counter and watches Misty start cutting slices of the pie and putting them onto the small plates. “And here I thought you were mad at us!” Misty can no longer keep up her fake smile and she rolls her eyes at Redemption Song’s powerhouse as she finishes with the cutting. “Just make sure I’m down there at ringside with you both next time, okay?” she says as she hands a plate over to Jim and one to Ben, who sits on the couch in glee as he watches himself and his half-brother dismantle Misplaced on national television. “Hey, what’s this?” Jim asks as he notices a piece of paper in the fax machine. The Tombstone, Arizona native ambles over to the fax machine and picks up the piece of paper and scans it quickly. “Holy crap! Ben! Holy crap!! Woo hoo!!!!” Ben is finally torn away from the television and answers with a full-mouthed: “Huh?” as bits of piecrust and cooked apples fall from his mouth. “We’ve been booked against Duzza and Makonie in a non-title bout for the next Turmoil!” “Really?” Ben asks in a bit of surprised disbelief. “Do you think they’re really ready for us?” Ben suddenly puts a hand to his forehead and drops his fork down onto his plate. “Man, I’m starting to feel pretty strange,” the Apache Ghostrider says as his voice quivers a bit with nervous energy. “But this pie! This pie is phenomenal! Must… eat….more…..” “Yeah man,” Surge begins confidently as he watches Ben with a bit of a disturbed look on his face, “I think we’ll probably break a sweat in this one but you know it’s already in the bag. Bad blood is already starting to brew between the champs and you can’t be teammates with someone you really hate…” Ben forgoes the fork altogether and just starts cramming the rest of his piece of pie into his mouth. After a few moments, a wild-eyed Campbell struggles to his feet and tries to make his way over to the counter for more but instead he collapses onto his back on the floor of the hotel room. “BEN!” Jim yells as he immediately gets down on his hands and knees along side his half-brother to check and see if he was still breathing. “Misty, quick, call for the paramedics!!” Misty walks over to the phone but instead of picking it up she teases Jim for a second before continuing over to the front door. “Sorry Mr. Surge,” Misty says as she opens the door. “Maybe if you thought well enough about me to include me at ringside I’d think well enough about you to help you out.” “What?!” Surge yells, his western accent finally emerging with his agitated condition. “You’re on about that and there’s a man dying here??” “Oh yeah,” Misty says as she stops at the door, “don’t eat the pie. There’s poison in it.” Misty slams the door and walks out into the hallway. She hears Jim scream after her as he panics and tries to make a hasty telephone call to the emergency paramedics. Misty smiles as she pulls out a very small and sleek black cellular phone. She presses and holds the #1 button and after a long moment, she is connected. “Tom? This is Misty. Everything is going just as you said.” A garbled voice can be heard from the other end of the line. “Yes, but it’s a non-title match. Exactly, I don’t know that either but…” The voice’s tone gets louder and starts rambling off what seems like a detailed list of very important instructions. “Okay, right. They won’t see me but I’ll be there in Wisconsin. Whatever you need Mr. Core. These guys don’t realize you’re just working in their best interests.” Job well done Misty you clever thing you! If only Surge had eaten some of the pie as well! At any rate job well done! Misty clicks off her phone and makes her way out into the Portland night.
