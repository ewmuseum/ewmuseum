The Hunt For Mr. Jones

2 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Title: The Hunt For Mr. Jones Featuring: Xavier Kannon Date: 02/01/03 “Here Kitty Kitty!” With the worried voice of PCW’s preppy Commissioner, Ellie~! we open up on her pulling sofa cushions up and looking under tables in her Greenwich Village apartment, calling for her Cat, Mr. Jones. Eleanor : “Er, like, where are you and stuff?” Feverishly she continues to search, looking in every small corner and crack far to small to hide the White furry Cat. Pausing for a second she looks down to the wooden floor, and more precisely, the White fluffy rug that houses the coffee table. Eleanor : “Great, he might be lost on the rug and all WAY camouflaged!” Dropping down onto her knees, she crawls around the edge of the rug, stroking it. Eleanor : “Here Kitty...” Funnily enough, the rug doesn’t seen to house the Cat and she pulls herself back up to her feet, looking around the room once more to see if she missed anywhere. Eleanor : “I, like, SO wish I was a Witch and stuff...” The look on her face is that dangerous one she gets whenever an idea passes into her head... Not the safest of places for your average idea to be. With her pale Blue eyes sparkling and a smile forming on her Pink lips she ponders. Eleanor : “.. I would, like, WAY cast a Cat finding spell.” Turning in a circle and looking out around the room that appears not to house the missing Cat, she continues. Eleanor : “How hard can witchcraft be?” Closing her eyes and holding her arms out to the side, Ellie~! lowers her head and starts to mutter something unintelligible. As she does so, the lights in the ceiling flash and the windows fly open, a strong wind swirling all around her and blowing leaves in a spiral around her body which is now bathed in Pink and Silver crackles. Eleanor : “Hocus, Pokus...” Opening her eyes, they’ve become 2 bright Pink glowing balls with Silvers stars over them, and her mane of Blonde hair is wildly blowing about in the wind, the electricity continuing to wash around her body. Eleanor : “...If there’s a Cat here, like, WAY show us!!!” The wind gets stronger, the slender figure of Ellie~! nearly being lost amidst the ferocity of all the leaf litter blowing around her as the skies outside turn dark Grey and thunder echoes, hot on the tail of lightening flashes. Eleanor : “....Er, as in, like, me... ELLIE~!” Suddenly, all the kinetic activity stops and the room turns an odd Red colour, everything becoming transparent so the little prep can look through them to see if Mr. Jones is hidden everywhere. Turning to the sofa, her eyes widen and she scurries over, finally seeing something. Eleanor : “Wow, my lipstick... I was wondering where that was!” Reaching down the back of one of the cushions, she grabs the small tube and tucks it into her pocket before the spell wears off and Ellie~! yawns, stretching her body fully, arching her back. Eleanor : “Wow, Magic is like, WAY tiring... No wonder David Blaine always sounds like he’s SO half asleep and stuff.” Skipping out of the living room, not even noticing the open windows and Brown leaves scattered all over the floor, Ellie~! continues on her search. Eleanor : “If I was a Cat.... Where would I be...” Looking in the closet and rummaging around, she contemplates her own question before the answer suddenly comes to her and a finger shoots excitedly up into the air. Eleanor : “I KNOW.... Like, YAY ME!!!” Closing the door, she now thinks again. Eleanor : “I wonder how I get to Cat Heaven Land...” Her attention is then distracted by the front door opening down the hall way and into the apartment steps the PCW CEO Xavier Kannon, looking a bit worse for wear. Xavier : “That was odd.... Suddenly turned from clear skies to a raging wind, darkness and lightning...” Shrugging it off, he tosses his coat onto the stand and suddenly looks down to notices the leaflet he has for the Cat Shelter in his hand, quickly stuffing it into his pocket when he sees Ellie~! in front of him. Eleanor : “DON’T... LIKE... OPEN THE DOOR AND STUFF!!!” As she screeches, Xavier looks around to the door to see it’s wide open as his preppy wife stamps her feet and crosses her arms in a huff. Eleanor : “Mr. Jones is missing and he could of, like, WAY run out!” The bright Green eyes of Xavier glisten and a smile breaks through his mouth... Realising he has the perfect scenario to play along with to hide the fact the evil, hissing furball was taken to a Cat Shelter and dumped. Xavier : “Oh.... Um... I’m sorry my little Poon Puppy...” Trying to sound a mixture of both sorry and innocence, Kannon holds his heart to feign shock. Xavier : “I think I saw your darling, friendly, furry little Kitty run out when I opened the door... Guess he’s gone now, oh well, wanna go back to bed?” Looking at him in annoyance, Ellie~! tilts her head. Eleanor : “Um.... HELLO?!?!? We, like, SO have to go and look for him and stuff!” Before Kannon can even protest, Ellie~! bounds past him and scurries down the stairs, Kannon shaking his head and sighing before closing the door behind him as he follows. Xavier : “We’re in New York, it’s going to take YEARS to search it all!” 2 flights of stairs ahead of him, Ellie~!’s voice is head as she continues down. “I think I’m free until, like, 2018, then I have my facelift booked...” Cursing himself for not just saying he took Mr. Jones to give away, Xavier follows her out through the foyer and onto the street where his wife looks in both directions, still calling for her fluffy White Cat. Eleanor : “Here Kitty!” Looking all around, Kannon shrugs his shoulders and goes to walk back inside. Xavier : “Well, I don’t see him anywhere. Maybe a massage will take your mind of him?” Ellie~!’s ears blank out his words as she starts to wander down the street, making a squeaking noise in-between calls for him. Reluctantly, Kannon turns back and follows her. Eleanor : “Now, where would a Kitty be...” Catching up to her, Xavier’s face looks as apathetic as they come. Xavier : “Eating rotting fish out of a dumpster?” His off the cuff remark is latched onto by Ellie~!, taking hope in it and her pale Blue eyes light up. Eleanor : “That’s, like, SO it.... “ Scurrying to the nearest alley, Xavier doesn’t even have a chance to say he was joking as the excited prep points down the nearest one to a Green dumpster overflowing with Black bin bags. Eleanor : “I WAY bet he’s in that one!” Stopping at the dumpster, she stands on tiptoes to try and look into it. Eleanor : “Here Kitty! Here Mr. Jones!” Walking up behind her, Xavier holds his nose at the stench of the dumpster as Ellie~! looks at the Green plastic bin from all angles then to Xavier. Eleanor : “Like, what are you waiting for?” Kannon stares back at her blankly. Xavier : “I’m not going in there!” Digging her heels in, Ellie~! glares at him. Eleanor : “You, like, SO were the one who let him out and stuff!” Sighing, Xavier shakes his head but still refuses. Xavier : “I’ll give you a leg-up.” Stamping her feet, Ellie~! reluctantly agrees and steps into Xavier’s cupped hand before he raises her up, her little hands stabilising her on the side of the dumpster where she looks in. Eleanor : “Kitty, you there?” Then, coughing is heard from the dumpster and an old man’s bearded head emerges from the bags and rotting waste, looking startled. “Huh... Wha?” Ellie~!’s face turns sour. Eleanor : “Ewwwwwwwwwwwwwwwwwwwwzies!” The startled tramp looks to her, still dazed from being woken up. “You? What.... Er...*COUGH*...” With the curious look on her face, the little prep studies him, seeing he has thick White hair and beard. Eleanor : “Mr. Jones?” Taking the time to notice how well turned out Ellie~! is, and the huge Diamond ring on her finger, the Tramp decides this might be a good one to play along with.” “*COUGH*.... Yeah....” Her faces, screws up as she calls for Xavier to lower her, leaving the confused Tramp in his dumpster. A disappointed look on her face, Ellie~! lowers her head and skulks off in Xavier’s arms as our shot fades out to Black. “Mr. Jones grew all icky...” ---- View Xavier Kannon's Biography
