The Brtitish Navy Save Some Scientological Ass...

2 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2/3/02 The world has been done a great favour. The 5 male members of the reality TV show’s island that Xavier and Ellie~! were castaway on have been slaughtered by the island’s native inhabitants, upset at the increasing number of people coming to their home. However Xavier must explain to the diving knife wielding remaining female members of the island just how he came to be standing over the hacked up remains of their friends. Add to this that he still needs to get to St Louis by tomorrow night in order to make the Main Event at March Madness, and things are looking rather troubled at this moment in time. We open up on Xavier and Eleanor stepping cautiously into the Red tinted surf, retreating away from the female members of the reality TV who are walking through the remains of their fallen comrades and brandishing their diving knives, ready to extract their own brand of justice. Ellie~! holds her hands up, trying to reason with them. Eleanor : “Um.....Like...Hello? What he said SO could have happened! I mean, is the thought that there is a band of tribal warriors that hate you on this island so hard to believe?” Eleanor is wearing the shiny Dark Blue bikini and see through wrap that the island girls gave her before the trouble started and her long Brown wavy hair with subtle Blond highlights is loosely tied back, looking in far to good a condition for someone who has gone through what Ellie~! has recently. The girls ignore her words and keep advancing as Eleanor whimpers and hides behind Xavier’s right shoulder. Xavier : “Nice try my little Shag Squirrel but for some reason I don’t think they believe me!” Xavi is topless, showing off his toned but not bulky physique and a pair of his Black boot-cut jeans with Green stitching and Purple embroidery down the left leg to his bare feet. The Scientologist then turns to the approaching hoard of bikini clad armed women. Xavier : “Look....Women, I know that being stuck on the island has probably made you a little cranky but do you see any weapons on me? What did I hack them up with? Harsh language? Now there is no need for this, all you need to do is find some way of getting me to St Louis by tomorrow evening and then I’ll be off the island and we’ll all be happy....Won’t we?” The 1st couple of Scientology see that their words are not working and are being forced further into the sea when suddenly a loud whistling sound is heard. Eleanor : “What’s that?” Xavier : “I don’t know but for some reason I have this strange desire to run further into the sea and get down!” Eleanor : “Er.....What-EVER!” They turn and start to bound into the sea and leap forwards, diving into the shallow water as 2 huge explosions are heard on the beach and bits of sand and human start to rain into the water above and around them. As the splashes around them die down Xavier pokes his head up above the water and looks to the beach where 2 overlapping craters are all of what remains of the islands inhabitants. Xavier : “Whoa.....Looks like that running and ducking thing was a good idea.....Thanks l Ron, nice bit of intervention there buddy.” Eleanor rises behind him and pulls the sodden wrap off her and looks to the beach where smoke is rising up from the scorched sand. Eleanor : “Shame....I kinda liked those girls...” The Scientologist turns to his valet in disbelief. Xavier : “What? They were armed with knives and were trying to kill me for something I didn’t do!” The prep shrugs. Eleanor : “Yah, but still.....” The 1st couple of Scientology start their walk back to the beach, noticing he water of the surf is a little warmer than before. As they reach the edges of the craters a voice over a loud speaker is heard coming from the sea. Voice : “THIS IS THE HMS HALESWORTH. RAISE YOUR HANDS AND AWAIT AN APPROACH!” They turn slowly on the spot, looking to each other during their rotation and as they look out to the ocean to see a Destroyer anchored as close as it can get to the island and a smaller boat speeding towards the island carrying what looks to be 4 marines. Xavier : “As grateful as I am for them blowing stuff up that was trying to kill us, forgive me if I say....Huh?” Eleanor : “Wow, it’s like Saving Private Ryan....Except we aren’t in France....And there are a lot less explosions.....And I don’t hear machine gun fire, come to think of it....It’s a little different....What does HMS mean?” The launch craft hits solid land and the 4 marines leap out and surround the couple, their weapons cocked and ready as one of them lowers his weapon and announces himself, the other 3 covering him. Man : “Commander Barker, her majesty’s Royal navy....State your name, rank and number.” The Scientologist looks more than confused and tries to think up his answer, not sure now to respond. Xavier : “Um......Xavier Kannon....Rank #1 in Cruiserweight Division....Er....Number 15 entrant in the March Madness Battle Royal?” Commander : “Ah, an American huh? Tell me, what are your lot doing out here during war time? Contemplating another late entry?” The Scientologist cocks his head, shocked at what the Commander just said. Xavier : “War?” The British soldiers laugh heartily at the words of the seemingly naive American. Commander : “Haha, typical American, haven’t you heard of a little thing that’s been going on for over 20 years called the Falklands War? We have been on deep patrol for the last 20 and now w are ready to join back in the fight. We’ve had all the enemy pranks from the Argies, messages pretending to be British telling us it’s over, false news broadcasts, pretending that we have been stripped of our rank of insubordination....But we didn’t fall for their tricks, we will take the Falklands back!” Xavier and Ellie~! look to each other, raising their eyebrows at the mans revelation and whispering to each other. Eleanor : “Didn’t that end like over 20 years ago?” Xavier : “Um.....Yeah.....It did!” Suddenly, thinking of how he needs to be back to America desperately, how he has a shot at the World Title, how since Wednesday he has been running, chasing and being threatened....he then has an idea. Xavier : “Yeah, I know your war.....Those dam Argen-.....Argies, if you hadn’t have bombed them we would have been goners.” He gets the attention of the other marines and the other 3 now lower their weapons to here what the American has to say. Xavier : “....I’ll let you into a little secret....We are not what you would call Civvies, we are on a special reconnaissance mission with the US Army to decide whether or not we should intervene in this conflict on your side to try and end this long struggle....But we need to get back to America by tomorrow morning to give them our answer.” At hearing he is also in the services the men take a much more relaxed attitude towards them. Commander : “Army hey? What division?” Xavier : “Um...I’m not allowed to say.....Only a few people even know I’m here and I was never meant to be seen by anyone, so if you don’t mind, I’d like to keep it hush hush and get back to America so we can help get your island back....Any chance of a lift?” The 4 marines look to each other and shrug before nodding, agreeing to let the pair use their vessel to get word back to America. Commander : “Sure, come aboard the Halesworth with us....We’ll get you home soldier.” Xavier : “Excellent Comrade.....Er...but not in a Russian way, you do know the cold war is over don’t you?” The 4 marines, Xavier, and the bikini clad Eleanor walk out into the sea and hop into the launch vessel. Commander : “Yeah, we heard that one....We are soldiers so we do know when wars end you crazy American!” Xavier : “Haha...Yeah....I’m crazy!” The sound of the motor starting up blocks out any other sound as it speeds off towards the Destroyer and the shot fades to Black.
