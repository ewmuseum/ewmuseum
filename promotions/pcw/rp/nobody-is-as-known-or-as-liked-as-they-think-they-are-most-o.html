Nobody is as known or as liked as they think they are. Most of you, especially in PCW, are just too ignorant to realize it.

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Sometime this week Dear Journal,“Oh my god! You’re, like, my favorite wrestler! I just LOVE you!” I don’t know how many times I’ve heard that from other people. And if you listen to every single wrestler in the wrestling industry, they’ll claim the same thing. But the difference between them and me is one simple thing. While I hear it at a wrestling arena, they supposedly hear it from pretty much any random person on the street. They just walk up to anyone and are, *gasp*, magically recognized. You’d think that any worthless wrestler was the most popular person in the world. I’ve been around long enough and am smart enough to know that is definitely not the case. Seriously, every wrestler can pretty much walk up to anyone and that person would instantly recognize them. And, surprise, it just so happens that they are the most favorite wrestler of that same person. Happens all the time. Give me a break. The cold, hard fact is that people just don’t give a shit about wrestling. Everyone in the world is not a wrestling fan. Hell, not even a quarter of them are wrestling fans. Out of all the people in the world, only 10%, if that, are actual wrestling fans. From that 10%, you have to realize that there are hundreds of different promotions out there. Be them big organizations or small independents, they are all out there. It is humanly impossible to follow every single company in the known world. So, out of the people left, only a small percentage, maybe 2 or 3% at the most, follow your respective company. Of those people, at least half of them follow at least one other company. Only about 25 to 30% follow your certain wrestling organization. Of those remaining people, a good bit of them are sheep and do what they’re told to do. I’d say over half of the “wrestling fans” are sheep. So they are unaware of who they actually like. If you’re a babyface, they love you. If you’re a heel, they hate you. And if you change your alignment, they change with you. So those people don’t know how to make up their mind in relation to wrestling and bear no significance. That leaves a small amount of people who religiously follow your individual wrestling promotion and know what they like and don’t like. Take into account that some of those people work for the company itself and that doesn’t leave a huge chunk of people. So, how is it that every single time a wrestler approaches a bystander on the street, they just happen to be a huge wrestling fan and love that particular wrestler? All sounds like a load to crap to me. Either it’s planted fans, ignorant wrestlers, or in PCW’s case… both. I’ve been away from PCW for a while, but I know that nothing has changed. I might not know the people that well, but I do know that they have an extremely high level of ignorance. From top to bottom, they’re all ignorant little punks. To prove my point on the fans and how all these stories sound like a load of crap, I went to the streets. Since I’ve been away from the PCW game for a while, I had to gain some knowledge on the guys in the company somehow. And if you listen to them, they all try making it seem like they’re hot shit. So I hit the streets and came out with about what I expected. Out of 100 random people I walked up to, three of them actually could tell me about PCW. And, honestly, that’s more that I expected. Out of those three people, one of them mistook me for a PCW wrestler in which I took great offense and ended it there. So, with just two people left, one of them said what I had figured one would say. They supported the likes of Rent-a-Hero and thought that Xavier Kannon was the biggest asshole in the company. And while I agree with his assessment on Kannon, he sounded like a total sheep. I ignored him after that. As for the last guy, he was the only smart fan I came across. He had his own opinions on people and things. He knew about the European title, the T&A title, the faked death of Judas Steele and how Alan Kriegman made a lame attempt at copying it, and just about everything else I’d have expected. So I used this guy to find out about the likes of Dante Jackson, Gaz Green, Jamie Celt, and all the other newbies. The guy was very helpful, and while I still don’t give two fucks about the people in PCW, at least I’m knowledgeable on them. As for his favorite wrestler, he said El Diablo and said that I’d be nowhere without Amanda (and he’s right). Of course, if I went out and said that I walked up to a random person and they said I wasn’t their favorite wrestler, nobody would believe me. *sigh*, it’s a shame wrestlers are too ignorant and self-centered to realize the truth. And truth is that they’re not as liked and/or known as they think. Oh well. All I can do is hope to beat some sense into them at Alliances. It’s all I can ask for. K-Dub
