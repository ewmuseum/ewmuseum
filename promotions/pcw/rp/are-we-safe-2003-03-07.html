Are We Safe?

7 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/7/03 There Brianna sat. She was scrunched up in a chair right next to the window, with her eyes transfixed on the world on the other side of the glass. At the moment all other things seemed irrelevant as watching the outside was all Brianna cared for. With every person that walked past, her eyes followed full of wonder. With all the crap that goes on in the world, how can we know we're safe? I mean that guy across the street could be a mass murderer and we would probably never know.... The man she is referring to is finely dressed in a golf shirt and khaki pants with a cell phone glued to his ear. Sure he may look innocent, and a model American citizen, but can we be sure? Can we be sure that he's not going to just go postal? Like they always say, looks can be deceiving. Her mind continues on a rampage pondering many thoughts at once. But why? Why is sitting in front of the window so important that she would let the phone continue to ring in the hall, and miss an inventors convention? To find that out you would have to revisit earlier in the day.... *The scene cuts and turns pitch black for a few seconds before it reopens with 'Earlier Today' flashing.* Brianna and Milo are in her car traveling down the roads. 'Raindrops keep falling on my head...nanananananana.' Brianna sings as she, not really knowing the words. 'So where are we going today?' Milo asks while checking out the new issue of Vogue. 'I don't know, where do you want to go?' Brianna asks as she turns. Milo takes a moment to ponder this before an idea pops into his head. 'How about that new restaurant? You know that French one that was all over the news? I hear it's good.' 'Le Peu Mignon? (The Little Ducky)' 'Yeah that's it.' 'Sounds good to me.' She makes another turn just before the light turns beat red. They drive on for several more minutes before they pull up to the restaurant which is littered with people. People are obviously packed in like sardines because the line stretches for a block. Milo looks a bit down about the line slamming his magazine shut and making a huffing noise like a little kid throwing a tantrum. 'Well it looks like no little ducky for us.' Brianna says as she starts to drive off. 'I guess so.' Milo spits back reopening the magazine not really paying attention to it. 'Hey while we're down here, would you mind if I stopped off at the bank? I need to make a deposit.' 'Feel free.' he says as he starts staring out the window. Just then his eyes light up. 'Drop me off!' 'Why?' 'Half off sale on Hawaiian shirts at Roderick's!' Brianna pulls the car over realizing how important this is to him. He gets out and throws his head back in one time to tell her to pick him up in an hour. He shuts the door and rushes to the store like a giddy school girl. As he does this Brianna drives off humming a tune to a song. 'Hmmm...hmm....hmmmmmmm....hm...hm...hmm.' After several more minutes she makes it to the FirstStar Bank and pulls into the parking lot. She gets out of the car and walks into the bank which only has about seven or eight people inside total. She walks up to an open teller and is about to give her a deposit slip when a man wearing a ski mask rushes into the bank with a gun pointed into the air. Everyone scrambles all over scared to death by the sight. Brianna has rushed over to the side. The man walks up to the teller she was just at as he waves his gun around threatening anyone who try to come near him. 'Give me all the money you got.' He says in a gruff voice. The teller complies shaking heavily as she empties out her station and puts it in the bag he holds out. As she puts it in a courageous man charges at the robber and gets a shot in the midsection for all of his troubles. His wife sits over in the corner crying her eyes out too afraid to tend to her husband. Feeling no mercy the robber looks down at the man who is now fighting for his life and shoots him once more, this one fatal. 'I am not afraid to shoot you all! Next one who charges ends up like this fool.' His wife looks longingly at her husbands unmoving body and rage fills her all over. She too rushes over like her husband and manages to slap the man hard before getting shot. He hears cop cars coming and takes the money and runs. He tries to get down the street but is tackled to the street, his robbery spoiled. Inside Brianna is the first to rush over to the people, and finds that they aren't breathing any longer. A sense of rage and fear hovers over her know. The scene turns to black and switches back over to her in the chair by the window. Today I witnessed the murder of two innocent people and it disturbs me greatly. How can we all be so sure we’re safe? How can we make sure that our families are protected? She continues to talk but the scene slowly fades out. With this on her mind, her match with Makonie just doesn’t seem that important.
