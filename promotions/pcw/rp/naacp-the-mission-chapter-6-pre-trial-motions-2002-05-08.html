NAACP- The Mission [Chapter. 6 Pre-Trial Motions]

8 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 05/08/02 they were more or less perverts. Well, at least they were to be classified as peeping Toms. The two men stood from their large hotel with binoculars, staring at the woman in the hotel across them changing. The two men were accomplished FBI spies; they had both gotten out of the business at the same time. The business they did now was just a bit tougher, studying potential jurors, getting their history, and making sure that they chose the jurors right for the trial that could possibly drag on for about a month or so. Great. She's fully clothed. FLASH! FLASH! Two pictures, good enough quality. This same routine had been going on for about a good thirty hours, in total. They were being paid handsomely by a man they knew all too well, Jake Jackson. They needed the exact right jurors for this trial; this was a trial of epic proportions. Jordan X Shabazz, PCW Wrestler, sues three different companies, for money in excess of $50 Million. They were finally complete, all potential jurors have had their background checks completed, and they've all had pictures taken of them...without their knowing of course. ********************Meanwhile, on the other side of town, a bunch of paralegals and groups of jury analysts studied the slides of pictures, and some papers on which the jurors were asked to answer a few questions. They had all been overworked they were all tired. Beer bottles, empty coffee cups, Twinkies, soda cans, they were all littered around the small room known as the 'War Room'. They had been in the room for hours, waiting, just waiting for the opportunity when Jake Jackson would come in and tell them they could all go home to their loving wives and their families. All of them feared Jake though. Jake Jackson ruled with an Iron Fist, a man who had single-handedly brought his small firm in downtown Toronto, Canada, that only had two business partners, to a great firm that now employs over 300. Hard work, dedication, not caring about having a family, and not caring about sleep. Jake ran everything during the pre-trial motions. This suit was huge not necessarily for him, but more so for Jordan X Shabazz. It's all Jake could do, payback Jordan for all the years the two men were friends, for all the years Jordan allowed Jake to fulfill his dream of becoming somehow involved in the sport of professional wrestling. Everybody in the room and the surrounding area had something else on their minds... All except Jake Jackson. 'BOOM~!' The door burst open with an angry Jake Jackson walking through. The floor crunched under his weight. His breath smelled of stale coffee and his hair was matted. It showed that he too had been hard at work trying to make his case seem legitimate. He looks at the screen that all 40 jury analysts are looking at he smiles. He knows their tired and afraid he'll tell them to work harder and for longer hours, but God Damn it he was paying them good money, and Jake would be damned if he didn't work them within an inch of their professional careers. The suit Jake wore was old, but it looked new. A greenish brown color that Jake wore when in a good mood. His tie was loosened showing his somewhat relaxed demeanor. The silence in the room was deafening, everybody afraid to speak. And then... 'You guys are tired I assume?' Jake asks in a nonchalant manner. Almost as if he's thumbing his nose at the jury analysts. Everybody in the room nods and murmurs something about wanting to go home to see their family. 'I assume I shall let you go...' Jake Jackson begins. 'Sometime after the Pay Per View,' he finishes, crushing the hopes of the entire room. Jake Jackson takes a seat in one of the many brown leather chairs that adorn the room filled wall to wall with legal books and such. 'Have you profiled all the potential jurors?' Jake asked nobody in particular. 'Yes sir. We are going over them again, this is our last one,' a faceless paralegal jury analyst answers. Jake Jackson was not only a mean, young, cocky, abrasive lawyer, but he was a dirty lawyer. He played very dirty. It was never below Jake's standards to bribe a juror or threaten them with physical violence to their families to get a vote. 'Excellent,' Jake says while skimming some of the files they've got on the jurors. 'This trial should be a piece of cake.' ****************************** Meanwhile in the dirty south of Dallas, Texas... Jordan X Shabazz sat in his home. Watching an old wrestling tape, this wasn't no regular wrestling tape, this was from his very last match in the New True Internet Championship Wrestling organization. He stops the tape, and he takes a few moments to type in his online journal. Hmmm.... As Supremacy nears, I think more and more about my past with Mark Makonie. We have developed quite the violent feud... It hasn't yet reached the violence and mayhem that we created in the NTICW, but at least people give a damn about us...Or me in particular. While in the NTICW, no matter what we did, it seemed as if we just weren't getting noticed, no matter how many awards those NTICW website bastards gave me, it just didn't feel as if I was up to par with some of the great wrestlers out there. Wrestlers with not nearly as much talent as Makonie and me were getting press... But that doesn't matter much to me anymore, finally my chance to step into the ring with Makonie to prove that I can defeat the racist... To prove that the black man is ten times superior to the white man. To prove that those bastards should be OUR SLAVES. Shoot, this match ain't just for the Number One Contendership, this match ain't just for bragging rights... This match is for the entire African American race... Jordan turns back on his television as the match between him and Mark Makonie intensifies, Jordan shuts off his television…Yet again. “I got to realize that watching tapes of our past matches isn’t going to help me… I got to realize that the only thing that can help me now… Is the man above,” Jordan says to himself while looking up into the heavens. “Oh God of Crunk, may you please help me this weekend when I step into the ring yet again… This isn’t any regular match, oh no it isn’t G, this match is something I’ve got to win… Please help me.” “PERHAPS I WILL!” comes a booming reply from somewhere. “What the f^ck?” Jordan says aloud. “Ah hell, I can’t hide, the lights are still on,” says a saddened Rush Mason who walks out of Jordan’s kitchen with some greasy KFC, a large juicy watermelon, a can of Crunkola~! and a basketball. “How the hell did you get in here?” “Bah~! I’m practicing so I can be incognegro for the future. You never know when somebody’s going to try and creep on a playa right?” Rush says while sitting down beside Jordan and looking over his shoulder. Rush Mason goes into his back pocket of his baggy fake Phat Farm jeans and he pulls out a tattered bible. It’s obviously a bible of the Crunk, because there’s a shot of the Black Like Tar character on the front. “Whoop there it is!” Rush exclaims excitedly. “The first ever Bible of The Crunk. Duke’s read it, Jason’s read it, and Tom’s read it. Now it’s your turn to read a few passages.” Rush says while stuffing his face with greasy chicken, and wet watermelon, while washing it down with a cup of the refreshing Crunkola. Jordan holds the Bible high above his head. He opens the sticky pages to find a wrinkled picture of Halle Berry inside, Jordan looks at Rush who quickly snatches the picture out of the bible. “How the hell did that get out of my room?” Rush asks. “Sorry about that, also, notice the stickiness on those pages…That’s from… Root Beer, yeah…Root beer.” Jordan shoots Rush one of those ‘You fake OJ Simpson look.’ Jordan changes the page and begins reading from the Bible of the Crunk. “And so it was said, white women would live the rest of their ages with flat bottoms, and no dancing abilities. Rush Mason: 4:98.” Jordan says aloud. “HALLELUJAH!” Rush screamed while spitting greasy chicken everywhere. Jordan drops the bible to the ground and he stands up. He looks at Rush and nods. “It’s time dawg.” “Time for what?” “It’s time we go downtown to meet with Jake, and the jury consultants, I’ve got to get ready to be on the stand.” “Why the hell do I have to go Dark master Flex?” “Because I’m sure that they’re going to send out their cracka agents to follow me, and try to catch me saying something that I shouldn’t say. Like that I’m just doing this to make a quick buck and there’s no racism in any of these cases.” “So you’re afraid of the crackas?” “Like a white woman would be afraid of OJ.” Fade.
