On the road to recovery (Part 1)

24 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: September 24, 2002 A very simple Motel 6 room nearby the town of Davenport is the beginning of our return to reality. In room 206, we see Eddie sitting in a chair watching the television wearing his standard street gear, and we see Spider going through some tedious paperwork, he’s wearing a pair of jeans, and his standard CC-F’N-W t-shirt. Eddie, clutching the remote tautly, is surfing through the 570 channels that are offered. Unfortunately, there is nothing on. Dicks…|click|…penis…|click|…pussy…|click|… When he clicks to the next channel, the most horrifying sight, and sound is heard. A sound containing a mix of all the audible frequencies thrown over the range of the frequency band, more commonly known as white noise. Spider’s eyes bug out as the noise pierces the calm and order that was there before. “CHANGE THE FUCKING CHANNEL! IT’S DRIVING ME MAD, MAN!” Spider yells over the white noise. Eddie hits the mute button on the remote, and turns to Spider. “Look man, I don’t need you to operate a television…” “But you need me to manage your wrestling career. Just shut up, and find a porno to watch or something, as long as I’m in the bar downstairs as you do the extremely nasty” Spider says with no shame. “Ugh, you are a nasty asshole. Anyway, have you figured out what’s wrong with me yet? Why I’m not winning?” Eddie requests. “Eddie, you have to get a better regime going. I mean, you can’t just sit there, watching the idiot box, and think you’ll win every match. You need to get a better training regiment, and better eating regiment, and a WAY better sparring regiment, I see so many things wrong right now…” “Yeah, and how do you plan on enforcing it?” Eddie enquires. “Simple. I have this” Spider says holding up a taser gun. “Ooh, I am so scared of something that requires D Batteries to run. Get a real weapon and come back to me later” Eddie says as he continues to channel surf. Spider takes the weapon points at Eddie and hits the trigger. The two wires shoot out of the black box and attach themselves to Eddie’s skin. The 50,000 volt current surges through Eddie’s body causing him to start to spasm. Eddie returns the favour by throwing his remote at Spider. The remote strikes Spider in the head, shattering into several pieces. Spider drops the weapon as he clutches his head. “You dirty mother-fucker!” Spider Exclaims. “Nervous Twitch. If you want to get me motivated, you’ll have to think of a different way to do it” Eddie Explains. “If you never want to win another match again, fine, since you don’t want to get motivated to my plans, then you know what, sit there, and rot. I simply don’t care anymore! I’m heading to the bar for Karaoke. If you want to get motivated, get your ass out of that fucking chair, and get to the gym! Otherwise, if you want to be WrestlingTattler Corp’s jobber bitch, sit there and don’t do anything. This is not like Bounty Hunting, where your victims are hapless civilians who are unsuspecting, and aren’t ready for a fight. This is pro wrestling, and it is a cutthroat industry. Notice that a lot of people are getting ahead of you…” Spider says as he concludes his tyraid. Eddie sits in the chair and ponders. “I wonder why that is!” Spider says as he leaves the room. Eddie continues to sit in the chair and deliberates his options. Yeah right, it’s MY problem. Who the hell does he think he is? He’s acting like he’s some sort of relative of mine. What a fucking loser. However…He might be right. I have to find a way to turn myself around…but there has to be another way than that asshole’s. Hmm…must think… Eddie continues to sit in the chair and deliberates his options long into the night…
