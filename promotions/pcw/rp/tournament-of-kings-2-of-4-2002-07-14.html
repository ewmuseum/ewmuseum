Tournament of Kings...(2 of 4)

14 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7-14-2002 (We find ourselves thrust back through the confines of time once more. The shot opens with a breathtaking view of a sunrise over a row of many multicolored tents. The retainers are already awake, however, and some are seen preparing food over open fires, others are walking fine horses. A few minstrels idly strum whatever instrument is their calling. The camera shot pans around the scene and comes to rest on the tent bearing the Pendragon Sigil, a golden crowned dragon on blue. The flaps are open and a flickering light reaches the day. The shot approaches the tent and enters the opening. The tent is empty, except for the bed and table, and the candle upon said table. The back flap is open and the camera moves on through. As the shot approaches the flap, we are met with a sound.... WHACK!!...............WHACK!.......WHACK! WHACK!........ Undeterred, the camera moves into the dawning sunlight. Behind the tent, a giant man in golden armor practices the sword with an even larger man who wears black armor. Their weapons are not swords of steel, merely bamboo rods. The practice flows almost like a dance. Venneman Pendragon, in gold, jumps in to attack and instantly jumps out to defend. His golden armor glittering as he does. The man in black meets each attack from Pendragon with a defense, and immediately launches into a counter attack...Neither man seriously gains an advantage. Suddenly, the man in black launches into a furious attack. Pendragon is forced back. After ten steps he holds his ground and the two look at each other across their locked swords...) Venneman: You've been practicing.... Man in Black: Of course...Didn't think you would always have the better of me, did ya Venny? (With that, Pendragon launches into a furious attack, swinging his 'sword with both hands. Each blow moves the larger man back until his back is against the tent of Pendragon's Hostler. Pendragon feints with a high strike and the Man in Black moves to counter when suddenly Pendragon goes low and trips the larger man. He brings the bamboo down on the shoulder of the man.... WHACK!!! And that is that. The Man in Black sits up and shakes his head. Pendragon drops his 'sword' and puts his hand out to his fallen friend. He pulls the man up and pats him on the back. Pendragon is all smiles, and his good natured jibing is hard to resist...) Venneman: Roland...I'll always have the better...Know why? (The man in black is actually Roland Stern...A former wrestler, he now does whatever he feels like with the wealth his short career afforded him. Right now, he is in the tournament...) Roland: Why is that, Venny? Venneman: Because I am me, and you are you....Everyone knows I own you in whatever combat we happen upon...Remember last years Jousting championships? (Roland rubs the side of his head and smiles...) Roland: I've been trying to forget...Those head shots are never any fun... (Pendragon's minstrel who has been standing silent beside the tent this whole time suddenly begins to strum his Lyre. He pours out a melody, interrupting the good-natured Stern...) Edward: Alas good Roland has fallen...But last year he was sure squallin...for he's no felon but he lost his melon... (Roland gives an angry look to the minstrel who instantly stops the song. Roland then smiles and shrugs...) Roland: Too bad I didn't get to face you in the finals...Would have been a helluva battle... Venneman: You were a pushover...It was all too easy! Roland: Anyhow, Venny...you better go...You have Lord Linden at swords in 10 minutes.... Venneman: Aye, I know...I just felt like catchin up with one of my only friends...Not everyday I get to see you Stern. Roland: I'll be watchin...You just look out for me when the time comes... (The scene switches to a small arena like setup...There are bleachers set up on three sides of a packed dirt circle. The bleachers are packed with people wearing, you guessed it...Medieval garb...A hawker walks between the bleachers selling turkey legs, and a booth to the side sells mugs of ale...This booth has a fairly long line. Ale is popular...And this ale is stout...The way it used to be. Not the watered down crap we have now...Anyways. In the center of the dirt circle, a man looks at the crowd...Dressed in bright red, he looks very bright in the drab surroundings. On his chest is emblazoned a sigil...Crossed swords and a Knights helmet. This man must be the shouter for Lord Linden...) Linden Shouter: Ladies and gentleman....Prepare for the battle...The man you are about to see is no stranger to carnage...he is like the devil incarnate in the fight. Unrivalled in swordplay...unmatched in horseman ship...Unpassed in virtue...I give you...LORD LINDEN!!!! (A few fans cheer madly as Lord Linden steps to the left of the circle. His shouter bows and they cheer more. The shouter leaves and Venneman's shouter, Benjamin, steps to the center of the circle. He shakes his head, slightly amused by Linden's entourage. He starts to speak in a voice that carries well for one so obviously full of humor...) Benjamin: Ladies...Gentlemen. How can anyone compare with the Virtuous Lord Linden? I am here to praise the man who is above petty strife. I have come to lie to rest the rumors that perfection has died in this cold...Cold...World. How often is it that we are able to see true art in action? How often are we given a look at a true champion? My friends...I do like to talk, but I do not speak to hear myself. I speak to give praise to the master of all he surveys...The Conveyer of Carnage...The Tempest of Torment; ladies and gents, he is the room with a view...your champion...I give you, Lord Venneman....The Man, The MYTH...the LEGEND!!!! Pendragon!!!! (Pendragon steps to the other side of the circle. The crowd goes nuts and stands for the occasion. Pendragon points his sword at the masses and then at his opponent. He holds the hilt before his face and whispers a quiet oath. He then enters the circle and waits for Linden. Linden, dressed in red armor is quick to oblige. He drops the visor on his helmet, draws his sword and leaps into the fray. His plan is a constant attack...Venneman goes helmetless in his golden armor, apparently unimpressed by Lord Linden's ability with the sword. He catches the first strike from Linden with a one armed parry. He then launches into an attack of his own that sends the lesser lord scrambling to keep the point of Excalibur from his armor. With a smirk, Venneman stops the onslaught and returns to his side. Linden regroups with a hasty look at Pendragon. He comes in a tad more warily this time. He attempts an underhanded thrust which Venneman easily deflects. Venneman then smiles at his foe and waves him on once more. The man, enraged by the subtle taunt, charges in full bore...Venneman sidesteps and kicks the charging fellow in the ass. Linden staggers and falls on his face. He rolls around, much like a turtle. Venneman takes a small bow to the audience, who are now laughing hysterically as Linden is unable to find his footing...) Benjamin: Friends!!! In grander times, this is the part where Lord Pendragon would show mercy to the embarrassed and end this charade that Linden calls a fight...Today though, I do not think that is the case. My lord wishes an example to be made...If this fellow has made it to the final of the swords, Venneman wishes to show the masses how outmatched Linden truly is.... (With that, Pendragon lends a hand to the struggling Linden and even assists in wiping the dirt from him. Linden, enraged, takes a hack at Pendragon. Pendragon deflects the death blow with his mailed arm and whacks Linden on the helmet with the side of his sword. He then lifts him one handed and throws him to the ground. He places one foot upon Linden's chest and uses his sword tip to push the helmet from Linden's head. He then points the blade at his neck...) Venneman: Do you yield, good sir Knight? (The crowd hushes as Linden ponders his situation. Finally he lets out a sigh....) Linden: Aye, I yield...T'would be nice if the rest of us could beat you every so often, Lord Pendragon... Venneman: What would the fun in that be? This tourney merely is used as a tool to prove my superiority at everything...Even sports that are not practiced much in today's world...There should be no question to my dominion...PERIOD!!! (Venneman steps away from Linden and turns to the roaring crowd, hands upraised in victory. He smiles a quick smile at the camera as Edward the Minstrel comes up behind him....) Edward: Champion he's once again...You can call him Venneman...Lord Pendragon!!! He rules all....Come and hear, hear my call...Any time, anywhere...Lord Pendragon, Lord the FAIR!!!! (The scene goes black as Edward elaborates on his absurd song....) >>>>>>TO BE CONTINUED
