Date: Season II, Episode 1 ﻿************************************The Snow ShowSeason II, Episode 1“Belly of Lead” (Season Premiere)************************************ Location: Shanghai Hospital - Shanghai, ChinaDate: January 27, 2003Time: 6:45 pm “There’s blood in my eyes,” Snow managed to choke to the Chinese nurse leaning over hisbedside. “No, Mr. Snow,” she said with a barely noticeable accent. “It only feels that way because of theswelling. Your eyes are fine.” “There’s blood in my eyes!” he repeated more sternly. “It fucking burns!” “It’s not blood, Mr. Snow,” the nurse answered, just as sternly. Without another look at theyoung and beaten man under her care, she turned and made her exit from the tiny hospital room. A blue curtain separated Snow from another patient - a woman, from what he can tell. He couldhear her sob from time to time, though he can’t understand what she says when she speaks. Snow made no sound, though every movement he made aggravated one of hundreds of woundson his tattered body. Women are weak, he thought to himself as he tried his best to remain still. He kept his blackened eyes closed to save him the pain of blinking. Breathing hurt above all else. Air past over all of the tiny gashes on the inside of his throat - he was sure there was still someglass stuck in there, although three separate doctors and a team of nurses had assured himotherwise. His yellow hair was still stained with a mixture of his own blood and Clyde’s. Thetop of his mouth, they told him, would be a painful mess for the next few weeks, but it shouldn’thamper his speech. Snow knew he could deal with the pain. He heard another whimper from the woman on the other side of the curtain. He rolled his eyespainfully beneath their lids. A doctor entered the room and made his way to the other side of the curtain to tend to theunknown woman. Within moments, her sobs became long and painful wails. Snow couldn’thelp but look over at the curtain, where he could see her vague outline by the lamplight. Sharppains burned his throat. He sat up on his elbows when he noticed the nurse re-approaching him. “There’s no blood in your eyes, Mr. Snow,” she said in her trimmed accent with a sigh. “What happened over there?” Snow asked her. Every word sent a shock through everythingabove his shoulders. “What’s wrong with her?” The nurse looked over to the curtain and the colour flushed from her youthful face. It was onlythen that Snow realized how young the nurse actually was. There was always something aboutwomen that wore their hair up that made Snow think they were older than they actually were. Slowly, her brown eyes lowered. “She was pregnant,” the nurse said quietly. It was all she needed to say, especially to Snow. Horrible memories of his own wife’s miscarriage crashed through his mind without a shred ofmercy. All the while he had been clutching his regained PCW Championship beneath the hospital sheets- beneath the gown even. The gold seemed colder now than it had only a moment ago. Now, Snow remembered what real pain was. He pulled the title belt tighter against his stomachand rolled over on his side, facing a blank white wall instead of the blue room-dividing curtain. As he closed his wet eyes, they felt bloody again, and he let out a soft whimper that would reachno audience. *** Location: Snow Residence, New York SuburbsDate: January 30, 2003Time: 5:30 pm Time heals all wounds, especially wounds of the flesh. Other wounds aren’t so easily repaired,but they’re much easier to hide. Snow stepped into his kitchen and dropped his light suitcase on the white tiled floor. At thatvery moment, he saw all three women of the house stampeding toward him. When they caughtsight of him, however, they stopped dead in their tracks. This was supposed to be a champion’swelcome home, but instead, his family wasn’t sure if Snow had won anything at all, from thelook of him. Both of his eyes were blackened, though the swelling was completely gone from them now. Hisface was covered in cuts both large and small, and there were bruises on his throat that weremade by the doctors while they held him down to fish the glass out of his throat. “You look surprised,” Snow said with a genuine smile. “I thought you watched the match?” “We did,” Raven answered, as she stared at her husband. She wanted to cry. “Well, eating glass takes a lot out of a guy,” Snow chuckled, trying to lighten up the moment. His wife hugged him gingerly and together they walked toward the living room. He didn’t evenspare a glance sideways at Krista, nor had he since she had revealed that she was pregnant lastweek. *** Location: Snow Residence, New York City SuburbsDate: January 30, 2003Time: 11:40 pm He knew that he would have to talk to Krista soon enough, but no one in the house waslooking forward to that moment. And as soon as she admitted that Marty Verrelli was thechild’s father, Snow would, of course, have to make every attempt to remove the young man’shead from the rest of his anatomy. He was too tired to do that just yet. Instead, he had settled comfortably onto his sofa with his PCW Championship title belt drapedover his right leg and his peacefully sleeping daughter cradled in his large arms. Her hair wasgetting so long now. It was as yellow as his own. He lost himself in the moment and the darkness of the room, knowing that it was simply thecalm before a vicious oncoming storm.
