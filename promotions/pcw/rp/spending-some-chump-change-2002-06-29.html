Spending some chump change

29 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: June 29, 2002 Tom hit Robert in his arm who was gawking at all of the high paid athletes in the room. He signaled over to Robert to go to the main desk and ask the secretary for more info. The girl at the desk was probably around twenty years old, long blonde hair and very attractive features. She was typing something at the computer when Tom came up to her. Legend Inc.: Hello Ms...? Secretary: [looks up] Oh, more people...I'm Sandra, the acting secretary for ESPN News Media. How can I help you? Legend Inc.: Yes, we're here for the bid. Secretary: [looks at Tom unphased] The bid, sir? Legend Inc.: Yes, the bid. Secretary: I'm sorry, but I'm not sure what you're talking about. There's no bid occurring here. Legend Inc.: [laughs] Look, you don't have to put up this charade. I know about the bid. Secretary: [shakes her head] I honestly have no idea what you're talking about. We're news correspondents - we just gather news here from the local sports teams and relay them back to ESPN headquarters in New York. Legend Inc.: Oh yeah? Then what are all these star athletes doing here? Secretary: There are sports games here all the time. It just so happens that they have made their way to this building at the same time. Legend Inc.: [shocked at the girl's logic] Look I wasn't born yesterday, ok? Just tell me where the bid is! Secretary: [stands up] What bid? I have no clue what you're talking bid! Legend Inc.: [shouting] The bid where athletes try to pay to have themselves on the ESPN Showcase on Sportscenter! Robert Yarney: [looks around and taps Tom on the shoulder] Tom...people are looking over here... Legend Inc.: [calms down] I'm not the police or anything...I'm an agent for a wrestler...I want to bid to have my client's match on the SportsCenter showcase. Secretary: [sits back down quietly] Oh...[begins typing]...your name? Legend Inc.: My name is...Tee Howard...I'm the agent for Legend Inc., Tom Lexian. Secretary: [checks the computer] You are registered to be in this bid. [hands Tom a name tag and number] Wear this and display it at all times. The bid will begin in thirty minutes. Legend Inc.: [takes the name tag and puts it on] Thank you. And...I'm sorry for yelling earlier. Secretary: No problem. We have to be careful around here. Usually we get really well known athletes in here registering...I suppose I didn't recognize you. Legend Inc.: [walks away with Robert ] Well, that was interesting. Robert Yarney: Yeah, you only had the entire room of athletes ready to flee the scene of the crime. They probably thought this was some sting operation. Legend Inc.: Do you know who we should go talk to? Robert Yarney: Who's that? Legend Inc.: Vince McMahon...I have some things I want to discuss with him. Robert Yarney: Why do you want to talk to that prick for? He's just a business man. He doesn't know anything about wrestling. Legend Inc.: Are you sure about that? I mean, he heads the biggest wrestling corporation in the world. That's no small task. Robert Yarney: Well I did say he's a business man - a damn good one at that. Besides, aren't you forgetting about someone? Legend Inc.: Who's that? Robert Yarney: [points over to a man near the door] Him. Legend Inc.: [looks over] Dick Vitale...he's here? Robert Yarney: Yeah, didn't the Joker order you to talk to him before the bidding begins? Legend Inc.: [looks at Robert ] Look, first of all the Joker doesn't order me around. I am an individual with free will. Secondly, if it was so damn important to talk to him why the hell couldn't the Joker tell me? Maybe he just has some grudge against him. Robert Yarney: [thinks about it] Maybe, or maybe not. Legend Inc.: It could be a swerve. I can't take the chance. It couldn't be that important to talk to some ESPN announcer anyways. [begins to walk over by Vince McMahon] Besides, that fellow over there is just standing by himself. Robert Yarney: [shakes his head] Tom.... Legend Inc.: [extends his hand to Vince] Hello there, Vince...it has been a long time. Vince McMahon: [looks at Tom] Who are you? Legend Inc.: Tom....erm, Tee Howard, agent to Tom Lexian aka Legend Inc. I believe we have had dealings before in the past. Vince McMahon: [shakes Tom's hand] I'm not sure if I...wait, what was the name? Legend Inc.: Legend Inc. Vince McMahon: Hmmm, actually I do remember a young man coming into the organization a few years ago with the name Legend. It could have been the same man. Legend Inc.: He was a prospect for your organization. He was actually hand picked by you - or that's how his story goes. Vince McMahon: Well I wouldn't doubt it. I call a lot of wrestlers in to our training facilities - of course, not many of them are able to make the final cut. Legend Inc.: [smiles] Apparently Mr. Lexian wasn't able to make the final cut either. Vince McMahon: [shrugs] Not many do. He was probably a little green to start out. How is his career going now? Legend Inc.: Rather good. He's currently competing in the PCW and he has a match this Sunday for the PCW Continental title. Vince McMahon: [surprised] Wait...are you here for the bidding? Legend Inc.: Yes we are. Vince McMahon: Mr. Lexian wants to have his match on the SportsCenter Showcase? Legend Inc.: That's right. You sound a little surprised. Vince McMahon: Well I should be. I've been trying to get matches on the Showcase for a long time now but usually the higher ups won't go for it. If you were able to manage getting on the bid list, that's rather impressive. Legend Inc.: Maybe they see things in Legend Inc. that you didn't. Vince McMahon: Maybe. Tell Mr. Lexian that he should come down to Connecticut some time and have a talk with me. Maybe we'd be able to work something out one of these days. Legend Inc.: [shakes Mr. McMahon's hand] You'd have to deal with me then, Mr. McMahon. And in that case, it wouldn't be cheap by any means. Vince McMahon: I don't doubt it. I'll see you in the bidding room. Legend Inc.: [nods and walks away] That was quite interesting. Robert Yarney: Did you have to show off to him? Legend Inc.: What do you mean? Robert Yarney: Oh, come off it. You basically rubbed it in his nose that you had a chance to be the first wrestler on SportsCenter and he was just drooling over you. Legend Inc.: Look, I don't have time for this. I have to go to the bathroom. Robert Yarney: [checks his watch] You'd better hurry. The bidding starts in fifteen minutes. * * * Tom ran down to the restroom that was only a few feet away from the main room. He thought about what Robert had said to him about Vince McMahon. Robert was right - as always. He did have something against Vince McMahon. He had something against his insincere smile, that god awful laugh, and that god awful man. He took Tom's dreams and flushed them down the crapper along with his contract. Could Tom blame him? Maybe. Tom was definitely not the caliber wrestler that he is today, that's for sure, but he could have given him a chance. Instead he was thrown out in the cold and left to fend for himself. Thank god he did, otherwise Legend Inc. may not exist today. Tom opened the door to the bathroom and was greeted by a horrible smell. Tom winced at the distinct, and very vile odor that enveloped the room and immediately headed to the stall. Public bathrooms were almost as bad as Akiyama’s wrestling skill.... Tom picked a middle stall since the others were only occupied. He quickly locked the door and sat down. As he did he looked over at the stall next to him. The shoes were Armani - probably some super rich athlete who had to take a dump at the stall next to him. Great. He'd probably sue him if he accidentally pissed on his - oh so expensive - shoes. Tom then looked at the shoes at the stall next to him. It was a weird habit he had - Tom's not a pervert, really! As he gazed down he saw a pair of blue Airwalks attached to a pair of green pants. Uhm, green pants? Legend Inc.: Joker? The Joker: I thought you'd come here eventually. I know you, Lexian. You don't go to the bathroom before you leave the house like you should. Legend Inc.: Wait, how do you know that? The Joker: Never mind that. Just tell me, why did you purposely ignore my directions? Legend Inc.: Look, why don't you answer my questions for once, like why you didn't contact me once I got the money. The Joker: I did contact you. Legend Inc.: The note? You call that contacting me? The Joker: It had all the instructions that you needed to pull off the bid successfully. You didn't need to meet me in person for that. Legend Inc.: Bullshit. The Joker: Let me remind you that I am still in control of this plan. You are merely carrying it out. I would think that if you were serious about being on the SportsCenter ShowCase that you would continue to follow my instructions. Legend Inc.: What difference does talking to Dick Vitale make anyways? The Joker: It doesn't matter anymore. You had your opportunity and you blew it. It's time we moved on. Legend Inc.: Ok, so now what? The Joker: The bid begins in a few minutes. Take your seat and wait for the prompter to begin the bidding. It usually starts around about a hundred grand. Just make sure that no one out bids you. Legend Inc.: You do realize that I only have four million dollars to bid with, right? I mean, what if someone bids above me? The Joker: First of all, you only have two million dollars to bid with. The other two million has to go back to the bank. Secondly, no bid has gone past two million so you should be fine. Legend Inc.: But if someone does...? The Joker: Then you're screwed. Legend Inc.: Great. The Joker: That's all you have to know. Just make sure you don't screw it up, time's running out and Tuesday Night Turmoil is right around the corner. Legend Inc.: [flushes the toilet] Alright, I'm heading out. Wait, Turmoil? What happened to the PPV? The Joker: No time, my friend. We’re already late. It’s time you cut a promo for your match against RAH. Legend Inc.: Dammit. The Joker: Oh..what did you and Mr. McMahon talk about? Legend Inc.: [pauses] .... The Joker: You didn't think I wouldn't notice, did you? Legend Inc.: Well... The Joker: It wasn't a smart move, Lexian. He knows your face. He's going to realize that you are actually Tom Lexian and not Tee Howard. Legend Inc.: What is he going to do? The Joker: How the hell should I know? I'm not psychic. Get out there, you're already late. * * * Tom pulled up his pants and headed out of the bathroom. He ran down the corridor and down to the basement where the bidding was being held. So the Joker was here. He had seen everything and was apparently everywhere. He would undoubtedly be watching the bidding and perhaps flexing his own influence to make sure that it occurred the way he wanted it to. The only question Tom was wondering was what the Joker would do if he actually did fail in getting the Showcase show... Tom looked around the bidding room and spotted Robert sitting in the back row. He was sweating and was already looking pissed off. Robert spotted Tom and signaled him over. Robert Yarney: It's about fucking time, Tom. I've already had to bid a couple of times for you. What took you so long? Legend Inc.: What bid are we up to? Robert Yarney: It's up to five hundred grand already. So, are you going to answer my question? Legend Inc.: What question? Robert Yarney: What the hell took you so long? Legend Inc.: I met up with the Joker in the bathroom. We had a nice conversation while I was taking a dump. Robert Yarney: Oh yeah? Did he make a comment about Dick Vitale? Legend Inc.: Actually he did. He said to forget it because it was too late. I don't know what he meant by it though. Robert Yarney: Are you blind or something? He's right there. Legend Inc.: [looks up] What! Dick Vitale is the prompter!?! Robert Yarney: Apparently he is. Legend Inc.: Oh man...we could have suckered him into our plan. Robert Yarney: I guess that conversation we had with Mr. McMahon wasn't quite worth it, was it? Legend Inc.: [looks over to the other side of the room and sees Mr. McMahon looking at him] I guess he hasn't forgotten about it. Dick Vitale: That's six big ones for the New Yorker baby! Derek Jeter is putting it down for those damn Yanks! Do I hear seventy G's? Anyone? Robert Yarney: Oh shit...damn Derek Jeter bid again. That's the third time already. Legend Inc.: What does he want with the showcase? Robert Yarney: He wants the Showcase for the NY vs Texas game. I guess he wants to show up A-Rod on TV. Dick Vitale: Is that him? It is! Seventy grand for the man known as Tiger Woods, baby! He wants it, and he's going to get it! How about eighty? Come on! Legend Inc.: Tiger Woods is here? Robert Yarney: Yeah, he just flew in from his game at San Jose. Looks like he wants more exposure - like he doesn't have enough already. Legend Inc.: A showcase with golf - that's odd. Robert Yarney: Not as odd as a wrestler who wants the showcase... Legend Inc.: [continues to look over at McMahon] Why does he keep looking over here? Robert Yarney: Who? Legend Inc.: McMahon...he won't stop staring at me. Robert Yarney: Just forget about him. He hasn't even bid yet....[looks over]...Tom, are you paying attention? Legend Inc.: What? Oh yeah... Robert Yarney: Oh yeah? The bid's up to 1.3 million and no one's adding to the bid... Legend Inc.: What! [raises his hand].... Dick Vitale: Going twice....no wait! We have a bid from Mr. Howard, agent to the 'World Renowned' Legend Inc. for 1.4 million! Wrestling is for real! Do I hear 1.5 million? Legend Inc.: Whew...that was close... Robert Yarney: Too close. [looks around the room] No one else is bidding...it's ours.... Dick Vitale: Oh c'mon baby! It's only at 1.4 million! Put out some more and have your game on ESPN Showcase! Going once....! Legend Inc.: Man, do you think that they pay Dick Vitale for this? Robert Yarney: You think he gets a piece of the proceeds for this? Legend Inc.: He must - or maybe he just likes the attention. Dick Vitale: I can't believe no one else wants a piece of the rock! C'mon, represent for yourself! Going twice....! Robert Yarney: I can't believe this....it was too easy.... Legend Inc.: [rubbing his hands] It's ours.... Dick Vitale: Wait! We have a bid! From the godfather of entertainment himself, Vinny Mac! Robert Yarney: What! Legend Inc.: [looks over at McMahon who is now smiling] That son of a....[raises his hand] Dick Vitale: 1.6 million! Unbelievable baby! Do I have more from...? Robert Yarney: It's at 1.6 million...he wouldn't bid above that.... Legend Inc.: He'd better not.... Dick Vitale: 1.7 mill! 1.7 mill! Bring it on, baby! Robert Yarney: I don't believe this shit...who is he going to give the Showcase to?? Legend Inc.: He's not going to win - not like this again....[raises his hand] Dick Vitale: Wow! 1.7 million from team Legend Inc. and.....wait, another bid from Vinny Mac....a double bid! 1.9 mill from the genius! Robert Yarney: Oh my god....1.9 million? Tom, we only have two million to bet....what if he goes over.... Legend Inc.: [staring over at McMahon with a hateful look] He won't....[raises his hand] Dick Vitale: A clean 2.0 million dead presidents! Do I hear anymore offers? C'mon baby! Going once.....going twice....sold to team Legend Inc. for two million smackeroos! Robert Yarney: [stands up and cheers] Alright! We got it! Legend Inc.: He wouldn't go over two million. How would he explain it to his wife... Vince McMahon: [comes over to Tom and drops a picture on the chair] Hello....Tom. Congratulations on your victory. Legend Inc.: In more than one way. Vince McMahon: I suppose. But you haven't heard the last from us. You can bet on that. [walks away] Legend Inc.: [picks up the picture of him and McMahon posing together in a photo which was three years old] Of all the broken promises he's made over the years...I actually believe this one.
