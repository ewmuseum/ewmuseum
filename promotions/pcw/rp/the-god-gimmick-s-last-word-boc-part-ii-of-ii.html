An old wooden infrastructure, square-shaped and fairly small, this gemstone of architectural crap looks as if it could collapse at any given moment. But Joseph doesn’t care, because nearby are his treasures. Piles of strewn-out hay line the floor, while an assortment of exotic farm animals line the walls! “Yes!” Joseph cries out, for you see, this is the happiest moment in all his life. “Oh Joey, it’s so romantic…..” “Here piggy-wiggy.” Winking in the general direction of a small group of hogs, one of the dirtier, fatter ones turns and trots over to Joseph. Gently stroking the animal’s ears, the pig begins to snort in delight. “Hey!” In a teasing voice, “How come you get all the fun?.....” Marry, hands on her hips, turns and nods toward another pair of the beasts. “Betsy, come here, Betsy.” Obediently, a small black and white cow with sagging utters slowly stumbles over to her master. Content, Marry lies down in her bed of straw, curling up with the cow. “Oh yeah, porker, that’s the spot…..” “Uh-oh…..Joey?” He turns to his husband. “My…..my tummy hurts…..” Marry stammers….. (And then, somehow, Jesus comes out of his penis-hole.) IN THE CLOUDS God Gimmick paces impatiently back and forth across the cloud. A deathly skeleton dressed in a darkened cloak of purple, he’s not what the everyday Christian would think of their God as. But you know what? They’re wrong! For this IS the God Gimmick. “Mwahahahahahahaha…….hey, what are you doing here!?” Eyeing the NBA star with a stern look, God Gimmick approaches Charles Barkley. “Uh…..you called for me, sir?” Shaking his head, “Well…..uh…..I guess I did!” Flames burning in his eyes, he points a crooked skeletal finger at Barkley. “But that doesn’t mean I won’t kill you…..and send you back to……MWAHAHAHAHA heaven again!” “Noooooo!” Barkley screams. Lunging forward, God Gimmick raises his hand and spouts forth a flame from his finger. This hell-blazing fireball ~OF DOOM!~ engulfs the poor young African American, burning him to a crisp. Momentarily finding himself back home on Earth, after dying again, his body crumbles and his spirit ends up back in heaven…….at the feet of the God Dimmick. “Hehehee! Just playin’ with ya……” “Damnit now, God! Why do you always have to do that shit to me!” “Ah, calm down Charlie, just a little fun…..besides, it’s not as if you’ve ever done anything bad in your life…..I mean, besides for your pathetic career in the NBA, you’ve enacted no real sins…..so you’ll just end back up here in heaven again……with……with ME! Mwahahaha!” His crown explodes in flames. Rolling his eyes, Barkley steps forward. “So uh boss, what’s the real reason you called me for? I was just playin’ a game of golf with Asa Fountain’s mom, who just yesterday was loved to death by the mailman, and I’d kinda would like to get back…..” God Gimmick nods his head in understanding. “Very well then.” He takes a seat in his dark seat ~OF DOOM~! and sets his dark can ~OF DOOM~! on the floor next to him. Sighing, “Well you see, old Saddam’s back at it again…..” “What?”, Barkley suspiciously eyes his creator, “but I thought that the SEALs killed him…..twice!” “Oh yes, they did, they did……but you know how it goes. Satan, master of evel and all that crap…..” shaking his fist, grumbling, “though I wanted that job….” Shaking himself out of it. “Oh sorry, uh anyway, as I was saying……The Devil has decided to give Saddam another chance…..you know, to be more evil, and so has sent him back in time……to Bethlehem, to…..” ~EVIL GASP OF DOOM~! “destroy baby Jesus!” ~DIDO~! “Noooooo!.....” Pausing a moment, Barkley reaches up and scratches his head. “Who’s that?” And then, thinking a little bit longer. “Oh yeah….gotcha.” “And so” God Gimmick continues on, “in order to prevent such a catastrophic event from occurring, I am sending you, Charles Barkley, defender of all that is holy and ~EVIL~! to save his life…..” Sharply saluting the God Gimmick, “Sir, I understand.” And turning to leave, God Gimmick cries out to him “And remember….he’s my only son…..so” wiping a tear from his eye, “please, for Christ’s sake…..yes literally, Christ’s sake save…...” Teardrop falls to the floor “save my baby….” “Can do Mr. God Gimmick sir, can do.” And then Barkley turns back to face God Gimmick. “But uh sir, one question……” God Gimmick nods. “Uh, how will you get me back to ancient Bethlehem?” Eyes gleaming with flame, God Gimmick raises his hands…. “Oh no! Not this again…..” ENTERING THE TOWN OF BETHLEHEM “Are we there yet?” “Yes.” “Are we there yet?” “Yes…..” “Are we…..there yet?” “YES! Yes we’re there! For Christ’s sake…..” Pulling his hair in anger, the Shaolin monk begins to pluck out the gray strands as his confused followers continues onward. “Wow Kai, you look like shit!” Shepard Marvin Harris notices. Turing back to him, “Yes, yes I do…..now can we please keep moving?” Stopping, and then jogging in-place Abner, Vanilla and Harris look quite ridiculous, kicking up dust in the desert sand. Kai lets out a heavy sigh. Now in the outskirts of Bethlehem, it’s been a long journey (forty-one minutes), and the four travelers are anxious to bed down for the night. Though the town of Bethlehem, next door to their own quiet village was within viewing distance, it’s a miracle that the three young shepards were able to find it at all…..but with the guidance of vigorous Chinese monk, they were. “For the love of God Gimmick, pick up your feet boys, the manger’s only a few mere yards away, and we’ve been awaiting this moment our entire lives!” Shrugging, Abner picks up his foot and coddles it one hand. Looking to each other, Vanilla Skillz and Marvin Harris, just as clueless, imitate their redneck/sheep-herding companion. Kai throws down his staff of ~CHINESE SHOALIN MONK GOODNESS!~, and takes in a deep breath. Slowly counting back from ten, he regains his posture and attempts to address the issue. “No, no, no! That’s not what I meant…..I meant let’s walk faster, that way, are you following me?” He stops to let this settle in their minds. They nod, and so they continue. “We can get there faster.” He stops , and again, they nod. “Okay?” They shake their heads no. “What….?.....” And then, changing their minds, they shake their heads yes. “Ah, forget it…..I’ll never understand you…..” The monk continues onward through the quiet little town of Bethlehem as the three sheep-herders follow on after him, every bit as clueless, and puzzling as ever….. ON THE OTHER SIDE OF TOWN The Smiley Face wiseman, though visually handicapped due to the narrow-sensory range of the two holes poked through the paper bag on his, somehow has managed to take the lead. Racing toward the manger, bottle in hand, the 69th wiseman is in a good position. You see the three wisemen, and one wisewomen, deciding to race to the birth-spot of the Savior, have made a wager: first one to Jesus gets……my (yes me, the narrator’s) ~RESPECT!~ Whooo! But anyway…… At one time forsaking their master, and turning toward Herod for leadership, they betrayed all that was good and holy. But all that has changed now, and they have turned back to God Gimmick’s side. You see, after finding out some vital info. from secret messenger and ~EVIL SIDE!~ insider, Happy Snappy (representing the official drink of PCW, Snapple), the wisemen, and wisewomen, have decided to change their ways. Evidently, as Happy reported, Herod and Satan were in cahoots, on a top-secret plan involving Saddam Hussein. Sent to Bethlehem to assassinate baby Jesus, he would then turn the manger into a plant producing Weapons of ~MASS DESTRUCTION~! And they, the wiseasses, could not allow this. “No”, Smiley Face grumbles to himself, “if Hussein does indeed turn the manger into a production center for Weapons of ~MASS DESTRUCTION!~, I would have to turn my bag upside-down and change my name to ‘Mr. Frowny Face’.” He begins to weep. Soon, the other three are caught up with him. “Heh, take that you ol’ coot!” Whipping out a rope and lassoing Mr. Smiely Face by the neck, wisewomen Tomas jerks it back and sends the poor man flying into the dirt. “Yee-haw!” “Ha! Cheap trick, Tomas!” The End shouts out in protest. “Cheap Trick?” Mordred replies. “I want you to want me…..” He begins to sing under his breath, rushing forward toward the finish line. “Shut up, idiot!” “No…..you shut up!.....Idiot!” Mordred retorts. “Now ya’ll boys back there best be hurryin’ up, see, this ol’ gal ‘bout to whup ya!” Only about a hundred or so yards from the edge of the manger, by a few mere feet, Tomas does indeed have the advantage over the other three now. “So!” Mordred continues on, “Even if you do win, Jesus will like me bestest!” “Nah-uh!” “Yes-huh!” “Nah-uh!” “No? Well then lookie here…..” Whipping out a dirty sock, Mordred flashes it in The End’s face. The End, unimpressed, begins to laugh. “What, that’s supposed to scare me?” “Nah, it’s just my gift that I got for Jesus…..and he’s gonna like it better than your’s too.” Mordred sticks out his tongue. “Yeah right.” Reaching down in his pocket and pulling out a nude photo of Jolene Blalock, he pushes it toward Mordred’s eyes. “Whoa!” Losing his balance , Morder trips over his own feet, landing head first in the sand. “Sucker!” Letting out a sigh, Tomas turns back to End. “So that’s what you really got for our Lord and Savior huh? Real cute…..” “No, I was just screwin’ with him……” He produces a nude photo of Asa Fountain’s sister and winks toward Tomas. “Oh, shit!” The End laughs. “Well that ruins my surprise…..” Pulling out another nude photo of Asa Fountain’s sister, Tomas chucks it on the ground. The End is taken back. “Don’t look so surprised…..” And then reaching down into her back pocket, Tomas pulls out a deck of playing cards, “I guess this’ll have to do.” Mr. Smiley Face and Mordred, caught up by now, empty out their out pockets. “I’m out.” “Same here.” And tossing out two more nude photos of Asa’s sister, they begin to scrummage through their pockets for more gifts. Mordred finally finds a freshly-made Cuban cigar, and Smiley a cheap bottle of liquor. Holding the green bottle tight to his chest, Smiley reaches up and covers the exposed spot on his bag, caused by a rip during the fall. “Playing cards, a cigar and some whiskey……what goes good with that?” Nodding his head in approval of his final thought, The End reaches down and whips out a nude photograph of Ozzy Osbourne…… THE MANGER “It’s…..it’s a boy!” Joseph cries out in happiness as he lifts the child above his head. “A boy……just like his father!” Marry adds in. “Yeah…..and just like his mother…..” “Shh….quiet!” Kai reaches back and jabs Vanilla Skillz in the ribs. “Sorry…..” And so gathering around the newborn, parents, the four shepards, Shoalin monk and all, pay tribute to their Lord and Savior. “Long live the king. Long live the king!” Breaking out in chant, Skillz, Harris and Abner prance around like crazed lunatics. “Enough already, enough!” With his staff, Kai pushes the three men back and ushers them toward the door. “Now the boy needs his rest, so we’d best be on our way…..” “Ahhhh!” A terrible scream of ~DOOM~! erupts from somewhere nearby. And then suddenly, rushing into the manager emerges the four figures of the three wisemen, and the wisewomen, all bearing rather…..odd gifts. “Are we too late!” “Where’s the child?” “Will be receiving birthday cake?” They all turn to face Skillz. “Jesus Skillz, you’re not even in their group!” The Shaolin monk shakes his head in disapproval. “Yeah, if you want cake, you’ll have to wait your turn!” Kai turns and gives Abner a good whack. “I mean……yeah…..somethin’……” But Skillz just shrugs. Ignoring all this, The End steps out in front. “Well, Joseph, Marry, where is he is? Is everything alright? Is he safe?” The two husbands turn and stare at each other confused. “Uh…..I guess….” BOOM “Well then you have guessed wrong! Mwahahahaha!” Suddenly, before their very eyes, the old carcass of Betsy the cow breaks open……and jumps an ~EVIL MAN~! And to everyone’s dismay, with a flaming head of afro-liciousness, and wearing an Iraqi military/disco jumper, it’s none other than Saddam Soul-Sein! “Mwhahahaha! Disco-Allah is here to bring down this manger, fo sho! And when it’s all said n’ done, this place be jivin’ with an arsenal of Weapons of ~MASS DESTRUCTION!~” And then adding, “Now ain’t that the shiznick?” “Oh no! We’re too late”, Tomas covers her face as Saddam produces a pistol from his afro. “You mean”, Abner gulps, “the cake is all gone!?” “Noooooo!” Charles Barkely suddenly appears out of thin air, sporting a cowboy hat with a golden star on it reading ‘God Gimmick’s Deputy’. “The cakes all gone?.....” And then turning and seeing all of the shocked faces around him, the NBA star realizes that he’s not quite in that God-awful predicament……but a similar one at least. And so for the first time, Barkley turns back and notices Soul-Sein. “Saddam Soul-Sein! Hussein’s evil-twin? Man, you’re the evil twin of an evil brother…..and so that must make you…….” GASP “Super ~EVIL!~” “That’s right jive-turkey. I’m evil, I got soul, and I just ‘bout to blow this baby’s head off, yo.” “Hi-ya!” Leaping into the air, Kai darts across the manger and in one swift motion, kicks the gun from his hand. “Oh…….shit!” Quickly reacting, Barkely whips out the ~BASKETBALL OF DOOM~! (barrowed from the God Gimmick), and tosses it at Soul-Sein. Dodging to his left, Soul-Sein narrowly avoids the ~BASKETBALL OF DOOM~!, and getting to his feet, barks out some orders in Jivish-Arabic. As soon as the words leave his mouth, from out from behind the haystack emerges his Muslim allies. UH-OH! Osama, Arafat and The Chef all appear, machine guns in hand. But all of a sudden, before the fight even begins, the entire room erupts in laughter. End’s the first to break it. “Ahahaha!” Not quite able to control himself, “Chef…..my old friend, what do you expect to do……‘cause you suck! Washed up for years now, we’re not afraid of you!” Soul-Sein shrugs and turns back to his contemporary. “It’s true…you haven’t done shit for ever, yo……” and then adding, “word?” The PCW hall-of-famer, head between his hands, replies, “word……” before slowly exiting the barn, sobbing all the while. Shaking it off, Soul-Sein prepares to fight. “Okay, biacth, time fo some business, shizzle?” Saddam readies a new pistol produced from his boots. “Uh……is ‘shizzle’ a type of cake?” Harris asks. “Yeah”, Abner adds. Reaching back, Kai simultaneously connects with backhands on both of his companions, knocking them out cold. Wiping his hands off, Kai replies “Okay, now I’m ready…..” And so they’re off…… Soul-Sein leaps into the air, landing on top of the crib. Covering him, Osama and Arafat take the front line. “Sorry lil’ babe, but I uh……need this manger for…….” Pause for dramatic effect….. “Weapons of ~MASS DESTRUCTION!~, yo?” Arafat and bin Laden grin in approval, high-fiving their master. But it’s all fun n’ games…. Until a redneck gets pissed…… “Yee-haw!” Tomas is the first to strike. Lassoing the gun out of Arafat’s hand, she runs forward and kicks him square in the Allah-Jewels. And falling to his knees, he lets out a volley of curses in Arabic directed toward Tomas. “Hey” Kai snaps, “that’s no way to speak to a lady!” And letting out his own volley of broken Chinese curses, he silences Arafat with a well-placed karate kick to the head. The End, quickly overpowering bin Laden, tosses the gun aside and whirls him back toward Kai. Spinning around in mid-air, Kai connects with Made in China, and sends Osama down to the ground. Evidently, the Muslims are down for the count. “No narrator, that’s ‘fo’ the count”, Soul-Sein comments. Oh…...Sorry….. “You’re next, Soul-Sein!” Barkley cries out. Releasing the basketball at a sub-Jordan speed, it flies across the manger, finally connecting with Saddam’s fro. “Nooooo!” Reeling back in pain, Soul-Sein drops his weapon and falls to his knees. “By Allah, by the Hand of Disco, I’ve lost my Jive…….Noooooo!” And then silence engulfs the manger as he falls down, dead. “Yee-haw!” “Settles that.” “Yep…” Abner grumbles, his mouth full of cake. Kai rolls his eyes. “Where’d you get that?” Abner shrugs. But to their surprise, it’s not over yet! Slowly melting away, Saddam’s skin falls to the floor revealing a metallic internal skeletal system. “A fro-bot!” Skillz stammers. And all of a sudden, before they can sort it out….. BOOM God Gimmick appears! And now it all makes sense…..it all comes together. “Mwhahahahaha…….April Fools everyone!!!!!” Lighting the manger up in flames, God Gimmick begins to prance around. “B….b…..but,” Barkely begins to protest. And then The End opens his mouth to speak as well. God Gimmick puts out a threatening hand, and they go silent. “Hahahaha, silly human fools, don’t you see?” “See what?” Kai asks. “That you’ve been had….” A big, collective “WHAT!?” “Yes, yes, yes you stupid, insolent fools…..it’s an April Fools joke…..that’s all!” He lets this soak in for a moment before continuing on. “You see, being God Gimmick and all, you can go back in any time in history you want…..and watch it repeat itself……and you know, as bored as I got with the original story of the birth of my son…..I needed some change…..ya know, I just had to! But of course, I couldn’t really let any harm come to him…..so along with the help of Charles Barkely…..who, by the way, is really ‘Data’ from Star Trek…..” The android rips off his disguise and exposes himself. Waving his hand toward his surprised company, “Greeting.” “……I built these Muslim fro-bots with him, programmed to scare the crap out of you all!” Pause for evil laugh of ~DOOM~! “Mwahahaha!” And getting control of himself…..“but also programmed them self-destruct if any real harm were to come to my son……so, everybody April Fools!” They’re in shock. But then, suddenly, Kai steps forward and speaks up. “But, I thought that Jesus was born on December 25th…………and April Fools is on April 1st……so……” God Gimmick seems to ponder at this for a moment. And then, flame engulfs his eyes. “Shut up! I am God Gimmick, and I can do whatever the hell I please!” The monk shrugs. “Fair enough.” After letting out well-deserved nervous sighs of relief, one by one the group steps forward to honor the baby Jesus…… And so only a few minutes later, cigar in one hand, a cheap bottle of wine in the other, and a deck of playing cards stuffed down his diapers, the baby awaits his next gift, as The End takes a step forward. But after receiving a quick look from Tomas, he thinks better of it and turns toward the android. “May I?” “Certainly.” Handing over the cowboy hat to the blind wisemen, Data looks on as The End places it on the infant’s head. And when The End takes a step back, admiring the figure of the child, the whole group giggles as it mutters its first word. In a weak voice, the baby cries out, “Hoss.” A big, collective “Awwwww!” And then, drunken from the cheap liquor, The Malice Baby, Jesus rolls on his back and falls back to sleep. “Aw….how cute!” Marry steps forward to admire his son. God Gimmick, doing the same, lifts the baby up out of the cradle. Doing a Darth Vader impression, “Jesus…..I am your father……” Everyone smiles at this. After a quick laugh, he sets the baby back down and turns to The End. And after a moment of staring at the wiseass-man, he begins to question him. Sternly, he asks, “End?” “Y-yes, God Gimmick?” “What was your intended gift for my son?” “Uh…..this hat?” He points over to the sleeping baby. God Gimmick shakes his head. The End sighs, finally giving in. “This picture…..” He pulls out the Ozzy picture, and the room goes silent. Shaking his head in disgust, God Gimmick raises his hand, and ~POOF!~, in one moment, The End is there, all fine n’ dandy, the next, he’s engulfed in the flames of hellfire, and then finally, he’s nowhere to be seen. Nodding his head in approval, the proud father turns and kneels down next to the crib. “Now, for a more appropriate gift…..” He reaches in his back pocket and pulls something out, ready to give it to his son. “Whoops! Wrong pocket……” Putting the naked picture of Asa’a sister away, God Gimmick searches for something else….. ---------------------------------------- The End!
