Magna Stella - Part 3: R-E-S-P-E-C-T

3 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3 - 3 - 03 Traveling constantly can weigh heavily on one’s well being, causing deep, brooding depression and a chronic feeling of irritating jet lag. Not matter how perfect your physical and mental make-up; the irresistible pull of travel can eventually begin to slowly tear your body apart. Everyone knows that, including the Shooting Star Champion Dorn Tracer. He’s had to fly to and fro to the various venues and it has been like a giant lead weight attached to his neck. His mind hasn’t exactly been in prime condition as of late, either. A brash newcomer by the name of Eric Wolfson has been trying to get to him, taunting him and denouncing him as a worthy Shooting Star Champion. Who is he to make such claims? No one, that’s who! Dorn thinks to himself with a burning rage evident only in the crevices of his brain. He wears a nasty scowl upon his face and squints behind his dark shades at nothing in particular, except the manifested image of Wolfson’s smirking visage. Standing alone in his cheap motel room, Dorn is grateful at the time to think. His best friend Jessica has been dragging him around and trying to ‘help him’, With what, though… The Astronomer asks himself, as she never clearly states. “I don’t know what her problem has been lately, but I’m glad she’s taken a day off from her quest,” Dorn exclaims quietly, his voice lowered as if he fears that someone is listening. And knowing his luck, someone is. “I mean, I appreciate her concern, but I’m quite capable of handling myself. After all, by the will of the stars above, I am the Shooting Star Champion. That should say a Universe of things about me.” He looks to the rickety wooden ceiling of the room as if looking for confirmation from the Lords Above. To the naked eye, nothing happens, but Dorn smiles anyway, and lowers his gaze to its regular medium. Ever so slowly, he lowers his leathered bottom to the rough material of the mattress, relaxing his tired legs. Placing his battered hands on his knees, he hunches forward and stares at the Shooting Star Title lying on the scratched, rough surface of the generic wooden dresser. He just stares at it, its golden face reflected against the lens of his shades. The golden rays of light even pass through the black lens and into his crystal blue eyes. So beautiful… like the moon itself… The championship means so much to him. It represents all of his hard effort and misery paying off, and his dedication to Astrology and the Tiamats. En’Flarim rewarded him by giving him the driving strength to defeat Jackson Rust and gain the Rising Star Title, the first trophy of many on the long road ahead. His blood… his sweat… and his soul all went into pushing towards the Rising Star Title, and ultimately achieving it. But now, he has to defend it against 3 other people, one of them being a player of mind games known as Eric Wolfson. Some cocky newcomer who thinks Dorn to be nothing…”But then again, who the fuck is he?” The Astronomer cries aloud, visibly angered. A no one, that’s who. But yet, Dorn finds himself bothered by the entire prospect. Why has this newcomer targeted Dorn? Is it his title? Is it the newcomer’s desire for an impact? Does he hate Dorn because of his religion? It’s probably all of them. Eric Wolfson wants to break Dorn down and steal the glory he has achieved. “Fuckin’ douche bag will do no such thing!” Dorn cries again, becoming ever more infuriated as the thought runs through his mind again and again. Deep within his stomach, he can feel a pit of fire conjuring itself onto his very being. Why is he so irritated by all of this? He knows there’s a lot riding on his next couple of moves, and he has to please En’Flarim. The Goddess rewarded him for supreme performance, but she can take everything away just as fast. “I must destroy Wolfson and the other blasphemous infidels who wish to bring me down… I WILL NOT BE DENIED!” The Astronomer rises from the bed with a defiant stand and slams his fist into his chest, standing before the Shooting Star Title. He stares deep into the face plate, seeing himself within it… the depth of its meaning manifesting itself on Dorn’s very being… The challenged has brought itself about in the form of Eric Wolfson. The tedious journey has revealed itself to be the Shooting Star Title reign. But what’s the ultimate prize? What will The Astronomer achieve if he overcomes the odds of the hateful crowd and his detesting enemies? Pride? Glory? Money? Love? No, it’s so much more than that. He will prove to everyone he’s not some nobody following a silly religion. He will gain what he has always wanted from anyone. He will have respect. [End.]
