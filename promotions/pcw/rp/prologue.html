Date: 9th December Duece 00 Duece It would be a couple of hours before the moon would rise to its full height. A lone figure moved quietly through a forest down a muddy path to a destination unknown. A streak of lightning illuminated the sky briefly chasing the shadows back to the trees revealing a man with a duffel bag slung over his shoulder. He proceeded down the path at a measured pace, his red headband slapping at the wind. His eyes were trained on the path ahead of him. One could mistake him at first glance as being oblivious to the world around him, but this was not the case. His ever- sharp ears listened to the faintest of noise coming from the creatures in the forest around him. He had been walking for better part of two days, hardly stopping for more then a quick drink from a water skin he carried with him. Most people would be exhausted from the rigors of where he travelled from; he showed little or no signs of fatigue. After making his way through the forest, he came into a clearing with what looked like the remains of an age and weather beaten hut. He had finally reached his destination after months of travel.... He was finally home. With a *sigh* and a look of sadness he continued on to the old structure as the first drops of a storm was about to hit. Once inside, he quickly secured a blanket over the doorway to keep out the blowing rain. He lighted a candle and surveyed the inside of the small hut. It was just as it was a left; a dusty old straw mat was in the corner, a few unlit candles in a small basket, and a small table sat in the middle of the hut. Setting the candle on the table before him, he knelt in meditation. Meditation use to come to him with the littlest of effort, but now his mind was a twisted mass of chaotic thoughts. He remembered old friends long gone from his life, old foes who swore vengeance upon him... and of course there was her. The mother he never had known. His thoughts always drifted to her. Some longing he never knew he had until recently. He wondered why his life had been so blessed and yet on the other side of the coin so cursed...truly like the yin and yang symbol. He thought to himself.... ~I have always sought out to find the TRUE meaning of the fight. But if I had known that it Would have lead me down this path I now journey, would I have continued my quest if I Had known then what I KNOW now?~ Slowly he rose up, and walked over to his duffel bag. He untied the drawstrings of the bag and produced from inside of it two rolled up blankets. He threw one of them over the old straw mat and proceeded to lie down on it pulling the other up over his body to keep the dampness away. As he slowly started to fall asleep, he remembered the last time he was with his old friend and training partner Ken. 'That was when it all started....' He thought as he drifted off into sleeps embrace. § Do not tempt the beast within me, lest it be freed upon this earth, a longing that first lied dormant, when it came at mortalbirth. A savage animal crying out, from the depths of my very soul, the raging hungernever ceasing, taking such a painful toll. I don't know how much longer I can fightthis growing desire, this passion thatconsumes and burns like seething volcanicfire. So tempt not the beast within me, seeknot to bring it out! I shiver, and dare notspeak of, what actions would come about... § 'Where I go from hence, let this be my parting word, that what I have seen is unsurpassable.'
