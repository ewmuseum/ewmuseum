Rolling into LA

14 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Title: Rolling into LA Featuring: Duke Williams Date: 03/14/03 -A big rig with eighteen wheels and a PCW logo painted on the side rolls down the highway. The speed limit is 55 but that doesn’t stop this notorious rapscallion from doing 75. The cargo is precious and time sensitive so the law is nothing but a forethought. For in the back of the truck sits the reinforced PCW ring that will be uses this Sunday on Pay-Per-View for the March Madness match.- CB: Ahhhh breaker one-nine this here is the cottonmouth I got a clear go from here to wind city and I recon we got ourselves a convoy. The driver of the PCW rig smiles as he looks out his front window and views nothing but other trucks. He then checks out the rare and finds himself being the last in line. He grabs the CB mic and pushes down on the black button firing up the Cobra 3000 radio. Driver: This here is the Malice Man and I got my barley pop in my right hand and the back door on the convoy. The camera zooms in on the face of the PCW truck to show none other then Duke Williams. His beard is a tad bushier then normal and his t-shirt is dirty. In his right hand is a can of Colorado coolaid and in his left is the wheel. Cotton Mouth: Did anybody get pulled over by that city kitty back in motor city? Malice Man: That’s a big 10-4 good buddy she almost gave me a ticket but I gave her a little something instead. Cotton Mouth: Your telling me you feed the bear with a little more then her fair share? Malice Man: I dropped back double time on her front rack then pulled her in back and banged her crap trap. Malice Style hoss. Duke smiles as he adjusts his chrome shades as the glare of the Michigan sun beats down onto them. Cotton Mouth: All that 5-0 gave me was a ticket and a smile. But I picked me up a lot lizard in Sun Town about a week ago that could have sucked the diesel out of an engine. You get any townie twat recently? Malice Man: Negitory Cotton Mouth, beside the city kitty I haven’t had any titty. Cotton Mouth: All advised I got a plain wrapper shooting pictures on the shoulder. Malice Man: Roger on that good buddy I see him clear as day he’s eyeing my rig. Cotton Mouth: Why is the county Mounty always picking on us and not roger ramjet? Another voice breaks into radio from the airwaves of the CB radio. White Knight: This here is the White Knight in the Tijuana Taxi behind the rolling wrestling ring. You got your ears on big dog? Malice Man: This here is the Malice Man in the rolling ring what’s going on county Mounty? White Knight: I have a bear in the air and five in the cave ready to shut down this convoy if you don’t back it down. Cotton Mouth: I got about fifteen diesel suckers saying you can’t. White Knight: I have some bears wall to wall that might disagree with you cotton mouth. The speed limit in this town is 5-5 I have your picture at 6-5 over. Malice Man: Mercy sakes no need to get all pissy hoss. White Knight: Hoss? Cotton Mouth: Yea Hoss! White Knight: Malice Man you wouldn’t happen to be thee Malice Man? Malice Man: Roger on that big bear this here is the in ring legend Duke Williams. Cotton Mouth: Did you hear that Smokey with ears that there is the Malice Man! White Knight: I’ll be dag bad. I ordered up that PPV this Sunday you gonna smoke the jokers? Malice Man: That’s a major 10-4 White Knight I’m going to be sending the rest of the boys a big Whiskey Bent and Hell bound. White Knight: The old lady and me have a big 52 inch one eyed monster to catch the action on and we’ll be cheering you on. Malice Man: Thank you hoss that mean a lot. White Knight: Why don’t you boys put the petal to the metal and do it like you own it I’m going to call off the bears and pick on some Willy Weaver’s. Malice Man: We thank you for the invitation that’s a big ten four!! Time to lay the hammer down and just for that I’m going to win the rumble for you Sunday night. White Knight: Do it whiskey bent and hell bound. Cotton Mouth: Ain’t that the honky tonk truth. Malice Man: Ten Four! The truck cruises down the highway headed for Craw town for the big P-P-view! =w= ---- View Duke Williams's Biography
