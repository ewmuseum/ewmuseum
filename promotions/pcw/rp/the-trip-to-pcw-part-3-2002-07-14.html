The trip to PCW (Part 3)

14 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7-14-02 A flight from New York to Mississippi, the very first trip to a PCW event for Scorpion now that he is an officially a PCW superstar. The flight is about a quarter full. The fights sturdiest are pushing the tray full of beverages through each aisle on the aircraft. The weather over Mississippi is very cloudy and the aircraft begins to move around in a whirlwind of turbulence. Scorpion is sitting in an aisle by himself, asleep with his headphones on listening to some music. The turbulence is so bad that Scorpion falls down off the seats he is laying on. As soon as Scorpion face hits the ground he wakes up suddenly, frustrated with what is going on. What the hell is going on? Where the hell are we now? Sturdiest: Excuse me sir; you need to sit in your seat. Scorpion: Sorry I fell while I was sleeping. The sturdiest gives Scorpion a dirty look as he walks by. Scorpion picks himself back up and sits back down on his seat. The turbulence continues to alter the balance of the plane as the lights on the airplane begin to fade in and out. The pilot comes over the intercom system to give the passengers and check up on where they are. Pilot: Lades and gentlemen where about fifteen to ten minutes away from landing in Mississippi. On behalf of American Airlines, we thank you for riding with us and please join us next time. Yeah right, I’m using Jet Blue, they got television. The turbulence begins to level off as the plane becomes more balanced as it reaches ground. The Ears of the passengers begin to pop as Scorpion feels a sharp pain within his ears. A baby on the airplane begins to scream to the discontent the their ears. My ears are killing me, I hate this part of the flight. Good thing I’m chewing this piece of gum or it could be worse. The plane begins to lower and lower towards the ground. A runway becomes more visible in Scorpion’s window. It’s a very cloudy day in Mississippi and the heavy winds in the air is what caused the turbulence on the flight. The plane begins to get lower and the plane begins to hover over the runway and then a loud wham noise comes from the bottom of the plane as the wheels from the plane have touched ground. This is it, this is the beginning of my career in PCW. The plane begins to slow down very fast and the pressure from slowing down presses the back of Scorpion’s head to his seat. As the pressure begins to decrease Scorpion’s body lunges forward and the he moves back to his seat in his normal position. A loud applause comes from the passengers and the plane begins to park into a gate. Thank god this is over. Pilot: Ladies and gentlemen it is 3:02 p.m. here in Mississippi; it’s sixty-eight degrees outside, it is partly cloudy right now with winds reaching thirty miles per hour today and a possible Thunderstorm tonight. Hope you enjoy your stay in Mississippi and we hope to see you again on another American Airlines flight again. Wow what I nice thing to tell the people, the weather is crappy but enjoy your stay in Mississippi. That’s like saying yeah it’s bombing in Afghanistan and you could get hit, but enjoy your stay here. What a dumb thing to say. The plane begins to take a complete stop and the seat belt light is turned off. Immediately after it is turned off the people on the plane get up and begin to open the overhead compartments to get their things. Scorpion is one of the first to get there things and begins to walk out of his row seven seat and out into the hallway until he is stopped by a family getting there things at a very slow rate. Oh give me a break could they be any slower? The family finds gets there stuff and begins to move up off the plane. The sturdiest great the passengers as they leave the plane. Scorpion walks up the ramp and heads out of the gate area where a bunch of people are sitting waiting to get on the plane that Scorpion just departed. Scorpion walks down a bit in the big hallway until he reaches a sign where rental cars are. He heads down to the National rental car system and heads over to the front desk of the area. National employee: Hello sir, how can I help you? Scorpion: I’m here to pick up a car. National employee: Ok sir what is your name? Scorpion: It should be under Primetime Championship Wrestling and Jeff Steiner. National employee: All right sir here is your key and it’s parked in lot number ninety-six. Scorpion: Thank you very much. Scorpion grabs the keys and takes his bags and walks outside to a huge lot of cars. Signs reading 1-50, 51-100 and 101-150 is where the cars are parked. Scorpion heads down the 51-100 row and continues to make his way down the oil stained road where the cars are parked. Finally Scorpion reaches lot number ninety-six, a black Subaru Forrester. Dam I hate this car, but it’s good for now, I just can’t wait to get to the arena. Scorpion puts the key in the ignition and the engine begins to start up. Scorpion then reaches in his bag as the car begins to warm up and pulls out a direction sheet towards the arena. Vaught Hemmingway Stadium is just about…twenty minutes from here. Ok so I take the route right in front of the car rental place about ten miles, three traffic lights and then I turn left on the third and the stadium will be on the right ten miles down the road. Scorpion backs the car up out of the parking lot and heads down on the road. Very few stores are around the area and there is a lot of forest area. Scorpion travels down the road at fifty-five miles per hour and heads down to the third traffic light where he makes a left. The road going to the stadium is very closed off with absolutely no homes or stores for about the first seven miles. Scorpion finally reaches the Vaught Hemmingway Stadium and the parking lot for the PCW talent and personal is packed. The sign at the stadium reads: “PCW Turmoil, This Tuesday night at 8:05! SOLD OUT!” Scorpion pulls into the parking lot where a security guard is at the beginning of the lot. Security Guard: Hello sir and you are? Scorpion: Jeff Steiner, PCW Talent. Security Guard: Ok, I see you on the list you can come in. Scorpion: Thanks. Scorpion drives the rental car into the parking lot and gets a space all the way at the back of the parking lot right next to a red minivan and a blue Ford F-150 pick-up truck. Scorpion takes the key out of the ignition as he comes to a complete stop. This is it, I finally made it to PCW. Scorpion takes his cell phone out of his pocket and begins to call his wife back in New York, after four rings he gets a recording. Hi you have reached The Steiner family we are unable to come to the phone right now, please leave a message after the tone and we will try to get back to you, thank you. BEEP. Scorpion: Hi Steph, it’s Jeff. I’m in Mississippi and I’m at the arena. The flight was a lot of turbulence and weather is pretty bad down here. I’ll talk to you guys later, bye. Scorpion ends the call on his cell phone and gets out of the car. He opens the backseat car door and takes out his bags. He shuts the door and locks the car up with the button on his car keys. Scorpion begins to walks down to the arena. This is it, the time is now and I’m ready for Primetime.
