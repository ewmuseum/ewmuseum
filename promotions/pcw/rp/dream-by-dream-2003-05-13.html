Dream by Dream

13 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 13/05/03 Do you ever dream? I’m sorry…. What a stupid question. Of course you do. We ALL dream. Dreaming helps us to live our lives because it gives us hope that someday, things might be better. Well, I want you to picture this. I want you to stop and take a moment to imagine a world where your dreams, your deepest desires have come true. A world where the one thing that you’ve always wished for all is right there in front of you, ready for the taking. It might be a new sports car, a beautiful wife, not having to worry about bills or taxes for the rest of your life, or even world peace. It could be anything, anything you want…. Done that? Doesn’t it feel good to know that that one thing is right there? Well, that’s exactly how I feel right now. I know what a lot of people might be thinking right about now. Thinking that I’ve never had this kind of opportunity before and do I have the skills to make anything of it. Even though I hate to admit it, I guess these people do have reason to believe that when push comes to shove, Manuel Labor can sometimes be the one left sitting on his ass. And yeah, maybe this opportunity has come around too soon for my liking. Maybe I did want to do more and move my way up the ranks once again before staking my claim for the top prize in this company. But that doesn’t mean to say that I’m just gonna lie back and accept defeat before the match has even started. That’s just not the type of person I am. No, what I’m going to do is the exact opposite. I’m sick of being the butt of everyone’s jokes. I’m pissed off with being told that I can’t make it to the top of the mountain, and I’ve had it up to here with being told to stop wasting my time. As a kid, I dreamt of becoming a wrestler. It was what I wanted more than anything else in the world. My desire was to step inside that squared circle and make the audience feel exactly the same way that I feel about this business. I imagined, and I dreamt, and I desired, and in the end, I had the opportunity to make it come true. God only knows that that kind of thing doesn’t happen often, yet I was lucky enough to be given the opportunity to live my dream. As is always the case when you accomplish one of your wishes, another takes its place. In this case, it was a dream in which I proved myself to be the very best that this business has to offer. And so, I’ve come to the realization that this match is somewhat of a godsend. It’s a test, to see just how far I’ve come. In a weird sort of way, the past was like being stuck in a prison cell. I was there, all alone, with no help and no allies. Nobody to talk to, nobody to help me get through it. I was alone, I was cold, and I was in the dark. There was no escape from that cell, and there was no way I was going to get help to find a way out. Yet somehow, I did. I rode it out and by God, I SURVIVED! And that is why I’m still here today. Still searching for ways to improve myself. Still trying to push my way up the card and back into contention for the one thing that has ever really mattered to me. Some people are content with wishing for cars, women and riches.… Not me. I just ain’t interested. Don’t get me wrong…. I’m not exactly well-off. I don’t have a fast car, or a beautiful wife, or rooms full of cash. I travel mostly in crappy rental cars, I live alone, and I still have to worry about the tax man. But hey? That’s life. There is only one thing I’ve dreamt of since breaking into the wrestling business. One thing that I have imagined, and hoped for right from day one. One thing that truly matters to me, and we all know what it is. So, maybe this’ll be my chance to make it big. This will be the moment I’ve been waiting for. The one thing that I do know for sure though is that regardless of whatever happens this Tuesday, I will continue to dream. I will continue to wish. I will continue to hope. And I will continue to imagine. After all, what the hell is there in this life if we don’t have that?
