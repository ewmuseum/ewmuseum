Hunted Saga Part 2: Danger Revealed...

21 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 21/3/02 The camera opens outside of the very military base, which John Crichton previously escaped from, except now it is day and the sun shines down upon the sodden woodland area. We can see guards surrounding the place, looking at the grass, looking at the shrubbery, to find any clue whatsoever on the escapee’s whereabouts. The camera then cuts to the very front of the military base, at the security booth as a man driving a black open topped Mustang is granted clearance and enters the compound. The camera follows the Mustang as it under takes various twists and turns and finally comes to a halt in a very secluded parking area. The Mustang door smoothly opens and a man, wearing a dark grey suit, white shirt and black tie steps out of the car confidently. He retrieves his black Oakley sunglasses from the breast pocket of his jacket and places them on his face. He also takes out a pack of Viceroy cigarettes from his pants pocket, closely followed by the inevitable lighter. The man lights his cigarette then enters the building, through the swing doors not too far from his car. The camera follows him as he opens the door and walks directly to the rather ‘friendly’ looking secretary sat behind the desk. He was beginning to like this General already he perceived. “It’s Mr. Fleming to see General Tranter, we have an appointment.” Fleming stated looking down the secretaries top nonchalantly. “Yes sir. General Tranter is expecting you, go right into his office and I shall get him for you immediately.” The secretary replied with a dumb blonde style giggle. “Thanks very much.” Fleming unnecessarily added with charm. The camera follows the man named Mr. Fleming into General Tranter’s office. Fleming opens the door to reveal a very spacious and very luxurious office indeed, even for a General, Fleming concluded. On the far side of the wall was a huge window, which covered over 75% of that wall. At the center of the room stood the General’s old looking mahogany desk. With two mahogany chairs, one at either side. Fleming decided that he liked this General indeed. Fleming walks further into the mans office and sits down in what he has to assume is the guest chair. Fleming couldn’t help wondering why he was called here. He was a special agent working for one of the governments ‘black’ divisions. His specialty was hunting people down and he was damned good at it as well. In fact, Fleming recollected The longest amount of time one of his prey had eluded him for was one week and then his prey was lucky. In fact, Fleming realized Luck was the one important factor in his line of work it decided everything. Fleming’s concentration was effectively severed as the office door open and a man in army uniform entered the office. Fleming made a note of the medals hanging from the man’s chest, he had to assume that this was General Tranter. The man walks in and sits in the seat opposite him and drops a file before Fleming’s eyes. “Good Morning General Tranter.” Fleming greated. “ I suggest we can past the pleasantries and get right down to business. You must be wondering why I have called for this meeting?” General Tranter replied. “Yes I am.” Fleming simply stated. “Well Fleming I called you here, because I have a little job for you to do. I hear you’re the best this country has to offer at what you do.” Tranter commented. “That has been said before, yes.” Fleming said eyeing up the file laid down upon the table by General Tranter. “Your being too modest Mr. Fleming, you are the best and we need the best for this assignment. I need a professional who won’t lose his head and simply terminate the target when antagonized.” Tranter explained. “I see, well can you tell me the basic details of the case first?” Fleming questioned. “Well last night one of my officers escaped this military compound in the middle of the night and we have no trace of his presence.” Tranter answered calmly. “I see and why is this subject so important to you?” Fleming probed. “He was chosen by the white coats as the perfect guinea pig for our little experiment. When we broke the news to him, he seemed really enthusiastic, but when we went back to him this morning he was gone. Quite frankly Mr. Fleming we need this man back and back fast, so I decided not to wait around in the off chance that we are able to find him ourselves. I want to employ you to go and get him for me.” Tranter explained. “Sounds reasonable, but the question is can you afford my prices?” Fleming coolly retorted. “No my boy, the question is will you be able to find the man I’m looking for?” Tranter furiously replied. “Well General, my record speaks for itself. I have caught each and every single target that I was assigned to and none of them lasted more than one week.” Fleming stated. “I concur that is a very impressive record, but can you come up with the goods on this one?” Tranter questioned. “Why is this target different?” Fleming replied getting off the subject of his competence. “Because Fleming he is, or should I say was one of us. He has been trained to the highest degree and is a superb soldier. The catch is we want him brought back alive. We can’t emphasize this point enough. The experiment is crucial to the United States National Security.” Tranter informed. Fleming blinked in disbelief. “Wow, so this was one was sent from high above?” Fleming replied. “It is indeed. These [Pointing to the files before Fleming] is all the info we have on the target. We’ve added a psychoanalytic review as well, to give you a better understanding of how his mind works. Tranter said with a smile. “Great. The best way to catch a man, is to think like the man.” Fleming replied confidently. “So I take it you in on this one then?” Tranted probed. “Ya know? In all my years of hunting people down, nobody has ever challenged me. This man you speak of seems to be quite a challenge indeed. Of course I accept. I’ll make this case the top priority.” Fleming replied. “Good, good.” Tranter says with a very far off look on his face. With that Fleming simply gets up from the very uncomfortable mahogany chair, taking the files with him as he rises. Fleming then turns his back on Tranter and exits the Generals office. Fleming wasn’t too sure about Tranter, right from the start he suspected that something was wrong with this guy. Fleming couldn’t put his finger on it, but silently decided that he would watch Tranter and take his orders with a grain of salt. The camera follows Fleming as he exits the military building and opens the black Mustang door and gets into the drivers seat and closes the door behind him. After a couple of seconds the engines flicker slowly on and Fleming drives away from the Military base and on the road in search of his prey. Three weeks after Crichton’s escape from the Military base… The camera opens up outside a building in central Chicago, Illinois. At the front of the gym are big letters stated quite clearly “SUNS GYM”. The camera enters the establishment through the main doors and once inside we can see the basic layout of the gym. Various weight lifting machines and fitness machines are scattered before us, all leading up to the centerpiece of the gym, a full sized wrestling ring. The camera probes forward, further into the gym, past all of the fitness machines and the sweating bodies and finally centers upon the gym. Crichton is stood in one corner of the ring, with Aeryn Sun standing upon the apron, seemingly whispering words of advice into his ear. In the other corner is some very transparent fresh-faced young trainee. An old guy standing outside with baseball cap on and a white towel over his shoulder, rings a bell and both men in the ring begin to square up with each other. They go into a collar and elbow tie – up. Crichton over powers the trainee, shrugging him over to one side then implanting a knee into his mid section. Crichton then tosses the trainee into the ropes. Crichton ducks too early to execute a back drop and the trainee boots him in the face and follows this up with a clothesline. The trainee drags Crichton up to his feet, but Crichton hits him with a low blow. Crichton then places the trainees head between his legs and perhaps his very own variation of a piledriver. Crichton goes for the pin and the ref counts, 1..2..3. The old coach guy rings the bell once more. Crichton then proceeds to help the trainee off his feet. “Not bad boy, your kinda good. You’ll go far here.” Crichton reassures. Then the old guy dressed as a coach steps up to the ring and gets Crichton’s attention. “Hey son, I need to talk to you in my office right now.” The Coach States. “Ok Hank, I’ll be right up.” Crichton replied with a worried look upon his face. Aeryn walks up close to Crichton and places her hand upon his shoulder. “Hey John, what does my dad want to talk to you about?” Aeryn questions. “I don’t know, but that’s what I’m going to find out. You coming?” Crichton answered. “Hell Yeah.” Aeryn replies without hesitation. The camera follows Crichton and Aeryn as they walk to the far side of the gym, and walk up the wooden rickety stairs towards Hank Sun’s office. They open the door and walk in. Hank is already sitting behind his desk. As soon as they enter Hank looks up at Crichton and says, “Crichton have I got news for you!” The camera fades to black.
