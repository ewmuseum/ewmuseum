Protesting Lips & Ass In Hubbardville.

8 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 08/05/02 Duke Williams has done it again. For over a month now he has antagonised the Gold Patron Meritorious...He has banned Scientology from PCW, he has made lewd remarks about Ellie~! and L Ron Hubbard, he has sent Kannon Whiskey Bent and Hellbound....Then when he joined Scientology Xavier could have been forgiven for thinking Duke couldn’t do anything more. He was wrong... For yesterday was the grand opening of Duke’s new business venture, a greasy, dirty, disgusting Hot Dog stand, similar to those found on his home streets of Chicago named Hubbard’s Red Hots has been opened up by none other than the PCW President himself....But Kannon will not stand idly by and allow Duke to enter Hubbardville that easily. For if there is one thing Kannon is good at it is moaning, whining and complaining. Also blessed with a sliver tongue he has managed to turn many a person to his perspective....But will it work this time? “RALLY AROUND IF YOU CARE ABOUT HUBBARDVILLE!” Out shot opens up outside Duke William’s brand new Hot Dog stand, Hubbard’s Red Hots, in Hubbardville, Wisconsin....What was until a few days ago Xavier Kannon’s fortress against the oppression of the outside world. We see the Gold Patron Meritorious himself standing beside his valet Eleanor Hall, both handing out placards to those who walk past, trying to get them to join in the protest while the bemused diners watch out of the window. Xavier : “MAKE A STAND MY FELLOW SCIENTOLOGISTS! BOYCOTT THIS EATERY, SHOW DUKE THAT SCIENTOLOGISTS ARE ABOVE HIS WAYS....ENJOY THE CULINARY DELIGHTS THAT HUBBARDVILLE HAS TO OFFER....” The Gold Patron Meritorious is wearing a long, Brown leather coat with an open Black silk shirt under it revealing a “DUKE OUT” t-shirt. He also has on a pair of Black worker-cut jeans with Purple and Green embroidery snaking down the left leg to his pair of Black, Green and Purple Skechers. Xavier : “....DINE OUT IN THE KELLY PRESTON PIZZA PARLOUR, OR THE LISA MARIE 100% PRIME BEEF STEAK HOUSE....” As more and more people who have heard Duke’s words walk past and grab a placard a crowd of around 100 has formed, all cheering on he words of their idol. Beside Xavi, Ellie~! stands there with her hands on her hips, grinning smugly at the sight of all the people joining their cause, the smug almost turning into a sneer out of loathing for the PCW President who has insulted her for the last month. Eleanor : “Beef......Steak.....House!” The crowd are now formed in an arc in front of the 1st couple of Scientology who themselves are stood with their backs to Hubbard’s Red Hots. Amongst the signs being waved are “HUBBARDVILLE SAYS NO TO MALICE STYLE” and “DUKE....OUT....NOW!” The new arrivals to the protest spur Xavier on, giving him more confidence. We zoom out to show the full crowd, now numbering 150 who are all listening attentively to Kannon’s words, and just in the corner of the screen we see a small stage set up on which stands the newest convert to Scientology Nelly Furtado. Xavier : “This business venture is just another of Duke Williams’ insults to Scientology. First he gives us the biggest slap in the face by actually JOINING US, which he did just to spite me...HE DOES NOT CARE ABOUT HUBBARD! I CARE ABOUT HUBBARD! IF HE WAS ALIVE NOW HE WOULD BE HERE EMBRACING ME AS HIS SON WHILE TELLING DUKE TO GET HIMSELF AND HIS BUSINESS OUT OF HUBBARDVILLE! HIS BUSINESS WHICH, MAY I ADD...DOES NOT SELL THE OFFICIAL DRINK OF SCIENTOLOGY AND PCW..... SNAPPLE~! LIKE ALL OTHER WHOLESOME BUSINESSES DO IN HUBBARDVILLE!” He raises his fist triumphantly and the crowd before him roar, attracting looks of disbelief from the workers inside who are all watching what transpires. As he does so, Ellie~! pulls out a bottle and Snapple~! and displays it as a trophy. Eleanor : “Do......In.......Hubbardville!” His preppy valet is wearing a skinny fit Pink Snapple~! t-shirt with Silver writing across her 32B chest. Along with this she wears a pair of Blue flared jeans with floral embroidery down the sides of each leg. Her long, wavy Brown hair with Blond highlights is loose thus allowing her to twiddle it around her finger when Kannon talks, and around her wrists she wears a plethora of bands and bracelets along with a chunky watch. Xavier : “This man has said things in the past that heap shame upon the fine faith of Scientology! He has said that L RON HUBBARD TAKES IT MALICE STYLE! That IS NOT A COMPLIMENT..... He said that the founder of our faith likes it....Um...In th-...IN THE ASS!!!” Ellie~! shakes her head, disgusted at Duke’s accusations both at her and Hubbard. Eleanor : “In......The......Ass!” Xavier : “He even HAD THE NERVE to imply that the beautiful Miss Hall here.... Eleanor..... Ellie~!..... THE FIRST LADY OF SCIENTOLOGY...... LIKED IT IN THE ASS TOO!!!” Again her face turns sour and the crowd mutter amongst themselves, disgusted at the words and actions of Duke Williams, the proprietor of the shop in front of them. Eleanor : “Um....Which I, like, SO don’t by the way!” Kannon takes a few seconds just to nod to his valet’s protests and again the crowd talk amongst themselves in disapproval before Xavier continues. Xavier : “Hear that? DUKE WILLIAMS LIES!” There are a few “YEAH”s shouted from the crown now and they are all glaring menacingly through at the employees who are looking troubled by the scene outside. Xavier : “Duke Williams was the man that not too long ago, at the beginning of April.... BANNED SCIENTOLOGY FROM PRIMETIME CHAMPIONSHIP WRESTLING! Everytime I mentioned Scientology, or even Hubbard himself it was BLEEPED OUT!” Calls of “THAT’S DISGUSTING” and “DOWN WITH DUKE” are heard being called out to Xavier who smugly nods as Ellie~! mimics him, standing to his left. Xavier : “THIS IS THE KIND OF NARROW MINDED SIMPLETON WHO HAS BEEN OPPRESSING US SINCE WE BEGAN TO EXIST!!!” Eleanor : “Been.......Oppressing.......Us!” Kannon, now figuring he has the protesters in the palm of his hand presses on, revelling in the fact that for once people want to hear about his hatred for Duke so he continues to preach his hate to the occupants of America’s newest City. Xavier : “Ask yourself this my fellow Hubbard followers...... WHAT HAS DUKE DONE FOR SCIENTOLOGY?” Eleanor : “Done.....For......Scientology! Yeah!” The silver tongued Scientologist pauses, using his facial expression to add to the question, prompting the people in the crowd to think about the points he poses. Xavier : “Has he delivered Nelly Furtado and John Malkovich?” The Gold Patron Meritorious and his preppy valet gesture over to the small, makeshift stage where Nelly is standing and she gives a little wave. Beside her, with a pair of Snapple~! sponsored Bongos in hand is top Hollywood thespian John Malkovich who casually raises a hand. Furtado and Malkovich : “Hi!” The Commish starts talking again and as one the crowd’s heads turn back to focus on him. Xavier : “So far all he has done is give us this cheap, second rate HOT DOG STAND!!!” Eleanor : “Hot......Dog.......Stand! Like....SO EUGH!” His fingers move from pointing at Hubbard’s Red Hots to gesturing with both arms all around him. Xavier : “Now.....Look what I have done for you..... I HAVE GIVEN YOU HUBBARDVILLE! I HAVE PUT SCIENTOLOGY ON THE AIR THREE TIMES A WEEK, EACH AND EVERY WEEK!” Even someone as highly versed in the art of not shutting up like Xavier is has to pause for breath. Xavier : “Oh, and I have NEVER said a bad word about Hubbard..... I have lost friends for my faith..... When Jeremy Howard couldn’t accept my beliefs I EVEN DESTROYED ASYLUM to end the oppression.....What has Duke done? DUKE HAS SUPPORTED CRUNK!” Toe tapping and shaking of heads begins in the crowd in disappointment at Duke’s past affiliation. Xavier : “CRUNK IS THE ENEMY OF SCIENTOLOGY! It stands for everything we don’t, it is a religion for UNDESIRABLES....IT IS WHERE DUKE BELONGS, NOT IN SCIENTOLOGY....HE BELONGS IN CRUNK!!! Eleanor : “Er.....Didn’t you, like, ban it?” Xavier : “HUBBARD DAMN IT! I’D REINSTATE IT JUST SO DUKE CAN LEAVE SCIENTOLOGY!” Eleanor : “Um....Should you be taking Hubbard’s name in vain?” Xavier : “ARGH!!! I HATE DUKE WILLIAMS! LOOK WHAT HE HAS REDUCED ME TO?!?!?!” As the couple debate things amongst themselves several face in the crowd begin to look tired, but this is tame compared to those who are openly yawning and the few who are rubbing their stomachs, looking with envy at those eating within the eatery. As the murmur rises in volume Xavier looks to them and sees a man leave the corner of the crowd and sneak around towards the entrance to Hubbard’s Red Hots, prompting the Scientologist to snap. Xavier : “I AM THE COMMISSIONER OF PCW! I AM THE PCW CONTINENTAL CHAMPION! I SHOULD NOT BE STANDING OUTSIDE GREASY HOT DOG STANDS WITH STUPID LITTLE SIGNS AND NELLY FURTADO WAITING TO SING HER CAMPAIGN SONG!” Over on the stage Nelly is looking confused as John Malkovich is off in his own world playing a tune on his Snapple~! Bongos. Furtado : “So....Do you want me to sing “Duke is Wrong”....The official song of your hatred for Duke Williams?” Xavier : “NO!!!!!” Eleanor : “No........No........No!” He now begins to pace around, agitated and stomping his feet. Xavier : “I DON’T WANT ANY OF THIS! I DON’T WANT TO BE PROTESTING LIKE A COMMONER! I DON’T WANT TO LISTEN TO NELLY FURTADO SING ABOUT HOW MUCH I HATE DUKE! I WANT HIM OUT OF HUBBARDVILLE NOW! I WANT TO CAVE HIS SKULL IN WITH A CHAIR IN ON FUSION AND THEN FINISH THE JOB AT SUPREMACY!” Eleanor : “Job........At........Supremacy!” He then turns his head quickly to look at his valet, his raging eye showing his frustration. Xavier : “NO I AM NOT GOING TO JOB AT SUPREMACY!” She just put her hands on her hips and looks away, muttering under her breath. Eleanor : “Touch-EY!” As he sees a few more people try and sneak in he decides he has had enough and bellows at those still holding up their protest placards to their bemusement. Xavier : “ARGH! THAT IS IT! I’M GOING....WILLIAMS, I WILL HAVE THIS PLACE CLOSED DOWN AND I’LL RETIRE THAT HILLBILLY ASS OF YOURS AT SUPREMACY!” He now isn’t even addressing the crowd but is off in his won private rant as he barges through the crowd which is slow in parting to let him through, most now heading into Hubbard’s Red Hots for something to eat. Xavier : “THIS TIME YOU GO.....YOU WON’T EVEN BE ABLE TO MAKE IT BACK TO THE ANNOUNCE DESK..... YOU’RE GONE WILLIAMS...... GONE FROM HUBBARDVILLE..... AND GONE FROM PCW!!!” Eleanor : “Gone.......From......PCW!” Ellie~! sneers at the few remaining people as Kannon storms off into the distance and the shot starts to fade out. Xavier : “You’re damn right.” As we hit Black only the sound of John Malkovich playing on the Snapple~! endorsed Bongos and Nelly Furtado’s voice can be heard. “Er.....Shall I sing my song now?”
