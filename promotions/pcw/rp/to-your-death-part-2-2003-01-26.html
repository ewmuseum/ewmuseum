To Your Death (part 2)

26 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1/26/03 A peaceful valley stretches on as far as the eye can see. Birds chirp, flowers sway and a single doggie trots through the green grass with a frisbee between his jaws. Puppy: Arf! Happily the little pup wags his tail, as something catches his eye....with a bark he drops his plastic disk, snatching up a small bone. Puppy: Arf! Arf! With his small brown eyes lighting up with glee, he bounces along with his newfound toy clutched securely....then a shadow falls over him, as he looks up slowly. Puppy: Arf? A bony hand reaches out like a bolt of lightning, snapping the puppy with a final choked 'arf'. We pan up to the leering and bony face of Dark Gimmick, who regards it evilly. Dark Gimmick: Arf indeed. DG then stuffs the dog into his mouth, chomping and swallowing it with a gulp. As he rubs his skeletal tummy, across from him an extremely large man in a half-skull sits and watches. Hessian: Not gonna share any of that, are you. Dark Gimmick: Bah. I share nothing with my own son, let alone my son's bastard son. Hessian: Feh. The Titanic Satanic Insaniac reaches into his picnic basket, producing a large bloody leg of....something. He takes a huge chomp out of it, gore falling from his lips. Dark Gimmick: Chew with your mouth closed. Hessian: Least I have a mouth, asshole. Dark Gimmick: Grr. Hessian continues to knaw on the slab of meat....then a thud shakes the ground. Hessian: (Chewing) The hell was that? Dark Gimmick: How the hell should I know? Another thud, then another. Then another. Then another. Hessian: The fuck? Finally the thudding ceases, only to be replaced by a faint roar that gradually grows louder. Then louder, then louder, then louder. Dark Gimmick: Eh? Louder and louder still it grows.....until the form producing it appears over the top of a hill. Hessian: Hm. Dark Gimmick: Now what in the..... Our mystery intruder races closer at an alarming rate....DG sticks a hand in front of his eye sockets, squinting. Nearly as soon as he does though the stranger closes in, tackling him to the ground like some reject from the Waterboy. Clyde: Yeeaaaaaagh!!!!!! The burned man rolls across the grass, his skeletal father in his grip. Dark Gimmick ends up on the bottom, then kicks Clyde off and sends him flying quite a distance. Dark Gimmick: (Standing) Feh. What's the idea? Clyde hits the ground, then rolls straight back to his feet. Clyde: You know the idea. Dark Gimmick: Now's not the time for riddles, dumbass. Spit it out. Clyde: You killed someone that belonged to ME. The demon leaps through the air, again tackling Dark Gimmick to the ground. DG bucks him off again, kipping up. Dark Gimmick: Feh. I kill lots of people, narrow it down. Clyde: Gruaaaaargh! Clyde again leaps, spearing DG right in the gut. The face of evil coughs up a glob of chewed up puppy, which lands directly in front of Hessian. Hessian: Will you fuckwads knock it off? Clyde: Quiet boy. OW!!!!! Dark Gimmick punches with all his might, sending a bony fist straight into Clyde's gut. He stumbles backwards, holding his stomach and growling. Dark Gimmick: I don't know the meaning of this, but you're going to die. Hessian: Again. Dark Gimmick: Again. DG points a finger, shooting a bolt of lightning out of his finger. Clyde ducks, causing it to strike a nearby tree....which promptly springs to life, yelping like a scalded dog. Tree: Arf arf arf! The tree runs off into the distance, as the demon shakes his head. Clyde: You killed Brendan Fraser before I could. For that you're going to die. Dark Gimmick: Not if I kill you first. Clyde's father snaps his fingers, causing what appears to be a large meteor to crash down from the sky. He jumps just clear, then another falls, then another. Clyde: Stop that! Dark Gimmick: Don't try to kill what you can't kill. Clyde: Oh I can kill you. Ducking and dodging various debris, Clyde winds his way towards his father. However just as he reaches him a burst of energy explodes forth, as DG's eyes blaze and his cloak sways madly in an unearthly tempest. Dark Gimmick: DESPAIR!!!!! Clyde: ARGH!!!!!! The burned man again tumbles across the grass, landing in a heap. Dark Gimmick slowly stalks towards him, producing a huge and wicked sword from the darkness of his garb. Dark Gimmick: Now we reach the endgame. Clyde: Indeed we do. Clyde abruptly throws his hand up, flinging a pile of dirt and mud into Dark Gimmick's eye sockets. Dark Gimmick: (Holding his face) I'm blind! Clyde: You're also dead. HIYAAAAA!!!!!! The demon leaps forth, once again knocking the grim reaper to the earth. The pair again rolls about in the dirt, throwing punches until a pair of heavy thuds ring forth, crashing into both of their skulls. Dark Gimmick & Clyde: OW!!!!!! Both of the monsters slump to the ground, as Hessian stands over them slapping a massive club in his hand. Hessian: Dumbasses. I.....WHAT THE HELL!?!? AGH!!!!! A siren screams, an engine roars and a police car races out of nowhere, slamming into the huge figure of Hessian and sending him flying straight over the landscape. The door pops open, and five uniformed police officers race out. Clyde: What is the meaning of....HEY!!!!! The cops surround Clyde, cuffing his hands behind his back. Another person steps out of the car, this one a middle aged woman in a suit. She steps up to the fallen monster, then stoops and picks up the mangled piece of puppy off the ground. Woman: Mr. Clyde, you are under arrest. Clyde: What!? Woman: You heard me. Take him away! Off the ground the burned man is hauled, then pushed rudely into the waiting squad car. Clyde: You can't do this to me, I'm a postal worker! I AM EXEMPT FROM ANY AND ALL CRIMES!!!!! Cop: Quiet you! The officer wheels back and bashes Clyde in the head with his baton. The killer wobbles, wobbles, then finally slinks into darkness.....the only sound he hears through the murky haze, the roar of the car's engine as he is carted to a destination unknown.....
