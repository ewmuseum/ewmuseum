Ray Ray The Uncomfortably Stereotypical Black Man Learns The Rules

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11.3.02(#2 TT, #1 FF) “You’ve got to be kidding me.” That would be the voice of Rush Mason that greets us as we open the promo in blackness, as we begin to fade in, although the picture is not clear. “Nah…I got muchies like whoa.” The second voice, that we can recognize as we fade in, is that of Rush’s new ‘muscle’ and assistant for the week, Ray Ray. We can see that the scene is that outside a KFC where Ray Ray seems to be heading for the door. Rush: Don’t you realize what you are? All you utter are stereotypical black phrases, and you’re going to eat…AT KFC!! Ray Ray comprehends this for a moment, scratching at his goatee. He then turns to Rush, and replies, in a most eloquent way. Ray Ray: Nah…I’m just keepin’ it real. Rush sighs, smacking his forehead as he follows the huge man, muttering something about keeping it ‘real stupid.’ Ray Ray’s mouth is already watering at the amazing selections of chicken and biscuits. Rush: Make this quick, we’ve got things to do. Knights to disembowel, Chef’s to poison the food of, more evil stuff like that. Ray Ray, however, is paying very little attention to his, at least temporary, employer as he looks at up at the delicious menu of food items that KFC has to offer. Rush taps his foot irritably, as Ray Ray turns to him, and asks in the tone that one might use to ask a question about philosophy. Ray Ray: Yo dawg…you think I should get the ‘Kickin Wings’ or the ‘Juicy Breasts.’ I love both of ‘em….fa sho. Rush looks up at the near 7 footer, who looks very out of place in such a mundane restaurant. He shakes his head again, calling up to man who has about 8 inches on him. Rush:You don’t really speak English, do you? Ray Ray looks at Rush, and the two men just stare at each other for a moment, Rush waiting for an answer. However, Ray Ray simply turns and steps up to the counter while Rush just looks on, amazed at this very strange man. Ray Ray: Yo…I’ma have the Crispy Chicken…with a biscuit and mashed potaoes…’cuz I’m hungry like whoa. Rush walks away for a moment, his brain about to be fried by the stereotypical black sayings, and goes to sit down at a booth. Rush: Of all the primates walking the earth…I get the cookie cutter mold of Statuz Quo Jr… Rubbing at his head, Rush looks up to see Ray Ray grinning like an idiot as he squeezes himself into the small booth, and Mason doesn’t even acknowledge his presence at the table, but Ray Ray make sure that Rush notices him. Ray Ray: Yo dawg…you want some chicken? It’s crunk like a- Rush quickly cuts him off, trying to silence him from saying the banned word, but it’s too late as Rush can hear cars pulling up outside. Ray Ray: Oh shit, dawg! Po po! Let’s bizounce! Rush doesn’t even flinch, looking up at the huge man, who even under pressure can’t stop being uncomfortably stereotypical. Rush spies men, from the corner of his eyes, coming in both sides of the restaurant, as Ray Ray looks around, fear in his eyes. Rush: You know, you really shouldn’t have said that. Rush nods to Ray Ray, who just continues to look around at the men, who are wearing gray suits, proceed towards the two men. Ray Ray: Dawg, that ain’t the po po… Rush nods accordingly, explaining something to Ray Ray. He puts his hands on the table, realizing that there is no escape. Rush: Those….would be the men in medium off-gray. Crunk is a banned word from PCW, and Kannon uses the Men In Medium Off-Gray to enforce the law. Ray Ray looks confused, already, but Rush continues, not noting the fact that Ray Ray is looking like a dear in headlights. Rush: The Men In Very Very Dark Gray are reserved for important situations, like getting Ellie~! nail polish, etc. It’s hierarchical system, with the end result being Men In Ugly Opaque. Ray Ray, now looks totally befuddled at Rush’s explanation. The Voodoo Villain sighs, and mutters to himself. He then turns to Ray Ray and gives him a reason to get both of them out of here. Rush: Ray Ray…you know that those are the people that shot Tupac?As any good stereotypical black person would do, Ray Ray goes into what can only be described to PCW fans as a mimic of the Void Trance, except wearing exponentially baggier clothes. Rush: You fight them off…I’ll…uhh…go investigate. And with Ray Ray rushing the MIMG, Rush strolls out of the restaurant, heading towards his Baby Blue 4.6 Range Rover, where he turns on, surprisingly, some Beethoven, and closes his eyes, listening to the beautiful sounds, while back in the KFC, Ray Ray is almost done dismantling his foes. The huge man waddles quickly out to the car. Ray Ray: I kicked they asses, yo….fa’ sho. Rush turns as he hears Ray Ray, and just rolls his eyes, speaking to no one in particular, although the sarcastic comment is meant for his large sidekick. Rush: Oh just fantastic. Ray Ray nods excitedly, then Rush puts the car in reverse, as the scene fades out and we hear a final commentary from Ray Ray on the music… “What’s this shit, dawg?” “It’s Called Beethoven, the most influential classical composer of our time.” “Classical? You mean like Grandmaster Flash?” *sigh*…
