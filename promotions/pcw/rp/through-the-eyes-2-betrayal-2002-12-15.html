Through The Eyes [2] Betrayal

15 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 15.12.02 Date: August 14th, 1977Place: Buffalo, New York (Hanson Civic Center)Time: 9:25 PM (EST) Sweat poured from the face of the champion, as Jackson picked himself up from the mat once more, checking to see if he had bled any during the match. Rolling his tongue around his teeth, he felt a wound open, and fresh to get more sightly in a minute. Spit of blood, get right back up. Chambers locked him in a headlock, wanting to swing his body out for the neckbreaker that would send Jackson back to the mat, and this time for good. Jackson countered it, pushing Chambers back into the turnbuckle, before leaping off of the second rope and onto the third. Leaping back, he caught the neck of Chambers, locked his arm around the head and clutched tightly around the neck before dropping the head back. 'Flashback!' the words of Mike Jones, the play by play announcer scream through his microphone and headsets. 'Its over!' his colleague Dennis Branch would retort as Jackson covered his foe. 1..2..Kickout. The odd hush by the crowd made it all the more defying of an order. Somehow, Jackson knew it when he closed his eyes that he wasn't the victor and the bell hadn't rung for a second time that night. Ron Chambers, kicked out of the 'Flashback', a mortal sin in NEW. Nobody kicked out of the leaping Reverse DDT, and Paul Hanson, the owner of NEW wasn't going to be pleased when he saw Chambers after the match. Looking down, Jackson found Chambers rolling along the mat, clutching his head in a feverish manner. 'What the hell are you doing?' Jackson gritted through his teeth as he began to argue with the ref. 'You're not supposed to kick out! We're supposed to go home after that!' Somehow, Chambers listened to Jackson, but it went out the other way, into a brick wall called devotion and desire. Ron Chambers wasn't going to lay down for anybody tonight - he'd put himself through too much to lose once again to Jackson. 'Fuck you,' Chambers uttered. 'They're loving this!' Eric grunted harsh, picking up Chambers with all he had left in him, going for another 'Flashback' that would surely put Chambers out for good and finally finish the match. He leaped once more, locking his arm around the head of Ron before dropping down. Only this time, Chambers held his ground before lifting Jackson up this time, dropping him back down with a powerbomb. Jackson clutched his back, the fans in total awe of what had occurred and wanted to cry. Little boys who had prided themselves on who Eric Jackson happened to be to them let it go, their tears running down until they couldn't hold it in anymore. Chambers went over to cover Jackson. 'Finish it,' the referee heard through a microphone piece placed in his ear earlier. 'finish it now!' Chambers lifted Jackson up once again, lifting him up and then slamming him back to the mat with authority with a Spinebuster. 'The hell are you doing Ron?' Jackson questioned as the running movement of the referee bouncing along the mat made it more and more obvious to him that this was to go on longer. And he was to take the rap for it. Chambers pushed his arms inward, lifting the leg of Jackson, before spinning into a figure four leglock. 'You're not going over Ron,' Jackson told him as he wailed his arms around, not wanting to be in the hold. 'We're fucking finishing this match, but NOT on your terms! Tonight isn't your night!' The attacker didn't care however, lifting Jackson up to complete something nobody had seen before in the career of Ron Chambers. It was Mike Chronos' finisher, the one he'd put away so many before hand around the country. Figure Four DDT. 'The hell was that?' Jones asked over his headset, completely stricken by the sheer performance of the move, he couldn't even say what it was. 'Doesn't Mike Chronos use that?' Branch asked him. 'Don't know, but whatever that was, Jackson isn't moving!' Jones screamed. The screams of the fans made it worse as they wanted their hero to get right back up, kick Chambers in the balls and then lay him out with another 'Flashback'. A booming round of boos rang around the Civic Center as Jackson laid motionless on the mat. Chambers smiled wickedly. 1...2...3. As academic as it was, nobody could hold back their words as 'Stairway to Heaven' cued up once again. The ring announcer made the decision as Chambers was handed the United States Championship by the referee. As the complete and utter sickness moved throughout the arena, somebody was distraught and another was completely in shock with the events. Eric Jackson felt both. As he picked himself up, helped by an official, the words of the ring announcer after the match made it ever more clear to him. 'Your new United States Champion...RON CHAMBERS!' Date: August 14th, 1977Place: Buffalo, New York (Hanson Civic Center)Time: 9:58 PM (EST) Soon, as the door to his office bursted open, Paul Hanson gulped hard as the lump in his throat was evident by it. Eric Jackson hadn't changed out of his wrestling gear, still stuck by what had occurred earlier. 'The hell was that?!' he barked at the owner of NEW with renewed vigor. 'He was supposed to go down when the first 'Flashback' landed his ass on the mat!' 'I know,' Hanson clutched his desk with a tight grip. 'I know.' he repeated. 'We were planning on this finish, puts you over in the Main Event scene nicely, more smooth for Chambers to have his run again.' 'No,' Jackson began to retort. 'you could have just told the fuck to get a draw on the both of us. He didn't listen and he tore away my heat with that Figure Four DDT!' Hanson nodded to Jackson. 'The World title, its now your time to go get it isn't it?' 'I wasn't finish with my US title run, it was perfect and now it got ruine -' The door slam shut behind the two men as Jackson turned his attention towards the door. Ron Chambers stepped through the door, dodging the body away from various photographers and interview men here for his face time. 'Thank you! Thank you!' he joyously nodded to the crowd, before turning to face the owner and the man he'd beaten only minutes ago. 'You just now explaining it to him?' Chambers asked, his point of view away from Jackson and towards that of Hanson. Hanson nodded, but Jackson spun Chambers by the shoulder and began to stare a hole into him. 'You know you weren't supposed to pin me. I've been champion too damn long to have you just try and take this from me.' Jackson didn't back up, his bulky 6'3' and 251 pound frame had been seen in magazines throughout Buffalo sporting and was maybe the sight of any woman he'd been an acquaintence of. 'Your times up.' Chambers smirked. 'Six years as the champ is good enough man, let go.' That title had been around the waist of Jackson since the age of nineteen, the youngest man to ever win a title in NEW's thirteen year history. And now, Chambers was insisting that Jackson was over his age? His blood boiled at the comment. 'So we're settled?' Hanson happened to interject between the two men as Jackson returned his stare into the face of Hanson. 'Whatever...' Jackson stormed off, causing a huff from Hanson as he leaned back in his chair. He turned his attention to Chambers, a solemn look on his face. 'You fucked up, you know that?' 'Better this way,' Hanson retorted. 'Way fucking better.'
