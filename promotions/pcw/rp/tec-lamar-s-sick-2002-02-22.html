TEC: Lamar's Sick

22 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2/22/02 “ACHOO!” The scene opens with Lamar lying sick in bed in the middle of The Cave Formerly Known as the Endcave. Lamar’s face is flushed and pallor, heavy bags under his red eyes. The bed has a wooden frame, with navy blue sheets covering Lamar’s entire body, save for the tips of his fingers that peek out from the sheets and his head. Hanging nearly out of Lamar’s mouth is a digital thermometer that measures his temperature, and the reading is holding steady at 103.8 degrees. That’s not good. Standing around the bed are Lamar’s friends: Richard (scruffy beard and curly black hair, zipped up brown leather jacket, gray denim pants, brown leather boots), Slade (clean-shaven and close-cropped hairstyle, blue shirt and jeans, Nikes), and Drek Redaxe (iron helmet with two 6-inch decorative horns, long brown hair going down the back, coming down the face as sideburns and meeting to form a moustache and beard, wolf’s fur tunic, bearskin boots, 2-foot bladed war-axe in right hand). Not present in the RP up to this point is The End. The End:“Why am I not present?” Handler:“YOU’RE NOT! NOW SHUT UP!” The End:“Fine. Geez.” As I was saying, Richard, Slade, and Drek Redaxe are standing around the bed, looking in pity at the pitiful Lamar. Richard touches the back of his hand against Lamar’s forehead. “Ow.” says Lamar, weakly. “Why did you slap me?” Handler:“He didn’t. You just misinterpreted the ambiance I wrote down. But you’re supposed to be sick and asleep anyway, so be quiet!” Just for that, I increase Lamar’s internal body temperature by one degree, which puts him in the category of “deathly ill.” Now, as I was saying, Richard touches the back of his hand against Lamar’s forehead to feel how hot he is. Lamar is burning up. Richard turns around to the other Misfits standing behind him. “Let us leave Lamar to rest,” he says in a somber voice. The other Misfits leave Lamar to rest, as I had Richard tell them to do. *Commencing Power Trip… Now* MWAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHA!!!!!!!! *Power Trip… Over* Upstairs, in End Manor— The End:“End Manor?” Handler:”Hey, I’m the writer, I should get some creative control here! Now, it’s End Manor and that’s final!”Upstairs, in End Manor, the other Misfits are sitting on a black leather sofa, which rests upon the clean white tile floor. In front of the sofa is a low glass-top coffee table with black metal frame. To the right of the sofa facing the table is a black leather loveseat. The walls are solid white, and the ceiling is Spanish-textured (you know, the building plaster wasn’t smoothed out). In the background, an authentic Renoir hangs upon the wall, along with some works from De La Vasquez. Another glass-top table, higher and used for dining, is about 10 feet behind the sofa. Fresh violets inside a black vase sit on a glass-top table. Six black cherry-wood chairs surround the rectangular table, placemats and decorative Japanese designed floral cloth napkins sprucing up the decor. The Misfits are all in a quiet and somber mood. Lamar’s illness, contracted during an outing to a Texas Rangers game, is quite serious. They don’t know what to do. There are no hospitals in the area that would admit a leper, and the Misfits don’t know how to treat Lamar themselves. Lamar’s childish laughter would once fill this room, but now it’s just sad and silent. Finally, Richard breaks the silence. “Richard is saddened by Lamar’s sickness,” he says, leaned toward the others from his position on the left end of the sofa with his arms propped on the back of the sofa. Drek looks over at Richard from the loveseat and says in his usual mean-sounding (though not always intentional) voice, “Since when did you start speaking about yourself in the third-person?” Richard returns Drek’s look and says, ”Since Richard’s handler made that decision. Please do not snap at Richard.” Slade, the sleepwalker who addresses people while operating on his subconscious mind, says something. “Ia oasihbdf oasdbfoeyrbfhj ciaubia ca ouasbciua scjk,” he says. Richard and Drek look at Slade with confused expressions. “Land of the Lepers?” Richard repeats in a query. Drek asks, “What would such a land as that be like?” Slade stands up and walks behind the sofa, where he paces back and forth, making random hand gestures and speaking in his incomprehensible (except to the other Misfits) tongue. Slade’s head hangs limply down at the floor, but he’s fully aware despite being asleep. “H iasbd jhdjfhabyc,” Slade says. “Rf iawcahb khad soiu ad ugasd fb jh x c ivsiasd.” Drek nods his head understandingly. Richard rubs his chin with his right hand. “So,” Richard says, “if we go to the Land of the Lepers, we can get Lamar his medicine so he’ll be alright….” Drek answers Richard’s self-question in the affirmative. “It sounds so,” Drek says. Slade stands between the sofa and the loveseat, leaning down slightly and propping himself on the armrests for the respective furniture. “Y IgdfI upb ybh,” says Slade, “oygoinsipf nadk uniuhb l.” Richard scratches his head. “But,” Richard says, reaching in his pockets and pulling out nothing but some dryer lint and a paper clip, “we’ll need money to get to The Land of the Lepers. And not one of us has a job.” Drek jumps down out of the high loveseat. With determination in his voice, he says, “We’ll get jobs then. Lamar needs his medicine.” Richard springs up and raises his left fist into the air, while looking off in its direction. This would be such a picture worthy moment. “Yes! Lamar’s our friend! If he needs medicine, we’ll get jobs! Right, Slade?” Slade at first does not answer. Drek and Richard look over at the sleepwalker and yell at his expectantly. “Slade!” they shout together. “L oasdk ae,” says the somnambulist. Richard looks back into the air, his fist still held high. “Okay,” he says. “It’s agreed that we’ll get jobs. Let’s all go out right now. I’ll write a note to The End telling him where we’ll be, but I don’t think he’ll need us, he’s got a match Friday against THE Multiple Man that he’s got to win, and then a match on Saturday against Nick Extreme in the GTT, followed by another match on Sunday night teaming with Drake Maxwell to take on Xavier Kannon and Bobby Stormbringer. He’s probably already on the road getting ready for his matches.” The End:“So, that’s where I am while all of this is going on.” Handler:“You’re not in this RP! Shut up!” Richard, who is not aware of the disruptions, continues speaking. “I’ll write a note letting The End know where we’ll be if he gets back before we do. In the meantime, let’s go!” Richard goes over to the table, which has a 5 x 3 inch and a blue ballpoint pen on it, and writes The End a note about their absence from End Manor for the next few RPs. Then, they all charge out the door and wait for the bus to come and pick them up. The next time we see the Misfits, they’ll have jobs (I planned it that way). Have fun until then!
