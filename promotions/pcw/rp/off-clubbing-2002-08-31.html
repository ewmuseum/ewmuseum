Off Clubbing

31 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 31/08/02 “Urgh.. Like, can you tell me WHY someone went and invented Cold?” With the familiar, whiny tone of PCW Commissioner Eleanor Kannon-Hall, our shot opens up on the little prep who is wrapped up in a thick woolly jumper, big coat and a bobble hat. She hugs herself and shivers as her teeth chatter. Eleanor : “I mean, like, HELLO?!?!? We already had hot and I didn’t hear anyone argue about it!” As we zoom away from her chapped, cold face we see that she is sitting beside her husband, Xavier Kannon on a small boat with one other man manning the engine and, being unaccustomed to the preps ways, he looks as confused as anyone else would. Eleanor : “I SO hope whoever gave us Cold gets the Nobel Prize for SUCK!” The boat that they are sitting in makes it’s way across the clear water, behind them huge and dense sheets of ice can be seen. Xavier, who is dressed snugly just like his wife looks at the ice sheets in glee, paying no attention to his wife much to her annoyance. Eleanor : “Hey, Xavier....” The Scientologist’s bright Green eyes are transfixed on the mesmerising expanses of ice.. Just above their floor a haze where the surface snow swirls around in the bitterly cold wind. Eleanor : “XAVI!“ She screams and nudges him, causing the distracted Gold Patron Meritorious to sway and nearly tip over the edge of the boat into the freezing water. He immediately thrusts his head around to yell at his wife, but as always her innocent looking face melts him and he simply sighs. Xavier : “Yes my little Poon Puppy...” He takes a quick look over the edge at the water and breathes a sigh of relief that he stayed in the boat. Eleanor : “Why are we, like, here?” As the prep looks away and gestures to the majestic environment around them Kannon goes to answer but she butts in before he can say a word, continuing on. Eleanor : “Not, like, as in “Why are we here?” on a Universal level.. That answer would be, like, WAY long and confusing.... And boring... I mean, why are we out here IN THE FREEZING COLD WHEN WE COULD SO BE SOMEWHERE NOT TOTALLY FREEZING!” As she bellows a him, not exactly taking to the cold climate around them, Kannon leans back to escape the loud brunt of her yell and again he nearly falls over the edge of the small boat and has to wave his arms around frantically to balance. Xavier : “DAMMIT! CAN’T YOU GET A BOAT WITH... WITH... WITH SIDES?!?!?!?“ The PCW Champion turns and rants at the man by the engine who ignores the Scientologist’s temperamental words and continues to pilot the boat to their destination. Xavier : “Well my little Fuck Ferret... After Fusion we’re running pretty low on down-right EVIL things to do, so we are having to “Broaden Our Horizons” as it were, to find something not very pleasant to do.” He looks at his wife as he speaks, hoping she’ll “Get It”. Xavier : “We’ve took over PCW, turned the Fan’s President against them, closed a Children’s Home to build a Mall, kicked a little girl out on the streets to whore for a living.... So I had to wrack my brains to think of what too do next.” Despite his clear and rather concise reasoning to why they are on a small and unstable looking boat on a freezing cold sea and surrounded by ice, Ellie~! still looks puzzled. Eleanor : “Since when has being cold been Evil?” He slaps his chapped forehead with a gloved hand as Ellie~! continues to question him.. Pondering his words. Eleanor : “If it was.. Then that Snowman I built when I was 8 would have been, like, SO evil!” Kannon and the Boats “Captain” exchange a glance... Both rolling their eyes. The CEO then puts his arm around his wife as she continues to wonder about the whole “Cold/Evil” relationship. Xavier : “How about I just say it’s a surprise?” This news wipes the concentrating expression right off Ellie~!’s face and replaces it with the first sign of enthusiasm we have seen so far. Eleanor : “YAY ME! Surprises, like, SO rule!” The boat begins to slow down and glide across the icy water to an ice sheet. Slowing to a crawl and gently bumping against the thick blanket of ice. Xavier : “Now, my little Shag Squirrel... All will be revealed.” They both stand up very carefully, not wanting to rock the boat too much as Kannon holds his wife’s hand while she steps out onto the ice.. Hopping off himself then turning to the man in the boat. Xavier : “Toss the bat, Pierre.” The man by the engine gives the thumbs up with his gloved hand then reaches down under the seats to pull out a wooden Baseball Bat that he throws to Kannon and it’s plucked out of the air with glee before the CEO gestures to Ellie~! the direction they are headed in. Eleanor : “Baseball? First Cold is Evil, and now, like, boring Sports are too?” They carry on walking across the ice sheet, braving the cold winds as Ellie~! sighs heavily and raises her frosted eyebrows. Eleanor : “This world is, like, WAY too confusing for me!” Through the snow, ice and frosty winds the 1st couple of PCW continue their trek until, as the winds die down and the snow stops shielding what is before them.... We see what they have come too. Eleanor : “Ah... That is, like, SO cute!!!” In front of the couple is a Seal breeding ground where the huge adults lay on the ice, intermingled with their cute, White, furry youth. Eleanor : “I’m still trying to, like, see how this is EVIL though...” Kannon’s eye shimmer and a smirk crosses his face and he begins to softly swing the Baseball bat into the palm of his left glove with glee when Ellie~! cottons on. Eleanor : “Ohhh, I like, SO get it now!” Xavier smiles and nods to her. Eleanor : “It’s evil because you’re going to let me have a cut little baby Seal and nobody else in PCW will have one!” She looks highly excited at the prospect of her new pet and bounces on the spot on the snow clapping with lustre. Eleanor : “That is SO EVIL!! YAY ELLIE~! and YAY BABY SEALS!!!” As she looks over the breeding grounds, trying to pick which one she wants, Kannon turns to her bemused. Xavier : “Um.. Ellie~!.... We’re not here to get you a Seal...” The face of the little prep morphs into a frown at the news and her bottom lip quivers as she points at the cutest baby Seal she can see.. It’s big Black eyes looking right at her pale Blue pair. Xavier : “We’re EVIL... We have to do something else HORRIBLE... We’re Here to CLUB baby Seals, not take them home as pets!” As he exclaims to his wife he swings the bat down into the snow to symbolise it and her face turns from one of disappointment to slightly angry shock. Eleanor : “WHAT?!?!?” As Kannon keeps demonstrating his Seal Clubbing swing Ellie~! quickly grabs the bat from him and wields it. Xavier : “Yeah, like tha-ARGHHHHHH!” The angry prep then swings the bat with all her might.. Luckily for Kannon that isn’t much.. Right at his left thigh and the Scientologist grabs his leg as his wife keeps swinging. Eleanor : “We...” *WHACK* Eleanor : “Like....” *WHACK* Eleanor : “SO...” *WHACK* Eleanor : “Aren’t doing that!” *WHACK* *WHACK* *WHACK* The Scientologist starts to back away from Ellie~! who still brandishes the weapon and points to the baby Seal she wants. Eleanor : “Now.. Go get me that one...” Seeing that his wife isn’t going to give in, Kannon drops his head and sighs at the death of his evil scheme and wanders over towards the baby Seal as our shot fades out on the hen-pecked Scientologist about to get pounced on by the Seal’s parent... Kannon just noticing as we hit Black. “ARGHHHHHHHHHHHHHH!!!!!“
