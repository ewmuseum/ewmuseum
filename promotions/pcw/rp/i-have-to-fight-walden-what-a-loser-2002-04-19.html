I have to fight Walden? What a loser.

19 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/19/02 -The camera opens up on Duke Williams who is in Gold’s Gym in Chicago, IL. His trainer, a big black man in a pink jump suit name Mac stands over him. Duke is wearing shorts, a Jon Kano tank top, and a Bears hat. Duke picks up and puts down four hundred pounds on the bench press.- Mac: Come on Williams three more times. Williams: Three more times?? What the f*ck are you talking about? I’m not training for the Olympics I’m a fighter not a weightlifter. Mac: Well we need to get you ready for a full time schedule, its time for your glass of carrot juice. -An other trainer hands Man a glass full of orange liquid. Duke pounds it down and makes a face when he finishes.- Williams: That tastes like’s Elli’s p*ssy, Hoss!! Foul baby, foul!! -Duke pulls a flask out of his gym bag and washes the terrible taste out of his mouth with some Tennessee pride Jack Daniels.- Mac: Duke you need to lay off the whiskey. -Duke gives Mac the one finger salute.- Williams: F*ck that Hoss, my wife and I are fighting their ain’t a chance in the world I stop drinking. -A PCW camera crew walks in from the back door. Mac greets them as the camera man hands him a piece of paper.- Mac: Duke you got your first singles match in the PCW!!!! Williams: Oh I can’t wait to get my hands on that mope Kannon. -Mac’s face sours.- Williams: Its not Kannon. Williams: Ok then Nelson needs to wear gloves when fighting me I don’t want to catch his aids. Mac: No, No, not Nelson. -Duke stops stretching and gets attentive.- Williams: Well who then?? Mac: Tim Walden!! -Duke sighs as he reaches for his flask.- Williams: Tim Walden?? -Duke is visibly upset as he turns to the camera man.- Williams: Tim Walden?? He stands about six-foot? -The camera guy shakes his head yes.- Williams: Weighs about two thirty, two forty? CameraGuy: Uh-ha Williams: Ugly bastards looks like maybe his family swung from trees? CameraGuy: Sure. Williams: The Personal Director? The meddler? The Invincible one? The kid who’s about half my age? The loser who thinks a wife beater T-shirt is a fashion statement? CameraGuy: That would be the guy. Williams: You want to turn on your camera, stick it in my face, and find out what I think of him?? CameraGuy: That’s the plan. Williams: Well ultimately I think he’s a fag!! -The camera man just stares at William- Williams: Well get the f*cking thing rolling hoss!! -The camera man gives Duke the count down to start his promo then the red light goes on- Williams: So I see the PCW has stuck me in the ring with that glorified nothing Tim Walden. Timmy you like to fire folk I see. Take away their income, stop supporting their family’s, leave them with nothing. After all you are the personal director!! I bet that makes you feel proud. Like a real man. A mean after all you’ve spent your whole character following and riding the coat tales of one wrestler after an other. You’re the definition of boy wonder!! PCW’s version of Ed McMahon. -Duke does his Ed impersonation, poorly I may add.- Williams: YES!!! YOU ARE CORRECT SIR!! -The smile drops from Duke’s face.- Williams: This is no time for levity hoss!! Hell I don’t even need to crack jokes when fighting a walking punch line. Tim Walden, it is a disgrace that you’re my first singles match back!! I see yet again your riding the coat tails of somebody by joining up with the UA. Are they going to cover you ass in this match? Does you twat worry about getting in the ring with me? I bet it does you f*ggot. I real man would go one on one with Duke Williams. A real man would leave the boys in the back and fight bell to bell. You however as history tells us, are nothing more then a follower and a p*ssy. -Duke walks over to his flask and takes a sip of the tasty beverage.- Williams: Does that make you mad or have you accepted the fact that your just a pawn?? Have you reached the conclusion that you are noting more then a lackey and a stepping stone for the rest of PCW? Or are you that f*cking stupid that it never crossed your mind? I personally think you sit somewhere in the middle as the lost Hoss that you are. -Duke smiles as he thinks how pathetic Walden is.- Williams: Walden, I’m on a mission to put the Ultimate Anals out of business starting with you skippy. So prepare your self, tell your boys in the back to get ready to save your ass, cus kid your going WHISKEY BENT AND HELL BOUND!!! HOOOOOOOSSSSSSSSSSSSSSSSSSSSS -Duke makes the cut motion with his finger.- Cameraman: Ok that’s a wrap. Williams: That sucked!!! -Duke shakes his head- Mac: It went well I thought. Williams: That sucked balls, it was about as good as a Walden promo. Mac: Come on it wasn’t that bad. Williams: All it was missing was banter with the Camera Man!! Cameraman: I could say something if you want. Williams: Nothing is more pathetic then seeing the camera man talk in a promo. Cameraman: Oh. -The camera man finishes packing up his stuff and exits.- Williams: This sucks!! First I get stuck fighting this no talent hump, my wife and I get into a fight, Kannon is still alive, and the senate won’t drill in Alaska. What’s next, the second coming of Christ? -Mac hands Duke today’s paper.- Williams: A Cosby show reunion? Jesus. Mac: Come on Duke cheer up I’ll let you off early. Williams: Can we hit the titty bar?? Mac: Sure. Williams: Oh hell yes!! It’s Malice Time!!! -The camera fades-
