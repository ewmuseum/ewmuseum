'For you.'

Date: #2 for Luke (you know it is) The scene returns back to the dojo home of the Black Dragon Master, Inphino Blitz. Sado Island is peaceful, even so at night. Crickets and cicadas put on a natural symphony under the cover of darkness. And with the sky painted with speckles of light around the sight of the full moon, one couldn’t ask for a more soothing, peaceful, even romantic scene. At least, Mina de Margo thought it was the perfect setting. She and her best friend and client, Blitz, sat on the wooden deck outside their parlor, the light from inside giving a cozy warm glow on them from behind. Mina sat cross-legged on the deck, her sweats pulled up to her knees so her bare feet and legs could get some of the night air as they dangled over the edge of the deck. Her arms braced herself with palms flat as she looked up into the sky. Such a beautiful night, she thought… “You know,” she began in a soft voice slowly. “It’s times like these that I’m proud of the choices I’ve made in life. I mean, if I think about what would’ve happened if I never met you, I probably would’ve been off… doing… something else… that’s not this…” Her brow wrinkled as her statement shut down quicker than Inphino shutting down the opposition… sorry Luke. She pretty much realized that you don’t make statements… without thinking them through entirely enough. That’s what you call one of them… wise decisions. Some would say that Luke wasn’t thinking the situation through entirely. He was thinking more with his heart and with his confidence in himself rather than with his brain, so Mina had thought before. That was fine and dandy, but like what just happened here, when you try and do something without thinking it through… you kinda get shut down, if not by your own brain, then by someone else… who’s probably the main reason why you should’ve thought things through. “I uh, I mean…” now she was just looking stupid stammering in front of Blitz. “I think… if I never became your manager… then I’d probably be an actress or something else famous. Probably off with Rai-kun most likely, before I met you that’s all I knew about, Rai and the streets.” She looked at him, and her brow wrinkled a bit. “Yeah, so I’d probably be off making porn with Rai’s company… you know, Raizo Tenzan, and the yakuza… and he’d probably be screwin’ me ragged, ya think?” “What?” Inphino’s voice sounded bothered. “Geez, Mina…” She sat up and folded her arms. “Well what do you expect? When you don’t listen I’ma start sayin’ stuff to make sure you hear me.” By her side of course, Blitz sat Indian style, his toned arms folded across his chest as he leaned hunched over slightly. His hair hung in his face, which didn’t matter anyways because his eyes were closed. “I was listening the whole time. Don’t assume I’m not listening just because I don’t respond to every little thing you say.” “Fine.” And so the silence returned. But not for long, as Mina never knew when to shut up… “So, what were you thinking about before?” “Before when?” She rolled her eyes. “Back in the parlor. You were thinking about something pretty hard to only listen to me halfway.” “Oh,” he muttered. “I was just pondering the Propaganda/Sagar situation some.” “That’s it?” she huffed as she looked off into the woods around them. “I think you’re completely overanalyzing the situation. It’s classic case of big dumb retard and little tyrant controlling said big dumb retard. Simple as that.” He shook his head stiffly. “No… I think it might be more than that… but the situation requires more thought. Maybe my newshound can sniff around…” Another roll of the eyes from Mina, “I’ll call her in the morning.” He sensed the tone in her voice. He heard it quite often lately. “Something troubling you?” She huffed as she turned towards him. “You’re putting a lot of focus into Propaganda, and not on the match coming up with Luke Chadbourne.” “Oh yeah…” he responded as his brow wrinkled high. “Don’t worry; I go this covered. But I’m pondering this Propaganda situation because… well, just because it seems so familiar to me. I just can’t put my finger on it yet…” “It’s familiar because it seems like the giants’ got all body and no brain, so there’s some little guy to come in and take the job. Ivan and Alexei, Penance and Solomon or whatever, Hessian and Minion.” Slowly Blitz lifted his head up. He turned towards Mina, and they locked eyes for a moment. “Akiyama and Cyclonica.” The thought came to them at the same time, the two’s eyes lingering for a moment before they returned their gaze to the ground. “Yeah,” Blitz started up again. “I thought that setup looked familiar. Brainwashed beast and the charismatic voice behind him.” “At least the ol’ Divine Prophetess is entertaining…” Mina smirked as she leaned back on her arms again. “Sagar’s getting downright repetitious with his hypocritical preaching.” “Her twisted libido was starting to get out of hand though,” Inphino stated, glancing off into the distance towards the infamous Jahana Castle, home of the nVg and Divine Prophetess about half a mile away. “I think her mind was becoming too chaotic, so she had to find peace in others… mainly in others’ bodies.” “Yeah that was one sick twisted bitch…” Mina sighed, her eyes looking back up to the stars. “I wonder what would’ve happened if Akiyama wasn’t around. How things would be then. I bet you probably would’ve been PCW champion a LONG TIME ago, and probably made Hall of Fame at that.” He groaned a bit, like he was clearing his throat. Mina paid it no mind and continued. “Yeah, you’d probably be a bigger star than you are now, seeing since you’re like… acknowledged as being talented, but you’re still looked at as an underrated person. I mean people still treat you like you’re a midcarder for Pete’s sake. And as for me, I’d be one of the hottest managers in the world.” “Is that so?” She nodded with a grin. “Yup! But… I’m not complaining about how things turned out. I mean we’ve had a hell of a time thus far. And I bet things’ll only get better from here, as soon as you beat Luke Chadbourne,” she hinted, shooting a sly gaze in his direction. “I mean it really doesn’t matter to me if you’re as big as Ivan or Kannon or anyone else that people praise over you. As long as we’re doing what we want to do it’s fine. I’m happy with the way things are right now.” Her grin stayed plastered on her face as her eyes closed. But everything changed with the few words that came from Inphino next. “… I’m not…” Mina’s eyes shot open, looking towards Inphino right away. He sat next to her with a stern look of disapproval on his face, eyes piercing into the darkness of the woods ahead of them. Mina sat up, placing her hands on her thighs and gave him a puzzled look. “What… what do you mean?” Inphino kept his gaze forward as he sighed deeply. “Ever since I left my dojo when I was younger to venture into this world, my happiness has left me more and more. Even now, I find it hard to think of moments where I’m truly content with my life. When I was younger I found happiness in the simple things, my training, the pride I took in honing my skills, but now, it doesn’t seem as meaningful as before. It only seems like a dance now, that I practice only so that it’s not forgotten. There was a time when I took great pleasure in battle, the opportunity to test my skills against others. That was the only time I was happy… when I was fighting. But now… I don’t know if that even exists. It all becomes so… predictable… and there’s a point where I stop caring and lose focus. That’s probably how Propaganda defeated me, and that’s probably how Luke could beat me. Either way, beating Luke Chadbourne won’t make a difference to me, because victory and defeat make no difference at this point. It’s all empty… I am empty.” Mina stared at him with concerned eyes. She’d never heard anything like this come out of Inphino, not in the few years that they’ve been working together. She watched as Inphino stood up and stepped down from the deck, his back turned towards her. “I may or may not defeat Luke Chadbourne… that depends on the amount of focus I have that night. But to win, and to advance in this tournament won’t be an accomplishment in my mind. I have no reason to fight… and without a reason to fight, there’s little reason for me to win. I’m sorry…” He started to walk across the yard, but Mina pushed herself down from the deck and followed him a few paces. “You want a reason to win?” her voice was cracking a bit, but she managed to keep it firm. “Win because I’m asking you to win. Do it for me, if you don’t believe that you can even do it for yourself. Can you do that, Inphino Blitz?” He glanced back slightly, locks of hair in his face. He could see the emotion on her face; the fists balled at her side trying to keep it together, the rigid posture. With a sigh, he gave her a short nod. “For you.” And with that said and done, the Black Dragon Master walked away from Mina de Margo. Ahead of him was his training hall, a two story structure with only two rooms, a back room and a wide open hall for training. Inphino stepped into the hall and flicked a switch, sending low lights glowing softly from the 4 corners of the room, and one from the ceiling. Walking across the padded floor barefoot, he stood in front of the back wall, where a pair of sliding doors awaited him. It was behind those sliding doors that revealed a small room with a shrine placed against the back wall. Inphino lit a stick of incense and placed it before the shrine. Then he knelt before it on his knees, placing his palms facedown on his thighs. He lowered his eyes and then his head… and his motion ceased as a steady flow of breath entered and exited him. Mina watched him across the hall at the entrance. There was something broken inside Inphino, and the only way he knew how to fix it was by meditation. She only hoped that things would be fixed in time, so it wouldn’t hinder him from competing against Luke Chadbourne…
