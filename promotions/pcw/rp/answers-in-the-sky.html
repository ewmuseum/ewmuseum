Date: 3/6/2004 Mmm...How beautiful. The same shit, just another day. Mike had to wonder why he even bothered anymore. What was his motivation within this world of retardation that he walked through? It was nice out, that was obvious for the bright light emitted from the sun filtered through talk Oak and Dogwood trees. The area around him glistened gently in the light, early morning dew and the happy sun mixing together to cause quite a sight for disillusioned eyes, but pretty shit could only go so far to improve a mood which had been set in the hardest of stone... The journey refered to as life had been a cruel one so far. Twenty one years of greif had been forced upon this man, whom simply appeared not to give a shit anymore, and it truly showed. From the dejected look upon his face, to the sag in his shoulders, Mike looked to be a defeated man. Assulted by years of untold torments and battered by the unforgiving perfection which he constantly forced upon himself. The training, the proper eating, the sleepless nights; all of it focused upon a single goal: To redeem himself. Mike slowly walked along the path, closing his eyes as he thought all the way back to his childhood. A breeze rolled by, making Mike pull his duster a tad bit closer to his form out of reflex. The skies were changing, although Mike was too deep in self-analyziation thought to notice, or infact truly care. As the first few drops of a spring shower began to desend upon him, it slowly became clear to him, why he was here in the first place. April 24th, 1997. 'What a way to kick off the teen years', Mike silently thought to himself as he stared into the bathroom mirror, looking at the bloodied boy who stood before him, painfully realising that it was infact himself. Another fight after school, which left his forehead bloodied as well as his nose. Oh, if only that were the end of it, though. His father, that drunken fuck of a man, looked down upon Mike; seeing a worthless human being rather than a son. His father, using his Jack Daniels rotted brain for something other than lifting a bottle, believed that maybe a few dozen smacks with a belt across the body would toughen up the wimp. Ah, don't you love drunken logic? Mike looked over himself in that mirror slowly, the tears of pain long since stopped since the display of violence and hatred toward his physical being. Bruises, blood and the long, wide welts left by repeated slaps of hard leather against young skin. In a twisted way, he found how he looked to be funny, like he had become the art work of a demented Picasso: His skin a canvas and his blood, a drying crimson paint. When Mike realized what he was thinking, he nearly threw up...Did he..Enjoy this pain? He gave it no more thought as he quickly got in the shower, finding himself to feel suddenly so dirty..So very dirty. Up in his room now, Mike sat by the window; pale moonlight casting a warm glow upon his youthful, albeit slightly bandaged face. The stars seemed so beautiful, just hanging there out of reach, untouched by any impurities..Lucky bastards. They were free, unlike himself. A modern day slave, bound into captivity by those stronger than himself. Their vulgar displays of power, which seemed to be there only to stroke their own pathetic ego's, focused on the beating of a child smaller and weaker than them. Sickening. Slowly, as Mike gazed off into the stars, he knew this couldn't be all there was for him..There had to be more, he just had to reach out and take it. He had to elevate himself to a status in which he would be like the stars which float above him, shining in all their heavenly glory. Current day-2004 It was all clear to him now, through the past year of wondering aimlessly, his true goal was before him..He had proven his strength to the world, as well as his relentless desire to become something great. Now he had that chance. Would he rise to the occasion, or falter like the nobody the specters of his past had made him out to be..That he was left to wonder as he stood within the rain, pale blue eyes staring off into the grey skies above...
