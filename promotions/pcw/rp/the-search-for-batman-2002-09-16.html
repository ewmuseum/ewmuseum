The Search For..... Batman?!?

16 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 16 - 09 - 02 Prologue: Our last encounter with the infamous city of Gotham saw a trio of super villains hatching a cunning plan centred around the retrieval of one of their comrades and the inevitable downfall of her captor, namely the Neo Void God Akiyama. An ominous car screeches along a winding dirt trail as the full moon glares over the city of Gotham. The stars sit motionless in the night sky, twinkling randomly, navigating those at sea but it is the activities on land that are kept under a watchful eye. The car continues to hurtle forward as it draws closer to a mansion that overlooks the suburbia below, obstructed only by large metallic gates used for security purposes. The gates seem reluctant to allow entry when suddenly they draw apart under a deafening creak and the automobile ventures on into the grounds of the building. The identified Cadillac crept up to the architectural masterpiece that gloomed over its flourishing surroundings and finally stopped allowing a party of three to exit. Again the Asylum masquerades as Gotham's leading super villains with Gaz 'The Jester' Green playing the part of the Joker, dressed in a dazzling suit, speckled with a array of colours and his towering compadres Nerezza concealed beneath a glass dome head guard continuing his portrayal of Mr. Freeze. Alexander Miyagawa cockily struts into view, languishing behind the collective known as Pranks With Attitude adorning a tight fitting green business tuxedo with question mark insignia's inserted all over. 'Explain to me again, why we are here?' hissed the behemoth Mr. Freeze. '.... We've been over this a hundred times already!' replied Gaz Green, sighing in the process. 'Well make it a hundred and one times then?!?' roared the cold-blooded assassin as he stared hard at his colleague. 'Ok - This isn't getting us no-where. We are here to form a temporary alliance with our enemy to ensure the safe return of Anar... Cat Woman and with his help we shall eradicate the thorn's in our sides, namely Akiyama and the Front Office!!!' injected the king of confusion, The Riddler. Resembling a scene from 'Reservoir Dogs' the evil of Gotham match forward in unison. Gaz taking lead grasps the brass knocker and crashes it against the door with a firm thud. - Scene switches to a view of the interior - Inside the place showcases taste with various expensive furnishings on display, ranging from coats of arms to pieces of art aligning the walls. A middle-aged gentleman sits across a long stretching table browsing over the tabloids under the light of an array of candles and then without warning he stood erect as the knocker crashed for a second time. 'Alfred could you please answer the door....' ordered the man with an out-stretched arm. 'Yes, Master Wayne!' retorted the lowly butler. The butler shuffled towards the door and finally opened it. His eyes grew wider before regaining his composure and addressing the three stooges, who remain obscured from view. 'Aren't you a little old for trick or treat? And somewhat early....' groaned the paid help. 'We would like to speak to the Lord of the manor, Mr. Bruce Wayne!!!' proclaimed the emerald menace pushing past Alfred and imposing himself on the master of the house. The three assailants stood before their nemesis in a Western style stalemate, neither wanting to back down to the other. The seconds began to feel like minutes and finally an irate Bruce Wayne bellowed a response to the events transpiring before his very eyes. 'WHAT IS THE MEANING OF THIS?' 'I know we have had our differences in the past but we wish to bury the hatchet so to speak...' murmured the Asylum leader Alexander Miyagawa, still in full-blown portrayal of The Riddler. 'Is this some kind of joke....' continued Bruce Wayne with a dumbfounded glare. 'No - We come before you, to offer a truce. Batman always stood for justice well it is now us who has been victimised courtesy of a new evil' groaned the titanic Mr. Freeze, played by PCW's vampire Nerezza. 'A truce? I've never set eye's upon you until tonight and as for Batman I'm sure there's been a mistake!!!' said the Lord of the manor trying to fathom the whole episode. 'Please Mr. Wayne, your secret is among us close collective?' yelled Gaz Green, his eyes roaming over the splendor of his surroundings, absolute elegance. 'SECRET? This isn't about that trip to Thailand because as far as I was concerned that prostitute was female, I mean the operation was a success!' said an embarrassed Bruce Wayne, his butler walking away in disgust. 'HUH!?!' sighed the three super villains in unison. Gaz going one further began to choke uncontrollably as though he were going to be sick, at one point wiping the taste from his mouth with the palm of his open hand. 'Well if it wasn't Thailand then....' whispered the millionaire. 'Ok - I knew it was wrong to get that orphanage closed and to sell the children as cheap labour in a third world country but I'm a businessman for crying out loud' 'YOUR A TYRANT~!' screamed The Riddler lunging forward as though to throttle his host. The two members of Pranks With Attitude sat their co-worker down in order to ease his built up rage that filters across his face. They comfort him momentarily, calming him to the point where he feels at peace to simply grind his teeth and revert back to the helpless Bruce Wayne. 'And again NO. We're talking about your secret where you are Batman....' questioned The Joker. 'ME?' replied Bruce Wayne before breaking into a mass of hysterics. 'Your joking right?' continued the supposed cape crusader. 'No need to be modest, Bruce, we know almost everything about you!' calmly said Mr. Freeze, his personality almost as cold as his body temperature. The Joker slowly walked over to a towering bookcase and grabbed a random book hoping for a secret passage to be unveiled when suddenly the novels came crashing down like a torrent of water. The conniving comic then tried his luck at adjusting the sword on a coat of arms, which had similar effects with a thunderous roar of metal falling to the ground, showering the floor. 'MY HOUSE.....' cried Bruce, watching in horror as Gaz tried countless idea's all of which falling short of his desired mark. 'Gaz... I mean Joker I think we have the wrong Bruce Wayne?' yawned Mr. Freeze ushering to the open door, held ajar by the butler Alfred. 'No, his Bat Cave is around here somewhere...' hissed the Lord Of Laughter, knocking aside pieces of fine china, looking for the elusive button that would grant access to Batman's secret layer. The Riddler and Mr. Freeze dragged the insane comedian away from the premises under a hoard of abuse from the evil Bruce Wayne who peers over the aftermath. 'YOUR PROBABLY AFTER THAT WEIRDO BRUCE BANNER DOWN THE DAMN ROUND...' 'I HAVE A GOOD MIND TO PHONE THE POLICE, NO WAIT, THE NATIONAL GUARDS!!!' 'THERE WON'T BE ANOTHER COSTUME FREAK LEFT IN THIS CITY AFTER I SPEAK WITH COMMISHIONER GORDAN, YOU MARK MY WORDS~!' ranted the highly regarded Bruce Wayne. As the door shut courtesy of a gentle breeze, the roaring of an engine filtered over the dense silence like the Angel Of Death and the car shot off. Left were the smell of burnt tire and a millionaire wallowing over the destruction of his property by a hoard of crazed comic fans, probably under the influence of narcotics as far as he was concerned.
