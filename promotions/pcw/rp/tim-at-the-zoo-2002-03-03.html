Tim at the Zoo

3 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Title: Tim at the Zoo Featuring: Tim Walden Date: March 3, 2002 (The scene opens upon a large brown building, viewed from afar, with paths strewn far around and about, to the left, to the right, one large, wide asphalt track extending for a hundred yards, through a veritable plantation of medium-sized trees to both sides, broken only by a river spanned by a wooden bridge about halfway to the building...A sign extends overhead, with pictures of animals upon it...Obviously, we are at a zoo, as people wander about the grounds in ordinary fashion, one standing out particularly in bright red-and-white cap as well as a black shirt and blue jeans...That, naturally, turns around to show that he is none other than repeated TV Title failure, Tim Walden, who looks up quickly to note the various sorts of wildlife that are to be showcased in this part of the zoo. He shrugs, and wanders down the path, the camera rushing to catch up with him..) Tim: Ah, what a beautiful morning, isn't it, Jeremiah? Jeremiah (we assume, and panting): Indeed it is, sir..I presume you're going to start your 'get on the Canadians' good side' project now? Tim: You betcha, Jerry! Why, I do believe there's already a woman stumbling, to assist in traversing the bridge a few meters ahead of me....And lucky me today, she's bearing a child at the moment! You tape this one, all right? Jeremiah: Very well, sir...Are you sure that this won't seem a bit overanxious to please in your videotaping it? Tim: Hey, if they can cheer something as immoral as that washed-up slime-bucket Big Daveylicious cheating as he usually does, I don't think anyone in Calgary'll have a problem with me videotaping my good deeds, then knocking some sense into Big D and the gang come Sunday...Now just watch me work, the ol' Waldencharm'll get me some new fans tonight! Jeremiah (sighing): If you say so, sir.... (The scene becomes distant from Tim, who rushes immediately forward, then steps in front of the woman, quickly making enthusiastic hand gestures as he's saying...whatever he's saying...He quickly takes a careful around the shoulders grip with his left arm on the woman, and begins to walk her across the bridge...She suddenly stops, then makes some enthusiastic hand gestures of her own, shaking slightly as she does so, Tim's eyes suddenly shooting wide with worry, as his hand motions become more stammering and briefly paced...He motions his arms forward slowly, as she continues to proceed...She suddenly falls to the bridge, making extremely panicky hand motions, as Tim enters a state of shock on his face, then quickly begins pacing back and forth with complete arm-waving motion taking place now...He says something, then rushes off quickly, as onlookers begin gathering at the now-somewhat-audible cries from the woman...The scene stops, with the word 'stop' appearing in fact, and we discover that it's being viewed from Tim's apartment, on the large-screen television in the main room....Tim is on the sofa, looking somewhat stunned by it.) Tim: Oh, how a good idea goes to waste when put into practice...I just wanted the Canadians to like me, not for a city that I've already been wrestling in to consider me a local hero for the day..I guess some things just don't work out the way one plans them...For instance, that TV Title match with Miyagawa..I couldn't pull out the magic that I had against Krayzie again, and I lost as I should have in that situation...IN SPITE of the illicit involvement of my somewhat close friend D-Kay Calyx in trying to influence the results just so he could get a title shot tomorrow night in St. Louis, a behaviour which I feel reflects badly both on himself, on me, and on the Wild Deuces as a group, behaviour which has been illegal since before the group's conception...I need to defeat him in the ring on Sunday, to teach him a lesson in fighting with grace and dignity, and in losing with the same. By the same token, it's imperative that I teach Big Daveylicious a similar lesson, as the rampant flagrant interference for the highest bidder that plagued us together in Thugs 4 Hire has remained dominant over his style of wrestling, cheap shots and things that would get people disqualified the order of the day, while superior technical wrestling skills have been overlooked by the man who jobbed to most of PCW's women and low-class wrestlers. Then, it gets to the people who don't matter so much to me..THE Multiple Man would be just another conquering of the same territory that I have consistently controlled on the mat in all of our in-ring encounters, so it would be like fighting Juan Borro again...Easy enough to defeat the first time, but after that becoming a matter of utter simplicity to dehabilitate the defenses of and render unable to reach upward any further..Afwa Kilimanjaro, the bigger, more unwieldy of PCW's Samoan contingent, bright enough to make his move into Management from an early start, but not so much enough that he realized that it would do nothing for his self-respect and such to keep fighting for them with Tiki torches and pineapples, to live up to all of the Samoan stereotypes, until Timo Bolamba showed him the way, and for that, I respect the man whom I defeated, thus making me the logical #1 contender for that OTHER federation's title...And you thought they had credibility! And lastly, there is Impact....What to say about Impact? He's dull, he can't wrestle up to the level of things removed from a septic tank, and he isn't worth any more of my breath to discuss...Now, if you'll excuse me... (Tim pulls out a videotape to his side marked 'PM Match'..) Tim: I'd like to watch my videotape in private. (The scene doesn't fade to black...Until Tim makes a thumb across the throat motion, at which point it obliges.) ---- View Tim Walden's Biography
