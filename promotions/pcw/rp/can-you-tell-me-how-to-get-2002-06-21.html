Can you tell me how to get....

21 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6/21 Sunny days sweeping up clouds away……….. -The familiar song plays as we visit the set of America’s most favorite street, Sesame Street. A human is working in the garden when Elmo walks by. Elmo seems overly excited as he’s hoping with joy.- Tina: Elmo what’s wrong? Elmo: Elmo is happy!!! Tina: Why, how come your so happy? Elmo: Wrestling is in town tonight! Tina: I know the PCW is here!! I can’t wait to watch all the action. Elmo: Do you think any of the wrestlers will sign autographs? Tina: Well actually my ex-husband is a wrestler. Elmo: Elmo doesn’t understand ex-husband? -Tina looks into the camera and smiles a nervous smile.- Tina: That’s a topic for another show. Elmo: So who is your ex? Tina: Duke Williams Elmo: Da Malice Man?? Tina: The one and only. Elmo: Will Elmo get to meet him? -Just then Duke Williams walks out of a cardboard house tucking his shirt into his pants. In his hand is a glass full of….carrot juice.- Elmo: Elmo scared. -Elmo hides behind Tina’s leg and starts shake as he peeks past her knees.- Elmo: He’s so big!! Williams: That’s why she married me!! -Duke winks at the camera through his chrome shades. Duke is wearing carpenter blue jeans, a Jordan Shabazz “good guys wear black” t-shirt, and a John Deere ball cap.- Williams: Its ok little fur ball I’m a nice guy. Elmo: Elmo scared you beat people up. Williams: Who the heck is Elmo? -Tina points at Elmo- Williams: Oh that third person syndrome he could be a wrestler. Elmo: Elmo can?? Williams: Sure you could. Elmo: Really? Williams: Sure, your about as tough as Kannon. Elmo: I am? Williams: Your as smart as the chef!! -Elmo starts to emerge from behind Tina’s leg.- Elmo: Elmo is as smart as the Chef!!! Williams: Most of all your more popular then Alexis Parshin!! Elmo: Elmo no know who that is. Williams: Exactly! Elmo: ELMO is more popular then Alexis Parshin!! You can by my likeness in toy stores and I’m on shirts. Alexis isn’t. Tina: Elmo nobody likes a showoff. Williams: We’re playing wresting. Its ok to pretend as long as you don’t act that when your not pretending. Try to cut a promo? Elmo: Promo like Ernie and Bert? Williams: No that’s homo….Ernie tried to show me his rubber ducky too. Lets just say you won’t have to worry about him for a while. -Duke smacks his fist into his hand.- Williams: A promo is where you show the world you mean business. Now if you a loser you don’t mention your opponent. If your good you’ll deliver some trash talk. Elmo: Should Elmo repeat the same stuff over and over like you do? -Duke bites his tongue to be respectful on the kids show.- Williams: Repetitive is one way to go. You don’t have to. -Elmo breaths heavy, flexes his muscles, turns his back to us, then spins around and lets loose.- Elmo: You know when they put Elmo on the TV the ratings skyrocket. Elmo is dangerous to competition. Mr. Rogers let me ask you this. When the last time tickle me rogers was the hottest selling Christmas toy? Here’s a hint. It starts with the letter of the day “n” as in never!! Say it with me old man. Never. Good!! All I can say is tickle this old man. I ran you into retirement. When I get into the ring with you in your return match we can work on numbers. Mainly me pinning you one….two…..three!!! -Out of nowhere a cloud of smoke arises.- Williams: What the heck? -A figure emerges from the smoke back wearing a cape with fangs hanging out of his mouth. It is The Count.- Count: Von Twoo Tree!!! You vant to count?? Then here I am. -The Count snaps his finger and more smoke arises. When it clears we see the count in a referees shirt.- Count: If anybody is going to count the treee it will be meeee!! I lot of us older Muppets are getting sick of little Elmo and his obnoxious voice. Vee have been around much longer and have respect for Mr. Rogers and his simple ways. While your COUNTing your money I’ll be COUNTing the treee on you. Elmo: Elmo says just bring it!! You can count all you want. As a matter of fact Elmo suggests you count backwards from this many!! -Elmo holds up five fingers.- Count: Dat vould be Five. Elmo: That’s pretty good!! That’s how long Elmo will give to get out of my face before I kick you butt!! -Elmo puts up his dukes as The Count vanishes into smoke.- Elmo: What a loser just want we need another dark gimmick. -From the back in runs a large Muppet and a smaller red one.- CookieMonster: Me Want Cookie!!!! -The red Muppet backs down the monster.- Telly: What he said Mr. Elmo is the Monster is back! CookieMonster: ME WANT COOKIE!!! Telly: He has just issued a challenge to you Elmo. CookieMonster: COOKIE MONSTER!! Telly: He wants in on the match with you and Rogers. Cookie: Ummm UMMMM COOKIES!! Telly: Watch your back Elmo the next big thing is here. Elmo: Elmo says the only thing he sees a cookie junkie and his fat big thing mouth piece. Telly: Now…… Elmo: Elmo says will you please be quite. You bring that cookie eating freak to the ring and I’ll beat him too then I’ll turn my attention to you red. Telly: Will see about that. -Telly guides the big cookie monster away.- Elmo: Was that good? Williams: That was a great job!! Elmo: Maybe I will be a wrestler!! Williams: Work out and get mean hoss and your on your way. Elmo: Hoss? Williams: Come watch Turmoil and see how its down when Kano, Steele and Williams send those other losers Apple Juice Bent and Heck Bound!! That kiddies is the honky tonk truth. -This promo is brought to you by the letters h,o,s s and the number 1,2,3. Camera fades.-
