Becoming Mad...

11 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5-11-03 A United Airline passenger jet appears as the scene opens up. Already in flight, the camera pans in through the clouds until it has found it’s way inside the plane itself. Focusing on keys of a keyboard, the camera begins to pick up what is being written as fingers quickly peck the keys in a rapid pattern. Stripping evident, lost foremost, apologies, but why?Standing, confronting, berating, but why?Truthfully lying, lying through your teeth, but smiling, eerily grimacing;Glancing beyond the target and past it’s words of sorrow and mistrust,Depleting into your own personal rendezvous with in a formidable solitude.Learning more, gaining knowledge, or pass it on, without feudJust leave it alone, don’t touch, hands off, already got burned once, remember?Flocking outward, sinking inward, both at once? Impossible, right?Closing towards stern, disappearance withheld, gone no sight.Say you were I, and I were you, can you imagine it any other way?Staring blankly, crying dryly, lost for words no more to sayAlone entrapped no eyes to see, casting into infinity for notI try but fail, fly to sail and maintain any delusions of grandeurYet to find me sitting here, where I began, typing, always typing.Type O, feel me now? Continue to look, I dare you.Are you fully aware of what it is your really might discover orIs your blindness your excuse for life.Just because you have eyes don’t mean you can see, see me.Forgot the frozen rope I threw you back a while ago? You should’ve because it never happened, remember? Nope.Striding fully beyond your reach, in attempt to outrun your wrathI find my self back again, typing, always typing, click, click, click (period)Ending punctuation, finalization, over addicted to pauses in life,When did existence become X’s and O’s, Flailing wildly out towards the light, the light I can only assume is youI find you in everything, and loathe everything as well.Tarnished, my soul rebounds to only be left to die again, wastedMatter, or is it madder, how about more mad, or sad trying not to,I did it, again, reminiscing of my typing, forgot for a minute but here I amThis must be me. Treated for a tough fight, trusted only by me.Lost in everything else, glimpses of past reel through my synapse,Only one, why not? If it is only one we need to live, or more..Seeing through glasses, dressed in a suit, 99 times I’ve foreseen my death,Why 99 because 100 would be plainly insane, too round, right?The people, sobbing, delivering from deliverance, gone beyond dead.Locked in my Prison with this ability to foresee, haven’t a clue, why me,Your future is locked and sealed, and I know, I know what people would pay to know,Back to Knowledge, always to what you know, not what you do.Life is a quest for knowledge, or experience, which ever you prefer.Leading myself as if I were my own witness, finalized, my thoughts.Individually different from everyone, everyone else, ever:Hence, why all and every being is significant in the ecosystem of life.Surrounded or drowned, flying high, or bottom feeding, know you role in the master plan.Play it, fulfill it, and be at peace when your body becomes the earth, and vice versaA lost memory is the only true definition of death, forgotten is spoiled splendor,And you part and disperse as your life, emblazoned on so many, or so you thought, Becomes forgotten with every sad eulogy spoken on your behalf, And thus, then and only then, you truly DIE. End Volume 1. Removing his glasses and wiping his sweat beaten brow, A.J. Trinidad relaxes his tense neck and upper back region as he places his lap top computer in his brief case along side the seat in this airplane headed for New York. Why New York, not even he knows for certain as a message was sent to him with only the knowledge that he would be met upon arrival by a neatly dressed man in a black suit, holding a purple folder. Sitting in coach, concealing his identity as best he can, nobody aboard has any idea whom this man really is, truly what his intentions are, or how disassembled his thought process and views on life. Yet the people fly on, caring for their children, lost in stigmatic trances locked into either their work or music, or if there music is work, then both, I guess. Closing his eyes he has about fifty more minutes in the air until his arrival at LaGuardia, no time like the present for a nap and an opportunity to repress some sickly odd thoughts conjured up in his most recent journal entry.
