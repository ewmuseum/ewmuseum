5 Days Of Dating Heck-Day 1

27 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7-27-2002 Day 1 Rent-A-Villain:'Of all the things I could be doing before preparing to sabotage the birthday bash of the nefarious nymphomaniac, Amy Campbell....things like drawing up plans, recruiting henchmen, BUILDING AN AWESOME SUPERWEAPON....what am I doing?' ..^../ Before the vile villain can even begin to answer his own question with an eerie darkness overtaking the now opening scene, an unrecognizable voice exclaims heartily as if he wanted to be heard by an audience ..^../Voice:'3...2....1!!' ..^../ Suddenly, the lights come up full, Rent-A-Villain's eyes blinded by the flashing cameras and the hot lights showering our spandex enwrapped fiend from above as our vile villain is sitting before a live studio audience filled with peoples of all races and creeds, clapping excitedly as a digital sign above them reads, 'Clap like mindless a**holes', cheesy party music setting the mood for what appears to be a game show. We find Rent-A-Villain sitting on a plush, looking slightly disenchanted with this situation as he sits across from a middle-aged man with pearly white teeth and slicked back hair, brandishing a gray suit with matching tie as he has his legs crossed, looking out to the audience while waving his hand to them, also sitting on a plush red couch. In the background is a large handpainted mural of Cupid and his infamous bow and arrow surrounding a large view screen in the middle, a large banner at the top of the stage reeading,'Two Of Hearts', the infamous dating show that never checks background info on their contestants. The music stops, the digital sign above the audience reading,'Shut the he** up' as the aforementioned man before, who looks to be the host, looks to the camera and says in a hearty tone ..^../ Host:'HELLO EVERYONE, AND WELCOME TO TWO OF HEARTS....the only show with guys and girls that will have you weak in the knees....and 99% guaranteed NOT to have STD's!!' *audience claps and cheers loudly, settling down so the host my continue* 'As you viewers at home know I'm your host, Chase James and tonight we have an exotic little prize for one of the three ladies here tonight who ends up on a date with him tonight....lets go to Heart-Vision shall we?'..^../ A digital sign reading,'Look at the screen like idiots' appears above the audience as they follow obediently it's order, the screen showing a picture of PCW's very own Rent-A-Villain, posing with his arms outstretched like a frightening creature...failing miserably I might add. Digital stats begin to run across the photo brandishing Rent-A-Villain in all his smiley face boxer wearing glory as Chase begins to read them off to the viewers ..^../Chase:'Rent-A-Villain is a full-time wrestling supervillain for the PCW, 22 years old, and coming off a bad relationship with in his words, 'a red-headed sex leech who used me for all my worth then went to screw a man with a monkey, breaking my heart in the process.' *audience seems a little troubled by that statement* 'Well...umm...she sure seems nice. His turn on's for women include,'Women who know how to play a variety of gaming systems, eeeeeeeeevil supervillainess replica costumes, evil laughs, and Gummi Bears, and 'booty-whoopering' villains who try to be more eeeeeevil then him...especially ones who's names start with 'S' and end with '-now', citizen.' His turn off's,'Redheads, baggy pants, girls with nicknames that end with 'Z', any girls named 'Amy', retarded monkey's, men named 'Reno', and Mettallica.' *Under his breath* 'We really need to start background checking.' *loudly, focused on Rent-Villain as the camera focuses on him and Chase now* 'So Rent-A-Villain, what do you have in mind for the lucky women who gets a date with you?' ..^../ Our vile villain scoots up on the couch, running his fingers through his streaked blonde and black hair before clearing his throat and addressing his plans in that villainous tone all his own ..^../ Rent-A-Villain:'Well...me and my date will probably spend a long, passionate night watching Dragonball Z reruns before attending....' *dosen't even want to say the redhead in question's name* 'SOMEONE'S, birthday party where I hope my damsel and I can have a good time...before ending our not with a nice, hot....bowl of apple and cinnamon oatmeal and some Twister! Then I'd pay her pay-per-view bill when she watches me beat down that vile Snow in a Jamaican jail cell. I mean who does Snow think he is, trying to be more malevolent and mischievous than me! My date will watch as I THRASH him with SUPER SAIEN STRIKES O'....EEEEEEEEVIL, which FAR surpass SUPER SAIEN STRIKES O' SILLINESS!!!' Chase:'Well.....that certainly sounds....ro...mantic....I guess.' ..^../ The digital screen above the audience flashes,'Laugh like dumba**es' as the audience laugh loudly to disguise how UNFUNNY the situation was at hand. Chase clears his throat and quickly tries to change the mood to a more festive one as he looks to the camera, flashing that million dollar smile as he exclaims ..^../Chase:'WELL LADIES AND GENTLEMAN...lets get to our THREE LOVELY contestants! First we have...'..^../ The Heart-Vision is overcome with a large photo of a beautiful woman with slightly pale skin, long dark hair reaching down to her firm backside, seductively luring red eyes, and the only thing that seems more deadly then her curvaceous body is her soft smile...which brandishes two fangs. ..^../ Chase:'This is Velina Mystique....single, 22 in her former life, and works for a local blood bank in the Transylvania area. Her turn on's are,'large veins, bats, dark cozy castles, oak coffins, and men who know how to suck better then she does.' Interesting indeed...and her turn off's,'Garlic, sunlight, crucifixes, holy water, religion, and men with large amounts of wood....en stakes.' She had this to say to you Rent-A-Villain...' ..^../ The stage speakers thundered with the soft and seductive tone of Velina, her heavy Transylvanian accent obvious as Rent-A-Villain gets a slight case of goosebumps from the very sound of her voice, Chase just smiling with a robotic fakeness as her voice message is played ..^../Velina:'Ohhh Rent-A-Villain, I'm looking forward to our meeting....hopefully in a secluded little place with VERY little light and no witnesses my dear....tell me, you like your neck sucked don't you?' ..^../ The crowd 'ooooo's' at that comment from the Transylvanian beauty, Rent-A-Villain rubbing his neck protectively as Chase begins to move into the next potential date, our vile villain saying under his breath ..^../ Rent-A-Villain:'She definitely won't be receiving a good night kiss...' Chase:'Your next potential date is Candy Jennings...she's 21, live's in New York City, New York and is an actress! Her cited works include 'Star Whores' and 'Something About Mary's Vagina'....wait...I think I may have those at home....I mean I mean, her turn on's are, 'Camera's, whip's, chains, whipped cream, body oils, hard co...' ummm....getting into her turn off's they include, 'Limpness, being tired, Viagra, and being in the same position....' ..^../ Chase motions off camera, where we find a pudgy stagehand. He rushes over to Chase after his gesture, lending him an ear as Chase whispers with a low brow pervertedness ..^../Chase:'Get me her number immediately...' *The stagehands nods, leaving abruptly as Chase looks up to Heart-Vision, realizing her picture hasn't been posted.* 'Where is her picture....James? James?!' ..^../ Chase gets up from his couch, angrily storming behind the background set, not coming out right away as it sparks the ire of one Rent-A-Villain. Our mischievous minion raises up from the comfort of the red plush couch, going behind the set as well, suddenly, a heart-stopping scream follows, both Chase and Rent-A-Villain walking out from behind the stage white in the face as if they were bulldogged into a pile of flour. Our fiend fixes his red and black vice-versa mask nervously as Chase fixes his suit, both men trying to come to grips with whatever they saw as Rent-A-Villain exclaims ..^../ Rent-A-Villain:'Who knew legs could bend like that...' Chase:'Well...it looks as if our production man is engaged in other affairs at the moment so let's go to our next date for Rent-A-Villain, Naomi Pyro. She's 22, makes her residence in Phoenix, Arizona and is between jobs at the moment. Her turn on's are,'Fire....pyro...inferno....and ummm....burning buildings.' *Looks over to the offstage stagehand* 'Double up on the insurance will ya?' *back to the audience* 'Her turn off's are,'firemen, water, anything nonflammable, and matches that don't stay lit.' Her message for you Rent-A-Villain....'Naomi:'Hi Rentie....I sure can't wait to spend some time with you...then I can show you my hehehe....match collection. Then maybe, we can watch a nice tall building buuuuurn all the way down to the ashes....hehe. Or maybe...I can...burn something...right now...' Rent-A-Villain: *sniff sniff* 'Rent-A-Senses are tingling......ummm...Chase....is someone barbecuing back here?' ..^.../ Sure enough, the set behind them is slowly catching ablaze, the digital sign above the audience reading,'Run like hell bi**hes!!' as the audience runs away in a startled frenzy, Chase jumping up out of his seat as well as Rent-A-Villain as they prepare to make a mad dash for the exit. But before they can escape, Chase exclaims ..^../ Chase:'WHICH ONE OF THESE WOMEN WILL GO CRAZY FOR RENT-A-VILLAIN....AND WHICH ONES ARE JUST CRAZY?! FIND OUT TOMORROW WHEN WE CATCH THIS VIVACIOUS VILLAIN OUT ON THREE DATES AND SEE WHICH ONE HE WANTS TO DATE AGAIN!! I'M CHASE JAMES EVERYONE AND REMEMBER....YOU CAN RECYCLE PAPER, PLASTIC, AND ALUMINUM...BUT YOU CAN'T RECYCLE A CONDOM!! GOOD NIGHT EVERYBODY!!' ..^../As the fire ablazes, the scene begins to close, our vile villain preparing to go out with THREE beautiful psycho's in an effort to find the perfect date for Amy Campbell's birthday party. WILL HE FIND THE GIRL OF HIS DREAMS, OR THE BI**H OF HIS NIGHTMARES?! WILL RENT-A-VILLAIN EVEN SURVIVE THESE THREE DATES TO EVEN APPEAR AT AMY'S BIRTHDAY?! WILL THE INSANELY DUMB SITUATIONS EVER END?! FIND OUT AS RENT-A-VILLAIN DOES THE SAME THING HE DOES EVERY OTHER RP....TRY TO TAKE OVER THE WORLD...ONE DUMB SCHEME AT A TIME!!
