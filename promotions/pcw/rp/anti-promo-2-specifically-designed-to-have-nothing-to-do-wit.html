Date: Bleh Rap... Or as Dante Jackson prefers... Battle-Rap. 'Cutting promos is the white man's answer to battle-rap,' he'd probably say. Though wrestling and promos for it have been around longer than the rap industry. But besides that, Dante Jackson brings the two together, battle-rap and promos. And the result is some jumble that more often than not does two things: first, it shows Dante's lack of ability to talk, rap, or think about anything other than his opponents; second, his opponents' IQ's drop 10 points each time they go near anything with his name on it. What Dante doesn't understand is that if you want to intimidate people, do it through a corny yet threatening name. I used to do that with 'The End' before I got some character development going grew out of that phase. Dante's still stuck in the same rut he always was. Another good name to use would be Darkstar. Simple, yet goth enough to make you afraid of him. So what if he's on a losing streak that odds say that continued on Turmoil when I beat him? It's still a cool name, you have to give him that. And anyways, battle-taps and cutting full promos against people is just a device; one used for making up for lack of... let's just say endowment. And I'm not talking just a little below average. As all the skanks and chickenheads will tell you, all rappers who rely solely on battle-raps have extremely small penises. We're talking so small that it techinically counts as a vagina. We're talking nanometers, here. Nanometers... Reminds me of the children's book I wrote about 8 years back. Short story, it explained the make-up of a human cell. Would you like to hear the story? Sure... Well, the main character in the story was named '0.07 Agent Nano Meters... but let's call him Dante Jackson instead. It's not really Dante Jackson, that just happens to be his name. You know, for symbolic purposes. But hey, this isn't a rip on Dante Jackson, it's for the kids. We have to keep them educated. The Chronicles of Agent Dante Jackson Washington, A.C. The Little Apple. This city provides the stage for one of the most daring, ingenious, and miniscule acts of espionage ever recorded. Though we do not know about it, there are microscopic spy molecules that steal genetic information from cells. One of them is Agent 0.07, Dante Jackson. We open inside the office of I.M. Tiny. The office is sparsely furnished, only a desk with an IMac and two chairs on either side. The desk and chairs are stainless steel, the IMac is green. The office is empty otherwise, with white walls and floors, no window and a simple glass door behind Dante Jackson, who sits opposite I.M. Dante Jackson wears a black tuxedo, I.M. Tiny wears a mink pimp coat that covers him up completely. Dante Jackson's brown face and prominent cheekbones give him a worldly look. I.M.'s pudgy face... yep, it's Horatio Sanz. 'Now listen, Agent Jackson,' says his employer, I.M. Tiny. 'Your mission is to infiltrate an undisturbed human cell and confiscate the genetic information inside.' 'You mean steal it's DNA?' responds Dante Jackson. 'Well... yes, but it has a more innocent ring to it when you word it the way I do. The scientists here will analyze the data and duplicate the cell. As always, you know the deal: the Secretary--' Dante Jackson points at the door with his thumb over his shoulder. 'You mean Charlene?' I.M. Tiny scratches his head. 'Yes. She will disregard all knowledge of your actions if you're caught, dissolved, digested, that sort of thing.' 'And I thought you didn't care about me...' Dante Jackson says with a chuckle. I.M. Tiny shakes his head in disapproval. 'Please stop fooling around. The cell may be defended, so be careful. Inside, you should meet up with one of our inside contacts. We have one stationed in every cell. He should give you information about the city and how you may complete your objective.' With a sigh, he continues. 'You have twelve hours to complete your mission. I realize that you are normally allowed a day or more to finish your assignment, but the window of opportunity will be closing fast. You'll understand why once you get out into the bloodstream. Good luck, Agent Meters.' 0.07 Dante Jackson leaves the headquarters of the secret spy organization MI 6 ^ -10. Flying around in his BMW Cell, he begins his search for a suitable cell to hijack. 'Bacterium. Virus. Amoeba. Virus. Virus. Virus. Virus. Virus. Doesn't this human have any healthy cells? Ah, there's one.' 0.07 flies through the bloodstream to a perfect specimen, the only cell around that isn't carrying a virus or just sickly in its own right. 'No wonder I only have 12 hours to complete my mission. Viruses are attacking the cells! Apparently this human, Totally Tubular Tommy, is infested with HIV or something! That's the reason why PCW doesn't get him involved in First-Blood matches, or hardcore matches!' [Editor's Note: Dante Jackson's last sentence was added in recently.] 0.07 sets about his task. He treis to gain entrance through the cell membrane, but he is not allowed access. With the harmful T-cells spreading, only the most important molecules are allowed to enter. Undaunted, Agent Dante Jackson spots a large oxygen molecule and flies in under it, below the sight of the guard stations. The cell membrane is opening to allow the oxygen to enter. 0.07 follows its movement as it comes in through the hole before the cell membrane closes. Once inside the cell, 0.07 assimilates into the crowd of molecules that are moving from place to place to perform their duties. In this city, you wouldn't know there was a viral invasion based on the demeanor of those within. Morale here is unexpectedly high, despite the situation just outside the city limits. 0.07 boards the Endoplasmic Reticulum Subway to get to the city faster. As he takes his seat and patiently waits for his stop, a small group of black-armorclad Cell Defenders enters the car. As an addes security measure, they are checking molecules again to make sure that no HIV T cells managed to sneak in. The Defenders are all the way on the other side of the subway car. As they check passengers for identification, Dante Jackson stays motionless and stares straight ahead. Being the only person on the subway dressed in a tuxedo instead of casual dress, he excites very little attention. Molecule by molecule (person by person), the Defenders draw nearer to Agent 0.07. He gets nervous, but his training has taught him never to lose his cool. His outside mannerisms have not changed. On the inside, he is a little worried. 'WHY HASN'T THIS FREAKING TRAIN STOPPED YET?!' Anticipating the arrival at the station ahead (the subway car noticeably begins to slow), Dante Jackson positions himself directly in front of the door. A Defender approaches him. 'Sir, may I see your identification, please?' asks the patrolling Defender. Dante Jackson nods his head with a smile. 'Yes, certainly.' Agent 0.07 begins to pat himself down in a pretend search, checking his person for an ID he doesn't have. Just then, the man sitting across from Dante Jackson anxiously rises from his seat and bolts for the door on his side of the car as the subway stops. Two other Defenders run after him and tackle him to the ground, the man turning out to be an HIV T-cell. When the Defender in front of Dante Jackson turn his head to assess the situation, 0.07 silently backs out of the door directly behind him. When the Defender turns around again, he is alone. In haste, he runs out of the subway. 'Hey, where did he go?' he asks. 'Where did who go?' replies his partner. 'The man I was just questioning?' 'I didn't see anybody. Get back on, it's about to leave.' The disappointed Defender slowly backs onto the subway, scanning the crowds of people but not seeing anyone like Dante Jackson. The doors close and the engine powers up. When the Defender turns his attention to someone else, Dante Jackson jumps down off the roof of the subway and checks his position. 'Subway Station 4. And I've got 11 hours left. I'd better see what the locals can tell me.' Dante Jackson stops a woman who is walking by. She is blond, white complexion. [Editor's Note: For this edition, we'll add in that she has a small ass, but large breasts. Just the way Dante likes them.] [Insert 3 minute long sex-scene here. We could make it longer, but that wouldn't be true to Dante Jackson's capabilities. That is, if the groupies are to be believed.] [Time compression: 3 hours. That's how long he apparently passes out for.] With 8 hours left to fulfill his objetive, he decides to ask that woman what he was going to ask her in the first place. 'Excuse me, but where should I go to get some tourist information.' The woman cocks her head sideways and says something in Spanish. Dante Jackson shrugs his shoulders. 'That explains why she kept screaming 'Aye, papi' 'Oh, mi Dios' 'Quien es su padre' and 'Como te llamas?' during the sex.' After going outside and asking someone who speaks English, Dante Jackson learns that the best place for information of any kind would be the Market Vacuole about ten minutes to the west of the cytoplasm expressway. 'It's right by the Golgi Apparatus Post Office.' Taking the protein carrier bus, Dante Jackson gets to the Market Vacuole with 7 hours and 40 minutes remaining. The area is a town square. Many people are walking around, frequenting the shops, or eating at one of the side restaurants; all of them, every single one of them are completely oblivious to the impending doom that awaits them if Dante Jackson cannot complete his mission in time. Deciding to get dinner first as the sun is getting low in the sky and he has about another hour before dark, Dante Jackson takes a seat at a table for one out by the street in front of an Italian0themed restaurant. A waiter comes over to him dressed in a red penguin suit. 'Welcome to Restaurant de Ribosome!' he says in a cordial voice. 'How may I be of service? Perhaps you'd be interested in looking at our menu?' The menu has 'I am your contact' scribbled in the corner. 'What do you have?' asks Dante Jackson. 'I'm hungry.' 'Well,' says the waiter, 'Slim Jim is $1, Laffy Taffy is $1.50, and Mountain Dew is $0.50.' 'You don't have anything else?' The waiter shrugs his shoulders. 'If Totally Tubular Tommy ate anything else, there might be a bigger selection. Anyway ,you can order those things separately; or, you can get all of it together in the special that costs $6.' Dante Jackson scratches his head. 'But they cost $3 bought separately...' 'That's what's so special about the special,' the waiter says with a grin. Shaking his head with a grin, Dante Jackson reaches into his tuxedo's inner left breast pocket and pulls out a cash clip, handing over $8. 'I'm in a good mood, so I'll give you a higher gratuity and order the special.' The waiter takes the money. 'Good choice, sir!' The waiter leaves. Agent Dante Jackson looks around and surveys the area. He see a park, post office, power plant, and two other buildings that he cannot identify. 'I'm back!' says the waiter, who took 20 seconds with the order, bringing back a lukewarm plate of a few Slim Jims, some Laffy Taffy, and a can of Mountain Dew. 'That was fast,' Dante Jackson says, taking the plate. 'To be honest, we keep this stuff just sitting around.' 'Good to know.' 'Anything else I can do for you?' 'Yes.' Dante Jackson says, swallowing some soda. 'What can you tell me about the city? Is there anything specific I should be seeing or doing?' The waiter responds with a wink. 'Well, that's the Golgi Apparatus Post Office. Their delivery service is called Fed Up. [/cheap joke] They deal with all the packaging and delivery of materials. Over there is the Mitochondrion Power Plant. It supplies the energy that the whole city needs. The smaller Mitochondrion in the distance gives power exclusively to the Nucleus Government Building, but power is rerouted to the main plant if our Mitochondrion goes down. 'In the government building, we have the Nucleolus city planning office, the public records office, and some special top secret records in the chromosomes. No on without porper authorization and the access key can see the chromosomes. The key is hidden somewhere in the post office. Some have tried to steal the chromosomes before, but they were fried by the nuclear envelope, an electrified force desgined to keep out intruders. That fence is deactivated during the power transference that occurs when the main Mitochondrion shuts off. You can only get in through the nuclear pores, and you need special clearance for that. I heard that there is a special key kept inside the post office that authorized personnel use to get into the chromosome storage room. As the waiter leaves to serve another customer, 0.07 quickly formulates a plan to gain access to the chromosomes. He gets up from the table and heads for the city's main Mitochondrion. Assuming the guis of a newspaper reporter, Dante Jackson asks the public relations director of the plant if he can take a look around by himself. Given that the plant has nothing to hide, he agrees to it so long as Dante Jackson does not bother any of the workers. 0.07 inconspicuously searches the building for the power transfer box that supplies the city with power. After having searched from top to bottom, Dante Jackson is about to give up when he catches sight of a tiny alcove underneat the stairs leading from the second floor to the third. The hallway is just barely wide enough for a single man to walk down. At the end of the smal corridor, he finds the power transfer box. He opens it and rips out the wire inside. 'FUCK!' goes Dante Jackson as 500,000 volts go through his body. [Editor's Note: 'Dangit' is to be replaced with 'FUCK!' when reading this story to children.] The plant goes dark. Groping along the wall, 0.07 finds his way back out of the plant. He rushes to the Golgi Apparatus Post Office to procure the internal security key needed to reach the chromosome storage room. Once at the post office, Agent Dante Jackson follows a worker with a flashlight behind the counter. In the back, the halls are empty. 0.07 feels the sight-imparied accomodating signs on the walls to locate the room housing the internal key. 'Internal Key Here...' Dante Jackson thinks. 'They aren't good at hiding things, are they?' he walks hurriedly to the room and obtains the key. When he exits the room, the power is restored. He tries to leave without being caught, but an employee sees him as he rounds the corner. 'Hey, what're you doing back here?' the Pimply-Face Employee Kid demands of Dante Jackson. 'I was looking for the bathroom when the lights went out,' 0.07 says, maintaining his composure. PFEK is fooled and says, 'Follow the hall behind me. Take the first left and it will be the door straight ahead of you.' The employee continues on his way. Agent Dante Jackson coolly walks out of the building as if nothing happened. 0.07 arrives at the nuclear envelope. The city's power is restored, so he picks up a rock and throws it at the fence. Nothing. The power from the Nucleus's Mitochondrion is powering the city, so the nuclear envelope is deactivated. 0.07 climbs the fence and sneaks up behind a security post to gain entrance into the government building. Inside, work continues as usual, with no indication of shared power between this building and the rest of the city. The lights are bright. The machines work fast an efficiently. Agent 0.07 sees a man going into the bathroom. Deciding he needs a change of clothes, he follows the man. That man enters a stall in the bathroom. 0.07 locks the bathroom door and hides in the one next to it. After the man is done and opens the stall, Dante Jackson jumps out and knocks out the man with one swift punch. Then he steals the man's dark blue blazer and hides his prone victim in a stall and locks it. When Dante Jackson comes out of the bathroom in his 'borrowed' plain gray suit, he approaches a secretary and identifies himself as a new resident to the city who would like to build a Mall Vacuole. Normally, he'd have to schedule an appointment, but because it had been a slow day, he is given a pass and allowed to take the elevator behind the secretary's desk (the secretary and desk are just metaphors for the box in the middle of the room that he had to talk into) up to the second floor. It is on this floor that the city planning records, public records, and chromosomes are kept. Totally ignoring the city-planning advisor, Dante Jackson heads for the public records office. He enters the room and heads for the far-left corner, trying not to look lost, when in actuality he has no idea what he's looking for or where to go. At the back of the room, he takes a quick look around trying to locate any conspicuous doors that might lead to the chromosome storage facility. He spies a promising door just to his right. He glides over to the door and sneaks inside. There is a small corridor with a single door at the end. Halfway to the other side, there is a small hole in the wall. Ignoring it, he continues through the hallway to the far end and enters the room. Then he kicks himself for having infiltrated the broom closet. Coming back out, he takes another look at the hole in the wall. It looks deliberate, someone meant for the hole to be there. Acting on intuition, Dante Jackson takes the internal security key from his pocket. He inserts the key and twists it counter-clockwise. His gut feeling pays off as a portion of the wall retracts and slides away. This secret door was hiding another door. Using the key again, he opens the door and steps into the next room. 0.07 grins with delight as he eyes the chromosome disks in front of him. Taking out a small bag he had in his pocket, he hastily collects the chromosomes, paying no attention to the security camera located directly above the door. Nothing happens at first, until Dante Jackson turns around and exposes his face to the cmaera. An alarm blares loudly as a low-pitched voice shouts into the PA system. 'Intruder in the CSR! Unauthorized access granted to intruder! SHOOT ON SIGHT!' Agent 0.07 collects the last chromosome disk and hurries out of the storage room, hiding his jacket in a file cabinet and tucking his small bag into his pocket. As he leaves the hallway, security is arriving at the door to the public records office. Dante Jackson sees a desk with some notes and a book on it. He quickly sits down and starts reading the book. Security runs by. As soon as the door is closed behind them, he gets up and walks out. He's in the elevator with the doors opening to the first floor when the guards come out yelling, 'HE'S NOT HERE!' 0.07 is about to leave when he sees more security guards searching people at the door. His instincts tell him to use the door on the right side of the room. Again, luck is on his side as he opens the door and steps out into the garage where a mail truck is about to depart. He wastes no time in boarding the truck before it leaves. At the gate, the truck is stopped by security. The guards check the driver first. He has nothing on him. Then they search the back of the truck. They look inside boxes, under piles of letters, and in cabinets userd for overnight mail. Nothing is found. The truck is cleared to leave the government facility. Once far enough away from the complex, Dante Jackson releases his grip from the truck's axles and rolls out of the road. Dante Jackson gloats by yelling back at the building. 'HA! BITCHES! Who's yo' fuckin' daddy?' Jackson doesn't notice the hundreds of red dots trained on his body, the snipers from the government building having a clear shot at him. 'Fire,' says the Head of Security. Dante Jackson died. He spent the time gloating, when he wasn't even finished with the job. Totally Tubular Tommy died. That HIV he got from the French circus mutant at the Versailles match turned into full-blown AIDS and he just wasted away into nothing. A sad story, yes. But I never said it wasn't. Have a nice day. Bye! *End*
