PCW's Number One Cruiserweight!

29 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4-29-03 ~ The scene opens up to Hank sitting in his apartment in Seattle, Washington lying on his long black leather couch. Hank staring at a picture nailed to his wall of him with the PCW Extreme Championship draped over his shoulder, no shit, just a pair of blue jeans and some blood running down from his face. Eventually Jenn enters the living room and sits in the chair right beside the couch. Jenn looks over at Hank and sighs. ~ Jenn: “What’s up?” Hank: “Nothing, just thinking on what was and what now is.” Jenn: “Huh?” Hank: “Thinking back upon my PCW past, which probably is a vital mistake, but I jus’ can’t help but think about how great I was back then and how washed up I am right now.” Jenn: “Look at your success Hank~! Just look at everything you have accomplished in your career as a professional wrestler.” Hank: “Keyword in all of that is ‘have’.” Jenn: “Well that’s jus’ even more incentive to stay. You have to prove you still have what it takes to captivate an audience.” Hank: “Yeah, yeah, and what win the cruiserweight match I have this Tuesday?” Jenn: “Or at least entertain the fans like you always do. You never used to care about winning before, what has gotten into you?” Hank: “Dunno, jus’ feel I’m missing something in my PCW comeback. I was only eighteen last time I was wit’ the PCW, now I’m what, twenty-two?” Jenn: “That’s not old; most newcomers are just breaking into the business at twenty-two.” Hank: “I’ve wrestled around but nothing compared to PCW talent.” Jenn: “I don’t like you when you’re like this Hank, show more confidence like the old Hank I remember falling in love with.” Hank: “Yeah then where’s Ganker so I can smack him around a little?” Jenn: (laughs) “Maybe you can convince PCW to resign Jobber’s Inc.” Hank: “Yeah and be forced to carry my team to a tag team title victory?” (laughs) “You may have a point.” Jenn: “Ganker’s been RSE Cruiserweight Champion!” Hank: “He was also forced to change his identity so he could obtain his first ever heavyweight and intercontinental title victory.” Jenn: “But still he’s still wrestling no matter what.” Hank: “I guess he jus’ has more heart than I do.” Jenn: “That’s not true or you wouldn’t have kept on trying to get back into the PCW. So why don’t you see if you can get PCW to resign Ganker and reform Jobber’s Inc?” Hank: “That’s not a bad idea. I do need a change and all.” Jenn: “Well anyway what about this cruiserweight match you’ve been thrown into for this Tuesday?” Hank: “What about it? I’m going to entertain, maybe even smash some people over the head wit’ some SoBe bottles. That seemed to put away Doug Troy pretty damn well.” Jenn: “It sure did.” Hank: “Hell I think that’s still talked about amongst the PCW roster, or those who remember it.” Jenn: “Those who were around to witness it.” Hank: “Yeah but all of them mainly ditched on out of here.” Jenn: “Yeah you seem to be one of the few PCW originals.” Hank: “That means I have to show these new school sons of bitches how it’s done right in that ring, how to do the job appropriately.” Jenn: “You don’t seem to have a problem with that so far.” (smiles) Hank: “Watch it you or you may have to ‘Go Down’ to the job.” (laughs) Jenn: “Really?” Hank: “Maybe.” (sits up as Jenn gets up and sits by him on the black leather couch, Hank places his arm around her) “So what keeps me from losing you? There must be billions upon billions of guys wanting to be wit’ you.” Jenn: (smiles) “Yeah, and sometimes I wonder why not leave you for them.” Hank: “HEY!” Jenn: “Only kidding, besides they don’t have what you have and that’s a passion for something you truly love to do.” Hank: “That’s the damn truth.” Jenn: “That and the only guys whom show they want me, only want me because they want me in their bed rub…” Hank: “Uh, nothing further on that subject. That’s more than enough.” (smiles) “Anyway I should head to the airport, you coming?” Jenn: “You know it!” Hank: “Then prepare to be bombarded by rednecks because we’re going to Mobile, Alabama. I mean no offense to them, but hell when you sleep with your sisters and cousins that is where I freaking draw the line! Hell my cousin lives down there and tried to sleep wit’ me the last time I went down there for a show!” Jenn: “Did you?” Hank: “Well I… NO! Of course not, that’s sick! AHGA~!” Jenn: “Relax it was only a question.” Hank: “One that already had an answer, or should have.” Jenn: “True.” Hank: “Well let’s jus’ go.” ~ Hank stands up and pulls Jenn to her feet as the two walk into Hank’s bedroom to pack. ~ ::Scene II:: ~ An hour or so after packing Jenn and Hank are on roundtrip to Mobile, Alabama. Hank has the window seat and Jenn has the aisle seat. Hank typing on his laptop as Jenn is reading a copy of Vogue she got at the airport, along with other miscellaneous magazines. Soon enough though Jenn sets her magazine down on her lap and lays her head on Hank’s shoulder, and Hank smiles. ~ Jenn: “Doing anything fun?” Hank: “Balancing my accounts.” Jenn: “I didn’t say making any scientific breakthroughs. I asked if you were doing anything fun!” (snickers) Hank: (laughs) “No, jus’ balancing. You know this career back in PCW may jus’ give me the funds I need to get out of debt?” Jenn: “You’re not in debt.” Hank: “A little, not a lot. I jus’ have some things I can’t pay off until this weekend when I get my paycheck from PCW.” Jenn: “How are you in debt?” Hank: “One of my friends loaned me some money and you know me, always have to payback old debts.” (smirks) Jenn: “Yeah, what friend?” Hank: (cough) “Ganker.” (cough) Jenn: “When did Ganker lend you some money?” Hank: “Awhile ago, but I jus’ never had the money to pay him back.” Jenn: “I’m sure he doesn’t want you to.” Hank: “They never do, but I do what is right and that’s paying back all my debts, no matter how old.” Jenn: “You’re weird that way.” Hank: “Yeah well live wit’ it.” (laughs) Jenn: “No, I live with you.” Hank: “Same thing.” Jenn: “True.” ~ Jenn falls asleep on Hank’s shoulder as Hank continues to type on his laptop. The scene cuts off to black. ~ ::Scene III:: ~ An hour or so later Hank and Jenn make their departure from the plane as it has landed. Hank with his arm around Jenn walks over to where their bags are. Hank grabs both his and Jenn’s and the two proceed to walk onward through the airport. Minutes later Hank and Jenn exit the airport and climb into the awaiting limo. Hank: “And we’re off to the arena in which I will job, job, and job some more!” Jenn: “Don’t be so down on yourself!” Hank: “Meh, we’re going to the hotel first anyway, I jus’ felt like saying that.” Jenn: “You’re such a clown, Hank.” (smiles) Hank: “Meh, it’s who I am. A natural born asshole.” Jenn: “Right.” (sarcastically) ~ Hank kisses Jenn as the scene cuts off to black. ~ OOC- Sorry so short.
