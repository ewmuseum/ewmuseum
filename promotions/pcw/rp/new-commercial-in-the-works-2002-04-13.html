New Commercial In The Works?

13 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/13/02 ((OOC: I know this is very late. I didn't realize until a few minutes ago that I had a match because I guess I was added on sometime this week. I know this won't count I just don't want to get fired. But this does teach me a lesson to check the cards every day. LoL)) Kramer had just recently come off of a victory over Rent A Hero, but it wasn't his own. Though he was deemed the winner of the match, he sure didn't feel like it due to Juan Borro's interference. Now take this match back a week or two and Kramer would be really upset. But for some unknown reason he couldn't give to licks about the whole situation. The only thing he cared about was that he had achieved victory. When Kramer went to the arena that night he knew that half of the people didn't like him, wanted him fired, or just thought he should fall into a turbine and that would be an end to him. But did Kramer care? Oddly enough no! He went in with a smile slapped across his face and left with a smile on his face. The scene opens up inside of KFC Enterprises. Sitting in the waiting room area which has a thick, plush red carpet, and pure white walls, are Kramer and Jason Alexander. Jason is wearing a plain white button down shirt with black suit pants, and Kramer is wearing a bright orange shirt reading 'Tang!', and a pair of dark blue jeans. Jason is busy flipping through the pages of Time Magazine, while Kramer is flipping through Teen Magazine. Suddenly a secretary wearing a white blouse and gray skirt comes waltzing up to the two men. Secretary: Mr. Sanders would like to see you know. Jason and Kramer stand to their feet as they follow the middle aged lady into an office. She opens the door for them and then closes it behind them. Sitting in a big oak chair is The Colonel himself. In his mouth is a chunky cigar which sprays small bits of ashes into his white beard every now and then. Colonel: Have a seat boys. They do as they are told without hesitating. The Colonel looks directly at Kramer after he sets his cigar down in an ashtray near his hand. Colonel: How are things going for you my boy? Kramer: Not bad. It could be better but overall not bad I suppose. The Colonel leans back with this answer and relaxes a bit as he looks at both men now. Colonel: I asked for ya'll to come because we have a situation on our hands. You see ever since last month KFC stock has dropped a dramatic ten percent. Now I am not sure why that is but all I know is we need to figure out a solution and fast. Jason: We can try to run another commercial with Kramer in it. I'm pretty sure the people are getting bored with the old one by now. Colonel: Do you fellow have any ideas? Kramer: How about midgets? People love midgets. Colonel: Yes but its quite hard to find a good midget these days. Jason: What about blowing something up? Both Kramer and Colonel glare at Jason. Jason: It was just an idea. Colonel: Well I'll tell you gentleman what. Go home and think about it and get back with me in about a week or possibly sooner. I have a feeling that the stocks will keep going down the shitter...... He drowns off as Kramer and Jason get to their feet. They shake his rinkly old hand before the walk out the door. Halfway through the hall Kramer stops in his tracks. Kramer: How about we have exploding midgets!!! Jason: You really need to stop watching so much television. Kramer: Oh believe me its not the tv. If you hang around PCW personalities long enough your mind gets really warped. Jason: That does sound right. They finally get outside and go straight to a white Mercedes parked in the lot. Kramer jumps in the drivers side and quickly revs up the engine. Then they are off down the road at a rapid speed of seventy miles per hour. Jason: So are you ready for tonight? Kramer: Eh...I know I lost. Just as long as I am back in time to watch MadTV I'm fine. Jason: Wait, your not even going to try? Kramer: I never said I wouldn't try. But you and the rest of the world know that I suck. So basically I see my ass getting the crap kicked out of me by three other wrestlers. Now I know its not a pretty thing to think about but thats my predictions on whats going to happen. The scene slowly fades.
