Back To School Part #1

27 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2/27/02 The camera fades in… The Chef is shown sitting in some hotel room watching the clip of President George W. Bush getting whacked over the back of the head with a mace again and again. The Chef: Ah, the sweet crack of the skull, a sound almost as alluring as a verse chanted by a Siren… Chester Bin Laden walks up to the Chef and looks at the TV/VCR console sitting on the table. Chester: Wow, you guys really got Bush there! Who was the one that laid the blow on him? Klaymore? Borro? Aymin? The Chef: Ha Ha Ha…nah, my men were just a distraction, the one who ousted Bush will be unveiled soon enough, and when that happens, the PCW will fall into my hands and Allah’s grace! The Chef holds his hand up, clinching his fist tightly as if he was figuratively grabbing hold of the federation. The Chef: I have prostrated myself before Allah many times. I’ve chanted, I’ve prayed, I’ve prayed some more, and then some more, and then a little bit more, but at last I can finally see that my faith is reaping great rewards… Chester: So you think you’ve got a shot? The Chef: A very good shot my dear Bin Laden… The Chef laughs maniacally as Chester just looks the other way. Chester: So how’s school? The Chef’s laughter quickly turns into a still and silent pause. The Chef: What…what school? Chester: Remember? The class I enrolled you in? Are you still taking the computer courses? You do want to be an official bachelor of Islamic Militancy don’t you? The Chef: I’ve…I…I don’t like school? Chester compassionately touches the Chef on his right shoulder. Chester: What’s wrong? Were the kids too mean? The Chef frowns and mutters an almost silent “Maybe”. Chester: Was there too much work? The Chef shakes his head. Chester: Then what?!?! The Chef: I…I…I was expelled!!! Chester gasps. Chester: What!?!?! All that work I did for you making sandwiches and ironing your goofy hat, for nothing!?!? The Chef nods. Chester: Well Buster, you better have a good explanation for this! The Chef: I do, maybe I can best describe it in a flashback sequence… Chester: Not another… The Chef: Hey, I’m not the handler! Through magic of PCW Productions and Hallmark Entertainment, we are transported back in time a few days to the campus of the Central Ohio School for Islamic Militants. ***The following is a dramatization of actual events that may or may not have happened but have certainly been conjured up by a young sex-starved, cocaine addicted writer… The Chef walks down a hallway, isolated from the rest of the kids in the school. He steps into the cafeteria and sits down at the table nearest to the door. He opens up his lunch bag and pulls out a Tupperware container full of caviar and some Keebler Town House crackers. He takes a plastic butter knife out of his bag and scoops some caviar onto the cracker. Some snot nosed kid comes up to him. Kid: What in Allah’s name are you eating? The Chef: It’s my specialty, truffle flavored caviar…on crackers! The kid shakes his head and mutters, “Dork” as he passes by. The Chef: I am not a dork! I am a chef, an Islamic Militant, and a professional Culinary Conquistador! The kids in the lunchroom don’t pay attention to the Chef’s comments. The Chef: Fine! Just ignore my existence! But I’ll tell you this, middle-aged adults taking high school course is no laughing matter! We are just like you, willing and eager to learn the ways of our supreme commander the MiracleGro guy! I hope you all set the tickers on your time bombs a few seconds to early so you get blown up before you get the chance to park that pick up truck in the basement of some important building! The kids still continue to ignore the Chef like he was an outcast from their “society”. The Chef begins to sob uncontrollably and runs down the hallway, trying to escape from the horrible prison that is school. He makes his way towards the exit; flying past the security guard but hears a high-pitched sound… “BEEP!!! BEEP!!! BEEP!!!” “Hey you stop!” The Chef is tackled from behind by the security guard, and handcuffed. The Chef: Sniff…sniff…what did I do sir? The guard points at a metal detector in the hall that the Chef must have passed. The Chef: A metal detector? Oh!!! Sir, I swear, I didn’t have any weapons! Guard: That’s the problem son, you are UNARMED! How do you expect to have success at an Islamic Militant without the proper arms?!?!? The Chef: What!?!? So you are saying that machine was a nonmetal detector…? Guard: Yeah, and you’re going to the principal’s office! The Chef: NOOOOOO!!! The Chef is yanked back up by his collar and dragged into the staff room, pass the secretary and the lounge, and dropped right in front of the principal, who sternly glances at the Chef from his giant desk. He drapes his head over his desk as the Chef rolls up into a ball, cowering in fear. “Mr. Alouette…” The Chef: It’s “The Chef” sir, I changed my name in 1995… Principal: Listen you little punk, I can call you whatever I want, I’m the principal! The Chef squirms in a display of sheer subjugation.The Chef: But I’m not little, I’m 6’3’’, and judging by the height that you’ve boosted your chair up to I’d say you’re around 5’6’’… The principal grows even angrier with the Chef. Principal: Oh, so you want to be a smart ass huh? Toss him in THE HOLE!!! The Chef: Please, don’t toss my salad!!! Principal: EWWW, we meant to through you into a place of solitary confinement… The Chef: Oh, then by all means… The couple of the guards drag the Chef towards what looks to once have been a Nuclear Attack shelter at the aging school. They both toss the Chef into the vault and slam the steel door shut. Darkness…utter darkness… The Chef: Anybody in here!?!? “Hey, wazzup bro!?!?” The Chef: Homeslice?!?! Is that you!?!? The Chef feels a slap on his right hand. The Chef: Hey, how’s it hanging dude? Homeslice: Not bad… The Chef: Hey, wait a sec, isn’t this supposed to be solitary confinement? Homeslice: Yeah, but I guess with budget cuts and all I get a roommate for a while. The Chef: How long have you been here? Homeslice: I’ve lost count after 18 days… The Chef: Eighteen days!?!? I have a match this Sunday!!! Homeslice: What? The Chef: A match, I’m in a 20-man battle royal to win the PCW World Title!!! Homeslice: Well dude, you are in the wrong place for that… The Chef: I couldn’t help it; those culinary school dropouts tossed me in here when I failed to carry a weapon outside of the school… What did they get you for? Homeslice: I painted a uni-brow on Professor Ahmet… The Chef: Hehe, that must have been quite a chuckle! Homeslice: Actually I did it while he was grading a paper; he’s pretty old and doesn’t have much feeling or sensation anymore… The Chef: Then how were you found out? Homeslice: Richard Gere told on me! The Chef: Gere! That piece of rancid pork!?!? First he’s Buddhist, now he’s Muslim…I tell ya, he’s just trying to cover all the bases in order to get eternal life. Homeslice: But at least he got to bang Julia Roberts… The Chef: Yeah, but then again, if I looked like a troll and played country music, I could too… Homeslice: True dat! Homeslice goes to high five the Chef, but accidentally slaps him in the forehead. The Chef: Hey! Watch my nose, it’s French… Homeslice: Isn’t the rest of your body French? The Chef: I’ll let you in on a big secret Homeslice; I’m not…totally…French… Homeslice: What!?!? Aren’t you a chef? The Chef: Yes, yes… I’m a chef. Well you see when I was born; my parents were on their way back to France from the Netherlands. Their car broke down along the way and my mom had to give birth to me right on the country border. After I was born, it was said that my nose was so long that when my father held me up it extended beyond the rest of the Dutch border and by a few millimeters it made it’s way into France, at least that’s what my brother said. Homeslice: I think he was just kidding… The Chef: Yeah, and I thought he was kidding about an allergy to linoleum too…and he died because of it… Homeslice: That’s tragic! The Chef: That’s life my friend, and that’s how Allah operates, in mysterious ways. Homeslice: I’m getting a tad pissed off at Allah right now… The Chef: Blasphemy!!! You cannot deny Allah’s will and grace, even in the bleakest situations! We aren’t some cockamamie followers of some polymorph divinity, some lighting tossing horn dog, or an eight-armed pregnant woman; we are the followers of the ONE TRUE GOD!!! Homeslice: Yeah, I think I need a little work on the “Islamic” in my Islamic Militancy… Suddenly the door opens… “Here’s your dinner!” The Chef sees the guard coming in with bowl of corn mush. The Chef: I am a culinary genius; you can’t expect me to eat mush! The Chef quickly charges at the guard and SPEARS HIM DOWN TO THE GROUND!!! The Chef: Praise Allah for Reeboks, I got good traction off that slick floor… The Chef and Homeslice run out of the vault and slam the door shut, trapping the guard inside, they take off down the hall. The Chef: We are almost out of this cesspool of corruption, and bratty kids! Suddenly the Principal steps in front of the two escapees as they run towards the only unguarded door in the school, the women’s locker room… Principal: Where do you two think you are going? Narrator: Will the Chef survive this escape from school?!?!? The Narrator pauses for a moment… Narrator: Of course he does, because he’s telling the freaking story! Well…I guess you’ll find out how the Chef escapes without perishing in our next promo! SAME CHEF TIME! SAME CHEF CHANNEL! NA NA NA NA NA NA NA NA NA NA NA NA NA NA NA NA THE CHEF!!! The camera mercifully fades to black…
