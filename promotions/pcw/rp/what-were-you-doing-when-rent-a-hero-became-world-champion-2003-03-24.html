What Were You Doing When Rent-A-Hero Became World Champion?

24 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Title: What Were You Doing When Rent-A-Hero Became World Champion? Featuring: Rent-a-Hero Date: 3-24-2003 ”What were you doing when Jeremy Howard came back to PCW?” I’ll tell you what I was doing citizen….I was backstage, just coming out of an extremely hot shower after a successful Crusierweight tile defense. I was on a high, having impressed my new sidekick, Teresa Tomas, AND retaining my coveted Crusierweight prize. I thought my day was over…I was ready to extradite myself to my new sector to do battle with new evildoers after getting back within my spandex….then it happened….”COME ON COME ON GET DOWN WITH THE SICKNESS!!!”That mere line with the familiar guitar riff sent chills up my spine. Everything stopped….everything literally disappeared before me….except the closed circuit monitor sitting on the table. On that very monitor, I saw him….The Krayzie One… A man I called my partner at one time… A man I wished farewell to in his final moment of glory… Jeremy Howard.My eyes were fixated in an awe-inspired trance as I watched the legend himself put some of Marvel’s GREATEST superhero’s to shame with his actions. So frozen, I couldn’t even work up the nerve to smile and cheer when he DECIMATED that felonious fiend, Xavier Kannon. All I could do was simply gaze upon the man I thought was gone, a man who I idolized on my road upward in PCW.Everybody has those moments. Those moments where you remember EXACTLY what you were doing when it happened… THAT was my moment. To see Krayzie back in commission warmed my Kryptonian heart…it put a competitive fire in me that burned brighter then a supernova in the farthest reaches of space.I felt that same feeling I had in the Lethal Lottery tournament, that competitive urge that ran through me when “The Krayzie One” and I teamed to battle PCW’s best….both of us knowing all too well that if we won, we’d do battle for the World title tooth and nail. At the time, Howard was quite the evil one….yet his poise, command, and power in the ring garnered my respect. I could not help but respect what the man was capable of and he was high on my list of hero’s, side by side with Big Alan Steele.That’s when it hit me…..I’m tired of coasting… Why am I settling for Crusierweight when I have what it takes to be THE World Champion?Every opportunity I’ve had to become something great….one of two things happens. Either I choke, as the public so aptly points out for me with their Smart-Arse-A-Senses…Or I attain victory….yet it feels tarnished or unchallenging. The only time I felt so alive....competitive….and strong, was when I stood side by side with Howard as we took out the mis-matched duos thrown our way on the road to Red October.Now this Tuesday….I hear myself and nine other men have the chance to be the World Champion. I want it….I want it more then any of those other men could ever imagine because I have a destiny to fulfill. Forget bragging rights citizens…or simply having a big gold belt over my shoulder….This is about DESTINY…this is about taking that step forward to become JUST like my hero’s, Jeremy Howard and Big Alan Steele. I don’t EVER want my fans to sulk years later, asking themselves why I NEVER became a World champion in the TOP wrestling company in the world. I never want to regret not giving my absolute best to attain greatness….and say, “I did it Jeremy. I did it Al.” Maybe it’s high time the hero STOPS idolizing…and becomes an idol.Destiny has faced me with 9 other men, with a fire to be the best like I’ve never seen ….But none of them have the fire I do. None of them feel the hand of destiny pushing them like I do….And NONE of them, will stand with the World title in their grasp like I will.I can’t run any longer from destiny and I’m not scared anymore….I need to face this destiny and hope that it’s the same kind that guided my heroes to the gold….Al…Jeremy….I can only hope a humble fan like myself, a mere spandex superhero with humble beginnings in the PWA…..could do you proud and win the big one.This one’s for you.Maybe….one day, if Tuesday is my day…and I win this World Title….some young Rent-Head who idolizes me the way I idolized the both of you will ask to one of his friends.”What were you doing when Rent-A-Hero won the PCW World Heavyweight title?”Destiny draws near…. ---- View Rent-a-Hero's Biography
