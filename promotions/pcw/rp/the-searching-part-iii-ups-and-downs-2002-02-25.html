The Searching - Part III - Ups And Downs

25 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2-25-02 Song listening guide: 'Parallel Universe' by Red Hot Chili Peppers, from Californication BOBBY STORMBRINGER The Searching - Part III - Ups And Downs Then.... B.S. yells over his shoulder without even bothering to turn around, still walking with a purpose. STORMBRINGER: Shaddup! I'm in need of a freggin' Snapple~! or three right now. Gawd help this village if they don't stock it! Whether it is a foolish move or not, he yaps this last threat loud enough for any cop in a thirty-yard radius to hear. STORMBRINGER: Carl, you will know when you are supposed to know! You all will. And baby that's no b.s. now get out of my face!! Quite the run-on sentence, eh? B.S. must of still been p!ssed when he edited this recording for public viewing later, because he cuts the promo off much more quickly than he normally would withou- ..............*snow and static*.............. And now.... * * * March Madness. The name couldn't possibly be more fitting. Miyagawa. Darkstar. Xavier Kannon. Bobby Stormbringer. Four out of a possible five Asylum members have qualified for the twenty-man Rumble, much like the Controversial Messiahs joining their leader and World Champion Snow into the mix, which would obviously equal all of New Breed. Asylum leader Jeremy 'Krayzie' Howard was the clear choice to be the personal pick of one PCW Prez Duke Williams. However, personal issues with Drake Maxwell and a hunt for Chet King's Continental title is what riddles the brain of 'The Krayzie One' these days, just as much as his quest for an eventual PCW Title Sweep. Just like Stormbringer. Of course, Jeremy is a couple of major steps ahead of B.S., namely the Rising Star title and the World Heavyweight Championship. The PCW World title is the pinnacle of this business, and the Rising Star belt has it's own special problems. You see, PCW rules state that it must be the first belt that you win, or you aren't eligible for it. Because if yo've already been a champion you're already a 'Risen Star', or whatevah. Even the TV strap counts?? Hmm. Okay. B.S.'s 'problem' is that he is getting lots more attention now after joining PCW and Asylum, much more praise than he's comfortable with and used to. Just eight months ago The Showstopper was retired for a year after toiling in the Indy Feds, convinced he would never recieve the big break that he would never get what he needed, the big break he felt he deserved. This is quite an adjustment for him, depsite the fact that all the kind words and respect recently are the simple things he has always wanted. Always tried to earn. In the eyes of many, including himself, B.S. has risen slightly, to the point where being Rising Star Champion just for the sake of it is pretty much pointless. Besides, he could always pull off something close to what Frisco did with that Mxican Lucha jobber wierdness. A little payback on Karl Leblanc in the ways of Extreme? Working out the kinks with Kannon to put a scare into the Tag Team division? Throwing himself into the TV division, just in case Miya gets screwed and drops it, or in case Miya wins it all at March Madness?? Why settle for that bottom title that now resides on Bell's waist when you beat half the people they throw at you in PTC!? They all aren't just newbies or pure jobbers, either. Although that is some of them, but still... Shodan. Outfoxing Dexter Thomas and Shane Wethers to become the number one contender to what once was Radder's Cyberslam belt. Like that's terribly difficult. HANK~! HANK I SAY~! Anton the Kid. Technically, LeBlanc and The Rush were along for that ride, but never had to tap out to Mr. Prime Time. Not yet. But they still lost. Tim Walden, in a clusterf*ck of all things. Jack Valentine. Brock Newbludd. Thrashing Drake Maxwell in a poignant and moving way. No, not really.. but it sure as hell must of hurt, despite the partial no-sell. Yet despite the fact there are some highly thought of names that have been heard from for a very long time, the one B.S. has been smirking about the most Sunday is definitely Ritchie Syxx. The Showstoper has absolutely no personal ill will towards the young man besides for his choice in feds, but finding a way to squeak out a win against him only proves the points of B.S. even further. After nearly halting EW from the brink of imploding with his fellow PWA Graduates, Wethers and Six Finger Cisco.. B.S. recieves a twelve seed? Puhlease. That's not even half as nutty as 'The Malice Man', new PCW Prez and former Continental Champion Duke Williams also having a twelve seed in the second Global Title Tournament. Ah, well. We are only humans that make errors in judgment. Ehh. Most of us, anyway, the ones without Godly powers. Although, come to think of it, they make plenty of mistakes in judgment as well. Stormbringer, Williams, and twelve other allumni of PCW showed just how vital these seedings really are. There is the twelve, and therefore fourteen, that is truly important. At the same time, the pairings did put B.S. in a tough position. Concievably, if The Showstopper wants to make it to his bracket's final round, then he may just have to get by a five seed. A four seed. Then the number one seed! Yow. Even if somehow, the former GWF Champion, Palithorpe, gets the mild upset on the red-hot Davidson then that would still be a difficult road to hoe for the proud Asylum member. To put it mildly. You can bet on one thing. Whether he finally gives in here under all the pressure and stress in this next round, or whether he can manage to survive the best in the world all the way to the 'Elite Eight' of the Tournament... there will be no simple matches for your favorite neighborhood anti-hero from start to finish. Whenever that finish is predestined to be. Life is so up and down, never steady for long. No matter what happens to him, B.S. has a wonderful lady to come home to every week, and no amount of wickedly close losses or cowardly attacks from behind during his freggin' matches could damper his spirit around her. The right one does that for you. With the right one? Everything is easy, everything is clear to you. Everything. * * * --2-25-02, 12:17 am, early Monday morning, after Sabotage-- Too many hotel rooms. Too many rent-a-cars and taxis. Too many airplanes, security checkpoints, necessary but intruding questions about his various cameras, all those changeovers. Why can't they just fly right to the city you want to go to? Ever!? Far too many buses.. and all those people. Some excellent examples of peasants, stick their face next to the definition of the term in Webster's... it's all just getting a little too out of hand these days. B.S. has to remind himself sometimes that even the peasants can work up fifty bucks to go see a show like the rest of the normal people out there. So they pay the smallest of portions to his terrific salary. By all rights, The Showstopper should respect that.. a little. Blah. Both Stormbringer and his handler are sick and tired of B.S. being cooped up in his hotel room and the lobby so much, so before B.S. has to get back and make the flight to ...... so he can support the rest of Asylum, it's time for a walk. Perhaps some fresh, Canadian air can loosen Bobby's lungs, get that flu moving and then out, once and for all. B.S. isn't booked for this week's Turmoil or Fusion. After a relapse of this flu virus, three matches in two days, a bloody back-of-the-head, and a bruised back, do you really think Mr. Prime Time'll complain about it?? If you picked the answer with the lack of common sense involved, then think again. B.S. is totally aware that the Gawdforsaken PCW aircraft leaves at three in the morning for Turmoil, so until aroud two or so Bobby just wants to take a walk through the streets of Calgary, his former home. What better chance to sigh and reminisce, like he does far too often for his own good. B.S. walks down the snowy sidewalks of lower downtown Calgary, fingerless, gloved hands jammed as deep as possible into his dark silver trenchcoat's pockets. It is just a bit open, revealing some sort of Mexi PCW shirt underneath. He wears black leather boots that should hopefully hold up through the trudging through the muddy snow and sleet below him. B.S. also wears his battered Orlando Magic cap and his custom, blue lightning-streaked Oakleys in a vain attempt to hide from the bitter Alberta winds that gust against The Showstopper. Mr. Prime Time isn't running around like one of those strange Green Bay Packer fans in his undies, so don't fret. He wears black denim jeans for the slightest of changes, in color, at least. He wears them like he does every day, so people don't freak out at something no one else in society really does. Run around pantless, I mean. Yet, these stupid jeans are not doing their real job, which is blocking out the cold! B.S.'s teeth chatter slightly at the fact that the gusting wind is sliping through the fabric, and it is slipping through far too often. He should also really think about buttoning up that trench! This is the place. This is where it all started. Cloud-filled night above, still plenty of people milling about in this bustling metropolitan area. B.S. enjoys having other human beings milling about. Just so long as he doesn't have to talk to many of them. As it is, Bobby has had to calm his shaking hands to the point where he could sign four of five autographs after his good and effective promo on Sabotage. B.S. moved to Calgary at the age of sixteen, back in 1993, with an uncle on his late mother's side. With what little money his wealthy parents were willing to give him, quite the coincidence occured, one of the most unlikely. Even in Stormbringer's hectic and unforgiving life. B.S. paid an obscure and unknown trainer a decent amount of cash to show him the bare basics of wrestling. How to take a back bump, throwing people around in the most basic of moves to get by without looking bad, and so forth. To simplify, the first few weeks of Tough Enough. Stormbringer picked this particular trainer because of his growing underground reputation in the area. That and he couldn't even come close to affording a session or two in 'The Dungeon'. So it turns out that the trainer ends up becoming 'Lance Storm'. Go figure. Hey, at least B.S. now knows he went to the right place, right? B.S. lived in the city for five or six months before returning home, and he maintains that it is still the most beautiful places he's ever seen, and the countryside is as about as peaceful and serene as you can get. Of course, the more time you spend in Central Florida after being born in the northeast, the thinner your blood gets. Coming back here after all this time and being so far removed from his childhood in Connecticut... weather-wise, this is pure hell, plain and simple. At least one of the first two of the nine total levels. B.S. ducks behind a tree of some kind and catches his breath from the struggling against the weather pushing him back, right in his face, and he begins to cut his 'keep-you-all-updated' promo, since his handler is throwing a tantrum at running out of time before Fear Factor and other such things. The tree provides an effective tool against the wind, at least enough for the teeth to stop chattering so much. B.S. isn't really in the mood to figure out what kind of tree this is, so let's just say it's 'elm' or 'oak' and all be happy, happy, joy, joy. Okay? Okay. STORMBRINGER: Augghh.. e-evenin', all! S-so much for me being the 'Bringer Of The Storms' like b-back in the day, eh? 'Cause I sure as freg didn't bring this one! B.S. rubs his arms and pulls his cap down over his eyes further in a pointless attempt to warm himself a bit. Now all we can see of his face are his nose and mouth,for the time being. STORMBRINGER: I love this place, but f*ck! I never realized how damn cold this place can be! I'm glad I always come here in the spring and summer.. 'til now. Now that the biggest match of my life is over... let's get on with the two new biggest, in back to back days no less! Urgghh.. gotta get inside somewhere. Stupid Florida softened me up. Um, anyway, I've made it to the second round of the GTT.. only what? Six more to go?? Heh, yeah right. Anyways, gotta at least keep going ith a good accounting of m'self. I got this guy who's one more seed higher than Syxx was.. Gaines? Damn me, but I don't know who this guy is. He's from the Academy, but with a rank this high it must be transitional. Total buttmonkey, Another cheap Nelson wannabe. But he must have some talent to get past Estep, and it's not like I ever treat anyone lightly. Eh.. not too lightly anyway. Kinda hard not to take Banshee lightly, if ya get m' meaning. Who knows? Maybe he'll underestimate me and it'll make the match that much easier. Either way I'm gonna leave it all out there. I may have a couple of minor injuries to rest from, but I'll be more than ready with five days with no matches before that. I never told anyone this early on, but I my personal goal was the third round. Since I have to also concentrate on PCW, too. I guess we'll see Saturday if Gaines can prove me wrong. Woops, more Benoit junk. I need to cut that out, I don't feel like hearing a Nelson lecture about copyrights and sh!t... Bleh. Guess I'll have to talk more about this youngin' once he actually cuts something that resembles a promo, since I know almost nothing about this guy, other than his gimmick is firing people. Or something. B.S. hugs himself and resists the urge to sit down, since snow is all over the place like the other three-hundred and sixty four days a year. Sixty-five on a leap year, duh. STORMBRINGER: Pffft-pheh. Must.. wrap.. this up. Besides for that match, there's onal a few.. 'personal' things I need to take care of, mostly back home! Where I wish I was right now. In Orlando.. gotta confirm some things for my basketball game.. and something a lot more important. Forget drama and suspense! I'll tell you now. After Turmoil, you'll be seeing me take down one of the fool b!tches that tried to take me out in January. I'll beat the stuffing out of him until he gives me the other names I'm looking for. Or until I break him like when we were all kids, like when we broke the little new Star Wars X-Wing fighters and video games for Christmas. What a shame. No really. I mean it. Then.. March Madness. Ooo yeah! Wait, not Macho Man. Forget about that.. just remember this. Asylum has a one in five chance by pure statistics. This weekend... just like the last one? One to remember for The Showstopper. Now let us all just hope that 'The Wheel Of Fate' lets it end up bring some fond memories. And baby... that's no b.s.! Alrighty, you all satisfied yet..? B.S. pauses as if he can actually hear a response from someone. STORMBRINGER: What do you mean, no!? Well, too bad! I'm freggin' freezing, I have bags to pack and a plane to catch, so Asylum in Canada? We out. B.S. counts to himself, quietly to three and he runs off to his nearby rent-a-car down the street. Quickly so he can get outta the bloody weather, arrive at Turmoil, rest somewhat backstage, and stop having to hear about those two lucky Olympic Canada hockey teams, already. We get the point, people. Enough is enough! Can B.S. recover from his 'No Holds Barred' tag match and spineless attack from Powell long enough for yet another loony weekend? Will B.S. finally have a small amount of time this week to spend with the new light of his life, Kira? Will B.S. suck it up and make his presence felt for the rest of Asylum on Turmoil on Tuesday, or Fusion this Friday? Will B.S. ever have the giddy pleasure of meeting NTICW's Aimz? Can B.S. actually find anything out about his opponent for the GTT, PWAer Brian Gaines? What number is The Showstopper going to draw for the 'Brawl For It All', anyway? Is he serious about putting Asylum before himself in this soon-to-be legendary match?? Will the former 'number one mark' be presented with the golden opportunity to choke out the Quintessential Teen Idol, or is that all still merely wishful thinking for now!? Finally, we've been asking for a month and a half now. The answer, apparently, comes this week. Who is one of the bastids that attacked Bobby Stormbringer before he ever stepped inside a PCW ring!?! Let the madness continue. TO BE CONTINUED. THIS HAS BEEN A B.S. PRODUCTION. (c) 2002. ALL RIGHTS RESERVED.
