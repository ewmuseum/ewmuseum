Skylar Green's (Not, not the PCW one) Attempt at Trash Talk

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Who cares? I&#039;m just trying not to no-show Turmoil, which I&#039;ve no intention of winning. And so we come to a man standing in a ring. Well, it's been green-screened out, and instead we see him walking around on a beach. It's so obvious, someone throws a frisbee, which flies right through his head. So we see the man, get a good look at him. He's about 6'2' tall, weighs around 250 pounds. He has on red Athletic Department training pants with two blue stripes running down the side of the legs. His shirt is a simple solid red T-shirt. He wears glasses and the hair is close-cut. Upon his chin is a half-goatee that does not reach around to a moustache. And no, he's not copying Mike Shinoda from Linkin Park. Geez, I wish people would stop asking that. It's possible that people just want to do that. Hell, I was doing it before Linkin Park became popular. Err... Anyway, the man is Skylar Green. He paces back and forth in the same 10 foot path, yet the beach scene always scrolls to the right. It's like a running cycle of Baywatch camera-panning angles. Sky: Hello, my name is Skylar Green. I'm not PCW's Skylar Green, I'm a different one. Why would I be talking to you? Simple, PCW's Skylar Green is not in the mindset for doing this type of thing. He's doing serious stuff right now, trying to infiltrate the Dreamlands so that he may start killing off Violence Jack's gods. So when something light-hearted comes along, it'll be up to me and the man sitting at his computer typing the words for me to say to make this happen. What's happening, you ask? Quite simply, it's late and we're strapped for ideas on what to write, so we'll go with the Chet King method of picking out other people's flaws to save coming up with something meaningful of our own. Works in a pinch, and hey, it got him this far. We'll try it and see how it works. So as I sit here and frown at the Miss USA pageant, which will probably never weight intelligence and talent more than how a woman looks in a bathing suit (I'm sure that Sports Illustrated is running it, with help in recent years from Maxim) I imagine Skylar Green's opponents for Tuesday Turmoil and do my best to produce a scathing trash-talk while remaining in-character. This will probably suck, but forgive me, I don't do this much. Akiyama The now revealed Neo Void Fraud is in the match. He came into March Madness after almost a month away with no action, and proceeded to allow Skylar Green and Duke Williams dominate. Got eliminated around the middle of the pack, was far from the last one to go. That's got to count for something. Dante Jackson should be getting a shot in this match before Akiyama, he's been more active over the past couple of months. Sure, Akiyama was suspended by Xavier Kannon, but when you think about it, was anyone OTHER than Akiyama upset with that ruling? Totally Tubular Tommy He's become synonymous with the Continental Title, it being the only singles title, in fact, the only title he's held in PCW. That's the problem. He'll be seen as a great Continental Champion coming into this match, but with at least four men in this match who have had direct experience with big-time clutch matches such as this one, Totally Tubular Tommy will still be seen as the underdog. Indeed, there will be many people thinking that he should try his luck at regaining the Continental Title before doing anything in the World Title division. Xavier Kannon Does he really need it? No. Why would Jeremy Howard put him in this match? Simple. Kannon's Snapple-dyed hair, obnoxious attitude, and relatively small size makes for a pretty large red bullseye painted squarely upon his ass. Kannon is in this match to get baptised with his own blood, plain and simple. Pigs will guide Santa's sleigh through the sky while Satan gives out free snow cones on that cold day in Hell before ANYONE in PCW lets Kannon become the champion again. And besides, he's siding with Saddam Hussein; the US government will invade and bomb Kannon before he can rebuild his World Title career. Venneman Pendragon He has clumsily ambled his way to the top by just 'being there.' He won the Rising Star Title from... *clears throat* TRIGGER! So are we really counting that as an achievement? He then went on to defeat John Crichton, an overall unimpressive character, for the Television Title, and since he lost it to Syko the Garbage Nymph, has not done anything but constantly lose to his opponents. That's grounds for being put into a World Title match. I get the feeling that his size got him the spot in the match more than anything else. Clyde Why Clyde is here and not Pharaoh boggles the mind. I'll just say that since Clyde already held the title for over 100 days, he's most likely not interested in it anymore. Indeed, it limits his options for death and destruction, since the division is only 4 people large normally. Chet King The egomaniac who has the marketing power equal to Adolf Hitler selling matzos during Channukha. His career highlights of the last, oh... 15 months or so are basically him getting dropped on his head repeatedly. Jeremy Howard no doubt felt bad for Chet King as a veteran of PCW, and thus put him in this match. Chet King retains his face status despite not acting like it at all. It's amazing what the PCW fans will do if you ask them. Rent-A-Hero AHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHA!!! Another man who has-- AHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHA!!! Done little more-- AHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHA!!! Than job to everyone he's faced in the past two months. Save for winning the Cruiserweight Title from El Romantico, then again he completely owes that victory to Tessa Windsor's distraction of Romantico. I'm surprised Rent-A-Hero hasn't tried to be a good sport about it and give Windsor a fair shot at te belt. Since RAH's supposed to be about good and virtue and fair-play and all that stuff. Hypocrite. Violence Jack Skylar owns him, and Jack is not winning this match. Simple as that. El Diablo Let me make something very clear: anyone who feuds with Flaymin Aymin is not World Title material. Let me repeat that. ANYONE WHO FEUDS WITH FLAYMIN AYMIN IS NOT WORLD TITLE MATERIAL! If Diablo were to win the title now, the dead would rise, the rivers would run red with blood, the moon would eclipse itself, the Four Horsemen of the Apocalypse would play polo with The Pope's head... you get the point. Do we really want that on our consciences? Okay, maybe we do (thanks Clyde), but still Diablo with the title would be pretty bad. ___________________________ Sky: And so, that just leaves Skylar Green, PCW Co-Continental Champion, unaccounted for. Skylar will not be winning Turmoil. He has no desire to. Some things are more important, namely his torture of Violence Jack. The World Title can wait, he will get shots at it at other times. Now, though... he has other priorities. What will happen? I'm forecasting that the arena explodes or something, who knows. Either way, none of the 10 men in the match are going to win the title. That's preposterous.
