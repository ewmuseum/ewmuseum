A look in to the past.

3 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/3/02 Steve walked into the bar with a bit of a swagger. He had spent the last couple of days searching for and finding a little peace in the crazy world he had found himself in since the death of his friend Stewart. He walked through the busy bar to the back of the room where he took a seat in a booth alone. The very nice looking waitress walked up to him and politely asked for his order. Steve asked for a beer and that she not make him wait to long for it either. Then he turned his attention to the crowded room of people just having a little fun, and others getting stumbling drunk. He shook his head at the actions of all of them. Steve wondered why he had ever wanted to please these people. Why had he ever begun to care about what they thought, or liked? Could it be that he had longed for some thing that outside of his few friends Steve had never got...acceptance? No that was the reason. He had never really cared about what others thought. The waitress returned with his beer and gave Steve a little smile. He did not return the gesture only laid the money in her hand and looked past her. She stormed off in a hurt rush. Why did he have to act that way, he wondered? Do I really want to treat people like that? Does it make me feel good to see people hurt and in pain? Yes he thought. It always felt great to inflict pain and suffering to the less fortunate than him, whether it be in the ring or out. But in the back of his mind Steve knew that things had not always been that way. He knew at one time when he first stepped into this business that he had wanted to be an ass hole that everyone hated, but really did not know how to forget what others thought. They all began to change on one day in May a couple of years ago. Flash Back Two Years Ago The gym is old and damp. The kind of place you might only see in one of those old boxing or wrestling documentaries on the Arts and Entertainment channel. The place was not very well lit and the equipment was not very state of the art. The two men who walk into the gym are very familiar although a couple of years younger and in Steve’s case about seventy pounds of muscle lighter. The two men are Steve Johnson and Michael Cade. They walk toward the back of the gym, where two other men are. As they get closer the camera focuses in on the two men who are in a ring practicing their wrestling against each other. The smaller of the two hits a belly-to-belly suplex on the bigger man. When the smaller one gets up and turns toward the other man we realize they are Jake Keeton and Stewart Hix. Keeton minus the facial hair he now has. The two continue on for a few minutes. Trading punch for punch, move for move. Michael: You will be glad you finally decided to meet this guy Steve. I am telling you the skill and knowledge of technical wrestling he can teach you would make your already impressive skills possibly the best in the world. Steve: We will see about that MC. I am still not completely convinced of that. Michael: Why not Steve? As he asked the question Stewart capitalized on one of the rare mistakes that Keeton makes when wrestling and locked on his dreaded 4:20 submission hold. Keeton tried to fight off giving in to the pain of the move but was eventually unable to do so. Moments later Keeton taps out to the hold and the two sit in the middle of the ring catching their breath for a moment. Steve looks away from the ring and back to Michael. Steve: I have heard a whole lot about this guy being a very arrogant and cocky ass. I just am not sure if I could trust him not to stab me in the back MC. Stewart: You would be much smarter than you look at first sight then. Stewart and Keeton walk over to that side of the ring and get a couple of bottles of water. They both take a drink and then pour the remainder of the bottles over their heads. Steve looks at the two who seem to mirror one another’s actions and just rolls his eyes. Keeton and Stewart climb out of the ring and walk over to where Steve and Michael are standing. They both shake Michael’s hand and then turn their attention to the new man in the gym. Stewart: So Michael are you going to introduce us to your friend here? He seems to be a rather interesting and smart mouth guy. Steve: The name is Steve Johnson. I do not need him to speak for me. If you have some thing to say you can say it to me. Michael: Look you two I did not bring you here to fight. Steve this man can help your career. Just trust me and let the man teach you. Steve: We will see if he can help me or not Michael. You have a deal Stewart. You will have my attention as you attempt to train me to be a better wrestler. For now anyway that is. Just don’t try and be a smart ass and I won’t have to teach you a lesson. Stewart just laughs and then the two stand face to face staring each other down. Keeton and MC just shake their heads in disbelief. The scene fades away.... Present Time Steve sits at his table with a smile on his face as he thinks about the old days. Sure they hadn’t seen eye to eye when they first met, but Stewart did prove very quickly to Steve that he could be trusted both in and out of the ring. Stewart had always been there to give advice and lend a hand hadn’t he? Yes he had. Stewart was not the one to turn his back on his friends. No it was me who turned on him. And for what? When and where had I let someone lead me down the wrong path? Steve shakes his head in disbelief of the thoughts that again have surfaced in his mind. When and why were not really important. The only thing that mattered now was that it is made up for ten fold. But could I ever repay him for all he did for my career? What does a person do when the man they owe the debt to is dead, and in reality they should be as well? No matter what the answer is, it will be done. Steve set there watching two guys playing pool and talking about something. Steve just set there emotion less as he listened to the strangers conversation. The two men continue to talk as they trade shots on the pool table. They begin to laugh about some subject they have been talking about. Blond Man: Oh by the way did you hear the news about Stewart Hix dying in a plane crash last week? Bald Man: Yes I heard that fantastic news. If you ask me the prick got what was coming to him. The world is better off. Blond Man: You said it man! I almost went crazy when I heard about it over the radio. Steve sits there his face still showing little or no emotion, even though common sense would tell you he is angry. Steve stood up slowly and quietly as the men continued their verbal bashing of the friend who had passed way. Did they know how much they sounded like ignorant jackass’? Do they realize that not every one here shares their view of Stewart? Did they care? Did Steve really care if they did? No he didn’t. By this point there would be no excuse they could make to save them from the pain he was about to unleash. Steve walked toward the two as he thought about his match on Sunday at the same time. Did his opponents really understand what horrible fate they had coming? Could they possibly comprehend the type of pain and suffering they would have to endure? No doubt they did not know or comprehend these facts. After all they are just three men with problems they seem more focused on. Tonight these two men however would get a small idea of what was in store for his opponents on Sunday. Steve walks right up next to the two men. They stop playing pool and look at the man who is standing there looking at them with anger dancing deep in his eyes. The two seem to drunk to really realize who they have offended. Most likely they do not even reconize who is standing there next to them. Steve reaches behind him and grabs a pool stick of his own. He looks over at the two men and gives them a evil grin. Steve: I was sitting over there and couldn’t help but over hear your conversation about Stewart Hix. I thought I would come over here and see if I could be included in the fun. You wouldn’t mind would you? Bald Man: We would mind in fact. This is a private game. Besides how do we know that you are not one of those few people who actually like that prick? If you are then we would have to mess you up for bothering us. And I would hate to have to take the time and mess up a waste of space like you. Steve: Let me clear all that up for you then. I was hoping you would say some thing to that effect when I walked over here. I am indeed one of those who like him. Stewart was one of my few friends. He was one of my closest friends. So I would have to say that you hurt my feelings by saying things like that. Steve just laughs loudly as the two look a bit shocked. Then they stand there as if they want to fight with him. In a split second Steve brings the pool cue around and smacks them both in the head knocking them to the floor. Steve grabs up the blond one and picks him up over his head. Then he tosses the man through the wall next to the pool tables. By this time the bald man has gotten up and takes a couple of swings at Steve that he quickly and easily dodges. Steve smiles at the man then boots him in the gut. In a swift motion he hooks the bald man up in the Silencer and drives the mans face right into the floor. Steve then stands up looks at the two men who lay motionless and bleeding. Steve then walked away form the mess and toward the door. The emotion less black stare back on his face. As he walked out the door he thought again about the Madness he would un leash in less than twenty four hours on three other men no more talented or threatening than the two he had just disposed of. The scene fades away to black.
