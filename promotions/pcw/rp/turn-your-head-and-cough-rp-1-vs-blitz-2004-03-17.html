Turn Your Head and Cough (RP #1 VS Blitz)

17 March 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/17/04 The room is small examining room has a chill in the air as out hero, Luke Chadbourne sits on the examining table wearing a pair of rustic looking Lucky Brand blue jeans and a pink polo shirt. The examining table is so cold that Luke can feel it through his jeans and it sends a chill up his spine as he patiently waits for the doctor to enter the room. Thoughts fill his mind as he sits there, not saying a word to Hawk, who is sitting in the room, next of the small desk in the corner of the room. 'What the fuck am I doing here? I don't need no doctor. It's not that bad. How did I let Hawk talk me into coming here? I hate doctors.' Luke's chain of thought is broken as the oak door to the small examining room swings open and a short slightly round man walks into the room carrying a clipboard. The man's long white coat swings from side to side as he walks over to the desk Hawk is sitting next to. The doctor sits his clipboard down on the desk next to Hawk and then turns toward Luke and speaks with a bit of wonder in his voice. 'Hello. My name is Dr. James Pepper. How are we doing today?' 'I have been better.' 'What's the matter with you today, Luke?' 'I got slammed on my head a few times on Saturday night and since then I have been having spells of dizziness.' 'Ok let me take a look at you.' Dr. Pepper reaches into the breast pocket of his long white coat and pulls out a small flashlight. 'Now I am going to need you to try as hard as you can to just look forward no matter how bright this light is, OK?' 'Yea, sure, I'll try.' Luke stares straight forward as the doctor gets right in front of him and shines the small flashlight into his right eye, then his left eye. The doctor looks deep into the eyes, looking for anything out of the ordinary. Dr. Pepper pulls the light away from Luke's eyes and clicks the off switch. He walks over to his clip board and writes something and then turns back to Luke. 'I am actually going to have to run a few more tests. But I think you may have a concussion. It's not all that big of a deal, Just stay off your feet for the next week or so and you will be just fine.' Luke's face is filled with shock and anger as he quickly replies to the doctor's orders. 'Stay off my feet for a week or so?? Fuck that, I got a match this Saturday.' 'Well, I suggest you cancel it, if you have a concussion that is.' 'Fuck that, I ain't giving up my shot at the world title over a little bump on the head.' The doctor rolls his eyes and then says in a very sarcastic tone of voice as he turns and walks out of the room. 'Do what want. What do I know, I am only doctor.' The door to the small room closes and Luke turns to Hawk. Hawk has a look of concern on his face at the revelation the doctor just laid on the table. Luke is still pissed off at the proposition of throwing in the towel for his match this week on Turmoil against a more then worthy competitor, Inphino Blitz. Hawk quickly breaks the silence that now hangs so heavily in the air. 'Luke, maybe you should listen to the doctor. You are not 100 % and to get in the ring with Blitz, you better be.' 'I'll be fine, there's nothing Blitz can do to me that I haven't seen before. Besides, I worked my ass off to beat Kanton last week, there is no way in hell I am just going to give up my spot to Blitz. This is my world title tourney, I am just allowing the others to take part in it.' Hawk rolls his eyes at Luke's 'tough guy, I am better then everyone' attitude. 'Luke, It doesn't really matter that much, you have no chance in hell of beating Blitz.' Luke is getting agitated by Hawk's negativity and voices his frustration loud and clear. 'I am going to beat Blitz. There is no doubt in my mind that I am going to move on in this tournament. I am going to be the next PCW World Heavyweight Champion and there is nothing anyone can do about it.' 'Ok, Ok say you do beat Blitz, Which I am saying is very unlikely, then you still have to go up against guys who are way out of your league, like 'The Buzz' and Propaganda, and Jon Kano, and the list goes on and on. There is no way in hell you are going to be the next World Champion. Why not let someone who has a chance take the shot.' 'I have a chance. By over coming all the odds last week, with the Primetime Players trying everything they could to keep Kanton in the runnings, I walk out of Turmoil last week with a victory. If I can over come 4 men in one match then this match should be easy, it's just one on one.' Hawk sees that he isn't getting through to Luke and lets out a sigh of frustration as he tries to hammer in the fact that Luke shouldn't wrestle this Saturday at Turmoil. 'Yea, you got lucky Luke. You beat Kanton, you earned your spot. But you were lucky. Your luck will run out eventually, you realize this.' 'You call it luck, I call it talent. I have worked my ass off to get where I am today. And noone, I mean noone short of God himself is going to take my spot from me.' 'You're fucking crazy. You are going to..... Hawk and Luke's heated conversation is quickly interrupted by the door of the room swinging open again. Again enter the short, slightly round doctor. 'Luke, if you are ready, I would like to run a cat scan now to see exactly what is going on in your head.' A bit ironic that the doctor would state in that way don't you think. Hawk thinks so and he quickly throws in his two cents. 'Yea, I'd sure like to know what's going on in that head of yours.' Luke shoots Hawk one of the meanest looks you can possibly image and then hops of the examining table. He follows the round doctor out of the room and the door swings shut leaving Hawk sitting there all alone. 'That guy is going to get himself seriously injured if he keeps this shit up. Fucking crazy bastard.' With that said, our scene quickly fades to black right before your eyes.
