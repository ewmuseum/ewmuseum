Decree of Darkness Saga -- Silencing the Upstarts: Volume 1

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: October 2 of 2002 A.D. (Diary entry from Father Randall Craven of St. Augustine’s Chapel, Boston Massachusetts; dated September 28, 2002) I regret to record in these pages that my long time friend and prized colleague within the Blackhelm Coalition, Bruce Shanahan, has gone utterly mad. I fear my associate’s endeavors while acting as part of the organization took a grave toll on his already-wavering mental stability. In recent weeks our correspondence had dwindled significantly, the little I received from Bruce being marked with abnormally bitter ramblings about the sin and depravity of the human species. His notes began taking a frankly macabre twist and were laden with a venomous dissent against our methods and a series of piercing allegations against our organizational doctrine. He claimed to grow weary with his charge of “protecting a weak species universally begotten with the blackest of perversion and decadence” with his rancorous accusations becoming increasingly absurd and malicious with each letter. Eventually our communication link became nonexistent, and upon his admission to the Greensburgh Sanitarium in the closing hours of August 25 it is believed the final remnants of reason were drained from his ravaged mentality. The psychological staff of his employer, the Primetime Championship Wrestling organization, were keenly interested and yet concurrently baffled by the mad contentions he expressed in custody. He spoke of alien gods lurking in ancient slumber across unplumbed points around the globe, something unknown even to me about the ‘acute symmetries of the Elder Keys’ and a range of other bizarre ‘delusions’. Startlingly, his release only days ago was endorsed by the very individuals who proclaimed he would spend the rest of his days incarcerated in the walls of that asylum. Agents of the Coalition, Captain Marcus Stryker and Lieutenant Ryder Slade, officers of the elite Delta Fox special forces unit and trusted allies of Bruce in the recent past before his commitment, were dispatched to pick him up from that ghastly sanitarium. The perceptive duo indicated that upon reaching the outer stoop of the building, meeting with both Shanahan and Doctor Samsanov Okoye, they detected a putrid odor wafting from appeared to be the direction of the doctor. Initially they simply dismissed the possibility and gathered the stench to be emanating from the foul marsh flanking the crumbling structure. And though the behavior of the good physician seemed somewhat queer at the time, they took Shanahan away from that terrible institution and journeyed home. Only a few minutes into the drive home, Stryker and Slade reported Bruce as going into a state of trance and his body being wracked by violent, shuddering convulsions. This was followed by my friend’s seemingly unconscious chanting of some destitute mantra which Captain Stryker claimed were incantation passages borrowed from Ludwig Prinn’s De Vermiis Mysteriis, all this while still under that awful stupor. The spell subsided and Bruce claimed to be fine, going so far as to claim having been “engulfed in a wave of enlightenment alive with the presence of God”. This was good news to us all, and even now we believe it to be true. We believe it to be wholly true, only the ‘God’ is different than the one we of Christian mind, or any of sane mind period, worship at the base of our altars. His normalcy continued for a couple days after his arrival back in Boston. He seemed possessed with a newfound vigor for our cause, and even requested the English-converted copy of that terrible Necronomicon from our Vatican branch in order to, in his words, ‘decipher the meaning behind the prophecies revealed in his dreams’. I now regret to say that we allowed this, even failing to question as to whether or not his disclosed motives were indeed true or if there were more sinister, ulterior reasons to his demand. Within 24 hours of his receiving that dreaded tome, Bruce had departed the area and vanished. Then the first disaster occurred. We got word of a tragedy just outside Anaheim in California, only miles from his encounter with FSW’s Kimbusa in that Golden Turnbuckle Tournament. An entire unit of police operatives were inexplicably slain in a most horrible and violent manner in the confines of an abandoned warehouse, their bodies mangled and ripped apart by some inhumanly powerful force. Found at the scene were a black altar adorned with a hideous string of glyphs and markings and a curious effigy carved in the shape of a monstrous squid-bat hybrid beast. Those who first entered the warehouse afterward claimed to have been consumed by some unexplained feeling of dread still lurking in those shadow-veiled walls, as if whatever had performed the unspeakable acts had left in its (their?) wake an aura of unimaginable evil. No traces of the attacker(s) were found. The Anaheim Police Department claims the case to rank among the most baffling cases they’ve ever investigated, as it has the tell-tale signs of a ferocious animal attack but without any of the customary evidence supporting that theory. We at the Coalition are most disturbed by the glyphs embroidering the walls. We received photographs, at our request, of the markings lining the blackened shrine. I dare not copy them here, as their very existence in this diary will taint its Godliness. I dare only say they are an incantation intended to bring demons upon this world. They call on the minions of the dark, and under the right conditions are capable of summoning into this world the Haunter of the Dark. Bruce now has the Church’s only copy of the Necronomicon. It pains and fills me with dread to say that this is not the expurgated, ‘censored’ version found in other libraries and private collections unfortunate enough to have it defiling their inventory. Not only is it one of the mere three English-translations on Earth, but it is complete and unabridged for the sake of research and contrast by those of the Vatican. There have been no alterations from the original manuscripts of the Mad Arab Abdul Alhazred. In his Bruce holds a weapon potentially more harmful and threatening than any single nuclear warhead; the vile book sustains more evil and more capability of instigating violence than any stash of nuclear arms. It holds incantations and secrets intended to raise beings far more malevolent than any mankind could ever produce. It is difficult to distinguish Bruce’s intentions at this point, but if the bitterness plaguing his personality hasn’t diminished in recent time than I fear he intends on destroying the world. Already the Vatican has been contacted and informed about these events. And as such, they have dispatched their greatest crusader to retrieve the precious volume and snap Bruce out of what manipulates him. I only hope their champion is in time. He was hand-picked by the Pope, his Holiness himself, and has yet to fail. I pray this night for Bruce. I pray he regains his sanity and pulls away form the side of the darkness. If he doesn’t, and the chosen of the Church fails, God help us all. * * * “And how do you feel about the chances of the PURE organization come Red October, Mr. Wilson?” “I feel PURE has both the talent and the power to put those overhyped, gimmick-reliant chump-stains in their place! I believe it’s about time our fair promotion got the recognition it deserves! Those pampered punks won’t know what hit ‘em!...” The television went silent with a hiss, accompanied by the hollow laugh of the shadowy figure bathed in darkness before it. “Insolent mongrels,” snarled the figure. “So presumptuous, so conceited….so foolish….” “M‘lord?” Dropping the hood of his robe, Bruce ‘Violence Jack’ Shanahan turned to squint in the direction of his quivering subordinate. “Brother Varaad…..is there a problem? I did request you leave me to my privacy.” “My most sincere apologies, m’lord,” stammers Brother Varaad with a hasty bow. “But a package has arrived. A package for you personally.” Shanahan’s eyebrows arose to form an arch, and his stocky, robe-shrouded form followed suit. In the background, the boisterous comments of Jamal Wilson continued to bombard the airwaves. “A package?” he growled. “Here?! This lair is secret! The Black Pharaoh himself assured me so! Brother Varaad, I truly hope this isn’t a joke. You know full well the limits of my patience when I request isolation and do not receive it…..” “I assure you, sir, I would do nothing to interrupt your preparations and worship if there did not exist due cause.” Shanahan stalked past the bowed Brother Varaad, his eyes burning with contempt and his muscles taut with dire agitation. Boards creaked beneath his girth and dust clouded the choking air of this ancient and vile mansion in the unpopulated mountains of Vermont. It was the lair of what had come to be the Sect of Black Wisdom by decree of the Black Pharaoh himself, conveyed through the blasphemy-touched dreams of the cult’s high priest, Bruce Shanahan. It had once been known as Wyatt Manor and played home to the Wyatt family, a band of notorious local cut-throats labeled with the reputation of practitioners of the darkest sorcery. And now, over a hundred years later, it harbored an even more insidious faction. Bruce crossed the threshold from his upstairs sanctuary and strode into the hall, brushing past several of his followers that shuddered in his wake. Ducking under the low beam that protruded above the stairs, he rumbled down the grimy stairwell and into the den. On the dust-ridden oak table in the center of the sparse-furnished room, lay a plain manila envelope. With a curious expression he lifted the letter, noticing a lack of return address typically placed in the upper corner. He eagerly wrenched a strip from along the envelope, drawing out a letter marked in carefully-typed font. His heart leapt into his throat and a growl emanated from his throat as the opening line grabbed his attention. My Dearest Brother in Christ, Bruce, It is I, Randall. It may be true that your retreat is hidden from prying eyes on the outskirts of civilization, but just as your agents of the dark have their intelligence, as do we. I speak to you not as an enemy, but as your friend. And a bearer of news. I implore you to return that hideous copy of the Necronomicon. Not even you have yet fathomed the perilous incantations housed in that blackest of volumes, Bruce. The Great Old Ones have no intention of repaying your services, old friend, I myself have seen the lives crushed by their worthless promises from beyond the void. They shall use you for what you can give them, and then when your purpose is all but gone they will destroy you. Return that hideous book and disband the cult of which you lead, and you will receive no punishment from the Blackhelm Coalition or the Church. But continue in this course and you will, alas, be seen in no different eyes than the treacherous forces were are dedicated to defeating. And we shall not differentiate. As of yesterday, the Vatican has dispatched their greatest slayer to kill you and retrieve the Necronomicon. I have never met the man, but have heard only stories of his skill and his talents. I beseech you one last time to give up the book and return to the side of the light, and turn your back on the darkness which feeds your mind with lies and deceit. I fear for you, old friend. I know the pain you suffered through for years, but this is not the answer. Aiding in the Old Ones’ return and the destruction of mankind is not the answer. I only hope you come to see the error of your ways before it is too late. With Regards and Prayers, Father Randall Craven, Roman Catholic Church Bruce crumpled the paper in his quivering right fist, his left upper lip curling in scorn. Brother Asenath, Shanahan’s second-in-command per se, slowly passed into view from the corner of the high priest’s eye. “Is there a problem, sir?” inquires Asenath, taking notice of their master’s perturbed visage. Shanahan’s vile sneer rapidly shifted to that of a cunning smirk. “No…..of course not. Just be sure to warn the perimeter scouts patrolling the grounds that we may have company very soon. But it is nothing that cannot be handled. Not with the power of the Old Ones holding us in their embrace……” He set the crumpled message on the table and grinned. He turned his back to head back upstairs to his ‘studies’, the balled up letter inexplicably erupting into flame. “You heard it here, folks! The PURE faction has guaranteed victory at PCW’s Red October this Sunday! We hope you can join us!....”
