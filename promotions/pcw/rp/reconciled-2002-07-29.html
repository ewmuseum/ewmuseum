Reconciled?

29 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 29th July A strange few weeks for El Diablo, indeed! Where has he been? And just what has he been up to? The Mexican Devil may have had crucial matches in PCW lately against such top names, Duke Williams, Snow, Rent A Villain and is awaiting his World Title #1 Contenders match on Turmoil against Rent A Villain once more, Chris Chase and Akiyama on the special Wednesday Turmoil. But other than that he has been unheard of on TV and off, just where has he been? The scene opens inside of El Diablo's home in Chicago, Illinois. Once more we are welcomed with the sight of El Diablo in his home environment. Diablo is seated in his favorite armchair in his living room. The living room he is seated in consists if that very chair, with a matching leather sofa in a 45% angle away from the chair. A large wide screen television at the front of the room just in front of the window that allows him a view to his garden. A coffee table sits in the center of the room with empty tequila bottles left on it, obviously not from recent drinking as El Diablo is more sober than a man that chooses not to drink, right now. So his mess just shows how much he has let himself go since his wife Angel left him just over three months ago. But she didn't just leave him in any normal circumstances, no, she left him for another woman, a porn star Jenna Jameson. But instead of dwelling on the past, El Diablo is getting on with things and looking to the future. And that looking to the future thing, continues now as El Diablo watches a film he has yet to see. 'Larry, Moe, Curly, you are hilarious,' laughs El Diablo as he watches The Three Stooges meet Hercules. OK, maybe he isn't looking to the future all that well. El Diablo is sitting down with his feet on the coffee table and is wearing an old 'Mexican Devil' T-Shirt he made for himself, while he was still with Angel, and some long black shorts. He is basically sitting in his own filth now, and is eating some spicy noodles as he watches the Three Stooges. 'Wow, I wish I could make great films like this,' comments Diablo to himself as he swallows some noodles, 'The Three Stooges are so funny, and it's great how the director made this film so full of suspense,' he finishes whilst wiping his mouth with his right arm. What an odd comment. Alfred Hitchcock would turn in his grave if he heard that. Of course El Diablo is aware of his upcoming match, but why he isn't training is something only he can know. 'NO THAT'S NOT HERCULES!' yells Diablo at the television, almost dropping the remaining noodles all over himself. Suddenly a loud bell noise is heard in El Diablo's home, which causes El Diablo to leap up and spill his noodles all over his top and some on the carpet. 'Shit!' comments El Diablo angrily, 'But who could it be? Aliens here to abduct me, again? No, they didn't ring my bell last night. Hercules? Shit, I better answer it,' panics Diablo in anticipation of who's at his door. He slowly wanders to the door, as to not alert the person that pressed it, that he is indeed home. He then goes to peer through his doors spy hole. 'Damn, I don't have a spy hole,' he whispers to himself. The Mexican Devil then slowly peers back, takes a deep breath and opens the door. Suddenly his jaw drops. 'Well, aren't you going to let me in?' asks a familiar female voice. Diablo steps back to allow this person in, and then slams the door shut behind him. 'What are you doing here?' Diablo interrogates her. 'I did used to live here,' she responds. El Diablo shakes his head in disbelief at seeing Angel again after all these months. 'Surprised to see me Raul?' she asks him. Angel still has her long dyed black hair. And she is wearing tight leather pants and a black jacket. El Diablo can only nod at the question Angel has asked him. 'So are we just going to stand and talk out here, or are we going to sit down in the living room?' Angel asks business like. Diablo goes to walk into the living room and his wife follows him in there. 'You can sit here,' states Diablo pointing to the sofa. She makes a point to take her jacket off first and places it on the sofa, so she can sit on it. As she does this, El Diablo sits down in his armchair and casually gets the television remote just to mute the television, rather than stop his video. 'Raul, you've really let yourself go,' she comments as she inspects the room, seeing noodles on the floor, tequila bottles on the coffee table and several other health hazards. Diablo looks around as well. 'What makes you say that?' he responds to her. Angel lets out a loud sigh. 'Look at you! You have stains on your shirt, it doesn't look like you've shaved in the months, and you can't even clean up around here,' she gasps at him. El Diablo shrugs. 'It's the way I choose to live,' he says while picking a noodle up off the floor and eating it. Angel looks away. 'Surely you make enough money to hire a house cleaner?' Angel asks in disgust, making sure not to lean against the back of the sofa. El Diablo stops moving and gives Angel an evil look. 'A house cleaner? Do you know what people like that do?' he asks in shock. Angel shakes her head. 'They set up video cameras, to watch your every move. They steal what they can from you, and worst of all, they try to control your life,' he states while looking around nervously. **writers note** Isn't that marriage? **writers note** Angel stares at him blankly. 'Don't you think you're being a little too paranoid?' Angel asks in concern. 'Shhhhhhhh,' Diablo hushes her while holding his right index finger over his mouth, 'The government might here you,' She sighs and rests her forehead against her inner right hand. 'Raul, is everything ok?' she asks him, while seeming deeply concerned about his well-being and state of mind. The Mexican Devil stands up and walks around calmly. 'Of course I'm fine, why wouldn't I be? I mean it's not like my wife left me is it?' speaks El Diablo in some anger. Angel looks up at El Diablo and plays with her hair. 'Well, that's what I wanted to talk to you about,' replies Angel while looking at El Diablo with her beautiful blue eyes. Diablo wanders around the room before sitting down next to Angel in a hump. 'What about it? What? You want a divorce a now? YOU'LL LET THE GOVERNMENT DO THAT TO ME?' questions and yells El Diablo at Angel. Instead of yelling back, she places her right hand over his left. 'When we got married, I made a life commitment, and I didn't mean to break it,' she says as Diablo watches on, 'Maybe I needed a break from you, I don't know. But all I know is that I'm not ending my marriage after only five months. In other words, I want us to have a second chance,' finishes Angel in such a pure voice. Diablo gulps in shock at the recent turn of events. 'Really?' he asks just to make sure. Angel nods her head to him. 'Under one condition, we go to marriage counselling classes,' she says while keeping firmly hold of Diablo's hand. El Diablo stares at her for a few moments. 'Fine!' he responds, 'But if I find out this is a trick, I won't be happy,' Diablo states in certain paranoia. Angel then goes to hug El Diablo. Angel was only the second person that El Diablo has ever cared about, so maybe this will ease his trouble in trusting people. Will this change El Diablo? And will El Diablo ever start to train for his HUGE match on Turmoil? Let's wait and see.
