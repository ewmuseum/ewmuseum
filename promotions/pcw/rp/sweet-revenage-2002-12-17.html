Sweet Revenage

17 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12-17-02 Revenage. It was heavy on my mind. I had been thinking about it more and more since the events that took place at Adrenaline. I had tasted it but it was just a sip of the revenage that I thursted for. They tried to destory my sweet Tessa but I was able to show up and save her. However the match was changed into a tag team match. I got my revenage when we won it but it wasn't enough for me. I needed more. But later they only added fuel to the fire when they cost me my debut match. A match I feel I could have won. But they stole it from me too. I coud still feel that shots to my lower back. The sickening crack of the kendo stick to my back. Each one more painful then the last and each only increasing my desire for their blood. Yet I would have to wait. Tuesday, today, I would once again have to step into the ring to do mortal battle. Once again I walked alone. Yet this time I was going to do what I couldn't do last time. I was going to win. This wasn't Josh Starwind. It wasn't my great rematch. It was a match against a man I never heard of before. But this was common as I was still new to the locker room and had not yet met everyone. But my opponent was going to have to face more then the Perfect Storm. He was going to have to face the Raging Storm also. The sun beat against my face. I could feel the heat on my flesh yet I didn't care. It was hard for me to wake up anymore. The troubles of life was getting to me. I rolled over in my bed and looked out the window. It was a beautiful day outside. I would love to take Tessa and the kids out for a walk in the park on a day like this but Christa. . .she was missing. A victim of the shameless act by the Angel Society. Now there was a giant dark cloud over the apartment. There was just a light missing in our life now. I forced myself out of the bed through. Sulking would do me no good. I needed to move around. I sat up and looked over to the small TV I had. There was a still shoot of myself getting beat down by the Angel Society. On top of th TV next to the case for the tape was a letter. The letter that informed me about my match. Had I been in a better mood I might have opened it a little sooner then the night before my match. 'Should I even both? I know Angel Society will just interfere and cost me the match. I will once again have another match stolen from me.' I told myself as I stood up. I picked up the case for the tape and was about to press eject when my head started to hurt. The room started to spin around me. I tried to lean onto the bed to catch my balance but the room was spinning so fast I missed the bed and fell to the ground. There was a ringing in my ears. I shook my head to get rid of it but a voice cracked the ringing and sent the room spiraling into silence. The room stopped spinning. I regained my sences and looked around the room. I couldn't see anything. 'Where did that voice come from?' I asked out loud. A sudden chill came through the room. A voice sang out again sending a vibe down my spine. 'Weakling' is chanted. I looked around to find the source of the voice. It sounded. . familiar. I knew it from somewheres. Looking around the room I saw I was still alone. The door was shut and has been. 'Who are you!' I shouted out. There was only laughter. That's when I saw it. He looked just like me. Only different. He seemed cold. The image just looked at me with those icy blue eyes. I backed down in fear. Who was he? Why was he in my room? Why did he look just like me? The questions ran threw my head. 'You are so weak. Pathetic really.' he told me. He was so serious in his tone. It was chilling. I reaching inside myself and shouted back to him. 'Shut up! You don't know me. I'm not weak!' I told him. He merely smirked and laughed to himself. I looked at him with a questioning look. Why was he laughing? 'I don't know you? I know more then you think I do.' he told me. I was left wondering. 'So are you just going to give up?' he asked me. I thought about that for a second. Give up? Chris Storm. I can not remember a single time where I gave up on anything. When have I ever rolled over for anyone else before? Never! So why should I give up now? Because two talentless monkeys had to kidnap a little girl as insurance to win a match. That shows they don't have enough talent to win a match on their own. So should I roll over for them? I don't think so. Come the PPV I will roll over them and get back Christa safe and sound. But I need send them a message before the PPV. And I'll use my match on Tuesday to do so. I looked over to the image and gave him a smirk. He gave a full hearted laugh. I knew what to do now. I was going to the revenage I thursted for. I was going bring that smile back to Tessa's face even if it meant I must go to the extremes. I almost feel sorry for my opponent now. He was just a pawn I was using to send a message to the real threat here, Angel Society. If they wanted to interfere in my match yet them. I will be ready for them. They can bring that Kendo stick with them. Because when they leave they will have it shoved straight up where the sun doesn't shine! They have walked into the storm and now they will have to learn to survive it.
