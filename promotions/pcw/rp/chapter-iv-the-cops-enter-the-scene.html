Chapter IV: The Cops Enter The Scene

2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2-2-03 ::Introduction:: Coming off of the surprising lost to Young Tiger Mark is not feeling too great. He is still broken by the match because its exactly the same that happened to him last time he entered PCW. But you have to forget the past and what has happened here in PCW now because Mark has a match against Teresa Tomas for a spot in the Battle Royal at March Madness. Now Mark is not familiar with the history at March Madness and hardly knows what the battle royal is about, but its something big with the World Champion in the royal and this could be a career breaker for Mark. ::End Introduction:: Chapter IV: The Cops Enter The Scene As Mark looks at the dead body right next to him, the streets of the little city are going crazy by what the witnessed. Little kids are going around everywhere shouting and the adults are all looking at Mark with a glare in their eye as they are eyeing the man who just killed one of their men. “What in the hell are you all looking at?” Mark asks as he shouts and then the black men and women all scatter with the kids along the streets. Mark bends down and looks closely at the man who he just killed and he puts his fingers up to the man’s neck and he feels that if he is breathing or not. Mark takes his fingers away and realizes that the man’s body is all cold as ice and is not moving at all. Mark then smiles down onto the body and he realizes what has just happened. Mark brings his arms out from his body at the side and he leans back as he starts to laugh an evil laugh into the sky which is now nearing dusk. “Finally people of this town will know now who the dominate race is, who rules all is me the white race!” Mark shouts as he looks around at the streets and the people who have stopped scattering and looking at Mark. The sounds of police sirens are heard as Mark looks around and he doesn’t see a police car. He then gets up off of his knees and he starts to walk farther and deep into the town’s surroundings as the black people start to gather around the dead man’s body. As Mark gets down the street a bit, a speeding police car pulls up next to him as the police men open up their car door and out comes two black police men. The hold up their guns as they hide behind their opened car doors. “Stop what you are doing and put your hands up!” One of the police men shouts as Mark turns around and notices who is behind him. “Do it NOW!” The other police man says as Mark smiles and gives them a look of evil through his eyes. Mark then turns back around and he starts to count to himself. “1…2…3.” Mark says silently to himself as he takes off down the street as he says three. Mark is hurdling over the obstacles the spectators have set when they all were going crazy throughout the streets. There are some orange crates spilt with oranges spread out all over the place trying to reach up and Mark and take him down. The police get out from behind their protective doors and they start to head down the street after Mark as he is still running down the street, sprinting as fast as he can. “Get that damn Nazi!” The police men say to all of the people around as they look at the spectators. People all then start to chase after Mark, throwing things in his way and also trying to put their bodies in front of Mark. He avoids most of them, but gets tripped up by a little kid who sticks his foot out from under a ice cream stand where he was hiding. Mark falls on his face and breaks the white athletic tape off of the top of his head and the injury that was left the night before is opened once again now as blood starts to fall down the middle of his nose and runs down to his mouth. “Fuckin cracker!” The young kid says as he comes out from underneath the ice cream stand. Mark is dazed for a bit as the kid is coming near Mark. The kid is smiling as Mark looks up from the ground and realizes the braids in the young black kid’s hair. The kid comes over to Mark and he looks him into the eyes. The kid doesn’t say anything except for giving him the bird and kicking Mark in the ribs. Mark gets up in a hurry when that happens and the little kid starts to run after his house in the ghetto. After being on the ground for some time, the cops have now caught up to Mark as it is nearing night time and the street lights are starting to flash with lights. “I am not going to let myself be caught by these apes again.” Mark says aloud as he can feel the heat of their breath on his neck. Mark’s hair on the back of his neck rises as the heat from the police men’s breath is a chilling feeling to Mark. He looks over to his left and he sees an open hole through the fence that is connecting a building to another. Mark heads over to the left towards the gate as the two police men don’t react as quick and over shoot the hole as Mark turns. The police men are trying to regain the ground that they had on Mark, but seems to be too late. Mark slips through the hole that is small enough for him to get through and starts to sprint down the alley between the two buildings. “There you go ruining our chances to get the Neo Nazi who has ruined this town once again.” One of the police men says as they start to shine their flashlights down the deep dark alley as they see Mark in the distance running and they realize they have no chance to catch him.“Well if you reacted quickly then you would’ve gone and caught him.” The other police man says as the two of them start to head towards the car. “So you are going to put this on me?” The first police man says as they go into the distance of the night to their car for another crime scene. Meanwhile Mark feels he is safe away from all of the commotion that was made during the day. Mark slows down and he finally comes to a stop and he leans on the red brick of the building. Mark can feel the roughness of the building go across his opened scrape on his forehead. The dried blood on Mark’s face is the showing of the hard work Mark has been through this day, the sacrifices he has taken for his community have been major and hopefully now this town knows who the main race is. “I need to get out of this place before I have a warrant out on me.” Mark says to himself as he puts his back up against the rough brick and slides down to the hard dirt that makes a cloud as he hits the ground. “Knowing after the events of today, these cops here are going to call the state cops so they can go and look for me and so they can finally through me in jail after all of these years.” Mark closes his eyes as the pain in his right leg is unbearable as he bites his lip to try and make the pain go away in his leg. Nothing is doing though as his head is throbbing in pain and Mark feels up at his scrape and notices the deepness of the gash. Mark can feel the cold brick across his back as his wife beater has been ripped as he went down across the brick and that ripped his beater “For the past couple of years I have tried to make this place realize who the dominant race is.” Mark starts to say as he is thinking back to a couple of years ago. “Doing everything on the black race except for actually killing a person. So maybe, just maybe this will sink into the community here and they will realize how much more powerful I am and my race is to them.” Mark sits down on the ground more thinking of his past and what his childhood is like. Then a crash of a TV is heard in the above apartment as Mark is out of his trance/train of thought. Mark grabs onto the grade in the brick and tries to get a good enough grip to help himself up. Nothing is doing though as Mark tries to use all of his hands to pull himself up, but he then puts his hands down on the ground with his palms against the rough dirt and he pushes himself up off of the ground. He wobbles as he tries to stand because of his bad leg and he starts to head towards the bus stop.
