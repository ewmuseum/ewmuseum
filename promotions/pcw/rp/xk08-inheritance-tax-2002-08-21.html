XK08 : “Inheritance Tax.”

21 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 21/08/02 XK08 : “Inheritance Tax.” Location : A Future Path.Date : 21st August, 2pm. Everybody holds a certain amount of control over their destiny. Barring a few acts of others and legislature of the law, we each have the ability in this free world to run our lives how we see fit. Nearly everyone can do that. That’s boring. Controlling the destiny of others is where the real fun is. “So, give me the numbers.” One such man who enjoys the manipulation of others that comes with control is Xavier Kannon, a man who on Sunday will fight for PCW itself. If he beats Tyler Nelson he will have the entire roster and staff at his disposal. “Attendance is up 22%, sir.” Where we see Xavier Kannon now is his promised land. His Oz. The place where he both desires and fully intends to be. The CEO’s office. “That’s understandable, with Tyler now demoted to a mere Peon jerking the curtain, the fans can freely flock to events to see the likes of Moi... That’s “me” in French incase you didn’t know... Hogging the limelight.” On the front of the elegantly hand-crafted Mahogany desk is a name plate. Not just a simple office decoration but a Gold and Ivory masterpiece that reads. XAVIER KANNON - CEO Telling both the reader that he not only holds the position... But that he WANTS them to know he is the CEO. “How about merchandise?” Behind the desk on a large Black leather chair, the Gold Patron Meritorious relaxes. His questions are elementary, he knows the figures he wants up will be up, and those he wants down will have fallen. This is just Kannon blowing his own trumpet. “Merchandise is up 37%. The new Xavier Kannon & Ellie~! line is doing better than we could have expected. The dramatisation of your story battling the oppression of the wrestling industry really shot your figures up.” It’s hard to believe Kannon is still claiming to be oppressed. He used it with Jeremy Howard, and with Duke... Yet still he used it against Tyler. Whining and moaning about any action the CEO levels at him, no matter how deserved. If he loses on Sunday. If he gets bumped down to just another member of the roster he will probably shout it. If he wins he will say he defeated it. Oppression will always be a part of Xavier’s vocabulary. Just as Sinner will be part of Tyler’s. “Good... It takes me owning the company to finally show that I’m the most marketable man in this company’s history. If only Nelson would have noticed earlier I wouldn’t have had to end his little flirtation with power. Say, how is his merchandise doing? If they are still running it that is.” In nature, many parents will only last for 1 generation. They will starve gathering food for their young. Their own stomachs going empty to fill those of their offspring.. The nutrients they need being regurgitated for their youth to feed on. Will Tyler Nelson die off to fill Xavier’s needs? He gave birth to Xavier at Spring Fling.. Made him Commissioner. When Kannon looked to be getting ahead of himself the Supremacy firings sent a warning shot across his bows and the Nelson/Kannon alliance was stronger than ever. Tyler was putting all he had into Kannon, and when he could willingly give no more... ... Kannon flew the nest. “Tyler Nelson merchandise is down 82% this quarter, and 67% for the year.” Nelson would give Kannon no more. The power hungry Scientologist could not be appeased so he had to ally with someone else.. Someone like the Phantom Booker whose sole aim was to bring about the downfall of Tyler Nelson. Now Kannon has the chance to take the final steam from Tyler’s engine. The final drop of fuel. That last bit of momentum. Tyler can no longer give him anything, so the “Give” has turned to “Take”. “That’s what’ll happen when you job to Jaden four straight in the opening match.” Just what will the victor do with the loser after Sunday? Will they keep someone around who’s new aim in life will be to take back what they lost? Will Kannon fire Nelson? Will Nelson fire Kannon? Their victories made all the sweeter by seeing the back’s of their victim walking back thorough the curtain for the last time. Will they keep each other around? A plaything... A human Executive toy? Maybe play a unique game where they see what they will have to do to make the other quit? “Yeah, Public Opinion went down a bit after the 3rd time.” But the victor must remember he is the owner of PCW. He is not in that position to carry out a vendetta. Could their rivalry be the thing that implodes PCW? Or will the 2 top men in the company’s disdain for each other be the thing that drives it on.. Both in the days before and after 3rd Dawning? “Fuck your “Public Opinion”. This is my company and I can do what I like... The public are a bunch of peons, never forget that.” Well, it’s not like either have run it for the fan’s benefit before. “Yes sir. I’ll make a note of it.” Is this what it would be like? Xavier Kannon the wrestling Scientologist becoming Xavier Kannon the manipulative business man? Can someone who’s life for the last year has been wrestling night in and night out really be comfortable in an office chair by day? Hate can make people change. The desire to get one up on Nelson may bring out a new side to Xavier... Every 1% rise in merchandise will be a victory over the Nelson regime. Any .1 rise for a TV show would be a declaration that he can do it better. Did the father teach the son too much? “PCW must feel like Europe when it came out of the Dark Ages.... Greece in the time of great Philosophers. The company has had a epiphany... And a worthy owner...” We zoom right in on the beaming Green eyes of Xavier.... The reflection of the documents before his mapping onto the sheen of his eyeball before he blinks and the camera zooms out. “4 days to go...” There is now no Mahogany desk. No Gold and Ivory name plate. No merchandise figures. No PCW employees. Just the Scientologist and this thoughts. His dreams. His visions. “Just 4 more days.” Those words are true. But are they in his context? Is PCW just 4 days away from a regime led by Kannon? Only one man can stop him. But the choice Tyler may have to make is what he wants to save more... Xavier Kannon... Or PCW.
