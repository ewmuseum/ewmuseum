Nothing is trivial

Date: not entered The city was at the time of rest for the late night/early morning in time. In the late minutes of 11:00, there was Eddy still up, staring among the ceiling fan circling like a tire wheel above his head. Eyes pointed up, hands resting behind his neck, bed untidy and floor covered in laundry, looking despond. Soft Native American music playing in the background, solemnly casting it’s milieu among the room. It was dark, cold and rather musty inside the room Eddy was in. It was an old hotel room in an effete Wyoming region. There wasn’t a TV in the room it looked like, and other then the radio’s red light showing the track number, which was track 3, no light. Eddy had this preference for awhile. He looked completely hopeless and burdened, but in this moment he felt as optimistic as ever. He had no honest reason to be either. He’s been idle from PCW momentarily and would admit lately he’s done nothing of significant importance to PCW’s roster. But on the bright side he had a chance to take or leave. He could fight for an honorable PCW spot. Fusion was the day to prove everybody who doubted him wrong. Young Tiger Vs. Rich Cannon was the match. Eddy knew Rich Cannon from awhile back, beating him in the first round of a tournament but other then that not having much contact. Eddy felt as he would win and would backfire at every anemic effort Rich Cannon did to become winner of the match. He would climb rankings. He would transcendent a spot in people’s minds and his opponent’s minds. Even more glorifying, he’d transcendent a spot in his own mind. All these optimistic thoughts were in Eddy’s tenebrous self sprawled among the bed, sleepless. He didn’t care. In every aspect, he knew he’d win. He stood up onto the wooden floor with a potential splinter harm about to happen if he walked without shoes. Fortunately, he slipped on a pair of blue house slippers and walked across the floor, flipping the light switch on. The room was surrounded with light for a couple of seconds as the light flickered for a second, darkening the room for an iota of a second and then flashing back on for another second. Redundantly, this repeated for about 10 seconds before the light completely dimmed into darkness. Eddy sighed and muttered over the cheap light bulbs as he opened the door for outside. The outside world was so austere it looked as if nothing could grow. The ground was hard dirt with fissures everywhere. He stared at his truck: a red Durango parked alongside the house in a parallel manner. He looked at Wyoming and it’s infertile soil and made a glance back to his truck. 'I have to get out of this place…For more flourished grounds and more pretty trees…Somewhere to meditate my thoughts…' Eddy walked back inside and grabbed two briefcases of clothes and other paraphernalia. He tossed his bags into the back of his truck as he used his keys to press a button to take the alarm off. In a powder blue bath robe and house shoes, Eddy stepped into the car, taking a glance at the barren Wyoming soils before moving outward and onward to someplace east. He ran over potholes in the soil as he drove, tires bumping up and down as he drove slowly. It didn’t help much but it helped enough to continue driving slow. 'Rich Cannon is a chance to earn more respect for the spot I have in PCW. I will climb upward into the heights of something respectable. I will exert all strength and defeat Rich Cannon on Fusion, I guarantee it on the flourishing and progression of nature.' Eddy drove off and away- a long way.
