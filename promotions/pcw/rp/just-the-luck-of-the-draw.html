Title: Just the luck of the draw.... Featuring: Tim Walden Date: Feb.2, 2003 Damn it....just....damn it. All I wanted was to earn my spot at March Madness, to get a World Title shot on pay-per-view, to have another major spotlight to prove my abilities in for the Front Office and for the fans...All I wanted was the one shot, to avoid the pain that came the year before..Flaymin freakin' Aymin for the umpteenth time in a month, killing my hopes yet again with his cheap tactics, I wanted a real opponent who wasn't going to be overly difficult to dispatch, but would not necessarily be a pushover either... PCW Financial Reclamation Office...Wednesday, January 29, 2003. I sat at the desk, thinking about the previous night's tainted victory over Chet..Not exactly legal, but at it'd teach him to leech out of the company funds with his extended injury leave. I looked over my newest set of tax forms off the printers, every box designated with its entry and every potential loophole closed and knotted shut....This would do very nicely for tax ti--The phone rang. Call display: Pimply? I looked out the window with a sigh as I picked up.....'Tim Walden, PCW FRE, CPA, ODB, QED, SUV, OLP on MP3.' Sheesh...I really had to find better ways to amuse myself. 'What can I do for ya, Pimpster?' 'Er....yeah...um...well...' I really wish he'd take some sort of assertiveness or self-confidence classes, get a little more prompt in telling me things...Of course then knowing my luck, his acne'd start to clear up, he'd get less clumsy...Hell, he'd probably turn normal, and what'd be the entertainment in that? I mean, he can't even call personnel without stumbling over entire sentences at a time..'Mr. Walden, you know it's around the time that the March Madness qualifying match pairings are arranged, and I was wondering who you'd like to be paired against.' Ack, I'd entirely forgotten it in the wake of the moment....I needed to make the most of an opportunity like this, at least from my perspective...I could easily just book myself against a jobber and get the easy road in, that's why they'd give me the opportunity anyway, to make it easier on us as a group..But what good would it be to beat up some loser in a lopsided match? They'd just jeer anyway because they don't like me in the first place, and there's no respect to be gained from that.... 'Er, sir, are you there?' 'Oh, yeah, I'm still here...Just taking a moment to think.' Bah..I didn't have anything better to do now, I really should pick Tomas and get her over with...but she's too tough, and it's not worth risking the double whammy of losing my spot to her, a repeat of the Aymin episode...'I'll tell you what, Pimply, I'll pick my opponent in person. Could you just clip out a list of the roster and put the names in a hat, but take out the World Title contenders and Teresa Tomas from the list?' 'Uh, okay sir..I'll be right over in about 5 minutes.' This was a relief to me...I had nobody to deal with except for at worst the Continental contenders, the other guys in the TV hunt, or the barely there Tag Division, nothi-- 'Wait!' I had something to worry about after all...I didn't even think of it until I got the ball rolling, the sort of thing that just tended to slip my mind. Had he hung up yet? I couldn't be sure, he tended to do things in rather quiet f-- 'Er, yes?' 'Take out Total Violation as well, okay?' Well, that oughta take care of any trouble I'll have...At least for now....I can breathe easy, the opposition has to be easy enough that I'll get through it without any major trouble. What did I have to fear? Gadget-Girl? Kano?? Surely not El Romantico again...But what if it was? 'All righty, I'll be there in a jiff!' He seemed almost too happy with having a new task to do...But I guess it made sense, he wasn't going to be doing anything better tonight...Manual labor might actually be the highlight of his day. I sat back, sliding the papers to a corner of the desk so that there would be more room for my coffee without spilling..It was probably worth my effort in making the request, even I had a need for a little luck of the draw in my day...Could be anyone from Nerezza to Mark Michigan, could be a real challenge or just an easy way in...But at least this was sort of fair. I leaned back and looked at the ceiling... Relaxed. Clunk. Whump. Clang! 'Ow!' A faint rustle... ...The sounds of the Pimply one making his entrance, hat full of names in hand. 'All right, only fair that I take a relatively random choice, it's just for my own self-confidence's sake....And if I pull out Flaymin Aymin, tear it up. I don't want to have to commit manslaughter against him.' I put my hand into the relatively small hat which acts as the draw bowl, closing eyes for the most part as I rustle around, then triumphantly remove a name... 'GAZ GR--Bah, that doesn't count, we both deserve singular shots...Here I go again!' I dipped my hand back in, pushing toward the bottom more than the first time, shaking around my hand back and forth to mix things up well before finally pulling another slip of paper out...Unfolding it, I could only look on stunned. 'TESSA WINDSOR'.... 'Hey, isn't she the unbeaten girl?'....What was it now...6-or-7-and-0.....Tag Team champion now...Worst of all, with a legion of close friends to help out if things went bad... 'Damn it.....just.....damn it!!' 'If you don't like it, we can just pretend it didn't happen...' This offended me to a point now. He was implying that I didn't feel up to the challenge put in front of me, the opposition I needed to beat to get to my first World Title match on Pay-Per-View...I didn't feel not up to it, at least not with certainty. What was bothersome here was that I had no true foreknowledge of my own, no way to determine if she was likely to beat me or not.. I could take one of two roads now. I could choose a new name from the hat, someone easier perhaps to gauge as a soon-to-be loser, someone I had the confidence to beat with a little better than the normal effort...Or I could take the risk. I could fight her...Win, and I'd be 'the streak-breaker', 'the guy that out-technicaled Tessa', 'The winner over Windsor'...Lose, and what would they think of me then? 'Just wasn't enough to stop her here', 'Fought as valiantly as one could have hoped from Walden', 'Just like last year...Too close, and yet too far. ...Either I'd make myself known, or I'd just add to someone else's credibility...Both ways lead to news. 'Eh, why not? I think I can take her.' ---- View Tim Walden's Biography
