Date: not entered When we last left off, Flaymin had convinced himself that the missing piece in the puzzle, better known as his life, was a pet.A simple pet.One that he could pat.And appear in bestiality porno’s with….. [Scene opens outside a pet store in a suburban area. The store, ‘Bob’s Animal Kingdom’ is only small in size and has a few puppies in the front window. Flaymin Aymin walks into the store and begins to browse through the aisles.He taps the fish tank and watches the goldfish swim around. He looks around and notices no sales assistant nearby, so he rolls up his sleeves and dips his hand into the tank in an attempt to score himself a free goldfish. Suddenly a sales assistant pops up from nowhere and hastily pulls Flaymin’s hand out of the tank] Sales Assistant: Can I help you sir? [Flaymin look’s at the Sales Assistant’s name tag which reads ‘Bob’. Flaymin: Yes Bob, I’d like that goldfish right there. [Flaymin looks closely at the tank for any other interesting fish] Flaymin: And I’ll take that big, chunky one with the blue spots. Bob: Very well, sir. [Bob fetches the fish net and puts it into the water as he tries to scoop up the selected fish] Flaymin: Oh and I’d like some tartar sauce with that, thanks. Bob: Excuse me?? Flaymin: With the fish. [Bob looks at the tiny goldfish and then back up at Flaymin] Bob: What are you talking about? Flaymin: You can’t eat fish without tartar sauce…. What kind of restaurant is this? Bob: This isn’t a restaurant… it’s a pet store!! Flaymin: Oh right…. [Bob puts the goldfish back in the tank and places the fish net on a table nearby] Bob: Are you after any animal in particular? Flaymin: Uh.….I don’t know what I want really. Do you have any giraffes? [Bob looks around the small pet store, which is filled with hamsters, puppies, kittens, birds and fish] Bob: Do you see any giraffes? Flaymin: No, but that doesn’t mean you don’t have any out the back. Bob: No, there are no giraffes- not out here or out the back. Flaymin: Not even a baby one? [Aggravated by Flaymin’s stupid questions, Bob raises his voice] Bob: NO, not even baby ones!! Flaymin: How can you call this a pet store if you don’t even have the king of the jungle!?!? Bob: Err… that would be the lion. Flaymin: Sure, I’ll take one of those! Bob: No, no, I didn’t say we had one in stock. Flaymin: No Giraffes…No lions….What the hell do you have? Bob: We have little household pets, like kittens. Flaymin: Pfft…. I’ve already got a cat collection. [Bob looks at Flaymin with genuine surprise] Bob: Really? Flaymin: Yep- they’re plastered all over my car tyres. Bob: Eck!Well it seems to me that you like unique and adventurous animals.Are you much of an adventurous person? Flaymin: Well I did hike around Mount Everest…… Bob: Wow, that’s impressive.How long did it take you to get to the top? Flaymin: What?I just said that I went AROUND the damn thing- not climb it. [Bob raises an eyebrow] Bob: What’s the point of that? Flaymin: Listen buddy, the only hills I climb are sized 36DD…. [Flaymin winks at a now increasingly frustrated Bob] Bob: I’m sorry but I won’t be able to help you out today, sir.You might like to try a larger pet store that caters more to your needs. Flaymin: You mean like a zoo? Bob: Sure, I’m certain that they’ll be able to help you out. Flaymin: Cool.Well thanks for your help Bob! [Bob shakes his head in bewilderment as Flaymin merrily strolls out of the store…. Destined for a trip to the zoo] Scene fades to black
