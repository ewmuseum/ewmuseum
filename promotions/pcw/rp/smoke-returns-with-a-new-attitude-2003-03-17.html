Smoke returns with a new attitude...

17 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: March 17, 2003 {{{{Scene}}}}The scene opens up with a movie clip of several clips of Semore Smoke in action. The first one has a shot of Smoke in his debut match against Violence Jack, and it shows the end where he and Violence Jack both are counted out. It then shows clips of his feud against Jon Kano and “Controversial” Corey Williams, the first being his win over the two at Wrestle Vessel, then the end is where he helped Kano end Williams’ career. The next clip shows him beating Eddie Mandrake, Scorpion, and “Blayze” for the PCW Rising Star championship at yet another Pay Per View. It shows his short feud with The End when he was PCW Extreme champion and his heated loss to The End for the title. It shows the last real match he was in, the clusterfuck known as Alliances, his team containing Jon Kano, himself, Mark Michigan, and Mark Amulet. His lost in that and the critical injury to his back, he was forced to leave and was said to never return... But last night, you saw him return to PCW, in the biggest Clusterfuck so far this year, the 30-man battle royal known as the March Madness Rumble. Of course he didn’t win, but he seems to have a new enemy in PCW, AJ Trinidad. Clips are shown of Trinidad and Smoke as they battle it out in the ring. It then fades from the clips and opens up outside a street in a clouded suburb. Semore Smoke is walking down the street with a grin on his face. He’s wearing what seem to be conservative clothes, a suit and tie, along with dress shoes to match. He’s grown his hair out though, and has a shaved goatee that looks a lot like WWE’s Stone Cold Steve Austin’s. He stops short from the camera and looks straight at it... Semore Smoke: So, you got the stuff? {{{{The camera shifts around and you see another man standing behind where the camera angle was, this man is dressed more scruffy looking then Smoke is and he seems like he might be homeless. He’s wearing a torn white shirt, and a pair of old faded blue jeans. His feet are bare and in his hand is a bag. The man looks down at the bag and lifts it into the air and then looks at Smoke with a grin on his face as well...}}}} Man: Yes sir. I’ve got it right here like you asked. Are you sure you don’t want to make this transfer in a secluded area? {{{{Smoke continues to look at the man cockily...}}}} Semore Smoke: Yes I’m sure. I own this town right now, my pap owned this town for years, and now that he’s dead, I own the town with an iron grip. {{{{The man shrugs and then slides the bag over towards Smoke who has his hands in his coat, reaching for something. He pulls out, and the man looks scared as if it were a gun that Smoke was pulling out, but it turns out being his checkbook. Smoke opens it and pulls out a pen and then asks...}}}} Semore Smoke: Shall I make it out to Andrew Smith? {{{{Andrew nods his head with a look of relief on his face...}}}} Andrew Smith: Might I say that truly I am glad to be doing business with you. I always liked working with your father, and to be quite honest, I loved watching you wrestle on TV last year as well in both NTICW and PCW. {{{{Smoke smiles as he signs the check and tears it out of his book. He walks over to Andrew and places the check in his hand...}}}} Semore Smoke: Thanks, I’m flattered that you liked my wrestling. I bet you also liked my return last night at the Battle Royal main event. {{{{Andrew chuckles and nods his head in agreement...}}}} Andrew Smith: Yes! I was extremely surprised to hear the news. Maybe with this money I can buy a home and have a TV again and watch you on your shows again. {{{{Smoke turns his back to the man and chuckles as well as he begins to bend down to grab the bag. He turns around and this time he has a gun in his hand, he must have pulled out of his pocket as he turned around. Andrew is shocked again and begins to back away from Smoke as Smoke lifts the bag and walks towards the man...}}}} Semore Smoke: I doubt you’ll ever see me wrestle again Drew... Why did you screw my father over a year ago? {{{{Andrew shakes his head in horror as he tries to defend himself...}}}} Andrew Smith: You have me all wrong! It wasn’t my fault! He forced me talk! {{{{Smoke pulls Andrew aside and sticks the gun in his gut, and you can get a closer look at the gun and see that it’s a PP7 with a Silencer, like a James Bond style gun. Smoke then asks angrily, losing all humor he had...}}}} Semore Smoke: Who made you!? Tell me! Who is it!? {{{{Andrew is now crying as he knows he’s dead no matter what...}}}} Andrew Smith: I can’t tell you! If I tell you then he’ll kill me! I can’t! Semore Smoke: If you don’t tell me, then, I’m going to kill you! Now tell me! Andrew Smith: I told you! I can’t! {{{{Smoke drags him into a nearby ally and takes him behind a dumpster. He then throws him against a wall and points the gun at him and says...}}}} Semore Smoke: OK, I’ll give you one last try. Who killed my pop? {{{{Andrew cowers into the corner and begins to sob in his hands...}}}} Andrew Smith: I can’t tell you, you know if I tell you, that he'll kill me and I'd rather die right now then die by him. {{{{Smoke sighs as he looks down at Andrew, he then fires the gun three times, hitting him once in the head and two times in the chest. Smoke then reaches down over the slumped body of Andrew Smith and pulls out the check that he had given him and tears it up. He throws the pieces into the dumpster beside the body and then walks away with his head high. Smoke walks onto the street and opens up the bag and takes out small bushel of marijuana. He then puts it back in and closes the bag...}}}} Semore Smoke: At least this wasn't too much of a waste... Today New Jersey, tomorrow PCW, and the day after that, the World! {{{{The scene fades on Smoke's grinning face...}}}}
