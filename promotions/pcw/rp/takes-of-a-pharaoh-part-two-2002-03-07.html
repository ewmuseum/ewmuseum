Takes of a Pharaoh [Part Two]

7 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7/3/02 Tales of a Pharaoh [Part Two] Intro: Well here we are again, the second part of the ‘Tales Of A Pharaoh’ saga. Last time we saw The Pharaoh he’d ran into a little bit of trouble, or to be more exact he’d ran face first into the deep, dark double barrels of a seemingly loaded shot gun! Well Apart from this you already know what other things are going on in the Egyptian Gods great life…this Sunday for Example he and Akiyama have to put their tag team titles on the Line against Legend Inc. and Frisco…anyway enough of this simplistic chatter and on with our story! Location: Unknown Locality, TexasTime: 4: 01 PM “Look, madam, I don’t mean to be more of an annoyance than I already have been but…well your Gun won’t injure me in any way, shape or form…I am immortal!” I stated with a look of arrogance. The Ugly woman in front of me did not, however, take to my statement and obviously made her own conclusion on whether her gun was suitable to hurt me. “I don’t think so, buddy! I suggest you stop running your trap and listen to what I have to say!” she smiled sadistically before adding “or Else there’s gonna’ be a hole the size of this state in your mug!” I contemplated her words with carefully thought, and stupidly, and fairly cockily shot back at her “I doubt it, perhaps the shot would result in being the size of a baseball but not the size of Texas…” Big mistake… “Why don’t you just shut your cheeky mouth, son!” she bellowed causing a few of the customers still hiding under their tables to cower further down towards the floor. “I could tell you and your friends were trouble from the moment I saw ya’!” She was obviously angry, but I truly doubted that she’d pull the trigger on the gun in her hands. “Why don’t you just put that gun away, I’m more than willing to pay for any damage that my friends and I have caused…” I said with a charming smile. This couldn’t fail; the smile of a god is more than enough to charm even the ugliest of women. But alas…it didn’t seem to have any effect on this lady…if that’s what she could be called… “Money isn’t a matter here!” she started “It’s the fact that you’ve driven half my customers into hiding or just driven them out of my café! It’s all down to respect and currently the amount of it that this place had has now has dropped!” She gave me a brooding frown as if contemplating a suitable punishment for what I had caused. Before long she spoke again. “I’ll tells ya what, young un’, if you and your friends wanna’ get outta’ here alive then I suggest you do the cleaning for the next two days!” She smiled victoriously but I would soon end her moment “Look, Miss, my friends and I are of a high calibre of personage, we need not worry ourselves with “suggestions” from stupid mortals like yourself! Now please, before you do something you might regret, put down the gun and let me be on my way…” Suddenly she loaded the shotgun up and pulled back the trigger, but not too far as to shoot a set of bullets from its two dark barrels. “Don’t insult me, boy, get your scrawny ass in the kitchen along with your friends and get down to cleaning up! It’s the only way you’re getting out of here!” she was obviously becoming more infuriated by the second but I ignored this and pushed the guns two barrels away from my face, she looked on in shock as I snatched the hazardous weapon away from her grubby hands and tossed it to the ground. “Get out of my way!” I shouted with a scowl. Immediately she removed herself from my wake and stepped aside to let me pass. I walked calmly to the café’s door and pushed, opening it. A tiny bell at the top of the door rang out as it closed the creaky old door behind me. Suddenly as if from nowhere a deafening bang rang out in the dusty valley in front of me, the sound of glass shattering followed and them a few hasty screams followed that. I instinctively drooped to the dirt-covered floor and placed my hands over my head. After a few seconds I looked up to see what was going on and to my surprise the woman had blown a hole the size of a cricket ball through her café’s front door! I gasped as I saw her looming towards the glassless frame and open it from the inside. “And just where do you think you’re goin’?” she grunted. She slowly walked towards me but resulted in being too slow to catch me. I leapt to my feet and began to run from the crazy old hag. Bullets flew about the area yet Akiyama and Jay were nowhere to be found. Where are they? They can’t be far… Yet as time went on and as the café became only a tiny image in the distance of time and space my thoughts became doubtful…my two friends and fellow Divinity members were nowhere to be found, nowhere at all! And now I was beginning to worry…a very unusual thing for a god to do, I know, but I truly was. I hadn’t seen my tag team partner and Jay for hours now and twilight was almost upon me…the harsh Texan roads, or dirt paths as they were more realistically in equivalent to, weren’t helping matters either. Every time I turned onto one it led off into a hazy space of sand and dirt…this was hopeless and I wasn’t getting anywhere, where on earth had Akira and Sylo got to…I remembered seeing them go off outside the café fighting with their biker opponents but since that moment I hadn’t spotted them…at all. I looked on over the red horizon that had begun to settle over the land painting it a crimson orange. I was going to have to find some sort of place of rest before twilight truly did fall upon me. I looked around in hope of spotting a small hut, well lit and warm from the soon to be freezing atmosphere of the Texan state but nothing could be spotted…nothing at all… “I am a God!” I said to myself “I can get through this!” I now concentrated on trying to find a source of fuel. A warm fire would keep me heated and food could be cooked over it, immortals don’t usually get hungry but I had only eaten a bite of that disgusting burger all day… Once again though, and to my utmost annoyance no such source of fuel was to be found. “Damn it! I hate this Mortal world,” I screamed out, my words echoed in the surrounding ravines and mountains. I was becoming more stressed than I had been when I first discovered who the divinity’s opponents for Sunday Sabotage were. Legend Inc. and Frisco certainly would be a force to overcome but right now I promised that if I overcame this situation then on Sunday they were going down at the hands of PCW’s two godly beings, Akiyama and The Pharaoh! I continued through the night, seldom stopping, I needed to find civilisation if I was to make it to Sundays event, or more importantly I needed to find Akiyama and Jay! Where in my Godly name were they? I had not previously dared to think this thought but right now, alone in the desert, I thought it…perhaps they had been defeated by the three bikers…it was a terrible thought to think, but it seemed like the only explanation for their abrupt disappearance… In the distance I could actually, and finally I’ll add, make out a small village, well lit and brightly shining against the dark hue of the night sky around me. “Ah!” I said, “Finally I find what I have been looking for…” This was a fond site indeed! When at last I reached the village perhaps I would find some sort of news on the whereabouts of my celestial friends…I truly needed to find them…obviously without Akira at my side on Sunday then things would become quite difficult. I jogged now, every step I took brought me closer to the tiny village. Finally I drew up outside its entrance. ‘Welcome to…’ the name had worn off the sign hanging loosely from a metal rafter high above the settlement’s gates, most likely the effects of weathering. I took a step closer and peered inside; I gazed upon a town at rest. Nobody knew I was here…nobody; for sleep had overtaken their tired eyes; for they were Mortals…all of them…you see Mortals have a different smell than Immortals, yet only us immortal beings can smell it; don’t get me wrong it isn’t a bad smell, just one that is easily detected by the ever-living, like me. Immortals too have a smell, a fresh smell: one that seems to linger in the air, bright, and radiant and loving, yet dark and brooding, and terrible. It can not be easily described; not without trouble… I sniffed the air; if Akiyama and Jay had been here then I would be able to smell them… I shuddered…I could smell nothing of my friends…”No,” I whispered… “It cannot be!” I sniffed again in hope of picking up a trace, but as before I smelled nothing of them. They were not here and I was alone again…in a place without a name; all who dwelled here were sleeping; waiting to be disturbed by a being like they had never laid eyes upon before…me…The Pharaoh… To Be Continued…
