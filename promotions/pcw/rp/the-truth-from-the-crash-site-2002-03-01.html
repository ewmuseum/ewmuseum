The Truth From the Crash Site

1 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/1/02 OOC: Italics means Rush’s thoughts. In many stories, a phoenix, a bird with magical regenerative power and healing, rises triumphantly from the ashes, reborn with a new life. Arising from the ashes is a symbolic metamorphosis, one both literal and figurative. Literally, the bird goes up in flames, and falls to the ground. Seeming dead, the bird lives out its last few seconds in agony, and then there is nothing but ashes. From these ashes form a small phoenix chick, one that will complete this cycle many times. In a figurative form, this is the cycle of life continuing as it does in the real world. We are born, and we die. What matters is what we do in this time, and some say, that if that is commendable, then we can return to a form of life, be born again. With March Madness around the corner, and Rush presumed dead in a car crash, this may mean a 19 man field or a replacement, but no replacement has been made and no change to the field has been made. Perhaps PCW believes this is just another case of death jobber syndrome, which states that anyone who dies and comes back is famous, or something like that. But how could real life be just part of PCW angle purposes. However, we’ve seen real life pulled into PCW many times. The Krayzie One’s pursuit of his brother and son, The End’s gruesome past, Delores Marx and Doug Troy. Is this just another attempt at fame for a new face in PCW? Is the Rush really in a state to wrestle? We will find out in this promo about whether Rush has survived the crash or not. A World Title Shot. Rush hasn’t been able to discuss his placement in the field much, as he has been presumed dead or kidnapped for most of the time that he has known about this turn of events. In fact, Rush may not even know that he holds the 18th spot in the Battle Royal. The only men who enter after him are current champ, Jason Snow, and PCW CEO and Commissioner, Tyler Nelson. This gives him a better shot than 17 other men in the Brawl For It All. Many people have failed to win a world title in their first shot at it. However, Rush, if alive, will make every humanly effort, with every last breath, to somehow make it to March Madness and walk out with Jason Snow’s belt. In the mean time, we have a car crash to check out. The shot opens up a deep canyon, apparently around where Rush was last seen alive. The canyon, deep and twisting, has water running through it, and it’s only lucky that Rush didn’t plunge off a cliff into the water below. Lucky if he is, somehow alive. The water carves away at the rock, time carves away at life. We go to the scene of the car, which is still in its compressed form. The sheer impact of the crash has taken its toll on the BMW M5, which was looking sleek jet black less than 72 hours ago. Almost everything is now wrong with the car, and the worst part is, there seems to be no movement from the inside of the car. All is still on the car’s interior. The camera somehow gets to a small part of the car’s inside. A terrible sight is seen on the inside. A figure can be seen in the dark, throat slashed and blood caked over the throat. This means that Rush is dead, that PCW has truly lost the Crunk as Hell God, a young superstar ready to make it time for his big break. There is no more reason for the camera to keep running; however for some reason it continues. Movement. Somewhere inside the car, there is the tiniest bit of movement caught on the corner of the camera. The camera turns to see a moving figure, this one who can clearly be identified as Rush Mason! He has very serious cuts on his forehead and his left arm is hanging a little limp, but other than that, he seems very well for a man who was dead in the eyes of many people. He crawls slowly, his left arm not very useful in pulling himself up to the front seat of the car, with the other corpse of an unidentifiable man. Finally, he sits exhausted from the work that would normally be a piece of cake, but he has been unconscious for almost two days, and his left arm is almost useless in its current state. He sits, exhausted in the front seat, contemplating his future. Well, this may be it. I made a stupid choice. I meant to go off the road. I thought that somehow I would make it out alive. Well I did and look at me now. I’m in the middle of nowhere, in a broken down car. I’m beat up as shit. My mouth is too dry to even attempt talking, and I smell like a corpse. I’m probably going to die out here, and miss out on my World Title shot. Rush decides to go for the gold, just like Sarah Hughes did against those INFERIOR COUNTRIES~! INFERIOR COUNTRIES~! I SAY! Rush decides to try and exit the car. He puts one leg out the smashed in window and quickly swing his other leg over, collapsing onto the ground as soon as he reaches the outside of the car. He cringes in extreme pain as he puts out his left wrist to stop his fall, and pain goes shooting through his arm. He opens his mouth to scream in pain, but no sound comes out. Fuck, this is the worst pain I’ve ever felt and I can’t do anything about it. Damn, a doctor sure would be nice now, but no I’m stuck out here in who the hell knows where, with a damn broken arm and I’m next to this piece of shit car that has brought me only pain and fucking agony. Well, at least I’m better than that guy who came down on top of me. I’m not sure where the fuck he came from, but he tried to throw me from the wheel down that canyon over there, but if it weren’t for him, I don’t think I’d be alive. I somehow got thrown to the backseat when he was at the wheel, and the last thing I heard before I blacked out was his scream, and then there was the crash The crash, I can’ t remember much about it, but I know that I never felt any pain. Wait a second, how Did I survive the crash? If I got thrown to the backseat, shouldn’t I have been thrown forward? Well I guess I’ll find out someday if I ever get out of this stupid canyon. Rush looks around at his surroundings, which just re-enforce the fact that he is utterly alone. A cold wind whips around the canyon, and the sun is setting. Rush looks at the beautiful things around him, but Rush can call not much beautiful at this time. The wind is just another factor of cold, and all he has on is a gray sleeveless t-shirt. Man, I’m cold as hell. This place sure would be beautiful though, if I weren’t so exhausted and hurting. How the hell am I gonna make it to March Madness? For one, my arm feels like it got run over by an eighteen-wheeler. Then there’s the simple fact that I keep being reminded of: I have no idea where I am, and I may not make it out of here anyway. Well since I’m complaining, why the hell did those crazy dudes have to kidnap me anyway? I mean, they could’ve at least had the decency to wait until after the Crunk as Hell God had won at March Madness to kidnap me. These Satanists lose more and more manners these days. I wonder if they’ve given up on me and put someone else in the match since I’ve been pretty much gone for 3 days. I hope they just think I’m in jail, acting like a stupid Portuguese guy or something. Oh great, just what I need. They’re probably talking about how they always knew the black guy would go to jail, because they could smell the weed when I walked into the room. Rush probably has more comments, but at this very moment he passes out from sheer exhaustion. Later, the five men who were sent by Damien in Rush’s last promo arrive at the seen. This cannot be good for Rush. The five men advance to where Rush was last seen, but he is nowhere to be found. They continue to search around this area, but there is no sign of the Crunk as Hell God. They continue to search as the camera switches to another shot. In the nearby cover of forest, Rush’s prone form is lying on a bed of pine needles, and all is silent. The voices of the five searching men can be heard all around, but Rush’s body has been moved somehow from its spot near the car to this hidden place in the woods. However, no one can be seen near the body. The five men head back towards the road, and soon after the sounds of their cars can be heard in the not so far away distance. With this, a strange sight can be seen. The black-cloaked figure of the Master rises from seemingly out of nowhere; he blends so well with the dark forest. He gets up and looks around, making sure he is not being followed. He picks up the body of Rush, which is cold from being exposed to the elements so long. Master realizes and runs off through the woods, as rain begins to come down upon the wooded area. The rain and fog rolling in make for an eerie sight, but the camera can now not see very far, so we must fade to black…
