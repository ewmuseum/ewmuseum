Phase In, Phase Out

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 29.11.02 (#6 FF) So with the mission underway, our hickish missionary from Vermont, Abner, is at the helm. He’s sailing a ship you see; a very important ship. He’s sailing a ship for freedom. Yes, that’s right, freedom. Freedom of oppression from the choking grasp of the masses, of the public…..of the people...... See, people suck. Opinion? No, it’s simply a fact. Oh sure, some will make you feel good, and some claim to be your “friends”, but in the end, we all know (though maybe not accept) the truth: their all in it for themselves. Greedy, conniving, back-stabbing bastards is all. The only term I can think of is “self-centered”, and not even that can sum it all up. Abner knows this, and Abner understands this, but then, you might ask, why does he love the people? Well you see, Abner, as strange as this might sound, is a very optimistic individual. He may not be too bright, he may not have any form of self-control, and hey, he may even be considered an alcoholic.....but is he good person? Emphatically, the answer is “yes”. Yes, Abner is a good person. And yes, Abner does truly care for the people. The paradox (as they continue to emerge in this story that is abner's life), is simply that Abner loves people, people who do not 'deserve' this gift from him…..the greatest one can give. People, as I previously mentioned, tend to be greedy, corrupt, phony…..etc…..etc…..and from a pessimistic point of view, maybe they don’t deserve anything “good”. But that’s not true. You see, as bad as they are, at least they try. Their very nature is seemingly “evil”, and so, when combating this 'evil', one would expect them to fall a few times along the way. Naturally “evil” I said? Yes, but not in the Puritanical sense: more in that we should accept the fact that what we perceive as “evil”,we are, but also understanding that what we perceive as “evil”, may in fact, not be so “evil”. So therefoe, that raises the question of whether or not any true evil really does exist. And furthermore, who's to judge this 'evil' then? And in the final stage, one would ask: is there even such thing as jusgement? Well, I'll let you be the judge of that. Abner does not believe in the judgement of 'evil'. Abner believes in the gray: not in the black and white. For as a great man once said, “A house divided cannot stand.” And so how can black and white exist without destroying each other? They cannot. With no compromise, all is doomed to failure. So how does the universe exist then? I’ll tell you how: balance. Yes, a middle of the road….. ---------------------------------------- Middle of the road; not unlike Abner’s current position. Except, for him maybe, a crossroad would be more appropriate. No, scratch that. Maybe a fork in the road is more appropriate. Yes, a fork in the road..... And so he’s now at a turning point in his life, a point where the decisions that are made in the near future are crucial to his existance. These desicions beg the questions: sink or swim? Right or wrong? Win or lose? Well to Abner, these answers all clear. Swim, right, win….. Abner does not, cannot and will not fail. Abner refuses to give up on himself......or on society for that matter. He’s come too far in this unfair life of his to quit now. Too many sacrifices, too many…..too many hours of training, too many sleepless nights of studying, too many weeks of preparing for his career…..and, ugh.....too many of The End’s beers! Ugh! And Abner’s always half drunk now….. Quite interesting. So basically, there ate yoo many “too manys” to quit….. “Ah….must…..have….Bud …..” And with that said, we find Abner lying face down on the floor. His shirt is off, but thankfully, his old blue jeans are still on. And what an odd sight indeed: A completely intoxicated professional wrestler, mere hours away from his first match! What hope is there for Abner indeed! And to further sadden the situation, it's also kind one one of those 'if that can happen to my hero....' thing. You see, if a two-hundred plus pound redneck from Vermont can't handle an infinite amount of beers, what hope is there for the rest of us! The odds are just too great to fight..... Curiously enough, his feet are disturbingly uncovered as well. Yes, so it's a relief that his pants are on....but so are….sorry….'is' rather, one of his boots. The other can be found, laces chewed off, lying a couple of feet away on the floor. Abner, drifting in and out of consciousness, is what we call in Vermont “drunk as a skunk”, or “dead drunk”. Half-witted and incalculably inebriated, the PCW newcomer is lucky enough to by lying on dry, “solid” ground. Sprawled out perpendicular to his bed, his face is submerged in the thick layer of the hotel’s beautiful red carpet. Beautiful,yes, but for how long? Holiday Inn, mistakenly giving him a first-class room (due to the incident the other night), has underestimated Abner's ability to taint even the most pure of environments. Even the holy, even the heavens, would surely crumble under this man's untidiness and blatant slobbery. But luckily though, for the time being, the carpet is urine and vomit free..... “Mmm….mmm….mmmore beer…...” Quickly trying to get to his feet, the pathetic figure crashes back to the floor. Crack. Somehow, he manages, of all of the four-hundred square feet of open room, to bang his head against his thick, Oak bedpost. The nerves, huh? “Ugh..…kay…..no more beer…..” And moments later, he’s out….. And so, our journey begins..... ---------------------------------------- Ding, ding. The bell rings and they're off. The two foes circle round and round each other, each looking for an advantage. But there aren’t any. You see, they’re too experienced to let down their guard, and not experienced enough to catch the other one in the act. So round and round they go again. Round n' round n' round n' round..... This time though, Abner seizes on an opportunity. Kai's legs are spread uneevenly. And so reaching out with one leg, Abner flicks his boot out toward Kai’s leg, tripping the smaller man in the process. Ha! For now, Abner's in control. But soon, Kai's back on his feet. Abner, turning to raise his arms in delight of his early accomplishment, doesn’t even see it coming. Whack! With one swift kick, Kai ends his fun. A solid boot to the back of Abner’s head sends him reeling, but not quite rolling. Quickly regaining his stature, he’s prepared for the next blow. Come on now, monkey, c’mon.. Again, Kai goes for the same spot. But this time, Abner’s ready. Deflecting the blow with one hand, he quickly slides under his opponent, thrusts upward, and drops the thief down in a solid scoop slam. Bouncing off the rope, Abner wishes to follow it up with a leg drop. But unfortunately, due to ill-timing and even less skill, Abner misses, and unlike him, Kai connects. Sending Abner back up against the ropes, the little guy is quick to his feet, swiftly over to his opponent, and immediately, before Abner can even get off from the ropes, begins to lay in some blows. “Come on! Get up…..” The smaller and more agile man exclaims. Abner now lies crumpled on the floor. And Kai, staring intently at his prey, is allowing him back to get back to his feet, but not for very sportsmanlike reasons..... So Abner, in standing, has signed his death warrant. It's all over with. Flawlessly, Kai had it all planned out..... Kick after kick after kick of the lethal martial art blows are expected to come at any moment. Kai's quick feet are more than a match for Abner's defensive capability. And in a last resort, he falls into the fetal position and closes his eyes…..hoping, just hoping, death to come quickly..... Lord have mercy on my soul….. But the swift blows never come. Phase out….. Abner, opening his eyes, realizes that he’s no longer……there? What? What is this? What's going on? Our questions are soon answered. His vision clears enough for him to tell that he’s somewhere dark…..somewhere very unlike the well-lit ring of PCW's Fusion. What the? And then, when he’s finally well enough to see, again he closes his eyes. No! For now, Abner is in the big house…..Abner is back in good ol’ Vermont…..Abner is in a federal detention system…..Abner is in a cell…..Abner is in D-29! Ahhhhh! Could it possibly get any worse? He just escaped this Godforsaken hell whole months ago, so what's he doing here now? Well whatever it is....it can't be very good.....and it's not. And it's not. You see, it’s much worse than that…. Abner......Abner.....Abner is being layed upon by…..by…..by that crazy Mexican guy that likes to bite people’s toes! No, not that guy! The freakiest, weirdest 'son-va-bitch' Abner's ever known in his life. This guy, only a little fella, is.....well, crazy! The only man that Abner dared not mess with at D-29 was the Mexican, and yet, here he was, trapped with the guy in a cell.....great..... Ahhhhh! Ahhhhhhhhhh! Ahhhhhhhhhh! Horseshit! “Hello there…..boyfriend…….”, starts out his old friend, his voice giving off a sort of 'sexy' Latino ring. Oh no! Don't come near me.....here!? Keep away from me! Kicking the Mexican in the gut, Abner rolls out of bed and desperately tries to make an escape. But there is none. Simply nowhere to go. He reaches the iron bars of the cell door, leans out between the bars, and manages only to squeeze in an inch or two. Being a two-hundred plus pounder, he knows that the odds are against him, but anything, anything to get him out of here now. He's even willing to go back to Kai for this! “I’m here, baby!”, retorts Abner's foe. A cold hand rubs up against Abner’s tush. Oh my sweet Jesus!!!!! “Ahhhhh!” Twirling around, he finds himself face to face with the muscular, horny Mexican. And though Abner is quite a bit taller, and maybe even outweighs the guy by sixty pounds, he’s no match for the lust of a Spaniard. Everyone knows that. “Well hello there…..” The Mexican raps his arms around the solid frame of the panicky redneck. Squeezing and wheezing, he attempts to lower his position on Abner's squirming body. Butthere’s no use trying to fight it, for it’s a well known fact: not even another man can resist the arms of a sexy Mexican……not even a man….. So curling up into a fetal position, again, Abner awaits his fate….. Phase out….. “Move aside!” Hhhhh….huh? “I said, move aside!” Oh know, here we go again! Horesshit! Horseshit! Horseshit! This time, Abner's in the worst imaginable place possible..... A hand comes out of nowhere and gently brushes him aside. Abner flattens himself up against the wall as the infamous leader of Third Party makes his way toward the alter. Yes, this is it, bacl at ol' Third Party heafquarters, Abner has fallen in the shadow of his arch-nemesis: Corn. “Corn!” Abner’s surprised that the word is even still somewhere in the back of his memory. He barely manages to let it slip through his tongue, such a vile sound as it makes. After all this time…. “Well, what are you waiting for? Hand me the candle…..” Impatiently, the leader is waiting. And as always back in the old days, he's bossing Abner around. Taking advantage of Abner's lack of intelligence, and his own manipulative mind, he ruled with an iron fist. Abner would obey him. Or would he? Abner, shaking it off, obediently complies. Stepping up next to Corn, he hands his master the candle. Now it’s impossible to tell who or what's behind that black hood, but Abner knows.....and it scares him. And there’s now others around him too. Five, six, seven of them…..all gathered in a circle. Each one is wearing the same, mysterious black robe, and each is holding their own candle. The candles, all except for Corn’s that is, are small, white and are engraved with the symbol of a half moon. Corn's, on the other hand, is twice as long, black and appears to be several times as thick as the others. Why am I here? What’s going on? “Come now!”, snaps the impatient leader. “We can’t wait around all night!” Abner steps forward, and is about to give his relic up, but then thinks better of it. As he sees his boss turn toward the alter, he takes advantage of the situation. Taking a step back, he drops the candle on the floor, and then looks up toward his master. In a quick one-eighty, Abner finds himself glaring into Corn’s scorching hot eyes. Taking a defiant step forward, Abner prepares himself for the inevitable assault. “So you have chosen…..so you have chosen”, growls Abner’s biggest enemy. No…..no……no…..not again….. And Corn steps forward. Raising the black candle above his head, he prepares to strike down upon his comrade, diciple, and supposed 'friend'. And so Abner, predictable as ever, bows his head, awaiting to be dealt a surely fatal blow…..he has no other choice. Fight, and he will die, do not fight, he'll still die, so why waste all the energy? But wait….. No, no, no…..not this time, pal! He looks back up into the fiery eyes of his opponent.For once, he will not sit their quietly waiting to be dealt a blow that's surely fateful…..no, he fights. Holding his ground, he prepares to himself to be struck. Well I already know what’s gonna happen….right before he gets me, I’ll leave, and reappear in some other goddamn place! Hippie horseshit! If I go this time, I go with a piece of his ass too…..anyway, one thing's for sure....no more runnning......those days, those days are over! Getting to his feet, he charges Corn, and meets the blow head on. And luckily for the hick, instead of getting Abner, the candle is driven back into Corn's own, ugly face. Corn lets out a shriek of terror as he catches fire…..first his hood, then his head, and then, and then all of him…..and poof, one moment he’s there, and the next he’s gone…. What the!?!? But there is no explanation. Phase out….. “Come here my darling!” Forcefully trying to mount his fellow inmate, the Mexican thrusts himself up against Abner’s body. Huh? Wha...wh....what? I....uh.... Quickly, the hick snaps out of it. Gaining his senses back, he knows what he's got to do, and sets to the task at hand. “Fuck off!” With one steady shove, the Mexican falls back a foot or two, and then Abner, raising his fist in the air, before the man can come back, strikes him……and strikes him hard. The Mexican falls back another foot…..another, and then another, until finally, finally, he disappears into the back of the cell wall….. '......Uh.....hey, I did it!' Abner lets out a muffled growl. 'Well, I guess you ain't so tough after all.....Mexican hippie!' And then adds, 'Cornhole that!' And now, the final stage? Phase out….. And so there he was, tail between his legs, Kai’s foot about to sweep off his poor redneck head. But could he really? No! 'Heeeellll no!' Unfortunately for our redbeck this time, he was false. In one steady motion, Kai brings both legs around landing two solid kicks. WHACK! WHACK! These blows land squarely on the back of Abner’s head, sending him flailing into the cold, hard mat. And looking up at the lights, he wonders 'why'd I fail this one?' I don’t get it? But he didn't, no, not by a long-shot. Covering his opponent…..Kai is ready for victory….. Abner’s shoulders are down.And so the count begins..... Must.....live.....must.....not....die.....nnn.....need beer..... ONE….. TWO….. THR…..NO!!!!! And at the last possible second, Abner kicks out! And with every last ounce of energy in his beaten body, Abner reaches down deep, pulls all the reserves he can grab hold of, and manages to peel his bloody carcass from off from the mat. Scrambling to his feet, Abner gets up just in time to meet another one of Kai's blows..... And deflects it. You're mine, monkity-monkey-monk! Phase out….. Back to life, back to reality. And it's over. The hung-over hick is awake. Ugh…….. Slowly sitting up, he tries again to pull himself up from off from the floor….and again, to no avail. But this time, in falling, Abner manages to brace himself…..temporarily that is…. He now knows what to do now..... Swim. Right. Win….. And so Abner knew that he couldn’t give up now, he just couldn’t. Come Friday Fusion, he was gonna give it his all. And good enough or not, even if he’s still not in the same technical “league” as Kai, he’s still, still going to win that damn match! The determination is simply boiling in his blood. And with every drop of sweat, every once of blood, and every tender strain of muscle in his body……Abner would fight.....fight his hardest, and as Abner knew, he would come out on top..... Uh-oh….. But not right now. Losing his balance, Abner falls back to the floor, bumps his head against the side of his bed......again(now just what are the odds?), and this time, openly welcomes the pain…..and the sacred silence that follows it…... ---------------------------------------- So yeah, in the final analysis, maybe Abner needs to cut back on the Buds (hey, you can blame The End for that),and yeah, maybe he has an overactive imagination, and yeah, maybe he didn't really even mind the Mexican so.....oh, I didn't just say that....uh.....moving on.....anyway, all in all, his head's certainly in the right place. And trust me, his liver will die far, far before his heart gives out….. :) And if you don’t believe that, than that’s “horseshit!”. So now, the only question is, 'can he make it to the ring tonight without being intoxicated?' Well, for his sake, hopefully, but as far as ratings go…..eh…..hopefully not. The odds though: yeah, I’d say he'll probably be drinkin'….. But hey, it's all in day's work for PCW's favorite ex-con/redneck/anarchist. Or something like that..... ---------------------------------------- Phase out…..
