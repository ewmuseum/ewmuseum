Transition: History (PCW, Part 2 -- Finale)

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: not entered *Five weeks ago, while Skylar was in FBI detainment.* After that last untelevised PURE show, I just went back to wrestling in PCW. I was ranked in the Cruiserweight division, had been wrestling there since the issues with El Diablo. In fact, I was the #1 contender to the Cruiserweight Title for most of the time he was bothering me. A combination of his pestering me, and Charlie Loc, Juan Borro, and the Great White Overhype that was Heaven Rayne (think that was her name... she wasn't around long enough to remember it) holdng the title to themselves, I couldn't contest it right away. I had the PURE Junior Heavyweight Championship, but while PURE was actively producing shows, I didn't feel a need to do anything with it in the states, though I had the option. But this time, there was a little issue of Charlie Loc and Miyagawa. They were having problems because Loc was a dick and Miya was coming back to the cruiserweight division. Loc thought Miya didn't deserve to be there, and Miya thought Loc should shut up. Actually, everyone thought that Loc should shut up. I can't remember any time when Loc was someone people wanted to listen to. So there was Loc, Miya, and at some point I came into it after El Diablo moved away from the division. Charlie Loc was the Cruiserweight Champion after winning a ladder match against Sunshine and Rayne (since Gold and Glory was the last we'll ever see of those two, I'll refer to them jointly as The Weather). Loc killed Sunshine afterward. Rayne just faded away, though she had been doing that for a while prior. Loc was the champion, effectively with no division since aside from The Weather, people hadn't wanted to be classified as Cruiserweights, and thus there was only myself there. They got Miyagawa to agree to go down to the division (this is when he weighed a little under 230 and thus could compete there) to add to the mix. I was a Junior Heavyweight Champion of PURE, so I was held away from Loc right away, Miyagawa instead being put into position to challenge Loc. So they fought for about a week. The week after I won the PURE Junior Heavyweight Title, I was booked in a match against Miyagawa. It was scheduled early in the card, as a simple exhibition match. But by that point, we had some history. I defeated Miyagawa in Round 2 of the first Global Title Tournament (the one that Vampir Nosferatu won). For us, this would be a rematch of that bout. I, however, wanted to make the match even more special, and to begin defending my PURE title because the untelevised show was the beginning of a two-months long hiatus for the company for restructuring. I put my PURE Junior Heavyweight Title on the line in my match with Miyagawa. The premise was simple, we were two honorable competitors. We both wrestled because it was our passion. Why not put the representation of that desire up for grabs? The Junior Heavyweight Title was the first and only PURE singles title to be awarded before the hiatus. Miyagawa never tried to win it; he did team with Ebola III for the PURE Tag Team Titles, though. Didn't win them, Inphino Blitz and Mang-A-Hero did. So, I also offered Miya the chance to win a puroresu title, one that he created. We didn't get too far into the match. In fact, we didn't even have the match. Charlie Loc came out, ran his mouth about how the PURE Junior Heavyweight Title belonged to him (even though he failed on two occasions to win it). There was a little brawl, match never happened. So, there it was. A prick of a PCW Cruiserweight Champion, the fan-favorite Miyagawa who was going for a fourth reign, and myself as a champion from a different federation who was also looking for success in PCW. Much like Chris Chase between Miyagawa and Boda, I was something of an X-factor. I was neither a favorite to win, nor one everyone wanted to lose. And we all know that in that situation, Chris Chase won the PCW World Title because Miya and Boda were so busy beating the crap out of each other that Chase just kinda walked into the World Title. And then he swapped it around with the New Breed. Ugh... Let us never speak of that again. Okay, so I was the guy in the middle. Miya and Loc wanted to get their hands on each other more than on me, but I was of some signicifance to either man, and it pretty much had to do with the PUR Junior Heavyweight Title that I had around my waist. Loc wanted to win it to prove that he would've been the best in PURE, even though he never won a match in the fed. Miya wanted to wrestle me because now I know after the fact that he saw something in me, an untapped potential, and wanted to bring it out. We went through the motions of a few, bothering each other on the air, doing matches against one another. Heh. I remember the Friday before the scheduled Pay-Per-View match that we had on the WrestleVessel in Kingston, Jamaica. Charlie Loc and I wrestled in a match for his Cruiserweight Title. He lost, couldn't kick out of a pin merely by being pushed off the top rope. I mean, he was small, so it was a long way down... but still, I didn't need a finishing move or anything. So, WrestleVessel comes, a Panes [of glass], Ladders, and Chairs match. I walked in the double-champion, putting both titles on the line. The PCW Cruiserweight Title would be accessible by ladder, but only after the PURE Junior Heavyweight Title was decided by pinfall or submission. It was a brutal match, the standard impact fare in the ladder and chairs being used. But the glass presented an extra element of danger, we could be cut in a major artery. Most people go for the head to cause blood loss. It does produce blood, but there's no MAJOR arteries in the head. I could even tell you where they all are. There's the Carotid artery in the neck. Brachial artery, that's right in the pit of the elbow. The radial artery that is most commonly used for taking the pulse is in the wrist; it's also the one the people slit open when trying to kill themselves. The femoral artery is at the inside of the groin. The popliteal artery is in the pit of the knee. Anterior tibial, the artery on the front of the ankle. Every single one of those points has so much blood rushing past it at such a high force that any of those points could be used for accurately gauging the pulse. And it any one of those arteries were to be ruptured, the blood loss could kill a person if not manually sewn up right away. With so many dangerous areas on the body that could be cut open in the PLC match, I knew that this wouldn't be a match that any of us would just get up and casually walk away from. It was a good match, the sleeper of the night everyone said. Loc pinned Miya to win the PURE Junior Heavyweight Title after I was momentarily taken out of the match because a big piece of glass was lodged in my throat (luckily, between it missing the artery, and my naturally stubborn tendency to heal quickly, I made a return in the match). Miyagawa managed to grab the PCW Cruiserweight Title and won it for a fourth time. I walked in with two, and by match's end, I had neither. Oh, but afterward, Miyagawa said his goodbyes. He wasn't retiring, but he was taking time off to deal with his then-wife Natalia, who was having a kid soon. He wanted to attend to the family, and being Cruiserweight Champion wouldn't do that for him. So he gave it to me. He said he saw something in me, and gave me the ball and the chance to run with it. I no longer held the PURE Junior Heavyweight Title (Loc never went on to defend it, and his version of the title was declared null and void as the new ownership of PURE came into control of the federation). I was the PCW Cruiserweight Champion. My reign was uneventful. Well... I did get to know Sandy, and here we are. But in PCW, my Cruiserweight Title reign was boring. No one wanted to be a cruiserweight. I had to get my own opponents together. After a month of just holding it with no challengers, I let Brandi beat Mark Makonie for the title, handed it over, and bulked up. Well, in a way. I can pretty much be whatever weight I want to be, just by seriously thinking it. So I was looking to rise in the rankings from the Cruiserweight Title. At the same time, there was a certain Black Attack member looking to get past the Extreme division. He enjoys moonlight strolls on the beach and the soap opera 'All My Children'... Rush Mason! He'd revived Black Attack, or rather failed in his attempt to do so because Jordan X Shabazz left PCW and Thug roC was fired for... well, sucking. He managed to scrounge together another stable consisting of Playaz, Inc. and Duzza. Just couldn't give that up. He was wanting to get pushed because he was black and bought the book 'Voodoo for Dummies.' He started up problems with me because he had some issues about life and death and humanity and non-humanity that I violated. So he tried to feud with me. He took his time, trying to turn me into the stereotypical black male that he used to be. I knew his game, he was trying to turn me into something that he could easily deal with. Something that he knew how to defeat. Simply put, he started a fight with me, not realizing what he'd get himself into. I humored him by going along with it, but I didn't let him change me. It led to a match at Red October. Uneventful, I'll just note that I beat him with my Celestial powers that he said I didn't have. Heh. As a result of jobbing Rush Mason, he got kicked out of Black Attack and replaced by... ALI KHADAFI! Yeah, I know, they actually brought in someone worse. Oh, well... After that... well, let's just say that I was looking to rise in the rankings. I'd beaten Rush Mason in a Last Man Standing match, and demonstrated powers only Akiyama could surpass. I thought, 'Hey, this would be good for at least a Television Title shot,' right? So, the week before the third Mall Brawl, I'm put into a #1 Contender's match for the Extreme Title. Yeah... right. Carl Cash, Scorpion, and Jon Kano... in a match with them. I don't know why, but I was there. I won, so the premature victory party was a good idea. After that, Semore Smoke. Smoke won the Extreme Title from Bernard Colt at Red October. Smoke and I had a history together. We were in some cross-country race, and I took a habit to burning his personal effects. Oh well, the Turmoil before Mall Brawl, I beat him for the Extreme Title. Beat him good. Goooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooood. Anyway, after that, there was the Mall Brawl. Didn't win, but 3rd isn't so bad. I had the Extreme Title, no need to hold two. After that, I rigged a ceiling to fall on me so I could drop the career-stagnating strap. Bob Vurdo won it, but he was a sap anyway. Alliances was coming up, and I wanted to get involved. So I got involved with the Celestials again. It was going to be a three-person team, myself, Cyclonica, and Akiyama. But PFIK suggested a fourth member: Boca Del Inferno. Needless to say, he still needs a full security detail when Akiyama is around. We had to take him, there was no one else who could fill in for him. Xavier Kannon wrote him in to where if he was injured, we'd forfeit participation. So, we took him and just kinda kept him out of the way. But then there was the No Ladders match. Myself and Boca on a team against 8 others. Meh. I did well, though Nerezza won by destroying a large scaffold while in vampire mode. Boca tried to rape the other competitors rather than win, but Kannon knew that's what he is like. After No Ladders, I had to wrestle Akiyama in a Last Man Standing match in the main event on the Fusion prior to the pay-per-view. I won, but by Darkstar's interference. Alliances... the Celestials' finest hour. Akiyama and I lasted until the #2 and #3 spots, respectively, until Kannon had RUSH come out on his behalf to help him win. I got put into the Continental Title Division anyway. It's as much as I could ask for, so I was fine. Akiyama wasn't too thrilled about that, though. He couldn't do much, though, as Cyclonica soon thereafter eliminated him with a sledgehammer to the head. The Versailles match... Rush won that. I got to the title first, but Chef gave it to Rush Mason because he needed the match to be about him and not the participants. Damn his crippled ass... Christmas was coming up, and I was in the giving mood, so I did a 'Wishmaster' thing where I'd grant Christmas wishes. Reasonable ones. This brings me to my next feud, Totally Tubular Tommy. He wanted a) for me to help him win the Continental Title and b) for me to give Sandy to Harry. I refused on both accounts, so there was the feud. I can't say much of it was memorable. A Treble Jeopardy match at Decimation for the Continental Title, the preliminary match was myself against Tommy. Through much interference on Harry's part, he managed to defeat me. Went on to win. He's been the Continental Champion since then. I said after that match that I would retire. I had a retirement party, had a promotional video... my last match was on the Adrenaline before the New Year. I beat 6-7 other men and won the #1 Contendership to the Continental Title on what was to be my last show. I'd choose someone to replace me. So I came back after the New Year, and was going to name a replacement. Rush Mason came out and said it would be him, so I had him go into two fake matches with Trigger and Azala Zameer. He won those, though by countout and DQ (and got blasted anyway). Last one was by distracting him with a Young Tiger lookalike, and pinning him. I named myself as the #1 Contender then. Well, Mason got his panties in a bunch and started bothering me, sometimes interfering in my matches. We had another match, this time a Loser Leaves PCW match, at Shanghaied 3. I won that and retired Rush Mason. Dante Jackson seems to be my new Rush Mason now, can't seem to get a win over me. And from there, the issues with Violence Jack that are very apparent. So, I've gone over my entire career. Historically, when I'm seen as a 'bad guy...' I get left alone. What is it about me that makes me seem a pushover as a nice guy? Why is it that when I decide not to be a prick, everyone thinks that they can use me for pushing whatever agenda that they have in mind? From Undertaker to Beast & Samurai to Rush Mason to Violence Jack now... I just can't get a break when I want to leave people alone and be left alone. Do I just seem more vulnerable, or is there something else? Violence Jack just says some 'masters' drive him to take me out. Why should I care? And why is this obviously insane man allowed to walk the streets? Questions we may never know the answers to. Oh well, I'll be out soon. They can't hold me too long without any evidence of wrongdoing. I haven't evaded any taxes, those receipts in the safe deposit box at Wachovia should prove that. Sandy, it'll be up to you to try to sway the courts to give the kids back. It's not my fault some wacko wanted to burn my house down. As for me... once I get out of here, I'll go and wrestle Stryker. March Madness is coming up, and I intend to do well this time. Winning would be nice, too. But Violence Jack... he's got my attention now. And that's not a good time.
