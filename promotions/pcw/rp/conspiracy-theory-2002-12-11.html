Conspiracy Theory

11 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11.12.02 I know its odd seeing me here writing stuff down, as I’d usually be in front of a camera, reciting rhymes or putting down my opponents. But last Friday night, I thought about being stepped on, almost killed in a No Ladder match for the PCW Tag Team titles with Quo. I thought about it once, twice, and maybe even three times before I came to a conclusion. We will always be second to none. Yet, we weren’t victorious, we just proved ourselves to be just what we’ve always told you – the new kings of the sport. Its not like I’m trying to prove it to you that either we’re better than you, it’s known fact! Oh sure, I know you’re going to tell me about the “losing to P.W.A” and “not winning the No Ladder match” but rest assure, we still are in the title hunt. Playaz Inc. isn’t going away and the end of the month calendar proves that fact. 2 title shots, one soda endorsement contract, and possibly the biggest match of the night containing all the elements of victory. Sweet isn’t it? We aren’t Nelly wannabes, like Kannon might tell you, no we are REAL HIP HOP, but as far as Turmoil goes, they’re giving us an easy lineup going into this Pay Per View called Alliances. Give us two rookies, and we’ll abolish them quicker than slavery. Give me a former NTICW champion, and I’ll prove why he went there, and I went here. Its self explanatory, because you know and I know that Jamie Celt has been given what, six title shots in the last two weeks and floundered on every opportunity? Its not trash talk I’m speaking the truth. Was I just going to throw up this morning as I saw the footage from Fusion with Vampire Nerezza coming out and taking away the title that I HAD IN MY HAND FIRST! Oh yeah, nobody saw that. Everybody was focused on the fans odd hush of silence, hoping by some slim chance of power; Nerezza would come out with a title in his hand. And nobody pays the black man attention when a sucker punch hits him and he coughs the belt up. Nobody sees that, its all a conspiracy. It goes all the way up, by Williams, by Kannon, and for God knows by Pranks With Attitude that they don’t want the black man in power. It happened once, and now they’re regretting that they pissed the black man of this time, because he is berserk, and willing to go WHUP SOME - wait, I’m the brains, not the stupidity. Hey, every black man has the idea that he should whoop a white person for doing some dumb shit (check Jackass for that one), but we get to do it on National Television, in front of the world. Many blacks would be in my position now, if they heard of beating white people, but I guess not. They don’t care about it anymore. Good, I was beginning to hate that stereotype anyway. But its still a conspiracy against us, no matter how you look at it.On one side, you have Pranks With Attitude who used a manager to beat us, for the uno, dos, tres, and then, you get the gal of Xavier Kannon mad at us for him signing us to a match at Alliances for the tag team titles. Now I kid you not, Kannon would leap in a heartbeat just to get that chance that WE beat him to. Hell, he’d get Williams and Walden and tax every single LETTER of this reading. Yes, Xavier Kannon is a big meanie, and yes Xavier Kannon’s a smart meanie. But never is he a kind meanie! So what do they all have in common? Greed, envy, lust? Or do they just hate Quo’s alteration of the English language? It might be point two, because in other to be revolutionary with the art of Ebonics, somebody has to be the test pilot. Enter Statuz Quo – preferably the #1 spokesperson for Ebonics in the world today. When you first heard him use the term, “cracka” he learned that from the Nabisco commercial. When he started saying, “bitch ass” he got it from a woman who thought she was good enough for JET Beauty of the Week. Well, she was wrong and Quo happened to be right. In the time honored words of Stuart Scott, BOOYAH! Yeah, that’s right BOOYAH! It’s a shame that my talents for this upcoming Cruiserweight tournament, pitting me against some of the best PCW has to offer, but I really doubt somebody other than Gaz Green, Xavier Kannon or another smurf will even land a punch on me. I’m too damn good, thought ya’ll knew that already. So on Turmoil they has us, the former PCW Tag Team champions of the entire world going up against people who aren’t even on our level. It shames me really, that Kannon decides to book us – the greatest TEAM on the planet against two scrubs from P.W.A! Not Pranks With Attitude, but those hoes back down where I used to work at a few months ago. But it’s all good though, just as long as this house thing goes all for the better. I mean, everybody’s coming from all over the world and they want to spend a night at the House of Crunk. Fine by me. Just as long as those punks pay for the food.
