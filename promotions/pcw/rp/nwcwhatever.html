Date: In the now (And it’s on the road for the world famous [provided your world is about three feet in diameter] wrestler, entertainer and general ne’er do well, THE Multiple Man. [For future reference, the ‘THE’ is obligatory.] His personal ride, a little diesel guzzlin’, primer gray Volkswagen Jackrabbit chugs quietly down the road… quietly when compared to a dryer full of rocks that is.) Tmm: “Mmm…I think we’re lost.” (Riding shotgun, Tmm’s action stunt double Raul surreptitiously rotates the map ninety degrees before looking at it again.) Raul: ”[Something in Spanish.].” Tmm: “Are you sure? Should there be Sargasso cacti about in New Westchester?” (Raul nods emphatically.) Tmm: “Well, all right… you’re the navigator.” Raul: ”[Something in Spanish.].” Tmm: “Okey dokey.” (Raul rotates the map once more before peering at it like it held the secrets of the universe therein. The miles roll on…) Tmm: “Just admit it, we’re lost.” Raul: (stubbornly) ”[Something in Spanish.]!” Tmm: “I know it, YOU know it, if she was awake, Keira would know it, and even THEY know it!” (Tmm jerks his thumb overhead. Through the sunroof, a number of vultures, confident of an easy meal circle the car lazily on desert thermals.) Raul: (more stubbornly) ”[Something in Spanish.]!” (Just then the car hits a rut in the road, jolting everyone inside. In the back seat, the previously mentioned out cold Keira pops upright like she was mounted on springs.) Keira: (Only half there despite physical movement.) “Wazza? There yet?” Tmm: (annoyed) “No, ‘cause Raul got us lost again.” Raul: ”[Something in Spanish.]!” Tmm: “You wish! I saw you turn the map!” Raul: (indignant) ”[Something in Spanish.]!” Keira: (groggily) “Oh good, there it is.” (This brings the two men up short. Sure enough, in the distance a distinct sign bearing the letters ‘NWCW’ is approaching.) Tmm: “Awful lot of cars there.” Raul: ”[Something in Spanish.].” Tmm: “Well, yeah… but where’s the arena then?” Keira: “Parking space! Parking Space!” (The little Jackrabbit car weaves a deft trail through the mass of mountainous SUV’s with the ease that conjures up images of cutlery and churned dairy products.) Tmm: (reading sign) “New Westchester Car World?” Keira: “The address is right, the letters are right, and they paid in advance. So what if it’s a car dealership? Makes more sense than most promotionals, I’m thinking.” (She kicks the tires of a towering SUV.) Keira: “Now help me with the sound equipment.” (Two hours later, at the same dealership. A lightly built elderly woman wanders the canyons of cars, looking for a salesman.) Woman: “Hello? Is anybody there? Hello?” (A shadow falls across her frail body as the sun is blotted out. Tmm holds the pose for just a moment [for the cameras, natch] before leaping and plowing her into the unyielding concrete!) Tmm: (getting up) “Huh. Can’t for the life of me imagine how this helps sales any, but who am I to argue with a paycheck?” (He feels a vice-like grip on his ankle.) Woman: “Want a piece of me, eh, whippersnapper? If you can’t respect your elders then by god you’ll respect your betters!” (She yanks him down hard and begins pummeling him mercilessly as the camera fades out.)
