Bygone Inscription Chronicles Part 1: Perish in Serenity

13 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7.13.02 7.13.022:34 pm.Oxford, Mississippi-- I must have fell asleep. I woke up in the same spot on the bank, trying to shield the light of the sun with my hands. As I stare up from my resting spot, a huge glare coming up from the sun. The sun was bright...vibrant enough to light up the whole area around. Luckily, that of a large Oak tree above me shaded most of the glare, and kept the heat to a low volume. Slowly I stepped up from where I was lying, and wipe off the excess material from the ground. Leaning against a tree, I look at the black journal that lay below my feet. “Ok, Kris. Get over it and open up the motherfucking book. Are you a pussy?” I may have been taking this too far on myself. Kris Burden was not afraid of anything. Kris Burden had nothing to fear. Everything that I had feared was taken away in an instant. The fear of losing the one I love. The fear of waking up without a family. All of those fears had happened...but are over. Sitting back down I grabbed the book and opened it to the bloodstained page at the beginning. I begin to read to myself, finally building up the courage to do so.-- 1.12.94 I am a problem slayer. I can take anything that comes my way. At 17 years of age, my life has been taken away from me. Ahh, who gives a shit. Supposedly, I’m crazy. I needed counseling, or so the doctors said, therefore sending me to a counselor. Heh....... “Kris, you do know that you have a problem, right?” My counselor, Mr. Smith, told me while I sat in a chair staring at him. His words still ring through my head like the fucking school bell. Damn, I wanted to kill him right there. Who the fuck was he to tell me that I had a problem? There is nothing wrong with me. Does he want me to spell it? “Who the fuck do you think you are, Mr. Smith? You think you can just tell me that I have a problem when..” “There’s no need to become angry, Mr. Burden. I’m here to help you,” He interrupted. Yeah, one and all are here to help me out. That’s what everybody’s goal is in this life, isn’t it, Mr. Smith? Yeah...that’s what I thought. Why should I listen to a man that has a perfect life, and comes from an unbroken home...with a beautiful wife and kids? You go home every night, thinking that you’re just as perfect as any. And, you are. You’re better than I am, and you know that. That’s why you think you can help me. In your eyes, I’m hurting with this pain of having a problem...Maybe I should be counseling you. “It’s just time to admit that you have a problem. That’s the first step and then we move up the ladder,” he told me with an assured look in his eyes. “Move up the ladder...that’s the way that you explain it?” “That’s the best way that I can explain it, yes,” he seemed to know what he was talking about, but I didn’t understand. Ok...let me get this straight. I have a problem, because I lost my father...my mother...my foster parents.... and my fiance. Maybe he’s right, but he has no right to tell me that to my face. This is what happened to me and it’s hard for me to admit it. I blew up. “Mr. Harvard graduate...have you ever lost anybody that you loved? The one that you‘ve wanted to spend the rest of your life with?” I asked, running my fingers through my hair, getting to my point...slowly ‘climbing the ladder.’ “Well...I can’t say that...” “Are your parents still alive?” I was set out to make this man see what my life was all about. I wasn’t finished. “Oh, that’s right. You have the perfect life. Although, you go to work...stay there late every other night. Your wife wonders where you’ve been all night and you tell her that you were stuck in traffic, when all reality you were fucking your secretary in the elevator. Oh, nobody will find out. Four days later, you find out that your secretary is pregnant and your whole life is fucked now fucked up...there’s nothing you can do about it. Every night in bed, you think about how you’re going to tell your wife what has happened and your secretary demands that you and her must get married, now. Nobody is perfect, Mr. Smith, and maybe you‘re the one who has a problem. You’re the head honcho here on Wall Street. You’re the top psychologist that helps everybody with his or her problems. You help your wife cry every night by never paying attention to her...never seeing your kids...adultery. And, she knows all of this, but yet you think she doesn‘t. She’s just scared of leaving her high school sweetheart, because you were to spend your whole lives together. She loves you more than you’ll ever know. But, the other day she found out she had cancer. She hasn’t told you this, afraid of what you may do. She hasn’t told you this yet, still pondering on if she loves you anymore. She tries to hide it...but she just wants to go out of her existence with you in her life. You made a promise that night at the prom to be together forever...and her forever is almost over. This makes it a clean shot for the pregnancy to go through with no trouble...but, don’t forget that your other lover is married. There’s only one way to get rid of that man, and that’s to cancel him out. You’ve thought about ways for days and days... Your goal isn’t out to help me, Mr. Smith. Your goal is to get this fucking session over as quickly as you can, so you can clock out and become wealthy over nothing. I have nothing against that...because in your eyes, you are perfect and everything will go right. Oh, but there’s more. Your 14-year-old daughter is not a virgin anymore...yeah, you remember the one you trusted? Looks like daddy’s little girl doesn’t tell daddy everything anymore. All hooked up on coke and fucking everything that she sees. All of this has been happening right under your nose, and you aren’t aiding. The best way for me to climb that ladder is without any help, Mr. Smith. It’s time for you to go fuck your secretary again...while I go home and sob over what has happened to my life. Just remember that one day...all will come crashing down on you, too. You’ve really helped me today, Mr. Smith. Thank you for your time.” He looked at me wide-eyed, jaw dropped...knowing that every single word I said was true. I walked out without looking back, hearing the papers drop to the ground. I’ll never go back and have counseling. I don’t need counseling from somebody that creates problems. Psychologists may help some, but they pull the trigger for others. Have fun with your implausible life...one in which you think seems to be perfect. I’ve opened your eyes...and yet it’s still perfect compared to mine. Try living a life with misery--hopefully you will get what you deserve. Just remember that one day...all will come crashing down on you, too. Go home and perish in Serenity. Goodbye, Mr. Smith.”
