Three Wishes

5 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5/5/03 Sunday morning always seem to be the most boring time of the week, especially when you have nothing to do but sit around and observe a mother that could easily be given the superlative most dramatic along with a child four years of age whom was only half awake sitting down on the couch watching a cartoon that as usual portraying one of those surreal situations that you could only wish for in your imagination. “You have three wishes that I can grant you, only rule is you cannot wish for more wishes.” The floating blue creature named genie from the Disney classic Aladdin said to the poor street boy who the movie was named after. Looking over at Evan I could not help but to notice a huge smile cross his face. Just the look on his face told me that he was contemplating what he would do if he had those three wishes. Knowing the child for the four months or so that I had guardianship of him for I could already predict what those wishes would be. They would be the same wishes that any child that had been through the shit he had been through would wish for. Every time I saw him my heart melted and I wished I could be like the magic genie to him and be the one to bring his mother back to life and his father out of jail. It would be the least I could do for the child I considered to be my own. As the movie continued on I took a seat next to Evan and pretend that I was watching along with him and getting into the movie that I had seen nearly a hundred times over and over again. It used to be the video that Christa and Alex would ask me to play time and time again every time the sun was hidden behind the clouds and drops of moisture fell from the sky. Evan looked over at me and his smile grew slightly larger since he enjoyed my company greatly, but it wasn’t long before his attention became one again fixated on the television. “Hmmmm I wonder what I would do if I had three wishes.” I pondered to myself as I curled up on the couch allowing my head to rest on the arm of the couch and my feet to extend to the end of cushion I was sitting on, so that they would not push Evan out of his spot. As the thought of having three wishes dwelled inside my mind it brought back images of the past, every one of them being a moment I wish I could have changed. The vision of the kids leaving out the door with Allison and Ein, the scene of pulling up to the window and having the vicar in Las Vegas pronounce Gaz and I husband and wife, and then the last of ever saw of Chris, lying beaten and bloodied beside the brick wall which was stained with his blood. “Damn it why do I have to have such luck.” I cursed silently to myself, thinking of how much worse things could get from here, but at least I wasn’t the one lying on the hospital bed at the conclusion of Spring Fling, but then again it didn’t matter much considering I still lost which meant that I had to continue on being known as Mrs. Windsor-Green. “That’s the first thing I would wish for if I had three wishes!” I proclaimed aloud, causing Evan’s attention to be directed towards me as I stood tall on top of the couch like a king addressing his people. “What would you wish for?” Evan questioned, confused as to what I could be so excited about and just wanting to go back to watching his video in peace. “Nevermind.” I told him, not wanting to bring up my last name considering that Evan was still clueless to the situation that had unintentionally made me a member of the Green family. “I wish I wasn’t a Windsor-Green.” I told the imaginary genie floating inside my mind wishing that he only existed for real, and that he could grant each and every one of my wishes instead of just three. “And I wish that I could have Chris and the kids back.” I continued on, as my mind continued to rewind back to the events of the pay per view and then Allison standing at the front door of my house coming to tell me that she was here for HER kids. “And last but not least, I wish that this Friday I can defeat El Diablo right there in Athens, Greece.” The last wish rolled off my tongue as I tilted the direction of my head over towards the genie to see what his response would be. “Your wish is my command!” The deep powerful voice of the genie roared in my mind as I sat back and waited for him to wave his magic and change the world just like that. POOOOOFFFF My baby blue eyes shot open and once again I was in my living room, not a thing had changed. There was no Chris, no kids, and no win against El Diablo. I sighed as I looked over at Evan who was still absorbed in his movie. “Damn it, why can’t I have my three wishes.” I sighed to myself, realizing that the possibility of my wishes ever coming true only existed in a daydream.
