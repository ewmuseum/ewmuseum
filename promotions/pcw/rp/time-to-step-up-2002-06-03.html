Time To Step Up

3 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 03/06/02 THUMP! AWWWW  JESUS!! The scream comes echoing from somewhere above as the scene opens in a long white corridor.  On either side, polished wooden cases filled with trophies and memorabilia from a long and illustrious career run along the walls.  As the camera begins to move slowly along the corridor, it pans to show the glittering title belts and trophies that are solidly crammed into the display cases. WHACK! Another sound comes echoing from above as the cameras zoom in on one of the hundreds of photographs that fill the cabinets.  It is a black and white shot, showing a bloodied Supreme Machine lifting the CCW Heavyweight Title after defeating the mighty Samson to win the coveted title for an unprecedented fifth time.  His eyes seem to burn out from the photo as he stands triumphantly in the ring. OWWWWW.......... FUCK! As the camera continues to make it's way slowly down the corridor, it turns to show an open door set into the white wall at the far end.  The camera suddenly speeds up as it heads towards the doorway. STOP!  ENOUGH! Come on, what am I paying you for? The trophy cabinets fly by as the camera speeds along the corridor and through the doorway, showing a spiral staircase of polished steel leading up to another level.  The camera zooms up the staircase at breakneck speed and comes to a sudden stop as it reaches the top. AAARRRGGGHHH!!! Get up damn it! We now find ourselves in yet another white corridor, identical to the previous one but for the lack of trophy cabinets.  The walls here are covered in ancient looking wall hangings showing various scenes from antiquity.  The camera pauses for a moment as it pans around the corridor, taking in the exquisite hangings, before once again speeding up and racing along the corridor. BANG! The sounds are closer now.  The camera comes to a halt at another doorway. BANG! The door slowly opens and the camera goes through into a massive room that has huge windows on one wall that show the familiar skyline of New York City.  As the camera slowly pans around the room, we see that it is a gymnasium of sorts, equipped with a wide range of weights and other fitness equipment. THUMP! The camera continues to pan around the gymnasium, eventually coming to rest on a full sized wrestling ring that dominates one half of the massive space.  Inside the blue covered ring, two men stand staring at each other as they appear to be catching their breath.  The shorter of the two is dressed in a pair of grey gym shorts and a sweat covered blue t-shirt.  He has bruises coming up across his face and a small amount of blood is starting to trickle from somewhere under his short blond hair.  The other man is dressed in plain black wrestling tights with tiger print boots and towers over his much shorter opponent.  You had enough? The shorter man slowly nods his head as he stands panting, hands on knees, in the ring.  The bigger man nods silently as he runs a massive hand through his man of long black hair.  Outside the ring, another young man sits on a polished wooden bench, nervously watching the blond man slowly leaving the ring.  The black haired giant turns to him and motions for him to get into the ring. You're up. The Supreme Machine watches silently as the nervous young man stands and slowly walks into the ring.  He turns to address his previous sparring partner and points towards a locker on the other side of the room. If you go into that locker over there, you'll find something to stop the bleeding. The blond man nods as he holds his bleeding head and walks slowly over to the locker.  The Supreme Machine turns back to face his new sparring partner.  He smiles menacingly at the nervous young man. You ready son? The young man nods slowly as he adjusts his elbow pads. I'm ready The Supreme Machine smiles back at him. Good.  Then let's go. Before the young man can answer, The Supreme Machine rushes at him and sends him hurtling across the ring and into the ropes.  As he comes rebounding back, he is sent crashing to the canvass by a devastating clothesline.  He hits the mat hard but instantly gets back up to his feet. Very impressive.  Now, let's get serious. The two men lock up but The Supreme Machine's superior size and strength soon overpower the young wrestler.  He drives a knee hard into the young man's stomach, doubling him over in pain, and then brings his right elbow down hard across the back of his neck.  He keeps a hold of the young man to stop him falling to the mat. Come on son, keep up The Supreme Machine pushes him effortlessly into a corner of the ring.  Before the younger man can compose himself, The Supreme Machine charges at him and squashes him hard into the turnbuckle, moving the ring.  As the young man staggers forward and falls to the mat, The Supreme Machine leaps on to the top rope and launches himself high into the air.  The young man groans loudly as the CCW Hall of Famer hits a perfect Shooting Star Press.  He gets quickly back to his feet and stands over the young man. What's wrong?  I was told that you two were tough enough for this.  It's only sparring for God's sake! The young man isn't capable of answering.  He lays on his back, clutching at his ribs as The Supreme Machine leaps effortlessly over the top rope and lands lightly on the polished wooden floor.  He looks back at the youngster in the ring and shakes his head in disgust. That's the problem with wrestling these days.  All these pretty boys think that just because they can do a few high flying moves or something they can beat the world! He walks over to the wooden bench and picks up a black towel that he uses to wipe the small amount of sweat from his brow. The sport is becoming riddled with these low talent punks.  They just don't realize that it takes more than a few death defying moves to get to the top.  It takes dedication.  It takes toughness.  It takes balls. He throws the towel back down onto the bench and takes one last look at the young man still writhing in pain in the ring before turning and walking slowly over to the nearby gym equipment. I should know.   I've been to the top of this sport and I aim on getting back there.  The Supreme Machine stops and turns directly into the camera. I haven't come to PCW just to make up the numbers, I think I should make that clear from the start.  I've heard all the backstage gossip.  I know what everyone is thinking.  It's just up to me now to prove them all wrong.  And I will.  Trust me.  I'll prove them dead wrong. Now Ultimate Authority seem to be quite pleased with themselves after Friday Fury.  Sure, they managed to throw us out of the ring, but so what?  Have they chased me out of PCW?  No.   Are they going to chase me out of PCW?  No!  You see, since Ricky Thunder and I arrived in PCW a few weeks ago, we've just been cruising along, taking time to settle in to our new surroundings, so to speak.  We've just been looking around and taking everything in.  Well, unfortunately for PCW and it's pack of wannabe wrestlers, we've settled. He wipes his black hair out of his face before continuing. Up until now, we've just been towing the line, doing what The Phantom Booker asked of us.  Well no more.  After Friday Fury, it's become apparent that this federation is just like all the rest.  Jealous little fools with narrow little minds.... but when you're as great as myself, you get used to that.  Well, no more towing the line.  From here on in, Ricky and I are going to look out for no-one but ourselves.  Now, I'm not saying that we're leaving The Phantom Phive.  I made a promise to The Phantom Booker that I intend to keep.  What I am saying is that from Tuesday Turmoil onwards, it's no more doing what other people want.  Starting with that freak, The Multiple Man, I'm going to cut a path of destruction through PCW like no-one before me.  I'm going to show you all just why I am a living legend. I don't care who they put up against me.  Xavier Kannon, Shabazz, Duke Williams.  It doesn't matter.  All of them are going to fall before me just like everyone else has.  You see, I'm going to have that PCW World Title and I don't care what I have to do to get it.  There isn't a wrestler here that is good enough to tie my damned boot laces, let alone stop me from getting what I want. The Supreme Machine lifts up a small dumbbell and begins to do some arm curls as he continues. Sure, some of the wrestlers in PCW come with good reputations but that doesn't mean shit, none of them have ever had to get into a wrestling ring with me before.  One by one, I'm going to dismantle every wrestler in PCW.  One by one I'm going to destroy whatever reputations they've managed to eek out for themselves over the years and one by one I'm going to make them rue the day that The Supreme Machine signed that PCW contract. I am the greatest and I will destroy each and every last one of you......... BECAUSE I CAN! The scene fades to black as The Supreme Machine continues his workout and the two young sparring partners limp painfully out of the gymnasium.
