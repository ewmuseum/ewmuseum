Carnies

19 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 19th March 2003 A serene hum came from behind the large white sealed bath room door. Running water lashed against the tiled walls in the vast steamy room. The humming which emanated from the shower increases in volume into a loud song. The song gets louder but then gets interrupted by a sputtering of water, like a quick release of water suddenly choked up. Then there is a bang as someone hits the floor with incredible force. Muttering and cussing fills the room alongside the gentle flow of steam. The door to the en suite shower flings open as the mist hidden figure crawls out onto the floor holding their hand to their head they sit up onto their hind legs. “Son of a bitch!” The female voice cussed as she pulled a crème towel around her barely visible caramel frame. As the lady pulled herself up on the porcelain sink she grunt. Still dizzy from the fall she had inside the shower she wipes the base of her fist wipes across the window, a trail of clarity following it. Her eyes shine as small tears form at the side of her white and brown eyes. The rest of the fog is cleared as the extractor fan begins to whir above her. Jade wipes her eyes and plucks a toothbrush from a jar inside of the cabinet. Her black/white mixture of hair clung to her face prior to being blown over her head. Jade begins brushing the ghastly tasting paste around her small oral cavity. Lacuna bounces around busily in this house and Jade is left to be the sensible one. Yeah, that’d happen is the most screwed up world. Lacuna was probably playing with Steve or busily kicking the crap out of her worker’s shins. If this arrangement was going to last any longer then Jade was going to have to do her damndest to get Lacuna to loosen up when Jade was around. She has so much money yet what does she do with it? Nothing! She has done absolutely squat. That will change. Once Jade gets her operations off the ground she will have her own money to have fun with but until then it is time for Lacuna to start having fun with her own inheritance. She needs to stop being such a stereotypical Jew and splash out here and there. -[Fade]- “Come on Lacuna! Get your coat.” Jade ordered as she bounced about in the hall. “Oh, silly me. Brook! Get your ass in here we don’t have all day you know!” The large, balding, reasonably obese butler walks into the hall looking a little flustered. He had been working on Jade’s last order, and so his hands were covered in black leather boot polish as he carried in one of Jade’s shin high leather heeled boots. “Jeez you’re a bit off the ball today aren’t you Brooky?” Jade gave him a second to puff and pant in front of her before she delivered his next task. “Hurry along and fetch two of my coats from the room.” Brook raised his eyebrows. Jade had emptied almost all of her collection into the cloak room and it was ready for bursting packed with the entire new and not so sensible wardrobe Jade had packed in there. “There are a lot of coats in there Jade, which ones might you be referring to?” Brook questioned through gasping breaths. He had been up since seven AM running about after Jade, her incessant constantly booming as she laid about doing nothing. “Something leather, something revealing yet flowing. There are more than five I know which fit that description. I want the one with the single sleeve. Get Lacuna the one with the fur trimmings.” Jade ordered. Brook little surprised that Lacuna would actually wear something like that with her being an upper class lady. He just followed Jade’s orders; it was the easiest way to keep her quiet. “Lacuna get your well shaped hiney down these stairs now before I leave with all of your money!” there was no response. Jade had been wearing Lacuna down with excitement and things of the like for days and she just was not used to it. “I’ll take Brook and give your servants the day off!” Lacuna came thudding down the stairs. “Good girl.” Lacuna trudged down the stairs with her arms flapping wildly and loosely in front of her. Her lips pouting as she gawped in the mirror her hair was a total mess. Lacuna whined and tried to do something with her hair. “I can’t go out like this! Oh well I guess I will just have to stay in and wash my hair.” Jade shakes her head at he bottom of the stairs. Her hands on her hips, her fingers gently stroking her red and black trimmed corset, she shakes her head. “What?! I am not going out like this! There is no way.” “MRS. AMORUSO!!” Jade barks. Lacuna looks away from the mirror to see Jade wipe the tiniest bit of spit from her lower lip. It was a bad habit of hers, usually happened when she was trying to get somebody’s attention by shouting. A small Puerto Rican leady with big red hair trudges down the stairs behind Lacuna. The woman does not take a moment to push Lacuna down to her ass while she brushes the knots and assorted crap which infests the higher class ladies hair. “Lacuna this is your new hair stylist Mrs. Amoruso. Mrs. Amoruso this is the lady who will be paying your wages.” Lacuna tries to turn her head to get a look of this woman who was breaking the knots in her head. The woman holds Lacunas head straight forward. “You really do have to start spending money on the necessities dear.” Lacuna shows off her pearly whites as she looks forward. Apparently she was in agreement with Jade’s comment; the one about her spending her money a little more easily. The woman pulls out a pair of scissors and Lacuna requests; “Please, no cutting of the hair today. I don’t have time.” She still felt a little uneasy having this new woman hired by Jade of all people cutting her long raven locks. The woman finally finished with her brushing Lacuna’s hair and Lacuna stood up looking into the mirror. Lacuna grinned again and began walking down the stairs. Jade was disrupted in her smiling and nodding by a crash coming from the cloak room situated further down the hall. Jade rolls her eyes and begins a quicker pace into the cloak room. Jade slams the handle down and opens the door. A disarray of coats and such lie all over Brook, he manages to get his head up through all of the mess. “Jesus are you bunch of worker’s freakin’ incapable?” Jade was beside herself in anger. She picked up to leather overcoats from the pile and closed the door behind her. Brook did not even get a chance to ask for a little assistance and in the process was left in the mess. Jade came back out into the average housed sized hall to see Lacuna tapping the pointed toe of her knee, string up, leather high boots. Lacuna looks up as Jade tosses a zip up leather over coat at her head. The small little Puerto Rican woman chases behind her with a comb. Jade stood behind throwing her single sleeved leather over coat over her shoulder. In the gravel drive waiting for Lacuna was Jade’s present for her. Although probably not as expensive or high classed as she would like there stood in Lacuna’s huge gravel drive a Brand new convertible Mercedes 4 x 4 ML55 AMG. Jade presented it to Lacuna and Lacuna did not take a second before she snatched the keys from Jade’s hands and dived over the door and into leather driving seat. Lacuna thumps her fingers down on the power button and the engine starts. The radio booms out an old English tune going along the line of ‘I go driving in my car.’ Lacuna pops on a pair of sunglasses as Jade turns off the radio. Lacuna slams down the accelerator and hastily careers out of the large black steel gates. -[Fade]- Night has fallen all around. The only light in the whole of the area is provided by the strangely colored lanterns and such. The silver Mercedes is parked outside the gates to what appears to be a carnival; mud is splattered up its exterior. Lacuna and Jade walk side by side into the muddy palace of fun. Jade takes the first opportunity to get some real junk food and grabs a burger, two hot dogs and a bag of pop corn. Hungrily she eats the hamburger while the rest are being prepared. Lacuna watches on in wonderment as Jade hands her one of these cheap hot dogs. Jade’s practically slides down her throat. “Come on then. I can eat the pop corn while we go and look at the freaks.” Jade enthused as she walked on. Unbeknownst to her there were a pair of small and beady eyes staring from the shadows, belong to that of a midget, err small person. Jade began to break into a slow jog as she looked ahead, Lacuna struggled to keep up with her as Jade got into a huddle of people. They were crowded around an arm wrestling contest. People were putting money down onto the table betting on whom they thought would win this round. The winner was a huge man who wore ripped jean shorts and a brown leather waistcoat. He had a patch over his right eye and muscles like tree trunks bulging where his biceps should be. His toothless grin scares the hell out of Jade as he begins to take advantage of the round and slams the other man’s fist into the wooden table almost snapping it in half. Jade gulped and stepped back, deciding it was time to be going look at the other people showing their weird and wacky ‘skills’ in here. Jade’s hand grasps Lacuna’s and begins to tug her backwards. Lacuna wants to see the ugly men beat on each other and decides that she is staying where she is. “Lacuna” Jade insists, in a nagging tone of voice. “They are looking for their next victim. I really don’t wanna be on the other side of an arm wrestle session with him. Do you?” Jade gets a prod in the ass and yelps, inconveniently for her right when the man is asking for a volunteer. It was the little midget err small person who lurked in the shadows before. Jade tried to hide but before she knew it she was sat in the chair with her arm being massaged. Thousands of dollars were being placed on the table opposite her. Her mood changed, she stopped struggling when she realized there was a chance to earn big money by winning it. Lacuna looked a little panicked as she grabbed Jade’s shoulder. 'Weren't you just telling us we should run away?' motioned Lacuna to the slowly calming Jade. A smile was developing on Jade's face as the relish of the challenge began to set in a little. The hands gripped hand, Jade's hand being dwarfed by that of the carnival arm wrestling champion. 'Not now, Hun, not now.' Jade's response was rushed as a roar erupted from the gathered carnies and spectators as the referee's hand left the grip of the pair and their arms tensed. Jade’s teeth gritted as the pain of her muscle filling through her arm let rip. The man was grinning as the crowd around them began laughing at the woman struggling; they taunted them to their hearts desire. Jade let out a feline like growl as she hissed to Lacuna. “If you want to do a girl a favor though you could always get me a drink of water quick style.” Jades attention never wavered from the man who sat in front of her, pure determination just oozed from her being. Lacuna turns back and sprints through the crowd, her booted feet sticking in the heavy mud as she does so. She glances at Jade who struggles with holding her arm upright and grabs a bottle of water from a stall, throwing a few coins in the direction of the carnie who sits behind it and sprints back towards Jade, fighting her way through the crowd. 'Lean back, open your mouth' instructs Lacuna, as Jade does so, some what unwittingly. Lacuna pours the water into her open mouth and throws the rest of the bottle over her head, soaking the spectators behind her. 'Oops. Sorry' she offers in way of apology as Jade leans forwards and swills the water around her mouth, spitting it out in one huge torrent towards the face of the arm wrestler 'Cheat' yells an infuriated Carnie, and reached forwards to attack Jade, but Lacuna has already whipped out 'Steve' the shotgun and she cracks the wooden handle down over the head of the carnie, sending him hard to the floor. The crowd moves back instinctively as Lacuna waves the shotgun around in the air. Jade is giggling in her chair so much that even though the man was put off by her cheating her arm is still so close to the wooden desk. Jade grunts ands extends her foot straight into the groin area of the arm wrestler. The stiletto heel must have skewered something because there was a lot of blood following it. “Jesus Christ man do you feel -no- pain?” Jade looks like she is running short on idea’s of what exactly to do as she looks around at the angry crowd. She persists in slamming her heel into his genitalia several times; the man doesn’t even seem to change his facial expression. More shouts and chants of ‘Cheat’ and ‘coward’ were coming from the furious crowd. The wind soared through the dry conditioned Raven locks of Lacuna. Jade struggled on, her eyes nearly rolling into the back of her head with the tremendous pressure she was applying to her arms. “That is going to sting in the morning.” 'Can I not take you anywhere without you getting in trouble?' sighs Lacuna as she strides confidently over to the chair the carnie is sitting on. With one swift move, she kicks hard at one of the legs, splintering it from the rest of the chair, which collapses to the floor taking its occupant with it. Jade takes the opportunity to slam the guys hand into the table as Lacuna picks up the chair and smashes it over his head violently as Jade quickly pockets the money on the table. 'Come on, we've only got one shotgun' shouts Jade as she grabs the hand of Lacuna and drags her through the crowd of now raving carnie freaks, some of whom give chase to the two ladies. Jade laughs as she slings her leather coat back over her shoulder. Lacuna picks a rock up and throws it at the midget she saw grab her ass. They both giggle and dive back into the Mercedes before letting the tires peel on the concrete street.
