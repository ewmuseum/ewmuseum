The Black Suits [5]

9 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: June 9, 2002 The look of utter shock on my face probably couldn't surpass the look on the man's face who was being held over Sporty's head. He was about five foot nine, with sandy brown hair, and a olive green suit. He even had dark green suits to match the god awful ensemble, so it made me feel better that he was about to be taken out by a flying half man/half butterfly alien. Bah! What the hell was I thinking? I wasn't glad that he was going to be hurt - I'm not that heartless. The guilt swelled up in my throat, and I realized that I had to help him. Sure, he was in a suit - a severely mismatched, ugly as hell suit - but I owed him that much. I was, after all, still a citizen of Earth. Besides, if I couldn't stop Sporty here, he was going to come after me sooner or later. Might as well try now. The scene was out of a B-movie or something, with the angry, ugly creature destroying property and taking victims, as other, luckier men and women were able to escape from the carnage. Most of men that left were suit born and headed for their BMW's to drive off. Good for them. Save your own hides in your fifty thousand dollar cars. Even the couple beside me had forgotten about their petty argument about who would order what, and they suddenly high tailed it out of the restaurant. They were smart, I had to give them that. If I had an ounce of their intelligence, and perhaps cowardice, I would have followed them right out of Micky D's at that moment. I don't know what it was - my ego, not being able to handle the fact that I had run away from a confrontation, or my testosterone, always wanting to fight a monster. Whatever it was, I was about to stand up to Mr. Butterfly man and I had no weapons. Go me! Wait a second, I have a gun! The thought grazed my mind like a sharp knife, and suddenly I realized that my best chance to survive was the gun sitting in my pants. Now, let's not get any misconceptions here - I was not Dirty Harry. I had never used a gun before, aside from the light gun that had come with my original Nintendo Entertainment system, so I was not a trained marksmen or something. And for some reason, I highly doubted that Sporty would just fly up to me like in the game Duck Hunt and allow me to get a clean shot. At the moment, he seemed a bit preoccupied with the man he was trying to rip in half, so I thought this was a good of time as any. I knew what I had to do - squeeze the trigger, keep your eyes on the target, and have no remorse. Easy, right? Not in the least... I saw Sporty fluttering over the dining tables, finally throwing the man through a nearby window. He snarled, as if he had just scored a touch down and was spiking the man as if he was spiking a football in the endzone. How cute. I was now coming closer to his position, and I had my gun drawn and pointed directly at his chest. I would have pointed the gun at his head, but if I had missed, I wouldn't have hit anything. I thought if I aimed at the chest, I would still be able to cause some damage. You see, I'm always thinking practically! If I missed high, I would hit him in the face, but if I missed low, I would have made him a new type of male butterfly...oh, the joys of owning a gun... I felt like a hero in a very bad action film, stalking my prey and carrying justice in my hand. I was the law! Ahhh! Get out of my way! A rush of kids hit me below the waist, as the tried as hard as they could to escape from the scene of terror. With all of the kids underfoot, I couldn't help but trip on a few of them, and I soon found myself on my knees. On the way down, I had lost my hold on the gun, which dropped out of sight. I looked around, trying to grasp it once more, but there were so many little feet everywhere, I couldn't find anything. I probably grabbed two or three feet, before feeling something that appeared to be a gun. I smiled, finally finding my gun, and pulled it out. I stood up and quickly pointed the gun at Sporty, who was now staring back at me with an amused look. I suppose he knew that I couldn't possibly hurt him, but I would show him. I threw the gun's barrel directly into the man's face, preparing to pull the trigger. That's when a flash of yellow, green, and blue flew into my face. I felt like a rainbow had just embraced me, but I realized that it wasn't a rainbow, but the gun I was holding. I looked at the gun for a moment, reading the label which read, 'Super Soaker 2000 GTL.' I had picked up a water gun instead of the real gun, and suddenly I became as dangerous as a tiger with no teeth. Well, I must have looked like an absolute fool. That's when I thought, 'Hell, what else do I have to lose?' I pulled the trigger, spraying a strong volley of water into Sporty's face. He writhed around, trying to avoid the splashes of water. He fell to the ground, crashing among the tables underneath him. I threw my arm in the air in victory! He had melted, just like the Wicked Witch of the West, and I was suddenly victorious! Oh, the pride I felt at the moment was equalled only by the pride of winning championships in wrestling. Rooooooaaaaaaar! I nearly jumped into the air startled, and dropped the water gun to the ground. Sporty was slowly getting back to his feet, and he looked rather angry. Unfortunately, he hadn't melted to the ground like the wicked witch as I had hoped...if anything, he had gotten stronger with rage and anger. I backed away slowly, not wanting to anger him anymore, and put my hand in the air, to try to calm him. He let out another bloodcurdling scream, and I immediately began to run back towards the cashiering tables. There was no one left in the store by now, and it was down to me and Sporty. In the background, I could hear the faint sirens of police cars...obviously, someone had been merciful enough to call the police for us. But the sirens were so faint, I knew they wouldn't have come in time to save me before Sporty ripped me a new mouth. This wasn't looking good. Behind me, I could hear the sound of tables crashing and chairs being flung to the wall. I knew it was Sporty, making his way from the dining area over towards me. I leaped over the cashiering counter, making my way into the preparation/cooking area. On the nearby table were some heads of lettuce and a large butcher knife. I grabbed the knife, figuring I could make a last stand. I looked back to see Sporty leap over the counter with incredible agility, and he stalked towards me with those hunting eyes of his. I flashed the knife in his face, giving him the most aggressive smile I could. 'Don't come any closer, bug brain....or you might be wingless in the next five minutes.' Sporty just snickered, shaking his head. 'I'd like to see you try....' I didn't give him a second to make fun of me, and I rushed him with the knife. I swung it at his head, but he ducked, ducking my second blow again, and then grabbing my hand. He kicked the knife out of my hand and then slammed his head into my head with a head butt. I fell backwards, feeling the blood trickle down my cheek and grabbed my face. I almost hit the ground, but was able to catch myself. I countered with several rights and lefts, each handily avoided by Sporty. He then grabbed me into a bear hug, squeezing the life out of me. I tried to take a breath, but I couldn't. I was gasping for air, but I couldn't even move, so I did what any self respecting person would do...I slammed my head into his. It would take four shots before Sporty finally loosened his grip on me, and I slipped out of his grasps. I would almost certain that I was more dazed then he was, and I crawled away, hoping that a secret entrance would open up for me to escape in. In our struggle, a head of lettuce had fallen off of the table and on to the ground. I grabbed it, and turned around, readying to defend myself. Sporty was making his way towards me, the knife in hand. A trickle of sweat poured down my face, and I closed my eyes, not realizing what was going to happen next. Blam! Fire! Blam! I opened my eyes, to see Sporty was staggering backwards. There were two holes in his chest, each bleeding profusely. He hissed loudly, but was not dying...his wings erupted into flight, and he suddenly flew out of the kitchen towards the dining area once again. It was there that I realized who had saved me - the two men in suits who had tried to kill me before. One of them continued to fire his gun at Sporty, who quickly flew out of the building and out of my hair. The other man, the asian guy in a suit, crouched near me, taking the head of lettuce out of my grasps. 'A head of lettuce, huh?' He asked me. 'That's what you were going to defend yourself with? Some vegetables?' I could hardly turn my head, so I just shrugged. 'The water gun hadn't worked, so I was desperate to try anything.' The asian man shook his head, busting out in laughter. He turned around, to see that the older white man was coming back over towards us. 'He got away, but I think I wounded him,' The white man replied. He handed a gun to the asian man, who accepted it with a bright smile on his face. 'I found your gun. Try to keep a closer hold on it.' The man looked at me, seeing me continually openeing and closing my eyes. 'Is he going to be okay?' 'I'm not sure,' The asian guy replied. 'I've already called reinforcements. We'll have to take him back to HQ for questioning.' For questioning? I wanted to protest, and tell them emphatically that no one was going to be taking me anywhere. But my head hurt too much and I was slipping in and out of unconsciousness. All I knew was that I was about to be taken somewhere I know I didn't want to be taken, and I could nothing about it. That's when I feel unconscious again.
