Nerezzident Evil (Part 5)

27 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 27th May 2003 “Maybe I’ll just leave you two to it, huh?” muttered the comic edging away from the battleground as the colossuses angrily gazed over each other. Suddenly as the Jester made his getaway the floor shock and the walls trembled as the giants collided but only feet away the exit loomed and come hell or high water there would be nothing to stop Gaz Green. Entering what appeared to be an elevator the Jester continued to cringe at the bloodcurdling cries of the combatants as they duelled to the death but not discouraged he pressed a button on the control panel and the cables creaked into life. “Come on…” cringed the Jester as his claustrophobic container slowly ventured to the summit of the eerie mansion. The cables continued to worryingly creak and the explosive action between the Living Nightmare and the unknown beast soon fizzled into murmurs and finally nothing. Courtesy of this whole ordeal our host the Jester stood a shell of his former glorious self with his once pristine emerald business jacket creased and ridden with an assortment of stains, his freestanding dyed green hair in a state of collapse and his face a beetroot red of exhaustion. With an introduction of impatience the Jester gazed down at his wrist to see the second hand of his watch slowly come full circle and aggravated he subtly tapped his shoe against the metallic floor of the elevator. After a few niggling minutes the elevator crashed to a halt with a heavy thud. Breathing a sigh of relief the Emerald Menace clutched the bottom of the wire-mesh door and lifted it vertically to unveil the latter half of the journey, a winding fire escape of metallic steps that stem down to the laboratory and up in the direction of the roof. Without so much of a choke of second thought the green tailored comic darted up the staircase with a clatter and clang as his foot crashed against the glistening surface of cold hard steel. Moving in a circular motion the Pranks With Attitude founder made a beeline for the roof when suddenly from the depths of this chamber filtered a ferocious growl that shot up from the ground level like an eerie breeze. “NOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO!!!!” sighed Gaz Green. However his progress had taken him to what he had craven most, the exit. Looming over him like his Vampire accomplice had done on so many occasions was a door marked with a neon light plaque that read ‘Exit’. Curling his lips to that of a smile the Jester pressed the bar and the door creaked open ajar. With the door gaping open somewhat a bitterly cold wind infiltrated, one that would make the hairs on the back of your neck stand erect but not discouraged Primetime Championship Wrestling’s resident comic pressed on. His black loafer shoes stepped onto the bare concrete floor and onto a wide-open platform leaving him vulnerable to the elements as moisture formed in the air. “What the fu…” bellowed the five time PCW Tag Team Champion thunderously as the sound of rotary blades erupted. Emerging from the cover of darkness was the distinctive sound of a helicopter; the short sharp slashing sound couldn’t belong to anything else, or could it? Suddenly from the heavens emerged a ray of intense light that pierced through the grim backdrop like a scene from ‘Close Encounters Of The Third Kind’ and waved over the platform. The spotlight continued to roam as though the concrete platform were a theatre stage and finally locked onto the target of one Gaz ‘The Jester’ Green who stood waving his heavy arms frantically, “Like, HEEEEEEEELLLLLLLPPPPP” - CRASH - An explosive sound bellowed from behind the Lord of Laughter and clouds of dust and smoke engulfed the platform, from the debris a silhouette clawed its way into the light. The Jester reluctantly peered back as the helicopter hovered feet above and expecting a Vampire may have been a luxury for emerging through the shower of sot came Vampire evolution manifested. “You must be kidding me?” choked our hero. Standing there was indeed the Living Nightmare with a twist. His mouth not only homed two piercing canines but row upon row of razor sharp teeth like a great white shark and his skin had turned from a pale white to that of a scaly green. His long flowing black trench coat had tore at the seams and with his added height his trousers had shredded allowing his bulky legs to protrude from behind their fabricated cover. The Jester stood in awe, his lower jaw hanging loosely and the pupils of his eyes widening to allow the green of his receptacles to engulf the remainder his eyes and his body cramped minimising movement. “Not now?” hastily said the Academy graduate trying to lift his legs. “Please god don’t let me die here, I mean don’t let me die in a pitiful state like Alabama and not until I’ve accomplished singles success, it’s going to happen any day now, I can assure you that!” whelmed the comic further with his hands locked in prayer. The Vampire edged closer and as though by miracle, his prayers had indeed been answered. His legs came away from the captive of the concrete and the duo darted around the platform as the helicopter observed from the night sky. Like a scene from the Benny Hill show the Conniving Collective ventured around and around in a track and field motion, the song ‘Dizzy’ by Vic Reeves would certainly apply here. Swiping waywardly with his talons, the Angel of Fear couldn’t quite bring down the green tailored Jester who ducked and weaved, sidestepped and used every possible evasive manoeuvre in his arsenal. From the Alabaman night sky erupted a voice, but not just any, the tone distinctively Soviet, “Gaz, hang in there, we have something here that may aid you!” Questions flooded the Jester’s mind, how did this female know his name and how many Russian can a warm blooded American know, one question in particular headlined the front of the queue, “Could it be Natalia?” The event continued, as did the chasing and the overwhelming feeling of defeat. Suddenly the Vampire stopped dead in his tracks, panting and wheezing, seemingly choking and from out of left field the beast collapsed in what appeared to be a seizure, violently shaking on the floor the Pranks With Attitude member curled up into a ball. A few seconds elapsed and the Vampire continued to roll about in agony, clutching his exposed chest and jerking to one side he turned over onto his bare knees and from his mouth emerged a torrent of green bile. “FINISH HIM!!!!” hissed the Russian voice from the chopper once more. With visibility minimum at best, the Jester squinted his eyes to see his archangel but to no avail; however something certainly came to light as a large wooden crate crashed from the blanketed night sky. With the Living Nightmare still suffering internally his tag team accomplice inspected the mysterious wooden crate. Pulling at the lid the rotted wood came away to reveal layers of straw; obviously used to cushion the cargo and rummages frantically the parcel soon looked up to the gleaming comic. Tearing aside the remaining pieces of the lid and throwing the straw like confetti the Lord of Laughter lifted the article from its container with a slight struggle. “Check me out – I got more ammunition than NATO here!” triumphantly wailed the Prince of Pranks placing a huge rocket launcher onto his shoulder. Taking aim, Gaz Green locked in the target of one Nascosto Ireul Nerezza… “Will the Jester turn all trigger happy and destroy his Vampire partner?” “We know Vampire’s are literally indestructible but can they really survive a rocket should the comic fire?” - To be continued -
