PCW Wars: New Hope part 4

17 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 17 - 10 - 02 Entering the streets of Mos Eisley via the small landspeeder, the Rebel force of Alexander Skywalker, Charlie Loc Kenobi, Rush-3PO and R2 D2 find themselves pulled over by combat hardened Stormbringers who browse over the two robots. Stormbringer: 'How long have you had these droids?' A. Skywalker: 'Too long! They're for sale if you’re interested....' Stormbringer: 'Don't you think on a salary like mine I'd be better off buying something a little up market....' Rush-3PO interrupts as the Stormbringer mocks him. Rush-3PO: 'Why, if you weren't holding that blaster I'd go 12 rounds with you! What a hypocrite... It's all well and good bullying a helpless droid but if a man with a throat infection and Halloween costume were here in my place you wouldn't dare cross him!' Stormbringer: 'Err-... Arr-... Well-... Can I please see you identification-... It's Empire policy that anyone with droids entering Mos Eisley must provide identification?' Alex fumbles about nervously, trying to find his ID whilst Charlie Loc talks to the Stormbringer in a controlled tone. C. Loc Kenobi: 'You don't need his identification' Stormbringer: 'I don't need to see his identification' C. Loc Kenobi: 'These aren't the droids you're looking for' Stormbringer: 'Well I knew that - Guys these aren't the droids we're looking for!' C. Loc Kenobi: 'We can go about our business, move along!' Stormbringer: 'Yeah - You can go about you business. Move along, move along!' Alexander hit the accelerator and the vehicle jerked forward leaving the Stormbringer to return to his post. Suddenly as the tail end left the shot, the landspeeder went into reverse and the Stormbringer crept alongside to see what the problem was. Stormbringer: 'What is it this time?' C. Loc Kenobi: 'TAP DANCE FOR ME BITCH~! I've always wanted to do something evil before I die and let’s face it, I barely make it to the end of the film' A. Skywalker: 'Huh, is there something your keeping from us?!?' As the craft ventured forward towards a rundown blockhouse cantina that sat ominously on the out-skirts of the spaceport, the inquisitive Stormbringer was left performing Michael Flatly re-enactments. A. Skywalker: 'That was like sooooo awesome I mean, for a rookie that Imperial Stormbringer had some groovy moves. Oh, and the mind trick wasn't too bad neither!' C. Loc Kenobi: 'The force can have a strong influence over the weak-minded. You will find it to be a powerful ally!' 'Some of the greatest pilots can be found in this water-hole, most would have life long bans if drink driving was against the law but tread lightly as this place is a little rough....' continued the Jedi Knight in a fatherly fashion. A. Skywalker: 'If anybody starts I'll-... I'll-... I'll say droids sick him!!!' The young adventurer and his mechanical servants follow Charlie Loc Kenobi into the smoked-filled cantina. The murky dwelling was clad in an array of weird alien creatures and monsters that littered the bar. Bartender: 'What will it be?' As Alexander leans over to make his order, his hired help Rush-3PO pushed past and demanded the bartender’s service. Rush-3PO: 'I'm not use to drinking in a white mans hive but there's always a first and I could do with a dose of fire water so bartender hit me with your best stuff!' As the bartender obliges, the murky bottle is gripped by the metallic hands of Rush-3PO who devours the content in one flick of the wrist. Rush-3PO subtly tapped the bars work surface and the bartender took the hint by brandishing another bottle of best, again its content consumed immediately by the thirst driven droid. Rush-3PO: 'UP THE EMPIRE! POWER TO THE WHITE MAN....' Rush-3PO’s vocal prowess draws the attention of some of the natives who glare with evil intent, heartily they snarl towards the helpless robot. Alexander peers over to his newfound mentor who happily converses with a behemoth of a creature, standing almost eight-feet-tall, the beast was covered in long flowing hair that hides away its face, yet it still remained awesome in appearance. As they continued to converse in whispers, Rush-3PO had began to feel the after effects of his alcohol binge, swaying and pivoting for balance he falls into the lap of a hideous being with a taste for trouble. Thrown to the ground, Rush-3PO stares back at the alien who now handicaps him two to one. Alongside the creature was a human with rodent-like features, short and grubby he hisses towards the mechanical monstrosity Rush-3PO before addressing the cowering Alexander Skywalker. Human: 'He doesn't like you pee-wee!' A. Skywalker: 'Err-... I'm-... Arr-... Sorry!' Human: 'I don't like you neither... So there!' Rush-3PO injects himself into the escalading spot of bother with a roar from his voice box. Rush-3PO: 'LET ME AT HIM - I'LL TEAR THIS BITCH A NEW ASS-HOLE!!!' Human: 'Don't insult us. You just watch yourself. We're wanted me-... Aliens. I have the death sentence in twelve systems, check me out I'm the man, bow before me bitches!' Alexander begins to kneel in homage, crouching alongside his fallen comrade of Rush-3PO he murmurs a final comment that only infuriates the creature further. A. Skywalker: 'I'll be careful....' Human: 'YOU'LL BE DEAD!!!' The rodent 'esque alien bellowed a loud grunt and the bar went silent, customers moving sheepishly away as the young Skywalker tries to remain cool. Suddenly the cavalry arrives in the form of Charlie Loc Kenobi, the Jedi Knight who tries to settle the dispute. C. Loc Kenobi: 'This little one isn't worth it.... That bronze coloured droid started this dispute so....' even before the tutor can finish, the creatures flooded over the fallen body of Rush-3PO and beat him further as he screamed helplessly, the R2 unit beeping solemnly. Rush-3PO: 'NOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO~!' Leaving the droid battered and bruised in a heap, Charlie Loc introduces Alexander to the creature he conversed with only minutes earlier. The towering monster has been unveiled as a two hundred year old Wookie who maybe able to assist the flock by supplying a craft capable of the treacherous journey to Alderaan. C. Loc Kenobi: 'This is Cherezza. He's first mate on a ship that may suit our needs. Strange creatures continue to play exotic band music with odd looking instruments as Alexander pursues the Jedi Knight Kenobi over too a booth, stumbling behind are the droid duo as always looking rather wary. Cherezza led the collective over to a tough roguish star pilot known as Gaz Solo who is a renowned mercenary, hired help if you would who cuts corners in his race for quick cash, a man with street credit. Gaz Solo: 'Gaz Solo. My real name is unimportant but it's 'Hands.' Anyway I'm Captain of the-....' A. Skywalker: 'HANDS SOLO - How did your mother come up with that, cum, get it?!?' Gaz Solo: 'Huh... Oh, this is some masturbating joke and there's me thinking I've heard them all. I thought I was called it because I'm good with my hands but your mother must've forgotten to mention it!' A. Skywalker: 'I never knew my mother - Nice going knuckle shuffler....' Gaz Solo: 'Checkout the cheeky farmers boy! While I was spanking the monkey, flogging the dolphin, pinching the one-eyed snake, you were out in the field treating the sheep to some T.L.C' As Alex opens his mouth to retaliate, his efforts are thwarted by his mentor Charlie Loc who refuses to sit by and listen to the squabbling of two supposedly grown men even though Alexander Skywalker could still be regarded as a teen. C. Loc Kenobi: 'ENOUGH - Please Han-... I mean Gaz could you continue?' Gaz Solo: 'I'm captain of the Millennium Eagle, but we tend to call her Roland, anyways she's fast and I can't see the voyage to Alderaan being a problem unless your plotting against the Empire?!?' Gaz concluding with a giggle as he looked upon the stern faces of his passengers. C. Loc Kenobi: 'Millennium Eagle?' Gaz Solo: 'Wha-... You haven't heard of the Millennium Eagle? It's the ship that made the Kessel run in less than twelve parsecs! Man, I've even outrun Imperial starships, the big Corellian ships so what’s the cargo squire?' C. Loc Kenobi: 'Only passengers. Myself, the boy, the two droids, and no questions asked...' Gaz Solo: 'What is it? You guys are on the run, the bestiality police, huh?!?' C. Loc Kenobi: 'Not quite... We would like to avoid an Imperial entanglement!' Gaz Solo: 'Well squire it'll cost you a little extra, ten thousand in advance and before your son there mentions that he can buy his own ship for that sort of money I'd remind him who's the one holding the blaster!' A. Skywalker: 'For the safety of my testicles and the fact that we really need your ship, how about two thousand up front followed by fifteen when we reach Alderaan?' Gaz Solo: 'Seventeen, huh?!?' A. Skywalker: 'No shit Sherlock you can count....' C. Loc Kenobi: 'We haven't got that sort of money, but we should be able to raise the cash by selling the droids, which means no mission, which in-turn means no saving the galaxy and....' Alex leans over and whispers in Kenobi's ear, reassuring the Jedi that he can pull a fast one over the Wookie and his business associate the artist formerly known as Hands Solo. A. Skywalker: 'He doesn't know that - Hell, we'll do a taxi run, keep them talking and leave the droids pick up the tab!' Charlie Loc nods his approval and the group exchanges a friendly handshake, sealing the deal. Gaz Solo: 'Dock ninety-four! We have only two conditions... No pranking aboard her and no attitudes, besides those its open house' The group leaves Gaz to his own comfort as Cherezza heads to the dock, preparing the ship before hand whilst Alexander Skywalker decides to sell his landspeeder the money covering the initial overhead of two thousand. Finishing his drink and about to leave himself Gaz is forced back into the seating arrangement by a slimy, green faced alien known as Greedo who has a history with the pilot and being a bounty hunter he has came to collect, the value of Captain Solo's head being dead or alive. Greedo: 'Going some where Solo?' Gaz Solo: 'I know where this is going. First I'm going to tell you a pack of lies about paying your boss Jabba, which obviously you won't believe and then I'm slowly going to draw my blaster from its holster and conceal it beneath the table before firing...' Greedo: 'You are.....' suddenly the slimy alien disappeared in a blinding flash of light. Gaz pulled his choice of weapon back into its holster, the booth engulfed in smoke, patron customers looking on in amazement, Gaz simply took his queue to exit. Gaz Solo: 'Sorry about the mess!' The scene switches to that of space as two TIE Fighters head towards the goliath space station, still under construction but operational nonetheless. As they approach the image fades to a murky black as another chapter concludes, baring the questions, 'Will Darth Pharaoh ever rid himself of that throat infection?' or 'Will Gaz Solo ever lose his masturbating title, hands?' and 'Does Tatooine law agree with bestiality?' - To be continued -
