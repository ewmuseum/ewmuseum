One Little Stumble & They Give You A City!

3 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/2/02 As if the list wasn’t long enough as it was.....Missouri to the South Pole, to a Cargo Ship that was over taken by Pirates, to floating on driftwood to the island of a Reality TV Show to surviving attacks by the islands natives to what seemed like the safety of a British Navy Destroyer, until he found out that they still believe the Falklands War was going on and have enlisted Xavier, under the guise that he is a US Special Forces man, to lead the assault by scouting the invasion area. We join Xavier and his valet Eleanor Hall in their launch craft, removed of any British navy markings, heading through the surf and up onto the beach of San Lorenzo, the chosen site for the foolhardy Destroyers invasion. Xavier is wearing his usual Black boot cut jeans with Purple embroidery running down the left leg to where he is wearing a pair of Black boots borrowed from a crew member. As well as these he has also been issued a plain Black shirt and a pain of sunglasses do help him blend in. His Auburn hair is slicked back and tied tightly, so that a single strand doesn’t fall down his face and what appears to be an earpiece is present just sitting in his left ear. Eleanor : “Don’t you think it will look a little strange...You know, just 2 people in a little boat pulling up on a beach?” Beside him is Ellie~! who is wearing a plain White t-shirt that appears ill-fitting against the skinny ones she usually wears. With it she has a plain pair of Blue jeans without the lavish effects and extras that her usual denim choices have. Xavier : “Ellie~!, in the last few days we have travelled to the South Pole under the hallucinogenic influence of some out of date Prawns, been boarded by Pirates, attacked by savages and been send on a special mission by a Navy Destroyer who still think that the Falklands War is going on....Compared to that a couple in a boat will just look as regular as a revolution to there people!” The boat comes to halt and Xavier walks over the front of it and leaps onto the beach, reaching up to take his valets hand as she drops down onto the White sand too. She looks up at the impressive bright skyline ahead of them then her faces turns to one of worry as she thinks back to what Xavier just said. Eleanor : “With you saying that....Does it mean there will be a revolution?” The Scientologist then runs that scenario through his mind, not to his approval/ Xavier : “I frickin’ hope not!” They walk up the beach, basking in the warm sun who’s rays are shining right onto their faces, relishing in the warmth of it after the hours they spent stuck in the Destroyer’s cabin. They then get to the top of the beach to where it meets the concrete of the city over looking it and while Ellie~! takes the small flight of stone steps up, Xavier places his Right hand on the surface and vaults up, not noticing that the earpiece he had dropped out and into the sand. Eleanor spins 360, grinning at the feel of something solid below her. Eleanor : “Ah......Land! It’s like, all solid, and you SO don’t sink in it...Well apart from Quicksand, but how many people do you know that have been caught in that?” Kannon looks to be deep in thought at Ellie~! words. Xavier : “Didn’t your cousin drown in Quicksand?” Eleanor : “Yeah.....But she had like, really bad hair so she doesn’t matter....Land is like, so much harder and less cold that water and there aren’t any crazy sailor people on it!” The Gold Patron Meritorious chuckles, thinking to his initial thoughts at finding out he was likely to go to Argentina on his trip back from the South Pole. Xavier : “No crazy people? You do realise we are in Argentina don’t you!” The Preppy valet starts looking around at the mass of busy humanity that they are walking into, busy commerce happening all around and workers on their way from and to their jobs. Eleanor : “They all look normal enough....Hi.....Hey......Didn’t you used to clean my pool?.....Whoa! Aren’t you the guy who worked at the Taco Bell in Hartford about 4 years ago?....Wow, I almost feel like I know these people!” As she waves to seemingly random people she starts to attract attention so Xavier takes her by the arm and they walk into the crowds, him whispering to her not to draw attention to them. Eleanor : “So, are there any Scientologists in this place?” Kannon shakes his head and sighs. Xavier : “Afraid not....This is what we call an R class country. It doesn’t so much have a Government as a guy put in charge to cause the next national revolution....So we guessed sooner or later we’d get blamed for something and hung in the street as the cause of the buses running late or something....Same in Equador, Peru.....Well, in Chilli we had Pinochet helping us, he’s really a Scientologist you know, but after he kinda started killing everyone who lived in his country we severed the ties.” His valet seems confused and furrows her brow. Eleanor : “What? Didn’t he kill enough?” Xavier looks away from her and lets out a long, silent sigh. Xavier : “You really don’t know much about Scientology do you my little Poon Puppy!” As with any criticism or her, Ellie~! is not interested and raises her right palm up to his face and gives a comical sneer. Eleanor : “What-EVER!” The 1st couple of Scientology then turn the corner and all of a sudden a ruckus expands around them with chanting, loud crashes, burning and police sirens. Eleanor : “Wow......This must be their equivalent of the Rio Carnival, I love street parties! Look, they are setting ethogies on fire! That must be a quaint local custom!” Kannon looks to his right where a man is being beaten with batons by 5 Police officers Xavier : “Um.....My little Shag Squirrel, I don’t think that this is a carnival!” The crowd then run over and start to attack the Police, forcing them to retreat behind a row of riot shields. The hoard then line up opposite the Police and start to throw bricks and petrol bombs at the line of authority. Crowd : “REVOLUTION REVOLUTION REVOLUTION!” Standing behind the main protest group, Ellie~! covers her ears to shield them from the deafening rhythmic chanting. Eleanor : “They could at least pick a decent tune to sing it to! What about the one that one Tracy Chapman did....” One of the men that was standing near her then taps a friend on the shoulder and a message is passed through the crowd....Then they stop their chant and start to sing in a gruff, deep voice. Crowd : “TALKIN’ BOUT A REVOLUTION TALKIN’ BOUT A REVOLUTION!” Kannon looks on in bemusement, shaking his head. Xavier : “No....What about the REM one....They did a song called Revolution....” The same thing happens again with Xavier’s suggestion being stooged to the rest of the crowd who adopt a higher, more nasal voice and start to sing in a more up tempo voice. Crowd : “LA LA LA-LA REVOLUTION.....THE FUTURE - NEVER HA-PE-END!” The Scientologist shakes his head at their rendition, not impressed. Xavier : “Hmmmm.....Maybe their 1st effort wasn’t so bad after all!” The crowd of 1000’s yet out a collective sigh and go back to their standard South American protest chant, shaking their fists at the Palace of San Lorenzo’s governor. Crowd : “REVOLUTION REVOLUTION REVOLUTION!” We cut way from the crowd to Xavier and Ellie, who are just standing off from the rioters, not looking at all sure what to do. Xavier : “This has kinda taken me by surprise....I am not really sure what the correct etiquette is for a Revolution.....Should we join in with the looting and the rioting or just play the spectator?” Ellie~! grins, wanting to have some fun while they are caught up in the lawlessness and points to a shop which the mob has so far left alone. Eleanor : “I want to break that window with.....This brick!” Xavier : “Okay, just pretend that the window has Chris Chase’s face on it and hit him right between the eyes!” She picks up the brick and lets it go with a typical girl throw, barely making the bottom of the window as the impact shatters the bottom foot or so causing the rest to collapse. Eleanor : “Whooo! I hit the Canadian in the mouth! Yay me!” As she goes to grab another Xavier blocks her hand and starts to walk away from the crowd. Xavier : “Okay, I don’t think Scientology wants me to cause a national crisis so from now on we just watch, oka-” Suddenly, due to the ultimate state of enlightenment of his brain due to his time as a Scientologist Xavier manages to turn around in a split second and pluck a brick out of the air just a foot or so infront of his face. Xavier : “HEY! WATCH WHERE YOU ARE THROWING THE BRICKS! YOU NEARLY TOOK MY SCIENTOLOGICAL EYE POUT OF MY SCIENTOLOGICAL HEAD!” He looks at the brick which already appears to have someone’s blood on it. Xavier : “Okay, now with my enlightened mind I can resist the urge to throw this brick and calmly put it down he-” He goes to walk off but trips, sending the brick in his hand on an arc up into the air, flying over the heads of the crowd and down onto the head of a balding man in a military uniform with a plethora of decorations that is emerging from the building behind the Police on the side of his head, dropping him to his knees. Governor : “Owwwwwwwwww!!!!!!” With this catalyst the crowd overpower the Police, sending the officers retreating back away from the injured mans residence. Crowd : “REVOLUTION REVOLUTION REVOLUTION!” Ellie~! watches the man drop with a satisfied smile on her face. Eleanor : “WOW! You hit him right on the temple.....I think he’s hurt........Wait......They are doing CPR....” The military man’s security pond on his chest and check for a pulse. Eleanor : “Whoa! You just SO totally killed the ruler of this place! Does that make you the leader?” The mob turn to Xavier who is dusting himself off and they warm around him, patting him on the back and trying to raise him up onto their shoulders in jubilation. Crowd : “EL PRESIDENTE! EL PRESIDENTE! EL PRESIDENTE!” They begin to carry him towards the dead man’s residence, with Ellie~! just about keeping up with him in the huddle. Xavier : “Um......Hi.....No, I don’t want to be your leader! No offence, I have to get to America!” Eleanor : “I think they like you! Cool, they are taking you to your Palace!” He looks down to his happy valet, feeling queasy about being carried about over the heads of the mob, being shaken with every step. Xavier : “No, it is not cool! Before I even have a chance to get out of it I’ll have my head on a spike for not waving with the right hand or something!” The preppy valet then holds both of her palms up infront of her hand looks between the 2 of them, confused. Eleanor : “Oh....What hand do I wave with?” Xavier sighs as he is lowered from their shoulders and down next to Eleanor as the crowd then back away from them as the 1st couple of Scientology stand on the pale Grey steps of the brilliant White building behind them. Xavier : “It was just an example my little Fuck Ferret!” They looks down from the steps to the silenced mob who are standing deadly silent before them in anticipation. Eleanor : “Whoa....They just gave you an entire city for tripping up! Shouldn’t you, like, be talking to them now as their entire lives, their family’s future and health are all in your hands?” She asks in her pushy, preppy annoying voice looking across to Xavier’s face which is turning pale, his Green eyes wide open and his bottom lip quivering as the shot fades to Black. Xavier : “This CAN NOT be good!” As we hit Black we just here the sneering voice of Ellie~! Eleanor : “Not..........Be.........Good!”
