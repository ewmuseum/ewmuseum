The Task at Hand Part 1

18 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/18/2002 'Mr. Kano, the conference room is yours for an hour. I will personally make sure that no one bothers you or your associates for the full extent of the hour,' says a middle-aged man in a business suit as he closes the doors to a huge room with several full length picture windows and a huge oak table in the center. 'Alright, gentlemen, lets get down to business, because I have many other things I need to take care of,' says Jon Kano to his associates; Semore Smoke, Mark Amulet, and Mark Michigan. Amulet and Michigan are each seated on the long sides of the table, with Semore Smoke seated at one head of the table and a blank spot at the other head, presumably for 'The Man who Rocketh the Mosteth.' Amulet states, 'Then why, Jon, are we even here?' Kano looks at Amulet with a bit of rage in his eyes and demeanor. 'Because, Mark, the time tested theory when it comes to Alliances is that it is a good policy to have a couple team meetings before we get in the nightmare from hell known as the Alliances match,' Kano wittingly snaps back. 'Jon is right, Mark. I have known him long enough to give him the benefit of a couple hundred doubts. For the three of us, it is our first Alliances, that is why I went out and got Mr. Kano, a personal friend of mine, and a man who has seen it all here in PCW,' Smoke says defending his friend. 'Hey, Michigan, you got something to say before I get to the task at hand?' questions Kano. 'Nada, I just want to get this thing done and go back to getting ready for Alliances physically,' Michigan answers. 'There is your first mistake, but not one that is uncommon. You can train your heart out. You can run ten miles a day until the day before Alliances. You can lift weights in the gym forty hours a day, nine days a week. But all that is futile, gentlemen, because as trying physically as Alliances is, it is the mental part of the match that is the most difficult. Today I am here to just give you a taste of what is to come,' warns 'The REAL Legend' Jon Kano to his three relatively new teammates. After saying this, Kano reaches into his briefcase, which was laying in front of his spot on the table. He pulls out three video tapes and tosses one to each of his teammates. 'What is this?' asks Michigan. 'The two Alliances matches prior to this one. I want each of you to watch this between now and the next time we meet. It will give each of you a new understanding of this match and appreciation of old schoolers like myself for surviving the match and sacrificing ourselves to set the bar a little higher for excellence in this industry. Gentlemen, that is all for today. We will meet again on Wednesday somewhere. Do watch the tapes. Good day, gentlemen,' Kano says. All three of Kano's teammates rise one by one, each shaking Kano's hand before they leave. After the last one, Semore Smoke, leaves, Kano flips open his briefcase and pulls out a cigar. He sits down in his chair, lights the cigar, and puffs away as he looks through the large picture windows across the room. 'I am so f*cked, none of them will ever understand where I am coming from until they are here as long as I have been.'
