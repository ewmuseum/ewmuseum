Missing: Chapter II: Getting Information

Date: MisSyNg It had been a few days since I had talked with the Krishna on my mission plan. I was starting to regret that I had taken the job. At first it seemed very easy. Just figure out who a guy was, Ivan Chistov. Turns out, it's alittle more complicated than that. I have a program where you enter a name into the database and it gets any information in has on that person/thing/place. To me, I thought that all I had to do was put Ivan into the search engine and problem solved. Right? Wrong. Here I was, on the plane to Greece on the PCW international tour. I had my lab top in my hand, ready to do some searching. The computer was fairly fast, only about four months old. An AMD Athlon 850 meant for more busines work. Yet, I had still managed to fit on my assortment of game, from NHL 2003 to High Heat Baseball 2003. I opened up my database program, ready to do some steady work on finding this Ivan Chistov before playing a game of hockey in the season I had going. I entered, 'Ivan Chistov' in to the database and waited as the 'loading' screen flashed with the hour glass that never moved. After the few seconds, the databse entry for Ivan Chistov popped up: Name: Ivan Chistov Age: 34 Other Aliases: Freddy Mystique, ???, ???, ???, ???, ??? Hometown: Russia Current Job: ??? Other Information: After being kicked out of the CIA in 1999, Ivan Chistov's location was never relocated until in 2001. In 2001, a phone call was intercepted from an Ivan Chistov that was being sent to one, Henry Clavel. The phone message script went like this: Ivan: Henry, I found four million dollars. Henry: Good, I'll see you soon. The phone went dead. After that, no information on Ivan Chistov has been found since. I realized that finding Ivan would be harder than I thought. If only I could find out what his past five aliases were, I could gain more information. The only information I had now was a small phone conversation. Wait a second, that was it! The only two names that were picked up on the information databse were Freddy Mystique and Henry Claval. Since Mystique was a past alias of Ivan, I decided to enter that name into the database. Name: Freddy Mystique Age: 34 Other Aliases: Ivan Chistov, ???, ???, ???, ???, ??? Current Job: Scam Artist as a fortune teller. Other Information: Freddy Mystique, who also goes under the name of Ivan Chistov is very dangerous and is considered armed. His relationship with con artist, Henry Claval in unknown, but Freddy's last appearance was in 1998 when he opened his own fortune telling business, taking customers money and shooting them if they made any struggle. Freddy's business was stopped by the cops in 1999 when they found out his scam. After getting released from jail by an unknown accomplice on a small bond, Freddy Mystique seemed to disappear. This time was when Freddy Mystique was thought to disappear and turn into Ivan Chistov. No other information on Freddy Mystique. No new information on that file, I only had one more name to try-- Henry Claval. I entered the name into the database and waited for the loading screen to finish. When it did, my eyes went wide open in shock.
