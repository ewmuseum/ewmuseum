Grave Visions

3 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3rd October 2002 Have you ever noticed how the strangest things happen in dreams? One minute you can be flying through the sky without a care in the World, the next you could have a whole army of anger people trying to kill you and whatever you do you just can’t get away! Then you come to even scarier situations like falling off of a cliff and waking up just as you hit the ground, But there’s one thing that always make people confused…. Is there a way to control what happens during dreams? Or are dreams just a further reach of what is to come during reality? Maybe that’s why some dreams cannot be remembered or are best left at the far reaches of your mind! Either way any dream where you for-see your own death is bad….. -=***=- Umph, I grunt as I fall face first into the soft ground of wherever it is I am. What the fuck…. for some reason nothing around me even look faintly familiar. As I pick myself up from my sudden fall, which came from absolutely nowhere, I notice that I in a small clearing surrounded by thick trees and green grass underneath my feet. I continue to look around and as I look up I see a starry night sky with a full moon hovering directly above my head, casting light down on me and the small clearing around. It kind of makes everything seem eerily strange, as though the clearing and myself and stuck in a spotlight….. I not bothered too much as I go to wipe my eye when I notice something strange on my hand. Huh? I shake my head trying to see if I imagining things but I not, Taking a look down at both my hands in front of my face I notice that I wearing two gray gloves which are relatively dirty as are my forearms. There something wrong here…. Thinking a little clearer I take look down at my body and stumble back when I notice that I wearing strange clothing. Not only am I wearing a pair of faded Denim overalls but I also wearing big thick black boots, the kind that only White Boy’s wear….I begin to freak out as for the first time in my life I show a sign of fear. “Ahhhhhhhh!” my voice echoes around as I stumble backwards and fall into what seems to be a knee deep hole. I sit up on my hands before I quickly leap out of the hole and begin to look around at the surrounding woodlands expecting someone or something rather to come out and jump me….nothing happens. “I-I-I not scared!” my voice quivers into the darkness as I feel myself stumbling back once more. Being a little more cautious and alert this time I look behind me a quickly lift my boots to avoid stumbling over a shovel with a wooden handle and stick and a metal tray. Once again things begin to creep me out. This like something out of Resident Evil… I begin to think to myself as I brush my hair back from my face with one of my gloved hands, All that missing now is some Ghoul to come and jump me! Being one to play a lot Playstation in my spare time I react quickly and pick up the shovel which is now in front of my feet as I hold up in front of my face, awaiting something to come running at me. I paus in the moon light for what seem like eternity, watching the surrounding woodlands and waiting for something, anything to suddenly run out of the forest and attack me….it doesn’t happen. Heh… I let out a sigh as I put the shovel down into the ground and lean on it, feeling a little tired when all of a sudden a though springs into my head, I feel like digging…. I not know where it come from, or why I wanting to do it but all of a sudden my head just telling me to start digging….so I turned around and began to dig further and further down into the hole I had just moments before fell into. It seem like every muscle on my body was tensed up as I dug and threw pile after pile of fresh dirt out of the hole and onto the soft, moist, green grass which was just outside it….. For some reason I began to sweat like a pig, What the fuck?! This not like me…. I’m usually one who is used to a lot of hard labor, coming from a place like Vietnam but after digging a mere few feet down into the Earth I was sweating like there was no tomorrow!! It didn’t bother me too much as the sweat just absorbed into my Overalls and cooled me down as my arms tensed after each push and pull action I did with the shovel, All of a sudden my arms then began to feel as though they were going to drop off, I began to feel like something out of Crash Test Dummies as every muscle in my body began to ache, but somehow the voice in my head managed me to push on and keep digging. Not one being one to argue I did as I was told and kept digging down into the hole, ignoring everything around me and forgetting about the Ghouls which I suspected to come running out of the woods, I was a good 3ft down into the Earth when I decided to take a rest. ….Never before in my life had I ever breathed so hard or heavy. It was like I had just had sex a million and one times, or wrestled the entire PCW roster at once. The pain that was pulsating through my body was excruciating… But once again something inside my head made me push on. The force that possessed me to go on was indescribable but for some reason it just made me keep pressing on. As I continued to dig thoughts start to surface in my head, What was I digging for?, How was I going to know when to stop?, When was I going to stop? they began to float around until my head felt as though it was going to explode. Suddenly I stopped digging and looked up at the full moon which was hovering directly above the hole which I had just freshly dug….it shone like a spotlight. Fuck… As I looked up at the moon and wiped a drop of sweat from my brow I noticed that I had dug the hole a good 5 or so feet into the ground and that to get out I was going to have climb or crawl back up the sides which would prove to be a difficult task. Either way the breather didn’t last for too much longer as I bit my lip and continued to dig….I was about to give up when I threw the shovel down in a swing and it made a clinking sound as I hit something. Finally everything began to make sense….I was robbing someone’s grave! I hope he a rich muh-fucker! I began to dig out around the coffin which was buried in the hole I had just dug when more strange questions began to float around in my head, Was this even a Coffin?, Why in Buddha’s name was I robbing a grave?, What on Earth make me dig here?! Somehow I managed to clear those thoughts from my aching head as I brushed away the dirt from the top of the rather small coffin with my glove as I threw the shovel into the side of wall of the hole and took a look down at the lid. What the fuck….this in Vietnamese! Surprisingly enough etched into the steel plate of the coffin were the words “Immortal Youth” and underneath was a blurry picture of a young Asian man with longish hair. “Holy Shit…” the words left my mouth and drifted off into the night air as moon suddenly began to shine down the hole and onto the coffin’s lid. I moved away a little and began to examine the rest of the lid of the coffin, noticing more Vietnamese etchings including “R.I.P 1983 – 2002” which surprise me a little as I living in the year 2002 now…This person must of just die or something! You’d think that robbing a grave would have some eerie effect on you but it doesn’t! Maybe it just because I used to doing things like this all the time, or maybe it just because I not afraid of death…either way I clenched the side of the coffins lid and began to haul up. Just like in the movies the lid flew open and there was a loud whooshing sound as air escaped the containment, my heart suddenly skipped a beat. Uh-oh, something not right…. My heart began to beat rapidly as the coffin lid fully opened and the moon began to shine down, slowly revealing a withered body behind the shadow in the coffin. As the shadow was slowly being lifted off of the body I began to notice small things, like a tattoo on his pectoral, which seemed strikingly familiar and something smudged in silver paint across his abdomen, the shadow continued to be lifted slowly. “Noooooooooo!” the shadow fully lifted off of the body’s face as I nearly died before letting a scream off into the night. Laying in the coffin in full view of the night was my own body, complete with the words “NvG” and “PCW” branded onto it but with no possible sign of death. Surrounding my body and lining the coffin were dollar notes….Wait a minute, as I reached down to touch one they became alive as a million and one cockroaches began to swarm around and escape the coffin, causing the bottom of the pit to become a living floor. I yelped aloud and scrambled up the walls of the pit, struggling and clawing to get onto the surface, gasping for air as I fell flat onto the soft wet grass. I turned behind me and looked on in horror to see cockroaches swarming over my face still leaving the words “NvG” visible on my chest before closing it over and then the hole suddenly filling with darkness. “Who-who-who there?!” I yelled into the darkness as whispering began to pick up around me. I tried to speak again but my voice had gone and I looked around in panic as I noticed the shadows begin to advance from the trees and into me standing in the middle of the clearing. The whispering was in Vietnamese and was piercing my ears as I could understand everything they were saying….and for the first time in my life I was truly afraid. I couldn’t move….I couldn’t speak….I couldn’t see…. I was nothing….. -=***=- I for-saw my death…. I now have a idea of what may lay before me and it not look pretty! For some reason I have a feeling I going to come into close contact with the Neo Void God, but I may be able to avoid it. People say that dreams are sometimes warnings, that what is seen in dreams is one of many outcomes following choices of possible paths….. Either way, I better chose carefully…if I follow wrong path I could end up dead….or worse!! In the hand of the Neo Void God himself, Akiyama
