The Searching - Part IV - Home Bitter Home

28 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2-28-02 .Song listening guide: 'No More Drama' by Mary J. Blige. The lyrics add to the surrealism, I'm thinking. BOBBY STORMBRINGER The Searching - Part IV - Home Bitter Home Then.... STORMBRINGER: Pffft-pheh. Must.. wrap.. this up. Besides for that match, there's onal a few.. 'personal' things I need to take care of, mostly back home! Where I wish I was right now. In Orlando.. gotta confirm some things for my basketball game.. and something a lot more important. Forget drama and suspense! I'll tell you now. After Turmoil, you'll be seeing me take down one of the fool b!tches that tried to take me out in January. I'll beat the stuffing out of him until he gives me the other names I'm looking for. Or until I break him like when we were all kids, like when we broke the little new Star Wars X-Wing fighters and video games for Christmas. What a shame. No really. I mean it. Then.. March Madness. Ooo yeah! Wait, not Macho Man. Forget about that.. just remember this. Asylum has a one in five chance by pure statistics. This weekend... just like the last one? One to remember for The Showstopper. Now let us all just hope that 'The Wheel Of Fate' lets it end up bring some fond memories. And baby... that's no b.s.! Alrighty, you all satisfied yet..? B.S. pauses as if he can actually hear a response from someone. STORMBRINGER: What do you mean, no!? Well, too bad! I'm freggin' freezing, I have bags to pack and a plane to catch, so Asylum in Canada? We out. B.S. counts to himself, quietly to three and he runs off to his nearby rent-a-car down the street. Quickly so he can get outta the bloody weather, arrive at Turmoil, rest somewhat backstage, and stop having to hear about those two lucky Olympic Canada hockey teams, already. We get the point, people. Enough is enough! And now.... * * * Before the chaos of the day, there is the obligitory catching up to do. Bobby 'The Showstopper' Stormbringer is currently resting up for the highly ranked PWAer Brian Gaines in the second round of the Global Title Tournament on Saturday afternoon, as well as something even more important to him that that is. Strangely enough, there is one full day and then into the next day's early evening to recover. So thanks to the early start time for the GTT contest, it should all equal out to a little over fourty-eight hours to get ready for 'The Big One'. Instead of the usual Sunday start time, this upcoming month's Pay-Per-View for Primetime Championship Wrestling, March Madness, actually takes place on Monday, March fourth. On that day, Bobby Stormbringer will be a part of the PCW Roster for the fifty-first day. Despite that, Bobby Stormbringer will already be in his first PCW Pay-Per-View main event. True, this is most likely only because of the twenty total people involved in this form of a 'Royal Rumble' of sorts. But who cares, you can't help but cherish your first Pay-Per-View match, let alone your first of any kind. At least in this current wrestling company. Besides, B.S. defested two ccapable wrestlers to get into it. Capable as in meaning... they actually cut some promos, actually trained for the match! Wow! What a concept. Plenty of motivation. Three stablemates in Asylum to support, to make sure they don't get thrown out. Not to mention himself, naturally. So many other people he resepcts, despises... or sometimes even both. Snow. Corey Williams. Tyler Nelson. The Chef. Above all else... that damn Kid Wonder. Quintessential Teen Idol? K-Dub? He is PCW?? Whatevah. B.S.'s fallen hero just might finally get a smidge of what he deserves from his former 'Number One Mark'. Even if it's just for twenty or thirty seconds in such a potentially chaotic environment. Technically sound mat wrestling and submission wrestling against a suicidal lucha style?? In a battle royal!? Looking at the blueprints of a possible showdown, K-Dub is in for a hell of a ride. Climbing the turnbuckles and bouncing off the ropes all the time when elimination is just leaving the ring from over the top rope is not advised. Not at all. B.S. will never say he is one hundred percent perfect. Just devastatingly versatile! Things have cleared up for the main event after Turmoil, and things possibly couldn't look any worse for Stormbringer's fellow Orlando resident! K-Dub has drawn number two, which might as well be number one. Since of course, two people start things out in the ring. B.S., meanwhile, has drawn the average number of eight. As for other Asylum members, he'll come out right after Miyagawa, who drew sixth. Also, Darkstar and Xavier Kannon will come out back-to-back very late in the deal, fourteenth and fifteenth respectively. So Asylum's luck pretty much averaged out.. just don't try to talk to Miya about that. Still, it must be said that B.S. is more concerned with making sure someone like Miyagawa becomes Champion, someone with much more PCW time under his belt. Although it must be said that B.S. is one of the oldest in PCW at twenty-five, with eight years already under his belt in the business. A fact people really can't seem to understand. Why is B.S. so willing to help not only himself, but his stablemates? Assuming it doesn't come down to himself and one or two of them at the very end? Simply because winning the World Title now is not in B.S.'s ever-changing master plan. Something low and humble like the Extreme title or the TV title is. However, if B.S. were to somehow shock all of Primetime Central and win this damn thing... Gawd only knows the emotional outburst we'd see after all the years of heartache, all the adversity, all the pain. Do you wish to see an example of the sorts of things that have plauged Mr. Prime Time in his sorted past? Then your wish is my command. * * * 'Every search for a hero must begin with something every hero requires. A villian.' -from Mission Impossible: 2 --2-27-02, Wednesday afternoon, 1:27 pm-- Not for the first time, today's scene fades in with Bobby Stormbringer staring at a door back in his hometown of Orlando, Florida. B.S., for some odd reason, is wearing part of his wrestling gear. Namely his dark silver trenchcoat and the black, sleeveless, and generic PCW Asylum t-shirt that he always wears to the ring can be seen peeking underneath it. The lower half of his attire is what you would consider street clothes from The Showstopper. Old blue jeans, fairly new blue and white T-mac sneakers. The accessories being dark blue fingerless gloves, the gray timex watch, the battered Orlando Magic cap that's hanging from a belt loop on his pants, and the custom Oakleys that he wears on his face virtually everywhere. B.S. is staring at the thick, wooden front door of a tremendously large house, something on a scale of a small mansion, if you will. Those loooong-time, diehard fans of The Showstopper in Central Florida and the greater New England area might be experiencing a bolt of nostalgia at the sight of this home, especially as the camera shot pans farther away for a wide shot to confirm it's identity. Brittle old maroon-colored bricks and white paint make up the prodominant colors of the mansion. Many windows, all with classy curtains and blinds. Only the most expensive will do. Yes, only that tiny collection of diehard fans could tell you that this was once the luxurious house of this same Bobby Stormbringer. From about 1994 to 2000 or so, this home was all his. For as long as B.S. can remember.. and up to this very day.. this has always been the Stormbringer family home. Well, once upon a time the name attached was Sheridan, but now it is Stormbringer. The surrounding area, mostly the front and back lawns, are also very impressive. Various kinds of bushes and trees, all crafted to the liking of the current owner's wife. A medium-sized garden, as well. One of those cute little stone paths from the front porch to the garage and driveway... Suburbia personified. Bobby Stormbringer couldn't freggin' care less. STORMBRINGER: RANDY!! B.S. is, erm, referring to one of the most selfish, heartless, self-centered bastids he has ever come across, even to to this day. To someone he owes a favor. To someone who ripped Bobby off from most of the money and all of the estate that his deceased parents had left only him. To the owner of this fine specimen of a place to live. To his identical twin brother. To Randy Stormbringer. Randy and Robert. How quaint. B.S. paces back and forth, uneasy to the fact that absolutely no one seems to be home. That just isn't possible. There has always been at least one person in there. Or a pet, or something. B.S. pounds on his old front door for about the tenth time in two minutes, and he's not really being silent with his mouth either. STORMBRINGER: Randy! Bring your wicked self on out!! Stacy!?! B.S. calls out to the somewhat normal wife of Randy. How any woman could be ignorant enough to marry a f*ck-up like that, B.S. can only wonder. STORMBRINGER: I'm not leaving here!! B.S. turns away from the house towards the street farther on, his eyes darting from side to side and his voice returning to normal levels. For the moment. STORMBRINGER: I'm not leaving until I get some freggin' answers. No really. I... wait a minute. B.S. has been searching through his brain, trying to reach for some memories of his old home. Anything that would help. B.S. knows that in the old Eternal Wrestling, on Halloween and a day after the birthday of the twins, Shane Wethers stupidly broke into this house, seeing old and outdated records in EW offices, thinking that this was still the other twin's home. Shane nearly got in deep sh!t for it because as paranoid as Bobby is? Randy is five times worse! Randy installed all sorts of 'home security' and caught Shane on tape when he (once again, stupidly) removed his mask! Bobby bribed those security tapes off of Randy so he could show them to the old EW owner Cecil Cassidy, instead of the police. All he could bribe Randy with, since B.S. hadn't collected the terrific advance on his EW contract... ...was a favor. But no ordinary favor. Any favor that the little punk wanted, and on B.S.'s word, he would come running. Bobby Stormbringer will never break his word. Which is why he doesn't promise things on it very often. It's been a few months. Randy has not collected on his 'bribe'. Yet. Since B.S. can't break in safely, he has remembered that when he owned this place, he used to do something very clever, yet very stupid. He hid one of the spare keys under the welcome mat right out front. After this flash of insight, and now with an outbreak of curiousity, B.S. rolls the strangely lumpy mat to the other side of the porch. While ants scatter for their lives, B.S. doesn't see a key. Just.. a miniscule tape recorder!? B.S. rubs the recording device against the wet grass nearby to free it from any lingering Florida fire ants. B.S. walks over to the driveway and sits on the warm concrete, his back facing the direction of the front door, his face towards the next door neighbor's house several yards away. B.S. places the tiny device down in front of him on its side before pushing PLAY. The voice is fairly clear, distinct. One B.S. will never forget until the day he dies. RANDY'S VOICE: Hello.. brother. Every time the two say brother to one another.. it become more dry, more uncaring. Nearly unfeeling. But there is too much disdain and hate between them for something as simple as apathy. That would be too easy. RANDY'S VOICE: Don't worry. Since I am positive you will have quite the time to make it this far in this 'crusade' of yours, I will keep it short and sweet. Hey. What is family for, eh? B.S. grates his teeth at the pompus sounds of Randy's trademark chuckle. Let's just say that if Drake Maxwell had it, he would be pleased with it's sinister qualities. RANDY'S VOICE: You must know by now that there were three people involved in the attack on you. Eighteenth, was it? Somewhere around there, yes. Of January. You must be slipping. I expected you to arrive at the beginning of this month. Or more likely you are distracted with that Kira b!tch, are you not? B.S. looks at the recorder with wild eyes. How could he know about the woman he has grown to adore? B.S. admits to himself that he hasn't kept the leash short enough on his twin these days. He is one of those people so self-destructive he is like a grenade, threatening to take out all gathered around him. With his nearly limitless monetary resources.. Randy just might be capable of anything. Certainly of knowing anything. RANDY'S VOICE: I will admit this to you. I hope you've figured this out by now, or have been told by the local authorities. Or as I said, I would be.. disappointed. I am one of the people who attacked you. Just not the one with the pipe, is all. Damnit! The main culprit is still unknown. RANDY'S VOICE: Oh yes, there were two others involved. Perhaps I shall tell you the names? Perhaps not. So I suggest you follow my instructions. Since you are family, brother, I will make sure I will not indispose you. No excuses for you getting maimed by Maxwell next week, or at your precious little Pay-Per-View before that. A few snickers from Randy this time, as B.S. just shakes his head in barely controlled fury. RANDY'S VOICE: Ah yes. So you will first do this. March ninth. Be here at our home. Orlando. Take yourself a quick vacation, perhaps to Disney World. It is funny, we have been here for years, you and I. Have we ever been there without being out-of-state tourists?? B.S.'s eyes grow wild again, now out of confusion. The Showstopper knows what is coming next. RANDY'S VOICE: Are you still follwing me, understanding me, brother!? You will show up at the new NTICW's biggest show of all. Magic Kingdom Massacre. That was your favorite theme park anyway, when we were kids. Youhated all that walking in Epcot. Damn World Showcase. STORMBRINGER: ...Why? RANDY'S VOICE: You just be there. Not just there, but in the middle of the ring before the telecast ends. I will give you further instructions. If you are a very good boy I'll even slip you a name. Or other important information. It's not during a PCW show. It's right at home. Very convienient, and I expect you then. If you don't find this tape in time for the Pay-Per-View. You have failed. If you show? You succeed. You know all that is needed for now. Goodbye... brother. There is a soft click, and the message is over just like that. A message of a meeting of some sort. B.S. makes sure to take the tape out, and then he stands. He stares bitterly at the recorder and then the camera shot takes a ground level view of it. The recorder is crushed under The Showstopper's heel. Your wish has been granted. So we cut right to blackness.... THIS HAS BEEN A B.S. PRODUCTION. (c) 2002. ALL RIGHTS RESERVED.
