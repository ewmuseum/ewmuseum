Missing: Chapter XIV: The Date of Project 84

Date: Chapter 14 I was just leaving the arena after the PPV. Most of the roster members here in PCW had left directly after the show last night but I, after three matches, had been too exhausted and decided to collapse in my dressing room where no room service at the hotel could bother me at all. Now, it was in the afternoon, the day after the PPV and I decided it would be best to head to the hotel to pack before heading on the road again. This Tuesday was a big night, once again. I would have a chance to earn another title shot at the title that belonged to me. I was against Adam Stryker, a man who had lost the Extreme title not to long ago. If I beat Adam, I would get a chance at Matt Jackson’s title. Jackson just kept it until I was ready to take it away from him. He knew it and I knew it. I wanted the title back as soon as I could muster it away from him. Adam Stryker, sure he had been in PCW a little longer than I had been, but I was a veteran at heart. He hadn’t been in NTICW or RUSH, had he? He hadn’t thrown Overweighter off the edge of a balcony in a devious plan, did he? He barely even qualified as an Extreme champion and he wasn’t special or anything. I wasn’t scared. Never in my life had I had a fear of height. Where did that sudden information come from? Last night, late after Supremecy was over and I was ready to collapse in my dressing room, VP-S approached me with a smile. In seven weeks from now, the PCW spectacular will happen. Is it Gold and Glory? I don’t think so. That idea was dropped for a devious plan called Cloud Nine. What is it? Cloud Nine is an airplane that is 600 by 100 feet. The plane will stay in the air for nearly a month up to the actual PCW PPV where it will touch down before the PPV begins, only to rise into the air a few minutes later. In this airplane, there are 10 decks where all the action will take place. The decks go as follows: First Deck: Functions as the sundeck. Has windows as the walls; huge, thick plexiglass windows. This deck has a juicebar and a pool. Second Deck: This is the deck on which the wrestling ring is maintained. It looks like a mini-arena, complete with bleachers and such. A small fed atmosphere is prevalent, and the floor can only hold three hundred people. This deck also functions as the communications deck, transmitting the camera feed via satellite to the Primetime Network. Third Deck: Residential Deck -- Wrestlers and VIPs (100 guest rooms, split up into two sides of the hallway). There is also a daycare center for small children whose caretakers want a break. Fourth Deck: Residential Deck -- Mid-Class Guests (100 guest rooms, split up into four sides on two separate hallways; smaller rooms, for those who can't figure it out) Fifth Deck: Entertainment Deck -- Current movies now playing are shown in rooms big enough to hold 30 people. Chamber music, string quartets, and small jazz bands and chorus also have sections dedicated to them (sorry, no rock, hip hop, or metal; bring CD players). Sixth Deck: The restaurant deck, almost any style of food is served here. The chefs are trained to fix almost any type of dish imaginable. It is separated into American food, Latin American food, European food, Caribbean food, Asian food, and Oceanic food. Seventh Deck: The game deck has racquetball, basketball, a video arcade, and an indoor skatepark that also functions as a lasertag battle arena after hours. Eighth Deck: The casino deck, has slots, blackjack, poker, roulette, baccarat, and other casino games. There is also a stage for Las Vegas style shows. Ninth Deck: Boiler room deck. All of the water heaters, heating and cooling systems, and such are on this deck. Tenth Deck: This deck is the engineering deck. All of the machines that regulate the airship and keep it in the air are located here. This deck and the ninth deck aren't general access anyway, so don't plan on going in there at your leisure. Now I know what you are all thinking. How am I going to be in the air for nearly a month and try to catch my own brother, Stephen Neirlinger? To be honest, I wasn’t quite sure but I needed to think of something fast. In the plane, I had my computer to communicate and figure out information, but I had no backup for my mission. “Sir.” I woke up from my think daydream to realize that I was in a cab, parked in front of my hotel. When did I get into a cab? When did I tell the driver to take me here? Either way, I stepped out of the cab, nodding. I headed into the fairly elegant hotel and walked by the front desk, proceeding into the elevator. I stepped into the elevator to head up to the eight floor where my room was. I could try to stop Sven before I took to the air on June 6th, but how could I stop him? I needed a big plan and more importantly, I needed a lead on the date that Project 84 would take place. ~DING~ The elevator door opened and I stepped out. My door was just a few feet from the elevator. Walking to the door, there was a piece of yellow sticky tape on it. That was odd. Who would put a piece of sticky tape on the door of my room? It was probably some punk kids who thought they were cool or something, I didn’t know. It took a few seconds to realize that there was writing in blue pen on the yellow sticky note. I grabbed the sticky note and read the words on it. “Told you I knew where you were. Have fun on Cloud Nine.” That was the entire sticky note. It didn’t take a genius to figure out that the note was from Clair Rivence, or one of her lackies. I read over the note again and it clicked in my head. I knew when Project 84 would take place!
