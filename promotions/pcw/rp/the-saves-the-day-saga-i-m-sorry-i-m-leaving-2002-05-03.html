The Saves the Day Saga: I'm Sorry I'm Leaving

3 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5-3-02 (OOC Note: Im choosing not to acknowledge the match on Sabotage, its degrading, and singling me out is a pisser, i have a shit workrate, but its not even as bad as half this fed. But whatever, count this towards SS if you like, But i refuse to label it as that. Fire me if you want.)--------------- His key turned the lock and the door swung itself open, like it always does. You would think that in the house of the Dream Lord this would be a magical occurance. Don't fool yourself though. The door was the last piece of the house that needs to be restored, it swings open and makes a creaking noise all the time. Its almost like a broken screen door. DarkStar smiled at the thought of a screen door. And his mind swirled. He sat down on the couch and the remembering began. Sometimes he forgets about the little things he and Talena would do before the relationship got complicated. When they could be normal. It was a few short years ago, at her house. The house was small and modest, mostly because of Talena's familys financial problems. They never liked him much but they allowed him to go out with their daughter. He was fine with it but he saw how they treated her and it made him want to sever the majority of they major veins and tie them together. He didn't, Talena strictly forbade it. One night however, her stepdad went too far.... ----------------- His phone rang. It was 12:34. Exactly 12:34. Stirring from his sleep he picked it up, fumbling with the reciever and eventually placing it up against his ear. 'Hello?' He said questionably and was answered with pained sobs. DarkStar: 'Who is this?' A female answered, her voice husky with emotion but still noticble. Talena: 'It's me...' Gavyn sat up and turned his light on, the pale white light illuminating his room. DarkStar: 'What is it? Whats a matter?' She answered hesitantly. Talena: 'Just come over... please...' He nodded, although she couldn't see that, and said something to the affect of 'Be right over' (It is hard to remember, it's been a while). Before he could take another breath he was starting his car and going on his way. ----------------- Wow. So long ago this happened. Yet it was burned in his memory like a sodering iron to a bowl piece. Sometimes, somethings are taken as a picture, your mind places them in the back, filed only to be remembered during important events of your life. This was one of them, it defined a relationship that began over the bleeding body of a lover. Good times... Good times. ------------------ DarkStar, known only as Gavyn at this point, skipped up the steps to Talena's modest house. He noticed that only her car was in the drive way. He knocked on the screen door (the inner door was open), and he heard her tell him to 'come in', he did, like he did a million times before, and walked into the house, Talena was sitting on that ugly orange couch that her mom loved so much. He walked up behind her and put his arm on her shoulder. She had her small hand curled around a damp napkin, her tears stained it with her black mascara. DarkStar: Talena, whats a ma-.... She looked up at him, and saw her eye, blackened to a deep shade of purple. Her tears streamed further as they reached her cheek bones and flowed down onto the couch, she buried her face in his arms and cried, racking sobs now. He knew what had happened. You see, Talena's father had died years earlier, her mother quickly remarried... this drove the rift between the mother and daughter, as her stepdad hated her. Various times he would mentally berade her because of how she dressed, because of her boyfriend... That night he got violent. She spoke out against him, tired of his speech, and he swung. DarkStar growled and took her hand. DarkStar: I'm sick of this... your coming with me... She shook her head, she didnt originally want to go. DarkStar: Or else im murdering your stepdad... you don't want me to go back, do you? He was referring to the mental institution, where he speant his time after the stabbing of a ex girlfriend. She said no, and smiled slightly, grabbing a bag full of clothes and leaving... She looked back, one last time, this was the last time Talena would ever see her house or her family. Mysteriously... the house burnt down two days later. DarkStar had nothing to do with it... But Talena did. The only thing that survived was a small doll that Talena had as a child. She picked it up and clutched it tight. Whispering its name to her as she went back to her car.... Miss Emily.
