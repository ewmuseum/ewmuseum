Shabazz Dreams... (Part 1)

16 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 06/16/02 “Ouch.” That’s what Jordan X Shabazz was saying as he left the Towson Center in Towson, Maryland. Jordan hadn’t wrestled, but he was hurt. John Crichton, the last Television Champion in the PCW before the mass roster firing, attacked him brutally, and unprovoked… Well.. Maybe not unprovoked, but the attack was totally unnecessary. Immediately after the attack Jordan X Shabazz stayed around in the locker room, he couldn’t get stitched up because the cut was too bad, they had to wrap gauze around his head to stop the blood from flowing out uncontrollably. H Like the Malice Crew soldier that he is, Jordan X Shabazz ran out to the ring later on in the evening and fought with the rest of his stable mates. Immediately after the card was finished Jordan left the arena, and headed to Towson University Hospital where he is to get stitched up. That’s where our scene opens up. Jordan X Shabazz sits in his regular clothing, blood staining his shirt, on a hospital bed. He is getting stitched up without the benefit of anesthetics, he‘s obviously angry. The doctor sitting in front of him is working rather hard in his attempt to get Jordan stitched up as quickly as possible, Shabazz demanded that the doctor hurry up. Doctor: “So son, how did this happen to you?” Jordan X Shabazz growls a reply. Jordan X Shabazz: “It isn’t any of your business, white boy. You’re just like Crichton, typical white boy…. And never in your life call me ‘Son’ again.” The doctor doesn’t want to cause trouble with a man who is as large as Jordan, and angry. As the doctor is finishing up on Jordan X Shabazz there’s a knocking on the door. The Doctor doesn’t even turn around, he just speaks. The Doctor: “No visitors in the stitching section.” The visitor pays no attention to the doctor, we see the visitor near Jordan, and toss him a t-shirt. The Doctor is filing some paperwork and doesn’t see Jordan catch the t-shirt, he does hear the footsteps however, and he turns around. He sees Dante Jackson, Jordan’s Malice Crew t-shirt is now replaced with a picture of Martin Luther King Jr. The Doctor looks at Dante Jackson. Doctor: “I said no visitors in this section of the hospital.” Jordan X Shabazz nods at the doctor and he begins walking towards the door with Dante Jackson. The last time Jordan saw Dante Jackson he left him in the midst of a rap concert after Dante used the ‘N’ word, and anybody who has seen Jordan X Shabazz as of late would know that Jordan now denounces Ebonics, drug use, and all around shoddy behavior from African Americans. Jordan plays it now a days as if he’s intelligent. Apparently he and Dante have made up. Jordan X Shabazz wears Nike sweat pants, a black Martin Luther King Jr. t-shirt that states “I Have A Dream” on the back of it. White Nike cross trainers adorn his feet. Jordan’s hair is shown and is cut into what is commonly known as a ‘Southside fade.’ Dante Jackson wears baggy blue Phat Farm Jeans, and a white Platinum Fubu t-shirt. His feet are covered by Blue ‘Lugz’ boots. He wears a Houston Astros baseball cap and a black doo rag to cover his hair. Shabazz and Jackson walk out of the hospital. Dante Jackson was in town filming a movie with El Diablo, and he found out that in Delaware, a few hours away, there was this drug spot, where a lot of African Americans frequented. He has convinced Jordan Shabazz that at this drug spot, he can convert some black druggies from the brink of death, to a high ranking person like himself. ********************************** 4 hours later. To quote Statuz Quo, “Jordan X Shabazz was tired like a bitch.” A night of being attacked from behind, then doing a run in later on in the night, coupled with the fact that he had to drive to the hospital to get stitched up, Jordan was almost dead. The scene opens up with Dante Jackson and Jordan X Shabazz getting out of a tiny Honda Civic, circa 1987. It’s beaten down and broken up, so as not to get stolen by the ghetto black folks in the area. Dante and Jordan look around for the area where the crack heads would frequent. And we don’t have to look far, a corner with a bunch of drug abusers is seen. Dante and Jordan look at each other, and they both begin the short trek over to the drug abusing group of fiends. When Jordan and Dante arrive at the group of fiends, the first thing they realize is how scruffy the men look. They honestly look like homeless men who peddled the streets, and as soon as they got enough money, went and bought some drugs. It’s weed they are smoking. The smell is strong, this is no ordinary weed, oh no, this is IMPORTED CANADIAN WEED! They men see Jordan and Dante approaching them, and they immediately call out. One man: “What do you guys want?!” Dante Jackson and Jordan X Shabazz don’t reply, instead they continue walking forward. Before Jordan can begin his preaching of sorts, Dante speaks. Dante Jackson: “We’re here to save you!” Jordan sighs loudly at Dante’s corniness as they reach the men. Some of them look angry. Jordan X Shabazz has intent on his face, he wants to get these men away from drugs. Jordan X Shabazz: “Listen y’all…” BOOM! Lights out Shabazz. They thought he was going to rob them. One of the men knocked Jordan unconscious, that’s the lifestyle these black men live in, in the ghetto. Now we are inside of Jordan’s unconscious mind, inside of his dreams. His desires. We are in a wrestling ring, through the eyes of Jordan X Shabazz. In the ring with him are John Crichton, and Jaden. Jordan is in the ring by himself. He’s got a wooden baseball bat in his hands. Jaden makes the first move. She does a high kick at Shabazz, Jordan ducks and takes her leg out with the bat. Crichton comes swinging wildly at Shabazz, Jordan ducks him, and nails him in the face with the baseball bat. Something grabs Jordan from behind, it’s his former PWA headmaster, El Diablo. Shabazz turns around. El Diablo: “Ha! It’s that former PWA sellout, Jordan Crazy. He changed his name for a white man! Ha!” BOOM! Shabazz nails him right in the kisser with the baseball bat, it was a Barry Bonds like swing. Diablo falls to the ground in a crumpled heap. Wait, what the fuck was that out of the corner of his eye? Shabazz is taken to the ground by the only blind PCW wrestler, The End. The End gets on top of Jordan and begins throwing right hands in Jordan’s face, barely grazing Jordan due to the fact that he can’t see. After a while he picks up and he begins smashing Jordan’s face brutally. El Diablo and John Crichton come from behind The End and they throw him off of Shabazz. Jordan gets up, still drowsy, looking around. BOOM! Drop kicked in the side of the head by Diablo, BOOM! Lariated stiffly by John Crichton. BOOM! Knee to the groin from Jaden. Back in real time, Shabazz’s cut has been reopened from the punch. The homeless men that attacked him are now gone, Dante Jackson is just waiting around for Jordan to wake up again. Back in dream world, Shabazz is fighting back, swinging wildly and screaming racial slurs at his opponents. Suddenly, Jordan’s dream becomes all blurry, his envisions of the upcoming PCW Friday Fusion. His four way match on the upcoming Fusion was huge for him, these three other men were highly talented guys. Jordan felt that the fact that he was black and a borderline racist, was the only thing that held him back. Plus, he supported the Malice Crew, sworn enemies of Tyler Nelson and his Ultimate Authority. Shabazz finally wakes up, blood pours from Shabazz’s forehead. Dante Jackson tosses some gauze that he retrieved from the car to Jordan. Jordan is still drowsy, yet he wraps the gauze around his head. Was it over for Shabazz? Hell no! Just then, two known drug dealers spotted Jackson and Shabazz. These two men were paranoid as fuck, and immediately thought that Jordan and Dante were selling drugs in there area. What happens next? YOU BEST COME BACK FOR THE NEXT RP TO FIND OUT! AND YOU WILL ENJOY! ENJOY IT! Fin
