The Haunting

11 March 2003

Date: 11/03/03 “More training? You have got to be kidding me!” Jade mumbled into a large black silk pillow. She tosses and turns, talking in her sleep was a major put off to men. Gladly though it only usually happened when there was something on her mind. Jade spins around again, tossing the only silken sheet off the bed and Jade indignantly lies nakedly open for the nothingness which fills the room. Her pierced nipple catches the moonlight and reflects to the top of the room. Jade sits up suddenly and covers herself. Her brow is slightly sweaty as her eyes chase across the room. Jade pulls the sheet back over her naked mass and grunts as she forces her head back into the pillow. Jade had been dreaming about the results of her party since Brook had locked her into her room. Like a little child she had been chased into the room, escaping the booming voice of Lacuna down the phone. All of the people had been escorted from the huge manor after almost four hours. Jade simply watched on with a glass of the expensive wine cupped into her scrawny hands. Now Jade slept on her repercussions a little here and there. This was like a first class prison, better than Jade’s digs by all means but it didn’t feel right to her. It didn’t feel like home and that is just what was affecting her. Insomnia was a bitch and Jade had come to full blow’s with it. Jade seemed to rest back into the plush four posted bed and began to drift. The night was as dark as usual, perhaps made even darker by the sounds which seemed to rattle around the mansion so early in the morning. Jade’s black and white hair is spread in its silky magnificence across the large pillow as she begins to such one of the painted nails on her thumb. Dazing back into a lack of self awareness the black silk sheet rises with her knee. Her arms rise behind her still wet head and she licks her lips. The pitter patter of small feet track along the pine wood which belongs in the hall. Jade’s hazel and white eyes flicker open as she rolls onto her elbow. “Who is out there?” Jade bellows, her eyebrows rise as she shouts into the dead of night. “Brook?” Jade thinks to herself. “Brook? If it is you I wouldn’t mind a small glass of water! I mean I have been locked in here for a day now. I want something to wet my lips or you will be paying for a new door!” Jade rolls out of her bed and to her feet, she pulls a long black dressing gown to her shoulders. “Please Brook?” Jade steps along the carpeted floor and tries to open the door. Her voice pleaded with the foot steps, whatever had plagued her dreams was now plaguing solidly into her conscious state. Jade tried to open the door but there is a lock in that damned golden handle. Jade steps back, she shakes her head in disbelief. “I can’t believe I have to go through this! I am a grown lady and deserve to be treated as so. Even if I have to force it that way!” Jade kicks at the lock on the door and it budges backwards slightly. “Son of a…” Jade grumbles as she pushes the door off its hinges. “Bitch!” She finishes her sentence as her head pokes out onto the dim lit corridor. Her white eye glows in the darkness of the corridor and looks left and right. “Brook.” Her voice lingers, slightly echoing as a soft ‘Heh’ escapes her sufficient Violet lips. She steps over the thick door and onto the warm pine floor. A bright fluorescent light placed against the opposite wall of the corridor reflects the shadows of the trees against it. The small hairs on the back of her neck stand to attention as she brushes the base of her palms against her eyes. Like a child deprived from sleep she stomps against the floor. I hate this fucking house! I hate this huge thing it creeps me out! Everywhere a girl goes she is followed by -something- Shadows and sounds here and there. I had to break out for the freakin’ room for heaven’s merciful sake. I am tired of this bull shit. Lacuna is going to be one pissed off mother when she gets home from her -other- manor. Crikey all I seem to think about here is what these high classed snobs think about me. Why the hell should I care what they think of such ‘scum’ like me. Maybe I can go and stay with Nathaniel or Damien. Oh wait I can’t go and stay with Damien seeing as though he is a two faced treacherous bastard who may just lose me my club. Then there is Carys who is never here any more. I doubt she even knows about the trouble in the club. Jade traces her hands along the huge elegant painted walls and rubbing her fingers in the corners of the painting. She scrutinises the shoddy cleaning work of Lacuna’s workers. Jade tucks her messy long hair behind her tiny ears and shuffle’s her feet along the wood. She stares down at the floor and dizzily walks around a corner. Another shadowy figure stands tall at the end of the corridor. Jade gasps as she looks up. Once again there is nothing there. “Get here you insolent little rats. I can’t wait to break your hands against the exquisite walls and listen to you scream in pain.” As convincing as Jade sounded she was very nervous and antsy about being alone in this dark corridor, petrified is an understatement. After a while Jade stumbles on something cold and rigid. Her stumble leads to a fall and she hurts herself falling to her hands and knees. “Son of a whore.” Jade turns around and looks to the ground, she sits on her ass and wipes her dirty hands on the dressing gown. Jade had found that she had tripped on a wooden log. She had found herself outside the back door. She was now in the back garden. Jade looks around and tries to steady herself on the wall as she climbs to her feet. Jade looked around, unable to see the surroundings she shudders, the cold getting to her. The little bit of grass she can see is grey, lining the floor. “Great! How can things possibly get any worse than they are right now?” The gods were obviously angry with Jade for whatever reason was unbeknownst to her. Obviously something serious as the heaven’s opened dowsing her in the misty rain from above. Jade is utterly beside herself in anger as she stamps her feet on the floor. Jade traces her steps in the darkened surroundings and back through the back door. The light remains of black eye powder which were wiped neatly along her eye lid are scattered round her eyes. The keys in the back door rattle as Jade forces the back door against the kitchen’s preparation tops. Jade holds her hands against the sides and towards the fridge. The almost transparent now drenched black silk dressing gown clings to her willowy body. Jade grabs at a hairnet left in ignorance of hygiene on a chopping board, unused Jade uses it to tie her disorganized hair behind her head tightly. Jade opens the huge fridge and moves her head about inside of it. Displeased she slams the door and head towards a cupboard. Finally she finds what she was looking for in this more structured and organised place. The potato chips! Jade rips open the bag and forces her face into the bag. Loud crunches escort the cheesy aroma that fills her nostrils. Jade stops chewing brusquely and drops the metallic bag to the floor, the chips scattering as the bag does so. Jade spins on her heels, it was three in the morning. The large room expands her fear of the nothing as the clocks in the house chime harsh in her ears. Jade gasped and shook her head as she flounced into the hall nervously. Jade heard a banging upstairs and Jade screams at the very top of her voice. Jade bangs her fist against the lights. The whole of the building lighting up at the master switch. The servant’s rooms light up at the sounds of piercing Jade screams as they roll out of bed. They make their way down to the kitchen to Jade’s horror. This was the last thing she wanted, and in a way everything that she wanted all in one. All of Lacuna’s workers were rushing quickly down the stairs and she was going to be in company of people without being left to herself. On the other hand she was going to be bothered by a huge amount of people she did not even know, no doubt they would be complaining to Lacuna on her return. She knew of Brook’s request for more money just so that they could cope with this great dead weight that they had seemingly temporarily got added to their shoulders. Lacuna would whine on and on about how much trouble this was and how she hoped Jade would get out of her face as soon as she could. That was how this whole thing led to their face off last time. That was how Jade lost her concentration enough to have her hand broken by some ex-prison dyke. How Jade lost to Lacuna. “Stop going over this.” Jade mumbled to herself like a mad woman, not the nice friendly Lacuna kind of mad, but the nasty dangerous priestess Jade kind of mad. The one which kills the people. Brook steps around the corner. Surprisingly most of the worker’s which ‘belonged’ to Lacuna decided it best to let Brook deal with her on a face to face matter. Jade was clearly shaken up with her tan arms folded nicely along her defined abdomen. Brook stepped in front of Jade his eyes sleepily trying to concentrate on the lady in front of him. “I think it best you return back to the room mi’lady.” He sounded similar to an order. “Get out of my face Brook!” Jade hissed, she turned her head away from him. “I think it is better for your health that you do as I say. I am in truly no state to be arguing to a glorified butler like your self.” Jade murmured. “I’m going to go for a little tour. You know to get a proper view of the place?” Jade informed him. She was still in control of the situation. “Didn’t you get a proper view when you wrecked the joint?” Brook wanted to see how far he could push Jade. “Are you still here?” Jade looked right through him. “Lacuna will be hearing about this in the morning when she returns Jade. Perhaps it is best for you to pack you bags now?” Brook sounded evil and malicious. He had his incorrect ideas about Jade. Those could probably change in time. Brook proceeded in nodding to the worker’s who waited on brook’s verdict. Once this was confirmed the lights were switched off as they headed back to bed. Jade leant back against the cabinet as mascara filled black tears traced down her usually flushed face. Her head returned back to her arms as she sat in a foetal position. The lights were once again off, and so those ghosts could return to haunt her once again. To drive her over the edge. Jade needed help from a friend, and pretty damned soon before she cracked and became incapable prior to her career crumbling. “Lacuna where are you?” Jade sobbed. Brook waited around the corner listening in on what was happening in the kitchen. His head fell back against the wall as his head lifted to the sky light in the impressive hall. The light of the sun reflecting in the full moon. He let out a sigh, unsure of what exactly was going to be done. End]-
