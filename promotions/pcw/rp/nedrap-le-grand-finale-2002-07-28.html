NedRap: Le Grand Finale!

28 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 28th July [The scene slowly fades up, inside a large lobby area of what could well be the recording studios for where Andy Murray and Rab McLean will make history, and record the World’s first, NedRap. The place looks just a like a hospital waiting room more than anything else, with several rows of generic sky-blue coloured chairs filling the rectangular room (can chairs be generic?). The walls are painted in the same sky-blue colour, whereas the flooring is just plain white tiles. At the far end of the room, in the far right corner, is a reception area, behind it stand two receptionists, one male, one female. Aside from them, the room is empty. A large set of double doors, leading to the individual recording studios, is to the left of this reception area, potted plants at either side of them. Directly across the room, is a set of revolving doors leading out onto the Glasgow streets beyond. Some cheesy elevator music is playing throughout the room. We cut to the reception area, both receptionists appear to be busy with something, both sitting at computers.] Male Receptionist: Do we have any more recordings scheduled for today Louise? Female Receptionist: Hold on, let me check … [The female receptionist keys in a few buttons on her computer, looking up the records, and scrolls down the screen to find two scheduled recordings.] Female Receptionist: Well, 20 minutes ago one was supposed to start, something about ‘NedRap’, and we’ve got another set for 2 hours from now, some country & western CD … Male Receptionist: What the hell is a ‘NedRap’? Female Receptionist: I don’t know myself. [The Male receptionist chuckles.] Male Receptionist: Probably one of these new experimental music genres that are floating around today … Female Receptionist: Could be. [The two receptionists continue to work, we pan out a metre or so away from the desk, when suddenly Rab and Andy step out in front of us. Today, Andy is wearing a black & white Nike shell suit, minus the cap, and has his hair proudly spiked up at the front with gel. Rab is wearing a blue & white Adidas shell suit, with the cap and all. The receptionist have not yet noticed our Nedly heroes. Andy and Rab look at each other, not sure what to do, obviously not noticing the large “Ring For Assistance” sign, with handy bell below. Just then, they both turn to face the receptionists.] Andy Murray: Ho! Bawbag! [Both receptionists turn, and cringe when they see the Neds standing before them. Obviously the receptionists are from a higher line of society than Andy & Rab.] Rab: Ho man, we’re pure here for our recordin’ Andy Murray: Aye, NedRap min. [The male receptionist gulps.] Male Receptionist: Alright … Erm … Let me just sort out a few things … Andy Murray: Get a move on, ya chube. Rab: Aye min, we don’t huv all day, ken? [The male receptionist turns back to his computer, and keys a few things in, before turning back to Andy and Rab, who seem to already be growing restless.] Male Receptionist: Through the double doors, studio 3, second on the right. Andy Murray: Awlright muckah. [Andy turns, and pushes open the large double doors, Rab follows, leaving the doors swinging to a halt behind him.] Male Receptionist: Jeesh, what a couple of freaks. Female Receptionist: Totally … [The receptionists share a laugh, as the camera cuts to the corridor beyond the double doors. The corridor is decorated with black walls & navy blue coloured carpets, 4 doors at either side, leading in to various recording studios, each one differentiated by the large numbers, 1 to 8, plastered across the front. Andy and Rab look around in sheer amazement, surely even they can’t mess this up.] Andy Murray: Awlright, time to pure find this studio min. Rab: Briwliant. [Andy and Rab walk along a couple of paces, until they reach a door with a large number ‘3’ plastered on it. Andy looks at Rab.] Andy Murray: Think this is the one min? Rab: Ah’m nae sure, think so, though. Andy Murray: Awlright. [Andy places his hand on the door handle, and steps through into the recording studio beyond. Rab follows, closing the door gently behind him. We are in a room full of dials, knobs and switches on a HUGE switch board, a large window above the switch board looks through into the actual recording area. There are also two large chairs inside the initial switching area, in which sit Phillip Parks, the NedRap record producer, and James McClaren, Andy’s seldom-seen business manager. Andy slaps his forehead when he notices James, whom he dislikes with a passion.] Andy Murray: Och! What ye doin’ here ya chube? James McClaren: Andy, relax, as your business manager, I am required to be here … Rab: This is pish min … James McClaren: Allow me to introduce you to Phillip Parks, he will be your record producer for today. [Phillip rises, and extends a hand to Andy, expecting a handshake.] Phillip Parks: How do you do? [Andy just stares at Parks with a confused look. A disappointed Phillip shrugs, and sits back down.] Phillip Parks: So … Uhhh … Have you memorised your lyrics Andy? Rab? Rab: Aye min. Andy Murray: Aye, they’re pure briwliant by the way. Phillip Parks: Good, because once we’ve recorded your voices, we are going to add a little background music, and then release the finished product to the stores tomorrow, the song should chart in the UK Top 40 next Monday … [Andy nods.] Andy Murray: We pure better get tae number one by the way, or Ah’m gonnae pure hammer you. Rab: Aye, ya toff. [Phillip clears his throat in discomfort.] Phillip Parks: Well, could you please step through into the recording area, and we can begin. [Andy and Rab step through the door into the recording area beyond the glass, and stare at Parks. Parks flips a few switches on the switch board.] James McClaren: Alright, I think you’re going to have to pull on those headphones, and speak into the mics. Phillip Parks: Yeap. [Andy says something, which cannot be heard due to the sound-proof glass, before pulling on a set of headphones, and picking up the mic, Rab does the same. Andy taps the mic, before speaking into it.] Andy Murray: Hurry up, ya chube. [Pulling on a headset, Phillip flips a few more switches.] Phillip Parks: Standby … Five … Four … Three … Two … One … Go! [Some typical Rap background music begins to play in the background, as Andy and Rab start the NedRap.] Andy Murray:”Comin’ fae the hard knock life o’ Glesga,Ah’m a f*ckin’ Ned livin’ on the Nappers Brae,Sprayin’ on the wa's and kickin’ in yer baws,Gee’s any cheek and I’ll mak ye shit in yer breeks … ” Rab:”Ah f*cked ye maw, I battered yer paw,Hoin’ down for a chippae ‘cos Ah’m needin’ feedin’, Ah’m gonnae hammer you if ye dinna like me, An’ Ah’m gonnae tell The Big Man if ye pish me off … “ Andy & Rab:”Ah’m no gonnae hurt ye, Ah’m no gonnae kill ye,All Ah’m doin’ is pure hammerin you!Ah don’t take no pish, and Ah dinna like fish,NedRap’s pure briwliant, and yer gonnae luv it” Andy Murray:”Batterin’ toffs, hammerin’ chubes,Watchin’ the fitba, bangin’ yer maw,Ah’m a Ned and Ah’m gonnae come right at you,F^ck Aiberdeen an’ Dundee to, Glesga’s number one an Ah’m number two” Rab:”Ah’m no P Diddy an’ Ah’m no Ja Rule,All I am is a Ned fae Glesga,I don’t put up wi’ chubes like you,Ah’ll f^ck ye up and throw ye in a barra’” Andy & Rab:”Ah’m no gonnae hurt ye, Ah’m no gonnae kill ye,All Ah’m doin’ is pure hammerin you!Ah don’t take no pish, and Ah dinna like fish,NedRap’s pure briwliant, and yer gonnae luv it” [Between verses, Andy and Rab start to do a weird NedDance type thing, as the background music continues to play. Finally, when the chorus music begins again, they repeat the chorus, funnily enough.] Andy & Rab:”Ah’m no gonnae hurt ye, Ah’m no gonnae kill ye,All Ah’m doin’ is pure hammerin you!Ah don’t take no pish, and Ah dinna like fish,NedRap’s pure briwliant, and yer gonnae luv it” [With that, the music and the song come to a halt, and Andy and Rab share a high five, before removing the headphones and dropping the microphones. Through the glass, Parks and James applaud everyone’s favourite Neds.] Andy Murray: That was pure briwliant man. Rab: Pure, mad, mental min! Andy Murray: Aye. [Andy and Rab high five again, before walking through back into the switch room.] James McClaren: Well done guys, that was excellent. Andy Murray: Shut yer mouth, bawbag. [Phillip Parks presses a couple more buttons, before removing his headset.] Phillip Parks: That was great guys, now all we have to do, is wait, we’ll be around next Monday … Hopefully we’ll chart highly, right guys? [Andy and Rab stare blankly at Parks.] Rab: Aye. [The scene slowly fades out to black.] 8 Days Later [Fade in to the apartment of Andy Murray & Rab, the lounge area. Andy and Rab occupy both of the new grey chairs, leaving Parks and James to sit on the horrid old brown couch. Andy and Rab are both wearing identical blue & red Kappa shell suits. James and Parks however, are both dressed smartly in black suits, for some reason or another. In the background the radio plays, pretty obviously the Radio 1 Top 40 Chart Show.] Radio: Hello and welcome to the official Radio 1 UK Top 40! I am Mark Goodier counting down the only official United Kingdom singles sales chart in the country! Andy Murray: Hoho min! Rab: Briwliant. James McClaren: Shoosh … It’s starting. Andy Murray: Shut up bawbag. James McClaren: Hmph. [The radio continues to play, counting down the chart from 40 right down to 1.] Radio: And starting the countdown as always at number 40, here is a new entry from Alvin & The Chipmunks! A cover of what was originally a hit for Right Said Fred in the 90’s, this is “I’m Too Sexy”! [The song starts to play on the radio, Andy huffs.] Andy Murray: What pish … Rab: Aye min … [The 4 sit in silence, as the song comes to a halt.] Radio: Number 39 next, another new entry on import, this is The Tweenies with “Oh No! Not Another Kiddie Song!” [Another 2nd rate children-aimed some plays on the radio, again the 4 sit in silence, waiting for the NedRap.] Rab: How long? Phillip Parks: Well, we are anticipating a top 10 entry for NedRap, we could be in for quite a wait … James McClaren: Especially if you wanna be number 1. Phillip Parks: Indeed … Andy Murray: Pish. [Finally, the Tweenies song comes to an end, as we reach #38 on the chart.] Radio: And now, a world first for the Radio 1 Top 40 Chart! A song recording by two professional wrestlers! Andy Murray: Ho min, another wrestler made a tune, pure pish man. Rab: Aye! Hoho! Radio: … This is Andy Murray & Rab McLean, with ‘NedRap’! [The familiar sounds of NedRap play in on the radio, Andy’s jaw drops.] Andy Murray: WHAT! Phillip Parks: Oh dear … James McClaren: Oh … Umm … [James notices a fuming Rab, staring right at him.] James McClaren: RUN! [With that, James and Parks jump out of their chairs, leaping over the back, and quickly run right out of the door, Rab tries to follow, but he is too slow.] Andy Murray: PISH CRAP BOLLOCKS BAWBAG CHUBE! AYYYYYYYYYYYYYYYYYYYE! [With that, our scene slowly fades out, leaving our disappointed neds listening to their chart failure … ]
