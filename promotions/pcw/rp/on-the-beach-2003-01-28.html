On the beach

28 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1/28/2003 *The scene opens up on the beautiful Austrailia coastline beach. Jon Kano, dressed in a white wife beater shirt underneath a Hawaiian shirt and khakis shorts, is walking with Cal Seaver, who is just wearing his trademark K-mart suit with his nose white with sunblock lotion.* Kano: This is going to be too much fun. Think about the possibilities. The Celestial Spear versus the Kanospear. Oh, Christ, it is gonna be sweet. Cal: Jon, do you think you can beat the one and only Neo Void God, Akiyama? *Kano's face gets very serious very quickly.* Kano: Cal, I believe I can beat anyone anywhere. Cal: Even the Neo Void God? Kano: Why not? Cal: He is a god. Kano: And I am a legend. Gods versus Legends. Sounds pretty enticing, doesn't it? Cal: You are beginning to scare me. Kano: Oh, sir Calvin of the Seavers, do not be afraid for I mean no harm. I only mean to explain my feelings entering this match with Akiyama. I don't know who is going to win. He is seven feet tall, so am I. He is three hundred pounds plus, so am I. He is a celebrated wrestler, so am I. He and I have similar wrestling styles. Regardless of anything that is going to happen on Wednesday, we are gonna have one hell of a match. Cal: I think you are crazy. Kano: Everyone is entitled to their own opinion, Cal. I just happen to be very confident in my abilities. Akiyama and me are gonna burn that bitch down and if both of us don't end up in the hospital, it will be a miracle. Cal: Alright, Jon, lets get off the Akiyama subject. You have been hinting around that you might be the new owner of PCW. Kano: Word. I will not lie, I expressed deep interest. I might have even made a bid, but Cal, you will have to just watch PCW television to find out if I did the unthinkable. And that, Cal is WHAT REAL LEGENDS ARE MADE OF. Now, if you will excuse me, I got an old friend to meet. Cal: Who? Kano: LoS. *The scene fades to black as Kano leaves Seaver at the beach.*
