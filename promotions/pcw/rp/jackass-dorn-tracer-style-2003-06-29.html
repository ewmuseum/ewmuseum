Jackass: Dorn Tracer Style!

29 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6 - 29 - 03 ”Get your foot out of my ass, Knoxville!” ”Uh, dude… that’s not my foot.” ”WHAT?!” Suddenly, a wavering screen of static fills the entire viewable area, severely distorting whatever the camera’s looking out. The sound of struggling movements and grunts of impact are the only clues as to what is happening before the camera finally begins to focus in after a sharp rap across the side of the unit. Thick, dark lines of snowy static race from the top of the “screen” and begin to rapidly dance across the wall of snow already there. “Get the fucking thing to work!” A familiar voice rings out, cutting the scratchy sound of static. A series of thumping and smack noises follow, with the whiny sound of another voice piercing through. “Alright already! I think I got… THERE!” The annoying static of a poor camera transmission finally breaks its series and focuses in on the vaguely familiar face of the MTV star known ONLY as Johnny Knoxville! The Jackass host, accompanied by his usual pair of shades and his leather jacket, holds the camera out in front of him and flashes a big smile, but speaks in a low whisper. “Hi! I’m Johnny Knoxville and you’re watching a very special edition of Jackass!” “Co-hosted by yours truly…” At that point, the camera jerks violently and pans to the right quickly, despite Knoxville’s protesting, to reveal ‘The Astronomer’ Dorn Tracer, “… Dorn Tracer! And tonight on Jackass, we’re paying a visit to a personal friend’s house.” The PCW Television Champion then moves the camera to the left and focuses it in on a white house some hundred feet in the distance, surrounded by trimmed groves of pine trees. A giddy chuckle is heard off camera and a smiling Knoxville takes the camera back. “We’ve got some really fucked up shit, too. Show ‘em, Dorn!” The MTV star commands, zooming the camera out and panning it towards the Astronomer. Dorn reaches down towards a cardboard box and flips it open, revealing its contents within. He rummages around through the odds and ends for a moment, narrating as he goes along, “Let’s see here… we’ve got a melon…” He pulls what appears to be a mango melon out and places it on the ground next to the box, “… a thing of fireworks…” Then, he pulls out exactly that, and they seem to be firecrackers, “… a rabid weasel…” Yup, you guessed it, foam and muzzle included, “…a vibrating dildo…” Need I explain? “… and finally…” What could it be? “… SUPER GLUE!” “Right on, man!” Knoxville exclaims behind his quiet fit of laughter, trying not to blow their already obvious position. “What’s the plan, man?” “Well…” Dorn begins, also trying not to laugh, “First off, we’re going to rig his toilet with this melon,” He grabs the melon and holds it up to the camera, “Which will be filled with firecrackers! Then, we’re going to spread super glue on his toilet seat so he can’t get up while the fucking melon explodes all over his shitty cornhole!” “Who’s him, man?” The Astronomer snaps out of his maniacal demeanor and gives the camera, or Knoxville, rather, a confused look before catching up, “OH! Well, you see, we’re at Brianna Washington’s house, who’s my annoying, destructive, and cruel arch enemy! The guy we’re pranking is some turd she knows. A best friend, I’m guessing. It’s going to be great!” “Cool shit! Let’s get started!” Knoxville chimes in, still trying to suppress his growing urge to laugh. Dorn quickly places his pranking tools back in their box, even the feisty weasel, and hoists the box into his arms. With Johnny Knoxville in toe, the Astronomer dashes across the field towards the house, trying to make the least amount of noise possible. The camera bounces and jerks as Knoxville clumsily follows behind, still in a giggling fit. As they finally reach the house, Dorn throws himself up against the wall in a pseudo-secret agent manner and slowly inches his way towards the nearest window as Knoxville crouches down behind a pine tree, slipping the camera through the branches as to see clearly. Tracer cranes his neck forward and turns to look inside the window with a single eye, not wanting to reveal himself too much. He stares inside for a moment before turning his head towards Knoxville with a big grin and nodding. “This is it! Pay dirt!” Dorn cries quietly, if that makes any sense. He peers back into the window and Knoxville zooms the camera in, revealing the room to indeed be the bathroom. “This is fuckin’ crazy… I love it!” The Television Champion nods, having heard Knoxville, and darts in front of the window. He grips the bottom ledge and pulls the window open with minimal effort. Tracer turns around and grins at the camera before turning towards the window and beginning his entrance… right into a storm screen. “What the fuck…” He mouths, a little surprised. “Just break the thing, man!” Knoxville cries out, a little louder than Dorn would prefer judging by the shushing motion the Astronomer gives him. “He won’t hear it… it’s just a screen!” Johnny cries out again, much more hushed this time. Dorn gives him an acknowledging look and begins to push roughly on the mesh screen. Only after a single push, the screen caves in and Dorn quickly climbs into the bathroom. He leans back out the window and retrieves his box o’ shit, completing phase number one. Knoxville comes out from behind the trees and starts towards the house, getting a closer look. He watches with a hushed chuckle as Dorn retrieves the melon from the box and examines it closely. The Jackass host gets right up to the window and zooms in on the melon as he talks, “So what’re you doing now?” “Well,” Dorn begins, his tone a little bland from concentration, “I’m gonna slice this baby open, fill it with firecrackers, and hope he doesn’t see it.” The camera zooms out a little and watches Dorn as he retrieves a knife from his pocket and slices a nice big gash in the melon, but not enough to divide it. “Awesome. So what’s the weasel for?” “If he tries to escape, then we toss it at him and watch the fur fly,” The Astronomer states with a chuckle as he presses firecrackers down into the fruit. After stuffing in about ten or twelve, Dorn carefully places the melon in the toilet and watches as it floats around. The camera focuses in on it for a second before turning back to a grinning Astronomer, who reading the label on the super glue squeezer aloud, “It’s clear, so the motherfucker probably won’t see it. So, let’s get to lubing the seat…” Giving the pack a quick squeeze, Dorn spurts a little glue onto the toilet seat and begins to rub it along the horseshoe with the pack itself, not wanting to get any on his hands. As he works, Knoxville continues to narrate, “So this poor Milo dude is not only getting an ass full of super glue, but exploded melon bits as well. If we get lucky, he’ll get attacked by a… “ Knoxville chuckles, “…rabid weasel!” “Damn straight.” After finishing with the toilet, Dorn quickly picks up the box containing the strangely calm rabid weasel and climbs out of the window, followed by Knoxville. The two dart over to the nearest tree and watch in excitement as, almost on cue, the bathroom door opens and a man steps in, probably Milo. “I hate when Brianna puts beans in the chili…” Milo blandly states with a groan, holding his mid-section. “I have to crap like a motherfucker…” Dorn and Johnny both chuckle, trying to keep their cool as Milo drops his pants and backtracks towards the toilet. “Yes! He’s not going to see the melon!” The Television champion exclaims in glee. However, Knoxville doesn’t exactly join him. “Uh, dude…” “What?” “Did you light the firecrackers?” There’s dead air for a moment and only the sound of crickets chirping and Milo sighing are heard. Suddenly, a sound of rage breaks out, “FUCK!” Dorn stands up, digs in his pocket, and pulls out a lighter. He reaches down into his box and pulls out a firecracker that wasn’t put in a melon. Knoxville starts bursting out laughing as Dorn makes a beeline right for the window, trying to light the firecracker. Milo sits himself down on the toilet and exhales in a satisfactory manner. “Ahhh… hmm, seems kind of mushy…” His peace is broken, however, as Dorn Tracer comes to a grinding halt in front of the window. When Milo sees what he’s doing, he tries to pull himself up off the toilet seat, but discovers himself to be stuck. “HEY! WHAT THE HELL IS GOING ON?!” “Eat shit and die!” Milo tries again, this time exerting enough force to actually stretch his skin to a near-tearing point. When it comes to naught, however, he grabs the towel rack to his right and pulls as hard as he can. Instead of dislodging himself, however, Milo comes right off the toilet with the seat glued to his ass! He falls face first to the floor and Dorn climbs into the bathroom, reaches into the toilet, and pulls the melon out. Cradling it in his arm, he lights the spare firecracker and drops it in the crack as Milo clumsily overcomes the pants around his ankles and gets to his feet. “Catch, dipshit!” Dorn cries, tossing the melon to Milo. The Astronomer quickly climbs out of the window and does a nose-dive to the ground as the fruit explodes in Milo’s face behind him, covering his back in fried melon giblets. Knoxville, in the distance, bursts out laughing. “HOLY FUCK, DUDE! That’s awesome!” He proclaims, seeing the melon mush-covered face of Milo. Dorn climbs to his feet and turns around, wanting to see the results of his labor. He doesn’t have much time to react as Milo bursts towards the window, pretty much tossing himself out of it… or tries, at least. His feet, tied together by his pants, get caught on the window sill and Milo smacks his face on the ground, leaving some melon debris on the grass. Dorn bursts out laughing and turns towards Knoxville, doubling over from Milo’s predicament. However, the THOROUGHLY pissed Milo finally gets to his feet and roars in a furious rage. “I’LL KILL YOU!” He cries, his face covered in melon jizz. “YOU BASTARD!’” Milo then breaks out in a hop, making a jumping beeline right for the Astronomer. Knoxville points and Dorn turns to see Milo coming towards him, which signals phase three. Tracer reaches into the box and pulls out the weasel, who struggles and scratches at Dorn’s coat. The Astronomer then begins to … well… power walk away from Milo, since the poor guy isn’t making very good time. “GET BACK HERE!” “Suck Brianna’s tit, asshole!” Dorn replies with a laughter-filled tone of sinister intention. As Milo tries his damnedest to catch him, Tracer stops and turns towards him with a big grin on his face. “Last, but not least…” Oh yeah, you guessed it. “AHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHH!!!” Knoxville and Tracer both crack the fuck up as Dorn tosses the weasel at Milo, who instantly goes into a panic. The weasel becomes a whirlwind of madness and starts clawing and biting at Milo’s feet, causing the man to trip all over himself. He falls to the ground and covers his head as the weasel goes nuts, biting at his thighs and shit-covered ass. Dorn watches for a couple more seconds before rushing over to Knoxville and looking into the camera with a big grin, “And this… has been Jackass!” ******* Later That Night ******* The front door to the house opens and in steps an exhausted Brianna Washington, tired from a rough day of training. She drops her keys on the living room table and steps into the entertainment room, where she sees Milo slouched on the couch, pressing an ice pack to his head and sitting there with his pants at his ankles. She gasps and rushes over to him in a panic, “What happened?” Milo looks at the TV on the wall and gives her an annoyed stare, “THAT happened to me…” “The TV?” “No, Brianna,” Milo replies, groaning in pain, “Watch what’s in the VCR!” Washington walks over to the VCR and notices the unlabeled tape sticking out. She pushes it in, presses the green Play button, and watches as her big screen TV flares to life. The familiar guitar rift of the Jackass theme plays and the beginning scene with Dorn Tracer and Johnny Knoxville starts. As soon as Brianna sees Tracer, her face goes dark and she frowns, “I should have known.” “How did he find us?” Milo inquires, a bit desperately. “I don-“ Suddenly, a weird, thumping music begins to play distantly. Brianna and Milo exchange looks and head over to the nearest window, where it seems to be the loudest. They both search the outside before their eyes fall upon a very ghastly scene… “Oh my…” Brianna gasps, a look of shock in her eyes. Milo doesn’t comment at all. Dancing to his familiar ‘Party Box’ music is Chris Pontius, another cast member of Jackass, wearing his trademark thong. He does a weird little jig before turning around and shaking his ass at them. That doesn’t disturb either of them as much as what is tattooed on his ass in big black letters: BRIANNA on one cheek and SUCKS! on the other. “That bastard…” Brianna growls furiously as her friend Milo finds himself strangely entranced by this sight, his foot tapping along with the beat. Brianna notices and gives him a funny look before storming off in a huff, probably to plot her next phase of torture for Dorn. [End.] NOTE: Jackass™ and MTV© have absolutely NOTHING to do with Dorn Tracer or any affiliated characters. I just thought this would rock as a roleplay. You be the judge, dude. *Jackass guitar rift plays again…*
