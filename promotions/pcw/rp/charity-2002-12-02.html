Charity?

2 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12-2-02 The two women stand in front of Brandi, with frustrated, yet determined, looks on their faces. One has her hands on her hips, as a parent might do when scolding a small child. The other walks up to Brandi and places one hand on her shoulder. Her face turns to a look of concern and she speaks slowly and softly. Woman- Are you sure that you won’t reconsider? Brandi turns around quickly, causing the woman to jerk her hand from Brandi’s shoulder. Brandi- No, I won’t reconsider Sam! I already told you. I’m not doing anything that would help out this godforsaken community. Sam- But if you would only do a small act of charity, such as a canned food drive… Brandi- And why would I want to do a canned food drive at the opening of my mall? Sam- To help out those that will be without food for the holiday season. They’ll be able to feed their children food that they never imagined. Their stomachs won’t feel the pain of hunger any longer. Brandi- Umm…are you trying to preach to me, or to convince me? Either way, I don’t give a shit. If they’re so damn hungry then tell them to get a job and buy their own food. I can’t believe that someone would actually be low enough to take hand outs from someone to begin with. Sam- Don’t think of them as handouts. Think of them as help until they can help themselves. Brandi- They can help themselves any damn time they want to! The other woman steps forward and chimes in with a cheery voice. Woman- Well, how about a toy drive? Sam turns her attention to the second woman and smiles. Sam- That’s a great idea! What about it Brandi? A toy drive. Customers can bring in a toy and we’ll give it to some poor kid and make their Christmas great. Brandi- Sam, Rebecca, do you have any idea how stupid you sound? Getting so damn happy over the thought of helping some ‘poor’ kid. I don’t want to help any snot nosed brat get something that they don’t even deserve. Rebecca- What’s to say that they don’t deserve it? Brandi- If they deserved it, surely they’d have it by now! Their parents hate them because they’re brats. Every day when they come home from work, they’re half hoping that the kid died in the middle of the night. Sam- That’s horrible! Brandi- Horrible, maybe, but very true. Besides, people are coming here to buy things, not to donate them. If they donate them, how the hell am I supposed to make any money off of them? Sam- Well, there’s a good chance that the item donated will be purchased from a store here. That means that your customers will be buying more, so more money will go into your pocket. Brandi- Well, I didn’t build a mall for the cash, but that sounds good to me. I only bought it to rid this town of that stinking orphanage. The place was so run down and filthy that it was keeping people away! Sam- So…you’ll do it? Brandi- Do what? Rebecca- The toy drive! Brandi laughs and walks towards the railing. She leans against it and watches the people below hurrying around, preparing for opening day. Brandi- Yeah, I guess. You can send the flyers out tomorrow. Sam- Great! You won’t regret it. Brandi- You’d better hope not. Rebecca- So, you’ll be at the opening? Brandi- The opening is on Tuesday. You damn well know that I won’t be at the opening. We’ve already been over this. Rebecca- So some stupid wrestling match is more important to you than the opening of your own mall? Brandi looks angrily at Rebecca and walks towards her. When she is inches from her face, Brandi looks down at her with a smug smile. Brandi- If you ever call a wrestling match involving me, stupid again, you’re fired. Got that? I’m sure as hell not missing a TV Title shot for the opening of this piece of shit. Sam- TV Title? I thought you already had the TV title. Brandi- No…HIT has the TV Title. Well, for now at least. Rebecca- But, didn’t you have the TV Title before? Brandi- No! I’ve had the Cruiserweight and Extreme. Both the women look at each other and shrug. It’s obvious they still don’t understand what Brandi is talking about. Brandi- Just don’t talk to me, ok? You have no fucking idea what you’re talking about, so just don’t mention wrestling. Sam- I can do that. Rebecca- Me too. Brandi- I don’t know who the hell hires these people, but they aren’t very picky! Now look, I have a plane to catch. You two think you can manage to not burn the building down until I get back? The women nod and Brandi manages a smile. Brandi- Good. Brandi starts walking down the stairs and pretends not to hear when Sam calls her name and motions for her to wait a second. She just rubs her temples and sighs. Brandi- Happy fuckin’ holidays.
