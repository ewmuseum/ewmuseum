Blind date...with a vampire?!

3 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6-3-02 Brandi is sitting at a small table with a red plaid tablecloth in a small, yet fancy Italian restaurant. She sets down her menu and digs around in her purse. She produces a small, compact mirror which she checks out her reflection in. Once she decides everything is in order, she places the mirror back in her purse. Brandi picks up her menu and starts looking it over again. She’s dressed up for tonight. She has on a straight black dress, which is strapless. Although she normally doesn’t wear much makeup because she can’t stand the stuff, she has slightly more on tonight. Her hair is pulled back except for two long strands on each side of her head. She spent a lot of time making sure she looked good tonight. Brandi puts down her menu and pulls out her mirror again. She tucks the strands of hair behind her ears before deciding against it and letting them drop again. She turns her head in different angles, making sure she looks alright from every point of view. Then she puts the mirror back in her purse once again. Brandi fidgets with the napkin sitting on the empty plate in front of her. Brandi- I wonder what’s taking him so long. He probably should have been here by now. This is why I hate blind dates. Brandi grows more nervous. She reaches into her purse and pulls out a couple pills. She places them in her mouth and washes them down with some water from a glass sitting on the table. A waiter stops in front of her table. Waiter- Miss, can I get you something? Brandi looks towards the door, then down at her empty plate. Brandi- I guess he’s not coming, so sure why not? The waiter pulls a pen and a tablet of paper out of his apron and gets ready to take Brandi’s order. Waiter- Well, what can I get you? Brandi- I’ll take the spaghetti with meatballs. The waiter scribbles her order down on his paper. Waiter- And to drink? Brandi- Just water. Thanks. The waiter goes back into the kitchen. Brandi takes her napkin off her plate, and places it on her lap. When she looks back up, she’s surprised to see a mysterious looking man standing next to the table. He is dressed in a black suit, with a black tie and has his black hair slicked back. His skin looks rather pale against all his black clothing. Man- You must be Brandi. For some reason Brandi has a hard time taking her eyes off the man’s face. She’s unable to answer, so she just nods her head at the man. The man gently kisses Brandi’s hand, before sitting down in the chair across from her. Man- I am Malvain. I’m sorry I’m late. Some pressing business came up that I just had to attend to. Brandi finally manages to take her eyes off the man. Brandi- Oh, that’s fine. It’s a pleasure to meet you. Malvain- The pleasure is all mine, my dear. Brandi- Oh! I hope you don’t mind. I ordered my dinner already. Malvain- Not at all. I’ll just call the waiter over here. Malvain looks around the room. He spots a waiter in one corner, so he raises a hand at them. Malvain- Waiter! He flashes Brandi a quick smile. Brandi looks startled. The candlelight reflected off of Malvain’s teeth, revealing fangs. She looks away quickly, hoping that he didn’t notice that she had seen. Brandi shakes her head in disbelief. She looks back over at Malvain, but by this time the waiter has come over to take his order. Brandi looks away slowly, trying to avoid catching Malvain’s eyes. Surely he would know she had seen. Or would he? Malvain- Ok, my dear. Our meals should be out shortly. Brandi- Ok. Malvain smiles at Brandi once more. She looks carefully, but she can find no sign of the earlier fangs. She shakes her head and tries to dismiss it as her imagination. Malvain- So, tell me about yourself. Brandi- Well, I’m 20 years old and I grew up in Sacramento. I moved here after I graduated high school. Now I’m a professional wrestler for PCW. Brandi sits back, waiting for the laugh that she is sure will follow. She is surprised to find that Malvain doesn’t laugh, that he looks genuinely interested. Malvain- Well, that must be interesting. Brandi- Yeah, it is. So, what about you? Malvain- I’ve grown up here in Los Angeles, and now I work in the disposal business. Brandi- The disposal business? Malvain- Yes. Well, I help dispose of bodies, if you catch my drift. Brandi looks at the man as if she is trying to figure out what exactly he meant by that. She finally gives up and decides to act like she knows exactly what he’s talking about. Brandi- Yeah, I do. Will you excuse me for a few minutes? I have to run to the restroom. Malvain- I’ll be waiting for you, my dear. Brandi grabs her purse and walks quickly to the back of the restaurant. She pushes open the heavy door to the women’s restroom and goes inside. Brandi- There is something seriously wrong with that guy. He creeps me out. All those black clothes. The slicked back hair. The way he keeps calling me “my dear”. Oh! I swear I saw fangs in his mouth. I don’t know what happened to them though. They just kind of…disappeared. Brandi walks over to the mirror and stands there, staring at her reflection. Her face turns pale as she thinks about it and finally puts two and two together. Brandi- He’s a vampire! I can’t go back out there! What if he tries to drink my blood or something? I’ll bet that’s his pressing business he had to take care of. Someone found out who he really was and he made sure they weren’t around to tell anyone else. That has to be it. That explains why he didn’t tell me very much about his life, too! Vampires are supposed to live forever unless you kill them, aren’t they? Brandi paces back and forth through the bathroom. Her heels click against the tile floor in a weird sort of song. Brandi- I can’t just kill him in the middle of a restaurant. I can’t exactly go back out there and have my meal with him either though. What if he tries to kill me? Maybe I can sneak past him to the door. Brandi opens up the door to the bathroom a crack, but her table is on the way out and it would be impossible to sneak past it unnoticed. She closes the door and leans up against it. There are no windows big enough for her to climb through, so that option is out. Brandi- Ok, I guess the only thing I can do is go back out there and finish dinner. I can handle that. I can’t just stay in here forever. If I no show on Friday, I’ll lose. I’m sure as hell not losing because I went on a date with a vampire. Lindsey is never going to hear this one down! Setting me up with a vampire. Brandi walks back over to the mirror. She tries to calm herself down. When she feels like she’s calm enough, she heads back towards the table. Malvain looks up at her when he hears her footsteps approaching. Malvain- I was beginning to grow worried about you, my dear. Brandi- Oh, sorry. You know us women. We have to make sure we look alright. Malvain laughs quietly and nods. Malvain- Yes, yes I do. He looks like he is about to say something else, when the food arrives. The waiter puts down a plate of spaghetti in front of Brandi, and a plate of noodles with a white sauce in front of Malvain. Malvain- This has no garlic in it, right? Brandi freezes and looks Malvain up and down suspiciously. Waiter- No sir, it doesn’t. Malvain- Good. Thank you. The waiter nods in reply and heads off to tend to a couple near the front of the restaurant. Brandi slowly cuts up her spaghetti and watches Malvain eat. Everything seems normal. No fangs pop out of his mouth. Well, none that she can see at least. Finally Brandi settles down long enough to take a few bites out of her spaghetti. Shortly into the meal, Malvain starts coughing and grabbing at his throat. Brandi jumps up, worried and a waiter rushes over to see if he can help. Malvain picks up his glass of water and quickly pours it down his throat. He holds onto the table for a minute, then he appears fine again. He grabs the waiter by the shirt collar. Malvain- There was garlic in my food! Waiter- I’m…I’m sorry, sir. Malvain- I told you I can’t have garlic! Malvain lets go of the waiter’s shirt. He turns towards Brandi with a look of apology. Malvain- I’m terribly sorry about that. It’s just that I’m allergic to garlic. I’ll be right back and hopefully we’ll be able to finish our dinners peacefully. Malvain wipes his mouth off before disappearing in the direction of the restrooms, leaving Brandi sitting alone at the table. Brandi is very suspicious, and slightly worried about what just happened. Then, something on the corner of the table catches her eye. Malvain’s napkin is streaked with red. Brandi looks over at his plate. White sauce, but a red liquid on his napkin. This is just about enough for her. She turns around and is glad to see no sign that Malvain is returning. Brandi- Goodbye Malvain, and thanks for the meal. Brandi picks up her purse and hurries out of the restaurant. Once she gets outside, she breathes a sigh of relief. Brandi- That’s the last blind date I go on, ever. Now if I can just remember where I parked…
