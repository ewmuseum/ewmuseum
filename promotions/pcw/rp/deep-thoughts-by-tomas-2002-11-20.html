Deep Thoughts by Tomas

20 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11-20-02 RP 1 Alliance Mismatched. The name couldn’t describe the team better. We have some sort of a masked devil and some guy whose name reminds me of winter. We also have a new guy. He calls himself a romantic. He claims to have his way with the women. Then you have myself, THE American Woman, THE Top Rope Temptress, and formerly known as THE Tease. Diablo, I’ve seen around backstage being his usual crazy self. I keep my distance more so with him than anyone else. Lord help me if he ever puts my ass on fire. I often wondered what his response would be if I asked him if he ever danced with the devil under a pale moonlight. Heh, I’ve never claimed to be very sane myself. Snow. What’s his first name? I’ve bumped into him a time or two. Seems to be a nice guy, though I hardly see him. I wonder if he’ll show or not. El Romantico. Ah, a rookie to the fed. So far, I’ve watched him swoon the valets and fellow roster member’s wives or girlfriends. He’s yet to make a pass at me, not that I blame him. If I was a male and saw a female kick ass like I do, I’d keep my distance too. Poor guy isn’t man enough yet to handle a real woman I don’t guess. With these thoughts in mind, Alliance for our team will be one of two things, well maybe a few. One, we could be a great team and work together and win with all that cheesy sugar coated good stuff. Two, we could do alright, not that bad, but not that good. Or three, worse can come to worse and we’d become on big cluster of a team and end up fighting each other including our opponents. Why so much negativity? It’s simple. Kannon. Yes my very own lovable boss. He started it, then came that gothic cowboy Duke who is going through a major identity crises. I can’t help but feel sorry for the guy sometimes. Heh, perhaps all he needs is some good southern comfort. He ain’t getting it from me. I had that match won against Walden, but HE had to start the interference then came everyone else. When shit flies, it does stink. As for Brandi, well, I’ll tell you this. One day I’ll have her job and the world will see whom the woman of PCW is. THE American woman always comes out on top. Tag Tam Championship. I had it all planned. Nothing would look better than the Cruiserweight Championship belt on one shoulder and a Tag Team Belt on the other. One shot is blown in the water, now it’s up to HIT and myself to grab the other. Granted, we do make a good team considering me and team mates doesn’t mix well, but if he screws our shot at the duo gold, then I’ll kick his ass along with anyone else’s in my way. No worries, I have no doubts that the mathematician will let me down. By my fans, I’m labeled as a good guy, a face. On my records, I’m listed as a neutral. Face is good if you like the popularity and little kids looking up to you. Heel is good when you get so fed up with all the candy coated goodness to go along with your milk and vitamins. A heel can get away with anything cause they are expected to be the bad guy. Behind every good face is a good heel pushing the angles. Personally, I like neutrals. They are unpredictable. They can sit on the fence and lean either way. Just when you think they’re running with the good guys, they’ll stab someone’s back and flee with the heels. Confusing group. My advice is not to attempt to understand them. Neutrals have one main thing in common with heels. They only look out for number one. Yes, I’ll wear the name face but deep down I know I’m neutral cause sometimes the best view is when you can see clearly both sides of the fence.
