Hitting the Radio!

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: not entered A time in the life of Rich Sherwin...A time in the life of Ryan Moore...Times in the life of the two members of the 'Pretty Cool Guys', both pieces reflecting both the musical sense and the effect that the 'Heartbreakers' song is having on the public as a whole. To steal the thought from the majority of people, their views were still the same with regards to the outcome of the match. Everyone was expecting the 'Pink' attack to come through and take their positions as the new 'PCW Kings of PoP' Music, and despite this contest having all the makings of one for the Year 2002, the leverage toward one team winning this thing was far beyond anything else that alot of Wrestling and Music fans alike could believe. We've spent time in both the cosy confinements of a 'Karaoke Bar' and a 'Record Store', each segment ending with the boys leaving their chosen complex and wondering off (or in Sherwins case, running and driving) into the distance. What kind of interesting musical adventure will we see the boys travel upon today? Would this theme of music continue all the way until the event itself?, or will we see the antics that made Sherwin and Moore such a joke replayed once more? Not even I'm sure what the collective intend to do today... but hey, maybe something good will come of all of this? All I do know... is that you're going to have to watch it... to know it! The scene slowly begins to open up in what would appear to be the corridor of somewhere rather plush. For this segment of the characters lives, we don't have the opportunity to see the immediate surroundings, since everything that we're able to look upon at the moment is both Rich and Ryan sitting in separate chairs, a window between the two chairs and a nice little flower painting positioned at head-height behind where Rich is seated. The first sound that comes to mind is the music that is softly playing over the tannoy of the buildings PA System. Instantly, it beings back memories of all that music from the early seventies.... maybe even the little song that plays in that elevator on Max Payne. Regardless of this little history lesson, the music played over the system for another few moments before fading out into nothingness. Following that track was the sound of a DJ or somebody.. it sounded like the host of a radio show. Could this be the fateful day? 'Good Morning Miami!... and welcome to yet another edition of your favorite swingin' seventies 'Breakfast Show'. As all of you should be aware by now, I am the 'Word' of this here joint... I am the very man who puts every line together, I'm Ronnie Batt and we're be with you... right after THESE important messages...' 'We should be getting the Word to be going onto this show in a few minutes Ryan... I say should but I'm not sure if everything was finalized for our appearence and everything.', Rich said, worried that he and his best-friend may be asked to remove themselves from the building if their names were missing from the 'Show Guest List'. Ryan looked to Rich but remained wearing a calm look. From the way his was seated, Ryan wasn't too fussed about appearing on this show or not... his mind was more focused on the Tag Team match in a mere thirty-six hours or so time. 'Do you have problems getting it up at night? Do you ever feel too tired to give your wife that special time? Hell, maybe the chick is so damn ugly you just can't get turned on? In any case, one dosage of 'The Little Members Jolly Pills' will ensure a prominent... long lasting errection, that'll even get you through the uglies and fatties... call 0-500-200-LIMP for more details or visit your local chemist...' 'Oh My God... can you believe the nature of that advert Rich? Its like, what kinda people are really going to be listenning to the radio station at this time of the day?', Moore stated, now looking a little worried about the nature of the show following the advertisement for pills that was just aired. The visual shifted away from the PoP boy duo for a few moments and rested casually on what appeared to be the secretary of the Radio Room. She looked to be in her early twenties and could easily be accused of having this job for the 'Summer Only' or what-not. 'What kind of listenners you ask? Well, the majority of people who even bother to tune into this show are usually within the twenty-eight to forty-five age bracket.', she informed both members of the 'Pretty Cool Guys'. It was information that could only really be greated by an appreciative nod... not nesscesserly one that meant that the information was even wanted, but one to signal that while what she did was good, it wasn't really the answer to the question they were looking for... especially since the question was more on the lines of rhetorical... all question, no real answer. The visual moved back over to the duo of Sherwin and Moore, whom at this second in time, had switched places for a reason unknown to man. They looked over to the girl seated behind the desk and sniggered to each other, actually finding their movement to be laughable... let alone interesting. 'I really wish we'd be able to get onto the show and deal with all of the match stuff soon... I'm beginning to feel a little uncomfortable about going behind the mic,' Rich admitted, looking nervously up at the tannoy that was still projecting various different parts of the 'Ronnie Batt Breakie Show'. Ryan placed a manly arm around his buddies shoulder and made him look over to the corner of the room. Making himself look like more of the agent figure, the guy with the ideas and the future path, Moore began to serenade Sherwin with a list of achievements that could go their way once this interview has been taken care of... regardless of how long they have to wait before getting access to the studio. 'Rich... lets imagine we've been on the show... and so far, the whole world has had a change of heart about us... they'll loving the music that we're putting on the shelves and better still, they're loving both you and I for having such perfectly defined abs of steel.', Ryan claimed, placing his hands in the air, just infront of him anyway, placing for the mental visual of the two becoming the most 'Loved Artists in the history of Music'. This for fact, is too far away to believe that anything could happen... especially since the majority of the adult world hate 'PCG' anyway. However, in his current state, Sherwin was in the mood to believe anything that would spell out a rise in the careers for this Wrestling Boy Band. Ryan could see the twinkle in Sherwins eye as the ideas began to settle into his mind... and without even thinking what his next move should be, Ryan just continued to play along the lines of superstar, '... you know what I mean?', he asked, giving Sherwin a few moments to take in the line and nod his head accordingly, '... this show could really be the beginning of the Ultimate for us... the Ultimate run that'll see us move out into the sunset... Platinum Records in tow... and soon after that, the 'Primetime Championship Wrestling' Tag Team Championship belts... hanging gleefully upon our shoulders.. or around the waist, your decision on how the belts are worn.' 'Do you... really think... that the appearence on this show... and the win against 'The Heartbreakers' this coming Friday... will surely allow the ball to begin rolling for us? Do you really think... that from that point forth, we will continue a meteoric rise to Superstar... eventually, shadowing over those... foolish children who like to be the cause of 'Civil Disobedience'?', Sherwin asked, his voice no longer bearing the nervous tone that had been featuring before... but instead, a tone that could capture the possible glam, glitter and all things nice that he was told that could be coming his way just weeks after the show. 'Good Morning Listenners and welcome back to the 'Ronnie Batt Breakfast Show'... that last advertisement that played was all about something very close to the hearts of men across the country. I'd just like to hollar out to all of the guys that already have to get rough with themselves in those fat chick situations... well, just to remind you... 'The Little Members Jolly Pills' is the name of the product... and you SHOULD be able to pick up a packet, no more than four... from your local pharmacy.' 'Sheesh... are we really going to have to wait much longer before getting onto the show?', Rich exclaimed with an obvious sense of irritation in his voice. Ryan looked away from Sherwin as he felt something of a rant coming on... or, possibly even one of those regular little temper tantrums that he seems to throw when Rich feel's that he's been waiting for too long or something. The girl behind the desk was quick to give Rich her fullest of attention, sensing the possibility that she may be able to get a better paid job as a 'Personal Assistant' of Sherwin himself. 'I don't think it's going to be too long until you go into the Radio room boys.', she responded, her voice filled with a caring aura instead of a harshful one. She looked over to Sherwin in something of an adoring state, a look that Sherwin brushed off with ease, nudging Ryan and making him look over to the hapless chick. Both members of the Boy Band begin to laugh out loudly in their chairs... but kill all sounds when they hear the now familiar voice of the show DJ. 'Yo everybody... we're still on the airwaves, sending out those shockwaves to everyone around the world! Right now we're about to reward the 2% of our regular listenning audience with another two full minutes of commericals... following through with a couple of special guests afterwards.. stay tuned.' Sherwin and Moore removed their view from the tannoy in the room before turning their complete attention to the girl seated at the desk... who in turn, had her complete focus placed on a little red light that had been positioned above the door. This was all due to fact that for the time being, Ronnie Batt was still on the air. A few moments passed and silence was the only thing standing tall in the room. Everyone around was caught by the sight of the red light... and then... it faded out into nothing, like a light switching off at will.The kind silence that had existed until a few moments ago was broken by the sound of a loud buzzer that originated at the desk of the girl. She looked down at a panel and pressed a button before leaning down toward the 'speaker' and answering the 'Buzz', 'Hello... this is the office of the 'Ronnie Batt Breakie Show', how may I help you?' 'Yeah, Michelle... you can send that.... Tag Team from PC... Dubya? into the studio now... I gotta get this interview out of the way before shit happens.', the voice replies with the young girl looking back up to Sherwin and Moore before pointing to the door leading to the studio. The scene around the two stars fades out deep into black as their get up from their seats to face the interview... that should in the idealistic nature of Ryan Moore, get the ball rolling again on a PCW Career that looks like its coming to something of a slow down! Will both Rich and Ryan be able to handle the kinds of questions that they may be asked? Will they be able to then get to their contest with the Heartbreakers in a safe mental state should this interview be nothing more than the harshest grilling they'd ever recieved? The answers to all of these questions and a whole lot more... possibly twenty-five percent extra free!... NEXT TIME... same location, same Radio Station... SAME... GOD DAMN... Day on THEIR Calender!
