Cheap Korean Toys

28 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Title: Cheap Korean Toys Featuring: Xavier Kannon Date: 28/12/02 “Okay, time to cash in on the stupid and vulnerable.” The shot opens up in one of the Executive Suites at PCW Head Offices in Atlantic City where we see the 1st couple of PCW, Xavier and Eleanor Kannon-Hall sitting across a large oval table from a team of Marketing Executives all decked out in their Grey suits, a huge pile of prospective merchandise on the desk beside them. At the CEO’s words, they all turn and look at Ellie~! Eleanor : “Um, like, stop looking at me and stuff!” Her eyebrows arch as Xavier quickly looks to her then back at the Executives, snarling. Seeing the look on his face they snap back into work mode. Executive One : “Well, we have the latest wave of PCW Action Figures ready to hit the market in the New Year... Most are the same as what we put out for Christmas, but they always get bought anyway.” Reaching down into the selection, he pulls out one of the figures at random... Not a good choice. As soon as the 5 inches of plastic hits the table, Xavier’s Green eyes lock right onto it with disdain. Executive One : “We have added a few new features though...” The figure is a miniature version of PCW’s very own President, Duke Williams. It comes complete with the appropriate paunch over his jeans, a plastic Stetson and a bottle of JD that can be gripped in the toy’s hand. Executive One : “... We have a removable Cowboy Hat, and it comes with a Cigar and a Bottle that can be held...” Kannon snarls as he looks at the figure, his mind fighting a war between his love of profits and his hatred for Duke... The hatred winning as he looks right at the figure’s smiling face, as if it is taunting him. Xavier : “I don't want.... THAT... On the market.” The trio from marketing look to one another with confused expressions as Ellie~! sneers at the Duke likeness. Eleanor : “On..... The.... Market!” They all look to their research figures and spreadsheets. Executive Two : “Mr. Kannon, this one is expected to be the highest selling of the whole line!” Whipping his right hand across the table, the CEO swats the lump of plastic away and it goes flying off onto the floor where it lands with a crack. The Stetson is knocked off and the JD bottle somersaults into the air. Xavier : “I believe my words went something along the lines of... I don't want that on the market, am I not correct?” All three mumble in agreement. Xavier : “It’s not like it’s quality merchandise, we probably have them made by 3 cents a day Peons somewhere in Korea for a quarter each...” As he speaks, the cracked figure on the floor suddenly starts to smoke... The plastic toasting to a Black colour before it bursts into flame. Fumes start to emanate from the burning and melting figure as a fly flies through the cloud, dropping dead the other side after breathing in the toxic fumes. Eleanor : “Um, is it, like, meant to do that and stuff?” After a few more seconds, the toy is nothing but a charcoal coloured pool of molten plastic that singes the carpet around it, the cloud of probably deadly fumes lingering. Xavier : “I assume you’ve undertaken the standard procedure to get by safety testing?” Executive Three : “All taken care off, we listed them all as Pest Control items... Put them in a Rat hole, light a match near them and they’ll leave every rodent stone cold dead.” Kannon smiles with no remorse on his face. Xavier : “Good... Just remember to scrap the Duke line... Who else do you have?” Excitedly, the Executive ducks under the table and brings a large box up with him. Eleanor : “We’re, like, selling boxes now? Can’t you at least make them WAY Pink and Silver and sparkly and stuff?” Wisely ignoring the little prep, the item is un-boxed to show an Abner figure, a Teresa Tomas figure and a plastic Monster Truck... All three of which don’t appear to be to Kannon’s liking. Xavier : “This is a joke, right?” Ellie~! looks at the Teresa Tomas figure and sneers. Eleanor: “A....... Joke...... Right?” Not quite sure what to say, one of the trio checks their figures and goes to pass the clipboard to Kannon after circling and highlighting some of the info. Executive One : “These are what the fans want... They love Duke and the Rednecks, and we’re expecting their monster truck playset to be our hig-” Growling, Xavier rips the clipboard from the man’s hand and throws it like a Frisbee against the wall, a chip being made in the magnolia walls where the edge of the metal clip impacts. All three shrink back into their seats, worried. Xavier : “I don’t CARE what the PCW fans want!” Seeing the three Executives squirm in their seats, Ellie~! leans forward in her seat and joins in the berating, finally finding something about a PCW meeting she enjoys. Eleanor : “Like... SO don’t care and stuff!” Kannon grabs the Abner figure and pulls the left arm back, snapping it off before throwing it over towards the trash can which explodes in a storm of paper as the cheap and dubiously safety-tested toy lands in it, the left arm seeming being the pin for the Grenade that is a cheap Korean toy. Xavier : “I don't care if a thousand Redneck parents are going to be buying the junk with the rest of tee country’s taxes.” Ellie~! grabs the Tomas figures and gives it the Prep Slap O’Doom~! Eleanor : “Like, I WAY couldn’t care like SO anymore less and stuff!” The little prep then sees the figure start to give off dark fumes where she hit it. Eleanor : “Eeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeek!” As Kannon grabs the Monster Truck off the desk, Ellie~! panics and throws the toy over her shoulder, blowing the Executive Suite’s door out into the corridor with the explosion. Xavier : “As for this?” Putting the Truck on the floor beneath him, Kannon stamps on it with all his might, cracking it into tiny pieces to make his point before the shards of plastic dissolve and melt through the floor below. Eleanor : “As..... For.... That!” Dusting his hands off, and moving his feet away from the acid burning through the floors of the building, Xavier looks back up to the Marketing trio. Xavier : “Now.... What else have you got me?” Looking between themselves, the middle one nervously speaks. Executive Two : “The... Um... Er Ky-.... Kya Brady blo-... Blow-Up Doll...” Xavier : “JUST GET OUT!!!” Snapping at them, the 3 quickly scoop up their papers and products. Eleanor : “Just..... Get..... Out!” Hurriedly the trio scarper for the door, one running through the fumes from the Duke figure and dropping down to the carpet, clutching his throat leaving the remaining 2 to drag his choking body from the room as the shot fades out to Black. ---- View Xavier Kannon's Biography
