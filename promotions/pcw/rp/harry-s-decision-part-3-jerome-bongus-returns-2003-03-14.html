Harry's Decision - Part 3 - Jerome Bongus Returns

14 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: March 14, 2003 [Hotel California, located in downtown Los Angeles, has been the place for some of the most bodacious parties in all of the United States. Hollywood stars mostly stop by after hard months of filming to let some of that steam off. They party until they puke, and then they party some more. Owner, Michael Sampson, encourages the parties and the magnet that draws the Hollywood stars to his hotel. Of all people, Michael owes large thanks to 'Totally Tubular' Tommy for throwing the first bodacious bash at the hotel. See, without Tommy's bash the hotel would have folded and gone out of business. Michael would have went back home to Wisconsin to become a farmer just like his father, but, obviously, that did not happen. Tommy shed light onto the hotel just when darkness was about to engulf it whole, and it's something Michael is very grateful until this day. In fact, to show how grateful he is, he gave Tommy an entire FLOOR to himself whenever he wanted to throw some gnarly parties or just hang out for a short time. Ever since the first one Tommy has always had his best and largest parties there. They are legendary] [The ninth floor is where all of the magic happens. It's the floor that Mike reserved for Tommy's use and that nobody else was to EVER use the floor. It has been renevated into one GIGANTIC room with a swimming pool, hot tub, pool table, bar, and a tiny wing with a line of ten bedrooms. The cost for the construction was just brutal but Tommy used his surfing cash to help Mike out with any extra costs and it's done nothing but pay off big time for both gentlemen. As 'Heavy Metal' Harry walks into the massive place he is actually impressed with it. Tommy's idea to come here and spark old memories seemed stupid but to see what he has done to the place is just amazing in itself. Harry begins to ponder about how when Tommy sets his mind to something that he CAN accomplish it without much of a problem. It's his natrual inner strength and will to keep pushing. Even in his 'super villain' state he's still pushing himself to be better than the rest even though he never seems like he is. Always talking about 'bodacious babes' and 'making gnarly green' just seems to be a way for him to escape his reality. It's the only way he'll stop himself from seeing the ugly truth that he's become something that he's disliked his entire life...a bogus dude] 'Heavy Metal' Harry: All right, I'm here and I'm really not feeling anything. Granted this room is VERY FAMILIAR...I'm just not getting any memories popping into my head. [As Harry walks by the love seats and heart shaped mini-beds Tommy stands at the doorway looking over his most bodacious creation. He smiles and his pearly white teeth sparkle underneath the lights above] 'Totally Tubular' Tommy: Dude, I am SO surprised that you haven't had any thoughts of this place. We spent a lot of radical times here...and you even LOST YOUR VIRGINITY HERE!! High or not I don't think a compadre can forget about losing his virginity. [Harry just looks around the room and shakes his head] Harry: I honestly don't remember that, Tommy. For some reason I get this odd sense that I almost got raped by a fat woman... [Tommy hides his smile and tries his best to hold back the laughter. Tommy remembers the night very vividly as he tricked Harry into one of the bedrooms that contained a very fat woman, who later that night almost got a hold of Tommy...that's when his laughter sort of dies out. Harry stands beside the pool looking down at the lights coming up from under the water. The shadows cast onto the ceiling making Harry look as if he's fifty feet tall. Tommy suddenly appears behind him and puts his hand on his shoulder] Tommy: Dude, you know that you don't REALLY have to leave. You can always start over here in California, and still live in the beach house. YOUR GONNA MISS OUT ON THE BODACIOUS BABES ON THE BEACH THIS SUMMER!! [Harry just giggles to himself as he DOES remember that part very well. All of the beautiful women that flocked from the beach up to their house just to hang out and have a good time. He also remembers how most of the women either went past him or just used him to get closer to Tommy. It may not have been flattering, but a man could have a good time just being used and abused by very beautiful women] Harry: Tommy, I just need a totally different surrounding. I know it would be very cool to hang around with you some more, have some more great times, but as human beings we just need to evolve. Whether you know it or not, California is not exactly my kind of place. I like things to be different...a little variety. New York City sounds like that place for diversity. Tommy: Dude, there is PLENTY of diversity here! Did you SEE the black dude working at the front desk?! He was like DARK BLACK...not that bogus kind of brown where your all like, 'Dude, I think he's mixed or something.' NO! He is pure, one hundred percent, bodacious African American. [Harry just looks at his brother with pure confusion, and can do nothing but smirk at his statement. Harry wonders how he really does get by sometimes. It could be just pure luck, but maybe Tommy is just leading people to believe something that he is not...who knows] Harry: I'm not talking about THAT kind of diversity. I'm talking about personality diversity and people that would be more on my wave length. Around here it's all a bunch of guys wanting to surf, get drunk, and then have sex with the hottest girl in the room. To me, that just gets REALLY old. Tommy: Well, we could go BOWLING or something sometime. I'm pretty rad at rolling those strikes and picking up those spares! There is cosmic bowling...that is some gnarly diversity right there with all the stuff glowing! Harry: HAHA! Bowling won't make it go away, bro. I know that it's going to be hard for you to adjust to my not being here but your just going to have to find a way to cope with it. Brothers and sisters always split apart from each other and go into different directions. It's not like we aren't EVER going to see each other ever again...it's just that we won't be together ALL THE TIME! [Tommy looks down at the pool water now and lets out a strong sigh. Harry just looks at his brother's tanned face and notices the depressed look that has come over it. He can't LIE to his brother and tell him that it's all going to be peachy keen that he's leaving. He has to give Tommy the benefit of the doubt and just be as truthful as possible. Give him the worst case scenerio right off the bat so that he's prepared for those horrible feelings. As Harry goes to sit down on one of the couches something jabs him in the butt. He leaps to the side quickly wondering exactly what it was, and as he looks down the memories fly through him like a jet plane getting to its objective] Harry: Why is there a football helmet on this couch?! [Tommy turns and looks at the Pittsburgh Steeler football helmet that sits on the couch, hooked up to a backpack system and a large hose leading toward the mouthpiece. Tommy just smiles with his arms folded, proud of the creation that his brother, who now has no idea what it is, created] Tommy: Now whether your memory is all messed up or not, I think you should remember this totally bodacious puppy! [Tommy picks it up and holds it out to Harry] Tommy: Put it on...see how it feels. [Harry pauses for a few moments giving his brother a very suspicious look. Harry stares at the helmet for a couple seconds trying to place what it is that it should mean to him. He slowly reaches for it and slings it onto his back as he leans backward not really expecting the weight to pull him back like that] Tommy: Now put the helmet on... [Harry does as he's told, feeling like he's suiting up for the big game. Going out there for the thousands of screaming fans and ready to run for four touchdowns in a single game. As he walks around the room a little bit he begins getting these visions of him with the mouthpiece in, sucking on something. He feels his lungs beginning to contract, his nerves racing with a rapid pulse, and his feet tapping like he's Sammy Davis Junior] Tommy: Now just stand still, I'm going to load up some...ummm...like...you know. I'm gonna put some bodacious materials in the little bowl thingie. Just stand back and watch the master... [Tommy covers up the bowl with his free hand as he begins to stuff something in there. Harry is too busy trying to figure out what the contraption is to notice what is REALLY going on. Tommy keeps looking up quickly to make sure Harry isn't objecting. What is it? Why are his nerves on fire now that he has this thing on? It doesn't make any sense!] Tommy: I am totally going to cure you, dude! [Tommy pulls out a green Bic lighter and slowly begins to go toward the bowl with it and that's when everything clicks into Harry's mind] Harry: DON'T YOU DARE!!! [Tommy stops right as he's about to put the flame onto the bowl. He looks up at his enrage brother who sort of resembles one of those bodacious looking bobble heads] Harry: Your trying to get me high again, aren't you? [Tommy looks like a child caught with his hand in the cookie jar] Tommy: High? You? Me? DUDE! Harry: Yes you are! This is a bong! This is JEROME BONGUS! I remember it now. I created this thing out of sheer BOREDOM! It broke, I buried it and then you gave me a new one! This is that new one! Tommy: Yeah, all of that is right except the...you know...bong part. I'm just trying to, you know, like...DUDE! STOP BEING BOGUS AND LET ME LIGHT IT!!! [Harry jumps back and runs around one of the couches as Tommy follows him closely, running with the flame in his hand. Harry dives over couches as if he has inherited the skills of one Jerome Bettis, except he's white and much more pudgy] Tommy: I am TOTALLY faster than you, dude! You should just give up now. Harry: Never...*pant*... [Tommy tackles him as if he is now the EVIL linebacker of the Cleveland Browns. He holds the mouthpiece to Harry's mouth and begins trying to light the lighter again, except it's not catching the flame] Tommy: HEINOUS! It's out of fluid! Harry: Serves you right...*pant*...you motherfu-- [The flame lights] Tommy: HAHA!! Harry: Oh shit. Tommy: This is for your own good, compadre. Harry: Don't do it! [As Tommy places the lighter onto the bowl, lighting the weed, Harry uses his quit wits and yanks the helmet off of his head. The smoke rises through the tube leading to the mouthpiece as Harry slithers his plump body toward the patio outside. He dangles the helmet for out so Tommy has no chance of reaching it] Tommy: Harry! You don't know what your doing! Your a most BOGUS prisoner if you don't toke on this gnarly hit. Your only cheating yourself of all the radical times we have ahead of us! Harry: I don't need weed to do that.... [Harry then drops the helmet off the balcony and it plummets to the ground outside, smashing onto the sidewalk into dozens of pieces. Tommy looks down over the balcony at his only REAL shot at getting his 'other' brother back. Not that this one is a heinous compadre, but the 'other' Harry was a blast to be with and knew how to party hard] Tommy: Bogus... [Harry stands at the patio door, still panting from the hard running he did earlier] Harry: Tommy...I know it seemed mean but I don't need that stuff anymore. That person that you knew before TRULY wasn't me. It was just a guy completely stoned out of his mind the entire time, do you catch my drift? [Tommy just stares over the ledge with sadness filling his blue eyes. He watches the by standers below looking at the broken pieces of Jerome Bongus, wondering who and what would drop such a thing. Who would waste all of that bodacious weed? Who is just THAT STUPID?!] Harry: Tommy...you've got a few days before March Madness. There are still some things that you need to do and there are things that I still need to do. We're just wasting time sitting around like this...time is wasting. Tommy: Who cares. Harry: I CARE, TOMMY! I want to see you go to March Madness and DEFEND that Continental Title and go on for a much longer reign. Then I want to see you go through all of those 'heinous compadres' and win the World Championship! I want you to make your father proud as you hold both titles over your head...showing him that you were able to keep the family business going and going STRONG! You know you can do it, Tommy, and I want to see you do it. You just don't need that other Harry to be there. It's just you... Tommy: We did things as a team, dude...you'll never understand.... [Tommy gets up from the balcony and walks out of the room. Harry just stares at the door not really understanding the bond that he had with his brother. Obviously it must have run a lot deeper than he suspected from the beginning] Harry: Well, maybe mom can put this into perspective... [Fade to black]
