Revenge Saga Pt 3: Dilolated Pupils...

29 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 29/5/02 Revenge Part 3: More Extreme Challenges… Crichton stood on the corner of the street opposite the place of his next target. After the biker fiasco, the previous night or previous morning, whichever way you look at it, he decided to pick a place in which he knew he would get a decent fight. This time he had picked a place that could even give him a run for his money. Dressed in his usual metallic grey t-shirt and black pants, covered by his knee-long leather coat, he finds it difficult to retrieve the packet of Malbro cigarettes from the inside of his jacket pocket. After finally retrieving the cigarettes, he tenderly places one in his mouth, then lights it. He takes a drag before placing both his lighter and the cigarettes back into his jacket pocket. It was necessary to eye the place up properly before making his grand entrance, simply to make sure that somebody was there when he did it. He had already been standing in his current position for about half an hour and he hadn’t seen anyone come or go. He was fairly sure that some old ladies walking down the pavement, across the street, thought him staring at the building, looking for any kind of class was weird, but he had given up caring about what other people thought about him. According to the opening times glued upon the door, the class should have been in session fifteen minutes ago and so far Crichton had seen didly-squat. He idly, discarded some of the ash at the tip of cigarette onto the concrete floor, as a man walks down the street in the direction of the building, Crichton is currently staking out. Crichton tenses more and more as the man gets ever closer to the door of the establishment. As the man walks past the building entirely, Crichton’s heart sinks and he angrily throws his cigarette to the floor. Again without taking into account his current, transformation he angrily punches the wall, with outside of his hand. A section of the wall caves in upon impact and his hand is digested into the building. Panicking, he quickly retrieves his hand and takes a quick look around. Great. Nobodies looking. he thought as he began to walk casually away in the direction of the dinner he spotted down the road. He wasn’t going to wait for this asshole to open the building, he decided to come back later when he was sure somebody was going to be there. Then he would strike. ************ Two hours later, Crichton once again stood in the same position as he did earlier in the day, on the corner across the street from his target area. This time, he could see that the building was fully occupied, through the window. The class was indeed in session. Now was the time to act, he had developed a very effective entrance and the means to do it. Casually, he walks towards the building, trying not to make eye contact with anyone inside the class, whilst also trying to keep from looking suspicious, which in his opinion wasn’t the easiest thing in the world to accomplish. He successfully makes it to the alleyway that lies adjacent to the building itself, and then locates the fire escape ladders, which lead up onto the roof of the building. The start of the fire escape stairs to Crichton’s estimation, are at least ten feet into the air. Crichton makes short work of this distance however, as he jumps up into the air and grabs onto the first rail of the fire escape. Once the grip is firmly placed, he easily pulls himself up and before long he is stood on the lowest platform of the fire escape. A couple of minutes after this he has successfully scaled the entire fire escape and steps on the roof. He takes a quick look around, then makes his way to the centre of the roof, where the glass ceiling lies. He kneels down next to the glass ceiling and places his hands upon the smooth, transparent, surface testing the density of the glass and checking to see if it would, indeed lead him to where he wanted to go. Excellent. The teacher was stood at the front of the class, instructing all of his pupils on what to do and when to do it. At last he would be reasonably challenged. He hoped. Crichton stood, from his crouching position and fastened up the top few buttons on his knee-long leather jacket. He then ran up to the glass target and hurled himself into the air, about a foot and half before it. The impact of both Crichton’s leg muscles and his weight, proved to much for the transparent, roof covering to repel and the glass shards followed Crichton down, fifteen feet to the pine panelled floor plating. Crichton lands comfortably, one knee resting on the floor. He only stood as the last of the glass fragments were done falling. Crichton slowly rises with a wide grin etched upon his face. The class teacher and all is pupils in ‘Griffin’s Karate’ edge to one corner of the room, in apparent terror at Crichton’s presence. In truth Crichton was a little disappointed at the sight that lay before him. Two of the pupils were under 16, the rest not reaching further than 18 and ‘Griffin’ himself was only 5’9”. He wasn’t going to turn back now that was for sure, even though he adamantly believed that his victims would not put up much of a fight at all. Crichton let out a little giggle, as the 5’9” tall teacher of the class, wearing the traditional Karate pyjama’s steps forward. “Hell of an entrance! What do you want? And how are you going to pay for that very expensive glass ceiling, you just smashed?” The Teacher questions squaring right up to Crichton. Crichton lowers his head a little and a grin appears once more. “Feisty, eh? That could be useful. I might get more of a fight that I expected!” Crichton declares with genuine surprise in his voice. The karate teacher nods in understanding. “Ah! So you’re just a lowly troublemaker eh? Well if you want a fight, you’ve got one buddy!” The Karate Teacher declares, striding forward confidently. Crichton extends both arms out in front of him and makes synchronous beckoning movements with his hands. “Bring it on!” He simply states, moving his limbs into a comfortable fighting stance. The karate teacher lunges forward blurring the air with lightning fast punches and kicks, connecting with nothing but air, a demonstration in an attempt to warn Crichton off. Within a couple of seconds the teacher stops with a final kick and makes a stupid sounding noise. Crichton simply extends his right arm and jabs the Karate teacher across the jaw. The karate teacher is lifted up from the air and thrown three meters back by the impact of John Crichton’s blow. The pupils stand back in amazement, as Crichton slaps his hands together and begins to rub them a little. “Right, who’s next?” He declares. The remaining five pupils all stride forward and begin to attack Crichton all at once. Crichton’s heightened senses allow him to either block or avoid the initial barrage of blows. “I admire your enthusiasm! But I grow tired of this minor confrontation!” Crichton declares grandly. The pupils do not heed Crichton’s words, and continue to fight him, impressing him more than a little. All five of the young warriors blows, force Crichton on the permanent defensive, not allowing him to make one single strike. He had to admire they’re tactics. The pace of attack Crichton however, was extremely fast and the pupils couldn’t keep it up forever, it was simply impossible. Inevitably one of the pupils made a mistake and stumbled a little, allowing Crichton one strike. Crichton’s arm extends and pushes one of the pupils to the far left away from him. The pupil slides across the floor and hits the wall hard, but remains conscious. As soon as the blow connects Crichton goes back onto the defensive, blocking a blow here, dodging another there. The best tactic Crichton had for this situation was to wait the fight out, and rely upon the super-soldier serum, which currently flowed through his veins, to have better stamina than the impressive pupils before him. Crichton could see the pupils breathing heavily before him and decided that it was time to put it in attack instead of defensive, regardless of the costs. The pupil furthest to his right, fires off an explosive kick, reaching the heights of six foot, Crichton grabs his leg with his right arm, grabs another student with his left hand, and throwing him into the rest of the pupils knocking them to the ground and in one swift movement, punches the pupil on his right in the face with his left hand. The pupil flies across the room, much like his teacher did, not five minutes before him. Crichton turns to face the rest of the pupils to catch a blow to the forehead. At first he wasn’t entirely sure what had hit him, the blow had caught him by surprise and as a result he was put off balance. Once his vision became clear again, he realised that the pupil he threw off initially had retrieved some kind of wooden stick, and whacked Crichton with it right between the eyes. The rest of the pupils, the ones that were still conscious at least, had these wooden sticks also. As soon as his vision cleared and he stopped staggering around the place, all of the students struck at once. Crichton did his best to avoid as many blows as he could, but he could not block any blow at all, it was too painful. The pupils back Crichton off against the nearest wall, all relentlessly beating him with the sticks. In one last-ditch effort, Crichton grabs out in the vain hope of grasping onto something, and connects with one of the wooden sticks. With all his might Crichton yanks the stick away, causing the pupil it was attached to, to impact against the wall at a ferocious speed indeed. And so the battle continued, Crichton’s ferocious rage taking over as he instinctively fights… Five minutes later, there was no longer a fight in progress, nor were any of the pupils still conscious. This had been a handy little exercise and it had taught him two lessons. The first was to never underestimate an opponent of any stature and the second was that when it came down to it, if he relied on his instincts he could win any battle. This was not battle tested enough for him to use it as a viable tactic though, something he had to remedy before exercising revenge upon the rich industrialist Miss Zemoff or for the extreme title match on Friday Fusion. He needed to be tested more and that’s when fate reared it’s ugly head. In the form of the Avengers! To be continued…
