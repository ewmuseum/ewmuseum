Stuck in the St. Louis Arch with Ana, Ganker, Hank, Janelle, Josh...

3 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3-3-02 --- Ganker, Ana, Hank, Josh, and Janelle are on their way up to the top of the St. Louis Arch and once they get up and get off they notice they are the only ones there. Hank and Josh start jumping up and down as a large creek can be heard and they all jump in fright as Ganker lears over the side and sees that the bottom of the actual arch is tilting back and forth. Ganker creeps back to go back down, but finds that the elevator has been shutdown for the time being, until the arch stops swaying back and forth. Ganker is wearing blue jeans, white dirty t-shirt, and a black leather jacket. Hank is wearing a long black leather trench coat, baggy black JNCO jeans, and a black Team Nirvana t-shirt. Josh is wearing blue jeans and a black PCW polo shirt. Ana is wearing a white t-shirt and blue jeans. Janelle is wearing black jeans and a light sky blue long sleeve t-shirt. Ganker then tries to pry the elevator door open, but finds himself to be way too weak to even budget it a milimeter. Hank then walks over to help, but finds the door is jammed shut. Josh then cringes up in a ball in a corner, hoping it will all just go away soon. Ana and Janelle start to converse. Ana: 'Just our luck to be trapped up here and the arch could fall.' Janelle: 'It's not going to fall, that'd be letting Hank off to easily for half the sh*t he has done in his lifetime.' Hank: 'I sincerely hope that Mark Estep does not take the exploding box too seriously, even though it may kill him.' Ganker: 'I'll ignore you even said that.' Hank: 'Right, Mr. I walk into my first PCW PPV for a shot to win the number one contendership for the PCW Cruiserweight Championship. Yo make me sick boyo~!' Ganker: 'Yo always make me sick, but I keep it to myself.' Hank: 'Well jus' feel lucky that yo get the shot to beat on Red Rock first. Actually Red Rock should be thankin' god that I'm not in this match wit' him. I want to beat on him so badly. I want to make him suffer for everything he does, has done, and will do to me. Red Rock is scum and deserves nothin' he gets, except the ocassional a** kickin' which really doesn't even deserve those, he's worthless and pathetic.' Ganker: (singing/taunting) 'I get to beat Red Rock before yo do, I get to beat Red Rock before yo do~! Nah, nah, nah, nah!' (laughs) Hank: 'Freak show.' Ganker: 'No, not freak show, its...' (thumb thing) 'Ganker Is God!' Hank: 'Mark Estep, I lost my title and am forced to lower myself to the likes of Mark Estep, what are they doin' to me~!?!?' Ganker: 'Takin' yo out mentally, don't let it get to yo too much.' Hank: 'But I was once a jobber, I refuse to go back to that life, not wit' out a fight that is.' Ganker: 'I won't ever go down the PCW path yo went down, I plan to win as much as I can, startin' by winnin' my first PCW title before yo even thought yo stood a chance. I get the number one contendership tonight, and then, I become PCW Cruiserweight Champion, why, cause...' (thumb thing) 'Ganker Is God!' Hank: 'I hate Mark Estep wit' a passion... He cost me my title.' (about to cry) Ganker: 'Suck it up baby...' Hank: 'No~!' (cries) --- Ganker kicks Hank and kicks him some more so he stops his crying. Ganker: 'I get to beat on Red Rock and yo don't, go make Mark Estep do the J-O-B to the H-A-N-K~!' Hank: (in pain) 'I'll imagine that Mark Estep is yo or Red Rock, whoever I am pissed at, at that time.' --- The elevator now starts working once more as they get on and go on down. The scene now cuts out.
