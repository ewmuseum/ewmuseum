Date: 05/24/03 OOC: This series has been leaving out PCW, wrestling, etc.. but next and last will contain a good amount of info and dialogue on it.) Lounging too proudly over the mid-sized, motel owned bed, the room illuminated and lighted by a single lamp.. to the side of him was his partisan, standing over him in his sleek black suit, Elliot Costa.. He’d been there for over an hour, running through the guidelines senselessly like a broken record.. and seeing that Elliot had been commanding this campaign, Stone seemed to follow but still went his ways instead of having Costa denigrate his image. 'Just be there in an hour.. I'll be calling the Police right after the ten minutes you're in there so limit your time and get out ASAP.' Elliot importuned Stone with the continuous run of repetition.. Stone just kept his pattern of nodding as he greeted him out with a handshake. Elliot walked out as Stone, in relief shut the door, and fell back to his bed in the darkness produced by the jerked drapes. He napped over uncomfortably in his tux but nonetheless still managing to close his eyes and fall in to a deep daze. ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ Waking up on his rear, he wiped the sleep off his eyes and stood up.. leaning over to the side, wooden-made table drawer, picking a watch from it and reading the time.. '6:32 PM'... and he was far overdue to being at Victim Number Two's home and he rushed out of his motel room, dropping the watch and not minding to close the door in the room. ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ In the darkness Stone stood.. Waiting at the porch of a rural-like and urban-like mix of an area.. the moon looked and hung down towards the ground, as the blur and scatter of clouds disappeared making room for the resplendent stars to show.. Before him was a door, as he gently knocked, cloaked in his mackinaw coat and his hood over his head.. From the back he heard the footsteps of an approaching woman, and the yells of.. 'Be right there!' He prepared himself, squeezing his blade, which was propped to his side, and sniffing the air in before he invaded.. the door opened and there stood a woman, more specifically, Candice Walker, brown hair effulgent by the strewn blond streaks.. she was clad in a pair of denim blue jeans, and over her a long sleeveless top.. Stone sprang forward with his blade, bringing her down in to her own house and managing to kick the door in before himself. Kicking in screaming, Stone was falling to disadvantage as she scrambled out of sight in to the scenery in her house.. she ran up a row of stairs in fright, leading to the second floor but before she got to the top Stone leapt over from the fewer stairs behind and caught her leg.. In effort to wriggle her legs free, Stone added more agony and difficulty as he bit the skin of her leg as she bawled and cried out to no avail. He'd dropped his blade and looked around for it frantically as he knew it was time to finish her.. Next to her was Stone's blade and in reaching distance but in so much shock from the incursion she hesitated as he seized her and pounded on her knee.. Finally, stumbling down the stairs was Stone from the reckless kicks and screams.. falling down to his neck, he shook it off being that to him it was infinitesimal and diminutive damage a woman exudes. But the mercurial misogynist he was she still found no way on what to do but scrambled up the stairs in to the second floor platform of her house.. She crashed in to the drawers and frames hanging over the passageway hall unsteadily.. seconds before her was Stone scurrying up the stairs, snatching his floored blade and through the walkway. Candice rushed in to her master bedroom, clean and well embellished with a mirror hanging over down towards her queen sized bed.. she looked at herself, rushed and frightened as she paced back and forth on an escape but there were no other branches or connections to her room so running back out would lead her in to the hands of Stone... she decided to drop on her chest and slide in to her opened bed on the floor, breathing lightly and most importantly inaudibly. Stone browsed through the walkway room doors and found the bedroom door opened so he came in dashing forward then stopping.. looking around with his blade and looking in to the mirror with the devilish and sadistic grimace across his face. The room's demeanor became more and more morose as the sickening look across his face bad the mood more eerie.. he looked over to the windowpanes and over covering it he blinded them. 'I'm going to find you!' he searched as he looked past the open door and behind It.. to the side of drawers but nothing.. over to the side of the beds but nothing.. Then ducking down he felt the noise and air of breathing.. He tiptoed around the bed, where Candice's feet were and laid on his chest.. then suddenly pulling her out from under, rendering a fanfare of screams and shouts.. Stone pulled her up, and coiled his pudgy hands around hers, slapping her on the face and hitting her with harsh blows to the face and body emasculating her further.. he took out his pocketed blade as he sent her over to her bed but she immediately rolled off pulling the covers on with her and trying to escape only to be grabbed in the hands of the Misogynist.. This for Stone had been the longest and most difficult kill out of all he'd pulled off in his 'career'. Unveiling the covers over her, he pricked her with the blade over her shirt as he stomped over her chest and bent down.. Stabbing her in her forearm and mawkishly giving her a kiss on her upper lip, to his amusement and for the obstruction of her cries.. then he sickeningly pulled out the blade from her forearm and lastly impaled it in to her heart, killing her instantly. He took his time to breathe then went off, tearing out the blade and running out of the room.. through the passageway of the second floor, down the stairs to the lobby alpha and exiting through the already opened door... Leaving the sirens of the police rang out, alerting him as he scuttled hastily leeward, leading him away from the sounds and uproarious noises produced from the Police vehicles. Swerving a corner, just feet from the highway he pulled from his tux breast pocket a mobile phone, flipping it and dialing multiple digits and listening to and from his ear.. 'It's done.. Now come pick me up... I'm on the corner of the Highway of her house.' Stone collectedly stated as he tapped his feet, and dug through his pocket while flipping his phone off and tucking it in to his breast pocket.. He’d pulled out a crumbled piece of paper, and flattened it just leafing through it... putting out a new victim. Victim # 3:Name: Lisa BuckerdHeight/Weight: 5'7/189Hair Color/Eye Color: Dirty blond/Dark brownAddress: 23 Bainbridge County, Georgia... Corner of 'Washer Laundromat‘. exactly two and a half miles from Victim # 2.Race: Black He shook his head in disgust for the attributes.. 'She's going to be hard.' He claimed before pocketing the paper and incontrovertibly musing for his campaign.. but also having to mention his other career.. the PCW.
