The 'Anti-Christ' Saga: Divided...

29 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3-29-02 OOC Note: I apoligize to anyone who was involved in the conclusion of this. I planned on developing everyone on the sides for a few RP's but really started loathing RPing during this saga. There will be one more RP after this titled Last Resort, and then I can get into doing RP's I actually WANT to do. -----------------------------------Rev 6:3 When the Lamb opened the second seal, I heard the second living creature say, 'Come!' 4 Then another horse came out, a fiery red one. Its rider was given power to take peace from the earth and to make men slay each other. To him was given a large sword. ::Flashback:: Miya starts to climb the turnbuckle, which causes Kannon to hop on the apron again and push Miya off to a sound disappointment in the crowd. Dave: And Kannon’s lost it too! Jeff: No he’s finally getting it!! The boos turn to cheers as the Krayzie one appears from the back and heads to the ring with a purpose in his stride. Dave: KRAYZIE'S HERE! WOOOHOOO! Duke: My hoss! He's here to restore order! Jeff: There went the ratings, if they could possibly get any lower. Howard gets in earshot of Kannon and lets him have it. Howard: What are you doing Xavier??? At the same time Walden takes a glance behind him to see how close Mexi is. What he failed to realize is that Mexi stopped running and Tim runs smack dab into the White Mexican’s chest. Dave: It appears that there is trouble in both the camps of the Deuces and the Asylum. Mexi pulls Walden up to his feet as Krayzie and Kannon are exchanging words not far away. Mexi: What are you doing Tim? I thought I asked you to give Boda his belt back? Mexi stands impatiently waiting for an answer when Tim undoes the belt and holds it in front of him. Mexi: Give me the belt Tim. Tim: Here’s your fucking belt! Mexi steps back in shock but it’s too late as Tim cold cocks him with the World title belt and that was all that Kannon and Krayzie needed to start coming to blows. Dave: Walden has snapped!!! He’s absolutely lost it!!! Tim stands over Mexi and starts to paint brush Mexi as he struggles to get back up to his feet. Walden: I'm not *SLAP* your God *SLAP* damn *SLAP* mother *SLAP* fucking *SLAP* lackey! *SLAP* Duke: I can't believe this! Tim Walden has just laid out his stablemate with the world title belt! Jeff: Woohoo! Splatter the rest of his brains. Dave: Tim, no! Don't do it Tim! Walden: I'm sick *SLAP* of you *SLAP* fucking *SLAP* asking *SLAP* me *SLAP* to do *SLAP* all *SLAP* your *SLAP* GOD *SLAP* DAMN *SLAP* work! *SLAP* Walden stands up and grabs his crotch as he gives a humping jesture. Walden: Fuck this you son of a bitch! Dave: Walden has lost it! This is unbelievable. Duke: I sure hope the parents put their kids to bed. Jeff: I hope Walden slams his head again for what he did to Nelson! Dave sighs. Mean while Kannon and Krayzie are trading blows on the outside and Xavier is starting to gain an edge. Jeff: KANNON!!!! Duke: Shut up. Kannon grabs Krayzie and whips him into the guard rail but Howard sucks it up and turns around meeting Xavier’s face with a stiff right hand. Dave: This can't be! ASYLUM IS FALLING APART! KANNON AND HOWARD! Jeff: Kill each other! Duke: This won't be good for the group. Jeff: What group. It’s gone! The referee sticks his head out of the rope, trying to break Howard and Kannon apart. They go at each like rabid dogs. With the referee's back turned, Darkstar comes out of the crowd and slides into the ring with a baseball in hand. Dave: What is he doing here??? Boda gets to his feet just in time to feel the home run swing from Darkstar to his head. Boda drops to the mat, bleeding from the head and out cold. Darkstar pulls Miya on top of Boda, and then slides out of the ring. The referee turns around and counts the pin. 1...... 2...... 3!!!!!!!!! Clay: THE WINNER OF THIS MATCH AND *NEW* PCW WORLD CHAMPION..... MIYAGAWA!!!!!! Dave: This is an outrage! I don't think Miya wanted to win this way! Jeff: Now that’s how you beat someone. Duke: You have no morals. Miya gets to his feet, and turns to Darkstar, beginning to bitch at him. Darkstar just shrugs, which upsets Miya and he grabs DarkStar’s arm. Kannon and Howard are taking it to each other. Fists fly everywhere. Boda slides out of the ring and over to Walden and rips him off Mexi. Boda becomes enraged, taking his frustration out on Walden grabbing him by the neck and bending him over the guardrail as he yells at him. Boda: WHAT THE HELL IS WRONG WITH YOU WALDEN!!!! Surprisingly Walden actually yells back. Walden: FUCK YOU!!!! Boda’s eyes light up in shock and rears back to hit him when D-Kay Calyx and Red Rock rush out from the back and separate the two men. Dave: Asylum is exploding! The Wild Deuces are exploding! Jeff: It’s about damn time! Duke: It’s a sad day in PCW history. Dave: One of PCW's oldest stables is about to implode from within! Miya and DarkStar start trading blows in the ring and the crowd is going nuts not knowing what to expect next and it turns out to be Bobby Stormbringer running down from the back. Dave: WE'RE OUT OF TIME FANS! WE'LL SEE YOU ON FUSION! Duke: THEY'VE EXPLODED! Jeff: EXPLOSIONS! WOOHOO! Dave: THE WILD DUECES ARE GONE! ASYLUM IS GONE! THREE NIGHTS REMOVED FROM TWO BIG FACE VICTORIES AT MARCH MADNESS AND THEY'RE STABLES EXPLODE! ------------------ Talena stirred, if slightly, but she awoke. Her eyes were watery and blood stained the ground around her. She was in bad shape, but the bleeding was stopped.. at least she was alive. She sat up, and felt the pain on her wrists and neck, the puncture and slash wounds were still there, and quite open... she couldnt speak, and was barely existing, her mind reeled and she felt like she would lose conciousness again... until she looked up... she saw, standing in front of her, Mexi... one of DarkStar's old friends, he kneeled down and tried to help her up, she barely knew what was going on and almost tried to fight him. Mexi: Whoa... Talena, calm down... Talena shook her head. Talena: Anti-... Lucifer... DarkStar... gone... I... gotta... dead... Mexi looked at her funny. Mexi: Right... tell me what happened! Is this one of those sexual things between you and DarkStar. She collapsed, weeping, Mexi felt bad (his thinking being that he caused this, maybe they broke up?) He kneeled down again and put his hand on her shoulder. Mexi: Did you two break up... do you need comforting? He had no idea how to handle this situation. But she shook her head no. Talena: Harle... she... him... Mexi seemed shocked, but attributed her thinking to the blood loss. Mexi: Talena... isn't Harle dead? Talena nodded violently and quickly. Talena: Yes... so was DarkStar... Him... Mexi realized she must not know about his return. Mexi: I know, Talena, It's amazing but I saw him come back with my own two eyes... It's... Talena finished his sentance. Talena: Not him... It's not him at all... Mexi was now signifigantly confused. Talena noticed this. Talena: T-... Take me to the hospital and i'll explain... ----------------------------------------------- The aftermath of PCW's stables exploding was clear, people who were brothers were now seperated, somewhere backstage Miyagawa stormed off, sweating and bloodied, he was angry and slammed almost right through a reporter. He looked down upon him and kept walking, muttering to himself... Miyagawa: What is this... World War III? ----------------------------------------------- Mexi sat across from Talena in the Triage area of the Hospital, her wounds were mended and bandaged, she flexed her slashed wrist a little and flinched, it was still tender. Mexi looked at her blankly, his stare seemed to go right through her, she had told him exactly what she saw, the Morningstar controlling the body of her true love, she said how Harle Quinn... the deceased Harle Quinn faught by his side as his princess. She confessed it all. Mexi was speechless. Talena: What? Mexi: ... Talena: Will you say something damn it! Mexi: ...are you sure you don't want a psychiatrist. Talena's eyes glowed a dark red. Talena: Do I LOOK like I need help! Mexi opened his mouth but closed it quickly, deciding not to answer. Mexi: I don't know what to believe... he raised from the dead, he helped his stable, he brought PCW together somewhat... until the stables incident... I could have sworn he was a new leader... Talena: Are you familiar with any sort of biblical prophecy? Mexi seemed taken aback from the abrupt question. Mexi: N-... Not really... Talena: That was prophecy, the stables, PCW collapsing, that is the coming of the 'Red Horse'. If you want to believe me or not, I'm not a scholar, but I know what I'm seeing. Mexi was still confused, wasn't Talena agnostic? Talena: I fucking hate God, he does nothing but screw me and Miss Emily over, but I do realize that my sweetie is imprisoned in hell because Lucifer and his minions finally grew a set, and I need you to help me get him back before Morningstar does anymore damage to the PTC. Mexi: But... Talena growled. Talena: I didnt say you had to accept Christ, who cares about that hippie, he's to busy watching a Phish concert to even begin to care about the rise of the antichrist, I need you to help me free my Prometheus... and you have no choice... For I have the power to castrate you with my mind... That easily... the Not-so-bad guys numbers rose to two... ---------------------------------------------- DarkStar's arms bled as he ripped the metal bonds from the walls, his head has been bleeding for quite some time, thanks to the roughness of the rusty chain layed across it. He stumbled slightly forward and fell to the dusty ground, noone was around this far into the depths of hell, but he was still cautious, He didn't want to be imprisoned here forever. He stumbled into a room across from his holding cell... It was dark, and he couldn't see much... Until the lights came up... There were the rest of the triumverate, Azazel and Beelzebub, DarkStar sighed as they began to walk towards him... He was not having a good year. --------------------------------------------- Lucifer (his soul inside DarkStars body) grinned down upon the few remaining people in the Locker Room, he was to discuss his plan of unity throughout PTC, everyone would come together outside of the ring... as one. The people gathered were, Argyle, Clyde, Mark Malonie, Tyler Nelson, Vampir, The End, Harle Quinn, Both members of Civil D., Corg, Bobby Stormbringer, Andy Murray, and Badge.... Lucifer quieted the crowd with a few looks, and stood on the makeshift podium, he smiled... and his smile comforted everyone. Lucifer: Well... you all have been loyal to me since I returned to the PTC family... you know I couldnt stay away from you guys. A few murmurs and some claps from the crowd, in the two months since DarkStars return from the dead, the people sitting in front of him were those that truely believed there was something special about him, something... Christ like.. Lucifer: As you guys may have heard rumors about, I am trying to unite the PTC as brothers, no more of this petty rivarly, only friendly competition. Many people have thought I was insane for bringing this up, and maybe I am... but It is my dream that the PTC can exist as one Community, under one name, as one entity! Loud cheers from the people who were in league with him, he smirked, knowing he had them all in the palm of his hand. ------------------------------------------------- On the other side of the spectrum, Talena rubbed her injured arm and made sure not to move her neck around much, it was still tender. She would have to get used to the pain. There would be alot more of it in the days to come... in the two months since Lucifer began to gain control of various members of the PTC family. There would be alot more of this to come. She looked at the few people that didn't trust 'DarkStar' and realized that this was her and Mexi's chance. The group didn't have numbers... since PTC has became so close it stood at Miyagawa, Ed, Juan Borro, Ben J Palithorpe, Rent-A-Hero, Jason Core and Kensuke Suzahara. None of them trusted DarkStar BEFORE there was suspition that he was now the antichrist, so why would they trust him now. Talena sat down, and pulled out a diagram from her purse, folding it out and placing it on the table. It was elaborate, and for all she knew a few people could die during this. She made it seem that she was concerned for the well being of the souls in the room, but it's Talena, in reality she looked past the bigger picture to see the smaller one. That was saving DarkStar. The plan was simple. They would lure 'DarkStar' to Talena, she would use her powers to expel the soul of the Morningstar from DarkStar's body... and hopefully... She didn't even want to think of what would happen if DarkStar couldn't return to his body. She shuddered at the thought but continued talking. Most people seemed confused but understood one thing... This was War. To Be Continued
