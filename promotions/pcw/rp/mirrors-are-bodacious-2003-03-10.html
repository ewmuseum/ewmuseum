Mirrors Are Bodacious!

10 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: March 10, 2003 [The mirror is a sacred thing, dudes and dudettes. When your beauty is one of your God given talents a man or woman must appreciate this mirror everyday. See, it reflects all that is bodacious about the person without flaw. This person gets the chance to see themselves and splender in what they can offer the world with their beauty. It's a bodacious feeling to be part of the beautiful people and love looking into the mirror. There are people who don't even WANT to look in the mirror because they are so ashamed of themselves. The beautiful people just sit back and wonder how that is so. How can these people damn the invention of the mirror so much? Why would they do such a thing to one of the greatest things ever invented? It's just a case of not walking in others shoes. The beautiful have never been ugly and the ugly have never been beautiful. Both coming from different sides of the tracks with different opinions and different outlooks. Their personalities clash so hard that it's been an ongoing feud since anybody can remember. Beauty versus ugly. Good versus Evil. Or is it Evil versus Good? Who is Good and who is Evil? Are all the beautiful people evil for the physical gifts that they were given? Are the ugly people cursed by some unknown force because of something their parents have done? Nobody really knows...but a wise man once said, 'Beauty is in the eye of the beholder' and it certainly was wise] ['Totally Tubular' Tommy faces directly into the scene with his tanned face looking straight ahead, emotionless. His blue eyes are so hypnotic that it's no wonder he's a chick magnet. The way the blue tints from darker to lighter just makes it easy to look. With Tommy's wide and strong facial features it helps take it for the kill when he pulls his facial muscles into a smile. His sparkling white teeth gleam in the lights above him and you can literally see no sign of plaque or one cavity. He then brushes his hands through his bright blonde hair, which looks almost white with the lighting, and rubs his clean shaven face. The smile only shifts wider as he sits further back letting his well defined chest come into view. He flexes his pecs and makes them do a little dance as he giggles to himself. He looks back up with no emotion and, for no reason, begins sticking his tongue in and out. It wiggles all around as he moves his shoulders from side to side along with his tongue as if he's making up some kind of odd new dance. He stops and wipes the spit from the side of his mouth and then makes evil eyes, arching his eyebrows up to make it TWICE as evil. He looks rather ridiculous actually as it just looks as if he's sucked on something sour and desperately wants to spit it out. Thankfully, he just smiles and laughs finding his last performed face a good attempt at humor. Then scene zooms out to show only a mirror's reflection of The Tubular One. He holds up his right arm and flexes his bicep a little bit looking at the arm, and then his arm in the mirror] 'Totally Tubular' Tommy: Look at that totally bodacious muscle! Your huge! [Tommy uses his left hand to squeeze his bicep and he moves it up and down as if it were talking] Talking bicep: Of course, Tommy! I'm totally bodacious because its YOUR MUSCLE! Tommy: Of course, dude! I always take care of my muscles. We have to look most gnarly for the babes. Taling bicep: I like when the ladies feel me. Tommy: I know another muscle that likes the ladies touching it. [Tommy smiles wickedly into the mirror and then looks down. He then looks back up to the mirror with the smile still on his face. Then he grabs his abs and does the same thing with them that he did with his right bicep] Talking abs: We love the ladies touch, Tommy! Thanks for doing sets of five hundred crunches to make us look rad! Tommy: No problem, awesome abs! I'll do anything to make sure you stay intact! [Tommy then goes back up to his right bicep and grabs it again] Talking bicep: What about us?! Tommy: DUDE! Don't be bogus, right bicep. I'll totally under work you tomorrow! Talking bicep: Then your left bicep will look much bigger and make you look most heinous. Tommy: You know...your right, compadre. Besides I was just busting your chops! I would NEVER under work you in a million years. But it's just that the abs are what the babes like to go for first is all. You have to have a most bodacious arsenal if your going to get ahead in the pimp game. [Tommy then holds up the PCW Continental Title into the mirror and his eyes grow with great amount of pride. The large lights above the mirror shine down on the golden belt making it sparkle like it's a truly a treasure, which it is. He pets it lightly with his left hand feeling the indentations of the gold writing, especially where his name is carved into the belt. A sort of an orgasmic smile grows now as he keeps feeling, watching himself in the mirror like some kind of pervert. He then looks at the belt and nods toward it] Tommy: We've been going through some totally rad times, compadre. I bet that you were most ecstatic when I won you back at Decimation. All those totally heinous compadres that had you before didn't know how to treat you, but I KNOW HOW to treat you! I shine you and handle you with the utmost care. I ESPECIALLY keep you away from all those other totally heinous dudes who want you. You don't want them do you? You don't want to be around their dirty waists...those heinous compadres like Rent-A-Jerkface, Venneman Pendragon, Chet King, Skylar Green, and Dante Jackson. I will never let you fall into their most bogus hands...not in a MILLION years! [Tommy then puts the belt in front of his face and shakes it up and down as if it's talking] Talkling Belt: Your so good to me, Tommy. You've done such a bodacious job of taking all of those guys out to make sure that I stay in your hands. Your extremely handsome AND your one of the best wrestlers in the world. Could you ask for anything more? [Tommy then takes the belt down and looks at it with a very assured look] Tommy: I'd like to be the first to combine the World and Continental Title! That's something to ask for, dude! [Tommy puts the belt back up to his face] Talking belt: Oh my goodness, Tommy! I would love that so much to be reunited with my totally rad brother! That would sort of like be splitting you and Harry up! Talk about HEINOUS! But if there is one man who can reunite us as one...then it's YOU! In fact, me and my brother would want it to be only YOU! Why? Because your the most bodacious wrestler, EVER! [Tommy pulls the belt back down and has a flattered smile across his face] Tommy: Dude, your most gracious! But you are right. If there is but one dude in PCW who can unite the titles, it's me...'Totally Tubular' Tommy! Hey...wanna see something gnarly, belt? Talking belt: Sure. [Tommy makes a muscle with his left arm as he uses his right to hold the belt and make it wiggle] Talking belt: Oh my god! It's huge! [Tommy smiles into the mirror] Tommy: I hear that ALL THE TIME! 'Heavy Metal' Harry: Sure you do, buddy! [Tommy almost falls out of his chair from the shock that Harry sent through him. He looks back at his brother really wandering if that's his brother or some heinous imposter posing as him. Harry is usually wrapped in black and leather, but this dude has on like a dress shirt and pants. It's weird. The hair is still long, his eyes aren't bloodshot, and he seems to have lost a little weight off of his gnarly gut! The dude posing as Tommy's brother walks right behind Tommy so that his reflect is hovering over his bro] Tommy: You look weird, dude. Harry: I found these threads in the closet behind all the leather pants and jackets. This is a pretty sharp outfit and I got a few compliments from Gina. Tommy: My Gina?! Harry: Well, I didn't know that she had your name branded on her ass, but if your talking about the Gina with the flowing brown hair, amazing green eyes, and stunning tanned body then I guess we are talking about the same one. Tommy: Dude. She always thought you were a loser! Harry: I guess she doesn't anymore. [Harry turns away from Tommy and walks somewhere to the back of the room. Tommy looks down at his belt and whispers to it] Tommy: I am going to have to get this dude high again. [He wiggles the belt lightly for it to respond] Whispering Belt: Totally, dude. [Harry walks back behind Tommy] Harry: I've come to inform you of your opponent on Turmoil. Tommy: Turmoil?! What in the name of The Beach Boys is that? Harry: Tommy...how long have you been in PCW? Tommy: One year. Harry: And you don't know what Turmoil is? Tommy: I have a dictionary, bro. I can look it up. Harry: Don't bother. Tommy: Ok. Harry: You have to go up against The Pharaoh on Tuesday, that's what I'm saying. [Tommy looks a bit perplexed in the mirror] Tommy: PCW has a show on Tuesday?! When the hell did that happen?! Harry: Are you sure your my brother? [Tommy looks into the mirror extremely confused now] Tommy: Dude, we are TOTALLY brothers! I came a year before you though, because the first kid is always the most bodacious one. Harry: Yeah, and the first also talks to his body parts. Tommy: Do not! Harry: I've got it on tape. Tommy: YOU DO NOT!! [Harry holds up the video camera as it slowly appears over Tommys shoulder in the mirror. Tommy's eyes grow as wide as silver dollars] Tommy: You give me that tape!! Harry: Here ya go. [Harry hands the Mini-DV tape to Tommy as a smile grows across his face. Harry then slowly starts to back away from Tommy] Harry: Just remember to get ready for The Pharaoh on Tuesday. He was a former NTICW World Champ and he's a guy you used to look up to in NTICW. Tommy: He totally looked up to me, dude. I am taller than him! DUH! [Harry is seen rolling his eyes in the background and then walking out. Tommy looks over the tape and is proud of himself for getting it off of Harry so easily. Tommy begins thinking about The Pharaoh and how he saw him in NTICW all those years back. Times were totally different back then when the dudes and dudettes never really gave The Tubular One a chance and he was lost into obscurity. But Tommy also thinks that all of that is over with now. He's a new man and one with the most bodacious title EVER! In fact, once March Madness rolls around he's going to have both of the bodacious belts! Like The Pharaoh even stands a chance against a gnarly dude like Tommy] Tommy: Belt...I'm totally going to send that heinous dude back to Egypt. Talking belt: Totally! [Fade to black]
