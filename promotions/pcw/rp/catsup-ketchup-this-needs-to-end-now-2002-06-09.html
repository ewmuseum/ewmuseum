Catsup....Ketchup...this needs to end now!

9 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6/9/02 -Duke Williams sits at a study at his mistress home in Chicago, IL. The Malice Man looks very studious almost Legend INC like with his reading glasses on and a button shirt. His hair flows long and free with out the trademark hat on his head. In his hand is a Webster’s dictionary, he studies it like a pervert studies a busty breast at a strip club. His look is very intense as he flips through the pages. The door to the study flies opens as Kesha Jameson walks through the door wearing just a silk black robe and nothing else. She walks up to Duke and starts to message his neck.- Kesha: What are you looking up baby? Williams: Nothing important. Kesha: Does it have something to do with your opponent for Fusion, Akiyama? Williams: No, that’s not the void that’s concerning me. Kesha: Then what is it?? Is it what Booker did to you Friday? Williams: All is fair in love and war. -Kesha cocks her head with a Clair Huckstable attitude.- Kesha: Then what the hell are you researching while I’m standing here half naked ready to f*ck? -Duke looks up for a moment and notices that Kesha is just about naked. He takes a double glance then returns to the dictionary. – Williams: I’ll be with you in a minute hossette. Kesha: Damn baby what are you so concerned with. Williams: You remember last week when we went shopping? Kesha: Yea, you bitched for an hour and a half about the price of beef. Williams: Yup, you do remember. -Duke reaches back and pinches her nipple as he smiles at her.- Kesha: So what about last week? Williams: When we were in the condiment aisle and you were buying some mustard I noticed that there were two kinds of ways to spell Ketchup. -Kesha looks at Duke like he’s nuts.- Kesha: What the f*ck are you talking about? Williams: When I grew up all we had was Ketchup spelled k-e-t-s-h-u-p. Then at the store I saw they had catsup spelled c-a-t-s-u-p. So who’s right? Kesha: Who cares? Williams: I do!! Somebody is always right. I don’t stand for spin or the gray area. Kesha: Do you smoke all that dope you bought yesterday in one sitting? -Duke pinches her other breast and smiles.- Williams: It’s Sunday and I got bored when you went to church. What did the pastor talk about? Kesha: Infidelity! -Both Duke and Kesha laugh as Duke is back together with his wife and is cheating right now.- Williams: Sucker!! -Kesha flicks Dukes ear as she is religious in a non-practicing, go through the motions kind of way (what ever the hell that means).- Williams: The damn dictionary doesn’t solve anything as it lists both spellings. This is all f*cked up. -Duke tries to rip the dictionary in half showing off his power through frustration. Unfortunately the book is too thick and doesn’t rip. Duke becomes more frustrated and throws it - Williams: I hate books!! We should burn them all and just have TV. Kesha: Your whacked. Williams: Why don’t you come over here and whack me. -Duke winks as he points at his crotch.- Keasha: Hold on while I go get the tweezers. -Keasha exits the room before Duke could retort to the insult.- Williams: Two names both are creating the same thing. Both pronounced the same, look the same, taste the same, smell the same, but there spelled different. What a f*ck up world hoss. -Duke shakes his head with disgust, at barriers our society has to over come, to create a better place. Starting with a unified spelling of Ketchup.- Williams: How many other words are in this dictionary, that have the same meaning and pronunciation but different spellings? I don’t know but I bet a lot. This is terrible!! Think of all the extra trees that had to be killed in order to print an extra page cus the corporate criminals in the Ketchup business can’t agree on a common spelling? This is not right. This is bureaucracy at it’s worse. This is everything Tyler Nelson stands for. The root of this controversy comes right back to him. I’m sure of it. He’s hell bent on keeping the good people of the catsup world away from the fine folk of the ketchup side. Cooperate trickery if you ask me. Delusion of separation so the A-1’s of the world don’t know whom to attack first. Where are the steak sauces going to go if there are two clearly similar yet different sides? They just stand their confused seeking advice from the mustard who’s in cohorts with the Ketchup criminals. It’s a sloppy mess of condiment corruption that represents everything the Malice Crew is against. -Duke sits there and meditates on his marijuana induced rant.- Williams: Wait a minute what if the Ketchup guys are not the criminals but rather the confused good guys. The Catsup and Ketchup people both market their products to the same crowd. They have the same flavor and serve the same propose of masking the taste of a bad piece of meat. They represent the same principles and commitments. It must be a ploy by the mustard industry to keep the Ketchup people from completely destroying the mustard men. What if the Malice Crew is the Ketchup and the Phantom Phive is the Catsup. Nelson would have to be the mustard. If the Catsup and the Ketchup people got together to fight for the common good they would be unstoppable. The condiment revolution would end abruptly and the tomato-based sauces would rule all! Too much ego in the camps of both tomato coronals. Would Heinz really team up with Hunt? Hell no they would lose too much in sales. Plus the Booker DDTed me. So forget that I think my first theory is more on the money. -Duke smiles as he satisfies his mind with an understanding only he can comprehend in his current state of deep thinking and herbal mediation.- Williams: You find them tweezers yet? -Duke shouts to the other room, a voice is heard in reply.- Kesha: Yea, there stuck between my legs I need your tongue to dig them out. -Duke smiles a big smile at the thought of that task.- Williams: Ok I’ll be there in a minute. -Duke starts to shut down the study first by turning off the computer.- Williams: This Friday on fusion I have to prove myself against Akiyama in a one on one match up. -Duke shuts the computer screen off, then turns his attention to the blinds.- Williams: This match means a lot more then just a Fusion filler match. This match is a true test of where I stand as a singles wrestler. If I can win this one on one match up with out any sideways garbage. Then I truly am back to full form. If I lose, who the hell knows where I stand in the eyes of my peers, the fans, and most of all myself. -Duke stokes his beard.- Williams: Akiyama is a wonderful wrestler and a great performer. You know this old Malice Man don’t dig that dark crap but hey every fruits got a seed. If it works for him, f*ck it. Unfortunately for Akiyama not only do I despise dark creepy guys. Not only do I have a drive to win this match and prove my value in the PCW. But it’s going to be on a feel good Friday and also the Day Of Malice. Kiddies its time to shine again. Put away your bible, grab the whiskey, grab your hottest piece of ass, eat some fried chicken and turn on Fusion. It’s Malice Time and nobody is going to be dropped in the void but rather sent WHISKEY BENT AND HELL BOUND. That buckaroo’s is the honky tonk truth. -Duke undoes his belt as he shuts off the lights and heads to the bedroom where the dark chocolate Kesha is waiting for him.- Williams: That can wait. Now it’s an OTHER kind of Malice Time with some Malice Style fun. -The voice ends as the screen cuts to a PCW logo.-
