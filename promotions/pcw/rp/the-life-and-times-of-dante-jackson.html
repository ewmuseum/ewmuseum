The Life and Times of Dante Jackson

Date: *shrugs* It started, the story that everyone already knows, the graffiti on the wall for the ramblings of thug life in Compton... That day, back on April 24th, Emmalou Jefferson woke up holding her stomach. The pain was tremendous. The little crack baby inside of her was paying her back for the nine months she spent poisoning it with the imported drug of the white man. She didn't care, it was hurting, but this pain was still not as bad as that of the crack withdraw symptoms. And she wasn't going to go through that. Not again. Emmalou:'UUNGH!' Man:'Shut up, ho!' It was dark, raining. The time: 3 AM. The hospital would be running the graveyard shift, and barely anyone would be around. Plus, the expectant mother would surely be blood-tested, with the crack and other drugs coming up. But she was in pain and she was at least responsible enough that she didn't want to have this baby out on the street, but in a delivery room. The engine of the many-times wrecked dingy brown (original color was emerald green, but the pain had been scratched, shot, and rusted off for the longest time) 1968 Ford El Dorado (bumper missing) sputters as the entire car seems to cough its way down the road, holding up traffic as angered late-night drivers speed past, flipping off the driver, V-man. The driver, her Emmalou's pimp and the father of her baby, weighed about 85 pounds. But he was a serious crack-fiend. He would go off it right before he was going out to collect his money. No one ever messed with him and he got his money. Emmalou:'It's coming!' V-man:'Bitch, gimme my money!' Emmalou: 'What the hell? V-man:'Force a' habit.' He drove as fast as he could down the highway. He wasn't very careful, but then again he wasn't on his way to the hospital. Not yet. He had to get another hit of crack. That's why he stole the car. Yeah, he was a pimp without a car. But when he was was on a crack-rage, he could run to all his blocks, all his bitches, faster than people could drive. Emmalou:'Where are we?' V-man: *Speaking to someone outside the stopped car*'Gimme 4 dimebags.' Emmalou: *Shaking head*'Dirty muthafucka...' Of course, with the detour, it wasn't until 4:30 AM that they actually got to the hospital. And if they just took the back road and went straight there he could have walked in 5 minutes. But they were there and Emmalou actually had to push the baby back in a couple of times to hold off. Her cervix was open wider than a sinkhole in California. Contractions were continuous, every second. Labor was in the heavy stages. V-man drove the car right through the front door of the hospital, destroying the car. Inside, he got out, tosses the receptionist the keys as though she was a valet, and went around to the other side. Pulling the door to the car clear off (though he didn't intend to, the door was hanging on with a piece of masking tape anyway), he yanked Emmalou out of the car and walks down the west hallway. Kicking the door open, he dropped her on the floor and stepped over the body of the pregnant crackwhore, walking forward into the drug storage room. He licked his lips and started going through the drugs. Emmalou tried to sit up on the ground. She turned her head to him, he busy with the drugs. V-man:'Aww, hell yeah.' Emmalou:'Fucka, I'm pregnant here!' V-man:'Just go ahead and do that there.' A nurse walked in and saw V-man in the drugs. But noticing that he was a crackhead, she wasn't about to do anything. Then she saw Emmalou on the floor. She called in some doctors and they put her on a stretcher and took her to the burn ward. Doctor:'Dammit!' Nurse:'They keep changing the departments on us!' After going back and taking a left instead of a right in hallway G2, the delivery room was opened up and Emmalou was taken in. 20 minutes later, Daunte Jefferson was born into the world. The Doctors and nurses all looked at him. Nurse:'Is it alive?' Doctor:'Damned if I know.' Nurse:'Slap its ass.' Doctor:'This thing look like a lizard monsta. You slap it!' Deciding to just leave it with the mother, the doctor and nurse left the delivery room and went into a closet to compulate. Oh well, no one said that the county hospital in Compton was a good one.
