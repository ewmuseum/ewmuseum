P.W.A Fears P.M.T (Gaz Green versus Natalia Miyagawa)

10 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10 - 11 - 02 'How did I get the task of carrying the bags?' murmured the sweet, innocent, yet distinctive voice of Primetime Championship's Russian diva Natalia Miyagawa. The scene opened as the dynamic duo's Gaz Green basked in the golden plaque before him. Gazing into the polished Championship of his Tag Team title, the Jester stood smugly, basking in what many have regarded as 'The greatest successful title defence of all time' before throwing his trophy over his shoulder. 'How did I get the task of carrying the bags?' questioned the recently acquired Natalia Miyagawa, searching her colleague for the answer. The green tailored Jester peered back, raising his sunglasses across his forehead, momentarily flattening his infamous spiked styled hair and only offering a roll of his eyes. Shielding his eyes from the intense sun that bathes his home city of Las Vegas, Nevada, the champion retorts what he regards as a flimsy effort of attention seeking. 'Oh! I'm aching all over after-....' said Gaz Green. His comment intruded upon by the irate Russian who has possibly reached the end of her tether. 'After Fusion's Main Event. Remember Gaz you wasn't the only competitor in that match and if it wasn't for my involvement in the Turmoil bout between Playaz Inc and Pranks With Attitude then you may have been nothing more than a challenger!' Capturing the slender physique of the petite Russian, her focal point at present was her glazed over eyes as she stared a hole through her prize asset of Gaz Green. Rage fuelled, the separated wife of Alexander Miyagawa took the bull by the horns and stamped her authority by dropping the heavy load of bags laid upon her by the self-indulging Jester, Natalia stormed into the apartment block via the large glass-paneled doors. With some awkward glares from the surrounding public, Gaz lifted the hoard of luggage onto his person and began to stagger one step forward, yet two steps back as he made the daunting task towards the buildings entry point. 'Life’s a bitch and then you hire one!' hissed the Emerald Menace as he fought the doors with his shoulder, still however trying to balance the PCW prestige. Finally fighting to victory, Gaz Green managed to claw himself into the lobby area, his back almost crippled under the deadweight of the luggage. Meanwhile Natalie drew a few drooling stares from the apartments clientele of high-profiled businessmen as she stands waiting for the elevator in what appears as a catalogue pose. Tapping her foot with impatience the scene switched back to the hunchback Jester who shuffles within feet of his beautiful valet when suddenly that of a teenage boy obstructs his chosen path. 'Hey Mr. your like Gaz Green and stuff!' triumphantly said the youngster, pulling his t-shirt tight, testing it's durability in a bid to brandish the bold lettering of 'Primetime Championship Wrestling'. 'Arr-... Err-... Well-... No kid, you've made a mistake. I'm Bruce Willis, a Bruce Willis in a hurry so if you could make like a tree and leave-...' retorted the Lord Of Laughter before the young lads interruption. 'You have too much hair to be Bruce Willis, but you look about the right age!' Gaz still snowed under with the Natalia Miyagawa shopping trip of bags, his prize procession of PCW gold and a few minor essentials taken to London England. The Bambi-esque blue eyes of this young boy almost turned the Jester to putty and slowly vanquished his built up rage to the point of gaining a gentlemanly response. 'You got me kid! Indeed I am Gaz 'The Jester' Green-....' 'No shit dude, like PCW Championship alert, I mean, hello!' injected the lone youngster, indicating the gold that adorns the Jester's shoulder with a stroke of his open palm. 'Oh! Need I remind you that figure heads have both touched and held this title so pull back those grubby mitts and tell me exactly what you want?' asked the Las Vegas native, his face filtering with a rush of blood as he strains to retain the bags lifted status. 'Is there something the matter?!?' murmured the inquisitive fan. Gaz could only shake his head sideways. Looking over towards Natalia he notices the reluctance of the elevator's arrival, an elevator that would benefit him at this point in time. 'Hey-... So can I have your autograph then?' said the youngster bouncing about, trying to draw a little attention his way, even though the Jester concerns himself over the awaiting transportation of the elevator. 'Yeah - Yeah!' The boy brandished a small pre-printed piece of paper and indicated for Gaz Green to sign along a line found at the bottom right. Unbeknownst to the PCW superstar that he has signed along the dotted line of what appears to be a cheque made out for a small fee, a fee that undoubtedly come from his own pocket. '....G-A-Z.... G-R-E-E-N. How's that kid?' 'SUPER~!' bellowed the youth, ushering away from the 5' 11' frame of the Champion and stepping hotfooted from away from the crime scene. Disappearing subtly the young boy heads towards the exit when suddenly it dawns on Gaz Green that he has signed a substantial cheque for a sneaky juvenile who's taken advantage of his state of mind. Dropping the luggage in frenzy Gaz pursued the miniscule mastermind out into the City street. With a dense silence swamping the lobby, we hear the half-hearted pleads of the youngster, followed closely by that of a thunderous thud and the re-emergence of Gaz 'The Jester' Green. Brushing down the green tailored business suit, Gaz entered the lobby, lowering the cheque into the pocket of his jacket. Watched on by the public and their star struck expressions, the high-profiled celebrity merely lifted the heavy load once more and swept along the tiled flooring towards Natalia Miyagawa who enters the confines of the elevator. 'WWWWWWAAAAAAAIIIIIIIIIIITTTTTTTTTT!!!!!!!!!!!!' roared our hero, making one last ditched effort. Throwing his body mass and falling, literally inches away, Natalia offered a sympathetic one figured gesture before closing the door via the elevator's control panel. Pulling at his dyed green hair in frustration, the Jester snarled, 'WHY ME?!?' Composing himself with heavy breathing, the 226 lbs Gaz Green lifted his mass along with that of the bags and tackled the winding staircase, a daunting task on level par to that of Fusion's Main Event. Fighting through a door, bags swaying violently Gaz stopped in awe at the metallic structure of the stairs as they spiraled into obscurity, placing one foot onto the initial step, the Jester embarked on a single man operation. 'Will Gaz Green make it to his apartment and more importantly Friday Night Fusion?' 'Will The Jester's back heal in time to square off against Eddie Mandrake in the first round of the Cruiserweight Tournament?' 'And is Natalia's tantrums a result of marital separation or P.M.T?' - To be continued -
