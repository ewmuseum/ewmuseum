You know Dustin Downey, don't you? ::Snow has a blank look on his face.:: Chris: My former tag partner? ::Snow's blank looks continues.:: Chris: Former member of the Wild Deuces? ::Snow is still blank, Corey butts in, having the opportunity to see this from both men's points of view, Snow having no clue and not really caring either, whilst Chris, although despising Dustin, has to admit that he has been a big part of his career.:: Corey: I think what Chris is trying to say is that Dustin is that guy who used to be Boda's bitch until he finally did the ULTIMATE job to Vampir in being killed. ::Finally Snow seems to realise who Dustin is.:: Snow: Well why didn't you just say so? Damnit man, you've got to be specific when talking to someone of superior intellect! Chris: Actually, it was Clyde who killed him, sacrificed him I believe is the correct term. ::Corey shrugs.:: Corey: I don't know satanic jobbers, but what I do know is that in some freak accident of nature somehow Boda's bitchboy managed to make it back. Of course, it never really made sense to me. Chris: This is PCW, things don't need to make sense, did it make sense that Tyler Nelson beat up Kid Wonder just hours before they aligned with each other? Does it make sense that both Kramer Wild Wing AND Tim Walden have been TV champs despite being the two biggest jobbers in the entire federation? Does it make sense that despite all I've done to him, Xavier Kannon STILL seems to think he can beat me? Corey, this is PCW, things don't make sense and things are never consistent. The only one thing in PCW that makes sense is the fact that New Breed is and always will be the greatest stable in professional wrestling. We are the only thing consistent about PCW, as Meanstreaks and Jeremy Howards come and go. Take Timo Bolamba for example, the man that ran away, unable to face the fact that he was defeated by 'The Messiah' Chris Chase. Corey: You mean, the man that ran away because he knew he had no chance of beating me on Sunday. Chris: I mean what I say Corey, it was because he couldn't face me. Corey: No! It was because he wouldn't face ME! Chris: Once again you are mistaken my friend, for Timo left after I defeated him. Corey: No! Timo left after I reminded him of Russia. ::Chris goes to open his mouth to disagree once again, but is cut off by the casual, yet convicting words of one Killin' Villain.:: Snow: Actually, Timo left because he knew that no matter what he did he would NEVER be able to match my superior OR greatness. ::And that ends the argument. For Jason Snow's words are backed up, as always, by that belt he wears round his waist, shinier than his polished snakeskin boots. During the short pause whilst Chris and Corey's tempers cool down, Snow seems distracted.:: Snow: Umm.... are you sure he's.... OK? ::The camera zooms out to reveal Dustin running around in circles, chasing his pet Gerbil.:: Chase: Oh yeh, just leave him there, he'll be preoccupied for the next.... two hours. ::Snow nods, half scowling at Dustin's inferiority, whilst still slightly curious about how someone so inferior manages to live with themselves. He gives a quick prayer to himself for.... well.... for being himself and then moves on.:: Snow: Right next order of business, why is Stacey Sloboda such a slut? Chris: Good question, I blame the depression. Corey: What depression? Chris: The ones where her tits should be. ::Corey can't help but grin.:: Corey: Now THAT was controversial!.... But seriously, you gotta feel sorry for her, I mean imagine having to live with that slob Boda. Boda, a nobody, a nothing, the guy who has been main-eventing for nearly half a year and still hasn't managed to turn even one head. You make me sick Boda, I can't stand guys like you who can't even seem to create one ounce of controversy. You're not special, you're not unique, you're just some big guy who drinks a lot, big deal. You ain't what 'the people wanna see'. And it's no wonder you can't satisfy the fans, you can't even satisfy you woman! Snow: Exactly, talk about not being able to satisfy your woman. Snow: Yes, and how many non-superior men, well at least that's what they call themselves, in the main event? Chris: Nineteen. Snow: Right again! Now, Corey, how many times the strength of an ordinary man does a superior individual such as ourselves have? Corey: Ten. Snow: Yes, allow for the 'superiority factor', making sure to add on 5 for one especially superior individual... ::Chris rolls his eyes.:: Snow: ...And how many average men do this three superior beings add up to....... Dustin? ::Snow eyes Dustin, a little unnerved by the fact that Dustin is lying, face down, on the floor and not moving.:: Dustin: Umm... er.....uhhh..... Hamburger? Snow: No you stupid piece of sh- ::Chris coughs out loud, Snows eyes flicker to Chris and then back to Dustin, a little annoyed at having to be nice to a peasant for once..... well at least nicER.:: Snow: No Dustin, I'm afraid that was incorrect, but it was a good try. ::Snow speaks through gritted teeth, as Dustin sits up with a big dopey, inferior, grin on his face.:: Snow: Chris? Chris: Thirty-five. Snow: Right again Chris, go to the head of the class. ::Snow adds a degree of spite on that comment, to get Chris back for making him be nicER to Dustin.:: Snow: Now if thirty-five plays nineteen, who do you think is going to win? Snow's cold blue eyes giving Dustin's a penetrating gaze.
