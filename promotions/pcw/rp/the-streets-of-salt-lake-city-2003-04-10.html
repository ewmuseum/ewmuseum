The Streets of Salt Lake City

10 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4-10-03 “Brianna, Brianna, where for art though Brianna”, A.J. Trinidad said to himself as he sat drumming his thumbs on the counter next to his phone. Sitting in his rather normal Holiday Inn hotel room A.J. had left Amira at home for this trip to Salt Lake City, Utah, home of the Mormons, as well as the Winter Olympics some years back. Doubtful Trinidad would remember after the beatings and booze he’s endured since. “Well, apparently she’s not going to call.” He murmurs almost incoherently under his breath, “guess I’ll just find something to do tonight, try to stay out of trouble the night before our tag match against Skitzo and that fuck sauce a-hole Smoke. Trinidad leaves his jacket slung over the chair trusting his long sleeve Polo shirt to supply ample warmth on an evening that a man residing in Chicago for the better part of the last 4 years would consider balmy. Passing the front desk Trinidad pushes his way through the revolving door and slides his hands into the pockets of his black cargo pants before beginning to hum the melody to “Streets of Philadelphia” by the Boss, until the song actually kicks in. I was bruised and battered I couldn't tellwhat I feltI was unrecognizable to myselfI saw my reflection in a window I didn't knowmy own faceOh Brother are you gonna leave mewastin' awayOn the streets of Philadelphia “Well, Stryker made his motives clear on Tuesday night, although I’m not quite sure what those motives were.” Trinidad thinks to himself as the insanely long refrain plays through in the background. “He’s a man who obviously had something to prove, and maybe he is interested in what’s best for Crespo, but I doubt it. With Smoke’s grip tightening constantly on the Crespo situation I can’t help but fear it all growing stagnant, something has to happen soon or I might just shoot myself.” His stroll continues on through the mean streets of the Morman city, too bad the song was titles, “Streets of Salt Lake City”, this would have all been perfect. I walked the avenue 'til my legs felt like stoneI heard the voices of friends vanished and goneAt night I could hear the blood in my veinsBlack and whispering as the rainOn the streets of Philadelphia “A random tag match this week with Brianna Washington,” A.J. continues to keep his monologue within his inner thoughts. “Too bad it wasn’t Brianna Banks, now she’s hot. What am I thinking, I have to remain focused here, besides, Washington isn’t an ugly duckling herself. Skitzo is a hardcore brawler, or at least it seems from that belt around his waist, he will most likely concentrate on Ms. Washington, while Smoke plays the role of the huge pussy staying out of the match all together. God I loathe him sometimes, all the times actually, stupid stoner.” Ain't no angel gonna greet meIt's just you and I my friendMy clothes don't fit me no moreI walked a thousand milesJust to slip this skin “I knew I shouldn’t have eaten Taco Bell tonight, my stomach is doing hoola-hoops around my asshole right now. Fucking Salt Lake City, I’d rather be stuck in the back of a van full of nuns singing “Running with the Devil” on my way to something better.” The night has fallen, I'm lyin' awakeI can feel myself fading awaySo receive me brother with your faithless kissOr will we leave each other alone like thisOn the streets of Philadelphia “Weird how this song keeps playing, and I wonder where the hell it’s coming from. I mean, Bruce Springstein ruled, he’s the Boss, but why here, why now, what does it all mean? Great, now my fucking head hurts. Curtain jerking a show in Salt Lake City, Utah for a bunch of Mormans, what a life I’ve chosen here and I don’t even have my best friend to share it all with” A.J. shrugs to nobody in particular. “At least it’s better than not wrestling for Rush, or being Tim Walden” Trinidad laughs as the music softens and begins to play out before looking up and seeing the moon. 'I can't believe that song is about Rocky, although the movie was awesome, the song is a real downer.' On any other night, in any other town, one might be captivated by the glowing bluish colored orb, but tonight, on this night, in this town, as Trinidad stares out into the horizon having obviously walked passed the city limits, the only thought left in his mind was simple. “Where the fuck am I?”
