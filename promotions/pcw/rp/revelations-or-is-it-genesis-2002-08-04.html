Revelations or is it Genesis?

4 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Aug. 4, 2002 (The scene reopens in John Crichton motel living room area. Jaden is holding hostage the TV Champion/Boyfriend John Crichton for wanting to break up with her and her gray-haired grandmother for finally announcing to John her real age, 17. At this time, she also wants her granddaughter to move in with her; however, Jaden refused to go with her holding them hostage and threatening to kill her grandmother first for invading in her life.) “Oh, see I just feel like shooting you right now.” Jaden says, shaking her head in anxiousness. “You always got to ruin something. John would have never known I was 17 and I could have stayed and made a life with him. You stupid bitch!” John looks to Jaden’s grandmother as she sits on the couch weeping. He looks back at the distressed Jaden and begins to approach her slowly, hands lifted in the air. He is still a hostage he does not know what Jaden might do. She might just flip off shooting them and even herself. “Come on Jaden, give me the gun,” he says slowly walking toward her, now gun pointed dead at his forehead! “You really don’t want to do this. We still love you; I still love you. I just believe we need time to separate and work things out between us.” He says reaching for the vibrating gun, grabbing Jaden’s arm and hold it steady. He slowly snatches the gun out of Jaden’s weak hands and lays it upon the table next to the door where she is standing. He then reaches and grabs her in his arms holding her, hugging her, even though in his mind he wants to strangle and kill her. However, what John does not know is Jaden is working him. Jaden is a very smart girl. She might be young but she is very clever. She has been in the game for a while since she was young. When she was young that’s how she got her way either by throwing tantrums or by giving people or showing them what they wanted or sometimes flirting with them. As they continue to hug and cry on ones shoulder Jaden stares at her grandmother in her bag eyes burning her soul with hatered towards her. The grandmother can not do anything but look away because it brings more pain towards her. When Jaden was 10, she moved in with her grandmother because of her mothers obsessive drug habits. Jaden was not allowed to be a woman and live in a woman’s world she said because her grandmother had to many rules. All she wanted was for her grandmother to treat her like an adult although she was a child. What she did not know was that her grandmother was just protecting her from the world and from her own beauty, looks, and immaturity. Men from ages 18 and up thought Jaden was older than 10 from how she appeared to them. Jaden did not stop them from believing that either. She liked the attention. One night, when Jaden came in late, from a hot date, her grandmother confronted her. They got into a big argument where they literally fought each other. They both walked away from this fight with bruises and Jaden’s grandmother received bruises not only from the physical fight but the suffering Jaden caused her, her own granddaughter. After their fight, Jaden stormed out the house once more going to the police saying her grandmother at that time was 40 abused her as she fought back to protect herself. {{{{{~~*Seven Years Ago, looking back in Jaden’s Grandmothers Mind*~~}}}}} (The scene reopens in Baltimore, Maryland inside of Arlene Johnson, Jaden’s grandmother’s house. Arlene waits for her granddaughter in her well-known rocking chair that she always sits in. She positioned the tired and worn-out chair in front of the door awaiting her granddaughter arrival, holding a switch that she got out her rose garden. She firmly holds the switch in her hand. In addition, she leaves the thorns on for a serious whipping.) 11:21 PM The door sneakily opens and in creeps Jaden in the dark. Just then, the light flicker on with her grandmother staring her dead in her eyes. All Jaden and see is the bloody thorns on the switch from her grandmothers rose garden. “Where the hell have you been Jaden!” She says, as she rises from her chair. “Answer me damnit!” She begins to point the stick in her face, waving it back and forth. “I was out. On a date and what is it to your old crumbly ass.” She says, stepping up to her grandmother, getting all in her face, rolling her eyes. “You need to be worrying about your damn daughter, my mother. You cannot even raise you own children so how u expect to raise me. Move winch. She says pushing her grandmother back down in her rocking chair as she stomps up the steps with a hot attitude to set the whole house on fire. “Smelling like old English. I know she is crazy stepping in Rica Ebony Jaden Lopez’s face! That bitch had better sit her happy ass down somewhere before I have to knock her ass out showing her what’s real up and what the fuck I am really about! Shit” Jaden grandmother makes her way to the top of the steps call her back down. “What did you say! Little girl, you ain’t going to disrespect me in my house. Oh hell nah! Get your shit and get the fuck out!” “What!” She says. “I don’t got shit to get! And you going to stop talking to me like I am some damn child!” Jaden grabs her coat at the top banister and begins to head downstairs, making her way out the door. Her grandmother becomes outraged. She begins to swing the switch hitting Jaden in her unclothed back, not covered all the way from her “hoeish” outfit. Her outfits are either to small to revealing. Sometimes even both. From her swing, it leaves bright red marks on her back. Jaden let out a wail, turning around and knocking her grandmother to the ground. “What the hell is wrong with your crazy ass!” Jaden says, as she rapidly picks up the switch and begins to beat her grandmother repeatedly. She then throws the stick at her leaving her grandmother lying on the ground as a bloody and beaten pulp. ~//*\~//*\~//*\~//*\~//*\~//*\~//*\~//*\~ …Jaden continues to look at her tormented grandmother blowing her a kiss letting her know she has won, once more. Pulling Jaden from him and gripping her by her shoulder, he moves her to the kitchen table. “Now Jaden, you know I love you but I do not think it will be right for an older guy like myself going out with something so young; Not to mention your not even legal yet and we had sex. I could have gone to jail, maybe even worse. I think it would be best for right now that you just go live with your grandmother. Who knows when your 18 we might hook back up or something ok.” He leans in to kiss her and so does she, closing her eyes and puckering her lips for a last kiss goodbye; but to her amazement, he kisses her on the forehead. “Well I guess its goodbye!” He says. “Yea, right, goodbye!” She says rolling her eyes. “Do you need me to help you pack.” He asks trying to rush Jaden out the house but not trying to make it so obvious. He wanted to seem caring though. “No thank you Mr. Constantinople!” She says getting up from the table. “Huh? Why did you call me that?” “Well I don’t want to be impolite. You are a lot older than me remember!” “Yea I guess.” “This crazy bitch,” he thinks. “How did I ever get hooked up with her?” She is angry with John now and she is acting like a pure bitch. As she gets up from the table, she begins to strain her brain thinking of schemes and plans to get him back as she walks back into the bedroom. “Jaden I will meet you in the car. Bring your bags down stairs.” *Jaden’s thoughts* ~I wish this bitch would leave me the fuck alone and get out my life.~I aint stupid I know I have to take the bags to the car. ~*~ Well she was just reminding you!~Well all I gots to say is fuck that bitch! “Jaden, are you ok; you look lost can I help you with anything” John says. “No I am fine!” *Jaden’s thoughts* ~Oh, how I love him, but how do I get him back?~I am Jaden. I can think of something or twist his words around to benefit me. As Jaden goes to collect her clothes the telephones rings. It is one of John old pals. “Hey, how are you...hold on.” Moving the receiver from his mouth, “You should hold that bag from the bottom before it busts.” “Thank you so much Mr. Genius!” “Ok, go ahead what did you say!” *Jaden’s thoughts* ~Did I ask for his help, NO! If he don’t want the bag to bust then get over here and carry the shit, you stupid ass bitch! Jaden lugs the bags into the hall sitting beside the motel elevator. She sits out on the bench at rests her back while John continues to talk on the phone and her grandmother sits in the car waiting patiently for her, thinking how life will be with her back home. “Oh, you know she died. Yea, she died awhile back. Then I met someone new. No it’s not good. She is one crazy son of a…yea,” he begins to laugh at his misfortune. Just glad it is now over with now. He wanted to break up with Jaden awhile back but did not know how to go about doing so. “Wait, I didn’t tell you the best part though. She is only 17. I though she was 22. And that’s what she told me, too. Oh well! She is out my life now, THANK GOD!” Jaden gets up from her long rest break and continues to tote all her many bags again. Some she didn’t even know she had. “Yea, but I am sad she gone. I really do love her. I was sad to see it end. Yea I know. I think about her every night too! I will always remember her smile. Even throughout the ruff times I still loved her, but just looking at her mad made me still love her even more.” Jaden just happened to walk in on what he was saying to the person on the other end of the line! “Bingo,” she thought, “I am back in the game. He still loves me. I will give him about a day and he will want me back.” “John I am finish getting all of my stuff you can lock up behind me!” She says, grinning from ear to ear. *Jaden’s thoughts* ~My baby still loves me! “Hold on ok…oh ok, here I come!” John gets up from his relaxed position to meet Jaden at the door, ending his relationship with her with one last hug. *Jaden’s thoughts* ~Oh, I just want to touch his soft lips one last time.~No, do not do that you might make him reject you even more. Just be calm and cool! “I will miss you Jaden.” *John’s thoughts* ~Yea, whatever. Just get out my house so I can have someone new over! “Bye John” she says. She watches as he shuts the door behind him. She thinks that it will be a brighter tomorrow. He sees it as another door closed, another page in his book, and another relationship ending in death but with death someone is born into the world another time, “John.” He is free from his death, “Jaden.” He rushes back to the telephone. “Hello, yea I am back. Oh how good does it feel to be single again.” “Huh, I thought you said you loved Jaden and you think about her at night?” “Hell no, I might think about getting some though. I was talking about Aeryn.” He begins to laugh putting up his feet and just relaxing. “You are crazy.” To Be Continued…
