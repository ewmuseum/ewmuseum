Ellie~!...This Sure Isn't Kansas City!

28 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 28/2/02 Xavier’s route to the last PCW PPV, Shang-Haied was very stressful. After being lost in the Colombian woods running from a Drug Dealing Scientologist afraid that Xavier was going to ruin his business, he only managed to find his way out with the aid of Aussie snake master Steve Irwin and even then he lost his bearings as it turned out he was in Malaysia. It was here that he discovered a Malaysian pretending to be L Ron Hubbard and stealing Xavier’s thunder in brining Scientology to the Far East. This time however, his arrival at March Madness has been much smoother. With PCW sticking to North America for it’s dates, World travel has been eliminated, reducing the complications due to missed flights and language clashes. Right know Xavier and his valet Ellie~! are checked into their Kansas City hotel ready to appear at Fusion if needed, just sitting back and relaxing, getting in the right state of mind for his 2nd PPV, and first glimpse at the World Title. Surely being so close to the March Madness arena is St Louis means that nothing could go wrong for Xavier? He could drive 250 miles in any direction and still be able to make it back before the sun set, no Oceans or Seas to cross, no continents to travel to.....But then again, his is an Asylum member, so let us not take anything for granted shall we..... The shot opens up showing Black. We keep with this for a few seconds before some light starts to force it’s way into the picture near the middle, flashing back to Black briefly before the light starts to turn into a blurry Black and White image as he light grows. Are we witnessing some kind of dimensional tear? No, we are looking through the early morning eyes of PCW’s resident Scientologist Xavier Kannon. He blinks again the appears to run his eyes and then as the image becomes less blurry we see what it is. Xavier : “AAAAAARRRRRRRRRRRGGGGGGGGGGGGHHHHHHHHHH!” As we cut to a 3rd person view we see Xavier sprawled on a snow laden floor, trying to get hurriedly to his feet but struggling through lack of grip. Scurrying off to flee his yell are 3 penguins who dive down into a poll of water. Xavier : “Where in Hubbard’s name.....Which is Hubbard....Am I?” He starts to brush the snow off his full length brown leather coat. Under it he has a Black v-neck jumper with a Grey round neck t-shirt under it and a pair of his usual Black boot-cut jeans with Green stitching and intricate Purple embroidery down the left leg to where they are tucked into a pair of brown arctic boots. As soon as he gets to his feet he starts shivering and pulls his jumper’s neck up to keep the wind from his lips. Eleanor : “Wow, 1st that Russian guy and now you......What is it with wrestlers and Penguins?” His valet Eleanor Hall then appears on the picture wearing a large Brown sheepskin coat with faux fur cuffs and hood trimming. Under it is a roll neck White jumper and a pair of Blue, flared, sandblasted jeans with a pair of Black boots. Her long, wavy brown hair with Blond highlights is neatly tied back and falls inside her large hood. Xavier : “Ellie~!? Where are we? This doesn’t look like Kansas City!” He looks around his surroundings and sees only snow, ice, freezing cold water and snow laded mountains. Ellie : “You don’t remember last night do you?” His valet looks at him with a curious expression on her face, wondering why the Scientologist doesn’t know the reason behind his location. Xavier : “Well so far it’s ranking up there with the time the Rush woke up next to a baby Horse on the ‘How The Hell Did I End Up Here?’ scale.....Where is this, and wh-....How did we get here?” The Gold Patron Meritorious spins on the spot, looking at the featureless surroundings that run for miles in each direction around him before turning to Ellie~! and raising his eyebrows to try and coax and answer from her. Eleanor: “The hotel......Dinner? Your idea.......You know, to get ready for your match? You know?” Kannon doesn’t seem to know what she is talking about as he tries to tuck his hands into the ends of his sleeves and Ellie~! pulls her hood up, both to cope with the huge gust of icy wind that sweeps through them. Xavier : “If I knew how I got here don’t you think I’d be back on it and flying back to America where....I don’t know...I have a World Title shot....And it is above freezing!” Ellie~! scowls back to him, upset at the aggravated tone of his voice as he screwed up his piercing Green eyes. Eleanor: “No need to shout at me Mr Angry Snowman! It wasn’t my idea to come out here, I wanted to watch Popular!” The Scientologist sighs and starts to rub his hands together to get warmth before holding them to his face and breathing into them, trying to spread some heat to his Red face. Xavier : “I’m sorry my little Shag Squirrel, I just want to know how I ended up in Antarctic so close to March Madness!” Eleanor : “See! You know it is the South Pole! It’s coming back to you!” Xavier : “No, I got that from the Penguins.” He gestures around to the crowd of penguins that are on the same sheet of ice as them, huddling together and rotating positions for warmth. Eleanor : “Well, it goes a little something like this....” As she begins Xavier stands shivering, wondering why she seems so content in the cold temperatures. Eleanor: “So we were at the hotel, that buffet thing was one so you decided to stack your plate full to help build up for that thing this weekend that you’ve been talking about a lot. Then after and hour or so, you vomited, then you went all Red, your eyes went glassy, you started mumbling about stuff, I heard Chris Chase’s name so stopped listing at that point...” The Asylum member’s jaw drops and his eyes widen as he is filled in on the details of the night before, which goes towards explaining how her got to this remote part of the Earth. Eleanor : “So when I got back about half an hour later you had threw up some more....We need to talk about your Bulhimia, Lady Di had it and I read about it so I am an expert....Anyway, you were rambling on about going somewhere, 1st off you wanted to go into space and see if you really could see the Great Wall of China, then you wanted to jump off the top of the hotel to see if you could fly, and then you said we should go to the South Pole!” About 10 seconds of silence passes as Xavier just stares at his valet who is getting uncomfortable with his glare. Eleanor : “Um....Hello? Rude to stare you know!” His valet puts her hands on her hips and sneers at him for looking at her. Kannon looks down to the snow at where he was laying, trying to get any recollection of the night before. Xavier : “THE PRAWNS!” His outburst shocks Ellie~! who slips and falls back into the show with a dull thud as the Scientologist begins pacing around, gesturing with his hands as he runs through his memories in his head. Xavier : “I should have never trusted the Seafood at that damn Hotel! They had probably been sitting there for 2 weeks, no wonder I was vomiting so had I nearly turned in side out!” Eleanor : “Nice image...” Ellie~! pulls herself out of the snow and whines as she brushes the snow off her coat. Xavier : “In my hallucinations I must have mentioned coming here like you said, well, I suppose it’s better then going into space or leaping off the roof....Wait....Why didn’t you stop me?!?!?!?!” Eleanor : “I didn’t want to disrupt your creative flow.” Xavier looks to the dull Grey sky and sighs as Eleanor looks at him, wondering what the problem is. Eleanor : “Well Soh-Reeee! It’s just he ideas you have been having lately were just all so...Well, EUGH! So I chose not to stop you....I thought it was for the best!” The Preppy valet explains to Xavier, trying to reason her way out. Xavier : “Well how did we get here? If I know how I got here we can get back early, maybe still in time for Friday Fusion....So?” Ellie~! takes a moment to think back to the night before and retrace their steps from the buffet to the South Pole. Eleanor : “Well, we got another Taxi from the Hotel, 1 a bit more sane than the last couple....Then we flew down to South Africa, into Johannesburg, then we hired a private plane to one of the small port villages on the coast, then we came to here.....Then you fell asleep.” Xavier : “Wait a minute....” The Scientologist reveals something that has been troubling him since he work up to see a happy and content Eleanor standing above him. Xavier : “How come you aren’t frozen my little Poon Puppy? We must have been sleeping here for hours!” Eleanor : “Oh, I took the Snowmobile to the little hotel I saw a few miles East and stayed in one of their rooms.....I hope you don’t but it’s on your card....Here’s your wallet back...” She hands him back his money and credit cards as he stares at it, puzzled. Eleanor : “They have these big open fires, people bringing you hot drinks all the time, then in the morning you get this huge breakfast and look....They gave me this coat!” Xavier : “So you left me in the snow, took my wallet, and got yourself a warm, cosy, heated room?” Eleanor : “Well I didn’t want to disturb you!” He shakes his head and starts to walk away from her and out across the White landscape, muttering in an agitated voice under his breath. Eleanor : “Um......That’s West.....The hotel is this way!” She points off in the opposite direction to which Xavier is walking, both directions looking identical. Eleanor : “I think.....Wait, does the sun set in the North or South?” The Scientologist ignores her and continues to trey across the Antarctic wasteland then suddenly cries out in pain, holding his foot and hopping up and own on he spot before eventually slipping and flying face first, spread eagled into the snow where he rolls about clutching his toes. Xavier : “Owwwwwwww!” Eleanor : “Oh, you found the Snowmobile, I was wondering where I left that!” Ellie~! skips over towards Xavier who is screwing his face up, wincing in pain at his soon to be bruised toes, looking towards Ellie~! with a shocked face. Xavier : “What are you doing burying our only way out of this place?!?!?!?!?!?!” Kannon sighs and drops back into the snow, wondering how he ended up in this situation and cursing the species known as the Prawn. Eleanor : “Well the rental place charges a pretty big deposit....That reminds me, it’s on your card as well.....So I didn’t want it to get stolen!” The Gold Patron Meritorious gestures to the featureless expanse that stretches towards the horizon all around them. Xavier : “Who is going to steal it Ellie~!? A Penguin, a Seal......A POLAR BEAR?” Eleanor : “They live at the North Pole....” Xavier : “I KNOW.....I was making a point that a bear is more likely to find it’s way down from the furthermost place on the planet to here and steal our Snowmobile than anyone within 5 miles of us stealing it!” Ellie~! doesn’t look to happy with the tone of Xavier’s voice, annoyed that her being conscientious has angered him. Eleanor : “What.....EVER! Anyway, are we going to go back to Kansas now? It’s a little nippy.” Kannon finally pulls himself up out of the show, still favouring his foot as he brushes the snow off the vehicle with his frozen hands. Xavier : “Yes, I think it would be a very good idea to get out of this, because I am tending to agree with you on the temperature thing...So lets go....You do remember the way we came don’t you?” Eleanor : “Um....The White way?” The Gold Patron Meritorious sighs as he tries to get the engine started with Ellie~! climbing onto the back behind him. Suddenly she points to something to their left. Eleanor : “Look, a Polar Bear!” Xavier quickly turns his head in a slit second to look before bowing it down and shaking it and then speeds off into the distance as the shot fades to Black. Eleanor : “Got ya....Yay Me!!!” To Be Continued....
