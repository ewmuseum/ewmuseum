Walking Around Milwaukee

25 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2-25-02 --- The segment begins with Ganker Ransovler walking down the streets of Milwaukee, hands in the pockets of his black leather jacket. Ganker is also wearing baggy blue jeans, and a plain black t-shirt. Ganker looks at the newpaper that just swooshes through the wind and whacks him in the face. He reads and it says, 'Major PCW Upset, LeBlanc wins PCW Extreme Championship.' Ganker laughs as he throws it back in the wind. Ganker walks by a television repair shop and looks at the black and white televisions replaying Sunday Sabotage. Ganker stands there awhile and watches Sabotage. Ganker rubs his chin as he he is intrigued. The current match is Red Rock vs Lance Bell. Of course Ganker knows the outcome already, but watches anyway, to learn a little bit more about Red Rock's wrestling style. Ganker then points at the screen then breaks out laughing. Ganker grins as Lance Bell picks up the win and the Rising Star Championship. Ganker then continues his walk as he comes onto a small PCW shop open late. Ganker enters to see all kinds of PCW merchandise for sale. Ganker nods in approval as he picks up a Hank poster wearing the PCW Extreme Title in one shot, and in another with the title over his shoulder, the final shot he looks at is Hank pinning CCW to win the PCW Extreme Championship. Ganker then picks up a white slip of paper that is labeled Friday Fusion and is marked March 1, 2002. He reads it down and reveals that it is the announced card for this weeks Friday Fusion. Ganker reads it down to reveal he is facing Legend Inc. Ganker's face forms a sly grin as he lays the card down and begins to pace back and forth through the shop. Ganker then picks up a poorly made Team Nirvana action figure pack and sees that no time was even taken to make his figure. Ganker then puts it down and grabs a LeBlanc t-shirt and blows his nose on it as he looks up to reveal PCW title replicas on sale. He walks over to register where a red headed babe stands looking damn sexy. Woman: May I assist you? (looking down) Ganker: Yeah, I'd like one of those PCW Cruiserweight Championship belt replicas, please. Woman: Sure, give me one second. (looks up) You're... You're... (choked up) Ganker Ransolver of PCW~! Hank's tag team partner~! Ganker: No, soon to be #1 Contender at the PCW Cruiserweight Championship. Woman: Yeah, you're great. (lusting glare) Ganker: I have a girlfriend, her name is Ana Lagunez. Woman: I know, but don't you want me? Ganker: Nah, I'd really like my PCW Cruiserweight Championship replica, at least until I win the real thing that is. Woman: Alright, I'll get it for you. --- She grabs a ladder and intentiall slowly climbs up it as slowly as she possibly can, and as sexy as she can. She grabs the Cruiserweight Championship and gets down and hands it to Ganker and walks over back to the register and punches a couple a buttons. Woman: You're total is $200.00. --- Ganker flips out a wad of hundreds and hands over $200.00 to the lady and leaves. Ganker then continues to walk down the sidewalk as he comes along Ana sitting on a park bench with a couple of kids hanging around her. Ganker walks over and sits by her and places his arm around her. Ganker: Hey babe, who they kids? Ana: Obviously you're not the only one with a fan base around here. They heard I was your valet somehow and they wanted to wait with me until you showed up to meet me. Ganker: Autographs I take it? Kid: No, I wanted just to hangout in the park withyou. Girl: Actually our dad left us here for a minute with Ana to get us a couple tenty ounces of Pepsi. Ganker: That's nice, but how bout some autographs jus' for the hell of it? Kid: (grins) Sure! --- Ganker is handed a photograph from his woman and he signs it and hands it to the kids. Their father returns and thanks them and hands his kids their Pepsi he got them. Ana: So, you are triple booked this entire week, I mean first it's AFL-CIO, hat's tomorrow night. Then you take on Legend Inc, and then you have a shot to become the number one contender at the PCW Cruiserweight Championship. You could do what took Hank two years to do, win a PCW Championship title belt. Ganker: First I'll have to go through and win my match at the Pay-Per-View, erm, March Madness. It's in St. Lous thought right babe? Ana: Yeah, it is. Ganker: Good, for a second there I thought I was not too sure. Savvis Center Arena too! Damn I'm on smart bastard! Ana: Do you know who's involved in this match with you? Ganker: Red Rock and a couple of other unworthy freaks of nature. Ana: If I'm correct, you and Red Rock had beef in your NZW days. Ganker: Yes and that's not a bad thing... (grins) That's a Good thing and unlike Mr. Caps Diamond Dallas Page, my pearly whites are all real... Maybe not all, just the two in the front, the rest are caps... Ana: (laughing) Oh stop it. --- Ana slaps Ganker's leg in a playful manner. Ganker: I'd like to let Red Rock into a little secret of mine though. Ana: What's that babe? Ganker: That this Sunday, March Madness, I will be bringing him back to the old NZW days when Team Nirvana kciked the AKR's a** from pillar to post. We beat his little team of the A** Kickin' Rebels for the tag team titles, from pillar to post. AKR were nothin' to Team Nirvana and this Sunday... March Madness, Red Rock may be my biggest competition and I might even be considered to be the underdog in this match, but what yo always have to remember Ana, I always have somethin' goin' for me. I mean I am Ganker Ransolver and everybody knows that, (thumb thing) Ganker Is God~! (Ana smiles) Yo liked that didn't yo Ana? (Ana nods) Well who wouldn't when Ganker Ransolver does it? I mean, (thumb thing) Ganker Is God~! Ana: Red Rock doesn't stand a chance. Ganker: Yeah, he's my toughest competition in the match, well, (laughs) if that's the case, I damn well f**kin' like my damn f**kin' odds of pickin' up this damn f**kin' win and the #1 Contendership at the Cruiserweight Championship. I am a former RingSide Entertainment Cruiserweight Champion and the best one at that. Ana: Wasn't he Triple Crown UWF Champion? World, Hardcore, Cruiserweight? Ganker Yeah, so? Who cares about the UWF? That fed a slow run federation with third rate wrestlers. Red Rock was there, I mean that speaks volumes to me bout how it was then. Ana: Weren't you there then too? Ganker: (grins) Oh yeah! I was now wasn't I. So that mean si know how bad UWF was then too, hell I know how bad Red Rock was then too. I mean I had to carry him throughout so many damn matches it wasn't even funny, yo know Ana, Red Rock was worthless then and I don't think much has changed since his UWF days, so he's still worthless pile of cow dung now. It'll be my pleasure to beat his a** like old times, yo know why? (thumb thing) Ganker Is God~! Ana: I'll be right there rooting you on baby. Ganker: I know yo will baby. --- With those words said, the scene now fades out.
