The Proof is in the PICTURE? MOM? part 2 of last one

23 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/23/2002 Natalie: It all started about 24 years ago. A man came to town........ The scene opens back in the Anaheim Memorial Hospital that Natalie Miyagawa is recovering in. Only, if you remember right, she’s not talking to her husband, or the doctors.... no, she’s talking to her long lost older brother, Boda. Yes, the enemy of her husband, Boda. Yes, the very man that almost ended her life, and hospitalised her for the past two months, Boda. But, why would she be talking with him? To reveal the truth. So, there we have it, Boda sitting next to the woman he hospitalised. Next to his PPV opponents wife. Sitting there, staring at a woman, who claims to be off his blood. Natalie continues to tell the story Natalie: You see, my mom lived in a poor Russian city, outside of the main providence that is Moscow. Well, seems a man, from Czechoslovakia came to town quite a bit. He was working with the military, but apparently from what my mom said, it was very top secret. Boda: Okay, so what has that got to do with me? Natalie: Well, you are Czech, are you not? Boda: Yeah. My father is from Czechoslovakia. He came to America to make money, and start a family. He worked as a business man for 30 years, till he was arrested for tax evasion. I was all of 6 then. That’s really the last time I saw him. When I was 6. Sure, we visited him at times, but I don’t remember him really. Natalie shakes her head, her pretty blue eyes shining in the fluorescent lights of the room. She sits up, straining to do so. She touches Boda’s face. Natalie: Not so. You do not have to lie to me, brother. You see, I am your sister. My mother met your father, while he was stationed in Russia. He worked for the Russian government, a spy in the Cold War. Well, he bedded my mother, and I was born 9 months later. But, by then, father was arrested by the US government, and I have never seen him since. So, when my mother married Boris, I was about 2, and I took his last name. Boda: No way. I don’t believe you. Natalie: Here, look. Natalie produces a picture, and hands it to Boda. He takes it, and stares at it. Boda looks at the picture, then up at Natalie. A smile of sorts crosses his face. Boda hands the picture back to Natalie. Boda lets out a sigh Boda: Yeah... that’s my dad... I mean our dad. There is an awkward moment, just then. Here is Boda, the PCW monster, the man that caused this woman so much pain, talking to her, and finally finding out the truth. Pictures do tell a thousand stories, but this 8 by 10 only had to tell one. Boda is indeed the brother of Natalie Miyagawa. Of course, this is made more awkward by the following from Natalie Natalie: Why did you do this to me? To my baby? To Alex? Boda: If I knew you were my sister, this would never of happened. Miya stole my World Title, and I got angry. It was all business. Just like Turmoil this week.... ALL BUSINESS. Of course, Boda is referring to the match on Turmoil against Frisco, Legend Inc, and the biggest asshole in the business.... Tyler Nelson. Boda stands, and looks down at his newly found sister. Natalie: What is it brother? Boda: Nothing. Listen, I have to run. I’ve got something to figure out, including us. I’ve got to let my mother know about this too. Natalie: She already does. Boda: WHAT? Natalie: She knows about our father’s adultery. She knows he was not pure. But, what could she do? She was married to a Czech who worked for the Russian government. She was afraid. Boda: Wait..... I’ve known my mother for 28 years. You’ve been in my life for about a month. How do you know about what my mother knows, or feels even? Boda is getting visibly angry at this point. Suddenly, there is a knock on the hospital room door. Natalie: Ask her yourself. Boda turns, and stands in front of his mother. She hugs Chris, but he kinda of shy’s away. She walks over, and gives Natalie a hug. Boda: Okay... this is getting as weird as a Springer show. Mom. What the fuck are you doing here? Mother Boda: Nice language. Don’t curse in front of your mother. You know better Chris. Now, I flew out here, because I knew this day would come, especially after you almost KILLED HER. Mother Boda smacks her bigger son in the back of the head. Boda snarls, then cracks his neck Mother Boda: Listen son.... this isn’t your little wrestling world anymore. Don’t play the tough guy here, especially with me. Boy, I changed your diapers, and I know your manhood ain't that big. Boda: MOM, shut up. There are cameras filming this. This is for my little wrestling world. Mother Boda looks around, and sees the cameras Mother Boda: Oops. Sorry. Well, Chris.... I’ll be down stairs. You look hungry, and I am starving, so lets go get some lunch, and we will talk some more about all this that I have tried to hide from you for 28 years. Boda: Ok. I’m in the mood for Sushi. I know this little sushi bar on the southside. Lets go. Bye Natalie. Mother Boda: I’ll be right down, son. Let me talk to your sister a moment. Boda: Whatever. Natalie: Bye, brother. Boda exits Natalie’s room 420 at the Anaheim Memorial Hospital. He heads back to the elevators, and climbs on, after the doors open. Boda climbs in, and hits the lobby button, as the door shut. Down the elevator car glides, finally stopping at his final destination. As Boda steps out into the hospital lobby, he sees a seat, for to wait on for his mother. The big former PCW World Champion, sits, and leans back, pondering the upcoming match at Turmoil, and the weirdness that just went down. So, Tyler thinks I am not worthy of being the PCW Champion again. Says I was merely a jobber, that took the belt to new lows. Fuck him. Who the hell does that bottom dweller think he is? CEO of PCW? Who the hell does that impress? Not me. Sure, he’s CEO, that means he’s got a few, maybe 4 or 5 personal bitches that will suck up to him, kiss his ass, and hand him the World Title. Well, I got news for Ultimate Authority.... you’ll have to KILL me first. Sure.... Nelson, you’re a player.... you’ve got the stroke..... but, I’ve got the game. Frisco.... Legend Inc. BAH. Those two are the bottom of the barrel. Nelson, you’re what you get when you look UNDER the bottom of the barrel. So, Turmoil, I regain that little step towards greatness, AGAIN. Oh, and if New Breed tries to decide this, let them. I’ll make an example out of them this time around. As for PPV time.... yes... finally, Boda vs Miya. Signed, sealed and delivered. Time to send my brother in law packing to his wife. The elevator bings, as Mother Boda steps out, and into the lobby. Chris stands up, and walks to his mother, and takes her bags. The two head out the revolving doors, and into the parking lot as the scene fades out.
