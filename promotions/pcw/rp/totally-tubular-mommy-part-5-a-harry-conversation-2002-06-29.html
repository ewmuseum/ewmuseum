Totally Tubular Mommy - Part 5 - A 'Harry' Conversation

29 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: June 29 [The scene opens up in the backyard of 'Totally Tubular' Tommy's home. The backyard is really nothing special, just a beautiful in ground swimming pool that is shaped like a surfboard and a small cemented area for partying. A deck from the second floor hangs over part of the yard which has been used to do man second floor cannonballs into the pool. One time Harry missed the pool and went face first into the grass. He was all right but he forgot how to do multiplication afterward] ['Heavy Metal' Harry holds the long, netted pole in his hand as he cleans out leaves and dead bugs from the pool. It's not his job but for some reason that cool net at the top of the pole just looked really interesting. As he picks up a dead fly he realizes that there is something clogging the filter just below him. He puts the long pole in between his legs and reaches down to grab the object. As he's doing that a group of very beautiful, tanned, and half naked women walk by the back yard. The low fence allows beach patrons to get a look inside the yard of the owner of the beach. The women notice the pole in between Harry's legs and they giggle to each other. Harry immediately hears the giggling and looks up to the women. He smiles brightly and wave sat them like a little child would do. The women laugh even more pointing to his crotch. Harry looks down and realizes the awkward situation, that really isn't that awkward to him] 'Heavy Metal' Harry: This isn't an exaggeration, man!! [The women all look to each other and begin laughing even harder. Harry just keeps nodding with a smile, not really picking up on the fact that the women are laughing AT him, not WITH him] Harry: It's a dangerous weapon in certain countries, man. [The women keep laughing as they walk away. Harry doesn't really understand how any woman could walk away from THIS. He then grabs the pole and realizes that it's the pool skimmer, and not his penis] Harry: ... they didn't know that, man... [As Harry goes back to his skimming the gate to the yard opens up. Harry immediately lifts his head up looking for the far out chicks that just passed by. He is met by disappointment when the person coming through is Tommy's mom. She has her shoulder length blonde hair pulled back in a pony tail as she wears a black T-shirt that is tucked into her white daisy duke shorts. Even in very casual wear Tommy's mom looks absolutely beautiful and sexy. She just has that something about her, and it's very unfortunate that she is a raging slut] Tommy's mom: Has my son recovered from the fight that we had? Harry: No, man. [Tommy's mom sighs, upset about that night at the pool hall] Harry: Do you think it's weird if you masterbate with a condom on, man? [Tommy's mom stares at Harry with pure confusion and disgust on her face] Tommy's mom: Just a little weird, Harry. Harry: Well, my hands were really dirty and I didn't want to get one of those diseases, man. Tommy's mom: Why didn't you just wash your hands? Harry: OHH YEAH, man!! I could have done that. [Tommy's mom sighs to herself, truly believing that Harry is his father's son even though he hardly knew the man] Tommy's mom: Are you even ready for this big match at Gold and Glory? I know Tommy has been working his hardest to get in shape for this thing. A friend of mine said she saw him at the gym yesterday for like three hours. Your just here cleaning the pool. [Harry makes a muscle, which produces nothing but a small amount of flab. He then uses his other hand to push the flab up into a muscle] Harry: See, I've got muscle, man. I don't need to work out. [Tommy's mom takes a seat on one of the lounge chairs, placing her purse on the ground. She then pulls out a box of cigarettes and lights one up as Harry curls the skimmer as if it's his daily workout] Harry: One, man. Two, man. Three, man. Tommy's mom: Harry, I've got a confession I need to make to you. [Harry keeps curling the skimmer] Harry: Ok, man. Tommy's mom: I left the pool hall and immediately went looking for any man to sleep with. Harry: You could have slept with me, man. I'm pretty good. [Tommy's mom shows a very large look of disgust] Tommy's mom: Ugh. I didn't sleep with a man but I did go looking for specific men. I went looking for The Heartbreakers. Harry: Why? Tommy's mom: Well, because of this whole upcoming tag team match I wanted to get a little revenge on my son for embarrassing me like the way he did. I went searching for Jack Valentine and Johnny Heart so that I could let them double team me, because lets face it, they are extremely attractive men. I was going to bring them here to show Tommy my prizes and see how he really liked it. Harry: Why didn't you, man? [She takes a long drag from the cigarette] Tommy's mom: Well...I couldn't find them... Harry: Cool, man. Tommy's mom: Since I couldn't find them I went looking for old rivals of his...namely ones that he would really have a fit if he found out about it. Politically Incorrect. Harry: WHAT?! Those guys are not far out, not far out at all, man. Tommy's mom: I figured since they were politicians that they wouldn't mind a nice, sexy, and successful intern working for them. Unfortunately, I couldn't find them either. Then I went looking for The Pretty Cool guys because they are cute men but I couldn't find them. I couldn't find any of those men, but I needed to have sex. I need to have sex right now! [Harry smiles and points to the giant pole] Harry: It's all gravy baby. [As she takes a drag of her cigarette she makes another look of disgust, not at the cigarette but at the fact Harry has proposed himself to have sex with her] Tommy's mom: I've come to a revelations, Harry and it's something that I should have done a long time ago. I'm going to stop having sex with strangers. If I see a good looking man I'm not going to just jump on him and take him home with me, I'm going to get to know him better. I'm going to actually talk to guys, get to know them A LOT before I even offer myself to them. I know I've made some pretty dumb mistakes in my life, Harry, and this isn't going to wash every bad thing I did to Tommy and you away. [She drops the cigarette on the ground and crushes it beneath her foot. She stares at Harry for what seems to be minutes. Neither of them makes a move as Harry studies her with close eyes. Tommy's mom gets a sense that maybe he's sizing her up for sex or something, but he has such a confused look on his face to be doing that. He's thinking about something, or at least trying to think about something] Harry: I think that your going to be able to do it, man. [Tommy's mom seems extremely curious] Tommy's mom: What makes you say that? Harry: Because you didn't jump up and have sex with me in the pool, man. I don't think any woman would pass that chance up. [Tommy's mom shows that look of disgust once again. This time it really makes her feel ill, almost like she wants to throw up] Tommy's mom: You know, I think your right. I will make it through this. [Harry nods with a smile, but disappointed that she didn't have sex with him in the pool] Tommy's mom: Do you think that you guys will win the tag team titles? [Harry shrugs, going back to his skimming duties] Harry: I don't know, man. Tommy's mom: Just not sure of yourself or just not trying to be overconfident? Harry: It doesn't really matter to me if we win or lose, man. We get to travel around the United States and the world wrestling. The only thing I'm really good at is wrestling so all of this really makes me happy, man. So, win or lose I'm not really going to be upset or let down at all. I just want to go out and do my best and see what happens, man. [For some reason that makes Tommy's mom smile] Tommy's mom: Harry...there is something that I really need to tell you. Harry: What's that, man? Tommy's mom: I'm your mother. [Harry keeps skimming as if the statement didn't bother him at all. He then looks up at Tommy's mom, and susposedly his mom, without a thought. Harry then goes back to skimming, then looks back up at MOM, and then goes back to skimming. He then drops the pole in the water and looks at MOM for the third time] Harry: WHAT?!?!?! Tommy's mom: I'm your biological mother, Harry. I had sex with your father. [Harry's eyes are bugging out of his head] Harry: That's far out, man....my dad had sex with you!!!! RIGHT ON, MAN!! [The scene then fades to black]
