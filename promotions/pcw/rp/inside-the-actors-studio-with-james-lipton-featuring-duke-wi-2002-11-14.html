Inside the Actors Studio with James Lipton featuring Duke Williams

14 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Title: Inside the Actors Studio with James Lipton featuring Duke Williams Featuring: Duke Williams Date: 11/14/02 The theme to inside the actors studios plays as the camera enters the dark set with James Lipton, Dean of the Actors Studio Drama School at The New School University sitting in one chair as the host and Duke Williams sitting in the other. James: Evening and welcome to inside the actors studios I’m James Lipton. Tonight we have a bizarre guest of sorts who uses his classically trained acting skills to dazzle and entertain in a brut fashion not on the stage or the film but rather the wrestling ring. He is the incomparable star of PCW he is a dear friend he is Elwood Duke Hiram Williams or as some of you may know him as “The Malice Man or “Dukestar The Goblin”. Welcome Duke. -Duke who is dressed in black wranglers and a black turtle neck shirt takes a sip of water and smiles.- Duke: Thanks James. James: How does one go from acting to wrestling? Duke: Lots of cocaine and coffee. -The place erupts at the mention of the piss poor joke.- Duke: Both sever the purpose of entertainment. No matter how you use your skills the out come is still the same and that’s to leave the audience with a good show. James: That is spot on sometimes I think we forget that. Duke: I think a lot of times actors forget that with the garbage come out at the theaters these days. James: HA HA HA another accurate statement. In the promo entitled “No Sex Makes For a Fighting Mad Malice Man” You astonish us with a tale of parenting from the point of view of a man know for cracking skulls. Very blue collar in your delivery is that something you aim at. Duke: Yes I believe that is my audience and I think when I portray the Malice Man I represent them and do things they cannot. Its an empowering task and I enjoy it immensely. James: In that same piece of promo poetry you start out with this quote “America, you think it’s easy being Duke Williams??? I’m telling you people that it ain’t.” How true is that statement? Duke: What, compared to the guy who busts his ass working for minimum wage at Wal-Mart? Shit James we have it easy. James: That sounds like a retort your wrestling alternative would respond with. How similar are you to the characters you portray Duke: I think 100% of everything I give has elements of myself in them. Duke Williams is more or less Elwood Williams. Well maybe Duke isn’t as big of a womanizer as me. -The audience and James crack up as Duke takes a sip of water.- James: We’ll talk about that and your turn to a nasty piece of work in the wrestling world when we return on Inside the Actors Studio with Duke Williams, I’m James Lipton. -The two men talk off camera as they cut to a break. They shortly return to the in-depth suck up fest that Inside the Actors Studio is.- James: We had a question from the audience for you. What exactly is a hoss? -Duke laughs.- Duke: If you don’t know what it is you probably aren’t one anyway. It’s a subculture term for another person. Its southern slang like a dude, a man, or a biznitch. I first heard it in a Waylon Jennings song. James: He truly is one of your major influences isn’t he? Duke: His attitude, demeanor, and his persona, is everything I try to be. A lot of people don’t realize he’s the guy who brought Buddy Holly’s sound to country music. He also brought attitude to the straight world of country music. He told them to f*ck off and did it his way. James: Who are some of your other mentors? Duke: As far as acting I try to emulate the way Pacino delivers a line with such dominance and authority. He could read penthouse forum and you’d be enthralled. Waylon as far as attitude and the way I want to live my life. John McCain for speaking his mind and telling it like it is. Randy Savage for being a great friend and wonderful athlete. Walter Payton for just being Sweetness. James: Yes wonderful. April of this year you had a wonderful feud with a future comrade Xavior Kannon. Duke: Yes that was a wonderful feud where we got to dive into scientology and really parody it. James: You enjoy making others laugh. Duke: Yes and I love to screw the ladies also. James: Is a good feud like a good performance. Duke: You can cut a great promo, an ingenious in card skit, or wrestle a great match. If its one sided no matter how good nobody will remember it. If both sides put up great work then excellence is reached and everybody remembers it forever. James: So unlike film where one person can carry a piece of visual art in wrestling it’s a two-part job. Duke: It is. Yes. James: Shortly after the feud Kannon went on to win the world title and you quickly joined him as a devilish heel. Duke: Yea if you can’t beat them join them. James: Is it tough to go from the most loved to just hated. Duke: The secret to longevity in any profession is to stay fresh and that is what I’m doing. James: But the turn to the grinch was just the start, soon you went from old hippie to Satanic God. Duke: Yes a total transformation. James: But you don’t come across as serious in your delivery as an Evil deity. Duke: Its really meant to be a farce a shot at all the garbage gimmicks that we put up with every day. Look James I’m getting old and I guess I just don’t understand some of the young competitors out their so I do what I do best and that’s make fun of them. James: That upset some people. Duke: Yea but you know what I always say. If you can’t laugh at it then fuck it. James: Very to the point I must say. Duke: Wouldn’t do it any other way. James: Has it upset you that you haven’t captured the world title, which is the panicle of your profession? -Duke sighs.- Duke: If my turn isn’t coming then I’m wasting my time. I’m not upset as I am determined to win the World tile. My day will come…I hope. James: So no retirement in the immediate future.? Duke: As long as I’m healthy I can honestly say no. James: Before we let you go would you do us the honor of cutting a promo right here live inside the actors studio? -The crowd cheers on the idea as Duke gets into character and puts his pair back in pig tails.- Dukestar: Hello filth of New York I’m YOUR GOD and Inside the Actors Studio Legend DUKESTAR THE GOBLIN!! -The crowd boo’s him.- Dukestar: Boo all you want you untalented losers cus this is as close as you will get to Greatness. Raise your hand if you’re a God? -Nobody raises their hand.- Dukestar: No?? How about president? -Again everybody is still.- Dukestar: Nothing there? What about any two time Continental champions please raise your hand. -Nothing- Dukestar: Oh so that means I’m the only two time continental champion, Goth God, President, here? James: Afraid so. Dukestar: Shut up you waste of human flesh. Look peons I’m here to tell you that your god and good looking stud Dukestar the Goblin will become a three time continental champion this Friday when I decimate my opponents in the three way dance match. You ever dance with in evil goth god president in the pale of the moonlight? Huh? -Duke hisses at the crowd.- Dukestar: You may ask how I plan on beating this mortal mental midgets? With my new contraption the Malice Death Sound Ray. -Duke grabs James Lipton’s mic and rubs it against his chest making a loud sound through the speakers. The crowd of mainly yuppie homosexual actor wannabe’s cover their ears.- Dukestar: Oh yes feel the power of this God!!! Those are Malice beams penetrating you ears and when I unveil this technique on Fusion there is now way I can lose. Kids that’s the Honky Tonk Front Office Evil Goth God Truth. You can bank on it. -Duke smiles as he hands the mic back to James who shakes his hand as the credits roll.- =w= ---- View Duke Williams's Biography
