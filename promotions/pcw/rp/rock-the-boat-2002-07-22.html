Rock the Boat

22 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: July 22, 2002 The fog began rolling in as I parked my car a few miles away from the boat that would take me to the next PCW PPV. It was huge, with large smokestacks on the top spewing columns of smoke into the air. I watched it for a moment as I removed the keys from the ignition, remembering what it felt like to be a normal person once more. I opened the car door and grabbed my wrestling gear which was sitting in the passenger side seat. As I did, I felt a large lump on the bottom, and I quickly opened it to see what was making the indentation. As I did, I found the amulet of orion which brought me the new life which I so fought for. Rubbing it, I still remembered the day that I first came in contact with it: 'So I've heard,' Ronald said, glancing over at Alexandria. 'Here's some advice, Tom, and I suggest you heed it. You can either leave the city now, tonight...pack up all of your belongings, and just leave. I suggest leaving the Bay Area as a whole. Their connections lie past this city and spills over into neighboring communities. Master vampires often speak with one another and share contracts. Do this as soon as you can, or else, you will be dead in the next month. Your other choice is much more gruesome. You can become an Executioner.' An Executioner? What in the hell was that? I shook my head in confusion. 'An executioner? What do you mean? When you say that I immediately think of big guys wearing black masks, executing people with a huge axe.' 'No, I believe that you watch too many films,' Ronald replied, smiling. I wasn't sure why he was smiling...after all, my life was on the line here. 'An executioner is a universal word used by both the vampires and the vampire killers. An executioner is a person who kills for the pleasure of a coldless heart not to mention a passion for no mercy. Sounds like Charles Manson, don't you think? Usually the executioner is the deadliest vampire killer a city has. We don't have one yet.' The most dangerous killer in the city? That would make that unfortunate person the main target for all of the monsters now, wouldn't it? This already didn't sound good. I wondered if I should ask if there was a third alternative. There probably wasn't, but... 'And you want me to be that person?' I asked, feeling like a piece of meat that everyone wanted. 'I don't know if you realize what you're asking. I'm just a regular person. I'm not a killer. I could barely make a dent into those vampires in the alley when I fought them. Hell, I beat Alexandria could kill me in a second. Why would you want me as the Executioner?' 'Because I think you can handle it,' Ronald answered. He went from the back of his desk to the front, and sat on it's surface. 'You are more than a person. You're a wrestler. You have the physical aspects to be an Executioner. You just need training. I've checked your background....you have vampire killer blood in you. Your family runs two branches...your father was Irish and your mother was Vietnamese. Your father's side has a vampire killer...your great, great aunt...so it's not entirely impossible that the skills have been passed to you. Actually, I'm betting on it. Now, this is your choice, of course. If you are the Executioner, it will make vampires less likely to attack you, since Executioners are regarded in fear by the monsters.' 'The monsters now how I fight, Ronald,' I replied. 'They saw me in the alley way. They were having their way with me...' 'Not if you have this...' Ron reached out on the desk and pulled out a small necklace. It was rather plain, except for a small amulet which hung from the metallic necklace. He handed it to me, and I put it on. The second I did, I could feel the hairs on the back of my neck stand up on end. I don't know what the necklace's purpose was, but I suddenly became more confident and aware of my surroundings. This was some weird shit. Ron looked at me. 'I can see in your eyes that you can feel the power. Most people can't handle it, when they first put on the necklace. They usually throw it off, screaming like a lunatic. You look relatively calm.' I looked relatively calm, but I wasn’t. No, not by any means. The fact of the matter was that I was so scared that I couldn’t even speak. They wanted me to be a murderer, and I don’t care if I was killing the undead, as much as an oxymoron as that is. But now I couldn’t handle that responsibility anymore. I had to look out for my life now, not the life that had me running around the city looking for blood suckers and trying to stake the living hell out of them. I’d leave that to the real professionals – I was more of a joke. I stuck the amulet back into my bag, making sure I didn’t feel it as I walked. It still called out to me, as if we were still connected somehow. I was amazed by the power it gave me, but I no longer had the will to want that power. I wanted the power gone. I wanted to live my life again. I had to win the Continental title back. No matter how dumb that sounded, it was my one and only goal. I wouldn’t let Duke Williams or DarkStar have a say in the matter. That title is mine, and in my mind, I never lost it. “God damn Duke Williams,” I murmured under my breath, feeling my heart racing. “You would think out of all the people, that man would no what being a part of a team meant. Now you’re expecting me to sit here and allow him to screw me out of everything?” I balled my fist up and punched my bag, still upset over Turmoil. He would never see the end of this from me. Not ever. The man who ran the Malice Crew new nothing about fair play or ethics, no matter how much he claimed that he did. He’s now looking out for his own selfish interests, which is fine by me, because I am too. And DarkStar? The guy’s a joke. He stole my title, and now he expects me to come get it back? The man should just hand me the title, because everyone knows I never lost it. As I came up on the ship, I saw a man standing at the entrance with a checklist in front of him. It appeared that he was checking off guests who were arriving. “Tom Lexian…” I stated, looking the man directly in his eyes. He tried to stare back, but quickly looked down, losing the battle. So few people could keep up that game. “Ah yes, Legend Inc…” He replied, handing me a ticket. “Hang on to that. It’s proof that you actually took this trip. A lot of guys are skipping out on the boat ride and collecting the leftover cash.” “I see. Well, I’ll try my best not to cause any trouble.” “See that you do,” He said. “We don’t want you guys starting brawls with your opponents before the matches even begin.” I smirked. “My friend, the brawls are inevitable. You just keep DarkStar and his goons away from me. And keep the booze away from Duke, he tends to get inebriated quite quickly.” I made my way into the boat, knowing full well that a drunk Duke is one that picks a fight. I hoped he would – I love knocking the poor bastard down.
