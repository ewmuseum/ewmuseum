Interlude: Bliss Tearing Eyes (the answer)

16 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: August 16th, 2002 ‘Cause you bleed all the time. He stared at the blinking computer screen. Its like it was mocking him... This is exactly what happened the last time, more then a year ago he tried to propose to his love and what did she do? She left him crying in the middle of Philadelphia International Airport. The pieces of a broken heart are wasted time. Why did he do this to himself. It always seemed like such a great idea... most people with a relationship like theirs would have been married a long time ago. But no, they never sat down and felt like they could control a actual marriage. Things were different though. Those words rattled through his head. 'Things were different now.' It was so true... things were completely different different. They both thought differently, they both were different then they were a year ago... a year ago when it was more about bloodlust and proving yourself then it was about gold and glory. A year ago when DarkStar and Talena revealed they were stupid enough to have cheated on eachother. And I can’t forgive myself for all the things I’ve done. It was stupid. They both speant the next three months paying for their idiocy in the beginning of their relationship. The mere thought of it brought the old DarkStar back to the surface, the bitter, depressed, over dramatic DarkStar. Why did that have to happen. By now he knows everything was decided by Destiny, everything. But why that? What lesson did he need to learn from that? Bleed one more time for me. He knew it though... ‘Cause my heart is filled with loneliness. That no matter what they went through...... And this world is filled with loneliness. No matter how much it hurt.... Bleed one more time for me. They were meant for eachother. ‘Cause the struggles of this world are blistering. Blistering. He looked outside as the rain began to fall, it seemed you could never get away from the rain, he stood up from his seat and began to walk away when the familiar ring of his phone went off. He was tempted not to answer it until he saw the number. 'Talena? Look... i'm sorry i sai-..' He was cut off, 'Yes.' Confused he spoke back, 'Yes? What are you yessing? I havent even apoligized yet!' She laughed, 'Did you forget already? Yes...' her voice was angelic and she reiterated the question, 'Yes, I will marry you. I love you and you know it, I just have a flare for dramatics.' He almost wanted to argue, but how could he? The camera panned out and away as the song ends, DarkStar and Talena to begin another day. Your cells run through my veins.
