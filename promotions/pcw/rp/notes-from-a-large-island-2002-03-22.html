Notes From A Large Island

22 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 22/3/02 I was an eighteen year old kid when my feet first touched American soil. I stepped off of the British Airways flight armed with $100 my passport and a few personal belongings. It was an exciting experience, the sights, smells and sounds of Boston and I was still on the runway. I made my way to the main terminal with my battered and beaten backpack over my shoulder. My first contact with an American was being frisked by a pimply faced young man at customs. 'Anything to declare?' he asked. 'No.' I replied nonchanantly. This appeared to have angered him as immediately I was taken aside and violated by his prying hands. 'I can honestly say im not hiding any drugs in my arse hole' I said, just in case he had an inclanation to check there. When he was satisfied I had no illegal substances on my person he proceeded to check my bag. He removed my clothes, he opened up my shower gel and checked it, he even checked my talcum powder, progbably expecting it to be cocaine. He finally decided that I had no illegal substances and he began examining my passport. 'When was the picture taken, sir?' he asked. 'Erm...november last year.' I replied. 'Are you sure of that sir?' he asked. 'Yes, positive.' I replied. 'Then yould you care to explain why you look so different?' 'Well you see, time has elapsed since then.' I replied in my most sophisticated tone. 'A wiseguy huh? We dont like wiseguys here in Boston.' he said. 'You obviously dont like English people here.' I replied. 'Erm..well...' he said nothing more as he motioned me through. I walked away from the young man and headed out onto the streets of Boston, well I headed out onto the taxi rank. I approached one of the yellow taxis and the driver rolled down his window, this would be my first experience of the stereotypical Indian cab driver in America. 'Hello, where are you going?' he asked. 'Erm, yes, im looking for the headquarters of the World Clss Wrestling Federation.' I replied. 'Where is that, I dont know that.' he said. 'Well you see, I was hoping you would know, because I dont.' I said. 'Where is it near.' he asked. 'No, I dont know, im from England.' I said. 'I cant take you there unless I know where it is.' he said. 'I know...' I replied. 'If you know then why dont you tell me.' he said. 'No, I know that you cant take me there if I dont know because you dont know.' I said. 'You are cofusing me. Where do you want to go.' he asked. 'The...Headquarters...Of...The...World...CLass...Wrestling...Federation' I replied VERY slowly. 'Where is that?' he said. 'I DONT KNOW!' I replied. With that he wound up his window and flipped me the bird, I decided to try the taxi parked behind him. 'Excuse me mate, could you take me to the headquarters of the World Class Wrestling Federation.' I asked. 'Sure guv'nor, hop in.' he replied in a thick London accent. 'Hey.' I said as I got into the car. 'Where you from?' 'Wimbledon mate, yourself?' he replied. 'Peckham.' I said. 'Ah, I know it well On...' he said. 'Only Fools and Horses.' I replied with a laugh. 'Yeah.' he said smiling. 'So, do you kow where the place im trying to get to is?' I asked. 'Yep, why?' he said. 'That other guy didnt know.' I said. 'Probably unlicsenced.' he replied. 'Yeah.' I said. I thought to myself how strange it was that the third person I meet on American soil, is from three train stops away from my house. My train of thought was lost however. 'So what brings you to the States?' he asked. 'Im looking for a job.' I replied. 'Doing what?' he asked. 'Wrestling.' I said. 'Oh, isnt that fake?' he asked. 'Well as this may likely be used in a future promo of some sort, no wrestling is not fake, it is completely real, all the blood is real, we as wrestlers do not cut ourselves for blood, and those chairs are extremely hard.' I said, desperately trying not to break kafaybe. 'Oh ok.' he replied and he was silent for a moment. What am I going to do I thought, I was alone in an alien country with $100 and... 'He we are mate.' said the taxi driver pulling up. 'That will be $4.' I handed him five dollars and stepped out of the taxi. What am I going to do I thought, I was alone in an alien country with $95 and I was about to have an interview for a job. I looked up at the large white building. On the front of the building surrounded by numerous lights were the large red letters 'WCWF'. I gulped this was it the World Class Wrestling Federation, it was time to make a good impression. I made my way into the lobby, I looked down at the marble floor, I looked around at the photos on the walls, Johnny Cruse, Nathanial Duke, Insane Cain, T. I was unaware of the legacy WCWF has, all I knew is I wanted to work there. I approached the reception desk and I was confronted by a prissy little woman with pretty framed glasses and a flowery blouse on, she had a cheery tone of voice, like all receptionists do, it comes with the job. 'Hello how may I help you.' she said, far too cheerily. 'Erm hi, I have a meeting with Maurice Lucan, the commissioner.' I said. 'Your name please sir.' she said. 'Pailthorpe.' I replied 'Ben Pailtorpe.' 'One moment please.' She said as she buzzed through. 'Mr Lucan a Mr. Pailthorpe here to see you, send him through? Ok.' she looked at me 'Just through there Mr. Pailthorpe.' She said as she pointed to a door with 'Commissioner Maurice Lucan' written on a gold plaque in black lettering. 'Thank you.' I said as I set off for the door. Every footstep seemed like a mile, every breath like a gasp for ever, the clock on the wall seemed to stop. I was unaware of everything around me, my eyes were focused on the plaque, my thoughts were focused on a job and my hand was reaching foor the door. Before I could touch the door it was abruptly pulled open and I was confrnted by the figure of a very disgruntled man, about 6'6 350 pounds, needless to say the shock made me jump, I managed to jump the whole 6 inches I needed to cover to match his height. I ducked past him into the plush office of a one Maurice Lucan, commissioner of the World Class Wrestling Federation. In my mind I had images of a tall stern man with beady eyes and a funny evil looking goatee. Instead standing infront of me was a short plump man with a buhsy moustahce, he reminded me of my old History teacher. 'Mr Pailthorpe I presume.' he said with a smile. 'Welcome to the headquarters of the World Class Wrestling Federation.' 'Erm, thanks, its nice to be here.' I said, bumblng on my words and not knowing what to say, I smiled brightly. 'Im sure it is, now you have been contacted by a J. Pape, right?' he asked. 'Y-Y-Yes.' I said stammering. 'Dont me nervous Mr. Pailthorpe, after all we contacted you, did Mr Pape tell you why he called?' he asked. 'No, all he said was he had a job for me.' I said. 'Ok, well J. Pape will be heading up a feeder federation for the WCWF and we want you to be the first guy on the roster.' he said. 'Oh...me?' I replied. 'Yes you, you are a promising young talent Mr. Pailthorpe and we think you can go along way in this business with us, the WCWF family.' he replied with warmth and a smile which was very reassuring to me. 'Erm..great.' I said. 'Yes it is great, we expect big things from this federation Mr. Pailthorpe and you will be the first man on the roster, you will also be the first entrant in the tournament for the International title.' he said. 'The international title?' I rpelied. 'Yes, the International title, the NWF's top prize!' he said with pride. 'The NWF?' I asked. 'The National Wrestling Federation Mr Pailthorpe, WCWF's feeder federation and your new home.' he replied. 'Ok.' I said hesitantly, why does this guy like me so much. 'I have taken the liberty of taking care of your accomadation Mr. Pailthorpe.' He said handing me a door key.' This is for the Plaza hotel accross the street Mr. Pailthorpe, go and have a rest, do a bit of sight seeing, there will be plenty of time to talk business, enjoy yourself.' With that he ushered me out of his office and shut the door, leaving me bewildered. I suddenly realised how tired I was, although it was only 5pm local time it was 10pm back home. I walked out of the lobby and back onto the streets of Boston, I took a moment to look around em at the hustle and bustle of life here, I realised how small and insignificant I was here, none knew who I was, noone cared who I was, I was all alone. I crossed the street and I was about to carry on walking when I was stopped by a booming voice. 'Stop right there punk.' boomed the voice. 'Huh.' I said as a spun around to be confronted by a 5'4 bald man in a police uniform. 'What do you think you are doing?' boomed the miniature police officer. 'Erm...im going to my hotel.' I said pointing to the Plaza. 'You were J-Walking!' boomed the little man. 'I was?' I said, bemused. 'Yes.' replied the little officer. 'Ok, thanks for warning me.' I said turning to walk away. 'Oh no you dont.' replied the midget grabbing my shoulder and spinning me around with suprising force for a little guy. 'I should take yo' ass down town?' 'Why?' I said, beffudled. 'You were J-Walking.' he replied. 'I was?' I asked. 'Yes.' he boomed. 'Ok.' I said. 'Do you know what that means.' he asked. 'No.' I said. 'You walked accross the street without being at a crossing.' he boomed. 'So I crossed the road.' I said. 'Yes.' he boomed. 'And you want to arrest me for that.' I said. 'Its a bookable offence.' he said. 'It is?' I asked. 'Yes.' he boomed. 'Oh.' I said. 'Your not from round here are you?' he boomed. 'No sir, im from London.' I said. 'Oh, that would explain it then, I will let you go this time, but dont J-Walk again.' he boomed before walking off. 'Ok.' I said as a turned and walked towards the beckoning doors of the Plaza. I entered the lobby of the huge hotel and I realise how out of place I was. People were milling around in suits drinking cocktails and laughing. I was there in jeans, a t-shirt and Reebok trainers. I approached the reception and another one of those prissy little women (literary joke~!) confronted me. 'Hello sir.' she said cheerily. 'How may I help you?' 'Yes, um i'd like to know where this room is.' I said showing her my key. 'Certainly sir, its on the third floor.' she replied. 'Thanks.' I said turning away and walking towards the elevator. I pressed the button on the elevator and waited as the little arrow came down the floors, fourht, third, second, first and ground. The doors sprung open with a 'Bing' and I walked forward into the empty elevator. I pressed the button the the third floor and the doors shut. The elevator slowly started rising untill it reached the third floor, the doors opened with another 'Bing' and I exited the elevator. I looked at the key, room number 234 was my destination. I looked up and staring me in the face was room number 234. I walked in and closed the door behind me. Preparing for my first night in America. TO BE CONTINUED
