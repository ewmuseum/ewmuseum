Headaches and The Waffle House

10 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/10/2002 *They say the Lord giveth, and the Lord taketh away. That may work in most life situations, but what perspired this week, definitely was not one of them times. The travesty that shook PCW was nothing close to the work of the Lord. No, but instead, it was the work of jealousy. Of friendship gone bad. If this had happened to most people, to ordinary people, they would become weak, shells of their former selves. But the one man in the middle of this is anything but ordinary. The one individual who won it all, only to have it all taken away is not weak, not a shell. He’s a monster. A bad ass, who’s tired of kissing other’s asses. Tired of listening to advice. Tired of the backstage banter directed at him. But now that all changes. Now the true feelings have been revealed. No more lies to hide behind. Now, it is all out in the open, and in the end, we learned two things. One, The Deuces are no more. Two, No longer will Chris Sloboda take shit from anyone.* **The scene opens at a 24 hour Waffle House just outside of San Marcos, Texas. Being two in the morning, this is a fairly empty Waffle House. In fact, there are but two patrons. One, the former World Champ, who just days ago was mugged, raped, and pilfered of his World Title. The other, a familiar PCW reporter, whom always seems to show up, be around at the right time. Other than those two, the only other people in this Waffle House are Eunice, the waitress, and Bubba, the cook. Our former world champ is dressed in his usual black jeans, with a whit wife beater t-shirt under a blue jean jacket. Our reporter is dressed in his usual bad $20.00 Wal-Mart suit. Of course, for those slow-witted people, ala Chris Chase, the former world champion and reporter are Boda and Cal Seaver respectively. As the two sit in a booth, reading the Waffle House menu, Eunice walks over, pad and pen in hand.** Eunice: [in a deep southern Texas draw] “Welcum to da Waffle House. My names Eunice. May I take ya order?” **Boda looks up from the menu, and is taken back by the site standing at his table. Eunice is a big girl, coming in at about 300 pounds, and stands barely 5’4. She smiles at Boda which makes it worse by exposing her summer teeth. You know... some are here, some are there, some are white, and some are green. Boda looks back down at his menu, and kicks Cal under the table, making Cal look up. Now Cal, being the nice guy he is, tries to ignore the hideous looks of Eunice. Eunice again asks Cal what he would like to eat.** Cal: “Well dear, I’ll take the pancakes supreme and a cup of O.J. And throw in some smothered hash browns.” **Now, the fact that Cal just called a fat, ugly woman ‘dear’ was enough to make any man cringe in horror. But what he did next would make even a drunk Duke Williams whimper** Cal: “Thanks hun.” **Now, the cardinal rule about fat chicks is DO NOT CALL THEM PET NAMES. It’s the same rule that applies to stray animals. DO NOT FEED THEM. Because, they will follow you home, and then you’re stuck with them. Boda snickers, and without looking up at Mrs Chase, orders his meal** Boda: “I’ll take the French toast, bacon, and a cup of decaff. I’m to high strung now, need to cut back a bit.” Eunice: “Otay... I’ll put them orders in, and bring your drinks right back.” **As Eunice waddles away, Boda drops his menu, and looks at Cal. Seaver gives Boda a ‘what?’ look.** Boda: “You trying to get laid?” Cal: “What? I was only being nice.” Boda: “Too nice I might say. Now, I bet you one hundred bucks that we can’t leave here, with out Rolly Polly giving you her number.” Cal: “What in the heck do you mean?” **Boda explains to Cal the ‘rules’, and Cal kind of double takes on what he just said to Eunice. Finally, it sinks in when Chris jokingly calls her ‘Mrs Cal Seaver’. That’s enough for Cal. He decides to be a jackass to the waitress as to avoid any further lusting she might develop for him. So, being Cal, he changes the subject of conversation** Cal: “How’s the head?” **Of course, Cal is referring to the bandage on the head of Boda. A direct result of the cowardly act from Corey and Chris at the end of Fusion.** Boda: “How do you think it is? I got super kicked off a 15 foot stage, and sent crashing head first through a production table. Lucky the fall was broken by Lenny the executive producer of the show. To bad he broke his leg in all the melee.” Cal: “What? Really, damn.” Boda: “Hey, he’ll be okay. But let’s get right to the point Cal. I know your beating around the bush. You missed the opportunity to interview me after I won at March Madness.” Cal: “Okay, let’s talk about that. Actually, I know how you feel about winning the title. And, well, after last night, the whole world knows how you feel about losing it already. So, let me ask you the one question that has of yet gone unanswered.” Boda: “Shoot. Got nothing else to do.” Cal: “What about the fans? For months, they have cheered you, now you suddenly turn your back on them.” **Boda lets out a laugh. Eunice looks on from behind the counter, waving at Cal.** Boda: “I didn’t turn my back on the fans. They turned on me. I never once came out there, and called the fans ‘fat, stinking, toothless bastards’. Never did I berate a fan for holding a ‘New Breed’ sign, or even yell at them last night for booing me. No. I haven’t turned my back on the fans, it’s them who have suddenly stopped appreciating what I do for them. They seem to have forgotten the blood, sweat and tears I’ve dealt for them over the past 18 months. Ye, they loved it when I won the title at March Madness. And, then, when I lost it, unfairly, to Miyagawa on Turmoil, the cheering suddenly stopped. They booed me for losing the title. Did they boo Mexi for his part in my losing the title? No. He gets cheered like a naked cheerleader during a boring football game. His jealousy caused me to lose my title. The Deuces were about being a family, and about looking out for one another, but did that happen? No. Because Mexi let Darkstar in the ring, and knock me the hell out. Now, Miya is the champion, and the fans cheer him. What did I do wrong for them to stop cheering me?” Cal: “I don’t know.... maybe your reaction after Turmoil. Maybe the beating tonight of Mexi.” Boda: “Cal... all I did was give Mexi a chance to apologize to me for his actions as of late. But the ungrateful little shit didn’t. It’s all his fault that he got a beat down. I tried to be the bigger man, let him make up for his performance, and he basically tried to throw it in my face. Well, why should I stand there and take it? I decided that if he couldn’t apologize to save our friendship, then I didn’t need him as a friend.” Cal: “Okay, but wouldn’t just telling him so, had been enough? You nearly decapitated him out there.” Boda: “I was angry. So, I took it out on him. No biggie. He’s an athlete. He knows what to expect every time he steps in the ring. So, I did what I was trained to do. Fight.” **Before Cal could retort, Eunice brings the breakfast for the two. She puts Boda’s plate down first, and as she puts down Cal’s she shyly smiles at him, and winks. Cal tries to look the other way. Eunice walks off, back to the kitchen.** Cal: “Okay..... so, what about attacking Miya during his match tonight? He already gave you a rematch this Turmoil coming up!” Boda: [mouth full of French toast] “Cal, I appreciate Miya’s attempt to rectify the unjust way I lost the title. And the reason I attacked him, was to let him know I am still bigger, better and badder than he is. To bad he lost the TV title, I’d of looked good as the World and TV champion. Specially after I planned on having him put both on the line this week.” Cal: “Okay, so it was a message then. But, what about the message that Chase and Williams sent you two. This is the second time in a week Chase has taken you out.” **Chris puts down the cup of coffee he was about to sip from. He puts it down with enough force that the mug shatters in his hand. Boda simple looks at Cal, oblivious to the cut on his hand and the burning hot coffee.** Cal: “Did I touch a sore spot?” Boda: “Cal.... let me tell you about this Chris Chase character. He is a cancer. He wants to be me. But he can not. Ever since his ‘lucky’ win, his ‘tainted’ win over me in PCW2, he has been a boil on my ass. Well, at Sabotage, I will pop that boil and show the world the oozing inners that really is Chris Chase.” Cal: “So, you plan on wrestling in the tag match with Red Rock?” Boda: “I plan on kicking the living hell out of Chase and Williams. The hell with Red Rock. I don’t need anyone’s help to destroy two low life ass pirates. I’m going to once and for all shut up both of those New Breed bitches , and then go to Turmoil and win back my title. Now, Cal, if you don’t mind.... I want to stop talking about PCW, and eat my food. Then I’m going to my hotel room, and sleeping. I’ve got a little headache from Chase.” **Boda starts eating, and so does Cal. About two minutes later, Eunice walks over, and hands Boda the bill, and hands Cal a piece of paper. Cal looks at it, then tries to hide it from Chris. Boda knows what it is, and laughs at Cal as the scene fades out** Boda: “You owe me a hundred bucks.”
