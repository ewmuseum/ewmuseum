TIME TO TAKE OVER THE WORLD....or Something of That Nature...

30 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6-30-2002 ..^../ Our story begins in the reaches of the darkest night...the city is bustling with night time activity from hard clubbing to drag racing. At every turn there is a new adventure within the city for those who want to explore....and there is one within the city who has some exploring of his own to do. The stars shine down, twinkling jewels set against the black satin background called the 'sky' as we go to a loose street on the bad side of town. The lone street light flicker off and on unreliably, the rest of the iron beacons of light not even working, mostly due to reckless vandalism by those who live within the area. The street is littered with loose pieces of trash, broken down and vandalised apartment complexes littering the way, unfit for human life. And in the midst of this street, walking slowly and cautiously is a beautiful young woman with short dark hair and wearing a dangerously short red dress with sphaghetti straps.. A few loose strands of her incubus ink dark hair blow in the cold wind as she looks about cautiously, this woman completely out of place within these bad streets. After making a long trek of walking, she ends up at a busy intersection, filled with hustling and bustling traffic, all kinds of cars from luxury to sport types racing through the streets, a pleathora of red and white lights streaking in so many directions. The young woman construes her mouth in sadness, knowing no matter how long she sits and waits at the lone crosswalk at this intersection that no one will have the kindness to stop for her. Suddenly, her head jerks around as she begins to hear quiet whispers in the wind, almost Jason-esque...knowing she couldn't possibly confuse it with the whir of cars passing by ..^../ Voice:'EEEE......VVVVIIL.....EEEEE....VVVVIIIL....EEEE....VVVIILLL...' ..^../Trying to believe she's just a little paranoid, the woman turns back around, now trying to ignore the voice as best as she can as she looks out to the intersection for her moment to cross. But the voice continues...growing slightly louder and louder each time as it irks away at her...too much to be able to ignore as she starts to tremble with a sharp fear ..^../ Voice:'EEEE...VVVVILLL.....EEEEEE....VILLLLL... WOMAN I SAID EEEEE....VIIILLLL!!!' Woman: *turning around angrily* 'WHO ARE YOU?! QUIT PLAYING GAMES!!'..^../ Silence overtakes the air once again, all except for the whir of cars as she looks about, trying to match a body with the voice but unable to. She slowly turns back around to the intersection...but jumps back in wrenching fear of what is now in front of her....a muscular man with spiked blonde and black hair, a twisted vice-versa style mask, body paint all over his ripped chest and arms in the shape of superhero insignias, and a pair of see-through pants with more insignias....also making his smiley face boxers visible to anyone who would look down. His smile is demented....as if he either wants to make her life or threaten her mercilessly as she starts to back away slowly, only making the PCW's newest mainstay advance closer....that mainstay being RENT-A-VILLAIN. With a slight pause, our vile villain asks ..^../ Rent-A-Villain:'Pretty cool huh? I saw it on a monster movie....did you think I needed a few more E's in my EEEEEVIL's womanly wench of cowering?' Young Woman:'Wh....who are you?' Rent-A-Villain:'WHO AM I?! WHO I AM?! WHY I'M THE MOST VILE VILLAIN IN THE CITY....THE FEAR THAT MAKES YOUR EYES BEGIN TO TEAR....I AM RENT-A-VILLAIN, AND THIS AIN'T THE TIME TO START CHILLIN'!! Wait wait...that sucked, this isn't Boyz N' The Hood...can we do that over.' ..^../ The woman dosen't know whether to scream in complete and utter fear at this villanious enigma or laugh at his utter stupidity. She strikes a haughty 'hands on her hips' type pose and exclaims ..^../ Young Woman:'What is this...SOME SICK JOKE?!' Rent-A-Villain:'I KID YOU NOT YOU SOON TO BE DISTRESSED DAMSEL YOU....I AM RENT-A-VILLAIN....HERE ME ROAR....' *growl* ..^../ The 'damsel' can't help but laugh out loud in the middle of the dark intersection at the childish growl of Rent-A-Villain, accompanied by a cuddly strike of the air as if he were a tiger or some other intimidating beast. She finally regains her composure, pushing back the bangs of her short hair and saying ..^../Young Woman:'Your the most LOW-BUDGET 'villain' I've ever seen. And if your so evil...how come I've never heard of you or probably no one else?' Rent-A-Villain:'LOW BUDGET?! UN-HEARD OF?! First off, I'll have you know I had to break a lot of piggy banks to get pants this clear, and this body paint isn't cheap either! Second off, haven't you read all five of those 'Death Of Rent-A-Hero' RP's?! What you think I did all that for my health?! Geez womanly wench on non-wisdom! And since you seem to be a non-beliver in my evil powers...I shall now be fored to TERRORIZE YOU WITH MY VILLAINOUSLY VILE VENOMAGNETIC EVIL POWERS OF EVIL STUFF, tilde coming soon mind you. COME HERE MY PRETTY, GYAHAHAHA....' *cough cough* 'Wait hold up...' *catches breath* 'Geez, how does Ivan hold his laugh for so long...okay, now I'm ready!' ..^../Our vile villain quickly snatches up the young woman, hoisting her up on his shoulder and flashing a thumbs up to the camera as he looks to the intersection, caring not for the cars hurtling through and running across it like a crazed madman, which he already is. With blinding speed and agility, he dodges car after car with young lady still hoisted on his shoulder, kicking and screaming in fearof losing her life in the arms of our villain. Finally, he safely makes it across the other side of the street, standing her up and flashing that demented grin again at her as she looks at him with shock, exclaiming ..^../ Young Woman:'DO YOU REALIZE WHAT YOU JUST DID?!' Rent-A-Villain:'Why of course you distressed damsel...I'VE MOVED YOU AGAINST YOUR WILL!!! HAHAHA, I'M SO EVIL!! NOW CALL THE LOCAL SUPERHERO SO I MAY THRASH HIM MERCILESSLY!! Go ahead...I'll wait.' *taps foot*Young Woman:'Why would I call a hero....YOU ARE THE HERO!! I would have NEVER got across that street by myself if YOU hadn't come and got me over there...your amazing! THANK YOU!' ..^../ Our vile villain shoots a wide eyed stare towards the young woman, shocked and appaled by the fact that she thanked him for his inadvertant good deed. He stomps in places and punches the air angrily, like a spoiled kid deprived of a toy he wanted before just standing in place solidly, folding his arms tight before saying ..^../ Rent-A-Villain:'DON'T THANK ME!!! HATE ME!!!! HATE ME!!! Please....hate me...' *wimpering* 'Pretty please?' ..^../ The young woman giggles, tossing back a few bangs of her short dark hair as she strikes a bright smile towards the loveably demented PCW demon. Reaching into the top part of her dress near her cleavage, she pulls out a small white card with a phone number on it, handing it to Rent-A-Villain graciously and before walkign away, lands a soft kiss in his uncovered cheek and steps away, saying to him as she continues her walk down the dark street ..^../ Young Woman:'Give me a call sometime...' ..^../ With that, she walks away, looking back one last time after flashing a sweet wink towards Rent-A-Villain as she fades off into the darkness. Standing their appaled and with card in hand, Rent-A-Villain just turns about and makes his way down the street, hanging his head in hsame as he says to himself in a mixture of failure and depression ..^../Rent-A-Villain:'You TERRORIZE A DAMSEL AND SHE GIVES YOU HER NUMBER...HAVE THEIR STANDARDS GONE DOWN THAT LOW?!' *Sigh* 'Kreigman made this look so easy...' ..^../ Well, it's back to the evil drawing board for Rent-A-Villain and his evil schemes...but you best believe that he shall not be dauntered in his neverending task to get people of the world to HATE HIM!! With that we leave our vile villain to his work until he says....IT'S TIME....TIME FOR WHAT.....TIME TO TAKE OVER THE WORLD...ONE MINISCULE PERCENTAGE AT A TIME!!! ..^../
