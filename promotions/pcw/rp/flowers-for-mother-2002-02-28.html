Flowers for Mother

28 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 28th Febuary 2002 We rejoin the intrepid trio as they are driving along the streets of Anaheim in Miyagawa’s BMW Z3 (in British Racing Green Metallic, of course). Steve is sitting in the passenger seat with Natalia sitting behind him while Miyagawa himself drives. The car stops at a set of traffic lights. Their mission... to meet Miyagawa’s mother. The reason... until this morning he didn’t know that he actually had one. Miyagawa turns his head to Natalia in the backseat quickly while waiting for the lights to change. Miyagawa: Do you know any florists around this part of town? Natalia: Not really, but I’m sure we’ll find one on the way at some point. Steve nudges Miyagawa in the side and points towards the lights; Miya looks back and sees that they’ve turned green. Something catches your eye on the dashboard... it’s a map on a small television terminal in the middle of the dashboard between the passenger and driver. The thing suddenly speaks. Computer: Next right. Miyagawa slows the car down slightly and steers the car around the right side turning off the main road with ease. He glances down at the screen to check that he’s meant to simply drive straight the way down this road; and upon finding that he does... well, he does. Natalia shuffles across the back seat to sit behind Miyagawa (unlocking the seat belt on one side and then putting it on, on the other side of course). She leans forward towards Miyagawa. Natalia: Nervous? Miyagawa smirks in the rear view mirror as he looks back at her, his wife in sense and feeling although not in law as they had just found out. Miyagawa: Of course. Although I’m not sure if I’m nervous about you saying “No” for some reason or about meeting one of my parents... my mom might even know who my father is. I might actually have a full set of parents... I never even gave that possibility much thought until now. Steve: Car. Miyagawa: What? Steve points out in front of himself. Steve: CAR! Miyagawa glances forward and sees them hurtling headlong into a stationary vehicle; he swerves right, up onto the pavement and around the car and back onto the road. Steve and Natalia sit there in a stunned silence. Natalia: And you said the standard of driving in this country was better than back in mother Russia. I say bah at that. Miyagawa: You’ve been watching OSW too much with all that bah’ing. Besides, we survived unhurt, didn’t we? No thanks to me though, but we did, right? FLORIST! Miyagawa slams the breaks on to the much annoyance of the cars behind him who all honk their horns. Miyagawa drives the car up to the pavement and stops. He undoes his seat belt and climbs over the door, slides over the bonnet of the BMW and lands on the pavement. Miyagawa: Wait there; I’ll only be a few minutes. Steve: Show off. Miyagawa: Hey, it takes skill to be this pretty. Damn, I sound like Dan Hollywood now... or Kid Wonder, although if that was the case then I would be dating my sister. Oh well, flowers! Miyagawa rushes off into the florist to get some flowers for his mother. Steve: This reminds me of something. Natalia: What’s that? Steve: When Miya and I was in Hawaii a while back... way before he met you, he inhaled a little bit too much paint fumes. Basically he beat up some tramp, really quit badly while screaming “Flowers for Mother! Flowers for Mother!”. He hasn’t really had a bad trip like that since thankfully... well apart from just prior to the incident where you two met, although that was more due to alcohol abuse than anything else. You really did save his life back then, after he met you he’s been virtually off the bottle. Natalia: I never really thought of it that way. Steve: He’s got a lot on his plate at the moment. He’s got that match against Walden which he isn’t overly worried about, but on the same hand something still might go wrong and damage his chances on Sunday. And Sunday is important to him... he might not talk about it much, but trust me it really is. Natalia: I know. He denies it, but the World Title does mean a lot to him. He’s put a lot of effort and time in his wrestling career into PCW and only got comparatively few returns. Steve: Exactly. The two people he’s always rated himself against have been Clyde and Dan Hollywood because one of his first matches was a triple threat against them. In fact, I think it might very well of been his first ever win in PCW. Anyway, Clyde has gone on to win all but the Tag Team titles and the World Title... well... strictly speaking he didn’t win the Cruiserweight either, but he claims he did because possession is sixth tenths of the law... anyway and Hollywood has won all the belts except for the World Title. Meanwhile Miyagawa there has only won the TV Title, the Cruiserweight and the Tag. It does leave him a little bit behind somewhat, but he just claims that titles don’t make the man, and instead the man makes the titles. Which I guess does sound kinda good on paper. Natalia: I’m guessing what your saying is that being supportive is the key... although I feel that I might not be able to continue to escort him down to the ring... with the pregnancy going on now further, it might not be in the best idea for the baby, you know? Steve: I’ll get Miya to suggest it to you. Steve grins back over towards Natalia who smiles back at him. Miyagawa surprises them both by sliding across bonnet of the car once again carrying a large bunch of roses. He stops by the passenger door of the open topped car and separates the bunch, revealing them to in fact be two bunches of roses. He hands one down to Natalia. Miyagawa: Those are for you. Natalia: Aww, thanks. Steve: Didn’t get me anything I see. Miyagawa: They were all out of Tulips. So I got you a bottle of Lizard Lightning from the newsagents next door. Miya tosses the bottle with his now spare hand to Steve who catches it. Miyagawa: Oh, and Steve...? Steve: Yes? Miyagawa: Hold these till we get there. Miya tosses the other bunch of red roses to Steve, who manages to catch them with both hands, although he did drop the bottle of Sobe down around his feet somewhere. Miyagawa slips over the top of the door and down into the drivers seat. He hooks up the seatbelt and turns the ignition before hitting the little button to start the engine. He puts the car into gear and... Steve: Miya. Miyagawa: Steve. Steve: You do realise you just put it into reverse gear, don’t you? Miyagawa looks down at the gear stick, which is something he didn’t do before and realises his mistake, correcting it and putting it into first gear. Miyagawa: Whoops. Steve: Please stop with the whoops, will you? I would like to survive this trip, you know. Miyagawa: I’ll do my best, and that goes for Sunday too... this belt is for our son. Natalia: Daughter. Miyagawa: Yeah... that too. And with that Miyagawa drives off with Steve and Natalia onboard. And that is where we end it.
