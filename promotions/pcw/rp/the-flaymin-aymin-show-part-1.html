Date: not entered [Scene opens in Flaymin Aymin’s living room. Flaymin picks up a chair and puts in the centre of the room. He then walks over to a nearby armchair, picks it up and puts it besides the other chair in the centre of the room. Flaymin Aymin then returns to pick up another armchair.Once again, he walks back over to the centre of the room and places the armchair next besides the other two armchairs. Flaymin then walks over to a nearby radio and turns it on.Suddenly, corny jazz music begins to blare out of the speakers of the radio. Next, Flaymin walks over to the door and opens it.Standing there in confusion is his valet, Heather. Flaymin leads Heather into the lounge room and shows her to a seat in the centre of the lounge room. He then walks over to the radio and turns it off, immediately cutting off the corny jazz music] Heather: What’s going on here?? Flaymin: Welcome to the Flaymin Aymin Show. [Flaymin pulls a cue card from his pocket and begins to read from it] Flaymin: It says here that your name is Heather. Heather: What have you been smoking this time? Flaymin: Heather, you are the valet of Flaymin Aymin, are you not? Heather: Uh…. Yeah.Are you going to tell me what’s going on?!? [Flaymin walks over to Heather] Flaymin: Lately I’ve been sensing a lot of tension between yourself, Angela and Fernando the Zebra. So I thought a Jerry Springer-esque kind of show would be a great way to get you all acquainted with each other. Heather: Do you really think this is going to work? Flaymin: Probably not, but its worth a try. Ahem, so where were we?Oh yes, Heather, how have you been enjoying your role as Flaymin’s valet. Heather: Well he’s a nice guy…. I guess. Flaymin: Really?It seems like your hiding something- what are your real thoughts of him? Heather: Ok, I think he’s seriously f*cked in the head. Flaymin: OOOOOOOOHHH!!!! [Heather raises an eyebrow] Flaymin: Sorry, but I’ve got to do the audience sound effects as well. Heather: F*cked… in…. the…. head…. Flaymin: Well Heather, we have a surprise for you- Flaymin Aymin is actually here right now. Heather: No shit. Flaymin: Nope, matter o’ fact HERE HE COMES NOW!!! [Flaymin runs over and turns the radio on again, which once again plays the corny jazz music. Flaymin then parades around the lounge room and waves to the imaginary crowd. He then turns off the radio and sits besides Heather] Flaymin: What’s this I’ve been hearing?I was standing around backstage and I heard you criticising me. [Heather looks at Flaymin and rolls her eyebrows] Heather: You’ve gotta be kidding me. [Flaymin raises his right hand and waves around his index finger] Flaymin: Giirrrl, if you’ve got something to say- say it to my face.Got that sista!?! Heather: I think its time you brought the next guest on. [Flaymin shrugs his shoulders] Flaymin: Eh, why not. [Flaymin gets back up and walks to the centre of the room] Flaymin: Well we just heard from Flaymin and he seemed pretty angry.Heather, do you think you can work out your troubles with him? Heather: I don’t know, he seems to talk to himself a lot… Flaymin: I know exactly how you feel. Well, moving right along, I have here in my notes that you were upset just over week ago when Flaymin recruited a new valet, Angela, who is also El Diablo’s wife. Heather: That’s right. Flaymin: How has this affected you?Has it made you feel unwanted? Heather: No, not unwanted- he’s just not paying enough attention to me anymore. [Flaymin places his hand over his mouth] Flaymin: OOOOOOOOHHHH!!!……….Wow, the crowd seem mighty upset by that. Heather: You sound like a jackass when you do that. Flaymin: Yeah but it livens up the program. But more importantly, as we were just discussing, you said that Flaymin isn’t paying any attention to you anymore.Is there anything you would like to say to Angela? [Heather cracks her knuckles] Heather: There sure is. Flaymin: Well by a total coincidence…. Angela isn’t here today. Heather: WHAT?!? Flaymin: Only joking- here’s Angela, everybody!!! [Flaymin runs over to the radio and turns on the corny jazz music.He then dashes over to the door and lets Angela into the room and ushers her to a seat besides Heather. Flaymin then walks back over to the radio and switches it off again] Flaymin: Welcome Angela.[Angela looks around the room in surprise] Angela: This is stupid. Flaymin: There is no need for profanity.I’m sure we can all sort this out like civilised adults. Angela: This isn’t going to work. [Flaymin shakes his head] Flaymin: Uh huh, well I believe that your troubles are caused by a lack of positivism attitudal responses. [Angela and Heather look at each other in bewilderment] Flaymin: I don’t know, I’m just putting random words together…. Anyways, Angela, while you were backstage….. pressing your ear against the wall- you would have heard everything that Heather said about you. Is there anything you would like to say back to Heather? Angela: Actually there is.Heather, you’re nothing but a fu- Flaymin: OH, I’M SORRY!!! [Flaymin places one hand over his ear and nods his head] Flaymin: I’ve just been informed by the director that we’ll have to head to a Commercial Break.When we come back, we’ll discuss this matter further as well as introduce our special guest- Fernando the Zebra!! [Flaymin runs over to the radio and presses play and soon after, the jazz music begins to start up again as the scene fades to black….] In the next instalment: Angela and Heather finally get it on!And what role will Fernando the Zebra play in proceedings? Tune in next time to find out!!!!
