Then I kissed her ear again. However this time it was a softer kiss and it did not last very long. I took the hand that was in her hair and put it on her bare back. I just rubbed her back lightly as she kept her head rested on my shoulder. I looked up at the ceiling. 'I will make the Angel Society pay for what they did.' I told myself.
