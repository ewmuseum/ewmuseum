Over the last few weeks or so, we had seen the 'Pretty Cool Guys' wonder away from their backgrounds... step away from the things that made them so unique around 'Primetime Championship Wrestling.' Visiting a hospital... prancing around dressed like 'Max Payne'... its all good and well for people watching, but what about Rich Sherwin and Ryan Moore themselves? Its all good acting like fools... but does foolish acting bring home the '#1 Smash Hits'? Does it bring home the Gold and Platinum Records that such artists like 'Britney Spears', 'The Backstreet Boys' and Aqua win for their emotionally filled pieces of master craft? The two friends began to walk off into the distance, away from their spot, although the camera stayed with them at all times. Sherwin was originally a few paces ahead of Ryan but the other half of PCG was quick to catch up. 'Rich...', he started, finally getting in line with Sherwin and walking to a pace that equalled his, '... you state that he won wins the war, shall be chosen to make the biggest impact... but we have won the war already, by technicality, we defeated both 'The Heartbreakers' and 'The Cowabunga Connection'... so, whats this impact that we're going to make upon the history of wrestling?
