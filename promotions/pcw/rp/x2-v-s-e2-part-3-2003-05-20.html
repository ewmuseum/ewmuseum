Date: 5 - 20 - 03 1 Year ago……….. Preston: [Joking] it’s now time to enter a realm of the comic book, a time to enter the realm of the X-Men…..Storm, Nightcrawler, Colossus, Cyclops and Wolverine Duh…..Duh….Duh!! Gargano: [Chuckling then gets serious] Yeah….Haha………..Dam! Hugh Jackman!!! Preston giggles the door handle to no avail, Gargano then steps in and tries his luck yet again to no avail. Finally Preston looks at John and John looks at Preston with the same smirk on there face, both at the same moment run at the door and smash it open leading inside a pitch black room. Preston: John? Is this X2? Gargano: I don’t know but this is really freaking me out! [Boom Chukka Bow Bow…] Both men turn in shock as a bright red strobe light starts to flicker on and off as the freaky techno music begins to play. Another strobe light starts this time but now it is a lighter white one which makes everything more clear to John and Tyler. Preston: Dude! The Techno Music…The Strobe Lights……. Gargano: It’s a Club? Preston: No….It isn’t a club….It’s a Porn Movie!!! Gargano: A Porn Movie? Dude this could all be one big coincidence. Pretty soon the lights come on and a Bed is shown with two barely dressed blonde woman sitting there studying there text books as there is a knock at the door. John and Tyler just stand in the dark and watch as the girls get up and run to the door. The girls open the door and there stands a delivery man in short shorts. Delivery Man: Anyone order a package? [Boom Chukka Bow Bow…] Preston: [Whispering] still skeptical? Gargano: Not at all. The delivery man throws the box down as the girls start to let there hair down and take off there tops. John and Tyler’s eyes pop open as John starts to nudge Tyler. Gargano: [Smiling] maybe we didn’t come in the wrong room after all Eh? Eh? Tyler just stands there nudging back as the girls jump on the bed and bounce and bounce. John and Tyler’s eyes follow up and down and up and down. The girls stop………..and the delivery man starts to take off his pants. Gargano: [Screaming] Wrong room! Really wrong room! The cameras stop rolling and the music stops as everyone in the room including the delivery man and the two blonde girls look to the back of the room where both John and Tyler are standing covering there eyes. Director: Yo! Who are you two? Gargano and Preston pull there hands down and open there eyes to look at everyone in the entire room staring back at them with a confused look upon all of there faces. Preston: Uh……..Uh….Is this the X2 set? Director: X2? No, No! This is X3, As in XXX Gargano: XXX? The movie with Vin Diesel? You know I was offered that role but that Dam Vin Diesel got it! You know I would have made a lot better agent then that Pitch Black piece of trash! You know….. Preston: John! Let it go man! Let it go! Director: Oh you’re an actor are you? Gargano: That’ll be me. Director: [Intrigued] Well…Well…..Well…That delivery guy isn’t quite working out so I want to make you an offer…..I’ll pay you 500 dollars to play that teensy weensy little role with those two beautiful babes, Huh? What do yaw say? John intrigued by this offer begins to ponder his chin thinking. John looks up and then looks at the girls and then smiles and extends his hand to the director. Gargano: Well another acting job would help, even if it is porn so………. John is suddenly cut off by Tyler pulling him over to the corner of the room away from everyone else staring at them both still in confusion of why they were there in the first place. Preston: John, We’re on a mission here, we have no time for you to make porn! Now let’s go. Gargano: But Tyyyyy…. I want to make the porn!!! Preston: Let’s go John. Gargano: O…..k Both Gargano and Preston leave out of the same door they came in thru leaving the crew of workers and porn stars standing there still confused. John and Tyler are still on there quest to get revenge on the movie X2. The scene fades as the actor playing the delivery man raises his fist in the air. Delivery Man: I still get to do the Porn!!!!
