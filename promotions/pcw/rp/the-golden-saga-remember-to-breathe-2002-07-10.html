The Golden Saga: Remember to Breathe.

10 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7-10-02 She fixes her lips they Always look perfect never a smudged line never too much 'Why is it snowing out?' 'You don't remember?' The light reflected off of the snowflakes as they fell onto the black asphalt, cars drove by slowly and casually as if it were nothing but a normal night in January. 'Happy anniversary lover.' 'This... is my present?' 'This is our past.' A small arena, six years and some months ago. The day they first met. The Theatre of the Living Arts on south street. The marquee tells the bands that will be there. Mellow. Some fast. Mostly slow, it was one of those weeks. South Street is a haven for those that choose to dress differently and be original. The meccha of strange. 'Is this...' 'Yes it is Talena. It is.' A black hoodie, hair spiked up and facial piercings. Tears slowly roll down the 15 year olds cheeks. The girl that he was supposed to be going with wouldn't come today. She was with her new boyfriend. The pain was his first real relationship heartbreak in his life. He wished everyone would just ignore him but he couldn't help his tears. 'I still hate her for that...' 'You fixed it later luv, its ok now.' 'I'm glad shes dead'. A girl, the snow collecting on her hair, she shakes it off just to let it collect again. She is alone again. Always alone. Her sister didn't want to come, her mom, her dad, none of them care. Too busy with eachother. It's so cold in her life and so cold outside. Her warmth was standing in line. 'The snow made everything so beautiful.' 'I think the fact you were there made me happier then anything else.' In a moment it was like someone picked up the snow globe of life and shook it, tears dried, memories faded. For now everything would be alright. 'Shhh... this is where I saw you for the first time.' 'I remember...' 'Hi.' It's all it took. Talena smiled slightly, she didn't want anyone near her tonight, she just wanted to let the music make her feel better and end it at that. She looked back down at the ground, waiting for it all to end mercifully. 'You gave me such a cold shoulder.' 'Shush my pretty.' He handed his ticket to the guy, who in turn asked for photo indentification. He handed it to him and took his hood down for the man to see. Talena's eyes lit up. Love. Is this love? This is love. She caught up to him, not knowing why, not knowing who he was or why she felt this way, not believing in love at first sight... or second sight for that matter, but she felt it. She grabbed his arm and turned him around to face her, his ice blue eyes were deep enough to see the future, and she caught his gaze, and she saw it all. I try on my blue shirt she told me she liked it she wonders what I'll wear she knows just what she'll wear she always wears blue It was years of love. She wanted it. 'I thought you were completely nuts.' 'The stars told me you would be there.' '...well I love you anyway.' The band started to play, the crowd surged forward and the pair stood idly in the back, alone in the spot between two spotlights. Love. Happiness. For now. Talena opened her black painted lips, her perfect white teeth showing through. She wanted to speak, to say anything to justify this moment that they both just had without saying a word. He moved down and kissed her, not a particularly passionate kiss, but one that dripped of romance. The moment. It was perfect. It was all just perfect. The band played on and on, and they stood there, and they held eachother. 'We didn't even need to talk.' 'It was Destiny, lover, the old fool needed to keep us both alive.' The music didn't matter. The people didn't matter. I'm starting to panic remember she asked you The moment was all that mattered. They were meant for eachother. It all made sense now, every single problem and heartbreak they both had has led to this exact moment in time. Etched into the great book of what is to happen. Destiny. Fate. It was true. It existed. Realms. Love. It was all something true. Not just tales told from grandfather to grandchild. It all existed. It all existed somewhere. You just had to find it. The right place and the right time. The young couple embraces, the boy with the weird piercings and the blue hair, and the girl that talked to her doll and was thought to be insane. Outcasts who were cast together in the same play in eachothers lives. Remember to Breathe The view pulled out, through the crowd, through the band, out the door, past the ticket taker, across the snow littered street where everything was beautiful and white, children were playing in it and adults were too. Everyone was together for this one moment, like the hurt and the pain was shut off for the entire world. Across the street. To where they stood now, six years after they met. This was true. DarkStar looked down to Talena as the scene around them faded back to the present, where they stood on the same spot. Her tears were still frozen and the snow still rested on the shoulders of DarkStars jacket. And the memories fade She held onto him tight. Like a light wind was going to take him away and out of her arms, out of her warm embrance on what was now a July 10th night. Today was the day they first started being officially together, but that day with the snow on the ground was the day their hearts first gripped eachother and never let go. 'I love you Talena', his words were heavy, dropping to the sidewalk as he said them. She looked up at him. Once again she could see the future in those pale blue eyes. And it made her smile. Flashes of the couple all those years ago, and the first kiss. Talena opened her black lips. DarkStar leaned down enough to reach her. They embraced. The view pulls out and away, into the sky as the people walk by the couple deep in embrace. Past the town, out of the city, off the continent, into the sky... And somehow... a single snowflake comes into view. And we'll be okay... we'll be okay.
