014 - Untitled (Thoughts And Reflection)

15 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10.15.02 Daylight entered the window as in all customary forms of life, there is that first light to wake them up. Life for the twenty-four year old man currently in hibernation for the moment hadn't changed since he was twenty-three. Maybe fame, and a horde of money might change a person, but not Dante Jackson. It evolves him into a greed ready, easy person who would love to take, and take away from people. That's the difference between a year - the thoughts and ideals of what that previous year happened to go into. None of it happened to scare it, rather it made him feel better about himself. Victories were inconsistent, but it turned him back into the person he wanted to be in the outset of it all. An Anti-Hero, the thing people love to hate. Statuz Quo had an impact on his life, because like Dante he didn't care who did this and who did that to him. As long as they brought it to his face, he'd come back with open aggression and payback to boot. You could have said that Statuz and Dante happened to be that perfect team that worked off one another with a bullet. No rock group could hit Dante off the charts, no gang of Cryps could even touch Statuz Quo. Gaz Green and Nerezza was becoming that sensible notion of hate in the two men, and they maybe brought the best out in them. But it wasn't about them this week, it was more about everyone in general. As potential lay for countless others, they were the potential to rise above them. Or vice versa. 'Wake up...' the voice trails off in his mind as he flutters his eyes open inside of a Kenoto Motel - 11:15 EST, a few hours Black Friday Fusion's Third Annual Mall Brawl before standing over him was M.W.L.L.F.B.A (Also Known As Bill Cosby) puffing away at a joint he'd lit only minutes before. The snow ran deep on the television screen as Dante had been up all night, planning and studying his opponent. The small, yet spacious hotel room proved enough theatrics for him, ran by some bastardized cracker with a toupee and a greasy head underneath that. 'Its, Monday?' Dante asked his friend who nodded silently and laughed it up. He raised the joint to his face, before taking a few more puffs. 'Why you hittin' that shit this early?' The man laughed, ''Cause I feel like it! You try going through a hard ass night without a joint...' 'I've done that two times, in the last two weeks and I seem to be getting better by it.' he snorted gruffly, clearing his nose. He'd slept in the same shirt he wore on the flight, a faded Bulls jersey and black Nike shorts. Bill only stared at his acquaintance before turning his head and walking out the door, citing he needed to get some fresh air for a change. Dante didn't care all that much about weed, notably using it when he needed to calm his nerves. He was becoming more of a sluggish beast, whose blood lust went deep into his days, rather than those Friday nights he happened to work upon. Walking into this hotel room at 11:45 last night, he happened to do one thing - plop on the bed and watch American Psycho. Something from Patrick Bateman read into his mind, an interesting tidbit while he was making himself, prettier in an essence. Dante wanted things that Bateman had, but it didn't really make sense to become a homicidal killer for the sake of being the curse of 'yuppie' life. He knew various things, and how other people, such as Veneneman Pendragon, Rent - A- Hero, The Pharaoh, and Akiyama plays around in a charade of things, hiding his true identity almost like your Bruce Waynes and Clark Kents. For all Dante could suspect, they could have been multi millionaires who hate wrestling but live around like they love it. 'What in the hell?' he questions out to the telephone - checking his messages. The robot like voice strung out in a chord wiht him. He had three messages between nine-fifteen, and ten-thirty this morning. For what reason he didn't even know. Checking the messages, he read off two people he knew, Tracy and Statuz. Simple enough, he called them back before looking at the third number. 'Reno...' it rolled off his tongue as if the name just would not happen to leave him and go away. The man who persuaded him to go into wrestling, and thus why he was here in the first place. He hated him for it, because it made Dante feel less important to himself. There is an idea of a Dante Jackson; some kind of abstraction. But there is not a real one: only an entity, something illusory. And though he can hide his cold gaze, and you can shake his hand and feel flesh gripping yours and maybe you can even sense your lifestyles are probably comparable... he is simply not there. 'RENO!' he growls before launching the telephone through the window. The shattering impact caused his neighbors to look out their various doors and see the telephone lying there, with glass all around it. The idiots, he thought. He wasn't an insecure person, not in the likely sense. But...he was a vain, callous and cold son of a bitch who took El Spoffy! and Livewire like a jagged little pill etched in his stomach. 'What in the hell is going on?' The Man Who Looks Like Famous Black Actors (Also Known As Bill Cosby) bursted into the room, waving around the joint as if it were a life preserve. Dante shook his head, grinning. 'Nothing, except a few ghost of the past creeping up.' 'Bull homie, I think you'll end up getting killed by them esse' who talks all of that shit into your mind.' The Man Who Looks Like Famous Black Actors (Also Known As Bill Cosby) stated in bold face. 'You think so?' 'Yea-' 'You don't know what the fuck to think, so don't even say anything.' Dante interrupted him in mid speech. 'For the past month or so, I've entertained you with some of my bullshit. So you know what? As soon as you figure out what my bullshit is about, then don't come to me with more bullshit.' He got up from the bed, and took a walk along the boardwalk without The Man Who Looks Like Famous Black Actors (Also Known As Bill Cosby). Time was passing away from Fusion's showtime start and his match with the likes of Pendragon, Syko the Goblin, Snow, Rent - A - Hero; everybody who posed a threat which he thought - no knows victory is his. But first, he thought he'd play a game with everybody, to see if he was awake. To himself he said, 'I think my mask of sanity is about to slip.'
