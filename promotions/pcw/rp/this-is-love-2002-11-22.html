This Is Love

Title: This Is Love Featuring: Xavier Kannon Date: 22/11/02 What motivates a man to step into the Alliances’ Cages and risk his health? Are the prizes on offer really that great that someone will have their face raked across wire mesh? Risk their very lives on walkways high above the arena floor? Or face the risk of injury from not just one opponent, but 56? It can’t all be about the Title shots, can it? “You’d be surprised.” Why would Xavier want to go through this to get a Title shot when he could just get Ellie~! to book him in one anyway? He has already held the belt once, for over 2 months, so why all this just for a match? Even if he doesn’t take the World Title shot, what else is there on offer? He’s already held the Continental Title, has his name in the Extreme Title history and the TV Title could surely only be a consideration if he wanted a sweep. So why? “It’s hard to answer what drives a man.” And we don’t think anyone will really ever know what makes Kannon tick. What are the reasons for wanting to win Alliances so badly then? If he doesn’t want it for the Title shot, could it be for the kudos? The right to say he won the first InterFed Alliances match, to say he beat the best the PCW family had to offer? Respect? “Why would I want you peons to respect me.” Scratch that idea, after all, if the Scientologist cared what people thought of him he would be a very different person, maybe even palatable. So it isn’t for the prize, probably not for respect... What’s left? To say he’s done what Jeremy Howard has done? “Don’t even mention that name around me.” Last year it was the Krayzie One himself, as part of Asylum with Miyagawa, Ed and Timo Bolamba who left the Cages victorious, if bloodied and beaten. “The same way he left the Cage last time we had a match.” At one time Xavier was called the next Jeremy Howard, being groomed by Krayzie as his successor... But Xavier couldn’t wait that long. He won the World and Continental just like Jeremy, does he want to win Alliances to show that he’s just as good? Of course, Xavier will never be the man Jeremy was. He wouldn’t want to be. “There’s a certain something that goes with winning.” What is the driving force of the arrogant Scientologist? There is something that drives him on, that flicks the right switches in his brain and gives him the desire to win. Greed? Does he want to win it just so nobody else can? “Greed was selected out, along with it’s out-of-date master.” Love. And not as in “One Blood, One Love....” There are very few things on this planet that Xavier Kannon can say he truly loves. His faith, his money... And his wife. “For once, you hit the nail on his head.” If both were better human beings, the relationship between Xavier Kannon and Eleanor Hall would have been a wrestling fairy tale. The failed actress and the rookie NTICW Champion paired together for show, who fell in love and went on to run the biggest wrestling promotion in the world. It would have been about a year ago now that they met, and very quickly the business became pleasure, before that pleasure evolved to love, and that love became devotion. Xavier does things because of Ellie. “Could you ever say no to her?” One night together spawned into more. One hotel room somewhere on the NTICW road schedule led to the birth of the new PCW wrestling empire in the Third Age. What would Xavier be like without his Eleanor at his side? He wouldn’t be Xavier for a start. “Winning makes her happy, therefore I win.” Say what you want about Xavier Kannon as a person, say what you want about all the terrible things that he has done, careers he has ruined and cheap shots he has taken... There aren’t that many men that would do what he does for love. Even giving an Orphan the Hallmark through the PCW announce table was done for love. Even though she shouldn’t of, Ellie~! enjoyed it thus Xavier did it. The first couple of PCW is what keeps the wheels turning. “And keep the Scientologist winning.” So on Sunday, when “Rock is Dead” hits and the Alamo Dome becomes a cauldron of hate for the Front Office to walk in to, Xavier will head into the cages and risk his body just like he does every week to make his wife happy. Her husband winning and brining disappointment and misery to others bringing a smile to her Pink lips. Xavier and Eleanor Kannon-Hall. Perfect for each other. And that’s hardly a compliment. “Trust me, she’ll still be smiling Sunday night.” Is there a stronger force to drive a man on? Are there any people in PCW who’d put a Title shot above true love and devotion? Who’d rather have the respect of strangers than a smile on their loved one’s face? The powerful driving force has driven him hard, and driven him well. “And I’m not going to stop loving her.” So there’ll be no end to it. ---- View Xavier Kannon's Biography
