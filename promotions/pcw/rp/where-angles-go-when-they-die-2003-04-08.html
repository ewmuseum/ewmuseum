Where Angles Go When They Die

8 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4 - 8 - 03 [The scene opens in what appears to be a barren, tundra-like land, completely void of vegetation and absolutely covered in a never-ending blanket of snow. Flakes of ice and snow rain down from the sky at a brisk, veiling pace, further encasing the terrain in a sheath of frozen water vapor. A sharp whistling noise sounds through the air, growing louder than softer with each change of the air jet currents. The sky above matches the ground in the fact that it too is blanketed, but it is instead blanketed by intimidating gray clouds, completely shielding the ground from the sun’s warming rays.] ”D-E-A-T-H… find out what it means to me…” [Creeping through the air like a mole through the dirt, a voice finds its way past the whistling wind and makes itself apparent. A dark shadow slowly moves through the frozen sea of snow, trudging along with difficulty but yet unhindered by the desolate frozen wasteland.] ”If only I had a angle…” [It takes on a singsong tune, strangely familiar. The blackened figure moves through the falling snow and bursts forth with a grunt, feeling the impact of the wind against his body. It’s Dorn Tracer, wrapped in his black leather trench coat with his hands jammed in his pockets.] Tracer, shivering: En’Flarim damn this desolate place of frozen Earth. Why does it have to be out here?! Why can’t it be in sunny Daytona, Florida?! Tracer: I can’t see why it had to die out here. There are MUCH better places to be deceased than this. [The Astronomer trudges along, giving a grunt every now and then from having to enact so much force upon the unforgiving terrain. He seems to be talking to himself, but yet, it’s a conversation. As if someone is answering.] Tracer: I don’t ever remember coming out this way. So how would it end up here? … Tracer, nodding: Ah, I see your point. That still doesn’t make up for the fact that I have to drag my ass out here and look for it!! … Tracer: Of course I want it back, but couldn’t we replace it somehow?! I mean, look where I’m at! I’ll be lucky to find my way out again! I’ll be lucky to even LIVE long enough to find it!! [Dorn shakes his head in agitated dismay and continues along, staring at the faceless white at his feet as he goes. The Shooting Star Champion seems to be in search of something, but what?] Tracer: Are you sure it’s here? … Tracer: Bah, where’s the North Star when I need it?! I don’t even know if I’m heading in the right direction. … Tracer: If you say so… [One has to wonder whom he’s talking to, but that’s not important right now. As the Astronomer trudges along, his faith dropping at a very rapid pace, his foot suddenly slams into something solid. He lets out a cry of agonizing pain and yanks his foot clear out of the snow, holding it with his hands and leaping around on one foot while barking obscenities.] Tracer: STUPID MOTHERFUCKIN’ COCKSUCKIN’ RAZZIN’ FRAZZIN’… … Tracer, VERY angry: No I will NOT calm down!! That fucking hurt! My toes are frozen solid and now I smacked them on some stupid rock! I wouldn’t be surprised if I pulled off my shoe and my toes were laying under my foot in pieces!! … Tracer: I am not being ridiculous! That seriously hurt! Tracer: Be damned… I want to know what that was! [Dorn stomps back over to the area of impact, still favoring his injured foot, and begins to kick the snow aside faster than the falling vapor can replace it. But when his foot begins to get tired and the object still isn’t uncovered, Dorn drops to his knees and begins to dig with his hands.] … [Tracer gives… something a dirty look and continues digging. After about a foot or so down, his fingers rub against something cold and solid. The Astronomer lets out a satisfied, if not slightly evil, grin and begins to feel around the object. Finding its squared edges, Dorn begins to dig around it as fast as he can, eager to see what it is. The object slowly manifests itself from beneath the snow, and it appears to be a big rectangular block of ice with something dark within. The Shooting Star Champion wipes away the excess snow from the top and gazes down into the clear ice… and gasps.] Tracer: What in the soiled name of Sheor sitting on a bloody rock while shitting and eating Frittos is this?!?! … Tracer: WELL THAT’S PRETTY OBVIOUS, DON’T YOU THINK? I mean, look! It’s him! [Indeed encased within the sarcophagus of ice is… Jonathan Drachen. Frozen with his eyes open wide, it looks as though something pushed him into the giant ice cube. His hands are pressed against the roof of cold above him and his body is completely unmoving. Dorn stands up and scratches his frozen chin, very confused by this whole thing.] Tracer: Correct me if I’m wrong, but I didn’t think we had someone bring him out here and bury him in the snow. … Tracer: Didn’t think so. So what happened, you think? … Tracer: Blasphemy! Why would he bury himself in the snow? …Tracer: I don’t understand… but alright, I’ll take a closer look. [Tracer gets on his knees again and kneels his face as close to the ice as possible without actually touching it. His crystal blue eyes squint behind their veil of dark shades and try to focus in on what else the ice cube contain. A faint object can be made out, and it appears to glint every once and awhile. Confused, Dorn gets up and walks over to the part of the ice where Drachen’s head is. The Astronomer leans down and grips the underside of the ice, then takes in a deep breath.] Tracer: Here we go!! [With all the might of the stars, Dorn lifts up the ice and heaves it forward, standing it up so he can get a better look. His face turns beet red from the moment of excruciating force and he wipes his now sweating brow, puzzled as to why that was so difficult. Ignoring his pain, however, he brushes the snow off the back part of the ice and gazes into the prison of cold. When his eyes fall upon the clear view of the object, he gasps again!] Tracer: This is VERY fucking strange… it’s… … Tracer: Exactly! Is that crazy or what?! I mean, look, I’m wearing it right now! [Dorn undoes his jacket and throws it open, revealing the brilliant visage of the PCW Shooting Star Title. He rubs its face gently, reveling in its golden aura, then turns back to the block of ice.] Tracer: How is it that my title AND Drachen are both in there, yet I saw Drachen not too long ago and I’m wearing the title right now? … Tracer: Oh boy… what else am I going to see? … Tracer, sighing: Alright, fine… [The Astronomer moves around to the right side of the ice and gazes to the inside. What he sees makes him swear that it’s SNOWING in that little area of the ice… but seeing as how that’s impossible, he deduces that a fucking T.V. is in that block of ice!] Tracer: It’s a fucking TV!! … Tracer: Nothing’s playing, though. … Tracer: Gah, fine! [Dorn stares at the strange monitor for a moment with an inquiring look in his eye, wondering what he’s watching for. Only a minute after he begins to stare, the TV tunes into something that hits home with Dorn. Playing on the monitor, in clear vision, is the image of a silver-haired man executing a brutal wrestling move to what appears to be Jonathan Drachen. Tracer tilts his head in puzzlement and watches as the TV’s reception goes out, then comes right back in with a different image. This time, it shows four men stuck in a cage, which then explodes.] Tracer: What is this…? Is it… … Tracer: … you’ve got to be fucking kidding. … … [Tracer’s eyes widen and his jaw slowly drops open as a feeling of horror envelops his body.] Tracer: It’s… MY ANGLE! … Tracer: In the name of Tartorus, what is going on?!?!?!? We have to get them out of there! … Tracer, in a panic: What are you talking about? It is not! Now, give me the strength to release them from their icy prison! [Dorn begins to pound on the ice with his fists in a futile attempt to break his ‘angle’ free, but alas, it comes to naught. It seems as if the more he tries, the tougher the ice becomes. After a minute or so, Dorn collapses against the ice, exhausted.] Tracer: It…can’t be… … Tracer: NO! I WON’T BELIEVE IT! ANNNGGGGGGLLLEEEEEEEE!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! [Dorn’s cry for revenge falls on deaf ears, however… or so he thinks. All of a sudden, a man wearing a black Alumni suit and a short haircut comes bursting out of the moving walls of snow and taps Dorn on the shoulder. The grief-stricken Astronomer gives him a sorrowed look and shakes his head.] Tracer: It’s dead… my angle is dead… Man: Yes Mr. Tracer, it is. Tracer: Who’re you? Man: Me? I’m… [The man points at a blue and white nametag that reads ‘Hi, my name is…’, and on the next line in black marker, it says R. LIFE. Dorn reads it again, and gives the man a confused stare.] Tracer: I don’t get it! Life: I’m REAL LIFE, Dorn! And you are in The Place Where Angles Go When They Die! Tracer, still confused: … come again? Life: Your angle is dead, Dorn. I killed it, and now it’s here. Tracer, becoming enraged: YOU KILLED MY ANGLE?! Life: Yes, Dorn, I did. And there’s nothing you can do about it!! You must now make a showing at Spring Fling that will be absolutely pathetic! [Dorn darts to his feet with a look that could curdle and evaporate milk on his face and grabs the grinning man by the collar of his suit.] Tracer: BLASPHEMY! You’re just some cocaine-addict out here to fuck with me!! I’ll get my angle back if it kills me! Life: Too late for your angle, Dorn. It was shaping up to be something special, too. But alas, it had to die because of outside complications. Your luck is rather shitty, wouldn’t you say? Tracer: LIES! ALL LIES! ANNGGGLLLEE!!!!! Life: IT’S TOO LATE! TOO LATE! HAHAHHAHAHAHAHAHAAH!! [The man breaks out in a sinister laugh as Dorn drops to his knees and raises his fists to the heavens, crying out in a fit of sadness and rage.] Dorn: NOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO!!!!!!!!!! [And with that, R. Life shoves Dorn face first into the snow and disappears into thin air, leaving the Astronomer to dwell in the icy cold of death and despair.] [End.]
