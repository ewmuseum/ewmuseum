I Come Back Only to Find Canadians!

3 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/3/02 The scene opens up with a shot of the Giant Arch that welcomes one into the city of St. Louis, Missouri. The same shot featured on the promotional poster for March Madness, that’s what the scene is now. Tonight will make or break some people in PCW. For Snow, it may be the definition of his championship reign, as just a stroke of luck, or the mark of a true champion. For Legend Inc, it may mean an over-hyped return, or redemption for his last trip to PCW. For Rush, it will mean conquering the odds either way. He’s come from “death” twice, beaten two tough opponents, both of whom were picked to beat him, to this one night. It all comes down to tonight. Tonight will be a night of Greatness… Our promo actually opens up on Rush on the highway. All that can be seen is his head, bouncing up and down. No he’s not doing that you sick freak. The camera pans outwards to see Rush atop his white stallion, which he has ridden all the way out here. On the side of the highway, while the cars are at a stand-still coming into the city, Rush swiftly rides the majestic animal along the grass on the side of the highway. As they enter the inner part of the city, many people stare at Rush, who stands out a lot, being a black guy riding a saddled horse through a major metropolis. However, Rush pays no attention and is just glad to be back in big cities. He looks at all the big buildings, newly appreciative of them. Through all this beauty, Rush realizes that he needs to find the hotel that he was scheduled to be checked into about five days ago. Searching through his pockets, he finds lots of stuff, but he also finds a small scrap of paper that says “The St. Raphael.” Realizing that he has no idea where to start looking, he pulls up on his horse next to a guy on the street. Rush: Hey there sir. Do you know where I could find the St. Raphael hotel? I need to get there pretty quickly and I’m not from around here. Man: Well I’m not quite sure I’ve ever heard of such a hotel. You see, I was just watching the Kansas vs Missouri game and I’m pretty pissed off that Clarence Gilbert fucked his team because he took a retarted shot when he could’ve passed the ball to Paulding who had already hit five 3’s. Rush: Oh, I see. Well that’s great but I need to find someone who knows the place I’m talking about. Thanks for your help anyway. Rush sees a bank across the street, and begins to bring the horse across the street, oblivious to the fact that he makes all the traffic come to a screeching halt. He arrives across the street, and tells the horse to sit outside, not realizing that horses aren’t dogs. He enters the bank, which has marble walls and is very official looking. He goes up to a teller window, where a middle-aged woman, who is filing her nails, looks up at him. He tries to put on his best manners, changing his voice to sound more respectable. Rush: Hello there ma’am. I’m looking for the St. Raphael hotel. Do you think you could direct me towards where I could find it? Teller: Welcome to Fleet sir, withdrawal or deposit? Rush: No actually I’m looking for a hotel, do you have directions around the city or even just a map that I could use? Teller: Withdrawal or deposit? Rush: What would you say if I told you that I’m rich, famous, and you can even touch my hand if you give me these directions, which I need very much by the way. Teller: Hmm…let me think: Withdrawal or deposit? Rush sighs, but realizes that he has no pocket money with him at the moment, so he decides that he might as well get some money, for food and drink before his big match tonight. Rush: I guess it’s going to be withdrawal. Teller: Can I see your card, please sir? Rush pulls out his wallet, handing his debit card to the women. She takes it into the back, and stays back there for a few moments. She emerges and hands Rush’s card back to him. Teller: Sorry I can’t process that card. Rush: What’re you talking about? This is a bank. I have a debit card. What’s so tough to understand. Teller: Sir, you’re going to have to go to the next window. Rush looks over at the next window, which also features a very large line up to it, with lots of irritated people waiting to get their money, all as disgruntled as this teller. Rush: Why do I have to go to that window? What’s so different about that one from this window? Teller: She’s got the card-scanning mechanism. Rush: So? Teller: So you’re going to have to go to that line if you want to be helped. Rush: Why don’t you bring the card scanner over to your window for a minute? Or even better, why don’t you scan the card over there and then you can give me my money? Teller: Sorry that won’t work. Rush: Why not?! Teller: Withdrawal or deposit? Rush: Can you tell me what country you’re from? Teller: Canada, why do you care? Rush: It figures. There are oot, I mean out to get to me. Rush walks out of the bank, steaming at his treatment inside the establishment. He hops back up onto the horse, and tries to devise a new plan, with which to find his way to the hotel. He decides that he will just head to the Saavis Center, and no one will be there yet, but maybe PCW officials can tell him where the hotel is, or even give him a ride. He begins to walk the horse, and soon they arrive at a large park. The park is very reminiscent of New York’s Central Park, with lots of people going through, so Rush tries to go on the path with the least people. He finds a nice little paved path that goes along the Mississippi River, with very few people on it. Rush: Now this is nice and relaxing. No stupid bank tellers to worry about. Rush continues to stroll down the path, atop his trusty steed, until he comes across a bench where a man and a woman are sitting, holding hands. Rush: That’s nice to see two young people in love. Man: We’re not in love, this is my sister. Rush: Your sister?! That’s incest son. Woman: Please sir, keep your disgusting comments to yourself. We aren’t doing anything inappropriate. The two then begin to kiss each other, rapidly, as Rush watches on in disbelief of their statements. Rush: Are you joking me? Are you absolutely bull-shitting me? You two are switching saliva, which is exactly the same type I might add, which is extremely disgusting. Man: Sir, that is how we show family affection. If you have a problem with how our family works, then you may go back to the comfort of your own home, but in public we can do as we please. Rush: If you could do as you please, your sister would be naked right now. Woman: That’s disgusting, you pervert. Why would he want to see me naked, he’s my brother? Rush: How the hell should I know, you two are the ones getting all freaky in the park, not me. The two now begin to passionately kiss, what is commonly referred to as making out. The brother kisses her neck and they begin to whisper into each other’s ears. Rush: Okay, this has gone way too far. Cut this out, I’m gonna puke Woman: What are you talking about? Listen buddy, if you’re not used to this kind of behavior, that’s your business, but we’re just exchanging a family welcome. Rush: Yeah, like a welcome for your sisters tongue down your throat. Damn, Missouri is a weird state. Man: Do you really think that you can treat us like this just because we oppressed your people into slavery for hundreds of years and still discriminate against blacks? Rush: Good question skippy. Do you think I like white people in the first place? Not particularly. Do you two give white people a bad name? If you don’t, then my name isn’t Rush Mason. The man ignores Rush’s last comment, and begins to bring his hand up his sister’s shirt, feeling around, and his hand obviously slips under her bra, now cupping her breast. Rush: What kind of sick people are you? You’re going upstairs on your sister. The only other person I know who does that is Kid Wonder on Amanda. And the fact that Amanda is hot, while she is a traitorous she-bitch, makes up for that. If I was Kid Wonder, I’d bang my sister too, but you two just take it to another sickening level. Man: That’s it sir. Do you want to fight me, because I’m ready? I will not have you insult me and my sister like that on such a derogatory level! Rush: What’re you gonna do, Poindexter? Are you gonna bitchslap me? Woman: Don’t bother listening to that loudmouth. He’s just jealous of what a good family relationship we have. He probably had crackhead parents or something. Suddenly, Rush’s horse, Barney kicks out with his front foot, nailing the woman right in her shin. She cringes in pain, and collapses as her brother brings her over onto the grass. Rush: Damn, Barney, good kick. Oh yeah, and bitch, my real parents were crackheads, thank you very much. Incest may resume now. The woman flicks Rush off as he begins to trot off the park. Rush looks back to see the woman’s brother laying on top of her, kissing her rapidly again. Rush: I forgot to ask, where are you two lovebirds from? Woman: Canada, so fuck off. Your horse is gonna pay, but for now I’ll just have to take care of you. Rush: No thanks, I’ll keep my eyes to myself for this one. Rush walks away from the disturbing scene, focusing on the arena that is now in front of him. The Saavis Center makes everything very real for Rush. Everything that he has gone through to make it here is all worth it. The only thing standing in his way is a tall white guy wearing a security jacket. Rush, still riding on Barney, walks up to the man and initiates a conversation. Rush: Hey there good protector of the Pay Per View venue. Security Guard: Are you on the list? Rush: Uhh…what list would this be. Security Guard: You’d know if you were on it, are you on the list? Rush: Yeah, I’m on it. My name is Jimmy Spinks, but you can call me Kid Wonder. I’m the most Wonderful superstar there is. Security Guard: You are not Mr. Spinks, he was here earlier today. Rush quickly regroups his thoughts, thinking of some sort of comeback. Rush: Of course I’m not. Can’t you take a joke? Security Guard: Sir, are you on the list? Rush: Yes, my name is Jason Snow. Security Guard: Looks like his name has been checked off as well. That’s two strikes buddy. Are you on the list? Rush: Wow, you really have no sense of humor. My name is Orpheus Grant. Security Guard: Strike three, you’re out. Nice try. Rush: I was kidding again! My name is Rush Mason, I guarantee I’m on the list. The guard looks down at his list, then up at Rush again. Security Guard: Oh that’s right you are on the list. The I’m aboot to kill these people list. Rush falls to the ground laughing at this man’s pronounciation of the word about. Rush: You’re what? Secuirty Guard: I’m aboot to kill you, I don’t see what’s so funny. Rush can’t stop laughing at this, but finally at least regains control of himself long enough to think up a plan. Rush: You’re from Canada right? Security Guard: Yes. Rush points as he yells out to the security guard. Rush: Oh my Crunk, look there’s a high-sticking penalty by a moose over there carrying Canadian Bacon! Security Guard: Where? Too late. Rush is already inside, when the man turns around. Rush’s vast knowledge of Canada’s hobbies: moose, hockey, and Canadian Bacon,; comes in handy once again. Rush is inside trying to figure out where his hotel is, while four pissed off Canadians think about one guy who annoyed them. If only every day were this productive…
