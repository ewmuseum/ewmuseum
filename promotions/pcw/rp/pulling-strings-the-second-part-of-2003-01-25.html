Pulling Strings, The Second Part of

25 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: January 25, 2003 Running a cold shower down upon his already blood cold body, Dante Jackson had a feeling that something was to change inside of him. Words couldn’t describe what he feels at the moment, just the sense of touch and scraping of skin cells from the body. He turned his face outward, a slight angle as he continued to have the cold water run down from his scalp down to his face and run through his eyes like tears of pain. 7:14 pm, a full two days until he would not be able to see his family for another week, the same usual trait he had been going through ever since he signed on the dotted line for his PCW contract. Old School Wrestling wasn’t going to accept him; he’d seen what they had done with their wrestlers such as Frankie Bones, Drew Hostyle and Jordan X Shabazz whom had left before he was ever seen on television. Looking down towards a schedule planner Tracy had brought for him late last year for a travel guide to see just where he would be on the road so Tracy could plan and spend money on the tickets to the cities. Next road was overseas, to an event he’d seen only last year, sitting down on his couch with a bowl of chips in his hand and a bevy of friends whom had come to the apartment complex to see whether or not the Rams would win the Super Bowl from the Patriots. “Are you just going to stand there, looking over schedules?” the hurried voice told him as he leaned up against the wall. Black shades, with a pair of Timberland Chukka Boots and black Brooklyn Xpress pants. The urban renaissance for dress apparel as it would be known on the street corners. “C’mon dawg, you know you have to understand what these fools are putting out. You’ve deciphered the music game and you’re panicking over the damn wrestling game.” Dante shakes his head, as the water runs down on his head more, “Bishop, this is not the time –” “For inspiration? Yeah, I know but with Chet King on the horizon, you sure do look like you need all the help you can get.” Bishop stamps his foot down, lightly not affecting the flooring. His wife beater sticks onto him like molasses; the bulk of his arms are completely rivaled by others on the block. “Remember, your wife barely notices you anymore.” “Bishop, don’t tell me about my wife,” he jabs non heartedly, pushing the water dial inward, the water no longer impeding him with cold daggers and hard reminders that you shouldn’t be apart of this treatment. “She’s not a gold digger.” “True, but when was the last time you went on a concert? She hasn’t been to those, either.” Dante hops out the shower, grabbing a towel from the rack stationed nearest to the glass door. Rubbing his face, soap, dirt, anything that has not been covered with the rag, now hanging ceremoniously from the hangar in the small box is being collected. “Concerts,” Dante starts up as he wraps the towel around his mid-section, the ripped area that has garnered him a few noticeable magazine shoots. “Do I seriously need them?” Bishop nods, “If you want to be considered just a musician/wrestler, durable to do things that normal men couldn’t do.” “That’s the point, I don’t want to be like them.” He moves over towards the closet, pulling back the closet door. He eyes up and down, checking to see what he would want to wear, or not want to wear, a picky decision that would usually be made by either his stylist or wife. Pulling out a long black Gucci dress shirt with faded blue pants to match with them, he hangs them up against the doorknob. Twisting the knob, pushing through he looks up into the mirror, seeing his face with the pretty white teeth and a point of which scars are barley noticeable. All your noticeable features for a face is decked out there for him to understand what he needs to keep his face in the tip top condition that Dante happens to keep his face in such condition. “You pretty boy…” “Called me one in high school didn’t ya?” Dante turns towards Bishop, chuckling at the comment. “Got me Prom King in the process.” “Teachers were on your dick because your father was a World Champion in Wrestling, they grew up watching him and expected you to do something just like him.” Dante nodded, “Just a question. Did anybody other than me not know that my father was a wrestler?” “Yeah, everybody knew.” Bishop tossed off his glasses, hanging them along his jean pocket. “Your teachers, your friends, everybo-” “Then why’d you hide it?!” he roars in the defined voice that larger men like Ivan Staninslav or the late Meanstreak would make towards their opponents if they threw a measly punch in their direction. “Why’d everyone hide it from me?!” Bishop shakes his head as Dante sinks down onto his bed, lonely and barren to everyone that would sit inside of it. There was no shoulder to cry on, just a friend that had been there ever since he could string together rhymes and sentences for a living. They were childhood friends, but yet they had grown farther and farther apart until they reunited, only for bitter rivals and beef to take them away once more. Its all apart of the game, Dante continues to think before a solitary tear runs down his face. “C’mon, you’ve got Shanghai to think about…” A nod from Dante as he sulks down, starring at the beige carpet, as if it were the holy grail he was starring into and his palms where within a slight tug of caressing it. “Shanghai,” he snorts before getting up from the bed. “night were I turn into something big eh?” “Sure is,” Bishop remarks with a tongued wit towards his face. “Night where you put Chet King out of his misery, correct?” Dante stifles, cracking his neck back before pushing his arms and shoulder onto the shirt, having the open hole at the bottom cover him like a suit of armor. “Tonight, you do your daily routine, I am in need of some help.” “Medicinal or strangely fucked up?” Bishop mused with a quickly arranged quip that sent Dante towards the door, his blood starting to boil once more. “How about both?” He smiled, before shutting the door close, leaving Bishop to sit there, wondering.
