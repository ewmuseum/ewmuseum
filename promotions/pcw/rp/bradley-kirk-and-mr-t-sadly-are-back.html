Date: ....Meh Now, everything is back to normal. Or is it? We won’t know simply. Eddy looks around and sees two young men perched on a bench, waiting for a bus. They looked familiar to say the least. One was short and chubby with short brown hair, and the other was tall and skinny with short brown hair. Eddy thought… Then he realized. BRADLEY AND KIRK! They were his friends, friends of very stupid promos when Eddy didn’t have the camera, and right beside them…none other than…Mr. T, who appear a lot in Eddy’s promos. Eddy stopped and rolled down his window. The men looked at him as in 'What the hell are you doing stopping?', but then, they remembered… '……Eddy?', said the short chubby one, Bradley. 'EDDY? Who da hell is Eddy?!', said Mr. T. 'Um, some dude…', said the tall one, Kirk. 'I know dat you foo’!', said Mr. T. 'Guys, Eddy, the wrestler, remember?!', Eddy said. '………' '………' '………' '…..Oh yeah! You’re that dude?', said Bradley. 'Yeah. Remember, you guys always stole my camera and made promos of you guys doing stupid stuff while I was away?' 'Now I remember!', shouted Kirk. 'Eddy? Ya mean dat dude?!!!' 'Eddy! F*cking great to see you…', Kirk said. 'Don’t cuss man', said Eddy. 'Oh, sorry.' 'You coming back to PCW?', Bradley said. 'Yup…' 'Awesome! We have the card right….Here…', said Bradley, pulling a piece of paper from his back pocket. Mr. T wasn’t even in the conversation, he was too busy eating a hot dog. 'Am I on it?', asked Eddy. 'Erm….Yes, you are…Against…Mark…Uh, Mark Makonie…' '…………' 'I don’t know who he is either dude.' 'OK…Mark Makonie. I have no idea, but whoever he is, I wish him a good match…Does he have a bio?' 'Uh….No. He has a picture.', Bradley said. 'Looks like a Nazi', Kirk said. Mr. T dumped a whole bowl of relish on his hot dog, followed by onions….Then shoved a whole half a hot dog down his throat. Bradley shook his head in disgust. Eddy glanced. 'Uh, he does, looks mean too. Anyway, I’m back around, so I’ll be around. You guys can steal my camera when you get a hand on it.' 'Noph! I wantpha comph!' said Mr. T which a hot dog in his mouth, spitting food as he talked. '…What?', said Bradley. 'I said I wantpha comph!' 'You understand what he’s saying Kirk?', Bradley asked. '….No.' 'I thought so.' 'Well, I’ll be off…Again. See y’all.' Both Bradley and Kirk waved as Eddy drove off, with Mr. T running after, going 'I wantpha comph!' The scene fades away, thankfully.
