Duke 'R. Kelly' Williams strikes again

6 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6/6/02 -Duke Williams sits in his chair with as sick look on his face. There is a puke bucket near his feet and a blanket over his back. He sits and talks right to the camera.- Williams: People I’m telling you ole Duke is getting old. I used to be able to party till the cows came home or Kannon’s lady friend which ever came first. Not anymore. Jesus I forgot how nasty kids came be!! -Duke gags, false alarm.- Williams: I’m not talking little demons either I’m talking college kids!! You see I may be too old to cut the mustard but I can damn sure lick the jar out. -Duke sticks out his long tongue and wags it around.- Williams: So with that I like to check out frat parties. Find me some young, dumb, horny, holes. The best booze, drugs, and rock and or roll. So that’s what I did last night. -An arrow points to Duke that reads “Last Night”. Duke is walking through a crowded party with lots of young, yuppie, college kids at there frat house. The ceilings are high as the building is old. Some couches litter the large living room as Duke walks through headed to the beer.- Kid: Hey man you want a beer? Williams: You got anything harder? -The kid opens the frigde to expose it over flowing with multicolored Jell-O shoots.- Kid: We have Jell-O shot for fifty cents a piece. -Duke reaches into his pocket and pulls out a big wad of crumpled up cash. He counts out three hundred dollars and hands it to the kid.- Williams: The Jell-O shot are now mine. Is that fair? Kid: More then. Williams: Good cus I’m putting you in charge. All that money is yours plus what you make with the new system. Jell-O shots are free for me, one dollar for the boys, and free for hot chicks your discretion. You dig? Kid: Yea! Williams: Plus I need a bottle of whiskey right now. Kid: I’ll get on that. -The camera returns back to the sick Duke Williams who is sitting in his chair in his home.- Williams: You see that America? Respect. What a beautiful thing. If only Willie Powell could understand that concept. You see at parties the guy watching the booze is general in the “in” crowd but the low man on the pole. He’s desperate to make friends and impress his “boys” so he’s easy pickings. Let’s get back to the party. Make sure you take notes. -We watch as Duke works the room towards a young blonde chick sitting on the coach. A yellow arrow points to her that reads “malice style victim number 1??”. Duke sits next to her on the coach and hands her a Jell-O shot.- Williams: Damn young lady your one hot piece of ass. I bet they just took you off the grill? -The girl smiles as they down their Jell-O shots.- Younglady: Thanks!! That’s a nice watch you most be rich. -Duke rubs his black Movoto watch.- Williams: Lets just say you and I can retire right now. -Duke puts his hand around the young lady as another frat kid comes up. He’s a big guy of about six feet and build like a rock.- BigKid: Hey look everybody, R. Kelly is here and he’s looking to score. -Duke becomes visually upset as is the young lady.- BigKid: Relax man I’m just kidding. -The camera cuts back to Duke in his chair at his home in Montana.- Williams: Hoss did you see that? Are you watching this? When a man is getting his mack on you don’t interrupt for all the tea in China. Is this not the code of party conduct? Have things changed that much in the last twenty years? Is this kid not looking to be sent Whiskey Bent and Hell Bound? Here’s you party tip number two. Just like when a dog pisses on a tree to mark his turf, men do the same thing to ladies. You see the arm around the ladies that means leave us the f*ck alone cus we’re going to f*ck. I know this bitch is after my wallet but that’s ok cus all I’m after is her back pouch. Don’t get her pregnant and no worries. But the problem is still there with this pud in the way. Look on buckaroo’s. -The camera cuts back to the coach with the lady and the bigkid. The big kid offers Duke a hand slap to apologizes.- BigKid: You a pretty big guy you ever play football? Williams: Nose tackle for the Patriots. BigKid; Wow that’s pretty cool. Did you actually touch the field? -Duke’s patience are running shorter then a Ty Nelson Stable life span.- BigKid: So what do you do now sell cars? Williams: I f*ck you mother sideways. Bigkid: Somebody has too. No really what do you do? Williams: I’m a pro-wrestler. BigMan: So you one of those guys who puts tights on and rolls around with other men in a fake sport. -Duke’s about to rip the kids head off and sh*t down his neck.- BigMan: Relax, I’m just kidding man. -The camera cuts back to Duke who just comes up from puking as he sits in his leather chair at his home.- Williams: Ok I admit I’m getting old. I should have know that this was the “just kidding Man” guy. Every party has this guy. He says what he thinks to be funny cus its offensive stuff. Then he says just kidding. A fat chick will walk in and he’ll say “damn Meagan your ass is getting real heavy.” She’ll get upset and he’ll say “just kidding.”. These people should die. Big f*cking tough guys with big f*cking mouths. You know how you shut these pricks up? Watch. -The scene returns to the party at the moment we left them.- Williams: You just kidding? BigGuy: Yea. Williams: You think your funny. BigGuy: Yea Williams: I don’t find you funny? BigGuy: Opinions are like assholes everybody has one and they all stink. Williams: I respect my opinion. BigGuy: I’m proud of you. -A crowd has now gathered as Duke is yelling and face to face with the big guy.- Williams: You think just because I’m old I can’t break your back and ruin your college football career? BigGuy: …… Williams: You think I don’t have a 45 in my boot that can blow you knee cap off? BigGuy: Look man I was just…….. Williams:…..Kidding. I know shut the f*ck up and back down bitch. Make like the Phantom Booker and disappear. -The big man backs down as a crowd claps on “The Malice Man” he takes a bow and sits down with his new found lady.- YoungLady: Your hot when your mad. Williams: Baby, I can get even madder if you want. -Duke winks as the two head up stairs. The camera fades and returns to Duke in his chair back in his home in Missoula.- Williams: Ha Ha!! Score one for the Malice Man!!! Woo!! Did we do it Malice Style? -Duke smiles as he holds up a hot pink thong from the young college freshman.- Williams: Nothing like the smell of stale p*ssy in the morning. Speaking of p*ssy’s did you see how that big mouth backed down?? You know a lot of guys talk a big game but most don’t follow it up. Not me hoss, I back up what I say. So come Fusion I’ll do just that when I pound that pud Nelson down to nothing. If Booker gets in my way I won’t back down I’ll kick his ass. If Willie want to tangle then its no. Fusion is the day of Malice. Somebody is going Whiskey Bent and Hell Bound and hoss that’s the honky tonk truth. -Duke gags again.- Williams: Oh yea why am I sick? Watch this. -Its later in the party, Duke has already scored, and now he’s drinking with the boys. The crowd of frat guys are chanting Duke as he down’s a punch bowl full of stuff. – Williams: Pay up kids!! Kid24: God damn that’s impossible! Williams: Never say never hoss and be Malice till you die. By the way what was in that? Kid19: A half a bottle of Jack, Jim, and Jose, Vodka, pureed Kiwi, a dozen eggs, a twelve pack of blue ribbon beer. Williams: Blue Ribbon beer? Kid19: yea why. -Duke turns green and pukes in the bowl.- Williams: I hate pabst beer. -The camera returns to the Malice Man in Missoula.- Williams: Always get the contents before the bet. With that I’m going to take a nap. See ya. -The camera fades yellow and black.-
