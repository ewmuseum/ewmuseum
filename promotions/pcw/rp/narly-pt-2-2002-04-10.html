Narly! Pt.2

10 April 2002

Date: 10/04/02 This is a Suburban Uprising PromoIt totally reeks of RetardnessIs that even a word?Thank you for your time Chris looked at him 'So are we going to do this or what, you know this only takes a few seconds and it'll show those two surfers what we are made of before the match on Friday!' he said gritting his teeth now, and holding his fists. Teddy knodded 'Of course, come on grab the stuff!' said Teddy pointing to the bag that they had been carrying for ages. Christopher and Teddy stood beside each door frame, and they looked at each other. 'To my calculations Chris, they'll be out in 30 seconds!' 'Really?' 'Yes' 'How do you know that?' 'Script!' 'What script? Totally cool dude, bro?' woundered Chris. 'Right infront of you!' screamed Teddy, he continued with what he was trying to say. 'LOOK AT THE DAMN CUE CARDS, YOU DICKSLAP! LOOK! LOOK!' screamed Teddy as he pointed at Chris and the 'invisible, he he, cue cards'. Chris just laughed and put a finger over his lips. 'Teddy?' 'WHAT!' he screamed. 'Aren't we supposed to be quiet?' giggled Chris, as Teddy sighed. Teddy then looks around noticing there are only five seconds left on his watch until there unpleasant assult begins. Teddy looks at Chris.... '5....4....3....2....On....' says Teddy as he charges. The door opens as Teddy and Chris maul into the lockeroom which is the Cowanbunga Connections, taking down two men at the same time they begin to punch and kick, grabbing their skateboards and beating the two men to a pulp. Chris suddenly pulls himself up and walks out of the room grabbing the bag of tricks, suddenly Chris' face as he comes into the room is full of shock. 'TEDDDDDDDDDDDDDDDDDDDDDDDDDDDD....' he screamed. 'DDDDDDDDDDDDDDDDDYYYYYYYYY....' he continued. 'YYYYYYYYYYYYYYYYYYYYYYYYYYYYYYYYYY' he screams out loud finishing off his sentence. As Teddy picks himself up he looks at Chris with anger in his eyes and face. 'WHAT?' 'I think we go the wrong time!' says Chris as a tear comes down his eye. 'Why?' asked Teddy not knowing what was going on at all, poundering his thoughts and his movements and holding a finger to his mouth. 'Just turn, Teddy!' said Chris getting to the point, but the....scene fades. We now come back as the shock look the faces of the team of Suburban Uprising looked on. They gasped in shock because they didn't know what they had just gotten themselves into. Chris frantically tapped Teddy's sholder again and again and started to scream out loud, as they both locked their eyes on the floor and what they had beaten. 'TEDDDDDDDDDDDDDDY!' he screams again. Again he frantically taps Teddy. But he just stood there. 'TEDDDDDDDDDDDDDDDDDDDDDDDY!' screamed the goffy little kid now slapping Teddy across the face. Chris now began to jump up on the spot and started tapping and slapping Teddy. Teddy's eyes then turned, so did his neck then the whole of his body as he smacked Chris right on the sholder. 'OWWWWWWWWW!' Chris cried out loud. 'What was that for?' he asked not knowing anything. 'DON'T SLAP ME AGAIN!' piped up Teddy. Both brothers looked down at the floor, as the two shadows of the men rising. Teddy and Chris looked at each both biting their nails, after beating someone that they knew maybe tougher than them. No not the Reem Team, anyway....After they had beaten someone that had been tougher than them they didn't know what to, what could you do. In this Christopher and Teddy knew one thing. Christopher put it into one little phrase. 'RUN, BIATCH RUNNNNNNNN!' he screamed out loud, as he dropped his bag and legged it out of the doorway. Teddy wasn't far behind him as we took a shot inside the Cowabunga Connection. Two men with surf shirts on looked at each other, and sighed. 'Was that who i think it was?' asked one of the men. 'Sure was, Dude!' As both men looked at each other they smiled, for some reason both men looked like the Cowabunga Connection but it wasn't then. As we now pulled away we saw both had backstage access tokens with them, they said. 'Thomas' and 'Henry'. What Cowabunga Connection's Cousins you could be thinking....well your wrong. Thomas looked at Henry for a second. 'You wanna have some fun, with the retards?' asked a chuckling Thomas. 'COOOL!' 'Lets go then, this is bodacious!' laughed Thomas as he carried his autograph Cowabunga Connection surfboard, what the hell would be in-store for the two retards from suburbia. They walked away as we faded back to Suburban Uprising who were now out of breath standing beside each other by the toilets. 'DUDE!' said Chris looking straight at his brother. 'Yeah?' 'I FORGOT THE BAG OF TRICKS!' he screamed as he started to pace around beside the toilets. Teddy just leaned against the wall taking a deep breath, and he just looked at Chris suddenly Teddy thought he heard something. 'What?' he woundered to himself. Teddy quickly pulled Chris away from pacing to over to him. 'Did you hear that?' he asked. 'What?' 'There it was again, brother!' woundered Teddy. Chris just patted Teddy on the back and laughed he looked straight into his eyes and smiled and laughed. 'DUDE!' 'Yeah....' 'That's only the taps in the toilets dripping!' he giggled, but perhaps he knew inside himself it wasn't. He's a retarded, goofy asshole what do you expect from someone like that. But again Teddy thought he had heard something, then a sound from around 30 metres down the corridor he heard something. 'There it is again!' 'It's probably Two Ton, you know how large he is. You could here him from McDonalds!' said Chris....'Isn't that where you could get a job' laughed Teddy. A tear came over Christopher's eye he pulled away from Teddy then spun around and looked at him. 'MUM TOLD YOU NOT TO BRING THAT UP, YOU BASTARD!' he shouted. Suddenly something came from around 20 metres away now. 'There it is again' said Teddy. 'Really!' 'NO!' said Teddy in a sarcastic voice. 'Told you' 'SHUT UP!' screamed out Teddy now as he started to get frustrated. Teddy looked around a few more times, and then started to notice some shadoweries figures from around 15 metres away. Now Suburban Uprising started looking at each other, both guys knodded as they heard a voice. 'YOU!' someone screamed. Teddy and Christopher squinted their eyes trying to make out who it was. 'YEAH YOU!' someone else screamed. 'Are you talking to us?' shouted Chris back as Teddy slapped him around the back off the head, before swearing straight at him, wagging his finger. 'DICKSLAP!' 'YOU'RE DONE FOR!' screamed one of the voices. Teddy and Chris gulped as the sick sense of fear came into their bodies, they looked at each other once more, as the two people from far away started to pick up the pace. 'Again?' asked Chris. 'Yeah!' Teddy said. 'Are you sure?' Christopher asked stupidly. 'COME ON!' screamed out Teddy, as they screamed out in fear. The two bodies of the voices now came into the scene and started running after them, the camera now noticed them as the two other idiots 'Henry' and 'Thomas'. Both guys kept running then decided to stop, they looked at each other and laughed. 'Tommy and Harry are going to be pleased!' laughed Thomas. 'THEY SURE ARE!' said Henry. 'It's good to have friends in the wrestling business....' continued Thomas. 'Yeah!' paused Henry. 'Lets go get some pus-' said Henry, but he was stopped as both he and Thomas laughed out loud. Both surfers now walked away. We heard Thomas say something before their voices started to fade away. 'BACKSTAGE ACCESS RULES!' laughed Thomas as he high fived Henry. We now faded away again into another scene. As we now saw Suburban Uprising they stopped in their tracks noticing they had lost the two Surfers. 'I think we lost them!' said Teddy. 'Why do we always get into this trouble?' asked Chris. 'I dunno bro, but come we need to get to the meating they are having backstage....' said Teddy as he pulled Christopher along, then from behind them they heard a voice, they stopped in their tracks and just stood with their backs turned to the voice. Chris gritted his teeth and whispered something to Teddy. 'It's not them again, is it?' 'DUNNO! But after three, we do the Matrix pose and then we will know!' 'Got it!' Both guys stood still as Chris whispered something. 'ONE....TWO....THREEEEEEE!' he screamed as both boys jumped around, as they just looked at a man with a darkened face, he let a go off his cigarette and looked at both boys. 'Exscuse me....' he said. 'Yes!' said Chris. 'I'm looking for someone from PCW, can you tell me where Suburban Upri....' he said before being cut off, as Christopher started jumping up and down, and looked at the man with a goofy smile. 'THAT'S US!' he smiled. The man turned his way, from what he thought was a mental patient, and looked at Teddy. 'Are you?' 'YES!' continued Teddy, sighing at Chris. The man then walked up to Teddy, and looked him up and down before extending his hand, Chris jumped in and slapped him up, and started to screamed. 'YOU MY RAH DIGGA, HIT ME UP ON THE FLIPSIDE, FOO'....' he screamed out, but both other men shook their heads. 'I am Raga....' he said before Chris screamed out something cutting him off. 'OOOOOOOOOOOOOOOK!' The man sighed and looked at Teddy. 'I WVILL SAY DIS ONDLEY VONCE....' Scene Fades.
