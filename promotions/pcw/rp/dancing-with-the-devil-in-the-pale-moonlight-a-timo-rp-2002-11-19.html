Dancing With The Devil in The Pale Moonlight: A Timo Rp

19 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/19/02 A large wooden door swings open and standing within is none other than The Lord and Master of All Things Samoan, Timo Bolamba. He looks up at the door and smirks as the plate reads “Tyler Nelson”. A creak echoes through the room as a chair slowly turns around, and the devil himself parks his posterior behind a gigantic marble desk. The greedy one seems none too pleased to see Timo, and rightly so. The two hated enemies have carried a feud that has lasted since September of last year. The smirk upon Timo’s face soon turns to one of disgust as he cant believe what he has come here to do. This man, the one sitting before him, has caused him more grief and torment than damn near any other man alive, yet…he was here to make a deal with the devil. “You rang?” Tyler’s sarcastic voice filled the room that had naught but marble to resonate off of. There was little padding in this graveyard-esque office, and it did not put The Samoan’s heart at ease one bit. Many challenges he had partaken, and this ranked right up there. “Yeah. I wanted to talk over a proposal with you.” Tyler offers him a seat in a chair that is no doubt purposely designed to make himself taller than whomever he talks to, and pours a snifter of brandy. Tyler leans back in his chair and glares at Timo. The two share this uneasy silence for a while before Nelson finally speaks. “Can I offer you a drink as long as you are here? I wouldn’t want to be…rude.” Timo shrugs, what had he to lose? “You know how to make a Pissed-Off Samoan?” Tyler laughs a bit. “From what I understand, I excel at it.” Timo sighs and rolls his eyes at the bad joke, then leans back in the hard chair. Not very welcoming Ty, he thought to himself. This place really was spartan outside of a giant stack of blue papers. “Tell you what, what about a beer, do you drink normal American drinks too, or are you too rich to enjoy the finer things in life?” Tyler scoffs and pulls a Budwieser from his refrigerator. The Gadzillionaire tosses it to Timo, and the Samoan wipes the neck and top a bit before popping the cap off in his hands and taking a swig. He wasn’t getting anywhere and he knew it. It was time to face the music. “Look Tyler, you and I both know we don’t stand a rat’s chance in a leaky boat at Alliances if we cant work together.” “What’s your point?” Timo sinks into the chair a bit and takes another drink. The thick head of his mental opponent had become obvious to him at this point, and he now needed to talk in what would best interest Tyler. Of course, that was the difficult point, seeing as Timo had never really made a habit out of it. “My point is that I want to go in there and show everyone what Five Star Wrestling has to offer, and I don’t need you or your crony thinking it’s a good time to show up the champion. You dig my ditch?” “Ditch digging is for peons Timo, something you would probably know quite a lot about. One thing has perplexed me however, and I do wish to discuss it.” Timo looks at the sly one, and he squints. There was something he was up to, and it just wasn’t quite apparent yet. Tyler always had an agenda. Always. “Okay, I’ll take the bait.” Nelson leans forward and leans on his elbows, almost looming over the desk, making Timo feel very small. He slowly tears small pieces of paper from a larger one, building the anticipation that was so thick it could be cut with a knife. “How is it…that a low card Somalian that no more than a year ago was jobbing to the likes of Six Fingered Cisco can be the world champion of my federation? I don’t understand it Tito.” “Timo.” “Whatever. Look, you don’t really have a place in the same ring with me, let alone Alliances. Hell, you and your moron friend couldn’t just leave well enough alone.” Timo took a drink of the beer again and stared a hole through Tyler. This was some sort of plot he had obviously hatched up to try and drive The Samoan from the competition, and like a midget at a urinal, he was going to have to keep on his toes. “What do you mean Tyler?” Tyler sniffs the brandy and takes a sip, smiling in delight, then licking a bit off his lips. “This brandy is worth more than your life Tito. You see I enjoy when everything goes according to plan, and right now, you went and fucked everything up. In fact, you have been a perennial pain in my ass, and I can’t have that. What’s it going to take?” Timo looked a bit shocked as he looked on at the face of evil. Even at his lowest point, Timo couldn’t have imagined Tyler trying to bribe him out of Alliances. It was just unheard of, if anyone at all knew Timo. Facing facts he snarled his response. “You don’t have enough money Tyler. You never will. This isn’t about money, its about what’s best for…” “What’s best for FSW? Oh, that classic good guy line. Don’t feed me that shit.” The brandy made its way to his lips again, and with every intoxicating sip, Tyler became a little more loose lipped with his opponent. This situation had gone from bad to worse, and now Timo was ruing the day he set foot in the office. “Tito, even your pack of mongrel idiots could do better in this contest than we will, and you know it. No matter what happens, I’m not going to take my eyes off you, and god help you if you take yours off me. I don’t want to work with you, and I never will. Get used to it “Champ”.” Timo slowly finished the beer and nodded, laughing to himself. Tyler had a look of pure intrigue on his face. After all, what had Timo to laugh about? This was a bad situation, and it wasn’t getting better. Finally as Timo rose from his chair and put the bottle on the desk, he turned and looked Tyler directly in the eyes. “I guess I’ll have to do it by myself then. Tyler…watch yourself.” Tyler snarls and slams his fist on the table. “Was that a THREAT I heard Timo?” Timo smiles at him and winks. “I knew you could remember my name.” The FSW World Champion turns around and exits the room leaving Tyler fuming in his chair. The true test was to come, could the World Champion take it to PCW, OSW, and RUSH? Who knows, all in all though, one has to think he has a chance, being the only serious competitor left that has actually won the event before. Only time and god know.
