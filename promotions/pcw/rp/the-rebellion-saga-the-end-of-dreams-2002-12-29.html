The Rebellion Saga: The End of Dreams...

29 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12-29-02 It was the day after Christmas when DarkStar stepped into his realm. Somewhere where he hadn't chosen to visit in a long time. Everything was how he left it to the carekeepers. The black ivory gates swung open, the white marble sidewalk, all the way through the fields and the ocean, the bridges and the hills, right to the front of the huge Castle of Dreams, across from the Castle of Nightmares. The two that existed solely to compliment the other. His castle was huge, four times bigger then the abandoned church that he and Talena called home in the Mortal Realm. He hadn't formulated a plan. He only knew how sick he was of this. How sick he was of having to represent unworshiped Gods. He walked into his study, past the paintings that a human mind couldn't comprehend. Right into the waiting arms of the one person he knew he would see. The white robed king of the Gods. Destiny. 'I knew you would be here Drazail.' His words were both loud and soothing at the same time, DarkStar stared unintimidated. 'Amazing. Thats what you do though, right? Know.' Destiny's face sood unchanging, from what you could see under his hood. 'Yes. It is what I was destined to do. As you are destined to feel.' DarkStar scoffed. 'Feel? How am I to feel? I can feel nothing but everything else. I don't even know my own feelings any longer...' 'That is how it was supposed to be, that was the path you cho-..' 'I CHOSE NOTHING' Drazail screamed, a slight sadness detectable in his voice, 'I WAS FORCED TO DO THIS! FORCED TO FEEL EVERYTHING AND ANYHING!' Destiny still stood without emotion, his voice monotone and neglected now, 'But it is what you must do, it is for the good of all mankind.' 'Fuck mankind. And fuck you. Fuck my job. Fuck my 'fate'.' He turned around and looked at the books that lined the walls. The books reached so high that they couldn't be seen, every book telling every dream or every person. 'Fuck my father, fuck everyone that you used to put my bloodline into this imbred little group of deities you put together.' That cocky smirk made its way back onto DarkStars face as he looked into Destiny's blank eyes. 'I quit.'
