The Great Escape Part 2

21 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: March 21st, 2002 (Mike Frampton is scene crawling through the air ducts. Steam is rising all around him and he begins to sweat. He looks down each time he appears over an exit to see if he can locate someone who is friendly towards himself. He looks down and sees nobody he knows and continues you on. A few seconds later he stops and looks down. He sees Big Daveylicious but doesn't see him as Big Daveylicious as he his been overcome by Paranoia. He punches out the duct and drops down into a bright room where Big Daveylicious is sitting in a chair. The room is empty and nobody notices Frampton. Mike walks towards Davey who turns his head and nods. Mike returns the nod.) Mike Frampton: M, I am so happy the haven't hurt you. Big Daveylicious: M? Mike Frampton: Oh no you've been drugged. Damn you Doctor No, damn you! Big Daveylicious: Nobody has drugged me Mike, but you look the worse than usually do. What happened? Mike Frampton: Who the hell is Mike? You have been drugged M. I will get you out of here, one day. Big Daveylicious: Damn your multiple personalities. I am not this M that you speak of. But who are you today? Mike Frampton: You know that. Big Daveylicious: Say it. Mike Frampton: The name's Bond, James Bond. Big Daveylicious: Oh boy! Mike, you aren't James Bond and I am not M, first of all I am not a woman. Mike Frampton: You are too hard on yourself M. Just the years have gotten the best of you, back in the day you were gorgeous. Heck, you still are. (Mike strokes Big Daveylicious hair. Big Daveylicious grabs Mike's hand.) Big Daveylicious: Mike never do that again. Michael Graves may kill you if he sees that. Mike Frampton: Michael Graves? Do you mean 069? Big Daveylicious: No, wait, yes, that is exactly what I mean. Mike Frampton: I thought so. Now M we must find our way out of here. Big Daveylicious: How do you suppose we do that? Mike Frampton: Perhaps through the air ducts, that is how I got here. Big Daveylicious: Perhaps. James, you need to hide, someone is coming. (Mike dives behind a chair. Doctor No charges into the room. He eyes Big Daveylicious.) Doctor No: Davey, have you seen Mr. Frampton? Big Daveylicious: I'm afraid I haven't. Doctor No: Damn it! (Doctor No turns around and storms out as fast as he came in. Mike comes back out and sits down beside Davey who he still believes is M.) Mike Frampton: Damn that Doctor No! Damn him. Big Daveylicious: Indeed! Mike Frampton: Before we escape we must have that man killed. Big Daveylicious: We can not go that far Mi... James. Mike Frampton: I do have a license to kill M. Big Daveylicious: Listen James, your license to kill is not to be invoked this time. Understand? Mike Frampton: M, please, I can take him out without a gun. Quiet, nobody needs to know. Big Daveylicious: NO! Mike Frampton: Fine, could I at least beat him up? Big Daveylicious: Hmm. Mike Frampton: Please? Big Daveylicious: Oh, fine. Mike Frampton: Excellent, that man will soon feel a British foot straight up his colon. Big Daveylicious: Now listen James. I need you to escape. You must do this James, if you can escape you can have me rescued, understand? Mike Frampton: Anything for you M. Big Daveylicious: Hop to it. Mike Frampton: With pleasure. (Mike jolts out the door. He looks around the corner and sees nobody. He begins to walk down the hall. A man in a white coat appears around the corner. It's Franky.) Franky: Hey, you there. (Mike sees Franky and charges at him. Mike spears Franky to the ground. Mike checks to see if anyone saw then proceeds to drag Franky back to the room that Big Daveylicious was in.) Mike Frampton: Hide this M. Big Daveylicious: He's dead? Mike Frampton: Of course not, you said I can not kill. (Mike then exits the room and proceeds to walk down a hall. Suddenly a door opens and Mike is yanked inside as the scene fades to white. A moment later the scene fades back in. Mike is face to face with a man in a white coat. However he is not quick to attack.) Mike Frampton: Who are you? Bones: The name's Bones. Mike Frampton: What do you want with me? Bones: I know what you're up to. I want to help. Mike Frampton: But why? (A violin begins to play in the background.) Bones: As a boy my father was institutionalized by Doctor No. I came to work here so one day I could take him down and free all the people who have been put in here for Doctor No's experiments. Mike Frampton: Experiments? What experiments? Bones: There are many. The one he tries first on everyone is shock treatment. I have no clue why he does this but if the patient survives he notes this and moves them on to more gruesome tests. One man had his chest crushed after a sledge hammer broke ice over his chest. Mike Frampton: That mad man! Bones: So, this is why I want to help you. You are only here because of your multiple personalities. Mike Frampton: Damn it not you too. Bones: I have seen the videos, I have seen you on television. You are a skilled wrestler. But multiple personalities are no reason to keep anyone locked up. So Mr. Frampton, join me and help me take down that son of a -(beep)- Doctor No! (Bones extends his hand.) Mike Frampton: Well... (Mike extends his hand and the two men shake as the scene fades to black.)
