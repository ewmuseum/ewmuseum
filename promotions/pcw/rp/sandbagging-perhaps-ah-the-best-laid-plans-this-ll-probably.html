Sandbagging... perhaps. Ah, the best laid plans. This'll probably suck, but stuff comes up. It always does.

Date: I don&#039;t even know anymore After Friday Fusion, July 19th, 2002 The End stands victorious, in the middle of the ring after his Cruiserweight Title Match on Friday Fusion (don't worry, this won't be an arena RP, it just starts off there). He fought hard and earned his win. The crowd cheers, not so much for The End but for the match that took place and since Charlie Loc lost his title. After all, The End is far from a fan favorite. Aside from his Misfits, few like him. He never understood why, not that he cared much. His no-nonsense attitude, his wrestling style that's as intelligent and powerful as it is flashy, his dedication to the business... The End's frugal lifestyle mixed with the money he could get from disability for being blind mean that if he wanted, he'd never have to work another day in his life. But he wants to. He chooses to. The End gives his heart, his body; he already gave his eyes to the business to get ahead, but that doesn't seem to be enough for the fans. They want something more. The End has stopped trying to win over the love of the fans, and just lives his life one day at a time. If they cheer, then great. If they boo, that's their prerogative. But tonight, the crowd does cheer. Charlie Loc is dethroned as the PCW Cruiserweight Champion in his first title defense in three weeks since becoming the champion. There is a new Cruiserweight Champion: The End. And now, with only a few short days Standing in the ring, The End thinks about his achievement... End:'Finally. Loc has held me back from the PCW Cruiserweight Championship for nearly two months, but now, vindication. I am the champion. And I'm going to bring credibility back to this title after what it's been through recently. Charlie Loc was pathetic as a champion. It took him two weeks to muster up the courage to defend it at the pay-per-view, but since he took too long, the bookers had him wrestle me anyway. I was the #1 contender, after all.' The End jumps over the top rope and lands on the floor. End:'And look what happened. Charlie Loc got his little novelty moves off, but from the outset, I overpowered him and out-wrestled him. Anytime Charlie Loc got a two-count it was because I chose to wait an extra second. And I'm sure the announcers thought Charlie Loc pulled me out of the pin after the top rope splash. Silly them, they'll never know that Loc tried to pull back on my mask to keep me down, but I still sat up from his attack.' With the Cruiserweight Title over his shoulder, The End walks away, patting it with happiness and excited delight. End:'I put myself through the table on Turmoil, but that couldn't be helped. It was my mistake, I'll admit that much. But tonight there was no error. He only attacked me because I allowed him to. All other times I tossed him around like a rag doll. 'It did seem a little odd that Charlie Loc would botch his own move and knock himself out, but if that be the case then Charlie Loc didn't deserve to be the PCW Cruiserweight Champion. He didn't deserve the title anyway, but then he wouldn't have deserved it even more than he already didn't deserve it. Wait, this doesn't even make any sense. Forget it.' The End disappears into the back. Everyone who dislikes Charlie Loc thanks him for getting the belt off Loc, but The End's only celebration is in self-achievement. He didn't do it for the people. He did it for himself, by himself. Of course, doing things your yourself is both the essence of and completely contradictory to the way this country is supposed to work. That's why he doesn't concern himself much with those issues. All The End really wants to do is just go home... 'YEAH!' 'WOO-HOO!' 'ALRIGHT!' 'MUNSINPLX!' 'GOOD SHOW!' 'WONK!' So sound off the congratulations of the Misfits as The End returns home with the PCW Cruiserweight Title and the PURE Junior Heavyweight Titles slung over his shoulders. There is no champagne in the house, but Slade has broken out the sparkling cider. Nelly's '#1' and 'Eye of the Tiger' by Survivor play simultaneously; Richard and Drek couldn't agree on music for the special occasion. Lexicon and Kay Fabe are throwing confetti and silly-string, everyone wearing leis and party-hats. And Juju is just being the normal badass albino iguana that he always has been, eating an entire chocolate cake with vanilla frosting and rainbow sprinkles. They all gather around The End and shower compliments and praise upon him for his hard fought match against Charlie Loc, winning the PCW Cruiserweight Title after nearly two months of being held back from it. Now, The End will walk into the next PCW pay-per-view with two WrestlingTattler-recognized titles, one of, if not the first PCW wrestler to do so. The Misfits are happy for him. The End is happy. Everybody's happy. But alas, there is a dark cloud looming, rain and lightning batter the earth mercilessly. Thunder booms and rolls across the landscape, nature's tanks and mortar hard at work. Gloom and despair, broken dreams and shattered hopes fill the one-room apartment above the China Town butcher shop and laundromat (with all the meat neatly-pressed and with a lemon fresh-scent. The clothes are well-ironed and steamed... just don't walk past a hungry dog in them). Sitting alone, he slumps and mumbles and grunts and grumbles, dreading the day he chose to face The End. Yes there's gloom and dread and sadness, no hope... all in the house of Charlie Loc (bum bum buuuuuuuuum) A knock on the door, and a yelling or Everyone is sitting around, playing Uno. The End is leaning against the wall. Richard looks up as he drops his second to last card. Richard:'Hey, are we on?' Lamar slaps the used deck and says, 'Uno!' Richard curses as he is forced to pick up the trash deck of 50 cards. The End:'Come on! Get into position! Shnell, shnell!' Drek:'Yoo're German?' The End:'No, but yelling in German always seems to get people to do what you want.' Everyone gets back to the same way they were before the story interruption, and resume the RP from there. Drek:'Yoo're going to be a greh't chahmp'yin. So what if Natalia Miyagawa goot invoolved?' The End grabs Drek by the face, palming his chin in his hand, and brings the dwarf to face level. End:'What did you say?' Drek kicks his tiny little legs in the air. Drek:'... So what if Natalia Miyagawa interfered to give you the win?' With a toss, The End flings Drek onto the couch. He then drops the PCW Cruiserweight Title to the floor. Hanging his head low, he ignores the Misfits and goes outside to sit down on the front porch. Everyone else just stays inside. Understandably, he's upset about it. The Misfits just go ahead and celebrate anyway. Indeed, there was much jubilation at the home of the Misfits. All their long days of rooting for The End had finally paid off as he captured PCW gold. The Cruiserweight strap had a new champion, and so did they. More importantly, though, The End did what the Misfits always knew he could do, and they were doubly proud of him for finally capitalizing on the opportunity presented him. Literally dancing and hopping about, all the Misfits celebrate their leader's (employer, mentor... whatever) win over Charlie Loc, a match that was actually fairly well placed on the card. Richard and Lamar are spinning each other around in their typical 'not quite right' mentalities. Lamar and Richard both possess brains that can only muster the faculties of children, but they are still nice enough to be The End's groupies. Richard:'End won!' Lamar:'Yay!' Lexicon, though excited, remains in his seat. Lexicon can give even more of a perspective to his happiness for The End, whom he has been associated with since before The End joined PCW. Lexicon:'Indeed, there is much jubilation, for The End has won a PCW title after a long, arduous seven month journey to gold. I was with him when he held EW's Primetanium Title, *boos and hisses at the mention of EW, but it's for historical purposes* and I can say that he felt even better about winning the Cruiserweight Title than he did about winning the Primetanium Title.' Slade:'Asdoim asfoiu ner fuinlnz iocxu hviaun daidun ijcv sado vnms ocivjzxk caw knjff.' Lexicon:'I know that the Primetanium Title was run on an angle-basis in an RP-based federation, and that if you merely showed up you won the title, but the PCW Cruiserweight surely has a great measure of credibility behind it!' Drek:'Whuh't a'boot TMM ah'nd Ant'in the Kid?' Lexicon:'Dammit, I'm trying to make our leader, mentor, and employer look good here.' Richard:'Sucks to be you, then, doesn't it?' One minute earlier in the county hospital... Kay Fabe is all bandaged up from head to toe. He resembles a mummy in his current state. Every bone in his body was fractured when the heavy Wal-Mart shelf fell on him while shopping for a present for the Miyagawa's expected child. The weight of the ceiling falling on him was even worse. Thanks to the stale water from the sprinkler systems that fell on him out from the hole in the ceiling, Kay Fabe also acquired a number of infections and illnesses. And his blood and organs were nearly fried inside his body when the live electrical cord fell out from the ceiling and into the puddle of water forming on and around Kay Fabe. So, he recovers, lying back in bed with his legs propped up in a lever-sling. He has managed to have a few days go by where he can just relax. There's a nice breeze coming through the open window, his hot nurse is like the carbon copy of Heather Graham, and the Misfits (sans Lexicon) come to visit every day. The only part of his body not injured is his index finger, which he uses to change the channel on television with his remote control, or call the nurse with the blue button. The show currently gracing the airwaves is 'The Many Down-Blouse/Up-Skirt Pictures of Vanessa Redgrave, 1999 edition.' Kay Fabe's eyes go wide open with shock (though he'd rather shut them in horror), and a muffled scream comes from underneath the bandages on his face. Kay Fabe: *muffled*'FCK!' Saturday, July 20th, 2002 The End is sitting in a house with Charlie Loc's mother. She's doing well enough. The house is in Chinatown, really an apartment. It has heirlooms from Vietnam all over the place. Charlie Loc must have had them shipped in. Loc's mother is as healthy as can be. Her hair is still black and body fit. She's getting on in years, but not by much. She was a young bride. The End sits across from Loc's mother as she pours him some tea. Loc's Mom:'I don't get many visits these days. Family has grown up and moved on. Little Charlie hardly comes by. You said you're one of his friends?' End:'A co-worker, yes. I just came because I was curious about him, but he's really tight-lipped about it.' Loc's Mom:'Oh, I can tell you everything I know, but it's not much past little Khiem's teenage years.' End:'Khiem?' Loc's Mom;'Yes, Khiem Jung Loc is his birth name. We tried to switch it with an American name when we first came over. There were a lot of do-overs. At first, we didn't know the female names, and for about a week he was Shirley Loc. Then once he enrolled in school, they told us that it was a girl's name and suggested that we choose something else.' End:'Really?' The End takes a sip of his tea. Loc's Mom:'Oh yes. I looked through a name book and thought I'd found something decent, so I sent him off to school. But that day around 12pm Khiem came running in the door crying and asking why I didn't love him. He was so traumatized by his experience that we had to take him out of that school and enroll him somewhere else. It was the first and last time we called him Petergay.' The End cracks up laughing. End:'PETERGAY?!' Falling out of his chair, The End holds his sides laughing. End:'Petergay... hahahaha!! GO ahead and end the RP here, I don't think I can go forward anymore.' In Kay Fabe's hospital room, three vultures land on the windowsill. The TV shorts out and sparks fly from it. The electricity overloads in the room and his machines shock him with 30,000 volts of electricity...
