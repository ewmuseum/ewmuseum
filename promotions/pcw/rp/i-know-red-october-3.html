I know.. (Red October #3)

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Sunday, October 6th (To the tune of Placebo's, 'I Know' off their first album.)I closed my eyes, and I just didn't know what to feel.  Everything just got harder for me as the temptation was now here.  My pain and my suffering under this devious thing were now being felt again.  The ache in my blood.. My bones.. My body now renewed.  Was I mad at Lady Cat, for brining this noxious, demon from my past?I know, you love the song but not the singerThe girls have all left, to do some shopping in Kyoto.  I was alone.  I was here, with my weakness, my pain.  It seemed as though, every time I wandered through the apartment, I would end up staring at what Savanda had brought me, her 'gift.'  I know, you've got me wrapped around your fingerIt just hurt so bad, my toes dug into the plush, white carpeting in the room, as I placed my hands on the hard wood desk, closing my eyes, and opening them, just to see if I was still staring at the Noir.  My nails dug into the wood of the desk, starting to leave little marks, as my blue eyes filled started to mist up.  This was so hard, not to take it.  Not to touch it, and be apart of it once more.  Have it in my system.  Have it strengthen me, and escape my mind with it.  It was so hard not to inject myself with it at this second.  I didn't feel strong now.  I didn't feel anything, except need. I know, you want the sin without the sinnerI wanted it so bad.. It hurt.I knowHow.. How could I go onto PCW with out it?  Could I do it?  Right now, I didn't think so.I knowI tried so hard to escape this, and the pain it brought me, for Akiko, only to have her slap me in the face with it again.  Like I could never escape.  No matter how fast I ran, it just stood there in front of me, as though I had never moved.  I know, the past will catch you up as you run fasterI slowly brought my hands back, a flood of memories invading the precious space of my brain, of things half shattered due to what was there in front of me.  I crossed my arms over my chest, feeling a chill, at having lost control.  What kind of a person was I?  What kind of a man was I?  What kind of a martial artist was I?  I am a failure, in all of these aspects.  And, deep down, I most certainly knew it.I know, the last in line is always called a bastardI just stared down, wanting to run from these feeling, from this pain.  Though, I knew I couldn't, I felt trapped.  I felt scared and angered, knowing that I was now caught in a cycle, that I couldn't escape.  There would always be a want now.  There would always be a yearning.I know, the past will catch you up as you run fasterIt was hard, I don't know if I can do this.I know'How can I do this?' I muttered to myself, only slightly audible to even my own ears, as I sat upon the plush carpeting, running my fingers through my hair, trying to think.  Trying to decide, what would be right, what would make everything fine in the end?I know…It wasn't that long ago, when I was yelling at Kyle 'Velocity' Lamen, about this.  Getting yelled at by Joey 'Ebola' Doyle, about the dangers of noir.  But, I didn't listen.  They weren't as smart as I was.  I was running CCW; I was on top of the fucking world.  What could touch me?  I was the champion.  Was, was the key word.   Everything slid down hill, not that I was really ever on top of the heap.  According to Walden, I was just at the top of the Indy fed, never really ready to make a big challenge, like PCW.  I broke my neck and everything fell.  I lost control.  I know, you cut me loose in contradictionI never really owned anything in my pathetic little life.  I've had to scrounge, and fight, and earn.  Unlike people like Tyler Nelson, some people just had a gift.  I had to work hard to get where I was, and then, it was taken away from me.  The Noir was a sweet salvation for me.  It helped me regain some sort of control, grab onto some aspect of my life, and it got me off my ass.  I was unmotivated after the break.  Uncaring, the sweetness of the Noir, at least gave me a drive, though a false one.I know, I'm all wrapped up in sweet attritionI remember how beautiful, it felt to be alive, to be awake, to be active.  Though, I remember the problems with the paranoia, the anger.  The blind devotion to one goal and how it hurt Akiko in the past, I don't know if I could bear to put her through that again..I know, it's asking for your benedictionMy hand shivered, softly, as it reached towards the desk, my nails scrapping against the wood, as I quickly drew it back.  This decision was tearing me apart.  Was I ready for PCW?  Could I really show them the fire that was burning in me, that I had for CCW?  That I now have for PURE and Puro?  The way and the life style?I knowI didn't know if I was strong enough.  I was scared.  I knowI remembered, painfully, my first experiences with PURE, and Alexander Miyagawa.  My arch nemesis was there, the man whose shadow, I had trying to drag myself out of, though he was six years my younger.  Kensuke Suzahara.  The past and the future caught up to me in one swift moment, as I let all my anger and hatred go, and slammed him outside the ring.  Everything was ruined.  I felt so unfulfilled, unlike after our other matches, where we had both strived, and fought with everything we had.  But, we both knew it was our bodies doing the striving, with no outside influences, or materials.I know, the past will catch you up as you run fasterFrom this un-fulfillment, I tried to make Miyagawa the next Kensuke, attacking him, striking him for the failures that I had believed he caused me, though, it was just because I was too blind to see that I was being used.  Was it the drug? Or my own intentions that I was blinded, to get to Kensuke.  I know, the last in line is always called a bastardWhen Miyagawa left, I had no one to abandon or blame, but myself.  No one left to throw stones at, or hide my own sins behind, because I hated myself, and refused to see it.  I've always hated myself, and now, when I finally find some GOD DAMN SELF-RESPECT.. The hatred comes back, for the weakness is there.I know, the past will catch you up as you run fasterIt's always there, staring me in the face.I knowLaughing at my stupidity, as Miyagawa did then, and Kensuke before him, and now Walden.  Because, I had always been missing something: The respect for myself.I know'This is no fucking time for self revelations,' I mutter to myself, as my hand lashed out, the struggle gone.  I knew what I had to do.  I had to wipe this in the hated PCW's face.  I had to wipe this in Tim Walden's face, in Alexander Miyagawa's and Kensuke Suzahara's.  I'm a big boy; I can sacrifice to get to where I need to be.I know, the past will catch you up as you run fasterSometimes, you have to make these sorts of sacrifices to be noticed, to be worshiped and adored, to be admired, and hated and feared.I know, the last in line is always called a bastardI frowned, and ripped off a piece of a dark gray shirt, that I had around my upper body, knowing well enough how to do this by know.   I carefully wrapped the torn fabric around my arm, slowing the blood flow up the arm, and making the veins protrude, so that I may set the venom into my body. I pulled the syringe into my hand, and pulled the cap off slowly.  My blue eyes focused on the tip of the needle, as I gently pushed the back, making sure no air bubbles were going to enter my blood stream.  I know, the past will catch you up as you run fasterWith out another hesitation, I tapped the vein, and stuck the needle in, closing my eyes, with a soft hiss, my head tilting back, as I felt what I thought was the rush of the noir.I knowI can make the sacrifices PCW.  I am not afraid, to do anything to get to you, and have my revenge.  My revenge….I know.[End.]
