Whatcha Gonna Do When Petsnakemania Runs Wild ON YOOU?!

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: While Viewing Xavier Kannon&#039;s Mom The scene opens up in a crowded airport terminal, where men and women in business attire are walking all around. People are seated or standing, but all are watching a huge airplane as it descends into the nearby hangar. But of course that’s not our scene. The camera pans away and shows a tiny terminal with four passengers sitting there and a person in street clothes with a microphone in his hand and a piece of paper in his hand. The four passengers are an elderly Caucasian women, a strapping young Arab man, a Caucasian male in a business suit, and none other than Rush Mason, PCW Superstar. The elderly lady seems to be looking around for something. Rush: Umm…miss, what are you looking for? Old Lady: You may call me Zelda, and I’m looking for my pet snake, Beethoven. Rush looks at her with a puzzled look on his face, repeating her statement as a question, raising his eyebrow when he thinks of a snake lost in the airport. Rush: You have a pet snake named Beethoven? And you don’t know where he is? Zelda: Yes, he sneaks away all the time, he just gets so irritated when I put him in my purse. Rush: You put your snake in your purse?! Zelda: Yes, but only when he starts to snap his fangs at me. I scold him and tell him that he’s a bad snake. Rush rubs his forehead and just sighs as Zelda goes back to looking for her pet snake. He then looks at the businessman, who is frantically going through his suitcase and initiates yet another conversation. Rush: Man, what’re you looking for? You need some help? The man almost screams back in response at Rush as he continues to frantically search. Businessman: I’m NOT LOOKING FOR ANYTHING MAN!! JUST CHILL OUT, NOTHING’S WRONG!!! Rush looks around to see if anyone else is noticing this man’s very strange behavior but the other two passengers in the terminal seem to be very occupied with their own thoughts. Rush: Umm…you sure that you’re ok? Nothing’s wrong? The man looks up at Rush, fiddling his hands nervously and almost screaming in answer once again. Businessman: I DON’T HAVE A FUCKIN’ BOMB, OKAY?! Rush thinks for a second and then his eyes light up in a surprised sort of look, then gives an insincere smiles to the businessman while he tries to slide away inconspicuously. He slides over next to the Arab man and looks through the book that the man is reading and sees that it is Rainbow Six by Tom Clancy and thinks out loud. Rush: Tom Clancy’s an awesome writer. Oh shit, sorry I was just thinking out loud to myself. The Arab man looks up at looks at Rush for a second before responding kindly. Man: No it’s alright, it’s true, Clancy is a very gifted writer. What’s your favorite book of his? Rush: Well actually I liked the Sum of All Fears but that moron Affleck will kill any passion I had when reading it. Man: Heh, me too. I’m deeply enjoying Rainbow Six though. By the way, my name’s Gheorge. Rush extends his hand and the two engage in a friendly meeting handshake. Rush: Well glad to meet you Gheorge, I’m Rush. I’ll talk to you on the plane, I gotta go do something. Gheorge slips some earphones on and continues to read his novel as Rush walks away, just as the small man in street clothes by the hangar entrance picks up his microphone, which is barely on and yells above the talking of the other terminals. Airport Staff: Flight 18 to Port-Au-Prince, Haiti will now be leaving from terminal A and a half. Rush: Oh great, now I can’t even get any food and I’m stuck eating cardboard sandwiches on the plane. Rush reluctantly walks over to his bags and picks them up, heading towards the security check for boarding the small plane. At the moment, the crazy businessman is trying to go through, though he is having repeated problems with the detector. Businessman: WHAT THE HELL KIND OF AIRPORT DO YOU RUN?! IT’S NOT LIKE I HAVE A BOMB IN MY COMPUTER! He is yelling, but with a nervous tone in his voice, while the moronic security guard can’t see that there is something strange about his laptop even though the detector goes off every time. Security Guard: I’m sorry about that sir, my detector must be malfunctioning, go right ahead. Rush lowers his head and opens his mouth in a look of utter confusion and shock, as the businessman beings to quickly mutter and smile to himself as he walks through the checkpoint. Businessman: No problem, I can’t wait TIL WE GET ON THE PLANE! Hehe. Zelda, the old lady, now steps up to security point and walks through the checkpoint, only to have the detector go off, but she just continues to walk, calling out for her pet snake. Security Guard: Aww, she’s just a harmless old lady, what could she do? Rush shakes his head as Gheorge steps up to the detector, which he goes through safely, but the security stands in front of him. Security Guard: Excuse me sir, could you step through again? Gheorge doesn’t even answer, he just complies and steps through again, successful again, but the security guard now grabs his bag and begins to rifle through it. He holds up an object that Rush can’t see and demands to Gheorge in a loud voice. Security Guard: WHAT IS THIS?! Gheorge replies in a perfectly normal voice, despite the obvious profiling because of his ethnicity. Gheorge: It’s a book. Security Guard: Yeah right, those paper edges could be used to slit the pilot’s throat. Gheorge begins to sigh as the guard continues to rifle things, while Rush just sits down in a seat, because this is going to take a while… The scene fades to black, but we hear one more snippet of the conversation. “What in the blue name of Allah is this, buddy?” “That’s my fingernail.” “We’ll just see about that, buddy.”
