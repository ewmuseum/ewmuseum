The Luck Of The Dice

21 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Title: The Luck Of The Dice Featuring: Xavier Kannon Date: 21/11/02 Thank you for buying the Alliances GameTM. Enclosed you should find the following: * 5 x Rings.* 5 x Sets of Cages and Walkways.* 10 x Ladders.* 60 x WT Wrestlers (In 15 colors)* 1 x 6 sided die.* 1 x Deck of Wildcards..* 1 x Instruction Manual. “Hmmm, time to play.” Above each ring the cages are placed with the walkways connecting them. That was easy, shame the game won’t be such a walk through. Now, which character to play with. “I’d have thought that was pretty simple, peon.” The figure of Xavier Kannon stands still and motionless by one of the Cages, the door staring right back at his still un-moving self. Time to make the first move. The die rolls.... 4. “And so it begins.” Walking up the Black metal steps, chipped with wear and tear to show the Silver metal beneath... The result of wrestler impact. Each step makes a dull metallic thud as it’s touched, leading the Scientologist onto the apron where the Cage door awaits, gaping open. Slowly but surely the dull, lifeless game is invaded by a dull roar of a crowd. The 4 takes him into the cage, stepping through the ropes before the clang of the door bolting is heard behind him. “That will only take me so far.” All around the figure of Xavier, men do battle. Bodies crash into steel mesh, backs land on the mats and forearms collide with heads and necks. For this game is a dangerous one. Another roll. 2. “Not a great number.” And so he’s introduced to the game. He staggers, an impact on his upper back, arm reaching around to grab the painful area. The die needs to be favourable. It rattles on the hard surface, waiting for the magic number to show. 5. “Good enough.” The right leg of the Scientologist launches into a mule kick, the halt to his progress dealt with an no doubt doubled over in pain. First obstacle of the Alliances Game dealt with. You play a game to win, and Kannon has every intention of doing so. “Shut up and roll, I don’t have all day.” Die. Roll. Number. 6. Perfect score, enough to move him up a level and even closer towards the point of Alliances, victory. Up the ladder he goes, if only there were something as tame as a Snake to knock him back down to the next level. He wishes, instead he has a Bear. “Uh-Oh.” The die rolls once more will luck favour him? When faced with the Russian Bear Ivan Stanislav only a 6 will do, not even a 5 can stop your back being introduced to the Cage wall with a devastating Red Scare. And it’s another Six... If you minus 5 first. 1. “And down I go again.” Down through the trapdoor he plummets, his fall broken by the ring below. Not quite back where he started, but as good as. Maybe this Alliances game isn’t as easy as it looked on the box. After every setback you have to bounce right back, a 5 will do, a 6 would be even better but either would get him on that promised land of the second tier, back where he was, back where he wants to be. Will the dice favour him? 5. “Just like I always manage to do, I pull one out of the bag.” Once more up the ladder, hopefully not continuing the up-down motion. Time to capitalise, another good roll, luck to favour him, chance to be on his side, favour to be dancing to his tune. Slowly the dice tumbles, taking its time with dramatic license to show it’s hand. “What do you know? I’m a natural.” Another 5, he’s working his way up quickly from the dangerous bottom level. The higher up the game the less rivals, but the higher up the game the greater the fall. Good job this Scientologist always seems to have luck on his side. He needed not to even read the instructions to this game, too many words and not enough actions for him. After all, this is a game of getting to the top and surviving, who knows more about that than Xavier Kannon? “That would be nobody, now roll. I feel lucky.” Doesn’t he always? Roll. 3. Not bad. It’ll have to do. Not enough to move him to the end, but he’s on the walkway, victory staring him in the face, but there are dangers. “The reward far outweighs them.” Roll. It’s happened again, low score. 2. Now, the Scientologist is stuck, dangling from 2 hands that grip the mesh with every part of his Scientological being. The win or lose moment has come. 1, 2 or 3 will send him plummeting to the ground below and elimination. 4,5 or 6 will get him back on the walkway with victory just a grab away. Do or die... And from this height that’s literal. “I still have plenty of luck left.” 5. “See?” The dice favour him once more. Back on the walkway, victory a favourable roll away, can the momentum of luck keep it’s steam? 4. Close but no cigar. Victory still eluding him despite the good fortune, but now resting in the hands of a Wildcard. The deck is shuffled and the dice are now redundant, the cards being mixed thoroughly, crossing over one another with only flashes of text being seen against their White background. Can the luck of the roll translate to the luck of the draw? “Time to see.” His fingers reach out and the card is chosen, fingers gripping the corner before the selected, fate-sealing piece of card is pulled out and slowly turned over. The yes or no, win or lose, do or die, just turned into a maybe. It keeps the secret as only one symbol sits in the middle, taunting. ‘?’ “Might want to check the instructions on that one.” The fate of Alliances rests with a Wildcard, Xavier Kannon just has to hope he draws he right one at the right time. Only then will we see. Won’t we? ---- View Xavier Kannon's Biography
