RENT-A-HERO VS. THE DEADLINE!!!(Sweet satire)

18 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Title: RENT-A-HERO VS. THE DEADLINE!!!(Sweet satire) Featuring: Rent-a-Hero Date: 2-18-2003 Rent-A-Hero:'WHAT IN THE SHIZZNITTLEBAM?!'..::/::..Our story begins with that familiar exclamation from PCW's very own, now deceased superhero...Rent-A-Hero. Adorned in a full Daredevil replica costume, complete with horns on the mask, our hero looks skeptically at a gaint, black metal door that stands before him with a gargoyles face carved upon it. The face has red rubies for eyes and the door has various lines of script written upon it which range from,'DANGER,' to,'GET YO A** OUTTA HERE FOO!!'. Standing next to our hero in this void of darkness aside from the door is a a robed skeleton of about four feet who we know as Dave(When he's not being refered to as 'Death'), thumbing through the pages of a Playboy magazine with his bony phalanges and sporting a toothy grin at the centerfold as he says..::/::.. Dave:'Man...I really need to renew my subscription. Damn shame UPS stopped delivering through Limbo, but still goes through the ghetto. Hypocrites.' Rent-A-Hero:'Hello...excuse me my flesh-void friend...but last my Rent-A-Senses checked, you were about to tell me how to return to the world of the living!' Dave:'Oh yeah right....sorry, I have a low attention span. That'd explain why I keep forgetting to kill Dick Clark...man that's an old fu**er. I think it's time he...' Rent-A-Hero: -Ahem- ..::/::..Our hero crosses his arms and taps his foot in the spirit of Sonic the Hedgehog, Dave finally coming around as he points his index finger in the air and exclaims..::/::..Dave:'Yeah...right. Okay....it IS possible for ANYTHING to rise up from the dead to return to the living. Zombies, cats, Hulk Hogan's career.....ANYTHING can slip through the cracks of of the world of the dead as long as their spiritual form isn't captured by....THE DEADLINE!!!'Rent-A-Hero:'What is this...Deadline?'Dave:'Well...the Deadline is an entity that kills of a person, place, thing, project, idea...whatever. Anything that has a soul can be killed of by Deadline, it's just a matter of him being able to get to it. Through this world is the 'Dead Zone'....a dark void where all the spiritual forms of all items and people are housed for Deadline to get to one at a time. Once he has murdered the spiritual form of the item....it is forever dead. What you need to do is find your SPIRITUAL you and reunite with him before it's too late.' ---- View Rent-a-Hero's Biography
