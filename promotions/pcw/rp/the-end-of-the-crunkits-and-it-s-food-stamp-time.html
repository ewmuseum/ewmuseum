The End of the Crunkits, and IT'S FOOD STAMP TIME!

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Whenever The Cock Crows, Whenever the Penis Roars The scene opens up on a crowded room, though its exact type can’t be identified by the sheer mass amount of people. Many of these people appear to be unfortunate, as shown by their rags/clothes. Rush Mason and Jordan X Shabazz can be seen off to the side chatting. Rush is wearing a gray Mecca t-shirt, black baggy jeans, and low-cut white T-Macs. Jordan is wearing a black NWA t-shirt and silver jeans with gray Jordans on. Jason Core walks onto the screen wearing a red collared t-shirt unbuttoned as Jordan and Rush chat. Rush: All these poor people are in the endorsement match? They all need food stamps too? Jordan: The white people are trying to oppress the bruthas once again. J-Core: Guys, can I ask once again why we’re in the welfare line? Rush: You idiot! I told you already! We’re getting food stamps? Jordan looks around at a few homeless people, who’re staring at him strangely. Jordan: WHAT?! STARING AT ME CUZ I’M BLACK?! The homeless people just shake their heads, even though usually you would see the normal people shaking their heads at homeless crazy people, though Jordan isn’t what this narrator would call normal. J-Core: Okay, so explain this to me one more time? Jordan gives a crazy eye to one of the homeless people as Rush starts explaining his “plan” to J-Core. Rush: Well, since CrunKola is going to be the new PCW official soft drink, I need a way to be able to get all that other crappy tasting soda to see if it’s any better than mine. J-Core: Rush, you do know that Duke sends your paychecks to your house right? Your large house that you pay for? Rush: Yeah, I know. J-Core waits for the reality to set in, but Rush isn’t going to be foiled by any sort of logic on this one. J-Core: Rush, food stamps are for poor people. Rush: Once again I know. J-Core: Rush, why do you need food stamps?Rush: So I don’t have to waste MY money on shitty drinks! J-Core lets out a big sigh, while Jordan is still glaring at an unfortunate looking man sitting on a milk crate in the lobby. He has a nametag on that says “HI! My name is Wellington.” Wellington: What? Jordan just continues his angry glare, while the man begins to ignore him, while back in the line; Rush and J-Core are still talking. Rush: Okay, so let’s see, how many food stamps do I need? J-Core: This is crazy! You make like ninety thousand dollars a year and you’re in here with people who would kill to make one third that much? Rush: Once again, I don’t see your point. J-Core: You ever think that was because you’re flirting with the line between sanity and insanity? Rush: No, I don’t understand your point. Therefore, it can’t be logical. Do you remember how many people are in that soda match Jordan? Jordan, meanwhile, is still looking over at Wellington, who has begun to doze off on top of the milk crate. He now begins to growl at the man, but stops to answer Rush. Jordan: Twelve. RAH, Makonie, Kannon, Chef, Syko, Borro, Heartbreakers, Clyde, Pharaoh, Darkstar and Rush. J-Core: Holy crap! How’d he remember that off the top of his head? Rush: He’s like a stat machine. He can remember anything at almost any time in PCW. Jordan, what was one of the events that happened before Hank~! Could win the hardcore title? Jordan takes another break from his growling and glaring to answer again. Jordan: That would be pigs flying and/or Clyde being sober. Rush just nods at J-Core, who once again resumes the side of the logic in the argument. J-Core: Okay, Rush, here. I’ll give you twenty bucks NOT to steal food stamps from these poor people. Rush: Sorry, no can do. That’d just be taking your money.J-Core: But you’d rather take the money from these people who don’t have any?! Back over where Jordan is leaning against a wall, he’s become fed up somehow with the behavior, or lack of, displayed by Wellington. He walks up to the sleeping man and shakes him from his sleep. Wellington: The hell do ya want? Jordan: YOU! You were staring at me because I’m black. You’re gonna die, whitey! Rush sees this and runs over with J-Core to try and save Wellington from a sack beating at Jordan’s hands. Rush: Jordan, now remember what we learned about NOT beating up Caspers in public, except in the ring. Jordan is not paying any attention and Jason has to hold him back to keep him from attacking Wellington, who has already hobbled away out of the office. Jordan: Damn crackers. Can’t live with ‘em, can’t pop a cap in public. Rush: Yeah, great job Jordan, but now I guess we’ve lost our place in line. Damn, and this queer circus was just about to let me get to the front. Whatever, I guess I’ll just take that twenty bucks J-Core. J-Core pulls his hand away from Rush and laughs. J-Core: Like HELL you will! Rush: Ugh…white people. Jordan: Damn, I need a catchphrase like that. Rush: You need a catchphrase like a white person needs a sack beating. Oh, that’s good. Write that down, junior. The three begin to walk away towards the exit, as Jordan stops short for a second and exclaims to really no one but himself. Jordan: I’m older than you Rush! Rush just keeps walking and calls back to Jordan. Rush: Sure you are junior. Let’s go. Jordan shakes his head and glares back at the white people standing in line as he jogs after J-Core and Rush to catch up, and boy does this feel good, the scene fades to black…
