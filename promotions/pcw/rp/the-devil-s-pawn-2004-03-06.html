The Devil's Pawn

6 March 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 03/06/04 OpeningA cool wind whips across the main decks of the U.S.S. George Washington, as a murmur of anticipation begins to spread through the seamen's quarters. With the faint smell of carnage on the horizon, the superstars of PCW rest uneasily, waiting for their moment to survive. A dangerously naïve atmosphere settles over the ship, as twilight’s last grasp to life slowly starts to fade, casting a soothing if foreboding sunset across the glassy water on the horizon. The faint hum of the ship's engines quietly echoes the sentiment of the U.S. Navy in one of their most decorated ships of war. The whitecaps beneath the hull of the ship are beginning to come to life, with the bridge lighting up, trying to compete with the sun’s dying struggle, and yet no one seems to notice all the things that are wrong in the world. The tribulations of man have no home on the U.S.S. George Washington anymore, not in the eyes of her children. And yet, at ship's end, an unspeakable terror is quickly approaching. The hours of freedom from oppression are numbered for the superstars of PCW, but no one seems to notice the horrors that two men are bringing with them. Your hours are numbered, fools… your hours are numbered, indeed… SceneThe deck lights are already glowing their orange haze over the stern, as the evening air sends holds an eerie glare over the calm seas. Two men, each divided and united by history at the same time, are standing at ship's end. One, the rich man’s prototype, is a slim, imposing figure wearing a clean, crisp black suit and a pair of Pac-Sun shades that have badass wrote all over them. The other, an imposing monster, wears nothing but an emotionless glare on his face. As the start time for a very special Turmoil draws near, the Dream Syndicate begin preparing for their first shot at true glory. Richards: The hour draws near, Dom. Are you ready? Tonight, we shall make history in front of our nation's greatest heroes. Am I right? DeMerius: Yeah, that’s pretty much the gist of it. Strange that we find ourselves standing on the deck of a mighty warship, eh? Richards: This is no time to pretend to be back in Norfolk, Dom. Besides, that bitch ended up trying to stiff you for some money. Dumb broad, we made her pay... DeMerius: Yes, we did. We got angry. Richards: Remind me to make men like Joe Mac, Speed, and Poe squirm tonight in the same manner. DeMerius: I'll enjoy making them bleed alongside you, brother. Friend, rather. Richards: Consider me the brother you never had, Dom. I'm an understanding person, even in my youth. I’m an athlete, what's more. An athlete that goes out of his way to make other people bleed. It’s the blood; that’s what I’m in it for, Dominic! Do you know how magnificent, how gratifying it is to see the blood of another man on your hands? To know that you wounded him in a way that few can, to know that you were the one that could control whether he walked or would be forever still? It’s all the rage if you’re a natural born fighter like me. DeMerius: Well, for the record, I am. I was fighting the day my family was murdered. I was fighting when murderers and gangs were running rampant on my street. I've been fighting for my life every day since! Richards: I know, and I respect that. But as I was saying, I’ve never been in this for the money or the fame. I could care less if the fans cheered me or booed the Hell out of me. I mean, does it really matter if you have little teenage chicks running around, screaming in lust whenever they see your poster hanging on a girlfriend’s wall? Or is it such a big deal to be idolized by a bunch of monotonous office clerks who wear pocket protectors and thick-rimmed glasses? I think I’ll stick to the underground, where people can accept you for who you are, thank you very much. DeMerius: I agree. Richards: I have a lot to live up to. I’ve been expected to perform as a champion! And I will not be denied tonight! Never will I be embarrassed again like I was at Shanghaied! DeMerius: Now you’ll know my pain! You’ll never understand what I went through as a child… to be mocked by children because you looked different because you sound different, or had a drunkard as a father? How would you like being cursed at in a foreign tongue everywhere you went, not being able to understand just why or how you were despised? What a business it is of yours and I to know why they hate people like us. Does it make me an evil person, just on the basis of not needing to here 15,000 prostitutes, crack addicts, street whores and wife-molesters cheering my name? There’s plenty evil in this world without a large group of ingrates chanting my name every night. To even mention my name is an injustice to my legacy. Richards: How true your words are. What a pathetic, disgrace these human beings are. They have no courage, no bravado, not even a trace of a spine in their bodies, the gutless wonders. It’s people like Shaun Styles, Joe Mac, and Mike E. Dangerously that give our kind a bad name. But then again, not many people in this day in age have any bravery left. We’re all content on shoving our tails between our legs and running from danger, every time it returns to fuck with us! Well not me, not us, damnit! I don’t run like a coward every time someone challenges me! It can be 2 on 1, 3 on 1, 5 on 1, 15 on 1... It doesn’t matter to me, Dom, however many people they throw at me, I’ll always stand toe to toe, face to face with them. I might kick all of their asses single handedly, or I might get beaten into the ground like a bloody pulp. But it doesn’t matter to me, because it’ll only make them beg for mercy down the line, when I rip their still-beating heart out and wave it in front of their face before they die! DeMerius: And I’ll be with you, every single step of the way. Richards: Ah, smell it, breathe it, live it! Our time is here, our time is now! DeMerius: I’ll truly enjoy watching Jesse Ramey and Kris Kanton squirm in pain like the cockroaches they are. It’s not enough that they have to soil our good name by entering this match as well, but to act all cocky like they have a chance of walking off this ship after we’re done crippling them. Richards: Hold it… someone’s coming. Without a word among them, Richards and DeMerius quickly spin around, ready to destroy anyone that so much as sneeze at them. They’re somewhat startled, if unsurprised, to see a fairly hot woman, carrying a camera and a small sketch pad, looking for an interview. DeMerius pats Richards on the back before brushing past her, intentionally letting his hand glide over her thigh as he leaves. Reporter: Um… Mr. Richards? Can I have a moment of your time? Richards: That depends on your intentions, when and where, and how good you taste. I’ve heard that blondes taste like strawberries when you’re on the ocean. Shall we? Reporter: As flattering as that offer sounds, I’d like to ask you several questions first. If you don’t mind, of course. Richards: Go right ahead, shortcakes. My, that’s a lovely blouse. I’d like to inspect it personally later on. Reporter: Are you prepared mentally for your involvement in the Extreme Title brawl later on tonight here aboard the U.S.S. George Washington? Richards: Is apple pie tasty? Does John Kerry look like Dave Navarro? Is Cal Roberts gay? The fact of the matter is this, sweet thing; there isn’t a single fucking person on this ship that can take me down! They can be as tough as they want to be, or they can bring some weak ass rhymes to try and pump up the troops. You know the drill, I’m off the hizzle fo’ shizzle nizzle? Go sizzle your dizzle, fizzle, because they’re all nothing but voodoo spoutin’ witches. See, I can throw off rhymes while I’m stealin’ their bitches! I don’t have to wear my pants off my ass to look like a snitch. Baby, I’m like Scarface on speed, everyone else looking like Tammy Sytch! What, you think that’s some weak ass shit? I go down on your girl twice before I take another hit! Step off, whore, you ain’t got nothing on me. Reporter: Uh… Richards: Or maybe I should take the Hannibal Lecter approach… my darling, Hannibal the Cannibal, wouldn’t you say? I do enjoy fresh, just-drawn blood from the face of a man, just as I prepare to wear his face on mine like a mask for a masquerade ball. Tell me, would you like to come to my private quarters later tonight, and have a taste of another woman’s thighs? They can taste quite good when you let them simmer just long enough- Reporter: Uh! That is enough! Richards: No, it’s not enough! It’ll never be enough, not as long as stupid ingrates like you try and stir the pot even more than it already has been! This isn’t some government conspired secret we’re talking about! Men are going to go home in body bags tonight, and I’ll be the one to personally deliver them! I don’t care if I have to strike the Almighty Creator himself, there is not a single damn person that will ever defeat me. As far as I’m concerned, I am God! And God’s word is the law, and if I were you, I wouldn’t plan on breaking that law unless you want me to skin you alive, inch by inch, pore by pore! I can make your hide into a macabre new suit for my friend, who is much less forgiving than I am! I can get you high, I can get you laid, I can get you drunk, and I can have you dead before Turmoil begins! So step off, before I throw you off this damn ship! Reporter: Stay away! Stay away from me, you monster! The reporter turns to run, but Ethan jumps in front of her, a look of amusement in his eyes. He places a finger on her lips, quieting her frantic pleas for help. The ship remains totally desolate, sans the lonely, empty ring across the way. He starts to move his hand down her neck, slowly inching towards her shirt’s collar, whispering in her ear. Richards: I’m trying very hard not to lose my patience with you, stupid whore. I can only tolerate so much incompetence from someone that I plan on ravaging! Now you listen here, and you listen good while you can still hear! I’m going to destroy each and every living creature that so much as breathes in my direction tonight at Turmoil! I’m going to treat these sailors with a medieval ass kicking, Dream Syndicate style! I’m going to mop up the ring with Joe Mac’s blood, and I’m going to make Luke Chadbourne cry. Kris Kanton, Mike E. Dangerously, Shaun Styles… none of them stand a chance against myself or Dom DeMerius. But what about you, my dear? You can fantasize it now. Your body pressed to mine, wondering how much longer you can live without dying from the excitement. And then I whisper into your ear… Diabole vertses en un veses. Diabole vertses en un veses. Diabole… Diabole… Diabole… and then you wake up, not knowing who you are or why you’re here. All you know is that a lucid dream has ended, and your nightmare in Hell is about to begin. You know that your time has come, your hour of judgment has dawned. You can feel the cold wave of fear creep down your spine like an epiphany, haunting you through your every breath! You can feel me lurking in the shadows, not sure when I’ll slay you upon the ground on which you stand! I am your maker, and I will make you bleed for your ungratefulness, just as I will the other pathetic bastards that think they have a chance against me! Live for this moment, you filthy whore, for it might be your last! Now get your things… and go, before I change my mind! The woman quickly pries herself from Richards’ grip, running straight past a glaring DeMerius, not fifty feet away from them. DeMerius only shakes his head before letting a bellowing, haunting laugh rise from his lungs, cursing the woman as he walks away. Richards can only smile faintly, though, knowing the task that lies ahead. A lot had to be done before he could rest again; men to maim, families to break, love to destroy. History to make. Ethan closes his eyes, wishing he could spend one more moment holding onto the woman, because in that instant, his life’s meaning had been escalated. He’d thought of just trying to intimidate her, but his words couldn’t have been more true. Soon, he thought, soon. The end truly was near, and he would be the one to bring it upon all those who’d made the mistake of standing in his wake. He would make sure, as the deck suddenly came alive with sailors starting to appear out of nowhere, that their sins would return to visit them. And he would make sure that the cost would be more than they could bare. As he turned to watch the last glimpse of twilight, he couldn’t help but feel a sense of anticipation build inside him. He had nothing to fear but fear itself, and fear was his greatest ally. It was time to play… Richards. Showtime, you sons of bitches. Showtime…
