Quiet Contemplation

15 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 15/04/03 Lacuna was less angry at the moment. Yeah, being taken away by the police on some damn hoax was understandable. It was an action of fear on behalf of Matt Jackson, a guy who was supposedly too good to fear, and she didn’t mind this. It showed her in a new light; that of the oppressor, and she really enjoyed the new take on life in which it presented her. He also attacked Jade, who wasn’t best pleased with the results, but Lacuna didn’t mind this either. What Lacuna didn’t like was when people, ala Haley Hilton, decided it was their turn to slander people. Lacuna was a hard working girl, and she had been since her parents died on her. She wasn’t blessed with money and she had made it for herself, she was a success story, mainly due to hard work and self-belief. Because of this, it hurt doubly when she was called a ‘rich English snob’. Haley needed teaching a lesson, and she had received it. Maybe Lacuna had gotten a bit angry and took it a bit far, but I bet the point was received loud and clear as the shotgun handle cannoned off her back again and again. Strangely enough, Lacuna remembered very little of the events of that night. She had been so caught up in herself that all she remembered was enjoying it one hell of a lot. She had hospitalised Hilton, who may not be back in action until the PPV, but this was a mere side effect, a little collateral damage if you will. The sweeping of the strong wind disturbed her thought as Lacuna looked up from the spot of the ground into which she had been staring endlessly for what seemed like hours. Above her, the sun beamed down violently, warming the lush grass that waved in the wind, somewhat hypnotically. The clouds were moving quickly today, as if sped up on a camera in her head, and she noted it down mentally. It seemed odd that everything was happening so neatly; as if it was finely tuned with the natural harmony or some of that other new age crap. She smiled to herself; she was doing it again, that stupid over-analytical bull that inhabited her somewhat messed up little head. She sometimes told herself that a link unworthy of analysis was a link unworthy of existence, and she had honed this skill into the obsessive process of thought she used every day of her living life. She could link nearly everything to an answer, even if not usually plausible. Today she had been trying to get inside the head of Hilton and see it through her eyes. So far all she had found was some fluff and three pence. “You’re still out here?” Brook had approached behind her and he settled down on the cold stone of the patio, eyeing her suspiciously. ”The question is, are you going to speak to me today?” Lacuna smiled and turned to face Brook “Yeah, sorry about that.” “Permission to speak, Ma’am” Brook joked. He knew that he was possibly the only person in the world Lacuna actually entrusted with free speech. He was kind of like the Clowns of the fifteenth century, allowed to say what they wanted, unless they said too much. “Permission granted, unless you ask a question, when on said occurrence, I may be forced to tear out your eyes through your nose.” Lacuna nodded knowingly, it was hard to see if she was joking or not through her facial expressions, but the smirk she was trying to hide kind of gave it away a little. “Okay… moving swiftly on…” his beady eyes scanned around worriedly for any sharp implements before continuation. “I presume you have calmed down a little now, I mean, after the whole Hilton and Jackson incident…” his words began to trail off as Lacuna gave him a one look kill glance that cut straight through him and scoured the rolling green hills to his rear. “Bad choice of subject Brook, they aren’t my favourite people as of late you know. And only one of them is in hospital” “Albeit the right one?” he phrased it as a question because he knew he was treading false ground which was likely to collapse under his bulk at any second. Lacuna looked away and let the breeze blow he raven black hair across her face as she closed her almond eyes to the light. Her thin arms closed across her chest as she turned a shoulder on Brook who stood up instinctively, but sitting down again as he realised the affects interrupting her thought could have on his genetelia. “You know… my heart says so, and they say you should always go with your heart…” her words trailed off as she lost track of the sentence in her mind. “But?” prompted Brook, who knew that getting Lacuna to open up a little would be the only way to get any sanity from her until Spring Fling. “But my head is saying the opposite. Hilton can’t wrestle, she’s admitted as much, but Jackson can and he’s one conniving son of a bitch as well. Look what he did on his own last week while Hilton lay in hospital. I can’t help but feel I’m doing this the wrong way around” Her right hand moved cautiously up to her forehead as her eyes closed again in sheer contemplation. She was open and prone, yet things were just as confusing now as they were before, maybe even more so with the new addition of emotions to the equation. She was laying herself bare to the erosion of her mind by the elements, and it was dragging her straight back down to the little place she no longer wanted to be a part of. She had had enough of thinking, but she couldn’t stop now. Lacuna was becoming one with her mind again, like a big mould of human flesh that, instead of moving, moved mentally, trying to sort out its problems by thinking too much about them, wearing them thin with argument upon argument. A war of attrition if you will. Brook stood to his feet and smoothed down his creased red polo neck top. It was his queue to leave now. His words had seemingly had the wrong effect on her, as she was doing what she usually ended up doing on her own anyway. He had hoped that she might open up to him a little; maybe even give out some fresh information on why she was being like this. But alas, she had just shrunk back into her shell of thought again. As he crossed the close cropped grass towards the house, he couldn’t help but feel sorry for Lacuna. It was unfortunate that her one fatal flaw was something so craved by nearly the rest of humanity as a whole. ----- Lacuna had found herself walking through the woods. She had no idea how long it had been, or how long she intended to walk for, but all she knew was that it was quiet and comforting. The trees were densely packed in and the light was nearly blocked out by the thick canopy of deciduous leaves. The ground beneath her feet was a thick compost of rotting leaves that sprung gently as her feet slowly dredged its surface. She had been doing this for far too long now, this thinking business, and she needed a break. But maybe being along wasn’t the best thing for her right now. “Why me? What have I done to deserve this? Have I not paid enough for this degradation trip I’m constantly presented with?” She sat down on the gnarled base of a tall oak tree, scratching idly at the thick layer of detritus forming at its bottom with the side of her right foot. She stared at the ground as the world began to fall down on top of her. “Hilton was the problem, now Hilton is sorted. Jackson is now the problem, and he will be sorted. He may be an agent of Kannon, a pawn in his own personal war, but this cannot continue. I will not and can not lose my job because of a trained lackey. Lackey’s will come and go, but the true of heart stay, and that is what I will do. There is no way I will be forced out of this company by a half-hearted dimwit and a clued up egotist…. No way.” An acorn fell from the tree and Lacuna looked up. It was the wrong time of year for acorns. Maybe this was a sign. She didn’t care, at Spring Fling, The Significant Other’s would end the joyless quest for domination of the two graduates, because there was no bloody way in hell that they would lose. Lacuna wouldn’t allow it.
