Rolling in and out of bars...

7 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 07/04/02 Rolling in and out of bars… I sat silently thinking over my current affairs. The kidnapping of Isiy, my winning of the Television Title, and more importantly Akiyama and his damned disgraceful acts of evil that he’d cast down upon my family, my friends, and me. He had done so much to anger me in the past few weeks that my feud with him had seemed to last years and not the actual amount of time that I had in fact been at loggerheads with the Neo Void God. He was a waste of space, an evil that not many could match up to, and yet that fateful Sunday I had to defeat him, I had to defeat him, I had to…or I’d never see her again. She was the one thing in my worthless immortal life that had ever kept me going through the years of depression and the years of wonder that I had lived. Isiy, my everlasting love, my eternal cure to my sorrows, my life, my whole damn life! And He…He had taken her from me! Things didn’t seem worthwhile now, but I knew I had to keep my head up and rise above Akira and his spiteful tricks. It would result in being a difficult affair to defeat the monstrous God, but I knew I could do it…and as I had already thought, I had to. Can the evil in one man’s heart drive another insane with anger? Right now I thought so. Damn him! And damn his ways! His very heart was as black as the interior of his eyes, and now on that Sunday night, on that PCW program they had named Spring Fling I had to do something, I had to get up and fight, fight for what I lived for…fight for her, fight for Isiy! What a battle it would be though. Akiyama Versus The Pharaoh… The crowds had been waiting for it for too long, too damn long and finally it was going to happen, finally these two Gods, I being one of them, were going to get down to business and sort out all their problems in the ring. I was going in there as the champion of course, but the champion of what? The ‘TV’? This was not a fight for a meagre championship! This was a battle because of someone’s love for something and for someone else’s hate for that other! Ah how he would hurt after our battle, it would be a great time for me, a great time indeed! Rejoice would fill the air as I reclaimed her, and the crowd would cheer me on as I set that worthless rat up for some horrible fate, a broken neck perhaps? I wanted to put him out of the business but it would in fact result in being harder than I first perceived. Yes, it was true! Akiyama was tougher and harder hearted than was known. Many had doubted him in the past but tonight I knew he’d give it his all to reclaim what he believed to be his one true love, something that I held of ‘his’. The ‘Akivision’ title…or MY TV title, as it was better known. How on earth he could have got himself into a state where he actually loved that title I don’t know, he reminded me of one significant character from a great book I had once read. The creature Gollum, and his quest for ‘his Precious’. Akiyama had more than one characteristic in common with the foul creature from J.J.R Tolkein’s ‘The Lord Of The Rings’ trilogy though. Indeed, Gollum had been like a foul linger that never seemed to leave the fellowship, Akiyama too was like that. He had been following me around, sabotaging my plans, stealing things from me, destroying people dear to me. And I’m sure, like Gollum, that he wouldn’t reconsider strangling me in my sleep! But all of this was off of the point; I was going into the Fling to squared up to the Neo Void God for the things I believed in. And I was determined not to fail in my valiant quest to rescue Isiy, defend my TV title successfully, and claim the name ‘God’ in our battle. And that, as far as I considered, was exactly what I was going to do! ‘You havin’ another, mate?’ said a distant voice. I was in a world of my own. I looked up from my dream-like state to vision a man, looking on quite perplexed as if he didn’t quite know what was up with me. I stared at him and then, only just realizing that he had asked me a question, I snapped out of it and rubbed my forehead strenuously. ‘Yes, yes I’ll have another. Sorry about that, I was in a world of my own.’ The man shrugged and walked off to get me another drink. I stared down and gazed into the glass under my nose. The stench of strong alcohol lingered into my nostrils opening them to the true smells of the world. This was reality, this was the truth, this was how it really was. Life, living on a mortal world, even if you were being tortured deep down by another immortal being like yourself, was poor… It always had been, and I’m not talking about the amount of riches I had stored away in the palace, back in Cairo! Even when I had ruled as the King of Egypt, now more than six thousand years ago, life had been poor. Constant arguments with my fellow Egyptian counsellors had given me a reputation as being a trouble-starter. I wasn’t that guy who just sat back and watched as the sands of time flowed quickly through the metaphorical hourglass, I was the guy who always got involved, who always said what he thought, well when he saw fit to that is. Akiyama wasn’t going to get away with the terrible acts he had cast upon my close relations and me. The bastard had killed my closest friend, Carmoon. Cast into that dark destructive place he liked to call the Void! He made me feel sick… Physically sick in my stomach! The man from earlier came back into my fairly blurred view and handed me a freshly poured glass of Stella. ‘Thanks’ I said. ‘No problem, man!’ He began to walk away but seeing that there was nobody else left in the bar to serve he came back over and began to talk to me. His voice boomed in my already thudding head, but I heard him out and gave him his answers. He was mainly bothered about the way I looked. Frowning, sometimes switching to a look of deep sadness. I suppose I couldn’t snap at him, he didn’t know what had happened to me lately, so I let him off and talked. ‘So what’s your story?’ he asked sounding genuinely sympathetic. ‘Took much to tell’ I replied softly… I didn’t really want to tell a Mortal of the things that were pestering me, and to be frank I couldn’t really be bothered either. I just wanted to sit here, in peace, drink my pint and then go home, wherever that was for the night…Horus had sold the old house, so basically I was back in Anaheim with nowhere to go and nowhere to stay! ‘Come on, buddy, it helps to talk.’ He certainly was interested…that was for sure, but I couldn’t be bothered, I shrugged my shoulders and then, pleasantly mind you, told him to leave me in peace. ‘Look, I don’t mean to sound like a jackass or anything, but please, too much has happened to me lately and I just don’t want to speak of any of it…not with a mortal anyway.’ The man sighed and then left me at last… I watched him walk away and then I sighed to myself. I had been rolling in and out of bars all day, trying to relax before my match, but nothing seemed to work…depression for the loss of Isiy had been driving me into drinking like a thirsty alcoholic. This wasn’t at all like me, I had always enjoyed a good drink at times, and when I drank I drank a lot, but even for me this was too much. I had gone way over my limit. The Gods knew what would happen when I saw his evil face in the ring… I actually feared for him… I might kill him, and what then? Life imprisonment most likely… One thing was for sure though I had to get out of here… I picked up my glass and downed the pint of Stella in a few select gulps. I wiped my mouth and sighed, stretched and then waved goodbye to the bar tender unenthusiastically and left the bar that had housed me for the past few hours. The doors seemed huge and as I opened them to the harsh view outside, a rainy city of depression, sadness awakened me. The raindrops clattered down upon him forehead as I looked up into the dark night sky… Finally, and at length I spoke silently to myself ‘It is time…’
