Robbery (II)

6 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6th December Time 2043 6th November Deuce 00 Deuce SA#2 He lives in a world of small recalcitrantThings in bottles, with tacky labels. He was alwaysA man who did-it-himself. Kai looked down the hallway knowing too well, an atrocity was written in the stars. ~± ¤ ±~ The woman looked up immediately, alerted by the intricate shadow cast on the floor before her. As Hollings’ hand emerged from the door, she exploded from her kneeling position and smashed against the swinging panel, catching his wrist. The gun fell. As she reached for it, Hollings hurled the door open again, hitting her outstretched arm. She kicked the gun into the darkness even as she recoiled. “Motherfucker,” she snarled, crouched low in the position of defence, her hands held crossed before her, calloused edges out. “You come near me, and I’ll do to you just like you did him.” Hollings leapt forward in a series of kicks. She spun to her left, and countered with a blow to the back. He evaded her. “Come on, you little shit,” she said. “I’ll kick your white ass.” Hollings was more audacious in his next manoeuvre. He leapt almost parallel to the ground and lashed out with a kick to the head. She moved her chin an inch, and the foot passed by harmlessly. As he tried to land on his hands to follow with another kick, she struck him in the groin with clenched fingers. He crumpled at her feet. She walked forward and probed him with her toe, then opened the door. Kai stood face to face with the woman. She roared, “A Chinese motherfucker huh?” Kai raised an eyebrow, and flicked his wrists forward. The sleeves of his hooded top rose to his elbows to reveal two neat scars: one of a dragon and the other of a tiger. She looked at his forearms, “No fucking way…” her knowledge was good, but it was the last thoughts she would ever have. Kai swayed to his right to avoid the woman’s right hand, as he swayed towards her he struck her on the nose with an open palm. Crushing the larger bone in her nose, splintering it, she recoiled… it would be her last action. The momentum of Kai’s strike led to his right arm swinging across his own body towards his left arm. He grasped his own hand and hit a trademark Taijiquan elbow uppercut, using his left hand to push his elbow up, under and through her nose. She flew through the air. Dead before she hit the ground… Kai turned to Pfister, who was cowardly hiding, and shook his head. He turned his attention to the fallen Hollings. Pfister moved towards the broken Hollings. Hollings stank of his own vomit. “I’m bleeding inside. It hurts. I can feel it. My balls.” He leaned forward and threw up again. “Listen we got to go. You know how the plan works. We got to go.” “I can’t go…” the words seemed to drip from Hollings mouth. Another last action…Kai cracked his heel down on Hollings’ throat with a perfectly measured axe kick. Instantaneous death. He was dying anyway. To reduce the possibility of identification, the shocked Pfister took everything from his pockets and threw it into the black plastic garbage bag. For good measure, he stepped behind him, grasped his chin, and wrung his neck. There was a satisfying crack. Kai thought for a minute about the girl on the floor. She would do nothing. She could not do anything. He shrugged and carried on down the hallway, Pfister in pursuit. Kai took the black bag and let himself into the service elevator, along with Pfister. He walked out into the street past the garbage cans with the sack over his shoulder. The security guard looked up shortly from his comic book, then went back to his reading. ~± ¤ ±~ “When I go from hence, let this be my parting word, that what I have seen is unsurpassable.” ~± ¤ ±~ Kai was bothered indefinitely as he and Pfister went their separate ways. He had not said a word throughout the whole robbery. Perhaps… perhaps his training had taken its toll. His mind was playing tricks. What did this mean? Adrenaline was dawning, and the tricks needed to cease. ~± ¤ ±~ The past never forgets…
