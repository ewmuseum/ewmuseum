Jaden Meets Hank and John Might Meet Death

7 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: June 7, 2002 Jaden leaves the motel ready to go search for this Aeryn Character. She stares up and down the streets looking for answers, in addition to looking at the tall skyscrapers. The only thing Jaden has to work with is John’s address book that was in his room. It has list of people’s names but two names stood out like a sore thumb, Aeryn Sun and Hank Sun, owner of “Sun’s Gym.” “First stop Aeryn Sun’s house…Forty-two…twenty-one E. Twenty-fifth Street,” Jaden says reading, out of the address book. “She doesn’t live to far away,” she adds. Jaden begins walking down the street watching the street signs so she wont miss Aeryn’s street as she passes by them…“Twenty-two…twenty-three…twenty-four…TWENTY-FIVE!” Jaden looks for Aeryn’s house as she walks down the street. She notices she is not in Kansas anymore. The sidewalks are clean, grasses cut, children are playing, and large homes just to die for. She wish she could have grew up in this kind of community instead of an urban environment where she could not have any of these luxuries. However, the only thing she does not like about the area is that the people are looking at her funny as if she is a parasite. Someone who is looking for trouble as if she has a past with these people but they do not bother Jaden as she walks onto Aeryn’s steps. Ding-Dong, the bell rings, as a tall man, very muscular answers the door. Jaden recognizes this man from one or two of the pictures in John’s motel room where he is shaking hands with this man in a gym. “Hi, my name is Jaden. Are you Hank, Hank Sun?” “Yes I am and what do you want?” “I was just wondering if you can tell me something about Aeryn, is she your daughter, sister, a friend, or a relative?” “You are another damn news reporter! I told you people a thousands times leave the death of my daughter alone. She is gone!” “I am sorry to hear that. I am not a news reporter sir, I am John’s Crichton…umm, John’s…close friend.” “Oh, then come on in. I am sorry for yelling at you but I am tired of all these reporters coming to my door asking me stupid questions like, is Aeryn really dead!” “That’s ok…I just came to you because I am looking for answers.” “What kind of answers?” “Well, John and I have been dating for quiet some time now and if we get on a romantic level of some sort he tells me that I remind him of this girl named Aeryn. In addition, for the past few days I have been staying at his house and her pictures are everywhere. Therefore, I thought the only way I can find out who this Aeryn is or was is by asking John who she is but I cant, he left to do something, like revenge. I didn’t get that part in the letter he left me.” “What, that crazy fool! I hope he knows what he is doing. I pray the kid doesn’t die, thinking of you or Aeryn!” “What-huh-who! What kid, is John going to die,” Jaden says nervously. “This cant be happening,” Jaden says, wiping the flowing puddles of water from her hazel eyes. “What are you talking about?” “John cannot be looking for revenge everytime he think of Aeryn. I told him before, I know we both are sad but just let the police take care of it.” “Who, what, please tell me. Maybe, just maybe there is something that I can do to help him!” To be continued…
