Date: 2-23-02 **Theme Music: 'When I Grow Up' by Garbage** ----------------------------------------Dear Jenna, Another day, another dollar, right? I've decided to take up that offer from American Eagle. And all I need is to go ahead and win the Extreme Title. But...I'm wondering...will it be possible to go over HANK~! LeBlanc...let's say I'm confident...but I've learned something new about HANK~! recently... He's a little prick. Love, Jason---------------------------------------- [Our scene comes up on sunny Tallahassee, Florida...where Jason talks on the phone with his 'little' nephew...the infamous third part of High Impact, 'Malicious' Mark Estep. Or should we say, the *recently re-signed to PCW* Mark Estep. We're able to hear Mark's voice through the phone...because this is a promo, and we need to.] Mark: Come ON, man! [Jason is bumming around in his post-workout clothes, a sweatstained Core Wrestling School tee, short nike workout shorts, and a thin sheen of sweat glossing his body.] Jason: No. Mark: Pleeeease? Jason: Absolutely not. Mark: COME ON!! Jason: Would you STOP it?! No, you can NOT RUN IN DURING MY EXTREME TITLE MATCH! [There's a brief silence.] Mark: But... Jason: NO! Mark: Okay, okay, okay... [Another brief silence.] Mark: So what if they bring gun- Jason: Would you QUIT IT!? [There's a third and prolonged silence, and then the sound of laughter is heard from the other side of the phone.] Mark: Heh...I'm just kidding, Jase. Jason: ...right... Mark: No, seriously. I'm trying to get you to loosen up a little bit. It really wouldn't kill you to, you know. [Jason plops down at his computer and flips the screen on, revealing the OFFICIAL PCW web site, and he wipes his sweaty fingers on his shirt.] Jason: And you need to get down here soon so we can get you back at a competitive level. Mark: Hey! PWA IS competitive! [This time, it's Jason who laughs.] Jason: No...seriously. PWA's about as competitive as a nuclear waste dump. Mark: You know, that's really unfai- Jason: Alright, maybe not EVERYONE in PWA is terrible...but I know that if I were running PWA, there'd be a LOT more transference going on. There's no WAY I'd let those Civil Disobedience guys have stayed in there long enough to get picked up by OSW, that's for sure... Mark: Well... Jason: At any rate, when are you gonna get down here for a little while and get to training? Mark: Well, we DO kind of have to be on tour all the time. Jason: Yeah, yeah...we'll hitch a ride back here before March Madness. Mark: You're not gonna make me pretend to be Karl LeBlanc, are you? [Jason can't BELIEVE he let himself so open...] Jason: Sorry to disappoint you, Mark...no prison rape skits... Mark: You KNOW what I meant! Jason: Nah...I think we'll be alright. Mark: Cool. Jason: Yeah...say, do you know anything about Hank Gunyon? Mark: Not a whole lot...why do you ask? [Jason lets out a deep sigh...] Jason: Well...you know I've got that match against him. Mark: Yeah, everyone knows. Jason: Well...I kind of am worried. Mark: ABOUT HANK~? Jason: Well...a little. [We see that J-Core's face belies a bit of embarassment at the idea.] Mark: What are you talking about? Jason: I mean...if I lose to this kid, I'll NEVER be able to live it down. I mean, it's HANK~! for chrissakes! Mark: Ouch...good point. Jason: I can't be another Doug Troy... Mark: Hey, Troy wasn't so bad, y'know. He was an Extreme Champ a coupla times, y'know! Jason: Yeah, and he was the only one to win both the Extreme and Heavyweight titles in the same match. Mark: Yeah, I heard that somewhere. Jason: At any rate, losing to HANK~! would be too much. I can't afford that at all. Not at this stage of the game. Mark: Well, just keep him from being TOO extreme...and try to let his buddy Ganker be the one to counter that scumbag Riley. Drop a Widowmaker on HANKY~! and it'll be over before ya know it. Jason: Alright... Mark: Be more worried about me! I've got a Leblanc of my own. Jason: Yeah...you're his warmup for me at the PPV. Mark: Poor guy. Jason: Yeah...to have his confidence built up in one match, only to have it brought back down the next night...sniff...what a disappointment. Mark: Rot in hell, Uncle. [Jason laughs, and Mark can't help but laugh as well. Jason surveys the recent press releases...] Jason: Boy...I guess Riley thinks he can make LeBlanc the complete package... Mark: I think he could make him a complete member of the Reamers, but that's about it. Jason: True. Mark: Man, I swear...you're too morose. You need to loosen up. As soon as I get down there...we're gonna go out, we're gonna meet some college freshmen chicks, and we're gonna get you the play you so desperately need. [Jason winces, at the idea of interaction with women...but smiles at Mark's idea of helping him.] Jason: Fine...but only if you promise to work out and let me undo all the damage they did to you down at PWA. Mark: It's a date. [Jason can't help but make another rib.] Jason: Date? You really DID hang out with that couple Chase and Downey too long... Mark: Can we not talk about that? Jason: Alright, alright... Mark: Well, I gotta ride out...you sure about the run-in thing? Jason: YES! I'll see you in a few. Mark: Right...later, Uncle! Jason: Later... [Jason hangs up the phone and returns to his studies on the HANKer~! ready for Sunday...]
