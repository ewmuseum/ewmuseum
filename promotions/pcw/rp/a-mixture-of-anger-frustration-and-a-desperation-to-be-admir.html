A mixture of anger, frustration and a desperation to be admired and respected.

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Now It was a deal made with the devil. Boda sold his soul to the New Breed in order to gain what he holds dear, to regain the PCW Championship Belt, which, after waiting so long to get, he disgraced by losing it to Miyagawa just a few days later. And now Boda is the PCW Champion, whoopdidoo. Only days after having one of the shortest PCW Heavyweight title reigns in history, Boda has now followed it up with what can already be guessed as the worst PCW Heavyweight Title Reign in history, and yes that is including one Stewart Hix. For at least Stewart Hix, the bastard of a man that he was, had some pride in himself, even Stewart Hix had some misplaced self-dignity. The moment Boda made that deal he lost all respect from both himself and the fans. The moment he shook hands he became a shadow of a man. The moment he signed his name of the dotted line he came to fully admit what many others had known, that he was nothing more than Chris Chase's bitch! Sure, the gap of personalities between Boda and New Breed has slimmed, and from the perspective of an idiot it might seem as if Boda has displayed what would be deemed by New Breed as more likeable actions, but this is not the case. For whilst Boda has sold his soul to the devil in Chris Chase, when New Breed sell their soul to the devil, the devil they sell it to is none other than the men they are looking in the mirror. How strange, after everything PCW did to try and get rid of Boda and his ratings-dropping ways, it was his own self that ended him, proving to the world the stupidity he had that had caused him to have so many damn enemies in the first place. ::The scene opens up in a store, the camera fades into an aisle of that store where we see Corey walking along the vinyl floor, the lights from the roof reflecting off of it. Corey walks up to a selection of power tools and stops, he turns to the shelf and inspects the tools available. Suddenly one of the shops employees approaches Corey with a courteous smile.:: Employee: Excuse me sir, can I help you. ::The employee seems to ignore the fact that Corey is wearing tight leather pants and a bear fur coat with no top underneath, Corey, however has never been any good at ignoring the truth, or at least the truth according to him. Corey's eyes look the employee, who is barely over 18, up and down, not impressed with what they see.:: Corey: I highly doubt it, but I'm sure it will be entertaining to watch you try. ::Corey gives a superior smirk.: Employee: Right then sir, what is it that you are looking for? Corey: Well my job and title require a lot of sneak attacking and prying on the weak, so I'm looking for something that says 'don't fuck with me.' ::The employee nods:: Employee: Uh-hunh, and what is it that you do? Corey: Excuse me? Employee: I said, what is it that you do? Corey: You can't be serious........ ::Corey laughs nervously, as if he is hoping it to be all a big joke, but the employee is still looking puzzled.:: Employee: I'm sorry, should I know you from somewhere? Corey: Should you know me from somewhere? Employee: Well I-- Corey: SHOULD YOU KNOW ME FROM SOMEWHERE!?!?!? ::Corey interrupts the employee and stares down at him, getting right in his face, his nostrils flaring. The employee doesn't know who Corey is but he sure as hell just got some sort of idea about it.:: Employee: I-I-I-I-- Corey: I'm 'Controversial' Corey FUCKING Williams! Employee: I-I-I-I-- ::Corey moves forward whilst talking.:: Corey: THE most Controversial man in professional wrestling. Employee: I-I-I-I-- ::Corey moves the employee back.:: Corey: Member of THE single most dominant stable in PCW's history. Employee: I-I-I-I-- ::Corey backs the employee against the shelf of power tools.:: Corey: And currently HEADLINING PCW goddamnit! Employee: P-P-PCW? I love PCW! ::This twist confuses Corey Williams for long enough for him to back up a little, the Employee sees his chance and tries to make an exit. But he is stopped by the sudden movement of Corey's arm thrusting up against the shelf, blocking off the employee's escape route.:: Corey: Wait.... ::Corey's head snaps back towards the employee.:: Corey: You mean to say that you actually watch PCW and you STILL don't know who I am? ::Corey grabs the employee by the scruff of his shirt, Corey gives the coldest stare yet, he poises himself, waiting for the man to give the wrong answer so that he can pounce.:: Employee: I-I-I-I... I haven't watched it for a long time. ::Corey pauses as he takes this in, deciding the fate of the employee.:: Corey: ........Well that's OK then. ::Corey let's go of the employee's shirt and shrinks back to his normal size. In fact, he is so normal that the employee can't help but be a little suss at first.:: Corey: Anyway, so I need something that can also symbolise what a TOOL Boda is... ::Corey spots a power drill on shelf, he picks it up.:: Corey: Like this maybe..... ::Corey tests it out by pushing a button, turning the battery-operated drill on.:: Corey: Hey, this reminds me of what I did to Jon Kano's Mom last night! ::Corey gives the drill another whirl.:: Employee: Say, I know Jon Kano! He rocks~! ::There it is, a bad answer. Corey swings around to face the employee, brandishing his new power tool and fuming angrily at him. The employee takes a big gulp of air and then slowly grabs Corey's hands and cautiously guides them back down on to the shelf, where he makes Corey let go of the drill.:: Employee: Let's just put that down shall we? We don't want any......accidents. Corey: Oh really? And why not? ::Corey flashes a sadistic smile, which makes the employee quickly turn and change the subject. Our camera zooms in on the employee as he walks down the row of shelves.:: Employee: Now what I think you're looking for is cold, hard, steel. Something that you can use to get the quick knock out on anyone who crosses y-- GUNK! ::The employee collapses to the floor. Our camera zooms out to reveal Corey Williams standing over the limp body, holding a wrench in his hand. He has seemingly snapped.:: Corey: If Jon Kano rocks so goddamn much then where the hell is he now!?!? How dare you put Jon Kano before me! How dare you disrespect my name! I'm 'Controversial' Corey Williams for the love of Chase, the man on everyone's lips! Kano can't hold a candle to me, I'm a PCW headliner! ::Corey talks down at the employee, that is, the formerly moving employee, out cold.:: Corey: Kano is NOTHING! Boda is NOTHING! Miyagawa is NOTHING! I am something, I am the man you should all be thanking! ::It's a mixture of anger, frustration and a desperation to be admired and respected that can make Corey such a dangerous individual, ready to attack at any point.:: Corey: Kano, I've just embarrassed your fans in front of you, and on Friday I'm gonna do the opposite and embarrass you in front of your fans. ::Corey looks up to the camera, breathing heavily and smiling satisfactorily.:: Corey: Y’know, maybe it's not me that's the pain in your ass, maybe it's Stephen Smith's throbbing cock! You sure as HEEL built this company from the ground up, unfortunately you never managed to get OFF the ground yourself. You're a legend all right Kano, but to me that's just a synonym for has-been. You say you ended Johnny Raindance's career? Well I say that Johnny Raindance never even had a career to begin with. You've made careers, and you've broken careers, but when push comes to shove you could never beat anyone who actually mattered. And though you've tried time after time, you have never, NEVER beaten me. It's called evolution Kano, each generation surpasses the last, you're time is running out, and you're just about to become extinct like the dinosaurs. ::Corey begins to laugh at his own words, slowly beginning to regain his grip on reality, no longer just ranting and panting. He returns to his cool, cocky, normal self. He looks at the wrench in his hand and smirks.:: Corey: Well I’ll be damned….. it really works. ::Corey reaches into his pocket and pulls out a fistful of bills, which he throws onto the lifeless body of the employee.:: Corey: .......Keep the change. ::Corey turns and walks off, the wrench in-hand. The camera focuses in on the unconscious face of the employee, and we hear Corey's final words.:: Corey: Clean up on aisle six.... ::The scene fades to black.:: !The End!
