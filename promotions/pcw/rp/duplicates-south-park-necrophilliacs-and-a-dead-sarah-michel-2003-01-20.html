Duplicates, South Park, Necrophilliacs and a Dead Sarah Michelle Gellar; Part One.

20 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: January 20, 2003. “Right…and…take one.” The scene fades into another relentless Jackson Rust promo, with several of the same editor’s the brought to you, “In Al’s Dreams…” standing around a room, looking bored as all hell, some even peering at the open window that would lead them down a seven story drop. Okay, okay…I lied…more like four feet. Jackson Rust: Dude…I don’t want another promo like last time! All’s I got was a bunch of grapes. Al: I had a wet dream. Narrator: *shudders* Steve: Sh-sharks. Kimberly: I was…dreamily raped. Al: Yeah, it was dreamy, wasn’t it? Kimberly: NO! A nerdy looking JacksonCO editor raises his index finger for attention. Editor #4: Yes…umm, Mr. Rust? Jackson doesn’t hear him, but continues chatting with Al…or…starts is more like it. Jackson Rust: So Al…how…was your day? Al: *shrugs* It was okay. Editor #4: Mr.. Rust? Kimberly taps Jackson on the shoulder, causing Jackson to turn around. Jackson Rust: Yes? Editor #4: Oh great, you can hear m— Jackson Rust: What is it Kimberly? Editor #4: My name’s not Kim— Kimberly: Yeah, uh…some nerdy dork is trying to ask you something. Jackson Rust: Funny…I couldn’t see, nor hear any nerdy editor. Narrator: *snarls* *SMACK* *SMACK* *SMACK* Narrator: ANSWER THE FUCKING DORK OR WE’LL LOSE RATINGS! Al: We have ratings? Narrator: *sighs* Not anymore… Jackson crosses his arms in an angered fashion. Jackson Rust: He doesn’t exist unless I say he does. The glove dangles in front of Rust’s face. Rust sighs, and instinctively turns about face to see the nerd. Jackson Rust: Yes? Editor #4: Uh, yeah…I had an idea for your promo and or gimmick! Jackson scratches his chin, pondering the thought. Jackson Rust: Nerds equal brainpower…shoot— Editor #4: Well, I had this— Jackson leans over to tie his untied shoe. Jackson Rust: My shoe came untied. Rust drops to a knee, and leans over to lace up his fashionable JacksonCO shoes, as something dawns upon him. Jackson Rust: Oh yes…what was it that you had an idea for? Al: Yeah…anything could be better than the last promo we had. Steve: D-def-defin-definat-definitely. Kimberly: Agreed. Narrator: Hell yeah. Editor #4: Yeah…well, you know how the End, Skylar Green has the Suburban Ninja Clan every so often? Narrator: Oh dear, you brought up “The End.” Al: Run. Editor #4: Why? What’s so bad about the End? Kimberly: Nothing…except Jackson doesn’t like reminiscing with past experiences with the End. The scene goes all wobbly, almost in “flashback form”. The posse all look toward Rust, who snarls angrily. Al: Aren’t you gonna stop the flashback effect? Narrator: Or should we go into it so you can say bad words? Jackson Rust: Yes that would be great. Steve: B-bad wo-words! *SMACK* The scene reverts to a quick wobbly effect, before indulging into the past Golden Turnbuckle Tournament, pitting Jackson Rust versus The End in a Bracket Finals match. The two trash each other, verbally and physically throughout the flashback. Narrator: Yeah, I bet you’d like to see the match, and or promos, huh? Well too damn bad. Yeah, the promos accidentally got deleted, and the match…well, it was never fully written. So suck it up, and use your God damned imagination, to visualize Jackson Rust facing off against a blind man, and losing. The readers use their imagine here, and result in laughing their asses off, before the scene re-wobbles back into the present tense scene, wearing Jackson is still cursing. Jackson Rust: FUCK SHIT DAMN BITCH FART ASSHOLE! Al: Any more? Jackson Rust: Bar— Eric Cartman: BARBERA STRAISAND! The camera reverts to seeing an animated fatass child standing there, with his pixilated eyebrow pointed inward to make an “angry face” type of effect. Jackson Rust: Didn’t we rid of you in an earlier promo? Where I kicked Smurfs around and stuff. Eric Cartman: No! You Jew! I was dragged off my Stan and Kyle, and Kenny! Jackson Rust: I’m not a Jew. Eric Cartman: RESPECT MAH AUTHORITAH! Al: Authoritah? Eric Cartman: Son…of a bitch. Narrator: Listen kid, this isn’t the place to be using your catchphrases. This is a licensed JacksonCO Jackson Rust promo. We don’t need you ruining it. Last time you showed up, Steve had the green apple splatters for days. Steve is reverted into a South Park character, with the same eyebrow effect as Cartman. Steve: Y-yeah! Steve is reverted back into a real life human being. Steve: I’M A REAL BOY! [name withheld]: Oops, forgot the stutter there. Steve: D-damn it! Three more South Park animations waddle onto the scene: Kyle, Stan, and Kenny. Al: Wait…didn’t you die? Kyle: Jackass, no. He was killed in your last crappy promo, and then was cremated; but then was mixed into a cup and drunken by fatass here. Stan: So for a while, Kenny’s soul was in Cartman’s body, which eventually led us into getting our candy-shopping spree. Then it all came down when Rob Schneider starred in “The Stapler”, the “Hot Chick”, and many other dumbass roles. Kenny: Mmmphhmmph. Eric Cartman: Good one, Kenny. Jackson Rust: How does that explain Kenny being alive? Stan, Kyle, Cartman, and Kenny all look at each other for a moment. Kyle: I…don’t know. But he’s alive and kicking, and he should be killed in approximately ten seconds. Kenny’s eyes bulge outward. Kenny: HMMPH?! Jackson, Al, Steve, Kimberly, Editor #4, Kyle, Stan, and Cartman all pull out watches, and count down ten seconds. Jackson Rust: Here comes two! Stan: One. Kyle: Zero. All of a sudden, several sandbags, lighting equipment, and stereos fall upon Kenny, squishing him, and allowing the animated blood to sprawl across the ground. The remaining cast thus far, looks up from where the miscellaneous items fell from, revealing one man… Narrator: Isn’t that Editor #1? Editor #1: Hith, hith boo hith! Steve: L-loo-looks l-like it. Kimberly: Is that… Al: SMG! Sure enough, Editor #1 unleashes his fangs into the already dead corpse of Sarah Michelle Gellar, “hithing” all the while. Al: SMG! Jackson snarls, and unleashes a kick to the balls of Al. Al: No! I need those for future use! They all break out into laughter. Jackson Rust: Yeah right! Kimberly: Maybe in your dreams…literally. Eric Cartman: Even mah mom wouldn’t lay you! Kyle: Stupid! Stan: Hopeless, sexless failure! Steve: Y-yeah! Narrator: That was a good one, Al! Jackson Rust: Wait…why didn’t he fall down? The group ponders for a moment. Narrator: *snaps fingers* Kimberly: What? Narrator: *snaps fingers* Steve: Wh-what? Narrator: *snaps fin--* Jackson Rust: Tell us already! Narrator: Oh…sorry, I was getting caught up in my groovy snapping beat. The entire cast blinks blankly. Narrator: Anyway…he didn’t fall down, because last time you kicked him in the nuts, he had the literally dangling from his ass! Kimberly: Oh yeah… Al: I thought that was hemorrhoids. Jackson Rust: Anyway…anyone seen Al, lately? Kimberly: Nope…haven’t heard one word from him. Al: I’m right here— Narrator: I wonder where he is. Steve: I d-don’t. Stan: Anyway, come on fatass, we have to prepare for another episode. Kyle: Why can’t we have funny shows like Jackson Rust promos? Stan: We do have funny shows. Eric Cartman: And these promos SUCK ASS! Jackson Rust: YEAH! All of a sudden, Mr. Garrison comes on to the scene, riding on the back of Mr. Slave, with Lemmiwinks, the gerbil trying to escape them. Eric Cartman: Okay, they’re not THAT bad. Al: Wasn’t Lemmiwinks saved? Jackson Rust: Who said that? Al: Me… Narrator: Who? Stan: Okay, let’s go… Kyle: Yeah, hopefully our sick queer of a teacher stays in this crappy 3D dimension. Eric Cartman: Yeah…2D kicks ASS! Jackson Rust: Definitely. As an unknown, super cool looking portal appears out of thin air, and the South Park trio enters it, a sharp noise is heard, as well as smacking. Mr. Garrison: Hurry up, Mr. Slave! The portal’s gonna close! Mr. Slave: Jesus Christ… Mr. Slave makes a leap, and enters the portal, causing Lemmiwinks to fall, and land in the 3D world, as the portal closes. Mr. Garrison: Hi, boys. Eric Cartman: SON OF A BITCH! The portal makes a cool warping sound, as he disappears, leaving Lemmiwinks with nowhere to go. Al: I am not gay! Narrator: Yes, you are. Jackson, Steve, and Kimberly ignore Al and the Narrator as they lean over, and pick up Lemmiwinks. Kimberly: Aww, poor thing. Jackson Rust: Can we keep him? Steve: Y-yeah! Kimberly: But he’s a 2D animal! Jackson Rust: So? He could make us millions. Jackson snatches Lemmiwinks from the palms of Kimberly, and curls him up onto his chest. Jackson Rust: It’s okay Lemmiwinks…that mean old witch won’t hurt you anymore. Kimberly: Hey! Jackson Rust: Chill, it’s just for dramatic effect. Steve: T-ten-tense. Kimberly: Oh…okay. They turn back to see Al arguing with a wall, where the Narrator is supposedly located, as Jackson strokes the fur of Lemmiwinks. Al: I am AL! HEAR ME ROAR! Narrator: Oh, I’m sure you’d like to roar with Mr. Slave and Mr. Garrison, wouldn’t ya? Al: Stop! Narrator: Queer bait! Queer bait! I betcha wanna stick Lemmiwinks up your ass, too, huh? Al: No! Narrator: Yes you do! Al: This is causing me years of psychological harm! Narrator: So? *SMACK* Narrator: How’d ya like that, huh? *SMACK* Narrator: Say you’re my bitch! Al: Never! *SMACK* Narrator: SAY IT! Al: NO! *SMACK* Jackson, Steve, Kimberly, and the newly acquired Lemmiwinks watch as Al resists, refuses, and gets smacked around by the cruel narrator. *SMACK* Narrator: Wait a minute! I didn’t call that last scene! Al holds his face in pain, snarling. Al: So? Narrator: That must mean there’s a second narrator! Narrator #2: Oh yeah, bith…I’m da new narrator! Hail me! I R GOD! Editor #4: Wait a minute! The posse all stops, and turn to the editor. Editor #4: Yes, I’m still here. Jackson Rust: Oh, right…yeah. Kimberly: You left? Editor #4: No…no, just been on the quiet side. Narrator: Oh. Narrator #2: Oh. Narrator: Stop copying me! Narrator #2: Stop copying ME! The editor walks a line between the groups, shaking his finger. Editor #4: Wait, wait, wait…Narrator…there is no second Narrator. Narrator: But there is! Listen! Narrator #2: Yeah! Editor #4: No! That’s just Editor #1 in disguise as Narrator #2, which fooled you, because you all expected him to return as Necrophiliac #3! Just then, the corpse of Sarah Michelle Gellar drops from above, and lands in front of Al with a thud. Kimberly: Who are the other necrophilliacs? The editor points to Al, himself, and then to hidden Generic Producer. Jackson Rust: How’d he get there? Generic Producer: I’m not here. Jackson Rust: But you are. Al: And I’m not a NECROPHILLIAC! Just then, a rope drops down, and Editor #1/Narrator #2/Necrophilliac #3 climbs down the rope, and walks to Al, placing his hand on Al’s shoulder. Editor #1: No use denying it…I tried that…but I only ended up killing my mother, Sarah Michelle Gellar, and a few other generics to please my urges. Kimberly: Ewww… Narrator: That’s a bit much. Jackson Rust: Yeah…hold on, I need a break for a moment. Kimberly: Okay…yeah, this promo has been going on for a while…I think we need to sandbag the series. Al: Definitely. Jackson Rust: Who said that? Al snarls as the scene takes a momentary break.
