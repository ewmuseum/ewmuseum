Natalia, Awake and Breathing

Date: not entered Walking forward, he stumbles slightly, the nervously moving feet of his hitting each other like two spoons at a cockney reunion. He reaches out for the doorway, the doorway into his past; his future; his present and opens it abruptly and steps inside. There she is, lying on the bed. She looks up at him; her hair sweated down, he eyes big and brown at him. Natalia: Hi. He sits down in the chair next to the bed and takes one of her hands in his and strokes the back of it gently, not exactly sure what to say to her after all, this is all his fault. Natalia: It’s not your fault. Miyagawa: Yes it is. Natalia: Don’t keep telling yourself that, or else you’ll never be able to forgive yourself. Everything has a purpose in life and this is just an action of fate. He smiles at her, her body more of a socket for various leads to shoot off into various machines around her. Her baby... no; their baby in amongst her body somewhere being looked after by the very best in twenty first century ignorance. The Greeks said that the entire body was controlled by things called “Humours” basically the same thing as the Asian Element system. In the middle ages, people were bled to cure ills. In the eighteenth century people were branded to stop bleeding... in the early twentieth century people were taking cocaine as a pain killer. One thing they all have in common... that people thought they were right at the time without bothering to think of a better way about it. And such is what happens when Miyagawa’s mind wanders and he doesn’t want to talk about it. Natalia: How’d the match go anyway, you don’t seem to have the belts on you? Miyagawa: I lost to Boda... I lost the world title. It wasn’t important, I was worried about you, I just wanted to get out of that stadium and see you... and when I got to the hospital... I just couldn’t bare to see you... I couldn’t bare to see you hurt. I was scared that you wouldn’t wake up, that we wouldn’t talk again. I didn’t want to end it like this. Natalia tightens her hand around Miyagawa’s, even though it has a drip line going into the back of it and of course the adhesive tape to keep that in place. Natalia: Don’t mind me, I’m just a medical experiment gone wrong. Miyagawa: Did the doctors tell you about the situation? Natalia: Da. And I’m sure that the good will out and success will reign in at the end. Life doesn’t stop living, it keeps going and you just have to go with the flow. Worst case scenario, we can air ship in some unfortunate immigrant, get JJ to marry her for an American passport and then drain her dry. Miyagawa pauses for a second as if that thought was something refreshing and his mind just had to review it that one more time. Miyagawa: Damn. Now why didn’t I think of that? Natalia: What would you do without me, eh? Miyagawa: Not a lot anymore. I lived for twenty four years without you, and only after being married for five months and I nearly loose you. It makes me realise just how much of my being is devoted to you. I could barely move without being in mental trauma. Jeremy gave me some cue cards that helped quite a lot; I keep one on me at all times. Miyagawa fishes a small white card out of his pocket, now slightly ruffled at the corners as if it has been taken in and out of that pocket on numerous occasions too many to mention. He holds it aloft and reads it out loud. Miyagawa: I will permit no man to narrow and degrade my soul by making me hate him. He places the card back into the pocket as Natalia nods at him supportively. Natalia: That’s sound advice. Don’t make this incident change what kind of person you are. I love the person you are, and I don’t want anything to change that. Nothing should ever change that. Don’t think that streamlining your emotions into a sharp blade will ever make better what has been done wrong because you will find that yourself comes up blunt in the end. Natalia pauses for a second and indeed, Miyagawa raises an eyebrow at her. Natalia: Sorry, went into one of those moments. Miyagawa: The moments of complete gibberish? I guess that proves my bad habits must be rubbing off on you or something, right? Natalia: Either that or I have a lack of oxygen. Miyagawa looks at her sharply and goes to grab the oxygen mask off the wall, but Natalia waves him to sit down. Natalia: If I wanted oxygen then I’d ask for it. Besides Oxygen is actually a poison and causes the aging effect, did you know that? Miyagawa: Try new Diet Oxygen from G.O.D. the ultimate in low calorie air replenishment. Miyagawa grins as Natalia giggles, but then clutches at her chest in pain. Miyagawa: Maybe jokes are a bad thing. Perhaps I should go; you look like you need your rest. Natalia: I’ve had far too much rest already as it is. I’ve missed three weeks of my life... of our lives. Tell me what’s happened, tell me what’s going on... just talk to me and everything will be right again. And that is how we leave the duo reunited for the first time since the attack on Natalia Miyagawa at the hands of Boda some three weeks ago... for the moment.
