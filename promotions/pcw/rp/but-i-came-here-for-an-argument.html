But I came here for an argument!

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Later than the last, but still before now. “He’s weirding me out.” (We return to the hidden confines of THE Multiple Man’s ultra-secret lair down the road from Wally’s Discount Steak Shack [It may be irregular meat, but dammit, it’s MEAT!]. Steve and Val continue their continuing observation of their enigmatic employer.) Val: “Think it’s an allergic reaction?’ Steve: “Couldn’t say, really. He just always acts like this when it comes to titles. It’s even worse if he actually WINS them. I ever tell you about the time he went bonkers…” Val: “Again.” Steve: “… and built a subterranean temple consecrated to himself deep in the murky sewers beneath the mean streets of San Jose?” Val: “Yup.” (Steve looks crestfallen.) Steve: “Oh.” (He perks right back up as a thought occurs to him.) Steve: “I ever tell you about the SECOND time?” (Val’s face scrunches up in concentration.) Val: “A lot like the first time, wasn’t it?” (All the enthusiasm falls away from him with the speed of a rocket-powered anvil.) Steve: “Oh.” Val: “Not my fault if you need new stories. Have you considered lying?” Steve: “I have, but no one believes them.” Val: “That’s because you lie about things to make you seem normal, while we all know you’ve got a twisted little stump for a psyche.” Steve: “Bah!’ Val: “See? There you go again, faking normalcy. Were you picked on a lot as a child for being different?” Steve: “We weren’t talking about my little quirks, we were talking about the boss’s large ones.” Val: “So this is an envy issue, is it? How positively Freudian.” Steve: “You hang around with Keira too much, you’re becoming as sarcastically bitter a cynic as she is.” Val: “You make yourself an easy target.” (Tmm walks by, a decidedly distracted look on his face, almost as if he is listening to silent voices coming from the ether.) Tmm: “What’s that, my golden lovely? You want me to take you? In front of ten thousand screaming people? My, aren’t we the adventurous type. No, no, I don’t suffer from performance anxiety, in fact, I kinda get a kick out of having an audience watching…” Val: “He’s REALLY weirding me out.” Steve: “This is actually pretty mild an episode, he hasn’t locked himself in a cupboard with crayons and a shotgun yet, and I haven’t seen signs of ANY high explosives at all.” Val: “A comforting thought.” Steve: “Considering the alternative? Yes.” Val: “It might really be an odd allergic reaction to something about the belts. Maybe the tanning process in the leather?” Steve: “I wouldn’t doubt it knowing Tyler Nelson, that cheap bastard. Dollars to donuts, every single one of those gold belts turns your skin green and is availible for ten dollars apiece from shady guys in trenchcoats, who’ll swear up and down that they fell off the back of a truck.” Val: “Me, I’d vote for electroplating or pounding it out really thin. Wonderfully ductile stuff, gold. You can mash it down to only a few atoms thickness. Either that, or he’s hollowed out the belts and left them like lead filled M&M’s with a crunchy gold covering.” Steve: “Oh, just in case you’re wondering, the gold does NOT cover a chocolate covering.” Val: “Uh-huh.” Steve: “Well, it doesn’t.” Val: “Is this from observation, or a more personal experience?” Steve: “Observation, but I did take a keen interest in the proceedings.” Val: “Mooch.” Steve: “Hey, if I’M not good to me, who will be? And I don’t see you turning down any free meals… or anything else for that matter!” Val: “One, I EARN my keep, and two, I don’t hover around like Unca Chen at a cat show, just looking for something tasty to wander my way!” Steve: “You profane the name of a genuine food artist whom you’ve NEVER MET, just to get your ridiculous point across. I hope you’re proud of yourself.” Val: “Food is not art, food is fuel. It’s because of people like you that puny dashes of sauce and miniscule slivers of meat scattered on a plate infest all the decent restaurants for thirty dollars a pop!” Steve: ”And I give daily thanks that the Health Department prevents people like you from installing feeding troughs on streetcorners!” Val: “Effiete!” Steve: “Animal!” (Tmm, passing by on one of his orbits, blinks.) Tmm: “Troughs? Cat Shows? Freud? You two are real weirdos, y’know that?” (He wanders off in his semi-random navigational course, again distanced from reality by a good twenty feet to the left. )
