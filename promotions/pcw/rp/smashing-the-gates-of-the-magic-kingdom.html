Smashing the Gates of the Magic Kingdom.

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Dedicated to Chef and all his PPV work. We rejoin our Big and Bold hero, Big Alan Steele, as he sits down to a nutritious breakfast of waffles within the kitchen of Stately Steele Manor. Trusted manager Sue Saksnussum enjoys a hearty Californian special, coffee and cigarettes, while reading the newspaper across from the Big guy. The Graces are bustling in the background doing dishes and various other sundry tasks. Big Alan Steele: (in between mouthfuls) “So, what purposeful training shall I, Big Alan Steele, engage in today to overcome the nefarious deeds of the culinary malcontent for yon Pay-Per-View?” Sue: (waving nonchalantly) “Eh. Nothing today. You need a day of rest to make sure you’re at your peak for tomorrow. I’d hate to send you out there worried that you’ve overtrained for such an important match.” BAS: “Rest? Overtrain? But I feel as if I could take on Cerebus himself at the Gates of Hades!!” Punctuated with a round of flexing. The Graces clap appreciatively while Sue never lowers her paper. Sue: “No way, Al. It’s nothing but R&R until the match. You see, muscles need recovery time after you put them through the training that we normally…” BAS: (crosses arms, looks away, sulking) “Fine. Whatever.” Sue: (bringing paper down) “Don’t you use that tone of voice with me! I’ve never failed you before, why would I WANT you to lose Sunday? With our newly ‘restructured’ contract, you’ll need to be in top form at all times. Losses could literally jeopardize my Jetta payments.” She uses the word ‘restructured’ with the sort of scorn you would reserve for ‘the vocal talents of Britany Spears.’ “How’s everyone doing?” Big Alan Steele’s partner in crime (fighting), the Sado-Masochist, waltzes into the kitchen, dressed in his usually patented pants and a black ‘Motorhead’ t-shirt. Sue: “Hey S&M. How’s things?” S&M: “Pretty good, pretty good. Just thought I’d stop in for breakfast and stuff. Oh, I picked up your mail on the way in.” BAS: (still huffy) “What brings your appearance this morn, yon Lord of Pain? Ms. Saksnussum shant allow us to train properly for the upcoming gladiatorial contest…” Sue: “Hush up, ya big baby!” S&M: “Hey man, relax. Sue had said yesterday that we weren’t going to be doing much, so I figured I’d come over and we could, you know, hang out. Go do something fun…” Sue: (picking through mail) “And restful! What a splendid idea! Now I can have S&M make sure you don’t head over to Gold’s Gym or the Downtown Athletic Club or Dr. X’s not-so-secret island lair just off the coast of San Diego.” BAS: (blurting out) “I protest most vehemently! I promise to not frequent Gold’s Gym or the Athletic Club!” Sue: “And Dr. X’s? I know you’ve been DYING to check it out since those CIA photos came in…” BAS: (mumbles something unintelligible) Sue: “That’s what I thought. And no heading to the slums of San Jose, either…” Big Al stands up, his chair flying backwards behind him, as he waves his arms maniacally around in anger. His toga ruffles in anger. BAS: (yelling) “Why do you not just force the eagle to not fly?! Why not just demand the sun to never shine upon the Earth again! By the gods, woman, you are taking away my very soul!!” S&M: “Er, did I come at a bad time?” Sue: “No, a perfect time. Lessee here, bill, bill, bill, bill, Ed McMahon, bill… say? What’s this? It’s for you Big guy.” Sue picks out a manila envelop and tosses across the table at Big Alan Steele, who angrily snatches it out of the air. Retrieving his lost chair, he sits and pulls out a ridiculously small pair of glasses, perching them on his nose, before ripping the envelop to shreds. Sue again starts reading the paper, while the Sado-Masochist begins diving into a steaming hot plate of once-frozen waffles. Steele reads the papers inside, before tossing it on the table with a ‘Bah!’ S&M: “Whatcha got there, Al?” BAS: (arm folded) “Nothing but frivolous entertainment of the placated masses! I shall have NOTHING to do with it.” S&M: “Mind if I take a look?” Without waiting for an answer, S&M snatches the papers and starts giving them the once over. A slow smile spreads across his face. S&M: “Hey! This is great!” Sue: “Hmmm?” S&M: “It’s from Disneyland! You know how they have their little specialized days now and then for groups, right?” Sue: “Oh, you mean like specific company days and whatnot?” S&M: (excited) “Yeah! But get this… they are having a special day to honor the heroes of the West Coast! (reading) ‘To give thanks to those special people who haves saved our small Disney community, California and even the world through the heroic deeds!’ It’s an superhero-only day!!” BAS: (gruff) “So?” S&M: “SO?! Big guy, they want you to attend! It’ll be you and a guest plus only 9 other superheroes and their guests! You’ll have a free run of the most fantastic playland in all the world! Hi-Keeba!” BAS: (indignant) “That’s my line!” S&M: “Sorry. Just getting excited for you…” Sue: “You know, that’s not such a bad idea, Al.” BAS: “Preposterous! I shall not mingle with those other faux-man-gods, nor shall I indulge in such a passive entertainment as watching animatronic mice dance to ‘It’s a Small World!’ Bah!” Slamming down her coffee cup, Sue growls angrily across the table at her bohunkular charge. Sue: “You moron! The nice people at Disney want to do something nice for you and you spit in their face? Do you know how much money Disney Inc. has? If they want to honor you and possibly hire you for commercials or be a spokesperson, then, by God, you’ll TAKE their money! With your new (spit!) contract, you could stand to possibly make a few bucks!!” S&M: “Uh, it doesn’t say anything about that, Sue.” Sue: “It doesn’t have to. Cripes, Disneyland pays a million to the MVP of the Superbowl, just to show up and float down their silly parade for 15 minutes! That’s a deal, in my book. What do you think they might pay to someone who recently saved the entire Californian coast from falling into the Pacific just last week?!” BAS: (shrugging) “I dunno…” Sue: “It’s time to start thinking in the grand scheme of things, Al. Times have changed a bit for us, and we need you as visible as possible.” BAS: “NO!” Sue: “YES! You’ll go to Disneyland and you will LOVE it! You got it!” Sue looks across at the Sado-Masochist, who is literally vibrating in his seat, his eyes wide with child-like exuberance. Sue: “And I think it’s only fair that you take S&M here along with you as your guest. After all, he IS your partner…” S&M: “WHOOOOOOO-HOOOOOOO!!!!” Sado-Masochist jumps up, pumps his fist in the air and starts doing back-hand springs down the hallway and out of sight. A crash later indicates he has stopped. Sue: “Look how excited he is. Take S&M to Disneyland, Al.” BAS: (thinking hard) “Hmmm… he does seem to enjoy the thought of wallowing in a world where cartoon animals dominate the landscape…” Sue: “And he does so much for you and you never take him anywhere but the slums or some secret bad guy hideout. He deserves a day trip vacation. And you need it. And Disney has lots of money.” BAS: “Tis a free amusement. I suppose I shall…” From down the hallway: “WHOOOO-HOOOOO!!” The collection of Graces in the kitchen all come over and give Big Al a Big hug. Steffi: (cooing) “Oh, Al! That’s so sweet of you!” Cindi: “Yeah, Big guy! You really are a hero!” Steele blushes and shoos them away as he stands away from the table. Sue tosses over the tickets. Sue: “And remember… be VISIBLE!” A slight frown from Steele indicates his not pleased with the planned events, but his silence tells Sue he’ll do it anyways. Taking a deep breath, he starts forward into the foyer. BAS: “Come, Lord of Pain! We must depart should we wish entrance to the Magic Kingdom at a pleasurable time! Hi-Keeba!” S&M: (still down the hall) “WHOOOO-HOOOOO!!!” =BAS= The Winnebago pulls into the massive, and empty, parking lot of Disneyland, pulling into a spot not too far away from the entrance gates. Before the huge vehicle is even shut off, the Sado-Masochist leaps out of the passenger side, literally hopping in place, unable to contain his excitement. The engine stops and Steele slowly emerges from the side, wary of his colorful surroundings. He produces his keys, and aims them at the vehicle. The Bago chirps and the lights blink, indicating ‘system armed.’ BAS: “Now remember, we are in the ‘Goofy’ lot.” S&M looks around the lot. Only three other cars are there. S&M: “I think we’ll be able to find the Bago. Now c’mon! We’re gonna be late!” Sado-masochist grabs Steele by the toga and drags him over to the entrance gates, a myriad of whimsical colors and cartoonish caricatures. Also noteworthy are the superheroes gathered at the gate: the Infragable Crunk, the Sloth, Death Adder, the Maestro, Manic Beetle, Ms. Demeanor, the Human Bullet, Janet Reno and Too-Much Coffee Man. They all stand eyeing each other, and the approaching duo, with disdain. There is no small talk, just an air of uncomfortable silence. Steel grins and steps forward. BAS: “Sloth! How hath thee been?” Sloth: (red eyed) “Tired… of waiting for you!” Ms. Demeanor: “Some hero you are… nice dress!” Crunk: “CRUNK WANT TEA-CUPS!!” Big Al frowns, still not yet used to the competitive world of superheroism. As the two step near the group, a powerful voice echoes across the sky, emanating from the inside of the park. Voice: “Welcome, strongest heroes of the world, to the Magic Kingdom – Disneyland!” The gates begin to slowly slide open. Manic Beetle pushes past eh others and starts screaming, running in a zigzag pattern into the park. The others roll their eyes and start inside, Big Al with a hand on the scruff of S&M’s collar to keep him from dashing off into the wondrous world of the Disney themes. BAS: “Heel, Lord of Pain Retention! We must stick together in order to not become separated and lost in the immensity of the Kingdom at hand!” S&M: (eyes dilated) “Okay, okay, okay. No problem. TOWER OF TERROR!! Tower of terror!! Let’s go!!” BAS: (looking around) “Wait for yon second… do you notice anything… odd?” S&M: “Yeah… I’m not at the tower of terror yet!!” BAS: (growling) “Refrain from such nonsensical ramblings!! I am referring to the lack of human vendors in the land.” The Sado-Masochist stops and looks around. There are, indeed, no other people in the park aside from the heroes that have already dispersed. The shops are all open, but unmanned. The animatronic Disney characters wave their hands, but in a clumsy, robotic motion. S&M: (impatient) “So? The park is OURS, Big guy! We rule the day! Disney has opened its gates to the heroes so we can have a free day of fun without worrying about the massive crowds that are always hounding us for autographs or sexual favors!” BAS: (eyebrows raised) “Sexual favors?” S&M: (embarrassed) “Well, a guy can dream, right?” BAS: (sigh) “Perhaps you are right. An enjoyable day released from the bonds that bind a demi-god into humanly servitude… quite a gesture from Lord Disney!” S&M: “Um, he died over 30 years ago. But hey! He’s dream lives on!” BAS: (warming up to the idea) “Indeed!” S&M: “So just wave to the hollow heads of the Disney characters and get on the tower of terror!!” The Sado-Masochist breaks off into a full gallop off into the direction of the stated ride. Steele grins at his partner’s enthusiasm for the day and looks at the close-by robot Donald Duck, quacking and waving at our hero. Big Al salutes the character and hustles to catch up with S&M… … as the eyes of Donald Duck slowly move sideways, following the man-god as he leaves. A darkened room. The only light source is a nine-monitor screen, all focused on Big Alan Steele, as he rushes off to catch up to the Sado-Masochist. A dark and sinister voice chuckles as he watches Big Al’s toga billow behind him. Voice: “Yes! Go and enjoy all the terror that Disney has to offer!! I’m counting on it!! Muahhahahaha!” =BAS= BAS: (bored) “Come! There are many other attractions to see at this kingdom that this infernal Tower of Terror!” S&M: (from inside) “Just one more time? Please?!” BAS: “Fine! One final time, but I swear it, you shall not have another opportunity as I will not wait here until doomsday, as you would have me!” S&M: (buckling up) “Why don’t you go on it with me then? It’s fun!” BAS: (harrumph!) “I shall not lower my demi-godular status by participating in such a lowly activity.” S&M: “Chicken! You’re scared!” BAS: (roaring) “Big Alan Steele fears nothing!!” S&M: (grinning) “Whatever… sayonara, sucka!!” The Sado-Masochist’s ride zooms off on another trip to the top of the tower, leaving a skulking Big Al behind. Steele looks around at the ‘horrific’ décor at the entrance and rolls his eyes warily. Somehow, he hasn’t been able to shake an uneasy feeling about the day. Trying to shrug it off, he listens to the surroundings. Somewhere up the tower, Steele can hear the zooming of the ride and S&M’s little-girl screams… SNAP!! Steele immediately goes into alert mode, the sound of a cable snap alarming him. The Sado-Masochist screams up above, more than usual. Big Al tenses up as the rushing of the ride becomes closer and closer until… CRASH!! The cart busts through the exit at ludicrous speeds, smashing into the floor and opening a huge gaping hole leading into pitch blackness. Steele, eyes wide, leaps to the side of the crevasse, listening for S&M to hit the bottom. And waits. And waits… Crunch! Steele counts a full 6 seconds before hearing the echo of the twisted metal below. Smashing the glass of a fire hose containment, he whips out the long hose and tosses it into the pit, then, with a deep breath, grabs hold and leaps off into the abyss, rappelling as far as he can. Steele slides down the rope at frightening speeds before reaching the end at the hose nozzle. Trying to gauge how far he has left, he scans below, but the pitch-black of the hole won’t even allow him to see his hand in front of his face. BAS: (shouting) “HI-Keeba!” Keeba! Keeba! Keeba! The echo is quick from the floor. Steele gives a silent prayer and lets go, hoping his echolocation is somewhat accurate. Freefall. Steele does his best to prepare for the jarring of the landing, but the force puts him over his center of gravity and causes him to roll forward. Coming up to a crouch in the inky black, he feels around blindly, hoping to find something that could tell him where he is. The only thing his fingers find is the smooth, cold granite of the floor. The feeling of something evil ebbing, breathing within the darkness quickens the pulse of our hero. Steele growls and stands to his full height, cupping his hands to his mouth. BAS: “LORD OF PAIN!! WHERE BE THEE?!?!” A groan from the right. Steele turns slowly and starts moving towards the sound… Just as spotlights flood the cavern with light, but as string as the beams are, they fail to illuminate the far edges of the room, the light seemingly swallowed up by the dark. Steele throws up his arms to protect his eyes from the piercing glare. Slow motion. Running towards Steele are three creatures of enormous size, at least ten feet in height each, silhouetted by the backdrop of the light. Their huge, muscular arms pump as they rush forward in unison, their massive leg muscles straining against orange and green skin grafts. Maniacal, human faces, the facial bones twisted and enlarged by experimental human growth hormones, register rage as they focus their hateful energies on the Greek hero before them. Electronic wires and LED lights glisten in the light, connected to their cranial bones. Steele takes a step back, still blind from the shock of the light. His eyes squinting, he manages to see the wreckage of the ride cart to the right, Sado-Masochist in the midst of the twisted and broken ride. Full speed. Instinctively, Steele dives forward, beneath the reach of the first monster, coming to a full tuck and roll. But he miscalculates the speed of the creatures, the second monstrosity managing to swipe a huge forearm, smacking Steele in the stomach and doubling him over. The third swings upward with an uppercut that sends Big Alan Steele airborne and horizontal, crashing to the floor a good distance away. Steele groans, as he watches the fireworks explode behind his eyes. Voice: (dark, sinister) “How pathetic! I certainly expected more from one such as yourself!” Steele struggles to a crouching position and spits out a mouthful of blood. Wiping a trickle from his chin, he grins and looks above for the omnipresent voice. BAS: “I have not yet begun to fight!! Hi-Keeba!!” With that, Big Al rushes the creatures, diving into the leg of the first with a violent spear that crumples the monsters legs inward, toppling him over. Steele keeps his momentum going and launches himself at the next creature, who is charging. With a grand display of dexterity, Steele leaps off the thing’s bent leg as it runs towards him, and uses it as a springboard to a devastating missile kick that whips its head back, the creatures arms flailing as it loses its balance and falls backward with a bang. Steele gains his balance and goes to turn, but the last monster grabs him by the head with a massive hand, picking him up into the air as if he was a child. Steele struggles, his powerful arms ineffective against the creature as he tries to free himself. The monster slams its fist into his gut over and over, Big Al whipping backwards in the grip helplessly. The creature than passes him off to another, as he grabs Steele’s legs and slams him into the floor. Big Alan Steele slides across the smooth granite, terrible pains wracking his body. He rolls to the side and vomits blood. Voice: “Come now… surely the great Big Alan Steele cannot be finished so soon by my lovelies?” The monsters gather themselves and start walking over towards our fallen hero. “Al! Al! Wake up, big man!” Steele opens his groggy eyes as the bloody Sado-Masochist shakes him awake. S&M: “C’mon Al! We gotta beat these guys… whatever they are!” Big Al coughs and manages to gather himself on all-fours. Breathing heavy, he looks up, his right eye beginning to swell over. And he grins. BAS: “Indeed we shall! I have their timing down now…” S&M: (waving frantically) “Timing?! What the hell are you talking about, Al?!” Steele gets to his feet and spits crimson. The creatures slow their advance, looking at their victim. Big Al walks over to the wreckage and picks out a long axle of the ride cart that was fairly undamaged. Spinning it around his head and swinging it forward, banging it off the ground with the sound reverberating off the stone floor, he holds it outwards, pointing at the monsters in challenge. The creations roar and charge. Steele grips his pole tightly and awaits their coming. The first rushes and swings, Big Al spinning under the attack and jabbing the pole directly into the back of its knee. The creature roars in anguish and collapses. Steele continues his move, swinging around and parrying a punch from the other creature, smacking the metal off the forearm just before it could reach him. Big Al plants the pole down and vaults himself into the air, planting his sandaled foot into the chest of the creature, knocking it flying backwards. Pushing off the massive form in air, Steele twists his body and rights his staff, The next creature unprepared for his change in direction. BAS: “Hi-Keeba!” Big Al slams the metal axel deep into the eye of the monster as he leaps on the monsters shoulders, tipping it over. The evil form screams in pain, a slick black ooze spurting out up and over the pole. Electrical currents buzz and alight around the puncture, as Steele twists around the pole and lands on his feet. Leaving the axel in, he grabs it and starts to spin, sliding the creature on the polished floor. Soon, momentum allows Steele to get it airborne and with a mighty heave sends it flying towards the second monster he kicked. The creature, now charging stops, just as its partner slams into it. The energy around the pole increases and bursts into flame, exploding with incredible force! Steele dives for cover as cybernetic body parts and scorching oil rains down from the dual explosion. Steele uncovers his head and looks over at the fallen cyborg, who is writhing and screaming on the ground, clutching at his chest. Steele gets up and looks on with a questioning expression as the huge monster cries out in pain, his chest stretching out. Suddenly, the Sado-Masochist bursts from his chest like that thing in ‘Alien,’ covered in oils and other ichors. He stumbles out and leaps, just as the creature explodes into a huge ball of flame, the concussive force spinning him across the granite. Big Al rushes over to help his friend, grinning. S&M is breathing heavily, in a panicked state. S&M: “I tried to help out, but you made him fall on top of me! I… I was trapped! So I did what I had to do… burrow out like a rat!” BAS: “Never you mind, Lord of Pain. Though once human, they had lost all manner of humanity through the obscene use of cybernetics.” S&M: “But that’s what I don’t get! Why would Disneyland have a cavern underneath it with these guys anyway!” BAS: (thinking) “Well, it is well known that Disney has pioneered robotic technology for years. Perhaps these were… mistakes.” S&M: “Steele. Big guy. Those… THINGS… used to be human! You don’t just go off and make someone into those creatures!! And what was with that voice?” Big Al gives him a tight lipped smile and clasps him on the shoulder. BAS: “I’m afraid we shall find out soon enough, Lord of Pain.” As if in answer, a beam of light kicks on, spotlighting a single doorway. S&M shrugs at Al and the two move on towards the only visible exit. Steele hesitates for but a moment before grabbing the handle and pulling. Stretching out before them is an entirely different scene than the sterilized environment of the previous cavern. This one is rocky and craggy, looking like a natural rock formation. The ground is uneven and treacherous. Stalactites send small droplets of water raining down from above. Big Al and S&M work their way across the rocks, climbing over a large formation to reach an underground lake, it’s dirty water swirling and hiding its inhabitants. Big Al rubs his eyes in shock, his jaw dropping at the structure before him. A huge pirate ship of the ancient world sitting in the murky waters of the cavern. S&M: “Cool! We’re at Adventure Island! This is the pirate exhibit and all. I hear the pirates are incredibly lifelike.” BAS: (snarling) “I should bet…” Steele is peering across at the deck of the ship, where activity can be seen. Al remains motionless, trying to gain an insight into just what is going on over there… S&M: “C’mon. Let’s get to the other side and try and salvage this day.” BOOM!! Wheeeeeeeeeeeee!!!! BAS: (screaming) “INCOMING!!” Big Alan Steele shoves the Sado-Masochist roughly, back and over a collection of stalagmites. Big Al then dives forward into a perfect swan dive into the lake. The cannon shell explodes right at the exact spot where the two were, a group of pirates cheering ‘arrr!’ at their accuracy. Steele, holding his breath beneath the depths of the water, starts to make powerful strokes, heading towards the ship at hand… when he is stopped suddenly! Steele tries to look through the water and can see a large, purplish mass behind him, a tentacle wrapped around his ankle. Big Al struggles to get the octopus to release its grip, but only succeeds in getting another tentacle wrapped around his neck. The suckers pull at his skin and Steele roars out in pain underneath the water, huge bubbles released to the surface. Running out of air, Steele desperately tugs at the tentacles, to no avail. In fact, the tentacles start pulling him downward, deeper into the waters below. Panicking, Steele swims towards the creature, hoping to gain some slack, and does. Reaching the bottom of the lakebed, he grabs two large rocks and cracks them together on the tentacles holding his angle. With an electric sizzle and a flash of light, the arm explodes and sinks to the bottom. Steele then does the same on his neck and makes a few powerful strokes, kicking off the lake bottom for more speed. BAS: (gasp!!) Big Al breaks the surface of the water and takes in a hug gulp of air, the octopus right behind him. He turns to try and escape… and looks right into a down-turned cannon on the ship. Pirate: “Arrrrrr! Swarthy human!” Steele dives under, just as the cannon fires again, the shell plunging into the water and swishing just past Big Al. It connects with the rushing robotic octopus… WHAAA-BOOM!! The explosion displaces a huge amount of water, and it sends Big Al flying into the air like a projectile. The water rains across the exhibit, as Big Al is deposited on one of the lower sails, desperately scrabbling at the material. The old cloth gives way, and Steele rips through the sail, speeding down towards the deck, a multitude of rusty sword wielding animatronic pirates gathering beneath his falling form. Fortunately, the sail is reinforced on the edges, and Steele’s momentum swings him back, the cloth acting like a swing, propelling Big Al over the angry robots and onto an upper deck. Steele runs a hand through his wet hair and gives his toga a once over. Filthy. Groaning, he gets into a fighting stance as the first wave of cursing pirate bots swarm up the ladders to his deck. Flexing his muscles, he waits until the very last moment, stepping into a sword swing, inside its deadly path, and quickly popping the head off the pirate with a quick twist and pull. The robot staggers without its head unit, until Steele whips out a leg into a powerful kick, sending the body flying back into the group climbing up the ladder, making them all fall backwards to the original deck. Big Al allows himself a smile… as he gets nailed in the back of the head with a club! The world tilts and Steele finds himself looking up at the cavern ceiling, an angry pirate staring down at him, a cudgel held high to strike. Big Al shakes the cobwebs and roars in anger, sweeping out the robots legs, leaping to his feet and smashing a fist THROUGH the body of the and cracking the wood of the deck. Rising up, he nonchalantly kicks the body over eth side, but then looks down over the side of the port. Pirates are swarming up the side, climbing the outside of the ship to get to his position. This, combined with the pirates still rushing from the lower deck, causes Steele some concern. He grabs a nearby mob and spins around, wildly lashing out with the handle, knocking quite a few of the pirates back and off the ship, splashing down into the waters below. But they just keep coming, and soon, they have Big Al completely surrounded, the press of the robotic bodies making Big Alan Steele do everything he can to keep from getting stuck with a rusty scimitar. S&M: “YOINKS AND AWAY!!!” The Sado-Masochist has climbed up on the netting to the crow’s nest. In his hands is rope ties to the top of the mast. Shouting out in fear, he leaps off, the rope pulling taut and swinging him across the ships deck. If robot pirates could look surprised, they would. Pirate: “Arrr?” WHAM!! S&M slams into the press of pirate bots, the force of his swing knocking many off like they were pins in a bowling alley, the air filled with pirate parts and bodies. Steele grins and gives S&M a thumbs-up, before swinging his meaty fists around and sending the last half-dozen robot spinning off the ship, devoid of headpieces. The water sizzles and roils from the electric energy of the bots, a few exploding of their own accord. S&M smiles wanly as he staggers to his feet, clutching at his side. S&M: “So, what kind of booty do you think robot pirates would have?” BAS: “I surely doubt that… BY THE GODS!! You have been injured!” S&M: “What, this?” The Sado-Masochist looks down at the large dagger sticking in his side. S&M: (shrugs) “No biggee. Just leave it in there, Al. If you take it out, I’ll bleed all over the place. It’s like a cork right now. It’s more inconvenient than anything.” BAS: (aghast) “Praise be to Zeus that you haven’t been confined to the other-worldly spaces of the Elysian Fields!” S&M: “Eh, I’ve had worse.” Big Al shakes his head in disbelief before motioning to S&M to move on, down the plank and off the ship. Small explosions pop in the background from the lake as the two exit the Cove into the sunlight of the park. A skull situated at the entrance of the cove, turns and follows our heroes as they walk by, a tiny camera sticking out of its hollowed-out eye socket. The image is projected onto the huge conglomerate of screens in the swirling darkness. The overhead voice can’t seem to contain its excitement. Voice: “Yes!! He’s the one! Such strength! Such power! I shall certainly enjoy using him as my new body! Set all traps to direct him here… with one final test!” =BAS= We rejoin our heroes as they almost literally collapse on a nearby bench, sucking wind. S&M: “I need a drink.” BAS: “I, too, could use a frost cold beverage to soothe my pangs of thirst.” S&M: “The shops are open. Let’s just… get us one.” BAS: (unbelieving) “But… wouldn’t that be stealing?” S&M: “It’s not stealing if you need it. And we need it. Besides, if they had wanted us to NOT have the stuff in the stands, they would have had people here. Right?” BAS: “I guess…” S&M: “Come on then. Let’s get us a few snacks and drinks from there.” Sado-Masochist points across the gaily decorated street to a nearby eatery with a German theme, a small yellow-haired boy in lederhosen depicted eating a sausage, or something.
