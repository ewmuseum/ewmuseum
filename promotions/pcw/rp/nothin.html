Reject the world Destroy the image Rebuild the self One matters not if only to ones self,The benefit of the one outeights that of the many,Biased is the view of the people, for they know nothing,Locked in fate they are, to perils which forever sentence themTo the death from which they so eagerly claim escape,He laughs as they pray; Mortal he is, and thus he fades now, as does their hope... Into the distance Into the darkness Into the night. | Spoken from the lips of a prisoned poet, living each day without hope, knowing he will never see behind these walls. I sat across from him in a dark corner where the moonlight glowed in from a hole in the wall, glistening off the metal bars cealing us in. Dirty beds, not to be slept in, crumbling toilet, not to be used. A place fit perfectly for rats, as they took the dirty beds and the crumbling toilet for they were to know use to us. Feeling as if I'd never get out, yeah I shot a man, but we was a Crip, he dimed out my boy, he was nothing. What more was another body count of an opposed gang? Family matters to the extreme reflecting incest, drugs, beatings, republicians saying freedom but not giving. Corruption is this world, I was just another pawn helping it along, so did I even really do anything bad? I thought not, but then again I don't think a lot of the things I do when the pendulum swings low is guilty of wrong doing. | 'So what are you in here for?' As smooth as an old poetic could say, 'Dealing? Killing?' It was that obvious, 'Killin'.' 'Stern, proud, no remorse... It takes a strong man to committ such an unsavory act of the sorts.' Said like he was the wises of all old men, I didn't have time to be catching up on wisedom. 'Do you have anotha poem to write 'n shit?' Shunning him away. But he was an assassin, in other words, he used words, combined them with feeling and expression which could shatter you inside, 'I understand what you're going though; the whole I'ma Blood, your an enemy if not thing. Uneducation to look good, slurred unpronounced words to fit with the style. I know your type all to well.' 'Behind college education, mother long dead, style chosen.' Correcting him and putting him back in his place, 'And you thought you knew me... right?' Putting an end to his 'peak-a-boo, I see you' game. | That was not away I was going out snapping out my delusional faze to watch Blood fend off the cops. Hell breaking loose, dirt fogging the air, bullet piercing wounded skin, while the full moon and twinkly stars above watch on. My work was done. The bottom was slain, the war begun... fucked up. Bullets wized by my head putting another end to my second delunsional faze, and with that, I pulled out my gat; shooting while running to my awaiting Mercades Bens dipped shinny black. Jumping in, quickly I started the ignition and took off from the scene. | 'Why come no one told me Big Momma is still alive?! I attending SIX funerals feeling straight embarrassed when the preacher called out someone elses name!' Sheeca arriving back home to greet Buuday about the Big Momma thing. 'Hold on one sec' Sheeca, I'm talkin' to Big Momma.' - 'So Big Momma wutz up?' - - - 'Fo' real?! HAHAHAHAHAHAHAHA, no, no, no, she didn't! ... HAHAHAHAHAHAHAHA' - - - *click* - - - *dail tone* - - - 'Hello? ... Big Momma?' Buuday started ending her laughter before looking up and seeing Sheeca twirling the phone cord in her hands. 'Why da fuck did you do dat fo' bitch?!' 'Did you here what the fuck I said? I said I went to SIX, count them; one, two, three, four, five, SIX,' Gesturing with her hands, 'funerals for Big Momma! I come back and yo ass is talking on the phone! What the fuck?!' 'You embarrassed?! I basically went to a funeral and poured my heart out over some old lady! I ran out the churrch becuz I wuz so fuckin' embarrassed! So you can't get mad a my ass.' 'Wasn't you the one talking about how you were going to her funeral this morning?! Lil' Thug told me that.' Sheeca seriously trying to figure out what the hell is going on. 'And where the fuck is Lil' Thug?' Odd moment when everyone gets silent because something just then comes on the news: (It is being reported that a gang related war has broken out over at the abandoned factories. I just got in that apparently the Blood gang where killing Crips when the cops stormed the place. We can't get our reporters there because of the all out war. We will inform you when we get some more news on this matter) 'Lil' Thug.'
