Moving Days Part 2

23 January 2003

Date: 1-23-03 The ride to the apartment was filled with chatter. The excitment of moving back to Massachuetts. The joy of getting temporary custody of Evan. The wins in PCW. Everyone was talking and having fun. All expect Chris Storm that is. He simples kept his hands on the steering wheel, looking out the window. He was deep in thought at the moment. Clear by the spaced out look in his eyes. The others didn't seem to notice his lack of conversation however and Chris was left to think to himself. His mind drifted around to different things. However they all revolved around one simple thing. Jackson Rust. A cocky smirk crosses Chris' face as he thinks about Jackson. 'I will show Jackson on Fusion. He'll learn not to mess with me or Tessa. I'll show him just what happens to those that do.' he thinks to himself. He lets out a little laugh to himself. The others don't seem to notice it. 'She is my Tessa Bear. I'm going to protect her from the disgusting perverts. They are all alike. They see my Tessa and they only think of one thing. Her as a sexual object. But I'll teach them better. Even if it means getting physical with them.' he tells himself. The smirk is gone now. A more serious expression dawns on his face. They make it to the apartment. They jumps out of the fan. Tessa and the kids hurry up to the apartment. The cool New York air whipping against their face. Chris uploads a stack of plain brown boxes from the back of the mini van. They make it up to the apartment room. Chris drops the boxes onto the ground in the kitchen and grabs two from the stack. Chris turns to Tessa. 'I'm going to start packing up my room. Can you help Alex and Christa pack up their things?' he ask her. Tessa simply nods and Chris kisses her forehead. Chris takes his boxes and goes to his room. He just throws them on the bed and walks over to his closet. Chris lets out a sigh. 'I've lived here for as long as I can remember. Now I have to move. Just pack my things up and leave like it was nothing.' He lets out a sigh. The sound of boxes hitting the ground causes Chris to look out of the door. He can see across the living room to the kid's room. Tessa is starting to help them pack. Chris starts to get a little depressed watching them pack everything so happily. They get to move back into the place they call their home. Does that mean this place with him wasn't a home to them? Chris shakes his head as the thoughts dig deep into his head. He closes the door, unable to stand looking at them joyfully packing their things. Looking into the closet Chris sees the black box. The box that contained his work. In Chris' mind there was nothing wrong with it. It was merely a tool used for helping him to remember the past. A souverign of the life he left. So why did it bother Tessa so much? Maybe it was the fact that it was full of videos and pictures of Chris having sex with other women. But it's not like any of them meant anything to him. They where just women paid to have sex with him for the public view. It was also a way for Chris to support himself when he couldn't live life paycheck to paycheck as a wrestler. Yet still, everytime Tessa even thought about it she would get deeply depressed. He let out a sigh. Chris picked up the black box and walks over to the window. Opening it up he sees the black book on the top. The box was still messed up from when Tessa had found it and went looking through it. He could still remember the painful look on her face. The agruement they had over it. Chris hated it when they fought. The shouting ringed in his ears still as he remembered it. Chris shook his head trying to forget it. He looked back down at the box, moving the book out of the way. The different movies he stared in and such littered the bottom on it. Chris started to sweat. He looks around and opens the window. The cool breeze relaxes him. Chris lets out a sigh of relief. He looks back down at the black box. He sees a cover of the Playgirl magazine he was in. Chris lets out a little laugh. He picks it up and starts to flip through the pages. He comes across his layout. The laughter quickly stops. He sees a picture of him having anal sex with a women. He doesn't even know the name of the women. Yet here he is having sex with her. It became clear to him why it hurt Tessa to see this stuff. She didn't want to remove his past or protect the children from finding it. It was simply painful to see him having sex with another women. Just how Chris hated to see Tessa hugging Jackson, she hated seeing him doing much worse with another women. Chris felt the ping of pain as he remembers just how much it hurts seeing Tessa showing Jackson affection. It must be even worst for her. Chris' fist clinch up. He grabs the box and with one quick motion., he throws the whole thing outside the window. The contents of the box shatter in the air as they make their descent to the concrete ground. Chris slams his window shut and leans against his. He lets out a sigh of relief. Surprisingly, letting go a apart of his past wasn't that hard. A smile crosses his face. Chris pushes off the window and walks back to his closet. He starts grabbing things and throws them into the box. It had finally dawned on him. He wasn't leaving his home. He was entering a new one.
