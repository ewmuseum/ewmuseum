Death to Cheffy: Part 2

16 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/16/02 The camera fades in… A still image of the Chef’s previous promo is shown. The Chef has just accepted his post as the host of a new daytime kid’s show. Chef Promo Narrator: Welcome back to another horrendously annoying Chef promo! The last time we left the Chef, he was signing on to participate in his own kid’s show! Everything’s wrapped up in a nice little package, right? WRONG!!! Danger looms on the horizon as Michael Jackson AKA “Jingle Jackson” is planning to oust the Chef in some way or form! Will the Chef survive Jackson’s deadly intentions!?!? WATCH!!! The still image begins to play. Richards: Chef, you’ve made the best decision of your life! You will not regret this! The Chef: I can’t wait to sing, dance, and cook for those little bastards…er…American swine dogs…er…children! Richards nods approvingly. Richards: Let me show you around the studio, we’ll take you over to props and costumes and get you fitted for your new uniform! The Chef: (Feeling woozy) All this attention…all these gifts…too much to take…at one time…going to…go…night night…now… The Chef instantly faints, keeling over face first onto the ground. Richards smiles and laughs heartily/ Richard: Great acting Alouette! I almost thought you did…(Realizes the Chef really did faint) faint…SECRETARY!!! As the secretary runs into the room with a cup of water to revive the Chef, the camera peers over to the window overlooking Time Square. The camera then zooms through the city at a fantastic speed towards the Penitentiary; the camera then goes through the front door of the jailhouse and through the various rows of cells until it stops at one cell. A figure, cloaked in black from the lack of light, watches at small black and white television. The television shows footage of the Kid Net/Chef deal with images of the Chef shaking hands with Richards, and fainting yet again after receiving his first weeks pay through a check. Bertha luckily has a wheelbarrow set up behind him, which catches him as he falls backwards and she rolls him back out of the sight of the camera. Figure: Grrr… The Chef will get his just desserts when I get out of this hellhole… Guard: Dinner’s Here! A security guard walks by, handing the inmate his dinner for the evening, which the dark figure snatches greedily from his hand. Figure: OOOOH, steamed broccoli!!! HE HE HE!!! DUN! DUN! DUN! The camera fades out to black Narrator: And now we continue with the series… The camera fades in again… “Cooking with Pierre” by Buckwheat Zydeco plays as a group of crazed children (probably set off with a few Pixie Sticks) storm the set and begin to dance and spin around to wear out that excess energy. My friend Pierre came into town,All he wanted was to take a look around,But he kicked off his shoes, and rolled up his pants,Got down in the mud, and learned how to dance… And we were cooooooooooooooooooooooooooookin!!! Kids: (Responding) COOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOKIN!!! The set is off course a kitchen, but not just any kitchen. The usually black and white tiled floors and wooden cupboards and counters are there, but lots of new and interesting appliances and decorations are there as well. The refrigerator door is actually a foam puppet, whose eyes begin to roll around and his Muppet-like mouth begins to move, singing the theme song with the passion only a Muppet refrigerator could muster. Little marionette knives, forks, spoons, and Senor Spork, a giant hand puppet with a mustache and a sombrero, dance around on the table. The volume of the song begins to get lower and lower, and soon all you can here is the wretched howling of the little punks dancing around the set. Suddenly, the children all shout with glee as the Chef, dressed in his usually chef attire runs out onto the stage. He high-fives the refrigerator, and then all the utensils. He picks up a lucky boy from the group of kids, and gives him a piggyback ride as the theme song begins to play loudly again and all the kids dance around gleefully. Well we fed all the chickens, and milked all the goats,And then he went fishin’, fishin’ on the boat,And he knew just what to do, didn’t take no chance,Grew up on a farm, over in France! Cooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooookin!!! The song then ends abruptly and all the kids fall down onto the tiled floor. The Chef: HEYYYYYYYY KIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIDS!!!! What’s the matter!?!? The Chef sets the young man down off his shoulders. One young girl replies to his question. Girl: Well Mr. Chef, sir, we are just so tired! All the other kids nod their heads in approval. The Chef: Awwww, well no one succumbs to dehydration, dysentery, or exhaustion in my kitchen! Boy: Why’s that? The Chef: Because we’ve got the FRIDGINATOR!!! The camera then peers over to the Muppet refrigerator, who is now sporting sunglasses and an XXX Large Leather Jacket. Fridginator: (In the best Arnold impression he can muster) I’ve got snacks!!! The Chef: You hear that kids!?!? SNACKS!!! The kids jump up and scream with joy. The Chef: Well don’t just stand there, run over there and get your Kid Net Chef brand Snacks!!! Kid #1: I’m already full… Kid #2: My mom’s making chilidogs tonight, I don’t want to overeat… Kid#3: I fart uncontrollably when I run… Kid#4: I’m sleepy… The Chef: Um, kids… Kid#5: I’m really more of a nighttime snacker… Kid#6: I’ll call my mom on my cell and have her get it for me… Senor Sporko: Ay carumba, no es bueno… The Chef: You said it Sporko… kids today are so lazy, they don’t want to even bother to get and up and walk over to the fridge to get a snack! Kid#2: Well your no Mister Universe either Chef… The Chef: Yeah, well at least I’m not a Romanian midget with homosexual tendencies, a library of kiddy porn, and a chronic case of irritable bowels! Little Girl: (Slapping the Romanian midget in the face) You told me you were 8 years old! Midget: Come on baby, what I lack in size, I make up in age!!! I’m like a fine wine, a pickled prune, or a moldy cheese… The Chef: Those last two analogies were… Midget: Shut up!!! CUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUTTTTTTTTT!!! The Director of the show, Allen Parsnip runs onto the set. Director: Why can’t we get through even one scene!?!? The Chef: The kids are lazy… Midget: I need some tail, and I’m prairie dogging… All of the kids: EEEEEEEEWWWWWWWWWW!!! The Chef: And that little wretch is tormenting and corrupting all our little girls!!! Director: (Sighs) This is the last time I’ll mix midgets with legitimate children… Senor Sporko: Estoy triste! The Fridginator: I’ll be back… Director: No, don’t leave! Kid#4: I’ve got to poop! Midget: (Grabs the kid by the hand) Come with me, I’ll show you a Port-A-Pot that’s like the Taj Mahal!!! Director: Come on people, we are paying you by the hour, now let’s get this thing over with so we can all go home, drink some heavy liquor, and act depressed! The Chef: Everyone’s leaving… Director: Dammit! This is the thirteenth kid’s show I’ve directed that there’s been a hiatus… The Chef: Well I know a few things about hiatuses since I’ve been in a few myself, heh…I even faked retirement!!! Director: Nice… Suddenly the sound of bells jingling comes closer and closer. The Director looks around. Director: Did you hear anything? The Chef: (Singing to himself) We’re tiny, we’re toony, we’re all a little loony…huh? Director: I thought I hear the sound of bells…jingling… The Chef: Jingling bells eh…hmmm reminds me of a song… Director: Yes, a song about jingling bells… The Chef: What could that song possibly be called? Driector: … The Chef: … “IT’S JINGLE BELLS YOU MORONS!!! HE HE HE!!!” Director: What was that!?!? “Um… nobody… HE HE HE!!!” The Chef and the director both shrug and walk off the set, probably going to round up the rest of the crew and the kids to finish shooting. “Soon this will be all mine again…all mi-uh-AAAAHHHHHHHHH!!!” Suddenly down from the ceiling falls Michael Jackson! He lads on the tiled floor with a sickening thud! Michael: Ouch, that feels worse than a rejection from Elizabeth Taylor, HE HE HE!!! He grabs his crotch and moonwalks. Jackson then pops his face (Being that it’s mostly plastic) back into the correct position and tip toes his way off the set as the camera mercifully fades to black… Narrator: Will Michael Jackson foil the plans of the Chef and get his kid show back!?!? Will the cast of the Chef’s show reunite and successfully sweep the Daytime Emmys? Is it ok to drug and have sex with a secretary from your office building at the company Christmas party if you don’t know her name!?!? I’ll find out in court…I mean WE’LL find out same Chef time, same Chef channel!!! NANANANANANANANANANANANANANANANANA…THE CHEF!!! The camera totally fades to black.
