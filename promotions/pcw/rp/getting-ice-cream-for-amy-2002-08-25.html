Getting ice cream for Amy

25 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8-25-02 Brandi sits in the hard wooden chair, more so playing with her small cup of ice cream than eating it. Amy sits across from her at the small table, huge smile on her face. In front of her sits a large cup of ice cream with hot fudge, whipped cream, nuts, and yes, a cherry on top. Amy had been both surprised and glad when Brandi told her to pick out anything she wanted. Now, as she sits there struggling to finish the remainder of her treat, Brandi smiles. She remembers the day before very vividly. The pain she felt when she had picked the small child up from Shady Acres. When Brandi had arrived at Shady Acres, Amy had only one small bag. Inside, she had one change of clothes and a small picture book. In her arms she held the teddy bear that Brandi had given her. Brandi had been unable to look at the tattered bag without feeling a sharp pain shoot through her body. She had felt so bad for the child. She was so helpless. So innocent. She hadn’t needed to think twice before driving to the mall. She just looked at Amy and smiled. The girl was peering out the window in awe. It was clear that she had never seen the mall before, let alone shopped in one. Most of her clothes had probably been hand-me-downs. The pigtails holding the six year old girls light brown hair had bounced up and down as she happily ran down the aisles of the store. Brandi craned her neck, trying to watch the small child as she darted throughout the racks of clothes. Her arms were weighed down with large bags from some many of the stores in the mall. Brandi hadn’t bothered with the price tags on the items Amy picked out. They didn’t matter to her. If they made they child smile, they were worth getting. If they made her forget, even for one minute, about her past life, they were worth getting. She had been through so much, and all she asked for was so little. In fact, Brandi had practically had to beg Amy to pick out some clothes and a few toys. Of course, once she got the hang of it, it was hard for her to stop. Brandi giggles. Yeah, she may be young, but she sure is a female. The selflessness of the young child had surprised Brandi. Not only had she not wanted Brandi to spend money on her, she had tried to pick out a few things that she wanted Brandi to buy for herself instead. How someone could neglect, just simply ignore, this wonderful little girl was beyond Brandi’s comprehension. As Brandi ponders this thought, Amy’s small hand reaches across the table and flings a small piece of whipped cream in Brandi’s direction. It surprises her by hitting her right on the nose. Amy slides back against her chair, almost afraid of what Brandi’s reaction will be. Of course, Brandi just laughs and soon Amy joins in. Brandi- Is that sundae good? Amy grins and nods, digging her spoon deep into the mounds of ice cream. Brandi giggles and wipes the whipped cream from her nose. Brandi- So, are you all ready to go on the tour? Amy nods, never taking her eyes off her ice cream. This forces Brandi to laugh again. Brandi- It’s not going to run away from you or anything. Amy looks up quickly. She doesn’t quite believe that the ice cream won’t just walk away. Brandi- Ok, if it does just get up and leave, I’ll buy you a new one. How does that sound? Amy nods vigorously. I’m glad that Ms. Davis suggested taking Amy on tour with me. I think it’ll be great for the both of us. I mean, I won’t have to concentrate on wrestling all the time and Amy will be able to get away from everything. Amy pushes her sundae towards the middle of the table and rests her head on the table. It’s quite obvious that she’s full, yet she doesn’t want to leave the sundae uneaten. Amy- I can’t eat it all. Brandi- That’s alright. If you don’t want it, we can just throw it away on the way out. Just then, a family walks past the table. One little boy carries an ice cream cone with icing to make it look like a clown. Amy jumps up when she sees this and looks at Brandi. She laughs, knowing what Amy wants and reaches into her purse. Amy runs off, money in hand, to buy one of the clown shaped ice cream cones. Brandi watches the family leave the ice cream parlor and head for their Yukon. The two children, one boy and one girl, half skip half run, trying to beat their sibling. Brandi watches with a grin. They’re so happy. Look at them. Not a care in the world. They’re just happy that they have each other. Sigh. They have no idea how lucky they really are Brandi turns her attention back to Amy, who is heading back towards the table with her ice cream cone. She looks at Brandi’s bowl, which is still full. Amy- You don’t like your ice cream? Brandi looks confused, but she follows Amy gaze towards her cup. Brandi- Nah, I’m just full. This answer seems to appease Amy, who starts in on the cone. Brandi looks back out the window, but by now the family has driven away. I wonder what that would be like. Not having anything to worry about. Just being able to enjoy who you are, and who you’re with. Hmm… Amy- What are you thinking about? Brandi- Oh, nothing much. Just life. Amy- What about it? Brandi- Well…I was thinking about how it can be so good so easily for some, and how others have to work and be patient for their life to turn out like that. Amy looks confused. She sits back, her clown forgotten, and tries to figure out what Brandi said. Brandi shakes her head and laughs. Brandi- Nah, it’s nothing you have to worry about. Just enjoy your ice cream. After we leave here we have a plane to catch. Sound good? Amy tries to respond, but she has a mouth full of ice cream, so she just nods. Brandi- Good. After Amy finishes her ice cream, the two stand up to leave. Amy- So, where are we going? Brandi stops and looks surprised. Brandi- Well, we have to head for Turmoil. That is, if you still want to come with me. Amy- Of course! Brandi- I’ll race ya to the car. Without waiting for an answer, Brandi jogs towards her car. Amy races to catch up, easily passing Brandi as she slows down to let her win. Amy- You’re sloooow, Brandi. Brandi just nods and laughs. She goes around to the passenger side door and unlocks the car. She’s about to get in when she feels something tug at her shirt, so she looks down to find Amy standing there. She motions for Brandi to kneel down, so she does. Amy throws her arms around Brandi’s neck, and whispers in her ear. Amy- I love you. Brandi hugs the girl back, and once again finds herself fighting tears at her sweetness.
