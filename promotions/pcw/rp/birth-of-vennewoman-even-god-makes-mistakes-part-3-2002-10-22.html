Birth of VENNEWOMAN!!! Even God Makes Mistakes, Part 3

22 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10-22-2002 The scene opens, once more, into the office of the Holy one himself, God. He smiles friendly at whatever device tapes him and begins to speak immediately… Bob: Hello, kids. When last we left, my old pal Lucifer was trying to get me to take the Crusader off of his hands. Well, I had already planned to, but I needed to get a little something in return…Hey, it’s all business up here too! Bob picks up the leather bound book and opens it. The shot zooms in on the open page and we see Bob on the phone with the Prince of darkness. This time though, the shot is a split screen. On one side is Bob, and on the other is an extremely handsome looking dark haired fellow. His voice is nothing one would expect the voice of ultimate evil to be; it is actually quite cultured sounding, with a hint of a British accent… Bob: Lucifer…My hands are tied! Why should I do this favor for you? Lucifer: You owe me, Bob. Bob: Owe YOU? ME? I seem to remember you causing quite a stir up here when you went crazy and tried to overthrow me…Me who created you…So, again…What do I owe you for? Lucifer: Hmm…Temporary insanity. You know I always looked up to you… Bob: Yep.. And from down there you forever shall… Lucifer: Haha…Quite the jokester in your old age. Remember your last party? Bob’s eyes perk up at the mention… Lucifer: Remember the booze? Remember the women? Do you think you’d find that stuff in heaven? Why, girls like that just don’t go to heaven, Bob. And I am sure all you have to drink up there of the alcoholic variety is light beer… God chuckles a bit, realizing that Lucifer has him… Bob: Hmm…Quite right you are my son…Right you are. That party was…Interesting. I shall take him away…But you must provide more of your excellent…Entertainment…Next time that require it… Lucifer: Why, I would deny you nothing. Just get him out of here… Bob: Give me a couple of minutes…. The shot fades to darkness as the two hang up their respective phones and switches back to Venneman Pendragon, still clad in black, still beneath the spotlight in hell. He starts as the voice booms out once more… YO HAVE YOUR WISH. MY FATHER HAS AGREED TO TAKE YOU FROM THIS ETERNITY OF DARKNESS. YOU JUST WOULD NEVER HAVE FIT IN DOWN HERE, AND I CANNOT HAVE A MERE HUMAN SOUL TAKING DOWN ALL OF MY DEMONS AND THEIR PETS. FROM THIS DAY, I BANISH YOU FROM HELL. Pendragon nods and then his body begins to become transparent. He gasps at his fading hand… Venneman: What sorcery is this? He finally fades and the shot switches to show a long white room, with a white desk topped off by a white computer manned by a slight man in white. Pendragon materializes in front of said desk, only this time he is clad in white armor, and Excalibur has become pure white steel. He starts once more as he realizes he is whole, and stares at the man behind the desk… Man: Greetings, Crusader…I am Peter. I will be your guide to Heaven. Venneman: Peter…Thou knowest I belong here not. I must return to my earthly realm. There is far too much for me to do! Peter shakes his head in sympathy… Peter: I am afraid that is not possible. Once you are dead, you are dead. Now, if you would, please follow me. Pendragon tries to speak again but is cut off by a curt gesture from Peter. He obeys silently and follows Peter through a door and into a beautiful sunlit field of white. Here and there, angels spread their wings, while others walk along, looks of pure bliss upon their faces… Venneman: So where are the battles fought? Peter: Oh Venneman! You are so humorous! There are no battles here in Heaven…Peace and love rule the cosmos here. Pendragon shakes his head in disbelief as Peter shows him to a castle shaped house… Peter: This is yours. I have some work to do, but I shall return to continue the tour shortly. Peter leaves Pendragon standing by his new home, but Pendragon does not enter. Instead, he walks along the path, looking at the souls who abide therein. Those he pass give him nothing but smiles, until he is stopped by a fair haired young man of an angel… Angel: That is quite a weapon, but you need it not here…I am Xavier, and I am at your service. The man attempts to remove the sword but Pendragon catches his hand… Venneman: And I am the Lord King Pendragon…Thy hand shall never come to rest upon my weapon. I swing not that way… The man tries again, but this time, Pendragon strikes the fellow down. The man screams murder and Pendragon hops on a passing cloud. He kicks two angels from it and steals one of their harps. Venneman: How can thou survive here? I have to get home…I shall FORCE them to send me home… Pendragon strums the harp and begins what sounds like a drunken melody…. Venneman: LORD PENDRAGON, FAIR AND TRUE!!! LORD PENDRAGON, BETTER THAN YOU!!! With that, the shot switches back to Peter’s office. A loud commotion comes from outside, and Peter stands up, visibly irritated… Peter: What is all this noise, that disturbs me now? He walks out the door and is met with a horrorful sight…Pendragon on his cloud, strumming away on his harp and bellowing nonsensical choruses, while all around him angels fight with one another just to get close to him. Pendragon stops singing when he sees Peter, but he floats the cloud his way. He leaps from it and grabs Peter by the color, lifting him off of the ground so he stands at eye level… Venneman: Thy will send me back, yes? Peter, cornered says… Peter: I’ll see what I can do…Perhaps reincarnation is possible for you. Venneman: Great. Same time, same place as I left would be perfect… He lowers Peter to the ground and follows when motioned to. The walk past fighting and singing angels until they reach a huge white building marked “RESEARCH AND DEVELOPMENT”. Peter: If anyone can send you back, it is these guys… Venneman: Let’s do it. As they enter the building, the shot backs away from the book and God closes it, smiling… Bob: Ahh yes…Research. Where would we be without it? Pendragon brought some much needed life to heaven, that is for certain. And, I must admit, it was fun to see my underlings afraid of something besides me. I gotta admit…I wasn’t quite finished…You see, the reincarnation thing did not go quite as planned; at least, it did not go how Venneman planned… God snaps his fingers and the shot switches to a hospital room. A woman lies with her feet in the birthing stirrups and a doctor bends between her legs… Doc: Here it comes….Here it comes! The nurses let out a gasp as the woman let’s out a shriek… Doc: Good Christ…I have never seen anything like this! RUN FOR IT!!! They run and from between the legs emerges a fully grown woman wearing a golden suit of armor, though more feminine than it had been. She is quite curvaceous and considerably beautiful. The mother passes out when she sees and Pendragon looks around the room, bewildered. She/he saunters over and catches a glimpse of him/herself in the mirror and lets out a wail… Vennewoman: GOOD CHRIST!!! WHAT DID THEY DO TO ME???!!! I AM A WENCH! The scene cuts out with the new and improved Vennewoman staring at his overdeveloped breasts…. >>>TO BE CONTINUED
