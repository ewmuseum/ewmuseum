3 Taxi Trips...The Crazy, The 70's & The French...

27 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 27/02/02 So Xavier still can’t beat Chris Chase. With the march Madness Battle Royal only 5 days away a win against the Messiah and the Cruiserweight Title over his shoulder would have given wrestling’s only Scientologist a huge confidence booster as he walks down the aisle as #15 to fight for the PCW Heavyweight Champion. But alas, it was not to happen. A double-team Superkick by Chris Chase’s New Breed Stablemates Snow and Corey Williams was enough for Chase to get the 3 count and hold onto the belt he won at Shanghaied from the Scientologist himself. So now, as Rock is Dead plays on Sunday night, Kannon will walk down the aisle with nothing draped over his shoulder and nothing strapped around his waist, he won’t even have to watch the undercard to find out who he will face next. All that he has over his shoulder for now are his bags as he makes his way with Eleanor to Kansas City, host to PCW’s Friday Fusion. Will he make his presence known when Chase and Corey Williams face Afwa Kilimanjaro, or will he now finally let it go and move on to new pastures...But the 1st question has to be how will he get to Kansas, after his train was diverted he is left 20 miles away from his hotel, and having a bit of spare cash floating around, as most Scientologists tend to do, he has decided to get a Taxi. We open up on the fist couple of Scientology standing in front of a set of Black iron gates set in a pale Grey wall. Xavier is wearing his usual full length Brown leather coat that blows in the Spring breeze. Under it he has a Black hoodie with a Green burning “X” emblazoned on the front and the hood draping outside, over his collar. With it he wears a pair of Black boot cut jeans with Green stitching and Purple embroidery down the left leg to wear a pair of Black, Purple and Grey Skechers adorn his feet. The Scientologist’s long, Auburn hair is neatly and tightly tied back behind his head as not to get caught up in his hood. “Don’t worry,” he assures Ellie~!, “I’m sure a Taxi will come along any time soon, that always do...” he then adds, looking down at the Green circle on the sidewalk that surrounds them, “I think that means that we are next or something....Why else is there a glowing circle on the floor around us?” Next to him, with her hands on her hips and a bored look on her face is his valet Ellie, wearing a long Brown suede coat with lighter Brown faux fir collar and cuffs. Under it she has a skinny fit dark Blue t-shirt with ‘Throwing Fire At The Sun” written in Pink letters across her 32B chest. Below the waist she wears a rainbow striped fabric belt and a pair of Blue sandblasted flared jeans and a pair of Red suede Adidas sneakers. Her long, wavy Brown hair with subtle Blond highlights in un-tied, also blowing in the wind and getting caught up in her collar. “I hope so...” she answers back in her usual whiny voice, “We have been waiting here nearly 2 minutes and hello? Trees, road, walls....Not exactly great scenery here is there?” As she finished speaking the sound of “All I Want” by the Offspring can be heard approaching from down the road and a Yellow open top Taxi is seen speeding towards them and screeching to a halt, the sound from the stereo nearly drowning out the voice of the driver as he shouts to the couple. “HOP IN!” he bellows, waving to the backseats, “Jump over!” The driver is in his 20’s with wild Green ruffled hair, a Hawaiian shirt over a White vest and Brown canvas shorts. Xavier puts his hands on the side of the car and vaults over into a sitting position on the back seat before reaching out a hand to Ellie~! who climbs over and sits next to the Gold Patron meritorious. “Where you goin’ to?” asks the driver, leaning around to ask the couple. “Kansas City.” replies Xavier and the driver just grins and winks, holding his thumb up before flooring the accelerator even before he has turned to face the traffic ahead. He accelerates well up to above the speed limit and weaves in amongst the traffic, dodging out into the other lane and cutting onto the sidewalk to get past the slower cars ahead. In the back Xavier and Ellie~! search in vain for their seatbelt, worried at not being able to find them. “Um......Isn’t he going a little fast?” asks Ellie~! “Ah well, I suppose it beats waiting around for a bit longer....The music could do with a bit of a change though, something more po-” She holds onto the “o” sound as the Taxi leaps off the road and lands 30 feet later down the hill they just took off from, landing with a crash as Xavier and Eleanor hold onto the doors for balance. “Well,” says Xavier, wiping his brow after the relatively smooth landing, “This could be good practice for Sunday, I’m learning to hold on when being thrown from great heights! Preferably I’d like o try it at less than 75 miles an hour but I can’t complain, at least we will get to the Hotel early!” The car screeches as it pulls off an unfeasible 90 degree turn at short notice, cutting across 2 lanes into 2 more of oncoming traffic to cut through a small park, causing pedestrians to leap onto the grass as the Taxi veers towards a hill and leaps over another 2 lanes of cars and back onto the road, pulling off a tight turn the moment the wheels touch the asphalt. “It’s good to see that urban Taxi rides are taking in the city’s and town’s nature spots,” Kannon says, trying to break the awkward silence that is lurking under the booming sound of the ride’s punk soundtrack. “Perhaps it would be better is they didn’t leave tyre tracks all over them, but ah well, they are a privilege not a right!” For a few seconds the driver actually obeys the driving laws, well apart from the ones about loud music, paying attention to the road and most likely having a driver’s license, this is then broken as he ducks out of the traffic and up the ramp into a multi story car park and he builds up good speed to fly out the car park and down onto the tarmac, the under carriage throwing out a hail of sparks as it rubs upon the road on landing. “Well that one would be good practice for taking an Atomic Drop!” exclaming the Gold Patron meritorious as he holds onto the seat before him for dear life, looking across to his valet who has gone a ghostly shade of White, queasy from the journey. “You wanna get out here?” he then yells to her, hoping to be heard over the music. Across from him she just nods, holding her mouth and looking ill. Kannon taps the driver on his shoulder and, while in the middle of navigating a junction, the driver turns around, taking his eyes off the road and shouting back to the couple. “Ya?” he asks, “You want me to slow down? Too fast for ya?” “You can just pull in here.....” Xavier says, beginning to look the same colour as Eleanor beside him and the driver slams on the breaks and the car screeches to a halt, and a few seconds later, so do Xavier and Ellie~! as they lean over the front seats that blocked their flight path out of the car. “Er.....Thanks man.....” Xavier then says cautiously as he hands a note to the driver and hops out, helping Ellie~! over the side after him. The driver then switches to CD to bad Religion and again plants the throttle and a fanfare of other driver’s horns sounds and he cuts across them on the way to his next fare. Eleanor staggers, trying to stand still but dizzy from the ride. “Like......So.....Like, What-EVER!” she exclaims, not at all happy with her last journey. “Great idea Xavier, lets take a cab to get to Kansas City for Friday Fusion....In fact, what are we going to Fusion at all? It’s not like you have a match or anything! This ranks right up there with you telling Asylum not to help you on Sunday and telling them to hold back last night.....A BAD ONE I mean by that!” “Well....he looked normal enough!” says Kannon, defending his choice, “Although maybe the loud music, Green hair and damages suspension may have been a give away...But bringing up the Chas thing was low....I know I’m now 0-3 against him, I know I am still a 1 time Cruiserweight Champion but on Sunday I will grab Chase by his cheating neck and I’ll throw him over the top rope and 2 seconds later he’ll hit the floor knowing that he may have beaten me for Cruiser Gold, but I will be the one that costs him the World Title!” From around the corner a 70’s style Yellow cab appears and pulls up beside them, the driver leaning towards the open window to greet the 1st couple of Scientology. “Can I give you a ride somewhere?” he asks in a strong Brooklyn accent, “I gotta stop off quickly at the garage but it’ll add 2 minutes tops and I won’t bill you for it!” Xavier and Ellie~! look at the cab then each other and raise their eyebrows, shrugging as they walk over and hop into the back of the cab. “Where are you going to?” the driver asks, looking at them through his rear view mirror.” “To Kansas City.” answers Xavier and the driver nods in acknowledgement and checks his mirrors before signalling and moving out into the streams of traffic moving in their direction. As he passes by 3 blocks the driver then pulls into a small garage which has 4 other identical cabs in, a mechanic, and a booth to handle Taxi bookings that is manned by a short, fat balding man. “Hey, where have you been?” the man in the booth shouts harshly at the driver, “If you come in late again you’ll be doing your pickups at the welfare office!”. The driver goes over to explain and points to Xavier and Ellie~! while mentioning Kansas City which seems to appease the controller somewhat. The mechanic then appears and opens up the hood to do an inspection of the engine, tightening and loosening things with he wrench before stumbling and falling into the engine, sending a jet of stream up into the air as the sound of a metal valve hitting the concrete floor is heard. The controller slaps his forehead and sighs as the mechanic staggers back out, holding his eye where the steam caught him before dropping the wrench on his foot causing him to leap about on his left foot whilst holding his right eye with both hands. He does a good job of keeping his balance until he accidentally hops onto one of his trolleys and rolls across the floor before the trolley hits a toolbox and comes to a stop causing the item to come to a halt and the mechanic to keep going right into a cupboard where the sound of crashing and items dropping is heard followed by mumbling in some East European dialect. “You think we should try and get another cab?” asks Xavier from the back seat as the 1st couple of Scientology watch what unfolds around them. “Er........Yah!” answers Ellie~! as she opens the back door and runs up the ramp onto the street followed by Xavi as the sound of the controller’s Brooklyn holler berates the staff for losing a client. On the street they try and hail another cab, walking towards the more dense pockets of humanity where they are more likely to have luck. Just as they start to walk the soft purring of a sportscar engine is heard behind them and a White suped up car pulls to a halt beside them, the driver wearing a France football shirt and gesturing for them to get in, raising an eyebrow as an invite. Guessing it can’t be any worse than the last 2, the couple get in and belt up, luckily for them as the car then speeds off along the street, a glowing behind the car suggesting it has afterburners fitted and an uneasy creaking of the spoilers desperately try to hold the car to the road. “To Kansas City please...” says Xavi, trying his hardest not to impose. The French driver just thrusts his shoulders around and turns his head, giving a broad grin as he shifts up a gear and hits the open roar before pressing down on a Red button mounted on the dash to give an extra burst of speed. “Um.....Hey.....” says Kannon, trying to get the driver’s attention as the Scientologist spots a Police speed gun team by the side of the road, “Um........Police, speed people!” The diver flips them off and mutters something in his native tongue before explaining to Xavi and Ellie~! that the Taxi is too fast to be picked up by Police radar. With a comfortable cruising speed of 160 miles per hour on the freeway, an unusually calm Ellie~! takes time to reflect. “So, like how many days until that match thing now?” she asks. “That match would be on Sunday my little Poon Puppy,” he explains, “Something tells me you don’t know the rules....” he says before he begins to explain the concept of the march madness Battle Royal as we fade on an exterior shot of the French turbo charged Taxi speeding off towards Kansas City.
