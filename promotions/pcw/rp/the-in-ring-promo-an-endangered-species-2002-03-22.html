The In Ring Promo: An Endangered Species

22 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/22/02 The camera fades in… Inside the arena the Muslim Call to Prayer is heard over the loudspeakers. Jeff Marx: Allah’s theme music! Dave Kern: No Marx, this is the Chef’s song… Jeff Marx: Well if Allah had theme music, this would be it! Duke Williams: Why are we even here, Hoss? Dave Kern: I guess this is some sort of in ring promo…? Jeff Marx: Well, it’s got the Chef, so we can expect lots of social commentary, handler jokes, cuss words, and humor by the bucketful! The Chef and Bertha Stewart enter through the backstage area and slowly walk down the aisle. The Chef is wearing his usual Islamic Militant garb and is holding Ol’ Woody, his trusty rolling pin. Bertha is wearing the usual black sweat pants and a T-Shirt with the text, “I Conquered the 64 oz Sirloin at Logan’s Steakhouse”. Dave Kern: They are a motley crew indeed… Duke Williams: A couple of Grade-A varmints if you ask this here cowpoke… Jeff Marx prostrates on the concrete floor chanting some sort of Muslim code. The Chef hops up to the apron and separates the ropes for Bertha. Big B then enters the ring, with the Chef following her. The Chef then calls for a microphone, and is tossed one by Clay Clayborne. Dave Kern: Don’t do it Clay! Duke Williams: It’s too late now, Hoss… The Chef: Greetings fellow gastronomers! I am the Chef, and I will be speaking to you tonight on the topic of…ALEXANDER MIYAGAWA!!! The Crowd pops wildly for the former Urban Samurai. Dave Kern: They love Miyagawa here in…(Kern is interrupted by the Chef’s reply) The Chef: Shut your flame-broiled beef infested mouths, you ignorant twits! The Crow boos the Chef’s remarks. Bertha: SHUT YOU MOTHER F*CKING TRAPS YOU D*CK @$$ PIECES OF F*CKING RAT SH*T!!! Jeff Marx: (Finishing his prostration) Praise be to Allah for Cheech and Chong rip offs!!! The Crowd is now silent. Dave Kern: What are you talking about Marx? Jeff Marx: I believe they once produced a skit called “Sister Mary Elephant”! Dave sighs. The Chef: Thank you my enchanting little piece of puff pastry. Now you culinary school dropouts wouldn’t know this, but Miyagawa and I have never fought one another in a singles match. This is very surprising because we have both been in the PCW for over a year, and have gone through many metamorphoses. Unfortunately for Miyagawa though, his changes have been for the worse… Dave Kern: How so!?!? Jeff Marx: Testify!!! Duke listens to Waylon Jennings on his Walkman. The Chef: Miyagawa was once a radical man that was proud of his heritage and an enemy of this cesspool that is America… But now he’s on your side! You scum slurping nobodies! You changed him! The Chef points his finger directly at the Crowd. Dave Kern: Let’s not play the blame game here… Jeff Marx: (Pointing at Kern) It’s you Kern! You changed Miyagawa! The Chef: If I have the time I would show each and every one of you Allah’s Grace! Bertha: F*cking true… Duke Williams: (Singing) I Recall a Gypsy Woman… The Chef: Now Miyagawa is just as much of an infidel as you! The Crowd boos incessantly. Dave Kern: This is just a load of crap! Jeff Marx: (With tears coming from his eyes) Nothing has been truer… The Chef: Allah has sent me here to exterminate those who don’t not wish to convert or comply, and with the greatest amount of reluctance, I will have to sauté his @$$… The Crowd is silent. The Chef: …In a Teriyaki marinade with water chestnuts, bok choy, baby corn, snow pea pods, and the finest cat meat this side of the Pacific!!! The Crowd begins to boo the Chef again. Bertha: Must I f*cking remind you to SHUT THE F*CK UP?!?!… Bertha continues her cussing spree. Bertha: …F*cking b*tch @$$ queer as f*ck sh*tty ass douche ho skank fairy c*nt @$$ b*tches full of sh*t, shot at and missed, sh*t at and hit, f*cking pieces of hog sh*t @$$es mother f*cking in the f*cking slut @$$ b*tch nuggeting f*ck @$$ mother f*cking @$$ arena...@$$ cunts! Dave Kern: I’m impressed…not! Jeff Marx: Praise be to Allah for Bertha’s talented tongue! Duke: (Still singing) Ride me down easy… The Chef: And after Miyagawa’s @$$ has been sautéed in the aforementioned recipe, I will proceed to sauté the @$$ of Big Alan Steele! The Chef pulls his handy dandy pocket sized Koran out of his pocket, flips through a few pages and begins to read from it. The Chef: In the name of Allah, the compassionate, the merciful… O unbelievers! I do not serve what that you serve! Nor do you serve him who I serve! Nor am I going to serve what that you serve! But you will serve what that I do serve!For if you do not serve, in you shall be hand of death! And Allah’s Grace will forever make you pay for your unbelief! The Chef closes the Koran. Dave Kern: That’s not the correct verse! The Chef is changing the Holy Muslim text and manipulating its words! Jeff Marx: You’re right, it sounds even better now! Praise Allah for revisions! The Chef: So says the book of Allah… Bertha: Nice f*cking introspection there, f*ckface… “I’m the Bullgod” by Kid Rock plays throughout the arena as a familiar Christian face enters the stage and walks down the aisle. Duke quickly removes his headphones. Duke Williams: Great horny toads! Dave Kern: It’s Billy Graham! Jeff Marx: Oh no, not a Christian! The Kid Rock tune quickly changes to “Whatcha Want?” by the Beastie Boys and Cypress Hill as the Vicar of Christ walks down the aisle and stands beside Graham! Jeff Marx: Ah! It’s Doug Troy! Dave Kern: No you idiot, that’s Pope John Paul II!!! Duke Williams: That varmint’s got some nice threads… “Whatcha Want?” changes to “Motown Philly” as a man with a familiar name walks down the aisle and joins Graham and the Pope. Dave Kern: Sweet virginal Courtney Love, that’s MOSES………..MALONE!!! Jeff Marx: Yuck, I hate the Sixers, they’re so…American… Duke Williams: I guess that varmint it representing the Jewish community since the handler can’t think of any famous Jewish leaders. The Boyz 2 Men song returns back to “I’m the Bullgod” once again as the Dalai Lama walks down the aisle, does a cartwheel, and stands by the other religious leaders. Dave Kern: This is truly amazing; all of the world’s greatest religious leaders and a retired NBA player have come out to take it to the Chef! Jeff Marx: Come on Allah, I’m waiting for some of that oh so sweet Wrath!!! The Dalai Lama pulls a microphone out of the pocket of his robe and speaks… Dalai Lama: Finally…the most electrifying Buddhist since Richard Gere has come back to…the P-C-W!!! The Crowd pops. Jeff Marx: When was he ever here to begin with? SMACK!!! Duke Williams: Shut yer yapper varmint! Dalai Lama: Chef, the Dalai Lama thinks you need to change your oh so hateful ways before he has to “Enlighten” your behind Buddha style!!! Dave Kern: Yes! Give it to him Buddha style! Jeff sighs. The Dalai Lama passes the microphone to Billy Graham. Billy Graham: This is Billy Graham, and I’ve got nothing important to say. The Crowd pops wildly for Graham. The Crowd: Bil-LY! Bil-LY! Bil-LY! Duke Williams: (Wiping a tear from his eye) That was beautiful… Graham passes the microphone to the Pope. The Pope: What does everybody want!?!? The Crowd: ABSOLUTION!!! The Pope: What does everybody need? The Crowd: INDULGENCES!!! The Pope: What does everybody love!?!?Dave Kern: Platonic male love! Jeff Marx: Allah! Duke Williams: Dave!!! Ahem, I mean…Ropin’ and Ridin’!!! YEEE HAWWHHHH!!! The Pope tosses the microphone over to Moses Malone. Moses Malone: Chef, you’ve been irking me and my peeps here for a long time, and you don’t want to irk a black man with the name MOSES! Malone points to the ceiling. The Chef and Bertha look up and see a cage full of big, green, slimy frogs above them. In a few seconds the cage opens and the hundreds of frogs come flying down onto the Chef and Bertha, engulfing them in a sea of green slime! Dave Kern: This is awesome baby!!! What do you think Dick? Dick Vitale: Meh…too predictable… Duke Williams: I haven’t seen that many frogs since my visit to the swamps of San Antonio! Jeff Marx: There are no swamps in San Antonio, and this is a TRAVESTY!!! SMACK!!! Jeff Marx: I’ll be quiet now… Moses Malone: Yeah, now that’s what I call a PLAGUE!!! Just after Malone finishes his statement, the Chef pops out of the frog pile. His head is beet red, and his grimace accentuates his already angered look. The Chef: You INFIDELS!!! You come here and try to pull a prank on me!?!?!? Well I will tell you this! Your vision of Allah is not a complete one, and when you die you will fall endlessly into a pit of fire, where an infinite amount of perils and torture awaits you!!! But…if you want that torture right now…BRING IT!!! Dave Kern: Cough!*Ripoff!*Cough! The Dalai Lama grabs the microphone. Dalai Lama: Hey, the Dalai Lama thought that he was the one parodying the Rock here! The Chef furiously answers… The Chef: Every single one of you out there, when you reach the hall of Hades, Satan, Lucifer, Beelzebub, Iblis, Hel, Asmodeus, whomever!!! You will remember who foretold this…you will remember who prophesized this…and you will never forget the man to SENT YOU THERE!!! ME!!! The Chef tries to free himself from the many frogs, but can’t quite do it. Dave Kern: The Chef is stuck in the pile of frogs! Jeff Marx: Allah, why have you forsaken me tonight!?!? The Dalai Lama turns to his comrades and asks… Dalai Lama: Hey guys, let’s say we go get tanked up, eh? The Pope: Sure… Billy Graham: I’m tanked up right now! Moses Malone: Uh guys, aren’t we supposed to be religious leaders that people turn to for guidance and stuff? The other three men look at each other, and then laugh. Moses Malone: Hehe, I guess I’m just trippin’… The four men all walk to the backstage area embracing one another. The Chef tries to free himself and Bertha from the mountain of frogs. Dave Kern: This has been a Chef promo; there will be no rebroadcast or redistribution of this promo without the expressed, written consent of the PCW. Now let’s all get tanked up!!! Duke Williams: Amen to that, Hoss… The camera fades out as Dave and Duke whistle down a beer vendor.
