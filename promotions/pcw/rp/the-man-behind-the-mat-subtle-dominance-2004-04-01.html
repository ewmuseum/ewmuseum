The Man Behind The Mat: Subtle Dominance

1 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/1/04 The little evergreens dotting the edges of the long driveway swayed slowly in the light breeze coming in off the Pacific. The driveway was nearly a quarter-mile long and led up to a great stone mansion. The home of the world acclaimed, Brian Arson. The large beach front estate was in the northern part of the town of Ventura, California. Behind Brian’s house lay nearly a fifty yards of land before the actual beach began, a good assurance for times like this when the ocean began to swell. The land was covered in luscious perfectly groomed grass, a few trees sprung up in different areas of the yard. On the sides, the land was surrounded by a large grove of trees that almost encircled the house and allowed for it to be hidden from the street. In the front, the drive formed a circle and in the center was a little patch of grass where a tall triple-tiered fountain stood, the water trickling down the sides. Brian Arson’s life had been a success so far, or at least he thought it had. He lived in a beautiful area, slept with beautiful ladies, knew the right kind of people, and had more money than he would ever need. Yes, Brian Arson was a success. He led a life that most people would only dream of having. The only question was where did Brian get all this money? Did he get it from his father? Well, yes… and no. Through college and into his early twenties Brian worked at his dad’s law firm for a little while, and his father supported pretty much everything he did so he did not have to buy anything with his own money. But he quit working for his father nearly five years ago after having a blow out with him and moving away from home. So even if his father had supported him, all the money that he had received would have been gone by now. There must be other ways Brian Arson attained his cash. Maybe he got the rest of it from pro wrestling. Of course, he had been in the business for six years now and had made quite a name for himself becoming one of the quickest rising stars in more than one federation. Yes, he had been paid hundreds of thousands of dollars over the years for wrestling, but Brian wasn’t one to make good use of his money and tended to squander it unintentionally. But even with his father’s help and the money he had accumulated from pro wrestling, it was not enough to pay for all his fancy cars and his beautiful coastal property. You might say, ‘but Brian couldn’t have had any other job while participating in the world of wrestling.’ This might be true if it were a true legitimate full-time job, he wouldn’t have had any time while traveling place to place. So that leaves us with very few options, but I think it’s becoming clearer as to what sort of operations Arson may be running and how he’s making his way successfully through life. The blue Viper pulled into the long driveway and stopped right at the top of the crest, just in front of the cement steps leading to Arson’s house. Brian stepped out and quickly headed inside, swinging open the large double doors. He went over to his phone and anxiously awaited his messages. Important business would have to be placed on hold as he his hectic life couldn’t handle it all at the moment. Tonight he’d be heading back to the east coast for PCW’s Turmoil in North Carolina where he would get ready to take on this GI Joe Mac in his third tournament match up. He hadn’t really allowed himself to stick out yet, but was now the time? Sure, he was going to beat Joe Mac, he had no doubts in that, yet at the moment he would just stand in the shadows of Jon Kano and play a supporting role in the Primetime Players. But come the beginning of the World Title tournament, the chains would be broken, the subtlety thrown out the window. Of course Arson would maintain his wild ways outside the ring, drinking and womanizing, but in the ring, it was time for Brian to focus and return to the ways that allowed him to achieve his early stardom. If no one was going to take him seriously, then he’d have to show by example. Mike Wade was the first, Joe Mac soon to be the second. Who’s next? Skylar Green? Cal Roberts? Skitzo? Or even the living legend himself, Chris Sloboda? He wasn’t calling them out, there was no need to... he would meet up with them eventually, and when the time came, the wise-cracking would be set aside, and the ass-kicking would begin... P.S.
