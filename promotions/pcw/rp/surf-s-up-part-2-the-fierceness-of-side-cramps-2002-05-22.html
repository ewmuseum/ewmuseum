Surf's Up - Part 2 - The Fierceness of Side Cramps

22 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: May 22 [The scene opens on blue and yellow running shoes. Most of the shoe is covered in blue as two flaming letters on the sides that say 'TTT' are covered in yellow. The foot moves quickly to whatever destination that it is going to, not missing a beat and surely not getting tired. The scene moves upward to reveal the tanned physique of 'Totally Tubular' Tommy, one half of PCW's most bodacious tag team, The Cowabunga Connection. Tommy only wears a pair of blue running shorts with a yellow waistline, other than that he's showing off his perfectly tanned, toned muscles to whatever bodacious babes that get the chance to see. His bright blonde hair is a little moist from sweat as it bounces from side to side with each stride that The Tubular One takes in his running. The camera passes Tommy and begins zooming further down the boardwalk of a local Los Angeles beach. About 500 feet down the boardwalk is a stocky figure that moves quite slow, but he's obviously trying to run] [The scene moves over back to Tommy as he jogs in place, barely even losing breath] 'Totally Tubular' Tommy: Come on, dude!! We've got five more gnarly miles to run. ['Heavy Metal' Harry, the other half of The Cowabunga Connection, lags way behind as his voice is heard off in the distance] 'Heavy Metal' Harry: My feet hurt, man. [As Harry gets closer you can see that he wears his Twisted Sister T-shirt, black leather pants, and black cowboy boots. He's jogging five miles in this...] TTT: Dude, your wearing cowboy boots. [You can see Harry's face grimace already knowing this fact that his best friend and tag team partner has pointed out] Harry: I know that, man. [Tommy rolls his eyes as he still jogs in place, waiting for Harry to finally catch up] TTT: You knew that we were going to be doing a most BODACIOUS workout today, you should have worn running shoes. It's totally bogus on your feet to run in cowboy boots. Harry: Shut up, man. They should make cowboy boots that you can run in. TTT: That would be radical. Harry: Yeah, man. [Harry has finally caught up to Tommy, who is still running in place. Harry is doubled over breathing heavily as he lets out a few bursts of coughing. His face is completely red with exhaustion and it looks like a waterfall is pouring down his forehead] Harry: Why the hell are we exercising, man? TTT: We have to stay in shape for wrestling, compadre! Harry: Oh yeah, that, man. TTT: Did you forget that we were professional wrestlers or something? Harry: Yeah, man. TTT: How could you forget something GNARLY like that?! [Harry shrugs as he still coughs and weezes] Harry: I smoke weed, man. [Tommy, still running in place, sort of looks up to the sky contemplating on that last thought. Harry has been using Jerome Bongus a lot lately, but is it possible to smoke that much marijuana and forget that your a professional wrestler in one of the most tubular and far out promotions of all time? It truly doesn't make any sense, but Tommy will take it because he wants to finish this run] TTT: Ok, dude. Now we have a most grueling tag team round robin tournament starting on Friday and you know that we have to totally be ready for this thing. If we are in good shape nothing will stand in our way, dude. Harry: Why do we have to run to train for wrestling, man? TTT: Because it builds gnarly stamina so that we can keep beating the bogusness out of our opponents, in this case it's the New Iconz. Harry: But why don't we just practice wrestling moves on people, man? TTT: That's what the New Iconz are for, dude. Harry: Then why do we need to train if they are the training, man? TTT: Because we have to be in shape so that we can wrestle the heinous training dudes. Harry: But in order to get in shape we wrestle the not so far out training guys, man. TTT: That will make us stronger wrestlers, but we have to make sure that we are in shape to at least do our moves with sharp speed and cunning strength, or is that cunning speed with sharp strength? I don't know, dude. [Harry still breathes wildly, but seeming to have been catching his breath, as he looks up at Tommy extremely confused. Tommy always makes things so hard to understand, as if trying to sound more intelligent than he already is. Wait...Tommy really isn't that intelligent to begin with, but there was that one time when he completed the Rubic's cube, now that was FAR OUT! All of that aside, Tommy is way into this running stuff, but Harry truly believes deep down inside himself that Tommy does it so that the women see him. It's a far out plan, but Harry can just stay at home constructing a new bong, one which is called 'The Bong That Should Not Be'] Harry: I want to go home and work on my new bong, man. TTT: NO, DUDE! We have to finish this run and then you can go home and work on the bong. Harry: No more running, man. TTT: This is the only way your going to become a most fierce and feared competitor, compadre. Harry: I'm fierce, man. TTT: How, dude? Harry: Remember when I sat on Rudolph Von Hackenmann's head, man? TTT: That wasn't fierce, that was just you being bogus!! Harry: Oh yeah, man. [Tommy slows down his pace of running as his mind wanders to fierce things that Harry has done. There was that time when Harry speared the linebacker of the high school football team into a set of lockers. Unfortunately, the dude got back up and gave Harry a most BOGUS ass kicking. It was a fierce ass kicking though] TTT: Face it, dude, you're not fierce. [Harry begins holding his side, his face changing from exhausted to a shade of pain] Harry: You know what else is fierce, man? [Tommy stops running in place, curious] TTT: What, dude? Harry: SIDE CRAMPS, MAN!! ARGH....man... TTT: Dude, I told you not to drink all of that Kool-Aid before we left. [Harry falls to the ground clutching his side as if he'd just been shot there] Harry: But...it's so good, man... [Tommy has his hands on his hips now as he cracks a smile thinking about the wonderful flavor of Grape Kool-Aid] TTT: I must admit, compadre, that Kool-Aid is most bodacious. You know what? Harry: What, man?.... TTT: Instead of running five more miles, we should just go back home and get some Kool-Aid. That sounds TOTALLY GNARLY. [Harry smiles, feeling the pain in his side subside with the thought of Grape Kool-Aid and the figures of dancing water jugs in his head] Harry: Far out, man.... TTT: We only have a ten mile jog to get back home, dude... [Harry's face shows absolute horror as the scene fades to black]
