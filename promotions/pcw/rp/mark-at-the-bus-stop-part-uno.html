Mark at the bus stop....(Part Uno)

undated

Date: I Lost Track ::Introduction:: What is a real true champion to Mark? I will tell you, someone who goes into a fed and works his ass off to be rewarded with a prize. You go into a fed and you are not expecting to be anything. But when you do become a champion then you feel that you have some power and that you will be recognized. What does the Rising Star championship mean to Mark? Well it means that you are one of the premier rising stars in the business. It means that you are going to be a wrestler in the future. It means you are going to be a top notch star sometime soon, but right now you are a champion and that is all that matters. How many times has Mark been a champion in his life? Zero, Mark has never been able to feel what it is like to be a champion. Now that he is in a tournament for the Rising Star title then Mark might be able to find out what it is like. Since he didn’t prove that he was extreme enough for the Extreme title then it seems that Mark has to go back down to the lowest title in PCW. Mark’s first round competition is Semore Smoke. ::End Introduction:: Mark is sitting on the bench on the side of the street waiting for the bus to come by so that it can take him to the museum. The hot sun of Houston, Texas is beating down on the bald head of Mark. He has his black bag with him as he is there fiddling with his fingers, rolling his thumbs around each other. There is no one else waiting with him on the bench and behind him people are flowing in and out of the stores. “I can do it.” Mark says as he is still fiddling around with his thumbs. “Focus, that is the key to winning, is to be focused.” What in the hell is this? This is not the Mark Makonie we are used to seeing, worrying about his competition. What in the hell is wrong with this guy? We have never seen Mark Makonie like this. “Why in the hell am I worried about a damn match?” Mark asks himself as he is looking around to make sure he is not disrupting anyone. “This is just another match with another wrestler. This is just the same thing I do every card. I go out there, perform my best and see what happens. I don’t worry about anyone in the business, not even Xavier Kannon. If I had the chance to face him, I would not be scared like Trigger. Now this Semore Smoke is a weed smoker from what I take it.” A bus pulls up to the bus stop and Mark gets up from the bench. He starts to walk on the gray cement towards the bus doors. The doors open and there is a lady driver who smiles and she has a couple of teeth missing in her mouth. The rest of the teeth are a corn yellow and she has a mustache starting to show. “Is this the number nine bus?” Mark asks as he is looking at the bus driver. “No this is the number seven.” The driver says as she closes the doors and starts to take off. Mark starts to head off to the bench once again with his black bag around his shoulder. He goes over to the bench and there still is no one sitting there. He places his bag down next to the bench and then he has a seat on the bench. ”Now where was I?” Mark asks himself as he puts both of his fingers on his temples and then he squeezes them together looking as if he is thinking hard. People behind him are walking by, looking at Mark and they start to go off by him. He closes his eyes, blocking out the world thinking in his world. The people keep on going by, some stopping having a worried look on their face and some that just walk by with a smile and they start to laugh. A rich guy in a plum colored suit, with his expensive dress shoes walks by. He has his hair slicked back, looking like a “Slick Rick” and he goes into his pocket and pulls out a round of bills with a rubber band around the bills. The guy then puts those back into his pocket and then he takes out some change that he had in his pocket. He throws it at Mark and he then starts to run as Mark takes his fingers away from his temples. Mark gets a real mean look on his face and he starts to look at the guy run. “Those are the fuck rags that get me pissed off.” Mark starts to say as he picks up the change. “People think that I am a bum or insane when I try to think. Those are the people who are just like the African American race. They are snobs and they flash what they have off in clothes, vehicles, and sometimes music.” Mark starts to throw the change at some vehicles in the street. He doesn’t care what happens at this point as the day has already started off bad. “Back to the white weed smoker.” Mark starts to say as he is still throwing some pennies in the road. “He shouldn’t even be in wrestling, he is showing the world that they should all smoke weed and then everything will be fine. That is worse than what I am doing because he is white, but trying to act like an African American. See most of the African American people just stay in their house most of the day, getting “high” and not seeing the world. They are taking up space in this world and shouldn’t even be here, like Semore Smoke in the wrestling world.” Mark goes over to his bag and he pulls it up to his feet. He starts to unzip the bag as some people are looking at what he is doing. People are starting to get suspicious as some are backing away from the bench that Mark is sitting on. Mark then doesn’t bother and he puts his bag back to where it was. “He was in a trial for the weed possession while coming into America.” Mark says as he watches the cars go by the street corner. “See in America we have some of the best video editors in the world. They can do what they did with Semore Smoke’s video. They can add footage, but leave the time the same and everything. So how are they really positive that it wasn’t him that didn’t smuggle that weed in.” Mark just shakes his head as another bus goes by and it stops. Mark grabs his black bag and he starts to walk on the gray cement toward the blue and black colored bus. He is heading toward the clear glass on the doors that are opening right as he gets to them. A lady is the driver and she has on some holy jeans with a flannel long sleeve shirt on. She smiles as she is missing her front tooth and the rest have a platinum covering on them. She has her ears pierced all the way down as Mark starts to walk back. “Well are you going to get on boy?” asks the lady as she winks her baby blue eyes towards Mark. “Is this the number nine bus?” Mark asks as he is almost towards the bench. “NO!” says the lady as she shuts the doors and she floors the gas real fast. The back wheels start to smoke as she goes out of the stop. Mark goes back to the bench with his black bag. He gets to the bench and he sets the black bag down next to the bench and he goes over to the white painted bench and he takes a seat. Mark looks next to him and there is an African American male sitting right next to him. Mark starts to think about all of the encounters he has had of African Americans as flashbacks start. The scene fades.
