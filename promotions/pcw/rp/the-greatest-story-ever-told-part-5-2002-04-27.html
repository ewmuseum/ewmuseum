The Greatest Story Ever Told Part 5

27 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: April 27th, 2002 *Monologue* The bell tolls for thee Big D! As your judgement day draws closer I continue to plot your demise. When we step out from the back Davey, the crowd will witness what they have prayed for since the day you entered the PCW, your last breath! You see Davey, your recent acts of aggression towards myself have pushed me over the edge. If an edge even existed in the first place. Davey, when a man is pushed and shoved long enough his aggression which builds up like soda being shook up inside a bottle with a cap on will be unleashed like a Russian rocket. Unfortunately for you Davey, you are sitting on top of the silo. The silo which will be opened. You are going to fall straight into an on coming rocket. The result will be destruction. I will like my lips and cleanse them of your blood, then I will smile. Say your prayers Davey. See your priest and testify your sins and beg for forgiveness. Why you ask? For your day of reckoning is drawing near and your soul is drawing nearer to the river of the damned! *End Of Monologue* Perhaps it was fate. The better of me believes it was simple luck. But this company would be the company that put myself on the map. Without the FSW I would never have wound up where I am now. But isn't it ironic that through this company I found my way to it's rival? Just because of it's untimely death I am now apart of this fine organization. Is it not odd that where you wind up all starts with where you begin? Working my way up the ladder in the FSW was the most difficult thing I have ever done. Over the coarse of six months and many wins and a tremendous amount of hard work, I only had one title shot. I won but nevertheless this proves how hard I had to work. I was only allowed to keep the title for a month before it was stripped from me. The irony is that the title was the Extreme title, the very title I am working just as hard to win here in the PCW. After my short reign as the Extreme champion I was pushed up the ladder even more. I soon was informed that I would be the new Intercontinental champion. This news drove me wild. My brain was spinning. This was the icing on the cake. Only a month earlier I had discovered my long lost brother. Now learning I would become the IC champion, I was ecstatic. I felt I had really earned this. Soon my world came crashing down around me. I was forced to drop my title a few weeks after winning it to a new kid. My first professional screw job and I hated it. Now I was without pride. My brother ran out of my life again hooked on the hooch. My life was going straight down the toilet. I was drowning in a sea of my own shit that I created. But soon someone through me a life jacket!
