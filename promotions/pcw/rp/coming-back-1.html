Date: Now In distress, he crumbled up the yellow sheet of paper and shot a 3 pointer right into the waste basket with a noise as it hit the other crumbled papers inside. The room was dark, quiet, and even quite eerie. No TV, furniture, just a figure of some man with a flashlight pointing down toward a piece of yellow paper. He did, however, sit in a desk like area. Other then the flashlight, the moonlight peeked through the window. It was kind of a depressing image too. A poor, young man with nothing except a dark room. He was poor. Young, and, well known strangely. He had legacy of a wrestler known as Young Tiger. Since he quit PCW, his life went downhill and he disappeared from media. His parents didn’t know where he was, and he couldn’t even reach them. He tried on his phone, but nobody had cared to answer. Then had moved away. 'God, I have to get this short story in to in the magazine…or else I’m being evicted…', he mumbled sadly. He picked up his pen and started writing. A man '…A man what?! God, why do I have to be put through this…' He wrote some more, but looks down the paper and shook his head. I just wouldn’t work. His stroked his goatee, and new grown one that wasn’t around in the wrestling biz. However, he still had long black hair. He looked different in a sense. No, he had no difference in facial arrangement or eye color, but he was different. He wasn’t happy. He looked depressed. Suddenly, the phone rang. 'Oh no. The magazine company. I’m in trouble.' He wanted to avoid it so badly. He had to. But he couldn’t. Without really knowing it, he reached his hand out and lifted the phone to his ear. 'OK, look guys, I’m sorry I couldn’t get a short story in…' 'What?', the voice said. 'Who are you?' 'Is this Eddy?' 'Yeah…' 'Wow. I never thought I’d reach you.' 'Erm…Excuse me, who are you?' 'Ah….This is Skylar Green.' The name blew up in his head like a bomb. He remembered the name and the person. The End. 'Skylar?!!! It can’t be you!', Eddy (Young Tiger), yelled, not in anger, but more in joy. 'Look, it’s been a definite while since I’ve ever talked to you. In fact, PCW’s been looking for you.’ 'What?', Eddy said strangely. 'They still care about me?' 'Yes. I’m lucky I reached you. It took me forever to find you.' Eddy was shocked. Somebody remembered him. Somebody found him. Somebody cared about him. 'Well, why is PCW looking for me? I wasn’t that good, was I?' 'Actually, you were. Since I’m an old friend, they wanted me to find you and ask you something…' 'What?' 'They want you back.' Eddy, already in enough shocked, was too shocked for words. He, a 19 year old kid without any strive for life since he quit PCW because he wanted to move on. However, he didn’t, and he’s stuck in debt. 'You have to be kidding me', Eddy said in that 'quiet-but-enthusiastic' voice. 'Yes. Seriously, I wouldn’t joke with you. I’m not like that. You know me.' Eddy coughed into the phone. He had been sick and tired for awhile, but this made his day. 'Why you coughing? You sick?', Skylar asked. 'Yeah…' 'Why don’t you buy some medicine?' 'Many reasons. I’m broke, the nearest drug store is a 5 hour walk, and I have no car…' 'Wow. Life got rough after PCW, eh?' 'You wouldn’t believe…', he thought. 'Yeah', he said out loud. 'I thought I could strive for something…' 'Well, yeah. You live in…Wyoming?' 'Yeah. In the middle of nowhere…', Eddy said. 'I donated a lot of my money to charity after PCW. Thought I’d get a good pay somewhere else. Now, I’m getting little for writing short stories for a local rinky-dink magazine…' 'Well, hey, give me your address, and I could drive out there and pick you up. I’ll pay your debt too.' A tear almost came to Eddy’s brown eyes. He was shocked and felt joyous that he had a friend in his life who could help him like this. 'Wow man…I can’t believe you’d do this for me…' 'Yeah, well, that’s was friends are for….' Eddy stood up and put the phone on the desk as he climb to his feet. He never felt so happy. He was going to be back to PCW. His memories cheered him up. He felt happy PCW was actually looking for his presence in there organization, and even felt happy a friend would help him get out of debt and out of his 1 bed trashy home. After the question from Skylar, he told him his address. In the middle of nowhere, Wyoming. He actually lived in Texas, but he moved to a nice home in Wyoming. Then got in bad debt. Got evicted. Then, here he was, living of scraps. But help was soon to come for him. 'All right Eddy. Anyway, tomorrow I’ll be there by 5:00 PM to pick you up. Pack your stuff.' 'I don’t have anything here except a waste bin, a chair and a desk. 'Hmm, well, whatever, I’ll see your there by 5:00. You’ll get to my girlfriend to.' 'What’s her name?' 'Sandy Wong.' 'Hmm. Well, anyway, talk to you soon', Eddy said. 'Bye…', said Skylar, followed by the sounds of a phone being hung up, and that annoying ring that come after that. The skinny Eddy stood up on his feet, got up and walked outside on his porch. This is the first time Eddy’s clothes have been visible. He has a gray windbreaker and pair of tattered jeans. He was shoeless too. His feet were cold. His face was cold. He was cold too. But something from the inside warmed him greater then anything outside would.
