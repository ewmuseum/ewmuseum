Monster Buck with Teresa

27 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9-27-02 RP 1 PPV RP 2 Fusion27th There he is. Ain’t he a beauty? Just look at that rack. He’s perfect. Teresa whispers these words as she sits in a tree stand. The surroundings appear to be the great outdoors. Nothing but trees and more trees company Teresa along with a small camera crew several yards away. She is dressed in dark green tree camouflage. Over her head is a matching cap with the words ‘Real Americans Hunt’ in gold letters. In her hand she holds a green bow armed with an arrow ready to be released. The cameras zoom in on a large buck following a couple of does behind a large oak tree. In order to succeed in this sport, you have to have patience. Some days you’ll sit for hours before you see one deer. Other days, it may not take as long. It depends mostly on the weather and time of day. Shortly our buck will come out from behind that oak and if we’re lucky, we can get him. Teresa also whispers these words. If she spoke too loud, she would spook the deer and all three would run deeper into the woods. She readies her bow keeping a close eye on the buck. Five minutes pass and finally the buck steps into view. Teresa pulls back the arrow aimed straight for the deer’s heart. For a moment only the sound of the wind and a bird or two is heard. The buck stops walking and looks toward the direction of the cameras. Teresa double checks her aim and releases the arrow. The buck jerks as the arrow pierces his skin and he darts back into the woods. Teresa speaks in an excited whisper. Yes! We got him. Right on target. Man, I can feel the adrenaline racing through my veins. It’s a feeling like none other. Ya know nobody ever gets used to the kill. Each time feels like the first all over again. I’m so excited, I’m shaking. Teresa holds her hand up to the camera to show it shaking slightly. The picture fades out as she climbs down the tree and returns with her following the buck’s tracks till she spots him lying on the ground about fifty yards away. Her voice now returns to normal. Her bow is hanging over her shoulder behind her. She steps behind the buck and kneels. Her hands grasp the horns and pull his head up so that the cameras can get a good shot of him. He didn’t run too far. My gosh. This buck’s prettier up close. Just look at these horns. Ten point and huge. This feller has seen his years. My heart is still pounding over this. Who says a woman can’t hunt? The segment ends with different shots of the 7 different hunters appearing on the video. Teresa’s shot was the moment right before she released her arrow. The camera had zoomed in on the arrow pulled back. Her face revealed she was concentrating on her target and in her baby blue eyes showed determination. Along with the various shot is a drum pounding beat with a fast crunching guitar. That’s a wrap guys, great job. The director’s southern accent speaks the cue to pack up and move out. It is now close to dark and it’s been a long two days for everyone. Teresa with the help of one of the crewmen picks up the dead deer and places it in the back of a pick up truck. Teresa hops in the passenger side of the dark green Dodge Ram along with the crewman that helped her with the deer behind her. The director sides in the driver seat and soon they’re on their way back to camp where everyone’s vehicles are parked. Thanks again for agreeing to his Teresa. Celebrity guest always sells and with you in this series, I’m sure we’ll make enough for new equipment and a couple new trucks. Teresa is sitting in the middle of the two men. She takes off her camouflage cap and tosses it on the dashboard. Her hair is in a French braid. She leans her head back feeling tired and hungry. It’s nothing Mark. I had a great time working with you guys and it’s been more of a mini vacation than work. Your check will be in the mail within a week or so. Also you will be sent v.a. UPS, the head already mounted. All you will need to do is hang it on your wall. Teresa nods as they pull into the camp. There are about 5 pickups still there. Among those are Teresa’s midnight purple step side she took back from her former traveling companion, Jack Slayter. That will be great. I only request the rest be donated to Tennessee’s Drive to Feed the Hungry campaign. The director nods and puts the truck in park. The crewman who has remained silent all this time slides out so Teresa can get out. He then climbs back in the truck and the two leave the camp. Teresa climbs into her truck and heads out herself.
