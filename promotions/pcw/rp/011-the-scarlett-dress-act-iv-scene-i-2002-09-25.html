011 - The Scarlett Dress; Act IV, Scene I

25 September 2002

Date: 09 - 25 - 02 AlleywayNew Orleans, Louisiana 1:19 am From his point of view, Peter Evans had been dropped, nearly drunk on the front porch of a brothel deep int he woods of the Bayou. He struggled to make it towards his feet, as he heard the wolves stack up against him, silence filling the air. In the smoke, and orange moon it only fueled Evans more to find out just where he happened to be. The hotel smelled like damped gasoline, swamped up in complete candle oils and gagging whiskey. It seemed close to home for him, as he continued to take constant swigs of dark Hubris Lite and struggled around the room. Two oak chair stood out, against a robust bar, lined with an endless supply of beer canisters, wine glasses, and shot glasses. Turning his back, he stared into the deep gun collection, staring him directly in the face. He smiles oddly, before picking the case with a nail imbedded into the wooden planked floor. Calmly, picked up the main weapon, a Beretta pistol, before taking the safety on. Unclasping the gun's holister, he ckeced to find no bullets in the weapon. Back when he was married, he always had an itch to carry one of these things, loving the brass feel of one against his sweaty palms. He loved to fire it often, creating distraction between himself and his first wife; Angela. She hated guns, finding a dead fern once, shot by her old man. Evans regreated the fact that he had given up his wife and son away to palm one of these machined weapons. He regreated a lot of things, but he wouldn't give up a new found taste for something. Just as found when he carries a Scotch case in his right breastpocket. He had a drinking problem, since the age of nineteen when he first let his lips savor the brew of Budwiser straight out of the bottle, it hounded him as a second itch in his stomach, something he can't replace with a new thing - it has to stay there. It was the only real way he could happen to function properly. Kicking on the stereo, adjacent from the fuzzy brown couch, he sunk back, sipping on the Scotch and mixing that up with a cigarette. If he wasn't drinking, he was smoking. If he wasn't smoking, he happened to have the case near his mouth. The song, 'Fine Again' by Seether began to kick in for him, as he began nodding his head to the music. The phone began to ring, almost shaking Peter out of his dark gaze, 'Uh? Who in the hell...'he began to himself, as he slugged his arm over to the side, picking up the phone on the second ring. 'If you got something to say, then talk.' ... 'What?' ... 'Just why the hell are you calling this number anyway?' ... 'Like I give a shit, now what do you want? You want a plea bargin with me? FORGET IT!' ... 'You have nothing on me,' ... 'Prove it.' ... 'No way...' ... 'Serious?' ...'Dammit!' ... 'Tell me about it.' ... 'I know you just did asshole, that's why you're calling isn't it?' ... 'All right, I'll be in to sort it all out Wednesday, you have a nice day.' ... 'Yeah, goodbye.' He slammed the phone down, before taking another puff of the cigarette. The smoke clouded through his mind as he began to relax, letting his mind off of the incident at hand, that moment when he struck April and those people calling him names. Evans shuddered, before geting up from the seat on the plush couch and pushing his way through the woods, wearing a faded denim blue jacket, with snakeskin army boots and a pair of black pants. A small rattling of forest green tree leaves happen to fall towards the ground as Evans continues his walk, coming up along a small ridge in the ground, made from the small wipeouts burning the hills of Louisiana in the past few months. Evans didn't care, as long as he was within his very own peace, he was free. In the odd moments, Evans might have gotten one problematic ideal after another, but this time after facing something down, he's gotten back up, and finally someone happened to notice how he happened to go through. From the silence, looking through the magazine cover, he sat it down, before crouching up against the ground, keeping his lame wrist keen on the trigger of his rifle. Sweat started to pour from his brow, as he continued to try and perceive who to shoot first. Taking a dry puff from his cigarette, a bullet grazed his lower side, missing by only inches. Evans cursed, before taking another puff and placing the cirgarette bud out. He hadn't eaten in hours, excitement gripping him from the very moment he had exited the small housing complex. He stared into the rifle's lens, eyeing his prey inside of the crosshairs. Gripping the trigger, he pulled on it, sending the bullet down from the barrel to the end of another's life. Evans picked up his rifle, and slugged it back into the house, smiling. He had goten his revenge, before Donovan could even think of it. Sweet Revenge * * * Locker RoomDavenport, Iowa, 11:31 p.m. Drenched back in my own sweat, I could hear the voice of Statuz Quo riddling through my head, continuing to speak in my ear, yelling and shouting of the sort. For some reason, it ached him to the very death that Duzza and Mark Makonie had lost the tag team titles, to the exact people that he and myself defeated only two weeks ago - Pranks With Attitude. I can't see a problem with it, but the main thing that itches me is that, we beat them. I pinned one of those freaks and they got the shot before us. The rightful #1 Contenders. 'Shut up and think for a damn second!' I interrupted the raging Quo while he continued to pace in his very own trance, almost close to the Void Trance by Akiyama. Mainly we stayed away from him, but when we happened to see him its been known that if there were a more serious man in the world - he'd take the crown. 'You know them boys are only there because the people have to have that evil against good way. It creates better publicity for Kannon's hate.' 'Yea, I kno dat n all, but y the fuc are dem bitches champs and we ain't?' he asked me, before I slightly raised my index finger to my skull. That thinking motion caused him to grow in more of a rage. It still couldn't shake his mind. He grunted before hawking a ball of spit from his nasals. Then it hit me, those beats from 'So Many Tears' by the legendary Tupac Shakur, somebody that those fools working with our belts knew nothing about. 'You see Quo, its like this...they want a damn name, and we're supplying them with one.' 'Are you serious?' 'We're the men to their boys, kill the damn drama, and kill those little ants with a sledgehammer...' He raised an eyebrow, 'You're being Jay-Z again.' 'Fuck him! He can't get off of my damn nuts!' I boasted, before slightly punching the wall. 'Those are my words he's biting, MINE!' I laughed it up, while Quo only shook his head, sarcastically. The white wifebeater stuck to my body, creased down in a pair of denim blue jeans and Timberland Chukka boots. It was that tough New York mentality aching in my heart, my soul. We didn't call it much of a takeover, just trying to fix things up to our very own enjoyment. No matter if we hated you, or liked you, it was never about the things that shaped your lifestyle, rather ours. I may have been a little bit narcissistic about the little PWA complexity facing us no only on Friday, but on October the 6th as well. The Jokers vs. The Playaz. As Quo stepped out of the room, that buxom figure began to show its true colors of a dapper red business suit, with the slit rising a bit against her thigh. 'James...out of all the people, you show up.' Kendra James had been a sequestered figment of my past for the little bit of two weeks now, ever since Peter Evans couldn't get his act right. My cemented reality if you will was before the actions of my sister even occurred to me, those pent up frustrations taken out on Mark Makonie, Duzza, every single person I don't call a friend or associate. And she comes back into my picture, wanting to break that newfound harmony up like a jagged shard of glass ripped in your palm. She strutted along the floor, as that never ending hum of fluorescent lights continued to circle my head. 'No time for your snazzy introductions today.' she remarked. 'Oh?' I questioned. 'And why not? Still can't pin something to my name?' She chuckled slightly, 'You know what? I thought Evans cracked because of the pressure, but you have cunningly, and undeniably gotten under my skin.' I began that tangy singsong voice, 'I've got you, underneath my skin.' 'Shut up,' she forced back in that shy schoolgirl kind of way before motioning over towards me, 'Whether your star may be high or not, it'll fall one day Jackson, and you'll slip. Just like everyone else.' 'What are you talking about?! I've got the #1 Album in the country again, 2 MTV Awards, a host full of fans, and yet you want to bring a brother down! Typical of our 'sisters'...' I trailed off, before she almost met my face with a slap, something I would have deserved, but knowing whose the better out of herself and me, I jerked her arm back. 'And you were going to hit me with that?' 'Egh..ugh...' she struggle under slight discontinued breathing methods, before I let go of her hand. 'You never told me about the business with your father, did you?' 'That's a matter left up entirely not for your discussion.' With a snarl, she took a few steps back as I began making a move towards her. She moved forward, not intimidated by my imposing male figure. Might as well not be, I had to qualm on that one, before beginning to tell her the story. Apparently, she ate it all truthfully down and gasped towards it, shockingly enough I didn't think that was her motive in the entire situation. Usually she'd be calm and what not, posing a threat to my liability, but no, she listened. 'I can't believe he'd do something like that,' she remarked as I held my head down in shame. Those old wounds had been re-opened as I raised my shallow head upwards once more, before smiling at her. Then, without much hesitation - we kissed. And for that minute, everything else didn't matter.
