The Wishmaster -- Prologue

Date: (Wednesday 11/27 Turmoil) 'Are you alright?' Sandy asked me when I met up with her in the Celestials locker room. We'd been assigned to the Alamo Dome boiler room. Dirty room, it smelled like any place that had been abandoned for years on end. We had to throw out some fat guy named Mick who complained about the necrophilia angle. Akiyama punched a hole clear through the wall, I felt the draft as the air rushed out of the gap in the wall when he got back to the room. He was rather upset as well, but took some consolation in knowing that Xavier Kannon couldn't defeat him on his own. The Celestials had proved their dominance in fair combat. If Kannon had to recruit an entire federation to get one over on the most dominant fighting stable that PCW has seen in months, then it only showed to prove how strong we truly were. 'I'm fine,' I said. A simple response, but no more wording was necessary. Physically, I was alright. Mentally, a little upset. I don't like to lose. But it happened that night, that Sunday when Xavier Kannon pinned me. I was fine at the moment, but not during the 15 second period that kept cycling on and on in my head in an endless loop. To hear the Spanish announcers call the match narration and the commentary from Dave Kern and Jeff Marx. Kannon sends the chair down into Akiyama and stomps it while Chris Matthews DRILLS THE END INTO THE CAGE WITH THE ALPHA OMEGA AND MCGLOCKLAN SPIKES RUSH’S HEAD INTO THE MAT WITH GUINNESS!!! Dave: THIS IS... A MOCKERY!!! Jeff: I LOVE PRESENTS!!! Both men are dragged over to Kannon who arrogantly covers both... 1........2.......3!!! THE END AND RUSH MASON HAVE BEEN ELIMINATED!!! Of course, my neck was aching me a little from the Back-to-Back Tiger Driver 91' that Chris Matthews uses as a finisher. Rush Mason and I were pinned at the same time but I'm calling him as being #4 in the Alliances match and myself #3. Whereas I was pinned due to falling victim to a man's carefully perfected finisher onto the steel floor of the cage, Rush jobbed to what amounted to a mid-match spot done because Caleb McGlocklan has an affinity for a Vertical Suplex dropped into a DDT, something that about 77% of all WT wrestlers have used in their careers claiming it to be an original move. That beside the point, I was understandably upset that Xavier Kannon screwed me out of the Alliances main event thanks to his new lap-dogs. Funny, you'd think it'd be enough that Ellie sucks him off 10 times a day but he needs additional workers to do it as well. Male workers. This is quite disturbing, rivalling the actions of a certain McMahon when he got off by his employees kissing his ass. After Alliances, we all made our trip back to Jahana Castle on Sado Island, Japan. Boca accompanied us, but only because it was his responsibility to remove his personal items from his quarters. We gave him the stables to stay in, so the electric items, no matter how hard we tried to screen them from the castle, always managed to make it beyond the doors into the castle. Boca's life was in danger more than once in the house, most often from Akiyama as the Reamer kept trying to hit on him. Akiyama never wanted to touch Boca, which was the only reason why he was somewhat safe from injury, but Cyclonica wasn't as worried about that and would occasionally slit his throat. At that point, it was left to me to heal him so that we wouldn't be in violation of the cockamamie rules set against us. Boca's quarters were full of things I don't even want to think about. None of us wanted to touch him, and if Cyclonica had her way, Boca would've been dumped into the Pacific on the flight back, and the stable torched. But Boca would be dismissed from the group for at least lasting outside of Cage 3, and given the chance to collect his belongings and never bother the Celestials again. And then the stable would be torched. The stable smelled of semen, the scent so strong that flies formed a thick cloud around the thing. Cyclonica and Akiyama didn't say a word on the flight back. Akiyama is a little quiet anyway, but even psionic speech was subdued. And Cyclonica plotted to finish off the man who eliminated her anyway, she wasn't going to say anything for a while. Sandy and I sat a little aways from Akiyama and Cyclonica, giving them their space to be alone as was their desire. When they wanted silence, Sandy and I did something that we hadn't really managed for about a month, since joining the Celestials in preparation for Alliances. We sat and talked. Interesting, how the simple pleasures like conversation are missed when you put everything you've got into work. Granted, this doesn't exactly count as 'work' since I wrestle for free to this very day, but my endorsements sustain my traveling lifestyle and PCW pays for my airfare. Sandy also works for free but she's got a manager's card anyway. She checked in with her PCW press contacts to check out the updated rankings. As it turned out I'm ranked #1 contender to the Continental Title. It seemed that Xavier Kannon wanted to appease me for the way I was eliminated in Alliances. Either that or he's not a total twat. But whichever is the case, he gambled correctly. I am appeased. I lost at Alliances. I don't like to lose. But putting that aside, challenging the Continental Title is all I really wanted to gain out of that match. We discussed things in advance. Akiyama would get his singles shot at the World Title, as he has been kept from for so long. Boca would get nothing. And Cyclonica would receive the Extreme Title shot. I would receive the title shots for both Television and Continental belts. May have been much, but I knew I could handle it and the other Celestials agreed with me. Well, Boca didn't, but a superkick shut him up. But I seemed content. The fighting instinct was still in me, I could feel it. But it wasn't a killer instinct as it once was. I would give my all but if I won, I won; if I lost, I lost. There came upon me a feeling of 'It'll be alright.' That's not something I feel often. I am usually more apathetic and don't care how things turn out. But now... it's like I know things will turn out alright for me. I'm... content. Possibly even.... Happy..... And I want to extend that feeling to others. Odd...
