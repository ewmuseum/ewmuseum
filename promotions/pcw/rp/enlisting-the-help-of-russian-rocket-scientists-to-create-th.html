Enlisting the help of Russian Rocket Scientists to create the perfect nut...

Date: not entered When we last left off, Flaymin and Hirraani had just touched down in Russia after a near-death flight.But why have they come to Russia? [Scene opens with Flaymin and Hirraani walking through the airport wheeling their little bags through the terminal. Flaymin looks around at the surrounding environment with a look of dismay on his face] Flaymin: There sure are a lot of Russian people here… Hirraani: What were you expecting? [Flaymin shrugs his shoulders] Flaymin: I dunno, Japanese people? [Flaymin and Hirraani exit the airport and jump into a waiting taxi. The taxi is old and rusted and smells faintly of body odour. The driver turns around to face Flaymin and Hirraani who are seated in the back seat. The driver then mutters a few words in Russian] Flaymin: What’s he saying? [Hirraani places her fingers on her forehead and slowly begins to rub them in small circles in order to gain a telepathic understanding] Flaymin: Well? Hirraani: He wants to know where you want to go. Flaymin: Oh. [Flaymin turns back to the driver and begins speaking in a very slow yet abnormally loud voice] Flaymin: TAKE…. US… TO… THE…. SPACE… STATION [The driver looks on in confusion. Flaymin then places his hands above his head in an attempt to form his body into a crappy looking rocket ship] Hirraani: You look like a sperm. Flaymin: In that case, maybe he’ll take us to a whorehouse? [The driver suddenly begins to understand the rocket signal Flaymin is making.He pulls the cigarette out of his mouth and grunts.He then turns around and begins to drive] Flaymin: I’m pretty good at this sign language thing! [Flaymin and Hirraani sit back in the taxi as it exits the airport and drives along the connecting highway. A few minutes pass before the taxi finally comes to a stop outside a large, flashy building guarded by a dozen or so security guards.Flaymin pays the taxi driver and then makes his way out of the taxi with Hirraani in tow] Flaymin: This is it! [Flaymin and Hirraani walk over to the main entrance of the building where a stout, bodyguard looks at Flaymin and begins to speak in Russian. Without saying a word, Hirraani reaches into her handbag and pulls out an invitation card and hands it to the security guard. The guard takes the invitation and puts it underneath the electronic scanner until a green light begins to flash. He then nods his head; hands back the invitation and opens the door, allowing Flaymin and Hirraani to enter the building. Inside the building, they both walk over to the receptionist and give her the invitation card.The receptionist looks at the card and then points towards a door in the far corner of the building] Flaymin: Don’t these people know how to talk?All they do is grumble and point. Hirraani: Do you speak Russian? Flaymin: No, but I’ve met the Rush. Hirraani: I don’t think he’s Russian… Flaymin: Sure he is.He’s always mumbling and grumbling in a foreign language. Hirraani: *Sarcastically* Yeah, ‘English’ is pretty foreign…. [Flaymin and Hirraani walk over to the door at the far end of the building where they were directed by the receptionist. Flaymin knocks on the door and then enters the room. The room is in fact a laboratory filled with chemicals, bubbling potions, rocket diagrams plastered all over the walls and odd bits and pieces of technological crap scattered all over the place. Two men wearing white coats approach Flaymin and shake his hand] Scientist #1: Hello, we have been expecting you.My name is Serge Romanov… but you can call me Tim. Flaymin: How did you get ‘Tim’ from Serge? [‘Tim’ pulls out a chart from underneath the table and begins pointing at different parts of it while making calculations] Tim: You see, I came up with the formula to transform Serge into Tim by… Flaymin: Alright, alright- we get the point! [The second scientist walks over to Flaymin and introduces himself] Scientist #2: My name is Kristof Fegan.You can just call me Kristof. Flaymin: How did you come up with Kristof? [Kristof looks at Tim and then walks over to a nearby chalkboard and begins to draw a graph] Flaymin: Dude, I was only joking! [The bemused Russian Rocket Scientists frown at Flaymin] Kristof: So what brings you to Russia? Tim: You requested an urgent meeting regarded as a critical international crisis. Flaymin: Damn straight, it’s a crisis!You see I lost a testicle a few months back and I’ve been looking for a replacement ever since.I’ve searched high and low… far and wide… left to right… back and forth… here and there…. Tim: ENOUGH!!What is it you want from us? Flaymin: I would like you guys to develop a new testicle for me.One that’s aerodynamic and can float as if it were held up by gravity in space. [Kristof looks at Flaymin with a perplexed look on his face] Kristof: What good is a floating testicle? Flaymin: It’ll be able to dodge incoming swift kicks to the balls. For instance, some monkey-f*cker tries to kick me in the left nut and then WHAMMO!!, the testicle quickly floats to the right side of the nutsack to avoid any impact. Kristof: Would this testicle be remote-controlled with an inbuilt motor? Flaymin: You mean like a vibrator? Tim: Um… Flaymin: Sure why not.A vibrating testicle would be a blast as a party trick! [Tim and Kristof huddle together and begin to whisper to each other about possibly undertaking this project] Hirraani: What do you think they’re saying? Flaymin: They’re probably creating an evil ploy to steal my idea.Floating, vibrating testicles will be the latest craze with mono-balled guys. Hirraani: Maybe you should have copyrighted the idea? [The two scientists turn back around and face Flaymin] Tim: May I ask what happened to your original testicle. Flaymin: I got kicked in the balls by a pornstar wearing metal shoes… and then the nut kinda fell out. Tim: I’ve seen you somewhere, but I can’t quite put my finger on it… Flaymin: Well then pull your finger out, you idiot because I’m a wrestler in America. Kristof: PCW?? Flaymin: That’s the one. Tim: Do you know Ivan? Flaymin: The hotdog vender? Kristof: No, the wrestler. Flaymin: Oh yeah I remember him.Did you know I was the guy that ran him out of PCW? [Kristof raises an eyebrow while Hirraani just slaps her forehead] Flaymin: Yep it was all me.I went into his locker room and said “Listen here you damn socialist, if you don’t join International Incident I’m sending your ass back to Kazakhstan”. [Hirraani closes her eyes and winces in pain as the twisted and historically incorrect rant continues. Tim eyes Flaymin up and down before spitting on the floor in disgust] Tim: I find it hard to believe that you drove Ivan out of PCW! Flaymin: You better believe it.I wasn’t going to let no freakin’ hotdog vender intimidate me. [Flaymin begins to speak in a stern voice with a serious look on his face] Flaymin: I said to him “Listen to me carefully, Vlad…. [Hirraani jumps in and corrects Flaymin] Hirraani: You mean Ivan… Flaymin: Whatever.As I was saying, I said “Listen to me carefully Vla..err.. Ivan the hotdog guy, I don’t want no damn mustard on my bun and if you don’t get me a mustardless hotdog I’m gonna chop the hotdog in two and shove it up your nostri… Kristof: We are not talking about some pathetic Hotdog Vender!!We are talking about the great Russian Bear!! Flaymin: Russian Bear??Sorry dude, I don’t watch many documentaries… unless you count the Pamela-Tommy Lee video as an informative documentary. [By this stage the Russian scientists grow extremely frustrated and call for security to escort Flaymin and Hirraani out of the building. The security guards rush into the laboratory and grab Flaymin by the collar and drag his carcass out of the room.As he’s being dragged out of the room, Flaymin shrieks in a loud voice] Flaymin: What about the floating testicle!!!! Kristof: Shove it up your ass! Flaymin: I would… but you’d have to create one for me first!! [Before Flaymin can say anything else he’s tossed out of the building and out onto the streets.He gets up and dusts off his jacket before turning to Hirraani who stares back at Flaymin with any angry look on her face] Flaymin: What?Was it something I said? [Scene fades to black]
