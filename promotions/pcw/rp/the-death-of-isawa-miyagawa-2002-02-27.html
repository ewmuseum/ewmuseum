The Death of Isawa Miyagawa

27 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 27th Febuary We’re travelling through Anaheim Central Airport (or if someone wants to be picky on the British author who’s never been to Anaheim or California in general, I guess you can replace that with whatever the correct name of the Anaheim Airport is... but I’m calling it Anaheim Central because I just happen to like that.) with Miyagawa and Steve... at a rather brisk walk. We pass a small jewellers and Miyagawa suddenly stops and looks at the shop, its rows upon rows of rings and necklaces shining like a star. Miyagawa: Wait here... actually, I might be a while. Miyagawa pulls several notes out of his pocket and hands them over to Steve in a hurried manner. Miyagawa: Go get yourself something to eat. Get me a coffee, I’ll phone you to find out where you are... you’ve got your mobile, right? Steve: Yeah, it’s in my shirt pocket. Miyagawa strides into the shop as Steve counts the notes in his hand. The look on his face is priceless. Steve: He just gave me three hundred and twelve dollars for lunch. Now I remember why I hang out with him... I wonder if I can get something edible in an airport for three hundred dollars... because I know for certain you can’t get anything edible for five bucks. Bring on the fifty year old bourbon! ******************************* We arrive just outside the Asylum Gym in bright beaming sunshine. The light shines off the white washed exterior walls and the over polished front wooden door. The place looks nice, with the front garden actually having been tendered for once... I guess Miya must have hired a gardener or something then. A car pulls up quickly, it’s a BMW Z3 in British Racing Green Metallic... which of course mean’s therefore that it has to be Miyagawa and Steve, returning from their little trip to Norway in Canada. Miyagawa: Come on Steve, move your sixteenth century ass already! Miyagawa is still wearing his Angel getup... Angel from the TV Series... the Buffy spin off that is (not “Touched by an Angel”) and Steve still looks like he would be more at home with an axe while cutting down trees in a forest. Miyagawa unlocks his seat belt and finally removes the keys from the ignition. He goes to scramble over the car in a nice Miami Vice fashion, but catches his foot on the top of the door (the window’s winded down for your knowledge) and thus the upper half of his body impacts directly with the pavement. Steve: Now I don’t believe you wanted to do that. What I believe you wanted to do was the following. Steve opens the door with the catch and steps out of the car; closing the door behind him. He walks around the front of the car and holds out his hand to help Miyagawa back up to his feet. Steve: What I believe you wanted to do was to open the door and calmly walk up towards the front doo... Miyagawa is already at the front door, already opened it and already inside. Steve: Ok then, I’d suppose I shall follow him seeing as how I had two glasses of fifty year old bourbon out of him earlier. We cut inside the Asylum Gym now, following Miyagawa as he walks down the main corridor connecting all of the rooms. He walks down the corridor, pushing each door open and checking inside briefly before moving on. Finally he gets to the bedroom and opens the door and standing inside is Natalia... next to a mirror, stroking her belly, to try and see the bump. Natalia: Can you see it yet? I think I can. Miyagawa: We’re going to go and meet my mom now. Natalia turns to him quickly in surprise. Natalia: You have a mother? Miyagawa: Apparently. And she only lives a few miles away, we’re going to go over there now and meet her... apparently my whole life has been this big sham, but Steve will explain that on the way. Miyagawa and Natalia stride out of the gym just as Steve was finally about to enter it. The trio stand there in the bright sunshine next to the shining BMW Z3; the light gleaming off it like a new born star. Steve: So Alex, are we getting going then? Natalia looks at Steve confused and then back at Miyagawa. Natalia: Who is Alex? Miyagawa: I am apparently. Alexander Isawa Miyagawa; nice to meet you. Miyagawa sticks out his hand towards Natalia, in some sort of joke greeting. She doesn’t get the joke however. Natalia: Wait... I married Isawa Miyagawa. If you aren’t Isawa Miyagawa... then who am I married to? Miyagawa: I thought of that too as we touched down. We should be able to easily get our marriage voided in the courts. Natalia: But we won’t be married, what then? Miyagawa: I thought of that also... Miyagawa gets down onto one knee on the pavement, the few passers by smiling as they all know exactly what he is about to do. Miyagawa: Now I know this isn’t exactly the most romantic situation in the world, but I thought well... that I could make it up to you by asking you out to dinner tonight at a rather nice Italian restaurant in town later. He fishes a small purple felt covered box from his pocket and holds it up towards Natalia. Miyagawa: I, Alexander Miyagawa... would like to ask you Natalia Schevchenko to marry me. Natalia looks down at her husband... well her live in boyfriend is closer I guess right now... and smiles at him. Natalia: I’ll let you know tonight over dinner. Natalia walks off towards the car, while Miyagawa gets up stunned. He turns to Steve who isn’t really all that surprised. Miyagawa: That woman never fails to amaze me, you know that? Steve: And that, my friend, is why you love her.
