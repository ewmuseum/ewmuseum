The Material Saga: The Wake Up?

25 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7-25-02 DarkStar was a little bit late, but Nelson said the boat would be held until he got on. It was, he pulled his car around and the man in charge of it took the keys. The car would be placed in the hold where the rest of the PCW'ers vehicles were located. 'Make sure you take care of it, or else you will never work again, friend' he patted the man on the back and walked up the long metallic ramp. The boat was teaming with activity, people were getting the ship ready to depart for Jamaica. This should be a really interesting trip. He reached the top and noticed the two men standing there to greet him, he smiled at the familiar faces and nodded to address them, 'Clyde, Tyler. Hows it going?' Tyler glanced over to the right and watched the Porsche being put in the cargo hold. 'Its fine Dark, did you like the gift?' A small laugh, 'Loved it, its perfect for me.' Tyler motioned to Clyde, 'I got this guy the prototype for the Sweet Tooth truck.' Clyde grinned evilly. 'Some little kids aren't going to be very happy when they get me as a Ice Cream man are they.' Laughing. Felt good to laugh about trivial things. They were here, however, for business. The call came over the PA that the ship would be departing. The large metal walkway was pulled up, the various PCW fans waved and screamed for their favorite wrestlers, DarkStar couldn't help but smile at the amount of Dark Age fans in the crowd. Something was different though, it wasnt what he and Clyde were used to, and even he could notice it in Clydes eyes. They were surprised by this. The crowd, the fans they had, they werent the normal gothic kids who have followed them religiously, it wasn't the cult they were used to. The kids, some of them wearing DarkStar shirts and Dark Age shirts weren't even 15 yet, some of them weren't even goth. DarkStar made a face. This wasn't why he was here. He didn't get into this business for these people, he got into it because this was him. This is what he wanted. Not to please a bunch of screeching teenage girls and poser goth guys. Why would they latch onto him, of all people? This was a sickening wake up call. The car didnt matter to him anymore, he was knocked back to earth, he was so close to selling out, to pushing himself over to the money making sides of this business. He shook it off, trying to put the oppurtunity in the back of his head. 'No,' he thought to himself, 'we are here because we want to show the world true darkness, to spit what they did to us back in their face. To prove that we are to be feared.' Talena came skipping around the corner, the crowd cheered a little and she blushed, 'Gavyn! Have you seen our room yet!' she jumped into his arms, 'Its amazing! Theres a hot tub, and a mini bar, and a pool table, and... and... ' DarkStar put a finger on her mouth, a bit of lipstick rubbed off. He was so used to staying in horrible hotels because they were never accepted anywhere else, it came with the territory. You put metal in your face, you dyed your hair, you wore make up, you talked to a doll, you murdered people in cold blood, therefor you cannot get the nice things. Noone really cared when you lived like that, him and Talena didnt have a estate because they wanted to flaunt their wealth, they had it because it was beautiful. Because they liked it. DarkStar didnt have that Corvette because it was a legendary car, he owned it because it had meaning. Now he was so close to letting go of it all and diving over the fence. It wasn't even the money that was attractive. Fuck money. It was the power, it was the oppurtunity to show everyone that he and the rest of the Dark Age were the true force around here. Everywhere. It was nearly intoxicating. He hoisted his messanger bag up further on his shoulder and smiled at the crowd, the boat began to float away from the dock, he waved, he smiled, Talena hung off his side. The crowd was eating it up. He glanced over at Clyde who was shooting him a look, 'Ok Captain Diplomat, thats enough, we need to talk strategy, we are only... a few hundred miles away.......' Clyde stalled for a second. 'Fuck that, lets go get really drunk in our massive rooms.' Tyler laughed and started to walk to that wing of the large cruise ship. 'Good plan knights, good plan', Clyde and DarkStar shook hands and it left Talena and DarkStar staring over the nearly empty part of the deck, the ship was beautiful, and brand new to boot, the water was beautiful, the entire thing was surreal. Instead of thinking of that though, DarkStar could do nothing but worry about where his head was, he seemed more hell bent on defending the worthless strap of gold he had then furthering the destruction that the Dark Age was supposed to cause. He shook his head, it will all pass. This feeling is just because he isnt used to the fame. Cult hits don't break into the mainstream well. Right now? He was mainstream.
