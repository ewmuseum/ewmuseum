A Californication Celebration or just a Total Devastation?

12 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/12/02 *Monologue* Spring Fling. A night of redemption who‘d be the fitting word to describe this awesome spectacle. A man who’s been in the PCW for 7 months without no gold. Big Daveylicious went in the title bout like always but instead of coming out with no gold and glory. He comes out of Anaheim, California with the PCW Extreme title and all the glory by his side. Proving critics wrong. Getting revenge on the “Cowardly” Core. Daveylicious Bombing Karl LeBlanc onto a chair before he eliminates LeBlanc to become the Extreme Champion. Not only did he get the gold and the glory but he got the gold, the glory, and the sex with three naked women! Finally, the PCW gots a Extreme Champion worthy of the gold. Just like...uh...and uh...okay, there never has really been a worthy Extreme champion. Big D has this oppurtunity to make the title worth something again. But to whom does he face for his title defense? A panty waste filled jobber by the name of Karl LeBlanc just as Core did over and over again? No! He’s actually fighting somebody that is actually GOOD! He fights Akiyama this Friday for his first title defense. Does he plan to let go of it and job the title to a man whom thinks he is a god? Ha! He’s fought too long. He doesn’t want to repeat his last process again. He don’t care who he faces because he’ll stop at nothing until he beats Akiyama this Friday. If he’s given a fair chance that is... (The scene fades to a shine of gold as we back away it is reveled to be the PCW Extreme title belt. As we back away from the shine and the glare of the gold we see the owner of this belt. Your favorite Extreme Champion and mine, Big Daveylicious. Dressed in a Big Daveylicious shirt, black Nike pants, and his Oakley sunglasses. Graves proudly follows his mentor. He struts around proudly walking in the morning of the streets of Hollywood Boulevard. Famous actors and singers stars lay upon the sidewalk. Along the sidewalk are tourists, pimps, prostitutes, and your average customers. ) Big Daveylicious: I’m a winner! No, even better a champion! Whoooooooooooooooo! (He continues to gloat around as he is stopped by a woman in a small tight red dress and a fake fur coat. Yes, a prostitute.) Prostitute: Hey, hon' want a date? Big Daveylicious: Well, that's sweet of you. Where are we going? The movies, candlelight dinner at a fine restaurant, or long walks on the beach? Prostitute: I mean do you want company? Big Daveylicious: Well, really no. I think owning one would be too much work for my wrestling career. Prostitute: S'cuse me, honey! But I am asking if you want sex in a hotel? (Big D turns to us and winks with a smile.) Big Daveylicious: Ah, prostitute. Behold boys and girls somebody who will have sex with you for money. Prositute: So what will it be? (Big D looks at her and than looks at the Extreme title around his waist. Than the prostitute. Trying to answer man‘s toughest question. Women or Gold? He smiles.) Big Daveylicious: Why? I’m the PCW Extreme Champion! I no longer need to flee money after you prostitutes for a night of pleasure! Besides I got female fans who do it for free! (Prositute rolls her eyes at the standing proudly Extreme Champion. Looks over at Big D’s helpless lackey, Michael Graves. He looks at her.) Prostitute: How about you, baby? Michael Graves: But don’t you have cooties? Prositute: Fag! (She slaps Graves on the face. He holds his face in wreathing pain. As Big D laughs.) Michael Graves: I don’t get it, Big D. Why does a dark evil man get accused of being gay when he asks a question?! Big Daveylicious: She didn’t have cooties. She had STD’s. (A pimp looks on at Big D. The pimp overhears the conversation and tries to make a profit as he walks in front of the two.) Pimp: Heyyyyyyy, macdaddys! How would you two like to ride the biggest ride in California? Michael Graves: Yay! He gots tickets to Disney Land! (The pimp just stares at Graves dumbfounded.) Pimp: Hell no! I got no Disney Land tickets! But if your looking for a ticket for sensation. I got that! Big Daveylicious: Sorry, but I’m not a homosexual. Michael Graves: What’s a matter with you, Big D? Don’t you realize he’s offering us tickets to Disney Land! I wanna see Mickey! Pimp: Fools! I ain’t talking about no Mickey! You don’t want to see mouses! No ,no, no! You want to see some hot looking cats that’ll make your head go up! Big Daveylicious: Sorry, not into the animal sex thing either. Michael Graves: Yeah, who’d wanna watch Mickey and Minnie go at it? Pimp: Screw ya’ll! (The pimp walks out of our view. Big D looks at Graves.) Big Daveylicious: Man, pimps aren’t what they used to be. I mean I thought pimps offered women. Not animals or themselves (Graves starts to sit down having a temper. He hits his hands against the floor of the street.) Big Daveylicious: What’s a matter, Graves? Michael Graves: I don’t get to see Mickey! I want to see Mickey! I wanna see him! Big Daveylicious: Why do I even keep you as a lackey? Michael Graves: Cause I’m the only one stupid enough to know that the Staff wants you to job the title to Akiyama if it’s close. Big Daveylicious: Wha...? Michael Graves: I’m not lying you better kiss the gold good bye, Big Daveylicious: That wouldn’t happen Graves. The staff wouldn’t do that to me. Especially, after 7 months trying to get this gold. They wouldn’t do it because I have no angle or etc. Now, would they? Michael Graves: Ye.... Big Daveylicious: Silence this is your Extreme champion speaking! Michael Graves: I don’t about this you could get in trouble. Big Daveylicious: It doesn’t matter... (He runs in in the middle of the road. He unhooks his belt. As he kneels down in the middle of the road. He tilts his bleached-blonde head complete with the ever-present Oakleys back and holds the Extreme title in the air with his arms outstretched in joy.) Big Daveylicious: Whooooooooooooooo! Because I’M THE EXTREME CHAMPION! Whooooooooooo...oh sh*t! (A car lunges towards him. He dives out of the way. The speeding car misses him. He dusts himself off. Dusts the title and looks on at the car. He shakes his head.) Big Daveylicious: Now, is that anyway to treat a champion? (Suddenly, a car horn beeps as the scene fades to a middle finger.)
