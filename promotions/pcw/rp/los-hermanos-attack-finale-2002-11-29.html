Los Hermanos Attack (Finale)

29 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/29/02 Continuation………….. Payback is Sweet. The scene begins with El Romantico in a room with two of the Los Hermanos. Romantico has devised a plan to get the proper revenge, and is taking all precautions to make sure it goes just as planned. Shut the door. Romantico holds out his gun, feeling as if he’s in complete control. He has two men on his back and he’s no longer defenseless. So who’s the big shot now? What you think you gonna do to us motha fucka. We will whipe the floor with yo rich white ass. Oh will you. Romantico lets out a fire shooting the man right in the foot. He screams in pain and blood pours out into the floor. The other gang members look on surprised. Romantico what the hell are you doing, I thought you said nobody was going to get hurt in this operation. The men on Romantico’s side start to get a little worried over what’s going on, but Romantico assures them that it’s all part of the plan. What’d you do that for you dumb fuck? I did it because I can. Now all of you get on the floor right now and take off all your clothes accept the under garments your wearing. You fuckin serious? Do I look like I’m joking around? Now do it or your face will end up like your friends foot, capisce? The men undress and then Romantico sends them all into the bathroom. When In there he handcuffs them to each other and then the final handcuff goes on the bottom of the sink. I’d like to see you guys get out of this one. One of the gang members then spits on Romantico, who laughs and then kicks the man straight in the face with his boot. When he enters the previous room, he sees the two men he brought with him, dressed in all gang wear. He then goes and puts on the costume he brought for himself. Now give the guys at Gang House B a call to make sure everything is set. One of the men takes out there cell phone and calls them over in house B. Is everything running smoothly over there? Couldn’t be any smoother. The scene shows three gang members knocked out in there own couch. Okay good, were meeting at point number one, in fifteen minutes, be on time. I hear you loud and clear. (Hang up) Okay Romantico were ready to go. Scene shows Romantico in a black and white tuxedo, with gelled back hair and a fake mustache. He smiles at the two men with him and then locks in the bullets for his gun. Let’s do this. They grab the keys to the gang member’s vehicles and are on their way. The men all arrive at the same time in the front steps of the main gang hangout. They join each other and go over some last details, before they make there way to the front. Everyone then goes up to the front door and one of the men ring the doorbell. Soon afterwards a drunken gang member opens the door. Hey boys, come in and get ready for the party, we got everything you could possible dream of. The best Puerto Rican rum you’ve ever laid eyes on. The gang member then passes out right on the floor. This is going to be too easy. Romantico sighs to himself, but right as he’s about to walk in a much bigger gang member stands in the way. Who do you guys think you are, I haven’t seen you around here before. It’s because you haven’t seen us around. You see were apart of the worldwide Los Hermanos Circuit in Valencia, you may know it as where the Los Hermanos first began. I’m aware of that, but what are you doing here. We have some business to do, so we demand to speak with the head of you unit right now. I’m sorry, but the great Sergio doesn’t speak to foot soldiers. He only talks to the heads in the Los Hermanos gang family. Would the founder of the Los Hermanos be good enough? The men all step aside as Romantico in his gang leader disguise walks in the house. Take me to the man you call leader of this slum. And make sure all of these drunks sober up before I come back down here, or else every single one of them will end up dead. Yes sir, let me take you to our leader now. The man bows in the presence of Romantico who puffs his cigar out on him. When they arrive upstairs, Romantico invites himself in the master bedroom where he throws open the door to see the Leader of the Los Hermanos humping away on some young broad. What the hell are you doing in here? The gang leader takes out his gun, and Romantico takes his out at the same exact time pointing it at him. Do you realize who I am? No, can’t you see I’m doing some business here. Does the name Salvador Rodriguez, ring s bell. The gang leader takes a huge gulp as he throws the broad off of his bed, throws on some pants and then bows down in front of the great Salvador. I am so sorry sir; I didn’t know it was you. You call this sick excuse for a house, your gang meeting place. You sicken me. As Romantico lures him in, he locks the doors behind himself, making it just him and the gang leader himself. I hear you have been going after some people in the Upper East Side. Yea, those rich bastards deserve what they get. Don’t think about going after them ever again. If I hear you step one foot in that territory I will have you killed, is that clear. Crystal. Good now that were on the same page, all I need is one thing and I’m gone. What’s that? There are two of your gang houses we invaded earlier in the day. We found some things against gang regulations in there, and then our men had to use physical force on them. We took away all of there clothing because we felt there not worthy to represent the Los Hermanos. We hope you understand. It’s been an honor. The honor is all mine. The gang leader then bends over and begins to kiss the feet of Romantico. Romantico then gathers his men and leaves as soon as possible. Right as they leave the six-gang members who’s clothing was stolen and houses invaded charged into the head quarter. Just a few seconds later, all of them flew out the second balcony window. Romantico looked back and laughed loudly to himself in a sadistic manner. REVENGE IS SWEET. Just goes to show, revenge can happen and nobody has to be hurt in the process. How about the guy you shot in the foot? Shut up. The End!
