Reunited Part 3

2 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6/2/03 As the reflection of the moon bounced off the shiny cold steel which was being pressed up against the skin covering my neck I gulped and realized that survival was very unlucky without going against my will. Hovering above me stood the man who would responsible for my death should I make the wrong decision, the man named Ein. There was seemingly no way out now other than to give into him and gave him what he wanted, but if I did that life wouldn’t be worth living anyhow even if I did have a chance to get a shot at the Television Title ahead of me on Turmoil. “You know you are quite a foolish young lady, it seems you are always getting yourself into yet another dilemma, first with being named Gaz’s wife against your will, and now you are about to hear your last breath all because once again you messed up. I bet poor Chris would be so upset to hear about the death of his beloved Tessa Bear, oh but wait he’s no longer around so I don’t think he’ll mind your departure from this earth that much.” He grinned from ear to ear as he leaned in closer to give me one last kiss before I left this earth. As he got closer and closer my mind flooded with thoughts all of which I planned on being my last. ..I hope Evan doesn’t suffer the same fate as me; he’s much too young to go through this… …How am I going to get out of this? I’ve got to get to Evan before they chose to give him the same fate… ...But how do I get out? THERE HAS TO BE A WAY OUT... … Oh my god why didn’t I think of this earlier… Suddenly I flung my free foot forward and nailed my attempted killer where it would hurt him the most. I hated to stoop so low as to take a cheap shot like that but desperate times lead to desperate measures and right now I had become more desperate than I was to get rid of Gaz and the last name Green. “You bastard, I hate you!” I felt a big dumb screaming out the obvious but it was the only way I could keep from killing the guy. While he was crouched over as the pain flowed throughout his body I took the opportunity to jump into the car and flee from the area the way a criminal flees from a crime scene. Without my seatbelt fastened and without looking back I hit the gas petal and took off down the road to get back to Evan as soon as I could but I couldn’t help but worry if I would be too late. …This is all my fault I can’t believe I could actually be so stupid as to forget that Chris had a twin brother… …Evan just hold on I’m coming I promise… …If you aren’t dead already… ...oh dear god I hope you’re okay… …But at least if I’m strong enough to survive through this I should be strong enough to survive teaming with Gaz along with Skylar on Turmoil, and if we can win it will bring the chance of winning the television title from Dorn…if I can survive another beating from him… I tried to convince myself that everything would be okay, even though that was only as guaranteed as my win against Dorn would be if I somehow managed to beat him in a second match right after having to team with the Gaz and Skylar against Dorn Tracer, The Pharaoh, and Dante Jackson should we get the win but still the hope clung to my mind the way lint clings to a static charged piece of cloth. But if I wanted to help Evan out I would have to be strong and believe I would stop any harm from coming his way, the way I would have to stop from getting in harms way on Tuesday so that I could somehow walk into a second match with Dorn Tracer and walk away the Television Champion.
