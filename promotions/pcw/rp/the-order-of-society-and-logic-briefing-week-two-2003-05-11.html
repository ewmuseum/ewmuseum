The Order of Society and Logic Briefing: Week Two

11 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: May 11, 2003 Begin Transmission. (The scene opens to a now familiar scrambled feed. The fuzz dissipates, as the letters “O.S.L.” drop down into view, in gigantic blood red letters, filling the whole screen. Rather than the voice of Charles Lin, more writing drops down across the screen, as the words “Order of Society and Logic Briefing: Week Two appear. We then cut quickly to Michael Chang and SOCIETY, live from another undisclosed location.) “Welcome to the Order of Society and Logic briefing. We have completed week two of Operation PCW. The results have been varied, but ultimately successful. To summarize, on Tuesday, SOCIETY suffered a set-back, losing to a man named Shane Wethers. This situation was rectified on Friday, as SOCIETY beat this Wethers to within an inch of his life, and then defeated Shane Robertson, to bring his total record to two wins and one loss.” (Chang smiles proudly. Despite the loss to Wethers on Tuesday, SOCIETY has been every bit as impressive as the leader of the OSL could have ever hoped for.) “If SOCIETY’s success continues, he will be able to advance on to Primetime Championship Wrestling’s Supremacy pay-per-view event, which takes place in the now liberated Iraq. His match would be with three other men. If he is victorious, he recieves a shot at the PCW Extreme Title. If he comes in second place, he shall be the PCW Rising Star Champion. But, in order to make it into this match, SOCIETY must continue to be victorious. And this path of victory hits two potential roadblocks this week. Explain this to them, SOCIETY.” (SOCIETY’s countanence is grave as usual. Still, the excitement in his voice is slightly evident, as he relays the matters at hand for the week.) “Greetings, fellow conformists. As Mr. Chang was saying, I have the two sternest tests of my career this week. This coming Tuesday, I will team up with one of the top athletes in PCW, Violence Jack, to take on the tandem of Brianna Washington and Matt Jackson. Mr. Jackson is currently undefeated in the Rising Star Division, and thus the leader in the round robin tournament I have been placed in. A victory over him and Brianna will go a long way to secure my place in the battle for Supremacy!” “SOCIETY,” Chang calls out, “don’t forget about your matchup on Friday. That is your proving ground. It is your CHANCE to prove that you are everything we say you are!” (Rubbing his hands together, SOCIETY glares out into the presumable television audience.) “Ah yes. On Friday Fusion, in Afghanistan, I will face two men. At once. It is what is known in professional wrestling as a “handicap match”. Of course, unfortunately for my opponents, SyN, and the neo-Nazi Marcus Makonie, THEY are the ones with the handicap! Because, let us face facts. I do not care whether they throw one man, two men, or even ten men at me! I have learned my craft well, and am thus ready to take on this new challenge of two men. They are two members of the division I have not yet battled, and so, I look forward to meeting them in combat in the former home of the Taliban. Know this, O.S.L. members. By the time Supremacy rolls around, you shall be able to watch the event, and cheer in glee as SOCIETY reigns victorious over the Rising Star Division!” (Michael Chang nods approvingly, before going into his list of other things that need to be done.) “As you know, members of the Order, our funding is low. We are in need of money, so, at the nearest possible convience, please, wire me $30,000 at the usual location. Also, we have received your artist’s concept for the SOCIETY t-shirt.” (Chang reaches into his bag, and pulls out a black T-shirt. On the front of it is SOCIETY’s now trademark “clutching hand” logo, taking up most of the front. On the back, written in a similar blue color, are the words “CONFORM TO SOCIETY!” Chang holds the garment up, spinning it around so that all the members of the Order can see the work.) “SOCIETY and I are very pleased with this. We will be submitting it to the advertising department of Primetime Championship Wrestling shortly, to see if they are interested.” (Suddenly, SOCIETY remembers something, and taps Chang on the shoulder.) “We must also elaborate on the newest member of the Order of Society and Logic!” “Ah yes, SOCIETY! Thank you for reminding me! As we told you last week, we planned on expanding our Order within the Primetime Championship Wrestling roster. As such, Friday marked the first conformation to the ideals of our order. Of his own free will, Sivasian Krikorian, the Ukranian Sniper, consented to conform to SOCIETY. As such, he is now a member of the Order of Society and Logic, with all of the rights and privileges due any other member of our esteemed order! He shall be an excellent addition to our Order, and we look forward to having him at our Week Three briefing, to introduce himself to you in a more formal light.” “Will that be all for today, Mr. Chang?” (SOCIETY was impatient, ready to return to training as soon as possible.) “Why yes, SOCIETY, that should be sufficient for today. On behalf of SOCIETY, I am Michael Chang. Remember members! CENSORSHIP!” “EQUALITY!” (Both men finish..) “CONFORMITY!” (As suddenly as the broadcast began, so too does it go off the air.) End Transmission.
