Turmoil Wars - Sivasian Krikorian (Final: Part III)

27 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 04/27/03 Stone grew more and more lethargic as wrong maneuvering would have his life absolved with a single round fired from the Soviet Sniper's pistol. On the ground lay the corpse of Matt Jackson, maintaining it's achromatic white shade and it's ramrod status. Emerged over him was Sivasian Krikorian, pistol drawn out, holding the gun in firm position, and readying his pointer finger to be pressed. Stone stood motionless but only giving Sivasian the chance to pull the trigger, having Stone cry out.. 'NO!!' With all the commotion exiting the Misogynist's mouth, only a slightly audible click was produced. Stone came back as Sivasian cackled, discarding the gun and holding his hips. 'Americans.' He stated as while abating his laugh, stomping over the deceased Matt Jackson and nearing Stone, who paced back and secretly held his bloodied blade to the side of him. As they continued on in the immured chamber scene it suddenly changed in to the Bainbridge church, where Stone Burns regularly but confidentially visited. He looked around but it was vacuous but as that distracted his mind The Soviet Sniper, Sivasian clenched a fist and jammed it in to his jaw. Stone staggered back, getting hit once again, spewing blood from his mouth. Stone fell on his back, leaping right back up but this time Sivasian had gone somewhere different. He scanned the church and it's heavens but nothing. Stone stood around, vigilantly moving his head around. But getting his attention was a missed bullet firing. Stone looked up and there was Sivasian, in a sniper-like position; lay flat on his chest, a long SR-25 in his grip and possession. He loaded the rifle once again, and aimed at Stone who scrambled, following the far from canorous rifle audio. He crawled in between the church benches, as Sivasian continuously and rapidly fired out reckless bullets, forming large and jagged holes in to the wooden-made benches. Sivasian seemed to be a maladroit sniper as he unsteadily fired the rounds. Stone leapt up; hoping to stay alive by running towards the exit, being followed by a barrage of bullets but all dodged by Stone. He reached the exit door but it abruptly transformed in to a blurry wall. The misogynist panicked further as he paced left and right; although guarded by the walls from Sivasian's sniping he pondered on how to get out. Finally, he rolled in to the rampage and scurried onward to see another exit door, which puzzled him but he went in a beeline for it. He got there unharmed but that also became a blur, not a door any longer but just an impassable blur. He circled around as the firing of the bullets ceased and he looked up around him but nothing. ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ He languidly came forward but nothing hit him. All seemed to be normal as the once blurred exit door became unblurred and the once holed benches were now amazingly unmutilated and rather well polished. He inhaled and exhaled the air left of his battles. But appearing before him were all three of his oppositions: James Edwards, Matt Jackson, and Sivasian Krikorian all athwart each other. Stone yielded as all of them held miniature yet authentic pistols. They stood in the chronological order of the quarrels. James Edwards of course coming first, with Matt Jackson aside from him and then Sivasian Krikorian. Stone shut his eyes as three straight bullets hit his chest, instantly killing him as his adversaries stood victorious. *OUT OF DAZE* Stone came awake from his dream, swinging his blade in fright but stopping. He viewed his Bainbridge pad but it was the same as always. But now he calmly thought as Turmoil came near, just days away, and his oppositions seemed to be more than ready to battle.
