LeBlanc back on track

22 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2-22-02 Scene opens with a helicopter fly-by of Amaranth Penitentiary, the maximum security prison in Truth or Consequences, New Mexico. The compound houses the nation’s most dangerous felons, more specifically, the “Swamp Dawg” Karl LeBlanc. The scene cuts to inside the prison weight room. LeBlanc and Warden Riley are seen standing inside their brand new wrestling ring. For the past two weeks, Warden Riley has subjected LeBlanc to a rigorous training program. Both men have been very disappointed with the Swamp Dawg’s 1 win/2 loss start in PCW. The Warden has decided to school LeBlanc in the art of submission holds. Considering Riley was an NCAA National Collegiate Wrestling Champion in his prime, the training sessions are expected to sculpt LeBlanc into a more complete, polished, bone-breaking machine. A reporter from The Squared Circle wrestling magazine enters the prison weight room. He approaches the training ring where LeBlanc and Warden Riley are sparring. Prison Guard: Excuse me, buddy… what the hell do you think you’re doing? You can’t be in here. Warden Riley: HEY, WHO IS THAT! GET HIM OUTTA HERE! Prison Guard: This is a closed training session. I’m gonna have to ask you to leave. Reporter: Easy now… no need to shove! Listen, I’m with The Squared Circle wrestling magazine. I have an interview scheduled with Mr. Riley. He agreed to it last week. I talked to him over the phone, and I’ve flown here from New York. PLEASE… just tell him who I am. The prison guard walks over to Warden Riley. He steps up onto the apron of the training ring and mumbles something to The Warden. Warden Riley: Oh, yeah… ok… I’ll be right with you. Go ahead and have a seat in my office and I’ll be right in. The reporter opens the door to Warden Riley’s office and takes a seat on the couch. Moments later the Warden walks in. He has a towel draped over his shoulders, which he uses to wipe the sweat off his brow. He turns to the reporter and speaks. Warden: Who the hell are you?! The reporter looks around as if The Warden might be speaking to someone else in the room. Warden Riley: Yes… I’m talking to you dumbass. The reporter is now thoroughly confused. Just two minutes ago, The Warden had told him to have a seat in his office. Now, he is acting like he had never seen him! Warden: What the hell are you doing in my office?! Reporter: You told me to come in here and sit down! You just saw me 30 seconds ago! Warden: Yeah, well I don’t know who the hell you are, son. Now get the hell off my alligator-skin couch you sonofabitch. I’m gonna have to throw the damn thing away now that your dirty ass has touched it. Reporter: I’m sorry sir, I didn’t know. You just told me to have a seat in your office, and well… the only seat in here is this couch. Warden: Well, sit your ass on the floor, boy. Now, what do you want? Reporter: Well, ummm… just a few questions. Where has the Swamp Dawg been? He has been on the PCW scene in over a week. Warden: There’s good reason for that. We’ve been training. Reporter: Training? What kind of training? Warden: Wrestling… grappling… submissions… this guy is gonna be the best all-around wrestler in the goddam world when we’re through. Reporter: All-around wrestler? You know a lot of people are criticizing his lack of mic skills. In order to be an all-around superstar, the guy is gonna have to talk. It’s all about adding depth to the character. Warden: There’s been plenty of superstars in the wrestling world who ascended to the top of the profession without speaking much. Reporter: Yeah, but he isn’t talking at all. Warden: Piss off. Reporter: Don’t be so defensive. I’m just trying to help. Warden: Did I ask for your help? If I did I’ve forgotten. I have a tendency to do that. Reporter: No sir. Warden: So… when are you leaving my office? Reporter: Well… uh, just a few more questions. LeBlanc has a match this Sunday against Hank and Jason Core. It’s another shot at the Extreme title. There’s a lot at stake. Hank outsmarted LeBlanc in their first match and beat him... then, we all saw Jason Core attack LeBlanc last week. What’s the mind-set entering this match up?Warden: All hell is gonna break loose. You ever seen a dog fight? That’s what it’s gonna look like. We’ll bring LeBlanc to the ring, “release him” he won’t say a word, but he’ll attack like a viscious pit bull. I feel sorry for Hank and Jason Core. They don’t know what they’re in for. The past 10 days have been the most intense training I’ve seen a man go through since I was in the Marines. Reporter: So he’ll be ready? Warden: He’ll be champion. Scene fades as the reporter nods his head. The Warden leans back in his chair.
