Lord of the Amulets: The Elves

Date: ..... We waited in the tall grass for a couple of minutes, even after we heard no sound of the horse anymore. We didn’t talk to each other either in case someone was still there. When I was sure that no one was behind us and far enough in front of us, I whispered. “Let’s go.” AJ ‘Pippin’ Palmer looked side-ways at me from the spot where he was laying. I glanced at him a few times and he had his head down, probably praying that nothing would happen. AJ nodded to me and I looked to my left, the opposite side of Pippin to where Sam was. He had his head down, half way to the ground, staring at a small piece of grass where a grasshopper sat on, for some reason, also staying still. I poked at Jesse ‘Sam’ Ramey once and then twice when he didn’t turn. Either he was in shock or he wasn’t paying attention. Jesse was known to have great hearing and it wouldn’t surprise me if he was listening for the sound of hooves in the distance. “Jesse!” I whispered louder, almost at a regular voice. I looked the other way at Pippin who was just as clueless as me. She shrugged her head at me. I got up into kneeing position to stand up to maybe get Jesse’s attention. I was almost at a full stance when a leg tripped me and pulled me down. “Wait! It’s coming back!” The voice was that of Jesse’s who was now back to normal. I got into my fixed position behind some tall grass and got comfortable again before, in a very small voice, whispered. “The horse?” I looked at Ramey so he wouldn’t have to speak. He nodded to me, once again getting really low. Palmer, on the other side of me was also getting lower so I followed them, getting lower and lower until I was at the lowest possible and could see between two tall stalks of grass. Now I could hear it too. A light clumping of horse’s hooves could be heard at a distance, maybe one hundred or two hundred feet ahead. Up the road was a small hill that was blocking the way of seeing anything coming until it was at the peak of the hill, coming down. Sitting in the clearing for about a minute without making any sort of sounds whatsoever was hard. Something was poking at my chest I ever so often, I tried shooing it away so it wouldn’t bug me anymore. Sam kept glaring at me so after some noise, in one swift motion, I pulled the stalk out, satisfied. The black rider came over the hill, on the road where it had been. It rode passed us once and then twice, as if waiting for us to show ourselves. We all kept as quiet as ever. Horse paced back and forth. Was he waiting for someone or something? In the distance, there was a new sound. It was a low humming noise and a bunch of footsteps coming. Looking at Sam for a reaction from him was hard because his face was once again buried. I looked at Pippin though and he was as scared as I was, if not more. The humming got louder and louder and was high-pitched when it was close up. The horse suddenly stopped pacing when the humming sound was getting quite loud. In a flash, the horse took off down the road, the opposite way of the music that was coming just over the hill the opposite way. The horse could no longer be seen and was off as the source of the singing and humming was shown. It was about ten or fifteen elves! I couldn’t believe what I was seeing. Never would I have thought that real elves existed. The leader of the elves looked rather wise and when he passed us he stopped. I looked at Pippin and Sam, getting worried once again. “Get up! I come in peace!” The leader of the elves moved apart the tall grass so sun shone on all three of us. We got up, not sure what to do. The elf had to be friendly, I didn’t know, I hadn’t ever met an elf before! “Hello?” It was Sam to talk first. All three of us had looked at the elves, about ten or twelve of them and how they all stood there without talking or joking, yet they seemed like they joked and laughed a lot. “I am Gildor the elf and I am the leader of this pack. I have heard of you!” He had heard of us? When we left, not many people knew about what we were doing and now I find out that someone we didn’t even know, knew about our trip! That isn’t very likely to know but oh well, things can happen. “You have heard of us?” It was Jesse who had spoken. Sam was usually the most nervous but yet he always seemed to break silence in any room. “Yes, we have heard of you from the one named Gandalf,” started Gildor. Already he had gotten my attention, just by saying the name of Mark ‘Gandalf’ Makonie. Gandalf continued, “It is very likely that my group has crossed your path. We shall now make a great and notorious feast for you must be tired from your hard work. Lets us find a clearing.” Gildo lead the way through small shrubs and I followed beside him while AJ and Jesse followed behind us and then finally the elf group, who hadn’t talked out loud but were now laughing and whispering amongst each other, no doubly about me and the journey I was making for the amulet. Gildor leaded us into a clearing, about the 10 by 12 feet, large enough for the huge picnic blanket t obe layed out that the elves had broughten along with them. Each elf had a pack on there backs and when Sam and Jesse went to help the elves create a meal, me and Gildor sat on the opposite side talking. I soon realized that what the elves were carrying was a lot more than it looked! A huge feast was soon being cooked with portable objects and gadgets. Me and Gildor sat in the corner, talking. “I have heard of your journey and fear danger.” Gildor got straight to the topic of the amulet and I was interested in listening. “Why do you fear danger?” “I have heard of the black riders coming this way. I don’t know when they will pass here and it is good that we crossed paths when we did or I fear the worst.” Gildor obviously hadn’t heard of the black riders and how they already did come by us! “But the black riders DID cross our paths. Right before you came, a big, black, horse came with a rider who’s face could not be seen. He stopped right in front of us and then you came, singing which drove them off which is the reason why you found us hiding in the bushes.” “I see! It all makes sense now!” said Gildor, “We have less time then I thought. The black riders are driven off, at least till you pass the ferry to Buckland where Brickethollow is.” There was finally some good news about the black riders. News that I can live with. Soon thereafter, supper was served and we all ate, laughing and cheering with the talk of the amulet behind. Into the night, we all stayed up reminiscing about the king back at the castle and Mark Makonie, too. Everything was fine for the time being, but the only question is. Is Mark ‘Gandalf’ Makonie still at the castle with the king or has he taken a trip of his own?
