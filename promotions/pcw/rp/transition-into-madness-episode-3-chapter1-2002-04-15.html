Transition Into Madness: Episode 3, Chapter1

15 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: April 15, 2002 (Brief OOC: This seems to be taking place at the IPPV, but don't worry.....I'll explain why in the next installment. Just wanted to point it out before I got in trouble for anything. Enjoy the RP.) Jack crashed to the ground in a tizzy, a warm stream of blood seeping from the corner of his busted lip and trickling from his chin onto the dirt. He cringed, uttering an indignant gasp as he clutched his throbbing ribs through his tattered shirt and watched the dripping blood mix into the dirt to form a sticky pool of brownish crimson muck. Anguished cries and groans echoed from every direction, meshing with the ringing of clashing metal and bone impacting flesh. Jack lifted his tired eyes and his aching neck to gaze with hazed vision on the towering frame of Jay Sylo. Sylo too appeared in a daze by this point in the battle, staggering several steps backward and leaning his weight against a mound of compacted cars. “As long as consciousness sparks me,” snarled the looming Sylo, “I won’t relinquish the title of Hardcore Icon. Especially not to a second-rate punk like you, Jack!” VJ grimaced, dragging himself to his feet and to a steadfast posture despite his wobbly legs. He wiped the corner of his lip and sneered. “F*ck you, Sylo,” he growled defiantly. “Nothing will stop me from claiming my crown! Especially not a lumbering, half-brained primate!” Sylo tore a bent chunk of tail-pipe from the chassis of a flattened car, VJ spinning around a picking up a dislodged car hood from earlier on. Meanwhile Rent-A-Hero and Darkstar passed behind him, exchanging a flurry of blows before RAH inexplicably bumps Jack and knocks him slightly offbase. Jack stumbled, quickly gathering the presence of mind to duck and fall into a commando roll as Sylo’s pipe cuts through the air where VJ’s head had previously been. It arcs around, nearly grazing the skull of Darkstar as he crashes to the ground with a grunt and avoids attacks by RAH and Steve Studd, who had burst out of virtually nowhere with a tire in his grasp. VJ arose to face Sylo, disarmed but taking high ground by scaling the pile of compressed vehicles. “C’mon, King Kong!” provoked Jack. “Ya want me? Here I am!” Sylo reared back with the pipe, preparing to bound up the stack of cars after his adversary. Several hundred yards off a thunderous explosion resounded through the night and drowned out the screams and shouts of the Junkyard match competitors, automobile parts and stripped car frames hurtling skyward before descending in a virtual shower of twisted metal to rain down on the baffled competitors. Competitors took cover around the ransacked junkyard, finding shelter underneath the rusted skeletons of cars and shielding themselves from the falling metal behind mountains of compacted vehicles. “Wow,” mumbled Jack as he covered his head with his arms. “That was a hell of a technique. I didn’t know Meanstreak was in this match…..” “That wasn’t no Meanstreak,” breathed Sylo. “Either some bastard brought a sh*tload of C4 to this shindig or we’re under attack by something…..” “Taliban?” retorted VJ in a sarcastic undertone. “Maybe we’re intrudin’ on Bin Laden’s hideaway or somethin’....” “What the f*ck is that??!!” Sylo and VJ crawled out from beneath their crude refuge at the sound of the bewildered cry and gazed into the distance at the epicenter of the blast. Through the gnarled metal beyond they could make out the towering silhouette of something set against the pale light of the crescent moon. The view provided a sketchy outlook at best, but whatever it was appeared to have multiple tentacle-like appendages that whipped erratically from both sides of its (body?). “In the name of all that is holy,” iterated a mystified Sylo. “When the hell did the Primetime Central big-wigs set this one up?!” “I don’t think they did,” replied Jack, trying to keep his composure. “I got a bad feelin’ about this….” He watched in horror as a group of shadows climbed the two heaps of scrap on either side of the thing, whipping poles like javelins at its gelatinous body. Some bounced off while others appeared to be absorbed into its jelly-like flesh, protruding outward and impaled on its outer skin(?) With a furious roar that may have shaken the heavens themselves, the tentacles groped outward and cast the screaming match competitors from their mounds and onto the floor of the junkyard. “Alright, this is bad…” muttered Jack. “Where the hell is the exit?!” Sylo shrugged. “This place is almost like a maze. Damn big scrapyard…..” Several other of the match competitors swept out from their concealment spots behind the mesmerized Violence Jack and Jay Sylo. “Holy Shiznittlebam!” exclaimed Rent-A-Hero. “I’ve come up against some freaky super villains in my time, but this is ridiculous!....” His comment was abruptly interrupted by a barrage of shrill wails from all around the assembled wrestlers. Their eyes all darted around, trying to pinpoint the source of the spine-tingling howls. Some clutched pipes and fragments of windshield glass, others wielding armaments they had carried into the match themselves. “Look at that!!” cried Steve Brent, jutting his finger westward between two lofty piles of scrap. A three-foot imp of sorts gawked at the cluster of wrestlers with apparent interest. Or so it appeared to gawk at them as no visible eyes adorned its stocky frame, yet its mass of thick tendrils whipped around excitedly and discernible tendons beneath its thin pulpy flesh tensed and stretched accordingly. It shambled along on what appeared to be three slender legs, and after emitting another shrill howl began to jabber loudly in an alien tongue, indiscernible to the group of confused wrestlers. There was no visible mouth per se, but a crevice adorned the center its upper body with two thick tusks sticking out from either side. “That’s a new one on me,” said Jesse Ramey as he grasped a tire iron. “Anyone mind filling me in on exactly what the f*ck that is?!” “Uh-oh….” Metal clattered and clinked all around them, the mass of men glancing around at their surroundings with trepidation mounting in their hearts. They had been surrounded from all directions, with a small horde of the beasts on each flank and a collection mounted on a junk heap to their rear. Several curses rung out among the confused individuals as they peered about their surroundings. The only visible escape route appeared to be directly ahead with the looming monstrosity in the distance. “Stuck between a rock and a hard place,” said Jack. “This might get ugly….” One of the squat fiends bounded from the peak of the east scrap pile, descending on Kwik FX and Rhett Locke. Locke parried the thing away with a thrust of a thick cylinder in his hands, Darkstar stepping in and nearly cleaving the monster in half with a sharp-edged hunk of metal as it crashed into the ground with a squeal. The razor-edged plate bit through its soft flesh and a foul-smelling black fluid sprayed forth in a thick jet. The noxious odor filled the air and several of the wrestlers resisted the urge to vomit, barely noticing at first as the demise of their brethren caused the horde of aggressive monstrosities to attack. The cluster of men dissipated about the junkyard as they fought to repel their adversaries, valiant war-cries rising forth in response to the life-or-death struggle now facing the competitors. The air was filled with the shrill shrieks of the otherworldly invaders, communicating (what one would consider) pleas of help and mock war cries. The various wrestlers fought valiantly on behalf of their own lives and even attempted to bail out others as needed. “Call me crazy,” shouted Blade Biohazard to RAH as he fended off one of the tentacled devils, “but I don’t think this was planned to happen. I’ve seen some absurd gimmick matches in my time, but if anyone can dig me up one where a pack of horror-movie reject monsters assaulted the wrestlers then I’ll give ‘em a hundred bucks.” The first thought that popped into VJ’s mind was obvious: the prophecies he had foreseen had been initiated. The hideous revelations haunting his dreams, all the ludicrous claims disclosed to him by Craven and McCarver and Stryker’s rogue unit, the Earthly rise of the numerous deities to strike around the world…..all coming to fruition.His soliloquy was broken as one of the screaming beasts tore like a bullet at him, exuding a revolting green foam from pores on its thrashing tentacles. Jack wiped a thin film of rust from the pipe he had just torn from a downed car, going into a one-eighty degree spin and sweeping its legs out with a low attack from his weapon. It collapsed with a squeal and Jack plunged the jagged tip of the metal pipe into what could only be described as its head. He raised his head in response to a low-end snort emanating from behind him, just in time to watch two of the minute devils pounce on him. He reached out to grasp the pipe now protruding from the gullet of his victim, one of the tentacles only inches from his face and the tip of the tusks gleaming as they caught the moonlight. An iron rod whistled through the air like a javelin, impaling one of the snarling fiends in mid air. VJ ducked as the other sailed over his head, landing harmlessly behind. VJ glanced to the side to catch Sylo rush up and punt the dwarf monster with a swift boot from his massive foot. It crashed into a heap of decaying metal, rust and scraps collapsing down onto it. “I don’t like you, you ugly bastard,” quipped Sylo as he wrenched the shaft from the carcass of its victim and wiped off a stream of black liquid. “But I’ll be damned if I job on account of a gang of alien things, or whatever the hell these are……” Sylo and Jack turned to lay eyes on a pack of the things gathered in cleared out area several yards away. One with lighter flesh and pale golden rings encircling its core stood at the front, squealing in a deeper pitch than its counterparts before falling silent. “Why must you continue to resist? It is futile.” The garbled voice echoed in Jack’s mind, Sylo apparently not receiving the same warning as he remained undaunted. VJ nearly his grip on his pipe as he faltered momentarily under the intense pressure that had suddenly enveloped his brain. “Soon Our Lord will arise and destroy your world, Jack. Your soul, your mind, your every thought….they will become His to bend and alter at will. Our hostility is not unfounded, nor is it of an ‘evil’ nature as your mankind defines the term. This world belongs to us. It is rightfully ours. And as such, Our Lord has decreed we cleanse it, starting here and now. Submit to us and yield your spirits to Our Lord, and receive your salvation!” “I’ll give you f*ckin’ salvation!” roared VJ, bringing up his pipe as the crowd of beasts spread out to rush the bold duo. “Then for your insolence, Our Lord dictates you die.” A frenzied burst of gunfire abruptly tore through the ranks of the onrushing pack of creatures, sending many collapsing to the ground in crumpled heaps of bullet-riddled flesh. Jack glanced right to survey a small troop of armed soldiers in military attire charge from behind a mass of stacked metal, a vast portion of the junkyard now lit up as they subdued the beasts under the frantic barrage of fire. High-pitched wails echoed above the relentless volley, many wrestlers opting to hit the dirt to avoid being struck by friendly fire. Jack shifted his attention momentarily to bludgeon one of his attackers with a thick pipe, sending it rolling along the ground and slamming into a metal plate jutting from the ground. “Hell of a fight you boys had going on here!” Jack swiveled his head to look on the amused face of Marcus Stryker, donned in his battle dress attire with a rifle cradled in his arm and a pinwheel-like weapon dangling at his side. He caressed his sweat-coated hair for a moment before glancing around, the intense struggle in the background reduced to isolated bursts and individual shots mixed with chilling death-wails. Sylo sneered at the new arrival. “Who the f*ck are you? Rambo?!” He trounced away to join a cluster of wrestlers as they merged together several yards away, Stryker beckoning VJ to follow with a nod of the head. “Mind tellin’ me what the f*ck happened?!” demanded Jack as he sauntered angrily behind the mysterious operative. “I’d like to have an idea how a whole posse of godd*mn rejects from a bad HP Lovecraft story got in here and tried to kill us!” “We were tracking their movements for over a week, and we found a disturbing pattern emerging. It seemed a whole colony made a mass underground pilgrimage to this area within the past several days. We did some checking and discovered this was the site of the Junkyard match for that wrestling company you’re in. Thank God we got here in time, I shudder to think of the consequences. Thankfully there weren’t quite as many of them as we anticipated. And bless the heavens the Yauk-Gth’eyh are one of weaker species under that accursed Shudde-M’ell….basic ‘foot soldiers’.” “Well marvelous,” mocked VJ. “If those suckers are such push-overs would you please enlighten me what that other thing was?? The relieved look on Stryker’s face abruptly faded to be replaced by pallid dread. He stood up quickly and grasped VJ by the shoulder, a trickle of blood running onto his hand from a wound on the Boston thug’s neck. “What ‘other thing’?!” he demanded urgently. VJ cocked an eyebrow, turning back to look at the point distant in the junkyard where the monstrosity had initially loomed. Mountains of scrap dotted the landscape and partially blocked the view of the waning moon, but no towering beast remained. “Well it’s not there now….” “What was it?! bellowed Stryker, spinning VJ around to face him. “I don’t know, Marcus! You’re the damn expert, you tell me! Looked like a giant freakin’ octopus or something…..tentacles all over it, exploded out of the ground with the power of a ton of dynamite…” Stryker shouted a loud curse, fumbling at his belt before grabbing a walkie-talkie and pulling it up. VJ became uneasy. “Alpha One to White Crow, Alpha One to White Crow, do you copy?!” There was a shrill hum of static before a voice interjected. “Yo Stryker, Norton here. What’s the problem?” “It’s a friggin’ ambush! We’ve been set up! They knew we were coming! Evac team in here STAT!!” “Copy here Marcus, deploying evac squad. I’m only a couple hundred yards off, I’m on my way. Out.” Stryker clipped the device back onto his belt before handing the bladed pinwheel to VJ. He drew up his rifle and cupped one hand around his mouth. “On alert!” he commanded assertively in a booming voice as his subordinates turned to look. “We may have trouble!” He cast a wary glance at Jack while readjusting his fingers on the weapon. “You sure it wasn’t your imagination? If it wasn’t then this could potentially be very bad……” “I know what I saw and I wasn’t f*ckin’ hallucinating! What is it....??” He halted in mid-sentence as the ground began to rumble below their feet, uprooting plates of steel wedged in the ground and toppling over nearby piles of compacted cars. Stryker expressed a muffled curse and staggered slightly before raising his weapon. “Jesus....”
