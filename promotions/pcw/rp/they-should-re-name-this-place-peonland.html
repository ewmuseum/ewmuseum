They Should Re-Name This Place Peonland.

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 22/07/02 (#1 for PPV). Everyone has always known that Xavier Kannon is as malicious as they come in PCW. At 229 pounds he is not a big, domineering monster... He doesn’t even outweigh half the roster... But as far as minds go, he has the roster beat. Seemingly every second of the day the cogs are turning within his head, plotting and scheming.... Usually of ways to ambush and attack his rivals. Since he failed to regain his Continental Title at Gold & Glory, this vicious streak as intensified... He has ambushed the Pharaoh on many occasions, always managing to take him by surprise and leaving him bloodied and beaten.. But at Turmoil, Titu Xansiv got revenge as he returned from Egypt, where Kannon send his mummified body, to cost Kannon a match against his bodyguard Zen Dula. While before he would have eliminated the thought of this event from his head, this is a new Kannon, a more evil Kannon and a Kannon who will not let anyone get the upper hand on him... So rather than taking the thought from his mind, he took Zen from PCW... Ambushing the 7 foot giant and electrocuting his body while it was tied to a fence as PCW security desperately tried to get to him... .... Now with Wrestle Vessel only 6 days away, all thoughts of Zen are gone, and his mind has switched back to the Pharaoh, the man he must lock inside a Sarcophagus to gain when he has always wanted... A 1 on 1 shot at the PCW World Heavyweight Title. “My Hubbard... This place is worse than my Scientological brain could ever have imagined....” Our shot opens up on the familiar face of PCW’s resident malicious Scientologist Xavier Kannon... His features contorted into a look of pure, unadulterated disgust. His nose turned up. His eyes staring with a mix of pity and contempt. His mouth open to breath the air without smelling it. “Like.... SO... Ewwwwwwwwwwwzies!” Zooming away from Kannon’s face we are shown that he is standing beside his wife, and PCW’s Preppy valet Eleanor Kannon-Hall... Her face showing signs of even more disgust as she visibly tries not to vomit. Eleanor : “I am like, SO hating you right now for bringing me here....” The camera zooms out even further to show a clear, bright Blue sky in the background behind them... Not a cloud in site and the horizon only broken into where the tops of some shabby buildings are seen. Eleanor : “.... In fact.... Why in the name of Preppyness are we here anyway?... It’s like rank.... And icky.... And rank again...” Pulling right back from the couple we see that they are standing on a hard mud street somewhere in the back streets of Cairo, Egypt.... Capital of the North African country that his arch nemesis the Pharaoh calls home... As well as his wife Isiy and their now comatose Bodyguard Zen Dula, who was viciously beaten and electrocuted at the hands of the Scientologist on Friday night. Xavier : “Because my disgusted little Poon Puppy... My shining beacon of beauty in this refuse tip of a country, this is the home of Titu. THIS glorified waste dump contains HIS people.... Urgh, it’s like claiming a maggot infested corpse as yours...” Ellie~! looks at his disgusted face, not quite understanding him. Eleanor : “As mine?” She looks around at the dirty, dusty street... The sides of which wallow large puddles of stool.... Outside every building emaciated children sit with their dirty faces. Her face screws up even more as she replies in her trademark whiny voice. Eleanor : “But I don’t want it!” They both then pull a scarf around their lower face... Covering their noses and mouths as they venture further down the street and Kannon locks eyes on a dirty, rank and disgusting street corner where a lone woman stands, touting herself to business.... ... Ellie~! takes one look at her and shudders as Kannon’s upper face gives away his chuckle under the protective scarf. Xavier : “Well that would explain Isiy coming from here....” As a fat, sweaty and dirty man shoves currency into her hand and forcibly pushes her into the makeshift hut behind her the 1st couple of Scientology walk further.. Eleanor making a point of turning to her husband and pointing to where the Egyptian whore once stood. Eleanor : “Before you get any ideas we are SO not doing THAT as role-play...” Each is now looking to their side of the street, attracting attention from the locals who aren’t used to seeing 2 such well dressed foreigners in their region. Xavier : “I think I’ll get some of the higher-ups to request that the United Nations force this place to have it’s name changed to PEONLAND....” All around them are sites of abject poverty and substandard living that might tug at the heart strings of any normal person... But to this couple they are merely disgusting scenes that they can mock and turn their nose at. Xavier : “Where in Xenu’s name does all that damn Charity money GO?!?!?! Everytime I turn on a TV outside Hubbardville they run the guilt trip on us. If they’re THAT desperate I’ll personally pay for this whole place to be levelled.... As long as they don’t move the people out first. Beside him Ellie~! lets out a giggly, spiteful chuckle at her husband’s words, sharing the same lack of respect for basic human rights as she does. Eleanor : “Yeah.. Then they’d like all, SO die!!!” He pale Blue eyes look at the downbeat faces of those who inhabit the area.. Skin ages way beyond it’s years and looks of no hope are all too frequent... Something the Prep just sneers at. Eleanor : “Icky... If I were in charge, I’d just like, kill them all and move clean people in here.... Why don’t they do that, that’d SO make it better...” Kannon looks down at his shoe and screws his face up, shaking something brown and crumbly from his foot as he stops and replies to his elitist wife. Xavier : “Because, my little Shag Squirrel... That would be Genocide...” She takes another look at the depths of poverty around her before continuing to walk alongside her man once again. Eleanor : “Well I say Genocide is good... More Genocide is what the world needs... YAY for GENOCIDE!!!” She does a little cheerleader dance for the act of mass murder, something likely to ONLY be seen from Ellie~!... She suddenly stops bouncing and clapping as Xavier looks to her with a bemused look on his face. Eleanor : “What?” Before Xavier can reply however.... One of the older women who are working, washing clothes or crafting cheap souvenirs on the side of the street rushes over to them, waving her arms and shouting out something in Arabic. Eleanor : “Um... Like... Speaker the American?” As Ellie~! and Xavi look to one another, confused, the woman continues to speak loudly to them, her old, tired and weary eyes showing a certain fondness to the couple. Eleanor : “Oh no.. I think she wants us to take her home.... Like SHE’D get through quarantine!” Both of them look at the women, not really knowing what to say to the rambling woman... Both of their upper bodies bending back to try and avoid her touching them. Xavier : “Maybe she’s related to the Pharaoh.. Or Zen...” He then shouts at her, hoping his raised voice will make her suddenly learn English and understand him... Something that must have rubbed off on him from Ellie~! Xavier : “ARE YOU A XANSIV OR DULA?” She ceases her rambling and looks at him confused... Old Mad Egyptian Woman : “Dula?......... Xansiv?...” Kannon nods, as does Ellie~!, looking at the woman as if she were a dumb pet. Xavier : “IF YOU ARE... I’VE REALLY BEEN ENJOYING KICKING THE CRAP OUT OF YOUR SON.... AND IF THAT BIG GOOF WA-... IS YOUR SON, THANK YOU FOR GIVING BIRTH TO HIM... ELECTROCUTING HIM WAS THE MOST FUN I’VE HAD IN AGES!” As the woman leans her head to the side, still confused, Ellie~! folds her arms in a huff and stamps a foot. Eleanor : “I WON’T take that as an insult THIS time...” The woman then drops to her knees, holding out her cupped hands and rambling on even more in her own tongue as the couple look to each other.... Ellie~! opening hers wide and fluttering her eyelids.. But Kannon immediately reads her intention. Xavier “No, we CAN NOT keep her...” She tries to argue her cause as the woman continues to ramble. Eleanor : “I know she’s all Ewwwwwwwww and everything, but she’s funny... Like an old wrinkled Furby!” The elderly woman thrusts her cupped hands further towards the couple, causing Ellie~! to leap behind Kannon for cover and peeking over his shoulder. Old Mad Egyptian Woman : “Dollar.......... Dollar?” Oddly, Xavier’s face softens, and almost becomes sympathetic to her cause. Xavier : “You want Dollar?” The woman nods, her eyes looking that little more alive and a smile breaking out on her face... One of her hands reaching out to touch Xavier’s face but it immediately recoils as the Scientologist bellows at her. Xavier : “THEN IN HUBBARD’S NAME GET A DAMN JOB!!!!” The frail old woman scurries off as Kannon shakes his head and Ellie~! emerges from behind him, laughing at the sight. Eleanor : “Hehehe.. You made her cry!” The Gold Patron Meritorious takes his wife in arm and they walk off down the street as the shot starts to fade out... Kannon’s eyes looking a bit worried as every face on the street turns to them with angry expressions. Xavier : “Ellie~!.....” His wife turns to him as we finally hit Black. “Huh?” “RUNNNNNNNNNNNN!!!!!!!!”
