Gaz just wants some attention

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 20 March &#039;03 Gaz, you had to open your mouth and make a pitiful little comment at March Madness, didn't you? What could of possibly possessed you to do it? Maybe it was because you weren't thinking straight. Maybe you were drunk. I think I know the real reasons you opened your mouth though. Just before I explain to the viewers out there why you opened your mouth, may I suggest you get checked out for Makonie/Green Syndrome checked out. You have all the classic symptoms; unintelligence, being naive, and being a bitch. You also demonstrated the fact that you have it with your little comment about me being able to see what happens in other people's promos. Guess what, since they're promos of course I can see what's happening in them, they're on TV after all. For a further explanation of Makonie/Green Syndrome I suggest you find yourself a copy of my promo addressing the syndrome, and then I suggest getting it checked out. Oh and as for the 'Chet King effect', I don't think you understand what the Chet King effect really is. It's where I whoop little bitches who don't even deserve to be in the same ring as me, which is something you know from first hand experience, don’t you? Which brings me to my first reason as to why you had a brain malfunction and decided to open your mouth. You're still crying over your loss to me. Get over it. You knew, just as everyone did, that you never had a chance against me. You knew what was going to happen, you knew you were going to be humiliated. Stop crying about it Gaz, you lost. You never were going to win and you never will. You still managed to get yourself into the battle royal, despite the fact that it did tarnish the battle royals image as it made people think that any bitch who pretends to be a wrestler is allowed in. Nonetheless you still got in, so stop crying over your loss to me. Then again, I guess you do have a new reason to cry, you saw my top four performance while you in the back after getting eliminated. You have every reason to be jealous Gaz, but from now on I suggest you keep your little comments to yourself so I don't have to embarrass you further. Another reason you opened your mouth was because you want some attention. You want attention from any body. You want attention from the fans, who don't give two shits about you. The only reason people ever made a little bit of noise when PWA entered the arena is because of your little vampire friend. Even then no one took PWA seriously, or gave them any respect. The fans didn't care about your un noteworthy run as a singles competitor, and even though your back in tag team action they still don't care about you. It's no wonder you opened your mouth, you just wanted a little bit of attention from the fans. You also wanted attention from me, which I'm giving you, so I guess you should consider yourself lucky that I've taken time out of my busy schedule to give a bitch like you some attention. I mean I've got other little insects like yourself to stomp over, namely El Romantico, but I'm still nice enough to give you a little bit of attention. The person you really want attention from though is Xavier Kannon. When you first joined the Front Office Xavier was paying attention to you by teaming with you for the tag titles. Over the next couple weeks though Xavier realized that you weren't worth putting effort into helping, and just left you and your little vampire friend to become Front Office lackeys #4 and #3 behind Tim Walden and Brandi. He realized the mistake he had made by hiring you in the Front Office, and has just left you to rust, and has decided to spend his time paying attention to helping the career of others, namely Ellie. How does it feel to know that Ellie is a bigger star then you'll ever be. People actually make a noise when they see her. People actually care about Ellie, unlike you. Don't take this as a knock soley on your part for not having wrestling skills, but take it as a knock to Xavier as well. Xavier's spending his time putting his wife out there, but has given up on you even if you don't deserve anything. Talk about a conflict of interests. It's no wonder you feel the need to get some attention by opening your mouth. Hopefully Xavier's going to pay some attention to you now, but if he doesn't just take it as meaning that he doesn't want to waste his time with you, and would rather spend his time trying to do all he can to help the career of wife aka slut aka ho aka whore Ellie. Gaz, I've given you the attention you so desperately needed, and I also hope you think before opening your mouth again. Do I expect a reply from you? Indeed I do as I don't expect you to think. Oh, by the way Gaz, have fun watching me win the World Championship at Turmoil after you lose to Enigma and Flaymin Aymin.
