Through The Eyes [3] Just Dessert

16 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 16.12.02 Date: August 14th, 1977Place: Buffalo, New York (Worchester Highway)Time: 11:21 PM (EST) The sweeping cars between each interchange made it more and more harder for Eric Jackson to find his way home. All he needed right now was a case of sleep, maybe a drink or two before he'd hit the gym in the morning. Hanson was asking for all of his wrestlers to get a strength test, not to mention a physical in case of the performance in the ring dwindles and he has merit reason to fire them. A smile couldn't be in his presence - he'd just lost a title he wasn't supposed to in the first place. For all of the arrogance inside of Eric, he knew it would end up being passed down from one generation towards another. With a sly smirk on his face, he cranked up the radio on his 74' neon Dodge Charger and let it rip from there. The heavy metal bass from Black Sabbth, the ripping chords of power bands relatively unknown like Great Acid Trip, and Just Cause made him wonder a bit in sort of a minute to minute voyage. It was clear that NEW had its shady deals going on in the back, for Chambers had something on Hanson to make sure he defeated Jackson, whether it be distinct or by complete faux. A thought would run through his mind and he wouldn't act by it, rather let it all pass. Changing lanes, he quietly purred the Charger into the left most lane, curving ever closer to the very edge of the road. Taking the exit, he ran down Hiens Street with reckless abandonment, not stopping. Residental neighbors and those of war hero and quiet fame stayed here, and they weren't in for the hot wings. Pulling into the small duplex on Covington, he killed the engine with a flick of the wrist. Not a clue into where he was going, he slung the gray duffel bag over his shoulder, carrying with him a replica of the NEW United States Championship. His belt. The one they took from him. As he keyed open the door, a voice ran through. 'Son, you home?' 'Yeah,' he whispered to himself, before flicking on a distant light. Inside of the room were the pictures of his various accomplishments, his father Jack Jackson had been a pioneer of professional boxing, 132-4 as a professional, before retiring in 1976, still the World Lightweight Champion. Well into his sixties, he was a Golden Bear of sort, rugged with high cheekbones and a mean left hook that took down opponents like a lawn mower. 'I'm here.' Jack Jackson may have been an old man, but Eric knew where he'd be at the end of the night, inside of his room looking at old boxing relics. Tossing the bag down, Eric sighed as the ever alert elder smiled to himself. 'Hard night at the office?' he commented. Eric turned his head, reaching for a glass of brandy. 'Way more than a hard night, pop. You can call it an abortion.' 'Well,' the older and much wiser Jackson began. 'you took this sport up. I wanted you to box, yet I guess Rogers got to you before I did.' A nod from Eric, 'Yeah - its like that. Instead of being some Aaron Pryor, one of those soon to be heavyweight champs, I wanted the glitz and glory of an NBA star, along with the same knowance that you could punish people and smile about it.' Jack looks down at the coffee table, sights and memories of how his son used to play, and frolic around children, shadow boxing as if he were his father. Then the eyes of the sixty one year old man turn into those of his wife, Eric mother Alice. The woman he met while on the road in Louisville, Kentucky became his wife in 1953. Soon after, a year later in San Diego, California - the son was born. She died in a car crash in sixty eight, causing Jack to go in deep drinking and depression. 'I still miss her,' he quietly mused. 'That warm smile - delighted me every time I stepped into the ring.' Jack picked the picture up from their wedding day, a slight smile crossing his lips before clutching the picture close to his heart. A smile would grow, and then disperse from him. He layid the picture down, upper frame first down onto the coffee table, before sighing to himself, 'The past is such a pain.' 'Pop,' Eric started subtle before releasing himself from the couch. 'time for you to hit the sack. You've got press conferences and stuff like that to see you gets your title. Remember, you went out like a champion. A true champion.' Jack nods, 'Wish I was still in there knocking heads around. Can't believe a old coot like myself could take it up for thirty years.' Eric sighed, 'Wish I could too pop.' The elder Jackson got up from the chair, a slight callous grunt in his step as his gray hair matted down onto his head. In a walk, he went alway silenced and ready to wake up in the morning. Looking over at the picture of his mom, Eric smiled warmly, before plopping back in the chair himself. 'Nice to know you're known.' he briefly stated. 'Nice to know.' Date: August 15th, 1977Place: Buffalo, New York (Galum Auditorium)Time: 11:21 AM (EST) The press lined up from the front row on back, looking at a chance to see just what was going on. In the eyes of the people in the room, not a one of them was going to miss out on the action. A brief hush came around the auditorium, as Paul Hanson crept out of the back, a purple suit with black dress shirt underneath. Flanking him on the left happened to be Clive Owens, the NEW World Heavyweight Champion, mortally despised by the fans, and loved by reporters for giving them all they wanted in an interview. The backdrop around them was the NEW logo, bright red with bubble letters and the red, white, and blue colors circling them. The logo of one even caught the eye of every man and woman set to be reporting this. Clash of the Titans. Another clamor came out of the reporters as Eric Jackson parted the curtains, a pair of black shades on his face and a black Black Sabbath t-shirt on his athletic frame. In all of the ways of Paul Hanson, this was his crown jewel, the one event that put the city of Buffalo on the map of wreslting. One reporter spoke to some of his co-horts. 'Seems like Hanson's pulling our legs, no way is he going to book the former US Champ against the best that the fed has to offer, no fuckin' way!' 'Damn right, Rick.' another chipped in. 'If Hanson's booking this - he's gold. Maybe they can put the product out on national level.' 'No way, McMahon'll have a heart attack if he heard that.' one more joined the fray. 'He and his son'll hire folks to eliminate Hanson by any means possible.' Soon, the many cameras in the room lined up, as the owner stood up, a smirk on his face as the podium leaned in upon him. THe micrphone was placed neatly in the center of the ring, with a little hole cut out for all of the support wires and plugs that accompanied it. 'Good morning,' he said before feedback ushered into the room. Tapping the microphone a couple of times, he whispers into it with a gesture. 'Is this thing on?' 'Come on Pauly!' one reporter joked as the rest filled in with laughter. Hanson himself laughed, just to get the joke over as he spoke into the microphone once more. 'Well, as we all know on September 1st, Northeast Entertainment Wrestling celebrates its fourteenth year of existence with maybe the biggest Main Event to Clash of The Titans in history. And that is -' 'Clive Owens vs. Eric Jackson for the World Heavyweight title.' The cameras clicked over and over once more, causing Eric to even widen his eyes at the announcement. He couldn't believe that for one night, Hanson delievered what he said he would. Owens on the other side of the owner, smuggly smiled at the cameras. Before turning his six foot four, two hundred and sixty pound frame to the direction of the challenger. Owens chuckled, before spitting back down, Jackson doing the same. 'Now I know ladies and gentlemen that the usual treatment for the number one contendership to the World Heavyweight title is usually though the Batte Royal at the June event, but Jackson placed second in that, the man that finished first was the United States champion, Ron Chambers. Due to the fact that Mr. Chambers holds the US title at the moment, he cannot participate in the main event.' That was the bullshit excuse for Chambers not being in the main event, because if you were there when Jackson was basically given the chance to face Owens, then you would know what actually went down. 'Shut up Hanson,' the sneer in the voice of the champion was evident, of course he was the one whom these people thought about since the very day he became champion. Eight years come Clash of The Titans and a glorious eight years in the eyes of Owens. 'time for me to step up to the mic.' Owens lifted himself upward, heaving the gold plated NEW Title into the air, showing that he was teh champion, and there wasn't a soul who could beat him for it. 'This is still my title, and no greenhorn's gonna beat me for it. Ask Chambers, last month the little cunt tried his best, before even he fell. Just like this pussy did to him last night.' 'You know Clive,' Jackson cut the champion off before he could go on more of his tirade. In sense, this was Jackson's time to shine and not even Clive Owens was to be in the way of that. 'You're funny. Sure you came come out and blast me and Roland Hughes every week for 52 weeks straight, but then what'll happen when you run out of material? You'll probably start masturbating to Doc River's basketball tapes and be amazed at the cock and balls treatment you've been giving people for years now.' Clive smirked, hoisting the title over his shoulder easier. 'I could just beat the shit out of you right now, but since this will happen at Clash of The Titans, I'll wait.' 'Clive Owens is no better wrestler than Ron Chambers, Ron Chambers had skill and class, while Owens here drags around the ring, throwing slow right hands that'll take four minutes to hit you. He's like the mummy!' Laughter ensues, as Eric drags his feet around mummyish, just playing up to the reporters and press fans alike with an Ali impersonation. Its good for publicity. 'I want you all to check your watches, check em, because when it hits 911, time for the show to go, and you will be looking at your next champion - of the world!' Claps follow as Jackson bows for the press, camera fumes burning widly as flashbulbs pop like fans at a Aerosmith concert. Jackson sits back down, before eying Owens without much of a hard gimplse, just the knowingst fact that he indeed was no joke. Sure, Jackson could make all the jokes he wants about Clive, but Clive has punished people for running their mouths. He's a walking legend in the business, and arguably the very best NEW Heavyweight champion ever. 'Alright press,' Hanson went up to the microphone again. 'Show's over. We will see you on the 1st!' The press chuckle, before the screeching sound of over two hundred and forty chairs fill the room. The clamor of footsteps and voices are evident as the reporters from magazine to magazine and television fixture to television fixture can all sit and wait for September the 1st to roll around. As the reporters left, clapping could be heard from the podium as Hanson sat back down, straightening up his tie. 'Can we hurry this up?' Owens tapped his fingers upon the table. 'Some of us have a family to look after.' Hanson nugged Jackson in the shoulder, 'You awake over there?' 'Just fine,' Eric whispered. 'Fine enough to know what's going down.' 'Alright look,' Hanson began. 'This is the plan - you drag this out and make this look good. Damn good alright? Owens you pin Eric at the 8:50 mark, him kicking out for the two. You smack the mat hard, forcing the ref to come over for you two to start jaw jacking before you go to the outside. Grab a chair, go into the ring and swing at Eric. Eric, you duck and drop the Flashback on Owens for the 1-2-3. Got it?' An awkward stare ensued, both men looking down at the floor mat below them, not satisfied with the ending. 'Wait, get a chair? We should just get a submission finish for him to win, I lock in the clutch, he begins to fade, before he slips into the Flashback, 1-2-3.' 'Sorry, no can do.' 'Well, why not dammit?!' 'Because, if that finish goes off, nobody is going to realize that this kid has potential. You lay him out and then get the chair. I like that finish better.' Eric interjected. 'Well, Clive's idea runs better. Its him getting his finisher locked in and I slip out of it. I'll be the first -' 'No,' Hanson stopped him right there. 'Nobody is supposed to get out of the Clutch, and I mean nobody.' Eric scratched his head, 'I don't like this Paul. Last night you get the belt off of me just to serve me up for him? The hell is that?' 'Like I said, it'll all work out for the best.' Hanson smiled devilishly. 'But where's he gonna end up?' The question stopped Hanson dead in his tracks, like a freight train hitting the brakes before he was set to hit another. Hanson leaned back up against the gymnasium wall, running his fingers through his hair. 'Clive's gonna feud with this new kid we're bringing in - Ox. He's big, and fucking vicious. Give him some heat, and let the fans get to know Ox as a super monster.' 'That's dropping me down the fucking card!' Owens barked at Hanson. 'No fucking way am I going to lose the title and just go ahead and fuck with a greenhorn!' Hanson leaned forward, starring at Owens with intense eyes and a look of 'say one more thing and I will fuck you up', 'Shut up, and be a good boy.' 'Fuck you,' Owens snarled. 'That's the Clive Owens I know.' Hanson greeted with that same devil smile from earlier. No matter how he got there, or how the night would end, Eric Jackson was going to become the World Heavyweight champion. 'And kid,' Hanson turned around to Eric with gleeming eyes. 'Don't fuck this up, or its you getting the ox.' He meant to say ox if it were axe, but still the words from earlier rang true to his heart. A Champion, he was soon to become.
