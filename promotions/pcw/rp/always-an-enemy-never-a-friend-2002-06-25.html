Always an Enemy, Never a Friend

25 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: June 25, 2002 The scene opens to The Crimson, Night Shard and Blizzard all sharing a sandwich outside of a downtown deli where half of the store is frozen. Ice stalagmites hang from above the store sign and the streets are frozen, but that’s not enough to hold back our faithful heroes from sharing a sandwich. After all, when you have to eat, you have to eat. Night Shard: [takes another bite] What is this, pastrami? The Crimson: I believe it is ham, actually. The mustard used in the sandwich masked the tangy flavor usually found in pastrami. Blizzard: Well, honestly, I don’t care what’s in it. It’s absolutely delicious. I mean, this dill pickle really gets me going. Night Shard: You like dill pickle? It’s disgusting! I mean, how can you put something green in your mouth like that? Blizzard: [sticks his tongue out] You put a lot of stuff in your mouth, though, so I don’t see any problem for you. Night Shard rushes over to Blizzard to attack him, but Crimson sticks out his foot that trips Night Shard. He slams hard into the ice riddled street and moans in pain. Blizzard: [looks over at Crimson] Do you usually try to hurt your tag team partner like that? The Crimson: I am known on occasion to do so. I don’t believe my partner is thinking with his head half of the time. Are you hurt, Robert? Night Shard: [shakes his head] No thanks to you. This is hard ice man. I could have broken something. Then how would I have helped you beat up on Snow and RAH? Blizzard: I could have tagged with him. I think Crimson would have appreciated someone on his team that isn’t always pandering to the fans. The Crimson: That’s true, Robert. You do have that unhealthy tendency. Night Shard: [complaining] That only happened once! The incident in question was a dark match that the Crusaders of Light fought in a few nights before. They had the match well in hand, but as Crimson was wrestling in the ring, Night Shard began pandering to the fans and flashing his buttocks at them. As he did that, the other team double teamed Crimson and eventually got the roll up win. Night Shard turned around in time to find Crimson incredibly upset and ready to rip Night Shard into a million pieces. The Crimson: I don’t like to lose, Robert. Not ever. Night Shard: Oh, and I do. Come on, Thinh. I apologized for that, but it won’t happen again. We can’t lose to two jobbers like Snow and RAH. I mean, did you see that last promo from RAH? The Crimson: It was intriguing. Blizzard: I was confused. He was talking about people bathing in the blood of an innocent man’s life and his death. Who does he think he is? Superman? Night Shard: Apparently. But I think he’s going off the deep end. I mean, he wears a spiderman outfit. And Snow…oh my god, I can’t get started on Snow. The Crimson: The man is going through some personal problems. He is obviously dwelling on his inner persona. Night Shard jumps up in the air scheming. You see, that’s what he does best. Since he is by trade an accountant, his schemes are world renowned for being clever, yet never successful. Night Shard: We can use that against him. Why don’t we kidnap his wife or something so that he goes haywire and leaves PCW just like Borro did? The Crimson: [shakes his head] Need I remind you that we are the good guys? Good guys don’t kidnap wives. Night Shard: [throws up his hands] Well, I’m fresh out of ideas. I mean, it seems like all of the bad guys get the great scams and plans. Why aren’t we bad guys again? The Crimson: We fight the good fight, help the poor, give back to the community… Robert finally gives in and finishes his sandwich. As he does, he grabs a few papers from inside of the Crusader mobile. They are all warrants for Blizzard’s arrest. As Blizzard sees them, he winces in guilt. Blizzard: Look, I know what you’re going to say… The Crimson: I don’t think you do…[takes out his handcuffs]…you are wanted in several cities for the freezing of their down town areas. What do you have to say for yourself? Blizzard: We go way back don’t we? I remember when you guys approached me…ME…for a shot at joining this crusader group of yours. Tom even made my suit for me. It had icebergs on it and everything. Night Shard: But you didn’t join. You decided that it would be more fun to freeze us in our own car and let us freeze to death. But you weren’t so smart, were you? No, you decided to leave us a blow torch so we could melt our way out. Blizzard: [shrugs] Even the shrewdest villain has his day off. What can I say? The Crimson: There’s nothing we can do, Blizzard. You have to be stopped. The ice man began to back away and pointed his finger at both of them as if they were crazy. Blizzard: So after all of this, you still want to try to arrest me? I froze this deli so that we could have a nice lunch and catch up on old times. This is how you repay me? This is bullshit. Night Shard: We don’t play double standards. And we certainly don’t let menaces to society like you get away. Night Shard uses his ability to move objects with his mind to cause a trash can to fly from the ground and strike Blizzard in the side. Blizzard goes flying into the glass window of the deli and disappears. The Crimson: [annoyed] Couldn’t you have at least kept him on the streets? Now he can use his ice powers to stop us! Night Shard: It’s two against one. How hard could it be? The Crimson makes his way into the deli through the door, where he sees the remnants of the glass window shattered on the ground. He follows the trail of blood over to the deli counter, where he can see Blizzard hiding behind it. The Crimson: Come out, Blizzard. It’s over. Blizzard: To hell with you guys… Frozen cutlets come flying towards The Crimson, one striking him in the head and knocking him down to the ground. He moans and rolls over to his side. A few frozen slices of ham are aimed at Robert’s head, but he uses his powers to cause them to slice right into the side of the wall. Night Shard: I won’t be so easy to take down, Blizzard. Or should I call you Paul? Yeah, I remember your real name. You thought you were some tough shit, but I knew you wouldn’t last as a good guy. You didn’t have the guts to play it by the rules. Blizzard: You should talk. You would have been a bad guy but you’re too ugly and fat. He leaps back to his feet, firing ice blasts from his arms. But Night Shard isn’t there anymore. He’s nowhere to be found, and the only thing visible is Crimson still hurt on the ground. Blizzard: It looks like your partner has left you, Thinh. Oh, I knew it was a mistake to let a man of your power be sided with a loser like Robert. He was too weak. Not a good complement to yourself. The Crimson: [trying to get up] I appreciate your comments, but I’d rather he be my partner than you. Blizzard: Really? And why is that? The Crimson: Because I wouldn’t trust you with the well being of my cocker spaniel. No offense. Blizzard: [touches his heart] I’m hurt, Thinh. Especially after you told me how much potential you thought I had. Well, you were right about the potential. I have plenty of it. I just didn’t use it in the way you and Tom had hoped. I fight for evil now, and I’m quite happy doing so. The Crimson: We should have never taken up your offer for that sandwich. Blizzard: You sold your soul to the devil for the sake of a sandwich. [raises his hand to fire his ice bolts] And by the way, that sandwich WAS pastrami, idiot. But before he can freeze Crimson, Night Shard appears from out of the shadows and strikes Blizzard on the side of the head with a pipe. He falls to the ground unconscious as Robert pumps his fist energetically. Night Shard: Hah! You’re the idiot, or did you forget that I could hide in the shadows? Robert offered his hand to Crimson and helped him back to his feet. The Crimson: Thanks for the eleventh hour save. I knew that shadow power would come in handy one day. Night Shard: Think about it Thinh…he could have put you on ICE! The Crimson: [rubs his head] No more lame puns, Robert. My head hurts too much. So another victory for the Crusaders over evil. Would there be another victory this Friday though?
