A Walk Through the Woods

18 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3-18-03 The mud pressed down under his shoes. Water drops dripped from the leaves of the trees surrounding the area. The ground is still wet from the recent rain fall. Everyone was so relaxing. The gentle call of the wild animals rang out in the air. The peacefulness of the scene is disturbed by the person walking through the woods. The woodland creatures scatter as he walks by. The person’s head hangs low as he walks on by, his short blond hair getting dripped on from the rain water. In a low tone, the man’s voice rings out through the trees. “How very tired I am; so tired of it all. I just want to lie down and go to sleep. To forget it all as if it was just a bad dream in my mind.” He says. His voice drags with each word. He continues to walk through the woods. The calls of the birds fill the air as they take flight into the clear blue sky. The man cast his head upward toward the flying creatures. He lets out a gentle sigh. “Look at them, able to escape so easily. If they do not like their current surroundings they merely take flight and leave it all behind them. If only man could be so free. Instead we are chained and bounded by our words, actions, and manners in which we carry ourselves. Everything we do merely ties us down more without us even knowing it. Even now with my every footstep, I can feel myself being bounded to this calling. The might trees and gentle grass make no sound and leave my mind free to wander.” The words roll out one after another onto this gentle place. He walks on past it all, keeping his gaze down toward the earth. On his back is a plain black t-shirt. The front of it is covered by his arms. Blue jeans cover his lower half as his white and black sneakers crush the grass underfoot. A voice rings out against the silence of the grass bending under his steps. His sharp blue eyes focus on the source of the noise. It was merely a rabbit running through the woods and nothing else. His nerves calm down with notices the harmless woodland creature. The rabbit darts by and disappears into the thicket of trees. The blond locks turn toward where the rabbit ran toward. “Run little rabbit, run as fast as you can. When your legs can no longer move and carry you on, that is when your enemies will strike. Like little leeches they sit and watch for you to be at your weakest. That is when they strike down upon you with no mercy. So run to your little rabbit hole and hide as best you can. You feel safe among familiar walls don’t you? No one can hurt you as long as you stay in your home where you are safe and warm.” An arm shoots out into the trees and into a hole in the ground. Pulling the arm out, it has a tight grasp on a brown rabbit. He pulls the rabbit toward his face and stares at it in its soft black eyes. “Do you feel safe now? Even within the security of your own home you was easily able to be plucked away. Now you know how I feel! Even in my own home I must watch my back. My foes would sink to any level to get to me, to crush my heart and spirit. But I won’t let it happen, not now, not ever again.” His hand violently shakes the rabbit. Another hand finds its way to the rabbit’s head. With a simple twist the struggling rabbit stops. Its life leaves the body. The hand moves the still rabbit toward his face. “Look at what happens when you assume too much. You become just another victim.” The hand releases its grip around the fur of the brown rabbit, letting it hit the grass. The white and black sneakers step right on over it as if nothing happened. The footprints left behind are the only evidence anyone even passed through these calm and gentle woods. The hand presses against the trunk of a tree, there is now a clearing in the woods leading to the outside. “The change is going on all around us. You may not notice it now, but soon you will. They have taken so much away from me now; my pride, my honor, my love for the sport, all of it gone. What do I have now besides my utter hate for them? When you leave a man broken on the floor, what else is there for him to do but get back up and fight once more?” His gaze turns from the scenery of the trees and green lush grass to that of homes and paved roads. The houses are a bit into the distances but still some detail is able to be made out. The one toward the middle is the same house that belongs to Tessa Windsor and Chris Storm, otherwise known as The Schmoopies to the PCW. “Know that I do this not for myself but for the one I love. It is for her that I shall cast aside what humanity I had left inside me. They wanted blood and now they shall see it. But this time it won’t be our blood that is shed. They will be the ones to suffer now for ever hurting my sweet beloved. They pushed and shoved and tempted me for far too long. Now I am ready to push back.” A woman walks out of the back door. Three small children follow beside her. A soft sigh escapes the lips of the person. He watches Tessa interact with the young children. His head rest against his hand pressed on the trunk. “I love her with every beat of my heart. I would do anything for her. I will protect her from everything I can. I know they mean to hurt her though. I can not stop that. All I can do is try to get to them before they can get to her. But that’s alright; play time is now officially over.” Those last words came out cold and cruel. He pushes off of the tree and walks toward Tessa and the children. Tessa turns her head and notices the man walking towards her. She stands up straight and smiles at him, waving her hand. Closer and closer the man moves to her. “Chris, where have you been? You have been gone for over an hour.” She asked him. Chris merely takes his hand and cups hers. “I had to go for a walk. I had some things on my mind that needed to be thought about.” He tells her, gently kissing her cheek. Tessa finally notices Chris’ shirt. She puts a hand over her mouth as she gasp. “Why are you wearing that?” she asked. Chris looks down at his shirt. “It’s a reminder about a different time, a time when I was going the pushing instead of the one being pushed.” Chris walks right past her and into the house. The glass double doors on the back reflect the logo on the front of the black t-shirt. “WIW: NG”
