The Golden Saga: Coma White

13 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7-13-02 Determined eyes. The pale girl looked beautiful standing there that day, the wind ruffled her pretty white dress and nearly knocked the full syringe out of her hand, she swayed back and forth but was happy. She knew what was going to happen. She loved every second of it. She smiled, it was a blank smile, the smile of someone who was trying to hide something. She was trying to hide her true self. But today everyone would see it, they would see it in a splatter of bones and red on the pavement below her, they would realize that she wasn't the good natured girl they could walk over. They would realize that the smile she always flashed them was just to cover up the scars that formed inside her. Well they always hurt her, everything people say to her hurts her and drives the knife further into her pale wrists, drawing blood sometimes, sometimes not. She looked down at the cars below her, her arms were outstretched in a cross position, holding onto the edge of the overpass. The cars passed under her, barely glancing up at all, the few that did were surprised to see a girl that young standing there. The police would come eventually. Her family life was always perfect, that wasn't the problem. Her mother and father were very, very wealthy, she was beautiful and alot of people liked her. She was fake though. The act she put on to make people like her was false, it was her fake skin that she could never shed. Everyone thought she was the good natured, loving girl that alot of people make fun of because they can get away with it. She went to a public school freshman year of High School. She was popular, everyone liked her because of it. Her parents didnt approve of any of it, no daughter of theres would ever be in a dirty and common public school. They enrolled her in a high class private school. She didn't make friends, she had to fake it all... with a smile. She would walk different, talk different, dress different, and act different. Like the rich kids wanted her too. Deep down she hated it, she hated herself and what she had become. Even after all the pretending, she couldn't fit into the high class society life. She started running away from home, cutting school, changing everything. Her grades diminished to almost nothing, barely keeping them high enough to stay in the hell hole of her school. She eventually found the one thing that could make her happy, the one thing that could make her feel safe and different... A pill to make you numb A pill to make you dumb A pill to make you anybody else But all the drugs in this world Won't save her from herself Her mouth was dry now, she began to remember the things that led to this. Taking her first shot of heroin junior year, the pills, the pain killers, the tranquilizers, it all made her better for a period of time. Depending on her dose. She always came down and back to her false life, the life that was hurting her the most. Pretending for her parents and putting a show on for her teachers. She started to curse, her mouth bled words like it never has. She cursed her parents. She cursed her school. She cursed her feelings. She cursed her 'friends'. And she cursed her addiction. It made her feel a little better as she opened up the bottle and swallowed the pills inside. Too many, but who cares at this point. She was riding a high right now, the wind made it feel like she was flying and the cars made it seem like the clouds were moving shards of metal. She felt like she was a mile up. It would be a miracle to survive this drop. She smiled now, looking up into the sky. She imagined rain falling onto her face and drama starting it all, the police finally arrived and began to talk to her, trying to talk her down. She was going to come down from this high, and she was only waiting to fall. Her perfect life. Her perfect parents. Her perfect school. Her perfect friends. Her perfect looks. They all haunted her, none of it was her, it was all some outer casing of her that she desperately tried to be. Oh, look, her parents were there, amazing they could drag themselves out for this. She decided to put on a show. Nothing could stop her now. She walked the guardrail like it was a tight rope, twirling and dancing the whole time. This was her. This was what she felt. This is what she felt as she plunged the syringe into her arm like a veteran junkie, it wont be much time now. Blaze of glory. A pill to make you numb They would never know, she thought as she popped the second to last of her pills into her mouth. She wasn't thinking rational anymore. Put on the show. Make it worth while. What can I do. Her dress blew a little more, she was so high. So high up. A pill to make you dumb One last one, last pill. Gone. Numb. She threw the orange bottle at the cops, she spun around, dropping the syringe and smiling at the crowd in front of her, alot had gathered to watch her finally play herself. To finally put on the show that mommy and daddy never let her. So good. So Numb. A pill to make you anybody else She stretched her arms out and smiled again, her parents rushed to her and so did the cops, here was the drama. She loved drama. It was perfect. What a way. She thought about how sick she felt to her stomach because of the pills, it wouldn't matter anymore. The heroin would kick in soon and she wouldn't feel a thing. But all the drugs in this world She was finally herself. Her parents grabbed each arm. She gave them something to talk about as she easily wrenched free, from the angle it looked like they couldn't save her in time. It would be replayed on the news for weeks.. she had immortality now. This would be viewed everywhere. Other people would realize that they would have to be themselves or this will happen. It will get to them all and this will happen. Halfway down. Still Smiling. Won't save her from herself Smiling to the end.
