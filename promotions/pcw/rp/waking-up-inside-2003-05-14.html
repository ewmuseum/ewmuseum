Waking up inside....

14 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 14-5-03 The old rusted out van strolls down the dry pavement on the road. The driver starts to head out of the town that they caught Mark in. The back of the van where Mark is at is all metal underneath and there is not any carpet back there. The cold metal against Mark’s head is soothing his brain. Before this his brain was going crazy, with different thoughts about what he was going to do and how he was going to keep a hold of Haley. As the van moves, Mark’s brain is settle, but still has some things on his mind. ”Matt Jackson thinks that he is going to figure me out joining into my lifestyle? Yea right, he can’t handle what he is about to get himself into. I have been in this game for almost a year and a half and I have been beat up by all types of minorities.” The road ahead is filled with broken out asphalt that created pot holes, which are not good on the suspension for cars. Especially the cars that are rusted out and the rods underneath the van are all covered in the dark rust color. There are some broken parts that are hanging down from the bottom of the van and come inches from hitting the pavement, not creating any sparks. The three captors in the black suits are all talking and joking around, they sound like they are all having a good time. The two wheels on the right side of the van go down into the hole, the front wheel going in first and then the back wheel going in next after that. The head of Mark goes into the back of the seat where there is not any padding at all. His brain wakes up and his nerves send a message down his spine and his body wakes up from the bump. His head his filled with cob webs again as he doesn’t know where he is at. He doesn’t remember being put in the van and he looks up over the seat and he sees the three captors. “What in the fuck are you all doing with me?” Mark screams aloud as he only has his head over the seat. All the captors look back as they hear Mark scream at the top of his lungs. The one who is closest to Mark starts to shake all around his body feeling frightened as Mark stares him down with a mean and wanting to beat some ass face. The other two look back at the road. “How far away are we?” The captor in the passenger’s seat asks the driver. “Only a couple more minutes then we will be there.” The driver says as he tries to not look back at Makoine. The third captor shakes off Mark looking at him and he turns back around. Mark looks at his skin color and body makeup and realizes that this guy is in security for the agent company. ”This is the chance that I have to get back at all the beatings the coons did against me.” Mark starts to think to himself. “All of the food throwing and hiding in dark allies to jump me and make me bleed. Now its time that they shed some blood on this country and tell them that they are not worth living here once and for all.” Mark goes back down into the little place that he is at in the back of the van and he looks around at the materials that are around. There is a little tiny piece of string stuck in the door which is going in the wind out the window, outside of the van. Mark looks around with his brain set on high now to get this accomplished. A red rag that has black oil faded on it is in the corner of the van. Mark looks around and he scoots on his ass towards the rear doors of the van. He outstretches his arm to grab the rag and tuck it away. What idea does Mark have with the rag?
