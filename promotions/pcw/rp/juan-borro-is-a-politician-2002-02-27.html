Juan Borro is a Politician??

27 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2/27/02 Time: 6: 38 P.MLocation: Lisbon, PortugalScene: Coffee House “You think you know, but you have no idea.” The scene opens with Juan Borro entering a small coffee house in the outskirts of Lisbon. Juan is here on a bit of a business proposition. The head of city council in Lisbon gave Juan a phone call last night. She said she’d like to talk to Juan in private about a certain job. Juan informed her that he is working as a full time wrestler at the moment, but she told Juan the two would not interfere. Juan doesn’t see the women anywhere, so he decides he’ll take a seat and wait for her. Juan sits down, and relaxes in his seat. He stares up and notices Porto of Portugal is playing Spain’s Real Madrid in an champion’s league soccer match, and it’s being televised in the café. Juan goes up to the counter and takes a seat. He then looks at the employee at the counter who is wearing Porto colors and watching the game religiously. Juan-Would you happen to know what the score in the game is? The man turns around, and notices the man who just called him is none other than Juan Borro. The old counter worker becomes ecstatic as the words can barely come out of his mouth. He’s stumbling and shaking, but finally responds. Employee- You…….You’re Juan Borro? Juan- Yea, well that’s what it says on my birth certificate I think. So do you know the score in the game, or am I going to have to call one of the guys right now to tell me the score? The employee is still in amazement. He looks up on the screen and then looks at Juan, who is chuckling to him self. Employee- It’s one to nothing Real Madrid is winning. Don’t worry though it’s only the first half, we still have time. Juan- That’s good. The man looks around and notices Juan is looking around for someone. The employee is still a bit nervous and doesn’t know how to act toward Juan, but he asks him his question anyway. Employee-So what are you doing in a small shop like this? Juan- I’m waiting for someone who is supposed to be pretty important. I think I’m on time, but I guess I’ll wait a bit longer. Employee-Well if it’s an important thing it must be an important person. Who are you waiting for? Juan- Well it’s kind of personal, and I’m not supposed to go around blurting things out in the open. Employee- Oh I see. The employee and Juan both pause for a while. The employee notices the championship ring Juan is wearing, and looks at in complete awe. Juan notices the way he’s looking at it. Juan then takes it off, and throws it at the man. Juan- Here you go? Employee- Oh thanks, it’s really nice. I just never actually had seen one of the rings in person. The employee takes the ring, grabs an eyeglass and begins looking at it. He grabs the light and holds it toward it, as it reflects right off the golden edges. Employee- Wow! This is really nice; it must have been an incredible rush to win something like this. Juan-Well you know, it was all right. Employee- Man I would do anything for a ring like that. I always wanted to be a soccer player, but was never really good enough. I was on the high school team, but that was about it for me. The employee goes to hand Juan the ring back, but Juan backs away, grabs the man’s hand and clutches it over the ring. Juan- Go on and have it. Consider it a present from me. I have a bunch of those at home anyway. Employee- Oh man are you sure? I mean you won this, not me. I feel kind of weird just taking this from you like this. Juan- Don’t mind at all, like I said it’s a present. You should just go home and show it off to everyone. I’m sure all of your friends will be jealous. Employee- Yea, this is great. Thanks a lot Juan, I’ll never forget this, and I’ll never lose this ring as long as I live. Juan- Good, because I know I would have. The two men share a laugh, as the head of the city council walks up to Juan. The employee turns around and gets working again, but peeks over to see what’s going on with her and Juan. City Council Leader- Hello Juan, it’s very nice to actually meet you in person. I’ve seen videotapes and pictures of you before, but I don’t believe the two of us have formally met. Juan- No, I’m kind of confused and nervous to what you actually want me to do anyway? I just hope it’s nothing bad. City Council Leader- Oh no, I would never have you do anything you would feel uncomfortable doing. Also the job I’m offering you today is just a proposition; you would definitely not have to give me a clear decision. Juan- Okay so what is it? An employee of the café, coming up to the two and asking what they would like to drink, interrupts Juan. City Council Leader- I’ll have a small café late, thank you. Juan- I’ll have the same. The two smile at the waiter, who writes down the order and then goes over to get the too highly popular celebrities drinks. City Council Leader- Well like I was going to say. I’ve seen your case and all of the publicity it has drawn from the people. I’ve also seen the way you draw attention from people all over the world with your job. I think it’s quiet remarkable the way you deal with your people, so that’s why I’m coming you today with this. Juan-Well what is it? City Council Leader- Well you see, we’re a bit short on people in City Council. We have four other council chairmen, but we’re looking for third. Were looking for somebody who is popular with the people of Lisbon, so we thought you would be a great person to fill in the job. Juan- Wow! A council chairman, that sure is a big job. I’m not sure if I have the time to take up such a job though. City Council Leader- Well this is where the good part comes in. We’re going to be working around our schedule, so you won’t have to work around ours. We pretty much want your opinion on things, so the people feel they are being represented. Juan- Well in that case, it sounds great. Sure I’ll do it. City Council Leader- Great, so everything is set. I’ll have one of my people call your people later in the week and we can work something out. Juan- Sure. City Council Leader- Well I should really be getting back to work, it’s been a pleasure finally meeting you, and this should be fun. The City Council Leader lightly has her hand breeze Juan’s leg as she smiles and walks off. She then smiles back at Juan who stays sitting there. Juan sits back and thinks about the position and responsibilities he was just given. He thinks of the job as a new learning experience, and it should sure make him more of a powerful man. The scene fades with Juan walking out of the café and back to his home. Scene Fades to Black.
