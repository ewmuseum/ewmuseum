28 February 2002

Date: 2-28-02 {The scene opens back up with Kevin as he drives around getting to his meeting place. As he waits by the corner a few prostitutes walk up to him and begin questioning him. One walking up looks Mexican, wearing a tight lime green dress that she fills out nicely. The other is wearing a short leather skirt and a small black shirt that exposes her stomach and neon green belly button ring.} Hooker 1: Aaaaa bebe, aaaaa bebe a. Hooker 2: Quiero hacer amor contigo Kevin. Kevin: Quisierais esto pie sabe tu culo? Hooker 1: Angel ven aca el no quiere nada. Angel: Estupido. Por que no se quieres? {Kevin pulls out his necklace that holds his engagement ring out of his tight black shirt. His ring glimmers like a star in the night sky. The ladies see this and get an instant look of disappointment on their faces. Not needing to answer the ladies leave as Kevin still waits alone.} {Out of the corner of his eye a black Lamborghini Diablo pulls up as the driver motions Kevin to enter.} Man: Don’t try anything funny! Just get in and let’s talk. {Kevin enters the car to see two well built bodyguards ready to pounce when needed. One is wearing a turquoise shirt made from silk and the other is wearing a black silk shirt with a red dragon flying over top a village.} Kevin: I take it that these guys are Jung Wang and Suzuki Yamaha. Man: You know then? Well where going to a location and talk business. Kevin: Why so formal PY? Why can’t it be like back in the day? Man: You mean when you showed me up in front of my family? When you showed me up in front of my father the Imperial Emperor when I was training to be a guard? Kevin: You still can’t be sore about that can you? I was just doin’ my best just what you were doin’. Man: Well because of that my father didn’t see that I was fit to follow in his footsteps because an American showed me up. Now I have to be an Ambassador to China, you know how much… well you know! Kevin: I know but I need a favor from you. Man: Not yet we’re almost there, then we can talk all you want. Kevin: Ok. {Kevin continues to sit and wait as the member of the Imperial Family of Japan makes turns making it impossible for anyone to follow. The bodyguards get out making sure their boss can walk in safety. Kevin gets out and follows them into a boat that is currently docked at a pier. Kevin looks at the night and reminiscing on his home in Spain.} Man: Hurry up I’m not safe out here for long. {Kevin realizing that his friend is right quickly enters the white boat on the pier. Before entering he notices a black dragon with a blue orb on several areas.} Kevin: All right let’s talk. Man: What do you want? You know I wouldn’t do this but I owe you one. Kevin: I know you do, if it weren’t for me you would’ve never met Leia. Man: Hey leave my wife out of this. It’s just between you and me. Kevin: And my friend because it is him that I’m doin’ this for. Man: Hmmm you really like this guy eh? Kevin: He’s been more than a friend to me. We’ve made it through the biz, and well he’s watched my back plenty of times. It’s kinda like what you and me had Phuc Yui. Phuc: All right what do you need? Kevin: Well he’s been imprisoned by the Chinese government for the murder of their emperor. He escaped from prison a while ago and is seeking sanctuary in Portugal. Phuc: And what do you want me to do? Kevin: Well I want you to pull a few strings and get Juan cleared from all charges. Phuc: WHAT??? Kevin: Hey he needs this! He needs to get his life back on track, and we need to try and get back on the tag title scene. Jung: Boss if you do this it breaks the Black Dragon Treaty. Suzuki: What about the Blue Orb alliance? Phuc: What about them? I owe this to Kevin and I gave him my word. {Kevin looks on in approval while the Imperial Guards look confused.} Phuc: Kevin all right I’ll do what I can. I can get him off with probation at least. I can guarantee it. Kevin: Alright thanks man. This really means a lot to him. Phuc: Well I’ll do this for him and don’t expect me to do this again. Kevin: Dude this is way beyond great. Thanks man. Phuc: Don’t worry about it. Sihanara Kevin: Sihanara. {Kevin exits out of the ship and looks at the sky. This sky reminds him of home. And soon Juan shall be free once again. Now it will be Kevin and Juan fighting the legendary Hank Gunyon and his partner in a number one contender for the tag titles match. Will Golden Game be victorious? Or will the Game be over? Only time will tell, and hopefully Kevin will get back on track. Back on track enough to gain number one contendership for the tag team titles. The titles that Kevin and Juan worked hard to win and fought their hardest to retain.} {Kevin walks out as the scene pans in and fades out to black. The Golden Game are getting back on track.} {END TRANSMISSION}
