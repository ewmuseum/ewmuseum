Title: BSing around before Sabotage Featuring: Politically Incorrect Date: Something. Authority. It's much more than something Cartman told all of you trash to respect in that TV show, South Park. Authority is Ultimate. The ultimate aprhodesiac to some. The ultimate goal to be attained for others. Hence, it is unsurprising that the alliance of William S. Powell, Tyler Nelson, Xavier Kannon, Eleanor Hall, Karl LeBlanc, Reginald V. Lovecraft, Orpheus Grant, and Tim Walden is known as 'The Ultimate Authority'. Simply put, the buck stops with those eight people in Primetime Championship Wrestling. With the tag team champions and Continental champion in their midst, and the leaders of the tag division and Commissioner respectively, it would seem that getting these belts off of these men would be quite challenging. And this assessment would be correct. But, before they can worry about that, Politically Incorrect has the Malice Crew, and a 'mystery partner' to worry about.. William S. Powell: 'Mystery partner', my ASS! ::Politically Incorrect sit aboard Politically Incorrect One, as it is flying towards Sabotage. Needless to say, Politically Incorrect will likely be arriving late this evening, but they own twenty percent of the company, so it is likely that such a minor transgression will be forgiven. The jet is flying at full speed, as the PCW Tag Team Champions are enjoying some fine cigars and brandy. Powell takes another puff on his cigar, and then continues his statement.:: William S. Powell: I mean, Legend Inc, Jason Core, The Rush? It's pretty obvious that the mystery partner will be none other than rootin-tootin sister-screwin Duke 'I'm a Hoss, hoss!' Williams. Reginald V. Lovecraft: Yeah! I mean, I remember talking to Personell Director Tim (explicitive) Walden about the good old days! When a mystery partner meant JUDAS STEELE, damnit! William S. Powell: Yeah, Walden said that his return sucked. Oh well, it couldn't possibly suck more than Duke Williams' return, because his return is going to begin with a loss at the hands of the Ultimate Authority. Reginald V. Lovecraft: It is obvious by the number of curse words Duke Williams throws around whenever we make an appearance. There are many reasons for this. Jealousy, perhaps? It is understandable that Mr. Williams is quite upset about virtually having no power anymore to run PCW into the ground with massive hiring failures and loss of money across the board. He's green with envy at our fancy suits, jets, limosines, and other amenities. He wants what we have..too bad! So does everyone else in the world! Everyone else wants to be just like Politically Incorrect! William S. Powell: It is unfortunate...our opposition is pathetic! We have Jason Core.....a man who lost the Extreme Title to BIG DAVEYLICIOUS. BIG DAVEYLICIOUS! That is simply pathetic! And then, we have 'Legend Inc.' Tom Lexian..a former IWO superstar, and a man who's steam has disappeared after March Madness. I'm not even going to talk about The Rush... ::Suddenly, the connection fades out abruptly.
