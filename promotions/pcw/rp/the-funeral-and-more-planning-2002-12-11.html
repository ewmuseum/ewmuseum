The funeral and more planning

11 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11-12-02 It's the funeral of Pietro Cuppi one of Bob's ex thugs who got killed when he went to do the mission against the blacks. About thirty people stand in the cemetery waiting for the burial service to take place. Bob stands alone as an outcast in the back of the crowd awaiting the arrival of Tony. The church minister is running late as his car has broken down a few kilometers from the cemetery. Bob thought he would pay his respects to a man who died working for Bob Vurdo. Bob looks down at his wristwatch as the time goes slowly by. As Bob adjusts the sleeve of his black tux Tony arrives with a pat on the back for Bob. 'Hey Bob how ya going?' Tony is obviously in a good mood as is Bob who turns around and smiles at Tony.. 'I'm great Tony and how are you today?' Tony smiles 'I'm also very fucking good.... You know why?' Bob looks at him in a manner to suggest he continues. 'Because I can see us being on top of America in no time as far as money and power goes. Think about the possibilities of you being the mayor of Amber Hill.' Bob smiles. 'So have you thought it through?' Bob nods his head and take a breath to prepare himself for his final decision. 'I will go through with it.' Tony smiles immediately. Tony always knew he was going to go through with it but it still makes him happy to hear Bob say it personally. 'Okay. We have to work out a plan.' Bob nods his head as he knows Tony has it all planned out by now. 'All right lets start with our policies that will win over the public.' Bob silently listens on as Tony continues. 'First we need a main policy we will base our campaign on.... This can be an empty promise but it has to be good and it has to appeal to the large majority of Amber Hill residents.' Bob doesn't know much about politics so he takes a moment to think. 'How about you give me some ideas and I will run with what sounds best.' 'Okay well the thing that is lacking with Amber Hill is the health system. How about you promise to press for more funds to upgrade our hospital with more staff and technology.' 'That sounds good.' 'Okay now you need a few more smaller things. Maybe you could get the public involved in your campaign by giving out an address to send letters to with their ideas on how to make Amber Hill a better place and you could promise to personally read each letter.' 'That's a great idea and then I can get the rest of the policies from the letters.' 'Now I think this wrestling thing you have happening could be used to our advantage. Hundreds of people in Amber Hill tune into the PCW shows every week.' Bob nods and waits for Tony to continue. 'After that we can move from there.' 'Tonight I will go over some things to educate myself on local politics.' The idea of being the mayor is growing on Bob quickly. 'This is going to be great.' Tony turns to look around at the crowd that has gathered when he notices Mrs. Cuppi walking up the hill toward them with an angry expression on her face. 'Look out she don't look to happy.' Tony politely nods in the direction of Mrs. Cuppi as she nears the men. She walks up to the two men as tears begin rolling down her cheek. 'Why did you have to do this to my son?!' Bob freezes as the rest of the people turn around to see what's the commotion's about. 'He was a good boy until you came along! You mother fucker I hope you burn in hell which is probably where my son is now thanks to your stupidity!' Bob doesn't respond he wasn't ready for an assault like this. 'Your the lowest thing I have ever seen in my life.' Mrs. Cuppi turns her back and begins walking away before turning back around and spitting right on the left breast area of Bob's tuxedo. This is enough to send Bob over the edge as he takes one quick step in the direction of Mrs. Cuppi before delivering a stiff, hard backhand slap to the mouth which knocks her to the ground. The crowd of people gathered who were watching the argument let out a simultaneous 'OH,' in shock because of what they have just witnessed. Bob decides it's time to leave as he turns his back and begins walking out with Tony. 'Why the fuck did you do that?' Tony asks. 'She got spit on my suit.... This suit isn't cheap ya know?' Tony laughs as if it is all a joke. Suddenly Bob is tapped on the shoulder and as he turns around he is greeted with a punch to the nose by Mr. Cuppi. 'You hit my wife and you killed my son you heartless bastard!' Cuppi lunges and punches Bob in the nose a second time this time drawing blood. Bob stumbles back startled by the ambush. The man follows up with another punch but this time Bob manages to block it and reply with a kick which was intended to hit Mr. Cuppi in the groin but he dodged the blow. Tony then decides to get involved and with one hard punch to Mr. Cuppi's unsuspecting jaw he knocks the man down. 'Take that fucker.' Tony then kicks the man in the ribs while he is on the ground. Tony delivers one more kick to the ribs of Mr. Cuppi before walking away from the crowd which is standing a few metres away. He puts his hand on Bob's shoulder and guides him toward the car park. 'That fucker aint getting up.' Tony laughs as he looks down at his clenched fist which is lined with various expensive rings. 'He made my fucking nose bleed.' 'You have an Italian nose, it's like steel so don't worry about it.' Bob and Tony each laugh as they enter the half full car park. 'I'll see you tomorrow and we'll get our plan worked out.' Tony walks over to his white Mercedes as Bob walks over to his black Mercedes. 'Yeah cya tomorrow.' Bob opens up the car door and hops in.
