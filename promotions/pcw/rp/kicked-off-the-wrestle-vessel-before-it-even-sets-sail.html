Kicked off the Wrestle Vessel... before it even sets sail

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: not entered PCW Wrestle Vessel.This Sunday. All abooooooooooaaaaaaaaaard!!! [Scene opens on a bright sunny day along a huge wharf. The surrounding marina is scattered with boats- big ones, small ones, crappy ones, ugly ones… you get the point. But overshadowing all these boats is none other than the PCW Wrestle Vessel.A large Ocean Liner, with a sparkling white colour that reflects off the clear, blue sea. A large ramp bridging the gap between the ship and the wharf has been set up and a dozen or so different wrestlers and B Grade Celebrities boarding the ship with their suitcases in tow. In the nearby car park, a shiny black Toyota Supra pulls up into a parking spot.The door flings open and out steps Flaymin Aymin decked in a bright blue Hawaiian shirt with little pink flowers all over it. From the passenger side of the car, Flaymin’s assistant- Hirraani, steps out.She lifts her sunglasses and looks at the Wrestle Vessel in amazement] Hirraani: Wow, that’s a big boat. Flaymin: Of course it is, its not like you could fit my tractor on a shitty, little tug boat. [Hirraani looks at Flaymin in confusion] Hirraani: You’re bringing the tractor along? Flaymin: Yep, if everything has gone accordingly, it should already be on the ship. [Flaymin opens the boot of his car and takes out his suit case.He hands Hirraani her bag and then closes the boot of the car and walks towards the ship] Hirraani: Hold on a sec, how did you get authorisation to take a tractor with you? Flaymin: Simple: Pimply Faced Intern Kid + Free Case of Beer= Hidden tractor on the ship. [Hirraani frowns and then cautiously warns Flaymin] Hirraani: I hope you know what you’re doing… Flaymin: Trust me, this will be easier than the bitch-slapping I’m gonna give Violence Jack at the pay per view. [Flaymin and Hirraani wheel their bags up to the check-in gate in front of the ship. The female attendant smiles and holds out her hand. Flaymin reaches into his pockets and searches for something.Finally he locates a condom and hands it to the attendant] Flaymin: You’re welcome up to my room anytime you want! [Hirraani slaps the back of Flaymin’s head.Meanwhile, the attendant gawks at the condom and then throws it into a nearby garbage bin] Attendant: Sir, are you booked onto this ship? Flaymin: Sure am. Attendant: And your name would be…. Flaymin: Aymin… Flaymin Aymin… at your service. [The attendant looks through her bookings folder in search of Flaymin’s name. Upon locating Flaymin’s name, she gasps and looks up at Flaymin in shock. She then looks back down at the folder for reassurance before finally looking back up at Flaymin once again] Attendant: Uh… one moment sir. [Flaymin shrugs his shoulders as the attendant picks up her walkie-talkie and begins to speak into it] Attendant: He’s here….*muffled voice*What do you mean ‘stall him’?*muffled voice*Will that work?*muffled voice*Ok… but you guys better get here quick. [Flaymin looks at the nervous attendant and questions her] Flaymin: What was all that about? Attendant: There have been some slight complications with your booking and I’m just waiting for the Captain to come down here and fix the situation. Flaymin: The Captain?Suddenly I’m feeling really important! Attendant: You can say that again… [Coming down the ramp of the ship escorted by two security guards is the Captain of the ship.He quickly strolls over to the check-in gate and whispers into the ear of the attendant. The attendant nods her head and takes a step back.The Captain takes off his Sailor’s Hat and steps forward to speak to a mystified Flaymin] Flaymin: So what’s going on? Captain: Are you Flaymin Aymin? Flaymin: Damn straight. Captain: The man that ran over 21 people in a tractor incident that cost The Phantom Booker his job? Flaymin: That’s me! Captain: The man also responsible for the great destruction of the Sunday Sabotage set? Flaymin: I recall something like that occurring… Captain: Yes…. Right….Well I’m sorry Sir but you pose too great a threat to be allowed on this ship.There wasn’t an insurance company in sight willing to cover us when they found out you’d be on the boat! Flaymin: Well then how am I supposed to get to Jamaica? Captain: Well we have two options for you. Firstly, we could strap you up head to toe like a mental patient and then stick you in the highly secure cellar at the bottom of the ship. Or we could arrange an alternate mode of transport. [Flaymin looks at Hirraani and they both begin to mumble their options quietly] Captain: Oh, keep in mind that the woman is allowed full access to the ship- its just you we’re scared of. Flaymin: Come on buddy, I won’t cause any trouble! Captain: No. Flaymin: I promise! Captain: NO. Flaymin: Pretty, pretty plea… Captain: NO!! Flaymin: This is nothing like the Love Boat.At least the Captain on that ship was friendly! Captain: Once again you’re confusing fact with fiction. Flaymin: I don’t care if I’m mistaking shit for sugar- you can’t deny me access to the boat! Captain: Are you willing to be tied up and maimed? Flaymin: In a bondage sort of way? Captain: No, in a ‘psychotic killer’ kind of way.And don’t worry about your tractor- we’ve already taken off the wheels so it can’t possibly cause any damage along the way. Flaymin: You bastard!! [Flaymin lunges forward and tries to attack the Captain.But before he can get to him, the two security guards step in and restrain Flaymin] Captain: So what will it be? Flaymin: Whatever it takes!Because no matter what you do, I’m still gonna sink the Wrestle Vessel whether you like it or not! Captain: Sure, sure, sure….Seeing as how you’d rather not be strapped, I’ve set up an alternative mode of transport- you’ll find it waiting for you just over there. [The Captain grins wickedly and points to the far end of the pier. Unable to break through the barricade of bodyguards blocking Flaymin’s entry to the Wrestle Vessel, Flaymin obliges with the Captain’s orders and walks over to the far end of the pier to his personal mode of transport. Upon seeing what has been set up for him, his eyes boggle open and his jaw drops. Flaymin looks back at the Captain in anger.The Captain simply smiles and waves to Flaymin] Captain: Good Luck.I’ll see you in Jamaica…. If you even make it that far! [Flaymin stares back at his personal sea vessel and shakes his head] Flaymin: How the f*ck am I supposed to single-handedly steer a rusty, freakin’ tugboat all the way to Jamaica? [Flaymin scratches his head and continues to gaze at the broken-down, rusted tugboat, complete with several little leaks here and there.The mast has been snapped in two and the steering wheel replaced with a car tyre] Flaymin: This is gonna be a long journey… [Scene fades to black] How will Flaymin get to Jamaica in time for the Pay Per View? You’ll just have to wait and see!!!
