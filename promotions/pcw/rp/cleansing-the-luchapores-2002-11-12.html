Cleansing The LuchaPores

12 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Title: Cleansing The LuchaPores Featuring: Xavier Kannon Date: 12/11/02 “Want to come in or are you just going to stand outside like, like, normal?” Our shot opens up outside one of London’s Body Shops, a stop on a last minute shopping trip before the 1st couple of PCW travel to France for Turmoil. Ellie~! looks to Xavier who seems perfectly happy waiting outside. Xavier : “It’s a shop you buy stuff from and it doesn’t sell underwear... Those are the usual conditions that lead to me waiting outside.” She shrugs. Eleanor : “Okayzzies!” The little prep skips over towards the Body Shop, disappearing in while Kannon stands around outside with all the other boyfriends and husbands... Leaning against whatever is there and counting down the minutes until their partners emerge from the ‘Chick Shop’. Sighing, the Scientologist leans back against the shop window as one of the young men outside looks at him directly, before smiling in recognition. “Didn’t you job to Boca~!?” He chuckles to himself as Kannon snarls and fakes a lunge, enough to send the teenager running away, hooting with laughter as Xavier leans back and waits for Eleanor. Xavier : “Peons...” Shaking his head, Kannon sighs and looks at his watch before noticing the sheer number of men loitering around outside he shop, looking at their watches and waiting for their other halves to come out. Xavier : “Why am I standing out here? I’m a modern man. I can be in touch with my feminine side when I want sex badly. I’m enough of a man, scratch that, a SCIENTOLOGIST.... I can walk into the shops Ellie~! likes and feel no shame!” A determined look on his face, Kannon turns around and goes to walk into the shop to take in it’s sights and beautifully scented smells before his eyes lock onto some of the things in the window. Xavier : “Peach Bath Salts.... Exotic Cranberry and Jasmine foot scrub....” Narrowing his eyes at some of the things in the window, he decides that standing outside isn’t so bad after all and wanders back to his spot, leaning against the window. *Da-Da Da-Da Da-Da Da-D-* From his Navy Blue suit’s inside pocket, his “Rock is Dead” ring tone sounds and the small model is pulled out, the Scientologist checking the caller ID before answering in an annoyed tone. Xavier : “I thought I told the office not to ring today?” His facial expression gives away his frustration, a shopping trip with Ellie~! enough to test most men and their credit cards. Xavier : “It’s bad enough I have to take Ellie~! shopping...” *Whistle.....* Then suddenly a spinning shadow is cast over Xavier and in slow motion he turns, looking up as his bright Green eyes widen.... Xavier : “Huh?” .... The shadow getting larger and larger, covering his whole form before.... *THUD!!!* .... Xavier is knocked down to the ground by the twisting little form of Ellie~! The little prep hops up to her feet and looks down at the dazed Kannon, hopping on the spot and applauding. Eleanor : “Como, YAY MÍ!“ Pulling himself up off the floor while people watch on bemused, Kannon looks confused as he hears his wife’s voice reciting Spanish. Slowly, he rubs his eyes. Xavier : “Eugh, what’s that?” Looking down at his hand he sees a smear of Cucumber Green ‘muck’ that he tries to wipe off onto a tissue before looking up at the grinning face of his wife.... Green, grinning face. Xavier : “Um... My little Fuck Ferret, you left that mud pack thing on....” Her eyes narrow and brow furrows, looking at her husband confused. Eleanor : “...........” Xavier holds his finger up, showing the Green residue from his wife’s face mask... Ellie~! looking insulted now. Eleanor : “Like, SO que? This is not a mud pack, this is totally the mask of the LUCHAPREP!!!“ The look on his face may be blank, but in his mind Kannon runs over the things that usually do this to his rather ‘Impressionable’ wife.... Shame it’s a LOT of things. Xavier : “Er... Ellie~!, did you eat a Fajita or something?” Dusting himself down fully, he turns to Ellie~! who is shaking her head.... Her lightly freckled face still hidden behind the mud pack. Xavier : “Accidentally click on a foreign film channel?” Again, her head shakes and the innocent grin remains. Eleanor : “El no!!! Last time that happened, I thought I was a German porn star called Helga Brattwurst, el remembero?” Kannon tries to ignore the bad faux Spanish and continues to investigate. Xavier : “Wait... You didn’t actually... No way, you wouldn’t of...” He shakes whatever he thought she could have done out of his head, but the thought remains. Xavier : “... Read the booking sheet for Turmoil? With El Romantico on?” The little LuchaPrep nods behind her Green mask. Eleanor : “Like...SO...Sí!!!” His head drops and shakes, knowing that once again reality has been purged from his wife’s brain in favour of something a lot more annoying, tiring... And in this case, odd looking. Xavier : “Okay, the bad Spanish I can handle... Maybe even having to eat a Burrito... But the mud pack?” Eleanor looks distraught, holding her hands up to her face... But not touching the gunk. Eleanor : “Like... WAY ninguna manera and stuff! This mask is handed down along the family LuchaPrep line.. Like, 4395 million generations of LuchaPrep have worn this mask... And El Ellie~! is the most recent. Kannon looks puzzled. Xavier : “El Ellie~!?” She sighs and gestures to herself. Eleanor : “El Duh! I’m El Ellie~! y el material!” Xavier : “So... Who handed the mask down to you?” She shrugs.... In a Spanish way. Eleanor : “Dunno, some woman...” Xavier : “Ahhhhhh......” As the CEO ponders what on earth to do next, Ellie~!’s eyes lock onto something across the way... A old look returning to her eyes as the reflection of a glass bottle is seen in her vacuous light Blue eyes. Eleanor : “El Snapple~!“ Before Kannon can even notice, the little and now speedy LuchaPrep skips off across the beacon that has called her too it... A simple glass bottle containing all of natures goodness.... Under the banner of “Snapple Apple” Snapple~!. Xavier : “This is going to be a loooooooooooong el día... DAMMIT! Now she has be doing it.” He sighs and wanders over to his wife who’s taking a sip of the sweet nectar, making sure not to get her “LuchaPrep” mud pack over the mouth. Eleanor : “You should, like, totally invite Tom Cruise and Penelope over for Tapas... Think she’s a LuchaPrep too?” Kannon rolls his eyes, deciding to maybe keep Ellie~! away from booking sheets... or just everything... now. Eleanor : “Huh?” She nudges him looking with pleading eyes.... Eyes that would normally work if it wasn’t for the rest pale Green gunge all around her face... Something that can be a bit... LOT... Off-putting. Xavier : “Well, if LuchaPreps wear masks and drink Snapple~!... Let us see what they eat shall we?” She shrugs again. Eleanor : “Like... Sí, and stuff.” The Scientologist walks across from the Snapple~! vendor, Ellie~! following in very Spanish fashion behind him while wondering where the nearest Bull Fight is. Xavier : “How would the LuchaPrep like some LuchaFood?” Handing over a $5 note, the Scientologist directs Ellie~!’s gaze to the sweaty Latino man wearing only a tight vest and jeans who is dropping a mixture of mince and his own perspiration into a Taco shell. Eleanor : “Like, what’s Spanish for ‘Ewwwwwwwwwwwwwwwwzies!!!’?” The man’s clammy hand passes the food to Xavier, who in turn holds it up for his wife. Xavier : “This should give you the fuel for a few more Twisting PrepSaults, huh?” If there wasn’t a mud pack covering it, we’d be seeing Ellie~!’s pale face going even paler now. Eleanor : “Um... I think it might be, like, WAY about time to hang the LuchaPrep.. Um, whatevers, up!!!” Breathing a sigh of relief, Xavier holds the Taco out to his side and drops it into the bin, the vendor looking confused as the couple walk off hand in hand. Eleanor : “Although, these LuchaPrep masks are meant to do wonders for the LuchaPores....” Then, in rather obvious and pre-staged fashion, the couple turn to camera, speaking right to it as a PCW logo appears in the corner. Xavier : “Have a LuchaPrep mask to remove?” Eleanor : “Want a, like, sugary way to do it?” Xavier : “If so, look no further than..... Snapple~!“ Ellie~! holds up the bottle as the shot fades out on their shill smiles, the last thing being seen on the screen are the Snapple~! logo and the phrase... “Snapple~! - For all your Gunk Mask removing needs (Also drinkable)“ ---- View Xavier Kannon's Biography
