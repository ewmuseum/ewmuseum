Chinatown Follies.....Part 1 of 4...

18 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8-18-2002 'To know ones enemy, one must know where that enemy came from. Jung Loc was an enemy. What had he done to me? He had the misfortune of being booked to try and wrest my precious TV Title from my grasp...Wait. Someone already did that, although Syko was neither booked nor victorious in battle for my gold. All I have is the name. He was after my name. That's it. He was after the right to truly be called the TV Champion. Pity; the lad looked to have potential...' The scene opens to show Venneman Pendragon, still without his TV Title, sitting atop his white mount Xerxes in the middle of a busy intersection. The sun is just setting and he does not look happy to be stuck in such slowly moving traffic. Passersby stare at the man clad in a golden shirt of armor and leather pants, but Pendragon pays no mind. He realizes that when one spots greatness, it is hard to look away. Occasionally he will give a nod to his awestruck subjects, but he tries not to show them that he notices. Finally, tired of the wait in traffic, he pulls the reins to the left and canters Xerxes up into the sidewalk. He passes a couple of mounted police officers who stare at him, dumbfounded. He stops and smiles.... Venneman: Can either of you lads tell me which way I need to go to get to China Town? I have some urgent business... The two cops smile knowingly at one another. The fellow before them is obviously in the market for some new drugs, and Chinatown could be the place to get them, if Opium is your poison... Cop 1: How come you wanna go to Chinatown? Your supply run out? Venneman: Excuse me? Cop2: Yeah...We can tell you're juiced up. Probably needing another fix of something nice huh? Venneman: Fix? I know of nothing that is broken...Why would I need to fix anything? The two cops look at one another and bust out laughing. Pendragon's eyes burn with an almost insane rage, and finally, he bubbles over.... Venneman: False Protectors of the peace in New York....Will you deliver to me the information I seek, or must I RUN YOU THROUGH??!! The two cops stop laughing and look at the strange spoken Venneman Pendragon. They also notice his sword, Excalibur that is belted to his waist. His right hand rests on the hilt, ready to unleash steel death upon the non-accommodating peace officers... Cop 1: Run us through...No need for that now...Head down one block and turn left. Ride straight on. When you see the Chinese billboards and such, you're there...Although, I must tell you that if you are looking for trouble in Chinatown, you will be sure to find it dressed like that... Venneman: Trouble is not what I seek, ye purveyors of infidelity. I seek information. Nothing more. Information that could affect events the two of you would never comprehend... With a nod, Pendragon kicks Xerxes into a trot. He weaves through the throngs of people, but the camera rests on the policemen a moment longer. One of them speaks into his radio.... Cop 2: Yeah, headquarters...Do us a favor and pull all of the patrolling units out of Chinatown right away....What's that? Yeah. Something big is definitely gonna go down there......No...There will be no need for police assistance....Ten four. Cop 1: Do you think it's wise to allow a man who is obviously tittering on the brink of insanity walk into Chinatown dressed like that? Cop 2: He looked like a big boy. He can handle himself. Besides...What gave him the right to bark orders at us? The other cop shrugs as the scene flashes back to Venneman. He is now crossing a street that goes from hustle bustle New York style to Hustle and bustle Asian style. Signs in Chinese dot the buildings of the borough known as Chinatown, and Venneman stares oddly about at all the Asian people who stare back at him. Obviously he is new to the place.... 'It's like they took a little piece of Asia and dropped it right here in New York. At least, it looks like they did. I only hope information on he who calls himself the 'Vietnamese Viper' is one easily found. I must admit, I do not fully comprehend where the deep seeded anger towards whites comes from in the young man. From what I know of history, the Vietnam Conflict was over long before he was born in nineteen eighty three. I guess that was why I now search for answers; And that looks like as good a place to start my search as any....' Pendragon heads off in the direction of a building whose English written undersign reads 'Saigon Sally's'. As he approaches, a young Asian man seems to almost rise up from the sidewalk in front of the joint. Dressed in black pants and a white wife beater tank top, the sunglass wearing young man stands directly before Xerxes.... Youth: Whatchoo doin 'ere in Little China? You look like you belong in a #$%@ing museum! Venneman: I have questions. I seek answers. Youth: you may have questions, but I tell you the answers are not in there.... Venneman: I believe I shall find that for myself. Pendragon starts to walk Xerxes past the fellow who immediately draws a switchblade and spins it quite deftly in his hand. Pendragon stops and looks at the youth with a bemused glare... Youth: Nobody goes in there without my OK....Without the Y.A.D.'s ok....You got that, whitebread? Pendragon's face lights up with the mention of Charlie Loc's crew, the Y.A.D...He hops from his horse and the fellow lunges at him with his blade. The blade meets Pendragon's chain mail shirt and bends. Pendragon chuckles as he grabs the fellow's wrist and takes the blade. He tosses it aside as he grabs the fellow by his collar and raises him up on his toes to better look into his eyes.... Venneman: Y.A.D....Perhaps you then can tell me about the Vietnamese Viper, yes? The youth's face turns ashen at the mention of his gangleader. He begins to tremble and Pendragon shakes his head.... Venneman: You would fear Loc more than you would fear a Knight of honor? Youth: I...But...He'll kill me! Pendragon draws his sword and holds the point to the fellow's throat.... Venneman: Worry about me...Worry about what I am gonna do to you.... As soon as the sword tip touches his skin, we notice a wet spot that grows larger on the fellows pant leg. Pendragon sniffs and looks down disgustedly. He shakes his head and gives the youth a toss... Venneman: Will Loc be so easily intimidated? How can you run with his crew? You are an embarassment. Go clean yourself up. I hope the people I talk to in here are not as easily intimidated as you.... 'I knew not if it would be wise to merely enter this stronghold of evil. Perhaps a good disguise is in order....' Venneman rubs his jaw and looks up the street a ways. The camera follows his view and we see what looks like an Asian thrift shop. He snaps his fingers and hops back in the saddle and gallops off to the store. He leaves Xerxes outside as he enters the place. The camera looks around, bored, when suddenly Pendragon reemerges. At least, we think it's Pendragon. He now wears a dark blue Kimono that comes down to about his waist. He has also found a straw hat, much like those worn on the stereotypical rice patty. He looks satisfied as he grabs the reins and walks Xerxes back down towards Sally's. He ties the mount to a light post and heads inside the establishment. The camera follows into the darkened gathering place. Asian men fill the joint, and every head turns to the door as Pendragon enters in his outlandish get up... Venneman: Me Asian...Me against Whitey too! Instantly, the place is roaring with laughter. Pendragon looks around and manages a small smile... 'I wasn't sure if this disguise was gonna be enough to let me infiltrate the place. The kimono barely covered my chest. I never realized Asians were so much smaller than me....' Venneman: I want to join the Y.A.D....As you can see, I am both. And, I am Asian! A man with a scar running down his face walks over and looks under the hat. He turns to his buddies with the hat in hand.... Scarface: Nice blonde hair on our Asian, hey boys? What say we teach this white bastard a lesson.... Pendragon's face lights in surprise, but he nonetheless rips the kimono off and backs into a defensive position. In his best voice of command, he speaks.... Venneman: I am the Lord King Venneman Pendragon. All I seek is information. I stand amongst you with hands open. I am your friend. I only wish to know about Charlie Loc, the Vietnamese Viper... Instantly, the laughter stops and the whole bar rushes the towering Pendragon. Soon he is covered in Asians and is wrestled to the ground. All at once, the pile rises up and bodies fly everywhere. Pendragon has found his feet and he now draws his sword... Venneman: IF YOU VILLAINOUS SCOUNDRELS WILL NOT HELP, THEN YOU CAN PERISH BY THE BLADE OF EXCALIBUR!!! The men in the room draw their weapons as well, guns and knives and what not. The place is ready to explode in violence when a voice from the back rings out.... STOP!!! Everyone, including Pendragon look to the back of the room. There stands a man who wears the robes of a monk. His head is shaven and his eyes sparkle with peacefulness. As he walks into the room, the younger Asians instantly calm. Even Pendragon appears to have calmed a bit. The monk walks closer and speaks for Pendragon to hear.... Monk: The still shall not last for long. If it is truth you seek, you must come with me. Venneman: Truth. Aye. For I am a Crusader, and my mission is important. Monk: Then you must follow.... The monk leads Venneman through the bar. Pendragon stops and waves his hand in front of one of the men's eyes and gets no reaction. He scratches his head.... Venneman: How... Monk: Never mind...Come now! Venneman follows as the monk reaches the back wall of the place. He places his hand upon the wall and a door appears. Pendragon stares as the door opens and the monk walks through. He shakes his head and follows. The door shuts and we are met with the wall once more.... 'I knew not what kind of sorcery the old man was using. I only knew that he told me that if I wanted to find the truth, I needed to follow. Who was I to mess with what could be nothing short of magic??' ****FLASH***** The scene is now in what would appear to be some sort of laboratory. There are vials of this and that scattered about a room that houses shelves, a desk some chairs and a small couch. The monk motions for Pendragon to sit on the couch as he grabs something from a shelf. He returns with a scroll, and he lays it on the table by the couch. He unrolls it and lets Pendragon take a peek... Monk: This is an ancient scroll of prophecy. It reads that a man of the past will arrive with a weapon of antiquity. He shall save us from ourselves and lead us to our brightest hour.... Venneman: And you think that's me? The monk nods and Venneman stares down at the scroll.... Venneman: What do I have to do? 'I had a feeling this was going to get me into something that I did not need, but I cannot turn my back on those in need. Though I had troubles of my own....Syko still has my title, and I was just itching to get my hands on that swine. Seems like every time I get close, though, someone is there to save him from me. That, or I fall prey to a villainous trick. Now I have to defend a title that I have not even worn against a common criminal. A man who is boastful of his misdeeds. If those in control of PCW are evil, then those who book the match must be controlled by an even higher power. Why else would the evil doers in the company have to face the wrath of the Lord King Venneman Pendragon? And now, it seems I am anointed of another calling....Where will I end up next?' >>>>>TO BE CONTINUED
