Green & Redneck: The Colors of X-Mas

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 17.12.02 (#1 FF) Abner scratches his head in confusion. “A handicap match? A freakin handicap match!” Abner lunges out in anger. Quickly sweeping the table with his arm, the glass wine bottle comes crashing onto the floor. Staining the light gray carpet, the dark colored liquid seems to be an omen of Abner’s fate: being beaten to a bloody pulp at the hands of two PCWers, Duzza and Ali Khadaffi. “Abner cool it now, just cool it,” Teresa instructs her companion. The hostess bends over and picks up the pieces of glass left by the shattered bottle. “Yeah,” Duke adds in, “For Christ’s sake, you just wasted an alcoholic beverage!” Abner lets out a sigh as he flops back down into his chair. “You’re right Dukester, you’re right…..alcohol abuse is not the way…..” His two fellow rednecks break out in laughter, and finally Abner joins in too, satisfied with his own smart remark. And it’s been quite a Christmas party. In the company of PCW prez, Duke Williams, and PCW great, Teresa Tomas, this redneck feels at home. But nonetheless, after Duke received the cell-phone call from the Front Office and then promptly relayed it Abner, the Director of Redneck affairs did not take it too kindly. What a situation. Barely a weeks vacation before he’s back in action to face not one, but two men……both more experienced, and both more “prepared” than him. But naw, when it really comes down to the wire, this redneck is prepared. Heck, Abner’s yet to have seen Duke lift a single weight since they first became acquainted nearly a month ago, and yet he’s world champion caliber. Besides, it’s common knowledge that for a redneck, the bottle is more effective than the gym. So with this in mine, Abner gulps down another shot of whiskey, compliments of Teresa Tomas. “Who there partner, not too fast!” Abner looks up from the finished glass, and Duke from his bottle. Both eyeing her suspiciously, she grins, “Just kiddin’!” And again, they burst out in laughter, the three rednecks having a marvelous time. “Heh! Osama bin Khadaffi, and Sadam Duzzain…..Arab bastards, I’ll show them who’s boss….” “And who’s HOSS!” Duke chips in. “Yep. Come hell, 747 or high water, those sons-a-whores be screamin’ for this ol’ boy to let ‘em be. I’ll teach those boys a thing or two ‘bout rumblin’! “And rum!” Tomas can’t help herself. “Yep, that too.” With that, Abner pushes his glass forward. Obediently, Tomas fills it with a shot of whiskey. In one quick motion, she whips back her head, raises the glass, and downs its contents. “Wh-wh-what!” Abner stammers. “Hell, who do you think I am, your bitch?.....bitch…..” Abner scowls as Duke lets out a chuckle. “Huh, well as Director of Redneck Affairs…..I……I…..I need another drink!” In one swift motion, Abner launches forward and retrieves the bottle from the table. Filled with what appears to be about 10-15 shots left of the vile liquid, he begins to chug away. “Yee-haw!” Duke gladly shows his report. Tomas on the other hand…..“Damnit, Ab, you’ve got yerself a lifetime supply of beer, and yet, you must go on a’ead and drink up all mah whiskey! Shame on ya Abner, shame on you!” Grumbling under his breath, not fully opening his mouth in fear of losing some of the precious liquid, Abner gurgles down the rest of the contents. “Well then, how ‘bout this…..Duke?” “Yeah, Hoss.”: You president of PCW, you apart of the budget planning committee, right?” “Yep.” “Then why don’t you file it in so that all those members on the roster who want some Goddamn beer, charge for it on PCW’s consent?” “Well Hoss…..what a great idea!” “But….” Tomas flashes Duke a dirty look. “I mean, what a stupid Goddamn idea! I mean, it just wouldn’t work ‘cause….well…..” He turns and looks toward Tomas for support, “well……I don’t really know why it wouldn’t work…..sounds good ‘nuf for me, Hoss!” “Yes, but Mr. Dukie sir, y’all know that ol’ Ass-Kannon be the one in charge of PCW’s financial board. Sure, you could make a suggestion, but the final say is his.” “Hoss shit!” Abner cries out, “I mean, Horseshit! Yep, that’s Horeshit alright.” “Horeshit, Hoss, Horseshit!” Duke tips back his head and takes another swig from his bottle.Suddenly, Abner has it. “Well then, how ‘bout you add in a stip’lation ta Dec’mation?” “Keep talkin’…..” “See, make a lil’ ol’ deal with Mr. Xavier-Whore there. You win the match, PCW pays for our alcohol…..” This time, it’s Tomas’ turn. “And if he loses…..” “Well if he loses…….we……still get free drinks?” Duke reaches his hand across the table and gives his director a smack. “No hoss, that ain’t how it works.” “Oh…..well then…..well then…..well if you lose then, then you gotta come with that part of the bargain then……Goddamnit, think I’m a rocket scientist of something?” Tomas and Duke look at one another and shrug. “Well alright then hoss, mighty fine idea there indeed…….So I’ll certainly suggest it. But I don’t know ‘bout my stipulation now, so I’ll have to think on it, and announce it shortly thereafter .” Abner nods in approval. Tomas seems a little worried…..(maybe it’s the whiskey though). “Okay, but one more thing”, the young women adds in. “Whatever happens……whatever…….happens……aw shucks, all this talkin’ makes me kinda thirsty.” “Here, here!” Duke nods, and they all reach for their respective bottles. And so together. glass after glass, bottle after bottle they continue their night of drinking and partying,
