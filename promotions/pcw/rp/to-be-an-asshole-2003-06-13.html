To be an asshole...

13 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6-13-03 ~ The scene opens up to Ganker Ransolver on the PCW airship a week before his match with Shane Robertson. Ganker is in his quarters reading a copy of PCW Magazine. Ganker puts down the magazine as his girlfriend Sheilla Hisser enters and sits next to him. Ganker wraps his arm around her and flips on the television. As soon as Ganker realizes there is nothing on, he shuts it off immediately. Ganker kisses his girlfriend on the top of her head and stands up to stretch. Ganker walks over to the door to his quarters and looks back at Sheilla.”Yeah I’m going to take a walk around. Maybe find some lucky fan and beat his milk money out of him.” (laughs0 “I always managed to find amusement and in the misfortune of others.”~ Sheilla laughs and lounges out on the couch to take a nap, the scene the shifts to Ganker stepping out of his quarters and shutting the door behind him. Ganker walks down the hallway and into the cafeteria of this airship. Ganker gets some food and finds a good place to sit. As Ganker begins to chow down, a fan approaches him with a 8x10 and a pen. Ganker signs it then holds out his hand as the fan looks at Ganker a bit confused.”Five bucks kid, or you don’t get this picture of yours truly back. The price of an autographed photograph of yours truly costs you.”~ The fan digs into his pocket a bit hesitant and hands Ganker five bucks. Ganker then hands him back the picture. As the fan turns around to walk away, Ganker kicks the fan in his butt and makes him fall. Ganker laughs as he picks up his food and throws it away. Ganker walks out of the cafeteria and down the hallway back to his locker room. Ganker bumps into a stagehand, or the stagehand bumps into Ganker. Ganker grabs the stagehand by his shirt and slams him against the wall across the hall. Ganker then throws the stagehand’s head through the wall and empties his wallet and shoves whatever money had into his own pocket. Ganker continues to walk on as he leaves the stagehand with his head in the wall. Ganker gets to his locker room then looks at his door. Ganker then walks past his door and further down as he notices the airship hasn’t left the ground yet. Ganker hurries off for awhile to do some last minute mugging of fans. Ganker walks around the PCW airship with his hands in the pockets of his jeans. Ganker bumps into a woman who is trying to board the airship with her three children. Ganker feels generous and helps out the woman. The woman then turns to Ganker.”Thank you sir.””You know you wouldn’t need help if you had each kids father here to take care of his respective child, but then again I wouldn’t expect a woman like you to be able to keep a man that long, let alone three. Hell you can’t even manage yourself. I helped you out of pity, not because you needed help, but because you are pathetic.””You’re mean!””Yeah you think I’m mean, you should see me when I’m all warmed up! Then I can really be one vicious son ofa b-itch. You don’t even know, you mangy w-hore.””You wrestle here don’t you?””You’re damn right I do and I’m their next World Champion! So you better be paying your respect you lil’ w-hore.””You can only be Ganker, the biggest loudmouth asshole in PCW!””You’re damn right I’m a loudmouth asshole and if you have a problem wit it you can suck on deez nuts!” (grabs his nuts with his left hand) “You’d probably prefer me putting them in you though.” (laughs as he takes his left hand and rubs one of the kid son the head) “You enjoy the show, you mangy w-hore.””Oh I will, every second you’re getting your ass kicked.””Oh trust me I’ll be getting my ass kicked, I’ve grown used to that, but I’ll also be kicking ass and winning the damn match! You know you’re jus’ as pathetic if not more so than my opponent, Shane Robertson.””Well I hope he breaks you in two!””Right…”~ Ganker roundhouse kicks the lady in the stomach and walks off laughing at her despair and suffering. As Ganker is walking away from her he is approached by some tall guy who is a stagehand for the PCW, helping load the aircraft before take off. This tall guy looks down at Ganker and then looks at the woman he kicked.”What kind of heartless person are you? How in the hell did a man like you get signed by the PCW?””Simple my friend didn’t want his opportunity so he threw it away to me and more importantly how in the hell did a dumb ass like you get signed to carry the ring!? I’m not sure I trust the ring if you’re putting it together. As a matter a fact you’re fired cause I don’t like you.””You can’t fire me, you’re only a wrestler.””Yeah well if the boss knew you were taking time off to pester one of the wrestlers about what he does out of the ring, then I’m sure you would be. Now get back to work you mangy asshole, hell maybe on your break you and that b-itch over there can work on her fourth kid. I’m sure she’d be most willing to do a one night stand wit’ you. Hell if I didn’t kick her I could have tapped that all night long! However I have a girlfriend whom which I love and she ain’t worth losing her.””I hope Shane Robertson beats some respect into you boy! You really need to grow up.””Yeah well you’re the second person who says Shane needs to beat me, but the thing is he wont’ beat me cause I’m so much better than he is boyo. As much as people want him to beat me and teach me to show some respect and pay homage, he won’t. There ain’t nobody big enough to maim my damn ego! I know I have a big ego and I’m damn proud of it! So shove off asshole, I have an airship to board and a match to contemplate, unlike you who simply throws a wrench around and drinks beer wit’ your buddies.”~ Ganker walks back to board the airship after tripping the guy for giving him trouble. The scene cuts off ot black.
