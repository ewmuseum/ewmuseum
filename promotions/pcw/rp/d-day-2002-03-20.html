D-Day

20 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3-20-02 **Note: Now I've introduced the school. You're welcome. Let the fun begin.** ----------------------------------------Dear Jenna, Back when I ran the CWS in Detroit, I had staff. I had assistants. I had money. But nope, don't have ANY of those now. It's just me and the kids, fighting for our lives in this big, abandoned warehouse. I haven't had this much fun since I was a kid. Love,Jason---------------------------------------- Jason: Alright! Let's take it from the top! [Here we are, in home sweet warehouse. Day One of the CWS. A fantastic Wednesday afternoon. Sitting in chairs or on the floor are nine of the ten CWS students, as J-Core looks on approvingly.] Jason: Alright...Gimmick Lesson One: How To Sell Yourself! [In the ring stands the hulking and sweating form of one of PCW's all time...uh, Extreme Champions, The Violator. Violator's countenance, usually grim and menacing, is confused and maybe...just a little...scared?] Violator: I...I... [Jason looks on encouragingly.] Jason: Sell it! Violator: I AM THE VIOLATOR!!! [Jason nods.] Jason: And? [The Violator puts on a trademark Violating Game Face.] Violator: PREPARE FOR TOTAL VIOLATION!!!! [Jason, and the rest of the CWS applauds heartily. Violator's evil countance is almost replaced by a beam of pride, but that's quickly brought into another grim look, as he exits the ring.] Jason: See, folks...THAT'S selling yourself. You must believe in yourself, and your gimmick. [Violator takes a seat on the floor next to 'Emo' Eddie Russo, the very stereotype of an 'emo kid', and Eddie gets up and hustles away, under the pretense of asking Jason a question.] Jason: Now, who wants to go up there, and sell themselves? [A couple hands raise quickly, but the perennial wise-ass, Nick North, stands up and begins walking to the ring.] Nick: I got this sh*t... [Nick slides under the ropes, and springs to his feet. He looks at the assembled class, and flexes...muscles bulging underneath his open polyester shirt. There are two wolf whistles from the crowd, from Jamie and 'Pegleg' Paula Hampton.] Pegleg: Arrr~!! That's a fine piece of ass! [Even Nick is caught off guard, and he blushes, as he turns to leave the ring.] Jason: Whoa! Where are you going, son? [Nick stops.] Nick: The ladies are sold, J-man! [The sixteen year old Jamie pipes up almost instantly.] Jamie: F*cking right we are, you stud! [Jason rolls his eyes.] Jason: Knock it off, Jamie. [He turns back to address Nick.] Jason: Do you wrestle for female-only audiences? [Nick winks over at Sandra Davis, the lovely young blonde, and flashes his cocky grin.] Nick: Only in bed. [Jason rolls his eyes again.] Jason: Are you going to take this exercise seriously? [Nick scoffs, and runs his hand through his bleached-blonde hair.] Nick: No way, man. Why should I? This 'selling yourself' stuff is just bullsh*t. Shouldn't we be working on real moves and stuff? [Jason shakes his head.] Jason: I told you, physical conditioning for a while first, at least a week. Not all of these guys are in competition shape. [Nick points at the heavily overweight Kyle Andrews, who sits, watching.] Nick: Hell yeah, his shape's ROUND! [Nobody laughs but Nick...as Kyle begins unbuttoning his flannel short-sleeve shirt. Kyle goes to stand up, but...] Jason: Sit down, Kyle. You'll have your chance. [Jason removes his shirt.] Jason: You wanna mock people, North? You want to prance around in that ring like a prick and flex, and play to the ladies, and to cut people down? [Jason walks calmly up to the ring steps, and steps in.] Jason: Is that what you want to do? Do you WANT to end up like Big Daveylicious? [A couple of students laugh, because they're intelligent, and know J-Core has a match with Big D on Sabotage.] Jason: Do you want to end up...like THIS? [Very quickly, Jason rushes Nick, and hooks him into the Second Chance! He keeps Nick pinned, despite his futile attempts to escape.] Nick: Hey! Lemme go! RAPE! [Jason's breath is a bit forced, with the struggling guy under him.] Jason: Jokers like Big D who think with their Small D will ALWAYS end up losing. [Jason releases Nick, and gets to his feet. He reaches down to help Nick up, who is still selling his back from the Second Chance.] Jason: For god's sakes, Nick. At least learn to cut a good promo, okay? Better to be Switchblade than Big Daveylicious any day of the week, huh? [Nick nods a little, his pride hurt. He walks to the back of the chairs, but stumbles and falls over Kyle's outstretched foot.] Kyle: Looks like your shape is PINNED punk! [Most of the crowd laughs at Nick's expense, but he just shrugs it off. Jason remains in the ring smiling.] Jason: Alright, Kyle...you're next. Get in here and show us what you've got! [And Kyle gets up, to try and sell himself, as our scene fades to black, on the first day of gimmick training in the CWS...]
