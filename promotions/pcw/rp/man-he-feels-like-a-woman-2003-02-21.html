Maybe. Maybe not…in any case this had happened and left Skitzo feeling strange. Very strange. Flash forward to today. Friday. Fusion was fast approaching and he was ready. Today was a new beginning, and while he had spent three and a half days reflecting on his current state, he was ready for the future, and the punishment Brandi would receive. For now however, he had a title to retain and a list of things to do, all to be revealed soon enough. He had suffered a Mental Breakdown of his own, and the time to HeadFuck someone was nigh! The End
