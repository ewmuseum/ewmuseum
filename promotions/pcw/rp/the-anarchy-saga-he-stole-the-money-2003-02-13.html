The Anarchy Saga: He Stole the Money...

13 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2-13-03 One thing has bothered me more then any other when it comes to the downfall of Intelligent America. It isn't necessarily the fact the President has a combined IQ of less then the coldest temperature in Minnesota, and although it pisses me off, its not the fact that the American people blindly follow stupid trends and stupid people as far as they can, without research, without education, without anything of importance. No, what really bothers me is the fact that with all this strife in the world, with all this pro-... Fuck exposition. I hate Kangaroo Jack. I hate the fact that this movie made hundreds of millions of dollars, and it is a sole example of how STUPID America has become. Movies like this make more money then nine-tenths of the world will ever have in their lives, when good movies that involve thinking and actually not just guffawing at the guy from Sliders and a fat samoan chasing a marsupial in a ugly leather jacket around for a hour and a half. So here is the story of the rise and fall of the creator of that god-awful movie. As told to me by Luci himself, so forgive me if there are any glaring omissions. He is about as reliable as a Republican led-government. --- There are only a few things that can produce a terrible idea in the mind of a human being. One, drugs. Two, stupidity. And Three? Money. Or in some cases, all three of these things combine to make one idea that shatters the bounds of all moronic diplomacy and proves points for years to come. Jerry Bruckheimer was one of those people that was ruled by money. It was the only reason he still did the movie thing, because he knew he could squeeze a quick seven dollars out of the American public. The American public didn't realize that his movies were all explosions and no substance, The American public never realized that there weren't talented actors... there was instead, Vin Diesel. So Jerry Bruckheimers pockets were ALWAYS lined with green, as long as there was a lack of intelligence to capitalize on, there would be people like him to do a job that Monkeys could do and get paid for it, and no... I don't mean basketball. All of his biggest movies were done the same way, except for this one. You see, Jerry Bruckheimer knew he was a untalented hack who had no actual skills in ANY part of the movie industry, he just happened to be in the right place at the right time with the right O shaped face that could give the right person in the right company the right kind of dick sucking. So the only way he could get jobs early in his career was to dress in a ballet outfit and take it in the ass from Movie Producers. They referred to him as Jerry Ass Fuckheimer. Yeah, it isn't creative, but like I said... these were the guys that are making Terminator 3, Rocky 19, Weekend at Bernies 3: Attack of Rigamortis, etc. You get the point. Creativity was the furthest thing from these guys minds. After a while, the money didn't make his asshole feel any better. He wanted out. But he did still enjoy the hard coc-... cash that came in his as-... pockets ever time he made a movie. So about a year and a half ago, he drank a bottle of Jack Daniels, took a few tabs of blotter acid, and laid down in the back of his 4-Runner and went to sleep, hoping something would come to him in his mind. It did. 'Psst.' The voice was faint at first, Bruckheimer wasn't sure if he was still assleep. 'Hello meat puppet. Pay attention. Are you really stupid enough that you can't see me in your own mind.' He opened his 'minds-eye' and saw him. Standing there in all his glory. Fire spit up behind the huge muscular man, his red tinted skin and well-defined muscles almost caused Jerry to have an orgasm right there. The long tail, the horns, the black goatee... it was obviously Satan, Lucifer, the Devil. What did the Devil want with a little man like himself though? 'Jerry, Jerry, Jerry. Wipe the makeup off your face... and take that stupid dress off. I have come to you to give you a offer you shouldn't refuse.' Jerry looked at him with hope in his eyes, wiping the smeared dick-sucking-lipstick off of his mouth, 'Wh--What could it be?' 'SILENCE AND LET ME FINISH!!!!!!!' Satans voice boomed loudly in Bruckheimer's skull. 'I have come to make you a offer, in exchange for unimaginable fame and wealth, and the power to have anything you direct become a multi-million dollar hit at the expense of the intelligence of the nation, you will give me your soul and face the hell-fires for all eternity after your death. I will give you twenty-fou-...' 'Deal!' Bruckheimer squealed like a little girl and clapped his hands together, Satan just stared at him with all the contempt that he could muster, 'Well, ok then. Uh... I'm not used to it being that easy, but... Yeah. I'll be leaving now.' With that, Satan dissapeared in a brilliant burst of flame. Bruckheimer woke up in a cold sweat in the back of that SUV. He decided to put this to a test, to see if it was only a Dream, or to see if Satan really did imbed the power in him to take advantage of the lowering IQ's of the public. So he decided to make a movie full of cool cars, Angelina Jolie half naked, Nicolas Cage being a tough guy, and more cool cars, he also decided to interject no plot, and no major points that need any intelligence to comprehend at all, figuring that if he threw a 67 Shelby in there, then the idiots would salivate at something they would never even be able to touch and not worry about their intelligence leaving, that was also the reason he decided to put Angelina Jolie in it. Gone in 60 Seconds then made 200 million dollar. It was true! Satan did give him the power! Bruckheimer rejoiced, realizing now that he didn't need to get plugged in the ass by Movie Execs to get jobs, thanks to Satan, and for three years he took advantage of this fact, making more and more money. However, he began to get cocky. He began to take on projects that had NO reason to even be close to 100 million dollar hits, he let his fame get the best of him and nearly forgot the reason he was making this money, the only reason he was making this money. He nearly forgot how untalented he was, and how less then four years ago he was tossing 50 year old married men's salads just for the oppurtunity to get a script to direct. He truely thought that his talent could make any movie popular, so when a script arrived in his mail box, he rejoiced... he could almost see his little naked gold man awards on his mantlepiece because of this one. He read over it and laughed as hard as anyone could at a movie, he realized that this would be his Waterloo (he said that not knowing that it was the last stand of Naploean, then again most American's don't know that nowadays anyway.) He muttered the catchphrase to himself, louder, and louder, revelling in its genius... 'He stole the money... and he's not giving it back!!!!' So Bruckheimer directed the monstrosity, and just as he thought, the money came rolling in. America loved it. They loved the fact a fat samoan would put thousands of dollars in the pocket of a jacket and then put it on a fucking Kangaroo, that would then STEAL the money and NOT GIVE IT BACK, because all Americans KNOW how much Kangaroo's LOVE money, RIGHT? Morons. Anyway... Bruckheimer loved this, he didn't care that this one fact would almost doom the educational system, he made so much money he could now afford a gold plated rocket car. He stood outside of his large mansion, under the palm trees he ordered to be uprooted and flown at great expense to his house, and he revelled in his corruption of intelligence. He revelled in it so much he didn't notice that noose lowering from the top of the palm tree. He certainly didn't notice it go around his thick neck. He did, however, notice when the blue haired future-murderer began to reel the noose up the tree, Bruckheimer dropped his coffee cup and watched it shatter on the ground, his eyes bulged, his face turned blue, he got higher and higher off the ground. His ear drums popped, but before they did he heard his killer say 'Don't take it personally, this is a blow against capitalism, not personally you. Plus a friend of mine wants your soul...... and he's not giving it back.' He faded away, DarkStar just let his body fall from the top of the tree. Its not like anyone ever caught him when he killed someone, and it had really been so long. It felt great, especially because it was for the cause. Hours passed, maybe even days, and Bruckheimer woke up. He thought to himself 'I'm not dead! It was just a dream!' He sat up and oriented himself, looking around at his surroundings. He noticed that everything seemed warm, fire spit from the cracks in the molten rock on the ground, rivers of lava flowed in every direction. It wasn't a dream.. He began to panic, even more so when he realized that he was wearing a pink ballerina outfit, smeared make up and all, tears ran down his cheeks as the irony set in. 'So, Jerry, do you want it rough and painful? Or... what am I saying, It's really my choice.' Satan undid his leather, evil pants, and let them drop to the ground. Bruckheimer screamed his heart out, but he would have to get used to it... Afterall, it was only eternity.
