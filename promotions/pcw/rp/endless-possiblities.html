Date: not entered “The great Caesar has decreed that this next PCW contest shall be scheduled for one fall…” The crowd around the arena cheers its approval, leaping from their marble seats and throwing their arms into the air! We rejoin our Big and Bold hero, Big Alan Steele as he walks out into the open air of the Roman Coliseum. Full plate armor, glinting gold in the sun, decorates his massive physique. He looks down and paws at the hard-packed dirt floor, checking his sandals for traction in this new environ. The fans roar at his appearance from the warrior’s tunnel, chanting his name and anointing him their favorite. Looking up, he drinks in the scene, the culmination of all his subconscious fantasies. He throws his arm into the air, pointing his scimitar to the heavens. Big Alan Steele: “Hi-Keeba!” The gathered throngs eat it up, raising their wine goblets in tribute, roaring ‘Hi-Keeba!’ in return. Steele grins ear to ear. Suddenly, the mood of the Romans changes drastically. Skulking his way out of the opposite warrior’s tunnel is The Chef, his charred blackened armor a striking contrast to the area, seeming to suck up the light around him. The fans, still standing, raise their arms and turn their thumbs down, their voices and gestures all disapproving. He snarls and turns, swinging his net and trident wildly, as if trying to deflect the thunderous chorus of boos from reaching him. Steele grits his teeth and starts forward, to the appointed starting area at the middle of the combat zone. Chef also makes his way there, both men eyeing each other warily. They both stand shoulder to shoulder, and face the finely adorned upper section, where the great Caesar sits among his Praetorians and high-ranking Roman officials. He lounges on a red satin bed, lavished with fine pillows. The two gladiators bow to the royal box, raising a hand high. Both: “Hail Caesar!” Caesar lazily raises his hand in acknowledgement. Both combatants face each other, growling nastily, and slowly back away from each other five paces. A huge man in a mask swings a monstrous baton into a wide bong three times. BONG! BONG! BONG! **Flash!** The scene drastically changes, and Steele looks around in confusion. Looking down, a leather jacket covers his expansive frame over his traditional toga, a cock-eyed leather hat hits atop his lush yet manageable hair. A bandana is wrapped around his thick neck. Steele reaches up to feel the uncomfortable material, but his raised hand holds a small knife. Craning his neck, he looks down his back: the word ‘Jets’ staring back up at him. His other hand is tied to the Chef, whose glint with madness, as he holds his own switchblade in the other hand. He is dressed similarly, leather and scarf, only darker and dirtier. Big Al grits his teeth as he gets into a stylized crouch, weapon held back threateningly. The Chef also gets low, and the two begin to circle each other ominously. Other PCW wrestlers circle the two combatants, faces a members of the ‘Jets’ and heels belonging to the ‘Sharks.’ They rhythmically snap their fingers while gracefully moving counter to the two tied together, acting as lumberjacks in this PCW match on the waterfront. The Chef snarls at Steele, his lips curled in a true expression of hate. Chef: (singing) “You afraid pretty boy? Afraid… chicken? You yellow-bellied chicken… dumb Greek!” BAS: (singing) “When you’re a Greek you’re a Geek all the way! Hi-Keeba!” The two slash at each other with wicked arcs of their blades… **Flash!** The sky is orange, red streaks staining its canvas. A huge fortress made apparently of copper floats in the sky, propelled to its heights by magnificent turbines underneath. Flocks of men fly in and out of the city in the sky, held aloft by their wings, breathtaking in their grace and beauty. A huge, robust bird-man sits upon a throne laughing, laughing, laughing. He carries aloft a huge hammer that three ordinary men would have difficulty even lifting let alone wielding with any efficacy. He tilts his armored helm forward and grins through his thick beard down below. A platform sits out in the open air connected only by a single pathway. Big Alan Steele steps out onto the path warily, his quasi-futuristic toga shimmering in the dying rays of the three suns. The bird-men warriors assembled along the sides of the fortress walls bang their hammers against the side of the wall, the sound deafening. The Chef then also emerges as the bird-men continue their salute to the combatants. Chef takes up a position opposite Steele, feeling the tensile strength of his whip as his chef’s hat glints menacingly atop his head. The loud banging stops as the leader of the bird-men stands, holding his arms aloft. “Here, in PCW, we settle our disputes with BATTLE!!” A rousing cheer from the assembled. Big Al levels his eyes on the Chef, whose eyes are burning holes in Steele’s chest.” Bird-Man Leader: “So may the gods above watch over you, as the winner shall for once and for all end your dispute eternally through the death of your adversary! Now… BEGIN!!” Steele cracks his whip against the platform, testing his weapon out. The Chef begins to advance, holding his whip back, poised to strike. Big Al snarls and does the same. Crack! The Chef rips through the strange material of Steele’s toga, gashing his tender man-flesh with a well-placed snap of his whip. Steele flinches at the pain, but never falters. Another crack echoes across the platform, as the Chef sends another shot Steele’s way, but cat-like reflexes allow him to catch it in his free hand with a wry smile. Big Al yanks hard on the weapon. Chef refuses to let go and falls forward. Steele rushes towards Chef, who has already rolled to the side, swinging his leg out for a foot sweep maneuver, catching Steele in the shin and toppling him over. The two then engage in some grappling, each trying to get the upper hand in the battle… The bird-man leader grows wary and yawns at the lack of deadly action. Flicking open a panel on his throne, he snaps a few switches. The platform groans and begins to rotate while tilting on a fulcrum. Steele begins to slide backwards as the tilt reaches him and he has top scrabble to maintain his balance and not fall off the platform to certain doom. The Chef tries to seize the moment and screams, charging at Big Al, who snaps his whip forward and wraps it around Chef’s ankle. A quick pull lifts Chef up into the air and crashing hard to the metal platform. The leader, still not completely amused, snaps another switch. The Chef, trying to regain his breath, hears a strange sound under the platform. He quickly leaps to his feet just as spikes shoot up from selected parts of the platform. They random slide up and down on the tilting platform, creating a deadly and unstable arena for battle. The bird-man leader guffaws loudly. **Flash!** The Chef chuckles, wiping a small trickle of blood from the side of his mouth. Chef: “I have to admit, Alan, you have impressed me. You truly are worthy of being a WRESTLORD. Join me, and together we shall destroy worlds like gods!” Big Alan Steele looks across the sky at his floating adversary, the ground far beneath him. His toga is in tatters, his face stained with dirt and blood. He breathes heavily, but his anger gives him strength. BAS: “I have told you once before, and it shall remain true! I am no longer a WRESTLORD! I am from Earth! Hi-Keeba!” Chef snarls, an aura of energy crackling around him, furious at Steele’s betrayal of his other-worldly ancestry. Chef: “You dare deny the blood of the WRESTLORDS running through your veins?! We are a rare breed Alan! The last of our fighting kind! We should live to preserve that heritage, yet you throw it away to live with these HUMANS!” The chef spits out the word ‘humans’ with about as much scorn as you would reserve for ‘Chicago Cubs.’ Chef: “Then so be it! Ready for the pain?!” BAS: “Have at you!!” The Chef strains and growls, tensing up in midair, his body shimmering with intense energy swirling around him in visible currents. Steele also powers-up, a huge halo of power flowing outwards form his body, shooting off sparks of intense power into the sky. Suddenly, both men’s hair turns golden, their eyes green, as the energy increases exponentially!! Great heaves of earth begin floating up into the air. The clouds scatter as if chased away. Both men explode in rage and power, their auras crackling as they touch. Chef rears back and screams, a huge ball of energy forming in his double-cupped hands. He brings his hands forwards, firing this massive torrent of power. Steele disappears and reappears safely to the side, a huge spinning disk of intense energy whirling in his overhead palm. With a ‘Hi-Keeba,’ he sends it screaming forth, the Chef somersaulting away from the deadly cutting force of the energy blade… **Flash!** Big Alan Steele grips the tethers of his chariot, gritting his teeth against the onslaught of dirt and dust kicked into his face by the team of eight horses in front of him. A whip crackles in the air, and Steele roars in surprised pain, as it sears his exposed flesh on his shoulder. The Chef, riding in a chariot of his own, grins maliciously as he snaps another crack at Steele. Big Al puts up a forearm to protect himself, coming away with a long welt from his elbow to his hand. Chef cackles with glee as he aims shot after shot at Steele as both men guide their horse drawn chariots around the first turn of the Hippodrome. The crowd cheers wildly as the bell rings, indicating the final lap! Chef guides his chariot dangerously close to Steele’s, spinning blades equipped on his wheels coming precariously close to Big A’s chariot, all the while cracking his whip at our hero. The blades begin sinking into the wood of the wheels, shards of the spokes flying off behind them. Steele desperately grabs at the reins with both hands to guide his horses away from the imminent danger. Chef’s chariot shoots ahead, leading the race. BAS: (frowning) “Hiyah!” Steele snaps his reins forward, urging his tiring horses forward. Up ahead, a previous wrecked chariot lies in ruins, but Steele’s sharp eyes see something of use. Leaning far to the side, Steele reaches out and manages to stab his arm out and grab a hold of a spear. The crowd cheers as the final turn comes up, Steele snapping his reins, his horse gaining on the Chef in the final turn. The Chef looks back and snarls at Steele, raising his whip menacingly. Steele gives him a tight lipped smile and raises his spear. Both men enter the straightaway, neck and neck. BAS: “Hi-Keeba!” With a powerful throw, Steele shoots the spear across. Chef’s eyes open wide in alarm, as the spear engages in the spokes of his chariot. The chariots lurches forward flipping over, just as the finish line is up ahead… **Flash** A huge tank of water, Big Al in a wetsuit, as well as the Chef. Both men desperately struggling, daggers held out, as sharks circle menacingly. **Flash** A dusty Old-West scene. Big Al spits out a toothpick, his hand poised over his holster. He tips his hat forward and steps backwards, eyes never wavering. The Chef, dressed all in black right up to his chef’s hat, licks his fingers and blows on them, confidence oozing from his features. He steps back as well, hand moving towards his gun. Chef: “Well partner, it’s your move. Draw!” **Flash!** Big Al snarls across the table, looking over a handful of cards. Dressed in a fine Armani toga, large crowds gawk behind him, staring at the huge sum of money piled in the center of the table. The Chef sits across the table, in a pin-striped suit, his hat tipped elegantly to the side, also peers over his cards across the table. Steele motions to Chef grandly, indicating, ‘Your move.’ Chef: “Do you have any nines?” BAS: “HA! GO FISH!!” Chef groans and grabs a new card. The crowd mixes its reactions, some people groaning as well, slapping money on the table, other crying out with glee, snatching up the very same dollars. The Chef looks across the table and grimaces, then, in a very, very feminine voice speaks. Chef (Girly voice) “Al? Hey Al? Yoo-hoo, Big Al!” **Flash!** We re-rejoin our Big and Bold hero, Big Alan Steele, sitting at the kitchen table in Stately Steele Manor. Candi, one of the Graces, is waving her hand in front of his face trying to gain some sort of reaction. Steele shakes his head and addresses her. BAS: “By the gods, woman, what is it?! I must determine what course to follow in taking on the accursed Chef this PPV!!” Candi: “Well, duh Big guy! Sue already sent that in like a week ago!” BAS: “Really?” Candi: “Yeah. She said, and I quote, that she didn’t want to wait for ‘some fool plan of yours that wouldn’t be feasible anyways.’ So she just made it an ‘Everywhere no DQ match.” BAS: (angry) “She dares interfere with my righteous and holy vengeance?! I shall have words with her… where is she?!?!” Candi: “Um, she said she was going to try and get you your old job back by trying to file another injunction.” Cut to a court room scene, where Sue is addressing one of the pale skinned lawyers of Doc Healey on the stand. Sue: “So, is it true that you are the un-living embodiment of pure evil?” A lawyer in the other side stands abruptly. Lawyer: “Objection! Relevance!” Sue: (with malice) “I withdraw the question. Still, would you mind if I poured this holy water on you…” Lawyer: (panicking) “OBJECTION! OBJECTION!!” Back to Stately Steele Manor. Steele frowns and slumps forward with a sigh. BAS: “Should she not be successful, I may not even be allowed to participate in the PPV!” Candi: “Oh, I would doubt that, Big guy. You’re a commodity!” BAS: “Huh?” Candi: (casually) “Oh, I don’t know. I heard Sue say it before. Another fluffer-nutter sandwich?” BAS: (growling) “No thank you, as I must prepare diligently for tomorrows contest of strength! Hi-Keeba!” Candi: 'Assuming they let you inside, natch.' BAS: (eyes narrowing dangerously) 'I'll be inside one way or another...'
