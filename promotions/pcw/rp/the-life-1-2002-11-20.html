The Life - 1

20 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11.20.02 Perhaps this story should start in the past... The locale was Dez's Harlem Eatery, a late night joint where many a rap star could be found schmoozing, dealing, or celebrating their so called artistic accomplishments. The night was in fourth gear, flying about as a fast as a Funny Car driven by John Force. Playaz Inc was present then, only three of them then, minus an actual leader. None of them had intentions to show some bloodshed and carnage that night. Somehow, someway a disorderly patron weaseled his way into the VIP circle and bounced off patrons like an out-of-control bumper car. Playaz Incorporated alumni Ali Khadaffi did not appreciate the fender-bender. The tight spot got even tighter. Understand this about Dante Jackson. First, he's a tough pretty boy, willing to let his mouth run and his ego burn for days. Second, he's big on appearances and this melee was not exactly a good look at the place he would usually consider Cheers. 'Everybody cool the fuck out!' Dante yelled. His command was that of Patton, no bullshit, just straight to the point. Resident gangster Statuz Quo was already going for his guns. Feuding parties, however, continued to bicker. You should also know this about Dante: Insubordination is a unwelcome thing around him. Khadaffi was again instructed to be the bigger man. The disorderly patron disappeared through the frenzied crowd, unheard from the rest of the night. 'It's alright,' Dante assured everyone as they quickly sat back down. Situation defused, bomb squad go home. The Present... 6:00 am... The alarm buzzes on the clock as if it were a median for life and death. No stopping, until his fist slams down on the timer, shutting it off permanently. He groans, picking himself up from the bed and stares outside a dark lit morning, 7 days away from what would be the absolute testament to his wrestling career. Alliances. The slight knowing of the Pay Per View happens to dawn upon Dante as he blinks quite a few times. He cracks his neck, before laying back down and rest his head along the pillow. In a matter of days, its going to be Playaz Incorporated, practically the somewhat favorites if Dante does not happen to become Cruiserweight champion which would pretty much cement him as a legitimate threat to any singles gold in Primetime Championship Wrestling at the moment. For a second, he thinks over the set-up for the match, the giant three ring structure, nearly every man on the roster ready for warfare. World War 3, a World Championship Wrestling pay per view created in 1995 solely for booking to think on who to place in the Main Event for the federation's big Pay Per View - Starrcade. Set up was like this, sixty men divided by three and placed into three separate steel cages. Last man standing wins that shot at the World title. Its how Alliances is, a juggling act for many superstars and their make or break motives. He sighs, before turning over the face his telephone, plugged into the wall circuit so that anybody and everybody who had his number could get reach of him. He reaches over, pulling the receiver off of the hook and holding it to his ear. [detail withheld]. Ring. Ring. 'Hello?' the voice picked up the phone before the third ring. Dante sighed to himself, 'Yes, may I speak with Kenneth Watson?' 'He's not here at the moment, can I leave a message?' 'Yeah, tell him that Dante Jackson called to tie up loose ends.' He gently placed the phone back on the hook, before lifting his legs up from their resting position and began to walk away from the bed. Stepping into his bathroom, he peered into the medicine cabinet looking over the vials of various over the counter drugs. Advil, Tylenol, all types of headache, cold, and herbal pills to make him feel better. Shutting it shut, he turns to look at himself in the mirror. After two days of partying in opening up the lucrative estate, he hardly could sleep anymore. He had tired eyes, red all around, body in ache and pain with a slight stench of Cristal` on his lips. 'I've got to tell Quo to ease up on the drinks. He'll kill a brother.' he groaned, before pulling back the drawer containing his toothbrush and toothpaste. An earthly voice rang through his ears, 'Morning honey.' 'Good morning.' The wife, just about as needy as anybody else is one of the focal points in Dante's life. It's only natural that when success comes knocking on the door, there are only few that can take it away from you. Tracy ran her fingers through her auburn hair, coming around in ends sort of a down home girl style which Eleanor Hall-Kannon has popularized during the past ten months in PCW history. She silks out of the bathroom in a bath robe, quietly known to the world as the prince to the black champion of PCW.Dante raises his head, minutes after shaving it completely bald, a Ja Rule impression as he steps out of the bathroom, a platinum Playaz Inc. medallion hanging around his neck. He sleeps in Playaz Inc., breathes Playaz Inc. In his words, he'd die for Playaz Inc. The next Cruiserweight champion walks back into his bedroom, looking over his closet which happens to be filled with just about every different fashion design in the popularized urban hip hop world. He eyes over all of the throwback jerseys and blue jean shorts to match, before picking one. An Old School San Antonio Spurs jersey, matching with black shorts dragging down past his buttocks. Looking down at his shoes, he dusts off his Air Jordan IX tennis shoes in custom sliver and black, a trait that he always carries for himself. Alliances is the biggest show of the year, as Dante begins to take his very first steps out of his house, nearly a lighthearted smile on his face. 'Where are you going Dante?' the wife of the star asks. 'Settle stomething up,' he remarked. 'My past is still treading along dark waters.' Dante hops into his 2003 Nissan 350Z, carefully weaving out as his wife looks on, a bit worried for her husband's safety.
