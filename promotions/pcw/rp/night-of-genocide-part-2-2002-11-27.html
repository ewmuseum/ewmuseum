Night of Genocide (part 2)

27 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 27-11-02 ***20/11/02** Joey Cuppi and Pietro Darilloare as nervous as they have ever been as they sit behind the dumpster outside the house where their first mission is set. They have to break into the house and shoot everyone in there within two minutes before moving onto two more houses and doing the same thing. They have to be in and out of each house in two minutes which makes a total of six minutes to kill everyone in these houses. The black gang that the attack is aimed at are having a meeting at the leader G-Dawg's house which leaves the black men's families home alone. Woman and Children will be slaughtered inhumanely tonight. There are two other teams that have the same missions only on different streets. Bob Vurdo has set all this up yet he doesn't have to do a thing. He doesn't have to kill a single person yet he will benefit from it all. Bob Vurdo has ordered a distraction for the police at 1.58am giving the men enough time to get in and out before the police can get to the other side of Amber Hill. Joey looks down at his wristwatch and presses the light button emitting a green glow from inside the watch revealing the time to be 1.57am. Pietro looks reluctantly over not wanting it to be time. If they do this they are made men if they back out their dead men. They have never been as scared as they are now. The two look over at the house on last time and quietly wish themselves luck. Joey turns on the light on his watch as it hits 1.58 the men get up from their crouching positions beside the bin and begin to move over to the house. They silently walks up to the door and pull their guns out. Joey kicks down the door knocking it cleanly off it's hinges. Pietro follows close behind Joey as they walk through the hallway and move toward the first door. Joey opens up the door to the right and Pietro opens up the door to the left. They point their guns into the bedrooms and walk in and take a look around to find no one. They merge together back into he hallway and move toward the last door in the small house. Joey opens up the door and barges in on the three black woman talking to each other while smoking inside the small kitchen. The woman look up in amazement and cower immediately Joey and Pietro move over behind the table where the woman are crouching. Pietro straightens his arm and prepares to fire the first gun shot of the night which will not be the last. Joey turns his head away from the gun and cringes as he fires five shots into the small area where the woman where crouching. Joey doesn't even look down at them instead he begins walking away. Pietro takes a look down when his face is over swept with a look of disgust. Pietro begins walking out of the house with Joey as the men prepare themselves for the next target. Joey and Pietro emerge outside as they hear another five gun shots go off from other parts of the neighborhood. They begins jogging down the street toward their next target for the night. ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ Bob Vurdo sits calmly in his chair sipping at his glass of wine as he watches over the city from the verandah on his mansion. He looks down at his clock and watches it tick for another five seconds before it hits 1.58. At the exact second the clock hits two o'clock an explosion is heard from the east side of the city. Bob laughs as his plan unfolds perfectly. The explosion is the perfect diversion away from the gun shots all the police and firemen will be rushing to the explosion site where they will find nothing. Five gun shots echo throughout the city followed by another five but they will go unnoticed until the morning or at least ten minutes later when the police think it was just some kids prank gone wrong. An evil grin comes over Bob's face as he takes another sip of his wine. ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ Pietro and Joey kick down the door of the next house they are meant to break into and slaughter everyone inside. Pietro and Joey storm into the house this time trying to do their job as quick as they can. They move through the hallway quickly and bust down the first door they come across. A woman is sleeping with her young son in their bed when the mother looks up at the two young Italian men with guns drawn. Pietro and Joey fire two bullets into both the woman and her son before walking back out the door. As soon as they enter the hallway tears begin rolling down Pietro's cheeks. He never thought he would ever kill a man yet alone an innocent child and woman asleep in their beds. He only got in the business to make some money as he was unemployed. Pietro slides down the wall into a sitting position where he holds his head in his hands and begins crying openly. 'Get up,' Joey says coldly he knows what he is doing is wrong but he is strong enough to ignore it while the adrenaline is pumping through his system. Pietro just continues crying. 'I'll have to kill you if you don't get up your only dead weight to me if you continue this crap.' Pietro places his arm against the floor and begins pushing himself up. 'I hate this.... I hate myself.... Why did I do this?' Pietro is hard to understand as he is crying so much. 'Lets keep going.' Pietro nods out of fear and reluctantly turns and starts walking down the corridor. The door at the end of the corridor swings open and a large black man bearing a handgun shoots two bullets down the corridor. One hits Pietro square in the head as his brains splatter all over Joey who is lucky to be alive as the second bullet missed by mere inches. Pietro falls to the ground in a dead heap as the blood begins gushing from his wound. Joey acts quickly and fires five bullets at the black man who is out of ammo. One hits him in the chest as he collapses to the ground clutching as his ribs. Joey emotionlessly wipes the mixture of his friends blood and brains off his face. 'This is fucked! Its fucked!!!' Joey can't believe what he is doing. He has lost his fearless approach now with the death of his friend. He can't go through with it. Joey turns around and runs out the door. He's going to run away from the situation. He hasn't even thought this through properly. Joey runs off down the street toward his house where he will cry himself to sleep.
