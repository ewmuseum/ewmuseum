The Dismissal Of The God?

16 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 16th of August, 2002 And so it came to pass that The Neo God, a being who himself had felt more pain and anguish than most mortals had in two lifetimes, was banished to the opposite of his own realm. Earth… The place was like nothing he had ever had to encounter in the thousands upon thousands of years he’d existed. It soon came to him that the people of this particular realm were vile and disgusting, and so with all his malice he travelled forward with a plan to bring down unstoppable pain upon those who challenged and opposed him… The year, you ask yourselves? 2002 A time when more goings-on filtered into the inferior atmosphere of the plains, a time when more people were dying through their own greed and cruelty, and a time when warriors were stepping up to meet their peers and prove to the higher-beings that they were not a passing breeze, but in fact a consistent storm. The God loved the challenge, he loved to see how these mortals would step up, as it were, and face off against some of the greatest challenges of their lives. He sat, with stern eyes, and gazed at the place about him. It was true to say that the rush of events didn’t stop at all…they just continued to flow. More and more revelations came to pass each and every day and he couldn’t help but become entwined within the strong and complex cobweb of the earth. The warriors he knew could be beaten but they had strong hearts and the spirit of man can easily overcome the callous of a God such as himself. Even a God like him –a God of the void… Thus he looked at the warriors and saw that they were not something to be easily underestimated… For a long while he watched, learnt and listened to all of their ways, and gradually he discovered something that he found more interesting than anything he had come across – it was possibly the key to his overthrow of the world. Man’s will power For the will of men could be broken by their unquestionable greed for power, and it was the soul of a young Egyptian man he took in and twisted until he was pleased and almost certain that the being in front of him was immortal…and filled with gluttony… But the true colours of that person shone through the God’s darkness. He was of good intentions. The mortal was guided by his will power to take on and defeat the God, driving his arrogance and superiority complex back to the void from whence it came. Victory The Egyptian won… and nothing more was heard of the two. Their roads fell aside and they walked upon different paths…constantly waiting until a time when they’d once again meet and fight one another in an epic battle of unadulterated hatred. DATE: 20TH of August… the year 2002… Titu Xansiv knew what sort of fate lied ahead of him. Once again he’d have to face the monstrosity he’d grown to know as Akiyama…The Void God, The Neo Void God… The Absolute Destiny Apocalypse. He didn’t want to face the creature again – who, with a head upon their shoulders, would? He groaned as he looked down at the paper in front of him. Upon it the words ‘The Pharaoh Vs Akiyama Vs Duke Williams Vs Chris Chase’ were found. Yes he’d be paid a great sum of money to face the men…and the God, but was it truly enough? He knew deep, deep within the caverns of his heart that it wasn’t. The beast had done more to the men who had grown to know, love and hate, with a single punch than a heavyweight boxer could ever do. The Hand of Fate That’s what he called it. The devastating punch that could even rip through the hull of an ocean liner with ease was what awaited him within the confines, if they could even be called that, of the squared circle. He shook his head mournfully. He didn’t want to face it again. It wasn’t that he was scared…he’d just had enough the first few times they’d met, that was all! Its terrible eyes…its deadly hands… its…devastating power… That’s what he feared – the God’s thirst for power… He knew what it thought like. He knew what it wished…he even knew, as unwanted as it was, what it desired to do to the human race. And destruction was all that could quench its undying thirst. The evil thirst for blood that fuelled its wretched body… He stood, looked out of the window and watched as the people milled about the ground below in the fashion of ants. Raising his fist, tightening it as he brought it near to his chest, he uttered a few choice words. “You will find your end in my destiny, beast.” He edged across the room towards the table that sat silently within the office’s boundaries. Sitting down he read through the paper again. “If El Diablo can keep it back, then so can I…I’ve done it before and mark my words I’ll do it again.” There was the other issue involving the God, however. “Cyclonia…” He sighed, eyes meeting the paper, but blankness enveloping him. She was a monster in her own right. But he had grown accustomed to bringing down the monsters of the organization he worked for. She wouldn’t stand in his way of destroying the beast… and what could she possibly do, anyway? He winced as he thought that through again. Actually she could do a lot, he realized. She was much more powerful than Akiyama…that had already been established, time and time again. Threat no more, though. For this was but wrestling, not some argument from outside work! He had no troubles with the monstrous creature now…right? Once again he’d have to defeat it… he knew that. But Frisco would possibly stand in his way of doing so… Frisco - the metaphorical roadblock in his path towards success. He sighed as the door flung open and a dozen men dressed in camouflaged military uniforms entered. “What are your orders, milord? Are we to go to Turmoil with you?” He clenched his fist. “No,” he said. “I have to do this alone…” And he would…and he HAD to…
