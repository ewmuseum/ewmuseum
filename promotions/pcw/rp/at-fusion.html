Date: ----- Skylar Green storms into the back from a door leading to the parking lot. He kicks the door open and storms in. Some nearby technicians jump at the commotion. PFIK is also in the area, and rushes over with a microphone, the camera following him. PFIK: Skyla--ack! A large right hand thrusts forward and clutches PFIK by his throat, stopping his air. He is lifted off of the ground from this hold as Skylar walks and is tossed to the side. Skylar walks off. PFIK rubs his throat. PFIK: I guess he's not in the mood to talk. The cameraman brings something to PFIK's attention. Cameraman: You've got something... in your pocket there. Looking down to the outer breast pocket of his black suit jacket, PFIK sees a note hanging out. It wasn't there before. PFIK: Did he? He didn't use both hands... The cameraman shrugs, bobbing the camera up and down slightly. PFIK removes the paper and reads it. PFIK: Hm... Cameraman: What? PFIK: It says that Violence Jack has his match. At March Madness, Skylar will fight Jack in a No Disqualification match. The roar of the crowd for the possibility of Violence Jack getting some comeuppance is heard through the walls of the arena, picked up over the camera's built-in microphone. PFIK: Well, if I was Violence Jack, I would be more than a little concerned about my safety. Cameraman: Speaking of which, you might not just want to step up to Skylar like that. At least not until he cools off. PFIK: It's okay, we're buddies. Cameraman: Didn't he push you into a cactus pit the week before Alliances last year? A look of pain comes over PFIK's face as he remembers the incident. PFIK: Hey, yeah, you're right! Screw him! Oh, God, he's coming back! PFIK and the cameraman scramble as Skylar returns from down the hall, dusting off his hands. He was only gone for a few moments, but there is a red substance on his ring attire...
