There Goes The Neighborhood

11 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5 - 11 - 03 Facing the dark, sinister face of his partner Titu Xansiv, Dorn Tracer is a little less than enthusiastic. But he’s not facing him in the sense of combat; he literally has to look at him. Titu’s face is still slightly bruised from the Fusion main-event against Skylar Green, but his demeanor and pride could very well require critical hospitalization. His eyes burn with a hatred beyond a thousand Egyptian suns, and his voice is gruff and blunt. You can almost SMELL the venom on his breath. As the Egyptian Madman stares down at a magazine article like a spider over its pray, Dorn merely cradles his head in his hand and props his arm up on the wooden motel table. ‘This is foolish, Titu. You’ve been staring at that article for at least half an hour. I thought we were hear to discuss strategy?’ Dorn inquires with obvious hints of irritation and impatience ringing through like the chimes of dark bells. The Pharaoh doesn’t even look up from the magazine, but The Astronomer knows he’d love nothing more but to give him a dirty look. ‘We are, my friend. First phase of strategy is to research and build a foundation on which to settle your operation. We cannot merely go running in blindly and hope to succeed. Strategy is very necessary, but forethought is vital.’ The Pharaoh roughly flips the page over and continues his skimming of the article, completely delved into it. Dorn straights up and throws his hands up in defeat, walking over to a chair sitting in the near corner and plops himself down into it. ‘Whatever you think. You realize, of course, that I have a match with Tessa Windsor this Tuesday that I could be preparing for?’ The tone of his voice is very sarcastic and Dorn knows it. In fact, he LOVES to irritate Titu just because the Egyptian king responds so hatefully. However, this time, Titu’s response is calm, and I dare say uncaring. ‘I’m aware. Are you worried?’ The dead air that follows Xansiv’s abrupt and somewhat rude comment is about as unsettling as a tornado alarm. The Pharaoh slowly turns his head and gives a snide look to The Astronomer, who returns it with a raised eyebrow and a frustrated smirk. Tracer adjusts his shades and clears his throat as if to deter the subject, but when The Pharaoh’s gaze doesn’t leave him, he suddenly bursts out angrily. ‘Worried, Titu?! Why would I be worried? It’s a woman, for Ar’Katan’s sake! The most she could do is choke me with her tits, and even then, she’d have to get close! No Titu, I’m not worried.’ ‘Then why the concern for preparation?’ The Pharaoh’s question catches Dorn by surprise and Titu realizes it. A grin crosses his dark face and he turns his head back to the magazine article, knowing he’s got the Astronomer boxed into a corner. That said Lunis Superia rises from his chair like an angry child and begins to pace back and forth within a diameter of about 3 feet. His boots click against the dusty wooden floor and send eerie creaking noises through the room. A concentrated Pharaoh looks up with his eyes every moment or so, wondering when it’s going to stop before going back to reading. When Dorn shows no signs of stopping, Xansiv suddenly smashes his fist onto the table, nearly splitting the weak furniture, and turning an enraged look to his partner. ‘DO YOU MIND?!’ He roars at the top of his lungs, causing the scurrying noises to sound from the next room. Dorn stops in mid-step and gives him a shocked look, ‘What are you talking about?’ ‘STOP THAT INFERNAL PACING! PLEASE!’ The Pharaoh’s plea comes out enraged and somewhat desperate, as if Dorn’s pacing is eating away at his person. The Astronomer raises an eyebrow at him, which Pharaoh returns with furious breathing and a deadly smirk on his face. The two stare at each other for a minute before the tension becomes unbearable and Pharaoh turns away, stomping towards the door. ‘I’ve had it with you! You’re such a child, and an infidel one at that. Grow up or we’ll end up tearing each other apart on Friday!’ Totally taken back by the comment, Dorn doesn’t even say anything for a moment before his face melts into a dark rage, ‘I’m not the only one that has to grow up. Your nerves are about as hectic with Tommy and Sky as mine are with the Druid. Bruce isn’t here because he went out to buy a knife to sacrifice Dante with. We’re all a little scattered right now with our own shit, and taking it out on each other is only going to make it worse. So lay off.’ His remains relatively calm, and it seems to pay off, as Pharaoh’s enraged look seems to die down a little. ‘I understand, but that’s no excuse to be a complete nuisance. I’m going out, figure out something for Friday,’ Pharaoh flings the door open, letting in a blast of cool air, before stepping into the door way and turning his head towards Dorn, ‘And try not to hang the motel attendants this time. Their weather jackets ARE NOT “Druid outfits.” Alright?!’ Although annoyed by the obvious attempt to anger him, Dorn simply nods and Pharaoh steps out, slamming the door behind him. The Astronomer sits down at the ‘strategy table’ and stares at the magazine article. The headline reads, “PCW’s New Power Stable Meets…Problems?’ There are pictures of Skylar Green, Dante Jackson, and the mysterious Druid. Dorn suddenly thrusts his hand forward, takes the page, and rips it out in one fluid motion. Crumbling it up in a sudden fit of rage, he tosses the magazine article away and sweeps the magazine off the table, then slams his elbows down on the surface and cradles his face in his hands. ‘Damn it all…’ [End.]
