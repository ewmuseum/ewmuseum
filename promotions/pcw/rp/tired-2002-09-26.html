Tired?

26 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 26th September 2002 Miyagawa: That’s it! Higher! Higher! It is with this call that we join Alexander Miyagawa and his tag team partner for this Friday Fusion, and also his former stable mate from last year, Jeremy “JJ” Johnson. Miyagawa: Come on you lazy assed referee! Miyagawa has JJ jumping through hoops. Literally, there’s tires strewn across the floor of this corridor in Miyagawa’s home and JJ is leaping through them like some sort of crazed person. Miyagawa: Get those knees up! One two! One two! JJ suddenly stops leaping about like a crazy bastard through all these tires and stops to look at Miyagawa, who in between shouting at JJ is drinking a can of Pepsi and had just finished eating a sandwich. Miyagawa finishes off the can and looks at JJ. Miyagawa: Don’t just stand there and look sheepish! Jump! Jump! JJ: When do I get to sit back and have a drink? Miyagawa grins at JJ. Miyagawa: When you’re back in shape. JJ raises an eyebrow at that statement. JJ: What the hell are you on? I was never in shape! I’m JJ! I win through crowd support! Miyagawa sighs as if in revelation. Miyagawa: Ah, so that would explain why you never used to win then. It’s all coming clear now. JJ lunges at Miyagawa, Miya sidesteps, catches JJ by the wrist and flips him over on his back. JJ sits up and stares at Miya before finally getting up. JJ: How long have I been jumping through tires? Miyagawa looks down at his watch to read the time and then looks back up again. Miyagawa: About twenty five minutes, why? JJ: Well don’t you think teaching me that little manoeuvre would have been a lot more useful than learning to jump through freaking tires? Miyagawa shrugs. Miyagawa: Is Duke or Bob lighter than you? JJ shakes his head as in no. Miyagawa: Well wouldn’t it be a better idea to learn to dodge then flip them over when you won’t be able to lift them. JJ stops for a second to try to think of an objection… but can’t seem to think of one. JJ: Oh… ok then. I guess that would be a better idea. So what next? Miyagawa grins at JJ. JJ: What? Miyagawa: Hop through those hoops again and on the way past the kitchen, grab me another Pepsi. JJ squints and stares at Miyagawa. Miyagawa: Ok, sheesh, I’ll get it myself then! Natalia walks out of a side room and nearly trips over the tires strewn over the floor. She looks towards Miyagawa. Natalia: Tidy these up. Now. I don’t want to know where they all came from. Miyagawa and JJ look at each and shrug before starting to collect them all together.
