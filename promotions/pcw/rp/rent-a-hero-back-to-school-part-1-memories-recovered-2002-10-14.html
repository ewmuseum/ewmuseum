Title: Rent-A-Hero...Back To School-Part 1-Memories Recovered... Featuring: Rent-a-Hero Date: 10-14-2002 OOC:EXPECT THE SWITCH TO GOOD OL' REGULAR SCRIPT WRITING AFTER THIS LEAD-IN:) Enjoy... It seemed like a normal day at my not-so-secret lair...also known as apartment '303'. Eh, what can I say, Rent-Cave's are hard to come by these days. I sat back on the infamous black leather couch of mine, the same couch where I laid back cadaverously for hours on end filling my mind with images of anmie and other cartoons...with not a care in the world in my mind....suprisngly, I wasn't stressing over how many ways I could make Xavier Kannon I could embarass Kannon on national TV....I wasn't stressing over having to defeat the ENTIRE PCW roster to retain my hard-earned PCW Continental belt...a belt I had to best Jason Snow AND Darkstar in....a match that left me with all sorts of mental and physical sscars to deal with....and with one order by Xavier Kannon, all that hard work could be for nought when I step into that Japanese Mall on Friday. But it had no real concern to me...right now...I was concerned about ONE thing... My daughter. It was 3:30...that meant she would be coming through my apartment door any minute...and the routine was always the same... My little princess with open the door, sitting her SpongeBob Squarepants bookbag down, don't ask me why she wanted that tacky thing...a Cheetara ThunderCats bookbag would have been soooooo much better, and removing hair long locks of golden hair from the pigtails I'd set her up in. Then, she'd rushes to me....locking me in a warm embrace that always made me feel special...Not a lustful embrace or a friendly embrace...but the kind you recieve from someone who loves you deeply, like family. Then she'd proceed to tell me AAAAAALL about her day...from the goofy kid who'd eat worms at lunch time, to her assumptions on who had cooties...I was glued to her every word. What kind of father would I be if I wasn't? With that....the door creaked open....and there she was...the number one woman in my life...my daughter... Amy A-Hero. She came through the door in her blue jean dress, her small frame always fascinating to me...for I found it incredibly intriguing for such a little girl to have such a large and adventerous personality. She sat her book bag down as expected....but as I rose from the couch...expecting that mad dash of hers that would end in that embrace I was always used to...the plan changed....something was different about today. I looked at her face...normal to me. Her clothes....dirty from wrestling with thsoe mean ol' boys, but normal. Then I looked at her twat and tiny little hands, the purple fingernail polish chipping already as I looked to a piece of white paper in her hand....she slowly approached me until she was right before me, looking up to me as if I was Goliath as she passed me this mysterious white letter before asking me with her usual inqusitiveness. 'Daddy.....tell me about your past. Tell me...how you became you.'I stood frozen for a moment....looking down upon her as her soft blue eyes pierced me with loving intrigue.....she REALLY wanted to know about who I was...where I came from...how I came to be what I was, I could most certainly tell. No one ever really wanted to know about my past before...it was a new and intriguing concept to me. But what prompted this question? Why today of all days? It wsn't until I unfolded that piece of white paper in my hand that it all came clear to me.'You are cordially invited to 'City High School Class of 1997' reunion party at Maxwell J. Richman's estate at 6969 Anywhere Street, Filthy Rich Estates. Feel free to bring guests and it will be a pleasure seeing you again after so long! Yours truly, Maxwell J. Richman' I can't believe it....'City High School'....1997....now that certainly was a blast from the past. I'm usually so busy looking forward to the newest challenge or escapade of the day...I forgot about how it all came to be...the days when I wasn't a superhero....The days when the PCW fans knew NOTHING of my existance...The days when I was living with my mom and dad in that three bedroom house on 1234 Happy Lane... The days when my only worries and cares were when Sky Surfers was coming on, if I'd ever pass the Algebra test next week, and if I needed to weara bigger msk to hide my pimples... The days when my heart belonged to the most PERFECT girl I ever met in my life...Erica Angel. Those memories...once vaue and locked away in the bellows of my mind, flowed freely through my mind yet again like a river through the most tropical of rain forests. I looked to Amy, her eyes still looking to me and obviously waiting for answers to her question...my past important to her. She was my little princess...my heart...my treasure...and whatever she wanted, she would have.She wanted my past....And she would get asmuch as I could provide her. I snatched her up without warning from the ground, my little angel kicking and screaming as if she HATED being lifted off the constrictive ground below and into the heavens via my arms....but I knew and she knew that the higher she could be in the air, the happier she was. I carried her over to the couch with the ease of carrying a backpack, plopping down upon it as I placed her on my knee, my little angel awaiting the story of how I came to be who I am. Reaching under the middle cushion of my couch...I unearthed something I never thought I'd see again.... My photo album. I smiled, brushing the dust away with my gloved hand as it scattered intot he air, Amy's eyes glued to the worn brown leather scrapbook as I opened it wide, the pages creaking with decay as it revealed glossy plastic pages....my finger pointing to one picture amongst the collage of pictures on both pictures.... A picture of a young man in a blue glitter suit and a Captain America mask next to a beautiful young women with cinammon stick colored hair...her dress the thinnest of white satin and looking nothing short of perfect upon her godly bodess as the two hold each other close, white and blue balloons floating about in the background as the two smile wide for the camera. With my eyes locked on the photo...my little baby's ears open...I began a tale I thought I would have never had to tell... 'A long long time ago...in 1997...before there was Rent-A-Hero, Amy...there was a 17 year-old superhero fanatic....named Jayson.' And so....the story begins ---- View Rent-a-Hero's Biography
