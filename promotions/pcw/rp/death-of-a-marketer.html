Death of a Marketer

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Thursday, December 5, 2002 Deep within the heart of the Transylvanian back alleys, the mysterious being known as The Dark Gimmick lives. He's a hard one to understand. Gimmick is an enigma to say the least. He has no family outside of Clyde. He has no friends outside of the 'satanic jobber' community. And even those guys aren't his friends. And Dark Gimmick surely has no one to look up to except for the Greater God of Evil, Satan. Nobody knows who Dark Gimmick is, where he came from, or why he is the way he is. The only 'known' about this man is that he's dangerous. Very... VERY dangerous. Any single, solitary thing that has no regard for anything else, and has no fear of any repercussions it may cause, is a danger to society. And that's what The Dark Gimmick is. That's what it appears he wants to be. The Dark Gimmick is a danger to the known world who, by all accounts, can't be stopped. That is The Dark Gimmick. Right now, it's pitch black in the back alley. There are a few dim lights, but not enough to clearly allow one to see what's ahead of him. We follow the path of a man in a grey business suit. He appears to be a middle-aged man, but we can't get a clear enough view of him in the darkness. As the man walks in and out brief moments of light, we see that he's carrying a brown briefcase. It appears almost as if he's come to the Transylvanian back alleys on a business trip. We move in a little closer on the man to see he's a white, middle-aged man. The guy has some wrinkles on his face signifying that he's probably in his 40's. Along with the grey business suit, the businessman is sporting a blue tie. He also appears to be slightly afraid. Is he afraid of the dark? Is he lost? Whatever the case may be, he looks a bit scared. But, still, our man with the briefcase marches on. Businessman: He...hello!? Is there any......ah! As he talks, he crashes. The man accidentally bumped his leg against a small, metallic trash can on the ground. The can falls over and some trash falls onto the ground. The business grabs his left shin and grabs it... stopping in his place. Businessman: Jesus christ. Someone should put a street lamp here or something. This is... Before the man can finish the sentence, a voice interrupts him. Voice: (interrupting) I... should kill you. The businessman lets go of his shin. He looks around to see where the voice came from. As he looks, we spot a shadow roughly five feet from the fallen trash can rise up. The businessman, however, doesn't see it. Businessman: Who... who's there? From out of the darkness steps the source of the shadow... Dark Gimmick. He walks into the dim light and stands right in front of the businessman. The businessman, now looking more scared than before, starts to quiver in fear. He gives up about a foot and at least 100 pounds to the deranged man. Businessman: (shaking) Who... who... who are you? Gimmick, dressed in his cloak, looks down at the puny man. Gimmick: I... am The Dark Gimmick. Businessman: Dark Gimmick? For some reason, the once scared businessman now breathes a sigh of relief. Businessman: Phew! For a second I thought I was in trouble. Dark Gimmick tilts his head, almost not believing what this man is saying. Business: Darky, my name is C. Morass. I am the senior vice president of marketing for Shazbot brand soda and Shazbud alcoholic beverages. Morass extends his hand as if he wants to shake hands with the sekeltal freak. DG stares at the man's hand with a blank stare. he does nothing. Morass mouths 'Ooo-kay' and pulls his hand back. Morass: Anyways, you are just the guy I came to this dump to see. Gimmick stares the businessman dead in the eyes with a blank stare. That, combined with Gimmick's silence, start to spook Morass out a little. Morass: Umm, like, you aren't, umm, the talking type, are you? Dark Gimmick extends his right arm out. With his hand, he grabs C. Morass by his throat and starts choking him. Gimmick: What... do you want? Dark Gimmick lets go of the Shazbot marketer's neck. Morass coughs a few times and attempts to catch his breath again. The look of fear slowly, but surely, comes back to his face. Morass: (voice trembling slightly) I... I... I came here to...... I came here to talk to you about our... our product. As Morass gulps, Dark Gimmick looks down at the briefcase Morass is holding. DG begins to focus all his energy on this brown briefcase. As he does so, the briefcase starts shaking. Morass: What... the... devil? Morass wisely drops the briefcase. Once it hits the ground, the briefcase pops open. Inside the briefcase sits a can of Shazbot Orange Soda. Gimmick tilts his head to the right slightly. He then bends over and picks up the can of soda. DG looks at the can, then looks back at C. Morass. Gimmick: What... is this? Morass: W... W... Well...... Morass begins to rub the back of his neck. His face starts to turn a little red. The marketing guy loosens his tie just a bit. Morass: (nervous) It... it's a can... a can of soda, Dar... Darky. C. Morass takes a couple of deep breaths as Dark Gimmick examines the can of soda. The Shazbot man calms down and regains his composure. Morass: We at Shazbot made some slight alterations to our product. And, as our spokesperson, we figured what better way to taste it than with you. Morass has a dorky smile on his face as he explains things to the dark man. Morass: So, if you would, could you taste it? Dark Gimmick continues looking over the can, completely ignoring what C. Morass has to say to him. Morass: I know it's probably a little warm, but it should still taste good none the less. Dark Gimmick feels that he's examined the can long enough beause he's stopped. The evil ref turns his attention back to Morass. Gimmick: You taste it. Morass: I... I don't think so. I'm... umm... allergic. Yeah! I'm allergic to Shazbot Orange Soda. As Morass makes up some lame excuse, a black cat runs into the picture. The cat leaps up into the arms of Dark Gimmick. Gimmick looks at the cat. Gimmick: You thirsty... kitty? Morass looks at Gimmick holding the cat. He looks a bit curious. Morass: Is that your cat, Darky? Gimmick: Hold this. Dark Gimmick hands the cat over to C. Morass. The business holds onto the cat with both hands. With Morass occupied with the cat, Dark Gimmick opens the tab to the can of soda. Gimmick: Enjoy! With his left hand, Dark Gimmick grabs the hair of C. Morass. He uses the marketing man's hair to yank his head back. With his right hand, Gimmick starts pouring the soda down the throat of Morass. C., not having any other choice, start drinking the soda. After downing the entire soda, Gimmick throws the can to the ground. Morass: Ugh! Morass looks down and notices that the cat is no longer in his arms. Morass: Hey! Where'd your cat go? Dark Gimmick's eyes start to glow an evil reddish color. Morass is confused for a second, but that goes away as his face starts to turn to a ghost white. There appears to be some movement in the businessman's stomach. Morass: Uhhhhhhhhhahhhhhhhhhhhh! Suddenly, from out of Morass' chest comes the black cat. Morass, of course, collapses to the ground and dies. His blood and internal organs flow out of the large hole in the middle of his body. The cat, for some mysterious reason, is completely clean. It stands in front of the dead body of C. Morass and meows. Dark Gimmick looks down at the black cat with a blank expression. He then casually walks over to the dead body and grabs a kidney that has fallen onto the ground. The cat meows again. Gimmick takes the kidney and chucks it down the alley. The cat, wanting the kidney of Morass, chases after and leaves. Dark Gimmick reaches back down to the body and grabs a rib. Then, without hesitating, DG throws the rib bone as hard as possible against a nearby wall shattering it into pieces. DG looks down at the shattered bone and grabs one of the long, pointy pieces. Dark Gimmick places the piece of bone in his mouth and chews on it almost as if it were a toothpick. Dark Gimmick walks back over to where the dim lighting ends and slumps back into the darkness. Fade out
