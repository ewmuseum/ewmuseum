-Reminiscence-

11 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 09.11.02 ~*~ _____________________________________________________________ Date: 09.11.02Time: 9:10 AMLocation: New York, New York.___________________________________________________________ Fade in... 9...1...1... The numbers that signify a phone number for medical assistance needed, on an emergency?Or the very date of which today is an anniversary of, when pandemonium and mass demolition hit New York City and the Pentagon? I think 9.11. was a day when both fit quite perfectly. The scene opens on a Manhattan street in New York, New York. The streets are mostly deserted, save for a few civilians passing by, watching something unseen with teary eyes. Most wear clothing and accessories in red, white, or blue...with screen printed American flags, pictures of the Twin Towers, nationalistic logos, eagles...and such symbols of a fateful day a year ago from today. Many walk with their heads low, stifling tears, remembering lost relatives, friends, companions, lovers...or just that very day—the tragic, disastrous one that made them sit up straight and change themselves. Unbelievable, and demeaning...how it took that much for Americans to start embracing patriotism and nationalism.For people to stand up and be “proud to be an American”; to bring out their little star-and-stripe-adorned flags, bandannas and t-shirts......For us to reflect on our past, and move to change ourselves in the future... We should have appreciated what we had all along...but at least we’ve learned now......Hard way’s better than no way. The camera pans to the street signs. One reads, in a pale green— WEST STREET Then, the other, to the left of it, reads— CHURCHILL AVENUE This area lies just blocks from Battery Park. The camera finally pans to the endless territory, all fenced up. The mass of rubble once known as the World Trade Center...the very place one blue haired goddess loved visiting in her little family trips as a little girl. She had lived miles away in Queens, New York...but by the Subway, it was just 45 minutes away. What was left of it was merely debris. A few construction sights are still up...but all that’s left is a big, gaping hole... Yes, the area once the immense building of several businesses...that the Twin Towers once rose out of... Heartbreaking, it was.Tourists stood by the site of the obliterated twin giants. Camera flashed, as these curious visitors snapped away with their cameras. Others held video cameras, as their film rolled, capturing a view of the site of mass destruction. The buzz of the President, the Governer and Mayor coming down to Ground Zero later that day to make speeches filled the lips of those tourists. Plans were made to appear at those events... I was awash in my memories...my thought...my feelings... as I stood by the site of ground zero one year from the day that left a gaping hole in the hearts of Americans. Oh, how it disgusted me to watch tourists photograph Ground Zero, and the immense wall that was built as a memorial for those who lost lives and those who sacrificed their own on 09.11.01.Did they do it in memory of the women, men, and children who lost their lives on that day? Such perverse interest it was...almost a violation, in my mind. It was a nail running down a chalkboard to see them taking their pictures of Ground Zero. Was this not the place they had once cried their eyes out over, holding their American flags high for? Now they stood, as many did soon after 9/11, taking images of this vicinity. It was a desecration...it seemed to me as thoughtless and shallow as taking pictures at an Open Casket funeral. Because that was so similar in my eyes to what Ground Zero looked like to me now......A graveyard... I collected a bottle of tearsDifferent colorsFor different types of pain Some taste like bloodSome taste like rainAnd there is an empty spaceFor the tears that never fell‘Cause I was too drainedI felt them repelWhen I tried to cry I couldn’t believe my eyes at all. They took pictures of the site where hijackers took the lives of thousands of innocent civilians. Did they not see the evil in it?!The outright disrespect? Ruins, they were...improved only by the hard work that was put into cleaning up the region. What an amazing job they have done...looking back to last year; when the building’s remainder was still strewn over the streets, and remains of carcasses remained for months undiscovered. When the stench was unbearable...the air, unbreathable... Now it just looked like an enormous graveyard...Almost like the site of a nuclear explosion......Just nothingness for blocks around. Too many timesI’ve tried to dieToo many timesI gave my heartToo many timesI’ve hardened up Too many timesI’ve lostToo many linesI’ve crossedBut yet they tell meI was just testing the watersThe waters are too roughedAnd I’m not old enough Tears sprang to my eyes as I remembered seeing on TV, last year, replays of those heartbreaking moments when the planes crashed into those towering wonders... ...Why...?The question haunted those who lost family members that day; or had loved ones that went missing for weeks—the fate of them unknown... Those who had been in that area of Manhattan at that unfortunate time...Those who worked in the Pentagon...those who lost family members in the flights that crashed into these buildings... And those who simply stood by, watching their televisions in horror, whether in Arkansas or New York...Canada, Japan, or Afghanistan...Wherever the Hell you were, and wherever those who mourned this tragic event were. Pain doesn’t discriminate. The Middle East wasn’t celebrating when word got out that America was bombed, and thousands had lost their lives.Maybe their governments were, who knows and cares...they’re just heartless politicians.I don’t think a single sane soul was celebrating upon hearing such news.And God help them if they did. I know I wasn’t celebrating.Being that I’m a small part Middle Eastern...I supposed you’d assume so.Like I’m some sort of terrorist, right?Isn’t that why hundreds were killed for “looking Arab” after the incident of 9/11? Don’t tell me these dying woundsThat they’ve never felt saltDon’t sayIt’s not whose fault Don’t say I’ve just wet my feetTasted loveOnly bittersweet Sometimes I believe that the fact that I hardly look “Arab” is a blessing.After September 11th, it might as well have been one.Or maybe I’d be lying six feet under today, unable to convey this controversial message today. After all, those who look like “white girls” can’t be to blame for 9/11...they’re not the ones who deserve to die for what a small handful of Egyptians; among others of Middle Eastern descent, organized. Do you think you hurt me with your assumptions...your ignorance...your propaganda-influenced beliefs?That all “Arabs” are murderers, September 11th, 2001 occurred due to a few civilians who collaborated with Osama Bin Laden. It was not a single nation that was to blame at all...we blame Afghanistan, yet the hijackers were identified to be Egyptian.And I recall that Egypt is identified to be part of Africa, whether anyone from there has the appearance that other Africans have. So you can live in paranoia of, and hate “The Arabs” for the rest of your life.You can blame every Indian and Pakistani taxi driver and Deli owner you see in New York who “look Arab” [whatever that means] for the unfortunate tragedy of 9/11. Remain in your ignorance. But you hurt yourself......And you hate yourself... ‘Cause too many timesI have wanted to leaveToo many timesNothing to believeToo many timesI’ve let go Too many timesI fallToo much to give it allBut still they tell meI was just tasting the icingI’m not old enough And that I wasn’t good enough... I just have a few words of advice left for you... Don’t contract yourself.Don’t hate others simply for being of a certain descent...it just shows your ignorant insecurities. Look at yourself in the mirror and alter a flaw before you hate another because of their background.Don’t lie to yourself.Love your country only because you love it, not because terrorists bombed it a year ago today. ...And Never Forget... Too many timesI’m scarredToo many timesI’m robbed Yet these blood tears still fall From my swollen eyesImmortalized in this bottle of tearsFrom which I cry Those words slip out of her dark blue lips, as she watches the tourists photograph, before she can stop them... “God help us all...” The scene fades with Anarchy standing in front of Ground Zero, a single tear frozen on her cheek...arms folded over her chest to preserve the remaining warmth, midnight blue hair whipping about in the ruthless, icy wind... Fade out... ~*~
