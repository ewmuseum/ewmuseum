Introspection.

Date: Friday, March 22, 2002 - 0009 EST Sue: “Hey Big guy, are you alright? You seem a bit… off today.” We rejoin our Big and Bold hero, Big Alan Steele, within the palatial confines of Stately Steele Manor. He stares out a picture window in the living room, surveying the now decaying plant life that once completely infested the lawn from the mind of the Groundskeeper. A concerned trusted manager, Sue Saksnussum, looks on as her charge heaves a great sigh that seems to emerge from the depths of his soul. He partially turns towards her, his face clearly not the jovial visage she is accustomed to seeing. Looking through his eyes, one can tell there is a lot on his mind… Big Alan Steele: (almost mumbling) “I think tis best that I should take a stroll…” Sue: (worried) “Uh… do you want some company?” But the front door is already closed. Big Al steps into the light of the sun, soaking in the warm Californian sunshine. Head bowed in great consternation, he trudges off down the suburban road. What manner of sins have I committed in this single lifetime to reap such a bounty of troubles! All has become quite… different than upon my first days within the Primetime Championship gladiatorial circuit. Allies have come and gone… foes have been fought and vanquished. But have I done my goals justice? Have I honored Zeus and the gods of Olympia in a manner that truly fits their omniscient eyes? I wonder… The suburban tree-lined streets have slowly given way to the concrete jungle that is San Jose, a bright and cheery place in the afternoon… before the shroud of night gives cover to all manner of villainy. But for now, families walk along the boulevard, pointing and whispering at the huge, toga-wearing man rubbing his chin as he follows his concrete trail to nowhere. The occasional fan runs up with a bit of paper and a pen for an autograph, which the disillusioned Steele signs, never pausing in his walk, never verbally acknowledging their presence. What has become of me? What has become of Big Alan Steele? I sought to bring a new era into the world – and era of proclamation of the true Greek gods! And while I serve as a gleaming example to all to see me, few have taken my messages to heart, and fewer still maintain that my gods even exist! I have become an anomaly in this modern world, destined to forever toil in futility at what was my most holy of missions… Step. Step. Step. Head bowed and brows furrowed in thought, he continues on stoically, impassive to the beautiful females tossing themselves at him and the more aggressive ones trying to shred parts of his toga off in an attempt to take home his finery as souvenirs. Gentle swats of his hands keep them at bay long enough for his monotonous and methodical path to disuage them from continuing their efforts. By the gods, have I failed so miserably to warrant such retribution from my angry gods on high?! My first appearances on the scene brought about much justice to a weary PCW and now I continue the path, but at what cost? I have yet to fully succeed at the highest of levels, the ultimate prize of PCW unable to be placed upon the alter of Olympus! My goal of gaining the title and then spreading the values of Zeus has been derailed time and time again… and by what? Hands behind his back and head raised to the sun with eyes closed, he takes in a deep breath of salty air and continues on, much to the delight of a ever growing group of children laughing at his comical Greek attire and constantly trying to lift his toga in an attempt to see just what lies beneath. Steele shoos them away over and over until they finally grow bored of the game and turn to petty vandalism of local properties. Each day I grow stronger. Each day I grow faster. Each day I become better and better, furthering myself to the ideals instilled in me by Olympia’s edicts. But have I become sidetracked by my current situation as a mediator of justice amongst not just PCW but the general populace as well? Clearly these have wide ranging benefits, but there have been just as clear examples of my new-found status in law enforcement having detrimental effects upon my person. While it surely trains me for the most unpredictable of matches, they drain up much time and energy, much like the battle against the vile Robot Vampires or my city defense from the scourge of the Lizardmen from Atlantia. Perhaps my focus should… become more so. Up ahead, a man in black rips a burgeoning purse from an elderly woman waiting to cross the street. She screams bloody murder as the man races away and towards our hero, the unfortunate thief looking back at his victim. A bored Big Al merely extends his arm. The meaty forearm of Big Alan Steele does not budge as the vagabond turns just in time to see his fate, eyes bulging. The man spins on the arm like a party favor, attached at the neck, and somersaults to the ground, landing awkwardly on the ground with a crunch. Absentmindedly, Steele catches the spinning handbag in midair. The woman rushes up, gasping for air and beaming. Woman: “My hero!!” Big Al doesn’t even look at her as he continues on, pressing the bag into her bosom as he persists down the road. The woman scratches her head in wonder, before laying in a few kicks into the prone and out cold thief as she pulls out a tazer… Everywhere I turn, I see the forces of Hades amassing at the gates of Olympia. There… a man jaywalking right before my authoritative eyes! A car parked across three handicapped parking spaces at the liquor store. A horse not wearing a diaper as per the local pet ordinances. Lex Luthor picking up electronics at Radio Shack for his new ultra-death ray he thinks I don’t know about… where does it end? The fight seems so futile at times, and yet each day I charge into the breach, eliminating some foul piece if excrement only to have it replaced by another. Is this really the best path to Olympic glory? Is this really the path set forth before my by my godly benefactors? The light turns red and Big Al stops, waiting for the green light. Suddenly, a lime green Cadillac pulls up to a screeching stop. In the back seat of the convertible is a monstrous looking mounted laser focusing disintegrator beam cannon, aimed directly at our malaise-laden hero’s head! The Joker hoots with glee as he leaps into the back seat of the Caddy and switches the cannon on, the machine humming with untold power! Joker: “Hoo hoo hoo hoo! Hope you’re wearing clean underwear for the medical personnel Big Alan DOPE!! BWAHAHAHAHAHA!!!” The light turns green, and Steele, head still down, strides forth, merely tipping the car over with a great display of fantastic strength. The Joker squeals in surprise as he now finds himself on his back, the car tipping over on top of him… Joker: (sigh) “Didn’t plan on that one…” BOOM!! The sullen form of Big Alan Steele, still walking, is silhouetted against the backdrop of a huge explosion, flames engulfing the Caddy. The Joker runs around comically, licks of flame peeling off his back as he flops and rolls ins a desperate attempt to put it out. Steele sighs and keeps moving, never looking back at the super-villain’s woes. So what is the solution? Do I stay a path that may be doing a great injustice to the Lord of Olympia, or do I subdue the very instincts that have made me become the man-god that I am today? It vexes me so, for there is no right answer, is there? Lord Zeus, I pray to you in this time of great need! Your son, Big Alan Steele, has possibly lost his way and is in desperate want of direction! To have your son bested by mere HUMAN opponent in the wrestling ring when those of the criminal world cannot defeat him must be humiliating! The villainous Chef has set loose Hades upon me, and I respond with singular tactics, blind in my rage to the pitfalls laid out before me! Now, another of his dark brood, Xavier Kannon wishes to interrupt my path to greatness… and have I fallen by the wayside? Should I keep my current ideals or respond with a differing strategy?! Big Al stops and looks up into the clear, azure sky. Rage builds up in his face, and his arms shake violently, raw emotive power surging through his being… BAS: (yelling) “ANSWER ME!! I, BIG ALAN STEELE, DESERVE A SIGN!!!” His face turns crimson with his outburst, a group of birds in a nearby tree flying off in fright. Steele holds his pose, arms outstretched and fist clenched, glaring at the cloudless sky, awaiting his response. Minutes pass, and he sighs, head down once again… BAS: (grumbling) “As I had thought… left to my own devices. My plight amuses the gods!” Steel turns and is startled to realize he faces the ornately carved columns on the gate of Stately Steele Manor. A grim smile finds its way onto his lips as he runs a finger across the “BAS” symbol with the lightning bolt, ivy running through it’s wrought copper bars. BAS: “All roads lead to Rome…” Big Al swings open the gate and starts to slowly make his way up the sidewalk, when the front door bursts open, a frantic Sue Saksnussum holding the HOTLINE phone! Sue: (angry when she’s beautiful) “Where have you been?! Haven’t you heard?! Los Angeles is under attack!!” Big Alan Steele’s eyebrows ump up in surprise. A blonde appears behind Sue. Candi: “Yeah! A giant sea monster crab thing is destroying the city!! You have to help!!” Big Alan Steele’s eyes twinkle. Another blonde appears behind Sue and Candi. Steffi: “Oh my god!! Another sea monster has appeared!! And they are working in tandem!!” Big Alan Steele’s face breaks into a big grin. The Sado-Masochist steps up behind the girls, pulling on one of his long patented leather gloves with a creaking sound, lowering his zipper-cowl into place. S&M: (smiling) “Yup. They called for us, man. You ready to hit the road?!” BAS: (with glee) “HAHAHAHAHA!!! The gods, the do work in mysterious ways!! Hi-Keeba!” **Thumbs up**
