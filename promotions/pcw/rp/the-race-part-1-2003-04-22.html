The Race Part 1

22 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 22/04/03 (The camera opens up. You can see everything in The Overweighter’s eyes. He staggering along and everything is a little fussy. He doesn’t have a clue what he is doing. Yes He is drunk. You change to another camera which is following The Overweighter. He is staggering down a pathway with shops and restaurants. He has been in many restaurants but they just throw him out. He sees this big line of people and they are queuing for something. Overweighter walks up to one of them.) The Overweighter: What…are…you…do…ing? Man in the line: I’m queuing up for the thing that will change my life! The Overweighter: Can…I…Cut…in…front…of…you? Man in the line: Yeah sure, but I would be careful I’m not sure if you can do the whole thing in your condition. The Overweighter: Ah…SHUT…UP! (The Overweighter gets to the front of the queue and there is a woman sitting behind a table with a pen and piece of paper.) Woman: Would you like to sign up? The Overweighter: No, I just waited this long for no reason! (He nearly falls over but the man behind him grabs him and is trying to hold him up.) Woman: Put your name, Address, Phone number and amount you wish to donate. The Overweighter: Donate? Woman: Yeah, this is a charity race. The Overweighter: What is the average that people are putting down? Woman: err 50. The Overweighter: Okay but me down for 50k. Woman: 50K okay!! (The Overweighter writes his details down and staggers off, the man is still trying to hold him up. He lets go because it’s his turn in the line. Overweighter falls over. BANG! There is a earthquake when Overweighter hits the floor.) --==The next Morning==-- (The Overweighter is lying on the floor where he fell over, there is droll coming out his mouth and going over the pavement. He gets woken up by a man.) Man: Okay your coming with me! (He tries to pick up The Overweighter and put him in a car but his to heavy. The man picks up a radio.) Man: This is 0 alpha 2 I request assistance, I have a drunk and his pretty big. I can’t get him in the car. Over! (Someone replies on the radio, while the man is having a conversation with the other person on the radio Overweighter has woken up. He sits up, looks around and rubs his eyes. He gets up unaware of the man next to him. He scratches his head. He begins to walk away. But the man notices him.) Man: Hey stop! (The Overweighter turns around to see a policeman.) The Overweighter: Not you again! Policeman: Me? The Overweighter: What have I done this time? Policeman: Your drunk and disorderly! The Overweighter: No I was drunk and disorderly and if you want you can give me a lift home! Policeman: Hang on, let me cancel the backup. (The Overweighter gets into the police car while the policeman is cancelling the backup. The policeman gets into the car.) Policeman: Where are you going? The Overweighter: Right there. Policeman: Why couldn’t you walk? The Overweighter: I’m lazy, why don’t you come in and I’ll read you some of my work? Policeman: Okay? (The policeman drives a few meters down the road and stops. They get out and walk over to Overweighter’s house. They walk in.) The Overweighter: Sorry about the mess, I’m a slob. (The Overweighter’s brother comes down the stairs.) Candy Cane: What has my brother done now? Policeman: Invited me in to listen to some of his work. Candy Cane: Okay, seriously what has he done wrong? The Overweighter: Look bro piss off! (Candy Cane walks up the stairs. Overweighter and the policeman walk into the front room.) The Overweighter: Sit there and I’ll be back. (The Policeman sits down and waits for The Overweighter. He comes back with a huge pad of paper. He opens it.) The Overweighter: Okay here is one of my favourites. A man is in a hotel lobby. He wants to ask the clerk a question. As he turns to go to the front desk, he accidentally bumps into a woman beside him and as he does, his elbow goes into her breast. They are both quite startled. The man turns to her and says, 'Ma'am, if your heart is as soft as your breast, I know you'll forgive me.' She replies, 'If your penis is as hard as your elbow, I'm in room 436.' Policeman: Ha, ha, ha. That’s your work, Your obviously The Overweighter right? The Overweighter: Yeah how do you know? Policeman: Well I hear all the guys in the police station talk about you. I’m new so I’m the only one that hasn’t seen you. The Overweighter: Okay, you want another one? Policeman: Yeah go ahead. The Overweighter: A guy comes home to his wife one evening with a big bunch of flowers and she says 'I suppose this means I have to get on my back with my legs open for the next three days'. The husband says 'Why? Don't you have any vases?' Policeman: That’s good but not as good as the other one. The Overweighter: Okay what about this one? A woman walks into a drugstore and asks the pharmacist if he sells size extra large condoms. He replies, 'Yes we do. Would you like to buy some?' She responds, 'No, but do you mind if I wait around here until someone does? Policeman: That’s the best one, ha, ha, ha. (The phone rings. Overweighter goes over and answers it.) The Overweighter: Hello? Voice on the phone: Hi, I would like to double check your sure about signing up to this race. The Overweighter: Race? What race? Voice on the phone: The one you signed up to last night. The Overweighter: No, not again! Voice on the phone: Not what again? The Overweighter: I always sign myself up for a race when I’m drunk. Last time it was a race around Europe. Voice On The Phone: Oh you’re the Overweighter! I heard about that! The Overweighter: Really? How? Voice On the Phone: I was the person in charge. But you disappeared after that? The Overweighter: Yeah, I got drunk and woke up in Japan somehow don’t bother asking. Voice on The Phone: So your good for the race, detail are coming in the post to you. The Overweighter: Okay bye. (The policeman walks up to The Overweighter.) Policeman: I have to go, see you around then Overweighter. The Overweighter: Yeah. Policeman: Don’t get into to much trouble. The Overweighter: I’ll try my best. (Candy Cane comes down the stairs.) Candy cane: Here this came in the post for you. The Overweighter: The race details, that was quick. Candy cane: Not another race, how many times have you entered into a bloody race. Last time you only just made it back for your match. The Overweighter: I still lost my match that day. Candy cane: Who was it that beat you? The Overweighter: That dude, triple T. Well that’s what I call him. Candy Cane: His in the same federation as you isn’t he? The Overweighter: Yeah, his the world champion last time I looked. (The Overweighter opens the letter.) The Overweighter: Oh no! Candy Cane: What? The Overweighter: The race, err it’s a long distance running race. Candy Cane: Oh well you can always not run. The Overweighter: err no, if I come first I keep the money I donated but the people that lost have to pay up. Candy Cane: Oh, how much did you put in? The Overweighter: It says here 50K. Candy Cane: What!!! The Overweighter: … Candy Cane: You better get training. The Overweighter: err later. Candy cane: When does this race take place? The Overweighter: In a months time. Candy Cane: we will make a training routine and get you fit for this race! The Overweighter: Does that mean a diet? Candy Cane: I’m afraid so. The Overweighter: No!!! First I have some psycho steal my food and replace it with healthy food and now I have this RACE!!!! Candy Cane: It could be worst! The Overweighter: Okay, I’m doomed. Candy cane: You better have a rest before we start training. The Overweighter: Okay. (The Overweighter goes and lies down on the sofa. He closes his eyes and it fades to blackness.)
