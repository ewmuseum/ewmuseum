Hunted Saga Part 19: Help Wanted!!!!

6 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6/5/02 Aeryn didn’t find it too difficult to hitch hike back to New York, although it meant degrading herself in ways that she would never repeat to anyone, especially Crichton. It had almost been twenty four hours since she and Crichton were attacked in Ohio, just after his Friday Night Fusion match against Jason Core. On this visit to New York, Aeryn was no longer caught up in the bright lights and the sight seeing, she wasn’t here on a social call. She was here to break free the man she loved. Aeryn hailed a cab and soon after a yellow cab pulls up to the curb where she stood. Another woman beside Aeryn attempting to get in the cab and claim it for herself, but Aeryn put one firm hand on the woman’s shoulder and pulled her away. The woman flew backwards, hitting hard upon the concrete floor, but Aeryn was too determined to care about anything that got in her way and so didn’t even turn around to see if the woman was okay. The cab driver just looked at her blankly and shrugged. Aeryn noted the stereotypical Indian features, which the cab driver was currently portraying and laughed silently. “Where to, Please?” The cab driver enquires in the thickest Indian accent Aeryn had ever heard. Aeryn breaks off the will to laugh. “Erm… The Avengers mansion please.” Aeryn replies. The cab driver pulls away from the curb and begins on the inevitable path to the Avenger’s mansion. Aeryn didn’t know how well she would be received at the mansion, especially appearance wise, she had been in the same clothes for at least twenty four hours and she hadn’t had a wash for the best part of the day. She was still wearing the same old black sleeveless top and beige pants, but she had no time for anything else – except find the Avengers and get them to help her. A variety of questions were plaguing her at the moment and they weren’t making her feel better about the situation she now found herself in. Would the Avengers betray their own government in order to save John Crichton? Would the Avengers be willing to give up the trust of the entire nation, simply to have another crack at the illustrious Thunderbolts, who according to Crichton’s explanation were once criminals? Aeryn forced these negative thoughts from her head entirely, she had no reason to be negative because she didn’t really know what to expect from the Avengers. ************ First of all came the familiar throbbing of the head, a par for the course when regaining consciousness, then his eyes began to hurt and then there was darkness. All Crichton had remembered was feeling the truck stop and being lower down to the floor, but this time up right. Green foliage was still surrounding the area, so he figured they hadn’t gone too far, but he did notice a familiar sight of mesh wire fences and barbed wire surrounding the huge army complex that stood before him. The two Thunderbolt’s that had captured him Mach 1 and Atlas were joined by the rest of the Thunderbolt team, who were escorted out of the lead barracks by a couple of white coat scientists. One of them leant forward and injection Crichton in the arm with something or other. After that his memory was a blank, he had the familiar ‘hang over’ of being drugged and he hoped to god that he was just in a very dark room and not blind, because all he could see before him was blackness. Quite frankly Crichton didn’t much care for his situation and began to worry about Aeryn rather than himself. He also began to wonder whether he’d ever see her again. All that he had was hope and hope would keep him going indefinitely, or so he believed anyhow. ************ The yellow cab pulled up outside the Avengers mansion rather sloppily for Aeryn’s liking, but she didn’t have time to stop and reprimand the man for it, she simply hoped out of the cab and paid the fair before running up to the Avengers gate and pressing the intercom signalling button. She impatiently waited for a couple of seconds, until Jarvis the Avenger’s very own butler, answers the intercom politely. “Yes, what may I help you with.” In a very stereotypical British accent. Aeryn ignored the second racial stereotype she had encountered today. “I need an audience with the Avengers!” She demanded bluntly. Aeryn could almost hear him tut at her lack of politeness. “Who is asking?” Jarvis questioned suspiciously. “It’s Aeryn Sun, I believe the Avengers have had a run in with Crichton before and he needs help, the Thunderbolts have captured him!” Aeryn declares. “oh dear. The Thunderbolts. You’d better come in and I’ll assemble them.” Jarvis replied with a worried tone clear in his voice. Aeryn quickly sprinted up the path to the Avengers mansion to be greeted by Jarvis at the door. He quickly ushers her in and escorts her into a back room, deep in the heart of the mansion. Aeryn enters the room to be greeted by an array of Earth’s mightiest warriors. Captain America, Iron Man and the Black Panther stood there staring at her as she entered the room, all of the Avengers fully dressed in their combat gear. She awkwardly smiles at them as signals her to sit down on a particularly comfy looking chair near the door. She sits down in the chair and looks up blankly at the three hero’s. Captain America steps forward and leans upon a highly polished mahogany table. “So what’s this about John Crichton and the Thunderbolts?” Cap questions getting right to the point. “Well as you may know, I am Crichton’s um..er…partner and he told me about the encounter he had with you in a motel parking lot.” Aeryn stated. “Ah yes, I remember that day, Crichton fought valiantly and persuaded us not to take him in. When we got back to the mansion, we verified his story and distanced ourselves away from the assignment as much as possible. Let me tell ya, that didn’t go down well with the government.” Cap explained. Aeryn simply nodding in understanding. “Well this time the Government hired the Thunderbolts to capture Crichton instead and they succeeded yesterday.” Aeryn replied. Visible concern grows upon Caps face. “Damn those Thunderbolts, they’ll do anything for money!” Cap simply stated. “That’s why I have come here today, to seek your help in taking the operation down and freeing Crichton before they have chance to do any more experiments on him!” Aeryn informed. Cap rubs his chin a little, in heavy contemplation. Black Panther turns to Cap in an attempt to get eye contact. “Remember Captain America that we are not in favour with the United States government as it is and providing any help now would undoubtedly worsen the situation beyond measure!” The Panther states. Cap nods slightly in agreement. “That is very true Panther, but as superhero’s are we not honour bound to help a comrade in need, whatever the situation?” Cap questions. Iron Man nods. “Remember all that commotion a while back with the Kree, the Skrulls and Captain Marvel, we defied the government then and we’re still here!” Iron Man states, then metallic voice masker working as designed. Cap looks Aeryn directly in the eye. “As acting chairman this month, I say we should go and help the man, but as you say this is indeed a difficult situation. So I’m going to let each one of you decided your own path. Nevertheless I am choosing to go.” Cap declares valiantly. “I’m in.” Iron Man replies. “Me too!” Panther states in unison. A smile grows upon Captain America’s face. “Then we are in agreement. Aeryn could you show us where you last saw the Thunderbolts and Crichton?” He questions. Aeryn rises. “Hell yeah, lets go.” She states.
