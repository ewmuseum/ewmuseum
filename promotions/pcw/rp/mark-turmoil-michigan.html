Mark 'Turmoil' Michigan

Date: Nov.12/02 As the elevator rose, I could feel the wind rocking the carriage from side to side. Several other people, including a lovey-dovey couple, were also on their way to the top. Naturally, everyone beside me spoke French . . . so it was going to be a long ride up. As I leaned against the wall, I listened in on the conversations, even though I didn’t understand a word they were all saying, but I listened none-the-less. I have always been a listener . . . and a follower. I’ve always done what I was told, never questioning authority . . . I was such a nice young lad. However, those days have passed . . . now I do things on my own terms. I listen to no one . . . I am my own boss . . . ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ After several minutes, the lift stopped and the elevator doors quietly slid open. As the other passengers and I stepped out of the carriage, the wind began to blow at violent speeds. An elderly man’s hat flew from his head, and drifted softly over the railing and down to ground below. The air smelled of cheap wine and perfume, but I suppose that is common in the ‘City of Love’. The sun was just setting over the horizon, and it’s ardent rays stretched out over Paris like a blanket of gold. Off in the distance, the bells of Notre Dame Cathedral rang out loud and clear. What an evening this was . . . but the peace and tranquility of Paris would soon be disrupted by the cheers and jeers of French wrestling fans, and the screams and groans of pain emanating from Stade Sebastien Charlety . . . for tonight Tuesday, November the 12th, the PCW would invade the French capital. And I will be there . . . right in the middle of it all . . . ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ Most people stare up in awe at the monumental site of the Eiffel Tower, but trust me, you haven’t seen anything until you view Paris from the top of ‘the tower’. I know I haven’t been myself lately . . . I’ve been acting and talking like I’m some sort of deranged psychopath . . . but there is still a part of me that yearns for intellectual stimulation. Sure bashing heads in with chairs is fun and all, but I still have to take pride in my roots. As a wrestler, it is my job to take care of business . . . to go to the ring each and every show and entertain the millions of fans world wide. However, as a highly educated man, I feel it is also my job to enlighten myself on an intellectual basis. My unquenchable thirst for knowledge will never cease to exist, so with each stop on the PCW’s world wide tour, I have learned as much as I can about each country’s cultures. Since day one, I couldn’t wait to reach France. This Western European country is rich with history, and nationalistic pride . . . something most countries severely lack. It was very refreshing to see people who took pride in themselves as much as I take pride in being Mark Michigan. As for all those countries that have nothing better to do than feud with its own people, I say piss on them. They have no class, no culture . . . they do nothing but gripe and complain . . . and that is something this world can do without. And this brings me to Playaz Inc. . . . Statuz Quo and Dante Jackson, the two most primitive dolts in the PCW. Instead of embracing their similarities to white folk, they do nothing but bash their ‘pale brothas’. Now is that anyway to treat your fellow man? I think not. ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ The reason why I visited the Eiffel Tower was to be inspired. The pure beauty of Paris at sunset was far more pleasing to the eye than the backstage area of a PCW show . . . thus it gave me more room to think. Think about Turmoil . . . not just the show of the same name, but the turmoil in which my life was now trapped. Tuesday Turmoil brings with it the very real possibility of putting me in danger. Not only am I fighting two dimwits that probably want to rip my skull off, but I am teaming up with my worst enemy to date, Mark Amulet. Ever since the one-two-three on Fusion that gave Amulet a victory over me, I haven’t been able to get him off my mind. Why were we teamed together? What did he have planned? Could we trust each other during the match? One of these questions I could answer myself . . . and the answer is, no . . . we can’t trust each other. Well, he couldn’t trust me, that’s for damn sure. I know the second that match is over, we will be enemies again . . . and the coexistence between us will cease to exist. But then again, in a few weeks time, I will be his partner again at Alliances. Team . . . ugh . . . ’Kano And Three Others’ . . . I’m not too crazy about that name . . . well, we’re just one of the fifteen teams to compete in the elimination brawl. Granted there are teams just as dysfunctional as us . . . but guaranteed, there is no other man in that match that loathes his partners as much as I. First off, Jon Kano . . . the ’REAL LEGEND’. He’s had it out for us rookies since we arrived . . . and now we’re teamed up with him. Yeah, there’s a real team leader for you . . . that old bag won’t last five seconds in that match. Next, we have Mark Amulet . . . and there’s not much more I can say out him than ‘keep your friends close, and your enemies closer’. My third teammate is Semore Smoke . . . I haven’t even seen this guy since I’ve been called up to PCW. I’m sure he’ll do nothing but drag us down . . . that is if he even shows up for Alliances. And finally, we have . . . me, Mark Michigan . . . the heart and soul of team ‘Kano And Three Others’. Now that I think of it . . . we should be called ‘Michigan And Three Others’ . . . it would make more sense, since I have the best chance of winning the tournament for us. ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ But enough of Alliances . . . I have more pressing matters to tend to. I have my tag match at Turmoil to worry about, my tag partner to worry about, and I still need to finalize my deal with Spishak Cola so I can represent them in the ‘Alamo Match’. My, what a busy schedule I have . . . I guess it’s just all part of the game . . . a game I plan on winning!
