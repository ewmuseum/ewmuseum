Battlefield PCW.....Part 3

12 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9-12-2002 'I knew that I dealt with a Psychopath. Crush Jason would torture my men for useless information. The bastard would pay...' The shot opens above the Pendragon encampment, and the camp is quite a bit larger than before. Seems as though the passing of the night has seen many men come to the banner of Pendragon. The shot pans around and finds the men all in formation, fully armored and facing their leader, the Lord King himself. He walks back and forth in front of the men, and they never take their eyes from him. Finally he stops, and begins to speak to them. Not in his voice of command, but in the voice of a father.... Venneman: My lads. You make up the finest fighting force that the Land of Gauze, or any land, has ever seen. Men: HUZZAH! Venneman: We are here for a preordained purpose. We are here to fight for the oppressed peoples of the land. With the influx of new recruits has also come new information. The regime here in Gauze is not like the one whose crown they usurped. The new regime here is one of totalitarianism. It is a rule by both force and fear. The once free peoples are taxed beyond any reasonable standards, and the new emperor, Cravier Dannon, watches over the masses with an eye of greed and degradation. Pendragon shakes his head and looks at the men with solemn eyes.... Venneman: A large battle looms upon the horizon. A battle where I will lead you to the very gates of hell so that a wondrous people might remain free. We shall fight, and as much as I hate to admit it, some of us shall not live to see the morrow. But to each of you, I want you to know this: those of us who die, our deaths shall not be made in vain. We fight against evil for the side of good...For the side of the right. Men: HUZZAH!! LONG LIVE THE LORD KING!! HUZZAH!! Venneman: Give 'em hell, boys.... The men break out in a chorus of cheers as Pendragon walks down each row, touching and giving words of encouragement to each man. Finally he reaches the rear and he keeps walking. He disappears from the shot, only to reappear, riding his huge white war horse, Xerxes. Excalibur is drawn, and a golden crown rests upon a chain mail headpiece atop Venneman's head. 'I feared for the lives of my men against so evil a foe, yet I felt secure in the knowledge that we were doing what was right. God would surely be with us today. I only hoped my plan would work....' ****FLASH***** The scene changes to show Venneman with his three commanders, Richard Smith, Jonathan James and William Davis. Maps are spread out upon a table that the four crowd around, and Pendragon dominates the conversation.... Venneman: We shall move our forces onto this ridge halfway between our camp and that of the enemy. Archers upon both our flanks.....Do you see how this ridge makes a 'U' shape? Pendragon points to the map.... Venneman: The archers shall take aim from the outer reaches of the U. They shall be reinforced by small bastions of infantry, but the bulk of the infantry shall stay back at the base of the 'u' and of the ridge. Jonathan James, the infantry commander, frowns at the map as he addresses Pendragon.... James: And how are we to know that the enemy will enter the 'U'? And then, my lord, what is to keep them from leaving? Pendragon smiles, obviously quite pleased with his plan... Venneman: The plan is simple, really....I shall lead a small contingent of our heavy cavalry. We will harry the enemy, and goad them into sending troops after us. We shall lead them into the 'U' and once we have passed through, the archers will open fire. Richard shall lead the mainstay of the cavalry, which shall be waiting upon a hilltop half a mile distant, here.... Pendragon points again at a map at a circle titled 'Skyler's Crest'. Venneman: When he sees the men enter the trap, he shall lead the charge and box the enemy in. They will have three choices....First, they can stand there ground and be decimated by the arrows, not a real choice. Second, they can try their luck against the horse, or thirdly, they can drive up the ridge to be slain upon the pikes of the infantry. Each choice spells disaster, and the plan is simple and effective. The three men nod and clap Pendragon on the back and he merely smiles.... 'I hoped it would prove effective. We were against stiff odds, though that is nothing new to me. I knew that there were certain variables that I could not control, but a good commander deals with those uncertainties when they arise. One must adapt to succeed, and adversity, if it does not kill us, merely makes us stronger....Wiser.' ***FLASH*** The scene cuts back to show the standing army, each rank a shining wall of armor and courage. In the center the cavalrymen sit their horses behind their leader, Venneman Pendragon. To the left stand the archers, behind their fierce eyed leader, William Davis. In a half circle that encompasses both the archers and the horsemen stand the infantry, their pikes held high. Upon Venneman's right, Richard Smith sits a black horse, and to his left, Jonathan James sits stoutly upon a thick looking brown mare. Pendragon raises his sword above his head and drops it, pointing it forward. At once, the army begins to move.... 'I wish I knew more about mine enemy, but that was the game you played when war was the riddle. I only know that as per the norm, I am outnumbered. I also know that I am the most gifted warrior upon the land and that my men will do anything for me. There is a lot to be said about the strength of an army's leadership. In battle, as in mine own life..'
