Welcome To The Pit - Part 3: Jupiter - Final Frontier

12 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3 - 12 - 03 An hour of so after completing his Knockdown Training with tainted methods, Dorn has moved on to a new task: Top Rope Training. His teacher and fellow follower of Astrology, Copernicus, is unaware of Dorn’s false victory over the mighty, rubber-wrapped tree and was quite pleased up seeing that the tree was felled. Deeming Dorn ready for the next session of the March Madness training, the two double time it over the Top Rope Training Area, which is a large square pit, dug at least 3 feet into the ground. There’s padding and canvas covering the ground, but it’s still just a little bit more than your average mud hole. Makeshift wooden poles act as sturdy turnbuckles and loose, fiber ropes pulled taut between the poles act as tight ropes. Rather ramshackle, Dorn mumbles within his mind, wearing an amused smirk on his handsome face. ‘Here we are, Dorn!’ Copernicus exclaims, his face beaming with pride as the two reach the pit. How Copernicus knows where he’s at is beyond Dorn, but he decides not to question his teacher. Copernicus folds his arms proudly across his barrel chest and lifts his chin up, beaming with confidence and self-approval. ‘What do ya think, boy?’ His question kind of puts Dorn off, as the Astronomer believes there isn’t much to think at all. It’s better he’s blind, so he doesn’t notice what a horrible fucking dirt hole it is… ‘It’s great, Copernicus. Someone truly blessed you with the savvy of Desima. Shall we get to work soon?’ Although he knows the gruff Astronomer is blind, Dorn cocks a false smile of eagerness as he stares at the pile of worn canvas, splintered wood, and crusted mud. It looks as if they should be washing a big retarded elephant within the pit instead of training a warrior of En’Flarim. The gruff, yet humorous teacher of Dorn Tracer takes a step back away from the pit and nods at Dorn, giving the go ahead. ‘Alright boy, get in there.’ The order confuses Tracer, as there’s no one else in there and it’s supposed to be top rope tossing training. Something in Copernicus must be hinting the man of Dorn’s uncertainty, as he raises his eyebrows and cocks a reassuring grin at Dorn, ‘Don’t worry, it’s all taken care of. Just remember, you are to toss your opponent OVER the top rope in order to complete the training. Sic?’ Dorn acknowledges with a nod rather absent-mindedly, but when he gets no response from Copernicus, he quickly remembers the man is blind. ‘Alright. Is that the basis of the regime, or is there something else I should know?’ A strange feeling begins to unnerve Dorn’s confidence, as Copernicus is wearing the same grin he did when they entered the Knockdown Training Area and Dorn found out about the actual process. ‘I don’t want to get in unprepared,’ He fibs, wanting to dig up whatever dirt Copernicus is hiding. Copernicus isn’t one to hide things, however, and quickly brings the situation to light with a beaming grin, ‘Of course, Dorn! I’m glad you brought that up! I’m afraid I’m going to leave it a surprise, though…’ His face suddenly contorts and lowers into a dark, brooding smirk. ‘Just get in the ring and I’ll bring your opponent out. ‘ The tone of his voice hints at humor, but dark humor. Humor that one gets from watching another suffer either for sport or for his life. The type of humor someone would get from burning the casket of a passed on loved one. Yes, Copernicus’ sense of humor is indeed dark… and Dorn is certainly going to find out what Copernicus has in his sick, strange mind. * * * The Shooting Star Champion has long since climbed into the pit of mud, having disrobed his coat and now stands in nothing but his black leather boots, pants, and wearing his shades upon his tanned face. He knows not what awaits him, and Copernicus didn’t shed any real light that would illuminate the dark, mysterious situation. The blind merely wore a grin on his face as he walked, leaving Dorn in his thoughts. Stars damn this fool, what has he got up his sleeve? Dorn wears a scowl of irritation as he waits within the smelly pit of makeshift top rope training, hoping to see Copernicus’ plan set itself before him. He doesn’t have to wait much longer past the twenty or so minutes he’s already waited, because the plan of the blind Astronomer brings itself up over the horizon… A cream-colored hippo wearing a muscle shirt, stretched jeans, and munching on popcorn. The mammoth of a man strides along with rough force, his many folds of fat slapping against his body like big meaty paddles. His golf ball-looking face gleams in the afternoon sun due to the coating of grease that hangs upon it, truly disgusting Dorn Tracer. The man has short black hair and a clean-shaven, yet porous face. Dorn’s disgust is nothing compared to his surprise, though. The realization that he is in top rope training slams him like an 18-wheeler, How will I get that quivering mountain of saturated fat over the ropes?! The Astronomer backs up as the fat monster comes closer, almost like Dorn’s will have a fatal allergic reaction if the guy comes to close. Copernicus soon emerges from behind the tub of lard with that grin on his face again. ‘Well Dorn, this will be your opponent for today,’ Copernicus exclaims with an amused cockiness swelling his tone. He then turns to the HUGE man and slaps him on the shoulder, ‘Jupiter, that will be your opponent. Don’t go easy on him, as we’re trying to show him the ropes…’ Amused by his own comment, Copernicus lets out a surprisingly loud laugh and pushes the monster known as Jupiter forward. The huge, chin-filled beast seems generally uninterested in the entire thing, but comes forward anyway, leading Dorn to believe the man is probably literally paid in snacks. This is crazy!! Dorn’s frantic look obviously shines through as he searches for a way out of his predicament. Jupiter drops his popcorn on the grass outside of the ground and literally LEAPS into the air. Dorn does nothing but stare in complete awe as the man’s disgusting rolls of flesh lift to form a parachute of fat and skin. Upon making an Earth-shaking two point landing, the man stances in a grappling position and bears a look of determination. Dorn, however, cannot return the look, for he has fallen to the dirt from the sheer force of the man’s landing prowess. Tartorus, give me strength… Dorn groans to himself, trying to find his courage. Copernicus just watches from on far, trying to hide his amusement as he hears Dorn scramble to his feet. Jupiter charges forward with a course, ringing roar, the cholesterol clogging his throat obviously vibrating from the force. The Astronomer’s eyes grow wide with fear and he makes a desperate dive to the left, not wanting to encounter the mobile mountain. The fat, disgusting human goes plodding into the ropes and steadies himself upon hitting the brakes. Dorn takes his opportunity and bolts to his feet. He races off to the human blob and grabs a tree trunk-like leg. Grasping all that he can and using the force and spirit within him, Dorn tries to lift the fat man over the top rope, but the guy barely gets a foot off the ground. This is crazy, this is crazy, this is crazy… The words cycle through Dorn’s mind like a horrid image of violence, and he’s unable to bring himself to think otherwise. He’s ALREADY exhausted from trying to lift the mountain of meat, and to make matters worse, the guy is yawning and Copernicus is laughing! Feeling the rage beginning to swell deep with his soul, the Astronomer lets go of Jupiter’s leg and backs up with the look of a pissed off lion on his face. ‘All right fat boy, we’ll play a different game, then! BRING IT ON!!’ ‘Remember, Dorn,’ Copernicus suddenly calls, breaking Dorn’s angered concentration, ‘Jupiter represents your opponents as a collective entity. Do you really have any hope of over-powering all 29 other men? Better rethink your strategy!’ Copernicus then folds his arms across his chest and grins, imagining Dorn’s confused look. He also thinks about warning the Shooting Star Champion about the impending danger, but he decides not to… WHAM!!!! Dorn’s body contorts at a damage angle and comes flying off the ground from a massive Jupiter clothesline! His body twists at least 3 times in mid air before slamming back down to the dirt with a sickening, bone-crunching thud. The Astronomer tries to rise back to his feet, but is unable to work his joints correctly. He just plummets back into the mud, hoping for it to all be over soon. Jupiter gives a throaty, fat-laden laugh and grabs Dorn by the hair. He pulls the Shooting Star Champion to his feet, and then pushes him against the ropes. Dorn rebounds off the surprisingly taut ropes and runs towards the awaiting Jupiter. Suddenly, Copernicus’ words cut into his mind, ‘DORN! THINK!’ Albeit the fact that Copernicus is being very vague, Dorn suddenly realizes what the blind teacher means. With only seconds to spare, Dorn gathers up all the force that he can and swings his right leg forward… SAAHHHHH-MACK! His booted foot collides with the genitals of Jupiter for THE BIG BANG, causing the fat man’s eyes to burst from their puffy hammocks in shock and agony. Unable to bring himself to shriek something, Jupiter just doubles over trying to regain his wind and get the pain in his balls to subside. Dorn moves out of the way and lets his blob opponent stumble forward into the ropes for support. Jupiter’s upper body from his stomach to the top of his head hangs over the ropes, trying to steady his now antagonized form. At the sight of this, Dorn suddenly gets quite the idea. Back up above the ring, Copernicus is still grinning, obviously aware of what’s going on after hearing that ‘kick heard ‘round the world’. ‘Go on, Dorn… you know what to do…’ He calls, his voice ringing with satisfaction. Dorn nods and gives his opponent a look, the very sight disgusting him. Jupiter’s big, fortress-like ass is the only obstacle between the Astronomer and victory. It represents his remaining opposition of March Madness, those not so easily vanquished. It’s the last step… the final frontier… ‘EN’FLARIM, GUIDE MY WAY!!!’ With a war cry that echoes across the forest, Dorn charges forward at full speed. His body becomes a blur as he leaps into the air, still quite a way from Jupiter. Dorn leans back and contorts his body into a strange, nearly 180 angle. He clasps his legs together and leans back just a little more, for if he doesn’t get the right angle, the entire move will fail and Jupiter will probably crush him. It’s the moment those who look for glory live for… that moment when it’s between victory and defeat… In mid-air, with agility like moving poetry, Dorn drops to his back and dropkicks Jupiter square in the ass with all the force he can muster. Jupiter’s body lurches forward and goes tumbling over the top rope in an event that has to surprise the shit out of the human hippo. His body suddenly enters a fall from grace and Jupiter goes tumbling to the dirt like a 100-ton meteor, causing a crater and a huge Dustbowl effect upon his Earth-shaking landing. Dorn collapses both from exhaustion and the force of Jupiter’s landing, sweating pouring from his face and body and his chest heaving. Though it was an incredibly short amount of time, it took a TON of force in order to kick that bastard over the top rope. I… did it… Meanwhile, back on the surface, Copernicus is clapping lightly at the Astronomer’s victory. He gives a slight, amused laugh and folds his arms across his chest once more. ‘You’re ready…’ [End.]
