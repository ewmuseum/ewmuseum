Title: 10 Ways To Hurt Tim Walden With A Melon Baller! P.1 Featuring: The H.I.T. Date: not entered A school kitchen. Usually, school kitchens are places spoken of only in horrified whispers by students who actually have to eat the food they produce. Let's be honest, folks. School cafeterias are bound by dietary restrictions. I mean, they can't turn out GOOD TASTING food, because it's unhealthy. So, they turn out HEALTHY food instead. So what if it looks and tastes like a tire, and probably has the same consistency? They're just doing their jobs! But today, a school kitchen will be used for more than turning out bad food. It shall be used to lay out the menu for a buffet of bloodshed In Versailles, five men will meet in what Iron Chef Commentator Kenji Fukui would call 'The Cooking Utensils Battle'. The culinary carnage is just about ready to commence. So grab your potholders! Hold on to your hot plates! Cradle your cheese knives! Cause it's time for a little cooking education with The HIT! :Lying on a table in the kitchen is a likeness of Tim Walden. It is not, however, a dummy, a blow up doll, or any similar time honored devices used to mock opponents. Rather, it is an exact replica.....except 1/10th Timmy's size, and constructed out of watermelon. It stands on its own two feet, blisfully unaware of the fate about to befall it.. The HIT: KKKKKKKKKKKKKKKKKKKKKIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIAAAAAAHHH!!! ::Trapezoid lowers a melon baller from the air, driving it into the skull of Watermelon Walden. Turning the baller carefully, he pops a perfect, reddish-pink sphere out of the melon.:: The HIT: Number one.....use the melon baller to carve out a chunk of Walden's scalp! :Grinning, Trapezoid raises the melon baller to his mouth, depositing the ball of melon inside, and chews happily: The HIT: Number two....eat said piece of scalp in front ot Walden....ooh, seed. ::Trapezoid spits, the black watermelon seed smacking hard into the ground. His gaze returns upwards, to explain himself.:: The HIT: Well, I explained to Walden BEFORE I lost my Television Title to.......grrr......BRANDI...I promised to show Timothy exactly how I could hurt him with a melon baller. And now, I will....all ten ways I've come up with! Laughing maniacally, Trapezoid places the baller at the joints of the melon fingers, taking them off, one by one.. The HIT: Method three....cut off the fingers.. Using the baller, Trapezoid cuts the melon fingers in half, and then in half again. The small pieces are then deposited into a simmering pot nearby. The HIT: Cut said fingers in half, and use them to make soup... :Grinning widely, HIT puts down the melon baller..looking straight ahead, as if to stare through Walden if he is indeed watching.:: The HIT: On Tuesday, Tim, I told you we'd be meeting in Trick or Treat IV. I'm sure, as a wrestling fan and connoseuir, you know what that means. Sadistic violence. Pain. Agony. Torment. And so, it's only appropriate that we meet in this contest in Versailles. Cooking utensils may pale in comparison to sadistically sharp barbed wire and flames, but they can still be dangerous in the right hands. In MY hands..in the hands of a man who uses weaponry like an artist uses a paintbrush. Of a deadly warrior....a man who knows no limits, no bounds! Trapezoid takes the melon baller, taking the nose of the likeness, and scattering it to the far side of the room. The HIT: Method five.....use the baller to de-nose Tim! Trapezoid's sadistic tendencies have taken over. His body appears more tense, muscles tightened, and his facial expression contorted into a cruel, almost demonic grin.:: The HIT: To Tim Walden, and all others I face on Fusion and Adrenaline...I leave a message. The message is simple, and concise. Versailles shall be the site of a new beginning for Primetime Championship Wrestling. Despite PCW's fame, and its notoreity, it has NEVER seen anything the likes of what The Hardcore Isosceles Trapezoid is capable of. With due respect to men such as Ed and Clyde, their pitiful hardcore powers pale in comparison to what I can do when angered. Through Mall Brawls, Carnivals of Carnage, No Ladder Matches, nothing has been done that can match the horrors of Trick or Treat. This Utensils match shall be the appetizer, as Tommy, End, Rush, and Timothy will discover. I shall leave with the PCW Continental Title, one of the prizes that has evaded my grasp. And I shall carry it on with me.....to Decimation, where that event WILL live up to its name. ::Cackling commences, coming as if from an evil genius rather than the fun-loving Trapezoid.:: The HIT: Ruin is emminent...it can be staved off by no one and no thing. Those on my side, such as Teresa Tomas and El Romantico, are not subjected to what I can do. But Brandi, Walden, Vilas, and my other Friday opponents will see the beginning. The beginning of the transformation...and the beginning of the end for all who oppose me. The light of judgment beckons.....and you all shall be absolved by it, or else burned to ashes...
