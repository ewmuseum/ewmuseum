A Reflection of Love

2 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3-2-02 She's got a smile that it seems to me, Reminds me of childhood memories Where everything was as fresh as the bright blue sky Now and then when I see her face, She takes me away to that special place And if I stared too long, I'd probably break down and cry There's not a passing second in my life that I don't appreciate my lovely Jessica and everything she means to me. It seems like every time I'm about to fall flat on my face and fail my fans, she's there with her emotional support and strength. She is, quite possibly, the only person in this world that can harnass Krayzie when he begins to spiral out of control. To be blunt, Jessica is my world. But, there was once a time when everything I believed in was put the test. In all of one day, the very thread of society that I put my faith into, had its wings clipped. It was on this day, a seed was planted within my soul that would later form the Krayzie-ness that I live to this day. To me though, this could of all been avoided had one man not tried to violate the one person I hold closest to my heart. That fateful day was nine and a half years ago. At the time, I was only twelve years of age. A pre-teen knocking on the walls of teenager-hood. And, just like any normal twelve year old, I was into the dating game. That's when I met the lovely Jessica. We met in the sixth grade at Monger Elementary School and have loved each other ever since. That love, brought about the phone call on this day. See, Jessica wanted me to take her to our last sixth grade dance as boyfriend and girlfriend. Me, being the non-social type, wasn't too sure about this. I can't dance to save my life. Though, as the conversation wore on, I began to cave in and agree to it. I figured, what the h#ll? Why not be with the one you love. Why miss the opportunity to hold her close, to smell the sweet scent of her hair. We'd been together for about four six months now. Why not? Then, all of the sudden, a frightening scream rings out within the phone, 'No!'. It startles me to the point, I dropped the phone. It's Jessica's sweet voice. I remember picking up the phone and trying to get someone, anyone, to answer the phone. I got nothing. The only thing I could hear, was a struggle taking place in the background, with a glass lamp falling over and shattering. No way was I going to just sit in my bedroom and find what happened the next day. I had to find out what was going on. I just had too. I turned to my door, thinking of how far the maze is to get out of my parents house and to the front door. Time is something I knew I didn't have. I turned toward the wall away from the door. There, sitting on the right side of the wall was a window. At this stage of my life, I'd never tried anything like jumping through a window. But, drastic times called for drastic measures and I didn't have time. So, with a rage filled yell piercing the air, I ran like the wind toward the window. This would mark the first time I had ever did a stunt like this. I remember not feeling any fear though. I leaped through the air and went directly through the glass. I can remember not even sticking around to see how much I shattered it. I took off like a predator going after its prey, racing out of my yard. I hopped over the hood of my parents 1989 rusty blue Ford Escort and out onto Main Street. Rain was pouring really badly that night, making the road a bit slippery to run on. Thankfully, I didn't have many cars to deal with. I just ran. Ran with the wind, wondering what had happened to my sweet Jessica. My initial thought, was that an attacker had busted into there house. But where is Jessica's father, I thought? And, why would they go right for Jessica, whose on the second floor? Questions swirled my mind as I cut through a yard to get onto Sterling Avenue. The rain begin to soak my clothes to a degree, slowing down my running. But, I couldn't quit. I had to get to Jessica. If something is wrong with her, I wasn't about to stand idly by and just let it happen. I had to get to her. As I ran through the pouring rain, I think that's when it really hit me just how much she means to me. Jessica is, simply put, my star that burns bright in the sky. Without her, my life would be dark. So, there was no way I was going to let anything happen to her. I took a right turn off of Sterling Avenue, crossing a few more streets until I came to Belmont Avenue. The street Jessica lives on. From the end of the road, I could see her house half way down the block. I told myself, right before I got to her yard, 'no matter what happens, this will be all turn out for the better'. I had no idea what I was about to enter. I got to her front door and burst through it like a battering ram. This was only the third time I had ever been in her house, but I knew where her bedroom was. I raced through the foyer and right up the stairs. I could hear a bit of commotion coming from her bedroom. I'll admit it, a bit of fear ran through my body. I had no idea what to expect. I always knew though, to expect the unexpected. And, that's just what I got. She's got eyes of the bluest skies, As if they thought of rain I hate to look into those eyes, And see an ounce of pain Her hair reminds me of a warm safe place Where as a child I'd hide And pray for the thunder and the rain to quietly pass me by Finally, I got to her bedroom door that sits just up a flight of stairs. When were twelve, I could distinctly recall Snoop Doggy Dogg's 'Gin and Juice' being the song everyone was into at our school. So, to no surprise, a massive Snoop Doggy Doggy poster donned her door's entire length. That is, until I kicked in the door with everything I had, blasting it clean off its hinges. The door smacked a small thirteen inch television set within the room, knocking it off its stand. But, that wasn't even an issue once I got into her bedroom. Not by a long shot. The scene I found when I walked in, is the reason why Krayzie was born. This is when I felt society betrayed me. There, laying half off her bed, is my sweet Jessica fighting for her life against a man that is trying to spread her legs like an axe would a tree log. Her pants are off, leaving her only in a blue Nike t-shirt and red panties. Her face is flooded with tears from intense crying. Never before had I had to deal with something this painstaking. I had no idea how I'd react. The answer came to me though, when I entered that room. As soon as Jessica's father saw me, he instantly sprung off his attempt at his very own daughter. What kind of sick freak tries to violate his own daughter? A sickening feeling of anger sweep over me. I could feel it. My fist clenched tight, my blood pressure began to rise and this overwhelming feeling of hatred consumed me. I lunged out at him and a brawl ensued. Jessica's father, a man that stands six foot three and weighing in at two hundred and forty seven pounds, easily towered over my twelve year old frame. Yet, this man suddenly began to play Russian roulette with all six chambers loaded when he pissed me off that day. I tried to grab his waist and do whatever I could to bring him down, but he elbowed me in the face. He's lucky I had yet to learn the wrestling moves that I've perfected. I stumbled backwards, thinking to myself how this should hurt really bad. But, it didn't. For some reason, my body wasn't registering the pain at all. He grabbed me by the head and rammed me into a wall. Still, as much as I knew I should be hurt or knocked out, I couldn't feel a thing. All I could think, was how I needed to quit letting this guy use me as a punching bag and teach him a f^cking lesson. This man just tried to physically damage my angel. No way in h#ll am I going to let him get away with this. Again, he grabbed me and sent me slamming into a wall. Like it was yesterday, I remember hitting the wall, then just turning around and facing him down. My eyes locked with his. This man tried with everything he had to injure me, to no avail. Here I am, a twelve year old teenage kid, standing face to face with him. I could sense his fear. He had no idea what to do..... I did. I don't know what erupted inside of me, but Krayzie was born the night I slammed by knee into his stomach and caused him to double forward. In that very second, I went from being Jeremy Howard, to being Jeremy 'Krayzie' Howard. Hanging on the wall, I saw a family picture of Jessica and her parents. The sight of that picture made me so sick to my stomach, that I needed to get rid of it. So, I grabbed the picture and broke it over the back of this man's head. Glass shattered everywhere. Blood poured from the back of his head. I wasn't done. Not for the pain I felt, but I wanted this man to pay for the emotional trauma he made Jessica feel. I was going to make this asshole pay. Jessica's father fell to a knee from the picture shattering over the back of his head. I took this opportunity to check on my beautiful girlfriend. To check on her condition. She was in shock, unable to say a word. Her eyes were glazed over. It was like she wasn't even in this world right now. She had gone into a shell to try an blanket herself from anymore pain. I turned my head back toward her father. By now, the sexual devient was back to his feet, staring right at me. I think thats when he realized just what he'd done. Though, I don't think this man had any idea who his daughter was dating. For, if he had no clue who I was before that point, I sure as h#ll was going to show him afterwards. That's when it hit me and hit me hard. This man tried to take Jessica's innocense. And, in pure Krayzie fashion, I charged this man with every bit of what I could muster in my twelve year old body. Her father backed up, praying to God that I'd stop. There was never a second thought about what I was going to do. As he backed up closer and closer to the only window in Jessica's second story bedroom, I leaped out at this man. I caught him high in the chest with a lame looking cross body block if you will, sending the both of us crashing through the window. As I plummetted the two stories down to the ground, the only thought that went through my head, was how great it felt to have put my own safety on the line for the sake of Jessica. I ended walking away from that fall with a fractured arm, a concussion and my first ever night in jail. But, that paled in comparison to what I taught that man. He ended up spending a bit of time in the hospital with some broken bones, a broken ego and a tough lesson to have learned..... Touching Jessica, is like asking for death! To this day, she's never had a problem of that sort with him again. And, what about Jessica's mother? That night, while I was handing Jessica's father a painful reminder of what he tried to do, she was at work. So, I would have thought this woman to protect her oldest daughter and divorce or separate from the father. How wrong I was. She kept him, forgiving him of his awful sin. But she, just like with the father, never raised another hand at all to Jessica for fear of having to face the wrath of the twelve year old kid that would become Krayzie. In the beginning of the night, we were just pre-teens who had no idea what love was, but were willing to explore it. By the end of the night, I knew what love is and I knew how much I love Jessica. A love that still grows to this very day, almost ten years later. Because, as I walk through some of the biggest matches of my career and wonder how I'll ever get the strength to face down certain obstacles, I just look at her and remember the even bigger obstacles this beautiful woman has overcome. She is my strength...... to black!
