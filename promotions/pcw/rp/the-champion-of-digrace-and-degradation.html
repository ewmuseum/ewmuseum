The Champion of Digrace and Degradation

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: April 23 Oddly enough, Snow is now the three time PCW Heavyweight Champion of the World. Does he deserve it? No... of course not. Snow picked up the championship through cheating and deceit - and your parents tell you those things don’t get you ahead in life. Of course, the Killin’ Villain will never admit to that. He wouldn’t admit that his second, short-lived, title reign was anything but the result of his “Greatness” and “Superiority”. At the moment, there is no sign of the “sort of” PCW Heavyweight Champ. We find ourselves in a small diner, looking down at a man in a police uniform who is tearing open a packet of sugar for his coffee - getting a start on the morning. The officer is a fairly large man. Were he standing, we guess that he would be no less than a couple of inches over six feet. He’s built sturdily as well, though he could probably use a couple of hours in the gym. He’s long since left his youth behind, being a man in his early fourties with thinning greying hair and ever-present lines around his eyes... Officer: “So you’re the PCW guys?” He smirks but his eyes never rise to meet the camera.... Officer: “I’m Officer Gerald Thomas - Jason Snow’s new probation officer. Well, until today. You’ve heard right?” His eyes rise and he looks into the camera for a moment... Officer: “We let the dog loose today.” Things make a lot more sense now. Today is Jason Snow’s last day of probation. Nearly two years ago he was sentenced to eighteen months of probation that was to begin after a two-month stay at the Betty Ford clinic in Chicago Illinois. The incident has been well documented - Snow was drinking at the time and Superkicked a man one night after an altercation at a bar. The judge noticed that Snow’s troubles with the law had always involved his alcoholism, and thought that a Betty Ford clinic would do more good than prison. Snow, however, was sternly warned that a single slip up would land him behind bars for a very... very... long time... Officer: “If it were up to me, of course, he wouldn’t be getting off so easy. Noooo sir...” The Officer begins stirring his coffee... Officer: “Sick bastard... can’t stop talking about his ‘Superiority’... his ‘Greatness’... yeah... well, one of these days I’d like to drive my night stick right up his ‘Superior’ ass. ...Guys like Jason Snow never learn.” The Officer sighs, leans back in his chair, and takes a long sip of his coffee... Officer: “He’ll always be a bad apple. Was from the time he was born, as I understand it. Started beating kids up by the time he was... what? Ten? Eleven, maybe? Nothing’s changed now. He still has no respect. No respect for rules... authority.... nothing but himself.” The Officer shakes his head while he speaks... Officer: “Yeah, I’ve seen what he’s been up to in PCW. It’s a damn disgrace, I tell you! Passing the title around like a joint - er, you didn’t hear me say that.” The Officer scratches his head and takes another drink from his white cup of coffee and leans down on his elbows. He’s sitting next to a window that overlooks the parking lot outside... Officer: “I don’t know why guys like him are allowed to wrestle. Sure... he may have talent. They say when he went to prison a few years back, no one in there would touch him - afraid of the bastard, mind you. But still, no matter how good you are, you have to have some respect for things. Things like the title. For the two years that I’ve been watchin’ PCW, the title has always been treated with respect...” He shakes his head again and looks down at his coffee... Officer: “Then this bastard comes along... F(CENSORED)ks up the whole system. That’s all this kid’s good for - f(CENSORED)king things up. Hell, I’m supposed to be on vacation right now, but the prick called me up Sunday and said he wouldn’t be able to make it until Tuesday... and now he’s twenty minutes late....” He clenches his fist... Officer: “...Should have sent his ass back to the slammer.” At that moment, the Officer’s eyes lift up and roll - he spots Jason Snow at the entrance way. Snow, standing tall and proud, removes a pair of sunglasses and looks around the small diner. The Officer gestures to Snow with two fingers, catching the Killin’ Villain’s attention and drawing him over... Officer: “You’re late.” Snow: “I’m also Superior.” The Officer rolls his eyes one more time... Officer: “Well, lets get this thing over with.” Snow takes a seat on the opposite side of the table and removes his heavy black leather jacket - looks a little too heavy, actually, for the weather. Next, Snow leans on the table and rubs his unshaven face with his hands. He looks tired - like he woke up not too long ago. Snow has never been much of a morning person. His long blond hair is tied back on this morning - the first time that it’s been tied back since his return to the ring three weeks ago. Other than that, and the stubble, everything is just the same as it has been since his return; leather pants and Snake Skin boots. He also brought in a gym bag, which is now resting beneath the table... Officer: “Jason Snow, this is our last meeting. Has everything gone well this week?” Snow: “Gone well?” The Officer knows that he’s not going to like whatever Jason Snow is planning to say... Snow reaches down into his gym bag and produces the PCW Heavyweight Championship of the World. He slams it down on the table... Snow: “How’s it look to you?” The Officer leans back in his seat... Officer: “...Looks like you had a good week.” Snow: “Damn straight I did! You should have seen it, plebe! I, the Killin’ Villain, locked in the heat of battle with Controversial Corey Williams -” The Officer interrupts... Officer: “Hey, hey! I saw the match. Don’t come tellin’ ME that it was some ‘great battle’. I know you two faked the whole thing.” Snow looks shocked. He puts his hand on his chest and gasps at the Officer’s words... Snow: “Faked? I’ll have you know that we faked NOTHING! By Greatness, our battle on Sabotage will go down in history as one of all time classic matches of PCW! And another thing, plebe - the next time you interrupt me, you’ll soon find yourself with a Snake Skin boot planted snugly IN YOUR ASS!” The Officer’s eyes narrow while Snow’s are burning wildly. He’s always hated being interrupted... Officer: “That sounds like a threat to me.” Snow: “Take it as you well, peasant! But you - an Officer of the law for Greatness sakes - should know that Superior Individuals are NOT to be interrupted while speaking! How would you feel if you were to miss some of the Greatness and Superiority that’s spewing forth from my mouth? Hmm? Huh? How would you!? HOW WOULD YOU!?!?” The Officer rolls his eyes, passing this off as just another example of Snow’s growingly eccentric behavior. He sighs... Officer: “Calm down, Snow. I just want to sit with you for a couple of minutes, act like we’re doing jack s(CENSORED)t, and be on my way to the Virgin Islands for the week.” Snow raises an eyebrow... Snow: “Did you say... the virgin islands?” Officer: “Yeah... you know: Warm weather, nice beaches, beautiful women everywhere.” Snow: “By Greatness, THAT must be where Big Alan Steele is from!” Snow points at the Officer... Snow: “Peasant! I demand you take me to Mount Olympus IMMEDIATELY!” Officer: “Mount Olympus? Snow, Mount Olympus is somewhere in Greece, isn’t it? I’m going to the Virgin Islands, for God’s sake!” Snow: “Exactly! The Virgin islands! Where else would a thirty year old virgin be hiding out?” Officer: “Hiding? From you? You think Al Steele is hiding from you? The way I see things, you’re the only one doing any hiding.” The Officer is talking with his hands now - obviously, he is a rather avid fan of PCW. He knows all about the shameless way that Jason Snow won the PCW Heavyweight Championship of the World, and the way that Snow and the rest of New Breed have been using the title in their game of “monkey in the middle” with Big Alan Steele... Snow: “Hide? Me? By Greatness, peasant! Do you not understand the pure buffoonery of that statement? Why would I - a Superior Individual - want to hide from that talentless toga-wearing peasant, Big Alan Steele? Have you no sense, man!? I’m the Killin’ Villain, damn it!” Officer: “Well, I don’t really give a damn who you are right now. I’m just glad that I don’t have to see your ‘Superior’ face anymore. Now, lets end this in a handshake and hope we never meet again.” Snow: “A fine gesture, plebe! By Greatness, if I thought I would have to spend but another second looking at your gruesome face, I would probably vomit all over you! ... May be an improvement, however...” While he’s getting up, the Officer stops to glare at Snow and accidentally spills his coffee. The black liquid splashes on the table and spreads quickly... Officer: “Ah s(CENSORED)!” Snow: “Worry not, fowl peasant! I’ll handle this - be on your way.” The Officer gives Snow a curious look - why is Snow suddenly eager to help clean spilled coffee? Usually, Snow would be in an uproar by now, ranting about how “peasants aren’t careful enough” or some nonsense. When he sees Snow’s solution to the problem, he is appalled. The Killin’ Villain grabs the PCW Heavyweight Championship - the highest prize in all of wrestling - and begins to use it like a wash cloth to clean the spilled coffee. The Officer is shocked, to say the least... and we can’t bare to watch anymore. How low will Jason Snow stoop? The scene fades to black...
