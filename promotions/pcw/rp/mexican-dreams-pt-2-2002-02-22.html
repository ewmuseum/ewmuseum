Mexican Dreams pt.2

22 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 02/22/02 What was that voice that El Diablo heard? Who was that voice? And just what is El Diablo thinking? El Diablo distinctly heard a voice in the wind, and upon rationalising he discovered just what he needed to do, but just what did he need to do is what is unknown. El Diablo, so usually clueless, now has some inspiration in his morbid little world. But just what can it be? The door bursts open at a fast pace as the Mexican Devil charges into the small wooden shack, his entrance startles Angel, who is seated at a table watching some American television. 'What are you doing?' asks the shocked Mexican to his wife. Angel still startled, turns to look at her husband. 'I was just watching a little Television,' she protests. Angel feels the anger from El Diablo, despite no words from him. 'I'm sorry, but I am from America, you know!' she replies hesitantly to the shocked Mexican. The shack door slams shut as El Diablo steps further into the room. Yes, the shack is a one room complex owned by El Diablo in the middle of nowhere, but somewhere in the United States. 'I despise America though,' snarls El Diablo at his now scared wife. She stands up from behind the table she was seated at. 'I'm sorry, but I'm American and my family is American and that is why you are still in America,' rationalises Angel calmly. Angel walks to the Television and switches it off and again heads back to the table, she sits her self down on top of it, as El Diablo seems to be cooling off. 'You're right. But, I have some news to tell you,' says a once again calm Diablo. Angel swiftly crosses her right leg over left giving El Diablo an ample amount of thong to see. 'What is it?' asks Angel rudely. Angel shuffles over as El Diablo sits down on the table right next to his wife. They look at each other. 'It's something that could change both of our lives,' claims El Diablo. Angel places her right hand on El Diablo's right hand, and looks him in the eye. 'I'm listening,' states Angel seductively at her husband. Is it a fake sense of seductiveness? Or has Angel really grown to start loving her husband? Or is she just being the usual and mysterious Angel? A clear glass table that Angel and El Diablo are seated on fill the majority of the shack, along with an old and small Television attached to the wall some way in the distance. The shack has some small gray carpeting that looks it came from an old folks home and has the same kind of smell, which is very off putting, to normal people that is. A few chairs surround the table and some papers are on it also, devil knows what papers though. Other than that the shack is a very baron and secluded place that El Diablo must scarcely visit, and to top it off, no windows on the shack. Very primitive some would say, and it certainly looks it, especially due to the area the shack is in. 'I heard a voice outside,' states El Diablo to his now confused wife. She removes her hand from her husbands and gives him a concerned look. 'No honey, that's just you thinking out loud,' Angel tells him. A look of utter disgust comes across El Diablo's face. 'Don't patronise me,' he whispers, 'I heard a voice and it was telling me to do things,' finishes the gleeful Mexican. Angel really now does look concerned. 'Are you insane?' Angel asks the obvious question. El Diablo still looks on in disgust. 'No, I am not insane, I definitely heard the voice and you will have heard it if you were out there,' claims the Mexican Devil. Angel steps up from the table and opens the door to the shack. El Diablo looks on in confusion. 'Lets go outside and see if I can hear it then, honey,' proclaims Angel sensibly. A thud is heard as El Diablo jumps off from the table and leads the way outside; Angel soon follows with a slam of the door following her. 'So, where did you hear this 'voice'?' Angel asks her husband sarcastically. El Diablo indicates by just looking all over the open area. 'It was just outside, wherever I was it spoke to me,' claims El Diablo. Angel simply nods and walks around some of the open area with El Diablo's left hand in her right, and then stops at a point. 'We'll try here,' states Angel to the stubborn Mexican. Silence echoes freely around the area as no voice is heard. Angel, however waits a few more moments and still no sound, let alone that of a voice. 'Well, where is it?' she asks Diablo sarcastically. El Diablo looks confused, himself. 'Uhh, maybe it's shy,' El Diablo states in his quick thinking state. Angel lets go of El Diablo's hand and turns to face him. 'Face it, honey, there was no voice,' Angel says calmly as she plants a kiss on El Diablo's lips and starts walking away. El Diablo turns around and chases after Angel, he startles her. 'It told me to make a Mexican restaurant,' says the Mexican. Angel smiles. 'No,' she says abruptly. El Diablo holds her around the waste and looks her in the eyes. 'I'm going to do it, with or without you,' El Diablo tells her, giving her an ultimatum. Angel stares back at her husband a few moments. 'Fine, but it better not cost too much,' agrees Angel. El Diablo leans down to kiss Angel on the cheek. 'You won't regret this,' El Diablo states happily. Angel starts to head back into the shack, alone. 'I always do,' Angel says under her breath. El Diablo fails to hear and waits outside as the wind blows heavily once more and the thud of the shack door closing is heard again. El Diablo smiles. 'Mexican, that is the culture that America needs,' he finishes as he turns and heads back into the shack. The door slams shut. 'Mexican?' a sigh is heard, 'Idiot, pure idiot,' says that same voice that El Diablo was speaking of. TO BE CONTINUED
