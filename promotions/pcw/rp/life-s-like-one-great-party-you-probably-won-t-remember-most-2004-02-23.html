Life's like one great party, you probably won't remember most of it...

23 February 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2/23/04 Arson awakened slowly, his blonde locks tangled in across his face. Minutes passed by as he tried to shake the grogginess off and figure out where the hell he was. Shang-tsung... Shang Wang... Shanghai! Right... He rolled over to find himself face-to-face with some Chinese chick. Squinting slightly, he tried to remember the past night’s occurrences, but everything was just a blur to him, and his splitting head hurt too much to think. Rolling onto his back, he stared up at the ceiling for a moment before forcing himself out of bed. “Hey, Connie Chung, nap time’s over, baby!” Brian shouted, and the young girl jumped up, nearly falling off the side of the bed. Brian went around scooping up the lady’s clothing and tossed them at her bare breasts. “Let’s get a move on, honey, I got stuff to do.” “(Insert Chinese words here).” The girl swung out of bed with an angered look on her face, quickly throwing her clothes on. She began yelling at him in Chinese as Brian sat down in a chair with one leg up on his knee and his arms folded. “Yeah, okay... here you go, okay? Money? Is that good? Yes? Just nod your head,” he pulled three one-hundred dollar bills from his wallet on the table. Holding them up to the girl, her tone changed, and she smiled gladly taking the cash. “All right then, domo arigato, sweetheart,” Brian waved as the girl sauntered out of the room with a confused look on her face. As soon as she had left, Brian walked over to the window and opened the blinds. The hotel PCW had them set up in had a beautiful view of the city. The sun was shining bright, not a cloud in the sky, and it looked as if it would turn out to be a wonderful day. “Fuckin’ commie bastards.” Brian threw the shades shut and grabbed the remote from the bed, flipping the TV on. A bunch of random Chinese ads flashed across the screen, and Brian turned it off with a look of disdain on his face. “Get me out of this damn place,” he mumbled. The thing is, Brian didn’t hate foreign countries... just China. Too many Chinese people, it was ridiculous, you’d think they’d get tired of reproducing. Arson enetered the bathroom and gazed into the mirror. The cuts and bruises from the vicious attacks Shaun Styles laid upon him weeks ago were almost gone, and his near-perfect visage was almost back to magnificent-ness. After fixing his hair, Brian stretched and flexed in front of the mirror. “Wow, you look fantastic...” He smirked, nodding slowly at his reflection. After taking a shower, Brian put on one of the many suits he had brought with him that were hanging in the closet. Aligning the suit gracefully in the mirror, he looked to the clock on the bedside stand: 1:15. “Damn... I need a drink.” Brian was never one to let a head-splintering hangover get in the way of drinking... or any other drugs for that matter. Ever since the PCW show had been put on hold, Arson had been stuck in Shanghai with nothing to do but party every night with strippers and whores. And with nights full of constant sexual enjoyment, one couldn’t help but feel sorry for him. Training was the last thing on his mind; he was frickin’ ripped, why would he need to train? You’ve seen the pictures. A wrestling phenom, whose figure never seemed to be tattered unless some inconsiderate fool jumps him on the way to the ring and proceeds to beat him senseless with a chair. Brian straightened his collar and made some last minute adjustments to his hair before putting on his trademark sunglasses. Another night on the hunt for the could-be model. Grabbing his gold watch and leather wallet, Arson opened the hotel door and stepped out into the hallway. Glancing down both directions, he headed toward the elevators, hands in either pocket, ready for action.
