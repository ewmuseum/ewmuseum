A Pinky Swear Is A Promise Kept...

9 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Title: A Pinky Swear Is A Promise Kept... Featuring: Rent-a-Hero Date: 10-9-2002 I hated lying to her...God, I hated looking in her soft blue eyes and telling her, 'No honey....daddy's just fine,' or ,'No sweety, daddy didn't get hurt by the bad man with the bleach blonde hair.' She just sat next to me, holding my large hand tightly with her two little hands, her eight year old innocence a comfort to me in my time of pain. My body was an aching and Red October was a blur...I managed to draw a few conclusions of what happened however through the good word of fellow citizens and good old Rent-A-Common Sense. One, I'm still the PCW Continental champ....evident by the beautiful leather strap encrusted with those fine, glittering medallions I came to love so much...currently in the lap of my little daughter, Amy. I was usually quite protective of my title....but whocould dare say no to her when she caressed the large middle medallion of the belt with great care, her finger tips tracing the engraved words as I watched on, laying up right in my hospital bed. Two, I beat the living daylights out of Frisco. I may not remember much of last night...but I do remember cavinghis skull in with a fire extinguisher. I'm usually not the top to revel in my hidden sadism....but in this case...I'll make an exception. He deserved it, he deserved to see his blood pour all over the mat on end with no means of making it stop. He paid for his sins in pain and the final payment was last night. THREE, I'm in this hospital bed...wearing this ITCHY and very NON-HEROIC hospital gown whichby no means matches my Spiderman 2099 mask, still never removed from Sunday...was because of that vile villain, Xavier Kannon. He rushed me....rushed me without warning and spiked my head to the mat with no mercy, he certainly could have called it a night right there. No siree Krypton, he felt he HAD to Hallmark me through the very stretcher that was carrying me out as well....what a generous guy huh? I guess losing the World title in the OPENING MATCH will do that to ya. Fact of the matter is, I've already had it in for Xavier since the day he brutalized Big Alan Steele and I was forced to watch it from a backstage monitor...last night...he just gave me all the more reason to cave his face in with a little old-fashioned Kryptonian justice the soonest I got back to a PCW ring. Last night really didn't anger me all that much at first....it wasn't until I was told Amy had managed to find a monitor backstage that I became furious...enraged if you will. Usually, I try to protect my little Lois Lane. I let her play in my locker room and keep her away from any and every monitor I can....I always feared the day I would let her watch me or anyone else wrestle...she'd see something she should have no business being exposed to at her young age... Brutality.Brutality is an acquired trait. I learned it from years of internal pain....and it was brought out by Jason Snow. The way I figure, if she is never exposed...she will never acquire it. Unfortunately...she found a monitor at Red October, and she saw me....she saw me beat another man to an inch of his life without even accepting his pleas of mercy. She saw a man I hated attempt to end my career, all I had worked for in less then three minutes....she saw human hatred, anger, and brutality...all broadcasted to her in the comfort of the PCW backstage locker room. That's when the questions came... 'Why did you hurt that man daddy?' 'Is it okay for you to bleed like that?' 'Did you just go tosleep for a reeeeeally long time after that man got you?' Lies...every answer I gave her...a lie. A lie so I could protect the one thing I cherished inher more than anything, amongone of the things that made me even fathom the responsibility of taking care of a child....her innocence. Never could I watch her become like the other orphans the social worker told me of... The prostitutes... Gang bangers... Baseheads... Kids who needed a chance who were forsaken...and just became apart of the problem instead of the solution. Amy had every chanceto be saved, and I grasped it without pause. What she saw on that monitor....was me in my worst two forms...weakened and sadistic. Never again....NEVER AGAIN will I lie to her about my pains...about the evils of man...about my weakness....because there will be no 'again'. When I have the power to leave this hospital bed...I have only one mission....Destroy Xavier Kannon, end him. Frisco is done, eliminated from the picture. Now I have my chance to get ym hands on Xavier and put an end to his reign of terror in PCW. The taxes...the injustices...the toying with lives and careers...I end it all. Many have faced Xavier and have tried to end his power trip....and many have failed. I can't afford to fail. It's not an option.This hospital bed is not an option... Lying to my daughter is not an option... I grasped her hands, squeezing them tight as she looked to me...her Superman. Her dad. With that little smile...that little tiny smile that lit my heart afire and gave me reason to go on...she asked. 'Daddy...are you going to get the bad man?' I smiled back at her, running my fingers through her honey blonde hair, my aches and pains an afterthought when she asked me that.... 'Yes sweety...I'm going to slay the bad man. Pinky swear..'My pinky, overwhelming in comparison to her minisucle pinky, locked around hers....locked for those few long seconds as we sealeda bond, a pact...between father and daughter. She smiled, for she knew when I pinky swore her it was promise that was to be kept, whether I swore I'd get her a roll of her beloved Sweet Tarts or I swore I'd move a mountain just because it got in her way. This was my daughter...my princess....and I just pinky swore her I'd bring Xavier Kannon down....in a classic battle of good versus evil no less...I have a promise to keep And I never make a promise that I can't keep...And that promise, means hell for Xavier. 'Daddy...what's a bedpan?' Oh brother...do I have to answer that? ---- View Rent-a-Hero's Biography
