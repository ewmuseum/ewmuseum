And Then Came the Call (Intro: III of III)

27 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 27.11.02 And then came the call. Yes, that’s right folks, not just any call, THEE call. A call that made it so that Abner was banished to the streets no more! Abner, for the one of the few occasions in his life, had a stroke of good luck.......some good news.....for opportunity had called, destiny had called......and most importantly, a member from Xavier Kannon's staff, had called! That's right, you see now Abner had hit the 'big time', Abner was in Primetime! PCW, watch out, there’s a hick with a stick and he’s comin’ fo’ yo’…..mother!.....or something..... And so our favorite redneck/anarchist was in business. From a drunken high school legend, to a cult-following zombie, to a 'favorite' inmate in Vermont's Detention 29 facility, Abner never really thought of himself as “pro-wrestling material”. And now, in light of recent news, Abner could now safely proclaim that he had seen and done it all, or at least a little! As a young, fairly bright man, one with his whole future lying out in front of him, Abner didn't necessarily have to jump at the opportunity. True, he was a gifted individual with well-rounded skills, some rather.....peculiar abilities, and was in top physical condition; so basically, Abner could do just about anything that he wanted (well, anything that didn’t require real thought that is......). So with nothin’ better to do, Abner made up his mind: Yes, he would join PCW..... So now, that begs the question: 'is PCW ready for Abner?'. Well, this answer might surprise you.....but, yes. You see, sure, Abner is quite a different character…..has some slight personality problems, eh, and may even need a slight attitude adjustment…..but Primetime has seen 'em all! Abner was barely freaky enough to make in a cult....and yes, true, it was a redneck cult, but no matter, for you see, PCW's had more freaks than all of the world's cults combined! From gigantic, middle-aged Russians, to cape-wearing superheroes, PCW makes Detention 29 look like a 'model citizen' environment! And that's no stretch of the truth. Now Abner, still not quite over the whole “Corn/Third Party” incident, wasn't quite sure as to how to approach this new stage in his life: trust has got him nowhere, and his brains even less further along…..sooo…..what about bronze? Well, as far as we can tell, from his D-29 surviving experience anyway, that seems to be a strong point of Abner. And defeating his PCW practice opponent in less than two minutes was definitely in agreement with this thesis. But of course, Abner defeated a somewhat “popular” AAA indy-league chub…..not a PCW superstar! And along those lines, Abner’s never fought a pro-wrestler in his entire life! But hey, defeating the indy-league chub, that was a start, and a good one at that. And of course, Abner's four long years in Detention 29 is partially responsible for his fighting (noticed how I didn’t say ‘wrestling?) abilities. You can thank three ex-Navy SEALS, two six-foot-five foot bikers, and a homosexual Mexican with a tendency to bite your ass for that. In D-29, it was 'kill or be kil....' nah, scratch that, it was more like 'kill or be raped'! And in that kind of situation, you either rose to the occasion, or woke up the next morning with sore spirits, and even sorer ass! And so yadi,-yadi-yada-blah-blah-blah, basically, the point is, Abner was prepared for PCW. Physically, and even mentally, but maybe not so skillfully that is. For to be totally prepared in a skill-level sense, you have to have a defined arsenal. And yeah, back in D-29, Abner sure had his arsenal: toilet handles, bench legs, eating utensils, and even a decent sized piece of gravel. But technically speaking, Abner didn't really acquire a set of moves then, and his skills now, certainly are not up to par with his fellow PCW members. But that can be fixed easily enough…… So after a quick trip to the store, 'Holy shit, Bud's $5.99 a six-pac now!', Abner made his way down to the PCW training center. And there, he made his peace with the monster that is 'skilllessness'. After training hard a few hours a day for a few weeks in a row, Abner was beginning to feel confident in his skills. His naturally ability is of course unparalleled, and his physical condition has always been superb. So basically, the technical aspects were all he had to work on. He worked hard, very hard, and soon, it paid off. So now, to bring us up to date, it’s November twenty-second, and Abner’s ready to make his PCW, but there’s still one question, a question burning deep inside of him: when? And maybe even more importantly, where? Well, that still remains to be seen. But rest assured: he’s fairly well prepared, his morale is high, and he’s eager to make a good first impression; in other words, expect him to start off with one in the ‘win’ column But where’s the match, where’s his opponent, and where’s Abner’s ticket to wrestling stardom? Only Xavier Kannon, owner and CEO of PCW, knows…. But don’t fret, for Abner’s time will come. There’s always a time and a place…..there’s always a time and a place….. But what until then? Who knows? Abner’s had a rough summer, both financially and mentally. Financially though of course, his new ‘rookie wages’ sent every week by the PCW offices, has helped out a lot. But not enough. He’s still forced to work part time at a local Wal-Mart, and as you can imagine, it’s not a pretty sight…… Through thick and thin though, Abner’s done it, Abner has survived. The odds may have been against him, but hasn’t it always been that way? It seems that way…… So with a wrestling career on his mind, and an ass-whoopin’ beer in hand (hey, it rhymed!), Abner’s a little bit…..anxious. But anxiousness, as we all know, if not fed properly on a daily basis, inevitably turns to boredom…..not good. You see, Abner’s still not over his natural tendency to be part of a “social club”. His Third Party, cult-like instincts are still in him, and the blood of anarchy runs warmly through his veins. These things, when combined with boredom, lead to some very nasty results…..very nasty indeed….. And so with his training “complete” (though there’s many things about the “biz” he still has to learn……the hard way that is), and his mind made up, Abner takes to the street, on a recruiting mission…You see, he’s going to start Third Party back up again…..or is he?..... Abner begins to think to himself, “There’s nothin’ better to do. My friends are gone, I’m a long way from home…..my family’s prob’ly forgot about me by now…..so what do I have to lose? It’ll just give me more experience. I can’t possibly train any harder…..or even much more for that matter…..The rest of my skills will have to be acquired by experience, not practice. So why not? It’s a big city (Boston), and I think I can catch a few good fish……So where to start…..” Well, with a man like Abner anyway, the answer is clear: “The bars!” So with alcohol, social gatherings and drunk-fights on his mind, Abner takes to the streets, direction: the closest pub in sight…..A bad choice for a PCW rookie? Probably……
