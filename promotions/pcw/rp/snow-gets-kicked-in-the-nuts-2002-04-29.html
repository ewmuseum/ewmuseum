Snow Gets Kicked in the Nuts

29 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: April 29, 2002 Snow remains PCW Champion, much to the distaste of the majority of the fans. There’s not much doubt that Jason Snow wrestles as well as anyone else who may have worn the PCW Championship if he did not, but the way that Snow won the Championship is nothing short of dispicable. But lately, we’ve never seen him without the PCW Heavyweight Championship of the World. There are those who say that he’s just keeping it warm for Big Alan Steele... There are also those who say that Tyler Nelson wants the title, and Tyler always gets what he wants. But does he? We all know that the Killin’ Villain is capable of coming up schemes of his own... The champ sits on a park bench and reads what appears to be the National Enquirer. The champ, however, doesn’t appear to be Jason Snow on this day - he’s in disguise. In fact, the only way we can tell that it, indeed, is Jason Snow, is that there are a pair of Snake Skin boots sticking out from beneath the long, grey trench coat. He’s also wearing a grey hat and a pair of dark sunglasses to complete his disguise... the camera closes in... Snow: “Damn it, plebe! Don’t you know that you’re not to approach me when I’m disguised as a mere peasant!? What if one of the more intellectually gifted of the other peasants around were to notice a television camera!? They would know that I was a Superior Individual, because no one wants to listen to what a mere plebe has to say! Off with you now! Go!” As ordered, our camera man backs away until Snow is well into the distance, and then zooms in on the reading Killin’ Villain... With our ultra sensitive microphones, we can hear Snow gasp... Snow: “By Greatness! There are three armed people being bread in Asia!? How the hell would they every be able to ride a motorcycle!? The free hand would just throw the balance off! What kind of inferior world do we live in!?” At that moment, Snow is approached by a young boy. The child is no more than eight years old and has golden blond hair that shines under the sunlight in the bright green park. He looks up at Snow with wide eyes... Boy: “Whatchya readin’?” Snow is surprised to hear another voice. He glances over the top of his paper... He groans... Snow: “Ugh... If there’s one thing I hate more than these damn peasants that plague places like this, it’s their offspring!” The boy doesn’t understand Snow’s rambling. Like a child, he tries to start a conversation... Boy: “Do you like the Power Rangers?” Snow: “Po-wer Ran-gers? What in the hell are you babbling about, child? Has the disease of utter inferiority struck you so hard, so soon?” The child doesn’t understand. Snow removes his sunglasses and rolls his eyes... Snow: “Go find someone else to drool on! Can’t chance you tossing your damn snot at me - unruly peasant child.” The child still doesn’t understand Snow’s words, but he does understand Snow’s tone of voice and he doesn’t like it. An angry look comes over the young boys face and he crosses his arms... Boy: “I don’t think you’re a very nice man!” Snow: “What in the hinges of hell does NICENESS have to do with anything!? I’m a Superior Individual, damn it! SUPERIOR! Do you not know what that is!?” Angered at the child, Snow gets down to his level. He comes off the bench and onto his knees to confront the small, stubborn, boy. With his knees on the grass, the Killin’ Villain begins a lecture... Snow: “That’s the probably with you little peasants! No one teaches you any respect! Well, by Greatness, I’ll have you know something!” The boy interrupts with a scream... Boy: “YOU WON’T HAVE ME KNOW ANYTHING!” The boy’s parents must have taught him self-defense, because he quickly lashes out with a right legged kick that strikes Snow directly in the groin. Snow’s face turns pale white as the boy’s parents rush up to the scene... Father: “What’s going on here!?” The mother, a woman in her late twenties who’s wearing a brightly flowered dress, kneels down to make sure her son is alright after the confrontation with Snow - a stranger to her. The father is a tall, thin man with thick black hair and glasses. He looks down at Snow, unsure of what to say... Father: “Uh...” With his face now turning ever-redder, the pain-ridden Killin’ Villain gasps for breath and begins cursing... Snow: “OOOOOOOOOOHHHHHHH!!!! Trials of Greatness and furies of HEEEELLLLLLLLL!!!!!” Snow, buckled over and clutching his groin, is trying hard to catch his breath. The child looks pleased... Father: “Um, are you going to be ok... sir?” Through the corner of his bloodshot eyes, Snow glares at the man... Snow: “Well I’d be a hell of a lot better had your wench never given birth to that little miscreant!” The man looks down at his son and then back to Snow... Father: “Well, what were you trying to do to him, sir? I assure you, we did NOT raise a violent child! He must have had some reason!” Snow: “Reason!? What in the hell kind of reason could there possibly be for kicking a Superior Individual like myself in his area of Supreme Greatness!?” The father glances down at his wife and the couple realize that they’re not dealing with their average civilian. Snow, still lying on the ground and clutching his groin, screams out another curse of pain... Snow: “OH FOR THE LOVE OF GREATNESS AND ALL THINGS SUPREME! FIERY PITS OF INFERIORITY! How can you peasants be so CRUEL to your Superiors!? Hooooowwww!??!?!” Snow closes his eyes and lies motionless, doubled over. The mother looks to her son and asks... Mother: “Why did you kick this poor man?” The child, who’s deceptively smart, thinks of every kid’s show warning he’s ever seen and tries to find one that fits his current situation... Boy: “He offered me candy!” Snow’s bloodshot eyes flash wide... Snow: “Candy!? I offered you no such thing you tiny peasant! If anything, I was offering you a Snake Skin boot in your puny peasant ass!” The parents of the boy both let their jaws drop at Snow’s comment. Snow, still burdened by the pain that’s shooting from his groin to his chest, doesn’t care. If anything, he wishes that he HAD struck the child - it would have saved him a lot of pain... Father: “What kind of sick-o are you?” Snow: “I HOPE that’s a compliment!” Snow, sensing confrontation in the father’s voice, tries to get to his feet but the pain is still too overwhelming... Father: “You think you can just pick on innocent children!?” Snow: “Damn it, plebe! I’m the Killin’ Villain, I can do whatever the hell I please! Be it picking on children, the elderly, or the F(CENSORED)KING WAR-AMPS!” Snow is in no mood to watch his language right now. He begins stripping his disguise off, letting his summer-yellowing blond hair hang into his red face. Immediately, the boy recognizes the PCW Champion... Boy: “You’re... YOU’RE JASON SNOW!” Usually, Snow can’t help but be flattered when he’s recognized. Now, however, lying on the grass with his hands trying to comfort his swollen testicles, he couldn’t care less about his fame... Boy: “JASON SNOW!! FROM WRESTLING!!” The father tries to calm his son... Father: “Son, this is probably just a man who looks like Jason Snow. What would Jason Snow be doing here?” Snow glares at the father... Snow: “Damn you, peasant! I now where this demon-spawn of yours gets his stupidity! Can you not see true Greatness when it’s lying in front of you, clutching it’s nuts!?” The father is speechless... the boy, however, is not... Boy: “You’re a big MEAN HEAD! And I don’t like the way you’ve been treating MY favorite wrestler, BIG ALAN STEELE! And here’s another thing for YOU to know, mister!” By now, Snow has struggled back to his knees. At the same moment that he removes his hands from his groin to brush his long blond hair away from his face, the child delivers another painful kick to the exact same area. Snow’s mouth opens and his face strains but now sound is made - other than that of air bursting from his mouth. It would have been a scream, had he not been so overwhelmed by pain. He doubles back over and jolts from time to time... Father: “Uh... you know, honey, I think we should leave.” The man grabs his son by the hand and leads him, along with his wife, away from the scene. Snow doesn’t move as the camera man hurries to his aid. When he gets there, Snow is lying on his face with his buttocks high in the air. He can only manage to mutter one word... Snow: “Hospital...” And on that note, the scene fades to black...
