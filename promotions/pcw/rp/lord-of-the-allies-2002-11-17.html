Lord of the Allies

17 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: November 17, 2002 Ere the Lord of the Allies reignsO'er kings with no sense to confound,Embrace must he within his mindQuiescent iniquity profound.Lust is rich, cooperation poor,Among generals teeming three score;At their foundation lies their demise:A shaky reliance on four.From fiery crash to chilled departure,Wanton degradation writhes unfazed;Morals crumble under neon signsAnd a bastion for the depraved.Withered masks of old strengths will fly,Eroding all tradition of right;Uncomely weakness will brave the dawn,Its former throne usurped by might.Greed and gluttony take their toll,Lacking all structure to guide;Corruption stirs like a supernovaAnd the bear retreats to hide.Past transgressions swallow whollyImbeciles who danced their tunes,Purity itself is raped and pillaged,Much to heavenly minister's muse.From darkened hearts red rivers rageWith hushed aid of razor tooth;Free as children in a midday sun,Deceit makes hostage of truth.Devils laugh as bears unhingeAnd cry unto the sky;The warning dies on deafened earsWith no will to wonder why.Savage beasts sacrifice the sowAnd order must walk the plank,But instead it turns and runs awayWhile hunters dart aft like snakes.The bepantied head doth come and roar,Tempering madness at its acme;With righteous rage the gauntlet decreesOur world is of one fidelity.The scampering office sighs reliefAs the saner skulls prevail,But all among would die of frightIf eyes glimpsed behind the veil.For in the end the swine returns,mocking Easter's sacred soul;Legions will, like, so mark out...Hey! Don't doubt me, punk... You will.
