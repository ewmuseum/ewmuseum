'Bob's Story Time'...Even God Makes Mistakes, Part 6

4 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11-4-2002 We find ourselves, once more, with the warm and inviting face of God, or Bob as he prefers. He smiles sincerely and his eyes light up as the camera clicks on from wherever cameras in heaven click on from. He sits on his white couch, popcorn and beers at the ready, and in his hand he holds a rather large remote control. This is presumably for the incredibly large Television that takes up nearly an entire wall of his office…Hey, this is God; do you think he would do anything small? Of course not. The camera zooms in a hair, and we can see that God is actually quite happy to share more of his story. When he speaks, he sounds excited, although his tone is tinged with a touch of what can only be regret… Bob: Why, welcome back, my children! It has been far too long since our last sharing of the Word. I had nearly forgotten this; time does seem to run together up here. I guess that is the price of being both omnipotent AND omniscient. I guess you good people would like to hear more about the time a great individual and I were able to spend, “Working” together. The Lord King , and your Lord God Almighty…Can I get an Amen and a Hallelujah? Before I get into my spiel, I should share a couple of things with you. Time DOES run together. It is not often that anything you mortals do catches my attention and is able to hold it for longer than a heartbeat. Today though, the man of whom we speak is no ordinary man. Pendragon caught the eye of God and held it for longer than anyone has in quite some time. I was sad when I truly had to send him back to you; after all, he did not belong here just yet. It dismayed me because I had found a man who was both humble AND unafraid before me. A fellow who I could talk to without worrying about him pissing himself as so many others have done in the past. Pendragon is one I can actually call a friend, and I look forward to the day when he is supposed to come home. Oh, and before I forget, I have a message for some of you….You who call yourselves Christians and who flock to church on Sunday to merely sin again on Monday, I have news for you; You’re going to HELL!!! That’s right. Rules are rules. Those of you who would attempt to pass judgment on others for living how they wish? Yep. Hell for you too I say….REPENT!! AND THEE SHALT BE SAVED! Bob smiles and clears his throat.. Bob: Ok. I’ll come down from my soapbox now. Isn’t it great to watch a program that entertains AND delivers a powerful message? I think so. Maybe I’ll get picked up on syndication. Now where were we? Ahh yes. I was about to relate another anecdote about my friend, Venny. Let’s see…When last we left our hero, he had just been sent back to earth, reincarnated as a blind man. A hefty task to place upon anyone, but more so to one who has never been without his sight for twenty six years. Let’s watch…. Bob presses a button on the oversized remote and the giant television screen comes to life. The shot zeroes in on it until it appears bob’s screen is our screen. A strikingly handsome blonde fellow wearing dark glasses stands as if he is lost. In his right hand he carries a white cane, trademark of the blind. A small child has grabbed his arm and attempts to lead him away…. Blind VP: Unhand me WHELP! I can make my own way…. Child: But mister…I am just trying to help. Everybody needs help from time to time. Blind VP: Not THIS body. Turn me loose! The child releases her hold on Venneman’s arm, and he stumbles awkwardly away. He holds his cane in front of himself as he walks, oblivious of everything that goes on around him…. “Blind. One more screw up in the process of getting me back to where I belong. How in the hell am I going to get out of this one? I can’t see to save my ass…Hmm. Perhaps that idea is not at all a bad one…” Venneman stumbles along, unaware of where he heads. He doesn’t notice the police roadblock, nor does he see the armed gunman who holds a young woman hostage in front of a pawn shop. From here, it all happens fast. The gunman has his back to Venneman, and Venneman walks right into him. Even in his blind form, Venneman is a large and powerfully built man, and the impact causes the surprised gunman to lose his hold on the hostage. He turns and points the gun at an awkwardly apologizing blind man… Blind VP: One thousand apologies good sir…I did not see thee standing in my way… Gunman: Yeah. Sometimes you just get in the wrong place at the wrong time. Venneman’s ears perk up as the gunman cocks the hammer. He quickly sidesteps as the man takes aim at the “defenseless” blind fellow… BLAM BLAM BLAM BLAM! RATATATATATATATATATATATATATATATATATAT! The gunman is riddles with bullet holes by the policeman and Pendragon stands, unscathed. The gathered onlookers let out a cheer and Venneman blindly looks around at the where the sound comes from. He starts across the road, following the sounds, until a woman screams. Pendragon freezes in his tracks. From the left of the screen, a large truck zooms in, breaks screaming. Blind VP: Good Christ…. The truck plows into the blind fellow, who instantly is turned to a red vapor…A semi truck will do that to you, and the scene switches back to the unsullied whiteness of Heaven. Pendragon stands angrily outside of the waiting room, unable to locate Peter. In one hand, he holds a white steel Excalibur… Venneman: PETER!!! YOU BLITHERING FOOL! PAYMENT COMES! Peter steps from the door marked “RESEARCH” and freezes as he sees the enraged Venneman… Peter: What is all this noise….. He trails off, and holds his one arm out in front of his body to try and Keep Venneman away. Pendragon advances anyhow and whacks the limb off. Peter screams with pain, but he cannot even rub the nub to soothe the wound, as Venneman had already taken his other arm. He just stands there in fear and awe of the soul who would dare to rock the Heavens… Venneman: Shall we try once more, Peter? Peter starts to shiver in fear as the shot pulls back from the large television and we find ourselves once more in the presence of God. He laughs out loud at what he ahs seen… Bob: Ahh yes….That Peter surely had a good time with my pal Venny. I just hoped he would get it right next time, although, you and I both know that he probably will not….Until next time, worship me, love one another and think nothing but happy thoughts of purity…GOODNIGHT! The scene abruptly cuts…. >>>TO BE CONTINUED
