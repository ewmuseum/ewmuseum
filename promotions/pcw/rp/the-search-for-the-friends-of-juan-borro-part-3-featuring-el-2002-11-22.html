The Search for the Friends of Juan Borro (Part 3) featuring El Diablo

22 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/22/02 {Fade} Wake up…. Come on man wake up! ***Confusion*** Where am I? Your in the car Romantico, we have to get going if we want to get to Manhattan by noon, there’s a lot of traffic ahead. Romantico rubs his eyes and sees outline of El Diablo. His mind finally sets in and he lets out a yawn. Done with your beauty sleep? You slept for about eighteen hours. Yea, I’m done. I haven’t been able to get much sleep the last few nights, so I guess I was tired. Still thinking about what it will feel like? Yea. I’m worried though, what if it’s not what I expect. Life is never what you expect, you just have to go with it, or hate everything about it, which is perfectly fine. You ever wish you were dead? Who do you think your talking to, my name is El Diablo of course I do every now and then. Sometimes I feel like I wasn’t meant to be in this world. Romantico then draws his attention outside the window. In just a few days he’s gone from just seeing a cow every one hundred miles, to now seeing about hundreds of people every few seconds. Life goes by too quick sometimes. And other times it goes way too slow. You know Romantico, you think you’re different, but a lot of people understand where you’re coming from. I know I just wish I knew where I came from myself. {Honk} You man can’t drive vehicle. What you do get fuck off road. El Romantico and El Diablo hear the screams from the little Pakistani man in the taxi behind them and they just break into laughter. Should I? Don’t. El Diablo gets out of the vehicle with a bat in hand and then makes his way to the taxi behind him. What you do man, I fuckin kill you, stupid man, get away from my car. El Diablo proceeds by bashing every window on the taxi and then sprints back to the car. HIT IT! Romantico takes the wheel, as they drive write through all of the traffic. OH CRAP! Watch out! Romantico just nearly skids out of the way of a construction crew as they finally make there way through the long line of traffic. That’s one way of getting out of a bad situation If I may say so myself. You know Romantico, your not that bad, but don’t think that means I like you or anything, because I still believe you’re a mellow depressive enlightening foul. Heh, you’re not so bad yourself. What did you say that address was again? It’s the first house on West 16th St. 4th Ave. Romantico follows the directions until he finally reaches the destination. This is it. Are you ready? Just give me a second. Romantico stares at the brick building, with a little display of flowers on the third window. The building looks strong and firm, surviving hundreds of years. Romantico then thinks about the work put into the building of the house. What beauty! Huh? Nothing, let’s just get going. Romantico closes the door of the car and takes a sigh as he steps closer and closer to the front door of the house. Are you all right? Yea, I’m fine. Romantico rings the doorbell and a large Russian man quickly and fiercely answers the door. Who are you two? The deep Russian voice rings through the ears of Romantico as he realizes he must have gone to the wrong house. Oh, I’m sorry, wrong house. Romantico and Diablo are about to make their way back to their car when Romantico hears a sweet and tame voice. Who was that daddy? Romantico runs back up to the door and screams out. Juliet! Juliet makes her way outside and then stares at Romantico. Can I help you? I…I… Juliet stares into the eyes of Romantico and then through some miraculous event sees the reflection of Juan Borro. OMG! El Diablo then stares at Romantico but sees nothing. Juan! Juliet slowly mutters the words out of her soft and graceful lips. I…I’m Juan’s brother El Romantico. Can…. can I see him? It’s been so long. El Romantico pauses for a moment and stares at El Diablo who slowly turns away wanting nothing to do with it. What’s wrong? Concern comes upon the face of Juliet as Romantico finally answers…. Juan has DIED! The world froze for that moment and so did Juliet. Her eyes became lifeless and her lips pale. Even though it had been so long since the two have been together it was as if her heart was still filled with their memories. And then it happened…. Tear flowed down the eyes of Juliet as she just threw herself into the arms of Romantico weeping. Romantico held his head his head down not knowing what to say or do to comfort her. WHY HIM? Her screams were heard vividly as she lifted her head and Romantico saw her eyes filled with sorrow and pain. {FLASH} Romantico pictures the eyes of Juan being pulled away on a stretcher the night he died. His eyes were filled with the same sorrow and pain. {FLASH} Romantico then sees a similar picture of his mother being killed right in front of his with nothing but that same sorrow and pain in her eyes. NOT AGAIN! EL Diablo went to check on Romantico and as Romantico turned around El Diablo notices a tear falling from his left eye. Romantico, look! El Diablo points at the eye of Romantico and Romantico wipes at his face and sees a tear lying there on his fingertips. He stares at it for a split second as it dissolves in his fingertip. {FADE} TO BE CONTINUED!
