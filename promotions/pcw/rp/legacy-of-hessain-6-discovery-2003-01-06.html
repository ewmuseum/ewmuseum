Legacy of Hessain 6: Discovery

6 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 06/01/03 Recap: While testing out his human disguise, Hessain entered a whorehouse. Badly in need of a meal, he took one lady of the night to seclusion under his peasant disguise. However, just as he was about to make his move, the illusion wore off and the whore found herself in the clutches of a vampire. The screams she makes alert the guards to Hessain's presence... * * * Legacy of Hessain Part 6: Discovery I dropped the pale woman to the wooden floorboards. Her head thumped and bounced like a stone as she rolled towards the door. I felt her life force empowering me, her very soul nourishing and reinforcing my being. This... this was the life I once had, now giving breath to my husk. I stood there, feeding for the first time in days, in awe of the feeling. If death for the dead was possible, I had just cheated it. However, my celebration was to be short lived. From below, I heard the heavy footsteps of the guards. It was time to draw my sword once more, as I had done so many times in my service to King Shigeru. The knights burst into the room, breaking down the oaken door with heavy blows, only to see the dead woman laying there and my triumphant look gloating over it. Their pole-axes spoke before they did, with a furious tongue. 'LOWLY CUR!' one screamed, plunging his weapon into the bed. His mouth was the first to be silenced as my blade cut his jaws in two, parting the head from the mandible with authority. His fountain of life-force gushed out and spilled all over the floor. Despite my continued hunger, I would not eat like a dog. For my distraction, I was awarded my first wound as the tip of a broadsword cut my cheek, opening my mouth up, literally. My own blood as well as that of the whore's dripped over my jaw, sending a shockwave over my very existence. I was not a true vampire, only one forged by the Necroprankster's magic. I would have to teach this guard and his companion about biting off more than you can chew. A lesson I taught literally as I lunged forward and returned the attack. An eye for an eye, a tooth for a tooth and a cheek for a cheek... sending my fangs through his gristly flesh and chewy face, I tore off a chunk of his visage. Never before had such a scream delighted me as he caught sight of himself in a mirror, as much a monster now as I. He took his sword and inverted the blade upon himself. Such vanity from one of Shigeru's knights. That just left one more of the pitiful cattle. One more that swung his sword. One more that was only human. One more that drew blood from me as my abdomen was pierced and one more that felt the pain reflected ten times back as I jammed my own sword into his throat and threw myself sideways, spraying his blood for all to see. At that moment, I hungered like never before for the blood. Then a curiosity happened - the blood began to flow in the air by hidden arteries, straight into my gaping, open mouth! I pondered for a moment... had my thoughts alone been enough to alter reality and take the blood from the bodies of my victims without drinking it? I gazed down at the idiot who took his own life. His long, blonde hair trickled from his helmet like the still warm blood from his cheek. I imagined that the flow was a waterfall and I was below it and sure enough, the blood no longer sunk to the floorboards but up towards me, rushing into my gaping jaw. Again, the wondrous feeling filled my body with promise and mind-shocking awe. Morgazius had clearly done well for such a random rebirth. But I knew that more guards would be on the way soon. It was time to leave and the villagers who had run from the brothel had already gathered in numbers with torches to turn this impure building into a flaming tomb. From the window, I saw my goal - the faintest outline against the dark sky of a spire in the distance against the mountains. I ploughed my sword through the glass and put one leg over the edge. I had no means of getting down without being injured so much that the villagers would have ease in dealing with me. Still, fire would consume my body no matter what. I would rather take my chances fighting the dirt that inhabited the down that be immolated. Grasping my sword, I put my other foot over the window and began my plummeting descent. I closed my eyes... and felt as though I was swimming, supported by unseen and thick liquid. Cries below me furthered my suspicion as my mind pondered why I had not hit the ground yet. 'The devil! The devil is among us in Vasserbunde!' I opened my eyes... and saw the images of many different views. It was as though I was no longer one single entity, but legion. I managed enough control to look across... and yet my vision was not so. I could not see, more feel... no, sense... the beating of wings and I could hear the high pitched squealing... was I... a bat? I was! Morgazius had gifted me in many ways, but none so incredible as this! Nevertheless, I needed to focus on the task at hand. I searched as best as I could through this unfamiliar shape for that spire that guided me to Skitzrapto's Keep. With no sense of up nor down, let alone the compass, I flew in a circle seeking this landmark. The faintest echo of a solid object returned to my ears, prompting my swift turn in that direction. I pitied the bats for their lack of appreciation for colour. To be condemned to such a bland world, such a tragedy. I recognised enough of the spire to locate myself at the entrance to Skitzrapto's keep. The echoes that returned to my ears designated a shape that I could not believe and would have to see with my own two vampire eyes. As my form came together as one again, the sight that greeted me would have sent any mortal man now fleeing. A giant skull, set in stone and carved with the precision that neither man nor nature could possess marked the door to the lair of the psychic that now rained insanity upon the land and sucked in reason. The doors to this stronghold were wide open - perhaps seeing that nobody would dare to enter unless their minds had no fear of being twisted into horrid perversions of their former selves. Then again, my mind was already far removed from what it once was. I stepped inside. Dank, echoing with the dripping of water. Inside, I heard things that must be the signs of Skitzrapto's power working... noises such as an angelic choir one moment then an instant of terrified screaming the next, mixed in with whispering and crying. I felt a tiny sample of the madness that Skitzrapto had forced upon Nosgoth. It must have been another universe inside the chaotic head that belonged to the owner of this fortress. I strode forward, up the stone staircase ahead of me. As I pressed on, I noticed two shades of light behind me. To my left, a deep green crystalline shone and on my right, ruby red shades cascaded across the floor. I decided that I would detour first to the green. Oddly enough, the light shone from a room that turned out to be the skull's left eye socket. The green glass showed a view of most of Nosgoth, as though some sort of map were encompassed within it's glass. Nothing to amuse nor hold me here any longer. I investigated the red eye socket next. What I saw was Nosgoth once more, but shattered and oozing magma from it's burned and desolate dry floor. The sky itself cracked open, vomiting lightning upon a damned valley. I had seen enough of this. This illusionary glass was wasting it's time showing a doomed Nosgoth, as many of it's inhabitants saw it that way without the aid of this trickery. I only prayed that this work was done AFTER Skitzrapto lost his rational thought. As I journeyed onwards through this maze of lies, I noticed that all the walls had some form of runic writing. I presumed these were the notes of the madman. Whether they were the findings of a great mind or the sorrowful poems of Aimziel's former lover, I could not read the ancient language on the sand coloured brick. The torches that lit the way into the fortress seemed to glow darker when you passed them, then brighter once you had gone away from them. Skitzrapto's mind tricks would not work on me. His death would mean my true resurrection... and it lay in wait for me at the end of this corridor. If the end would ever come. I had trudged onwards past these torches for what seemed to be an hour, even for me. My patience was wearing thin. 'ENOUGH TRICKS, MENTALIST!' I screamed. As I did so, my eyes closed and my gauntlet tightened around my sword with the thoughts I would live out when I found the coward. When my eyes opened, I was face to face with a door. With haste, I reached out to it, then thought again... Skitzrapto was not the kind to take the direct approach. Instead, I remembered an old phrase... and knocked the door. A giant sound came from behind as though a hefty object had been moved. As the door opened, I saw a specter like Aimziel, except it had no seeming interest in me. The room I had entered was that of a gigantic library, full of uncountable books. Strewn across the floor, the specter would pick them up and move them elsewhere, then drop them as untidily as he found them. His hooded robe hid the features of his face - probably for the better. I stuck close to the walls to avoid this supernatural entity and whatever his deranged psyche told him. Then a voice spoke from ... where was it? It came from all sides as though each and every single wall had a voice. 'GETCHO ASS OFFA MY TURF, BIOTCH, THIS IS MAH KEEP!' 'NO, IT'S MINE!' 'HEY, I OWN THIS PLACE!' 'WAIT, IS THIS WHERE I LIVE?' 'YES!!' 'OH, CARRY ON...' Such madness... Skitzrapto had been said to have inherited the wisdom of each great sage that he had descended from. Had Aimziel and he had a child, it too would have grown to be a wise man. Now the cycle was broken, those minds, corrupted and ruined would have nowhere to channel and would probably become manifest inside Skitzrapto as many instead of one. My path came to a small room annexed from the rest of the library. Chained to the wall was a girl no older than the one at the gypsy camp. Her face had been changed by magic so that she had no eyes, just smooth skin... how ghastly. Even my face, skinless as it was, was something that might occur naturally. She stood still, reeking of her un-cleaned self and only half coherent. As she opened her mouth, her teeth were re-arranged like piece of a jigsaw puzzle. I barely understood what she said. 'hhhhhee.. d-did jis... sh-ssswed ..shut hissh eyes and nouff... to f..feee alone.' she hissed. 'toush... me...' I did as she requested. The girl was a psychic. She showed me brief images of Skitzrapto. A needle and thread floated before his face, diving in and out of his skin and eyelids, closing his eyes forever to the world that had become a horror to him. The same needle plunged into his lips and tightened the thread, sealing his mouth shut that he may never speak or eat of the land. Truly, he wished to rot while taking one last prolonged stab at Nosgoth. I fell backwards, scattering a pile of books as I withdrew my hand from her head. As I stood, I heard an intolerant snarl behind me. I turned just in time to see a huge burst of flame coming at me. With no time to guess it's source, I threw myself at the ground and rolled away. As I rotated onto my back, I saw the spirit that was dropping the books earlier. My accident must have angered his... or her... or it's arrangement of the tomes. The creature picked up an open book and opened the pages. Another flame seemed to ignite directly towards me. I had no time to dodge it this time and took the full blast of it... ... ... ... but there was no heat? The scorching flames were as cool as the air around me... another mockery? The sorcerer would pay for his tricks. I swiped my sword at the false ghost, just to be sure. Perhaps these were tests of bravery, helping the travelers that came here to face their fears and not need the assistance of Skitzrapto, leaving a wiser man. Then again, maybe this twisted apparition was more of a ghost of his willingness to help, a former reminder of his past good deeds. Old habits die hard, after all. I would make sure that he who held these habits didn't follow the same path. Before I left the library, I had one more task to perform. Turning my attention to the former servant of Skitzrapto, I raised my sword above my head... and gave her peace at last. As a forced favour, she gave her blood to be in return. It carried an aftertaste that exerted nothing but bitterness. All I needed to do now was to find the exit to this labyrinth of books and walls. As if my mind was speaking out loud, a door appeared in front of me in the same manner as the spirit. How could I refuse? I had no other options and it seemed that perhaps Skitzrapto sought a confrontation. I grasped the transparent handle and pulled. Although I felt nothing in my hand, the door opened unto me. Inside, it was pitch black. With no alternative, I stepped through. Suddenly, I found myself inside what appeared to be the remains of Skitzrapto's audience chamber. The door I stepped through was no longer there. Instead, I noticed yet another spirit appearing. These ghosts and shades made me weary and... wait... this is not a wraith appearing... this is someone - teleporting! Before my eyes, I saw a set of armour like those of the Serafan Knights. It was as solid as I, except for it's wearer. Also transparent, his face was of a young man like myself, with sunken cheeks and a wave of dark brown hair flowing down his back. The reflection of my sword and armour came back at me from his. Finally, a huge spear with a hooked end like a pike materialised in his hand. There was no doubt that this was a Serafan Knight. The Serafan were an order of priests that were also trained in heavy combat skills and also had a sprinkling of magic. They were also supported by the former Circle Of Nine and instructed by them to purge Nosgoth of vampires. I knew not much of the Serafan, but they were always considered the chief enemy of vampires everywhere. I also knew that the figure smirking in the throne with an unshaven face and mutilated face was my goal. 'Skitzrapto - when I have cut down this phantom, I shall take you next!'
