It's BACON!

7 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3-07-03 RP 1 Turmoil 11th It’s a bright sunny day. Clouds are scarcely scattered over the pale blue sky. A few birds already taking flight and a few dandy lions begin to decorate the fields. March brings a taste of the spring yet to come while its winds whispers a reminder winter is still lurking. In the middle of nowhere surrounded by the security of a mountain horizon, Teresa sits on the tailgate of her four wheel drive Chevrolet. The once midnight purple step side is now camouflage. The tires are still 36 inches in height giving the pick up a nice lift. Across her lap is her shotgun and at her side is her new dog, Bacon. The Jack Russell Terrier sits happily beside her master soaking up the gorgeous day. The various shades of brown blended with white reminds Teresa of nice slab of bacon, hence the poor puppy’s name. Whether Bacon likes her name or not, we will never know. “This is the best time of the year, Bacon. It’s not too hot and not too cold. The atmosphere this time of year gives sense of new beginnings. Don’t you think ole girl?” The dog just looks at Teresa as if to say, “What you been smoking woman?” A jet plane soars high overhead. Bacon perks an ear and looks up watching the ant-sized object leave its trail of smoke. In the meantime, Teresa continues her mindless ramblings. “Just think, oceans away, people don’t have the opportunity to enjoy this peaceful serenity. Hell, there’s a war brewing and sitting here in the middle of this field, one would never know a thing. You know girl, many years ago men fought on this land so that we can enjoy it. Now our land is being threatened with those damn weapons.” Teresa pauses to take a breath and scratch her dog behind the ears. Bacon wines and lays her head on her master’s lap not quite understanding Teresa but with the serious tone of her voice, she knows it must be important. A gush of wind passes over and soon the only sounds heard are the sway of the trees. It would be easy if we all can just get along, but we can’t, so fighting are sometimes necessary, and if disarming that sonofabitch to protect what our ancestors fought to save, then so be it, right?” Bacon sits up and barks shaking her tail. Teresa pulls a tennis ball from a small bag lying in the bed of the truck. She then throws the ball and Bacon immediately jumps off the tailgate and runs after it. Shortly after she returns with the ball in her mouth. The dog drops it at Teresa’s side waiting for it to be thrown again. Teresa picks the ball up and continues yet again as another thought comes to her mind. While my brother is overseas preparing what could be a huge war, I’m home fighting my own war in PCW. I came in as a nobody. It took me months to get recognized. I found out quick how hard you have to work to get anywhere, and even yet, that sometimes isn’t enough. I’m TV champion now, probably the longest reigning TV champ too. Maybe. Come March Madness, they ain’t gonna know what madness is until I waylay Walden from one end of the ring to the other. After I and done with him, he will never want to mess with me again. Until then, I have to face a couple new rookies Tuesday, Jade and what’s-her-face, Lacuna, yeah that’s it. I admit, they have potential. Those two gals could go places in the future, with the emphasis on future. Like I said, I’ve worked hard to get where I’m at and I’ll be damned if some rookies runs me over. Ya know what I’m sayin’? Bacon, already given up on retrieving the ball again, lays her head back on Teresa’s lap. Teresa pats the dog’s head and continues to think out loud. Now Tessa, she got lucky. The only reason why she pinned me that one time is because Brandi put her two bits into the match and screwed me out of the win. It’s ok. I put her in her place real quick. Soon PCW will know who the dominant female is. Who else but THE American Woman, Teresa Tomas? Speaking of my tag match. We can’t forget my poor little spandex buddy, Rent A Hero. He tries so hard ya know. I don’t have the heart to tell him spandex looks better on females. He wants to prove we can make an excellent tag team. Hell, he even wants me to join him in his quest to rid PCW if it’s ‘evil’. We’ll see Tuesday now won’t we? By now the sun is sinking behind the mountain and twilight has begun. Teresa hops off the tailgate of her pickup waking the now sleeping Jack Russell. The sound of the truck door opening and shutting echoes off the mountains as well as the rumbling of the engine as the truck disappears into the woods.
