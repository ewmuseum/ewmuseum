Date: 12/25/02 Inside a large room, three men sit behind a large table, in the center of the room. Lights are shining against one wall, which is covered with a large white sheet. The men are wearing buisness suits, and chattering quietly. Suddenly, the door bursts open, and a big man with his shirt off busts into the room. Chains have been drawn on his body, as he screams and yells. He walks over to a chair and kicks it, injuring his foot in the process. He seems to get angrier as he walks to the wall with the sheet. He stands in front of it, screaming at first, but then starts to choke, cough, and hack. He stands up straight and screams again. Pete: PETE-STREAK! SMASH! Pete raises his fists above his head, and screams again. The wig that was on his head falls off, revealing a powdered skull cap. Pete looks embarassed, yet screams more, even angrier that his wig's fallen off. Pete: PETE-STREAK...MAD! Pete again screams and yells, and starts to walk to the table. On the way he falls down, and hits his head on the floor. His screams degenrate into cries, as the older, balding man begins to cry. Man #1: Stop. Enough. That's not going to work, damnit. Try the next one. Pete: O...ok. Pete stands up, and walks out of the room, holding his head. Man #1: Man, this is not working out. We never should have sponsored this slob. Man #2: Now, now. We just need to find him the right gimmick that's all. I mean, a fat man that talks to food? Who wants to see that? Man #3: Apparently, no one. I mean we polled the whole RUSH audience. Pete wasn't even on their radars! Man #1: Pete isn't a part of RUSH. But, those fat rednecks I guess don't want to see anyone that reminds them of themselves. We;ve got a whole list of successful gimmicks here that would be perfect for Pete. Let's just see what he can fit into. The door opens again, and Pete walks in wearing sunglasses. A wig again is on his head. Man #2 throws some music on, which is strangely reminiscent of a former PCW wrestler's. Pete walks slowly around the room. He wears a shirt that says 'Mildly Insayne Until I Perish!!' He grabs a fake microphone and starts to speak. Pete: Ladies and Gentlemen, I am MILDLY INSAYNE! IT'S TIME TO GET MILDLY INSAYNE! WHO'S WITH ME??? Pete poses a bit, shaking his giggly belly and looks around again. Pete: Xavier Kannon, I hate you! I hate you damnit! Me and My wife Jessi- Jessica? Jessica, get out here... The doors open again, and slowly, General Tso walks out, dressed like a woman. He looks angry. Tso: ...? Pete: No, it doesn't make you look fat! Get out here, and get MILDLY INSAYNE! Tso rolls his eyes and walks over to Pete. Pete puts his arms around the chinese midget and smiles. Pete: Jessica, I'm going to kill Kannon! Man #1: This has potential Pete. Put it aside, and get the other ones ready and we'll see those too. Pete: Ok. Pete again walks out of the room. The men start to discuss something about politics when Pete opens the doors. Pete: Excuse me, Mr. Redd? Mr Redd: Yes, Pete? Pete: I'm having a hard time with this one. I'm not really sure what to do with it.. Mr. Reed: Well, just try it on, and come out, Let's see what we can do with it. Pete walks out with a pair of long shorts on and a long spikey wig. He just stands there, and looks around confused. The men are flipping through their papers, and whispering about something being missing. Man #2: Oh! I know what's wrong! Here! The man reaches into a bag sitting next to him. He pulls out a basketball, and bounces it to Pete. Pete smiles, knowing what to do now. Pete: I used to play in the CBA! I R PETE! Pete dribbles the ball and shoots at an imaginary hoop. Again, the doors pop open and in walks a midget Tso, wearing a Lakers #34 jersey. He walks up to Pete and pushes him down. Pete falls rather slowly and falls flat on his back. Tso-Neil climbs up on top of Pete and covers him. Pete: I R JOBBING TO SHAQ! Mr. Redd: Pete! Enough! That's terrible. The man was a PCW Champion, two times over. Give him some credit! Pete: Sorry Mr. Redd. Here, is this better? Pete stands up and just stands there. He doesn't walk, Just kind of stands around looking around. Mr Redd and his associates look at each other and shrug. Mr. Redd: Hell, if Boda did it for 2 world titles, Pete can. Let's see what's next Pete. Pete: Mr. Redd? Mr. Redd: Yes, Pete? Pete: Can I go to the bathroom first? I really gotta pee. Mr. Redd: Sure Pete. Pete runs out of the room, leaving Tso-Neil in the room. Tso-Neil looks at the three men with narrow eyes, and they look back and nod. Tso walks out of the room. Mr. Redd: Damn midgets. (to be continued...)
