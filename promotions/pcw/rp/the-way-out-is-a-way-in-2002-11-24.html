The way out is a way in

24 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Title: The way out is a way in Featuring: Tim Walden Date: November 24, 2002 This wasn't the way that I needed to be headed emotionally, going into what might be the biggest night of my life so far on Sunday..Two situations, each with differing complexity and mental involvements, distracting me from the tasks at hand.. One, the lesser of the two troubles, my endorsement campaign continuing along with the efforts to drop a track in time for the A&W plugs..This, I could probably manage to deal with in time. It was a difficult task, but not so much so that performing it would really hurt anything outside of momentary loss of concentration, nothing to distract me by the time I got into the ring... ...I only wished that my other trouble could be that simple. I had a chance to hopefully end it right there, just shake his hand and try to be the bigger man about it...But it wasn't satisfactory. When he extended the hand toward me, I saw red... When Nathan had attacked me with that paperweight, he made a big mistake...He'd tried to come between me and my work in cowardly fashion, all because he was jealous..Did he think I would let him get away with that? I had taxed people extra for less than that! I wasn't just going to forgive and forget the fact that my co-worker's intern had tried to take me out of the picture... I might've acted a little irrationally myself in flooring him, but I had to take the situation into account..I could no longer hold trust in him, the thought that he would just try and take me out again weighed on my mind. It was justifiable for me on the superficial level, I'd had fear put into me by him, and the best way to conquer that fear was to deal with him in a manner that might show him what it's like on the other side of the coin.. Having security throw him out was probably more a matter of keeping him away from me afterward, I just didn't want to have to deal with his emotions right then, but now I realized that I probably might end up doing so anyway...Maybe this would drive him into further jealousy, the fact that she basically picked my happiness over his consciousness, in fact, it probably was going to end up doing so.. I didn't think he'd been fired, after all Brandi had only been confronted with being stuck dealing with him now, and maybe she thought that he might still come around to be sensible...It was a nice hope, and besides, one couldn't just fire her boyfriend without a little more time to think about it.... At least I was assuming that she still considered him like that for the time being. That in itself really didn't bother me, almost all love has a second chance eventually to prove itself, there were enough country songs and romantic plays to back up that opinion..His chance was going to have to wait for the time being, though, maybe after Alliances he could manage to get back together and things'd be fine, but for all I know now he might mess it all up again.. This whole situation left almost an uncomfortable awkwardness hanging over us as a tag team, at least from my perspective....I wasn't sure which way she was leaning on the issue, just had to assume that she still had him on her mind, it'd be a lot easier to work that way, at least for me...Whatever way things worked out, the match was still about beating up the other teams, not getting distracted by the out-of-the-ring antics..I had to keep thinking about the matches, and this one would be the most difficult to get the job done in, simply for the sheer number of people who wanted the belts.. There would be some cheating, undoubtedly, in fact I didn't think there was a single team in the match who didn't either make their wins through cheating or at least tend to attack opponents before the match even got underway...This wouldn't be something we could just waltz through, not unless we managed to somehow overpower the other teams through a stroke of luck, and even then they could manage to turn the tide easily with a few well-placed interferences..That might actually be the best opportunity for the team so artfully referred to as 'the bitches', being that with Saddle doing the officiating, she might be biased toward her would-be Southern belle, and this bothered me... I should think about another match, that one would probably have such a large chance element involved that even with our obvious superiority, some other team could luck out the win.. It wouldn't be so hard to face off in the ladder match from the gruelingness perspective, because at least then victory was typically a matter of who the better wrestler was one-on-one...I knew I could put the Trapezoid down..I knew I could keep him down, but I'd never had a match with him where being able to keep him down long enough to reach the prize would be the focus... This couldn't be won in the usual manner of my bouts with him, where one of us would reach victory in exceedingly cheapened fashion with a roll-up or a missed tap-out. No, when victory was attained by one of us, it would be clear to every single person watching the match who had the Cruiserweight Title..And I was determined not to let it be him. I'd taken enough abuse at his expense, he didn't even think of me as a threat when I went hold-for-hold with him, and that was his one major flaw.. The Trapezoid was growing arrogant, only used to thinking of me as an inferior with friends...It was funny how these sort of things worked, to think of it. Nathan was a pain through his overestimation of my position, while the HIT did not think enough to consider me instead of those who ranked higher on the totem pole, unable to avoid being brought back down to earth soon when I would show that not only they, but I as well could keep pace against him in the ring when need be.. Sometimes in school in my youth, I was left amazed at the inability of my fellow students to get the right answer, but now I knew where this sort of thing could stem from.. ..The teacher that couldn't get accurately estimate his own skill level. ---- View Tim Walden's Biography
