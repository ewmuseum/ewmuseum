The Superior King

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Today, March 31st, 2004 The hordes of people filled the seats long before his grand march was set to begin. They had to make sure they had a good seat for the bizzare pagent set to unfold before their very eyes. A strange audience indeed had set forth to view this gathering. A woman sat in the front of the audience, wearing a black gown with a hat drooping over her face. A stout man stood on the edge of the bleachers, sweating like a pig as he watched at the entrance of the tent, the wind forcing itself to make an appearence. The ringmaster parted through his own curtain, yelling and shouting into the microphone he gripped tightly in his hand. 'Welcome one, welcome all to the corination of the knighted ruler of this land.' The crowd cheered, using a bleek and known foreray that it was not acceptable for anyone in the country to even make a slight and cynical remark otherwise the ruler would slight his hand in the following mannerisms. To embark for his trusted officials, who despised him just as much to loot and pillage through the town and furthermore cripple the economic status of the offender in the process. Or the latter portion, which would call for your very own death at the hands of he and a trusty razor aimed to take from the achillies tendon to the temple. A sleek, and stylish way the ruler thought this would prove to be. Yet, when he made his appearence in the tent, the entire crowd was forced to approve him by his pocketed ringmaster. The crowd applauded in the sense that the ruler was in fact the man who had once said he was there to save them all, to lead them into a era of blossomed fruits and cherished images. His lie and dumping of his co-horts who originally believed this idea made it certain that no one could trust the man, no matter how powerful his rule had become over the land. His eyes peered over the men and women in their seats as he took the microphone, smirking in his traditional smile and gander. He parted his lips for a split second, when an advisor of his came from behind him and stared at his black hair from behind. The same hair which had been shorter before, sculpted out in a simple style before it had grown out and been tied in the common valor that former kings had over their countries. With a blade, the man behind the ruler raised his shoulder over the neck of the king and began to run his hand along the neck, before stopping. Nobody said anything, no words were even used to make it a spectacle. The woman in the black gown stood up however, and took her exit out of the tent, while the king struggled to breathe. The crimson ran down the man's hand as he flung his right hand to the ashy ground below, before embarking off with the dear and dead silence creeping behind him. The ruler gagged and choaked, hoping someone would come to his aid, yet no one would come. Not even his most trusted advisor whom he had waged wars with and let bloodshed upon one another. None of the sort would come now, as the blood of the mighty had fallen upon the men. Along the blade which was stuck on the podium where the ringmaster stood held a message along the ribbon strapped to the blade. 'Decido supernus regnator.' Death of the superior king.
