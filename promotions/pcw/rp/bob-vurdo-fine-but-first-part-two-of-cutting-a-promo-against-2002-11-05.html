Bob Vurdo? Fine, but first PART TWO OF CUTTING A PROMO AGAINST PLAYAZ INC!

5 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/5/02 A familiar setting... A red oriental carpet covers the floor, with two tan leather loveseats set opposite each other though facing the camera. Seated upon the right chair is the stereotypical ghetto black man: wearing blue Fubu bubble vest over a white Karl Kani shirt, Sean Jean black pants, and black Timberland boots. He's got a goatee, dark skin, shaved head with a do-rag over his head matching the vest. Wears sunglasses over his eyes inside, a band-aid over his face, chewing on a toothpick. The white man has his hair slicked back in a standard 'Geek cut.' He's wearing a tight (though not flattering his atrophied physique) white shirt, black pants, and black dress shoes. He's got black bottle-frame glasses over his face pasty white face, and... A POCKET PROTECTOR! The two men are played up with neutral Latin jazz, and the white man talks to the camera to start up the show as the music fades out. White Man:'Welcome. Neither me nor my cohost are much for introductions so let's get right down to it.' THE TOP 20 THINGS THAT SCARE BLACK PEOPLE 20) Italians Black Man:'Ya'll be in mafias and stuff. It was even issues back in the 1800's and 1900's when we was both livin' in the cities and ya'll had yo' political machines and would be holdin' the black man down to get yo' own stuff.' White Man:'Don't look at me, I'm of Anglo-Saxon descent.' Black Man:'Don't be disrespectin' my mama, foo'.' White Man: *Sighs*'Anyway, the scariest Italians around are the Sopranos, and they're fictional anyway. All Mob-affiliated Italians are.' Black Man:'Riiiiiiight....' 19) Crazy white people Black Man:'Ya'll just act crazy.' White Man:'Give me one example.' Black Man:'Jim Carrey, Ted Bundy, Charles Manson, Jeffrey Dahmer, European soccer fans, Jaime Kennedy, George W. Bush...' White Man:'I said ONE example. Can't you count?' Black Man:'I might be able to if you white people wouldn't go on lynch mobs whenever we ask for funding for inner-city schools....' White Man:'I don't lynch black people!' Black Man:'Sure, you don't...' 18) Crazy white people with guns White Man:'White people aren't crazy, so their owning guns is okay.' Black Man:'White people are the ones who went crazy as mailmen and shot everybody. White kids shoot their peers. Look, we do contract hits. Ya'll just go nuts in a crowd.' White Man:'So?' 17) Charleton Heston Black Man:'The PINNACLE of the crazy white man with a gun.' White Man:'The word you're looking for is epitome.' Black Man:'I think I got that and epitaph mixed up on the SAT's. How about some good textbooks?' White Man:'No, white people have to fund our own tax breaks. It's trickle-down-economics, so get an umbrella because we're pissing on you. Oh, and Charleton Heston isn't bad.' Black Man:'You realize that Planet of the Apes series was just an allegory for black and white relations?' White Man:'You know the word allegory?' Black Man:'Yeah, yo' wife explained it to me last night when I was tappin' dat ass.' White Man: *Produces rope for lynching*'What did you say?' Black Man:'I KNEW IT!' 16) Department Store Security White Man:'They watch everybody.' Black Man:'THEY LEAVE THE JEWELRY CASE OPEN in front of white people because they think they won't steal. Meantime I come in and try to buy some socks and a pair a'shoes and they got like 15 o' dem flippin' around followin' me like ninjas or some shit.' White Man:'Black people can't be trusted not to steal.' Black Man:'We don't steal, you plant evidence!' White Man:'Prove it.' 15) David Duke Black Man:'KKK member. Ran for governor or Congress or something. AND HE WON. And you mean to tell me white people ain't still racist?' White Man:'Not counting Louisiana, yes, but the entire country, white people included, considers LA a backwater.' Black Man:'Got a point there.' White Man:'SCORE ONE FOR WHITEY! BOOYAH! IN YO' FACE, DAWG!' Black Man:'Stop stealing ebonics! Damn, I wish white people were still afraid of that instead of trying to copy it...' 14) Survivor Black Man:'This is just Hold the black people down. You seem 'em conspiring right, like As long as we get the Darkies off the show it'll be all good.' White Man:'Everything's a conspiracy with you black people.' Black People:'Ya'll the ones sayin' you get raped by aliens or something. Like you got to be white for a legitimate conspiracy. I hope ya'll did get ass-f*cked with a hot metal pipe, ya'll deserve it.' White Man:'Now who's disturbed.' Black People:'I'm not disturbed, I'm just spiteful and angry about the whole, ya know, paying reparations to EVERY OTHER ABUSED ETHNIC GROUP IN THE COUNTRY'S HISTORY except for the descendants of African slaves. I can hear you white people. No, no, it was a good system. That whole Civil War just got in the way.' White Man:'We can't pay your reparations if you didn't own any property.' Black Man:'We couldn't own any property if ya'll wouldn't let us be citizens.' White Man:'Excuses, excuses. You could've tried to gain your independence.' Black Man:'WE TRIED! YOU SHOT US FOR IT, DUMBASS!' 13) Mike Tyson Black Man:'We ain't as scared of him as you, but he can still rip a guy's head off.' 12) Andrew W.K. White Man:'I saw that MTV special where he was fraternizing with black women. I was disappointed in him.' Black Man:'I was disappointed in the black women!' White Man:'Did he touch them?' Black Man:'No.' White Man:'Good. Wouldn't have to do any lynchings today.' Black Man:'And I wouldn't have to send Pookie and Scoopball to curb his ass.' 11)Bryant Gumbel White Man:'Huh?' Black Man:'SELLOUT! That is all.' White Man:'Wha?' 10) Hockey White Man:'WOO!' Black Man: *Shakes head*'Damn...' White Man:'Here is one sport that we can say white people truly OWN! You've got two people in the whole league and that dark skin is like a bullseye!' Black Man:'Don't you import all yo' hockey playa's from like Russia or Estonia or some European country where they got 10 last names and $5 in life savings?' White Man:'We call those countries 100% White Ghetto.' 9) Socially rejected white kids in public schools White Man:'You've got me there.' Black Man:'Eat it, bitch.' 8) Stephen Tyler White Man:'What's wrong with him?' Black Man:'What the fuh? Have you seen his mouth? YOU COULD FIT SHAQ IN THERE!' White Man:'Shaq?' Black Man:'Oh, yeah, you're afraid of basketball.' White Man:'GAH!' Black Man:'What's worse... Mick Jagger's big lips, with Gene Simmon's long tongue, Stephen Tyler's big mouth, Adam Copeland's (Edge) chin, and Peter Gallagher's freakishly large eyebrows all combined on one face would probably weigh down the entire body.' 7) Paternity Tests Black Man:'You put that on here.' White Man:'No, this was a poll of 1000's of black men.' Black Man:'Who all happen to live in the Hamptons? Right...' 6) Child Support White Man:'This goes hand-in-hand with #7. All you black men are like 30 baby-daddies or whatever you call it. Having to give up that much money a week must be terrifying. You should all invest in Norplant or at least some Depo-Provera.' Black Man:'You ain't sellin' that shit to white women, damn punk-ass eugenics...' 5) Da Man Black Man:'Keenan Ivory Wayans dealt with this in Don't Be A Menace To South Central While Drinking Your Juice in the Hood. White Man:'Wayans? Argh!' Black Man:'White people aren't afraid of Wayans...' White Man:'But I am.' Black Man:'In Living Color.' White Man:'Argh!' Black Man:'Blankman.' White Man:'Argh!!' Black Man:'Scary Movie!' White Man:'YEARGH! WOULD YOU STOP IT?!' 4) Large groups of white people. Black Man:'One of ya'll might shoot something off or shout rape or bloody murder and EVERYBODY thinks it's the black guy.' White Man:'It usually is.' Black Man:'You like that old Jewish guy on Boston Public.' White Man:'Michael Rappaport?' Black Man:'Foo'...' 3)Large groups of white-hooded white people. White Man:'There is no more KKK.' Black Man:'They hold recruiting rallies at Morehouse, dumbass...' White Man:'Well, they don't just hate you.' Black Man:'Yeah, 'cause EVERYBODY is a priority over us.' 2) White people's sports riots. Black Man:'Basketball, football, soccer. ESPECIALLY soccer. Win or lose ya'll trash the stadium and start rioting. Why don'tcha just do that BEFORE the game, get it over with?' White Man:'It's--' Black Man:'I'm gonna interrupt you and go on to the next one.' 1) Black people getting blamed for white people's sports riots. White Man:'But it's your fault!' Black Man:'Man, black people know that the police are trained and ordered to beat and arrest the black people first. That's why you white people always riot. Hey, I saw some black people back there; the police have to arrest him first so that still gives us 10 minutes per black person.' White Man:'You're racist.' Black Man:'Racism requires a system of power. You're the only one who's racist.' White Man:'We're losing the country!' Black Man:'Ain't like we winnin' anything!' White Man:'You need to get back on the field and pick my cotton!' Black Man:'You need toothpaste...'
