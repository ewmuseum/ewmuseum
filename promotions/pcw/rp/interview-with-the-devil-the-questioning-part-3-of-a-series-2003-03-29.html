Interview with the Devil: The Questioning (Part 3 of a series done in another fed, DO NOT count toward match)

29 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3-29-03 Not being able to sleep after that frightful ordeal, Shane crawls himself up into his bed, flopping his legs over the side. Shane places his hands upon his face, rubbing it thoroughly trying to regain sight before stepping down from the bed. Reaching his arm over to the desk, Shane flips on the lamp setting minimal light across the room. Finding his feet first, Shane then stands, and begins to walk groggily into the bathroom portion of the room. There isn’t much of one, considering where he is located. Shane is dressed in only his boxers, and we know see the actual build of his muscles. Most a man wouldn’t find Shane to be very intimidating because of the nature of his ways, but his build is certainly something that should be taken into consideration. Shane finds his way to the sink, knocking against the way a couple of times; he places both hands on the countertop. Standing still for several more second and releasing numerous amounts of sighs, Shane brings his hand up and flips the bathroom light on. Nearly stumbling backward, Shane keeps his balance and his eyes begin to twitch a bit as they adjusted to the now beaming light. Shane looks into the mirror, not liking what he can see one bit, as he is in dyer need of a good shave. “Was that all a dream, or did it actually happen?” Shane asks himself while staring into the mirror and finally beginning to turn the faucet of both the hot and cold on, “This is beginning to become almost unbearable for me. To think of all the problems I have had since entering this forsaken place and then to be told that it will all be fine within a couple of weeks.” Shane then reaches down into the streaming flow of water, catching a handful of it and splashing it into his face to further the waking process. “They were most definitely wrong!” Shane exclaims to himself while the water drips profusely from his face. “With time, the problems only progress for me and I don’t believe anyone can help me now.” Shane then begins to remember what he was taught so often during church service. He turns and makes his way out of the bathroom and back into the bedroom; the water still dripping from his face. Each drop hits the rugged floor of the room with a soaking splash, as Shane sits back down on his bed once more he reaches into his desk and plus out his Bible. He places the Bible upon his lap and a smile illuminates from his face. “Yes, indeed.” Shane says with very large smile upon his face, “I wasn’t mistaken to remember that it says in here that through the Lord anything can be done.” Shane then lies back on the bed and lays the Bible off to the side of him as he just smiles up at the ceiling of the room. A grimace look then comes over his face as he places his arms across his chest, “Then again, what did that dream mean, and like I asked earlier; was it even a dream?” The sight of Shane’s chest beginning to breath a little more heavily is seen, “This is so stressful that it isn’t funny. I have the answers to everything right here before me,” with that said Shane hold his Bible over his head, “Yet, the confusion surrounds me like a ever lasting cloud of disarray.” Shane lays the Bible down to his side once again and places his hands behind his head, “So many questions and so few answers. Lord, if only there was some kind of sign that you could show me.” Shane says as he continues to stare at the ceiling, “If only you could just show me the path that you want me to walk, then I would know exactly what I am here for.” Quickly sitting up in the bed, Shane throws a fist into it, “But there isn’t a sign, all I get are these equations that I can not solve. I have dreams that leave me defenseless and render me with so many queries that I am at my breaking point.” Shane then begins to shake his head and places his hands on his temples, “This is what he wants though, and I know the tricks of Satan so well, yet I fall so short of the glory of God that it isn’t funny. Clouded? Of course my thoughts are clouded and that’s the reason he was allowed to enter my dreams. I’ve fallen short of the glory, but all men will do so; this is something I have to keep in my mind so that it never happens again.” Shane stands to his feet, Bible in hand, and begins to jump, “Satan I rebuke you!” The scene freezes on the sight of Shane jumping in the air, with raised high. ~Series To Continue~
