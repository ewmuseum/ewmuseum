Date: Sometime Last week! {{{{Scene}}}}The scene opens up onto a partly cloudy day, the few clouds that are in the sky are scattered though, so the day looks to be a beautiful one. The scene is that of a highway, you can see several cars headed in one direction, all headed towards the same place, the start of the Cannonball Run. On the four lane highway the camera zooms in on one blue Dodge Viper, looks to be a the year 2001 model as well. The camera continues to zoom in as it zooms into the sun roof showing the face of Semore Smoke, you can now hear the music he has playing, none other then “Because I got high” by Afroman. In fact, Semore Smoke is singing along to the song in his normal attire of a white T-shirt and a black pair of baggy pants, and even though you can’t see them, he’s probably wearing a pair of tan Timberland boots as well… Semore Smoke: “I was gonna go to class, but then I got high… I could have cheated and I could have passed, but then I got high, and now I’m taking it next semester and I know why, hey hey! Cuz I got, cuz I got, cuz I got high… La da da da da da da da….” {{{{Smoke follows the traffic in front him, knowing who they are and where they’re going. It’s none other then the all right RUSH wrestler, Stimpo, and his partner is the former fWo diva, and current OSW diva, Tempest. Semore Smoke never liked OSW wrestlers, but Tempest was different, she can actually wrestle, unlike most of the PCW rejects in OSW. Smoke cuts the music down and starts to beep his horn as Stimpo stops suddenly in the middle of the road…}}}} Semore Smoke: Stimpo you asshole! Get that electric blue Mini Cooper S out of my fucking way! Or I’ll be forced to run over you! {{{{As if Stimpo heard him, even thought it’s unlikely that he did over all the noise. Stimpo picks up the speed and gets back with the traffic, it seems that he saw someone in front of him that scared him and forced him to hit the brakes. Smoke squints to look in front of Stimpo and then he sees about twenty yards in front of Stimpo, is one of the scariest cars he’s ever seen. On the back of it there is a huge face looking back at you, coming from the face are huge sharp fangs, and the car itself would scare the pants off of Darth Vader if it had a chance….}}}} Semore Smoke: Well, that has to be OSW’s dumb asses, of course PCW rejects, Minion The Imp and Hessian. Move that busted ass vehicle! {{{{Smoke can’t take it anymore. He pulls his ashtray open and pulls a blunt out from inside it. Why man? Cuz I got high, cuz I got high, cuz I got high… La da da da da da da da…” {{{{The lighter pops and Smoke pulls it out and lights his blunt with it. He then places it back on the dash after shaking it a few times to make sure it has cooled down. He then hits his right turn signal to get over for the exit off the highway. Semore looks around himself and his car as he merges into the huge line going off the exit. But the traffic is going at a steady speed of about sixty miles per hour in a seventy mile-per-hour speed zone. Smoke looks down in his passenger and looks at the map that was there the whole time. Sitting there is a huge circle around where the exit would be…}}}} Semore Smoke: Ok, our starting spot should be right around the corner from here. In fact, I can see it! {{{{Smoke has a thing with talking to himself, that way he can remember things. He takes a drag off the blunt and then opens up his car, making sure he grabs his keys. He hits the lock as he steps out and then closes the door behind himself. End: I could smell the reefer all the way over here. Semore Smoke: Oh! End, can I talk to you for a minute? End: Sure! {{{{Semore Smoke walks away from the group and The End follows him. They walk to Smoke’s car, although Smoke is leading him to the car. Smoke stops him at the car and Semore sticks the key into the door and unlocks the door.
