Enter: Dominion Part II

2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3-5-03 (Quick Notes: The last role I posted had that weird left column thing goin' on and if this one does too I apoligize. I'm on an iBook and it tends to get weird with forms and such. Also, I probably don't have to tell you guys, but this role jumps around from past to present. Just so you guys have a heads up beforehand. Thanks.) “Excuse me, can ya spare a dollar?” [ The woman wrapped her arms around her body, cuddling her coat tighter in an effort to block out just a little bit more of the cold air that surrounded her. She turned her body just a little, doing her best to make it seem as if she didn’t even hear the voice of the man standing a few feet away. ] “Ma’am…” The voice called out. “Anything, even some change.” [ Again, the woman didn’t reply. She stood outside the lobby area of Minneapolis St. Paul International Airport with nothing more than a single piece of luggage. The man, now slowly approaching her, wasn’t too hard to recognize. He was dressed in dirty threads with a gray hood covering the upper portion of his head. Obviously down on his luck, the man reached out and tugged on the arm of the woman he’d focused on. ] “Please…” The man begged. “Spare an old man some change.” [ Without instance, the woman reeled back and punched the man in the face; almost as if by instinct. Just as soon as he’d grabbed her arm he’d been knocked back. Gripping his jaw, the man regained his footing. ] “You’re crazy!” The man shouted, bracing himself against the concrete wall nearby. “Keep your money!” [ The woman merely turned away again, not sharing another look at the man, who just sulked off into the morning fog. The woman shook her head in annoyance, brushing her arm off. She was obviously appalled by the fact that someone of the stature of this man would come into contact with her. She believed he deserved just what he got. ] ********** [ Evelyn’s head fell back onto the cushion directly behind it. She closed her eyes, taking a deep breath before exhaling and getting the first real moment of true rest she’d had the chance to get all day. Normally, she’d be at the office today, as it was a workday. However, she’d taken the day off today because she was expecting the delivery of the vast majority of her deceased father’s possessions. A man that she’d never had the pleasure of knowing, upon finding out who her father was, and the fact that he was deceased, set the wheels in motion. She was his firstborn child by paternity and although she was forced to fight in court, she was granted the right to own his possessions. ] [ Eve didn’t fight out of greed. More so for the fact that she was eager to find out more about her past. Where she’d come from. Who she was. What kind of family she’d possibly have out in the world. And now she had her chance. All of her father’s pictures, papers, documents and so forth now belonged to her. ] [ Just as soon as the moving men left, Eve found herself eagerly digging through the mountain of boxes they’d left in her living room. While it was indeed a mess, she didn’t care as there were more pressing matters at the moment. She’d been reading through some of her father’s documents for quite a while now. Digging back into the closest box, she pulled out a thick document and wiped it’s exterior. Upon realizing what it was, she covered the bottom part of her face with her free hand. It was a birth certificate. And not her own. ] ********** [ Rubbing her eyes, Eve leaned back against the wall behind her. Her ride still hadn’t arrived. It was rather late, actually. She didn’t care, though. Today was a big day for her. She’d be meeting her half brother for only the second time. Sure, she’d been in contact with him via the telephone pretty regularly for the past few months, but to be in person and have a face to the voice meant so much more to her. Her ride would show up eventually. While she waited, she couldn’t help but remember the events that led up to this day. ] ********** [ Having found out that her father had a son a mere year after he had her, Eve found herself obsessed with finding him. So much so that even at work, she would dig through the internet researching her family tree for any bit of clue she could find. She’d even hired a private detective to dig for her. ] “Hello?” Eve said, picking the telephone up and pressing it to her ear. “You’ve for him??” [ She paused for a moment, unable to fight the smile the formed on her face. The detective had found her brother. He’d gotten her the works, too. Name, address, parents, etc. Everything seemed to match. Holding the phone to her ear with her shoulder, Eve eagerly grabbed a pen and began scribbling down the information she was being given. ] “Eric Wolfson… Florida, United States…” Eve began, repeating the information to make sure she wasn’t getting it wrong. “Telephone number…” [ Hanging the phone up, Eve remained quiet just staring down at the notes she’d just scribbled down on the paper in front of her. While her stomach now felt like the Super Bowl was being played inside it, she mustered up the courage to pick up the phone again and dial the number she’d just been given. ] “Hello?” Answered the deep voice on the other end. [ Eve paused, finding herself at a loss for words. ] “Hello??” The voice repeated, this time with a hint of annoyance. “Mr. Wolfson?” Eve inquired. “Yeeees…” The voice answered. “Listen, if you’re trying to sell something, screw off! If not, state your friggin’ business, dig?” [ Eve smiled, finding the man’s response a little funny. ] “Mr. Wolfson.” Eve continued. “I know this is probably going to sound really weird, but your father’s name was Joseph Desecourt, correct? I mean, your biological father.” [ A brief pause of silence. ] “What’s this about?” The man on the other end asked. “Who is this?” [ Eve breathed a quick sigh of relief. ] “I hope you’re sitting.” Eve replied. “My name is Eve Desecourt.” ********** [ Eve reached down and pulled her suitcase a little closer. After standing upright again, she chuckled to herself a bit. As people all around her made their way to and from their destinations, Eve found herself comparing their faces to that of the face of her brother from the pictures he’d sent her. Perhaps she was just really eager to begin her new life. ] ********** “You sure you can handle a world like that?” Wolfson asked, pulling his long blonde hair back and securing it with a tie. “I mean, it can get pretty dangerous. Especially for a woman.” [ Eve just tilted her head, narrowing her eyes. ] “Give me some credit, Eric.” Eve requested. “I told you all about my life growing up. Trust me, I can handle myself.” [ Wolfson shrugged, agreeing. ] “Ok.” Wolfson replied. “Just thought I’d throw it out there.” [ Eve nodded. While she didn’t want to come off as insane, she couldn’t help but keep her eyes focused on the man sitting on the opposite side of the table. She’d talked to him several times on the telephone over the past few months but today was the first time ever that she’d actually seen him. Just knowing that he was her brother intrigued her as to who he was. Not only that, but the two had decided to join forces and combine their professions. While Wolfson was a career fighter with a very marketable reputation, Eve was in the business of marketing. ] “So tell me about this Dominion thing.” Eve said, pouring herself a glass of soda from the pitcher on the table. “What’s the significance of such a moniker?” [ Wolfson grinned, reminding himself of the past he’d had with said name. ] “It’s sorta like the name I’ve given my darker side.” Wolfson admits. “Back when I thought I lost my wife, I kinda lost my mind and found myself fighting underground in Japan to mask the pain I was feeling. At that time, I was a very violent man and that just wasn’t Eric Wolfson. That was someone completely different. And this person needed a name. That brings us to today. In my business, you have two options. You can, one, be the hero and answer to every call and beckon the masses make. With that comes a lot of baggage, though. If I did that now, while I’m just preparing to enter a new promotion, I’d have to go out there every night and say something witty in an effort to win over the people before getting myself shots. The other option, the one I’ve opted to accept, is like night in comparison to day, being the first option. I can walk into this promotion and look out for number one and number one alone. Just walk in there, kick all the ass I can kick and not look back. But to do that, I’d need to dig deep within myself and find that darker side again. Bring it out, y’know?” [ Eve nodded, leaning forward. She cupped her hands, resting her chin on them. ] “Well…” Eve started, considering what she’d just heard. “It’s not unmarketable. I mean, if you really wanted to milk this thing for all the money it’s worth, being the hero would be the better choice but trust me, I can get you some merchandising options from this Dominion thing.” [ Wolfson nodded, accepting the response. ] “Sounds good.” Wolfson replied. “And you’re ok with your role in the business?” [ Eve opened her hands and held them out a little. ] “Remember, Eric.” Eve challenged. “I work in marketing. I know how to play a role. I can talk the talk when I need to and if it comes down to getting physical with these big boys, I don’t have a problem with that either. Trust me, anyone gets in my face I’ll slap the taste out their mouth.” [ Wolfson smiled, amazed by how alike the two were. While they only shared a father, it was obvious their father was the dominant gene in the people they’d become. ] “Ok then, partner.” Wolfson replied, holding his hand out across the table. “Put ‘er there.” [ Eve reached out and shook her brother’s hand. The two would take the world of professional wrestling by storm. ] ********** “Hey lady… You want a ride?” [ Eve was so deep in thought that she hadn’t even noticed the dark red Mustang pull up in front of her. Eric Wolfson stepped out of the car and hugged her before picking her suitcase up. ] “What the…” Eve started, watching her brother walk around toward the back of the vehicle. “Wait a second…” [ Popping the trunk open, Wolfson set the suitcase inside. Sighing, he rolled his eyes. ] “What is it with everyone?” Wolfson asked. “Does no one change their hair these days?” [ Eve shrugged, giggling as she pulled the passenger side door open. ] “It’s just all the pictures…” Eve replied. “Plus when I saw you last month, you had all that blonde hair. Especially in the pictures. What happened to my hippy brother?” [ Wolfson started the vehicle, laughing sarcastically. ] “Gotta look the part.” Wolfson replied. “Already a few people in PCW think I’m an asshole. And while yes, this is true, my new look solidifies that. Heh.” [ Eve looked on at her brother sitting beside her as the vehicle began to move. ] “Let’s get down to business.” Eve said, getting serious. “What’s the target this week?” [ Staring forward at the road in front of him, Wolfson reached over with his free hand and opened the glove box. ] “Dorn Tracer.” Wolfson answered. “Take a look at that picture. He’s the current Rising Star Champion. Well, Shooting Star Champion, I suppose. But I’ll explain all of that later.” [ Taking the picture in her hand, Eve observed it for a moment. ] “And there’s two girls going for the title at the same time?” She asked. “Four-way thing, eh?” [ Wolfson smiled, chuckling to himself. ] “Yeah, I still can’t get over that.” Wolfson replies. “I’ve got to roll around with a couple of chicks this week. But Larry’s drilled it into my head pretty good, so I’ve got to take them seriously. I mean, if one of those girls pinned me… I mean, say I took them super lightly and they rolled me up real quick. Man, I’d never live that down. But yeah, it’s called a four corners match.” [ The vehicles becomes silent, prompting Wolfson to glance over at his sister. She’s still looking the photograph over. ] “What?” Wolfson asks. “You have a problem remembering faces or somethin’?” [ Shaking her head, Eve smiled lightly. ] “I dunno…” Eve whispered. “He’s kinda hot.” [ Wolfson quickly shot a look at his sister. ] “What??” Wolfson retorted. “Tracer? He’s the enemy, Eve! Bad guy. Very bad guy! So just get it out of your head, dig?” [ Eve broke out into laughter. She tucked the photo back into the glove compartment and closed it. ] “I was just sayin’.” Eve replied, giggling. “I believe you that he’s strange and all, but that doesn’t mean the guy isn’t good looking. Ok, next subject…” [ Wolfson snorted, steeping on the accelerator. ] “Whoa…” Eve whispered, grabbing a hold of the arm of the seat. “You wanna take it easy on the driving?” “No time.” Wolfson replied. “We’ve got a lot of business to take care of this week and little time to do it.” [ Eve leaned deep into her seat, staring forward through the windshield in front of her. It was just the second meeting with her brother and already she’d learned he was a lead foot. ] To be continued…
