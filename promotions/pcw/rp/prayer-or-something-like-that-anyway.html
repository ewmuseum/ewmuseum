Prayer…or something like that, anyway.

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Recently Trying to ignore recent events involving his greatest nemesis yet, Clyde, and looking forth into his shot at the March Madness main event, Titu Xansiv looked up into the cold, stone eyes of Ra. The statue was immense, a marble pinnacle among the pillars holding up the roof. The catacombs beneath the mansion were more than ominous; Titu had personally made sure of it, and as the gentle flames of the torches flickered briefly, he wondered if making the sanctuary this portentous had been such a brilliant idea. As usual, two of the Palace’s finest guards had located themselves outside the great brass doors of the shrine-room and kept a vigilant watch over the winding corridors of sandstone ahead. They too felt the threatening atmosphere consume them - the Gods were powerful beings after all, and powers of unearthly element lingered in the catacomb's bowls. ‘Oh great and powerful Ra, divine creator of all I live for, hear my plea…’ The statue remained unmoved, its icy visage staring down at its only remaining follower. Pharaoh looked up, a gleam of hope in his eye and then he sighed. Damn… ‘Oh, c’mon, Ra, this concerns you! I need some advise.’ His voice was filled with curious uncertainty, as well as a touch of hopeless implore. He stared up at the ancient Egyptian deity and grimaced. ‘Ok,’ he sighed, rolling his shoulders enthusiastically, ‘I’ll try again then, shall I?’ He took a deep breath, frowned a little and went on. ‘Oh great and powerful Ra! My master, my ruler, my inspiration… your divine speaker requests your attention! Matters of great importance call upon you, Oh great lord!’ There was a quaint rumble in the temple’s foundation before a faint grey light erupted before Titu. The Pharaoh took a step back, watching inquisitively as the shape fragmented and then, from it, was formed the silvery figure of a man, thin limbed and elegant in appearance. ‘Who dares to call upon the great Ra of the Egyptian Gods?!’ he scoffed, before recognizing the man in front of him. ‘Oh, Titu… it’s you.’ Ra’s stance loosened, the furious bright light surrounding him deteriorating, leaving the frame of an aged man, still striking and royal, but not as much so as he appeared a few moments ago. ‘You should have guessed it was going to be me, Ra; I mean, I am you only remaining follower.’ Xansiv said, an edge of cynicism present about his voice. 'Well, to be perfectly honest, Titu, I did know it was you. I just have to do the whole 'presence' thing, you know? In case a new follower emerges from the abyss that is the earth, you understand?' He clapped his hands together and grinned. 'Speaking of which, how is your campaign to bring about a new era in our religion going? I do hope you've convinced a few mortals of our power and ultimate superiority!' Pharaoh's face twisted, 'Not too great, frankly. You see, not everyone believes what I preach. They say I'm talking nonsense!' He chuckled for a moment, 'I mean, the time those guys from the asylum came around to talk to me about my sanity was terrible. Ha! Looking back now, it was quite amusing…how the Duat could anyone question my grasp on reality for the sake of Horus?!’ Ra shook his head, taking a seat on the foot of his own statue. 'You really do need t get your act together, Xansiv. We're relying on you!' Pharaoh shrugged, 'I know, I know…but other things have stalled my progress. My wrestling for one thing!' Ra waved a hand, frivolously. 'That damned wrestling takes up too much of your time - time which, I'll have you know, you could be spending sending forth our great Egyptian gospel!' He sighed mournfully. 'And I hear Isiy has had a change in her attitude too.' Pharaoh hung his head, clearly embarrassed by his wife's latest attitude adjustment. 'As I've said time and time again, my lord, I can't change the way she thinks. She's of her own freewill to do as she wishes.' He paused, 'Although I'm not at all happy about her abandoning her precious faith, believe me!' Ra peered about himself, sighing. 'This chamber is quite poor, Titu. You could have made it a bit bigger.' 'Actually I couldn't. If I had made it any bigger we'd probably have drilled straight through next-door’s basement, and that's a strange place indeed. Much stranger than here!' 'Why? What's so strange about it?' Ra seemed curious; the tint of impendent interest in his voice was more than enough to suggest it. Pharaoh leaned up against the wall, waving his hands this way and that as though attempting to bring some sort of image before the deity. Of course Ra was a God, and therefore didn't really need his follower to describe his neighbours. He could, if he wanted, know everything about them, but seeing Pharaoh explain things was always interesting – he really was such a character. ‘Well, they’re bloody Christians aren’t they?’ sighed the Egyptian wrestler, rolling his eyes. ‘They portray us as pagans! It’s really beginning to annoy me! The damned nerve of it all!’ Ra chuckled, ‘Well, the modern world will have its quaint fancies, won’t it? Why, I remember years ago, couple of thousand to be exact, when Christianity wasn’t even believed. That’s when the world was only just beginning to accept it as something worth its while.’ Titu sighed, shaking his head to and fro. ‘It still annoys me! I mean, right now in PCW we have so many religiously tolerated wrestlers its hard to keep track!’ ‘Yes, I’ve heard of these new followers. Dorn Tracer and Violence Jack; they follow that damned octopus god, don’t they?’ Pharaoh shrugged. ‘Bruce does, but I’m not sure what the Duat Dorn follows. Anyway, the real reason I asked you to come before me today involves an up-and-coming match of mine. I have to face a fellow named Bob Vurdo, an Italian of some sort, you know the kind…the mobster sort—’ Ra let out a hoarse laugh, cutting Titu off in mid-sentence. ‘And you were worrying about him?! By my own name, Titu! You do realize we follow your progress in PCW don’t you? You do realize this Vurdo person is absolutely nothing in comparison to your skills in the ring, don’t you? You do realize that after a few punches and a suplex here and there he’ll have nothing on you…right?’ Titu cocked an eyebrow. ‘Well, I guess. But this match…it’s for a chance to get into the March Madness match itself! A lot’s on the line, my Godly lord!’ Ra smiled cynically. ‘Oh get over it! You’ll beat this Vurdo guy, I promise you! You’re a representative of the Gods, my friend; you can’t lose!’ The God let out another chuckle before evaporating into the air. So abrupt was the action that Pharaoh leapt back in astonishment, his eyes wide and unprepared for the event and picking himself back up off the dusty ground, he wiped himself down and scowled and muttered: ‘Thanks! I had so much more to talk about, you know?!’ Huffing, Pharaoh left the chamber, silence returning to the quickly darkening shrine…
