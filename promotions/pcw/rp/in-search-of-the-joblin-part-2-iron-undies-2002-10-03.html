In Search of the Joblin...Part 2...'Iron Undies'

3 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10-3-2002 The scene opens with a shot through a window where stands the Lord King, Venneman Pendragon. He is shirtless, and the moonlight illumines his massive chest. His eyes have a faraway look to them as if he is contemplating events that have no bearing to where he is, and what he is about, yet his small frown makes us curious about what’s on his mind… “’Tis a fair blessing that the blasted camera does not roll all the time during my adventures. There are always some things that I would rather keep to myself…Private.” Unfortunately for Venneman, the scene switches to show Jenna sprawled on a massive bed, covered only by a thin sheet that accentuates her luscious curves. Suddenly a door opens and Venneman swaggers in, naked except for a chain-mail pair of undies set off by a golden “codpiece”. Jenna starts to laugh at the sight… Venneman: Thou wouldst laugh at the Lord King’s under garments? One can never be too careful with one’s jewels. I like to keep mine protected from thievery… Jenna: Well, there is surely nobody getting in there without your permission, sire. Venneman: Aye…But thou hast permission, my pretty lass. Thou hast permission… Venneman walks over to the bed and turns down the lamp to a dull glow. Only as he removes the “drawers” does the camera turn away. The sound keeps rolling though, and the bed springs creak as Pendragon climbs in… Jenna: My lord, what is THAT! Venneman: ‘Tis King’s Iron, my lass… Jenna: But, why is it flaccid? Venneman: Why, ‘tis before the forge, my lass… With that, the sound cuts out and the shot switches back to show Pendragon through the window. He smokes a pipe as he peers out into the night… “Aye, ‘tis a very fortunate thing that the camera was not there last night. I need no ridicule for mine apparel selection. One can never be too careful. Jenna is a fine lass, and she served her purpose well; served her Lord King well…Again, and again and again. But I cannot become bogged down in the affairs of commoners. I have important business, and thanks to my new friend Kona, I know that that business lies to the North, in a rat hole the name of Domfilla.” Pendragon absently begins to blow smoke rings… “Lyco the Cruel…Syko the Goblin. They are the same. Though they may not know one another, they are the same. What happens to one befalls the other. This much I know. Here, in the land, I was at an advantage. Lyco would know not of my coming. What fool would return to a land that has given him naught but evil and ill? What fool would chance the return to a hostile world where evil would see good men trampled ‘neath a tide of darkness? There were no heroes left in the world…” The smoke stops and Pendragon frowns at the pipe. He disappears from the window and returns a moment later with a small pouch from which he draws some “smoke” and places it in the pipe. A strike of a match and he smokes once more, the rings coming faster now… “This Land has fine tobacco, though perhaps not up to Semore’s liking. That, though, is not my affair…Heroes. ‘Tis true; in THIS world, perhaps the hero is extinct. Perhaps here there are no strong men to throw off the yoke of slavery imposed by the evil men who would dictate harsh rules and ridiculous taxes. Aye, perhaps the hero was dead here. But, I am not from this world. I am from a land where heroes are still born every day. A land where heroes still strive for peace and for glory both. In my world, it takes more than a crazy ruler to break a man’s spirit. In my world, I am a hero. In this world, I am the only hero the people have…“ “Lyco and Syko. Two heads of the same dime. Both men, er, boys, er, whatever….Both creatures driven by hate to bring destruction upon the hearty peoples of two lands. Lyco here in this world, and Syko in PCW…My world. Both men whose hearts are ruled by deceit and lies; treachery and fear. Aye, fear is the biggest motivator of these two creatures. Fear of those greater than they; fear…Of me. Though I hate to leave Jenna in this way, I really must be off. I needed to seek out the barbarian. His would be a strong sword to have along…” With that, Venneman tamps out his pipe and disappears from the window. The shot switches to show Pendragon emerging from his room, a cloak thrown over his steel mail armor and leather pants. He walks purposefully down the hall and descends the stairs into the common room. He looks around and sighs, for in the corner lies the barbarian, passed out from too much of the wine. He walks over and picks him up… Venneman: Like it or not, Kona…Thou art coming with me. We have great things to accomplish, thou and I. Kona belches as Pendragon hoists him onto one massive shoulder. He looks around before carrying the barbarian from the inn and into the night. The shot switches to the front of the inn and follows Pendragon as he makes his way to what is obviously the stable…A sign above the huge double doors reads “STABLE”. Pendragon enters and soon emerges, riding the great white war horse, Xerxes. He also holds the reins of another horse, a thick chested black stallion. Kona is strapped to the saddle, legs on one side, head upon the other. Pendragon gives Xerxes a small kick and the two horses both spring into motion. He canters down the main street and passes from the town beneath the huge arched gateway. The guard pays the group no mind, as he is also passed out from excessive drink. Pendragon looks upon the sleeping guard with apparent disdain as the group rides forth… “A man of uniform should be alert at his post. What id marauders were to ride into the town? Where would the city watch be, without a gate guard to suppress such a group’s travel? Dead in their beds, as they probably sleep the sleep of the slovenly as well. I guess that people shall get what they deserve, whatever it may be. There is no one who can change that. Destiny awaits us all, I suppose…And yet, I believe that we all have a part to determine in our own destiny. Red October approaches, and I have one more cance to make my destiny what I wish it to be.” “In PCW, I believe my destiny shall be to rule the federation with a fair and just hand. It starts with the reclaiming of my lost Television gold. Here in the land, I believe my destiny is different, but the same in a way. I am here to liberate the free peoples and make them see that tyranny is not the best form of rule. These are the same for one reason; this Land mirrors PCW, and in turn, PCW mirrors this land. The effect would be much like casting a stone into a pond. Toss it in the middle, and the ripples reach the furthest shore, however they may diminish. What I do hear is much like that. I am the stone, and the ripples of my splash are felt all the way back in my world, in PCW.” The two horses gallop into the night, Pendragon sullenly looking towards the horizon, Kona passed out, strapped to his mount. Pendragon casts a glance at him and shakes his head, a smile coming to his lips… “’Twas hard to tell how the barbarian would feel about this ride. I am sure a headache is in his future, and, after all, he did say that he would accompany me. Be an extra sword along the way. I wondered if our ways would conflict…He and his barbarian code, myself and my rules of chivalry. I know not. All I know is that if he possesses the heart I believe he does, we shall be a force of light in an otherwise dark and dank world of despair. And destiny awaits those with the strength of heart to persevere through the desperate times of the Land…” >>>TO BE CONTINUED
