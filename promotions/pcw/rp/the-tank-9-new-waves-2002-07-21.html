The Tank 9 - New Waves

21 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: July 21, 2002 Birds with clipped wings sing songsof every beauty they can no longer reach... Misery comes quietlywith blades of excess,slashing relentlessly until bloody wings dry on the floorand the only freedom leftis the song. ~Leigh Donovan *** Saturday, July 20 - Day 15 It didn’t take long to find out who Boris Valinov’s murderer was. Two pairs of his shoes were found under Nam’s bed, among the war hero’s collection of footwear. Now, all of Unit B watches as a trio of C.O’s rummage through his personal belongings in search of a murder weapon. “Think they’ll be able pin it on him?” Harvey Blake asks his companion, a young skin-head named Derek. “Not if they can’t find the blade,” responds Derek. “See what you can do about that,” Blake says, turning to his young companion as they stare down across the cell balcony at Barney Bishop’s - Nam’s - cell. “He’s a good man. Crazy as fuck, but he fought in Vietnam. Look out for him.” Derek nods. Some ways away, Doug Hattoni leans on the barred gate of his cell. He returned from Bruno’s funeral yesterday and is now determined to get back to business without the man who had been so loyal to him for so many years. “Shit,” he says to Jason Snow, who’s standing next to him. “Can you believe that fucking nut did in Boris? Not like I care, mind you, I thought you killed him anyway.” Snow is silent. His mind, however, can’t stop replaying the scene by the Television that occurred earlier in the week - Nam told him he’d do any favour Snow needed. He must have seen Snow go into Boris’ cell, followed, and finished the job. “Can’t prove shit on him though,” snickers Doug. “Not without the blade. They’ll just put him in the hole for a couple of weeks for stealing the old bastard’s boots, I would think.” *** From the Office of the Warden “Everyone in the Tank knows that Barney Bishop killed Boris Valinov,” starts the Warden while leaning against his desk, “but without proof, we can’t do shit about it. We can stick him with petty theft, but there’s no real point of pressing charges for that, now is there? Not when the man’s already going to die in these walls. Instead, we stick him in the hole for a few weeks and then watch the sick bastard walk out and return to normal life... that is... if one can consider life in the Tank normal...” *** “My boots!” screams Nam as a pair of C.O’s uncuff him and hurl him into the dirty, empty, cell that the prisoners refer to as ‘the hole’. “I need my f-f-fucking boots!” The fifty-something war hero falls flat on his face, naked, and cries like a baby. Ever since he nearly froze his feet some thirty years ago, he’s been obsessed with footwear - all part of lunacy. “Fucking nutcase,” sneers one of the C.O’s as he pulls the heavy steel door closed and locks it. Alone inside the dimly lit dirty cell, Nam screams like an animal, crying relentlessly on deaf ears. “No!” he shrieks as if being tortured. “My boots!” *** From the Office of the Warden “I often wonder just who does run this prison,” the Warden sighs as he turns toward his window. “I mean, technically, I run the place. But really, the inmates are running things - lets cut through the bullshit.” “If someone comes to me and tells me another inmate physically harmed them, I would discipline the attacker. But I don’t have the power to keep that attacker secluded from everyone else for the rest of his life, so sooner or later, he or one of his cronies are going to do much worse to the inmate who came to me in the first place. It’s a fucking school yard.” *** Sunday, July 21 - Day 16 Cautiously, Miggy steps through the barred gate back into Unit B. It’s been over a week since he’s been here - not since his brutal beating at the hands of Harvey Blake and his Aryans. Now, as he returns, he can’t help but be overcome by fear. The first thing he notices are all the shaved heads and all the Nazi tattoos - things that he’s always been surrounded by, but he’s never been so terrified of them until now. At over sixty years of age, there’s very little that Miggy can do physically in his own defence, and he’s well aware of that. He also knows that he has very little value to the Latinos or the Italians, the only groups in the Tank that might take him in, so he’s basically left to fend for himself within the confines of these horrid walls. “Miggy!” a familiar voice startles the old man. “You’re back!” Miggy turns around and finds himself staring into the lone evil eye of his worst fear - Harvey Blake. Overcome by fear, the little Latino turns his back to the one eyed Nazi and hurries up the stairs and soon disappears into his cell. “Come on, spick!” Blake yells after him, chuckling, “Can’t we just let bygones be bygones?” The pair of skinheads beside him can’t help but laugh. The entire idea of Harvey Blake terrifies Miggy. What makes Harvey Blake’s persona even more disturbing is the fact that he’s not just some immature punk kid - he’s a grown man well into his forties... and a very powerful man at that. “Hey Harv,” a voice booms out from behind the one eyed Nazi - a familiar voice, but one that doesn’t belong to either of his companions. He turns around to see that standing just behind the pair of skinheads, is none other than Jason Snow. “I think we should have a little talk.” “Well, well,” Blake chuckles, “look what we have here. What do I owe the honour? Or should I say... who?” With his one blue eye, Harvey spots Doug Hattoni watching from the shadows of his cell just above. “I know what you did to Miggy,” Snow says quietly, stroking his beard as he shoulders his way past Harvey Blake’s companions. Suddenly, Blake finds himself nose to nose with the young lion. Since joining forces with Doug Hattoni, Snow has had a much more dangerous aura about him, fully living up to his lion-esque physical appearance. “Oh yeah?” Blake asks with a rebellious smirk, secretly hoping his two dumfounded companions will step in. Secretly, Harvey Blake fears the young lion breathing heavily in his face, bearing his fangs from within that ever-thickening golden beard. “Well, I guess the question is now - what are you going to do about it?” Snow laughs in Blake’s face, “What am I going to do about Miggy? Nothing,” he chuckles. “Well, officially, I’m not going to do anything about Miggy.” The smile suddenly disappears from Snow’s hairy face. “But that’s not your problem, Blake. Your problem is that Mr. Hattoni knows what you did to Bruno Gasperoni... and he’s not too happy about it.” Snow can see all of the color flush out of Harvey Blake’s face. He knew Hattoni wouldn’t hesitate to point the finger at him, but the thought of dealing with Hattoni’s Italian gang and Jason Snow terrifies him - despite all of his efforts, the aging Nazi can’t quite conceal that horror. “So, Mr. Harvey Blake,” Snow says, the grim smile reappearing on his handsome young face, “i guess in the end it doesn’t matter if I get revenge for Miggy, or Mr. Hattoni gets his revenge for Bruno through me... the result will be the same.” Snow winks at Blake (the aging Nazi realizes the wink is subtle ridicule about his one eye) and walks away, not even giving the Nazis what respect it would take to look over his shoulder, just to make sure they weren’t planning to attack him behind his back - so cocky, Jason Snow. When Snow’s out of ear shot, Blake’s companions finally move in. “Who does he fucking think he is?” one of them asks. Most of you know him as the famed Australian lead singer of one of the biggest bands in North America - Cataract.” “Like most rockstars, he came in on a drug charge,” sighs the Warden. “And just like with Jason Snow, as soon as he comes in, every fucking reporter in California is trying to hop the walls to get an interview with someone who’s in contact with him. Just when the Jason Snow hype was dying, this shit starts... how the hell can I run a decent prison with all of this hooplah?” *** Leigh Donovan sits quietly in his cell, staring down at a blank piece of paper. He can’t think of anything to write at the moment, as tears stream down his pale face. He would never let the Correctional Officers see him like this. When they brought him to his cell, no more than an hour ago, he would only let them see a cocky exterior. He tried to act like prison meant nothing to him. Now, as he sits alone in his cell trying to write, he can only hope that his cellmate doesn’t show up and see him like this - what a first impression that would be; a heroin addict rockstar, sitting alone on his bunk, crying his eyes out. “Bastards,” he whispers in a shaky voice as he thinks about the C.O’s refusing to let him bring his guitar into Unit B. A guitar always seems to soothe him when words are hard to come by. But he’s left with nothing but a few sheets of paper and a pencil - the Unit Manager, Geoffery Rankin, told him he is lucky to even get this much. “Hi roomie!” says a low, booming voice - unbelievably low. Leigh turns his head and is horrified by the sight of the massive leader of the Thugs, Jeremy Doris - J-Thug, the most feared black man in the Tank, and probably the most dangerous of anyone. “What the fuck you crying for, motherfucker?” “I’m not crying,” Leigh says in his firmest of tones. His thick Australian accent makes J-Thug chuckle a bit. “Oh no?” J-Thug says in his thick low voice. “What the fuck’s all that water on your face then?”J-Thug points a thick finger at Leigh’s tears. “What do you care?” Leigh sneers, trying to act tough and turning his head away from the massive man. He knows that showing weakness is like negotiating a sentence to death. What he doesn’t know, is when you’re sharing a room with J-Thug, your life is in his hands no matter how tough you act. “Hey,” J-Thug says in calming tones, “I know what you’re going through, man. You ain’t used to this shit. You’ve got money, you’ve got fame. Well, I never had either of those on the outside, and it still was hard to get used to. But you know what? I survived. And so will you.” Leigh turns back toward J-Thug. He can’t believe that this monster of a man is showing such empathy. He sets his pencil and paper down on the bunk and wipes the tears from his cheeks. “I just can’t believe they put me here. I’ve never been in prison before - I’ve never even been arrested! Now I’m in the Tank - one of the most dangerous prison’s in the country.” “It is,” J-Thug nods. “But you’re going to do fine. Trust me. I’ll see to it that you walk out of here when you’re time is up - and you won’t have a scratch on you.” “Really?” Leigh says with wide eyes - he knows what the benefits of befriending this mammoth would be. No one would cross a man protected by J-Thug, who Leigh thinks must obviously dominate the entire prison because of his sheer physical presence. “You mean... you’ll help me?” “Sure,” says J-Thug while pulling his t-shirt over his head, revealing the bulky body beneath. He takes a seat on the lower bunk beside Leigh and puts his arm around him, his expression taking a sudden serious turn - “Now blow me.”
