Blitzkrieg

Date: ........BUT I HATE IT ANYWAY! “Damn it, where is my godforsaken AKA-47 automatic pulse rifle?!” In today’s world, you have got to be the initiator. The guy who strikes first. There is no time to waste. The scene opens up upon the frightening sight of Syko the Goblin armed head to toe with an arsenal big enough to suffice a small platoon. Garnishing him with the weaponry, his assistant Dr. L.A. Borodinsky plays the role of a tailor, only customizing a warrior. They reside here within Syko’s personal armory (Goblin HQ), and the name of the game seems to be “Turn the loose cannon into a walking landmine.” LAB: “Zire, pleaze hold still for a moment! I’ll vill try and find it!” LAB takes another look at Syko, who is pretty much a walking bombshell… LAB: “(Said slowly, and clearly.) Hold… perfectly… still!” Squeezing his eyes shut, and crossing his fingers, LAB disappears into the plethora of racks and shelves loaded with diabolical devices. Syko the Goblin: “BAH! Can you not see I am heading into battle here?! Make it snappy, Gramps!” Now here is a guy with the right idea… Strike hard, and fast. With a rather brisk hop in his step for his age, LAB returns to the irate goblin with a hefty looking, rapid destroying AKA-47, and hoists it into his arms with a heave. LAB soon folds at the waist, propping his hands onto bent knees, attempting to catch his breath, but before he can… Syko the Goblin: “THIS IS NO TIME FOR NAPPING!! You… (Pointing the rifles barrel at LAB’s head, unknowingly.) …still have to get the C-4’s, and the missile turrets! Oh, and lets not forget the blah, blah, blah… LAB takes a rather wide-eyed look at Syko, weighted down by, well… A pair of steel spiked combat boots, a Bazooka slung over his shoulder, a strap of photon grenades hung diagonally around his torso, a pair of .45 caliber pistols holstered at each hip, two katana sheathed alongside the .45’s, a quiver of napalm tipped arrows on his back, the perfect ammunition for the ready to spring crossbow clamped to his right hand wrist, heat/night vision goggles placed neatly onto his large cranium, a modified AKA-47, and a four foot clip of armor piercing bullets to compliment that, as well. HARD AND FAST. Syko the Goblin: “Blah, Blah, and if you think I’ve forgotten the…” LAB: “Don’t you zink zat you have gone a bit overboard, Master?” Syko the Goblin: “…You’re in for a big… Hmm? (Interupted, the Syko raises an eyebrow.)” Here it comes. Syko the Goblin: “(Indignantly, in fact a tone of disbelief.) Overboard? Did you just say ‘overboard?’” LAB: “Vell… (His eye’s shift nervously.)” Syko the Goblin: “Have you totally lost you mind?! There is no overboard in WAR!” LAB: “I vas just saying…” Syko the Goblin: “WAR is no game! I, Syko the Goblin, mustn’t walk into a battle so dire and life changing as this totally unprepared!” LAB: “B-b-b-but…” Syko the Goblin: “SILENCE!” The ancient scientist crumples into a ball at Syko’s feet, his index fingers jammed deep into his ears, as he rocks back and fourth. Syko the Goblin: “This is no ordinary fight… Not when Continental gold is on the line!” Licking his lips passionately, the Goblin contemplates the reward placed neatly on his wall, right next to the head of Legend Inc. Syko the Goblin: “Soon… we STRIKE AT DAWN!” LAB: “(Whining.) It’s already half past noon…” Syko the Goblin: “Oh crap! We’re late!” Soon we find ourselves trailing behind the Goblin in 44,906kgs of hard steel and pure raw power. Its treads crushing the pavement it rolls upon, the out of place M-48 battle tank rolls at a swift 48km/h. Standing halfway out of the hatch door, the Goblin thrusts a glimmering blade forward, his will seeming to guide the way. Syko the Goblin: “Forward now! The time is most certainly nigh, Duke Williams and Chris Chase, for you will pay for your constant foiling of the Dark Age’s deeds! And Legend Inc… The bearer of the treasure. I must smite your good ways, and take what I want quickly, while I still can! Blitzkrieg!! Blitzkrieg!! Blitzkrieg!!” The wind blows dramatically through Syko’s hair as the tank rolls into a large town… Welcome to Oxford, Mississippi The Goblin barely catches a glimpse as they bowl over the sign, demolishing it in their path. Suddenly, popping out of the cockpit beside him, Dr. L.A. Borodinsky squeezes beside Syko. LAB: “Master! I zink ve got company!” The panicked old man directs Syko’s attention to the chasing cops behind them. The red and blue lights flash over Syko’s furious expression. An officer in the passenger side of the leading car hangs out the window with a megaphone. Cop: “Pull over! We are prepared to shoot…” Syko the Goblin: “BAH! What the hell did we do?!” Meanwhile, not far up the road there seems to be a situation… Innocent Bystanding woman: “(Blubbering.) Oh God, somebody save my baby!” Syko turns his attention dead ahead to see a baby stroller left in the middle of the street. Syko the Goblin: “FULL SPEED AHEAD!!!” Soon… We rejoin the villainous Goblin within the cockpit of the behemoth tool of destruction, along with the assisting scientific madman, and now Esther who has (Wo)manned the wheel. They all watch a monitor closely; their eyes steady on a still image and its glow, the only cast of light shed over their faces. LAB: “…Vat now?” Syko the Goblin: “Hmph… Well, as expected, those insolent piss ants we face on Tuesday have yet to show up. So, with the early bird advantage on our side, I have devised a perfect ambush…” LAB: “It is Zursday, Master. Tuesday is vive days away.” Syko the Goblin: “And we will lie in wait…” Esther leans back into her seat, grumbling about this and that, deciding to light up a cigar. LAB: “Errm… Lord Syko?” Syko the Goblin: “Yes?” LAB: “Vat about them?” The scene cuts to the outside of the tank finally, to another shot of the tank from the outside. The tank in the massive parking lot of the Vaught Hemmingway Stadium, a trail of destruction leading to its exact location now. And, oh yeah… The local police force that is surrounding the tank, in a situation worthy of negotiation. “Bah, if they wanna talk… They can wait till Tuesday.”
