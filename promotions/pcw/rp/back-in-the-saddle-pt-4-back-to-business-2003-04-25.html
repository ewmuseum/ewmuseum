Back in the Saddle pt 4 - back to business

25 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4-25-2003 Our scene begins in the elaborate home of our resident Adonis, Dan “Big O” Osbourne, where Dan is getting prepared to complete his mission given to him by Elizabeth Montoya. We watch as Dan dresses in an Armani Collezioni Heather Charcoal three-button suit with matching patent leather boots. Donning a pair of Armani 1517 sunglasses, Dan grabs a set of keys from their hanger and heads out in the direction of his carport. I the carport we find a series of different automobiles ranging from a classic ’69 GTO Judge to a custom Ferrari Enzo. Dan heads towards an eerily black 2003 Jaguar XKR. As he starts the supercharged V8 engine, the 400 horses roar from under the hood. The car shifts into gear and the 18-inch tires scream as they leave their calling card on the asphalt. Dan doesn’t seem to be in the best of moods. Dan screeches to a halt in front of a large white building with two flags hanging from the corners. One corner rests an American flag, the other, the symbol of the popular brand of clothing, Tommy Hilfiger. Dan steps from the car and determinedly walks towards to entrance where long-time friends, Karolina Kurkova and Gabe Aubrey meet him. “Dan? Dan Osbourne? Is that really you? “Yeah, KK, it’s me.” Karolina rushes up and throws her arms around Dan’s neck, hugging him tightly. Gabe extends his hand whereby it is met with mutual admiration. Karolina releases her hold and takes Dan’s hand into her own, leading him past security and inside the building. “Oh my God. Everyone will be so happy to see you” “It’s good to see you both again.” “So what brings you here, man?” “Well, Gabe, I wish it were under better circumstances, but I’m here on business.” “Business? Of what sort?” “I’m here to shut you down.” Karolina steps back, openly aghast, “Shut us down? What in the world do you mean?” “Mikey and Molly have been kidnapped. The only way to get them back is to ensure that Tommy Hilfiger, Inc. does not make it to their show this evening, thereby ruining the entire company.” Back in the now familiar studio where Dan is being interviewed, “What? What do you mean Dan? Weren’t you looking for this place? It seems like you knew exactly where it was. Why all the difficulties then? The Hedgehog, the lion, the &$%#-off, everything. I just don’t get it?” “Well, I wasn’t after the location of this place. Bear with me and I promise, your questions will be answered.” With tears welling up in her eyes, Karolina stands in disbelief at the bomb just dropped on her. Gabe does his best to remain calm. “Dan, I don’t understand. Who would do such a thing?” “Sorry, Gabe, if I have any hope of getting Mikey and Molly back, I can’t tell you. I just need information.” “You know we can’t do that, Dan.” “Please don’t make me do this the hard way. Just tell me what I need to know and I’ll leave.” “Dan, please don’t do this!” sobs Karolina. “We can’t tell you where the show is and you know it. Don’t force us to call security.” “KK, you know security can’t stop me. No one in this building can. Please, for the sake of our friendship, just tell me where it’s at.” Just then, another beautiful woman accompanied by four large men, obviously security, steps forward. “Dan, I’m going to have to ask you to leave.” “Sorry, Ally, but I’m not leaving until someone tells me where the fashion show is being held tonight.” The largest of the four men steps forward, “I believe Ms. Hilfiger asked you to leave, Sir. Please do so before we have to force you to leave.” “Look, I’m only going to say this one more time. Where is the fashion show being held?” Silence. “Very well. I’m sorry.” With that, Dan leaps towards the large security guard, smashing him in the mouth with a leaping knee. He immediately grabs the nearest guard and lays him out with a belly-to-belly suplex. The remaining two guards each grab one of Dan’s arms. Dan leaps into the air, taking them both out with a mid-air split kick. As the head guard gets to one knee, Dan hits him with a shining wizard. Four men. Twenty seconds. It’s over. Dan adjusts his sunglasses and looks towards to startled supermodels. “Now will you tell me or do I have to get it out of you. As much as I would hate to harm any of you, Mikey and Molly are that important to me.” Ally steps forward, “Find, Dan. I’ll tell you. My Dad’s show is being held at the Beverly Garland Holiday Inn in North Hollywood. Now will you get out of here?!” “Thanks, Ally. And for what it’s worth, I’m sorry.” Karolina, Gabe, and Ally stand outside watching as Dan’s Jaguar drives off to ruin the company they work for. Hours later, a long black limousine pulls up to a long red carpet where onlookers, journalist, and photographers await the emergence of the next star. Bulbs flash and people scream and yell and the limo door opens. Out steps, dressed in a stunning black tux… “Armani?” “Ralph Lauren.” “Oh” Dan steps from the car and waves at the astonished crowd. He turns back towards the open car door and extends his hand. A tender looking hand accepts the invitation and out steps Ali Landry, dressed in an amazingly gorgeous red dress… “Ralph Lauren?” “Versaci.” “Oh.” Ali takes hold of Dan’s arm and the couple begins walking down the red carpet all the while the paparazzi hounding them with questions. “Dan, where have you been?” “Ali, are you two a couple?” “Dan, did you get fired from PCW?” “Ali, who designed your dress?” “Are you two an item?” Inside, the scene is almost as hectic and exciting. Stars from all aspects of entertainment shake hands, hug, talk, and introduce one another. Dan and Ali are greeted by some of the top stars from Movies and television, sports, and fashion. After excusing himself, Dan heads backstage where twenty plus models prepare to put on one hell of a show. After scanning the mass of people, Dan finally gets a lock on his target, the one and only Tommy Hilfiger himself. Dan makes his way towards the fashion tycoon. Without warning, Dan feels the barrel of a gun pressed into his ribs and a large man speaks. “Mr. Osbourne, please come with me. Mr. Hilfiger would like to have a word with you.” In a private room in the back of the Beverly Garland Dan sits in a somewhat drab room. Tommy Hilfiger turns around in his chair to face our daring hero. “I understand you assaulted some of my guards today, Mr. Osbourne? I know why you’re here, and I’m sorry for your loss, but this show will go on without a hitch.” “I know. That’s why I was coming to find you. Not to bring you down, but to talk to you. I have a plan, but I need your help.” “What do you have in mind?” Hours later A dark room flickers with the lights of many candles. Dark figures stand cloaked in the shadows. An alter sits in the center of the room. A young girl struggles against the restraints. Her naked body shivers against the cold stone. A cloaked figure raises a dagger. The cloaked figure begins to speak, “Oh great Angel of Light. Take this sacrifice in exchange for the power to crush our enemies in your name.” With that, the figure begins to plunge the dagger towards the sobbing girl. With the sound of metal striking metal, the dagger flies from the hand, landing unceremoniously on the floor. The figure turns to face the interloper only to meet eye to eye with our resident Adonis, Dan “Big O” Osbourne, but he is not alone. From behind him steps black clad figures. Ninja. The cloaked figure removes the shroud to reveal Elizabeth Montoya. “Mr. Osbourne. I’m sorry to see that you’ve changed your mind about helping me.” “Get bent, bitch. Ninja, ATTACK!” Upon command, a wave of ninja rushes the room. The remaining observers remove their cloaks in preparation of the onslaught. An epic battle ensues. Many lives are lost. Dan and Elizabeth refrain from the combat unless it comes their way, only staring at each other intensely. Elizabeth raises her hand and her combatants stop suddenly. “Cease this conflict. The battle is lost. The day is yours, Mr. Osbourne.” “Where is Molly and Mikey?” “They are in the adjacent room.” Back in the studio. “Wow, Dan. Were they okay?” “Yup, they sure were.
