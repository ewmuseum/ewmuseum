Going to a bar in good ole Texas...and this doesn't involve PINK!

31 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5.31.02 [previously] We last saw Mike and Ron returning home from New Jersey, the site of the last Fusion. They had talked about a magazine that they recieved called The Insider that talked about the new tag teams in the PCW. We now flash forward.... [fade in] It's the middle of the night and I can't sleep worth a damn, I wonder if Mike is having the same problem. I don't know if it's because I have been driving for awhile or am I just now feeling the effects of our match against Cowabunga Connection. I mean I shouldn't be since it has been a week now. Or maybe the reason I can't sleep is because of our second match in the Round Robin Tournament, but I'm not sure. Let me go and see if Mike is having the same problem as am I. [seconds later...] 'Hey Mike, you awake?' 'Yeah I am, for some reason I can't fall asleep,' he stated. 'I know what you mean, I've been having the same damn problem.' 'You know what I think it is Ron...' 'What's that?' 'I'm thinking maybe its our match against the Heartbreakers...' 'You think?' 'Yeah I believe so. Tell you what bro. Let's get some clothes on and goto the bar or something and talk about our upcoming match...what do you say?' 'I'm game. I'll meet you downstairs.' I walk out of Mike's room and back to mine. I grab some jeans, a PCW shirt, socks and my shoes. I put my socks, jeans then my shirt on. I grab my shoes and take them downstairs. I walk downstairs and sit on the couch and start putting my shoes on. I can hear Mike walking down the stairs. 'Ron you ready yet...' 'Yeah let's go.' I grab my keys off the table and we leave the house. I hit the alarm for the car as Mike gets on the passenger side and I on the driver's side. 'Now don't drink to much tonight Mike, we gotta do a workout tomorrow.' 'I know, and you don't neither,' he responded. 'I'll probably just have like two beers or something.' 'Same with me.' [half hour later...] 'Alright Mike, we are here.' 'Let's go in.' We get out of the car and walk inside the bar. We have been going to this bar for the longest time. The reason we go here is because our best friend works here and of course we get free beer, ain't nothing wrong with that right. 'Mike,Ron what's up fellas?' 'James is that you,' I asked. 'Yeah. What the hell are you two doing out so late man? It's good to see you guys.' 'Me and Ron couldn't sleep so we decided to come down to The Friendly Spot and get some brews and talk about our match on Tuesday.' 'I hear ya. So how is that wrestling buisness going for you guys,' James asked. 'It's going real well. We had our debut match about a week ago and knocked off one hell of a tag team in Cowabunga Connection in the opening round of the Round Robin Tournament to crown the tag champs, and it doesn't get any easier.' 'So I heard. How you been doin Mike?' 'James, I'm hanging in there. How are you doin man?' 'I'm doin good but I need to get out of here though. It's not paying the bills for me you know.' 'I hear ya,' Mike responded. 'Hey I read something on you guys on that wrestling magazine The Insider. What's up with that guy?' 'He's just a critic doin his job that's all. You know stuff like that just fuels the fire on me and Mike.' 'Yeah I know Ron. You guys want something? On me...' 'Let's just get two bud lights.' 'Two bud lights coming up..there ya go. So what's up with this match that has you guys not sleeping?' 'I think the reason is, is because these guys are one hell of a tag team and another reason maybe because they held the titles before the whole PCW roster was fired,' Mike stated. 'I mean I know that they use to hold the belts, but I still don't understand why we can't get to sleep Mike.' 'Maybe cause our match against Cowabunga Connection was a brutal one and now we realized what we got ourself into here in PCW. If CC was one of their top tag teams and they gave us a great match, we go up against the team that held the titles in The Heartbreakers. So that's maybe why.' 'But you know it's going to be a challenge for us, and one I sure am looking forward too.' 'You and me both Ron.' 'You guys are up against The Heartbreakers...' 'That's right. Don't let the name fool you. The name may be soft, but they are a tough tag team,' Mike says. 'I know you guys can do what you put your minds too and I know you guys can move on in this round robin tournament. You guys have a chance to become tag champs in a short amount of time. So do what you can to get those titles.' 'There is no doubt in our minds James, that me and Mike are going to go out there and whoop some ass...' 'Hey Ron, I wouldn't say that now...' 'Why Mike. That's what we are going to do right?' 'We are yes, but I wouldn't want to say 'whoop some ass' to The Heartbreakers cause they really might want us to and I don't swign that way and I hope to hell you don't!' 'HAHA. Hell no Mike! I know what your saying though. Well let me put it this way...we will destroy the Heartbreakers on Tuesday night and move on in the tournament and another step closer to becoming the PCW Tag Team Champions.' 'That's better.' [fade out]
