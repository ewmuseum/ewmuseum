America's Youth Sure is Dumb

28 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/28/02 Time: 3: 45 P.M Location: MexicoScene: Spring Break “Golden Game hits main stream television.” The scene opens with Juan Borro and Kevin Klaymore hanging out over by the beach. Kevin has been out surfing for a while, but Juan decided to just sit back and read some of the newspaper from Portugal. The sun is shining quite brightly on this afternoon. The usual loud noises and chaos is going on all around the beach. For the next few moments thought, the screams out shouts seem to become a little bit louder. Juan notices the commotion and quickly wants to go back to his hotel and leave all of the rowdy teenagers. On his way out though, a camera team and a reporter of some type stop Juan. Reporter: Excuse me, but would you like to be on television? Juan: Excuse me, but who the hell are you? The man puts his hand out and responds. Reporter: I work for MTV. You know it’s a world famous station. Were here at Spring Break and we want to see what kind of crazy stuff we could get kids to do for certain amounts of money. Juan: I’m no kid. I’m a highly intellectual adult. I’m quite insulted by your relentless and foolhardy approach. MTV show host: Umm…sure, what ever you just said I didn’t understand. So how would you like to be part of this all? The host extends his hand to Juan who frowns and spits on the hand in front of him. The host then shakes his hand off quite upset, as Juan let’s out a few laughs. Meanwhile, in the water, Kevin notices a crowd around Juan, and goes running over to see what’s actually going on. Kevin arrives at the scene and notices the furious host, with spit rolling down his palm. Kevin: Juan, what’s going on? Juan: Nothing, this American idiot over here, was trying to waist my precious time by flashing money in my face. The host wipes the spit off of his finger, and then goes right up to Juan, poking him in his chest with his small fingers. Juan becomes enraged that the man even laid a hand on him. Juan quickly reacts by lifting the man up by his shirt. The host gets nervous and screams for help. MTV show host: SECURITY! Suddenly, a group of security officers come charging toward Kevin and Juan. Juan quickly drops the host, and gets ready to run off, but before he does, Juan hears a laugh from the MTV show host as the security officers approach quickly. Juan wants a little payback, and when the host turns around….BAM! GOLDEN GOAL straight to the head of the host. The host falls to the ground, and the cameraman tries to run away also, but Kevin gives him a big boot to the face, knocking him down. Kevin then throws the two men on his back as he goes rushing away from the security along with Juan. Up ahead the two men see a dark alley. They approach it and makes a sharp turn into it, where they hide behind a dumpster. They watch carefully as the security scatters around looking for them. Security Guard 1: I think we lost them. Security Guard 2: Damn! Security Guard 1: Oh well, I heard they have a wet t-shirt contest going on with over sized women, let’s go. Security Guard 2: Woohoo! The security guards go off on their way, as Juan and Kevin just look at each other in disgust after they just heard. They both scream “EWWW!” simultaneously and then leave the dark alley. Before they completely leave Kevin stops them. Kevin: Hey man we should grab their equipment before we get out of here, we could sure make some money off of the equipment. Juan: Good thinking, go grab it. Kevin grabs the equipment, and the two men go off back to their hotel, when a young man suddenly stops them. Young Man: Hey I know who you guys are, you work for MTV and do that “ What would you do for money for money” segment. Can I please be in it? I mean please dudes, you’d so rule if I could be. Juan doesn’t know what’s going on until he takes a look at the MTV equipment and then thinks back to the show the real host asked him to be on where you make people do certain things for money. Kevin is about to tell the man they have nothing to do with the show, when Juan quickly stops him from speaking. Juan then pulls Kevin aside. Kevin: Why did you stop me? Juan: Just follow along with this and don’t say anything. We can make the United States look like idiots and get some airtime for free. Kevin: Great. Juan: Go over and grab the camera. Turn it on, and then we’ll be live on the air. This should be good. Kevin follows Juan’s commands. As Juan turns on the microphone and agrees with the young man to be apart of the show. (On the air) Juan: Hello America! Here we are for an exciting addition of what stupid American kids will do for a buck. Right here, we have one of those idiots with us right now. This stupid kid is actually willing to do anything. The kid is confused at the fact of being called dumb, but the second Kevin focuses the camera on his he’s all smiles. Juan: Now, are you sure you’re willing to do anything? Young Man: Hell Yea! Juan: Okay, well our deed for today will be the exciting and always so pleasurable burning of the American flag, are you up for it? Young Man: Totally! Kevin who’s behind the camera can’t help but laugh as the sight of the stupid American kid he videoing. Juan: You know what, we’ll give you a whole five dollars to do it. Now are you game for that or what. Young Man: I’m game this is knarly. Juan hands the kid the flag as he quickly burns it thinking he’s the coolest thing in the world. Juan and Kevin laugh at the sight and when the young man is finished Kevin focuses the camera back on Juan. Juan: Yes! You’ve seen it folks, these Americans really are the dumbest things you’ll ever encounter on earth. If you don’t believe it, just remember this moment. The young man pops his head back in front of the camera and speaks. Young Man: Where is my five bucks? Juan just nods and backhands him in the face as he falls down to the ground. Juan: AH! So let’s take this time, to talk about that great team in PCW called Golden Game. Yes America, we know you love them. Let’s say a few words about our gracious competition. Kevin whispers over to Juan. Kevin: Was it me or was that kid just now who burned the flag one of the long lost Cowabunga Connection members? Juan: No, I think he was also. That was pretty retarded now wasn’t it. Oh I’m sorry the word retarded is politically incorrect. That brings us to that team. Wow! They sure have an influence on America’s youth like we just saw. Kevin: They sure do, but let’s not forget about other great teams like Fear Factory and Misplaced. Juan: Yeah, I mean wow Fear Factory can get you scared, but can they actually beat you, I don’t think so. Oh and I think Misplaced might of misplaced their there brains when they decided to step in the ring with us. Don’t even get me started on Suburban Uprising that new team, they make it seem like people are scared of the suburbs. Uh oh, not the attack of team Abercrombie. Kevin: Maybe them and Fear Factory should have a child called “No Talent”, maybe they’ll get the clue then. Juan: Well America, like you have just seen, you’re people are pretty dumb. Until next time, learn how to read and pass the secret on to your president. Kevin cuts the videoing and the scene the promo fades to black.
