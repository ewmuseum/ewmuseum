R-E-S-P-E-C-T...and now I know what it means to me

8 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: January 8th 2003 NB: The original format of this piece was written in German. It has been checked and translated into English, for the benefit of a predominantly English-speaking audience. My back is still a little sore since that German Rules Ladder Match as Decimation. It's not 'life-ending' sore, but I constantly get a little niggle every now and then which causes some discomfort and aggreivation. In fact, my lower back has been a constant problem for a number of years now but I just ignore the problem. I know of a few guys who have been in more pain and they've just got on with the job...so why shouldn't I? When I first suggested the concept for that match against Poe, I was more concerned about how the match would broadcast my abilities. I never looked back...only looking forward to the biggest match of my career since...well...the last 'biggest match of my career' However, when I let some people in on the 'masterplan'...it got a lukewarm reaction. I knew what they were thinking... 'He's 6'5' and weighs 290lbs for crying out loud! He can't fly around the ring like a damn risk taking luchadore in that type of match atmosphere!' I began to have second thoughts as December 22nd approached. I know that this match wouldn't be no walk in the park and I knew that Poe had some evil intentions in store...as did I. But with Christmas just a few days after...did I really want to be laid up in a hospital bed on Christmas Morning, mumbling 'Merry Christmas' to my wife and child while under some oxygen mask? It ran through my mind for days until I convinced myself that this was the right thing to do. Plus, I was the defending Network Champion. The Network Title means more to me at this moment in time than anything. Worth more than my career? My life? After much deliberation...yes, yes it was. So I went through with the match and in the process, I aggreivated the pain in the lower back that I mentioned eariler. It was a brutal match and I think I surprised the majority of those skeptics backstage by retaining my title and actually walking out of the arena. The more I think about it, the more I realised that I had done the right thing. I had a job to do and I had to do, no matter what. In this business, everybody takes calculated risks to climb the ladder of success. Hell, Kannon himself took an almighty risk with his sudden pruchase of RUSH...but it was a calculated risk that is, so far, proving to be the right decision. I could have shrugged off the throbbing in my back and continued to compete. With the Carinal of Carnage match coming up on Christmas Eve, I was taking another risk. But I had no time to think about the repercussions of that crazy match. No sooner had I had the vision of being laid up in that hospital, that I was competing in the CoC match and testing the lower back that was ready to give up. Thankfully, no permanent damage was done and I felt that made a pretty good showing of myself in that match when the whole world was watching. Just as a precaution, I decided to have a specialist take a look at my back. So I returned to Germany for a few days over the holiday period and saw the specialist in Munich. The results were excellent. A little wear and tear, but nothing too serious to ruin my career at this 'early stage'. So with my back been given the all clear, I made the trip up north to my hometown of Hamburg and paid a visit to the family. I was expecting some kind of verbal tirade on how I have disgraced the family name, etc, etc...especially from the parents. But, to my surprise, they welcomed me home with open arms and stated their utmost proudness of their middle child. As I spent a traditional German christmas, I began to realise certain things. That blood is thicker than water. That family morals and values is possibly more important than any Network Title. That family itself is probably THE most important thing in your life. While being surrounded by my loved ones, some I hadn't seen since I was a teenager, my respect and love for my family grew beyond anything I could have ever imagined. With that and everything else said in this little commentary, I have decided to show my respect to the family and I will unveil this new found respect when the time is right. But for now, and as some General Manager often says...I'm baaaaack and I'm betteeeerrrrr than eeevvveeerrrr!
