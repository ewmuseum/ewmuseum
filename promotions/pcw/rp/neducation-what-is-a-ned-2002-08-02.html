NEDucation: What Is A Ned?

2 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2nd August [Slowly our scene fades in, our does it? That is the question … Actually, for a moment, we are in total darkness. Scary eh? Anyway, after a few seconds, the darkness is broken, when none other than British TV Personality and all-round pain in the ass, Lloyd Grossman steps onto the screen. Mr. Grossman is wearing a pair of thin, black glasses, and a black tuxedo, making him look very smart indeed. In his hands, he clutches a little switch, you know, like the one’s the weather men have? Stopping at the left hand side of the screen, Grossman looks into the camera, and starts to speak.] Lloyd Grossman: So what exactly is a Ned? How do they think, and what do they do? Well, over the course of this short TV Documentary, I intend to ‘Neducate’ further, and answer all these questions, and more, that are in your mind at the moment. [Lloyd pauses, to clear his throat, before clicking the button. The words ‘Neducation – Introduction’.] Lloyd Grossman: In the beginning, there was dinosaurs, Then there were the Egyptians, the Romans, The Vikings, The Nazis, The Taliban … But now, there is a new threat … [He presses said button again, this time a picture of a couple Neds, track suited out, running away with a television in hamnd.] Lloyd Grossman: … The Ned. [Pressing the button once again, the words ‘Indentifying And Classifying A Ned’ appear on the screen.] Lloyd Grossman: There are 3 definitive types, styles & classes of Neds, each of them posses similar weaknesses, strengths & attitudes. [Click. A picture of a pre-pubescent boy, dressed up in a black & white tracks, gel in his hair, much like you’d see Andy Murray. The words ‘The Wee Man’ appear below it.] Lloyd Grossman: The Wee Man is the first classification of a Ned. The Wee Man is a pre-pubescent origin, and like all Neds, are highly flammable. They where grotesquely over-sized tracksuits, and in attempt to be like the other Neds, often steal their father’s cheap, stanching aftershave. Heights usually range from 4’ to 5’, and their hair is supplemented by 1 scoop of hair gel. The Wee Man’s first instinct is to insult anyone or anything he sees, making him a particular menace to those with little tolerance and patience. He has a squeaky, irritating voice that can shatter windows, and upon confrontation, he will always hide with the 3rd classification of a Ned, ‘The Big Man’. The Wee Man will always seek refuge with The Big Man, which automatically develops his ego. However, if The Big Man asks him to do something, The Wee Man will always oblige. He will frequently ask the Big Man to harm others in an act of physical violence. He has two major weaknesses: More insults leading to more enemies, and relying on The Big Man means he will have to be in the Big Man’s presence at all times, or face beatings if found alone. [Grossman clicks the switch again, this time, a picture of your average Ned, Andy Murray, dressed in Nedly attire, appears behind him, the words ‘THE Ned’ below.] Lloyd Grossman: The second classification of a Ned, is THE Ned, you’re average, everyday Ned. The Ned is larger in size than The Wee Man, and has a heavier Glaswegian accent. The Ned can be found wearing Adidas/Nike/Reebok/Umbro/Kappa tracksuits. He will also wear a matching baseball cape, and a pair of expensive training shoes, 2-6 sovereign rings and occasionally, a football scarf. His hair is supplemented with 2 scoops of gel. The Ned has several attributes making it what it is. Unlike The Wee Man, they can fend for themselves, and often gloat about the sovereign rings that they posses, that The Wee Man cannot afford. Neds often meet down by the local play or football park, to smoke narcotics and drink Buckfast, the alcoholic beverage of choice for the Neds. They have strength in numbers also, and beign drunk can often lead to brutal Nedly fights. However, their tracksuits and hair gel make them highly flammable, making them susceptible to fire. [Lloyd clicks the button again, a picture of an older, tougher looking Ned, dressed in a fleece and a pair of jogging trousers. The words ‘The Big Man’ below.] Lloyd Grossman: The Big Man is our final classification, and is the leader of all Neds. He is the dominant enforcer of The Wee Man, and has slurred speech. His height can be anything above 5’5”, and he dresses similar to THE Ned, but will often wear a fleece & jogging trousers, and shown in the picture. He wears 3 or 4 scoop of gel in his hair, and lacks intelligence, but has great strength. The Big Man is big, dumb, and intimidating, and is always in the mood for a fight. Brute strength leads to lone ventures, and he is loved by few, but repulsed by the majority. His obvious lack of intelligence leads to many a violent conflict. His main weaknesses are obviously his lack of intelligence, and his lack of working lungs, due to excessive consuming of narcotics. [As Lloyd clicks the button, the background switches to an image of a Ned, sitting in a room, clutching a microphone, the words ‘The Speech Of The Ned’ appear.] Lloyd Grossman: 9/10 Neds speak with a heavy, unmistakable accent, the remaining 1/10 speak with a fully Scottish accent, unlike their comrades, who speak in the native tongue of Glasgow, or Glesga, as a Ned would say. My colleague Tam Donnelley and I shall now recite and translate a few populate Ned phrases … [Tam sits up in his chair, and quickly recites a Ned phrase.] Tam: Ho you ya chube! Ah’m gonnae pure smash you in f’r sayin’ that aboot ma Maw! Lloyd Grossman: Translation … Excuse me you young scallywag! For verbal crimes committed against my Mother, I am going to attack you in order to gain some measure of revenge for your insolence! Tam: Mind and phone me when the fitba’ is on, ‘cause ah’m gwan oot fer a chippy, awright muckah? Lloyd Grossman: Translation … Remember to call my cellular phone and remind me when the football game begins, for I am going in search of nourishment in the form of a carbon based fatty product slathered in “batter”, accompanied by a Haddock caught in our fine Scottish rivers, OK chum? Tam: Here, are ye comin’ oot cruisin’ wi’ me fer some burds doon the Strathie? Ye should see the talent doon their man! HOOMPH MAN! Lloyd Grossman: Translation … Excuse me, would you care to join me for a journey in my Corsa in search of some females for which to court at Strathclyde Park? I swear the women there are more than likely clean and fresh for the taking my friend! TOP TOTTY MY FRIEND! Tam: See if you dinnae stop annoying me ya wee bampot, ah’m pure gonnae chib this brick aff yer nut…GOT ME??? Lloyd Grossman: Translation … I must inform you that if you do not refrain from annoying me, you little wretch, I will honestly throw this orange cuboid commonly used for construction purposes against your cranium …do you understand? Tam: This is pure borin’ min! Ah’m gawn doon the park fer a fag an’ a wee dram o’ buckie, embdy wan’ tae come wi’ me? Lloyd Grossman: Translation … This event is not stimulating enough for my mind! I think I may venture down to the local play or football park and partake in a cigarette composed of narcotics and a full bottle of Buckfast, would anyone care to join me? [And with that said, Lloyd clicks the button, and The Ned disappears, replaced by a list of Nedly words, and their translations. The words ‘Ned Dictionary’ at the top.] Lloyd Grossman: You may wish to familiarise yourself with the following words, should you ever come in contact with a Ned … Pure, Mad, Mental Min! Absolutely spiffing my friend!Gonnaegeesa… Please May I borrow a…Haw/Ho! Ha!Bawbag! The testicular area of a male. I didnae mean it… Please excuse my intentional action Ah’m gonnae chib ye! I’m going to attack you with a projectile!This is pure hummin’ man! Nasal receptors detect negative smells!Ah’m gawn tae watch the fitba’! Aah! Another chance for hooliganism!‘His music’s pure bangin’ man! These synthesized songs are top quality!WHO AAAARE YE!? IDENTIFY YOURSELF!Min Man/Bro/DudePish Crap/Rubbish/PissChippae Fish & Chip ShopToff Someone of a higher class [Giving the viewers enough time to take in the translations, Lloyd clicks the button once more, this time, we see a view of Andy Murray’s apartment, accompanied by the words ‘The Habitat Of The Ned’] Lloyd Grossman: The Ned lives in a small apartment, or an extremely small, badly decorated house, filled with over-priced electronic goods, usually with one or two other Neds. The typical home of the Ned is also filled with extremely bad quality furniture, football scarfs, bed covers, wall-paper & other tat. The Ned has no real use for it’s kitchen, as they usually order something from the local Fish & Chip Shop opposed to cooking their own meals. [Click. This time we see a picture of a group of Neds, even a couple of female Neds mixed in. The words ‘Ned Relationships’ appear.] Lloyd Grossman: Normally, the Ned will only mix with their own kind. They will not mix with upper-classes, or cockneys, for example. The reason behind this is that other Neds are usually the only people that will tolerate them, and whom they themselves tolerate. Ned friends will usually meet up in groups, and consume large amounts of fish & chips, alcohol and cigarettes, although they will often go to football matches, if they get in, that is. Nedly females are more focused on fake sun-tanning, and cheap jewellery instead of football, however the do tolerate the game from time to time. [With another click, the screen turns black again, with the words ‘Final Analysis’ in the middle of the black.] Lloyd Grossman: In short, the Ned is a wearer of cheap tracksuits, and lots of hair gel. They all have a heavy accent – without the accent, they are not a Ned. Neds enjoy smoking, drinking, watching and attending football games, causing mayhem and listening to Ned music, classified as Trance/Rave by most. They dislike real music, having nothing to smoke or drink, or days when there is no football on the television. The can be found in their homes, down at the local park, in shopping centres, anywhere in the evening or at a friend’s home. [And with a final click, the words ‘Final Conclusion’ appears.] Lloyd Grossman: In conclusion, the Ned is a poorly dressed, violent, stupid, abusing person. They dislike everyone apart from fellow Neds, and of course famous football personalities. However, this is not the case with some Neds, which means that a ‘Ned’ is not a person or a body, but is indeed, an attitude. [All words slowly disappear from the screen.] Lloyd Grossman: I’m Lloyd Grossman. Goodnight. [And with that, Grossman walks away. The show is over.]
