Spiritual Stuff...or something - Part 3 - The Spirit

30 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: January 30, 2003 [It's almost like it's a fight of the century. A redneck versus Martin Luther King, no holds barred and pinfalls DO count anywhere. As Wise Andy throws his clubbing fists toward Harry with nothing but pure hatred behind his eyes, Harry is actually leaning against Andy's cave getting ready to light a bowl up. It's amazing how one can occupy a drunken redneck with a couple pillows. Harry is not the sharpest tool in the shed but when desperate times call for desperate measures, then he'll call in the pillow clan. Harry watches as Andy pummels the pillows into the ground, making dirt and sticks fly in the air like crazy. The ol' redneck has OFFICIALLY snapped big time. Everyone knew that ever since his demolition derby days he's had a short fuse, but Harry wasn't prepared for anything like this. Andy already hated him for blowing up his Hot Rod, which he didn't do in the first place, and once Harry announced that Martin Luther King was reincarnated as him, that's when Andy snapped and went into attack mode. It's when Harry used the pillow clan as decoys that he came to the realization that he couldn't have been made from Martin Luther King because of that whole listening to heavy metal music. But who knows, maybe MLK got down to the smooth sounds of rock n roll. But, Harry doubts it] Wise Andy: STUPID BLACKY!! YOU BLEW UP MY GOD DAMN HOT ROD!! [As Harry enjoys his bowl, and watching a crazy redneck try to kill pillows, Tommy wanders through the back woods searching for the force that seems to be calling him. He really shouldn't be walking through the woods, the black circles under his eyes say so. He needs to be home and get some rest for his big title defense on Fusion. But, no, he's out in the woods searching for some kind of bodacious cosmic force that is pulling him deeper into the woods. The trees are so large, such intimidating tools of nature. It's like hundreds of giant hands are in the air ready to swoop down at any moment to pull the poor person down to the depths of hell. The way the branches just keep going off of each other it's almost like veins inside the human body. As Tommy just stares blankly at the trees his mind wanders, just like it did when the green 75 Malibu almost took his life] Deep Voice: One time you were such a carefree man. You always gave to those around you and it's something that set you apart from all of the rest. It's why you were trained by the man who trained your father to wrestle. It's why everyone around you was so drawn to you like a magnet, why all those women enjoyed your company. There is nothing better than a caring, confident, giving man in a woman's life. Especially at the age your starting to head toward when women want closure and to move on to the next level of life. Tommy, you used to be such a nice person...what has happened to you? Why have you turned to these evil ways? Is it because of humiliation? Is it because of the greed? The pressure? What is it, Tommy? Why have you turned like this? [Tommy no longer can see the trees around him. He can feel his body floating upward and blue is all that surrounds him. It's as if he's being swallowed by the sky, being engulfed into the heavens by this force of nature. His stomach feels like it's floating into his throat, sort of like when your riding a totally righteous roller coaster. It's a constant feeling like that, except he knows that he's not really moving anywhere...or is he? ] 'Totally Tubular' Tommy: Who are you, dude? Deep Voice: A close admirer. TTT: Dude, don't get too close. Deep voice: What do you mean? TTT: You know, like when I'm with babes or something. A compadre NEEDS his most excellent privacy sometimes. Deep Voice: It's nothing like that, don't worry. TTT: Gnarly. So, why are you being all...like...you know...BOGUS! Deep Voice: I think that your the one who's being bogus, Tommy. Your the one who has taken your opponents and made their lives a living hell. Your the one who feels that since your booed by the fans that you have to do evil deeds. When deep down there in your heart your nothing but good, in fact, your beyond good. TTT: Dude, don't act like you know me or anything because you don't. I'm 'Totally Tubular' Tommy, the PCW Continental Champion. I am a most radical super villain, so be advised to tread lightly. Deep Voice: If I had a quarter for every time you told people that I'd be a very rich man. I could retire, build my dream home, and date Kirsten Dunst. TTT: DUDE, she is a most BODACIOUS babe. I must say, you have a great taste in women. Deep voice: Thank you, thank you. But we're not here to talk about women, what we're hear to talk about is YOU, and how you can change. You know, your NOT a super villain. Your not even any kind of villain. The crowd boos you because of your sudden attitude change. Once you and Harry broke up the Cowabunga Connection its as if you threw away that happy go lucky personality. Almost like your forgot exactly who you were. Well, here in these woods there are a lot of people who lose things, and get lost themselves. Maybe it's time you lost that attitude of yours and start from scratch because it's never too late for redemption, and it's never too late to be the man you once were. TTT: DUDE! What's it going to take to convince you that I AM A SUPER VILLAIN?! [For some reason, unknown to any person except Tommy, a goose comes by] TTT: Watch... [Tommy tosses a rock at the goose and makes it fall out of the sky] TTT: See? Pretty evil, huh? Deep Voice: Your trying to hard to be evil. Honestly, what kind of super villain hits a goose with a rock? TTT: A BODACIOUS SUPER VILLAIN, DUDE! Deep Voice: Of course. TTT: Damn skippy. Deep Voice: I've tried my best, shown you the error of your ways. The rest is all up to you and you alone. I suppose your stubborness is really being used against you now. Maybe you feel that with these evil ways that your gaining success in PCW. I don't know what your thinking, to be honest. I just wish it would 180 and get you back on track. [Tommy seems confused just floating there] TTT: Dude, I have NO IDEA what your talking about! Deep Voice: Figures. TTT: Listen, if your like God or something, do you think you could tell me that meaning of life thing? It would be a TOTALLY GNARLY pick up line for the babes. I bet you use it all the time. Deep Voice: I am not God, and I'm not telling you the meaning of life. It's really not that hard to come up with...you just make life whatever you want it to be, it's your choice. TTT: That's bogus. Deep Voice: See ya around, Tommy. [Tommy's body suddenly begins floating out of the sky. Tommy isn't let down or anything by this event, he's more confused as to why any kind of heinous spirit would call him up for all of that stuff. It didn't really make much sense, but there isn't a lot that makes sense to The Tubular One. His beautiful body returns to the surface to tackle the meaning of life as the spirit behind the voice can only watch] Deep Voice: Good luck, son. [Fade to black]
