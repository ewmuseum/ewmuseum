The Torture Continues..... RP #2

22 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/22/04 Big Al: Welcome back to the Al Larcher show, only on 98.5 FM, in the Wabash Valley of Illinois. We’re back with PCW’s Boda, my guest for the morning show today. Last segment, we covered a little bit of the upcoming PPV tournament PCW is holding. Boda, seems to be the odds on favorite to win it all, but that’s only because Duke Williams is dead and can’t compete. Boda: Al, there you go again with the Duke Williams lovefest. Actually, Duke stands as much chance of winning the World title as does Shaun Styles. And that’s slim to none, because he’s got to face me first. And, if I was Styles, and I had to face me, I’d run home, tucking tail along the way, and hide in the closet. Big Al: Are you ok? Because that made no sense. Boda: Yes it did. I simply stated that I’d hate to face Boda in the tournament if I wasn’t me. Big Al: Sure. So, what else are you doing in PCW, besides this Face to Face tournament. Are you trying to live past that defeat to Duke Williams 2 years ago? Okay, this is getting annoying. I know Al Larcher is a Duke Williams fan, but come on. Can’t he like forget the past, and let’s talk about the now and the future of PCW? Boda: Okay, well, I’ve surely come a long way since Duke’s Fluke win over me. But Al, you got to know, me and Duke were friends the whole time. I did him a favor, because I knew I was moving onto bigger things. Big Al: Allrighty then. Boda: But that was then, this week, I won’t put anyone over, but myself. I’m going to pin Shaun Styles like I do everyone that faces me, then I’ll move on to the next round, where it don’t matter who I face. Also, I got a match coming up at the PPV with Kano, for the G.M position. I’m also, outside of PCW, working on a few commercials, mainly for EA Sports and their upcoming wrestling videogame. Big Al: Ya married? Boda: Uhmm, no. Didn’t ya read up about me before I got here? Big Al: Uhmm.. no. I do know you ……… Boda: LOST TO DUKE WILLIAMS. Big Al: Yeah. See, I do follow PCW. Boda: What ever. Let’s talk about something else, anything. Big Al: Okay, so, this Saturday night, in the United Center, you are the main event against Shaun Styles…. A man that has never lost to Duke Williams by the way…. And it’s for the right to move on to the semifinals of the World Title Tournament. How are you so confident that you can beat Shaun Styles. After all…. He’s never lost to Duke Williams like you have. Okay, at this point, I’m getting a little angry. I’m not in Chicago, as I had planned on being. I’m in a small ass radio studio, with a hillbilly dj. I should have been on a real show, with a real wrestling fan, but no, I’m stuck with Duke Williams #1 fan. He’s really trying my nerves with this Duke talk. Hell, even Skitzo isn’t as annoying, with all his complaining about having to face Blitz, as Al Larcher is with his Duke asskissing. Boda: Listen, man, with all due respect, I didn’t get sidetracked in Paris Illinois……… ‘WOOT, HE REMEMBERED WHERE HE IS’ She’s still around? Someone, please gag her. Boda: ……… to talk about Duke Williams. I’m here to tell you and all the fans that they can tune into FOX this weekend, and watch 7 exciting PCW matches. A #1 contenders match for the only title Shaun Styles deserves to hold. A match between newcomer, and Face to Face challenger with the 0-1 record, Shane Crockett against Darkstar, whose on the outside of the tourney looking in also. Then there are the 4 Face to Face matches, all with a lot on the line. And even that little cockroach Jon Kano has a match against Skylar Green. All in all, Chicago is in for a hell of a night. Wait, why am I vibrating? Oh, that’s right, the cellphone. Boda: And, I’ve got to take a phone call, and try to get to Chicago, before Styles is lucky enough to win by forfeit. It’s been….. fun. I guess. Thank you. Big Al: And thank you. Before you go, I’d like to wish ya good luck in winning a third PCW World title. At least you got it easy, no Duke Williams around this time. Boda and Al shake hands, and Boda walks out of the studio, taking his cellphone out of his pocket as he does. Boda: Hey, Bobby…. Talk to me. Bobby is a childhood friend, who recently…. About a few days ago, became Boda’s agent. Boda: What? Are you kidding me? Kano did this to me? He got me lost in fucking Illinois? Well, I’m taking the next train, bus or plane to Chicago. Meet me there. This Saturday, Kano has it coming, right after I dispatch Styles from the tournament. Later. And there it is. The reason for my travel plans being fucked up. It wasn’t Bobby’s fault, though I did think so for a bit. No, it was that assbag Jon Kano, trying to play mind games with me. Well, he’ll see this Saturday, that he can’t fuck with my mind. Now, how to get to Chicago.
