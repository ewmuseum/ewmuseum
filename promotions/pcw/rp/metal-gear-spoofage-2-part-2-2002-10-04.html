Metal Gear Spoofage 2 (part 2)

4 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/4/02 On the deck of a tanker headed for god knows where, the female commando known as Brandi scoots away from Solid Snake. She hides behind a large tarp, then yanking out an automatic pistol she taunts her foe. Brandi: Whoever you are, you're not stopping us! Snake: Lady, I'm not getting within 100 feet of your pits if I can help it. Brandi: DIE! The soldier takes aim, pumping bullets in rapid succession towards Snake who crouches in terror. Snake: Bobby, come in! Bobby: No officer I don't know where I left my pants. Honest. Snake: UGH! Angrily, the hero rises again, then ducks just in time to avoid being ripped to pieces by gunfire. Brandi: I've been with the unit since I was born. I grew up on the battlefield. Conflict and victory were my parents! Snake: Yeah, did you know I'm a clone? I was born in a freaking glass tube. Slime and salt compounds were my parents. Brandi: ....wow, you have had it rough haven't you. Snake: Sucker! Snake pops up, whipping out his tranquilzer gun and firing. A dart launches forth, striking Brandi directly in the forehead. Brandi: Ow! The Russian's eyes cross, she stumbles drunkenly to the railing of the ship. Then with a final groan she flips overboard, landing in the water with a crash. Snake: (Reloading) Hmph. Snatching up the fallen commando's pistol, Snake heads back the way he came....a rush of intense flame greets him though as he opens the door. Snake: Uh, not that way. Turning around, the intrepid hero makes his way to the other door and heads down. Into the holds he goes, sneaking past several terrorists. Terrorist A: Yes sir. We will secure your exit, with our lives if necessary. Ivan: There is one more thing....my daughter. Keep her safe. Terrorist B: Psst, Louie, should we tell him she fell overboard? Ivan: What? What was that? Terrorist A: Nothing. Ivan: Das vida. I will be coming down now. Terrorist A: Yes sir. The man signs off, then angrily slaps his partner in the back of the head. Terrorist A: You moron! Terrorist B: What? What did I do? Terrorist A: I swear you.... Snake: AIYEEE!!!! Rather than be caught, Snake takes the approach instead of charging straight up and over the two guards while screaming like a maniac. Surprisingly it succeeds, as he runs through both men and successfully locks himself into the next room. Terrorist A: (Muffled) This is all your fault! Terrorist B: Was not! Snake: Heh heh. Pleased with himself, Solid Snake turns again....then gasps and falls over as he sees another guard right in front of him. Snake: Dammit! Hiding behind a corner, the hero looks on as an older gentleman, very familiar looking rounds a bend and approaches the lone guard. His spurs clink off the metal of the boat, standing out starkly. Terrorist: Ah, Shamahaska. Why are you here? The man known as Revolver Hosselot looks to the Russian soldier quizzically. Hosselot: What did you just call me? Terrorist: Forgive me, I was born with a slight tooth misalignment....it affects my pronunciation of certain things. Shasalaska.... Hosselot: (Shaking his head) It's Shalashaska. Shal-a-shask-a. Say it right. Terrorist: Sha....Sha....Shaherazmaska! Why are you here? I thought you were with the colonel. Hosselot: Idiot. The cowboy reaches to his side, then as quickly as a bolt of lightning whips out his single action army pistol and aims, putting one bullet cleanly through the head of the poor soldier. Hosselot: The colonel will be with you soon.....comrade. Kicking the dead body in the head, Hosselot makes his way through the door the poor man was guarding. After waiting a bit to avoid detection....Snake follows. Snake: (Huffs and puffs) Jogging along, the former GREYHOUND member heads all the way into the bowels of the ship. Here he emerges on top of a balcony, looking down at a platoon of Marines. Snake: Uh....there are a bunch of them. Snake begins to grab his ear....then thinks better of it. Snake: Second thought, I'd rather NOT talk to Bobby anymore. Sliding carefully to the ground floor, Snake looks to a huge video monitor covering the wall. The Marines look on, as their leader stands on screen speaking. Violator: I am the Violator. Prepare for total violation. I am the Violator. Snake: Huh? Very puzzled, our hero continues onward through a nearby doorway. Here he stands, walking to the video camera.....prepares to pass it, but stops. Snake: Hehe! Giggling mischievously, Solid places his fingers in front of the light emanating from the camera. On the screen a shadow appears, taking the form of a bunny. Snake: Hehehe! Snake shifts his hand a little....the shadow changes to a dog, barking playfully. Snake: Hehehehe! Again he moves....this time the image changes to what appears to be a man engaged in coitus with a bunny and a dog. At this every Marine turns around at once. Marines: Huh? Snake: (Freezing) Oh uh....don't mind me. I'm just uh....yeah. As fast as his feet can carry him Snake bolts from the room, leaving the platoon to shrug simultaniously. Marines: Hmph. Into the next chamber darts the agent....he stops and looks up. Slowly, his gaze falls upon a huge beast.....cold steel rising several stories. Snake's jaw drops. Snake: Metal Gear..... In front of the beast, the Marine commander speaks on. Violator: I am the Violator. Prepare for total violation. I am the Violator. Giving the man an odd look, Snake creeps around the machine and hides. He then takes a few pictures, snapping photographic evidence of Metal Gear. Voice: Excellent speech my friend. Violator stops short, looking on in shock as an older man steps forth, clapping his hands. All eyes fall to him, watching in disbelief. Hosselot: I might have been moved, if I had the slightest clue what the hell you were talking about. Violator: (Angry) I am the Violator! Hosselot: Yes, well.....I am Shalashaska! Also called Revolver..... Snake and Hosselot: Hosselot! Hosselot: Who said that. Snake: (Hiding) Oops. The Violator stands firm, glaring at the intruder. Violator: Prepare for total violation! Hosselot: I think not. Suddenly a hand grips Violator around the throat, a gun points to his head. Ivan Gurlucovitch holds him tightly, snarling at the Marines. Ivan: Da, you will stay back. Violator: Prepare for total violation! Hosselot: Wrong again. Hosselot motions to his right, as a flock of terrorists march into the room. They begin freeing Metal Gear from its binds, as Gurlucovitch digs his gun into the Violator's temple. Ivan: I was raised in Snezinsk, formerly known as Chelyabinsk-70, the nuclear research outpost. Hosselot: Get on with it Ivan, we've all heard this story before. Ivan: .....anyway. So basically, your country screwed mine. But now Russian will rise again. Hosselot: (Untying Metal Gear) Oh yes, I forgot to tell you comrade. I'm keeping Metal Gear for myself. Ivan: Come again? Both Gurlucovitch and the terrorists slowly look to the old man, who smiles slightly. Hosselot: Yes, I shall return it.....to the Patriots. Violator: I am the Violator! Hosselot: I still don't have the slightest clue what you're saying. Ivan: When, Hosselot? When did you turn!? Hosselot turns around, sneering evilly. Hosselot: I'm glad you noticed Comrade. Ivan: How could I not? You just turned around right now! Hosselot: ...... Ivan: ....... Hosselot: Nitwit. No matter....you, all of you will die here! Ivan: DAMN YOU!!!!! Gurlucovitch shoves the Violator forward and draws his gun. However just as he does Revolver Hosselot turns around, draws his own pistol and fires. Ivan: DYA!!!!! Blood trickles from Ivan's mouth, he falls to his knees. The Violator does the same, dying on his feet....smirking, Hosselot spins his gun on his finger. Hosselot: Looks like you were long overdue for retirement. Breaking out in a grin, Hosselot continues to whip his gun about on his fingertips....then slips, the weapon flying free. It launches into the darkness, the impact of its fall causing a single shot to ring out. Snake: AGH!!!! AGH!!!! AGH!!!!! Hosselot: What the? From out of the black hops Solid Snake, holding his foot in agony. Snake: Why the foot? Why always the foot? Hosselot: You! Suddenly Hosselot screeches in pain. He holds his arm in agony, biting down upon it.....then looks up, his expression and posture strangely changed. A familiar voice escapes the old man's lips. Liquid Clyde: It's been a while, brother. Snake: Liquid? How are you still alive? Liquid Clyde: I should ask you the same thing. Didn't I shoot you in the head? Snake: I had my mission saved. Why are you now just an arm? The last time I saw you you were a whole person. Liquid Clyde: Well..... Liquid flashes back to an earlier time, just after the events of Shadow Moses. Far from the snow of Alaska, he enjoys the pleasures of a sunny island.....adorned in black shorts with white trim, he surfs the waves. Liquid Clyde: Wheeeee! A huge wave comes at the terrorist leader, which he effortlessly climbs with his board. Rounding the top, he gives a hearty yell of glee....which turns to pain as he vanishes beneath the waves. Liquid Clyde: No, NO!!!!! Under the endless water the man is pulled....blood seeps to the surface, staining the blue water red. After several minutes, a shark surfaces and moves its head about. Shark: Hey....where's the cream filling? And a short time later, on the beach a little bit away various flotsam floats to the sands....trash, refuse. And.....one severed arm. The limb comes to a rest on the beach head.....as it does, a man runs up and snatches it, sneering and waving his own nub of an arm. Hosselot: Yoink. Away skips the old Russian, gleefully laughing as he makes his way down the shore..... Liquid Clyde: So, yes. Snake: Uh..... Liquid Clyde: In any case..... Suddenly a massive explosion rips behind Liquid Clyde/Hosselot. He looks curiously, then glances at Snake. Snake: What? Liquid Clyde: (Looks) Snake: What? What are you looking at? It's not like I pushed random buttons on the control panel upstairs and blew a hole in the ship. Liquid Clyde: (Looks) Snake: (Hangs his head in shame) Liquid Clyde: I believe it's time to get while the getting is good. Hiya! With a mighty leap, Clyde bounds to the top of Metal Gear Kreller. He then climbs into the cockpit, snapping it shut. Liquid Clyde: DIE!!!!!! The huge robot stands tall.....an ungodly (yet strangely whiny) shriek erupts from it. Metal Gear then leaps into the air just a little, crashing down and tearing a hole straight through the bottom of the boat. Snake: AGH!!!! AGH!!!!! AGH!!!!! The hero attempts to swim to safety, but it is no use. His body is dragged by the pull of the water, down into the briney deeps...... Bobby: Snake! SNAAAAAAAKE!!!!! ......dammit, I needed to ask you how to get around a restraining order. Now what do I do..... The tanker sinks beneath the waves, collapsing in on itself.....balls of fire burst from it, a massive conflagration erupting as we fade to black...... To Be Continued
