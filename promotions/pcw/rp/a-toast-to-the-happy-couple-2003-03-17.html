A Toast To The Happy Couple

17 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 17 March 2003 March Madness ‘What does this mean?’ Well the war has silenced and the dust has settled. All that is left to do is inspect the aftermath. Champions have been dethroned and their victors installed as replacement monarchs, but the question that hangs on the tongue of everybody associated with Primetime Championship Wrestling be it the stars, the staff and even the fans is, ‘What now?’ Well Teresa Tomas ran a marathon against her opponent Tim Walden, falling short as she reached the final stretch and so begins a new era for the Television Championship, an era that once again involves The Front Office. New Champions soon became the trend as the self-proclaimed ‘Lyrical Messiah’ Dante Jackson defeated now former Champion Totally Tubular Tommy in a Fatal Four-Way bout that also involved Venneman Pendragon and Rent-a-Hero. With titles changing hands you could only imagine the jovial locker room of Chris Storm and Tessa Windsor who’s Tag Team Championships were given pardon for March Madness, however such a luxury never found its way to the top of the corporate ladder. By that I mean the World Heavyweight Championship was put on the line. The match, an event that never once favoured the Champion. Thirty superstars of Primetime Championship Wrestling wrestled in an Over The Top Rope Battle Royal. The victor, a man making his last stand in open country, ‘The Malice Man’ Duke Williams but through the celebrations there was one other event that transcended this in terms of importance, The televised wedding of Chris Storm and Tessa Windsor Or The impromptu appearance of Pranks With Attitude during that same ceremony Where there’s Pranks With Attitude, mischief isn’t too far behind and March Madness was no exception. As the besotted couple exchanged vows, unknown to them they were playing lead in yet another prank, which was scripted by the Conniving Collective. Those who watched will know that the priest turned out to be the masquerading Jester who quenched the proceedings in a way he only knows how, buying enough time for Nascosto Ireul Nerezza to make an unexpected appearance. I think Jeff Marx said it best, “Where there is smoke there’s fire and where there is Gaz there’s Rezz!” However, what Pranks With Attitude didn’t expect was Chris Storm’s swerve as he informed his nemesis that their actions had just cost them any opening for a rematch. As long as Storm and Windsor are in control of the titles, then P.W.A will not be granted their opportunity to become five time PCW Tag Team Champions, coincidentally the benchmark set by ‘The Blood Brothers’ and ‘Da Westsidaz’. So how do the pranksters plan to overcome this obstacle? Good question. It is now a few hours after the gruelling event of March Madness and the scene opens within a dimly lit room. The room itself is incredibly spacious with circular tables littering the edge of what looks to be a dance floor and the source of what light that is available flickers from the rafters in an array of different colours. The lights jig and bounce about as though they were moving to music but the room sits in an eerie silence. Looming over the dance floor is a raised pedestal draped in an over-hanging white tablecloth and sitting above the fabric is a three-tiered wedding cake, which coincidentally answers any questions with regards to the location. “Nice of you to join us squire!” The voice that filters through the silence belongs to a silhouette that sits off centre from the wedding cake and seems to be accompanied by two other members who sit motionless. The assailant gently tapped his hands together and on queue the lights came on unveiling the person to be PCW’s resident Jester Gaz Green who sits beneath a large banner with the bold lettering of, ‘CONGRATULATIONS TO THE HAPPY COUPLE!’ With the lights illuminating the room we can clearly make out the two other silhouettes as those of two sex aid dolls who have been touched up (Not in the literal sense) to look like the persons they’re portraying. In this case a blonde wig for the male to represent Chris Storm and a brunette for the Tessa Windsor doll. “Really that banner could quite easily be deemed false advertising!” said the Emerald Menace as he pointed towards the happy couple banner that hangs over his head. “What do you two think? Oh, that’s right you two missed your own reception because one, your most probably nursing your wounds in a Las Vegas medical facility and two, your not a happy couple!” “Since you two couldn’t be here tonight we decided to substitute you in favour of using these dolls who in my opinion have more personality!” continued the Front Office Jester smiling evilly into the camera lens. The camera then focuses on the lifeless dolls as they sit slumped into their seats, their mouths gaping open, their bits only partially covered by the height of the table. The Jester meanwhile sits smugly wearing a black suit that makes for a welcome change to his usual forest green business attire, sitting smartly awaiting his queue he plays with the flower on his lapel before beginning again. “Seriously – You two dimwits think you’ve gone one up over Pranks With Attitude by incorporating some stipulation into your airtime thus preventing us from facing you for what is our rightful property, the PCW Tag Team Championship!” The Jester erupts into a series of hysterics when suddenly a projectile fell from the rafters. Not just some piece of debris, standing erect in a crimson red funeral suit is the behemoth of Pranks With Attitude, Nascosto Ireul Nerezza whose presence and entry made the cameraman step back in fear. Fidgeting with his talons, the beast leaned forward allowing the camera to capture his burning red eyes and saliva soaked canines. “We can go about this two ways…” injected the Living Nightmare. “Firstly we could interfere in a title defence and cause you to lose the gold and thus your childish court order would be deemed annulled or…” The vampire pauses momentarily to add a feel of intimidation to his comments. “Or we can make your lives hell for the next few weeks. So Chris Storm and Tessa Windsor, while your lying next to one another feeding from the same drip of morphine and murmuring sweet nothings through the only gap of your mouths that doesn’t hurt I’d suggest paying attention to the rest of this tape… As they say, there is no time like the present!” Backing off from the camera we can see the Jester standing erect in the background with a cringe etched along his face. Putting his hand under the table Gaz Green removed four tins of paint and aided by Nerezza they threw the content all over the room, smearing the dance floor in green and purple. Not contempt with just graffiti the devious duo then proceeded to smash up the furniture as wooden debris littered the ground and the room looked completely vandalised. “Would you like the honours squire?” questioned the Jester, his eyes rolling towards the three-tiered wedding cake. Nerezza grunted his response and taking hold of the base of the cake he lifted and with a false look of sympathy he simply tipped it over ridiculing the floor further. With the building in chaos the only freestanding things left to destroy were the two rubber dolls but as Gaz ushered over with a pin at hand, Nerezza once again injected himself. “NOOOOOOOOO~! Somebody else would like the honours…” “Huh?!?” Suddenly the silence was disrupted by the sound of hissing as Fluffy appeared alongside his vampire master. With a nod the ball of fluff spluttered and from its mouth came a piercing tongue that impales the head of the Chris Storm doll, deflating it in the process and the same applies to that of the Tessa Windsor doll. “Good shooting Tex!!!” jovially cried the Lord Of Laughter. “What a shame, this reception is just like Storm and Windsor’s relationship… A MESS!!!” concluded the Angel Of Fear. With the concluding comments of PCW’s resident bloodsucker the scene slowly drew to a closure as the image eventually faded to black.
