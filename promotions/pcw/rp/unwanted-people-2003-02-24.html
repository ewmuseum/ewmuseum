Unwanted People

24 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2/24/03 Trepidation overcame Brianna every time she began to think about her developing involvement in Kramer’s predicament. It seemed that in just two short months her life had turned from something mediocre and small, to a huge jumble. She started out as a hopeful. Wanting greatly to become a big inventor. Along the side she had been training and developing her wrestling abilities which eventually got her a contract deal at PCW, a dominant wrestling federation. This addition made her life more complete, but also added on stress as well as pain. Now when she has just found her niche this whole ordeal about protecting Grimer the Mouse has come up and it goes far beyond anything she has ever had to endure before. The risk with this addition is her existence. There Brianna stood on the sidewalk outside of the Beauty Salon wearing a black silk shirt and pants which contrasted nicely with her newly cut reddish brown hair. She stood there for several minutes patrolling the streets obviously waiting for someone or something. Several cars whizzed by with immature males who whistled and threw out vulgar remarks, but Brianna didn’t let the lewd remarks bother her too much. “Assholes…” she whispered while managing to keep a consistent smile flashing her pearly whites. Several more minutes went by and Brianna began to grow irritated as she shuffled her feet trying to keep the pain of standing so long to a minimum. “Where in the world is that taxi? I called over twenty minutes ago.” Brianna says while watching the people pass by her at a constant pace. She could have been home by now, but Milo had insisted that his need for her car was more important, so Brianna agreed to lend it to him. If she knew this would have happened, she probably would have given Milo some change to catch the bus. Sometimes the good guys get the short end of the stick. “Finally!” Brianna let’s out as a yellow taxicab pulls up in front of the salon. Brianna hastily goes to the back door and pulls it open. She steps inside and takes a seat closing the door and putting on her seatbelt. Though she was overcome by the musty smell of the cab, she agrees that this is better than waiting outside any longer. “Sorry miss but traffic was backed up a few miles back. I hope you didn’t have to wait too long.” the Arab sounding man said as he pulled away from the curb and into traffic. “Oh that’s fine.” Brianna replies agreeably while peering out of her soiled window. “So where to?” “Fifth and State please.” The atmosphere in the cab becomes completely silent giving Brianna some leeway to rush into thought. On Turmoil I battle in yet another opening match against three newcomers to PCW. It’s good to see some fresh aspiring faces, particularly women. It’s really cool to see PCW pull in some more females because the testosterone in the locker room is way too thick. Ah but that’s just another match, nothing real important about it. If I win I win, if I lose, well I lose. Friday is where the real challenge begins. On Friday I face Skitzo for a title I think I am well ready for. The first go around at it, I wasn’t prepared enough, and I was actually happy I didn’t win because I just wasn’t ready. But this time I am, and I am going to bring all I got and hopefully gain another win and a title as well. Brianna’s thoughts are interrupted as she hears a hissing sound coming from the front area of the cab. She looks up to see a mangled black and white colored cat clawing at the divider looking at something to Brianna’s right. Still feeling a bit shocked by the sudden appearance; Brianna manages to shift her eyes to her side and sees Grimer walking all around the backseat. “Sorry ma’am, Hissy must have seen something. He doesn’t normally act like this.” the cab driver says apologetically not noticing the mouse prancing around in back. “No problem.” she replies managing to scoop Grimer up and set him softly back into her black leather purse which is well accommodated for his needs. Several more minutes pass by and they are finally at Brianna’s destination. “So how much do I owe you?” Brianna asks while pulling out her wallet ready to pay for his services. “Nothing.” “Nothing? How so?” “Well I was wondering if we could make a trade. You see, I am not yet a legal US citizen, and I need a way to stay in the states. The only way I know of is to marry a US citizen. So become my bride and the ride is free.” He turns back to flash a toothy smile, but Brianna has already split not caring any bit for what he had to say. The man’s smile slowly fades to a frown as he notices this. “It wouldn’t have worked out anyway.” he tries to convince himself as he drives off. The scene cuts from the cab driving off to Brianna, who is walking briskly down the sidewalk in a rhythmic fashion. A man reading a comic book walks right passed her and then stops as he recognizes her face. “Brianna?” Brianna turns around and seems a little perturbed at the man standing there. I mean you would too if you saw him. He was wearing a checkered shirt tucked tightly into dark blue high-water pants held up by suspenders. To top it off he was wearing the ever so famous “Geek Glasses” (thick, black, with tape in the middle). “Kurby….” Brianna says lifelessly. “So how have things been?” he says obviously trying to impress her by crossing his arms over his chest, which is puffed out as far as his scrawny body can manage. “Things have been ok. What about you? Still living at home with your mom?” she asks getting a few chuckles from passing women who look at Kurby, which evolves into more laughing. “I’m my own man now, I live by myself.” “Kurby it doesn’t count if you live upstairs from her.” “Oh…” “Well I have to get going now.” Brianna throws out trying to find a way to get away from this walking asthma case. “Hey I was wondering, do you think maybe we could go the Comic Book World and spend the day reading the latest addition of Kryptoman Unlimited?” “Are you asking me on a date?” “Yes but I prefer the term rendezvous.” He replies in a poor excuse of a French accent. “We tried this once before Kurby and I didn’t like you then, and unfortunately now isn’t any different.” Brianna turns to walk away. “They say once you go Kurby, you always go back.” he throws out, hurt by the rejection. “I believe the correct terminology is once you go Kurby you’re scarred for life.” a man laughs emerging from a coffee shop with a newspaper under his arm. “And who are you?” “I’m Brianna’s boyfriend.” Upon hearing this, Brianna whips around and is disgusted to see Thomas standing there. “Thomas just stop.” she pleads. “Don’t worry baby I got this under control.” He winks at her and then redirects his attention to Kurby who looks as determined as ever. Not wanting to witness a confrontation, or look at these men any longer, Brianna takes off down the sidewalk. After nearly making Kurby cry due to insults, Thomas catches up to Brianna who just looks at him and shakes her head. “I was saving you from nerdman back there. I thought you would be appreciative.” “You’re a real jerk you know that?” Those are the last words spoken between the two as Brianna quickly crosses the street leaving Thomas just standing there. “I wait and pray for the day we’re back together Bri.” he whispers to the sky as he walks away. The scene slowly fades to black.
