Image Is Everything: Part 2

6 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: May 6, 2003 We last left Tyler Preston as he was receiving some image consulting from John Gargano’s agent. He has some great ideas to turn Tyler into a star. All they have to do is find the right image, and then the world can say hello to the new and improved Tyler Preston. It’s that easy, right? The scene fades in to a trendy clothing store. Many non-conforming patrons scatter around the store looking at off colored clothing with a retro look to it. Tech music blares providing a low thud for the bass. Mick Varelli, John Gargano, and of course Tyler Preston enter from the main entrance and instantly look like fish out of water. Unlike the others, they wear normal casual wear that consists of T-shirts and jeans. They receive many stares and gawks from the ‘culturally experienced’ shoppers, but continue on their business. Mick leads them around the store, attempting to locate clothing that will spice up Tyler’s image to go along with his new attitude. “John, why would Mick take me to one of these trendy clothing stores. If I would have known this is what would go on, I wouldn’t have agreed to this,” Tyler says in disgust. He eyes the countless racks of trendy clothing that is considered ‘hot’. Tyler doesn’t get it. He doesn’t even think that half of the merchandise in the store would be worthy using as a handkerchief. “Relax Tyler, Mick has given life to the careers of many people. He knows what he is doing. I’m sure that he has a good reason for bringing you here,” John assures him. He has had many experiences with Mick Varelli before and therefore has become accustomed to the methods of his madness. That doesn’t help for Tyler who is feeling well out of place in this territory. “Yea, but to a clothing store? I thought we were creating an image for me, not shopping around like a bunch of little middle school girls,” Tyler says slyly. He was happy that Mick had agreed to help him, but now his mind is changing rapidly. “Hey, clothing has a lot to do with the image. You must not get out much. Just take a good look at me. Anyone would know that I’m a great actor just by the clothes that I wear,” John explains as if he has had a lot of experience with the matter. Tyler rolls his eyes and John, knowing that John doesn’t know as much as he is pretending he does. “That’s a pretty ugly way to say ‘I’m a good actor’ if I do say so myself,” Tyler jokes, trying not to let John think that he is right. Tyler gazes at John’s ridiculous, all-black attire. John looks down at himself after Tyler’s comment, but is content with his clothing. He doesn’t see anything wrong with it. “Alright Tyler, the very basis of any image is to get the outer appearance to fit the role. There are many directions that we can take you in. I’ll try a few things that might work first,” Mick finally says after quickly browsing around the store. He drags Tyler over to a rack with black leather attire hanging from it. Tyler looks at it in disgust, knowing that he would never wear anything to that nature. John follows with a big smile, happy that Mick is manipulating someone other than himself. “I’m not sure if this is the type of clothing that I would wear just about every day,” Tyler attempts to convince Mick. He can’t picture himself in leather and figures that he would look like a wannabe biker if he so much as attempted to. “Something has got to change Tyler. You’ve been wandering around the stage in that red bow tie and suspenders for quite sometime now. You have to refresh things by sprucing up your outer appearance,” Mick snaps back, criticizing Tyler’s favorite outfit. Tyler thinks it over, and is happy with his outfit that has come to be normal for him. He is closed minded on the matter and already has his mind made up. “I’d rather wear the same old thing every day of the week instead of getting in to one of these things. Why the black leather?” Tyler asks, wondering what on earth Mick could be thinking by even suggesting such an absurd thing. “It goes with the image. Face it Tyler, even though you are a funny and gentle guy on the stage, you are supposed to be thought as a major threat. E2 is one of the hottest things in professional wrestling and you have to start acting tough,” Mick explains as he sorts through the various leather attire. The pieces of clothing screech as they rub together. “Look at me Mick, I’m not the biggest guy that you have seen. I can’t really pass for a big and tough bruiser,” Tyler explains. Mick glances at his 6’3 body that holds 225 pounds. Mick sizes Tyler up and grabs a pair of black leather pants to go with a vest of the same color and material. Tyler rolls his eyes in disgust that Mick won’t take anything that he says in to account. “You won’t be big and tough with that attitude. You especially won’t if you prance around in a bow tie. Now try these two things on and I guarantee that you will like the change,” Mick convinces as he shoves the leather material into Tyler’s face. Tyler has no choice but to grab them as he turns and stomps to the dressing room doors. Mick and John both smile, finding the matter a little humorous. “Man, that guy is tough to shop with. He acts like a four year old,” John says jokingly as Mick continues to scour the store for anything else that could spice up Tyler’s image. “It wouldn’t hurt for you to look around either. There are a lot of movies coming out as we near summer. If you want to be in any of those, you should think about making some changes to,” Mick comments in a less than friendly voice. John’s eyebrows jet down over his eyes. He felt confident about his career until that small jab from Mick. “Tyler’s working me just about every day getting me in shape for ‘To the Ring and Back’. I’ll be ready for that, and things should go well from there, right?” John asks, wanting conformation from a man that has some clout on the matter. Mick just nods as he examines a pair of neon colored sweat pants. John feels uneasy, but the mood is lightened as Tyler busts out of the blue colored dressing room door with his leather attire on. He frowns as he walks with a squeak over to the duo. The short leather vest exposes his cleanly shaven chest. The pants squeeze tightly to his stocky thighs. “That is the look that we have been looking for. What do you think of that Tyler?” Mick asks in an energetic voice. Apparently he thinks that this is the missing piece of the ‘Tyler Preston Success Puzzle’. “I think that I look like a goober. There is no way that I’m going anywhere in this get-up. Not even to the wrestling arena,” Tyler says stubbornly as he looks down at himself and still finds himself in amazement on how stupid it looks. “I don’t know Tyler, you do look more like a professional wrestler with those clothes on. If I didn’t know any better, I would say you look like the World Champion at first glance,” John says with a crooked smile on his face. He pressures Tyler to lean more towards wrestling then with comedy. Deep down he wants a partner that strikes fear in the hearts of all opponents so he does not have to. Tyler doesn’t pay any heed to John’s empty compliment. “I don’t think any of us are on the same page. I don’t want to look like a professional wrestler just because I am a professional wrestler. I’m a comedian Mick, I want to make people laugh. This thing will scare little kids away,” Tyler says as a stale joke. He isn’t in the mood for full-fledged laughter. He wants Mick to realize what he is saying. “Ok Tyler, it’s your choice. If you don’t want to be intimidating then that’s fine,” Mick says in disappointment, trying to score some pity from Tyler. Tyler is unsympathetic and doesn’t mind shooting down Mick’s so called ‘genius idea’. “That’s right, because I don’t want to be intimidating. It’s kind of hard to make people laugh when they are all running away from you in fear,” Tyler says flatly as he turns around to head back to the dressing room. Mick stops him before he can begin the journey and hands him the neon green sweat suit that he had been examining. “Here, just try this one. Funny is what you want correct? This should do the trick,” Mick says as he tosses it at Tyler. Tyler rolls his eyes once again, realizing that it was indeed a mistake to allow Mick to have so much power. Mick isn’t disheartened by a single ruined idea, so he continues on, trying to make Tyler in to a star. Tyler sighs an evident deep breathe to send a signal to Mick as he walks back to the dressing room with the new outfit in his hands. He slams the door behind him, causing a few patrons of the store to turn their heads to find the source of the disturbance. “Is there anything that I should check out?” John inquires, beginning to feel a little left out of the ‘fun’ that Mick and Tyler are enjoying. “Actually no. We got your sizes from the last fitting and you’re bound to get a whole new wardrobe for the wrestling movie coming in on any week now,” Mick says. This excites John. One of his favorite parts of doing movies is keeping the wardrobe after. John understands the situation, but wanders around looking at a few things anyway. “Are you kidding me?” Tyler’s voice carries all the way from behind the blue dressing room door. John laughs at Tyler’s expense and Mick just shrugs. “The man is never satisfied,” Mick says under his breath as he moves on to the shoe department of the store. He grabs a pair of fiery red sneakers as Tyler charges out of the dressing room decked out in a bright lime jump suit. Tyler himself even has to laugh at how this looks. Mick doesn’t see anything funny about it as he sizes it up. “Wow, you look you should be dangled over the brim of a glass of Corona,” John says with a booming laugh. He doesn’t score big laughter from the peanut gallery, but he is still satisfied at the joke he found to be very tasteful. Tyler stares at Mick who is actually giving this outfit some thought. “There is no way that I could ever use this on the stage. I would look like a clown. What are you trying to pull here?” Tyler questions as if this is the dumbest thing anyone has ever done to him. Tyler has already made up his mind, and it doesn’t even matter what Mick will say at this moment. “It’s very urban,” Mick says with his fingers to his chin as if he is really intelligent on the matter. “You’re right Mick, this is very hip-hop style. I saw a special on ‘BET’ and bright colors are what all the gangsters are wearing,” John says, making sure that any garbage that he spews out of his mouth will comply with what Mick is saying. Mick nods in agreement, thinking he can turn Tyler in to a pop star. Tyler has doubts the size of Mick’s ego. “Do I look like the ‘Original Kings of Comedy’ to you? You expect me to pull of a Cedric ‘The Entertainer’ look?” Tyler asks in joking words, but takes the matter very seriously. He wants to honestly know if Mick is serious about the entire deal, or if this is just another one of John’s pranks. “It’s not the content of your act, it’s the type of people that you appeal to. This will bring you a more diverse crowd,” Mick explains, proving somewhat of a point, but Tyler isn’t falling in to that trap. John nods in agreement. “You know what? I think our little shopping spree is over. I’m going to stick with the Knoxville Native gimmick and wear by red bow tie every single day,” Tyler says as he stomps back to the locker room. John and Mick shake their heads in dejection. “Come on Tyler, don’t give up, we were making huge leaps,” John attempts to bring Tyler back. Tyler doesn’t even turn around to acknowledge him. He knows that this whole image workshop thing was nothing but a waste of time. “He’ll come crawling back, John, just like they always do. Once he falls right on his face, he’ll be back for advice,” Mick says in a sinister voice, almost as if he is planning something. He sincerely believes that, but on the other side, Tyler believes that he will remain successful. Tyler enters the dressing room and slams the door one last time. John and Mick raise their eyebrows at each other and give a little shrug. They make their way to the main exit and leave Tyler changing in the dressing room. The scene fades out.
