Promise Kept

25 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2/25/02 Scene shows Warden Riley and his Security Chief sitting aboard their prison transport plane. They are leaving Calgary after this week's Sunday Sabotage. The men are obviously ecstatic over 'Swamp Dawg' Karl LeBlanc's monumental victory to capture the PCW Extreme Title. Security Chief: Yes, sir... this is what it's all about Warden. In the back of this plane... we're transporting the Extreme Champ of Primetime Championship Wrestling! Warden: Did you doubt him? Security Chief: HELL NO! I'll tell ya what though... all that training you put him through last week must have paid off. I could tell he was wrestling much smarter, not telegraphing his moves anymore. Warden Riley: Yeah, did you like those new submission holds I taught him? Security Chief: Oh, man... those moves are gonna make a huge difference. Now he has a few moves in his arsenal to really wear down those opponent's. LeBlanc's lucky to have a former NCAA wrestling champion like yourself as his manager. Warden: Well, he's not there yet. He's still incomplete. But give me time. I'm gonna teach this sumbitch to wrestle like a real champ. A World Champ! Security Chief: You think Swamp's ready for a World title run? Warden: No, no, no. He's still quite a way off before we worry about that. I figure he'll be poised for PCW's grand prize by next year's Shanghaied. It should be very symbolic, since LeBlanc made his first PCW appearance at Shanghaied. Security Chief: Well, I dunno Warden... you do seem to have a knack for telling the future. After all, you predicted LeBlanc's victory last night. Warden: That's right. I knew Swamp was ready, especially after all that training we did. Hell, I put LeBlanc through the ringer for almost two weeks straight. That training ring we bought turned out to be a great investment. Security Chief: You better believe it. Hey, I hope Hank took your advice. Warden: What's that? Security Chief (laughing): Remember, you told Hank he should log on to the PCW website and print out a copy of the main page so he could remember what it looked like to have his face among the other champions atop the page. Warden: Oh yeah, that's right. And I promised that come Monday morning it would be LeBLANC'S face atop that PCW webpage. Security Chief: And you were right. I checked out the website a little while ago... and sure enough, I saw Swamp's big mug up there with the other champs. Warden: Yeah, and to think... he captured his first Title less than a month after his debut. How many wrestlers can say they won a championship in only their fifth match as a professional. This man has superstar written all over him. Security Chief (nodding): The sky's the limit. Warden Riley and his Security Chief walk to the back of the plane. They stop and stare at the holding cell in the plane's rear. They gaze through the bars to see Karl LeBlanc sitting on the floor in the dark. He is tightly clutching his new PCW Extreme Title and murmuring to himself. The scene fades as the plane lifts off the runway.
