Date: Dec.6th/02 The more I think about it, the more I begin to realize that thinking about it won’t help . . . And the more I try not to think about, the more I think about it . . . It’s just a vicious cycle that I must break . . . ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ It was eight in the morning and I was trying to think up some excuse to get out of bed. Outside, the ground was white, and the wind swept the snow up into tiny tornados. The grey clouds in the sky prevented any sun from reaching the Earth, leaving it cold and lonely. It was one of those mornings where you just don’t want to move . . . you could just stay in the warmth of your blankets til night came once more. I could go for a walk . . . I could go train . . . I could go do anything I wanted too . . . But I just laid there in bed . . . not moving an inch . . . My mind was racing . . . I was wide awake . . . but I didn’t want to move. What could I do? So, I made a compromise. As I laid there in bed, I began to think about my up coming weekend. Two shows . . . two matches . . . Mark Amulet . . . twice! Thinking about Amulet stirred demons within my soul. My blood began to boil, and a feverish sweat came across my brow. That man . . . that lowly, insignificant man . . . How could he effect me so? What was it about him that churned my stomach? Why does the sound of his name create in me such a primitive rage? Just the thought of that man made me sick . . . and what made that feeling worse is that I’m being forced, once again, to team up with him. The last time we had a match like that, I had to do all the work, and we ended up losing. Not that I’m saying I didn’t try hard enough, or that I’m not good enough . . . I’m just saying that a Michigan/Amluet alliance will not, and will never, work. I guess this is just the Front Office’s way of keeping powerful threats off their backs. As long as me and Amulet are at each other’s throats, we won’t cause any problems for the CEO and his pals. Well . . . after I finish of Amulet once and for all . . . the Front Office will have no choice but watch their backs . . . cuz you can’t keep a good Michigan down forever! ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ With all those thoughts coursing through my brain, I felt motivated more than ever to get out of bed, and make the best of a gloomy day. After washing up a little, I decided to treat myself to the fanciest breakfast a wrestling bachelor could make for himself. So I went to the cupboards and drawers, and fixed myself a continental breakfast. Scrambled eggs . . . Eggo waffles . . . blueberry of course . . . Chocolate milk . . . low fat or bust . . . Bacon . . . as far as the eye can see . . . And to top it off . . . a piping hot serving of home fries . . . just like mom used to make them . . . This was the breakfast to end all breakfasts! You couldn’t buy as nice a breakfast at any fancy-shmancy restaurant if you wanted too! Truly a breakfast fit for a king! ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ After filling my gut, I decided to go for a little walk to clear my head. I needed some fresh air to cleanse my soul, and some frigid winter wind to blow away all the negative energy surrounding me. If I was to win the tag match for my team, I’d have to be in the right frame of mind. I didn’t need all these feelings of hatred toward Amulet clouding my judgement in the ring. I just had to keep my eyes on the prize and go for the gold . . . figuratively speaking. Anywho, I grabbed my coat and boots and headed out the door. Just as I had expected, the wind blew sharply across my cheek, and cold shivers shot up and down my spine. Just the jolt of freshness I needed to wake me up completely. DAMN YOU AMULET! No, I have to stop thinking about that son of a bitch . . . I SHOULD HAVE BEEN THE EXTREME CHAMPION! Gotta stop thinking about the past . . . STUPID AMULET . . .WHY COULDN’T HE BUG SOMEONE ELSE FOR A CHANGE!? Gotta start thinking about the future . . . I’M GONNA PUT AN END TO AMULET’S IDIOCIES! Dammit, I just gotta put up with this shit for one match . . . it can’t be that hard can it? I’ve done it before . . . but we lost . . . THIS HAS GOT TO STOP! But when . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . SOON!
