Dark- The Color of Skin AND The New Gimmick?!

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: With Expert Skill The scene opens up in a small dressing room where we see Melanie, girlfriend of Rush Mason, waiting outside. She is tapping her heel impatiently against the edge of the door. Melanie: Let’s go! A voice resounds from inside, but it is a male voice, a familiar voice… Voice: I’m coming, hold on! Suddenly, the door opens, and out walks none other than Rush Mason! The newly re-hired PCW’er is looking regular except for the fact that there is a pink wig on top of his black do rag that covers his head. Melanie: You can’t be serious… Rush: What?! I can become Rushdust! See look! Rush now brings back his arms and makes a sucking noise, much like the noise made by a certain Gold attired man in the WWE. Melanie: I can’t believe I’m going out with a queer… Rush: Fine! No Rushdust, but I need a new attitude, I mean hating white people is fun, but it gets old. Melanie: Well, you don’t have to be gay to have a new gimmick! Rush: I’m not so sure, I mean look at New Breed. They got together and what little heterosexuality was left went right out the wedding door. Snow’s the dominatrix, always telling them that he’s better. It’s sick really. Melanie just looks at Rush wide-eyed; half believing what she is hearing and half thinking that her boyfriend may be on crack. Either way, the two start walking towards another part of the store, Rush with his arm around Melanie. Rush: So what should be the NEW Rush Mason to go with the NEW PCW? Melanie: How about winning some matches for a change? Rush rolls his eyes and continues to walk throughout the mall and the two walk past a store display at Hot Topic. Featured is a red boa and a strange looking shirt that says “Jesus Made Me Do It.” Rush’s eyes light up with an idea. Rush: I KNOW! I could be controlled by Jesus! Hey, it works for Kannon with that Hubber guy. Melanie: Why would Jesus control you? Rush: Because…he only has jobbers to control? Melanie just continues to walk, ignoring Rush’s thoughts that are out loud. Rush: Well how the hell am I supposed to figure out who the NEW Rush Mason is if you won’t even listen to my great ideas? Melanie: Great ideas? How is being controlled by the Son of God a great idea? Rush: Well, it’s original, and at least it’s something, so that already makes me better than Mike Frampton. Melanie: If you weren’t better than Mike Frampton I would’ve stayed at OG’s place. Rush: Well…I wouldn’t have blamed you if I were bad enough to NOT be FSW’s best wrestler, especially when Jobbylicious is your only competition. Melanie steps into Hot Topic and Rush looks at her puzzled, wondering why she is stepping into the strange store. Rush: What are you doing? This store is for people who worship shoehorns and stuff like that. Melanie: Don’t be such a baby, I’m just going to look for one thing. Rush: What could you possibly want out of this store? A shrine made in memory of the greatness of Kevlar or something? Melanie just ignores Rush and steps further into the store, out of Rush’s view. Sacrificing most of his dignity, Rush follows her into the store, noticing the vast shelves of miscellaneous things that would not be in a normal store. Melanie’s voice comes from the back of the store, calling out to Rush. Melanie: Rush, come check this out! Rush reluctantly agrees, walking towards the back of the store, looking for sight of the blond hair of his girlfriend. Finally he spies her leaning over looking at something on a shelf. Rush: C’mon, those white people with pierced jugular veins are starting to look at me funny, and I feel pretty weird. This do rag might not leave here alive if we’re not out of here soon! Melanie: Calm down, check this out though.She picks up a small silver pentagram necklace, and Rush begins to back away as she holds it up. Rush: I’m NOT going dark jobber! Melanie: I’m not asking you to, I’m asking you to live a little! Why don’t you just attend one of those meetings where they sacrifice things? Rush: BECAUSE I’M ONE OF THOSE THINGS! Melanie: That’s not true, those guys are just looking for a little fun. Rush: Oh, sorry, I forgot how fun the human barbecue tends to be for the shish kabob. Melanie just shakes her head back at Rush, almost having given up trying to get any fun out of Rush. Melanie: Here, how about we just go to one little gathering, or we can even just talk to one of the guys in PCW, I just don’t want you to keep living like white people inserted sticks into your ass. Rush: Well, technically, during the years of slavery… Melanie shushes him with the swipe of a hand. Melanie: I don’t want to hear it. You’re going to do SOMETHING about investigating dark religions and practices. Rush: Fine, but only because I’m such an open-minded individual. Melanie: Right, that’s why you just pounced on the idea of trying something new? The two begin to walk out the store and the conversation between two of Hot Topic’s salesclerks can be heard. One is a pink-haired girl wearing a shirt that is half-sweater half-tank top in bright green with a skirt made up of what looks like newspaper articles. The male clerk has blue streaks in his half-buzz cut and half mullet hair cut and is wearing an army t-shirt with orange shorts that are tight but stretch down almost to his feet. Male Clerk: Man, what was that guy doing in here? Female Clerk: Yeah, we shouldn’t let weirdoes like that into our store again, they’ll hurt sales. The two go back to work while the irony sinks in on and you and the irony sinks so far that it makes the scene fade to black…
