Date: MATCHWRITER READ INSIDE! (There'll be one more from me yet up in a few minutes so keep that in mind.) It was over. If your clueless, I had just finished my detective work and had stopped Project 84, a bomb that could've set the world in terror. As we ended, I was left unconscience as a small explosion went off as my brother, Sven fled away. Waking up in the hospital, I found out that one of the PCW staff who was working on the airplane called the ambulance and got me to safety. Besides a few cuts and bruises, I was ready as ever for my Rising Star title match on Friday. I was ready and confident that a new champion would be crowned and that the new champion would be me. I was in the hospital for a day, just to rest and to make sure that nothing was broken on me. To be honest, I felt great. I felt I needed to record all the events that had went through leading up to me stopping Project 84 so I grabbed my journal and used the food tray in front of me for support. I grabbed the pen that hung inside the journal and wrote a whole recap of my detective work: It all started with the Missionary back in New York. The guy told me about a mysterious person, Ivan Chistov who had stolen four million dollars from the Hare Krishna for an unknown reason. After disagreeing at first, I finally decided that I would take the job and locate this Ivan Chistov to get the money back. Using my trusty database program on my computer, I typed in the name of Ivan Chistov, which popped up this profile: Name: Ivan Chistov Age: 36 Other Aliases: Freddy Mystique, ???, ???, ??? Hometown: Russia Current Job: ??? Other Information: After being kicked out of the CIA in 1999, Ivan Chistov's location was never relocated until in 2001. In 2001, a phone call was intercepted from an Ivan Chistov that was being sent to one, Henry Clavel. The phone message script went like this: Ivan: Henry, I found four million dollars. Henry: Good, I'll see you soon. The phone went dead. After that, no information on Ivan Chistov has been found since. That one profile of Ivan Chistov set me on the right path in finding this mysterious person. One thing I noticed in the profile was that Ivan Chistov had five different aliases. I was now on the right path with two of those aliases. Excited to get the mission over with, I entered in the second name I had received, Freddy Mystique to get this profile pop up: Name: Freddy Mystique Age: 34 Other Aliases: Ivan Chistov, ???, ???, ???, ???, ??? Current Job: Scam Artist as a fortune teller. Other Information: Freddy Mystique, who also goes under the name of Ivan Chistov is very dangerous and is considered armed. His relationship with con artist, Henry Claval in unknown, but Freddy's last appearance was in 1998 when he opened his own fortune telling business, taking customers money and shooting them if they made any struggle. Freddy's business was stopped by the cops in 1999 when they found out his scam. After getting released from jail by an unknown accomplice on a small bond, Freddy Mystique seemed to disappear. This time was when Freddy Mystique was thought to disappear and turn into Ivan Chistov. No other information on Freddy Mystique. With this, I was hot on the trail. I remembered thinking that this mission would be easy and that I would have it wrapped up within days. Boy was I wrong. Not only was I just beginning my mission, but more names began to pop up. Who was this 'Henry Claval' that I had read about in these profiles. I knew that this Henry must have some relevance in the mission so I popped in his name: Name: Henry Claval Age: 38 Other Aliases: None Known at this time Occupation: Con Artist, Chief Detective, Former CIA Agent Other Information: Henry Claval's location has been unknown for almost eight years and is believed to be hiding in the arctic or middle east. Henry is considered very armed and dangerous and only a few selected people know where Claval is. Henry Claval's top student, Fredrick Thomas is believed to be the only real person who has been keeping in touch with Claval. Henry taught Thomas in 1994 the trick of the trade and the secrets of being a con artist. The last time police got in touch with Fredrick Thomas was in 1995 when he was picked up for minor theft in New York. After Thomas was released from jail, the police realized who they were dealing with and sent out a look-out team for Thomas to no avail. Soon after, Fredrick Thomas' name was totally forgotten, as he was believed to go under another alias. No other information at this time. I now knew who the hitman was! TO BE CONTINUED.
