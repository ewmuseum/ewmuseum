Date: Wahhhh! The prisoner paced around his cell, going insane. 4 walls, actually only 3 if you don’t consider the bars a wall, but claustrophobia drove him insane of this area. He despised it, and wanted out, badly. 'PLEASE! Let me out! I didn’t steal anything!', said the man with jet brown hair and a goatee. Nobody was around to hear him, until a black security guard entered the corridor. 'You have to let me out!' The guard narrowed his eyebrows. 'You have a person for you here to…' 'GET ME THE F*CK OUTTA HERE????!' 'No, he’s going to talk with you', said the guard, slinging his keys onto his finger and into the key slot to let the prisoner out. The prisoner was cuffed as the guard walked down the hall into another room. The guard turned the knob and walked into the other room. In this room, a glass barrier stood with holes inside for talking. On the other side, Eddy Ramirez/Young Tiger stood, hands in jeans pockets and staring straight foward at the prisoner. 'OK, you guys have 10 minutes', the guard remarked as he left the room. The prisoner stared at Eddy for a moment. 'Eddy! Get me out of here! Jail sucks!' 'Darrell, calm yourself down. We need to talk…' 'TALK?! TALK?! I’m falsely accused of stealing and all you want to do is talk?!!' 'Darrell…' 'Bail me before I get blamed again and become part of prison! I didn’t steal any computer, Eddy, YOU have to stand up for me in court.' 'I’m afraid to say I have no idea exactly what came down so I have no case to say…' 'Eddy! You bastard!!! I thought you were a friend!' 'Darrell, CALM DOWN. God, take a chill pill, let’s talk about something.' Darrell sighed and positioned himself in his seat. He sighed and stared through the glass. 'What is it?', said Darrell. 'Darrell, you had been accused of having drugs by your friends, right?' 'But I-.' 'Darrell, yes or no.' 'Yes. I honestly did have drugs.' 'Who sold them to you?' 'How the f*ck does that matter?' 'Darrell, answer. Who sold them?' 'FINE!!! Your sister sold me drugs. There, I said it. Your sister, who’s also really f*cking hot, sold me drugs and I got caught. Is that whore still drugged?' Eddy, ignoring Darrell’s question, talked again. 'When did she sell them?' '2 months ago. How do YOU know about this?' 'I found out.' 'Well, it’s true. Your sister sold me drugs and I lost FRIENDS by that. It’s all her fault. ALL HER FAULT THAT WHORE!!!!!!' 'Don’t call my sister a whore!!!' 'I just did! Whore, slut, bitch-.' 'That’s enough!', said Eddy, enraged and onto his feet now. 'Darrell, it’s not her fault. Bob Vurdo sold her drugs, and Bob Vurdo is a PCW wrestler. He got my sister on them.' 'Your point….?' Eddy sighed. 'Nevermind, Darrell, I just got from you all I need to know.' Darrell pounded on the glass as Eddy walked away. 'Wait!! Where are you going?' 'Away.' 'You’re a f*cking dick! Stand up for me in court dammit!' Eddy, not trying to be rude to Darrell, had already left the room and was walking away. 'EDDY!', screamed Darrell loudly. Darrell pounded on the glass. 'GOD DAMN YOU EDDY!' Eddy, hearing all this from the other room, feeling sorry for his friend, glanced at his feet, then walked away, trying to forget about this conversation.
