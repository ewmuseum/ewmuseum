Three From the Past......Richard. PART 4

12 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3-12-2003 “So much coming to pass at once, and it would seem that I am merely along for the ride…” NAY, BROTHER. WE ARE THE MAIN CHARACTER IN THIS PLAY. THE SCENES REVOLVE AROUND THE TWO OF US, WHILE ALL THE OTHERS REMAIN BUT BIT PLAYERS IN THE LARGER SCHEME… “It just seems as though I have no control over what transpires. I plod through this whole scenario with the intent of becoming whole. I do not place much faith in the golden warrior prophecies. I only know that I need to protect mine sanity to deal with what is important.” GOLD… “Aye…The gold of a champion around the waist of a hero. The thing is, I do not need a prophecy to cement myself as a hero. The masses already look upon me as being so.” AND WHAT OF THE REST OF THE WORLD? THY PEOPLE LOVE THEE, BUT WHAT OF THOSE WHO TOIL IN THE DARKNESS? “I realize that some are misfortunate and do not bask in the light that my very name casts, but eventually my kingdom will transcend the boundaries of light and dark.” AND IT ALL LIES AT STONEHENGE. “Aye….” The scene opens to show Venneman Pendragon, resplendent in his golden armor, sitting tall upon the massive war horse Xerxes. Joseph rides beside him, his skin pale in the overcast light. He looks nervously towards the horizon, and seems unnerved by something…. Joseph: Something feels wrong, my Lord… Pendragon reins in and looks towards the horizon, a frown invading his strong face… Pendragon: Aye. Something awaits us….Or someone. I can sense a….Power. Up ahead, but still out of view. Joseph: Perhaps it would be better to take a more roundabout route to Stonehenge… Pendragon slowly brings his eyes to rest on those of Joseph, rage building in the deep blue… Pendragon: Never in mine life have I backed away from a challenge, and now shall be no different… Joseph: But this means more. What if what is waiting for us kills you? Pendragon smiles darkly…. Pendragon: Then I shall meet death with open arms. I fear it not….. Joseph tries to hide the appalled looks that has come across his face, but Pendragon shakes his head and starts Xerxes ahead on the same route he was taking before. Joseph rides next to him, but he appears even more nervous than he was before. As they go, Pendragon begins to look a tad bored, and begins to speak just so the airtime is not so dead…. Pendragon: Dost thou know what I must return to when I go back to America? Joseph: More combat? Pendragon: Aye. Combat is mine life. By the sword, by mine hand…Either way, foes fall. In the PCW, many look at me as an oddity. Honor is a virtue that is fleeting in that arena of combat. There is never a clean fight, and I stand alone…. Pendragon drones on, and Joseph starts to look annoyed….. Pendragon: Blah blah blah….. Joseph: Can we talk about something else? Seems like you’re just trying to talk to hear yourself speak….Filler. Pendragon laughs a little before squinting at the smaller man… Pendragon: All right…How about I give thee a detailed run down of my career? It all started in an Indy fed that went by the name NCWA…Boy, weren’t those the days… Joseph listens to Pendragon drone on about something that he cares not about, until he can take it no more… Joseph: AAAAAAAAAAAAARRRRRRRRRRRRRRGGGGGGGGGGGGGHHHHHHHHHHHH! Pendragon: What vexes thee? Joseph: Who cares about your career? Do you really think trhat I care? I DON’T!!! I only care about saving the damned world… Pendragon looks away, a miffed look on his face… Pendragon: Fine. Then speak to me of what we are doing here. I guess we should really bring some relevance to the conversation… Joseph: Finally the man sees reason!!! Pendragon glares at his companion and the fellow quickly changes his tune… Joseph: I mean…Finally, we can get down to business. We will go to Stonehenge, and there the high priest of the Nobles will perform the joining ritual…. Pendragon interrupts the man, annoying him further… Pendragon: Thee speaks of nobles…I myself am a noble, and I know not the group of which thee speaks… Joseph: the nobles are the sworn enemies of the Druids of Darkness. We seek only to illumine the darkness of the world, and bring peace to the chaos… Pendragon: So, like the Druids, thou seeks to use me? Joseph pales even more as Pendragon eyes him shrewdly, but keeps his composure and his voice only quivers a little… Joseph: We seek to help the world, and we will do this by assisting you in your crusade…. Pendragon: All I wish to do is to keep mine sanity. I fight a man from the past who was called forth to vanquish me on the word of some unseen foe… AMEN. Pendragon: Not now… Joseph stares at Pendragon quizzically…. Joseph: the voice grows bolder, does it not? Pendragon: ‘Tis of no matter. Soon I will be complete… Joseph: And we must hurry. The two men top a rise and stare down into the valley where waits Richard. He is the picture of nobility, his armor overlaid with a white cloth embroidered with a large red cross. His beard has the slightest touch of gray and he looks at the men expectantly. The two ride closer and Pendragon’s eyes grow wide at the sight of the long dead Crusader. He flies from his horse and runs up to the man whom he has looked up to for many years. Upon reaching him, he kneels on the ground before him and Richard can not help but be taken aback by the earnest show of respect… Pendragon: I humbly introduce mineself, Lord King Richard…. Richard: Stand, boy. You needn’t prostrate thyself at mine feet…Believe thee me, the honor is mine, young Pendragon… Pendragon: Why waitest thou here? What is thy task? Richard: I am to kill thee, Pendragon… Pendragon: And do thee plan to finish thy task? Richard looks around and shakes his head. He then draws his sword and holds it on his palms as he hands it to Pendragon, at the same time taking a knee… Richard: He said he would torment mine progeny…But as a descendant of Arthur such as thee, thou art mine progeny, and I cannot go through with the task. My blade belongs to thee. I ask that thee allow me to help thee in thy tribulations to come… A tear falls from Pendragon’s left eye, but he refuses the blade… Pendragon: Stand, my Lord. Thou art too great a man to kneel to one such as I…Thy help is appreciated, and accepted… Pendragon puts his arm out and Richard returns the gesture, and the two clasp wrists… Richard: We have much work, thee and I… Pendragon: Aye…We must defeat the darkness… Richard: And the bastards have one more surprise for thee…Arthur. Pendragon’s knees wobble a bit and Richard catches him before he loses his balance… Pendragon: Arthur? Of family Pendragon? Richard: Exactly…And he wants Excalibur… GOOD CHRIST… “Arthur is here. How does one deal with a legend? I feel like all of mine opponents. How does one prepare when one faces one such as mineself? Arthur…He was ne’er evil. Perhaps he will see the light as good Richard has…If not, this scene could get uglier as it moves forward….God help us all…” Richard puts a hand on Pendragon’s shoulder as Joseph glares in the background….
