Title: Abuse of Powe. Featuring: Politically Incorrect Date: 22nd Abuse of power. A term synonmous with Media, now has become synonmous with the Ultimate Authority as well. The two men in charge of the tag team division of PCW have booked themselves two STRENOUS matches this week. They face Jon Kano and Misplaced. Okay, okay, you can stop laughing already! Sure, the three men suck. Sure, Politically Incorrect has destroyed Misplaced on two occassions. Certainly the Cowabunga Connection DESERVE a shot at the tag titles. But, don't Misplaced? I mean, with COREY WILLIAMS winning the World Title, Misplaced COULD win! And sure, Jon Kano may be the least threatening big man in the history of wrestling. But, if you were Politically Incorrect, wouldn't YOU abuse your power? I mean, conceptually, Politically Incorrect will be the PCW Tag Team Champions until THEY don't want the titles anymore. And that's the way things seem. Politically Incorrect? Of course it is! ::En route to tonight's Turmoil, the men atop PCW's Tag Team ranks sit happily aboard Politically Incorrect One, each enjoying a shot of Jack Daniels. William S. Powell and Reginald V. Lovecraft smile, sipping their drinks while munching absent-mindedly on some popcorn. Both men grin, enjoying their handiwork.:: William S. Powell: So, how about Suburban Uprising quitting already? Reginald V. Lovecraft: It's obvious that they were afraid of the beating they would recieve at the hands of Politically Incorrect, so they just quit before they could face us. Ah well, not our loss, although, the tag team division now kind of sucks. I mean, there are only four other teams besides us! William S. Powell: Indeed....and Friday, we begin defending against each one of them, starting with Misplaced. Why the hell are Misplaced still even on the roster? Reginald V. Lovecraft: I have no earthly idea. I think we need Tim Walden to fire them. William S. Powell: No, Reginald, this is one we can handle ourselves, in the wrestling ring. Misplaced seem to never want to give up. They take asskicking after asskicking at the hands of Politically Incorrect, and yet they keep coming back for more. Cairns and Carey must be quite the masochists to take our Politically Incorrect beatings. But, this time, our PCW Tag Team Titles are on the line! ::Powell executes an expression of mock fear.:: William S. Powell: WHAT IF THEY WIN?! ::Both men laugh, hysterically doubled over for several minutes.:: Reginald V. Lovecraft: That's a laugh! Those two idiots, defeating Politically Incorrect? NEVER! William S. Powell: They haven't even won one match in PCW! There is no way they can defeat the greatest tag team ever! Reginald V. Lovecraft: Understood. Now, let us move on to today's challange....Jon..Kano. ::Both men chuckle once more:: William S. Powell: Jon Kano is one of many impudents that have come up in the past few weeks...rising up in an effort to become famous by crushing The Ultimate Authority. Unfortunately...Mr. Kano does not grasp that he cannot do this. If you face The Ultimate Authority, you face reprecussions. Namely, having to face the PCW Tag Team Champions, by yourself. Reginald V. Lovecraft: The Man Who Rocketh the Mosteth is trying to rise from career jobber by facing us. Jon Kano is nothing more than a man who craves attention. he craves the spotlight, but does not have the skills to reach it. A man who... A man who.... A man who....the hell?! ::Suddenly, the plane jerks from side to side, as our camera abruptly cuts out.:: ---- View Politically Incorrect's Biography
