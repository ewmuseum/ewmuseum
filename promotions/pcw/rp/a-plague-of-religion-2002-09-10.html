A Plague Of Religion

10 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10 - 09 - 02 Your average bus, nothing out of the ordinary traveled along a beautifully crafted cobbled path as a tranquil setting passed by. The passengers looked upon the scene as the bus remained at a low but comfortable speed allowing the parties on board to soak in their surroundings. Passing rich green mountains, flourishing with plant life the scene switches to the interior of the bus and most notably the thorn in the side of 'The Front Office' known collectively as the dynamic duo Pranks With Attitude. Standing in the shadows of his partner Gaz 'The Jester' Green brushes his hand over the velvet fabric of his emerald business attire, looking incredibly smug, most likely reminiscing over his caper involving PCW's first couple Xavier Kannon and Eleanor Kannon-Hall. Nerezza stood erect focusing on the road ahead and their destination, which has remained a mystery since the opening scene whilst adorning his crimson red sleeve-less trench coat. 'Didn't you once say that our heads would be in the chopping block if it ever came to light that it was indeed us who garnished Ellie's handy-work?' questioned the Jester, still portraying his look of arrogance. 'YES!' hissed the Angel Of Fear. 'A chopping block for remodeling Hubbardville House and....' said the jovial Gaz Green before being interrupted upon by his clued in comrade. 'And, career suicide for our next stunt' A look of concern filtered onto the Jester's gleaming face, maybe this episode they have installed for the 'Gold Patron Meritorious' could be deemed a little excessive. 'Maybe we shouldn't go through with this?' retorted the emerald menace, obviously having second thoughts. 'DOUBT IT!!!' roared the trickster team in unison. The bus continued in hot pursuit of their destination, still unknown to the masses but having to take a guess then Hubbardville would certainly be a top three contender. As always the bus follows the long winding roads still traveling up a slight gradient, passing picture 'esque scenery of a well preserved mountain range. Swaying away from the 'Italian Job' finale the camera returned to the bus with perfect timing as Gaz masqueraded as the dizzy diva Ellie during her long bus journey to Hubbardville accompanied by her ever present husband Xavier Kannon. 'Er.. You might wanna play because there’s, like, a Bomb on the bus that will now go off if you drop below 5 insults a minute...' murmured the Prince Of Pranks in a sweet but childish voice. 'THEY can’t.. But ELLIE~! could! I made it when I had the residual Evil Super Genius powers from Alan Kreigman, in case we were ever stuck on a boring bus journey and I needed, like, entertainment!' Nerezza looking at his colleague with disgust rolled his eyes and eventually ushered a Kannon one-liner. 'IN THE NAME OF HUBBARD....' bellowed the pale vampire. 'MY POON PUPPY, FUCK FERRET, SHAG SQUIRREL....' continued the behemoth, still in full portrayal of the present owner of Primetime Championship Wrestling. '.... Easy Tiger! I think that's enough Front Office acting for one day, I was waiting for you to get down on one knee and propose?' injected the conniving comic, his eyes wide open, looking every bit the Animee character. Suddenly a voice swamped over the silence taking both Asylum members by surprise. 'How much longer?' groaned the voice, yet to be unveiled by the camera. Gaz Green tilting his head to acknowledge the individual, took a firm grasp of a over hanging microphone, soaking in a deep breath in preparation of his announcement he finally roared a response. 'Not much further.... And then you shall have every opportunity to preach your respected faiths in a place that has sheltered itself from beliefs. No longer will people will be brain washed by Scientology, they shall see what the outside world can offer and it shall be you fine people that purifies a place spawned by evil, namely Hubbardville' said the Lord Of Laughter to his congregation of fellow travelers. Nerezza taken back by the Jesters inspirational speech whispers, 'To quote a man, Easy Tiger, your taking this assignment a little serious!?!' 'Not really - A couple of hours of the GOD channel' chuckled the devious comedian with a grin. As the Asylum combination bask in their own accomplishment, knowing full well that this will be the prank to end all others, the view switches to the other side of the bus unveiling a hoard of strangers clad in tunics associated with their respected faiths. Every religion imaginable is present, ranging from the renowned Catholics and Christians to the more obscure Muslims and a true rarity Prankology, represented by a youth in streetwise clothing and a full arsenal of pranks including pea shooter. Immersed in conversation the strangers have no conflict and simply bide their time as they approach 'Hell On Earth' in this case Hubbardville. 'Hubbardville will need cleansing after this! An Exorcism of faiths....' cried Nerezza grinding his teeth with impatience. 'AMEN TO THAT BROTHER~!' yelled his co-worker, crossing himself. As the bus turns a blind corner, low and behold Hubbardville in all its glory. A complex of pure splendor with its variety of fine buildings basking in the intense sun that beats down harshly, sitting in the midst of Wisconsin's finer locations. Standing, as the only obstacle was a pair of gates, large metallic frames guarding the sacred home of Scientology causing the bus to stop abruptly. 'Can I help you people?' said a voice from the outside, followed by taps to the glass paneled doors of the transportation. 'Err... Yeah... Listen up squire; Mr. Kannon wanted to surprise his wife with... This circus act!' whispered Gaz 'The Jester' Green adding a subtle wink. 'Well I've got to stop everybody nowadays.... Must be wary of strangers, with the whole pranking that’s been going on!' replied the lowly security guard. 'Job well done then. But seriously, Scientology all the way!!!' screamed the King Of Komedy with a cheap blag. 'Agreed - All appears well, so run along!' retorted the paid help, pulling a lever to allow access. The bus shuffled along the cobbled pathway and into the actual grounds of Hubbardville, parking in the town’s central area, only feet away from a lecture of Scientology that has just finished, conveniently. The occupants of the bus flood the outside and wait for further instructions. 'PREACH THE GOSPEL MY FRIENDS~!' cried Nerezza. The flock spread out and bombarded a small group of youngsters who have just completed a tutorial of Scientology. The group then began pressing their beliefs on the traumatized individuals who usher backwards as though they had been stalked by a mob of pedophiles, a complete nightmare materializing before their very eyes. 'Would you like to take part in Holy Communion, young man?' asked a Catholic minister. Suddenly the suspicious gatekeeper came hurtling up the high street hailing abuse and prompting the duo of Pranks With Attitude to make their hasty escape via the bus as the hilarious scene closes.
