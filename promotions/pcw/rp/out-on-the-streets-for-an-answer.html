Out on the streets for an answer...

2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5-5-02 Thinking about what Jordan X Shabazz has done with Mark's family. How he has destroyed everything possible between his sister, Jane, and himself. How him having an intimate moment with her on Mark's own bed with the Confederate flag blanket on top. Thinking about this coming up Sunday with Supremency and the number one contender match with Jordan to find out who is going to go on and be a rising star champion with Kid Krazie now the champion and either Mark or Jordan could whoop his ass. With all of this on Mark's mind he is trying to relieve some stress to find out what the people think about the African American race. Mark is on the street to find out what people do think about the African American race. To find out how hard the white race work to get their money and to keep America going strong on the economy with their hard work. Mark is doing this probably while the African American race is sitting at home and smoking their weed. That is what Mark hates the most is that the African American race can get away with all of the things that they do. “That Duke Williams, I can’t stand him.” says Mark waving his microphone around that he has in his hand. “He says that I still live with my mother although I have my own apartment. Now how can I live with her when I am on the other side of town than her? Boggles my mind a lot too because I cannot take a family visit to my parents house and then this Duke suspects that I live with my parents. Well for your information Duke I do not live with my parents and about your Malice show, the only reason they tune in for your show is because you beg them to. They don’t do it for your mic skills they just want PTC to be up in the top in whatever the company does.” Mark gets to his first destination where it is a park that moms have their children playing at. There are kids swinging in swings and there are other kids swinging on the monkey bars. Mark starts to head over to the park bench having his black Nike shoes go against the soft California grass. Not to mention that there are not any African American people are around and how peaceful the birds are chirping and the flowers are in bloom. It is just a beautiful place to be without any African American people around to bother any of the white race from getting some time off of their busy schedule. “Miss could I ask you a question?” asks Mark, going over to a lady that has a stroller to the side of the bench and she is watching her young boy swing on one of the swings. “Go for it, but I have little time to spare since I am watching my children in the playground.” says the lady looking over from the monkey bars and the swings to talk to Mark. “I am doing a poll and I was wondering what your thoughts were on the African American race around here in America.” asks Mark looking at the lady, staring in to her blue eyes sparkling in the sun. 'Well my thoughts on the African Americans are that they do what ever they want.' says the lady looking back into Mark's big blue eyes. 'I think that they do not to anything for America, only to provide us people to make work for us.' 'Thanks for your input very much.' says Mark nodding his head at the lady and winking at the same time. Mark leaves the playground area, having to look back at the lady a couple of times to see what she was doing. One time when Mark looked back she was looking right at Mark and his body, turning her head when she saw Mark look at her. Mark heads over to a street corner where a white man has a beer in his hand and he is slunched over in the corner. He has on black sweat pants with a white shirt, torn at the neck and white velcro shoes. Mark goes over to him and he comes up to him with the microphone in his hand. 'Sir could I ask you for your input on the African American race?' asks Mark looking at the man and the man's can of Bud Light. 'You mean those black people that spend all of their money on that marijuana?' asks the bum looking at Mark asking a question. 'Yeah those people that do not do anything.' says Mark in response to the bum. 'Well I think that they are worthless and do not know what they are talking about.' says the bum taking another sip of his beer. 'Like I said, all they spend their money on is the weed so that they can have a high for about a half an hour.' 'Is that all you think about them sir?' Mark asks. 'They also think that they are better than the white race and will do anything to make us think they can overpower us.' says the bum thinking more and more about the black race. 'Ever since that Martin guy made his speech they think that they are better than us, but in reality we are the true people with the power.' After that said by the bum, he takes another drink of his beer and then he falls over to the side, passed out. His beer can falls out of his hand and rolls on the sidewalk a while to be crushed by a little white boy jumping on the silver can. Mark takes the microphone away from the bum's mouth and he heads on towards his other place of destination. Mark is looking on the street going to his last place when he sees a PCW fan with a shirt that has the PCW logo on it. Mark runs over to the older guy and he stops him in his tracks. 'Sir could I ask you about your input of the African American race?' says Mark grabbing the guy on the elbow and turning him around. 'Holy s*** you are Mark Makonie.' says the older gentlemen astonished about who is in front of him at the moment. 'Oh man you have a match coming up in a week with Jordan X Shabazz don't you?' 'Well yes I do have a match, but I am not here to talk about that.' says Mark into the microphone. 'Well I think that you are going to make him blacker than what he already is for what he is doing with your sister.' says the white older gentleman. 'He makes those comments about the white race that are uncalled for and then saying that you live with your mother at her house.' 'Well I see your point there since I did do my own promo in my own apartment.' says Mark making sure that he gets this straight about him visiting his family. 'I was only there for dinner that night because they wanted to know how my life has been. Then Duke and Jordan have to go and say that I live with my mother. They surely didn't get where I have been with my last promo, inside my apartment.' 'I agree with you Mark.' says the PCW fan smiling to talk with his favorite wrestler. 'I think that the African American race is rude and make rude comments about life in general. They think that the white race is going to lay down everytime that they walk to face of the earth. But come Sunday at Supremency Jordan will learn the lesson about the white race.' 'Hey kid come on now, you are making it sound as if you were fighting Jordan.' says Mark cutting off what the kid was going to say next. 'That is my job to talk about how it is going to be a great matchup with our feud heating up since we have been in PCW. It has been non stop brutality since we both have been in PCW and I don't see it stopping soon. But on the 12th we will both find out who is the rising star between us. Who is going to make it big in this company faster than the other person.' Mark stops talking about his match and he looks around him. He is surrounded by a grounp of white people who want to hear what Mark has got to say. He feels as if he is a preacher giving a sermon to a whole church congregation. 'Makonie...Makoine...Makonie' says the crowd chanting his name and making him feel good. 'The African American race thinks they are better than us, but are we going to let them take us down?' Mark preaches to the crowd as they respond. 'Hell no!' says the crowd in a chant. 'Who is going to step up and make sure that this does not happen?' Mark preaches once again. 'We are!' says the crowd again in a chant kind of form. 'Who is going to win the match at Supremency between Shabazz and myself?' preaches Mark once again with the crowd now listening attentively. 'Makonie is!' says the crowd again looking up at Mark as he is on a wooden box. Mark gets down from the box and he motions to the crowd to move away because he is running short on time. He makes his way oiut of the crowd and he heads down the street. He turns a corner and he comes to his white apartment complex. He heads up the stairs and he takes out his keys to his own apartment room. He puts in the gold key and he turns it, pushing on the door to make it open on the way in. He has not been in the apartment since he has seen the tape of Duke and his mom. He goes in to his room and he sees that his room was taken over by African American merchandise. There is a blanket that has the Confederate flags crossed out and a big poster of Jordan X Shabazz on the empty wall. 'What in the f*** is this that I see here?' yells Mark not meaning it to be a question. 'That n***** is going to pay for one sleeping with my sister in my own bed and then putting up this kind of stuff in my own room.' Mark takes the blanket off of his bed and he takes the poster off of the wall, taking them into his living room. He then opens up his door and he goes down the stairs of the apartment complex and outback where there is an open yard. He sets down the stuff and he reaches into his pocket and pulls out a lighter. He takes the poster and he sets it into a flame, putting it down on to the blanket watching both of the items burn to ashes. He goes back into his apartment and goes into his fridge to pull out a Kahlua Iced Coffee, the drink that will win the contract at Supremency. 'That goes to show that African Americans do not have any respect for anything around them.' Mark says taking a drink of his Kahlua and leaning on the counter. 'Drink Kahlua Iced Coffee, the next drink of PCW!' says Mark as he heads into his room, laying down on his bed and closing his eyes for a while.
