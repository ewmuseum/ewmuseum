So tired.....tired of waiting.....tired of waiting for MY title!

7 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 05/07/02 “Enough is enough and it’s time for a change!” A great man once said that, the name escapes me, however. Not important, because if it wasn’t Tyler Nelson who said it then he wasn’t that great to begin with. As we all know, the CEO of PCW is the end all-be all of wrestling, and the man who holds all of the power in the wrestling universe. He has made it his mission in life to recapture what was stolen from him unjustly by the new acting CEO of OSW, Howard Porter. That nerdy son of a bitch made a ludicrous stipulation on the CEO’s title reign, setting him up for that greasy, grimy Stewart Hix to get his fed-whoring ass on it and forever tarnish the belt forever. It was Porters jealousy of the CEO and the fact that he could never grab the brass ring that drove him to make such outlandish and unprecedented stipulations on the newly crowned PCW World Champion. He conspired with Professor Herbert D. Dorkiman to rob the CEO of what he had worked so hard for, and they were successful. Tyler fought valiantly that night, taking on all comers and succeeding for the most part. Eventually the treacherous numbers caught up with him, and he was attacked from behind by the cowardly Stewart Hix. He snuck away with the CEO’s title, never to be seen again. That fateful night never sat well with the CEO, but with the entire world against him he couldn’t dwell too long on that setback. But it lingered….that stinging defeat at the hands of a gaggle of conspirators just ate away at the CEO like a plague. He’s watches as unworthy champion after unworthy champion have come and gone, from the lowly Hix to the worthless Jeremy Howard, on to Ivan Stanislav and finally to Jason Snow. The CEO can sit by no longer. He now has the opportunity to right that dreadful wrong and put the most coveted prize in the sport today around the most deserving waist there is, that of Tyler Nelson. And who stands in his way? Two men who have considerably less talent and certainly not as much charisma as the CEO. Jason Snow, leader of a group of upstart punks, and overall loser. Big Alan Steele, the illegitimate bastard child of Zeus, or so he would have us believe. Are these the types of men that are worthy of the PCW World Title, a title that Tyler Nelson has held twice previously? Unquestionably no. There is only one man that has the ability to hold the PCW World Title with strength and courage, skill and ability, charisma and cunning, and most of all money, power, and respect. That man is without a doubt, the CEO of PCW Tyler Nelson. Speaking of the greatest wrestler walking the face of the planet, what’s he up to these days? Last we saw him he was in the receiving end of a cowardly and unprovoked attack from his two challengers at Supremacy, Big Alan Steele and the current champion Jason Snow. Those two chickenshits tried to take the CEO out before the pay per view so they wouldn’t have to contend with his pure and unadulterated fury. Too bad they didn’t finish the job and only succeeded in pissing the CEO off further. Not a smart move from two mental midgets. Par for the course I suppose. Today Tyler is locked in seclusion, in the preliminary stages of planning his forthcoming victory at Supremacy to regain his coveted and stolen PCW World Championship. So we take you to where he is, because it’s fairly obvious that you all want to see him. As the scene opens up we find that he is in what looks to be a conference room of some sort, a long oval shaped table situated a tad off center toward the right side of the room. It’s solid oak finish shines from fresh polish as the overhead track lighting illuminates the room. Surrounding the table are a plethora of black high-back leather chairs, comfortable looking to the eye. All the way at the end of the table is a large dry erase board with a whole bunch of scribbling and illegible writing on it. As we pan to the left we run across a wet bar that is built into the wall. It appears to have been ravaged by an angry mob, with dirty glasses and empty champagne bottles strewn about haphazardly. The refrigerator door is ajar and we can plainly see that the inside is totally empty. Moving along to the other side of the room from the conference table we find an area much akin to a living room. There are two large, comfortable looking black leather couches positioned in an ‘L’ shape with a large glass top table in front of them. A large flat screen television is fastened to the wall and plays footage from the April 20, 2001 edition of Friday Fusion where the CEO completely dominated and dismantled Meanstreak, Frisco, and Stewart Hix to walk away as PCW World Champion for the first time. Water is heard from inside an off-room of the office, obviously a bathroom. It runs for several seconds, then stops. After a few more seconds the CEO comes walking out of the bathroom, dabbing his face with a towel. He finishes and then dries his hands, tossing the towel to one of the couches when he’s done. He looks exhausted as he checks his platinum Rolex watch. “Three a.m.,” sighs Tyler. His expensive, custom tailored suit is a mess. The shirt is untucked and only buttoned about halfway. The sleeves are unbuttoned and rolled up haphazardly. His tie, long since removed, lays over the back of the couch. There is a pair of shoes kicked onto opposite sides of the room. “I never had to go through this shit when I won the first time,” moans the CEO. He plops down on the couch and reaches forward, grabbing a remote form the table. He begins to press various buttons, sending the television screen into a frenzy of forwarding and reversing. “It must just be the excitement of me getting my title back that has me so edgy,” mutters Tyler to himself, his voice dragging from exhaustion. “I wasn’t restless like this the first time. I slept like a baby as a matter of fact. Must be the over a year’s worth of pent up frustration and anger that is getting ready to explode that is keeping me awake. No one gets away with screwing over the CEO, but for over a year now that’s exactly what’s been happening.” Tyler depresses the ‘play’ button and the image on the screen is of Tyler putting Meanstreak in GREED IS GOOD. The weary CEO slowly raises a finger to point at the television. “Look at that! So in control of the so-called Monster they call Meanstreak I was,” boasts Tyler. “I had my way with that overgrown ape. He, Frisco, and Hix were eating out of my hand and tasting the back of it as I slapped them around like two dollar whores.” Fast forwarding once again past the part where Meanstreak ADD’s the CEO off the top of the cage. He stops just as the referee raises his hand in victory. “Total domination.” Tyler freezes the picture with his hand raised in the air and the PCW World Championship draped across his chest. He leaves it that way as he tosses the remote onto the table. It makes a loud clamor as it lands on the glass top. The CEO leans back into the comfort of the leather couch, releasing a long breath. “Soon enough, that will be me again,” promises Tyler to himself. “This Sunday, the quest for MY title will be over. Once again I will have the possession that has evaded me for over a year. The frustrations that have built up will be unleashed on Snow and Big Alan Steele with unbridled fury. I’ve been pissed off for way too long, now it’s time to satisfy my greed by regaining MY title.” Tyler’s eyelids grow heavy and almost close. Before they do, he opens them and takes one last look at the frozen screen. A smile creeps across his lips as his eyelids slowly lower down until they are shut. Soon the CEO is fast asleep, the smile still engraved on his face. He will have sweet dreams tonight, with visions of HIS PCW World Title dancing in his head. The scene slowly fades to black.
