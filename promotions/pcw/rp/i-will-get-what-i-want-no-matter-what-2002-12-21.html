I Will Get What I Want, No Matter What.

21 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: December 21, 2002 Normally inside the Windsor-Storm household there would be smiles instead of frowns, laughter instead of tears, and happiness instead of sorrow. But how can you manage to have all this when the one who brought it to you most is missing? I couldn’t take it any longer. I thought at any moment I might breakdown and lose it all. For hours I stared blankly at the bed she had slept on while we stayed with Chris inside his New York apartment. I was lost without her and I could not deny it. I had lost everything after I lost her and should I lose her for good to Mani and Shin that I might not ever manage to smile or laugh again. As the overwhelming feeling of sorrow took over me I fell onto the bed holding back the tears that had been stinging my eyes for days. I pulled the pillow she had slept with close and hugged it hoping that there was some chance that she was still alive. But as the days passed by that hope slowly started to fade away. Nearly a week had passed by and yet still no sign of Christa. My hope had turned into forced beliefs that she was gone and I’d never see her again. That I failed at being a parent, even if these weren’t my kids in the first place. Sure Sunday would come and I would get my chance at revenge on The Angel Society, but was it really worth it if it meant perhaps costing Alex his innocence too. Did I really want to take the chance that Alex might be dragged into the same fate as Christa simply because of the career I had chosen and the opponent I was assigned to defeat. Maybe I shouldn’t bother to show up on Sunday, but I didn’t care brush it off for the possibility of somehow getting Christa back. Maybe they would bring her. Perhaps they would feel some pity and give Christa back. Christa did nothing, she didn’t deserve this. It was me who deserved this fate. Why did they have to drag the innocent into this, are they really that desperate for a win? Surely Christa had no understanding of why she had to be the one stuck in the middle of this heinous act, and be the one who suffered the most. I wrapped my arms around the pillow tighter and let out a frustrated sigh, trying to release the images of Christa’s suffering from my mind. It wasn’t working though. Over and over again the pictures struck my mind. I could see Christa sleeping innocently in the bed. The silence being of such a great amount you could hear a pin drop, and then the silence being broken with a muffled scream as Mani and Shin carried her away, gagged and bound with rope. I fell down face first into the pillow crying, my tears the only source of comfort as I thought to myself. &lt;I&gt;I should have never closed my eyes.&lt;/i&gt; I walked into the room and saw Tessa on the bed. She was crying. It was killing me inside the way this affected her. Christa was gone and she blamed it all on herself. Hell I can&#039;t blame her, I was putting the blame on myself too. But in life there are things you just can&#039;t change. I had to come to realize this was one of them. But there is one thing I can change. My future. And Angel Socitey&#039;s future. I walked over to the bed and sat down next to Tessa. I put my hands on her shoulders and begun to rub them. Tessa looked up from the tear stained pillow. I gave her a warm smile. &#039;Need a good back rub?&#039; I asked her. I positioned myself behind her and sat on the bed. Looking over at Chris I could not help but to smile. He was the one who understood me better than anyone and there he was to the rescue once again. Unable to choke back the tears long enough to answer in words, I simply nodded my head and let him take control. I sat there as I felt my shoulders release all their tension with just the touch of a hand. His hands moving up and down m back was all I needed to make me believe that everything would be fine. Someday I would wake up and Christa would be there waiting to jump into my arms and then greet Chris in the same manner. Feeling the calm take over me I listened to Chris breathe as he worked his hands up and down my back. He laid himself down beside me, planting a kiss on my cheek as he removed one hand from my back, using it to clasp onto my hand. I looked into his twinkling blue eyes and felt a smile creep its way across my lips surely nothing could be greater than being here with Chris knowing that in the end time would heal itself and Christa would be safe once again, inside the arms of Chris and I. All I had to do is close my eyes and she would be there. After all doesn’t Disney have that motto “The dreams that you wish, will come true.” Maybe it seemed a little farfetched but I had to think positive. I would get Christa back no matter the circumstances. I would get her back, even if it meant the death of me.
