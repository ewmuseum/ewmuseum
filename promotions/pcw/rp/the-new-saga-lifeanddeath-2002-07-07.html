The New Saga: lifeanddeath

7 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7-7-02 Talena and DarkStar cuddled up on the black velvet couch in the living room of their estate. They held hands and watched TV like they had before they were unemployed, like old times. Reminiscing, it came down to the one subject that they always talked about together but never around others. DarkStar and Talena are a relationship of the supernatural. DarkStar is a demi-god. He is the seventh son of the seventh son of the seventh son. Heir to the throne of Dreams and the cloak of Nightmares. He holds his job like a cross, ignoring it as much as he can. He filters the dreams and the nightmares of all people through himself and into the Dream Realm as to not disrupt the delicate balance of the earth. If one dream ever got through or one nightmare ever got loose, all hell would break loose. He exists because he needs to. Talena was born with the power of magicks. She didn't realize it until tragedy opened her eyes up to the world of the supernatural. She realized her powers as a sorceress and her powers of second sight, which manifests itself in her doll, Miss Emily. Talena is a beautiful and amazingly intelligent woman, however because of her exposure to the unseen and the unknown she acts like a child the majority of the time. They know Gods on a first name basis and talk to spirits dailey, folklore is proven through the pairs existence as the Prince and the Siren that have been talked about since the middle ages. Even with all this connection the the spiritually unknown, only one time have they ever ran into something even they couldn't explain. Tonight, they talk about that story. ~~~~~~'~~,~~'@'~~,~~'~~~~~~ The black Vette sped down I-95 like it had a hundred times before this night, DarkStar had one hand on the shifter and another hand on the wheel. The CD player blasted Cradle of Filth. Beneath the Howling Stars to be exact. 'I can't believe this is happening,' DarkStar had a grin on his face almost the size of the windshield. Hours before he was called by the owner of PCW himself and told to come down to Atlantic City right away. This was his big break, no more toiling in worthless indies for mere pennys. Sure, the recognition was great, he grew quite the reputation on the east coast. This was his big break though. Talena leaned over and kissed him on the cheek, 'I am so proud of you Gavyn, this is exactly what we needed,' she looked out the window as the highway began to fade from industrial businesses and spotty forests to distanced houses. 'It's kinda pretty out here, when you look past the decadence and destruction of the human race.' DarkStar glanced out his window quickly and noticed that the highway was indeed fading into a rural area, the next off ramp would lead to the halfway point between Philadelphia and Atlantic City, the home of PCW. Finally traffic lights began poking up more often as the corporations turned into mom and pop general stores and little corner foodstands. The town they were in was so beautiful, not typical to the normal central New Jersey inbred towns. A past rain that they must have just missed slickened the road in front of them as they pulled to a red light. 'What town is this?' DarkStar asked, realizing he had no clue to what this town was or where it was on the map, the area was new to him. He has driven through it but never stopped to notice the quaint sweetness of the town. Like those that you would see on TV Land or something of the sort. Talena glanced around for some sort of sign, but her attention was drawn to the girl sitting on a green park bench. She wasn't a day over 15, her hair dyed various shades of purple, blue, green, and red. She seemed pale, like she hadn't eaten in days. Talena looked over at DarkStar with the puppy dog look in her eyes, they reflected shattered hopes and broken dreams of a child. 'Hunny, she looks like she needs food or something... want to do a good deed?' DarkStar wasn't used to them unless they were warranted, but something made him take pity on the girl. She was dressed in old clothes, looking a bit like the poster girl for crust punk, he nodded and made a sharp right, almost pulling up onto the grass of the small park in the center of the small town. A flag poll was next to the girl on the bench, at closer inspection she was a pretty little thing, she just seems like she has seen better days, she looked up at the slowing vintage Corvette and smiled, standing up and walking to the car. Talena noted she had a camera around her neck, not thinking it was important at all, she lowered her window and smiled nicely at the girl, DarkStar reached over and turned the radio down. '...hi..', the girl was very shy, holding her hands behind her back and clasped together, Talena held her hand out the window for the girl and she took it. 'Hi, i'm Talena, this is my boyfriend Gavyn, we noticed that you were alone and... well... I don't want to sound rude or anything but it looked like you needed some help.' The girl looked at the ground and nodded slowly. DarkStar leaned over Talena a ways and the girl seemed to recognize him from somewhere. What was it about this girl... 'Well, what do you need, we are happy to help our kind in need.' The girl seemed confused. 'Err... those that dabble in the alternative dress... unless you are one of those Blink 182 Punk Rock Girls, then we are leaving you here for the wolves.' The girl laughed and seemed to cheer up, Talena wondered how a man who has killed and hurt so many can pick and choose in a way that even makes depressed runaways perk up. 'My name is Rebecca, I guess I could use a ride back to my parents house... I... I havent been there in a long time.' Ah, so she was a runaway. Talena hit a switch on her chair and it leaned forward, Rebecca sat complacently in the back. 'So, what do your friends call you?' The girl got quiet and it seemed DarkStar had hit a nerve. 'I... I don't really have many friends...' DarkStar just smiled, 'Well, what would you want them to call you?' she looked up, her dejected look melting away. 'Becca, I guess...' hesitently she looked at DarkStar who turned back towards the road and began to pull away from the park. 'Well, we will call you Becca then, if you don't mind.' She nodded her head, and DarkStar pulled back onto the road. Talena struck up a conversation with the girl as DarkStar switched CD's too the more mellow Saves the Day. 'Are you hungry Becca? We havent eaten yet today and were about to get something.' The girl smiled. 'I could eat.' 'Good, since you're from around here, do you know any good diners or anything.' She thought for a second. 'There's Joes, but I don't like that place... memories... theres a McDonalds up town.' DarkStar looked into the fading sun and then back to the girl. 'Well Becca, lead the way.' They pulled up to the golden arches a few minutes later, DarkStar shut the door and locked it after Becca and Talena got out. 'We look like The Strange Family out for picnic.' Everyone chuckled. In the light Becca looked incredibly pale, paler then Talena. Talena went out of her way to stay that white, odd. He opened the door for the two as they got into the McDonalds. 'What do you want darling,' Talena thought for a little bit and smiled. 'A Happy Meal!'DarkStar sighed but never lost the smile on his face, he looked to the young girl and asked her, she thought for a second, 'Whatever you are having, I don't want to be a bother.' He smiled and motioned to Talena with his head. 'Go find a seat, i'll be over with the food in a few' Talena led the girl to a booth in the middle of the place, she supressed the urge to jump into the ball pit and instead tried to get more information from the girl about her life and about why she was out there. 'So... what were you doing out there all by yourself. Could have been dangerous.' The girl gave a shrug and spoke, 'Nothing ever happens in this town, noone has even died from anything more then natural causes in about six months.' Talena smiled. 'Thats a good thing though, right?' The girl said nothing. Talena looked over for some sort of saving throw from DarkStar, but he was still getting food. She looked back to Becca who seemed paler then her in this bright light. Noticing the camera again she reached out and started playing with some salt packets, 'Are you into photography?' the girl looked down at the camera and back up to Talena. 'Yeah, to an extent, I'm not that good.' Talena smiled, her second sight was failing her with this girl, but she could tell she was just being modest. 'I'm sure you're great.' The girl shrugged again. 'Eh..' DarkStar walked back over and placed a tray on the little corner table, he handed the happy meal to Talena and her eyes lit up, going for the cheap plastic toy before anything, he smiled at Becca and plopped down a large brown sack of food, and took his own for himself. 'What... this is like 25 dollars worth of food! I said I didn't want to be trouble!' DarkStar laughed, 'You also said you would just have what I was having,' he opened up his bag and pulled out the first Big Mac, 'well I eat alot.' She shook her head with a half smile on her face. 'I'm going to pay you back' 'If you do i'll just have to haunt your dreams.' He said it joking, but something clicked inside the girl with the multicolored hair. 'Dreams... wait... you're DarkStar arent you!' He raised a eyebrow, he never really got recognized unless someone was into the east coast wrestling scene. This was strange. 'Yeah, you are. Oh my god this is so weird, I love NJPW.' DarkStar smiled but in the pack of his mind knew he hadn't wrestled there in at least seven months, opting instead for more exposure with NWA affiliates. 'Thanks... I don't get many fans.' She put her camera down on the table as DarkStar finished up his sack of food. Talena idly wound up her plastic duck. 'Can I get a picture with you?' DarkStar grinned wide and the girl sat down in between him and Talena, in front of the camera, a few seconds went by, they all smiled, and the flash went off. She smiled shyly and took her seat again, putting the camera around her neck. DarkStar masterfully threw his trash towards the can, it missed barely. As he stood Talena noticed that Becca wasn't paying attention, she squinted and the trash put itself back in the trash can. With a smile she looked up at DarkStar. He gave her the 'not in public' look that he normally reserves for when shes kinky. She frowned but looked at her windup duck and was made happy by its motorized walking. 'We should get going, you still need to show us where you live Becca.' They left the Golden Arches and got back into the mint Corvette, getting back onto the main road. There wasn't much talking, as Talena didn't want to hit a bad nerve. The girl reminded her alot of herself when she was that age, interested in art, not interested in parents or anything else but that. Just like her. Well... except she probably wasn't setting things on fire with her mind. It was close enough though. The streets weren't to hard to find, some were dimly lit and others were backstreets, but within minutes they pulled up to Beccas parents house. They said their goodbyes and she thanked them for everything multiple times, DarkStar waved goodbye as she got up and out of the car. 'What a nice child.' Talena said, smiling at her lover. 'We should have one of our own.' DarkStar turned his head to her quickly with a look of fear on his face. He lived through his own house burning down, the death of his entire family, multiple failed relationships, the brutal stabbing of his ex girlfriend by his current girlfriend without closing his eyes. That however, was a scary statement. 'They don't start off that way Talena.' She looked dissapointed. 'Well we can wait then.' Fwew. 'Yeah though, she seemed sweet, I felt bad for her, all alone in this world with noone to talk to. I doubt her parents are that good to her if she was forced to run away like that.' Talena aggreed. DarkStar turned around to look out the rear windshield and to back out of the driveway when he noticed Rebeccas camera sitting in the back seat. 'Talena, she forgot her camera.' Talena looked at it now and picked it up. 'Come on luv, lets go give it to her.' They both got out of the car and left the motor running, walking up to the doorway of the small ranch house. There were gnomes in the front yard. DarkStar liked gnomes. They are funny little creatures, he rang the doorbell and thought about how everyone puts these plastic and ceramic gnomes in their front yards, yet if they ever had the real thing they would be drinking beer, having sex with eachother, and taking shits all over the place. Gnomes were surly creatures. The door swung open and Talena politely said hello, 'We gave your daughter a ride home from the park tonight and found out she left her camera in the car, I figured we should return it.' The man who answered gave them a weird look, like he had seen a ghost. DarkStar sensed something odd, 'Hunny, come over here...' he called his wife over and explained why they were there, her eyes teared up and she took the camera, walking away from the front door quickly as to hide her tears. The man motioned for them to step inside, they did, and he explained to them something they could never understand: Rebecca had died 6 months earlier. She was taking pictures of a resturant called Joe's across town when someone abducted her and took her to the park, she was beaten, raped, and killed. She was incredibly into photography, it was evident on the pictures on the walls. She was gifted, her mother walked over, still crying, and showed them a picture of Rebecca that was placed on a end table in the hallway. The camera was around her neck, there was no questioning it was her. Her mother looked up with tears streaming, 'She... she was buried with that camera... she loved photography so much....' The rest of the night, til his contract signing, was a complete blur for DarkStar. ~~~~~~'~~,~~'@'~~,~~'~~~~~~ In aftermath, neither of the two could ever understand why they didnt know that the young girl was a ghost or a spectre or whatever. DarkStar has dealt with ghosts since he was born, good and bad ones, and Talena see's them with her second sight clear as day. Not this time, and the camera. If it was just a ghost, why the camera, and the eating... never could either of them understand it. It defied every supernatural conviction. Her parents had the film on the camera developed, everything came out perfectly, including the pictures of her killer. He was caught the next day. The only thing people couldn't explain is the picture of DarkStar, her, and Talena, the Police said that it must just have been exposed onto the last bit of film. Everyone involved knew the truth though. Talena fell assleep in his arms that night, he fell assleep shortly after. Its a weird thing, life and death, no matter how exposed to it you are some things just will never make sense. Even in life you can never tell the living from the spirits... and sadly, even in death you can never tell the spirits from the living. Death is perhaps the biggest critiscism that life has ever recieved. So live it all to your fullest. You never know when it could be taken away in a short breath.
