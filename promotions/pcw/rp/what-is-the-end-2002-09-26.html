What is The End?

26 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 09/26/02 One thing that makes Celestials that much better than humans is that they all share a common universal knowledge. Of course, they each have their own minds and their own personal experiences. But there is a knowledge of the world and the universe that they all possess. The only others who may have access to this knowledge would be Hybrids, half-human/half-Celestial creatures. They do not have this knowledge within their heads; to the contrary, even their minds couldn't handle the vast information Celestials have access to. Rather, through a guide to show them, Hybrids may temporarily enter the Celestial Realm and travel to the Halls of Knowledge. Here, the material form of all Celestial knowledge is stored. Why serve you this information, you may wonder? Well, my significant other, Rumiko, has asked me to give some tutelage to The End. I was brought in to be an oracle under Sho the Oracle. I don't really feel like answering such questions as who I am, and who is Sho or Rumiko. Frankly, if you don't know, you haven't been paying attention. But why serve you this information? Perhaps by giving it to you here, you may all be able to understand exactly what The End is, how he can be what he says he is, and perhaps Rush Mason will finally understand why The End has violated the laws of his 'god.' Mechanics of Human/Celestial Conception Celestials may want to have sex, just like humans. Sue 'em. Hey, they came first and we're modeled after them, just less perfect creatures than they are. Anyway, just like humans, Celestials can impregnate each other. But things work a little different. Celestial females may only be impregnated by Celestial males, it's reasonable enough. But Celestial males may impregnate anyone. And so, what happens then? When mating with a human female, the Celestial male does not fertilize the egg cell, but rather 'endows' it. The egg is still incomplete, and will not develop further until there is a human male to fertilize the egg. Until then, the egg goes into stasis and all menstrual cycles are suspended. When the egg is fertilized by the human father, the stasis ends and the egg enters its developmental stage. The End is half-Celestial, but he also claims to be half-black and half-Japanese. And as I've revealed, this is not an erroneous statement. By human standards, The End is half and half. He just has 200% genetic material, half of that he hasn't yet learned to tap into. That's why I'm going to have to be his oracle. Not that I want to, I'm only in this because Rumiko asked me to. Man, for a woman who can't actually talk, she's damn persuasive. Forget that. Anyway, The End is 25 now. At the earliest, he should be 28 by human standards, but he's not. You see, a normal term of pregnancy for a woman carrying a hybrid is 36 months! I believe The End's was actually 30, making him an early born by Celestial standards. Once birthed, the newborn hybrid ages as normal through early and late childhood. However, once the child reaches the agespan of 20-35 human years, aging slows to 1/12th its normal rate. This number does vary depending on the time between the endowment and fertilization of the egg cell. The least recorded age-slowing is 1/1.5, the highest an astounding 1/32. The End has slowed to 1/16th the normal rate. Even if he has children, he will most definitely outlive them. Grandchildren and great grandchildren as as well. Hybrid Natural Lifespans Celestials are not immortal, but to the limited conception of humans they live infinite lives. But Celestials, like everything else in the universe, have lifespans and will one day burn out and die as does a star. Hybrids are the same, only to a lesser extent. While Hybrids do live longer natural life than normal humans, but not nearly as long as full-blooded Celestials. My guess, The End will live to be about 473 years old if he doesn't get killed first. Unnatural Death The only way to kill a Hybrid is to cut off its head or remove its heart. You may also stop its heart. The End has had this happen three times to him so far. When the heart stops the body enters a regenerative phase that lasts about 36 hours. During this time, it is possible to stop the heart indefinitely by casting the Spell of Binding. Spell of Binding The spell is recited in a language incomprehensible to humans; in fact, only hybrids and Celestials can utter it. The Spell of Binding is spoken in the Universal Celestial tongue, Psionics. For the uninitiated, which is basically everyone save for Akiyama, Cyclonica, and The End, Psionics is a form of telepathic communication. Only Celestials or hybrids may perform the Spell of Binding because only they may speak Psionically. The words of the spell roughly translate to: 'Bind this spirit within the shell,Bind this life from rebirth.Locked inside a soft tomb,To escape until released and never before...' Despite the simplicity of the message in human understanding, the Spell of Binding takes 12 hours to recite. It would be rather easier to simply remove the heart while the body is in the regenerative stage, but the body cannot be further harmed at this time, making it impossible to kill a hybrid during its regeneration. However, there are risks to performing the Spell of Binding. The body does completely regenerate during this time, and the excess energy stores up inside the body. If the Spell of Binding is dispelled, the body must immediately release the energy. Normally, this causes the hybrid to go into a fit of insane bloodlust, ripping to shreds any living creature near it (as well as inanimate objects). By reciting the Spell of Binding backwards to awaken the trapped Hybrid, the risk of dying because of it is extremely severe. I know this hasn't been in the least bit enlightening to you mere humans. You've heard more about Celestials and humans than most would hear in twenty lifetimes. But you haven't learned anything. There's nothing to do with Britney Spears or winning a million dollars, however, so you'll quickly forget this. Insignificant specks in the universe, that's all that you are and everything you deny to be. Fools...
