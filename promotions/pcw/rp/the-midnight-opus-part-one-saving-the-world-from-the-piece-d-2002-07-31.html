The Midnight Opus: Part One – Saving the World from the Piece de Resistance…

31 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 31st July 2002 Quick OOC Note: Meh…this should have been out earlier today…be prepared for another one close to 12pm EST, probably a little longer than this one… The Chris Chase Continued Chronicles – Book Three: Deus Ex Toaster…… RP#2 for Turmoil (28th July 2002)… Chapter Three: The Midnight Opus: Part One – Saving the World from the Piece de Resistance… - - - - - - - - - It’s strange how things are depicted these days. How something so simple, so subtle can be woven into a complex web of deception and deceit. Like a Story. There’s the clichés built up over centuries of story telling, and most of them go like this: Boy Meets Girl – Girl causes Disaster – Boy falls in love with Girl – Super Intelligent Monkeys take over Manhattan – Boy saves world from imminent Super Intelligent Monkey Domination – Boy gives Girl a good banging behind a shed. This story however, is a short masterpiece that cannot be summed up in that form. Or else this idiot handler might spoil it and you all can go walking away to leap off some fifty-foot cliff. This is the Midnight Opus - a Grand Medley of Chasic Proportions. Let’s hope that you all don’t fall flat on your face in glancing over this with an ever-cynic eye. The drink made you cynics; you weren’t born to be like that. You were born to be turned over and shouted at…now listen… - - - - - - - - - ~(Chris Chase – Religious Lunatic Extraordinaire)~ I will destroy. You will obey. It’s simple as that. I will preach. You will listen. Even simpler than that. It’s not hard to understand how this world works. All – even those lousy bureaucrats - fight the fight for survival. Decrepit and bland – your attitude’s getting tiring. Dustin Downey’s raping another hamster. Chris Chase’s gone lunatic on the world. What’s different?Indeed…what’s different? Welcome to the Midnight Opus – the Masterpiece of the Millennium – two and a half years late. I guess this should have came earlier, what with the ever growing influence of Satan on the world – but digital observations were never that accurate. It’s the whole One-Zero thing again – the Yes-No scenario. Black or White, never shades of gray – or even purple. The Midnight Opus – the beginning of the beginning – the dawn of a new era. How many times has THAT been said before? Let me see – that’s about twenty satanic eras and counting now. I guess the forces of good were long overdue an era…those neutral bastards just wanted to hog the limelight. The Midnight Opus, beautifully random in form…and I retort “How?” Now, if we’re done over baking the promotion…we can begin. Don’t move yet. Did I tell you to dance? Putting paid to the plans for Global Domination was never an easily accomplished task. Heck, with Dustin Downey overseeing the whole thing it was damn near impossible, his attention span, or lack of it for that matter, even rivaled the failure of my own. I never really paid attention in the Math Class – I guess that passing off Pythagoras’ Theorem as something involving triangles wasn’t the greatest idea that I’ve ever had. It was the first in a long line of bad ideas. New Breed was a bad idea. Bringing together the hugest egos in the Northern Hemisphere was a recipe for disaster…I was the disaster. Thinking back, it’s not as bad now. After all, it brought about a truer and higher knowledge that I otherwise would not have embraced, or perhaps even mocked. The vapor trails of this knowledge swirl around the recess that is my head, my empty skull. Being insane was never brought up before at the convention – in fact, I considered myself sane when I was over-reacting to a small incident involving Downey and a bear trap… It’s a little known joint known only by the name “Jim’s” – maybe it’s little known due to only being known by it’s little name. It’s a somber occasion in the world of Chris Chase…something to remember me by… The brilliance of some Canadian guy grew brighter by the very second…and the opponents cringed once more. Lock up the goth children, the Bravado Kid’s in town… “GYAHH! SABOTEUR! HOW DARE YE INTERUPT MOI IN ME KITCHEN! ESPECIALLY ZIS STATE OF THE ART SHANTY KITCHEN COMPLETE WITH BARREL UPON BARREL OF ZE GROG! SACRE BLEU! GET ZAT OUT OF HERE YOU CAPED MORON!” “WHOOO~! I AM JESUSMAN! Stand back citizens…your Savior has indeed arrived. Aha! So, you’ve finally decided to show yourself Mister Chef De Minion for what you really are. Now, I must ask you to come quietly or face a good kicking from…JESUSMAN! WHOOOOOOO~! Your cake-baking evil powers are no match for the sheer idiocy and outrageous fashion sense of yours truly!” “GYAHH CHASE! How could you let zis idiot back here again?” “Aha! My trusty sidekick! MisterSarcastoReligiousGimmickMan! Hand me the spatula…I’m going to teach this French speaking mofo the language of Whoop Ze Ass…spoken by 26 known Nations! Now with 100% added French!” ::.~(Musings of Chris Chase)~ It’s Midnight. It’s an Opus…it’s the Midnight Opus. Better than your average masterpiece…indeed. Jim’s French Restaurant is open all night long, and probably gets better business from the newspaper covered hobos that litter the streets of this city than the rest of the populous – who seem all too idle in the company of the dynamic combination of a Canadian wrestler and his deranged Texan Rich Kid pal. It’s the end of July, phew. What a frickin’ month! I return, do the job no less than three times, and have yet to pick up my first true win after returning from a so-called ‘Vacation’ as a dubbed ‘Saint’. I’m still trying to figure this all out, and where I now belong in this crazy mixed up universe where Xavier Kannon holds the World Title belt and Argentine Death matches are all too damn common. Maybe I’ve been drinking too much coffee again, maybe I’ve taken too many smacks to the head and I’m hallucinating again…but maybe, just maybe this world exists. It won’t exist for long at this rate, only to end with a catastrophic explosion that even Nostradamus didn’t see coming. Two years on from the eve of the Millennium, and I guess the guy gave up with the cosmic predictions and landed a particularly safe job at a McDonald’s branch. Unpredictability has a bit of a following these days. You could put a price tag on it and reap millions from the very sale of unpredictability – especially from Arab War Barons who’d like nothing but launch yet another surprise attack against their oil baron neighbors. Then again, that negates the whole ability of unpredictability – by doing something predictable with it… This has forced me to come up with one simple solution to the whole of this, to dress Downey up in a cape and spandex, and let him save the world for me. What? I’m not that lazy, honestly. If you thought that Dustin wouldn’t prevail – then you’ve fallen into my trap. I sold unpredictability to Dustin so therefore he’ll reach a compromise with the nefarious benefactor and setting for a cup of coffee over a fine game of the noble sport of tiddlywinks. My plan couldn’t fail…until Dustin decided to raid a quaint little French Restaurant on the break of Midnight. Apparently, blue isn’t sacred – and by any account, it’s blasphemy or desecration of the good name of the lord. I’m not making this up…it’s a true story. As I’ve said before…the Midnight Opus…the masterpiece to end all masterpieces – a yarn of deceit, all-out action, the occasional dancing girl and high octane adventures with your host – yours truly. How could you possibly ask for more? .:: Jim Le Chef: No! No! Not ze Spatula! I’ll give you anything, I’ll DO anything, but please…do not hit me with ze Spatula, Monsieur Downey avec le cape! Sacre Bleu! Dustin: WHOOOOOOOOOOOO~! FEEL THE ALMIGHTY POWER OF THE COMMON SPATULA, VILLAIN! ::. Jim Le Chef cowers in the far flung and over-preened corner of his kitchen, clutching his hat donning head with his rubber gloved hands as he shakes with much aplomb. Looming above him is a caped Dustin Downey, replete with spandex tights, a crucifix bracelet and a Spatula, gesturing in a manner more akin to the Power Rangers as opposed to the usual Super Hero. The kitchen, otherwise, is devoid of people…bar myself and a handily placed cameraman who wields a small hand held camera with much excitement as the action unfolds before our very eyes. Previously, Dustin and I were promoting the latest Action-Hero Spoof series on Canadian Network television…until Dustin took his JesusMan costume far too literally. Subsequently, I was dragged for a good two hours around the city, before the eventual assault of some back-water French Restaurant, bringing us to this situation right here. It’s midnight now, the children are asleep, and the lunatics are out in force tonight….:: Cameraman: Do you think that’s he’s really going to hit him? I mean, as useless a weapon the spatula is – if it’s wielded by the likes of an utter moron, it’s bound to do some damage… Chris: Yeah…to public property or a cupboard. Dustin couldn’t hit a barn door with an obese housewife and her couch… ::. As if appearing to have Super Human hearing, Dustin AKA JesusMan shoots his head around, frowning beneath a painted on mask, threatening with the Spatula as the Frenchman beneath him continues to cower….:: Dustin: JESUSMAN NEEDS NOT AN OBESE WOMAN AND HER COUCH TO HIT A BARN DOOR! WHOOOOOOOOOO~! JESUSMAN HATES IT WHEN YOU REVEAL HIS SECRET IDENTITY! AND JESUSMAN’S SIDEKICK SHOULD BE ASSISTING JESUSMAN GET SHOT OF THE BLASPHEMOUS MENACE! FOR HEAVEN! ::. Oh sure…and people call me a deranged religious lunatic/cocky bastard….:: Jim Le Chef: Please, for the love of ze God. Let me go. I have children, all thirty of them. Yes, I did sleep with multiple women, but I’ve paid for that…quatre-hundred dollars a pop! Please, if you have a merciful bone in your body, you won’t hit me with a Spatula! Cameraman: Shall I keep filming? ::. Rubbing my hands in glee, I nod with a grin on my face at the sight before me. Having Dustin on tape wearing a Superhero costume is golden…it’s the kind of thing that you’d bring up at company meetings or weddings for the sake of embarrassment or all-out humor. With Downey, it’s both – it’s always good to have someone so naïve around. I plant my ass on a nearby table, only to yelp in minor pain after sitting on a carving fork. OUCH! The Midnight Opus is only beginning. Now beginning to pull himself off the floor, scraping for the sideboard with tears of fear streaking down his face, Jim Le Chef averts his eyes from Dustin in a cowardly motion, looking for the exit with a hopeful glance. Snapping his wrist back, Dustin almost sends the spatula flying out of his hand, across the room into the toaster, before bringing it down onto the Frenchman’s hand…resulting in a loud shriek from the poor dolt that Dustin’s harassing at this time….:: Jim Le Chef: GYAHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHH! Dustin: DYAHAHAAHAHAHAHAHA! FEEEEL THE TRUE MIGHT OF THE COMMON SPATULA VILE VILLAIN! Did that hurt you? Do you need to see a doctor? Well…Doctor JesusMan’s in town, and do you know what he prescribes? Another dose of Whoop Ze Ass! TAKE THAT! Jim Le Chef: GYAHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHH! ::. Snapping his wrist back again, Dustin brings the spatula down and lands another shot to the hand of the Frenchman, who begins to roll along the sideboard in pain. Outside, there is a bit of a commotion, almost like something had ran into a brick wall, a screaming of agony in the distance past the door and along the dark alley that leads to the jealous kid’s place. With some curiosity, I slip Dustin’s attention as the cameraman follows me out of the backdoor, towards the long winding alley that extends quite far into the underbelly of this strange world. Kitted out in the half-assed costume of MisterSarcastoReligiousGimmickMan or whatever Dustin calls it – which encompasses a pair of neon blue trousers and a sleeveless white shirt – stepping out into a narrow alleyway never did feel so foreboding… I’m scared of being raped by the hobos….:: Dustin: ~(Behind door)~ DAMNIT VILLAINOUS CUR! I SAID HOLD STILL WHILST I POKE YOU WITH A PLASTIC FORK! ESPECIALLY THE PLASTIC FORK OF DEATH~! WHOOOOOOOOOOO~! I AM JESUSMAN! PREPARE TO BE JESUS-FRIED! Jim Le Chef: But ze no, Downey! That doesn’t make ze slightest zidbit of sense! JESUS WAS NOTA FRIED! HE WAS CRUCIFIED! Dustin: Ah…who gives a flying Crusade, French-o?!? HOLD STILL! Jim Le Chef: NO! NO! NO! HELP MEEEEE, MISTER CHASE!! ::. The screams continue to be emitted from behind me, and towards me…those of the kitchen being far more vocal and louder than the incoherent babbling of the more interesting phenomenon around the two corners ahead. However, lest I spoil the next episode with what awaits me…let’s recap on my new found situation. Four-Corners of the Squared Circle… Why do they call it that anyway? .:: To be Continued…
