Repost and SS#1

23 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2-23-02 Segment One: Hank and Ganker are walking throughout Portland, Oregon with their hands in their pockets, looking through the windows of the many different shops downtown. Ganker then looks down at his boot, then up at one of the windows. Hank shakes his head and Ganker snaps his fingers in a disappointed fashion. They then see Josh Young flirting with some chic on the street corner who is sliding her fingers together, seemingly wanting Josh to pay her money for some kind of service she provides. Josh fails to look down as Ganker shoves him out of the way and grins at the beautiful you lady. Ganker: 'Hello there young lady, how about fifty for two night?' Attractive Chic: 'How about I make that an hour.' Ganker: 'Damn, that takes away from my midnight entertainment.' (snap fingers in disappointment again) 'Damn...' Hank: 'Yo have Ana Lagunez remember man?' Ganker: 'Oh right~! Thanks man, sorry lady, I'm taken, I was jus' messin' wit' yo.' The young lady slaps Ganker across the face as Josh slips his arm around her and they walk off into a cheap motel and aren't seen for two whole hours. After those hours Josh comes out of the cheap motel with a big grin on his face and an empty wallet. Hank: 'She took the sap for all he had.' Ganker: 'Poor fool...' Josh: (walking over) 'No, no, no, she didn't charge me, she in fact gave me her phone number and told me to give her a call sometime and maybe we could get together... Damn I'm in there good~!' Hank: 'Wonder how yo f**k this good thing up this time.' (laughs) Josh- 'Well maybe I'll sneak up on her to...' SMACK~! Ganker: 'Never ever describe how yo plan to have sex to us again boy.' Hank: 'Or I smack yo again.' (laughs) Ganker: 'Worse yet, yo job to LeBlanc... Wait, Hank already did that...' WHACK~! Hank: 'Stupidity factor goes for yo too boyo.' Ganker: 'Damnit, thought I was special.' Hank- Shows what yo know.' Ganker: 'Well here's what I do know, yo facin' Jason Core and Karl LeBlanc this Sunday Sabotage in your third Extreme Championship Title Defense.' Hank: 'Tell me yo jus' made that up?' (Ganker shakes head) 'Tell me yo f**kin' wit' me.' (Ganker shakes head again) 'A f**kin' great, I have to fight the jobber of all jobbers again and this newbie who doesn't even deserve to breathe the same air as I do, let alone wrestle fo my PCW Extreme Championship. Who the hell is Jason Core anyway?' Josh: 'From what I hear he's tough man, yo may wanna take the guy a little bit more seriously that you are now.' Hank: 'F**k that sh*t up man~! I ain' takin' nobody seriously. That fat f**k can kiss my fat a** for all I care. Jason Core wants some of me, he wants to try to take my PCW Extreme Title I had to work two years to acheive, let the sad motha' f**ka' try his best. I have defended this baby proudly and I'll continue to defend the title proudly through Sunday and even further. I am the best PCW Extreme Champion to day, nobody ain't more Extreme than me.' Ganker: 'Janelle?' Hank: 'Erm, that's my personal business bro.' Ganker: 'Oh right, cool.' Josh: 'Damn that woman was good, and good to talk to afterwards too.' Ganker: 'Yo talk after?' Josh: 'Erm, yeah, when the chic is talkin' to me, other than that, I await seconds.' (grins) Hank: 'What's her name, didn't catch it before yo whisked her away?' Josh: 'Tanya Lorcreania.' Hank: 'She looked foreign, so it makes sense.' Josh: 'She's gorgeous and she likes me. This is a new experience for me.' (grins) Hank: 'Think there'll be a future wit' this one?' Josh: 'Erm, that depends on if she's my type past two weeks.' Ganker: 'If she still wants to see yo ugly face for longer yo mean?' Josh: 'For the most part ye... HEY~!' Ganker: 'He, he, he~! Sucka fell into that one.' Josh: 'I fell for Tanya...' (grins) Ganker: 'Sucka got mad issues.' Hank: 'Sucka needs shut the f**k up before I slug sucka...' Ganker: 'Yeah Josh~! Sheesh, geez...' Josh: 'Oi...' Hank: 'Tryin' to concentrate here~!' Both Josh and Ganker stop bickering and glare at Hank bedazzled. Josh: 'Explain, pronto.' Hank: 'My match this Sunday.' Josh: 'Oh... The boy's mind is warped...' Ganker: 'Tellin' me?' (Josh nods) 'I've been tagged wit' the freak for years as Team Nirvana, the man's mind is never off wrestling and from what Janelle tells me, it's no different at night.' Josh: 'Obsessive.' Ganker: 'I think it has something to do with people around Seattle tellin' him he'd only be used by the big companies and they never would give a sh*t bout'em. Well he figured since he didn't give a sh*t bout himself and he was used anyway, why not got for his dream anyhow?' Hank: 'Don't bring up the past boyo, I must concentrate on the future, which is right now the two a** clowns, Jason Core and Karl LeBlanc, Swamp Freak.' Ganker: 'Though yo were Swamp Freak?' Josh: (mutter) 'Smells like it...' SMACK~! WHACK~! SMACK~! WHACK~! SMACK~! Josh whimpers. Ganker and Hank both laugh. Hank: 'This will be number two for wins over LeBlanc and one over Jason Core.' Ganker: 'I see dead people...' Hank: (slaps forehead and sighs) 'Oi... Stop that or die bytch~!' Ganker: 'Wonder when PCW will remember to book me?' Josh: 'They don't wanna bring the ratings.' (laughs) Hank: 'MAJOR RAITING DROP~!' Ganker: 'I'm always held back, jus' remember, when the day comes for my time, I'll take yo precious title.' (poking Hank in the chest) Hank: 'In yo dreams loser boy.' Josh: 'Calm down ladies, Hank go back to the hotel, Janelle wanted to talk to you about something.' Hank: (grinning) 'Oh really now?' Josh: 'She seemed really homesick.' Hank: 'We were jus' home for a couple days.' Josh: 'True, so true.' Ganker: 'I have to go, Ana probably misses me, as much as I'm missing her right now.' Josh: 'Whatever happened to Lindsey Carter, your old woman?' Ganker: 'Met old boyfriend, something still left between them, I got the usual speech bout how I was an old bum wit' no more wrestlin' future. I had no talent, nothin' left in me.' Josh: 'Damn...' The scene fades out to absolute/complete/utter nothingness.
