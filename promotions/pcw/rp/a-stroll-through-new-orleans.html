A stroll through New Orleans

Date: N/A A typical Sunday, or so did the city of New Orleans think. The day was beautiful, the warm delicate sunlight shined down upon a large parade of happy people. Soft music was heard from a band playing at a resturant patio. The smiling faces of all the people walked pass, continuing with there day. Children played on the street with numberous toys while the parents let them run around the street, beside the small buildings shops and apartment buildings. In the middle of this happy time, a being, in the form of a man stood close to the patio, watching silently. He was tall and broad with his long blonde hair and tied back. His face was of the cleanest skin, his clothes were jeans and a red t shirt with a black leather jacket over top. The man's light blue eyes were closed, but he could still sense the happiness around him. The laughter of all the people roamed in his ears. He felt happy, and just stood there beside the gate of the patio. Laughter and happiness is something he loved, and is used too. Before he arrived, the city experienced a serious car crash, he could still sense the danger and fear that filled the area. Afterwards, the street became empty, and the clouds covered the beautiful sunlight. He could sense this from a far, he ventured here, and spread his delightful magic upon the city. This man's name is Andariel, and he was no man, but an Archangel. An Archangel is a keeper of peace in the heavens, and they very rarely ever visit the living world. Archangels are rare to come across, because only the bravest, good hearted people could become one in heaven. They are very powerful and possess great powers. Andariel was the strongest of Archangels and second only too God in power and magic. Andariel travelled to Louisiana, not in helping people, but too find someone. He was searching for 'The Messiah Of Evil'. A vampire that is the son of Satin was loose in the living world. Andariel came too put a stop to his rain of terror, but took a break to lighten the spirits of these townsfolk using a spell of well being. A minor, but affective spell. Suddenly, a loud disturbing horn was heard down the street. Andariel's eyes opened quickly and veiwed the area. A grey catalac was behind a green van which was about to turn. The man in the grey catalac grew impatient and got out of the car and immediately headed towards the green van. A man walked out of the van and held his hands up, 'I am sorry, I don't want any trouble!' The man tried to explain, but the other man in the catalac grabbed him and tossed him to the sidewalk while pressing a cruise control letting the van filled with children into a brick wall on the opposite side of the street. Andariel took action and ran in front of the car and wall and repelled it with his own body until he slammed his fist into the hood of the car, and using his supernatural strength, disabled it. 'Waaa!' a child creid from the other side of the street. He veiwed it, and saw a young child that tripped on the sidewalk crying. His friends started to laugh and point while his friend lies in pain. 'Shut up allready! I hate that crap music!' A angry man yelled from the patio at the band. All this misfourtune had happened at once, and it was impossible for this to have happen on its own, it must be... him. Andariel from that point, could sense The Messiah Of Darkness, he was arriving closer. Andariel moved to the corner of the street swiftly to get a veiw of the surrounding crowd. 'I have a gift for you, my apprentice.' A tall man similar in looks with Andariel, except for a shaggy goatee and dangerous looking eyes and his blonde hair down to his shoulders. His skin was fleshy, and pale, making veins visible in his frehead and cheeks. He reached his hand out of his pocket in his blue jeans, brushing his hand accross his black muscle shirt. He reached his hand to the shoulder of the man's neck beside him and ripping a chain that was worn around his neck. The man beside him looked curiously at him, with his dark drown eyes that matched his bald head and similar palish skin. Hewas a little shorter, about the heightof 6', and the other about 6'6. He wore a light ADDIDAS t shirt with some no name brand blue jeans, and a black sweatshirt tied around his waist. The taller man held the chain that was revealled to him as a pair of dog tags. 'We need the soul of a virgin' he said veiwing his surroundings, that is, until his eyes caught a glance of a small child laying on the sidewalk wimpering, the taller man stopped walking. 'As you allready know, my powers are useless during the day, so the tasks I am performing are of this small medalion. It is engraved with magic, not strong, but enough to put the misfourtune spell I had just summoned. The power grows the more you use it, I will make one for you, but you must harness and unleash its power correctly. Do you understand my young apprentice?' The taller man asked. 'Yes master, but why can't I not have powers like your own?' The smaller man answered with another question. 'I have demonic blood flowing through my veins making me immortal. But, since I am half demonic (Mother is human, father is demon), I am immortal only half of the day, or in other words, demon at night, and human at day.' The taller man explained. The taller man, he was speaking the pure truth, his name is Frank Ameson, and goes by his wrestling nickname, The Freak. The smaller man, Freak's apprentice and tag team partner, is Igor Castro. They both may be powerful with immortal properties, but both have a similar love for wrestling under their contracts in PCW. They payed much more attention to their true purpose for the world, to gain entry to The Afterworld. Freak pulled out a necklace that was hidden by his shirt. It had a gold amulet that was round and had spikes on the surface, resembling the sun. 'I made this into the shape of the sun, because I only use it in the day, at night I have no use for it. I used this to make those curse spells, and I will use it to embue your new medallion.' Freak walked towards the little boy on the street. He bent down and gazed into the youngsters eyes. Freak patted his shoulder with his large hand, and moved it towards his face. The child began to scream, as did his parent who just noticed him on the floor. The crowd of once happy civilians broke into fear and backed away from Freak feeling the evil energy pulsing. The child floated into the air as his eyes went back into his head. The child's father from the crowd ran to help, but Igor intercepted and pulled out a large handgun from his muscle shirt and pointed it in his face. His mother appeared too, and began to cry next to her husband. A white fog appeared out of the boy's chest and met with the hand of Freak. The boy suddenly dropped and Freak tightened his palm into a fist while walking towards Igor. 'Stop crying! He is not dead, I just took some of his life force, he will recharge in a matter of weeks.' Freak said as the child's parents ran too him. Immediately, Freak held Igor's dog tag over his right palm, releasing the life force. The fog appeared once again and entered the tags changing there shape, into a sun, and moon. Freak handed it back too the smiling Igor, and they continued on. Andariel soon felt the disturbance and was allready running to the source. He ran around the corner to veiw the parents and there child crying at the sidewalk. His senses met with the two men walking away from the incedent. Andariel rose his right hand and directed it towards the small family, he released his holy energy and healed the child. 'Stop you monsters!' Andariel yelled. Freak and Igor ignored, due to the empty threats they had earlier. Andariel grew angry and ran towards them, intercepting just at the next corner. 'I told you to stop!' Andariel said with a low rugged voice and eyes darted at his new targets. 'Ha!' Freak laughed. Freak rose his hand concentrating his energy. A black shadow appeared over his fingertips and shot out in a blaze of energy in almost an instant. The energy wave was deflected easily by the mighty Archangel, leaving Freak and Igor surprised. Freak, still confused, rose his hand again and shot a frenzy of energy shots, but again deflected by the Archangel. Andariel removed his jacket and shirt revealing his broad and muscular chest, and also six large glowing wings, three on each side, growing out of his back. The wings were thin, but stretched out at twenty feet each giving immense flight capabilities. Also, a fade appeared over his body, and golden armour appeared. His hole body was covered, but head exposed. On his side, he had a sword holster about a meter long from the gemmed golden handle. 'I remember now... you are only powerful at night...' Andariel said sarcasticly. 'A mere cupid could finish you off, but in any case, I will.' Andariel said pulling his long sword out of the holster. He held it above his head, but a blade did not appear, but lightning. His whole sword was made of lightning bolts. Freak stepped back, filled with fear, as did his dark apprentice, Igor. Out of despiration, Igor pulled out his handgun and began to take fire, but the lightning from his sword melted the bullets before damage was done. Andariel drew the sword in front of them and a smirk appeared. Freak grabbed the weapon out of Igor's hand while reaching out to the side of the corner, a pedestrian that just exited a building ran across them, but met with Freak's arm. He held the gun at her head. 'Can you let one of your precious humans die just to kill me?' Freak said taking an offensive move. Andariel's face grew sour as he put his sword back in its holster.
