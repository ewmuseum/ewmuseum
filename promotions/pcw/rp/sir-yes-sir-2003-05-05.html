Sir, Yes Sir!

5 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: May 5, 2003 Begin Transmission. (After a long flight, The Order of Society and Logic’s leaders have made it to Yemen. Standing in the Middle Eastern country seems to disgust Michael Chang, who looks around him with a repulsed look on his face. SOCIETY stands stoically, looking around him at the Middle Eastern culture, at the bazaar he is standing near, and all of the people hustling and bustling to and fro.) “SOCIETY, welcome to one of the reasons the Order of Society and Logic exists. This is the Middle East, site of conflict since the cradle of civilization. Disorder, anarchy, and chaos have reigned here. Amazing, since much of the region is wealthy due to oil. Yet, religion is what makes these people unhappy. Mere faith! It is unfathomable!” (SOCIETY nods along, recalling the Second Tenent of the OSL Code. Chang nods to him, as if asking him to recite it, and he does so.) “The Second Tenant of the OSL Code resolves that once the new world society is established, all religion shall be banned. Religion is a divisive force, and all such forces must be eliminated in the interest of creating a perfect society.” (Chang nods, glad to see SOCIETY has taken to his lessons so well.) “Indeed, SOCIETY. Here, Muslims, Christians, and Jews have battled over the holy city of Jerusalem in the Crusades. Now, however, a group of displaced Arabs called Palestinians clamor for their own state. The Israelis have issues with this, and the two groups are battling each other. The world is divided into two different camps, supporting each group.” (SOCIETY nods, yet looks quite quizzical.) “I know this. I have studied this. Why is this significant however?” (Chang nods, solemnly.) “Because, Yemen is the native home of Osama bin Laden, the terrorist who launched the attack on America in 2001. And, consequently, he is guilty of destroying the order of the world.” SOCIETY was quick to respond. “Then, is he on the list of people we need to….remove?” (SOCIETY smiled at the thought of carrying out his first execution.) “Unfortunately, no. No one knows where he is really. But, our political purposes must go on hold for now. We have more important business. You are here to win an important match.” (SOCIETY looks solemn, as he continues to look around him. Despite his size, and age not being known, it is evident he does not have much world experience, as he seems quite awkward in his surroundings.) “I know this, Mr. Chang. And yet, it is difficult to concentrate fully. For the first time, I have seen this part of the world. It is completely different…and yet so the same. As different as this country Yemen is, my goals here are the exact same thing. I want to win. And I will win. That much I can promise!” (Chang nods at his partner, who appears to be more than ready for the impending battle.) “Excellent, SOCIETY. To most men, this would be a test. But to you, SOCIETY, one of the largest, most imposing, and dominant athletes that Primetime Championship Wrestling has ever seen, this match will not even be exercise! Your strength and smarts will guide you to victory, in the name of the Order of Society and Logic!” (Chang reaches over, and punches SOCIETY on the shoulder. The blow seems to psych up SOCIETY, who begins bouncing in place, warming up his arms and legs for battle.) “Yes SOCIETY! THIS IS HOW YOU MUST BE HENCEFORTH! FROM NOW ON, YOU MUST DESTROY OPPOSITION! IT IS ONLY BY LEAVING THEM BLEEDING IN TWITCHING MASSES THAT YOU SHALL SERVE OUR ORDER!” (SOCIETY is caught up in the moment…) “SIR, YES SIR!” “YOU WILL DESTROY KRIKORIAN!” “SIR, YES SIR!” “YOU WILL DESTROY EDWARDS!” “SIR, YES SIR!” “YOU WILL DESTROY WETHERS!” “SIR, YES SIR!” “AND, ON FRIDAY, YOU WILL DESTROY THE CHRISTIAN, SHANE ROBERTSON! HE IS DIRECTLY OPPOSED TO OUR ORDER! HE IS THE MOST IMPORTANT OF ALL!” “SIR, YES SIR!” (Chang slowly begins to calm, panting from the effort of his screaming. He now whispers, forcing the incensed SOCIETY to listen ever more carefully.) “….And what will you make them all DO?!” (SOCIETY’s response is equally softly whispered.) “Conform….to SOCIETY.” (Chang looked into his pupil’s eyes…) “He is READY.” (End Transmission.)
