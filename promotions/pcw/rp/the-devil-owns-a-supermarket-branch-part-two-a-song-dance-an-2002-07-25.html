The Devil Owns a Supermarket Branch – Part Two: A Song, Dance and Courtroom number…

25 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 25/7/2002 The Chris Chase Continued Chronicles – Book Three: Deus Ex Toaster…… RP#1 for PPV (28th July 2002)… Chapter One: The Devil Owns a Supermarket Branch – Part Two: A Song, Dance and Courtroom number… ::.~(Musings of Chris Chase)~ It’s a day, much like any other day, complete with twenty-four hours and the continual bleeping of a watch. However, what makes a day truly memorable is down to the very personal experiences that go on in such a day. For myself, they can be as varied as talking to a giant head and finding myself on the receiving end of a particularly long line of mockery from various benefactors. Yippee. Life is a cliché, it’s over-used…over-referenced, and over-abused by those wacky over-emotional curs that draw highlighting spirals around the word ‘Tedium’. Nothing spectacular, not even worth reaching out into the wide blue yonder and releasing a loud, lengthy scream that’d deafen the ants below. I don’t hate life, I’m pretty sure that it’s quite fond of me too…but what it’s not so fond of is Satanists. And I’m not too fond of them either. Still…I’ve seen more effectiveness in a broken vacuum cleaner than the Dark Ages of late. Notably their effectiveness in trying to end my life… They’ve crucified me, fried me with a mega volt of electricity, beaten me down, chased me around arenas, attempted to hang me…the amount of times that I’ve escaped death nearly parallels the number of times that Xavier Kannon’s thrown a tantrum at me. Like that Scientological loser, the power of the Dark Ages is exaggerated. Not all that long ago, they were unemployed bums picking on the lowest of the low, massacring less-than-innocent jobbers for pennies, to ensure that their rather meager legacy lives on. And then, as soon as a certain Chris Chase returns from a long-overdue vacation, they seize the chance to over-run the PCW for about two cards, before their little alliance with Tyler Nelson becomes particularly trivial as they fail in their efforts to actually kill someone. It was inane, nearly as pointless as the time that Dustin and I had to entertain a Giant floating head to save the Universe. Perhaps if we hadn’t, we would have saved the world from Satanic Jobbers and the whining of former Continental Champions… Don’t blame me…you did call me a Saint after all. Call me conceited, (heck you’ve done so for the past six months, why stop now?), but to call me a Saint is perhaps an understatement. Yes, yes…I know as a man of understatements myself that that can’t truly be an understatement if I’m not a willing Saint. What? I didn’t ask if I could save you all. Was there ever a time that I stopped to consider the ramifications of what I could well be letting myself in for? Or did I simply blunder through without so much as a care, only truly bearing opinions on my own brilliance and the ineptitude of others. Heck, if you need ME to save YOU…how inept are you? And I thought Jesus was a lost cause. PCW referee, moron and long-time friend Dustin Downey is engaged in a courtroom battle of slightly less than epic proportions. For those of you who have managed to keep up so far, you’d remember that episode from about this time last week, when, for some unknown reason, Dustin mauled a vendor at a local Wal-Mart, after signing for forty-four cans of Canned Sprouts and a box of cereal….:: “All rise in the court of law, Madam Justice Anne Quincy residing. Case #2..0..3..2..1..8 – Wal-Mart versus Dustin Downey, with charges of alleged assault on one Harry Good, employee of Wal-Mart…” “Bailiff, Dustin Downey? Haven’t I sentenced him before?” “Yes your honor, on three charges of negligible assault, fourteen charges of minor harassment and seventeen charges of damage to private property…all individual cases I believe…” “Oh great, prepare for a rather long and drawn out case Mister Bailiff, and ensure that the jury do NOT fall to sleep this time. We don’t want another repeat of the January fiasco again do we? If they end up doing the otter impression again, the Superintendent will have my head…do you know just how messy that’ll be, Bailiff?” “I have no idea, your honor. However, I believe we’ve sufficiently drugged the jury hopefully the effects should last until the very end of this case. Of course, they were particularly wary about such drugs, so we had to wave a slice of money in front of their noses…the Wal-Mart Corporation have really pulled out all the stops this time.” “Indeed, Mister Bailiff…” ::. The subtle wooden tones of…well…mahogany add somewhat to the tense atmosphere of the courtroom, as if you could call a court battle involving Dustin ‘Tense’. It’s a day that could be truly memorable for all the wrong reasons. If it ends up being drawn out and long, I could lose my window for launching scathing anti-Satanist propaganda over the media prior to this Sunday’s Pay-Per-View, and Dustin could well possibly miss out on the opportunity to job Xavier Kannon like the infernal bitch he is. Like all good Anti-Kannon protesters – it’s the Pharaoh all the way, baby! Ahem…the Judge for this case is one Anne Quincy, whom Dustin and I have had run-ins with many times in the past. It was she that oversaw the withdrawal of the charges placed on me for the destruction of one factory during the Waldenator Incident, and the whole charade involving Dustin and the midgets. A stout, evil looking woman with glasses that make her eyes bulge out at you, like a pair of ever expanding balloons. She overlooks the rather downbeat and trodden on state of the court, where Dustin and I sit on the defense…opposite the representatives from Wal-Mart and their attorneys on the prosecution. The drugged looking jury sit in rather slumped positions to one side of the room, whilst behind us sit a rather rowdy crowd. Rather than protesting outside the hall of law, due to the hilariously stupid manner of organization from the court representatives, about two dozen Pro-Downey supporters have been allowed to picket inside the courtroom – most of which being Satanists. Maybe there’s something that someone’s FORGOTTEN to tell me again, I don’t know. Still…the room falls quiet as Quincy looks down authoritatively and rather impatiently raps on her desk with a rather large mallet as opposed to the usual hammer….:: BANG! BANG! BANG! FRICKIN’ BANG! Anne: ORDER! ORDER! Or else I’ll hold you ALL in contempt! ::. Sure…like they’re really going to listen to that. Female judges never held that much of a command over a rowdy court. Come on, they aren’t exactly intimidating to hardened courtroom regulars such as these bizarre protesting Satanists. Sure, they may have a wife and kid to go home and torture, but that’s not to say that they’re not terrorizing poor children in their spare time. Say, I wonder how many times Clyde’s been in the lock-up. Beside Quincy stands a particularly shady Bailiff. Why shady? Well, you’d be shady too if the bribe money was pouring out of your shirt pocket. There’s a vile stench of bribery in the room, not that there’s much need for that. Seriously, if anyone should be bribing anyone, it’d be me. The evidence placed on my moronic cohort’s rather substantial, I did actually see Dustin assault the vendor, and the only reason that I can think of for Dustin’s behavior was being ripped off by Wal-Mart. Despite being highly likely, it’s not exactly enough to justify ramming a couple of cans of Canned Sprouts up the Vendor’s nether regions now, is it? Dustin rises from his seat and tweaks his messy tie, clearing his throat in a manner befitting that of many of these uptight courtroom types, looking down at a pocket watch held in his palm….:: Dustin: Ahem…your honor, if you must indeed be aware, my esteemed colleague here, Mister Christopher Chase, will be representing the defense on this particular case – if you would like. Artemis Foul…Prodisium Moronicus… Anne: Yes, Yes Mister Downey, indeed. In the great length of time that I’ve come to know you, oh what a ‘pleasure’ that was, I’ve come to notice that Mister Chase here nearly always ends up bailing you out. Now then, what was all that meaningless babble, are you wishing to further the charges placed on you by harassing the court of law, ARE YOU?!? ::. Still looking at the relic from times since past, Dustin sniggers with much aplomb, apparently attempting to mock Quincy, he squints up with a grin. Tipping his overly large purple hat, he rocks back on his heels…whilst I bury my head in the sheer hilarity of it all….:: Dustin: It’s Latin, your honor. Such tongue is only available to those classes who have enough panache to wander around with gigantic purple hats and large cardboard cutouts of Hall of Famers. You do know what I mean, yeah? Latin? Anne: Why of course I know what Latin is, you pitiful excuse for something semi-unintelligent! And I have, in my right mind, enough knowledge of Latin tongue to tell you that that is NOT Latin. It’s not even worth being called ‘meaningless babble’, Mister Downey. Now then, if you have any decently intelligent bone in your body, could you please leave the desecration of languages until after this hearing? Dustin: As long as I get to play guitar whilst doing so, then I’m ALL yours, baby! ::. Yep, you can feel the reverberations of Quincy’s shudders all the way out in the middle of the Outback, Australia – where a certain Steve Irwin just happens to be wrestling another Viper to the death, but then again, that’s totally irrelevant in the total scheme of things. Unless you consider that Quincy’s interjections are just as venomous as the Viper’s bite…perhaps even more so. With a flick of his wrist, Dustin shoves the pocket watch back into its pocket, and falls to his seat with a sly, yet highly moronic grin plastered all over his face. Yeah, Dustin is complete dimwit, defending him is nothing but for my own gratification, that I can sleep at night knowing that Dustin isn’t terrorizing innocent convicts or letting an abundance of gophers into a prison compound. Glaring down at the two benches, Quincy smirks rather arrogantly whilst creating a large dent in her desk with that overly sized mallet of hers – which seems in proportion to the size of her eyes as she holds up the blasted thing. A representative from the Wal-Mart legal team pushes his chair backward as he stands up, glaring around at the court with a cocky smirk that would make me proud – if I could stand the guy. With a snort, he barely passes little more than a glance at the defense as he walks up to the Judge’s booth….:: Quinton Flynn: Your Honor, delegates of the jury…esteemed Professional Wrestlers… ::. Obviously he’s one of Jim Rome’s biggest fans…as his voice grates at the very mention of my profession…if you could call it that..:: Quinton Flynn: …I’m here to prove without a shadow of a doubt, that this “Dustin Downey”, if that’s his REAL name, is much more than some guy who assaulted an employee of the Wal-Mart Corporation, that Mister Downey is one lowlife who must be stopped at all costs, for the preservation of peace and sanity… Dustin: HA! You villainous cur! Shiver me timbers and hoist the Jolly Roger, you sir are a foul liar. I suggest we keelhaul the slimy landlubber before he gives us all scurvy! ::. A murmur ripples through the attending crowd as that loud, moronic, clichéd, piratey side to Dustin Downey known as Cap’n Dustin rears its ugly head once more…bringing a fairly sinister smile to the lips of Quinton Flynn who swaggers around in front of the crowd of Satanists in attendance, who swipe at Flynn and boo Wal-Mart. I guess it’s their code of honor to completely disrespect major corporations. Funny, considering that Tyler Nelson’s aligned with the Dark Ages of late, somewhat of a major spin factor for the forces of Darkness, these supposed Dark Knights crusading against all that makes this world good and precious. I can’t help but wonder what the top man, or devil of it all is thinking right now. Pardon my musings, but I’ve got that weird feeling again, as per usual…some kind of Satanic tracking device built into me ever since those pests decided that crucifying people such as myself should become an Olympic sport. They’re still petitioning for that, by the way. Flynn, with a glance to Quincy, addresses the court….:: Quinton Flynn: See…the man’s clinically insane, he’s a volatile, vile excuse for a human being who sees it fit to align with everything that makes this wonderful modern society so bloody wrong. I tell you, we should jail the man right now for that ridiculous outburst! But seeing as we can’t…I call Mister Harry Good to the stand… Chris: ~(Sarcastically)~ Yippee… Twelve hours Later… Quinton Flynn: And thus, prior to his assault on Mister Good, Downey seems to be in somewhat of a pathetically insane daze, loitering on private property with intent to maim such a poor individual as Harry Good. Notice as I show this clip for the twentieth time in slow motion, that Downey exhibits symptoms of “Vicious-Attack-Propensity” otherwise known as VAP. Now, on a subsequent DNA examination, and several closer examinations, it can determined that Dustin is genetically a sufferer from VAP…and therefore a threat to HUMANITY! SMACK! Chris: Objection! The only threat to humanity here is the coma-inducing tedium that this hearing has degenerated into. Your honor, this idiot has done nothing but repeat the same argument over and over again, and you’re willing to let him continue for another HOUR? ::. Twelve hours of inane bantering from Flynn’s left me a little sore, much like queuing for a single packet of Wheaties in a communist state, or having to sit through another pro-Satanism conference. And I’ve had enough of not being able to open my mouth in that aforementioned twelve hours. Not a word for twelve hours. Do you know just how painful that is? So I can’t preach, can’t educate these sorry individuals on the merits of their supposed Saint, Chris Chase. Following my outburst, Quincy hammers on her desk for the umpteenth time…glaring at me as I hold my arms up in protest….:: Anne: Well…it’s only in the best interests of the court that Mister Flynn here continues. Objection Over-RULED! Dustin: Aye Laddie…Cap’n Dustin says that we gut the lass and feed her to the Satan Sharks. ARRRRRRRR~! ::. And with that…I slap my forehead….:: Meanwhile…Deep in the depths of hell… “HEY YOU! YES…THE MINION!” “Yes…oh evil one?” “Do these cretins have any idea what they’re letting themselves in for?” “Well…it’s a Chris Chase Promo…it’s pretty damn random if you ask me…” “Have you sent the others?” “Complete with Tap Dancing shoes and a pipe organ? Yes evil one…” “Dyahahahaha! My plan’s almost complete! Where’s that BYRON CALVER?” “Obsessing over Canadian women again sir…” “Go figure…” TO BE CONTINUED!
